On complete tripartite graphs, threshold-two three-colour cyclic competition has only primitive periods \(1,3,4,6,12\), with a sharp nine-step transient bound. The exceptional periods occupy disjoint graph-size regimes: four-cycles require two parts of size two and one of size at least four, six-cycles occur only on \(K_{2,2,2}\), and twelve-cycles require exactly one part of size at most three and two of size at least four; explicit formulas count every such recurrent state.
