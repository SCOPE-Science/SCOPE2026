The three-token graph of \(K_{2,n}\) has domination number \(n^2/4+O(n)\), with explicit bounds \(\binom n2-\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\le\gamma(F_3(K_{2,n}))\le\binom n2-\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+n-2\) for every \(n\ge4\). A Mantel-type pair-covering projection proves the lower bound, while a three-layer construction supplies the upper bound.
