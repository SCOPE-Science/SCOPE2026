Among support meanings definable in finite bilateral state-based logic, non-upward-closed meanings are asymptotically vanishingly rare: their fraction is \((1+o(1))NM_{N-1}/M_N\), where \(N=2^q\) and \(M_k\) are Dedekind numbers. Conditioned on the rare failure of upward closure, almost every support meaning omits exactly one valuation from its largest supporting team.
