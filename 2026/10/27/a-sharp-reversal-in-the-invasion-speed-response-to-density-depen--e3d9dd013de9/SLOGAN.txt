For a strong-Allee population whose individual motility follows \(G(u)=(1-u)(1+bu)\), the first-order change in bistable invasion-front speed is exactly \(\sqrt{2\rho D_0}(1+2a)(4a^2-17a+9)/60\). At the sharp Allee level \(a=(17-\sqrt{145})/8\), increased motility changes from slowing the retreat of an already declining front to accelerating its retreat, even though the sign of the unperturbed invasion speed changed earlier at \(a=1/2\).
