# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
An exact calculation checks all 13 normalized combinations. For each, the binary quartic discriminant is squarefree and independent Groebner Jacobian checks give the unit ideal on all four affine charts; no extension-field truncation. The three coefficient vectors are linearly independent. The upper bound is the three-dimensional first-jet target at any rational point; the no-rational-basepoint corollary follows by the same rank argument. Smooth (2,2) divisors are connected genus-one curves by Kunneth and adjunction.
