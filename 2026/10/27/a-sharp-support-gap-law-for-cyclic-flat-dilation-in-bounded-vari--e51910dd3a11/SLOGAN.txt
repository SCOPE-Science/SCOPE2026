On a finite cycle, every consecutive-window maximum is nonexpansive for *differences* in discrete total variation, but removing the central tap from a three-site window yields exact amplification factors \(1,2,3\) in sizes \(3\), \(4\)–\(5\), and \(6\) or more. The gapped filter's one-input variation factor never exceeds \(2\), exposing a strict distinction between variation reduction and perturbation stability.
