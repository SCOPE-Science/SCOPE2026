For stationary Gaussian analytic functions whose spectral density vanishes to even order \(2m\) at a boundary angle, the mean zero intensity has a sharp universal crossover within angular width \((1-r)^{1/(2m)}\), with profile \(1-[b/(b+a x^{2m})]^2\). This yields the precise leading expected zero count in shrinking boundary sectors and distinguishes the angular regime from previously known fixed-direction asymptotics.
