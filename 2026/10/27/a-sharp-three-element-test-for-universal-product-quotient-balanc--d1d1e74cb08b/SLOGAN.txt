A finite group has \(|AA|=|AA^{-1}|\) for every subset \(A\) exactly when it is an elementary abelian \(2\)-group or \(C_3\), \(C_4\), or \(C_5\). Every other finite group has an unbalanced subset of size at most three; the minimum size is two exactly when two noncommuting elements have unequal squares, and otherwise is three.
