For an instantaneous tracer pulse in a homogeneous advective–diffusive medium, three exact readings from two stations identify the unknown mass, diffusivity and positive drift globally precisely when the repeated station is sufficiently downstream and \(x_2/x_1<2\sqrt{t_1/t_2}-1\). Outside that sharp geometry there are ambiguous positive parameter triples, while the fourth corner of the station–time rectangle always removes the ambiguity.
