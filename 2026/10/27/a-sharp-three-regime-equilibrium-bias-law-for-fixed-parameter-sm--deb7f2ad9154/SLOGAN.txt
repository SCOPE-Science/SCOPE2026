For fixed-parameter smooth-clipping error feedback on three equal-curvature quadratic clients, a unique biased equilibrium undergoes a sharp transition at clipping amplitude \(\varphi=2a\): persistent bias below it, cube-root recovery at it, and square-root recovery above it. With sufficiently strong feedback retention and a small step, this biased equilibrium is locally attracting even though the true objective is strictly convex; two clients cannot exhibit the same bias in the equal-curvature class.
