Two strengthened springs at separation \(d\) in an infinite harmonic chain support a second localized phonon mode exactly when \(2(2d-1)\alpha\beta>\alpha+\beta\); under a fixed stiffness budget, equal splitting uniquely best protects this mode. At equal strengths the new squared-frequency branch opens quadratically above the band edge with coefficient \((2d-1)^4/d^4\).
