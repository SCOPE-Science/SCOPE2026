## Correctness
PASS. The omitted reversal-pairs have total divergence \(k(k-1)\) over the \(k(k-1)\) left-semi-symmetric triples. Uniform triples force at least \(k\) omitted pairs, each contributing \(-1\); at most \(\binom{k}{2}\) nonuniform pairs can contribute \(2\), and all other nonuniform pairs contribute at most \(1\). This gives \(r\ge\binom{k}{2}+2k\) and the stated period bound.
