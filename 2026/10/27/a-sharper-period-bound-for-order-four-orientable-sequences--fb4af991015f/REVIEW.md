## Correctness
The cyclic factors must be distinct up to reversal across all starting positions in a period, and none may be palindromic. Under these hypotheses, the two cyclic walks have disjoint edge sets and their union is balanced. Counting the omitted reversal-pairs by divergence on the left-semi-symmetric vertices, and using parity at uniform triples, proves the upper bound. The two exact cases also use explicit published cycles whose lengths match the upper bound.

## Originality
The graph model and parity constraints are present in Theorem 2.22 and its setup in *New orientable sequences*, arXiv:2507.02526. The new uniform bound is smaller by \(3k/2\). The previously exhibited cycles of lengths \(106\) and \(603\) supply lower bounds, not an all-even-alphabet upper bound. The distinct contribution assessed here is the uniform upper bound, not either witness or a standalone priority claim about an isolated alphabet size.

## Value and limitations
The stronger bound determines the maximum at \(k=4\) and \(k=6\) when combined with the cited constructions, and sharpens the earlier general bound for every even \(k\ge4\). No general attainment result or classification of extremizing cycles is claimed.
