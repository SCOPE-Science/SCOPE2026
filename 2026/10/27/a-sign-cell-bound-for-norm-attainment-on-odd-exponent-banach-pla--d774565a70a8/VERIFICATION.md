The bound was checked by a symbolic proof: (1) at most four projective sign sectors occur; (2) on each sector the norm-attainment equation is a nonzero polynomial of degree at most \(p\); (3) every maximizing root is multiple; and (4) root multiplicity bounds the number of maximizing directions by \(4\lfloor p/2\rfloor\), hence the number of unit vectors by \(4(p-1)\) for odd \(p\). The identically-zero case is excluded by the mixed coefficients: they either force a scaled signed permutation or, if a row or column is missing, contradict a positive pure-power coefficient. No numerical experiment is used as a universal certificate. The bound is not claimed sharp. Independent audit and independent validation have not been performed.
