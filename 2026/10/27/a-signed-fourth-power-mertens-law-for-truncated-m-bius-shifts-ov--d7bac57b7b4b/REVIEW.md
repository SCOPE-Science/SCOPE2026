# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** A local classification by the three valuations of each prime in the shift gives the exact degree-bounded CRT correlation. The union bound for large prime squares is uniform in degree. The elementary prime polynomial identity gives an exact Mertens expansion with an exponentially small truncation correction, and the signed Euler factors have an absolutely convergent nonvanishing normalization. Their unique negative cases are linear primes not dividing the shift. The proof covers every fixed nonzero binary shift and every cutoff satisfying the explicit capacity inequality; exact finite calculations support the local formulas but are not used as infinite proof.

**Originality (PASS; residual literature risk).** Chatterjee's recent article studies the same truncated Möbius correlations through a bivariate Erdős–Kac theorem, not this exact signed binary Euler constant and two-term correction. Darbar–Mukhopadhyay's accessible correlation results are set up in odd characteristic and their truncated application uses Liouville rather than this squarefree truncation. Carmon's characteristic-two work holds the polynomial degree fixed and grows field size. Semantic mathematical-record searches under shifted-truncation and characteristic-two aliases did not surface this implication. Nevertheless, an unstated local-CRT corollary of general sieve theory, or an unindexed source, is a genuine residual risk; search absence is not uniqueness proof.

**Value (PASS).** This determines the precise rate and signed leading constant of a two-point correlation in the binary-field case, exhibits a substantive change of sign depending on the two linear factors of the shift, and proves two universal relative correction terms within a natural logarithmic cutoff range. It is not a reparameterization of the recent qualitative cancellation statement, nor a finite table recomputation.

**Limits.** The uniformity window is \(N\ge D_M\), the shift is fixed, and this does not settle all cutoff regimes. Implicit prior coverage and independent mathematical validation remain outstanding.
