For the truncated Möbius function on \(\mathbb F_2[t]\), every fixed nonzero shift has a sharp two-point correlation law \(\kappa_a M^{-4}(1-2/M+7/(3M^2)+O_a(M^{-3}))\) when the polynomial degree exceeds the complete small-prime-square CRT modulus. The nonzero leading constant is negative exactly when the shift has one root in \(\mathbb F_2\), and positive otherwise.
