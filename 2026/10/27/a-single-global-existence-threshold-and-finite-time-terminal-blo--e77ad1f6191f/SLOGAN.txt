In \(x'=\sin t\,x^2+(a+\cos2t)x^3\), the unique repelling positive cycle exists exactly above one intrinsic threshold \(a_*\in(-1,0)\), and its profile decreases strictly with \(a\). At the lower threshold the orbit disappears by a finite-time blow-up from a finite positive initial condition, while at zero its amplitude is asymptotic to \(2\sqrt{-a}\).
