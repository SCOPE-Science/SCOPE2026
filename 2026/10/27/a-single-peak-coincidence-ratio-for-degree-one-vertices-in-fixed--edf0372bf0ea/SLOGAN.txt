In a uniform \(m\)-edge labelled graph with \(n\ge6\) vertices, the ratio of the probability that two fixed vertices both have degree one to the product of their individual degree-one probabilities is strictly log-concave in \(m\) and peaks uniquely at \(m=\lceil n/2\rceil\). Positive covariance can therefore occur only in one contiguous edge-count window; for eight vertices the window is exactly \(m=4\).
