The centered total path length of a positive-weight recursive tree has a linear-scale almost-sure and \(L^2\) limit exactly when its weighted depth-innovation energies are summable. For attachment fractions \(p_j\sim cj^{-a}\), the variance changes law at \(a=1/2\) and crosses from superquadratic to quadratic at the sharp threshold \(a=2/3\).
