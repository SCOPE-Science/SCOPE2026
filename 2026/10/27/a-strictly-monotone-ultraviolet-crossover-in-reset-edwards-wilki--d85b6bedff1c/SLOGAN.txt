For a Poisson-reset Edwards–Wilkinson interface with a sharp Fourier cutoff, stationary one-point excess kurtosis increases **strictly** with the dimensionless reset-to-cutoff rate \(r/(2\nu\Lambda^2)\), from \(12/\pi-3\) to \(3\). Consequently one fourth-moment measurement uniquely identifies the ultraviolet cutoff when the relaxation diffusivity and reset rate are known.
