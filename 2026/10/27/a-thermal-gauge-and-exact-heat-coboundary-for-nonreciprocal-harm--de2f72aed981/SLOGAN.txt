For a nonreciprocal harmonic chain, a positive diagonal thermal gauge makes \(cQ_L(t)+bQ_R(t)\) exactly an energy difference at every finite time; at calibrated bath temperatures its stationary long-time law is the difference of two \(\operatorname{Gamma}(N,\Theta)\) variables. On general connected harmonic networks, directed cycle products give the sharp obstruction to such a gauge and, with baths at every site, to ordinary velocity-reversal detailed balance.
