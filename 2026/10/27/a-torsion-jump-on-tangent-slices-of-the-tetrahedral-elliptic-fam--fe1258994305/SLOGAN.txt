On the tangent tetrahedral slice \(P=mt+4/m^2\), the section \((X,Y)=(m^2t^2+16t/m,0)\) has order two. Together with the inherited order-three section, it gives the full Mordell--Weil group \(\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\), while the generic non-tangent slice has rank one and torsion \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\).
