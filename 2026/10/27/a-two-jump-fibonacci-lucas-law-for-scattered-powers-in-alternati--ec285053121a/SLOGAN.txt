For every power exponent \(q\ge2\), the number of distinct nonempty roots \(u\) with \(u^q\) embedded as a scattered subsequence of \((01)^k\) follows a closed Fibonacci law and changes only when \(k\equiv0,1\pmod q\). The complete root-length distribution has an explicit rational generating function, and the two jump sizes are consecutive even-index Lucas and Fibonacci numbers.
