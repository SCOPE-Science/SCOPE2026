For every characteristic-three one-pole genus-six Artin–Schreier curve \(y^3-y=x^7+a x^5+b x^4+c x^2+d x\), the Jacobian's a-number is exactly \(3\) when \(a\ne0\) and exactly \(4\) when \(a=0\). The Cartier-iterate ranks are respectively \((3,1,0)\) and \((2,1,0)\), so the higher-a-number locus is an irreducible hyperplane in the monic reduced coefficient chart.
