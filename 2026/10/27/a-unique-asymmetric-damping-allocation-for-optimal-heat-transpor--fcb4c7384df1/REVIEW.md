# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For strictly positive spring constants, the deterministic drift is Hurwitz by a mechanical-energy Lyapunov argument. The exact stationary second-moment identities derive the heat current for any positive frictions and distinct positive bath temperatures. Minimizing its denominator is a strictly convex one-dimensional problem with divergent endpoint barriers, establishing uniqueness for every parameter value. Explicit asymptotic manipulations yield the finite weak-contact friction limit and the strict balanced-versus-optimal current gap. Eight exact-rational Lyapunov calculations and six optimizer-grid comparisons passed, but the proof does not rely on finitely many computations.

## Originality — PASS, subject to priority uncertainty

The nearest 2013 primary treatment concerns **overdamped** thermally coupled Brownian particles; the 2015 symmetric oscillator heat-flow paper lacks stiffness asymmetry. A 2021 paper supplies a general spectral-integral current for coupled quantum oscillators, including classical limits, but its inspected model and current sections do not state or prove the fixed-total-Ohmic-friction optimization, the unique asymmetric split, or its weak-contact saturation limit. These broader formulas may facilitate derivation; priority beyond examined accessible sources is not guaranteed. Targeted literature searches and a public mathematics-findings search found no stated equivalent claim in the material returned, which is evidence of search coverage only, not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A fixed friction budget is a natural reservoir-contact resource constraint. Rather than merely evaluating a heat-flow expression, the theorem provides its global optimal design over the entire positive friction simplex, shows how trap asymmetry controls the direction of optimal allocation, and resolves a singular large-budget regime where the weaker optimal contact approaches a nonzero finite constant. The enhancement over the symmetric design can be large for strongly unequal traps, while vanishing at exact symmetry. The result is deliberately restricted to the classical dimer and makes no unproved general transport claim.

## Limitations and closest literature

The result assumes exact model parameters, equal unit masses, independent white-noise baths, and a sum-of-frictions budget. Ciliberto et al. (arXiv:1301.4311), Barton (DOI:10.1088/0953-8984/27/21/214005), and Henkel (DOI:10.1002/andp.202100089) were compared. The full 2015 Barton paper was not obtained. The 2021 spectral formula is materially broader at the level of forward heat-flow representation, and specialized prior optimization studies remain a priority risk. There has been no external validation.
