In discrete-time SI spreading on a star with a uniformly random index case, all finite detection horizons rank hub-containing and leaves-only monitor sets identically, and the optimal type switches at the sharp equation \(1+q+\cdots+q^{k-1}=n-k\). Continuous-time exponential spreading has no rate-dependent switch: its optimal type depends only on whether \(n-k\) exceeds \(k\).
