For every nonsingular real symmetric indefinite matrix, a one-vector Gaussian Nyström approximation has a sharp spectral-error tail \(\Pr(E>t)\sim C(A)/t\); its expected spectral error is infinite, with positive moments finite exactly below order one. Rejecting nearly zero scalar sketch cores makes the mean finite but incurs the sharp cost \(C(A)\log(1/\epsilon)+O_A(1)\), where \(C(A)\) is the area-weighted null-cone coefficient.
