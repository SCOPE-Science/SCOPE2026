For any norm-one operator \(G:H\to K\) between complex Hilbert spaces with \(\dim K\ge2\), an explicitly constructed square-zero partial isometry \(S\) satisfies \(m_G(SG)=1\) but \(v_G(SG)\le1/2\), a globally sharp uniform gap. Thus relative spear operators between complex Hilbert spaces are precisely the norm-one operators with one-dimensional codomain, without a norm-attainment assumption.
