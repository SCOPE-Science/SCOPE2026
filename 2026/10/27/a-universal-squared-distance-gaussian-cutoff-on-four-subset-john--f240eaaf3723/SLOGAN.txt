On every Johnson graph of 4-subsets with at least eight ground elements, the kernel \(q^{d(A,B)^2}\) is positive semidefinite exactly for \(0<q\le\rho\) or \(q=1\), where \(\rho\) is the unique root of \(1-4q+6q^4-4q^9+q^{16}\) in \((1/4,1/3)\). At the sharp nontrivial boundary its rank is precisely \(\binom n3\), and an eight-point obstruction rules out every intermediate damping value.
