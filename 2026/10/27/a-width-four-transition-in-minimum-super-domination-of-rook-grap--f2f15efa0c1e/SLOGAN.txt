For rectangular rook graphs, every minimum super dominating set is the complement of a non-surjective row/column transversal or a uniquely specified pair of crossed stars, with the precise cases classified. The exact number of minimum sets changes from exponential growth at fixed widths \(3\) and \(4\) to the quadratic count \(p(p-1)q(q-1)\) at every fixed width \(p\ge5\); the previously established minimum cardinality alone does not determine these counts.
