All minimum \(1\)-limited dominating sets of the generalized Petersen prisms \(\operatorname{GP}(n,1)\) and the skip-two graphs \(\operatorname{GP}(n,2)\) are classified by constant or period-four/period-five column words. Their exact counts are \(2+4\mathbf 1_{4\mid n}\) and \(2+10\mathbf 1_{5\mid n}\), respectively, revealing arithmetic jumps in the number of optimal sets although the minimum size is always \(n\).
