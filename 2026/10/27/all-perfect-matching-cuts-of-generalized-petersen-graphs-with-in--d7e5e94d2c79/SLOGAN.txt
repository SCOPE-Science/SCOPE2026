For every \(n\ge5\), the generalized Petersen graph \(\operatorname{GP}(n,2)\) has exactly \(1+5\mathbf 1_{\{5\mid n\}}\) perfect matching cuts. Its perfect two-colorings with exactly one oppositely colored neighbor per vertex consist only of the layer partition, except at multiples of five, where five cyclic period-five patterns appear.
