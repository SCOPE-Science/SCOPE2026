For high-order centered cardinal splines, the Zak-transform values of order \((2/\pi)^m\) occupy an exact frequency layer of width \(1/m\), with an explicit universal complex profile and exact limiting sublevel-area function. The nondegenerate critical zero has a sharp real Jacobian asymptotic \(8\pi m(2/\pi)^{2m}\); the earlier uniqueness of that zero is not claimed as new.
