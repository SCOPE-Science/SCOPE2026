The complete Jordan-type frequency distribution for multiplication by homogeneous quadrics in the sixteen-dimensional squarefree complete intersection \(\mathbb F_q[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]/(x_i^2)\) has seven explicit strata for every odd prime power \(q\). A joint rank–hafnian classification makes the frequencies jump between three arithmetic regimes according to whether \(q\) is a power of three, \(q\equiv1\pmod3\), or \(q\equiv2\pmod3\).
