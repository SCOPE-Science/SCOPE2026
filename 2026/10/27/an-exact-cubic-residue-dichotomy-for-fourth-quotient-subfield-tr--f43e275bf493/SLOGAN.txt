For every prime power \(q\equiv7\pmod{12}\), the number of elements representable as a subfield element plus a \(3(q+1)/4\)-th power in \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) is exactly \(q(q+2)/3\) or \(q(2q+1)/3\), according as \(2\) is or is not a cube in \(\mathbb F_q\). The three non-base subgroup cosets project onto at most two cubic-residue classes, determining all representation multiplicities and showing that neither branch covers the extension field.
