# Scientific review

The cyclic-product trace identity and common determinant reduce spectral-radius comparison to products of the first entries of powers of the baseline matrix. Positive off-diagonal transitions force adjacent-year strict maximality. Positive spectral weights and the sign of the second eigenvalue give the three complete minimizer regimes. Exact-rational direct products checked 1,989 placements across 17 parameter families and 13 periods, including the six-year factorizations. The proof addresses all permitted real parameters; the finite program alone would not establish it.

Tuljapurkar and Haridas (2006) consider a stochastic environment and Altenberg (2013) treats different spectral-product comparisons. None of the consulted material supplied the exact all-period rank-one two-favorable-season minimizer theorem. The 2002 primary full text and some broader matrix theory remain inaccessible or incompletely checked; an equivalent prior theorem remains a residual originality risk. This assessment is not independent validation.
