# Scope of the result

The result treats the physical drift optimum at fixed diffusivity and well spacing. The known current is used as an input, and the exact conclusions concern global uniqueness, strict dependence on asymmetry and the limiting rate scale. The strict derivative inequalities and the optimizer equation give an all-parameter proof; the checker tests a finite set of roots. No unwrapped dispersion result, dispersion optimum, or priority over unidentified equivalent results is asserted.
