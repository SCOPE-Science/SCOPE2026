# Scientific assessment: asymmetric instantaneous-reset Brownian ratchets

## Correctness — PASS

Exponential Brownian excursions have symmetric Laplace endpoint distributions, whose offset-basin integer-tail sums give the exact first two reset-site step moments. Differentiation with respect to the exponential rate gives the **joint** waiting-duration/step moment, which a naive independent-increments treatment omits. The renewal reward central-limit variance rate supplies the asserted diffusion coefficient; the finite exponential-age residual is negligible on long-time scales. Strict inequalities for \(u\coth u\) and \(u/\sinh u\) prove the sign and unique optimizer with correct positive-parameter quantifiers. The packaged checker separately numerically integrates Gaussian excursion tails and passes 44 assertions. Limits are long-time only; no finite enumeration replaces the proof.

## Scientific value — PASS

The central exact formula quantifies a directly interpretable transport statistic (diffusivity) under the asymmetric capture geometry that induces net current. It identifies a non-negligible negative duration–step correlation and a robust low-rate regime of **simultaneous** rectification and diffusivity enhancement—distinctions not obtained from a steady current alone. Strict uniqueness and asymmetry monotonicity of the physically optimized velocity give a quantitative rate-selection principle. This is a motivated non-Gaussian transport problem, not a parameter substitution into the already-published current or symmetric-diffusion formulas.

## Scope and unresolved comparisons

The result excludes the current formula as a priority claim; it applies to pure Brownian unwrapped displacement, instantaneous Poisson capture in period-\(L\) asymmetric basins, and \(0<c<1\). There is no claim of finite-time variance, optimal diffusivity, uniqueness of diffusion-threshold crossings, or general finite-time physical return. An inaccessible or unlocated more general transport result could encompass the derived exact dispersion; the literature search and peer review are not exhaustive.
