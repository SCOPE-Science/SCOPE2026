For the free-boundary \(3\times3\) Ising grid, the unique Poisson-union intensity attached to the central five-site cross changes sign exactly at \(\beta_*=0.2328565913441155\ldots\). Its negativity proves that the nine-spin law is not Poisson representable for any \(0<\beta<\beta_*\), but positivity of this one intensity above the threshold does not prove representability.
