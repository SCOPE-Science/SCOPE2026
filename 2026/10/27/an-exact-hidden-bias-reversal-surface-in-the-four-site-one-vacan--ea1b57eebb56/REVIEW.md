# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The four-state vacancy generator has explicitly specified jump rates and is irreducible for every admissible parameter choice. Its four stationary cofactor weights are positive; direct determinant expansion gives \(\sum_iw_i=P/8\) and the unnormalized signed current \(abcd\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon^2)/2\). The quotient establishes the rational current. Its exact even part factors as \(-4abcd\varepsilon^2(1-\varepsilon^4)(a-c)(b-d)(a+c-b-d)/(P(\varepsilon)P(-\varepsilon))\). Strict positivity of the two denominators proves the necessary-and-sufficient all-bias classification and the fixed rectification sign. Independent symbolic-generator tests validate each calculation. No finite experiment is used as a universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified best-knowledge

Sakai and Akimoto formulate the model and discuss bond-ratio/inversion symmetry and generic quadratic rectification. Derrida's arbitrary-period hopping formula includes the raw four-state vacancy drift as a special case, so the rational current is *not* claimed as a newly discovered general formula. The new mathematical content is the explicit complete full-bias oddness classification in this particular exclusion sector, including a codimension-one balanced-sums family of fully asymmetric four-site profiles, and the exact global sign of the even response. Related earlier two-particle four-ring cancellations are not all-order classifications for three particles. Targeted mathematical-record, earlier claim, and published-literature searches found no explicit equivalent classified surface; this is not evidence of world-wide uniqueness. Stronger general-walker theory remains an important qualification and independent literature-wide priority is unverified.

## Value — PASS

The motivation is the physically concrete question of whether absence of both standard microscopic symmetry mechanisms really forces directional rectification in a fixed disordered exclusion sector. Rather than one tuned zero Taylor coefficient, the result classifies **every** trap profile with exact current oddness **for all allowed biases**, exhibits nonreflection examples with entirely distinct trap rates, and proves no finite-bias sign reversal of rectification in the complement. These are qualitative transport-symmetry boundaries, not arbitrary computed digits. The one-vacancy reduction and absence of a general-density assertion limit extrapolation.
