**Correctness — PASS.** The exact fitness polynomials are reconstructed from the 2023 source, and the Fourier transform proves that the center is the only interior equilibrium. The planar Jacobian gives the sink threshold. The two hyperbolic vertex eigenvalues and the standard saddle transition give an attracting boundary whenever the explicit contraction ratio exceeds one. The basin boundary, compactly contained in the interior and without equilibria, then contains a periodic orbit by Poincaré–Bendixson. The symbolic identities were executed from the accompanying `verify.py` and passed; no numerical trajectory is substituted for proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual publication-priority uncertainty.** The 2023 Griffin–Wu paper already defines the model, states the interior stability boundary and observes subcritical cycles; neither those ingredients nor the mere phenomenon of cycling is claimed as new. Its periodic-orbit regime is assessed numerically, while the new contribution is the exact all-parameter nonempty sufficient interval (a quantitative guaranteed persistence range), global basin-separation proof, and independent Fourier exclusion of other interior equilibria. The 2023/2024 spatial follow-up uses different interaction matrices and studies diffusion-induced traveling waves. General planar heteroclinic separatrix arguments are classical but do not furnish the source-specific interval without the new computations. Related model-specific priority could remain in less easily searchable literature.

**Value — PASS.** The interval provides a rigorously guaranteed region of bistability and cyclic separatrices in a previously numerically mapped game. Its explicit positive width varies with triadic competition and contraction parameters, so it is usable as an analytic benchmark for numerical bifurcation surfaces. The claim is not a generic textbook deduction in isolation: the necessary global equilibrium exclusion and sharp local inequalities are established for a structurally specified model.
