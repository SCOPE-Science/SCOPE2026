# Scientific assessment: quadratic-conjugate Bergman approximation

## Correctness — PASS
The normalization integrates to one and gives the exact radial moments \(m_n=\Gamma(n+1)\Gamma(\alpha+2)/\Gamma(n+\alpha+2)\). The orthogonal Bergman projection of \(\overline z^{\,2} e_n\) vanishes for degrees zero and one, and otherwise has coefficient \(\sqrt{m_n/m_{n-2}}\) in mode \(e_{n-2}\). Distinct residual modes have distinct angular frequencies and are orthogonal, so their exact squared norms give the entire sharp inequality and equality spaces by Parseval. This diagonalization is proved in `RESULT.md` and does not require spectral assumptions.

The exact rational formula for \(V_n\) holds for all integers \(n\ge1\) and real \(\alpha>-1\), while the degree-zero mode is strictly suboptimal. The sign of \(V_{n+1}-V_n\) is the sign of a quadratic whose unique root in the admissible real-weight range is \(\tau_n\). Its sign decreases strictly with \(n\), proving single-peakedness, successive sharp thresholds, and precisely two adjacent extremizing degrees at each threshold. The radical asymptotics prove the large-weight degree and constant limits. `verify.py` recomputes these algebraic identities exactly and corroborates projections and finite mode decisions over a range of weights. It is corroboration rather than infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, best knowledge, with explicit priority risk
The full accessible arXiv text of Bourass–El-Fallah–Marrhich–Naqos (arXiv:2102.01743v1) was inspected for its introductory theorems and Section 8. Its results concern trace bounds and asymptotic singular-value decay, while Section 8 gives a linear-conjugate example; they do not state the precise all-weight quadratic approximation-maximizer staircase and equality spaces. The accessible full text of Peláez–Perälä–Rättyä, *Hankel operators induced by radial Bekollé–Bonami weights on Bergman spaces* (2019 online), provides boundedness criteria for radial weighted Hankel maps rather than sharp coefficient-mode optimization. Pau's arXiv:1502.04721 addresses Schatten-class membership. The distinct Fourier-frequency-staircase result in the mathematical-result index concerns circle restriction/trace inequalities, not minimization of quadratic-conjugate approximation over the entire Bergman space. A separate Hardy polynomial Hankel result concerns a different operator and geometry. These comparisons address mathematical implication, not just titles.

An early Arazy–Fisher–Peetre paper (1988), *Hankel operators on weighted Bergman spaces*, is pertinent, but the full text was inaccessible; the references available through later articles emphasize boundedness and ideal membership. This creates a genuine historical priority risk, although it supplies no identified stronger statement that subsumes the exact all-weight staircase. A hidden equivalent expression in classical monomial Hankel calculations remains possible. Index searches alone do not prove novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The second anti-holomorphic monomial is the first natural test beyond the linear symbol. The exact infinite staircase is qualitatively different from merely proving compactness, boundedness, or asymptotic singular-value decay: the best norming degree changes infinitely often as the weight changes, the first bifurcation occurs at \(\sqrt7\), and all extremizers—including two-dimensional transition spaces—are classified. This is a complete exact sharp constant problem motivated by approximation on a standard Hilbert family, not an arbitrary parameter slice. It is useful for testing sharp analytic projection bounds and finite-mode norm approximation. No claim is made for higher symbols or unrelated domains.
