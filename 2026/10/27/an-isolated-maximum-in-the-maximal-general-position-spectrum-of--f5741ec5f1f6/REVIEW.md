# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The geodesic-interior condition for a chosen cycle vertex is translated exactly into independence in the graph of cycle pairs whose geodesic runs through that vertex. An explicit matching gives the universal upper bound. A matching-sized independent set produces a witness, and every possible one-vertex extension is excluded. The full leaf set is therefore uniquely largest. Exhaustive small cases and separate finite matching tests confirm the boundary but are not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS
The established maximum general-position number of a cycle corona, the existence of the all-leaf configuration, and the smallest maximal general-position number are not claimed as novel. The new proposition distinguishes the unique maximum from every other inclusion-maximal set and evaluates the second-largest possible maximal cardinality, exhibiting an unbounded interval of missing maximal-set sizes. Comparison with the first systematic lower-general-position paper, the general-position-polynomial paper, the six-sunlet polynomial calculation, published research-index results, and accessible earlier statements did not reveal this sharp all-cycle-length assertion. A logically equivalent unpublished or differently indexed theorem remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The maximal general-position spectrum refines both the largest and smallest maximal general-position numbers. Establishing a linear-sized forbidden interval in an infinite class of connected maximum-degree-three unicyclic graphs provides a structural boundary rather than a numerical restatement of the known maximum. It also distinguishes maximal configurations from ordinary general-position-polynomial coefficients, which remain positive for all leaf-subset cardinalities.

## Closest literature and limitations
The relevant antecedents are Ghorbani et al. (arXiv:1903.04286, exact maximum of corona graphs), Di Stefano et al. (arXiv:2306.09965), Iršič et al. (DOI:10.1007/s00025-024-02133-3), and Rather (arXiv:2603.06930). The result does not enumerate all maximal sets, nor classify the lower part of their cardinality spectrum, and it applies only to one-leaf cycle coronas. It has not undergone independent mathematical validation.
