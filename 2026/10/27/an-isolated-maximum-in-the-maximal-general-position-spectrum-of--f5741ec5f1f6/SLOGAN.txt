Every sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\), with \(n\ge4\), has a unique largest general-position set of size \(n\), but its next-largest inclusion-maximal general-position sets have only \(\lceil n/2\rceil+1\) vertices. Thus connected subcubic unicyclic graphs have arbitrarily long gaps in the cardinality spectra of their maximal general-position sets.
