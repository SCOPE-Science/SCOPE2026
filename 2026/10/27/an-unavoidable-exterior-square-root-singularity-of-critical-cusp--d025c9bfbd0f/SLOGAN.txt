The unique invariant density of every critical square-root cusp map in the parameter interval \(1<\vartheta<2\) is holomorphic near its physical interval, yet its analytic continuation has an unavoidable inverse-square-root singularity at the exact exterior point \(1+(2-\vartheta)^2/(8\vartheta)\). The singular coefficient is strictly nonzero, excluding every rational invariant density in the family.
