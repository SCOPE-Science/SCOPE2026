For a single lossy vertex on a cubic Möbius ladder, a particle starting across the opposite rung is completely absorbed except when the half-size satisfies \(n\equiv3\pmod6\) or \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), when its exact survival is \(2/n\) or \(4/(3n)\), respectively. A cyclotomic sign argument proves these are the only resonances and determines the full absorbing-subspace rank.
