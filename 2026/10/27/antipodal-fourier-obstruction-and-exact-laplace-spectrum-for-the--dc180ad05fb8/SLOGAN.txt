Taking the maximum of two shortest-arc circle distances destroys the positive definiteness of every nontrivial Laplace covariance \(e^{-aD}\), even though the corresponding Euclidean supremum-norm kernel and individual circle kernels are positive definite. An antipodal Fourier boundary term obstructs every \(C^2\) profile with \(f'(\pi)\ne0\), and an exact two-dimensional Fourier calculation locates all negative Laplace modes.
