Two positive symmetric quadrature rules with the same affine exactness and vanishing \(O(\varepsilon^2)\) discrepancy can force a one-degree-of-freedom HJB policy solver into an exact, globally reached two-cycle of size \(5\varepsilon^2/6\), even for fixed smooth coefficients and a uniquely solvable consistent discrete equation. The residual-form counterpart converges instead with exact factor \(2\varepsilon^2/3\) to a biased root.
