Every ordinary Kloosterman Artin–Schreier curve \(y^p-y=x+c/x\) over \(\mathbb F_p\), for prime \(p\ge5\), has exactly \(p+3\) arithmetic forms, all given by four explicit model families with a complete rational-point spectrum. Each such geometric curve has at least one form with no \(\mathbb F_p\)-points.
