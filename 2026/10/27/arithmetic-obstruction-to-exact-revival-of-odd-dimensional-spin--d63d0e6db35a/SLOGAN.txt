For every odd hypercube dimension \(d\geq3\), the central eight-mode harmonic sector of the spin-coupled Grover walk has no exact scalar or projective global revival at any positive integer time: its characteristic coefficient \(A_d\) is not an algebraic integer. In particular, non-reviving pure states form an open dense full-measure set, sharply contrasting with the known fixed harmonic sector in even dimensions.
