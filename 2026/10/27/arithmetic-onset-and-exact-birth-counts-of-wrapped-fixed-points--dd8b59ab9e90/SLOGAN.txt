For a unit-weight path of \(m\) discrete Huygens oscillators, the very first noncritical torus fixed points appear exactly at \(h=\pi(1-1/p)\) when \(p\) is the smallest odd prime divisor of \(m\), and at \(h=\pi\) when \(m\) is a power of two. Their exact threshold and immediate-post-threshold multiplicities are explicit and depend on the arithmetic of the network length.
