# Arithmetic Picard lattice and zeta function of the characteristic-two 21-node K3 double plane

## Finding

Let \(Y\subset\mathbb P_{\mathbb F_2}(1,1,1,3)\) be the weighted-degree-six surface
\[
w^2=x_0^4x_1x_2+x_1^4x_0x_2+x_2^4x_0x_1,
\]
and let \(X\to Y\) be its minimal resolution. The smooth surface \(X\) is a supersingular K3 surface with geometric Picard rank \(22\) and Artin invariant \(1\). The integral geometric Néron–Severi lattice is generated by the \(42\) \((-2)\)-curves indexed by the \(21\) points and \(21\) lines of \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_4)\), as established by Dolgachev and Kondō.

For this specific model over \(\mathbb F_2\), the complete **arithmetic** Frobenius and invariant-lattice calculation is:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\det(t-\operatorname{Frob}\mid\operatorname{NS}(X_{\overline{\mathbb F}_2})\otimes\mathbb Q)
=(t-1)^{15}(t+1)^7,\\
\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{NS}(X/\mathbb F_2)=15,\qquad
\operatorname{disc}\operatorname{NS}(X/\mathbb F_2)=256,\\
\operatorname{NS}(X/\mathbb F_2)^\vee/\operatorname{NS}(X/\mathbb F_2)
\cong(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^6\oplus\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z.
\end{gathered}
\]
**Integral generation:** the \(28\) sums of the geometric curve classes over their \(\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb F_4/\mathbb F_2)\)-orbits generate the **entire** invariant Néron–Severi lattice, not merely a finite-index sublattice.

Consequently the complete Hasse–Weil zeta function is
\[
\boxed{Z(X/\mathbb F_2,T)=\frac{1}{(1-T)(1-4T)(1-2T)^{15}(1+2T)^7}.}
\]
Equivalently, for every integer \(n\ge1\), with \(q=2^n\),
\[
\boxed{\#X(\mathbb F_q)=1+q^2+q\bigl(15+7(-1)^n\bigr)
=\begin{cases}1+q^2+8q,&n\text{ odd},\\1+q^2+22q,&n\text{ even}.\end{cases}}
\]
Thus \(\#X(\mathbb F_2)=21\), \(\#X(\mathbb F_4)=105\), and every divisor class becomes defined over \(\mathbb F_4\).

## Assumptions and scope

The equation and resolution are taken **over \(\mathbb F_2\)**, not merely over an algebraic closure. The minimal resolution exists and is defined over the ground field. The arithmetic Néron–Severi group denotes divisors on this fixed \(\mathbb F_2\)-model modulo algebraic equivalence; over a finite field it identifies with the geometric Néron–Severi classes fixed by absolute Galois. No assertion is made about arbitrary twists of the supersingular Artin-invariant-one K3 surface, or about integral generation of its Picard group by only the \(14\) individually rational curves.

Dolgachev–Kondō's geometric construction supplies the verified \(21+21\) configuration, its integral generation, the absence of additional geometric divisor directions, and the ordinary \(A_1\) singularities of \(Y\). The new calculation concerns the action of the specified ground-field Frobenius and the **primitive** integral fixed lattice.

## Proof

**1. The \(\mathbb F_4\) configuration.** Over \(\mathbb F_4\), the singular locus of \(Y\) consists exactly of the \(21\) points of \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_4)\), each an \(A_1\) singularity. Let \(A_p\) be its exceptional \((-2)\)-curve and let \(B_\ell\) be the inverse-image \((-2)\)-curve corresponding to an \(\mathbb F_4\)-line. The latter construction and the assertions that these \(42\) curves generate \(\operatorname{NS}(X_{\overline{\mathbb F}_2})\) **integrally** are established in [1, Sections 3.3 and 4.2]. In the ordered basis of formal generators, their intersection matrix is
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}-2I_{21}&P\\P^t&-2I_{21}\end{pmatrix},
\qquad P_{p,\ell}=\begin{cases}1,&p\in\ell,\\0,&p\notin\ell.\end{cases}
\]
The resulting integral lattice is \(\mathbb Z^{42}/\ker M\). Its rank is \(22\), and its nondegenerate intersection form has discriminant \(-4\), as independently reconstructed by the verifier and consistent with the established geometric \(U\perp D_{20}\) lattice.

**2. Arithmetic Frobenius.** Squaring coordinates is an involution on \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_4)\), fixing exactly the seven \(\mathbb F_2\)-points and seven \(\mathbb F_2\)-lines. As the model is defined over \(\mathbb F_2\), arithmetic Galois permutes \(A_p\) and \(B_\ell\) by the same coordinate squaring. There are \(14\) fixed curves and \(14\) two-element orbits, hence \(28\) orbit-sum generators.

Construct an integral basis for the image lattice of \(M\) using column Hermite normal form, thereby quotienting the full integral relation lattice rather than taking only rational linear combinations. In this basis the Frobenius matrix \(F\) is integral, satisfies \(F^2=I\) and \(F^tGF=G\), and has trace \(8\). Its \(22\)-dimensional rational representation therefore has \(15\) eigenvalues \(+1\) and \(7\) eigenvalues \(-1\).

**3. The primitive fixed lattice.** Apply unimodular integer column operations to \(F-I\). They produce a primitive rank-\(15\) integer kernel basis \(W\) (the last \(15\) columns of an integral unimodular transformation matrix). Direct exact determinant and Smith computations on \(W^tGW\) yield
\[
\det(W^tGW)=256,\qquad
\operatorname{SNF}(W^tGW)=\operatorname{diag}(1^8,2^6,4).
\]
Here \(\operatorname{diag}(1^8,2^6,4)\) abbreviates eight \(1\)'s, six \(2\)'s, and one \(4\). Expressing the \(28\) orbit sums in the primitive basis \(W\), their column Hermite normal form has determinant \(1\). This establishes index-one generation of the full arithmetic invariant lattice. Every step is an **integer** or rational matrix operation reproducible by `VERIFIER.py`. In particular, neither the Gram rank nor a numerical approximation alone is used to establish primitivity.

**4. Cohomology and point counts.** For a supersingular K3 surface of geometric Picard rank \(22\), the geometric divisor classes span \(H^2_{\mathrm{\acute et}}\) with \(\mathbb Q_\ell\)-coefficients for \(\ell\ne2\). Frobenius on the second cohomology therefore has eigenvalues \(2\) (multiplicity \(15\)) and \(-2\) (multiplicity \(7\)). The first and third cohomology vanish and the top eigenvalue is \(4\). The Lefschetz trace formula gives the stated zeta function and all extension-field counts.

There is also a direct check of the counts: the purely inseparable map \(Y\to\mathbb P^2\) is bijective on \(\mathbb F_q\)-points for every finite \(q=2^n\). Resolving a split \(A_1\)-point replaces one point by a rational \(\mathbb P^1\), increasing the count by \(q\). For odd \(n\), exactly seven singular points are rational; for even \(n\), all \(21\) are. This gives \(q^2+q+1+7q\) or \(q^2+q+1+21q\), independently confirming the formula.

## Verification

`VERIFIER.py` uses exact \(\mathbb F_4\) arithmetic and SymPy integer matrices. It rebuilds the \(21\) projective points, their \(21\) dual lines, the incidence matrix, all \(42\) divisor intersections and the Galois permutation; it computes a primitive integral basis for the geometric lattice, verifies determinant \(-4\), obtains the primitive integral fixed basis, and checks discriminant \(256\), Smith invariants, and orbit-sum index \(1\). It separately enumerates points on the smooth Mukai \(\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2\) model over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\), finding \(21\) and \(105\), respectively.

Run `python VERIFIER.py` with SymPy installed. Successful output begins `VERIFY_OK`. These computations verify **finite exact matrix assertions**. Their connection to the infinite geometric claim uses the specific geometric generation theorem in [1], and the statement for all field extensions follows from cohomology or the separate resolution point-count argument.

## Relationship to prior work

Dolgachev and Kondō [1] establish the supersingular surface, its \(21+21\) configuration, integral geometric divisor generation and the involution induced by \(\mathbb F_4\)-Frobenius. These are foundational inputs, not new discoveries here. Katsura and Kondō [2] reconstruct the same configuration via a generalized Kummer model, and Dolgachev [3] provides the broader projective-configuration viewpoint. These primary sources describe the geometric object and Frobenius's action on labels, but the inspected passages do not state the **integral \(\mathbb F_2\)-invariant lattice**, its Smith invariants or trace index, nor the displayed surface zeta function. The present claim is their arithmetic lattice-theoretic extraction, not a new geometric construction.

## Limitations

This theorem pertains to the particular \(\mathbb F_2\)-descent given by the equation. It does not classify other descents or prove anything about their rational divisor lattices. Its integrality depends critically on the published fact that all \(42\) classes generate the *full* geometric lattice. The explicit calculation is a finite certificate reproducible in SymPy, not a formal proof assistant certificate or an independent external audit. Since the primary literature already determines the full geometric intersection pairing and Frobenius permutation, the main residual originality risk is an existing but unlocated calculation of their primitive fixed lattice under equivalent notation.
