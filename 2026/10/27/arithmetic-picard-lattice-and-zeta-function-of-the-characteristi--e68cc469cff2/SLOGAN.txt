For the explicit Artin-invariant-one K3 double plane over \(\mathbb F_2\) with \(21\) geometric nodes, the arithmetic Picard lattice has rank \(15\), discriminant \(256\), and discriminant group \((\mathbb Z/2)^6\oplus\mathbb Z/4\); it is integrally generated by the \(28\) Frobenius orbit sums of the classical \(42\)-curve configuration. Its Hasse–Weil zeta function is \(1/((1-T)(1-4T)(1-2T)^{15}(1+2T)^7)\).
