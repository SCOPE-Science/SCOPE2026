Changing only the zero-th amplitude of a cubic Alltop chirp gives linearly complete finite Gabor tomography for every nonnegative amplitude other than \(1\) whenever the prime length is \(3\pmod4\). At prime lengths \(1\pmod4\), the *entire* exceptional-amplitude set is an explicit finite quadratic-residue resonance list; its first demonstrated failure occurs at length \(17\).
