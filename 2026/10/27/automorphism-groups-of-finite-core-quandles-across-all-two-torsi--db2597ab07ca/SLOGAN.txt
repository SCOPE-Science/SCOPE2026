The full automorphism group of every finite abelian core quandle has an explicit two-torsion coset normal form and the exact order \(|A|\,|\operatorname{Aut}(2A)|\,(u-1)!(u!)^{t-1}t^{ut-1}\). For products \(R_m\times R_n\), the canonical affine subgroup has index exactly \(1\), \(2\), or \(4\), completely resolving the even-modulus cases; in particular \(R_2\times R_4\) has \(128\) automorphisms, twice its affine subgroup.
