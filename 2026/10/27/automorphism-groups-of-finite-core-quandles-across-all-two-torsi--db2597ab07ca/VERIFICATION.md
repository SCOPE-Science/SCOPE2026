# Checks and scientific limits

The proof establishes every finite-order assertion by group actions and congruences. A separate deterministic verifier enumerates all bijections for small test quandles and checks the defining binary operation pointwise, then enumerates normal-form lifts independently for representative mixed and equal 2-power types. Running `python verify.py` returns `VERIFY_OK`.

Exhaustive enumeration is finite evidence only; the infinite collection of finite groups is covered by the mathematical proof, not by testing. The verified claim does not concern nonabelian groups or a complete abstract group decomposition. Independent audit has not been performed.
