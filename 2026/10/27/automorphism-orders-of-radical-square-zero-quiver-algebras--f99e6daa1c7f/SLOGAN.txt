For the radical-square-zero path algebra \(A=\mathbb F_qQ/J^2\), the automorphism-group order factors into inner motions from non-loop arrows, linear changes within arrow blocks, and vertex permutations preserving arrow multiplicities. The exact order is \(q^{E-L}|\Gamma_Q|\prod_{u,v}|\operatorname{GL}_{m_{uv}}(\mathbb F_q)|\).
