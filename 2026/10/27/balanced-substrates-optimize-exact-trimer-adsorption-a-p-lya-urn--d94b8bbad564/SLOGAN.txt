For equal-rate three-vertex-path adsorption on \(K_{a,3t-a}\), perfect coverage is most likely on the most balanced bipartition, and its full composition profile is exactly a two-colour \((2,1)/(1,2)\) Pólya urn. At the extreme feasible imbalance its probability is asymptotic to \(\Gamma(1/3)(2/3)^t t^{1/6}/\sqrt\pi\), while the normalized profile has a Gaussian width of order \(\sqrt t\).
