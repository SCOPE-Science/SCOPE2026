Below the logarithmic Bergman boundedness threshold, the degree-\(N\) Hilbert-matrix sections grow like \((\log(N+2))^{1-\alpha/2}\) for \(1<\alpha<2\); after this normalization their full fixed-rank singular spectrum is exactly governed by the positive zeros of \(J_{(\alpha-1)/(2-\alpha)}\). A logarithmic-coordinate Hilbert–Schmidt limit identifies a weighted Volterra operator and yields the complete leading singular-value mass.
