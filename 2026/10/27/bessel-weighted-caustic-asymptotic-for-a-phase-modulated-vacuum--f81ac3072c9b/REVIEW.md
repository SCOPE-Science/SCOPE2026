# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The trace-free profile is vacuum. The Jacobi--Anger expansion gives the mean profile \(J_2(2\epsilon)\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\). After the time scaling \(\tau=\sqrt a\,u\), a bounded-primitive averaging argument yields the constant-coefficient Jacobi system; its determinant has a simple first positive zero at \(\pi/\sqrt{|J_2(2\epsilon)|}\). A uniform small-time expansion excludes earlier zeros.

## Originality

**PASS, with stated residual risk.** The inspected general-plane-wave paper establishes the Jacobi/variational framework but not this phase-modulated asymptotic. The inspected homogeneous-plane-wave paper treats constant-rate rotating profiles and exact oscillator/VVM formulas; the nonconstant rate \(\theta'(u)=\Omega(1+\epsilon\cos\Omega u)\) falls outside that class. Semantic literature searches for phase-modulated plane-wave caustics, Bessel sidebands, and weak-amplitude conjugate-time laws returned no statement matching this object and parameter regime. Older work on astigmatic conjugacy was identified bibliographically through the primary paper, but its full text was not inspected in this run; general periodic-Jacobi literature remains a residual priority risk.

## Value

**PASS.** The first caustic time controls the focusing scale of null geodesics and the zero structure relevant to wave propagation. The formula identifies a physically interpretable modulation coefficient and the leading weak-amplitude focusing time, rather than merely restating an exact trajectory formula. For small fixed modulation, \(J_2(2\epsilon)=\epsilon^2/2+O(\epsilon^4)\), so the coefficient also quantifies the modulation-depth penalty.
