For the vacuum plane wave with polarization angle \(\theta(u)=\Omega u+\epsilon\sin(\Omega u)\), fixed modulation with \(J_2(2\epsilon)\ne0\) gives first conjugate time \(T_c(a)\sim\pi/\sqrt{a|J_2(2\epsilon)|}\) as \(a\downarrow0\). The phase modulation creates a nonzero mean tidal component that determines the leading focusing time.
