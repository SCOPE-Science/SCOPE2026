# Verification and limits

The proof checks the vacuum condition directly from \(\operatorname{tr}A_a=0\), computes the mean profile from the Jacobi--Anger identity, and proves convergence of the rescaled first-order Jacobi system using a bounded periodic primitive and a near-identity transformation. The limiting determinant has a simple first positive zero; the uniform small-time expansion \(Y_a(\tau)=\tau I_2+O(\tau^3)\) establishes that this is the limit of the *first* zero, not merely of some zero.

No finite numerical experiment is used as proof. The result assumes fixed \(\Omega>0\) and fixed \(\epsilon\) with \(J_2(2\epsilon)\ne0\). Higher-order corrections and the joint limit near zeros of \(J_2\) are unproved. The independent audit channel has not been performed.
