# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With the stated Brinkmann sign convention R_uu=-tr A, so the metric is vacuum and the transverse equation has sign B''=AB. The entry combination Q_11+iQ_12 is exp(2i theta); Jacobi–Anger picks J_{-2}(2epsilon)=J_2(2epsilon), a real coefficient, so the mean is cD. Put h=sqrt(a), tau=hu and Y=hB(tau/h); the rescaled Jacobi data are unchanged. For the lifted system M, choose the bounded primitive P of M-mean(M) with P(0)=0. Differentiating I+hP(tau/h) gives exactly M-mean(M), so conjugation gives W'=(I+hP)^(-1)(mean(M)+hMP)W. This yields an O(h) compact-interval bound by Grönwall for both Y and its derivative. The averaged determinant is sinh(sqrt(|c|)tau)sin(sqrt(|c|)tau)/|c| for either sign of c. It crosses zero simply at pi/sqrt(|c|). Finally the Volterra integral equation and the uniform bound on Q give Y/tau=I+O(tau^2) for small positive tau with constants independent of a; its determinant is then positive on the same initial interval for every a. Together with positive determinant on compact intervals away from zero and opposite signs across the simple crossing, this proves existence and convergence for the first positive zero, not just for a subsequence of caustics.
