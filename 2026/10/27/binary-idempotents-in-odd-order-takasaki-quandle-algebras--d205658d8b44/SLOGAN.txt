For every finite odd-order abelian group \(A\), all idempotents of the binary Takasaki quandle algebra \(\mathbb F_2[T(A)]\) split into independent zero-or-norm-one choices on Frobenius character orbits, giving an exact product count. On \(R_5\) there are \(12\) idempotents, on \(R_{15}\) there are \(768\), and on \(T(C_3\times C_3)\) all \(512\) elements are idempotent.
