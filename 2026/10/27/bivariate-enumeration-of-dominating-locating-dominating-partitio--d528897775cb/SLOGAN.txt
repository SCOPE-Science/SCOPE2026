For every labeled cycle \(C_n\) with \(n\ge5\), typed partitions into a dominating class and a locating-dominating class are exactly the cyclic binary words avoiding `000`, `111`, and `00100`. The full distribution by the number of locating-class vertices is determined by a rational bivariate generating function with denominator \(1-uz^2-u^2(z^3+z^4+z^5)-u^3z^6\); its already-known univariate coefficient sequence is credited, not claimed as new.
