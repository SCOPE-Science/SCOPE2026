The sharp supremum norm of the quadratic-reproducing Richardson combination \(2B_{2n}-B_n\) converges to an explicit Poisson variational constant exceeding \(1.395\), while every Gaussian interior evaluation has the strictly smaller limiting norm \(1+4[\Phi(-\sqrt{\log2})-\Phi(-\sqrt{2\log2})]\). Consequently, maximizing evaluation points concentrate in boundary layers of width \(\Theta(n^{-1})\), a stability effect invisible to interior Gaussian approximation.
