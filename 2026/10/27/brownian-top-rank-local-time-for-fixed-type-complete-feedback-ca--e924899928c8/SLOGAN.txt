For a uniformly shuffled deck with a fixed number \(n\ge2\) of card types and growing multiplicity \(m\), the greedy complete-feedback score satisfies \((T_{n,m}-m)/\sqrt m\Rightarrow\tfrac12 L_1^{0,\mathrm{sym}}(G)\), where \(G\) is the gap between the two largest centered Brownian bridges. This identifies the fixed-type score law for \(n\ge3\) through the local time of top-rank collisions, extending beyond the previously known compensator limit.
