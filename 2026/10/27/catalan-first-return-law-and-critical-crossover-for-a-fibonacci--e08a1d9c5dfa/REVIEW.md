## Originality

PASS, subject to historical priority. The closest primary literature is Ji and Ma (2023), section 5.1, which already provides the auxiliary map, level transition kernel, and recurrence threshold. The proposed contribution is specifically the map-normalized all-time **first return to the natural four-level core**, its exact critical survival and parameter-window interpolation. Wang (2012) establishes related classical random-walk first-passage machinery, and Garcia-Millan et al. (2014) describe a different branching-generation survival observable. Actual published-finding corpus searches for the source, map aliases, Catalan first-return, and critical error-function crossover found no statement implying this exact combination. Unindexed papers remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The core separates the four nonhomogeneous levels from the homogeneous geometric-jump tail. Quantifying returns to it provides a natural statistical refinement of the source's qualitative conservation/dissipation dichotomy. The exact first-return distribution and critical scaling function are informative even without proving any downstream theorem for the original bimodal dynamics.

## Boundaries

This is **not** a theorem for physical-time returns of the original Fibonacci map or its induced map. Classical Catalan generating-function identities are not claimed original. The pointwise fixed-\(u\) crossover is not advertised as uniform in growing \(u\). No independent mathematical audit was performed.
