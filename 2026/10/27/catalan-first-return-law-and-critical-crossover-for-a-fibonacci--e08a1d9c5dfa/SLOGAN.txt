The auxiliary countable affine Markov model used for Fibonacci bimodal maps has an exact Catalan first-return distribution to its natural four-level core. At the recurrence-to-escape threshold \(\lambda=1/2\), its exact survival tail is \((2/15)\binom{2n}{n}4^{-n}\), and the full \(n^{-1/2}\) parameter window has an explicit error-function crossover; this does not describe physical-time returns of the original map.
