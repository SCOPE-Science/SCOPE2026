For every radical-square-zero quiver algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), the minimum number of additive commutators needed for a prescribed radical target is exactly \(\lceil\log_{q^2}\chi(H)\rceil\), where \(H\) is the graph of its nonzero arrow coefficients. A weighted coloring formula also counts every prescribed commutator fiber, and complete graphs give arbitrarily large commutator width over a fixed finite field.
