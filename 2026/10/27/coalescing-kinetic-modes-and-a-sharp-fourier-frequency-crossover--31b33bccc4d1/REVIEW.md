# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The claim concerns the precisely defined spatial Fourier response of the uniform-momentum BGK kinetic equation, not the microscopic quantum chain. Its renewal transform follows from the Poisson first-refresh decomposition and the standard Bessel transform. Rationalization yields an exact two-mode term plus a regular square-root resolvent. An explicitly paired branch-cut contour produces the positive-frequency oscillatory remainder, whose square-root endpoint admits a classical Fourier endpoint expansion. The remainder is uniform on compact positive frequency intervals; the critical-frequency identity is then exact in its elementary part, proving the stated uniform error. The zero-frequency normalization and the necessity of absolute rather than relative oscillatory errors are stated. The replayable independent-numerical-route program passed 47 finite comparisons; neither these comparisons nor any sampling is substituted for the analytic limit proof.

## Originality — PASS, with a material residual risk

The starting kinetic equation, renewal construction, arcsine velocity law and transform are known from Coppola (2025), including the transform factor in Appendix A. The precise same-object comparison is therefore an *explicit implication at the transform level*: the published formula already makes it possible to derive the present claim. The inspected primary text primarily establishes real-space ballistic-to-diffusive behavior, not a complete fixed-wave-number pole classification, sharp branch-edge coefficient, or the critical \(t^{-2}\) crossover with a compact-uniform remainder. The literature comparison included Romatschke (2016), whose pole/branch-cut onset analysis concerns a different relaxation-time kinetic system; a general claim of pole onset would be covered, and is not made here. Focused research-record searches and accessible earlier claims did not identify the present quantitative statement. The material remaining risk is that this exact sharp analysis might be regarded as already implicit in the published kernel or in broader BGK spectral methods. Independent originality validation is not performed.

## Value — PASS

Coppola's spatial-density crossover does not determine when a fixed Fourier density mode ceases to relax monotonically, nor how finite measurement time resolves the transition. The exact threshold and critical \(t^{-2}\) layer answer a natural kinetic relaxation question, while the nonzero \(t^{-3/2}\) branch-edge amplitude isolates a physically different continuous-spectrum contribution. The full profile (which can change sign) is relevant to interpreting time-resolved intermediate-wavelength density modes; no unestablished experimental sensitivity is claimed.

## Limitations

The conclusion is restricted to the continuum arcsine-velocity BGK model with initially uniform momentum, fixed positive refresh rate, and positive frequencies bounded away from zero. No microscopic error estimate, uniform zero-rate limit, or finite-noise experimental validation is provided. The transform and the general concept of onset transitions are known; the originality judgment is self-review rather than independent.
