In the arcsine-velocity continuum kinetic model of a dephased lattice gas, two Fourier relaxation modes coalesce exactly at \(|k|=\lambda\), turning nonoscillatory decay into damped oscillations. The critical response is \(2\lambda t e^{-\lambda t}\), with a sharp \(t^{-2}\) frequency crossover given by an explicit sine/hyperbolic-sine function and a quantified \(t^{-3/2}\) branch-edge correction.
