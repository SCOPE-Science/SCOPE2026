# Scientific scope

The arrow-local coefficient calculation gives independence on a forest by a change to root labels and edge differences. Conversely the rank deficit of a circuit makes a positive contribution to the difference between the actual joint zero probability and the independence prediction. The labelled independence system, not a complete prescribed-fiber formula, is the claim. The supplied finite enumeration verifies the full marginal factorization test on a small multigraph but does not prove the general theorem.
