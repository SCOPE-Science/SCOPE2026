# Self-review

## Correctness (PASS)
The object is the full order-preserving contraction monoid on a labeled \(n\)-chain. The normal form follows from unit-step images and idempotency. For two interval retractions, direct clipping composition shows that overlapping intervals give exactly the intersection retraction, while disjoint intervals give two different constant compositions. The one-dimensional Helly property and singleton-point retractions prove the maximal commuting subband classification. For every ordered \(m\)-tuple, specifying the exact intersection independently constrains the maximum of the left endpoints and minimum of the right endpoints; their counts are two finite differences of powers. Convolution proves the generating functions; Eulerian-pole coefficients and uniform convolution estimates yield the stated density and probability limit. Boundaries \(n=m=1\), disjoint intervals, and the fixed-\(m\) condition are included. The independently constructed exhaustive checker validates 30 finite parameter configurations and 102,917 candidate tuples, but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality (PASS within checked coverage)
The 2021 Umar–Zubairu treatment of full contraction semigroups and Zubairu–Umar–Aliyu's 2022 treatment of their idempotent subsemigroups establish basic structure and individual idempotent counts; those are specifically not claimed new. Their inspected mathematical material does not state the present all-tuple commutation/rank distribution, limiting conditional Beta law, or maximal commutative point-star classification. A 2023 paper treats a different partial order-decreasing contraction semigroup and was accessible only by abstract. Numerical coefficients \(1,7,26,70,155,301,\ldots\) for the pair-total sequence already occur in OEIS A293311 and A061926, so they are not claimed new as numbers. Searches for interval-retraction, pairwise-commuting, Eulerian, and Beta formulations in relevant records did not return a semantically equivalent result. This is a best-knowledge originality assessment, not a proof of priority; an inaccessible relevant full text and differently phrased interval-graph literature remain risks.

## Value (PASS)
The commutation relation is the intrinsic multiplication obstruction in the idempotent band. Classifying all maximal commutative subbands is a structural classification, while the two-variable Eulerian expression resolves the complete natural rank invariant of products of an arbitrary fixed number of commuting idempotents. The limiting common-product rank law describes the typical internal size of such commutative configurations and retains nontrivial information even though the pair-total integer sequence is known. No downstream theorem or publication-scale significance is asserted.

## Independent validation and limitations
The verification is computationally bounded and independent mathematical validation is absent. The statement excludes general nonexpansive, partially defined, order-reversing, and nonidempotent transformation families; the rank limit has fixed tuple length. Full access to the 2023 partial-contraction paper was unavailable, so its abstract alone does not certify noncoverage.
