# Scientific scope

The finite count distinguishes every possible common-product rank for ordered tuples at every arity. Its proof counts both endpoint extrema of the common image and leads to an exact conditional tail ratio. The Beta limit is an application of this finite law, not a new order-statistic distribution. Interval clamps, their pairwise test, and the point-star consequence are structural background rather than separately claimed original theorems. The finite composition checker corroborates the rank counts on its parameter grid without proving the general identities.
