The idempotents of the finite monotone-contraction monoid are interval retractions: they commute exactly when their intervals meet, and their maximal commuting subbands are the point-stars. Every ordered tuple length has an exact Eulerian-polynomial generating function for the common-product rank; at fixed tuple length \(m\), the commuting probability tends to \(2^m/\binom{2m}{m}\) and the conditional normalized product rank tends to \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,2m)\).
