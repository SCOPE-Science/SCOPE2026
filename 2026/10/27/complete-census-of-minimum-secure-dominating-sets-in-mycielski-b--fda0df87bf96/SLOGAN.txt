For every \(m,n\ge5\), the Mycielskian \(\mu(K_{m,n})\) has exactly six possible class-occupancy profiles for minimum secure dominating sets, yielding the labelled census \(m^2n^2+m^2\binom n2+n^2\binom m2+(m+n+1)\binom m2\binom n2\). A fixed 196-profile exhaustive classification proves the formula uniformly, beyond the previously known minimum cardinality.
