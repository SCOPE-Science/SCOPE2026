For every \(n\ge4\), all minimum total dominating sets of the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\) are classified by a short list of labelled forest component profiles, and their exact number is a closed factorial expression depending on \(n\bmod3\). Although the known optimum cardinality varies monotonically, the numbers of optimum configurations oscillate markedly, and almost every optimum for \(n\equiv0\pmod3\) leaves one underlying vertex unused as \(n\) grows.
