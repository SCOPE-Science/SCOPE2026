For a complete graph with two equal-size communities and uniform cross-community conductance \(x^2\), the exact canonical cut–cycle endpoint norms exhibit a cycle-edge reentrance at community size \(n=5\), with thresholds \(x=3/7\) and \(x=1\). The sharp norm suprema over all positive conductances and finite community sizes are \(1+\sqrt5\) for cuts and \(2+\sqrt5\) for cycles; neither is attained at a finite size.
