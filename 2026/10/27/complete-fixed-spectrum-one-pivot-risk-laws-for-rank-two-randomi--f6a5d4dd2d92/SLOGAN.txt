For one diagonal-weighted randomized Cholesky pivot on a real rank-two positive semidefinite matrix with nonzero eigenvalues \(\lambda_1>\lambda_2>0\), the possible residual-trace laws, allowing the matrix dimension to vary, are **exactly** all finite probability laws supported on \([\lambda_2,\lambda_1]\) with mean \(2\lambda_1\lambda_2/(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)\). This gives attained, spectrum-sharp convex-risk, variance, and upper-tail bounds even though identically spectrally conditioned matrices can have deterministic or maximally variable errors.
