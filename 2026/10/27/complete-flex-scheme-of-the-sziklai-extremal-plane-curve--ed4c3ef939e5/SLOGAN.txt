The smooth degree-\(q\) Sziklai extremizer in every odd characteristic has Hessian section exactly \(q(2q-3)P_0+(q-3)\sum Q_t\): one rational point of tangent contact \(q\) and \(q\) conjugate points of contact \(q-1\). For \(q>3\) these are all its flexes, and the nonrational flexes split into \(q/p\) Frobenius cycles of length \(p\) when \(q=p^e\).
