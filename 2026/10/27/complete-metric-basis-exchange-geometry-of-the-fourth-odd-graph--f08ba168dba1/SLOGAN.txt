The fourth Odd graph has exactly \(149{,}184\) minimum metric bases; their single-vertex-exchange graph is connected, has \(6{,}331{,}500\) edges and diameter \(6\). Exactly \(3{,}024\) bases have eccentricity \(6\), and all others have eccentricity \(5\); the same classification holds for the equivalent resolving sets of \(J(7,3)\).
