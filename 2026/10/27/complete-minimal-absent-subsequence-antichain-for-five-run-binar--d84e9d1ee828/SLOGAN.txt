The complete minimal-absent-scattered-subsequence antichain of every binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d0^e\) has cardinality \(7+B+C+D+BC+CD+BCD\), where \(B,C,D\) record whether the three interior runs have length at least two. Its sharp maximum is \(13\), attained exactly when all three interior lengths are at least two, and its complete fixed-length distribution has a rational bivariate generating function.
