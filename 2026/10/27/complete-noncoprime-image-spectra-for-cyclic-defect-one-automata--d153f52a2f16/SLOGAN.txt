For cyclic defect-one automata with noncoprime displacement \(d\), a state set is reachable exactly when it meets every residue class modulo \(\gcd(n,d)\); the full rank polynomial is \(\big((1+z)^{n/\gcd(n,d)}-1\big)^{\gcd(n,d)}\). Every reachable target also has an explicit optimal contraction schedule, and its number of canonical optimal schedules is a multinomial coefficient determined by the missing-vertex runs.
