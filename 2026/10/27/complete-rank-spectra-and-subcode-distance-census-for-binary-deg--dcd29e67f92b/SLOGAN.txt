Every binary linear subcode of the degree-two square Gabidulin family has a rank enumerator and full subcode-distance profile determined by its intersections with two canonical coefficient axes, and an explicit Gaussian-binomial formula counts all subcodes realizing each profile. For a uniformly random codimension-one subcode, the unique exceptional index retains optimal rank distance with probability exactly \(2/(2^m+1)\), tending exponentially to zero as the extension degree grows.
