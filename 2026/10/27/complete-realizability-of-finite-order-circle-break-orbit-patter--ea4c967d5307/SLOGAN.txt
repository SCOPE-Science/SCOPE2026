Every multiplicatively compatible pattern of derivative jumps on finitely many \(q\)-periodic circle-rotation orbits is exactly realizable by a piecewise-linear order-\(q\) map, with no extra breaks. For fixed \(K\) break-containing orbits, the possible break counts fill precisely \(2K\le n\le qK\); unrestricted counts are positive even integers for \(q=2\) and every \(n\ge2\) for \(q\ge3\).
