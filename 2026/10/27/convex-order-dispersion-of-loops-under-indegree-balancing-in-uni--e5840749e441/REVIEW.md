# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The joint fixed-event probabilities for arbitrary distinct indices are products of their target multiplicities divided by the falling factorial. This gives the exact probability-generating polynomial. Balancing two entries changes the elementary-symmetric generating polynomial by a positive quadratic factor, hence changes the loop-count generating function by a positive coefficient times \((z-1)^2\) times a genuine auxiliary probability-generating function. The auxiliary injection exists because the remaining multiplicities sum to at most \(n-2\). Convex-order and strictness follow from nonnegative (strictly positive) second differences, and integer majorization is generated by unit balancing moves. Exact direct enumeration through eight elements and 4,372 arithmetic assertions independently corroborate the finite formulas.

**Originality (PASS, provisional).** Hansen–Jaworski (arXiv:math/0603529, inspected primary text) treats mappings with prescribed/exchangeable indegrees but focuses on cyclic points, components and component sizes. Leckey–Wormald (arXiv:1803.02667, accessible abstract) treats first-repeat lengths. Stark (arXiv:2608.00710, inspected HTML full text) studies a different multiset matching statistic whose mean is not identically one. No checked source stated or implied the present full convex-order transfer formula. Risk: classical rook-polynomial, assignment and majorization literature could supply an equivalent theorem; no historical priority certification is claimed.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** Fixed loops are a natural local statistic in degree-constrained random functional graphs. The theorem determines the effect of every balancing move on *all* convex losses, gives an exact nonnegative second-difference measure rather than only a variance bound, and identifies both extremal indegree profiles for all sizes. It is not merely a numerical cutoff or a routine variance recomputation.

The theorem applies only to uniform maps conditioned on target indegrees; no converse to the majorization condition, stochastic dominance, or general graph model is established. Independent scientific validation remains outstanding.
