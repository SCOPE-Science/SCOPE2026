# Review: corrected D4-triality species table

**Correctness — PASS.** Summing the six stated S3-orbits of D4 positive roots gives dimension vectors \((1,0,0,0),(0,1,1,1),(3,1,1,1),(3,2,2,2),(1,1,1,1),(2,1,1,1)\). Since \([M:K]=3\), the corresponding species dimensions are \((1,0),(0,1),(3,1),(3,2),(1,1),(2,1)\). These do not agree with the printed orbit assignments. The last printed pair \((M,K)\) is ill-typed under the paper's stated convention.

**Originality — PASS, bounded.** indexed finding database searches using the paper title, arXiv identifier, D4 triality, G2 species and trace/inclusion descriptions returned no directly matching correction. The paper's own general theorem implies the correct orbit classification but does not validate its mismatched explicit table. Absolute priority is not claimed.

**Value — PASS.** The printed table has no valid representative for the required dimension \((3,2)\), and one entry is not an object of the stated category. Correcting the table is needed to reproduce the paper's six-object illustrative classification. The main theorem is unaffected.

Closest literature: Januszewski, *Rational quiver representations: tame and wild*, arXiv:2510.00924v1, especially Theorem 2.3 and §3, pp. 6–7. The concrete \((3,2)\) example in RESULT.md is only for \(M=\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{2})\); the general table correction follows from the root-orbit sums.
