For the classical logarithmic Dirichlet kernel and its coefficient-shift family, \(N\) equispaced points exponentially close to the disk boundary have a universal critical Gram spectrum: the Fourier-mode eigenvalues are uniformly \(1+1/[c(j+\tau)]+O(\log N/N)\). All distinct kernel vectors become pairwise asymptotically orthogonal, yet the sharp condition number tends to \(1+1/(c\tau)>1\).
