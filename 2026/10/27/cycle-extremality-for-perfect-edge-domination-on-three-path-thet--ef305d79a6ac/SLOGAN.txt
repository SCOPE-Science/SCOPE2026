For every three-path theta graph \(\Theta(a,b,c)\) with \(a,b,c\ge2\), the number of perfect edge dominating sets is at most the corresponding count on the cycle with the same number of vertices; equality holds only for \(\Theta(2,2,2)\cong K_{2,3}\). A four-state exact transfer formula and a positive-difference recurrence establish this infinite bicyclic case of the connected-graph cycle extremality conjecture.
