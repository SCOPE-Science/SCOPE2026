# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For n≥2, one-line ordinary successions S=Σ_{i<n}1{Π(i+1)=Π(i)+1} and C_j counts cycles of length j in that SAME permutation. Conditioning on arrows Π(i)=b and Π(i+1)=b+1 partitions into double-loops L, two-edge path P, and disjoint arrows D. Completion is uniform permutation on n-2 marked contracted objects. Expected j-cycles with s marks use C(2,s) C(m-2,j-s)/[j C(m,j)], so L_j=P_j=D_j=1/j in middle band 3≤j≤n-2. The boundary cases yield Cov(S,C_j)=[2 1(j=1)-1(j=2)-1(j=n-1)]/[n(n-1)]+1(j=n)/n², plus the stated sitewise spectrum for n≥5. Small n=2,3 checked separately and overlaps added. Exhaustive independent enumeration of 5912 permutations through size seven and 119 rational comparisons corroborated, while contraction counting proves all n. Zero covariance is not independence.

## originality

PASS

The Reilly–Tanny counting of ordinary one-line successions is classical. Diaconis–Evans–Graham equate the marginal succession set law with a fixed-point subset in distribution, not joint law with cycles of the original Π. Mansour–Shattuck count successions inside disjoint-cycle notation; Foata-style correspondence changes cycle type, so cannot infer this same-permutation cycle-length covariance by relabeling. Earlier total cycle covariance 1/n² is credited and does not imply exact signed length-by-length cancellations. An unindexed marked-cycle cross-moment identity remains a priority risk.

## value

PASS

Exactly vanishing covariance simultaneously for all intermediate cycle lengths and at every individual succession position is a meaningful structural orthogonality law, with nontrivial positive/negative boundary effects. It refines known marginal successions and total-cycle results and can be used in joint permutation-statistic asymptotics.
