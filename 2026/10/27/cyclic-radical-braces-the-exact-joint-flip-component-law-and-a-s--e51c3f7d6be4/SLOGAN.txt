For the canonical Yang–Baxter solution of the cyclic radical brace \(a\circ b=a+b+p^sab\) on \(\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z\), every indecomposable component is either internally flip-complete or flip-free, with the sharp size threshold \(2j\le n-s\) for components of order \(p^j\). Its entire orbit-size multiset and the joint flip/component probabilities are explicit; as \(n\to\infty\) with \(p,s\) fixed, flip probability tends to zero while same-component probability tends to \(p^{1-s}/(p+1)\).
