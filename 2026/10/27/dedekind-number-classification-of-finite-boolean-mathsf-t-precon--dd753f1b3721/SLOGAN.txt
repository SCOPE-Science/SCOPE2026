On a finite Boolean algebra with \(n\) atoms, the negations realizable by Chen \(\mathsf T\)-preconditionals are exactly normalized antitone semicomplementations, and their number is \((M(n-1)-1)^n\), where \(M\) denotes the Dedekind numbers. Every such negation has an explicit same-carrier binary lift, and the stronger \(\mathsf F\) negations form the previously known graph-polarity subfamily; the first strict separation occurs on the four-element Boolean algebra.
