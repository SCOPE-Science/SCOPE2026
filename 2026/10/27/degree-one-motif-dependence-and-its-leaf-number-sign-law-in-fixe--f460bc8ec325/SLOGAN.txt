For a uniform fixed-edge random graph, the exact covariance of degree-one vertex count with the number of embeddings of a motif depends only on the motif's order, edge count, and number of leaves, and its normalized sign changes at most once with edge density. In \(G(12,10)\), a six-vertex path has negative covariance while a six-vertex star has positive covariance, despite having the same numbers of vertices and edges.
