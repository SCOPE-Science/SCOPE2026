For every full matrix ring \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\) with \(n\ge2\), the graph joining two nontrivial idempotents whenever either ordered product vanishes has a complete Gaussian-binomial neighbor-rank distribution. Its vertex degrees strictly identify matrix rank in dimensions at least three, and just the minimum degree and its multiplicity recover both \(q\) and \(n\), so this unlabeled graph determines the matrix ring up to isomorphism.
