For a lossy two-mode optical quantum battery struck by one coherent pulse, exact resonance supports infinitely many charging peaks, but any nonzero charger–battery detuning leaves only finitely many. As the detuning \(\Delta\) vanishes in the underdamped regime, the peak count obeys \(N(\Delta)=\frac{4(g^2-\gamma^2/16)}{\pi\gamma|\Delta|}\operatorname{arcosh}(4g/\gamma)+O(1)\), with an explicit final turning-time envelope.
