In finite aligned-Zipf weighted-quorum collection with \(0<s<1\), the quorum time is asymptotically Gaussian on the \(\sqrt N\) scale about its exact mean crossing, with an explicit variance. The crossing has a computable \(N^{s-1}\) displacement, creating a sharp \(s=1/2\) transition between centered and shifted continuum-centered Gaussian limits, and a dominating drift above the threshold when its coefficient is nonzero.
