For the full endomorphism monoid of a finite dihedral quandle, every ungraded disrespectful polynomial of every nonempty subquandle filtration collapses exactly to its ordinary coloring count. The singleton/full two-stage filtration nevertheless has an explicit graded disrespectful polynomial on every positive odd \((2,k)\) torus knot, with one monomial per divisor of \(\gcd(n,k)\) and coefficient \(n\varphi(r)\) at divisor \(r\).
