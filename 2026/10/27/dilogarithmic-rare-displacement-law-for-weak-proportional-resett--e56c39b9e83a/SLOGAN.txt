The weak-resetting Gaussian limit hides an exact, non-Gaussian stationary rare-displacement law: after rescaling by \(\varepsilon=-\log(c^2)\), the rate function is the Legendre transform of \(\operatorname{Li}_2(\theta^2)\). Its cost crosses from \(y^2/4\) near the origin to \(|y|-\pi^2/6\) in the far tail, quantitatively delimiting the Gaussian approximation.
