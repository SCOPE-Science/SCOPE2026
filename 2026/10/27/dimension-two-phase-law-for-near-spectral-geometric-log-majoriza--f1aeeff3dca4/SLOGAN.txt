Conjecture 5.6 on the reversal of near-spectral and ordinary spectral geometric log-majorization holds for every complex positive-definite \(2\times2\) pair, with the unique phase boundary at \(t=\tfrac12\). Outside \(t=0,\tfrac12\), the nontrivial eigenvalue inequality is strict exactly when the inputs do not commute; an exact hyperbolic-sine trace formula quantifies the reversal.
