For Brownian resetting to a point outside a small spherical trap, the optimal leading-order reset rate rises strictly with spatial dimension and obeys \(2(d-2)<R^2r^*/D<2d\). Its first finite-dimensional correction is explicit: \(R^2r^*/D=2(d-2)+8/(d-2)+O(d^{-2})\) in the iterated small-target, then large-dimension limit.
