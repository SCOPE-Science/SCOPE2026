In the two-community network replicator with a strict interior mixed ESS and positive action-blind constant migration, the balanced equilibrium is locally exponentially stable for **every positive directed contact matrix**, even without reciprocal contact weights. Directed interaction can nevertheless produce damped oscillatory eigenmodes: the exact example has \(-209/80\pm\sqrt{6479}\,\mathrm{i}/80\), alongside \(-1/10\).
