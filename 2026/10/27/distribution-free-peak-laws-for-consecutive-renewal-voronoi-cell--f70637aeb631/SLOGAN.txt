For one-dimensional renewal Voronoi cells with any atomless i.i.d. gap law, the probability of a cell being longer than both neighbors is exactly \(1/4\); peak covariances are negative at lags one and two, positive at lag three, and zero thereafter. A window of \(n\ge4\) consecutive cells has peak-count variance \(5(n+4)/144\), and its centered peak count obeys a Gaussian limit with asymptotic variance \(5/144\) per cell.
