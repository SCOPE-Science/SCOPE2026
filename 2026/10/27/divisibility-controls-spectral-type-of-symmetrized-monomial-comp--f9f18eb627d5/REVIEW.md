# Review: Divisibility controls spectral type of symmetrized monomial composition products

## Correctness — PASS
The operator identity \(C_{z^m}C_{z^n}^*e_{nk}=e_{mk}\) reduces the full problem, including the constant mode, to a graph of a partial bijection. Distinct integer degrees exclude cycles; bounded nonnegative prime valuations exclude infinite tails when neither degree divides the other. The explicit prime-valuation recipe constructs infinitely many paths of every size. Divisible pairs reduce to infinitely many unilateral rays. Sine transforms verify both spectral types, complete eigenvector expansions, and full Fredholm essential spectra. The proof treats the endpoints: \(2\) is a constant eigenvalue and \(-2\) never is. The finite diagnostic is supplementary only.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Actual searches of published mathematical findings for monomial composition-adjoint symmetrizations, divisibility spectral types, multiplicative partial permutations, and product spectra found no matching theorem. Closest related result records address affine composition commutator Calkin spectra and a monomial composition Aluthge example on a weighted Hardy space; neither implies the classical Hardy symmetrized-product spectral-type law. Primary full-text review of Leng–Zhao (arXiv:2610.15472v1, Introduction, Sections 3.2–3.3 and Theorem 3.24) identifies Schatten-class criteria rather than this arithmetic pure-point-versus-absolutely-continuous dichotomy. Residual risk: a preexisting spectral graph decomposition under number-theoretic shift terminology was not exhaustively ruled out.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem classifies all distinct monomial-degree pairs without extra hypotheses and shows a discontinuous invariant: divisibility changes the spectral type of a natural self-adjoint operator even while its spectrum and essential spectrum remain identical. It supplies full eigenvalue sets, normal forms, and multiplicities. The result is a coherent structural classification, not a finite parameter-table calculation or an isolated improvement in a constant.

## Boundaries
Only two-term symmetrizations of monomial Hardy composition-adjoint products are considered. The proof is self-contained except for directly exhibited standard sine-transform diagonalizations. Independent validation and historical-priority confirmation are pending.
