For two independent equal-weight Schottky systems with widely separated gaps, the temperature of the intervening heat-capacity valley obeys \(r\Delta/(k_BT_{\mathrm{val}})=2\log r+\log\log r+\log4+o(1)\). Its height is asymptotic to \(k_B(\log r)^2/r^2\), and the minimum is unique within the explicit interval \(\log r<r\Delta/(k_BT)<3\log r\).
