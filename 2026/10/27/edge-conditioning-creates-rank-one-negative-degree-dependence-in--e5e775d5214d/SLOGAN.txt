For a uniform spanning tree of \(K_{a,b}\), excluding one marked cross-edge makes its endpoint degrees independent, while including it produces two independent binomial increments plus a three-atom negative-dependence coupling. This yields an exact rank-one covariance formula for *every* pair of endpoint-degree statistics; in balanced large graphs the edge-conditioned degree correlation converges to \(-1/5\) although the unconditioned degrees are independent.
