The finite étale scheme of lines on the characteristic-three \(58\)-line quartic \(x_1^3x_2-x_1x_2^3+x_0^3x_3-x_0x_3^3=x_0^2x_1x_2\) has Frobenius cycle type \(1^{10}8^6\). Exactly ten lines descend to \(\mathbb F_3\), while the other forty-eight first descend to \(\mathbb F_{3^8}\), giving line-scheme zeta function \(1/((1-T)^{10}(1-T^8)^6)\).
