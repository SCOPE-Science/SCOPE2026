In characteristic three the classical Barth–Nieto quintic \(V(e_1,e_5)\) has twenty singular coordinate lines **and eleven isolated ordinary nodes**, one more than the characteristic-zero model. The extra diagonal node \([1:1:1:1:1:1]\) is rational, and the complete reduced singular locus has \(46\) rational points among the quintic's \(91\) \(\mathbb F_3\)-points.
