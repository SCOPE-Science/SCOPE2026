For every finite monogenic inverse monoid \(S_{n,k}\) with \(n\ge2\), the image sizes of all unital endomorphisms have an exact divisor-and-link-length enumerator, and the number of idempotent endomorphisms is \(n(n+1)/2+2^{\omega(k)}+1\). Every proper retract is commutative, even though the ambient monoid is noncommutative.
