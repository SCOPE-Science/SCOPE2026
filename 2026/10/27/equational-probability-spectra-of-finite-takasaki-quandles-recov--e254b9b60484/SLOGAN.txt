The equational probability spectrum of the single-operation Takasaki quandle \(x\triangleright y=2y-x\) on a finite abelian group \(A\) is exactly \(\{|A[d]|/|A|:d\mid\exp(A)\}\). Its *unordered* probability values recover every elementary divisor of \(A\), and the spectrum is minimal precisely for elementary abelian groups of prime exponent.
