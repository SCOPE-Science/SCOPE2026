For every \(q=3^m\), the coefficient-marked smooth Ciani quartics over \(\mathbb F_q\) have an explicit polynomial count in each of the four possible Jacobian \(3\)-ranks, and their \(a\)-number is always \(3\) minus the \(3\)-rank. In particular, none of the sixteen smooth marked equations over \(\mathbb F_3\) is ordinary, whereas ordinary members occur over \(\mathbb F_9\).
