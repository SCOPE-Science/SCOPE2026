# Review of exact Abelian-complexity trichotomy

## Correctness: PASS
The count telescopes to occupation of a moving arc relative to a set of equally spaced marks. Ordered entry/exit events yield the finite list of possible Parikh states; the only repeated states are the empty and full subsets at the extreme arc lengths. Exceptional coinciding cuts occur at the explicitly handled initial lengths. Minimality, cylinder interval geometry and irrational equidistribution settle word complexity and length densities. Exact integer phase-enumeration checks corroborate numerous finite cases. The finite proxy is not an infinite proof.

Relevant primary full-text material was compared, not merely titles. The 2020 Karhumäki–Puzynina–Whiteland paper treats the related three-letter heavy/light factor question and general Abelian closures of minimal-complexity words; it does not state the full arbitrary-\(m\) multiplicity formula or high/low density law. The 2025 billiard balance paper gives bounded-discrepancy statements, not this Parikh-vector census. The 2026 Rauzy-theorem article concerns rationally independent frequencies, absent here. An equivalent formula in older literature on morphic Sturmian words remains a residual risk.

## Value: PASS
Minimal ordinary complexity \(n+m\) does not itself determine Abelian complexity; the theorem exhibits and completely quantifies an infinite alphabet-size family with two recurrent Abelian-complexity levels and their exact natural densities. The coexistence of arbitrary \(m\), an explicit short-length transition, and an irrational arithmetic dichotomy gives a substantive symbolic-dynamical invariant beyond a single example.
