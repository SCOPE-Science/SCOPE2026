For an \((m+1)\)-letter coding of an irrational rotation with \(m\) consecutive intervals of length \(\alpha<1/m\), the exact Abelian complexity equals \(m+n\) for \(n<m\) and thereafter alternates between \(2m\) and \(m+1\) according to the position of \(\{n\alpha\}\). The respective asymptotic frequencies are \(1-2\alpha\) and \(2\alpha\), while ordinary factor complexity is identically \(n+m\).
