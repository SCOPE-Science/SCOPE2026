For the fixed gradient-regularized Newton iteration on an SPD quadratic, the lowest-curvature eigenspace present in the initial vector determines the limiting direction and the exact \(3/2\)-order constants: \(\|x_{k+1}\|_2/\|x_k\|_2^{3/2}\to\sqrt{H/\lambda_*}\) and \(\|\nabla f(x_{k+1})\|_2/\|\nabla f(x_k)\|_2^{3/2}\to\sqrt H/\lambda_*\).
