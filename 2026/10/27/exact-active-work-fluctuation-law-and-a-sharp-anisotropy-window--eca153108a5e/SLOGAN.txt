For a harmonically confined run-and-tumble particle with unequal right/left speeds and tumbling rates, stationary active work satisfies an exact Gaussian fluctuation law whose variance-to-mean ratio has a unique positive-confinement maximum **exactly** when \(1/2<\alpha_R/\alpha_L<2\); outside this anisotropy window it decreases strictly with confinement. The threshold depends on switching-rate asymmetry alone, not on propulsion speeds or thermal noise strength.
