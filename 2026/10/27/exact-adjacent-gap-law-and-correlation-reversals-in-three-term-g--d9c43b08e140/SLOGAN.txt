The consecutive gaps of three-term generalized Zeckendorf expansions have an explicit two-variable rational limiting generating function for every \(p,q\ge1\), including the zero-coefficient ranges omitted by earlier small-gap counting arguments. Their adjacent-gap covariance is exactly zero for \((p,q)=(2,1)\), positive for \((3,1)\), and negative for \((20,1)\): a rigorously certified sign reversal.
