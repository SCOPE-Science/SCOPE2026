In one-shot antagonistic two-species adsorption on the line, the entire adjacent-site law has an exact two-cavity representation: accepted like-species pairs have probabilities \(a^2\) and \(b^2\), while an adjacent vacancy pair has probability \((1-a-b)^2\). At every positive observation time and nondegenerate species mixture, neighboring like-species occupancy is strictly positively correlated but total occupancy is strictly negatively correlated.
