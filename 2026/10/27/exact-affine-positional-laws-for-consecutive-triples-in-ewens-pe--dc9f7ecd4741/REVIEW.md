# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The cycle-weighted three-arrow completion lemma reduces every local probability to a cubic numerator. Marking closed cycles gives an explicit bivariate polynomial of total degree at most three in the numbers of sites before and after the window. The ten-node triangular interpolation theorem, plus exact integer counts at all ten nodes, yields an all-length identity; independent Ewens-weighted permutation enumeration checks the formulas as well. Boundaries \(n=3\), \(\theta>0\), and \(i=1,n-2\) are included.

**Originality (O): PASS on inspected material, with residual risk.** The closest exact published treatment is the Pinsky–Schickentanz pairwise inversion formula (arXiv:2510.20654, Theorem 1). It does not imply the three-point order law, as pairwise marginals do not determine six joint pattern probabilities. Kammoun (arXiv:2012.05845, Proposition 31, Section 3.1) treats asymptotic consecutive patterns and descent processes but not the exact finite positional classification. Féray (2013) gives asymptotic Ewens pattern statistics. No equivalent statement was found through the recorded claim/equivalence searches. Possible implicit older character-theoretic identities remain the principal priority risk.

**Value (V): PASS.** The finding isolates the first window length where position dependence becomes visible despite position-homogeneous adjacent inversion marginals. It completely classifies all six minimal higher-order patterns for all sample sizes and parameters, including sharp, oppositely oriented positional slopes, so it is a structural dependence result rather than an arbitrary numerical instance.
