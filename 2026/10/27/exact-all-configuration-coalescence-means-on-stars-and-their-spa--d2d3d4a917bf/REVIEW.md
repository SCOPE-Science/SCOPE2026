**Correctness — PASS.** Every admissible occupied set is represented by the star's center-occupied or center-vacant symmetry class. The first-step equations correctly use rate one **per walker**, including mergers when an occupied leaf jumps to the occupied center and when the occupied center selects an occupied leaf. With boundary data \(a_0=b_1=0\), solving the exact finite-state recurrence proves the all-size harmonic formulas. The independent full-generator verification passes 219 exact-rational comparisons on stars with 2–7 vertices, but finite testing is not used as the infinite-size proof. The complete-graph comparison uses the same rate normalization, and the endpoint \(m=1\) and \(m=2\) are treated separately.

Searches of published research records for all-configuration, harmonic and star-coalescence aliases showed no implication-equivalent formula; the closest spider coupling record concerns single-walker coupling and mixing instead. An unlocated specialized coalescence calculation or a general finite-state formula could still subsume this specialization.
