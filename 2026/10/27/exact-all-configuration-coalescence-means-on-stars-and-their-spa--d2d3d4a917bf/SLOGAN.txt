On a star \(K_{1,m}\), independent rate-one coalescing walkers have explicit expected one-particle absorption times from **every** occupied-vertex set, with full-occupancy mean \((mH_m+m-1)/(m+1)\). An occupied center creates no additional mean delay when all leaves are occupied, and for at least four vertices the fully occupied star coalesces strictly faster than the same-order complete graph under per-particle total-rate normalization.
