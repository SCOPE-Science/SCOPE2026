The thirty vertices of the regular icosidodecahedron admit all fifteen antipodally compatible exact-degree spherical-harmonic samples from degree \(15\) onward, but degree \(6\) has rank \(10\) and degrees \(8\) and \(14\) have rank \(14\). Exact algebraic and modular certificates plus a Legendre Bernstein bound classify every degree, including the nonmonotone earlier full-rank degrees.
