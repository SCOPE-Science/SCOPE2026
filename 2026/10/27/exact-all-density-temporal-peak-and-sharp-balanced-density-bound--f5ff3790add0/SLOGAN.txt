Every strictly positive density ratio in the cold longitudinal two-stream plasma admits exactly one fastest-growing wavenumber, explicitly parameterized by a unique positive root of \(b/a=r^3(r+2)/(2r+1)\). At fixed total squared plasma frequency \(P\), the optimized temporal growth obeys the sharp bound \(\gamma_*^2\le P/8\), with equality only for equal stream densities.
