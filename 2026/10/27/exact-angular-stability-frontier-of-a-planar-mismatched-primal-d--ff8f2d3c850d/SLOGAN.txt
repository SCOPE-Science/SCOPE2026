For the equal-step planar Chambolle–Pock iteration with a rotated surrogate adjoint, all non-antipodal rotations are linearly stable through the exact golden-ratio step \(t=(1+\sqrt5)/2\); above it, the admissible angles obey a sharp cosine frontier until stability disappears at \(t=1+\sqrt3\). This gives an exact direction-sensitive boundary beyond norm-based sufficient mismatch bounds.
