For an ideal binary-aperture optical Type-II Tikhonov preconditioner on real symmetric circulant systems, the right-preconditioned matrix is an idempotent projector and fixed GMRES cannot improve after one residual minimization. Missing Fourier modes additionally attenuate all retained reconstruction modes by the exact factor \(\|b\|^2/(\|b\|^2+\|A(I-\Pi)b\|^2)\), even on a condition-two positive-definite example.
