On a bouquet of \(r\) triangles, synchronous three-state Greenberg–Hastings dynamics reaches a fixed point or a three-cycle in at most four updates, sharply so for \(r\ge2\). Exactly \(\binom rk2^k\) three-cycles have \(k\) active triangles and each has basin size \(3\,7^{r-k}\), while the zero state attracts exactly \(3\,7^r\) initial configurations.
