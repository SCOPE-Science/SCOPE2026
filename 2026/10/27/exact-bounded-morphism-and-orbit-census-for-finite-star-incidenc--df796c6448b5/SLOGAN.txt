Onto bounded morphisms between finite reflexive star-incidence frames are exactly tagged arm assignments in which every target leaf receives a hit; their indexed count is \(\sum_{k=0}^s(-1)^k\binom{s}{k}(2s-k)^r\). Modulo source and target frame automorphisms, their classes are counted by \([u^s z^r]\prod_{n\ge1}(1-u z^n)^{-n}\) for \(r,s\ge2\).
