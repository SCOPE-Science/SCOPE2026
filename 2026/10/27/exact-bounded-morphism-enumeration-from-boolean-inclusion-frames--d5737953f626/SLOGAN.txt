Globally surjective bounded morphisms from the Boolean inclusion frame \(B_n\) onto a three-world chain are exactly the nested downset pairs whose maximal zero worlds have immediate middle-valued successors. An exact inclusion–exclusion formula yields \(0,0,3,63,3604,3242450\) maps for \(n=0,1,2,3,4,5\), respectively, separating genuine modal quotients from ordinary monotone maps.
