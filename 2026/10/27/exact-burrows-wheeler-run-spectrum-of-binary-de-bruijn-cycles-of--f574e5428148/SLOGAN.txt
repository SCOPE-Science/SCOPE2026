The Burrows–Wheeler transforms of all 2,048 binary order-five de Bruijn cycles have exactly six run counts, with multiplicities 38, 426, 840, 608, 130, and 6 at 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, and 30 runs. Order five is the first binary de Bruijn order where the elementary bound \(2^{k-1}+2\) cannot be attained.
