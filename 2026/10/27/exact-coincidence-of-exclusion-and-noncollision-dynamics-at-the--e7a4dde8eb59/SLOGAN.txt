For \(2\le k\le n-2\) independent walkers on a strictly positive directed complete site graph, noncollision conditioning is exactly ordinary exclusion if and only if every pair of sites has the same **sum** of forward and reverse jump rates. At the single-vacancy boundary \(k=n-1\), equality requires only equal symmetrized weighted degrees, leaving genuine anisotropic examples whose first-collision times are independent of the exclusion path even though the colliding pair depends on the configuration.
