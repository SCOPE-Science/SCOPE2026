# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every majority-stable labeled-cycle coloring is exactly a cyclic binary word with runs of each color of length at least two. Marked-run enumeration proves each coefficient; a four-state transfer matrix proves the generating polynomial and characteristic factorization. Perron eigenvalue derivatives give the fluctuation constant and standard analytic moment-generating-function convergence proves the central limit theorem. Exact independent exhaustive profile checks for lengths 3 through 16, symbolic derivatives, and numerical moment checks support the steps but do not substitute for the infinite-length proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Zehmakan's original 2023 manuscript, including Section 2 and Appendix A.6, establishes only the asymptotic order of the number of stable cycles, not the number at every blue population nor the Gaussian distribution and its coefficient. Its 2024 journal account uses the same fixed-point characterization. A targeted comparison of related run-restricted word enumerations and published mathematical findings revealed no identical weight-resolved fluctuation statement. The total count is itself a classical-type cyclic binary-word enumeration; techniques and preliminary characterization are known. A related weighted-run treatment in an unindexed source remains a residual originality risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The source leaves unquantified how stable majority opinions partition by blue support. This exact spectrum is an entire natural family of fixed-point invariants, and the Gaussian theorem supplies a quantitative polarization/diversity baseline for uniformly sampled stable profiles with a variance coefficient distinguishing it from independent binary assignments. It measures the equilibrium landscape, not unproved dynamic selection.

## Limitations
The theorem is for labeled cycles and deterministic self-retaining ties. Uniform equilibrium statistics need not coincide with final-state statistics from random initial conditions. No independent audit or external validation has been performed.
