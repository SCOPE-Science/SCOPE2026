# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness
The proof forces half cardinality by summing representations; its Fourier analysis distinguishes odd and even frequencies and converts the remaining product condition into an exact disjunction. Parity and involution orbit counts establish the labeled formula; stabilizers and Burnside establish the orbit formula. Exhaustive exact ordered-triple counts and translation-orbit checks for seven small moduli corroborate all branches. The claims beyond these cases follow from the given symbolic proof.

## Originality
Qu (2014), Theorem 3, covers existence, while Lemma 7 provides a general root-of-unity test and Problem 4 asks to determine the solution sets. Our specified order-two third coefficient yields a concrete **two-mechanism classification** and an exact arithmetic **translation-orbit enumerator**, neither given by the paper's existence statement or its different Example 5; a derivation from Qu's general criterion is possible and is acknowledged. Searches for the exact weight family, translation orbits and synonymous complementary representation functions did not locate equal or stronger published counts. Residual risk is unlocated subsequent literature, including later work on complementary finite groups.

## Value
The paper's open set-determination problem motivates this natural index-two weight regime; explicitly distinguishing parity balance from half-period invariance resolves the structural mechanism and quantifies the number of solutions and symmetry classes for all even moduli. The orbit formula distinguishes genuine inequivalent witnesses instead of merely reproducing the existence criterion. General triples remain open here.
