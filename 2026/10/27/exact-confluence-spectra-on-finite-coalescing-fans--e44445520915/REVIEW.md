# Scientific review — exact confluence spectra on finite coalescing fans

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reduces both quantified confluence properties on the one-top antichain cone to explicit conditions on the top successor set and leaf rows. There are three disjoint exhaustive cases. Counting the independent leaf-row choices gives the exact all-frame expression and the reflexive subcount via two independently checked binomial sums. Asymptotic statements follow by expansion in \(2^{-(m+1)}\) with \(m2^{-(m+1)}\to0\). The standalone finite checker exhaustively evaluates all 16, 512 and 65536 binary relations for \(m=1,2,3\), respectively, against the source axioms, and the unrestricted conclusion rests on the proof, not on finite extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS within investigated evidence

Sedlár–Vigiani (arXiv:2610.10008v1) supply the exact generic Nelson-modal confluence axioms, which are not claimed as new. Balbiani–Gao–Gencer–Olivetti (arXiv:2403.06772v1) use the same older axioms in local intuitionistic logic. The closest published complete KI census proves an endpoint formula on chains only; it does not apply to the incomparable lower points of the coalescing fan. A separate published birelational chain census uses different interaction conditions. Targeted searches covering star/fan, downward/forward, Fischer–Servi, Egli–Milner-style aliases, reflexivity and initial counts did not identify a result implying the complete three-stratum normal form or both exact spectra. The risk remains that older ordered-set-valued-relation literature contains a stronger equivalent result.

## Value — PASS

The finite coalescing fan is a canonical genuinely non-chain Kripke frame shape, so its complete confluence census tests the interaction constraints beyond the linearly ordered case. The exact reflexive census measures modal \(\mathsf{T}\) expansions, while the distinguished-top-row limiting law describes the typical combinatorial frame. All three results follow from one reusable structural classification and concern naturally defined, arbitrary-width structures, not isolated small numerical cases.
