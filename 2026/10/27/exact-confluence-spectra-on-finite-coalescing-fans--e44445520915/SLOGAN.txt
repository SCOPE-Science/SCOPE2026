On a finite coalescing fan with \(m\) incomparable lower worlds and one top, the two modal confluence conditions have an exhaustive rowwise normal form and exactly \(2^m+(2^{m+1}-1)^m+(2^{m+1}+2)^m-2^{m(m+1)}\) accessibility relations. Exactly \((2^m+2)^m\) are reflexive, and almost every doubly confluent relation has the top world accessing only itself.
