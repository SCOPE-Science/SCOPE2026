# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exact root-bound criterion is proved in both directions. The lattice-point sum is evaluated exactly, and the exclusions for zero parameters and root-of-unity ratios are disjoint and complete. The universal statement follows from the proof, not from the finite script.

## Originality

**PASS (self-review).** The closest primary literature studies the same bounded-root Lucas-sequence count but gives upper and lower bounds. Neither the arXiv nor the inspected published version states the exact integer-radius formula; the published version only improves the integer-radius lower bound. Literature searches returned nearby recurrence results but no matching count. This assessment is not an exhaustive novelty guarantee.

## Value

**PASS.** The formula gives an exact answer to a natural parameter-counting question and sharpens the previously available bounds for every integer radius \\(T\\ge2\\).

## Closest literature and limitations

L. Hajdu and R. Tijdeman, *Integers represented by Lucas sequences*, arXiv:2408.04982, Theorem 2.2 and Section 4, is the closest source; the published version's Remark 2 improves the integer-radius lower bound to \\(4T^3-9T^2\\) but does not give an exact count. Akiyama and Pethő study volume/distribution for real-coefficient polynomials with bounded roots; only their abstract was accessible here. The result is limited to integer radii and has not received an independent audit.
