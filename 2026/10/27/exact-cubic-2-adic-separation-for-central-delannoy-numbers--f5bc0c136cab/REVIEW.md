The proof separates the two noncritical residue classes modulo four, establishes \(\operatorname{Li}_2(2)=0\) by an independently differentiable 2-adic functional equation, and factors the critical analytic expansion as \(64w^3\) times a unit. The infinite tail has a uniform Gauss-valuation bound, and reflection covers all odd indices. A standard-library verifier checks the finite rational polynomial certificate and small indices; the proof, not the examples, supplies the unbounded quantifiers.
