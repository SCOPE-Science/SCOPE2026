For the central Delannoy number \(D_n\), the exact 2-adic distance to \((-1)^n\) is three times \(\nu_2(n)\) when \(4\mid n\), three times \(\nu_2(n+1)\) when \(n\equiv3\pmod4\), and exactly \(2\) in the other two residue classes. A 2-adic reflection identity and a dilogarithm cancellation explain the cubic contact.
