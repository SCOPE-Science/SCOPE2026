Their accessible detailed abstract does not resolve the per-vertex, cycle-rooted **preperiod-two branching fibres**. Lu, Dai and Li (arXiv:2605.21819) address ternary-ring Chebyshev permutation degrees, whereas degree two is not a permutation modulo three. The unresolved full text of arXiv:2605.04417 is a specific priority risk, as is unindexed equivalent literature.
