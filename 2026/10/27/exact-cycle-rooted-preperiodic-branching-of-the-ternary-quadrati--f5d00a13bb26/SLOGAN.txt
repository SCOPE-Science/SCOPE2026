For the quadratic Chebyshev map \(x\mapsto x^2-2\) modulo \(3^k\), the complete depth-two branching profile is explicit: every periodic point has one depth-one predecessor, but just one cycle at each even valuation \(2v<k\) receives \(2\cdot3^v\) depth-two predecessors per vertex, besides the fixed point \(2\). Every other cycle has none.
