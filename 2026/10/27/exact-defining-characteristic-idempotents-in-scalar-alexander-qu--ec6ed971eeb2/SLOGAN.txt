In every finite scalar latin Alexander quandle algebra \(\mathbb F_q[Q_a]\), the exact number of idempotents is \(1+q^{d_q(a)}\), where \(d_q(a)\) counts exponents \(j\in\{0,\ldots,q-2\}\) satisfying \(a^j+(1-a)^j=1\). All nonzero idempotents form an elementary abelian group under ordinary additive-group-ring convolution; for \(q=7\), the counts jump from eight at \(a=2\) to fifty at \(a=4\).
