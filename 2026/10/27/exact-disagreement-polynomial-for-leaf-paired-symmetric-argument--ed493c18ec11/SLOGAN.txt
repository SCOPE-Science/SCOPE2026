On symmetric argumentation frameworks formed by giving every vertex of a graph its own mutually attacking fallback leaf, the complete ordered-pair stable-extension distance polynomial is an exact sum over induced bipartite subgraphs and independent shared cores. Every even distance occurs up to \(2(n-\tau(G))\), where \(\tau(G)\) is the odd-cycle-transversal number, and the coefficient at this sharp endpoint is the sum of \(2^{\kappa}\) over largest induced bipartite subgraphs.
