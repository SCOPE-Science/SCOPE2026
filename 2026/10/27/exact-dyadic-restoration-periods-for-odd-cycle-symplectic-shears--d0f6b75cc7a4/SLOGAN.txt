For every odd cycle of length \(n\ge3\), the unequal-shear symplectic cat lattice has exact global period \(2n\) modulo two, \(4n\) modulo both four and eight, and \(n2^{s-1}\) modulo \(2^s\) for every \(s\ge4\). The unexpected modulus-four/eight plateau follows from an idempotent cyclic projector, and a uniform-mode witness makes every later period doubling sharp.
