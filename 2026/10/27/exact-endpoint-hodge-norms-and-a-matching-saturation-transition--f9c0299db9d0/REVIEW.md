# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The vertex-potential formulas in RESULT.md solve the full graph Laplacian for each of the three relevant edge types, including the possibility of unpaired vertices. Seven current classes and their multiplicities give every absolute cut-column sum; the diagonal-identity gives each cycle-column sum. All sign changes occur only at the explicit comparison of the matched-pair mate with other right vertices. Strict inequalities exclude the added matching edges from maximization. Exact rational grounded-Laplacian reconstruction of all edge columns over 333 parameter triples (17,298 edge columns), plus symbolic checks, agrees with the proof. Finite checks corroborate rather than replace the all-parameter argument.

## Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic risk

The closest readily accessible projection paper, Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii (2023), studies graph-cycle projections and proves an exact Hamming-cube formula but does not give the matching-size plateau or saturation transition. The exact single-edge situation is only the first nonzero matching size and does not imply invariance for additional disjoint insertions or the final-edge transition. Ge and Dong (2019) treat cross-part matching **deletions**, not additions internal to one class. Ge (2021) and Cheng–Chen–Yan (2022) study resistance-distance formulas on matching-augmented graphs, apparently overlapping the graph objects; their available abstracts do not state absolute-current projection norms. Their full texts could not be inspected. Because complete resistance matrices can encode projection entries, implicit derivability is an outstanding bibliographic risk; the present claim adds explicit absolute-value signs, maxima, and an all-matching-size sharp threshold. No covering endpoint-norm theorem was identified in the searches made, and absence of such a match is not a proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

Graph perturbations by an entire matching are natural, and adding edges need not monotonically affect Banach endpoint norms of canonical complementary projections. The complete three-parameter classification shows an unexpected plateau for *all intermediate matching sizes*, followed by a sharp joint or single-projection drop only when the matching covers the whole chosen vertex class. These two independent parameter thresholds are motivated, exact, and not merely a sample of isolated graphs.

## Scope and risks

The proof is confined to real, simple, unweighted graphs and canonical orthogonal projections. There is no claim of minimal projection constants. Relevant 2021 and 2022 same-object resistance articles were accessible only via abstracts, so the precise implications of their complete proofs remain unresolved, and an independent scientific audit is outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
