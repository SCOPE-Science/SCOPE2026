On the unweighted cycle with periodic distance coloring, the graph-entropy trace-probing deficit is exactly \(F(m)-F(mL)\), with a sharp \(2\zeta(3)(1-L^{-3})m^{-3}\) leading term. On even cycles with antipodal paired colors, independently signed probes turn this deficit into an exactly binomial unbiased error with root-mean-square size smaller by precisely \(\sqrt m\) using the same number of matrix-function quadratic forms.
