For a finite connected graph with Hermitian edge-flip Lindblad jumps, the full-rank exponential Euler channel is unital exactly for weighted-regular graphs; otherwise it loses entropy even when applied to the continuous dynamics’ maximally mixed equilibrium. For at least three vertices its unique quantum stationary state has the explicit degree-biased law \(\pi_h(i)\propto d_i/(1-e^{-hd_i})\), with a first-order equilibrium bias and a fourth-order one-step entropy defect.
