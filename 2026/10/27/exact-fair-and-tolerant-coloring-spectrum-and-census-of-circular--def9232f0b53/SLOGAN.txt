For every \(n\ge3\), the FAT chromatic number of the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) is four exactly when \(4\mid n\), three when \(3\mid n\) but \(4\nmid n\), and two otherwise. The exact numbers of named FAT four- and three-colorings are respectively \(24\) or zero, and \(36\), \(6\), or zero, governed by rigid four- and six-column periods.\n
