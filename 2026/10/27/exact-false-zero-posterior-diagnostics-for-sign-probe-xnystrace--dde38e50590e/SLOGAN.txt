For the conceptual Rademacher XNysTrace estimator on any rank-two coordinate-diagonal positive-semidefinite matrix, the posterior squared-error diagnostic underestimates mean-square trace error by exactly a factor of \(m+1\) at every sample count \(m\ge3\), and falsely reports zero on a nonzero-error event of probability \(2^{1-m}\). At \(m=2\), the diagnostic is identically zero although the trace estimate is wrong with probability one.
