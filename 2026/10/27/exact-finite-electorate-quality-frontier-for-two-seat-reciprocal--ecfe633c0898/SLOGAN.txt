With exactly two committee seats, every \(n\)-voter approval election with supported candidates admits reciprocal JR and reciprocal EJR committees retaining at least \(\frac34+\frac1{4(n-1)}\) of maximum total candidate quality, and this ratio is best possible for every \(n\ge2\). One replacement of a highest-quality two-candidate committee suffices for reciprocal JR; both axioms have equal best attainable quality at two seats.
