# Scientific review: finite-electorate reciprocal-JR quality price

**Correctness — PASS.** Reciprocal JR is reduced without rounding to the condition \(2h_c|N(c)\cap U(W)|<n\) for every candidate. A violating group for the quality-optimal committee has at most \(n-1\) members because every optimal candidate has a supporter; this yields the sharp quality lower bound \(n/[2(n-1)]\). A subunit-quality violating candidate has strict majority support, and including it with a highest-quality candidate repairs every possible entitlement. The unit-quality edge case is handled separately, including an exact-half supporter set. A three-candidate construction attains equality. The equality geometry follows by tracing equality through the proof. Standalone integer-arithmetic regression checks passed; they are not substituted for the proof.

**Originality — PASS (with priority risk).** The closest work is Boehmer–Dong–Kreisel–Utke, arXiv:2609.08830v1 (2026), particularly Proposition 5.6, Theorem 5.5, and Example G.1. That paper proves the unrestricted \(4/3\) price and contains the sharpness family, but does not give a fixed-electorate upper bound uniform over all supported candidate sets or the equality necessity described here. Previously recorded lower-quality-floor analysis fixes the candidate quality domain while ranging over electorate sizes, so it does not imply the fixed-\(n\) result. The lower-bound construction is explicitly not claimed as new. Searches of mathematical results and the pertinent arXiv text did not locate the full new equality, but cannot establish priority absolutely.

**Value — PASS.** The result quantifies the exact cost of proportional representation in the smallest meaningful committee size across *all* electorates and arbitrary numbers of candidates, improving an asymptotic worst-case guarantee to a sharp practical finite-size law. The extremal condition explains the only support mechanism at the bound. This is a natural finite-parameter quality-price, not a numerically chosen valuation slice.

**Limitations.** Only reciprocal JR and two seats are covered. The all-candidates-supported assumption is essential to the upper-bound proof. Additional reciprocal axioms, other committee sizes, and unsupported candidate sets are excluded. Independent audit has not been performed. Literature priority risk remains for equivalent implicit results in earlier literature.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
