For two-seat reciprocal justified representation with \(n\ge2\) voters and any number of supported candidates, the exact worst quality-price is \(4(n-1)/(3n-2)\), not the asymptotic \(4/3\). A three-candidate profile attains the bound, and equality forces an unrepresented bloc of \(n-1\) voters and a candidate exactly at its reciprocal support threshold.
