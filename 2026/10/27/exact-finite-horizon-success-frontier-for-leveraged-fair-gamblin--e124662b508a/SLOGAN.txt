The exact maximum probability of reaching a target with at most \(N\) leveraged fair bets is \(\left[L/(L+(M/x)^{1/N}-1)\right]^N\), even if every adaptive bet may use an arbitrary distribution. For leverage \(L>1\), equal proportional gains followed by immediate ruin on a loss are uniquely optimal, and the deficit from unlimited betting is asymptotically proportional to \(1/N\).
