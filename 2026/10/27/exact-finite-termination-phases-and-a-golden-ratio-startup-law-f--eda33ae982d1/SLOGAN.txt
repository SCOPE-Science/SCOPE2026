For original AdaBB on any isotropic strongly convex quadratic, the source's informed initialization yields a sharp, fully classified termination bound of seven gradient updates, with a discontinuous change from four to seven at the normalized initial step \(1/(7\sqrt2)\). With uninformed zero initialization, the exact startup count instead grows as \(\log(1/a)/\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)+O(1)\) as the normalized initial step \(a\) tends to zero.
