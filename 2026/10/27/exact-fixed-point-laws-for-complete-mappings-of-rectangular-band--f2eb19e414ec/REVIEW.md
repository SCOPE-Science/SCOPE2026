# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The complete-mapping parametrization is bijective because the orthomorphism independently permutes each source row, while the original permutation independently permutes source rows over each output column. Fixing a prescribed cell set imposes independent fixed-point requirements on the row and column permutation families, yielding the exact generating polynomial. Three pair-incidence classes give the variance, and uniform fixed-order factorial moments prove Poisson convergence. Exact brute-force checks against an independently programmed subset formula agree for four small grids and give an additional exact \(3\times3\) distribution.

**Originality — PASS.** Sections 6.1 and 12 of Araújo et al., arXiv:2608.25092v1, concern Brandt-semigroup Latin-square counts and band existence/rigidity; Problem 15.6 explicitly calls for the cycle structures of complete mappings. The exact fixed-point generating law on zero-free rectangular bands, its inflated variance, and its two-parameter Poisson limit are not implied by those results. Searches for rectangular-band complete-mapping enumeration, transversal counts, fixed-point distributions and Poisson limits, and comparisons against available nearby findings, yielded no statement dominating this theorem. This is a best-knowledge originality determination, not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** Fixed points are the first nontrivial cycle statistic requested by the recent cycle-structure problem for semigroup complete mappings. The theorem determines the entire distribution for a canonical two-parameter semigroup family, detects a strict \(1/(ab)\) excess over random-permutation variance at finite size, and proves asymptotic Poisson behavior despite correlated grid constraints. This is more than an existence proof or a small numerical table.

**Closest literature and remaining limits.** The 2026 paper has an exact Brandt-semigroup count through Latin squares, but its Brandt zero-product multiplication differs decisively from rectangular-band multiplication. Related earlier discussion addresses Rees-matrix complete-mapping existence rather than random fixed-point counts. Possible unindexed earlier combinatorial formulas remain a residual originality risk. The result does not establish general cycle distributions or one-side-fixed asymptotics.
