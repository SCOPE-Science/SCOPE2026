For a uniformly random complete mapping of the rectangular band \([a]\times[b]\), the entire fixed-point distribution has an exact bipartite-subset formula, its mean is \(1\), and its variance is \(1+1/(ab)\) for \(a,b\ge2\). As both side lengths grow, the fixed-point count converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(1)\), supplying a distributional answer to the one-cycle case of the complete-mapping cycle-structure problem.
