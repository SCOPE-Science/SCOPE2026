For every quadrilateral book \(B_r=K_{1,r}\square K_2\) with \(r\ge2\), the entire minimal-fort hypergraph has exactly \(2^r+\binom r2\) edges and gives \(r((r+3)2^{r-2}-2)\) minimum zero-forcing sets. Its fractional zero-forcing number is exactly \(\max\{2,r/2\}\), so every \(r\ge4\) attains the integral-to-fractional factor two within planar two-connected bipartite graphs.
