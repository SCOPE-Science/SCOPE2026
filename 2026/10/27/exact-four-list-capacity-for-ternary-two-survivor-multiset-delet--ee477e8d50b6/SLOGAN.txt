For a ternary unordered multiset-deletion channel that leaves exactly two symbols, the maximum number of four-list-decodable source multisets is \(6\) at length \(2\), \(10\) at length \(3\), and **exactly \(11\) at every length \(n\ge4\)**. A sharp incidence obstruction keeps the code one below the natural monochromatic-output upper bound of \(12\) for all these longer lengths.
