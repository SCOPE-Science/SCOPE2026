For four attractive all-to-all Kuramoto oscillators with frequencies \((-3,-1,1,3)\), the complete equilibrium and unstable-index phase diagram has three exact thresholds \(K_*\approx4.7506574\), \(9/\sqrt2\), and \(12\), with equilibrium counts \(0,2,4,8\) on their open coupling intervals. Exactly one phase-locked equilibrium is asymptotically stable whenever phase locking exists away from the first threshold; all additional branches are saddles, including a symmetric unstable junction and two simultaneous folds.
