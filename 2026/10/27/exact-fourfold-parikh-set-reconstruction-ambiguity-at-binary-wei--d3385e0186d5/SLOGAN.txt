For binary words with exactly three ones, the set of compositions of all contiguous factors determines the word up to reversal, except for a sharp family of fourfold ambiguities obtained by independently swapping the inner and outer zero gaps. Exactly those words with unequal inner and outer gap pairs and \(\min(b,c)\geq\max(a,d)\) lie in four-word fibers; their exact fiber generating function is \(z^7/((1-z)(1-z^2)(1-z^3)(1-z^4))\).
