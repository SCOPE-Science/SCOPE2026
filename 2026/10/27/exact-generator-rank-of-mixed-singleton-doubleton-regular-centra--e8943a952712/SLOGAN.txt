The centralizer of an idempotent having one singleton kernel class and \(b\ge1\) doubleton classes has exact generating rank \(2\) for \(b=1\), \(4\) for \(b=2\), and \(5\) for every \(b\ge3\). Explicit optimal generators and three indispensable nonunit types establish this mixed regular case of a classical centralizer-rank problem.
