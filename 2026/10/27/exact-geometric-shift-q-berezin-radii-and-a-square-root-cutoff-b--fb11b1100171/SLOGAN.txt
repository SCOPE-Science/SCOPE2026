For geometric Hardy-space shifts \(T_\beta z^n=\beta^n z^{n+1}\), \(0\le\beta<1\), the \(q\)-Berezin range is exactly a closed disk whose radius is specified by a uniquely rooted quartic, with all extremizing kernels explicit. As \(\beta\uparrow1\), its radius approaches \(q\) with a sharp square-root deficit and its boundary attainment disappears at the limiting constant-weight shift.
