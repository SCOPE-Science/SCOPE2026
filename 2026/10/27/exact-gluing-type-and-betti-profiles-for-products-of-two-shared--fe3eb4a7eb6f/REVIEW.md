# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The product-to-gluing generator identity is checked directly; the uniqueness of the bounded gluing normal form is proved modulo the common multiplicity. The pseudo-Frobenius, Hilbert-series, Frobenius, genus and defect formulas are derived from that normal form. The two Betti patterns are obtained by a regular-binomial mapping cone combined with the classical three-generator theorem, and the arithmetic criterion uses the standard three-generator complete-intersection classification. Direct exact computations across 897 parameter triples and 97 boundary instances reproduce the stated invariants. General statements are proved, not inferred from enumeration.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The 2026 ideal-arithmetic paper supplies the product operation and a sufficient congruence case, but not the complete distinct-residue product-family dichotomy with exact Betti tables and quantitative defect, as verified from its relevant primary sections. Classical gluing and three-generator classifications cover separate ingredients and are acknowledged explicitly. The 2026 almost-complete-intersection result implies a weaker non-almost-symmetry obstruction; it does not provide the exact defect formula for this family. Published searches and the accessible mathematical-record search did not locate the identical unified product classification. Equivalence hidden in older general results or unindexed sources remains a real originality risk, especially because the proof synthesizes known techniques.

**Value — PASS.** Multiplication of numerical semigroups as ideals is a newly motivated structural operation. This is a natural complete classification for the first broad four-generator family produced by multiplying two symmetric factors with a shared multiplicity; it sharply separates complete intersections from type-two non-almost-symmetric rings, gives all ungraded minimal Betti numbers, a computable exact symmetry condition and a quantitative defect lower bound, and identifies a clean period-three transition in an extremal infinite subfamily. It is not a claim of a new general theory of gluing.

The scope excludes products without a common generator, noncoprime offsets and larger generating sets. Literature searches cannot certify global novelty.
