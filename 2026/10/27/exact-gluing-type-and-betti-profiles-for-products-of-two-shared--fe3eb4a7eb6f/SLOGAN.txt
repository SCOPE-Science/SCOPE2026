Products of two coprime two-generated numerical semigroups with a shared multiplicity and distinct offsets have a sharp four-generator homological dichotomy: only Betti vectors \((1,3,3,1)\) and \((1,4,5,2)\) occur, decided by one explicit gcd-and-membership test. Their pseudo-Frobenius numbers and almost-symmetry defects admit exact gluing formulas, and the extreme-offset family is symmetric precisely when \(m\equiv2\pmod3\).
