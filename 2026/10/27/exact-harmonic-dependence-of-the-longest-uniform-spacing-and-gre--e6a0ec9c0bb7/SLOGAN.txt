For uniform spacings, the longest gap and Greenwood's sum of squared gaps have strictly positive covariance \((H_m^2+H_m^{(2)}-2H_m)/[m(m+1)(m+2)]\) for every \(m\ge2\). Their exact Pearson correlation vanishes only at the slow rate \((\sqrt6/(2\pi))(\log m)^2/\sqrt m\), quantifying how strongly two classical spacing diagnostics overlap at finite sample sizes.
