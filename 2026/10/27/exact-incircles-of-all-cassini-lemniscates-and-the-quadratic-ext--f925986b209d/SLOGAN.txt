Every two-focus Cassini lemniscate has an exact largest-inscribed-disk radius: it is \(\sqrt{1-s^2}\) for half-focus separation \(s\le1/\sqrt2\) and \(1/(2s)\) thereafter; the maximizing center bifurcates from the midpoint to exactly two off-center locations. Consequently, the smallest inradius among monic quadratics with both zeros in the closed unit disk is \(1/2\), attained only for antipodal unit-circle zeros.
