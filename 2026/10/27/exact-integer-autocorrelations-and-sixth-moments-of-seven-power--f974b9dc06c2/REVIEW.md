The very-odd coefficient identity gives all and only \(2^k\) choices. Unique balanced base-seven differences convert the integer pair counts into powers of three. The very-odd condition prescribes oddness only and leaves integer magnitudes open. This distinguishes a meaningful inverse-correlation invariant from sequence-count data.
