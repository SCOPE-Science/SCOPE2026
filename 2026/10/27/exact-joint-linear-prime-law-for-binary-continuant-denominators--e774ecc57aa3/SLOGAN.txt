For degree-\(d\) binary continuants with letters \(T\) and \(T+1\), the two linear-prime avoidance events have exact joint counts and become independent with limiting probability \(4/9\), but their covariance oscillates in sign with a sharp decay scale \(2^{-d/2}\). The law applies to orthogonal-multiplicity-weighted polynomial denominators, in contrast to the uniform-monic-polynomial probability \(1/4\).
