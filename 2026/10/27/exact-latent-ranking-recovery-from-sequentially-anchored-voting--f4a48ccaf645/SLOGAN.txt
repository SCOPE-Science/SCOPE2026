Recording the order in which alternatives were displayed in a sequentially anchored voting process identifies an exact rooted-tree order on the voter's possible preferences, despite an unknown acceptability cutoff. The number of compatible strict rankings is \((m-1)!/\prod_{h=2}^k(r_h-1)\), and the order log reveals exactly \(\sum_{h=2}^k(r_h-h)\) additional pairwise comparisons beyond the ranked ballot alone.
