The open-neighborhood concept class of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)=J(n,2)\) has Littlestone dimension \(0,1,3,4\) on the sharp size ranges \(n=2\), \(3\le n\le4\), \(5\le n\le6\), and \(n\ge7\), respectively. Thus realizable online prediction needs at most four mistakes for all \(n\), despite \(\binom n2\) potential edge-indexed hypotheses.
