For elementary cellular automaton rule 18, precisely the odd-weight, cyclically `11`-free length-\(n\) periodic outputs lack a length-\(n\) periodic antecedent but admit one with period dividing \(2n\). At length \(n\), their exact number is \((L_n-2\cos(n\pi/3))/2\), asymptotic to half the \(n\)-th power of the golden ratio; every other periodic output has a same-period antecedent.
