An even cycle of \(2k\) unit edges admits an isometric embedding into complex \(d\times d\) matrices with Schatten trace distance exactly when \(d\ge k\). Equivalently, the sharp one-coordinate trace-class metric-cotype ratio \(k\sqrt{3/2}\) can be attained in matrix dimension \(d\) exactly at and above that threshold; a direct polar-support argument rules out every nonlinear noncommutative embedding below it.
