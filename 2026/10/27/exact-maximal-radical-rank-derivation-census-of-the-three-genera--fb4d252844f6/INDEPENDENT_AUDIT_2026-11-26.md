For odd characteristic, differentiating x_i^2 first forces the scalar term to vanish and forces the quadratic image of x_i to be y_i wedge x_i. Differentiating each anticommutator identifies the coefficients of y_i and y_j in the remaining coordinate. Thus a common y suffices, giving three quadratic parameters; the nine linear and three cubic parameters are free.
