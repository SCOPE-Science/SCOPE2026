# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The derivation-space parameters are reconstructed from the Grassmann quadratic relations (dimensions \(15\) and \(21\)); the associated graded determinant is \(\det(B)\det((\operatorname{tr}B)I-B)\operatorname{tr}B\). The subspace Möbius calculation explicitly counts matrices of trace one avoiding eigenvalues zero and one; every higher-degree coefficient is freely lifted. The embedded checker tests the derivation identities and verifies the leading counts over fields of sizes \(2,3,4,5\). Finite enumeration is not the proof for arbitrary fields.

The inspected materials do not state the exact maximum-radical-rank derivation count with both characteristic regimes. This does not certify absolute priority: relevant eigenvalue-free matrix literature was partly accessible only by abstract, and general matrix-distribution formulas may yield indirect alternative counts.

## Value
PASS. The maximal-radical-rank stratum is canonical for a local algebra, and the theorem gives its full arithmetic cardinality across all finite fields. The exceptional characteristic-two change reflects distinct geometric/linear sources: extra derivation parameters and a trace resonance in the matrix problem. The finding does not classify every derivation rank or all numbers of exterior generators.
