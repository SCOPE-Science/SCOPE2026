For the three-generator Grassmann algebra over every finite field, the exact number of ordinary derivations that preserve the radical and act bijectively on it is \(q^9(q-1)(q^5-2q^4-q^3+q^2+3q+2)\) in odd characteristic and \(q^{15}(q-1)(q^5-2q^4-q^3+q^2+3q+1)\) in characteristic two. A two-kernel Möbius calculation explains the extra characteristic-two trace obstruction.
