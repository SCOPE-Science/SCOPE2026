For uniform random spacings, the largest gap and the sample Gini coefficient have strictly positive covariance \(\frac{(m+1)H_m-2m}{2m^2(m+1)}\) at every finite dimension \(m\ge2\). Their correlation nevertheless decays as \(\frac{3\sqrt2}{\pi}(\log m)/\sqrt m\), quantifying how an extreme gap progressively decouples from overall spacing inequality.
