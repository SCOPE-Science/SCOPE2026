For every coprime three-term arithmetic progression with odd first term, the full-period mean-square error of the canonical quadratic denumerant approximation is an explicit sum of three rational polynomials in the denominations. Orthogonal Sylvester waves and exact mixed cosecant moments reduce the average square to a closed form, giving root-mean-square growth \(a/\sqrt{480}+O_d(1)\) for fixed step \(d\).
