For scalar quadratic gradient descent with an iid one-step stale-gradient probability \(q\), the exact mean-square stable step-size range is \(0<\eta\lambda<\mu_*(q)\), where \(\mu_*(q)=(\sqrt{1+8q}-1)/(2q)\) for \(q>0\) and \(\mu_*(0)=2\). The sharp ceiling decreases from \(2\) with no delay to \(1\) when every gradient is one step stale.
