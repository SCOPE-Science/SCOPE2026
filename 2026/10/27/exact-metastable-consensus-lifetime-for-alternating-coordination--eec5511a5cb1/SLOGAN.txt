In a fully overlapping two-layer coordination game on a clique, randomly alternating two opposed best-response thresholds produces an exponentially long-lived balanced population even though unanimity occurs almost surely. The exact finite consensus time is a binomial sum, and its sharp per-player logarithmic growth rate is \(\log2+\eta\log\eta+(1-\eta)\log(1-\eta)\) for thresholds \(\eta\) and \(1-\eta\).
