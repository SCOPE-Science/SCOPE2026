For every modulus \(m\ge2\) and nonnegative shift, the minimal least-significant-first automaton for the shifted binary digit sum is counted exactly by distinct nonzero-carry/residue pairs plus \(m\) zero-carry states. At power-of-two shifts \(2^r\), the count is \(m+\sum_{h=0}^{r}\min\{h+1,m\}\), exhibiting a sharp modulus-dependent transition from triangular to linear growth.
