For \(0^m1^m\) and \(1^m0^m\), every deletion-minimal common supersequence has length \(3m+j\) for exactly one \(0\le j<m\), and the count at that length is \(2\binom{m+j-1}{j}^2\). The complete factorization into complementary three-block constructions shows that asymptotically three quarters of the minimal supersequences have the maximum length \(4m-1\).
