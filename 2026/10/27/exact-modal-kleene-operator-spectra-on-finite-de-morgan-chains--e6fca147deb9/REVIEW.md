PASS. The displayed (MK), (Pos), and (Con) axioms are reduced to monotonicity, one midpoint inequality, and a positive-prefix rigidity condition. Exhaustive, disjoint normal forms in each parity give explicit multiset counts, top-preserving counts, and an asymptotic ratio of one-half. `checker.py` independently checks all equations and the two type predicates for every monotone map on sizes \(2\) through \(9\). These finite checks corroborate rather than replace the universal proof.

## Value

PASS. The count is a complete classification over a canonical infinite family of De Morgan logical algebras and quantifies the role of the non-normal operators explicitly contemplated by the motivating modal semantics. A sharp parity-sensitive enumeration and asymptotic half-normal law are natural invariants, not an arbitrary bounded census; they can benchmark finite modal model generation and constrain example searches.

## Limits

No result for arbitrary finite Kleene algebras or additional modal axioms is claimed. Primary-source full-text priority risk remains; independent validation is not performed.
