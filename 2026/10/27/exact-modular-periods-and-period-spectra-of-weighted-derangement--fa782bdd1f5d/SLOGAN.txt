For each modulus \(m\ge2\), the weighted-permutation count \(R_n(u)=\sum_{\pi\in S_n}(1+u)^{\operatorname{fix}(\pi)}\), when \(u\) is invertible modulo \(m\), has exact least modular period \(\operatorname{lcm}(m,\operatorname{ord}_m(u))\). As the weight varies, all and only the periods \(md\) with \(d\mid\lambda(m)/\gcd(m,\lambda(m))\) occur; ordinary derangements have least period \(m\) for even \(m\) and \(2m\) for odd \(m\).
