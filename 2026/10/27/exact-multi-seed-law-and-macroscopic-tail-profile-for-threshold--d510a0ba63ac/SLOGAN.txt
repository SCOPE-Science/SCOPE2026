For a fully mixed threshold cascade with \(s\) fixed initial seeds, the exact final-size law is \(\Pr(K_{N,s}=k)=\binom{N-s}{k-s}\frac{s\,k^{k-s-1}}{N^{k-s}}\left(1-\frac{k}{N}\right)^{N-k}\). For every fixed macroscopic cutoff \(\epsilon\in(0,1)\), the chance of reaching at least \(\epsilon N\) agents is asymptotic to \(s\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\sqrt{\frac{1-\epsilon}{\epsilon}}\,N^{-1/2}\), with conditional size density \(f_\epsilon(x)=\frac{\sqrt{\epsilon}}{2\sqrt{1-\epsilon}}x^{-3/2}(1-x)^{-1/2}\) on \((\epsilon,1)\).
