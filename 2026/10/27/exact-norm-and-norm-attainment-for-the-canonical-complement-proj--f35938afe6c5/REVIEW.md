# Review: exact norm and norm-attainment for the canonical complement projections

## Correctness

**PASS.** The upper bound follows from \(\|P_D\|=1\). For every proper club, the published gap decomposition supplies \([\xi,\eta)\) with \(\xi\) zero or a successor and \(\eta\in D\). The compactly supported continuous function \(f=\mathbf 1_{\{\xi\}}-\mathbf 1_{[\xi+1,\eta]}\) has norm one and yields \((Q_Df)(\xi)=2\), proving the matching lower bound. The full club gives \(Q_D=0\).

## Originality

**PASS, with bibliographic residual risk.** The primary source constructs these projections and proves only the bound \(\|Q_D\|\le2\) in the relevant statement. Targeted searches of published-finding corpus and primary literature found nearby projection-norm results for different spaces or operators, but no statement of the exact norm for this family. The claim is limited to the canonical net and does not claim a global lower bound for all approximate identities.

## Value

**PASS.** The result determines the exact norm at every nontrivial index of the published bounded approximate identity, rather than merely improving or repeating its uniform upper estimate. This identifies the construction-specific bound as sharp and gives an explicit witness; it leaves the distinct question of optimal bounds for arbitrary approximate identities open.

Closest literature: Kania–Koszmider–Laustsen, arXiv:1303.0020v1, Lemma 2.14(v) and Corollary 1.9. The proof uses their gap decomposition but adds the explicit norm-attaining witness.
