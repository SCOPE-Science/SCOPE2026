For a two-type group-composition model, homophily can make threshold adoption rise, fall, or reverse direction exactly once, with a sharp decision boundary at the active-type fraction \((m-1)/(n-1)\) for a group of size \(n\) requiring \(m\) adopters. Four-person groups are the smallest that can exhibit an interior reversal, and an exact three-of-four example has its unique adoption minimum at homophily \(8/13\).
