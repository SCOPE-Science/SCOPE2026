The number of optimum strong odd colorings of every wheel is given by a closed six-polynomial root-sum census, including exactly 65,409,120 named optimum colorings of the wheel with a 14-vertex rim. Along even rim orders this count grows like \(5\cdot2^n/8\); along odd rim orders congruent to \(1\) or \(5\) modulo \(6\) it grows like \(6\cdot3^n/16\), but it remains exactly \(24\) along the third odd residue class.
