Among all \(9{,}765{,}625\) binary five-state deterministic automata, the maximum of the shortest merging lengths over all state pairs has an exact ten-value distribution, and its mean among synchronizing automata is \(5{,}205{,}565/1{,}612{,}677\). Exactly \(240\) automata attain the sharp radius ten, all isomorphic to the classical Černý automaton up to exchanging the two letters.
