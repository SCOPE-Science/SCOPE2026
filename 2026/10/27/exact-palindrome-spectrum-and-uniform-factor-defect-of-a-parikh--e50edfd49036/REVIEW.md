# Mathematical scope

The complete palindrome catalogue for this fixed point is known. The new calculation concerns occurrence gaps, not membership in that catalogue. It proves that length 172 is sufficient for simultaneous occurrence of all 37 palindromes and locates a length-171 factor omitting one of them. Thus 172 is the exact onset of the uniform factor-defect law.

The certificate is exhaustive because every length-172 factor lies in the image under the fourth iterate of one of four binary bigrams, all of which are realized in the fixed point. The computation uses those four images, not the assumption that a long prefix represents all factors. The result concerns one morphism; it does not give a general saturation theorem for Parikh-collinear morphisms.
