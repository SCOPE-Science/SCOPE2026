## Correctness — PASS
The final formula concerns distinct contiguous palindromic factors, with the empty factor accounted for in the defect convention. A partition by zero, one, two and three ones gives an exact palindromic-factor count for every nonnegative gap quadruple. The key four-gap min-max identity is proved by considering all sixteen Boolean support patterns at each integer zero-length level. The equal-inner-gap correction cancels exactly; no random, asymptotic, or finite-enumeration extrapolation is used. Independent substring-generation code checks all 6561 quadruples with each gap at most eight, and checks the length-restricted maximizers through length 22. The code is finite corroboration, not an infinite certificate.

## Value — PASS
This class is the first possible minority-symbol sparsity level at which a finite binary word can lose a palindrome. The formula isolates the exact geometric obstruction—an asymmetric pair of short interior gaps enclosed by two longer exterior gaps—and quantifies every unit of lost richness. Its sharp length-dependent extremum distinguishes the weight-constrained regime from the known unrestricted minimum-length defect problem. The result is a natural structural classification, not merely another smallest-example enumeration or a recomputation of the length-eight examples.
