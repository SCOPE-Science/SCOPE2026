Every binary word with at most two ones is palindrome-rich; with exactly three ones, its palindromic defect is zero when the two inner zero gaps coincide and is otherwise exactly \(\max\{0,\min(a,d)-\max(b,c)\}\). This yields the sharp fixed-length maximum \(\max\{0,\lfloor(n-6)/2\rfloor\}\) and identifies every extremizer in the three-one class.
