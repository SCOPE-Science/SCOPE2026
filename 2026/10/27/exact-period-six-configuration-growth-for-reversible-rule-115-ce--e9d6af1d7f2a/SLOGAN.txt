For reversible second-order rule 115, the number of finite-support initial pairs in an \(n\)-site window returning within six steps has generating function \((1+5z^3)/((1-z+z^2)(1-z-5z^2))\), whereas three-step returns follow Fibonacci numbers. Consequently genuine six-cycles have exact window counts and grow exponentially at the sharp rate \((1+\sqrt{21})/2\), even though they form an exponentially vanishing fraction of all initial pairs.
