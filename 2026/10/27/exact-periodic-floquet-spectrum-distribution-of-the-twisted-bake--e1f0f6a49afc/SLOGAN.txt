For fixed dimension, return time and right-branch visit count in the twisted baker map, every attainable periodic-Jacobian eigenvalue multiset is classified by a partition of the number of left visits; the count of distinct spectra is exactly \(p_{\leq\gcd(M,k)}(n-k)\). Each spectral class has an explicit stars-and-bars multiplicity, and the largest-to-smallest eigenvalue modulus ratio has sharp, attained endpoints determined by the same partitions.
