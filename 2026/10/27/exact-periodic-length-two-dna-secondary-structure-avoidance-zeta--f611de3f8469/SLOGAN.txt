For four-letter Watson–Crick DNA blocks repeated periodically, avoiding every pair of reverse-complementary dinucleotides has an exact rational zeta function and exact necklace counts at every length. The number of positioned length-\(n\) blocks is \(8\alpha^n+O(2^n)\), with \(\alpha=1+2\cos(2\pi/7)\); the growth rate was known, but the complete finite-length enumeration and leading multiplicity eight are the finding.
