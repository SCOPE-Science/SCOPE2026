# Review of the exact discrete Sobolev deficit

## Correctness
PASS. The binomial Gram matrix is positive definite; a nonintersecting-path construction shows that all inverse entries are strictly positive. Two exact algebraic Hilbert-space identities yield the nonnegative phase and amplitude components, equality characterization and quantitative phase bound. The attached deterministic checks cover 640 examples but are not substituted for the universal proof.

## Originality
PASS, with historical-priority risk. Hindov–Nitzan–Olsen–Rydhe, arXiv:2610.19010v1, supplies the original sharp constant and a Landau equality sequence, not this full complex two-term stability remainder. Related discrete half-line interpolation and higher-dimensional lattice results concern different inequalities; the nonintersecting-path method is classical, and an older equivalent remainder statement might exist.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The exact complex-valued phase-synchronization energy and complementary amplitude energy provide structural all-order rigidity and quantified near-equality, not merely a recalculation of the published norm constant. It is useful for stability of extremal discrete Sobolev embeddings, while the displayed phase coefficient is not claimed sharp.

## Limits
Finite support, integer difference orders, and the \(\ell^1\)-to-\(\ell^2\) endpoint. No general weighted-stencil result or higher-dimensional extension. No independent audit has been carried out.
