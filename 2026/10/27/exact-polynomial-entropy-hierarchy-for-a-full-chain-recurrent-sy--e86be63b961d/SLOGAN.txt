For the canonical two-transition binary shift after identifying its two constant points, the induced map on finite subsets of size at most \(n\) has polynomial entropy exactly \(2n\), and collapsing all singleton subsets leaves the same value for \(n\ge2\). Although the base has only one nonwandering point, it is entirely chain recurrent, the second symmetric product has infinitely many nonfixed nonwandering points, and the full hyperspace has infinite polynomial entropy.
