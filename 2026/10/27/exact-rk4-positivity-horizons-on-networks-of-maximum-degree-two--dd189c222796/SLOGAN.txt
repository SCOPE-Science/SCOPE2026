The completed classical RK4 diffusion update has an exact, fully classified positivity horizon on every finite unweighted network of maximum degree two: paths stabilize at the sharp bound \(h=1/2\) from six vertices onward, while cycles have the same sharp bound at order six and from order eight onward. Small-network exceptions, particularly the seven-cycle's longer horizon \(4/7\), reveal precisely when fourth-order stepping can exceed the forward-Euler positivity restriction without losing stochasticity.
