For a nonsingular real \(2\times2\) matrix, the complete zero/one/two-column stopping-rank law of a single-Gaussian-probe matrix-free QB look-ahead rule has an explicit pair of arcsine-weighted integrals. Even when the target tolerance is below the best achievable rank-one error, the probability of stopping too early stays strictly positive as the spectral condition number diverges; at tolerance half the smaller singular value, the limit is \(0.247126056147589\ldots\).
