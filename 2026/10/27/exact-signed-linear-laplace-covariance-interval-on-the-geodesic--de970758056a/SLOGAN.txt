On the geodesic circle, the signed affine correction \(e^{-a\vartheta}(1+b\vartheta)\) to the Laplace covariance is positive definite exactly within an explicit closed, asymmetric interval in \(b\), with strict positive definiteness even at both endpoints. The largest admissible positive correction lies \(\sim\pi a^2e^{-\pi a}\) below the geodesic Matérn-\(3/2\) coefficient, however large the decay parameter becomes.
