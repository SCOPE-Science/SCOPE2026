For reciprocal-sum sequences \(x_{n+1}=h^2/(x_0+\cdots+x_n)\), if each prime divisor of \(h\) is \(2\), is \(3\pmod4\), or is \(13,17\pmod{20}\), then \((2/h)a_n+b_n\) is always an integer square for \(n\ge3\). The sole possible failure is at \(n=2\), and its exact natural density among starting values is \(1-\prod_{p^a\parallel h}(1-r_p/p^{a+1})\), with \(r_p=1\) for \(p=2\), \(r_p=2\) for split primes in the stated classes, and \(0\) otherwise.
