# Review: exact squared balancing-window GCDs

## Correctness — PASS
The integer Pell and balancing identities telescope the squares to an explicit prefix. The odd-index binomial expansion proves the sharp two-adic valuation because the first two nonzero terms have strictly smaller valuation than all later terms. Strong divisibility computes the shift-difference GCD, and exact prime-by-prime valuation comparisons treat two and three separately. A standard-library checker verifies finite cases; these are corroboration, not the proof of unbounded claims.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
Question 30 of Mbirika–Schrader–Spilker (2023), arXiv:2301.05758, reports an even/odd relationship without proving it. The known Lucas-balancing squared-window companion is explicitly excluded from the new claim; it does not alone imply the balancing result. Mbirika–Spilker (2022), arXiv:2204.12626, treat coefficient-one generalized Fibonacci recurrences. Searches for equivalent formulations and more general Lucas-square GCD theorems found no covering theorem in inspected evidence. Unindexed derivations or a wider theorem remain risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The formula resolves a named natural square-window GCD problem uniformly for every positive window length, and the sharp prefix valuation explains the two-adic correction. No cobalancing analogue is asserted.
