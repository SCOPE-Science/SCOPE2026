For every \(m\ge1\), the number of distinct sums of an exactly \(2m\)-digit binary palindrome and an exactly \(2m+1\)-digit binary palindrome satisfies an explicit eighth-order integer recurrence, beginning \(2,7,27,95,345,1259,4629,17019\). A reproducible \(37\)-state carry automaton proves the recurrence for all lengths and yields a rational generating function.
