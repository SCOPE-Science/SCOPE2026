For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\) with \(n\ge4\), all successful local three-colour forcing assignments admit an exact closed cardinality polynomial. Exactly \(12n(n-1)\) assignments use the minimum of three initial vertices, whereas the complete bipartite graph \(K_{n,n}\) requires \(n\) initial vertices under the same rule: removing one perfect matching creates an unbounded forcing advantage.
