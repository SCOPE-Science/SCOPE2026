The three-dimensional spherical radial--angular fourth-order Fourier moments have an exact rational Legendre-polynomial spectrum at every harmonic degree. Their high-degree tail obeys \(I_k=[\log(k+1/2)+\gamma+\log32+o(1)]/[2\pi^2(k+1/2)]\), and the sharp fourth-order quadratic stability coefficient evaluates to \(8\pi^{5/2}(1-\sqrt{7/15})\).
