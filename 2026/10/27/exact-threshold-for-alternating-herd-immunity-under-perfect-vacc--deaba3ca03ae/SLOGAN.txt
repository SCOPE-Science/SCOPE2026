For perfectly effective annual vaccination governed by the Papst–O'Keeffe–Strogatz behavioral map, a unique two-cycle alternating between an outbreak year and a herd-immunity year exists exactly above an explicit morbidity threshold. Remarkably, it exists for every positive morbidity when \(1<R\le5/2\), whereas \(R>5/2\) requires a unique positive threshold; every strictly crossing such cycle is locally attracting.
