For the sharp \(L^2\to L^6\) finite-field parabola extension problem in every characteristic greater than three, the complete transverse Hessian has exactly two curvature levels: \(3(q-2)/q\) for phase directions and \(3(q^2-2q+4)/q^2\) for amplitude directions. The first is the sharp local stability constant at every quadratic-character global maximizer.
