For the signed Thue–Morse count of multiples of seven, both maximum and minimum discrepancies over all prefixes of a dyadic window obey exact twelve-level recurrences: \(U_{r+12}=49U_r+32\) and \(L_{r+12}=49L_r-32\), for every \(r\ge7\). This yields exact extremal values on an infinite subsequence of window sizes and proves sharp two-sided power-law oscillation.
