For two consecutive originally susceptible arrivals in a stationary marked \(\mathrm{M}/\mathrm{M}/1\) infection queue, the exact zero-threshold escape correlation is \(2\rho/(1+\rho+2p\rho)>0\) for every \(0<\rho,p<1\). In heavy traffic, rare paired escapes remain clustered: the conditional chance that the second escapes given that the first escapes tends to \(1/(1+p)\).
