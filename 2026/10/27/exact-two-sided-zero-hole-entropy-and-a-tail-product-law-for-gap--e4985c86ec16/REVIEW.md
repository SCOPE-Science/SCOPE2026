# Scientific review: zero-ended holes in gap shifts

## Correctness
The proposed two-threshold formula follows by forbidding a single ordered pair class of consecutive permitted gaps. A two-state weighted renewal matrix gives the determinant equation, and the nonpositive second eigenvalue eliminates extraneous scalar roots. Finite-gap graph approximations prove the entropy lower bound; a direct word-count generating-series bound proves the upper bound without assuming the gap set is finite or eventually periodic. First-derivative tail convergence gives the displayed sharp product asymptotic. The 288 executable finite checks corroborate, but do not replace, the infinite-set proof.

## Originality
Cheriyath (arXiv:2506.20750v1) handles different endpoint conditions in its general S-gap theorem and a special sofic d-gap case; this is acknowledged as prior knowledge. The compared published results on gap-shift period sets, modular embeddings and geometric-gap entropy concern different claims. The new statement includes infinite non-eventually-periodic allowed-gap sets with a two-ended zero hole and a uniform asymptotic. An alternate classical countable-renewal formulation remains a historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value
The calculation answers a motivated missing endpoint case in symbolic-dynamical perturbation theory: a family of natural zero-ended holes with exactly one central marker. It supplies both an exact, computable entropy equation and a sharp leading asymptotic valid for all infinite allowed-gap sets containing zero, including nonsofic examples. It does not purport to classify arbitrary zero-ended words with more than one marker.

## Limits and independent checks
Only one-central-marker holes are covered; general multiple-marker holes remain outside scope. Prior sofic special cases are not counted as new. Complete historical priority and independent expert verification remain outstanding.
