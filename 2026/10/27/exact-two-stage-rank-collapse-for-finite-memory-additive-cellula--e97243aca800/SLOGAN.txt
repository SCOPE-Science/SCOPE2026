A prime-field additive cellular automaton with \(k\) memory layers on a ring of \(p^a\) sites has exactly one long nilpotent Jordan block and \(p-2\) shorter ones: its image rank first falls by \(p-1\) per step and then by one. This gives its sharp extinction time \(k(1+(p^a-1)/(p-1))\), every transient population count, and a universal final-step extinction fraction \(1-1/p\).
