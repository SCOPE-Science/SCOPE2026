For the additive update that sums the same \(p\) consecutive sites in each of \(k\) layers over \(\mathbb F_p\) on a ring of \(p^a\) sites, there is one long nilpotent Jordan block and there are \(p-2\) shorter ones. For odd \(p\), the image rank falls by \(p-1\) per step and then by one. The blocks give the sharp extinction time \(k(1+(p^a-1)/(p-1))\) and the full extinction-time distribution; exactly a fraction \(1-1/p\) of layered states vanish for the first time at the last step.
