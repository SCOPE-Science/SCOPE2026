# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A stable linear oscillator driven by independent stationary Brownian-reset wall positions has a unique stationary convolution law with finite moments. The generator recurrence is exact, and the homogeneous degree-two and degree-four moment matrices have explicit nonvanishing determinants for positive reset rates. Exact fourth-moment elimination, two-wall independence, and a factorized derivative prove the complete turning-point classification; Descartes' rule of signs and exact-rational signs rule out extra positive extrema. Independent conditional-Gaussian Monte Carlo checks agree numerically without serving as proof.

**Originality — PASS within the inspected scope.** Sarkar and Roy's 2023 source gives the resetting-wall model, stationary velocity two-point correlations, and numerical finite-chain kurtosis behavior. It does not provide the particular single-oscillator fourth-moment rational function or its two algebraically unique reset-rate extrema. The earlier active-chain papers use different driving noises; their shared second-order spectra are insufficient for this fourth-order claim. The strongest nearby prior record establishes positivity of the fourth cumulant for Brownian reset reservoirs, but that universal sign theorem does not determine the nonmonotone reset-rate phase shape. A general or unpublished equivalent formula might exist; scholarly priority is not independently certified.

**Scientific value — PASS.** Velocity excess kurtosis is a directly motivated measure of boundary-noise non-Gaussianity in a paper that reports it numerically. The exact minimal oscillator, natural critical-damping normalization, and strict two-extremum classification demonstrate a qualitative conflict between monotonically falling kinetic temperature and reentrant standardized fluctuations. Continuity proves the phenomenon is not confined to one finely tuned damping value. No broad arbitrary-network phase theorem is asserted.

The claim is restricted to two independent identical Brownian reset walls, no additional thermal noise, and the instantaneous stationary velocity. The exact number of extrema is certified only at critical damping; the near-critical statement guarantees at least two. Nearby active-reservoir studies were accessible in abstract but not fully inspected; this remains a priority risk.
