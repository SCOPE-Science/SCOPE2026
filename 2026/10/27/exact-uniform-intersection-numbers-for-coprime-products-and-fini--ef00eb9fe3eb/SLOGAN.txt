For nontrivial finite groups of coprime orders, the uniform intersection number satisfies \(u(G\times H)=u(G)+u(H)+\max\{m(G)-u(G),m(H)-u(H)\}\). This gives an explicit formula for every finite nilpotent group and a sharp counterexample \(u(C_2^2\times C_3)=4>2+1\) to previously asserted product subadditivity, with an unbounded family of failures.
