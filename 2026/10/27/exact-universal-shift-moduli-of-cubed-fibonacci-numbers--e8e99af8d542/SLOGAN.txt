For every positive shift \(k\), the greatest modulus making \(F_{n+k}^3\equiv F_n^3\) for all \(n\) has an exact four-class Fibonacci–Lucas formula, including a special factor of \(3\) when \(8\mid k\). In particular the cubed Fibonacci sequence has period \(8\) modulo \(9\), even though the original sequence has period \(24\).
