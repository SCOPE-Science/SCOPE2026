# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
PASS. The normalized Bergman monomial basis diagonalizes the radial indicator compression. In the squared-radius coordinate its two intervals have endpoints \(a=R/(R+3)\), \(b=2R/(2R+3)\), \(c=R/(R+1)\). The piecewise affine discrepancy has strictly positive interior minimum candidate \(H(b)=R(4R^2+9R+9)/((R+1)(R+3)(2R+3)^2)\), so the integration-by-parts deficit is positive for every integer \(k\ge1\). Exact rational computation checks finite instances but infinite quantifiers are discharged analytically.

## Originality
PASS within inspected literature, with residual priority risk. Abreu, Mouayn, and Voigtlaender, arXiv:2201.11705 (first version 2022-01-27), Sections 4.1--4.4, provide all-mode eigenvalue formulas and two-sided norm bounds; their use of the constant-mode eigenvalue is a lower bound, not identification of a global optimizer. Searches for spectral aliases, beta-prime formulations and disconnected radial symbols located no stated exact first-stage comparison. Related accessible Cantor claims concern Gaussian asymptotics or a different infinite-exhaustion Bergman model. Unindexed stronger radial Toeplitz literature could still cover the claim.

## Value
PASS. It gives the exact norm and complete extremizer classification for the canonical first disconnected localization set of a published Bergman fractal model, at every dilation. Unlike connected centered disks, generic disjoint annuli need not have their largest eigenvalue at angular degree zero. The exact all-degree ordering is therefore a motivated sharp structural fact, not a numerical sample or a generic radial-diagonalization tautology.

## Limitations
Only Cantor depth one and the classical unweighted Bergman disk. No statement for every Cantor depth, every beta-prime shape, or other radial supports. The primary source's general approximation bounds and spectral formulas are credited; independent priority assessment is outstanding.
