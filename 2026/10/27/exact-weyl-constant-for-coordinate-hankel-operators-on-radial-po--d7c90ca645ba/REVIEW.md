# Scientific review: exact coordinate Hankel Weyl constant

**Correctness — PASS.** Exact radial/spherical moment integration yields \(\|z^\alpha\|^2=(\pi^d/m)\alpha!\Gamma((|\alpha|+d)/m)/\Gamma(|\alpha|+d)\). The difference of the multiplication-compression diagonal forms gives exact squared singular values. The gamma-ratio expansion is uniform over each multi-index degree shell, including the extreme coordinate directions; the radial limit set has zero-measure boundary, so a lattice Riemann-sum argument yields the precise coefficient. Inverting the counting law proves the critical trace limit. The quartic two-variable value is checked with rational arithmetic; finite numerical checks agree with the shell asymptotic.

**Originality — PASS, with stated residual risks.** Searches of semantic mathematical findings, recent source literature, equivalent singular-value/Weyl formulations and classical Hankel/Dixmier work found only established membership thresholds, two-sided asymptotic comparability, and one-variable trace formulas. In particular the 2007 Schatten threshold is explicitly treated as prior work. Inspection of the primary 2026 paper, the 2024 singular-value paper and the 2018 vector-valued source gave no covering exact multidimensional leading Weyl coefficient. Full texts of the relevant 2007 and 2014 sources were not retrievable, so the comparison is necessarily qualified; no global uniqueness or exhaustive priority is asserted.

**Value — PASS.** The exact natural spectral density at the sharp critical ideal boundary is a quantitative invariant, needed to distinguish operators with identical Schatten membership conditions and to evaluate their measurable logarithmic traces. Its angular beta-average is genuinely multidimensional; the nontrivial rational value \(31/640\) illustrates the information missing from rate-only classifications. The proof applies across the entire \(m>1\), \(d\ge2\) family, rather than an arbitrarily selected finite parameter slice.

**Limitations and closest literature.** The statement is restricted to the coordinate anti-holomorphic symbol and radial power weights. The strongest direct antecedents are Bommier-Hato–Youssfi (2007) for Schatten thresholds, Fan–Wang–Zeng (2024) for comparable singular-value rates, and Bommier-Hato–Engliš–Youssfi (2014) for one-variable Dixmier traces. Some of their original texts were inaccessible; an unpublished or unindexed broader theorem could imply the coefficient. No independent validation has been performed.
