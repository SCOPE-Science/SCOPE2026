# Scientific review of the sharp sixth-moment theorem

## Correctness — PASS
Schur--Weyl duality decomposes the sixth-order trace moments into explicit highest-weight multiplicities; equality of restricted special-unitary highest weights occurs precisely under a common determinant shift. The only noncentral moments arise in ranks 3, 4, and 6 and have exact values 5, 4, and 1. Expanding the cube of the two-character linear combination gives the stated polynomial. Its subtraction from the proposed sharp constant factors into nonnegative balance and phase terms. The factorization proves all complex equality cases, not merely real coefficients. An independent Weyl-denominator torus integration checks exceptional exact moments, while a second representation computation checks ranks 3–13 and 27,695 finite assertions. Finite computation is not used as proof of the all-rank statement.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge)
The nearest foundational work uses symmetric functions, Schur--Weyl duality, and Haar trace moments, so the underlying integration mechanism is classical. The Jonnadula--Keating--Mezzadri preprint (arxiv:2003.02620, initial public posting 2020-03-05) concerns trace-moment frameworks and Hermitian invariant ensembles, not, in inspected material, the exact SU(N) coefficient optimization. Meckes's 2019 book describes U(N) trace-moment formulas; that does not state exceptional SU(N) two-character optimizers. Indexed research comparisons involving small-rank fourth moments or abelian sixth moments cover different objects or powers. The contribution claimed is only the all-rank sharp sixth-moment constant, exact global deficit, and relative-phase classification. A specialized equivalent statement may exist and cannot be categorically excluded by the search.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The central Fourier pair of the defining character and its dual is a canonical smallest nontrivial spectral packet for SU(N). The theorem gives the complete, nonmonotone rank dependence of its sharp L2-to-L6 norm, distinguishes three determinant-induced exceptional ranks, and gives an exact global balance-and-phase stability identity with all equality cases. It offers a concrete exact test case for moment norm and representation-theoretic Fourier optimization. A general trace-moment identity alone does not supply the optimized constant or equality/deficit classification without the rank-specific analysis.

## Limitations and priority risk
Only SU(N) with N >=3, sixth powers, and this two-character complex span are proved. The numerical tests span finitely many ranks and Gaussian-integer coefficients; the proof handles all complex coefficients and ranks. The inspected primary text does not eliminate older specialized calculations; independent scientific and originality review remains outstanding.
