The Tutte–Coxeter graph has blocked and porous exponential domination numbers \(\gamma_e=\gamma_e^*=4\); its exactly \(45\) optimum four-vertex sets are the point–line flags of \(\operatorname{GQ}(2,2)\). Distinct optima require at least three simultaneous vertex replacements, but the resulting exact-three-replacement graph is connected, \(20\)-regular, and has diameter \(2\).
