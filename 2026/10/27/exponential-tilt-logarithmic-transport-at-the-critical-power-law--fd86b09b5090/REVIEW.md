# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem fixes the restart waiting law separately at each observation horizon, explicitly identifies the backward age and the position observable, and proves a triangular renewal limit. Its nonstandard ingredient is the local renewal estimate uniform for times proportional to the observation horizon. A direct geometric-mixture representation makes the survival sequence log-convex; coefficient induction makes the renewal mass nonincreasing. Concentration of truncated renewal sums and an exponential-MGF bound give proportional-time integrated renewal asymptotics, and monotone averaging upgrades these to local mass. The age exponent then has the normalized exponential-tilt limit. The published flat-band/ballistic spectral limit combined with this age law yields the stated logarithmic-position mixture. The standalone finite verification program tests survival normalization, renewal convolution, numerical monotonicity and Fourier flat-band weights. These finite checks are not substituted for infinite asymptotics.

## Originality — PASS, best-knowledge

Das (arXiv:2609.08973v1, 2026), inspected in full in the relevant spectral, renewal, fixed-exponent and appendix sections, establishes the quantum walk's flat-band weight, renewal decomposition and power-law threshold at fixed exponent, but not the moving critical exponent, the uniform triangular local renewal estimate or the resulting logarithmic-position distribution. Bodrova and Sokolov (arXiv:2001.01350) analyze classical continuous-time random walks under power-law resetting, not this quantum crossover. A previously published geometric-reset result on the same quantum model gives a weak-geometric-restart position density at an entirely different scaling; neither statement dominates the present power-law/logarithmic critical-window theorem. Searches for alternative formulations and broader coverage did not identify equivalent statements. This is a best-knowledge assessment only; a general uninspected triangular-array renewal theorem could imply the probabilistic age lemma.

## Value — PASS

The infinite-mean threshold at exponent two is the natural nonstationarity boundary of the motivating walk. A finite-length experiment cannot use a fixed-exponent asymptotic to resolve an exponent within inverse-logarithmic distance of that boundary. The finding supplies a continuous, normalized and nontrivial family of scale exponents, with a flat-band atom whose weight can be switched exactly to zero by choosing the dark coin. It is therefore a structural critical-window transport law rather than a routine fixed-parameter coefficient. It does not predict the full position-density shape, measured first detection, or uniformity for unbounded critical-window parameters.
