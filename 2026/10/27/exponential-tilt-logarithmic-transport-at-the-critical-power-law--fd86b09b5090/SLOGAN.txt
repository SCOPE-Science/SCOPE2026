For the three-state weighted Grover quantum walk with power-law full-state restart exponent \(s_N=2+\lambda/\log N\), the logarithmic distance at observation time \(N\) converges to an exponentially tilted uniform law on \((0,1)\), together with an origin atom of exactly the unrestarted flat-band weight. The result identifies a one-parameter finite-horizon crossover at the infinite-mean restart threshold and separates persistent localization from dispersed ballistic transport.
