For two nonadjacent false twins, the inverse covariance of their single-color indicators is always nonpositive in the Potts coloring model; at every nonunit positive edge weight it is strictly negative exactly when they have at least two common neighbors. At the uniform proper-coloring limit, a clique common neighborhood makes that precision entry vanish, while a nonclique forces strict negativity once the palette has at least as many colors as vertices.
