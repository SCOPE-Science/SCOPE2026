For each prime \(p\ge5\), a primitive-normal arithmetic progression in \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\) can reach the maximal nonrepeating length \(p-1\) exactly when \(p\) is a Fermat prime. In that case there are precisely \(\varphi(p^2-1)\) ordered progressions, all given by \((g,2g,\ldots,(p-1)g)\) for primitive \(g\).
