For two sequentially arriving agents with binary chore costs, the exact optimal expected MMS loss with \(m\) known chores is \(1+(s-1)/(2(2s-1))\), where \(s=\lfloor m/2\rfloor\). Optimal policies on a fully costly first-stage inventory are characterized by balanced pair-retention probabilities; on power-of-two sizes, affine designs attain the optimum using only linearly many possible bundles.
