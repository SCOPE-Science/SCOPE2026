For the Fefferman–Szegő kernel of the quartic egg \(\|z\|^2+|w|^4<1\) in complex dimension \(n\), every dimension \(2\le n\le10\) is zero-free, while \(n=11\) has exactly two simple conjugate zero fibers. Exact rational Hurwitz determinants and Routh arrays certify the first transition, distinguishing Fefferman–Szegő zeros from previously studied Bergman-kernel and diagonal-geometric phenomena.
