For the first pivot of a Gaussian unitary ensemble matrix, real-coordinate \(\ell^p\) comparison yields an exact three-regime asymptotic: a continuously changing power for \(1\le p<2\), the exceptional Hilbert logarithm at \(p=2\), and a universal leading constant for every fixed \(p>2\). The crossover when \(p-2\) is of order \(1/\log n\) is a nonconstant binary-entropy angular integral.
