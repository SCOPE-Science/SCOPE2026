For two iid categorical observations, the Good–Turing singleton estimate and the realized missing mass have strictly negative covariance for every nondegenerate law on at most four categories, but all three covariance signs become possible on five or more. A sharp power-sum inequality proves the four-category obstruction, while the distribution \((4/5,1/20,1/20,1/20,1/20)\) gives covariance \(1/1600>0\).
