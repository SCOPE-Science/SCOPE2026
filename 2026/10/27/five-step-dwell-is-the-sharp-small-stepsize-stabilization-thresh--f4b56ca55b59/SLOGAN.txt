For the current-operator reflected-gradient method on a planar zero-sum game that switches between opposite rotations in equal blocks, dwell lengths \(m=1,2,3,4\) cause exponential instability at **every** positive stepsize, whereas each fixed \(m\ge5\) admits a convergent small-stepsize interval. The exact five-step boundary follows from a real quadratic Floquet polynomial despite the complex-valued two-state recurrence.
