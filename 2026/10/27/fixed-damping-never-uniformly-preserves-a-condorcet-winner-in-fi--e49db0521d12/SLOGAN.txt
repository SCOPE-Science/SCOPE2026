For every fixed damping \(0<d<1\), five-alternative BallotRank can rank a non-Condorcet winner above a unique strict Condorcet winner, even under complete strict rankings. An explicit finite-electorate family has limiting score gap \(\frac{d(d+2)}{5(d^3+4d^2+4d+4)}>0\), whereas taking \(d\uparrow1\) before the electorate-size limit recovers the Condorcet winner.
