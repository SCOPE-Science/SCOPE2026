Uniform-rate irreversible dimers on the complete tripartite graph \(K_{n,n,r}\), with fixed \(r\) and two growing classes, leave a vacancy count converging to \(|2B_r-r|\) for \(B_r\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(r,1/2)\), with an explicit total-variation error of order \(n^{-1}\). In particular, adding only two impurity vertices to \(K_{n,n}\) produces a limiting probability \(1/2\) of imperfect terminal coverage.
