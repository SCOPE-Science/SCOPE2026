For the clique bouquet \(W_{k,m}=K_1\vee(kK_{m-1})\), \(k\ge2\) and \(m\ge3\), the complete locally forced \(m\)-colouring enumerator by initially uncoloured vertices is \(m((m-1)!)^k[(1+t)(1+(m-1)t)^k-(m-1)t^{k+1}]\). The single correction counts the patterns that leave the same peripheral colour absent in every petal, and the minimum seed size is exactly \(k(m-2)\).
