# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Delete the pairwise diagonals of codimension n>=2. On the free quotient, the divisor class group is the equivariant Picard group upstairs; restriction across the complement preserves the upstairs Picard group Z^d and its constant units. Perfectness of A_d for d>=5 kills the character kernel. The invariant diagonal generator has the permutation linearization on the tensor product of the hyperplane lines, with every stabilizer trivial on its fibre by equality of the lines within each block. It descends to the ample H, so all global divisor classes are Cartier, and every algebraic local ring is a UFD by localization of Weil divisors. For the completion at a complex closed point, use identical coordinate systems within each equal-value block: the formal stabilizer action permutes n-dimensional blocks, and the quotient completed ring is its invariant power series ring. A nonidentity even permutation has d minus its cycle count at least two, so fixed codimension is at least 2n. For the resulting small action the character-group formula can also be seen by pulling Weil divisors to the factorial power series ring, unramified in codimension one. An invariant principal divisor gives a unit cocycle, and Hilbert 90 gives the reverse construction. Units split into constants and 1 plus the maximal ideal, whose first cohomology vanishes by formal logarithm and averaging. This leaves exactly the constant characters of the stabilizer. For r=0 the stabilizer is trivial; for r=1 it is A_m, with abelianization zero for m=2 and m>=5, and C3 for m=3,4 (A_4 has derived group V_4). For r>=2 the sign image is the even sign vectors. Every 3-cycle in a given block is conjugated to its inverse by a transposition on its support multiplied by one in another nontrivial block. Its image in the abelianization must therefore vanish, and such 3-cycles generate the whole product of alternating blocks. This proves that the sign image is the entire abelianization. The tangent determinant is one and the quotient is Cohen–Macaulay in characteristic zero, giving Gorenstein; age n(d-cycles)/2 is at least n>1, giving terminality. The canonical line on the product has the even-permutation tensor linearization without extra wedge sign; no divisor ramifies, giving -K_Y=(n+1)H and Fano. The two representative torsion cases follow, without identifying the Zariski local ring with its completion. The supplied finite checker was inspected, not executed; the argument proves the full group structure for all the stated partitions, not just the orders in its ten small test cases.

## originality

PASS

The supported distinct claim is the complete collision-partition computation for the oriented symmetric powers and their formal factorial locus. Neither the character formula, the general use of perfect-group linearizations, the mechanism for algebraic/formal disagreement, nor the determinant criterion for Gorenstein quotients is novel here. The closest ordinary symmetric-power formula is materially related but does not give the oriented quotient's character calculation, notably the isolated triple and quadruple 3-torsion.

## value

PASS

The orientation double cover of a symmetric power is a natural family. Its global algebraic UFD property does not recover any of the completion class groups. The table resolves the entire formal factorial locus: in particular a lone triple or quadruple block can produce a 3-primary obstruction, but adding a second nontrivial block kills that obstruction and produces a different 2-primary one. A researcher studying divisor algebraization or local quotient models could use exactly those groups on any stratum. The contribution is the complete and usable stratification, not the previously known phenomenon of algebraic/formal disagreement or routine terminality alone.
