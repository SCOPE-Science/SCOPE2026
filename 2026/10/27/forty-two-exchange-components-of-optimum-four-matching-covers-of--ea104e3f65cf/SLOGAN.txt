The five-petal flower snark \(J_5\) has exactly \(200\) minimum four-perfect-matching edge covers, but one-matching replacement splits their state graph into \(42\) components, including \(10\) isolated optimal covers. The complete component spectrum is ten singletons, twenty five-state graphs, ten seven-state graphs, and two ten-state graphs; the known four-cover minimum is not a new assertion.
