## Correctness — PASS
The exact analytic-symbol product identity reduces the commutator to fourfold Bergman decimation of the defect function. For polynomial symbols, each weighted matrix diagonal differs compactly from its constant asymptotic coefficient. Grouping four residue classes gives an analytic Hardy row multiplier, whose norm and essential norm coincide by a normalized-kernel argument. Discrete Fourier Parseval produces the four-orbit formula. Uniform approximation proves the full disk-algebra scope. Both algebraic boundary cases (including zeros) and the exact normalization factor are resolved. The packaged supplementary checker passed 5,444 comparisons; the proof does not rely on finite experiments.

Relevant mathematical-record searches exposed related Toeplitz, composition and commutator essential-norm results with different operator objects. Search incompleteness and inaccessible older sources leave residual priority uncertainty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The formula computes the quantitative obstruction to compactness for an entire natural disk-algebra family in terms of a four-point boundary functional. It upgrades a yes/no commutativity result to an exact Calkin norm, handles nonpolynomial symbols, and exposes genuine interaction among Taylor residues. For example, the combined symbol \(z+z^4\) gives exact commutator essential norm \(2\sqrt6\), not deducible by separately quoting the two monomial norms. This structural norm identification is not presented as a renaming of qualitative compactness or an unmotivated parameter table.
