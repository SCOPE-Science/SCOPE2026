For every pair of analytic disk-algebra symbols, the essential norm of their classical Bergman slant Toeplitz commutator is exactly the largest four-point orbit energy of the defect \(P(z)=g(z)f(z^2)-f(z)g(z^2)\): \(\|[B_f,B_g]\|_{\mathrm{ess}}=\max_{|\zeta|=1}(\sum_{k=0}^3|P(i^k\zeta)|^2)^{1/2}\). The formula quantifies noncommutativity beyond previously known zero-versus-nonzero criteria.
