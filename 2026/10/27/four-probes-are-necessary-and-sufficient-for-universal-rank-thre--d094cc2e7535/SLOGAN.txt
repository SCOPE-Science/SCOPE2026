For \(d\)-level channels of exact Choi rank \(r\), universal fixed-probe identification requires at least \(\left\lceil(2d^2r-d^2-r^2)/(d^2-1)\right\rceil\) input states. In particular, rank-three qubit channels have exact universal probe minimum four: every fixed triple leaves a generic two-dimensional local ambiguity, while four linearly independent states identify any qubit channel.
