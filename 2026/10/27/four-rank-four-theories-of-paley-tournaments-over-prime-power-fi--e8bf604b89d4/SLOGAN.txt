Finite-field Paley tournaments have exactly four first-order theories at quantifier rank four, represented by field orders \(3,7,11,19\), and all orders at least \(19\) become indistinguishable at that rank. At rank three there are only two theories, with stabilization at order \(7\); at rank four the existential-prenex fragment stabilizes at order \(11\), exposing a sharp quantifier-alternation gap.
