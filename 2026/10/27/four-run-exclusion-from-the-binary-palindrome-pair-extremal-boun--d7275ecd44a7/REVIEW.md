# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The proof partitions all factors by run count without double-counting. The four-run factor condition is derived from the possible palindrome-split positions; four exhaustive parameter regions yield the global strict bound, with the finite boundary independently checked by a direct factor-set enumerator. The independent direct enumeration agrees with the closed expression for all 715 four-run words of lengths 4 through 13. The finite check is not used to infer an infinite theorem.

**Originality (PASS, with residual priority risk).** The 2015 Borchert–Rampersad paper proposes the unrestricted maximum and gives a three-run lemma but does not give this four-run exclusion. The 2015 Guo–Shallit–Shur paper provides a characterization of individual palindrome pairs, not the simultaneous distinct-factor enumeration and length-uniform strict comparison. Targeted literature and published-mathematical-record searches found no statement that covers the restricted theorem. Exact database or unindexed specialized results cannot be ruled out absolutely.

**Value (PASS).** The excluded class has a natural structural definition (exactly three changes of bit), lying immediately beyond the three-run candidate extremizers of a published open maximum-factor problem. The result establishes a whole infinite run class that cannot be extremal, using an explicit formula and tight-enough inequalities, rather than just checking particular lengths. It deliberately stops short of an unrestricted answer.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Borchert–Rampersad (2015), Section 4 and Lemma 13; Guo–Shallit–Shur (2015), Proposition 5 and Section 3. Potential unindexed priority remains; words with five or more runs are untreated. No independent audit or validation has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
