Among all length-\(n\) binary words with exactly four alternating nonempty runs, the maximum number of distinct contiguous Lyndon factors is \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor-1\), attained only when the two middle runs have length one and the outer runs differ by at most one. The full factor-length spectrum has an exact four-parameter formula, while random four-run words have a nondegenerate \(n^2\)-scaled Dirichlet limit of mean \(9/80\) and variance \(19/6400\).
