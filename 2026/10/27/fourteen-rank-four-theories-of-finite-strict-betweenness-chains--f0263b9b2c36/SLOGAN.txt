The nonempty finite chains considered with **only strict ternary betweenness** have exactly fourteen first-order rank-four theories: \(\mathcal B_n\equiv_4\mathcal B_m\) precisely when \(\min(n,14)=\min(m,14)\). Forgetting orientation collapses the final boundary one point earlier than for pure linear orders, and fourteen elements are necessary and sufficient as a uniform representative bound.
