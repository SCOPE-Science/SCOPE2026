For the Frobenius-incidence threefolds \(X_m=\{\sum x_i^{p^m}y_i=0\}\subset\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2\), the integral Chow cubic has discriminant \(81p^{2m}\), so the unmarked graded integral Chow ring recovers the exact Frobenius height \(m\). Yet all members have mutually isomorphic rational Chow rings, isomorphic Chow rings over every prime field, and identical zeta functions over \(\mathbb F_p\).
