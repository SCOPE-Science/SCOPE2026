The coefficientwise characteristic-three Schur quartic is a nonnormal integral quartic whose normalization is the Hirzebruch surface \(\mathbb F_2\); its singular line is a degree-three Frobenius pinch with a length-nine Jacobian thickening. Its exact point count is \(\#X(\mathbb F_{3^r})=(3^r+1)^2\), distinct from the smooth good-reduction Fermat model.
