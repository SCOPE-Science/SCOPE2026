For isotropic Itô diffusion with diffusivity proportional to a negative power of radius in every dimension at least three, Poisson resetting produces an exact product-of-two-gammas law for a power of the stationary radius. The density at the reset point changes sharply from divergent to finite to zero when the sluggishness exponent crosses \(d-2\); beyond that boundary it has a unique off-origin mode, while the stationary small-ball probability always has exponent \(\alpha+2\), independent of dimension.
