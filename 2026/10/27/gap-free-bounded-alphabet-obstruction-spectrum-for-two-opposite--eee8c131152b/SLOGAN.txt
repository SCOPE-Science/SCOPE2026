For the team formula \(\operatorname{dep}(x,y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(y,x)\) on finite alphabets of sizes \(a,b\), the minimal failing subteams have **every** size from \(6\) through \(2\min(a,b)+1\) when the alphabets are equal, or through \(2\min(a,b)+2\) when unequal, except that none exist if \(\min(a,b)=1\) or \(a=b=2\). This gives the exact sharp bounded-alphabet obstruction cutoff and its complete gap-free spectrum.
