On each finite cycle, weakly reinforced edge urns have an explicit Fourier-mode Gaussian covariance until the reinforcement exponent reaches \(\alpha_c(d)=1/(2m_d)\), with \(m_d=1\) for even cycles and \(m_d=\cos^2(\pi/(2d))\) for odd cycles. At equality, precisely one even-cycle direction or two odd-cycle directions acquire \(\sqrt{t\log t}\) fluctuations, while all other directions retain \(\sqrt t\) Gaussian scaling.
