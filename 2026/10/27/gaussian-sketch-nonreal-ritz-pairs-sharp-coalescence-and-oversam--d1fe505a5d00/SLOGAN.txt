In a real-Gaussian sketched Rayleigh–Ritz reduction of an explicit three-state positive-definite matrix, the chance of a nonreal conjugate pair grows as \(C_s\sqrt t\) near a compressed eigenvalue collision and decays as \(K_s t^{1-s}\) when the gap grows. Extra sketch rows strengthen the large-gap decay but leave the sharp collision exponent \(1/2\) unchanged.
