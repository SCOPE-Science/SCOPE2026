In Yuan's finite-dimensional pseudoconvex examples, an explicit gamma-weighted polynomial determines the Bergman semimetric and its branch-point rank loss. Its positive transverse curvature at the two \(x=0\) points occurs exactly for genera \(2\) and \(3\), switching to negative in every genus \(d\ge4\); this is curvature of a degenerate ambient Bergman form, not a positive-definite Bergman metric.
