For the two-row ladder \(P_n\square K_2\), all geodesic transversals admit a closed counting polynomial, and exactly \(3n\) of them are minimum-size sets. Inclusion-minimal transversals have size two until five rungs, while for every \(n\ge6\) there are exactly \(n(n-4)(n-5)/3\) additional inclusion-minimal triples and no larger ones.
