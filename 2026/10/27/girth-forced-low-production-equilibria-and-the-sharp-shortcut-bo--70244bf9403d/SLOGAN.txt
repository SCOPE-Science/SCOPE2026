For strict exact-two-neighbor production on every graph of girth \(g\ge5\), the entire equilibrium-count polynomial below degree \(2g-4\) equals the simple-cycle enumerator: \([z^k]E_G(z)=c_k(G)\). The cutoff is sharp for every girth, and the minimum nonzero equilibrium production is exactly \(g\), attained once per shortest cycle.
