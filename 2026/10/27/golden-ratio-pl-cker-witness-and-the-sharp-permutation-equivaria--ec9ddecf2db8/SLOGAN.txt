The real third Plücker determinant has a canonical permutation-equivariant family whose exact norm is governed by \(r+r^{p-1}=1\) for every \(p>2\). At \(p=3\) the resulting golden-ratio lower bound \((1+\sqrt5)/2\) strictly exceeds the putative interpolation bound \(4^{1/3}\) from the two sharp real endpoints; no unrestricted sharp constant is claimed.
