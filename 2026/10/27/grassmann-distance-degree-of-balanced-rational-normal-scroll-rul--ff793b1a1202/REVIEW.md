# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For each integer \(m\ge2\), the proof derives the rational objective \(f_Q/s\) and checks the orthogonal-frame tangent constraints. Three explicit kernel vectors make the numerator differential surjective, so a generic rank-two projector supplies generic numerator coefficients. The derivative has exactly \(4m-2\) finite simple zeros off the denominator poles. The Schubert-divisor incidence calculation proves that no such zero lies in the algebraic cut locus generically. Injectivity and immersivity of the ruling curve complete the degree count. The standalone checker corroborates samples at \(m=2,3,4,5\). The computations alone are not a universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by residual priority risk

Friedman–Hoşten–Rosana (arXiv:2610.02018, inspected full HTML, sections 2 and 4) compute the ED degree of scroll rulings and set up GD degree but do not compute its GD degree. Friedman–Rosana–Sturmfels (arXiv:2601.22843, inspected full HTML) develop line-Grassmannian GD degree without the balanced-scroll ruling theorem. Targeted public searches and semantic mathematical-record searches found no equivalent or stronger claim. Closest records describe unrelated scroll degeneration or ED degrees of complete intersections, not projection-data cut-locus incidence. Unindexed older work remains a residual priority risk; lack of a match is not proof of unique priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The scroll-ruling model is a concrete, naturally motivated family in the originating GD-degree paper. The new exact infinite-family count resolves whether restricting the data from general symmetric matrices to orthogonal projectors changes the critical-point complexity, and the proof establishes the geometric reason (numerator dominance and avoidance of cut-locus incidence). The claim is not a mere recomputation of the known ED number.

## Limits

No assertion is made for unbalanced or higher-rank scrolls, or for the number of real critical points. Independent external verification has not occurred.
