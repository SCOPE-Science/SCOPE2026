Poisson reorientation to either \(+\alpha\) or \(-\alpha\) can produce isotropic centered positional fluctuations at every positive observation time despite strictly positive directional drift. The complete nontrivial cancellation curve is \(0<r/D_R<1/2\) with \(\cos^2\alpha=(1+r/D_R)^2/(2+(r/D_R)^2)\); randomized reset directions add transverse noise that cannot be obtained by averaging fixed-direction covariance tensors.
