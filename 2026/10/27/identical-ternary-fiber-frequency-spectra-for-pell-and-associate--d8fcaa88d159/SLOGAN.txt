For every \(k\ge1\), the Pell and normalized Pell–Lucas sequences have exactly the same histogram of residue frequencies modulo \(3^k\), counted over their least period \(8\cdot3^{k-1}\). Exactly two critical branches in each sequence obey \(3\)-adic quadratic normal forms; their square-root fiber counts give a complete explicit multiplicity classification at every exponent.
