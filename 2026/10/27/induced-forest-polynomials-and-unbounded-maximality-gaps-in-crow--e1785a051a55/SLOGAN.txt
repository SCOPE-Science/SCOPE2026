For the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), the entire induced-forest polynomial and the polynomial of inclusion-maximal forests admit closed formulas. The graph is well-\(f\)-covered only for \(n=3,4\); from \(n=5\) onward its maximal induced forests have sizes exactly \(5\) and \(n+1\), yielding an unbounded size gap.
