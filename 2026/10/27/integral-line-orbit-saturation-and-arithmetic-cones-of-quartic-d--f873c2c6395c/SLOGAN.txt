The sixteen geometric exceptional curves of a degree-four del Pezzo surface give Frobenius-trace generators for its arithmetic effective cone, but their integral span can have index as large as \(16\) in the Picard group. All eighteen \(W(D_5)\) Frobenius classes have explicit trace quotient, discriminant, effective-ray and nef-ray classifications; the unique index-\(16\) case has quotient \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\).
