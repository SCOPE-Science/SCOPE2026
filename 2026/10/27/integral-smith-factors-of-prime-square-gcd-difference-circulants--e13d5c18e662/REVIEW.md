# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The complete row/column reduction uses integral unimodular basis transformations. Differences of the relations split off \(p(p-2)\) copies of \(p(p-1)\); conjugation of the all-ones operator yields a fixed four-by-four block and \(p-2\) two-by-two blocks. Determinantal divisors of the small matrices are computed exactly, including \(p=2\). A finite exact checker independently verifies small full matrices and ten prime-specific core cases; those tests corroborate rather than prove universality.

## Originality — PASS under inspected evidence
The archival Fourier-transform article computes eigenvalues rather than integral Smith factors. Gcd Smith matrices on factor-closed index sets have different entries; general circulant Smith-form reduction papers provide methods, not the present explicit formula. Semantic mathematical-record searches for the exact matrix, prime-square modulus, cokernel, and Smith invariant factors produced no equivalent or dominating theorem. The previously known eigenvalue formula determines the product of Smith factors only, not the factors themselves.

## Value — PASS
Prime-square gcd circulants are a canonical first nontrivial nested-congruence family of integer convolution matrices. Their complete torsion cokernel is a natural exact arithmetic invariant not recoverable from the known rational Fourier spectrum, and the formula holds for arbitrarily large primes without a size cutoff. Its uniform proof exposes the small integral core responsible for the exceptional terminal factors.

## Closest literature and residual limits
Van der Kamp's Fourier result (arXiv:1201.3139v1) gives eigenvalues but no exact integral Smith decomposition. Ilmonen–Haukkanen's Smith gcd matrix and the general companion-matrix approaches have different hypotheses or conclusions. Unindexed prior work remains a publication-priority risk; independently checked originality is not claimed. Higher prime powers, general composite moduli, and independent validation are outside scope.
