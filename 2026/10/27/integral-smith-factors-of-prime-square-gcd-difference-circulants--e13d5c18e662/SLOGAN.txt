For each prime \(p\), the gcd-difference circulant on \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\) has six explicit groups of integral Smith factors with respective multiplicities \(1,p-1,p(p-2),1,p-2,1\). A unimodular decomposition reduces the growing matrix to scalar blocks and one four-by-four core, determining the whole integral cokernel rather than merely its Fourier eigenvalues.
