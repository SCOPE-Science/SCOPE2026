The variance of total column ascents under independent uniform row and symbol relabellings of any order-\(n\) Latin square is exactly \(\frac{n(n+1)}{12}-\frac{(n+1)I(L)}{3n(n-1)}\), depending only on its intercalate count. The statistic is constant on its isotopy orbit precisely for squares isotopic to an elementary abelian \(2\)-group table; the maximal-intercalate classification used in this corollary is classical.
