Self-inverse partial automorphisms of an \(n\)-vertex path have an explicit interval-run rank enumerator and grow like \(\exp(\tfrac14 n\log n+O(n))\), whereas the full monoid grows like \(\exp(\tfrac12 n\log n+O(n))\). A uniform self-inverse element has domain size and number of domain components both asymptotic to \(n/2\) in probability.
