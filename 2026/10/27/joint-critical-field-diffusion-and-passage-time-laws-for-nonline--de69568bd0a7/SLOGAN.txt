At the critical zealot density of the complete-graph nonlinear \(q\)-voter process, a susceptible-count displacement of order \(N^{3/4}\) converges dynamically to a cubic-drift diffusion with an explicit quartic stationary law. Tuning the total susceptible count by order \(\sqrt N\) and the absolute zealot imbalance by order \(N^{1/4}\) produces independent quadratic and linear crossover terms, while directional switching times between fixed scaled thresholds grow as \(\sqrt N\) with explicit quadrature constants.
