For fixed numbers of independent finite S5 agents sharing one sparsely true proposition, the full counts of information cells where that proposition holds everywhere converge jointly to independent Poisson processes with size-\(j\) mean \(c^j/j!\). For a uniformly fixed number of true worlds, the agents' marked-cell processes are exactly independent even at finite sizes, and the simultaneous first-order knowledge-witness probability tends to \((1-e^{1-e^c})^r\).
