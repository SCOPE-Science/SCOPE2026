For binary cellular automata on any finite group, adjoining arbitrary new rules completes the monoid generated by invertible and lazy rules exactly when the new rules meet one irreducible orbit-collapse double-coset for **each** Klein-four quotient kernel. The sharp minimum number of new rules is \((2^r-1)(2^r-2)/6\), where \(r\) is the binary rank of the group's maximal elementary-abelian quotient; minimum completions are precisely transversals of these double-cosets.
