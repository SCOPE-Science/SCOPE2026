The strong resolving graph of the full one-subdivision of \(K_n\), for \(n\ge4\), is \(KG(n,2)\) plus isolated original vertices, giving an exact polynomial for every strong resolving set. The minimum-basis exchange graph is a cube \(Q_3\) at \(n=4\), but for every \(n\ge5\) no movement is possible below a simultaneous replacement budget of \(n-2\), when the graph becomes \(K_n\).
