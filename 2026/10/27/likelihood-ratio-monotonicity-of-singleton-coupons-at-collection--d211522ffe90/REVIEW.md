# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The conditional-on-completion surjection reduction is exact. The reverse order-two minor theorem is proved by a log-concavity convolution lemma, a monotone adjacent-column ratio, and explicit support-boundary handling. The marked-singleton generating function gives \(\mathbb E[TS]=q(H_q^2-H_q+H_q^{(2)})\), hence the stated negative covariance. A standalone program independently checks integer counts, minors, short-word enumeration and a high-precision moment comparison. Finite checking is not substituted for the proof.

**Originality (PASS, with risk).** Myers–Wilf's 2003 formula (46) is the exact joint distribution and is explicitly prior work. Their fully inspected Section 4 does not state the order-two minor inequality, global conditional likelihood-ratio ordering, or the closed mixed covariance. Barbour–Gnedin's 2008 full text addresses infinitely many boxes at fixed time. Relevant published-finding corpus searches found only distinct reset-collector, richness, missing-mass and occupied-indicator theorems. The remaining risk is that the new inequality could be a previously recorded consequence of a general total-positivity theorem; the present result is not claimed to have an independently verified priority status.

**Value (PASS).** The conditional ordering is distribution-wide: conditioning on a longer collection time decreases the expectation of every increasing singleton-count statistic, not merely its mean. The exact covariance then quantifies this stopping-time dependence on a harmonic scale. It is structurally motivated by classical stopped occupancy statistics, not by an arbitrary finite slice. The result is restricted to uniform independent coupons.
