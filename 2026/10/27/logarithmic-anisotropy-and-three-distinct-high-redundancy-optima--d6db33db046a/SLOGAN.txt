For the two-sided exponential Gabor window at increasing integer redundancy, the uniquely optimal rectangular lattices for lower bound, upper bound and conditioning live in one logarithmic boundary layer but retain distinct additive shifts \(\log6\), \(\log3\), and \(\log4\). The optimally conditioned anisotropic lattice beats the square lattice by a diverging factor \(n/[4(\log n)^2]\) in excess condition number.
