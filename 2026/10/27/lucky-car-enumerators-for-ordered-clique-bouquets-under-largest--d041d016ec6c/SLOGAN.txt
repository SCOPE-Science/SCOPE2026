For consecutive clique bouquets with the common hub labeled first, weakly increasing largest-neighbor parking words have the exact lucky-car polynomial \(u+\sum_{j=1}^{r}u^j(1+u)^{\sum_{i<j}(m_i-1)}((1+u)^{m_j}-1)\). Ordering larger peripheral cliques first maximizes this polynomial coefficientwise, and ordering smaller ones first minimizes it.
