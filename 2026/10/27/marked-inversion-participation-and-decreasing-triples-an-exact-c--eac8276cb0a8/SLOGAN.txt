For a uniform random permutation, the inversion participation count at any marked position is positively correlated with the total number of decreasing triples, with an exact quadratic position profile. More sharply, its correlation stays nonzero only in a central window of width \(\sqrt n\), where the limiting profile is \((5/4)\sqrt{3/13}/\sqrt{1+2s^2}\), peaking near \(0.60048\).
