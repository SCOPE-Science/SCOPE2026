For a finite one-lag causal iteration, the best one-sweep Euclidean relaxation has an exact three-regime classification: nilpotent cancellation is optimal precisely when the largest coupling magnitude is at most \(\cos(\pi/(L+1))\), where \(L\) counts the longest consecutive run of maximally weighted edges; the zero-relaxation boundary is the weighted shift's numerical radius \(1\). Rearranging exactly the same four edge weights can therefore switch the uniquely optimal relaxation from complete nilpotent cancellation to a strictly interior value, despite identical asymptotic spectra.
