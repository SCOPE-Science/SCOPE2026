Every wheel partial-isometry monoid on a rim of \(n\ge4\) vertices has square-zero element ranks exactly \(0,\ldots,\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). The exact number of maximum-rank square-zero maps is a weighted cyclic-run composition sum with hub corrections only at \(n=4,5,6,7\); odd rims have a sharp one-rank obstruction compared with the full symmetric inverse monoid.
