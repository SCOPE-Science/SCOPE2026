# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof derives the fort criterion using exact open-neighborhood counts and exhausts all membership-size cases: a fort with at least three vertices on one side contains a same-side triple; otherwise its only possibility is a matched double-pair. The zero-forcing/fort transversal equivalence has a direct proof. The enumeration counts intersections of two omitted-index sets, yielding all five coefficients. Independent implementation of direct definitions across five finite orders corroborates the boundary conditions; it is not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS (bibliographic priority qualified)
Boyer et al., arXiv:1801.08910, Proposition 4 classify the zero-forcing polynomial for complete multipartite graphs, not the matching-deleted crown graphs. Becker et al., arXiv:2404.05963, Proposition 9 classify minimal forts for complete bipartite graphs; their family section and full-document crown search do not state the matching-deleted classification. The 2023 fort-hypergraph paper develops general tools, and no located exact crown-graph enumerator or stronger theorem implies this formula. The six-cycle specialization was already known and is not novel. Unindexed or equivalent descriptions remain a priority risk.

## Value — PASS
The result gives a complete natural solution-space enumerator rather than just a numeric invariant. It records a sharp structural distinction between complete bipartite graphs, whose smallest forts are same-side pairs, and matching-deleted graphs, whose minimal-fort hypergraph contains same-side triples and crossed quadruples. This supplies a finite-sized hypergraph description of all zero-forcing sets on an infinite family, including exact optimizer multiplicities, useful for fort-cover formulations and comparison under edge deletion.

## Limits
The theorem is confined to the standard zero-forcing rule on one-perfect-matching-deleted balanced complete bipartite graphs. Independent validation and exhaustive bibliographic priority verification are not performed.
