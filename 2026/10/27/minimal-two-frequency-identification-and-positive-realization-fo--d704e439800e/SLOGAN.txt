Two exact phase-resolved forcing harmonics determine all four strictly positive parameters of the standard two-layer climate energy-balance model, with an explicit if-and-only-if physical-realizability test. A single harmonic—even when paired with the equilibrium response—leaves a continuum of indistinguishable models, and the exchange time is recovered simply as \(\tau=(J_1-J_2)/(R_2-R_1)\).
