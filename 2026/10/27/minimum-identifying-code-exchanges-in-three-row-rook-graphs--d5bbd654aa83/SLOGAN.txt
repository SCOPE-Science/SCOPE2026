For \(m\ge4\), the minimum identifying codes of \(K_3\square K_m\) are exactly the three distinctly row-labelled singleton columns together with arbitrary double columns. Their \(m(m-1)(m-2)3^{m-3}\) configurations form one connected, \(6(m-3)\)-regular one-vertex-exchange graph, with explicit moves and natural symmetry orbits indexed by partitions of \(m-3\) into at most three parts.
