All finite-field evolution algebras with a nonzero loop and successor edge at each vertex of a directed \(n\)-cycle (\(n\ge3\)) are classified by multiplicative necklaces, with exact automorphism-group and absolute-simplicity spectra. The singular necklaces have a unique codimension-one proper ideal, and their isomorphism-class count is \(n^{-1}\sum_{e\mid n}\varphi(e)\gcd(e,q-1)(q-1)^{n/e-1}\).
