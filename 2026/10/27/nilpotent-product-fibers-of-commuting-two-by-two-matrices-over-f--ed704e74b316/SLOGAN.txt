For commuting \(2\times2\) matrices over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), each nonzero nilpotent product has exactly \(2q(q-1)\) ordered factorizations, and the full nilpotent-product count is \(5q^4-2q^3-4q^2+2q\). The extra nonzero-product stratum quantifies the first nodal thickening beyond the previously counted mutually annihilating pairs and makes nilpotent products strictly more likely after conditioning on commutation.
