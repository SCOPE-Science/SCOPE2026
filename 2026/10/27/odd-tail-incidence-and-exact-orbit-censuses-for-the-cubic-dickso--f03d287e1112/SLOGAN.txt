For the nonpermuting cubic Dickson map \(x\mapsto x^3-3x\) modulo \(2^k\), every even residue is periodic and every odd residue is exactly one step from a cycle. At each nontrivial cycle length reached from odd inputs, exactly two of ten cycles receive all odd leaves, with an explicit uniform number \(2^{s+3}\) of odd predecessors at every vertex; all tail periods and multiplicities are determined for every \(k\).
