For the radial logarithmic-moment Bergman kernels \(P_s(t)=\operatorname{Li}_{-s}(t)/t\), each positive even parameter \(s=2m\) is a simple local zero-count transition: one negative zero enters the disk through \(t=-1\), while exactly \(m-1\) others remain inside. The entrance speed is the explicit positive odd-zeta ratio \(\frac{\pi^2}{2m+1}\frac{2^{2m+1}-1}{2^{2m+2}-1}\frac{\zeta(2m+1)}{\zeta(2m+2)}\).
