Removing a single edge from the balanced complete tripartite graph \(K_{m,m,m}\) strictly increases its canonical cut-projection endpoint norm for every \(m\ge2\) and its canonical cycle-projection endpoint norm for every \(m\ge3\), with a common leading increase \(1/(3m)\). Exact rational formulas and sharp edge-orbit transitions at \(m=3\) and \(m=4\) identify where the amplification occurs.
