The logical closure of perfect-matchings-as-models in \(K_{2n}\) has an exactly minimal, one-pass Horn basis with \(2n\binom{2n-1}{2}+n(2n-1)!!\) rules for \(n\ge3\). Its incidence lattice has all-interval Möbius invariants governed by probabilists' Hermite polynomials, while its proper top intervals carry the simple-homotopy types of ordinary complete-graph matching complexes.
