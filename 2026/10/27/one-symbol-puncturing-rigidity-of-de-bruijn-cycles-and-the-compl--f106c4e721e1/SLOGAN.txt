Every cyclic word missing just one length-\(n\) window of a \(q\)-ary de Bruijn cycle must miss a constant window. Exactly \(qn\) single-symbol deletions preserve distinct windows in every full cycle, and the complete number of shortened cycles up to rotation is \(\frac{(q!)^{q^{n-1}}}{(q-1)q^{n-1}}\).
