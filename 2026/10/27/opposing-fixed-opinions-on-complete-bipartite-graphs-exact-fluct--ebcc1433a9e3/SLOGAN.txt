One positive and one negative fixed voter, placed in opposite parts of \(K_{m,n}\), produce the exact stationary positive-count variance \((m-1)(n-1)(m+n)^2/((m+n-1)(3m+3n-4))\). When both part sizes grow in a fixed ratio, the positive fractions synchronize into a nondegenerate beta law; balanced communities converge to a uniform distribution on \([0,1]\), not to deterministic consensus.
