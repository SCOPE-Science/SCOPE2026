The 192 minimum connected-dominating sets of the four-cube \(Q_4\) are exactly the translated and coordinate-permuted six-vertex paths with flip word \(a,b,c,d,a\). Their optimum-preserving one-vertex-exchange graph is connected, four-regular, and has diameter seven, with complete distance shells \((1,4,10,23,45,76,30,3)\).
