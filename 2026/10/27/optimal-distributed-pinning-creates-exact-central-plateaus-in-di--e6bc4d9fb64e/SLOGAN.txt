A fixed total budget of quadratic anchors optimally sharpens the finite Dirichlet \(\ell^1\)-Sobolev inequality by creating a contiguous centered plateau in the Green vector. The unique allocation undergoes exactly computable support transitions, and the sharp squared constant is \(\frac{m(m^2-1)}6+\frac{(d+1-m)^2}{\Lambda+2/m}\) in its corresponding budget regime.
