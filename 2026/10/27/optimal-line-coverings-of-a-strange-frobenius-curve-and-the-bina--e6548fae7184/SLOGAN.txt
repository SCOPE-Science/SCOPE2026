For the nonreflexive Frobenius curve \(YZ^{p^2-1}=X^{p^2}\) over odd-degree finite extensions, every minimum rational-line covering in odd characteristic consists of an affine \(\mathbb F_p\)-line spread of the rational graph together with one line through infinity; exponentially many nonpencil choices exist. In characteristic two, optimal coverings with overlapping maximal secants exist for every odd extension degree at least three.
