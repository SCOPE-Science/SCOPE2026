Every ordinary Mycielskian of an odd cycle of length at least five needs exactly three vertex deletions to become bipartite: there are \(2n\) optimal deletion sets for odd \(n\ge7\), and a five-set exception raises the Grötzsch graph's count to fifteen. Their optimum-preserving single-vertex exchange graph is a triangularly decorated cycle, except at \(n=5\), where it contains a Petersen core; exact diameters and distance distributions follow.
