For the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), paired-dominating sets are exactly the selected edge sets leaving at most one original vertex isolated and having even edge count in every nontrivial component. This classifies every set, yields the formal polynomial \(n![x^n](1+x)e^{-x}\sqrt{A(x,z)A(x,-z)}\), and determines the minimum size \(2\lceil(n-1)/3\rceil\) and its complete residue-class minimum-set census.
