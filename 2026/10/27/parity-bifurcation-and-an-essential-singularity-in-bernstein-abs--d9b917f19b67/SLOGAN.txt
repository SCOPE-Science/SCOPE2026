For the standard Bernstein absolute central moment, the maximizing location stays exactly central near quadratic order when the degree is odd, but splits into two off-center maximizers just below order two when the degree is even. Their separation has the non-power-law asymptotic \((2-r)\log(1/d)\to\log(1+1/(n b_n))\), where \(b_n=2^{-n}\binom{n}{n/2}\); the improvement over the centered moment is exponentially smaller still.
