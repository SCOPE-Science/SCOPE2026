The proof uses the binomial variance identity, uniform first-derivative convergence near exponent two, and a local even-degree central-atom decomposition with uniform Taylor remainder bounds. These give a unique near-center critical point on each positive half interval for even degrees below exponent two. The logarithmic displacement and gain follow from its exact stationarity relation. Sample high-precision finite-sum checks may be replayed using `check_moments.py` with Python and `mpmath`.

Only degree-dependent local exponent neighborhoods are proved. Sample calculations cannot establish uniformity in degree or a global phase diagram in the exponent. Independent validation has not been performed.
