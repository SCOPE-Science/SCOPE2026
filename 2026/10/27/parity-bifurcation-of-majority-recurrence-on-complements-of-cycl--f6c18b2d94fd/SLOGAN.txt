Synchronous majority voting on complements of cycles has only \(2n+2\) fixed recurrent states when \(n\ge5\) is odd, but exponentially many recurrent states, including primitive two-cycles, for even \(n\). The exact even-size count is \(H_{n/2}+2n+4\), where \(H_{n/2}\) is the already-known balanced no-zigzag circular-word count; the new fact is the graph-dynamical parity classification.
