# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The Hermitian magnetic ring and local anti-Hermitian sink are normalized explicitly. At zero flux, the distinct Fourier sine modes vanishing at the absorber give all simple, isolated nondecaying eigenvalues. The local rank-one secular equation yields the exact quadratic imaginary shift \(-2\sin^2(2\pi k/n)/(\gamma n)\) for each dark branch; the complementary modes retain a negative imaginary-part gap. Spectral projections, distinct branch frequencies, and a roots-of-unity Parseval identity complete the integrated-survival coefficient. Bounded source positions follow from vanishing leading slow-mode overlaps, with all such overlaps \(O(\phi)\). A finite independent Lyapunov calculation corroborates these claims but is not substituted for proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest inspectable source, Krapivsky–Luck–Mallick (arXiv:1311.6298, especially section 4.2), treats unthreaded quantum-ring trapping and dark-state survival. Agliari–Mülken–Blumen (arXiv:0903.3288) also treat quantum trapping without a weak-flux lifetime law. Finocchiaro et al. (arXiv:2507.10669) use discrete projective monitoring rather than continuous loss; Gou et al. (arXiv:2001.01859) treat a different experimental dissipative ring. Relevant research-record searches revealed magnetic ring *band densities* and phase-gauge analogues but no evaluated equivalent mean-lifetime coefficient. Claim-vs-prior implication comparisons indicate these cited results do not logically imply the displayed exact parity-dependent coefficient without new flux perturbation and source-projector analysis. Historical priority remains a risk: broader observability and perturbative-resonance treatments may subsume the calculation.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Weak magnetic flux removes coherent dark-state protection in a finite lossy ring. The result quantifies the divergent lifetime rather than merely the appearance/disappearance of dark states. The finite source-distance prefactor has a sharp odd/even dichotomy and identifies exceptional antipodal starting positions where there is no divergence. This supplies a reproducible structural transport classification and is not simply the well-known existence of dark states.

## Boundaries
Fixed finite system, positive fixed loss rate, one localized continuous absorber and uniform hopping/flux. No claim of independent scientific validation, historical exhaustiveness, or uniform thermodynamic limit.
