For one-point types over a long truncated geodesic, consecutive even and odd integer distance bounds have the **same** Perron exponential growth rate but **different** polynomial prefactors: \(T_{2r}(n)\sim C_{2r}\lambda^n\) and \(T_{2r+1}(n)\sim c_{2r+1}n\lambda^n\), with explicit positive constants for every \(r\ge1\). The odd linear factor comes from a bounded cluster of minimally short distances whose location can vary along the path.
