# Scientific review: derangement cycle-count covariance sign

**Correctness — PASS.** Marked-cycle counting yields the exact mixed moment. The sign depends on a nonstandard determinant inequality for alternating exponential partial sums. The four parity cases reduce to inequalities across the even and odd subsequences; the proof establishes strict log-convexity of the even subsequence and strict log-concavity of the odd subsequence, including their first indices. Alternating-series integral remainders provide the factorial asymptotic. Exact-rational checks cover 38,779 admissible triples through size 80; independent sample-space enumeration checks 13 cases through size eight. Boundaries \(r=0\) and \(j=2\) are included, and tests are not treated as infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS under the documented comparisons.** Arratia–Tavaré treat conditioned permutation cycle counts and asymptotic approximation. Da Silva–Jamshidpey–Tavaré (2020), §2.1 equation (6), explicitly supply stronger general factorial moments: they *do* imply the covariance identity, which is not claimed new here. Their full primary-text §2.1–2.4 does not state the strict sign alternation or its signed first-order asymptotic. The determinant lemma provides extra work not supplied by moment subtraction alone. Searches for parity-dependent derangement covariance and related aliases found no equivalent published-finding corpus record; however older permutation/derangement inequalities could establish the same sign result without matching these words. **Mathematical value — PASS.** The cycle-count coordinates of unrestricted uniform permutations have zero covariance for distinct lengths whose sum fits, whereas derangement conditioning creates nonzero covariances with signs that switch at every increase of the unused-label count. The strict all-offset inequality and exact factorial-scale signed asymptotic explain how small conditional correlations emerge despite asymptotic Poisson independence. The classification is uniform over every admissible size and pair of lengths, rather than a routine evaluation of a single small parameter.

**Limitations.** Only ordinary uniform derangements and distinct feasible cycle lengths are covered; the asymptotic fixes the lengths. The general moment identity is prior work, and unseen historical sign theorems remain possible. No independent audit has occurred.
