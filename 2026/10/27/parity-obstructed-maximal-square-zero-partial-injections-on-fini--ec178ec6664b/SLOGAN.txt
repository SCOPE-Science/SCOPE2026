For order-, fence-, and parity-preserving partial injections on \(n\) vertices, square-zero elements reach rank \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) except when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), where they lose exactly one rank. At \(n=4k\), all maximal-rank elements are classified by paired even-length binary runs, giving exactly \(2^k\) extremizers.
