For three-bounded multiset Oddtown families, the universal parity-pattern upper bound is sharp at three and four coordinates: \(O_3(3)=4\) and \(O_3(4)=8\), with exactly six labelled four-coordinate extremizers in one orbit. Disjoint-support constructions yield \(O_m(n)\ge2n-2\) for every \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge4\), nearly doubling the classical one-bounded Oddtown scale.
