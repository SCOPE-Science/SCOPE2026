For the cycle-of-cliques graph \(C_n\square K_m\), the exact globally fastest symmetric chain has two explicit edge weights and a sharp layer-saturation transition: at odd cycle lengths \(n\ge5\), clique size \(m=8\) is the first that forces zero optimal holding, while even cycles never enter that saturated regime. For fixed \(m\ge3\), its spectral gap beats the standard uniform-edge prescription asymptotically by the factor \((m+1)/2\).
