For any finite length-two commutative chain ring with residue field \(\mathbb F_q\), the probability that two uniform \(n\times n\) matrices have zero product is \(q^{-3n^2/2}(C_{n\bmod2}(q)+o(1))\), where the two distinct constants are explicit cubes of half-integer Euler products. Conditioned on zero product, the first matrix has a universal, tight joint Smith-defect law with nonvanishing torsion probability.
