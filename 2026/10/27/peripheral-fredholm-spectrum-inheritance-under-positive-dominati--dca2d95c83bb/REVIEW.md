# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The statement is restricted to \(c_0\), where the essential norm has the exact coordinate-tail formula \(\|A\|_e=\lim_m\|AQ_m\|\). The proof derives the complex-parameter domination estimate for every power, transfers it to essential norms and then to the Calkin spectral radius. Vesentini's theorem and openness of invertibility supply the boundary persistence. The zero-radius case is handled separately.

## Originality — PASS
The closest recent result is Dobrick's ordinary peripheral-spectrum inheritance theorem (arXiv:2610.10093v1; abstract and submission metadata accessible, full text unavailable). Räbiger–Wolff (1997, Theorems 1.4 and 3.1) give related domination results under additional hypotheses, while Martínez (1993) studies essential spectral radii. The claim here is the Fredholm-essential boundary inclusion under equality of essential radii on \(c_0\); the cited inspected statements do not imply it directly because the Calkin quotient is not used as a Banach lattice and the older essential-spectrum theorem assumes an ordinary spectral-radius Riesz point. Residual risk: the full Martínez text was unavailable.

## Value — PASS
Fredholm essential spectral values are precisely the parameters at which the operator fails to be Fredholm, a central stability notion under compact perturbations. The result gives a concrete order-theoretic persistence rule for these obstructions, without requiring an ordinary spectral-radius growth hypothesis.

## Closest literature and limits
The theorem does not assert cyclicity of \(\sigma_F(T)\), does not compare interior essential spectral values, and does not claim validity on general Banach lattices. The literature search surfaced related essential-norm and positive-operator results but no exact match; non-retrieval is not proof of novelty.
