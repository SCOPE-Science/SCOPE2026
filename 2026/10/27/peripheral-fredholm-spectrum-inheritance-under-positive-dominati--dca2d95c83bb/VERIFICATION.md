# Verification

The essential-norm formula is checked from finite-rank coordinate truncations and compactness of the adjoint on \(\ell_1\). For every \(z\in\mathbb D\), the modulus domination \( |(S+z(T-S))^n x|\le T^n|x|\) is proved by induction, then passed through tail projections and the Calkin spectral-radius formula. The boundary argument uses the subharmonic maximum principle and the openness of invertibility at the norm-limit endpoint.

The claim is limited to bounded complex-linear operators on \(c_0\), with coordinatewise positivity and equal Fredholm essential radii. The case of zero essential radius is treated separately. The full texts of Dobrick (2026) and Martínez (1993) were not accessible through the available public routes; neither is represented as fully inspected, and both are retained as residual literature risks. Accessible prior-work records were checked for claim overlap; those checks do not prove global uniqueness. Independent audit and independent validation remain unperformed.
