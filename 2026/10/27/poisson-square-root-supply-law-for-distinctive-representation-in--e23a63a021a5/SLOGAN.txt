In independent approval elections with fixed committee size \(k\), distinctive representation has an exact candidate-supply threshold of order \(\sqrt n\) when approval probability lies within \(O(n^{-1/2})\) of \(1/k\). At that threshold the number of valid committees converges to \(\binom Zk\) for an explicit Poisson variable \(Z\), because virtually every committee with the required exact candidate support automatically satisfies justified representation.
