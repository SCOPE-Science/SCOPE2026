For polynomially weighted nonlinear dilations \(P(z)f^{(n)}(z^q)\) between coefficient Dirichlet Hilbert spaces, a single exponent difference yields the complete boundedness/compactness/Schatten threshold. At the noncompact boundary, a \(q\)-component polynomial residue symbol gives the exact essential norm, the full essential spectrum of \(T^*T\), and a sharp common-boundary-zero criterion for closed range.
