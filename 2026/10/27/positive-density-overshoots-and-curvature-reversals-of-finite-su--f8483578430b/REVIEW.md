For every finite nondegenerate gcd-one interarrival support, the generating function has one pole at \(1\) and finitely many nonpositive-real poles beyond the unit circle. Dominant partial fractions produce a real nonzero trigonometric sum of Cesàro mean zero and mean square strictly positive. This already implies the qualitative claim of infinitely many ratio rises/falls in that parameter range; it does not imply either positive-density statement. Berenhaut–Lund (2001) focuses on geometric renewal convergence rates; its abstract cannot settle a possible full-text overlap. The inaccessible Liggett (1989) chapter remains a named priority risk. ## Mathematical value — PASS
The main result rules out one-sided convergence and both eventual log-curvature shapes on a positive fraction of all sufficiently late indices, for every finite gcd-one interarrival law. This is a structural tail-shape obstruction beyond known convergence rates and qualitative nonmonotonicity, relevant to finite-support recurrence and renewal approximations.
