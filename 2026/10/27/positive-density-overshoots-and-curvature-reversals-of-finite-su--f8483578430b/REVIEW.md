# Scope of the revised finding

The unquantified positive-density claim is a consequence of Bell and Gerhold's theorem for real recurrence sequences. The revised statement gives a sharp maximum-step guarantee on the length of blocks needed to see both signs, including for the nonlinear curvature sequence. It is a different claim, not an originality endorsement of the original formulation. The historical self-review and the original finite-window data pertain to that earlier formulation. The revised proof is in `RESULT.md`; the exact-arithmetic examples check the extremizing family only.
