For every integer base \(b\ge2\) and fixed prime-factor count \(r\ge1\), at least \(c_{b,r}X/(\log X)^r\) squarefree integers up to \(X\) have the digit strings of all their nonunit divisors forming a prefix-free code in base \(b\). The construction settles the existence conjectures behind OEIS A090335 and A088862 for every \(r\), by separating leading digits of all subset products of carefully spaced large primes.
