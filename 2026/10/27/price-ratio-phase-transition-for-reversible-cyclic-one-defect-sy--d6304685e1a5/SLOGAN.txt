For a cyclic automaton with two individually priced opposite rotation letters and one merge \(0\mapsto k\), synchronization is possible exactly when \(\gcd(n,k)=1\). The unique optimal reset word switches direction at the sharp cost ratio \((n-k)\alpha=k\gamma\); on that boundary, exactly \(2^{n-2}\) optimal reset words coexist, all with the same explicit minimum cost.
