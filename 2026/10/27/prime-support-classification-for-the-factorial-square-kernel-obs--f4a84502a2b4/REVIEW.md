# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof uses the exact identity \(s(ac!)=a s(c!)/\gcd(a,s(c!))^2\) for squarefree \(a\), so the membership quantifier is explicit. The \(R_3=17\) and \(R_5=41\) Ramanujan-prime thresholds, together with five initial exact values, prove the two uniform lower bounds on \(s(c!)\); the finite remainder is 21 prime pairs and integers \(0\le c\le40\). A standalone verification program independently constructs the factorial squarefree kernels in two ways, checks every necessary case, and yields the five stated exceptions. Finite cases are not used as proof of the infinite tail.

## Originality — PASS (subject to independent priority review)

The closest motivating primary work, Yudin (2026), Section 7.1, defines the obstruction and proves positive lower density but does not provide a two-prime-support classification. Erdős and Graham (1976), Table 1, record five-factor factorial-square examples, including all five excluded \(\mathcal R\) products; however, their table also includes members of \(\mathcal R\), so their five-factor classification/examples do not imply the present criterion. The exact equivalence, support cutoff and complete exceptional list were not found in the inspected primary texts or accessible comparison records. Residual risk: a separately published but unindexed computation of the same obstruction set.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The set \(\mathcal R\) is a natural auxiliary obstruction in the five-versus-six factorial square-product problem. The theorem supplies its complete first interacting-prime stratum, identifies exact finite exceptions, and gives a sharp uniform eventual threshold \(q=29\). The obstruction is not multiplicatively closed even though every sufficiently large pair of admissible primes is allowed. The statement is not a general determination of factorial-square minimality.

## Limitations

The classification is restricted to squarefree integers with at most two prime factors; published formulae for the full counting density of \(\mathcal R\) and exact \(F(n)\) values are not supplied here. No independent audit or priority certification has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
