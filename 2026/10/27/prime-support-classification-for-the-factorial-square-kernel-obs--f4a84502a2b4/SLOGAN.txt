For the factorial square-kernel obstruction \(\mathcal R=\{a\text{ squarefree}:s(ac!)\ge a\text{ for every }c\ge0\}\), all members with at most two prime factors are classified: \(1\) is admitted, the admissible primes are exactly those at least \(13\), and the only excluded products of two admissible distinct primes are \(13\cdot17\), \(13\cdot19\), \(17\cdot19\), \(17\cdot23\), and \(19\cdot23\). Consequently every product \(pq\) of primes \(13\le p<q\) with \(q\ge29\) lies in \(\mathcal R\), and that uniform threshold is sharp.
