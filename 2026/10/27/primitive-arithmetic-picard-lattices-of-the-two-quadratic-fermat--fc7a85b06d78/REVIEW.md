Targeted published-finding corpus, primary full-text and terminology searches found no equivalent stated classification. Known diagonal-hypersurface zeta methods cover the cohomological formulas, which are corroboration rather than a separate novelty claim.

## Mathematical value: PASS
Integral arithmetic Picard groups are natural divisor-theoretic objects, and the pair demonstrates that quadratic descent changes the primitive intersection lattice, not only Frobenius trace. Full discriminant groups retain nontrivial information invisible in ranks or point counts. This is a motivated complete classification for the standard double-cover quadratic twist pair, not an arbitrary lattice fragment.
