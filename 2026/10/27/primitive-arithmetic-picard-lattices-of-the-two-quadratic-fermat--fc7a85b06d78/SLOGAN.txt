The two quadratic \(\mathbb F_5\)-forms of the characteristic-five Fermat sextic double-plane K3 surface have primitive arithmetic Picard lattices of ranks \(12\) and \(11\), with discriminants \(-5120\) and \(10240\), respectively. Their discriminant groups are \((\mathbb Z/2)^9\oplus\mathbb Z/10\) and \((\mathbb Z/2)^{10}\oplus\mathbb Z/10\); both follow by exact integral saturation of the published Frobenius and deck-involution actions.
