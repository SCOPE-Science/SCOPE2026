# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The classes of the 27 lines generate P: the e_i occur, and h is obtained by adding e_i+e_j to the class h−e_i−e_j. Averaging over the finite cyclic action shows that orbit sums span the rational fixed space; the integral fixed kernel is saturated. Thus the quotient order is the gcd of maximal-rank minors of a trace basis, not a rational rank or eigenvalue statistic. An independent enumeration used ordered pairwise-skew sextuples rather than reflection words: there are 72 unordered sextuples, giving 51,840 distinct permutations by recovering h'=(H+Σe'_i)/3 and all line images. Every Weyl element lies in this set by the action on the basis, and cardinality gives equality. Conjugating under the whole group gives 25 classes. Euclidean integer elimination on the trace vectors followed by fraction-free maximal minors reproduces all 25 rows. Only the types (order,rank,cycles) (3,3,3^9), (6,2,3^1 6^4), (9,1,9^3) have index 3, with class sizes 480, 4320 and 5760. Index 3 forces a cyclic quotient; every order-nine trace is 3H, and H is primitive. Finite-field Brauer vanishing identifies the fixed group with arithmetic Picard. The finite enumeration is exhaustive in the Weyl group, not a claim that all types occur over every field. The slogan that traces give rational cone generators should not be used as an effective-cone theorem without separate justification.

## value

PASS

The integral generator question for the distinguished 27 curves is natural; the invariant tells when these Galois-stable orbit sums suffice for a primitive arithmetic lattice basis and what obstruction remains otherwise. The full answer has a nonuniform outcome across the geometric Frobenius types, and the order-nine witness is an effective primitive hyperplane class missing from the trace span. This is a usable new column of integral information beyond the classical type table, not a renaming of its index or a recomputation of its cycle counts. It makes no new claim about Frobenius realization or effective cones.
