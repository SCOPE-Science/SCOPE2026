On a smooth cubic surface over a finite field, Frobenius-orbit sums of the twenty-seven geometric lines fail to generate the integral arithmetic Picard lattice in exactly three of the twenty-five \(W(E_6)\)-types, with quotient \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) in each case. In the order-nine type the three orbit sums are each \(3H\), rather than the primitive hyperplane class \(H\).
