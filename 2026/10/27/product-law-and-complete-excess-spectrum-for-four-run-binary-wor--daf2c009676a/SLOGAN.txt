Every binary word with exactly three symbol changes has exactly \(n+1+\min(a,c)\min(b,d)\) distinct closed factors, including the empty word, when its consecutive run lengths are \(a,b,c,d\) and total length is \(n\). At fixed length, every attainable excess and no other is a positive product \(pq\) with \(2(p+q)\leq n\), revealing a sharp quadratic departure from the bitonic minimum class.
