The qutrit Arosh quantum oscillator has two exact population-relaxation exceptional-point rays, \(a/b=3\pm2\sqrt2\), enclosing an interval of infinitely recurrent photon-occupation overshoots. These defective eigenvalues survive any diagonal Hamiltonian perturbation and arbitrary diagonal Lindblad dephasing because the classical population generator is an exact quotient of the full quantum Liouvillian.
