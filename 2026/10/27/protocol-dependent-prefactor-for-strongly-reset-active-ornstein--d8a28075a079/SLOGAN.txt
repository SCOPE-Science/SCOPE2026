For an active Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle completely reset inside a reflecting/absorbing interval, the frequent-reset search time has the Brownian exponential scale but an exact velocity-protocol prefactor \(\mathbb E[e^{-x_0V_0/(2D)}]^{-1}\). Stationary Gaussian velocity redraws improve the leading mean search time over zero-velocity resets by the finite factor \(\exp[-x_0^2D_v/(8D^2\tau_p)]\), at arbitrary fixed positive activity.
