For the five-element dihedral quandle algebra over a characteristic-five field, a target in the commutator subspace fails to be one commutator exactly when its first truncated-logarithm coordinate vanishes and a canonical quadratic discriminant is nonsquare. Over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic five, this yields precisely \(q(q-1)^2/2\) elements of commutator length two, explaining and extending the previously known count of forty over \(\mathbb F_5\).
