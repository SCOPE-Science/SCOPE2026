## Originality: PASS under examined evidence

The source paper on negative-doubling holes studies kneading admissibility, survivor subshifts, entropy and dimension; its inspected arguments do not provide the exact finite-time Lebesgue polynomial on this asymmetric two-parameter chamber. Alcaraz Barrera's asymmetric-hole results are for the positive-doubling map and topological properties; Dettmann's small-hole work treats asymptotic escape rates for ordinary doubling, not a large-hole finite-time law. A published symmetric negative-doubling zeta classification and a prior symmetric finite-time law cover only \(b=1-a\) with \(a\le2/5\) and cannot imply this claim on \(a>5/12\) without extra analysis. Equivalence and stronger-coverage searches found no matching law. The standard transfer-matrix method and generic escape-rate concepts are known; they are not asserted as original. Residual priority risk persists for unindexed Markov-hole special cases and inaccessible bibliographic material.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The result exactly quantifies first-exit survival across a genuine open region in the two-parameter hole plane, not an isolated numerical slice. Its nonzero quadratic prefactor contrasts with the previously obtained linear prefactor along smaller symmetric holes, while both have exponential escape rate \(\log2\) and entropy-zero trapped dynamics. The coefficient depends only on the right endpoint and produces a calculable \(1/n\) correction to the conditional hazard. This distinguishes transient transport from invariant-set classification.
