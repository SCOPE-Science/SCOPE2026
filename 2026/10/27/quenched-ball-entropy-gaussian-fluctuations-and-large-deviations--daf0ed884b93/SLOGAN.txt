For uniformly random \(q\)-ary words, the radius-one limited-permutation ball has a strictly smaller typical exponential growth rate than its published arithmetic mean, and its log-volume obeys an explicit Gaussian and full large-deviation law. The typical-versus-mean gap is positive for every finite \(q\) and is asymptotic to \(\bigl[\log(\sqrt5/\varphi)-1/(\varphi\sqrt5)\bigr]/q\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\).
