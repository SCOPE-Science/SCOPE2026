# Scientific review of the quenched cardinality-bootstrap limit

**Correctness — PASS.** The probability space and row-by-cardinality independence are explicit. Lucas's theorem gives exactly \(2^{\operatorname{pop}(s)}\) active Bernoulli factors. Uniform ellipticity bounds the product bias, while a binomial atom bound shows that low-popcount paths carry vanishing mass. Independence of environment rows produces independent bounded variables in \(L^2\) of the driving walk; a Hilbert-space strong law yields the almost-every-environment cross-bracket convergence. The conditional martingale invariance principle then gives a Brownian pair with identity covariance. The provided verifier checks the nontrivial parity and finite-law formulas. Finite checks are not substituted for the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to priority risk.** Hamza–Tournier (2026), arXiv:2610.01658v1, explicitly list the independent time–cardinality inclusion-induced model with annealed convergence (Corollary 9) but not quenched convergence, stating that the unlisted quenched cases remain unresolved. Their independent-by-subset quenched theorem is not applicable because coefficients at distinct subsets of the same size are identical here. Their totally unordered result uses a different basis and parameter hypothesis. Collevecchio et al. (2022) characterize the deterministic bracket criterion without establishing it almost surely for the present random coefficient array. Searches of closely related bootstrap-walk and quenched-invariance literature did not identify an implication yielding this complete conditional result. Priority risk remains because the full 2026 manuscript and all possible independent treatments were not exhaustively inspectable.

**Value — PASS.** The statement resolves a named conditional-versus-unconditional gap for a natural class of strongly dependent Boolean coefficients and allows arbitrary heterogeneous non-degenerate Bernoulli parameters. Its mechanism combines binary combinatorics and a Hilbert-space law of large numbers; the result is a genuine strengthening from annealed to almost-sure quenched functional convergence, not a normalization or finite experiment.

**Limitations and closest literature.** The independence of time–cardinality blocks and uniform ellipticity are necessary assumptions of the proof, not claimed to be sharp. No conditional rate or pathwise bracket limit is asserted. Relevant antecedents: Hamza–Tournier (2026), arXiv:2610.01658v1, and Collevecchio–Hamza–Shi–Williams (2022), DOI:10.1214/22-EJP818.
