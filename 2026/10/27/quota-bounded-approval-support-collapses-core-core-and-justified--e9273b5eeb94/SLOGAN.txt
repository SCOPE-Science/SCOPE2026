In approval elections where every candidate has at most the unrounded Hare quota \(n/k\) of supporters, justified representation, the ordinary core, and the fractional core+ criterion coincide for **every** committee, and a disjoint-support greedy algorithm constructs one. This threshold is exact: for every integer quota \(q\), a three-candidate election with just one candidate supported by \(q+1\) voters has a justified, core-stable committee failing core+.
