In block-preserving Gaussian-sketched Householder QR, the rank \(r\) of the deterministically retained top block gives the sharp unconditional basis-norm moment cutoff \(p<\ell-m+r+1\) whenever \(r<m\); full-rank retention makes all moments finite. At minimal Gaussian oversampling, retaining just one independent top-block direction changes the Euclidean Gram defect from infinite mean to finite mean, even for perfectly orthonormal input columns.
