For a uniformly sampled characteristic-two evolution-algebra structure matrix of **any fixed rank** \(s\), the nonzero idempotent count is \(2^D-1\) with an explicit Gaussian-binomial distribution for \(D\), including all nonperfect rank strata. At finite field size the singular-rank laws vary with \(q\), but at fixed dimension and rank they converge as \(q\to\infty\) to the fixed-space distribution of uniform \(\operatorname{GL}_s(\mathbb F_2)\).
