# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The radial diagonalization gives the normalized gamma integrals in the monomial basis. The sign of the j-to-j+1 difference changes at most once, with at most one adjacent equality; at a=0 all differences are negative. Therefore a rank-boundary tie has at most two endpoints, and all modes strictly above it are forced by Ky Fan equality. The difference of consecutive block sums is e^{-a-s}P. The coefficients through degree k are strictly negative and all later coefficients strictly positive; Descartes plus the signs at zero and infinity give a simple crossing from negative to positive. At that crossing, unimodality gives μ_{k+1}>μ_{k+N+1}, so the next crossing must be strictly later. On bounded a intervals the gamma-integrand adjacent difference is negative once j+1 exceeds the outer area parameter; hence the thresholds cannot accumulate there. These facts give every block, the stated one-dimensional freedom at the tie, and the positive derivative jump. For fixed k,N, the root tends to zero as s grows, and the lowest power of H at A versus the highest power at A+s gives the factorial-prefactor equivalent. For fixed s,N, the root integral changes integrand sign at ρ=((k+N)!/k!)^{1/N}, so A<ρ<A+s. Centering the window about ρ, the leading odd term gives the center displacement O(k^{-1}), and the even x² term contributes s²/(12k) to it. The factorial mean contributes -(N²-1)/(24k) to ρ. This checks the stated coefficient and fixed-parameter remainder, also consistent with the exact N=1 solution. The threshold variable a is enclosed area, not radius.
