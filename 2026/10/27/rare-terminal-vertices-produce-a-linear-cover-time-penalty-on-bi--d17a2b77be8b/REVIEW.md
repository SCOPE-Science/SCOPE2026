# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The explicit conditional first moment is derived from two endpoint orders, the gambler's-ruin absorption probabilities, a solved weighted exit-time recurrence, and the strong Markov property. The uniform exponential-error estimate follows by bounding the minority endpoint order and the geometric corrections to conditional exit times. A separate full visited-subset Markov-chain solver checks the exact formula on multiple small cycles. The finite checks are corroboration, not infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS under the recorded accessible comparison, with a priority limitation.** Idnani–Salem (2026, arXiv:2610.15115) give the marginal last-site distribution rather than the terminal-location-conditioned cover-time penalty in their accessible abstract. The 2021 cycle-walk discussion supplies conditional-time reasoning for the symmetric walk, which does not establish the biased rare-terminal regime. Neither the checked prior claim summaries nor the available published comparison statements yield the sharp \((2-x)\) bias-conditioned penalty or its uniform exponential remainder. The complete 2026 preprint was inaccessible, and an earlier derivation from general gambler's-ruin theory remains a priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Unlike the last-vertex marginal, this result quantifies the temporal cost of a spatially atypical coverage ending: a terminal location with exponentially small probability carries a macroscopic delay, varying continuously from the typical time to almost twice that time. The exact conditional expectation and explicit error make the effect reproducible for finite cycles and suggest a natural rare-event time/space joint limit question.

The fixed-drift scope, full-text access gap, and lack of independent mathematical validation limit the strength of the priority and generality claims.
