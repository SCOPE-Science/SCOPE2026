For geometrically smooth plane quartics over \(\mathbf F_2\), the joint numbers of rational ordinary tangent points, ordinary flexes and hyperflexes realize exactly 34 profiles among 10,920 equations in 78 projective classes. There are exactly 10,920 rational ordinary-flex incidences, so the mean number of rational ordinary flexes per equation is one; at most four ordinary flexes or three hyperflexes occur.
