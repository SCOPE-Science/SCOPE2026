The Klein cubic fourfold \(\sum_{i\in\mathbb Z/6}x_i^2x_{i+1}=0\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) contains exactly \(39\) rational points, \(75\) rational lines, and nine rational planes arranged in a \(3\times3\) grid. Exactly \(18\) rational lines lie on no rational plane, while three lie on all three planes of a grid column.
