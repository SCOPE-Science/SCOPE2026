For right Leibniz algebras with one-dimensional derived ideal in characteristic different from \(2\), reciprocal zero products force every bracket to be symmetric or Lie. Over each odd finite field, the \(n\)-dimensional class has exactly \(n+2\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) isomorphism types and a closed formula for its labelled laws, while a four-dimensional example shows the dichotomy fails with a two-dimensional derived ideal.
