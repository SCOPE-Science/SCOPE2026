# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The circle Palm geometry is explicit: each interacting pair splits into overlap mean \(\tau-u\), exclusive means \(u\), and a mutual-Palm shift for \(u\le\tau/2\). The exact mean, excess variance, and polynomial integral follow from Mecke identities. For the signed distribution expansion, graph configurations with two or more short edges contribute uniformly \(O(k^4n^{-2})\) to the \(k\)-point factorial moment; the exponential generating series gives uniform analytic errors, and Cauchy's estimate converts them to total variation. Exact rational interval certificates verify the sign changes.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The arbitrary-rule two-point correlation formula and finite-range cumulant approach are already in Hlyniana's arXiv:2511.10083. The first-order thin-process limit is already in arXiv:2609.25733. Neither paper identifies reentrant cancellation for the natural acceptance band of one to three neighbors, proves existence of two distinct critical scale windows, or states the corresponding faster count-TV limit. The claimed novelty is only this identified sign-reversal phenomenon and its explicit distributional consequence. General Charlier and connected-graph tools are acknowledged as prior, and further unindexed specialized literature remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Band-pass survival is an interpretable local density filter, rewarding points in nonempty but not overcrowded neighborhoods. Its finite-size variance changes from overdispersed to underdispersed and back as the crowding scale grows. At two resulting exact tuning parameters, the leading Poisson approximation error cancels even though pointwise dependence persists. The strong, unexpectedly reentrant behavior is a test case for second-order stochastic-geometric approximation rather than an arbitrary isolated numerical invariant.

## Closest prior literature and limits
Hlyniana's 2025/2026 paper supplies the pair-correlation machinery and all-order connected-cumulant estimates; these are important ingredients and preclude any claim of inventing generic local-thinning Poisson approximation. The 2026 thin-process paper establishes first-order convergence. The new statement is restricted to the unit circle, Poisson input, acceptance band of one to three neighbors, and count total variation at fixed \(\tau\). Root uniqueness, spatial-process total variation, and independent validation are not established.
