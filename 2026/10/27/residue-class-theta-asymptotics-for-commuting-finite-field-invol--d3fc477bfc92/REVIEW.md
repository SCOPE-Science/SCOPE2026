# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Characteristic odd makes both matrices diagonalizable with eigenvalues \(\pm1\). The four simultaneous eigenspaces give a bijection with ordered four-summand decompositions. Their exact stabilizer orders give the probability formula. Uniform estimates for the truncated Gaussian sums and the Euler-factor tail prove the explicit dimension-rate remainder; direct exhaustive checks agree in small dimensions.

**Originality — PASS within investigated literature.** Bate and Gullon's general homomorphism enumeration already covers the finite four-summand orbit formula, which is not presented as novel. Fulman–Guralnick–Stanton treat single involutions, Baek–Han treat commuting-graph geometry, and Burness et al. give general group-theoretic probability bounds. The four-residue shifted-\(A_3\)-theta leading law at fixed field size and a quantified error were not identified in these inspected works or in the accessible research-claim comparisons. An equivalent expression in theta or homomorphism enumerations remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The rate of decay and all arithmetic residue-class leading constants of a canonical random-group compatibility probability are specified. This is stronger than qualitative rarity or degree in the field-size variable, and it isolates the nontrivial \(A_3\) lattice of balanced simultaneous eigenspace dimensions.

**Closest work and remaining risk.** The strongest overlap is Bate–Gullon (2017), which gives the known exact finite orbit formula and the different \(q\)-degree limit. This sharpens the required novelty boundary. Priority remains susceptible to an unlocated analytic extraction of a similar fixed-\(q\) theta limit from older homomorphism studies.
