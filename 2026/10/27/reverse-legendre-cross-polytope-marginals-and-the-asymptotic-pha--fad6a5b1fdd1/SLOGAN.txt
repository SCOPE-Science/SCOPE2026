The diagonal projection of the uniform \(d\)-dimensional cross-polytope has the exact reverse-Legendre density \(\frac{d}{2^d}s^{d-1}P_{d-1}(1/s)\), positive and strictly log-concave on its support for \(d\ge3\). Its axial Fourier transform is positive through frequency \(\pi\), and its sufficiently distant zeros are all simple and real, with one near each \(n\pi\) shifted by \(\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}{2n\pi}+O_d(n^{-3})\).
