In the binary projective Steiner triple system \(\mathrm{PG}(m-1,2)\), every shortest sequential burning sequence is forced to choose an ordered basis in its first \(m\) rounds and a redundant source last. For \(m\ge3\), its complete number of ordered shortest sequences is \((2^{m-1}-1)\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}(2^m-2^j)\), with the same doubling-then-completion burned-count profile for every optimum.
