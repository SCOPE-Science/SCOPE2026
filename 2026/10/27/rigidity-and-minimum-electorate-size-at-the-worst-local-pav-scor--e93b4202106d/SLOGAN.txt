At the known sharp \(2k/(3k-1)\) score loss of exactly local-optimal proportional approval voting, all equality elections have a rigid pair-and-singleton ballot structure. Their minimum number of voters is \((3k-1)(k+1)/4\) for odd \(k\), but \((3k-1)(k+2)/2\) for even \(k\), giving an eight-voter sharp example with three seats.
