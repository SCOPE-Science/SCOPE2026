For \(P_h\square P_n\) with \(h=2,3\) and \(n\ge2\), every largest inclusion-minimal dominating set is a checkerboard parity class, a full horizontal row (only when \(h=2\)), or one of two period-four full-column stripes (only for even \(n\)). The exact counts are \(4\) or \(6\) for height two, and \(1\) or \(4\) for height three, according to the parity of \(n\).
