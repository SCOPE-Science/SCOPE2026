For \(r\) independent uniformly partitioned S5 epistemic frames, a size-\(m\) public announcement undergoes a sharp **second structural transition** at \(m\asymp(n/W(n))^{2/3}\): three-world common-knowledge components appear as independent Poisson counts of monochromatic triples and mixed-agent wedges, with respective means \(c^3/6\) and \(c^3\). This gives a complete first-order limiting law whose random finite part consists precisely of those rare three-world components.
