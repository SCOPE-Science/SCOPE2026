In two-machine unit-length interval scheduling, arbitrary nonnegative weights shared by the machines admit an EF1 schedule with at least \(2/3\) of optimal total weight, and the \(3/2\) price of fairness is sharp. A minimum-weight one-in-three deletion of interval-conflict paths leaves only disjoint pairs and singletons, which can be oriented to satisfy EF1 without further loss.
