For the canonical cut and cycle Hodge projections on the unweighted square torus \(C_n\mathbin\square C_n\), both real endpoint operator norms equal \((4/\pi)\log n+O(1)\), with the sharp coefficient \(4/\pi\) arising from the angular absolute mass of the infinite-lattice second-order Riesz kernel. This determines the leading asymptotic for the *orthogonal* projection in a previously posed finite-torus projection problem, without claiming an asymptotic for minimum-norm projections.
