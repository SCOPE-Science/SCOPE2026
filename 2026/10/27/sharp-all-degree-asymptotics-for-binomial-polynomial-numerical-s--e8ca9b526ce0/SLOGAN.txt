For polynomially generated numerical semigroups \(S_d(a,b)=\langle ai+b\binom{i}{d}:i\ge1\rangle\), all fixed degrees \(d\ge2\) have sharp Frobenius and genus asymptotics with universal leading coefficients \((d!)^{1/d}\) and \(\frac{d}{d+1}(d!)^{1/d}\), respectively. After the elementary \(b\)-terms are removed, the errors save a positive power uniformly over all coprime \(a,b\).
