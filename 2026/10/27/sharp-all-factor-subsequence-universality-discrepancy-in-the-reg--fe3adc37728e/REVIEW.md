# Scientific review: sharp regular-paperfolding subsequence-universality discrepancy

## Correctness (C): PASS
The proof defines one fixed binary paperfolding convention, every finite factor, the fixed-alphabet index, and its greedy complete-arch scanner. Direct odd-part arithmetic gives exactly four eight-letter blocks. Twelve state transfers reduce every contiguous interval to three exhaustive endpoint regimes; full middle blocks telescope, regardless of their number or positions. The finite-case verifier returns ranges \([-10,6]\), \([-11,7]\), and \([-11,7]\), and the two displayed words show sharpness. Symbolic proof plus finite exhaustive reductions cover all positions and lengths. Finite-prefix tests are independent checks, not an infinite proof.

## Originality (O): PASS, with limits
Relevant sections of the primary papers by Schnoebelen–Veron (arXiv:2304.11932, 2023), Shallit–Xu (arXiv:2311.14961, 2023), and Adamson et al. (arXiv:2311.10658, 2023) were compared at the level of definitions, theses, and proof implications. Schnoebelen–Veron provide arch scanners and compositional signatures generally; this finding derives a particular paperfolding transfer, exact dyadic law, and sharp all-factor discrepancy. Shallit–Xu address factors admitting repetition factorizations, a different property; Adamson et al. address regular-language universality and enumeration algorithms. Related published summaries about paperfolding prefix-normal defect and small string attractors concern other invariants. Searches for the decimal density, integer discrepancies, transfer aliases, and paperfolding subsequence universality identified no covering statement. Absence from search is not proof of global novelty; inaccessible or unindexed equivalents remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS
The exact sharp universal factor bound is a structural theorem for a classical automatic word, not an arbitrary short-length computation. It supplies the exact asymptotic subsequence-universality density, **a position-uniform bounded** discrepancy (stronger than a logarithmic error), a complete transfer identity and sharpness examples. The question is naturally prompted by arch factorization methods and automatic-word structure.

## Scientific limitations and risks
Only the regular paperfolding word and its complement are covered; arbitrary fold-instruction sequences may behave differently. General subsequence-universality algorithms have substantial conceptual overlap, so the assessed novelty is specific to the quantified sharp constants and automatic-word law. Searches are not exhaustive, and independent mathematical audit is outstanding.
