For Drury–Arveson contractive multipliers vanishing at the origin, the exact Bohr set in every dimension at least two (including countably infinite dimension) is the Hilbert ball of radius \(1/\sqrt2\), rather than the unrestricted radius \(1/3\). At every boundary point a nonlinear Blaschke-composition multiplier attains equality, and an exact coefficient-modulus identity provides a quantitative rigidity law.
