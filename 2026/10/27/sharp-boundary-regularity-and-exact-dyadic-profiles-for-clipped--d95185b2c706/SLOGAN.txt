In a clipped reinforcement-driven choice between \(z\mapsto z^2\) and \(z\mapsto z^2/2\), both escape-probability boundaries have the same exact sharp Hölder exponent \(\log_2(1/(1-\varepsilon))\), independent of reinforcement strength. After finitely many same-digit updates, both boundary laws share an exact dyadic profile; at \(\alpha=1/2,\varepsilon=1/4\), this profile exhibits genuine non-power oscillation.
