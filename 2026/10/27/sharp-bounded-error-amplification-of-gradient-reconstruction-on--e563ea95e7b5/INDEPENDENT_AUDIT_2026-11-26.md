# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Cycle-space projection on a coherently oriented theta is weighted projection of the three path means onto z1+z2+z3=0 with weights equal to path lengths. This gives the displayed entries of Q=I-P and, after summing the diagonal, within-path and cross-path terms with their signs, the stated row norm R. At fixed m, for length l>=2, use 1/u+1/v>=4/(m-l); the resulting bound (5l+3m-8)/(3l+m) has derivative (24-4m)/(3l+m)^2 and gives B_m=(3m+2)/(m+6). The shortcut row is strictly increasing in uv at fixed u+v, and at the balanced pair the exact odd/even differences from B_m are positive for m>=7. At m=5,6 the legal triples give respectively 3/2 for (1,2,2), 18/11 for (1,2,3), and 5/3 for (2,2,2). For exhaustiveness delete bridges; cyclic components have minimum degree two and ranks summing to two. Each rank-one component is a cycle; one rank-two component has either one degree-four vertex or two degree-three vertices. Suppressing degree-two paths yields, respectively, two loops meeting at a vertex or three parallel paths: the alternative of two loops linked by one path would leave a bridge. Thus the listed cactus and theta cases exhaust the cyclic topology. Bridge rows decouple with norm one. Cacti lose by the longest-cycle bound 2-2/(m-3); a smaller theta loses by strict growth, since increasing a shorter long arm of the balanced shortcut increases its row (with the small-m transitions checked separately). The formulas give M_9=2 and the stated asymptotic. A sign vector realizes a maximizing row: the phrase 'attained exactly on sign-valued edge inputs' is supported as exact attainment by a sign choice, not as a classification excluding other inputs. For example bridge coordinates can be chosen arbitrarily when a cyclic block supplies the maximizing row.
