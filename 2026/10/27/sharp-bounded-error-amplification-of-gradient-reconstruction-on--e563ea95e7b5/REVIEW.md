# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Every hypothesis is explicit: finite connected simple unweighted graph, cycle rank two, \(m\ge5\), canonical Euclidean edge-gradient projector, and edgewise maximum norm. The full proof constructs the rank-two circulation projection for three-path theta cores, derives each sign-resolved row sum, optimizes path lengths with an algebraic strict gap, classifies all bicyclic cores including attached bridges and edge-disjoint-cycle cases, and proves uniqueness and the threshold. The independent exact-rational checker reconstructed incidence nullspaces and passed 6,953 checks. The computer checks are supplemental, not proofs of unbounded claims.

Two full published mathematics records concern respectively transportation-space basis suppression and algebraic gnarledness of theta graphs; their claims use different operators or invariants. Searches of relevant literature and the mathematical-results index did not surface a stronger result implying this one. An older unindexed electrical-network derivation is a residual priority risk.

## Value
**PASS.** The two-cycle class is the first connected graph class with a non-one-dimensional circulation obstruction that may be arranged in interacting or disjoint cycles. A fixed-edge extremal classification is a natural stability question for edge-flow least squares, rather than an arbitrary parameter slice. The result identifies a unique topology, a genuine six-edge exception, an exact ten-edge amplification threshold, and an approach to amplification three. It gives a sharp dimension-specific stress test for reconstructing potentials from bounded edge errors.

The result neither minimizes over alternative projectors nor treats weights, higher cycle rank or multigraphs. Prior-work overlap checks distinguish the standard projector formula from the new topology optimization. Historical priority remains provisional and independent review has not occurred.
