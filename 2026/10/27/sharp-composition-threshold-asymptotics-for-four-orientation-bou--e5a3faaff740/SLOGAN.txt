For four-orientation stacking words with a run cap of three and balanced diagonal plies, every fixed per-orientation minimum below \(25\%\) leaves the leading count \(0.623563\ldots\,(2.919640\ldots)^n/\sqrt n\) unchanged; at exactly \(25\%\), the count instead has order \((2.919640\ldots)^n/n^{3/2}\) and is supported only at lengths divisible by four. Requiring reflection symmetry cuts the exponential growth from length exponent \(n\) to \(n/2\), with an explicit prefactor.
