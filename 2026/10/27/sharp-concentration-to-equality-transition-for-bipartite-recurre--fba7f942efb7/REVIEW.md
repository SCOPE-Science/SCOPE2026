# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS for the precise deterministic-booster, two-equal-group, bipartite mixing assumptions. The stationary vaccine-age distribution is uniform in each vaccinated group; integrating the linear waning curve gives an explicit two-branch susceptibility function. The cross-population next-generation matrix has Perron root equal to the geometric mean of both susceptibilities. The proof exhausts the four exhaustive budget regimes and verifies every equality condition. The embedded standard-library program supplies exact rational checks but the analytical inequalities establish the continuum result.

## Originality
PASS under the accessible literature and recovered preceding findings. Foutel-Rodier, Charpentier and Guérin prove fair allocation for assortative mixing only; the strict cross-group endpoint lies outside that assertion, while their general model supplies the known renewal and matrix premises. Gavish and Katriel analyze one-time leaky vaccination and final epidemic size, not this recurrent-booster Perron optimization. Residual risk: a broader unpublished or unlocated theorem might subsume this specialization.

## Value
PASS as a complete natural boundary classification for the regime deliberately excluded from the source's fair-allocation theorem. The result specifies a universal nondimensional transition, changes the identity of the global minimizers, and explains exactly when the entire allocation simplex becomes neutral. It supplies a simple benchmark for the interaction of contact disassortativity, waning curves and vaccine supply; it is not a recommendation for allocating real vaccines unequally.
