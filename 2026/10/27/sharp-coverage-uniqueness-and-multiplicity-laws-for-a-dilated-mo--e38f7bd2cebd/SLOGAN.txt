Replacing the classical unique Moser–de Bruijn decomposition \(n=a+2b\) by \(n=a+3b\) gives exactly \(\big((2+\sqrt2)^{k+1}+(2-\sqrt2)^{k+1}\big)/4\) representable integers below \(4^k\), of which exactly \(3^k\) have a unique representation. The maximum number of ordered representations is precisely the Fibonacci number \(F_{k+1}\).
