# Scientific review

## Correctness
The classification of a degree-n unicritical proper disk map as a Möbius transform of a monomial, together with exact derivative optimization, yields the full radial problem. The parameter derivative has the strict sign needed for unique maximization. The envelope derivative and strict decrease of scaled hyperbolic sine give monotonicities. Uniform endpoint decay justifies passage to the sharp degree limit. Finite numerical tests are corroboration, not proof.

## Originality
The full accessible 2022 Baranov–Kayumov–Nasyrov article proves a weaker general lower estimate and notes the monomial limiting obstruction, not this complete sharp critical-value profile. The 2013 Kraus–Roth article supplies critical-set theory, not the Bloch profile. The 1987 Colonna paper was available at abstract level only: it is a genuine historical-priority risk. Relevant indexed results concern other Bloch spaces or two-generator Blaschke factor approximation, not this result; no assertion of exhaustive literature coverage is warranted.

## Value
The general lower-bound problem naturally singles out the monomial obstruction at \(2/e\). The finding provides an exact critical-value-resolved sharp improvement in the geometrically canonical unicritical subclass, characterizes every maximizing point, and establishes the strict degree and critical-value orderings.

## Scientific limits
The theorem does not settle the lower constant for arbitrary finite Blaschke products. An unindexed classical equivalent result may exist. No independent assessment is claimed.
