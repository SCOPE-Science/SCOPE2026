For a squeezed two-phonon oscillator initialized in either dressed dark state or any coherent superposition, a phase velocity mismatch \(\Omega=\phi'(0)+4\omega\) causes a strictly positive, exactly computable \(t^3\) purity loss. The coefficient is a hypergeometric number moment, diverges as \(\gamma\Omega^2e^{6r}/(12r)\) at strong squeezing, and vanishes for the exactly phase-tracked dark sector.
