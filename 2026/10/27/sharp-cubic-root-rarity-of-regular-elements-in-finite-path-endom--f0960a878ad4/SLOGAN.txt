Among all endomorphisms of an undirected path on \(n\) vertices, the regular elements have proportion \(\exp[-c n^{1/3}+o(n^{1/3})]\), where \(c=\frac32\pi^{2/3}(\log 2)^{2/3}\). The sharp exponent balances the cost of one image-spanning monotone run against the spectral cost of confining the remaining nearest-neighbor walk; the underlying monotone-run criterion is prior work.
