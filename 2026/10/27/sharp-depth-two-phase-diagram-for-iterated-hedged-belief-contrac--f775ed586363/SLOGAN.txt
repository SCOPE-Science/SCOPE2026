The four depth-two triggers \(\Diamond\Box p\), \(\Box\Box p\), \(\Diamond\Diamond p\), and \(\Box\Diamond p\) have sharp hedged-contraction lifetimes \(1,2,n-1,n\), respectively, on unrestricted \(n\)-world one-agent Kripke models when \(n\ge3\). The order of box and diamond alone separates constant from linear stabilization, with explicit extremal frames.
