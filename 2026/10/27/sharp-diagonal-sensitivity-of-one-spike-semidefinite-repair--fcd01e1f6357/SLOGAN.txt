The sharp diagonal-entry sensitivity of positive-semidefinite spectral clipping at a matrix with one positive mode and a flat negative bulk is determined in every dimension by a unique cubic root. It is nonattained at any fixed positive spectral gap, first exceeds two at dimension four, and grows as \(n/4+\sqrt n/2+3/4+O(n^{-1/2})\); this gives a lower bound, not an equality, for unrestricted entrywise sensitivity.
