An exceptional qubit channel and a diagonalizable Pauli channel have exactly the same spectrum, yet their optimal \(n\)-step diamond distance is \(n(1-\lambda^2)\lambda^{n-1}\), attainable without entanglement. Although both single-step maps approach the identity as \(\lambda\uparrow1\), their optimally repeated versions retain diamond distance \(2/e\) in the limit.
