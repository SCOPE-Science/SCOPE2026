The sharp maximum-coordinate norm of the Euclidean gradient projection on an unweighted prism graph is attained on a rung at \(n=3\), ties at \(n=4\), and is attained on rim edges for every \(n\ge5\). Its deficit below three has a sharp \((\log n)/n\) leading term and a nonconvergent, explicitly log-periodic order-\(1/n\) correction.
