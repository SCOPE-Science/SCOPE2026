For every period \(p\), the doubling map with one periodically switching two-bit hole has exactly \(2^p\) zero-entropy hole schedules, all with exact survival probability \((n+2)/2^{n+1}\). Every other schedule has entropy at least \(p^{-1}\log((p+\sqrt{p^2+4})/2)\), with equality precisely at the \(p2^p\) schedules having one compatibility defect; this creates a sharp forbidden interval of escape rates below the maximal rate \(\log2\).
