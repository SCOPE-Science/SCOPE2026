For every binary gap shift admitting adjacent ones and at least one positive zero gap, forbidding \(0 1^k0\) with \(k\ge2\) has an exact renewal-entropy root and an entropy loss equal, to first order, to the maximal-entropy measure of that cylinder. The sharp coefficient and \(O_S(kq^{2k})\) remainder hold even when the allowed gap set is nonsofic and arbitrarily sparse.
