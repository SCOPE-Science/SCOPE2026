For single length-two palindromic duplication, the largest inverse list among length-\(N\) received words over a \(q\)-letter alphabet is exactly \(\lfloor(N-2)/2\rfloor\). The number of maximizing received words has a closed formula for every \(q\ge2\) and \(N\ge4\), and every even-length maximizer with \(N\ge6\) is an \(abba\)-periodic prefix with \(a\ne b\).
