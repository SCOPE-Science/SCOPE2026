In a positive-gap two-bath qubit pump, the unique contour maximizing adiabatic heat flux at fixed Euclidean field area is a curvature-superlevel contour. Its small-area gain has a sharp \(A^{5/3}\) loss below the maximal local slope, while its large-area gap to the available Landauer flux closes as \(\sqrt{\pi A}\exp(-4\sqrt A/(\sqrt\pi T))\).
