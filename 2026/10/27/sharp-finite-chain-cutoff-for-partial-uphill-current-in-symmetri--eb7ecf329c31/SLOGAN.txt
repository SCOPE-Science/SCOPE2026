For a finite two-species exclusion chain with opposing total-density and colour-density gradients, partial uphill species current points against its own reservoir gradient on **every** link exactly when \(F_{N+1}<R\), where \(F_L\) is an explicit strictly increasing function of chain length. Crossing the unique size threshold reverses the currents at the two reservoir links while preserving a positive interior current, and an exact physical example changes regime between three and four sites.
