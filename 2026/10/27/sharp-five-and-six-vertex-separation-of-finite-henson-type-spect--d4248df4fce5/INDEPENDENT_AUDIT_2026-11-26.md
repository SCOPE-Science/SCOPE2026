The two independent old neighborhoods may overlap when the new pair is nonadjacent, and are disjoint exactly when the new pair is adjacent. For the sharpness check I classified triangle-free graphs directly by vertex and edge counts. The five-vertex classes have multiplicities 1,1,2,3,4,2,1 at edge counts 0 through 6. At four edges they are the three five-vertex trees and C4 plus an isolate; at five edges the cycle is C4 with a leaf or C5; at six edges an odd cycle is impossible (a chord of a spanning C5 creates a triangle), so the graph is K2,3. In a triangle-free graph i3=binom(n,3)−(n−2)e+sum_v binom(deg v,2); at n=5, i4 is the number of vertices incident to every edge. These formulas reproduce the sole collision at size five and distinguish all classes through size four. Exactly one member of the size-five collision is connected.
