# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Gaussian polar integration gives all three trace-power moments with probability normalization. Lagrange multipliers and a constrained second-variation argument prove global sharp third and fourth trace-power bounds and identify compatible equality cases. The final one-dimensional quotient has a unique interior maximum; a complex-to-real Cauchy–Schwarz step supplies every complex extremizer. The independent symbolic checks test critical formulas but the analytic proof covers all integers \(n\ge2\).

## Originality
PASS (best knowledge, not a priority guarantee). The 2019 full-text lacunary spherical Nikolskii paper concerns general asymptotic orders with harmonic gap at least three; it does not imply a sharp exact bound for the gap-two packet. The 2017/2020 general spherical polynomial Nikolskii paper concerns growing-degree constants rather than this finite packet. The closest published pure-degree-two fourth-moment result gives the separate homogeneous component, not the mixed constant or new extremizers. Separate previously recorded degree-one-plus-two sharp moments on the two-sphere do not imply the even constant-plus-degree-two packet. Equivalent quadratic-form extremal inequalities from older literature are a residual originality risk; no independent priority audit has taken place.

## Value
PASS. The full space of even spherical polynomials through degree two is a natural rotation-invariant spectral packet. The calculation establishes a uniform all-dimensional sharp embedding law, strict mixing of the two harmonic degrees, and complete complex equality cases; it is not an arbitrary numerical slice or a restatement of the pure-degree-two bound.

## Scope and limitations
The sphere has normalized probability measure, \(n\ge2\), and no odd or higher degrees occur. The general sharp coefficient is characterized by a cubic. Full bibliographic priority is not certified.
