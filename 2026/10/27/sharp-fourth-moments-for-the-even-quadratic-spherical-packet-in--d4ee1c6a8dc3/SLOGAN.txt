The exact fourth-moment norm of \(\mathcal H_0\oplus\mathcal H_2\) on every sphere is given by a unique positive cubic root, with all complex maximizers equal to scalar multiples of a single zonal quadratic plus its optimal nonzero constant. For the circle the sharp fourth-moment quotient is \(15/7\), and on \(S^3\) it is \(21/5\).
