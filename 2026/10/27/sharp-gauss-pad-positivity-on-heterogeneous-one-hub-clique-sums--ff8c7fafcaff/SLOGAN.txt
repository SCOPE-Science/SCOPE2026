For the two-stage fourth-order Gauss–Legendre diffusion step on every unweighted heterogeneous one-hub clique sum, positivity holds exactly until the minimum positive zero of explicit petal-dependent quartics; it holds for every timestep exactly when every petal is a singleton (the graph is a star). Adding just one edge to a star can introduce a finite positivity horizon even though Euclidean spectral stability remains unconditional.
