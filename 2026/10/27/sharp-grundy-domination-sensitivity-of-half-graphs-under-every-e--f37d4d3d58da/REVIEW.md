# Sharp Grundy-domination sensitivity of half graphs under every edge deletion

Correctness: PASS. For strict half graphs with n at least three, the universal one-edge upper bound is matched by four explicit classes of legal dominating sequences for every nonpendant edge; pendant edges are treated with the previously established exact chain-graph algorithm. The 4,900 explicit-sequence tests and 119 independently computed edge-deleted maxima corroborate the all-order proof and verify boundaries.

Originality: PASS, with bibliographic uncertainty. The closest primary literature is Brešar–Pandey–Sharma (arXiv:2310.10566v1) on unmodified chain graphs and Brešar–Gologranc–Kos (arXiv:1603.05116v1) on an arbitrary-graph change bound. Neither implies the complete edge-by-edge equality pattern. Published mathematical literature and result-record comparisons did not identify subsumption. An unindexed equivalent remains possible.

Value: PASS. Full edge sensitivity is naturally motivated by determining equality in the sharp atomic-change bound. The result provides a two-parameter structural classification over all orders and edges, not just a finite table or parameter substitution.

Scope: Strict half graphs and exactly one deletion, \(n\ge3\); the \(n=2\) exception is stated. Independent verification of originality and mathematics has not been performed.
