# Scientific review

## Correctness
The one-cycle Laplace-transform renewal equation is obtained by conditioning on the first target hit or exponential switch. On a negative switch position, Brownian return has a square-root Laplace defect; on a positive switch position, both exit boundaries are bounded and yield analytic order-\(s\) corrections. This gives the coefficient \(rI/p\), which the monotone Tauberian theorem converts into the claimed full-completion tail. The killed-OU discounted Dynkin identity independently gives the alternative coefficient expression. The positive-stiffness hitting-time backward equation and bounded-convergence argument yield the matching weak-confinement mean coefficient. Numerical checks are supportive and not substituted for limits.

## Originality
The closest source is Dubey–Pal (arXiv:2610.18814v1), which establishes the model and positive-stiffness mean formula. Biswas–Kundu–Pal (arXiv:2307.16294v3) supplies broad renewal methods, and Singh (arXiv:2407.02071v1) assumes no target detection on return. The precise harmonic-search total-time free-return survival amplitude, all fractional moments, and cross-limit amplitude matching are not stated in inspected texts or identified semantic results. This is best-knowledge originality rather than a proof of priority; a general uninspected result could imply portions.

## Value
Vanishing confinement on a realistic noninstantaneous return channel is a structural boundary at which **any** positive switch rate destroys the finite mean of the otherwise positive-recurrent OU search. The sharp tail and equal-amplitude singular regularization quantify experimentally relevant rare-duration risk that the prior average-time phase diagrams do not capture. The negative-start occupation functional is an interpretable model-dependent amplitude, rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limitations
Fixed \(k>0,r>0\), target at \(1\), unit diffusion, Gaussian return and one-dimensional state. Brownian hitting-time asymptotics, OU resolvents and renewal machinery individually are classical. Literature-wide novelty and independent validation are not established.
