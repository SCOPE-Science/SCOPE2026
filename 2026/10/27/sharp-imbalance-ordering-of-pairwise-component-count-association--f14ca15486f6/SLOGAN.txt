In \(G(n,p)\), pairwise component-size factorial association changes sign at an exact threshold that strictly rises as the two sizes become less balanced, for fixed total size. At every order \(n\ge5\), there are probabilities where counts of components of sizes \(2\) and \(3\) have positive covariance while counts of sizes \(1\) and \(4\) have negative covariance.
