## Originality — PASS, with explicit priority risk
Filliger–Reimann (2007) already gives the general **forward mean torque**, Dotsenko and collaborators (2013) the Gaussian circulating steady state, and Mancois and collaborators (2018), Eq. (30), the **general harmonic forward mean angular frequency**. The last paper's parametrization encompasses all SPD two-by-two stiffnesses. The present claim is **not** the forward formulas: it is the complete range, exact global inverse-fibre classification, fold singularity and global disambiguation using one stationary variance difference. Direct implication comparisons and semantic searches did not locate an equivalent inverse theorem; this cannot prove historical uniqueness. Because the ingredients are classical algebraic identities, the principal remaining risk is coverage by an implicit or unindexed prior inverse treatment.

## Mathematical value — PASS
An exact image and fibre classification has operational meaning for inverse reconstruction: generically two physically distinct confining traps generate the same rotation and torque, along a sharp boundary they merge, and adding only one coordinate-variance difference restores global identifiability. The explicit square-root fold makes the onset of ill-conditioning mathematically precise. This addresses a motivated full-domain inverse problem, not an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Scope and limitations
Known unequal temperatures, known positive trace and mobility, steady Gaussian linear dynamics, and exact ensemble observations are essential. No claim is made for underdamped or nonlinear systems, finite-sample uncertainty, or priority over unseen literature. The observer must use the specified potential-torque sign convention and current integral.
