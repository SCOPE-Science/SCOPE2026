The \(Q^2\) Gauss–Lobatto Poisson discretization on a symmetric \(3\times3\) graded square has an exact inverse-positivity cutoff: with outer-center-outer element lengths \((1,t,1)\) in both axes, the discrete maximum principle holds precisely for \(0<t\le4.1999378918007026\ldots\). The cutoff is a uniquely defined degree-thirteen algebraic number; above it an explicit discrete Green-matrix entry becomes negative.
