For every nonzero single parity check over \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\ge n\), the exact worst-case one-deletion list size is \(1+(q-1)z+g\), where \(z\) counts zero check coefficients and \(g\) counts adjacent nonzero merged-check vectors escaping the span of punctured zero-check vectors. This rank-based invariant distinguishes codes with identical check supports but different exact deletion ambiguity.
