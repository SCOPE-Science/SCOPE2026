For a uniform composition, the covariance between its number of parts and its largest part is asymptotic to \(-\frac12(\log_2(N-1))h(\{\log_2(N-1)\})\), with an explicit nonconstant log-periodic function. The correlation decays at the sharp order \(-(\log N)/\sqrt N\), ruling out the previously conjectured slower power of \(\ln N\).
