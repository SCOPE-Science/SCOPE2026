# Review of the logarithmic parameter covering theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The proof checks domain \(0<u<v<r\), the source rotation staircase, uniform series convergence, the strictly positive denominator, the exact plateau jump, and endpoint membership. Rational plateaux give a uniform exponential separation bound; Möbius inversion gives quadratic growth of reduced fractions. A finite symbolic truncation has at most quadratic many cells and exponentially small tail, proving the opposite covering inequality. Universal conclusions are analytic, with 7,139 exact rational/symbolic checks as supporting reproducibility. The proof does not infer an infinite result from finite enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, with literature-priority risk
Janson and Öberg (2017), Theorem 7.1, gives gauge-Hausdorff bounds and an upper-component argument for fixed common-slope contracted rotations. Laurent and Nogueira (2019) and Gaivão, Laurent and Nogueira (2022), Theorem 5.2, give inverse rotation formulae and rational plateau intervals. Gaivão (2022), Theorem A, covers zero dimension for common-slope contractions. None of these inspected theorems asserts the matching quadratic logarithmic **uniform** covering law across all interior two-slope windows, particularly when the second slope exceeds one. The proof adds the uniform plateau-gap packing needed for the lower bound and a convergence argument under average contraction. A covering result in unindexed literature could affect historical priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem measures the metric resolution needed to distinguish genuinely aperiodic dynamics where both box and Hausdorff dimensions are zero. Its exact logarithmic exponent provides information strictly finer than a dimension-zero corollary, matches rational-tongue growth to truncated symbolic complexity, and remains valid in a non-branchwise-contractive parameter regime. This is a natural parameter-geometric invariant; neither a general statistical theorem nor the exact leading constant is claimed.

## Limitations
Only fixed compact rotation windows away from the endpoints support the stated uniform constants. The full irrational-parameter set has zero Hausdorff dimension by countable exhaustion, but no global box-counting statement is claimed near the critical upper endpoint. The comparison with prior literature cannot establish uniqueness against unindexed work. The source results on the staircase, rational jumps and one-slope upper estimates remain credited to their authors.
