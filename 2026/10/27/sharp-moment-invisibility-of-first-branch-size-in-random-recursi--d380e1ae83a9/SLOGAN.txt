In a uniform recursive tree, conditioning on the size \(B_n\) of the branch starting at vertex \(2\) cannot change even the first \(2h\) moments of the total leaf count when both sides have at least \(2h+1\) vertices. The bound is sharp: moving a branch to size \(2h\) changes the \(2h\)-th cumulant by \(-\beta_{2h}\ne0\), while all lower moments remain unchanged.
