# Scientific review: Rule 54 second-image language and entropy

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is fully quantified over bi-infinite binary inputs and their spatial image languages after exactly one or two updates. Local finite-preimage equivalence proves that a de Bruijn subset automaton recognizes every admissible output word. Complete reachable-state closure gives 11 and 39 states including the empty sink; minimization gives 10 and 18 states. Exhaustive enumeration through length ten, independently repeated by direct local input enumeration, establishes the first nine-block discrepancy and its four exact witnesses. The unique nontrivial recurrent components have sizes 8 and 16, with exactly computed characteristic polynomials; rational Collatz–Wielandt inequalities prove strict Perron separation and thus strict entropy reduction. The replay code and complete labeled automata are embedded. No numerical extrapolation is used as an infinite proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Spatial image entropy quantifies which information survives deterministic evolution. The Rule 54 glider automaton is a canonical nonlinear elementary example with intricate space–time structures. The theorem identifies an eight-symbol finite-observation blind spot: local block statistics detect no change before the ninth symbol, yet the global exponential language growth drops immediately under the second update. The exact algebraic entropy decrease and four sharp witnesses are natural invariants of the genuine image filtration, rather than an arbitrary parameter slice or a recomputation of one-step statistics.
