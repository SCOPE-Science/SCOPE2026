# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
Trace-free spectral reduction covers all nonzero real degree-two spherical harmonics. For generic oriented great circles, the intersection count is four precisely when the compressed quadratic form is indefinite. The normalized Crofton constant is \(L=\pi\mathbb EN\), giving the exact integrated length profile. Pairing perpendicular angles and simplifying the two exact polynomial deficits proves the universal sharp lower bound and the unique uniaxial equality class; the upper equality class is obtained separately. Two exact symbolic identities and eight independent numerical arclength comparisons were reproduced, but are only supporting checks.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge)
Gichev's paper is the decisive earlier full-text comparison: its general sharp upper bound covers the upper inequality, which is not claimed as new; its discussion explicitly identifies zonal nodal length as a possible sharp lower bound and does not establish the degree-two sharp lower classification. Todino and Marinucci–Rossi–Wigman concern random high-frequency nodal lengths, not this deterministic fixed-degree minimum. Relevant searchable mathematical findings address degree-two fourth moments rather than nodal length. Alias/equivalence, stronger-bound and exact-table searches did not locate an implication establishing the result. An unindexed earlier equivalent treatment of quadratic nodal curves remains a residual priority risk; novelty is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result settles the first nontrivial degree of the natural zonal lower-envelope question for spherical harmonic nodal length, with an exact universal constant and both endpoint equality classes. It is structural geometry of a complete rotational representation, not a cherry-picked matrix or computational example. It does not solve the all-degree conjecture or give a higher-dimensional version.
