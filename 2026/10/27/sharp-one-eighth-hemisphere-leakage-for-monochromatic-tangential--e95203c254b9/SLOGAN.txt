For a tangential vector field supported on one exact spherical-harmonic degree, at least \(1/8\) of its energy lies outside any prescribed hemisphere; the constant is sharp only in degree \(1\). For degree \(\ell\), the maximizing polarizations have azimuthal order \(\lvert m\rvert=\ell\) and concentration \(1/2+(2\ell+1)\binom{2\ell}{\ell}/[2(\ell+1)4^\ell]\), strictly decreasing to \(1/2\).
