For continuous piecewise-affine \(L^2\) projection with a single interior knot at \(a\), the exact \(L^\infty\) amplification is \(1+4/[3(1+\min\{a,1-a\})]\): the balanced knot minimizes it at \(17/9\), while knots approaching the boundary give the unattained supremum \(7/3\). This boundary instability survives even as the projectors converge strongly on \(L^2\) to the ordinary affine projector of sharp norm \(5/3\).
