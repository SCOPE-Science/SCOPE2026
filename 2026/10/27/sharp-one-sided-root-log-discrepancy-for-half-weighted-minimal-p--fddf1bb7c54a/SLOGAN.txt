For the minimal-power divisor function \(\tau(M_r(n))/2^{\omega(n)}\), the mean-value error has a fixed negative sign and the sharp size \(X^{1/(r+1)}/\sqrt{\log X}\) for every integer \(r\ge2\). An exact nonnegative Dirichlet convolution exposes a hidden half-order zeta singularity and supplies explicit positive bounds on the normalized discrepancy.
