Periodic nodal quadratic semi-Lagrangian interpolation has the exact one-step Euclidean norm \(\sqrt{1-2u+2u^2+2u\sqrt{1+(1-u)^2}}\), with \(u=\{s\}(1-\{s\})\), independent of the mesh size. Its sharp global value is \(\sqrt5/2\), attained exactly at half-integer Courant displacement by strictly positive data—even though the method is power stable.
