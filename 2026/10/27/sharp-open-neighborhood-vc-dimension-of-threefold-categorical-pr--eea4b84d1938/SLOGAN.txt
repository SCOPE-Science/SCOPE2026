For a categorical product of three complete graphs whose factor sizes, sorted, are \(a\le b\le c\) with \(a\ge3\), the open-neighborhood VC dimension is exactly \(3\) when \(b=3\), exactly \(5\) when \(a\ge4\) and \(c\ge5\), and exactly \(4\) otherwise. A reduction to unions of blocks of three set partitions gives a finite exhaustive six-point obstruction and sharp witnesses at factor sizes \((3,3,3)\), \((3,4,4)\), and \((4,4,5)\).
