On a two-terminal graph with one path of length \(a\) and \(r\) paths of length \(b\), the exact least \(\ell_1\)-norm of a projection onto the cycle space changes formula at \((r-2)b=ra\), while the Euclidean orthogonal projector has a different explicit norm. For one path of length \(2\) and \(r\) paths of length \(2r\), the orthogonal-to-optimal norm ratio is exactly \(r/2\), so its inefficiency is unbounded on simple series-parallel graphs.
