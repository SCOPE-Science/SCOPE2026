For a symmetric positive four-atom measure supported on \(\{\pm n,\pm(n-2)\}\), every Fourier-transform zero is real exactly when the outer-pair mass \(p\) exceeds \(1/2\) for even \(n\), or \((n-2)/(2(n-1))\) for odd \(n\). Below this sharp parity threshold, exactly four nonreal zeros occur per \(2\pi\)-strip on two vertical lines, collapsing to double zeros in even degree and triple zeros in odd degree at the boundary.
