# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. With strictly positive physical denominators, the exact identity \(D_b(t)-rb=rP(t)(h(t)-b)/[(t+r)(t+ar)(\eta t+2)]\) reduces the global phase diagram to a unique-sign polynomial derivative. The derivative has either no positive root or exactly one positive root. Compactness of maximizing run times below threshold and the reciprocal-time stationary expansion establish the left limits and both divergence exponents. A symbolic executable independently evaluates the central identities and the critical coexistence point. These exact algebraic checks are supplementary to, not substitutes for, the stated all-parameter proof.

Related crowded-medium RTP work does not imply this model-specific algebraic classification. Search-based originality remains qualified by the possibility of unindexed prior work, and no claim of exhaustive novelty certification is made.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The source's optimizing quartic leaves the transition threshold and discontinuous coexistence point untreated. Reducing comparison to the infinite-run plateau solves that physically motivated exact optimization problem without quartic roots. The borderline exponent \(1/2\) separates a conventional continuous loss of finite optimal time from finite-time coexistence, and the exact jump threshold quantitatively explains the source's second figure. The result is restricted to the idealized four-direction coefficient; it is not a new general porous-media transport law.
