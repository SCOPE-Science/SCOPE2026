For the exact slit-confined four-direction run-and-tumble diffusivity, the sign of \(\delta=\alpha\eta r-2(1-\alpha)\) completely decides whether optimal run time diverges continuously or jumps abruptly to infinity as wall speed increases. The boundary \(\delta=0\) exhibits a previously untreated \((b_c-b)^{-1/2}\) divergence, whereas the jump regime has an explicit finite coexistence run time and critical wall speed.
