The directed two-community logistic threshold model has a sharp, stimulus-optimized polarization cap \(d\), determined by \(4\operatorname{artanh}(d)/d=\beta(1-a-b)\), and heterogeneous equilibria are impossible below this threshold. The two maximizing stimuli determine the directional mixing asymmetry exactly; at the critical boundary, all community contrasts decay at most like \(\sqrt{3/(2t)}\), with equality achievable.
