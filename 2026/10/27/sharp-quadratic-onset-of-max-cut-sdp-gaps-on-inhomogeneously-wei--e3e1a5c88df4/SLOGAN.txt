On an odd cycle with arbitrary positive path weights, the Max-Cut SDP integrality gap opens *quadratically* when the closing edge crosses the harmonic-weight threshold. Its exact curvature is \(3S^4/[4(S^3-T)]\), where \(S\) and \(T\) are the first and third reciprocal-weight sums; heterogeneity strictly increases this curvature at fixed threshold.
