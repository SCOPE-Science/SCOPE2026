For Rhaly matrices with two-sided coefficient decay \(|\eta_n|\asymp(n+1)^{-\alpha}\), \(\alpha>1\), the sharp Schatten threshold is \(p>1/\alpha\), even for arbitrary coefficient phases and quasi-norm exponents below one. Consequently every nontrivial finite shift-covariance defect of the classical Cesàro operator is in \(\mathcal S_p\) exactly when \(p>1/2\), sharpening its classical Hilbert–Schmidt classification.
