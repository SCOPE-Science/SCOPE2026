For the vertices of the Euclidean unit square and cube, simultaneous real-linear Lipschitz extension to every edge midpoint has exact minimum norms \(\Lambda_2=(2+\sqrt2)/(1+\sqrt5)\) and \(\Lambda_3=1+4\sqrt2(1+\sqrt2-\sqrt5)/(4\sqrt{10}+2(1+\sqrt2-\sqrt3))\). Positive symmetry-equivariant averaging kernels attain both values and strictly improve upon arithmetic midpoint interpolation.
