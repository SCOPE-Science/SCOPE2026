For the BDFL2 method with alternating reciprocal step ratios, the exact zero-stability window is \(t_*\le t\le t_*^{-1}\), where \(t_*\) is determined by \(4(t_*+t_*^{-1})^2-10(t_*+t_*^{-1})-49=0\). In the strict subinterval \(t_*<t<1/4\), periodic switching stabilizes the method even though one frozen companion is unstable.
