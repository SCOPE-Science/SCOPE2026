For Brownian motion resetting between unequal partially absorbing point targets at \(-a\) and \(2a\), the farther target can lose, win, and lose the ultimate-capture majority as resetting increases if and only if its dimensionless reactivity \(B\) exceeds \(1\), for a suitable nearer-target reactivity \(A\). The exact open window is \(B/(1+B)<A<\max_{t\ge0} 2Bt e^{-t}/[2t+B(1-e^{-t})(1+e^{-3t})]\); unlike the known two-defect flux formulas, this is a sharp reentrance classification.
