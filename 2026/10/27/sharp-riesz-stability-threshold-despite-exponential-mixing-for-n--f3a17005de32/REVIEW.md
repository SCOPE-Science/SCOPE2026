# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The Haar pushforward and first-Taylor-coefficient identities yield the entire non-autonomous Gram matrix. The finite weighted bidiagonal innovation transform exactly diagonalizes it; its inverse gives both spectral bounds, and two-term almost-collisions give the converse when the derivative supremum is one. Constant and alternating trial vectors prove sharpness on a single degree-two Blaschke family. The sparse-spike example satisfies every hypothesis of Theorems A and C(a) of arXiv:2609.31476v1; a power-of-two two-by-two principal minor proves exponential ill-conditioning. The analytic proof, not the sample computations, proves the unbounded statements.

## Originality
PASS against the inspected evidence, not an assertion of exhaustive historical priority. Nicolau's 2021 autonomous norm bounds and the underlying first-coefficient correlation mechanism are established prior work. Evdoridou–Fagella–Rippon–Stallard's 2026 paper gives non-autonomous mixing and entropy, not the if-and-only-if Riesz criterion. Its Theorem A cannot yield stable Riesz bounds, as the constructed exponentially mixing system demonstrates. The new claim concerns a uniform condition across arbitrary time-dependent derivatives and the explicit high-entropy/mixing separation, not an autonomous specialization. A close indexed 2026 finite-tail calculation treats summable derivative deficits and zero entropy, so neither its assumption nor its claimed conclusion covers the sparse-spike example. A general older theorem on weighted autoregressive covariance or non-autonomous Hardy compositions could subsume some formulas; this remains the principal literature-priority risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Uniform Riesz bounds govern stability of reconstruction and linear synthesis from a trajectory's harmonic observables. The result provides a natural necessary-and-sufficient criterion, exact optimal constants and finite precision matrices for a whole holomorphic dynamical class. The counterexample separates spectral linear conditioning from two strong dynamical mixing/complexity properties, showing that positive entropy plus fast mixing offers no uniform Gram conditioning control. This is a structural boundary, not an arbitrary one-parameter numerical refinement.
