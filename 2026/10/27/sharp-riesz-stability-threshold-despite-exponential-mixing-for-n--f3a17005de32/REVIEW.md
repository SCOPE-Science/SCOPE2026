# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Positive Fourier moments of the boundary law of a centered inner map vanish, so it preserves Haar measure. Composition on boundary L² agrees with analytic composition for H² functions by first using polynomials and then the Haar-preserving isometry. This reduces the (i,j) Gram entry for i>j to the first Taylor coefficient of the centered tail composition, the product of its derivatives. Subtracting c_nF_{n−1} from F_n annihilates all earlier correlations and leaves squared norm 1−|c_n|²; the triangular change of basis yields both inverse and determinant. If q<1, the row-sum bound gives ||C||≤(1+q)/(1−q); ||L||≤1+q and D≥(1−q²)I give the same bound on ||C⁻¹||. If q=1, normalized two-term innovations tend to zero in norm. The constant-derivative Toeplitz example with constant and alternating vectors reaches both bounds in the limit by summing geometric series; q=0 is orthonormal. In the spike example the product is ≤2^(−N/2), the average deficit tends to 1/2, and all derivatives are ≥1/2. These satisfy the cited Theorems A and C(a), giving the exponential arc-versus-measurable-set rate and positive lower entropy. The paper calls the liminf quantity lower entropy; the example also satisfies C(b) if metric entropy denotes the separate limsup quantity. The last 2×2 block's smaller eigenvalue 2^(−N) at the spikes and the diagonal Rayleigh quotient 1 prove the condition-number claim by interlacing.

## originality

PASS

The derivative-product covariance itself has exact prior coverage (Ferreira–Nicolau, Lemma 3.2 with exponent 1). Its precision factorization is the usual first-order innovation algebra, and autonomous estimates are established background. Credit is for the sharp non-autonomous orbit-stability classification as an application of these facts, in particular the explicit separation with exponentially bad conditioning along spikes despite exponential mixing and positive lower entropy. None of the strongest inspected sources supplies that separation or the joint statement. No priority is claimed for the covariance identity, the matrix machinery, or the external dynamical estimates.

## value

PASS

The example gives a precise obstruction to using strong dynamical statistics as proxies for conditioning of the basic orbit observables. At a sparse near-rotation consecutive observables almost coincide, although the cumulative derivative product and averaged deficit have the required decay and positivity. Quantifying the resulting ill-conditioning and identifying the exact uniform threshold are useful beyond the familiar factorization itself.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
