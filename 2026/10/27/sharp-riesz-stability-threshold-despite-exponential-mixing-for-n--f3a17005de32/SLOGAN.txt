Centered inner-function orbit observables are uniformly Riesz-stable exactly when their one-step derivatives stay uniformly below unit modulus, with sharp bounds \((1-q)/(1+q)\) and \((1+q)/(1-q)\). A sparse degree-two Blaschke construction is exponentially mixing and has positive lower entropy, yet its finite orbit Gram condition numbers grow at least as \(2^N\) along power-of-two horizons.
