For binary codes decoded from just two surviving ordered symbols, the sharp universal list-capacity ceiling is \(\min\{2L,\lfloor(4L+8)/3\rfloor\}\). It is attained at every source length at least three when \(L\le4\); for \(L\ge5\) it is attained precisely when the source length reaches \(\max\{4,\lceil L/3\rceil+1\}\).
