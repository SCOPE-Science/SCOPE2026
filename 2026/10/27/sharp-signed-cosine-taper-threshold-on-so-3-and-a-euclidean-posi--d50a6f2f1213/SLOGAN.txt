The quadratic geodesic taper on \(SO(3)\), multiplied by \(1+b\cos\theta\), is positive definite exactly for \(b\ge-8/23\); at equality precisely the degree-two, 25-dimensional Peter--Weyl sector disappears, yet finite distinct-site interpolation remains strictly positive definite. The boundary kernel is not a Euclidean radial covariance: its three-dimensional Fourier transform equals \(-361/3726\) at radial frequency \(3\).
