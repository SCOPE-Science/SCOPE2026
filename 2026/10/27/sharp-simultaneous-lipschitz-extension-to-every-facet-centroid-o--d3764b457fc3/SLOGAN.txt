The exact least norm of a real-linear Lipschitz extension from a regular Euclidean \(d\)-cross-polytope's vertices to all its facet centroids is \(\sqrt{d/2}\,[1+\sqrt2(d-1)]/[1+d\sqrt{(d-1)/2}]\) for every \(d\ge3\). A positive symmetry-equivariant optimizer assigns nonzero mass to every original vertex, strictly improving on facet averaging; the sharp constants approach \(\sqrt2\).
