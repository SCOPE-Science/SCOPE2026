The optimal real-linear Lipschitz extension from a regular simplex's vertices to all \(k\)-vertex face barycenters has an explicit positive, symmetry-equivariant minimizer for every \(k\ge3\). Its sharp supremum over simplex sizes and face cardinalities is \(17\sqrt{17}/(1+34\sqrt2)\), approached only as the number of simplex vertices grows while the face cardinality stays \(17\).
