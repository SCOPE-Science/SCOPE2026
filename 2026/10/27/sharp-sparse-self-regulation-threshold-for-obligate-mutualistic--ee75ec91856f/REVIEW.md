# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The principal-block necessity uses the full Jacobian, not a symmetric-subspace shortcut. The construction solves exact equilibrium equations and checks the remaining full-space transverse eigenvalues. Nondegenerate fold and positivity boundaries are explicit. The rational arithmetic checker covers 964 small configurations; the analytic argument covers all admissible integer populations and positive real coefficients.

## Originality — PASS

The closest direct literature is Çelik et al. (arXiv:2609.09286v1, 2026): pairwise obligate-mutualism impossibility and rescue by unrestricted signed higher-order interactions. Those do not entail a sharp count for negative self-only nonlinearities. Hale and Valdovinos (2021, doi:10.1002/ece3.8453) summarize known two-species Allee thresholds; stable/unstable branch pairs are not novel alone. A broader earlier sparse-control equivalence remains an acknowledged risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The minimum number of regulated species is a motivated resource-allocation boundary for stabilizing a strongly mutualistic community under a sharply delimited intervention. The explicit critical regulation strength and full-state stability classification make this more than a generic self-limiting example. Applicability is restricted to symmetric complete mutualism, and no global stability is asserted.
