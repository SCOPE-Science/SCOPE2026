In a strongly mutualistic community with identical pairwise benefits and losses, adding negative cubic self-regulation to exactly \(m\) of \(n\) species can create stable positive coexistence precisely when \(m\ge n-\lceil d/b\rceil\). This sharp sparse-intervention threshold comes with an explicit saddle-node cost ceiling and a stable upper branch, despite the baseline pairwise ecology admitting no stable positive coexistence.
