The proof of the Mellin sign-variation lemma accounts for repeated roots and uses the extended Chebyshev property of exponential polynomials. A negative large-parameter tail limits all threshold superlevel components, and separated gamma-scaled annuli attain the bound for every weight and threshold. The independent finite checker passed 55 formula and example tests; the universal statements are proved analytically.

Classical total positivity itself is not claimed original. The conclusion relies on inspected theorem statements and searches; full older coverage cannot be excluded.

The method is related to familiar variation-diminishing machinery; value is the sharp domain-specific spectral result, not a new generic zero-count theorem.
