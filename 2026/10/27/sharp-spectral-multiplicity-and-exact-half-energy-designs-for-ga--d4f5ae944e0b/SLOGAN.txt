An indicator mask comprising \(m\) concentric Gaussian phase-space annuli can never have a nonzero Hermite localization eigenvalue of multiplicity greater than \(2m\). This limit is attained at the **top eigenvalue \(1/2\)** by a unique \(m\)-annulus design whose first \(2m\) eigenvalues coincide and whose remaining eigenvalues strictly decrease; its radii are the zeros of a weighted \(L^1\)-extremal monic polynomial.
