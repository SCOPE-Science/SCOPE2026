For a symmetric matrix and a two-dimensional trial space, the sketched Rayleigh–Ritz eigenvalues are guaranteed real whenever the squared-norm embedding distortion obeys \(\varepsilon\le\Delta/\sqrt{\Delta^2+4r^2}\), where \(\Delta\) is the unsketched Ritz gap and \(r\) the residual norm. The threshold and exact imaginary-part envelope are jointly sharp, with a positive-definite four-dimensional witness that becomes defective at equality and acquires nonreal values above it.
