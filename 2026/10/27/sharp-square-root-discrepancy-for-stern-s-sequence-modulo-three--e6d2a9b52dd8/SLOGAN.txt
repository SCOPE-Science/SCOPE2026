For Stern’s diatomic sequence, the number of indices below \(N\) where the term is divisible by \(3\) differs from \(N/4\) by \(O(\sqrt N)\), and the square-root exponent is sharp. Its exact dyadic discrepancy satisfies \(d_{k+2}=-d_{k+1}-2d_k\), with the characteristic roots having modulus \(\sqrt2\).
