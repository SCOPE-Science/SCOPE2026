For cyclic two-component aggregated-gradient descent on commuting positive quadratic losses, the exact stability ceiling is \(\alpha<2/\max\{\lambda_{\max}(A),\lambda_{\max}(B)\}\). In the scalar case, a unique critical-damping step \(\alpha_*=2/(a+b+\sqrt{ab}+\sqrt{(a+b+\sqrt{ab})^2-ab})\) minimizes the asymptotic two-step rate, but its repeated eigenvalue carries a polynomial transient.
