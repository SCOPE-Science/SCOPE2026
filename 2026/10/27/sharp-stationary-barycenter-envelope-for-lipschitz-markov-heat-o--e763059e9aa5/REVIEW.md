# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The ordered-kernel norm identity follows from one-dimensional transport duality and simultaneous attainment by the coordinate potential. The equilibrium ceiling is a finite rearrangement inequality with the exact mass constraint, and each cut is realized by a stationary two-block conditional expectation. Nearest-neighbor heat flow preserves stochastic order by a Metzler edge-difference generator. Strict heat nonattainment follows from strictly positive transition entries, and Duhamel comparison with a disconnected two-block chain proves singular sharpness. The proof includes arbitrary positive gaps and stationary masses. The packaged exact and numerical checks passed, but finite tests are not used in place of the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with a documented risk

The claim is a sharp optimization over reversible path conductances at fixed invariant law for all path lengths and all positive metric gaps, plus an encompassing sharp theorem for stationary stochastic-monotone kernels. The known three-state unit-edge result is included as a specialization and not claimed independently. Ollivier (arXiv:math/0701886), Veysseire (arXiv:1202.0420), and Huillet (doi:10.1155/2013/939308) give curvature, contraction and monotone-chain background, not this fixed-equilibrium cut-barycenter optimization. The closely related Pflug--Schachermayer (1992) article concerns metric-sensitive ergodicity coefficients for a fixed chain; its full text was inaccessible. Equivalence in unindexed sources remains a risk; no search alone establishes novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result identifies a complete natural invariant of stationary mass placement and edge metric for a whole class of semigroups, regardless of individual conductances. It gives an exact extremal mechanism (fast equilibration on the two sides of a slow cut), simultaneous norming of all finite-time semigroups by one potential, a sharp unbounded \(n/2\) uniform-path law, and a counterexample showing why order preservation cannot be removed. This is a structural classification, not merely a numerical example or a routine restatement of a single-chain estimate.

## Scope and uncertainty

The result is finite-state and real-valued, assumes all equilibrium masses and metric gaps positive, and makes no claim about infinite paths, general graphs or exact optimizing positive conductances at fixed time. The 1992 article remains a specifically identified literature uncertainty. The novelty and correctness judgements here are not independent external validation.
