For every finite weighted path and every prescribed positive stationary law, the largest Lipschitz amplification of a reversible birth--death heat semigroup, optimized over conductances and time, equals the maximum distance between the two conditional stationary barycenters across a single cut, divided by that cut's metric edge length. The same constant is sharp over all stationary order-preserving Markov kernels; in the uniform unit-edge case it is exactly \(n/2\), approached by making one edge slow and each side rapidly equilibrating.
