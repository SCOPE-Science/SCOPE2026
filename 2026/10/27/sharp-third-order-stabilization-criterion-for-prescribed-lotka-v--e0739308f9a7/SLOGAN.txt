For an arbitrary fixed pairwise Lotka–Volterra ecology and prescribed positive population vector, unrestricted three-body terms can make that vector linearly stable **exactly unless** the quantities \(2r_i+\sum_j S_{ij}z_j\) all coincide at a nonpositive value. Whenever these quantities differ, an explicit square-partner construction makes every Jacobian eigenvalue equal to \(-1\); the exceptional class genuinely needs fourth-order interactions for Hurwitz stabilization.
