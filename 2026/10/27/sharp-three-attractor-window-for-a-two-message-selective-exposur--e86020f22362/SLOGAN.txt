In a two-message selective-exposure model, unbiased incoming messages can support three distinct attracting long-run opinions when \(2<\beta<5/2\), even with no intrinsic stubbornness. The five-equilibrium window has exact thresholds at \(\beta=2\) and \(\beta=5/2\), a square-root separator collision, and persists under sufficiently small message-frequency imbalance.
