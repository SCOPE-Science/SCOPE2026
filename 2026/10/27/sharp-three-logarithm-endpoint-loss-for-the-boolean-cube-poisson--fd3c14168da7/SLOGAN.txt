The Moore–Penrose inverse of the unnormalized Boolean-cube Laplacian has an exact radial, strictly sign-ordered Green kernel and sharp endpoint norm \(\frac12\log d-\frac12\log\log\log d+O(1)\). The negative three-logarithm correction comes from requiring one Hamming-ball sign extremizer for the entire time-integrated heat kernel, rather than optimizing each time separately.
