For one-step sketched GMRES on any real SPD system of condition number \(\kappa\), a squared-norm \(\varepsilon\)-embedding gives the *sharp* worst true-residual factor \((\kappa-1)/[(\kappa+1)\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2}]\). Uniform geometric decrease is possible exactly below \(\varepsilon=2\sqrt\kappa/(\kappa+1)\); on the boundary a valid fixed sketch can force perpetual stagnation.
