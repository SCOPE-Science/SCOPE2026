With exactly two unit-sum alternatives, the sharp worst-case deterministic welfare distortion of common \(t\)-threshold reports is \((1+w)/(1-w)\), where \(w\) is the widest utility interval induced by thresholds and their reflections. Equally spaced reflected cuts attain the optimal distortion \(1+1/t\), and every optimal threshold set consists of exactly one choice from each of \(t\) reflection pairs.
