For centered maximal averaging on a star with at least two leaves, the sharp two-data weighted-variation Lipschitz factor is \(1+\frac{W-w_*}{(m+1)w_*}\), where \(W\) is the total edge-variation weight and \(w_*\) its minimum. Equal weights uniquely minimize this instability at fixed total weight, while one vanishingly cheap edge makes the amplification unbounded.
