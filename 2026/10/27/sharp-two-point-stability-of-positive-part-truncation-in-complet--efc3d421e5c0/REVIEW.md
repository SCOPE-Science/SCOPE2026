# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The analytic proof is complete for all real vectors, every finite \(n\ge2\), and \(\gamma>0\). Coordinatewise truncation is piecewise linear; maximization of the induced mask norm reduces to a strictly convex inactive-coordinate minimization and two eigenspaces, yielding the sharp factor and actual extremizing pairs. The one-point contraction and two scalings follow by direct inequalities and substitution. A standalone checker passed 2,552 independent numerical/eigenvalue and rational consistency comparisons, which supplement rather than replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Primary full-text comparisons distinguish the result from Cai and Wang's one-point graph Dirichlet-energy contraction, van Gennip and collaborators' graph threshold dynamics, and Musina and Nazarov's continuum fractional Sobolev truncation theory. Closest indexed projection results concern different spaces, mappings, or norms. No inspected result implies the exact global all-coupling formula or balanced-mask attainment. An older unindexed formula for coordinate projections in this quadratic geometry cannot be ruled out; originality has not been independently confirmed.

## Value — PASS
The result quantifies a concrete nonlinear conditioning gap between decrease of each signal's graph energy and amplification of perturbations under the same activation. It identifies the unique balanced-support extremizer class and exhibits the dimension-dependent transition between fixed edge coupling and scaled coupling. The full-parameter constant is structurally motivated by graph thresholding and finite-graph Sobolev stability.

## Limits
Identical edge weights and positive vertex masses are assumed, with the exact formula restricted to a complete graph. The comprehensive classification of all norm-attaining input pairs and a corresponding theorem for general weighted graphs are not claimed.
