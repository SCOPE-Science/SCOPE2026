For coordinatewise positive-part truncation on a complete graph with unit vertex masses and coupling \(\gamma>0\), the sharp graph-Sobolev Lipschitz factor is \(\bigl(1+\gamma^2\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/(1+n\gamma)\bigr)^{1/2}\), attained in precisely the balanced sign orthants. Thus truncation reduces each vector's graph energy but may amplify differences by order \(\sqrt n\).
