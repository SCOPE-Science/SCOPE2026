For two inviscid fluids of unequal finite depths, the Kelvin–Helmholtz shear threshold first selects a unique nonzero wavenumber precisely when \(g(\rho_{\mathrm l}-\rho_{\mathrm u})/\gamma>3M_1/M_3\), with \(M_j=h_{\mathrm l}^j/\rho_{\mathrm l}+h_{\mathrm u}^j/\rho_{\mathrm u}\). The selected wavenumber obeys a sharp square-root emergence law, and its onset boundary can lie on either side of the familiar capillary–gravity phase-speed transition.
