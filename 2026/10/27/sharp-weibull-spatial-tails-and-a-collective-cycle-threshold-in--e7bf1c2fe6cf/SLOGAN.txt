For Brownian motion alternating between Weibull-length free excursions and finite harmonic recovery episodes, the exact stationary spatial tail is \(\log\Pr(X>x)\sim-K_p x^{2p/(p+1)}\). The confining history changes the leading coefficient precisely for Weibull shape \(p>1\), when rare excursions must cooperate across earlier free intervals; in the Rayleigh case the exact stretched-exponential power is \(4/3\).
