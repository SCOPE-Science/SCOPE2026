On every weighted metric star with at least three arms, the sharp uniform norm of the continuous piecewise-affine least-squares projector is \(7/3-4\mu_*/[3(M+\mu_*)]\), depending only on the smallest weighted arm mass \(\mu_*\) and total mass \(M\). Equal weighted masses uniquely minimize amplification at fixed valence, yet this minimum approaches the unattainable universal sharp envelope \(7/3\) as the number of arms grows.
