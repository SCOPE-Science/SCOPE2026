For a two-agent, one-way quadratic network game with heterogeneous effort costs, the exact price-of-anarchy interval over all nonnegative incentives is \([1/(1-t^4),\,1/(1-t^2)]\), where \(t\) is the normalized spillover strength. Both endpoints diverge as \(t\uparrow1\) although the directed strategic-response matrix has spectral radius zero throughout.
