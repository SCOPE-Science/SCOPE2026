A two-vector shift-and-invert Krylov approximation to positive diagonal exponential decay eventually acquires negative coordinates for every positive shift as soon as three distinct decay rates are active, with an exact first sign-loss time. Yet on \(\operatorname{diag}(1,2,L)\), one fixed shift retains a strictly positive limiting horizon of \(0.0796717425\ldots\) as \(L\to\infty\), while ordinary two-step Lanczos loses positivity at time asymptotic to \(2\log L/L\).
