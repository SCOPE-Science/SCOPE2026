For a uniformly random acyclic orientation of \(K_{2,n}\), \(n\ge2\), all edge-direction covariance and inverse-covariance signs admit a strict three-type classification, with a complete positive covariance spectrum. Between different leaf columns, marginal correlations tend to \(\pm1/9\) while full-order linear partial correlations vanish at rate \(n^{-1}\) with signed constants \(\pm3/4\).
