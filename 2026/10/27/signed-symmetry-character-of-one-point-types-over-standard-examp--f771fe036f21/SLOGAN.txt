For the dimension-extremal standard-example poset \(S_n\), the complete nonalgebraic one-types have an exact signed symmetry character: an index permutation fixes \(3^c+2^{c+1}+2f-2\) types, while its composition with order duality fixes precisely \(3^e\), where \(c,f,e\) count cycles, fixed points, and even cycles. The resulting \(3^n+2^{n+1}+2n-2\) labelled types reduce to \((n+1)(n+6)/2\) classes under index permutations or \(2n+2+\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\) classes when order duality is also allowed.
