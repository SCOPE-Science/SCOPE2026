PASS. The union-of-arrows graph has no walk of the forbidden length, so each candidate semigroup embeds in a complete upward-layer semigroup. Disjoint increasing chains yield the exact multivariate exponential formula. The two-variable decomposition \(F_0+(x+y)F_1+xyF_2\) and factorwise falling-factorial comparison prove coefficientwise dominance; the one-arrow coefficient proves strictness and the equality classification. Direct enumeration and an independently implemented chain recursion corroborate finite cases. The argument does not extrapolate from those cases.

This proof verifies that stronger inequality and exact equality conditions. An implicit historical formulation is possible; a negative search result is not a uniqueness certificate.
