The result determines every-prefix optimality for arbitrary numbers of partner groups and capacities, clarifies exactly when degree-first ranking loses, and gives the minimum census and unique obstruction for robust failure among all simple two-partner incidence networks. Extinction area is an established ecological target, so these structural results solve a motivated optimization gap rather than a freely chosen numerical example.

## Limitations

The general classification concerns only product-incidence binary interaction structures under fixed sequential deletion and one-stage secondary loss. The sharp small-graph census excludes duplicate edges/weighted plants; heterogeneous networks, adaptive rankings and demographic dynamics are outside the theorem. The census is finite, and its reproducibility certificate is not external validation.
