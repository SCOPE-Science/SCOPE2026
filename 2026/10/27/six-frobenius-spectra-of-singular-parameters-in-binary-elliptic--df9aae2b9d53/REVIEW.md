# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The finite algebraic problem is completely specified: all two-dimensional subspaces of H0(P1xP1,O(2,2)) over F2 whose three rational members are geometrically smooth. In characteristic two, first partial derivatives on each of the four affine charts depend on one coordinate each; the finite-extension argument reduces smoothness to tests over F16, including vanishing derivative cases. Independently implemented GF16 projective-grid evaluation found exactly 144 smooth nonzero forms and 804 unordered pencil triples, and reconstructed all 72 rational automorphisms and 13 orbits of sizes 72x10, 36x2, 12x1. For every orbit representative the original package records four-chart lex Gröbner elimination and independent resultant factors: weighted reduced singular-parameter degree spectra are (2,8):72, (3):192, (3,4,5):72, (3,6):108, (4,8):216, (12):144, summing to 804. Independent exact Gröbner recomputation confirmed the degree-3 and mixed (3,4,5) representatives. The invariant counts reduced geometric parameters, not discriminant multiplicity, singular points or Kodaira types; the proof is an exhaustive FINITE classification and does not pretend to hold over arbitrary finite fields.

## originality

PASS

Compared mathematical objects and implications, not merely similar titles: Asgarli–Ghioca 2022 Theorem 1.3 proves existence of finite-field smooth-member pencils using intersections with a discriminant and large-q bounds; it does not count all (2,2) pencils on the split quadric over F2. Geometric Kodaira configurations or existence of rational-smooth pencils do not imply arithmetic Frobenius-parameter weights across literal coefficient pencils. A direct formula in Lang 2000 could still dominate and remains a material risk. Residual priority risks documented, including uninspected plausible original sources; absence of search hits is not assumed to certify novelty.

## value

PASS

The Frobenius closed-point decomposition of singular values in a complete rational genus-one pencil is a natural geometric arithmetic invariant, genuinely different from previously enumerated base-point schemes. A full finite-field census with exact orbit frequencies and several possible geometric counts is a worthwhile sharp classification, despite finite scope.
