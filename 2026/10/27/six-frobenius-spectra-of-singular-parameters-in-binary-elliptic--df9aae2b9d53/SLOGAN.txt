Every anticanonical pencil on the binary split quadric whose three rational genus-one members are smooth has a reduced singular-parameter locus with one of exactly six Frobenius degree spectra: \((3)\), \((2,8)\), \((3,6)\), \((4,8)\), \((3,4,5)\), or \((12)\). Their exact respective counts among the \(804\) pencils are \(192,72,108,216,72,144\); these classify singular fibre locations, not the previously studied geometric base points.
