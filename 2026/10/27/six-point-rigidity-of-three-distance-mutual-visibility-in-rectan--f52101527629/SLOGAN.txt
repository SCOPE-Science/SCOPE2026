In every rectangular grid \(P_m\square P_n\) with \(m,n\ge3\), six vertices are three-distance mutually visible unless the grid is \(3\times3\), where the optimum is five. Every six-point optimizer is exactly a translated \(3\times4\) six-point pattern or its transpose, giving precisely \((m-2)(n-3)+(m-3)(n-2)\) optimizers.
