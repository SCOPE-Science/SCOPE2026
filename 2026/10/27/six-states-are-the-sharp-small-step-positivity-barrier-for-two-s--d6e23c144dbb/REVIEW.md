This proves generator-dependent small-step positivity universally without graph enumeration. ## Value — PASS

The minimal conservative state count answers a natural methodological question: in what smallest finite-state CTMC can reducing the timestep create negative transition probabilities in an implicit, A-stable, third-order method? The construction is irreducible with unit rates, avoiding artificial scale extremes and absorbing-state artifacts. The complete exact six-cycle interval, including eventual recovery and the exact critical root, is more informative than a single local counterexample. The positivity mechanism is a rigorous, reproducible benchmark contrasting the earlier fourth-order eight-state example and the markedly different undirected-graph cycle thresholds. The precise sixth-state and step-boundary facts can guide Markov-structure-preserving integrator tests, without claiming practical superiority or nonlinear guarantees. Finite matrix algebra is used to prove a natural minimal classification, not to enumerate an arbitrary slice.

## Limitations and nearest evidence

The scope is completed linear end-step positivity; intermediate stages and nonlinear positivity are unproved. For arbitrary generators of size at most five, only a local, generator-dependent interval is shown. For directed cycles above six, only small-step failure and eventual positivity are classified, not the entire step set.
