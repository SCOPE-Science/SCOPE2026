Two-stage, third-order Radau IIA has a sharp six-state barrier for arbitrarily-small-step Markov positivity: every generator with at most five states is locally positivity-safe, but a unit-rate irreducible directed six-cycle is not. On that cycle the entire nonnegative-step set is exactly \(\{0\}\cup[\tau,\infty)\), where \(\tau\) is the unique positive zero of \(h^5+15h^4+60h^3+90h^2+36h-12\); positivity returns as the timestep grows.
