A single homophilic rewire from an adopter–nonadopter edge to a nonadopter–nonadopter edge can increase eventual strict-fraction threshold adoption on a connected graph exactly from six vertices onward; no connected graph on at most five vertices permits it. A six-vertex tree doubles its adopters from three to six throughout \(1/3\leq\theta<1/2\), and the effect lifts to connected trees of every larger order.
