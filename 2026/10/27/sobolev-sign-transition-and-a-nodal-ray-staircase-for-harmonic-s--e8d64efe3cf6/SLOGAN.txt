Boundary Fourier Sobolev regularity has an exact sign threshold for the real harmonic disk reproducing kernel: it is strictly positive for every \(\sigma\ge0\), while every \(\sigma<0\) forces zeros arbitrarily close to the boundary diagonal. Away from negative even exponents, the zeros approaching that diagonal form exactly \(2\lceil-\sigma/2\rceil\) local arcs, with explicit limiting angles and first-order angular drift.
