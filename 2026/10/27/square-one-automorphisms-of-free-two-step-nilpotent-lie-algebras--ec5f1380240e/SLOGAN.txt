For the free class-two nilpotent Lie algebra \(V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\), every finite field admits an exact closed-form count of square-one automorphisms, with different semisimple and modular-Jordan formulas in odd and even characteristic. In odd characteristic there are exactly \(d+1\) conjugacy classes, and every square-one automorphism fixes at least \(\lfloor d^2/4\rfloor\) dimensions, sharply.
