On a natural three-agent fractional-delegation cycle with nonnegative cardinal preferences, the unpenalized game has no Nash equilibrium, whereas every positive geometric penalty has one symmetric equilibrium, with retention \(q_\varepsilon\sim\sqrt{3\varepsilon}\) as \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\). The equilibria converge to an entirely void directed cycle, but their penalized voting-power vectors converge to three fully counted votes: the equilibrium and zero-penalty limits do not commute.
