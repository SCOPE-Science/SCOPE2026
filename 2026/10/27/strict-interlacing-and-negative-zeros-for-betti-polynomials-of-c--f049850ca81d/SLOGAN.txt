For every \(r\ge2\) and \(n\ge1\), the even Betti polynomial of the connected cyclic rational-curve compactification \(\overline{\mathcal L}^{r}_{n}(\zeta)\) has \(n\) distinct negative real roots, and for \(n\ge2\) is strictly interlaced by the preceding polynomial. This proves and strengthens the log-concavity conjecture of Clader–Damiolini–Li–Ramadas uniformly in \(r\) and \(n\).
