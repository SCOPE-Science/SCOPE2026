# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
A strict-TP2 calculation for folded beta-binomial weights and a TP2 composition kernel proves all n and all 0<alpha<beta. Explicit adjacent kernel ratios identify exactly the even-to-odd equality columns and the odd-to-even strict minors. The Cauchy–Binet sum has no cancellation. The fixed-count composition identity proves universal exchangeable parity, and finite-sample sufficiency is established with the likelihood criterion. Reconstructed boundaries n=2 and terminal singleton and verified by integer/Fraction enumeration; finite checks are not presented as the proof.

Original comparison concerns strict total-run likelihood ratio across symmetric concentration and minimal parity-pair compression, not existing PMFs of success runs or comparisons among run-counting schemes. Direct overlapping theorem was not found in published-record semantic comparisons or the materially inspected primary sections. A later relevant paper and older distribution papers were not fully readable, so independent historical priority remains uncertain.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The concentration parameter measures latent heterogeneity/correlation, a central diagnostic in reinforced or overdispersed binary data. The theorem yields a sharp finite-sample monotone likelihood-ratio test statistic for that parameter, plus an unexpected lossless reduction of the number of run-count states by nearly a factor of two; an exact equality class is stronger than a mean or stochastic-order calculation. The general exchangeable parity identity and folded-count TP2 representation are reusable structural lemmas, not a routine relabeling.
