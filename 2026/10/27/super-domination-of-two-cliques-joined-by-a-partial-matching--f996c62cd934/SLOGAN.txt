For two \(n\)-vertex cliques joined by a matching of size \(t\), every super dominating set has either one omission in each clique or a one-sided omission pattern restricted to matched vertices (apart from singletons). The complete polynomial \(\mathcal S_{n,t}(z)=2z^{2n-t}(1+z)^t-z^{2n}+2(n-t)z^{2n-1}+n^2z^{2n-2}\) yields an inclusion-minimal cardinality gap of \(t-2\) for \(t\ge3\), unbounded even among connected graphs.
