An omitted leaf needs its core selected, excluding the doubly omitted arm. A selected leaf privately witnesses its omitted core automatically. A selected core witnessing its own omitted leaf cannot be adjacent in the base to an omitted core. These are precisely the adjacency rules of P3 with all loops, with the double selection at the middle. The mapping is invertible and the spin weight is z² at the middle and z at each endpoint. The two-particle target is the connected reflexive path; the corrigendum's component issue does not remove this case.
