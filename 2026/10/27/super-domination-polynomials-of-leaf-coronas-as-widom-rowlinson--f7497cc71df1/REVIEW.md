# Review of the leaf-corona super domination polynomial

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof exhausts all possible states of each core–leaf pair, derives the necessary and sufficient exclusion of a \(B\)-to-\(C\) base edge, and proves the bijection with homomorphisms to the reflexive three-vertex path. Fixing the singly selected arms forces their induced connected components to be monochromatic. The coefficient for one doubly selected arm follows exactly, and the \(\#\mathrm P\)-hardness transfer invokes Dyer and Greenhill's published dichotomy and their subcubic Widom–Rowlinson specialization. The included checker tests 183 graph instances directly and agrees with the proof-derived expressions. It is finite corroboration, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to the stated literature risk.** Klein–Rodríguez-Velázquez–Yi determine the scalar minimum of ordinary coronas; Ghanbari–Jäger–Lehtilä address minimum counts in selected classes and the different neighborhood-corona operation. The published one-leaf-corona minimum-set census gives \(2^{c(H)}\) but expressly does not cover nonminimum sets. The published Widom–Rowlinson model and its graph-homomorphism complexity are prior knowledge: this proof specializes that partition function to a newly identified graph-domination problem, and does not claim the partition function itself new. Earlier specific-tree and forest enumerators cover some acyclic special cases but not the general-base bridge and hardness. The Kaidash 2024 article's inaccessible full text leaves genuine priority uncertainty; its abstract alone cannot establish exclusion or overlap.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact all-cardinality enumerator, natural three-state bijection and parsimonious hardness transfer distinguish a graph family whose minimum super domination number and minimum count are easy from the full enumeration problem, which is \(\#\mathrm P\)-complete even under a degree-four bound. The second coefficient recovers an intrinsic connectivity obstruction (cut vertices). This is structural and complexity information for an infinite class, not only another minimum computation.

**Scope and limitations.** One private leaf per base vertex, finite simple base graphs, no claim for general corona attachments, no claim of discovering Widom–Rowlinson counting complexity, no claim of independent validation, and a residual priority risk from incompletely accessible 2024 literature.
