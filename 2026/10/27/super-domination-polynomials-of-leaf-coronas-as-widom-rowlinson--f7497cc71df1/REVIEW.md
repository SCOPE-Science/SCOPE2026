# Scientific assessment of the narrowed finding

**Correctness.** Selected core–leaf pairs have three states; an edge between opposite endpoint states is the sole obstruction, so the map to reflexive-path homomorphisms is reversible. Dyer–Greenhill §5 supplies the degree-three Widom–Rowlinson hardness bound, the corona map preserves the count and raises maximum degree by at most one, and subset verification puts the count in #P.

**Originality.** The heterogeneous pendant-corona record `08492630563f` already computes the full polynomial, and its admissibility proof specializes to the local constraints here. The weighted spin-model formula and fixed-target hardness are also prior. The retained result is the transfer of exact counting hardness to super dominating sets on this graph class, by interpreting the one-leaf constraints as a fixed-target homomorphism count. It is not a novelty claim for the enumerating polynomial or a coefficient extracted from it.

**Value.** The transfer gives a complexity classification for coronas of subcubic graphs, where the minimum size and minimum-set count are both easy. The all-set count can be #P-complete even with degree at most four and one private leaf per base vertex.
