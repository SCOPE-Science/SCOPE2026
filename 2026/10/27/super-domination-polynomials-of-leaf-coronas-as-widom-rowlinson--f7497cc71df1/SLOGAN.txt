Every super dominating set of a one-leaf corona \(H\circ K_1\) is a three-state Widom–Rowlinson configuration of its base graph, giving the exact polynomial \(\sum_{U\subseteq V(H)}2^{c(H[U])}z^{2|V(H)|-|U|}\). This correspondence makes exact counting \(\#\mathrm P\)-complete even for coronas of subcubic bases, while the coefficient immediately above the minimum detects cut vertices of connected bases.
