For every integer \(d\ge2\), the non-projective Oda–Miyake toric threefold \(Z''_{13}(-2,2,0,d)\) has no torus-invariant curve of canonical degree zero, yet its canonical bundle is not tensor-generating because a torus-invariant blowup of \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) obstructs it. Smooth non-invariant canonical-trivial curves in this surface have genus \(d-2\), giving an explicit surface-only obstruction within the four-parameter family posed in Ito–Zeng–Zeng's Question 6.6.
