\(\cos\theta=\cos(2\pi k)+(\alpha+\beta)\sin(2\pi k)/(2k)+\alpha\beta\sin^2(\pi k)/(2k^2)\).

On every admissible half-interval the branch function has strictly negative derivative, which proves complete root enumeration. The identical-barrier monodromy equals minus the identity at each root, proving geometric multiplicity two.
