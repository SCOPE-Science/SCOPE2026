For automorphisms of \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^{md+1})\) with a prescribed order-\(d\) linear multiplier and \(2\le m\le\operatorname{char}(\mathbb F_q)\), the fixed algebra has exactly \(m\) possible isomorphism types: explicit monomial-tail algebras. Their probabilities follow a truncated geometric law determined by the first nonzero resonant coefficient, independently of the multiplier order.
