For every \(x\ge3\), the quotient \(\langle3x-r,3x-r+1,3x-r+2,3x-r+3\rangle/3\), with \(r=1,2\), has an explicit two-branch Apéry staircase that determines its entire pseudo-Frobenius set and a period-three Frobenius formula. Its genus is exactly \(x(x-1)/2+\lfloor(x+1)/3\rfloor\) for \(r=1\) and \(x(x-1)/2\) for \(r=2\).
