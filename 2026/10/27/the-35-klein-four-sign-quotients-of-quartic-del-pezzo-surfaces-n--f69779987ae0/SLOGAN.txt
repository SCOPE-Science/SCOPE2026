Among the \(35\) Klein-four coordinate-sign subgroups of a smooth complex quartic del Pezzo surface, exactly \(10,15,10\) have quotients with baskets \(\varnothing,4A_1,6A_1\), anticanonical degrees \(9,4,1\), and smooth-locus fundamental groups \(1,\mathbb Z/2,(\mathbb Z/2)^2\), respectively. The six-node quotients admit explicit degree-six weighted equations and universal quasi-étale covers by the original quartic del Pezzo.
