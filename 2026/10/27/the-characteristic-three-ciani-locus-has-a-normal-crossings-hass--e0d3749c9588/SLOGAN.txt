In characteristic three, the nonordinary locus of the smooth marked Ciani quartic family is a simple-normal-crossings union of three explicit Hasse surfaces. For every \(q=3^n\), the exact numbers of smooth marked quartics with Jacobian \(3\)-ranks \(0,1,2,3\) are respectively \(1\), \(3(q-3)\), \(3(q-3)(q-5)\), and \((q-3)(q^2-7q+18)\); these count parameter triples, not isomorphism classes.
