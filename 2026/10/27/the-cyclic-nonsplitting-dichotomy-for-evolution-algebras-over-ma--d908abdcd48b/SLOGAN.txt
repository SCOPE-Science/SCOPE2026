Over \(\mathbb F_4\), simple evolution algebras supported on one weighted directed cycle have one isomorphism type in odd dimension and exactly two in even dimension. For even \(n\), the twisted class has cyclic automorphism group \(C_{3n/2}\) and a nonsplit permutation-automorphism extension exactly when \(6\mid n\); the untwisted class has a split extension and three nonzero idempotents.
