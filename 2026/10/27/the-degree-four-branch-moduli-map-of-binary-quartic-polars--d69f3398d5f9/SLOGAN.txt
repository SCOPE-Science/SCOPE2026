The branch divisor of the degree-three polar map of a smooth binary quartic has absolute invariant \(j_{\mathrm{branch}}=j(j+13824)^3/[64(j-1728)^3]\) away from the harmonic value \(j=1728\), where the branch quartic becomes a quadratic square. This degree-four source-to-branch moduli map is a tetrahedral Belyi cover with the same rational covering formula as \(X_0(3)\to X(1)\); it does not canonically attach a three-isogeny to the quartic.
