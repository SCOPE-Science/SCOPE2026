The single-alternating-cycle flip graph of perfect matchings of the triangle-expanded prism \(F(C_n\square K_2)\) is the join of two copies of a binary circular-interval Cayley graph, for every \(n\ge3\). It has \(2^{n+1}\) vertices, degree \(2^n+n(n-1)\), and diameter exactly two: any two perfect matchings differ by at most two legal single-cycle flips.
