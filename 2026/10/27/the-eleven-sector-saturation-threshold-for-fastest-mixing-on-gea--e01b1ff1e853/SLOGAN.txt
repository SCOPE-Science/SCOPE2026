Every gear graph with at least eleven rim sectors has an exactly fastest-mixing symmetric walk that exhausts the hub and original-rim transition budgets: spoke weights are \(1/n\), outer weights are \((n-1)/(2n)\), and the optimal SLEM is \((1+\sqrt{1+4(n-1)^2\cos^2(\pi/n)})/(2n)\). Eleven is sharp for this saturated invariant design: ten sectors favor leaving some original-rim holding probability.
