For every fixed integer \(p\ge2\), the Frobenius number of \(\langle a,a+1\rangle/p\), as \(a>p\) varies, has **least** quadratic-quasipolynomial period exactly \(p\), including every composite modulus. An explicit gap construction distinguishes the residue-zero branch from every proper-divisor residue class, ruling out all smaller periods.
