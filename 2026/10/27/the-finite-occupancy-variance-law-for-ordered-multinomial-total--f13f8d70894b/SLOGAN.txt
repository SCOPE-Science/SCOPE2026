For an ordered uniform multinomial histogram with sample size proportional to bin count, the variance of discrete total variation is \(n\,v(N/n)+O(1)\), where an explicit Poisson–Bessel coefficient includes a nonvanishing negative correction from conditioning on the total count. The sharp correction is contributed collectively by \(\Theta(n^2)\) individually weakly dependent, disjoint edge pairs; \(v(\lambda)\sim4\lambda^2\) at low occupancy.
