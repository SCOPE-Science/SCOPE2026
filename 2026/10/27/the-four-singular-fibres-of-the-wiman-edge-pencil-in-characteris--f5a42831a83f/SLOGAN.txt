For the Wiman–Edge pencil reduced modulo \(3\), exactly four fibres on the degree-five del Pezzo surface are singular: a five-component \(K_5\) fibre, a ten-component Petersen fibre, and two irreducible six-nodal rational fibres. The finite nonzero plane sextics have four wild cusps in place of the characteristic-zero fixed nodes; these resolve after one blowup at each of the four distinguished points.
