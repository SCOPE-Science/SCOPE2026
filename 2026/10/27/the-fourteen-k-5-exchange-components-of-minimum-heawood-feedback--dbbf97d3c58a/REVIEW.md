# Self-review

## Correctness: PASS
The \(14\)-vertex, \(21\)-edge cubic counting identity forces a size-four minimum decycling set to be independent and its complement an induced tree. The Fano-plane cases with point/line counts \((4,0)\), \((3,1)\), \((2,2)\), and their duals are exhaustive. The \((2,2)\) case contains an explicit eight-cycle. Every surviving set is one four-subset of a unique line- or point-based five-element configuration. Distinct configurations overlap in at most two vertices, forcing exactly fourteen exchange cliques. Independent exhaustive constructions corroborate the classification, counts and three-connectivity. No infinite extrapolation is made.

## Originality: PASS with identified residual risk
The closest primary reconfiguration literature [2] establishes a general framework and connectivity for certain subcubic graphs, and constructs disconnected examples for odd minimum sizes at least five; the present even-size-four Heawood result with all seventy configurations and exact exchange components does not follow. The exact graph construction is classical [1]. Another cubic decycling paper [3] has an existence result, not an enumerative claim in the accessible abstract. Targeted searches of mathematical records, graph-invariant tables and available published research entries identified no exact or dominating statement. The full 2023 and 2022 papers were not read, so no claim of exhaustive priority clearance is made; additional unindexed coverage remains possible.

## Value: PASS
The classification gives an explicit nonplanar, 3-connected, cubic, bipartite, girth-six graph with a disconnected exchange graph at minimum feedback size four, including the complete component geometry. It supplies a small even-parameter test case next to the cited 2023 odd-parameter construction, and a structural explanation in terms of Fano incidence duality rather than an isolated count. The exact number and component type of all optima are natural invariants for reconfiguration complexity.

## Relationship and scope
The theorem is finite and graph-specific. Disconnection is asserted only under equal-cardinality single-vertex replacement among minimum feedback vertex sets; allowing an additional token is outside the claim and covered by broader positive theory. Remaining originality risk concerns inaccessible full text and unindexed tables.
