For the non-symplectic diagonal order-six quotient of the Fermat sextic fourfold, the complete Chen–Ruan Hodge–Deligne polynomial has untwisted primitive multiplicities \((0,70,290,70,0)\), two age-two nine-point sectors, and two age-\(3/2\) genus-four curve sectors. It refines the previously known orbifold Euler value \(441\) by retaining the full fractional Hodge grading.
