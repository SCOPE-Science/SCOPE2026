# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The algebraic proof establishes the local Frobenius decomposition of the finite group algebra, counts all common and inverse root pairs, and verifies the zero-primary nilpotency index separately at the central and noncentral zero blocks. The maximum-depth proportion follows from rank of the penultimate power on the nilpotent component. No inference from small cases is used for the arbitrary-size theorem. The standalone direct-coordinate verifier found agreement for 144 dimension pairs, exhaustively enumerated 74,240 small configurations for recurrent counts, and independently counted 262,144 six-column torus states for the exact peak-depth proportion. The limit is that individual primitive periods are not classified.

**Originality — PASS, with stated historical overlap risk.** The closest exact common object is the sigma-game on a torus in Barua–Ramakrishnan (1996, §5), which proves noninvertibility without the gcd/two-adic Fitting dimension, sharp index and peak-depth law. Kawahara et al. (1992 technical report) use null rather than periodic boundaries; Ekhad–Sloane–Zeilberger (2015) use infinite-lattice single-seed population sequences. Martin–Odlyzko–Wolfram (1984) establishes foundational algebraic dynamics rather than these evaluated torus-specific formulas. Guan–He (1986) gives a broad method and remains a documented unresolved full-text risk. Searches of published-finding corpus for equivalent graph/dynamics/parameters and of accessible prior research claims did not reveal a dominating result; absence of search hits is not proof of global novelty.

**Value — PASS.** The object is the natural nearest-neighbour binary torus; its recurrent dimension and transient depth are basic global invariants. The exact formula reveals the coexistence of a gcd-controlled recurrent codimension and a separate sharp two-adic time scale, including complete nilpotence for power-of-two tori. The peak-depth fraction quantitatively describes how representative the worst transient is across the full state space. This combination is not a renaming of a general matrix-rank formula or an isolated finite calculation.

**Residual limitations.** The full text of Guan–He (1986) could not be accessed; a 1995 study uses the same local rule but null boundaries. The exact primitive cycle counts and the complete transient-depth histogram were not determined. The result has received no independent expert audit.
