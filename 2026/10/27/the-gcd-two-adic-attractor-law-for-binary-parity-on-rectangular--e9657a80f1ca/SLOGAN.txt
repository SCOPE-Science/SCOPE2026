On the binary four-neighbour XOR torus \(C_m\square C_n\), the exact number of recurrent states is \(2^{mn-k\ell(2g-1)}\), where \(k,\ell\) are the two-adic parts of the circumferences and \(g\) is the gcd of their odd parts. The sharp time to reach recurrence is \(\max(k,\ell)\) for \(g>1\), but only \(\max(1,k/2,\ell/2)\) for \(g=1\); all power-of-two tori extinguish.
