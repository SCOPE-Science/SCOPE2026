In characteristic \(5\), the monic squarefree cyclic genus-four quintic family \(y^3=f(x)\) has exactly two Jacobian \(5\)-ranks: \(0\) on the explicitly computed absolutely irreducible weighted-degree-\(16\) coefficient hypersurface \(H=0\), and \(2\) elsewhere. This is a coefficient-level equation and irreducibility statement, distinct from the previously established irreducibility of the abstract trielliptic moduli stratum.
