On a fixed labelled finite loop-free undirected frame, non-contingency logic sees every edge except those forming isolated two-vertex components: erasing those edges gives the exact observational kernel, and a single \(\Delta p\) test distinguishes unequal kernels. A kernel with \(s\) isolated vertices has exactly \(\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor s/2\rfloor}s!/(2^j j!(s-2j)!)\) indistinguishable graph lifts; their complete size distribution follows from a bivariate labelled-graph generating function. The resulting univariate graph-count sequence is previously known as OEIS A093352.
