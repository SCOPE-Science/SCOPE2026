For every prime \(p>9\) with \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), three-dimensional inverted Kloosterman sums over \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\) are equal exactly for Frobenius-conjugate parameters, with explicit degrees \((p-1)/4\) or \((p-1)/\gcd(8,p-1)\). The sole prime excluded by the earlier quadratic-extension theorem, \(p=73\), is resolved by replacing its vanished second logarithmic coefficient with fourth- and sixth-power traces; exactly \(2700\) values occur in that field.
