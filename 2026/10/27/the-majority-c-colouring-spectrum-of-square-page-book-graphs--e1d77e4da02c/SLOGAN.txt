For the square-page book graph \(B_b=K_2\square K_{1,b}\), every majority C-colouring is classified by the colours of the two spine vertices and the page types, yielding an exact Stirling-number formula for the entire partition spectrum. The maximum number of colours is \(1+\lceil b/2\rceil\), and there are exactly \(\binom{b+1}{\lfloor b/2\rfloor+1}\) unlabelled optimal partitions, belonging to two explicit structural families.
