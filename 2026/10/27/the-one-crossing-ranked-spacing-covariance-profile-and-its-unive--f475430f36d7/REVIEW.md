**Mathematical value — PASS.** Both ranked spacing lengths and quadratic spacing concentration are canonical descriptors of how a random interval partition is distributed. The theorem identifies *which ranks* increase with concentration, determines all mixed covariances and proves a non-obvious, universal dividing fraction \(e^{-2}\). Marginal spacing formulas or the concentration mean cannot determine this joint sign profile; the sharp rank phase diagram is a motivated structural result.
