# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The 2015 timing law gives \(t=(T_r/2\pi)[\chi-(V_2/V_1)e^*\sin\chi]\). Multiplication by \(n=2\pi/T_r\) fixes the coefficient as \((V_2/V_1)e^*\). Direct substitution verifies the two exact boundaries: \(k=1\) gives \(V_1=V_2=1\), while \(k=2\) gives \(V_1=1-(e^*)^2>0\) and \(V_2=0\). Thus the printed reciprocal is undefined in the harmonic case but the corrected coefficient gives \(nt=\chi\).

## Originality — PASS

The full texts of the 2014 inversion paper and 2015 central-orbit derivation were compared, along with relevant sections of the 2023 improved cubic method. Bounded semantic literature-record and public searches did not surface a matching correction. The 2023 method treats the classical Kepler equation, where the discrepancy is invisible. An unindexed erratum or correspondence remains possible; the search is not proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The correction restores a canonical exactly solvable boundary case of the advertised general-orbit formula. It is not merely a normalization preference: the printed parameter is undefined for the harmonic oscillator, while the corrected one recovers its exact linear timing.
