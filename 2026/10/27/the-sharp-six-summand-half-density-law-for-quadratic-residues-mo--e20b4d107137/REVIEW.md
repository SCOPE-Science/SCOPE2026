# Scientific review

Kneser's stabilizer bound gives an explicit contradiction for primes at least eleven and reduces seven to the unique index-seven subgroup. Six sums of two distinct classes cover the residue field, contradicting any proper stabilizer. The strict inequality in density and the modulus-seven sharpness witness are treated exactly. The finite `verify.py` checks are supplementary, not an infinite proof.

## Mathematical value: PASS
Determines the sharp **uniform** summand cutoff of a natural finite local Waring density problem across every exponent, with a matching obstruction at the smallest allowed prime. It improves the directly comparable ten-summand half-density bound by four summands in this prime-power regime. It does not assert individual-modulus minimal orders.
