The literal characteristic-three reduction of the six-coordinate symmetric Burkhardt model \(e_1=e_4=0\) is a cone over a \(15\)-nodal quartic K3 surface; its reduced singular support is the union of \(15\) lines through a quadruple vertex. Every node and singular line is defined over \(\mathbf F_3\), and the threefold has \(76\) \(\mathbf F_3\)-points.
