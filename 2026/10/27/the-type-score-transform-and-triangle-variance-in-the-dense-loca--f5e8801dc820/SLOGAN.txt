For every finite parameter tournament in the homogeneous dense local order, the nonalgebraic one-type outdegree polynomial is exactly \((1+z)\) times the parameter score polynomial. Consequently the variance of a uniform one-type's outdegree is \((n^2+2)/12-2c_3/n\), where \(c_3\) counts directed triangles, with sharp regular-to-transitive extremal bounds.
