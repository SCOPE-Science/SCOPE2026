Among smooth hyperelliptic genus-four curves over \(\mathbb F_3\), the curve \(y^2=x^9-x\) is the unique \(\mathbb F_3\)-isomorphism class with no closed point of degree four. Its higher-field analogue \(y^2=x^{q^2}-x\) makes the universal closed-point threshold sharp for every odd prime power: \(\rho(q,(q^2-1)/2)=5\).
