# Review of the characteristic-eleven Klein cubic local equation

**Correctness — PASS.** All projective critical points have nonzero coordinates. The exact affine critical ideal has one point with colength \(11\), and the equation lies in its ideal. A Hessian block of determinant \(6\) makes the formal Morse reduction legitimate, while an explicit polar-curve function has initial term \(6u^{11}\) and is a unit multiple of \(u^{11}\). These independent steps establish the claimed completed local ring, not just a numerical experiment. The bundled checker reproduces the exact algebra; the infinite formal coordinate step is reasoned in the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with a significant identified discrepancy.** Debarre–Laface–Roulleau already give the exact singular point, so that portion is prior art. Their Section 4.5.2 states type \(A_2\), which under the hypersurface local-equation convention stated in the same article (Section 4.4, footnote 4) would have Tjurina length \(2\), and does not supply the exact ring. The computed ring has intrinsic Tjurina length \(11\), so it is neither an equivalent formulation nor an implication of that published type designation. Roulleau's earlier cubic paper studies its smooth characteristic-zero geometry. General positive-characteristic simple-singularity classifications do not compute the specific mod-eleven member in their inspected descriptions. Exact-equivalence searches found no other matching theorem, but historical overlap and the contradictory prior label remain material risks.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exceptional reduction of a classical cubic threefold is a natural algebraic-geometric object. Specifying its precise completed local ring and intrinsic Tjurina scheme supplies local structure relevant to deformations; the distinction between lengths two and eleven is substantive rather than a routine name change. The claim does not require a global resolution to be meaningful.

**Limitations and closest literature.** Source inspection is concentrated on the relevant Klein section of arXiv:1510.05803 and the defining sections of arXiv:1001.4853. The abstract of arXiv:1206.3742 was consulted as general context only, not full-text proof of absence. A specialist should independently reconcile the published \(A_2\) label before treating the result as historically settled. Right equivalence of the original polynomial, semistable reduction, and Fano-line geometry are outside the claim.
