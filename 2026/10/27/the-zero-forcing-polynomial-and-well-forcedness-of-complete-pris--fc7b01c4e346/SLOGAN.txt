The full zero-forcing polynomial of the complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\) is determined for every \(n\ge3\) by the numbers of initially white vertices in its two matched cliques. Every successful initial set contains one of exactly \(2n(n-1)+2\) minimum sets of size \(n\), so the entire infinite family is well-forced; the fair-random success probability is exactly \((n+1)/2^{n-1}-(n^2+6n+1)/4^n\).
