# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For fixed k, the ordered unrestricted count is the cumulative additive k-fold convolution of d(m), and distinct sizes give exactly k! orderings. To check smoothing, the signed difference between the divisor measure and (log y+2γ)dy on [1,∞) has cumulative mass O(√Y). Against the decreasing residual-allocation convolution its integral is bounded by O(√X) times that test's maximum (use its interval-layer decomposition, including end atoms). Telescoping over the k replacements costs O_k(X^(k-1/2)L^(k-1)); the integrable log tail on y in (0,1) costs at most O_k(X^(k-1)L^k). The scaled simplex integral has volume 1/k!, and log derivatives of its two-coordinate moment Γ(1+s)Γ(1+t)/Γ(k+1+s+t) are -H_k and, for the mixed log derivative, H_k^(2)-ζ(2). Adding the product of first derivatives gives exactly the unrestricted coefficients. An equal-size pair has cumulative mass ∑_a binom(floor(X/a),2)=ζ(2)X²/2+O(X log X), by the convergent square-sum and the harmonic error. Replacing this measure by ζ(2)y dy and smoothing the other k-2 factors by the same monotonicity estimate gives the leading collision coefficient ζ(2)/k! since the scaled y-moment is 1/k!. The error from replacing the pair is O_k(X^(k-1)L^(k-1)), lower than the claimed third-order remainder. For any equality block of size r≥2, summing O((X/a)^r) over its common size costs O_r(X^r); intersections of two or more equalities leave at most k-3 singleton blocks and hence at most k-3 logs. This proves the stated third coefficient after subtraction of binom(k,2) equal-pair terms and division by k!. At k=2 the simplex polynomial terminates, there are no higher intersections, and both the cutoff extension and pair error are dominated by O(X^(3/2)L). This checks real cutoffs, k fixed, and the separate k=2 error claim.
