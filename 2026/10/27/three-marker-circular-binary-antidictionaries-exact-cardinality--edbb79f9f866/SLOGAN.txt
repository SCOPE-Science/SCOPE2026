For a binary circular word with exactly three one-markers, the number of distinct minimal absent periodic factors is determined solely by the longest zero gap \(M\) and the number \(d\) of distinct gap lengths: it is \(M+1\), \(M+3\), or \(M+4\) for \(d=1,2,3\), respectively. The explicit bispecial-factor classification also determines every weight-three fixed-length minimum and maximum, including the sharp maximum of \(n\) at length \(n\ge4\).
