For the dihedral genus-four family \(y^2=x^{10}+ax^8+bx^6+bx^4+ax^2+1\) in characteristic \(3\), the entire nonordinary locus is the smooth part of \(a=0\), with exactly one supersingular but non-superspecial parameter \((a,b)=(0,0)\). Over every \(\mathbf F_{3^m}\), the ordinary, intermediate and supersingular marked-parameter counts are \(q^2-4q+5\), \(q-3\), and \(1\), respectively.
