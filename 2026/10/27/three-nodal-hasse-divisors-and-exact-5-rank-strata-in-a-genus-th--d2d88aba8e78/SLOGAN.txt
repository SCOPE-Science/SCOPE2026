For the smooth genus-three palindromic hyperelliptic Howe family \(y^2=x^8+a x^6+b x^4+a x^2+1\) in characteristic \(5\), the three Hasse divisors meet only at three transverse rational points: Jacobian \(5\)-rank zero is impossible, while rank one occurs exactly there. For each \(q=5^n\), the exact marked counts in ranks \(1,2,3\) are \(3\), \(3q-9-6\delta\), and \((q-3)^2+6\delta\), where \(\delta=1\) for even \(n\) and \(0\) otherwise.
