Exactly 84 smooth point-free \((3,3)\) divisors on \(\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\times\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\) occur, forming precisely three \(\mathbf F_2\)-isomorphism classes of genus-four curves with two trigonal pencils. Their projective orbits have sizes 24, 24 and 36, with distinct extension-field point-count profiles.
