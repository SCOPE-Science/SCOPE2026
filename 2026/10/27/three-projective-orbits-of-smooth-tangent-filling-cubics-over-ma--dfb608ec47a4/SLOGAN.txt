Over \(\mathbf F_3\), exactly three rational projective classes of smooth plane cubics have tangent lines at their rational points covering every point of the plane; their rational-point counts are \(4,6,7\). Their exact orbit sizes are \(936,1872,936\), and the minimum degree of an irreducible tangent-filling plane curve over \(\mathbf F_3\) is \(3\).
