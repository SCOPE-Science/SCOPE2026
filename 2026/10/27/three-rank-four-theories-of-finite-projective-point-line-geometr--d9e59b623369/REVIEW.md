# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The four-round classification is established by a complete three-object incidence-extension analysis, uniform two-prefix extension arguments, and two distinguishing sentences. The exceptional count \(q(q-2)\), the skew-line threshold, and the 50-element representative bound are explicitly derived. The independent finite-type programs agree for the listed small examples. **Scope:** finite projective planes of arbitrary order at least two and higher-dimensional finite Desarguesian projective spaces, with named point and line sorts only. Computations are corroborative, not proofs for arbitrary dimensions.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks
The closest reviewed full text, Venema (1999; 1998 report), establishes completeness and decidability in a modal language for projective planes, not the three rank-four first-order classes. Hirschfeld (1998) establishes geometric incidence properties, not bounded-depth logical equivalence. Cardinal–Payne–Solomon (2015) concerns geometric Ramsey problems on lines in three-space. Berger–Myasnikov (2024) studies a richer all-subspace inclusion signature and field interpretation. The point/line-sort separation between the Fano and ternary planes already appears in earlier related logical work; **that separation alone is not asserted to be new.** Neither it nor known uncoloured Levi equivalence implies the uniform classification of all higher dimensions and all larger planes. The 1998 Stebletsova report and possible unindexed geometric-logic sources remain residual overlap risks.

## Value — PASS
The result gives a complete, sharp finite test set for all rank-four sentences across a natural unbounded geometric family, and isolates exactly which small incidence features remain visible: planarity and the special seven-point plane. The 50-element finite representative bound is optimal. The distinction between sorted incidence and uncoloured incidence illustrates a structural expressiveness threshold, not a parameter substitution.

## Limitations
The theorem is signature- and rank-specific. Independent proof review is not yet performed. The independent Python computation only compares the small planes, while the C++ program checks selected prime-field geometries. Broader fields and non-Desarguesian planes are covered by the geometric argument. Unindexed prior work cannot be excluded by search alone.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
