For a smooth Haar-preserving cosine shear of the two-torus, ordinary Bowen balls have area of order \(n^{-1}\) at almost every center, but order \(n^{-1/2}\) at two critical centers. Their spatially averaged area is asymptotic to \(4\varepsilon^2\log(n)/(a\pi^2n)\), a sharp logarithmic enhancement invisible to the known polynomial-entropy exponent.
