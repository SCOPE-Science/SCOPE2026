For independent-parent recursive trees with attachment weights \(i^\alpha\), the global path-length variance constant collapses at the strongly root-biased endpoint through three sharply distinct scales: \(\sigma_\alpha^2=18^\alpha+32^\alpha-4\,36^\alpha+O(48^\alpha)\) as \(\alpha\to-\infty\). The first two terms encode the earliest nontrivial attachment innovations, and the negative third is their normalization correction.
