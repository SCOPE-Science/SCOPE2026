For a uniformly random labelled finite model of \(f^3=f^2\), the number of roots is asymptotic to \(n/[\log n\,W(\log n)]\), the number at depth one to \(n/W(\log n)\), and almost all elements lie at depth two, with exponentially small fixed-relative-error tails. The exact joint enumerator is \(\exp(uz\exp(vz\exp(wz)))\).
