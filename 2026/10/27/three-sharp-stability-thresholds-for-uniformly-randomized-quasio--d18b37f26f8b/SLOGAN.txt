Uniformly randomized quasioptimal projections between any two intersecting planar lines have an angle-independent sharp mean-square tolerance \(\sigma=2\), but almost-sure convergence survives strictly beyond it. Their deterministic all-selection guarantee, mean-square guarantee, and pathwise guarantee have three different exact boundaries; for perpendicular lines these are \(\sqrt2\), \(2\), and \(\sqrt{1+e^2}\).
