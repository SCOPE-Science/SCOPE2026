On every labelled complete bipartite graph, synchronous three-state excitation with a common threshold \(k\ge2\) reaches a fixed point, a three-cycle or a four-cycle within five updates, and five is sharp whenever both parts have at least \(2k\) vertices. The entire periodic-state census has closed multinomial and binomial forms, with distinct survival thresholds at \(2k\) and \(3k\).\n
