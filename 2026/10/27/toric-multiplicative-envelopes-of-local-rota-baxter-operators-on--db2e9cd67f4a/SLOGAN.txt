Composing local weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators of a complex maximal-nilindex evolution algebra produces a semigroup whose Zariski closure has exactly \(n-r-2\) rational-normal-cone components, each of dimension \(n+2\), with a common pairwise intersection of dimension \(n+1\). This is a one-dimensional jump beyond the locus of individual local operators, and the complete finite-product semigroup is explicit.
