# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In each bipartition class a selected vertex is distinguished by its zero coordinate; two unselected vertices are distinguished exactly by their incidence patterns to the opposite selected class. Opposite classes have different distance parities. For a four-set, take the side having at least two selected vertices: with 2, 3 or 4 selected there, respectively 5, 4 or 3 unselected vertices must be separated by at most 4, 2 or 1 patterns. For a five-set only the 3+2 split can work by the analogous pigeonhole bound. Two selected lines partition points into incidence classes of sizes 1,2,2,2. The three selected points must take one from each two-element class. A collinear point triple would give only four line-signature types, too few for the five unselected lines. A noncollinear triple gives all seven distinct line-signature types (three secants, three tangents, one external). The two lines must therefore be distinct single-incidence tangents, which also suffice; dualize for the other split. This gives 28 times 3 bases on each side. The independently run replay checks the classification on all 3,473 sets of sizes 0–5; it builds edges by the shared four-subsets and gives 168 vertices, degree 5, 420 edges, 56 triangles exactly those of the canonical partition, and distance layers (1,5,18,49,71,24) from each vertex. In particular all eccentricities are five. These are exact finite checks on the intended token-jump graph.

PASS

Credit is for the exhaustive classification of minimum sets distinguishing vertices, with their exchange structure. The dimension value and a witness are known. A distinct indexed edge-metric finding has exactly the same landmark set family and hence exactly the same jump graph, including the basic graph invariants. This is material coverage of the graph as a combinatorial object, not credit for a separate graph discovery. The surviving distinction is why precisely that family, and no additional five-set, distinguishes the vertices rather than the edges. The inspected sources do not supply this converse, and no priority between the related records is assigned by their dates.
