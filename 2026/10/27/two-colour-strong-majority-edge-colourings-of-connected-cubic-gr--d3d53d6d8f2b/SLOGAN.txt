For every connected cubic graph \(G\), named two-colour strong-majority edge-colourings correspond exactly to an efficient dominating set \(D\) and the choice of which colour is used on edges touching \(D\). Hence the count is \(2p(G)\), where \(p(G)\) counts efficient dominating sets; existence forces the order to be divisible by four, or by eight for bipartite cubic graphs.
