For the explicit smooth \(\mathbf F_2\)-cubic threefold without rational lines exhibited by Debarre–Laface–Roulleau, its eight \(\mathbf F_4\)-lines form exactly two conjugate triangles and a conjugate pair of lines meeting no others. The triangles are the unique \(\mathbf F_4\)-split three-line plane sections; this refines the previously known line counts with the first-extension incidence geometry.
