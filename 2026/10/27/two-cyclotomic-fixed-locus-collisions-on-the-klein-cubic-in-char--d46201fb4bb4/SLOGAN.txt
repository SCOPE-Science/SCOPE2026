The order-five automorphism of the Klein cubic and its Fano surface has two different characteristic-five collisions: four fixed points become one curvilinear point of length \(4\), while two invariant lines become one curvilinear point of length \(2\). Their integral fixed schemes are the fifth cyclotomic extension and its quadratic subfield, and their special-fiber tangent actions form unipotent blocks of sizes \(3\) and \(2\).
