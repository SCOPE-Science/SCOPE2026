# Scientific scope note

The independent claim is the nonbinary rank and site-resolved law. The binary two-absorber rank dichotomy and binary sitewise law are prior results: see the October 12 *Two absorbers on a quantum hypercube* record cited in `RESULT.md`. The calculations for the binary case in the accompanying numerical checker are benchmarks only. The nonbinary argument uses strict inequality in every nonconstant spectral block, including the top level, and the proof covers all alphabets of size at least three and dimensions from one onward.
