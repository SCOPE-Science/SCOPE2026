For two irreversible detectors on the \(q\)-ary Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\), the exact uniform-initial-state absorption efficiency is \((2d+1)/q^d\) when \(q\ge3\), independent of detector placement and positive rates. On the binary hypercube it is \(2d/2^d\) for nonantipodal detectors but only \((d+1)/2^d\) for antipodal detectors: an antipodal second detector provides no new accessible mode.
