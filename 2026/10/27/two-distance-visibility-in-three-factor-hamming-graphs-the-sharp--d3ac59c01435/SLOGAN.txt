For three-factor Hamming graphs, every two-distance mutually visible vertex set lies in a rectangle-free coordinate plane, a radius-one ball, or a four-point parity tetrahedron, yielding an exact extremal formula in terms of Zarankiewicz numbers. In \(K_q\square K_q\square K_q\), ball configurations win for \(2\le q\le5\), tie the planar optimum at \(q=6\), and the planar Zarankiewicz extremals dominate for every \(q\ge7\).
