# Scientific review: two-face fluctuations of multiset deletion balls

## Correctness
**PASS.** The theorem's domain is explicitly uniform weak compositions with fixed alphabet size and fixed deletion radius. The argument counts all allowable zero-sum difference vectors, identifies boundary violations, and uses exact one-face and two-face inclusion-exclusion. The probability of three or more boundary coordinates is at most of order \(n^{-3}\), with constants controlled by fixed \(q,r\). Binomial-ratio expansions establish both orders for each fixed moment. The finite ternary spectrum exhausts six disjoint coordinate categories for every \(n\ge6\) and its raw-moment sums give the exact variance. Standalone exhaustive checks confirm the local counts and finite formulas; the analytic steps, not their finite instances, prove the theorem.

## Originality
**PASS, with a qualified priority risk.** Kreindel, Essayag, and Zabokritskiy, arXiv:2603.18322v1, give a general center-specific generating function and exact average volume; the interior volume and its distance interpretation are prior work. Sections 4.1, 4.4, 4.5 and 5.3 of the accessible complete HTML source were read. The published text does not state a two-face moment expansion, rare-defect spectrum, second-order variance constant, or the complete six-class ternary radius-two frequency table. The previously available single-center formula makes these new statistics derivable in principle but does not alone evaluate their distribution, and the new inclusion-exclusion calculation supplies that missing evaluation. Earlier multiset-code work treats construction and optimal code sizes, while indexed results on random sequence-edit balls use another center measure and channel. The possibility of an unindexed equivalent or a short unpublished derivation remains.

## Value
**PASS.** Multiset deletion balls are inherently nonhomogeneous; the motivating literature uses their minimum, maximum, and mean for finite-block coding bounds. The new result quantifies the remaining natural variability: it identifies every leading rare boundary deficit, gives exact first and second corrections to all fixed moments, and supplies a complete finite-block ternary radius-two spectrum. This is not an arbitrary parameter sample: ternary radius two is the first two-coordinate nontrivial boundary-intersection case, and the universal two-face coefficient explains its finite behavior. The result does not purport to improve an existing code-size bound without a separate argument.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is *Polynomial Constructions and Deletion-Ball Geometry for Multiset Deletion Codes* (arXiv:2603.18322v1). Additional background: *Multiset Deletion-Correcting Codes: Bounds and Constructions* (arXiv:2601.05636) and *Codes in the Space of Multisets—Coding for Permutation Channels with Impairments* (arXiv:1612.08837). The asymptotic regime fixes alphabet size and radius; its distribution is uniform across multisets, not across ordered sequences. The exact frequency table covers ternary radius two and lengths at least six. No independent mathematical audit has been conducted.
