Among the previously classified \(864\) all-smooth plane-cubic nets over \(\mathbf F_2\), exactly \(16\) have a nonempty geometric base: they form two projective orbits, and each base is a nonreduced length-six scheme supported at one Frobenius degree-three closed point. The other \(848\) nets are basepoint-free and define finite degree-nine morphisms of projective planes.
