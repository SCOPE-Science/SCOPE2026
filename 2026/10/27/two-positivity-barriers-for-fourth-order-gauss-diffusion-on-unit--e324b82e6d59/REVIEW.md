# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The completed fourth-order Gauss rational function is defined for every nonnegative step. All independent four-node entries have exact positive-denominator factorizations, and the only sign-changing numerator is a quartic with one positive zero. The pseudoinverse/resistance identity proves the negative large-step entry for every path with at least four nodes. The unique length-seven path walk and the known Padé Taylor coefficient prove arbitrarily-small-step negativity in every path with at least eight nodes. An exact all-positive integer matrix confirms the symmetric eight-node recovery at step four; the executable symbolic check passed. Finite computation is used only for exact finite certificates.

## Originality — PASS
The strongest close public results give a nilpotent/directed eight-state Padé failure, a directed pure-birth stochasticity window, and general absolute-monotonicity barriers. The zero sixth coefficient and negative seventh coefficient, and the general dimension-seven protection for small steps, are previously established and are not claimed as new. The inspected statements do not imply the exact four-node path cutoff, the symmetric eight-node positive recovery matrix, or the pathwise large-step sign law. The oldest concrete source was publicly deposited on 19 June 2012. Earlier specialized reversible-tridiagonal coverage remains a possible originality risk.

## Value — PASS
The two natural path-size barriers delimit distinct failure mechanisms for a standard high-order integrator. The exact four-node threshold and eight-node sign-window recovery show that positivity-preserving step choice can depend nonmonotonically on step size even for conservative reversible diffusion, with a reproducible exact certificate useful for numerical-method design and testing.

## Literature, scope and limitations
Zappavigna, Colaneri, Kirkland and Shorten (2012) provide the Padé coefficient and nilpotent shift but not this path classification. Lóczi and Ketcheson (2014) provide general rational-function radii rather than path-specific exact matrices. The published eight-state Markov result classifies an absorbing pure-birth generator rather than a symmetric path. The current theorem does not classify all positivity intervals for larger paths, nor claim stagewise positivity. A 2005 general RK paper remained accessible only by abstract, which is a residual novelty uncertainty.
