The fourth-order Gauss–Legendre heat step on a unit path has two distinct dimension obstructions: four vertices first permit large-step negativity, while eight vertices force negativity at arbitrarily small steps. The four-vertex threshold is the unique positive root of \(h^4-6h^3-60h^2-108h-72\), and the eight-vertex path is nevertheless strictly positive at \(h=4\), so even reversible nearest-neighbor diffusion exhibits loss–recovery–loss of one-step positivity.
