For the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre heat step on binary cubes, \(d=3\) is the sharp dimension where sufficiently large steps lose entrywise positivity, while \(d=7\) is the sharp dimension where arbitrarily small steps already fail. The entire positivity window on the three-cube is \([0,\alpha]\), with \(\alpha\) the unique positive root of \(h^4-6h^3-20h^2-18h-6=0\).
