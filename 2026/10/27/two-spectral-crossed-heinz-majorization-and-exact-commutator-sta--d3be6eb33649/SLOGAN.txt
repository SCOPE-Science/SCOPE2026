For positive matrices having at most two eigenvalues apiece, even with completely different spectra and arbitrarily large dimensions, the crossed-Heinz matrix is singular-value weakly majorized by the ordinary Heinz matrix for every \(0<t<1\). Their squared Frobenius-norm gap is exactly a product of positive scalar spectral-gap factors times \(\|[A,B]\|_{\mathrm F}^2\), so equality holds precisely at commutation.
