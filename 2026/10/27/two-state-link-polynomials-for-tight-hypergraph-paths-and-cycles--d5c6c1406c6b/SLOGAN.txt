Over tight paths and tight cycles in the homogeneous tetrahedron-free 3-hypergraph, every nonalgebraic one-point type is counted by an explicit two-state link-graph transfer polynomial. For a tight cycle of length \(n\ge5\), the exact count is \(2^{\binom n2-2n}(\lambda_+^n+\lambda_-^n)\), where \(\lambda_\pm=(3\pm\sqrt{17})/2\); forbidden-triangle overlap changes the exponential admissibility rate from the independent benchmark \(7/8\) to \((3+\sqrt{17})/8\).
