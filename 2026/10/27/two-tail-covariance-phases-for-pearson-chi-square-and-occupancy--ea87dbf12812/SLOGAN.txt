For uniform multinomial sampling, Pearson's chi-square statistic has an exact binomial-curvature covariance with every occupancy-frequency count, producing positive covariance for unusually sparse and unusually crowded cells but negative covariance between two explicit thresholds. Singleton covariance reverses at \(n=2m\), while doubleton covariance exhibits a reentrant sign transition whose exact zeros form an infinite negative-Pell family.
