Among odd weights at most \(X\), the number of odd strongly unimodal sequence counts congruent to \(2\pmod4\) is \(\frac{\pi^2}{12}X/\log X+B X/(\log X)^2+O(X/(\log X)^3)\), with an explicit constant \(B\) involving \(\zeta'(2)/\zeta(2)\). Its two natural prime-square families contribute leading constants \(\pi^2/36\) and \(\pi^2/18\), respectively.
