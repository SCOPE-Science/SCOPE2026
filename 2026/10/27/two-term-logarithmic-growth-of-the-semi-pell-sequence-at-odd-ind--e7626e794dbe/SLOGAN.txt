For odd indices, the semi-Pell recurrence has the sharp two-term logarithmic growth \(\log_2 s(n)=\tfrac12(\log_2 n)^2-(\log_2 n)\log_2\log_2 n+O(\log n)\). A constructive family of odd-multiplicity binary overpartitions supplies the matching lower bound, beyond what unrestricted binary-partition asymptotics alone guarantee.
