# Research review

## Correctness (C)
**PASS.** Under normalized area on each disk, the negative logarithm of the radius ratio is exponential with rate \(2\). This gives an exact expectation formula for upper oscillation. The square-root Taylor remainder is bounded by \(u^2/8\), and the needed exponential moments are finite for fixed \(B>0\). The incomplete-gamma expressions follow by direct integration; \(c(B)>0\).

## Originality (O)
**PASS, with residual literature risk.** The closest primary source is Chen and Wang, *Bergman kernel and oscillation theory of plurisubharmonic functions*, arXiv:1906.10170, especially Theorem 3.5 and Lemmas 3.6–3.7. It proves qualitative unboundedness for the same counterexample but does not state the fixed-\(B\) two-term expansion. Exact-claim searches did not locate an equivalent statement. This does not prove that no unindexed source exists.

## Value (V)
**PASS.** The result quantifies a canonical obstruction to uniform upper-oscillation bounds by giving its exact square-root logarithmic growth coefficient and first correction. Its scope is deliberately restricted to this example and fixed \(B\); no universal rate is claimed.
