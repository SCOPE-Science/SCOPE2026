For the canonical plurisubharmonic counterexample on the bidisc, fixing \(B>0\) and shrinking one radius as \(e^{-A}\) gives \(\operatorname{UO}_{P_{A,B}}(\phi)=c(B)\sqrt A+d(B)/\sqrt A+O_B(A^{-3/2})\). The leading coefficient \(c(B)=2^{-1/2}e^{2B}\Gamma(3/2,2B)-\sqrt B\) is strictly positive, so the known failure of uniform upper oscillation has an exact square-root logarithmic growth rate.
