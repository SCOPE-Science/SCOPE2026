For a nilpotent Jordan matrix of type \((n,2)\) with \(n\ge4\), the reduced commuting square-zero locus has two geometrically irreducible components for even \(n\) and three for odd \(n\), all of dimension \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+3\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), its exact cardinality is \(q^{n/2+1}(2q^2-1)\) for even \(n\) and \(q^{(n+3)/2}(3q-2)\) for odd \(n\).
