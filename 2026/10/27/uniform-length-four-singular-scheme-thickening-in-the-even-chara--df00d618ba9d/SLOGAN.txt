In the even-characteristic Asgarli–Ghioca family \(C_k\) over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the geometric singular locus always has exactly \(1+q(q+2)/4\) points, independent of the parameter. The Jacobian singular scheme is uniformly thickened with completed local algebra \(K[[U,V]]/(U^2,V^2)\) at each point, so its total length is \(q(q+2)+4\).
