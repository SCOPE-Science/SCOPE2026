# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The explicit potential has a double stationary point at \((\rho_*,M_*)=(\sqrt5,\sqrt{27/8})\). Its first nonzero radial derivative is cubic, while the mixed derivative \(V_{\rho M}\) is positive. Taylor expansion under \(\rho-\rho_*=\sqrt\delta X\) yields the cubic profile; substitution in the deflection integral gives the exponent \(\delta^{-1/4}\) and exact prefactor \(8/125\). The proof also separates the local expansion from the tail estimate.

## Originality

PASS. The closest primary sources are Patil–Mishra–Narasimha (arXiv:1610.04863), Tsukamoto (arXiv:2008.12244), and Igata–Sasaki–Tsukamoto (arXiv:2603.08778). They provide the dihole's separate regimes, a fixed-metric marginal-orbit expansion, and a general fixed-degenerate-orbit expansion. The joint limit with fixed \(X=(\rho_0-\rho_*)/\sqrt\delta\), its two branch domains, and the explicit integral profile were not found in the inspected statements.

## Value

PASS. The profile quantifies the crossover between logarithmic strong deflection and the critical power-law divergence near the physical merger of a stable and unstable photon ring. It gives a normalized intermediate regime and precise branch restrictions for the equal-mass dihole model.

## Limitations

The result is confined to equatorial scattering in the static equal-mass Majumdar–Papapetrou model. It does not cover off-plane null geodesics, realistic binary dynamics, or higher-order corrections.
