For \(q\) even, the numbers of order-two matrices over \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\) with each prescribed residue-field nilpotent rank have an exact centralizer-cancellation formula. Although involution ranks over the field concentrate, the corresponding reduction ranks over dual numbers approach the discrete uniform law at total-variation rate \(O_q(n^{-1})\).
