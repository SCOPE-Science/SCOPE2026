Finite-depth Wilton resonance wavenumbers obey a universal *uniform-in-harmonic-order* weak-tension crossover: when \(B\downarrow0\), \(x_n(B)\sim X(nB)\), where \(\alpha X^2=\tanh X\), interpolating between \(\alpha^{-1/2}\) and \(\alpha^{-1}\). At fixed tension, \(n x_n\) approaches the exact long–short wave crossing with a cubic correction; near critical tension, \((n^2+1)x_n^2\sim45(1/3-B)\) uniformly in \(n\ge2\).
