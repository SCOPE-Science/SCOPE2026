For \(1<p<2\), the sharp Schatten sum-versus-sum-of-moduli ratio among equal-norm rank-one pairs has a unique pair of overlap magnitudes, characterized by one hyperbolic equation and already attained by real \(2\times2\) matrices. Its trace-norm-endpoint gain is quadratic with coefficient \((z_0^2-1)/2\), and it converges to the sharp Hilbertian constant; no unrestricted Schatten inequality is claimed.
