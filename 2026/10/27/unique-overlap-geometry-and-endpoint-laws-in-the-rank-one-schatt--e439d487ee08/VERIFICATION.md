# Verification

The proof in RESULT.md is self-contained after the standard singular-value and Gram identities. The indispensable nonstandard step is the strictly increasing ratio of two ordered \(\operatorname{sech}^2\) integrals: it is proved directly from the strict monotone likelihood ratio. The phase-alignment step uses trace/determinant, not a numerical maximization. Every overlap in the proven range is realized by real two-dimensional rank-one operators.

Run the standalone file `verify_schatten_pair.py` with a standard Python interpreter. The completed run reported 6,004 passed checks: 6,000 random feasible phase/overlap comparisons at five exponents, two endpoint families (four checks), plus exact-formula checks at all five exponents. These finite tests are ancillary and do not certify universal inequalities. The proof covers every \(1<p<2\) and every finite matrix dimension; the known general low-exponent optimal constant is **not** determined here.

The source-priority assessment is based on the accessible full texts of arXiv:2608.17565, arXiv:2608.15558 and arXiv:2510.16846 and on other indexed summaries. arXiv:2608.18112 was available only as an abstract, leaving priority uncertainty. No independent audit is represented.
