For every starting site \(m\ge2\) of the semi-infinite Lindblad absorbing quantum walk, eventual absorption has exactly one optimal loss-to-hopping ratio \(\eta_m\in(1,\sqrt3)\), and it is strictly greater than the boundary-mode threshold. The precise shift is \(\eta_m=1+3/(4m^3)+9/(8m^4)+O(m^{-5})\), with an absorption gain \(3/(16\pi m^6)+O(m^{-7})\); the finite-distance reciprocal bias toward the stronger of two inverse loss ratios is strictly positive and explicit.
