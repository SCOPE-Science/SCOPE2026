For a pure qubit undergoing isotropic projective Brownian diffusion, Poisson resetting to the initial state improves first passage into a target-fidelity cap at weak reset rates exactly when the initial fidelity exceeds a unique cap-dependent threshold \(x_c(b)\). This threshold interpolates between \(x_c(b)=b/3+14b^2/243+O(b^3)\) for \(b\downarrow0\) and \(x_c(b)\to1-e^{-1}\) for \(b\uparrow1\), with a sharp inverse-logarithmic correction.
