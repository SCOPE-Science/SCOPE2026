# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every Rees idempotent is uniquely \(e_{i,\lambda}=(i,p_{\lambda i}^{-1},\lambda)\), so direct multiplication reduces idempotence of an ordered product to equality of two group-valued column ratios. Counting collisions gives the exact deterministic Pearson-statistic identity, including all normalization factors. Pairwise independence of row-pair ratio sequences and column-pair collision indicators yields the exact mean and variance. A finite-dimensional multivariate central limit theorem on the sum-zero occupancy hyperplanes gives the joint independent Gaussian limits and the stated chi-square law, without falsely claiming finite-size mutual independence. The summable second-moment bound gives almost-sure convergence even across arbitrarily coupled matrix sizes. The independent direct-multiplication verifier exhausts five labeled parameter configurations, including two nonabelian \(S_3\) cases, and checks finite moments.

**Originality — PASS against checked material, with residual priority risk.** The Rees-matrix product and characterization of idempotents are classical. Inspected complete texts of Quinn-Gregson's homogeneity treatment (arXiv:1908.10665), Radović–Mudrinski's congruence-commutator analysis (arXiv:2208.05237), Russell's 0-simple semigroup enumeration thesis, and Paulista's study of commuting graphs (arXiv:2412.06560) address different conclusions. The new assertion here concerns the *labeled random* fraction of ordered idempotent pairs with idempotent product, an exact Pearson collision identity, finite variance, and universal non-Gaussian fluctuation law. Queried records on unrelated idempotent factorizations and occupancy or chi-square statistics do not entail this specific semigroup identity. A possible unindexed equivalent in random-sandwich-matrix or orthodox-semigroup literature remains a priority risk; search absence is not proof of publication priority.

**Value — PASS.** The probability that idempotents are closed under a randomly selected multiplication is a natural near-orthodoxy statistic for finite completely simple semigroups. The exact lower bound and equality classification, group-universal finite moments, and a nondegenerate chi-square limiting law demonstrate what aspects of the random semigroup are structural and which fluctuate, even over nonabelian groups. The triple-ratio dependence makes the simultaneous fluctuation law more than an invocation of a single-bin multinomial limit. This is a motivated infinite-family invariant, not a selected small numerical recomputation.

**Limitations and risks.** Only completely simple no-zero Rees matrices with fixed row number and fixed finite group are covered; matrices are sampled by labeled independent entries. Pearson chi-square convergence is classical probability and is used, not claimed as a new theorem. Results do not classify semigroup isomorphism types. Source priority cannot be conclusively established. Computational verification is finite, not a substitute for the proof.
