# Universal contraction dynamics from depth-one believed announcements

## Finding

**Theorem (complete support-dynamics representation and positive-fragment boundary).** Fix an integer \(k\ge1\), put \(T=\{0,1\}^k\), and write \(m=|T|=2^k\). Let \(p_1,\ldots,p_k\) be the propositional letters and give each world \(t\in T\) the indicated distinct truth assignment. For every \(S\subseteq T\), define a one-agent Kripke model \(M_S\) on the same world set with accessibility \(R_S=T\times S\). Thus every world sees exactly the worlds of \(S\).

For a fixed, announcement-free epistemic formula \(\varphi\), the **believed-public-announcement support operator** is
\[
F_\varphi(S)=S\cap\{t\in T:M_S,t\models\varphi\}.
\]
Under believed announcement, \(M_S\) changes exactly into \(M_{F_\varphi(S)}\). The complete class of operators \(F_\varphi\) is the class of all contractive set maps \(F:2^T\longrightarrow2^T\) satisfying \(F(S)\subseteq S\). **Modal depth one suffices** for every such operator. Distinct operators are identified by equality on *all* \(2^m\) supports; their exact number is
\[
\boxed{2^{m2^{m-1}}}.\tag{1}
\]

For the **positive-diamond depth-one fragment**, generated by propositional literals and constants under \(\wedge,\vee\) and diamond tests \(\Diamond\theta\) where \(\theta\) is propositional, with no negation of any modal subformula, the realizable operators are precisely the **isotone contractive maps**:
\[
F(S)\subseteq S,\qquad S\subseteq U\Longrightarrow F(S)\subseteq F(U).
\]
They number
\[
\boxed{D_{m-1}^{m}},\tag{2}
\]
where \(D_r\) is the classical Dedekind number counting all increasing Boolean functions of \(r\) inputs, including the two constant functions. The modal-depth-zero fragment realizes precisely \(S\mapsto S\cap A\) for fixed \(A\subseteq T\), giving exactly \(2^m\) operators. In particular the expressivity inclusions between depth zero, positive depth one and unrestricted depth one are strict for \(k\ge1\).

Every descending support orbit stabilizes after at most \(m\) strict updates. That bound is attained starting at \(M_T\) by a **positive-diamond depth-one** announcement. Moreover every \(M_S\) is reachable from the initial \(M_T\) by one factual announcement, so the operator comparison on all supports concerns accessible models, not artificially postulated inputs.

For example, when \(k=1\), the three operator counts (propositional, positive diamond, unrestricted) are \(4,9,16\). When \(k=2\), they are \(16,160000,4294967296\).

## Assumptions and scope

Believed public announcements are **arrow-target restrictions**, not ordinary truthful public announcements (which delete worlds). Following the one-agent instance of the source's Definition 5, the update of a model \(M=(T,R,V)\) by \(\varphi\) keeps all worlds and valuations and replaces \(R\) by
\[
R'=R\cap\bigl(T\times\llbracket\varphi\rrbracket_M\bigr).
\]
The proposition letters have the fixed valuations \(V(p_i)=\{t:t_i=1\}\), and no new propositional letters are introduced by the announcing formula. Other unused propositional letters, if part of the ambient language, are fixed to false. The family \(M_S\) has \(\mathsf{K45}\) accessibility for every support, including the empty one, and \(M_T\) itself has universal \(\mathsf{S5}\) accessibility. No seriality or reflexivity is claimed for intermediate supports.

Equivalence of announcing formulas here means equality of their induced **support transformations on this fixed family**, not global equivalence at every arbitrary Kripke model. There are no formula-size bounds: the constructive depth-one formula may be exponentially or doubly exponentially long in the number of atomic letters. The positive fragment allows negative *atomic* literals, but no negative occurrence of a diamond test.

## Proof

**Step 1: closure and reachability of the support family.** Each \(M_S\) has \(R_S=T\times S\). Its believed-announcement update is
\[
R'_S=R_S\cap\bigl(T\times\{t:M_S,t\models\varphi\}\bigr)=T\times F_\varphi(S).
\]
This proves that every realized operator is contractive. If \(\alpha_S\) is the propositional disjunction of the exact type formulas for all \(t\in S\), announcing \(\alpha_S\) once from \(M_T\) yields exactly \(M_S\). (For \(S=\varnothing\), use \(\bot\).)

**Step 2: universal realization by modal-depth-one formulas.** For every \(t\in T\), let \(\tau_t\) be the conjunction of the \(k\) literals selecting precisely valuation \(t\). For each \(A\subseteq T\), define the depth-one support test
\[
B_A=\bigwedge_{u\in A}\Diamond\tau_u\;\wedge\;\bigwedge_{u\in T\setminus A}\neg\Diamond\tau_u.
\]
Empty conjunctions are true. As each \(\tau_u\) holds at exactly one world, and all worlds in \(M_S\) see exactly \(S\), we have, **at every world \(t\)**,
\[
M_S,t\models B_A\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A=S.
\]
Now fix *any* map \(F:2^T\to2^T\) with \(F(S)\subseteq S\). Define
\[
\varphi_F=\bigvee_{t\in T}\left(\tau_t\wedge\bigvee_{A\subseteq T:\;t\in F(A)}B_A\right).
\]
At world \(t\) in \(M_S\), the sole surviving outer disjunct is indexed by \(t\), and its inner support test holds exactly when \(t\in F(S)\). Therefore \(F_{\varphi_F}=F\) on **all supports simultaneously**. Every diamond in \(\varphi_F\) applies to a propositional formula, so the modal depth is one. Combining this with Step 1 proves the universal classification.

**Step 3: exact count of unrestricted operators.** For every pair \((t,S)\) with \(t\in S\), independently decide whether \(t\in F(S)\). No other output bits are available to a contractive operator. The number of such pairs is \(m2^{m-1}\), since each \(t\) belongs to exactly half of all subsets of \(T\). Different choices give different support maps, and Step 2 realizes all of them. This proves (1).

**Step 4: exact positive-diamond boundary.** For each fixed \(t\), the propositional literals have truth values independent of the support \(S\), and every positive diamond \(\Diamond\theta\) (\(\theta\) propositional) is true exactly when the current support contains some world satisfying \(\theta\). It is therefore increasing under \(S\subseteq U\). Induction under conjunction and disjunction shows that the truth of any positive-diamond depth-one formula at fixed \(t\) is increasing in \(S\). Consequently \(F_\varphi\) is isotone as well as contractive.

Conversely, fix any isotone contractive \(F\), and for each \(t\in T\) define the Boolean function \(f_t\) on \(2^{T\setminus\{t\}}\) by
\[
f_t(B)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad t\in F(B\cup\{t\}).
\]
Each \(f_t\) is monotone by isotonicity. Conversely any arbitrary independent selection of \(m\) such monotone Boolean functions defines exactly one isotone contractive \(F\), by setting \(t\in F(S)\) precisely when \(t\in S\) and \(f_t(S\setminus\{t\})=1\). Let \(\mathcal A_t\) denote the inclusion-minimal supports where \(f_t=1\). Its positive disjunctive normal form is
\[
f_t(B)=\bigvee_{A\in\mathcal A_t}\bigwedge_{u\in A}[u\in B].
\]
The constant-zero and constant-one cases correspond respectively to an empty outer disjunction and an empty inner conjunction. The single positive-diamond depth-one formula
\[
\varphi_{F}^{+}=\bigvee_{t\in T}\left(\tau_t\wedge\bigvee_{A\in\mathcal A_t}\bigwedge_{u\in A}\Diamond\tau_u\right)
\]
induces exactly \(F\). There are \(D_{m-1}\) choices for each \(f_t\), independently for every one of \(m\) coordinates. This proves (2), including surjectivity onto every isotone contraction.

**Step 5: depth-zero operators and sharp orbit length.** A propositional formula is true at a fixed world \(t\) independently of \(S\), hence induces \(F(S)=S\cap A\), where \(A\) is its fixed truth set of types. Every \(A\subseteq T\) is definable by a propositional disjunction of exact types, proving the depth-zero count. Such an operator is idempotent, hence no more than one strict update occurs.

Every contractive orbit \(S_{r+1}=F(S_r)\) descends through subsets of the \(m\)-element set \(T\), so at most \(m\) updates can be strict. For sharpness, index \(T=\{t_0,\ldots,t_{m-1}\}\) in any order and choose
\[
\psi=\bigvee_{i=1}^{m-1}\left(\tau_{t_i}\wedge\Diamond\left(\bigvee_{0\le j<i}\tau_{t_j}\right)\right).
\]
The update retains \(t_i\) exactly when it belongs to the current support and some lower-indexed type is also present. Starting from \(S_0=T\), the minimum-index member disappears at each strict update:
\[
S_r=\{t_r,t_{r+1},\ldots,t_{m-1}\},\quad0\le r\le m,
\]
where \(S_m=\varnothing\). Each of the \(m\) updates is strict. The formula belongs to the positive-diamond depth-one fragment. This also exhibits strict separation from the idempotent propositional fragment.

For \(m\ge2\), the positive fragment does not realize arbitrary contractions: e.g. removing \(t_1\) when \(t_0\) is present but keeping it when \(t_0\) is absent is not isotone. The strict count inequalities also follow from \(2<D_{m-1}<2^{2^{m-1}}\) for \(m\ge2\). This completes the proof.

## Verification

The standalone \(\texttt{checker.py}\) implements the ordinary Kripke truth definition for atoms, Boolean connectives and the diamond modality, with accessibility \(R_S=T\times S\), and implements the believed-announcement support update directly. Run \(\texttt{python3 checker.py}\) with Python 3 and no third-party packages. The checker reports \(\texttt{ALL\_CHECKS\_OK 7280}\) after the following diagnostics:

- Complete reconstruction for all \(16\) contractive operators when \(k=1\), checking all four supports per operator.
- Independent random contractive operator samples when \(k=1,2\), compared against explicit announcing-formula evaluations on every support.
- Independent enumeration of the first Dedekind numbers \(D_0=2,D_1=3,D_2=6,D_3=20\) and complete isotone-map enumeration on supports of size at most three, checking the \(D_{m-1}^{m}\) formula.
- Direct semantic simulation of sharp descending orbits for \(k=0,1,2,3,4\), including the \(16\)-type example.

The general classification is proved symbolically in the preceding steps; no finite experiment substitutes for its universal quantifiers.

## Relationship to prior work

Yamada, *Eventual and Strong Eventual Notions in Public Announcements*, arXiv:2609.24006 (first public 21 September 2026), supplies the exact believed-public-announcement update in Definition 5. Its Lemma 6 proves single-agent \(\mathsf{K45}\) stabilization through successive loss of finitely many propositional valuation types, but does not classify the full set of simultaneously realizable transformations across every support, their positive-diamond subfamily, or their operator counts. The earlier *Sharp state-count stabilization bound for finite believed announcements* proves an all-frames finite-state bound and sharpness for a different general-frame construction. The \(m\)-update upper bound itself is **not presented as a new standalone theorem**.

The standard Dedekind numbers enumerate monotone Boolean functions; this known enumeration is invoked in the precise count (2), not rediscovered. A separate finite height-two modal-depth enumeration has a matching numerical factor \(2^{m2^{m-1}}\) but concerns formula-equivalence classes on reflexive antisymmetric transitive partial-order frames, not believed-announcement support transformations on the \(\mathsf{K45}\) family \(R_S=T\times S\); **the bare numerical factor is not claimed independently original**. The present mathematical distinction is that every contracting transformation is realized **as an actual repeated announcement on the same finite model family**, with a sharp positive-diamond/Dedekind boundary and a witness orbit from an initially \(\mathsf{S5}\) model.

Kuijer, *The Expressivity of Update Logics*, discusses the expressivity of related dynamic epistemic update languages; it does not establish the same support-contraction transform classification in the material inspected. Search coverage is not a proof of priority; the residual risk of an equivalent older presentation is stated below.

## Limitations

The theorem is for one agent, finitely many distinguished Boolean valuation types, and the **fixed complete type-support family** \(R_S=T\times S\); it makes no claim that arbitrary contractive maps are realizable on arbitrary finite frames or that intermediate models remain serial, reflexive or \(\mathsf{S5}\). After a nontrivial believed announcement, the relation is generally merely \(\mathsf{K45}\). Equivalence is equality of support operators, not equivalence of modal formulas on all frames. The full binary presentation of an arbitrary contractive operator is inherently large, and formula length is not bounded. The result does not prove that a given formula's operator can be computed efficiently in the number of atomic letters.

The tight stabilization bound and Boolean-function counting methods have antecedents; only their integration into the full modal-update dynamics and the exact positive fragment is assessed as a separate scientific claim. It remains possible that a theorem in earlier dynamic-epistemic-logic or semigroup literature proves an equivalent transform representation under different terminology. Independent external validation has not been performed.
