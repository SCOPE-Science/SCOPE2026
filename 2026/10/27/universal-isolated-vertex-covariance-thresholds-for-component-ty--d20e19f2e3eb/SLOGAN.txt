For every size \(2\le k<n\), the covariance between isolated-vertex count and any nonnegative statistic of size-\(k\) connected components in \(G(n,p)\) changes sign at the same exact threshold \(p_{n,k}=1-(1-k/n)^{1/k}\), regardless of component topology. These thresholds increase with \(k\) and bridge the \(1/n\) and \((\log n)/n\) scales.
