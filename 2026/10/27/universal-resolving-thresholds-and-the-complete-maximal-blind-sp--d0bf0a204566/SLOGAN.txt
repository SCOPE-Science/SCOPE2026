In every \(q\)-ary Hamming graph \(K_q^{\square d}\), the size at which **every** landmark set resolves has an exact formula, and every largest failing set is one of a small family of explicit coordinate cylinders. The ternary alphabet is exceptional: single-coordinate hyperplanes and two-coordinate permutation graphs tie, with exactly \(3d+6\binom d2\) labeled maximal failures.
