For every regular Euclidean polygon, the sharp real-linear simultaneous Lipschitz extension norm from the vertices to all edge midpoints equals the quotient of the total vertex-to-vertex and midpoint-to-vertex chord-distance sums. A universal positive kernel attains the value for every real Banach target, and its midpoint-layer transport is nonexpansive; the proof combines a cyclic distance-matrix inverse, strict Ptolemy inequalities and a positive convolution factorization.
