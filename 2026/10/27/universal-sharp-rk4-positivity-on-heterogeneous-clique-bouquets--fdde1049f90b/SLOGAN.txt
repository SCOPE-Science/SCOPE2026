On every heterogeneous bouquet of complete graphs sharing one hub, classical RK4 preserves nonnegative diffusion states exactly up to the Euclidean spectral-stability limit \(h=\rho/n\), where \(\rho\) is the negative-real-axis RK4 stability radius and \(n\) is the total vertex count. This sharp threshold ignores all individual petal sizes, holds even for nonmultipartite networks with triangles, and fails irreversibly through hub-to-leaf transitions.
