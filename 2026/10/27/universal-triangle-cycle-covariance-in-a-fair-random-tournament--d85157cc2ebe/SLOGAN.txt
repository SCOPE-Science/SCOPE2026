In a uniformly random tournament, the covariance between cyclic triangles and simple directed \(k\)-cycles is exactly \(k/4\) times the expected number of those cycles, for every \(3\le k\le n\). This gives an all-length, finite-sample relative variance lower bound \(\operatorname{Var}(C_k)/(\mathbb EC_k)^2\ge k^2/(3\binom n3)\).
