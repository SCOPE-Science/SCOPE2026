# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The multiplication operator is a two-by-two matrix over a truncated discrete valuation ring. Raising it to any power gives two diagonal entries and one explicit off-diagonal entry; invariant-factor valuations recover every vector-space rank. Valuation fibers have exact cardinalities. The sharp maximal-block criterion follows directly from the highest potentially nonzero power. The standalone exhaustive checker independently tested twelve finite configurations, including a nonprime field; it is corroboration rather than an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS within investigated coverage.** General Jordan-type loci (Iarrobino–Macias Marques–McDaniel), linear-form complete-intersection Jordan partitions (Altafi–Iarrobino–Khatami), and the monomial strong-Lefschetz existence classification (Nicklasson) are prior work. The claimed contribution is the simultaneous all-power, all-radical exact finite-field census for this specific complete-intersection family. A distinct published result on Jordan-block lifts over dual numbers counts nilpotency indices for arbitrary matrix lifts, not multiplication operators in this algebra. The precise full-distribution formula was not found in the examined claims or targeted sources. Residual risk: earlier Smith-normal-form, modular tensor-product, or Jordan-strata literature could imply the exact formula without the present terminology.

**Value — PASS.** Jordan type is a natural invariant of Artinian algebras. The formula gives every stratum's finite-field size for a growing family, not just the generic partition or a finite example; its arithmetic behavior distinguishes the divisible and nondivisible characteristics uniformly, including the exact proportion of maximally Lefschetz-type elements. The existence portion of that latter statement was previously known and is not itself asserted as novel. No generalization to arbitrary bivariate complete intersections is claimed.
