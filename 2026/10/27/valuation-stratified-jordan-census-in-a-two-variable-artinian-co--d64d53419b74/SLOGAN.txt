Every radical element of \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x^2,y^n)\) has its full multiplication Jordan partition determined by the two \(y\)-adic valuations in its expression \(f(y)+xg(y)\); an exact weighted formula enumerates all partitions over every finite field. The strong-Lefschetz Jordan partition occurs with probability \((1-q^{-1})^2\) when the field characteristic does not divide \(n\), and with probability zero otherwise.
