For a finite comb with a fixed total number of reflecting tooth sites, the most reproducible absorption time has variance \(\frac{2M^3}{3(\sum_{k=1}^{N}k^{-1/2})^2}+O_N(M^2)\), attained asymptotically by allocating tooth lengths in proportion to the inverse square root of their distance in backbone edges from the target. In the smallest two-tooth comb, the variance optimum departs from the mean-optimal placement at the exact budget \(M=3\).
