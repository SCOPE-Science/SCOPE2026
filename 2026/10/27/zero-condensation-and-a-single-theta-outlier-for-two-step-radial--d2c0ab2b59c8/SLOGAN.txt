For an explicit two-level radial Bergman weight whose inner mass grows like \(q^{-N}\), exactly \(N\) kernel zeros condense near the radial jump \(|z\overline w|=q\), while one isolated simple zero approaches \(-1/\tau\). The zero count within every fixed radius \(q<r<1\), \(r\ne1/\tau\), is eventually exactly \(N+\mathbf1_{\{r>1/\tau\}}\).
