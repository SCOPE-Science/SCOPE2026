The inclusive radius-\(2\) Euclidean Vietoris–Rips complex on the twenty-four type-\(D_4\) roots admits 736 explicit elementary simplicial collapses onto a ten-vertex triangulated \(3\)-sphere. An exact fifteen-tetrahedron ball shelling and an octahedral vertex link certify the PL sphere model, strengthening previously published homology data without claiming those Betti numbers as new.
