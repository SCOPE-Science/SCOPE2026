The growing-power multiplicativity defect \(T_{z^N}-(T_z)^N\) on every beta-weighted harmonic Bergman space is square-zero of rank \(N-1\), yet its norm converges to \(2^{-(\alpha+1)}\). Its complete nonzero singular-value distribution converges to the pushforward of Lebesgue measure under \(t\mapsto[t(1-t)]^{(\alpha+1)/2}\), giving exact sharp asymptotics for every Schatten quasi-norm.
