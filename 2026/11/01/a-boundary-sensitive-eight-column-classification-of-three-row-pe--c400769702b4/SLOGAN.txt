For the first four even strip lengths \(n=2,4,6,8\), the perfect-matching complexes of \(P_3\square P_n\) and \(C_3\square P_n\) are exactly classified by complete acyclic discrete-Morse matchings. At eight columns, closing the transverse boundary changes a wedge of two 3-spheres into a wedge of twelve 3-spheres, providing an exact finite instance of the rectangular-grid sphere-wedge conjecture and a contrasting periodic-boundary case.
