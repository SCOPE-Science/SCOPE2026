On a clique with one pendant leaf, deterministic binary majority has a sharp parity switch: an even number of nonhub clique vertices yields a three-update consensus cutoff, while an odd number yields immediate primitive two-cycles and a two-update cutoff. The exceptional three-step or cyclic population is exactly \(2\binom{r}{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}\), asymptotically a fraction \(1/\sqrt{2\pi r}\) of initial configurations.
