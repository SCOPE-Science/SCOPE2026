The first smooth superspecial members of the marked bielliptic genus-two pencil \(y^2=(x^2-1)(x^4-t)\), among odd characteristics, occur at characteristic \(17\), precisely at \(t^2-t+1=0\); both elliptic quotients then have supersingular \(j\)-invariant \(8\). The four remaining nonordinary marked fibers have rank one, yielding a complete three-stratum Newton classification with explicit arithmetic fields of definition.
