In the pure ternary graph of modular addition without named constants, two-variable first-order logic remembers exactly the power of two dividing the modulus and whether its odd part supplies nonzero doubling two-cycles and longer cycles. For each fixed two-adic valuation this produces four theories, with least representatives of orders \(2^a\), \(3\cdot2^a\), \(5\cdot2^a\), and \(9\cdot2^a\).
