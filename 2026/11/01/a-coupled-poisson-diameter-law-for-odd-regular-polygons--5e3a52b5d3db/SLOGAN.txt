For independent area-uniform samples in a fixed regular polygon with an odd number of sides, the diameter deficit times \(\sqrt N\) converges to a minimum of sums of *coupled* Poisson cone minima, with an explicit bivariate survival law. Its small-tail coefficient is \(1/[6m\sin^2(\pi/(2m))\sin^2(3\pi/(2m))]\), revealing a sharp odd–even contrast with the established even-polygon law.
