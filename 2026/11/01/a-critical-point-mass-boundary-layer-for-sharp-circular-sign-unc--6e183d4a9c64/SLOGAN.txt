A vanishing point mass of order \(1/m\) is the exact crossover scale for sharp sign uncertainty of degree-\(m-1\) trigonometric polynomials: the Haar \(m^{-1}\) radius changes to the fixed-atom \(m^{-3/2}\) radius. The full critical constant is the unique root of \(\tan(x/2)/(x/2)=1+1/c\), and the sharp extremizers have an explicit microscopic limiting profile.
