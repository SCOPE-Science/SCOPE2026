For the random radius-\(R\) ball-convex hull of \(n\) independent uniform points in the unit disc, the probability of **exactly two vertices** decays as \(n^{-3}\) with sharp coefficient \(\pi^4/8\) at \(R=1\), but as an explicit multiple of \(n^{-1/2}q_R^n\) for every fixed \(R>1\). The boundary layer \(R=1+\kappa/n^2\) has an explicit, strictly decreasing integral crossover profile; the underlying all-\(n\) moment identity is previously known.
