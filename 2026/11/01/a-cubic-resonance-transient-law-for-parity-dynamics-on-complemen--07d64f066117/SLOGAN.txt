For neighbor-sum evolution over a characteristic-two field on a complete graph with a spanning union of cycles removed, exact first-periodic-entry counts depend only on each deleted cycle's divisibility by three and its power-of-two factor. Removing two cycles of length \(3\cdot2^s\) from an even-order complete graph creates a sharp transient of length \(N/6\), attained by a proportion \(1-q^{-4}\) of all initial states even as the surviving graph becomes asymptotically complete.
