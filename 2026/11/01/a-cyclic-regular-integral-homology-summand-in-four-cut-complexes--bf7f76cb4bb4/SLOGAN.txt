For every \(n\ge13\), the ordinary four-cut complex of the third-power cycle \(C_n^3\) has a split rank-\(n\) integral homology summand one degree below top dimension: it comes from a cyclic regular module of explicit, integrally paired Alexander-dual sphere cycles. The exceptional twelve-vertex case still has a primitive integral class, and the entire family is nonshellable; the new content is the cyclic rank and the explicit integral certificates, not nonshellability alone.
