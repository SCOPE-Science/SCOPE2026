Balanced complete bipartite metrics with every matched cross-edge stretched to \(s\in[1,3]\) have a sharp three-branch Euclidean distortion formula, including a nontrivial interval of \(s\) on which the least distortion is exactly constant. Every optimal Hilbert embedding is rigid up to similarity, and its minimum affine dimension drops from \(2n-2\) to \(n-1\) at the first transition.
