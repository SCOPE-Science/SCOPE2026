# A four-step mod-two rotation block in square-grid matching homology

## Finding
Let \(G=P_3\square P_3\) be the simple \(3\times3\) nearest-neighbor grid, and let \(X=\mathcal M(G)\) be its ordinary matching complex. Its natural group \(D_4\) acts on the integral homology lattice \(L=\widetilde H_2(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^5\). Set \(r(i,j)=(j,2-i)\), a quarter-turn, and \(d(i,j)=(j,i)\), a diagonal reflection. On the five conjugacy classes \((1,r^{\pm1},r^2,\text{axis reflection},\text{diagonal reflection})\), the rational character on \(L\otimes\mathbb Q\) equals
\[
\chi=(5,1,1,-1,1),\qquad L\otimes\mathbb Q\cong A_1\oplus A_2\oplus B_2\oplus E,
\]
where \(A_1,A_2,B_2,E\) are the usual rational irreducible representations of the order-eight square dihedral group, with \(E\) the two-dimensional rotation representation. In contrast, the action of \(r\) on \(L\otimes\mathbf F_2\) has Jordan form
\[
\boxed{J_4(1)\oplus J_1(1).}
\]
Equivalently, the ranks over \(\mathbf F_2\) of \((r-I)^j\), for \(j=1,2,3,4\), are \((3,2,1,0)\). In particular, the rational decomposition displayed above **does not lift to an integral direct sum of \(D_4\)-stable sublattices** carrying the four rational irreducible constituents individually. This is an integral representation obstruction, not a claim that the five-dimensional integral module is indecomposable.

## Assumptions and scope
Vertices of \(G\) are the nine pairs \((i,j)\in\{0,1,2\}^2\); edges join vertices differing by one in exactly one coordinate. A matching is a set of pairwise vertex-disjoint grid edges and is a simplex of \(X\). Simplicial orientations order these twelve graph edges by scanning grid vertices in increasing row and column, adding a downward edge (if present) followed by a rightward edge (if present). Symmetries act on the oriented matching simplices with the induced permutation sign. All homology groups are reduced. The mod-two matrix in the proof depends on the specified computational cycle basis, whereas its Jordan form and the character are intrinsic.

## Proof
**1. Integral homotopy concentration.** The complete face counts by cardinality, including the empty face, are \((1,12,44,56,18)\). Apply the following deterministic acyclic matching to the face poset: scan graph-edge indices \(v=0,1,\ldots,11\), and in the currently unmatched faces pair each \(F\) not containing \(v\) with \(F\cup\{v\}\) whenever both are still present. There are exactly 63 such pairs, including the pair from the empty face, and five remaining faces, all of cardinality three. To check acyclicity, for each paired lower face \(F\) with partner \(U\), create arcs \(F\to F'\) to all other codimension-one faces \(F'\) of \(U\) which are themselves paired lower faces. A complete topological-sort check removes all 63 vertices of this directed graph, proving the absence of closed gradient paths in every dimension. Delete the empty-face pair, which cannot create a cycle: in the ordinary face poset this leaves one critical zero-cell, the same five critical two-cells, and no other critical cells. Elementary discrete Morse theory therefore gives \(X\simeq\bigvee^5 S^2\) and integral homology \(L\cong\mathbb Z^5\) without torsion. The 131 augmented faces are exhausted, not sampled.

**2. Rational character.** For any symmetry \(g\) of a finite simplicial complex, the reduced Hopf trace formula computes \(\widetilde\Lambda(g)\) by summing signed contributions of invariant oriented faces, including \(-1\) for the empty face. Enumeration of all 130 nonempty matchings gives, respectively, \((5,1,1,-1,1)\) for the five \(D_4\) classes in the statement; the standalone verification enumerates every face and permutation sign. Since the homology established in step 1 is supported only in even degree two, these Lefschetz numbers equal traces on \(L\otimes\mathbb Q\). The five standard \(D_4\) irreducible characters give \(A_1\oplus A_2\oplus B_2\oplus E\) by the exact class-weighted character inner product, with respective class sizes \((1,2,1,2,2)\).

**3. Exact mod-two chain calculation.** Enumerate all 56 oriented degree-two simplices (three-edge matchings), their 44 codimension-one faces, and all 18 degree-three simplices (four-edge matchings). Over \(\mathbf F_2\), the resulting boundary maps have ranks \(\operatorname{rank}\partial_2=33\) and \(\operatorname{rank}\partial_3=18\), so \(\ker\partial_2/\operatorname{im}\partial_3\) has dimension \(56-33-18=5\). The verifier constructs a deterministic cycle basis from exact binary column elimination, places the 18 independent tetrahedral boundaries first, and then appends five independent kernel cycles. In that last five-vector quotient basis, the rotation and diagonal-reflection matrices have **row bitmask** presentations
\[
R=(28,1,19,11,13),\qquad D=(8,11,13,1,19),
\]
where integer bit \(j\), numbered from zero, is the entry in column \(j\) of the specified row. These are explicit five-by-five binary matrices, not numeric evidence inferred from homology ranks. Direct exact matrix multiplication gives \(R^4=I\), \(D^2=I\), and \(DRD=R^{-1}\). Moreover the matrices of the first four positive powers of \(R-I\) have ranks \((3,2,1,0)\), yielding precisely \(J_4(1)\oplus J_1(1)\).

**4. Integral nonsplitting.** If \(L\) split over \(\mathbb Z[D_4]\) into four integral summands whose respective rationalizations were \(A_1,A_2,B_2,E\), their ranks would be \(1,1,1,2\). Reduction modulo two would preserve the direct sum because the summands are free abelian. Each rank-one summand is fixed by \(r\) after reduction, since its integral rotation eigenvalue is \(+1\) or \(-1\). The rank-two \(E\) summand reduces an order-four integral matrix whose rational eigenvalues are \(\pm i\): its characteristic polynomial \(t^2+1\) reduces to \((t+1)^2\), so it has at least one fixed vector over \(\mathbf F_2\). Thus a split integral model would have \(\dim\ker(R-I)\ge4\). The computed Jordan form has fixed dimension \(2\), contradiction.

## Verification
From the package directory run `python3 verify.py`, using only the Python 3 standard library. It builds all 131 augmented faces from the twelve grid edges, verifies the cardinality vector, constructs all 63 pairs and checks the complete gradient digraph for cycles, enumerates all five signed Lefschetz traces, builds both mod-two boundary maps, computes quotient homology coordinates, reconstructs the stated \(R\) and \(D\), tests the group relations, and computes all four nilpotent-power ranks. The expected final line begins `r-I power ranks: [3, 2, 1, 0]`. Every test is finite, exhaustive and based on the formal definition of a grid matching; no unexamined external certificate is needed. The exact chain calculations provide the finite proof rather than a heuristic experiment.

## Relationship to prior work
Chauhan, Sharma and Shukla's September 2026 manuscript treats the same \(3\times n\) matching complexes and includes the nonequivariant case \(n=3\), but it does not state the integral action of the square's quarter-turn or the Jordan type over \(\mathbf F_2\). Published calculations of the two reflections and the half-turn on arbitrary \(3\times n\) grids give *alternating rational Lefschetz numbers*, not this 90-degree rotation or its two-primary lattice obstruction. For \(n=3\), their values agree with the corresponding three entries of the displayed character. Matsushita's older small-grid theorem concerns the width-two family, not the square's integral symmetry module. A 2021 analysis of width-three grids was retracted and supplies no premise here. Thus the already known wedge type and rank five are used as context and independently verified, not presented as the mathematical novelty.

## Limitations
The theorem concerns a single canonical nine-vertex graph and does not assert an all-width classification, determine the integral \(D_4\)-module up to isomorphism, or establish indecomposability of that entire module. The unsplitting statement excludes only direct sums respecting all four specified rational constituents. Exact enumeration makes the finite proof complete, but does not rule out equivalent unindexed computations elsewhere. Independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
