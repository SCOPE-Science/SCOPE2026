For balanced Sobolev orthogonal polynomials on the circle with a single positive Fourier-harmonic derivative weight, the leading monic squared norm is independent of the harmonic order \(m\), but its first finite-degree correction is exactly \(2m|a|^2\lambda^2/[n((1+\lambda)^2-4|a|^2\lambda^2)]\). An exact tridiagonal Gram reduction and a uniformly contractive asymptotic recurrence prove the \(O(n^{-2})\) remainder for every fixed \(m\), \(|a|<1/2\), and \(\lambda>0\).
