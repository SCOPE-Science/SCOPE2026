For the ordinary sixteen-cut complex of the squared 21-cycle, the exact integral homotopy type is \(S^3\vee\bigvee^{637}S^4\). Its 42 dihedral symmetries fix the three-dimensional homology generator, while the fourth-degree rational character has traces \(637\) at the identity, \(7\) at the two order-three rotations, \(0\) at other nonidentity rotations and \(-7\) at reflections; the quotient has rational Betti ranks \(1\) and \(12\) in degrees three and four.
