An angular-cone Toeplitz commutator with Gaussian Fock creation has no eigenvalues but has both ordinary and Fredholm essential spectrum equal to the exact disk \(\{|\lambda|\le\sqrt{(1-t)/(2\pi)}\}\). It is an orthogonal sum of compact quasinilpotent shifts, and at \(t=4/5\) its operator norm strictly exceeds its essential spectral radius.
