For the Pelikan random map near its intermittent threshold, an exact positive-part binomial identity gives the finite-time mean coordinate. When the expanding-branch probability is \(1/2+\alpha/\sqrt n\), the scaled mean converges to the explicit Gaussian crossover \(\sqrt{2/\pi}e^{-2\alpha^2}+4\alpha\Phi(2\alpha)\), interpolating between contraction and expansion.
