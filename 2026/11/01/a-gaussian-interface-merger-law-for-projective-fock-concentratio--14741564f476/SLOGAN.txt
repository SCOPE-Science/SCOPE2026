In homogeneous degree-\(n\) Gaussian Fock quantization, the spectral projection defect of a projective latitude band is exactly half the expected symmetric-difference size of two coupled binomial intervals. When the two band boundaries coalesce at separation comparable to \(n^{-1/2}\), its normalized defect has an explicit, strictly concave Gaussian error-function crossover connecting zero width to the separated-interface constant.
