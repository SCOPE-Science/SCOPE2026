For growing rectangular windows in analytic Fock space, the third localization spectral cumulant converges exponentially fast to the universal positive constant \(\frac{3}{2\pi^2}(\sqrt5-1-\log\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2})\), independently of the aspect ratio. This is also the limiting third number cumulant for the infinite complex Ginibre process and exposes a nonzero rectangular-corner contribution after exact cancellation of the area and perimeter terms.
