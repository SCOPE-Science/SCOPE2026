For two unordered collision-free points on the complete graph \(K_7\), the \(70\) natural disjoint-triangle torus classes have \(35\) elementary four-term relations, but these generate an index-\(2\) sublattice of all integral relations. One alternating ten-term six-vertex relation removes the otherwise spurious \(\mathbb Z/2\) and completes the integral presentation of \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{35}\).
