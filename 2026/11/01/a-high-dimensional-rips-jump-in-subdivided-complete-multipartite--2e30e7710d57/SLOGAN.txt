For every once-subdivided complete multipartite graph with at least three parts of size at least two, the inclusive radius-three vertex-metric Vietoris--Rips complex is exactly a join of the original clique complex and its line-graph clique complex; it is a wedge of \(bc\) spheres of dimension \(k+2\), with \(b=\prod_i(a_i-1)\) and \(c=v-e+t-1\). This determines its complete integral homotopy filtration and all-field barcode; the radius-two stage is previously known.
