For uniform random points in a spherical cap whose radius differs from a hemisphere by \(\lambda\varepsilon\), nearly antipodal pairs have a universal all-dimensional boundary-layer configuration law: their rescaled signed latitudes and tangent displacement become uniform on an explicit cut-off unit-ball region. The resulting probability is \(\varepsilon^{q+1}\Psi_q(\lambda)+o(\varepsilon^{q+1})\), with an exact cubic-branch profile on \(\mathbb S^3\) and sharp support changes at \(\lambda=\pm1/2\).
