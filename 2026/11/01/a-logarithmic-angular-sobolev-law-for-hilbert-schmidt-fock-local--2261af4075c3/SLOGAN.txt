For disk-truncated analytic Fock Toeplitz operators with an arbitrary angular \(L^2\) profile, the Hilbert–Schmidt energy lost relative to the radial symbol grows logarithmically with an exact coefficient equal to one half of the angular \(H^1\) seminorm squared. The normalized defect is finite exactly for \(H^1\) profiles; a charge-\(k\) vortex contributes \((k^2/2)\log R+O_k(1)\).
