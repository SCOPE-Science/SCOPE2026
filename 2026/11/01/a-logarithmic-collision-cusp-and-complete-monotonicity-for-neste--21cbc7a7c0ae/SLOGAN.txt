For independent endpoint-uniform chords drawn on two nested concentric circles of radius ratio \(R\ge1\), the crossing probability is strictly completely monotone and the rescaled probability \(Rp(R)\) decreases sharply from \(1/3\) to \(8/\pi^3\). Coincident radii exhibit a singular geometric crossover: \(p(1+\varepsilon)=1/3-(2/\pi^2)\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)+O(\varepsilon)\), with an exact positive series and globally sharp bounds at every radius.
