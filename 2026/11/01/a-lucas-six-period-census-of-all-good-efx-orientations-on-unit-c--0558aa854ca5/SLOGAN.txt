For the labeled unit-valued simple cycle \(C_n\), exactly \(L_n+2\cos(n\pi/3)\) of its \(2^n\) edge orientations satisfy all-good EFX, with \(L_n\) the Lucas number. Fair orientations are precisely cyclic binary words without consecutive bit changes; their random-orientation probability decays as \(((1+\sqrt5)/4)^n\).
