For every positive symmetric competition matrix and every positive all-to-all mutation rate, a nonconservative Lotka–Volterra system has an exact coupled mass–frequency entropy potential whose increase is the sum of squared population-speed terms. Every nonzero nonnegative trajectory converges to a single positive equilibrium, even when several competing equilibria coexist; the result gives a global answer on the exchange-symmetric slice of the mutated May–Leonard model.
