The complexes of nonattacking bishops on the first three positive even square boards have integral homotopy types \(\mathcal B_2\simeq S^1\), \(\mathcal B_4\simeq\bigvee^9S^3\), and \(\mathcal B_6\simeq\bigvee^{196}S^5\vee\bigvee^{84}S^6\vee\bigvee^9S^7\). Thus the six-by-six board is the first even square board whose bishop-placement complex has reduced homology in more than one dimension.
