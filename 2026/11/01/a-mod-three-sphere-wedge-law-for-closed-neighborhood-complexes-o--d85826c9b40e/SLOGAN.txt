Deleting any spanning bipartite two-factor with cycles of length at least six from \(K_{n,n}\), for \(n\ge7\), produces a closed-neighborhood complex homotopy equivalent to \(n^2-4n+1\) two-spheres together with \(2^{c+1}\) spheres of dimension \(n-2-\sum_j\lfloor(m_j+1)/3\rfloor\), where \(c\) counts cycles whose half-length is divisible by three. Thus graphs with the same vertex and edge counts exhibit distinct, explicitly classified integral homotopy types according to their forbidden cycle partitions.
