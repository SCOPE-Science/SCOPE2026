For the least missing odd part in the complete support of an overpartition, the weighted sum differs from the overpartition count modulo \(16\) by an explicit two-valued correction governed by \(\lfloor\sqrt{n-1}\rfloor\), \(n-1\), and \(\lfloor(n-1)/3\rfloor\). Modulo \(8\), its coefficients have a complete four-way classification supported on odd squares, even squares, twice squares, and their complement.
