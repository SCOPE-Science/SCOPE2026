The perfect-matching complex of the dodecahedral graph is homotopy equivalent to the Moore space \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\) wedged with twenty three-spheres, revealing an integral two-torsion class in degree two. An exact acyclic matching and a determinant-two boundary minor prove \(\mathcal P(\operatorname{GP}(10,2))\simeq M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{20}S^3\).
