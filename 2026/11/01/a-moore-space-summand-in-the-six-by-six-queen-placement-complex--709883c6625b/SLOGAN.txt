The simplicial complex of nonattacking queen placements on the ordinary six-by-six board has homotopy type \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{19}S^3\), giving the first integral torsion among the square-board queen complexes of sizes one through six. Its full square-dihedral rational third-homology character is \((19,-5,7,-5,1,1,-5,-5)\), with one invariant line.
