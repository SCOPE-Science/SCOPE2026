# A one-bar avalanche filtration from two adjacent grain pairs on a directed path

## Finding
For every pair of integers \(k\geq1\) and \(n\geq k+4\), consider the directed path of \(n\) nonsink vertices \(v_0\to v_1\to\cdots\to v_{n-1}\), with one outgoing edge from \(v_{n-1}\) to an absorbing sink. Start with one grain at each of \(v_0,v_1,v_{k+2},v_{k+3}\), and none elsewhere. All unstable nonsink vertices fire once simultaneously in each round, where instability means holding at least one grain. Let \(A_t\) be the simplicial complex generated by all firing supports in rounds \(0,1,\ldots,t\), and let \(A_t\) remain constant after the configuration empties.

Then \(A_t\) is contractible for \(0\leq t\leq k\), and \(A_t\simeq S^1\) for every \(t\geq k+1\). Every transition inclusion except \(A_k\hookrightarrow A_{k+1}\) is a homotopy equivalence. Consequently, for every field \(\Bbbk\), the complete persistent homology has exactly one degree-zero bar \([0,\infty)\) and one degree-one bar \([k+1,\infty)\), with no other bars. The same statement holds integrally at the level of homology groups and transition isomorphisms.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph is a consistently oriented directed path, not a directed cycle, and the terminal sink is omitted from the vertex set of the avalanche complex. The initial configuration has four binary grains in the two consecutive pairs, separated by exactly \(k\) empty nonsink sites. A firing support is the set of nonsink sites firing in a single parallel round; the avalanche complex is the downward simplicial closure of the supports observed up through the stated integer time. No claim is made for asynchronous firing, different sandpile thresholds, or arbitrary initial configurations.

## Proof
Write site indices as \(0,\ldots,n-1\), and put \(U=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\). Since every nonsink vertex has one outgoing edge, each grain moves exactly one site to the right at each parallel step until absorbed. The initial grains never collide, because all move at the same rate. Thus the round-\(t\) firing support is exactly
\[
F_t=\{t,t+1,t+k+2,t+k+3\}\cap U\qquad(0\leq t\leq n-1).
\]
It is empty for \(t\geq n\). Write \(A_t=\bigcup_{s=0}^t\Delta(F_s)\), where \(\Delta(E)\) denotes the full simplex on \(E\). In particular \(A_0\) is a tetrahedron and is contractible.

For \(1\leq t\leq n-1\), set \(J_t=A_{t-1}\cap\Delta(F_t)\). Intersections of two full simplices are full simplices on the intersection of their vertex sets. Comparing the four potential shifted sites of \(F_t\) with earlier supports gives the following exhaustive description (a simplex on a singleton is just a point):

* If \(1\leq t\leq k\), then \(J_t=\Delta(\{t,t+k+2\}\cap U)\).
* If \(t=k+1\), then \(J_t=\Delta(\{t,t+k+2\}\cap U)\,\sqcup\,\Delta(\{t+1\})\). Both components are nonempty and disjoint.
* If \(k+2\leq t\leq n-2\), then
\[
J_t=\Delta(\{t,t+1\})\cup\Delta(\{t,t+k+2\}\cap U),
\]
  a contractible tree consisting of one or two edges meeting at \(t\).
* If \(t=n-1\), then \(F_t=\{n-1\}\subseteq F_{n-2}\), so \(A_t=A_{t-1}\).

To see that these are all intersections, consider the difference \(d=t-s\) for an earlier index \(s\). Nonempty overlap is possible only when \(d=1,k+1,k+2,k+3\). At \(d=1\) it lies in \(\{t,t+k+2\}\); at \(d=k+1\) it is \(\{t+1\}\); at \(d=k+2\) it is \(\{t,t+1\}\); and at \(d=k+3\) it is \(\{t\}\). These overlap descriptions hold after intersecting with \(U\); furthermore the relevant earlier indices must be nonnegative. This gives precisely the four cases above. In particular, the second component first appears exactly at \(t=k+1\), while the connecting edge \(\{t,t+1\}\) first appears at \(t=k+2\).

Each \(A_t\) is obtained from \(A_{t-1}\) by gluing the simplex \(\Delta(F_t)\) along \(J_t\). Simplicial inclusions are cofibrations, so their ordinary pushouts model homotopy pushouts. If \(J_t\) is nonempty and contractible, attaching the simplex preserves the homotopy type of \(A_{t-1}\) and induces a homotopy equivalence from \(A_{t-1}\). This applies at every step other than \(t=k+1\). At that exceptional step, \(J_{k+1}\) consists of two contractible components; since both \(A_k\) and \(\Delta(F_{k+1})\) are contractible, the homotopy pushout has the homotopy type \(\Sigma S^0\simeq S^1\). This proves the asserted homotopy types and the inclusion statements. Taking homology over any field or over \(\mathbb Z\) gives the persistence conclusion.

## Verification
The self-contained `verify.py` independently constructs the parallel-firing configurations and all simplicial faces. It checks the displayed firing-support formula and each attaching-intersection formula for every \(1\leq k\leq12\) and \(k+4\leq n\leq k+19\): 192 parameter pairs, including clipping at the absorbing sink. It also independently computes mod-two simplicial boundary ranks at every filtration level for \(1\leq k\leq6\) and small admissible \(n\). Execute `python3 verify.py`; no third-party packages are required. These finite tests supplement, but do not replace, the all-parameter proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
Riihimäki and Smith introduce avalanche homology and prove, for a different three-grain configuration \((1,1,0^k,1,0,\ldots,0)\) on a directed path, a final complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{n-k-2}S^1\) (Proposition 3.9 of arXiv:2606.26786v1). The present four-grain configuration adds one grain immediately after the isolated right-hand grain, yet its terminal first Betti number is always exactly one, instead of growing with \(n\). The argument also determines its entire parallel-time persistence barcode, not just the terminal homotopy type. General persistence definitions and the existing three-grain proposition do not directly imply this two-block result.

## Limitations
The theorem applies only to the stated two-pair configurations on directed paths and the stated parallel threshold-one sandpile convention. It does not classify general binary configurations or longer blocks, nor does it determine maps between filtrations from different initial states. Independent review and a universal absence-of-prior-work guarantee are not claimed.
