For synchronous binary majority dynamics on a crown graph, odd shore size permits exactly one primitive two-cycle orbit, while even shore size has \(\bigl(\binom{n}{n/2}+1\bigr)\bigl(\binom{n}{n/2}+2\bigr)/2\) such orbits. Nevertheless a uniformly random state reaches a two-cycle with probability tending to one-half in both parity classes—exponentially fast from below for odd size, but only at square-root rate from above for even size.
