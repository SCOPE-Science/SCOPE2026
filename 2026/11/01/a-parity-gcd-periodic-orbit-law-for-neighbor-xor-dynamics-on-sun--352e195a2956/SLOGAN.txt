For synchronous binary neighbor-XOR on every sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\), the exact number of configurations returning after \(t\) updates is \(2^{2\gcd(n,t)-\mathbf1_{\{n,t\ \mathrm{odd}\}}}\). All configurations are periodic, with sharp maximal period \(n\) for even \(n\) and \(2n\) for odd \(n\), and Möbius inversion gives every primitive-orbit count.
