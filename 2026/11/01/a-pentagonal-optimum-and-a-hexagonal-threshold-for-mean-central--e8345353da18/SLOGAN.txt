For a Haar-uniform central plane, the expected vertex count of a right regular \(n\)-gonal prism (\(n\ge5\)) has an exact two-angle formula. A pentagonal base has a unique maximal mean at half-height-to-circumradius ratio \((3+\sqrt5)/4\), whereas every base with at least six sides has a strictly increasing mean; the six-gon is the sharp threshold where the large-height deficit improves from order \(h^{-1}\) to order \(h^{-3}\).
