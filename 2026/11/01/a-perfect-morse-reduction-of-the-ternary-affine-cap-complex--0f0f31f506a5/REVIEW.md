## Originality — PASS, with qualifications
Conant and Thistlethwaite establish general hypergraph independence complexes, not this specialized homotopy classification. Lew (2017), Chapter 6 Figure 6.2, already reports exactly the integral homology groups in this same complex; the new result cannot be described as a first integral homology computation. The claim adds a perfect explicit acyclic Morse reduction and a wedge-of-spheres homotopy identification. Searches for the precise homotopy type, alternate cap/independence terminology, and published comparable results found no stronger covering statement. There remains an unindexed-literature risk.

## Value — PASS
The cap complex of the smallest nontrivial ternary affine plane is a canonical finite-geometric topological object, used as an extremal spectral-gap example. An explicit perfect Morse reduction upgrades its known homology to a full homotopy type, resolving potential attaching-map ambiguity and providing a reproducible finite certificate. The result is limited to this natural finite case and does not claim a family-wide theorem.
