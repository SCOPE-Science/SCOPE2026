For first returns to the top binary cell of the Pelikan random map, a pole–square-root collision at \(p=4/5\) changes the return mass from parity-dependent \(n^{-3/2}\) times an exponential to \(n^{-1/2}(4/5)^n\), then to a pure exponential. The sharp time-tail classification separates return statistics from the already known position-correlation resonance and refines the earlier absorbing-excursion asymptotics.
