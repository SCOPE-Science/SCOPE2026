The closed-neighborhood complex of the triple Kneser graph \(\mathrm{KG}(8,3)\) has exact integral homotopy type \(\mathbb{RP}^{2}\vee\bigvee^{405}S^{2}\), so a pure ten-dimensional simplicial complex carries genuine degree-one two-torsion. An exhaustive collapse and attaching-word calculation proves the projective-plane summand, not merely mod-two Betti numbers.
