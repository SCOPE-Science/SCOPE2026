The closed-neighborhood complex of the Clebsch graph has the complete integral homotopy type \(\mathbb{RP}^{2}\vee\bigvee^{55}S^{2}\), witnessed by explicit elementary collapses and attaching-word cancellations. Unlike the previously calculated open-neighborhood sphere wedge, the closed construction has fundamental group and first integral homology \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\).
