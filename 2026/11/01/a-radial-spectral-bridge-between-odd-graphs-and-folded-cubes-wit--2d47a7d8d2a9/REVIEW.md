# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
For every integer \(k\ge2\), the odd-graph distance permutation applied to the top Johnson harmonic gives the coefficients of \((1+(-1)^kz)(1-z^2)^k\). Even Walsh weights \(j=k+(k\bmod2)\) on the antipodal cube have the same coefficients. For \(k=3\), the complete centered spectra and multiplicity counts yield a single shared first zero at \(3(3^p-2^p)=1\); the remaining eigenvalue branches stay negative until that point. A positive eigenvalue thereafter excludes negative type. Endpoint centered Gram ranks are \(20\) and \(28\). Direct BFS and matrix spectral tests corroborate but do not replace the analytic proof.

(arXiv:1808.02051, Sec. 2) define folded cubes as antipodal quotients. Sánchez (arXiv:1108.0451) gives general negative-type criteria. None of the inspected primary passages or retrieved published-finding corpus records covers the universal cross-family coefficientwise radial identity or its joint sharp \(k=3\) negative-type boundary. This is not a novelty proof by lack of search hits. The main residual risk is an earlier equivalent identity implicit in distance-regular/association-scheme monographs not fully inspected. Older same-owner records addressed different families or Johnson Gaussian kernels and do not imply the present correspondence.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The all-order all-radial equality links Kneser odd-graph Johnson harmonics to antipodal-cube Walsh harmonics and transfers spectral obstructions between graphs of different size. The fully resolved smallest nontrivial matching case has an exact noninteger Schoenberg threshold and distinct Euclidean endpoint ranks. It is a structural classification with a motivated kernel/embedding interpretation, rather than a small computed invariant.

## Limitations
No claim that the entire distance spectra coincide, or that every higher-order graph pair has the same critical exponent. Computations cover finitely many cases; universality rests on symbolic coefficient comparison and inequalities. Earlier equivalent association-scheme formulations may exist. External independent validation has not been performed.
