For the eleven-vertex Grötzsch graph, the first higher-independence complex simplicially collapses onto the ordinary independence complex in 105 elementary pairs: \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(\mu(C_5))\searrow\operatorname{Ind}_1(\mu(C_5))\simeq S^2\). Thus allowing induced components of size two creates 210 extra faces but does not change the integral homotopy type or the inclusion-induced homology.
