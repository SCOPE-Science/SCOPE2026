A positive quintic velocity-feedback coefficient creates an exact boundary between closed orbits, infinite-time separatrices and finite-time escape in a non-global planar center. The periodic-orbit period diverges as \(2\sqrt\alpha A^2\) without quintic restoring force, but only as \(4\sqrt{\alpha/\beta}\log A\) when its coefficient is positive, producing distinct square-root-logarithmic and double-logarithmic energy-edge laws.
