The remainder-added linear map changes sharply at the neutral seed multiplier: every bounded set is absorbed when \(k>1\), sufficiently remote points escape when \(k<1\), and all non-eventually-fixed trajectories drift to infinity when \(k=1\). At the noninteger golden-ratio phase, the critical transport has exact speed \(1/\sqrt5\) and all-time variance with asymptotic coefficient \((5+3\sqrt5)/50\), obtained from a two-mode covariance law.
