For Haar-circle monic Sobolev polynomials with one boundary derivative mass \(\lambda|f'(1)|^2\), exactly one zero can escape the unit disk, at the sharp threshold \(\lambda=6/[n(n^2-1)]\). Under a degree-dependent mass \(\lambda_n=\tau n^{-s}\), the exceptional zero has a cubic-scale phase transition and a precise logarithm-plus-loglog interior approach when \(s>3\).
