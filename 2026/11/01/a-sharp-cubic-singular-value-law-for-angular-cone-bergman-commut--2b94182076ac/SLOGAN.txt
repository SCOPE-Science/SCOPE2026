For the weighted Bergman space on the complex two-ball, the commutator of coordinate multiplication with an angular-cone Toeplitz operator has a complete binomial singular spectrum and sharp counting law \(N(\varepsilon)\sim\sqrt t(1-t)^2\varepsilon^{-3}/(3\pi\sqrt3)\). It lies in \(S_p\) exactly for \(p>3\), with the leading singular-value coefficient independent of the radial weight and uniquely largest when the angular proportion is \(t=1/5\).
