Equally spaced normalized Fock kernels on a circle of squared radius \(\rho N\) have an exponentially large squared interpolation condition number with a sharp change at \(\rho=e^{-1}\): below and at the threshold one endpoint Fourier residue uniquely controls the minimum, whereas above it two Poisson aliases compete. The exact leading supercritical coefficient has an explicit fractional-phase modulation, while the subcritical coefficient is \(\sqrt\rho\).
