The first even harmonic degree **certified by the Feng–Zhang affine-surface-area/capacity counterexample coefficient** diverges at the sharp rate \(2(T_n/(p-1))^{1/(n+2)}\) as \(p\downarrow1\), with an explicit dimension-dependent constant \(T_n\). Its normalized coefficient has an exact sign-changing crossover when \((p-1)j^{n+2}\) passes \(T_n\); the statement is about the sufficient certificate, not the least degree of every possible counterexample.
