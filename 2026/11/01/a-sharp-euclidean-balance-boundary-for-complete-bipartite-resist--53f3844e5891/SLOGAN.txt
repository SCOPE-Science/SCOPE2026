Effective resistance on the complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) is itself Euclidean exactly when its two part sizes differ by at most one, even though its square root is always Euclidean. The full sharp negative-type exponent is the unique root of a two-term equation, with a one-dimensional critical equality space and a transition from exponent \(2\) near balance to exponent \(1\) under persistent proportional imbalance.
