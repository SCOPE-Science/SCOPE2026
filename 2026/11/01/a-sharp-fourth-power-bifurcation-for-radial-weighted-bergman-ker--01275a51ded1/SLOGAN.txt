For the weighted disk Bergman space with radial weight \(|z|^{2m}(1-|z|^2)^q\), every real \(m\ge0\) is zero-free for the four lowest boundary exponents \(q=0,1,2,3\). At \(q=4\), the kernel has exactly two simple conjugate off-diagonal scalar zeros precisely when \(m>1\), with a sharp unit-circle crossing at \(m=1\).
