For a positive disk weight with exactly one Hölder-rough angular Fourier mode, planar Bergman orthogonal polynomials have a sharp exterior correction of order \(n^{-\alpha}\) with a rotating complex-gamma phase, while their leading normalization changes only at order \(n^{-2\alpha}\). The nonzero first correction gives an explicit obstruction to uniformly improving finite-regularity exterior estimates to \(o(n^{-\alpha})\).
