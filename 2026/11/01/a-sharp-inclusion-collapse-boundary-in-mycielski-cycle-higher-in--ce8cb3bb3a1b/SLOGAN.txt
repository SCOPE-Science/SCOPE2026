For the cyclic Mycielskian, the natural inclusion from ordinary to two-independence complexes is an elementary simplicial collapse for cycle lengths \(3,4,5\); in particular, the Grötzsch two-independence complex is \(S^2\). At cycle length \(6\), the first obstruction is exact: the relative integral homology is \(\mathbb Z^6\) in degree \(3\) and zero elsewhere, so the inclusion is not a homotopy equivalence.
