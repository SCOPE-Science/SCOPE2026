For the smallest symmetric positive-definite tridiagonal Krylov example, the first above-tridiagonal coefficient produced by Gaussian-sketched Arnoldi has finite absolute \(p\)-moment exactly when \(p<\ell-2\), where \(\ell\ge3\) is the number of sketch rows. It has an exact nonzero power-law tail despite its rescaled distribution converging to \(N(0,2)\) as \(\ell\to\infty\).
