Nine equally spaced samples on one latitude circle recover all degree-four spherical harmonics with a sharp optimal squared condition number \(3.218558589055428\ldots\), independent of any additional samples on the same regular ring. The unique optimal squared height is the root \(\tau\in(1/4,1/3)\) of \(147\tau^3-147\tau^2+33\tau-1=0\); a five-interval exact rational certificate proves global optimality.
