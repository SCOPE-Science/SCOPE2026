The unique geometric-vector optimizer for a four-periodically damped weighted shift always increases with the second modulation, but its response to the first modulation reverses across an exact threshold \(t=\tau(q)\), on which it is completely independent of that first modulation. The threshold is strictly decreasing and has a sharp cubic-root boundary layer \(\tau(q)\sim(4/5)^{1/3}(1-q)^{1/3}\).
