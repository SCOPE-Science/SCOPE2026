A nontrivial nilpotent group \(N\) can preserve the Sylow-integral value \(\gamma(A_5\times N)=9/2\) only if at least nine distinct primes divide \(|N|\), and the published nine-prime example is therefore sharp. Every minimizing \(N\) has exactly a \(C_2\) Sylow-two component, no Sylow-three or Sylow-five component, and eight additional prime divisors.
