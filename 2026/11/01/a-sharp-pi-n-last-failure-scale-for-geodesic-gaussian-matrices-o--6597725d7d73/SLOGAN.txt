For the squared intrinsic-distance Gaussian matrix on every finite cycle of order \(n\), the last possible failure of positive semidefiniteness occurs at scale \(t\sim\pi/n\). Above \((\pi+\varepsilon)/n\) the matrix is strictly positive definite at every larger parameter, whereas at \((\pi-\varepsilon)/n\) a near-Nyquist Fourier mode is negative for all sufficiently large \(n\), regardless of parity.
