For the Muñoz–Nuño two-parameter probabilistic cellular automaton on every ring \(N\ge3\), finite-state Markov detailed balance holds **exactly** when the tie-retention probability is \(p=1/2\). On that line the stationary state is an explicit distance-two Ising measure: adjacent spins are independent on every even ring but exhibit a sharp \(\operatorname{sech}\)-shaped weak-noise crossover on odd rings.
