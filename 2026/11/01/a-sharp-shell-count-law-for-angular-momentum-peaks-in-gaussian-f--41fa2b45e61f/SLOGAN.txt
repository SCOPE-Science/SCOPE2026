For Gaussian analytic Fock localization on exactly \(q\) disjoint concentric annuli in any fixed complex dimension, the degree-indexed concentration spectrum has at most \(q\) strict peaks. This bound is attained by actual \(q\)-annulus sets at **every** prescribed nontrivial Gaussian observation mass, so spectral multimodality is controlled sharply by the number of radial observation components rather than by their total Gaussian mass.
