# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For q>=3, b>=2, h>=1, uniform proper q-colourings are exactly a tree broadcast process with one-edge nonconstant character eigenvalue -a=-1/(q-1). Character blocks for different Fourier frequencies are orthogonal and sign-conjugate to K(v,w)=a^dist(v,w). For Q=I+a²(deg-I)-a A_T, multiplication along unique paths verifies KQ=(1-a²)I. Orthogonal level-radial and b-1 child-contrast bases split Q into B_h plus D_{h-ell} with multiplicity (b-1)b^ell; exact dimension sum 1+Σ(h-ell)(b-1)b^ell=N and strict trailing principal-submatrix interlacing put both extrema in B_h. Positive quadratic form x*B x=(1-a²)|x0|²+Σ|x_j-t x_{j-1}|² gives invertibility, determinant 1-a² and exact Riesz frame. Largest Jacobi eigenvalue tends (1+t)²; for t<1 the smallest tends (1-t)², using diagonal comparison and long interior trial vectors. At t=1 the leaf-Neumann/root-Robin cosine solution gives h² λ_min→π²/4, hence 16/π² coefficient. At t>1, independent inversion of the bidiagonal form and interlacing isolate the unique exponentially small eigenvalue; tr B^-1=[Σ_{k=0}^h t^(2k)]/(1-a²)+Σ_{m=0}^{h-1}(h-m)t^(2m), so t^(2h)λ_min→[(1-a²)(t²-1)²]/[t²(t²-a²)]. Combining yields exactly the three stated limits for the nonconstant-coordinate squared condition number. Independently assembled radial Jacobi matrices and eigenvalue ratios in 6 parameter regimes at depths 6,12,30 and verified approach to the claimed sharp constants (slow subcritical convergence near t=1). Neither finite depth tests nor broadcast threshold alone prove the asymptotic constants. No whole system condition number or leaf-only reconstruction claimed.
