# Scientific review: Fourier conditioning on proper-coloured regular trees

**Correctness — PASS.** For every integer \(q\ge3\), branching factor \(b\ge2\), and finite height \(h\ge1\), the single-edge transition character eigenvalue is \(-1/(q-1)\). The sampled Gram therefore splits into identical positive exponential-distance matrices. The precision-matrix inversion follows directly by multiplying along the unique tree path, and the radial plus child-contrast decomposition accounts for every vertex dimension. Principal-submatrix interlacing identifies the sharp finite-height Riesz extrema. The factorization of the radial Jacobi matrix gives the exact inverse trace. At the critical parameter, a positive Sturm eigenvector and its Robin boundary equation produce the \(\pi^2/(4h^2)\) smallest-eigenvalue law; above it, rank-one interlacing isolates one exponentially small eigenvalue. Thus the constants in all three regimes follow from analytical proof, with the embedded checker providing only corroboration.

**Originality — PASS under bounded literature comparison.** The Kesten--Stigum threshold itself is known from broadcasting: Mossel--Peres (arXiv:math/0107033, sections 1 and 5--6). The exponential-distance/q-Laplacian inverse is known from Bapat--Lal--Pati (2006; original abstract and complete identity as quoted in Nagar, arXiv:1711.09787, section 2.2). DeFord--Rockmore (arXiv:1903.07134, sections 1--2) gives adjacency spectral decompositions of homogeneous trees, but not the finite all-vertex proper-colouring Fourier Gram spectrum. Searches of the accessible mathematical findings ledger and published records found nearest work on proper-colouring Fourier frames of cycles and complete bipartite graphs, not on the rooted regular-tree coordinate frame. The finite block multiplicities plus sharp conditioning trichotomy and coefficients were not established by any inspected source. This is not a proof of historical uniqueness; the remaining risk is equivalent treatment in older Bethe-tree Jacobi and broadcast covariance literature, especially an inaccessible full original article.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Uniform proper-colouring constraints define a canonical, highly structured sampling ensemble, and the first Fourier band measures recovery of all single-site observables. A complete exact spectrum is useful for interpolation and stability analysis; the new result distinguishes uniformly stable, polynomially ill-conditioned, and exponentially ill-conditioned reconstruction with exact leading constants. Crucially the change occurs at a meaningful branching-versus-channel threshold, rather than being an arbitrary finite parameter slice. It also separates conditioning of the full all-vertex coordinate band from the distinct leaf-only reconstruction problem.

**Scientific limits.** No inference about nonlinear root reconstruction, varying-alphabet or varying-branching limits, or leaf-only observations is made. The condition number is defined on the nonconstant character band. A direct independent literature and proof audit remains necessary before stronger novelty or certification statements.
