On uniform proper colourings of a complete \(b\)-ary tree, the complete first-coordinate Fourier Gram spectrum splits into explicit radial and child-contrast Jacobi blocks. Its nonconstant squared condition number stays bounded for \(b<(q-1)^2\), grows as \(16h^2/\pi^2\) at equality, and grows exponentially above the threshold, with an exact leading constant.
