On the complete split graph \(K_a\vee\overline K_b\), synchronous lazy-neighbor-majority dynamics reaches a fixed point or two-cycle within three rounds, and three rounds occur exactly when \(a>b\), \(a+b\) is even, and \(b\ge3\). The maximally delayed configurations form an explicitly counted central-binomial layer, whose fraction is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/(\pi(a+b))}\) as \(b\to\infty\) within that regime.
