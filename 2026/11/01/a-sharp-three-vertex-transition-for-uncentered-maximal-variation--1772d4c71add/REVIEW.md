# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Exhausted the actual graph-ball types of the simple unit-weight K_{r,r}: singletons, radius-one center-plus-opposite-class balls and radius-two whole graph. For indicator with a selected A vertices and b selected B vertices, Var input D=r(a+b)-2ab>0. Derive exact zero-class maxima alpha,beta and edge-orbit output variation. Separate a=0, a=b, and 1<=a<b. In mixed case beta=(b+1)/(r+1) and alpha>=b/(r+1); the claimed global bound follows from two exact polynomial identities P(a,b)D(1,b)-P(1,b)D(a,b)=-r²(a-1)(r-b) and P(1,b)D(1,2)-P(1,2)D(1,b)=-(b-2)[b(3r-4)(r-2)+r(r²-2)]. Both identities were independently expanded and verified symbolically, all denominators positive. The extremizers are singleton r=2..5 and one-plus-two vertices r>=6; 1+2 belongs to a valid class only r>=3. The gain first exceeds 1 for integers r>=9 and tends to 4/3. Independently enumerated all actual metric balls and all occupancy cases (a,b) for r=2..10 with exact fractions, found unique stated extremizers and threshold; witnesses at r=6,8,9 are 43/49,44/45,233/230. This is only indicator-cut gain, not unrestricted BV operator norm; all-size proof is symbolic identities, not finite sampling.

## originality

PASS

PASS (qualified). Earlier 2006/2010 uncentered interval/Z theorems apply to a line, not complete balanced bicliques. González-Riquelme and Madrid 2020 and González-Riquelme–Kovač–Madrid 2026 study primarily centered graph-ball maximal functions, sharp results for complete graphs, stars or graph orders <=5, not the uncentered indicator optimization for K_{r,r} of arbitrary r. The result is not implied by a theorem about centered averages or a different graph. Full Liu–Xue 2021 text not independently accessed and earlier unindexed cut comparisons remain possible.
