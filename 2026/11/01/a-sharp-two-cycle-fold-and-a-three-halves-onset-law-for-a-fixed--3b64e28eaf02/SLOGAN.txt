The fixed-point-free cosine–memristor map has exactly two, one or zero prime-two orbits on either side of an explicit sharp fold threshold throughout \(1<\beta\le2\). The fold threshold grows like \(8(\beta-1)^{3/2}/(3\sqrt3)\) at the onset \(\beta=1\), and every two-cycle is found by a single strictly log-concave scalar equation.
