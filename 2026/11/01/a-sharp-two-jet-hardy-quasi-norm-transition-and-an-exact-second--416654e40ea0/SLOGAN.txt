The exact norm of \(a_0+\tau a_2\) on analytic \(H^{1/2}\) changes extremal shape sharply at \(\tau=1/2\): a unique even fourth power gives way to two symmetry-related fourth powers, with a jump in the second derivative of the norm. It also gives the full fixed-origin second-coefficient frontier for \(81/256\le t\le1\), and establishes \(\Phi_2(1/2,t)=\Phi_1(1/2,t)\) precisely on the terminal interval \(81/100\le t\le1\) within that range.
