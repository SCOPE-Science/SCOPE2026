On every nonsingleton complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), the maximum possible difference between any two chip labels seen anywhere in the two phases of a synchronous integer-diffusion two-cycle is exactly \(a+b+\max(a,b)-2\). The bound is attained for every \(a,b\ge2\) and gives the smallest universal translation-normalized alphabet needed to enumerate all such recurrent orbits.
