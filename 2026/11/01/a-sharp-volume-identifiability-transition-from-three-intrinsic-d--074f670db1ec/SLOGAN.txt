For any rectangular flat three-torus, the first three Haar pairwise squared-geodesic-distance moments reconstruct its three circle lengths, while the first two give an exact sharp interval for its Riemannian volume. The lower volume bound collapses at the precise moment ratio \(\mathbb E[D^4]/\mathbb E[D^2]^2=7/5\), and three-moment inversion has optimal uniform Hölder exponent \(1/3\).
