In the neutral-band stock-market map, weak sentiment \(0<d<h\) can coexist with two attracting five-cycles and an attracting equilibrium. On the zero-trace slice \(b=1/4\), \(c=5/4\), the two cycles first appear exactly when \(d/h>205/252\), while no nonfixed periodic orbit of period at most four exists anywhere in this weak-forcing regime.
