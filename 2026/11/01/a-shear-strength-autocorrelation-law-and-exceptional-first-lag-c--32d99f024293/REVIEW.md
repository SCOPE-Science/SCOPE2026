# Mathematical assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact all-shear proof uses the Fourier coefficients of the centered first Bernoulli function, absolute summability of the constrained lattice sum, a three-pole partial fraction identity, the cotangent residue formula and finite cosecant sums. The exceptional two-lag computation uses a complete rational polygonal decomposition. The standalone checker reproduces fourteen one-lag values and the exceptional two-lag result in exact fractions, but the infinite parameter proof is analytical.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** Hu–Rosenhaus (arXiv:2204.13655, especially §3 Eq. (3.20)) derived an implicit Fourier series and explicitly described the difficulty of summation; the reported closed all-shear law does not appear there. Hill–Sturman–Wilson (arXiv:2112.05463) supplies qualitative orthogonal-shear mixing, not the evaluated coefficient. Indexed searches did not locate equivalent closed values or dominating implications. Crawford and Cary's 1983 correlation article remained inaccessible in full text and poses a residual priority risk; absence from a search does not prove historical priority.

**Value — PASS.** Exact autocovariance is a natural statistical invariant of canonical hyperbolic integer-shear automorphisms. The unique first-lag decorrelation parameter, its nontrivial second-lag correlation, and its algebraically quantified negative correlations for all larger shears isolate a meaningful observable-specific boundary invisible to qualitative mixing estimates.

**Limitations.** The result concerns one explicit bilinear sawtooth observable; it neither classifies general observables nor asserts an all-lag closed form. Independent external scientific audit and exhaustive publication-priority confirmation have not occurred.
