# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The full-cube heat-kernel representation proves that each edge row has positive unit mass in its own coordinate direction and absolute mass one-half in each other direction. The grounded Laplacian rank-one downdate is legitimate since removal of one cube edge leaves a connected graph. For an edge parallel and one transverse step from the deleted edge, the sign pattern divides the other directions into one canceling direction and the rest reinforcing, yielding the stated exact rational row sum for every integer dimension at least two. Independent grounded-matrix and Walsh-spectral constructions, performed with fractions rather than floating point, exhaustively certify the global norms in dimensions two, three, and four. The sharp witness is the sign vector of a norming row. No general exact global deleted norm beyond dimension four is claimed.

The surviving assertion is the explicit deletion response and the sharp four-dimensional threshold.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Graph-gradient fitting to incomplete pairwise measurements is a natural inverse problem: removal of one observation might be expected to reduce constraint sensitivity, yet the exact row formula shows that the maximum-norm operator becomes strictly more amplifying once the cube has four coordinate directions. This is an all-dimensional geometric threshold, with a completely resolved first amplification case and a quantified first-order excess. The result distinguishes energy-norm contractivity from endpoint-norm robustness under a structured edge failure.
