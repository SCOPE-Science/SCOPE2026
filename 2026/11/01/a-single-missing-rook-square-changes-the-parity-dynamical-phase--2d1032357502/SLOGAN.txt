A single deleted square of a rook graph induces an exact characteristic-two phase switch: even-by-even XOR dynamics extinguishes in three steps, odd-by-odd dynamics supports two-cycles, and mixed parity reaches fixed points in at most five steps. In the mixed case, exactly the fraction \(1-1/q\) of all initial states require the full five steps, for every size and every \(q=2^h\).
