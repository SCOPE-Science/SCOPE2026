For every \(n\ge3\) except \(n=4\), the open-neighborhood complex of \(K_3\mathbin{\square}C_n\) collapses in exactly \(6n\) elementary moves onto an orientable PL torus; the four-cycle exception has Euler characteristic \(6\). For every multiple of three with \(n\ge6\), this gives an infinite noncirculant family excluded from earlier circulant classifications.
