For every bouquet of at least two unfilled four-cycles meeting at exactly one vertex, the full strong discrete-Morse matching complex simplicially collapses to the join of one six-cycle per square and therefore has the integral homotopy type \(S^{2m-1}\). Its entire face enumerator is \((1+6z+6z^2)^m+2mz(1+3z)(1+4z+3z^2)^{m-1}\).
