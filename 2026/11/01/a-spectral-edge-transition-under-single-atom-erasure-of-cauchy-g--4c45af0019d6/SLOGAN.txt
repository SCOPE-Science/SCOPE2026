For integer-oversampled Cauchy Gabor frames, deleting one atom always preserves the frame but can either preserve or strictly lower its optimal lower bound. An explicit finite spectral-edge integral decides the outcome, and its limits \(E_q(a)\sim1/(q\sqrt{\pi a})\) and \(E_q(a)\to1/2+1/q\) prove both behaviors at every fixed redundancy \(q\ge3\).
