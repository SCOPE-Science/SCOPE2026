The sum of all coordinate \(k\)-cross-polytopes in \(\mathbb R^n\) has a unique largest inscribed ball of radius \(\binom{n-1}{k-1}\min\{1,\sqrt n/k\}\), for every \(n\ge k\ge3\). Its tangent facet count undergoes an exact square-law transition: \(2^n\) below \(n=k^2\), \(2^n+2n\) at equality, and \(2n\) above it.
