On each of the twelve face-median half-segments of a regular tetrahedron, the uniform-surface-target mean squared intrinsic distance is exactly \(a^2/3\), independent of source position, even though the entire geodesic distance distribution changes. An exact circular-cap formula gives that distribution; its fourth distance moment rises strictly from \(a^4/6\) to \(8a^4/45\), while source eccentricity rises from \(a\) to \(2a/\sqrt3\).
