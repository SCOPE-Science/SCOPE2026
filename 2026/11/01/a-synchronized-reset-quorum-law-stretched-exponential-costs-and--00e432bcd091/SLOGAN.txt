For a group of \(N\) Brownian searchers required to **all** hit a target before one shared Poisson reset erases that round's progress, a fixed reset rate makes mean quorum completion grow like \(\exp\{3(rd^2/(4\pi D))^{1/3}N^{2/3}+o(N^{2/3})\}\). Allowing rate optimization instead makes the optimal clock scale as \(N^{-2}\) and the best mean completion time scale exactly as \(N^2\) times an explicit universal one-dimensional integral minimum.
