The doubles of cyclic six-polytope boundaries on \(7\) through \(16\) vertices have completely determined integral homology, including a torsion staircase of orders \(2,3,4,5\) at \(10,12,14,16\) vertices. The first order-five class in this natural range is \(\widetilde H_1(D(\partial C(16,6));\mathbb Z)_{\mathrm{tors}}\cong\mathbb Z/5\); no infinite-family claim is made.
