The multipath complex of the fully bidirected \(K_{2,5}\) is \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{31}S^3\), with genuine integral two-torsion. For every \(n\ge6\), the fully bidirected \(K_{2,n}\) instead has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{n^4-8n^3+19n^2-14n+1}S^3\), proved by an explicit uniform discrete-Morse matching and finite-locality reduction.
