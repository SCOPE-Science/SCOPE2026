Three interacting shores eliminate the period-four behavior possible with two: every three-color cyclic automaton on a complete multipartite graph with at least three nonempty shores satisfies \(F^5=F^2\). On \(K_{a,b,c}\), the entire consensus basin has the exact size \(3(2^{a+b+c}+3^a+3^b+3^c-2^{a+1}-2^{b+1}-2^{c+1}+2)\), with a complete primitive-three-cycle orbit polynomial; for balanced shores, consensus has probability asymptotic to \(3(8/27)^n\).
