For signed-coordinate cubic orbits \((r,1,1)/\sqrt{r^2+2}\), a specific two-dimensional degree-nine harmonic sampling obstruction occurs at precisely the two Archimedean ratios \(r=\sqrt2-1\) and \(r=\sqrt2+1\). The regular truncated cube has exact-degree harmonic evaluation ranks \(2k+1\) for \(0\le k\le5\), rank ten at degree nine, and rank twelve in every other degree \(k\ge6\).
