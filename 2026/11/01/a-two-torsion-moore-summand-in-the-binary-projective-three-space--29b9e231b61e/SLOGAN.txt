The no-three-collinear cap complex of the fifteen points of \(\operatorname{PG}(3,2)\) has a genuine two-torsion Moore summand: \(C_p(3,2)\simeq M(\mathbb Z/2,3)\vee\bigvee^{77}S^4\). An explicit acyclic matching on all \(3048\) nonempty faces reduces it to one three-cell and \(78\) four-cells with attaching-degree gcd \(2\).
