For ordinary parallel chip firing on a complete graph with \(k\) disjoint edges removed, the primitive two-cycle orbit count is \(e^{-2k/N}+O(N^{-1})\) times the complete-graph count, uniformly over all \(0\le k\le\lfloor N/2\rfloor\). Deleting a perfect matching removes only a vanishing fraction of the edges yet asymptotically eliminates \(1-e^{-1}\) of the primitive two-cycle orbits.
