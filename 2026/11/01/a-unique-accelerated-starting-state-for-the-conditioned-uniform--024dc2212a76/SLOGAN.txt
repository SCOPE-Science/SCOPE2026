The symmetric uniform moving-average chain conditioned to retain positive consecutive sums has a unique accelerated initial state: at \(x=1/3\), its total-variation relaxation has exact base \(1/5\), while every other fixed admissible starting point has exact base \(1/3\). Its invariant density is a squared sine function, and these sharp rates and constants are unchanged when the uniform innovation interval is extended from \([-1,1]\) to \([-a,1]\) for any \(a\ge1\).
