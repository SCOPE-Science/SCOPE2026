For every regular even \(n\)-gon, clipping two opposite vertices with parallel cuts makes its Mahler product rise and then fall, with exactly one strict interior optimum and an explicit cubic equation for the optimal depth. As \(n\to\infty\), the best gain is asymptotic to \(\pi^4/(2n^3)\), exactly \(3/8\) of the gain achievable by replacing the polygon with a regular \((n+2)\)-gon.
