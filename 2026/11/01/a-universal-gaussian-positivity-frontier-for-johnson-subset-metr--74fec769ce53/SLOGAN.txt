For every integer \(k\ge3\), the squared-Johnson-distance Gaussian on all \(k\)-subsets has the same sharp first positivity-loss parameter \(\rho_k\), regardless of the ambient size \(n\ge2k\); \(\rho_k\) is the unique zero of \(\sum_{r=0}^k(-1)^r\binom kr x^{r^2}\) below \(1/(k-1)\). At the boundary the exact nullity is \(\binom nk-\binom n{k-1}\), and the obstruction is the top Boolean-lattice incidence sector.
