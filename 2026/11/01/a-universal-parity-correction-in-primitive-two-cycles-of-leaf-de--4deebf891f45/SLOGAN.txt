On a cycle with \(r\ge1\) pendant leaves per vertex, primitive parallel-chip-firing two-cycles admit an exact interface-resolved transfer-matrix census. Their total count is \(\frac12\bigl((r+1+\sqrt{(r+1)^2+1})^n+(r+1-\sqrt{(r+1)^2+1})^n\bigr)+(-1)^n\), with the same exact alternating correction for every positive leaf multiplicity.
