For power-diagonal commutators of generalized Hilbert moment Hankel matrices, the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm has the sharp small-exponent law \(\pi^2|c|^2\alpha/6+O(\alpha^2)\), where \(c\) is the leading moment coefficient. For a Hölder density, \(c\) is simply its boundary value at one: the entire leading singular cusp is insensitive to interior changes of the density.
