For every norm-one positive contraction on a separable infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space, the numerical index relative to its near-norm-attainment operator seminorm is exactly \(1/2\) when its squared defect is zero or compact and injective, and zero otherwise. Compact injective defects identify the relative numerical radius with the essential numerical radius, while even arbitrarily small nonzero finite-rank defects collapse the relative index to zero.
