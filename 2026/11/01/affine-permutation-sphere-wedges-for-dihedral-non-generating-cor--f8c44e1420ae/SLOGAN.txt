For every dihedral group of order \(2n\), \(n\ge3\), its Frattini-core non-generating complex has an equivariant sphere-wedge model with one \(\bigl(\omega(n)-1\bigr)\)-sphere per residue modulo \(\operatorname{rad}(n)\). The full group-induced automorphism action on integral homology is the **affine permutation lattice** on those residues, giving exact characters and a unique invariant direction.
