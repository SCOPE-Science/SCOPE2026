For the \(d\)-fold Cartesian product of equilateral triangles on \(S^{2d-1}\), exact-degree \(k\) spherical harmonics have precisely all ternary Fourier characters of support at most \(k\), apart from a missing constant mode in degrees one and two. All \(3^d\) values can be interpolated at one exact degree if and only if \(k\ge\max\{d,3\}\); an explicit Krawtchouk–Gegenbauer formula gives every sampling Gram eigenvalue and multiplicity.
