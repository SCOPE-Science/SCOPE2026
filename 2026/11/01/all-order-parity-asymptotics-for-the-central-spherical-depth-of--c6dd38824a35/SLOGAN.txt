For a regular odd \(n\)-gon, the probability that its center lies in the diameter disk of two independent uniform interior points differs from \(1/2\) by \(-(-1)^{(n-1)/2}(5\pi^4/(192n^5)+7\pi^6/(720n^7)+O(n^{-9}))\). The oscillation has an all-orders expansion in odd inverse powers with rational multiples of powers of \(\pi\); the previously known mod-four sign law and exact Fourier representation are credited.
