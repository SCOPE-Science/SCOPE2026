For every \(n\ge6\) and \(3\le k\le n-3\), a seven-term rational representation-ring identity determines the complete symmetric-group action on \(\widetilde H_2(\operatorname{Cl}(J(n,k));\mathbb Q)\). Although these Johnson complexes have many two-dimensional homology classes, both their \(S_n\)- and \(A_n\)-quotients are rationally acyclic.
