For the full \(s\)-ary Hamming cube of every dimension \(n\ge2\), the squared-Hamming Gaussian \(e^{-t h(x,y)^2}\) becomes positive semidefinite at the same sharp positive bandwidth for **every alphabet size**: exactly \(t\ge-\log\rho_n\), plus the isolated constant-kernel case \(t=0\), where \(\rho_n\) is the unique root of \(\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k\binom nk u^{k^2}\) in \((1/n,1/(n-1))\). At the threshold its nullity is exactly \((s-1)^n\), whereas every greater positive bandwidth gives strict positive definiteness.
