For every \(k\ge3\), the first rational homology of \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_2,\operatorname{KG}(2k+1,k))\) decomposes multiplicity-free as \(\bigoplus_{j=1}^{k}S^{(2k-j,j,1)}\), and swapping the two source vertices acts on the \(j\)-th summand by \((-1)^{j+1}\). A sharp mixed-character dichotomy and an explicit integral graph-cycle lattice describe the full natural \(S_{2k+1}\times C_2\) symmetry.
