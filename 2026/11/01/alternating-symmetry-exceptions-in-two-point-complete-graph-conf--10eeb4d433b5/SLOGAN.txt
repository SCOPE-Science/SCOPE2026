For two unordered collision-free particles on any complete graph, quotienting by all vertex relabelings produces a contractible space, but quotienting by even relabelings leaves a circle precisely on three or four vertices. Explicit equivariant deformation retractions and finite orbit-cell models show that the four-vertex exception is a pinched disk collapsing to a circle, whereas every alternating quotient from five vertices onward is contractible.
