A mixed advanced–retarded scalar feedback network is globally exponentially stable at **every finite negative-feedback gain** once sampling passes the exact phase threshold \(s_*=(ah)^{-1}\log[2/(1+e^{-ah})]\); before that threshold, stability has a sharp gain ceiling. For hyperbolic-tangent activation the ceiling separates exponential attraction, universal inverse-square-root critical decay, and an explicitly computable attracting antisymmetric two-cycle for every supercritical gain.
