In the discontinuous power family of Lawnik–Moysis–Volos, an explicitly contracting region has a unique globally attracting two-cycle and unique invariant probability for \(a>b\), but has **no invariant Borel probability at all** when \(a=b\), even though every orbit remains bounded and its empirical measure converges. The stationary measures tend to a nonstationary two-point limit under uniform convergence of the maps; for every \(d>1\), the disappearing cycle approaches the discontinuity with exact exponent \(d\) while its multiplier tends to zero.
