For the Möbius–Kantor graph \(\operatorname{GP}(8,3)\), the higher-independence complex \(\operatorname{Ind}_3\) collapses simplicially onto \(\operatorname{Ind}_2\) in exactly 4,192 elementary free-face pairs. Thus adding 8,384 faces, including top-dimensional nine-simplices, does not change the simple-homotopy type, and the natural inclusion is a homotopy equivalence.
