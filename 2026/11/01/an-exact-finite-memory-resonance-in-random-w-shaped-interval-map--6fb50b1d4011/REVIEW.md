# Review of the finite-memory resonance theorem

## Correctness — PASS

For every real \(a>2\) and \(0<p<1\), each affine branch has a complete union of partition cells as its image. Uniform conditional densities propagate through each finite symbolic history. This gives a genuine four-state stationary Markov chain, not merely transition frequencies. The displayed rows \(E,C\) are derived from exact image lengths, and \(\pi=(1-\alpha)E+\alpha C\) is strictly positive and stationary. Exact multiplication yields \(P^2=\boldsymbol1\pi+r(P-\boldsymbol1\pi)\). At \(r=0\), the Markov property upgrades the identity \(P^2=\boldsymbol1\pi\) to separation of *whole* past/future sigma-algebras; \(P\ne\boldsymbol1\pi\) proves strictness. The center-indicator chain has multiplier \(r\), giving the all-lag covariance and ruling out finite dependence off resonance. A symbolic checker verifies the polynomial identities and 24 exact rational test cases; only the analytical arguments establish the universal and infinite-time scope.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The source's full preprint [1], Section 6.2, defines the maps and proves the cellwise-constant density, and hence supplies necessary prior structure. It does not state a four-state temporal transition matrix, the unique exact two-step memory-loss curve, or a strict one-dependent symbolic process. The distinct deterministic spectral-instability theorem of Li–Góra [2] does not imply the claimed random-switching law. The W-map perturbation/Ulam research [3] concerns stability of densities, not the present temporal classification. Searches of mathematical research records and published literature using W-map, finite dependence, exact symbolic mixing, two-step rank-one transition, switching probability and resonance aliases found no directly covering claim. A general finite-state Markov projection method can reproduce the result once this transition matrix is derived; this is not claimed as an original proof technique. An unindexed equivalent computation remains possible.

## Value — PASS

This is a natural, parameter-complete answer about **when a stationary code loses its temporal memory in exactly two transitions**. The resonance is not a chosen numerical parameter: it is the unique curve across the whole two-parameter family. Exactly there the four-symbol code is strictly one-dependent while a nontrivial coarse observation becomes iid; on either side correlations persist for every lag and switch their signs. This is a motivated structural boundary for a W-family originally studied through invariant-density and spectrum stability, rather than a recomputation of the existing stationary density.
