For the stationary four-cell coding of the random W maps of Kalle–Maggioni, the **unique** finite-memory switching probability is \(p=(a-1)/a\), where the full four-symbol process is strictly one-dependent although its center/outer indicator is iid Bernoulli. Away from this curve, the indicator's exact covariance is \(\alpha(1-\alpha)r^n\) at lag \(n\), with \(r\) crossing zero at precisely the same parameter.
