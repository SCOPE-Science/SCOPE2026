For the unit-edge Johnson metric graph \(J(n,k)\) with \(k\ge3\), the exact probability of an arclength-uniform pair lying within \(\varepsilon<1/2\) of the continuous diameter changes from linear in \(\varepsilon\) at \(n=2k+1\) to quadratic at every \(n\ge2k+2\). Both regimes have explicit finite-parameter coefficients and exact formulas on the entire stated interval.
