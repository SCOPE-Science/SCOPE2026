The rank of \(\widetilde H_2(K_n;\mathbb Z)\) for the integer-partition transfer graph's clique complex is the sum over partitions \(\lambda\vdash n\) of \(\binom{s(\lambda)-2}{2}\) plus one for every repeated interior distinct part size (with zero contribution when \(s(\lambda)\le2\)). This all-\(n\) nonnegative formula computes the exact number of two-spheres in the previously known wedge decomposition without enumerating cliques.
