For the superstable quadratic two-cycle \(0\leftrightarrow1\) driven by arbitrary scalar noise of amplitude \(\varepsilon\le1/20\), two separated invariant interval bands containing that cycle exist exactly when \(\varepsilon\le\sqrt{73-22\sqrt{11}}/4\). The two boundary solutions collide in an algebraic saddle-node while the spatial gap remains greater than \(0.58\); at the critical parameter their one-sided endpoint relaxation is proportional to the inverse number of two-step returns.
