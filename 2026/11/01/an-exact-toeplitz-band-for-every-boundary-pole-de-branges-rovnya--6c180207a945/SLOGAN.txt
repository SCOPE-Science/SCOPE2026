For any rational de Branges–Rovnyak quotient with all poles on the unit circle, multiplication of the monomial Gram matrix by the squared denominator shift gives an exact upper-triangular Toeplitz band, apart from at most twice the denominator degree in boundary rows. The band symbol is explicitly \(Q^2+\kappa^{-1}PP^{\#}\), valid for repeated or distinct poles and arbitrary complex coefficients.
