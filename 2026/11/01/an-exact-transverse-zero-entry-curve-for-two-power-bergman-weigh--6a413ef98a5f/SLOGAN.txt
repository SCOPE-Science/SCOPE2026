For the natural disk weights \(|z|^{2s}(1-|z|^2)^\alpha\), the first conjugate pair of weighted Bergman kernel zeros crosses the unit circle along a unique analytic curve through \((s,\alpha)=(1,4)\). Its exact transverse slope is \(-49\sqrt3/(60\pi)\); locally the kernel is zero-free on or below the curve and has precisely two scalar product zeros above it.
