# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Cauchy–Binet gives sum_{i<j} p_ij²=1 for absolute 2x2 Plücker minors. If p_ij>=sqrt(2/3), the other projected generators in W=[w_i,w_j] coordinates satisfy det(I+H)=p^-2<=3/2, hence tr H<=1/2 and each l1 coordinate <=1, forcing projected hull to be the pair parallelogram and area=2p_ij. Only one pair can be this large. Ivanov sharp area bound 2, with equality precisely at coordinate 2-planes, and compactness of K_n={max p<=sqrt(2/3)} make positive delta_n=min(2-max_K area, 2(1-sqrt(2/3))) rigorous, although unevaluated. Haar p_ij=R|<v,a>|; independent squared Beta variables (1,(n-2)/2) and (1/2,(n-2)/2) yield moments k!(n-2)!/(n+k-2)! and hence p_ij~Beta(1,n-2). Disjoint cap events give the exact polynomial; iid extreme-value limit and expected deficit follow via tail integration and exponentially controlled complement. Independently simulated QR Haar frames in n=3,4,6, with observed one-minor tails and E[p] matching predictions; these support but do not prove all n.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge novel exact near-max Haar-area distribution for all ambient dimensions, not novelty of the extremal area bound or Beta minor marginal. Ivanov 2018 and 2026 prove deterministic maximal area and equality; Böröczky–Henk treats expected face counts of random regular-polytope projections, not the upper-tail area law; the closest Resultary cross-polytope record concerns random hyperplane *sections* and their diameter. Filliman 1988 is relevant older extremal literature not inspected in full and remains a bounded priority risk.
