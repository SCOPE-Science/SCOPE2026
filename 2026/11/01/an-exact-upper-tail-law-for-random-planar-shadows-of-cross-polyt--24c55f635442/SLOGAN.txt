For every dimension \(n\ge3\), the area of a Haar-random planar projection of the regular coordinate cross-polytope has an **exact** upper-tail law \(\Pr(A\ge2-\varepsilon)=\binom n2(\varepsilon/2)^{n-2}\) throughout a nonempty interval next to its maximum. This yields a sharp Weibull law for the best area among independent random orientations, with an explicit dimension-dependent constant.
