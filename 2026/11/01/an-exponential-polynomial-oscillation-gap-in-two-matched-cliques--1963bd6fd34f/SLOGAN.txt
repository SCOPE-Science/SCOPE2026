For synchronous binary majority on two complete cliques joined by a perfect matching, the probability of eventual oscillation is \(\sim 2/(\pi n)\) when each clique has even size \(n\), but \(\sim n^{3/2}2^{-n}/\sqrt{2\pi}\) when it has odd size. Exact orbit and basin formulas hold for every \(n\ge2\), and every trajectory becomes periodic within three updates.
