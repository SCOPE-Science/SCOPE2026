For a binary directed-cycle sandpile supported on any cyclic planar difference set, the avalanche complex has a translation-equivariant simplicial collapse onto a graph with a spanning translation cycle and freely translated chord-orbits. Its integral first-homology lattice is \(\mathbb Z_{\mathrm{triv}}\oplus\mathbb Z[\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z]^{\oplus(k-2)}\); Singer's prime-power family has first Betti number \(q^3\) and a nonidentity rotation trace of \(1\).
