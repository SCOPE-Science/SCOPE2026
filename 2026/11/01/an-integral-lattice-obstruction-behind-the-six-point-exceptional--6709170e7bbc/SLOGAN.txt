For the simplicial complex of pairwise edge-disjoint one-factors of the six-vertex complete graph, the integral second homology is \(\bigwedge^3 W_T\), where \(T\) is the exceptional six-element set of one-factorizations. Its rational \(S_6\)-character agrees with \(\bigwedge^2 W_X\), but the two natural integral lattices are distinguished by ranks \(4\) and \(3\) of a transposition minus identity modulo \(2\).
