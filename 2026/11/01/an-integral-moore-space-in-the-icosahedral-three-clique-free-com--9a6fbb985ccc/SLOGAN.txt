The three-clique-free complex of the twelve-vertex icosahedral graph is an integral Moore space \(M(\mathbb Z/2,4)\), not a wedge of spheres: its only nonzero reduced integral homology is \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z/2\). An explicit acyclic matching reduces the pure five-dimensional complex to one zero-cell, one four-cell and one five-cell attached by degree two, hence proves non-shellability.
