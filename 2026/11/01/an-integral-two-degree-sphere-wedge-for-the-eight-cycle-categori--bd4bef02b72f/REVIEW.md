# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Every face is generated by an exact one-vertex induction and independently counted by a cyclic column transfer. The matching covers all nonempty faces except 52 critical cells and its full oriented Hasse graph is acyclic (196226 vertices and 1457216 directed incidences). The exact signed Morse differential has rank 21 and all 21 nonzero Smith factors equal one; an explicit maximal unit minor provides an independent integrality witness. Since the Morse CW model has a single zero-cell and only cells of dimensions four and five, the integral attaching matrix determines the full homotopy type by Hurewicz and cell cancellation, not merely its homology.

## Originality
PASS under current evidence. Actual comparisons include the 2025 path-factor graph-product results and the 2023/2024 cycle-product paper's principal theorems and displayed small-case examples. The latter leaves open Moore summands when cycle length is not divisible by three; its printed examples include the eight-cycle with three colors, but not this four-color case. Related indexed records and semantic-result searches returned a seven-cycle/four-color calculation, which is neither the same graph nor a dominating result. No source found implies the exact two-dimensional integral wedge classification; the searches cannot prove uniqueness, and unindexed computational work remains a risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The eight-cycle is the smallest even cycle beyond the published four-cycle result in the residue class two modulo three, and four colors is the first count beyond a published eight-cycle/three-color example. The earlier classification permits nontrivial Moore-space summands; resolving torsion and the precise integral homotopy type for this natural missing case has concrete value for testing the general torsion question. The result supplies an exact nontrivial mixed-dimensional CW calculation rather than a routine face count.
