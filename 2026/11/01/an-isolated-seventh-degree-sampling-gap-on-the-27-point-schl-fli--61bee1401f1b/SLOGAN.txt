On the 27-point Schläfli spherical four-design, exact-degree harmonic sampling has full rank first at degree \(5\), unexpectedly loses only the constant sector at degree \(7\), and is full rank at every degree \(k\geq8\). The complete normalized Gegenbauer Gram spectrum and all nine exceptional low-degree ranks are explicit; the only positive harmonic-index cancellation degrees are \(\{1,2,3,4,7\}\).
