For the 462-vertex odd graph \(O_6=KG(11,5)\), the Gaussian kernel \(e^{-t d^p}\) is positive definite at every \(t>0\) exactly when \(0\le p\le\alpha\), where \(\alpha\) is the unique positive zero of \(2-2\cdot2^p-7\cdot3^p-2\cdot4^p+8\cdot5^p\) (approximately \(0.355636232374\)). The sharp obstruction comes from the fourth, not the top, Johnson harmonic; the boundary squared-Euclidean embedding dimension is exactly \(296\).
