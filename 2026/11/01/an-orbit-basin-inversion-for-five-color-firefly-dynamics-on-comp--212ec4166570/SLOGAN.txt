For the five-color inhibitory firefly automaton on every complete bipartite graph, all trajectories become periodic within \(25\) updates, and the only periods are \(5\), \(6\), and \(18\), with exact primitive-orbit and basin formulas. On balanced growing bicliques, almost every primitive orbit has period \(18\), but almost every initial configuration enters a period-\(5\) orbit after exactly \(8\) updates.
