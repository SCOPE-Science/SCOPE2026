For two normalized tokens with positive softmax similarity scores and a negative-definite anisotropic value map, an antipodal eigenvector pair becomes a robust period-two attractor exactly when \(\tanh\alpha>s_2/s_1\) and \(\eta>2/[(s_1+s_d)\tanh\alpha]\). The second-strongest negative mode controls whether attraction is possible, while the weakest negative mode controls its sharp gain threshold; isotropic repulsion has no exponentially attracting antipodal eigencycle.
