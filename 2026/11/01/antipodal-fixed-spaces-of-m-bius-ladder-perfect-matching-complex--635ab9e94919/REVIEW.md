# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact orbit correspondence, the finite good cover by spoke stars, and the classification of complete cycle-dimer tilings prove the parity theorem for all \(n\ge2\). Independent original-graph enumeration through \(n=8\) confirms every orbit-face description and all nonzero reduced mod-two Betti numbers. The finite tests are not an infinite proof; the nerve theorem and cell-attachment argument provide that proof. The \(n=2\) case is handled directly. The even Smith equality uses the published ambient homotopy type in [1].

## Originality — PASS, residual risk noted

The exact monomer/dimer description was already stated in [2], which computes a homological involution character. This finding makes no novelty claim for that orbit description, ambient Betti ranks, or a derived Lefschetz value. The new complete geometric fixed-space homotopy classification follows from a contractible core with zero or two missing top facets, neither of which is asserted in [1–3]. The stronger odd-width character in [3] also does not determine the actual fixed set. Equivalently stated but unindexed research remains possible.

## Value — PASS

This settles a natural all-width topological fixed-point question, gives arbitrarily high-dimensional two-sphere bouquets versus contractible odd cases, and shows Smith total-Betti equality at all even widths. Unlike an ambient rational character computation, it captures fixed-space homotopy rather than traces.
