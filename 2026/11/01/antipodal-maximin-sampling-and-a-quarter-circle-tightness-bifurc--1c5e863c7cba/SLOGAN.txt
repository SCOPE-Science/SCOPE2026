For three consecutive Fourier modes observed on two equal circular arcs, antipodal placement uniquely maximizes worst-case captured energy, but minimizing departure from tightness favors a strictly non-antipodal geometry whenever each arc has half-width below \(\pi/4\). The two design criteria exchange agreement at an exact square-root bifurcation, and the tightness-optimal design loses asymptotically one half of the worst-case energy as arc width tends to zero.
