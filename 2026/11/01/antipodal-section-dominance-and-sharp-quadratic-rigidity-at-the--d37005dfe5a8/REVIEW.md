# Review of antipodal section dominance

**Correctness — PASS.** The pair-of-sections formula follows from affine slicing; the exact identity for the two radicands and the unique maximum of a one-variable quotient prove the sharp inequality. The critical-radius converse uses volume-preserving tracefree exponential changes of shape. The anisotropy/translation Hessian and spherical moment contraction were calculated symbolically and checked numerically. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** Full public text of the 2026 global ellipsoid Barker–Larman result confirms that the affine section-volume formula is previously available and not being claimed as new. Its injectivity theorem does not state a fixed-volume universal paired bound or the joint stability Hessian. A second full text treats normalized nested-body cap fractions rather than pairs of opposite section volumes. The new optimization is algebraically short, and earlier undocumented coverage remains the main risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A global sharp all-direction antipodal section extremum, unique critical radius and positive definite joint translation/anisotropy stability are natural quantitative complements to noncentral geometric tomography. The result treats arbitrary ellipsoid center and shape, not a special axisymmetric family.

**Limitations.** Ellipsoids only; no general-convex-body theorem, and no global quantitative stability constant beyond exact local Hessian. Independently audited validity or historical priority is not asserted.
