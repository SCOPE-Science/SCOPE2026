## Correctness — PASS

Each Hardy monomial is mapped into the corresponding orthogonal target monomial with exact amplitude \((k+1)^{-\alpha}|\sum_j a_j\rho_j^k|\). The counting law follows from character equidistribution on the actual compact cyclic subgroup and an independent slow-variable Riemann sum. ## Mathematical value — PASS

The sharp coefficient is a natural invariant of compact inclusion combined with a finite rotation sum. Interference prevents inferring it by addition of single-rotation singular-value constants. The two-map case gives a complete arithmetic classification, including even/odd rational denominators, and the unusual contrast of a globally norm-Lipschitz operator family with a leading spectral coefficient discontinuous at every rational angle. The exact weak-Schatten endpoint and logarithmic coefficient provide quantitative information beyond class membership.

## Scientific boundaries

Exact coefficient Hilbert norms are essential for the constants. The statement is not an asymptotic for general nonlinear composition maps, and no quantitative convergence rate is asserted. Broad historical almost-periodic rearrangement theory is a residual originality risk. Finite tests serve solely as checks on algebra and sample behavior.
