Finite sums of circle-rotation composition operators from \(H^2\) to coefficient-weighted Dirichlet spaces have a sharp singular-value residue determined by the Haar mean of their combined oscillatory symbol. In a norm-Lipschitz two-rotation family, that residue is \(4/\pi\) at every irrational angle but takes explicit, parity-dependent values at every rational angle, making it discontinuous exactly at the rationals.
