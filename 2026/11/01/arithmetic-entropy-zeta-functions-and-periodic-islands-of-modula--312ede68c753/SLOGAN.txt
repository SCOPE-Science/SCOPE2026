For the continuous affine interval map interpolating modular addition by \(q\) on \(n\) equally spaced knots, topological entropy depends only on \(n/\gcd(n,q)\), while each nontrivial common divisor creates positive-measure identity-return islands. For coprime shifts, every iterate has finitely many fixed points and the complete Artin–Mazur zeta function is \((1-z-\cdots-z^{n-1})^{-1}\).
