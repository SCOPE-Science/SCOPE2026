For the four-dimensional \(\ell^1\) unit ball, integer-frequency Fourier coefficients vanish except when at least three absolute coordinate frequencies coincide, leaving exactly \(64N^2-8N+1\) nonzero frequencies in the cube of half-width \(N\). Every positive integral dilation has exactly three integer-lattice translate-coverage levels, with fixed volume fractions \(1/2,1/3,1/6\) and an exact quadratic scaling rule.
