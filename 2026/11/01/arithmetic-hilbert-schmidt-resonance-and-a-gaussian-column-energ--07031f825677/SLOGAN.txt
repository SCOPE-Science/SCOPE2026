For finite atomic Hausdorff operators on Hardy space, commutators with the bounded oscillatory multiplier \(h_\tau(z)=(1-z)^{i\tau}\) are Hilbert–Schmidt exactly at simultaneous logarithmic resonances of the atoms. Outside resonance their canonical degree-truncated squared Hilbert–Schmidt norms grow as \(\sqrt K\), with an exact additive Gaussian coefficient that cannot be canceled by complex phases.
