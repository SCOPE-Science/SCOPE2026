On \(GP(n,3)\), binary neighbor-parity dynamics is reversible exactly when five does not divide \(n\); otherwise a squared fifth-cyclotomic factor produces a sharp transient clock of \(2^{\nu_2(n)-1}\) updates for even \(n\) (one update for odd \(n\)). Exact stable ranks and fixed-point counts quantify the resulting arithmetic collapse of periodic configurations.
