The singular-tip radial Toeplitz spectrum on a generalized Hartogs triangle undergoes an exact rational–irrational transition: for reduced \(\gamma=P/Q\), power and inner-collar compressions have discrete infinite-multiplicity spectra, while for irrational \(\gamma\) both have full essential spectrum \([0,1]\) with dense simple eigenvalues. Their sharp inverse, concentration, and Hausdorff spectral-gap constants give spectral-set continuity precisely at irrational exponents and discontinuity at every rational exponent.
