A three-parameter family of simple perfect evolution algebras has an exactly computable cyclic automorphism extension: \(\operatorname{Aut}(E)\cong C_g\times C_{m(2^h-1)/g}\), where \(g=\gcd(m,2^h-1,r)\). The diagonal-by-rotation extension splits precisely when \(\gcd(m,2^h-1)\) divides the twist \(r\), producing nonsplit examples of unbounded order.
