# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each fixed 0<theta<pi, Lu–Zu Theorem 3.4 supplies a core spectrum consisting of 1 and the signed pairs ±|alpha_n|; multiplicity two is necessary. The two-point Fisher–Hartwig OPUC expansion at distinct unit roots supplies alpha_n=2cos((n+3)theta)/n+O_theta(n^-2), with theta fixed. For t→0, discard n<t^(-3/4) (o(1/t) terms) and bound the remaining coefficient error by O(t^(3/2))=o(t); equidistribution of irrational rotations or exact finite rational orbits then yields t*N_C(t)→4*mean|cos|. Positive counting coefficient plus monotone inversion gives N*s_N→Lambda rather than merely a logarithmic Cesàro result. The exact cosine averages give irrational 8/pi, rational odd q 4 csc(pi/(2q))/q and even q 4 cot(pi/(2q))/q, with sharp bounds 2 and 8/3, extremizers q=2 and q=3. This is fixed-angle only, excludes root coalescence theta=0,pi, and does not claim a quantified rate. Independently reconstructed Toeplitz moment solves at 20 (angle,index) pairs checked the oscillatory phase; those checks cannot prove the infinite asymptotic.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge original claim is the decreasing-rearrangement Weyl law, the complete rational/irrational angular coefficient, and its extremizers/continuity—not the OPUC spectrum or the Fisher–Hartwig asymptotic themselves. Lu–Zu already gives the entire unrearranged core spectrum and trace-class characterization; Martínez-Finkelshtein et al. already gives the general Verblunsky leading term. Both are powerful direct inputs, but neither identified published theorem states the full rearranged counting result and arithmetic extremum, and no dominating multi-frequency counting theorem was found. The overlap is mathematically close and this priority judgment has explicit substantive residual risk. The Lu–Zu entire preprint could not be read: arXiv and lawful reader attempts failed, and authorized institutional follow-up was safety-blocked; title/abstract and available secondary scientific description only were inspected, not a whole-paper noncoverage conclusion.

## value

PASS

A genuine spectral invariant at the critical weak trace-class threshold of the simplest two-distinct-toral-root homogeneous bidisc core family. An exact, discontinuous angular Weyl coefficient and sharp extremal root arrangements are finer than generic Schatten membership or the unrearranged OPUC asymptotic and can be used to distinguish modules. Although derived from two strong established theorems, the counting-law step and the arithmetic classification answer natural all-angle questions rather than a single numerical substitution.
