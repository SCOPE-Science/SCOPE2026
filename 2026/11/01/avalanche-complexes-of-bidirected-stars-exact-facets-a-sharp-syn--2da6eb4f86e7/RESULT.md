# Avalanche complexes of bidirected stars: exact facets, a sharp synchronization threshold, and degree-zero persistence

## Finding

Let \(n\geq1\). Give the star with centre \(z\) and leaves \(V=\{v_1,\ldots,v_n\}\) both directed arcs along every edge. For arbitrary initial nonnegative integer chips \(h\) at \(z\) and \(a_i\) at \(v_i\), apply parallel chip firing: a vertex fires exactly when its chips are at least its outdegree, and all firing vertices fire simultaneously. The **avalanche complex** \(\mathcal A\) is the simplicial complex generated by all sets of simultaneously firing vertices, including repeated-state dynamics.

Put \(s=h+\sum_{i=1}^n a_i\), \(I=\{v_i:a_i>0\}\), and let \(\Delta(U)\) be the full simplex on \(U\) (with the realization of \(\Delta(\varnothing)\) empty). For \(n\leq s<2n\), set
\[
\tau=\min\left\{t\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}:h+\sum_{i=1}^n\min(t,a_i)\geq n\right\},\qquad
R=\{v_i:a_i>\tau\}.
\]
Then the following are **equalities of abstract simplicial complexes**, not just homotopy equivalences:
\[
\boxed{\mathcal A=\begin{cases}
\Delta(I),&s<n,\\
\Delta(V)\cup\Delta(R\cup\{z\}),&n\leq s<2n,\\
\Delta(V\cup\{z\}),&s\geq2n.
\end{cases}}
\]
Here all unions retain the displayed vertex labels. Consequently the geometric realization of \(\mathcal A\) is empty if no vertex ever fires, is homotopy equivalent to \(S^0\) in exactly the cases described below, and is contractible otherwise. Define \(M=\max_i a_i\) and \(m=|\{i:a_i=M\}|\). The disconnected case has the **initial-data criterion**
\[
\boxed{|\mathcal A|\simeq S^0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad n\leq s<2n\ \text{ and }\ s-m<n.}
\]
The simultaneous firing set is **all \(n+1\) graph vertices** at some time if and only if \(s\geq2n\). Thus \(2n\) is the sharp synchronization mass threshold for the bidirected star.

The time-prefix filtration \(\mathcal A_t\), generated by firing sets at integer times \(0\) through \(t\), has no reduced homology in any positive degree for any \(t\). Its entire positive-degree persistence is zero over \(\mathbb Z\) and every field. Its reduced degree-zero persistence is either zero or one infinite interval. The latter occurs precisely in the boxed disconnected cases: its birth time is \(\tau\) if \(M>0\), and time \(1\) if \(M=0\). There are no finite nonzero reduced degree-zero intervals.

## Assumptions and scope

The graph is a finite simple undirected star interpreted as a digraph with both orientations of each edge; it has **no sink**. Every initial chip count \(h,a_1,\ldots,a_n\) is an arbitrary nonnegative integer. The outdegree of \(z\) is \(n\), and each leaf has outdegree \(1\). Firing is synchronous, with threshold \(\geq\), and chips are conserved. The time-prefix filtration starts at \(t=0\); if there are no firing vertices, its abstract complex has only the empty simplex and its geometric realization is empty. Reduced homology of an empty realization is not being classified as ordinary degree-zero persistence.

## Proof

Write \(H_t\in\{0,1\}\) for the centre-firing indicator at time \(t\), \(L_t\subseteq V\) for the leaves firing then, and \(b_i(t)\) and \(c(t)\) for the corresponding chip counts. Their exact recurrences are
\[
 b_i(t+1)=b_i(t)-\mathbf1_{\{b_i(t)>0\}}+H_t,\qquad
 c(t+1)=c(t)-nH_t+|L_t|.
\]
Before the centre first fires, \(b_i(t)=\max(a_i-t,0)\) and \(c(t)=h+\sum_i\min(t,a_i)\). In particular, if \(s<n\), the centre can never fire. The leaf firing sets are then nested decreasing subsets of \(I\), whose first set is \(I\). This proves the first case, including the empty configuration.

For \(s\geq n\), the centre fires eventually: otherwise the finitely many leaf chips are exhausted and the centre acquires all \(s\geq n\) chips, contradicting nonfiring. Let \(\tau\) be its first firing time. At that time the leaf set is \(R\). Any earlier firing set consists solely of a subset of \(I\).

After the centre fires, **every leaf fires at the next time**, since
\(b_i(\tau+1)=b_i(\tau)-\mathbf1_{\{b_i(\tau)>0\}}+1\geq1\). Thus \(\Delta(V)\) is present in the final avalanche complex. If the centre also fires at time \(\tau+1\), the whole vertex set fires simultaneously and \(\mathcal A=\Delta(V\cup\{z\})\).

Suppose instead that the centre does not fire at \(\tau+1\). Since all \(n\) leaves fire then, it necessarily fires at \(\tau+2\), with a leaf set contained in \(R\): its chips have increased by \(|R|\) relative to time \(\tau\), whereas leaf chips at \(\tau+2\) are \(\max(b_i(\tau)-1,0)\). The same argument repeats. **Until an all-vertex firing event occurs**, successive centre firings are separated by one all-leaf firing step; their concomitant leaf sets are nested decreasing subsets of the original \(R\), and each leaf's count at successive centre firings decreases by one while positive.

If the process never fires all vertices together, after finitely many such pairs the centre fires with no leaf, has all \(s\) chips, and the next step has all leaves active. If \(s\geq2n\), the centre still has at least \(n\) chips at that next step, so **all vertices fire together**, a contradiction. If \(n\leq s<2n\), on the other hand an all-vertex firing is impossible by chip conservation: it would require at least \(n\) chips at the centre and at least one chip at each of the \(n\) leaves, hence at least \(2n\) total. All centre-containing firing faces are then contained in \(\Delta(R\cup\{z\})\), which is itself a firing simplex at time \(\tau\). Combined with the all-leaf simplex, this proves the middle case and the last case.

For the homotopy classification, if \(R\neq\varnothing\), the two simplices \(\Delta(V)\) and \(\Delta(R\cup\{z\})\) meet along the nonempty simplex \(\Delta(R)\); the union collapses onto \(\Delta(V)\). If \(R=\varnothing\), the middle case is the disjoint union of an \((n-1)\)-simplex and the isolated centre vertex, hence has type \(S^0\). Both outer cases are simplices except the expressly empty first case.

It remains to eliminate the stopping-time variable from the disconnected criterion. For \(M\geq1\), \(R=\varnothing\) is equivalent to \(\tau\geq M\), which is equivalent to
\[
 h+\sum_i\min(M-1,a_i)<n.
\]
Since precisely \(m\) leaves attain the maximum \(M\), the left side is \(s-m\). For \(M=0\), all leaves initially have zero chips, and the centre fires at time zero whenever \(s\geq n\); here \(m=n\), so under \(n\leq s<2n\), the inequality \(s-m<n\) also holds. The boxed criterion follows.

For time prefixes, before the first centre firing the firing sets are nested leaf sets and thus form at most one simplex. The first centre-containing simplex either intersects this leaf simplex (if \(R\neq\varnothing\)) or gives an isolated centre. At time \(\tau+1\) all leaves form one full simplex; all subsequent centre-containing faces are nested within \(\Delta(R\cup\{z\})\) unless the entire vertex set fires, in which case the filtration becomes a full simplex. Thus every nonempty prefix is contractible or is the disjoint union of a contractible leaf component and an isolated centre. The second possibility persists forever exactly when \(R=\varnothing\) and \(n\leq s<2n\). When \(M>0\), leaf firing begins at time zero and the centre appears alone at time \(\tau\), giving birth \(\tau\). When \(M=0\), the centre fires alone initially and the leaves first fire at time \(1\), giving birth \(1\). No later firing set joins those components. This proves the full persistence assertion.

## Verification

The independent standard-library checker `verify.py` constructs the complete parallel-firing orbit until stabilization or the first repeated configuration. It builds **all nonempty faces** from the actual firing sets, then compares those faces exactly with the three-case simplex formula. It independently checks the inequality \(n\leq s<2n\) and \(s-m<n\), as well as the number of connected components in **every time prefix** and its predicted birth time.

It completed 27,954 initial configurations across \(1\leq n\leq7\), checking 65,453 firing-time prefixes and identifying 243 predicted two-component configurations: `VERIFY_OK cases=27954 firing_steps=65453 two_component_cases=243`. The checker is corroborative; the unrestricted theorem follows from the displayed recurrences and argument, not from bounded enumeration.

Run `python3 verify.py` in any standard Python 3 installation.

## Relationship to prior work

Riihimäki and Smith introduced avalanche complexes and the associated persistent homology, with homotopy calculations focused on directed paths and directed cycles [1]. The present result concerns arbitrary chip counts on a **bidirected star**, which is not classified there. Scully, Jiang, and Zhang determined broad restrictions on periodic parallel chip-firing patterns [2]. Their firing-pattern results give valuable dynamical background, but do not identify the finite-transient avalanche simplices or this exact all-initial-data facet classification and filtered homotopy. In particular, knowing eventual firing period alone does not determine the avalanche complex, which retains all transient firing sets. Work on firing periods of directed graphs [3] is similarly distinct from exact simplicial topology.

## Limitations

No claim is made for two-hub trees, arbitrary larger trees, nonsymmetric orientations, or different firing thresholds. The original graph is always a bidirected star, with no dissipative sink, and the filtration is by ordinary parallel-firing time. The homotopy statements are non-equivariant. Independent external mathematical validation has not been performed.
