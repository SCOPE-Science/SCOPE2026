Every avalanche complex of a bidirected star, for arbitrary nonnegative initial chips, is exactly a simplex, two simplices glued along a face, two disjoint simplices, or the empty realization; its only possible nontrivial homotopy type is \(S^0\). The complete-chip synchronization threshold is exactly \(2n\), and the time filtration has at most one infinite reduced degree-zero persistence bar and no positive-degree bars.
