For Haar-random hyperplanes through a point moving from the centroid toward a vertex of a regular simplex, the expected number of section vertices has an exact two-angle formula and strictly decreases in dimensions at least three. The full vertex-count distribution develops a sharp axial critical window: displacement of order \(1/\sqrt n\) modifies the central chi-square fluctuation coefficient, while every fixed positive displacement produces a nondegenerate quadratic-Gaussian-CDF limit after scaling by \(n^2\). The vertex endpoint has an exact uniform-rank law and a discontinuous mean.
