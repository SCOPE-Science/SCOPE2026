On every standard weighted analytic Bergman disk space, Toeplitz compression of the non-decaying boundary chirp \(\cos(c/(1-|z|^2)+\vartheta)\) belongs to all Schatten classes but has infinitely many eigenvalues of each sign. Its positive and negative eigenvalue counts both have the sharp law \(N_\pm(t)\sim\log^2(1/t)/(4c)\), independently of the weight and the phase.
