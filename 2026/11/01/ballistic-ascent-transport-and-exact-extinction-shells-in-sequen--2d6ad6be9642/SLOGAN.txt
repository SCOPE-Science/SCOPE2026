For the left-to-right sequential closed-neighborhood bi-threshold rule with thresholds \((1,3)\) on every finite open path, each \(01\) ascent travels exactly one vertex left per sweep, forcing all configurations to die in at most \(n\) sweeps. The exact extinction-shell sizes are \((n-t+1)2^{t-2}\) for \(2\le t\le n\); under uniform initialization the deficit \(n-T_n\) converges to a negative-binomial law with probabilities \((k+1)/2^{k+2}\).
