For a frustum joining any convex base to a homothetic copy of relative size \(\lambda\), the maximum-volume inscribed ellipsoid depends only on the base's John ellipsoid, even when the base is asymmetric or nonsmooth. In ambient dimension \(n\), its contact structure changes sharply at \(\lambda=(n-1)/(n+1)\), and its exact volume profile is continuously differentiable but not twice differentiable there.
