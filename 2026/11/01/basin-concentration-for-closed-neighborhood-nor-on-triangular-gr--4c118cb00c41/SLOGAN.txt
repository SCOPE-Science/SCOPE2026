For closed-neighborhood NOR dynamics on the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\), all attractors are two-cycles, yet one uniquely dominant cycle attracts \(1-\binom n2 2^{-(2n-3)}(1+O(n2^{-n}))\) of uniformly random initial states. Every individual attraction-basin size has an exact formula in terms of labelled graphs without isolated vertices, even though the number of distinct cycles grows exponentially.
