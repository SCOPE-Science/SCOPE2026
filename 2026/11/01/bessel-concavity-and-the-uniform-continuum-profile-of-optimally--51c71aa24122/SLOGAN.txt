The optimally smooth second-difference averaging kernels of Richardson have a universal, compactly supported, strictly concave Bessel-law continuum profile: \((n+1)u_n(k)\to K(k/(n+1))\) uniformly over every coefficient. Its transform is \(-\pi\cos\sqrt{t^2+\pi^2/4}/t^2\) (continuously extended at zero), giving an exact, nonparabolic density for the nearly Epanechnikov-shaped optimizers.
