The Lebesgue-moment Hilbert operator fails even to map the constant Dirichlet function into the Dirichlet space, so the approach to this boundary is mathematically forced rather than an arbitrary tuning. The result provides the whole fixed-rank blow-up profile, including the sharp operator norm and Hilbert–Schmidt divergence, with nontrivial Bessel zeros rather than only upper and lower growth estimates. The logarithmic Volterra limit identifies why the spectral profile is universal for this precise beta-moment family. The result does not claim a general theorem for other measures or growing singular-value ranks.
