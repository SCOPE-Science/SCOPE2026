As the beta weight of the classical Dirichlet-space Hankel operator approaches Lebesgue measure, every fixed singular value diverges with an exact constant given by the reciprocal of a positive \(J_0\) Bessel zero: \(\varepsilon s_k(H_\varepsilon)\to 1/j_{0,k}\). The Hilbert–Schmidt norm has the simultaneous sharp law \(\varepsilon\|H_\varepsilon\|_{\mathcal S_2}\to1/2\), arising from a logarithmically rescaled Volterra kernel.
