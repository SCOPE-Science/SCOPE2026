Synchronous binary neighbor-parity on every gear graph has one of three exact nilpotent Jordan profiles: \((1)\) for odd rim parameter, \((3,1,1)\) when the parameter is twice odd, and \((e,e,1)\) when four divides it, with \(e=2^{\nu_2(n)}\). A polynomial-gcd formula determines every return-time population, while these Jordan profiles determine all transient-entry populations; power-of-two gear graphs are nilpotent.
