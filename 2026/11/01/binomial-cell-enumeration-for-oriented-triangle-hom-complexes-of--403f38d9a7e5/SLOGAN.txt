For the oriented three-cycle mapped into the standard cyclic tournament on \(2m+1\) vertices, the entire directed Hom cell polynomial is \((2m+1)\binom{m+1}{2}(1+t)^{m-1}\) for every \(m\geq1\). Target translations act freely on cells, and all cases with \(m\geq2\) have Euler characteristic zero and are noncontractible; the statement does not classify their homotopy types.
