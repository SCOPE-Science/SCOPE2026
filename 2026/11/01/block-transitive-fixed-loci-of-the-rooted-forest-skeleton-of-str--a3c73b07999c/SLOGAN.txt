For the canonical rooted-forest subcomplex of strong discrete Morse matchings on a simplex, independently transitive permutations of \(t\) nontrivial label blocks leave a geometric fixed locus depending only on \(t\) and the number \(f\) of fixed labels—not on the block sizes. The locus is empty for \(f=0\), contractible for \(f=1\), and a wedge of \((f-1)^{f-1+t}\) spheres of dimension \(f+t-2\) for \(f\ge2\).
