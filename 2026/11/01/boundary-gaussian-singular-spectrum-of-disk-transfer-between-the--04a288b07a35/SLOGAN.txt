The hard-disk projection from the analytic Gaussian Fock level into the first excited Landau level has exactly computable singular values \(a_n(x)=e^{-x}x^{n+1}/\sqrt{n!(n+1)!}\). Their spectral mass lives in a \(\sqrt{x}\)-wide Gaussian angular-momentum window, obeys sharp Schatten and counting laws, and approaches—but never attains—the universal operator norm \((2\pi)^{-1/2}\).
