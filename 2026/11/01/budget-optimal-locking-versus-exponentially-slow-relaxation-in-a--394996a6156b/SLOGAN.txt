For a symmetric frequency-detuned Kuramoto triad under a fixed off-diagonal coupling budget, the exact locally attracting locking threshold falls strictly with the frequency-weight exponent, approaching but never reaching two-thirds of the extreme frequency. Yet the slow transverse relaxation rate decays as \(2^{-p}\) at large exponent, so every budget in the onset-crossover window has an optimal finite exponent for locking speed rather than simply favoring maximal reallocation.
