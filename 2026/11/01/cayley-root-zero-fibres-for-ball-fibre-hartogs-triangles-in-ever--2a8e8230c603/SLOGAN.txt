For \(\Omega_n=\{(z,w):\|z\|^2<|w|<1\}\), every Bergman-kernel zero on a positive radial-product slice is a Cayley transform of an explicitly known root of unity, giving an exact dimension-dependent count. The zeros form a sharp \((1-t)^{2/(n+1)}\) boundary fan, and every dimension \(n\ge2\) has such zeros on every slice.
