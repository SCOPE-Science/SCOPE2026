The closed-neighborhood complex of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus a perfect matching is a circle at \(n=3\) and, for \(n\ge4\), a wedge of \(n^2-3n+1\) two-spheres and two \((n-2)\)-spheres. For \(n\ge5\), its integral homology symmetry modules and all \(S_n\times C_2\) character values follow from a two-piece crown-graph intersection and a signed Mayer--Vietoris connection.
