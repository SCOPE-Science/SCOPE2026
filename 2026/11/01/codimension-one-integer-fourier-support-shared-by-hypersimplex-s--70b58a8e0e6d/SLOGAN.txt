For every nontrivial hypersimplex \(\Delta(s,n)\) with \(n\ge5\), the only possible nonzero integer Fourier coefficients lie where at least two frequency coordinates coincide, and every frequency with exactly one such equality has an explicit nonzero coefficient proportional to \((-1)^{s-1}\binom{n-2}{s-1}\). Across all interior slice levels the number of nonzero coefficients in an \((n-1)\)-dimensional frequency box has the same sharp leading term \(\binom n2(2N)^{n-2}\), up to an error \(O_n(N^{n-3})\).
