For radial power-weight Fock spaces with \(m>2\), Berezin transforms at arbitrarily many weight parameters admit a common escaping wave-packet family whose approximate eigenvalues trace the Gaussian heat curve \((e^{-s/\alpha_1},\ldots,e^{-s/\alpha_d})\). This gives explicit essential-norm lower bounds for polynomial combinations and a strictly positive quantitative essential-norm gap for every pair of distinct parameters.
