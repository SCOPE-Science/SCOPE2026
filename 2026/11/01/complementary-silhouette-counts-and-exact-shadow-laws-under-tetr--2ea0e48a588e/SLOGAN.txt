For every nondegenerate tetrahedron, complementary sets of parallel-to-opposite-face vertex truncations produce orthogonal shadow polygons whose vertex counts always sum to twelve when viewed from the same generic direction. For a regular original tetrahedron, all five truncation stages have explicit and depth-independent shadow-vertex distributions; full truncation yields only octagons and nonagons, with nonagon probability equal to the original triangular-shadow probability.
