# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The complete pointwise claim is derived, not inferred from enumeration. For each ordered hyperbola pair, the two prescribed sum coordinates must have the same ring valuation; when both are zero there are exactly \(U\) ordered pairs. At common finite valuation \(a\), the two equations reduce to a monic quadratic congruence modulo \(\pi^{k-a}\), with exactly \(q^a\) parameter lifts per root. Unit discriminants have either two or no roots by the odd-residue-characteristic Hensel lemma. Unit-coordinate pairs reduce to square roots of \(D=s^2-4s/t\), giving \(q^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\) zero-discriminant roots or \(2q^j\) for even valuation \(2j<k\) and square unit part. These exact root counts exhaust all output positions and establish the table of multiplicities. Summing the table with weights \(r\), \(r^2\), and \(1\) yields respectively \(U^2\), the energy formula, and the support cardinality. The limiting density follows by two geometric sums. Independent exhaustive exact-integer tests cover eight cyclic rings and three noncyclic chain rings, including residue-field cardinality nine. Limit: finite tests are not a universal proof; the proof is valid only for commutative chain rings with odd residue characteristic.

## Originality — PASS, bounded comparison

The claimed contribution is the complete **planar two-sum multiplicity function**, its disjoint multiplicity strata, and ring-universal support/density for all finite commutative chain rings with odd residue field. Kowalski's 2010 Kloosterman fourth-moment exposition explicitly includes the finite-field symmetric-sums argument; that special case is prior work, not claimed new. Gong–Veys–Wan, arXiv:1306.4104 (2013), cover aggregate classical Kloosterman moments over prime-power cyclic rings, so the aggregate energy formula is explicitly not offered as an independent priority claim. Bower–Evans–Luo–Miller, arXiv:1212.2930 (2012), study coordinate sums and differences of modular hyperbolas, not the planar Minkowski self-sum. Their inspected central statements do not imply the complete ring-universal pointwise multiplicity table. Related published mathematical records include a finite-field sharp fourth-moment theorem, a prime-power hyperbola orthogonality classification, and a twisted reciprocal-hyperbola Fourier sampling result. Their claims concern other operators or outputs, not the complete pointwise two-sum distribution here. The comparison supports only a bounded originality assessment, not a guarantee of global priority. Residual risk: an older local-congruence or representation-number theorem could contain the classification in equivalent notation.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The full additive twofold sum of the modular hyperbola is a canonical discrete-harmonic invariant of a natural algebraic curve over a nonfield. The result is an exact classification at every output point for **all** odd finite commutative chain rings, not an isolated calculation for a chosen modulus. It reveals that the nonzero support is controlled by equality of valuations and a single square class, and that different ring presentations with the same \((q,k)\) have precisely identical multiplicity distributions. The precise limiting support density, including its exceptional behavior at residue-field size three, is useful when measuring additive expansion and Fourier fourth moments of this surface over thicker local rings. The energy identity alone would be less distinctive and may already be implicit in historic Kloosterman-moment work; value is assessed for the joint structural classification.
