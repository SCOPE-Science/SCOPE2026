For the modular hyperbola \(H=\{(x,x^{-1}):x\in R^\times\}\) over every odd finite commutative chain ring, every value and multiplicity of the planar twofold additive convolution is determined by valuations and square classes. Its full value distribution is ring-universal and yields exact sumset densities and the fourth Fourier moment \(3(q-1)q^{2k-2}(k(q-1)-1)\) before Fourier normalization.
