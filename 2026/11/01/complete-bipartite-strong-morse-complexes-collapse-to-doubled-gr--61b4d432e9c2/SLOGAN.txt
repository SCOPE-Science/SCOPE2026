For every \(m,n\ge2\), the full strong discrete-Morse matching complex of \(K_{m,n}\) simplicially collapses onto a graph and has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{2mn-m-n+1}S^1\). Its graph spine contracts to the graph formed by doubling every edge of the original complete bipartite graph, sharply contrasting the higher-dimensional ordinary Morse complexes.
