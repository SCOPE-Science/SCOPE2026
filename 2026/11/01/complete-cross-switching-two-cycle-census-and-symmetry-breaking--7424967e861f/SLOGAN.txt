For a reflection-symmetric cubic voltage map, the exact number of interior cross-switching primitive two-cycles is a piecewise algebraic function and reaches four. Two asymmetric cycles merge with a symmetric cycle at \(\beta=(\mu+12)/(6\sqrt3)\), while an independent fold of symmetric cycles occurs at \(\beta=(\mu+8)^{3/2}/(6\sqrt{3\mu})\) for \(\mu>4\); near the symmetry merger both asymmetric cycles attract locally.
