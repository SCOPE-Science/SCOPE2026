For two unordered particles on every wheel with at least four rim vertices, the full dihedral action on rational first homology has trace \(n+1\) at the identity, trace \(1\) at nonidentity rotations, and reflection traces \(-2\) (odd rim) or \(-1,-3\) (the two even-rim reflection types). Exactly two classes are rotation-invariant but none is fixed by the full dihedral group; hence the ordinary full-symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
