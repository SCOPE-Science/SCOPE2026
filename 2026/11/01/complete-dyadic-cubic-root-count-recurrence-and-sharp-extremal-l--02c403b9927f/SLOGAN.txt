For uniformly random monic cubics modulo \(2^k\), an explicit three-step recursion determines every root-count probability from three small distributions and the classical quadratic square-root law. The largest attainable number of roots follows the exact three-phase progression \(2\cdot4^t,3\cdot4^t,5\cdot4^t\) at exponents \(3t+1,3t+2,3t+3\), and the numbers of maximizing cubics are \(2\cdot16^t,8\cdot16^t,16\cdot16^t\).
