Every orthonormal exponential basis of the dyadic parabola \(\{(x,x^2):x\in\mathbb Z/2^k\mathbb Z\}\) is specified uniquely by a 1-Lipschitz map on \(\mathbb Z/2^{k-1}\mathbb Z\) and one of four top-bit edges over each input residue. The exact number of unordered ambient frequency spectra is \(2^{2^{k+1}-2}\) for every \(k\ge1\), in contrast to the single-valued strictly contracting graph classification over odd prime powers.
