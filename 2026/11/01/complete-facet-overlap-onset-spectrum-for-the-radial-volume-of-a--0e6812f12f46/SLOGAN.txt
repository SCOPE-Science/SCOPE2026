For a uniform point in a centroid-centred regular \(d\)-simplex of inradius one, the Euclidean radial distribution has an explicit finite facet-exterior integral and \(d\) strictly ordered overlap onsets \(ho_{d,k}=\sqrt{dk/(d-k+1)}\). Every \(k\)-fold overlap starts with an exact positive gamma-function coefficient multiplying \((t-ho_{d,k})^{(d+k)/2}\), including the final vertex threshold.
