On every finite complete graph, synchronous three-colour cyclic dominance with an integer neighbour threshold has only fixed points and primitive three-cycles, with every other state reaching a fixed point in at most two updates. The exact orbit census exhibits a \(\sqrt N\) threshold window and, at \(\theta=\lfloor N/3\rfloor\), a sharp \(1/N\) periodic-state law whose coefficient depends on \(N\bmod3\) through factors \(1,3,6\).
