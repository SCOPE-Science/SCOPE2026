The complete integral graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of the unique McGee \((3,7)\)-cage passes from a wedge of thirteen circles through a single five-sphere before becoming contractible. The six distance-four four-cycles control a nontrivial orientation character on the sphere: half of its 32 graph symmetries reverse the top class, so the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic at that scale.
