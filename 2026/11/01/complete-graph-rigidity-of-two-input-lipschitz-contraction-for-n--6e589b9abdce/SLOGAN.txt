For every nonlinear exponent \(1<p<\infty\), \(p\ne2\), the weighted graph \(p\)-heat flow contracts the edge-Lipschitz distance between *all pairs of solutions* on a finite connected simple graph if and only if that graph is complete. Every noncomplete graph supports arbitrarily large normalized instantaneous two-input Lipschitz expansion, for every choice of positive conductances and vertex masses.
