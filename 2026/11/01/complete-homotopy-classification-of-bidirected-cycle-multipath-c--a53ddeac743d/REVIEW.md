# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The directed-path condition is explicitly fixed for bidirected simple cycles. The complete top-dimensional simplex classification follows from the forest edge bound and the fact that a Hamiltonian path of an underlying cycle has one consistent direction. The top boundary groups split into forward-only and reverse-only chains; each contributes the primitive fundamental cycle of an actual embedded sphere. Thus top homology has precisely two integral sphere-carried generators, with no index issue. For large polygons, the exact published connectivity and integral homology hypotheses allow a Hurewicz lift of every lower generator; the map from a bouquet of these spheres and the two embedded top spheres is an integral homology equivalence between simply connected CW complexes and hence a homotopy equivalence. The possible one-degree connectivity gap in the divisible-by-four case is closed by the first Hurewicz isomorphism and the published zero homology in that degree. The five-vertex case is separately shown simply connected by a connected ten-bridge triangle-relation graph; the four- and three-vertex cases have elementary explicit decompositions. The dependency-free finite checker independently enumerates faces for the smaller instances, verifies exact top cycles, and reproduces mod-two reduced Betti numbers through nine plus one vertices. Finite checks do not substitute for the all-parameter argument or independently validate the cited theorems.

## Originality — PASS
The nearest primary work is Caputi–Collari–Smith, arXiv:2601.05670v1 (first public 2026-01-09), whose full accessible text, especially Theorems 2.8 and 2.10 and Section 3, provides the homology and connectivity but explicitly leaves the wedge-of-spheres homotopy classification open. The published published-finding corpus result on dihedral rational homology contains the two coherent-orientation top cycles, which are acknowledged as prior knowledge; its theorem concerns rational group actions rather than an integral homotopy type. The new result combines the top-sphere embeddings with Hurewicz/Whitehead and verifies the two low-dimensional boundary situations, establishing all bidirected cycles rather than just homology representations. The older 2022 study treats directed polygons and other families and does not supply this bidirected classification. Search results did not expose an already stated covering homotopy theorem. **Residual risk:** because the top-sphere basis and integral connectivity were separately available, the main large-parameter argument is a short, previously unstated consequence of earlier ingredients; an already known but unindexed equivalent application is possible. This makes explicit claim-level comparison essential, not a claim of independent discovery of the input facts.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The nearest primary paper formulates the full sphere-wedge classification as a genuine open problem while proving that these complexes are never shellable. The new classification proves the predicted homotopy type for every cycle on at least four vertices, determines all sphere dimensions and multiplicities, and gives the disconnected three-vertex exception exactly. It supplies a conceptual topological mechanism (embedded primitive top spheres plus Hurewicz lifting) rather than merely recalculating known Betti numbers, and the five-vertex simply-connectivity bridge argument addresses the parameter not covered by the quoted connectivity bound. This is a natural complete classification of a family explicitly investigated in current literature. It is nonequivariant; no infinite-family checker or independent audit is claimed.
