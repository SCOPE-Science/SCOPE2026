For every bidirected simple cycle on \(n+1\ge4\) vertices, its multipath complex is an explicit wedge \(\bigvee^{b(n)}S^{k(n)}\vee\bigvee^2S^{n-1}\), where \(b(n)=3\) when \(n\equiv3\pmod4\) and \(1\) otherwise, and \(k(n)=n/2\) when \(4\mid n\), or \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) otherwise. The bidirected triangle is the exceptional disjoint union \(S^1\sqcup S^1\).
