## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is an exact statement for the full \(384\)-element automorphism group, not an infinite conjecture. The supplied standalone checker generates every one of the \(743\) faces of \(\operatorname{Ind}(Q_4)\), verifies a \(367\)-pair acyclic matching over all \(2816\) Hasse incidences with critical cells one vertex and seven three-cells, and recomputes ordinary simplicial mod-two boundary ranks. This proves homology is confined to degree three, rank seven (in fact the integral homotopy type is a seven-sphere wedge). Independently generated signed fixed-face Lefschetz sums and fixed-orbit independence-polynomial sums agree with the stated formula for all \(384\) automorphisms. Comparison of finite-group characters proves the rational module decomposition and its invariant dimensions. No finite data are extrapolated beyond this finite domain.
