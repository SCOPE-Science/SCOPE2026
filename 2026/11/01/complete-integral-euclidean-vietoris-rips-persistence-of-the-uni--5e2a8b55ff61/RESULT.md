# Complete integral Euclidean Vietoris–Rips persistence of the unit hexagonal prism

## Finding
Take the twelve vertices \(v_{i,h}=(\cos(\pi i/3),\sin(\pi i/3),h)\) in Euclidean three-space, where \(i\in\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\), \(h\in\{0,1\}\). Let \(K_r\) be the **inclusive** Vietoris–Rips complex: a subset is a simplex precisely when every Euclidean pairwise distance is at most \(r\). The complete homotopy filtration is
\[
K_r\simeq\begin{cases}
\bigvee^{11}S^0,&0\le r<1,\\
\bigvee^{7}S^1,&1\le r<\sqrt2,\\
S^1,&\sqrt2\le r<\sqrt3,\\
\bigvee^{3}S^2,&\sqrt3\le r<2,\\
S^5,&2\le r<\sqrt5,\\
\ast,&r\ge\sqrt5.
\end{cases}
\]
The complete *reduced integral persistence* barcode is eleven copies of \([0,1)\) in degree zero; six copies of \([1,\sqrt2)\) and one of \([1,\sqrt3)\) in degree one; three copies of \([\sqrt3,2)\) in degree two; and one copy of \([2,\sqrt5)\) in degree five. There are no other reduced bars or torsion. In particular six independent graph cycles die at the geometric diagonal scale \(\sqrt2\), but the longitudinal loop survives until \(\sqrt3\).

## Assumptions and scope
The distance is **ambient Euclidean chord distance**, not shortest-path distance along the prism graph or a continuous metric on its edges. All endpoints are included at their birth scales. Distinct squared pairwise distances among the twelve points are exactly \(1,2,3,4,5\). If \(d=\min(|i-j|,6-|i-j|)\), then
\[
\|v_{i,h}-v_{j,k}\|^2=(0,1,3,4)_d+\mathbf1_{h\ne k}.
\]
This identity enumerates all filtration simplices; scales strictly between its five square roots make no change.

## Proof
**Scales zero and one.** Below one the complex is twelve isolated points. At one its one-skeleton is precisely the ordinary hexagonal prism graph, and there are no triangles. It has twelve vertices, eighteen edges, one connected component and therefore seven independent integral cycles.

**The annular scale.** At squared threshold two the only maximal positive-dimensional simplices are the six tetrahedra on adjacent pairs of columns, one tetrahedron per rectangular lateral face of the prism. Their intersections are the existing shared rung edges or their subsets. Each tetrahedron admits two elementary collapses reducing it to a two-triangle triangulation of its corresponding lateral square, compatibly for all six faces; the remaining six squares form a triangulated annulus \(S^1\times[0,1]\). Its first homology is generated by either horizontal hexagon. Both circles were present in the threshold-one graph, and their images agree with the primitive annulus longitude. Thus the rank of the map in degree one from threshold one to squared threshold two is exactly one, killing exactly six of the seven graph cycles.

**The scale-three wedge.** At squared threshold three, all permissible simplices can be exhaustively constructed from the displayed exact integer-valued distance formula. There are \((12,42,52,18)\) simplices in dimensions zero through three. The program `prism_proof.py` reconstructs all 124 nonempty simplices, checks each of 29 free-face/coface elementary collapse pairs by recomputing current coface counts, and leaves a two-dimensional complex with \((12,31,23)\) cells. A deterministically seeded spanning tree collapses the one-skeleton to a bouquet with twenty loop generators and twenty-three triangular attaching words. Every substitution made by the standalone code is an elementary two-cell/generator elimination: the chosen generator occurs precisely once (with exponent \(+1\) or \(-1\)) in the selected relator, and that relator can therefore be used to eliminate its one-cell and two-cell, substituting its attaching path in the other words. The checker explicitly performs **twenty** such eliminations, leaving **three empty two-cell attaching words and no one-cell generators**. These are homotopy-preserving elementary CW expansions/collapses, proving a wedge of three two-spheres rather than merely a numerical homology calculation. The already proved annulus has first homology only, whereas this target has no first homology, so the surviving longitude dies at \(\sqrt3\). The analogous scale-two *graph-distance* homotopy type is known and is not independently asserted as new.

**The last two scales.** At squared threshold four, the sole forbidden vertex pairs are the six opposite-layer antipodal pairs \(\{v_{i,0},v_{i+3,1}\}\). This is a perfect matching on twelve vertices, and the flag complex of its complementary graph is the join of six zero-spheres, namely the boundary of a six-dimensional crosspolytope \(S^5\). At squared threshold five every vertex pair is allowed, so the complex is an eleven-simplex. The stage at squared threshold three has reduced homology only in degree two, while the stage at threshold four has it only in degree five, hence the three degree-two intervals die at two. The degree-five class dies at \(\sqrt5\). Together with the degree-one calculation, this determines all integral persistence maps and all bars. None of the stages has torsion.

## Verification
The accompanying `hexprism.py` independently enumerates all maximal cliques and simplices at each squared integer threshold. `prism_proof.py` repeats the middle-scale enumeration, explicitly validates the 29 elementary collapses, and checks the twenty generator eliminations, returning the final certificate `VERIFY_OK`. Python 3 and NetworkX are required (`requirements.txt`). The recorded executable output is `verification_output.txt`; rerunning the code in a fresh directory containing the embedded scripts reproduces it. The proof of the other stages rests on the exact distance and explicit annulus/crosspolytope identifications, not on a finite homology guess.

## Relationship to prior work
Adamaszek and Adams' 2015 preprint concerns Vietoris–Rips complexes of samples of the circle, not the twelve Euclidean vertices of a two-level prism. An earlier publicly archived classification, *Scale-two Rips homotopy types of finite circular ladders* (published-finding corpus, 2026), determines that the hexagonal prism's **graph-metric squared graph at graph radius two** is homotopy equivalent to three two-spheres. That existing conclusion exactly supplies one already known homotopy type in the present filtration. However, graph radius two simultaneously includes the Euclidean distance classes \(\sqrt2\) and \(\sqrt3\), and therefore does **not** recover the intermediate Euclidean annulus or the two distinct integral first-homology death times. The full Euclidean five-threshold barcode is the present claim; the known graph-radius-two wedge is expressly not claimed as new. Published classifications of Rips complexes of the regular Platonic solids concern other vertex configurations.

## Limitations
This is an exact result for one natural twelve-point Euclidean configuration with unit height and unit side length. It does not assert a formula for arbitrary heights, polygon sizes, continuous prism surfaces, or metric graph edge interiors. Reproducibility of the finite scale-three reduction is provided by an ordinary computer checker, not by an externally certified proof. The completeness of literature indexing cannot be guaranteed, and an unindexed equivalent Euclidean-prism computation is a residual originality risk. No independent audit has been performed.
