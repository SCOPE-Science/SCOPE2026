For the centered furtherness filtration on the finite poset of nonempty subsets of \([n]\), every positive-length reduced integral persistence interval has the exact scale-doubling form \([u,2u)\). If the center has size \(s\), degree \(a+b-1\) has precisely \(\binom{s-1}{a}\binom{n-s}{b}\) intervals with \(u=2^a(2^b-1)\), for \(0\le a<s\) and \(1\le b\le n-s\); ordinary degree zero additionally has one essential class born at \(0\).
