The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of any finite affine-plane incidence graph after deleting one complete parallel class has an explicit integral homotopy type at every threshold: circles, two families of sphere wedges, a higher-dimensional sphere wedge, then a point. For the order-three Pappus graph the successive nontrivial stages are \(\bigvee^{10}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{26}S^2\), and \(\bigvee^{64}S^5\), with complete all-field persistence.
