For a Haar-random orthogonal shadow of a regular tetrahedron, the entire joint distribution of projected area and its triangular-versus-quadrilateral silhouette has two explicit elementary densities. On the nonempty area band \(4\sqrt{2/3}<A<2\sqrt3\), the conditional probability of a triangular silhouette is exactly the area-independent constant \(8/(8+3\sqrt3)\), before dropping to zero at larger areas.
