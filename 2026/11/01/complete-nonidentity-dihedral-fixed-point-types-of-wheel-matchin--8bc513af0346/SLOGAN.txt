For every wheel \(W_n=C_n*K_1\) with \(n\geq5\), the actual geometric fixed-point spaces of its ordinary matching complex under **all nonidentity dihedral symmetries** are explicitly identified with independence complexes of paths, cycles, and their elementary disjoint unions. The classification distinguishes empty primitive-rotation loci, contractible odd-reflection loci, and integral spheres or two-sphere wedges determined by congruence classes.
