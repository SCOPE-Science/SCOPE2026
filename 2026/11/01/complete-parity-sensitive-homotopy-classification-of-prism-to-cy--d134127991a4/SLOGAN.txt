For every polygonal prism \(C_n\square K_2\) and every cycle target \(C_q\), the graph Hom complex has an explicit complete homotopy classification: odd targets yield one circle per nonextreme winding, even targets of length at least six yield two per winding when the prism is bipartite, and exact divisibility produces \(4q\) isolated maps. The exceptional square target folds to two points for even \(n\), while all even targets have an empty Hom complex for odd \(n\).
