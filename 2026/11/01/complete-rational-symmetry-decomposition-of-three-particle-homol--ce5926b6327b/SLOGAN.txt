For three unordered particles on \(K_{3,4}\), the full natural \(S_3\times S_4\)-action on rational second homology has seven explicitly determined Specht constituents, one with multiplicity two; neither positive-degree homology group contains a full-symmetry invariant. The ordinary Betti ranks were already known; the contribution is the complete equivariant decomposition and the resulting rational acyclicity of the symmetry quotient.
