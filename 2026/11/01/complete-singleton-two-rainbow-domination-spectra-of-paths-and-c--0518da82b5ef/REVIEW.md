# Review of complete singleton two-rainbow path and cycle spectra

## Correctness
PASS. Every unlabeled zero must have two differently coloured neighbours, forcing an independent zero set. On paths, and on cycles with a pair of adjacent positive positions, the induced inequality graph is a forest, so exactly one factor of two is lost per zero. The unique exceptional alternating cyclic family produces two zero sets and two proper colourings precisely when the number of coloured positions is even. These cases partition the entire configuration space without omission or double counting. The independent-set cycle transfer and the path recurrence reconstruct the claimed rational generating functions. An independent direct enumeration checked 531426 assignments, and formal coefficient comparisons covered orders through 100; only the structural argument establishes the infinite assertion.

## Originality
PASS, with residual historical-priority risk. Lange (2026, arXiv:2610.06906v1, Proposition 12) proves the minimum weight for cycles, not these enumerating polynomials or minimum multiplicities. Žerovnik (2025, DOI:10.1007/s40314-025-03201-2) treats singleton domination on cylindrical products. Bouchou–Blidia (2016) concern criticality for unrestricted set-valued two-rainbow domination, and Bonomo et al. (2018) classify and compare parameters rather than providing this cycle and path cardinality census. Publicly searchable nearby polynomial statements on degree-two graphs concern different forcing-colouring conditions; a complete-multipartite singleton enumeration concerns different graph families. A previous general cyclic-word enumeration with equivalent coefficients might nonetheless exist; absence of a located match does not establish global priority.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The full cardinality distribution is a natural, motivated strengthening of a recent exact cycle-minimum theorem. It has a short structural description valid in every order and reveals a boundary-induced quantitative trichotomy that the minimum value alone does not determine: constant, linear, and quadratic minimum-function multiplicities in distinct congruence classes. The path–cycle pair explains this phenomenon as one extra global compatibility constraint, and the exact rational functions make every coefficient reproducible.

## Scope and limits
The theorem requires exactly two distinct singleton colours and counts labelled functions on simple paths and cycles. It is not a theorem about unrestricted two-rainbow functions, all regular graphs, or colourings modulo symmetries. Some prior sources could be inspected only in abstract or selected sections, and independent assessment remains outstanding.
