Singleton two-rainbow domination on every path and cycle has a closed cardinality polynomial: isolated zeros impose independent two-colour inequalities, except at the alternating cyclic boundary. Consequently the numbers of minimum functions on cycles are exactly \(2n\) in odd orders, \(n^2\) in orders congruent to \(2\) modulo \(4\), and just \(4\) in orders divisible by \(4\).
