Their inspected texts do not present the full missing-volume spline or its exact differentiability jumps. The van Hintum paper's indexed abstract addresses iterated Minkowski averages under Hausdorff distance; its full text was not inspected. Searches against other published mathematical records surfaced volume splines for different Hadamard polytope truncations and a lattice-octahedron coverage profile, neither of which has the same translated vertex configuration. An earlier unindexed \(\ell_1\)-ball union-volume formula remains a genuine priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The deficit is a canonical quantitative refinement of finite convexification for a centrally symmetric simplicial polytope. It measures **how** the initially disjoint vertex translates fill their convex hull, not merely **when** they do so. Its exact all-dimensional polynomial pieces, distinct first and later smoothness orders, and sharp final-chamber power provide reproducible information about the geometry of overlaps at every scale. This is a natural complete invariant for the family and does not follow from the published qualitative convexification criteria.
