For every dimension \(d\ge2\), the volume missing from the union of the \(2d\) vertex-centered radius-\(t\) cross-polytopes inside its convex hull has a complete explicit piecewise-polynomial formula. Convexification becomes exact at \(t=d-1\); the deficit has one \((d-1)\)-st derivative jump at \(t=1\), then nonzero \(d\)-th derivative jumps at every remaining integer threshold.
