For rational Fuss–Dyck valley complexes of every length \(n\ge3\) and slope parameter \(b\ge1\), a single isolated vertex is the only obstruction to connectedness. At length three, the connected component is a wedge of \(\frac{(3b-4)(b-1)}2\) circles, and the complete nonpure \(f\)- and \(h\)-triangles admit explicit formulas for every parameter.
