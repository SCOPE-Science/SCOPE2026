For all \(n,q\ge2\), the geometric coordinate-permutation fixed space of the total two-cut complex of \(K_q^{\square n}\) is a wedge of \(q-1\) spheres of dimension \(\binom{n+q-1}{q-1}-3\). The residual alphabet-permutation action on integral top homology is the coaugmentation lattice twisted by the sign of its permutation on histograms.
