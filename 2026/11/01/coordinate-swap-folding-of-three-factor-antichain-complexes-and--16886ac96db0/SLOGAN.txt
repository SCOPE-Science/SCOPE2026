Swapping the first two binary coordinates folds the **geometric fixed-point antichain complex** of \([2]\times[2]\times Q\) PL-homeomorphically onto the antichain complex of \([3]\times Q\), for every finite nonempty poset \(Q\). When \(Q=[n]\), this fixed space always has three components and gains its first integral two-dimensional homology at \(n=6\), with rank one.
