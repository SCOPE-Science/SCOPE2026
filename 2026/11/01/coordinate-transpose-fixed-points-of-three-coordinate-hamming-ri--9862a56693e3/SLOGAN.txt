The geometric fixed space of the radius-two Hamming Rips complex on any three-coordinate \(q\)-letter cube under interchange of two coordinates is a wedge of exactly \(\frac{(q-1)^2(q^2-2)}2\) two-spheres. Across all radii, its complete integral barcode has only reduced degree-zero intervals ending at scales one or two and degree-two intervals \([2,3)\).
