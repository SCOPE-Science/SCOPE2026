For every generalized theta graph with at least three parallel paths and every odd target cycle of length at least five, a finite set of integral relative winding vectors determines the exact numbers of circular and contractible components of its Lovász Hom complex. Each winding vector allowing multiple common path displacements contributes one \(S^1\), while a unique feasible displacement contributes exactly \(q\) contractible components, where \(q\) is the target-cycle length.
