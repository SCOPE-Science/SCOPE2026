For every integer-expanding triangular modular map, even with arbitrary Borel coupling, the number of points fixed by the \(n\)-th iterate is exactly \((A^n-1)(C^n-1)(E^n-1)\). Their empirical measures approach normalized volume with a forcing-independent \(\ell^1\)-Wasserstein error at most \(\frac\beta2\sum_{D\in\{A,C,E\}}(D^n-1)^{-1}\), and the exponential rate is sharp when the last-coordinate slope is smallest.
