For hyperbolic-harmonic functions in the real four-ball, a radial boundary collar of squared thickness \(a/N\) has a nontrivial limiting concentration spectrum governed by a three-component gamma mixture, whereas every other power scale gives a degenerate spectral limit. The normalized projection defect has sharp small- and large-collar coefficients, including \(3/10\) and \(15837/800\) in the unweighted case.
