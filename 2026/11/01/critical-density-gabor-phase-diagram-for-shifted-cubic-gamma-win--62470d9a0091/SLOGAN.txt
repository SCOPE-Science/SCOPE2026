The critical-lattice Gabor frame property for the causal cubic-gamma window \((t+c)^3e^{-\lambda t}\mathbf1_{t\ge0}\) is governed exactly by the largest positive root of a depressed cubic. A second Zak zero appears at \(e^{-\lambda}=2-\sqrt3\), yet the frame region remains a single sharp half-line; its optimal lower bound turns on quadratically.
