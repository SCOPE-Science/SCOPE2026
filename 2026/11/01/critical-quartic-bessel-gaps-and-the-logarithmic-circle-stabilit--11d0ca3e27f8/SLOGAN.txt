Although individual critical quartic Bessel moments on the circle diverge, every matched-cutoff difference has the exact odd-harmonic-number value \(2\pi^{-2}\sum_{j=1}^{k}(2j-1)^{-1}\). These gaps generate a sharp logarithmic Poisson-semigroup stability form and determine the square-root critical decay of each fixed Fourier mode, with degree one optimal on every fixed finite band sufficiently close to \(q=4\).
