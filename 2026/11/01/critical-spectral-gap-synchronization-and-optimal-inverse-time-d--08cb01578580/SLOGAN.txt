At the exact spectral-gap boundary \(c\lambda_2=\mu\), every initial complex configuration of a connected identical Stuart–Landau network synchronizes with the explicit inverse-time disagreement estimate \(S(t)\le 2NS(0)/(2N+tS(0))\). The inverse-time decay order cannot be strengthened uniformly: on a two-vertex graph, an antisymmetric trajectory has \(S(t)\sim t^{-1}\); strong-coupling and almost-global synchronization away from this quantitative endpoint already have prior precedents.
