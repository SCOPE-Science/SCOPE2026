A Crofton-random line crossing a unit \(d\)-cube connects opposite facets with an exact Gaussian one-dimensional integral probability that decays like \(\exp[-\frac32(\sqrt{2/\pi}\,d)^{2/3}+o(d^{2/3})]\). Conditional on that rare event, the chord length is of order \(d^{1/6}\) and has explicit Gaussian fluctuations about its natural scale.
