For every \(n\ge4\), the open-neighborhood complex of the complement of the crown graph is \(S^{n-2}\vee S^{n-2}\vee\bigvee^{n^2-3n+1}S^2\). The corresponding crown-graph dominance complex has torsion-free integral homology of ranks \(2\) in degree \(n-1\) and \(n^2-3n+1\) in degree \(2n-5\), combining to rank \(7\) in degree \(3\) when \(n=4\).
