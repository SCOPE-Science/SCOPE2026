For every crown graph \(H(n,1)\) with \(n\ge3\), the two-independence complex is an equivariant suspension of the complete graph and has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{2}}S^2\). Its top homology is the signed exterior square of the standard \(S_n\)-lattice, yet both the bipartition-swap quotient and the full-symmetry quotient are contractible.
