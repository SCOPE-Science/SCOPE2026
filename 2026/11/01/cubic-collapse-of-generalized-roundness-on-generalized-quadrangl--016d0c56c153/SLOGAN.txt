Every finite generalized quadrangle of equal order \(q\ge2\) has an exact critical negative-type exponent determined by \(q^3 4^p-q^2(q+1)3^p+q(q+1)2^p-(q+1)=0\). Its critical equality kernel is exactly the point-versus-line sign vector, the minimum Euclidean snowflake dimension drops by one, and the exponent decays as \(1/(q^3\log(4/3))\).
