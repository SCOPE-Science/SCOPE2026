For Haar-random central sections of the Euclidean cube, the largest inscribed-ball radius has an exact Dirichlet integral law and typically exceeds the cube inradius by order \(n^{-3}\), with a squared-exponential limit after cubic rescaling. Generically the optimal ball is not unique: its full center locus has dimension \(n-2\), while both endpoints of the radius law have sharp explicit asymptotics.
