# Scientific review: cubic mixed sixth-character moments on \(SU(3)\)

## Correctness
PASS. Probability Haar and trace-character normalizations are fixed. The mixed sixth moment reduces by Schur orthogonality and Pieri to a sum of nonnegative triple multiplicities. The exact general 1992 coupling formula, specialized to three selfdual highest weights \((i,i)\), produces the minimum-of-six formula; one integer slack variable converts the computation into counted triangles. The two branches follow from exact disjoint-complement and upper-corner counts, and their boundary agrees at \(r=m\). A quadratic first difference yields the peak; the binomial tail gives strict decay and cutoff. A separate Weyl constant-term computation validates small cases. The remaining proof dependency is the established general coupling theorem, which is explicitly cited.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This is a complete exact family of genuinely nonabelian central Fourier coefficients, not an arbitrary numerical slice. It identifies positivity, a sharp finite spectral bandwidth, the location of the strongest Fourier channel, an asymptotic \(4/5\) peak law, a polynomial diagonal sixth moment and exact saturation. These are natural invariants for product decompositions of symmetric-power characters and provide immediate analytic benchmarks for \(SU(3)\) central harmonic analysis.

## Limits
The result is restricted to \(SU(3)\) symmetric powers and the particular two-equal-degree mixed moment. General triple-degree profiles and higher even moments remain unproved. Historical priority is provisional; the 1992 tensor-product formula is substantially more general at the level of individual multiplicities. No independent audit has been performed.
