## Value — PASS

The cuboctahedron is the rectified cube, a canonical vertex-transitive polyhedral graph and the smallest cubic-hypercube line graph outside the trivial low-dimensional examples. Its two-independence complex is an edge-cluster analogue of the ordinary matching complex and has a surprisingly large exact homotopy type despite a small ground set. The complete \(B_3\) character provides a symmetry distinction not recoverable from the Betti number alone. The central inversion splits the \(32\)-dimensional rational homology into \(21\)- and \(11\)-dimensional eigenspaces, and the full group leaves two invariant classes. This is a natural complete finite classification, not an arbitrary numerical slice or a restatement of the known graph families.
