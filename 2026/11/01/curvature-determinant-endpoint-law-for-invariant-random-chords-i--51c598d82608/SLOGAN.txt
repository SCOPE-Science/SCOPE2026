For a smooth convex body in \(\mathbb R^d\), \(d\ge3\), with finitely many nondegenerate diameter pairs, the probability that a Euclidean-invariant random chord comes within \(\varepsilon\) of the diameter is asymptotic to an explicit curvature-determinant constant times \(\varepsilon^{d-1}\). The coefficient couples the two endpoint curvature-radius operators through \(D I-R_--R_+\), without requiring them to commute.
