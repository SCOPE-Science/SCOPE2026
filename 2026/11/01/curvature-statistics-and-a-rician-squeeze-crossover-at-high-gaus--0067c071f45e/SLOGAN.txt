At a Palm-selected local maximum of a Gaussian white-noise spectrogram, the unsqueezed curvature condition number has an exact power-law tail, while conditioning the squared peak height to exceed \(h\) produces a Rayleigh limit at scale \(h^{-1/2}\). Squeezing its Gaussian window by \(b_h\sim\eta/\sqrt h\) replaces this limit by an explicit Rician law, whereas fixed nonzero squeezing yields a normal fluctuation around the deterministic ratio \(e^{4|b|}\).
