Primitive two-cycle orbits of parallel chip-firing on any finite connected graph are exactly counted by a cut-degree generating polynomial; their minimum conserved total is \(2|E|-\operatorname{MaxCut}(G)\), with one orbit per maximum cut at that threshold. For paths the total number of two-cycle orbits is the Pell number indexed by the number of edges, and arbitrary trees admit a linear-arithmetic-operation recurrence.
