# Scientific review: Dedekind spectral finite parts and inverse-residue duality for balanced Hartogs concentration

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof directly establishes monomial-basis completeness, the norm integral, the two-cutoff diagonal eigenvalue formula, exact residue parametrization, rational Schatten moments, Dedekind expansion, isospectral inverse-residue symmetry, missing shells and the sharp leading counting coefficient. Finite exact-arithmetic checks verify boundary and residue cases and are supplementary to the infinite proof.

## Originality

**PASS, provisional.** The closest primary references are Balay et al. (2020), arXiv:2009.07260, which provides the Laurent-integrability setting and \(L^p\) estimates for other radial symbols, and Chakrabarti–Tang–Zhang (2024), DOI:10.1007/s10231-024-01495-3, which gives \(L^p\)-\(L^q\) results for boundary-distance-power symbols on monomial domains. Those papers do not imply the characteristic balanced-cusp Toeplitz eigenvalues or its Dedekind finite part. Bergman-kernel sources [arXiv:2303.14268, arXiv:2308.05834] establish different quantities. Classical cyclic Hilbert series and Dedekind relations are not represented as new in isolation. Historical overlap through arithmetic spectral interpretations remains a risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The complete exact spectral law gives compactness and all positive Schatten moments, shows forbidden spectral shells, and isolates a geometric inverse-spectral ambiguity: a Dedekind sum appears as the first shape-dependent coefficient beyond universal divergences. The duality of reciprocal residues is meaningful even when the domains have distinct defining exponents.

## Scope and limitations

Only positive coprime rational Hartogs exponents and balanced two-cutoff radial cores are proved. The finite-part data do not uniquely recover the exponent; no general irrational/nonradial assertion is made. Any assertion of priority remains provisional.
