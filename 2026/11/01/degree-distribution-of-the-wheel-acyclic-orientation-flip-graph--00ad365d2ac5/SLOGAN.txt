For every wheel \(W_{m+1}=K_1\vee C_m\), an explicit coefficient formula determines the degree of every state of its single-edge acyclic-orientation flip graph, including all local-degree multiplicities; the number of simple zonotope vertices is \(L_{2m}+4m-4+2\cos(m\pi/3)\). The least degree is \(m\); for \(m\ge4\), the greatest is \(m+\lfloor m/2\rfloor\), attained exactly \(4\) times when \(m\) is even and \(4m\) times when \(m\) is odd.
