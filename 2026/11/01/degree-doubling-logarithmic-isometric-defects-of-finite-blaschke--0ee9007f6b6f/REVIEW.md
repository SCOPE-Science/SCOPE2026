# Mathematical review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The normalized harmonic Bergman projection and positive isometric defect are precisely defined. The orthogonal multiplication decomposition produces a positive compressed symbol plus a trace-class Hankel remainder. Fourier monomial calculations establish Hilbert–Schmidt summability of this remainder for rational finite Blaschke symbols. The positive-symbol bound by the radial defect gives the weak-trace-class estimate, and the boundary Poisson-kernel derivative computes the exact two-tail logarithmic diagonal trace. The Ky Fan rank-truncation inequality upgrades that diagonal trace to the full eigenvalue sum without asserting a pointwise eigenvalue limit. The trace-class correction does not alter the leading coefficient. Supplementary finite checks passed 22425 assertions, but no extrapolation from experiments is used.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The isometric defect is a canonical quantity measuring the failure of a boundary-inner multiplication operator to remain isometric after harmonic projection. The precise coefficient, its doubling and invariance under moving Blaschke zeros, and the sharp Schatten endpoint determine a natural infinite-family invariant not fixed by existing class-membership conditions.

## Scientific limits

No pointwise singular-value asymptotic is proved. The result assumes an unweighted complex harmonic Bergman disk and a finite Blaschke product.
