Every least-period-two orbit of the higher-dimensional delayed Lozi family is classified by the parities of its two delays: there is either none, exactly one mixed-sign orbit, or a continuum on an explicit resonance line. For even nonlinear delay and odd linear delay, the unique orbit emerges through a two-sided pole when \(0<\nu<1\), a one-sided pole at \(\nu=1\), and a finite negative-phase continuum when \(1<\nu<2\).
