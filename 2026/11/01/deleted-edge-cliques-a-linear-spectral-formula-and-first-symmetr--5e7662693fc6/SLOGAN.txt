For every complete graph with one deleted edge, the squared centered strong Hilbert maximal norm is exactly the maximum of at most \(4(m+1)\) spectral problems of dimension four, and deletion strictly increases the complete-graph norm. The first mixed universal-clique extremizers occur on \(K_5\) with one missing edge: its squared norm is the root above one of \(400\lambda^3-655\lambda^2+126\lambda-1=0\), with exactly three nonnegative extremizing rays.
