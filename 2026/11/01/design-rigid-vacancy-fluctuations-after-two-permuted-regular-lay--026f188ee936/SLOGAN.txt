For two independent permuted regular layers, the exact variance of the number of empty destinations is minimized, whenever feasible, by a symmetric \(2\)-design; at the critical scale \(d=r^2-r+1\), its unique equality structures are projective planes. The excess variance is an explicit nonnegative sum of incidence-biclique counts, and along prime-power plane orders the sharp variance per vertex tends to \(e^{-1}-\tfrac52e^{-2}\).
