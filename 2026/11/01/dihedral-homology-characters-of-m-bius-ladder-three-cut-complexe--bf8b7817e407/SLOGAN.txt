For every Möbius ladder on \(2n\) vertices with \(n\ge5\), the ordinary three-cut complex is an integral wedge of \(2n^2-8n+1\) spheres in dimension \(2n-4\), and its complete rational dihedral homology character has an explicit parity formula. The space of homology classes fixed by all dihedral symmetries has dimension \(\lfloor(n-3)/2\rfloor\), despite the full Betti number growing quadratically.
