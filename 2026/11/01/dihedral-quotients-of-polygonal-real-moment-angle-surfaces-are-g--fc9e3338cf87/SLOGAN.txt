For every polygon with \(n\ge5\) sides, quotienting its real moment-angle surface by *all* dihedral coordinate symmetries produces a bordered surface homotopy equivalent to \(r_n=1+2^{n-3}-\frac{1}{2n}\sum_{d\mid n}\varphi(d)2^{n/d}\) circles wedged together. When \(n\) is odd, this quotient has exactly \(2^{(n-3)/2}\) boundary circles; the known genus formula for the rotation-only quotient is not claimed as new.
