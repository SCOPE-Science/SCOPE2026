For every \(n\ge13\), the dihedral action on the rational homology of the open neighborhood complex of \(C_n^3\) has one reflection-sign line in degree one, and for even \(n\) has two additional one-dimensional lines with rotation sign \(-1\). Consequently every ordinary full-dihedral quotient is rationally acyclic, while its rotation quotient has the rational homology of a circle.
