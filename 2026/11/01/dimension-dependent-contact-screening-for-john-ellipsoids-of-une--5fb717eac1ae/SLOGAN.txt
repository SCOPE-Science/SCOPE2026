The maximum-volume inscribed ellipsoid of an unequal-radius two-ball lens has exact scalar formulas in every dimension \(d\ge2\). Its contact geometry switches from one axial pole plus a latitude to two latitudes exactly when the radius ratio exceeds \(1/d\) and the center separation crosses \(d(1-q^2)/(d-1)\); below that radius ratio, the larger sphere never develops a nondegenerate contact latitude.
