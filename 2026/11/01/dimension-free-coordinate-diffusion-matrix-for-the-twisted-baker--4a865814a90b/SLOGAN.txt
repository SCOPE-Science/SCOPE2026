All spatial coordinates of the stationary twisted baker map have a joint Gaussian diffusion with exact covariance \(V_M=\frac1{12}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}4&-\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}\\-\mathbf1&I+\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\) in every dimension \(M\ge2\). Passive coordinate sums acquire the same pairwise long-run correlation \(1/2\), even though their deterministic update order is asymmetric.
