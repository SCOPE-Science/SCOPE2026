For the eigenfunction normalized by \(f(0)=1\) of an elliptic affine Bergman Toeplitz operator, approaching its spectral ellipse in direction \(\theta\) produces the sharp norm law \(\|f_{r,\theta}\|\sim C_\theta(1-r)^{-\operatorname{Re}(1/(1-(c/a)e^{2i\theta}))}\), with a fully explicit Gamma-factor coefficient. The growth exponents determine \(|c/a|\), and after microscopic boundary rescaling the eigenvector mass converges to an explicit complex-power density on a half-plane.
