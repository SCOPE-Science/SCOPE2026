For every \(m,n\geq2\), the space of two unordered, collision-free points on \(|K_{m,n}|\), modulo all graph automorphisms, strongly deformation retracts onto a triangulated closed disk. The finite disk model has \((11,22,12)\) simplices by dimension when \(m=n\), and \((19,42,24)\) when \(m\ne n\).
