As the square-root cusp disappears in a full-branch interval map, its invariant densities converge to the tent-map density in \(L^1\) but develop two distinct, explicit \(\varepsilon^2\)-scale boundary layers. The endpoint derivatives diverge with opposite constants \(-8\) and \(8/3\) after scaling by \(\varepsilon^2\), forcing an \(\varepsilon^{-2}\) second-derivative obstruction.
