For every finite nonempty triangle-free graph \(H\) and \(m\ge2\), the four-clique-free complex of \(K_m\vee H\) is a wedge of \(\binom m2\) double suspensions of \(\operatorname{Ind}(H)\) and \(\binom m3\) two-spheres. This uniform splitting makes the complex simply connected and transfers any higher integral torsion of the independence complex upward by two dimensions.
