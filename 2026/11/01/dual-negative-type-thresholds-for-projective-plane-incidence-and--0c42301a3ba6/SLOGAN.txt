For every finite projective plane, incidence and nonincidence graph metrics have explicitly classified sharp negative-type exponents: incidence is controlled by a \(v-1\)-dimensional singular-vector sector, whereas nonincidence switches at order \(q=3\) to a one-dimensional part-contrast obstruction. Their large-order exponents scale respectively as \(q^{-1/2}/\log3\) and \(q^{-2}/\log2\), independently of the plane's coordinatization.
