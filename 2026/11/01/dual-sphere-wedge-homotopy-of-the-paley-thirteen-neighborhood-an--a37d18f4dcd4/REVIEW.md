# Review of the Paley-thirteen dual homotopy classification

## Correctness — PASS

The two graph complexes are defined on the thirteen-element field using the explicit six quadratic residues. Direct exhaustive predicates enumerate 598 neighborhood faces and 7592 dominance faces. The deterministic vertex-toggling matching has 286 and 3783 pairs, respectively, and independently verified acyclic directed Hasse diagrams on the complete face sets. One zero-cell plus exactly 25 cells of a single positive dimension in each Morse CW model gives the asserted **integral homotopy equivalences**, not merely mod-two Betti numbers. Independent binary boundary reductions yield rank 25 in degrees two and eight, respectively. The checker recomputes the complement isomorphism and Alexander-duality condition on all 8192 subsets. Each assertion is restricted to the fixed Paley-thirteen graph.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

The 2023 preprint by Matsushita and Wakatsuki states the general dominance-neighborhood Alexander-dual relationship. The relationship itself is prior work, and no claim of novelty is made for it. The directly inspected portions and full-document term search of this primary source contain no Paley-thirteen computation; its examples and family results concern other graphs. Shukla's 2018 neighborhood-complex classification covers maximum degree at most three and four-regular circulants, not this six-regular graph. The reported theorem on third powers of cycles does not imply the claim: the thirteen-vertex third power has 39 triangles whereas Paley-thirteen has 26. Other accessible exact records concern Paley-13 *clique* topology or neighborhood complexes of the different Paley orders 17 and 25, without any covering parameter-family statement found. Actual searches of research records and indexed literature yielded no mathematically equivalent or stronger Paley-thirteen neighborhood/dominance homotopy classification. Failure to find one is not proof of universal uniqueness; an unindexed equivalent calculation remains possible.

## Value — PASS

The Paley graph of order thirteen is a canonical self-complementary, six-regular, strongly regular conference graph. Its Lovász neighborhood complex is central to graph-coloring topology, and its dominance complex is a natural Alexander-dual companion. The **complete integral** homotopy types provide an exact topological classification beyond the graph's strongly regular parameters: 25 sphere generators in each of two distant dimensions. In particular, the neighborhood complex is simply connected and its nonzero second homology gives a concrete topological coloring bound; the dominance result provides an independently verified dual homotopy classification. The computation is finite yet motivated by the existing gap between published low-degree circulant classifications and general dominance-neighborhood duality, rather than by arbitrary parameter slicing.

## Closest sources and limits

Matsushita–Wakatsuki (arXiv:2312.02639, initial 2023-12-05) gives the duality but not the Paley-thirteen Betti numbers. Shukla (arXiv:1802.04526, initial 2018-02-13) does not include six-regular circulants. The accessible records for Paley-17, Paley-25, and the Paley-13 clique complex do not logically imply this finding. The checker is finite and complete; it does not establish results for other orders, nor can an incomplete bibliographic index guarantee that no equivalent computation exists.
