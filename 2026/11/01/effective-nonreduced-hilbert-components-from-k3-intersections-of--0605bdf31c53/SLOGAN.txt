Nasu’s generically nonreduced Hilbert-scheme components arising from triple-quadric K3 surfaces already exist for every \(m\ge2\), rather than only for unspecified sufficiently large multiples. The first such effective member consists of degree-\(34\), genus-\(69\) curves in \(\mathbb P^5\) and has component dimension \(122\) with general tangent dimension \(125\).
