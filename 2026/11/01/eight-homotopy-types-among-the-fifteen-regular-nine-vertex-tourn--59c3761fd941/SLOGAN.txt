The directed out-neighborhood complexes of all fifteen regular nine-vertex tournaments have exactly eight integral homotopy types, ranging from \(S^1\) and \(\bigvee^{7}S^1\) to \(S^2\), \(\bigvee^{3}S^2\), and \(S^1\vee S^2\). Complete acyclic discrete-Morse certificates determine every type, rather than only the homology ranks.
