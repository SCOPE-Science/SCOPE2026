For five-color inhibitory firefly dynamics on every complete tripartite graph \(K_{a,b,c}\), only least periods \(5,6,9,18\) occur and every trajectory becomes periodic within \(40\) updates. Primitive \(18\)-cycles exist exactly when different shores have sizes at least \(3\) and \(4\), and their exact orbit count is \(\frac16\sum_{(i,j,k)\in S_3}2^{n_i}U_3(n_j)U_4(n_k)\), with \(U_r(n)\) the number of surjections from \(n\) labelled vertices onto \(r\) specified colors.
