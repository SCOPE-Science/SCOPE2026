The induced-matching complexes of the dodecahedral and Desargues graphs, two cubic double covers of the Petersen graph, are respectively \(\bigvee^{104}S^2\) and \(\bigvee^{104}S^3\). They have the same number of spheres and the same maximum induced-matching size but different integral homotopy dimensions, certified by exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matchings.
