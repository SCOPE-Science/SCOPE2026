For the well-covered generalized Petersen graph \(GP(7,2)\), the first four higher-independence complexes are respectively \(S^3\), \(S^5\), \(\bigvee^8S^5\), and \(\bigvee^6S^6\). Their exact four-stage mod-two persistence and full dihedral rational homology characters reveal a surviving degree-five inclusion and a change from sign to augmentation representations.
