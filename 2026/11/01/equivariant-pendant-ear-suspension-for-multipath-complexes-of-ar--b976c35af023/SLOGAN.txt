Attaching any number of fully bidirected two-edge pendant ears at arbitrary vertices of a finite digraph makes its multipath complex an equivariant strong deformation retract onto the corresponding iterated suspension, with no source/sink restriction. In particular, the multipath complex of the bidirected once-subdivided \(m\)-arm star is \(S^{m-1}\), and its top integral homology carries the arm-permutation sign character multiplied by \((-1)^m\) under global arrow reversal.
