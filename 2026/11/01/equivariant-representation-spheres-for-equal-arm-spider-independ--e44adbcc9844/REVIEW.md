# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The same-arm graph fold is an explicit simplicial homotopy inverse up to contiguity, giving the path complexes modulo three. The center deletion/link is an equivariant cofiber; either its link is equivariantly contractible, its deletion is equivariantly contractible, or its inclusion induces equivalences on every subgroup-fixed space. Equivariant Whitehead and identical, armwise joins give exact real representation spheres. The orbitwise affine PL fixed-point map verifies every subgroup, not only generators. The induced top integral character is the determinant sign power. The independent finite checker matched all fifteen mod-two Betti vectors and 142 signed Lefschetz comparisons. Risk: numerical tests do not replace the equivariant topological argument, and the equivalence is not claimed as a PL homeomorphism.

**Originality: PASS, with residual uncertainty.** Daundkar–Panja–Prasad already computed non-equivariant independence homotopy types for terminal wedges of paths; Pham–Vu supplied a nonequivariant tree contractibility criterion. Neither primary source's inspected theorem or proof asserts the full permutation-representation sphere, subgroup geometric fixed spaces, or integral group action. Closest published-finding corpus records concern ordinary Morse matchings of extended stars or generalized Mycielskian independence complexes, with different graph-complex definitions and representations. Generic equivariant graph folding can overlap the method, and equivalent unindexed literature remains possible; no claim of proof of absolute novelty is made.

**Value: PASS.** A complete equivariant **homotopy** classification for a natural infinite family is structurally stronger than known ordinary sphere dimensions, with unbounded fixed-sphere dimensions for all subgroups and a group-theoretic integral orientation character. It is a useful benchmark for equivariant independence-complex methods and differentiates real fixed loci from invariant homology.
