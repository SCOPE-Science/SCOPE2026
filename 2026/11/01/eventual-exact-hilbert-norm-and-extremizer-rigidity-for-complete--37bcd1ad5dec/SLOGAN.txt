For each fixed smaller class \(m\ge2\), the centered \(\ell^2\) maximal-operator norm on \(K_{m,n}\) eventually equals an explicit two-by-two singular-value radical, with a unique two-level extremizing modulus and a sharp \(mn/(m+1)^2\) leading squared-norm law. A uniform analytic argument proves exact stabilization for \(n\ge10^6(m+1)^6\), rather than merely giving asymptotic lower bounds.
