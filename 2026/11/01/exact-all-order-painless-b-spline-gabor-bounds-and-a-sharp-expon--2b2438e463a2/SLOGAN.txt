Every cardinal B-spline of order \(n\ge2\) has exact optimal painless Gabor frame bounds at integer and half-integer translates, with the maximizing translate switching according to the parity of \(n\). For integer oversampling \(q\ge n\), the optimal frame condition number satisfies \(\kappa_n-1\sim 4(4/\pi^2)^n/\sqrt{3(1-4/\pi^2)}\), including its sharp leading constant.
