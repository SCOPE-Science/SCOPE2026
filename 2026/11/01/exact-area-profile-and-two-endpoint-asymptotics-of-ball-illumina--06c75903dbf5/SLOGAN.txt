The \(R\)-ball illumination body of a Euclidean disk of radius \(r<R\) has an exact globally invertible circular-arc area profile. Its first new \(\delta^{4/3}\) correction changes sign precisely at \(r/R=2-2\sqrt3/3\), while the finite terminal illumination threshold is approached quadratically rather than with the initial \(\delta^{2/3}\) law.
