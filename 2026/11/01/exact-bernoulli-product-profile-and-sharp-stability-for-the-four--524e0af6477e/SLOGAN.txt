For independent, arbitrarily biased Bernoulli weights on the four-cube, the exact normalized simplex-volume Hypercube ratio is \(1-4e_3(q)+18e_4(q)\), where \(q_i=p_i(1-p_i)\). Its unique minimum is \(105/128\) at uniform weights, and the sharp global quadratic improvement is \((23/128)\sum_i(p_i-1/2)^2\).
