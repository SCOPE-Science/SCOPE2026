Erasing the representative of character order \(d\) from a minimal cyclic Fourier sampling mask makes two binary signals indistinguishable only if they differ at \(\frac Nd 2^{\omega(d)}\) or more coordinates, and this bound is attained for every \(d\mid N\). Across all possible one-sample erasures the sharp smallest collision distance is \(2^{\omega(N)}\).
