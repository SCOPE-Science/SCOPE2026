For identical binary-valued goods located at the vertices of a complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), \(a\ge b\), connected outer-EF1 allocations are guaranteed for exactly the agent counts \(k\le b\) and \(k\ge a\). Cyclic balancing and a value-compatible matching provide the positive constructions, while all-one valuations rule out every count strictly between the two part sizes.
