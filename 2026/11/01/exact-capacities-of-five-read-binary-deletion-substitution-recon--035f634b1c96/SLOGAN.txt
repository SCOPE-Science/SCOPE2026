For binary words passing through exactly one deletion and at most one substitution, the maximum sizes of codes reconstructible from five distinct reads are \(8,5,5,6,8,12,16\) at input lengths \(3,4,5,6,7,8,9\). In particular, an optimal length-\(9\) code has exactly \(16\) codewords, certified by a reproducible exhaustive clique bound.
