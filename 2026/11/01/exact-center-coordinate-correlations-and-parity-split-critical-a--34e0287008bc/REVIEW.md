# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The claimed map is a full-branch, Lebesgue-preserving planar skew product. An independent fair alpha/beta itinerary gives exact \(m\)-adic push–pop dynamics of the center coordinate. The shared initial tail has conditional covariance \(m^{-(S_n+2M_n)}/12\); a reflection-principle count gives the finite-lag expression. A binomial-walk resolvent yields the algebraic generating function. The adjacent-binomial ratio and uniform summable local central limit estimates prove both sharp parity amplitudes with \(O_m(n^{-5/2})\) remainder. Evaluation at \(z=1\) and absolute summability yield the variance. The packaged checker passed 24 independently evaluated exact-rational cases. Only analytical arguments prove all times and asymptotics.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest works are H. Takahasi, arXiv:2307.08119 (critical mixing setup) and H. Takahasi–M. Tsujii, arXiv:2408.16618 (optimal polynomial order of mixing). The latter already establishes the general \(n^{-3/2}\) scale and uses random-walk tools. The accepted contribution is **not** that exponent or the walk comparison, but the distinguished observable's all-lag covariance and algebraic generating function, unequal leading even/odd amplitudes, and exact Green–Kubo variance. The relevant inspected portions do not state those stronger quantitative identities. Saiki et al., arXiv:2401.00836, review the family. Published indexed heterochaos neutral-fiber results address periodic fixed intervals and counts, not two-time stationary covariance. A nonzero priority risk remains: earlier transfer-operator formulas could implicitly imply the specialization, or it could appear in further literature.

## Value — PASS
The center coordinate is intrinsic to the neutral direction causing slow mixing. Establishing two distinct parity constants shows that a naive single asymptotic coefficient does not exist, a feature invisible in general rate bounds. The algebraic generating function supplies all finite-lag covariances and the exact integrated variance for fluctuation and comparison studies. This is a natural complete statistical invariant of the critical model, not an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Scope and limitations
Lebesgue state, symmetric critical planar map, integer \(m\ge2\), center observable only. The general \(n^{-3/2}\) decay order belongs to earlier work. Complete literature priority and independent external proof checking are not established.
