For the critical planar heterochaos baker map, the centered height observable has an exact algebraic autocorrelation generating function \(G(z)=(1+t(z)^2)/(12(1-t(z)/m)^2)\), with \(t(z)=(1-\sqrt{1-z^2})/z\). Its known \(n^{-3/2}\) rate conceals two distinct, explicitly calculated even- and odd-lag amplitudes, and the Green–Kubo variance is \((3m^2+2m-1)/(12(m-1)^2)\).
