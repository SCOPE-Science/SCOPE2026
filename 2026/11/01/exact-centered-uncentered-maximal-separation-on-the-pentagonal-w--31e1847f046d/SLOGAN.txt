On the pentagonal wheel \(K_1\vee C_5\), the centered graph-ball maximal operator has sharp \(\ell^2\) norm \(\sqrt{3/2}\) and one rotationally symmetric nonnegative extremizing ray, whereas the uncentered norm is \(\sqrt{\lambda_*}>\sqrt{3/2}\), where \(64\lambda_*^3-120\lambda_*^2+27\lambda_*-1=0\) and \(81/50<\lambda_*<1621/1000\). The uncentered equality set consists of exactly five symmetry-breaking nonnegative rays, one for each rim peak.
