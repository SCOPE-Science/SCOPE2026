For the vertex set of every odd regular polygon with at least five sides, the union of its translates by a scaled copy of the polygon has exactly one central polygonal hole until the sharp finite-convexification threshold \(\sec(\pi/m)\). Its missing-area fraction is exactly \(\bigl((\sec(\pi/m)-t)/(1+t)\bigr)^2\) throughout the final one-hole regime \(1\le t\le\sec(\pi/m)\).
