A uniformly oriented central plane cuts any three-dimensional parallelotope into a quadrilateral or hexagon, with an exact eight-orthant formula for the hexagon probability that applies to arbitrary nonorthogonal generators. Among the equal-edge equiangular rhombohedra, the Euclidean cube is the unique **minimum**, in contrast with its known maximum among orthogonal boxes, and the sharp quadratic increase is \(\rho^2/(\sqrt2\pi)\).
