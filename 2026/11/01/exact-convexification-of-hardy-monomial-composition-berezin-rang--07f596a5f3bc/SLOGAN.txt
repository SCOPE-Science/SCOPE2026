For Hardy-space composition by \(z\mapsto z^m\), the closed convex hull of the Berezin range is exactly the tangent-disk hull of \(1\) and \(\overline D(1/(m+1),1/(m+1))\). Its area decays like \(1/m\) and it approaches \([0,1]\) at exact Hausdorff distance \(1/(m+1)\), even though the operator's closed ordinary numerical range remains the full unit disk.
