# Scientific review: wheel spanning-tree covariance spectrum

## Correctness — PASS
The object is the probability-normalized covariance of centered one-edge incidence functions on all uniform spanning trees of the simple wheel with \(n\ge3\) rim vertices. The proof derives the grounded Green kernel from the exact cyclic recurrence, applies the Burton–Pemantle two-edge determinant identity, and identifies a wheel-specific equality of spoke/spoke and rim/rim covariance blocks. Discrete Fourier diagonalization gives both exact eigenvalue branches, with the constant edge-count identity producing the sole zero mode. Monotonicity proves that the first harmonic controls all nonconstant spectral extrema. Rational interval bounds establish the finite crossover cases \(3\le n\le10\); the monotone exponential comparison establishes every \(n\ge11\). A separate local Taylor expansion yields the quadratic condition-number growth.

The standalone checker verified exact rational covariance matrices by exhaustive spanning-tree enumeration for \(3\le n\le8\), independently inverted reduced Laplacians, checked Fourier eigenvalue formulas, and ran rational interval certificates. Those finite computations support but do not substitute for the all-size proof. The statements are limited to uniform, unit-weight wheels.

## Originality — PASS under bounded available comparison
The nearest general predecessor is Burton and Pemantle's transfer-current theorem, giving all finite-edge inclusion probabilities in principle. It is fully credited and implies the entrywise covariance formula, but its inspected principal results do not provide the **wheel-specific complete Fourier covariance spectrum**, the identity of within-spoke and within-rim covariance blocks, the precise change of largest-eigenvalue eigenspace at \(n=9\), or the sharp quadratic asymptotic for nonzero Riesz conditioning. Published wheel Laplacian results concern the graph Laplacian, not the covariance matrix over its uniform spanning trees. Accessible previous sampling results about spanning trees of complete and complete bipartite graphs are on different graph families and do not logically contain the cyclic wheel calculation. Searches covering transfer-current covariance, wheel spanning-tree spectrum, Fourier frame bounds and aliases found no explicitly dominating treatment. A specialized previous electrical-network derivation could exist and remains the principal residual uncertainty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete exact sampling-stability classification for a canonical infinite family of constrained finite ensembles. The absence of any unexpected linear constraints, the finite spoke–rim covariance symmetry, and the switch of the worst-conditioned sampling mode at exactly nine rim vertices are structural statements, not a numerical table or a fixed small-size slice. The coefficient \(4/(5\pi^2)\) describes unavoidable quadratic conditioning deterioration as the wheel grows and supplies a testable benchmark for edge reconstruction under tree constraints. The analytic theorem and exact calculations are reproducible without external validation infrastructure.

## Closest literature, scientific limitations and residual uncertainty
Burton–Pemantle (1993; openly available initial arXiv posting 2004-04-02) establishes the general transfer-current determinant principle. Zhang–Liu–Yong (2009) treats ordinary wheel Laplacian spectra. Neither inspected material asserts the present covariance eigensystem, though uninspected specialized electrical-network work may. The result makes no claim about weighted wheels, higher-order indicators, arbitrary planar graphs or independent validation.
