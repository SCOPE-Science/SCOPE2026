Repeated Minkowski averages of an orthotope's closed \(k\)-skeleton are exactly the \(mk\)-skeleton of its uniform \(m\)-grid, becoming the whole orthotope precisely at \(mk\ge d\). The description gives sharp Hausdorff errors for all coordinate \(\ell_p\) norms and an exact wedge-of-spheres homotopy type with explicit multiplicity at every subcritical stage.
