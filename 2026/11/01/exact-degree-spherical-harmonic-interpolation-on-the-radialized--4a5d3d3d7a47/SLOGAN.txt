On the thirty-two radialized vertices of the rhombic triacontahedron, a single spherical-harmonic degree first interpolates all antipodally compatible data at degree \(17\), loses that property at several later degrees including \(44\), and regains it permanently at degree \(45\). A complete degree-by-degree rank law follows from exact icosahedral field arithmetic, finite-field determinant certificates, and a uniform Legendre bound.
