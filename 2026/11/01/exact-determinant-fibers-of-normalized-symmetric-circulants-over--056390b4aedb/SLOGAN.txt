Among symmetric circulant matrices over any odd finite field with row sum \(1\), nonzero determinants are uniformly distributed when the order is even, while at odd orders not divisible only by the field characteristic they are uniformly distributed over nonzero squares and never nonsquares. At odd orders that are pure powers of the characteristic, every determinant is \(1\); exact zero and nonzero fiber counts follow from Frobenius–inversion orbits.
