For uniform measure on any \(d\)-simplex, the exact depth against translates of its scaled copies has a sharp global phase at scale \(d/(d+1)\): above it the centroid is the unique median and every nonbaseline depth region is a homothetic simplex independent of scale. The nonbaseline mass emerges to the \(d\)-th power, the entire depth-value law is explicit, and even arbitrarily large dilates remain a fixed positive distance above halfspace depth at the centroid.
