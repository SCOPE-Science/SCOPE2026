# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The full CDF is obtained from twelve congruent spherical Voronoi pentagons. The five removed spherical side segments are pairwise disjoint before the polygon's circumradius; their area evaluates to the displayed inverse-trigonometric expression. Local sector asymptotics prove the \(3/2\) and quadratic exponents with sharp coefficients. The standalone coordinate-based verifier and moment quadrature provide independent numerical corroboration, not a substitute for proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** Finch's primary width-paper full text treats the tetrahedron and cube, not this law. The closest published full-text icosahedral result calculates the second moment and explicitly excludes full distributions. The corresponding complete octahedral distribution uses a different vertex-site arrangement. A general 2026 polyhedral-width treatise gives a much broader representation; its available abstract names other evaluated solids, but its complete text could not be read, so priority is not certified.

**Value — PASS.** The icosahedron is a canonical three-dimensional test body. A complete elementary width law and its exact critical exponents give probability data that the known moment tables do not provide. The result distinguishes side tangencies from five-corner saturation within a natural spherical Voronoi cell.

Limitations include the three-dimensional regular icosahedron specialization, historical search incompleteness, and lack of independent audit.
