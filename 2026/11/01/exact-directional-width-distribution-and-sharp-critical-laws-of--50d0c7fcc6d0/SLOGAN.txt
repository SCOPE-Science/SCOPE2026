For the circumradius-one regular icosahedron, the Haar-random directional width has a complete elementary CDF supported on \([1.589308944584\ldots,2]\) with an exactly linear upper branch and a sharp \(3/2\)-power correction at its first spherical-cap overlap. Its lower tail vanishes quadratically, with an explicit coefficient determined by the five corners of each spherical Voronoi pentagon.
