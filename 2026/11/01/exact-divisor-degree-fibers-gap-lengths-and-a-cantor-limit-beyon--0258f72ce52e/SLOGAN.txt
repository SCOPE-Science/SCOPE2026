Beyond the \(\varphi\)-practical boundary \(p>2^a+1\), monic divisors of \(X^{2^a p^b}-1\) have an exact digit-indexed degree multiplicity spectrum, with \(2^{b+1}-2\) uniquely represented interior degrees and a complete formula for every missing-degree gap. Their normalized degree supports converge to a deleted-digit Cantor set of dimension \(\log(2^a+1)/\log p\), while uniformly sampled divisors converge to an exact-dimensional measure of dimension \(\bigl(\log(2^a)+(\log2)/2^a\bigr)/\log p\).
