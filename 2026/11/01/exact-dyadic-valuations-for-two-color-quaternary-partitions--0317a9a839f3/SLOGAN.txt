For the two-color quaternary partition numbers \(S_{4,2}(n)\), the exact binary valuation is \(3\) at odd indices, \(2\) when \(\nu_2(n)\) is odd, and \(4\) when \(\nu_2(n)\) is positive and even. This settles Ulas–Żmija Conjecture 6.6(4), proves \(4\)-automaticity in this case, and gives the three exact frequencies \(1/2,1/3,1/6\).
