On a uniformly branching \(b\)-ary probability tree, the depth-alternating martingale transform has the exact attained \(L^\infty\) norm \(\frac{2(b-1)m}{b+1}+\frac{2(b-1)}{(b+1)^2}(1-(-1/b)^m)\), while its \(L^2\) norm stays one. Its sharp endpoint amplification grows linearly in tree depth, with a branching-dependent coefficient and a parity-sensitive finite-depth correction.
