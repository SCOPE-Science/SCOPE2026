For every simple two-terminal network of internally disjoint paths, the electrical gradient projection has an explicit, attained \(\ell^\infty\) norm depending only on its path lengths. Among such networks with \(N\) edges and minimum path length at least a fixed \(s\), the sharp worst-case amplification is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{N}/(2\sqrt{s})\), quantifying precisely how forbidding short terminal shortcuts suppresses instability.
