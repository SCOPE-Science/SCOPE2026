On a balanced complete \(r\)-partite metric graph with \(m\) vertices per part, the exact maximum-norm amplification of arclength-orthogonal edgewise-affine projection is \(\frac{24mr^2-70mr+51m+4r-5}{3(2r-3)(2mr-3m+1)}\). Its sharp amplification crosses \(2\) exactly when the part size reaches \(m=4\), and every vertex supports an explicit sign-function extremizer.
