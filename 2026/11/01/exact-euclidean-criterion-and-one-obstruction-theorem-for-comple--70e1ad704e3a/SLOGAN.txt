For every unit-conductance complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, a single explicit rational expression in the part sizes decides whether effective resistance itself is a Euclidean distance, and the centered squared-resistance Gram matrix has at most one negative direction. Two ten-vertex profiles with the same largest part, \(K_{1,1,3,5}\) and \(K_{1,2,2,5}\), lie on opposite sides of this sharp boundary.
