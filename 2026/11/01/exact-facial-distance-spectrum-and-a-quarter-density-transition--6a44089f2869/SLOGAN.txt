Every nonvertex coordinate face of the Euclidean hypersimplex \(\Delta_{n,k}\) has exact squared separation \(n/[n(a+b)-(a-b)^2]\) from the convex hull of the other vertices, where \(a\) and \(b\) count coordinates fixed to one and zero. Optimizing these face separations gives an all-rank sharp facial-distance formula and a quarter-density transition between two-free-coordinate minimizers and increasingly high-dimensional minimizing faces.
