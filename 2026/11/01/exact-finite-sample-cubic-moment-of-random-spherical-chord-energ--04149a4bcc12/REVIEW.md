# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The chord density \(r/2\) implies the moments \(4/3,2,16/5\), variance \(2/9\), and centered cube \(-8/135\). Leaf-conditioning proves joint independence of chords indexed by every forest. This reduces the cubic moment to one-edge and genuine triangle terms, with the exact factor \(6\binom n3\). The Funk--Hecke eigenvalues are evaluated using Rodrigues' formula and an explicit beta integral; they decay as \(\ell^{-3}\), justifying uniform harmonic expansion and the trace-cube formula. A checked rational partial-fraction identity sums that trace to \(15\pi^2/64-64/27\). The finite-cutoff central limit theorem and a uniform expected-tail bound establish the infinite weighted-chi-square law. The code independently checks exact integrations through degree ten and evaluates the trace and a genuine spherical-triple simulation. These finite checks alone do not prove the theorem, which is supplied by the analytic steps.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical assessment; earlier limit explicitly acknowledged)
Bakshaev (2010), Theorem 4, already establishes the full weighted chi-square limit for this exact spherical chord statistic, including the Legendre-integral coefficients; that limit is **not** an originality claim. The deterministic connection between chord energies and cap discrepancy is established by Stolarsky and analyzed explicitly by Brauchart--Dick (2011) and Bilyk--Dai--Matzke (2016). Brauchart--Dick explicitly mention that the chord kernel's harmonic eigenvalues are known; those eigenvalues themselves are **not** asserted as new. Borda--Grabner--Matzke (2023) give expected energies and discrepancies for determinantal ensembles and mention the iid expectation. The inspected primary material did not state the exact **third** centered moment at every finite sample size or its \(\pi^2\) closed form; the limiting skewness value is its immediate consequence. The chi-square series itself is prior work. Bakshaev and generic degenerate U-statistic theorems cover the weighted-chaos law, but do not explicitly supply the evaluated finite cubic formula in the inspected materials. ## Mathematical value — PASS
The chord energy is the classical spherical energy measuring point-set uniformity through the Stolarsky discrepancy. Its finite cubic moment determines a first non-Gaussian asymmetry statistic and yields a closed-form benchmark for random independent configurations at **every** sample size. The previously known nonnormal reference law receives an exact skewness evaluation; the triangle interaction is substantive and invisible in the pairwise covariances. No claim of a general U-statistic theorem or of a convergence rate is needed to establish that value.
