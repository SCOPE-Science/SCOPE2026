For the chaotic logistic map \(x\mapsto4x(1-x)\), invariant-measure Chebyshev compressions of its \(n\)-th multiplication observable are exactly scalar until the observation rank exceeds \(2^{n-1}\); in the ensuing sub-\(2^n\) rank window all eigenvalues lie in \(\{1/4,1/2,3/4\}\). A complete residue-path formula at every rank yields a sharp transition from a scalar point mass to the full arcsine spectral law when rank grows faster than \(2^n\).
