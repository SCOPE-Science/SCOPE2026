For the nearest-neighbor lattice Hardy inequality at the continuum coefficient, the smallest squared Euclidean support radius witnessing failure is exactly \(24\) in dimension nine, \(3\) in dimension ten, \(2\) in dimension eleven, and \(1\) thereafter; no violation exists in dimensions three through eight. In the exceptional first dimension, exact 90-orbit and 98-orbit integer certificates establish respectively strict positivity through radius \(23\) and strict failure at radius \(24\), without assuming the test functions are symmetric.
