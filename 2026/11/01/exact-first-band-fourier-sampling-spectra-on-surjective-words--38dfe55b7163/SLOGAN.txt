On the uniform set of onto \(q\)-color words of length \(n\), the constant and all first-coordinate Fourier characters have exactly three explicitly known Gram spectral levels, governed by a ratio of Stirling numbers. Reconstruction is injective exactly for \(n>q\), with sharp squared condition number \(q+2\) at the minimal injective length \(n=q+1\), and becomes exponentially close to tight for fixed \(q\).
