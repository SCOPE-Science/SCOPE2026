For every complete multipartite graph, its four-path-free simplicial complex is a wedge of two-spheres, with the exact multiplicity \(\binom{N-1}{3}-\sum_i[\binom{n_i-1}{3}+(N-n_i)\binom{n_i-1}{2}]\). A uniform acyclic matching proves the classification even in non-shellable cases such as \(K_{3,3,3}\), which yields \(38\) two-spheres.
