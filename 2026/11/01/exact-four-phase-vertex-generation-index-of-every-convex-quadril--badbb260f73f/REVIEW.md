# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The four-point fiber of nonnegative affine barycentric representations is a segment; each coordinate's maximum is therefore achieved at one of its two triangle endpoints. The two diagonal subdivisions give exactly four local linear minimax problems. The proof supplies sharp lower inequalities and witnesses in every parameter branch of the two independent sectors; exchanging coordinate axes gives the other two. Taking the minimum yields the global four-phase formula including overlap boundaries. The relation to the Schneider threshold and parallelogram rigidity follows algebraically. The independent exact-rational vertex-enumeration script passed 101 nondegenerate parameter systems; these finite cases are not treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, provisionally
The closest primary sources examined are Schneider, *A measure of convexity for compact sets* (1975), Artstein-Avidan–Falah–Slomka, arXiv:2407.20604, and Meyer, arXiv:2610.26744. Schneider defines the original index and establishes general bounds; Artstein-Avidan–Falah–Slomka provide the equivalent vertex-generation parameter and broad extremal statements; Meyer gives general finite-convexification criteria. Neither inspected text supplies the evaluated two-parameter, four-phase quadrilateral profile, its exact active sectors, and the all-shape sharp threshold. Other published mathematical summaries mentioning quadrilateral affine invariants concern different quantities and do not imply this formula. Residual risk: an equivalent earlier covering-index calculation under other names could exist. Priority is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This exactly evaluates a natural, previously general but not explicitly class-calculated convexification invariant for every point in the two-dimensional affine moduli family of quadrilaterals.
