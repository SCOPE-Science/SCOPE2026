# Scientific review of the exact square-antiprism shadow law

## Correctness
PASS. The eight explicit antiprism vertices have equal edges; the ten outward facet normals and their incidences are reconstructed in the proof and independently regenerated by `verify.py`. For almost all viewing normals, a vertex is on the convex silhouette precisely if incident facet normals have both signs. The proof enumerates every sign regime on a symmetry sector and integrates its sphere measure exactly; the four probabilities sum to one. The numerical checker agrees with direct independent planar convex hull computations for 20,000 deterministic normals and with an unrelated dihedral-angle formula for the expected side count.

## Originality
PASS, within the accessible comparisons, with residual historical priority risk. Glisse--Lazard (2007, arXiv:cs/0702087) derive an expectation formula and general mesh bounds; neither determines the joint side-count distribution of this exact solid. Glisse et al. (2016, DOI:10.20382/jocg.v7i1a5) address a Poisson-polytope ensemble, not fixed antiprism view angles. The nearby known shadow laws for octahedra and rectangular pyramids do not contain these geometric face normals. Searches for a square-antiprism pentagon/hexagon/heptagon/octagon distribution found no exact prior table; this is not a proof of historical uniqueness. Kettner--Welzl's 1997 general contour analysis was bibliographically identified but its full text was inaccessible.

## Value
PASS. The problem is a natural, fully specified random-view invariant of the first nontrivial square antiprism; it requires a correlated facet-sign calculation beyond the well-known first-moment dihedral identity. A complete exact four-outcome law, including positive but small heptagon probability and a direct mean cross-check, provides a reusable benchmark for geometric probability and polyhedral silhouette algorithms. The value rests on the exact joint law, not on the mere fact that numerical projections can be counted.

## Residual limitations
No independent historical-priority or scientific audit has yet been performed. The shape-height parameter is fixed by equilateral triangular faces; arbitrary-height antiprisms are not covered. Numerical diagnostics are not substituted for analytic universality.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
