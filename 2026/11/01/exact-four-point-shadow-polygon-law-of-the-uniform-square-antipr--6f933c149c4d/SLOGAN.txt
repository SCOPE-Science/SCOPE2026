A Haar-random planar shadow of the uniform square antiprism has exactly \(5\), \(6\), \(7\), or \(8\) vertices, with an explicit four-point inverse-trigonometric probability law. The rare heptagonal case has exact probability \(\frac8\pi[2G(\pi/8)-G(\pi/4)]\approx0.011795388796146\), where \(G(t)=\arcsin(\rho\sin t)\) and \(\rho=\sqrt{2/(2+(\sqrt2-1)^2/\sqrt2)}\).
