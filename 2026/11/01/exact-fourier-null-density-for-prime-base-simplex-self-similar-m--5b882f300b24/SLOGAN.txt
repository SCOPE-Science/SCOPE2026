For the prime-base simplex self-similar measure in dimension \(p-1\), the vanishing integer Fourier frequencies are exactly those whose first nonzero \(p\)-adic coordinate digits form a permutation of \(1,\ldots,p-1\). Their exact lattice density, equivalently the density of null torus-convolution modes, is \((p-1)!/(p^{p-1}-1)\).
