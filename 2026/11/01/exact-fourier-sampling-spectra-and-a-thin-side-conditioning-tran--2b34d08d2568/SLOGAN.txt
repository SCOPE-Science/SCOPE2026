The complete first Fourier-band Gram spectrum for uniformly sampled proper \(q\)-colourings of \(K_{r,s}\), for every \(q\ge3\), has just four nonconstant spectral sectors and is always positive definite. For fixed \(q\) and one side fixed, increasing that side from one to two vertices changes the squared conditioning growth as the opposite side expands from quadratic to exponential.
