The Fourier–Stieltjes coefficients of the classical Thue–Morse diffraction measure vanish **exactly** at the frequencies \(\pm5\cdot2^r\) and \(\pm7\cdot2^r\); every other integer frequency survives. At every odd frequency \(n\ge9\), the exact dyadic denominator is controlled by \(v_2(\widehat\nu(n))=2-\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor\), yielding a complete Fourier-multiplier nullspace classification.
