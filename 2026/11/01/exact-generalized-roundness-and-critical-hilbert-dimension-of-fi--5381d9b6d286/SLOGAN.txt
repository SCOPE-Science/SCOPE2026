Every finite projective-plane incidence metric has exact generalized roundness given by the unique \(p\in(0,1)\) satisfying \(\sqrt q(3^p-1)=2^p\), irrespective of the plane's isomorphism type. At this boundary an explicit \((q^2+q)\)-dimensional space of negative-type equalities opens up, and the least Euclidean dimension of the critical snowflake drops from \(2q^2+2q+1\) to \(q^2+q+1\).
