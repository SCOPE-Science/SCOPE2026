The connected crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M\) has generalized roundness \(1\) for \(n=3,4\), but for \(n\ge5\) its exact exponent is the unique \(p\in(0,1)\) solving \((n-1)(2^p-1)=3^p\). Deleting the perfect matching strictly improves the exponent relative to \(K_{n,n}\), and the same spectral decomposition determines the attained negative-type gap and minimal endpoint Euclidean dimension.
