For logistic excitatory threshold networks with arbitrary nonnegative row-stochastic connectivity, the scalar hysteresis wedge is the exact frontier for unique global attraction, even at arbitrarily strong coupling outside the wedge. At its critical cusp, the optimal squared-amplitude envelope is \(3/(8t)\); every generic orbit in an irreducible network has the shared sharp correction \((x_i(t)-1/2)^{-2}=(8/3)t-(8/5)\log t+C+o(1)\).
