For every positive diagonal stretch of the regular rhombic dodecahedron, a Haar-random central plane cuts either a hexagon or an octagon, with an exact three-term arctangent probability law. The undeformed solid is a strict local minimizer of octagonal-section probability modulo scale, with Hessian coefficient \(1/(\pi\sqrt2)\) in centered logarithmic stretch coordinates; strong axial stretching makes hexagons asymptotically rare.
