# Verification of the exact central-section probability law

The included `verify_dodecahedron_haar.py` was executed successfully with standard Python 3. Its exact arithmetic represents every coordinate in the integer quadratic ring generated by the golden ratio; comparisons use integer square tests, never floating-point approximations. The program exhaustively checks all 1,024 signed-hemisphere patterns, all 90 candidate great-circle intersection rays, all 20 vertices, all 12 supporting pentagonal facets, and all 30 edges.

The resulting chamber table contains 20 spherical hexagons corresponding to six-sided sections, 60 spherical triangles corresponding to eight-sided sections, and 12 spherical pentagons corresponding to ten-sided sections. The code certifies every chamber angle multiset from the exact pairwise vertex dot products. Spherical excess and the area normalization are analytic steps written in `RESULT.md`, not numerical integrations.

The finite computational enumeration is exhaustive because a full-dimensional chamber of a central great-circle arrangement is a pointed polyhedral cone whose extreme rays are pairwise plane intersections. A separate mathematical proof of this completeness statement is supplied in `RESULT.md`. The computed counts are mathematical evidence for this particular finite dodecahedral configuration only. No inference is made about generic polyhedra or noncentral sections. The work has not received independent validation.
