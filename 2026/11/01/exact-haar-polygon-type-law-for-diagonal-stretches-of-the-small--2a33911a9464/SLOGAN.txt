A uniformly oriented central section of the small rhombicuboctahedron, after any positive diagonal stretch, has almost surely 8 or 12 vertices, decided by the sharp inequality \(a>(1+\sqrt2)b+c\) for its ordered weighted normal coordinates. Its complete three-parameter Haar probability is a six-solid-angle arctangent sum; the regular solid has eight-vertex probability \(0.20786393703415482\ldots\).
