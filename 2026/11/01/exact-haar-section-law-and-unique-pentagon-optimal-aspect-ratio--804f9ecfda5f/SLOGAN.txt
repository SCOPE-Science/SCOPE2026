Every right regular triangular prism has an exact three-point law for the side count of a Haar-uniform central section: only triangles, quadrilaterals and pentagons occur, with all probabilities explicit in three arccosines of the aspect ratio. The pentagonal-section probability has a unique optimal half-height \(h_*=0.5314695510636\ldots\) when the base circumradius is one, attaining \(0.3851558056742\ldots\).
