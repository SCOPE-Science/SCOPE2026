On the 56-vertex Gosset polytope in \(S^6\), exact-degree spherical-harmonic evaluation has ranks \(1,7,27,21,27,28,28,\ldots\), with every degree at least five spanning its full antipodal-parity sector. The first consecutive pair of degrees that interpolates arbitrary values at all vertices is \(5,6\), while degrees \(4,5\) miss exactly one direction.
