A uniformly oriented planar shadow of a regular icosahedron is almost surely a hexagon, octagon, or decagon, with exact probabilities \(0.638408811559\ldots\), \(0.238989468900\ldots\), and \(0.122601719541\ldots\). The same three-point law governs random central planar sections of the polar regular dodecahedron; an exact icosahedral spherical-triangle decomposition gives the probabilities.
