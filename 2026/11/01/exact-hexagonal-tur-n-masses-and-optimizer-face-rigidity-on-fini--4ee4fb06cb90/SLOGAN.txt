On the finite square torus, positive-definite functions supported on the seven-point root hexagon have exact maximal mass \(1-3/\sigma_N\), where \(\sigma_N\) is a balanced-three-gap cosine minimum. The optimizer is unique even among complex-valued functions unless three divides the modulus; at those resonant orders its optimal face has exactly four affine dimensions, and the nonresonant excess has a signed cubic residue correction.
