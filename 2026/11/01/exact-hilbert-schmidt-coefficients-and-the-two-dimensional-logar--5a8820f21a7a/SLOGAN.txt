The homogeneous Rhaly operator on the \(d\)-variable Drury–Arveson space is Hilbert–Schmidt exactly when \(\sum_N A_d(N)|a_N|^2<\infty\), with a closed combinatorial formula for every coefficient \(A_d(N)\). Precisely in two variables, \(A_2(N)=(N+1)H_{N+1}\), creating an additional logarithmic endpoint penalty and a sharp \(\tfrac12(\log K)^2\) critical truncation law.
