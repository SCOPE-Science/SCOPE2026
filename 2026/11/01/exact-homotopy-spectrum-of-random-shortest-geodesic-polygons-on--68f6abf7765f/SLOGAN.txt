For every length \(n\ge3\), the probability that a polygon formed by independent uniform vertices and shortest geodesic edges on the round projective plane is noncontractible has an exact Catalan–Legendre spectral series. It increases strictly from \(1/\pi\) for triangles through \(4/\pi^2\) for quadrilaterals toward \(1/2\), with an explicit exponentially small, parity-oscillating correction.
