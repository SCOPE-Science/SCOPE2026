For a star with \(m\ge3\) equal-mass leaves and hub-to-leaf mass ratio \(q\), the exact centered maximal \(1\)-variation constant is \(\max\{1-1/(m+q),1/(1+q)\}\), attained respectively by a leaf and hub indicator. The unique optimal hub mass balances these mechanisms at \(q=(\sqrt{(m-1)^2+4}-(m-1))/2\).
