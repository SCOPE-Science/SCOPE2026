The intrinsic inradius of a Haar-random central plane section of the unit regular octahedron has an explicit elementary distribution, obtained from its reciprocal relation to the circumradius of a projected cube. Its cumulative distribution is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at the sharp interior radius \(\sqrt{3/8}\), where its second derivative jumps by \(-256\sqrt3/\pi\).
