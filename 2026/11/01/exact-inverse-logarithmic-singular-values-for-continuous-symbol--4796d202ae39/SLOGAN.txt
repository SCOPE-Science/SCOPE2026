A continuous radial-angular symbol on the weighted Bergman disk produces a compact big Hankel operator whose singular values decay at the exact rate \(a\sqrt{\psi_1(\alpha+1)}(\log j)^{-a-1}\) for every fixed \(\alpha>-1\) and \(a>0\). The operator lies in no finite Schatten class, although replacing its slowly vanishing radial perturbation by zero restores \(j^{-1}\) decay.
