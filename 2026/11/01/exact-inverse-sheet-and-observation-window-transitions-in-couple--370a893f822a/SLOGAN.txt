For positive cubic parameters \(a,b\) and \(0\le\varepsilon<1\), a nearest-neighbor network of \(N\) coupled Gumowski–Mira maps is globally left-right equivalent to \(N\) independent cubic inverse problems, yielding exactly \(3^N\) preimages of the origin regardless of coupling strength. On the bounded observation cube \([-L,L]^{2N}\), the *maximum* inverse multiplicity has two sharp transitions, \(1\to2^N\to3^N\), at explicit radii determined by the local cubic parameters alone.
