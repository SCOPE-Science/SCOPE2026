For every \(d\ge2\), the capped paraboloid \(\{(x,z):\|x\|^2\le z\le1\}\) has explicit unique John and Löwner ellipsoids and sharp volume ratio \(\frac{d+1}{2}((d+1)/(d-1))^{(d-1)/2}\). Its Löwner ellipsoid is unchanged if the entire quadratic body is replaced by the strictly smaller circular cone with the same apex and top rim, because those two contact orbits form a complete optimality certificate.
