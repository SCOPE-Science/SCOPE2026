For a Haar-random hyperplane through \(te_d\) in a regular crosspolytope, every face count is an explicit polynomial of a single Gaussian absolute-rank statistic, giving a complete one-dimensional-integral joint law. As \(t\) approaches the vertex from inside, the rank becomes uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,d\}\), and the full face vector has \(d-1\) distinct limiting values with probabilities \(1/d,\ldots,1/d,2/d\).
