For every irrational contracted rotation with contraction \(0<\lambda<1/3\), the closed Cantor attractor needs exactly \(n+1\) intervals at the natural scale \(\lambda^n/(1-\lambda)\). When the rotation number is of bounded continued-fraction type, this attractor has strictly positive and finite Hausdorff measure at the critical inverse-logarithm gauge, resolving a published lower-bound question for this family.
