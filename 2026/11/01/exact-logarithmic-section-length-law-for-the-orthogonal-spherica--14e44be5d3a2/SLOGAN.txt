For a Haar-random great circle cutting the orthogonal spherical lune, the entire intrinsic section-length law is \(F(t)=(t+\cot t\log|\cos t|)/\pi\) on \(0<t<\pi\), continuously extended at \(t=\pi/2\). Its density has an integrable logarithmic peak at \(\pi/2\), while the mean and median are exactly \(\pi/2\).
