For determinant-optimal single-vertex reconstruction of the first two, three, or four graph-Fourier modes on a normalized path, the optimal sensor locations have exact size-dependent transitions and exceptional ties: endpoints dominate small paths, while their immediate neighbors dominate sufficiently long paths. The inward migration holds for every bandwidth \(m\ge2\) once \(n\ge\lceil3m^{3/2}\rceil\), independently of the injective spectral evolution.
