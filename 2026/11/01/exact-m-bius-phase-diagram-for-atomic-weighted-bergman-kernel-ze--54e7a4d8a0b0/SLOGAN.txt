A single positive point mass added to the standard weighted Bergman norm gives a complete, Möbius-invariant phase diagram of reproducing-kernel zero fibres, with explicit entry thresholds and at most \(2\lceil(\alpha+2)/6\rceil-1\) simple scalar zeros. The first nonreal pair can occur exactly when \(\alpha>4\), and for fixed positive mass the zero count grows asymptotically like \((\alpha+2)/3\).
