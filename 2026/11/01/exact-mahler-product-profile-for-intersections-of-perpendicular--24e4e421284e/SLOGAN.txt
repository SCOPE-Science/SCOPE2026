The intersection of two congruent perpendicular ellipses has the exact Mahler product \(16\arctan(1/t)^2+8(t-t^{-1})\arctan(1/t)\), which strictly decreases with eccentricity \(t\ge1\). It continuously connects the disk value \(\pi^2\) to the square value \(8\), with sharp quadratic endpoint rates.
