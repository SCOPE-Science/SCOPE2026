For synchronous chip-firing on the simple complete graph \(K_n\), the primitive two-cycle mass polynomial has an exact binomial–geometric formula, and its total orbit count grows as \(n^n/4\). Under uniform sampling, the firing-set imbalance and centered chip mass converge jointly to independent Gaussians at scales \(\sqrt n\) and \(n^{3/2}\), with variances \(1/16\) and \(1/48\).
