For two independent synchronous lazy walkers beginning on opposite sides of \(K_{a,b}\), the exact first-meeting mean has a unique optimal holding probability. If both sides grow, the optimum is asymptotic to \((16ab/(a+b))^{-1/3}\), whereas on increasingly large stars it converges to \((\sqrt3-1)/2\); on balanced bicliques, this tuning saves 25% of the leading meeting time compared with holding probability \(1/2\).
