For every pair of nonempty finite simple graphs, the complete bivariate weight-and-defender enumerator of pointwise-minimal Roman dominating functions of their join is the sum of the factor enumerators, corrected only by all-one labelings, isolated vertices, and pairs of nonuniversal vertices. In particular, \(c(G\vee H)=c(G)+c(H)-1+i(G)+i(H)+p(G)p(H)\), yielding an explicit polynomial recurrence for the same census on cographs.
