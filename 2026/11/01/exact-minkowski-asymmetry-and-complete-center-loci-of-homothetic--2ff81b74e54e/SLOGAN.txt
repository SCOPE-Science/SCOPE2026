Every homothetic frustum of an arbitrary convex base has Minkowski asymmetry equal to its base asymmetry plus the untruncated fraction of the height: \(s(F_t(B))=s(B)+1-t\). All minimizing point-reflection centers are explicitly inherited from the base for \(t<1\), while an additional interval of centers emerges at the prism endpoint whenever the base is asymmetric.
