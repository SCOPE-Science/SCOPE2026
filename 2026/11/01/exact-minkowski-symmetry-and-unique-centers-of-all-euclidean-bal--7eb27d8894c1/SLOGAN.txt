Every full-dimensional Euclidean ball truncated by one hyperplane has exact Minkowski reflection symmetry \(\frac{a+\sqrt{(1-a)^2+1}}2\), with a unique center and no dimension dependence. The full cap-depth law extends the previously known half-ball instance, and its thin-cap paraboloid limit has the golden-ratio symmetry \((\sqrt5-1)/2\).
