The all-vertex metric of a unit star with \(q\ge3\) once-subdivided arms has an exact negative-type exponent given by \(q(1+2^{p/2})^2=1+(q-1)(3^p+2^{3p/2})\), with a unique critical equality direction and a one-dimensional Euclidean-rank drop. The exponent decreases strictly with the branching number and tends to \(1\) with an explicit inverse-branching law, strictly below the exponent of the unsubdivided star.
