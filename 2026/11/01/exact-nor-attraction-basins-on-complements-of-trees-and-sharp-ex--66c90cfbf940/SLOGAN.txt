For open-neighborhood NOR on the complement of any finite tree with at least four vertices, every state is periodic after at most one update, and every fixed-point and two-cycle attraction basin has an exact degree-based formula. The unique largest basin is always a two-cycle; across trees on \(n\) vertices its sharp size ranges from \(2^{n-1}-3\) for a broom to \(2^n-2n+2\) for a star, with the path maximizing it among nonstars.
