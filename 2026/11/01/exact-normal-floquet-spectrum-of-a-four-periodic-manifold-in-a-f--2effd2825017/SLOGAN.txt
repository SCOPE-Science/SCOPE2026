For a natural autonomous family of fifth-order four-component rational recurrences, every positive four-periodic state lies on a smooth 12-dimensional periodic manifold whose exact 20-dimensional return spectrum is \(1\) twelve times and \(p^2\) eight times. The manifold is locally attracting when \(0<p<1\), while every such periodic state is unstable when \(p>1\); the published \(p=2\) examples have eight expanding multipliers equal to \(4\).
