For the regular-polygon version of a recently constructed piecewise-affine oscillator, every one-sided forward-sector radial crossing has an exact principal-Lambert map and a sharp strict-entry interval. Moving the initial radius toward the polygon center produces a new, parameter-tunable trichotomy—linear contraction, square-root passage, or finite-radius ejection—even though the target cycle retains period \(N/\omega\) and Floquet multiplier \(\exp(-Na)\). This is a one-sided section statement, not a global hybrid-basin claim.
