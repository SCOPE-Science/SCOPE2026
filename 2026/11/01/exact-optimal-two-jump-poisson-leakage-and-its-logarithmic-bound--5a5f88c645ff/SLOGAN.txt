For periodic two-jump binary boundary traces, the Poisson \(L^1\) leakage is strictly concave in the occupied arc length, uniquely largest at half duty cycle, and has an exact dilogarithmic value at every smoothing radius. The sign waves in a recent Hardy–Littlewood argument consequently have the sharp boundary asymptotic \((8/\pi)N(1-\rho)\log(1/[N(1-\rho)])\), with a globally sharp quadratic stability bound.
