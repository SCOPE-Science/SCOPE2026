On a complete graph with equal numbers of synchronous majority-following and minority-following agents, all but a parity-dependent collection of exact two-cycles flow into one universal four-cycle within two updates. When the population half-size \(k\) is even, precisely \(\binom{k}{k/2}^2\) states belong to those two-cycles, with a sharp Gaussian critical-window attraction probability; for odd \(k\), not a single two-cycle exists.
