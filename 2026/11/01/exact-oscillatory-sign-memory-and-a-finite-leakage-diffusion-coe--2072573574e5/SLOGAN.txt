At the first exactly Markov symmetric paired tent map, left/right occupation has exact oscillatory covariance \(C_n=(\sqrt2q)^n[\cos(n\pi/4)+\sin(n\pi/4)/\sqrt3]\), where \(q=(\sqrt3-1)/2\), and its stationary sign-sum CLT variance is \(1+2/\sqrt3\). Despite a Markov six-cell itinerary, the sign process is not one-step Markov: its one-step-calibrated two-state Markov surrogate overestimates the long-run variance by precisely \(\sqrt3\).
