The sequence counting binomial coefficients not divisible by \(p^\alpha\) has exactly \(2\alpha-1\) independent \(p\)-kernel subsequences over \(\mathbb Q\), for every prime \(p\) and \(\alpha\ge1\). A truncated two-borrow-state digit representation is both reachable and observable, resolving the precise rank question posed by Rowland in 2010.
