For three-state synchronous excitation on two equally sized cliques joined by a perfect matching, every initial configuration reaches a fixed point, a three-cycle or a four-cycle in at most four steps, and exact formulas determine the number attracted to each period for all sizes. The probability of eventual period three equals \(1-9(4/9)^n+18(2/9)^n-9^{1-n}\), so it tends exponentially to one.
