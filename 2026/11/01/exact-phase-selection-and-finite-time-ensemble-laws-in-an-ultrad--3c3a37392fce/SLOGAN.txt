In the ultradiscrete FitzHugh–Nagumo map, the two phases of an attracting cycle capture exactly one third and two thirds of uniformly sampled initial states until a parameter threshold, after which the phase weights vary rationally; nevertheless every symmetric neighborhood of the unstable fixed point splits exactly half-and-half. The entire finite-time ensemble has a closed atomic-plus-uniform law with sharp even-time total-variation error \(4^{-n}\).
