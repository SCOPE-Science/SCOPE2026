Complete labelled Haar-magnitude data and the signed sum of a binary dyadic signal have exactly \(2^{b}\) binary preimages, where \(b\) counts balanced internal splits with nonzero contrast. At Hamming weight \(k\), the largest fiber has exactly \(2^{\min(k,N-k)}\) elements, and the unique globally largest fiber consists of all signals with one occupied site in each adjacent pair.
