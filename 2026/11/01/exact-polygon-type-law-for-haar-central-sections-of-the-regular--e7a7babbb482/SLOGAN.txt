A Haar-uniform central plane through the regular truncated octahedron produces a hexagon, octagon, or decagon with exact probabilities \(0.4984506899177035\ldots\), \(0.31610885558019075\ldots\), and \(0.18544045450210572\ldots\), respectively. Five explicitly described spherical normal cones and a pointwise three-threshold formula yield closed solid-angle expressions for the complete law.
