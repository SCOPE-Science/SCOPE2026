For three-dimensional Euclidean capsules \(K_{r,a}=rB_2^3+[-a,a]e_1\), the normalized Blaschke–Santaló deficit is exactly \(\tfrac14(a/(r+a))^2\). The volume product strictly decreases from the ball value \(16\pi^2/9\) toward the circular-cylinder value \(4\pi^2/3\), giving a sharp quantitative model along this natural Minkowski-sum family.
