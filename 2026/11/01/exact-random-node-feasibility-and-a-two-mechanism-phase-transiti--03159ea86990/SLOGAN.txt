With \(n\) independent uniform nodes on \([-1,1]\), positive quadratic-exact quadrature at target second moment \(q\in(0,1)\) has an exact all-\(n\) probability obtained by subtracting extreme-node and central-gap product tails; for \(n=3\) it is \(\tfrac{3q}{2}(\log(1/q)-1+q)\). Its failure probability has the sharp exponential root \(\max\{(1+q)/2,1-\sqrt q\}\), with a crossover at \(q=3-2\sqrt2\).
