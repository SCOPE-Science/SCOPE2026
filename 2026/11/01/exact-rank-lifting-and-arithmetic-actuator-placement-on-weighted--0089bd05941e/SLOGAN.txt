For any positive, identically decorated cycle with a weighted pendant path of length \(\ell\) at every vertex, the rank deficiency from leaf actuators equals \(\ell+1\) times the number of invisible cycle Fourier pairs, independently of every positive edge-weight ratio. Two terminal leaves at separation \(d\) control the entire graph exactly when \(\gcd(n,2d)\le2\), and the complete rank for any nonempty sensor set follows from a single gcd.
