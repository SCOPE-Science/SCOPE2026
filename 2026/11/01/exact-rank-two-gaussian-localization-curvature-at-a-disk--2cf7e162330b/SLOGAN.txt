For every area-preserving smooth perturbation of a disk in analytic Gaussian Fock space, the sum of the two largest localization eigenvalues has an exact nonradial Fourier shape Hessian: the first harmonics are flat and every higher harmonic strictly lowers the sum. The sharp positive transverse coefficient comes from the quadrupole mode \(k=2\), with value \(e^{-t}t(3+2t)/((t+2)(t+3))\) in the positive-frequency Fourier normalization.
