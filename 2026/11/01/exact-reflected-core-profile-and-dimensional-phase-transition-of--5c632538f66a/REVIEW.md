# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The horizontal sections of the reflected overlap are nested centered homothets when the horizontal reflection shift vanishes. Any nonzero shift strictly decreases overlap over a positive-height interval. The exact one-dimensional objective, derivatives on the two center-height regimes, unique critical point, threshold and both integrated formulas follow without regularity assumptions on the base. Monotone-likelihood-ratio ordering proves strict parameter monotonicity; direct expansions establish the dimensional crossover. The standalone numerical and exact-rational tests passed, and are supporting checks only.

## Mathematical value — PASS
For an arbitrary centrally symmetric convex base, the theorem gives a complete exact frustum symmetry profile, a transition in the unique maximizing center, strict invertibility of the aspect ratio from the overlap fraction and a sharp high-dimensional critical window. These are structural claims for a natural geometric family, not numerical evaluations of a single shape.
