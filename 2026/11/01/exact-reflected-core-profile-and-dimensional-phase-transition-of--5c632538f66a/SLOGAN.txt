For every centrally symmetric convex base, the maximal point-reflected overlap fraction of its homothetic frustum is shape-independent and obeys an exact two-branch law, with the unique optimal center switching to mid-height at \(t=(2\cdot2^{1/m}-1)^{-1}\). The sharp \(m^{-1}\)-scale approach to the prism produces a nonconstant piecewise exponential crossover between limiting ratios \(1/2\) and \(1\).
