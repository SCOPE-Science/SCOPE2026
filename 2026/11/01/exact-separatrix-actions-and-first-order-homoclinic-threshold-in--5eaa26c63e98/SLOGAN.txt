For the equal-depth sextic triple-well oscillator, the Hamiltonian separatrix divides its three bounded lobes into the exact symplectic-area ratio \(2:1:1\). Under reversible odd-position damping, its persistent outer homoclinic curve has the new explicit tangent \(b_0=-(19/21)a^2b_1+O(b_1^2)\), leaving a Hopf-to-homoclinic window of width \((2/21)a^2|b_1|+O(b_1^2)\) for small negative \(b_1\).
