For the three-color inhibitory firefly rule on every labelled star \(K_{1,r}\), the first synchronization times have the exact seven-layer census \(3,2^r,2^r,2^r,2^r-2,3^r-3,3^r-2^r-1\), with \(3^r-3\cdot2^r+3\) colorings never synchronizing. Every synchronizing start aligns within six updates, and as \(r\to\infty\), first synchrony at times five and six and permanent nonsynchrony each occur with probability tending to \(1/3\).
