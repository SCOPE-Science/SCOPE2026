For nilpotent \(3\times3\) matrices, fixing the entire numerical-shadow distribution still allows exactly a computable interval of singular spectra: \(\|A^2\|\) ranges between the two nonnegative endpoint roots of \(2r^3-\kappa r^2+|\tau|^2=0\). The lost spectral information collapses with the sharp square-root law as the shadow approaches its rigid boundary.
