# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact update on two complete bipartite shores factors through 49 ordered nonempty three-color support pairs. The complete table supplies an exhaustive finite argument, and its recurrent-support cycles are explicitly lifted back to genuine labelled periods one, three and four; support period alone is not mistaken for labelled period. Each preperiod is at most two. Weighting each support pair by the exact surjection count proves all six formulas for every positive shore size, not only the finite samples. The independent labelled-state simulator tests 72,720 complete configurations and the symbolic formulas for 196 parameter pairs; its recorded results agree. The rare-event limit follows from an exact difference identity and asymptotic domination.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff (2016), arXiv:1610.07320, already supplies the same cyclic update, general synchronization criterion, and the diameter-two synchronization bound; these are prior work, not new claims. Its inspected Introduction, Theorem 3 and finite-graph section do not provide a labelled six-class complete-bipartite census, the exact primitive period-four orbit population, or the asymptotic *conditional* concentration on one-step four-cycle entry. The earlier biclique firefly model has a different inhibitory local rule; in particular, its asynchronous periodic states have no one-step transient basin. Searches of published mathematical records by original rule, graph aliases, support method, transient depth, period-three/four census, and class formulas found no equivalent. An unindexed previous equivalent remains possible; this is a self-review, not independent originality certification.

## Value — PASS

Bicliques are a canonical dense bipartite test family for three-color excitation. The theorem gives exact recurrent orbit populations and every transient-entry stratum rather than merely an existence or period bound. Its structural contrast with the separate inhibitory firefly automaton is mathematically meaningful: under this cyclic rule, the oscillatory period-four basin has a nonempty one-step transient part, and that part asymptotically dominates all exceptions to immediate period-three dynamics. The latter is a natural and non-obvious conditioned fluctuation statement. The result does not purport to be a general-graph classification.

## Residual scientific limitations

The support reduction relies on complete bipartite neighborhoods and does not transfer unchanged to arbitrary graphs. The short synchronization-time bound alone is already known from the diameter theorem. No independent scientific audit has occurred. An unindexed exact historical census is a residual originality risk.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
