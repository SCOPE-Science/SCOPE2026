For uniformly oriented planes through the center of a regular truncated tetrahedron, the numbers of intersected triangular and hexagonal faces have exactly six possible joint types, all with explicit \(\arccos\) probability formulas. The generic section has \(4\), \(5\), or \(6\) sides, with probabilities approximately \(0.215936\), \(0.520247\), and \(0.263816\).
