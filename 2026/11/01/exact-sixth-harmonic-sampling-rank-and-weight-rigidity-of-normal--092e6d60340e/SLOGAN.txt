For the normalized type-\(B_n\) root system, exact degree-six spherical-harmonic evaluation has rank \(6\), \(13\), or \(n^2-1\) according as \(n=3\), \(n=4\), or \(n\ge5\). From dimension five onward every antipodally symmetric annihilating weight is forced to be constant on each root-length orbit, with short-to-long ratio \((16-n):4\); the invariant ratio itself was already accessible from hyperoctahedral design theory.
