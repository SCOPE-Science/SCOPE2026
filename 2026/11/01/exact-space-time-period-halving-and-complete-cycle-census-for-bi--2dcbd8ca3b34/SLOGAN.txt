For binary nearest-neighbor coupled cat maps on a ring of \(2^m\ge4\) sites, the exact temporal period of a state with joint spatial period at least \(4\) is half that spatial period; joint spatial periods \(1\) and \(2\) both give temporal period \(1\). This identity yields every primitive-cycle count, and the full evolution permutation has order precisely \(2^{m-1}\).
