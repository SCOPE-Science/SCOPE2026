For an equilateral finite metric space with one added equiradial hub, the Lipschitz-free \(p\)-norm of every positive one-sink molecule is exactly a subset-routing minimum, not a general spanning-tree optimization. For equal sources, the strict norm-loss threshold is \(m>(t^{-p}-1)^{-1/(1-p)}\), with exponentially growing critical support as \(p\uparrow1\) for fixed \(t>1/2\).
