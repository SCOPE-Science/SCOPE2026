A single exponentially reset impact oscillator supports two positive bouncing cycles—and stable rest—under a drive initially weaker than gravity, precisely when restitution exceeds \(1/2\) and the forcing lies in an explicit sharp wedge. The fold has a quadratic cusp at restitution \(1/2\), where impacts instead slow as \(T_n\sim16\tau/n\) and accumulate only at infinite physical time, in contrast to the finite-time Zeno sequence below this restitution threshold.
