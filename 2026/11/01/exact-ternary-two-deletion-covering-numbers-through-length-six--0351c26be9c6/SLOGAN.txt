For a ternary alphabet, the exact minimum sizes of codes covering every output after two arbitrary deletions at source lengths \(4,5,6\) are respectively \(2,5,9\). Four length-five sources can cover at most \(26\) of the \(27\) possible outputs, sharply; the length-six optimum follows from a nine-center cyclic-alphabet construction and an exact \(90/11\) weighted-shadow lower certificate.
