For every fixed pure Landau level \(r\), the finite polyanalytic Ginibre intensity has a strictly positive exact \(\sqrt N\)-scale disk-approximation error: \(\|\rho_{r,N}-\mathbf1_{D_N}\|_1\sim c_r\sqrt N\), with an explicit central-binomial formula for \(c_r\). This proves sharpness of the previously established upper order at every level and gives \(c_r\sim4\sqrt r/\pi\) for the sequential high-level limit.
