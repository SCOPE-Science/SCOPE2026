# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
Equation (1) follows from a fourfold parametrization of smooth ellipses. The singular part of the derivative is localized near the two minor-axis tips and is exactly evaluated by a beta integral after rescaling the angular coordinate. The other derivative term is bounded by \(O_p(a\log(1/a))\), negligible relative to \(a^p\) for \(-1<p<0\). Integrating the derivative gives the negative gamma-function cusp, while evaluating at the circle supplies the positive terminal slope. Both full-dimensional Hausdorff separation and the exact nested-pair sharp quotient are proved analytically. High-precision quadrature confirms the formulas in representative cases but is not substituted for a proof.

## Originality — PASS, with risk
The earlier symmetric fixed-perimeter needle extremal inequality (Csató--Giovagnoli--Roy, arXiv:2608.19851v1) is an upper bound under a varying major axis and does not imply a signed cusp for fixed-axis nested ellipses. The origin-containing positive-weight inclusion theorem and different negative-weight examples (Csató--Giovagnoli, arXiv:2610.11677v1) do not resolve the all-negative-exponent ellipse subclass. The unrestricted-pair weight-rigidity result (Saracco--Stefani, arXiv:2306.07770v1) uses translations and is also insufficient. A published global Hölder-modulus theorem establishes the exponent \(p+1\) and is credited; it uses balls and does not imply the exact signed ellipse coefficient. Similar unindexed historical computations cannot be ruled out.

## Value — PASS
The result closes a well-posed sharp inclusion-monotonicity threshold on a canonical smooth centrally symmetric class, gives a complete exponent-level failure classification and a nonzero exact singular stability coefficient, and forces a genuine interior optimizer. The same exponent sharpness survives even when both comparison bodies are smooth full-dimensional ellipses of the same maximal semiaxis. No new global Hölder exponent, all-convex-body phase classification, or uniqueness of the interior minimizer is claimed.
