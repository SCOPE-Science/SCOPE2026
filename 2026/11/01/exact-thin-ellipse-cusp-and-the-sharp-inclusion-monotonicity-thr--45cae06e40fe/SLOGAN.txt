For the nested, centered ellipses \(K_a=\{x^2/a^2+y^2\le1\}\), the radial-power boundary integral is inclusion-increasing for all aspect ratios exactly when \(p\ge0\). Every \(-1<p<0\) instead has a strict thin-ellipse decrease with the exact negative \(a^{p+1}\) cusp coefficient \(2p\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma((1-p)/2)/[(p+1)\Gamma(1-p/2)]\), so the sharp non-Lipschitz exponent occurs even for pairs of smooth, full-dimensional, centrally symmetric ellipses with fixed major axis.
