On an \(m\)-page triangular book, three-color cyclic cellular automata and Greenberg–Hastings excitation have exactly the same \(2(2^m-1)\) primitive three-cycles and the same oscillation basin. Yet among nonperiodic initial states that eventually oscillate, the cyclic automaton always enters its cycle after one update, while Greenberg–Hastings entry times are exactly equally distributed among one, two and three updates for every \(m\ge2\).
