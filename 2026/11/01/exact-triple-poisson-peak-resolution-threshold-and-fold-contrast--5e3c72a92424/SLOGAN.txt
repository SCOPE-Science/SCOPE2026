For three equally weighted point masses at \(-1,0,1\), their upper-half-plane Poisson extension has exactly three peaks precisely below height \(h=1/\sqrt2\), then only one peak. The vanishing outer peak has sharp peak-to-valley contrast \(\frac{8\sqrt2}{9\pi}(1/2-h^2)^{3/2}+O((1/2-h^2)^2)\), quantifying the fold at the exact resolution threshold.
