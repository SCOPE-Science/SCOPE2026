For synchronous binary majority with tie retention on \(K_{a,b,c}\), pairwise distinct positive part sizes admit exactly one primitive temporal two-cycle, while **exactly** two equal parts of size \(p\) admit \(B(p,q)=\sum_{j=0}^{q}\binom qj\mathbf1_{\{|2j-q|<p\}}\) opposite-phase two-cycles with the third part of size \(q\), plus one more when \(q>p\). This exact all-unequal-size classification reveals a sharp equality-driven jump in oscillation counts; the previously known fully balanced case is not claimed as new.
