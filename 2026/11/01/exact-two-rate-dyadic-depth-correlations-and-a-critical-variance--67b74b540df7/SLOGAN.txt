For a random interval map with identity and doubling branches, the stationary dyadic-depth observable has an exact two-exponential covariance \(C_n=2(1-q/2)^n+\frac{2q(1-q)}{(1+q)^2}((1-q)/2)^n\). Its accumulated variance has an exact finite-time formula, a stationary Gaussian limit with variance asymptotic to \(8/q\), and a sharp critical crossover when the observation window has order \(1/q\).
