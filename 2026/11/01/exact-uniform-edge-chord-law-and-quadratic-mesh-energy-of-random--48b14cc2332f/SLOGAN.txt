For the convex hull of \(n\ge4\) uniform points on the unit sphere, the complete Euclidean length law of a uniformly chosen edge is a product of an explicit universal angular variable and a \(\operatorname{Beta}(n-1,2)\) radial factor. The expected sum of squared edge lengths is exactly \(60(n-1)(n-2)/((n+1)(n+2))\), always \(15/\pi\) times the expected surface area.
