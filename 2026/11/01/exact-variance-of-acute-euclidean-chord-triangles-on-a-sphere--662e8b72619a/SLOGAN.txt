For \(n\) independent uniform points on the two-sphere, the number \(T_n\) of strictly acute **Euclidean chord triangles** among all triples has exact variance \(\binom n3/4+(\pi-3)\binom n2\binom{n-2}{2}/4\). Two acute-triangle indicators sharing exactly one edge have covariance \((\pi-3)/8\), whereas indicators sharing at most one vertex are independent, revealing an exact order-\(n^4\) fluctuation law.
