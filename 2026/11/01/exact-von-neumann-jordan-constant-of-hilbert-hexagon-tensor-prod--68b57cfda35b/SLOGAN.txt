For the real affine-regular hexagon \(G=(\mathbb R^2,\max\{|a|,|b|,|a+b|\})\), both its injective and projective tensor products with any real Hilbert space of dimension at least two have exact von Neumann–Jordan constant \(1+\sqrt3/2\). This sharp, dimension-independent value quantifies the strict non-squareness that was previously known only qualitatively for these tensors.
