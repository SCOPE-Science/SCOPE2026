The Radon mean-section body of any Euclidean orthotope has an exact all-dimensional inradius, a finite water-filling classification of its circumradius and all maximizing normals, and a sharp radius ratio \(R/r\ge\sqrt{d/(d-1)}\), with equality exactly for cubes. In three dimensions the circumradius changes formula precisely when the largest squared facet area crosses the sum of the other two.
