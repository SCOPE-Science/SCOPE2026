For the Heawood graph, point–line duality changes the rational homology character of the perfect-matching complex by exactly the bipartition-exchange sign: \(\widetilde H_2(\mathcal M_{\mathrm p}(H);\mathbb Q)\cong\widetilde H_4(\mathcal M(H);\mathbb Q)\otimes\varepsilon\). The perfect-complex character has no invariants under the full \(336\)-element group, although one invariant line survives under the \(168\)-element collineation subgroup; its full-symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
