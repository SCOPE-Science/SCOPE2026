The global antipodal quotient of the simplicial double of the six-dimensional cross-polytope has \(\widetilde H_1\cong\mathbb Z^{46}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^{15}\) and \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{195}\). A free involution thus creates fifteen independent first-homology two-torsion classes even though the covering complex is a torsion-free wedge of circles and two-spheres.
