Among all planar constant-width bodies whose support functions have Fourier degree at most five, the sharp minimum area is \(15\pi w^2/64\), and the only minimizers are translated and rotated pure third-harmonic supports. The previously numerically observed cosine-only optimum extends rigorously to all independent third- and fifth-harmonic phases, with the quantitative bound \(\operatorname{area}(K)-15\pi w^2/64\ge6\pi(c_5^2+d_5^2)\).
