Every nonzero generalized Hata–Yano perturbation admits an explicit finite stationary core measure: a symmetric reflecting random walk on \(2r+2\) rationally located points. This settles the generalized Hata–Yano half of the source's core-measure existence problem and activates its ordinary arcsine law for every point in that finite support; the separate Gharaei--Homburg problem remains open here.
