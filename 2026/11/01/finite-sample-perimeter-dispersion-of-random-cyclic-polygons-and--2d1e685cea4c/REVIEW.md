# Scientific scope of the revised finding

**Correctness.** Rooting the cyclic order at a sampled point gives exchangeable Dirichlet gaps. Integrating a conditional pair of gaps at fixed sum evaluates the required chord-product moment, including when one of the gaps exceeds a semicircle. Summing the diagonal and ordered cross contributions and subtracting the squared mean gives the exact finite-sample formula. The triangle and quadrilateral values are consistency checks.

**Originality.** The evaluated formula for arbitrary finite sample size is the contribution retained here. The four-point variance appears in Resultary `1c13b772d78d`; the higher-order variance coefficients are direct consequences of the perimeter Taylor expansion and exact monomial spacing covariances in `c0156d4b4464`. Neither is claimed as a separate new result. The Dirichlet framework, mean and leading asymptotic also precede this finding. The potentially relevant Bélisle (2011) and Wang–Xu (2018) full texts were unavailable, so historical priority is not certified.

**Value.** The perimeter is a natural finite-sample estimator of circle length. Its exact variance accounts for chord dependence across all gaps and supplies a calibration quantity when the leading asymptotic is too coarse.
