For the convex hull of \(n\ge3\) iid uniform points on the unit circle, the full perimeter variance has an exact finite-sum expression at every sample size. Beyond its known leading \(40\pi^6/n^5\) scale, the next two coefficients are \(-672\pi^6/n^6\) and \((6728\pi^6-320\pi^8)/n^7\), quantifying finite-sample dispersion.
