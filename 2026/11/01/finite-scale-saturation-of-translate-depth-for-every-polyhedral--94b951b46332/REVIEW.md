The explicit cutoff turns the entire continuum of translated-polytope depth queries into finitely many one-dimensional projection-tail evaluations in bounded data domains, including discrete samples. A sharp positive onset for the uniform square shows that saturation is not a zero-scale tautology. This is a structural polyhedral-versus-smooth boundary phenomenon, rather than a routine reformulation of the limit theorem.
