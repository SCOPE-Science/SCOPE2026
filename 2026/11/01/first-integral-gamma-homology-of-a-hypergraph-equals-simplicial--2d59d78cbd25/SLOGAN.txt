For every finite hypergraph with supported vertices, the newly defined \(\Gamma\)-cubical homology in degrees zero and one is naturally the ordinary integral simplicial homology of its downward closure. This gives an exact general computation method and shows that \(H_1^{\Gamma}\) can contain torsion: the six-vertex projective-plane hypergraph has \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) first homology.
