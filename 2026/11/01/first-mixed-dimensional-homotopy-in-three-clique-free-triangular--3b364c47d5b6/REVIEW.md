# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The claimed complexes consist of all triangle-free simple two-matchings of a complete graph: a triple of edges forming a triangle in its line graph is exactly a three-star or triangle. All possible edge subsets are tested, with exact face vectors. The explicit repeated toggling yields fully verified acyclic face-poset matchings. The critical-cell counts and exact signed integral Morse boundaries have Smith diagonals with 35 units at seven vertices (one unit at six), giving the stated torsion-free groups and, by degree classification of attaching maps between consecutive simply connected sphere-wedge skeleta, the actual homotopy types. Independent mod-two ranks from the uncollapsed chain complexes agree. The computation is exhaustive **only** for the displayed finite range, and the proof does not extend it without justification.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual risk.** The recent clique-free-complex paper introduces the framework and sufficient shellability criteria but does not imply the homotopy of these nonchordal triangular graphs. The earlier triangle-free-two-matching algorithms concern optimization and do not compute simplicial homotopy. Ordinary two-matching complexes allow three-cycles and are strictly larger. The closest indexed triangular-graph finding studies an open-neighborhood complex, not \(\mathrm{CF}_3(T_7)\); the known icosahedral three-clique-free calculation has a different graph and homotopy type. Actual source/alias/implication searches found no covering theorem. No search proves global novelty: a possibly unindexed exact finite calculation remains the principal originality risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The first homotopy transition to simultaneous dimensions four and five in a fundamental family \(\mathrm{CF}_3(L(K_n))\) is a motivated, complete small-parameter classification. It distinguishes two different topological behaviors of triangle-free simple two-matchings that cardinality optimization and ordinary bounded-degree complexes cannot determine. The 14,430-face calculation and primitive signed 35-dimensional differential provide a reproducible benchmark for further clique-free-complex homotopy research. No infinite-family theorem, equivariant type, or shellability claim is asserted.
