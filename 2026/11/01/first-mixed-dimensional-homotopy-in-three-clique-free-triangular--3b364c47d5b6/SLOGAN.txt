The three-clique-free complex of the triangular graph \(T_7=L(K_7)\), equivalently the simplicial complex of triangle-free simple two-matchings of \(K_7\), has integral homotopy type \(S^4\vee\bigvee^{146}S^5\). This is the first mixed-dimensional case among \(T_n\) for \(2\le n\le7\), whose preceding noncontractible cases are single-dimensional sphere wedges.
