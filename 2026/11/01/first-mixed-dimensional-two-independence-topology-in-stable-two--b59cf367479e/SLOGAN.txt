For the stable two-subset Kneser graphs \(SG_{2,k}\), the first seven two-independence complexes are exactly classified: the stage \(k=6\) is \(\bigvee^{26}S^2\vee\bigvee^2S^3\), while every earlier stage has zero third homology. An exhaustive acyclic matching and integer unit-pivot certificate prove the homotopy types without extrapolating beyond \(k=6\).
