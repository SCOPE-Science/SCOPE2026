The geometric column-reflection fixed spaces of the \(3\times n\) grid matching complex at even widths \(n=2,4,6,8,10\) have homotopy types point, \(S^2\), \(S^3\), \(\bigvee^4S^4\), and \(\bigvee^{11}S^5\). An exact orbit-complex identification and exhaustive acyclic Morse certificates upgrade previously known reflection Euler numbers to full homotopy information, including the first multiple-sphere fixed locus at width \(8\).
