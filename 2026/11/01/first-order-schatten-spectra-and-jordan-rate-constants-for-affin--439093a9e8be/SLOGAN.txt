For strict affine contractions on finite-dimensional Gaussian Fock space, the error in convergence of composition powers to fixed-point evaluation has, up to a **quadratic trace-class remainder**, exactly the singular values of a finite matrix \(e^{|u|^2/2}G_u^{1/2}(A^n)^{\mathsf T}G_u^{1/2}\). A unique dominant Jordan block produces a sharp \(n^{k-1}|\lambda|^{n-k+1}\) convergence law with an explicit positive constant at every Schatten level.
