The ordinary matching complex of the five-layer triangular cylinder \(C_3\square P_5\) has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{102}S^4\), certified by a complete acyclic face matching. Its full \(S_3\times C_2\) action on fourth rational homology has character \((102,0,0)\) without column reversal and \((12,2,0)\) with reversal, on the identity, transposition and three-cycle classes of \(S_3\), respectively.
