For every \(n\ge2\), the Mephisto–Waltz iterate \(\sigma^n(0)\), with \(\sigma(0)=001\) and \(\sigma(1)=110\), has exactly five minimal unique substrings, all of length \(3^{n-2}+2\). Their precise zero-based half-open occurrence intervals are \([j\,3^{n-2}-1,(j+1)\,3^{n-2}+1)\) for \(j\in\{2,3,5,6,7\}\).
