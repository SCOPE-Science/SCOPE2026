The Euclidean least-squares projector onto convex sequences has sharp coordinatewise Lipschitz constants \(1,4/3,7/5,11/7\) at two through five equally spaced sites, with the five-site maximum forced by separated active curvature constraints. Its sensitivity is nondecreasing with sample size and asymptotically at least \(1+1/\sqrt2\) along an explicit alternating-curvature family.
