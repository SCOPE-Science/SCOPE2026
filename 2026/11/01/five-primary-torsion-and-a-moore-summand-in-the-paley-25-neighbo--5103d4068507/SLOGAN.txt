The Lovász open-neighborhood complex of the \(25\)-vertex Paley graph has the integral homotopy type \(M(\mathbb Z/5,3)\vee\bigvee^{205}S^3\vee\bigvee^4S^4\). Its genuine five-primary torsion survives an exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse reduction and distinguishes this natural pure eleven-dimensional complex from a sphere wedge.
