The support bound is literal: every selected petal contributes a mutually independent private vertex, so every admissible face occupies at most three petals. Every face with at most two occupied petals remains admissible after inserting the hub because the only hub-nonneighbors in the private parts are the independent middle vertices, at most two of which may occur. Hence the low-support complex and each triple-block attachment interface are cones. The finite three-petal calculation is certified by a complete enumeration of 537 masks, 268 disjoint matching pairs, an exhaustive 2,130-edge directed face-poset acyclicity check, and one unmatched five-simplex. No inference of the all-parameter statement from finite samples is made.

Unindexed literature and an implicit specialized corollary remain residual risks, and this is a self-review rather than independently validated review.
