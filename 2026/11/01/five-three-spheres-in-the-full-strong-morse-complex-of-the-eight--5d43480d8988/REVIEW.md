# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The precise finite vertex-filtration definition yields 1,060 nonempty faces from all 40,320 orderings; an independent cyclic-sign predicate checks all 65,536 possible incidence subsets. The 527-pair matching is verified against all 4,064 actual codimension-one incidences and its reversed Hasse digraph is acyclic. Exactly one critical zero-cell and five critical three-cells imply a wedge of five three-spheres, integrally, by elementary discrete Morse theory. Checks of mod-prime chain ranks corroborate but do not substitute for this proof. The statement is a finite classification, not an infinite extrapolation.

**Originality — PASS subject to residual source-access uncertainty.** Knudson–Owens–White's accessible publication description explicitly distinguishes its pure-cycle homotopy classification from its full-cycle homology calculations. A previously published result classifies alternating dihedral homology characters of full cycles; another treats the pure-cycle rotation module. Neither implies a perfect acyclic matching or integral homotopy for the full eight-cycle. Exact-claim searches including synonymous notations and comparisons of logical implications found no covering theorem. The original preprint full text was inaccessible here; an equivalent result hidden in it or in unindexed literature remains possible, so this is not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** This is the first cycle length at which the pure/full strong distinction produces different simplicial complexes: four additional maximal tetrahedra beyond the pure complex. The proof identifies the resulting exact integral homotopy type, upgrading known homology data and resolving potential attaching-map and torsion ambiguities in a natural boundary example. The sharp minimum and explicit perfect Morse reduction are structurally motivated, not a generic small-graph enumeration.

The result does not cover larger cycles, nor does it classify integral symmetry actions.
