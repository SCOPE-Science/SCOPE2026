For planar even-exponent Fermat balls, Fourier-mean radial integrals first diverge in exactly four axial directions at \(p=2+2/q\), while every other direction remains finite until \(p=3\). The axial critical divergence has an exact gamma-function residue \(4(1+1/q)q^{2/q}\Gamma(1+2/q)\), interpolating from the Euclidean disk to the square.
