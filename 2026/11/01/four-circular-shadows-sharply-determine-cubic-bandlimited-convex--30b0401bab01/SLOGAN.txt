Among three-dimensional convex bodies whose support functions have spherical-harmonic degree at most three, four explicitly chosen circular planar projections force a Euclidean ball, while any three planes admit nonball smooth strictly convex counterexamples. After three coordinate shadows are disks, the fourth view measures the entire nonsphericity exactly: the minimum Hausdorff distance to a ball is \(\sqrt2\) times its third angular Fourier amplitude.
