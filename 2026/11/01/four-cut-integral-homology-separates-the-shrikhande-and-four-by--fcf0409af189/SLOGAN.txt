The ordinary four-cut complexes of the Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs distinguish these two graphs with identical strongly regular parameters and equal reduced Euler characteristic \(-75\): their complete integral reduced Betti numbers are respectively \((\widetilde\beta_9,\widetilde\beta_{10},\widetilde\beta_{11})=(12,2,65)\) and \((0,21,96)\). Both complexes have torsion-free integral homology and are non-shellable pure eleven-dimensional complexes; no homotopy equivalence is asserted.
