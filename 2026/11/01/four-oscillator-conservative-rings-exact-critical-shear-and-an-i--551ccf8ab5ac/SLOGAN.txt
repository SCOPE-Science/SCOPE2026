In a four-oscillator conservative ring with mixed cosine and oriented sine interactions, precisely two conserved alternating-bond-sum leaves become pure shears. On either leaf the exact primitive-period tail for \(t\ge P_{\min}\) is \(\Pr(P>t)=2\arcsin(P_{\min}/t)/\pi\), giving infinite Haar-mean period, whereas every other leaf has finite Haar-mean period and the small-amplitude period diverges sharply like \(\pi|a-a_\pm|^{-1/2}/\sqrt{cd}\).
