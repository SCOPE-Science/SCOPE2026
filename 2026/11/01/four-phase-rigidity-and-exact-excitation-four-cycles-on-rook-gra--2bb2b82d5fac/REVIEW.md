# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The local four-word lemma excludes permanently resting sites in a nontrivial periodic orbit on a connected graph and yields two necessary and sufficient phase-label constraints. The row-column clique argument turns those constraints into the four-rectangle normal form for every \(a,b\ge2\). Counting unordered nontrivial bipartitions and the two temporal chiralities gives the orbit formula, with no missing equal-size or smallest-board boundary. The independent state-transition enumeration checks 20,280,861 configurations across nine boards, with the phase-construction set compared exactly. Finite enumeration is not substituted for the all-size proof.

## Originality

**PASS, qualified.** The 2016 first-public arXiv version of Gravner--Lyu--Sivakoff was inspected through its definition, general activity theorem and proof. It concerns asymptotic activity and cycle circulation on arbitrary graphs; the row-column four-cycle count and phase rigidity do not follow directly from its stated formulas without substantial additional case-specific work. Relevant earlier excitable-media literature focuses on lattices and stochastic or asymptotic behavior. Direct enumeration on the smallest rook board agrees with the formula, but does not imply this full two-parameter result. OEIS A087291 reproduces the square-board numerical sequence under an unrelated coprime-polynomial interpretation, so that numerical sequence is not offered as novel. The remaining priority risk is a possibly unindexed equivalent phase-coloring theorem or graph-homomorphism count, and an older primary paper whose full text could not be inspected.

## Value

**PASS.** This identifies every primitive four-cycle, not merely possible cycle lengths, for a natural infinite family of line graphs of complete bipartite graphs. It exposes a nonobvious rigidity: a high-dimensional vertex-level dynamical orbit can only occupy four rectangular phase classes, with exactly two chirality classes per pair of nonempty row and column cuts. The closed two-parameter orbit count is a natural recurrence invariant; it gives quantitative four-wave populations and the boundary cases without exhaustive state exploration. No larger classification or basin theorem is claimed.

## Limits and related literature

The result is restricted to synchronous threshold-one three-state excitation and to temporal primitive period four. It neither classifies periods other than four nor computes attraction basins or universal transient lengths. The general graph activity theorem in arXiv:1610.07320v1 is an antecedent, not an independent confirmation of the new count. Full independent scientific audit remains outstanding.
