On each labelled rook graph \(K_a\square K_b\) with \(a,b\ge2\), every primitive four-cycle in three-state Greenberg--Hastings dynamics is a pair of nontrivial row/column cuts carrying one of two chiral four-phase excitation waves. Exactly \(\frac{(2^a-2)(2^b-2)}2\) such temporal four-cycle orbits occur.
