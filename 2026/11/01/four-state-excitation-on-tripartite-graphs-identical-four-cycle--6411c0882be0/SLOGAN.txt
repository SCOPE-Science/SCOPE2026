On every labelled complete tripartite graph, four-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation and four-color cyclic successor-contact dynamics have exactly the same primitive four-cycle configurations and identical transitions along them, although their transient behavior differs. Four-state excitation becomes periodic in at most six steps, and its exact extinction polynomial gives balanced-shore extinction probability \(4(27/64)^n(1+O((2/3)^n))\).
