The matrix-square graph \(\{(X,X^2):X\in M_2(\mathbb F_q)\}\) has a completely classified Fourier-null set and exact convolution singular-value multiplicities. Its Fourier support occupies a fraction tending to \(1\) for odd field orders but only \(q^{-2}(1+o(1))\) for fields of characteristic two, a sharp dichotomy caused by the anticommutator map becoming a commutator.
