For the regular \(d\)-cross-polytope, the volume of a Haar-random hyperplane projection, normalized by \(2^{d-1}/(d-1)!\), has an exact finite-binomial second moment. Its variance is asymptotic to \(1/(3\pi d^3)\), and its centered \(d^{3/2}\)-scaled fluctuations are Gaussian, governed by a degree-four spherical harmonic rather than the usual quadratic anisotropy.
