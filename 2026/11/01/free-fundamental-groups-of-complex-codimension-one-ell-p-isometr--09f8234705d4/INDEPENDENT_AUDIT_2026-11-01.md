# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every finite n>=2 and real 1<=p<infinity other than 2, isometry forces distinct columns to have disjoint supports: the scalar Clarkson-equality identity for the images of two different basis vectors is strict off disjoint supports. Coordinatewise z -> z|z|^(p/2-1), with zero extended continuously, sends disjoint lp-unit columns to Euclidean-unit columns and has a continuous inverse. With n-1 columns among n rows the only strata are one unused row or one two-row support. The n! injection tori meet through exactly ((n-1)n!)/2 closed S^3 x T^(n-2) edge pieces, indexed by star transpositions of the unused row; this support graph is connected. The free T^(n-1) phase action is locally trivial and its quotient is one S^2 per edge attached at vertex points. Van Kampen yields a free group of rank E-V+1 for the quotient. The principal-bundle connecting map from each edge S^2 is the Hopf generator for that moving column; every column label occurs, so pi_2(Q)->pi_1(T^(n-1)) is onto and the total-space pi_1 equals the quotient free group. This gives rank 1+(n-3)n!/2, including n=2 (S^3), and its H_1 abelianization. Rebuilt injection/transition graphs and checked ranks 0,1,13,121,1081,10081 for n=2..7; finite checks do not establish the Hopf argument.
