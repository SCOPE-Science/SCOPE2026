For the logistic map \(F(x)=4x(1-x)\), a complete strict up/down itinerary with minus frequency \(p\) has an initial-state reconstruction fiber of exact Hausdorff dimension \(p\), provided minus symbols occur infinitely often and never consecutively. Under the invariant arcsine law, a typical directional itinerary has minus frequency \(1/3\) and hence leaves a \(1/3\)-dimensional ambiguity even after infinitely many observations.
