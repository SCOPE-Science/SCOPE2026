For two unordered particles on the 18-vertex Pappus graph, the complete 216-automorphism rational homology characters have dimensions 10 in degree one and 27 in degree two and are explicitly determined on all 13 conjugacy classes. Both the full symmetry quotient and the bipartition-preserving symmetry quotient are rationally acyclic, while every cyclic order-three subgroup preserves nine independent degree-two classes.
