For \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_7\times K_3)\), the natural \(D_7\times S_3\) action has \(\widetilde H_3=\varepsilon\boxtimes\operatorname{sgn}\) and \(\widetilde H_4=\mathbf1\boxtimes(\operatorname{sgn}\oplus\operatorname{Std})\) over \(\mathbb Q\). Thus its full symmetry quotient has no positive-degree rational homology despite the original complex having a sphere-wedge homotopy type.
