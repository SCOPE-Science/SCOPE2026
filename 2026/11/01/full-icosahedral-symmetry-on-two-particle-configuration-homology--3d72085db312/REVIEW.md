# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The independent cellular boundary and mod-two linear-algebra checks establish \(\operatorname{rank}\partial_2=215\) over \(\mathbb Q\) by matching lower and upper bounds. The 100 explicit cycles are integral boundaries-zero and mod-two linearly independent, hence form a rational basis. All 120 automorphisms were exhaustively enumerated and their signed action on every basis cycle was verified. Cellular Lefschetz traces then yield the complete first- and second-homology characters, with separate irreducible-character inner products. Exact definitions, finite domain and rational coefficients are recorded in `RESULT.md`; no unperformed integral torsion claim is made.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Barnett–Farber's ordered planar face-basis theorem strongly covers the *general basis mechanism* and is not the claimed novelty. Their inspected Sections 6–8 do not state the ten-class icosahedral two-particle homology character or its precise rational irreducible decomposition. The checked full dodecahedral and cuboctahedral two-particle results have different input graphs and distinct Betti ranks, so neither implies the exact icosahedral decomposition. Targeted exact-object, dual-graph, face-pair and module searches in the indexed research records and relevant literature identified no covering published formula. An unindexed equivalent specialization remains a risk; failure of search is not treated as a proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The icosahedron is a canonical Platonic graph, and the full \(A_5\times C_2\) action is a natural equivariant homology invariant. The exact decomposition separates a one-dimensional rotation-fixed first-homology class from the fully invariant subspace, giving different rational homology for two natural quotients. The 100-facial-torus signed basis is a geometric organization of the entire top representation, not an arbitrary numerical slice or a mere recomputation of ordinary Betti numbers.
