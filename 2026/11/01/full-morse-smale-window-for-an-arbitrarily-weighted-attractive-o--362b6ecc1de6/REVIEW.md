# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** Quotient reduction, exact Hessian signs, the weighted Laplacian eigenvalues \(3\) and \(1+2w\), strict potential descent, invariant diagonal and anti-diagonal circles, absence of saddle-to-saddle heteroclinics, and analytic null-preimage avoidance are all proved. The invariant-circle argument is essential; generic strict-saddle avoidance alone does not give the Morse–Smale conclusion. Critical limits: \(w\ne1/2\), \(h<1/\Lambda\) for the diffeomorphism/Morse–Smale statement and \(h<2/\Lambda\) for all-orbit convergence and Haar-a.e. synchrony. Script checks polynomial/trigonometric identities but not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS under inspected evidence.** The closest exact 2024 Kuramoto triangle paper determines the four/six fixed classes and their continuous-time stability, and none of those facts is claimed new. The 2026 discrete-Huygens paper proves Morse–Smale for paths, stars and sufficiently weak added links, not all positive weights on the complete triangle. The closest published-finding corpus triangle theorem is repulsive at a degenerate critical weight and analyzes parabolic relaxation. General \(2/\Lambda\) saddle avoidance is also prior art. The novel joint structural assertion is the arbitrarily strong-edge Morse–Smale theorem with the explicit invariant-circle obstruction to saddle connections. A broader previously published abstract criterion could still cover it.

**Value — PASS.** Arbitrary positive extra-edge coupling can change the number and types of equilibria and is outside a perturbative weak-edge justification. Nonetheless the exact global Morse–Smale property holds on the maximal diffeomorphism window, while synchrony remains a.e. attractive on a larger, potentially noninvertible window. This is a qualitative global classification of a natural model, beyond finite checking or restating standard descent.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Buescu–D'Aniello–Oliveira, arXiv:2610.10993; Zhao–Zhou, arXiv:2407.11314; Schaeffer–McCalla, arXiv:1908.01753; published published-finding corpus repulsive triangle study (2026). The theorem excludes degenerate weight \(w=1/2\), does not assert Morse–Smale for \(h\ge1/\Lambda\), and is not a larger-network theorem.
