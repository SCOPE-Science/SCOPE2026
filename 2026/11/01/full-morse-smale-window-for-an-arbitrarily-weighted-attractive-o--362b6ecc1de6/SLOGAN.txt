For every positive closing-edge weight other than the exact degeneracy \(w=1/2\), the attractive discrete three-oscillator triangle is a globally Morse–Smale diffeomorphism throughout its full invertibility interval \(0<h<1/\max\{3,1+2w\}\), with no saddle-to-saddle connections. Synchrony attracts almost every phase class throughout the larger interval \(0<h<2/\max\{3,1+2w\}\), even where the map is noninvertible; the four/six equilibrium census was known previously.
