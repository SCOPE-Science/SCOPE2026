The unordered two-particle configuration space of the truncated-octahedral graph has rational Betti vector \((1,14,55)\), and its complete \(S_4\times C_2\) representations in degrees one and two are determined on all ten conjugacy classes. A primitive full-symmetry-invariant two-cycle is the 24-term sum of tori from disjoint square–hexagon face pairs, while all four same-shape face-pair orbits are killed by face-swap symmetries; the rotational quotient retains two rational one-cycles. The classical face-torus theorem supplies the integral top-cycle basis, while the new calculation is the full orientation-sensitive symmetry character.
