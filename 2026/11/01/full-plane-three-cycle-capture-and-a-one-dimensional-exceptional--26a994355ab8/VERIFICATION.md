# Verification and limitations

The analytic proof in `RESULT.md` reconstructs the two-cycle dichotomy from the published graph results, the integral affine-coefficient invariant under composition, rank-by-rank inverse-image geometry, and a ray giving the sharp dimension. The original full-text primary article was read for the cited graph capture, the b >= 10 result, and its stated planar-basin research question; the follow-up preprint was read for its graphwise full-measure scope. The standalone `check.py` uses only Python's standard library and checks finite symbolic polynomial-in-parameter identities exactly. A finite checker does not establish measure or Hausdorff dimension: those are consequences of the written argument.

Parameter restrictions: irrational b >= 10 only; rational b and smaller b remain unproved here. Publication-priority searches are not a uniqueness proof. Independent audit was not performed.
