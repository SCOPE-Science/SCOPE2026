The first triple-Kneser independence complex with two rational homology degrees has a complete \(S_7\)-representation classification: \(\widetilde H_6\cong S^{(7)}\oplus S^{(4,3)}\oplus S^{(2,2,2,1)}\), while \(\widetilde H_9\cong S^{(1^7)}\oplus S^{(2,1^5)}\). An exact fifteen-class Lefschetz census upgrades the known Betti numbers \(29\) and \(7\) to irreducible characters.
