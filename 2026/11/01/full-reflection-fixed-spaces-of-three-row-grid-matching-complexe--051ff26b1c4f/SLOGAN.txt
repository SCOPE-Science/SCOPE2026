For the simultaneous row and column reflections of the three-row grid matching complex, the geometric fixed-point space is PL homeomorphic to \(|\operatorname{Ind}(P_q\sqcup P_q)|\), with \(q=n/2\) for even width and \(q=(n-3)/2\) for odd width. It is empty at width three, otherwise contractible or a single odd-dimensional sphere according to \(q\bmod3\), with sphere dimensions growing without bound.
