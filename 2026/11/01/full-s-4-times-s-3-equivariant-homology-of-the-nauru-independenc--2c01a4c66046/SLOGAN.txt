For the Nauru graph \(GP(12,5)\), the full \(S_4\times S_3\)-action on independence-complex homology is \(\widetilde H_5\cong(\varepsilon_4\otimes\mathbb Q^4_{\mathrm{perm}})\boxtimes\varepsilon_3\) and \(\widetilde H_6\cong\varepsilon_4\boxtimes\mathbf1\). The full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic, even though the two homology groups have dimensions \(4\) and \(1\).
