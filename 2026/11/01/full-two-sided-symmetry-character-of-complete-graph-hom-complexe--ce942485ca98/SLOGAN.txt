For every \(2\le m<n\), a closed cycle-length formula determines the complete \(S_m\times S_n\) rational top-homology character of \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_m,K_n)\). Every full color-symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic, while quotienting by even color permutations preserves exactly \(\binom{n-2}{m-2}\) top-dimensional rational classes.
