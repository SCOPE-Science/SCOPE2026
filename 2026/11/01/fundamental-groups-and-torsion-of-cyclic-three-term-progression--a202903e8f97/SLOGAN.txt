The cyclic three-term progression-facet complex on \(\mathbb F_p\) has an explicit free-product fundamental group for every prime \(p\ge5\), controlled by the signed doubling order of two modulo \(p\). Its integral first homology exhibits exact, sometimes large cyclic torsion, including \(\mathbb Z/3\) at \(p=11\) and two \(\mathbb Z/25\) factors alongside free homology at \(p=41\).
