The ordinary shortest-path metric on the generalized Petersen graph \(GP(n,2)\) yields a positive-definite Gaussian kernel \(e^{-t d}\) for every \(t>0\) exactly when \(n\in\{5,6,9,10\}\). A universal eight-vertex signed-distance witness rules out every \(n\ge18\), while exact anchored Gram polynomials give squared-Euclidean embedding dimensions \(5,7,9,10\) for the four exceptional survivors.
