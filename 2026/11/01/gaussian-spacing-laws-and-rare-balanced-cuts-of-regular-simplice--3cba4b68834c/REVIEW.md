**Correctness — PASS.** Invariant offset measure weights normal directions by their simplex score range. Gaussian polar decomposition makes this equivalent to integrating the offset between Gaussian extrema. Exactly \(k(m-k)\) crossed edges generate section vertices, yielding the full finite-dimensional survival function as an expected Gaussian spacing. Summing edge-crossing lengths proves the mean. Central Gaussian order-statistic convergence, concentration of the Gaussian range, and binomial tail concentration prove the two limits. Checked definitions, indices, floor/ceiling boundaries, normalizations, and the fact that a finite numerical experiment is not an asymptotic proof. The reproducible program checks the finite law and tetrahedral benchmark.
