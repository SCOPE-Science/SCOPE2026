For invariant random affine hyperplanes cutting a regular simplex, the entire vertex-count law is a ratio of expected Gaussian order statistics, with exact mean \(m(m-1)/(2\sqrt\pi\,\mathbb E\max_{i\le m}G_i)\). Typical sections have a nondegenerate complexity exponent \(2-U^2\), while the chance of reaching any fixed fraction \(q\in(0,1/4)\) of quadratic complexity decays at the explicit rate \(\Phi^{-1}((1+\sqrt{1-4q})/2)/\sqrt{2\log m}\).
