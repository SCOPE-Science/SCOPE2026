At the noncompact boundary of quadratic Gaussian weighted composition on Fock space, the squared modulus becomes exactly Gaussian multiplication, with a universal twofold absolutely continuous spectrum and essential norm \(q^{-1/4}\). Its spectral radius nevertheless detects the phase of the composition slope: \(1\) for nonreal slopes with compact square, but \(q^{-1/4}\) for real slopes with every power noncompact.
