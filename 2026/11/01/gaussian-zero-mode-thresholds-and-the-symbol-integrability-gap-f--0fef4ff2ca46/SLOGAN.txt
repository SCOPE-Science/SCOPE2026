For \(z^m f+c f^{(n)}=0\), Gaussian Fock zero modes exist in exactly three explicit parameter regimes; at balanced unit-modulus order \(m=n\ge3\), all \(n\) zero modes exist but none remains integrable after multiplication by the corresponding harmonic polynomial symbol. The gap distinguishes maximal differential solvability from the natural Gaussian symbol-integrability condition.
