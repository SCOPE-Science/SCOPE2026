Every element of the natural dihedral-times-layer-swap symmetry of a circular ladder has an explicitly classified geometric fixed-point space in its independence complex: it is either empty, a known circular- or Möbius-ladder independence complex, or one sphere of a completely specified dimension. Mixed reflections produce new path-ladder models, and at width twelve two reflections have fixed spaces \(S^3\) and \(S^1\) despite identical Lefschetz numbers.
