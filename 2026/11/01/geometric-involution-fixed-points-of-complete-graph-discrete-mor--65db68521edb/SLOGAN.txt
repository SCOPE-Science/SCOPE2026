For an involution on the ordinary complete-graph discrete Morse complex, its actual geometric fixed space is always empty, contractible, or a torsion-free wedge of equal-dimensional spheres. With \(f\) fixed labels and \(t\) transposed pairs, the noncontractible multiplicities are \((2t-1)^{t-1}\) for \(f=0\) and \((f-1)^f(f+2t-1)^{t-1}\) for \(f\ge2\).
