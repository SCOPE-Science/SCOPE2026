For any finite graph \(H\), the geometric layer-swap fixed space of its prism perfect-matching complex is the matching complex of the fully whiskered graph; it is contractible if \(|V(H)|\) is odd and otherwise a wedge with exactly one sphere per perfect matching of \(H\). Its unique nonzero reduced integral homology module is the oriented permutation lattice on those perfect matchings, giving two spheres for an even cycle and \(m!\) spheres for \(K_{m,m}\).
