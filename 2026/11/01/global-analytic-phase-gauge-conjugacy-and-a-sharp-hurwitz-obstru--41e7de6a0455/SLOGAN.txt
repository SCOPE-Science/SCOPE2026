A height-dependent real-analytic rotation globally conjugates every angular-parameter value of the Castañeda–Rebollo-Perdomo polynomial oscillator, giving exact whole-basin isochrons for its attracting circle. Yet the untwisted system has a non-Hurwitz Jacobian at a fixed point of the inner periodic circle for **every** positive radial coefficient: an explicit example of dynamics preserved while pointwise Jacobian-Hurwitzness changes.
