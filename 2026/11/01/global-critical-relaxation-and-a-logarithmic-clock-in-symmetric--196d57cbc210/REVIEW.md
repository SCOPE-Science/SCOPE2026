## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem resolves a precise dynamical question at the natural endpoint \(r=s=2\) of a globally convergent species-competition system: how rapidly do all positive orbits converge? The result distinguishes full-measure algebraic relaxation from a measure-zero exponentially convergent family, proves a two-term asymptotic clock including an exact logarithmic coefficient, and identifies nonzero curvature of the exceptional manifold. Those statements cannot be inferred from mere convergence or a numerical bifurcation diagram; the boundary is mathematically motivated by a multiplier of \(-1\).
