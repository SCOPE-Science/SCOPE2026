At the critical symmetric Ricker competition parameter \(r=s=2\), almost every positive trajectory has the sharp normalized mean-coordinate law \(q_{2n}^{-2}=2n/3+(2/5)\log n+C+o(1)\). The only exponentially convergent trajectories lie on a measure-zero preimage family of a curved strong-stable manifold, whose curvature depends explicitly on the competition coefficient.
