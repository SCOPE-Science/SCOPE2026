In a two-patch logistic Keller–Segel model with exponential movement preference, all nonnegative equilibria are classified exactly: a unique symmetry-related pair of nonuniform states exists if and only if chemotactic sensitivity exceeds the linear threshold, with no folds or hidden stationary branches. The total patterned population decreases strictly with bias, but both patches retain positive limiting populations at infinite bias exactly when \(2r>\eta_n\).
