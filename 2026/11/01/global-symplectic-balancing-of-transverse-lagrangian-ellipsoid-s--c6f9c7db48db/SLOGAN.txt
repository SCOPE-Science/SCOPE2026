The global linear-symplectic minimum of mean width for the Minkowski sum of two transverse Lagrangian ellipsoids is exactly \(4\mathbb E\sqrt{\sum_i\sigma_iZ_i^2}/\gamma_{2n}\), where \(\sigma_i\) are the singular values of their symplectic cross-pairing. Every minimizer balances the two ellipsoids into identical orthogonal Lagrangian copies, and the fixed-trace spectral extrema are sharp.
