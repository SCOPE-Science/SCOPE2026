For distinct positive integers \(p,q\), the difference \(C_{z^p}-C_{z^q}\) has an exact essential singular interval \([|p^{-(\alpha+1)/2}-q^{-(\alpha+1)/2}|,p^{-(\alpha+1)/2}+q^{-(\alpha+1)/2}]\) on every weighted Bergman Hilbert space with \(\alpha>-1\), and its range is always closed. At the Hardy-space endpoint the interval becomes \([0,2]\) and the range is never closed; the Bergman difference is Fredholm precisely when one power equals one.
