The Fano-plane incidence graph has \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(H)\simeq\bigvee^{13}S^5\), while its full 336-element automorphism group acts on \(\widetilde H_5(-;\mathbb Q)\) as the augmentation of its fourteen-vertex permutation representation. Consequently the full orbit space is rationally acyclic, although the quotient by the bipartition-preserving subgroup retains one degree-five rational homology class.
