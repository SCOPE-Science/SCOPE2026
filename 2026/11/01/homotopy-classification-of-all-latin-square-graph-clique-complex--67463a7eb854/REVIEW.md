# Review: homotopy classification of Latin-square graph clique complexes

## Correctness — PASS
For every Latin square \(M\) of order \(n\ge5\), the rook subcomplex is a good-cover nerve of \(K_{n,n}\), hence its fundamental group is generated by rectangular loops. Each extra symbol edge is homotopic to a two-edge rook path. Every rook rectangle either admits a diagonal or bounds the explicit embedded disk of six clique triangles. This kills all generators *as loops*, proving simple connectivity. Cioabă and Mim [1, Theorem 3.12] independently establish the all-field homology ranks; the integral universal coefficient, Hurewicz, and homology-Whitehead steps are verified in RESULT.md. The packaged checker independently enumerated all cliques and computed mod-two ranks for cyclic orders \(5,6,7\) and the nonabelian group-table Latin square of order \(6\).

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The closest exact-object source [1] computes homology, not the nonabelian fundamental group or full homotopy type. Its Proposition 3.3 gives exactly the six triangles as a *bounding chain*, which is acknowledged as substantial prior coverage. A chain filling is not by itself a nullhomotopy. The additional geometric disk interpretation, rook nerve, and surjectivity argument prove the missing statement for every Latin square. A restricted earlier result applies to odd cyclic Latin squares only, and neither it nor the published all-field Betti theorem determines the homotopy type for arbitrary even and non-group examples. Targeted exact and alias searches did not locate a stronger full-domain theorem; an implicit or unindexed derivation remains a real risk, not a proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The claim supplies a complete homotopy type for a canonical infinite family of flag complexes depending on arbitrary quasigroup tables. The new simple-connectivity input excludes spaces with the same all-field homology but nontrivial perfect fundamental group. At fixed order six, cyclic and nonabelian-group tables give different wedge ranks \(116\) and \(98\), illustrating sensitivity to the natural intercalate invariant. This is a structural homotopy upgrade, not a numerical-table recomputation; its scope and limits are explicit.

## Closest literature and limitations
[1] Sebastian M. Cioabă and Mutasim Mim, *On the homology groups of clique complexes of strongly regular graphs*, arXiv:2606.27328, first public 2026-06-25, especially Proposition 3.3 and Theorem 3.12. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27328

The theorem is nonequivariant, does not exhibit a canonical sphere basis or a simplicial collapse, and uses the source's homology computation. The disk triangles already occur in [1]; novelty rests on the geometric nonabelian consequence and the full-domain homotopy classification. Independent expert assessment and global completeness of the originality search are not claimed.
