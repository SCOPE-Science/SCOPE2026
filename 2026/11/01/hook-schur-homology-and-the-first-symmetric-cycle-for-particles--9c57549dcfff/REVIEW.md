# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof invokes the established functorial reduced Świątkowski comparison for graph configurations and spells out the two-term complex in each particle weight. The differential is multiplication by the augmentation-lattice linear forms. Its image is the homogeneous ideal of differences, whose quotient has one generator in each nonnegative weight. The Koszul resolution on the regular sequence of differences justifies the integral presentation; after rational equivariant splitting, the kernel of each symmetric-power multiplication is the hook Schur functor by Pieri's rule. The group character follows from an exact sequence and a cycle-index determinant identity. The invariant threshold is obtained by a separate Burnside orbit calculation. Independent finite cubical Lefschetz checks cover all conjugacy classes for eleven small parameter pairs and agree. No finite experiment is used to infer the all-parameter theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The nearest direct source is An–Drummond-Cole–Knudsen's `arxiv:1708.02351` Section 5.1, which explicitly gives the same star-chain complex and generators/relations, but does not state the hook-Schur direct sum, closed all-element arm-character series, or the uniform five-particle invariant threshold. Farley–Sabalka `math/0410539` and Maciążek–Sawicki `1606.03414` compute free groups/Betti numbers, not this symmetry representation. Lütgehetmann `1701.03490` concerns ordered particle configurations. Searches of a mathematical findings index under star braids, arm permutation characters, hook Schur functors and invariant thresholds did not identify an equivalent result. A direct specialization of the existing complex is not itself novel; the explicit uniform equivariant consequences are the assessed contribution. Equivalent unindexed results remain possible, and deriving these consequences from the prior complex is fairly economical.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The first rational homology of star graph-braid spaces is a basic motion-planning invariant whose rank was known, but whose full natural arm-symmetry character is not determined by that rank. The result gives a uniform rational module formula, cycle-type trace generating function for arbitrary particle counts, an integral syzygy presentation, and a sharp family-independent onset of symmetry-invariant first cycles at five particles. This is structural information about a natural infinite family, not a parameter-forced one-off calculation. It does not claim a new graph-configuration chain model or an irreducible Specht multiplicity table.
