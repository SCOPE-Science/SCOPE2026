For every \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge2\), the complete rational arm-permutation homology of \(n\) unordered particles on an \(m\)-armed star is \(\bigoplus_{q=1}^{n-1}\mathbb S_{(q,1)}(A_m)\), where \(A_m\) is the arm-permutation augmentation representation. Exactly five particles are needed before a nonzero first-homology class is invariant under every arm permutation.
