On a homogeneous tree, every bounded horocycle-dependent symbol has a diagonal harmonic-Bergman Toeplitz compression whose labelled eigenvalue profile is an explicitly invertible one-sided convolution. Its sharp inverse stability constant is \(\kappa(q,\alpha)=\frac{(1+r)(1+rs)^2(1+rs^2)}{(1-r)(1-rs)^2(1-rs^2)}\), with \(r=q^{1-\alpha}\), \(s=q^{-1}\), and alternating symbols realize equality. The unordered spectrum alone does not suffice for reconstruction.
