# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The known exact homotopy equivalence \(\Theta(\operatorname{Cube}(4,2))\simeq\bigvee^{15}S^6\) supplies the homological concentration. The new proof derives a signed fixed-simplex Lefschetz formula valid for any finite simplicial permutation, with explicit proof of its orbit-sign exponent. A complete exhaustive finite calculation evaluates that formula on all 384 symmetries and 20 signed conjugacy classes. The independently stated target table is checked by executable assertions. A class-average calculation proves the invariant subspace vanishes, and the central order-two element has maximal trace, forcing trivial action. The code is exact integer arithmetic; there is no unsupported passage from finite experiments to a variable-size theorem.

## Originality
PASS, with residual bibliographic risk. The 2008 original paper explicitly computes the homotopy type and Euler characteristic of the *same* four-variable theta complex; neither is reclaimed. Section 6 concerns congruence of Euler characteristics under finite groups, not the full rational homology character. Searches for hyperoctahedral homology characters of four-cube cut and Čech complexes returned different spaces, including a 129-dimensional cut-complex module and a 24-dimensional Čech module. The new assertion is the twenty-class character of this particular fifteen-dimensional theta-complex module, not a restatement of a homology rank or an application to a different cube complex. Equivalent unindexed specialized representation work remains possible; the search cannot certify uniqueness.

## Value
PASS. The natural full automorphism action on a canonical complex attached to four-variable Boolean satisfiability has genuinely informative structure absent from the already known fifteen-dimensional homology: no fixed rational class, a central element acting trivially, and negative traces precluding an unsigned permutation basis. Determining all twenty conjugacy classes gives a complete symmetry signature for this natural 2-SAT example, not an arbitrary numerical refinement.

## Limitations
Only rational equivariant homology is classified. The existing nonequivariant wedge-of-spheres theorem is assumed, not reproved. Possible equivalent specialized calculations in unindexed literature and the lack of independent mathematical audit remain residual risks.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
