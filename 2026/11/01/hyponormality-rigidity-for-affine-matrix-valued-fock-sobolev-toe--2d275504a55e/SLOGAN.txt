For degree-one matrix-valued Toeplitz symbols \(zA+\bar zB\) on every normalized Fock–Sobolev space, closed hyponormality is equivalent to \(A^*A-AA^*+B^*B-BB^*=0\), \(B^*A=AB^*\), and \(A^*A\succeq BB^*\). The bounded self-commutator form has an exact vacuum correction \((I+mE_{00})\otimes(A^*A-BB^*)\), even for genuinely noncommuting nonnormal matrices.
