The proof uses the exact invariant dense domain and reproducing-kernel normalization of Liu–Qin–Huang (2026), Theorem 1.2 and equations (4.2)–(4.3). Its new separation lemma is proved: derivative bounds ensure Borel-transform convergence; the shift identity is verified by power series; a finite union of disjoint excluded disks has connected exterior; analytic continuation and Liouville force each nonzero separated term to be independent. An arbitrary number of nonexceptional evaluation sites then forces unbounded algebraic rank. Zero symbols yield the zero operator on the domain. No experiment is used as a universal proof.
