For the source/sink flex complex of \(K_{2,n}\), \(n\ge3\), the full integral first-homology automorphism module is the unsigned permutation module on nonempty subsets of \([n]\): exchanging the two hubs complements proper nonempty subsets but fixes the full subset. The resulting combined hub-swap character has an exact even-cycle resonance, equal to \(1+2^{c}\) only when all leaf-permutation cycles are even and otherwise equal to \(1\).
