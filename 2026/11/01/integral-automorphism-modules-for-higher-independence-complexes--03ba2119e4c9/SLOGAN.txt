For every double star with \(a,b\ge1\) leaves at its two hubs and each higher-independence threshold \(2\le r\le a+b+1\), the sole nonzero reduced integral homology is the full automorphism lattice \(\chi_{uv}\otimes\bigwedge^{r-2}\ker(\mathbb Z^L\to\mathbb Z)\) in degree \(r-1\). Outside that threshold range reduced homology vanishes; the hub-exchange sign and all symmetry characters refine the previously known ordinary sphere-wedge classification, which is not claimed new.\n
