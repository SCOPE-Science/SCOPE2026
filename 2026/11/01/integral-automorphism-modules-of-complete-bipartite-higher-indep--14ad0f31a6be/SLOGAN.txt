For complete bipartite graphs with both parts of size at least two, the integral homology symmetry module at every nontrivial higher-independence threshold is an explicit quotient of an exterior power of the vertex augmentation lattice, with complete characters even when equal parts are exchanged. At threshold two, its torsion-free first homology has nonzero symmetry coinvariants precisely when a part has size two, revealing sharp two-torsion invisible to rational Betti numbers.
