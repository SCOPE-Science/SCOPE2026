The proof has been checked step by step against the source's exact statements. The integer-coefficient claim is obtained by combining a direct geometric inverse for Hilbert–Chow on zero-cycles, first cohomology of surface Hilbert schemes, Roitman's torsion theorem, and the published factorial-torsion Chow–Euler formula. No independent mathematical audit or computer theorem prover has been performed.

The punctual-fiber cell description (one zero-dimensional affine cell in every punctual factor) and the rational section construction over the function field of a curve are both necessary. Mere connectedness of fibers, or the rational-coefficient isomorphism in the source, would not prove the integral theorem. A numerical check for the K3 example gives the symmetric-square coefficient 300 and the Hilbert-square coefficient 324; numerical checks do not prove the theorem.

The general positive-irregularity case and an all-purpose integral CSM class formula are not covered. Earlier Chow-theoretic literature may contain an implicit equivalent statement; historical uniqueness has not been independently certified.
