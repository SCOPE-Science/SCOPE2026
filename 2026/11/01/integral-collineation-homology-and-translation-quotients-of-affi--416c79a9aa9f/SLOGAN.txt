For every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge3\), the radius-two incidence-graph Vietoris–Rips complex has a complete integral collineation representation: its degree-two homology is the graph cycle lattice, and its degree-\(q\) homology is the signed tensor of line-class augmentation lattices. For finite-field planes, the translation quotient is rationally acyclic at \(q=3\), but has top rational Betti number \(18\) at \(q=4\), with an exact positive formula for all larger prime powers.
