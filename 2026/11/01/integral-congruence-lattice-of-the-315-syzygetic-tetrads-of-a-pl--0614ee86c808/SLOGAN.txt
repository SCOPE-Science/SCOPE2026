The \(315\) syzygetic tetrads of a smooth plane quartic generate precisely the formal bitangent combinations whose total coefficient is divisible by \(4\) and whose six binary theta-coordinate moments vanish. Their incidence cokernel is \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^6\), with a complete index-\(256\) determinant certificate.
