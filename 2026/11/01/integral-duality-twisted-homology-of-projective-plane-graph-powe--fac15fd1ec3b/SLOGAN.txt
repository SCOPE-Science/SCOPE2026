For every finite projective plane, the complete integral symmetry action on the degree-two homology of its scale-two incidence-graph Rips complex is the ordinary incidence-graph cycle lattice twisted by the sign of point–line duality. This produces fixed-flag character formulas for every automorphism; for the Fano plane the resulting eight-dimensional rational representation is absolutely irreducible, while its canonical polarity has integral lattice \(\mathbb Z^2\oplus\mathbb Z[C_2]^3\).
