For every clique-by-tree graph \(K_m\square T\), its total two-cut complex has a single nonzero reduced integral homology group, whose full natural symmetry lattice is \((\mathbb Z^m/\mathbb Z\mathbf1)\otimes E_T\) with an explicit orientation-sign twist. Its color-permutation coinvariants can carry exact \(m\)-torsion or \(2\)-torsion—even though the complex itself has torsion-free homology.
