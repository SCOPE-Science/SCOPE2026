The fourteen Euclidean vertices of the rhombic dodecahedron have a complete integral Vietoris–Rips filtration with successive noncontractible types \(\bigvee^{13}S^0\), \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\), \(S^2\), \(S^3\), and \(S^6\), at exact thresholds \(\sqrt3,2,2\sqrt2,\sqrt{11},2\sqrt3\). An explicit eighty-pair simplicial collapse identifies the intermediate three-sphere with the crosspolytope boundary on the eight cube corners.
