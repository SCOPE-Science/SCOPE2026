For two unordered particles on the truncated-tetrahedral graph, all integral homology is free with Betti ranks \((1,8,10)\), and ten product tori from disjoint face pairs are an integral basis in degree two. The full tetrahedral symmetry acts on rational homology as \(H_1\cong2\operatorname{sgn}\oplus2(V\otimes\operatorname{sgn})\) and \(H_2\cong\mathbf1\oplus2V\oplus(V\otimes\operatorname{sgn})\), distinguishing the full-symmetry and rotation-symmetry quotients.
