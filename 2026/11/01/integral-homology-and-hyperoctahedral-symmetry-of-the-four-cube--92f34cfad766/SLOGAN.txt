The four-cube dominance complex has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{10}\) in degree seven and \(\mathbb Z\) in degree nine, with no other reduced homology; its complete \(384\)-element hyperoctahedral rational character is determined by an exact signed-cycle and Lefschetz computation. Published dominance--independence duality then determines the integral homology of the ordinary five-cube independence complex, in degrees five and seven.
