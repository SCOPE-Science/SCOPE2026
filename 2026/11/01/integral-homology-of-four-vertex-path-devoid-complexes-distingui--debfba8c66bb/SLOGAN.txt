The ordinary four-vertex-path-devoid complex of the Shrikhande graph has torsion-free reduced integral homology in dimensions three, four and five with ranks \(32,45,6\), whereas its \(4\times4\) rook-graph counterpart has ranks \(73,18\) only in dimensions four and five. Thus this natural simplicial invariant distinguishes the strongly regular pair despite their identical parameters \((16,6,2,2)\).
