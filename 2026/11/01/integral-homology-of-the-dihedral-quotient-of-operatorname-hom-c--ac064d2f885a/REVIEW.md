PASS. The claim is about one finite, fully specified regular CW complex and one explicitly specified free action. The quotient cellular chains are exact orbit coinvariants, with the product-of-simplices incidence signs and Koszul factor-permutation signs stated in RESULT.md. Every one of the 4,554 orbit cells is generated. Signed cellular boundaries square to zero. Unimodular elimination shows that the five boundary operators have unit pivot counts \(101,468,831,667,206\), followed only by residual rank-one matrices with Smith factors \(2,5,2\) in degrees \(2,4,5\); no nonunit factors occur elsewhere. These exact integral forms imply the claimed groups. The executable contains the complete independent replayable construction and no external data dependency. A four-colour control matches the expected binary-dihedral first-homology calculation.

Later projective and circle Stiefel-quotient calculations study specified different actions. Targeted literature queries and semantic research-record searches did not locate this exact integral quotient homology computation.
