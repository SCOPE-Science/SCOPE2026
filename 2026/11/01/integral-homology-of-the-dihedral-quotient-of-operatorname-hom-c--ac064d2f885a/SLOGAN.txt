The ordinary dihedral quotient of the five-colour pentagon complex \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_5,K_5)\) has \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/5\), \(H_1\cong H_4\cong\mathbb Z/2\), and no other positive-degree integral homology. Its order-five torsion records a symmetry-quotient phenomenon invisible in the unquotiented Stiefel-manifold homology.
