The integral homology of the five-cube independence complex is \(\mathbb Z\) in degree \(5\) and \(\mathbb Z^{10}\) in degree \(7\), with no other reduced groups. Alexander duality consequently determines the previously unresolved integral homology of the associated cubical theta complex: \(\mathbb Z^{10}\) in degree \(22\) and \(\mathbb Z\) in degree \(24\), without torsion.
