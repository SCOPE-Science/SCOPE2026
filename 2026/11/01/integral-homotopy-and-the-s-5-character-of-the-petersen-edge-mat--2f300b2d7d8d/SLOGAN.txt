The ordinary edge-matching complex of the Petersen graph is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{5}S^2\vee\bigvee^{5}S^3\). Although its two nonzero reduced rational homology groups both have dimension five, their natural \(S_5\)-representations are respectively \(\operatorname{sgn}\otimes\mathbb Q^5\) and the irreducible \(S^{(3,2)}\), with no invariant vectors.
