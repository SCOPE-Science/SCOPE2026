The ordinary Lovász open-neighborhood complex of the 29-vertex Paley graph has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^7 S^3\vee\bigvee^{64}S^4\), with no torsion. Because the graph is self-complementary, combinatorial Alexander duality also gives dominance-complex homology \(\mathbb Z^{64}\) in degree 22 and \(\mathbb Z^7\) in degree 23, vanishing otherwise.
