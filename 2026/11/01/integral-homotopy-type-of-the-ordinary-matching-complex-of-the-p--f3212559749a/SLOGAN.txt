The ordinary matching complex of the ten-vertex pentagonal prism \(C_5\square K_2\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(S^2\vee S^2\vee S^3\vee S^3\). An exhaustive acyclic Morse matching and an integral unit-minor boundary computation give the full mixed-dimensional sphere-wedge type, contrasting with the known contractibility of the prism’s perfect matching complex.
