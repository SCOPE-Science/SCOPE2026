For every cross-polytope face hypergraph with \(n\ge2\) and \(1\le k\le n-1\), the only nonzero reduced integral homology of its theta complex is the sign-flip twist of the **dual** exterior-power augmentation lattice \(\mathbb Z_\eta\otimes(\bigwedge^k U_n)^*\) in degree \(2n-k-2\). At \(k=1\) and \(n\ge3\), the resulting weight lattice is rationally—but not integrally—isomorphic to the root augmentation lattice, as witnessed by fixed spaces modulo any prime dividing \(n\).
