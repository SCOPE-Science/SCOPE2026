# Scientific review of the integral hexagonal-chain homology lattice

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof imports only the published integral homotopy type \(\operatorname{Ind}(G_n)\simeq S^n\vee S^n\) [1]. The row-exchange fixed locus is shown directly to be a nonempty simplex; after equivariant subdivision, the relative cells form free \(\mathbb F_2[C_2]\)-modules. The one-row hyper-Tor spectral sequence and the periodic group resolution force a nonidentity unipotent action on rank-two mod-two homology. Integral involution classification gives the regular permutation lattice. The end-reversal orbit graph, exact two-rail transfer recursion, integer centralizer, and primitive cross-cycle pairing determine the remaining action for every \(n\). The packaged checker corroborates mod-two matrices through \(n=5\), recurrence through \(n=40\), and primitive maximal faces through \(n=14\). Finite checks are not substituted for the proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual risk.** Chandrakar--Singh [1] prove the nonequivariant sphere wedge. The closest published result [2] determines all rational reflection traces and explicitly excludes integral lattice splittings from its conclusions. Two integral involutions, the signed diagonal and swap, have identical rational characters; the prior rational theorem therefore does not imply this new integral classification. Searches for the integer module, mod-two Jordan class, Smith/free-relative formulation, and hexagonal-chain alternatives found no directly covering prior record. Generic group-homological machinery is acknowledged as standard. Residual risk: an unindexed application of that machinery to this graph family may already exist.

## Value
**PASS.** The integral \(2\)-torsion obstruction to rational eigenspace splitting is a natural missing invariant after the published topological and rational-representation classifications. Its order/index is exactly two for *every* chain length, and the full commuting second reflection is determined integrally. This is structural equivariant topology, not a routine Betti-number recomputation or an arbitrary finite slice.
