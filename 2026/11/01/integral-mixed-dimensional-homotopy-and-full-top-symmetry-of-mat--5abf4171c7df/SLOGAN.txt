The Lovász open-neighborhood complex of the triangular graph \(T_7=L(K_7)\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{246}S^3\vee\bigvee^{7}S^4\), upgrading the known mod-two Betti numbers to an exact homotopy classification. Its natural \(S_7\)-action on integral fourth homology is the sign-twisted seven-point permutation lattice, determined by a full acyclic Morse certificate and integral cycle calculations.
