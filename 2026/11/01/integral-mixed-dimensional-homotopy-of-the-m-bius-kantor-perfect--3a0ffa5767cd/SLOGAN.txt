The perfect-matching complex of the Möbius–Kantor graph \(\operatorname{GP}(8,3)\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^4S^2\vee\bigvee^8S^3\). An exhaustive discrete-Morse reduction and a primitive rank-five integral attaching matrix prove the mixed-dimensional wedge, which differs from the graph's ordinary matching complex.
