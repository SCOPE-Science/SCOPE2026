# Mathematical review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The allowed faces are exactly subsets inducing maximum graph degree one, equivalently the independence faces of the path-of-length-two hypergraph. Both recursive and exhaustive mask enumeration agree on all 20,651 nonempty simplices. An element-toggle matching has 10,320 pairs and eleven critical faces; all 120,222 directed incidences pass a global topological sort, proving acyclicity. The signed integer gradient-path recursion, run on all 933 encountered five-faces, gives the zero nine-entry cellular six-to-five degree vector. Since the five-skeleton is a wedge of nine five-spheres and its fifth homotopy group is its integral fifth homology, the single attaching map is nullhomotopic. Independent full simplicial mod-two boundary ranks corroborate the homology but are not used as an integral proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual publication-priority uncertainty.** Conant–Thistlethwaite introduce the general hypergraph independence/Alexander-dual viewpoint, not this incidence-graph calculation; Deshpande–Singh treat complete multipartite, whiskered, paths, cycles and perfect rooted trees, not this nonchordal cubic Pappus graph. The published 40-sphere Pappus matching-complex theorem is for simplices of graph edges, not subsets of graph vertices, and cannot imply the present homotopy type. Other indexed Pappus independence and 2-independence records use respectively the ordinary independence condition or another graph. Targeted searches found no covering theorem. An equivalent unindexed result remains possible.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The Pappus graph is a canonical highly symmetric 18-vertex cubic incidence graph. Its first higher-independence complex is the hypergraph independence complex for induced three-vertex paths, a natural object beyond the prior closed-form graph families. Its exact integral mixed-dimensional homotopy type, including one sixth sphere in addition to nine fifth spheres, is a useful structural benchmark not recoverable from known ordinary matching/independence results or from field homology without attaching-map data.

## Limitations

The result is a finite exhaustive computation with a complete replayable certificate, not a classification of all graphs or higher parameters. No symmetry action or external audit is determined. Publication priority cannot be deduced from absence of indexed search hits.
