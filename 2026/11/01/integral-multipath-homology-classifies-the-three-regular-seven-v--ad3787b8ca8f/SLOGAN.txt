There are exactly three regular tournaments on seven vertices up to isomorphism, and their arc-set multipath complexes have distinct, torsion-free reduced integral homology signatures \((\widetilde\beta_3,\widetilde\beta_4,\widetilde\beta_5)=(13,43,17),(12,44,15),(15,38,24)\). Two signatures and the exhaustive distinction extend an earlier calculation of the Paley case; no homotopy-type classification is claimed.
