The multipath complex of the upward Hasse digraph of the four-dimensional Boolean lattice is integrally homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(225\) seven-spheres. Coordinate permutations act on its top rational homology with \(S_4\)-character \((225,1,1,0,1)\) on the classes of cycle types \((1^4),(2,1^2),(2^2),(3,1),(4)\), including a ten-dimensional invariant subspace.
