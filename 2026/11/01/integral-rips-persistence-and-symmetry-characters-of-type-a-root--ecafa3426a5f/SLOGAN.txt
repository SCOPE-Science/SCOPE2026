For every \(n\ge4\), the Euclidean Vietoris–Rips filtration on the \(n(n-1)\) roots \(e_i-e_j\) of type \(A_{n-1}\) passes through a \((n^2-3n+1)\)-circle wedge, the sphere \(S^{n-2}\), and then the unexpectedly high-dimensional sphere \(S^{\binom n2-1}\), with exact integral persistence intervals. The full \(S_n\)-homology characters are explicit, and its quotient retains a rational top-dimensional class at the third stage exactly when \(n\) is odd.
