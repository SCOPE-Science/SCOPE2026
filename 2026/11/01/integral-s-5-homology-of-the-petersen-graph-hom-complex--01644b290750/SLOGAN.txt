The graph-Hom complex \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_2,\operatorname{KG}(5,2))\) has integral first homology equal to the cycle lattice of the 20-vertex bipartite double cover of the Petersen graph. Its complete rational \(S_5\)-homology is the multiplicity-free sum \(S^{(3,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,2,1)}\), which is invisible in its rank-eleven Betti number.\n
