For every integer \(3\le k\le30\), the ordinary \(k\)-cut complex of the squared cycle \(C_{k+5}^2\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(S^3\vee\bigvee^{(k-3)(k-2)(k+5)/6}S^4\). Exact acyclic Morse certificates and primitive signed attaching degrees rigorously confirm this finite range of the published borderline conjecture, extending beyond its original numerical tests; no claim is made for larger \(k\).
