For the Pappus graph, the action of its 216 automorphisms on \(H_3(\operatorname{UConf}_3(|P|);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^7\) has kernel exactly a normal subgroup of order three. The faithful 72-element quotient acts with three inequivalent absolutely irreducible rational summands of dimensions \(1,2,4\), and there are no global invariant degree-three rational classes.
