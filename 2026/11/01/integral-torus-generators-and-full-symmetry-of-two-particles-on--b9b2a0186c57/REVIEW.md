# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The graph and particle count are explicit. The signed cubical model is valid on the unsplit complete bipartite graph. A complete exact integer computation yields boundary ranks 27 and 60, with Smith factors of the latter equal to fifty-nine ones and one two; the eighteen geometrically defined tori generate a saturated rank-twelve subgroup of the integral two-cycle lattice. All 1152 full automorphism traces match the induced Specht character and an independent cellular Lefschetz computation. The verifier is complete finite enumeration, not empirical extrapolation. No conclusion is asserted for larger graphs.

## Originality — PASS with stated literature risk
Farber–Hanbury compute ordered complete-bipartite ranks and maturity, and Barnett–Farber establish graph intersection-form techniques, but neither accessible primary result specifies the unordered integral torus relation lattice and full \((\mathfrak S_4\times\mathfrak S_4)\rtimes C_2\) homology representation. published-finding corpus searches for exact graph, equivalent terminology, Specht constituents, tori and collision conventions revealed no dominating result. A closely related Durham thesis was accessible by abstract only; it is a legitimate residual risk, not evidence of novelty. General results that top second homology is spanned by product cycles do not give its canonical eighteen-cycle relation lattice or full group character.

## Value — PASS
The first complete bipartite graph with room for two vertex-disjoint four-cycles is a natural transition from first homology to geometrically generated second configuration-space homology. Determining the full integral relation lattice and the wreath-product module links disjoint geometric motions to all automorphisms, including the two bipartition classes exchanging, and separates symmetry structure from known Betti counts. This is a self-contained exact finite topological invariant with reuse in braid-group and configuration-space examples, not an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Literature and limitations
The two ordered-configuration papers and the product-cycle paper supply genuine overlapping background but do not state the induced representation. The complete 2010 thesis was not accessible: an equivalent result there or elsewhere cannot be excluded. The computation neither proves a general family theorem nor gives an integral homotopy classification.
