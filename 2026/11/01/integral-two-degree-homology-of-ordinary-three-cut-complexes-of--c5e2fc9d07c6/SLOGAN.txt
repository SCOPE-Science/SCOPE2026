For every binary hypercube \(Q_d\) with \(d\ge3\), its ordinary three-cut complex has exactly two free reduced integral homology groups, in degrees \(2^d-5\) and \(2^d-4\), of explicit ranks \(2^{d-3}(d^2-5d+8)-1\) and \(2^{d-1}\sum_{j=3}^{d}\binom dj\). An elementary collapse of its Alexander dual onto the cubical two-skeleton with long edges proves the all-order classification and non-shellability.
