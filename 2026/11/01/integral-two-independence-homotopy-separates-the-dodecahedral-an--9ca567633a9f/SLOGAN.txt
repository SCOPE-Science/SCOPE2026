The first higher-independence complexes of the twenty-vertex dodecahedral and Desargues graphs have different integral homotopy types: \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(\operatorname{GP}(10,2))\simeq S^6\) but \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(\operatorname{GP}(10,3))\simeq\bigvee^{41}S^7\). Exhaustive acyclic matchings and primitive integer Morse boundaries prove the sphere and sphere-wedge classifications.
