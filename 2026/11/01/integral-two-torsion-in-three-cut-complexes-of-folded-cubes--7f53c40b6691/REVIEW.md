# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For d≥5 the folded graph is the antipodal quotient F2^d/⟨1⟩, with N=2^(d−1) vertices and degree d; nontrivial coordinate words of length ≤4 cannot equal all-ones, so triangles are absent and each two-edge path lies in a unique coordinate square. Dual to ordinary Δ3 are all edges, length-two-path triangles and precisely square tetrahedra, no larger faces. Two free-face elementary collapses per square leave the square-filled 2-complex S_d plus exactly L_d=N(N−1−d−C(d,2))/2 long edges. S_d is the free twofold quotient of the simply connected cubical Qd two-skeleton; the antipodal fixed point lies in the cube interior, not its 2-skeleton, so π1(S_d)=H1(S_d;Z)=Z/2. Square CW Euler characteristic gives free H2 rank B_d=2^(d−4)(d²−5d+8)−1. UCT and integral combinatorial Alexander duality place Z^B⊕Z/2 in cut degree N−5 and Z^L in degree N−4; other reduced groups vanish. B>0 yields pure non-shellability and failure of Cohen–Macaulayness over every field. Fresh independent enumerations d5: N16, squares40, B15,L0; d6: N32,squares120,B55,L160 confirm local counts but infinite proof is the covering argument.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge PASS for the exact integral all-d folded-cube formula and natural persistent Z/2, conditional on a genuine prior-coverage risk. The earlier general Alexander-dual square-filling reduction and edge-disjoint-square theorem explain a common mechanism, but the published edge-disjoint-square theorem explicitly requires square cycles sharing no edges, whereas each folded-cube edge lies in d−1≥4 squares. Known Bayer two-cut theorem applies to Δ2, not Δ3. The new nontrivial ingredient evaluates the folded square-filled complex via a free antipodal two-sheeted cover and Euler characteristic, thus computes h1 torsion and all B,L. Prior engineered RP2 cut torsion uses another graph. The 2017 rectagraph article was only assessed at abstract level; equivalent unindexed folded-cover computations are possible.

## value

PASS

Folded cubes form a canonical family of highly regular graph quotients, with the Clebsch graph as first case. Their square-filling complex carries a natural nontrivial twofold covering and exact fundamental group; this gives a complete infinite family of natural ordinary cut complexes with persistent integral two-torsion and explicit free ranks. The result is structurally motivated and not a computationally arbitrary finite slice; it clarifies ordinary vs total cuts and sharp d≥5 boundary.
