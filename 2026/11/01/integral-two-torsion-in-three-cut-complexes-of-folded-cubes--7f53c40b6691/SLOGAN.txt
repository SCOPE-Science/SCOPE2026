For every folded cube \(F_d\) with \(d\ge5\), the ordinary three-cut complex has a \(\mathbb Z/2\) summand in reduced integral degree \(2^{d-1}-5\), with explicit free ranks in that degree and in the top dimension. The torsion comes from the antipodal double cover of the cube's square-filled two-skeleton; the smallest case is the Clebsch graph, with \(\widetilde H_{11}\cong\mathbb Z^{15}\oplus\mathbb Z/2\).
