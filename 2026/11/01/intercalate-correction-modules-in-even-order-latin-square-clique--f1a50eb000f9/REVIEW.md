# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The complete clique classification and all-field homology vanishing used in the proof are precisely stated by [1, Section 3.3; Theorem 3.12]. Every nonzero order-two element defines an intercalate orbit with stabilizer \(H_h\) and orientation-preserving double-transpositions. The relative long exact sequence gives the integral permutation-module injection. Independent Lefschetz counting on the line subcomplex plus rainbow triangles gives the uncorrected augmentation-tensor character. Removing the intercalate tetrahedron modules, followed by Fourier decomposition, gives the character, all multiplicities and invariants. The standalone checker reconstructed simplicial boundary matrices and validated every translation of four test groups. Finite tests supplement but do not replace the unrestricted proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature risk

The closest full primary text is Cioabă–Mim [1], Section 3.3 and Theorem 3.12. It proves the nonequivariant rank and identifies intercalates, but does not compute translation representations, a coset-module exact sequence, or invariant dimensions. The earlier published spectral paper [2] gives neither. The existing odd-order augmentation-tensor formula does not imply the even-order correction: the nonzero order-two elements generate genuinely new relative three-cells and change the invariant dimension. The nearest indexed findings concerned other graph-complex symmetry actions and did not cover these exact objects. Risk: equivalent unpublished or unindexed equivariant calculations could exist; no uniqueness assertion is based merely on unsuccessful searches.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result resolves the translation-equivariant information lost by the known ordinary Betti-number formula on a natural, infinite graph family. The integral short exact sequence identifies the intercalate corrections as modules, not just a scalar dimension decrement. Fourier multiplicities determine every translation isotypic component. The vanishing of all invariant degree-two classes for elementary abelian \(2\)-groups is a qualitative new phenomenon invisible to ordinary homology and impossible in the previously considered odd-order family.

## Scientific limitations and closest literature

The integral extension need not split integrally; only the rational representation is classified. The primary published sources provide the nonequivariant homological input, not independent validation of the new equivariant theorem. A complete exclusion of unknown parallel work is not possible.
