For every even-order finite abelian group of order at least six, the translation action on second rational clique homology of its Latin-square graph is an augmentation-tensor representation minus one coset module for each nonzero involution. The invariant homology has dimension \(n-|A[2]|\) and vanishes for every elementary abelian \(2\)-group of order at least eight, despite large nonzero homology.
