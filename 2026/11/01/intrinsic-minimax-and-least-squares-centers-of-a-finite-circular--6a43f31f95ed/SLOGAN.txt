On every finite right circular cone's lateral surface, the complete intrinsic Chebyshev-center set changes from a parallel circle to the apex at the exact shape threshold \(R/L=1/2\), while the least-squares Fréchet-center set remains a nonapical circle. These center sets coincide at exactly two explicitly characterized cone angles, and the sharp cone-family covering-radius/diameter bound is \(\rho_C/D\le\sqrt3/2\), with equality exactly at \(R/L=1/3\).
