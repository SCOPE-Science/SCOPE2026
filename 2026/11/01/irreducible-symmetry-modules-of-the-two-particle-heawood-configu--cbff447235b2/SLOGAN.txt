The unordered two-particle configuration space of the Heawood graph has rational first and second homology of dimensions \(8\) and \(14\), with full \(336\)-automorphism modules \(V_8\) and \(V_8\oplus V_6\), where \(V_8,V_6\) are distinct absolutely irreducible rational representations. Neither positive-degree homology group contains a full-symmetry invariant, so the geometric symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
