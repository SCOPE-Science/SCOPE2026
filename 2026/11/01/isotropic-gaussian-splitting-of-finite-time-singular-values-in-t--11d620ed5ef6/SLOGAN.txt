In the twisted baker map, logarithmic finite-time singular values have an isotropic rank-\(M-1\) Gaussian limit, with covariance \((\log2)^2(I_M/M-\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/M^2)\), even though individual histories are cyclically dependent. The logarithmic condition number grows on the sharp \(\sqrt n\) scale with a Gaussian-range limiting law and explicit expected-growth constant.
