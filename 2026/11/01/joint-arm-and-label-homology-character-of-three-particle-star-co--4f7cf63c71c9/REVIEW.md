# Scientific evaluation

## Correctness

**PASS.** The claimed domain is all radial stars with at least three arms, three distinct labeled particles, and the full commuting arm and particle-label groups. The known Ghrist deformation identifies degree zero and degree one as the only rational homology degrees. The fixed-locus descriptions are explicit: identity label permutation reduces to a smaller fixed-arm star; a label transposition requires a two-cycle of arms; a label three-cycle requires a three-cycle of arms. The ordinary Euler characteristics of these fixed loci are computed exactly, using a cubical cell enumerator for the identity-label case and contractible components for the other two cases. The Lefschetz identity and Burnside averages reconstruct the stated character and quotient claims. The standalone verifier agrees for all \(45\) conjugacy-class comparisons in the first three nontrivial arm sizes. The proof does not promote finite testing into infinite certification.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual priority risk.** The Ghrist 1999 and Farley–Sabalka 2004 papers determine the nonequivariant homotopy rank and radial-tree graph reduction; those are excluded from the finding. Wawrykow 2024 supplies generators, relations, and particle-label representation stability but no displayed complete arm-by-label character in the materially inspected Sections 1 and 3 or remaining accessible full text. The earlier unlabeled-star character is exactly the trivial label-isotypic projection, so that projection is not presented as new. The two other isotypic characters and the alternating-arm low-size exceptions are not implied by that one projection. The literature's general presentations could allow indirect extraction, and no definitive priority claim is made.

## Value

**PASS.** The topology of ordered graph configurations measures collision-free motion and has an established representation-stability framework. The complete simultaneous symmetry character answers a natural representation question for an infinite class, rather than a hand-selected finite instance. It exposes both nontrivial label sectors and the exceptional rational homology of alternating-arm quotients, phenomena that the known nonequivariant Betti ranks and the unlabeled projection cannot determine. The result is a complete rational representation answer for exactly three particles, not a claim of a general particle-count theorem.
