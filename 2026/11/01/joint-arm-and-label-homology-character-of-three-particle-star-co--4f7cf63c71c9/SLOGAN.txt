For every star with at least three arms, the complete joint \(\mathfrak S_m\times\mathfrak S_3\) character on first rational homology of the collision-free ordered three-point configuration space is determined solely by the counts of fixed arms, transposed arm-pairs, and arm three-cycles. The full arm quotient is rationally acyclic, while the alternating-arm quotient retains first-homology ranks \(5\), \(2\), and \(0\) for three, four, and at least five arms, respectively.
