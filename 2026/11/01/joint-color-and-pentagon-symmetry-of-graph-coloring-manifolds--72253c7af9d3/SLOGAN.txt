For every \(n\ge4\), the complete rational \(D_5\times S_n\)-homology representation of the pentagon coloring manifold \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_5,K_n)\) is a sum of at most three explicit one-dimensional parity characters. Forgetting both pentagon and color labels yields a rationally acyclic quotient for odd \(n\), but a rational homology sphere for even \(n\).
