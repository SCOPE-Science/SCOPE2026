# Self-review

## Correctness (PASS)

All parameters are fixed with \(d\ge3\), \(R,H>0\). The diameter manifold is explicitly classified, and the four smooth cap/side endpoint charts capture every sufficiently long chord. The leading deficit is a positive sum of two inward distances and an angular quadratic with uniform remainder. The invariant-line boundary-pair Jacobian is computed with its directed orientation factor and agrees with Cauchy's surface-area normalization. The simplex-beta integral yields the exponent \((d+2)/2\) and exact coefficient for each ordered endpoint-type pair. The limiting scaled density gives the Dirichlet parameters \((1,1,(d-2)/2,1)\). Reproducible exact-distance quadrature agrees with the derived constants in dimensions three to five. The finite calculations are supporting evidence, not proof of asymptotic convergence.

## Originality (PASS, with historical risk)

Full text was examined for Kellerer (1971), including the exact scalar chord-length distribution of three-dimensional cylinders; that scalar law subsumes the unmarked three-dimensional tail and is not claimed original. Vlasov's full text addresses signed chord conventions rather than the marked endpoint law. Mäder's 1980 circular-cylinder chord distribution is the closest unresolved primary work: its abstract was available but the full paper was not. In the accessible material and the actually searched result database, no equivalent four-type marked asymptotic or conditional Dirichlet limit was found. Unknown historical treatments remain a residual risk; lack of a database hit is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The previously established marginal length law loses which boundary component terminates the almost-longest chords. The joint limit recovers this information and identifies a simple aspect-ratio statistic, \(\Pr(C\mid L>D-\varepsilon)\to H^2/D^2\), plus type-independent scaled shape fluctuations. The non-smooth diameter seam and continuum of diametral pairs place this example outside both finite-vertex polytope and smooth strictly convex isolated-diameter asymptotic regimes. The result therefore describes a geometrically distinct and structurally motivated class, not a mere re-expression of an isolated constant.

## Boundaries

The marked law is not asserted for alternative sampling schemes, varying aspect ratios, degenerate cylinders or dimensions below three. The oldest exact public posting date verified among date-complete methodological references is the 2007-11-29 arXiv v1; Kellerer and Mäder's substantially older publications are fully acknowledged despite lacking independently verified first-public calendar days.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
