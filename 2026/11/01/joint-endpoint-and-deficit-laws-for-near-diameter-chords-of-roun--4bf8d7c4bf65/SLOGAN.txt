For near-diameter invariant random chords of any round cylinder in dimension \(d\ge3\), the top and bottom cap/side endpoint labels asymptotically become independent, with cap probability \(H^2/(H^2+4R^2)\), while the chord-tail exponent is \((d+2)/2\). Conditional scaled radial/axial and angular deficits share the universal \(\mathrm{Dirichlet}(1,1,(d-2)/2,1)\) law, independently of endpoint labels.
