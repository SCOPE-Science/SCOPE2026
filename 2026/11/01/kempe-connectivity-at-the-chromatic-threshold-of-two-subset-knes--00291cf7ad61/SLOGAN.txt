All optimum \((n-2)\)-colourings of \(KG(n,2)\) communicate through Kempe-chain swaps for every \(n\ge4\), even though their proper single-vertex recolouring graph breaks into \(\binom n3(n-2)!\) disjoint hypercubes. Explicit triangle-moving, star-label exchange and singleton-switch Kempe moves give a constructive all-parameter proof.
