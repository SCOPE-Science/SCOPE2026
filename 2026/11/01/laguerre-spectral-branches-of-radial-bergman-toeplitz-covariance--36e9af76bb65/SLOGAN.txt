For the matrix of multiplicativity defects of the first \(m\) boundary-distance powers on a weighted Bergman ball, the \(k\)-th singular spectral branch has the sharp decay \(s_J\sim k!(\alpha+1)_k/(d!J)^{2k/d}\). Its \(m\) distinct Schatten thresholds \(p=d/(2k)\) arise from an exact beta-covariance Gram determinant and the monic Laguerre norms.
