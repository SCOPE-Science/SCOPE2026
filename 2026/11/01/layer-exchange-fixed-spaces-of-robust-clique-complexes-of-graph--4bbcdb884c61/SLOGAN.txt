Exchanging the two layers of any graph prism turns the geometric fixed-point space of its independence-bounded robust clique complex into an explicit induced-bipartite-subset avoidance complex. For every bipartite base, the fixed space is universally a simplex skeleton, independent of the base graph's edges; on two-row ladders the three-robust fixed circles saturate Smith's mod-two total-Betti bound against ambient three-spheres.
