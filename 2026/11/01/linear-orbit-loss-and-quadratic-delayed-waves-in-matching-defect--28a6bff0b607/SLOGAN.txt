Deleting \(k\) disjoint edges from a sufficiently large complete graph loses exactly \(2k(2^{n-2}-1)\) primitive three-cycle orbits in three-color cyclic dynamics, but adds just \(6k\) synchronizing initial states. The first-entry census is exact: \(12k(k-1)\) colorings develop a two-defect interaction that delays entry into a three-cycle until the second update.
