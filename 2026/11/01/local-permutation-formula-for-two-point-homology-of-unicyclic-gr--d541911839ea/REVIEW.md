# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The finite connected simple unicyclic hypothesis guarantees an appropriate two-particle cubical model. For each edge on the unique cycle, the fixed-edge component of a square-cycle boundary is a one-cycle on a forest, so it vanishes. All remaining square coefficients are supported on the off-cycle forest and vanish by the same argument; thus the second boundary is injective over the integers. Cellular classes in rational automorphism representations are assembled with correct orientation signs: distinct unordered vertex pairs, oriented edge–vertex pairs, and antisymmetrically oriented edge pairs. The graph chain exact sequence and elementary symmetric/exterior-square identities yield precisely the cycle orientation line plus local exterior squares of augmentation modules. Finite-group rational semisimplicity upgrades the representation-ring identity to an isomorphism. The exact finite verifier confirmed all sixty-six automorphism traces in eight graphs, with independently reconstructed cells and injective mod-two second boundaries.

## Originality — PASS, subject to the specified risk

The earliest motivating source is Barnett–Farber, arXiv:0903.2180v1 (March 12, 2009), which studies two-point graph configuration homology and cohomology and does not supply this full unicyclic graph-automorphism character. Ko–Park, arXiv:1101.2648v1, especially Theorem 3.16, determines underlying first-homology ranks; that rank is already covered and is not the new claim. Lütgehetmann, arXiv:1701.03490, studies representation stability of ordered configurations under repeated graph attachment, not an explicit character for the full automorphism group of each unicyclic graph. Related work on collision-allowing or Borel–Moore configurations has different domain hypotheses. The full generality and local fixed-halfedge formula were not found in the available literature or mathematical records. This is a best-knowledge comparison, not a proof that no unindexed equivalent exists.

## Value — PASS

For every finite connected unicyclic graph, including arbitrarily complicated attached trees and automorphisms that permute branches, a complete rational representation is computed using only local halfedge permutations and the orientation of one cycle. This resolves all character values without constructing the usually much larger two-point configuration complex; it strictly refines the established rank formula. It also makes symmetry-fixed and quotient rational homology dimensions calculable from local group actions. The invariant is a natural functorial topological quantity, and the conclusion applies to an infinite class rather than an arbitrarily selected finite sample.

## Scope and residual risks

An integral representation-lattice decomposition is not proved. Higher-particle configurations, multiple independent graph cycles, fundamental-group actions, and Borel-equivariant homology are outside the result. A broader unindexed equivariant formula could imply the theorem; that publication-priority possibility remains unresolved and requires independent review.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
