For two whole-plane anisotropic Voronoi cells with site-assigned positive-definite metrics, both cells are star-shaped at their own sites if and only if the matrices are equal. For incomparable metrics, almost every relative site direction creates exactly one disconnected orphan component, and the exact probability of which cell owns it is an arctangent of the eigenvalue ratio of \((B^{-1}-A^{-1})^{-1}\).
