The Villarceau transition of a solid ring torus creates an explicit two-sided \((\beta-\beta_*)\log|\beta-\beta_*|\) singularity in central-section area, with coefficient \(4(R^2-r^2)\). Under Haar-random central planes, this forces the section-area density to vanish logarithmically at a computable interior area, while annular and two-component sections have exact aspect-ratio-dependent probabilities.
