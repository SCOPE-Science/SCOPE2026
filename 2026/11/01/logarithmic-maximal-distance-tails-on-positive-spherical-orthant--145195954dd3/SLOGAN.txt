For two independent area-uniform directions in the positive spherical orthant of \(\mathbb R^d\), the probability that their intrinsic distance is within \(\varepsilon\) of the diameter \(\pi/2\) is asymptotic to \(2^{2d-2}\Gamma(d/2)^2\varepsilon^d[\log(1/\varepsilon)]^{d-2}/(\pi^d(d-2)!)\). The logarithmic exponent \(d-2\) comes from products of transverse coordinate pairs near two different axis vertices, and the coefficient is exact in every fixed dimension \(d\ge2\).
