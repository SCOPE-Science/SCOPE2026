For recruitment-to-mortality ratio \(\rho\to\infty\), logarithmically growing mortality \(\mu d^2/D=x\log\rho\) yields a Gumbel law for the reciprocal first-arrival time and an explicit second-order mean expansion. Within the fixed-coefficient logarithmic scaling class, the unique asymptotic mean-minimizing coefficient is \(x=4\); no unrestricted finite-ratio optimality is asserted.
