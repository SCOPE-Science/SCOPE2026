For every fixed odd \(n\ge3\), the probability that two area-uniform points of the width-one regular Reuleaux \(n\)-gon are within \(\varepsilon\) of its diameter is asymptotic to \(\frac{n}{3A_n^2}\varepsilon^3\log(1/\varepsilon)\), where \(A_n\) is its area. The sharp logarithmic cusp distinguishes these curved-corner bodies from the disk's \(\varepsilon^{5/2}\) endpoint law and reveals the number of corners.
