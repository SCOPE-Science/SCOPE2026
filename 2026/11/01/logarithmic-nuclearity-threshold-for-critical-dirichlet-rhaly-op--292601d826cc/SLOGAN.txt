For logarithmically damped Rhaly averaging on the classical Dirichlet Hilbert space, compactness begins at exponent \(b>1\), but trace-class membership begins only at \(b>2\); between these thresholds, finite-section nuclear norms diverge with an exact power of \(\log N\) or, at the endpoint, \(\log\log N\). Every positive Schatten exponent above one is allowed once compactness begins, while no exponent below one is ever attained.
