For the geometric random-walk polynomial family of Kahler and Obermaier, the spectral points approach the two edge points at a sharp logarithmic counting rate \(1/(2\log q)\). The sum of all squared edge gaps is exactly \((q^2+q-1)/(q(q^2-1))\), and their complementary product is the Euler product \(\prod_{n\geq1}(1-q^{-n})\), for every real \(q\geq5\).
