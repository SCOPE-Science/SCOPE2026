At Hamming scale two, a cyclic permutation of at least four binary-cube coordinates has a geometric Rips fixed space which simplicially collapses to **two disjoint copies** of the Rips complex on the untouched coordinates. When at least three coordinates remain untouched, both components carry a wedge of \(c_f=\sum_{j=0}^{f-3}2^j\binom{j+3}{3}\) three-spheres; the sharp three-cycle boundary can instead give a circle.
