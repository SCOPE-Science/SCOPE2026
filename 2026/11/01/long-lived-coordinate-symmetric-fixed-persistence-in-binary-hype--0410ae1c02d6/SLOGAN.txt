The full coordinate-symmetric geometric fixed locus of the binary hypercube's Hamming Rips complex at scale \(r<n\) is a single sphere \(S^{\lfloor\lfloor r/2\rfloor/(n-r)\rfloor}\). Its exact reduced integral barcode contains the interval \([\lceil2qn/(2q+1)\rceil,\lceil2(q+1)n/(2q+3)\rceil)\) in degree \(q\), when nonempty; positive-degree fixed-space bars can grow arbitrarily long even though successive ambient Rips inclusions are nullhomotopic.
