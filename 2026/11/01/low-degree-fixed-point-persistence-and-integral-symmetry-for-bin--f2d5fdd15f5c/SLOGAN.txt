For every binary-translation subgroup whose nonzero vectors have weight at most two, the geometric fixed-point Vietoris–Rips filtration either reduces at all scales to a shifted lower-dimensional hypercube filtration or has a sharp exceptional two-coordinate-flip transition from a cube graph to simply connected spaces. The complete reduced degree-zero and degree-one barcodes follow, and the exceptional graph carries an explicitly determined integral residual-symmetry cycle lattice and rational character.
