In a directed ring of inhibitory clipped logistic maps, fixed points are exactly the independent-set patterns of the cycle together with one positive homogeneous state: their number is the Lucas number \(L_n+1\). Yet exactly two checkerboards attract locally when the ring size is even, whereas on odd rings only the positive state can attract, and only within a sharp inhibition window of width \(\sec(\pi/n)-1\sim\pi^2/(2n^2)\).
