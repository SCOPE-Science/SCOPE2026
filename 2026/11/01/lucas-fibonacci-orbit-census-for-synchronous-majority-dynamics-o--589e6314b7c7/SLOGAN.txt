On every open two-row ladder, synchronous tie-retaining binary majority has exactly \(2(f_{n+1}+1)\) fixed states and \(\ell_{2n}+1+3\mathbf1_{3\mid n}\) initially periodic states, where \(f\) and \(\ell\) are the Fibonacci and Lucas sequences. Their difference gives the primitive two-cycle count, with exponential growth \(((3+\sqrt5)/2)^n/2\) and a genuine modulo-three resonance in the exact finite census.
