For every prime \(p\), the maximal-cyclic coset-sum transform on \(C_{p^2}\times C_p\) is injective with exactly \(2p\) indispensable directions and a two-level nonconstant squared-singular-value spectrum \(p,p^2\). Its sharp mean-zero condition number is \(\sqrt p\), and no positive direction-wise scalar weighting can improve it.
