For any cyclic planar support directions with angular gaps below \(\pi\), the labeled convex-vertex parameter cone has exactly \(2n\) facets and its extreme rays modulo translation are precisely doubled-incidence antipodal pairs and origin-containing triples. For regular \(n\)-gonal directions their number is \(n(n^2-1)/3\) when \(n\) is odd and \(n(n-2)(n-4)/3+2n\) when \(n\) is even.
