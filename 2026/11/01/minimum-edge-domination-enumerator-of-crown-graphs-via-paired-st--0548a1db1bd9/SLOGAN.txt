Every inclusion-minimal edge dominating set of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\) is exactly a restricted fixed-point-free functional edge set, a perfect matching, or a star forest after omitting one matched vertex pair. This classification gives the complete cardinality polynomial and, for \(n\ge5\), exactly \(n(n-1)\) largest minimal sets of size \(2n-4\), while the \(nD_{n-1}\) smallest ones have size \(n-1\).
