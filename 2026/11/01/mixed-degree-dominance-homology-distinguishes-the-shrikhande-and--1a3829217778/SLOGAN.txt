The sixteen-vertex Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs share the strongly regular parameters \((16,6,2,2)\), but their dominance complexes have different torsion-free integral homology: the first has \(\widetilde H_9\cong\mathbb Z^{11}\) only, while the second has \(\widetilde H_9\cong\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_{11}\cong\mathbb Z^2\). Their dominance face counts nevertheless agree through dimension ten.
