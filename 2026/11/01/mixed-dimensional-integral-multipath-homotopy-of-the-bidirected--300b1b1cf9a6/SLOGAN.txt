The multipath complex of the fully bidirected balanced graph \(\overleftrightarrow{K_{3,3}}\) has exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{19}S^3\vee\bigvee^{12}S^4\). A complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching and primitive integral attaching maps establish the two-dimensional sphere-wedge pattern, beyond the already understood top Hamiltonian-cycle classes.
