The closed-neighborhood (radius-one Čech) complex of the self-complementary Johnson graph \(J(6,3)\) has exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{11}S^3\vee\bigvee^{30}S^4\), despite being pure of dimension \(9\). An exhaustive acyclic Morse matching and a primitive integer attaching matrix establish both sphere dimensions and the multiplicities.
