The multipath complex of the six-vertex bidirected complete bipartite graph \(\overleftrightarrow{K}_{3,3}\) has exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{19}S^3\vee\bigvee^{12}S^4\). A complete finite acyclic matching and zero integer Morse differential certify the mixed-dimensional wedge, beyond its Betti numbers.
