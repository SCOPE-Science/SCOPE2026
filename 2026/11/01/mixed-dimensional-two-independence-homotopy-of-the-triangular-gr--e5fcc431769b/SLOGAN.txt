For the triangular graphs \(T_n=L(K_n)\) on five, six and seven underlying points, the two-independence complexes have integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^6S^1\), \(\bigvee^{24}S^2\vee\bigvee^{10}S^3\), and \(\bigvee^{400}S^3\), respectively. The middle mixed-dimensional wedge is certified by a complete acyclic matching and vanishing integral attaching degrees; the next case is resolved by a unimodular \(8\times8\) attaching minor.
