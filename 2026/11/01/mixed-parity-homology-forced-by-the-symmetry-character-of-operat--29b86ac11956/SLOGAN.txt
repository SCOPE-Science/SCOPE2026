The complete \(\mathfrak S_5\times\mathfrak S_4\) virtual rational homology character of the Petersen graph's four-color Hom complex has coefficients \(+1\) on \(S^{(3,2)}\boxtimes\mathbf1\) and \(-1\) on \(S^{(2,2,1)}\boxtimes\operatorname{sgn}_4\). Thus its positive-degree rational homology necessarily occurs in both even and odd degrees, although the individual homology groups are not classified.
