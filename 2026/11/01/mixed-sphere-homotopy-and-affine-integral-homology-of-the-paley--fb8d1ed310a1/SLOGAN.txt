The pure three-dimensional two-independence complex of the thirteen-vertex Paley graph has mixed integral homotopy type \(S^2\vee S^3\vee S^3\), so it is not shellable. Its full affine symmetry acts on \(\widetilde H_2\) by the order-two sign character and on \(\widetilde H_3\) through the integral Eisenstein order-three representation, leaving no positive-dimensional rational homology in the quotient.
