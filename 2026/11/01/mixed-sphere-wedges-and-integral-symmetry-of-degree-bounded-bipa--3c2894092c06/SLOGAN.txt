The simplicial complex of vertex subsets of \(K_{m,n}\) inducing maximum degree at most \(r\ge2\) is an explicit mixed-dimensional sphere wedge, with homology only in degrees \(r\) and \(2r-1\). Its full integral part-permutation homology modules are exterior powers of augmentation lattices, and exchanging balanced parts acts on the top tensor product with the parity sign \((-1)^r\).
