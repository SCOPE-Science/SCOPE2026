For the eight-point complex of pairwise-intersecting triples, the natural \(\mathfrak S_8\)-action on \(233\)-dimensional sixth rational homology is multiplicity-free with exactly nine irreducible Specht constituents, including one trivial and one alternating-sign module. Its \(28\)-dimensional ninth homology is the signed permutation representation on the six-point subsets, splitting into two hook Specht modules.
