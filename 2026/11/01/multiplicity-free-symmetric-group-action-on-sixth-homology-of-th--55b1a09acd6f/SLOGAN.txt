For the pairwise-intersecting-triple complex on seven ground points, the natural \(\mathfrak S_7\)-action on \(\widetilde H_6(-;\mathbb Q)\) splits multiplicity-freely as \(S^{(7)}\oplus S^{(4,3)}\oplus S^{(2,2,2,1)}\). Its 29 dimensions therefore contain exactly one globally invariant rational homology direction.
