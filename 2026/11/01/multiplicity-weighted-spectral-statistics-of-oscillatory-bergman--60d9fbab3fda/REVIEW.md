# Mathematical review

## Correctness (C): PASS

For fixed d, Looi proves the exact two degree-eigenvalue asymptotics. Their o(1) errors vanish under normalized multiplicity summation; weighted Weyl via Euler integral comparison and Abel summation proves the Fock arcsine limit; a truncated weighted Riemann sum gives all Bergman log-phase limits. The wrapped Exp(d) angular density yields sharp arc extrema, and the Euler-product gamma tail proves the stated d-asymptotics. Boundary zero sets have zero mass, justifying passage to negative-eigenvalue proportions. Numerical checks are corroborative only.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS

The fraction of negative eigenvalues is a natural quantitative index of the severity of failure of essential positivity. The full eigenvalue distribution, weighted by intrinsic monomial multiplicity, is a stronger invariant than existence of negative essential spectrum. The contrasting universal Fock law and extremely intermittent Bergman negative fractions, whose extreme values tend to 0 and 1 with sharp dimension rates, give a motivated exact classification for explicit actively studied counterexamples.
