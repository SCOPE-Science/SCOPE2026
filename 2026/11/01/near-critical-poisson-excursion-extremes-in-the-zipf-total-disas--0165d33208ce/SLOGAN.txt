For the Zipf total-disaster chain with \(\beta_n=1+c/\log n\), excursion peaks over a horizon of \(n\) steps converge, at scale \(n/\log n\), to a space-time Poisson process with explicit intensity \(\lambda(c)\,dt\,z^{-2}dz\), where \(\lambda(c)=c/(e^c-1)\) (and \(\lambda(0)=1\)). The path maximum has the corresponding Fréchet limit, smoothly crossing the null/positive recurrence threshold.
