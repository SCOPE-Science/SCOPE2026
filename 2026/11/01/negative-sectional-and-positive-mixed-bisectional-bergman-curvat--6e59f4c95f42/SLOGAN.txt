For every higher-dimensional intersection of disk cylinders \(\{|z_1|^2+|z_j|^{2p_j}<1\}\), the Bergman metric has negative holomorphic sectional curvature in every tangent direction along its central analytic spine, yet positive bisectional curvature between any two distinct transverse coordinate lines. This holds even for the convex quadratic intersections, where every such normalized positive bisectional curvature is exactly \(1/(n+1)^2\).
