# Verification and limits

The theorem's algebraic inputs are the monomial beta-integral norm and the first two coefficient degrees of the resulting reproducing kernel. The curvature convention and the factor of four for repeated indices are explicitly specified in `RESULT.md`. After deriving the exact coordinate tensor, a weighted Cauchy–Schwarz estimate proves strict negative holomorphic sectional curvature for all nonzero complex tangent vectors. Disk automorphisms with holomorphic fractional-power factors establish the extension along the spine.

Finite tests are supplementary and do not prove the quantified infinite family. The source material reviewed includes the curvature statement on the symmetrized bidisc, the exact kernel for intersected cylinders, and the predecessor ellipsoid-kernel construction.
