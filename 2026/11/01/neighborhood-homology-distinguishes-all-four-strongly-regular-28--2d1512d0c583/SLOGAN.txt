The Lovász neighborhood complexes of the four cospectral strongly regular graphs with parameters \((28,12,6,4)\)—the triangular graph \(T_8\) and the three Chang graphs—have four different complete reduced mod-two Betti signatures. Exact exhaustive boundary calculations show that these neighborhood complexes are pairwise non-homotopy-equivalent, even though the underlying graphs have identical strongly regular parameters and adjacency spectra.
