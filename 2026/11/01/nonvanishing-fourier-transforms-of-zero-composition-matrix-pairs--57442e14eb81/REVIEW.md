# Review of the zero-composition Fourier theorem

## Correctness — PASS

The statement specifies a finite field, \(a\ge b\ge2\), exact matrix-map directions, a nontrivial additive character, and unnormalized Fourier coefficients. Orthogonality over the space \(\operatorname{Hom}(W,\ker X)\) is valid because the trace pairing is nondegenerate. Exact-kernel Möbius inversion on the subspace lattice gives a complete Gaussian product for each summand; the prime-power integer \(q\) divides all nonzero-kernel terms one further time than the injective-map term. The injective-map term is a unit modulo \(q\) after dividing by \(q^{\binom b2}\), proving strict nonvanishing. Surjective \(A\) and injective \(B\) attain the sharp smallest multiplier. The embedded checker independently evaluates all characters in five finite configurations, including an extension field; it does not substitute for the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS under bounded literature review

Gadouleau–Yan (arXiv:0706.1751, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1) transform rank distributions of one matrix, not the coupled zero-composition subset. Ram (arXiv:1509.08053, Definition 2.3) defines *zero kernel pairs* by an intersection of kernels of powers, which is different from \(XY=0\). Britnell–Wildon (arXiv:1001.0811) and Browning–Sawin–Wang (arXiv:2409.01920, section 3) address commutation or commutators. Searches of published research records for zero-product, matrix composition, incidence Fourier support, equivalent formulations, and stronger rank-metric implications identified no direct covering statement. The accessible local historical claims include a scalar zero-product convolution result over chain rings, but it does not imply the higher-dimensional all-frequency nonvanishing theorem. An unlocated general bilinear-incidence transform identity or association-scheme specialization remains a residual priority risk; failed search alone is not novelty proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This classifies the Fourier support of a canonical singular matrix-composition incidence variety simultaneously in all stable rectangular dimensions and every field characteristic. Despite many nilpotent and singular pairs, the convolution operator is invertible with an exact universal divisibility congruence and sharp minimum singular value. The nonvanishing is global (all \(q^{2ab}\) frequency points), is not a finite table, and is not implied by the ordinary cardinality of the variety. The exact kernel-stratified formula supports further spectral study.

## Limits and nearby literature

The result excludes the opposite rectangular range and finite nonfield rings. The general formula does not count each distinct eigenvalue multiplicity. The standard Gaussian-binomial and rank-metric technology is prior art. The connection to prior scalar zero-product results is an analogy, not a claimed independent discovery of that scalar case. Broader historical identities could subsume the theorem. Independent validation was not conducted.
