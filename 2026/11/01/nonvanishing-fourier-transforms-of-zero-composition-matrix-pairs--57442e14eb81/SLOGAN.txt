For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) and integers \(a\ge b\ge2\), the indicator of composable matrix pairs satisfying \(XY=0\) has **no vanishing additive Fourier coefficient**: all multipliers obey \(F\equiv(-1)^bq^{\binom b2}\pmod{q^{\binom b2+1}}\). Consequently its convolution operator is invertible with sharp smallest singular value \(q^{\binom b2}\), in every characteristic.
