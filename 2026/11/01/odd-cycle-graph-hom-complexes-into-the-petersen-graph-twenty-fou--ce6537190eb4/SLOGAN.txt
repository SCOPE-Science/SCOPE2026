For the Petersen graph \(P\), the first three odd-cycle graph-Hom spaces have homotopy types \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_5,P)\simeq120\,\mathrm{pt}\), \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_7,P)\simeq\coprod^{24}S^1\), and \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_9,P)\simeq(\coprod^{24}S^1)\sqcup360\,\mathrm{pt}\). In both circle-bearing cases their integral first homology is the induced \(\mathfrak S_5\)-module \(\operatorname{Ind}_{C_5}^{\mathfrak S_5}\mathbb Z\), while the isolated nine-cycle maps are exactly the 360 oriented based nine-cycle traversals.
