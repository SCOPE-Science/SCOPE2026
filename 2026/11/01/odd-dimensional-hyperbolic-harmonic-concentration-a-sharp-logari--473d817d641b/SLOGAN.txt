In every odd real dimension \(n\ge3\), compact-subball concentration of weighted hyperbolic-harmonic functions has \(\frac{2(n-2)}{(n-2)![-\log T]^{n-1}}[\log(1/\varepsilon)]^{n-2}\log\log(1/\varepsilon)\) more eigenvalues above \(\varepsilon\) than the corresponding Euclidean-harmonic compression, up to a lower-order term. The coefficient is independent of the Bergman weight exponent; the proof controls nonterminating hypergeometric radial factors through a beta-to-gamma boundary layer.
