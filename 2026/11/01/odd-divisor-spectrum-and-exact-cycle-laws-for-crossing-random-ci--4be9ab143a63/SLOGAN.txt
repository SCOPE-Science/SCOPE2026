For endpoint-uniform random chords of a circle, the crossing integral operator has exactly the signed nonzero eigenvalues \(\pm 4/(\pi^2d)\) for odd positive integers \(d\), with divisor-count multiplicities and a square-sensitive negative branch. Consequently every probability of a labeled cycle of pairwise chord crossings has an explicit zeta-function formula and is rational, including \(1/15\), \(1/35\) and \(31/2835\) for cycles of lengths three, four and five.
