For the first two odd Hermite modes of the one-dimensional \(L_t^8L_x^4\) Strichartz inequality, the complete sharp deficit is an explicit phase-independent quartic polynomial, giving an exact fourth-order stability coefficient. Adding a carefully chosen \(h_5\) component uniquely cancels that quartic loss, but leaves a rigorously computed positive sextic deficit \(525/4096\) along the resulting three-mode curve; no unrestricted odd-data optimality is asserted.
