For Haar-random affine sections of high-dimensional regular simplices, a fixed nonzero standardized offset produces rank-one Gaussian face-vector fluctuations with a sharp studentization-corrected variance. At physical centroid displacement of order \(m^{-1}\), every fixed face-rank deficit instead converges to a common **noncentral chi-square** factor, revealing a precise Gaussian-to-chi-square crossover.
