In the power-normalized Lorentz sequence quasi-norm with \(1<p<\infty\) and \(0<q<1\), every single-block averaging projection is contractive, yet two equal-sized blocks expand some binary vectors. Their exact asymptotic binary amplification is \(R_{p,q}>1\), with a sharp exponentially small noncontractivity excess as \(q\uparrow1\); no formula for the full unrestricted two-block norm is claimed.
