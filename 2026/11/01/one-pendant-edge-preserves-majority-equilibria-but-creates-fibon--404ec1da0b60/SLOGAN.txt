For synchronous binary neighbor-majority on a labelled cycle with exactly one pendant leaf, the pendant edge leaves every equilibrium count unchanged but adds exactly \(F_{n-2}-q_{n-3}\) primitive temporal two-cycles, with \(q\) six-periodic. The new oscillations arise from alternating motion of the leaf and attachment vertex against a fixed run-constrained cycle word, yielding Fibonacci-rate growth even on odd cycles that originally had no two-cycles.
