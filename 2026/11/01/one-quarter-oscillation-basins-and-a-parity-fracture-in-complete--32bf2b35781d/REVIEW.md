# Scientific assessment of the narrowed basin theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The vertex rules partition the labelled initial states by their eventual outcome. The balanced total is entirely oscillatory, and bit complementation exchanges the two other oscillation regions. Their combined mass is the binomial square below the midpoint less its diagonal. Vandermonde, binomial symmetry, and Stirling give the finite and asymptotic formulas. The argument applies even to sizes one and two and explicitly distinguishes labelled oscillations from constant occupancy counts. Finite verification is corroboration only.

**Originality — PASS for the narrowed theorem.** The indexed complete-split classifications 517ed2dd3465 and 46996c042071 already cover the recurrent-state census and the two-round upper bound in the balanced case. The update dynamics used here also overlap their proofs. The distinct contribution is the closed enumeration of the whole two-cycle attraction basin and, from the weighted population sum, both parity corrections. The inspected triangular-book and crown results concern different graphs and do not establish these formulas. Unlocated equivalent work remains possible; the full Goles–Olivos article was not inspected.

**Value — PASS.** The basin count measures a different property from recurrent-state multiplicity: despite the known exponential disparity in the number of cycles, both parity classes have the same nonzero basin limit. The signed leading corrections quantify the finite-size difference. The result is restricted to the stated balanced graph and update rule.
