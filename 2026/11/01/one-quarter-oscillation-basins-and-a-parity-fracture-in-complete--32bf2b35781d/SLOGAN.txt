On the balanced complete split graph, the exact synchronous majority oscillation basin has size \(\binom{2n}{n}+A_n^2-Q_n\), where \(A_n\) sums the binomial coefficients below the midpoint and \(Q_n\) sums their squares. Under unbiased initial opinions the attraction probability tends to one quarter, from above for odd sizes and from below for sufficiently large even sizes.
