On the balanced complete split graph \(K_n\vee\overline K_n\), synchronous strict majority has only one primitive oscillatory orbit for every odd \(n\), but \(1+\tfrac12\binom{n}{n/2}^2\) for even \(n\). Nonetheless, under uniform random initial opinions the attraction probability tends to exactly \(1/4\) along both parities, with opposite \(n^{-1/2}\) corrections, and every state is periodic by its second update.
