For every connected finite graph without triangles or quadrilaterals and with minimum degree at least three, the Lovász open-neighborhood complex of its line graph is a wedge of \(m+b\) circles and one sphere \(S^{\deg(v)-2}\) per original vertex, where \(m\) is the edge count and \(b\) the cycle rank. Its higher integral homology is a natural signed permutation module on the vertex-stars, and projective-plane incidence graphs give exact mixed-dimensional sphere bouquets.
