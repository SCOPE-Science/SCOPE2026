# Reproducibility and scientific limits
Run `python3 verify.py` from the directory containing the standalone program. The program uses only the Python standard library and constructs finite projective planes of orders two and three, the Petersen graph, and two Petersen graphs joined by a bridge. For each example it checks the required absence of triangles and quadrilaterals, minimum degree three, exact open-neighborhood intersections of line-graph edge vertices, all simplices generated by the actual open-neighborhood facets, and direct mod-two boundary ranks.

The accompanying `verification_output.txt` is the recorded complete stdout of the successful command and ends with `VERIFY_OK`. In the order-three projective-plane example the face counts from dimension zero through five are `(52, 624, 1040, 780, 312, 52)` and the mod-two boundary ranks, with the zero initial and terminal values, are `(0, 51, 494, 520, 260, 52, 0)`. The corresponding reduced mod-two Betti numbers are 79 in degree one and 26 in degree two. The mixed-degree bridge example has first and second Betti numbers 61 and 2. These data check nontrivial higher-dimensional and mixed-degree cases.

The program does not compute integral Smith normal forms, all graph automorphisms, or an exhaustive list of graphs; none of these finite checks is an all-graph proof. The integral wedge homotopy type and canonical higher-degree orientation action are established in `RESULT.md` by explicit subcomplex, intersection and homotopy-pushout arguments. No independent mathematical audit is claimed.
