# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The NOR state map is the antitone open-neighborhood polarity \(A\mapsto V\setminus N(A)\), so adjacency symmetry implies \(F^3=F\) exactly. Partitioning a join's states by whether their support meets neither, one, or both factors gives a complete classification of possible periodic states. The unique factor orbit containing the empty state exists precisely when that factor has no isolated vertices, accounting for both correction terms. Fixed points correspond exactly to maximal independent subsets; counting total dominating sets supplies the universal two-cycle's entire basin. All hypotheses and exceptional cases are explicit. Direct bit-mask enumeration passed every state of 5,625 graph pairs of factor orders 1–4, plus 120 additional deterministic-seed pairs with factor orders up to 7. These computations check the derivation but do not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The 2008 conjunctive/disjunctive network result uses positive Boolean updates; the NOR update here is antitone and no conjugacy from that result yields the join claim. The 2011 AND-NOT article treats fixed-point correspondences, not an established join law for primitive two-cycles and their basin counts in the accessible material. The 2017 and 2020 published maxterm/minterm analyses explicitly use a vertex's own state as an input, whereas the present update excludes it; their no-coexistence conclusions therefore do not cover this map. The 2023 Rule 5 formula covers a one-dimensional special graph but no arbitrary joins. Searches for graph joins, NOR oscillations, total domination basins, and equivalent formulations located no prior statement or stronger theorem covering all three formulas. A full version of the 2011 fixed-point paper could not be inspected, and historical priority cannot be conclusively ruled out.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Joining arbitrary graphs is a fundamental graph operation, and complete oscillation-count and basin laws are natural invariants of a nonlinear graph dynamical system. The formula singles out an exact zero-versus-positive isolate transition in the number of primitive oscillations, while clique coning can preserve every oscillation even as all but a linearly growing number of initial states fall into one universal two-cycle. This is a structural family-wide result, not a renamed finite enumeration or a minor numerical improvement. It also distinguishes recurrent-orbit abundance from dynamical accessibility.

**Scientific limits.** These results require simple undirected loopless graphs, nonempty join factors, neighbor-only synchronous NOR, and binary states. No independent audit has been performed. One relevant historical article was accessible only in abstract form; unindexed equivalents remain a possibility.
