Among all positive-weight samples of a Gaussian-Fock polynomial of degree at most \(n\) at exactly \(n+1\) points on one centered circle, the unique best-conditioned geometry is the equally weighted regular polygon at \(\pi R^2=(n!)^{1/n}\). Even this optimal configuration has sharp exponential condition growth \(e^{n/(2e)}\) times an explicit power of \(n\).
