On a normalized path, an endpoint's consecutive heat observations have a unique D-optimal uniform time spacing for every spectral dimension \(m\ge3\), while two modes have no finite optimum. As \(m,N\to\infty\) with \(m/N\to\theta\), the optimally scaled spacing converges to a uniquely characterized constant \(s_\theta\); the low-bandwidth law is \(\tau_{N,m}\sim0.709523189686346\,((N-1)/(m-1))^2\).
