For the shortest-path metric of \(K_{m,2,\ldots,2}\) with \(t\) two-vertex parts, the exact Hilbert distortion is \(\sqrt{3-2/m}\), independent of \(t\), and every optimal embedding is exactly an orthogonal regular simplex plus a spherical line code of coherence at most \((m-2)/(2m)\). Its least optimal affine dimension grows linearly in \(t\) when \(m=2\) but logarithmically for every fixed \(m\ge3\).
