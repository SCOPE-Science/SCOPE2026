For a unit-circumradius regular hexagon rotated through \(\theta\) against the square lattice, the exact fixed-orientation translative covering radius is the maximum of two explicit trigonometric linear forms. The smallest radius is \(\sqrt{(11+6\sqrt3)/39}\), attained precisely at \(\theta\equiv\pm\arctan((4-\sqrt3)/13)\pmod{\pi/6}\), whereas the previously known worst-orientation radius is \(1/(3-\sqrt3)\).
