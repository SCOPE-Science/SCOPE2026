For distinct monomial degrees, the Toeplitz mixed commutator on every standard weighted harmonic Bergman disk has doubly paired nonzero singular values, an explicit zero block, and sharp decay \(s_j\sim4(\alpha+1)mn/j^2\). The exact decay supplies the universal Schatten cutoff \(p>1/2\) and the critical logarithmic coefficient \(2\sqrt{(\alpha+1)mn}\), beyond qualitative noncommutation.
