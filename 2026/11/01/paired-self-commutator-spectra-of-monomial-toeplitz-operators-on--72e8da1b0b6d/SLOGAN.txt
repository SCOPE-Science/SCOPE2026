The self-commutator of harmonic Bergman Toeplitz multiplication by \(z^m\) has an exactly paired positive-negative eigenvalue spectrum for every beta weight \(\alpha>-1\). Its singular values satisfy \(s_n\sim4m^2(\alpha+1)n^{-2}\), giving the sharp Schatten threshold \(p>1/2\), a finite formula for its trace norm, and an exact unweighted operator norm.
