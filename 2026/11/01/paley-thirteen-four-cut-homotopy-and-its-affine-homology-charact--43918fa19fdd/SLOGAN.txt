The ordinary four-cut complex of the 13-vertex Paley graph is a pure eight-dimensional non-shellable complex with integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{25}S^6\). Its natural order-78 affine symmetry acts on \(\widetilde H_6(-;\mathbb Q)\) as one trivial module plus two copies of the twelve-dimensional vertex augmentation module; the quotient retains one rational sixth-homology class.
