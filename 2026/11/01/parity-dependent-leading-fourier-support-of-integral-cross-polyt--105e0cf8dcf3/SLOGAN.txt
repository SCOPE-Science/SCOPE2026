Integer-frequency Fourier coefficients of the unit \(d\)-dimensional cross-polytope exhibit a sharp parity split: their nonzero-frequency count in \([-N,N]^d\) grows as \(2^d\binom d2 N^{d-1}\) for odd \(d\), but only \(2^d\binom d3 N^{d-2}\) for even \(d\), up to lower-order terms. Exact residue formulas identify the respective double-collision and triple-collision mechanisms.
