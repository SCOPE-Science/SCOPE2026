The simplicial double of the boundary of the cyclic four-polytope \(C(n,4)\), for every \(n\ge5\), is connected exactly when \(n\) is odd. For even \(n\), it splits into two circle bouquets of ranks \(n(n-4)/4+1\) and \(3n(n-4)/4+1\), giving an infinite parity obstruction to stronger connectedness bounds for doubles of three-spheres.
