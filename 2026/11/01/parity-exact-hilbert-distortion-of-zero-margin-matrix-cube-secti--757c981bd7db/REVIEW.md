# Scientific review: parity-exact Hilbert distortion of zero-margin matrix cube sections

## Correctness
PASS. The unrestricted Euclidean comparison problem is reduced to the Frobenius quadratic form by an explicit averaging argument under independent row and column permutations; the four pairwise coincidence classes show invariance implies a scalar form on the zero-margin subspace. The minimum square ratio is derived from the exact squared norm of projected coordinate functionals. The maximum square ratio follows from sharp parity-sensitive zero-sum cube bounds on each row and column, with explicit checkerboard, zero-row/column, and circulant-plus-opposite-column witnesses. The standalone checker reproduces rational projection values and witnesses for 576 pairs of dimensions, and performs one independent ternary enumeration. The finite checks are corroboration only; the analytic argument handles all real matrices and all sizes.

## Originality
PASS on the inspected evidence, with bibliographic risk. Actual searches compared two-margin Banach--Mazur distance, tensor-product zero-sum hyperplanes, and row/column centered cube sections. The closest full published finding on cycle transportation spaces analyzes a **one-index** zero-sum \(\ell_\infty\) hyperplane, including parity; it covers a degenerate one-factor instance but supplies no implication giving the simultaneous two-margin classification. The complete-bipartite transportation result has dimension equal to the sum of the side lengths minus one and concerns a different Lipschitz-free norm, not the cycle/interaction subspace. Passer's general estimate and Hinrichs--Schneider's anchored/ANOVA interpolation provide context but no exact formula. A classical tensorial symmetry theorem not located in these searches could in principle subsume the result.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This is a complete natural two-parameter classification of the optimal Hilbert distortion of the pure interaction component in discrete factorial ANOVA and of the zero-circulation-flux (cycle) subspace of a complete bipartite graph under an entrywise capacity norm. The sharp even-even extremality and odd-side strict losses quantify a structural parity obstruction to balancing all entries at capacity; the Frobenius optimality controls **all** Euclidean coordinate systems, not just the obvious one.

## Limitations
The theorem is restricted to real arrays, uniform maximum norm, and full rectangular grids. No complex, weighted, or sparse-graph extension is asserted; exhaustive bibliographic novelty is not certified.
