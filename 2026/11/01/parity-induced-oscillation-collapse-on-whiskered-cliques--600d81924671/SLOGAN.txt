On the fully whiskered clique \(K_n\circ K_1\), synchronous binary majority dynamics becomes periodic after at most three updates, but the exact two-cycle basins undergo a strong parity transition. Under uniform initialization, oscillation has probability asymptotic to \(2/(\pi n)\) for even \(n\) and \(\sqrt{n/\pi}\,2^{-n/2}\) for odd \(n\).
