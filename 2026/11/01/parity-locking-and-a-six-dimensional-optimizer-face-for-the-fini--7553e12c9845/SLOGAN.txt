For the twelve-root neighborhood of type \(A_3\) on a finite three-dimensional torus, the sharp positive-definite Turán mass is exactly \(4\) at every even modulus and \(1+3/[\cos(\pi/N)(2-\cos(\pi/N))]\) at every odd modulus. Odd moduli have one complex optimizer, while even moduli have a six-real-dimensional optimizer face; the odd-modulus excess is asymptotic to \(3\pi^4/(4N^4)\).
