For mirror-symmetric weighted ladders, the exact two-corner controllability losses are classified by the parity of path-eigenvalue gaps equal to twice the rung conductance. On the unit-weight ladder, six rungs is the first size where both same-rail and opposite-rail endpoint placements can fail at the same coupling: at \(g=1/2\) their exact ranks are \(11\) and \(10\) out of \(12\).
