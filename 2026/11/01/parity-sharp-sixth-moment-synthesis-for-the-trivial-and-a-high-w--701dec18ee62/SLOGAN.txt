For the normalized central \(SU(2)\) character plane \(\operatorname{span}\{1,\chi_n\}\) at every \(n\ge2\), the sharp sixth moment is attained by \(\chi_n\) alone exactly at odd weights, while every even weight requires a unique positive trivial-character admixture. At large even weights the optimal admixture is \(5/(4n)+O(n^{-2})\) and raises the sixth moment by \((75/32)n+O(1)\).
