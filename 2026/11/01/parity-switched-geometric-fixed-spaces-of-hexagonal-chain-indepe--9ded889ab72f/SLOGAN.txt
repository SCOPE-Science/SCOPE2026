For a chain of \(n\) hexagons, end reversal and the half-turn have geometric independence-complex fixed spaces that exchange roles with parity: exactly one is a wedge of two spheres, of dimension \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), and the other is collapsible. Row exchange and the full Klein-four group have fixed spaces that are actual simplices, giving a complete fixed-point homotopy diagram rather than merely a homology character.
