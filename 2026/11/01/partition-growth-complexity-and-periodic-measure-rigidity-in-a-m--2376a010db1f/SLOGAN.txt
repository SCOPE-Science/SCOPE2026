A binary subshift with nondecreasing successive marker gaps has exact partition-rate word complexity \(c_n\sim\sqrt3\,\exp(\pi\sqrt{2n/3})/(2\pi^2)\), although every invariant ergodic probability measure is periodic. Its periodic-point zeta function is \(\zeta(z)=P(z)/(1-z)\), where \(P\) is Euler's partition series, and its coefficients satisfy the exact identity \(c_{n+1}-c_n=[z^n]\zeta(z)\).
