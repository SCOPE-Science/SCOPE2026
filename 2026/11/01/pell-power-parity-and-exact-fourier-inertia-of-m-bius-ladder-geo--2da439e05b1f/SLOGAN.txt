The ordinary geodesic distance matrices of all Möbius ladders have a complete, nonsingular cyclic Fourier spectrum, with determinant alternating between a signed power of two and a negative square of a Pell–Lucas number times the distance row sum. A Möbius twist destroys the exponent-one conditional negative type enjoyed by untwisted circular ladders, with a linearly growing family of explicit positive Fourier obstructions.
