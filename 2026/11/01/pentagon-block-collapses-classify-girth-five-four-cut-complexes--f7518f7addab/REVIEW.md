# Scientific review of the girth-five four-cut theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The exact domain is arbitrary finite simple graphs of girth exactly five on at least nine vertices. The dual has the complete two-skeleton, tetrahedra indexed by induced four-vertex paths or stars, and four-simplices indexed by pentagons, with no larger simplex. Every star tetrahedron has a private leaf triangle; every path tetrahedron outside pentagons has a private disconnected triangle. In each pentagon the five path tetrahedra form one five-cycle along disconnected-triangle incidences; collapsing the four-simplex with one tetrahedron breaks this cycle, leaving four tetrahedra removable in a path. Girth rules exclude interfering completions and overlap between these blocks. The dual therefore collapses to a simply connected two-complex, whose free second homology has rank given by the Euler characteristic. Integral duality and the complete primal two-skeleton give the stated top-dimensional sphere wedge. The standalone checker checks each elementary collapse and two independent mod-two formulations on representative graphs. No finite computation substitutes for the universal argument.

## Originality — PASS against inspected material
The 2024 *Topology of Cut Complexes II* Proposition 2.18 already states the five-cycle-corrected face poset and Euler characteristic, so neither is claimed new. The 2023 foundational work establishes skeleton and homological restrictions, but not this girth-five homotopy type. The 2026 high-girth wedge theorem requires girth at least six for four-cuts, which explicitly excludes pentagons. Earlier Petersen-specific four-cut homotopy calculations are subsumed but not separately claimed. A four-cut calculation for the triangular graph concerns girth three. The new claim is the uniform pentagon-block simple-collapse of the dual and resulting integral wedge homotopy for every girth-five graph. Actual full scientific results and primary sources were compared for implications, not merely titles. Unindexed equivalent results and undiscovered indirect stronger theorems remain priority risks.

## Value — PASS
This sharpens a structurally motivated girth threshold: pentagons are exactly the first obstruction to the previous private-face collapse, and a new, explicit cyclic-block collapse resolves the obstruction for all four-cuts. It supplies a complete integral homotopy type, whereas the published five-cycle formula gave only Euler information. Natural highly symmetric instances include the Petersen and dodecahedral graphs, but previously known individual computations are not counted as independent new discoveries. The result is not a shellability theorem or an equivariant classification.
