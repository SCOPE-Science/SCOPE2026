For every graph of girth exactly five on \(n\ge9\) vertices, the ordinary four-cut complex is a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}{3}-c_4(G)+p_5(G)\) spheres of dimension \(n-5\), where \(c_4(G)\) counts connected induced four-sets and \(p_5(G)\) counts five-cycles. Explicit independent collapses of five-cycle blocks establish the integral homotopy type, going beyond the previously known Euler-characteristic correction and girth-six homotopy classification.
