For every polygonal prism \(C_n\square K_2\), the pentagon Hom complex has exactly one circle per nonextreme admissible signed winding and precisely twenty isolated graph homomorphisms when \(5\mid n\), with none otherwise. The number of circles is \(1+2\lfloor(n-1)/10\rfloor\) for even \(n\) and \(2\lfloor(n+4)/10\rfloor\) for odd \(n\), including the empty case \(n=3\).
