Along the natural nonlinear parameter curve where a Blaschke–Anosov torus map has exactly the cat map's full fixed-point derivative, its two primitive two-cycles nevertheless split in trace by \(7\sqrt5\,\mu^2+O(\mu^3)\). Their distinct positive quadratic corrections give a rigorous period-two obstruction to \(C^1\) conjugacy for all sufficiently small positive perturbations.
