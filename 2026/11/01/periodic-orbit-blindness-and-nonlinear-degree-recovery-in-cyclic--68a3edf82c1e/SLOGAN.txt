For an integer-degree cyclic Chebyshev three-map, the exact periodic counts are \(B^n\) and the maximal-entropy dynamics are the uniform one-sided \(B\)-shift, where \(B\) is the product of the degrees; thus only that product is remembered up to metric isomorphism. Yet while all positive-lag ordinary coordinate covariances vanish, the nonlinear one-step Chebyshev correlation spectra recover every labeled degree.
