Under normalized monomial identification, every decreasing compact radial mask produces a weighted Bergman Toeplitz operator that dominates its Gaussian Fock limit mode by mode. The positive trace-class defect has an exact finite-scale integral; for a disk of scaled squared radius \(t\), its trace norm is \(t(t+1)/(N-t)\), yielding a sharp \(N^{-1}\) law.
