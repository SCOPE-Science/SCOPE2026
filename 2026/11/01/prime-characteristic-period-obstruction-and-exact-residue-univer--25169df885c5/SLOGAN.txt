For synchronous neighbour-sum dynamics modulo any odd prime \(p\) on arbitrary complete multipartite graphs, every least period divides \(p\operatorname{lcm}_{1\le j\le p-1}(p^j-1)\), no least period is divisible by \(p^2\), and every state becomes periodic after at most \(p+2\) steps. When all part sizes are congruent modulo \(p\), the exact period-orbit and transient-depth census exhibits characteristic-divisible cycles precisely when the common residue is nonzero and the number of parts is divisible by \(p\).
