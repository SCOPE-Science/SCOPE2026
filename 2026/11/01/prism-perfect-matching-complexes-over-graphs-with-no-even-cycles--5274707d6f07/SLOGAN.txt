For every finite even-cycle-free graph \(H\), the perfect-matching complex of its two-layer prism \(H\square K_2\) is contractible if \(H\) has no perfect matching and is \(S^{|V(H)|/2-1}\) otherwise. A uniform acyclic face matching proves this dichotomy for arbitrary forests, branching odd-cycle cacti and disconnected bases, extending previously known path and odd-cycle special cases.
