For nonzero rank-one square-zero matrices over any finite field, every maximal commuting family consists precisely of operators with images in one proper nonzero subspace and kernels containing it; this yields exact maximal-clique counts and the full clique polynomial by subspace Möbius inversion. Their commuting graph is disconnected in dimension two, has projective-incidence diameter three in dimension three, and has diameter two in all higher dimensions.
