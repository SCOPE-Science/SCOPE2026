The ordinary **open** neighborhood complexes of the Shrikhande graph and the four-by-four rook graph are simplicially isomorphic and each has homotopy type \(\mathbb{RP}^{2}\vee\bigvee^{55}S^{2}\). An exhaustive integral Morse reduction exhibits the degree-two attaching map responsible for the nontrivial \(\mathbb Z/2\) first homology; this torsion is invisible in previously reported higher-degree rook-graph homology.
