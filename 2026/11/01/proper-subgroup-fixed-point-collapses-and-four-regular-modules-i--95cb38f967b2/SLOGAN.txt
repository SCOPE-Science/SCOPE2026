For the ordinary tetrahedral discrete-Morse matching complexes, adjoining the top face adds a relative fourth-homology module whose rational tetrahedral action is exactly \(\mathbb Q[S_4]^{\oplus4}\). Nevertheless the inclusion induces an elementary simplicial collapse on geometric fixed-point spaces for **every nontrivial subgroup** of \(S_4\); the rank-\(96\) relative group itself was previously known, whereas these equivariant refinements are new to the inspected sources.
