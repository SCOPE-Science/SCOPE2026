In a weakly forced circle rotation, two nonzero Fourier harmonics whose frequencies add to the rational rotation denominator open its Arnold tongue already at quadratic order, with exact width \(\pi|AB|\sqrt{q^2+(n-m)^2\cot^2(\pi pm/q)}\,\varepsilon^2+O(|\varepsilon|^3)\). For odd denominators this frequency-sum condition is necessary and sufficient for a nonzero quadratic width among distinct two-harmonic forcings below the denominator, and the width is independent of the forcing phases.
