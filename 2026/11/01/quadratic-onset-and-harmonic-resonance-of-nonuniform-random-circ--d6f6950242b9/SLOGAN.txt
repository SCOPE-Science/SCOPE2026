The first appearance of a circle in the Vietoris--Rips complex of \(n\ge4\) nonuniformly sampled circle points has an exact simplex-integral law, a vanishing first relative correction, and a conditional Dirichlet gap-shape limit. Its leading probability reverses across the sharp harmonic resonance \(n\mid k\): a sinusoidal sampling density decreases it for nonresonant frequencies and increases it for resonant ones.
