For a double-broom tree whose four-vertex spine has \(a\) pendant leaves at one end and \(c\) at the other, the three-path-free complex has the complete integral homotopy type \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(B_{a,c})\simeq\bigvee^{ac-1}S^3\) for all \(a,c\ge1\). A vertex-deletion/link cofiber calculation shows why every new pair of endpoint leaves contributes exactly one three-sphere, apart from one global relation.
