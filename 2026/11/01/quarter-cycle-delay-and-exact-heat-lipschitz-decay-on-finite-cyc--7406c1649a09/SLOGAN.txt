On every unit-edge cycle with at least seven vertices, the sharp heat-semigroup graph-Lipschitz norm is an explicit four-residue-class median of the cyclic heat kernel. Its first contraction occurs exactly at order \(\lfloor(n+2)/4\rfloor\), with residue-class coefficients \((2,1,4,3)\) divided by the corresponding factorial, while its long-time spectral amplitude converges to \(4/\pi\).
