Three independent arclength-uniform points on the boundary of a regular \(m\)-gon have an exact normalized triangle-area mean \((1+\frac32\cot^2(\pi/m))/m^2\) and an exact second moment. The mean decreases strictly from \(1/6\) toward \(3/(2\pi^2)\), with a surprising quartic rather than quadratic finite-sidedness correction \(\pi^2/(10m^4)\).
