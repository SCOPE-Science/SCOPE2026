The proof uses the generalized-hexagon intersection array and full powered-distance eigenspace recurrence. The accompanying standalone verifier reconstructs the recurrence symbolically, checks the closed shell polynomials and the elementary domination inequalities on sampled orders, and locates the roots with high precision. These computations do not substitute for the uniform analytic comparison covering all admissible orders.

Command: `python verify_hexagon_negative_type.py`

The spectral identity, the point–line equality kernel, and the critical Gram-matrix rank are proved in `RESULT.md`. The origin of the strictness step is Li–Weston's theorem, not a computational extrapolation. No independent audit has been performed.
