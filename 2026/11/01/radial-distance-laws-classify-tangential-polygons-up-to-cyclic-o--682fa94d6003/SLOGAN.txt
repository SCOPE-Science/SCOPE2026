For every convex polygon tangent to one circle, either the uniform-boundary or uniform-interior distance-to-incenter law recovers exactly its inradius and the multiset of tangent-normal gaps, but forgets their cyclic order. The transformed boundary law is a finite hinge spline, and a generic \(n\)-gon law belongs to exactly \((n-1)!/2\) noncongruent tangential polygons.
