For exact post-step polar spectral decay on a centered strongly convex matrix quadratic, generic initial ranks never decrease: all surviving singular values converge to one positive level, with an exact countable exceptional set of finite rank-collapse resonances. The matrix limit changes sharply from a two-cycle to a scalar-multiple partial-isometry fixed point across the unique one-step zeroing stepsize, while true singular-value soft thresholding terminates at zero.
