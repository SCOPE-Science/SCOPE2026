In real \(\ell_p^n\) with \(n\ge3\), the duality map preserves affine independence of every unit-sphere \(k\)-tuple for \(4\le k\le n+1\) exactly when \(p=2\). For every other exponent, an explicit one-parameter family of tetrahedral unit-vector configurations has four coplanar contact covectors with a nonzero, closed-form primal volume determinant.
