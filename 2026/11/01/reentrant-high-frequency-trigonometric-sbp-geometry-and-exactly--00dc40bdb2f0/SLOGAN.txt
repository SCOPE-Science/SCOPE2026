For \(\pi<\kappa<2\pi\), three-node trigonometric summation-by-parts norms regain positivity on two aliased off-center grids even after the midpoint grid becomes impossible: the number of admissible interior nodes changes exactly from three to two to zero at \(4\pi/3\) and \(5\pi/3\). Requiring the positive diagonal norm to integrate constants exactly leaves precisely three grids in the entire band—one centered at a uniquely determined frequency and two reflected off-center grids at another.
