On the unit-edge six-cycle, the kernel \(e^{-t d^p}\) is strictly positive definite at \(t=7/25\) and \(t=3/5\), but has exactly one negative eigenvalue at the intervening \(t=2/5\), simultaneously for every \(647/300\le p\le653/300\).
