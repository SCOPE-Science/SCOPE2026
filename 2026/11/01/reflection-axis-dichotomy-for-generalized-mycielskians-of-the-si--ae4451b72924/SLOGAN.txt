For every generalized Mycielskian of the six-cycle, the vertex-axis reflection has a contractible geometric fixed space, whereas the edge-axis reflection has an exactly classified three-periodic homotopy type: contractible at \(\ell\equiv1\pmod3\) and a wedge of exponentially many integral spheres otherwise. The nontrivial fixed spaces arise from a looped three-orbit quotient whose two-layer graph is itself a six-cycle.
