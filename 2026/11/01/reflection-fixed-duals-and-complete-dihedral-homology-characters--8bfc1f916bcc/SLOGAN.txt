For every \(p\ge2\) and \(n\ge6p-3\), the reflections of the dihedral symmetry of the ordinary three-cut complex of \(C_n^p\) have explicit top-homology traces depending only on \(p\), \(n\), and the reflection axis type. An explicit collapse of the reflection-fixed Alexander-dual complex to contractible components supplies the missing reflection terms and hence the complete dihedral character and invariant dimension.
