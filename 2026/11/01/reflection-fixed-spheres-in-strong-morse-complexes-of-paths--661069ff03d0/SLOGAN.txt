The geometric reflection-fixed locus of the full strong Morse matching complex of a path with \(n\geq2\) edges is PL-homeomorphic to an explicit graph independence complex. It is a sphere \(S^{k-1}\) when \(n=4k\) or \(4k+1\) with \(k\geq1\), and is contractible for all other \(n\geq2\); for one edge the fixed locus is empty.
