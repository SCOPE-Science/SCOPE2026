The geometric reversal-fixed space of the two-independence complex of a path on \(n\) vertices is canonically PL-homeomorphic to a shorter forbidden-triple complex with one blocked boundary pair. It is contractible for \(n\equiv1,2\pmod4\) and is a sphere \(S^{k-1}\) at \(n=4k-1,4k\), proved by a uniform acyclic matching.
