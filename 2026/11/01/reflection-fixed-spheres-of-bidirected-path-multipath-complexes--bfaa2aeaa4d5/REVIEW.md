# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Arc incompatibility is exactly pairwise on the bidirected underlying path: the only directed cycle is a two-cycle and all possible indegree/outdegree conflicts are pairwise. Reflection exchanges \(p_i\) with \(q_{n-1-i}\), giving the stated two tails. Their orbit incompatibility is exactly the width-one bipartite band condition. The orbit-barycenter homeomorphism handles nonvertex geometric fixed points, and the explicit neighborhood-dominance reductions give a uniform collapse to \(\lceil t/2\rceil\) independent edges. Euler–Lefschetz then forces the integral reversal sign from the published ambient sphere theorem. Finite verification checked all orbit subsets for \(3\le n\le13\) and 120 band-graph fold instances. The finite tests support, but do not replace, the all-width proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The complete 2026 primary manuscript arXiv:2601.05670, especially Proposition 2.2 and its surrounding path and polygon results, was compared directly; it classifies the ambient bidirected linear multipath complex but not geometric reversal-fixed spaces. The earlier arXiv:2208.04656 manuscript studies other multipath homotopy data and does not contain this geometric fixed-space theorem. Closely related published findings on dihedral characters of bidirected *polygons*, discrete-Morse complexes of paths, and bidirected double stars have differing objects/invariants and do not imply the theorem. Targeted public-literature and mathematical-record queries did not identify equivalent coverage, but unindexed equivalence is possible.

**Value — PASS.** The fixed-space theorem computes a natural symmetry invariant for every bidirected path, with unbounded dimension about one quarter the length. This cannot be read off from the previously known ambient sphere dimension. The explicit PL model and cross-polytope collapse additionally recover a sharp mod-eight integral orientation character, giving both geometric and representation-theoretic information useful for comparisons with related multipath complexes.

**Limits.** The result only handles one reflection and does not give an equivariant ambient sphere equivalence. The PL fixed-space model is followed by homotopy, not PL-sphere, equivalence. Finite computation is not an infinite proof; the proof is supplied separately. Publication-level novelty and mathematical correctness are not independently certified.
