In a natural integer-slope specialization of the grazing-sliding border-collision map, the physical law has uniform return times \(1,\ldots,m\), but the unique maximal-entropy law has return probabilities \(\beta_m^{-r}\). As \(m\to\infty\), physical entropy tends to zero while topological entropy tends to \(\log2\), and the physical and maximal-entropy orbit-occupation profiles live on incompatible excursion scales.
