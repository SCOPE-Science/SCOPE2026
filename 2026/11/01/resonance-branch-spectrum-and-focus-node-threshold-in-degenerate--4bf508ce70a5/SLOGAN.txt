Every right-block length of a rank-one border-collision map has a complete, real and interlacing zero-slope resonance spectrum, with a sharp focus-to-hyperbolic crossing at \(d=a^2(1+1/n)^2\). As the block length grows, the resonances fill the focus band with an arcsine law while one exceptional positive branch appears exactly for \(d>a^2\) and converges exponentially to \(a+d/a\); these algebraic resonances do not alone guarantee physical periodic orbits.
