## Value — PASS
This classifies a canonical involution of a natural infinite two-dimensional polyhedral-product family, including the sharp disk exception and a uniform nonorientable genus/boundary formula. It goes substantially beyond the known nonequivariant wedge-of-circles homotopy classification: the same fundamental-group rank can arise from many compact surfaces, while this result resolves the homeomorphism type. No paper-sized generalization is claimed.
