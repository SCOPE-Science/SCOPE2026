Coordinate reversal turns the real moment-angle surface of an \(n\)-vertex path into a disk at \(n=3\) and, for every \(n\ge4\), into a nonorientable surface with \(2^{n-3}\) boundary circles and \(2+(n-4)2^{n-3}-2^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor-1}\) crosscaps. A complete fixed-arc and boundary-gluing argument determines the homeomorphism type, not just its Betti numbers.
