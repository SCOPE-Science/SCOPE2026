# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The face condition for two-independence is checked separately for the root absent and present. The deletion is exactly a join of star complexes; for each star, a Mayer–Vietoris isomorphism gives the integral leaf-augmentation lattice. The root link decomposes into a full leaf simplex and one cone per branch, with an explicit equivariant piecewise-linear contraction to the leaf simplex. Equivariant gluing justifies root deletion. The join Künneth theorem determines the homology and the even interchange sign for degree-one factors gives the unsigned tensor permutation. The monodromy trace reduces to fixed leaves minus one. Seven exhaustive finite mod-two homology comparisons and 140 signed Lefschetz comparisons passed; neither replaces the infinite proof. No independent validation is claimed.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
Sawant (arXiv:2605.25150v1, Theorem 3.6) proves vertex decomposability of higher-independence complexes of all trees, not explicit integral symmetry characters or the root-deletion equivalence. Deshpande–Singh (arXiv:2001.05448, Theorem 3.10) requires each nonleaf vertex to have an attached pendant leaf, which fails at the root here. Their Theorem 5.6 covers the ordinary homotopy type of a balanced perfect-tree subfamily, which is expressly excluded from any novelty assertion. Related chordal/caterpillar work gives structural decomposability, not the present full representation. Targeted statement-level searches did not surface an implication covering the nonuniform and integral equivariant theorem. This does not establish uniqueness against unindexed or implicit work.

## Value — PASS
The question is motivated by the structural tree vertex-decomposability theorem, which cannot by itself determine sphere numbers, degrees or the action of tree symmetries. An entire unbounded nonuniform family receives one exact decomposition, a root-deletion equivalence and its natural full integral symmetry representation. The trace formula distinguishes branch monodromy types beyond the ordinary Betti rank. This is not a trivial reformulation of the known regular perfect-tree subfamily.

## Scientific boundaries
The result applies to the \(2\)-independence complex of a rooted height-two tree with at least two nonempty branches. The symmetry group fixes the root. No assertion about higher parameters, all trees, or equivariant homeomorphism is made. Independent validation remains outstanding.
