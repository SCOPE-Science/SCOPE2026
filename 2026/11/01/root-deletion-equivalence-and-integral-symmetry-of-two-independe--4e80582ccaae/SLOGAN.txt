For a height-two rooted tree with at least two branches and positive leaf counts \(q_1,\ldots,q_m\), deleting the root preserves the \(2\)-independence complex up to equivariant homotopy, giving an exact wedge of \(\prod_i(q_i-1)\) spheres in dimension \(2m-1\). Its entire top integral symmetry module is the unsigned tensor product of the branches' leaf-augmentation lattices, with an explicit monodromy fixed-leaf trace formula.
