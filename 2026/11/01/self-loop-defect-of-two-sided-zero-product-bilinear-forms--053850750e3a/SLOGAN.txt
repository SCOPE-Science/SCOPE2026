For every radical-square-zero quiver algebra, two-sided zero-product-preserving bilinear forms have dimension \(n+2m\), while those factoring through the two ordered products have codimension exactly the number \(\ell\) of self-loops. Thus the algebra is two-sided zero-product determined exactly when the quiver is loopless, even if it has oriented cycles; over \(\mathbb F_q\) there are precisely \(q^\ell\) times as many preserving forms as product-factorable forms.
