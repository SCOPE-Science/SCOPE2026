For two prescribed linear automorphisms of \(\mathbb F_q^2\), the number of linear permutations simultaneously completing both is controlled exactly by the eigenlines of their relative matrix; the entire count has seven rational-canonical families with explicit multiplicities. Every pair has at least \(q^4-3q^3+5q-1\) such mates when \(q\ge3\), while over \(\mathbb F_2^2\) precisely the three nontrivial unipotent relative automorphisms admit none.
