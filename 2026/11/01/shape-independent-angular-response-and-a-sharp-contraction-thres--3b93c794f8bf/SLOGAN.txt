For every planar triangle of area \(A\), the mean centroidal polar inertia after one uniformly oriented \(\lambda\)-Steiner step equals \(\tfrac12 I(T)+A^2/(4\pi)+A^2(\lambda-\tfrac12)^2/(3\pi)\), so the parameter response is independent of triangular shape. The step contracts inertia in expectation for **every** triangle precisely when \(\lambda\ge\tfrac12-\sqrt{\pi\sqrt3/6-3/4}\); below this threshold even an equilateral triangle exhibits mean expansion.
