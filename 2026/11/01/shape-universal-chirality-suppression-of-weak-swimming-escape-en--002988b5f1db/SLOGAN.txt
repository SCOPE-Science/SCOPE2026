For a chiral active Brownian particle started with uniform independent position and heading in a completely absorbing planar domain, the exact quadratic-in-swimming reduction of mean exit time is a strictly completely monotone Stieltjes function of squared angular velocity \(\Omega^2\). Its positive Dirichlet spectral weights work for every bounded smooth domain, so chirality strictly weakens this weak-swimming speedup even where finite-speed, fixed-start optimization can behave differently.
