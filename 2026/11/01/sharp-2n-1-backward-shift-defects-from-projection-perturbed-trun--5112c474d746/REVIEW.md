# Review of the projected truncated-shift defect family

## Correctness

PASS. The domain, Hardy normalization, compressed monomial multiplication, and rank-\(n\) orthogonal projection are explicitly defined. Direct kernel equations identify a free \(r\)-parameter graph contained in \(zH^2\). The quotient map \(L\) annihilates exactly that graph; the calculated matrix \(LS^*|_M\) has \(2n+1\) independent columns. An elementary independent argument provides the matching general upper bound. Exact-rational checks cover ten finite model sizes without being substituted for the uniform proof.

## Originality

PASS at best knowledge, with publication priority unverified. Khan–Lata–Singh address finite-defect invariant-space descriptions for vector-valued Hardy Toeplitz operators, not exact extremizing defects of scalar truncated monomial shifts. Liang–Partington and Chattopadhyay–Das–Pradhan treat full Hardy Toeplitz kernels. Chattopadhyay–Khan prove the universal upper bound \(2n+1\) for truncated Toeplitz kernel defects but do not exhibit equality for all ranks under orthogonal projection perturbations in the inspected main results and rank-one/two discussions. The upper bound itself is clearly credited, not claimed. Targeted record searches and accessible own claims returned no implication of the exact positive-projection family; older unrecognized constructions remain a risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Sharpness of a rank-dependent universal structural bound is a natural missing extremal question. A positive orthogonal projection is a much more restrictive perturbation than an arbitrary finite-rank operator; a monomial on a finite model space is among the simplest truncated Toeplitz symbols. Attainment for every rank settles whether the coefficient and additive term can be improved under these restrictions. The result does not classify extremizers or optimize model dimension.

## Limitations and closest work

The cited October 2026 universal upper bound is prior scientific input, independently rederived only for exposition. The precise new contribution is equality achieved by an explicitly defined all-rank family. Full novelty against all prior literature is not proven. The automated finite checks cannot replace analytical or independent scrutiny.
