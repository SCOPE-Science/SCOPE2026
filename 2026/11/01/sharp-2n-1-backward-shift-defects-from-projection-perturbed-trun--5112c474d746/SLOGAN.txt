For every rank \(n\ge1\), subtracting a positive rank-\(n\) coordinate projection from the truncated Toeplitz monomial shift on \(K_{z^{4n+4}}\) produces a kernel whose exact minimal backward-shift defect is \(2n+1\). Thus the known universal upper bound is attained even by this elementary finite-dimensional operator family, without claiming the upper bound itself as new.
