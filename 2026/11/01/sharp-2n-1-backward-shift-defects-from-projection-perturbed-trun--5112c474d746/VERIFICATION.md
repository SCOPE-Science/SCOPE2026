The proof is a finite-coordinate identity valid for all positive integers \(n\). The quotient map \(L\) is surjective and has the displayed kernel, and \(LS^*|_M\) has exactly \(2n+1\) independent coordinate outputs. A separate elementary argument reconstructs the known universal upper estimate, without asserting novelty for it.

It verifies the projected operator, kernel parametrization, and defect rank in those finite dimensions.
