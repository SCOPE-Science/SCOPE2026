On every Cartesian product of two simplices, the globally affine least-squares projector has an exact rational \(L^\infty\) norm determined by the negative part of a sum of two scaled beta variables. For large balanced factors its sharp amplification grows as \(4e^{-2}\) times the total dimension, strictly below the single-simplex coefficient \(2e^{-1}\); a balanced split uniquely minimizes the asymptotic two-factor coefficient.
