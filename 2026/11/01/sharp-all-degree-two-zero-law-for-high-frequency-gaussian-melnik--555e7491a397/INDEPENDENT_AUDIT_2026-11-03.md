## correctness

PASS

For the exact first-order right-homoclinic Duffing Melnikov trigonometric polynomial with independent Gaussian damping and harmonic coefficients, conditioning on the harmonic field gives the Gaussian CDF range formula. At N=1, integrating a Rayleigh amplitude against the Gaussian offset gives p_1=t/sqrt(1+t^2), with t=3 sqrt(2) pi omega sech(pi omega/2)/4. The extremal-value Lipschitz bound and summable k q^k coefficient tails yield the uniform all-N error O(omega q^2), q=exp(-pi omega/2). For the multiplicity assertion, if T=sum_{k>=2}s_k(k+k^2)R_k<s_1 R_1/(2 sqrt(2)), the first derivative has a fixed sign on the two broad arcs and the second derivative a fixed sign near its turning points, hence exactly two critical points. Rayleigh small-ball conditioning and Minkowski give P(Z>=4)<=8[(1+q^2)/q sum_{k>=2}(k^2+k^3)q^k]^2=O(q^2). As all noncritical phase roots are simple almost surely, subtracting P(Z>=4) proves P(Z=2)=(3 pi/sqrt(2)) omega q+O(omega q^2), uniformly in every finite N including N(omega). Independently recomputed four numerical Rayleigh integrals and coefficient-tail identities. This is a theorem only about first-order Melnikov zeros, not a uniform finite-epsilon horseshoe probability.

## value

PASS

The sharp rare-event prefactor and the proof that almost every high-frequency crossing event has exactly two simple phase zeros answer a natural probabilistic separatrix-crossing question not settled by a mean zero count or a one-sided upper bound; importantly the limit is uniform over a growing Fourier cutoff. This is a motivated structural asymptotic, not a restatement of the one-harmonic Rayleigh formula.
