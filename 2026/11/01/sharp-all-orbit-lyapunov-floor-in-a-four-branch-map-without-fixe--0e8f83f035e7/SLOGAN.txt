In the García-Grimaldo–Campos-Cantón four-branch interval map, every smooth orbit in an explicit open parameter chamber has Lyapunov lower limit at least \(\frac14\log(sr^3)\), and a periodic witness attains equality. Consequently every smooth orbit expands uniformly and exponentially exactly when \(sr^3>1\); for the originally published \(r=4/5,s=5\) map the sharp floor is \(\frac14\log(64/25)\).
