For Poisson orientation resetting of an active Brownian particle, fixing the mean reset-direction polarization \(a\) gives an exact sharp interval of longitudinal-minus-transverse diffusivities, with unique two-angle extremizers. When \(\sqrt3/2<a<1\), a rigorously determined bounded interval of reset rates forces transverse dominance for **every** admissible reset-angle distribution, while longitudinal dominance is again achievable both below and above that interval.
