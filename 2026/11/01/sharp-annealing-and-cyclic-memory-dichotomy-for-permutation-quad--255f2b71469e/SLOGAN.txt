For time-varying convex combinations of permutation quadratic stochastic maps, the cumulative mixing quantity \(\sum_n\alpha_n(1-\alpha_n)\) gives a sharp transition between universal cycle-balance and residual finite cyclic memory, except for exact one-step cancellation on involutions. On a three-cycle, the schedule \(\alpha_n=(n+4)^{-p}\) has its transition exactly at \(p=1\), with critical imbalance decay \(\Theta(n^{-3/2})\).
