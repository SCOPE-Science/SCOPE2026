The expected common area of \(m\) independent planar Brownian hulls shrinks as \(\pi^4/[4m^2(\log m)^2]\), while that of \(n\) independent Brownian-bridge hulls shrinks as \(\pi/(n\log n)\); both admit explicit next logarithmic corrections. A bridge population of order \(m^2\log m\) is exactly critical, and its full crossover is governed by the integral \(\int_0^\infty x\exp[-x-(\pi^3\lambda/4)x^2]dx\).
