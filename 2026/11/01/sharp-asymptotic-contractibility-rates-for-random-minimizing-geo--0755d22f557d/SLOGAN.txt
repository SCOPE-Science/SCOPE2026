For independent uniform vertices connected cyclically by shortest geodesics on any Euclidean flat two-torus, the entire winding-class distribution is a sampled Voronoi-cell convolution. The null-homotopy probability has sharp large-vertex rate \(6/\pi\le\lim_{n\to\infty}n p_n\le18\sqrt3/(5\pi)\), with equality precisely for rectangular and equilateral lattices, respectively.
