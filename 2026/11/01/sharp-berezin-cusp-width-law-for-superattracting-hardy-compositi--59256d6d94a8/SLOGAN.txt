For every analytic Hardy-space composition symbol \(\varphi(z)=a_m z^m+O(z^{m+1})\) with \(m\ge2\) and \(a_m\ne0\), the vertical width of its Berezin range at real coordinate \(1-\delta\) is \(2|a_m|\delta^{(m+1)/2}(1+O(\sqrt\delta))\). Thus the unparameterized range locally determines both the order of the superattracting zero and the magnitude of its first nonzero Taylor coefficient, regardless of subsequent analytic terms.
