In a product-recycling glycolysis oscillator with conversion capacity \(\sigma>\nu+\sigma_i\), the net product-removal rate \(\lambda=\kappa_s+d\) has a sharp global boundary: all positive trajectories are bounded for \(\lambda>0\) and unbounded for \(\lambda\le0\). At zero removal the product grows as \(q\nu t\) with an exact additive offset, while at negative removal it grows as \(Ce^{-\lambda t}-q\nu/(-\lambda)+o(1)\) with \(C>0\); in both cases the substrate converges to \((\nu+\sigma_i)/(\sigma-\nu-\sigma_i)\).
