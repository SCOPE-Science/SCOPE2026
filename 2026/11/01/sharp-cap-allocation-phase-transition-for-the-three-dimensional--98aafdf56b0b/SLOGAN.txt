For a tetrahedron with two disjoint vertex caps removed, the Godbersen ratio \(V_3(K,K,-K)/\operatorname{vol}_3(K)\) has a sharp fixed-removed-volume optimization: equal cuts minimize it until one quarter of the original volume has been removed, after which all edge-contact minimizers share the exact value \(5/3\). The entire two-parameter ratio, both sharp endpoints, and all equality cases are explicit.
