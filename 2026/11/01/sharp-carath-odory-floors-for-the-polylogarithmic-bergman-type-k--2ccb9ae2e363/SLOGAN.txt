For the polylogarithmic reproducing kernels \(F_\alpha(t)=\sum_{n\ge0}(n+1)^\alpha t^n\), the exact infimum of \(\operatorname{Re}((1-t)^2F_\alpha(t))\) jumps from zero to one at \(\alpha=1\), then equals \(4\eta(-\alpha)\) until \(\alpha=2\), and drops to \(-\infty\) for every \(\alpha>2\). The sharp lower envelope is attained in boundary limits at \(t=1\) below order one and \(t=-1\) between orders one and two, yielding a complete positive-real-part phase diagram and a sharp kernel zero-free range.
