**PASS.** The bounded finite problem reduces without loss to \(729\) choice matrices; exact integer Sylvester tests rule out \(723\) selectors above \(29/20\). The six remaining matrices form one graph-automorphism orbit. An independently computed integer characteristic polynomial shows that their top squared singular value is \((123+\sqrt{7641})/144\). The positive two-level eigenvector obeys \(34a^2+73a-17=0\), and its strict maximizing-radius conditions are verified for \(0<a<1\). The spectral root is simple; these steps classify all nonnegative rays. ## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The triangular prism is the smallest nonbipartite connected cubic graph and the only other isomorphism type besides the cubic biclique among connected six-vertex cubic graphs. Its exact maximal Hilbert norm is a natural analytic invariant, and the complete equality classification exhibits a structurally meaningful distance-two-pair localization with six equivalent but distinct labeled extremizers. These features are not a reparameterization or a nearby finite-size increment within a prior prism formula, and they provide a precise nonbipartite benchmark for finite-graph maximal inequalities. The contribution is a complete small-graph classification, not a general asymptotic theorem.
