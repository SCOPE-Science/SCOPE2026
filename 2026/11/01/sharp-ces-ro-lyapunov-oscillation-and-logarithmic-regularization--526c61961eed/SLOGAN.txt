For the positive-area wandering rectangles in a nonconformal planar expanding map, ordinary Cesàro smoothing of coordinate-axis finite-time Lyapunov exponents still oscillates, with exact accumulation interval \([c-dR_\gamma,c+dR_\gamma]\) and \(R_\gamma=2\gamma^{\gamma/(\gamma+1)}/(\gamma+1)-1\). The same exponents admit a logarithmically weighted mean converging to \(c\) at rate \(O((\log N)^{-1})\).
