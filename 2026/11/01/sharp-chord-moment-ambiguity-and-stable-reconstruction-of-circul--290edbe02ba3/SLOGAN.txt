For area-uniform random points on a nondegenerate circular cone sidewall, the second and fourth Euclidean chord moments have exactly two noncongruent geometric realizations precisely when their normalized ratio lies strictly between \(1107/770\) and \(5/3\). Adding the sixth moment yields a unique reconstruction of both cone dimensions, and a simple dimensionless moment contrast has the sharp global inverse Lipschitz constant \(4725/281\).
