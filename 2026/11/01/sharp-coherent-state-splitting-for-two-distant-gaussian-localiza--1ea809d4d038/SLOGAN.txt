For two equal Gaussian time–frequency localization disks receding to infinite separation \(d\), the top spectral pair splits as \(\lambda_0(1\pm e^{-d^2/2})\) with an error exponentially smaller than the coherent-state overlap. The universal sharp coefficient is the single-disk optimal concentration \(\lambda_0=1-e^{-s}\), where \(\pi s\) is each disk's area.
