The sharp fourth-power norm quotient of the second Hardy \(H^4\) backward-shift iterate over constant-corrected lifts of all first-iterate extremizers is \(1.707521792401\ldots\), attained exactly by disk-automorphism lifts. This gives the strictly improved bound \(\|B^2\|\ge1.143119317106\ldots>\|B\|=1.127838485561\ldots\) without claiming the unrestricted second-iterate norm is known.
