For the complex weighted-harmonic Poisson operator on the circle, the exact \(L^2\) contraction threshold changes at \(\alpha=0\), and positive weights exhibit a sharp radius beyond which one negative Fourier mode dominates constants. As the boundary is approached, the maximal mode grows like \((1-r)^{-1}\) and its amplification converges to the unique maximum of a Gamma-survival profile; at \(\alpha=1\), the exact staircase begins at the golden-ratio radius and the limit is \(2/\sqrt e\).
