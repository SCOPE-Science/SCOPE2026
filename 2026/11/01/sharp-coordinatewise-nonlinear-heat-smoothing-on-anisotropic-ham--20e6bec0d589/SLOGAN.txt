On every finite anisotropic Hamming graph, nonlinear \(p\)-heat has an exact, dimension-independent scalar upper envelope for the edge gradient in each coordinate, with sharp rate \(c_i[2+(q_i-2)\alpha_p]\) and \(\alpha_p=1\) for \(1<p\le2\), \(\alpha_p=2^{2-p}\) for \(p>2\). One separable initial condition simultaneously realizes equality in every coordinate for all times, yielding a sharp finite-time consensus bound when \(1<p<2\).
