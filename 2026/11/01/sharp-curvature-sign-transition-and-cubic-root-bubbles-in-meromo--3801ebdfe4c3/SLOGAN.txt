The kernel-induced metric of the weighted meromorphic Bergman space on a punctured disk has positive curvature near the puncture exactly when the fractional weight parameter \(\delta\) lies in \((0,\sqrt2-1)\), and then its curvature changes sign across exactly one circle. That circle shrinks at the sharp rate \(2^{-1/3}\delta^{1/3}\) as the weight approaches an integer from above, despite the metric converging to the ordinary negatively curved Bergman geometry at each fixed nonzero point.
