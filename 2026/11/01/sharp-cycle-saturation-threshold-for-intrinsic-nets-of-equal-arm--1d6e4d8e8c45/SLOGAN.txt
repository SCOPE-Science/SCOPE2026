For a compact metric graph with one arm of length \(L\) and any number \(m\ge2\) of equal parallel arms of length \(s\le L\), its optimal intrinsic \(p\)-point covering radius equals the isometric-circle bound \((L+s)/(2p)\) **exactly when** \(L\ge(p-1)s\). At and above the sharp threshold, all \(p\) optimal covering sites can be placed on the long arm, independently of \(m\); below it the circle lower bound is strictly unattainable.
