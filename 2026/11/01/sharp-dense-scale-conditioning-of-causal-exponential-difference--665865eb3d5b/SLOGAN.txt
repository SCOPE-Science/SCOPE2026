Normalized geometric dilates of a causal difference-of-exponentials wavelet form a Riesz sequence at every scale ratio \(c>1\), with an explicit Mellin–hyperbolic-secant Gram symbol. As \(L=\log c\downarrow0\), the sharp coefficient-recovery condition number grows like \(L^2e^{\pi^2/L}/64\), revealing an exponentially severe dense-scale instability not visible from channelwise norms.
