For every integer sphere dimension \(d\ge2\), the ordinary degree-\(n\) affine Bernstein operator preserves all zonal positive-definite kernels on \(S^d\) exactly when \(n\le d+1\). Below this sharp threshold, embedding a Boolean cube shows every output has nonnegative ordinary power coefficients and is therefore positive definite on spheres of **every** dimension; above it, a normalized Gegenbauer input witnesses failure.
