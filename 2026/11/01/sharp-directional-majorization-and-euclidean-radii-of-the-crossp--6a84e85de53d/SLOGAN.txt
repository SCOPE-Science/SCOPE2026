For a volume-uniform point in the regular crosspolytope, the first absolute projection moment is strictly Schur-concave in the squared coordinates of its unit direction. Its centroid body's exact Euclidean inradius is \(1/(d+1)\), the circumradius is \(2\sqrt{d/\pi}\,\Gamma(d+1/2)/((d+1)\Gamma(d+1))\), and the complete sharp contact directions are the axes and signed diagonals, with global quadratic rigidity.
