With a fixed mean and bounded allowed reset distance, the two-endpoint reset-location mixture uniquely minimizes the **entire first-passage-time law**, while resetting always to the mean uniquely maximizes it, for one-dimensional point targets and exterior three-dimensional spherical targets under every independent positive renewal clock. More generally, any mean-preserving spread of reset radii makes completion stochastically earlier; the sharp result follows from strict radial concavity of free Brownian survival and a cyclewise quantile coupling.
