In a box-constrained linear program, the dual weights that perfectly recover the full linking-constraint bound through one aggregate have a sharp Euclidean total-collapse radius of \(1/\sqrt m\) and coordinatewise radius of \(1/m\). Independent Gaussian weight errors of standard deviation \(c/m\) exhibit a complete-loss threshold at \(c=\sqrt{\pi/2}\), with an explicit normal critical window.
