Tilting its symbol probabilities by the unique positive Cramér root reverses its drift; the finite first-overshoot interval turns the exit formula into matching positive upper and lower powers. None of these inspected results states or implies an exact two-sided boundary exponent for all positive-Lyapunov AM parameters with the complete pair of negative-moment cutoff criteria. The change-of-measure argument is a standard probabilistic mechanism; only its precise application and all-parameter AM dynamical consequences are claimed. Mathematical index searches on endpoint dimensions, Cramér roots, equivalences, and negative moments showed no directly covering statement. Priority remains subject to specialist literature review.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The earlier regularity theorem admits arbitrary sufficiently small Hölder exponents and gives no sharp lower mass estimates. The present exponent equations replace that qualitative estimate with precise endpoint local dimensions, all positive and critical negative-moment thresholds, and sharp phase surfaces that distinguish the two endpoints of one stationary law. Such endpoint information is directly relevant to integrability of singular observables and to how singular/full-support stationary laws accumulate at the common fixed points. The proof remains valid in resonant and non-resonant regimes.

The novelty comparison is limited to inspected primary texts, related papers, and mathematical-record searches; general renewal theory may contain equivalent boundary results for broader random systems. The claim is restricted to positive-endpoint-Lyapunov AM systems with constant strictly positive selection probabilities.
