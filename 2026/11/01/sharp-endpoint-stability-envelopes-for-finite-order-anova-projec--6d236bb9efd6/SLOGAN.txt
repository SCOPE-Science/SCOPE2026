The exact \(L^\infty\) norm of every finite-order ANOVA projector on an arbitrary finite product probability space is a one-dimensional rare-atom Bernoulli expectation, and uniform coordinate laws minimize the norm among all laws with the same alphabet sizes. For interaction orders at most \(k\) in \(d\) variables, the sharp probability-uniform supremum is \(\sum_{j=0}^{k}(-1)^{k-j}2^j\binom dj\), and the resulting envelopes can fall as the target interaction order increases.
