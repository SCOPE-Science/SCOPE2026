For approximating \(z^2\) in the analytic Bergman disk by two reproducing kernels of equal evaluation norm, the unique optimal nodes have squared radius \(t_*=0.390780720319\ldots\) and relative angle \(1.686047946218\ldots\) radians, neither coincident nor antipodal. Their squared projection is the exact algebraic quantity \(0.157418166572\ldots\), characterized by a quartic stationary equation and proved globally optimal by exact elimination and boundary inequalities.
