For ordinary Dirichlet-series translations by \(\sigma+it\), the largest centered disk in the closed numerical range shrinks as \((|t|/(e\sigma))\exp(-\pi\sigma/|t|)\) when \(t\to0\) with \(\sigma>0\) fixed. Three carefully selected diagonal eigenvalues make the exponentially small bound sharp, exposing a nonoscillating numerical-range boundary layer.
