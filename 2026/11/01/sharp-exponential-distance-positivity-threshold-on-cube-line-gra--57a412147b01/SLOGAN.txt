The exponential shortest-path-distance kernel on the line graph of a binary \(d\)-cube is positive semidefinite at scale \(t>0\) exactly when \(t\ge-\log z_d\), with \(z_d(1+z_d)^{d-2}=1\) for \(d\ge3\), and is positive definite at every positive scale for \(d=2\). A complete Walsh spectrum identifies a \(d-1\)-dimensional singular space at the sharp threshold and proves that the underlying metric is not of negative type in dimensions \(d\ge3\).
