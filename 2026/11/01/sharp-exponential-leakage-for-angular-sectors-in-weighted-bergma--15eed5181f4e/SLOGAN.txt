For each fixed angular sector strictly inside the upper half-plane, the sharp fraction of weighted Bergman mass it can capture has a unique optimizing Mellin frequency. The missing mass has the exact asymptotic form \(K\lambda^{-1/2}e^{-\lambda\Delta}\), with explicit positive geometric constants and an explicit order-one correction to the optimal frequency; the previously known angular Toeplitz diagonalization is only the starting point.
