A signed three-node feedback network has an exact stability tongue whose weak-damping existence is decided by the sharp ratio \(\rho_*=8/(3\sqrt3)\). Below this gain threshold, zero-mean nodal heterogeneity stabilizes an otherwise unstable equilibrium over a window of width proportional to damping; at the threshold the window survives but collapses cubically, bounded by distinct oscillatory and zero-eigenvalue crossings.
