For the uniform three-ball, the exact excess of radius-\(t\) ball-translate depth over Tukey depth at interior radius \(r\) is \(3(1-r^2)^2/[16(t-r)]\) for every \(t\ge1\). Its sharp global error occurs on one noncentral sphere and equals \(3r_t(1-r_t^2)/4\), where \(r_t=1/(2t+\sqrt{4t^2-3})\), giving the best asymptotic coefficient \(3/16\).
