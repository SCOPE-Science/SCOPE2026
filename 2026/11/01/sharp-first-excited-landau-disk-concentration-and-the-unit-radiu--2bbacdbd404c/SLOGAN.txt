On the first excited Landau level, sharp Gaussian disk concentration changes maximizing mode exactly at radius \(R=1\): the optimizer switches from \(1-|z|^2\) to \(\overline z\), with a three-dimensional tie at the transition. The sharp concentration fraction is \(1-e^{-R^2}(1+R^4)\) below the transition and \(1-e^{-R^2}(1+R^2)\) above it.
