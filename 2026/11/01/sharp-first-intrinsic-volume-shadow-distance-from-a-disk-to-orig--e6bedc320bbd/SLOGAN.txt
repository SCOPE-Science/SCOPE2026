Among all convex polygons containing the center of a unit disk and having at most \(N\ge3\) vertices, the first intrinsic-volume shadow-distance minimizer is uniquely a centered regular \(N\)-gon of circumradius \((1-\sin^2(\pi/N)/4)^{-1/2}\). The exact minimum is \(\pi-2N\arcsin(\sin(\pi/N)/2)\), with leading error \(\pi^3/(8N^2)\).
