# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof is a direct integral chain argument for all \(n\ge3\) and all \(0\le k<n\). Allowed two-paths shorten every long edge to consecutive unit edges; unit-edge one-cycles form the integer line generated by the Hamiltonian cycle. For \(2k<n\), an integer positive-residue winding functional annihilates the entire two-boundary group, including combinations whose individual shortcuts are forbidden, and detects the generator. When \(2k\ge n\), a reciprocal two-path plus the explicit telescoping chains gives a literal \(n-1\)-term integral filling. This proves the sharp barcode with no coefficient or parity gap. The checker successfully reconstructed the independent degree-one boundary span for 65 parameter pairs over four primes and validated 28 explicit integral fillings; finite ranks are corroboration, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with bounded risk
Tang and Yau, arXiv:2602.04140v2, Theorem 3.15, already cover the \(n>2k\) part over fields and prove stronger all-degree stability there. Those results are credited; no novelty is asserted for that stable part. Their hypotheses exclude the new \(2k\ge n\) transition. Kapulkin and Koyanagi (arXiv:2607.26226v1) prove general persistent-path-homology stability, not this exact barcode. The published cyclic-tournament calculation covers only the odd-order last predeath case, as established by inspection of its full public result. Searches of the indexed mathematical results and primary paper under cyclic, interval-circulant, bidirected, reciprocal-arc, and persistence aliases did not produce a stronger covering statement. Unindexed equivalent work is a residual possibility, and the general Cayley-digraph source was inspected only at abstract level.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The sharp disappearance of first integral path homology at the first pair of reverse arcs is a natural structural cutoff for a standard directed graph filtration. The exact death threshold and uniform integral filling complete the low-degree persistence information missing from the strongest cited interval-circulant theorem. The closed description provides an explicit analytic benchmark for persistent directed networks, not an arbitrary parameter slice or a known-table recomputation. The result does not assert higher homology or a broad classification of all circulants.

## Closest coverage and limitations
The prethreshold case is explicitly prior knowledge at field level, whereas the postthreshold integral vanishing and displayed death-chain are new within the examined literature. The complete classification is limited to GLMY homology in degrees zero and one for forward interval connection sets on cyclic vertex groups; not all digraph path-homology representations are classified. The finite checker is not a proof of unrestricted parameters.
