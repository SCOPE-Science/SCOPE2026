For whole-disk analytic Bergman spaces weighted by \(|z|^{2\beta}(1-|z|^2)^m\) with \(\beta\ge0\), every integer \(m\le3\) yields a zero-free kernel, but the first transition occurs at \(m=4\): precisely two simple scalar zeros appear when \(\beta>1\). They enter transversally at \(\beta=1\), with squared-modulus derivative \(-49/360\).
