The optimal rank-\((j-1)\) approximation error of the \(n\)-th iterate of a strict affine Fock-space composition operator, after subtracting its fixed-point evaluation limit, is asymptotic to \(|a|^{jn}\) times an explicit Fock-jet constant. The constant involves a truncated exponential sum and differs from the corresponding singular-value law for the uncorrected composition operator.
