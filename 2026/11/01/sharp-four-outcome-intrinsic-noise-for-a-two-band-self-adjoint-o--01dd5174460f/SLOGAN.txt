An unbiased four-outcome measurement of any self-adjoint observable with spectrum \([-1,-a]\cup[a,1]\) has minimal intrinsic-noise norm exactly \((2-\sqrt{1+3a^2})^2/9\). The unique optimal outcome set is \(\{-1,-s_a,s_a,1\}\), with \(s_a=(2\sqrt{1+3a^2}-1)/3\); noncommuting positive effects cannot improve the bound.
