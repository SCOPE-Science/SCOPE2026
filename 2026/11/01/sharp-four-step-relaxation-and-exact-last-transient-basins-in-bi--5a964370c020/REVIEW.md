# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The closed-neighborhood convention is explicit and matches the inspected primary source. The induced update on each shore is exactly one of zero, one, retain, or complement, so the seven-comparand quotient preserves every labelled configuration for all positive integer thresholds. The exhaustive 80-order, 56-cut argument proves the universal time-six/time-four identity, and an independently programmed full-state evolution verifies 4,884,036 finite cases. The last-layer criterion and binomial formula follow from the exact symbolic exceptions. Tie handling and the distinction between equal population counts and different actual patterns are addressed in the proof. Computation is a finite exact proof over all abstract comparison classes, not a graph-size extrapolation.

The earlier general period-two theorem and the model definition are explicitly credited. Searches for equivalent statements, alternative terminology and bipartite phase diagrams found no dominating result. Published parity-automaton and majority-network results have different local rules and are not implications of this claim. Unindexed equivalents remain possible.
