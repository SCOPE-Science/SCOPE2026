For rank-two Gaussian Fock Toeplitz localization, the disk's complete nonradial area-preserving shape Hessian is explicitly diagonal in Fourier modes: first harmonics are translation-null, every higher mode strictly decreases the spectral sum, and the sharp least-stable mode is quadratic, with coefficient \(e^{-x}x(2x+3)/((x+2)(x+3))\). This is a sharp local quantitative refinement of Abreu's established global Ky Fan disk inequality.
