Small fourth-power norm distortion of an arbitrary complex linear map \(\ell_4^d\to\ell_4^n\) forces proximity to a genuine linear isometry at an explicit target-dimension-independent rate \(O_d(\varepsilon^{1/4})\). A two-to-three-coordinate perturbation proves the exponent \(1/4\) is sharp even when approximants may be restricted isometries.
