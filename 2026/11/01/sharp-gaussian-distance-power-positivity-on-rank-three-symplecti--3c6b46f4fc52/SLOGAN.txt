For every prime power \(q\ge2\), the radial Gaussian on rank-three symplectic dual polar graph distance powers is positive semidefinite at every positive scale exactly for \(0\le p\le\alpha_q\), where \(q^2(3^{\alpha_q}-2^{\alpha_q})=1\). At the sharp endpoint the centered powered-distance matrix loses exactly \(q^6+q^4+q^2\) ranks, so the minimum squared-Euclidean embedding dimension collapses to \(q(q^2+1)^2\).
