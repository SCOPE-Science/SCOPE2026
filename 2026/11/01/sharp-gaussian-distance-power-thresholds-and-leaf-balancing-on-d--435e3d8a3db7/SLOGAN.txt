For every double-star tree, the sharp exponent for universal Gaussian-kernel positivity is the unique zero of an explicit two-by-two spectral obstruction. With the total number of leaves fixed, balancing them between the two hubs strictly improves the sharp exponent; the balanced critical equation is \(3^p=1+(1+1/m)2^p\), with \(p=1+2/[m(3\log3-2\log2)]+O(m^{-2})\).
