For unit-edge rooted trees with \(m\ge2\) hubs and \(k\ge2\) leaves per hub, the all-scales Gaussian distance-power positivity threshold is the unique root of an explicit two-by-two determinant. It strictly decreases in each branching count, has a unique global-symmetry endpoint null mode, and approaches \(1\) with sharp and different \(1/m\) and \(1/k\) constants.
