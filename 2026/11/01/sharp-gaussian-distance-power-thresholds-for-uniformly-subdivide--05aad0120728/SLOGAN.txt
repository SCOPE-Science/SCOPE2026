For a star in which every edge is subdivided once, universal Gaussian distance-power positivity has a unique sharp exponent determined by an explicit two-by-two branch-symmetry equation; the endpoint loses exactly one Euclidean embedding dimension. The critical exponent decreases strictly with the number of arms and has the sharp law \(p_m=1+c/m+O(m^{-2})\), where \(c\approx1.247636634086\), strictly below the ordinary-star coefficient.
