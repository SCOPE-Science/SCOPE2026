For any two cliques joined by one bridge, the exact generalized-roundness exponent of their unit shortest-path metric is the unique root of an explicit two-by-two spectral equation, with a one-dimensional critical equality space except for the four-vertex path. The exponent decreases strictly as either clique grows and converges, when both grow, to \(\log2/\log(3/2)\), with a sharp first-order size correction.
