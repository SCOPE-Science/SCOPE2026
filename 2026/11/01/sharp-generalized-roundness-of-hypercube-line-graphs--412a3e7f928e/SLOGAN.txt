For the line graph of the binary \(n\)-cube, the sharp power making all powered shortest-path distances conditionally negative definite is the unique \(p_n\) satisfying \(\sum_{h=0}^{n-2}\binom{n-2}{h}((h+2)^{p_n}-(h+1)^{p_n})=1\). This critical exponent decreases exponentially, \(p_n\sim n/2^{n-1}\), with an exactly \((n-1)\)-dimensional equality space and a sharp Euclidean embedding-dimension drop.
