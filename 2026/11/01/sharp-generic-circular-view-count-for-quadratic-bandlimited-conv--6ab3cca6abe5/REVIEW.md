# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The result is reconstructed from the exact support-function definition and the trace-free normalization. The disk criterion follows by separating angular modes \(0,1,2\). The nonstandard rank-two separation lemma is proved by comparing compressions through eigenvectors and explicitly constructing an orthogonal plane that detects any nonaffine pair of diagonal eigendata. Greedy rank dropping constructs \(\lceil(n(n+1)-2)/4\rceil\) successful planes in every dimension. Analytic minors justify the generic statement, while the codomain dimension and positive-definite curvature matrix give universal minimality. Exact modular ranks for dimensions \(3\) through \(12\) agree with the general proof but do not replace it. Risk: the analytic proof must be read as an unrestricted exact-arithmetic argument, not extrapolation from the finite tests.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority
The primary full text arXiv:2609.27192v1 proves rigidity under **all** planar projection symmetry tests; it neither quantifies generic finite experiments nor establishes minimality in the quadratic harmonic class. Kiderlen's 2004 projection-sum injectivity results aggregate all projected bodies instead of checking circle shape on individual finite planes. Earlier indexed three-dimensional cubic-shadow results give a fourth-view test at a different harmonic bandwidth and do not imply the new formula. Targeted semantic database searches for quadratic restrictions, scalar compressions, finite circular views and exact counts found no covering statement. Risk: equivalent finite-dimensional matrix-measurement results could be embedded in older matrix tomography or poorly indexed work; priority is not established by search failure alone.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The sharp number of finite observations for identifying a sphere in a natural bounded-bandwidth convex-geometric model is a well-motivated tomography question. The result is complete for every ambient dimension and has a meaningful obstruction: the rank-two separation lemma rules out degeneracies not excluded by dimension counting. It distinguishes minimality for **every** smaller view family from success for **generic** optimal-size families and produces explicit smooth nonball examples at the bound.
