For threefold-symmetric planar shadows of cubic constant-width bodies, the unique symmetry-center field is \(-\varepsilon\nabla_{S^2}H/4\), and no single ambient point projects to all the centers when the harmonic cubic is nonzero. For the canonical \(H(u)=u_1u_2u_3\), the best possible uniform mismatch is exactly \(|\varepsilon|/8\), attained uniquely by the origin; the shadow centers also have seven zero planes, six maximal-displacement planes, and exact mean-square displacement \(\varepsilon^2/140\).
