The risk-sensitive multiplicative-weights game map has an exact global invertibility threshold: for \(k<1/(1+cp_1)\), two fold lines emerge sharply at \(a=4(1-k)/(1-k(1+cp_1))\), opening a three-preimage region with width proportional to the \(3/2\) power of the threshold excess. A three-to-one example exists entirely within the invariant belief-probability rectangle, while above the belief-adjustment threshold the map is globally one-to-one regardless of learning intensity.
