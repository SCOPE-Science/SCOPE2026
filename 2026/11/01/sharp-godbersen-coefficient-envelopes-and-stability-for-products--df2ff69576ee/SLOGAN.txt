For every Cartesian product of \(m\) planar convex bodies, all reflected mixed volumes have a complete sharp coefficientwise envelope governed only by their planar Rogers--Shephard ratios. The exact best global affine deficit coefficient is \(([t^k](1+4t+t^2)^m-\binom{2m}{k})/m\), with triangle and centrally symmetric products attaining its two endpoints; the maximal central Godbersen ratio decays as \(\sqrt{3\pi m/2}(3/8)^m\).
