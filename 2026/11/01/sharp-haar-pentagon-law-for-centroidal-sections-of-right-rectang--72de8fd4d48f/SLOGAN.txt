For a Haar-random plane through the volume centroid of a right rectangular pyramid, the section is almost surely quadrilateral or pentagonal, with the exact pentagon probability \(\frac4\pi[\arcsin(2a/\sqrt{4a^2+4b^2+h^2})+\arcsin(2b/\sqrt{4a^2+4b^2+h^2})+\arcsin(h/\sqrt{4a^2+4b^2+h^2})]-2\), where \(a,b\) are full base side lengths and \(h\) is height. The sharp maximum \(12\arcsin(1/\sqrt3)/\pi-2\) is attained uniquely, up to scale, by square bases with \(h=2a\); the infimum zero is unattained.
