The Hadamard product of any two normalized bounded-turning functions belongs to the holomorphic-generator filtration \(\mathcal A_\beta\) exactly when \(\beta\ge(3/4-\log2)/(\pi^2/12-\log2)\). Below that threshold, the exact universal radius is the unique zero of an explicit positive-kernel integral, with sharpness witnessed by the same logarithmic extremizer at every radius.
