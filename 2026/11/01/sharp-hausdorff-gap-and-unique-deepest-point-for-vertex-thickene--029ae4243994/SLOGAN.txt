For every regular Euclidean \(d\)-simplex of side length \(\ell\), the Hausdorff gap between the union of its vertex-anchored \(\lambda\)-dilates and the full Minkowski convex hull is exactly \(\ell(d-\lambda)_+/\sqrt{2d(d+1)}\). Before the sharp threshold \(\lambda=d\), the enlarged simplex centroid is the unique deepest uncovered point, with a strict quantitative bound for every other point.
