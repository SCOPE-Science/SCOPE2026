# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every m,n>=2 and 0<u,v<=2, normalize least pair stretch to 1. Hilbert variance identities yield C²>=VA+VB+||centroidA-centroidB||²>=L. Orthogonal regular simplices of side lengths u,v attain C=max(1,sqrt L); a perpendicular centroid offset sqrt(1-L) appears exactly when L<1. In the L>1 equality case every intracluster distance is minimally stretched, every cross distance maximally stretched, and centroids coincide; subtracting four squared cross distances proves orthogonality. For L<=1 zero distortion forces isometric distances, and the same orthogonality argument gives the unique placement. Affine dimension m+n-2 versus m+n-1 follows. The split-graph specialization L=5/2-2/m-1/(2n)>1 follows algebraically. No finite check is used for universal quantifiers.
