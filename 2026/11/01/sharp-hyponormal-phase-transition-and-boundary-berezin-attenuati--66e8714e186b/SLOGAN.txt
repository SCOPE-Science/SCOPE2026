The phase-twisted Bergman Toeplitz operator \(T_{z^k(1-|z|^2)^{i\tau}}\) is hyponormal exactly when \(\tau^2\le k\); equivalently its norm equals its essential norm. At the sharp boundary \(\tau=\sqrt{k}\), the boundary Berezin modulus divided by essential norm tends to zero as \(k\to\infty\), showing extreme boundary-kernel attenuation within a hyponormal Toeplitz family.
