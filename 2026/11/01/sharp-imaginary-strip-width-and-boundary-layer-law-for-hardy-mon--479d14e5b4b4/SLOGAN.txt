For every nonlinear Hardy monomial composition symbol \(a z^m\) with \(m\ge2\), the exact imaginary Berezin-range half-width is determined by one unique hyperbolic critical point, and all attaining kernels are classified. As \(|a|\uparrow1\), the compact-operator widths approach the nonattained isometric width \(1/(m+1)\) with a sharp square-root deficit and complete boundary-extremizer localization.
