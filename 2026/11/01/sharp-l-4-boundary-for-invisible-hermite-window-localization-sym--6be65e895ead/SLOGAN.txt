For every true-polyanalytic Fock order \(k\ge2\), bounded Hermite-window localization symbols are uniquely determined by their Toeplitz operator when they also lie in unweighted \(L^p(\mathbb C)\) for \(1\le p\le4\). The cutoff is sharp: for each \(p>4\), a nonzero Bessel-wave symbol annihilates the operator, and its critical fourth-power mass grows with an exact logarithmic coefficient.
