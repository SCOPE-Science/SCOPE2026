For continuous piecewise-affine splines on a unit-edge complete-split metric graph \(\overline{K_m}\vee K_k\), \(m,k\ge2\), the exact arclength-\(L^2\) projector norm is \(\frac{7m^2+26mk+24k^2-23m-44k+20}{3(m+2k-2)(m+2k-1)}\). Exactly the independent-class vertices norm the projector, and the norm exceeds \(2\) precisely when \(m\ge4\), independently of clique size.
