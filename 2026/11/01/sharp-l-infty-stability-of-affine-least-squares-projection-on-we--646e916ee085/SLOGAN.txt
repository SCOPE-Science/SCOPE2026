For continuous edgewise-affine least-squares approximation on any weighted metric star, the sharp \(L^\infty\) amplification is \((7+3q_*)/[3(1+q_*)]\), where \(q_*\) is the smallest fraction of total edge mass. The least-mass leaf is always worst, equal edge masses uniquely minimize amplification at fixed degree, and four or more arms force an operator norm above two.
