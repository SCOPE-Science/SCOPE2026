The nonattaining atomic hyperplane projection of Kania–Lewicki has an exact minimum norm under a Lipschitz correction budget, with a sharp finite feasibility threshold. Its excess over the unconstrained projection constant decays stretched-exponentially but retains a nonconstant geometric-lattice phase; for the original dyadic example the normalized liminf and limsup are \(2\) and \(4/(e\log 2)\).
