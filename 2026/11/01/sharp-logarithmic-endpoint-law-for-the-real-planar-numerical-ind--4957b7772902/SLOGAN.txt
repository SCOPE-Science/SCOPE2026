The real two-dimensional \(\ell_p\) numerical index approaches its polyhedral value \(1\) with a sharp logarithmic boundary layer: at large \(p\), its deficit is \((\log(2p)+1)/p-(\log^2(2p)+1)/(2p^2)+O(\log^3p/p^3)\). The maximizing hyperbolic coordinate has a matching two-term expansion, and the numerical-index profile is strictly convex in \(\eta=|1-2/p|\).
