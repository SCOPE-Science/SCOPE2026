On a finite magnetic cycle, the sharp real \(\ell^1\)-to-difference-\(\ell^2\) constant is governed by an exact rational function of the holonomy cosine, while the sharp complex constant is the reciprocal magnetic spectral gap. Every non-antiperiodic twist has a unique real parabolic extremal ray and a unique complex Fourier-wave ray; the previously known antiperiodic boundary has many real sign-domain-wall extremizers.
