The quasihyperbolic complement of every nonempty compact convex obstacle in \(\mathbb R^n\), \(n\ge3\), is globally \(\mathrm{CAT}(1)\), improving the unrestricted-domain \(\mathrm{CAT}(2)\) bound, and \(1\) is the sharp constant even for round balls. For smooth uniformly convex obstacles, logarithmic distance from the obstacle is concave along every quasihyperbolic geodesic and excludes all closed local geodesics.
