For every integer \(\Delta\ge3\), the exact largest Crank–Nicolson half-step guaranteeing positivity on **every** finite connected unit-weight graph of maximum degree at most \(\Delta\) is \((2\Delta-1)/(2\Delta(\Delta-1))\). The bound is strictly safe on each individual finite graph and is sharp as the center of finite balls in the \(\Delta\)-regular tree approaches the infinite-tree return threshold.
