For a rank-one complex \(2\)-partial isometry, the canonical reflexive generalized inverse becomes Moore–Penrose under one equivalent Hilbert inner product exactly when \(\|T\|^2<2\); above this sharp boundary no such metric exists. Away from two degenerate values, the minimum admissible metric condition number is exactly \(2(2-s)/(3-2s)\), where \(s=|\operatorname{tr}T|^2\), and it diverges at the boundary.
