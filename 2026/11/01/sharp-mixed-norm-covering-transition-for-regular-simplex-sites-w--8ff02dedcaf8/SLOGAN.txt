For the mixed Euclidean–axial \(\ell_p\) norm, the \(d\) translates centered at regular-simplex vertices are universally covered by two axial translates precisely when \(p\ge 2\log2/\log(2d/(d-1))\). Below this sharp threshold, explicit configurations have a missing positive-volume region despite the same truncated Voronoi nerve, and the nerve-restricted inclusion–exclusion error changes sign with dimension parity.
