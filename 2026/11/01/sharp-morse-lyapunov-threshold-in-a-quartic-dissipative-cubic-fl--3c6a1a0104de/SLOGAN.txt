For a globally asymptotically stable smooth homogeneous vector field of degree greater than one, a smooth strict Morse Lyapunov function exists exactly when some positive quadratic form has a nonpositive Lie derivative. The quartic-dissipative cubic flow \(\dot x=-x^3+\kappa y^3\), \(\dot y=-y^3-\kappa x^3\) stays globally attracting for every real \(\kappa\), yet admits a smooth Morse certificate exactly when \(|\kappa|\leq2\sqrt2\).
