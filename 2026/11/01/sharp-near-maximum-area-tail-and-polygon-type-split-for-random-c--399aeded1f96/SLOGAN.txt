For Haar-random central sections of \([-1,1]^3\), the probability of area within \(\varepsilon\) of the sharp maximum \(4\sqrt2\) is \(3(\pi+4)\varepsilon^2/(16\pi\sqrt2)+O(\varepsilon^3)\). Among such nearly maximizing sections, the proportions of quadrilaterals and hexagons tend to \(2/(\pi+4)\) and \((\pi+2)/(\pi+4)\), with a complete piecewise-homogeneous limit law for the rescaled normal direction.
