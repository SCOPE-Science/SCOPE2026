The four-generator Cayley metric on the 27-element Heisenberg group has sharp negative-type exponent \(p_*=0.677622399811669\ldots\), the unique root of \(2\cdot4^p+2\cdot2^p-3\cdot3^p-2=0\). Exactly four abelian character directions become flat at the threshold, reducing the squared-distance Euclidean embedding dimension from 26 to 22; the associated exponential-distance kernel is positive definite for every positive bandwidth precisely up to this exponent.
