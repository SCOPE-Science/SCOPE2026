For power-tent windows \(g_p(t)=(1-|t|)_+^p\), every noninteger separation of two integer-frequency modulation cosets is a Gabor frame, with exact optimal bounds \(A=4^{1-p}(1-|\cos\pi\delta|)\) and \(B=2\). Half-integer separation uniquely optimizes conditioning, whose exact minimum \(2\cdot4^{p-1}\) exposes an exponential localization penalty across all real \(p\ge1\).
