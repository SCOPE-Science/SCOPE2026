For the canonical quartic lemniscates \(\{|z^4+iA|<\sqrt{1+A^2}\}\), the outward normal at \(z=1\) re-enters the domain exactly when \(A>11.6114237715\ldots\), with the sharp threshold specified by a unique algebraic root. The return interval has two different large-parameter endpoint scales, \(A^{-1}\) and \(A^{-4/5}\), each with an explicit second term.
