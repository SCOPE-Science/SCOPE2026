A lacunary one-circle compression of the seven-variable Hinrichs–Vybíral counterexample has a sharp odd-base cutoff: its specified eight-point kernel satisfies the conjectured matrix lower bound strictly for all odd bases \(3\le q\le23\), but violates it for every odd \(q\ge25\). Exact integer interval certificates bridge the finite cases, while a uniform \(1040/q^2\) error bound proves the infinite tail. The same sharp cutoff persists after a fixed constant admixture making the kernel strictly positive at every point.
