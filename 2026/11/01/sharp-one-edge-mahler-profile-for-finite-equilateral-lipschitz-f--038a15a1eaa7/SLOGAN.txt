For a real Lipschitz-free space over an \(N\)-point equilateral metric with one distance shortened to \(r\in(0,1]\), the exact Mahler product is a strictly concave quadratic in \(r\). For \(N\ge4\), its unique optimum occurs at \(r=(N-1)/(2(N-2))\), and the maximal gain over the equilateral value tends to \(9/8\).
