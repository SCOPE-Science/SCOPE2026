# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any m>=3 and equal radius r>0 disks centered (with repetition allowed) on the radius-R circle, partial probe-circle coverage equals Σ_j min(g_j,2 alpha(rho)), where alpha(rho)=arccos((R²+rho²-r²)/(2Rrho)) and Σg_j=2pi. This immediately proves equal-gap regular centers maximize every nonnegative radial integral. Equality is all pairwise disjoint centers for r<R sin(pi/m), and uniquely regular centers at and above that threshold because a strict gap inequality appears on positive-measure radii. Exact radial-ray integration reproduces three area branches for 0<r<=Rs, Rs<r<R and r>=R. At first tangency, two-disk lens asymptotic (4/3)sqrt(r)(2r-d)^(3/2) yields deficit coefficient and 3/2-power negative-gap cusp; above tangency, twice differentiating exact gap area gives positive quadratic H in two regimes, continuous at r=R. Parallel-body asymptotics follow from Steiner expansion. Independent radial quadrature tested 28 (m,r/R) configurations against all exact area branches, maximum absolute error 6.73e-8; checks corroborate but do not prove the universal coverage and cusp.

The key angular truncation principle resembles prior radial coverage theorems for regular POLYGON unions, but the actual objects here are congruent DISK unions with centers on a circle. Published circular-gap area laws on disk INTERSECTIONS have the opposite set operation.
