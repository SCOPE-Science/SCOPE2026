For \(m\ge3\) equal-radius disks whose centers lie on a fixed circle, equally spaced centers maximize union area at every radius, but become the **unique** optimizers only at and above the exact threshold \(r=R\sin(\pi/m)\). Their maximum area is explicit in three regimes, and the loss under angular perturbations changes from flat to a sharp \(3/2\)-power cusp at first tangency and to quadratic stability above it.
