For synchronous strict-neighbor majority on three-row rook graphs \(K_3\square K_n\), every trajectory enters a fixed point or two-cycle in at most three steps when \(n\) is odd, but the sharp bound jumps to four for every even \(n\ge6\). An explicit eight-column-type multinomial formula gives every first-entry/least-period count for all \(n\ge2\).
