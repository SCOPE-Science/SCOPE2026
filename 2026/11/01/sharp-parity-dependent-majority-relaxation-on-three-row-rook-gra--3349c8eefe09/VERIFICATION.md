The two SMT-LIB queries are generated by `scripts/verify_symbolic.py`, and their unsatisfiability was checked using the system Z3 library. They quantify over **all** positive integer parameters \(k\), all nonnegative column-type multiplicities whose sum is the corresponding strip width, and all six-step trajectories under the exact threshold rule. `unsat` rules out a failure of the two universal iterate identities; it is not a bounded-size experiment. The source generator exposes all constraints and can be checked with any sound solver of quantifier-free linear integer arithmetic. Solver-independence and a fully handwritten elimination proof have not been performed.

`python3 scripts/verify_direct.py` separately evolves all labelled initial states for strip widths 2 through 6 with a vertex-level routine, for a total of 299,584 states. The output matches `scripts/count_profiles.py`. For all \(n\), the counting algorithm uses exact multinomial arithmetic on \(\binom{n+7}{7}\) profiles and derives the first periodic entry and least period from complete state words. The manually parameterized sharpness witnesses are in the proof. These computations are consistency checks and do not substitute for the unbounded Presburger result.

The underlying identity uses an explicitly specified tie-retention convention; exchanging update conventions invalidates the inference. The symbolic checker depends on a separately installed Z3-compatible integer arithmetic engine. This evidence has not been independently audited.
