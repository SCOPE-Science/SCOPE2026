For nonnegative unimodal data on a finite path, the centered Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator preserves the peak and satisfies the conjectured sharp global \(1\)-variation bound. When both endpoints vanish, fixing the interior peak at vertex \(k\) gives the sharper optimal factor \(1-1/(2k)-1/(2(n-k+1))\), attained only by a point mass, with an explicit stability remainder.
