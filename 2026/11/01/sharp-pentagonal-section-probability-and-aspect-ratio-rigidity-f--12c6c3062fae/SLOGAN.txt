Among all right rectangular pyramids, a uniformly oriented section through the volume centroid is pentagonal with probability at most \(6\arccos(1/3)/\pi-2\), with equality only for a square base whose apex height is four times its base half-side. An exact all-aspect-ratio law, a unique height optimum and a sharp two-mode quadratic deficit establish this rigidity; the square-case numerical constant was already known through random-shadow duality.
