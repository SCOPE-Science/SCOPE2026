For \(n\) independent uniform points in the orthogonal spherical lune, the expected geodesic perimeter of their spherical convex hull approaches the lune's perimeter \(2\pi\) with sharp deficit \(\frac\pi2 B(3/4,1/2)n^{-1/2}\). A great-circle Crofton formula and a Gaussian-orthant identity give the exact finite-sample perimeter integral and identify the degenerate boundary layer responsible for the beta-function constant.
