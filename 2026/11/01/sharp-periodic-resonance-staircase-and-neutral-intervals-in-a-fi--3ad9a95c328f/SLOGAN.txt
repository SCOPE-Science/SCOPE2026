A four-branch map without fixed points has an exact finite staircase of one-inner-block periodic cycles: for its experimentally studied fivefold branch, only period-four and period-six members can attract, with sharp parameter windows \(0<\alpha<1/5\) and \(1/4<\alpha<1/\sqrt5\). At the two stability boundaries the return map becomes the identity on an explicit nonempty interval, creating a continuum of neutral periodic orbits.
