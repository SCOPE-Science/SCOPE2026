For two unequal synchronized clusters of a finite discrete-time Kuramoto–Sakaguchi system at every strictly intermediate phase lag \(0<\beta<\pi/2\), a unique coupling threshold exactly separates one-step phase-slip-free motion from possible crossing of the synchronization cut. Below threshold all nonexceptional two-cluster separations converge exponentially to synchrony, and immediately above threshold the first-slip interval opens with an explicit square-root law.
