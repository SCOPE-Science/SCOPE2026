The Wigner-corrected isoperimetric deficit of every equiangular convex \(2n\)-gon controls the maximal deviation of its opposite-facet widths with the optimal constant \(16n^2\tan^2(\pi/(2n))\). All nontrivial sharp configurations of the width data are cyclic translates and multiples of an explicit discrete Green kernel; the constants approach \(4\pi^2\) as \(n\to\infty\).
