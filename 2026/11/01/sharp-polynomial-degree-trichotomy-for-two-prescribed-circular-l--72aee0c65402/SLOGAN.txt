For two specified concentric hyperbolic circular limit cycles with arbitrary positive physical periods and prescribed transverse Floquet multipliers, rotation-equivariant polynomial flows with positive angular speed exist without other periodic orbits exactly when the two stability types alternate. Their sharp least planar polynomial degree is \(5\), \(7\), or \(9\), determined by explicit inequalities between the normalized logarithmic multipliers; a degree-\(9\) example proves that alternating stability alone does not guarantee degree \(7\).
