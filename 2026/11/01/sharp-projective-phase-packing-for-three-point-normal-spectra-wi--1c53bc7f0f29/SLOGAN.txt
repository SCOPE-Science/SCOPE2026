For a normal three-point spectrum with fixed eigenvalue moduli \(1,r,r\), the global maximum of the squared \(L^2\)-Cartesian numerical radius occurs **only** when the two weaker projective phases are \(\pm\arccos(r/2)\) relative to the strongest phase. The maximum has a closed arcsine formula and rises from the single-mode benchmark by \(4r^3/(3\pi)+O(r^5)\) as \(r\downarrow0\).
