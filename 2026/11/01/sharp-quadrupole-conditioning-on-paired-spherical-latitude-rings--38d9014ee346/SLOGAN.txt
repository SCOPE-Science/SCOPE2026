Sampling all five exact-degree-two spherical harmonics on two mirror-symmetric \(m\)-point latitude rings, \(m\ge5\), has a three-level Gram spectrum independent of relative ring rotation. Its unique optimal squared height is \((6-\sqrt{21})/15\), with sharp squared condition number \((5+\sqrt{21})/4\); only the height squared \(1/3\) loses rank.
