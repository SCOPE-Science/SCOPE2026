For the canonical discrete Mellin defect \(D=C^2-W\), the exact Schatten threshold is \(D\in\mathcal S_p\iff p>1/2\), with a sharp weak \(\mathcal S_{1/2}\) endpoint. Its Fredholm determinant is \(\sin(\pi\sqrt z)/(\pi\sqrt z)\), recovering the simple eigenvalues \(j^{-2}\); the trace-class status and trace \(\zeta(2)\) were previously known.
