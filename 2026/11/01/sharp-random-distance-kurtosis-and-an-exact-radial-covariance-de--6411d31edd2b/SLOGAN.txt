For any Euclidean convex body, the normalized fourth moment of the distance between two independent uniform interior points is at least \((d+2)(d+3)/(d(d+4))\), with equality only for balls. The entire excess is exactly the sum of a weighted radial-variance defect and a covariance-anisotropy defect, giving a sharp quantitative rigidity identity in every dimension \(d\ge2\).
