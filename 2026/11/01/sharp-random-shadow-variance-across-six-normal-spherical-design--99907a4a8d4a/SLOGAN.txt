Among equal-facet-area Minkowski polytopes built from exactly six spherical \(2\)-design normals, the orientation variance of planar shadow area increases strictly with the twist between two equilateral normal triples. It ranges sharply from approximately \(0.030694143418\) for the aligned triangular-bipyramid configuration to \(\pi(16-5\pi)/9\) for the cube, with no other equality cases.
