For the three-dimensional reciprocal-sine map, the resonances \(b=\pm c\) are exactly the parameters causing an everywhere-singular Jacobian: every forward-defined orbit collapses onto \(y=z\) after one iteration, and every invariant probability becomes singular in three-dimensional space. Away from these two surfaces the map has full-rank Jacobian almost everywhere; on the resonant surfaces it still possesses infinitely many distinct fixed points.
