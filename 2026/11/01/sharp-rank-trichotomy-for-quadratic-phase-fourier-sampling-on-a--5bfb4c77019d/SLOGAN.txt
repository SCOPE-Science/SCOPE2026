For every odd prime \(p\), the quadratic-phase finite Fourier matrix \(\bigl(e^{2\pi i(as t+b s^2t^2)/p}\bigr)_{s,t\in\mathbb F_p}\) is invertible for **every** quadratic coefficient \(b\) exactly when its linear coefficient \(a\) is nonzero. When \(a=0\), its rank drops sharply to \((p+1)/2\) if \(b\ne0\), and to one otherwise, giving a complete Fourier self-sampling phase diagram for the prime-field parabola.
