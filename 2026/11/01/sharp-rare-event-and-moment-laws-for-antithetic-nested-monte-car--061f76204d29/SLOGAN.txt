For a nested positive-part payoff, antithetic corrections are positive with probability asymptotic to \(2\sigma f(0)\sqrt{2/\pi}\,n^{-1/2}\), while their \(r\)-th raw moments have explicit sharp \(n^{-(r+1)/2}\) constants. This forces a \(\sqrt n\)-scale outer pilot just to detect a nonzero correction and gives an exact finite-sample half-normal-difference law in the Gaussian case.
