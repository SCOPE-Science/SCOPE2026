For a Haar-random central section of the \(d\)-cube, the rare event of attaining the minimal \(2^{d-1}\) vertices has probability \(\frac{d2^{d-1}\Gamma(d/2)}{e\pi^{d/2}\Gamma(d)}(1+\frac7{2(d-1)}+O(d^{-2}))\). Conditional on that event, the rescaled non-dominant normal coordinates converge jointly to independent unit exponential variables, while the normal concentrates at the sharp angular scale \(\sqrt{2/(d-1)}\).
