For a zero-mean heterogeneous Stuart–Landau triangle with growth rates \((52,172,292)\), the full transverse stability transition under reactive coupling is determined exactly by one positive root of a cubic in \(\gamma^2\). Below \(|\gamma|\approx10.58941958\) the phase-locked state has precisely two unstable directions; above it the same rotating polygon is exponentially orbitally attracting, with a simple imaginary-pair crossing at the boundary.
