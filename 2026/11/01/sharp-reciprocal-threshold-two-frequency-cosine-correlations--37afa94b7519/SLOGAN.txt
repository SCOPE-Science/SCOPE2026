At every reciprocal threshold \(\alpha_m=\cos(2\pi/m)\) for integer \(m\ge5\), the smallest probability that two distinct integer-frequency cosines are both above \(\alpha_m\) or both below \(-\alpha_m\) is exactly \(4/(mL_m)\), where \(L_m\) is the largest odd integer below \(m\). All minimizers are integer dilates of \(\{1,L_m\}\), with the additional orbit \(\{1,9\}\) only at \(m=5\); the law also holds for every strictly monotone antipodal waveform at the corresponding level arc.
