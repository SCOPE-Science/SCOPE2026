Every ellipse has a sharp, explicit largest Euclidean regular pentagon, even allowing arbitrary translations: its circumradius is \(b/\min\{U((b/a)^2),V((b/a)^2)\}\) for the two explicit functions in the theorem. At one algebraic aspect ratio, the maximizing orientation switches from a three-contact minor-axis configuration to a four-contact major-axis configuration; the optimal area fraction also uniquely encodes the ellipse's aspect ratio.
