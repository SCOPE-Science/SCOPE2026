# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a nondegenerate tetrahedron, affine reduction S=conv(0,e1,e2,e3) is legitimate. Its four barycentric directional derivatives sum to zero. Independently reconstructed the orthant formula vol(aS+b(−S))=(a³+9a²b+9ab²+b³)/6 and the 14 facet projection contributions, which combine into G_v(a,b)=T(a²+b²)/2+Hab. Segment extrusion slices give vol K=(1+3tT)/6 and vol(K−K)=10/3+2t(T+H), hence ratio=(20+4q(1+H/T))/(1+q), q=3tT. The zero-sum quadruple sign patterns give T≤H≤2T; equal balanced pair ±s gives lower endpoint, one-versus-three gives upper, continuous w=(1,1,−u,−(2−u)) realizes every intermediate ratio. Three-dimensional mixed-volume expansion gives vol(K−K)=2vol K+6V(K,K,−K). An independent script formed hulls of 150 random tetrahedral extrusions and all pairwise vertex differences; volume ratios agreed with the closed form to 1.1e−14. Equality cases include zero-coordinate boundaries.

## originality

PASS

The global Rogers–Shephard and Godbersen inequalities and sharp simplex equality cases do not determine the complete fixed-q, direction-sensitive spectrum of a simplex plus segment. The 2026 simplex-cap truncation profile changes K by removing a cap, not by Minkowski sum with [0,v]. Closest current Godbersen papers were read at their main results, but older polytope mixed-volume facet formulas could contain an equivalent parameterization.

## value

PASS

The directional spectrum is a natural sharp two-sided classification of a canonical first non-simplex Minkowski perturbation, at fixed affine-invariant volume increase. The balanced-edge midpoint minimum and one-versus-three maximum are genuine geometric information beyond a universal upper bound.
