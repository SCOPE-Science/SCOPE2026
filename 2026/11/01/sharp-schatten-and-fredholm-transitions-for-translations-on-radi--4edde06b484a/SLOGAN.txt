On radial power Fock Hilbert spaces, every nonzero translation has a compact difference from the identity for \(0<m<1\), with exact singular-value decay \(s_n(T_b-I)\sim |b|(\alpha m/2)^{1/m}n^{-(1-m)/m}\) and a sharp Schatten cutoff. At \(m=1\), the same translation instead exhibits an exponentially folded Fredholm essential spectrum, integer-valued logarithmic-preimage indices and exact complete polynomial essential norms.
