For invertible operators with modulus \(cI\) plus a compact self-adjoint perturbation, the exact least Schatten-\(p\) cost of leaving at most \(m\) singular directions below \(c\) is the \(\ell_p\) tail of the modulus's negative spectral deficits, and one spectral clipping correction simultaneously minimizes every singular value. Repairability of reducing-subspace norm attainment is equivalent to Schatten summability of that negative tail, even for noncommuting corrections; infinite-rank defects admit arbitrarily small ideal repairs but no finite-rank repairs.
