For neutral periodic compositions of \(T_a(x)=((x+a)/(1+ax))^2\), the Cayley-squared distance has an exact linear-plus-logarithmic law with coefficients determined by ordered prefix products. Sorting each period from smaller to larger parameters is uniquely optimal for boundary approach at completed periods, and a grouped-versus-alternating example yields an exponentially growing coefficient ratio despite identical zero Lyapunov exponents.
