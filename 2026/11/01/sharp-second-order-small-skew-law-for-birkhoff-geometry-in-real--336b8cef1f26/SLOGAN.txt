For the newly defined Birkhoff skew-isosceles constant on real \(\ell_4^2\), the small-parameter law is \(D_t^B=(4/27)^{1/4}+t/\sqrt3+(4/27)^{1/4}(1-13\sqrt3/18)t^2+O(t^3)\). The maximizing Birkhoff pair is unique up to signed-coordinate isometries for small positive \(t\), and its coordinate ratio moves only quadratically: \(r_t=2-\sqrt3+(3\sqrt3-6)t^2+O(t^3)\).
