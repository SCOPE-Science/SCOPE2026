The ordinary three-cut complex of the fourth power of a cycle is void for \(3\le n\le10\), nonshellable for \(11\le n\le16\), and shellable for every \(n\ge17\). Four complete boundary shelling certificates close the previously uncovered range and give \(\Delta_3(C_n^4)\simeq\bigvee^{(n^2-17n+2)/2}S^{n-4}\) for all \(n\ge17\).
