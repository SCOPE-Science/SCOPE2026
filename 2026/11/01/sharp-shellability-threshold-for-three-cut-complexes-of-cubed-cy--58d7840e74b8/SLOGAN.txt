The ordinary three-cut complex of the cubed cycle \(C_n^3\) is shellable exactly when \(n\ge13\), settling the sharp cutoff for this cycle-power family. Complete facet-order certificates at \(n=13,14\) give \(S^9\) and \(\bigvee^8 S^{10}\); exact mod-two homology obstructs shellability at \(9\le n\le12\), and a published theorem covers all \(n\ge15\).
