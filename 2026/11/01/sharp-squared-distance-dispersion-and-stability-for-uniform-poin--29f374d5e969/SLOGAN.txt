For two independent uniform points in any nondegenerate Euclidean \(d\)-simplex, the normalized fourth distance moment lies sharply between \((d+1)(d+2)(d+7)/[d(d+3)(d+4)]\) and the unattained supremum \(6(d+1)^3/[d(d+3)(d+4)]\), with equality at the lower endpoint exactly for regular simplices. An exact sum-of-squares identity quantifies covariance anisotropy and squared vertex-radius imbalance, and every value in the resulting half-open interval occurs.
