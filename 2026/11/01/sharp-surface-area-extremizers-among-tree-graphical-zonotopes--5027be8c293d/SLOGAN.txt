For graphical zonotopes of unweighted trees on \(n\ge4\) vertices, the intrinsic Euclidean surface area is \(2\sum_e\sqrt{s_e(n-s_e)}\). Stars uniquely minimize this area and paths uniquely maximize it, despite all the zonotopes having the same volume and mean width; the maximal-to-minimal area ratio grows like \((\pi/8)\sqrt n\).
