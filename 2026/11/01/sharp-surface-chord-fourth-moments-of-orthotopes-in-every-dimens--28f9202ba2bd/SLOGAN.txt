For every \(d\ge4\), the fourth-to-squared-second Euclidean distance moment of two surface-area-uniform points on an orthotope has exact range \([1+1/d+2(d-1)/(5(d+2)^2),\,12/5)\); cubes uniquely attain the minimum. An explicit anisotropy remainder gives optimal square-root inverse stability, and the sharp lower endpoint decreases strictly with dimension.
