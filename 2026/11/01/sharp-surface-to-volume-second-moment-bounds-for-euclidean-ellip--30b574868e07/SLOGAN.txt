For every full-dimensional Euclidean ellipsoid in dimension \(d\ge2\), the ratio of mean squared distances between two surface-uniform points and two volume-uniform points lies sharply between \((d+2)/(d+1)\) and \((d+2)/d\), with the upper equality characterizing balls and the lower constant approached by flattening. A sharp all-direction covariance comparison follows from a Gaussian representation of Euclidean surface measure.
