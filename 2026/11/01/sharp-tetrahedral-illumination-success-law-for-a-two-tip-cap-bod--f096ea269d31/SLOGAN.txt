For a centrally symmetric three-dimensional ball with two opposite spikes, a uniformly rotated regular tetrahedron of illumination directions has zero success probability at and beyond spike radius \(\sqrt3\), but its success probability just below that threshold grows sharply as \(\frac{4\sqrt2}{\pi3^{3/4}}(\sqrt3-r)^{3/2}\). On the entire interval \(\sqrt{3/2}\le r<\sqrt3\), that probability has an exact elementary spherical-lens formula.
