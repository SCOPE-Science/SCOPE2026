In a three-stage exponential stormwater wash-off model with continuous fresh pollutant supply, the ordering that maximizes time-integrated exported mass switches sharply at initial-mass-to-supply ratio \(z_c=(1-e^{-ch})/c\), while the minimizing order switches at \(z_a=(1-e^{-ah})/a\), independently of the intermediate rate. Two wash-off stages cannot exhibit a supply-driven ordering reversal; three are the first possibility.
