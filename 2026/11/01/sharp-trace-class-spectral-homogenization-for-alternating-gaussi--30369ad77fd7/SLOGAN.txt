The analytic Gaussian Toeplitz operator for infinitely many alternating unit-width annuli is a trace-class perturbation of \(\tfrac12 I\), with exact trace excess \(\tfrac14\). Its eigenvalues oscillate infinitely often across \(\tfrac12\) at the sharp exponential scale \((1+\pi^2)^{-(n+1)/2}\), despite the underlying radial symbol never settling to a limit.
