For unequal linear output weights on the skew Hilbert pair, the exact \(\ell_p\)-normalized constant has a collinear/Hölder branch below \(p=2\) and an oblique-angle/equal-coefficient branch above \(p=2\), with a strict entropy-controlled corner at the transition. All maximizers are classified for every \(0<a<1\) and \(1\leq p\leq\infty\).
