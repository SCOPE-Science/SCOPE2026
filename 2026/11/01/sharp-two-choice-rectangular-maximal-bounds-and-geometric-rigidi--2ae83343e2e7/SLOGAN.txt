For the two-choice rectangular maximal average on \(\ell^2(\mathbb Z^d)\), the exact operator norm is \((2/(2-\theta))^{d/2}\), so the ordinary two-point average gives \((4/3)^{d/2}\). In one dimension a nonnegative exact deficit formula forces every extremizer to be a scalar multiple and translate of a one-sided geometric sequence; its tensor products prove sharpness in all dimensions.
