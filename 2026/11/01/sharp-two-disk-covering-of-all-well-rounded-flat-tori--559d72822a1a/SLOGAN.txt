Every well-rounded flat two-torus has exact unrestricted two-geodesic-disk covering radius \(L/2\), where \(L\) is its systole: a half-diagonal period shift gives an optimal pair with exactly two farthest torus points. Its sharp optimal disk-density profile \(\pi/(2\sin\theta)\) ranges from \(\pi/2\) at the square lattice to \(\pi/\sqrt3\) at the hexagonal lattice, and the associated inversion pillowcase has the same Chebyshev radius.
