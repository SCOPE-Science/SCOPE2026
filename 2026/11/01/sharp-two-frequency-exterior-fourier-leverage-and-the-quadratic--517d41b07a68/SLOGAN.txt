Every two-term Fourier sum with distinct real frequencies has squared exterior leverage strictly below \((1+3x^2)/2\) for \(|x|\ge1\), and this frequency-uniform constant is sharp only in the collision limit. At any positive frequency gap \(d\), the exact optimal constant is \((1-\operatorname{sinc}(d)\cos(dx))/(1-\operatorname{sinc}(d)^2)\), with explicit maximizing sums and a strictly positive quadratic collision defect.
