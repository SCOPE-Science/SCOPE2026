For endpoint-preserving three-point medians on an \(n\)-vertex path, the exact two-input weighted-variation Lipschitz factor is the maximum of explicit three-edge/one-edge weight ratios, despite classical one-input variation smoothing. Optimizing all positive variation weights uniquely gives \(1+2\cos(\pi/(n-1))\) for \(n\ge3\), attained by a half-step sine profile; hence the filter is never two-input nonexpansive for \(n\ge4\).
