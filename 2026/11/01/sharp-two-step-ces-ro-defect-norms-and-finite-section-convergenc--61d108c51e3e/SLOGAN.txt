For the finite prefix Cesàro average \(C_n\), the exact maximum-norm amplification of the twice-applied defect \((I-C_n)^2\) has a finite harmonic-threshold formula and a uniquely maximizing last row. It increases strictly to \(2+2e^{-2}\), with sharp finite-size deficit \((5-e^{-2})/n+O(n^{-2})\).
