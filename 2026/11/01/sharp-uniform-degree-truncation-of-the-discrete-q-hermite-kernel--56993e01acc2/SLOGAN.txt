The exact unnormalized discrete \(q\)-Hermite kernel has a uniformly convergent infinite-degree limit, but its best uniform degree-\(N\) error decays only as \((q^2;q^2)_\infty^4/(eN)\). The worst truncation error concentrates in a boundary layer at distance \(1/(2N)+O_q(N^{-2})\) from either endpoint.
