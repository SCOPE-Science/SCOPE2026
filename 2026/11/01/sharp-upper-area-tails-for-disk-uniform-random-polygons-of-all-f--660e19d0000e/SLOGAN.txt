For any fixed \(n\ge4\), the area deficit of the convex hull of \(n\) independent area-uniform points in a disk has a sharp upper-end probability law with exponent \((3n-1)/2\) and an explicit gamma-function coefficient. This higher-order family extends the previously known triangle case. The exponent consists of \(n\) linear boundary defects plus \(n-1\) quadratic angular defects, in contrast to circle-boundary sampling.
