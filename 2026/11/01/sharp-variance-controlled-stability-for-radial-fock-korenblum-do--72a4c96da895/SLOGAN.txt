For every radial Hilbert Fock weight, exterior Korenblum domination has a sharp global quadratic deficit controlled by the relative radial variance \(\delta_W=1-m_1^2/(m_0m_2)\). The exact coefficient is attained by degree-two Möbius pairs and collapses at rate \(d^2/(3+d^2)\) for thin annuli, despite the unchanged sharp domination radius.
