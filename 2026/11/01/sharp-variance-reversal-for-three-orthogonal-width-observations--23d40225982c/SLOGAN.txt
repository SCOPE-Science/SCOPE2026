Sampling the widths of a rectangular box in three random perpendicular directions reduces the variance of the three-view mean-width estimator for elongated boxes but increases it for cubes. A sharp aspect-ratio invariant gives the exact sign transition, and the attainable orthogonal-to-independent variance ratio is \(16/\pi-5<R\le1.66771623397995\ldots\), with the upper equality unique to cubes.
