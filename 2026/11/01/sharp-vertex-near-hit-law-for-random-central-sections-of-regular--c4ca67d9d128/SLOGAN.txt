For a random centroidal hyperplane section of a unit-circumradius regular \(d\)-simplex, the probability that its origin-centered radius squared is within \(\varepsilon\) of one equals \(\sqrt{d(d+1)}\,\mathbb E\max_{j\le d}Z_j\,\varepsilon/\sqrt{2\pi}+O_d(\varepsilon^2)\). The full distribution in dimension two is explicit, and independent random orientations have a sharp exponential best-radius deficit.
