# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim has explicit iid, atomlessness, two-sided tail, and scaling hypotheses. Conditional independence gives the exact product. Outside-origin sites contribute at most \(O(L)\) to its negative logarithm because no exterior site can enter with probability one and the total expected hit count is at most \(L+1\). For interior origins, the log of a sum of the two escape tails equals their smaller cost up to \(\log 2\); the assumed logarithmic-tail limits make the approximation uniform except for a bounded number of boundary sites. Uniform shifted-grid Riemann convergence and an explicit single crossover integral finish the proof. The Gaussian coefficient follows by Mills' formula. 107 finite numerical checks supplement this argument and do not certify infinite quantifiers.

The two most directly relevant inspected primary papers are Flimmel (arXiv:2610.24650v1, §3.1 Proposition 2) and Elboim–Spinka–Yakir (arXiv:2506.16873v1, §2.3 Lemma 2.5). Searches for asymmetry, equivalent Weibull-tail formulations, exact Gaussian void coefficients, and those source identifiers disclosed no complete dominating theorem. Lack of a match is not proof of novelty. A general unlocated large-deviation theorem is a residual priority risk.
