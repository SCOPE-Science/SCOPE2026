For one-dimensional iid perturbed integer lattices with two one-sided logarithmic Weibull tail rates \(c_-\), \(c_+\) and common exponent \(\alpha\), the stationary large-interval void probability has exact decay rate \(c_-c_+/[(\alpha+1)(c_-^{1/\alpha}+c_+^{1/\alpha})^\alpha]\). In the Gaussian case its sharp logarithmic coefficient is \(1/(24\sigma^2)\) on the \(L^3\) scale; asymmetric tails produce a precise left/right escape crossover.
