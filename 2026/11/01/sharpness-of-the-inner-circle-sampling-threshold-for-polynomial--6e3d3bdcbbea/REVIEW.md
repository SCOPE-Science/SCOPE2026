# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The known upper bound is reproduced with an explicit top-Fourier-coefficient cancellation and a zero-count argument; the two-circle phase uniqueness step follows by reflecting the quotient and comparing its two dilation identities. Every lower-bound witness is proved directly by exact norm-squared expansions. The degree-\(2\) degeneracy of the higher-degree construction is not overlooked: a separate quadratic witness handles it. Degree \(1\) is handled independently. The checker confirms finite numerical instances but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority

The upper bound \(2d-1\) is **not new**: it is Lemma 6 in Pohl–Li–Boche (2017). The precise new contribution is the matching necessity for every \(d\) and every inner radius, witnessed by reciprocal polynomials. Inspected Lemmas 5–6, Theorem 7 and conclusions in that paper prove sufficiency but give no such all-degree, all-radius lower-bound construction. Chalendar–Partington (2024) covers related holomorphic full-circle uniqueness, but only its abstract and bibliographic information were accessible here; undiscovered earlier sharpness constructions remain possible. The relevant published-finding corpus searches returned no matching claim; this alone cannot establish uniqueness.
