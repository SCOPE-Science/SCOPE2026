The volume-normalized distance histogram of a Euclidean orthotope has an exact short-range polynomial whose \(d-1\) nontrivial coefficients recover all its side-length ratios; exactly \(d-1\) sufficiently short scalar distance-probability measurements suffice, and fewer generically do not. Its first failure of polynomial continuation occurs at the shortest side, with a sharp \((d+3)/2\)-power onset that detects the multiplicity of shortest sides.
