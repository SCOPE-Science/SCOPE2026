For a binary renewal shift with Dini power-tail potential \(\phi(0^k1\cdots)=-c(k+1)^{-p}\), the second low-temperature pressure correction changes abruptly at the unique \(p_*\approx1.553201524280\). Below and at this exponent, collectively long excursions dominate with a \(\beta e^{-\beta c(p-1)\zeta(p)}\) law; above it, the next short excursion dominates with \(e^{-\beta c(1+2^{-p})}\).
