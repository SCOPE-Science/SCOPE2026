For the complete \(5\times5\) chessboard complex, the shortest integral two-cycles carrying its known \(\mathbb Z/3\) torsion have exactly eight triangles, and exactly \(1,800\) octahedral supports attain this bound. All form one symmetry orbit; each board square lies on \(432\) supports, and an attacking versus nonattacking pair lies on \(54\) versus \(108\).
