# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The signed degree-one chain group is reduced via explicit backtrack boundaries to twisted edge incidence. The allowable two-chain kernel splits by forbidden signed shortcuts; its remaining boundaries span exactly balanced triangles and quadrilaterals. In a cactus their circulations have disjoint support, proving both the stated ranks and that no later short-cycle relation can kill an earlier degree-one class. The automorphism trace follows from three natural short exact sequences and explicit signed component/cycle eigenlines. Finite verification independently reconstructs the signed path complexes and checks 272 high-girth cases, 320 general short-cycle cases, 544 cactus filtration stages, and 14 symmetry instances. Those finite calculations are checks, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (conditional on presently accessible literature)

The closest source, Bai–Li–Yau [1], defines signed GLMY homology and already handles balanced components and a particular two-unbalanced-triangle example. Kempton–Münch–Yau [2] gives the unsigned short-cycle-space quotient and therefore substantially anticipates the bridge lemma. Chen [3] gives the classical signed flow space. None of the compared statements supplies the full signed-cactus persistence-injectivity theorem together with the sign-preserving automorphism character, and the cited example alone does not imply the all-parameter group representation. The bridge lemma itself is not claimed to be independently original. Remaining risk: the cactus classification could be implicit in classical gain-graph cycle-space methods or unindexed subsequent work; no uniqueness certification is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result identifies a natural class of graphs where a recently introduced signed path-homology theory has an exact homological and functorial classification. It gives a sharp persistence nondeath law and an explicit residual representation that distinguish unbalanced cycles from positive short cycles. The friendship-graph standard representation is a uniform, natural family rather than a single numerical benchmark.

## Scope and limitations

Real coefficients only, simple cactus graphs, bidirected same-signed edges, and fixed-vertex sign-preserving inclusion filtrations. Degree two and above are unclassified. The published signed-double-cover and cycle-space ingredients are prior work.
