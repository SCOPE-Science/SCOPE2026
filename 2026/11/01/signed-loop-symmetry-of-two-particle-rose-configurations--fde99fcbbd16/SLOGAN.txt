For two unordered distinct particles on a rose with \(m\ge2\) petals, the entire signed petal-permutation action on rational first homology is determined by the numbers of fixed orientation-preserved petals, fixed orientation-reversed petals, and transposed petal pairs. Its character is \(1+(3a^2+2ab-b^2-3a-3b)/2-r\); ordinary petal permutations fix a two-dimensional homology subspace, whereas adding independent petal reversals eliminates every invariant class.
