For rooted depth-two trees with at least \(r\ge3\) branches, the higher independence complex retracts up to homotopy onto its root deletion and is a wedge of \(\prod_i\binom{s_i-1}{r-1}\) spheres of dimension \(rm-1\). Its full integral branch-automorphism homology is a parity-signed tensor of exterior powers of leaf augmentation lattices; for uniform trees this produces an irreducible rational wreath-product representation.
