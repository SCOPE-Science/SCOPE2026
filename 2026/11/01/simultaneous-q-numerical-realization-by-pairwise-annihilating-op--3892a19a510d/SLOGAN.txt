Every nonempty norm-compact, absolutely convex \(K\subset C(\Omega)\) is simultaneously realized as the family of joint \(q\)-numerical ranges \(\mathcal W_q(F)=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-|q|^2}}2K\) for one norm-continuous, norm-preserving family with \(F(s)F(t)=0\) for all parameters. The entire closed \(q\)-disk, not merely the unimodular endpoint, is realized by the same explicit square-zero construction.
