For Euclidean-motion-invariant random chords of the unit \(\ell_p\) ball, every fixed \(1<p<2\) yields a sharp near-diameter probability exponent \(2(d-1)/p\) with an explicit gamma-function coefficient. Conditional on a nearly maximal chord, its scaled transverse endpoint coordinates become uniform in an \(\ell_p\) ball, giving a universal Dirichlet endpoint-deficit law.
