# Six-square census for four-robust clique complexes with unrestricted square gluing

## Finding
For every simple graph \(G\) constructible from a four-cycle by at most five additional four-cycles glued either along an existing edge or along an existing two-edge path, the four-robust clique complex has the integral homotopy type
\[\mathsf{Cliq}_4(G)\simeq\bigvee^{\beta(G)}S^5.\]
Here \(\mathsf{Cliq}_4(G)=\{S\subseteq V(G):\alpha(G[S])<4\}\), \(\alpha\) is the independent-set number, and \(\beta(G)\ge0\) is the unique number in the accompanying exact census.csv row for the graph's graph6 isomorphism class. The zero-sphere-count wedge is a point.

Exhaustive isomorphism-class counts for square-sequence lengths \(1,2,3,4,5,6\) are respectively \(1,2,7,29,179,1410\). The corresponding numbers of classes with positive \(\beta\) are \(0,0,3,22,169,1396\), and counts of contractible cases are \(1,2,4,7,10,14\). Thus all 1,628 classes have been classified; exactly 1,590 have nonzero reduced homology. At six squares the maximum \(\beta\) is 20 and the sum of \(\beta\) over the 1,410 isomorphism classes is 19,210. This is an exhaustive finite-range statement, not a proof for arbitrarily long square sequences.

## Assumptions and scope
All graphs are finite, loopless and simple. A square sequence of length \(s\) starts with a copy of \(C_4\). Each of the following \(s-1\) steps unions an additional \(C_4\) whose intersection with the existing graph is exactly either a single edge (adding two new vertices) or a two-edge path (adding one new vertex). There are no other identifications. Only \(1\le s\le6\) is asserted. Isomorphic graphs appearing through distinct construction sequences are counted once at their unique cycle rank \(s\). The simplicial complex contains the empty face, as usual.

The attached CSV uses graph6 for exact identification up to isomorphism, with columns level, vertices, edges, graph6, beta5, nonempty_faces, matching_pairs, and hasse_incidences. Each row certifies a specific graph; all 1,628 possible isomorphism classes are represented.

## Proof
Every graph generated by these square gluings is bipartite, inductively: the two new vertices of an edge-glued square and the new vertex of a corner-glued square receive the uniquely forced two-coloring. In every bipartite graph, any seven vertices contain at least four in a common independent bipartition class. Thus the four-robust clique complex has no faces with more than six vertices, regardless of graph order.

First enumerate the graph classes inductively. Edge gluing ranges over all existing edges; corner gluing ranges over all unordered pairs of neighbors of every existing vertex. These are exactly all legal identifications with an incoming four-cycle. The first level is \(C_4\); at each step exact graph isomorphism removes repeated children. Graphs at different levels cannot coincide: every gluing increases cycle rank \(|E|-|V|+1\) by one, so the level is intrinsically determined. This produces the class counts stated above; graph6 encodes every representative.

For each representative, enumerate all nonempty subsets of cardinalities 1 through 6. Construct the list of all independent four-subsets from the graph's adjacency relation, and include a candidate face exactly when it contains none of them. This directly implements the definition, with no assumptions about facets. The resulting complete face sets have 2,116,899 nonempty faces across the table, checked per representative and per level.

For each full face set, apply a fixed ordered toggle matching. For vertices in increasing order, pair each still-unpaired face \(\sigma\) not containing the vertex with \(\sigma\cup\{v\}\), whenever both remain unpaired. Each pair differs by exactly one vertex, so it is a valid discrete-Morse pair. The executable constructs **every** nonempty face-to-facet codimension-one incidence, reverses matched edges in the Hasse digraph and topologically sorts the entire digraph; there are 9,103,719 incidences across all 1,628 complexes. The topological sort visits every simplex in each graph, certifying the matching is acyclic for every representative.

For every class this matching leaves precisely one unmatched vertex and no unmatched cells outside dimension five. The number of unmatched five-cells is exactly the CSV field beta5; its level-by-level sum is \(0,0,3,60,1098,19210\). By the discrete-Morse CW theorem, the complex is homotopy equivalent to a CW complex with one zero-cell and \(\beta(G)\) cells of dimension five and no others. All five-cell attaching maps land in a point, hence this CW complex is exactly a wedge of \(\beta(G)\) five-spheres. This establishes the **integral homotopy type**, not merely homology over a field, for every listed class.

## Verification
Run Python 3 with NetworkX 3.x available, then execute `python verify.py` in a directory containing the supplied `verify.py` and `census.csv`. This independently regenerates each graph class, enumerates all faces, rebuilds all matching pairs and full oriented Hasse incidences, checks every topological sort and compares the complete regenerated byte sequence of the CSV with the provided table. A successful complete execution ends with `ALL_VERIFY_OK classes=1628 table_bytes=60908 positive_beta=1590 contractible=38`. No stored matchings or previous computation logs are trusted: matching and acyclicity are computed anew from graph6-level graph generations. `requirements.txt` provides the sole external Python dependency. This finite certificate establishes the stated classification because every allowed graph occurs in its exhaustive, isomorphism-deduplicated enumeration.

## Relationship to prior work
Filakovský, *The Topology of k-Robust Clique Complexes in Grid-like Graphs*, arXiv:2602.11365v1 (first publicly posted February 11, 2026), Definition 8 and Conjecture 5, studies these exact robust clique complexes of square sequences. Its Theorem 2 proves the three-robust case under both gluings and Corollary 3 proves all thresholds under **edge-only** gluing. Conjecture 5 asserts the five-sphere-wedge conclusion for threshold four under unrestricted corner gluing as a special case; this result verifies the complete finite initial range through six squares. The present exhaustive theorem does not follow from its edge-only corollary, since 1,269 six-square classes have fewer than the fourteen vertices required by six edge-only squares.

The Alexander dual relation to total cut complexes and the existing grid-specific results establish motivation but do not supply this exact six-square isomorphism census. Earlier published numerical or homotopy evaluations of selected grids are subsumed as examples, not offered as separate new claims.

## Limitations
Nothing is proved for seven or more squares. The finite census does not itself provide the unknown infinite recurrence or a uniform collapse for arbitrary square-sequence length; it also does not compute automorphism representations. Source inspection and database queries cannot exclude every unindexed equivalent finite census, and independent scientific validation has not been performed.
