# Scientific review: six-step synchronization and rare four-cycles

## Correctness — PASS
The assertion covers every integer \(n\ge3\) and every labelled initial three-coloring of \(K_{2n}\setminus M_n\). The critical reduction is exact: the six unordered matched-pair types determine the update once their counts are capped at two, since only a global blinker count of zero, one or at least two can affect any vertex. The displayed transition table is derived directly from the graph adjacency rule. `check_symbolic.py` independently constructs the same table from vertex-level update logic and checks all \(722\) admissible capped types, including that a four-cycle type is never reached later than time zero. It also verifies the exponential generating-function identities in a formal integer polynomial ring, with no numeric fitting. `check_direct.cpp` separately uses full adjacency on \(597861\) labelled initial configurations. The six-update bound is attained, and the four-cycle's exact least period is checked explicitly. Residual limitation: the exact finite-state lemma is provided as a reproducible program rather than as a printed manual case table; no independent audit has been performed.

## Originality — PASS (historical-priority qualified)
The 2014 firefly rule and tree/path synchronization results are prior work. The 2014 full manuscript was read at its definition, main results and concluding scope discussion; it does not provide a cocktail-party classification. The 2016 full paper concerns finite trees rather than these complete multipartite graphs. Prior results on complete bipartite and crown graphs have different forbidden-edge structures; a result on the identical cocktail-party family concerns Greenberg–Hastings excitation, with a different refractory transition. The new statement is not a relabelling, specialization, or corollary of those claims: it supplies a six-update cutoff, a four-cycle *existence and isolation* classification and seven exact synchronization-layer counts for the inhibitory firefly map on \(K_{2,2,\ldots,2}\). Targeted semantic-record, terminology, graph-alias, primary-source and web literature searches found no equivalent covering statement. Residual risk: unindexed earlier notes or papers may exist, and database retrieval is not a mathematical proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A perfect matching of missing edges is a natural, highly symmetric perturbation of all-to-all coupling. The result proves a uniform sharp synchrony bound despite arbitrarily many oscillators and completely classifies the exceptional asynchronous orbits. The difference between a polynomial-size exactly periodic four-cycle set and an exponentially large synchronizing basin is a sharp, quantitative network-dynamics statement. The exact first-entry distribution exhibits concentration at synchronization time two and gives more information than eventual periodicity or an isolated finite-graph example. The problem has a motivated two-parameter local mechanism (the isolated blinker's exempted partner) and is not merely a renamed version of a known formula.
