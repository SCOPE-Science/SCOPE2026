Buffière–Marchal (arXiv:2303.18045) concerns lattice-sampled convex polygons. The known uniform-direction deficit series and single-spike angular-independent deformation are explicitly excluded from any novelty claim. No examined result implies the conditional equivalence between full scalar limit laws and arbitrary finite angular Fourier power spectra. Exact historical priority remains a risk, especially from general local-alternative results for degenerate \(U\)-statistics and unindexed geometry literature.
