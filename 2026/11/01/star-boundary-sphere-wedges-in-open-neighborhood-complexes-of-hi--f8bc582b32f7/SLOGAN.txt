For every finite connected graph \(H\) of girth at least five and minimum degree at least three, the open-neighborhood complex of its line graph collapses to a graph of star boundaries with homotopy type \(\bigvee^{2|E(H)|-|V(H)|+1+n_3}S^1\vee\bigvee_{v:\deg_H(v)\ge4}S^{\deg_H(v)-2}\), where \(n_3\) counts degree-three vertices. In particular, the projective-plane incidence graph of order three produces \(79\) circles and \(26\) two-spheres, demonstrating natural higher-dimensional topology beyond the existing block-graph and low-degree classifications.
