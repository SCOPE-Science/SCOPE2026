For unit-edge complete bipartite metric networks with a fixed number of vertices, balancing the two parts strictly increases the entire arclength-sampled geodesic-distance distribution in first-order stochastic order, even though the graph becomes more densely connected. The exact CDF yields sharp total-variation and one-Wasserstein gaps between any two partitions; from stars to balanced bicliques, both gaps tend to \(2/3\).
