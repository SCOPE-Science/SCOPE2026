For any noncircular ellipse, an area-maximizing inscribed \(n\)-gon with \(n\ne4\) can always improve its Euclidean perimeter by moving one vertex; the perimeter loss against the unrestricted optimum has an explicit, phase-uniform positive lower bound. Among maximum-area inscribed polygons, independent-vertex perimeter stationarity occurs only for the axis-aligned affine-regular quadrilaterals.
