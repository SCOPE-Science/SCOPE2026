Uniform points in ratio-constrained simplex cores have a deterministic high-dimensional sorted-coordinate profile \(g_s(t)=\frac{1+s}{s}\log(1+s)-\log(1+s-st)\). The associated limiting isotropic-constant ratio increases strictly from \(e/\sqrt{12}\) to \(1\), and the classical trisection core has exact limit \(4\sqrt{1-2\log^2 2}\).
