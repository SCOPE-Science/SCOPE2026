For every convex body, the \(\log\cosh\) transition bodies obtained by choosing different interior origins have a strictly concave volume-to-the-power-\(1/d\) profile and an exact Minkowski inclusion under interpolation of origins. The symmetry center uniquely maximizes this volume for centrally symmetric bodies, and the centroid does so for every simplex; the same bodies interpolate quantitatively between polar \(L_2\) and classical projection geometry.
