For the graph of \(m\) triangles joined at one vertex, the full strong discrete-Morse matching complex is an integral sphere wedge: \(\mathcal{SM}(F_m)\simeq\bigvee^{3^m+2m}S^{m-1}\) for every \(m\geq1\). The key mechanism is a join of four-point sets augmented by \(2m\) cones attached along nullhomotopic joins of two-point sets.
