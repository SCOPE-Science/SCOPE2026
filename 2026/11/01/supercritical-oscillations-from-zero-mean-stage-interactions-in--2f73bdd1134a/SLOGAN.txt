Zero total interspecies stage interaction does not prevent oscillation: a two-species, two-stage community has a sharp supercritical Hopf threshold \(w=2d+2\) and a full-dimensional attracting positive periodic orbit just beyond it. The exact normalized first Lyapunov coefficient is \(-du/[4(u+2)]<0\) for all \(u>2\), \(d>u+2\).
