# Suspended \(A_m\) singularities on odd binary-form quadratic hulls

## Finding
For each integer \(m\ge2\), set \(r=2m+1\) and let \(C_r\subset\mathbb P(\operatorname{Sym}^r\mathbb C^2)\) denote the degree-\(r\) rational normal curve. The quadratic hull
\[
X_m=\mathcal Q(T^{m-1}C_r)
\]
has set-theoretic singular locus exactly \(C_r\). For every \(o\in C_r\), its analytic germ is
\[
(X_m,o)\simeq\bigl(\mathbb A^1\times\{z_1^2+\cdots+z_{2m-3}^2+t^{m+1}=0\},0\bigr).
\]
Its singular subscheme consequently has transverse Jacobian length \(m\) at every point. The transverse hypersurface is a higher-dimensional suspension of the simple \(A_m\) singularity.

## Assumptions and scope
The base field is \(\mathbb C\). A binary form has the normalized expansion \(f=\sum_{i=0}^{r}\binom ri a_i x^{r-i}y^i\). We use the scheme structure generated by every quadratic equation of the osculating variety, not merely the reduced support. The single highest transvectant \((f,f)_{r-1}\in\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbb C^2\) has three coefficients \(q_0,q_1,q_2\) and generates those quadrics: since \(r=2m+1\), the only index above \(m-1\) among nonzero even transvectants is \(m\), with order \(2m=r-1\). The complete-intersection theorem of Massri [1, Theorem 1.1] applies with its parameter \(p=m-1\) and \(N=\binom{r-2p-1}{2}=1\), so \(X_m\) is a three-quadric complete intersection in \(\mathbb P^r\), of dimension \(r-3\) and degree \(8\). The proof below does not assume generic smoothness along any stratum.

## Proof
**1. Global singular support.** Put \(V=\operatorname{Sym}^r\mathbb C^2\). As \(r\) is odd, the top transvectant is a nondegenerate alternating \(SL_2\)-invariant pairing \(\omega\) on \(V\). The quadratic moment map of this symplectic representation is
\[
\mu(f)(\xi)=\tfrac12\omega(\xi f,f),\qquad \xi\in\mathfrak{sl}_2.
\]
Under the equivariant identification \(\mathfrak{sl}_2^*\simeq\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbb C^2\), its three coordinate quadrics span the coefficients of \((f,f)_{r-1}\): the latter is the unique \(\operatorname{Sym}^2\)-summand in \(\operatorname{Sym}^2(V^*)\), and the moment map is a nonzero equivariant map to that summand. Differentiation gives \(d\mu_f(e)(\xi)=\omega(\xi f,e)\). Nondegeneracy of \(\omega\) therefore implies
\[
\operatorname{rank}d\mu_f=\dim(\mathfrak{sl}_2 f).
\]
On the zero fiber, the radial tangent direction is in the kernel. Thus the same rank criterion applies in projective coordinates. Because the scheme is a codimension-three complete intersection, its Jacobian has deficient rank exactly when \(\mathfrak{sl}_2 f\) has dimension less than three. A nonzero semisimple element of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) acts with weights \(r,r-2,\ldots,-r\), none zero since \(r\) is odd, so its kernel is zero. A nonzero nilpotent element has kernel the one-dimensional highest-weight line, consisting of scalar pure powers after change of variables. Hence a nonzero \(f\) has a nonzero infinitesimal stabilizer precisely when \(f=\ell^r\). Conversely such powers belong to the zero fiber, and have infinitesimal stabilizer. Thus
\[
\operatorname{Sing}(X_m)_{\mathrm{red}}=C_r.
\]

**2. Two pivot quadrics and the transverse Hessian.** Work at \(o=[x^r]\) in the chart \(a_0=1\). The unipotent \(SL_2\)-orbit gives a local analytic coordinate \(s=a_1\) and a transverse slice \(a_1=0\); this is an analytic product by the equivariant local orbit map. Normalize the three quadrics, up to common nonzero factors, as
\[
\begin{aligned}
q_0&=\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}(-1)^k\binom{r-1}{k}a_k a_{r-1-k},\\
q_1&=\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}(-1)^k\binom{r-1}{k}(a_k a_{r-k}+a_{k+1}a_{r-1-k}),\\
q_2&=\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}(-1)^k\binom{r-1}{k}a_{k+1}a_{r-k}.
\end{aligned}
\]
These are the derivative formula for the order-\((r-1)\) transvectant in divided-power coordinates. On the slice, \(q_0\) has linear part \(2a_{r-1}\), while \(q_1\) has linear part \(2a_r\). Eliminate these two variables by the analytic implicit-function theorem (in fact, polynomial pivot formulas). The residual local equation is \(F(t,z)=0\), with \(t=a_2\) and \(z=(a_3,\ldots,a_{r-2})\), so there are \(r-4=2m-3\) transverse \(z\)-variables.

Assign weights \(\operatorname{wt}(a_i)=i\). Each \(q_j\) and its pivot expressions are weight homogeneous; the residual \(F\) has weight \(r+1\). Its quadratic part in \(z\) at \(t=0\) is
\[
Q(z)=\sum_{i=3}^{r-2}(-1)^{i-1}\binom{r-1}{i-1}a_i a_{r+1-i}.
\]
Every antidiagonal coefficient is nonzero, so the symmetric Hessian of \(Q\) is invertible. Indeed, in \(q_2\) the only terms involving the eliminated variable \(a_{r-1}\) pair it with \(a_2\); they vanish on \(t=0\), while all terms involving \(a_r\) pair it with \(a_1=0\). Thus elimination cannot alter \(Q\).

**3. Nonzero order of the remaining one-variable germ.** By the holomorphic Morse lemma with parameter \(t\), there exist local analytic transverse coordinates giving \(F=Q'(z)+h(t)\) with \(Q'\) a nondegenerate quadratic form and \(h(0)=0\). More precisely, the equation \(\partial F/\partial z=0\) has a unique analytic solution \(z=z_*(t)\) by the invertible Hessian. The positive-weight \(\mathbb C^*\)-action preserves that equation; uniqueness makes this critical section equivariant. Hence the critical value \(h(t)=F(t,z_*(t))\) has weight \(r+1=2m+2\), while \(t\) has weight two. Its power series therefore has exactly the possible form \(h(t)=c\,t^{m+1}\).

If \(c=0\), the singular set of the residual hypersurface contains the whole curve \(\{z=0\}\) in the Morse coordinates. Along with the free orbit coordinate \(s\), this yields a two-dimensional germ of singular points of \(X_m\), contradicting Step 1, where the entire singular support is the one-dimensional rational normal curve. Thus \(c\ne0\). Rescaling \(t\) and diagonalizing \(Q'\) gives exactly \(\sum z_i^2+t^{m+1}=0\), times the smooth \(s\)-axis. The \(SL_2\)-action is transitive on \(C_r\), so this local type holds at every point.

**4. Singular-scheme transverse length.** For the stated complete-intersection analytic normal form, after eliminating the two smooth pivot directions the Jacobian ideal is generated by \(2z_1,\ldots,2z_{2m-3},(m+1)t^m\). Its local quotient is \(\mathbb C\{s,t\}/(t^m)\). It has length \(m\) in every transverse slice \(s=s_0\). This describes the local singular scheme, not just its reduced set.

## Verification
The accompanying `verify_binary_moment_stdlib.py` uses only the Python standard library and exact rational arithmetic. It directly reconstructs the three transvectant quadrics from the displayed formulas, performs the weighted slice pivot elimination and computes the unique critical value for \(r=5,7,9,11,13\). The respective nonzero critical coefficients are \(24\), \(405\), \(8960\), \(984375/4\), and \(8083152\). It checks the nonzero antidiagonal coefficients and gives `VERIFY_OK`. This finite check validates low-degree algebra but is **not** a proof for all \(m\); the universal claim is proved in Steps 1–4.

## Relationship to prior work
Massri's 2026 complete-intersection theorem [1] supplies the essential codimension-three premise and a general tangent-cone description, but does not classify the global singular locus or its exact transverse analytic type and Jacobian length. Massri's earlier 2013 result [2, Theorem 12] already proves codimension three, degree eight, and states that the rational normal curve consists of singular points; this previously known inclusion is **not** claimed as new. Its statement does not exclude other singularities or identify a transverse \(A_m\) type. Massri's 2026 revision of the general stable-range work [3] determines reduced quadratic hulls in another parameter range, not these local analytic singularities.

## Limitations
Only odd degrees \(r\ge5\) and complex-analytic germs are treated; no higher-codimension even-degree hulls are classified. The model is a local product, not an assertion about a globally trivial tubular neighborhood. The Jacobian length concerns the singular subscheme in a transverse slice, not the degree of a globally defined thickening. An implicit equivalent classification in unlocated literature cannot be completely excluded. Independent external verification has not been carried out.
