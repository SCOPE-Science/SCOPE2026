For every odd binary degree \(r=2m+1\ge5\), the three-quadric hull of the \((m-1)\)-osculating rational normal curve has singular support exactly the rational normal curve. At each point its transverse singularity is a suspended \(A_m\) germ \(\sum_{i=1}^{2m-3}z_i^2+t^{m+1}=0\), with singular-scheme transverse length \(m\).
