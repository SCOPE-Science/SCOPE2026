The four-simplex has exactly \(3234\) relabeling classes of optimal discrete Morse matchings, refining the known total of \(380125\) labelled matchings. The complete stabilizer spectrum distinguishes \(3107\) asymmetric classes, \(114\) transposition-symmetric classes, \(5\) double-transposition classes, \(7\) classes with \(S_3\) symmetry, and one class with full \(S_4\) symmetry about its critical vertex.
