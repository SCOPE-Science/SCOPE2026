In a multiharmonic kicked rotor, \(W-1\) distinct fixed points undergo a synchronized supercritical flip at \(K=8/W\), while the origin flips earlier at \(K=8/[W(W+1)]\). All newborn local two-cycles are linearly elliptic; remarkably, the first-order two-step return trace is \(2-4W(K-8/W)\) at every one of the \(W-1\) simultaneous resonances, despite their different square-root opening amplitudes.
