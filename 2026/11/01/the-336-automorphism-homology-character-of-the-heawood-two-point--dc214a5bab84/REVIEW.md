## Originality — PASS (publication-priority caveat)
Ko–Park (2011), Theorem 3.16, already determines the **nonequivariant** degree-one group for 3-connected nonplanar graphs, including this graph; its rank and the elementary cubical Euler characteristic also determine the degree-two Betti number. Those results are **not claimed as discoveries**. Barnett–Farber (2009) treat the ordered two-particle graph space; they supply general foundations but no full-Heawood-group character of unordered second homology. Kurlin (2009/2012) supplies braid constructions and subdivisions rather than this representation. The result adds signed duality traces, explicit complete character on 336 symmetries, its multiplicity-free character inner product, and a rationally acyclic quotient. A published calculation of the **ordered mod-two** Heawood configuration space cannot determine this rational full-group representation because the spaces and coefficient fields differ. Other indexed Heawood symmetry work concerns independence or matching complexes, not the unordered two-particle space. The closest available publication texts were inspected for the relevant mathematical assertions; no covering equivalence was found. A general formula capable of implying these eight values or an unindexed exact computation remains a residual originality risk. Search absence alone is never treated as proof of priority.

## Value — PASS
The Heawood graph is the canonical incidence graph of the Fano plane and smallest cubic graph of girth six, with an exceptional 336-element full automorphism group including point–line dualities. Determining its complete rational second-homology character is a natural invariant rather than a selected arbitrary numerical slice. The character exhibits two distinct complex irreducible constituents, in contrast to the previously known rank fourteen; all positive-degree invariants vanish, distinguishing the full symmetry quotient rationally from the substantial homology upstairs. The calculation is exact and reconstructible, and the duality rows carry additional data unavailable from the collineation-only action. It is a meaningful finite structural classification even without a graph-family theorem.
