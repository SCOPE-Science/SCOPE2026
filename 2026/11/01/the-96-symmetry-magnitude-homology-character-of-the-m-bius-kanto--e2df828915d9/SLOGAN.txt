The complete rational \(\operatorname{Aut}(GP(8,3))\)-character on \(\operatorname{MH}_{3,4}\) takes values \(304\), \(4\), \(-4\), and \(0\), on \(1\), \(8\), \(12\), and \(75\) automorphisms respectively. Consequently, exactly three independent rational magnitude-homology classes are fixed by the entire symmetry group, a refinement of the previously reported Betti number \(304\).
