The full collineation group \(\operatorname{GL}_4(2)\cong A_8\) acts on the \(77\)-dimensional rational top homology of the binary projective three-space cap complex as the sum of the \(7\)- and \(70\)-dimensional irreducible Specht restrictions \(S^{(7,1)}\oplus S^{(4,3,1)}\). Consequently its quotient by all collineations is rationally acyclic, even though the original complex has large top homology.
