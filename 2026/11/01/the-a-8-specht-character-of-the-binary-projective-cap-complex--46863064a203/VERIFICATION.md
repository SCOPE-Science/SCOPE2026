# Exact reproducibility and its limits

The included scripts are standalone Python 3 programs using only the standard library; each can be executed directly. Their recorded standard output is `CHECKS.txt`. The concrete commands are `python3 verify_integral_morse.py`, `python3 verify_gl4_character.py`, and `python3 verify_a8_specht.py`.

`verify_integral_morse.py` enumerates every subset of the 15 nonzero four-bit vectors, filters the forbidden three-point lines, and obtains 3,048 nonempty cap faces. It constructs 1,484 prescribed matching pairs and verifies acyclicity by a full topological sort of the reversed Hasse graph. Its exact signed integer gradient recursion gives a degree-four Morse boundary with 78 entries, of which 39 are zero, eleven are -2, and 28 are +2. This certifies rational homology concentration without relying solely on finite-field ranks. It also independently corroborates the known integral homology (\(\mathbb Z/2\) in degree three and free rank 77 in degree four), but the integral result is not the novelty claim.

`verify_gl4_character.py` enumerates all 20,160 invertible binary matrices and their fourteen conjugacy classes generated by elementary transvections. For all 20,160 group elements it computes the character through union-of-vertex-orbits inclusion-exclusion. For each of the fourteen representatives it separately computes the signed oriented simplicial fixed-face trace; the methods agree. It checks the total class size and the zero weighted character sum.

`verify_a8_specht.py` checks the even permutation cycle-class sizes and orders of \(A_8\), including the split classes. Its independent Jacobi–Trudi character routine produces rational \(S_8\)-Specht rows of degrees 7 and 70 whose sum equals every computed entry, with integral exact inner-product checks. All three scripts print `ALL_CHECKS_PASS` when their assertions succeed.

These checks establish exact finite combinatorial and representation-theoretic statements for this one complex. They do not independently verify any external paper's complete proof, classify projective cap complexes in higher dimensions, compute integral equivariant lattices, or provide independent mathematical certification.
