The numerical radius of the degree-\(N\) compression of the weighted Bergman shift has the sharp Airy correction \(1-r_N=(\alpha+1)/(2N)+a_1(\alpha+1)^{2/3}/(2N^{4/3})+o(N^{-4/3})\) for every fixed \(\alpha>-1\). Its maximizing polynomial coefficients concentrate in an \(N^{2/3}\)-degree boundary layer converging to the normalized Dirichlet Airy ground state.
