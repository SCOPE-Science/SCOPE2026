For \(n\ge2\) uniform random sites on the unit sphere, the expected total length of all Voronoi interfaces is exactly \(4\pi(n-1)\binom{2n-2}{n-1}/4^{n-1}\). At a point sampled by **annealed expected interface arclength**, the transformed generating-site height and angular chord factor are independent beta variables, giving all finite-sample generator-chord moments and an exponential scaling limit.
