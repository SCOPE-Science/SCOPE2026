For the projective-geometry matroid in every rank \(r\geq2\), the full projective linear group fixes a nonzero top-homology class exactly when \(q\) is even and the coefficient group has \(2\)-torsion. All fixed classes are precisely the constant-coefficient sums of projective bases, giving \(\widetilde H_{r-1}(K_{r,q};A)^G\cong A[2]\) for even \(q\) and zero for odd \(q\).
