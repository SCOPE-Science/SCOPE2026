# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
An–Drummond-Cole–Knudsen's natural reduced chain comparison gives an exactly two-step complex for this tree. Separate left and right edge-difference gradings force any degree-two top cycle with four particles to have bilinear coefficients alternating in both index pairs. Explicit integer cycles form an integral basis, and the signed leaf/hub actions yield the characteristic-dependent invariant line. The standalone checker verifies small instances exactly in characteristics two and five. No finite computation is substituted for the uniform proof.

## Originality — PASS, with a concrete reduction risk
The general reduced complex, integral cutting theorem (ADCK, Proposition 5.22), and toric generators (An–Knudsen, Theorem 1.1) are prior work. They strongly anticipate the nonequivariant rank and geometric cycles, which are not presented as new. The distinct claim is the natural integral signed leaf-permutation module together with the universal characteristic-two fixed homology class and nonexistence over other characteristics; those explicit statements were not located in relevant inspected sources or indexed records. The available general theory makes this a fairly accessible calculation, and an equivalent calculation outside accessible sources remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The result identifies the first top-degree homology possible for a tree with two essential vertices, its integral basis, and a parity-specific global symmetry class invisible to rational homology. This is structural information beyond a scalar rank, for all branch counts, with direct applications to interpreting equivariant graph configuration-space cycles.
