# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The precise vertex set, complete edge list, mask predicates and matching algorithm determine the object without reliance on external software. Two distinct face tests agree on all \(4096\) masks, giving \(424\) nonempty faces and \(72\) forbidden path triples. The embedded certificate contains \(211\) matched codimension-one pairs; the checker reconstructs every pair from the specified deterministic rule and verifies that reversing the \(211\) corresponding arrows in the entire \(1428\)-incidence Hasse graph produces a directed acyclic graph. The only critical cells have dimensions zero and three. Finite discrete-Morse theory therefore supplies a CW complex with precisely these two cells, which must be \(S^3\). This does not rely upon modular Betti numbers as an infinite or integral proof. An exhaustive eight-element group enumeration and signed Lefschetz traces determine \(r_*=-1\), \(s_*=+1\) on the rank-one integral third homology. Boundaries over three finite fields independently corroborate, but do not replace, the homotopy argument.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Chvátal graph is the canonical smallest four-regular triangle-free four-chromatic graph. The parameter-two complex is its first nontrivial enlargement of ordinary independence, a natural graph-complex invariant with a pre-existing, exactly computed comparator on this **same graph**. The fact that the enlargement changes the *complete integral homotopy sphere dimension* from two to three and reverses the specified reflection's orientation character is structural, not a routine numerical consequence. The explicit Morse certificate makes the homotopy claim reproducible, and the integral character gives a nontrivial comparison with the known topological chromatic-bound model. The result makes no unsupported promise of a general classification.
