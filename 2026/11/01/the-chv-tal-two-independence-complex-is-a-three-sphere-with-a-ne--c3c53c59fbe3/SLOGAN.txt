The first higher-independence (\(P_3\)-devoid) complex of the twelve-vertex Chvátal graph has integral homotopy type \(S^3\), rather than the \(S^2\) of its ordinary independence complex. Its full dihedral action on top homology has orientation character \((r^as^b)_*=(-1)^a\), so the specified reflection acts trivially, in contrast with the ordinary-independence sphere.
