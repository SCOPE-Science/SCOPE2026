# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The graph is explicitly the 16-vertex folded five-cube. A complete face enumeration agrees with an independent 160-wedge exclusion test. The 2,142 nonempty faces admit 1,065 acyclic matching pairs, checked by a full 9,280-arrow Hasse DAG topological sort. The critical cells have dimensions \(0,3,4\) in counts \(1,5,6\); the exact signed integer \(5\times6\) Morse matrix has a first-five-column minor equal to \(-1\). Because the 3-skeleton is a wedge of 3-spheres, that primitive attaching matrix yields a contractible subcomplex after the first five 4-cells and leaves a single 4-sphere. A direct independent mod-two boundary-rank calculation agrees. The automorphism group is proved to have 1,920 elements; every signed Lefschetz trace was computed and agrees with translation-trivial, direction-permutation sign action. These are finite exhaustive calculations and a topological argument, not finite extrapolation to a graph family.

**Originality — PASS, qualified by access risk.** Conant–Thistlethwaite arXiv:0808.0739 (full accessible HTML article, hypergraph independence and cubical theta examples) and Deshpande–Singh arXiv:2001.05448 (accessible PDF text, definitions, full identified graph-family theorem list and examples) give the framework but not the Clebsch two-independence calculation. The related published Clebsch records located in indexed searches concern ordinary independence, ordinary matching or the Lovász neighborhood complex; these are inequivalent face conditions. A previous recorded ordinary-independence calculation on this same graph does not imply the higher-independence homotopy or its distinct orientation character. Exact-claim and alias searches found no covering result, but searches cannot establish universal novelty. Relevant published-record texts outside the accessible source summaries were not all available; equivalent unindexed work is a residual priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The Clebsch graph is the unique strongly regular graph with parameters \((16,5,0,2)\), a canonical folded cube with exceptional five-direction symmetry. The first higher-independence construction here is a natural forbidden-wedge hypergraph complex; its sphere homotopy type is a nontrivial structural determination despite 7-dimensional simplices. The computed integral sign character and rationally acyclic symmetry quotient supply information not implied by the ordinary independence or neighborhood complex. This is a motivated exact finite boundary case, not an arbitrary sliced parameter or a known-table recomputation.

**Closest literature and limits.** Hypergraph independence and theta complexes [Conant–Thistlethwaite, 2008]; higher independence classifications for complete multipartite, whiskered, path, cycle and m-ary tree graphs [Deshpande–Singh, 2020]; Clebsch graph automorphism description [DistanceRegular.org]. Some nearest indexed results were available only as published summaries, not entire original text. No claim is made about all folded cubes, integral orbit-space torsion, or equivariant homotopy equivalence.
