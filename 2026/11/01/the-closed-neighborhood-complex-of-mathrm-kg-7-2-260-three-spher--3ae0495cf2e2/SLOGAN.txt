The closed-neighborhood complex of the 21-vertex Kneser graph \(\mathrm{KG}(7,2)\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{260}S^3\). Its degree-three rational \(S_7\)-representation has nine Specht constituents, including two distinct irreducibles of multiplicity two, unlike the already known multiplicity-free six-ground-element case.
