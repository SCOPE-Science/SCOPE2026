# Scientific review: the co-Heawood two-independence complex

## Correctness — PASS
The graph is defined by explicit seven-point line sets and their bipartite nonincidences, so no external graph labeling is needed. The complete nonempty face poset has 1115 elements. A deterministic sequential-element acyclic matching has 553 pairs and one critical vertex plus eight critical four-cells. The full 4298-edge oriented Hasse graph is explicitly checked for acyclicity; the critical-cell count gives the integral wedge of eight four-spheres by discrete Morse theory. An independent boundary calculation reproduces the mod-two Betti numbers. Every one of the 336 automorphisms is generated from the exhaustive seven-point line-preserving permutations and the duality involution; full signed Lefschetz enumeration yields the character and exact self-inner-product one. No finite-to-infinite inference is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The co-Heawood graph is the canonical point-line nonincidence graph of the Fano plane, a connected distance-regular graph of valency four; its two-independence complex is a natural forbidden-short-path construction, not a parameter-engineered example. The nonpure complex has facets in dimensions four, five and six yet retracts in homotopy to eight four-spheres. The complete automorphism character strengthens the numerical homotopy classification: its top rational homology is absolutely irreducible and has no invariants. These data provide a concrete symmetry-sensitive benchmark for projective-incidence graph topology. No extrapolated classification, equivariant homotopy equivalence, or larger research consequence is claimed.
