The complete shortest-path Vietoris–Rips filtration of the \(30\)-vertex icosidodecahedral graph has types \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\), \(S^2\), and \(S^{14}\) at its three nontrivial stages. Explicitly verified elementary collapses \(\operatorname{VR}_{\le3}(G)\searrow\operatorname{VR}_{\le2}(G)\searrow S^2\) show that its unique two-dimensional persistence class survives intact from radius \(2\) to radius \(4\).
