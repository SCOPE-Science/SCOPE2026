The complete ordinary cut-complex filtration of the Petersen graph has exactly one nonempty mixed-dimensional homotopy stage: \(\Delta_5(P)\simeq S^3\vee\bigvee^5S^4\). The five-cut stage is pure four-dimensional yet nonshellable; all the other nonvoid stages are sphere bouquets of a single dimension, and the eight-cut stage onward is void.
