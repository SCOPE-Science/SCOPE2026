Three unordered particles on the Petersen graph have a 25-dimensional rational degree-two homology module decomposing as \(S^{(2,1,1,1)}\oplus S^{(3,2)}\oplus2S^{(2,2,1)}\oplus S^{(3,1,1)}\) under the full \(S_5\) symmetry. The symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic, although contractibility and integral torsion are not established.
