Every tetrahedron has the same exact reflected mixed-volume and difference-body profile under equal barycentric vertex caps: the dimension-three Rogers--Shephard ratio runs from \(20\) at either tetrahedral end to \(8\) at the unique octahedral midpoint, strictly decreasing and then increasing. The complete rational profile detects a quadratic minimum that is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\) at the change of facet combinatorics.
