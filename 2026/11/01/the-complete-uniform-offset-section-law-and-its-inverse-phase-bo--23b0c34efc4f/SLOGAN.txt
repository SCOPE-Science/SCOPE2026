For uniformly offset parallel planes of any fixed orientation slicing a three-dimensional parallelotope, the number of section sides follows one of exactly all distributions on \(\{3,4,5,6\}\) with mean \(4\); every such distribution is obtainable by varying the normal in any fixed parallelotope. The law uniquely recovers the sorted relative generator projections whenever the hexagon probability is positive and at one exceptional zero-hexagon endpoint, while the rest of the zero-hexagon boundary has an exactly characterized continuum of indistinguishable projection profiles. The variance has sharp range \([0,2]\).
