The seven-dimensional rational sixth homology of the Coxeter graph independence complex is an **absolutely irreducible** module for all 336 graph automorphisms: the sign twist of the augmentation representation on the eight Sylow-seven subgroups. The full-symmetry and index-two even-symmetry quotients are rationally acyclic, while quotienting by a Sylow-seven subgroup preserves exactly one sixth-homology class.
