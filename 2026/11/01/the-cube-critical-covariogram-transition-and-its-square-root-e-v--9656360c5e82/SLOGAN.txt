For the volume-one cube, the critical smooth-covariogram level sets converge—despite the cube's corners—to the exact Orlicz body \(\{y:\sum_i\log\cosh y_i\le1\}\). Its volume relative to the radius-\(\sqrt2\) Euclidean ball tends to \(\sqrt e\), with the sharp first correction \(-5/(4n)\) and an exact finite-dimensional Dirichlet representation.
