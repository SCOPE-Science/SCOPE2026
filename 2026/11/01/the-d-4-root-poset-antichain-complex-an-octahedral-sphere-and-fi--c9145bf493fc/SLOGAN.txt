The antichain complex of the first nonshellable type-\(D\) root poset, \(D_4\), has homotopy type \((S^2\vee\bigvee^5 S^1)\sqcup\{\mathrm{pt}\}\sqcup\{\mathrm{pt}\}\). Five explicit elementary collapses reveal an octahedral two-sphere with five independent graph loops, determining the free fundamental group rather than only the previously tabulated homology ranks.
