A single generalized Steiner step on the Euclidean ball produces two joined half-ellipsoids whose unique John ellipsoid has explicit semiaxes in every dimension. Its volume efficiency is of order \(n^{-1/2}\) at shaking, tends to \(2\lambda\) at each fixed positive parameter, and has an exact nontrivial crossover when \(\lambda\sim n^{-1/2}\).
