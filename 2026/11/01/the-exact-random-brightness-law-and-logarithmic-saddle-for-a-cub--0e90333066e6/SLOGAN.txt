For a Haar-uniform plane normal, the normalized brightness deficit of the constant-width cubic body with support \(1+\varepsilon u_1u_2u_3\) has an exact arcsine-mixture distribution on \([0,8/27]\). Its density has a genuine interior logarithmic singularity at \(1/4\), with sharp coefficient \(6/\pi\) arising from twelve spherical saddle directions, whereas the previously studied axial cubic has no such interior singularity.
