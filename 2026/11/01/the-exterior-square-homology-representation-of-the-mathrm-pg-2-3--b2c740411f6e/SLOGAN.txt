The rational third homology of the thirteen-point \(\mathrm{PG}(2,3)\) cap complex is the exterior square of the twelve-dimensional reduced point-permutation representation of \(\mathrm{PGL}_3(\mathbf F_3)\). Its \(66\)-dimensional homology has no nonzero class invariant under all projective collineations; the integral representation and homotopy type are not claimed.
