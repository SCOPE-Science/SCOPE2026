# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The local forbidden-triangle decomposition gives exactly \(2\cdot3^r\) concepts. The Boolean recurrence enumerates every possible coordinate at every surviving state, and induction proves its equivalence to adaptive-tree shattering. All seven exact results are independently cross-checked by complete, explicitly supplied leaf-witness trees and direct DAG checks. The seven-page nonexistence test traversed \(236276\) memoized subproblems without a cutoff. The result is only for \(r\le7\).

**Originality — PASS under accessible comparisons.** Kozma–Moran proves the previously known static forest criterion. Blagoev studies enumeration of complete-multipartite acyclic orientations, including these book graphs. Cardinal and coauthors analyze Gray-code generation of chordal-graph orientations. These are not adaptive-query dimension results, and the first seven-page loss of the entropy upper bound is not a formal consequence of their counts or static structure. Semantic searches for the same book graph, the alternate \(K_{1,1,r}\) notation, and adaptive orientation queries found no stronger recorded claim. Historical priority remains a risk.

**Value — PASS.** This is the first sharp threshold for a natural increasing family of constraint-coupled orientation systems where the crude logarithmic concept-count bound ceases to be realizable by adaptive edge membership tests. It isolates a noncardinality obstruction and supplies replayable exact evidence for a natural small parameter, without pretending to solve the unbounded-range case.

Relevant literature: Kozma–Moran (2013); Blagoev (2018); Cardinal et al. (2022/2023). Limits: finite-range exactness, executable upper-bound certificate, and residual priority uncertainty.
