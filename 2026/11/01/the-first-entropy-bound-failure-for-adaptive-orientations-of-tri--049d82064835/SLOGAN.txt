The acyclic orientation class of the triangular book \(K_{1,1,r}\) attains its cardinality upper bound on Littlestone dimension for every \(1\le r\le6\), but first falls short at seven pages: \(\operatorname{Ldim}=11\) versus \(\lfloor\log_2|\mathcal A|\rfloor=12\). Exact recursive searches and explicit full adaptive trees determine all seven dimensions.
