For the Fefferman–Szegő kernel of quartic eggs \(\|z\|^2+|w|^4<1\), the first failure of zero-freeness occurs exactly in complex dimension eleven: dimensions \(2\) through \(10\) are zero-free, while dimension \(11\) has exactly two simple nonreal conjugate roots in its auxiliary unit disk. An exact rational Routh–Hurwitz certificate establishes the sharp cutoff.
