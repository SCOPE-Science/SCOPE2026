The ordinary matching complex of the pentagonal prism is \(\bigvee^{2}S^2\vee\bigvee^{2}S^3\), the first mixed-dimensional case among circular ladders, while that of the hexagonal prism is \(\bigvee^{12}S^3\). Exact acyclic discrete-Morse matchings and unimodular integer attaching maps establish both integral homotopy types; the smaller cases locate the transition.
