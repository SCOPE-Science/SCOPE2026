For hypercube graphs \(Q_d\) of dimensions \(1\) through \(4\), the two-independence complexes have homotopy types \(*\), \(\bigvee^3 S^1\), \(\bigvee^3 S^2\), and \(\bigvee^{16}S^3\vee S^4\), respectively. The four-dimensional cube is the first mixed-dimensional case, proved by an exhaustive acyclic Morse matching and a zero integral attaching-degree vector.
