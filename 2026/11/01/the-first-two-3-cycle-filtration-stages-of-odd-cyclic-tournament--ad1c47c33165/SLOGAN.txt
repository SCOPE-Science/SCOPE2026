For the odd cyclic tournament on \(n\ge3\) vertices, the first 3-cycle directionality stage is a wedge of \(n(n^2-1)/24-1\) two-spheres, with dihedral homology equal to the reflection-sign-twisted augmentation lattice on directed cyclic triples. The integral one-dimensional class dies at filtration value \(1\), while all those two-dimensional classes are born at \(1\) and die at \(2\).
