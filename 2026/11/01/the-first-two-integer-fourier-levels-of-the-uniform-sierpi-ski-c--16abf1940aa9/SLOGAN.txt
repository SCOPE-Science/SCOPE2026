For the canonical uniform Sierpiński carpet measure, the largest nontrivial integer Fourier coefficient modulus is \(A=0.10072687247\ldots\), attained only at signed coordinate-unit frequencies and their common powers of three; the second is \(B=0.08075493227\ldots\), attained only at independently signed diagonal-unit frequencies and their common powers of three. Every integer Fourier coefficient is nonzero, yielding an injective carpet-convolution operator with sharp first and second mean-zero Fourier contraction levels.
