For normalized ordinary Jacobi polynomials, the first two consecutive first-index relative-extremum comparisons have exact rational–radical parameter tests; passing the first does not imply passing the second. As the right-endpoint weight exponent approaches \(-1\), the second comparison changes sign at the unique root \(b_c=-0.341032918\ldots\) of \(b^3+7b^2+14b+4=0\), creating an explicit open zone of mismatched comparison outcomes.
