The four-robust clique complex of the \(4\times4\) square grid has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{84}S^5\), as certified by a complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching. Its \(D_4\) symmetry action on rational fifth homology has class character \((84,0,4,-10,-10)\), settling this concrete higher-parameter corner-gluing case and its representation.
