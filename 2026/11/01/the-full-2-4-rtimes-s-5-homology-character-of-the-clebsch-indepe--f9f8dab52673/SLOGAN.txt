The full \(2^4\rtimes S_5\) symmetry of the Clebsch graph acts on the nine-dimensional rational third homology of its independence complex by a four-dimensional inflated sign-standard module plus a five-dimensional induced signed-character module. The complete trace is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)(f(\sigma)-1+\sum_{\sigma(i)=i}\epsilon_i(t))\), so exactly four classes are translation invariant and none are invariant under every automorphism.
