The twelve-dimensional rational third homology of the Franklin graph's ordinary matching complex splits under its full \(S_4\times C_2\) symmetry as \(\eta\oplus S^{(3,1)}\oplus(\eta\otimes S^{(3,1)})\oplus S^{(2,2)}\oplus S^{(2,1,1)}\). It has no full-symmetry invariant classes, but retains one under the canonical index-two \(S_4\) subgroup, distinguishing the rational homology of the two geometric quotients.
