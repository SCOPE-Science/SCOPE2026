The ordinary four-cut complex of the Heawood graph has previously known homotopy type \(\bigvee^{188}S^9\), but its full \(336\)-element symmetry character has traces \(188,-12,2,0,-1\) on the collineation orders \(1,2,3,4,7\) and zero on every point–line duality. Neither the full group nor the collineation subgroup fixes rational homology, so the symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
