# Self-review

## Correctness (PASS)
The proof uses the published dimension-one homotopy theory of two-point configurations on trees, a natural equivariant cellular model after uniform subdivision, exact signed fixed-cell counts, the subtree identity equating vertex and edge two-cycles, and the exterior-square character identity. Every conversion from a virtual character to a genuine first-homology representation is justified by vanishing higher rational homology, connectedness, and characteristic-zero semisimplicity. The accompanying independently enumerated six-example checks agree for all graph automorphisms; the mathematical proof is unrestricted and does not rest on those finite checks. Scope: nonempty finite trees and rational coefficients only.

## Originality (PASS, with stated risk)
Barnett–Farber (arXiv:0903.2180), Farley–Sabalka (arXiv:math/0410539), and Farber (arXiv:1701.02083) establish the topology and Betti ranks or particle-exchange equivariance, not the full graph-automorphism representation for arbitrary trees. Aguilar-Guzmán–González–Hoekstra-Mendoza (arXiv:1911.12522) treats primarily labelled-particle symmetry, not this graph symmetry character. The existing star formula is a special case, whereas the present result encompasses trees with several branching vertices and automorphisms exchanging them. Searches for all-tree direction augmentation exterior-square representations and equivalent Lefschetz traces found no stronger covering claim. The main residual risk is equivalent work in unindexed or inaccessible publications.

## Value (PASS)
The formula specifies an entire family of representations uniformly for all trees, rather than a one-off finite invariant or a mere reformulation of the known rank. It gives a branch-local and computable character for every tree automorphism, capturing the interaction between exchanges of branching vertices and permutations of local directions. The nonequivariant rank and known star calculations are properly identified as covered cases. This symmetry refinement supports computation of invariant motion classes under arbitrary finite graph symmetry groups.

## Limitations
Neither an integral natural decomposition nor a result for three or more particles is established. Finite verification is corroborative, and equivalent unindexed work remains a possibility. Independent validation was not performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
