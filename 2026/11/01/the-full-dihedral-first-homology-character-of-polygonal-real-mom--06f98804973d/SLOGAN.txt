For the real moment-angle surface of every polygon with \(n\ge5\) sides, the complete rational dihedral first-homology character is explicit: each nonidentity rotation has trace \(2-2^{\gcd(n,k)}\) and every reflection has trace zero. For prime \(n\), the entire representation is a direct sum of regular dihedral modules, a symmetry distinction invisible from the previously known surface genus.
