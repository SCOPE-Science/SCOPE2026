For the ordinary matching complex of the \(4\times4\) Cartesian grid, the complete rational square-symmetry homology representations are \(\widetilde H_4\cong A_1\oplus2A_2\oplus B_1\oplus E\) and \(\widetilde H_5\cong E\), where \(E\) is the standard \(D_4\)-plane representation. The underlying six-four-sphere/two-five-sphere homotopy classification was known; the dihedral characters and an explicit integral basis for the top action are the additional results.
