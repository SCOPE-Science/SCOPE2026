The complete \(S_4\times C_2\)-character of the cube matching complex's four-dimensional rational second homology is \(\big(S^{(2,1,1)}\boxtimes\mathbf1\big)\oplus\big(S^{(1,1,1,1)}\boxtimes\varepsilon\big). Central inversion retains three rational homology classes, whereas full rotational symmetry retains none; the established four-sphere-wedge homotopy type is not the new result.
