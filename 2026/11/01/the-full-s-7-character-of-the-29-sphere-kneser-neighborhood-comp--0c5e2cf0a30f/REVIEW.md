The exact object is the open-neighborhood complex of the graph on two-subsets of seven labels. The 2018 primary theorem identifies the only nonzero reduced rational homology as 29 classes in degree three. Every face of the actual 21-vertex complex is enumerated, with an independent inclusion-exclusion face-vector check. For all 15 conjugacy classes and all 5040 permutations grouped by cycle type, signed simplicial traces give exact reduced Lefschetz numbers. The character formula holds on every class; the Specht splitting follows from explicit permutation and exterior-square characters and unit character norms. The finite quotient statement uses rational averaging after barycentric subdivision. The checker does not replace the independently cited topology theorem.

## Originality — PASS
A full reading of Nilakantan–Singh (2018) confirms the nonequivariant wedge-of-spheres theorem and its proof, with no full-symmetric-group homology character in its identified results. Schultz (2010) and Braun–Zeckner (2011) concern stable Kneser graphs, with dihedral rather than the ordinary graph's full symmetric action. The closest public indexed character result concerns \(\mathrm{KG}(6,2)\), not the seven-symbol graph; it cannot imply this computation merely by specialization. Search-based evidence is not a uniqueness proof: an unindexed or independent equivalent calculation remains possible, and the indexed six-point source was only available through its summary.
