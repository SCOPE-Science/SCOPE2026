The ordinary neighborhood complex of \(\mathrm{KG}(7,2)\) has a 29-dimensional rational third homology whose full \(S_7\)-action splits as \(S^{(2,2,1,1,1)}\oplus S^{(3,1,1,1,1)}\). Its character depends only on the permutation sign and number of fixed symbols, and its full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
