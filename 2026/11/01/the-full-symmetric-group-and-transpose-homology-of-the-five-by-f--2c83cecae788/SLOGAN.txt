On the five-by-five chessboard with its diagonal deleted, the matching complex has integral homology \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{13}\), with the complete rational action of simultaneous square-label permutations and row–column exchange determined by three explicit Specht summands. The degree-two class changes sign under transposition, whereas degree three has no class invariant under all simultaneous relabelings.
