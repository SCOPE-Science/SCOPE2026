The icosahedral graph's ordinary independence complex is a two-sphere with six antipodal edges attached, giving \(S^2\vee\bigvee^6S^1\). Its outer symmetry action on \(\pi_1\) is the signed permutation of the six chord generators; on rational homology the degree-one module is the rational six-dimensional icosahedral irreducible with antipodal sign, and the degree-two class has antipodal sign.
