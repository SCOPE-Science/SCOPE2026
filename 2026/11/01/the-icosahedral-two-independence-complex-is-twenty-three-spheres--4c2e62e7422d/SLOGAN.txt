The two-independence complex of the twelve-vertex icosahedral graph has exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{20}S^3\), proved by a complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching. On rational third homology the antipodal involution has even and odd eigenspace dimensions \(15\) and \(5\), and the full \(A_5\times C_2\) representation is \((\mathbf1\oplus V_4\oplus2V_5)\boxtimes\mathbf1\oplus V_5\boxtimes\varepsilon\).
