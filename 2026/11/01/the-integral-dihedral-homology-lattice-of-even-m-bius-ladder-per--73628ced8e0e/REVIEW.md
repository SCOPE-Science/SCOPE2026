# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For all even spoke counts n=2k>=4, the proof classifies perfect matchings via independent cyclic tile sets I, with opposite rim-edge pairs H_i and remaining compatible spokes u_j. A simplicial map merging each opposite rim pair has a section contiguous to the identity, so it is an equivariant homotopy equivalence. Removing two alternating all-tile facets leaves the spoke-star subcomplex: every retained face admits a spoke, and all finite nonempty star intersections contain the full all-spoke simplex, giving a contractible nerve. The pair long exact sequence then identifies absolute H_(k-1) with the integral relative group generated by the two oriented exceptional (k-1)-cells, yielding Z² and the wedge of two spheres, not only rational Betti ranks. Rotation sends E0 to E1 with + sign and E1 to E0 with (-1)^(k-1); reflection reverses order of k tiles, sign (-1)^(k(k-1)/2), so given integral matrices follow. When k odd the +-eigenlattices have coordinate determinant 2; when k even r²=-I yields a free Z[i] rank-one lattice. I independently enumerated ALL original graph perfect matchings and their tile-family counterparts for n=4,6,8,10 (7,18,47,123 facets), checking both dihedral orientation signs at each n. These corroborate rather than replace the all-n proof.
