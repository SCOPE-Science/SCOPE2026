# The integral dihedral homology module of cycle-to-cycle graph Hom complexes

## Finding

For every pair of integers \(m\ge3\), \(n\ge5\), write \(X_{m,n}=\operatorname{Hom}(C_m,C_n)\) for the standard polyhedral graph-multihomomorphism complex. The commuting source- and target-cycle dihedral actions determine a canonical **signed permutation structure on the entire integral first homology**.

Set
\[
W_{m,n}=\{w\in\mathbb Z:|nw|<m,\quad m-nw\equiv0\pmod2\}.
\]
If \(n\) is odd, then \(H_1(X_{m,n};\mathbb Z)\) is the free abelian group with basis \(u_w\) for \(w\in W_{m,n}\). If \(n\) is even, then it is free with basis \(u_{w,p}\) for \(w\in W_{m,n}\) and \(p\in\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\). Empty basis means zero homology.

Write a source symmetry \(a(j)=s j+k\pmod m\) and target symmetry \(b(z)=t z+\ell\pmod n\), where \(s,t\in\{1,-1\}\). On the complex they act by \(f\mapsto b\circ f\circ a^{-1}\). **For all such parameters, their complete integral first-homology action is**
\[
 (a,b)_*u_w=t\,u_{st w}\qquad(n\text{ odd}),
\]
\[
 (a,b)_*u_{w,p}=t\,u_{st w,\ p+k+\ell\bmod2}
 \qquad(n\text{ even}).
\]
In particular the rational first-homology character at \((a,b)\) is \(t\) times the number of circle-component labels fixed by these permutations.

There is a further **integral evaluation-index dichotomy**. For every non-point connected component \(Y\subseteq X_{m,n}\), let \(e_0:Y\to |C_n|\cong S^1\) evaluate the first coordinate using the natural length-two arc for a two-valued coordinate. Then
\[
\operatorname{im}\bigl((e_0)_*:H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow
H_1(|C_n|;\mathbb Z)\bigr)
=
\begin{cases}
2\mathbb Z,&n\text{ odd},\\
\mathbb Z,&n\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
\]
The bases above are uniquely oriented by the requirement that evaluation send each generator to the positive integer \(2\) or \(1\), respectively.

Consequently, the quotient \(X_{m,n}/(D_m\times D_n)\) has **no positive-degree reduced rational homology**. This makes no assertion of contractibility, simple connectivity, or integral acyclicity of that quotient.

## Assumptions and scope

The cycle \(C_q\) has vertices \(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\), with edges joining residues differing by \(1\). No loops are allowed. A cell of \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_m,C_n)\) is a tuple \((A_0,\ldots,A_{m-1})\) of nonempty subsets of \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), such that every vertex of \(A_i\) is adjacent to every vertex of \(A_{i+1}\), with cyclic subscripts. Each cell is a product of simplices. Because \(n\ge5\), each \(A_i\) is either a singleton or a pair of vertices at target distance two; the cells are cubes. We use ordinary singular homology of the geometric realization with integral coefficients.

At a vertex \(f\), there is a unique step sequence \(\epsilon_i\in\{-1,+1\}\) satisfying \(f(i+1)-f(i)\equiv\epsilon_i\pmod n\). Its integer winding is \(w=\frac1n\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\epsilon_i\). For even \(n\), the initial-color parity \(p=f(0)\bmod2\) is also invariant under cellular paths. The strict condition in \(W_{m,n}\) excludes monotone maps, which are isolated vertices of the Hom complex, and do not contribute to first homology. Empty Hom complexes are included in the zero-homology statement.

## Proof

**Step 1: component and homology input.** The component classification and good-cover argument of Čukić and Kozlov (2004, Lemma 5.5 and Theorem 6.1) state that every non-point component is homotopy equivalent to \(S^1\). The non-point components are indexed by precisely \(w\in W_{m,n}\), together with one initial-color parity \(p\) when \(n\) is even. Their theorem supplies the rank-one integral first homology of each such component; this preexisting nonequivariant classification is *not* asserted to be new.

**Step 2: evaluation-index computation.** Fix such a component \(Y\). Use the published cover by contractible subcomplexes \(X_i\), each generated by cells whose first coordinate contains target color \(i\). Each nonempty intersection \(X_i\cap X_j\) is contractible, occurs only when \(j=i\pm2\pmod n\), and every intersection of three distinct \(X_i\)'s is empty. The nerve is exactly the cycle with edges \(i\longleftrightarrow i+2\). For odd \(n\), the nerve has all \(n\) colors; for even \(n\), it has the \(n/2\) colors of parity \(p\).

To define \(e_0\), send a singleton first coordinate \(\{i\}\) to vertex \(i\) of the geometric target polygon. On a two-valued first coordinate \(\{i,i+2\}\), map its interval linearly onto the *two-edge* polygonal arc \(i\to i+1\to i+2\) (the common neighbor gives the specified arc). These prescriptions agree on cell faces; \(n\ge5\) makes the arc unambiguous. A path across the overlap \(X_i\cap X_{i+2}\) and the two adjacent contractible sets traverses this arc from \(i\) to \(i+2\). Thus a positively oriented generator of the nerve circle maps under evaluation to a polygonal walk of successive \(+2\) steps. Its winding number around \(|C_n|\) is \(2\) if \(n\) is odd (\(n\) steps) and \(1\) if \(n\) is even (\(n/2\) steps). Because the cover and its overlaps are contractible, nerve generators give primitive generators of \(H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)\), establishing the exact subgroup and its injectivity. This calculation supplies the *oriented integral* generator used in the claim.

**Step 3: comparison of all source evaluations.** For each source vertex \(j\), construct \(e_j\) by the identical coordinate-evaluation rule. The maps \(e_j\) and \(e_{j+1}\) are homotopic: for any cell, its two adjacent coordinate sets are mutually completely adjacent in the target cycle; hence either both are adjacent singletons or one is a singleton at the midpoint of the other's unique two-edge arc. Interpolating along the resulting target arc of length at most one defines a homotopy that varies continuously over each cell and agrees on faces. Repeating gives \(e_j\simeq e_0\) for every source index \(j\). In particular every source-cycle automorphism has the same map on evaluation homology, even if it reverses source orientation.

**Step 4: exact integral symmetry signs.** For \((a,b)\) as above, the new winding is \(st w\), because source reversal and target reflection independently reverse winding. If \(n\) is even, every adjacent source step changes color parity, so at source vertex zero the new initial parity is \(p+k+\ell\bmod2\), independently of \(s,t\). Moreover \(e_0\circ(a,b)=b\circ e_{a^{-1}(0)}\) as maps to the target polygon. Every target dihedral symmetry acts on \(H_1(|C_n|;\mathbb Z)\) by multiplication by \(t\), and the source evaluations have equal induced maps by Step 3. Hence \((e_0)_*(a,b)_*=t(e_0)_*\). Since evaluation is injective with the same positive index \(1\) or \(2\) on every circle component, the displayed signed integral basis-permutation formulas follow, with no rationalization or unspecified orientation choices.

**Step 5: quotient.** The source reflection \(j\mapsto-j\) sends \(u_{w,p}\) to \(u_{-w,p}\) without a sign; the target reflection \(z\mapsto-z\) sends the same basis vector to \(-u_{-w,p}\). For odd \(n\) omit \(p\). A class invariant under both reflections is therefore zero. All other reduced positive-degree rational homology of \(X_{m,n}\) is zero by the known component classification. A finite cellular group action can be made admissible by barycentric subdivision; the rational cellular chains of the quotient are the coinvariants, and taking coinvariants of a rational finite-group representation is exact. Thus the positive-degree rational homology of the quotient is zero.

## Verification

The accompanying Python programs build actual graph homomorphisms, adjacency edges, higher cubical cells, and finite group actions with no third-party dependencies. For ten finite pairs, the vertex-graph cycle-cocycle calculation confirms the exact odd/even integral evaluation index and component tags, including isolated maps. A separate signed-cubical Lefschetz calculation verifies the proposed character in representative cases. A third program verifies the character for **every** element of \(D_m\times D_n\) in the three cases \((m,n)=(6,5),(7,5),(8,6)\): respectively \(120\), \(140\), and \(192\) group elements. In particular it enumerates \(2,496\) cubical cells for \((8,6)\). All checks terminate successfully. These finite computations are independent stress tests, not an extrapolation or substitute for Steps 1–5, which prove the all-parameter statement.

Run `python3 cycle_check.py`, `python3 cycle_action_verifier.py`, and `python3 cycle_full_group_verifier.py` in one directory. Text files with the corresponding recorded outputs are included.

## Relationship to prior work

The ordinary component counts and circle-or-point homotopy types are due to Čukić–Kozlov (2004); their published good-cover nerve is reused in Step 2. Babson–Kozlov (2003) introduced and analyzed graph Hom complexes, including different graph families. Fujii–Iwamasa–Kimura–Nozaki–Suzuki (arXiv:2408.04802, Theorem 5.1) generalize the circle-or-point classification to all connected source graphs with cycle targets and construct explicit universal covers, without a complete source-and-target dihedral homology action. The more general later square-free-codomain classification of Matsushita (arXiv:2412.19144, Theorem 1.2 and Theorem 5.8) establishes possible component homotopy types for connected source graphs and discusses fundamental-group structures, but it does not give the displayed simultaneous \(D_m\times D_n\) integral character, its component-label action, or the full quotient consequence. Earlier graph-coloring equivariance involving complete target graphs also has different hypotheses. This result claims only the explicit signed integral equivariant refinement and evaluation-index proof, never the previously established nonequivariant homotopy classification.

## Limitations

All statements concern the standard graph-multihomomorphism polyhedral complex for unweighted simple cycles with \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge5\). No result for target \(C_3\) or \(C_4\), arbitrary graph codomains, integral homology of the quotient, or equivariant homotopy classification is asserted. The rational quotient need not be contractible. The source-exhaustiveness search does not constitute a proof of publication priority; equivalent unindexed calculations remain possible.
