Every two-token graph of the \(2m\)-vertex cocktail-party graph, \(m\ge2\), has an entirely integral Laplacian spectrum supported on \(0,2m-2,2m,4m-6,4m-4,4m-2\), with explicitly determined multiplicities. A signed-pair decomposition yields the exact spanning-tree complexity of these nonregular configuration graphs for every \(m\).
