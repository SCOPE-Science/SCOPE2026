The circumradius and circumcenter-distance of three area-uniform points in a Euclidean disk have an exact elementary joint density \(j(r,s)=2r^3s\Phi(\alpha(r,s))/\pi^2\), with \(\Phi(a)=24[a^2+(a/2)\sin(2a)+\cos(2a)-1]\). Its one-dimensional marginal integral explicitly includes the previously unresolved case of subunit-radius circumcircles that cross the sampling disk's boundary.
