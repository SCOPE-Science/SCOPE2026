# The natural \(S_6\)-character of the scale-four Tutte–Coxeter Čech nerve is induced from \(C_5\)

## Finding
Let \(T\) be the thirty-vertex Tutte–Coxeter graph, realized with unit-length geometric edges, and let \(K=\mathcal N(T,4)\) denote the intrinsic Čech nerve of its thirty closed radius-two balls. Label the vertices of \(T\) by the fifteen duads (unordered two-subsets) and fifteen synthemes (partitions into three duads) on a fixed six-symbol set; a duad and syntheme are adjacent when incident. The natural relabeling group \(S_6\) acts simplicially on \(K\).

The complete character on the only nonzero reduced rational homology group is
\[
\chi_{\widetilde H_2(K;\mathbb Q)}(g)=
\begin{cases}
144,&g=1,\\
-1,&g\text{ has cycle type }(5,1),\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
In particular, if \(C_5\le S_6\) is generated by a five-cycle and \(\psi:C_5\to\mathbb C^\times\) is any nontrivial linear character, then, as **complex** representations,
\[
\widetilde H_2(K;\mathbb Q)\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb C
\cong_{\mathbb C[S_6]}\operatorname{Ind}_{C_5}^{S_6}\psi.
\]
The real-valued induced character is independent of the chosen nontrivial \(\psi\). Equivalently, the rational module is the following direct sum of irreducible rational Specht modules:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde H_2(K;\mathbb Q)\cong {}&S^{(2,1,1,1,1)}\oplus2S^{(2,2,1,1)}\oplus2S^{(3,1,1,1)}
\oplus S^{(2,2,2)}\\
&\oplus3S^{(3,2,1)}\oplus2S^{(4,1,1)}\oplus S^{(3,3)}
\oplus2S^{(4,2)}\oplus S^{(5,1)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The dimension sum is \(5+18+20+5+48+20+5+18+5=144\). Neither the trivial nor the sign representation occurs, so \(K/S_6\) and \(K/A_6\) have vanishing reduced rational homology.

The **nonequivariant** homotopy type \(K\simeq\bigvee^{144}S^2\) was calculated previously [3]; the new claim is the natural symmetry character, its induced-module identification, Specht decomposition, and fixed-homology consequences, not the preexisting sphere count.

## Assumptions and scope
A duad is a two-element subset of \(\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}\); a syntheme is a partition of that set into three disjoint duads. There are fifteen of each, with a graph edge between a duad and a syntheme precisely when one belongs to the other. This is the canonical Tutte–Coxeter incidence construction [1]. The relevant symmetry is the specified **720-element label-permutation subgroup** \(S_6\), not the 1440-element full graph automorphism group, whose outer symmetries may interchange the two vertex classes.

The Čech scale convention is the one in [2]: \(\mathcal N(T,r)\) is the nerve of closed balls of radius \(r/2\) centered at graph vertices inside the metric realization of \(T\). At even scale four, [2, Lemma 2.2] identifies this nerve with the simplicial complex generated by the **closed graph-distance-two neighborhoods** \(B_2(v)\). This distinction from the graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex is essential. All asserted homology representations are over \(\mathbb Q\); the induction description is asserted over \(\mathbb C\). No integral representation-lattice classification is implied.

## Proof
**1. Exact finite simplicial model.** Enumerate the duads lexicographically and the synthemes as sorted perfect matchings on six symbols, obtaining thirty labeled vertices, forty-five incidence edges, and exactly thirty closed radius-two neighborhoods. Every neighborhood has ten vertices. Form \(K\) by taking every nonempty subset of every neighborhood. Exhaustive enumeration gives exactly 27,535 faces and dimension-zero-through-nine face vector
\[
(30,435,2620,5625,7290,6255,3600,1350,300,30).
\]
Every simplicial face occurs in this list by the generating-neighborhood lemma [2]; thus subsequent finite computations are exhaustive rather than samples.

**2. Independent homology control.** Repeatedly select a codimension-one face \(\tau\) having a unique immediate coface \(\sigma\), require that \(\sigma\) be maximal, and remove the pair. At each removal, \(\tau\) is genuinely free and \((\tau,\sigma)\) is an elementary simplicial collapse. The supplied deterministic algorithm removes exactly 13,495 pairs. The resulting complex \(L\) is two-dimensional and has exactly 30 vertices, 200 edges and 315 triangles. Its one-skeleton is connected, which the verifier checks directly. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), the boundary map from its triangles to its edges has rank exactly 171. Over \(\mathbb Q\), this rank is at least 171; as the image lies in the cycle space of the connected one-skeleton, whose dimension is \(200-30+1=171\), its rational rank is exactly 171. Consequently \(\widetilde H_1(L;\mathbb Q)=0\), \(\dim\widetilde H_2(L;\mathbb Q)=315-171=144\), and all other reduced rational homology vanishes. Because collapses preserve homotopy type, the same holds for \(K\). This directly checks the known nonequivariant conclusion [3] without treating its earlier proof as a computation certificate for the present result.

**3. An exact signed Lefschetz formula.** Given \(g\in S_6\), compute its induced permutation of the thirty duads and synthemes and let \(\mathcal O(g)\) be its set of cycles on these thirty vertices. A nonempty face of \(K\) is stabilized setwise exactly when it is the union of a nonempty collection \(I\subseteq\mathcal O(g)\) for which that union is contained in at least one of the thirty neighborhood facets. If a fixed face is the union of \(|I|\) cycles, its contribution to the simplicial Lefschetz number is \((-1)^{|I|-1}\): the dimension sign and the orientation sign cancel all dependence on the cycle lengths. As \(H_0\) contributes trace one, while the only nonzero reduced rational homology is in even degree two, the required character equals
\[
\chi(g)=-\sum_{\substack{I\subseteq\mathcal O(g)\\
\bigcup_{O\in I}O\subseteq B_2(v)\text{ for some }v}}(-1)^{|I|}.
\]
The empty selection is included in the sum, making the formula valid uniformly for the identity. The verifier enumerates all such selections as bitmasks, checks every element of the 720-element group, and independently evaluates the signed fixed-simplex Lefschetz sum for representatives of all eleven cycle classes. The two implementations agree. Only identity and cycle type \((5,1)\) have nonzero traces, respectively 144 and \(-1\).

**4. Induction and irreducibility data.** Let \(c\) be a five-cycle and \(C_5=\langle c\rangle\). A nontrivial complex character \(\psi\) of this cyclic subgroup has \(\sum_{k=1}^4\psi(c^k)=-1\). The induced character has degree \([S_6:C_5]=144\). It vanishes on permutations not conjugate to elements of \(C_5\). For a five-cycle \(g\), the centralizer in \(S_6\) has order five, giving the induced value
\[
\bigl(\operatorname{Ind}_{C_5}^{S_6}\psi\bigr)(g)
=\frac{1}{5}\cdot5\sum_{k=1}^4\psi(c^k)=-1.
\]
Thus the induced character agrees with the calculated homology character on all eleven conjugacy classes. Complex representations of finite groups are determined by their characters, proving the induced description. Independently, the verifier constructs the full \(S_6\) Specht-character table by an exact alternating Young-tabloid formula, checks character orthogonality, and computes the integer multiplicities displayed above. The Specht modules are defined over \(\mathbb Q\), so this also identifies the original rational module.

**5. Quotient consequence.** Since the character is supported only at identity and 5-cycles, both \(\frac1{720}\sum_{g\in S_6}\chi(g)\) and \(\frac1{360}\sum_{g\in A_6}\chi(g)\) vanish: there are 144 five-cycles, all even, and \(144+144(-1)=0\). For any finite group acting simplicially, rational cohomology of the quotient is the invariant rational cohomology (apply barycentric subdivision and rational averaging); duality over \(\mathbb Q\) gives the asserted reduced homology vanishing. Connectedness follows from the collapse in step 2.

## Verification
Run `python3 verify.py` from the directory containing the supplied standalone Python 3 file; no external packages or inputs are required. The program builds the complete incidence graph, verifies its 720 vertex relabelings preserve every closed two-ball, enumerates all 27,535 faces, performs all 13,495 elementary simplicial collapses with free-face and maximal-coface tests, independently checks connectivity and the two-dimensional mod-two boundary rank, enumerates fixed orbit-unions for all group elements, and cross-checks all eleven classes by signed fixed-simplex Lefschetz sums. An independently generated exact character table gives the stated Specht multiplicities and vanishing invariant averages. `verification_output.txt` is the recorded stdout from this code; `VERIFY_OK` is emitted only after all assertions. A finite mod-two rank by itself is not treated as an integral homology calculation. The result proves rational equivariance for this exact finite complex; it does not establish any general theorem for other generalized quadrangles.

## Relationship to prior work
Adams, Shukla and Singh [2] establish the intrinsic Čech definition, the even-scale closed-neighborhood formula, and results for hypercubes. The previously published **same-graph, same-scale** calculation [3] proves \(K\simeq\bigvee^{144}S^2\) but states no representation of the natural \(S_6\) action. Knowing the dimension and homotopy type alone does **not** determine an equivariant homology character: distinct 144-dimensional \(S_6\)-modules have the same underlying vector-space dimension. Here the new character has zero invariant dimension, which does not follow from the wedge count. The published scale-three *Vietoris–Rips* calculation [4] gives a different 256-dimensional homology representation with a fixed line, and its join-complex argument does not calculate the scale-four Čech action. The related Čech calculation on \(KG(6,2)\) [5] treats only fifteen duad vertices and gives 64 two-sphere classes with a different character; it does not determine the present thirty-vertex complex. The historical exposition [1] establishes the natural label-permutation action and its relationship to the outer automorphism, but contains no topological homology calculation.

## Limitations
The induced representation is an isomorphism of complex \(S_6\)-modules, not a canonical geometric basis or an induction theorem for arbitrary graphs. Only the natural order-720 subgroup is considered, not the order-1440 full automorphism group. No integral representation lattice, equivariant homotopy type, Steenrod operation, or orbit-space homotopy type is claimed. The nonequivariant homotopy classification was already known [3]. Literature coverage is finite; an unindexed equivalent equivariant calculation remains a residual priority risk. Independent validation has not been performed.
