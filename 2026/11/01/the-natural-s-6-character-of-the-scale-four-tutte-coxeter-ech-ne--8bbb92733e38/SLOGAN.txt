The natural \(S_6\)-representation on the 144-dimensional second rational homology of the Tutte–Coxeter graph's scale-four intrinsic Čech nerve is induced from any nontrivial complex character of a five-cycle subgroup. Its trace is 144 at the identity, \(-1\) at five-cycles, and zero otherwise, so both the \(S_6\) and \(A_6\) quotients are rationally acyclic; the nonequivariant 144-sphere wedge was known previously.
