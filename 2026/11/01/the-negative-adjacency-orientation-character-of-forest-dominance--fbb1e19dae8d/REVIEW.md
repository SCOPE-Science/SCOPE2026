# Review: the forest dominance orientation character

## Correctness — PASS
The result concerns every finite simple forest with at least one edge, and every automorphism. The proof explicitly uses the known integral one-sphere homology of forest dominance complexes, establishes that negative adjacency inertia has dimension equal to matching number, and constructs primitive integer topological generators from arbitrary maximum matchings. It proves that coordinate restrictions of the negative eigenspace to every minimum vertex cover are invertible. A local quadratic-form calculation shows that the spectral determinant changes sign under a minimum-cover flip in exactly the same way as an oriented simplicial facet coefficient. The minimum-cover flip graph is connected because contractions of matching edges in a forest yield acyclic implication constraints. Naturality therefore identifies the two integral sign characters. Independent exact combinatorial Lefschetz and finite numerical spectral checks agree for 344 automorphism actions; these tests support, but do not replace, the all-forest proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to publication-priority uncertainty
The 2007 primary text of Marietti and Testa already proves sphere homotopy type, matching-number dimension and an integral cycle generator from a maximal resolution; these premises are acknowledged and excluded from the new claim. Their companion forest-complex study is similarly nonequivariant. The later Alexander-dual/neighbor-complex work of Matsushita and Wakatsuki does not determine the forest automorphism character or its negative-adjacency determinant interpretation. Searches of equivalent formulations, stronger known results, exact tables and indexed prior claims found related but structurally distinct equivariant complexes (in particular discrete-Morse complexes) rather than this assertion. Full publication-priority certainty is unavailable: an unindexed equivalent computation or a stronger unexamined treatment remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem assigns the full integral automorphism orientation character to every forest, without fixing a particular forest shape, and identifies a topological sign with a graph-spectral sign. Neither the previously known sphere dimension nor existence of some cycle generator specifies the sign representation. An exact combinatorial formula using any maximum matching and minimum cover makes equivariant calculations possible without diagonalizing a matrix, while the spectral identity provides an independent structural interpretation. The theorem is an infinite-family classification with a genuinely equivariant object and a natural graph-invariant motivation.

## Scope and limits
No equivariant sphere model, canonical integral lattice equivalence with the negative eigenspace, or extension to graphs with cycles is claimed. Numerical diagonalization in the verifier is not an exact spectral proof; the proof of the infinite family uses exact negative-definite quadratic forms. The highest novelty risk is an equivalent result under alternate terminology or an unindexed source.
