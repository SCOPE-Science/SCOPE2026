The first nondegenerate positive-dimensional rank-three Möbius-ladder independence homology has a nonsplit integral rotation lattice: its rational square-plane and sign-line constituents glue by the unique nonzero extension of order two. In particular the twelve-vertex graph has integral rotation coinvariants \(\mathbb Z/4\), not the \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\) of the rationally equivalent split lattice.
