The ordinary open-neighborhood complex of the thirteen-vertex Paley graph is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{25}S^2\), via an explicitly verified sequence of \(227\) elementary collapses and \(47\) fundamental-group eliminations. Its rational homology under all \(78\) affine symmetries is the thirteen-point permutation representation plus its twelve-dimensional augmentation quotient, with character values \(25\), \(-1\), and \(1\).
