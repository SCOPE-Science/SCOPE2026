The exhaustive verifier reconstructs all 42 perfect matchings in two independent ways and compares their downward closure against an independent extendibility test on all 35,368 ordinary matchings. It enumerates 15,819 nonempty faces, validates 7,901 Morse pairs by topological sorting of all 78,921 directed Hasse incidences, and computes the signed integral critical-cell differentials. The five-by-ten differential has a unit minor (determinant \(-1\)), while the ten-by-one differential vanishes. The resulting three-skeleton is simply connected and homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^5 S^3\); the remaining four-cell has nullhomotopic attaching map by Hurewicz. Independent simplicial mod-two ranks agree. Limit: this is a finite computation for one graph, with the executable finite certificate integral to the proof.
