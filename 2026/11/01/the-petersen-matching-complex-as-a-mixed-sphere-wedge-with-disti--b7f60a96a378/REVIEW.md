# Scientific review of the Petersen matching-complex classification

**Correctness — PASS.** A completely specified fifteen-edge graph produces 331 nonempty simplices. An explicit 160-pair acyclic simplex/coface matching has one critical vertex, five two-cells and five three-cells. Integral unit-pivot cancellations have zero remaining boundary, and the Hurewicz theorem makes each three-cell attaching map nullhomotopic, proving the integral mixed sphere-wedge type. Separate rational boundary ranks \((14,61,79,6)\), modular image-character calculations and exact character identities prove the signed point permutation module in degree two and \(S^{(3,2)}\) in degree three. The accompanying standalone programs are actual reproducible certificates. Risks: the argument requires faithful reproduction of the finite masks and incidence orientations; these are exhaustively checked in code. The representation decomposition is rational, not an integral lattice classification.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Primary sources on ordinary matching complexes of complete graphs (Björner–Lovász–Vrećica–Živaljević 1994; Wachs 2003), the Petersen graph as the matching *graph* of \(K_5\) (Larrión–Pizaña–Villarroel-Flores 2009), and general Gorenstein/Cohen–Macaulay matching-complex criteria (Nikseresht 2021) have different claims. No consulted source presents the topological classification or homology character of the *ordinary matching complex of the Petersen graph*. Comparisons with indexed research findings distinguish the two-matching complex, Morse complex, cut complexes, and graph-particle configurations from this ordinary matching complex. The closest indexed findings are `c0442780c8c1`, `402d43c0c821`, `0eae16654cf3`, `db6c6c11f317`, and `c700eceb52bb`; none determines this object. A matching complex of \(K_5\) is the Petersen graph itself, not a matching complex on the Petersen graph's fifteen edges. Search incompleteness and a possible uncatalogued equivalent calculation are the remaining publication-priority risks.

**Value — PASS.** The canonical smallest cubic bridgeless non-three-edge-colorable graph has a natural matching simplicial complex. The calculation gives the complete integral homotopy type, not merely numerical Betti ranks, and explains the two equal-dimensional homology vector spaces as **different** representations of its full symmetric relabeling action. Consequently, the rational homology of the quotients by the full and alternating symmetry groups differs sharply. Neither the homotopy classification nor this quotient distinction is implied by standard matching-number data or prior classifications restricted to complete graph matchings.

**Limitations.** The finite certificate is independent of sampling but has not been independently reimplemented or mathematically audited. No equivariant wedge splitting, integral representation lattice or integral quotient homology is asserted. Unindexed published coverage remains a risk. Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
