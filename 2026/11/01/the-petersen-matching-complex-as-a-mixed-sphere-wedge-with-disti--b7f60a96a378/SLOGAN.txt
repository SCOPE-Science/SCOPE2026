The ordinary matching complex of the Petersen graph is \(\bigvee^5S^2\vee\bigvee^5S^3\), but its two five-dimensional rational homology groups carry different \(\mathfrak S_5\)-actions: the signed natural permutation representation in degree two and \(S^{(3,2)}\) in degree three. The full-symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic while the alternating-group quotient has one rational two-dimensional homology class.
