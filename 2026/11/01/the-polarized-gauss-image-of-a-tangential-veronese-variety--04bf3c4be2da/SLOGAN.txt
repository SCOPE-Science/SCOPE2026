The ordinary Gauss image of the tangential degree-\(d\) Veronese variety \(\tau(\nu_d(\mathbb P^n))\) is an explicitly polarized, projectively normal two-step flag variety. Its full Hilbert function and Plücker degree follow from the weight \(((2n+1)(d-2)+n,n)\), giving \(\binom{2n-1}{n}(2n+1)(d-2)n^{n-1}((2n+1)(d-2)+n)^{n-1}\) for every \(n\geq2\), \(d\geq3\).
