# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
PASS. For every finite connected bipartite graph of diameter four and girth at least eight, parity of graph distance gives a literal decomposition of the radius-three flag complex into a join of its two within-side distance-two clique complexes. No four-cycle makes each pair of same-side vertices have at most one common center; no six-cycle forces any clique to share one center. Coning over the resulting neighborhood simplices gives a common expansion, with explicit free-face collapses to both the within-side clique complex and the original incidence graph. The join consequently is a wedge of the square of the graph's cycle rank in degree three. Adamaszek's 2011 Proposition 2.2 certifies that inclusion from scale one to scale two is a collapse. Diameter four terminates the filtration. Two independently implemented exhaustive finite checkers reproduce the structural conditions and mod-two chain homology in the cycle, grid, and symplectic small cases, including the complete full-complex boundary ranks for the order-two symplectic quadrangle. The general proof does not extrapolate from the tests.

The theorem produces a complete integral persistent-homology classification from two elementary graph invariants, shows a topological transition from first to third homology, and explains a nontrivial fourth-power multiplicity law.
