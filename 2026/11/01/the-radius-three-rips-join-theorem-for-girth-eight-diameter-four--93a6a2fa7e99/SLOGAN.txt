For every finite connected bipartite graph of diameter four and girth at least eight, the radius-three graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex is a wedge of \((|E|-|V|+1)^2\) three-spheres, and the full integral persistence consists only of degree-zero, degree-one, and degree-three intervals. Applied to every finite generalized quadrangle of order \((s,t)\), it gives exactly \(s^4t^4\) three-spheres at radius three, including \(6561\) for the symplectic order-\((3,3)\) case.
