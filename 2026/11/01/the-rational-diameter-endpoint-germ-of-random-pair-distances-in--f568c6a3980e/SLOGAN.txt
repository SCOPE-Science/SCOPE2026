For independent volume-uniform points in a regular Euclidean simplex of dimension \(d\ge4\), the probability of a separation within fraction \(t\) of its diameter has a convergent rational endpoint germ beginning with \(\frac{d(d+1)2^{2d-2}(d!)^2}{(2d)!}t^{2d}\bigl(1+\frac{3(d-1)}{2d+1}t+O_d(t^2)\bigr)\). An explicit Dirichlet-moment series calculates every higher coefficient, including the \(\frac{34}{15}\) second relative correction in dimension four.
