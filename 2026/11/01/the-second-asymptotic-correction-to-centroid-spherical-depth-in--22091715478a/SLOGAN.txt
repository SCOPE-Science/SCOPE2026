For a regular Euclidean \(d\)-simplex, the probability that its centroid lies in the diameter ball of two independent uniform interior points equals \(1/2+[2/(3\sqrt{d+1})-4883/(270(d+1)^{3/2})]/\sqrt{2\pi}+o((d+1)^{-3/2})\). The negative second coefficient quantifies the first correction to the positive high-dimensional spherical-depth bias.
