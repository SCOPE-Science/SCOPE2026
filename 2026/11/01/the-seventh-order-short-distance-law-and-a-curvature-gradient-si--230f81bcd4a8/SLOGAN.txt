For every positively curved smooth planar convex body, the seventh-order term of the normalized short-pair distance law is an exact curvature-gradient invariant proportional to \(\int_{\partial K}(9\kappa^4-8\kappa_s^2)\,ds\). Unlike the earlier positive curvature-squared term, its sign changes at one explicit curvature-amplitude threshold in each harmonic support-function family, and a smooth threefold-symmetric example has a strictly negative coefficient.
