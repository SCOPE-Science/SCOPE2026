# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The prism parallel-body formula is derived by slicing through the slab and integrating both spherical end caps, with no differentiability assumption on the planar convex base. The interlacing argument supplies three distinct negative roots for all heights below the quotient threshold; at equality an explicit factorization gives exactly one double-root height; above it a cubic-discriminant identity with certified coefficient signs gives an explicit common failure height. Exact symbolic and rational verification plus 1,063 floating-point test configurations corroborate—but do not replace—the continuum proof. Assumptions, multiplicities, and normalization are stated.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
The relevant published full texts establish (i) general product formulas, (ii) general root-location bounds, and (iii) the special case of circular cylinders. Their inspected mathematical statements do not imply the exact all-base all-height quotient threshold, universal witness height, or six-to-seven regular-prism boundary. Public research-record searches comparing Steiner roots, right prisms, polygonal prisms, equivalent parameters, and real-rootedness did not identify a dominating or equivalent theorem. Absence from these searches does not prove priority. An earlier unindexed specialized study remains a possible risk.

## Value — PASS
Real-rootedness is a natural invariant of a convex body's outer parallel-volume polynomial, and the result gives a complete, sharp, dimension-three classification over every compact convex planar base and every positive height. It separates an entire class of universally real-rooted prism shapes from those admitting nonreal roots, with a single explicit witness height, and explains a natural regular hexagon/heptagon transition. This is materially stronger than checking one arbitrarily chosen base or a finite root sample.

## Scope and limitations
No complete supercritical height-phase diagram is claimed. The circular-cylinder special case and general polynomial product identity are prior work. The proof is self-contained modulo the planar Steiner formula and standard elementary facts, but scientific priority remains provisional without independent audit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
