The ordinary Steiner cubic of a right prism over any planar convex body has three real negative roots at every height exactly when the base's isoperimetric quotient satisfies \(4\pi A/P^2\le15\pi-9\pi^2/4-24\). Beyond that sharp threshold, one universal perimeter-normalized height produces a nonreal pair; among regular polygonal bases the dividing line is exactly six versus seven sides.
