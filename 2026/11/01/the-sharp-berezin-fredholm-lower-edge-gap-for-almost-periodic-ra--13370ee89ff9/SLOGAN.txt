For real continuous almost-periodic radial symbols of supremum norm at most one, the greatest possible gap between the limiting Berezin lower edge and the Fredholm essential spectral lower edge of a Gaussian Fock Toeplitz operator is exactly \(2[\operatorname{erf}(\sqrt{\log 2})-\operatorname{erf}(\sqrt{\log 2/2})]\), in every finite complex dimension. The bound is not attained by any such symbol, but smooth periodic symbols with increasingly separated negative bumps approach it sharply.
