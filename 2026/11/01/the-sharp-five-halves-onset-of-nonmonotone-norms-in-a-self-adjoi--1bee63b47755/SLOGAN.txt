For the self-adjoint matrix pencil \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-b&t\\t&bt^\alpha\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), norm reversal occurs exactly when \(\alpha>4\) and \(b>2/\sqrt{\alpha(\alpha-4)}\). Its globally largest backward overshoot at \(t=1\) has the sharp onset \(\Delta(4+\varepsilon)\sim\varepsilon^{5/2}/(100\sqrt5)\), with optimal scales \(\sqrt\varepsilon b\to\sqrt5\) and \((1-t)/\varepsilon\to1/10\).
