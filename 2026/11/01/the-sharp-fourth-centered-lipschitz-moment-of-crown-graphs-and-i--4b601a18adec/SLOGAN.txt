For the crown graph with \(n\ge3\) matched pairs, the exact maximal fourth centered moment of a real one-Lipschitz function is \(\max\{1/16+5/n,\ 1/2-\mathbf1_{n\,\mathrm{odd}}(1/(4n^2)+3/(16n^4))\}\). Every optimizer uses a single distance-three matched pair for \(n\le11\), but a balanced two-level split of one bipartition class for \(n\ge12\): a sharp moment-specific transition at twelve pairs.
