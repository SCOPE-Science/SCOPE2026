For independent uniform translates of the unit \(d\)-cube, the number needed to cover any fixed fraction \(\alpha\) of their doubled ambient cube has the almost-sure sharp logarithmic threshold \(\log T_{d,\alpha}=d+\Phi^{-1}(\alpha)\sqrt d-\gamma-(1-\Phi^{-1}(\alpha)^2)/3+o(1)\). In particular, half-volume coverage needs asymptotically \(e^{-\gamma-1/3}e^d\) translates, including a nontrivial constant factor beyond the Gaussian crossover.
