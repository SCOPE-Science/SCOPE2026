The ordinary three-cut complex of the cubic cycle power \(C_n^3\) is void for \(3\le n\le8\), nonshellable for \(9\le n\le12\), and shellable for every \(n\ge13\). For \(n\ge13\), its exact integral homotopy type is \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-6}{2}-20}S^{n-4}\); explicit finite shellings settle the two previously unproved boundary cases \(n=13,14\).
