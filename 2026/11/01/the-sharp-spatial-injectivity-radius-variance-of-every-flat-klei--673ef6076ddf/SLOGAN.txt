Among flat Klein bottles, the area-normalized spatial variance of pointwise injectivity radius has a unique maximizing aspect ratio \(a/b=0.2400998114\ldots\). The sharp maximum is \(64(\sqrt3-\log(2+\sqrt3))^3/[27(2\sqrt3-\log(2+\sqrt3))^4]\); a strict contraction of the normalized radii rules out all larger ratios beyond the first transition.
