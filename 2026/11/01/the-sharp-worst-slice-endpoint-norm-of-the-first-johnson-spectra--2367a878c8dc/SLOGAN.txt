Across all nontrivial Johnson slices of an \(n\)-element set, the first harmonic projection has asymptotically sharp worst \(\ell^\infty\) norm \(\sqrt{2n/e}\); all maximizing widths satisfy \(\min(k,n-k)\sim\sqrt{n/2}\). The critical Poisson crossover has descending maxima at half-integer overlap intensities, while balanced slices attain only the smaller constant \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}\).
