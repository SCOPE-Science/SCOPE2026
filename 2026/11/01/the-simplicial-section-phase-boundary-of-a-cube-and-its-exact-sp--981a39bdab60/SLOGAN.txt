For Haar-random hyperplanes through an interior point \(z\) of a \(d\)-cube, simplex sections have positive probability exactly outside the sharp crosspolytope \(\|z\|_1\le d-2\). Their full probability is an explicit finite Gaussian-cone sum, and moving diagonally past the boundary produces a sharp \((d-1)\)-st-order onset with an elementary exact law in dimension three.
