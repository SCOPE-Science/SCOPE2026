For the unfilled six-vertex pentagonal wheel, the **full** strong Morse matching complex simplicially collapses to a connected graph of rank \(16\), hence is \(\bigvee^{16}S^1\). Its rational first homology under the rim dihedral group \(D_5\) is \(\varepsilon^{\oplus4}\oplus U^{\oplus3}\), where \(\varepsilon\) is reflection sign and \(U\) is the four-dimensional rim-permutation augmentation module; in particular, it has no invariant vector.
