The full strong discrete-Morse matching complex of the unfilled triangular-prism graph simplicially collapses to a connected graph, so it has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{25}S^1\). Its first rational homology, under the full prism automorphism group \(G\cong S_3\times C_2\), is \(\mathbb Q[G]^{\oplus2}\oplus\mathbb Q_{\operatorname{sgn}_{S_3}}\), a sharp contrast with the ordinary Morse complex of the same graph.
