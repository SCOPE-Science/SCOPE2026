For the twelve Euclidean vertices of the regular truncated tetrahedron, inclusive Vietoris–Rips homotopy types pass through three circles, five two-spheres, and then a single two-sphere before becoming contractible. The oriented polyhedral boundary survives as a primitive integral homology generator across the transition at radius \(2\), giving one degree-two bar \([\sqrt3,\sqrt5)\) and four shorter bars \([\sqrt3,2)\).
