For the convex hull \(\operatorname{conv}([-1,1]^d\cup\{a e_1\})\), the unique minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid has exactly three sharp phases at \(a=\sqrt d\) and \(a=d\): a round cube ball, a centered anisotropic ellipsoid, then a translated ellipsoid of fixed transverse radius. In the final phase, the added apex and negative-facet cube vertices determine the ellipsoid while all positive-facet cube vertices remain extreme but cease to touch.
