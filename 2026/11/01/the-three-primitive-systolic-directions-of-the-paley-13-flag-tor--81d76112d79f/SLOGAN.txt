The Paley-13 flag torus has exactly 39 shortest nonzero integral-homology four-cycles, split into three equally populated primitive directions and permuted transitively by its affine symmetries. Its entire marked closed-edge-walk length spectrum is the explicit hexagonal lattice norm \(\ell(a,b)=\max(|a|,|b|,|a-b|)\) on \(a+3b\equiv0\pmod{13}\).
