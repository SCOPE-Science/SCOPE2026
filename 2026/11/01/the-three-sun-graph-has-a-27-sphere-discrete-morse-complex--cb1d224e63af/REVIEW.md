# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The graph, orientation rule, and complete face set are explicit. Two independent enumerators agree on the 1,444 nonempty faces. The pivot-order certificate pairs 708 faces; the exhaustive 5,388-edge Hasse DAG verification leaves one critical vertex and 27 critical four-cells. Forman's theorem directly yields the integral homotopy equivalence; the independently computed mod-two boundary ranks and exact Laplacian determinant corroborate the result. The check addresses the entire finite graph, not an extrapolation.

**Originality — PASS against inspected evidence.** The closest general primary results are Chari–Joswig's rooted-forest characterization and Euler polynomial, Celoria–Yerolemou's computations for complete graphs, Donovan–Scoville's extended-star/path/cycle results, and Jojić's complete-multipartite/dominant-pair shellability theorems. The three-sun is neither complete multipartite nor has a complete source or dominant pair. Consequently those theorems do not imply the claimed integral homotopy classification. Searches for three-sun/3-sun, directed forests, discrete-Morse matching complexes and the exact four-sphere rank did not identify covering statements. An unindexed equivalent calculation remains possible; search absence by itself is not a novelty proof.

**Value — PASS.** The three-sun is the first canonical sun graph and a natural boundary case in chordal versus strongly chordal graph theory. The result gives a complete integral topology for its rooted-forest complex in a graph class not handled by the nearest shellability formulas. The Laplacian determines only the alternating face count, so an exact 27-sphere homotopy decomposition supplies structurally stronger information. No unsupported generalization is asserted.

**Scientific limits and risks.** Finite case only; no full classification of sun graphs; an unindexed computation could coincide. Degree-four concentration was established by a complete matching, not deduced from Euler data. No independent audit has been carried out.
