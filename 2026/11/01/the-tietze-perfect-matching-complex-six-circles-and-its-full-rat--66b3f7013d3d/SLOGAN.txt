The perfect-matching complex of the Tietze graph collapses through an eight-vertex nerve to a connected graph with six independent cycles, so its integral homotopy type is \(\bigvee^6 S^1\). Its full twelve-element automorphism group acts on rational first homology with trace \(6\) at the identity, \(-2\) at three distinguished involutions, and zero elsewhere, leaving no nonzero invariant class.
