The open-neighborhood complex of the triangular graph \(T_7=L(K_7)\) has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{246}S^3\vee\bigvee^7 S^4\), upgrading its previously computed mod-two Betti ranks to a torsion-free mixed-dimensional sphere wedge. A complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching and a zero signed integer attaching matrix establish the homotopy equivalence.
