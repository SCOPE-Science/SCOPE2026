For every six-distinct-normal circular \(2\)-design, the area of its equal-edge Minkowski hexagon is an exact monotone function of the magnitude of its third Fourier moment. The regular hexagon is rigid at zero third moment, and the resulting area deficit satisfies sharp global quadratic inequalities with best coefficients \(2\sqrt3/9\) and \(\sqrt3/2\) after normalizing by squared edge length.
