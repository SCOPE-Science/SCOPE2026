# Three consecutive sphere dimensions in clique-free complexes of three-block clique paths

## Finding
For each integer \(t\ge3\) and each triple of integers \(p,q,r\ge t\), take pairwise disjoint sets \(A,B,C\) of respective cardinalities \(p,q,r\), and two different further vertices \(x,y\). Define a graph \(G\) whose only maximal cliques are \(A\cup\{x\}\), \(B\cup\{x,y\}\), and \(C\cup\{y\}\). Write \(X=\operatorname{CF}_t(G)\) for the simplicial complex of all vertex subsets containing no clique of order \(t\).

Put \(d=3t-4\) and
\[
\begin{aligned}
 b_d&=\binom{p-1}{t-1}\binom{q-1}{t-1}\binom{r-1}{t-1},\\
 b_{d-1}&=\binom{p-1}{t-2}\binom{q-1}{t-2}\binom{r-1}{t-1}
         +\binom{p-1}{t-1}\binom{q-1}{t-2}\binom{r-1}{t-2},\\
 b_{d-2}&=\binom{p-1}{t-2}\binom{q-1}{t-3}\binom{r-1}{t-2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Then the exact nonequivariant homotopy type is
\[
 |X|\simeq\bigvee^{b_{d-2}}S^{d-2}\ \vee\ 
              \bigvee^{b_{d-1}}S^{d-1}\ \vee\ 
              \bigvee^{b_d}S^d.
\]
All three multiplicities are positive. In particular, the entire reduced integral homology is torsion-free and supported in **exactly three consecutive dimensions**.

There is also an exact integral symmetry statement. Define \(U_A=\ker(\mathbb Z^A\xrightarrow{\mathrm{sum}}\mathbb Z)\), with analogous \(U_B,U_C\). Under the natural \(S_p\times S_q\times S_r\) action on private vertices, the three nonzero reduced integral homology representations are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widetilde H_d(X;\mathbb Z)&\cong
    \bigwedge^{t-1}U_A\otimes\bigwedge^{t-1}U_B\otimes\bigwedge^{t-1}U_C,\\
 \widetilde H_{d-1}(X;\mathbb Z)&\cong
   (\bigwedge^{t-2}U_A\otimes\bigwedge^{t-2}U_B\otimes\bigwedge^{t-1}U_C)
 \oplus
   (\bigwedge^{t-1}U_A\otimes\bigwedge^{t-2}U_B\otimes\bigwedge^{t-2}U_C),\\
 \widetilde H_{d-2}(X;\mathbb Z)&\cong
   \bigwedge^{t-2}U_A\otimes\bigwedge^{t-3}U_B\otimes\bigwedge^{t-2}U_C.
\end{aligned}
\]
Here all tensor products are over \(\mathbb Z\). This claim is about the homology representations, not an equivariant homotopy equivalence.

For the smallest uniform case \(t=p=q=r=3\), the type is
\[
 |X|\simeq\bigvee^4 S^3\vee\bigvee^8 S^4\vee S^5.
\]

## Assumptions and scope
There are no edges between two private vertex sets \(A,B,C\). The middle block contains **both** articulation vertices, but the left and right blocks each contain a **different** one. The graph is a block graph with precisely three maximal cliques. All theorem parameters are integers in the stated ranges; no limiting process or extrapolation is assumed. A binomial coefficient is zero outside its usual range, although all displayed multiplicities are positive under the theorem hypotheses.

## Proof
**1. Canonical augmented chains for simplex skeleta.** Let \(K(V,k)=\{F\subseteq V:|F|\le k\}\) for a finite set \(V\) of size \(n\) and \(0\le k<n\). For \(k=0\), this is the complex consisting only of the empty face, interpreted by augmented reduced chains. The reduced simplicial chain group in degree \(j\) of the full simplex is \(\bigwedge^{j+1}\mathbb Z^V\), with boundary the contraction by the coordinate-sum map \(\varepsilon:\mathbb Z^V\to\mathbb Z\). Because \(\varepsilon\) is split surjective, writing \(U_V=\ker\varepsilon\) gives
\[
 \widetilde H_i(K(V,k);\mathbb Z)=
 \begin{cases}
 \bigwedge^k U_V,&i=k-1,\\
 0,&i\ne k-1.
 \end{cases}
\]
This identification is natural for permutations of \(V\), including \(k=0\), when \(\bigwedge^0U_V\cong\mathbb Z\) lies in augmented degree \(-1\).

**2. Filter by articulation vertices.** Give the augmented chain complex \(\widetilde C_*(X;\mathbb Z)\) the filtration \(F_s\) generated by faces using at most \(s\) vertices from \(\{x,y\}\), for \(s=0,1,2\). Since a simplicial boundary only removes vertices, each \(F_s\) is a subcomplex. Every face can be uniquely partitioned into its articulation subset \(S\subseteq\{x,y\}\) and a private part. In the associated graded quotients \(F_s/F_{s-1}\), the differential deletes only private vertices: deleting a cut vertex lowers the filtration. Thus the summand indexed by each such \(S\) is a degree-\(|S|\) shift of the augmented chains of
\[
 K(A,k_A(S))*K(B,k_B(S))*K(C,k_C(S)),
\]
where the four cases, in the fixed order \(k_A,k_B,k_C\), are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
 S& (k_A,k_B,k_C)\\\hline
 \varnothing&(t-1,t-1,t-1)\\
 \{x\}&(t-2,t-2,t-1)\\
 \{y\}&(t-1,t-2,t-2)\\
 \{x,y\}&(t-2,t-3,t-2).
\end{array}
\]
These capacities follow by requiring at most \(t-1\) selected vertices in each of the three maximal cliques. The join formula over \(\mathbb Z\), together with Step 1 and the fact that all relevant homology lattices are free, identifies the sole nonzero graded group indexed by \(S\) with \(\bigotimes_{V=A,B,C}\bigwedge^{k_V(S)}U_V\). Its **absolute** homological degree is \(k_A(S)+k_B(S)+k_C(S)+|S|-1\).

**3. No differentials and no extensions.** For articulation size \(s=0\), the sole nonzero associated-graded homology lies in absolute degree \(d=3t-4\). For \(s=1\), the two summands lie in degree \(d-1\). For \(s=2\), the sole summand lies in degree \(d-2\). Every differential of the homological spectral sequence decreases filtration and reduces total homological degree by one. From filtration \(s\), a target of lower filtration would have to be in degree at most \(d-s-1\), whereas every nonzero group there is concentrated in degree \(d-s'\) for \(s'<s\); hence every possible differential is zero. The spectral sequence collapses at its first homology page. Moreover, for each total degree there is only one contributing filtration index, so no additive or integral-module extension is possible. This proves the three stated integral equivariant homology isomorphisms and vanishing elsewhere. Ranks follow from \(\operatorname{rank}\bigwedge^k U_V=\binom{|V|-1}{k}\).

**4. Upgrade to homotopy type.** This graph is a block graph. By Ghosh and Selvaraja, Corollary 3.4 of [1], its clique-free complex is nonpure shellable for every \(t\ge3\). By Björner and Wachs, Theorem 4.1 of [2], a nonpure shellable complex has the homotopy type of a wedge of spheres, with sphere multiplicities equal to its reduced integral Betti numbers. Step 3 computes every one of those numbers, giving precisely the displayed three-dimensional wedge. Since \(p,q,r\ge t\), all its binomial-product multiplicities are positive. This completes the proof.

## Verification
The companion file `verify.py` is a self-contained Python 3 program using only the standard library. Run `python verify.py`. It enumerates every permitted face in eight finite configurations and computes all reduced boundary ranks over \(\mathbb F_2\), comparing with the theorem's formulas. It additionally checks reduced signed simplicial Lefschetz traces against the proposed exterior-augmentation-module characters on every product conjugacy class for three configurations, covering respectively \(27\), \(30\), and \(125\) product cycle-type triples. All \(8\) homology and \(182\) symmetry comparisons passed in the packaged run. These finite checks are corroboration; the integral, all-parameter proof is Steps 1–4.

## Relationship to prior work
Ghosh and Selvaraja [1] prove shellability of clique-free complexes of arbitrary block graphs, including this graph, but their theorem does not give these explicit homotopy multiplicities or the integral private-vertex symmetry modules. Björner and Wachs [2] supply the general wedge-of-spheres implication for nonpure shellable complexes, but no Betti counts for this specific family. Earlier work of Khosh-Ahang and Moradi [3] studies clique ideals and shellability conditions rather than this three-block-path homology classification. The present result combines those structural inputs with a new articulation-filtration computation. The three-block path has two **distinct** cut vertices, unlike a multi-clique windmill with a single common hub, and it has three maximal cliques, unlike two-clique overlap families.

## Limitations
The homotopy equivalence is nonequivariant. The integral representation description concerns only internal permutations of the three private vertex sets; it does not describe possible symmetries interchanging the end blocks. No homotopy classification is asserted for arbitrary longer block trees, noncomplete graph petals, or threshold \(t=2\). The exact integral result is established by the structural proof, not by finite computations. Priority relative to unpublished or unindexed equivalent work cannot be ruled out; no independent mathematical audit is claimed.
