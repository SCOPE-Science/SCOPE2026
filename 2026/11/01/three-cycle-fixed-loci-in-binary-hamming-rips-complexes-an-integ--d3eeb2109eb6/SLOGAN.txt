At Hamming scale two, cycling three coordinates of a binary cube leaves a geometric fixed space homotopy equivalent to two smaller hypercube Rips complexes joined by \(2^{m+1}\) paths: \(K_m\vee K_m\vee\bigvee^{2^{m+1}-1}S^1\). Its integral first homology is the augmentation lattice on those paths, even though the ambient Rips complex is simply connected.
