Two reflection-coset directions together with rotation-coset sums recover every function on the dihedral group of order \(2n\) exactly when the reflection gap is coprime to \(n\), and their exact nullity otherwise is \(\gcd(a,n)-1\). The full singular spectrum yields a unique optimal weighting of the three direction families, improving sharp condition-number growth from order \(n^{3/2}\) to order \(n\).
