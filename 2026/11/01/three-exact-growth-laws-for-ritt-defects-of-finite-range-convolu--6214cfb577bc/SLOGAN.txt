For every nondegenerate finite-support probability walk on \(\mathbb Z\), its \(\ell^2\) convolution-power first defect grows either linearly with an explicit root-of-unity constant, as \(\sqrt n\) with coefficient \(|m|/\sqrt{ev}\), or converges to the universal value \(e^{-1}\). The precise case is determined by support congruences and drift; the Ritt property occurs exactly in the centered arithmetic-neutral case.
