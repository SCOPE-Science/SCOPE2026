For the disjointness graphs on maximum independent sets of the squared cycles \(W_8,W_{11},W_{14}\), the open neighborhood complexes have exact integral homotopy types \(S^2\vee S^2\vee S^3\), \(S^3\), and \(S^3\), respectively. Each differs from the homotopy type of its total-cut nerve, so the corresponding canonical nerve cover is not good.
