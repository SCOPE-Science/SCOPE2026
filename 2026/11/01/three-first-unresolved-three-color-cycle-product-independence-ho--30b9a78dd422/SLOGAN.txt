The three first unresolved categorical three-color cycle-product independence complexes have integral homotopy types \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_7\times K_3)\simeq S^3\vee\bigvee^3S^4\), \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_8\times K_3)\simeq S^4\vee\bigvee^3S^5\), and \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{10}\times K_3)\simeq\bigvee^3S^5\vee S^6\). Exhaustive discrete-Morse matchings with unit-maximal-minor integral differentials settle these three finite cases of a published torsion-freeness question, without asserting a uniform theorem.
