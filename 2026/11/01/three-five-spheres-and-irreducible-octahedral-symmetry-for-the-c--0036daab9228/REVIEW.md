# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The object is explicitly the capacity-two edge-subset complex of the simple unfilled three-cube. The reproducible complete enumeration yields 1698 nonempty simplices and is independently checked against eight forbidden three-edge stars, which are all triangles of the cuboctahedral line graph. The 847 edge-index toggling pairs are checked individually against the face poset, and all 7980 codimension-one incidences are used in a complete acyclicity test. The four critical cells are one zero-cell and three five-cells, so standard discrete Morse theory proves the full integral wedge homotopy type without a homology-to-homotopy guess. Signed Lefschetz traces are independently computed for 48 actions in two group parameterizations; exact agreement identifies the irreducible rational homology representation. The code and decoded proof were checked on the actual standalone file.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The cube is the smallest nontrivial cubic hypercube and the crown graph on four pairs, giving a natural graph and a natural hollow chessboard model. Its nonpure capacity-two complex extends through dimension seven, but the verified Morse collapse has homotopy concentrated in dimension five. The completely identified 48-element rational symmetry action is an irreducible three-dimensional module that changes sign under swapping the bipartition. This information is not a textbook deduction or a simple relabeling of the known ordinary matching or complete-bipartite calculation; the forbidden-diagonal geometry and exact equivariant topology motivate this particular finite invariant. A general all-hypercube theorem is not claimed.
