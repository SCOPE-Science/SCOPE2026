The two-matching complex of the three-dimensional cube has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^3 S^5\), despite having facets through dimension seven. Its full 48-element automorphism action on fifth rational homology is the irreducible \(S_4\times C_2\)-module \(S^{(2,1,1)}\boxtimes\varepsilon\), with the bipartition interchange acting by minus the identity.
