For the planar quartic disk, the Fourier-mean body is compact below exponent \(5/2\), unbounded but of finite area from \(5/2\) to \(3\), and the whole plane at and above \(3\). Its first loss of compactness forms four logarithmic horns: the critical radial power has sharp angular coefficient \(20\sqrt\pi/3\), while the approaching axial residue is \(5\sqrt\pi\).
