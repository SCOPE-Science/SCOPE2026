The ordinary multipath complex of the bidirected octahedral graph \(K_{2,2,2}\) is simply connected, with torsion-free reduced integral homology of ranks \(3,92,32\) in degrees \(2,3,4\) and no homology elsewhere. An explicit acyclic discrete-Morse matching and its signed integer differential establish these exact groups, without asserting a sphere-wedge homotopy type.
