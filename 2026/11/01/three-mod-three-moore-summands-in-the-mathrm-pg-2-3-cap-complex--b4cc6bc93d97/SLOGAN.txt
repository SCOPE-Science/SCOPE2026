The natural thirteen-point noncollinearity complex of \(\mathrm{PG}(2,3)\) has the unexpected integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^3M(\mathbb Z/3,2)\vee\bigvee^{66}S^3\), exhibiting three independent mod-three Moore summands. Its lower-dimensional torsion is invisible to rational Betti numbers and does not follow from the classical finite-projective-plane arc counts.
