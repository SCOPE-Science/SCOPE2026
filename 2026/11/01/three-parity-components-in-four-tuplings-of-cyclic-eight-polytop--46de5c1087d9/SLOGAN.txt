The four-tupling of every cyclic eight-polytope boundary is a graph: for odd \(n\ge9\) it is a connected wedge of circles, while for even \(n\ge10\) it splits into exactly three parity-class components, each with an explicit circle-rank formula. Four is the smallest tuple size for which the parity-complement invariant can have three values, and the theorem realizes all three as actual components.
