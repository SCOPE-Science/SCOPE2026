# Scientific review — three rank-three theories of flag-adjacency graphs of finite Steiner \(2\)-designs

## Correctness (C): PASS
For every design \(2\)-\((v,k,1)\) with \(2\le k<v\), the proof establishes the block-replication equation and the uniform pair-extension invariant for the graph on incident point–block flags. It explicitly handles all equal, adjacent and nonadjacent pairs, both common-neighbour options and the disjoint-neighbour option. The general three-round Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé back-and-forth is reconstructed. The exceptional six-cycle is analyzed exactly, and two rank-three sentences separate all classes. The sharp representative count and the separate Johnson comparison with rank-four induced-claw separator follow algebraically. Finite direct enumeration and independent exact logical-type recursion agree on the checked sample families; their finite coverage is not mistaken for a general proof.

## Originality (O): PASS, with residual priority risks
The main comparisons are foundational Ehrenfeucht game methods (Verbitsky, arXiv:math/0401361v1), point-line Levi graph extremal theory (De Winter–Lazebnik–Verstraëte, DOI:10.37236/867), the known rank-three Johnson classification, and results about Steiner design point collinearity, uncoloured projective-plane Levi graphs and Hamming line graphs. Neither the definitions of the latter objects nor their stated logical claims imply the universal flag-adjacency classification. In particular, replacing a Levi-graph vertex by an incidence edge is not a quantifier-rank-preserving transformation. The exact cross-equivalence with \(J(7,3)\) is proved rather than inferred from the prior Johnson theorem. Accessible searches found no covering implication; this is not a claim of exhaustive priority verification. Possible more general results on low-rank line-graph types remain a risk.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS
This is an exact classification of a natural graph construction over **every** finite Steiner \(2\)-design (including the two-point-block boundary) rather than a sample or an unmotivated parameter slice. Its sharp \(6,12,21\) representative sizes, insensitivity to design isomorphism and the common three-round theory with Johnson graphs, followed by the first four-round separation, are structural and reusable in shallow finite-model-theoretic comparisons of incidence geometries.

## Limits
The three-class assertion concerns pure adjacency/equality through quantifier rank three only. The rank-four theorem is restricted to the cross-family Johnson separator, not a general rank-four classification. Self-review is not independent proof certification. Primary papers were inspected to the material specified in the structured audit; literature priority is not exhaustive.
