The line graphs of Levi incidence graphs of **all** finite Steiner \(2\)-designs \(2\)-\((v,k,1)\), with \(2\le k<v\), have exactly three first-order rank-three theories, represented by graphs on \(6\), \(12\) and \(21\) flags. Every design with block size at least three shares its three-round theory with the Johnson graph \(J(7,3)\), although an induced claw separates the two graph families at the sharp quantifier rank four.
