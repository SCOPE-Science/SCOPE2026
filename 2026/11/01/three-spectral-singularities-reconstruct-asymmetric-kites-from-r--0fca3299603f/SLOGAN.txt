The random-direction projection-width density of every Euclidean kite is an explicit sum of at most three inverse-square-root branches, with a sharp one-minimum versus two-minimum angular phase diagram. In the ordered three-peak region, its three square-root singularities have amplitudes \(2/\pi,4/\pi,2/\pi\) and uniquely reconstruct both axial tip ratios and the scale, without direction labels.
