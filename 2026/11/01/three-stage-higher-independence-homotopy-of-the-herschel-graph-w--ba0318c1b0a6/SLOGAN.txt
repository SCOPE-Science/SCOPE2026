For the eleven-vertex Herschel graph, the first three higher-independence complexes have integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^2S^2\), \(\bigvee^{10}S^3\), and \(\bigvee^3S^4\vee S^5\), respectively; the first was previously known. Both successive natural inclusions are nullhomotopic, and the new third stage exhibits a mixed-dimensional sphere wedge.
