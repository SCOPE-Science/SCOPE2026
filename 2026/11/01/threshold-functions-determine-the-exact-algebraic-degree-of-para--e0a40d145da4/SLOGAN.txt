For a \(d\)-dimensional parallelotope, the least degree in the volume variable of a polynomial equation valid for all hyperplane-clipped volumes is exactly \(T_d-2\), where \(T_d\) counts the Boolean affine threshold functions on \(d\) variables. The first four sharp degrees are \(2,12,102,1880\); the proof identifies a different indispensable rational volume branch for every nonconstant threshold function.
