For every once-subdivided star with \(m\ge3\) arms, its higher-independence complexes have a sharp contractible–sphere-wedge–contractible threshold pattern: the middle range has exactly \(\binom{m-1}{r-m-1}\) spheres of dimension \(r-1\). Its unique nonzero integral homology carries the full arm-permutation lattice \(\operatorname{sgn}_m\otimes\bigwedge^{r-m-1}\ker(\mathbb Z^m\to\mathbb Z)\), not just the predicted Betti number.
