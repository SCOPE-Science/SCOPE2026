For matched-base affine random interval maps, swapping the probabilities of expansion and uniform inverse-branch contraction reverses the Lyapunov exponent but leaves every stationary coordinate autocovariance unchanged. The integrated covariance has an exact cusp at equal probabilities, and the critical \(n^{-3/2}\) decay has parity-dependent constants and a Gaussian \(n^{-1/2}\) probability-window crossover.
