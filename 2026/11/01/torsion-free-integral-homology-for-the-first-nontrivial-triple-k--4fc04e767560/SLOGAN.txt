The independence complex of the Kneser graph on the thirty-five triples of a seven-element set has torsion-free reduced integral homology: \(\mathbb Z^{29}\) in dimension six, \(\mathbb Z^7\) in dimension nine, and zero elsewhere. This verifies the first nontrivial case of the integral torsion-freeness question left open by the rational classification; the Betti numbers themselves were already known.
