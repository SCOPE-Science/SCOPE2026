For every odd-order finite abelian group \(A\) with \(|A|\ge5\), the translation group \(A\times A\) acts on the second rational homology of its group-table Latin-square graph clique complex as the tensor product of three pulled-back augmentation representations (row, column, and sum). Every complex character \((\alpha,\beta)\) appears with multiplicity \(|A|-|\{\mathbf1,\alpha,\beta\}|\), so the translation-invariant homology has dimension \(|A|-1\).
