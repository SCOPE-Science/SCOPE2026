For the Latin-square graph of every elementary abelian two-group of order \(q\ge8\), exchanging rows and columns has a geometric fixed-point space homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{(q-1)(q/2-1)}S^1\). A canonical orbit-clique triangulation exhibits one central simplex, \(q-1\) peripheral simplices, and the intercalate interfaces that create these integral circle classes.
