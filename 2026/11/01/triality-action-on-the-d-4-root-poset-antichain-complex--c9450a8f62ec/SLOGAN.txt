For the first nonshellable type-\(D_4\) root-poset antichain complex, triality acts on rational first homology as \(\mathbf 1\oplus 2V\) and reverses the unique second-homology orientation under diagram transpositions. Its three-cycle and full-\(S_3\) geometric fixed sets agree exactly, while each diagram transposition fixes two independent circles and two isolated points.
