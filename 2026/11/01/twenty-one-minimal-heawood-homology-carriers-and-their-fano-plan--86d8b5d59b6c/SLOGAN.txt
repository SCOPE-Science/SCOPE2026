The Heawood independence complex has exactly twenty-one ten-vertex supports for nonzero mod-two third homology, each inducing a graph \(C_8\sqcup K_2\); every induced-subcomplex inclusion is injective on that homology. Their twenty-one distinct classes span a six-dimensional space, and their exactly twenty-eight three-term relations are indexed by a point distinguished in the complement of a Fano line.
