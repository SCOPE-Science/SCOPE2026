# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The precise objects are graph edges of the Fano point-line incidence graph; a simplex is an induced matching, not an arbitrary matching. The two independent face algorithms agree on \((f_{-1},f_0,f_1,f_2)=(1,21,84,84)\), no higher faces. The included concrete certificate contracts 20 spanning-tree edges and sequentially cancels 64 one-cells using triangles with precisely one previously unresolved boundary edge. The 20 surviving two-cells attach to a point and establish the integral homotopy equivalence \(\mathcal S(H)\simeq\bigvee^{20}S^2\). Signed chain maps satisfy \(\partial_1\partial_2=0\); modular ranks are \(20,64\). All 336 automorphisms are constructed, checked simplicial, and their full signed Lefschetz character calculated. Risks: the geometric collapse uses standard CW/Tietze cancellations, not a claim of literal elementary simplicial collapse of the original triangulation; no infinite family proof or integral representation splitting is asserted.

**Originality (PASS, with residual risk).** Joos, Rautenbach and Sasse's arXiv:1312.1110v1 supplies the 2013 induced-matching definition and an extremal bound, not simplicial topology. The accessible full text of Skupień's paper on induced matchings of the same Petersen and Heawood graphs studies strong edge colouring and matchings of size three, not a topological complex or automorphism representation. Primary full scientific statements for the ordinary Heawood matching complex (sixteen four-spheres), its full homology character, the extendible perfect-matching complex (sixteen two-spheres), and the bounded-degree two-matching complex (six ten-spheres) were inspected. Their simplex rules differ and their conclusions do not imply the present 20-sphere classification or the 336-element degree-two character. Searches for squared-line-graph independence complexes, the exact face counts and sphere number, and Fano-flag interpretations did not identify covering literature. Risks: unindexed/differently indexed exact or stronger results may remain; database partitioning is not a novelty theorem.

**Value (PASS).** Strong edge matchings are a natural established graph invariant underlying strong edge colouring. The finite Heawood geometry is a canonical highly symmetric graph, and this establishes the *complete integral homotopy type* and rational *full-automorphism homology character* of the induced edge-matching complex, including the vanishing invariant rational class. These are nontrivial structural invariants not supplied by maximal matching cardinalities or existing homotopy calculations of different complexes. This is a finite benchmark, not a family theorem.
