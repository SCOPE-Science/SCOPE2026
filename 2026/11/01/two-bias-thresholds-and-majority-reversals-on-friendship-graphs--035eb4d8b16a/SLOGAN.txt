On the friendship graph of \(n\) triangles, synchronous binary majority always freezes by round two, but under unbiased initial opinions the final global majority reverses the initial one with probability \(1/2-1/(2\sqrt{\pi n})+O(n^{-3/2})\). With an initially dissenting center, peripheral bias produces separate thresholds at \(p=1/2\) for a first-round flip and \(p=1/\sqrt2\) for a permanent flip, both with Gaussian critical windows.
