One eastbound and one northbound car on a periodic rectangular BML traffic grid have three sharply different fates determined by the side-length gcd: eventual free flow for \(\gcd(m,n)\ge3\), one block per species every \(\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)+1\) updates for gcd two, and perpetual one-species blocking at a modular-inverse-determined period for coprime sides. Thus persistent traffic obstruction survives at arbitrarily small car density on coprime rectangles, while the analogous square grids clear after at most one block.
