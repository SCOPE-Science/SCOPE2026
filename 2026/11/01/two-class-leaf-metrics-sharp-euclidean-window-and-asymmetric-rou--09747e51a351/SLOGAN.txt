For the leaves alone of a weighted star with two non-singleton arm-length classes, the exact negative-type exponent is the unique root of \((1-1/m)(2a/(a+b))^p+(1-1/n)(2b/(a+b))^p=2\). Its Euclidean-embedding window is an explicit interval of length ratios, and near the equilateral limit its generalized roundness diverges with two different one-sided constants whenever the class sizes differ.
