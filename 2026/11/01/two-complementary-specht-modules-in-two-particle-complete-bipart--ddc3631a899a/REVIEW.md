# Review of the two-particle complete-bipartite representation theorem

## Correctness — PASS

All assumptions are explicit: \(m,n\ge3\), unordered collision-free points on a topological graph, rational representations. Ko and Park's Theorem 3.16 applies to nonplanar three-connected \(K_{m,n}\), giving the first Betti rank \((m-1)(n-1)\); the natural total-trajectory map is equivariant and surjective since four-cycles span graph cycles. The deleted-product cubical model is valid without further subdivision for two points. Its three signed chain modules are decomposed by disjoint-cell types and orientation of exchanged edges. An exact representation-ring Euler identity and rational semisimplicity yield the two Specht constituents. The full-part-swap trace is independently deduced from signed cells. An explicit executable finite checker verifies chain compositions, ranks and complete symmetry traces in the named small cases. Finite-field results are not used to infer the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks

Published source [1], Theorem 3.16, gives the needed first-homology rank and Corollary 3.17 gives the general **ordinary second-Betti-number formula**, but neither determines the second-homology representation. The full thesis material relevant to complete bipartite graphs in [2, Section 5.2] derives ordered-space Betti numbers and intersection kernels, not the stated unordered \(S_m\times S_n\)-irreducible decomposition. A published similar complete-bipartite *sink* configuration result allows collisions at graph vertices, unlike the ordinary configuration space here, and its metadata do not claim to cover this object. published-finding corpus searches including Specht-partition notation and the balanced interchange character found no identical or strictly dominating claim. One previous exact \(K_{4,4}\) homology-basis record gives an integral rank-and-torus theorem at one parameter only, not the all-parameter symmetry classification. Residual risk remains from unindexed equivariant literature or an implicitly equivalent theorem not recognized by semantic search.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result resolves the full row-column permutation character for a natural infinite family of nonplanar graph-configuration spaces. The surprising cancellation to exactly two complementary irreducible exterior-product constituents reveals much more than the known Betti ranks, identifies an induced representation for equal-sized parts, and forces absence of any rational class invariant under all graph automorphisms. Its invariant and symmetry statements have direct relevance to configuration spaces on unlabeled bipartite networks. This is not a classification of arbitrary graph braid spaces or an independent integer-lattice result.
