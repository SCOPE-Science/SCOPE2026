The second rational homology of two unordered collision-free points on every \(K_{m,n}\), \(m,n\ge3\), is precisely \((S^{(m-2,2)}\boxtimes S^{(n-2,1,1)})\oplus(S^{(m-2,1,1)}\boxtimes S^{(n-2,2)})\), with each module of the form \(S^{(k-2,2)}\) defined as zero when \(k=3\). This determines every part-relabeling character, proves the absence of fully symmetric homology classes, and gives zero trace for every side-interchanging symmetry in the balanced case.
