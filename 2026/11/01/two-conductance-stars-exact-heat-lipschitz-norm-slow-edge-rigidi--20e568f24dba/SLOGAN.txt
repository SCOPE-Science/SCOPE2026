On a star with one heat edge of conductance \(a\) and \(k\ge2\) other edges of conductance \(b>a\), the exact graph-Lipschitz heat norm is a two-exponential function and its unique maximizing edge is always the slow edge. At fixed star size its sharp transient amplification has the unattained envelope \(2-1/(k+1)\), approached only as the conductance ratio diverges; all positive-time extremizers are rigid three-level ramps.
