Deletion–link then splits as a homotopy cofibration. The proof treats \(r=1\), the zero-multiplicity cases, and \(r\geq a+c+3\) separately. No finite computation is used to infer an all-parameter result. The finite checker verifies 128 parameter triples with exact mod-two boundary ranks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete natural family at the first missing-whisker boundary of the fully-whiskered theorem, not an arbitrary selected graph or small numerical increment.
