For two equally likely bilinear oracle slopes \(1\) and \(2\), the optimal coupling between the prediction and correction calls of stochastic extragradient switches from common to complementary sampling at squared step \(2/9\) for mean-square error, but already at \((13-\sqrt{105})/16\) for typical-path exponential decay. The opposing objectives select opposite couplings on an explicit interval, and complementary sampling extends the sharp mean-square stability ceiling to squared step \(3/8\).
