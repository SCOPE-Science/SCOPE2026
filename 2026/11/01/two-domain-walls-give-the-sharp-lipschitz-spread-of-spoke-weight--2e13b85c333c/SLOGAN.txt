A spoke-weighted wheel has an exact two-branch Lipschitz-variance formula: below spoke length \(1/2\), balanced arbitrary rim signs are optimal, while above it every optimizer has precisely two domain-wall vertices separating opposite extreme blocks. The sharp variance has a downward derivative jump of \(4/(n+1)\) for even rims and \(4n/(n+1)^2\) for odd rims at that geometric threshold.
