Two even Euclidean chord-distance moments of independent area-uniform points determine every spherical-surface cap in \(\mathbb R^3\), including its unknown sphere radius: the feasible normalized fourth-moment ratio is exactly \([4/3,5/3)\). The inverse cutoff-height map has an optimal global square-root bound with sharp constant \(2\sqrt3\), while recovery of the cap opening angle has the optimal uniform Hölder exponent \(1/4\).
