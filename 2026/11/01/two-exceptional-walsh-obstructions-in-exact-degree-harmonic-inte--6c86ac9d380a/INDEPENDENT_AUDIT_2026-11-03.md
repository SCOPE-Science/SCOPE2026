# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For d>=3, sign-change symmetry splits evaluation image of homogeneous degree-ell harmonic polynomials on the normalized Boolean cube into one-dimensional Walsh sectors. A sector k=|S| is nonzero exactly when the moment polynomial ℓ![t^ℓ]∏_{i∈S}sinh(tz_i)∏_{j∉S}cosh(tz_j) is not divisible by q(z)=Σz_i². Fischer pairing plus irreducible complex null cone justify the test. Explicit null vectors and coefficient extraction show all mixed sectors (1<=k<=d-1) occur whenever k<=ell and correct parity; extremes k=0 and k=d disappear only at ell=2 and ell=d+2 respectively. Thus rank is sum C(d,k) over 0<=k<=min(d,ell) of matching parity minus the two indicated unit deficits. An independent exact rational Gegenbauer/Krawtchouk sum checked 663 (d,ell,k) sectors for 3<=d<=8 and 0<=ell<=16 with correct signs; finite spectra are diagnostic, not a proof of infinite ranges.

PASS

Moore–Russell Appendix A already supplies implicit general spherical-harmonic-to-Walsh energy formula for this same cube and is explicitly credited. No published simplification identified in inspected Appendix or Resultary; a mechanical or previously known reduction remains a priority risk.
