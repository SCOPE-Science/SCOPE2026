# Review: exact-degree hypercube harmonic interpolation

## Correctness — PASS

The evaluation image is a Walsh-isotypic sum because the coordinate sign-flip group acts on the harmonic space and the sampled data space. The proof converts each candidate Walsh direction into a homogeneous moment polynomial. Orthogonality under the Fischer pairing and irreducibility of the complex null quadric (for dimensions at least three) give a necessary-and-sufficient harmonic nonvanishing test. Exact null-vector substitutions prove that all mixed Walsh weights occur; the extreme weights have exactly the constant-degree-two and full-product-degree-\(d+2\) exceptions. These are the sole missing directions in the claimed range. An independent exact-rational finite calculation checked 741 degree/weight/dimension sectors, but the universal result is proved analytically.

## Originality — PASS, bounded

Bannai–Okuda–Tagami (arXiv:1308.5101v1) frame harmonic-index design questions through vanishing of spherical-harmonic sums. Moore–Russell (arXiv:1408.5425v1), Appendix A, obtain the complete *implicit* Krawtchouk/Gegenbauer energy expression for Walsh restrictions on this same cube. That formula and the general harmonic-to-Walsh connection are prior knowledge and not claimed here. The present contribution is the **uniform closed zero-sector and rank classification** for every ambient dimension and exact degree. Matched literature searches did not identify that stated classification; independently unindexed earlier work remains a residual originality risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Complete ranks and missing data directions of a canonical symmetric spherical sampling problem are natural interpolation invariants. The theorem replaces an unevaluated infinite parameter family of special-function sums by a simple binomial formula, and exhibits an isolated full-product Walsh dropout after an already surjective degree, followed by permanent recovery. This is a structural all-dimension statement rather than a numerical census.

## Limitations

No statement is made about conditioning or positive singular values beyond their nonvanishing, or about perturbed or non-cubical point sets. The originality check is bounded; independent validation remains outstanding.
