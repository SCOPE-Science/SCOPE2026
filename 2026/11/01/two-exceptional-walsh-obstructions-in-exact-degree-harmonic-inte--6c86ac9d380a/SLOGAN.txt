For exact-degree spherical harmonics sampled at the \(2^d\) vertices of a regular \(d\)-cube, \(d\ge3\), every parity-compatible Walsh mode of weight at most the degree occurs except the constant mode at degree \(2\) and the full-product mode at degree \(d+2\). Consequently the evaluation rank is \(\sum_{k\le\min(d,\ell),\ k\equiv\ell\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 2)}\binom dk-\mathbf1_{\{\ell=2\}}-\mathbf1_{\{\ell=d+2\}}\), and full parity interpolation permanently returns at degree \(d+3\).
