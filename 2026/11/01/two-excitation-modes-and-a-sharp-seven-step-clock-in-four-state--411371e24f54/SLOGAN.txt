For synchronous four-state Greenberg–Hastings dynamics with excitation threshold two on every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), all trajectories satisfy \(F^{11}=F^7\), and the only nontrivial periodic states comprise two rigid four-phase families. The exact four-cycle orbit count is \(\frac12S(a)S(b)+\frac14T(a)T(b)\), with a sharp seven-update entrance time appearing precisely when the two shores have at least seven and eight vertices.
