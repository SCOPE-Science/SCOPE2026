The first higher-independence complexes of the strongly regular twins are \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(\mathrm{Shrikhande})\simeq\bigvee^{57}S^3\) and \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(4\times4\text{ rook})\simeq\bigvee^{105}S^3\), despite identical adjacency spectra. Complete acyclic matchings show this integrally, and the sphere-count ordering reverses that of their known ordinary independence complexes.
