The cutoff-two higher-independence complexes of the two nonisomorphic strongly regular graphs with parameters \((16,6,2,2)\) are \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(K_4\square K_4)\simeq\bigvee^{105}S^3\) and \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(\mathrm{Shrikhande})\simeq\bigvee^{57}S^3\). Their low-dimensional face counts agree, yet explicit acyclic Morse matchings and integral unit attaching degrees separate their complete homotopy types.
