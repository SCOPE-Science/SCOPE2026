For sequential characteristic-two neighbor-parity dynamics on even-order complete graphs, deleting one edge shortens the longest cycle, but deleting two disjoint edges and scanning each missing pair consecutively raises the maximum sweep period from \(n+1\) to \(2(n-3)\) for every even \(n\ge8\). An explicit linear conjugacy determines every primitive orbit count for all matching sizes and every finite characteristic-two field.
