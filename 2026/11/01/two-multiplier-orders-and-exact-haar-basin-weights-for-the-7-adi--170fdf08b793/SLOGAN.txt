For \(x\mapsto x^2+1\) on \(\mathbb Z_7\), every nontrivial minimal component is an explicit two-cycle or six-cycle odometer shell, and all of their global Haar basin weights are determined. The first six-cycle shell receives a critical preimage branch of measure \(1/7\), giving mass \(31/49\), while the two fixed-point neighborhoods attract basin masses \(2/7\) and \(5/7\).
