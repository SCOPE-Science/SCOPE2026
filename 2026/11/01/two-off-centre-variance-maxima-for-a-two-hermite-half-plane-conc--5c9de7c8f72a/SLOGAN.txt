In the first two Hermite levels, the half-line concentration defect \(\operatorname{tr}(A(t)(I-A(t)))\) has exactly two global maxima at nonzero opposite thresholds, even though the one-level defect peaks at the origin. Their unique positive location is characterized by an explicit error-function equation and equals \(0.748111391996\ldots\) in standard Hermite units.
