For a unit-edge regular simplex in every dimension \(d\ge3\), the random directional width has two exact upper-tail regimes: a disjoint-edge-cap law above \(\sqrt3/2\), and a pairwise spherical-lens correction down to \(\sqrt{2/3}\). The first correction is a negative explicit multiple of \((\sqrt3/2-t)^{d/2}\), while triple overlaps begin sharply at the second threshold.
