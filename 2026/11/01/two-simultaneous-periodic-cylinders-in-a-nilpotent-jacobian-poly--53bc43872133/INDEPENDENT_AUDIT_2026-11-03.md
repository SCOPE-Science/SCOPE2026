# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The exact global polynomial shear (u,v,w)=(x,y+x²,z) transforms the vector field without reparametrizing time to u'=v²−K, v'=w, w'=(v²−K)²−r². Independently differentiated the original-coordinate Jacobian J and verified symbolically J³=0 at all points, not inferring it from shear (nonlinear conjugacy would not suffice). U'(v)=r²−(v²−K)², so planar H=w²/2+U(v) is conserved; no fixed point since g=0 forces z'=-r². For r>0,K>=1.5r, critical v=-sqrt(K+r), -sqrt(K-r), +sqrt(K-r), +sqrt(K+r) alternate minima/maxima with two disjoint compact regular oval annuli on strictly positive left and strictly negative right H-levels (possibly zero saddle at the boundary K=1.5r, still open-energy separation). On shrinking left oval mean g→+r, at its outer separatrix saddle mean→−r; right annulus reverses the signs. The separatrix limit follows by logarithmically divergent saddle residence versus bounded time away, not numerical quadrature. Continuity yields an interior zero of D(E)=∫g(v(t))dt in each annulus. Each lifted R×oval at D=0 is a smooth isochronous periodic cylinder with minimal original physical-time planar period, distinct by energy sign. Independent exact symbolic J³=0,U'+g²−r²=0 and energy ranges checked for (K,r)=(1.5,1),(2,1),(3,1). No uniqueness or total count of cylinders claimed.

## originality

PASS

Castañeda–Rebollo-Perdomo arxiv2209.06986 has the same ambient general skew form (formula 9) and Section 5 a quadratic P1 with two planar annuli, but it explicitly asks how many 3D periodic surfaces; its Proposition 1.3 proves one isochronous cylinder for a different linear P1 case. A separate published Resultary even-kinetic skew flow has one annulus, nonnilpotent Jacobian and power-law period limits; no logical implication of the present simultaneous two-cylinder existence. The audited new step is to show both axial drifts change sign, using the double-well symmetric potential plus energy separation.

## value

PASS

The construction answers an explicit natural question left by the highly relevant original article: two planar period annuli do not automatically give two 3D periodic cylinders. This proves an actual simultaneous axial zero-drift witness in an everywhere-nilpotent Jacobian, equilibrium-free polynomial field for a full nontrivial two-parameter region, a meaningful dynamical structural phenomenon.
