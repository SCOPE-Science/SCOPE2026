# Scientific review: two simultaneous periodic cylinders

## Correctness — PASS

The exact polynomial shear conjugates the original flow without time change to an axial skew product over a separable Hamiltonian. Direct computation in the original coordinates gives a nilpotent Jacobian with third power zero; the flow has no equilibria. The quintic potential has two nondegenerate centers and distinct saddle barriers, which are separated by energy sign for all parameters in the theorem. A continuous axial return displacement has strictly opposite signs, in its normalized average, between each center and its hyperbolic saddle. The intermediate-value theorem supplies two genuinely periodic invariant cylinders. Critical endpoint and equality cases are covered. The six numerical witnesses are supplementary, not the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Castañeda–Rebollo-Perdomo's 2022 work contains the exact general construction and an **example with two planar period annuli**; those are not claimed as new. Its Proposition 1.3 establishes one periodic cylinder in a linear subfamily, and its closing question expressly distinguishes the number of actual periodic surfaces from the number of annuli. The present statement establishes simultaneous vanishing of the two axial drift integrals, which is not implied merely by the two-well phase portrait. A separate, inspected even-kinetic skew-flow result concerns a single annulus and a non-nilpotent field. Neither source supplies this two-cylinder existence region. This priority conclusion is bounded by inspected sources, not a proof of uniqueness in the literature; the later journal full text and unindexed general theorems are residual risks.

## Value — PASS

The mathematical motivation is an explicit open structural multiplicity question for three-dimensional nilpotent-Jacobian flows. The answer exhibits not just a pair of planar periodic annuli but **two independently closed original-time axial lifts**, for every parameter in a nontrivial region, in a nonvanishing polynomial flow. The construction separates the easy integrability input from the decisive sign change of each return displacement and gives concrete structural examples for further study of cyclic invariant surfaces.

## Scope and limitations

At least two cylinders are proved, not an exact maximum, uniqueness or sharp parameter threshold. No global attraction, period uniformity between cylinders, or perturbation persistence is asserted. Finite computation only tests representative parameters. The later journal full text is not inspected; independent validation has not been conducted.
