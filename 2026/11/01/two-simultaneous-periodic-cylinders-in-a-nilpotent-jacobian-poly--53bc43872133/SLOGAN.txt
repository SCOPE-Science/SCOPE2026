A single everywhere-nilpotent polynomial vector field on three-space, with no equilibrium, possesses at least two distinct invariant cylinders completely foliated by original-time periodic trajectories throughout the region \(r>0\), \(K\ge3r/2\). The two cylinders arise from opposite-sign zero-drift levels of disjoint Hamiltonian wells, strengthening the previously known existence of two planar period annuli.
