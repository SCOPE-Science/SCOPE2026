For a Haar-random central hyperplane section of the unit cross-polytope in each fixed dimension \(d\ge2\), the probability that its intrinsic Euclidean inradius falls within \(\varepsilon\) of the known optimum \(1/\sqrt{d-1}\) has an explicit two-term asymptotic with exponent \(d-1\). The coefficients arise from an exact signed-partition discrepancy identity and local spherical-simplex geometry near coordinate normals.
