The ordinary multipath complexes of the fully bidirected graphs \(K_{2,4}\) and \(K_{2,5}\) have exact integral second homology \(\mathbb Z^7\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) and \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\), respectively. Each entire digraph is an unstable dynamical module, giving two explicit counterexamples to the proposed implication that homology torsion forces dynamical-module stability.
