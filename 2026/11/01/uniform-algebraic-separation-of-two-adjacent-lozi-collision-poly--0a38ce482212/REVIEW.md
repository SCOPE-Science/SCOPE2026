The algebraic implication from a nonzero resultant to no common complex root is exact, not extrapolated from finitely many samples.

Direct checks against nearby research records for the same words, resultant, aliases and border-collision equations identified no equivalent stronger coverage. The two words are the adjacent length-six/seven instances displayed in the published study of creation-order phenomena, rather than arbitrary parameter selections.
