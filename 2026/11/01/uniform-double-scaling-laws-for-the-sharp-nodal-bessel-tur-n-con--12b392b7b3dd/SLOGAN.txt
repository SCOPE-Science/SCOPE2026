When the positive Bessel order and the nodal index grow at the same rate, the sharp normalized Turán minimum converges to the nonconstant profile \(1-t^{-2}\), where \(\sqrt{t^2-1}-\arccos(1/t)=\pi\lim(n/\nu)\). Uniform second-order formulas determine both the minimum and its unique nodal location, including explicit rational corrections.
