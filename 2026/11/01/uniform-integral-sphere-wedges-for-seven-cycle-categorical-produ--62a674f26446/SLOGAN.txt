For every \(n\ge2\), the ordinary independence complex of the categorical product \(C_7\times K_n\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{2}}S^3\vee\bigvee^{\binom n2}S^4\). A color-first Morse matching reduces every number of colors to the same two fixed, explicitly checked seven-row sectors; this settles the torsion-free question for the complete seven-cycle subfamily, not for all cycle lengths.
