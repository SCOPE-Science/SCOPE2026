For globally coupled Chialvo maps with heterogeneous positive inputs, every bounded complete orbit has the same leading strong-coupling spatial voltage imprint \(\mathbf x_t-\overline x_t\mathbf1=(1-\varepsilon)(\mathbf k-\overline k\mathbf1)+O((1-\varepsilon)^2\|\mathbf k-\overline k\mathbf1\|)\), uniformly in time and network size. The recovery-variable imprint is \(-b/(1-a)\) times that voltage profile at leading order, independent of whether the common dynamics are chaotic.
