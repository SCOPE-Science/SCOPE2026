# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The uniform proof reduces the forbidden-path condition to a sharp maximum-part occupancy inequality. Every face above the full lower skeleton is removed by a uniquely defined pivot pairing; no nontrivial alternating gradient path exists. The remaining CW model is low-dimensional and highly connected. A signed Euler sum determines the exact wedge multiplicity. Packaged exact computations corroborate the occupancy lemma, pairing acyclicity, mod-two homology and count for finite cases, but the proof, not enumeration, establishes the universal quantifiers.

**Originality: PASS, with access risk.** The nearest dated primary source, Deshpande–Goyal–Sawant, arXiv:2607.05358v1, announces an explicit multipartite structural decomposition and shellability criteria but a complete homotopy type only for bipartite graphs. This result covers all multipartite graphs with at least three parts at every threshold at least five, including nonshellable instances, and gives an exact sphere count. Existing four-path-free calculations concern only the excluded threshold four. Semantic searches of available mathematical records did not produce a covering theorem. The complete 19-page primary text was inaccessible: an unseen corollary or appendix could change the originality assessment, and absence from a search index is not proof of novelty.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** A natural family with a recent general structural classification now has a uniform integral homotopy theorem and an explicit sphere-count formula. The result addresses a structural gap across infinitely many graph sizes and path thresholds rather than a numerically isolated case.

**Scope and limitations.** Complete multipartite graphs with at least three nonempty parts; simple paths with distinct vertices; thresholds at least five. Other graphs, equivariant homology, and independent validation are outside scope. Primary full-text comparison remains incomplete.
