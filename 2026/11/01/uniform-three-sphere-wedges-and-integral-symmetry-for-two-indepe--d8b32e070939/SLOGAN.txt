For every \(n\ge4\), the two-independence complex of the Kneser graph on two-subsets of \([n]\) has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{4}}S^3\). Its third integral homology, under natural ground-set permutations, is precisely the fourth exterior power of the integral augmentation lattice; the wedge equivalence itself is not asserted equivariant.
