A cubic global center with positive restoring and velocity-feedback coefficients has a strictly decreasing period, and its original-time high-amplitude period admits a two-term expansion uniform across the crossover \(\gamma A^2\asymp1\). The first relative correction contains the exact factor \((2\log2-1)\gamma A^2/[2\beta A^2(1+\gamma A^2)]\), distinguishing the restoring regime from the already studied zero-restoration axis.
