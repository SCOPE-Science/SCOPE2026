The normalized \(\theta\)-convolution-body volumes of every ellipsoid and every simplex cross exactly once: an explicit incomplete-beta equation determines the crossover in each dimension. It occurs beyond the previously known spherical-ball exclusion range and has the sharp high-dimensional scale \(\theta_d\sim[2^{9/10}/(\pi^{7/30}3^{3/5})](3/5)^d d^{-7/30}\).
