For normalized square systems whose inverse row-Gram matrix is forest-sparse, the unique globally optimal fixed-iid randomized Kaczmarz probabilities are proportional to the precision matrix's absolute row sums. On every Gaussian Markov tree with common edge-correlation magnitude \(r\), the sharp optimum is \((1-r)/(N+(N-2)r)\), independent of tree topology, although the optimal node probabilities depend on degree.
