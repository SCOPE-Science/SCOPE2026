Among four-dimensional ellipsoids of fixed volume with two equal long and two equal short semiaxes, a **unique nonspherical ellipsoid** maximizes the expected diameter of a Haar-random central planar section. The exact mean-radius integral is strictly log-concave in logarithmic anisotropy, and the optimal long-to-short semiaxis ratio is approximately \(4.843418606\), rather than \(1\).
