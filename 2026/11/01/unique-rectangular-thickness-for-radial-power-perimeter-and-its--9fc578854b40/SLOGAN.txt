For radial power-weighted perimeter on centered rectangles with one fixed half-width, every negative exponent has exactly one optimal transverse thickness; the optimizer crosses the square precisely at exponent \(-1\). As the exponent approaches zero from below, its normalized optimal thickness undergoes the exact essential singularity \(\lim_{q\downarrow0}(b_*/a)^q/q=\pi/2\), while for large negative exponent the thickness grows linearly with exponent magnitude.
