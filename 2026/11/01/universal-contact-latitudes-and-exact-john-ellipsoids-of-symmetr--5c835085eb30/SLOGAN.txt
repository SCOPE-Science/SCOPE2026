The unique maximum-volume ellipsoid in the intersection of two congruent Euclidean balls has explicit semiaxes in every dimension. After affine normalization, its entire contact set lies on the two universal latitudes \(\lvert u_1\rvert=1/\sqrt d\), independent of the balls’ separation, while the axial semiaxis shrinks on the order of \(d^{-1/2}\) at fixed separation.
