In an underdamped Brownian particle whose velocity is independently Gaussian-resampled during position resets, the \(|x|^{-1/3}\) singularity at zero velocity variance crosses over to a logarithmic singularity at every positive variance. The small-variance transition has a universal, explicitly integrated profile on the spatial scale \(\varepsilon^3/(2D_v/3)\), with exact constants at both ends.
