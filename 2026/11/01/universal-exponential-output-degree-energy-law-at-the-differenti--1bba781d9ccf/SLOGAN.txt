For the cross-weight differential Hilbert operator on weighted Bergman spaces, the near-critical squared Hilbert--Schmidt energy spreads over exponentially large output degrees: after the rescaling \(s=\delta\log(n+1)\), its full distribution converges to \(1-e^{-s}\) with uniform \(O_\alpha(\delta)\) error. The exact critical logarithmic coefficient is \(\Gamma(\alpha+4)\Gamma(-\alpha)\), for every fixed \(-1<\alpha<0\).
