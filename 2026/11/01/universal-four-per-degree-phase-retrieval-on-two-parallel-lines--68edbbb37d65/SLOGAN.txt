For every \(d\ge1\), any \(2d+1\) distinct points on one line and any \(2d-1\) distinct points on a different parallel line recover every complex polynomial of degree at most \(d\) from its \(4d\) moduli, up to global phase. The two full intensity traces are obtained by exact linear interpolation because their highest two coefficients agree; \(4d\) is sharp for this ambient trace-interpolation task, not asserted minimal for nonlinear phase retrieval.
