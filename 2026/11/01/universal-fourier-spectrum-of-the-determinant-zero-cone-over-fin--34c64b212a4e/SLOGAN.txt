The determinant-zero cone in two-by-two matrices over **every** finite commutative chain ring has a completely integral, ring-universal additive Fourier spectrum, depending only on the residue-field size \(q\) and length \(k\). Its convolution rank is \(q^{4(k-\lfloor k/2\rfloor)}+(q-1)(q+1)^2\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor-1}q^{3k-2-2j}\), and the proportion of surviving Fourier modes decays exponentially in \(k\), although the field-level operator is invertible.
