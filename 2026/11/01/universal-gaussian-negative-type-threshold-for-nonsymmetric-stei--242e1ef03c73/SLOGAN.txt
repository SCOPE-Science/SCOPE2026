Every nonsymmetric Steiner \(2\)-design with block size \(k\) has the same sharp Gaussian distance-power threshold \(p_* = \log_2(k/(k-1))\), regardless of its number of points or isomorphism type. The endpoint squared-Euclidean embedding dimension is exactly \(2v-1\) for \(k\ge3\), but \(v\) for pair-block designs \(k=2\).
