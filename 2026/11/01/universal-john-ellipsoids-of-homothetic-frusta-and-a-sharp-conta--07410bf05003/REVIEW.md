# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof includes explicit affine normalization, full quantified assumptions, an analytic containment inequality, nonnegative John weights and their four exact moment identities, exact geometric contact conditions, and a direct volume integration with a strictly monotone closed form. The decisive step is a finite John decomposition obtained by lifting *arbitrary* contact data of the base; hence the argument is not restricted to rotationally symmetric frusta. Rational tests in dimensions three through ten and representative nonspherical polygonal bases, plus independent numerical constrained optimization, agree with the formulas. Numerical tests alone would not establish the theorem; the displayed identities and John's theorem do.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)

The closest general theorem is Ivanov–Naszódi (arXiv:2212.11781), which provides functional John/Löwner optimality principles, not the explicit sharp transition for homothetic frusta. Mordhorst (arXiv:1509.06023, §6.2) obtains a John-ellipsoid coincidence for a particular simplex frustum, not arbitrary bases and every truncation parameter. A previously indexed homothetic-frustum result studies Santaló points and volume products, rather than an explicit John ellipsoid; only its indexed description was available, so no whole-document noncoverage claim is made. Search results did not identify a stronger previously proved theorem that implies the combined universal transition and strict volume-fraction ordering. Older cone, prism, simplex, and trapezoid results remain an unresolved historical-priority risk; no absolute novelty certificate is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem classifies an infinite-dimensional family: every convex base, irrespective of symmetry, smoothness or polyhedral structure. It identifies a sharp and geometrically visible change of contact geometry, proves unrestricted optimality rather than optimality among symmetric ellipsoids, and supplies an exact affine-invariant volume-efficiency factorization with strict ordering across all truncations. These are structural geometric properties, not small finite benchmarks or routine parameter substitutions.

## Scope and residual risks

Only homothetic frusta are covered. No robustness to arbitrary section deformations is claimed. Literature priority is provisional, and independent validation has not been performed. The source concerning same-object Santaló invariants was accessible only through its indexed summary; it has not been judged by a full-text negative search.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
