As a planar parallelogram with unit long side and short side \(\ell\) collapses at any fixed nonzero angle, the boundary-minus-interior mean pairwise distance is universally \(\ell/3\) to first order. Its next term is \((\sin^2\vartheta/3)\ell^2\log(1/\ell)\), with an explicit angle-dependent finite correction, quantifying how the sharp planar distance inequality saturates and revealing the edge angle in the logarithmic boundary layer.
