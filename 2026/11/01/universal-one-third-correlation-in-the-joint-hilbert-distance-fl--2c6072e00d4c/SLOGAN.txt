For any fixed number of Euclidean-volume-uniform points in a simplex of growing dimension, all Hilbert distances minus \(2\log N\) converge jointly to explicit sums of independent-denominator Galambos extreme-value blocks. Each limiting edge distance has variance \(\pi^2/3\); two edges sharing a vertex have universal correlation exactly \(1/3\), while disjoint-edge distances are independent.
