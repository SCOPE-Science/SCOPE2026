# Scientific review: universal period-area spectrum in an invariant translated-cone-exchange trapezium

## Correctness

**PASS.** The new theorem is a statement about the specific identity-permutation three-cone map, not arbitrary piecewise isometries. The proof independently converts the published horizontal exchange to a bijective three-interval map with exactly stated half-open branches; its invariant base \([0,1)\) is a rotation by \(\lambda\), with a single delayed step over a subinterval of length \(\delta\). For rational \(\lambda=p/q\), every orbit of the base has least period \(q\), and the delay count on one base orbit is either \(\lfloor q\delta\rfloor\) or its ceiling. Weighting these base sets by tower height gives the precise within-slice period fractions. Integrating the resulting triangular weights over height yields the final affine area spectrum. For irrational \(\lambda\), unique ergodicity and minimality follow from the exact finite-height tower over an irrational circle rotation. The area fractions sum to one for every coprime \(1\le p<q\). The standalone exact-arithmetic checker passed 8,091 rational orbit and least-period tests plus 383 area-integration identities; these are supplementary, not infinite proofs. Endpoint assignments and the unbounded strip are explicitly excluded from the planar area law.

Searches of published mathematical records and relevant primary texts did not identify an equivalent formula or stronger result that implies it verbatim. ## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The cited source highlights the bounded trapezium interpolating between two circle rotations but does not quantify its orbit-period geometry. The complete positive-area census solves this concrete natural question for every rational translation: there are exactly \(p+1\) least periods, their relative area weights have a universal affine profile, and the cone angles affect only overall geometric scale. This distinction between a vertical period staircase and its simple integrated area distribution is an informative structural invariant of a nontrivial planar piecewise isometry, not an arbitrary finite slice or a mere recounting of known tables. The irrational case completes the dynamical dichotomy and explains why the measure-weighted rational spectrum has no irrational counterpart. No theorem about general rotating cones, transients, or all invariant measures of the unbounded global system is claimed.

## Closest literature and limits

The primary geometric/IET construction is Cockram, Ashwin and Rodrigues (preprint first posted 2023-12-29, later published 2025). Their related 2024 renormalization paper treats a different return-map question; Boshernitzan's 2012 interval-exchange work supplies general context, not the computed period-area spectrum. The principal remaining risk is that these exact fractions may be recognized as a routine but unstated consequence of published interval-exchange machinery; their publication priority merits independent comparison. Boundary conventions and a restriction to the finite trapezium are necessary. Exact-rational tests cannot replace the all-parameter analytic proof.
