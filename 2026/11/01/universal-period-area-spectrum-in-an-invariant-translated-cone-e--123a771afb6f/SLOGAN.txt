For a natural invariant trapezium in a three-cone piecewise translation, a rational parameter \(\lambda=p/q\) produces exactly the \(p+1\) positive-area least periods \(q,q+1,\ldots,q+p\), with a universal angle-independent area weight proportional to the period and half-weights at both endpoints. Irrational parameters instead give minimal, uniquely ergodic horizontal dynamics.
