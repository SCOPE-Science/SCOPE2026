For every double cone over an arbitrary centrally symmetric \(m\)-dimensional equator, the maximal point-reflection overlap depends only on the ratio \(t\) of the two apex heights: it is exactly \(2((1+t)/(t+2\cdot2^{1/m}-1))^m\). The maximizing center is explicitly located, the ratio reconstructs \(t\) uniquely, and the one-sided-cone value approaches \(1/2\) as the dimension grows.
