Independent random segment lengths leave the isotropic zonogon's limiting generator-count-normalized isoperimetric deficit in the same explicit non-Gaussian spectral family, rescaled only by the radial second moment; its exact finite-sample variance and independence from Gaussian perimeter fluctuations are determined. A length standard deviation of order \(n^{-1/2}\) gives the sharp crossover in centered area from this non-Gaussian law to an independent Gaussian convolution.
