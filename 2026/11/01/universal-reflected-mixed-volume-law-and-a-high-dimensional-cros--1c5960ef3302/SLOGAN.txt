Every centered homothetic frustum with a centrally symmetric base has the same normalized reflected mixed-volume sequence, \((2-a^j-a^{n-j})/(1-a^n)\), whatever the base shape. Its difference-body ratio decreases strictly from \(2^{n+1}-2\) to \(2^n\), with an exact logistic high-dimensional crossover when \(a=e^{-c/n}\).
