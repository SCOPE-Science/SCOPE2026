Every skew-Schäffer power-mean radius of real \(L_4\), from arithmetic through maximum mean, has the same sharp small-skew expansion \(1-h/(2\sqrt2)+(9/16)h^2+O(h^3)\), uniformly over the outer exponent. An exact quartic-square defect forces the common second-order curvature even though the full parameter profiles need not coincide.
