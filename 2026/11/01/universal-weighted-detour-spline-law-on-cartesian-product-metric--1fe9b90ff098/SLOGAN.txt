For every finite Cartesian product of geodesic-edge weighted metric graphs, the excess of intrinsic one-skeleton distance over ambient \(\ell_1\)-product distance has an exact positive-part quadratic survival law. Its derivative jumps recover the weighted aggregate edge-length spectrum, and in the equilateral case every positive detour has the same scaled \(\mathrm{Beta}(1,2)\) law in every product dimension.
