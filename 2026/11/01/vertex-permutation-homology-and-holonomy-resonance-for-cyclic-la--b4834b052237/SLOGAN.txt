For every cyclically layered complete forward digraph, the \(q=N-1\) first Mayer-path homology is canonically the vertex-permutation module, for every root order \(N\ge3\). Its degree-zero counterpart exhibits a sharp arithmetic holonomy resonance: it is one-dimensional exactly when the number of layers is divisible by \(2N/\gcd(N+2,2N)\), with an explicit rotation character.
