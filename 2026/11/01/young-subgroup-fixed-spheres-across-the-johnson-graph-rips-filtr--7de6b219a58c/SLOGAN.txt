For every \(k\ge1\) and every integer Rips scale \(0\le r<k\), the geometric fixed-point space of \(\operatorname{VR}_{\le r}(J(2k,k))\) under the two-block Young subgroup \(\mathfrak S_k\times\mathfrak S_k\) is exactly one sphere up to homotopy, of dimension \(\lfloor r/(2(k-r))\rfloor\). An explicit orbit-barycenter model collapses to the boundary of a cross-polytope; at scale \(r\ge k\), the fixed space is contractible.
