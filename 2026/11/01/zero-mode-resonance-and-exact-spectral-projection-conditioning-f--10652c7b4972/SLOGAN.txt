For finite-Fourier triangular weighted rotations on \(L^2(\mathbb T;\mathbb C^2)\), the exact Riesz-projection norm is \(\sqrt{1+\|\sum_n c_n e^{in\theta}/(b-ae^{in\alpha})\|_\infty^2}\). As the two spectral circles merge, only the mean coupling \(c_0\) causes reciprocal-gap projection blowup and linear growth of normalized powers; all other Fourier modes can be removed by a bounded similarity.
