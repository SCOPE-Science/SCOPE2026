In characteristic seven, the bielliptic genus-four trigonal family \(y^3=x^6+ax^4+bx^2+1\) is ordinary off the hyperbola \(ab+1=0\), and every smooth fiber on that hyperbola has \(a\)-number two. Exactly 24 smooth coefficient-marked fibers, forming eight degree-three Frobenius orbits, have \(7\)-rank one; all other smooth fibers on the hyperbola have \(7\)-rank two.
