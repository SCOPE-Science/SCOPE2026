The sharp completely-unclustered Burrows–Wheeler run bound \(U_k(n)=\lceil n/2\rceil\) holds for every \(31\le n\le64\) and every \(k\ge3\). Each of the 34 new length certificates uses a ternary last column differing from a binary alternating word in at most six places; the published arithmetic families already cover six of these lengths.
