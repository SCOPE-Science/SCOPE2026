Subdividing every edge of \(K_{m,n}\) once preserves its sharp Hilbert-snowflake exponent \(\log_2(2/(2-1/m-1/n))\) exactly when \(m=n\), but strictly lowers it whenever the part sizes differ. The unequal-case threshold is the unique root of an explicit two-dimensional quadratic-form determinant, has a one-dimensional endpoint equality space, and decays like \(1/n\) with one part size fixed.
