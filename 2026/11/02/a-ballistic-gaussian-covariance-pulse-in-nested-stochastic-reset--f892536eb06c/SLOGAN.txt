In a Brownian chain whose particles independently reset to their immediate predecessor, instantaneous source-to-depth covariance decays exponentially, but the largest positive-lag covariance decays only as the inverse square root of depth. Its unique optimal lag grows linearly with depth, and the moving covariance profile becomes a Gaussian pulse of width proportional to the square root of depth; the normalized peak Pearson correlation is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/\pi}/n\).
