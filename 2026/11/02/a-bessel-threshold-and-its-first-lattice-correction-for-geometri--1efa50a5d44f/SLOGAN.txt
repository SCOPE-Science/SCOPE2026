For a geometrically regular unilateral shift with weights \(\sqrt{1+\lambda h^2e^{-jh}}\), the numerical-radius threshold has the sharp expansion \(\lambda_h=(j_{0,1}^2/4)e^{-3h/2}+O(h^2)\). Above the Bessel threshold, excess numerical radius is of order \(h^2\), and its extremizer approaches an explicit Bessel-function bound state.
