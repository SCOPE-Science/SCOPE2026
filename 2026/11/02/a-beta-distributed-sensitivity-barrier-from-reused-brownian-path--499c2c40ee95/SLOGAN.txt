In a Gaussian constant-drift SDE, reusing only \(\ell<d\) Brownian paths across arbitrarily many parameter samples traps the estimated derivative-informed eigendirections in a random \(\ell\)-plane, with squared alignment bounded by a \(\operatorname{Beta}(\ell/2,(d-\ell)/2)\) random variable. The best possible one-coordinate predictor from that plane has exact positive mean-square risk \(1/12-\arcsin(P_\ell/2)/(2\pi)\); with one centered path the entire estimated matrix is independent of the outer samples.
