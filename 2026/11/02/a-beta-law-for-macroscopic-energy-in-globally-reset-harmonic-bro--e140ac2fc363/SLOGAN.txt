A harmonically interacting Brownian gas subjected to simultaneous Poisson resetting retains beta-distributed macroscopic mean-square energy fluctuations even with infinitely many independently heated particles. Its stationary energy has an exact exponential autocorrelation and a Gaussian time-average limit, while for independent particles the squared relative-energy-noise excess of a global over a local reset clock is exactly \(\frac{a}{a+2}(1-1/N)\), with \(a=r/(2\kappa)\).
