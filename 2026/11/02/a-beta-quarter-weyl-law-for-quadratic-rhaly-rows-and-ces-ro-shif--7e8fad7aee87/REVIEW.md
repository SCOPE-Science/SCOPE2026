# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The hypotheses fix a positive finite quadratic row-modulus limit, and a trace-norm convergent rank-one decomposition establishes existence and compactness. The full proof obtains a singular-value counting law by exact finite triangular-block spectra, a rank bound on all off-diagonal block interactions, a quantitative low-index rank bound, dyadic upper counting, and an explicitly norm-small infinite tail. The beta integral and monotone inversion then yield the universal coefficient and exact threshold. The formula for the Cesàro shift difference and its trace is exact. Numerical checks independently corroborate finite identities but are not the infinite proof.
