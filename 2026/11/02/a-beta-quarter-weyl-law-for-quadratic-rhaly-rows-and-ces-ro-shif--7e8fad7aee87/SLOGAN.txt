Quadratically decaying Rhaly rows have a universal sharp singular-value coefficient: if \((n+1)^2|a_n|\to c>0\), then \(j^2s_j(R_a)\to c\,[\mathrm B(1/4,1/2)/(\pi\sqrt2)]^2\). The first and every higher Cesàro shift-conjugation defect therefore has the exact critical Schatten exponent \(1/2\), with coefficient proportional to the shift order.
