The binary one-deletion confusability graph restricted to odd-length palindromic codewords is exactly a rooted binomial tree; even-length binary palindromes never conflict under one deletion. At lengths \(2m-1\) and \(2m\), the sharp maximum code sizes are \(2^{m-1}\) and \(2^m\), respectively, and the number of optimal odd-length codebooks is \((m+1)\prod_{j=1}^{m-1}j^{2^{m-1-j}}\).
