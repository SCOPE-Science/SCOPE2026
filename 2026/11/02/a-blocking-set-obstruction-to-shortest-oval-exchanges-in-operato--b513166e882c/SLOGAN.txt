For the \(234\) ovals (four-arcs) of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,3)\), the one-point-exchange graph is connected and \(12\)-regular with diameter \(4\), and its exact distance is \(d(A,B)=4-|A\cap B|+\mathbf1_{\{A\cup B\text{ is nontrivially blocking}\}}\). The strict detour occurs in exactly \(1638\) unordered pairs and has six two-point-overlap and eight one-point-overlap exceptional partners from every oval; earlier static counts of blocking conic unions are not claimed as new.
