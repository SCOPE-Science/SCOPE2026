For any fixed number \(r\ge2\) of shifts from a divisibility chain with unbounded reciprocal-divisor sums, every nondegenerate positive-quadrant-coercive binary quadratic form in two prime variables has a zero-density additive representation set whenever \(N_a(X)^r=O((\log X)^2)\). This includes genuine \(r\ge3\) borderline families with \(N_a(X)\asymp(\log X)^{2/r}\), going beyond the known two-square, two-shift case.
