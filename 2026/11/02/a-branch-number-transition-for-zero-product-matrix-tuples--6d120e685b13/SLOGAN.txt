For ordered \(m\)-tuples of pairwise zero-product matrices, invertibility of their sum makes every weak rank composition of \(n\) occur **exactly** \(|\mathrm{GL}_n(q)|\) times; the same uniformity holds motivically. With two branches, invertible sums become generic as the field grows, but with four or more branches they become asymptotically negligible, while the three-branch case already has a competing square-zero family in even dimension.
