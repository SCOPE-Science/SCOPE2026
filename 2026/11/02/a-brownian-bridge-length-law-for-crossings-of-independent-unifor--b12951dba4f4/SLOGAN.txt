For independent uniformly sampled planar chords, the empirical distribution of minor-arc lengths asymptotically determines all crossing-count fluctuations on the \(n^{3/2}\) scale through an explicit Brownian-bridge integral. A length-based additive projection explains exactly \((n-1)/(n+3)\) of finite-size crossing variance, and the short-arc count most correlated with crossings uses the unique threshold \((7-\sqrt{17})/8\), irrespective of sample size.
