The projective magic-square-of-squares surface gains exactly 128 additional ordinary double points when reduced modulo three: it has 384 nodes there, versus 256 in characteristic zero and every prime characteristic at least five. In characteristic three, the nine branch lines become the affine-plane configuration, yielding a faithful node-transitive subgroup of automorphisms of order 110592, compared with characteristic-zero automorphism order 2048.
