The reduced first-jet scheme of ordered commuting square-zero \(2\times2\) matrices has exactly \(2^m\) irreducible components in odd characteristic but only two in characteristic two, for every \(m\ge1\). Exact geometric dimensions, motivic classes, and finite-field point-count formulas follow from the support-stratified infinitesimal lifting equations; the first jet is taken before reducing the square-zero equation scheme.
