# Review of the classification of self-annihilating principal ideals

## Correctness
PASS. Characteristic two: squarezero of all maximal-ideal elements; invertible linear substitutions preserving the square-zero presentation; a unique normal form \(L+g\) after unit multiplication; equality of ideals forces the same \(g\); the coordinate-annihilator identity transfers under an automorphism. Odd characteristic: independent squarefree quadratic monomials force a single supported linear coordinate, and direct-sum comparison of \(f^2\) eliminates any complementary nonlinear term. The projective point count and affine-fiber dimension are explicit. Exhaustive independent enumerations cover eight cases, including \(\mathbb F_4\).

## Originality
PASS, with stated residual risk. The earliest exact-zero-divisor paper establishes general structural definitions; the 2013 homogeneous-exact-pair work explicitly supplies coordinate examples in exactly such a ring, but not the finite-field census or characteristic-two family of distinct nonhomogeneous ideals. The 2024 general-linear-form study is characteristic zero and gives existence criteria rather than the pointwise count. No equivalent or stronger formula was located in the checked literature and mathematical-record searches, but unindexed/implicit priority cannot be excluded. Previous accessible local records were compared for the same claim and equivalent parameterizations.

## Value
PASS. The object is a standard Artinian complete intersection and its exact-zero-divisor ideals are a natural homological invariant. The theorem turns a known existence fact into an all-dimension geometric classification and arithmetic enumeration, with a sharp jump from a discrete \(d\)-point locus to a positive-dimensional fibration of dimension \(2^{d-1}-1\) in characteristic two. The strict initial-degree restriction is essential and explicit.

## Limitations
No orbit quotient, arbitrary exact pairs, higher-order initial forms, or nonreduced moduli-scheme classification. Independent publication-priority checking is not exhaustive.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
