In \(k[x_1,\ldots,x_d]/(x_1^2,\ldots,x_d^2)\), all self-annihilating principal ideals with nonzero linear initial form reduce to exactly \(d\) coordinate ideals in odd characteristic, but in characteristic two their parameters form an affine-space fibration over \(\mathbb P^{d-1}\) of relative dimension \(2^{d-1}-d\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) their exact number is \(d\) for odd \(q\), and \(q^{2^{d-1}-d}(q^d-1)/(q-1)\) for even \(q\).
