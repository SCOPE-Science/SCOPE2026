For any finite \(n\)-valuation universe, the inclusion lattice of all convex union-closed team properties has exactly \(n+1\) coatoms and a proper-part order complex homotopy equivalent to \(S^{n-1}\). In particular, the \(k\)-variable propositional team-semantic quotient has a \((2^k-1)\)-sphere as its order-complex homotopy type, and its Möbius number is \(-1\) for \(k\geq1\).
