In random serial dictatorship, if at least two students share one guaranteed favorite school and everyone else ranks independently uniformly, the complete distribution of the number of unenvied students equals the singleton-coupon distribution for only \(n-1\) schools, regardless of how many students share that favorite. With zero or one such student, the corresponding coupon dimension remains \(n\), giving a sharp distributional change and an exact mean drop of \(1/n\).
