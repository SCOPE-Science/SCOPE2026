For every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph, two homogeneous nonnegative Markov rounds have a unique optimal unordered gain pair, with a sharp switching threshold at the common-neighbor relation \(\lambda<\mu\). Below that threshold, the optimal two-round kernel collapses exactly to \(R_*I+(1-R_*)J/N\), and becomes perfect two-round averaging when \(\mu=d\); above it, repeating the loopless random walk is optimal.
