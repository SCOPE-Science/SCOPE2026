For independent Hill-law inhibitory mechanisms sharing a fixed dose, the global optimum never assigns unequal positive doses: it activates exactly \(k\) mechanisms at equal doses. For cooperative exponents \(h>1\), the optimal active count passes through every \(k=1,\ldots,n\) in order as the budget grows, separated by unique sharp thresholds; for \(0<h\le1\), all mechanisms always receive equal dose.
