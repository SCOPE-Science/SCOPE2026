The complete distinct circular-palindrome spectrum of the dyadic Thue–Morse word \(T_k\) obeys a two-step palindrome–antipalindrome recurrence. Its total oscillates exactly between \((7\cdot2^k+8)/6\) at even ranks and \((4\cdot2^k+4)/3\) at odd ranks, revealing different limiting circular-palindrome densities along the two parity subsequences.
