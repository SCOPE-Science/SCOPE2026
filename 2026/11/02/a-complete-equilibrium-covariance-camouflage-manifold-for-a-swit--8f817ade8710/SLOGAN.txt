For every \(\rho,D>0\), an entire two-parameter family of genuinely switching harmonic traps has precisely the equilibrium Ornstein--Uhlenbeck covariance \(C(t)=D\rho^{-1}e^{-\rho|t|}\). The family is exhaustive among two distinct positive stiffnesses and positive switching rates, yet each member is detectably non-Gaussian, with an explicit strictly positive stationary fourth cumulant.
