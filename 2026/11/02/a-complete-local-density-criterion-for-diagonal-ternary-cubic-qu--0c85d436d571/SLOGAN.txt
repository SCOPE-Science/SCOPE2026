For any diagonal ternary cubic with three nonzero integer coefficients and any prime \(p\ne3\), its value-quotient set is dense in \(\mathbb Q_p\) unless \(p\equiv1\pmod3\) and all three coefficient valuations are distinct modulo three. The new mixed-valuation case uses Hasse's elliptic-curve bound to show that an anisotropic binary cubic represents every \(p\)-adic unit, making one additional differently valued cubic term enough for density.
