For every connected affine quandle on \(\mathbb F_p\) in characteristic \(p\), the nonzero idempotents of its bilinear quandle algebra form an explicitly determined affine quandle, with one coordinate for each exponent \(j\) satisfying \((1-\lambda)^j+\lambda^j=1\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) there are exactly \(1+q^{d_{p,\lambda}}\) idempotents; the previously known dihedral specialization is excluded from the novelty claim.
