For a closed three-site stochastic mass-exchange chain with beta splitting laws \(\operatorname{Beta}(t,pt)\) and \(\operatorname{Beta}(t,qt)\), the stationary means remain \((1,p,pq)/(1+p+pq)\) while the adjacent covariance changes sign as the common concentration \(t\) varies exactly when an explicit inequality \(A(p,q)>0\) holds. This gives a sharp full bond-bias phase diagram, including the exact transition \(t=13/16\) for \(p=4,q=2\).
