For every graph power \(C_n^k\) with \(k\ge3\), its shortest-path distance has negative type only at the complete and cocktail-party orders, plus precisely four sporadic pairs \((3,12),(3,14),(3,20),(4,18)\). The same list exactly characterizes all-scale positive semidefiniteness of the exponential distance kernel, and the exceptional squared-Euclidean embedding ranks are \(6,7,8,7\).
