For a harmonically trapped coordinate driven by Brownian noise reset at Poisson rate \(\rho\), its fourth-order time-reversal asymmetry is negative at every lag for \(\rho\le1\), positive at every lag for \(\rho\ge(3+\sqrt{33})/4\), and changes sign **exactly once** at intermediate reset rates. The complete phase diagram strengthens an earlier exact transform and existence-only crossing result by proving that no additional lag reversals can occur.
