# Review of the three-spoke Padé positivity theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** All denominators are positive because the denominator is a polynomial of a positive-semidefinite Laplacian with strictly positive scalar spectrum. The update has positive spectral eigenvalues and unit row sums. The six symbolic off-diagonal identities were independently checked by exact matrix algebra in the supplied verifier. The ordered quartic coefficient sign changes and cubic leading-sign dichotomy rigorously establish unique finite crossings and the global interval structure. The reciprocal-price inequalities are necessary and sufficient. Exact examples and 540 direct numerical comparisons supplement, but do not replace, the proof.

## Originality
**PASS (subject to specialist-literature uncertainty).** The inspected 2010 foundational paper provides sufficient conditions and general Padé positivity discussion, not the exact all-step four-state star classification. The 2012 manuscript demonstrates failure of Padé positivity with a nilpotent directed eight-state example rather than conducting the six-entry four-state symmetric-star calculation. The publicly available eight-state Markov record analyzes short-step failure and disconnected windows for a directed pure-birth chain; it neither subsumes the finite star classification nor gives the resistance-polytope criterion. Searches for Padé/Gauss--Legendre positivity combined with weighted stars, three-leaf stars, and Laplacian pseudoinverses did not locate a dominating theorem. This is not exhaustive priority certification; specialized earlier work remains possible.

## Value
**PASS.** Fourth-order Padé is A-stable and mass conserving on conservative graph generators, but those properties do not ensure entrywise positivity. This theorem gives a complete, sharp, readily testable characterization across all positive conductances on the first star with three independently tunable branches. It also supplies an unambiguous exact step-size horizon and a qualitative critical scaling change. The parameter space and numerical method are natural, rather than an arbitrary bounded enumeration or an isolated counterexample.

## Scope and limitations
The theorem covers precisely four vertices in a weighted three-spoke star and exact arithmetic. No assertion is made about larger stars, other graphs, internal Runge--Kutta stages, or numerical roundoff. Specialized historical priority remains the main unresolved scientific risk.
