For the fourth-order \([2/2]\) Padé method on a weighted four-state star, unconditional nonnegative transitions occur exactly when every leaf resistance \(r_i\) is at least one quarter of the sum of the three leaf resistances. Outside this sharp region, all positivity-preserving steps form one finite interval whose endpoint is a root of explicit degree-at-most-four polynomials; critical transitions decay as \(h^{-2}\), instead of the usual signed \(h^{-1}\) tail.
