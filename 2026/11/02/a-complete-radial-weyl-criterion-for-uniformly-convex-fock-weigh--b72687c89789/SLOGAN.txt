For every uniformly convex radial Fock weight in any fixed complex dimension, a single geometric slope \(\phi'(r)/r\) controls the entire scaled counting profile of the Toeplitz operator with symbol \((1+|z|^2)^{-\beta/2}\), with an explicit error term. A classical Weyl constant exists exactly when that slope converges; otherwise the complete set of scaled spectral limits is the interval determined by its liminf and limsup.
