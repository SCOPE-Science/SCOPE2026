The intrinsic distance between two independent area-uniform points on a flat Möbius strip is strictly stochastically smaller than on the equal-area, equal-total-boundary-length matched cylinder, with an exact one-dimensional CDF difference and sharp \(3/2\)-power and cubic endpoint laws. Under a specified coordinate coupling, the probability of a strict shortcut has an explicit two-branch aspect-ratio law that is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\) at the square-scale transition.
