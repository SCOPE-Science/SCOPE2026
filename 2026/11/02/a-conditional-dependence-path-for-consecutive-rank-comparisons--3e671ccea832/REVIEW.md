# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact conditional covariance is reduced to covariance of a decreasing and an increasing endpoint response, after observing that the unconditioned comparison boundaries split independent underlying blocks. A missing intermediate comparison disconnects the relevant blocks exactly. When all intermediate comparisons are fixed, every ascent/descent indicator supplies a TP2 order kernel, composition retains TP2, and an explicit strictly interlaced positive-density witness forces strict endpoint TP2 and thus strictly negative covariance. The adjacent case is handled separately by a one-variable symmetrization. Every binary conditioning event is feasible under a shared positive-density interval. The standalone exact-enumeration script checks every conditioning pattern on all permutations of lengths three through ten (105,796 sign tests) and a six-assertion non-stability certificate; finite computations are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Borodin–Diaconis–Fulman (arXiv:0904.3740, Proposition 5.2 and Theorem 7.1) prove ordinary one-dependence and a determinantal representation, neither of which asserts this all-conditioning sign classification. Borcea–Brändén–Liggett (arXiv:0707.2340, Theorems 4.1 and 4.9) give a stronger general negative-dependence framework under strong Rayleigh stability, which the ordinary descent-set law demonstrably lacks at length five. Kahn–Neiman (arXiv:1001.0610) concerns competing urns and graph-orientation bounds; Crane–DeSalvo–Elizalde (arXiv:1609.01370) develops Mallows consecutive-pattern avoidance and weak dissociation. A potentially implicit older all-conditioning statement remains a historical-priority risk, and the result has not had an independent scholarly novelty audit.

## Value — PASS

The theorem gives an exact, parameter-free and sharp conditional-independence/negative-dependence graph for a foundational rank-statistic process under every possible observed ascent/descent pattern. Conditioning can turn initially independent nonadjacent descents into strictly negatively correlated ones, and the positions at which this occurs are classified exactly. Applicability to independent nonidentical continuous observations prevents the conclusion being an artifact of uniform permutation symmetry. The result is structural, not merely a marginal count or textbook covariance calculation.

## Limitations

No extension to tied distributions, generally dependent observations, arbitrary monotone functionals or other permutation models is asserted. Research comparisons cannot rule out undiscovered equivalents in older total-positivity literature. Independent audit has not been performed.
