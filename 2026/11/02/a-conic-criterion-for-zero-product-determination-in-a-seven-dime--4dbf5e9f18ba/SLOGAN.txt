For a natural seven-dimensional quaternionic-type class-two Lie algebra, zero-product determination is controlled exactly by the rational points of the conic \(a^2+b^2+c^2=0\): the defect is \(3\) in odd-characteristic anisotropic fields, \(0\) in odd-characteristic isotropic fields, and \(1\) in characteristic two. Nevertheless, every finite field of size \(q\) gives the same commuting-pair count \(2q^{11}+q^{10}-2q^9\), while the associated commuting-plane scheme degenerates from a smooth conic to a double line in characteristic two.
