# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The global map and labelled sample space are specified over \(\mathbb F_2\). The proof derives the original grid's exact \(J_L^{\oplus(L-2)}\oplus J_1\) decomposition by an explicit truncated-polynomial-module quotient, establishes the swap-involution cancellation of all positive corner-return moments, and reconstructs ranks of every modified update power via a rank-one resolvent identity and a direct-summand/orthogonal-complement argument. This gives the entire Jordan partition, the terminal period-one eigenspace and the exact entry-time shells without extrapolating from finite runs. Standalone integer-bit-matrix checks matched the derived rank laws on four grid sizes and exhaustive trajectories on the smallest grid. Potential error locations checked include characteristic-two signs, the zero multiplicity at \(L=4\), the precise endpoint \(2L-1\), and the rank-one projector's normalization.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Barua–Ramakrishnan (1996) covers the unperturbed path-polynomial and grid-kernel setting, including dyadic lengths, but its original two-dimensional rules use homogeneous nearest-neighbor or homogeneous all-site self terms; the new single-corner feedback operator is neither form. Choudhury et al. (2008) treat broad two-dimensional linear rules and image patterns, without the displayed all-parameter corner-defect Jordan partition or first-entry law. Chang–Akın (2016) gives Kronecker-sum methods and Jordan algorithms for uniform multidimensional rules; its theorems do not state the inhomogeneous rank-one modification or the resulting \(2L-1\) nilpotent block. General Jordan partitions for an unperturbed Kronecker sum likewise do not determine its rank-one perturbation. Accessible records and semantic research-result searches found no implication-dominating equivalent. The complete text of Dihidar–Choudhury (2004) was inaccessible; its abstract refers to exceptional two-dimensional transformations, so historical overlap remains possible and the negative comparison is not made on whole-document grounds. Full universal novelty cannot be certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is an exact dynamical classification on an infinite, geometrically canonical family of finite grids. The local perturbation produces a structural transition from universal extinction to two absorbing fixed configurations and almost doubles the sharp maximal transient; every layer population and its terminal one-half probability follow. This is a motivated boundary-defect response, not a relabeling of the unperturbed Jordan problem, and distinguishes homogeneous tensor-sum methods from site-local feedback. No general theorem beyond the specified grid family is claimed.
