On every binary square grid with side \(2^a-1\) (\(a\ge2\)), adding self-feedback only at one corner turns an everywhere-extinguishing four-neighbor parity rule into dynamics with exactly two fixed states, changes the sharp transient length from \(2^a\) to \(2^{a+1}-1\), and gives an exact closed census of every entry-time layer. Exactly half of all initial states attain the new maximal delay.
