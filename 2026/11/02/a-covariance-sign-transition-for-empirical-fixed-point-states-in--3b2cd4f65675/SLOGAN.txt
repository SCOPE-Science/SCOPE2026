In the Ewens Chinese restaurant process, the long-run empirical fractions of stages with zero and exactly \(d\) fixed points have limiting covariance that changes sign exactly once as the Ewens parameter increases: an explicit one-dimensional integral determines the threshold. These thresholds are strictly increasing and satisfy \(\theta_d=d+(1+o(1))\sqrt{d\log d}\), despite the two fixed-point states being mutually exclusive at any individual stage.
