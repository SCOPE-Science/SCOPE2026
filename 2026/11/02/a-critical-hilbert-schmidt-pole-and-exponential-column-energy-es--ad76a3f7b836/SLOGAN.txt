For the Hilbert matrix from logarithmically weighted to ordinary Bergman space, Hilbert–Schmidt membership begins exactly above \(\alpha=2\), where the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm has the sharp pole \(1/(\alpha-2)\) and an explicit convergent finite part. As the threshold is approached, the normalized squared column norms exhibit a universal mean-one exponential law on the scale \((\alpha-2)\log\log n\).
