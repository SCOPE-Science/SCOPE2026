When random labeled-coupon draws contain \(k_n\) coupons with \((k_n/n)\log n\to c\), the last unidentified labels have two asymptotically independent obstructions: unseen coupons and pairs with identical nonzero draw histories. Their Poisson intensities are \(e^{-x}\) and \(\tfrac12(e^c-1)e^{-2x}\), giving distinct explicit full-recovery limit laws according to whether the complete label alphabet is initially known.
