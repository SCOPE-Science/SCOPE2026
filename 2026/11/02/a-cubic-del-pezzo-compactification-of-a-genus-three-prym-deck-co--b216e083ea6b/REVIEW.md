# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The object is the explicit symmetric coefficient chart of the smooth hyperelliptic genus-three family, restricted to nonzero coefficient sum. The involution in Borówka–Shatsila Proposition 4.6 becomes \(u\mapsto-u^{-1}\), \(v\mapsto-v\); the full polynomial invariant ring follows from the sign involution in \(z=u+u^{-1}\) and \(v\). The exactly localized quotient, its fixed-point-free finite étale double cover, and the cubic closure follow without assumptions about coarse moduli identification. Full Jacobian calculations identify only two affine \(A_1\) points and one infinity \(A_3\) point; the latter has an exact completing-square normal form. Algebraic elimination proves the double collision divisor and its intersection length four with the other boundary conic. The verifier checked all stated critical identities symbolically, plus complete finite-field orbit tests over \(\mathbb F_7\) and \(\mathbb F_{13}\). Residual mathematical limitation: the claimed compactification concerns the coefficient quotient, not the moduli Prym morphism.

The published article's abstract and the 2023 thesis's record were checked separately, but their complete contents were not both inspected; an older or unindexed presentation could cover some or all of these calculations. General facts about rational involutions and ADE cubics do not alone determine the specific quotient or boundary.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The paper's final question asks for compactifications of the source, target and Prym map. The invariant quotient provides a concrete, normal projective degree-three del Pezzo model for one explicitly motivated coefficient-level deck operation and reveals three singularity types and the nonreduced collision boundary invisible from the birational formula. The doubled divisor and exact boundary tangency describe how two natural degenerations interact; this is more than a coordinate renaming or an arbitrary finite experiment. This **does not** solve the entire modular compactification problem, and no uniqueness claim is made.

The journal publisher pages supplied metadata and an abstract, not a second full-text examination.
