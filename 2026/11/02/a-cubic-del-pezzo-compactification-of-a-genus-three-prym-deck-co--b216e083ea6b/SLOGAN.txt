The explicit Borówka–Shatsila Prym-deck involution on the unordered genus-three coefficient chart has a smooth affine quotient whose projective closure is the normal cubic del Pezzo surface \(W^2Z=T(Q^2+4Z^2)\), with ADE basket \(A_3+2A_1\). Its excluded collision divisor is a doubled rational curve tangent to the other affine boundary conic; the result compactifies the coefficient-level quotient, not the full moduli Prym map.
