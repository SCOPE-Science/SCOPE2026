For sharp weighted harmonic Bergman traces on a circle deformed into a centered ellipse, the first-order cusp vanishes at the unique radius \(r^2=3/(2\alpha+5)\), with an exact quadratic response that changes sign at \(\alpha=2\). At \(\alpha=2\) and \(r=1/\sqrt3\), the first nonzero variation is the sharp cubic cusp \(\mathcal K(\varepsilon)/\mathcal K(0)=1+(9/16)|\varepsilon|^3+O(\varepsilon^4)\).
