On the triangular prism, the uncentered graph-ball maximal operator has exact sharp real \(\ell^2\) norm \(\sqrt{\lambda_*}\), where \(\lambda_*\) is the largest root of \(64\lambda^3-124\lambda^2+33\lambda-2=0\). Its eighteen nonnegative extremizer rays split into two graph-symmetry orbits of sizes six and twelve, and its norm is strictly larger than the centered norm.
