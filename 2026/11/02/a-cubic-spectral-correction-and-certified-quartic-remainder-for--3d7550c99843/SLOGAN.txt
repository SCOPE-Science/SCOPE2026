The spherical coefficient governing the ultra-hard edge of preconditioned random matrices has an exact, sign-sensitive cubic spectral approximation and an everywhere nonnegative, explicitly bounded quartic remainder. For every diffuse inverse spectrum, the coefficient deficit is asymptotic to \(\frac{n}{4(n+2)}\operatorname{tr}(H-I_n/n)^2\), sharpening the previously stated upper-bound coefficient by a factor of four and distinguishing equally pure spectra at cubic order.
