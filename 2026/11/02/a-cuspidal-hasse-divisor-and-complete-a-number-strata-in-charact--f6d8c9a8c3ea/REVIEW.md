# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The defining polynomial has degree eleven, so each squarefree fiber has genus five. The coefficient extraction \([x^{7i-j}]f^3\) produces the displayed matrix; exact arithmetic in characteristic seven gives determinant \(-9ab^3F\) and the nonzero rank-minor \(-3b^3c^4\). For \(c=0\), independent-support entries give the complete rank classification, and the only rank-three locus is \(b=-a^2\ne0\). Eisenstein irreducibility, a Jacobian-gradient calculation and a formal square-root change of variables prove the component and transverse \(A_4\) claims. The asserted cusp points are genuinely smooth curves. Both verifier programs executed successfully; finite enumeration is corroboration, not the proof over an algebraic closure.

## Originality

PASS under the checked sources, with residual priority risk. Novoselov (arXiv:1902.05992v1, Section 2, Theorem 4, Table 1) proves a universal coefficient formula *within* the three-monomial family \(x^{2g+1}+ax^{g+1}+bx\), and covers its Cartier cycles and mod-characteristic Frobenius polynomials. Specializing that result to a genus-five curve without a cubic perturbation cannot give the new three-parameter component \(F=0\). The general hyperelliptic coefficient formula alone is not the four-term factorization, irreducibility, cuspidal transverse normal form or complete rank stratification. Frei (arXiv:1703.03516) establishes genus restrictions for extreme \(a\)-numbers, not the present locus. Searches for both exact equations and equivalent terms (Cartier, Hasse–Witt, nonordinary strata, genus five and characteristic seven) produced no known equivalent or stronger classification. This does not exclude unindexed specialized computations.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The unbroken dihedral family is a natural reference point for arithmetic curve algorithms; the single cubic perturbation changes its sparse monomial Cartier matrix and exposes a geometrically nontrivial nonordinary divisor. A transverse \(A_4\) singularity coinciding *exactly* with an enhanced \(a\)-number locus is a structural phenomenon in characteristic-seven Hasse geometry. The statement classifies all smooth marked parameters in the family, including the nonordinary divisor's components, singularities and their interaction. It is not an isolated finite-field coincidence and does not claim new methods for arbitrary hyperelliptic families.
