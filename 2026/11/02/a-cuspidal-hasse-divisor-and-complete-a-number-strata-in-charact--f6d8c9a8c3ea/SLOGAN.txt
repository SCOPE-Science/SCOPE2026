For the smooth genus-five curves \(y^2=x^{11}+a x^6+c x^3+b x\) in characteristic \(7\), the nonordinary Hasse divisor consists of a smooth component and an irreducible component with transverse \(A_4\) cusps. Its cusp locus is exactly where the Jacobian \(a\)-number jumps from at most \(1\) to \(2\), giving the complete \(a\)-number classification of the family.
