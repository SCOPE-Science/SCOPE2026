For equal-magnitude signed cycles, natural-order zero-fill incomplete Cholesky loses its final positive pivot at \(t=[2\cos(\pi/(n+2))]^{-1}\), independently of edge signs; a positive-definite breakdown interval occurs exactly when the edge-sign product differs from \((-1)^n\). The same sharp classification gives the global diagonal-shift infimum and a condition-number failure boundary growing as \(n^3/\pi^2\).
