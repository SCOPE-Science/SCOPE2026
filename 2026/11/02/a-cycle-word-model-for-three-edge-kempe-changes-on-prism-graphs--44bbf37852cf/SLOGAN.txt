The complete fixed-vertex three-edge Kempe graph of every ordinary prism is a simple graph of proper cyclic three-letter words, whose edges swap colours along one maximal interval avoiding the fixed endpoints; even prisms add exactly one exceptional leaf. This explicit all-size adjacency model reveals a sharp odd-girth transition: the graph contains a five-cycle for every prism of rim length at least five, despite the prisms of even rim length being bipartite.
