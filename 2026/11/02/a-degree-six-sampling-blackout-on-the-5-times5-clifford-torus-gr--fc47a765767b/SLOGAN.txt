On the \(5\times5\) fifth-root grid of the Clifford torus in \(S^3\), exact-degree spherical harmonic interpolation is surjective at degree five, loses exactly the constant-data direction at degree six, and is surjective at every degree from seven onward. All sampling ranks are determined exactly, using a finite \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) spectral certificate and an analytic all-degree diagonal-dominance bound.
