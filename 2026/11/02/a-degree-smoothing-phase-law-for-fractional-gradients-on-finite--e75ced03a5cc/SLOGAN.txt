For unweighted \(m\)-leaf stars, fractional incidence gradients \(BL^{-s}\) have exact \(\ell^1\), \(\ell^2\), and \(\ell^\infty\) norms and a sharp degree–smoothing transition at \(s=1/p\). At that line, for every \(1<p<\infty\) other than \(p=2\), the limiting norm is strictly larger than the classical mean-centering norm, because the hub coordinate couples nontrivially to the leaf fluctuations.
