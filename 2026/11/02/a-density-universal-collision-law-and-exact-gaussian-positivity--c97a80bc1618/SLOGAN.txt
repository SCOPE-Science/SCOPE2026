For unmodified affine-exact minimum-norm quadrature with \(n\) iid nodes, the probability of large total-variation amplification has a sharp universal \(t^{-(n-1)}\) tail, with a density-specific explicit coefficient. For Gaussian nodes, the three-point probability of *positive computed weights* is exactly \(\frac3{\pi\sqrt3}F(\pi/3\mid2/3)\), below the \(3/4\) chance that positive exact weights merely exist.
