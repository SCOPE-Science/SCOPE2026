For a nondispersive two-layer periodic wave crystal with unequal impedances, every irrational ratio of layer traversal times has the same exact high-frequency forbidden-frequency fraction, \(4[\operatorname{Li}_2(\kappa)-\operatorname{Li}_2(-\kappa)]/\pi^2\), strictly exceeding the fraction \(2\arcsin(\kappa)/\pi\) at exactly equal traversal times. High-order rational traversal-time ratios converge to the irrational fraction, creating a sharp arithmetic discontinuity in the infinite-frequency spectral occupancy.
