For Brownian motion subject to resets of strength \(s\) at rate \(\lambda/s\), the stationary rare-position law has a dilogarithmic large-deviation rate \(I\), while its Gaussian-noise diffusion approximation has the logarithmic rate \(J\). The exact strict inequality \(I(w)>J(w)\) for every nonzero \(w\), beginning only at order \(w^6/36\) near zero, exposes a global tail error hidden by their common Gaussian limit.
