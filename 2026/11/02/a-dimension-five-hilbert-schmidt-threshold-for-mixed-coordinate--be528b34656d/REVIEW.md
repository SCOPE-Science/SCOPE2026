# Mathematical review

## Correctness
PASS. Starting from the published bounded coordinate-operator formula, the proof computes the unique output coefficient for every input multi-index and two independent nonnegative shifts. Weighted monomial norms yield an exact generating-function identity for each homogeneous degree. A summable reciprocal-binomial majorant establishes the positive Hilbert–Schmidt range, and dominated convergence gives the sharp critical asymptotics and the negative range. The script checks 11,591 finite exact rational comparisons independently of the prose algebra; it is not used as a substitute for the infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS on the accessible primary-literature and mathematical-record comparison, with a priority caveat. The initiating article arXiv:2606.24710 supplies coordinate boundedness and single-operator spectral properties, not a mixed-commutator Schatten criterion. The companion arXiv:2606.25885 is about norm and Rhaly-type constructions; full text could not be inspected, leaving an implicit-equivalence risk. General Drury–Arveson coordinate-multiplier commutator results involve \([M_{z_j}^*,M_{z_k}]\), whereas this finding studies \([C_j,C_k]\); neither statement implies the other. Searches for both formulations and related critical-dimension estimates found no dominating statement. Independent publication-priority verification has not occurred.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The question of whether the new multivariable coordinate tuple is Hilbert–Schmidt almost commuting is a natural joint-regularity problem, not a scalar norm calculation. The sharp transition at dimension six and the exact logarithmic and power growth of truncated Hilbert–Schmidt energy quantify a genuine dimension obstruction. It gives a reproducible benchmark for later operator-ideal and joint spectral investigations without claiming results on other ideals.
