For a run-and-tumble particle in any diagonally anisotropic harmonic trap, squared positions in different axes are always negatively correlated through dimension four, but in every dimension at least five their covariance changes sign exactly once as the tumbling rate increases. The critical rate is bounded below by the corresponding equal-stiffness threshold, with strict delay for unequal stiffnesses, and the result is insensitive to independent thermal noise.
