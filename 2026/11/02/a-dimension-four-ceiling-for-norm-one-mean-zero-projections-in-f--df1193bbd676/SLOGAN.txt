For the real finite weighted Lorentz norm \(\sum_jw_j|x|_{(j)}\), the coordinate-sum-zero hyperplane is one-complemented in every dimension two, exactly when \(w_1=w_2+w_3\) in dimension three, and only at the zero-tail boundary \(w_4=0\), \(w_1=w_2+w_3\) in dimension four; it never is in higher dimensions. An explicit signed-support maximum gives the optimal projection norm for every nonincreasing weight vector.
