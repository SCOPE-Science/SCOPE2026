For every complex quadratic polynomial of norm one on \(c_0\), restricting to any finite coordinate set loses at most \(8\sqrt{e^4+2e^2+1/2}\sqrt\delta+\delta\) in norm when the restricted polynomial has deficit \(\delta\), uniformly in the number of coordinates. A three-coordinate family proves the square-root exponent is best possible, although the displayed prefactor is not claimed sharp.
