In a planar Brownian shear flow with full Poisson resetting, the stationary projection transverse to the flow is exactly Laplace and moment-determinate, whereas every shear-sensitive direction has a sharp \(|z|^{-1/2}\exp(-B\sqrt{|z|})\) density tail and is Hamburger moment-indeterminate. The full leading tail constant explicitly records the projection angle, making zero effective shear the unique directional determinacy boundary.
