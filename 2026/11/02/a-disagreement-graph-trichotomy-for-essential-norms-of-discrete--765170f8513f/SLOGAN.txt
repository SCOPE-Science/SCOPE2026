For two discrete composition operators, the essential norm of their difference on \(\ell_\infty\) is exactly \(0\), \(1\), or \(2\), determined by whether their disagreement graph has finitely many edges, a finite vertex cover but infinitely many edges, or an infinite matching. For proper symbols on \(c_0\), the intermediate phase disappears: every noncompact difference has essential norm \(2\).
