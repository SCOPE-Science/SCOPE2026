For the sharp mixed constant/third Taylor-coefficient functional on analytic \(H^{1/2}\), the first departure from binomial fourth-power extremizers occurs at a parameter \(t_*\) strictly between \(1/5\) and \(47/100\), while those binomials remain strict local maxima all the way to \(t=1/2\). At \(t_*\) a separated non-binomial fourth-power extremizer coexists, proving a discontinuous optimizer transition without claiming the exact value of \(t_*\).
