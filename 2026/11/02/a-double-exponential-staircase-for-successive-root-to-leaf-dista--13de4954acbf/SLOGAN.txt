For the critical binary Kesten tree, the distance \(R_{m+1}\) to the \((m+1)\)-st closest leaf satisfies \(\Pr(R_{m+1}>r)\sim2^{-2^{r-m+1}+r-2m+2}\) for each fixed \(m\ge0\), so each extra permitted nearby leaf halves the leading double-exponential cost. Conditioned on this rare event, the initial \(m\) offspine subtrees are single-vertex leaves and all other visible offspine subtrees avoid leaves with probability tending to one.
