In characteristic five, the genus-three order-four family \(y^2=x^7+a x^5+b x^3+x\) has a double nonordinary Hasse divisor \(ab+1=0\), with exactly six smooth marked parameters at which the \(5\)-rank falls from one to zero. Those six points form Frobenius orbits of lengths \(1,1,2,2\) and represent a single geometric isomorphism class.
