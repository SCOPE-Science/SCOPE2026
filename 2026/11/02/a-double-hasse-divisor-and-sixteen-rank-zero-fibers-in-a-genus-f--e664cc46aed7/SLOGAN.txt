For the smooth characteristic-seven genus-four family \(y^3=x^5+ux^3+vx+1\), the nonordinary Hasse divisor is twice the conic \(u^2+2v=0\). Along this conic the Jacobian has \(7\)-rank two except at exactly sixteen reduced marked parameters, where its rank is zero; no rank-one or rank-three members occur.
