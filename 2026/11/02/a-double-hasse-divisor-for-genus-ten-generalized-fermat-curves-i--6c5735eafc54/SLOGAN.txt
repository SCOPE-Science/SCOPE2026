For the genus-ten generalized Fermat \((3,3)\) pencil in characteristic seven, every Jacobian is ordinary except at one unordered branch-moduli point \(j_{\rm br}=4\), where the \(7\)-rank is exactly \(8\) and the \(a\)-number is \(2\). The corresponding Hasse divisor is exactly double, supported at six ordered cross-ratio parameters.
