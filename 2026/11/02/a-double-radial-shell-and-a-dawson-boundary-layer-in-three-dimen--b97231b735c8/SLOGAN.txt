Uniformly averaging a three-dimensional Boltzmann breather over its oscillation phase produces at least two separated radial shells at sufficiently strong breathing, even though every instantaneous Maxwell radial law has one mode and the spatial volume density is strictly decreasing. The inner shell lies at radius \(r\sim 2.1241137947\sqrt{1-q}\) in mean-variance-normalized units, with a universal Dawson-function profile.
