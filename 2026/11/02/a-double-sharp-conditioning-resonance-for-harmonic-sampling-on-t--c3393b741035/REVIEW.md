# Scientific review: \(A_4\) harmonic sampling

## Correctness — PASS
The \(S^3\) addition theorem reduces evaluation rank to an exact Chebyshev Gram matrix. The odd signed-incidence and even Johnson-graph eigendecompositions give explicit eigenvalues and multiplicities. Since the only off-diagonal Chebyshev inputs are \(0\) and \(\pm1/2\), the eigenvalues are exact integers divided by \(k+1\). Finite low-degree evaluation and uniform analytic bounds above the cutoff prove every quantifier and the unique sharp degrees. The packaged exact-rational checker independently constructs the matrices for 301 degrees; finite checks supplement the proof.

## Originality — PASS, bounded comparison
The arXiv full text of Bannai–Okuda–Tagami (arXiv:1308.5101, sections on harmonic-index designs and the reproducing kernel) was inspected. It supplies general tools, not the specific \(A_4\) all-degree matrix spectrum, tight-degree classification, or two sharp condition-number resonances. Searches on exact root counts, equivalent Johnson-scheme formulations, Gram eigenvalues, and condition-number identities identified no directly covering result. Previously accessible results on type-\(D_n\), \(F_4\), type-\(B_n\), and simplex-edge-midpoint configurations concern different sampling objects or kernels and do not imply the stated \(A_4\) constants. Older association-scheme, cubature and root-representation literature remains a possible equivalence risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact spectral frame bounds control interpolation stability uniformly over infinitely many harmonic degrees on a natural classical root configuration. The unexpectedly repeated worst condition number at degrees three and ten, the unique worst lower frame bound at degree ten, and infinitely many exactly tight degrees supply a complete, motivated quantitative classification, not just a finite rank computation.

## Limits
All statements concern the twenty normalized roots of \(A_4\) in \(S^3\), parity-constrained exact-degree interpolation, and the specified unit-diagonal Gram normalization. No generalization to arbitrary type-\(A_n\) roots is claimed. Literature coverage is bounded, and independent validation remains outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
