On the twenty \(A_4\) roots, exact-degree spherical harmonics realize every antipodally parity-compatible datum for all degrees \(k\ge3\), with a uniform normalized Gram spectrum in \([2/11,22/13]\). Sampling is exactly tight precisely for \(k\equiv5\pmod6\), whereas its sharp condition number \(6\) occurs exactly twice, at degrees \(3\) and \(10\).
