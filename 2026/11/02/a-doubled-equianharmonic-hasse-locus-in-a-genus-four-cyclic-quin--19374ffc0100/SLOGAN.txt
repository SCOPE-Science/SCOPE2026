In the genus-four cyclic-quintic family \(y^5=x(x-1)(x-\lambda)^2\) over characteristic \(11\), the Jacobian is ordinary except at the two roots of \(\lambda^2-\lambda+1\), where its \(11\)-rank is \(2\) and its \(a\)-number is \(2\). Each exceptional parameter is a double zero of the normalized Hasse determinant, and the two parameters represent one unordered branch configuration.
