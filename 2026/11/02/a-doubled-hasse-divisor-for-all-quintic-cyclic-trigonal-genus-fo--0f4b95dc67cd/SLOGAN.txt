For every smooth cyclic trigonal genus-four curve \(y^3=F(x)\) with \(F\) an arbitrary monic quintic in characteristic seven, the coefficient-normalized Hasse–Witt determinant is \(\Delta(F)\bigl([x^6]F(x)^2\bigr)^2\). Hence the coefficient-space nonordinary divisor is a smooth reduced hypersurface with multiplicity two, and no member has Jacobian \(a\)-number one.
