# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Directly substituting the density \(ab=3/4\) into the published rational Zibulski–Zeevi matrix produces a \(3\times4\) fiber. On the entire real interval \(b\in[11/4,13/4]\), its last two rows equal \(2-3/b\), while its first entry differs by \(|b-3|/b\). Hence rank is strictly below three everywhere, and continuity prevents a Gabor lower frame bound. The stand-alone exact-rational checker passes but is not the infinite-parameter proof.

## Originality
PASS (subject to historical-priority risk). Lemvig–Nielsen's 2016 all-rational theorem covers only \([23/8,25/8]\) for this density, and their separately enlarged theorem is for density \(5/6\). The 2023 Ghosh–Selvan families and Lemvig's 2026 family do not imply a \(q=4\) extension. Gröchenig's 2015 denominator constraints likewise exclude the \(q=4\) case. published-finding corpus and web alias searches did not return this precise extended slit; that absence is not proof of novelty.

## Value
PASS. Determining exact nonframe portions of the triangular B-spline's still-incomplete Gabor frame set is a motivated question. The theorem doubles the general guaranteed halfwidth at the smallest relevant reduced denominator \(q=4\), and gives a simple exact row-rank mechanism and exceptional central rank-one collapse. It does not purport to settle global boundaries.

The strongest residual risk is an unlocated earlier equivalent nonframe interval. No independent validation or optimality beyond the stated domain is claimed.
