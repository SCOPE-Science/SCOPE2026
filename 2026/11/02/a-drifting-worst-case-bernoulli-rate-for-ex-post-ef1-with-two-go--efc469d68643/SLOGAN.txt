For the smallest nontrivial equal-bundle EF1 experiment—two goods per participant with independent binary utilities—the chance that a fixed ex-ante envy-free allocation remains EF1 is minimized at a unique Bernoulli rate \(p_n\), which strictly decreases with population size. Its sharp scale is \(p_n^2=(\log n+\log\log n-\log2+o(1))/(2n)\), and the minimum fairness probability is \(\exp[-\frac12n\log n+\frac12n\log\log n+\frac12n\log2+o(n)]\).
