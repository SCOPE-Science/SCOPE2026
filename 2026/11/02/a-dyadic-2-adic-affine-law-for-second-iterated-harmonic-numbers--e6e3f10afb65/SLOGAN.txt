For the second iterated harmonic numbers, dyadic block increments converge 2-adically to an odd constant with an error divisible by \(2^r\) at block \(r\), yielding an exact affine 2-adic asymptotic law. In particular, \(G(2^r)\equiv113r+11\pmod{256}\) for every \(r\ge3\), and every reduced denominator of \(G(n)\) is odd.
