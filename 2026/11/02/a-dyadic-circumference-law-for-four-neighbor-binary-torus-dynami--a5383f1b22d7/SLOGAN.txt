For the binary four-neighbor sum rule on an \(m\times n\) torus with one dyadic circumference \(m\ge4\), all nilpotent Jordan blocks have the same exact length \(\max(m,2^{\nu_2(n)})/2\), and their number is \(2\min(m,2^{\nu_2(n)})\). This yields the complete first-periodic-entry distribution and shows that its normalized form is unaffected by the odd factor of \(n\).
