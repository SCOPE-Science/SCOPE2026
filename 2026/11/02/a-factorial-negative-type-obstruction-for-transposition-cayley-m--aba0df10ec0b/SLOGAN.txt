The generalized roundness of the complete-transposition Cayley metric on \(S_n\) admits a strictly decreasing, explicitly specified representation-theoretic obstruction that is less than \(100/(n-3)!\) for every \(n\ge53\). For \(S_6\) this obstruction is sharp: the critical exponent is \(0.154137773765\ldots\), with a 100-dimensional zero-sum nullspace.
