The \(2640\) labelled regular seven-player tournaments form a connected, bipartite, \(14\)-regular triangle-interchange graph of diameter \(9\), with exactly three relabelling-invariant distance profiles. A tournament reaches its converse in the minimum seven reversals exactly when its directed triangles contain a Fano-plane decomposition: this occurs twice in each of two isomorphism types and not at all in the third, whose converse is its unique distance-nine antipode.
