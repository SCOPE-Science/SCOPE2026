For \(k\) hard-core bosons hopping on a complete network of \(N\) sites, one lossy site eventually captures a maximally mixed sector with probability exactly \(\min\{2k/N,1\}\); spinless fermions instead have capture probability \(1-(N-k)(N-k-1)/[N(N-1)]\). Hard-core bosons become completely capturable at half filling, while a half-filled fermionic system retains a dark fraction tending to \(1/4\).
