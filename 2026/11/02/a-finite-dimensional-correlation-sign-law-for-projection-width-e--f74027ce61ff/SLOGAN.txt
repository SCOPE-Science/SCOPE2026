For the same uniformly random Euclidean-sphere direction, the covariance of the \(r\)-th power of a dual-norm-ball projection half-width with any even power \(2m\ge4\) has the strict sign of \(r-2\) in every dimension \(d\ge2\). An exact Dirichlet-moment formula and a telescoping product identity prove the sharp Euclidean-exponent transition without a high-dimensional limit.
