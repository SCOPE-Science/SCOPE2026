For uniformly random finite structures with one bijective unary operation and at least two named colours, the entire automorphism-group isomorphism type converges in total variation to an explicit finite random product of cyclic wreath groups. The exactly-two-automorphism stratum has a closed necklace-series generating function and a strictly positive limiting probability (about \(0.2618482364\) for two colours), while the uncoloured case has automorphism groups growing without bound.
