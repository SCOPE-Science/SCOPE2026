At the critical quarter shift, every balanced two-block periodic binary Fourier system contains an exact positive Hilbert-matrix corner. This gives an explicit finite-period bound for its least squared synthesis value and proves the previously conjectured one-sided estimate \(A_p=O((\log p)^{-2})\), with \(\limsup_{p\to\infty}(\log p)^2A_p\le\pi^4/2\); the matching lower bound remains open.
