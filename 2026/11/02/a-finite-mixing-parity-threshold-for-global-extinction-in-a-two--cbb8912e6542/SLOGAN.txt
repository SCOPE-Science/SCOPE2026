In a two-patch infection map with repeated exchange followed by saturation, strong swapping creates an exact odd–even extinction paradox: every even number of movement rounds supports a unique globally attracting endemic state, while a finite initial range of odd numbers eliminates infection. The sharp cutoff is \((2p-1)^k\geq(\bar r-1)/(\bar r-r_1r_2)\) for odd \(k\), assuming \(r_1r_2<1<\bar r=(r_1+r_2)/2\); the infinitely fast-mixed limit is nevertheless endemic.
