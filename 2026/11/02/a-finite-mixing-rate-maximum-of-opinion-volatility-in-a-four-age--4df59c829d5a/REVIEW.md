The specified four independent Poisson events determine one unambiguous stochastic process. A two-anchor-event coupling gives uniqueness of its invariant distribution on the compact state square. The stationary generator equations for the linear and quadratic observables yield the exact means and variances. The derivative factors into an everywhere-positive term and a quadratic having precisely one positive root for partial trust, with a separate monotone full-copying endpoint. The standalone generator reconstruction separately passes 48 exact-rational cases and symbolic moment/derivative identities. The analytic proof, rather than the finite checks, establishes the infinite parameter assertion. Residual risk: independent proof review has not been performed.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The exact general gossip model, stationary-moment methods, and finite tree analyses are prior work of Acemoglu, Como, Fagnani and Ozdaglar (arXiv:1009.2653). Their Section 5 explicit tree variance is specialized to full copying, not the partial-trust finite rate optimum. Yildiz et al. (preprint 2011) likewise use discrete full-copying voters, while Frasca et al. (arXiv:1304.2268) use personal prejudice and focus on ergodicity. Inspected texts and targeted scientific-record and literature searches did not give a theorem subsuming the unique interior volatility peak or its divergence as trust approaches one. Although the computation is a specialization of existing general moment equations, the global parameter classification is not an immediate restatement of them. Search coverage cannot establish historical priority; an unlocated explicit computation remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The smallest natural chain with two flexible mediators and opposing fixed influences exhibits a quantitative tradeoff: mean disagreement falls strictly with more communication, whereas fluctuations peak at a finite communication intensity unless trust is complete. The sharp formula, parameter-wide uniqueness, and singular full-copying boundary make a useful diagnostic when interpreting fluctuating opinions under stubborn influences. This is a structural statement about a specified stochastic mechanism, not a claim about any real electorate or measured social outcome. Four-agent symmetry and common trust are restrictive.
