In a four-agent stochastic gossip chain with two opposed stubborn opinions, stronger peer contact always reduces the mean opinion gap but, for every partial-trust fraction \(0<\theta<1\), individual stationary opinion variance first rises and then falls. Its unique optimum is an explicit finite peer-to-stubborn contact-rate ratio; at full copying \(\theta=1\), the finite optimum disappears and variance increases monotonically.
