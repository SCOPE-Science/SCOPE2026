For every wheel with \(n\ge5\) rim vertices, the complete domain-rank polynomial of its metric partial-isometry monoid is \((1+z)\) times the induced-subgraph partial-automorphism polynomial of the rim cycle, plus the explicit four-term correction \(2nz+5n^2z^2+2n^2(n-1)z^3+2n^2z^4\). Exactly \(2n+5n^2+2n^3\) maps fail to either avoid or fix the hub, and none have domain rank greater than four.
