# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact elimination is on the zero-total-mass subspace of dimension \(n-1\), where both frozen Markov generators are invertible. Detailed balance annihilates the constant-in-rate current term, and the adjugate of the affine resolvent has degree at most \(n-2\). Its determinant cannot vanish at a positive switching rate because this would introduce a second stationary direction in the irreducible joint chain. The explicit three-site example is established by exact rational elimination. The embedded standalone checker independently compares full and reduced stationary systems, but does not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS on the inspected evidence, with residual risk

The closest published antecedents include the discrete-time minimal Brownian ratchet of Lee et al. (2002/2003), Jarzynski–Mazonka's specific thermal ratchet (1999), the no-pumping theorem of Rahav–Horowitz–Jarzynski (2008), and the two-state continuum flashing diffusion of Levien–Bressloff (2015). Those works establish discrete ratchet currents, restrictions on pumping and continuous flashing transport, not the checked statement that a two-phase reversible \(n\)-state stationary edge current has a switching-rate numerator of degree at most \(n-2\). The specific 1999 thermal-ratchet paper was not inspected in complete full text; historical-priority uncertainty and possible coverage in general finite-network response theory remain. An absence of a direct hit in literature/database searches is not a proof of global novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result is a model-wide upper bound on the number of transport-direction changes under a single experimentally tunable kinetic parameter. It is sharp already in the smallest nontrivial cyclic setting, and it gives a structural falsification criterion for two-configuration finite-state ratchets independently of their energy landscapes and barrier values. Both the upper bound and the example matter; merely solving one small linear system would not give the universal constraint.

## Limits

The theorem does not extend as stated to site-dependent phase switching, three or more phases, nonreversible frozen dynamics, or changes in the underlying rates during the scan. Independent priority review and independent mathematical validation remain unperformed.
