For truncated coordinate-cross algebras \(A_{n,d}\), the reduced unital endomorphism variety has respectively one, \(n^n\), and \((n+1)^n\) irreducible components at lengths \(d=2,3,4\); non-equidimensionality starts sharply at \(d=4\), \(n\ge3\). Every component is explicitly affine-linear, and all endomorphisms admit uniform exact finite-field counts in every characteristic.
