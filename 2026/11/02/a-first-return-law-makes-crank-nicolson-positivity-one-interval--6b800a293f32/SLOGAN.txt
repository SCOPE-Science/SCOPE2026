The backward-Euler return resolvent of every finite irreducible Markov chain has strictly decreasing diagonal entries, even without reversibility: a first-excursion renewal law makes Crank–Nicolson positivity hold on exactly one initial interval of step sizes. For a directed \(n\)-cycle this sharp interval ends at \(h=2r_n/[a(1-r_n)]\), where \(\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}r_n^j=1\); the three-cycle threshold is \((1+\sqrt5)/a\).
