For uniformly arriving cars on the complete rooted \(b\)-ary tree of height two, the first parking failures appear at the nonclassical load scale \(b^{5/4}\), or equivalently \(N^{5/8}\) parking spaces. At critical load \(m\sim tb^{5/4}\), the number of failed cars converges to \((\operatorname{Poisson}(t^4/8)-1)_+\), generated by rare pairs of duplicated leaf preferences within a common parent.
