Reusing a fitted regression control variate on Gaussian-quadratic low-fidelity observations is exactly unbiased once \(n\ge3\) paired samples are available, but has infinite variance until \(n\ge5\), despite all source observations having finite moments of every order. Above that boundary, a Gamma–Dirichlet factorization gives the exact variance and a sharp signal-to-noise threshold for improvement over uncorrected Monte Carlo.
