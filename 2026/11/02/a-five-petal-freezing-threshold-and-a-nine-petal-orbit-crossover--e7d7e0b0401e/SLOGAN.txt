On friendship graphs with \(m\) triangles sharing one hub, five-color firefly dynamics has exactly \(4^m-2^m\) primitive six-cycles and \(1+2U_m/5\) primitive five-cycles, where \(U_m\) counts onto assignments of \(m\) labelled petals to five colors. Nonsynchronous five-cycles first appear at five petals, five-cycle orbits first outnumber six-cycles at nine petals, and all configurations become periodic within a sharp maximum of 33 updates.
