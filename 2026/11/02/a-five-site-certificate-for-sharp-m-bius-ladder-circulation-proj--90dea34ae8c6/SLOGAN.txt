Every real \(\ell_1\) cycle space of a Möbius ladder with \(2n\) vertices, \(n\ge10\), has exact minimum projection norm \(12/5-16/(5n)\), certified by one finite-support dual inequality and an explicit dihedrally invariant projection. This unexpectedly equals the corresponding norm for planar circular ladders of the same width, although the graph families have different topology.
