# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The arbitrary-prime proof is reconstructed from the cyclotomic independence of normalized open-path energy modes, the classification of swapped-pair and zero-band degeneracies, a finite-dimensional absorbing-semigroup lemma, and the exact three-term bright-projector decomposition. The symmetric zero-band diagonal is computed with a trigonometric fourth-moment identity. The remaining rank-one zero-band contribution is the local matrix kernel \((4I-A^2)/(2p)\). Each piece has been checked for boundary cases, including \(p=3\), and their combination gives the claimed five cases, labelled counts, and complete-capture criterion. The finite checker reproduces 1,364 site probabilities and three dissipative evolutions; those are corroboration rather than infinite proof.

## Value — PASS

Local absorption is a natural quantum transport observable. This complete all-site result identifies precise source locations of unity capture and an otherwise unexpectedly small catalogue of values despite growth of the square lattice. It distinguishes sites not distinguishable by the previously known averaged capture, including a separation-two correction localized on one coordinate parity sector. The exact values and multiplicities are useful for comparing initial-state preparation in coherent capture networks; no downstream general theorem is required for the intrinsic mathematical benchmark.
