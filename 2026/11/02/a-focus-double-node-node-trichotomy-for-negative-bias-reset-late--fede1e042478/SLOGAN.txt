For a negatively biased max-plus feedback map with a stable positive branch, a fixed positive seed reaches the absorbing state after a uniformly bounded delay in the focus regime, a logarithmic delay in the distinct-node regime, and a logarithmic delay with an additional \(\log\log(1/\varepsilon)\) term at the repeated-root boundary. The node regimes admit exact finite-index bias thresholds, including equality at first switching.
