For the cycle \(C_n\), \(n\ge5\), minimum isolating sets form a one-vertex-replacement graph whose size is \(\frac nk\binom{k+r-1}{r}\) and degree is always \(2r\), with \(k=\lceil n/4\rceil\) and \(r=4k-n\). The graph is connected exactly when four does not divide \(n\); at residue three it is itself the cycle \(C_n\), while at residue zero it is four isolated vertices.
