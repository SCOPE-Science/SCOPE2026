For unmodified second-order Richardson extrapolation of backward Euler on a unit-rate directed Markov cycle, the first negative diagonal occurs sharply at fourteen states, although four or fewer states are positive at every step and every cycle with at least five states fails at sufficiently small steps. On the fourteen-state cycle the exact nonnegative timestep set unexpectedly has three components, \(\{0\}\cup[\alpha,\beta]\cup[\gamma,\infty)\), with rigorously isolated thresholds near \(3.06637\), \(5.56568\), and \(7.99444\).
