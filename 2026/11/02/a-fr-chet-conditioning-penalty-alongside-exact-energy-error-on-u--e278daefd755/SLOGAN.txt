For piecewise-linear Poisson solves on meshes made from independent uniform random knots, the mean squared energy error is exactly \(1/[2(n+1)(n+2)]\), but the unpreconditioned nodal stiffness condition number has infinite mean for every \(n\ge3\). The scaled condition number \(\kappa_n/n^3\) converges to a shape-one Fréchet law with scale \(2/\pi^2\), while the random-mesh expected energy error is asymptotically six times that of the equally sized uniform mesh.
