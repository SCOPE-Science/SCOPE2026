For simple random walk from the hub of a friendship graph with \(n\) triangles, the centered cover-time distribution has a sharp positive correction of order \(n^{-1/3}\) to its shifted-Gumbel limit. The resulting expected-cover-time correction is negative and of order \(n^{2/3}\), with explicit coefficient \(4\Gamma(4/3)/2^{4/3}\); both effects reflect rare entirely unentered triangles.
