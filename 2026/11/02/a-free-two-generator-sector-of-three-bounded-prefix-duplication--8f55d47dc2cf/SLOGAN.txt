For every binary seed beginning `010`, all descendants that still begin `01` under prefix duplication of length at most three form the uniquely derivable regular language \((01\mid010)^*s\), with length–distance series \((1-uz^2-uz^3)^{-1}\). Consequently, every same-parity pair of finite Fibonacci words with indices at least two has a unique derivation, with the exact minimum number of steps equal to the difference of their numbers of `1` symbols.
