In every dimension \(d\ge2\), shrinking product-coordinate collars of weighted symmetrized-polydisc Bergman spaces have a unique \(N^{-1}\) spectral window: their finite-section eigenvalues converge to gamma-convolution probabilities on an ordered simplex. The full projection-defect coefficient has two sharp, explicit limits, proportional to \(a^{d(\alpha+1)}\) for small collar parameter \(a\) and to \(a^{-1}\) for large \(a\).
