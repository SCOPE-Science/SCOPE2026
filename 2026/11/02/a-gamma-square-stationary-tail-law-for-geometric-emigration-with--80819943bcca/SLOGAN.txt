For a critical geometric branching process with geometric emigration but no post-reproduction immigration, positive recurrence begins at \(p>1/2\), and the invariant distribution has the sharp Gamma-square tail \(\Pr(Y>n)\sim\Gamma(a)^2n^{1-a}\) for \(1<a=p/(1-p)<2\). At \(a=2\) a logarithmic transition occurs, while above it the stationary mean equals \((a-2)^{-1}\).
