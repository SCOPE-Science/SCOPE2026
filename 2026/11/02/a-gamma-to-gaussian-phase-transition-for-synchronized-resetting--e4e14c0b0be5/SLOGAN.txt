For Brownian searchers reset together by one Poisson clock of rate \(a(\log N)^2/t_0\), the single-attempt success probability crosses sharply from a Gamma-weighted Gumbel law to a saddle-point power law at \(a=1\), with a Gaussian \((\log N)^{-1/2}\) crossover. After scaling by the reset rate times that success probability, the earliest absorption time is asymptotically exponential.
