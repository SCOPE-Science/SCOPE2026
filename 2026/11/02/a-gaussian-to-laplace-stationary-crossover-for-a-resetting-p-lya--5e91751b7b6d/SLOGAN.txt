In a balanced Pólya urn completely reset at independent random times, the stationary red proportion has an exact beta-binomial/geometric mixture law and correlation decay \((1-r)^h\). As the reset rate crosses the reciprocal initial population, its fluctuations pass through a continuous family of Gaussian mixtures between a normal law and a Laplace law; the latter comes from a Brownian process with Poisson resetting.
