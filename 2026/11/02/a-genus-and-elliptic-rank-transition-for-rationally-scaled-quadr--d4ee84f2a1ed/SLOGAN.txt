Allowing a positive rational scale on a fixed quadratic irrational shift turns simultaneous rationality of \(r+\lambda\sqrt d\) and \(r^k+\lambda\sqrt d\) into a smooth plane curve of genus \((k-2)(k-3)/2\). Infinite families occur at \((d,k)=(6,3)\) and \((15,4)\), while for every fixed squarefree \(d>1\) and \(k\ge5\) there are only finitely many rational scales and inputs.
