The smooth genus-five complete intersection \(xy+z^2+2u^2=4xu+yz+3uv+3v^2=x^2+2y^2+4z^2+4uv=0\) over \(\mathbb F_5\) has gonality exactly five. Its thirty-one rational containing quadrics include only four singular members, all nonsplit rank four, giving a sharp arithmetic obstruction to degree-four maps.
