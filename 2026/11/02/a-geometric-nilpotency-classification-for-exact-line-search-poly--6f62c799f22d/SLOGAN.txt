For exact-line-search PolyCD with away steps on a positive-definite quadratic over a triangle, an interior target lying on a metric Thales arc has quadratic local convergence after either one or two sweeps, determined solely by whether its orthogonal vertex pair is consecutive or separated in the update order. All other interior targets have a nonzero linearized spectral rate, and a rational example shows that ordinary forward-only PolyCD loses the quadratic cancellation.
