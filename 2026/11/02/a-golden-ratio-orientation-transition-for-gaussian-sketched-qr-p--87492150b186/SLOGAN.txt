For the first QR column pivot selected from a two-row Gaussian sketch of a matrix with squared singular values \(L\) and \(1\), the worst expected loss of squared pivot norm switches from axis-aligned to obliquely oriented columns at the sharp threshold \(L=2+\sqrt5\). Above that threshold the maximum is exactly \(((\sqrt5-1)/2)^{5/2}\sqrt L\), despite the aligned orientation having bounded expected loss.
