For the homophily-weighted static-network opinion model, every binary sign arrangement with a like-minded neighbor at each vertex produces a locally exponentially attracting equilibrium at sufficiently strong finite homophily. At sufficiently strong homophily, such stable genuine polarization is possible on a connected network exactly when it contains two disjoint edges, so a four-vertex path is the smallest example; incompatible nonzero sign arrangements cannot be equilibria at any homophily level.
