A finite positive Markov chain killed at state-dependent intensity \(c_i/k^\gamma\) has a sharp survival-memory threshold at \(\gamma=1/2\): at criticality, its survival probability gains the exact positive factor \(n^{\sigma^2/2}\), where \(\sigma^2\) is the stationary Green–Kubo variance. Even with the stationary hazard distribution fixed, a two-state family realizes every positive polynomial exponent through its temporal correlation.
