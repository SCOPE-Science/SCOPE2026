In a Poisson-reset harmonic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process, the largest dimensionless pre-reset potential energy among \(n\) complete cycles has a Gumbel limit with an independent Gamma-distributed exponential-age mark. Its maximizing reset interval grows only on the \(\log\log n\) scale, in sharp contrast to the longest interval's \(\log n\) scale, and the cycle-energy exponential moment at the natural boundary is finite exactly when the reset rate exceeds the relaxation rate.
