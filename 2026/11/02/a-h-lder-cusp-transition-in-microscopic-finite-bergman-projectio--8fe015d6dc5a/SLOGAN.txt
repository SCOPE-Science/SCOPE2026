For finite Bergman polynomial projections on a disk with a positive radial Hölder cusp, the first microscopic boundary correction is of order \(N^{-\alpha}\); its second correction switches sharply from cusp-square order \(N^{-2\alpha}\) to the smooth/discretization order \(N^{-1}\) at \(\alpha=1/2\). Explicit uniform two-point coefficients identify the crossover and quantify the failure of second-order boundary universality under finite regularity.
