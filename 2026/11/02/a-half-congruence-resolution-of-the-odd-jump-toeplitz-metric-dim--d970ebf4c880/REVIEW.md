# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. For every integer \(k\ge3\) and \(n\ge4k+3\), the proof establishes an exact finite-interval graph-distance formula, an injective three-landmark residue signature, and a lower bound excluding every two-vertex resolving set. The distance formula is independently checked by breadth-first search on thirteen test graphs (21,945 source--target distances); the proof establishes the infinite parameter range and does not extrapolate from these checks.

## Originality

PASS under the checked literature with residual publication-priority uncertainty. Nadeem--Qu--Ahmad--Azeem (2022), DOI 10.1155/2022/9155291, explicitly state the odd-jump assertion as Conjecture 2 and prove only even jump values in their pertinent theorem. Liu--Nadeem--Siddiqui--Nazir (2019), DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2938579, address three-generator jumps through six, not the present family. The 2022 and 2024 Toeplitz partition-dimension works concern a different invariant and do not imply the constructed metric basis. Targeted mathematical-record searches yielded no equivalent published theorem; search results are not a guarantee of absolute priority.

## Value

PASS. A uniform explicit basis proves a full congruence subfamily of a stated infinite-parameter conjecture, rather than treating isolated numerical instances. The boundary-independent distance identity and periodic residue separation explain why all graph lengths admit the same three landmarks.

## Limits

The jump values \(b\equiv3\pmod4\) are not settled, and independent verification of the proof and comprehensive publication priority has not been performed. A 2025 broad review was not obtainable in full; this is a documented coverage risk, not a claim that it lacks the result.
