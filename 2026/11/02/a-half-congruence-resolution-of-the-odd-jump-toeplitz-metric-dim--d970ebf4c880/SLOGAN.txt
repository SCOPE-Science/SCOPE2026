For every \(k\ge3\) and \(n\ge4k+3\), the Toeplitz graph with allowed vertex differences \(1,2,4k+1\) has metric dimension exactly \(3\), witnessed by the length-independent landmarks \(\{0,1,2k+1\}\). This settles the \(b\equiv1\pmod4\) subfamily of the 2022 odd-jump Toeplitz metric-dimension conjecture.
