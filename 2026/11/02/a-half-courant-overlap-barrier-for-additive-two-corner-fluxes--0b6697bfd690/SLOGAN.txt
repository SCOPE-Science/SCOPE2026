For a strictly hyperbolic two-contact conservation law, adding exact isolated lower- and upper-corner flux corrections ceases to give the exact finite-face flux once the tangential Courant number exceeds \(1/2\), even when a full characteristic Courant bound remains below one. The missing flux is an explicit squared-hinge term times the mixed finite difference of the normal flux, and the rule is universally exact precisely for additively separable normal fluxes.
