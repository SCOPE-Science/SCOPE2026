# Review

**Correctness — PASS.** A direct level-set decomposition of nonnegative inputs reduces the norm to binary indicators. Every row of the positive geometric Cesàro matrix has sum at most one, and its column sums strictly decrease, so the first \(k\) indicators attain the optimal combined endpoint norm among all \(k\)-supports. Exact harmonic algebra proves the staircase; the included exact rational checker exercises 128 parameter cases and 3,885 sign tests. The proof covers all stated real parameters analytically.

**Originality — PASS (self-review, qualified).** The closest published primary source, Curbera–Ricker, arXiv:2302.08750v1, provides the operator, compactness, spectrum and a space-independent norm upper bound, not the exact finite mixed-endpoint norm or harmonic transition law. Albanese–Bonet–Ricker, arXiv:2305.04805v1, addresses spectral questions; Jameson–Lashkaripour (2000) addresses lower bounds of averaging operators. Search-based overlap checks included exact mixed-norm aliases and published mathematical indices. An unindexed general matrix theorem or earlier exact two-level Lorentz formula remains possible. The latter two sources were not inspected in complete full text.

**Value — PASS.** The interpolating endpoint norm is natural; the finding supplies an exact norm with explicit extremizers throughout the parameter range and detects a genuine support-rank phase transition (first at \(n=4\), \(\varepsilon=1/13\)), plus the limiting optimal rank proportion \(1/e\). It sharpens a broad norm estimate into a structural phase diagram rather than adding a generic finite computation.

**Limitations.** This is not an infinite-dimensional bound, does not cover arbitrary Lorentz weights, and does not classify every extremizing vector. Bibliographic comparison and independent assessment remain incomplete.
