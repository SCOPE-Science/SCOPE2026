On the finite mixed endpoint norm \(N_\varepsilon=(1-\varepsilon)\|\cdot\|_\infty+\varepsilon\|\cdot\|_1\), the exact norm of every geometric Cesàro matrix is attained by an initial-support indicator and has an explicit maximum over support sizes. For ordinary Cesàro averaging these optimal sizes form a strictly ordered harmonic staircase, with the first non-singleton phase appearing in dimension four and the small-mixture rank asymptotic to \(n/e\).
