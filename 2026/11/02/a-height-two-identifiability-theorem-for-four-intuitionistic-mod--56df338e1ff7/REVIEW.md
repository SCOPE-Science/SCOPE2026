## Value — PASS

When an intuitionistic modal semantics starts with one underlying accessibility relation, it is natural to ask whether the family of modal truth-set operators retains or forgets that relation. This theorem determines the exact structural boundary of lossless four-connective observation on every preorder, and gives the inverse using atomic one-step tests. Branching height-two orders remain faithful, whereas a single nontrivial equivalence pair or a three-point strict chain creates undetectable edges. The two-world witnesses show that the quartet is irredundant even in the smallest meaningful case. This separates full relational identifiability from modal validity or eliminability and supplies a reusable semantic diagnostic rather than an arbitrary numerical census.
