For the generalized Cesàro operator \(C_t\) and the compact diagonal \(D_\alpha=\operatorname{diag}((n+1)^{-\alpha})\) on \(\ell^2\), the squared Hilbert–Schmidt commutator norm has a sharp, three-phase cusp as \(t\uparrow1\): power \((1-t^2)^{2\alpha}\) below \(\alpha=1/2\), a squared logarithm exactly at that threshold, and a single logarithm above it. The endpoint at \(\alpha=1\) equals \(\zeta(3)-3\zeta(4)/4\).
