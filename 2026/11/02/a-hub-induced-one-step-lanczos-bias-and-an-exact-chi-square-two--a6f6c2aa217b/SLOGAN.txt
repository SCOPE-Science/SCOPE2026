On the unweighted star Laplacian, a single Rademacher-probe one-step Lanczos estimate of \(\operatorname{tr}(tI+L_n)^{-1}\) typically underestimates by order \(n\), while two steps have an exact finite-binomial nonnegative quadrature error and a shifted chi-square trace-error limit. For fixed \(t>0\), the two-step limiting bias is \(-1/[t(t+1)^2]\) and variance is \(2/(t+1)^4\), so outlier detection eliminates the extensive bias without eliminating all stochastic error.
