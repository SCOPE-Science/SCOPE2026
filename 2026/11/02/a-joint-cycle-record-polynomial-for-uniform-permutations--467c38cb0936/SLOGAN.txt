For a uniform permutation, the joint cycle-count generating polynomial and the event of an upper record at a specified position differ from the independent model by one explicit rising-factorial term. Every noninitial record position has positive covariance \((i-1)/(i^2\binom ni)\) with the total cycles of the same permutation, while cycle-weighted Ewens sampling shifts its record probability below or above the uniform value exactly when its parameter is below or above one.
