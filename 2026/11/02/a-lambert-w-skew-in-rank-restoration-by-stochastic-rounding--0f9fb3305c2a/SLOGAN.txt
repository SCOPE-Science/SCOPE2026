Independent one-bit rounding of a constant rank-one matrix restores full column rank most reliably at a slightly *above-midpoint* input fraction: for fixed width \(d\), the optimal fraction is \(1/2+W(n/(d-1))/(2n)+O_d((\log n)^2/n^2)\). This cuts the asymptotic rank-deficiency probability by the factor \((d-1)/(d+1)\) relative to midpoint rounding, while for two columns the failure probability is exactly \((p^2+(1-p)^2)^n+2(1-p)^n-2(1-p)^{2n}\).
