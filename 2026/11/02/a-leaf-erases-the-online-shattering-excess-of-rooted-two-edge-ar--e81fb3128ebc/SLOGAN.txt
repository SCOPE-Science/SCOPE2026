For every rooted height-three poset formed from \(t\ge1\) two-step arms and \(s\ge0\) extra leaves, ideal membership has VC dimension \(t+s\) but Littlestone dimension \(t+1\) when \(s=0\) and \(t+s\) otherwise. Appending just one leaf therefore erases the adaptive/static gap while preserving the larger hypothesis class.
