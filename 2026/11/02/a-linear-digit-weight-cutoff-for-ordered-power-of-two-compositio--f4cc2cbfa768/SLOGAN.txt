Ordered compositions into powers of two satisfy a uniform linear digit-weight cutoff: \(s_2(n+N)\geq2N\) forces \(2^N\mid\vartheta(n)\). Hence the number of indices below \(x\) with \(2^N\nmid\vartheta(n)\) is at most \(O_N((\log x)^{2N-1})\), improving the exponential-in-precision cutoff from earlier work.
