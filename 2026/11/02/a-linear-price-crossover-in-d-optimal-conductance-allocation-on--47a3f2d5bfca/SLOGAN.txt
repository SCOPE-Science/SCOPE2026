On every square torus with independently priced horizontal and vertical conductances, the weighted spanning-tree determinant has a unique unrestricted optimum. When the vertical price grows as \(Q=\kappa n\), its optimal vertical-to-horizontal conductance ratio has a sharp \(n^{-2}\) law and an explicit \(n^{-3}\) correction governed by an integral hyperbolic-cotangent crossover; the high-price power law changes when network size and price limits are interchanged.
