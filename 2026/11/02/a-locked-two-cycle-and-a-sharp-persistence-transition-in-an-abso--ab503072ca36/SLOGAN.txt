The absolute-value FPFM2 map has exactly two prime-two orbits for every forcing strength once \(\beta\ge4/(4-\pi)\), but below that threshold its two-cycles disappear above an explicit finite forcing cutoff. Exactly at the transition, one orbit locks to \((\pi/2,-\pi/4)\leftrightarrow(-\pi/2,\pi/4)\) independently of the forcing, and the smooth sign-crossing orbit has a sharp, explicit sink criterion.
