Weighted complete-graph coronas have an exact generalized-roundness threshold determined by one radial equation, and the critical snowflake loses exactly one Euclidean dimension. As private pendant edges shrink, the exponent approaches \(2\) only at the logarithmic rate \((2-\wp_m(t))\log(1/t)\to\log(m-1)\), despite the limiting quotient becoming equilateral with infinite generalized roundness.
