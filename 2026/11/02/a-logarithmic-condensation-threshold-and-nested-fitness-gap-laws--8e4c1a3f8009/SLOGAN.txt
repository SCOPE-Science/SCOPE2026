For a locally reset interacting fitness model with near-optimal resetting rate \(r(1-u)\sim cu[\log(1/u)]^\theta\), stationary condensation occurs exactly for \(\theta\le1\), with density growing by four distinct logarithmic laws. The condensate's fitness gap has a universal uniform or beta distribution on logarithmic scales, and at \(\theta=1\) an additional logarithm is needed to recover the uniform law.
