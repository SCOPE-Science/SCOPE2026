For the Minkowski sum of two centered circular disks in distinct planes, Euclidean surface area strictly increases with their acute dihedral angle, even though mean width is constant. As the planes coalesce, the area excess has the sharp nonanalytic law \(\pi RS\,\theta^2(\log(8/\theta)-1)+O(\theta^4\log(1/\theta))\), with an exact elliptic-integral formula at every angle.
