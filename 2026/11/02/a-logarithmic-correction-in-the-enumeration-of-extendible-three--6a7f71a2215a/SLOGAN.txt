Among binary prefixes with exactly three ones, the proportion extendible to an Abelian pattern Sturmian word (with either letter orientation, for all sufficiently large lengths) is \(\frac12-\frac9{\pi^2}\frac{\log N}{N}+O(N^{-1})\) at length \(N+1\). The logarithmic correction comes from a gcd-weighted family of cycles in a two-distance incidence graph; the underlying forest threshold was previously known.
