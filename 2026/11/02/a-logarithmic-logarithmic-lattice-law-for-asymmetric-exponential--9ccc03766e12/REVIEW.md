# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** An exact Fourier fiber diagonalization establishes the sharp scalar Gabor frame symbol, and the two-sided exponential correlation gives the first time-lag correction. The uniform small-cell Bernoulli expansion, autocorrelation tail, and a global localization argument yield the three optimizer constants and quantitative penalty function. Independent real-space kernel sums and finite spectral approximations were run from the standalone checker; they are supplemental, not a proof of the limiting theorem.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** Faulhuber–Shafkulovska's *Gabor frame bound optimizations* (arXiv:2204.02917, 2022-04-06) derives sharp frame bounds and optimization for the symmetric two-sided exponential. The primary work of Gröchenig–Stöckler (arXiv:1104.4894) and Gröchenig (arXiv:2301.00857) establishes the total-positivity frame set, not the optimized unequal-tail bounds. The nearest related published semantic record is shifted integer-translation sampling, not a modulated Gabor lattice. General implications from other uninspected results remain a priority risk; database non-coverage is not claimed to be a uniqueness proof.

**Value — PASS.** The explicit \(-\log\log n\) correction and three separately optimal shifts identify an unexpected change of lattice geometry when the exponential tails are unequal. The strict convex exponential penalty is a structural sensitivity law for high-redundancy time-frequency frame design, rather than a small numerical improvement.

**Limits.** Fixed positive contrast only; no finite-redundancy uniqueness, uniform equal-rate limit, nonrectangular optimum or external independent validation. All cited publication times are first-public dates where used for scope.
