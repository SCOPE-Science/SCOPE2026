For asynchronous rumor spreading on an unequal double star, the larger hub is always the stochastically faster hub source, and the complete finite-time law has an independent-exponential representation. When the smaller star has \(b\sim\rho\log a\) leaves, broadcast from the larger hub has the mixed limit \(\max\{1,\rho Z\}\) on the \(\log a\) scale, with an atom marking which side finishes last, whereas broadcast from the smaller hub has limit \(1+\rho Z\), for \(Z\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1)\).
