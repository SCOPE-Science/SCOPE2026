An expanding-color reinforced tree with per-step weight increment \(ct^\alpha\) has a sharp activation transition: the number of vertices ever chosen as parents grows as a power for \(0<\alpha<1\), logarithmically for \(\alpha=1\), and remains finite almost surely for \(\alpha>1\). In the finite regime, the final time that a new parent is activated has an exact power-law tail, while the divergent regimes have Gaussian count fluctuations.
