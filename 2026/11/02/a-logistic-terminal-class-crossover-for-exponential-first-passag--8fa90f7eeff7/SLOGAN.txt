In rate-one exponential first-passage infection on \(K_{n+\lfloor\lambda n/\log n\rfloor,n}\), the last infected vertex lies in the first part with limiting probability \(e^\lambda/(1+e^\lambda)\), revealing an \(n/\log n\) two-class crossover. At exact balance \(K_{n,n}\), the two final-class probabilities are exactly \(1/2\) for every \(n\ge2\).
