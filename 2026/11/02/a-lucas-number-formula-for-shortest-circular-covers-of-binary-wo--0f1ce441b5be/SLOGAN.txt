Every finite binary word of length \(n\geq3\) whose constant runs have length at most two can be covered by one circular binary string of exactly \(2(F_{n+1}+F_{n-1}-1)\) symbols, and no shorter circular string exists even if forbidden extra windows are allowed. A sharp potential inequality and an explicit balanced Eulerian augmentation show that precisely \(2F_{n-1}-2\) additional windows are necessary and sufficient.
