For cycle powers \(C_n^k\) with \(k,m\ge2\) and \(n=(k+1)m+2\), filling every triangle and four-cycle of the maximum-independent-set token-sliding graph gives a compact surface. It is a Möbius band for even \(k\) and an annulus for odd \(k\); the graph is 4-regular and has exactly \(n\) triangles and \(n(m-1)/2\) squares.
