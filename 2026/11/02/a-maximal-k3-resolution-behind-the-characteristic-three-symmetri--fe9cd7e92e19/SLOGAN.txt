The six-coordinate symmetric Burkhardt quartic in characteristic three is a normal cone with fifteen concurrent singular lines, whose quartic base has fifteen rational ordinary nodes. Resolving those nodes gives a K3 surface maximal over \(\mathbb F_9\), with Frobenius eigenvalues \(+3\) twenty-one times and \(-3\) once, and determines the singular threefold's zeta function over every \(\mathbb F_{3^m}\).
