For a uniform Dyck excursion, expected peak count conditioned on midpoint altitude is strictly concave across feasible altitudes, and its initial direction reverses sharply at even semilength \(n=28\) and odd semilength \(n=65\) (with a tie at \(n=63\)). The altitude maximizing the expected peak count is asymptotic to \((n/2)^{1/3}\), even though the excursion's unconditioned midpoint height is typically on a square-root scale.
