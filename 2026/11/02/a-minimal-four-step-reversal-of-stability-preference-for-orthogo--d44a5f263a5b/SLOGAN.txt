For two orthogonally similar, individually stable planar shears, the grouped and alternating four-step schedules have different exact Floquet stability thresholds, and which schedule is safer reverses at the unique damping value \(a^2=1+\sqrt3-\sqrt{3+2\sqrt3}\). Four is the shortest balanced schedule length at which rearrangement can change the multipliers, despite identical one-step eigenvalue and singular-value statistics.
