For the discrete hyperbolic staircase, every pair of distinct jump indicators is negatively correlated and the total jump count has a Poisson-binomial law, yet the first three jump indicators are strongly Rayleigh exactly for \(0<p\leq2\sqrt6-4\) or at the independent-record endpoint \(p=1\). The sharp three-time boundary gives a minimal example where benign count statistics coexist with failure of stronger multivariate negative dependence.
