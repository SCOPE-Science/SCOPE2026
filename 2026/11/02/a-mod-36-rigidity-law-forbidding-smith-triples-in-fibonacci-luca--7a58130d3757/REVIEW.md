The new arithmetic statement is directly motivated by a recent paper's only known common Smith index and its doubling-based method. It closes a natural triple-Smith possibility for every index, instead of increasing a computational search range or requiring factorization of \(F_{2n}\). The exact \(2\)-adic/\(3\)-adic valuation classification explains the obstruction structurally, separates its congruence classes, and provides infinitely many further base-dependent exclusions. Its value is as a sharp natural boundary for the recent search, not as a deep new analytic theorem.
