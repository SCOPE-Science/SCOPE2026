For analytic monomial slant Toeplitz commutators of any fixed order \(s\ge2\) on weighted Bergman space, the exact essential norm has two arithmetic values, switching precisely when the nonzero degree difference is divisible by \(s^2\). At the same threshold, the positive product's essential spectrum changes from a singleton to an interval; the theorem covers both monomial and orthonormal-basis slant normalizations.
