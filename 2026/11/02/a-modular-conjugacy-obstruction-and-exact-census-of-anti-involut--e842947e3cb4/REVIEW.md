# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every anti-automorphism uniquely has linear part \(L\) and quadratic correction \(h\). Its square is the identity precisely when \(L^2=I\) and \(hL+(L\otimes L)\tau h=0\). Odd-characteristic eigenspace decomposition and the characteristic-two representation of a two-cycle give the claimed parameter dimensions. An exact centralizer order counts linear parts. Conjugation by identity-linear algebra automorphisms determines the distinct modular classes. Direct matrix checks corroborate the proof in small cases, but cannot replace the arbitrary-rank argument.

**Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority.** The Andrews (arXiv:1311.1730) and André–Freitas–Neto (arXiv:1502.01512) primary preprints were inspected in their main construction and definitions. They start with a fixed involution and investigate associated algebra-group supercharacters, rather than the enumerator and nonlinear IA-class obstruction. Lewis's broader 2006 survey is a plausible source of a more general result; only abstract and contents could be read. No unsupported claim of an exhaustive publication search is made. See AUDIT.json for exact comparison categories and residual risks.

**Value — PASS.** This is a complete family-level classification for a natural universal cube-zero algebra: it counts anti-involutions across all ranks and prime powers and measures an actual failure of equivariant linearization in characteristic two. The identity-linear orbit invariant is meaningful independently of a full automorphism-group classification.

The result excludes higher nilpotence classes, arbitrary quotient algebras, and full automorphism conjugacy. Independent scrutiny of priority, especially the inaccessible survey, is still required.
