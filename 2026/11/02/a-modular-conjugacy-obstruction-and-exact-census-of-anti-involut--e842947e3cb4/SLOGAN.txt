For the free cube-zero associative algebra \(V\oplus(V\otimes V)\), every finite-field anti-involution is counted explicitly by the Jordan type of its linear part. In characteristic two, fixing that linear part leaves exactly \(q^{rn}\) identity-linear conjugacy classes, where \(r\) is the number of singleton Jordan blocks; this gives a sharp and quantifiable failure of simultaneous linearization that disappears in odd characteristic.
