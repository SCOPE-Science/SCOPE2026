For the supremum-norm Banach space spanned by edge Walsh characters of a connected unicyclic graph, the exact minimum projection constant depends only on the edge count and the unique cycle length. Odd cycles leave the independent-sign constant unchanged, cycles of length divisible by four lower it, and even cycles of length congruent to two modulo four raise it; the correction has an exact binomial formula and decays as \(m^{1/2-\ell/2}\) when the cycle is fixed and trees are attached.
