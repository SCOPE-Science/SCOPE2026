On \(K_{a,b}\), repeating a fixed single-visit sequential parity scan yields only periods \(1,2,4\) for odd \(a+b\), whereas even \(a+b\) has periods \(1,2\) or \(1,3\) according to one residue modulo three of the update order. All orbit populations and the exact number of labelled schedules of each kind have closed formulas.
