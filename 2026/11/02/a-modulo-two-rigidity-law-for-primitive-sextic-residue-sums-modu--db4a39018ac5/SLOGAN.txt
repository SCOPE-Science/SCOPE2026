For the primitive sextic cyclic subgroup modulo \(7q\) defined by coupling the modulo-seven sign to the Legendre symbol modulo \(q\), the centered residue-sum defect is always divisible by \(7q\). Its quotient is odd precisely when \(q\equiv9,13,25\pmod{28}\), excluding centered sums in half of the six possible prime congruence classes.
