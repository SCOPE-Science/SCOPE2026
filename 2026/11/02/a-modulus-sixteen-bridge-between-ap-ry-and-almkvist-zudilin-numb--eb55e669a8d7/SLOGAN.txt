For the classical Apéry numbers \(A(n)\) and the signed Almkvist–Zudilin numbers \(B(n)\), the exact congruence \(B(n)-A(n)\equiv8n\pmod{16}\) holds at every nonnegative index. Hence \(B(n)\equiv1+4\beta(n)+8n\pmod{16}\), where \(\beta(n)\) counts binary runs, proving its residues modulo sixteen are not eventually periodic despite their known periodicity modulo eight.
