For degree-\(N\) polynomial evaluation on a symmetric \(q\)-ray star with uniform arclength, the squared junction norm is exactly a gamma quotient growing like \(N^{2/q}\). On the geometric scale \(N^{-2/q}\), the entire reproducing kernel converges locally uniformly to an explicit positive-definite sum of \(q\) distinct Bessel-type sectors, rather than just a scalar endpoint law.
