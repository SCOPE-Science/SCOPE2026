In a static, independent, uniformly elliptic Pascal-sign environment, the entire predictable cross-bracket of the associated two-dimensional bootstrap walk is almost surely at most \(\sqrt N\exp\{C_\varepsilon(\log\log(N+e^e))^2\}\) through time \(N\), under almost every quenched law. This gives an essentially square-root quantitative rate for arbitrary heterogeneous Bernoulli parameters; the exponent \(1/2\) cannot be uniformly improved because the fair environment has nondegenerate Brownian-scale fluctuations.
