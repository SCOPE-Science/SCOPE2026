# Review of the rank-one Grassmann Rota–Baxter classification

## Correctness
PASS. The rank-one identity is reduced, over arbitrary characteristic, to the annihilation relation \(\phi(Au)=0\). The exterior multiplication forces one bihomogeneous incidence equation. The gradient and local nondegenerate quadratic chart establish the unique singularity. Direct automorphism formulas give six exhaustive, pairwise distinguished orbits and their closure order; counting annihilators supplies all stated finite-field formulas. The standalone verifier exhausts small fields and tests the automorphism orbit partition independently of the symbolic argument.

## Originality
PASS, with historical-priority qualifications. Benito–Gubarev–Pozhidaev (2018) already give a coarse normal form for every weight-zero operator on the Grassmann plane over fields of characteristic different from two. Shakoor–Noor-ul-Ain (2026) classify all real matrices; a published \(\mathbb F_2\) census already gives the rank-one count \(25\). The assessed claim is the reduced rank-one incidence threefold and singularity together with six **exact** conjugacy strata, closure relations and uniform finite-field orbit formulas, not first existence of these maps. Older moduli literature may still have an equivalent formulation.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The singular geometry and orbit closures give a complete structural description of a natural rank stratum in a previously classified operator family. They distinguish degeneration behavior and provide exact arithmetic counts and symmetry types across every finite field. The scope excludes higher-rank operators, nonzero weight and nonreduced ambient scheme structure.
