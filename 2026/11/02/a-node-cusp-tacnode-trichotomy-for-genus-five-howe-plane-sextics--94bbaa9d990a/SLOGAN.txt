Every genus-five Howe sextic obtained from two genus-two covers with four common branch points has exactly one of three analytic singularity baskets: \(5A_1\), \(A_1+2A_3\), or \(A_1+4A_2\). The trichotomy is controlled by the unique node or cusp of a rational quotient cubic, and the four-cusp case occurs for an explicit admissible characteristic-zero example.
