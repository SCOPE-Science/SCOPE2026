In characteristic two their defining equations are exactly the square-zero constant-coordinate equations; invertibility is an open cotangent determinant condition after reduction modulo a nilpotent ideal. In other characteristics, quadratic coefficient comparisons force a monomial linear matrix and annihilator decomposition forces each image to be a coordinate variable times a uniquely normalized unit. Group-scheme dimensions and tangent dimensions follow from explicit coordinates and derivation equations. The packaged checker enumerates every admissible generator image in six small finite-field configurations and computes tangent ranks independently. The checker does not establish the universal proof.

The published coordinate-cross-algebra finding concerns a ring with vanishing cross products. The 2012 source concerns algebraic K-theory, not automorphism schemes. The finite-field point-count part could follow from a classical modular group-algebra formulation not found in these searches; this remains a genuine priority risk.

The theorem explains a sharply characteristic-dependent failure of smoothness in every embedding dimension, identifies the missing infinitesimal translations, and supplies exact group sizes and component dimensions. The unbounded dimension gap between the reduced automorphism groups across characteristics and the excess tangent dimension are structural, not an isolated numerical recomputation.
