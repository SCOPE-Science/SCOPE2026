For \(A_d=k[x_1,\ldots,x_d]/(x_i^2)\), characteristic two changes the full ungraded automorphism scheme from a smooth monomial-linear group to a **non-smooth** scheme with reduced group connected and cotangent image \(\operatorname{GL}_d\); the excess infinitesimal dimension is exactly \(d\). A complete coordinate description gives the automorphism counts over every finite field, including \(|\operatorname{GL}_d(\mathbb F_q)|q^{d(2^d-d-1)}\) in even characteristic.
