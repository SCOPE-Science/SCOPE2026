# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Invertibility of the evolution structure matrix makes squares of all six natural basis elements linearly independent. Thus from f(e_i)f(e_j)=0 for i!=j every row of the invertible change-of-basis matrix supports at most one nonzero coefficient; all automorphisms are monomial. The only permissible permutations preserve the directed 6-cycle and are rotations r mod 6, with scalars obeying t_{i+1}=t_i² w_{i+r}/w_i (w_0=zeta, others 1). For r=0 the consistency t_0^{63}=1 yields gcd(63,q-1)=21 solutions. r=2 has the same count, while r=1 needs t^{63}=zeta, impossible with exact 3-adic valuation v3(q-1)=1. Thus rotations 0,2,4 each have 21 lifts, group order 63 and kernel C21. For any r=2 lift, its cube acts on e0 by zeta^{-1} and has order 3, so the lift has order 9 and quotient C3 cannot split; other nontrivial lifts likewise have order 9. A normal C7 kernel and cyclic C9 lift generate C7 semidirect C9, with nontrivial order-three action. Independently enumerated all F43 monomial candidates, checked all 63×63 compositions and the full element-order histogram {1:1,3:2,7:6,9:42,21:12}. The infinite field-family theorem rests on the recurrence, not finite extrapolation.
