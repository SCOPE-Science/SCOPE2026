# Review of a non-split order-63 symmetry on a weighted evolution cycle

## Correctness — PASS
The claimed domain is every prime power \(q\) with \(21\mid(q-1)\) and \(9\nmid(q-1)\), and every primitive cube root \(\zeta\in\mathbb F_q\). The coefficient matrix is monomial and invertible. If \(f(e_i)=\sum_j a_{ji}e_j\), the equations \(f(e_i)f(e_k)=0\) for distinct basis elements force disjoint supports in every row because the six target squares are linearly independent. Invertibility then forces every automorphism to be monomial; multiplication forces its permutation to be a six-cycle rotation. The exact scalar recurrence \(t_{i+1}=t_i^2w_{i+r}/w_i\) provides complete counting without omitted linear maps.

For rotation zero, \(t^{63}=1\) has \(21\) solutions. Rotation two has the same consistency equation, while rotation one would demand \(t^{63}=\zeta\), impossible because the field's multiplicative group has three-adic valuation one. The image subgroup is exactly the even rotations and the group order is \(63\). Every rotation-two lift cubes on \(e_0\) to \(\zeta^{-1}e_0\), excluding a section. Its order is nine because the cube lies in a diagonal group of order \(21\) and has order three. The normal order-seven subgroup and any order-nine rotation lift generate all \(63\) elements, with a faithful order-three conjugation action on \(C_7\); this proves the nonabelian semidirect-product type and order distribution. These proof steps were reconstructed independently of the executable checks. The verifier exhaustively checks the monomial possibilities in prime fields of orders \(43,211,337\), including group closure and the forced cube. Its finite checks are corroboration, not a proof for all fields.

## Originality — PASS within inspected literature; priority provisional
The monomiality and diagonal-by-permutation exact sequence are known and expressly credited. Wei–Zou (2023, arXiv:2310.12368) proves the general monomial-orbit classification and enumerates *all* perfect evolution algebras in dimensions up to four; it neither specifies nor implies the field-congruence construction of a six-cycle with a nonabelian order-\(63\) group and a non-split order-\(21\)-by-order-\(3\) extension. The original 2023 paper's introduction, main orbit reduction and search-relevant sections were checked, not just its abstract. Costoya–Muñoz–Tocino–Viruel (2023) and Costoya–Mayorga–Viruel (preprint 2024, published 2025) prove far broader *group and permutation realization* results, but those existential statements do not determine the automorphism group and splitting obstruction of this specific weighted cycle. Kaygorodov–Martín González–Viruel (2026), Proposition 21, already constructs a six-dimensional non-split extension, \(C_3\hookrightarrow C_9\twoheadrightarrow C_3\), with a different graph; the present result has a **different nonabelian group**, a cyclic support graph and an infinite arithmetic field family. Their Question 33 identifies exact splitting behavior as a meaningful open classification problem. The accessible previous claims about unweighted cycle evolution algebras and a binary five-dimensional census did not contain this weighted-cycle extension. Targeted semantic searches of published records for weighted six-cycles, \(C_{21}\)-by-\(C_3\) extensions, and \(C_7\rtimes C_9\) found no matching statement.

The closest remaining risk is an unlocated full treatment of automorphism extensions for *weighted* cycles. Sriwongsa–Zou (2022) was accessible in abstract but not inspected in full; its stated results concern realization, symmetric groups and maximal diagonal kernels. Accordingly, the novelty assessment is not an independent priority certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The example contributes a nonabelian, non-split extension to a natural and particularly restrictive class—perfect evolution algebras supported on one directed cycle—rather than a generic group-realization construction. Its diagonal order, three allowed support rotations, exact group isomorphism type, complete element-order spectrum and uniform arithmetic field condition form a natural complete automorphism classification for the displayed family. The non-splitting mechanism is structural: a cube of *every* lift of rotation by two is the same fixed nonidentity cube root of unity. The same support graph with all weights one permits every rotation, isolating why the coefficient twist matters. It is an explicit case relevant to the splitting question in the 2026 survey, but no broader classification of arbitrary weights is claimed.
