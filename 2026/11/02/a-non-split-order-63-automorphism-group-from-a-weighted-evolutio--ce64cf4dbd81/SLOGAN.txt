A single weighted directed six-cycle yields a perfect evolution algebra whose full rational automorphism group is the nonabelian order-63 group \(C_7\rtimes C_9\) over every finite field with \(21\mid(q-1)\) and \(9\nmid(q-1)\). Its natural diagonal-by-rotation extension \(1\to C_{21}\to\operatorname{Aut}(E)\to C_3\to1\) does not split, giving an explicit nonabelian counterpart to known cyclic non-split examples.
