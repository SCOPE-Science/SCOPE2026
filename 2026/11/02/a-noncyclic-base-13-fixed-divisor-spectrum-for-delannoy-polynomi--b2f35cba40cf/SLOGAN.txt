For Delannoy polynomials, the indices giving a universal congruence \(D_n(z)\equiv1\pmod p\) are exactly the base-\(p\) digit words whose nonvanishing digit-value vectors multiply to the identity. The first noncyclic digit group occurs at \(p=13\), where it is \((\mathbb Z/12\mathbb Z)^2\) and the exact spectrum requires two independent digit-count congruences; its count below \(13^k\) is asymptotic to \(5^k/144\).
