For threshold-two three-state Greenberg–Hastings dynamics on every complete tripartite graph, the only primitive periods are \(1,3,4,12\) and the maximum transient is \(11\). Period twelve exists exactly when the smallest shore has at most three vertices and the middle shore has at least four, so increasing the smallest shore beyond three destroys twelve-cycles; an exact \(17{,}576\)-signature reduction also yields every labelled transient/period population.
