The characteristic-three Auer–Top quartic at \(\lambda=t^2\) has a stable fiber of two Frobenius-conjugate conics joined at four rational nodes, each with thickness two. Its generalized Jacobian has nonsplit torus \((R^1_{\mathbb F_9/\mathbb F_3}\mathbb G_m)^3\) and \(64\) rational points, while the geometric Néron component group is \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\oplus\mathbb Z/8\).
