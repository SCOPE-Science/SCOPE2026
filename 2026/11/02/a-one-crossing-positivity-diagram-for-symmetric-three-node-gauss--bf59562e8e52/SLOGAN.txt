A three-node Gaussian-kernel quadrature rule on \([-1,1]\) has positive weights at every shape if and only if its symmetric outer nodes lie at \(\pm a\) with \(a\ge1/\sqrt3\). For smaller \(a\), its central weight changes sign exactly once as the Gaussian shape increases, providing a sharp boundary between positive numerical integration and a nonnegative integrand with negative quadrature output.
