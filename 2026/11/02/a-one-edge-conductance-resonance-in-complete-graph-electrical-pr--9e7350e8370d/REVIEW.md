# Review of the sharp single-edge electrical projection result

## Correctness — PASS
The weighted Laplacian is the rank-one update \(nQ+(c-1)b_0b_0^{\mathsf T}\). Its inverse on the mean-zero space and the resulting transfer entries are derived algebraically. Three exhaustive edge orbits give the complete row-sum formula, checked independently by 1,071 exact grounded-inversion comparisons. Differentiating the exceptional row yields \(t_*=(1+\sqrt{2n-3})/2\); direct difference factorizations and three elementary low-dimensional estimates bound the other edge rows globally. Endpoint cases \(n=3\), \(c=1\), and \(c\to0,\infty\) are covered. Finite computations are not mistaken for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Queries for the exceptional-edge complete-graph transfer-current row norm, alternative projection terminology, Sherman--Morrison edge updates and square-root amplification were compared against published mathematical finding summaries and accessible historical claim titles and statements. The closest primary full text, Kelner--Maymounkov (arXiv:0909.2859v1, PDF pp. 2–3 and Appendix A p. 14), proves operator bounds and a parallel-path lower-bound example but not the exact rank-one weighted clique formula. Existing unweighted multipartite projection formulas do not imply a one-edge conductance deformation; complete-graph products with direction-constant conductance cover only \(c=1\). The remaining risk is an equivalent calculation in older circuit and transfer-current literature, so search noncoverage is not equated with definitive priority proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A dense, maximally symmetric reference network acquires increasing endpoint sensitivity from a single altered conductance. The exact optimized constant, optimizing conductance, all-parameter row formulas, and sharp \(\sqrt n\) scale make this a natural quantitative stability benchmark, rather than an arbitrary small finite example or a slight numerical improvement.

## Scope and limitations
Real finite complete graphs, one exceptional positive edge conductance, and conductance-rescaled edge \(\ell^\infty\) norm. No conclusion for general networks or physical-current coordinates. Independent scientific audit has not been performed.
