In a stationary wet/dry Markov weather model with exactly half the days wet, the chance of seeing two wet days in a row is maximized by a nontrivial persistence: it is \(q=3/4\) over four days, while the optimal persistence converges to \(q=3/5\), or lag-one correlation \(1/5\), over long windows. The optimal finite-window persistence has the sharp correction \(4/(15n)+O(n^{-2})\), despite the daily wet frequency never changing.
