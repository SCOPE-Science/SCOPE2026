For synchronous integer diffusion on a labelled star, every primitive two-cycle is encoded by \(r\) independent gaps in \(\{1,\ldots,r\}\), and its exact orbit count at a fixed total is almost equidistributed modulo \(r+1\), with a one-orbit discrepancy in exactly one residue. A rational generating function also counts precisely the cycles whose every state has nonnegative chips.
