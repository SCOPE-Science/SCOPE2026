A single vertex with self-feedback transforms binary neighbor-XOR dynamics on the \(d\)-cube: for even \(d\), only two fixed configurations survive and transients have a sharp three-step shell law, while for odd \(d\), every configuration is periodic and primitive periods \(1,2,3,6\) all occur. The complete nilpotent Jordan forms and orbit counts hold for every \(d\ge2\).
