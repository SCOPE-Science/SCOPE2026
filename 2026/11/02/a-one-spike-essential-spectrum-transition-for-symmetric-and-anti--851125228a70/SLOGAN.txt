For a unilateral shift whose only exceptional weight is \(w_0=a>0\), the symmetric and antisymmetric products with its adjoint have the same Fredholm essential spectrum: the interval \([-1,1]\) until \(a=\sqrt2\), then that interval plus exactly two detached points \(\pm a^2/(2\sqrt{a^2-1})\). A single rank-one perturbation of the underlying shift can therefore create isolated essential spectral points after tensor symmetrization.
