The Grundy total domination number of every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) equals \(m+n-2\), except that it increases by one precisely when the factor orders differ by one. In that exceptional regime all longest ordered sequences arise from a saturated bipartite matching followed by successive attachments, and there are exactly \((a+1)!(a!)^2(2a-1)!!\) of them, where \(a=\min\{m,n\}\).
