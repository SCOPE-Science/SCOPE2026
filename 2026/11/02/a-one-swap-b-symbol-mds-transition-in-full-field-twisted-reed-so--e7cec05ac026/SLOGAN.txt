For full-prime-field one-twist Reed–Solomon codes with highest hook, a cyclic order is \(b\)-symbol MDS for every \(b\ge2\) exactly when no consecutive \(k\) evaluation points sum to \(-\eta^{-1}\). Every arithmetic ordering misses the Singleton bound by one for \(2\le b\le k\), but one explicitly located adjacent swap attains all the bounds simultaneously for \(2\le k\le p-2\); the dimension-\(p-1\) boundary cannot be repaired by any permutation.
