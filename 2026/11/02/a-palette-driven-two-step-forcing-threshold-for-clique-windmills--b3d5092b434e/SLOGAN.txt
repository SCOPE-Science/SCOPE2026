For clique windmills \(K_1\vee(mK_r)\), adding even one colour beyond the chromatic palette makes every petal vertex permanently unforceable, and the entire forced-colouring polynomial collapses to two coefficients given by a sharp surjective-injection count. The minimum seed size is \(rm\) for \(r+2\le Q\le rm+1\), but jumps to \(rm+1\) for \(Q\ge rm+2\), with an exact coupon-coverage limit at growing palette sizes.
