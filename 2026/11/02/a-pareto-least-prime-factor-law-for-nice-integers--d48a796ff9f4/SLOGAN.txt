Among integers whose divisor-moduli admit the nonconflicting residue assignments characterized by Adenwalla and Jia–Li, the counting function is asymptotic to \(e^{-\gamma}X/\log\log\log X\). Conditional on this property, the scaled logarithm of the least prime factor converges to the Pareto law with tail \(\Pr(Y>t)=1/\max\{1,t\}\) for \(t>0\).
