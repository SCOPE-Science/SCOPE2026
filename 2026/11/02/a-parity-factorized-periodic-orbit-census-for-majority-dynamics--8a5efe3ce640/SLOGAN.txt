Synchronous majority dynamics on a cycle with one private leaf at each vertex has an exact recurrent-orbit census: its two-step-return states correspond to a single length-\(2n\) no-singleton-run word for odd \(n\), and to two independent length-\(n\) words for even \(n\). The resulting Lucas–trigonometric formulas show that the full two-step-return population grows like \(\phi^{2n}\), while fixed points grow like \(\phi^n\).
