For the cyclic radical-square-zero Nakayama algebra, finite-field rank strata of uniformly dimensioned labeled modules have an exact Gaussian-product count. As the field grows, generic modules are a uniform mixture of \(d+1\) projective types for even cycle length, a unique projective type for odd cycle length and even \(d\), or a uniform mixture of \(n\) types having exactly one simple of infinite projective dimension when both parameters are odd.
