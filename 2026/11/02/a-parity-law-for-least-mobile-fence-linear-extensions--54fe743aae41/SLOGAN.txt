Among the linear extensions of the \(n\)-vertex zigzag fence, the least possible number of legal adjacent swaps is \(\lceil n/2\rceil-1\). At even length exactly one extension has that degree; at odd length \(n=2m+1\), exactly \(2^m\) do, and their induced adjacent-swap graph is a perfect matching \(2^{m-1}K_2\).
