For the shortest-path metric of a wheel with \(n\ge4\) rim vertices, the attained \(\ell_2\)-normalized negative-type gap is exactly \(2c_n-(2c_n-1)2^p\), where \(c_n=1\) for even \(n\) and \(c_n=\cos(\pi/n)\) for odd \(n\). Thus even rims have maximal negative type \(1\), while odd rims have a strictly larger sharp exponent and lose two rather than one critical Euclidean Gram dimensions.
