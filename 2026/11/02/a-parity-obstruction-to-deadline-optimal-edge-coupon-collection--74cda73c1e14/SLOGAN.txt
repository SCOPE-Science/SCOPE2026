For static random edge sampling, a bipartite graph with no isolated vertices and a matching saturating its smaller side has an exact all-deadline optimum: sample uniformly from an edge cover with one edge per vertex in the larger side, giving the ordinary uniform coupon-collector law. In contrast, every odd cycle of length at least seven has no edge-sampling law that is optimal at every deadline: a sparse cover wins at the earliest deadline, while uniform sampling beats every nonuniform law eventually.
