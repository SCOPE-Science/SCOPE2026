For a normal strongly monotone linear extragradient iteration, the unique minimum-noise coupling of two isotropic Gaussian oracle marginals rotates the second noise by the polar factor of the operator; all finite-horizon and stationary error minima have exact spectral formulas. Perfect cancellation with strict deterministic convergence is possible in even dimensions but impossible in odd dimensions, and the improvement disappears entirely when adaptive second-call conditional unbiasedness is required.
