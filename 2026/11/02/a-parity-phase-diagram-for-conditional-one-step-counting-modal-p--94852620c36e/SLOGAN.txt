For fixed depth-one counting-modal formulas that condition on at least half of the leaves satisfying a Boolean property, the conditional proportion has a complete phase diagram: a strict two-cycle for odd excess thresholds when the property's leaf probability is below one half, and convergence otherwise. The conditioned excess obeys a geometric law; for two fair propositional letters the even and odd limits can be \(1/9\) and \(1/3\).
