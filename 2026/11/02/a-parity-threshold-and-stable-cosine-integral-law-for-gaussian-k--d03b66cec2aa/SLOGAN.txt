The graph metric on a cycle with one leaf at every vertex has supremal negative type exactly \(1\) in even orders, but strictly greater than \(1\) in every odd order. Despite the added vertices, the odd-order excess has the same sharp cosine-integral leading coefficient as the undecorated cycle: \(n^2(\wp_n-1)\to2\pi^2/\operatorname{Cin}(2\pi)\).
