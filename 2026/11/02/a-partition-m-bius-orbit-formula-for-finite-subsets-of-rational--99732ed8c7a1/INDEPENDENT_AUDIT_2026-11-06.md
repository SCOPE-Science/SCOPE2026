# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Lattice automorphisms of Q^r preserve factor chains up to permutation, so configurations of n distinctly labelled points correspond exactly to r total preorders of [n] whose kernel intersection is discrete. S_n relabeling and S_r coordinate permutation act on these tuples. Partition-lattice Möbius inversion removes point collisions, then fixedness under (g,pi) reduces each coordinate cycle of length ell to total preorders on b(g,P,ell) equivalence classes generated by g^j(P) and g^ell-orbits. Burnside yields 1/(n!r!) sum_(g,pi,P) mu(P) product_c F_b, valid all n>=1,r>=3 with ties allowed. An independent orbit enumeration of *all* admissible tuples and orbits produced a_(3,2)=5, a_(3,3)=80 and a_(4,3)=320 (notation a_(r,n)); small checks corroborate the all-n proof.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge new all-arity coordinate-symmetric orbit profile for r>=3 rational chain factors. The r=2 transpose-incidence-matrix problem has known orbit counts 1,3,15,108,1045 and is acknowledged, not claimed new. Higher-dimensional coordinate-injective pattern theorems omit ties, while possible prior multidimensional incidence-array Burnside formula remains a priority risk.

## value

PASS

The orbit formula is a complete exact natural oligomorphic invariant for finite subsets of products of dense chains, treating coordinate ties and point collisions uniformly; future lattice/model-theory work could use the exact orbit numbers even without asymptotics.
