A single missing parity walk proves a sharp, all-order positivity limit for Taylor diffusion: on every weighted regular bipartite graph with diameter at least one below the polynomial order, the exact safe timestep interval is \([0,1/d]\), where \(d\) is the common weighted degree. The result includes anisotropic hypercubes and classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta, and gives an explicitly negative matrix entry at every larger timestep.
