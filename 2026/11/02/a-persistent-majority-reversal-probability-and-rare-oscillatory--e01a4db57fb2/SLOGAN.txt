On balanced three-shore networks, synchronous majority dynamics reaches a fixed state or two-cycle in at most two updates, and the exact oscillatory basin occupies only \(\frac{6}{\pi^{3/2}}\arctan(1/\sqrt2)n^{-1/2}+o(n^{-1/2})\) of all binary initial states. Yet under unbiased initial opinions the final unanimous decision opposes the original global majority with the strictly positive limiting probability \(1-3\arctan(\sqrt2)/\pi\approx0.08774\).
