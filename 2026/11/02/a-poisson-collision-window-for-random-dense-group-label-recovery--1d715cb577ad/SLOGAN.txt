When every labeled-coupon draw reveals a uniformly random fixed positive fraction of types, full label recovery occurs in a sharp Poisson pair-signature collision window centered at \(\log\binom n2/[-\log q_n]\), with discrete rather than continuous Gumbel fluctuations. Knowing the label alphabet saves asymptotically no draws, and balanced Type-I random sampling uses twice the minimum number of planned samples on powers of two.
