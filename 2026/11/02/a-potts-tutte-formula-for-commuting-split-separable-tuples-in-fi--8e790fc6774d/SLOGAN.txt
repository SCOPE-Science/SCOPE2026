Over every finite field, each joint-diagonal fiber of an ordered commuting tuple of split-separable elements in a finite incidence algebra is a single unitriangular orbit of size \(q^{b(c)}\), where \(b(c)\) counts differently colored comparable pairs. Summing these fibers gives the Potts–Tutte polynomial of the poset's comparability graph, allowing its full two-variable random-cluster polynomial to be recovered from all arities of commuting-idempotent tuple counts.
