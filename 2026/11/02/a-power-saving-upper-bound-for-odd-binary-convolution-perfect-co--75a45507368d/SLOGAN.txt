Odd composite integers whose every nontrivial binary factorization has one-bit convolution height \(1\) are sparse: their count through \(X\) is \(O\bigl(X^{1-\log_2(4/3)/6}\log(2X)\bigr)\). A matching-versus-sparse-digits dichotomy gives an explicit power saving, answering the quantitative upper-bound question posed for these numbers.
