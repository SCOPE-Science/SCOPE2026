# Verification

The complete derivation is contained in `RESULT.md`. The companion `checker_ordering.py` is standalone Python 3. Its exact-rational calculations reproduce the crossing and the posterior-order reversal with the specified Poisson rates and survey lengths. Independent direct integration of expected labour was compared with the derived pairwise formula in 2,500 randomized informative two-state models with up to three prior blocks; 200 additional checks tested order-invariance of the joint no-alarm probability. The program reports 2,700 passing randomized checks in addition to exact rational assertions.

These finite computations corroborate the analytic proof but cannot certify arbitrary real parameters or historical originality. No independent scientific audit has been performed. The model is not a likelihood-identification result, and it assumes known alarm rates and fixed survey-block durations.
