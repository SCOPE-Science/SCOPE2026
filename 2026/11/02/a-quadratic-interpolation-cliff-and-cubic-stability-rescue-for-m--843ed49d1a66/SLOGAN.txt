For \(d\ge3\), quadratic complex-projective harmonics interpolate arbitrary values on any union of at most \(d\) mutually unbiased bases, but completing the \((d+1)\)-st basis destroys exactly one quadratic sampling direction. Cubic harmonics restore full interpolation on the completed configuration with sharp Gram condition number at most \(9/2\), whereas the quadratic condition number with one basis omitted is exactly \(d+1\).
