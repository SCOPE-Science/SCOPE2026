In a detuned three-dot quantum battery, extracting work into a single thermal load for finite time can be profitable even though leaving that same load attached indefinitely eventually reverses the accumulated electrochemical work. The sharp reversal window scales as \(\Delta^2/(\Gamma\Omega^2)\), with its dimensionless zero given explicitly by \(\log[(1-f)(p_0+p_d)/p_0]/(1-f)\) under the stated positive-extraction condition.
