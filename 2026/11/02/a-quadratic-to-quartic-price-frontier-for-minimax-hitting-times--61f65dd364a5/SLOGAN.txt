For reversible continuous-time paths with fixed stationary masses, the unique budget allocation minimizing the worst directed mean first-hitting time is given by one strictly concave scalar maximization and an exact square-root conductance formula. With one surcharged edge, the worst-case direction switches at an explicit finite price frontier that grows quadratically when the defect is macroscopically off-center but quartically when it is a fixed number of edges from the midpoint.
