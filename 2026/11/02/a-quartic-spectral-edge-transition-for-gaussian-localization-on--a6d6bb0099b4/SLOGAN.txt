For Gaussian short-time Fourier localization on two symmetric, frequency-unrestricted time bands, the optimal observation location splits from one peak into two at the exact threshold \(b^2-a^2=\log(b/a)\). At the merger, the continuous spectral edge changes from a square-root superlevel law to a sharp quartic law \(\mathcal N(s)\sim2D^{-1/4}s^{1/4}\), with an explicit splitting and norm-gain coefficient.
