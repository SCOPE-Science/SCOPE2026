On the closed surface of a circular cylinder, the known diameter \(h+2R\) is realized by every pair of opposite cap-antipodes through the sharp height \((\pi^2-4)R/4\), then by only a shrinking radial interval, and by the two cap centers alone once \(h\ge2R\). An exact one-variable focal-ellipse formula classifies all diametral pairs above the first threshold.
