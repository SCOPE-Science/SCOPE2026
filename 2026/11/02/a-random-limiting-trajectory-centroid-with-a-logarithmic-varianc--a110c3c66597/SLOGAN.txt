For a uniform-history preferential-relocation walk with exploration probability \(0<p<1\), the empirical centroid of all visited positions converges almost surely and in \(L^2\) to a nondegenerate random center with exact variance \(\sigma^2p(2+p)/(2(1-p))\), even though the endpoint spreads logarithmically. Its finite-time variance approaches that limit with an explicit negative \((\log n)/n\) correction of coefficient \(\sigma^2p(1+p)/(1-p)^2\).
