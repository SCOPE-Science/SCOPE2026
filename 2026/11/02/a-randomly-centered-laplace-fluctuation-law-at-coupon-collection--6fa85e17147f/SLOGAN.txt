For a uniform coupon collector at full coverage, subtracting the completion-time-dependent center \(n(\log n)e^{-T_n/n}\) from the number of singly seen coupon types and dividing by \(\sqrt{\log n}\) yields a centered Laplace, rather than Gaussian, limit. Jointly with the usual Gumbel-normalized completion time, its fluctuation is Gaussian with a random exponential variance, giving a two-scale stopped-occupancy limit.
