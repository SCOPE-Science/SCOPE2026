# Scientific review: rank-jumping genus-four bielliptic net in characteristic three

## Correctness — PASS

The elliptic base has a nonzero cubic derivative and supersingular Hasse invariant. For the double cover, the fixed simple zero at the point at infinity and the quintic resultant give precisely the nonsingular parameter locus. The genus and canonical embedding follow from degree-six simple branching and the complete-intersection adjunction formula. Independently rederived Koszul exponents give the transposed four-by-four Hasse–Witt coefficient matrix with a three-by-three block of determinant \(s\) and a permanently invertible two-by-two block, proving both stable ranks, one-step Cartier ranks, and the reduced rank-jump equation. The exact restriction \(\Delta(0,t)=-(t^2+1)^3\) and local triple branch prove the cusp normal form, intersection multiplicities, and genus-three normalization at both boundary points. The exact verifier passed from its packaged path. Its finite symbolic identities support, but do not replace, the geometric arguments.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

The full texts of Ogasawara–Ohashi–Sakata–Harashita (arXiv:2403.08139, introductory results, nonsingularity and Hasse–Witt sections), Kudo–Harashita (arXiv:1607.01114, Appendix B), and Chang–Dragutinović–Groen–Lin–Pacheco-Tallaj–Singhal (arXiv:2502.09540, main rank-stratification theorems and geometric arguments) were compared for hypotheses and implications. The first treats the general nonsingularity discriminant outside characteristics two and three; the second supplies a general coefficient method, not this calculation; the third proves moduli-stratum dimensions/existence, but its principal explicit family in Proposition 5.4 requires \(p\nmid(g-1)\), which does not hold for this characteristic-three genus-four case, and does not give these equations or boundary. Searches for equivalent bielliptic, Prym, complete-intersection, and fixed supersingular elliptic formulations revealed no covering statement. Absence from searched sources is not a proof of priority; unindexed examples and alternative normal forms remain a genuine risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The family genuinely varies in two moduli dimensions, and the precise codimension-one jump is controlled by a single nonconstant Hasse coefficient, so the example is not an arbitrary isolated finite-field calculation. The complete smoothness discriminant and its precise cuspidal contact with the arithmetic rank-drop divisor give a geometric boundary phenomenon not supplied by general existence theorems. Together, the Newton polygons and both \(a\)-number strata form an exact structural classification within a natural complete-intersection family. No statement is made about all bielliptic genus-four curves or all discriminant singularities.
