A two-dimensional characteristic-three net of genus-four curves doubly covering a fixed supersingular elliptic curve has an exact reduced rank-drop divisor \(s=0\): its Jacobians have geometric \(3\)-rank \(3\) off the divisor and \(2\) on it. The divisor meets the discriminant in precisely two characteristic-three cuspidal members, each with intersection multiplicity \(3\), and the complete Newton polygons and \(a\)-numbers are explicit.
