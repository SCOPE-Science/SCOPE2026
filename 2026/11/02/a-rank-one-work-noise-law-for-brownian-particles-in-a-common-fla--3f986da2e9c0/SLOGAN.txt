Particles with independent thermal noise but a shared on/off harmonic trap have a universal positive long-time cross-covariance of injected work, \(\Sigma_{ij}=\mu_i\mu_j/q\) for distinct particles, even when their stiffnesses and diffusivities differ. The complete work-noise matrix is diagonal plus rank one, so cross-work measurements identify the common switching rate and an identical-particle gas exhibits quadratic-in-population work fluctuations.
