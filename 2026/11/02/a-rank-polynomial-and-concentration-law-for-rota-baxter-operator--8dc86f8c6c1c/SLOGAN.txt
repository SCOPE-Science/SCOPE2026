For nonzero-weight Rota–Baxter operators on \(K^n\), matrix nullity is exactly the number of white vertices without white children in the classical colored-rooted-tree correspondence. This gives the full rank polynomial and shows that the rank of a uniformly chosen operator, divided by \(n\), converges in \(L^2\) to \(1-\frac12e^{-1/2}\), independently of the field.
