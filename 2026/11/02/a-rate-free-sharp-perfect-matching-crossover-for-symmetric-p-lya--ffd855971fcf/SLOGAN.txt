For symmetric Pólya threshold graphs, the perfect-matching probability has the rate-free sharp asymptotic \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\sqrt{1/n+1/\kappa_n}\) whenever the even order \(n\) and initial-to-reinforcement concentration \(\kappa_n\) both diverge. The critical reinforcement scale \(\kappa_n\asymp n\) connects the classical independent threshold-graph regime to a reinforcement-dominated regime.
