For a uniform overpartition of \(n\), the least missing positive odd part (counting overlined and non-overlined parts alike) has scale \(n^{1/4}\) and an exact Rayleigh-type limit: \(\Pr(M_n/n^{1/4}>y)\to e^{-\pi y^2/16}\). Every fixed positive real moment converges, so its mean is asymptotic to \(2n^{1/4}\) and its variance to \((16/\pi-4)n^{1/2}\).
