In a complete-graph classical XY magnet with disjoint antiferromagnetic bonds, the exact ground-state boundary is the reciprocal-coupling equation \(\sum_i(1+K_i)^{-1}=1\). Crossing it forces a discontinuous collapse of zero-temperature magnetization, while the critical surface admits an explicit continuum of ground states and heterogeneous couplings produce a unique partially canted ordered pattern.
