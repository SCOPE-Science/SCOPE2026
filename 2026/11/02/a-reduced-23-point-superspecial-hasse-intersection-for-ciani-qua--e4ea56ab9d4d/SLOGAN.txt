For smooth normalized Ciani quartics in characteristic \(7\), the three elliptic-quotient Hasse hypersurfaces meet transversely in exactly \(23\) geometric parameter points, giving a reduced superspecial locus of degree \(23\). All points are over \(\mathbb F_{49}\) and form four orbits of sizes \(1,6,4,12\) under the specified coordinate-permutation and even-sign subgroup; these are not claims about unmarked isomorphism classes.
