For every prime \(p\equiv1\pmod3\), the smooth genus-four cyclic trigonal pencil \(y^3=x^6+ax^3+1\) has exactly \((p-1)/3\) reduced geometric nonordinary marked parameters. Its Jacobian \(p\)-rank drops from four to two exactly there, while the elliptic quotient remains ordinary and the entire rank drop occurs in the Prym threefold.
