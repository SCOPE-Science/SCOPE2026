For the symmetric trivial extension of the \(r\)-arrow Kronecker path algebra, every reduced commuting-tuple variety is irreducible at \(r=1\), but has exactly two geometrically irreducible components for every \(r\ge2\) and tuple length \(m\ge2\), in all characteristics. The components are a determinantal rank-one cone and a symplectic-isotropic matrix locus, and their complete finite-field tuple census is a finite Gaussian product sum.
