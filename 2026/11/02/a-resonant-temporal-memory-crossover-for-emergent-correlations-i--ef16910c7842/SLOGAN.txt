In a common two-stiffness harmonic trap, squared-position correlations between distinct noninteracting particles always decay strictly, yet their dominant temporal relaxation switches exactly at the harmonic-mean rate \(r_c=2\mu_+\mu_-/(\mu_++\mu_-)\). At this unique rate, the leading decay has an exact positive \(h e^{-2rh}\) resonance prefactor, invisible to stationary equal-time statistics.
