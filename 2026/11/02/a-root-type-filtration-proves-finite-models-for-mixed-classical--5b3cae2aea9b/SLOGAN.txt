Every formula in classical–intuitionistic mixed necessity semantics has a truth-preserving finite reduction with at most \(2^s\) outer worlds and \(2^{2s}\) total local worlds, where \(s\) counts distinct subformulas. This establishes the finite model property and a \(\mathrm{coNEXPTIME}\) upper bound for theoremhood in \(\mathsf{iK}+\mathsf{bem}\), using its known completeness theorem.
