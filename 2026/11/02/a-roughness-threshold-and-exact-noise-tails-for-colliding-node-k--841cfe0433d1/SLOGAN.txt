For noisy, two-node, uniformly sampled power-exponential kernel quadrature, the kernel exponent \(\alpha=3/2\) is the exact boundary between finite and infinite unregularized mean-squared error. The error has explicit \(t^{-1/(\alpha-1)}\) two-sided tails when \(\alpha>1\), and a diagonal nugget produces finite, logarithmic or power-law expected squared-weight costs on the three sides of this boundary.
