In Kingman coalescent trees, the cherry count and total external branch length have the exact covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(C_n,L_n)=-2/9\) for every sample size \(n\ge4\), when unordered lineage pairs coalesce at unit rate. A quartic formula for covariance with singleton-lineage counts at each ancestral level explains why this topology–length dependence is independent of sample size.
