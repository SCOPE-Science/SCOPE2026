In a finite-memory sigmoid Rulkov neuron, the creation of three stationary points at \(\kappa=1\) precedes the possibility of a locally attracting *resting* point. In the weak-slow-update limit the exact stability onset is \(\kappa\ge\operatorname{artanh}(\sqrt5-2)/(\sqrt5-2)\), including the singular endpoint, and the positive outer fixed point remains unstable.
