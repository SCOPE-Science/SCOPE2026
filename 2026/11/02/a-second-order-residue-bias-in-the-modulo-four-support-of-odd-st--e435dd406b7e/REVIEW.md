The proof uses the exact published odd-index support characterization of arXiv:2610.20135v1, separates the two disjoint \(2\)-adic families, and checks uniqueness from the distinguished exponent \(4k+1\). It justifies removing higher prime powers and the coprimality condition with an explicit \(O(\sqrt X)\) error. Fixed-modulus prime asymptotics are applied uniformly on the small-square range; the large-square tail and logarithmic moments are absolutely summable. Differentiated Euler factors give the exact main and secondary coefficients. A separately implemented exact-integer coefficient and prime-square enumeration agrees through weight \(1400\) and index \(40000\). The numerical checks do not prove the asymptotics.
