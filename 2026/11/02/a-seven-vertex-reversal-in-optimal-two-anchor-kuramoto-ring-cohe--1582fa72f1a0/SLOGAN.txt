For the unpinned-only order parameter of a weakly disordered, locally synchronized Kuramoto ring with two permanently pinned vertices, the optimal pin arrangement reverses sharply at seven vertices: adjacent pins maximize leading coherence for \(N=4,5,6\), whereas the most widely separated pins do so for every \(N\ge7\). A closed concave quadratic in the arc-product variable proves the classification and separates it from conventional fixed-reference leader tracking.
