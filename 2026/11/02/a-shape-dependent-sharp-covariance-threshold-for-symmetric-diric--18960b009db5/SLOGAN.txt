For every symmetric Dirichlet concentration \(\alpha>0\), the covariance between the number of coordinates exceeding \(t\) and any strictly convex separable concentration score crosses zero exactly once as \(t\) increases. For quadratic concentration, the transition is exactly \(t=(\alpha+1)/(n\alpha+2)\), interpolating sharply between \(1/2\) and \(1/n\); the uniform-spacing case \(\alpha=1\) was known previously.
