For a qubit driven by \(N\) independent symmetric telegraph fluctuators, a covariance-matched Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck bath upper-bounds the *entire* absolute Ramsey-coherence curve exactly when the individual dimensionless noise variance obeys \(\sigma^2/(Nk^2)\leq1/2\). Above this sharp threshold, the telegraph coherence overtakes the Gaussian comparator at arbitrarily late times, even though its first non-Gaussian short-time correction is negative.
