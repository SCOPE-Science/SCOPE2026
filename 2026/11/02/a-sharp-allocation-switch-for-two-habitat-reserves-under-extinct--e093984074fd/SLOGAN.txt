For a two-reserve stochastic metapopulation with local extinction proportional to inverse area and rescue proportional to recipient area, the expected extinction time is a strictly convex quadratic in the product of the reserve areas. At the sharp rescue threshold \(\beta_c=\theta/[m(A-m)+A^2/4]\), the global optimal area division jumps from the two maximally unequal permitted splits to equal halves, with an exact three-way tie at equality.
