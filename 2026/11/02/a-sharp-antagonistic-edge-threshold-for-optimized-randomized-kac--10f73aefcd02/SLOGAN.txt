For normalized randomized Kaczmarz systems whose inverse row Gram has positive equal interactions on a complete graph except one negative edge, the unique fastest fixed-iid one-step row sampling law changes exactly at \(t=(n-2)/2\). An explicit rational probability vector and matching primal-dual semidefinite certificates determine the sharp contraction in every dimension \(n\ge5\), with the optimal slack nullity jumping from one to two.
