## correctness

PASS

The two zeroth-moment orthogonality equations have determinant u(1-e^{i theta})≠0; solving gives Q(z)=z²-(v/u)(1+e^{i theta})z+v e^{i theta}. Phase rotation yields real monic q(y)=y²-2(v/u)cos(theta/2)y+v with |v|<1; the strict degree-two Schur/Jury test gives the iff condition and exactly one unimodular root at equality. Separated arcs give |cos(theta/2)|<c=cos alpha, while u>=c and v>=2c²-1. If alpha<=acos(1/sqrt3), v>=-1/3 and 1+v>=2|v|, ensuring strictness. Beyond it, symmetric endpoint atoms yield a strict reversed inequality for angles just above 2alpha, and weak approximation by smooth everywhere-positive-on-interior endpoint bumps preserves escape. The endpoint pair phase angle and always-stable uniform density follow from moments and tan t>t. Independently checked 40 finite configurations, not a universal proof.
