For original degree-two multiple orthogonal polynomials formed from two disjoint rotated copies of an even circular-arc measure, all zeros are forced inside the disk for every admissible measure and rotation exactly up to angular half-width \(\alpha_* = \arccos(1/\sqrt3)\). Beyond this sharp boundary, strictly positive smooth densities with the same full arc supports can force an exterior zero, while uniform arc densities never do; the exact moment criterion is \((1+v)u>2|v||\cos(\theta/2)|\).
