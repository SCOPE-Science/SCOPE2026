On the Kneser pair metric, \(\exp(-t d^p)\) has a unique sharp positive-definiteness bandwidth threshold exactly when \(p>\log_2((n-3)/(n-4))\), with an \((n-1)\)-dimensional nullspace at the transition. At the critical scaling \(2^p=1+c/(n-4)\), the transition times rise strictly toward the universal positive root of \(e^T=1+cT\), with an explicit first finite-size correction.
