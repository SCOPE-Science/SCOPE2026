With two agents, one fair-binary good of value \(2r\), and \(r\) independent fair-binary goods of value \(2\), every ex-ante envy-free preallocation ceases to maximize the probability of ex-post envy-freeness exactly when \(r\ge3\). Binomial tails give the exact success probability for every allocation and a single-crossing test identifying the optimizer, which assigns asymptotically half the ordinary goods with the distinguished one.
