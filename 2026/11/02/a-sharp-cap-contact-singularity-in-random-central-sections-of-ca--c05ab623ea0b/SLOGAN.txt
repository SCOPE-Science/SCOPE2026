For uniformly oriented central planes of a three-dimensional capsule \([-a,a]e_3+rB_2^3\), the section area has an exact two-branch distribution with the universal reciprocal CDF \(1-\pi r^2/z\) up to a sharp cap-contact threshold. Beyond that threshold its CDF acquires a nonzero \((z-z_*)^{5/2}\) singular correction, while the maximal-area upper tail is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{\varepsilon/B_{a,r}}\) with an explicit positive coefficient.
