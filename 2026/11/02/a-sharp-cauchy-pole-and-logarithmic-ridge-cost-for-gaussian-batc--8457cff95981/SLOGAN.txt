For scalar Gaussian regression with noninterpolating minibatches, an uncapped zero-target stochastic Polyak step has an exact \(t^{-1}\) magnitude tail and infinite expected absolute size at every finite batch size \(k\ge2\). Gradient-ridge regularization incurs exactly half the tail coefficient times \(\log(1/\varepsilon)\) in expected absolute displacement, whereas using the true batch minimum gives a stable affine recursion with finite stationary moment orders precisely below \(k\).
