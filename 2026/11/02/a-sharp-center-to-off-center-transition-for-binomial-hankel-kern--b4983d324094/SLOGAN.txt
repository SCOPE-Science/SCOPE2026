For the two-term Hardy Hankel symbol \(z^m+cz^n\), the reproducing-kernel norm changes from an off-center optimum to a center optimum precisely when \(m\ge3n\) and \(c\) crosses an explicit threshold; at \(m>3n\) the maximizing radius jumps. Near the monomial limit \(c\downarrow0\), kernel testing gains only order \(c^{2m/(m+n)}\) while the full operator norm gains linearly, with explicit sharp coefficients.
