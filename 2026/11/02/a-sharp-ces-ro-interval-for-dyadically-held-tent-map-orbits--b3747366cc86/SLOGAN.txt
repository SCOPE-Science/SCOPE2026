For the full tent map held on dyadically growing time blocks, every dense underlying orbit has physical-time Cesàro accumulation set exactly \([0,4/5]\), although the ordinary chaotic orbit averages \(1/2\) almost surely. An explicit affine calibration gives the sharp universal finite bound \(A_N(x)\le4/5+x/(5N)\), and a right-branch backward orbit attains the upper endpoint.
