For the normal toric threefold \(X_n\) cut out by the lattice cone spanned by \((1,0,0)\), \((0,1,0)\), and \((1,1,n)\), with \(n\ge3\), the normalized Nash-blowup fan changes in characteristic \(p>0\) precisely when \(p\mid n\). The changing Newton vertex is \((2,2,n)\to(2,2,n-1)\), moving an interior fan ray from \((n-1,n-1,-1)\) to \((n,n,-1)\), so every prime occurs as an exceptional characteristic in dimension three.
