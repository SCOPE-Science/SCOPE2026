For a two-state active-matter battery held under one fixed discharge load, the unique expected-work-optimal load moves upward or downward with the allowed discharge duration according to whether the prepared excited-state population is below or above \(p_{\mathrm{crit}}=1/2+\beta E/4\). At the threshold the optimal load equals the intrinsic gap \(E\) at every duration, and explicit charging and thermal-storage times determine when this directional behavior reverses.
