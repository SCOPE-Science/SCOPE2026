For a controlled walk on a complete rooted \(b\)-ary tree, taking the parent whenever offered is optimal, and exactly \(\lfloor\log 2/\log(1+1/b)\rfloor+1\) independent neighbor offers are needed to change optimal leaf-to-root hitting from exponential to linear growth in tree height. The crossing is also distributional: inward-biased times obey a Gaussian central limit law, while outward-biased times, normalized by their means, converge to an exponential law.
