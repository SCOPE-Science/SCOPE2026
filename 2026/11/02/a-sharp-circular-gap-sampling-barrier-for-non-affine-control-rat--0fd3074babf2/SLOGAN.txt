For the sinusoidal non-affine time map, nodally rate-feasible affine controls have a sharp continuous-rate inflation \(\frac{1-a\cos(\pi g)}{1-a}\), determined by the longest circular gap \(g\) between nodes. Equispaced nodes uniquely minimize the worst-phase inflation; two-node flipped-Radau sampling can miss a rate bound by a factor \(29/2\) at mapping amplitude \(a=9/10\).
