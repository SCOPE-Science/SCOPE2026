For every recovery-to-transmission ratio above a clique–star community's own spectral SIS threshold, the most outbreak-sensitive endpoint of one new intercommunity contact is one of its two connector vertices; the optimal connector switches once at \(q_*=s(r-2)/(s-r+1)\) exactly for \(r\le s<\zeta_r\), and otherwise never switches. The critical contact weight factors into the diagonal Green-function scores of its endpoints, making this intrinsic ranking independent of any recipient network that is also subcritical at the chosen ratio.
