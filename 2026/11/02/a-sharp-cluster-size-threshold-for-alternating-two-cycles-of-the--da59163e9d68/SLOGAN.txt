In the zero-lag, equal-frequency discrete Kuramoto network, an exact alternating two-cluster orbit is attracting modulo common phase precisely when its larger block occupies less than \((\sin u-u\cos u)/(u(1-\cos u))\), where \(\kappa\sin u=2u\) and \(2<\kappa<\pi\). This sharp fraction falls from \(2/3\) to \(2/\pi\), making the three-oscillator network exceptional: every such bipartition cycle is unstable, while balanced partitions attract for every other network size at least two.
