Exact depth-one Anderson acceleration on a positive scalar affine contraction terminates in finitely many steps precisely when its coefficient \(\ell^1\)-cap exceeds \((3-\sqrt{1-q})/(1+\sqrt{1-q})\). The smallest cap that guarantees termination for every \(0<q<1\) is three, and at that cap the first zero occurs at step \(\lceil\pi/(2\arccos\sqrt q)\rceil\).
