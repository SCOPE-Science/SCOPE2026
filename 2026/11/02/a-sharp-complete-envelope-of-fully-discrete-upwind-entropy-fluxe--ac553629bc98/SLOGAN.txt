For first-order upwind advection with quadratic entropy, the fully discrete cell inequality holds for every real state and every consistent shared two-point entropy flux exactly when \(0<\nu\le1\) and \(U(a)-(1-\nu)(a-b)^2/2\le H(a,b)\le U(a)\) at every interface. The half-cell sufficient interval in the motivating 2026 construction is strictly narrower and becomes empty on nonconstant interfaces already at \(\nu>1/2\), even though the complete admissible range remains nonempty up to \(\nu=1\).
