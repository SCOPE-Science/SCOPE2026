Geodesic Gaussian Schur multipliers on an \(n\)-cycle undergo a sharp complete-positivity transition at exponent \(t\sim\pi/n\): every fixed \(nt>\pi\) is eventually completely positive, while every fixed \(0<nt<\pi\) is eventually not even positive. Explicit Fourier modes quantify the failure, with an extra factor of \(1/n\) in odd cycle lengths.
