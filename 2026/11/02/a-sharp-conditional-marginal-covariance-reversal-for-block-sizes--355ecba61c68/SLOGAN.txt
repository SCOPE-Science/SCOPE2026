In a uniform random noncrossing partition, singleton and size-\(r\) block counts are always negatively correlated without conditioning, but conditioning on exactly \(k\) blocks makes their covariance change sign sharply at \(rk=2(n-1)\), apart from explicit degenerate boundary cases. For every fixed \(r\ge5\), the two covariances have opposite signs and both have order \(n\) at the typical block count.
