# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The norm claim concerns the bounded extension of \(C=[M_1,T]\), initially defined on the natural coordinate-multiplication domain. Exact gamma/beta integration and binomial CDF recursion give the weighted-shift matrix. A two-point binomial maximizer argument, monotonicity of the central Wallis ratio and Stirling's formula prove the sharp norm, strict nonattainment and equal essential norm. A threshold-counting expectation identity for two independent binomial variables and a symmetric random-walk identity prove the exact finite-rank trace and its asymptotic. The arithmetic checker passed 3,073 finite assertions; no infinite conclusion relies on enumeration.

Search results cannot certify global novelty, and a differently formulated Gaussian interface result remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Two natural operator invariants of the same geometric cone are determined exactly: a sharp, nonattained essential commutator norm and a closed finite-energy nonprojection defect with a universal leading coefficient. The latter is valid for every cutoff, not solely asymptotically. Both quantify an interface that survives arbitrarily high Fock energies; the general quasi-radial integral formula does not by itself provide their constants.
