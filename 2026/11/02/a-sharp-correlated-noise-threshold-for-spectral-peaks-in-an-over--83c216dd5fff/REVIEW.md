# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The specified stable real-eigenvalue feedforward Itô system has an explicitly invertible triangular complex transfer matrix. Its downstream power spectral density is exactly \(2D_x(\omega^2+c)/[(\omega^2+a^2)(\omega^2+b^2)]\) under the stated two-sided Fourier convention, with \(c=b^2+t^2+2bt\rho\) and \(t=k\sqrt{D_y/D_x}\). The derivative with respect to \(z=\omega^2\) has the strictly decreasing numerator \(-z^2-2cz+a^2b^2-c(a^2+b^2)\), proving all peak/monotonicity cases, equality included. AM–GM proves the sharp optimized correlation threshold. Fourier inversion and exponential ordering prove the exactly-one-sign-reversal covariance claim. The independent numerical checker compares complex resolvents, Lyapunov solutions and matrix exponentials, and extremum alternatives; it is corroboration, not a substitute for the proof.

## Value — PASS
Frequency-domain maxima are often used as signatures of oscillatory modes or feedback. This natural two-component null model shows precisely when that reading is unsafe: negatively correlated white forcing can create a pair of off-zero spectral maxima with only negative real deterministic eigenvalues. The exact coupling-optimized correlation requirement and the single negative autocovariance lobe supply discriminants not captured by merely plotting an individual transfer spectrum. The classification covers all physical positive decay, gain and diffusivity parameters, not a handpicked example, and provides an exact non-oscillatory countermechanism for spectral-peak inference.
