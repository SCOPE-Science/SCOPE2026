Two stable, strictly overdamped, feedforward Langevin variables can have symmetric nonzero-frequency spectral peaks without any oscillatory deterministic eigenvalues. The peak exists for some feedforward coupling exactly when the driving white noises are anticorrelated beyond the sharp boundary \(\rho<-b/\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\); whenever it exists, the downstream autocovariance changes sign exactly once rather than oscillating indefinitely.
