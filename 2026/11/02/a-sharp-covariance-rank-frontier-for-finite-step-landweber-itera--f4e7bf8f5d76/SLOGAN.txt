For \(K\ge2\) Landweber steps, retaining the largest eigenvectors of the normal operator retains the largest propagated noise-variance directions uniformly exactly up to a sharp spectral ceiling \(t_K\sim1.2564312/K\). Beyond that frontier, even-step stable two-dimensional systems can make the rank-one covariance truncation error larger by an unbounded factor \(\asymp\epsilon^{-2}\), and standard residual diagonal-probe variance larger by \(\asymp\epsilon^{-4}\).
