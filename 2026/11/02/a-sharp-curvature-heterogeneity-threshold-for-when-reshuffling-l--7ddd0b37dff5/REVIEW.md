# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The stationary formulas follow from strict contractions and two explicitly reconstructed scalar affine recurrences. The exact ratio identity reduces the comparison to the sign of a quartic, and the Sturm/resultant/subresultant certificate distinguishes the one admissible double-root event from an inadmissible second event. A standalone exact-rational verifier confirms the critical algebra and step-domain root counts. The decimal constants are only approximations, not numerical substitutes for the proof.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The exact stationary formula for identical-curvature reshuffling has precedents in the shared-Hessian quadratic treatment of Ying et al. (2018, arXiv:1803.07964), and its high-step reversal is not claimed as independently new. Koloskova et al. (2023, arXiv:2305.19259) address broader convergence rates and shared-Hessian examples. The new exact heterogeneous-curvature stationary comparison, algebraic threshold and merging two-root window were not shown to follow from the inspected primary results. Residual priority risk remains from specialized related work, including a 2026 small-step dominance paper whose full text was not inspected.

## Value
PASS. A fixed evaluation-budget comparison between independent SGD and reshuffling has practical mathematical motivation. The sharp universal curvature-ratio boundary distinguishes unconditional dominance within the componentwise contractive regime from a genuine stable-step reversal, with an explicit high-curvature-disparity region where the reversal cannot occur.

## Scope and limitations
The result is for two one-dimensional noninterpolating quadratics with fixed stepsize and fresh independent epoch permutations. It does not apply automatically to tuned algorithms, finite-horizon comparisons, momentum, larger datasets or nonlinear problems. The critical algebraic root and its intervals are verified exactly by the accompanying certificate. Independent validation has not been carried out.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
