Independently randomizing the two Lie–Trotter orders of a damped planar shear splitting yields the exact squared-norm multiplier \(e^{-4ah}(1+(Kh)^4/2)\) on every step. A single sharp shear threshold \(Kh=2.783928804\ldots\) separates regimes in which randomization destroys mean-square stability of two otherwise stable fixed orders and regimes in which it restores mean-square stability while both fixed orders are unstable.
