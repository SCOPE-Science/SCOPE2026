For same-sample-per-stage, even-stage stochastic Chebyshev descent on a two-component quadratic, positive damping yields an exact stationary-noise ceiling and a sharp topological phase diagram: below or at one damping threshold the noninterval-support steps form one window per stage, while above it they merge into one. The merger occurs exactly when the worst stationary variance drops strictly below three times the squared component-minimizer offset.
