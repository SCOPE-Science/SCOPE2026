The ordinary affine Bernstein operator on spherical covariance kernels preserves positive definiteness on \(S^2\) exactly at degrees \(1,2,3\); every higher degree fails, with an explicit negative Legendre coefficient for the positive-definite input \(P_{n+2}\). In every dimension \(d\ge2\), the analogous Gegenbauer coefficient becomes negative whenever the Bernstein degree exceeds \(d+1\), giving a universal dimension-dependent obstruction without claiming sufficiency in higher dimensions.
