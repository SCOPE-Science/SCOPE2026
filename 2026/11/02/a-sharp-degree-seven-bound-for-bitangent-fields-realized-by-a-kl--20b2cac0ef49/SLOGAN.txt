Every smooth plane quartic over an odd finite field has a geometric bitangent defined over an extension of degree at most seven; the least possible extension degree is restricted to \(\{1,2,3,4,7\}\). The bound is sharp: \(X^3Y+2Y^3Z+Z^3X=0\) over \(\mathbb F_{29}\) has four degree-seven Frobenius orbits on its 28 bitangents and none over a smaller extension.
