For the canonical degree-two Blaschke multiplier \(B_a(z)=(z^2-a)/(1-a z^2)\) on the even-power subspace of weighted Bergman \(A^2_\alpha\), its one-dimensional wandering space generates the subspace exactly when \(a\le\tan^2(\pi/[2(\alpha+2)])\), including equality. A closed even-binomial kernel formula yields every wandering-vector zero and shows that the first nonwandering configurations occur precisely for \(\alpha>0\).
