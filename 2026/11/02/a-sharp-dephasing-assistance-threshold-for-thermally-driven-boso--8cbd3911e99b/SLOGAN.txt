For a bosonic three-site chain coupled to equal-strength thermal reservoirs and dephased only at its detuned middle oscillator, the stationary number current has an exactly optimal dephasing rate \(\gamma_*=\max\{0,2|\delta|-\kappa-4J^2/\kappa\}\). Noise strictly enhances transport precisely when \(|\delta|>\kappa/2+2J^2/\kappa\); at and below the threshold zero dephasing is uniquely optimal.
