The sharp norming-functional diameter for the real cube–crosspolytope interpolation \(\|x\|=\max\{\|x\|_\infty,\|x\|_1/s\}\) is strictly below two exactly for \(n=2,3\) and \(1<s<2\), with respective values \((1+(s-1)^2)/s\) and \(2/s\). Four dimensions already force the maximal diameter two for every parameter, and both endpoint norms have diameter two.
