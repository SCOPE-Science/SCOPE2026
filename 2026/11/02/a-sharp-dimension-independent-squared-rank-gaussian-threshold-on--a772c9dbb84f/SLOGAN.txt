On all binary \(m\times3\) matrix spaces with \(m\ge3\), the squared-rank Gaussian kernel is positive semidefinite exactly for \(0\le u\le\rho\) or \(u=1\), where \(\rho\) is a single algebraic threshold independent of \(m\). At the nontrivial endpoint exactly the full-rank Fourier sector vanishes, with nullity \((2^m-1)(2^m-2)(2^m-4)\), and no further positive-semidefinite window appears.
