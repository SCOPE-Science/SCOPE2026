# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the real Hamming free space, shortest paths express every normalized molecule as an average of unit edges. In each direction the parallel edge molecules use disjoint pairs; an arbitrary choice of edge signs is realized by a two-valued 1-Lipschitz function, so their span has the exact ℓ1 norm. Outside-coordinate flips convolve the edge with the product flip law; the direction-coordinate flip gives the factor r_i. The only nonnegative coefficient of the image of that edge under I−T_t is at the original pair (allowing zeros). Its exact ℓ1 norm is the asserted direction formula. Translations are isometries, hence the heat average is a contraction. The n=1,2 values are strictly below one at finite times; for n≥3 each direction is 1+r_i b_i(t), where b_i tends to 1−2^(2−n)>0 and b_i′ tends to zero, so the max eventually exceeds one and decreases to one. In the isotropic case the subtracted factor in the derivative with respect to ρ is strictly increasing from 2^(2−n) to n+1. This gives the unique maximum, and setting the original distance equal to one gives the entry time (n≥3). With ε=1−ρ_n the log equation and −log(1−ε/2)≥ε/2 first give ε≤2 log(n+1)/(n−2); uniform Taylor expansion then gives ε=2log(n)/n+O(log²(n)/n²). The peak identity 1+ρ_n−ρ_n(1+ρ_n)/(1+nρ_n) gives the claimed second term as well as the leading deficit. Independently ran 30 primal transport LPs through dimension four with zero reported discrepancy and evaluated the peak equation at four increasing dimensions; these are finite diagnostics only.

## originality

PASS

Supported as the exact heat distance profile and its sharp temporal consequences, not as a new coordinate-shadow norm technique. The prior projection argument is very close and extends to general real multipliers, but its stated theorem for indicator symbols does not give the norm of a non-idempotent heat difference, and projection norms alone give at most triangle bounds on mixtures. No implication-equivalent result was identified in the compared statements.
