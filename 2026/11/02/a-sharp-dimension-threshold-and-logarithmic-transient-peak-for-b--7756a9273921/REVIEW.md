# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The unit ball is generated by oriented graph-edge molecules because the graph distance is a shortest-path distance. Each parallel-edge family embeds isometrically as an \(\ell_1\) space, witnessed by explicit two-valued Lipschitz functions. Independent-coordinate convolution gives the stated signed coefficient distribution and the exact operator norm. The dimension threshold follows directly from the product limit. Derivative monotonicity and a uniform logarithmic expansion prove uniqueness of the isotropic peak and its sharp two-term height asymptotic. Exact rational dual checks and separate transport optimizations provide additional finite corroboration, not a substitute for proof.

## Originality

PASS with residual bibliographic risk. The closest published Hamming Lipschitz-free result addresses norms of Walsh spectral **projections**, while the present non-idempotent heat operator and its transient norm-distance profile are not included in that statement. Its gradient argument is technically adjacent and can be adapted, so the new content should be understood as the temporal peak law and the all-rate exact distance formula rather than as an independent invention of a shadow-norm method. Inspected literature on heat-flow geometry and group-action projections does not imply the stated transient peaks. No exhaustive global literature guarantee is claimed.

## Value

PASS. The result distinguishes dimensions one and two from all higher dimensions for a natural semigroup behavior, for **every** positive anisotropic rate vector. In the isotropic family it identifies a unique peak, the exact entry into the overshoot phase, the logarithmic time scale, and a sharp two-term approach to the triangle-inequality ceiling \(2\). These are quantitative geometric properties of a canonical family of contractions, not ad hoc choices of a single matrix.

## Closest sources and limitations

A. Eskenazis, arXiv:2310.01868v1, analyzes discrete cube heat-flow inequalities and embeddings, not this free-space operator norm. M. Cúth and M. Doucha, arXiv:2104.11519v1, study projections arising from group actions. The published *Coordinate-shadow norm formula for Walsh projections on Hamming Lipschitz-free spaces* provides the nearest methodological antecedent, with a different operator class. Equivalent formulas may occur in earthmover or Markov-chain operator literature under different nomenclature. The theorem is restricted to real, unweighted Boolean Hamming free spaces and independent-coordinate heat noise.
