For the classical deterministic diploid viability-selection map, any fixed list of positive selection strengths favoring the same allele maximizes the final allele frequency by acting strongest first whenever the common dominance coefficient satisfies \(0\le h\le1\). This ordering rule is sharp: under overdominance \(h>1\), two selection epochs have an exact initial-frequency threshold below the common polymorphic equilibrium where the favorable order reverses.
