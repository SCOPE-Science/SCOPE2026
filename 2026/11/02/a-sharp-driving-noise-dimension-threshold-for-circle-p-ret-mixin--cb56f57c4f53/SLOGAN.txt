For p-Ret Brownian heat simulation on the circle, replacing one spherical driving-noise coordinate by two redundant coordinates leaves the target diffusion unchanged but changes the numerical walk from having no \(L^2\) spectral gap and no bounded discrete Poisson inverse to having both for every fixed step. Every driving dimension \(r\ge2\) moreover gives geometric total-variation mixing, whereas \(r=1\) has dense arithmetic resonances and remains atomic at every finite time.
