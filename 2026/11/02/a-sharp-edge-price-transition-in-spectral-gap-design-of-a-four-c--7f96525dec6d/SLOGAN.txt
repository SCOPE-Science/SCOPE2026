An independently weighted four-cycle with three unit-price links and a closing link of price \(\tau\) has an exact maximum spectral gap \(4B/(5+\tau+2\sqrt\tau)\) below the sharp threshold \(\tau=9\), and \(B/5\) above it. Every optimum retains the closing link below threshold, every optimum deletes it above threshold, and at equality an interval of different optimal allocations coexists.
