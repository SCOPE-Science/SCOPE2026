# Scientific review: directed-edge Kemeny constants on cycle bouquets

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof specifies the two arc transition matrices and their uniform stationary measure, derives the full non-backtracking characteristic polynomial from deterministic cycle tours, obtains Kemeny's constant by a simple-root derivative, and independently derives the ordinary edge constant from resistance and edge/vertex spectral composition. The explicit derivative bound proves strict coordinate monotonicity of the comparison for all real cycle lengths at least three; four integer base cases therefore establish the infinite classification. The standalone exact-rational checker builds the actual finite transition matrices and passes 799 assertions. The checker is corroboration, not a replacement for the symbolic proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The 2022 Breen–Faught–Glover–Kempton–Knudson–Oveson paper covers edge-space Kemeny constants for regular, biregular and cycle-barbell graphs with distinct junctions and path connectors and numerical small-graph examples, not the all-size unequal-length one-hub bouquet classification. Small exceptions may have appeared in those numerical enumerations and are not claimed new individually. The 2025 Breen–Kempton–Knudson–Shumway pinwheel analysis uses vertex-projected non-backtracking constants and equal cycle lengths; the present edge-space observable and arbitrary lengths are distinct. Ordinary resistance and spectral lemmas are credited. Semantic research-record searches did not reveal the combined theorem, but negative searches do not establish definitive priority.

**Value — PASS.** This all-parameter classification answers the mathematically motivated question of when forbidding immediate reversals increases, rather than decreases, mean stationary-target directed-edge hitting time in a natural family of graphs with a cut vertex. It gives explicit formulas, two rare reversals, a unique tie, and a proof that no larger exceptions occur. Such a complete boundary result has value beyond enumerating small graphs and clarifies which graph structures govern the comparison.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Primary comparisons: arXiv:2203.12049 (all main sections including numerical remarks and cycle-barbell proofs); arXiv:2510.06650 (Section 4.4 pinwheels and defining vertex-based constants); arXiv:2007.03103 (flower-graph resistance and ordinary walk quantities). The theorem is for unweighted one-hub cycle bouquets with \(m\ge2\), not arbitrary cactus graphs, and compares arc-space constants only. Some chains are periodic, so no mixing-time inference follows. Independent historical priority and mathematical audit remain open.
