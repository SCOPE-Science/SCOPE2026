## Mathematical value — PASS

Fixed total degree is a natural grading of analytic trigonometric polynomials on the three-torus. The restricted Laplacian's exact eigenvalue multiplicities first obey classical full-lattice arithmetic, then lose them at a sharply determined finite spectral boundary. The same geometric boundary becomes a nonanalytic cusp in the macroscopic distribution. The integer cutoff, quantitative all-degree distribution and explicit cusp constant together characterize a meaningful arithmetic-to-geometric transition, rather than a chosen table entry or a routine increment of a previous bound. No individual local spectral gap theorem or higher-dimensional classification is asserted.
