On degree-\(3M\) positive-frequency Fourier shells of \(\mathbb T^3\), the complete Eisenstein-divisor multiplicity formula holds until the sharp threshold \(\lceil3(M+1)^2/4\rceil\), where the first representation is excluded. Across all degrees, the normalized squared-frequency spectrum converges at uniform CDF rate \(O(N^{-1})\) to a circle–triangle distribution with an exact square-root density cusp at \(1/2\).
