For explicit two-stage order-two Runge–Kutta on a damped planar oscillator, the sharp Euclidean energy-nonexpansion timestep attains its unique global maximum \(8/(3\gamma)\) when \((\omega/\gamma)^2=3/8\). At that threshold, the propagator has spectral radius \(1/3\) but operator norm \(1\), exhibiting a sharp distinction between eigenmode decay and energy safety.
