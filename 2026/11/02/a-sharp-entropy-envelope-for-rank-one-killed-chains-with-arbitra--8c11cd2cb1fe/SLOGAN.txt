For every strictly positive rank-one killed transition kernel, the spectral survival rate under **any** fixed ancestral-relocation law is bounded by the sharp entropy envelope \(B=\max_q(\sum_s q(s)k(s))e^{-D(q\|\pi)}\), with a quantitative lower gap controlled by the probability that two relocation lags coincide. As the largest lag atom vanishes, the rate converges to \(B\) without tail or moment assumptions, whereas relocation concentrated at one arbitrarily distant lag retains the strictly smaller memoryless rate when survival probabilities differ.
