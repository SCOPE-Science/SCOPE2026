A wheel with unit rim conductances and common spoke conductance \(r\) has a Euclidean effective-resistance metric exactly up to one sharp threshold \(r_m\), with a unique radial obstruction beyond it. Its sharp threshold scales as \(r_m\sim34.84767129506\ldots/m^2\); at equality the minimum Hilbert dimension drops by one and the supremal negative-type exponent is exactly \(2\).
