For Qiu's BDF3 method with Simpson–Adams–Bashforth exponential-memory quadrature, the scalar tail recurrence is strictly Schur stable exactly when \(\beta<12(1-e^{-\theta})/[\theta e^{-\theta}(23-16e^{-\theta}+5e^{-2\theta})]\). This sharp threshold exceeds the continuous instability boundary \(\beta=1\) at every positive timestep, so the method can falsely damp an exponentially growing Volterra mode.
