For a confined Brownian particle exposed to finite-rate Robin reaction pulses separated by uniform diffusion gaps, eternal survival has positive probability exactly when \(\sum_n\min\{\kappa_n\delta_n/L,\sqrt{D\delta_n}/L\}\) converges. Even divergent integrated reactivity can leave permanent survivors when the pulses shrink faster than their diffusion-limited \(\sqrt{\delta_n}\) exposure scale.
