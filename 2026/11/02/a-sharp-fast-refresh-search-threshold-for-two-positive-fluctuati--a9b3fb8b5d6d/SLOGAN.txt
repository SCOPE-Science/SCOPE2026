For Brownian search with two strictly positive, autonomously fluctuating reset rates, the fast-refresh effect changes sign in a sharp \(\nu^{-1}\) window around the optimal constant reset rate. The threshold is shifted upwards by \(\bigl(h(r_*)/h''(r_*)+\eta^2/2\bigr)/\nu\), and exactly at the constant-rate optimum fluctuations necessarily increase the mean search time to second order.
