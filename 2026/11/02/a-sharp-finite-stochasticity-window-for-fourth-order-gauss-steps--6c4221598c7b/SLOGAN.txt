For the unit-rate irreducible directed eight-state Markov cycle, the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre endpoint is stochastic exactly at zero and in a finite positive window \([6\alpha,6\beta]\), whose algebraic endpoints are the unique positive roots of explicitly given degree-six and degree-twelve polynomials. Both arbitrarily small positive steps and all sufficiently large steps violate nonnegativity, despite exact mass conservation.
