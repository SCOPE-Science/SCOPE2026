In an on/off chiral swimmer with fixed stationary motile fraction, the exploration diffusivity has a unique finite optimal switching rate precisely when the deactivation-to-activation ratio exceeds \(r_c(y)=(y^2+2y-1)/(1+y^2)\), for \(y=\Omega/D_R>1\). The optimizer escapes to infinite switching as \((r-r_c)^{-1}\), with exact prefactor \(y-1\); below threshold, faster switching always improves long-time diffusion.
