# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The main identity is an exact decomposition of stopped physical-time variance: condition on the full embedded trajectory, then use a finite-state Poisson corrector for the centered edge conditional variances. Its bounded-increment martingale is uniformly integrable at first crossing because any finite signed positive-drift edge flux has an exponential first-crossing-index tail. The boundary term is bounded by the oscillation of a computable potential independent of the flux. Bounded overshoot for mean-holding-time marks supplies both non-asymptotic sharpness bounds and the limit; a one-state renewal system attains the coefficient even at finite thresholds. Rational recursion checks 24 instances, not an infinite proof. Key restrictions: finite-state irreducibility, independent edge-conditioned holding times, finite second moments, renewal-epoch start.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The source explicitly identifies the first-passage semi-Markov inequality and its saturation as an unresolved question. Here a single exact stopped-path identity simultaneously yields the sharp physical-time uncertainty coefficient for arbitrary positive-drift fluxes, a constructive optimal weighting, bounded finite-threshold error, and a flux-independent computable correction. This connects temporal-noise fluctuations and clock design beyond exponential Markov waiting times; it also covers signed edge weights and periodic embedded chains. The result does not claim an exhaustive class of saturating fluxes or stationary-age finite-level control.
