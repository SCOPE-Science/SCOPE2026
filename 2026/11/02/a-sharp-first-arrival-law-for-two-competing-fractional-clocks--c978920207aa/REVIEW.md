# Scientific assessment

## Correctness

**PASS.** The clock-race fairness identity is proved from an explicit normalized even density of the logarithmic exponential-mixture rate. The embedded walk has an exact killed-walk root \(2-\sqrt3\) and a two-step geometric domination. Conditional on all marks and step signs, holding times are independent, so the fixed-path regularly varying convolution asymptotic applies. Summability follows from exponential moments of the stopped event count. The mean follows by Tonelli and the sharp Mittag-Leffler product survival. A separate script passed 943 supplementary assertions. The script alone does not certify asymptotics.

## Originality

**PASS, subject to historical-priority uncertainty.** The 2026 competing-clock CTRW source establishes a broad process framework, but the inspected material does not give this exact lattice arrival-tail coefficient, rare-long-delay cause or the fair-race mixture argument. The 2026 Mittag-Leffler queue scaling paper keeps its transition probability as an unspecified parameter, and the 2023 renewal-queue paper explicitly posed equality of unequal-index equal-scale races as Conjecture 5.1. The 2023 source already gives the *one-event* sum-of-exponents tail, Mainardi's spectrum is classical, and the general stopped-sum method is established by Denisov, Foss and Korshunov; none of those components is represented as independently novel. Broader renewal-reward and stochastic-reset first-passage literature could contain indirect equivalences; definitive historical priority needs independent expert assessment.

## Value

**PASS.** The single theorem quantifies first arrival to a discrete target in a recent two-clock resetting mechanism, including the exact tail constant, a finite/infinite mean boundary and the observable type of the unusually long episode. The exact fair-clock identity makes the coefficient universal across distinct clock indices and resolves the queueing-model balance ambiguity which persists as a parameter in a subsequent 2026 study. This is a motivated structural result, not a numerical point evaluation.

## Scope and literature limitations

The theorem is restricted to independent, equally scaled Mittag-Leffler clocks renewed after every event, a fair nearest-neighbour jump direction, reset to zero, and target \(+1\). A continuous-space point-target first-arrival model need not obey the same theorem. Parts of the derivation use classical known tools and could be implied by a not-yet-identified more general model theorem. The 2026 stochastic reset primary source was read in accessible HTML material but later fetch attempts intermittently failed, limiting further comparisons. No independent audit was performed.
