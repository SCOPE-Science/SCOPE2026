In a nearest-neighbour renewal-reset search with two equally scaled Mittag-Leffler clocks, unequal fractional indices still produce exactly fair jump-versus-reset events. The first-arrival tail has exact exponent \(\alpha+\beta\) and constant \(2(1+\sqrt3)/[\Gamma(1-\alpha)\Gamma(1-\beta)]\); its mean is finite exactly when \(\alpha+\beta>1\), and the rare long delay is a jump with limiting probability \(\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)\).
