The first initially nonbinary coefficient at index \(r\) forces simultaneous binary carry normalization to finish within \(\lfloor\log_2 N\rfloor-r\) rounds, where \(N\) is the preserved weighted value. For every pair of positive integers, this proves Iizumi's 2026 open conjecture on binary multiplication carry depth and strengthens it to the sharp bound \(L_{\mathrm{par}}(a,b)\leq\max\{0,\lfloor\log_2(ab)\rfloor-1\}\).
