For three agents and five online goods, each agent having one value \(H\) and four values \(1\), the optimal worst-case ex-post EFX approximation is exactly \(\min\{1,\max\{2/H,1/2\}\}\). This sharp finite-horizon law has breakpoints at \(H=2\) and \(H=4\), despite the absence of any horizon-uniform positive EFX guarantee in the larger model.
