For two-periodic variable-step BDF2 on scalar decay, fixed total time per pair yields a sharp rate-optimal step-ratio transition at \(\lambda(h_1+h_2)=1\): equal steps are optimal below the threshold, while above it the optimum occurs at a repeated Floquet multiplier. At the isolated budget \(\lambda(h_1+h_2)=2\), sufficiently imbalanced positive steps make the asymptotic two-step spectral radius arbitrarily small, although this does not control transient gain or finite-time accuracy.
