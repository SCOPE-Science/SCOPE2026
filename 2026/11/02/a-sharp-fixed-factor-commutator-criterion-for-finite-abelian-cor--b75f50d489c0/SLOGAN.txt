For every finite abelian core-quandle algebra over any nonzero commutative coefficient ring, the obstruction to representing every commutator-generated element as \([u,e_0]\) is a free module whose rank is the number of \(-2\)-orbits on the odd-order component minus \(|A/3A|\). This obstruction vanishes exactly for two-groups times elementary abelian three-groups, giving a constructive uniform commutator-width-one theorem whenever the two-primary component is nontrivial.
