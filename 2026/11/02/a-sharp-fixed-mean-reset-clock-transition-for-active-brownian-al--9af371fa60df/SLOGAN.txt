For a completely reset active Brownian particle in a harmonic trap, the best long-run alignment at a prescribed mean renewal interval has one sharp clock transition: above a unique critical interval, deterministic resets are uniquely optimal, while below it only increasingly bursty two-point clocks approach the optimum. At equal trapping and angular-relaxation rates, the globally optimal renewal clock improves the mean alignment by about \(19.37\%\) over the best Poisson clock; all results concern signed displacement and instantaneous, independent complete resets.
