For \(s\) Gaussian sketch rows, \(k\) Krylov steps, and symmetric positive-definite condition bound \(K>1\), the exact worst-case expected squared relative sketched-GMRES residual is \((s-1)\operatorname{sech}^2(k\operatorname{arcosh}((K+1)/(K-1)))/(s-k-1)\). At every fixed \(K\), the unique optimal depth for all sufficiently large \(s\) leaves precisely \(1+\lfloor(\sqrt K-1)^2/(4\sqrt K)\rfloor\) rows of mean-square oversampling reserve beyond the Krylov dimension.
