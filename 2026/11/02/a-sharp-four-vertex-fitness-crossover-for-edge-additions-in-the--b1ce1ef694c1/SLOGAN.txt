In the four-vertex birth–death Moran process, adding a triangle-closing edge to a path changes mutant fixation from inhibition to enhancement at one unique fitness \(r_c\approx4.8875341047\), the positive root of an explicit ninth-degree polynomial. All six possible connected four-vertex edge-addition classes have exact all-fitness signs, making four vertices the sharp minimum for an edge addition whose effect reverses with fitness; the qualitative path–paw reversal was already observed numerically.
