The planar harmonic convex-hull perimeter deficit has an exact, strictly positive Fourier-diagonal Hessian at every rotational disk automorphism, and its sharp local stability coefficient is \(1-\max\{r^2,((3r^2-1)/2)^2\}\). The least-stable perturbation changes from a second boundary harmonic to a first harmonic precisely at radius \(r=1/3\), where both frequencies tie.
