The stationary state of a closed quantum system completely reset at integer-shape Erlang renewal times has purity equal to reset-state fidelity at shape \(k=1\), and strictly greater at all nontrivial spectral gaps for \(2\leq k\leq13\). Shape \(k=14\) is the first at which fidelity can exceed purity; such witnesses exist for every \(k\geq14\), and the threshold shape has exactly two gap-driven ordering reversals.
