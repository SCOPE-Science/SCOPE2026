For a one-column Gaussian funNyström approximation to the spectral spike \(\operatorname{diag}(L,I_m)\), the expected excess nuclear error for the power \(A^\beta\) is asymptotic to \(C_{m,\beta}L^{\beta-q+1/2}\), with an explicit positive gamma-function constant. At one pass the sharp boundary is \(\beta=1/2\): the typical square-root error tends to the optimum, yet its expectation remains separated by an exact positive constant.
