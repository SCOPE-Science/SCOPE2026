For a scalar stable plant with fixed zero-order-hold feedback, independent equiprobable sampling intervals \(h\pm d\) can stabilize in mean square even when periodic sampling at the same mean \(h\) is unstable **if and only if** the feedback gain lies in \(1<k<2\). The sharp \(k=2\) obstruction follows from an exact two-point second-moment law and a geometric product bound, and a rational-parameter witness gives strict stability rescue.
