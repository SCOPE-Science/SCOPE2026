For the 2023 diffusive coupling of two Gaussian-cubic maps, every real orbit is eventually trapped in a common compact set exactly when \(-1/2\le b<1/2\). At the excluded boundary \(b=1/2\), every escaping orbit satisfies the sharp law \(\bigl((x_n-y_n)/2\bigr)^2=\log n+2\log\log n+\log(2a)+o(1)\), revealing an asymmetric logarithmic escape transition.
