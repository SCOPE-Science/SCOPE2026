For the sinusoidal jerk flow, **every real trajectory converges to an equilibrium exactly when** \(cd\le b\), including the critical endpoint, as certified by an explicit coercive periodic Lyapunov function. Just above the threshold, infinitely many translated stable Hopf cycles emerge with displacement \(\sqrt{8(cd-b)/(bd^2)}+O(cd-b)\), and every nonconstant cycle has the sharp, unattained period bound \(T>2\pi/\sqrt a\).
