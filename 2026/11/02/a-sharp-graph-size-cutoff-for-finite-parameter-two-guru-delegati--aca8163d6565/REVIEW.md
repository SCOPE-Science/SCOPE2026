# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The full claim is derived from the first-step harmonic equations and an exact two-terminal unit-current potential. The graph-independent upper bound uses the sharp elementary resistance inequality \(R\le N-1\); path endpoints attain equality. The growing broom construction follows from a direct sum of its electrical potentials, and its limiting shares are exact rational limits. The verification program independently constructs the absorption matrix and the grounded resistance potential with rational arithmetic, covering 840 graph/parameter instances plus path and broom tests. These tests are supplementary rather than infinite certificates.

## Originality

PASS at best available knowledge, with the following specific scope. The 2023 liquid-democracy source already introduces the limiting ranked-cost model and its finite-parameter random walk; the 2021 Directed Probabilistic Watershed paper already proves general absorption–Gibbs equivalence, gives a general Laplacian system, and exhibits a qualitative finite-temperature change of preferred sink. The new asserted conclusion is specifically the **attained worst-case all-graph formula**, its sharp \(N^{-1/2}\) uniformity threshold, and the diverging-graph, vanishing-parameter family where the more expensive caster asymptotically receives a strict \(3/5\) majority of fractional weight. Searches and full-text comparisons did not locate this combined quantitative conclusion. As general electrical methods are classical, specialist treatments may provide an equivalent result; independent literature priority checking remains necessary.

## Value

PASS. The asymptotic delegation rule is mathematically well-defined at each fixed graph, but finite-parameter implementations need a quantitative bound on how small the parameter must be as networks grow. The sharp and attained network-size threshold answers that natural numerical and interpretative question. The broom example proves the uniformity issue has operational significance at aggregate caster-weight level, with a macroscopic reversal that does not vanish in the coupled limit. It is not merely a small parameter correction or a new notation for the harmonic system.
