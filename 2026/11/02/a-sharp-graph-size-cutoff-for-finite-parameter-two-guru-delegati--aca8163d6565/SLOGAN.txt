For two-caster ranked-cost delegation on a connected \(N\)-vertex reciprocal network, finite-parameter absorption approximates the minimum-cost allocation uniformly over all networks exactly at the sharp scale \(N\varepsilon^2\to0\), with attained maximal discrepancy \((\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2(N-1))/(1+\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2(N-1))\). An explicit family of increasingly large broom networks lets the more expensive caster receive asymptotically \(3/5\) of all fractional voting weight even as \(\varepsilon\to0\), revealing the noncommutation of size and regularization limits.
