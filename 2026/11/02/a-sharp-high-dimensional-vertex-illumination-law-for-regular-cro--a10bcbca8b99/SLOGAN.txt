For the regular cross-polytope in dimension \(d\), the probability that a uniformly random direction illuminates any vertex is asymptotic to \(d\pi^{(1-d)/2}/(e\Gamma((d+1)/2))\), with sharp first relative correction \(7/(2d)\). Conditionally on illuminating a fixed vertex, its finitely many rescaled transverse coordinates converge jointly to independent symmetric Laplace variables; the underlying finite-dimensional solid-angle identity is classical.
