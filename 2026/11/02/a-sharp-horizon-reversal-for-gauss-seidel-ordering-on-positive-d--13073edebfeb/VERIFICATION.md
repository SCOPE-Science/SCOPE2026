The exact proof reduces an arbitrary SPD arrowhead to an active three-coordinate energy model and then uses symbolic polynomial identities. `verification/verify_star.py` recomputes those identities and cross-checks full-matrix sweep norms from independently assembled coordinate updates for dimensions three through seven and multiple permutations and horizons. The code uses SymPy for exact polynomial algebra and NumPy for finite-dimensional floating-point consistency tests. These tests do not establish the theorem for untested dimensions; the proof does.
