For double stars with \(p\ge2\) leaves on one hub and \(q>p\) on the other, a sharp imbalance threshold tending to \((1+\sqrt2)p\) separates a rational fastest-mixing regime from its strict failure. Above that threshold, the optimal second-largest eigenvalue modulus is exactly \(q^2/(q^2+q-p)\), attained with no hub holding probability, even when individual edge weights may be asymmetric.
