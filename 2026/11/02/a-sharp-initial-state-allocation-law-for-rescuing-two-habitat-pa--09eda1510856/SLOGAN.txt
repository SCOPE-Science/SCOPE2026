For a closed two-patch metapopulation, the unique allocation of a fixed directional colonization budget maximizing mean time to extinction is \(c_* = \operatorname{clip}_{[0,B]}((B+a w_{10}-b w_{01})/2)\). The optimum is balanced from full initial occupancy but can be entirely one-directional from a singly occupied landscape, even with identical habitats; the objective has the universal strict curvature \(-2/[ab(a+b+B)]\).
