The supremal negative-type exponent of the odd sunlet graph \(C_n\odot K_1\) has the sharp law \(\wp_n=1+T/n^2+o(n^{-2})\), where \(T=2\pi^2/(\gamma+\log(2\pi)-\operatorname{Ci}(2\pi))=8.097627356866536\ldots\). The asymptotic loss of negative type is controlled by the second cycle Fourier mode, rather than the fundamental one.
