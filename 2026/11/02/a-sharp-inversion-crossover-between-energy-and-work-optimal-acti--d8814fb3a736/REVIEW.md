# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact state-to-KL differentiation and closed-form capacity root were independently reconstructed. The root is strictly inside the positive design domain, and the derivative changes sign once. The internal-energy derivative has a unique sign change by a strictly increasing hyperbolic criterion. Evaluating this criterion at the population-inversion boundary proves a unique critical active affinity. The capacity optimum's derivative condition makes the internal-energy derivative strictly positive there, proving strict ordering. A local-uniform second-order relative-entropy expansion yields the weak-drive retained-capacity constant, not a finite-grid conjecture. The accompanying checker passed 3,000 exact symbolic and high-precision numeric assertions, but the universal conclusions rest on the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The source *Active-Matter Battery* (arXiv:2610.00428v1) explicitly provides the thermal and affinity-biased Bernoulli populations and the relative-entropy capacity. Those model equations and their thermodynamic meaning are not new. Its full accessible main text and Appendices A–C were compared: their extrema concern time, SOC, affinity-dependent charging efficiency and housekeeping costs, while no fixed-affinity global energy-gap design theorem or simultaneous comparison with excess energy is stated. The 2025 RC charging article concerns current/time optimization; the related discharging paper addresses output protocols and was compared only through accessible bibliographic/abstract material. Published-record searches for equivalent log-odds/relative-entropy formulations, stronger battery optimizers, tabulated critical constants, and direct implication from prior studies returned no stronger claim. This is a qualified best-knowledge determination, not a guarantee of publication priority: the source already contains enough pointwise formulas to permit subsequent differentiation, and unindexed derivations may exist.

## Value — PASS

The invariant model, distinct objectives and tunable design parameter are all natural. The theorem quantifies when maximizing raw energy gives the opposite population-inversion decision from maximizing recoverable work, supplies an all-affinity sharp crossover and demonstrates that weak-drive energy design sacrifices approximately 41.98 percent of maximum free-energy capacity. Those design-level implications are not merely a computation of an arbitrary scalar parameter.
