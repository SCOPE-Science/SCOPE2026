For a two-state active battery at fixed affinity, the gap maximizing recoverable nonequilibrium free energy always creates population inversion, whereas the gap maximizing excess internal energy does so only above the sharp threshold \((\beta a/2)\tanh(\beta a/2)=1\). In the weak-drive limit, optimizing for internal energy retains only \(0.5802019684\ldots\) of the maximum recoverable capacity.
