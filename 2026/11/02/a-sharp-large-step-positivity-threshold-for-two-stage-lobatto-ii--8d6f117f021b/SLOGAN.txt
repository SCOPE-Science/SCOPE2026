For unmodified two-stage Lobatto IIIC on an \(n\)-state unit-rate absorbing birth chain, endpoint nonnegativity holds for every nonnegative step when \(n\le4\), but for \(n\ge5\) holds exactly at zero and for \(h\ge\cot(3\pi/[4(n-1)])-1\). The sharp large-step recovery threshold grows like \(4n/(3\pi)\), despite the loss of positivity at arbitrarily small positive steps.
