For the direct lexicographic three-flow decoder driven by binary van der Corput points, explicit nilpotent matrix generators attain the otherwise upper-bounded logarithmic loss: at \(N_k=(5\cdot4^k-2)/6\) steps the \((1,3)\) error is exactly \(-(2k-1)h_k^2/2\). Thus the known \(O(h^2\log N)\) estimate is order-sharp for this decoder, not merely a byproduct of its proof.
