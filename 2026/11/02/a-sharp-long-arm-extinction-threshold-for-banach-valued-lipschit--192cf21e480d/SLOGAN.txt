For a metric star with \(m\ge3\) unit arms and one arm of length \(R\ge1\), the exact sharp real Banach-valued one-point Lipschitz extension constant has a two-phase formula, switching at \(R=m/(m-2)\). Beyond this threshold the optimal permutation-invariant extension discards the long leaf entirely, and the constant freezes at \(2-2/m\).
