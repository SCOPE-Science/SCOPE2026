# Review of the longitudinal anticorrelation threshold

## Correctness — PASS

The complete-reset planar active Brownian generator closes the stationary moments of \(\cos\Theta\), \(\cos^2\Theta\), \(X\), \(X\cos\Theta\), \(Y\sin\Theta\), and the two coordinate squares. Conditioning the coordinate regression on the initial state gives the exact two-time covariance formulas. The longitudinal sign is the sign of a strictly monotone bracket \(V_x+vJf(h)\), so no more than one zero can occur. An exact cubic factorization gives the one positive parameter threshold (Descartes' rule and endpoint signs); the resonant case is a continuous limit. The rational reentrant certificate and standalone numerical checks agree with the proof. Brownian simulation does not replace any infinite-time or all-parameter reasoning.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Shee, arXiv:2409.06920v1/v2, gives the exact same full-reset trapped active-Brownian model, stationary displacement moments, and time-dependent *one-time* moments. Its Appendix D is particularly close because it gives the stationary longitudinal variance; these formulas are explicitly credited. Its orientation-memory discussion refers to orientation relative to the initially prepared heading, not the stationary two-time longitudinal position autocovariance and its sign. Kumar–Sadekar–Basu, arXiv:2008.03294v1, address complete resets without a harmonic trap. Chaudhuri–Dhar, arXiv:2005.14234v1, address harmonic confinement without resetting. No inspected material directly states or dominates the exact time-lag sign-classification theorem, cubic threshold, anisotropic sign contrast, or resonance/reentrance statements. A standard Markov-regression derivation is available from the known model and one-time moment formulas, and broad regenerative correlation theory remains a plausible priority risk. Database absence is not a proof of uniqueness; these concerns warrant independent verification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The central observable is the stationary position time-lag covariance, relevant for temporal experimental tracking and active-matter memory. The proof produces a sharp all-lag zero count and a complete sign criterion over positive parameters, not just a local perturbation or an arbitrary parameter slice. The unique threshold for \(k\ge a\), independent of activity and translation noise, is a structural boundary; the change from logarithmic to reciprocal critical-lag divergence at \(k=a\) is an observable mode resonance. The nonzero-temperature reentrant example and the strict transverse positivity show why equal-time variance and static kurtosis do not determine the effect. Negative long-lag correlations may be extremely small. The claim is not extended to other protocols, interactions, or all possible reset laws.
