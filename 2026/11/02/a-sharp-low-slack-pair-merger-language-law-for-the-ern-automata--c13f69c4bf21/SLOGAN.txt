For a classical \(n\)-state Černý automaton, the complete merging-word count for any pair remains \([z^t](1-z)^{-s}(1-2z)^{-1}\) through a sharp slack window \(0\le t\le\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\), where \(s\) counts idling positions along the unique shortest pair merger. At the very next slack, the hardest pair acquires exactly one additional word through a unique upward jump.
