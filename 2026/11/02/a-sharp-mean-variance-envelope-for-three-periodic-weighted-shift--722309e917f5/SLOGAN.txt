For three-periodic Hilbert-space weighted shifts, fixing the sum and squared pairwise differences of the three weight magnitudes determines a sharp two-phase lower envelope for numerical radius: its minimizer switches from two equal positive weights to a weight-zero boundary at normalized variance \(1/2\). The sharp quantitative gap above the arithmetic mean is \((1/\sqrt2-2/3)\,Q/S\), with equality at the uniform triple or the balanced two-edge boundary.
