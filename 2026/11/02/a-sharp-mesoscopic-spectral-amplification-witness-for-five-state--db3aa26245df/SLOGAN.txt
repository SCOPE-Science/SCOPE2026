In the vanishing slow-rate limit, a five-state directed Markov cycle with two bottlenecks has a sharp mesoscopic-to-zero-frequency power-spectrum amplification of \(12/5\) among observables supported on its three fast states. The explicit optimizing observable yields the rigorous five-state global oscillation-efficiency lower bound \(\eta_5^*\ge7/12\), without asserting the unknown global optimum equals this value.
