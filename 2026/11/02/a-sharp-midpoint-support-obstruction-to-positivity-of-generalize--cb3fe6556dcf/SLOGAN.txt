A reflection-symmetric generalized Hilbert matrix can have strictly positive entries and be bounded self-adjoint yet fail operator positivity whenever its defining measure has a gap around the midpoint. Distant two-coordinate compressions violate Cauchy--Schwarz at the exact exponential rate \(-\log(2\sqrt{s(1-s)})\), where \(s\) is the support point nearest \(1/2\); the midpoint point mass itself produces a positive Gram matrix.
