For the \(q=0\) minimal random walk with symmetric positive two-point step sizes, the centered self-normalized noise has uniform Gaussian relative tails on every range \(x=o(n^{p/4})\), sharply beyond the general logarithmic guarantee. At \(x=c n^{p/4}\) the Gaussian-tail ratio instead converges to \(\mathbb E\exp[-c^4/(12W_p)]\), a strictly subunit Mittag–Leffler mixture whenever \(c>0\).
