# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For \(0\le b,c<1\), \(z>0\), direct matrix multiplication yields \(\det M=bc\) and \(\operatorname{tr}M=1+bc-(2+b+c)z+z^2\). Applying all three strict quadratic Jury inequalities gives exactly \(0<z<2+b+c\) and \(F(z)>0\). The discriminant substitution with momentum deficits proves the sharp \(3+2\sqrt2\) threshold. Signs at \(z=0,2\) locate both instability-band endpoints below \(2\), and evaluations at \(\lambda=-1\) classify the unit-circle boundary and the interior unstable root. The exact rational recurrence with \(b=0,c=9/10,z=3/2\) diverges from rest. An independent self-contained rational verifier passed 6,400 parameter triples, 36 recurrence updates and five contrast checks. No finite experiment is used to establish the universal claim.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated residual historical risk

The primary 2026 manuscript arXiv:2604.14108v1 studies fixed-momentum mean-square dynamics and the resulting fixed-parameter scalar threshold; arXiv:2610.09912v1 treats the heavy-ball method at arbitrary but fixed tunings. Neither inspected treatment gives the two-periodic coefficient-product criterion or the universal ratio of momentum deficits. Jin–Huang arXiv:2404.03218v1 uses varying momentum under a specific adaptive selection rule for inverse problems, not arbitrary alternating coefficients. Closest published mathematical-record results concern constant momentum, heterogeneous simultaneous momentum buffers, cyclic *block* updates, or general two-periodic *first-order* recurrences; their statements do not logically imply the presented two-step heavy-ball contrast theorem. Search absence is not a proof of novelty; incomplete historical review of classical discrete Hill/switching literature is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem completely classifies an elementary yet practically motivated periodic-momentum stability problem, with a closed-form forbidden step-size band and a sharp uniform contrast separating stable schedules from those allowing exponential divergence. The strict distinction between individually stable choices and their unstable alternation is relevant to schedule safety. The explicit rest-initialized witness and arbitrarily small absolute coefficient variation near one are substantive boundaries, not merely a reformulation of the classical single-parameter Schur test.

## Scope and residual risks

The proof is exact but restricted to a scalar quadratic, nonnegative momentum values below one, fixed step, and precise period two. Finite tests are supplementary. A stronger equivalent older special-case result may exist in switched-systems or discrete-Hill literature; the accessible full-text comparisons do not exhaust this field. Independent external validation has not occurred.
