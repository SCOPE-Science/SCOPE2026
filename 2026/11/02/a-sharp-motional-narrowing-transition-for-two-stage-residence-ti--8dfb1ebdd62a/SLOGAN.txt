For symmetric stationary frequency switching with Erlang-two residence times, the spectrum changes from one central line to exactly two side lines at the unique positive root of \(y^4+11y^3+10y^2-24y-32=0\), where \(y=(\Delta/\kappa)^2\). Relative to exponential switching with the same mean residence time, the required detuning is greater by the exact factor \(\sqrt{2y_*}=1.7518015\ldots\); no three-peak intermediate regime occurs.
