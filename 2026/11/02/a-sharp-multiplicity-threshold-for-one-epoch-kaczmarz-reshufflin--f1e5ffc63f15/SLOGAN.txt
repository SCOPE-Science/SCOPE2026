For unrelaxed Kaczmarz on \(m-1\) repeated planar constraints and one exceptional constraint, uniformly reshuffling the rows is worse than IID sampling after one epoch exactly when the squared cosine \(k\) exceeds a unique threshold \(\kappa_m\) for every \(m\ge3\). The threshold is \(1/2\) for three rows but shrinks as \(m^{-(m-1)}/(1-e^{-1})\); fixed-angle excess risk ratios approach \(e^{1-k^2}\), and the reversal also occurs with pairwise distinct rows.
