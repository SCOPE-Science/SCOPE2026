For the two-site stochastic heat equation, positivity-preserving geometric-Brownian/heat splitting has a strictly decreasing sharp mean-square noise threshold \(v_*(h)/b\), falling from \(3\) at vanishing timestep to \(2\) at large timestep. Thus a continuous-time mean-square stable system can become unstable under large positive, positivity-preserving numerical steps, with an exact rational second-moment witness.
