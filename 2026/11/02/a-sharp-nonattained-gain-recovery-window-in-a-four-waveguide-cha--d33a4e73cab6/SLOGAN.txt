In a four-mode waveguide chain with balanced end gain/loss and total link strength \(J=2a+b\), a genuinely recovered real-spectrum gain interval exists exactly when \(2<a^2/b^2<1+\sqrt2\). Its width is strictly less than \(J(2-\sqrt3)/(2\sqrt2+1)\), and the sharp supremum is unattainable because approaching it collapses the intervening broken-symmetry phase.
