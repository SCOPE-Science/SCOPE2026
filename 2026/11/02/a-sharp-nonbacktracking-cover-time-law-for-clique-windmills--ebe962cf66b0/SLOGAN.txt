On a windmill made of \(n\) copies of \(K_{m+1}\) sharing one hub, with fixed \(m\ge3\), genuine nonbacktracking exploration has a Gumbel vertex-cover law with scale \(2m(m+1)n/(m+2)\). Its expected cover time is asymptotically \((m+1)/(m+2)\) times that of ordinary simple random walk, even though both walks have the same mean hub-excursion length.
