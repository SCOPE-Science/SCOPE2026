A two-state index-one port-Hamiltonian DAE has an energy-contracting regularized Strang step whose fixed-step singular limit traverses a full circle instead of approaching the exact DAE flow. The sharp lower accumulation value of its regularized-energy error is \(\min(e^{-h/r},1-e^{-h/r})>0\), despite convergence of the unsplit regularized solution.
