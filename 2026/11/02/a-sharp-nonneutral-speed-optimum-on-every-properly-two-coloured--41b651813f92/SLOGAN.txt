On a properly two-coloured Moran star, a red mutant introduced at the red centre has fixation probability \(1/(\ell^2+1)\) independent of habitat advantage \(r\), yet its conditional mean fixation time is exactly \(A_\ell r+B_\ell+C_\ell/r\) with \(C_\ell>A_\ell>0\). Thus for every \(\ell\ge2\), fixation is fastest at a unique nonneutral advantage \(r_\ell^*=\sqrt{C_\ell/A_\ell}>1\), and increasing advantage past that point slows it.
