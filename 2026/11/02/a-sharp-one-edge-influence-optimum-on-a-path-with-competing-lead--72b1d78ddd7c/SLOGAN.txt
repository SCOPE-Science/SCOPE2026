On a path with two opposing fixed-opinion leaders, the globally best additional edge among *all* missing links necessarily joins the opinion-one leader to a follower about \(\sqrt L\) steps from the opinion-zero endpoint. The exact gain is \(a(L-a)^2/[2(L+a(L-a))]\), and that single link pushes the mean follower opinion from \(1/2\) to \(1-1/\sqrt L+O(L^{-1})\).
