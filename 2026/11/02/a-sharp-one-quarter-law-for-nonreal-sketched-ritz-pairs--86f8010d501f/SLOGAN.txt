For two-dimensional sketched Rayleigh–Ritz with a rank-one residual and uniformly oriented fixed-length cross-coupling, the probability of nonreal Ritz eigenvalues is at most \(1/4\), sharply attained by balanced residuals at coupling-to-gap ratio \(\sqrt2\). A positive-definite three-dimensional family retains exactly this \(1/4\) probability under arbitrarily small whole-space sketch distortion, even though the trial plane is embedded exactly.
