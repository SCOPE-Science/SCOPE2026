In the one-cell cold homogeneous mode of the five-stage VA-II particle-in-cell splitting, modified-energy preservation coexists with a sharp finite-time Riccati obstruction at \(h=\sqrt{8/3}\,R/|e|\). Before that barrier, the physical energy increases by the exact positive amount \(C_0e^2h^4/[8R^2(1+h^2e^2/(8R^2))^2]\), even though the modified energy is exactly conserved.
