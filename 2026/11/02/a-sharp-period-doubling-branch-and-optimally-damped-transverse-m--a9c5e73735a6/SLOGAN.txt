Every even discrete Huygens ring has an exact alternating two-cycle for \(h>1/2\). In the common-phase quotient it attracts for \(1/2<h\le\pi/4\), including nonlinear attraction at the endpoint where every Floquet multiplier equals one. Its linearized two-step spectral radius is minimized at a unique ring-size-dependent coupling, with minimum \(\bigl[(1-\sin^2(\pi/n))/(1+\sin^2(\pi/n))\bigr]^2\).
