After phase opposition loses stability at \(h=1/2\), every even discrete Huygens ring has an exact attracting alternating two-cycle for \(1/2<h<\pi/4\), with a sharp full-transverse Floquet stability boundary. The fastest local two-step contraction is attained at a unique ring-size-dependent coupling and equals \(\bigl[(1-\sin^2(\pi/n))/(1+\sin^2(\pi/n))\bigr]^2\).
