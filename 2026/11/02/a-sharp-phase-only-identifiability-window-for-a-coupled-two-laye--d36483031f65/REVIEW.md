# Scientific review: phase-only exchange ambiguity in a two-layer climate model

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is a full analytic all-positive-parameter theorem for the deterministic two-layer linear thermal model, not an extrapolation from numerical experiments. Substitution of the sinusoidal solution gives the inverse transfer exactly. Rationalizing its fractional-linear interlayer term gives the open semicircle with positive real displacement. The derivative of its positive phase lag factors into a strictly increasing quadratic in positive exchange; this establishes a unique positive turning point and proves strict decreasing/increasing branches and their endpoint limits. A phase-slope quadratic and branch count give every alias multiplicity and exclusion of the two open-endpoint limits. For a bounded exchange interval, sign changes of the same strictly increasing derivative polynomial give the exact strict frequency window, with injectivity at equality. Injectivity of the full inverse transfer proves distinct amplitudes for a phase twin. The standalone exact-rational checker independently replayed 149742 assertions. Finite enumeration is not asserted to prove the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS within examined literature, with residual historical-priority risk

The model and its transfer function are **known**. Held et al. (2010) describe the two-temperature-box equations; Geoffroy et al. (2013), Appendix D, explicitly derive periodic-forcing transfer, gain, phase, and frequency-domain Bode curves **with fixed physical parameters**. These do not themselves state the exchange-parameter semicircle, the phase-only coupling fold, its complete two-to-one inverse image classification, or the strict forcing-frequency window for a bounded unknown exchange. These statements follow by additional parameter-direction analysis rather than by simply restating a prior Bode plot. Fredriksen and Rypdal (2017) establish linear multibox impulse responses and stochastic frequency-domain scaling but do not classify fixed-frequency phase-only exchange ambiguities. Cummins, Stephenson and Stott (2020) estimate stochastic box-model parameters using surface-temperature and flux time series with state-space likelihoods, so their broader observation scheme does not establish single-frequency phase-only identification. Scientific-result record searches for harmonic climate two-box transfer, coupling phase, and inverse ambiguity gave closest results concerning ENSO feedback and unrelated phase-inference models, with no identified dominating claim. An earlier equivalent network-impedance or thermal-circuit classification remains possible despite these checks; the present review is not an independent priority certification.

## Value — PASS

The question is motivated by a concrete measurement design issue: with all other coefficients known, can one infer deep-ocean heat exchange from the lag of an imposed periodic signal alone? The theorem gives a sharp yes/no frequency criterion for **every** prespecified positive coupling range, proves that the obstruction is a two-way fold rather than uncontrolled nonidentifiability, and shows exactly how an accompanying amplitude observation resolves it. This separates measurable information in phase-only versus calibrated complex responses in a recognized climate energy-balance model. It is a mathematical benchmark, not a claim that the idealized experiment can be performed with precise enough climate observations.
