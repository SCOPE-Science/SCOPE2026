In a sinusoidally forced two-layer climate energy-balance model, the surface phase lag has exactly one strict minimum as interlayer exchange varies; a single observed phase can therefore correspond to two distinct positive exchange coefficients. For exchange constrained to \(\kappa\in[a,b]\), with \(0<a<b\), this ambiguity occurs at a chosen forcing frequency exactly within the open interval \(\Omega(a)<\omega<\Omega(b)\), where \(\Omega(s)=\sqrt{((C+D)s^2+2\lambda Ds)/(CD^2)}\). The inverse complex response traces a semicircle, and including amplitude removes the two-way ambiguity.
