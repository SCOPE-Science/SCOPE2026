On the \(k\)-th power of an \(n\)-vertex path, with fixed \(k\ge2\), independent blue seeds of density \(p=c n^{-1/(2k)}\) zero force with asymptotic probability \(1-\exp(-(2k-1)c^{2k})\). The factor \(2k-1\) counts distinct minimal interior forcing patterns, and a single legal initial force always completes the entire path power.
