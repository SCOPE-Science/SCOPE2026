For reciprocal-power omission with \(n-1\) facilities, the worst-case social-cost ratio is infinite for \(q<1\), equals the previously known \(n/2\) at \(q=1\), and for every \(q>1\) is the attained root of \(2(R-1)R^{q-1}=(n-2)(q-1)^{q-1}/q^q\). Although increasing the exponent improves the sharp welfare bound, every \(q>3\) is manipulable on a three-agent line instance; the known inverse-square guarantee remains prior work.
