For a symmetric continuous-time Markov star with arbitrarily priced edge rates, the unique allocation minimizing the worst mean first-hitting time keeps every rate equal until the largest price \(p\) exceeds \(k\) times the combined price \(P\) of the other \(k-1\) edges. Beyond that sharp boundary, only the premium edge slows, and the exact improvement over equal rates is \((\sqrt p-\sqrt{kP})^2/B\) under budget \(B\).
