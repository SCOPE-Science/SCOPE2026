On \(K_{m,m}\) with a designated perfect matching, continuous determinant-optimal conductance design deletes the entire matching exactly when its per-unit price exceeds the other cross-edge price by the factor \(m(2m-3)/[(m-2)(2m-1)]\). This critical surcharge is only of order \(1/m\), and the matching-to-other-edge conductance ratio has the sharp scaling limit \(\max\{0,1-\gamma\}\) at relative price \(1+\gamma/m\).
