At order \(121\), the Paley and Peisert graphs have identical strongly regular parameters and agree on all first-order graph sentences of quantifier rank at most four, but a rank-five sentence distinguishes them: the Paley graph is 4-existentially closed and the Peisert graph is not. The first 4-existentially closed Peisert graph occurs at order \(361\), where every four-vertex adjacency pattern has at least ten realizations, and ten is sharp.
