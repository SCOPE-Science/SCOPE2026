For the positive compound-symmetric matrix \(\alpha I+\beta uu^\top\), every intermediate-rank optimal truncated SVD is nonnegative exactly when \(\beta\ge(n-2)\alpha/2\), yet a strictly positive nonnegative-factorizable optimum exists at every rank. Arbitrarily small positive-matrix perturbations can force a unique SVD truncation with an order-one negative entry while the Frobenius cost of enforcing nonnegative factors tends to zero.
