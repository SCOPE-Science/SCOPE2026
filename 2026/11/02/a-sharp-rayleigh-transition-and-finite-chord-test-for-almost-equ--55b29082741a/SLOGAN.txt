For independently uniform nodes on the circle, the smallest relative adjustment of equal quadrature weights that exactly matches \(1,\cos\theta,\sin\theta\) has a finite chord-normal formula and a sharp critical law: \(\Pr(T_m\le c/\sqrt m)\to1-\exp(-4c^2/\pi^2)\). This identifies the precise random-node success probability for positive near-equal first-harmonic quadrature beyond general high-probability existence guarantees.
