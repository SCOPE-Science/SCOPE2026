For a fixed nilpotent Jordan matrix of partition \((n-2,2)\), \(n\ge5\), the variety of commuting nilpotent \(m\)-tuples in its centralizer is irreducible through \(m=2\) and has exactly two components starting at \(m=3\). Over every \(\mathbb F_q\), its exact count is \(q^{nm}+q^2(q+1)(q^{(n-1)m}-q^{(n-2)m})\).
