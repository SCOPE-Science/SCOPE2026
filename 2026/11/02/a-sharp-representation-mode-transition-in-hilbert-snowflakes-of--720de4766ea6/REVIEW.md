The verified claim is instead a uniform all-eigenspace inequality identifying the second Johnson representation as uniquely critical from the exact sharp index, plus endpoint rank and exponential asymptotics. Searches of published material and mathematical research records returned no equivalent or dominating statement, but unindexed older Johnson-scheme calculations remain a historical risk. Generic spectral machinery alone does not state the sharp cutoff or prove all competing representations are lower.
