In the first diameter-three Kneser family \(KG(3k-2,k)\), every order \(k\ge8\) has an exact Hilbert-snowflake threshold controlled by the second Johnson representation, with an explicit exponentially small critical exponent and an exact endpoint dimension drop. The control is genuinely sharp: at \(k=7\) the third representation already obstructs negative type while the second remains strictly negative.
