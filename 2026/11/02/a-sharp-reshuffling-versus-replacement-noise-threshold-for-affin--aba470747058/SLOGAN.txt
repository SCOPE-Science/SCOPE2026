For same-sample stochastic extragradient on two equal-skew affine saddle operators with opposite offsets, random reshuffling has lower or equal mean-square noise than independent sampling at every epoch for all offsets exactly when every nondimensional rotation frequency obeys \(\eta\omega\le1/\sqrt\kappa\), where \(\kappa>1\) is the extrapolation-to-update step ratio. Between that threshold and the individual-step stability limit \(\sqrt{2\kappa-1}/\kappa\), reshuffling is strictly worse on the active rotation modes; for the published affine ratio \(\kappa=4\) a stable planar example has an exact stationary-noise penalty of \(1521/971\).
