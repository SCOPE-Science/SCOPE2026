For the four-dimensional quantum complete intersection \(A_\lambda=\mathbb F_q\langle x,y\rangle/(x^2,y^2,xy-\lambda yx)\), every nonexterior parameter \(\lambda\ne-1\) has the same graph of nontrivial commuting algebra retractions: \(K_{q,q}\sqcup2q(q-1)K_1\), whereas the exterior boundary has \(q(q+1)/2\) disjoint copies of \(K_{q,q}\). The complete endomorphism counts additionally distinguish exterior, commutative nonexterior, and generic quantum parameters.
