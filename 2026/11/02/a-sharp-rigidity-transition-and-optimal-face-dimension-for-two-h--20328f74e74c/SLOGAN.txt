For fastest symmetric Markov mixing on the Boolean cube with moves of Hamming distance at most two, **global uniqueness holds exactly in dimensions \(2,3,4\)**, and from \(d=5\) the translation-invariant optimizer set has local affine dimension \(\binom{d}{2}-1\). Every optimum in dimensions \(d\ge4\) still has zero holding and fixed per-coordinate flip marginals \(2/(d+1)\), even though infinitely many unequal-edge optima coexist with the uniform walk.
