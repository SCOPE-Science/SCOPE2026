Two roads can have strictly increasing travel time in every stochastic scenario yet admit three risk-averse Wardrop equilibria, two stable and pure. In a two-scenario affine model, strict coexistence is possible precisely above the sharp mean-variance coefficient \(4(v+d)/(\Delta(2H-\Delta))\); an exact rational instance exhibits bifurcations at \(\gamma=11/10\), \(7/5\), and \(45/32\).
