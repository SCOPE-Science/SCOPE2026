For an iid-uniform periodic Matérn covariance Gram matrix, Fourier truncation has an exact finite-sample squared-Frobenius error with a diagonal/off-diagonal crossover precisely when the number of sites is comparable to the number of retained Fourier modes, \(N\asymp m^d\). A dimension-free relative-variance bound makes the crossover self-averaging under every joint sample-size/cutoff scaling and gives an explicit critical constant.
