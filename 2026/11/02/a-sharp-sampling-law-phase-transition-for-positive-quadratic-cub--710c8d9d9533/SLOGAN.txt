For independent Chebyshev-arcsine nodes, the failure of positive degree-two quadrature with target moments \((1,0,q)\) has an exact sampling-law phase transition at \(q=1/3\), with a sharp \(\sqrt m\) prefactor on the low-variance side and a pure exponential prefactor above it. For uniform integration the failure is asymptotic to \(0.880406\ldots\sqrt m\,(0.608173\ldots)^m\), improving the known uniform-node exponential base \(2/3\).
