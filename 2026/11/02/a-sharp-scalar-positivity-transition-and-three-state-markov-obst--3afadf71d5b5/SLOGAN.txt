For the Huo–Su fourth-order L-stable two-derivative integrator, the same threshold \(C=5/183\) that cancels the leading stiff-decay term exactly separates globally nonnegative from eventually negative scalar stability factors. At and above that threshold every two-state continuous-time Markov chain remains endpoint-positive for any step, yet a fixed three-state pure-birth chain has a negative transition entry at step \(12\); below the threshold two states already suffice.
