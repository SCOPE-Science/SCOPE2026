For binary shifts with exactly \(k\ge2\) one-bits, the variance of the digit-sum change has an explicit pair-interaction formula, and the familiar consecutive-block minimum is isolated by the sharp positive gap \(1-2^{1-k}\). The next variance level is exactly \(5-3\cdot2^{1-k}\), attained only by inserting one zero immediately after the first or immediately before the last one-bit of a consecutive block.
