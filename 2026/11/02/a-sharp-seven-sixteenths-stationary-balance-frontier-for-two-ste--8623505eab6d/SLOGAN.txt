For the optimized A-stable two-step stiff integrators, unconditional probability-simplex preservation on an irreducible two-state Markov chain holds exactly when \(1/3\le\kappa\le(1-m)^{-1/2}-1\), where \(m\) is the smaller stationary mass. Consequently, such an integrator exists precisely when \(m\ge7/16\); the result requires every independent pair of probability-vector starting histories and every finite positive timestep.
