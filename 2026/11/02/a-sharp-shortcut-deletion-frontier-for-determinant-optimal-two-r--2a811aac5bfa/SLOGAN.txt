On a two-route network with one direct shortcut, maximizing the weighted spanning-tree determinant under unequal route prices gives a unique conductance allocation—even when every edge is adjustable separately. The shortcut is present exactly below an explicit quadratic price threshold \(r_c\); at equal long-route prices \(p=q=s\), the threshold simplifies to \(r_c=ab\,s/(a+b-1)\), and the determinant advantage vanishes quadratically at the transition.
