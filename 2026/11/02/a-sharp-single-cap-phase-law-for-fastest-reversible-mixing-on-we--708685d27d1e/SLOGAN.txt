For any finite reversible birth–death chain with positive node masses, arbitrary priced edge conductances and one capped link, the exact unique budget-optimal spectral gap has a sharp two-phase law: either the familiar uncapped path profile survives or a single quadratic gives every new optimal rate. In the severe-bottleneck limit the gap is \(U(1/M_L+1/M_R)+O(U^2)\), independently of prices and total budget.
