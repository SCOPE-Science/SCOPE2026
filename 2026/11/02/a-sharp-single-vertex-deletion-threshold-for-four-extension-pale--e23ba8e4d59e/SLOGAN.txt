For each odd prime power \(q\equiv1\pmod4\), every single-vertex deletion of the Paley graph \(P_q\) agrees with the countable random graph through first-order quantifier rank five if and only if \(q\ge89\) and \(q\ne125\). The sole later exception \(q=125\) has a four-point pattern with exactly one witness; an exhaustive finite census and a uniform character-sum bound prove the sharp threshold and exceptional hole.
