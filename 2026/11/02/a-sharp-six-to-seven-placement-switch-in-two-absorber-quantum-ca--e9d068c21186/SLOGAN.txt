For coherent single-excitation capture on the triangular graph \(T_n=J(n,2)\), two equal absorbers capture the same fraction \(5/\binom n2\) whether adjacent or disjoint, but the optimally tuned conditional lifetime favors adjacent placement at \(n=5,6\) and disjoint placement at every \(n\ge7\). Exact rational lifetime functions also reveal rate-dependent placement reversals only at \(n=6,7\).
