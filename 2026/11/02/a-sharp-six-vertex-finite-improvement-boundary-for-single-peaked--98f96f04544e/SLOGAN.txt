Among all two-color single-peaked jump Schelling games on connected complete bipartite graphs with at most six vertices, an improving-response cycle exists exactly on \(K_{3,3}\), with three agents of one color, one of the other and two vacancies, when \(\Lambda>2/5\). This sharp graph-size and preference-peak transition holds for every allowed utility shape, while all other cases in the stated range have the finite improvement property.
