For centered two-atom gradient noise, curvature-normalized clipped SGD with cap \(S/N\) enters a finite reflecting random-walk lattice from every real start and has an exact geometric stationary law. The sharp skewness window \(p-1/2\asymp N^{-1}\) produces an exponential-tilted uniform limit; at symmetry, stronger clipping reduces stationary iterate variance but drives sample-mean variance cost up quadratically in \(N\).
