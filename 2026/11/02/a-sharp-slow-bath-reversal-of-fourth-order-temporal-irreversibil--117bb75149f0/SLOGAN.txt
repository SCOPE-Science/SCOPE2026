In an overdamped viscoelastic tracer whose position resets but bath memory survives, a fourth-order two-time arrow-of-time statistic is initially positive for every bath relaxation parameter. When the bath ratio exceeds the sharp threshold \(\gamma_*=3.06121757563033\ldots\), its slowest exponential mode becomes negative, forcing a genuine positive-to-negative time-lag reversal despite the absence of inertia.
