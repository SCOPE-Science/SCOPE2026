For two uniform points in a shrinking centrally symmetric convex body, the probability that their Euclidean unit-ball spindle captures the center has a sharp \(\varepsilon^{d-1}\) law whose coefficient is exactly a \((3d-1)\)-st radial moment. Among all equal-volume bodies, the Euclidean ball uniquely minimizes this coefficient; a quantitative radial-variance deficit measures the gap, and there is no universal upper bound.
