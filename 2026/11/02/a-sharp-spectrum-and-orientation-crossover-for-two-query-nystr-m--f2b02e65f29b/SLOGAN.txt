For the same two-matrix-vector-product budget, a Gaussian rank-one Nyström sketch followed by a Rademacher residual probe beats two-probe Rademacher Hutchinson on a real \(2\times2\) positive-definite matrix exactly when its eigenvalue ratio and eigenvector orientation cross an explicit sharp boundary. The critical condition number is approximately \(3.072767951539\), and, beyond it, rotating the same spectrum can reverse which estimator has smaller variance; this reversal persists after appending a sufficiently small positive spectral tail in every higher dimension.
