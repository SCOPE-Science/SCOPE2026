The modal classification is established analytically by differentiating the exact rectangular convolution, locating every stationary point in a bounded interval, and reducing it to intersections with one scalar odds-ratio curve. The log derivative of this curve has either zero or two stationary points. Strict convexity of \(z/(e^z-1)\) establishes the unique storm-duration cutoff, and four exact rational signs at \(D=\log2\) prove the two-crest witness.

Reproducibility: with standard Python 3, execute `python3 checker.py` from this directory. Scope limits: The theorem assumes one rectangular rain-excess pulse, linear independent parallel two-reservoir paths, strictly positive routing rates and mixture weights, and no baseflow.
