For each \(t\ge2\), counting-free \((t+1)\)-variable logic sees exactly three possible phases among full Steiner systems \(S(t,t+1,v)\) and their one-point deletions: full, punctured at \(v=2t+2\), or punctured above that threshold (with absent phases omitted). The phase classification holds at unrestricted quantifier depth, and \(t\) variables cannot distinguish any of these structures.
