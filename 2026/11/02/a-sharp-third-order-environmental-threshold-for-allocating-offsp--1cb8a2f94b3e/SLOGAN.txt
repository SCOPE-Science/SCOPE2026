When each pair of three habitats has independent favorable years, pairwise covariance still cannot always decide whether a lower-yield habitat deserves any offspring. The exact logarithmic-growth inclusion frontier is \(b=a(3a+4)/[4(a+1)+ta^2]\), where \(t=4\Pr(X_1=X_2=X_3=1)\) is invisible to the pairwise data.
