For three equispaced atomic singularities with masses \((a,1,1)\), all connected components of the strict inner-function sublevels are classified by exact critical heights: the topology is \(3\to2\to1\) precisely when \(0<a<1\), and \(3\to1\) otherwise. The intermediate window collapses at equal masses with the sharp law \(h_+(a)-h_-(a)\sim\frac89(1-a)^{3/2}\).
