The canonical upper-half-plane Bergman Gram kernel for real quadratic differential evaluations \(f+a f'+b f''\) is zero-free exactly in the sharp asymmetric cone \(-\frac{3+\sqrt{30}}{14}a^2\le b\le\frac{\sqrt{30}-3}{14}a^2\). Outside that cone it has exactly two distinct accessible scalar zero levels, while at its nontrivial boundary both zero levels collide into double real, unattainable roots.
