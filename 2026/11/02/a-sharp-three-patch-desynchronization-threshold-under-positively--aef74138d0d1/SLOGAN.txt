In an equally regulated three-habitat chain, strictly positive environmental covariances do not ensure that dispersal initially synchronizes the endpoint populations: a two-shared-driver environmental model has the sharp sign threshold \(\delta_c=(5\sqrt{10}-14)/2\). Unlike the universally positive two-habitat initial response, the three-habitat response below this threshold decreases before eventually converging to perfect synchrony, and an explicit positive-definite example has a certified unique interior minimum.
