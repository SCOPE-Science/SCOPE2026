Genuine variable-coefficient BDF4 on the three-periodic grid \((1,R,3)\) has a sharp Floquet threshold at the unique positive root \(R_*=59.78365454\ldots\) of an explicit degree-seven polynomial: it is parasitically damped below the threshold, neutral at equality, and exponentially unstable above it. In the wider singular family \((1,R,s)\), the sharp large-\(R\) stability boundary occurs at \(s=2\).
