# Scientific review: exact unary evidence-logic obstruction theorem

## Correctness — PASS

The quantification includes every finite or infinite set of formulas generated by propositional variables with weak negation and classicality alone. A signed core's two bits are independent unless a positively asserted circle constrains the core of its argument. The necessity proof uses precisely bivaluation clause v6; sufficiency is the explicit global assignment of core pairs, extended through the entire binary language using FDE positive/negative recursion. No compactness theorem, hidden congruence assumption or finite-experiment extrapolation is required. The syntactic distinction between a circle applied to a double negation and a circle applied directly is maintained. Exhaustive finite tests cover 153 rooted forests, 129,444 premise sets and 2,048 representative literal-syntax sets. Remaining correctness risk concerns interpretations that change the semantic clauses or add propagation rules, explicitly outside the theorem.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Grandinetti–Rodrigues (2026, arXiv:2610.24203v1) explicitly establish the classicality explosion rule and full-language decision method. Carnielli–Frade–Rodrigues (2024, arXiv:2412.10588v1) give the related general tableau. Those results imply the impossibility of each displayed triple but do not state its converse for **arbitrary unary theories**, complete minimal-unsatisfiable-core classification, or an exact sharp three-Helly theorem. Searches of mathematical finding records, titles and equivalent search terminology found no direct covering statement. Closest indexed results on variable-free modal KB, cardinality syllogistic and finite-chain preconditionals have different objects. Full-text availability for an earlier alternative-semantics article was limited, and priority under alternate terminology remains a real risk; search failure is not treated as a novelty proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Arbitrary nesting of the non-truth-functional classicality operator might appear to permit longer inconsistency certificates. The theorem proves that it does not: every incompatible collection, including infinite ones, contains a *complete* and *minimal* triple with one local classicality witness. The three-Helly property is tight, and the proof gives an input-linear recognizer with a concrete minimal certificate. This is a meaningful structural refinement of general proof search in a natural reduct of a currently studied evidence logic, not a routine counting exercise or isolated counterexample.
