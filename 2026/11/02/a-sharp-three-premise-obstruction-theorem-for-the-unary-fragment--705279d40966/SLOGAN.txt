For the \(\{\neg,\circ\}\)-fragment of minimal evidence logic, every unsatisfiable theory—even an infinite one—contains exactly the familiar kind of three-premise obstruction: a positive and negative assertion about the same syntactic core, together with a positive classicality assertion about that core. This gives a sharp three-Helly theorem, a complete classification of minimal inconsistent unary theories, and a linear-time satisfiability test despite arbitrary nesting of classicality.
