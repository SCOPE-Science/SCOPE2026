For a rank-one matrix with five nonzero columns, exact volume sampling of two columns has the sharp universal amplification-tail envelope \(\sup_X\Pr\{\|X_S^\dagger X\|_F^2\ge t\}=1\) up to \(t=5/2\), then \(3/t-1/2\) up to \(t=3\), and \(3/(2t)\) thereafter. The phase change is attained by two distinct positive squared-column mass patterns even though every such matrix has the same mean amplification \(5/2\).
