For Kamijima–Marumo–Takeda's one-step TV-APG on three-periodic strongly convex scalar quadratics sharing the stationary minimizer zero, the exact three-round Floquet stability threshold is \(s=7/64\): acceleration is locally unstable below the threshold even with its prescribed initialization, while it is locally exponentially attracting above it. On the identical objectives, ordinary proximal gradient is globally contracting for every \(0<s<1\), with three-round factor \(16s/25\).
