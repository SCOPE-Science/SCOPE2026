Reversing any periodic crowding calendar in the constant-growth Beverton–Holt model leaves the entire autocorrelation sequence of reciprocal population density unchanged. Yet with three pairwise-distinct seasonal crowding values, the phase-averaged saturating population response \(x/(1+qx)\) strictly distinguishes the two calendar orientations for every \(q\ge0\), with its sign determined exactly by \((b-c)(b-d)(c-d)\); three seasons are minimal.
