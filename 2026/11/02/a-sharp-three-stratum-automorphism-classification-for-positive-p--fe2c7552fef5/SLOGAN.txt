For the positive-parameter quaternion-core family \(A_0(a,b,c,d)\), the full real automorphism group is \(O(2)\) when \(a=b=c\), exactly \(C_2\) when two of these coefficients agree, and trivial when all three differ. The fourth positive coefficient \(d\) changes no isomorphism type within a fixed \((a,b,c)\) triple.
