# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The first n arrivals can be assigned to distinct agents, preferring an unassigned recipient who values the item at b, with the final item going to the earliest previous a-recipient. A later a-valued singleton necessarily valued that first low item at a, while b-valued singleton holders can remove one of the two goods and match the other value. For n=2 three levels, a complete first-good c/a/b and second-good a/non-a case split maintains a singleton valued at least one two-good component. For four ordered levels a<b<c<d, start (c,c), then (d,b) relative to first winner; if the second good goes to the first winner, reveal (a,a), otherwise reveal (d,c). Each final allocation violates terminal EF1. The independent enumerator checks n=2..4 binary profiles, all 729 ternary two-agent profiles and the adversarial leaves, but the unlimited and real-level claims follow from the case proofs.
