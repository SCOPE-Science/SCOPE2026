Among finite commutative rings, the unitary Cayley graph has the unrestricted three-variable first-order theory of the random graph exactly for products of at least two finite fields, each of order at least three; no such graph is four-variable equivalent to the random graph. The exact two-anchor witness formulas additionally classify which of these graphs lose the extension property after a single vertex deletion, while the common three-variable theory includes graphs of unbounded chromatic number.
