Optimal total Roman labelings of \(K(n,k)\) come from \(k+1\) disjoint \(k\)-sets, as implied by previous domination results. Their token-sliding graph has no edges for \(n<k(k+2)\), becomes a union of cliques \(K_{k+2}\) exactly at \(n=k(k+2)\), and is connected for all \(n\ge k(k+2)+1\); the degree is \((k+1)\binom{n-k(k+1)}{k}\).
