For a fixed susceptible population in the symmetric finite nonlinear \(q\)-voter model, simultaneous increases in conformity group size and balanced zealot counts have two explicit sharp likelihood-ratio cutoffs: the stationary susceptible polarization is smaller below the first, larger above the second, and has an interior relative-likelihood peak between them. The cutoffs follow from a strictly increasing ratio of two logarithmic kernel integrals, yielding strict convex-order comparisons in both ordered regions.
