For the fixed-point-free symmetric cosine interval map of Lawnik–Moysis–Volos, a unique analytic threshold \(c_*(a)\) separates zero, one neutral, and two primitive two-cycles throughout \(1/4\le a<1/2\), \(0<c<1/2\). The two cycles emerge with exact square-root separation, one repelling and the other attracting; no other primitive two-cycles exist in this region.
