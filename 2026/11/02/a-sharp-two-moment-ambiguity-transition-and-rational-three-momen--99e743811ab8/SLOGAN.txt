For a uniformly sampled finite circular cylinder sidewall, the normalized fourth Euclidean distance moment has a sharp two-to-one inverse regime \(26/19<\mathbb E[D^4]/\mathbb E[D^2]^2<3/2\), although the feasible range extends to \(12/5\). Adding the sixth moment yields an explicit rational reconstruction of radius and height, with a sharp positive separation between the two ambiguous branches.
