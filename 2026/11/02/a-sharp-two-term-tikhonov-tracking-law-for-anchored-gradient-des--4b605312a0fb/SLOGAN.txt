For the standard anchored-gradient schedule on every invertible finite-dimensional monotone linear problem, the residual has the sharp vector limit \(\sqrt{k+\gamma}\,Mu_k\to\gamma Lb\), even on strongly convex quadratics. Beyond the leading Tikhonov bias, the discrete iterate trails its instantaneous regularized zero by exactly \(\frac{\gamma L^2}{2(k+\gamma)}M^{-2}b+o(k^{-1})\), a nonzero second-order effect for every nonzero anchor.
