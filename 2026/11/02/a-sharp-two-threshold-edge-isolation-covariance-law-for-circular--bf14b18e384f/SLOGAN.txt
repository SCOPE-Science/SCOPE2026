In a uniform circular random geometric graph, the number of isolated vertices and the number of edges have strictly negative covariance at every radius for \(n\le5\), but for every \(n\ge6\) the covariance is positive on precisely one interval between two explicit radius thresholds. The lower threshold scales as \(4/(3n)\), whereas the upper is exactly \(1/2-1/n\); independent-edge graphs never exhibit this positive covariance.
