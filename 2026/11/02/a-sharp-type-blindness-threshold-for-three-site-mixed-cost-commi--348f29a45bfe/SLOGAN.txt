For three distinct agent-owned sites, a shortest pair of sites is optimal for **every** mixture of maximum-distance and average-distance facility costs precisely when \(3p\le q+r\), where \(p\le r\le q\) are the three metric distances. Every three-site line satisfies this condition, but a triangle with distances \((3,4,4)\) fails it even though both homogeneous cost types choose the same pair; four collinear sites can fail already between homogeneous types.
