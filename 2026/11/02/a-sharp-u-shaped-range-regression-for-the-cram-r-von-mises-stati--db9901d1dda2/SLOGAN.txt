For an i.i.d. uniform sample, the conditional mean of the classical Cramér–von Mises statistic given the sample range is a strictly U-shaped quadratic with its unique minimum at \(r=(2n+1)/(2(n+2))\). Yet its covariance with the range is always negative, exactly \(-(n-1)/((n+1)(n+2)(n+3))\), and their correlation decays as \(-\sqrt{45/2}/n\).
