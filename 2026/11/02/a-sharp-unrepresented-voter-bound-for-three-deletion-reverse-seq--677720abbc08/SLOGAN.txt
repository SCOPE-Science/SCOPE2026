With exactly three deletions, reverse sequential PAV leaves at most \(2(2k+1)/((3k+1)(k+2))\) of voters completely unrepresented while they agree on an omitted candidate, and that fraction is attained for every \(k\ge2\). Hence it guarantees justified representation through five seats, but a nine-candidate, 252-voter instance with six seats violates it under strict deletion choices.
