For Nikolov's all-degree Jacobi finite-increment inequality, the **first** Jacobi parameter must satisfy \(\alpha\ge\tfrac12\): below that sharp boundary, a fixed degree residue class produces an exactly quantified negative \(\sqrt n\)-scale defect at the origin. On the ultraspherical diagonal the admissible range is therefore **exactly** \(\alpha=\beta\ge\tfrac12\), while a phase-dependent necessary condition further restricts the critical boundary.
