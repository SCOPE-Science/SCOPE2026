With periodic booster schedules and exponentially waning immunity, two equal-sized disassortative contact groups have a unique vaccine-dose threshold at which fair allocation switches from a strict local maximum to a strict local minimum of disease-invasion potential. The exact threshold is governed by \(Q(u)=s(u)s''(u)/(2s'(u)^2)=(\alpha-1)/\alpha\), with \(s(u)=1-u(1-e^{-1/u})\); at maximal cross-contact its dimensionless value is \(0.2380367767\ldots\).
