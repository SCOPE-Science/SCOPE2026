Three circular arcs encode the phase values sampled by three distinct-orbit sessions. The proof computes the exact conditional Bernoulli two-success probability, separately bounds double and triple coverage when the union leaves a gap and when it fills the phase circle, and compares attaining clustered and evenly spaced arrangements. The crucial complementary-arc lemma is proved from the impossibility of three intervals with empty triple intersection having union shorter than two interval lengths. This includes every \(0<e<1\) and \(0<d\le1\) with boundary equalities. A standalone Fraction checker passed 15,540 rational assertions, but the continuous theorem rests on the proof.
