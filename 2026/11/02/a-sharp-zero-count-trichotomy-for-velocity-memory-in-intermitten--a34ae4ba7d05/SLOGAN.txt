A stationary intermittent swimmer with negative mean turning-angle cosine has exactly three sharp velocity-memory regimes: no positive-lag zero, infinitely many alternating zeros, or exactly one positive-lag zero. For perfect reversal and exponential running/turning with no rotational diffusion, the transitions occur at run-to-turn/turn-to-run rate ratios \(3-2\sqrt2\) and \(3+2\sqrt2\).
