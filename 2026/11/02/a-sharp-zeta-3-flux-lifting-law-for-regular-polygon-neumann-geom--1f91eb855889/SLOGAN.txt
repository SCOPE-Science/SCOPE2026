For regular polygons inscribed in the unit disk, the harmonic lift of the geometrically induced Neumann flux has the sharp energy limit \(N^3\|\nabla v_N\|_{L^2(D)}^2\to2\zeta(3)\|f'\|_{L^2(\mathbb S^1)}^2\). This isolates an exact \(N^{-3/2}\) normal-flux consistency benchmark, not a complete finite-element error constant.
