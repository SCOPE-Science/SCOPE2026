With exactly one repulsive tie in an otherwise attractive connected network, the synchronous bounded-confidence model has locally attracting equilibria where every edge stays receptive **if and only if** that tie belongs to a cycle. The antagonists' exact separation is the confidence radius multiplied by their unit-resistor effective resistance, \(cR_G(u,v)\), and the receptivity margin is \(c(1-R_G(u,v))\).
