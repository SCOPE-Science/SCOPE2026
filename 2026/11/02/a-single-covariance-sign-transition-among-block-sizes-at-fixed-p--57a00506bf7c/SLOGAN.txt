At fixed block count in a uniformly random set partition, the covariance of singleton count with the number of size-\(s\) blocks, normalized by the expected size-\(s\) count, rises strictly with \(s\) and crosses exactly once from negative to positive (with a sharply described two-block exception). The same exact finite-sample sign law holds for occupancy frequency counts conditioned on richness in any finite uniform-box scheme, independently of the total box count.
