For maximum unnormalized Laplacian spectral gap under a linear conductance budget on \(K_n\), pricing a matching above twice the ordinary-edge price makes every optimizer delete all marked edges if the matching has at least two edges; a single marked edge instead has the sharper threshold \(2(n-1)/(n-2)\). Below its respective threshold, the unique optimum uses equal conductance on every edge, and the exact spectral-gap value is a two-piece closed formula.
