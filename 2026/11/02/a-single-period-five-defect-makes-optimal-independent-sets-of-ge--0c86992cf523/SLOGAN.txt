For generalized Petersen graphs \(GP(n,2)\), optimum independent sets are frozen at every length \(n=5k\), but deleting one column from that period-five regime gives a single token-jumping and token-sliding cycle of length \(3n\) at every \(n=5k+4\), for \(k\ge1\). This exact connectivity change is witnessed by one mobile defect in the forced \(0ABBA\) pattern.
