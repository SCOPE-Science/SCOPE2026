In the binary best-shot public-goods game on a ring, one extra social tie spanning three existing links uniquely minimizes the number of pure Nash equilibria; an exact Perrin–Padovan formula gives the count for every possible tie. Surprisingly, adding a tie spanning only two links never reduces the equilibrium count for rings of six or more players and usually increases it; the optimally placed tie eliminates an asymptotic \(17.7008\ldots\%\) of the equilibria.
