An interacting Poisson-reset spin pair exhibits stationary Bell violation in the singular window \(R=q/(2a)\): its CHSH value tends to \(2\sqrt{1+[q/(1+q^2)]^2}\), peaking at \(\sqrt5\) when \(q=1\). Every \(a\ge2\) already gives strict CHSH violation at the finite choice \(R=1/(2a)\), although the fixed-rate strong-coupling limit is Bell-local at threshold.
