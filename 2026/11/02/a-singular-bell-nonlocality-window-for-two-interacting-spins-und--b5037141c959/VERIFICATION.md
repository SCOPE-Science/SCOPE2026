The exact generator and Pauli trace identities are derived in `RESULT.md` and checked by the standalone `verify.py`. The checker verifies the rational finite-rate witness, the complete matrix scaling limit, and direct energy-basis renewal at several finite parameters. The nonlocality statement is exact for projective CHSH measurements, with the Horodecki singular-value formula used as established theory. A finite sample calculation alone is not a proof of the parameter-uniform strict inequality; the polynomial bound in the proof supplies that argument.

The limiting number \(\sqrt5\) is an optimum **within the singular scaling family** only. It has not been proved to be a global finite-parameter supremum, nor to quantify device noise or statistical detection accuracy.
