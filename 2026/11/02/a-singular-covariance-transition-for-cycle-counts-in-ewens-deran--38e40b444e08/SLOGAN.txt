For Ewens-weighted derangements, different fixed cycle-length counts have covariance \(\theta^2(1-\theta)n^{-2}+O(n^{-3})\) whenever \(\theta\ne1\), regardless of the chosen lengths. At the singular uniform weight \(\theta=1\), this becomes an everywhere-nonzero, factorially small covariance whose sign alternates exactly as \((-1)^{n-r-s}\).
