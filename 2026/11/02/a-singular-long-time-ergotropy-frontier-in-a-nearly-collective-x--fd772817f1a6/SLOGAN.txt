An arbitrarily small departure from perfectly collective damping restores the same nonzero asymptotic ergotropy to an XX two-qubit battery, even though a perfectly collective bath can leave its cold Gibbs preparation passive. The exact slow-time limit \(\mathcal W\to2(J-h)[1-2p_\beta e^{-u}]_+\), with \(u=\gamma(1-\alpha)t\), yields a sharp delayed-work and observation-time frontier without asserting exact finite-time passivity.
