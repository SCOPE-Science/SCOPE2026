For a two-mode dissipative system evolved by unbiased reciprocal-probability random exact subflows, the unique sampling probability minimizing worst-case expected squared energy lies below \(1/2\), whereas the established generator-variance minimizer lies above \(1/2\). As the mesh tends to zero the energy-optimal probability is \(p_h\sim\kappa h\); the fast mode consequently has a nondegenerate Poisson-jump limit and a strictly positive mean-square error against the exact solution, despite attaining the exact semigroup's worst-case decay rate.
