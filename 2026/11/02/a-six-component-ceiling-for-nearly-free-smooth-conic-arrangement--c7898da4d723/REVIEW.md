# Scientific review: a six-component ceiling for nearly free smooth-conic arrangements

## Correctness — PASS
The precise assumptions are distinct smooth complex conics, only \(A_1,A_3,D_4,A_5,A_7\) singularities, and nearly free Jacobian-syzygy structure. The referenced bounds give \(7\le k\le8\) and force \(r=k\). Each singularity's Bézout intersection weight and local Tjurina number is stated in RESULT.md. Exact elimination of two identities and Zieliński's Theorem 1.1 yields \(271u+333n+462s\le2800k-458k(k-1)\). The right-hand side is negative at eight, while at seven it permits only three pairs \((u,n)\), none compatible with \(3u+5v=42+n\) modulo five. The executable independent integer-vector check passed. The proof does not depend on computing any infinite curve family.

## Value — PASS
The motivating article explicitly identifies seven and eight conics among four open component counts without known examples. Excluding both sizes settles the top half of that finite classification boundary by a geometric obstruction, sharpening its published uniform upper bound from eight to six. This is not a routine substitution: the borderline seven-conic case survives a simple sign test and requires eliminating all weak combinatorics via a modular arithmetic obstruction. No claim of existence for five or six conics is made.
