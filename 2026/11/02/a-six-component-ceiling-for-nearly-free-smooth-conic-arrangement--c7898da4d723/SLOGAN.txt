A nearly free union of distinct smooth plane conics over \(\mathbb C\) with only \(A_1,A_3,D_4,A_5,A_7\) singularities has at most six components. Combining the Jacobian-syzygy bound with an orbifold conic-arrangement inequality excludes eight conics immediately and seven by a modulo-five obstruction; existence for five or six remains unresolved.
