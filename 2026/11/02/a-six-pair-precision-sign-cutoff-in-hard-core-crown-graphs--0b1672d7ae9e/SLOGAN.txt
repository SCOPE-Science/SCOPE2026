In the hard-core model on the crown graph with \(n\) deleted matching edges, the inverse-covariance entry of a deleted pair is negative for \(n=3\), changes sign once as fugacity grows at each of \(n=4,5\), and is strictly positive at every fugacity for the first time at \(n=6\). These pairs remain strictly negatively correlated marginally and independent conditional on all other occupations, so the precision sign records a genuinely different linear dependence pattern.
