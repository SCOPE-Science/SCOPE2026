# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The symmetric characteristic-two cubic is explicitly transformed into the cone over the smooth four-coordinate cubic surface \(S\). Projection from its vertex classifies the reduced support of its line Hilbert scheme as \(S\) together with the twenty-seven dual planes associated with lines of \(S\). Normal bundles of cubic-surface lines give generic reducedness of the plane-dual components.

At the generic point of the vertex-line component, a Grassmannian chart reduces the line-containment equations to one base equation, one linear transverse equation, a quasilinear transverse quadratic, and a transverse cubic. After a geometric residue-field extension and linear elimination, independent tangent forms give \(u^2\) and the absence of a cubic-surface line through the generic point gives a nonzero \(v^3\) coefficient; the mixed term is removable in characteristic two. The exact Artin algebra is therefore \(k[u,v]/(u^2,v^3)\), of length six and Hilbert function \((1,2,2,1)\). A finite exact witness proves the required open condition is nonempty.

The standalone verifier exhaustively checked \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\) line populations, Frobenius pairings, and the \(\mathbb F_{32}\) witness. These checks are supporting finite certificates, not substitutes for the proof of all geometric components and all finite extensions. Mathematical boundaries and normalization are explicitly stated.

## Originality — PASS, with bounded historical priority risk

Earlier work already determines the cone structure, the smooth base surface, its twenty-seven lines and their splitting behavior; the previously described *plane* Fano scheme is not this *line* Fano scheme. The present claim does not count those antecedents as discoveries. A separate known classification of line-Hilbert thickening on a Hermitian **surface** cone over a plane curve has one transverse variable and a different local algebra, so it does not imply the present cubic-threefold result. The genuinely additional datum is the line-Fano scheme's generically **nonreduced length-six geometric local ring** along the vertex-line surface, together with its complete reduced-component and extension-field consequences. Those conclusions are not implied by the smooth-base classification, the twenty-seven-line theorem, or a finite étale plane-Fano scheme.

The complete accessible source on cubic surfaces over characteristic-two fields and the relevant Segre-cubic and characteristic-two model discussions were inspected. Searches for the exact object, the local normal form, multiplicity six, alternative terminology, and general Fano-cone formulations produced no covering primary source. Similar nonreduced Fano schemes of *other* geometric objects occur in published records and are not taken as establishing uniqueness here. Residual risks: an equivalent unindexed classical local calculation or a stronger general scheme-of-lines-on-cones theorem not identified through accessible sources.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Generically nonreduced components of Hilbert schemes encode infinitesimal deformations that disappear from geometric line counts. The sixfold vertex-line thickening is a structural invariant of a classical degenerate cubic threefold, not an arbitrary parameter slice or routine point-count increment. It connects an explicit Artin local algebra with the component geometry of the Fano scheme and explains how the 27 classical lines, their Galois action, and cone incidence contribute to the rational-line enumeration. The precise local normal form can be used to study deformation, Hilbert-scheme singularity, and degeneration questions. The existing geometric counts alone would not yield this scheme-theoretic statement.

## Limitations

Only the geometric-generic transverse scheme along the vertex-line component is classified. The full global nilpotent structure, intersections with the twenty-seven plane families, and possible special-point degeneracies are not settled. The explicit normal-form change of coordinates may require algebraic closure of the generic residue field. No priority claim is made for the cone model or the 27-line configuration. Historical literature that is not indexed or not available in complete form could contain an equivalent general argument. Independent audit and validation have not been performed.
