On the characteristic-two symmetric Segre cubic cone, the surface of vertex-passing lines is a generically sixfold nonreduced component of the line-Fano scheme: its geometric transverse local algebra is \(k[u,v]/(u^2,v^3)\). The reduced line scheme has twenty-eight geometric surface components and exactly \(16q^2+5q+1\) or \(28q^2+7q+1\) rational lines over \(\mathbb F_q\), according as \(q\) is an odd or even power of two.
