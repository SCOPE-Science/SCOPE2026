For independently uniform Boolean bootstrap transformations of a simple random walk, the quenched cross-covariance has a non-Gaussian \(O(1)\) random limit even as the diffusively scaled walks become independent Brownian motions. Its exact dyadic truncations are uniform modulo \(2\), while the limit has a real-analytic density, fourth moment \(19/7\), and a two-copy smoothing fixed-point law.
