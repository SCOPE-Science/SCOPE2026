For smooth characteristic-three genus-five curves \(y^2=x^{11}+ax^7+bx^5+cx^3+x\), the Jacobian \(a\)-number is always \(1\), while its \(3\)-rank is exactly \(4\), \(2\), or \(0\) according as \(T\ne0\), \(T=0\ne E\), or \(T=E=0\), where \(T=a^3b^3+c^3(a^4-b)-1\) and \(E=b^3+ac^3+a^{27}\). The rank-two stratum is a smooth irreducible divisor and the nonempty rank-zero stratum is a smooth transverse complete-intersection curve.
