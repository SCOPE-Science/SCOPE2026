# Mathematical review

**Correctness: PASS.** The explicit invariant-subspace decomposition proves the exact spectrum and positive definiteness. Averaging in the diagonal Loewner feasibility problem establishes global restriction to two orbits, and the strictly positive top generalized eigenvector forces every optimum to respect those orbits. A trace/determinant argument and monotone eigenvalue crossing prove both branches and uniqueness, including the joining boundary. A deterministic standalone experiment checks 126 admissible parameter sets, 1,799 numerical assertions, and 35 asymmetrically initialized optimization problems. The analytic proof, not the experiments, establishes all real parameters.

**Originality: PASS on examined evidence, with residual risk.** Van der Sluis gives Jacobi approximation bounds, Shapiro and Braatz–Morari give general diagonal-scaling frameworks, and Kolotilina addresses general optimally conditioned block matrices. No examined accessible full text gives this exact sparse paired-leaf family, its two-regime formula, or its exceptional Jacobi locus. General block criteria may potentially imply this specialization; the full Kolotilina and Shapiro papers were not accessible for a decisive whole-document comparison. The dense equicorrelation model is not this block-matching model, and a generic optimization procedure is not itself an explicit classification of the structured instance.

**Value: PASS.** Positive correlation matrices with a hub and disjoint paired interactions are a natural sparse block structure for diagonal preconditioning. The sharp switch between a pair-block balance and an antisymmetric-pair eigenvalue constraint changes the best rescaling, while the precise condition \(ma^2=b^2\) identifies when the ubiquitous unit-diagonal heuristic is genuinely exact. Both statements are useful in selecting and benchmarking simple diagonal solvers, and are not determined by eigenvalues of the unscaled matrix alone.

**Limitations and closest literature.** The result concerns positive couplings, identical pairs, positive definite matrices, and spectral condition number under positive diagonal congruence only. Related primary sources: van der Sluis (1969), Shapiro (1985), Braatz–Morari (1994), Kolotilina (2002). More specialized, inaccessible prior work could still cover the family; no independent priority or validation is claimed.
