On every even binary Rule-90 ring, exchanging only the first two update outputs preserves the entire characteristic polynomial but changes the nilpotent Jordan blocks from \((d/2,d/2)\) to \((d-1,1)\), where \(d=2^{\nu_2(n)}\). This yields the exact all-state transient-entry distribution, and on power-of-two rings increases the worst-case time to zero from \(n/2\) to \(n-1\).
