Grouping three symmetric random-trapezoid cells by coordinates of a uniform sphere point makes every quadratic integral exact on every sample path, without extra function evaluations. For \(C^3\) integrands this changes the sharp squared-error decay from \(n^{-5}\) under independent cell sampling to \(n^{-7}\), with exact leading constant \(\int_0^1(f''')^2/720\).
