For the octahedral platonic elliptic pencil, the full geometric group of sections has rank one, but its generator descends to a characteristic-zero field \(k\) precisely when \(-r\) is a square in \(k\). Hence the real Mordell–Weil rank jumps from zero for \(r>0\) to one for \(r<0\), while the two-torsion section survives in both cases.
