In fixed-width complete bipartite networks, a weak SIR infection rate of order \(n^{-1/2}\) produces a nontrivial directed cascade among the finitely many core vertices, while the number of infected peripheral vertices is of order \(\sqrt n\). The limiting core reachability and peripheral outbreak size are jointly explicit, and with two exponential-lifetime core vertices the chance of reaching the second core vertex tends to \(c^2/(1+c^2)\) at rate \(c/\sqrt n\).
