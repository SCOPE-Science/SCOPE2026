When all but one ball is drawn from a two-color urn, the composition maximizing the hypergeometric-to-binomial KL divergence is not the most balanced one: its minority class has size \(n/2-\sqrt n/2+o(\sqrt n)\). The best divergence exceeds the most balanced divergence by exactly \((1/6+o(1))/n^2\), with both scales established analytically.
