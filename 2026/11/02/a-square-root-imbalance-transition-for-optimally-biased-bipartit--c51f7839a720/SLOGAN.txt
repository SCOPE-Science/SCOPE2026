On every complete bipartite graph, first-visit-greedy control of the \(\varepsilon\)-biased random walk stochastically minimizes cover time and yields an exact maximum of two independent sums of geometric waiting times. For any fixed \(0<\varepsilon<1\), an imbalance of order \(\sqrt n\) produces a Gaussian terminal-class crossover and an explicit order-\(\sqrt n\) cover-time correction.
