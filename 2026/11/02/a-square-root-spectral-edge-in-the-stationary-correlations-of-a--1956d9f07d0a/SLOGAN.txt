For the Lorentzian Kuramoto order parameter under global Poisson resetting, stationary two-time correlations are strictly positive and have sharp exponential decay on either side of the synchronization threshold. At critical coupling the covariance instead has an explicit positive square-root spectral edge and the exact leading law \(\mathcal C(t)\sim[\mu'(\lambda)/(2\sqrt K)]e^{-\lambda t}t^{-3/2}\), including its nonzero amplitude.
