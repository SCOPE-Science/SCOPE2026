For every prime \(p=6m+1\), the Prym of the smooth bielliptic Picard curve \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+b\) is superspecial precisely when the explicit polynomial \(H_m=[z^{3m}](z^2+az+b)^{2m}\) vanishes. Its normalized polynomial has \(m\) simple smooth roots, giving exactly \(\lceil m/2\rceil\) geometric marked classes; otherwise the Prym is ordinary.
