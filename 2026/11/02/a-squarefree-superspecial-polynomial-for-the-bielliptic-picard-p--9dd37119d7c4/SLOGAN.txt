For every prime \(p=6m+5\), the smooth bielliptic Picard pencil \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+1\) has exactly \(m\) distinct superspecial geometric parameters, cut out by a separable degree-\(m\) coefficient polynomial, representing exactly \(\lceil m/2\rceil\) geometric isomorphism classes. At every other smooth parameter in these characteristics the Jacobian has \(p\)-rank two, while no member is superspecial when \(p\equiv1\pmod3\).
