At \(M_n=\lfloor n(\log n+c)\rfloor\), the complete stationary missing-coupon count for deterministic sliding expiration converges to a Poisson-equilibrium immigration–death chain. Its first stationary all-present time inherits the known \(M/M/\infty\) zero-hitting transform, establishing a critical-window process limit for the expiring model rather than a new queueing identity.
