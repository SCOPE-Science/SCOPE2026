For Brownian diffusion with Poisson resetting to the nearest site of an infinite regular lattice, the long-time fourth-cumulant growth is strictly positive at **every** positive resetting rate, although its no-reset Brownian value is zero. The mechanism is a sharp waiting-time coefficient-of-variation corridor with unattained endpoints \(2/3<\operatorname{Var}(T)/(\mathbb ET)^2<1\), derived from the model's exact renewal transform.
