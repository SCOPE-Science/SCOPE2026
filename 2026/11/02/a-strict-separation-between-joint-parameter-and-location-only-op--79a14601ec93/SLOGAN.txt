For three independent Poisson sensors at symmetric crosswind positions around a Gaussian plume, jointly estimating the unknown center, source strength, and ambient background always requires a strictly wider D-optimal transect than center-only c-optimal estimation at equal exposure. The two unique widths obey different monotone scalar equations, and as the positive ambient background tends to zero the D-optimal spacing diverges while the center-optimal spacing approaches \(\sqrt{2}\) times the plume width.
