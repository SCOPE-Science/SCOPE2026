A symmetric measure-preserving exchange at both endpoints of a Paley–Wiener concentration interval makes the improvement from reoptimizing the top eigenfunction strictly quartic: \(\mathcal D(E_m)-\eta(E_m)=W_sm^4+O_s(m^6)\) with an explicit, strictly positive reduced-resolvent coefficient. Consequently, the quadratic error in the known weighted-discrepancy comparison is genuinely sharp, not merely an artifact of its proof.
