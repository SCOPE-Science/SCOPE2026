Two independent uniformly chosen Lie subalgebras of the \((2n+1)\)-dimensional finite Heisenberg algebra generate the entire algebra with a nontrivial fixed-field limiting probability given by an explicit Gaussian-theta double series. The convergence rate is \(O_q(q^{-n/2})\), and the effect of the noncentral subalgebras is superexponentially small in \(n\).
