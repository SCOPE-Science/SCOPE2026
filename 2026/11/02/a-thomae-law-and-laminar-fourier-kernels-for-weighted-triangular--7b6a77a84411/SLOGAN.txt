For an odd-prime triangular self-similar measure with digit weights \(1-2t,t,t\), the integer Fourier-zero density is positive exactly on a countable dense set of cyclotomic weights, where it equals \(2/[p^{2(k-1)}(p^2-1)]\). The resulting Fourier convolution kernels form a laminar family: any two are nested or orthogonal, and the density is a Thomae-type function of the weight.
