For the six-dimensional strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) Lie algebra over any field, the reduced variety of commuting \(m\)-tuples has exactly three geometrically irreducible components for every \(m\ge3\), with dimensions \(4m\), \(3m+3\), and \(3m+3\); it is equidimensional precisely at \(m=3\) in this range. The two-tuple case has two components and was previously known in characteristic zero.
