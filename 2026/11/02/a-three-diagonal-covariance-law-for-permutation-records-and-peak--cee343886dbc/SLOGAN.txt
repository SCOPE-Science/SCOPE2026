For a uniformly random permutation, upper-record indicators and interior-peak indicators have a three-diagonal cross-covariance matrix: off-band entries vanish, yet each peak has strictly positive covariance with the total record count. The exact total covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(R,P)=1/12-1/[6n(n-1)]\) approaches \(1/12\), while the correlation decays on the scale \(1/\sqrt{n\log n}\).
