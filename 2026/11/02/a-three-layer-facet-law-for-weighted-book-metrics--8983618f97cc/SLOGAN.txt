For finite weighted book metrics with \(n\ge2\) triangular pages and common-edge length \(0<t\le2\), the real Lipschitz-free unit ball has exactly \(3\cdot2^n\) facets for every \(0<t<2\), dropping to \(2^n+2\) exactly when the common edge becomes redundant at \(t=2\). All dual extreme potentials lie on precisely three explicitly classified hub-difference layers.
