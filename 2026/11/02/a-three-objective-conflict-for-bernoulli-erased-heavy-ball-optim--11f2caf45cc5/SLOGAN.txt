With an unbiased gradient that is independently erased with probability \(1-p\), scalar heavy-ball momentum raises the sharp mean-square-stable step ceiling exactly when \(p>1/2\), and can lower the additive stationary-noise floor at a fixed step. Nevertheless, from zero initial velocity, the unique finite-horizon mean-square-optimal constant-parameter method for every horizon \(n\ge2\) is **no momentum** and step \(\gamma=p\), attaining the unobserved-gradient limit \((1-p)^n X_0^2\).
