A matched-pair deformation of crown-graph metrics has an exact three-phase real Hilbert distortion law: decreasing, then constant, then increasing, with thresholds \(t^2=2(n-1)/(n-2)\) and \(t^2=8(n-1)/n\) for every \(n\ge3\). Two correlated regular simplices attain all three sharp phases, and the entire plateau admits antipodal simplex embeddings.
