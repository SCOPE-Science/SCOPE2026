Balanced complete-bipartite single-peaked Schelling swap games undergo a sharp three-regime equilibrium transition at \(\Lambda=1/2\): there are exactly two stable colorings below it, every coloring is stable at it, and only midpoint occupancy colorings are stable above it. Every strict-improvement trajectory has an exact choice-independent length, with mean \(n/2-\Theta(\sqrt n)\) below the threshold and \(\Theta(\sqrt n)\) above under random initialization.
