For mixed monomial harmonic symbols \(z^p+\eta\overline z^{\,q}\) with \(p\ne q\), the dual Toeplitz self-commutator has exactly one eigenvalue of each sign in every nonnegative angular mode. Across the balance condition \(|\eta|=p/q\), the positive and negative tails change from quadratic/quartic decay to simultaneous cubic decay, with explicit \(\sqrt{13}\) constants and sharp Schatten thresholds.
