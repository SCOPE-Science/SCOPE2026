In the Fikioris--Fikioris inverse monoid of partial translations between intervals of \([n]\), the probability that two uniform elements commute tends to exactly \(3/10\). Asymptotically, almost every commuting pair has zero product in both orders; the mutually annihilating nonzero pairs admit the exact polynomial \(n(n-1)(n+1)(n+2)(n^2+n-1)/30\).
