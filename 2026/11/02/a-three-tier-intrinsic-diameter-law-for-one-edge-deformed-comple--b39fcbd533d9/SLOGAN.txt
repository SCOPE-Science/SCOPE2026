For a complete metric network with one edge of length \(\lambda>0\) and every other edge of unit length, the continuous diameter is exactly \((\lambda+2)/2\) for three vertices, \((\lambda+3)/2\) for four, and \(\max\{2,(\lambda+3)/2\}\) from five onward. Normalizing by the network's total length makes equilateral lengths uniquely optimal from five vertices onward, whereas the four-vertex ratio improves strictly by shortening the exceptional edge and the three-vertex ratio remains constant.
