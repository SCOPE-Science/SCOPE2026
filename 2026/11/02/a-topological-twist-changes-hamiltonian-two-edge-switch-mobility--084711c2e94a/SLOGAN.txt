For every \(n\geq5\), no Hamiltonian cycle of the cylindrical ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) admits a Hamiltonian-preserving two-edge switch, yet the Möbius ladder obtained by crossing its seams has a switch graph consisting of a star \(K_{1,n}\), with exactly two additional isolated vertices when \(n\) is odd. This determines the entire local reconfiguration graph, not merely the previously known number of Hamiltonian cycles.
