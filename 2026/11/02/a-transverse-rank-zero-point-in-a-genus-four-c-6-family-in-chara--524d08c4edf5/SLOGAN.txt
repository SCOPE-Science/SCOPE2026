In characteristic five, the genus-four hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\) has all four \(5\)-ranks from zero through three but never rank four. Its rank-one and rank-two parameter curves are smooth and geometrically irreducible on the squarefree locus, intersecting transversely at the **unique coefficient-marked rank-zero fiber** \((a,b)=(-1,2)\), whose Jacobian has \(a\)-number two.
