# Scientific review: additive-envelope collision law

## Correctness — PASS

The finite Alexandrov envelope is the minimum over the upward cone. For any finite nonempty cone and real-valued functions, the minimum of the sum equals the sum of the minima exactly when all functions have a common minimizer. With atomless independent valuations each minimum is almost surely unique. Recursive interleavings of uniform rank permutations give independent local minimum-block selectors, with block probabilities proportional to their sizes. Their nodewise agreement is equivalent to complete subtree-argmin signature agreement. The product follows exactly for every finite rooted tree and every integer \(k\ge2\). The rooted-star, rooted-path, and perfect-binary specializations follow by counting subtree sizes; the limits follow from convergence of the relevant products/series. A standalone checker performs 462 independent exact rational collision comparisons and direct semantic comparisons for the smallest shapes. Finite testing is supporting evidence rather than a proof for arbitrary sizes.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical-priority risk

The July 2026 paper *Affine modal propositional logic* gives the motivating lower-semicontinuous semantics and the general superadditivity inequality. Its accessible full text was compared across the semantic definitions, inequality, proof system, completeness and compactness portions: it does not establish a probability law for simultaneous equality over random valuations on finite rooted trees. Classical random-priority and permutation results give rank uniformity and recursive decomposition, but are not, in their examined accounts, an exact \(k\)-way collision theorem for modal envelope additivity on arbitrary fixed rooted trees. Searches for aliases involving lower envelopes, common subtree minima, matching priority signatures and random permutations returned no verified equivalent result. The closest semantic ingredient and the closest probabilistic ingredient are explicitly separated in the proof. A related old result under record-tree or priority-signature terminology remains possible; no independence or formal priority certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

The inequality \(Lf+Lg\le L(f+g)\) is a structural property of affine topological modal semantics, and equality is its natural sharpness question. The exact probability for simultaneous equality at all worlds is not obtained by treating vertices independently; the nested-cone dependence must be resolved. The theorem provides a complete distribution-free formula in a natural tree-shaped model class for any number of summands and demonstrates an exponential-versus-polynomial shape transition. The work is scoped to a mathematical semantic question rather than presented as a general modal completeness theorem or a classification of all partial orders.

## Residual limitations

The result does not address arbitrary overlapping-cone posets, dependent or tied inputs, or arbitrary distribution families. Classical permutation-tree priority literature may contain equivalent descriptions not indexed by modal vocabulary. Source comparison and proof review are not substitutes for independent audit.
