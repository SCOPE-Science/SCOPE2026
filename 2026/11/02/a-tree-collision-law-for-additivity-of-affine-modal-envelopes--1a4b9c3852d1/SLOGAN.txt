For independent atomless valuations on a finite rooted Alexandrov tree, the chance that the lower-semicontinuous envelope distributes over a sum of \(k\ge2\) atoms at every world is exactly \(\prod_v(1+\sum_{c\in\operatorname{Ch}(v)}|T_c|^k)/|T_v|^k\). The equality event has polynomial probability on paths and stars, but exponential decay on perfect binary trees.
