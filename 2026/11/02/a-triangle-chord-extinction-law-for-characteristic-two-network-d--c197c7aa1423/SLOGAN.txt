Adding one distance-two chord to a finite binary cycle produces a sharp arithmetic extinction law: at every length \(n=2^a+1\) with \(a\ge2\), synchronous neighbor-XOR is nilpotent of exact index \(n\), while every even length has only one transient update. For all other odd lengths, a single nilpotent Jordan block of size \(2^{\nu_2(n-1)}+1\) gives every first-periodic-entry shell exactly, over any finite field of characteristic two.
