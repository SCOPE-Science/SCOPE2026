For the isolated toric surface germs \(\mathbb C\{u,v^n,\ldots,v^{2n-1},u^nv,\ldots,u^nv^{n-1}\}\), the Lipschitz saturation fills exactly the triangular monomial gap \(1\le a,b<n\), \(a+b\ge n+1\). Its correction has length \((n-1)(n-2)/2\) while the embedding dimension remains \(2n\), giving an unbounded exact defect law beyond the previously published \(n=3\) example.
