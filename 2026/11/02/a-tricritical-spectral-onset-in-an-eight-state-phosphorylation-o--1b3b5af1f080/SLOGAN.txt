For a four-state phosphorylation cycle with an independent binary gate, the exact onset of a dominant nonzero-frequency spectral peak is controlled by a unique cycle-bias threshold: below it the peak emerges continuously, while above it the peak first overtakes zero frequency at a finite separation even with negative zero-frequency curvature. At the separating bias the leading peak frequency has a quarter-root, rather than a square-root, switching-rate onset.
