For the centered quadratic B-spline, the Zak transform along \(x=1/2\) changes from one simple frequency zero to a triple zero at the exact scale \(b=(99+2\sqrt{42})/39\), then to three simple zeros. The new pair is born with a square-root law and approaches the integer-scale limiting frequencies with a distinct \((3-b)^{2/3}\) law.
