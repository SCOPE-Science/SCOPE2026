# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The five elliptic quotient parameters are checked against the explicit decomposition in Carvacho–Hidalgo–Quispe. The Deuring polynomial factors into four distinct irreducible quadratics over \(\mathbb F_{17}\); all eight roots are consequently enumerated over \(\mathbb F_{289}\). Permuting the five branch labels reduces every pair of quotient supersingularity equations to a pair of distinct roots, so the 56-pair exact certificate is geometrically exhaustive. It gives 44 double-only and 12 fivefold points, with no triple-only or quadruple-only points. The direct full-field enumeration reproduces the counts. Simplicity of the Hasse roots proves pairwise transversality. Incidence and field-of-definition arguments prove all-extension formulas. The degree-of-two-power quotient isogeny preserves the entire \(17\)-divisible group and thus the \(a\)-number and rank.

## Originality
PASS, with residual historical-priority risk. Carvacho–Hidalgo–Quispe (arXiv:1507.02903) already give the five elliptic factors and an all-isomorphic golden-ratio configuration; Auer–Top (arXiv:math/0106273) already give the Hasse-root criterion, and Ohashi (arXiv:2604.18074) gives a more general existence theorem for superspecial genus-five curves. The claim does not assign novelty to those facts. The full smooth characteristic-seventeen Humbert arrangement, absence of multiplicities three and four, twelve-point unique marked orbit, and exact extension-field rank-stratum counts were not identified in the inspected material or mathematical-record searches. A historical parameter census not surfaced by those searches remains possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. An exhaustive, natural arithmetic-geometric stratification of the two-dimensional Humbert moduli family is obtained, including an isolated superspecial stratum and a gap in the possible Jacobian ranks. The answer is not an arbitrary finite slice: it determines every geometric pairwise divisor intersection and the number of marked rational parameters over every finite extension. The exceptional fivefold intersection has a natural classical golden-ratio interpretation; the result substantially refines that existence example.

The characteristic is exactly 17 and the five branch points are distinct. Counts are for marked parameters, not moduli isomorphism classes. Earlier published existence and component-isomorphism statements remain prior work; full priority is qualified by possible inaccessible or unindexed computations.
