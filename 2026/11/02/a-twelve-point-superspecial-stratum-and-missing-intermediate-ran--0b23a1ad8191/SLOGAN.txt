In characteristic \(17\), the five elliptic-quotient supersingularity divisors of the smooth marked genus-five Humbert family have \(440\) exclusive transverse double points and a single \(12\)-point orbit of fivefold intersections, with no triple-only or quadruple-only points. The Jacobian ranks are exactly \(\{0,3,4,5\}\), and the full rank stratification over every \(\mathbb F_{17^m}\) has explicit counting formulas.
