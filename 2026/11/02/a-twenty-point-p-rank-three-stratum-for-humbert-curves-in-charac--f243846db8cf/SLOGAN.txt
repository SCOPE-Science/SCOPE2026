In characteristic five, the Jacobians of genus-five Humbert curves have \(p\)-rank exactly \(3,4,\) or \(5\); the rank-three locus consists of precisely \(20\) transverse intersection points among five natural equianharmonic divisors. Those points form ten Frobenius pairs over \(\mathbb F_{25}\), and every finite extension admits explicit counts of each rank stratum.
