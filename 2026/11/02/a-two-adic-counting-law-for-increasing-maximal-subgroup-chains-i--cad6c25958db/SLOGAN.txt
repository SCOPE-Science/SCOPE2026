For every finite abelian kernel \(A\), the number of maximal subgroup chains of \(\operatorname{Dih}(A)\) with nondecreasing indices is exactly \(F(A)(2|A|-|A_{\mathrm{odd}}|)\), where \(F(A)\) counts sorted prime-index flags of the abelian kernel. In ordinary dihedral groups this becomes \(2n-n_{\mathrm{odd}}\); for odd squarefree \(n\) with \(k\) prime factors, such chains make up at most \(1/k!\) of all maximal chains.
