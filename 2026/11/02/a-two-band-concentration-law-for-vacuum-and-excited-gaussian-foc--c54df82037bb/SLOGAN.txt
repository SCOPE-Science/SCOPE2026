For simultaneous small-area concentration of the vacuum and the \(m\)-th Gaussian Fock number state, \(m=1\) has a unique optimal annulus at every area, whereas every \(m\ge2\) exhibits a sharp two-radius split: nearly optimal masks concentrate a fixed fraction of area near the origin and the remainder near an explicitly determined excited-state ring. The sharp robust coefficient strictly exceeds what any single concentric annulus can achieve, by a factor that grows exponentially with \(m\).
