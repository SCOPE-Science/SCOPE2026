The two-real-parameter Blaschke quotient \(\Gamma_{a,b}(z)=zB_a(z)/B_b(z)\) preserves circle orientation exactly on an explicit endpoint-constrained region with a removed, same-sign algebraic band. At \(|a|=\sqrt3/2\) the admissible parameter section bifurcates into two branches; the excluded band produces four angular turning points even though both diametral derivatives are positive.
