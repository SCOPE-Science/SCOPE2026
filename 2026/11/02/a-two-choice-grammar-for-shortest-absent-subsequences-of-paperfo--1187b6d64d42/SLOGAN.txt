For regular paperfolding prefixes of length \(2^k\), every shortest absent scattered binary subsequence is obtained by independently choosing one of two explicit six-bit tokens for each sixteen-bit block, then appending either final bit. This complete grammar gives shortest absent length \(3\cdot2^{k-3}+1\) and exactly \(2^{2^{k-4}+1}\) shortest absent words for all \(k\ge4\).
