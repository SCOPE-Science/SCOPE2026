For a complete bipartite random walk resetting to a fixed root with probability \(r\in(0,1)\), unequal coupon-collection rates create two asymptotically independent Gumbel cover clocks. When the rooted part has \(\lfloor(1-r)n+\lambda n/\log n\rfloor\) vertices and the opposite part has \(n\), the chance that the root-containing part finishes last tends to \((1-r)e^{\lambda/(1-r)}/(1+(1-r)e^{\lambda/(1-r)})\), exhibiting a sharp \(n/\log n\) imbalance window.
