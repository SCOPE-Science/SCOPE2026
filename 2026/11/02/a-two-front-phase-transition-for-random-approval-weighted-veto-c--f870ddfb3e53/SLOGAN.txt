In an independent binary-approval electorate, approval-weighted veto consent of a fixed candidate has a sharp large-electorate threshold \(p_*(m,q)=\max\{1/2,mq^{m-1}/(1+mq^{m-1})\}\). At the unique double-critical point \(p=1/2\), \(q=m^{-1/(m-1)}\), the limiting consent probability is \(1/4+\arcsin\sqrt{2/(m+1)}/(2\pi)\), giving exactly \(3/8\) for three candidates.
