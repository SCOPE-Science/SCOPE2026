For binary subsequence patterns with at most three runs, strong Wilf equivalence is exactly reversal/complement equivalence: a run-length multiset and one raw second moment at host length \(\ell+2\) recover the pattern up to those symmetries. The moment has a closed formula whose only order-sensitive term is \(-2ac\,\mathbf1_{\{b\geq2\}}\) for run composition \((a,b,c)\).
