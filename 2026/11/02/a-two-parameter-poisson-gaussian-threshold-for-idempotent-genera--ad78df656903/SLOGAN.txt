At the connectivity threshold of a balanced random bipartite support, a random Rees zero-matrix semigroup with its elementary-abelian group dimension tuned to the fluctuating cycle rank is idempotent-generated with limiting probability \(e^{-2e^{-c}}[1-\Phi(t)]\). The two factors represent independent Poisson isolated-vertex and Gaussian cycle-surplus obstructions; an exact finite conditional law determines the entire size distribution of the idempotent-generated subsemigroup.
