## Value (V) — PASS

Both class densities are natural network observables. Their difference has a smaller, nondegenerate scale than the consensus density; this theorem identifies the exact conditional fluctuation law, its non-Gaussian unconditional shape and a closed excess-kurtosis statistic across the full critical noise range. The formula remains valid under arbitrarily imbalanced growth of the two parts and adds information unavailable from the one-population density limit or a second-moment calculation alone.
