On a complete bipartite graph, noisy voters have an exact finite-size hierarchy of within- and cross-part correlations. At critical reset rate \(\theta/(1/a+1/b)\to\lambda/2\), their common opinion density has a \(\operatorname{Beta}(\lambda,\lambda)\) limit, while the scaled interpart density difference is conditionally Gaussian with variance \(P(1-P)/2\), giving positive excess kurtosis \(3/[\lambda(2\lambda+3)]\) for every \(\lambda>0\).
