In fixed-size Yule trees, a uniformly sampled leaf belonging to a cherry is on average exactly one edge deeper than a non-cherry leaf, with local depth–cherry covariance \(2/9\), independent of tree size. In contrast, the covariance between the whole-tree cherry count and the Sackin path-length sum is exactly \(-\frac{(n-3)(n+2)}{9(n-1)}\) for \(n\ge3\), and is strictly negative from four leaves onward.
