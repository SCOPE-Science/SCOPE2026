For a harmonically trapped two-dimensional particle that tumbles between four discrete directions, the radial density has two integrably divergent concentration shells, at \(\rho=1\) and \(\rho=\sqrt2\), separated by exactly one valley when \(0<\gamma/\mu<1/2\). At and above the sharp threshold \(\gamma/\mu=1/2\), the outer radial interval is strictly decreasing, even though the square-supported stationary planar density remains anisotropic.
