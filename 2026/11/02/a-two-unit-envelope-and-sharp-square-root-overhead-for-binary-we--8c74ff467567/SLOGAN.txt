For every \(k\ge2\), the shortest binary word containing every length-\(k\) word with exactly two ones as a scattered subsequence has length between \(k-3+\lceil2\sqrt{2(k-1)}\rceil\) and \(k-1+\lceil2\sqrt{2(k-1)}\rceil\). A two-residue marker construction attains this two-unit envelope, proving the sharp asymptotic overhead constant \(2\sqrt2\) without asserting a finite-length exact formula.
