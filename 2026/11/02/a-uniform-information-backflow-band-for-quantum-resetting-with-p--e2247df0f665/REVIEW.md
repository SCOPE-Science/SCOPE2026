# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS (self-review).** The central claim is the quantified statement over every real \(\lambda\in[1/5,1/2]\), not merely a table of examples. The source equation gives an exact Kummer function for the qubit's coherence. The analytic Poisson superposition coupling provides an explicit Lipschitz extension from the rational centers to all real rates. A standalone exact-rational verifier establishes 786 local hypergeometric inequalities, checks their Taylor remainders and confirms their intervals cover the target band with strict backflow exceeding \(1/400\). Positive trace-preserving intermediate maps cannot increase distinguishability, so the final divisibility conclusion follows. Computation certifies only the finite inequalities and interval cover; it is not being used to infer any unexamined infinite-dimensional statement.

## Originality

**PASS (qualified best-knowledge, self-review).** The closest primary paper, de Mauro–Kulkarni–Majumdar (arXiv:2608.02297), already derives the exact hypergeometric factor for every energy gap and resetting rate and its asymptotic algebraic decay. Its accessible two-level treatment does not assert finite-time trace-distance revival or a uniformly certified continuum rate band; the band inequality needs an additional finite arithmetic proof and an all-rate coupling argument. Carollo–Wald (arXiv:2601.13367) studies resetting to a fixed reference state in discrete time, a materially different protocol: their genuine nonnegative reset probabilities contract state distinguishability, and their backflow example uses effective negative reset probabilities. Published results on random-telegraph dephasing concern different noise. Residual priority risk: an unlocated general theorem for the same memory-sampling channel, or implicit earlier numerical results, could cover the qualitative backflow; the known exact Kummer expression substantially simplifies deriving particular instances. No absolute priority guarantee is claimed.

## Value

**PASS (self-review).** A positive classical reset clock need not make the resulting quantum channel distinguishability-contracting if resetting chooses a state from its history. The five-pair sufficient testing scheme and nonzero **uniform** lower bound over a continuous dimensionless parameter band offer an operationally measurable, robust distinction from fixed-reference resetting. The result is not a parameter substitution or an isolated decimal example: its essential content is a certified continuum nondivisibility theorem. It does not determine the exact boundary of the revival regime.

## Limitations and evidence

The physical model requires a nonzero qubit Bohr frequency, positive Poisson reset intensity and uniform sampling of the full prior history. The numerical values of the five observation-time pairs are sufficient, not optimized. The 786 rational rows and independent checker are included in the same scientific package.
