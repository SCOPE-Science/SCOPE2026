Even with exclusively positive-rate Poisson resets, uniformly selecting a previously visited quantum state produces a certified revival of distinguishability: for every resetting-to-Bohr-frequency ratio in \([1/5,1/2]\), one of five fixed observation-time pairs increases a qubit trace distance by more than \(1/400\). A finite exact-rational certificate proves this for the entire continuous rate band, not just sampled parameters.
