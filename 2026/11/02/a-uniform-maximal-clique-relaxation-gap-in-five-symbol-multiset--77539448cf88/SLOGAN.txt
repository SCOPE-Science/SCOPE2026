For every \(n\ge8\), the five-symbol one-deletion profile graph has a feasible fractional assignment for **every** clique inequality with total mass at least \(\binom{n+4}{4}/5+4/3\), while its known maximum code size is only \(\lceil\binom{n+4}{4}/5\rceil\). Thus maximal-clique bounds have a uniform additive gap of at least \(8/15\) and cannot certify the already-known exact code size at any of these lengths.
