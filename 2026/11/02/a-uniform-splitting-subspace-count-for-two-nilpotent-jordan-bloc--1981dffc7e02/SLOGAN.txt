For every nilpotent operator with two Jordan blocks of lengths \(d+r\) and \(d-r\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), the number of two-dimensional \(d\)-step splitting subspaces equals \(q^{4d-4}\) for \(r=0\) and \((q-1)q^{4d-5}\) for every \(1\le r<d\). The imbalance-independent unequal-block count follows from a uniform finite-field Hankel nonsingularity law; its closed formula for \(r\ge2\) is the new assertion.
