In a four-patch landscape, the better of two alternative restoration corridors can switch **exactly once** as investment grows, even though both interventions increase connectivity. The reversal occurs precisely when \((\alpha^2-\beta^2)(a\alpha-c\beta)<0\), and the unique effort threshold is determined by \(t\Lambda_B(t)=-2b(a\alpha-c\beta)/(\alpha^2-\beta^2)\).
