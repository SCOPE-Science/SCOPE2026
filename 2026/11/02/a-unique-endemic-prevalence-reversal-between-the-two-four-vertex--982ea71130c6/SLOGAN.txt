The star and path are the only connected four-vertex trees with three edges, yet their deterministic NIMFA endemic-infection rankings reverse exactly once: the star has greater total infection for \(1/\sqrt 3<\tau<1/(2^{2/3}-1)\), and the path has greater total infection above that cutoff. The exact unique positive crossover is \(\tau=1/(2^{2/3}-1)\), despite the star's lower epidemic-onset threshold.
