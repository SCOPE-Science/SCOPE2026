For a frozen randomly composed public-goods group whose high-value environment persists with probability \(c\) and recovers with probability \(c^\beta\), the leading finite-size effect on stationary high-state frequency reverses sign exactly once as the cooperation probability varies. The unique crossover obeys \(\beta(1-p_\beta)=\log\beta-\log\log\beta+o(1)\), showing how sensitivity pushes the reversal toward near-unanimous cooperation.
