# Review of the phase-robust Gaussian heat Fourier coupling

## Correctness — PASS

The exact Gaussian characteristic function gives a diagonal cosine/sine covariance matrix. Its two entries cross uniquely on the negative-correlation interval, and positive correlation cannot improve the worst entry. This proves the unique minimizer and its exact variance for every \(q>0\), including degenerate endpoint couplings. The independent Gauss–Legendre verifier checks Gaussian double integrals, endpoint comparisons and supplementary finite grids; finite checks do not replace the proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result supplies a unique closed-form correlation setting for a natural phase-robust Fourier random-walk test, quantifies strict improvements over both standard endpoint designs for every positive noise level, and identifies a sharp \(q=\log3\) switch in the ranking of those endpoint strategies. It also quantifies the small-noise order improvement while preserving unbiasedness. This is a benchmark for two-sample one-frequency Gaussian coupling, not a claim about general particle or PDE solver performance.
