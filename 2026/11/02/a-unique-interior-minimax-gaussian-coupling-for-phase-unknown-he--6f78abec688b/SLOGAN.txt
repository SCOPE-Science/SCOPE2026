For a two-sample Gaussian heat estimator of an unknown-phase real Fourier mode, neither independent nor perfectly antithetic noise is phase-minimax: the unique optimal correlation is \(r_*(q)=-\log(2-e^{-q})/q\in(-1,0)\). Its sharp worst-phase variance \((1-e^{-q})^2/[2(2-e^{-q})]\) is strictly below both endpoint choices at every \(q>0\), and improves the small-noise independent-sampling variance order from \(q\) to \(q^2\).
