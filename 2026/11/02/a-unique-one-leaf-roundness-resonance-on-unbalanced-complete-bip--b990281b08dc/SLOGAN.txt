Attaching a single pendant leaf to \(K_{m,n}\) preserves its supremal negative-type exponent at every length only for \(K_{2,2}\); otherwise preservation occurs at exactly one tuned length when the leaf is attached to the larger vertex class, and never when it is attached to the smaller or equally sized class. The unique resonance length has a linear near-balanced growth law and a singular power-law decay in the strongly unbalanced regime.
