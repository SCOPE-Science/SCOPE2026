In a balanced two-block Gibbs permutation, the fixed-point indicators of any two distinct labels in the same block change from negatively to positively correlated at exactly one parameter value. The finite-size threshold is unique for every block size at least two and converges to \(1/4\), with the exact scaled covariance limit \(x(3x-1)/2\) for \(x=\sqrt\theta/(1+\sqrt\theta)\).
