For the centered quadratic B-spline, the Zak transform on the symmetry line \(x=0\) has exactly one conjugate pair of frequency zeros throughout \(2<b<8/3\), joining the rational frequencies \(1/2\) and \(1/3\). The pair emerges with a sharp square-root law at \(b=2\) and attaches quadratically at \(b=8/3\); a cubic reduction proves the classification for the entire scale chamber.
