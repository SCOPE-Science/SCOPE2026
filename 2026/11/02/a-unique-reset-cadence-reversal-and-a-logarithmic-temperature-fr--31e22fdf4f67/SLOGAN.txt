For a Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck reset cycle with any finite reset width, Poisson intervals yield a larger endpoint symmetrized-KL rate than equally frequent periodic intervals only above one sharp reset-rate threshold; below it the ordering reverses. As the reset width vanishes, the critical rate grows like \(b^{2/3}/(\log b)^{1/3}\), revealing a logarithmic boundary layer omitted by the singular-width approximation.
