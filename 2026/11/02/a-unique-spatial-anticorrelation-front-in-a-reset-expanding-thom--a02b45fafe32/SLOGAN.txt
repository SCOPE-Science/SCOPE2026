In a Poisson-resetting, self-similarly expanding Thomas–Fermi condensate, the stationary reset-age-induced covariance between central and local macroscopic densities changes sign at exactly one distance: positive inside, negative outside. Its threshold approaches \(x_0/\sqrt3\) for rapid resetting but diverges as \(2x_0/[3\alpha\log(1/\alpha)\log\log(1/\alpha)]\) for slow resetting, where \(\alpha=r/(\sqrt2\,\omega)\).
