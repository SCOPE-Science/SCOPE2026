# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The source-established radial decomposition into a one-dimensional frequency \(-1\) block and two-dimensional nonnegative blocks is applied to the weighted beta distribution. An exact two-step Jacobi recurrence gives the commutator \([F_m(t),F_m(t^2)]\) and the coefficient \(2(\alpha+2)\sqrt{\alpha+1}\,m^{-3}\). Third-order Taylor control in the weighted two-dimensional blocks makes the general remainder \(O(m^{-4})\). A twofold singular-value counting argument gives the \(j^{-3}\) law and exact weak-Schatten endpoint. The accompanying code passed 121 finite corroborative assertions. The infinity claims are proved analytically.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk
The 2020/2023 Barrera-Castelán–Maximenko–Ramos-Vazquez papers already provide the Jacobi matrix model and compactness-type scalar tail; this theorem explicitly credits them. The new statement is the nonzero universal boundary two-jet coefficient, quantitative spectral asymptotic and optimal Schatten threshold. The 2025 paper by Shi et al. addresses commutativity of a related 2-analytic Toeplitz family but only its abstract could be inspected, so priority remains uncertain. Searches of published result records for aliases, equivalent singular-value statements, and stronger weighted commutator coverage found no dominating result; absence in search is not a novelty proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact \(1/3\)-Schatten transition sharply measures a functional-calculus failure of commutativity that the general scalar-limit algebra does not quantify. It holds for an entire class of smooth radial symbols, depends on an explicit nondegenerate boundary invariant, and has a sharp polynomial-symbol witness.

## Limitations
This applies to the full order-two weighted polyanalytic disk Bergman space, with fixed \(\alpha>-1\), \(C^3\) radial symbols, and nonzero boundary determinant for sharp equivalences. The theorem does not classify vanishing-determinant pairs, higher polyanalytic order, or nonradial symbols. Independent validation has not been performed.
