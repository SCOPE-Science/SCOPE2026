For \(n\ge2\) polynomial interpolation nodes sampled with Vandermonde repulsion exponent \(\theta\ge0\), the random Lebesgue constant has a finite \(q\)-th moment exactly when \(q<\theta+1\), independently of \(n\). The same sharp threshold governs unconditional Gaussian observation-noise error at each fixed point, and for two nodes the full Lebesgue-constant tail is explicit.
