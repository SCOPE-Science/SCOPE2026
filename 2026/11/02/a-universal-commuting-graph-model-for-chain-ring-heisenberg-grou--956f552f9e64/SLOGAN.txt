For any finite commutative chain rings \(R,S\), the complete commuting graphs of \(UT_3(R)\) and \(UT_3(S)\) are isomorphic exactly when their residue-field sizes and nilpotency lengths agree. Consequently \(UT_3(\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z)\) and \(UT_3(\mathbb F_2[t]/(t^3))\) are non-isoclinic class-two groups of order \(512\) with identical commuting graphs, providing an explicit counterexample to a published class-two isoclinism conjecture.
