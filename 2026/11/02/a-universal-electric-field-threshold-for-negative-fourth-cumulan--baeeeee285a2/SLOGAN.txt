For an infinite dephased tight-binding quantum walk with symmetric incoherent jumps, the long-time positional fourth cumulant becomes strictly positive at every coherent hopping strength once the uniform Stark field satisfies \(|E|/(\gamma+2w)\ge\sqrt{(5+2\sqrt7)/3}\). Below this sharp universal field ratio, increasing coherent hopping crosses a unique threshold from positive to negative fourth-cumulant growth.
