For a resetting-driven diffusion law with feedback \(D=\alpha\Gamma^\beta\), the bistable stationary region terminates near \(\beta=1^+\) at a universal field cusp \(|h_{\mathrm{cusp}}|\sim\sqrt{1536/3125}\,(\beta-1)^{5/2}\) and diffusivity \(D_{\mathrm{cusp}}\sim(16/25)(\beta-1)^2\). An explicit limiting curve proves the local one-to-three stationary-branch transition without assuming dynamical stability.
