For synchronous binary neighbor-XOR on the Levi graph of any finite even-order projective plane, deleting a single incidence forces every state to zero in at most five steps, while adding a missing incidence produces exactly one fixed state, one seven-cycle, and four fourteen-cycles, with all states periodic by step two. Exact basin and entry-time counts follow from the plane's binary incidence rank; the period spectrum is independent of the plane and the edited pair.
