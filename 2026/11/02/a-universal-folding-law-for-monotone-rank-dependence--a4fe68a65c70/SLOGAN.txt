For any bivariate copula, inserting \(k\) independently randomized equal-width predictor branches preserves its full Chatterjee rank correlation exactly but divides each monotone directional component and Spearman's rho by \(k\). The lifted copula lies within \(1/(4k)\) of independence in uniform norm, sharply, showing a universal separation between total predictive dependence and order-constrained dependence.
