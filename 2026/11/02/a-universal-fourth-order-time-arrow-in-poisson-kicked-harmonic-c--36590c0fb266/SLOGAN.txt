In a Poisson-kicked harmonic particle, a centered fourth-order time-arrow correlation is exactly \(-\lambda\mathbb E[J^4]e^{-\gamma t}(1-e^{-2\gamma t})/(4\gamma)\), with a unique optimal lag \(\log 3/(2\gamma)\). Its profile determines the relaxation rate and aggregate fourth kick intensity, and survives addition of any independent stationary time-reversible observation noise with finite fourth moments.
