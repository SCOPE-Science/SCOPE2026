# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The exact claim reduces the Tjurina algebra of a separated sum to the cokernel of a Kronecker sum of multiplication operators. Every nilpotent block is realized as the raising operator on an irreducible complex \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\)-module, yielding the sum of weight-zero and weight-one coefficients. A lattice local central limit theorem supplies the sharp coefficient and handles the two parity regimes. The variance is positive if and only if the original analytic germ is not quasihomogeneous in the Jacobian-ideal sense. The plane-curve formula follows from \(f^2\in J_f\), and an explicit semiquasihomogeneous germ supplies a nontrivial example. Finite exact checks corroborate, but do not replace, these proofs.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest two-factor results are Zheng–Zuo, arXiv:2610.08290v1, Proposition 5.1, and Almirón, arXiv:2205.12806v1, Theorem 1. They determine pairwise Tjurina defects, not the exact iterated coefficient formula or the sharp asymptotic in the number of repeated summands. Targeted overlap queries of mathematical records and accessible related findings identified no equivalent asymptotic. Clebsch–Gordan theory and the lattice local limit theorem are classical ingredients; the scientific assertion is their precise application to repeated isolated hypersurface singularities. Residual risk: a more general earlier tensor-product asymptotic may imply this result without naming Tjurina numbers.

## Value — PASS
Repeated Thom–Sebastiani sums are a natural operation on isolated hypersurface singularities. The theorem resolves the asymptotic relation between two basic invariants, proving a sharp and universal inverse-square-root decay or exact equality dichotomy, with a computable leading constant. This is not another small-parameter computation: it describes every tensor power and its asymptotic across all complex isolated hypersurface germs and provides an exact closed formula for plane curves.

## Scientific limits
The result is restricted to complex isolated hypersurface germs and separated sums. The asymptotic holds for each fixed original germ, not uniformly across varying germs. Filtration-refined spectral data and positive-characteristic sums are not determined. Historical-priority assessment has not been independently confirmed.
