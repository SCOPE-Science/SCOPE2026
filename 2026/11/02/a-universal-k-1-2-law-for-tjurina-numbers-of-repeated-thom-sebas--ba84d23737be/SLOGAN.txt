For any complex isolated hypersurface germ \(f\) with nonzero class in its Milnor algebra, the Tjurina-to-Milnor ratio of the sum of \(k\) independent copies is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/(\pi\sigma_f^2 k)}\), with an explicit positive variance determined by the nilpotent Jordan blocks of multiplication by \(f\). Every finite \(k\) has an exact two-coefficient Laurent-character formula, and isolated plane curves admit a formula using only their Milnor and Tjurina numbers.
