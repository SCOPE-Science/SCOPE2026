Sharing a single random interpolation shift across every cell of order-two stratified-control-variate quadrature produces a universal non-Gaussian normalized error law for every nonaffine \(C^2\) integrand, with sharp limiting kurtosis \(101/27\). Independent cell shifts preserve the exact finite-sample variance but restore Gaussian limits, while a two-residual within-cell variance estimator makes the shared-shift error asymptotically Gaussian after studentization.
