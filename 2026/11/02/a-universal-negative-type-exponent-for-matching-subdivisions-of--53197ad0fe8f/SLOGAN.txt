Subdividing at least two disjoint edges of any unit-edge complete graph produces a graph metric with the same sharp negative-type exponent, \(p_*=0.706863663237331\ldots\), no matter how many original vertices or subdivided edges there are. At this universal threshold the exact zero-sum equality-space dimension is \(r-1\), where \(r\) is the number of subdivided edges, and the minimum Hilbert-snowflake affine dimension drops to the number of original vertices.
