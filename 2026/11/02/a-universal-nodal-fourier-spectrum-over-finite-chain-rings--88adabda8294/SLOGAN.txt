For the nodal cone \(xy=0\) over every finite commutative chain ring, the complete additive-convolution spectrum depends only on residue-field size \(q\) and length \(k\). Its exact rank is \(q^k[1+k(q-1)]\), except that residue-field size two creates one additional Fourier-null antidiagonal, subtracting \((k-1)2^{k-2}\) when \(k\ge2\); the only negative spectral level is \(-q^{k-1}\).
