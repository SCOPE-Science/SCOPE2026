Every real Hadamard sign matrix loses exactly \(2/[n\sqrt n+(n-1)\sqrt{n+2}]\) in its Schur multiplier norm when any single entry is flipped, independent of Hadamard type or defect location; the loss is asymptotic to \(n^{-3/2}\). The order-four specialization was known, while the uniform all-order identity has matching explicit real orthogonal witnesses and positive Gram certificates.
