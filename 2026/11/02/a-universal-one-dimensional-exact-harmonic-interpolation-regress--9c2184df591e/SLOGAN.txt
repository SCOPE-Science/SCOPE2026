For every dimension \(d\ge3\), exact-degree spherical harmonics sampled on the \(2^d\) hypercube vertices attain full antipodal-parity rank at degrees \(d\) and \(d+1\) but lose exactly one dimension at degree \(d+2\). The unique additional missing mode is the alternating Walsh character \(\prod_i\varepsilon_i\); an explicit all-dimensional harmonic-witness construction determines every evaluation rank through that degree.
