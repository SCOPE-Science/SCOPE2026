For harmonic functions on an annulus measured in the conformally invariant cylindrical metric, concentration on its logarithmic inner half has an exact complementary-pair spectrum and the sharp, modulus-independent defect \(\|T-T^2\|=1/16\). Its exponentially small spectral tails recover the annulus modulus, and this central cut is the unique concentric cut with complementary eigenpairs in every Fourier block.
