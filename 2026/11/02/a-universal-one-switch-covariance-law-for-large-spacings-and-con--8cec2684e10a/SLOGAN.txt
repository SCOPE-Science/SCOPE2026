For uniform spacings, the covariance between the count above threshold \(t\) and **every** strictly convex additive spacing statistic switches exactly once, from negative to positive. For Greenwood's sum of squares the switch is precisely \(t=2/(m+2)\), and at scale \(t=c/m\) the correlation tends to \(c(c-2)/(2\sqrt{e^c-1-c^2})\).
