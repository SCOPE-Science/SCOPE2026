For a symmetric two-patch, locally stable predator–prey linearization driven by spatially imperfectly correlated prey noise, stationary *local prey variance* is uniquely minimized at predator migration rate \(v_*=\sqrt{bc}/2\) in the absence of predator noise, independently of prey damping, prey movement and noise intensity. With predator noise, an exact radical gives the shifted optimum; perfect spatial prey-noise correlation instead produces a sharp finite-minimizer/nonattainment/indifference trichotomy.
