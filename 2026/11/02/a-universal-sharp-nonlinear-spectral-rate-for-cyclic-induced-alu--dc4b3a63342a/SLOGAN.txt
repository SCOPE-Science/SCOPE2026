For every nontrivial Kubo–Ando mean, induced Aluthge iteration of a strictly positive periodic bilateral weighted shift has a sharp worst-case operator-norm convergence factor \(\sqrt{1-4\lambda(1-\lambda)\sin^2(\pi/m)}\), determined only by the mean's derivative \(\lambda=f'(1)\) and period \(m\). The derivative \(\lambda=1/2\) is uniquely fastest, and the two-period case then converges supergeometrically.
