The three-state cyclic softmax map \(F_{\beta,\varepsilon}=((1-\varepsilon)I+\varepsilon P)\sigma_\beta\) loses stability through a supercritical invariant-circle bifurcation at the exact temperature threshold \(\beta_c=3/\sqrt{1-3\varepsilon+3\varepsilon^2}\), except at an explicitly excluded fourth-order resonance. Its radial normal-form coefficient is universally negative, \(-3/[4(1-3\varepsilon+3\varepsilon^2)]\), and at criticality all nearby nonfixed trajectories relax at the sharp rate \(n^{-1/2}\).
