In a reset Brownian ring with one symmetric membrane, every nonzero stationary membrane current yields exactly one permeability maximizing the interfacial entropy-production rate, even for arbitrary Borel reset distributions. The sharp dimensionless optimizer always satisfies \(0.385933275998194\ldots<\kappa_*\tanh(\sqrt{r/D}L/2)/(\sqrt{Dr})<1/2\), and its value decreases strictly with a single reset-source asymmetry parameter.
