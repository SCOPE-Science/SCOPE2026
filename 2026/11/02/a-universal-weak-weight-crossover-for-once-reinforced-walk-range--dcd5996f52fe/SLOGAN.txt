When the traversed-edge weight of a once-reinforced walk satisfies \(c_n\sqrt n\to\gamma\), both the full line and reflected half-line have the same exact limit for \(R_n/n\): \(\Pr(Z_\gamma\le r)=2\Phi(\gamma r/\sqrt{1-r})-1\) for \(0<r<1\), with \(Z_0=1\). If \(c_n\to0\) but \(c_n\sqrt n\to\infty\), the common limit instead becomes \(c_nR_n/\sqrt n\Rightarrow |N(0,1)|\), identifying \(n^{-1/2}\) as the sharp crossover scale.
