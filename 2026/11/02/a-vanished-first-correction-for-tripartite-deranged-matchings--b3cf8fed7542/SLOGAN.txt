For a uniformly random perfect matching of \(K_{2m,2m,2m}\), the probability of avoiding a fixed perfect matching is \(e^{-3/4}(1+3/(128m^2)+O(m^{-3}))\). The previously known Poisson-limit probability has **no first-order \(m^{-1}\) correction**: its first nonzero correction is a positive, explicitly determined second-order term.
