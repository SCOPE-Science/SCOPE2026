For real-valued Gödel-Kripke frames, possibility is witnessed under **every** valuation precisely when each accessibility row has finite positive superlevel sets; this is equivalent to product-topology continuity of its max-min operator, with exact finite-truncation error equal to the largest omitted accessibility weight. Necessity has the strictly stronger universal witnessing criterion of finite positive support, and the fuzzy star with edge weights \(1/n\) separates them.
