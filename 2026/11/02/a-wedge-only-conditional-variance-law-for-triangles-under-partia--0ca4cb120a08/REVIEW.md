# Review: wedge-only conditional triangle variance

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem fixes the entire induced graph, leaving every other edge independent Bernoulli. A full partition by inside-vertex count gives the three random triangle indicator types and deterministic internal triangles. Every unordered dependent pair shares exactly one *random* edge, and the six possible pair classes are exhaustively counted. Direct substitution derives the stated variance polynomial, and cancellation at fixed edge count leaves precisely the positive wedge coefficient. There are no asymptotic assumptions; boundary cases with zero or one outside vertex are covered by zero-count conventions. A separately implemented exact enumerator confirms all 612 cases described in `verify.py`, including the strict path/claw gap and equal-variance prism/bipartite example. A finite check is not substituted for the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Material comparison: Ellis–Ferry (DOI 10.1016/j.dam.2009.11.012) provides general *unconditional* copy-overlap variance formulas, and Kunegis (arXiv:1402.5835v1) specializes unconditional exact triangle variance. Neither inspected text supplies the explicit induced-revelation conditional variance law or its wedge-only dependence across observed graph shapes. Ginoza–Mugler (arXiv:0907.4680v1) studies correlations generated by network edge swaps, not independent-edge partial exposure. Comparison with related mathematical formulas identified a fixed-edge conditioned-triangle **mean** formula and connected-component/motif covariance formulas; neither implies the present variance comparison without the new pair classification. Searches of relevant aliases and mathematically stronger coverage yielded no exact covering result; this is *not* proof of globally unique priority. The residual risk is implicit coverage by a general conditional graph-exposure second-moment framework or an older overlap theorem.

## Value — PASS

In a basic random-network model, revealing equal numbers of internal edges need not leave equal uncertainty about the global triangle count. The precisely motivated variance invariant is the number of adjacent observed edge pairs; its all-parameter coefficient is positive, and a claw versus path provides the smallest connected four-vertex, equal-edge triangle-free witness with equal means and unequal variances. This is a genuinely second-order structural distinction, not an arbitrary numerical slice or a restatement of an unconditional moment identity. It can matter for finite-sample uncertainty quantification when part of a network is exactly observed. No downstream performance improvement is asserted.

## Scope and remaining risk

The model is independent Bernoulli edges after complete induced-graph conditioning, not a fixed-edge or degree-preserving model. Full uniqueness and applied utility beyond the exact structural classification remain unproved. Independent human review has not occurred.
