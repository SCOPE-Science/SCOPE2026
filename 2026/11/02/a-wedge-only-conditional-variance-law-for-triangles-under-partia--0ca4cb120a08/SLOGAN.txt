Revealing an induced subgraph of an Erdős–Rényi graph changes the conditional variance of its global triangle count through only the revealed edge and wedge counts, never directly through its triangle count. At equal edge counts the variance gap is exactly \(2(n-h)\Delta w\,p^3(1-p)\), so a revealed four-vertex claw has strictly more residual triangle variance than a revealed four-vertex path despite identical conditional means.
