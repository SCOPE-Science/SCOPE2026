For a three-dimensional polynomial family previously known to have exactly two periodic circles only for \(\mu\leq588/6575\), the inverse-radius Dulac multiplier extends the certified no-extra-cycle region to \(\mu\leq1764/15625\), optimally among all radial power multipliers. Throughout this enlarged interval, every forward orbit approaches the origin or one of the two circles, and all invariant probabilities form the three-vertex simplex generated by those invariant sets.
