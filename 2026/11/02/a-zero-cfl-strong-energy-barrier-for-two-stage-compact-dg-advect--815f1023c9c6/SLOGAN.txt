For uncorrected piecewise-linear two-stage compact DG advection, the physical \(L^2\) decrement on the alternating Fourier mode has determinant \(-4\nu^4\), so **every** positive Courant number admits a one-step energy increase. The mode's exact worst squared-norm excess is \(3\nu^3+O(\nu^4)\) as \(\nu\downarrow0\), even where the scheme is eigenvalue-stable.
