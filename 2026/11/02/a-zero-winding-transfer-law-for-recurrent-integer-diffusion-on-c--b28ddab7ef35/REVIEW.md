# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The object is the simultaneous sign-gradient integer diffusion rule on labelled simple cycles of length at least three, with negative values permitted. The argument derives an edge-flux identity from the two-step equation, forces edge sign reversal using zero cyclic winding, converts it into exact integral bounds on each edge, and applies constant-term transfer counting to close the cycle. Each closed weighted transfer walk corresponds bijectively to exactly one configuration modulo common translation. The unique fixed class is constant, and every other recurrent phase class pairs with its temporal partner. The sharp maximum edge gaps have explicit infinite-family witnesses; the triangle exceptional bound is proved. The literature theorem of eventual periods one or two is used only for exhaustiveness among all recurrent periods. Standalone direct enumeration and transfer replay for finite lengths agree; the proof, not enumeration, covers every length.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Duffy et al. already established the diffusion rule, eventual periodicity for paths and cycles, and a degree-two edge-gap inequality; Long and Narayanan proved period-at-most-two for arbitrary finite graphs; Mullen et al. established an explicit periodic-configuration recurrence on paths. Those claims neither state nor logically deliver the present exact zero-winding Laurent transfer census on labelled cycles. The distinction is substantive: path edge choices do not obey the cyclic sum-zero condition, which changes the count. The accessible primary treatments of those articles were examined, and research-record comparison found no direct implication or equivalent. A relevant historical thesis was not fully accessible, and unindexed equivalents remain possible; a search miss is not a proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

The natural missing cyclic case of a previously studied exact path-counting problem requires an additional global closure constraint. The resulting complete recurrent-orbit counts for every labelled cycle, compact finite transfer representation, and sharp recurrence alphabet supply a motivated structural invariant and finite reproducible benchmark. This is neither a fixed small-parameter count nor a reformatting of the path recurrence.

## Limits and nearest literature

The classification concerns primitive period-two orbit classes up to integer translation, not fixed chip totals, dihedral isomorphism classes, transient catchment basins or transient durations. The general eventual-period theorem is externally sourced, while the explicit cyclic census is derived here. Primary sources: Duffy et al. (arXiv:1609.05792), Long–Narayanan (arXiv:1704.04295), Mullen–Cox–Nowakowski (arXiv:2010.04750). Additional unpublished or unindexed cycle enumerations may exist.
