For synchronous integer diffusion on every labelled cycle \(C_n\), a nine-state Laurent transfer matrix with a zero-winding coefficient gives the exact number of primitive two-cycles modulo common chip translation: \(N_n=([z^0]\operatorname{tr}(T(z)^n)-1)/2\). Every recurrent edge gap has absolute size at most three, sharply so for every \(n\ge4\); on the triangle the sharp bound is two.
