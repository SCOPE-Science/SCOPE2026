For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) and \(d\ge1\), the abstract automorphism group of the full scalar-affine transformation monoid on \(\mathbb F_q^d\) is exactly \(\operatorname{A\Gamma L}_d(q)\): every automorphism is induced uniquely by point conjugation by an affine semilinear map. The monoid also recovers \(q\) and \(d\) intrinsically from its size and left-zero ideal, while the \(d=0\) boundary is governed by automorphisms of the cyclic unit group.
