For minimum feedback vertex sets of the dodecahedral graph, requiring each exchanged vertex to slide along an original graph edge still connects all configurations: the local reconfiguration graph has exactly \(8880\) edges, radius \(8\), and sharp diameter \(10\). This is strictly more constrained than previously determined unrestricted one-vertex exchange, and the local eccentricities are exactly \(8,9,10\) with multiplicities \(120,2340,60\).
