# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The admissible coefficient ideal is derived from \(D(t^N)=0\), including the exceptional case where the characteristic divides \(N\). An invertible coefficient substitution \(g=uh\) reduces the centralizer equation to distinct monomial constraints and yields every centralizer subspace, dimension and multiplicity. The summation for characteristic-divisible truncation lengths is carried out symbolically. In characteristic two, explicit even/odd bases prove a Lie isomorphism, after which the adjoint image is exactly the principal ideal generated by two coordinates. The finite-field program checks 24 complete coefficient spaces and 10,678 derivations. No finite verification is substituted for a general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with publication-priority risk

Premet's regular-derivation analysis has overlap with the \(p\)-truncated Witt setting in odd characteristic. The inspected primary results do not contain the characteristic-two consecutive-truncation Lie isomorphism or the all-exponent uniform full finite-field centralizer polynomial. Published neighboring adjoint-rank and commuting-pair calculations concern different Lie families. Search coverage cannot eliminate every prior characteristic-two modular-Lie-algebra treatment; that remains the main risk.

Centralizer strata govern commuting varieties, adjoint orbit sizes, and finite-field commuting probabilities. This classification provides every stratum for an unbounded family, a closed arithmetic count at all characteristic-divisible truncations, and a structural explanation of the otherwise unexpected characteristic-two equality between adjacent truncation lengths. The affine-algebra normal form gives reuse beyond one numerical census; it is neither an arbitrary slice nor an unsupported isolated small example.

## Scope and limitations
