For every finite field, the complete adjoint-centralizer distribution in the derivation Lie algebra of \(k[t]/(t^N)\) is determined by explicit valuation and residue-class conditions. In characteristic two, the derivation Lie algebras at lengths \(2r\) and \(2r+1\) are actually isomorphic to the affine Lie algebra of \(k[z]/(z^r)\), giving identical exact centralizer spectra and commuting-pair counts.
