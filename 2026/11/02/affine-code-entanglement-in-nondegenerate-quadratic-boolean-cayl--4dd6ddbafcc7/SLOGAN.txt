For a qubit graph state on the Cayley graph of any nondegenerate quadratic form on \(\mathbb F_2^{2m}\), every affine-flat cut has an exactly flat Schmidt spectrum with entropy \(k+1\) or \(k+2\) bits according to whether its \(k\)-dimensional direction is symplectically isotropic, while every affine hyperplane has entropy \(2m\) bits. The spectra are unchanged by the affine offset and even by changing the quadratic form's Arf class while keeping its polar form.
