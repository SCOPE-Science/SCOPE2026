For every odd \(m\ge1\), the complex idempotents of the even dihedral quandle algebra \(\mathbb C[R_{2m}]\) form a smooth disjoint union of affine spaces, with the component above each idempotent of \(\mathbb C[R_m]\) identified with its \(+1\) right-Peirce eigenspace. The entire component-dimension polynomial is an explicit Fourier-orbit product, with zero as the unique isolated idempotent and maximal dimension \((m+1)/2\).
