For the Paley-23 tournament, the affine group acts on rational directed-flag homology as \(\mathbf1\oplus W\) in degree three and \(\mathbf1\oplus2W\) in degree four, where \(W\) is the 22-dimensional permutation augmentation module. Its previously known \(\mathbb Z/23\) homology torsion carries a nontrivial order-eleven character: multiplying vertices by 2 multiplies the torsion class by 12 modulo 23.
