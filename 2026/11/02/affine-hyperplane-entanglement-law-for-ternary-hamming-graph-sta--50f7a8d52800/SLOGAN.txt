For the pure qubit graph state on the ternary Hamming graph, **every** affine-hyperplane cut has a flat Schmidt spectrum with entropy \(3^{d-s}(3^s+(-1)^{s-1})/4\) bits, depending only on the number \(s\) of nonzero coefficients in its hyperplane normal. Among these natural cuts, normals supported on exactly two coordinates uniquely minimize entanglement and one-coordinate normals uniquely maximize it.
