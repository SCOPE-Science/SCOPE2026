# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Rank-nullity excludes diagonalizable repeated-root spectra for a regular three-dimensional nilpotent Jordan matrix. The matrix determinant lemma supplies exact coefficients; a normalized rank-one presentation yields a polynomially invertible \(\mathbb A^2\)-chart for each split squarefree spectrum. Distinct spectral triples and the number of square-zero matrices are counted without multiplicity. Direct, independent finite-field enumeration checks all fibers for \(q=2,4,8,16\). This verification does not replace the symbolic proof.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The closest characteristic-two matrix-decomposition paper (arXiv:2604.15286v1) proves existence rather than counts or complete fiber geometry. A published binary three-dimensional classification gives the known \(q=2\) nonexistence boundary, not counts for \(q\ge4\). The rank-one factorization and two-dimensional torsion-unit multiplicity records concern different claims. No checked source implies the affine-plane fiber theorem or its uniform density law. A 2022 publisher article was not available in full, leaving historical-priority uncertainty; lack of search hits is not proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The theorem quantifies a natural Jordan-block obstruction at the smallest exceptional field in a contemporary decomposition problem, resolving every spectral stratum rather than an arbitrary sample. Its affine-plane structure explains the exact multiplicities and the limiting density \(1/6\), and yields a uniform result across all even finite fields. The result does not treat arbitrary source matrices or matrix dimensions.
