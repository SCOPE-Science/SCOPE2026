Affine self-maps of the nonnegative integers representable by three squares admit a complete 2-adic classification: after removing common factors of four, positive intercepts require either a multiplier divisible by four with intercept \(1\) or \(2\pmod4\), or a multiplier divisible by eight with intercept \(3\pmod8\). Exactly \(3/20\) of positive integer slope–intercept pairs in growing square boxes preserve this three-square set.
