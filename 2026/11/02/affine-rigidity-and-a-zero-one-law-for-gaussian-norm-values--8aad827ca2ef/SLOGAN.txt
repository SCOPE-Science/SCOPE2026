Every integer affine map preserving all sums of two squares is multiplication by another sum of two squares; no positive translation survives. More strongly, for any other affine coefficients the proportion of lattice pairs \((x,y)\) for which \(a(x^2+y^2)+b\) remains a sum of two squares tends to zero, with exact local obstruction density \(1/q-1/q^3\) at every admissible prime \(q\equiv3\pmod4\).
