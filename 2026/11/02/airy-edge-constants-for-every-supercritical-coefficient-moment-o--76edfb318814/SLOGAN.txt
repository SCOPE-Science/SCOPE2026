For powers of any fixed finite Blaschke product with at least two distinct zeros on a common positive radius, every supercritical coefficient moment \(p>4\) has an exact \(n^{(1-p)/3}\) asymptotic. Its leading constant is a universal Airy \(p\)-moment multiplied by the sum of the two cubic-fold curvature contributions; the full coefficient range contributes no additional term.
