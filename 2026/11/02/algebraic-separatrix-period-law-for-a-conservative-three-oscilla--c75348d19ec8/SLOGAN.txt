For a conservative three-oscillator phase ring at quadrature lag, the two reduced periodic families have an exact energy–period quadrature, with a cubic-degenerate separatrix that slows the orbit at the sharp rate \(T(E)\sim2B(1/3,1/2)|E|^{-1/3}\). Unlike an ordinary hyperbolic separatrix, the divergence is algebraic, with a fully explicit physical-time coefficient.
