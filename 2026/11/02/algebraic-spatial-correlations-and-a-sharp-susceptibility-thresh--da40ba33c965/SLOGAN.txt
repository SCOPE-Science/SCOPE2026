A one-dimensional, zero-temperature Ising chain with simultaneous Pareto resetting has stationary connected spin correlations \(C_n\sim K_{\alpha,m,\tau_0}n^{-2(\alpha-1)}\) for every \(\alpha>1\), with an explicit Gamma-function coefficient. The spatial susceptibility changes from infinite to finite sharply at \(\alpha=3/2\), creating three exact asymptotic regimes for block-magnetization fluctuations.
