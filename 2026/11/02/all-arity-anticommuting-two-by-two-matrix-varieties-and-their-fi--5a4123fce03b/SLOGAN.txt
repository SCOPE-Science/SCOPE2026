The reduced variety of ordered pairwise-anticommuting \(2\times2\) matrices in odd characteristic has a complete all-arity component classification, and its exact finite-field tuple counts are \(N_m(q)=(q+1+m(q^2-1))q^m-q+m q(q-1)(q^2-1)+\binom m2 q^2(q+1)(q-1)^2+\binom m3 q(q+1)(q-1)^4\). For fixed odd \(q\), these counts satisfy a minimal order-six recurrence with characteristic polynomial \((T-q)^2(T-1)^4\); the known pair case and known standalone orthogonal-frame geometry are excluded from the novelty claim.
