For each \(q=2^f\), the number of pairwise commuting square-one \(m\)-tuples in \(UT_4(\mathbb F_q)\) is exactly \(q^{4m}+(2q+1)q^{3m}-(q+1)^2q^{2m}+q^{m+2}\) for every \(m\ge0\). The group has exactly \(2q+2\) inclusion-maximal elementary abelian \(2\)-subgroups: one of order \(q^4\) and \(2q+1\) of order \(q^3\), classified by the two rulings of a hyperbolic quadric.
