For the three-vertex path algebra with \(r\) parallel arrows followed by \(s\) parallel arrows, every ordered commuting-tuple count has an explicit all-arity finite-field formula retaining all \(rs\) length-two paths. The maximum commutative-subalgebra dimension is \(1+rs+\max(r,s)\), with a unique maximizing subalgebra for unequal arrow multiplicities, two for equal multiplicities at least two, and \(q^3+3q^2+q+1\) in the single-arrow boundary.
