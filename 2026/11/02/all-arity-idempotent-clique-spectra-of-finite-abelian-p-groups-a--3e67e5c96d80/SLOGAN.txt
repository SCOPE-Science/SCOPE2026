The entire commuting-idempotent clique polynomial of every finite abelian \(p\)-group has an exact automorphism-orbit formula, and its maximal cliques are Boolean families indexed by indecomposable cyclic decompositions. Lengthening a uniquely largest cyclic factor preserves every clique count, yielding infinitely many nonisomorphic groups with indistinguishable idempotent-commutation clique spectra.
