For binary words with exactly three \(1\)'s, every length-three scattered-subsequence deck has at most two preimages, and all nontrivial collisions arise from a single explicit exchange of the two interior zero gaps. The exact number of collided deck classes is encoded by \(X^4/((1-X)^2(1-X^2)(1-X^4))\), yielding the number of distinct decks at every length.
