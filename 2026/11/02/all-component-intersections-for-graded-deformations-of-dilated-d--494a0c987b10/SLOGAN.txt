For every integer \(k\ge1\), the homogeneous miniversal deformation base of the toric pair defined by the cone over the \(k\)-fold dilated del Pezzo hexagon has exactly \(k+1\) reduced irreducible components of dimensions \(3k,3k-1,\ldots,2k\). Every nonempty reduced component intersection is irreducible; its dimension is \(3k-2\max I+\min I\), and it depends only on the extreme component indices.
