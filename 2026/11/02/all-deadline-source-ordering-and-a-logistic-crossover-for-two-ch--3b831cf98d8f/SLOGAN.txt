For an optimally controlled two-choice walk on \(K_{a,b}\), starting in the larger vertex part minimizes the cover-time distribution at every deadline, although the expected gain never exceeds one step. When \(b=n\) and \(a=n+\lfloor\delta n/\log n\rfloor\), the signed source advantage converges to \(\tanh(\delta/2)\), while the normalized cover-time law is a Gumbel shifted by \(\log(1+e^\delta)\).
