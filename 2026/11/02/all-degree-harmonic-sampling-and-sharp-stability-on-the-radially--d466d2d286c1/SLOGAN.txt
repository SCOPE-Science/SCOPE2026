For the radially normalized fourteen-vertex rhombic dodecahedron, exact-degree spherical-harmonic evaluation has ranks \((1,3,5,4,6,6,6,7,6,7,6,7,7,7,6)\) through degree fourteen and full parity-compatible rank seven at every degree \(k\ge15\). Its normalized Legendre sampling Gram matrix has a unique global worst condition number \((1337+\sqrt{889441})/(1337-\sqrt{889441})\) at degree seven; all other full-rank degrees have condition number strictly below \(11/2\).
