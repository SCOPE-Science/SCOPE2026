For continuous edgewise polynomial least-squares projection of **any** degree on unit metric graphs, the reproducing kernel at a vertex of \(K_{p,q}\) is precisely \(q^{-1}\) times the kernel on each cloned edge of the \(p\)-leaf star. Its sharp vertex-evaluation norm is therefore independent of the opposite part size and nondecreasing in the size of its own part, with an explicit all-degree integral formula.
