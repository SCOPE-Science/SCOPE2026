Every exact-degree spherical-harmonic sampling Gram matrix on the 126 roots of \(E_7\) has a complete two- or three-level nonzero spectrum. The evaluation ranks in degrees \(0\) through \(4\) are \(1,7,27,56,62\), while each degree at least five fills its full 63-dimensional antipodal parity sector; hence the first pair of consecutive degrees that interpolates arbitrary root data is \(5\) and \(6\).
