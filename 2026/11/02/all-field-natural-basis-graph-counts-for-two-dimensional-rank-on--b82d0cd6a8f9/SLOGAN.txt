Every two-dimensional nondegenerate finite-field evolution algebra with one-dimensional square has exactly \((q+1-\epsilon)(q-1)^2\) ordered natural bases, with an exact two-point graph distribution controlled by isotropic lines and the square generator. Its natural basis is unique up to scaling/permutation precisely over \(\mathbb F_2\) or for a split binary form over \(\mathbb F_3\), giving sharp small-field counterexamples to the unrestricted formulation of a uniqueness criterion in a foundational preprint.
