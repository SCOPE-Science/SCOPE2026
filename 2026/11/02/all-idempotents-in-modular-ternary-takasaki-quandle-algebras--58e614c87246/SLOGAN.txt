For every \(d\ge2\), the idempotents of the characteristic-three Takasaki quandle algebra of \((\mathbb F_3)^d\) are exactly zero and the elements \(1-w-w^2\) indexed by the inversion-anti-invariant augmentation space. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) there are exactly \(1+q^{(3^d-1)/2}\) idempotents, and the nonzero ones themselves form an elementary abelian Takasaki quandle.
