For every \(N\ge6\), inclusion-maximal pairwise-intersecting rank-two flats of the complete-graph matroid occur in exactly four structural forms, of sizes \(\binom{N-1}{2}\), \(3\), \(4\), and \(5\). Their total number is \(\binom N2+15\binom N6+10\binom N5+3\binom N4\), with explicit exceptions at \(N=4,5\).
