For every alphabet and every source length \(n\ge2\), a word has the largest possible two-step length-two palindromic-duplication descendant sphere exactly when adjacent source symbols are unequal; thus there are precisely \(q(q-1)^{n-1}\) maximizers on a labeled \(q\)-symbol alphabet. Under reverse-complement duplication, replace “unequal adjacent symbols” by “no adjacent pair related by the complement involution.”
