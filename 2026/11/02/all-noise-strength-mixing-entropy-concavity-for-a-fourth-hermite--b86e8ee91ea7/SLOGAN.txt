Every positive fourth-Hermite Gaussian perturbation of amplitude \(0<a\le1/100\) has strictly concave mixing entropy after **any** Ornstein–Uhlenbeck smoothing, including no smoothing: \(F_{a,q}''(t)\le-7a^2q^4\) for \(0<q\le1\) and \(0<t<1\). It extends the previously implied effective-amplitude domain from \(aq^2\le1/256\) through \(aq^2\le1/100\), not the general symmetric entropy-concavity question.
