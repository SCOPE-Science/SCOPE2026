Every optimal four-class majority C-colouring of the ternary Hamming three-cube is constant along one coordinate, with class sizes \((6,6,6,9)\). There are exactly \(288\) unlabeled optimal partitions (\(6912\) with four named colours), in three automorphism orbits of sizes \(18\), \(54\), and \(216\); the known four-class optimum is credited to earlier work.
