For the alternatively ordered Legendre-interval quantum bootstrap, every diagonal entry factors into a central-binomial coefficient times squared spectral gaps divided by explicit pole factors. This yields a one-diagonal sign certificate proving, for every matrix size \(K\ge2\), that its positive-semidefinite energies below \((K-1)^2-1/4\) are exactly the first \(K-1\) physical levels \(E=n(n+1)\), excluding undefined pole energies.
