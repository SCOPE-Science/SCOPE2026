For every \(s\ge3\) and positive \(r,n\), the floor of the inverse reciprocal-square tail of \(U_{j+2}=sU_{j+1}-U_j\) equals \(U_{rn}^2-U_{r(n-1)}^2-1\). A uniform geometric argument removes the sufficiently-large-parameter restriction from Park's partial result on the Amdeberhan--Chen--Moll--Sagan conjecture.
