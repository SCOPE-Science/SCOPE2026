For every finite Ky Fan rank, the Gaussian Fock disk has an explicit nonradial Fourier shape Hessian; the first harmonic is a translation zero mode and the quadrupole coefficient is strictly positive. At squared disk radius equal to the spectral rank, the entire fixed-frequency Hessian converges to the universal \(k^2-1\) angular Jacobi stiffness with exact \(N^{-3/2}\) scaling.
