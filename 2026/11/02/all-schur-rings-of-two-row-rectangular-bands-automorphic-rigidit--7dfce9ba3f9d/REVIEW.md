# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. For any two blocks, the product coefficient on a cell factors exactly as the row count of the left block times the column count of the right block. Summing over partitions recovers the separate row and column profiles; this equivalence is both necessary and sufficient. The unequal/equal row-profile dichotomy exhausts all partitions. The balanced case is a uniquely determined collection of self-paired or even-sized paired column components. Explicit row/column permutation subgroups have exactly those block orbits. The weighted exponential formula follows from an unambiguous labeled component decomposition. Exhaustive product and independent profile tests confirm the first five sizes, but are not used as the infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS, with a historical-priority limitation. The closest full-text primary source is Marrow–Misseldine, arXiv:2601.09940, Sections 1, 2.1–2.2, 5.4, 5.6 and 6. It defines the relevant notion, provides automorphic and direct-product constructions and only a general lower bound for rectangular bands, while recording small cases 5 and 13. Those prior facts are excluded from the novelty assertion. The all-size two-row classification, non-direct-product automorphic completeness and weighted enumerator are not asserted in the inspected relevant sections. Searches for alternate names and generalizations, and comparisons against accessible prior findings, produced no covering assertion. An unindexed or differently phrased earlier enumeration remains possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The recently introduced semigroup Schur-ring framework identifies rectangular bands as a class whose Schur-ring counts are not given by the naive product construction. The result gives a complete classification for an infinite natural family, proves the stronger automorphic-realization property, and produces exact counts beyond the source's small-order census (48 already at order eight). The classification explains rather than merely tabulates the extra non-direct-product partitions. It is not presented as a solution for all rectangular bands.

## Closest literature and limitations
Marrow–Misseldine, *On Schur Rings Over Semigroups*, arXiv:2601.09940 (initial submission January 14, 2026), is the directly comparable paper. Its listed values 5 and 13 are reproduced as consistency checks, not discoveries. General Schur-ring structural results or future papers could imply the characterization without using the same terminology. The current assertion concerns the labeled two-row case, over rational coefficients, and carries no independent audit.
