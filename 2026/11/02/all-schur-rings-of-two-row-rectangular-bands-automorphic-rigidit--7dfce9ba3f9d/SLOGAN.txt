Every semigroup Schur ring on the two-row rectangular band \(B_{2,m}\) is automorphic, and its partition has one of two explicit row-profile forms. Their exact number is a Bell number plus a weighted Bell sum with component weights \(w_{2k}=1+\tfrac12\binom{2k}{k}\) and \(w_{2k+1}=1\), giving \(2,5,13,48,184,\ldots\) for the first sizes.
