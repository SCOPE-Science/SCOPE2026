Every total mutual-visibility set in a three-factor Hamming graph decomposes into disjoint coordinate-line cliques and isolated triples, giving an explicit three-variable exponential generating function for its entire cardinality distribution. In particular, when \(a\ge b\ge c\ge5\), there are exactly \(a(a-1)b(b-1)c(c-1)\) maximum sets, all equivalent under coordinate relabelings; when \(a\ge b\ge3\) and \(c=2\), there are exactly \(2ab\).
