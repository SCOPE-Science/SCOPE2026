For every finite cactus graph with arbitrary positive edge weights, any two vertex-pair connectivity events are nonnegatively correlated under both the arboreal-gas forest law and the random-cluster law for every \(q>0\). An exact nonnegative four-cell polynomial and independent cycle-block factorization establish the conjectured inequalities throughout the non-FKG regime \(0<q<1\), without assuming large fugacity.
