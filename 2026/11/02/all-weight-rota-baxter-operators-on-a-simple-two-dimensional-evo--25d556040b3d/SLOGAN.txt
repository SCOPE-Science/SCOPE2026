For the simple perfect evolution algebra \(e_1^2=e_2,\ e_2^2=e_1+e_2\), every nonzero-weight Rota–Baxter operator splits along two distinct roots of \(T^3-T^2-1\), apart from zero and minus the weight times the identity. Weight-zero operators vanish except in characteristic \(31\), where an affine line of square-zero operators appears at the cubic’s repeated root.
