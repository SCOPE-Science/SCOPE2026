For labelled one-agent inquisitive epistemic frames on \(n\) worlds, the exact count is the exponential set-partition transform of the established antichain-cover numbers. Almost every such frame has a single information cell, and the first nonuniversal contribution is sharply \(n c_{n-1}\): conditional on having more than one cell, almost every frame has one singleton cell and one cell of size \(n-1\).
