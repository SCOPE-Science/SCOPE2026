For every integer \(k\ge1\), the two critical scalar zeros of the odd weighted Bergman kernel at \(\alpha=4k-2\) cross \(s=\pm i\) in alternating directions as the origin-power parameter decreases from zero: inward for odd \(k\), outward for even \(k\). The resulting number of numerator zeros in \(|s|<1\), for sufficiently small negative parameter, is exactly \(2k+(-1)^{k-1}\), refuting a proposed unrestricted boundary-entry extension for infinitely many integer boundary exponents.
