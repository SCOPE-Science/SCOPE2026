For the first \(q\) normalized Hermite functions, the half-line concentration defect has a nondegenerate central extremum whose type alternates with the parity of \(q\). Its exact curvature is \((-1)^q(2/\pi)\sum_{k<\lceil q/2\rceil}\binom{2k}{k}^2/16^k\), with logarithmically increasing magnitude as \(q\to\infty\).
