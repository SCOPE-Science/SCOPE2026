# Scientific review: alternating connected-energy correlations

## Correctness — PASS

A stationary two-sided marked Poisson construction gives a common last-event age plus one categorical-marked excess age. Conditional Brownian Gaussianity transfers mixed moments of distinct squared displacements to mixed moments of those ages. The exact cumulant generating function yields the signed product-of-odds formula at all permitted orders. The sharp bound follows by multiplying \(k\) elementary arithmetic-geometric-mean inequalities, with separate treatment of the \(k=2\) equality cases. A generator recursion and set-partition expansion were checked independently in exact rational arithmetic for 798 cases. Those finite tests corroborate, but do not prove, the all-order theorem.

## Value — PASS

The all-order sign reversal, sharp bound and contrast between absolute and mean-normalized cumulants answer a natural experimental question: whether sacrificing exactly one reset per global event necessarily weakens all multibody correlations. The answer is no for odd connected orders, even though every pair is weakened. Heterogeneous exemption weights let one quantify this effect without exchangeability. The result is confined to the specified stationary noninteracting Poisson model and does not assert dynamics, higher repeated-label cumulants or direct applicability to interacting fluids.
