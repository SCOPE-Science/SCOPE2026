In a heterogeneous Brownian gas where every Poisson event resets all but one randomly exempt particle, the stationary connected cumulant of \(k\) distinct squared positions differs from full synchronous resetting by an exact correction of alternating sign and magnitude proportional to \(\prod_i\frac{w_i}{1-w_i}\). Every even-order connected fluctuation is weakened, every odd order from three upward is strengthened, and the relative correction has the sharp bound \((k-1)^{-k}\) for \(k\ge2\); this concerns unnormalized cumulants at a fixed global event rate.
