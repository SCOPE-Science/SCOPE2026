On every balanced complete bipartite graph \(K_{n,n}\), \(n\ge2\), two independent synchronous walkers starting in the same part at distinct vertices and two walkers starting in opposite parts have cover-time CDFs that cross at arbitrarily late consecutive times, with exact alternating exponential scales. Consequently no initial placement, even randomized, stochastically minimizes the two-walker cover time among all placements.
