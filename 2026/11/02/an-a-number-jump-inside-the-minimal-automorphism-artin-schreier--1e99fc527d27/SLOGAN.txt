For the characteristic-three genus-nine curves \(y^3-y=x^{10}+ax^8+x^7\), the full geometric automorphism group is always exactly \(C_3\) and the \(3\)-rank always zero, yet the Jacobian \(a\)-number jumps from \(4\) to \(5\) precisely at \(a=0\). This explicitly realizes two distinct fine invariants inside one minimal-automorphism stratum.
