For a priced perfect matching in \(K_{m,m}\), total-resistance minimization deletes the matching only at \(P/Q\ge r_A(m)\), while spanning-tree determinant maximization deletes it already at the strictly lower price \(r_D(m)\). The two thresholds approach one at speeds \(2/m\) and \(1/m\), creating a sharp objective-dependent network-support disagreement.
