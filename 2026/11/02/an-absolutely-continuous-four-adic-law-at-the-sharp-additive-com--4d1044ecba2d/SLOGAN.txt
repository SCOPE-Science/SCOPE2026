For the sharp base-four additive complements \(A\) (digits \(0,1\)) and \(B=2A\), the ratio \(A(n)B(n)/n\) over a complete four-adic block converges, at quantitative rate \(O(2^{-k})\) against Lipschitz tests, to an absolutely continuous law supported on \([1,3/2]\). Its density contains the universal component \(2/y^2\) of mass \(2/3\), plus an explicit Cantor-gap series of mass \(1/3\).
