For every affine resolvable design with at least three blocks per parallel class, the incidence metric has two competing negative-type obstructions, but design-parameter integrality forces a clean dichotomy: affine planes lose negative type at \(\log_2(n/(n-1))\), whereas every higher-index design loses it at the unique positive root of \(n\mu(3^p-1)^2=2^{3p}\). The critical nullspace jumps from parallel-class contrasts of dimension \(r-1\) to incidence-coupled contrasts of dimension \(v-1\), and the higher-index exponent decays as \(1/((\log3)\sqrt{n\mu})\).
