The stationary covariance of absolute position in fully Poisson-reset scaled Brownian motion is strictly positive and has a sharp large-lag prefactor transition at \(\beta=1\): its algebraic factor switches from \(t^{-\beta/2}\) to \(t^{\beta/2-1}\) alongside the universal reset factor \(e^{-rt}\). At the critical Brownian exponent, shared Gaussian noise supplies three quarters and stochastic excursion age one quarter of the exact leading coefficient.
