On the six-cycle, a fully dihedrally invariant 1-independent percolation law can have every edge open with probability equal to a specified sextic root \(p_*=0.5973455474\ldots\) and still be disconnected almost surely, and independent thinning gives zero connectivity at every smaller marginal. Explicit positive orbit weights turn the previously numerical \(0.59733\) threshold indication into a rigorous algebraic lower-bound witness, without claiming global optimality.
