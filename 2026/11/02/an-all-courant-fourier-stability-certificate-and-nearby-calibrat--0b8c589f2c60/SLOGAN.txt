For the original uniform-grid AdHImEx transport scheme, the reported \(0.7\) implicitness calibration is rigorously Fourier-stable at every Courant number, not only at sampled values up to \(100\). An exact Bernstein certificate establishes the guarantee, while changing the calibration to \(0.69\) produces a provably unstable four-cell Fourier mode at sufficiently large Courant number.
