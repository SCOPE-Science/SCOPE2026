# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exact harmonic equations for extinction before return to the fully infected state yield, using a superharmonic geometric barrier, a controlled expansion of the rare excursion probability. A finite-state renewal identity establishes the mean extinction-time coefficient. The ordered-coordinate integral has unit Jacobian on every ordering chamber, and the tree edge-difference map has determinant of absolute value one; hence the edge product is exact. Adding an edge strictly reduces the positive integral. The independent exact-rational checker passed 1,576 listed tests, of which 1,441 cover labelled trees with asymmetric weights; finite checks support rather than replace the general proof.

## Originality

**PASS within the inspected record, with historical-priority risk.** The graph-size limit of Mountford et al. (arXiv:1203.2972v1) and Schapira–Valesin (arXiv:1509.04133v1) differs from the fixed-graph, high-transmission coefficient. Qu et al. (arXiv:1408.6959v1) study directed and heterogeneous SIS dynamics through mean-field prevalence, not exact absorption times. The closest accessible survival-time account is van de Bovenkamp–Van Mieghem (doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.92.032806), whose abstract gives exact results for complete and star graphs, and does not establish the all-tree weighted product. Its complete paper remained inaccessible, so a stronger or equivalent result in that source or another general first-passage treatment cannot be excluded. A previous exact weak-colonization expansion of the same contact process concerns a nonoverlapping singular regime and does not determine the present large-intensity leading coefficient.

## Value

**PASS.** The theorem replaces an exponential-size Markov-chain inversion with a simple all-tree product. The unexpected universality over *all* tree topologies, extension to heterogeneous directional transmission/recovery, and strict asymptotic effect of added contacts make a useful benchmark for comparing epidemic persistence networks. The result is exact for the stated model rather than an empirical intervention recommendation.

## Residual limitations

The result does not establish all-transmission-intensity comparisons, graph-size-uniform rates, or robustness to one-way transmission. Complete access to a directly relevant 2015 survival-time study and independent priority review remain outstanding.
