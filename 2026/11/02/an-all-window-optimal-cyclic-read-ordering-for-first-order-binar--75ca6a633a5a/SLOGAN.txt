The complete cyclic \(b\)-symbol distance hierarchy of binary first-order Reed–Muller codes, optimized over every coordinate permutation, is exactly \(2^m-2^{m-b}\) for \(1\le b\le m\) and \(2^m\) for \(b\ge m+1\). One primitive-field-power ordering with the zero coordinate inserted achieves **all** these sharp optima simultaneously, improving the standard lexicographic read-distance hierarchy.
