On the complete \(r\)-ary Hamming cube of every dimension \(n\ge2\), the Gaussian matrix with entries \(q^{d_H(x,y)^2}\) first loses positive semidefiniteness at the smallest root of \(\sum_{j=0}^n(-1)^j\binom njq^{j^2}=0\), independently of the alphabet size \(r\). At the transition its nullity is exactly \((r-1)^n\), and the critical inverse bandwidth is \(\log n-1/(2n^2)+O(n^{-3})\).
