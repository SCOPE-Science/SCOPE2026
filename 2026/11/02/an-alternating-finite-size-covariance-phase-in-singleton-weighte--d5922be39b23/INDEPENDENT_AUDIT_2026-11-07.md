# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each noncrossing set partition of [n] weighted q^{C_1}, including q=0 with 0^0=1, the Kreweras block-profile enumeration T_n(r)=n!/((n-m+1)! prod r_j!) gives integer polynomial partition function Z and moment sums A=Sum C2 weight, B=Sum C3 weight, M=Sum C2*C3 weight. Their covariance is (MZ-AB)/Z²=-nP_n(q)/Z². For n=5,6,7,8 the complete coefficient vectors in the actual RESULT.md are explicitly finite and independently regenerated by ordinary set-partition generation and crossing rejection on 42,132,429,1430 states. Z has positive constant and nonnegative coefficients. P6 and P8 have strictly positive constants and all other coefficients >=0; P5 and P7 have negative constants and strictly positive derivative for q>=0, so each has exactly one positive zero. Rational q=1/20 gives strict signs +,-,+,- and the two displayed unique thresholds. The polynomial sign argument proves all real q>=0 for exactly these four n; it does not imply a pattern for n>=9.

## originality

PASS

Kreweras/Arizmendi provides the complete general block-profile count, so neither the coefficient-generation algorithm nor the Gibbs model is newly invented. Kortchemski–Marzouk (2015) covers simply generated noncrossing partitions and asymptotic block frequencies; Kargin (2019) establishes negative asymptotic size-count covariances for uniform q=1. The assessed claim is a new exact finite-size q-dependent covariance sign diagram (and two thresholds) at the first four feasible sizes, including positive covariance at some q>0 in odd sizes. These signs are algebraically computable from the older general profile formula but are not an explicit prior implication without carrying out the finite polynomial extraction and root/sign analysis. A prior specialized finite q-fugacity table could cover the answer; residual risk retained.
