For singleton-weighted noncrossing partitions, the covariance between the counts of size-two and size-three blocks changes sign twice across the first four feasible orders: at \(q=1/20\), its signs for \(n=5,6,7,8\) are \(+,-,+,-\). The exact finite-order phase diagram has unique positive fugacity thresholds at orders five and seven, but strict negativity at orders six and eight for every nonnegative weight.
