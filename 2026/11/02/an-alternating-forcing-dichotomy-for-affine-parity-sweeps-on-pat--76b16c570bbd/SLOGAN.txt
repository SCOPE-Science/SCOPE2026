For affine self-inclusive neighbor-parity updates on any finite path, every order of one update per vertex gives a reversible map of exact order \(n+1\), with a complete orbit census independent of the order. An alternating-index forcing sum determines whether primitive periods follow the ordinary divisors of \(n+1\) or are restricted to divisors containing its full power of two; when \(n+1\) is a power of two and forcing is incompatible, every state lies on a maximal-length cycle.
