For an unweighted fractional hedonic game formed by adjoining one universal agent to a degree-at-most-two graph with no triangles or four-cycles, social welfare is at most \(m/2+1\), and this bound is attained exactly by a hub-plus-edge or hub-plus-three-vertex-path coalition whose complement has a perfect matching. Every partition attaining the bound is Nash stable, yielding exact welfare and full optimizer counts on fans and long wheels.
