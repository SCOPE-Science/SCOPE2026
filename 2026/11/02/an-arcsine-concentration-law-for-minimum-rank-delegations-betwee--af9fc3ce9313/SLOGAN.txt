In a two-guru path with independent fair ranked preferences, every minimum-rank delegation branching cuts the path at a random-walk minimum, and its guru-weight share converges, regardless of optimal tie-breaking, to the arcsine distribution. Consequently a guru receives at least three quarters of the total vote with asymptotic probability \(2/3\), even though rank optimization improves the expected number of second-choice choices over balanced breadth-first delegation by only \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}+O(1)\).
