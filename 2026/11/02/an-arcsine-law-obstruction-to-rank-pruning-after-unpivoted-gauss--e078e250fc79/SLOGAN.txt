For the unpivoted Gaussian QB Frobenius-pruning method on \(\operatorname{diag}(1,c\tau,0,0)\), the final error indicator is exactly zero yet the algorithm retains rank two instead of the feasible rank one with limiting probability \((2/\pi)\arcsin c\) as \(\tau\downarrow0\). A two-row SVD rotation of the already-formed coefficient block removes the excess rank without new matrix actions.
