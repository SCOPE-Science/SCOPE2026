For the normalized first-Hermite window on square Gabor lattices of reciprocal density \(q\), the sharp high-density condition-number defect depends on arithmetic flux: \((\kappa_q-1)/(qe^{-\pi q/2})\to8\pi\) along integers but \(\to4\sqrt2\,\pi\) along half-integers. The split comes from the exact norms \(4\) and \(2\sqrt2\) of the nearest-neighbor Janssen operator, with an explicit exponentially smaller remainder.
