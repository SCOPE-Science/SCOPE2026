For the standard filiform Lie algebra \(L_n(\mathbb F_q)\), \(n\ge4\), the exact number of fixed-point-free automorphisms is \(q^{2n-3}\sum_{t\mid(q-1),\,t>1}\varphi_{\mathrm E}(t)(q-1-\min\{t,n-1\})\). Such automorphisms exist exactly when \(q>n\) or \(q-1\) is composite; this gives a sharp, characteristic-independent arithmetic transition in every dimension.
