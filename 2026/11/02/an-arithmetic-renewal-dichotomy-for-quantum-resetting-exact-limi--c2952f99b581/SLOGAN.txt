For independent finite-mean integer quantum reset clocks, the greatest common divisor \(d\) of possible waiting times determines a sharp dichotomy: span one yields an ordinary stationary density matrix, while span \(d>1\) yields an explicit \(d\)-phase limit cycle. That cycle collapses to one state exactly when the reset state commutes with the unitary gate; a zero-free renewal Fourier multiplier proves there can be no cancellation of the other phases.
