For a coherent quantum walk on the prime square torus \(C_p\square C_p\), \(p\) absorbers placed on one affine line have exactly three possible eventual capture efficiencies, determined only by the line's slope. Slopes satisfying \(r^2=-1\) in \(\mathbb F_p\) uniquely maximize capture within the affine-line class when \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), and the law is unaffected by arbitrary strictly positive absorber rates.
