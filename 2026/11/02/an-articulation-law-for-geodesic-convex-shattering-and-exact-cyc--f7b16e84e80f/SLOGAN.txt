At a graph articulation vertex, the complete VC-shattered-support polynomial of geodesically convex sets satisfies a rooted product-plus-correction identity. For a bouquet of \(t\ge2\) cycles, it yields an explicit all-coefficient polynomial, VC dimension \(2t\) independent of the cycle lengths, and exactly \(\prod_{i=1}^{t}\lfloor(m_i-1)/2\rfloor^2\) maximum shattered supports.
