For the \(k\)-fold shuffle of the ordered-block word \(a_1^{p_1}\cdots a_d^{p_d}\), the complete minimal DFA has exactly \(1+\sum_{r=0}^{d}e_r(\mathbf p)\binom{k}{r}\) states. The entire exact state count is unchanged by permuting the positive block lengths, even though the languages generally change.
