For the equisingular plane branches \(x^{p+1}+y^{p+2}+\lambda x^{p-1}y^{p-1}=0\) in characteristic \(p\ge5\), the \(F\)-pure threshold jumps from \(1/p\) to \(1/(p-1)\) when \(\lambda\) becomes nonzero, while the Milnor number stays \(p(p+1)\). In characteristic five the Tjurina number also stays 30, so the semigroup, Milnor number, and Tjurina number fail together to detect the distinct formal contact classes.
