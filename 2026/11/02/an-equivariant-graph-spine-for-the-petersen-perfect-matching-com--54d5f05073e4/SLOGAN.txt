The perfect-matching simplicial complex of the Petersen graph strongly deformation-retracts \(S_5\)-equivariantly onto a barycentrically subdivided complete graph on its six perfect matchings. Its integral first homology is the exterior square of the matching-permutation augmentation lattice, with rational decomposition \(S^{(3,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,1,1,1)}\); the full-symmetry quotient is contractible while the five-cycle quotient is a wedge of two circles.
