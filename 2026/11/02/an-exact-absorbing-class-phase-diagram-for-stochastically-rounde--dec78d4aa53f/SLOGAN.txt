For the parameter-four logistic map evaluated exactly and rounded once stochastically to a uniform grid with denominator at most 65536, every orbit is eventually absorbed: the only closed state is zero unless four divides the denominator, when \(3/4\) is also absorbing. A complete exact-integer enumeration proves this finite 16-bit phase diagram, while the sharp discrepancy from the continuum arcsine law remains at least \(0.1556879\) in Wasserstein distance even when both absorbing states coexist.
