For an Erlang-three self-exciting earthquake cluster, the mean aftershock rate acquires successive secondary peaks exactly when the branching ratio crosses explicit thresholds \(q_1<q_2<\cdots\uparrow1\). Erlang-one and Erlang-two kernels never admit a secondary rate peak; each fixed subcritical three-stage cluster has finitely many peaks, but their maximum possible number is unbounded.
