Three positive-rate outer-corner absorbers on a level-\(m\) finite Sierpinski gasket access exactly \(5\cdot2^{m-1}+1\) of its \((3^{m+1}+3)/2\) coherent quantum modes for every \(m\ge1\), independently of the absorber rates and any Hermitian corner-supported potential. The maximally mixed eventual-capture fraction consequently decays as \((5/3)(2/3)^m\), providing an exact all-generation extension of earlier finite-level numerical trapping data.
