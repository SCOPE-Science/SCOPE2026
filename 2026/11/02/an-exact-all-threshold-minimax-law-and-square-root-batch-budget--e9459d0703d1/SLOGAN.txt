For exact line minimization of a planar positive-definite quadratic, the worst-start failure probability after choosing the best of \(m\) independent Gaussian candidate directions is exactly \(\bigl[(2/\pi)\arctan\sqrt{Kt/(1-t)}\bigr]^m\) at fractional-gain threshold \(t\), where \(K\) is the condition number of the energy-transformed sampling covariance. Inverse-Hessian Gaussian sampling is the unique all-threshold minimax covariance up to scale, while isotropic sampling requires a sharp \(\Theta(\sqrt\kappa)\) candidates for fixed worst-case success probability.
