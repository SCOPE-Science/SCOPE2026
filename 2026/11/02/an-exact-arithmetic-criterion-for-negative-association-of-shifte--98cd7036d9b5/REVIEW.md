# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The proof is self-contained: a local baseline-and-single-departure lemma gives full negative association for odd-prime and two-adic local gcd vectors; CRT independence and a conditional-covariance induction preserve it under coordinatewise product. The converse explicitly constructs two identical, nonconstant increasing divisibility-event indicators at every forbidden odd prime or modulo \(4\). All quantifiers include \(n=1\), repeated shifts and any finite number of coordinates. A standard-library verifier independently tests complete upper-set inequalities for small pairs and triples, and direct moments. Finite tests alone do not establish the general result.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, residual risk)

Closest arithmetic literature: Tóth (2013) provides Menon-type gcd-sum identities; Tóth (2023) surveys polynomial gcd-sums and independent-coordinate multivariable sums. Their inspected relevant statements do not classify dependence of several gcds sharing one uniform unit residue. General negative-association stability is established by Joag-Dev and Proschan (1983); the new content is the full if-and-only-if arithmetic criterion, its precise modulo-four boundary, and pairwise-to-full equivalence. The illustrative opposite-shift expectation is already implied by earlier gcd-sum techniques and is not claimed new. Targeted literature, research-index and published-literature searches found no equivalent classification; broader or unlocated literature may still subsume it.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A complete local-to-global classification replaces separate Menon-type average identities with a simultaneous inequality for all increasing observables and every finite set of shifts. The exact obstruction at \(4\) is not detectable merely from the parity of shifts, while coincident residues force explicitly positive covariance. The theorem makes strong negative dependence auditable by finite congruence tests and identifies a structural property where checking every pair suffices for arbitrary-size families. This is a motivated infinite classification, not a numerical bound or a renamed scalar identity.

## Scientific scope and limitations

Uniform reduced-residue sampling and the divisibility order are essential. The theorem does not discuss nonuniform sampling, all integer residues, or dependencies in ordinary numeric order. The moment corollary is covered prior art. The use of the general negative-association closure theorem is acknowledged. Independent audit and literature-completeness certification have not occurred.
