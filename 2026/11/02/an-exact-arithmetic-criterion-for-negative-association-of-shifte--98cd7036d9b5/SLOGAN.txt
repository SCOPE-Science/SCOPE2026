An explicit congruence criterion characterizes exactly when any finite family \(\gcd(U-a_i,n)\), sampled from a common uniform unit residue \(U\), is negatively associated under divisibility. Remarkably, pairwise negative association is already equivalent to the full multivariate property; the only local obstructions are coincident nonzero residues modulo odd primes or coincident odd residues modulo \(4\).
