In site-percolated uniform recursive trees, the exact finite-size covariance between closed vertices and open singleton components has an explicit Gamma–harmonic formula. Its linear-order sign reverses at the unique retention probability \(p=\sqrt2-1\); at that critical point the covariance instead decays positively like a constant times \(n^{-p}\log n\), and the two centered counts have independent Gaussian limits.
