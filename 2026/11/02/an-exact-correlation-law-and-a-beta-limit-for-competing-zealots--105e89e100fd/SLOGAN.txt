In a voter model on \(K_{a,b}\) with one fixed opinion-\(1\) zealot in the first part and one fixed opinion-\(0\) zealot in the second, every stationary pair covariance has an exact rational formula. As both parts grow proportionally with \(a/(a+b)\to\rho\), their flexible opinion shares become identical in the limit and follow \(\operatorname{Beta}(2(1-\rho),2\rho)\), giving a uniform, nonconcentrating vote share for balanced networks.
