For every circular binary word avoiding \(11\), the complete Burrows–Wheeler transform after the period-doubling substitution \(0\mapsto01,\;1\mapsto00\) is given by an explicit four-block reversal-and-complement formula. Its \(m\)-th fixed-point iterate has exactly \(2m-2\) BWT runs for every \(m\ge2\), sharpening general logarithmic bounds to an exact identity.
