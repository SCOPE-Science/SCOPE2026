For a full-column-rank, three-row inconsistent linear system, two-reflection randomized Douglas–Rachford has an exact discrete-Laplace invariant law with least-squares mean but squared-error floor \(d^2/(4q)\), a factor \(1/q\) larger than the matched one-reflection Kaczmarz method. As the sampled orthogonal row becomes rare, the centered stationary error converges after \(\sqrt q\) scaling to a continuous Laplace law.
