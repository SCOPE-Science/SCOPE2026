A uniformly sampled semantic class of one-agent S5 announcements extinguishes any specified nonempty atomic-type support with probability exactly \(1/2\). Yet the fraction that extinguishes **every** support is \(\prod_{j=1}^{m}(1-2^{-j})^{\binom mj}\), where \(m=2^k\) for \(k\) propositional letters; its logarithm is \(-(3/2)^m-(5/4)^m/2+O((9/8)^m)\).
