## Correctness — PASS
The explicit integer-interval candidate has 57 affine components below the five-block cutoff. Its exact distinct-pair sumset is represented by 1640 intervals. An independently rerunnable integer certificate proves that these two sets partition every integer after the second seed through the cutoff for all \(f\ge8\): eight small cases are checked directly, and a complete affine-endpoint arrangement calculation shows no ordering/adjacency change for \(f\ge15\). The proven van Berkel--Bosma four-block continuation theorem extends this equality to the entire infinite greedy sequence. A cyclic gap argument proves minimal period and a boundary mismatch proves minimal preperiod. The checker is supporting evidence with an explicit infinite-parameter justification; finite tests alone are not substituted for the theorem.
