For every integer \(f\ge8\), the strict greedy 2-sumfree sequence seeded by \(f\) and \(2f+4\) has a complete explicit interval-and-arithmetic-tail decomposition. Its successive differences have exact minimal preperiod \(2f+10\) and minimal period \(f+5\), proved using an infinite-parameter affine-endpoint certificate and a published periodic-continuation theorem.
