# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The radiation/reflecting Laplace boundary-value problem fixes the dimensionless survival transform uniquely. Renewal gives the stated mean time. A positive-coefficient proof establishes the global inequality for every reset rate, including the critical equality case; when the coefficient changes sign, the small-rate asymptotic gives an improvement and divergence at high rate forces a positive minimizer. The key symbolic identity and six coefficients through order twelve were checked from the independent checker, with 600 randomized parameter tests. These tests do not prove the infinitely quantified claim; the series argument does. No uniqueness of a positive optimum is claimed.

## Originality — PASS on available evidence
The 2011 and 2013 sources establish renewal resetting and partially reactive infinite-domain targets, not this sharp bounded mixed-boundary threshold. Christou–Schadschneider (2015, DOI:10.1088/1751-8113/48/28/285003) study a different, interior trapping geometry between two reflecting walls and report an optimal-rate phase transition. Their abstract, figure captions, and first published page were examined, but the entire 18-page paper could not be obtained; the exact finite-reactivity boundary specialization remains a real priority risk. Benkhadaj–Grebenkov (2022, arXiv:2209.00947) present a general renewal treatment and a worked interval with both endpoints reactive; full available HTML, especially Sec. III.1 and Eq. (83), was read. It does not state the sharp global one-Robin/one-reflecting if-and-only-if threshold or the proof that local reset benefit exactly determines global benefit. Targeted research-index searches did not identify a covered statement. Absence of search hits alone is not claimed as uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
A real experimental decision concerns whether introducing resets at all can help search at a reactive wall when a remote boundary reflects. The complete all-rate no-improvement criterion, sharp reaction-dependent initial-position boundary, and global-from-infinitesimal theorem constitute a natural phase classification, not a mere substitution into the renewal formula. Unlike a numerical optimal-rate chart it proves that no disconnected beneficial resetting window exists in the no-reset regime. This is a specific solvable physical model, not a universal resetting principle.

## Limitations
The model assumes fixed diffusivity, memoryless Poisson resetting to a fixed interior point, one Robin surface, and one reflecting endpoint. Global minimizer uniqueness in the beneficial regime remains unproved. Earlier equivalent derivations are possible, particularly in the inaccessible 2015 full article. No independent external assessment was conducted.
