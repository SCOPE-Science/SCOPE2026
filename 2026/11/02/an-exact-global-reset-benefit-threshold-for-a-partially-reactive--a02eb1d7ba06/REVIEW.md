# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Solving Robin/Neumann Laplace boundary problem gives phi(r,x0)=cosh(beta y)/(cosh(y)+eta*y*sinh(y)); renewal yields the stated T(r). The sign-relevant Q(y) has even-series B_n; for u<1/3 all C_n>0, n>=4 A_n>0, and B3 at B2=0 is strictly positive by -2(u-1)^3(9u-1)>0. Thus B2>=0 means every Q(y)>0. If B2<0, small y makes Q negative; T(r)->infinity at large r ensures a finite beneficial minimizer. The positive threshold root and limits follow; independently checked two exact B2/B3 polynomial identities. Does not prove unique positive optimizer.
