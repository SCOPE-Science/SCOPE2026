In a reflecting interval with one partially reactive endpoint, Poisson resetting to an interior starting point lowers mean absorption time at some positive rate exactly when \((1-u)(1-5u)+4\eta(1-3u)<0\), where \(u=(1-x_0/L)^2\) and \(\eta=D/(\kappa L)\). If the inequality fails, zero resetting is the unique global optimum among all resetting rates, including on the sharp boundary.
