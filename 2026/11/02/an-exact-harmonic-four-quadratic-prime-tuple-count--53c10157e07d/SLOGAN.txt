For every prime power \(q\equiv7\pmod8\), a harmonic four-point set of constant shifts has exactly \(q\lfloor(q-7)/16\rfloor\) simultaneously irreducible monic quadratic translates. A sign-reversing projective involution makes the defining quartic character sum exactly \(-1\), yielding a sharp zero/positive threshold at \(q=7\) versus \(q\ge23\).
