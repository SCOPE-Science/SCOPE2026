For every finite abelian group \(G\) of odd order, doubling/inversion orbits on \(\widehat G\) give a closed product that counts **every** idempotent of \(\mathbb C[\operatorname{Core}(G)]\) and its full right-Peirce eigenvalue multiplicities. The formula distinguishes \(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\) from \((\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^2\): their same-dimensional core algebras have \(80\) versus \(512\) complex idempotents.
