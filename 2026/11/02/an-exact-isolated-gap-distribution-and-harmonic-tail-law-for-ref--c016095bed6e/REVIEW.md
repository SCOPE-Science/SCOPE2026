# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The exact gap decomposition of reflective numerical semigroups is explicitly sourced to Shor. Direct neighbor tests yield the isolated-gap formula and its exceptional multiplicity-two case. Counting multiplicities in floor-quotient intervals gives the exact tail; summing produces the divisor-function mean. The uniform bound and asymptotics follow from standard divisor estimates, not from extrapolating finite data. A standalone verifier independently checked the full semigroup gap sets and isolated positions for 4,668 parameter pairs and 467,488 exact tails. The external parametrization remains an essential assumption from published mathematics.

## Originality
**PASS within inspected evidence, with significant prior-implication risk.** Shor already gives a complete description of the input family and its gaps. The local isolated-gap formula is an elementary derivative of this known structure, **not** the claimed new contribution. Singh and Ram classify isolated gaps of all two-generator semigroups and therefore cover the simplest special cases. The inspected primary works and indexed mathematical-record comparisons did not yield an exact fixed-genus distribution, a uniform probabilistic tail approximation with a sharp-order obstruction, or the mean-growth relation. Because the first ingredient is so explicit, implicit earlier coverage remains a material risk; Smith's 2022 full paper could not be accessed and was not represented as fully checked.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** This is a complete arithmetic histogram for a naturally defined invariant over a canonical finite population. The phenomenon is not merely a small correction: the limiting law has a universal \(1/k\) tail and infinite mean, despite finite-genus means behaving like \(\log g\). A single exceptional semigroup can have every gap isolated. Exact formulas, an order-sharp uniform error estimate, and a structural interpretation make the result reusable for further distributional work.
