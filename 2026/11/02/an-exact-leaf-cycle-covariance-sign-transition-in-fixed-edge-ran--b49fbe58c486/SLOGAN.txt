In fixed-edge \(G(n,m)\), the number of degree-one vertices and the triangle count have strictly negative covariance for \(3\le m\le n\) but strictly positive covariance for \(n+1\le m\le\binom{n-1}{2}+1\), for every \(n\ge5\). Every non-induced motif of minimum degree at least two obeys a topology-independent exact covariance formula with at most one negative-to-positive sign change as the edge count increases.
