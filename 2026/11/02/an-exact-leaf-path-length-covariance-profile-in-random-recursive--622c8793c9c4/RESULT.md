# An exact leaf–path-length covariance profile in random recursive trees

## Finding
For every integer \(n\ge2\), a **uniform random recursive tree** on the labeled vertices \(1,\ldots,n\) is generated by attaching each incoming vertex \(j\) independently to a uniformly chosen parent in \(\{1,\ldots,j-1\}\). For every vertex \(v\), let \(d(v)\) be its depth from root \(1\); put \(T_n=\sum_{v=1}^n d(v)\) (internal path length). For \(2\le i\le n\), let \(I_{i,n}\) be the indicator that vertex \(i\) has no children, and put \(L_n=\sum_{i=2}^n I_{i,n}\), the number of leaves.

With \(H_0=0\) and \(H_m=\sum_{r=1}^m1/r\), the exact conditional expectation and covariance are
\[
\mathbb E[T_n\mid I_{i,n}=1]-\mathbb E[T_n]
=-(H_{n-1}-H_{i-1}),
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(I_{i,n},T_n)
=-\frac{i-1}{n-1}(H_{n-1}-H_{i-1}).
\]
Consequently,
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,T_n)=-\frac{n-2}{4}.}
\]
Let \(a_{n,i}=-\operatorname{Cov}(I_{i,n},T_n)\) for \(2\le i\le n\). Its adjacent differences obey
\[
a_{n,i+1}-a_{n,i}=\frac{H_{n-1}-H_{i-1}-1}{n-1}
\quad(2\le i<n).
\]
Therefore the nonnegative profile is unimodal (with at most two adjacent maximizers); for any maximizer \(i_n\),
\[
\frac{i_n}{n}\longrightarrow\frac1e,
\qquad \max_{2\le i\le n} a_{n,i}\longrightarrow\frac1e.
\]
In particular every nonterminal nonroot vertex is negatively correlated with total depth when it is a leaf; the newest vertex is deterministically a leaf and has zero covariance.

## Assumptions and scope
All parent selections are independent, with vertex \(j\) selecting each smaller label with probability \(1/(j-1)\). The root is vertex \(1\), and is never a leaf once \(n\ge2\). No asymptotics are assumed in the exact results. The limiting peak statement is for integer \(n\to\infty\) and maximizers over the indicated full range.

## Proof
Write \(P_j\) for the parent of vertex \(j\), and \(A_i=\{I_{i,n}=1\}\). Because incoming parent choices are independent,
\[
A_i=\bigcap_{j=i+1}^n\{P_j\ne i\},\qquad
\Pr(A_i)=\prod_{j=i+1}^{n}\frac{j-2}{j-1}=\frac{i-1}{n-1}.
\]
Conditioning on \(A_i\) leaves the parent choices through arrival \(i\) unchanged; at every arrival \(m+1>i\), its parent is instead uniform on the \(m-1\) vertices other than \(i\). In particular the mean depth of the distinguished vertex remains \(\mathbb E[d(i)]=H_{i-1}\), independent of the later parent restrictions.

For \(m\ge i\), set \(t_m^*=\mathbb E[T_m\mid A_i]\) (where \(A_i\) always refers to the final tree of size \(n\)), and let \(t_m=\mathbb E[T_m]=m(H_m-1)\). Indeed \(\mathbb E[d(j)]=H_{j-1}\), so the latter sum follows directly. Under \(A_i\), the expected next depth conditioned on the current tree is \(1+(T_m-d(i))/(m-1)\), whence
\[
t_{m+1}^*=\frac{m}{m-1}t_m^*+1-\frac{H_{i-1}}{m-1},
\quad t_i^*=t_i.
\]
Inserting \(t_m=m(H_m-1)\) and proceeding by induction in \(m\) gives
\[
t_m^*-t_m=-(H_{m-1}-H_{i-1})\qquad(i\le m\le n).
\]
At \(m=n\) this is the conditional-mean identity. Multiplying by \(\Pr(A_i)\) gives the indicator covariance. By summing over leaf indicators,
\[
-\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,T_n)
=\frac1{n-1}\sum_{i=2}^{n-1}(i-1)\sum_{k=i}^{n-1}\frac1k
=\frac1{n-1}\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}\frac1k\frac{k(k-1)}2
=\frac{n-2}4.
\]
For the profile, expand the two consecutive expressions for \(a_{n,i}\) to obtain the displayed adjacent difference. The harmonic tail decreases strictly in \(i\), so these differences change sign at most once. For \(i/n\to x\in(0,1)\), the asymptotic approximation
\[
a_{n,i}\longrightarrow -x\log x
\]
holds. The continuous function \(-x\log x\) has unique maximizer \(x=e^{-1}\) and value \(e^{-1}\). The profile's boundary values vanish uniformly as the fraction of labels approaches zero or one (using the elementary bound \(a_{n,i}\le (i-1)(1+\log((n-1)/(i-1)))/(n-1)\)), establishing convergence of every maximizing label and of the maximal value.

## Verification
The included standalone `verify.py` enumerates all increasing trees on \(2\le n\le9\) vertices, using exact rational arithmetic to check unconditional means, all marked-leaf probabilities, conditional mean shifts, every indicator covariance, and the summed covariance. It separately checks the finite harmonic-sum and adjacent-difference identities for \(2\le n\le200\). Two larger floating-point checks provide nonproof sanity checks of the asymptotic maximizing index. Running `python verify.py` requires only the Python standard library. The symbolic argument above, not enumeration or floating-point checks, proves the unrestricted theorem.

## Relationship to prior work
Dobrow and Fill (1999) study marginal moments and limiting laws of total path length in uniform recursive trees. Leckey and Neininger (2012) study both the number of leaves and internal path length, including their separate means, variances, and limit laws, in the more general Hoppe-tree family. The present statement instead measures the conditional change in *total* path length when a specified insertion-age vertex is finally a leaf, identifies a nonmonotone insertion-age profile, and obtains its sharp aggregate covariance. The inspected 2012 primary full text does not present this joint leaf–path-length covariance profile. Separate marginal moments alone do not imply it. The 1999 work was available for comparison only at abstract level; historical priority is not certified.

## Limitations
Only the uniform recursive-tree model is addressed. There is no claim for root-biased Hoppe trees, weighted attachment, independent sample trees, other path-length statistics, or the joint distribution. The exact covariance does not imply stochastic ordering or independence. Literature searches and inspected sources cannot establish absolute uniqueness; older mixed-moment identities may subsume this fact. The large-size assertions concern the location and amplitude of the local covariance profile, not a distributional limit theorem.
