In a uniform random recursive tree, the total depth and number of leaves have the exact negative covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,T_n)=-(n-2)/4\). The contribution from a leaf at insertion label \(i\) has an explicit harmonic profile, with its strongest anticorrelation attained near \(i=n/e\) rather than at either end of the arrival sequence.
