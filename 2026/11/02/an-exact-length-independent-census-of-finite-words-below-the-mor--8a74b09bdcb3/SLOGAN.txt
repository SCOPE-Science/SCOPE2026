For every alphabet size \(q\) and factor length \(k\), the number of length-\(L\) words with at most \(k\) distinct length-\(k\) factors becomes exactly constant once \(L\ge2k\). A unique primitive periodic core with two short boundary defects gives a Möbius-inversion census and, for fixed \(q\ge2\), an asymptotic population \(\bigl((q-1)/(2q)\,k^2+O_q(k)\bigr)q^k\).
