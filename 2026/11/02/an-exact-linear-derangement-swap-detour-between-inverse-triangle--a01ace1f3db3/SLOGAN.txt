For the crown graph on two parts of size \(3k\), the perfect matchings represented by a product of \(k\) forward three-cycles and its inverse require exactly \(2k+2\lceil k/2\rceil\) alternating four-cycle flips for every \(k\ge2\). The fixed-point prohibition imposes a sharp, linearly growing detour of \(2\lceil k/2\rceil\) beyond the unrestricted transposition distance.
