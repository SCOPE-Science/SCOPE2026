For averaged two-row Kaczmarz, the exact mean-square stability threshold is \(4/[1+\sqrt{\rho^2+(1-\rho^2)/\eta^2}]\), strictly above the uniform one-step contraction threshold whenever the row normals are nonorthogonal and the batch has at least two independent samples. Averaging makes even relaxation \(\alpha=2\) converge geometrically in mean square, although each standalone row update is a norm-preserving reflection.
