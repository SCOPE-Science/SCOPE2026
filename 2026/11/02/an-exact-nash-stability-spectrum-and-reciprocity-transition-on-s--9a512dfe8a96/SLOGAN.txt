Signed unit-weight star hedonic games admit an exact Nash partition classification and labeled-block counting formula, despite the known hardness of arbitrary-weight stars. If reciprocal hub-leaf signs agree with probability \(\rho\), equilibrium existence switches from probability zero to one across \(\rho=1/2\), has a Gaussian \(n^{-1/2}\) transition window, and at exact criticality has an explicit even-versus-odd parity correction.
