A harmonically confined Brownian particle resetting to uniformly chosen points in its own past can retain the exact equilibrium Gaussian position law at every time while the variance of its time-averaged position crosses at \(r=\mu\) from \(T^{-1}\) to \(T^{-1}\log T\) to \(T^{-2\mu/(r+\mu)}\). An exact hypergeometric formula gives the complete finite-time occupation variance and all three sharp asymptotic constants.
