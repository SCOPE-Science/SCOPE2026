At every shortened-Hamming length \(n=2^r-2\) with odd \(r\ge3\), forcing a binary distance-three code to contain **all** threefold diagonal words lowers the maximum dimension from \(n-r\) to exactly \(n-r-1\). Equality is achieved by three-column blocks from \(\mathbb F_4\)-lines, and every optimum is represented by a spanning partial binary line spread.
