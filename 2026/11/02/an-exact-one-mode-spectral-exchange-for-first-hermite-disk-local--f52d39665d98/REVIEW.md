Separate semantic searches returned finite Fourier/Slepian, Gaussian Korenblum and half-line Hermite studies with materially different operators. The exact known eigenvalue formula is credited and is not claimed as new. The ordering is derivable from it with additional inequalities, which remains a historical-priority risk.

The exact identification of their difference is therefore a naturally motivated selection problem rather than an arbitrary spectral slice. The new all-radius formula improves the prior growing comparison to a sharp uniform constant, resolves precise tie-breaking, and shows the concentration-energy advantage decays as the projector distance remains fixed. The fact is special to the first excited level and cannot be extrapolated to higher levels.
