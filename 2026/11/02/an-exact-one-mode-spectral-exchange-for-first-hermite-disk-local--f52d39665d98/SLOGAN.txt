For first-Hermite disk concentration, the optimal finite-rank selection differs from the initial-index Landau projection by exactly one exchanged mode at every noninteger disk area, giving trace-norm discrepancy exactly \(2\) rather than merely an \(O(\sqrt N)\) estimate. At each positive integer area the cutoff instead has a threefold eigenvalue tie; the gain away from ties is an explicit Poisson mass times \((x-k)(k+1-x)\).
