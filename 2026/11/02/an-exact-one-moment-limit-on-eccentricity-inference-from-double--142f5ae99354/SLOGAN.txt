In the unsaturated geometric double-eclipse model, **every** heterogeneous exoplanet eccentricity distribution produces a periapsis-angle posterior determined by just one transit-weighted mean eccentricity \(\varepsilon\): \(p(\omega\mid\mathrm{both})=(1-\varepsilon|\sin\omega|)/(2\pi-4\varepsilon)\). A single even Fourier moment identifies that mean exactly, but distinct eccentricity distributions can have the same full selected angle distribution and conditional occultation rate.
