# Review of the scalar affine infinite-GMRES residual resonance

## Correctness (PASS)

For the original unscaled right-preconditioned companion of Gao and Jiang, the Krylov basis can be replaced by the first \(m\) canonical coordinate vectors without altering the minimizer. The corresponding rectangular matrix has full column rank, with a one-dimensional left null space generated by an explicitly stated vector. Its orthogonal projection gives \(R_m^2=1/W_m\), while the first two residual coordinates and the exact recovery row of \(L_0^{-1}\) give the signed physical residual \((1-a/s)/W_m\). Every formula, quantifier, boundary case and asymptotic limit follows from these finite-dimensional equalities and a geometric series. An independent standard-library normal-equation implementation verifies 288 exact rational instances; the finite check is supplementary evidence rather than a universal proof.

## Originality (PASS, qualified)

The motivating full text, arXiv:2610.01740v1, defines this companion, recovery, and a lifted residual lower bound but not the all-iteration physical-residual square identity, exact physical one-step resonance, or three-regime limit. The relevant sections of arXiv:2102.04082 and arXiv:2206.05153 discuss companion infinite GMRES and inexact preconditioning without this scalar classification. Exact orthogonal projection itself is standard; the contribution is the sharp lift-versus-physical distinction and its parametric classification. General GMRES least-squares theory may imply the calculation, and bibliographic searching cannot establish global uniqueness; those are residual priority risks.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

A source-paper lower bound outside the unit disk constrains the lifted least-squares residual, not necessarily the accuracy of the recovered physical solution. The exact scalar law supplies a reproducible diagnostic of this distinction: \(s=a>1\) forces zero physical error despite a strictly positive lifted floor, whereas for \(0<s<1\), off resonance, the physical residual has twice the asymptotic exponent of the lifted residual. This is a useful rigorous benchmark for interpreting termination criteria and convergence curves in parameterized Krylov methods, not a claim of practical gain over a direct scalar solve.

## Closest sources and limitations

- Gao and Jiang, *Toeplitz Infinite GMRES for Parameterized Linear Systems*, arXiv:2610.01740v1, Sections 2, 3 and 5 (October 1, 2026): defining construction and general lifted-residual bound.
- Jarlebring and Correnty, *Infinite GMRES for parameterized linear systems*, arXiv:2102.04082: foundational companion and convergence analysis.
- Correnty, Jarlebring and Soodhalter, *Preconditioned Infinite GMRES for Parameterized Linear Systems*, arXiv:2206.05153: inexact preconditioning and physical residual experiments.

The theorem is restricted to exact arithmetic, positive real parameters, a scalar affine system and the specified unscaled companion, with a limited diagonal embedding. Unknown historical equivalent derivations remain a risk; no independent audit is claimed.
