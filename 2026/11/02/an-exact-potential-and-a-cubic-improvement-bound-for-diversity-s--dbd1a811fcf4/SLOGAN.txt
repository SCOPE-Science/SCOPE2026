On every complete bipartite network \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\) and exactly one vacancy, fraction-of-unlike-neighbors jump games admit an **exact** normalized within-type-pair potential, even when the two sides have unequal sizes and some agents cannot move. Every sequence of unilateral strict improvements reaches equilibrium after at most \(\lfloor (a+b-2)\operatorname{lcm}(a-1,b-1)/2\rfloor\) moves, regardless of the number of agent types.
