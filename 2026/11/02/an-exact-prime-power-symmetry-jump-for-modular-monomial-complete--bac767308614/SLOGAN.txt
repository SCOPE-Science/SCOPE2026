For every finite field of characteristic \(p\), the truncated monomial complete intersection \(\mathbb F_q[x_1,\ldots,x_d]/(x_1^{p^e},\ldots,x_d^{p^e})\) admits every invertible linear change of variables, but raising each exponent by one forces its tangent automorphism to be monomial. Exact automorphism counts are \(|\mathrm{GL}_d(q)|q^{d(p^{ed}-d-1)}\) at the prime power and \(d!(q-1)^d q^{d(p^e(p^e+1)^{d-1}-1)}\) at its successor.
