For uniformly random monic polynomials of any degree \(d\) modulo \(p^2\), an explicit coefficient formula gives the complete root-count distribution, which stabilizes at the sharp degree threshold \(d=2p\). In every degree \(d\geq2\), its variance is exactly \(2(1-1/p)\), revealing a rare-lift second-moment defect even though the root counts converge in law to permutation fixed-point counts as \(p\to\infty\).
