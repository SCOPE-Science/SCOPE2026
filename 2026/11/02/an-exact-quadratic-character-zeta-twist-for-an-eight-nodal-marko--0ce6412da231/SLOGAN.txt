For the eight-nodal Markoff surface \(x^2+y^2+z^2+x^2y^2z^2=4xyz\), its natural K3 double cover has zeta quotient \(Z(X,T)/Z(W,T)=(1-\chi_q(1+i)qT)^{-8}\) over every \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\equiv1\pmod8\). The eight anti-invariant Frobenius eigenvalues all equal \(\chi_q(1+i)q\), and the two associated invariant point sets have exactly computable imbalance \(8+8\chi_q(1+i)q\).
