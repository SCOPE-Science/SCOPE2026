The nonzero linear-rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\) admit an exact census: their image lines are either square-zero or contain a unique nontrivial idempotent, and each class has an explicit Gaussian-binomial formula. Every such operator squares to zero as a linear map, and for fixed \(n\ge3\) their total number is asymptotic to \(q^{n^2-2}\).
