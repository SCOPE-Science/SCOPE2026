For uniform-marginal KL unbalanced transport with a strictly positive Gibbs kernel having equal row and column sums, the three-block G-Sinkhorn iteration has exact local spectral factor \(\tau^2\max\{\varepsilon/(2\rho),s^2\}\), compared with \(\tau^2\) for conventional F-Sinkhorn. The added optimal translation is locally faster precisely for \(\varepsilon<2\rho\), tied at equality, and slower above it; the decisive extra mode is negative and can oscillate.
