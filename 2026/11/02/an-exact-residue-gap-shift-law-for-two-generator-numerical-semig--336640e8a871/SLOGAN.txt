Translating both generators of \(\langle a,a+k\rangle\) by a modulus \(m\) changes every residue-class gap count by an explicit formula controlled by \(\gcd(a,m)\) and \(\gcd(a+k,m)\). The complete change profile has a sharp CRT-distributed range \(d+e-2\) and variance \((d^2+e^2-2)/12\), even when the modulus shares prime factors with the generators.
