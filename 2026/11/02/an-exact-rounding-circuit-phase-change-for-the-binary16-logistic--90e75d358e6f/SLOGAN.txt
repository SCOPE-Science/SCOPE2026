At \(15{,}361\) nonnegative binary16 inputs, ordinary staged rounding of the logistic polynomial \(4x(1-x)\) yields a 40-cycle with basin 10,267, whereas evaluating the polynomial exactly and rounding only once yields a 5-cycle with basin 117. The resulting Cesàro invariant distributions from uniform input encodings differ by the exact total-variation distance \(10267/15361\); the staged 40-cycle and its basin were previously reported, while the one-rounding comparison is the finding here.
