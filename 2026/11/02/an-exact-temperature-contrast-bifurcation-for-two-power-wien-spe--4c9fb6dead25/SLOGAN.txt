For a cool-component \(x^3\) Wien tail and a hot-component \(x^4\) tail, two distinct spectral peaks are possible exactly when the hot-to-cool temperature ratio exceeds the unique root \(r_*=2.1566408087\ldots\) of a quartic; an explicit interval of relative strengths gives the complete two-peak criterion. At the threshold, the admissible log-strength window opens with a sharp \((r-r_*)^{3/2}\) law.
