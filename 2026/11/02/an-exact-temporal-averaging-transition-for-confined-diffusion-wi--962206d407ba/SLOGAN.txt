A confined particle that repeatedly resets to a uniformly chosen position from its own past keeps exactly Gaussian thermal marginals, yet its time-average variance has a sharp transition at \(r=\kappa\): inverse-time decay below, \((\log T)/T\) at criticality, and \(T^{-2\kappa/(\kappa+r)}\) above it. The full finite-window variance is an explicit confluent-hypergeometric function.
