For central trinomial numbers \(T_n=[x^0](x^{-1}+1+x)^n\), divisibility by \(9\) has a complete ternary-digit criterion: either at least two digits are \(2\), or the unique \(2\) is preceded immediately by \(1\) and followed by a digit. Consequently, among \(0\le n<3^k\), exactly \(3^k-(k+6)2^{k-2}\) are divisible by \(9\) for every \(k\ge2\).
