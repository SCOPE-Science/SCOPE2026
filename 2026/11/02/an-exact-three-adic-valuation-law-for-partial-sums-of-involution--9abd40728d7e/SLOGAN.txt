The three-adic valuation of the partial sum \(a_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n}I_j\) of involution numbers is exactly \(2\) for \(n\equiv4\pmod9\), \(1\) for \(n\equiv6\pmod9\), \(\nu_3(n+1)\) for \(n\equiv8\pmod9\), and zero otherwise. In particular, for every \(k\ge3\), \(3^k\mid a_n\) precisely when \(n\equiv-1\pmod{3^k}\), providing a complete rigorous classification beyond a previously published observation.
