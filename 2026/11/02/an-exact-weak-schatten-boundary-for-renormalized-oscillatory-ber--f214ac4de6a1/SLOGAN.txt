For a unimodular logarithmically oscillating symbol on weighted analytic ball Bergman space, its Toeplitz semicommutator is noncompact but has a unique scalar renormalization that becomes positive compact. In complex dimension \(d\), the renormalized singular values decay with sharp exponent \(1/d\) and coefficient \(c_{\alpha,s}s^2/(d!)^{1/d}\), yielding an exact weak-Schatten boundary.
