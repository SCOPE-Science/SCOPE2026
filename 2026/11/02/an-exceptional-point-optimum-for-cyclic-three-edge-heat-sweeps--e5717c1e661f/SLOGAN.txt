Three reversible edge heat steps around a triangle have a uniquely fastest **asymptotic rate per completed sweep** at \(t=\log(2+\sqrt3)\), where their nonstationary eigenvalues collide in a defective negative double root. The optimal rate \((2-\sqrt3)^3\) is about \(6.50\) times smaller than the rate \(1/8\) obtained by fully equilibrating every edge.
