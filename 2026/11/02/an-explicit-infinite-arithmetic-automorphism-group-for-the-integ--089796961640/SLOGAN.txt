The integral dihedral quandle algebra \(\mathbb Z[R_8]\) has an infinite automorphism group whose complete faithful image in \(\operatorname{SL}_4(\mathbb Z)\) is given by eight affine cosets of an explicitly parametrized norm-one integer-matrix family. This determines the arithmetic image of the previously known faithful four-dimensional representation and resolves the order-eight instance of an open even-dihedral quandle-ring automorphism problem.
