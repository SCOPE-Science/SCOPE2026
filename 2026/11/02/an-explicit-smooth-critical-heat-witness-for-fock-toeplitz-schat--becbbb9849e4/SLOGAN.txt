A fixed smooth real Fock Toeplitz symbol, built from derivative-mollified radial monomial projections and widely separated translates, produces an operator in every \(\mathcal S_p\) for \(p>1\) but not in \(\mathcal S_1\). Its critical Gaussian heat transform rises at least linearly along a deterministically constructed sequence of centers, supplying an explicit witness for a previously nonconstructive endpoint existence theorem.
