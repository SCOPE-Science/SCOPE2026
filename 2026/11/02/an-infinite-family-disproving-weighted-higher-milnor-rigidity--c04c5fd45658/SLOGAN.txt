For every integer \(a\ge3\) and \(b>2a\), the semi-quasihomogeneous germ \(x^a+y^b+z^b+xz^{b-2}\) has the same ordinary Milnor number as its weighted principal part but its \((b-1)\)-st higher Milnor number is exactly one smaller. This infinite family disproves unrestricted weighted higher-Milnor rigidity while leaving the known equal-weight theorem intact.
