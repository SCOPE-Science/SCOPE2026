The six-monomial cubic Togliatti surface extends to an osculating-self-dual toric surface \(X_d\subset\mathbb P^5\) of degree \(d^2-3\) for every \(d\ge3\). Beyond the classical smooth cubic, six boundary lines are nonnormal in alternating ways: three generically identify \(d-2\) sheets and three carry transverse \(\langle d-2,d-1\rangle\) cusps; their second-osculating contact divisor factors into three lines and \(h_{d-3}\).
