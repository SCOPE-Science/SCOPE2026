For every \(r\ge1\), the nonisomorphic caterpillars \(C_{(3,r+1,r,r+2)}\) and \(C_{(3,r+2,r,r+1)}\) have identical third principal specializations of the \(t\)-chromatic symmetric function. Their ordinary fourth principal specializations differ already at \(q^8\), by \(-14\), \(-4\), or \(-2\) for \(r=1\), \(r=2\), or \(r\ge3\), respectively.
