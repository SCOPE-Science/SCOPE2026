For every complex projective scheme of dimension at most one—including reducible and nonreduced curves—the integral Chow-valued Macdonald identity \(\sum_{n\ge0}c_{SM}(S^nX)_0t^n=\exp(c_{SM}(X)_0)\) holds without factorial denominators. The normalization-branch formula yields sharp vanishing above degree \(\sum_i(2g_i-2)+\sum_p(b_p-1)\) when all normalization components have positive genus; two elliptic curves joined at \(r\) nodes give precisely \(\prod_{j=1}^r(1-[p_j]t)\).
