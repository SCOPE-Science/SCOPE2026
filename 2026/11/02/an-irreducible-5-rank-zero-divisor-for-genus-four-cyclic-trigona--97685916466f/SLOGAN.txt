For all smooth cyclic trigonal genus-four curves \(y^3=F(x)\) with monic quintic \(F\) in characteristic five, the Jacobian \(5\)-rank is either zero or two, and the rank-zero parameters form one explicitly defined, geometrically irreducible degree-eight hypersurface. The precise Cartier calculation also identifies its general \(a\)-number-two stratum and the known superspecial \(a\)-number-four locus.
