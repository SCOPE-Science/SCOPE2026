Over \(\mathbb F_{41^2}=\mathbb F_{41}[w]/(w^2-3)\), the primitive constant \(2+16w\) makes \(X^{41}+X+2+16w\) irreducible but not primitive: its root has order dividing \((1681^{41}-1)/83\). Exactly \(64\) of the \(384\) primitive constants have this order-\(83\) obstruction, disproving Sharma's universal primitivity conjecture for the first prime beyond its reported computational range.
