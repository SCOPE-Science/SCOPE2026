The open-neighborhood class of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) has VC and Littlestone dimensions equal for every \(n\ge2\) except \(n=7\), where its 21 vertices exhibit the exact gap \(\operatorname{VCdim}=3<4=\operatorname{Ldim}\). The all-size proof combines a two-vertex-cover trace bound with a 16-leaf adaptive decision-tree certificate.
