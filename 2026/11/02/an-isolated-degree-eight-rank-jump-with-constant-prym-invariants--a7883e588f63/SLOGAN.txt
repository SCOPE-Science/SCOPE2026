In characteristic five, the genus-seven pencil \(y^3=x^8+a x^6+1\) has constant \(a\)-number three and a single degree-eight closed point where its Jacobian \(5\)-rank drops from four to two. The genus-three involution quotient absorbs the entire rank jump, while the four-dimensional Prym retains \(5\)-rank two and \(a\)-number two everywhere on the smooth locus.
