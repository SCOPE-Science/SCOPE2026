# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The stated positive coexistence state obeys all three reaction balances exactly. The linearization matrix follows by differentiating the complete three-variable reaction terms and the diagonal diffusion. An independently replayed standalone exact rational determinant computation produces the three explicit cubic coefficient polynomials and the Hurwitz determinant. Positivity of all polynomial coefficients for nonnegative squared wavenumber excludes nonnegative real eigenvalues, while Descartes's rule and three exact sign evaluations prove the cubic Hurwitz determinant changes sign at exactly two simple positive roots. Cubic Routh–Hurwitz, together with the Neumann spectrum of the interval, proves precisely one oscillatory unstable spatial mode. No finite numerical sampling is used to certify the infinite spectrum.

## Originality — PASS under inspected sources
Zhao–Zhu–Zhang–Cooney (arXiv:2603.21025, Sect. 3.2.1 and 3.2.3, and Appendix B) derive this nonlinear model and analyze a stationary instability regime with *small* cooperator and public-good diffusion; Sect. 3.2.3 explicitly suggests searching for oscillatory instabilities under other parameters. Their general cubic Routh–Hurwitz criterion does not give a positive admissible parameter witness or an all-Neumann-mode classification. Piskovsky (arXiv:2405.14682, Theorem 1 and Lemma 1) provides **general** three-component wave-instability conditions, not this model-specific realization. Related public recorded findings on IL-2 tumour–immune wave instability and on discrete Neumann modes concern other reaction systems and cannot imply the present coexistence balance and mode census. The novelty is the concrete strictly positive rational public-goods instance, the all-wavenumber real-eigenvalue exclusion, and the isolated first-mode instability—not discovery of Routh–Hurwitz or generic wave instability.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The motivating public-goods study expressly identifies possible oscillatory diffusion-driven instabilities outside its stationary parameter regime as an open direction. The construction supplies an exact counterpoint: without any directed motion, and without any stationary unstable frequency, the first spatial mode grows by a complex pair while the uniform and all higher modes decay. Positive biological parameter admissibility and full infinitely many-mode control make this a substantive model-specific boundary rather than a generic toy cubic.
