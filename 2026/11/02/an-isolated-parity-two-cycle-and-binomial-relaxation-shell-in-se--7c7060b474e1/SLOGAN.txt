For a complete-graph Boolean bi-threshold process with a fixed sequential update order and down-threshold two above up-threshold, every state becomes periodic within two sweeps, and a single isolated two-cycle exists exactly at the odd-size central threshold. The exact second-entry population is \(\binom{n-1}{k}-2\varepsilon_{n,k}\), so its uniform-initialization probability has a sharp inverse-square-root central scaling.
