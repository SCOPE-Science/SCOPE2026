For every odd prime \(p\), the checkerboard entropy of the \(p\)-level graph state on an even toroidal grid \(C_m\square C_n\) is exactly \(mn/2-\gcd(m,n)+\mathbf1_{\{v_p(m)=v_p(n)\}}\) dits. The one-dit jump at equal prime-adic valuations comes from a one-dimensional defect at each of two intersecting finite-field spectral branches, not from a finite-size numerical extrapolation.
