For the two-tacnode degeneration of the genus-three quartic pencil \(X^4+Y^4+Z^4+aX^2Y^2=0\), the two lemniscatic elliptic components of its stable limit meet at two nodes whose attachment-point differences are nonzero \(2\)-torsion. Hence the toric extension class of the limiting generalized Jacobian has exact order two, not merely a prescribed stable graph and component \(j\)-invariants.
