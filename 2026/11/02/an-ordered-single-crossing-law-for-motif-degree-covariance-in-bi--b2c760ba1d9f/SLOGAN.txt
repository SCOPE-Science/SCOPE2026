For every non-induced motif in \(G(n,p)\), its covariance with the number of degree-\(k\) vertices has a degree-multiset-only factorization and, whenever \(k\) reaches the motif's minimum degree but remains below \(n-1\), changes sign **exactly once** as \(p\) increases. These transitions are strictly ordered across \(k\), with an explicit sparse-scale root and second-order correction.
