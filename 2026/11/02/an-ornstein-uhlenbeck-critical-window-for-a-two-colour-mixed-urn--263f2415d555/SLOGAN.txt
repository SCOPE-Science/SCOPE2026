A Bernoulli-mixed two-colour urn tuned to its spectral boundary by \(p_N=1/3+2\tau/(3\log N)\) has an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck rather than a Brownian limit on logarithmic time, with drift \(-\tau\) and diffusion variance \(12\). Its sharp variance crossover is \(12(1-e^{-2\tau})/(2\tau)\), with the continuous critical value \(12\).
