For the Lucas recurrence \(U_{n+2}=U_{n+1}+9U_n\) with \(U_0=0,U_1=1\), a distinguished residue modulo \(2^k\) appears exactly \(2^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\) times per shortest period for every \(k\ge5\). The growing frequencies prove nonstability in the first coefficient family left unresolved by the classical stability classification.
