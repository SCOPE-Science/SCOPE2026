For \(R_n=k[x,y]/(x^2,y^n)\) in characteristic two and \(n\ge3\), all unital algebra automorphisms admit an exact four-polynomial normal form, giving a closed finite-field count. The tangent dimension exceeds the reduced automorphism-group dimension by \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\), an unbounded family of non-smooth symmetry schemes.
