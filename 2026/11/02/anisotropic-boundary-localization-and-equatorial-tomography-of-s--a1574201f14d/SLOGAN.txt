The normalized expected arclength on the boundary of a fixed-pole cell in an anisotropic random great-circle tessellation has an explicit angularly mixed Gamma scaling law, with rate equal to the circular cosine transform of the equatorial normal density. Its angular law and limiting mean perimeter together reconstruct that equatorial density exactly, and the sharp perimeter lower bound \(R\ge\pi^3/(2h_0)\) has equality precisely for constant equatorial density.
