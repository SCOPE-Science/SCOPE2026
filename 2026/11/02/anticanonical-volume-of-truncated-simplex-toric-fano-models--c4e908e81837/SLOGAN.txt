For the \(n\)-dimensional toric Fano variety determined by the zero-sum box slice with coordinate bounds \(-(2n-1)\) and \(2\), the exact anticanonical degree is \(\frac{n(n+1)}{2^{n-1}(2n-1)}\). Its degree decays asymptotically as \(n/2^n\), providing a quantitative invariant of the small-alpha-style toric construction.
