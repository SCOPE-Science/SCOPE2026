The general proof reduces the anticanonical degree to the polar volume of two opposite homothetic simplices with scale ratio strictly between \(1/n\) and \(1/(n-1)\). One cap lies beyond each positive-simplex facet. This is a symbolic all-dimensional argument. A standalone exact-rational formula is compared with independent `scipy.spatial.ConvexHull` numerical volumes for dimensions two through six by `verify_volume.py`; the finite tests do not establish the theorem independently of the written proof. The cited paper's full primary text was not retrieved. Independent audit has not been performed.
