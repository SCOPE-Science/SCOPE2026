For every aperiodic binary word, antipalindromic factors are negligible compared with factor complexity: \(P_E(n)\log(P_E(n)+1)/C(n)\to0\). It follows that every aperiodic binary language invariant under reversal and complementation has unconditional generalized reflection-complexity ratio \(C_H(n)/C(n)\to1/4\), removing a convergence assumption in a 2026 theorem.
