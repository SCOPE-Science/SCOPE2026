In a three-dimensional field-aligned active Brownian swimmer, reversing the swimming direction into an antiparallel angular cap is exponentially rare, but its mean has a sharp \(\xi^{-1/2}\) cap-width crossover governed by the exponential integral \(E_1\). In that same strong-field, shrinking-cap regime the complete waiting-time distribution, normalized by its exact mean, converges to \(\operatorname{Exp}(1)\), with all moments.
