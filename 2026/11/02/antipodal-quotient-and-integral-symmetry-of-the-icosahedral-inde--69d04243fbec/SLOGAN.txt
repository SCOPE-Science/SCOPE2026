The icosahedral graph has an independence complex formed by a two-sphere and six antipodal chords, so it is homotopy equivalent to \(S^2\vee\bigvee^6 S^1\). Its full integral symmetry action is explicit, and quotienting by the antipodal involution produces \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), including its \(\mathbb Z/2\) first-homology torsion.
