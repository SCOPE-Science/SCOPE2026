For normalized volume on the solid Euclidean bipyramid \(\{(x,t):|t|+\|x\|_2\le1\}\), the affine \(L^2\) projector has exact \(L^\infty\) norm \(1+\frac{d+3}{2}(1-\frac{2}{(d+2)(d+3)})^{d+2}\). In dimensions \(d\ge2\), its worst endpoint amplification occurs only at the two axial tips; its sharp positivity defect is half the norm excess over one.
