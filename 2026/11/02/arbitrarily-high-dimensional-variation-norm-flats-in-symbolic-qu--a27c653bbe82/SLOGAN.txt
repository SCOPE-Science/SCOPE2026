For every positive-entropy mixing subshift of finite type, the symmetrized periodic-length moduli space of positive word-quasimorphisms contains isometric copies of \((\mathbb R^k/\mathbb R\mathbf 1,\max_i t_i-\min_i t_i)\) for every \(k\ge2\), realized by strictly positive locally constant potentials. Hence its global metric geometry is not Gromov hyperbolic.
