For every odd \(q\ge3\), an explicit rational target has a greedy three-term Egyptian underapproximation that is globally best and has exactly \(\bigl(\tau(q^2)+1\bigr)/2\) nondecreasing denominator-tuple realizations, of which \(\bigl(\tau(q^2)-1\bigr)/2\) use distinct denominators. The exact multiplicities are unbounded as \(q\) ranges over odd squarefree integers.
