In a uniform recursive tree, conditioning the size of vertex \(2\)'s descendant subtree to be \(s\) leaves the whole-tree leaf count distributed as the sum of independent leaf counts from trees of sizes \(s\) and \(n-s\), with one boundary correction. Every interior split matches the unconditional first \(r\) moments for all \(r<\min(s,n-s)\), despite giving a genuinely different distribution; a nonzero subtree-size/leaf covariance comes entirely from the two boundary split sizes.
