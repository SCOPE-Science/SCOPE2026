For every radius \(1<r<\sqrt2\), an unweighted backward shift on a tree using only one-child and two-child levels has full spectrum the closed disk of radius \(\sqrt2\) but point spectrum precisely the disk of radius \(r\), with its boundary circle either included or excluded at will. Both branching types occur in arbitrarily long blocks; all other spectral points are continuous-spectrum points, and no residual spectrum occurs.
