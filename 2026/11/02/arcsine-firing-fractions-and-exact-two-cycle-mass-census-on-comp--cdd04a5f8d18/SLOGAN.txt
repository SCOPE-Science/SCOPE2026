Balanced complete-bipartite parallel chip-firing has exactly \(\frac{n^{2n}}2\sum_{k=0}^n((n)_k/n^k)^2\) primitive two-cycle orbits, asymptotic to \(\sqrt{\pi n}\,n^{2n}/4\). In a uniformly chosen orbit and temporal phase, the firing fraction on one shore tends to the arcsine distribution, not a Gaussian concentration; an explicit polynomial also counts orbits at every conserved chip mass.
