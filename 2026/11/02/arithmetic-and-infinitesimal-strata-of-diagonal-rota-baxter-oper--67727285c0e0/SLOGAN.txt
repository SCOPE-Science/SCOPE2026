For the perfect cyclic evolution algebra \(e_i^2=e_{i+1}\) in every dimension \(n\ge3\), diagonal Rota–Baxter operators of nonzero weight form exactly two isolated reduced points and the punctured cyclotomic scheme \(t^{2^n-1}=1\), \(t\ne1\). This yields sharp modular nonreduced loci and exact finite-field conjugacy-orbit counts governed by the squaring map.
