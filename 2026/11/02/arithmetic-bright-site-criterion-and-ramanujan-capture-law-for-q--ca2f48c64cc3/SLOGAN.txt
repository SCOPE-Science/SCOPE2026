For a uniform-hopping quantum spider with a single absorbing hub, the eventual first-loss probability of every initial site is an exact Ramanujan-sum expression in the arm lengths. A vertex at distance \(t\) on arm \(i\) is captured with certainty exactly when \(\operatorname{lcm}_{j\ne i}\gcd(\ell_i+1,\ell_j+1)\mid t\); in particular, universal capture is equivalent to pairwise coprimality of the shifted arm lengths.
