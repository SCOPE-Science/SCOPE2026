For any weighted cyclic evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), the quadratic evolution operator has an exact arithmetic cycle census and the same preperiod-depth counts \((1+h2^{\min(j,s)})^n\), where \(q-1=2^s h\) with \(h\) odd, regardless of nonzero weights. Every cycle vertex has an explicitly uniform finite rooted preimage tree, while the cycle lengths depend on one computable coefficient obstruction modulo \(\gcd(q-1,2^n-1)\).
