For a uniform quantum path with one absorbing site and arbitrarily selected positive-rate local dephasers, all surviving dark states are indexed by a single integer \(g=\gcd(N+1,c,S)\). There are exactly \(g-1\) such modes, and the eventual capture from an initially occupied site \(a\) is \(1\) when \(g\mid a\) and \(1-g/(N+1)\) otherwise; complete capture for all preparations occurs precisely when \(g=1\).
