In a fixed–free uniform harmonic chain, any positive pattern of ground and interparticle viscous dashpots has exactly \((g-1)/2\) undamped modes, where \(g=\gcd(2n+1,\{p\},\{2b-1\})\); all other modes decay exponentially, irrespective of dashpot strengths. A velocity kick at mass \(j\) retains precisely the fraction \(g/(2n+1)\) of its energy when \(g\nmid j\), and none when \(g\mid j\), revealing a common arithmetic rule for two physically different damping mechanisms.
