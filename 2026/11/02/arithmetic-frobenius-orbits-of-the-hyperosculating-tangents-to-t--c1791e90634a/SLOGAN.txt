For the standard Klein quartic \(X^3Y+Y^3Z+Z^3X=0\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), the 28 geometric hyperosculating tangents form Frobenius orbits of sizes \(1,3,6,6,6,6\). Exactly \(1+3\mathbf1_{3\mid n}+24\mathbf1_{6\mid n}\) of them are rational over \(\mathbb F_{3^n}\), and they all split over \(\mathbb F_{3^6}\).
