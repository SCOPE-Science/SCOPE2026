For uniformly sampled perfect evolution algebras with monomial structure matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), the full idempotent-count law factors over permutation cycles and has exact cycle-index formulas for every moment. The fields supporting idempotent-free examples, their sharp first dimensions, and their polynomially decaying probabilities are controlled by multiplicative orders of \(2\) modulo odd primes dividing \(q-1\).
