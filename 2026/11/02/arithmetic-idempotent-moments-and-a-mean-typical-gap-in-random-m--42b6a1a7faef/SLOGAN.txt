A uniform labeled weighted-permutation evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) has exactly \(n+1\) idempotents **on average**, but its typical idempotent count is only \(n^{\kappa_q+o_{\mathbb P}(1)}\) with \(\kappa_q<1\). Explicit finite-field arithmetic determines every moment and gives a parity-sensitive idempotent-free law over \(\mathbb F_4\).
