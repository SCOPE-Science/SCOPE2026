For the smooth quartic \(X^4+Y^4+XZ^3=0\), all twenty geometric flex supports split into six rational Galois orbits of sizes \(1,1,2,4,4,8\) over the exact field \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{24})\). In every characteristic above three, the number of rational flexes depends only on the field size modulo twenty-four, and all flexes become rational over the quadratic extension.
