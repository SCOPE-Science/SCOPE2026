Every finite medial commutative quandle has exactly \(2^{|Q|}\) complex idempotents, while its real idempotents and all their Peirce characteristic polynomials admit an exact character-orbit census. The two nine-element midpoint quandles from \(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\) and \((\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^2\) have respectively \(80\) and \(512\) real idempotents, despite equal complex totals.
