For the affine torus map of Wang and Ding, the translation difference \(\delta=e-2f\) divides all dynamics into an irrational phase with no periodic points and full-torus Haar-typical orbit closures, versus a rational phase with exact Möbius-counted circles of periodic points and one-dimensional typical orbit closures. In the published numerical example, there are exactly \(349525\) primitive ten-period circles, while almost every orbit is dense in only ten parallel circles.
