On a single branch of a nondegenerate hyperbola, complex polynomials are determined by their magnitudes up to global phase at every degree precisely when \(\arctan(b/a)/\pi\) is irrational (for positive semiaxes \(a,b\)). At rational angles, the complete ambiguity quotient factors through a Dickson polynomial, and the first ambiguous degree is exactly \(q/\gcd(q,2)\); below that cutoff any \(4n+1\) distinct branch samples suffice for degree at most \(n\).
