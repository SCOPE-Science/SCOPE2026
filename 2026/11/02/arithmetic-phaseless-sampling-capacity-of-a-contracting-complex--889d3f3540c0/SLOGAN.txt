Squared-modulus samples at a single contracting spiral \(\rho^n e^{in\theta}\) recover every holomorphic germ up to phase exactly for irrational \(\theta/\pi\); for rational \(\theta/\pi=p/q\), they recover all degree-\(d\) polynomials exactly when \(d<q\). In the recoverable polynomial regime, \((d+1)^2\) consecutive samples invert a complete Hermitian Vandermonde phase lift, and this count is sharp for linear lifted recovery.
