In particular, the 2023 theorem's five-branch-point and characteristic hypotheses fail here. Targeted mathematical-record and primary-literature searches found no statement covering the full claim, but an unindexed previous calculation is a residual risk; search nonappearance does not certify world-first priority.

The question addresses the natural Jacobian \(p\)-rank and \(a\)-number stratifications of a genus-six cyclic trigonal family. Beyond an isolated computation, it identifies an entire reducible nonordinary divisor with a genus-three component, a rank-zero line, a finite reduced exceptional set and the exact collapse pattern of the Cartier rank. This furnishes explicit boundary phenomena relevant to arithmetic stratification of cyclic covers. No claim of a theorem about all trigonal curves is made.
