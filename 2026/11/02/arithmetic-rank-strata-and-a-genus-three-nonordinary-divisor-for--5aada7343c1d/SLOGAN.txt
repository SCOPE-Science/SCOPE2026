For the smooth characteristic-five genus-six cyclic covers \(y^3=x^7+ax^3+bx+1\), the Jacobian \(5\)-rank is exactly \(0\), \(2\), or \(4\), with the non-maximal locus formed by a line and an irreducible genus-three divisor. Besides the rank-zero line, there are exactly \(91\) isolated reduced rank-zero parameters in thirteen seventh-root orbits, while the \(a\)-number is \(2\) everywhere except for the unique parameter \((0,0)\), where it is \(4\).
