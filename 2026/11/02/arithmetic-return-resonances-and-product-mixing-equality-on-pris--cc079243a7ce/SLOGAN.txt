The long-time return probability of a quantum excitation on the prism \(C_n\square K_2\) has exactly two arithmetic resonances: multiples of \(4\) and of \(6\) each raise it by \(2/n^2\). Equivalently, the Cartesian-product average-mixing inequality is an equality precisely for prism lengths divisible by neither \(4\) nor \(6\).
