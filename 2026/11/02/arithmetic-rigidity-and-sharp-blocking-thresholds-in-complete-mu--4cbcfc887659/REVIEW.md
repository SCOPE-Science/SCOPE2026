# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof checks unrestricted unilateral moves, lexicographic friend and enemy counts, all positive type sizes, and the two cases of a genuine coalition deviation. In each Nash partition, a largest coalition must meet every type; comparing possible moves in both directions forces all coalitions to have the same type composition. Multinomial counting then gives the complete divisor sum. For blocking, summing type-specific friend inequalities forces a candidate deviation to have at least the original coalition size and excludes equality as a strict improvement. The decisive integer step at one additional participant differentiates enemies from neutrals; two additional participants from different types suffice for strictly improved payoffs. Exhaustive exact finite checks are supplementary and not taken as infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with reservations

The direct self-review is Chen, Csáji, Roy and Simola (arXiv:2203.09655), whose Observation 2 and Appendix B.3 prove existence for all symmetric friendship graphs. General existence and the known grand-coalition protection are **not** claimed new. A previously reported equal-sized two-type signed-additive Nash characterization implies some restricted instances but not the arbitrary multipartite enumeration and enemy/neutral blocking thresholds; the previous two-type fractional result is a different utility model. The foundational 2004 general-existence work was accessible in abstract form only, so its unseen full text is an explicit priority uncertainty. No reviewed source gave the complete all-size classification and both sharp coalition-blocking radii. Search nonmatches do not establish worldwide uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The family is a natural model of cross-group friendship with within-group nonfriends. The theorem completely enumerates Nash outcomes for arbitrary positive social-group populations and identifies the **least collective coordination needed to overturn each nongrand Nash equilibrium**. The one-person difference between neutrals and enemies, despite equal Nash sets, gives a useful structural boundary beyond the generic existence guarantee.

## Scope and limitations

Symmetric complete multipartite friendship and lexicographic friend-first utilities are essential. No claim is made for fractional/additively separable utility, restricted group formation, acceptance-based moves, or general social graphs. No external independent validation has occurred. Residual unindexed-literature and older inaccessible-full-text risks persist.
