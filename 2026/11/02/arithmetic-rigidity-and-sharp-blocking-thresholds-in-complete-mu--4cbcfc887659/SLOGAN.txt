On complete-multipartite friendship networks, every lexicographic friend-appreciation Nash partition has identical membership of every social type in every coalition, yielding an exact divisor-sum count. For any nongrand Nash partition with coalition size \(s\), an all-strict blocking coalition first exists at \(s+2\) agents, while weak blocking first appears at \(s+1\) under neutrality or \(s+2\) under enmity.
