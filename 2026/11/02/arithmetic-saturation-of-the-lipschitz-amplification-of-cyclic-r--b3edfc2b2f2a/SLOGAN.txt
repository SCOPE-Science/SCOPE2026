The cyclic running median has maximal possible global \(\ell_p\) perturbation amplification \((2r+1)^{1/p}\) exactly when an explicit two-weight subset-sum condition holds; this is automatic on odd cycles but can fail arithmetically on even ones. On the shortest nontrivial even cycle, its exact amplification drops to \((r+1)^{1/p}\), although the window covers \(2r+1\) sites.
