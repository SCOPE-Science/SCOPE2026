The canonical 48-point complex sextactic configuration of the Fermat quartic has a finite-flat integral model whose only nonreduced reductions are at \(2\) and \(3\). In characteristic \(3\) it becomes sixteen curvilinear triple points, exactly the non-coordinate \(\mathbb F_9\)-rational points of the Hermitian quartic; in characteristic \(2\) it becomes three noncurvilinear length-16 points.
