The twelve classical undulation tangent lines of the Komiya–Kuribayashi quartic retain their 66 distinct intersections in every characteristic at least seven, but degenerate precisely at the rational primes 2, 3, and 5. Their 66 Gaussian-primitive marked pair intersections specialize with respective weight distributions \((48,4^3,2^3)\), \((6^6,1^{30})\), and \((9^6,1^{12})\), revealing a characteristic-three Hermitian-line configuration and two distinct nonreduced/line-split quartic fibers.
