## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem gives a mathematically motivated complete classification for an infinite family of finite coherent networks. It quantifies a sharp loss of capture due to exact lattice symmetry, proves when rational directional anisotropy removes all dark modes, and exposes the placement cost of an ostensibly second absorbing corner. The explicit irrational-ratio collision prevents a misleading unconditional robustness claim. The result concerns eventual capture in a finite network, not transfer time or disorder tolerance.
