On prime-sided open quantum grids, a single corner absorber captures a maximally mixed excitation completely for every positive rational hopping ratio, except on a homogeneous equal-sided square, where the exact captured fraction is \(1/2+1/(p-1)^2\). In that square, adding an opposite-corner absorber is entirely redundant, while an adjacent-corner absorber raises the accessible dimension to \(3d^2-d+2\) for side length \(2d=p-1\).
