# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact monomial-basis formula gives a directed partial injection on positive indices, hence disjoint paths. Reduction of the index multiplier to coprime numerator and denominator proves the three chain regimes, and the explicit \(d q^N r\) construction supplies infinitely many blocks of every finite size in the incomparable regime. The spectrum, Fredholm failure, complete matrix-polynomial norm and strong-power assertions then follow from standard unilateral-shift theory and weakly null tests. The accompanying checker verifies selected finite path identities; it does not establish the infinite statements.

## Value — PASS
The two-symbol arithmetic comparison is intrinsic: each composition is an isometry, yet reversing divisibility switches infinite forward chains to backward chains, and incomparable reduced degrees create finite Jordan blocks of unbounded size. This classifies point spectrum and strong-power behavior while revealing a common full Fredholm spectrum and exact amplified quotient norm despite those distinct dynamics. It directly refines the structural understanding of the product class singled out in recent Schatten research. It does not classify general inner symbols.
