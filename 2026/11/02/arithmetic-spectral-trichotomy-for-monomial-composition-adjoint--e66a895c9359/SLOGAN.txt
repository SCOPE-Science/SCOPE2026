The Hardy composition–adjoint product \(C_{z^m}C_{z^n}^*\) has an arithmetic trichotomy: forward unilateral shifts, backward unilateral shifts, or finite Jordan chains according to divisibility of \(m\) and \(n\). Every unequal pair nevertheless has the full closed disk as its Fredholm-essential spectrum and an exactly isometric, completely amplified polynomial calculus modulo compact operators.
