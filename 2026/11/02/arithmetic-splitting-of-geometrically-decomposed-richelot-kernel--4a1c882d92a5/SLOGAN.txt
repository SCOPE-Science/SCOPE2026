For the genus-two pencil \(y^2=x^6+sx^3+1\) in every characteristic at least seven, the full Frobenius cycle structure of the geometrically decomposed Richelot-kernel scheme is determined by \(q\bmod3\) and the exceptional loci \(s=0\), \(s^2=-50\). All kernels are rational when \(q\equiv1\pmod3\), while when \(q\equiv2\pmod3\) the nonrational ones occur in pairs and become rational over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\).
