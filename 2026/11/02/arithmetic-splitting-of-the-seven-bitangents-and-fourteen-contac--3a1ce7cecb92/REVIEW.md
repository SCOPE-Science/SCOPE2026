**PASS.** The projective smoothness test and full two-chart line parametrization prove that all geometric bitangents are \(z+t^3x+ty=0\) with \(t^7=1\). A separable quadratic produces the complete fourteen-contact incidence scheme and explicit coordinates. Frobenius square maps on \(\mu_7\) and on the two nontrivial cube roots give cycle types \((1,3,3)\) and \((2,6,6)\), hence the zeta formulas. The accompanying GF(64) verifier independently checks all contact coordinates, every one of seven Fano triple vertices, and orbit partitions.

## Originality
**PASS (qualified).** Wall 1995, as quoted and geometrically spelled out in Dolgachev--Kondō Proposition 5.8, already determines the seven geometric bitangents and Fano arrangement of ordinary quartics in characteristic two. Van Lint's Example 3.12 explicitly concerns the same Klein equation and records its \(\mathbb F_2\)-line, the two \(\mathbb F_4\)-contacts, and absence of \(\mathbb F_8\)-contacts. Neither inspected source states the full explicit seven-line Frobenius parameterization, the remaining twelve contact flags in two six-cycles, or the two finite-scheme zeta functions. Elkies treats a different arithmetic twist and its point-count Frobenius, which does not fix the descended bitangent cycles for this model. An earlier unlocated arithmetic bitangent table remains a residual priority risk.
