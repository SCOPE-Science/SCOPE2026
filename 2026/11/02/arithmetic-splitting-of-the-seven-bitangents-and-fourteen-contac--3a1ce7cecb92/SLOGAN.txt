The characteristic-two Klein quartic \(x^3y+y^3z+z^3x=0\) has seven geometric bitangents and fourteen distinct tangency flags; on this arithmetic model Frobenius acts with cycle lengths \((1,3,3)\) on lines and \((2,6,6)\) on contact flags. The complete degree-six contact orbits refine the known seven-line Fano arrangement and the previously recorded degree-two contacts.
