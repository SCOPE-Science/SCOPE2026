For the characteristic-eleven Picard slice \(y^3=x^4+a x^2+b x+1\), the nonordinary divisor has a single ordinary twelve-fold point on the smooth parameter base, with tangent slopes \(r^{12}=3\) and Frobenius orbit lengths \((1,1,2,2,2,2,2)\). Exactly five of its \(111\) smooth \(\mathbb F_{11}\)-parameters have geometric \(p\)-rank zero.
