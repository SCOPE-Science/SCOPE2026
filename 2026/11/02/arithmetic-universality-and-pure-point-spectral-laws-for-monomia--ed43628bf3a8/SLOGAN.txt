Every positive-degree cutoff of the Hardy monomial cross-composition product \(C_{z^p}C_{z^q}^*\), for distinct integers \(p,q\ge2\), has a complete unweighted Jordan-block description determined only by \(\gcd(p,q)\) and the larger exponent; these parameters are also necessary for unitary equivalence at every cutoff. Its symmetrized finite-section eigenvalues converge to an explicit **purely atomic law with dense support** on \([-1,1]\), independent of the smaller coprime exponent, and its numerical-radius deficit has an exact logarithmic-square constant.
