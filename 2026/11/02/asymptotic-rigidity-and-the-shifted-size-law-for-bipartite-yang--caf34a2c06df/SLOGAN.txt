The number of bipartite set-theoretic Yang–Baxter solution classes on \(n\) points has a three-term logarithmic asymptotic with a nonzero \(n^2/\log n\) correction. Almost every class is rigid, and its canonical permutation block has size \(n/2+n/(4\log n)+o_{\mathbb P}(n/\log n)\), revealing a systematic bias away from equal-sized parts.
