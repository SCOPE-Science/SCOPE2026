The nonempty-subset power semigroup of a finite rectangular band has a completely explicit automorphism group: cardinality-preserving permutations of row and column projections, combined with arbitrary permutations inside nonrectangular support fibers. It preserves singleton subsets under every automorphism exactly when both band dimensions exceed one (or the band itself has one element), and its smallest two-sided example has automorphism group of order \(2880\).
