For the incidence algebra of any three-level ordinal sum with two minimal elements, the automorphism orbits in the square of its radical are classified by projective-column multisets under an explicit dihedral-type action. A closed rational generating function counts all orbits over every finite field, with rank-zero, rank-one, and rank-two orbit counts \(1\), \(2t\), and \(O_t(q)-2t-1\).
