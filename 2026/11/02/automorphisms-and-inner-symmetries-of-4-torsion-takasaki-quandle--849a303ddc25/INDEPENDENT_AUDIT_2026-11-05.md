## correctness

PASS

For A=(Z/4Z)^d, d>=1, residues x=a+2b with a,b in F_2^d give exactly (a,b)*(c,e)=(a,b+a+c). Inner orbits are fixed-a fibers. Preserving this operation forces the induced fiber permutation pi to satisfy pi(a+u)+pi(a)=Lu for one invertible F_2-linear L; the fiber map is Lb+t_a, with arbitrary t_a. Conversely all F_{L,v,t} preserve *. The kernel and affine section give V^V semidirect AGL_d(2). The right-translation functions r_c(a)=a+c have span generated by the nonconstant identity function and d constant-coordinate functions, of dimension d+1; pair products give d-dimensional displacement. Ordinary affine holomorph order 2^{2d+d^2}|GL_d(2)| yields index 2^{d(2^d-d-1)}. Independent exhaustive F2^2 orbit/permutation assignments reproduce 6144 automorphisms, 24 affine fiber permutations and right-translation rank 3; d=1 is affine-only, and the all-d proof does not depend on enumeration.

## originality

PASS

Qualified PASS for the explicit arbitrary-rank even-dihedral product classification, nonaffine index and especially a genuine correction of a false earlier inner-group rank. The 2017 source actually states Inn(T((Z/4)^d)) is elementary abelian of rank 2^d; direct linear dependencies establish rank d+1, a different result for d>=2. Hou 2011 claims an automorphism group of any Alexander quandle, which might imply the full-group part by specialization, but its exact theorem was unavailable; this is a substantial unclosed implication risk. The 2026 Latin formula does not apply to a non-Latin even-core family, and that paper still asks about even-dihedral products. Do not claim priority over Hou without reading the paper.
