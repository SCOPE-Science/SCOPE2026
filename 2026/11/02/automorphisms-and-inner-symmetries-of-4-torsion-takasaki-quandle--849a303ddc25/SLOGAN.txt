For every \(d\ge1\), the full automorphism group of the Takasaki quandle on \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^d\) is the split extension \(\mathbb F_2^{d\,2^d}\rtimes\operatorname{AGL}_d(2)\), with arbitrary translations on its \(2^d\) intrinsic fibers. Its inner automorphism group has order \(2^{d+1}\), correcting the larger value previously stated for \(d\ge2\), and the first nontrivial even-dihedral product \(R_4^2\) has exactly \(6144\) quandle automorphisms.
