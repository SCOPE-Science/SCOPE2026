# Scientific review: commuting tuples of the \(B_2\) positive-root Lie algebra

## Correctness — PASS

The Lie algebra is unambiguously presented over the integers by the two structure constants \(1\) and \(2\). For each pair the expanded bracket is \((a_i b_j-a_jb_i)e_3+2(a_ic_j-a_jc_i)e_4\). When two is invertible and \(a\ne0\), the two vanishing-minor families force \(b,c\) to be multiples of \(a\); at \(a=0\) the two equations impose no restrictions. This is precisely the union of the linear component and a rank-one determinantal component. The intersection, dimensions, and singular loci follow from a nonzero-minor chart and the rank-zero tangent-space dimension. In characteristic two only the \(a,b\) minors remain, so the irreducible determinantal classification applies. The count uses a disjoint inclusion-exclusion identity for rank-one matrices. The exact original-bracket enumeration over four prime/power fields and ten reduced test cases matched all predicted counts and geometric-locus criteria. Residual correctness risk: no scheme-theoretic reducedness statement is attempted; the theorem is explicitly about reductions.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge priority)

Goodwin–Röhrle (arXiv:1209.1289, §§1 and 3.5) already prove the characteristic-zero pair result, and Farnsteiner (arXiv:1802.09223, Lemma 5.2.1) treats the good-characteristic commuting pair; those cases are excluded from novelty. Ngo (arXiv:1401.6820, §2.1) studies tuples of restricted nilpotents in the full Lie algebra under good-characteristic restrictions; Roman (arXiv:2602.02935) addresses full classical Lie algebras in bad characteristic, not the Borel positive-root nilradical and arbitrary tuple lengths. The characteristic-two formula **is already implied** by the established all-arity commuting-tuple classification of the three-block \(1,1,1\) Heisenberg Lie algebra, after adding one central coordinate to each tuple entry. The novelty asserted here therefore excludes that specialization as well as the known good-characteristic pair case, and concerns the good-characteristic \(m\ge3\) components, singularities, arithmetic counts and their comparison with the existing bad-prime formula. Published mathematical-record searches and earlier claims did not identify a complete dominating statement. Priority is not certified; a stronger historical result using an equivalent rank-one description could cover the finding, so that remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS

Commuting tuples are natural representation-theoretic parameter spaces, and the positive-root nilradical of the smallest non-simply-laced root system is the first place where the bad-prime coefficient \(2\) vanishes. The finding precisely describes how the geometry changes: two components versus one, different dimensions, singular-locus geometry, and exact arithmetic/motivic counts for every tuple length. In particular, for every \(m\ge3\) the good-characteristic variety has two components of unequal dimensions even though the known pair variety is equidimensional. The complete comparison across the bad-prime boundary is informative, while the bad-prime determinant description itself is not novel.
