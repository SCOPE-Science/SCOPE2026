The reduced commuting \(m\)-tuple variety of the four-dimensional \(B_2\) positive-root Lie algebra has exactly two components for \(\operatorname{char}k\ne2\), but becomes a single determinantal variety in characteristic two, for every \(m\ge2\). The classification gives exact component and singular-locus dimensions together with uniform finite-field point counts and motivic classes; the characteristic-zero pair case and characteristic-two Heisenberg specialization are covered by earlier results. The additional odd-characteristic classification for \(m\ge3\) is the principal new contribution.
