The exact zeta functions of the Wiman plane sextic at the three exceptional primes \(2,3,5\) are determined, including \(\#W_5(\mathbb F_{5^n})=5^n+1-4(-1)^n\). The alternating correction arises from four rational but nonsplit nodes in characteristic five, while the characteristic-three fibre is a conic and its four rational tangents and the characteristic-two fibre is three doubled concurrent lines.
