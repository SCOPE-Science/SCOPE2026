For a disjoint union of cliques, the inverse monoid of partial graph automorphisms admits a nilpotent of the largest possible index precisely when all component orders differ by at most one. If \(n=hr+t\) for \(r\) components, its exact multiplicity is \(\prod_i s_i!\) times \(r!\) when \(t=0\), or times \(t!(r-t)!\) otherwise; these maps are exactly the whole-vertex shifts along periodic clique-label words.
