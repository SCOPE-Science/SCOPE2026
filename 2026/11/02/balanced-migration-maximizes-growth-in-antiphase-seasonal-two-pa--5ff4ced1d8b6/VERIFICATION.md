# Verification and evidence limits

The all-parameter statement follows from the exact normalized-monodromy trace and the Volterra identity with an everywhere-positive causal kernel. The central proof is analytic; no finite computation is substituted for positivity on the entire parameter domain.

`VERIFY.py` is a standalone Python 3 standard-library script. Command: `python VERIFY.py`. The observed deterministic result was 2,400 comparisons of the hyperbolic closed form with independently evaluated two-by-two matrix exponentials, 950 monotonicity families, five signed viability sample points and 100 checks at zero seasonal contrast.

The verifier checks representative numerical instances and guards against transcription, sign and normalization mistakes; it is not a formal proof of the continuum claim. Floating-point comparisons are subject to stated tolerances. The strict result assumes positive season contrast, fixed positive total migration, and positive period; the code's zero-contrast checks deliberately exercise the excluded equality limit. Full-text reading and literature searches cannot establish absolute publication priority; the science review explicitly records residual priority uncertainty.
