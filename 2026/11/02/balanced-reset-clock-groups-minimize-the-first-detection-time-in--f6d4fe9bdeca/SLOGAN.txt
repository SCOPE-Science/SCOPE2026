For identical Brownian searchers sharing \(K\) independent reset clocks in disjoint synchronized groups, allocating the \(N\) searchers as equally as possible among clocks uniquely minimizes the entire first-arrival-time distribution in stochastic order. The same balanced-partition principle holds weakly for arbitrary renewal clocks, and splitting a group across an extra independent clock never slows detection; Poisson–Brownian search makes every nontrivial improvement strict at all positive times.
