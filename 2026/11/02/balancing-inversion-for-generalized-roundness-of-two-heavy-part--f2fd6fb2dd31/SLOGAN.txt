For the graph metric of \(K_{a,b,1,\ldots,1}\), the supremal negative-type exponent strictly **decreases** under balancing the two variable parts if their total size exceeds the number of parts, and strictly **increases** if it is smaller. At equality, every split has the same exponent \(\log_2(r/(r-2))\); the exact exponent in all cases is the lower root of a two-variable quadratic Rayleigh equation transformed by \(-\log_2(1-\mu)\).
