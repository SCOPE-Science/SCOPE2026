# Scientific review — Bessel-ratio basin weights for a kinetically constrained spin ring

## Correctness — PASS

The final claim fixes the labelled cycle, two local flip rules, distinct-site activation hazard, independent Bernoulli initial law, and all-empty hitting event. Any adjacent occupied pair makes success impossible; conditioning on success excludes every facilitated birth. The recursion for a maximal alternating block accounts for precisely \(m\) removable occupied sites and \(m+1\) activatable zeros, and yields a uniquely determined Riccati series. The hypergeometric differential equation proves the closed Bessel ratio. Labelled cyclic block enumeration and a separately evaluated strictly alternating even-cycle case account for every initially admissible pattern without omission. Bessel-zero location, positive Taylor coefficients, the two nonzero leading odd powers and the resulting isolated simple pole prove the universal unit-prefactor exponential asymptotic. The equal-rate tangent reduction is elementary.

The standalone `VERIFY.py` program passed 174 checks: 40 finite-state generating-coefficient equalities, 90 recurrence identities, 40 probability interval tests, and 4 explicit-rate bounds, all with exact rational calculations except the final four numerical bounds. High-precision independent root calculations agreed with the displayed rates. These finite checks support, but do not replace, the proof for all positive parameters and all finite ring lengths.

## Originality — PASS, with stated priority risk

A targeted semantic research-record search and primary-source searches checked the object under aliases including irreversible spin-chain absorption, facilitated creation, isolated-spin removal, frozen occupied pairs, cyclic Bernoulli basin weights, Catalan/Riccati recurrence and Bessel/hypergeometric generating functions. No matched record was found that implies the displayed all-length, all-density and all-rate generating function or its unit-amplitude asymptotic. The numerically closest identified records concern unrelated spin dimers, deterministic cellular automata or geometric first exits, and their statements do not imply this theorem.

The closest primary literature was inspected at substantial length. Marić–Garrahan–Zadnik (2026), arXiv:2609.18882, develops low-temperature time-scale expansions for detailed-balance East/Fredrickson–Andersen chains; it neither specifies this irreversible two-rate generator nor derives its all-empty basin probabilities. Krapivsky–Luck (2022), arXiv:2211.12815, and Godrèche–Luck (2004), arXiv:cond-mat/0412077, establish attractor statistics for a different constrained Glauber-Ising descent rule in which *both* signs of isolated spin can flip; it maps to dimer deposition and is not the present facilitated-birth/frozen-pair process. These are claim-level, not just title-level comparisons. Cao–Jiang–Zhang (arXiv:2610.36829v1, released subsequently) explicitly obtains the equal-density, equal-rate special-case exponent \(2/\pi\) using Euler zigzag numbers. That case is excluded from the newness claim; their Appendix B was inspected and does not cover the full density-and-rate family or its full finite-size generating function. A general theorem not identified in those sources or in the targeted searches could still subsume the formulas; independent publication-priority assessment remains outstanding. Search absence by itself was not treated as novelty proof.

## Value — PASS

The object is a natural irreversible limit of facilitated lattice-spin kinetics, with an attracting empty phase competing with kinetically permanent adjacent occupied pairs. The exact probability of reaching the empty phase from independently prepared spins is a physically meaningful basin weight. The two parameters independently encode preparation density and kinetic competition, and the theorem determines *every* finite-size basin probability together with a sharp large-size rate and leading coefficient. The exact Bessel-ratio description is more informative than a finite enumeration or a small numerical improvement, and can serve as a benchmark for kinetic simulation and absorption-phase calculations. No unrelated downstream theorem or general universality claim is required for this value.

## Limits and independent assessment

The theorem does not apply to arbitrary spin-flip rates, finite-temperature leakage, absorption-time distributions, or infinite-volume absorbing-state transitions. The special-function singularity proof invokes the classical Bessel real-zero theorem. The listed numerical checks are finite and do not prove the asymptotic. Potential subsumption by a wider adsorption/kinetic-constraint formula has not been eliminated, and publication-priority verification by a separate investigator is absent.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
