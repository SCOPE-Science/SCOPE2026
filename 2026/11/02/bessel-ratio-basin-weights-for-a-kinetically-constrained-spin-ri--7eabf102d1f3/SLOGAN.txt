An irreversible one-dimensional constrained spin ring has an exactly computable all-empty basin weight for every Bernoulli starting density and every positive ratio of activation to removal rates. Its full finite-size generating function is a Bessel-function ratio, and its large-ring survival rate is the unique positive pole with unit leading amplitude; equal rates give \(\Lambda(p,1)=\sqrt{p(1-p)}/\arctan\sqrt{p/(1-p)}\).
