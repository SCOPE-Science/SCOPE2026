Independent \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,2)\) sampling of a single endpoint-linear-interpolation residual per cell yields an unbiased quadrature rule that is pathwise exact for quadratics and has the sharp \(m^{-7}\) variance constant \(\int_0^1(f''')^2/25920\) for \(C^3\) functions. The beta density is uniquely quadratic-exact within the fixed-density independent residual family, and the centered quadrature error obeys both a Gaussian limit and a finite-sample subgaussian bound.
