For independent binary letters with probabilities that may vary arbitrarily but stay uniformly away from zero and one, the residue of the number of distinct nonempty scattered subsequences converges exponentially to an explicit universal arithmetic law. Modulo \(m\), the probability of residue \(r\) tends to \(\prod_{p\mid\gcd(m,r+2)}(1-p^{-1})/[m\prod_{p\mid m}(1-p^{-2})]\), independently of all source biases.
