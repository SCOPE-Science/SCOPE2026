The multipath (directed linear forest) complex of the fully bidirected \(K_{3,3}\) has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{19}S^3\vee\bigvee^{12}S^4\), unlike the one-way orientation's \(\bigvee^4S^1\). An explicit acyclic matching and zero signed integral Morse boundary establish the mixed-dimensional wedge, not just its Betti numbers.
