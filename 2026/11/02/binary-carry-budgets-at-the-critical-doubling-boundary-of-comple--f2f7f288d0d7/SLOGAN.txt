At the critical exact-doubling boundary, a floor-scaled sequence represents every nonnegative integer by distinct indexed terms precisely when a finite Brown-deficit budget can pay for every one-bit in the scale's binary tail. All admissible scales are rational and form a zero-box-dimension set, yet explicit finite-prefix families have exactly \(\sum_{j\le(m-1)^2}\binom{L-2}{j}\) admissible dyadic scales at binary resolution \(L\), giving unbounded polynomial growth in \(L\).
