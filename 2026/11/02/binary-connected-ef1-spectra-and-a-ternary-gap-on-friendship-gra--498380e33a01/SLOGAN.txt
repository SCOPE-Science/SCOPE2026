For the friendship graph of \(k\ge3\) triangles sharing one hub, every common binary additive valuation permits a connected ordinary EF1 allocation into exactly \(n\) nonempty bundles if and only if \(n=1\) or \(k\le n\le2k+1\). At the sharp positive boundary \(n=k\), for every \(k\ge4\) a common three-level valuation in \(\{0,1,2\}\) destroys the guarantee, separating binary fairness from general identical additive fairness.
