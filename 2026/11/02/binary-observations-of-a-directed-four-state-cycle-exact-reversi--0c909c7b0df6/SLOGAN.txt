A stationary clockwise four-state kinetic cycle can look perfectly time-symmetric in every two- or three-snapshot binary measurement, while four evenly spaced snapshots reveal its direction at cubic order. For parity observations, complete apparent reversibility occurs exactly when one pair of opposite microscopic jump rates agrees; otherwise the four-frame reverse-path information is \(J(bc+ad-cd-ab)\log((bc+ad)/(cd+ab))h^3+O(h^4)\).
