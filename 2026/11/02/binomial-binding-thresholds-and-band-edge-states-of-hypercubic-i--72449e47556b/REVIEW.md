# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The Hamiltonian and band normalization are explicit. The free resolvent is represented both by Fourier integration and a product Bessel heat kernel. The finite-rank coefficient system is equivalent to the out-of-band eigenvalue problem, and Walsh characters exactly diagonalize the impurity matrix. Strict positivity, energy monotonicity, and weight ordering of the Bessel integrands yield the claimed unique roots and binomial eigenvalue multiplicities. At equality, the Fourier numerator vanishes to order equal to the Walsh weight, giving the precise \(d+2k>4\) square-summability test. The adjacent-corner ratio follows from the verified Bessel recurrence and vanishing integration-by-parts boundary terms. The 79 numerical checks passed but are only corroborating evidence.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence; priority provisional

The closest directly relevant comprehensive primary study located, Hiroshima–Muminov–Kuljanov (2018), classifies a different origin-plus-neighbor delta-potential arrangement and uses the known Birman–Schwinger method. It does not give the all-corners, arbitrary-spacing, binomial staircase, exact adjacent-corner ratio or Walsh-weight threshold-state classification. Trinkle (2006) addresses the Green function for extended defects and its numerical computation, not these bound-state counts. Slager et al. (2015) study impurity-bound states of topological multiband insulators, a different Hamiltonian and mechanism. General finite-rank impurity principles are prior art; the assessed novelty is the complete, nontrivial explicitly ordered hypercubic geometry specialization. Broader treatments of group-symmetric impurity clusters might imply parts of the result, and full historical priority is unverified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

For a natural cubic cluster of identical defects, the theorem determines the exact number, multiplicities and thresholds of high-energy bound states without diagonalizing an exponentially large impurity matrix. It distinguishes ordinary bound states from true square-summable band-edge states and from non-normalizable edge resonances, a physically important distinction for localization. The three-dimensional 8-defect cluster has a four-stage \(1,3,3,1\) onset and a precise factor-three gap between its first two thresholds. The closed formula extends to arbitrary integer spacing and dimension, giving direct reuse for defect-cluster design and threshold comparisons.

## Closest literature and limits

The most consequential overlap risk is broad finite-rank lattice spectral theory, especially other symmetry-adapted arrays. No equivalent hypercube-specific theorem was identified in the literature material inspected or in the searched finding summaries. Lack of an exact match is not a proof of global novelty. The theorem is restricted to equal positive point couplings, uniform adjacency and strictly above-band modes. Independent scientific priority and proof review are outstanding.
