Identical impurities at the corners of any spaced hypercube in a lattice of dimension \(d\ge3\) create localized eigenstates in a strictly ordered staircase with binomial multiplicities \(\binom dk\), and critical band-edge modes are square-summable exactly when \(d+2k>4\). For adjacent corners, the second binding threshold is exactly \(d/(d-2)\) times the first, giving an exact threefold ratio on the ordinary cubic lattice.
