For alternating binary words, deleting any embedded \((ab)^k\) from \((ab)^{m+k}\) yields exactly the concatenations of \(m\) blocks \(ab\) or \(ba\) with at most \(k\) runs of \(ba\). The exact number of distinct residuals is \(\sum_{j=0}^{\min\{2k,m\}}\binom{m}{j}\), attaining all \(2^m\) block words precisely when \(m\le2k\).
