# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The group law and square map are explicit. Central-character twisted group algebra has radical equal to the weighted alternating adjacency kernel, giving precise character degrees and multiplicities. The Frobenius–Schur average reduces to the quadratic Gauss sum, with the zero/Arf dichotomy proved via translation and nonsingular quadratic Gauss sums. Bipartite graphs have a bilinear quadratic form and an explicit involutory inverting conjugator; an odd cycle produces a radical vector on which the quadratic form is nonzero. The degree-four quaternionic example is calculated exactly. An exhaustive small-graph checker corroborates the identities, but is not an infinite proof.

**Originality: PASS on the checked literature, with residual historical risk.** O'Brien–Voll's 2012 character formulas assume nilpotency class below the field characteristic and cannot include the characteristic-two class-two case. Rossmann–Voll's graphical group work and Rossmann's conjugacy-class enumerator concern class numbers and rank distributions, neither of which determines character types or the existence of inverting involutions. No checked statement implies the graph-bipartiteness equivalence or the indicator-trichotomy formula. General quadratic Gauss sums and the classical indicator theorem are acknowledged ingredients. An unindexed result on special two-groups could cover an equivalent formulation; priority is not independently certified.

**Value: PASS.** The theorem characterizes an important finite-group property—every element being inverted by an involution—by a single intrinsic combinatorial condition across every graph and every even-characteristic finite field. It simultaneously computes all real/complex/quaternionic irreducible representation types, with explicit odd-cycle and quaternionic witnesses. This is structural information absent from class-count and character-degree enumerators.

**Closest sources and limitations.** arXiv:1203.3050v1 (class less than characteristic), arXiv:1908.09589v2 (graphical groups/class zeta functions), and arXiv:2107.05564v1 / DOI:10.1112/blms.12665 (graphical conjugacy class sizes). The result addresses only the stated even-characteristic graphical-group family. The finite experiment is limited to 75 binary graphs and three additional quaternary-field examples.
