For the maximal Kummer plane model \(y^{q+1}=x^n(x^n+1)\), the Gauss map is birational in every odd characteristic, and its dual has degree \((q+1)(n+1)\) except for the exact degree drops at the half-exponent boundary and at \(p\mid((q+1)/n-2)\). The latter case produces an infinite family of wild polar-cancellation drops of precisely \(2n\).
