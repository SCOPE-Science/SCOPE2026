In a uniform plane-oriented recursive tree, leaf survival by birth label has an exact multivariate product law and an explicit two-label covariance multiplier depending only on the younger label. The full birth-age profile of leaf counts has the closed covariance scaling kernel \(K(s,t)=s^{3/2}(6-3\sqrt t+t^{3/2})/9-s^2/3\) for \(0\le s\le t\le1\), refining the known variance of the total leaf count without claiming a new negative-association theorem.
