For iid uniform coupon draws, conditioning on a later first repeat makes full-collection time strictly smaller in stochastic order, at every alphabet size. This yields negative quadrant dependence and sharp decorrelation: \(\operatorname{Cov}(B_m,C_m)\sim-\sqrt{\pi m/8}\) while \(m\operatorname{Corr}(B_m,C_m)\to-\sqrt{3/(2\pi(4-\pi))}\).
