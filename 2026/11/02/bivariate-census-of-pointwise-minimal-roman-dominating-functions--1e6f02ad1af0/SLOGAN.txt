All pointwise-minimal Roman dominating functions on a labeled cycle admit a two-variable weight-and-defender census: for \(n\ge3\), the coefficient \([x^n](-xD_x/D)\), where \(D=1-zx-yz^2x^3-y^2z^4x^4-y^2z^4x^5\), counts them by their number of vertices labeled \(2\) and total weight. Cyclic gap rigidity yields a five-term recurrence and exact exponential growth \(\lambda^n\) with leading coefficient one, where \(\lambda\approx1.685137110\); the unweighted numerical sequence is already known independently, but its minimal-Roman interpretation and bivariate refinement are not established in the inspected literature.
