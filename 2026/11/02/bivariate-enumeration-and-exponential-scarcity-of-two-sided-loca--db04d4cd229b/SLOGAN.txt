For every labelled cycle of length at least five, both dominating/locating-dominating partitions and the stronger partitions into two locating-dominating sets have explicit bivariate rational-logarithmic enumerators. The latter partitions form an exponentially shrinking fraction, asymptotic to \((0.963525132395470)^n\), of all partitions of the first kind; the previously catalogued unweighted one-sided sequence is not claimed as new.
