Every open packing of \(K_m\square P_n\) for \(m\geq3\) is encoded by a four-state weighted word, giving an exact rational bivariate generating function by packing size and induced matching edges. The maximum-packing multiplicities admit explicit three-residue formulas in \(n\bmod3\), refining the previously known maximum cardinality \(\lceil2n/3\rceil\).
