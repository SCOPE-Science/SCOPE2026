A finite odd antiferromagnetic spin ring bonded symmetrically to two mutually antiferromagnetic apex spins has an exact first-order ground-state transition at \(K_{\mathrm c}=\sqrt{2J(1+\cos(\pi/n))L/n}\): the apex spins jump from antiparallel to parallel and a chiral conical rim appears. The transition supports a continuum of coexistence ground states, followed by exact cone saturation at \(K=J(1+\cos(\pi/n))\).
