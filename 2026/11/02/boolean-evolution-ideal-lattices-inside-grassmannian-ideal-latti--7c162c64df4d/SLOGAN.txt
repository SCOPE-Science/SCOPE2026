For independent-fibre blowups of connected graphs with nonsingular base adjacency over \(\mathbb R\), the ordinary ideals form a full Grassmannian subspace lattice above the unique minimal square ideal, while the evolution ideals with extendible natural bases form a product of smaller fibrewise subspace lattices. For double blowups the latter is a finite Boolean lattice, and the construction remains nonsimple even along uniformly expanding graph families.
