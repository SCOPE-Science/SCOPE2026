For a midpoint-launched finite-speed persistent particle, optimally splitting a fixed sum of endpoint collision-capture probabilities switches sharply from maximally unequal to exactly equal when the mean run length crosses the corridor length. At the critical rate every split has the same mean, but maximally unequal capture uniquely minimizes its variance (up to left–right exchange), with a sharp budget-independent variance advantage of \(4h^2/(3v^2)\) whenever \(0<Q\le1\).
