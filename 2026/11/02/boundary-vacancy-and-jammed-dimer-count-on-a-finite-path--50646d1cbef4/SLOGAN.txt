In discrete dimer parking on a finite path, the covariance between vacancy of the left endpoint and the total number of accepted dimers is strictly negative at every size \(n\ge4\) except \(n=5\), when it is zero. It converges to the nonzero constant \(-\bigl(e^{-1}+e^{-3}\bigr)/4\), despite both statistics having classical separately known marginal laws.
