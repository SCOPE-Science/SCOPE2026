For every fixed \(c\ge1\), the proposed binary valuation formula for Stirling numbers \(S(c2^h,k)\) fails at no more than an explicit \(c\)-dependent number of positive indices, uniformly in \(h\). A dominant-partition estimate proves Adelberg–Lengyel's density-one Conjecture 3.1 with the quantitative error \(O_c(2^{-h})\), while leaving the individual boundary exceptions unclassified.
