The number of cyclic vertices with no attached branches in a uniform random mapping admits an exact compulsory-root occupancy law, with total-variation binomial error at most \((r-1)/n\) conditional on \(r\) cyclic vertices. Its fluctuations around \(e^{-1}\) times the cyclic-vertex count are of order \(n^{1/4}\) and converge to a Rayleigh-mixed Gaussian law.
