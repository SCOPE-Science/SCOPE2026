For every finite Kripke-representable closure model, a cycle-index formula counts all nonisomorphic same-world Kripke presentations using only the complete modal-theory class sizes and required successor-type supports. The first one-type cases have exactly \(70\) and \(2340\) isomorphism classes on three and four worlds, respectively; the result counts presentations of fixed pointwise truth, not frames with merely the same validities.
