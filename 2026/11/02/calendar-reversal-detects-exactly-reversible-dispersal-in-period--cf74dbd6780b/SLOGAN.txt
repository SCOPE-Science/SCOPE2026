For a fixed irreducible dispersal network, reversing **every possible** positive periodic habitat-growth calendar preserves its entire period spectrum if and only if dispersal obeys detailed balance; testing spectral radii for calendars of length at most the number of habitats suffices. Three habitats and three seasons are simultaneously minimal for a strict reversal effect. An exact rational witness even switches a deterministic population between exponential decline and growth when only the calendar direction is changed.
