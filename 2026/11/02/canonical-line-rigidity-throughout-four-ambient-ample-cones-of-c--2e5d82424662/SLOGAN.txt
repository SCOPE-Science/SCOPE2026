Every smooth anticanonical Calabi–Yau fourfold in a blowup of \(\mathbb P^5\) along a linear \(\mathbb P^k\), \(0\le k\le3\), has four simple imaginary Hilbert-polynomial roots for every ambient-induced ample class \((aH-bE)|_Z\) with \(a>b>0\). The same computation yields the uniform strict inequality \((c_2(Z)\cdot L^2)^2>192L^4\).
