For every smooth complex hypersurface \(X\subset\mathbb P^5\) of degree \(d\ge6\), its scheme of pointed six-contact lines is a three-dimensional Gorenstein local complete intersection with ample dualizing bundle and explicitly computed canonical volume. For very general \(X\), the Plücker degree of the unpointed six-contact-line image is \(3d(d-5)(5d^3+25d^2-100d+48)\), equal to \(25{,}704\) for a sextic.
