# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses the published toric orbit-closure criterion [1, Theorem 1.1]. The canonical class follows from the point-blowup formula, and the orbit closures exhaust all coordinate strict transforms and exceptional coordinate subspaces. On an exceptional orbit closure the anticanonical bundle is ample. On a coordinate line the restricted canonical degree is nonzero except when \(n=3\) and both endpoints are blown up. For higher-dimensional strict transforms, the degree-\(n+1\) exponent polytope with selected upper bounds \(2\) is full-dimensional exactly under the claimed inequality. The opposite canonical restriction has no positive-power global sections by the codimension-two pushforward calculation, precluding a missing anti-big case. These arguments determine all dimensions and all selected center subsets. The embedded checker confirms 567 finite orbit-subspace cases but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The nearest primary source [1] proves a general criterion and classifies torus-*line*-centered blowups of \(\mathbb P^3\) in Proposition 6.7, not the point-centered tower or the exact cutoff in each dimension. The older paper [2, §5.3] proves tensor-generation for positive ample pullback with positive exceptional coefficients and for point blowups of a base with ample canonical bundle. Neither establishes the anti-ample-base canonical classification. Semantic mathematical-record comparisons found no implication-equivalent statement; such searches cannot prove historical uniqueness. The leading residual risk is an unlocated equivalent combinatorial specialization.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an exact all-dimension characterization of a natural geometric family for a recently introduced derived-positivity property. It identifies explicit minimal obstructions, a sharp dimension-four exception, and infinitely many examples where tensor-generation coexists with canonical non-nefness in both directions. These are structural phenomena rather than isolated computations.

## Remaining limitations
Only coordinate-point blowups over \(\mathbb C\) are covered. The proof imports the fully stated toric criterion; independent mathematical validation and definitive historical priority have not been performed.
