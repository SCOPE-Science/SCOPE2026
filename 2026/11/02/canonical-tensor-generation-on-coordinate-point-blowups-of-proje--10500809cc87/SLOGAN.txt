Blowing up any set of coordinate points of \(\mathbb P^4\) preserves tensor-generation of the canonical bundle, but blowing up two coordinate points of \(\mathbb P^3\) or three of \(\mathbb P^n\) for \(n\ge5\) obstructs it. In the positive cases with at least two centers and dimension at least four, the canonical bundle is neither nef nor anti-nef.
