For any high-carrier weighted Bergman spectral packet whose width \(L_m\) satisfies \(L_m\log m=o(m)\), the sharp \(A^2_\alpha\to A^4_\alpha\) dilation cutoff has an explicit carrier term plus the logarithm of its exact circle fourth-moment constant. The result yields a bandwidth-dependent exponent and the asymptotic ratio \(\lim 4m\log(R_{\mathrm{Sidon}}/R_{\mathrm{AP}})=\log(9/7)\) for two three-frequency packets.
