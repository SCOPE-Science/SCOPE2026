Among smooth plane quintics over \(\mathbf F_2\) containing all seven rational points, the Jacobian \(2\)-rank can only be \(2\), \(5\), or \(6\); the joint \((2\text{-rank},a\text{-number})\) spectrum has frequencies \((2,3):168\), \((2,1):252\), \((5,1):336\), and \((6,0):756\) for equations in fixed coordinates. Exact Cartier matrices and independent extension-field point counts verify the classification.
