In the cyclic-six genus-three hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^7+a x^4+x\) over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_{31}\), the marked Cartier-rank strata have ranks \(0,1,2,3\), with exactly \(3,2,12\), and infinitely many smooth parameter values, respectively. The three superspecial fibers occur at \(a=0,\pm12\); the nonzero examples are the first such marked smooth superspecial parameters in this family among prime characteristics at least five.
