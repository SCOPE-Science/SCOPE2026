For smooth characteristic-two cyclic trigonal genus-four curves \(y^3=f(x)\), the \(a\)-number is always \(2\), and the geometric \(2\)-rank is \(0\) or \(2\) according as the normalized quintic's cubic coefficient vanishes. All \(48\) rank-zero normalized models over \(\mathbb F_4\) nevertheless have non-supersingular Newton slopes \(1/4\) and \(3/4\), each fourfold, an exact small-field obstruction.
