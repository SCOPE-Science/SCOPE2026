On a finite intuitionistic chain, every proper inhabited constructive-neighbourhood frame is modally determined by two nondecreasing endpoint sequences; exactly \(\binom{2n-1}{n}^2\) distinct pairs of modal operators occur. Precisely the fraction \(2/(n+1)\) validates \(\mathsf D\), and every such observational class has a serial single-interval relational representative, even when its original neighbourhoods are disjoint.
