In the type-A 0-Hecke monoid, maximal pairwise-commuting families of **monoid idempotents** are precisely the parabolic longest elements indexed by ideals of unique binary search trees. There are \(\frac{1}{m+1}\binom{2m}{m}\) such families, and the size of each is determined by the recursive law \(s=1+s_Ls_R\), yielding the entire size distribution.
