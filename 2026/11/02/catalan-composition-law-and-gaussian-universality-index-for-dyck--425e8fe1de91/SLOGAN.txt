The complete semilength/universality-index distribution of binary Dyck words is the Catalan composition \(C(tu(1-tu)/(1-t))\), where \(u\) records scattered-subsequence universality. For a uniformly chosen Dyck word of semilength \(n\), the index has asymptotic mean \(2n/3\), variance \(4n/27\), and a Gaussian limit; this goes beyond previously known fixed-defect rational series.
