For Rule 184 on an indexed cyclic binary ring with \(k\) cars, the longest time to eliminate all adjacent minority symbols is exactly \(\min(k,n-k)-1\), and exactly \(n\operatorname{Cat}_{\min(k,n-k)-1}\) initial states attain it. Those extremizers have a unique distinguished particle and a Catalan plane-tree encoding; at fixed excess vacancy count \(h\), the probability of extremal delay tends to \(2^{-h-1}\).
