For any fixed non-eigenvector direction of a symmetric positive-definite matrix, a finite real Gaussian sketch makes its one-step harmonic Ritz value have an exact reciprocal-Student law and an infinite absolute first moment, regardless of sketch dimension. Averaging arbitrarily many independently sketched harmonic Ritz values does not concentrate: the averages converge to an explicit nondegenerate Cauchy distribution with scale \(\pi f_R(0)\).
