For every odd finite Kleene chain, the exact number of modal operators satisfying the three Celani–Menchón–Miguelez axioms is \(\binom{2m}{m}^2+\binom{2m}{m+1}\), with an analogous explicit normal-subclass census. Operators failing to preserve the negation center form only an asymptotic \(\sqrt{\pi m}/4^m\) fraction, and conditioned on that failure the positive displacement of the center converges to a geometric distribution of mass \(2^{-c}\) at height \(c\).
