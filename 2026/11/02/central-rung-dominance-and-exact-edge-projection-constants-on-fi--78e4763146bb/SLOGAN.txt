The induced \(\ell^\infty\) norm of the real electrical edge-gradient projector on every unweighted two-rail ladder has an exact rational recurrence; except for the four-edge square, only its central rung rows maximize the norm. Amplification first exceeds two at exactly five rungs. The sharp finite-ladder envelope is \(1+2/\sqrt3\), approached from below with an explicit parity-dependent exponential defect.
