# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** For any two ordered partitions, the products have identical nonempty intersection cells, and commutation is equivalent to absence of a reversed pair of cell indices. A commuting partner is bijective to independent ordered partitions on the specified blocks together with one merge bit for each adjacent interface; this proves the exact centralizer cardinality with no field or rank restriction. Strict log-convexity of ordered Bell numbers, by their positive moment representation, proves both extremal cases. The exponential generating function and simple-pole analysis prove the aggregate count and asymptotic. An independent direct pairwise multiplication census through size five reproduced all centralizers and the totals.

**Originality — PASS relative to checked literature.** Commins (arXiv:2404.00536, §2.1) states the multiplication and studies invariant/representation theory, not centralizers or commuting-pair profiles. Steinberg (arXiv:2404.03475) treats homology and left regular bands of groups, not these counts. Explicit semantic comparisons with the available existing claims gave no same-object/parameter implication. A050352 already contains the identical total-count sequence, its Stirling sum, its generating function and asymptotic; these numerical formulas by themselves are **not** claimed as original. The proposed contribution is the semigroup commutation criterion, complete per-element centralizer profile, extremal block-size classification and the resultant commutativity law. Residual risk: a stronger published theorem for Tits semigroups might imply these facts without using the same vocabulary.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** For the canonical face semigroup of a Coxeter arrangement, the centralizer distribution of every element (and its extremal behavior at fixed number of blocks) is a natural structural invariant not supplied by an enumeration of trees. The result also quantifies strong noncommutativity with a precise asymptotic probability. The existing tabulation alone is not presented as a contribution.

**Limits.** The proof applies to type-A face monoids, not arbitrary hyperplane face semigroups. Finite exhaustive checks cover sizes through five only. Prior-source and historical-access checks do not establish worldwide publication priority. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
