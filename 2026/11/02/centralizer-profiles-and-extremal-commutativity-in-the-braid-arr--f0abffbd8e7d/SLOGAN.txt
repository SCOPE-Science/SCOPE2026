For the braid-arrangement face monoid, every ordered partition with block sizes \(m_1,\ldots,m_k\) has exactly \(2^{k-1}\prod_i F_{m_i}\) commuting partners, with explicit balanced and unbalanced extremizers. This yields a complete centralizer profile and a superfactorially small commutativity probability; its total-count sequence is already listed as OEIS A050352 under a different interpretation.
