For \(A_n=k[x,y]/(x^2,y^n)\), the Jordan rank distribution of square-zero multiplication is determined exactly for every finite field. In characteristic two every reduced rank-bounded locus is linear, whereas in odd characteristic the high-rank rank-bounded loci split into a chain of \(r+\lfloor r/2\rfloor-n+1\) equidimensional linear components. The sharp transition occurs at rank \(\lceil2(n+1)/3\rceil\).
