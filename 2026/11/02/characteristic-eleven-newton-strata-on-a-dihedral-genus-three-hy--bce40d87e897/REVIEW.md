# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** Smoothness holds precisely off \(a^2=4\). The three independent pulled-back differentials prove a geometric isogeny to two explicit elliptic quotient types. Their characteristic-eleven Hasse coefficients are \(a(a^2+1)(a^2-3)\) and \(6a\); hence they give a complete classification of Newton slopes for every smooth geometric parameter. An independently extracted genus-three Cartier matrix checks all \(a\)-numbers and superspeciality at the origin. The exact symbolic certificate reproduces these identities and the nine finite-field coefficient counts. The classification does not extrapolate from the coefficient census.

**Originality — PASS with priority limits.** The precise dihedral normal form and elliptic-quotient splitting are present in Gutiérrez–Sevilla–Shaska (arXiv:1209.2938, Section 3/Table 1), so they are not new. The isolated superspecial case is already covered by the known \(y^2=x^7-x\) family, as restated in Ohashi et al. (2025, Theorem 4.4); an earlier parameterwise study of the same dihedral pencil also establishes that the characteristic-eleven superspecial marked locus is only \(a=0\). Neither result is a novelty claim. Achter–Pries (arXiv:0902.4637) and Pries (arXiv:1311.5846) provide moduli-wide information but do not imply these exact parameter equations. The final assessed contribution is the **complete characteristic-eleven Newton and \(a\)-number stratification of this classical dihedral locus**, including both intermediate rank-two parameter pairs and the exclusion of rank one. published-finding corpus searches for the self-review, parameter factorizations, elliptic quotient aliases and Newton strata returned no dominating result. Historical priority remains provisional.

**Value — PASS.** This is a complete stratification of a canonical one-dimensional automorphism locus within genus-three hyperelliptic moduli. It determines which mixed Newton polygons actually occur and proves that the formally possible rank-one geometry is absent on the entire locus. The two elliptic quotient types explain the restriction structurally, rather than as a sparse arithmetic table. The characteristic is fixed and the claim does not infer any general statement about unrelated families.

**Closest literature and remaining limits.** Gutiérrez–Sevilla–Shaska: precise curve and automorphism locus, not the computed \(11\)-rank/Newton strata. Achter–Pries and Pries: global stratification/generic polygons, not equations for this one-dimensional locus. Ohashi et al. and an earlier same-pencil superspecial parameter study: the special superspecial endpoint and its uniqueness are covered, so neither fact alone is novel. Other unindexed old computations of this explicit family remain possible. Independent verification is not asserted.
