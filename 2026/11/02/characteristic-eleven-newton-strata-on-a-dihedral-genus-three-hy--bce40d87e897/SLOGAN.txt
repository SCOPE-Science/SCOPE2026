The classical genus-three dihedral family \(y^2=x(x^6+a x^3+1)\) in characteristic \(11\) has exactly three geometric Newton strata: a superspecial fiber at \(a=0\), four mixed-slope rank-two fibers at \(a^2=-1\) or \(a^2=3\), and ordinary fibers elsewhere on the smooth locus \(a^2\ne4\). Rank one never occurs, and the \(a\)-numbers are respectively \(3\), \(1\), and \(0\).
