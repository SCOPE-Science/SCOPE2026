Over characteristic \(5\), the symmetric plane sextics \(X^6+Y^6+Z^6+tX^2Y^2Z^2=0\) are smooth exactly for \(t^3\ne3\), with Jacobian \((5\text{-rank},a\text{-number})=(0,10)\) at \(t=0\), \((9,1)\) at \(t^3=1\), and \((10,0)\) otherwise. For every nonzero smooth parameter the elliptic Hesse quotient controls the only possible rank loss, while the nine-dimensional complementary factor is ordinary.
