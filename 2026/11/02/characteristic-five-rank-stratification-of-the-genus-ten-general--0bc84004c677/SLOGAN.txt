For every smooth genus-ten generalized Fermat curve of type \((3,3)\) in characteristic five, the Jacobian has \(5\)-rank \(6\) and \(a\)-number \(4\), except at the three harmonic marked parameters \(\lambda=-1,2,3\), where these change sharply to \((4,6)\). The three exceptional parameters form one branch-permutation orbit, and an exact six-entry Hasse–Witt matrix proves there are no others.
