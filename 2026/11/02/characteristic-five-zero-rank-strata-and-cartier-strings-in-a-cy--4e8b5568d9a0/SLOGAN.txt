For the genus-six tame cyclic septic family \(y^7=x(x-1)(x-\lambda)^6\) in characteristic five, the Jacobian is ordinary except at precisely three parameters, where its \(5\)-rank drops to zero and Cartier has two nilpotent strings of length three. These form two distinct ramification-labeled branch-moduli types, with a squared Hasse determinant at each ordered exceptional parameter.
