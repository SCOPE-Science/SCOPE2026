In characteristic \(p\), the power equation \(a^{p+1}=a\) in any finite incidence algebra splits canonically into an idempotent and a radical element whose \(p\)-th power vanishes, giving an exact low-height census and the first chain correction. Over growing finite fields of fixed characteristic, its leading counting coefficient is the number of antichains whose deletion reduces poset height to at most \(p\), and this coefficient is positive exactly when the original height is at most \(p+1\).
