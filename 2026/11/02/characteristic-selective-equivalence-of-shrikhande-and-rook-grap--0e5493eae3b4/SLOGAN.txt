The sixteen-qubit graph states of the Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs are locally Clifford equivalent, with an explicit four-step local-complementation witness. At every odd prime qudit dimension they are not even locally unitarily equivalent: exactly \(12\) versus \(36\) of their four-site subsystems have entropy \(3\log p\), and all the other four-site subsystems have entropy \(4\log p\).
