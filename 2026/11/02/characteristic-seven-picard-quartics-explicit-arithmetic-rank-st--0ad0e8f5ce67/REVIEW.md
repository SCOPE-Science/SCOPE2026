# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem concerns the genus-three smooth models of depressed monic Picard quartics in characteristic seven with \(\Delta\ne0\). Cartier's coefficient-extraction matrix is reconstructed from the regular differential basis; the identities \(UZ-VW=5a\Delta\) and \(\det H=3a^2\Delta\) are exact. At \(a=0\), the rank-one outer-product block and its Frobenius-twisted contraction prove the geometric \(7\)-rank dichotomy, rather than extrapolating from finite samples. Both standalone programs were run successfully: symbolic determinant/census and independent finite-extension point counts. A finite sample alone would not establish the infinite geometric classification.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated source risk
The Picard normal form and discriminant in Bouw–Koutsianas–Sijsling–Wewers and the existence/dimensions of characteristic-congruent-to-one cyclic-cover rank strata in Özman–Pries–Weir are prior results. The asserted incremental statement is the complete explicit characteristic-seven coefficient-stratum equations, the \(a\)-number restriction, and \(294\)-triple census. The accessible 1991 Estrada-Sarlabous abstract concerns ordinary and supersingular Hasse–Witt conditions; its unavailable full text could contain partially overlapping formulas. The inspected main sources and available related search results provided no theorem that establishes the full claimed package; lack of a search hit does not establish global priority. The particular examples of Kazemifard–Tafazolian are not claimed as new.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The marked Picard parameter family is a natural genus-three cyclic cover family whose \(p\)-rank strata are of established geometric interest. The equations \(a=0\), \(c=0\), and \(b^4=4c^3\) locate the full exceptional strata, rather than just establishing existence or expected dimensions. The rigid \(a\)-number two on all nonordinary members and the exact arithmetic distribution across all smooth coefficients over the first relevant split-prime field provide precise, reusable geometric and arithmetic information. The classification is not an isomorphism census.

## Sources, risks, and audit boundary
Relevant full texts were inspected for arXiv:1902.09624 and arXiv:1907.05684; the second establishes broader existence/dimension conclusions but no comparable explicit marked-parameter stratification. The publisher-hosted Estrada-Sarlabous (1991) article and Kazemifard–Tafazolian (2015) had only abstract/indexed material accessible, so unexamined formulas remain a residual priority risk. No Newton polygons, full Ekedahl–Oort classification, or supersingularity claims are inferred from rank zero.
