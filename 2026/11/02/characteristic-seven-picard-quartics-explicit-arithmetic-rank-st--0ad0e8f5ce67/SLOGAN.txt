For smooth characteristic-seven Picard quartics \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+bx+c\), the marked-coefficient Jacobian \(7\)-rank and \(a\)-number strata have explicit equations: ordinary exactly when \(a\ne0\), and rank zero on the smooth part of \(a=c=0\) or \(a=0,\ b^4=4c^3\). Among \(294\) smooth \(\mathbb F_7\) coefficient triples, the respective \((f_7,a\text{-number})\) counts are \(252\) for \((3,0)\), \(30\) for \((1,2)\), and \(12\) for \((0,2)\).
