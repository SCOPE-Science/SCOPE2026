# Self-review: characteristic-three bad reduction of the \(C_6\) pencil

## Correctness — PASS
The exact Frobenius factorization \(X^{10}+X^7+aX^4+bX=Xh(X)^3\) is valid in every characteristic-three perfect field. The map between the hyperelliptic degree-ten and quartic models preserves every finite-field point count, including roots of \(h\) and the two points at infinity; the quadratic-character formula independently proves this. The discriminant \(b=a^2-a^3\), coordinate boundary \(b=0\), local multiplicities and \(\delta\)-invariants are explicitly resolved for all parameters, including the two intersection points. Rational normalization and Frobenius action at the single two-branched point yield the exact singular zeta functions. The elliptic supersingularity criterion follows from the coefficient-of-\(t^2\) Hasse invariant in characteristic three. Finite-field checks passed for all \(819\) pairs over \(\mathbb F_3,\mathbb F_9,\mathbb F_{27}\), plus \(500\) pairs over \(\mathbb F_{81}\). The general proof is symbolic; enumeration alone would be insufficient.

## Originality — PASS, conditional on unindexed priority
Kudo–Nakagawa–Takagi (arXiv:2210.14822v1), Introduction, Sections 2–3, Conclusion and Appendix A, introduce the family and investigate smooth superspecial genus-four curves and automorphisms; the appendix assumes characteristic at least seven, and their Cartier and cyclic-cover calculations split primes by nonzero residues modulo three. They do not give the characteristic-three reduction, its rational/elliptic normalization strata, the supersingular normalized subfamily, or singular zeta functions. Other results on this same family in characteristic five and seven are not dominating because the characteristic-three Frobenius factorization, changed genera, and singular boundary behavior do not occur in those characteristics. Targeted searches and mathematical-record comparisons found no exact equivalent. Because singular hyperelliptic reductions and elliptic Hasse invariants are classical, an equivalent calculation in older unindexed work remains possible. Search non-detection is not a proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The integral coefficient pencil, known for genus-four curves with a cyclic-order-six symmetry, undergoes a global wild degeneration in characteristic three: even its generic geometric genus drops from four to one. The complete exceptional locus is two explicitly intersecting curves in the coefficient plane, and the zeta functions of all fibers, including the nonintegral fiber, reduce to elliptic or simple rational factors. The geometric genus, point counts, elliptic \(p\)-rank, and exact coefficient frequencies are all natural invariants of this arithmetic degeneration, not arbitrary small-parameter samples.
