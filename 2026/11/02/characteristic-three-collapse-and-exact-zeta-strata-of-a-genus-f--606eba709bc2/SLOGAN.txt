In characteristic three, the genus-four \(C_6\) hyperelliptic pencil \(Y^2=X^{10}+X^7+aX^4+bX\) has precisely the same point counts over every finite extension as the quartic \(V^2=X(X^3+X^2+a^{1/3}X+b^{1/3})\). The normalization is elliptic off two explicit parameter curves, supersingular exactly at \(a=0\); on the boundary, every singularity and every zeta function is determined, including nodal and reducible exceptions.
