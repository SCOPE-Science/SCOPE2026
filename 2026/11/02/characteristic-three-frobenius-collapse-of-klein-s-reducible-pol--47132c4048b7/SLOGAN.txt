In characteristic three, the integral degree-42 polar-conic divisor associated with Klein's quartic becomes the double of the reduced Klein 21-line divisor, and its entire degree-\(21\cdot3^k\) pullback tower becomes that same support with multiplicity \(3^k\). The 21 lines have exact Frobenius-degree distribution \((3,18)\) in degrees \((3,6)\), and their 28 triple and 21 quadruple intersections have an explicit field-of-definition classification.
