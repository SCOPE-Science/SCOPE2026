In characteristic \(3\), the seven-dimensional Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{psl}_3(k)\) has only scalar Hom–Lie structures, while every Hom–Lie structure on \(\mathfrak{sl}_3(k)\) is a scalar map plus a map into its one-dimensional center. The resulting nine-dimensional Hom–Lie space is an associative triangular algebra with square-zero radical of dimension seven.
