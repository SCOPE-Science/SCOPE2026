Every smooth genus-five Humbert–Edge curve in characteristic \(3\) has Jacobian invariants \((f_3,a)\) equal to either \((5,0)\) or \((4,1)\): at most one of its five elliptic branch quotients can be supersingular. The nonordinary locus splits into five pairwise-disjoint rational curves, with exactly \(5(q-3)\) ordered normalized points over \(\mathbf F_q\) for each \(q=3^m\), \(m\ge2\).
