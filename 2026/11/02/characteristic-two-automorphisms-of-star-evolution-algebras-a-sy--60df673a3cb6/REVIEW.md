# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Direct algebra multiplication forces every automorphism to be block diagonal (the rank-one bilinear obstruction excludes off-block entries when the number of leaves is at least two). The characteristic-vector identity forces the central scalar to be a cube root of one, and all resulting maps preserve multiplication. The orthogonal stabilizer decomposes into symplectic cases for odd dimension and a split elementary-abelian-by-symplectic extension for even dimension. The bundled checker exhausts complete low-dimensional matrix spaces over characteristic-two finite fields and agrees with the exact formulas; finite checks are corroboration only.

## Originality
PASS (provisional historical priority). Elduque--Labra (arXiv:1312.4685) studies evolution algebras and graphs but its general finite automorphism theorem concerns the perfect case, whereas these stars have a two-dimensional square. Existing odd-characteristic star results do not cover the characteristic-two characteristic-vector argument and the parity-dependent elementary-abelian extension. Classical symplectic group isomorphisms and orders are credited; the claim is the algebra-specific group identification and parity transition, not the established classical-group facts. A complete priority survey of all less-accessible graph-evolution-algebra treatments remains outstanding.

## Value
PASS. Automorphism groups are natural invariants governing isomorphism and symmetry of graph-derived nonassociative algebras. The result gives all matrices, abstract group structure, and exact finite-field orders for an infinite characteristic-two star family. The unexpected even/odd split between pure symplectic and elementary-abelian-by-symplectic groups is structural, not a routine parameter count.
