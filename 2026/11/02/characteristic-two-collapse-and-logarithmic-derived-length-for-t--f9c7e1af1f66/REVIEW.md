# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The algebra presentation gives a basis of pure branch monomials and forces every derivation to map each generator to its own positive powers plus highest-degree powers on other branches. The proof checks the differentiated relations in both directions, derives all Lie brackets, and shows that characteristic two kills every bracket between even-index branch derivations. In other characteristics the two candidate coefficients for each output index differ by two, so no interior degree is lost modulo an odd prime; this establishes the complete doubling filtration and exact derived length. The packaged standard-library checker independently solves presentation constraints and verifies derived ranks for twelve prime-field cases. These tests are finite corroboration; the proof supplies unrestricted quantifiers.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact derived series refines coarse solvability criteria for a natural class of finite-dimensional commutative monomial local algebras. The characteristic-two collapse is uniform across arbitrary truncation heights, while odd-characteristic derived length grows without bound logarithmically in the longest branch. The derived-series profile is a Lie-algebra invariant of \(HH^1\), giving concrete structural distinctions. For instance, branch lengths \((3,9)\) and \((6,6)\) have equal dimensions both as algebras and as \(HH^1\) Lie algebras, yet different derived lengths in odd characteristic and different abelianization dimensions in characteristic two. The novelty claim does not depend on presenting a general Hochschild solvability theory.
