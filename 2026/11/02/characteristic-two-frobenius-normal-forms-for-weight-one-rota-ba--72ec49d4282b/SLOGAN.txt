For the characteristic-two chain evolution algebra \(e_i^2=e_{i+1}\), every weight-one Rota–Baxter operator has a unique sparse Frobenius normal form determined by its first image. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact count is \((2n-3)q^2-(2n-4)q\), with exactly \(q(q-1)\) operators of each intermediate rank and \((n-1)q(q-1)\) invertible ones.
