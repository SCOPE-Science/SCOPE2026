For Heisenberg Lie algebras of dimension at least five in characteristic two, the reduced variety of Lie homomorphisms to \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) is \(\mathbb A^{2n}\times D_{2,2n}^{\le1}\): its positive-dimensional singular locus and exact finite-field image-rank spectrum exhibit a sharp jump from the three-dimensional source and from odd characteristic. The odd-characteristic commuting-variety description and the existence of a faithful three-dimensional-source representation in characteristic two are acknowledged antecedents.
