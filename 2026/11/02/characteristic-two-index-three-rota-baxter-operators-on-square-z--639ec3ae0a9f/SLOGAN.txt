For the local algebra \(k\oplus V\) with \(V^2=0\), characteristic two admits a dense family of weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators of exact nilpotency index three, whereas every such operator in odd characteristic squares to zero. The reduced moduli dimension jumps by \(\lfloor\dim(V)/2\rfloor\), and an explicit Gaussian-binomial formula determines the joint finite-field rank and nilpotency-index distribution.
