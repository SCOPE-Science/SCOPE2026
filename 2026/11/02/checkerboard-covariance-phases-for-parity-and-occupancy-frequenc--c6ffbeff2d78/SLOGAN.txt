In uniform balls-into-boxes allocations, the number of odd-load boxes has strictly negative covariance with every even-frequency count, strictly positive covariance with singleton boxes, and exactly one negative-to-positive time transition for each higher odd-frequency count. For fixed odd frequency \(r\ge3\), that transition occurs at \(N=\lambda_r B+O_r(1)\), where \(e^{2\lambda_r}+2\lambda_r+1=2r\).
