Under independent binary rounding of a forward-Euler heat stencil, every pair of interior sites is positively correlated in stationarity below the monotone CFL edge \(h=1/2\), while at that edge the complete odd-site and even-site configurations become independent even though within-parity correlations persist. For two interior sites the exact covariance \((1-2h)/(2(4-5h))\) becomes negative beyond that edge, up to the two-cell stability limit \(h=2/3\).
