# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The group law yields an exact alternating commutator on its central quotient. Because this form is perfect over the finite Frobenius chain ring, character orthogonality proves \(|U|\,|U^\perp|=|R|^{2n}\) and \((U^\perp)^\perp=U\) even for nonfree submodules. For arbitrary subgroups, the two loss factors \(|H\cap Z|/|R|\) and \(|\pi(H)|/|R\pi(H)|\) are at most one, and equality forces exactly the claimed full center and stable quotient. All centralizers are inverse images of submodules, and double orthogonality realizes each. No assumption divides by two, so characteristic two is covered. The standalone checker reproduces 67 vs 15, 212 vs 23, direct centralizers of a 64-element group, and selected higher-rank cases. Its finite outputs are not the proof of the general theorem.
