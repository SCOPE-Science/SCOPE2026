For every non-field truncated polynomial ring \(R=\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^\ell)\) and every symplectic rank, the Chermak–Delgado and centralizer lattices of the Heisenberg group \(H_n(R)\) are both exactly the full lattice of \(R\)-submodules of \(R^{2n}\), including nonfree submodules. An exact factorization of every subgroup's Chermak–Delgado measure detects both missing central elements and failure of \(R\)-linearity.
