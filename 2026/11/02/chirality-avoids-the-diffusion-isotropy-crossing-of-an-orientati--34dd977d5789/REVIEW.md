An absolutely integrable velocity covariance gives the stated position-diffusion tensor with the correct factor of two. The traceless complex part reduces to a rational function with imaginary numerator \(-2d\omega\): no real zero is possible at nonzero chirality. An exact first-order two-variable Taylor expansion establishes the universal avoided-crossing coefficient. Symbolic identities, quadrature comparisons and independent finite-state generator approximations corroborate the proof. The mathematical argument, not a finite numerical grid, establishes the all-parameter theorem.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The previously derived achiral longitudinal/transverse diffusivities and their zero crossing are excluded from the asserted novelty. Baouche et al., arXiv:2405.06769v1, study the absolute-heading-reset particle without intrinsic rotation. Olsen and Löwen, arXiv:2405.14326v1, treat chiral *relative* tumbling, which maintains rotational symmetry. Chaki et al., arXiv:2406.17550v1, treat passively diffusing shape anisotropy; Ghosh and Mandal, arXiv:2501.05149v1, treat anisotropic active mobility without intrinsic angular drift. Those results do not establish that a nonzero constant chirality eliminates every finite-rate isotropy point in the absolute-reset model, nor the explicit full-tensor avoided-crossing coefficient. Targeted literature comparisons did not reveal a covering result. Risks: the comparison is not exhaustive and an independent publication-priority determination has not occurred.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact zero set of a natural two-dimensional transport observable changes structurally when intrinsic rotation is switched on. A rate-tunable degeneracy of the diffusion tensor is replaced by a nonzero eigenvalue gap at every finite positive resetting rate; the linear avoided-crossing law predicts the local gap scale and limiting laboratory principal-axis directions. This is stronger than describing single-coordinate diffusivities, since nonzero chirality creates off-diagonal covariance that invalidates a coordinatewise isotropy test.

**Limitations and risks.** Only one laboratory-fixed heading is reset, only at exponential times. The positive-diffusivity assumption is essential to the numerator obstruction. The expansion is local to the former degeneracy, and anisotropy still becomes arbitrarily small at extreme rates. Comparison with all unpublished or inaccessible works is not certified.
