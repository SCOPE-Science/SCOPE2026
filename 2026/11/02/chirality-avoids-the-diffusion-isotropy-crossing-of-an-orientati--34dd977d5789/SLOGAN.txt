A two-dimensional active Brownian swimmer whose heading resets to a fixed laboratory direction has an isotropic long-time diffusion tensor at a positive resetting rate only in the achiral case, at exactly \(r=d/2\). Every nonzero intrinsic rotation removes this degeneracy at all finite positive rates, and the former crossing opens into a universal linear tensor-eigenvalue gap \(16v^2|\omega|/(243d^2)+O(\omega^2)\) when \(r=d/2\).
