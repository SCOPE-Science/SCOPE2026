For ordinal sums of finite antichains with reflexive order modality, generating the entire modal powerset algebra is exactly a proper-colouring problem: colours must separate each level and avoid the unique point of the immediately higher level when it is a singleton. The resulting chromatic polynomial is \(\prod_i(q-\delta_i)_{n_i}\), giving the sharp generator number \(\lceil\log_2\max_i(n_i+\delta_i)\rceil\) and a Poisson-type saturation window for fixed layer width.
