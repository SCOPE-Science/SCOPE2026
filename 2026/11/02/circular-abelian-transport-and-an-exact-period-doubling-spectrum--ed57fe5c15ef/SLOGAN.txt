For the binary morphism \(\mu(0)=01\), \(\mu(1)=00\), the total number of length-tagged circular Parikh classes satisfies \(S(\mu(w))=2S(w)+|w|-|w|_1\) for every nonempty finite binary word, with explicit recurrences for the entire lengthwise spectrum. In particular, the complete period-doubling iterate \(P_k=\mu^k(0)\) has \(S(P_k)=((3k+10)2^k-(-1)^k)/9\) such classes.
