For the commuting graph on nonzero rank-one idempotents in the Boolean relation monoid on \([n]\), the entire clique polynomial and the size distribution of inclusion-maximal cliques have explicit inclusion–exclusion formulas. A commuting family is maximal precisely when its rectangular supports cover \([n]\); maximal cliques occur in every size from \(1\) through \(n\), although the sole size-\(n\) clique is the diagonal family.
