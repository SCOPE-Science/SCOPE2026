Exponential first-passage flooding of \(n\) fixed complete petals sharing a hub has a Gumbel limit centered at \((\log n+\log\log n+2\log(m-1))/m\) for every fixed petal size \(m\ge2\), whereas the star boundary \(m=1\) has no \(\log\log n\) correction. Starting in a petal rather than at the hub adds an independent finite random delay in the limit and exactly \(H_m/m\) to the asymptotic expected flooding time.
