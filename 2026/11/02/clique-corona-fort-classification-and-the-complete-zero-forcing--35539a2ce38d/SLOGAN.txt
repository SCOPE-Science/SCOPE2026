For any finite graph, attaching private complete graphs of orders at least two gives an exhaustive classification of its inclusion-minimal forts and an exact transformation of the entire standard zero-forcing polynomial. For uniform private clique order \(r\), the identities are \(\mathcal M_{G\odot K_r}(z)=|V(G)|\binom r2z^2+\mathcal M_G(rz^2)\) and \(\mathcal Z_{G\odot K_r}(z)=(rz^{r-1})^{|V(G)|}\mathcal Z_G(z(z+r+1)/r)\); mixed clique orders admit an explicit multivariate local-product sum.
