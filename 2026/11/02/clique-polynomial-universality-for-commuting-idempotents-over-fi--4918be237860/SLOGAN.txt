For matrices over every finite commutative local ring, the entire clique polynomial of the idempotent-commutation graph depends only on the ring's order and residue-field order, with an exact coefficient formula for every clique size. All maximal commuting families are Boolean projection sets of size \(2^n\), and their number is \(|\operatorname{GL}_n(R)|/(n!|R^\times|^n)\).
