For free two-step nilpotent Lie algebras on \(n\) generators over \(\mathbb F_q\), a bitwise-AND exterior-square weight formula determines every ordered commuting-retraction tuple count and its joint-rank refinement. Every maximal commuting family contains exactly \(2^n\) retractions, and the number of such families is \(q^{n\binom n2}|\operatorname{GL}_n(q)|/[n!(q-1)^n]\).
