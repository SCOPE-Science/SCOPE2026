A stationary telegraph-noise qubit retains nonzero optimized long-time capacity after one precisely timed rotation, but blind Gaussian clock error of size \(\sigma=a/\nu\) suppresses the strong-coupling capacity by exactly \(e^{-2a^2}\). Observing the actual pulse time changes the universal crossover, and when \(\sigma\to0\) yet \(\nu\sigma\to\infty\), a time-informed sign choice recovers the fraction \(2/\pi\) of the ideal envelope while blind retention vanishes.
