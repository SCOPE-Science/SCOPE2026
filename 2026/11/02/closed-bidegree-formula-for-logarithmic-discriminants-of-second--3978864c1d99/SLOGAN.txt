For the second hypersimplex \(\Delta(2,n)\), the logarithmic discriminant has exact bidegree \(\bigl(n(n-1)(2^{n-2}-n+1),\ n(2^{n-2}-2)\bigr)\) for every \(n\ge4\). Each singular torus-fixed vertex contributes precisely \(2(n-3)\) to the extraneous Hurwitz factor, turning the motivating paper's four numerical examples into a uniform formula.
