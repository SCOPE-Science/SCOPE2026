For every odd prime power \(q\), all nonzero coefficients of \(x^q+a x^2\) over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) have the same value-set size \((5q^2+3)/8\), but their complete fiber histograms split by cubic coefficient class. When \(q\equiv2\pmod3\), the previously unclassified two-thirds of noncubic coefficients yield exactly \((q+1)/2\) triple fibers and \((q^2-4q-5)/24\) quadruple fibers.
