The leading number of distinct positive coefficient-weighted sums of \(r\) base-\(k\) powers below \(N\) is \((\log_k N)^r/\prod_d m_d!\), where \(m_d\) counts coefficients equal after removing maximal powers of \(k\). Coefficient digit patterns otherwise affect only an \(O((\log N)^{r-1})\) remainder, which is sharp already for \(2^a+3\cdot2^b\).
