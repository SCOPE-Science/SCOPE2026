# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The result is proved by an explicit strictly positive first-moment observable \(X\) satisfying \(\mathcal L^\dagger X=-I\) entrywise. Its positivity proves total absorption and justifies the infinite-time trace integral rather than assuming convergence. Compression to the bright state gives the sole input-state invariant; two AM--GM inequalities establish the unique joint minima for positive dark weight. The zero-dephasing boundary is solved on the exact invariant bright/dark blocks and differs discontinuously from the positive-noise limit. The standalone independent full-Liouvillian checker passed \(180\) numerical comparisons with relative error no greater than \(5.330\times10^{-15}\). The proof, not finite testing, carries all quantifiers.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, priority provisional

Rebentrost et al. (arXiv:0807.0929) and Plenio--Huelga (arXiv:0807.4902) concern dephasing-assisted transport, but the accessible full texts do not state this arbitrary-coherent-state uniform-leaf star mean-time identity or its exact two-rate optimum. Kassal--Aspuru-Guzik (arXiv:1201.5202) analyze ordered-network capture efficiency with recombination. Novo et al. (arXiv:1412.7209) construct symmetry reductions for coherent walks, without local Lindblad dephasing. Closest is Pepe et al. (arXiv:2308.15857), whose full paper treats extended-star trapping and dephasing but focuses on energy-defect optimization, symmetric bright initial input, and absorption quantiles, with a high-dephasing classical approximation rather than this exact mean-time formula. Also considered was a source-only dephasing star calculation; distinct bath placement and the arbitrary coherence-dependent law prevent a direct implication. Literature coverage is bounded, and a sufficiently general earlier resolvent identity could subsume the case.

## Value — PASS

Bright and dark state populations are natural physical preparation parameters on a symmetric quantum network. A closed mean-time law valid for every mixed/coherent preparation yields experimentally interpretable trade-offs between dark-state release at weak noise, quantum-Zeno suppression at strong noise, and loss-rate optimization. The exact discontinuity between vanishing and absent dephasing is a nonperturbative order-of-limits effect, not merely a recalculation of a localized input or arbitrary small numerical improvement. Uniform all-leaf baths are physically different from dephasing only a selected launch site.

## Limitations and risk

The result is restricted to a uniform finite star in a single-excitation Lindblad model, positive leaf dephasing, and a hub sink. No arbitrary network, unequal edge, competing loss, or general non-Markovian conclusion is claimed. Originality remains provisional, and there has been no external audit.
