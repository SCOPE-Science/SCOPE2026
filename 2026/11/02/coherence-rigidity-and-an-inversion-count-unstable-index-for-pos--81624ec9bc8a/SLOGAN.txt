For positive-Hermitian gain coupling, every positive fixed point of the matrix logistic map commutes with the gain matrix, despite possible noncommuting trajectories. With distinct squared gains between \(1\) and \(3\), the map has exactly \(2^N\) hyperbolic equilibria whose unstable dimensions are an explicit gain-order inversion count, and the unique hyperbolic sink disappears at the sharp gain threshold \(3\).
