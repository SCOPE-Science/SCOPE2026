At the topological transition of a dissipative two-band quantum walk, a single-site input has infinite mean survival time, but a finite coherent preparation has finite mean time exactly when its staggered amplitude sum vanishes. Among all normalized preparations on \(L\ge2\) consecutive nondecaying sites, a discrete sine-convolution envelope uniquely attains the sharp mean-lifetime minimum \(2/\gamma+(\Delta^2+\gamma^2/4)/(4\gamma h^2\cos^2(\pi/(2L)))\).
