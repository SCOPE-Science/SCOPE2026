For the unit-edge complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), the exact supremal negative-type exponent is \(\log_2(1/\Lambda)\), where \(\Lambda\) is a concrete part-size diagonal Rayleigh quotient on the zero-sum hyperplane. A strict collective obstruction occurs: for \(K_{4,2,2,2}\) at \(p=\log_2(3/2)\), every two-part induced subgraph has strict \(p\)-negative type, but the full graph fails it by an explicit positive quadratic witness.
