# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

The differential of each degree-two component is the explicitly
homogenized quadratic \(W_i\). The unramified condition implies rank at
least two. The exact normal sequence gives globally generated
\(N_f(-4)\) of degree two. Serre duality gives
\(h^0(N_f(-6))=3-\operatorname{rank}M_f\), which separates the **only**
two possible splitting types. In odd characteristic the discriminant
identity \(\operatorname{disc}(W_i)=4\operatorname{Res}(P_i,Q_i)\)
identifies the smooth projective plane of branch divisors; the
ramification-divisor map has local \(\operatorname{PGL}_2\)-fibers.
The rank-two determinant stratum is smooth and irreducible after excluding
the common-root locus, so its smooth irreducible preimage is the claimed
divisor. Exact witness maps show that the odd-characteristic jump and
balanced strata are both nonempty; characteristic two forces the middle
coefficient to vanish. The standalone checker replays symbolic witness
computations. The argument treats every algebraically closed field,
including characteristic two, without extrapolating from finite tests.

## Originality (PASS, with historical risk)

The closest source is Iwasaki, arXiv:2609.39309, Sections 1, 3, 5 and 6.
It gives general relative cohomology methods and expected classes for
toric normal-bundle jumping, but its worked examples concern blowups of
projective spaces. It does not state the exact tridegree-\((2,2,2)\)
classification on \((\mathbb P^1)^3\), the collinear branch-divisor
characterization, the proof that the entire jump divisor is smooth and
irreducible, or the characteristic-two collapse. Prior work on normal
bundles in projective space has different ambient geometry. Exact
statement searches for the three-factor product and ramification
quadratics found no stronger covering theorem. Generic rank-minor
methods could in principle yield this case after nontrivial calculation;
an equivalent earlier calculation under different notation is a remaining
priority risk. Absence from searches is not treated as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The tridegree-\((2,2,2)\) family is a symmetric minimal-degree toric
threefold setting in which each of the three projections can ramify.
Its normal bundles control deformations of parameterized rational curves.
The result gives the **entire** splitting spectrum rather than generic
balancedness alone, a geometrically described first jump divisor with
proved smoothness and irreducibility, its natural coefficient line-bundle
class, and a sharp boundary between odd characteristic and characteristic
two. These are motivated geometric facts for this exact natural family,
not an arbitrary isolated numerical slice.
