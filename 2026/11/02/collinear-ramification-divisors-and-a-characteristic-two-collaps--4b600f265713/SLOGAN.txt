For every unramified tridegree-\((2,2,2)\) map \(\mathbb P^1\to(\mathbb P^1)^3\) over an algebraically closed field of odd characteristic, the normal bundle is \(\mathcal O(5)^2\) except on a nonempty smooth irreducible divisor where it is \(\mathcal O(4)\oplus\mathcal O(6)\); the divisor consists exactly of collinear ramification quadratics and has coefficient multidegree \((2,2,2)\). In characteristic two every such unramified map has the latter unbalanced splitting.
