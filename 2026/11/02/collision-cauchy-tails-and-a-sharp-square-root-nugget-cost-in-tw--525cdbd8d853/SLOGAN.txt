For two i.i.d. uniform nodes, Gaussian-kernel quadrature with arbitrarily small independent Gaussian observation noise has sharp two-sided error tails \(\Pr(\pm E>t)\sim C/t\), even though the noiseless rule is uniformly bounded on every fixed Lipschitz integrand. Adding a nugget \(\eta\) restores finite noise variance but incurs the exact amplification law \(\mathbb E\|w^\eta\|_2^2\sim B/\sqrt\eta\), making the mean-squared error of the noise-matched rule scale linearly rather than quadratically with the observation standard deviation.
