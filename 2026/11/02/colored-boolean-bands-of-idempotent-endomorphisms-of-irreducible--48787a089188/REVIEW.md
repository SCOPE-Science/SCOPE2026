# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The endomorphism classification follows from the characteristic elementary-abelian kernel, irreducibility, and the Frobenius-power description of its invertible intertwiners. The singular composition formula is established directly on generators. Chinese remainder residues yield Boolean idempotents, their commutation criterion leads to a bijection with field colorings, and the partition/falling-factorial census and centralizer sizes follow without reliance on finite experiments. The standalone verifier checks two complete small endomorphism sets and a larger explicit idempotent family.

## Originality: PASS relative to inspected literature

Gould, Grau, and Johnson prove that the idempotents of the endomorphism monoid of a *full transformation semigroup* form a band, but their endomorphisms are not self-homomorphisms of the Frobenius groups here. Wang describes the automorphism groups of elementary-abelian cyclic-complement Frobenius groups and covers the unit component of the present calculation; no novelty is attributed to that automorphism part. Edmunds studies commuting pairs of idempotent homomorphisms in interchange-ring theory and concentrates the classification on abelian groups. Published scalar Frobenius-group character-codegree and invariable-generation results concern distinct invariants. Accessible historical results for generalized dihedral groups cover a one-prime complement rather than the multi-prime family. Precise statements and consequences were compared; absence from these sources is not evidence of publication priority in every literature venue.

## Value: PASS

The principal result classifies an infinite family of nonabelian-group retraction bands, exactly enumerates every maximal compatible system by \(q^r\) and supplies all cardinalities through a partition polynomial. The primes of the complement furnish an intrinsic mathematical motivation for the coloring, while the centralizer spectrum detects support size.

## Limitations and risks

Irreducible scalar complements only. The conclusions do not cover arbitrary Frobenius kernels or arbitrary commuting endomorphisms. Wider retraction-band classification may yield overlapping equivalent results under other terminology. Numerical checks are finite; the universal assertion is deductive.
