# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The image of the kernel V under a group endomorphism is a p-subgroup and stays in the unique Sylow p-subgroup V. Its linear restriction A has an a-invariant kernel, so irreducibility makes it zero or bijective. If bijective, conjugacy of scalar multiplication by a and by a^j forces a^j to be a Frobenius conjugate of a. Composing A with the inverse of that field Frobenius makes it commute with multiplication by a^j and hence with the full field, leaving a nonzero scalar multiplier. The allowed quotient residue p^s is a unit and the endomorphism is an automorphism; only identity is then idempotent. If A=0, t may map to the identity or to v t^j with j nonzero. Each such v is uniquely (1-a^j)c, and the geometric sum for the m-th power is zero, verifying all the relations. Thus composition of the kernel-killing maps retains the left color and multiplies the residues. The Chinese remainder theorem identifies idempotent residues with subsets of the prime-power factors and multiplication with intersection. This verifies the product, count, and left regular band identity, including zero and identity. The commutation criterion makes the support unions for different colors disjoint. Maximality fills in their nonempty subsets and every missing coordinate; conversely, a coloring with all these subsets represented cannot be enlarged. Distinct colorings give distinct singleton idempotents and hence families, giving q^r. The fiber sizes give the maximum (one block), minimum (singletons), and the partition polynomial via the number of unordered blocks of each size times the falling factorial of colors. For centralizers in E, add the two universal elements to all same-color nonempty supports and the nonempty disjoint supports at each other color; this yields the stated strata and multiplicities. These arguments also cover r=1.

The pass is for that combined result, not the automorphism theorem or the generic left regular identity.
