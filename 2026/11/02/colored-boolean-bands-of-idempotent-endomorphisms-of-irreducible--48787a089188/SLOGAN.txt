For irreducible scalar Frobenius groups \((\mathbb F_q,+)\rtimes C_m\), commuting idempotent endomorphisms form Boolean-support bands colored by points of \(\mathbb F_q\). If \(r\) is the number of distinct prime divisors of \(m\), their inclusion-maximal commuting families correspond exactly to the \(q^r\) colorings of those prime-power factors, with an explicit size polynomial and centralizer spectrum.
