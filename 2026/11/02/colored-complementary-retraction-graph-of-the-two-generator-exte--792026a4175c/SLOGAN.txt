Over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the nontrivial idempotent algebra endomorphisms of the four-dimensional exterior algebra have commuting graph \(\frac{q(q+1)}2K_{q,q}\). Exactly \(q^3(q+1)/2\) maximal commuting families exist, all four-element Boolean monoids, and the idempotent commuting probability is asymptotic to \(q^{-2}\).
