Finite Boolean algebras with LFI negation and consistency have an exact independent colored-incidence normal form, yielding \(6^{m2^{m-1}}\) labelled expansions on \(m\) atoms and an explicit Burnside formula for their isomorphism classes. The standard consistency-recovery and classical-negation subfamilies have corresponding bases \(2\) and \(4\), and uniformly sampled expansions in all three families are asymptotically automorphism-rigid.
