# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The natural row/column-sum map is a surjection onto a codimension-one hyperplane with constant fiber size. The four centralizer strata and the rank-two criterion follow from explicit rank-one matrix identities, including characteristic two and one-dimensional edge cases. The resulting histogram partitions all \(q^{2mn}\) ordered pairs, and eight complete independent enumerations agree with the formulas. Rank-two genericity is proved using openness and an explicit witness.

## Originality — PASS with priority risks
The original operator-band commutator theorem addresses products of basis idempotent operators, not arbitrary linear combinations and their coefficient-array rank histogram. The cohomology of band semigroup algebras and commutable-rank matrix-semigroup theorems address different invariants. The commuting-graph work on Rees matrix semigroups concerns semigroup elements, not algebra elements. Searches for a rectangular-band semigroup-algebra commutator histogram did not locate dominating coverage. Nonetheless, an implicit derivation from older semigroup-ring theory or an uncatalogued count is possible. Publication priority is not independently established.

## Value — PASS
Commuting probability, element centralizer profiles and commutator rank are natural Lie-theoretic statistics of a finite associative algebra. This gives a complete nontrivial two-dimensional rank spectrum over the entire unbounded two-parameter family, with a geometric generic-rank threshold, rather than a fixed small enumeration or a renaming of a known result. In contrast to the nowhere-commutative basis band, many distinct linear combinations can commute, and the formula quantifies the phenomenon.

## Closest literature and limitations
Livshits et al. (2001, Section 4) establishes structural statements for basis-band commutators; Choi et al. (2010, Sections 2--3, 7) establishes different homological statements for band semigroup algebras. Livshits et al. (2003) concerns rank commuting, not commutator rank. The 2025 Rees matrix semigroup commuting-graph result concerns a different level of linearization. The full 2003 paper was not accessible; its abstract does not resolve all possible implicit implications. The theorem does not give prescribed-target commutator fiber counts, general-band extensions or classification up to automorphism.
