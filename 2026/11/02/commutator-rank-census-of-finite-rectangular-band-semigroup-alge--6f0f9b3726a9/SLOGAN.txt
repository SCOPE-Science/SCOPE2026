In the finite rectangular-band semigroup algebra \(\mathbb F_q[S_{m,n}]\), every element commutator has coefficient-matrix rank at most two, and the numbers of ordered pairs realizing ranks zero, one, and two have exact formulas for all \(m,n\ge1\) and all finite fields. The same classification gives every element-centralizer size and shows that rank two is generically attained exactly when both band dimensions exceed one.
