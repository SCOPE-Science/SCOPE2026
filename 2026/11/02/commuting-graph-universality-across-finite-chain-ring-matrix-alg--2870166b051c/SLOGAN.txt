For any two finite commutative chain rings, their full \(2\times2\) matrix commuting graphs are isomorphic exactly when their residue fields have the same order and their maximal ideals have the same nilpotency length. The resulting classification identifies the commuting graphs of \(M_2(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)\) and \(M_2(\mathbb F_2[t]/(t^2))\) despite the underlying matrix rings having different additive-group exponents.
