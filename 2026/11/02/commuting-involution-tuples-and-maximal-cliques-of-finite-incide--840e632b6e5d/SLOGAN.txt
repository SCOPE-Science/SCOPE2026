For every finite poset and odd finite field, the entire spectrum of pairwise-commuting square-one element tuples in its incidence algebra equals an explicit Potts/random-cluster evaluation of the comparability graph. Every maximal commuting involution clique has \(2^n\) elements, and exactly \(q^m\) such cliques occur, where \(n\) is the number of poset elements and \(m\) the number of strict comparable pairs.
