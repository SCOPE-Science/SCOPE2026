In the fourth radical truncation of an odd-characteristic Grassmann algebra, the exact ordinary commuting probability is \(q^{-2n}[1+(q^n-1)(q+1)q^{-\binom{n-1}{3}}]\); for every odd finite field, the strict majority of commuting pairs switches from nonzero to zero degree-one support between five and six generators. The untruncated five-generator algebra also has precisely four element-centralizer dimensions, \(24,28,30,32\), with explicit multiplicities from alternating-form ranks.
