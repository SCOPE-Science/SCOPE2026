For the standard four-dimensional ternary Lie algebra over every odd finite field, every nonzero square-zero derivation has rank \(2\); compatible ordered tuples of arbitrary length have an explicit multivariate rank-counting polynomial. The reduced compatible-tuple variety has exactly \(2^m\) irreducible components for \(m\ge2\), including components of dimensions \(m+1\) and \(m+2\).
