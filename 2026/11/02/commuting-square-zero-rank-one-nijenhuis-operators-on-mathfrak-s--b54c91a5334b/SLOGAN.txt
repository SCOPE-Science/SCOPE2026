On \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) in odd characteristic, a rank-one operator \(N=v\otimes\varphi\) is square-zero algebraic Nijenhuis exactly when \(4uw+z^2=0\) and \(\varphi(v)=0\), and any commuting tuple of such operators annihilates pairwise. For every odd prime power \(q\) and positive tuple length \(m\), the exact total is \(1+(q+1)(q^{2m}-1)+\binom{q+1}{2}((2q-1)^m-2q^m+1)\); the reduced compatible-tuple variety has exactly \(2^{m-1}\) geometrically irreducible components for \(m\ge2\), of two different dimensions.
