For the semigroup algebra of every \(a\times b\) rectangular band, a rank-one margin reduction gives exact pairwise-commuting tuple counts for every length and every finite field. The reduced commuting-tuple variety has exactly \(1+\mathbf 1_{a\ge3}+\mathbf 1_{b\ge3}\) irreducible components for tuple length at least two, with a sharp transition from a single determinantal component to additional linear components.
