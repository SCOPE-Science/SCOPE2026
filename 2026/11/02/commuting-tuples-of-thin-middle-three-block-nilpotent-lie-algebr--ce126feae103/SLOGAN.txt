For the three-block nilpotent Lie algebras with middle block of size one, every pairwise-commuting tuple lies in one of two linear coordinate loci or a rank-one determinantal cone, giving exactly \(1+\mathbf1_{a\ge2}+\mathbf1_{c\ge2}\) reduced components for tuple length at least two. The same decomposition yields uniform finite-field formulas for all tuple lengths and a complete census of maximal linear abelian subalgebras.
