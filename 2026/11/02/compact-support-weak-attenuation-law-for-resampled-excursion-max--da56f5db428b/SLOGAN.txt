For spatially attenuated excursion walks with a freshly uniform parameter per excursion, the weak-attenuation limit of rescaled maximum height has a \(3/4\) atom at zero and a positive component supported below a universal finite cutoff \(t_*=1.705428\ldots\). The positive-height tail is \(p_t(1-p_t)\), with a logarithmically thin upper edge of order \((t_*-t)/\log[1/(t_*-t)]\).
