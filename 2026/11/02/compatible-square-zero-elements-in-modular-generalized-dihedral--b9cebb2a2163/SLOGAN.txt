Compatible square-zero elements of \(\mathbb F_q[H\rtimes C_2]\), for \(H\) odd abelian and \(q\) even, mutually annihilate rather than merely commute, and their arbitrary-length ordered tuples have a complete multivariate left-rank polynomial. The same classification gives every maximal compatible subspace and distinguishes \(C_9\) from \(C_3\times C_3\) by \(22{,}720\) versus \(40{,}000\) compatible pairs over \(\mathbb F_2\).
