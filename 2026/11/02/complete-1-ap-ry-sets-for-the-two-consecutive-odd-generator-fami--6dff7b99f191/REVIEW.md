# Scientific review of the complete \(1\)-Apéry formulas

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem counts distinct unordered multiplicity-vector representations. The parity and index-sum identity in RESULT.md restricts all shorter candidate sums, and the explicit distinct factorizations establish at least two representations at every proposed threshold. The remaining smaller representatives are checked case by case for multiplicity at most one, including a possible positive residue representative \(2s-b<b\), which cannot contain a generator. Addition of \(b\) preserves two distinct factorizations and proves exhaustive upward closure. The genus and singleton formulas then follow exactly. Exact independent numerical checks were reproduced from `verify.py` (299 parameter/family cases, 45,597 residue classes, 251,679 integers, 19 independent recursive cases). The unrestricted statement is proved algebraically, not inferred from this census.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk
The closest primary work is Komatsu–Hyun–Song, arXiv:2608.14053, Sections 1–4. Its stated theorems give \(p\)-Frobenius numbers for these very families. Section 4.1 constructs upper-bound witnesses in every residue and verifies one extremal \(1\)-Apéry member; it does not establish that every witness is minimal, or list the whole singleton locus or the \(1\)-genus. The source's Section 3 similarly derives upper bounds and extremal values. The general \(p\)-Apéry identities of Komatsu, arXiv:2111.11021, give the counting method but not the evaluated family-specific minima. Related arXiv:2304.00443 concerns Fibonacci triple generators instead. Targeted searches of phrases and exact formulas found no covering result. Strong general reductions and the original upper-bound witnesses are explicitly credited rather than claimed. Remaining risk: independently unknown equivalent earlier evaluations and partial rendering of mathematical displays in the accessible full-text presentation of the closest preprint.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Unlike an isolated \(p\)-Frobenius maximum, the result determines every first two-representation threshold for two natural variable-embedding-dimension families from a current study. The evaluated \(1\)-Apéry sets give all singleton-representation integers, the complete rational generating functions of integers with at least two representations, and exact \(1\)-genera. These are natural full distribution data not determined by the published maximal element, and the all-residue classification is an independently motivated object. The theorem does not extend to \(p\ge2\), and no general \(p\)-Apéry formula is claimed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
