For the two families of consecutive odd generators \(\{2a+1,2a+3,\ldots,4a+1\}\) and \(\{2a+1,2a+3,\ldots,4a+3\}\), the *entire* first-double-representation Apéry sets have explicit three- and two-piece formulas. These determine the complete singleton-representation loci, rational generating functions for numbers with at least two representations, and exact \(1\)-genera \(5a+10\) and \(5a+6\), respectively, for every \(a\ge2\).
