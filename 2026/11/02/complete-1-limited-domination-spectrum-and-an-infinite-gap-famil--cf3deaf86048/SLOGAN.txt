The complete size enumerator of \(1\)-limited dominating sets on every circular prism \(C_n\square K_2\) is a logarithmic derivative of an explicit factored transfer determinant. For every even \(n\ge4\), the coefficients at sizes \(n\) and \(n+2\) are positive but the coefficient at size \(n+1\) vanishes, giving an infinite family of nonunimodal domination spectra.
