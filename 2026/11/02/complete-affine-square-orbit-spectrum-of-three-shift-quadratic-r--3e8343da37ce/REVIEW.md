# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The universal exclusion follows from an explicit character-indicator identity, the standard quadratic pair-sum identity, and Hasse's bound for a squarefree cubic; it reduces the question to eight finite fields. Their complete orbit spectra follow from exhaustive exact enumeration of every three-element subset using two independently implemented covering tests, followed by exact group-action orbits. The standalone program and its arithmetic are part of the same package, not external logs. This is not an extrapolation from finite samples.

## Originality — PASS, subject to priority risk
The known Paley domination tables already give the presence or absence of size-three coverings, and an earlier exact finite-field result gives the one-orbit classification and 100 covers for the field of order 25. Those components are specifically **not** claimed as new. The new object is the complete affine-square orbit spectrum across all orders, including the two size-six orbits in order nine, and the unequal orbit decompositions at orders seven and thirteen. Searches for minimal dominating triples, affine-square orbit counts, and covering-shift-set classifications found no stronger published orbit classification. The 2026 general covering theorem determines bounds and subfield conditions, not this orbit census. An earlier unindexed classification or orbit computation remains possible; self-review is not independent priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Classification of all extremal small covering configurations up to the natural symmetry is a canonical structural refinement of a sharp covering-number threshold, not an arbitrary truncation. The sharp Hasse cutoff makes the eight-field certificate exhaustive, and the existence of multiple inequivalent minimal configurations in orders seven, nine and thirteen carries information not present in domination-number tables. The fixed invariant of interest is the orbit partition, not only a recomputed list of domination numbers.

## Limits and closest work
Zhou–Wu (2026) provides general finite-field additive coverings; Brouwer's Paley tables give the domination numbers and cover existence; the known \(\mathbb F_{25}\) classification subsumes the order-25 row, including its orbit. No assertion is made about all shifts beyond size three, characteristic two, or full semilinear orbit classes.
