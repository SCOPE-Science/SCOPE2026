Three translated sets of squares including zero cover an odd finite field only at eight field sizes, all at most \(25\). Beyond the previously known covering-number and \(\mathbb F_{25}\) data, the exact affine-square orbit spectrum of every three-center covering is \((1),(5,5),(7,21),(6,6,36),(55),(26,39),(68),(100)\), revealing two distinct small orbits over \(\mathbb F_9\).
