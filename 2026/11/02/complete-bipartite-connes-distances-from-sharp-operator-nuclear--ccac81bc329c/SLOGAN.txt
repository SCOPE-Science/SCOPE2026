For complete bipartite graphs with the unweighted adjacency Dirac operator, the Connes distance has sharp same-part values \(\sqrt{2/q}\), \(\sqrt{2/p}\) and cross-part value \((\sqrt{p-1}+\sqrt{q-1})/\sqrt{pq}\). Explicit rank-one same-part witnesses, a rank-two cross-part witness and nuclear-norm dual certificates prove all cases for integers \(p,q\ge2\).
