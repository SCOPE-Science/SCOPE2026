Among all \(2048\) binary de Bruijn necklaces of order five, the sentinel-free circular Burrows–Wheeler transforms have exactly six possible ordinary linear run counts: \(20,22,24,26,28,30\), occurring respectively \(38,426,840,608,130,6\) times. A complete \(2^{16}\)-orientation enumeration and an independent Euler-tour enumeration certify the distribution.
