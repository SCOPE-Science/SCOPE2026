# Review: Cartier strata for a genus-four cyclic trigonal family

## Correctness — PASS

The discriminant \(\Delta=b^4+3a^5\) describes the smooth locus, and Riemann–Hurwitz gives genus four. A complete basis of regular differentials yields an explicit Cartier matrix with only three potentially nonzero entries: \(P=b(9a^5-b^4)\), \(R=6ab^2\), and \(3b\), with \(11\)-th roots taken for the semilinear action. Cartier has rank zero at \(b=0\) and rank two elsewhere. Its stable rank is zero exactly where \(b=0\) or \(b^4=9a^5\), and two otherwise. The exceptional smoothness condition holds algebraically. Two different exact-arithmetic verifiers successfully reproduced all \(110\) prime-field smooth pairs; their finite checks support, but are not substituted for, the general-field proof.

## Originality — PASS with explicit limitations

The superspecial genus-four enumeration of Kudo and Harashita (2017) and the existence constructions of Kudo (2018) and Kudo–Harashita–Senda (2019) were checked for statement-level implication. Their established superspecial examples and general methods are not claimed as novel here. Six targeted semantic record queries for cyclic trigonal, (3,5), Hasse–Witt, Cartier–Manin, characteristic eleven, and the exceptional parameter equality found no direct equivalent. The new assertion is the full three-way rank and \(a\)-number coefficient stratification, especially its nilpotent nonzero Cartier stratum. Possible unindexed earlier tables and a covering general superelliptic coefficient formula remain originality risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is an entire natural genus-four Hurwitz subfamily, not a single ad hoc example. It distinguishes two intrinsically different rank-zero degenerations and identifies their exact coefficient loci; it also rules out other rank values. The exact finite-field census makes the arithmetic boundary reproducible without asserting a general moduli-class census.
