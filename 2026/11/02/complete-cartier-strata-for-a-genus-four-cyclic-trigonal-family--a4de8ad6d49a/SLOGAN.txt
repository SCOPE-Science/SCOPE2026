For the smooth genus-four cyclic trigonal family \(y^3=x^5+ax+b\) in characteristic \(11\), exactly three \(11\)-rank/\(a\)-number strata occur: \((0,4)\), \((0,2)\), and \((2,2)\), with explicit coefficient equations. In particular a smooth rank-zero stratum with \(a\)-number two lies beside the Cartier-vanishing superspecial stratum.
