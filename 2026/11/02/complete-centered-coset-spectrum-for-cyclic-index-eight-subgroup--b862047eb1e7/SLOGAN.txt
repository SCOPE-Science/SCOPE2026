For cyclic mixed index-eight subgroups modulo products of two distinct odd primes, the possible numbers of centered multiplicative cosets are exactly \(0,2,4,6,8\), and all five occur infinitely often. A fixed conductor \(41\) gives the explicit formula \(N(H_q)=8-2\min(h,8-h)\) and positive, exactly determined prime densities for every value.
