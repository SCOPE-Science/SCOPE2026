For the smooth characteristic-13 Vermeulen quartic pencil \(uX^4+Y^4-Z^4-2X^2Y^2-4XYZ^2=0\), the Hasse–Witt matrix is \(\operatorname{diag}(u^3B,B,T)\) with explicit coprime cubic factors \(B,T\), giving exactly three parameters of Jacobian \(13\)-rank \(1\), three of rank \(2\), and an ordinary complement. The exceptional inflection quartic \(V_{-1}\) has \(13\)-rank \(1\), whereas the other exceptional quartic \(K\) is ordinary.
