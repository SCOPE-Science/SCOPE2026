# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each factor a unique highest letter a_r identifies its i,j extensions in the ruler word. When both extensions lie in one 2-adic shell, translating the candidate border by 2^(t+1) yields two—and only two—occurrences using the local a_(t+1) marker. Unequal shells create another translated occurrence, preventing closedness; zero-side exceptions are covered. Squaring shell widths gives H_r=(4^(r-1)+2)/3 and C_n=(4^n+6n-1)/9, with triangular lengths. An independent direct factor/border brute force for ranks 1..7 returned 1,3,9,31,117,459,1825 and matched the exact classification; this finite corroboration is not proof of all ranks.
