For cyclic Rule 30, two distinct inputs can have the same one-step output of Hamming weight \(k\) exactly when \(k=0\) or \(k\ge3\) and \(k\ne5\); the smallest ring length is then \(1\) for weight zero and \(k+2(k\bmod3)\) otherwise. A factored bivariate rational generating function counts all ordered collisions simultaneously by ring length and output weight.
