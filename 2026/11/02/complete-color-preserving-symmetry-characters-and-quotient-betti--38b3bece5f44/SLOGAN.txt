For all complete multipartite graphs with at least three color classes of size at least three, the full color-preserving rational homology characters of unordered two-point configurations admit explicit fixed-point and two-cycle formulas. After quotienting by every within-color permutation, the rational Betti numbers depend only on the number \(k\) of colors: \(\left(1,\binom{k-1}{2},3\binom k4\right)\).
