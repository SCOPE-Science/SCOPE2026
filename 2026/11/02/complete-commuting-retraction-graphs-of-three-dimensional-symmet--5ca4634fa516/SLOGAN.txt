For odd \(q\), split symmetric three-dimensional nilpotent Leibniz algebras have an idempotent-retraction commuting graph \(K_{q,q}\sqcup2q(q-1)K_1\) away from zero and identity, while nonsplit forms have no nontrivial idempotent retractions. Consequently all maximal commuting retraction families and the complete clique polynomial are determined exactly.
