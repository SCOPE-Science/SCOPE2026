Every ordinary \(P_3\)-convex subset of a sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\) admits a cyclic-run classification: a nonconstant core choice with \(m\) selected runs leaves exactly \(n-2m\) pendant decisions free, yielding an explicit complete bivariate core–leaf enumerator. Its scalar count agrees with the previously known sequence OEIS A073313; the contribution is the joint polynomial and structural classification, not a new scalar recurrence.
