At the already known pebbling threshold \(4m\) for a friendship graph with \(m\ge2\) triangles, **every** critical nonstackable and nonclearable configuration of size \(4m-1\) belongs to a single orbit of size \(2m\). Its representatives put \(2m+1\) pebbles on one peripheral vertex, zero on its mate and the hub, and one pebble on every other peripheral vertex.
