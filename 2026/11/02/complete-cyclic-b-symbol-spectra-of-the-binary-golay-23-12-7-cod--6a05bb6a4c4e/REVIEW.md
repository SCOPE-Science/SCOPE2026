# Scientific review of complete cyclic Golay read-weight distributions

## Correctness — PASS

The claim concerns the finite binary cyclic Golay code generated by the standard polynomial of degree 11; no generalization to other lengths is inferred. The generator divides the length-23 modulus, and the message-polynomial representation traverses exactly 4096 distinct codewords. A proved zero-run identity counts every zero cyclic window, and the complete integer run-partition census yields every spectrum coefficient. A separate standard-library verifier generates the same code by systematic polynomial division, reads the cyclic windows directly, and checks every spectrum coefficient, all partition counts, the ordinary Hamming distribution, 178 nonconstant rotation orbits, and the exact minimizing orbit for every width 3 through 11. The claim that all nonzero words have maximum read weight for widths at least 12 also follows by a triangular divisibility argument. Residual technical risk is ordinary software or transcription error, not reliance on sampled inputs.

## Originality — PASS, with historical priority risk

The original binary Golay code and its Hamming enumerator are classical and not claimed as novel. Chee et al.'s symbol-pair MDS constructions do not give the Golay census. Yaakobi–Bruck–Siegel's general pair bound already gives the lower bound 11. More importantly, Guan et al. (arXiv:2511.15207, Example 4) explicitly report *all eleven Golay minimum read distances* at widths 1 through 11; our generator is the reciprocal of theirs, so those distance numbers are definitely covered and are **not** claimed novel. Shi–Özbudak–Solé (DOI:10.1109/TIT.2021.3069772) also establish the general high-width enumerator stabilization; Panraksa's 2026 ternary Golay paper uses the already-standard zero-run identity and gives a different code's minimizer orbit. None of these supplies the binary length-23 full weight multiplicities at widths 2 through 11 or the exact same cyclic orbit of 23 minimizers across widths 3 through 11. Vega's irreducible one/two-weight enumerator theory has hypotheses not satisfied by the reducible-check, many-weight Golay code. A related historical result on optimizing the length-24 extended Golay pair distance across coordinate permutations concerns different code and quantifiers. Targeted full-source claim comparisons and coefficient-table searches did not identify a prior complete table of the nontrivial width-2-to-11 multiplicities or the binary common minimizer orbit; the known distance profile and stabilization are correctly excluded from novelty. An unindexed or older table could affect priority; search non-coverage is not a proof of historical novelty.

## Value — PASS

The cyclic Golay code is a foundational perfect code, and cyclic contiguous-window read metrics have a direct information-theoretic meaning in symbol-pair and multi-symbol read channels. The exact 23-by-24 distribution coefficient table, 81 zero-run classes, all exact minimum multiplicities, and a shared minimizing orbit across nine nontrivial read widths give complete structural information unavailable from the standard Hamming parameters or a minimum-distance bound alone. The finite domain is the full natural set of widths for this code, not an arbitrary parameter slice; these data are reproducible benchmarks for read-channel decoding and bound comparisons.
