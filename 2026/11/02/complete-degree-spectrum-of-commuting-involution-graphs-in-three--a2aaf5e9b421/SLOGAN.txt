For three-block unipotent groups of block sizes \((s,t,1)\) over every finite field of characteristic two, the entire commuting-involution graph degree multiset and edge count have closed formulas: a rank-stratified zero-terminal-vector family and one uniform nonzero-terminal-vector family. Already for block sizes \((2,2,1)\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), the graph has 111 vertices, 3225 edges, and four distinct degrees.
