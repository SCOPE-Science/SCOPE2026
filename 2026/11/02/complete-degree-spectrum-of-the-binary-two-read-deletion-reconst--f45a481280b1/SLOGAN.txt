The binary two-read single-deletion reconstruction graph has an exact all-length degree spectrum: if the binary difference word has one-run lengths \(h_i\), then its degree is \(\sum_i\binom{h_i+1}{2}\), and the complete distribution has generating function \(2(1+R)/(1-z-zR)\) with \(R=\sum_{h\ge1}z^h u^{\binom{h+1}{2}}\). Its uniform-vertex degree has exact variance \(6n-30+(2n^2+6n+17)/2^{n-1}-4^{1-n}\), hence variance asymptotic to \(6n\).
