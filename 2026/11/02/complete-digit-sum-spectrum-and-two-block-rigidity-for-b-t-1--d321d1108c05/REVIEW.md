# Review

## Correctness — PASS

Digit-sum subadditivity makes the strictly decreasing modular folding operation preserve a positive multiple of \(b^t-1\) while never increasing digit sum. The terminal number is necessarily \(b^t-1\), proving the lower bound. A checked symbolic expansion yields the explicit residue-class witnesses \(W_r\), and separated digit blocks realize every larger admissible digit sum. For numbers below \(b^{2t}\), a complete two-block congruence partition leaves exactly two digit sums. The exhibited intermediate witnesses attain the exact length threshold \(2t+1\). These arguments cover all integer bases and all positive exponents, including boundary cases.

## Originality — PASS, subject to independent priority review

The strongest close prior work is Phunphayap–Noppakaew–Pongsriiam (arXiv:2303.13017, first public in 2023), which proves an arithmetic congruence restriction and an inclusion of multiples of \(s_b(n)\), not the complete digit-sum spectrum or the two-block sharp cutoff. Elsholtz (2016) and the OEIS A000120 note give the binary minimal-weight bound; Dartyge–Luca–Stănică (2009) treats repeated weights in nontrivial multiples. Toumi (arXiv:2504.02784, 2025) studies digit-sum distributions in arithmetic progressions and highlights first-hitting thresholds in Remark 1.1, but gives no exact first feasible length for intermediate integer-valued digit sums in the modulus family here. Explicit searches of published research records and accessible previous results disclosed no stronger covering statement. A differently named earlier formulation is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is the complete digital-weight support of a natural infinite family of progression moduli, not merely a modular congruence or unproved numeric pattern. The new short-length rigidity pinpoints the first length at which each intermediate missing weight becomes available and is attained by an explicit witness. For binary Mersenne moduli it supplies an exact Frobenius cutoff. The result does not handle arbitrary moduli or all higher length minima.
