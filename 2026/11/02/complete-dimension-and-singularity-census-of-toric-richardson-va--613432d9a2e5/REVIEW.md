## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The small-rank boundary \(S_7\) is directly motivated by the already-published complete *Boolean* table, while the new geometric toricity criterion creates the natural finer question: how many of the additional lattice intervals represent **singular** toric Richardson varieties? The full ten-dimensional profile is an exact census of natural finite flag-geometry invariants, not an arbitrary slice. It exposes a sharp nontrivial boundary: \(11{,}080\) singular unexpected toric Richardson varieties in dimensions above the standard torus rank, and none at the maximal dimension \(9\), although \(712\) occur in dimension \(8\). This comparison distinguishes smoothness from toricity quantitatively in the first computationally substantial seven-letter case.
