In \(\mathrm{Fl}(7)\), exactly \(1{,}157{,}440\) embedded Richardson varieties are toric; exactly \(123{,}862\) of them are singular, with a sharp singular-dimensional cutoff of \(8\). The \(64\) top-dimensional toric Richardson varieties (dimension \(9\)) are all smooth; the previously published Boolean-interval counts are explicitly distinguished from the new full toric/singular census.
