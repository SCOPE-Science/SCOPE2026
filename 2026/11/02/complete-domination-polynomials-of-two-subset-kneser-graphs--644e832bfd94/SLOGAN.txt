The domination polynomial of every two-subset Kneser graph is given by a closed coefficient formula: non-dominating sets form the union of intersecting-pair witness families, with a special four-edge correction from disjoint witnesses. For \(n\ge5\), every set of at least \(2n-3\) vertices dominates, and precisely \(\binom n2\) sets of size \(2n-4\) do not.
