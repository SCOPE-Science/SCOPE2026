For \(x\mapsto x+x^3\) modulo every \(2^k\) with \(k\ge5\), the complete functional graph has only periodic even vertices and depth-one odd leaves: valuation \(r\) gives exact cycle length \(2^{\max(k-3r,0)}\). Precisely the even residues \(\pm2\pmod{32}\) receive eight extra odd predecessors, producing a sharp three-level indegree spectrum.
