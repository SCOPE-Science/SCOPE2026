For every odd integer \(c\), the two-adic closure of quotients of values of \(X^4+cX^2\) is exactly \(\mathbb Q_2\) when \(c\equiv7\pmod8\), and otherwise zero together with one of three distinct index-two open subgroups of \(\mathbb Q_2^\times\), determined by \(c\pmod8\). The three non-dense phases impose, respectively, a unit-modulo-four restriction, even valuation, and a parity-twisted unit restriction.
