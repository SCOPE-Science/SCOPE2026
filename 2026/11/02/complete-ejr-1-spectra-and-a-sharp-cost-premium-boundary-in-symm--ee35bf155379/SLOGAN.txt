In a symmetric approval budget with \(k\) voters, \(k\) shared projects costing \(h\) each, and \(h\) private unit-cost projects per voter, cost-based and cardinality-based EJR-1 are simultaneously attainable exactly when \(h\le k\). Complete criteria characterize all fair outcomes under each measure, and the unavoidable private-representation deficit when \(h>k\) is exactly \(h-k+1-\lfloor h/k\rfloor\).
