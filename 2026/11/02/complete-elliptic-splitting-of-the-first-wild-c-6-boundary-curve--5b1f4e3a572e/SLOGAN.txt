The smallest characteristic-two wild \(C_6\) boundary curve, \(y^2+y=x^3+x^{-3}\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), has a completely elliptic-split Jacobian with Frobenius traces \(-2,2,-1\). Its zeta numerator is \((1+4T^4)(1+T+2T^2)\), its Jacobian has \(20\) rational points, and its geometric \(2\)-rank equals \(1\).
