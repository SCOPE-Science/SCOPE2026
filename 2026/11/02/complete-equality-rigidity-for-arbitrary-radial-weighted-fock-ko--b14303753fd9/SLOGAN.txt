At the sharp exterior-domination radius \(R=\sqrt{m_1/m_0}\), every radial weighted entire-function Hilbert space has exactly the same norm-equality pairs: unimodular multiples and affine Möbius pairs, with no assumption that the weight is decreasing. Strict norm attainment for a moment-weighted backward shift and a Schur transform excludes all nonlinear extremizers, even for peaked or annular weights.
