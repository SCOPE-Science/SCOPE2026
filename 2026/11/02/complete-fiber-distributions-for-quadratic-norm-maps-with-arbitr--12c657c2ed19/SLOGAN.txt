For every prime power \(q\), every map \(x\mapsto x^{q+1}+a x^q+b x\) on \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) has one of exactly two complete fiber profiles. It takes \(q\) values precisely when \(b=a^q\), and otherwise has \(q(q+1)/2\) values with exactly \(q\) singleton fibers and \(q(q-1)/2\) double fibers, including in characteristic two.
