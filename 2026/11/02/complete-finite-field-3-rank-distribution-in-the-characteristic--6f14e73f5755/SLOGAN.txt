For smooth characteristic-three normalized Ciani quartics, the three elliptic-quotient supersingularity divisors are individually \(ab+c=0\), \(ac+b=0\), and \(bc+a=0\), meeting pairwise on coordinate axes and all three only at the Fermat quartic. For every \(q=3^m\), the exact numbers of smooth \(\mathbb F_q\)-coefficient triples of geometric \(3\)-ranks \(0,1,2,3\) are respectively \(1\), \(3(q-3)\), \(3(q-3)(q-5)\), and \((q-3)(q^2-7q+18)\).
