For a uniformly random integer partition, the presence of two distinct prescribed part sizes is negatively correlated except for five exact ties, eleven finite exceptional positive pairs, and the persistent positive pair of sizes \(1\) and \(n-1\). From \(n\ge17\) onward, that complementary pair is the **only** positively correlated one; a finite exact defect certificate and partition-number log-concavity prove the full classification.
