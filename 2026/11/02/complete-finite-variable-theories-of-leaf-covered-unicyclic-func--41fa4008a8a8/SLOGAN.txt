For finite connected functional digraphs consisting of one directed cycle with at least one pendant in-leaf at every cycle vertex, unrestricted counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{2}\) sees only five coarse phases, while every \(\mathrm{FO}^{k}\) with \(k\ge3\) recovers the entire cyclic sequence of leaf counts truncated at \(k\). The resulting exact theory counts are the \(k\)-ary necklace numbers, with unique smallest representatives obtained by clipping each leaf fiber.
