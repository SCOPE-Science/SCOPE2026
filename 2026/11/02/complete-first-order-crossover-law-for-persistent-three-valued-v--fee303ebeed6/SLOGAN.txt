On complete two-level frames, uniformly sampled persistent, glut-free three-valued valuations have exactly three asymptotic first-order phases, with mixture weight \(2(2^a-1)/(3^b+2(2^a-1))\). The full limiting probability spectrum is explicit, and the critical scaling \(a=\lfloor b\log_2 3\rfloor\) produces a whole interval of subsequential probabilities rather than a first-order convergence law.
