For Aitken \(\Delta^2\) extrapolation of three jointly Gaussian, equal-variance noisy samples of a geometric contraction, finite mean absolute error is possible only with exactly identical noise in all three samples; every other covariance produces explicit symmetric \(t^{-1}\) tails and infinite mean error. Under equicorrelation the heavy-tail coefficient vanishes essentially exponentially as correlation approaches one, but moments recover only at the perfectly common-noise endpoint.
