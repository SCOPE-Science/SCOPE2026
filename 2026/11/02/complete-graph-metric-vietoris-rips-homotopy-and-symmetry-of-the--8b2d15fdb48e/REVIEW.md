# Scientific review: Desargues graph Rips filtration

## Correctness — PASS
The defining graph is rebuilt from the 2-/3-subset incidence rule, and the entire finite face poset is reconstructed at the only nontrivial Morse stages. Exact matching data cover all faces: 120 pairs plus one critical vertex and nineteen critical triangles at scale two; 1,599 pairs plus one critical vertex and one critical tetrahedron at scale three. The checker verifies all codimension-one incidences, disjointness and directed acyclicity, proving the sphere-wedge equivalences without reliance on sampled experiments. The graph-cycle, cross-polytope and full-simplex stages are elementary. Signed simplicial Lefschetz traces, including all 240 group elements, agree with the rational character decomposition; the scale-four orientation is determined explicitly.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest full primary paper is Saleh–Titz Mite–Witzel, *Vietoris–Rips complexes of Platonic solids*, arXiv:2302.14388v1, whose dodecahedral calculation does not cover the Desargues graph. Adamaszek et al.'s metric-gluing paper (arXiv:1712.06224) has restricted constructions, not this complete graph. Indexed results for the Desargues Čech nerve, independence complex, and matching complex have different defining functors. Relevant generalized Petersen and other cubic-graph Rips records concern nonisomorphic graphs. An indexed same-graph Čech result was available only in summarized form; possible undisclosed statements and unindexed duplicate work remain bibliographic risks.

## Value — PASS
This is a complete natural all-scale vertex-metric homotopy and persistence classification of a canonical 20-vertex distance-transitive graph. The comparison with the 20-vertex dodecahedron reveals significantly different middle-dimensional homology despite equal elementary graph parameters. The full natural group characters also distinguish rational homology of quotients invisible to ordinary Betti numbers.

## Boundaries
The claim is only for the Desargues vertex shortest-path metric and the stated homology group representations. It does not assert equivariant homotopy wedge models or a general theorem for other bipartite Kneser graphs. No independent audit has been performed.
