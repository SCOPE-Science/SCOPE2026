All algebra homomorphisms between finite-field evolution algebras whose natural-basis squares are permutation cycles are classified by divisibility of cycle lengths, shifts, and roots of unity. Their number is \(\prod_j(1+\gcd(q-1,2^{n_j}-1)\sum_{i:m_i\mid n_j}m_i)\), and a squarefree-source-variable product gives the exact rank distribution, including nonmonomial embeddings.
