# Scientific review: complete bipartite graphical Lie-algebra ideals

## Correctness — PASS
For all fields and all positive vertex-part sizes, the stated Lie bracket is alternating and two-step nilpotent. The ideal condition is exactly \([U,A\oplus B]\subseteq I\cap Z\); bracketing with the two pure vertex parts proves that the bracket image is \(P\otimes B+A\otimes Q\). The tensor quotient, unique central subspace and unique lift map account for every ideal without overcounting. The horizontal Goursat data give the Gaussian coefficients and \(\mathrm{GL}_c\) factor. The stratum geometry follows from smooth, geometrically irreducible Grassmann, isomorphism and affine bundles. The proof remains valid in characteristic two.

An independent exhaustive subspace-closure checker agrees with all dimension counts in seven finite-field configurations. The largest check tests all \(417199\) binary subspaces of the eight-dimensional algebra for \(K_{2,2}\). Computations support the algebraic argument but do not prove its arbitrary-parameter statements.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
Mainkar's 2013 full text proves a graph-isomorphism criterion and recalls the original graphical Lie construction, not a full ideal lattice. Godoy Molina and Lagos's 2023 full text gives a criterion for special induced-subgraph graph-ideals (Section 3) and graded derivations of complete-bipartite algebras (Section 5); it neither parametrizes mixed subspaces nor counts all ideals. Rossmann's 2022 work on graphical groups calculates conjugacy-class distributions, a distinct invariant. Related classifications of maximal abelian ideals or subalgebras of graphical Lie algebras do not imply a census of all ideals. Similar tensor-lift descriptions for associative cube-zero algebras are not implications of the present Lie bracket problem: the horizontal spaces and induced bracket ranks differ.

Searches for synonyms, Goursat parameterizations, ideal Grassmannians, exact small counts, and broader nilpotent-Lie-algebra ideal formulas identified no covering theorem. This is a best-knowledge mathematical comparison; a historical ideal-zeta formula or an unindexed general theorem could still imply the result.

## Value — PASS
A complete description of the ideal lattice is a natural structural invariant of the graphical Lie algebra, and the complete-bipartite family is canonical in the Dani–Mainkar construction. The non-coordinate mixed subspaces, encoded by an isomorphism of quotient spaces, are essential and are absent from induced-subgraph graph-ideal lists. The formula supplies exact counts in **every dimension** and for **every finite field** over an infinite family, and already its degree and one-dimensional-ideal coefficient recover the graph part sizes up to interchange; the smooth, geometrically irreducible strata give geometric information that a total ideal count omits. The eight-dimensional \(K_{2,2}\) algebra already has \(247\) distinct ideals over \(\mathbb F_2\), showing that restricting to graph-supported ideals misses a large natural space of possibilities.

## Limitations
Only the complete-bipartite graphical Lie algebra with distinct edge labels is treated. The proof does not determine irreducible components or nilpotent structure of the full fixed-dimensional ideal scheme, automorphism orbits, or the ideal census of arbitrary graph algebras. The general proof is symbolic; only seven small configurations were exhaustively checked. Publication priority and independent validation remain open.
