Every linear Lie ideal of the two-step algebra attached to a complete bipartite graph admits a unique Goursat–tensor–lift parametrization. For every finite field, this yields a closed Gaussian-binomial formula counting ideals in each dimension, and every fixed-parameter reduced ideal stratum is smooth and geometrically irreducible; for example, \(K_{2,2}\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) has exactly \(247\) ideals.
