All square-zero \(2\times2\) matrices over any finite commutative chain ring have an explicit image-length spectrum determined solely by the ring length and residue-field size; the nonautomatic entry-valuation layers are exactly equinumerous. For the centralizer of \(J_n(0)\oplus J_n(0)\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), this gives every square-zero Jordan-type multiplicity, the total census, and an exact maximal-rank count.
