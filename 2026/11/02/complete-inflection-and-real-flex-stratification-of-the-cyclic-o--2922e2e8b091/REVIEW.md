# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The full four-variable polynomial Hessian is verified coefficient-by-coefficient with exact integer arithmetic. The branch quadratic has discriminant \(\lambda^2-4\), establishing the smooth range. The residual Hessian quadratic has discriminant \((\lambda^2-4)(\lambda^2-196)\), and reduction modulo the branch equation proves disjointness of residual and branch divisors. Explicit perfect-square factorizations establish the two exceptional strata, and the degree formula \(144=3\cdot8\cdot6\) checks the complete intersection multiplicity. The local Hessian-contact correspondence is standard in characteristic zero. For real parameters, positivity and a strict inequality establish exactly two ovals/four real hyperflexes when \(\lambda<-2\), and emptiness otherwise. The executable checker supports but does not substitute for the universal argument.

## Originality (PASS with residual historical risk)
The nearest inspected primary work, Badr--Harui (arXiv:2603.26180, especially Sections 1, 3, 4 and the low-degree tables), explicitly contains the symmetry-enhanced octic at \(\lambda=14\) but deals with automorphism groups, not the complete flex divisor over the pencil. The classical octahedral covariant \(X^8+14X^4Y^4+Y^8\) is known (Elkies, 2021); the present evaluated all-parameter flex stratification and real law are different statements. General superelliptic inflection theory (Cotterill et al., arXiv:2110.04813) supplies a broader framework but not this explicit wall-and-contact-order classification. A search of published mathematical-record summaries and focused literature searches found no dominating statement. The possibility of an older equivalent computation under different terminology remains a priority risk.

## Value (PASS)
This describes a complete, natural moduli-pencil invariant rather than an arbitrary numerical instance: each smooth member's entire ramification divisor of its natural plane hyperplane series is known with exact support, weights, collision loci and real realization. The special collision \(\lambda=\pm14\) coincides with the independently characterized octahedral automorphism jump. The real classification links the tangent-contact strata to the topology of the real curve. These facts are useful when comparing plane embeddings and symmetry-enhanced cyclic covers; the result does not claim a downstream general theorem.

## Limits
All assertions are for the specified smooth complex plane octics, with a separate statement for their displayed real models. Other embeddings, singular parameters, positive characteristic and dual-curve singularities are outside scope. Historical-priority coverage and independent external validation are not complete.
