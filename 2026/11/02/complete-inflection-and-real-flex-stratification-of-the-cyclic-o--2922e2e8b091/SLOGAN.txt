For the smooth cyclic octics \(Z^8+X^8+\lambda X^4Y^4+Y^8=0\), the entire flex divisor has a uniform Hessian factorization and exactly three strata: generically \(8\) contact-eight, \(16\) contact-four, and \(64\) contact-three flexes; at \(\lambda=\pm14\), \(8+48\) higher flexes; and at \(\lambda=0\), just \(24\) contact-eight hyperflexes. For real \(\lambda<-2\), the curve has two ovals and exactly four real hyperflexes, while for real \(\lambda>-2\) its real locus is empty.
