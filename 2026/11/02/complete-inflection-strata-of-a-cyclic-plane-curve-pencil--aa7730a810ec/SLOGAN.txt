The smooth complex plane curves \(Y^n=X^n+tX^{n-2}Z^2+Z^n\), \(n\ge5\), have a complete explicit flex classification: away from the Fermat fiber, the only residual jumping condition is \(t^{n/\gcd(n,2)}=K_n\), producing exactly \(n\gcd(n,2)\) additional tangent-contact-four points. Every smooth fiber has total flex weight \(3n(n-2)\), with all contact orders and counts determined.
