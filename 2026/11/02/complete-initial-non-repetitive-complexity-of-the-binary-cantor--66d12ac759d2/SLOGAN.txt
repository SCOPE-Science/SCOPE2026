The binary Cantor word has a complete three-adic staircase for initial non-repetitive complexity: at lengths \(3^k<n\le2\cdot3^k\), \(2\cdot3^k<n<3^{k+1}\), and \(n=3^{k+1}\), the first repeated factor begins respectively at \(2\cdot3^k\), \(3^{k+1}+1\), and \(5\cdot3^k\). A palindrome-gap argument and two distinct-window block decompositions prove the classification for every length.
