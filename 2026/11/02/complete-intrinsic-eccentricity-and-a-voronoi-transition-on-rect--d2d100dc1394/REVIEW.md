# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The quotient-distance reduction to the orbit \(\mathcal O\) is exact. The listed half-plane cell is equivalent to all orbit nearest-site inequalities, including edge cases. Its vertices are exhaustively classified in the two sharp regimes, and squared-radius comparison gives the asserted global maximum. Differentiation proves strict monotonicity and the unique optimal source-circle strata. The rational verifier reconstructs intersections without assuming the formula, while the general theorem is established by geometry, not finite sampling.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority uncertainty

Conway and Rossetti (2003) prove the diameter, so that portion is explicitly not claimed. Davis and Recio-Mitter (2019, §§3–4) give Voronoi vertices for the unit-square Klein metric, a legitimately covered restricted case; our arbitrary aspect-ratio piecewise classification and the \(w>L\) four-vertex transition do not follow by any isometry or metric-preserving reduction of that fixed square. El Mir and Yassine (2012) treat conformal and systolic inequalities, while Hubard and collaborators (2016) treat shortest-path graph embeddings. Searches of published mathematical research records for source eccentricity, covering radius, Chebyshev centers, Voronoi transitions, and equivalent formulations found no stronger full-family coverage. Unindexed priority remains possible; no independent audit was performed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The complete pointwise farthest-distance profile supplies a natural metric-measure invariant of a compact nonhomogeneous nonorientable flat surface and determines its full intrinsic Chebyshev-center locus. The interior non-differentiability and six–four–six combinatorial switch for short-glide shapes are substantive new geometric behavior absent from the well-studied square example. This is not a recomputation of the classical diameter or a relabeling of the corresponding Möbius-band problem.

## Limitations

General nonrectangular and nonflat Klein metrics are outside scope; the existence of older unindexed calculations cannot be ruled out. The exact rational finite checks are supporting evidence, not a replacement for the proof.
