## Correctness — PASS

The graph-to-Euler-circuit bijection is exact: five-bit words index all directed edges of the four-bit overlap graph, and fixing the unique `00000` window removes rotation duplicates. All next-edge choices are traversed by finite depth-first search. The complete list contains 2048 distinct canonical representatives; an independent 65536-ordering Hierholzer enumeration gives exactly the same set. For each representative, literal palindrome window enumeration and independent odd/even center expansion give the same palindrome set. The full 2048-row census and two aggregate tables are regenerated byte-exactly by the attached reproducibility code. Thus the support, frequencies, sharp endpoints, all 23 joint classes and 51 length profiles are exhaustive and correct relative to explicit finite algorithms. A general proof for higher orders is not claimed.

## Originality — PASS, with disclosed source-access limitation

The full original Anisiu–Anisiu–Kása 2006 PDF was inspected for the relation between palindromes and de Bruijn words; the analyzed results do not contain this order-five joint distribution. Baláži–Masáková–Pelantová 2006 concerns infinite recurrent words and different quantifiers. Simpson 2014 gives an unrestricted circular-palindrome bound, not the de Bruijn-restricted joint histogram. OEIS A247000 concerns unrestricted binary circular words, and A016031 counts de Bruijn cycles rather than their palindrome spectrum. These statements do not imply the new frequency table, even when restricting domains. Relevant database searches for the exact family, aliases and palindrome-profile statistics yielded no matching published table. This does not prove absolute novelty. Simpson's original full text could not be inspected, leaving a genuine historical-priority risk, but no known inspected statement dominates the classification. A previously recorded de Bruijn order-five Burrows–Wheeler run-spectrum invariant is mathematically different; cycles with identical BWT run counts can have distinct palindrome totals, so that invariant does not determine this table.

## Value — PASS

Palindromic complexity is a basic pattern statistic; de Bruijn cycles achieve every possible length-five window, making the first nonforced longer palindromes a canonical object of inquiry. At order five, the joint invariant exhibits gaps 25 and 26, upper length 15, and 51 distinct lengthwise profiles. The result is a complete exact classification over a natural finite family of 2048 rotation classes, with reproducible full census, rather than a small isolated numerical improvement. In particular it supplies an exact conditional benchmark absent from the broader unrestricted circular-word bounds.
