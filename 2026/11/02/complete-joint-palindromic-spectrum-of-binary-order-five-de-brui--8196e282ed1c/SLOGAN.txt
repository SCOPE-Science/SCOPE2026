Across all 2048 binary order-five de Bruijn cycles up to rotation, the number of distinct nonempty circular palindromes is exactly one of 22, 23, 24, 27, 28, 29 or 30, with respective frequencies 32, 32, 200, 192, 920, 544 and 128. An exhaustive two-method certificate gives all 23 joint classes by palindrome count and longest palindrome (at most 15), and all 51 lengthwise profiles.
