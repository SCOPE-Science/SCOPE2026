Every multiplication operator on \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x^2,y^n)\) has a Jordan partition determined by just two truncated valuations and the characteristic, yielding an exact finite-field multiplicity polynomial for all partitions and all \(n\ge2\). When \(n\ge3\), divisibility of \(n\) by the characteristic precisely removes the top type \((n+1,n-1)\) and changes the count of the type \((n,n)\).
