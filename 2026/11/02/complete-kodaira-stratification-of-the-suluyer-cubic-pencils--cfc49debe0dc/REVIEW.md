# Review of complete Kodaira stratification of the Suluyer cubic pencils

## Correctness

**PASS (self-review).** The binary-quartic reduction of the explicit cubic pencil produces a Jacobian with degree-four and degree-six coefficient invariants. The exact identity for \(4I^3-J^2\), the quartic discriminant and seven algebraic local-valuation cases exhaust all parameter collisions. The discriminant has total degree twelve; every local discriminant order is at most three, so the Jacobian equations are minimal. Kodaira's characteristic-zero table gives the displayed types. The base scheme has length nine, including one infinitely near point on \(a^2-a+1=0\), and the fibration is relatively minimal. Shioda–Tate yields the geometric Mordell–Weil ranks. The standalone exact-arithmetic checker reproduces the crucial polynomial and local-order identities. No numerical extrapolation is used as proof.

## Originality

**PASS (with historical-priority qualifications).** The closest primary source, Suluyer's arXiv:2412.06928, Theorem 4.1 and Example 4.2, constructs the four conic-line members and analyzes the fiber configuration only at \(a=2\). The later open-access journal article repeats the same special example; neither inspected text supplies the general quartic discriminant, all exceptional parameter loci, the additional \(I_2\) fiber produced by an infinitely near base point, the \(II\) collision locus or the Mordell–Weil rank table. General binary-quartic invariants, Tate–Kodaira classification and Shioda–Tate are not claimed as new. A semantically similar general six-\(I_2\) elliptic-surface record concerns a different fiber basket and does not determine this four-conic-line family. Targeted mathematical-record and literature searches found no direct equivalent, but absence of search hits is not uniqueness proof. The cited 2024 PhD thesis was not accessible beyond metadata and could contain previously unrecognized coverage or an earlier public presentation of the pencil.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The source gives a new explicitly parameterized family of cubic pencils but leaves the singular fibers outside one example unspecified. The theorem gives the complete parameter-wide Kodaira stratification, separates four geometrically distinct collision mechanisms and explains the exceptional infinitely near base point. The geometric Mordell–Weil rank jumps from four to three on two genuine geometric strata. These are standard natural invariants of the full elliptic fibration, not arbitrary numerics or a reformulation of the generic four conic-line components.
