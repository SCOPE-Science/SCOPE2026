The elliptic surfaces associated with Suluyer's four-conic-line cubic pencil have a complete parameter-wise Kodaira classification: generically \(4I_2+4I_1\), with precisely four classes of exceptional collisions producing \(III\), \(I_3\), an additional \(I_2\), or \(II\) fibers. Their geometric Mordell–Weil rank is exactly \(3\) or \(4\), as determined by the same collision strata.
