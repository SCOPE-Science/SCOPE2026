For the opposite binary blocks \(0^m1^m\) and \(1^m0^m\), the exact number of minimal common supersequences of length \(3m+j\) is \(2\binom{m+j-1}{j}^2\) for \(0\le j<m\), with no other possible lengths. This gives a complete structural enumeration and a geometric limiting distribution for the deficit below the maximum length \(4m-1\).
