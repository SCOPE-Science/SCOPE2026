Every Lyndon factor of the finite Zimin word \(Z_n\) is exactly \(Z_{i-1}a_j\) for some \(1\leq i\leq j\leq n\); at a one-based endpoint \(p\), its Lyndon suffix lengths are precisely \(\{2^r:0\leq r\leq\nu_2(p)\}\). There are \(n(n+1)/2\) distinct Lyndon factors and \(2^{n+1}-n-2\) occurrences in total.
