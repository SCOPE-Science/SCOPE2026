For the genus-five Artin–Schreier family over \(\mathbb F_4\) with nonzero coefficient of \(x^2y\), all \(192\) coefficient quadruples realize exactly fourteen Jacobian isogeny classes, whose complete Weil polynomials and frequencies are determined. Exactly \(48\) quadruples are supersingular (precisely when \(c=b^2\)); the remaining \(144\) all have Newton slopes \(1/3,1/2,2/3\) with multiplicities \(3,4,3\).
