Every finite binary Cantor substitution word has a complete, explicitly located maximal-repetition spectrum: the only nonconstant run periods are \(2\cdot3^j\), with two short and a prescribed number of long runs at each scale. The exact run count is \(3\cdot2^{k-1}-3\), while total run length is \(4\cdot3^k-13\cdot2^{k-1}+1\) at level \(k\ge1\).
