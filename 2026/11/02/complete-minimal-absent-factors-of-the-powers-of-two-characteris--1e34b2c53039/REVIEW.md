The candidate forbidden basis is checked element by element for absence and the occurrence of both maximal proper factors. Conversely, forbidden-factor avoidance forces every consecutive gap to be a power of two, successive gaps to double, and both boundary padding lengths to fit the unique corresponding occurrence.
