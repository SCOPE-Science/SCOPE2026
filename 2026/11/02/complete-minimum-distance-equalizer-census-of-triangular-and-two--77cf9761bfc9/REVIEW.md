The adjacency predicates give exact distance formulas for each unselected vertex against each selected landmark. The proof reduces the universal equalizer quantifier to the absence of complementary incidence words, proves no two landmarks suffice beyond root order four, exhausts the five isomorphism types of three-edge root graphs, supplies explicit excluding vertex pairs for the three nonexceptional types, and verifies both exceptional positive types by complete signature analysis. ## Originality — PASS (subject to residual source risk)

The original distance-equalizer paper of González, Hernando, and Mora first defines the invariant, while Kratica, Čangalović, and Kovačević-Vujčić explicitly prove the previously known scalar equidistant dimensions for these graphs. Their relevant published/preprint discussion provides one triangle witness and exact minimum cardinality, but does not classify every minimum selected set or enumerate the two exceptional nontriangle families. The result distinguishes known minimum value from new complete optimizer structure. Related results for complete multipartite distance equalizers and rectangular Hamming distance equalizers have different vertex families and do not imply this two-subset classification. Priority under other terminology cannot be completely excluded.
