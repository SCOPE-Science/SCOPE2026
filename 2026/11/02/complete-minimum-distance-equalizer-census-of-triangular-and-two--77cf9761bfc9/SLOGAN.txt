For \(n\ge7\), every minimum distance-equalizer of the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\) or the two-subset Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\) is exactly a root-graph triangle, giving \(\binom n3\) labeled bases. The exceptional counts are three perfect matchings at \(n=4\) for the triangular graph, forty bases at \(n=5\), and thirty-five at \(n=6\).
