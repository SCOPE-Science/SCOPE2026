The full cardinality polynomial of monitoring edge-geodetic vertex sets of \(C_n\) is \((1+z)^n-1-nz(1+z)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}+\tfrac n2\mathbf1_{\{2\mid n\}}z^2\). Its minimal successful sets are exactly suitable triples and, for even cycles, unions of two antipodal pairs, while its maximal failures are exactly the consecutive blocks of \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) vertices.
