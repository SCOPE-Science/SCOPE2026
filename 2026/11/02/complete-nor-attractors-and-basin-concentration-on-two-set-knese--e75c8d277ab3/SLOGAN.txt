Synchronous open-neighborhood NOR on \(KG(n,2)\), for every \(n\ge5\), has a complete explicit six-basin-size attractor census: its fixed points are exactly stars and triangles, and all other attractors are five precisely classified kinds of two-cycles. The empty/full two-cycle uniquely dominates and captures more than half of all initial configurations exactly when \(n\ge6\), with exceptional attraction probability asymptotic to \(\binom n2 2^{2n-3-\binom n2}\).
