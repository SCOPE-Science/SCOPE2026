# Review of the moving-bigram overlap-code classification

## Correctness

PASS. The all-length prefix formula is an exact rank-two factorization of the \(b\)-state adjacency products. Positivity of the second coordinate in the induced row recurrence makes the change in the next prefix count strictly positive and proves the difficult converse. Every binary code has a concrete realizing schedule. Periodic-code dimension uses a positive periodic transition product, its nonzero-eigenvalue equivalence to the two-dimensional cyclic product, and the previously proved homogeneous moving-hole dimension theorem. Exhaustive finite cases and the independent direct checker corroborate the indexing and arithmetic but are not used as infinite proofs.

## Originality

PASS under the inspected sources. Kong–Sun–Wang already establish full-matrix prefix counting and dimensions, and their periodic-block theorem covers some cyclic-code consequences, including the two-phase specialization. The asserted contribution is *not* those known dimension statements, but the arbitrary-schedule rank-two formula and the converse classification of all finite-prefix count sequences by the entire binary overlap code. The 2026 inhomogeneous-IFS pressure extension does not state that invariant. Earlier fixed-hole correlation-polynomial results do not cover moving sequences. Record searches found no closer implication, though this is not a proof of priority.

## Mathematical value

PASS. A nonstationary symbolic survivor family with \(b^2\) choices of moving holes at each time admits a complete and precisely recoverable one-bit temporal descriptor of all finite prefix census data. The classification is exact even for irregular schedules, not merely numerical dimension examples; it replaces arbitrary \(b\)-dimensional products by explicit two-dimensional products and converts periodic overlap codes into finite algebraic dimension evaluations. The period-four example shows timing carries information lost by mere overlap frequency. This provides a structural classification of a natural subfamily of recently studied moving-hole systems.

## Scientific limitations and closest results

The established dimension/pressure results of Kong–Sun–Wang (2025 preprint) and Kong–Li–Ma–Simon (2026 preprint) are credited, not reasserted as novel. Theorem 1.8 of the former subsumes special periodic-pattern corollaries. The statement covers only one forbidden two-digit word per time, alphabet size at least three and homogeneous base-\(b\) projection. A broad historical theorem for low-rank products could potentially imply this classification; historical priority is not independently certified.
