For every binary block power \(W=(0^a1^b)^k\) with positive \(a,b,k\), two explicit symmetric-wing families determine its complete palindromic-factor length enumerator \(H(z)\) and the minimal-absent-palindrome length polynomial \(B(z)=(2z^2-1)H(z)+1+2z\). The longest minimal absent palindrome has length \(k(a+b)-\min(a,b)+2\), while the total count is \(k(a+b)+4\).
