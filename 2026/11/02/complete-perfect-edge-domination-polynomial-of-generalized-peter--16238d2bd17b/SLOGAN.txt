In the simple generalized Petersen graph \(GP(n,3)\), the complete perfect-edge-domination polynomial has only the full-edge term unless \(5\mid n\); at odd multiples of five exactly five induced-matching solutions appear, while even multiples of five additionally have thirty nonmatching solutions of precisely two new cardinalities. An adjacency-kernel reduction and a ten-period binary-word certificate prove the exact formula for all \(n\ge7\).
