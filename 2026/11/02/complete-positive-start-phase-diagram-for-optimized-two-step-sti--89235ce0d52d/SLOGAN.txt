For the recently optimized second-order two-step stiff-integrator family, every positive starting pair stays nonnegative exactly on a sharp parameter-dependent interval, uniquely widest at damping parameter \(\kappa=1/2\) and step \(h\lambda\le3/2\). Beyond the robust interval, higher-damping parameters admit a remarkable but fragile strip in which precisely one tuned positive eigenhistory survives and every other positive starter eventually becomes negative.
