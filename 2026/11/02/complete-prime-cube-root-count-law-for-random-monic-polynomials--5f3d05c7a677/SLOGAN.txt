For every prime \(p\), the complete distribution of the number of roots modulo \(p^3\) of a uniformly random monic polynomial stabilizes by degree \(3p\) and is an explicit \(p\)-fold convolution of a five-outcome local law. It converges weakly to Poisson(1) as \(p\to\infty\), yet its third moment grows like \(5p\) and its fixed higher moments exhibit the prime-cube singular-lift scale \(p^{2k-5}\) for \(k\ge4\).
