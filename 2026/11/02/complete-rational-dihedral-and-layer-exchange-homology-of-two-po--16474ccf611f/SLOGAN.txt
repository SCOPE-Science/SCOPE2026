For every circular-prism graph with at least five rungs, the full rational first- and second-homology representations of its unordered two-particle configuration space are given by a reflection-and-layer-twisted face module and the exterior square of a rim-edge augmentation module. Its dihedral quotient retains one rational second-homology class, while the quotient by dihedral symmetries together with layer exchange is rationally acyclic.
