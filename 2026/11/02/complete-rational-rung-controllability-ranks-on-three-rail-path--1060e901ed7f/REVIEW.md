The tests are only supporting finite checks; the proof addresses all specified lengths.

## Originality — PASS on checked evidence, with residual risk

The 2011 path paper and 2012 grid paper give underlying eigenmodes and general unweighted PBH conditions, not the rationally weighted three-rail phase formula. A related two-rail result gives a distinct width-two classification; it does not imply the new width-three mod-five and middle-rail laws. The 2024 Cartesian-product work offers a broader general criterion, but its checked abstract does not provide the present explicit arithmetic classification. The full text of that specialist comparison was not available; the possibility of prior equivalent explicit formulas is a residual risk, not a claim of exhaustive absence.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result is a complete natural classification, not a finite numerical table: it simultaneously determines reachability at every vertex, singles out a finite exceptional weight set valid for arbitrarily long grids, and isolates novel mod-five spectral collisions caused by adding the third rail. An implementer can select a fixed rational weight such as \(4/3\) and one corner input to avoid all structural rank loss at every length. It does not quantify the energy or conditioning of control.
