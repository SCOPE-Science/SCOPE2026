The sixteen-vertex Möbius–Kantor graph has an unexpectedly mixed Rips stage \(\bigvee^9S^2\vee\bigvee^2S^3\), followed by a seven-sphere, and every integral persistence interval lasts exactly one scale step. Its full 96-element automorphism group removes all rational degree-two classes but preserves one degree-three class and the seven-sphere orientation class.
