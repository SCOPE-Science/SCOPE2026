Every super-dominating set of the rectangular rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) is counted by an explicit two-variable star-forest generating function plus two nonsurjective-function corrections. For \(m,n\ge5\), exactly \(m(m-1)n(n-1)\) minimum sets exist, and their complements are precisely a pair of disjoint perpendicular stars; the minimum cardinality itself was already known.
