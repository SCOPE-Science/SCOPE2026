For every binary cyclic ideal \(\langle(1+x)^i\rangle\) of length \(32\), \(0\le i\le32\), the complete exact cyclic three-symbol weight enumerator is determined in a reproducible 33-row integer table. Equal minimum distances can mask different minimum-weight multiplicities: the codes with generators \((1+x)^3\) and \((1+x)^4\) have respective triplet-minimum multiplicities \(144\) and \(112\).
