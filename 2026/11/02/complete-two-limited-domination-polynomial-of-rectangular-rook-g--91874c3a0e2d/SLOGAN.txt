For every rectangular rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge4\), all two-limited dominating sets admit a complete omitted-square classification and an explicit cardinality polynomial. Consequently \(\gamma_2^{\mathrm L}(K_m\square K_n)=mn-2\max\{m,n\}\), and all minimizers are complements of pairs of full long rows or columns.
