For the finite-field graphs \(C_a=\{(t,t^4+at^2)\}\), the full two-point ordered representation histogram is explicit over every prime power, including characteristic two and three. In odd characteristic at least five, nonzero coefficients fall into two quadratic-character classes whose exact sumset-size gap is \(-(q-1)/4\) when \(q\equiv1\pmod4\) and \((q+1)/4\) when \(q\equiv3\pmod4\).
