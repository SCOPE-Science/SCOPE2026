All unbordered factors of a finite Zimin word are exactly the fresh-central-letter factors whose two flank lengths have disjoint binary supports. At rank \(n\), their total number is \((3^n-1)/2\), and the precise number at length \(\ell\le2^{n-1}\) is \((n-\lceil\log_2\ell\rceil)2^{s_2(\ell-1)}\).
