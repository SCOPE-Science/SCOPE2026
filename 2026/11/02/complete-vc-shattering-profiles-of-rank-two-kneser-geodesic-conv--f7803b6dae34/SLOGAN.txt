For the geodesically convex subsets of \(KG(n,2)\), the complete VC-shattering polynomial has exact exceptional profiles at \(n=5,6\); for every \(n\ge7\), all shattered supports of size at least three are precisely matchings of \(K_n\). The VC dimension drops from \(4\) at \(n=5\) to \(3\) at \(n=6\), then equals \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) for \(n\ge7\).
