Two canonical integral spike-code families \(\bigl(I_k\mid J_k-I_k\bigr)\) and \(\bigl(I_k\mid J_k-I_k\mid\mathbf1_k\bigr)\) have explicit complete Hamming weight enumerators for every \(k\ge2\) and every integer modulus \(q\ge2\), expressed by binomial powers and a finite gcd sum. In contrast with previously known full-support counts, the formulas determine every weight multiplicity, including exactly \(\binom{k}{2}(q-1)\) minimum-weight words in either family for \(k\ge5\).
