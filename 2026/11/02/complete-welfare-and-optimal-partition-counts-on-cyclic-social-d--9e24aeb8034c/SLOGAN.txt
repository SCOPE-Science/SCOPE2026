For the canonical normalized social-distance game on a labeled cycle \(C_n\), the grand coalition is uniquely optimal for \(3\le n\le5\), ties with three triple-pair partitions at \(n=6\), and is strictly suboptimal for every \(n\ge7\). Every larger-cycle optimum consists of contiguous blocks of sizes \(2,3,4\), with an explicit exact welfare formula and exactly \(3\), \(n\), or \(n(q+1)/2\) maximizing partitions in the three residue classes \(n=3q,3q+1,3q+2\).
