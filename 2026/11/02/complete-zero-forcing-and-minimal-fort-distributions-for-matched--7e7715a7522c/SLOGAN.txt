The complete zero-forcing polynomial of the matched-clique graph \(K_n\square K_2\) is determined for every \(n\ge2\), together with an exact classification of all inclusion-minimal forts. The coefficients give \(2n(n-1)+2\) minimum zero-forcing sets for \(n\ge3\) and a sharp random-deletion window with limiting success probability \(1-\bigl(1-(1+\lambda)e^{-\lambda}\bigr)^2\) when the white probability is \(\lambda/n\).
