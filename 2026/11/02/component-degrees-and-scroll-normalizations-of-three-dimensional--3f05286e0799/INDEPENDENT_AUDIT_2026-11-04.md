## correctness

PASS

For each h>=4 the Witt-index-one argument confines pairwise orthogonal 3-vectors either to three supported anisotropic columns or to one distinguished anisotropic vector and a shared isotropic line in its perp. The A_J locus is the irreducible complete intersection of 3 independent Gram quadrics in P^8, dimension five, degree 8. Over the isotropic conic Q=P^1, the incidence bundle E_i=K+S^(h-1)=O(-1)^2+O(-2)^(h-1) has rank h+1; P(E_i) maps finitely, birationally and properly to W_i, giving its normalization, dimension h+1 and degree -deg c1(E_i)=2h. The tautological coordinate series has dimension 3h, one less than full h0(E_i*)=3h+1. At generic points of A_J and W_i, direct Gram-Jacobian linear constraints have ranks 3h-6 and 2(h-1), equal to their expected codimensions; thus generic multiplicity one (not global radicality). I read the supplied SymPy rank checker covering 4<=h<=12; the general proof is the geometric incidence and rank argument, not finite extrapolation. At h=4 the four A and four W components have degree 8, total 64; for h>=5 the top cycle degree is h*(2h)=2h^2.
