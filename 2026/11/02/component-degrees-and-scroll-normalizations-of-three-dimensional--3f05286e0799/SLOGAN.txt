For orthogonal \(h\)-frames in a complex three-dimensional quadratic space, the projective component degrees are exactly \(8\) for each of the \(\binom h3\) triple-supported components and \(2h\) for each of the \(h\) isotropic-line components. The latter normalize to explicit rational normal scrolls, yielding a degree-\(64\) eight-component complete intersection at \(h=4\) and top-dimensional degree \(2h^2\) for \(h\ge5\).
