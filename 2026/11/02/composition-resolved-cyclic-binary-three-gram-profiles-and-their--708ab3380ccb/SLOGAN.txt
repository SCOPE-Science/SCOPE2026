The complete Hamming-weight-resolved zero-error capacity of the cyclic binary three-gram multiset channel has an explicit bivariate rational generating function. At each fixed weight \(w\ge2\), its exact capacity stabilizes at length \(3w\) to \((w^3+8w)/8\) for even \(w\), or \((w^3+7w)/8\) for odd \(w\); exactly one profile is missing one length earlier.
