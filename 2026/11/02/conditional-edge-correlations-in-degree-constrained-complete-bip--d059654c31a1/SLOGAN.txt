Conditioning a uniform complete-bipartite spanning tree on every labeled vertex degree preserves nonpositive covariance for incident edges but can reverse the sign for disjoint edges. The exact two-edge probability formulas give a sharp conditional covariance of \(1/4\) already on \(K_{2,3}\), and positive covariance with all four endpoints of degree two on \(K_{t,t}\) for every \(t\ge4\).
