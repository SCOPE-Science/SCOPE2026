On a rectangular flat Klein bottle with glide length \(a\ge b/2\), the mean squared intrinsic distance from a fixed point follows an exact nonconstant quartic profile, with sharp oscillation \(b^4/(192a^2)\). The covariance of squared distances from a shared random endpoint is \(b^8/(302400a^4)\), determining the aspect ratio after area normalization and governing the leading variance of pairwise-distance averages.
