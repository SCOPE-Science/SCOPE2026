# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Conditional exchangeability yields exact factorial moments \(r!e_r(c)/(n)_r\). Pollak's canonical circular rotation converts the collision sum into the ordinary occupancy collision count with expectation \(\binom n2/(n+1)\). The elementary distinct-sample bound makes every fixed conditional factorial moment tend to one; Poisson moment determinacy, tightness and the countable-state implication to total variation give expected conditional total-variation convergence. The packaged checker verifies small cases independently. The result does not claim a finite rate or use finite enumeration as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with qualified priority
The 2016 Diaconis–Hicks work already supplies the circular occupancy law; the 2022 Paguyo paper proves marginal Poisson limits for parking cycles and fixed points; the 2025 Rubey–Yin paper gives a full marginal fixed-point distribution. None of those marginal statements implies conditional asymptotic independence from the complete, growing labelled histogram. Comparison with Stark's 2026 multiset-target matching model distinguishes its sorted-word target from the index-specific parking statistic. Exact priority of the conditional formulation is uncertain; the 2025 full text could not be accessed.

## Value — PASS
The distinction between marginal Poisson behavior and conditional Poisson behavior is significant: complete frequency information could a priori change predictions about fixed positions. The theorem rules this out uniformly over all histogram events and thus gives a natural infinite-dimensional decoupling principle, not a single routine covariance calculation. The exact conditional moments reveal finite-sample residual dependence, making the limit genuinely substantive.
