# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The entire assertion is scoped to one packet-reversal step from the identity. A packet of length \(\ell\) contributes exactly one fixed point when \(\ell\) is odd and exactly \(\binom{\ell}{2}\) inversions, with no cross-packet inversion. Increasing a Bernoulli cut never decreases the number of fixed points and strictly decreases inversion count, giving the all-size negative covariance and strictness by product-measure association and a positive-probability strict coordinate influence. Conditional on \(m\) packets, every composition is equiprobable; parity-constrained stars-and-bars plus the two factorial moments of uniform weak compositions gives an exact regression. Its strictly positive increment proves the conditional covariance sign and all equality cases. The geometric renewal-reward representation establishes the bivariate central limit, with finite exponential-moment formulas yielding the explicit covariance matrix. The terminal remainder has a geometric tail. An exact-rational verifier independently enumerates finite sizes \(2\le n\le12\) and checks the moment algebra (536 assertions). Finite enumeration is corroboration, not an all-size proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The motivating full-text article by Jiang (arXiv:2610.00137v1) develops cutoff, coupling and a one-card spectrum for precisely the same cut-and-reverse Markov kernel, but does not state the positive *conditional* fixed-point/inversion association, its exact regression or the negative *unconditional* association. The nearest structured mathematical records identified concerns uniform permutations, or the correlation between part count and maximal part of a uniform composition; neither implies this exact dependent-pair statement because the sampling law or observables differ. Bender–Canfield and classical renewal-reward results provide broad Gaussian-limit methods, and that general component is not claimed as a new central limit principle. Archibald et al. use a different notion of inversion (between composition parts, not within a reversed packet). Search did not identify an equivalent theorem, but negative search alone is not priority proof. Source-material limitations remain documented in the structured review.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A complete conditional/unconditional sign reversal is a nontrivial dependence classification for two standard statistics of a natural shuffle, not a renamed or arbitrary small subcase. The conditioning variable is directly observable as \(n\) minus the number of descents. The theorem supplies a closed conditional support law, strict equality boundaries for every finite size, an explicit regression and an asymptotic covariance matrix. These separate the effect of the number of packets from that of packet-length variability and quantify the dependence at arbitrary fixed cut probability. The result does not claim sharp mixing consequences, multiple passes, or a general theorem for all shuffle models.
