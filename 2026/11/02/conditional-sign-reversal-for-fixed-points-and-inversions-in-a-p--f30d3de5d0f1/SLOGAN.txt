For one Bernoulli packet-reversal shuffle of an ordered deck, fixed points and inversions have strictly negative unconditional covariance, but their covariance is nonnegative after conditioning on the observable packet count, and is strictly positive in every nondegenerate such stratum. An exact conditional regression explains the reversal, and an explicit bivariate Gaussian limit gives asymptotic correlation \(-[1+(1-p)^2]/(2-p)^2\) for every fixed cut probability \(p\in(0,1)\).
