Fixing all parent edges among labels newer than \(i\) turns the subtree size of birth label \(i\) into a sum of independent Bernoulli-marked component sizes. Consequently, every earlier subtree size is mean-independent of the entire later subtree-size vector: all birth-ranked subtree sizes are pairwise uncorrelated despite being dependent, and every deterministic birth-weighted total path length has an exact diagonal variance formula.
