For \(xy=z^{2m}\) in characteristic dividing \(2m\), the ordinary Nash blowup is nonnormal along an entire exceptional \(\mathbf P^1\), with conductor a multiplicity-\(m\) thickening. Its normalization restricts to the squaring map on that curve, giving a purely inseparable double cover exactly in characteristic two, although the normalized charts have only \(A_{m-1}\) singularities.
