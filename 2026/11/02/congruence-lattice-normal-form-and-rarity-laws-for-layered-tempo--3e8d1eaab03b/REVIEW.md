# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact domain is a finite ordinal sum of nonempty antichains with arbitrary independently selected loops. In the general all-size proof, the unique least included layer of an upset gives its trace. The finite archival interval-witness condition of Alvarez [1, Proposition 2] is both necessary and sufficient exactly under the two displayed restrictions. The published congruence correspondence [1, Theorem 2] turns this classification into the full congruence poset, ordered by reverse inclusion. Maximal proper archival upsets characterize the unique monolith. Combinatorial level decomposition gives all labelled and unlabelled formulas, and dominant-pole residues establish the limiting proportions. The independent checker agrees with direct subset tests and direct reachability on every labelled example of sizes at most five and with rational series coefficients through size eight. Boundary cases for one level, one world, no loops, and all loops were included. Finite tests do not replace the all-size argument.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual priority risk

The closest source, Alvarez, *A dual characterisation of simple and subdirectly-irreducible temporal Heyting algebras* (arXiv:2505.08014), is inspected at its defining relations, Theorem 2, finite archival-witness Proposition 2, and simple/irreducible characterization. It proves the general correspondence used here but does not tabulate the explicit two-condition description for loop-marked weak orders, the exact three generating functions in both labelled and unlabelled settings, or the opposing asymptotic rarity laws. The unrestricted count is the existing ordered-Bell count with independent loop bits (OEIS A216794), explicitly excluded from originality. An existing congruence-lattice claim for incidence transits of finite graphs gives a downset lattice of a contracted graph; its underlying height-two incidence structures generally do not coincide with the ordered sums studied here and it gives no such global census. Published results about weak-order mapping spaces, KI-frame endpoints, and other modal weak-order counts concern different operations and do not imply this theorem. Search did not establish an equivalent exact census or stronger covering result. Older equivalents under different terminology remain possible; a database partition or unsuccessful search alone is not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The motivating theory explicitly identifies simple and subdirectly-irreducible temporal Heyting algebras as key finite algebraic semantics, but gives only general reachability criteria. The present complete congruence-lattice normal form is structurally usable to identify **all** congruences of an infinite parameter family of non-chain finite algebras. Exact enumeration and the sharp separation between exponential simplicity rarity and positive irreducibility density make the result a motivated quantitative classification, not an arbitrary tiny finite slice or restatement of the duality theorem.

## Closest literature and limitations

The principal prerequisite is Alvarez's 2025 arXiv preprint, updated 2026, DOI:10.1007/s00012-026-00939-6; its general congruence theorem is not new here. The total-count control is OEIS A216794. The result is limited to the specified loop-marked layered frames, not all temporal algebras. Equivalent older labelled-order/loop-structure counts have not been ruled out with certainty.
