For temporal Heyting algebras dual to loop-decorated ordinal sums of antichains, every congruence has an explicit two-condition archival-upset normal form, yielding exact labelled and unlabelled generating functions for simple and subdirectly-irreducible members. Simplicity is exponentially rare with decay base \(\log(2)/(2\log\phi)\approx0.72021\), whereas the subdirectly-irreducible proportion converges to \(2-\sqrt2+(\sqrt2-1)\log2\approx0.8728974\), where \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\).
