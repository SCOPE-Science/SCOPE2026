In a chain semilattice times a finite group, every congruence is an interval partition decorated by decreasing normal subgroups, and it preserves the natural maximum operation exactly when the decorations are constant. For elementary abelian groups, the enriched-congruence proportion has an explicit periodic-theta prefactor times \(p^{-\frac{m-1}{4(m+1)}d^2}\), while its proportion after quotienting by automorphisms decays only as \(2^{m-1}m!d^{1-m}\).
