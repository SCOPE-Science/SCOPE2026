For ordinary elliptic products in characteristic \(p\ge3\), the connected finite étale Kummer strata of normalized \(p\)-closed turbulent foliations with \(d\) distinct poles correspond exactly to zero-sum multisets of nonzero residues modulo \(p\). Their degrees and numbers have explicit formulas, and every prescribed reduced pole divisor supports \((p-1)^d+(p-1)(-1)^d\) geometric foliation points.
