For every pair of connected graph motifs, conditioning on a prescribed isolated component makes all motif copies crossing its boundary disappear, giving an exact covariance identity for induced and non-induced motif counts in \(G(n,p)\). Induced nonclique motifs can exhibit two strict covariance-sign reversals; for three-vertex path motifs, equal-order tree components have sharply different thresholds, with paths and stars the extremizers.
