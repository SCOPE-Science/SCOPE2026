For triangular graphs \(J(3q+1,2)\), reconfiguring minimum total dominating sets by replacing one selected vertex gives a connected \(6q\)-regular graph, while restricting replacements to adjacent vertices still gives a connected \(4q\)-regular graph. Both have \((3q+1)!/(2^q q!)\) vertices; their known minimum-set count is used as an input, not claimed as new.
