Every Dilcher–Stolarsky Stern \((0,1)\)-polynomial has maximal consecutive coefficient-one runs of length at most three, and \(P_5=1+x+x^2\) attains three. Across \(P_1,\ldots,P_{2^r}\), the exact counts of run lengths one, two and three are \(2\cdot3^{r-2}+1\), \((3^{r-2}+1)/2\) and \((3^{r-2}-1)/2\), respectively, for every \(r\ge2\).
