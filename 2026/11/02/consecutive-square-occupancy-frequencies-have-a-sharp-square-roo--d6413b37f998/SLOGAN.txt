In uniform balls-into-boxes sampling, the numbers of boxes containing \(k^2\) and \((k+1)^2\) balls have positive covariance only in a sharp window \(N=k(k+1)B\pm\sqrt{k(k+1)B}+O_k(1)\) for large numbers of boxes \(B\). Within that square-root window, the covariance has a nonzero, explicitly sign-changing second-order limit, revealing an arithmetic criticality invisible to the usual first-order occupancy fluctuation law.
