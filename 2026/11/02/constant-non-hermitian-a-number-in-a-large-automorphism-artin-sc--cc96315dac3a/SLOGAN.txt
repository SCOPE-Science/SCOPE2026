For the large-automorphism Artin–Schreier curves \(y^p-y=x^{p+1}+ax^2\) in every odd prime characteristic \(p\ge5\), the Cartier rank is exactly \((p^2-1)/8\) for **every** nonzero parameter \(a\), but is zero at the Hermitian parameter \(a=0\). Thus the Jacobian \(a\)-number is constant on the entire non-Hermitian family despite a sharp jump at its boundary.
