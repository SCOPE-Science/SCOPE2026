For finite intersections of congruent Euclidean balls approaching their minimum common radius, the volume vanishes with sharp power \(\varepsilon^{(d+k)/2}\), where \(k\) is the positive-spanning rank of the enclosing-sphere contact set; the leading coefficient is an explicit beta-weighted polar-polytope volume. In full contact rank the next coefficient is a strictly negative polar-inertia defect, with a closed formula for regular simplices.
