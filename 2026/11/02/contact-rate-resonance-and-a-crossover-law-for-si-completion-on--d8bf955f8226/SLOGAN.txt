For SI transmission started at the common hub of \(m\) identical cliques, equality of hub-to-leaf and within-clique contact rates produces an additional \(\log\log m\) delay in the complete-infection time. An explicit Gumbel crossover with factor \((k-1)^2(1-e^{-d})/d\), interpreted continuously at \(d=0\), governs the transition when the two rates differ by order \(1/\log m\).
