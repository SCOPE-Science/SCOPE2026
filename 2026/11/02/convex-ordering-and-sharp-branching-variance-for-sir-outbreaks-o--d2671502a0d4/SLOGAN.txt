For a finite rooted-tree SIR outbreak with independent individual transmissibilities of fixed mean \(p\), greater variability in convex order makes the entire final-case count greater in convex order without changing its mean. The exact variance penalty is \(\operatorname{Var}(\Theta)B(G,r,p)\), where the sharp rooted-tree coefficient ranges from zero only for an endpoint-rooted path to \((n-1)(n-2)\) only for a center-rooted star.
