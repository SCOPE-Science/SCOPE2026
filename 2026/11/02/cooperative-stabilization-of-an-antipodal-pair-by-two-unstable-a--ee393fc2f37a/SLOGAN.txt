Two individually unstable attention heads with different softmax temperatures cooperate to stabilize a genuinely antipodal two-token equilibrium in a discrete normalized transformer map. The joint tangent spectrum is \(\{0,4/9,4/9,4/9,4/9,4/9\}\), whereas the isolated heads have expanding multipliers \(10/3\) and \(-12/7\); the attracting equilibrium persists under small parameter perturbations.
