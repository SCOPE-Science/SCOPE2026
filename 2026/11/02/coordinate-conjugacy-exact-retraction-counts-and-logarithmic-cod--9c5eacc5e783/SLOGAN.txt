Every unital algebra retraction of \(\mathbb F_q[(C_p)^d]\) is conjugate to a coordinate projection, and the number with image dimension \(p^k\) is \({d\brack k}_q q^{k(p^d-p^k)+(d-k)(p^k-k-1)}\). For a uniform retraction, the number of discarded generators is asymptotically confined to three integers around \(\log_p d\), so the image has dimension on the order of \(p^d/d\).
