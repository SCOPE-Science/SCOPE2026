A two-block SparseStack sketch on a coordinate subspace has Poisson-distributed asymptotic column nullity below half the row count, with a sharp sign-correlation-dependent mean. Reusing signs across the two blocks lowers the limiting probability of full column rank from \((1-c^2)^{1/4}\) to \((1-c^2)^{1/2}\) at column-to-block-row ratio \(c\in(0,1)\); at \(c\ge1\), nullity diverges in probability.
