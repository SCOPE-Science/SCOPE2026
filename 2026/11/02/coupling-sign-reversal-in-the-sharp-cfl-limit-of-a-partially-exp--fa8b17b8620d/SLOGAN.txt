For the two-mode weighted partially explicit wave scheme, cross-mode stiffness coupling raises the sharp stable squared-step ceiling when \(\omega<1/2\) and lowers it when \(\omega>1/2\), with a coupling-independent dividing ceiling of \(4/B\) at \(\omega=1/2\). The exact power-boundedness threshold is \(h^2<8/[\sqrt{(A+B)^2+4(2\omega-1)C^2}-(A-B)]\), and the stabilizing gain can diverge near a rank-one positive-definite stiffness limit.
