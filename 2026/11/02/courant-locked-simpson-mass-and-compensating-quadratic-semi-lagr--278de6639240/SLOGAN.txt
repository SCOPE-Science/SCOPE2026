For periodic quadratic semi-Lagrangian interpolation, the conserved mean is Courant-dependent and agrees with the interpolant's Simpson mass only at fractional displacements \(2/3\) and \(4/3\); its sharp long-time checkerboard bias approaches \(1/3\) at vanishing displacement. Two individually nonconservative steps can exactly compensate, with all such pairs characterized by an explicit quadratic equation.
