On a noisy one-way directed ring, every optimal placement of \(k\) perfectly pinned leaders has cyclic follower gaps differing by at most one, for every predecessor gain \(0<\rho\le1\). Near \(\rho=1\), attenuation on the sharp scale \(1/n\) produces an explicit crossover between critical \(n^{3/2}\)-scale variance and the linear, attenuated regime.
