A unilateral Hilbert-space weighted shift whose exceptional edge is at depth \(L\) has the sharp numerical-radius threshold \(t^2=1+L^{-1}\). Under the critical scaling \(t^2=1+\beta/L\), its outlier develops an exact inverse-square depth limit, and the normalized eigenmode converges to a sinh/exponential boundary-layer profile with an explicit mass-partition law.
