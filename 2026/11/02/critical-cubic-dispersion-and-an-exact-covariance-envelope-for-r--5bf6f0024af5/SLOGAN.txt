At the exact repulsive-feedback threshold of the linear delayed Brownian model, the impulse response lies between \(2t/\tau\) and \(2t/\tau+1\), forcing cubic position variance with coefficient \(8D/(3\tau^2)\). An all-lag covariance envelope and an explicit finite-scale coupling show that the entire rescaled path converges to integrated Brownian motion, without asserting that the nonlinear feedback particle shares the same long-time law.
