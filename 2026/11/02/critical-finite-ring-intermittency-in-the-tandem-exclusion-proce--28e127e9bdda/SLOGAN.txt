In a strongly cooperative finite-ring tandem exclusion process with follower rate proportional to the square of the ring length, the instantaneous stationary current per bond tends to zero in probability while its mean tends to \(\alpha\rho\). Rare order-\(N\) intensity bursts of order-\(N^{-1}\) probability produce the positive mean and a stationary intensity variance asymptotic to \(\alpha^2\lambda\rho N\).
