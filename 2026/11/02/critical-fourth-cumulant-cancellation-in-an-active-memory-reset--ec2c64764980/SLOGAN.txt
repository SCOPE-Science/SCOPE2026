In the uniform-memory resetting telegraph walk, the long-time fourth cumulant changes sign precisely at the resetting-to-flipping ratio \(r/\gamma=2\). At this critical ratio, the logarithmically growing term cancels and the fourth cumulant converges to the positive constant \(v^4(18-\pi^2)/(8\gamma^4)\), so standardized excess kurtosis falls as \((18-\pi^2)/(2\log^2(rt))\) rather than the generic inverse logarithm.
