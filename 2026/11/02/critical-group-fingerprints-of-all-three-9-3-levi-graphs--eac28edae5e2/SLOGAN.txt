The three combinatorial \((9_3)\) incidence types have distinct exact sandpile groups: the known Pappus class has only \(2\)- and \(3\)-torsion, whereas the two other classes exhibit \(5\)- and \(107\)-torsion, respectively. A complete point-labelled census and exact integral Laplacians determine both non-Pappus groups and their spanning-tree counts.
