Checking all 1128 unordered pairs gives 144 within-family and 192 cross-family edges; 48 vertices have degree 14 and the graph is connected. Sorted factorwise valuations produce exactly the stated 47 invariants with determinant/product match and exponent 5760. All calculations are exact finite arithmetic, not floating point or inference from eigenvalues alone. The completeness of the 48 geometric lines is the explicitly cited classical theorem.
