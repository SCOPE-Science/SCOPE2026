The \(48\) lines on the complex Fermat quartic form a connected, \(14\)-regular graph whose entire sandpile group is determined by the reduced Laplacian Smith form. This integral invariant has exponent \(5760\) and order \(2^{115}3^{28}5^6\), refining the classical line-incidence geometry without identifying it with the quartic's Picard discriminant group.
