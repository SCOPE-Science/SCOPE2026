For every integer \(m\ge2\), a bounded pseudoconvex complete Reinhardt domain with \(C^m\) boundary exhibits a genuine \(\delta K/S\gtrsim\log(1/\delta)\) separation along one inward-normal sequence. The sharp logarithmic scale is attained for a single finite-regularity domain, in contrast with the known smooth-domain constructions that defeat each sublinear gauge separately.
