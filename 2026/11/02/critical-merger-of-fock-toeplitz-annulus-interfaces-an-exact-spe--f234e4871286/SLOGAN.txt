A moving Fock Toeplitz annulus whose two boundaries are only one coherent-state width apart has a complete spectral transition governed by the difference of two Gaussian distribution functions. Its rescaled Schatten-one semi-commutator trace is exactly \(R[2c\operatorname{erfc}(c)+2(1-e^{-c^2})/\sqrt\pi]\), interpolating strictly between collapsed and independent boundary interfaces.
