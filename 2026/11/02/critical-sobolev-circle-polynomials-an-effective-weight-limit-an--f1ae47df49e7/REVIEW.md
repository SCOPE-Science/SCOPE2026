# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact Gram matrix is tri-diagonal, its coefficient equation has uniformly bounded inverse in the maximum norm, and the explicit characteristic root and quadratic corrector cancel terms of order one and inverse degree. A bounded polynomial times a decaying geometric sequence controls the remaining residual uniformly in the coefficient index. Summation proves the exterior expansion for all allowed parameters. Exact rational full-Gram tests and independent large-degree tridiagonal checks are supplementary.

## Originality — PASS under inspected evidence
Majumder–Swaminathan (arXiv:2610.31994v1, Theorem 3.2) is the closest source: it addresses precisely two measures with critical \(\lambda/n^2\) derivative scaling but prints a second-measure-only Szegő limit. The present admissible model has an explicitly different, strictly parameter-dependent exterior limit and a sharp first correction. Older Castillo–Garza–Marcellán results use one common measure and Marcellán–Sri Ranga (2017) uses fixed-penalty pairs. Detailed alias, broader coverage, table, and implication comparisons and read extents appear in AUDIT.json. Inaccessible older primary material and undiscovered equivalent work remain risks.

## Value — PASS
Balanced critical scaling is structurally meaningful; this two-positive-measure model identifies the effective spectral weight that can replace a naive second-measure-only expectation. Its exact correction establishes a sharp convergence rate and a concrete benchmark for more general two-measure asymptotics.

## Limits
The theorem covers a single cosine Fourier mode and fixed positive \(\lambda,c\), not all Szegő-class weights or parameter-uniform endpoint limits. The comparison to the recent source is to its publicly printed statement, not to future revisions. No external or independent audit was performed.
