For a balanced degree-dependent Sobolev inner product on the unit circle with Lebesgue mass and a first-cosine derivative weight, the monic polynomial's exterior limit is governed by the effective mixture \(1+2\lambda c(1+\lambda)^{-1}\cos\theta\), rather than the derivative measure alone. An exact \(n^{-1}\) rational correction and a uniform \(O(n^{-2})\) coefficient remainder show the transition is genuinely parameter-dependent and quantitatively sharp.
