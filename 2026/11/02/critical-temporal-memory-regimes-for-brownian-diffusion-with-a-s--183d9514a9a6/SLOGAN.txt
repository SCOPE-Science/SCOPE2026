For Brownian diffusion with complete resets driven by an independently Poisson-refreshed random rate, the weak-refresh limit has two sharp temporal-memory thresholds: the stationary variance diverges at low-rate exponent \(\alpha\le1\), while the integrated normalized correlation time diverges already at \(\alpha\le2\). The full correlation crosses over to an incomplete-gamma profile for \(0<\alpha<1\), and at \(\alpha=1\) follows the exact logarithmic-time shape \((1-q)_+\).
