For a completely reset inertial active particle, the scale \(r\kappa_r\) decides whether high-frequency resetting produces a propulsion-directed quadratic-age ray or an isotropic integrated-Brownian noise mixture. At the critical scale \(r\kappa_r\to\eta>0\), the full joint position–velocity law has an explicit Gaussian-age mixture, and the planar position density has a sharp angle-dependent \(\exp[-I_\eta(\theta)\sqrt\rho]\) tail that decays strictly faster against the reset heading.
