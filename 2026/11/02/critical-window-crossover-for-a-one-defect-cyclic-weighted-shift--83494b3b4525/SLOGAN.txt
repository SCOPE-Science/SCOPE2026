A single exceptional weight \(1+a/n\) in an otherwise unit cyclic Hilbert-space shift has a sharp numerical-radius crossover: multiplying the excess radius by \(n^2\) produces one explicit trigonometric branch for \(a<0\) and a hyperbolic branch for \(a>0\). Fixed subcritical defects approach the ordinary-shift radius quadratically in cycle length, whereas fixed supercritical defects have a localized spectral outlier with an exponentially small finite-size correction.
