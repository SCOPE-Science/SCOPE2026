For every crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\), locally and globally minimal defensive alliances admit explicit two-degree cardinality polynomials: the only local-but-not-global configurations have exactly two shared matching indices. At odd parameters \(n=2k+1\), the number of locally minimal alliances is exactly \(\binom{2k+1}{k+1}^2\), while the fraction failing global minimality decays exponentially.
