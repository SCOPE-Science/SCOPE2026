For three-color inhibitory firefly dynamics on a crown graph with \(n\ge3\) matched pairs, every configuration becomes periodic within thirteen steps, with sharp bounds nine at sizes three and four. A primitive eighteen-cycle occurs only for \(n=3\), whereas every \(n\ge4\) has only three- and four-cycles, with an exact closed formula for the four-cycle attraction basin and exponentially predominant three-cycle attraction.
