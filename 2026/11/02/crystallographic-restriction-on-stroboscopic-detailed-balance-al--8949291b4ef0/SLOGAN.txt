For homogeneous biased nearest-neighbor Markov walks on finite rings, an exactly reversible positive-time sampled chain can arise only for ring sizes \(3\), \(4\), or \(6\), at explicitly classified discrete observation intervals. Alternating resonances are either indistinguishable from a reversible continuous-time walk or merely reversible in discrete time with a negative eigenvalue; every other ring retains a strict stationary time arrow at every finite sampling interval.
