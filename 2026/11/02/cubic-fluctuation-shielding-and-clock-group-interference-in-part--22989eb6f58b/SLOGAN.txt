For a finite connected harmonic oscillator network, changing which reset nodes share a Poisson clock leaves every stationary mean unchanged but can change the unreset-node covariance at order \(r^{-3}\) (with a leading coefficient that can vanish), through an explicit grounded-Laplacian Gramian. On a three-node star, synchronized resetting cancels center fluctuations exactly for opposite leaf frequencies, while independent resetting produces positive variance \(\nu^2/[(r+1)(r^2+5r+5)]\); for equal leaf frequencies the large-rate ordering reverses.
