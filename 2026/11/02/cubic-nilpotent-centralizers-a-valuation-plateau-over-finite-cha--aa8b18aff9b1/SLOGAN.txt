For every finite commutative chain ring of length \(s\) and residue-field size \(q\), the exact number of \(2\times2\) matrices satisfying \(X^3=0\) is \(q^{4(s-\lceil s/3\rceil)}+\lceil s/3\rceil(q^2-1)q^{3s-2-\lceil s/3\rceil}\). More strikingly, every exact entry-valuation stratum below \(\lceil s/3\rceil\) contains precisely the same number of cubic-nilpotent matrices; equivalently this counts cube-zero operators commuting with two equal nilpotent Jordan blocks.
