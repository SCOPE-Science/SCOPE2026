Stretching one edge of \(K_{n,n,n}\) to any fixed length \(1<t\le2\) strictly decreases its exact negative-type exponent, with a unique critical mode and a one-dimensional endpoint kernel. The loss has the sharp cubic law \(\wp_n(1)-\wp_n(t)\sim(\log t)/(3n^3(\log2)^2)\), while the snowflake endpoint affine dimension increases by one.
