The universal third-order fast-refresh expansion of a one-dimensional walk in an iid regenerating environment contains a negative cubic correction \(-8s^2(1-p)^3\), which makes complete refreshing a strict local **maximum** of diffusivity at zero mean drift. At near-zero drift, the crossover occurs when \(m^2/(1-p)\) passes the sharp threshold \(2s/3\), reversing the known nonzero-drift quadratic behavior.
