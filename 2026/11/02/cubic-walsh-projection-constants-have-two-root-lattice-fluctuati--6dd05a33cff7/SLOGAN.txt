The real cubic Walsh projection constant has a sharp \(N^{3/2}\) leading term followed by an oscillatory \(\sqrt N\) correction governed by a central parity phase and two moving Gaussian-root phases. Exact limsup and liminf of the scaled correction are \(-\phi_0/4\) and \(-3\phi_0/4-2\phi_0e^{-3/2}\), with \(\phi_0=(2\pi)^{-1/2}\).
