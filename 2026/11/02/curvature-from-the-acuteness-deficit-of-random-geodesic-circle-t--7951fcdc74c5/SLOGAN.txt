For three arclength-uniform points on a shrinking geodesic circle of any smooth Riemannian surface, the probability of an acute intrinsic triangle is \(1/4-3K(p)r^2/\pi^2+O_p(r^3)\). Thus the infinitesimal acuteness deficit gives a direct, pointwise Bernoulli characterization of Gaussian curvature, without a constant-curvature assumption.
