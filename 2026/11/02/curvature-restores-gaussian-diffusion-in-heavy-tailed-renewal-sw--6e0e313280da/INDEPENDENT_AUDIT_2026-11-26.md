# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Integrating one curved flight produces the centered iid complex reward v exp(i Theta)(exp(i omega L)-1)/(i omega), with modulus at most 2v/|omega|. Its per-coordinate covariance is v^2 E[1-cos(omega L)]/omega^2 by isotropy. A functional CLT for the indexed rewards and the finite-mean renewal law of large numbers give a time change by 1/mu. The joint convergence does not require reward-duration independence because the limiting clock is deterministic. At equilibrium the delay is almost surely finite, and unfinished and initial arcs are uniformly negligible after rescaling; the same limit holds starting at a renewal. Zero covariance occurs exactly for full-circle-multiple durations. In equilibrium the velocity dot-product correlation is v^2 cos(omega s) E[(L-s)_+]/mu; after any renewal the two headings give zero expected dot product. Integrating by parts and using integrability of the fresh survival function gives E[1-cos(omega L)]/(mu omega^2) for the scalar residual-survival cosine integral, even when the original cosine integral is only conditionally convergent. The planar MSD slope is 4D. For Lomax, rescaling its density and dominating by (1-cos y)y^(-1-alpha) gives the stated positive gamma factor for 1<alpha<2. At omega=0 the equilibrium residual survival is (1+s/ell)^(1-alpha); the integrated correlation then gives the stated t^(3-alpha) law with the beta-integral prefactor. This last asymptotic is for equilibrium preparation, not a claim of the same prefactor after a fresh start.

## originality

PASS

The Poisson diffusivity, equilibrium residual-life formula, classical straight Lévy-walk behavior and general invariance-principle machinery are prior. The supported distinct increment is the fixed-curvature functional Gaussian result with its exact diffusivity for arbitrary independent finite-mean renewal durations under uniform refresh, including degeneracy, and the quantitative singular diffusivity as curvature vanishes for Lomax flights. The zero-curvature equilibrium MSD is a classical-renewal comparison, not standalone novelty.

## value

PASS

A finite-mean heavy-tailed flight clock can generate either superdiffusion or a finite-variance Brownian limit in essentially the same continuous trajectory model. Full circular flight closure, not a change of renewal law, accounts for the difference and for the arithmetic degeneracy. The exact diffusivity and power-law blowup quantify why convergence at fixed curvature cannot be interpreted as a regular zero-curvature limit.
