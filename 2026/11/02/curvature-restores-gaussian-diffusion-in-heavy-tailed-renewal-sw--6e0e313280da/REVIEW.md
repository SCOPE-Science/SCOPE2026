# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Every nonzero-curvature renewal increment is bounded by the geometric diameter and isotropic due to the independent uniform heading. An iid-vector functional central limit theorem and the finite-mean renewal strong law, with negligible initial/final arcs, give the asserted Brownian covariance. The equilibrium covariance is the probability of an unbroken residual flight times the cosine phase; this proves the exact mean-square identity. The Pareto asymptotics follow from a dominated-convergence cosine integral and an exact beta integral. Special support laws permitting zero diffusivity are included as a degenerate boundary. The supplied finite tests reproduce, but do not establish, the infinite-time theorem.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. Poisson chiral swimmers (Olsen--Löwen), straight long-flight Lévy walks (Magdziarz et al.), continuous stochastic-curvature swimmers (Rutenberg et al.) and trajectory inference under curvature noise (Tromer et al.) do not, in their inspected forms, give the combined exact finite-mean renewal Gaussian law and singular weak-curvature Pareto coefficient. A later experiment by Raghavendra et al. runs the two modes separately. Generic regeneration and Green--Kubo tools are standard and credited, not claimed novel. The publisher version of one broad classical Lévy-walk limit paper could not be fully inspected; potential uncatalogued broader treatments remain a priority risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The result identifies a sharp qualitative change in transport under an arbitrarily small deterministic angular drift: a renewal process with infinite interarrival-time variance and superlinear mean square at zero curvature becomes diffusive in the path-level Gaussian sense for every fixed nonzero curvature. The exact coefficient and the singular exponent quantify how curvature suppresses long-flight dispersion and are natural observables for intermittent chiral swimmers. This is not a mere recomputation of a known exponential-clock formula.

## Scope and limits
The initial renewal environment is stationary. Only iid positive finite-mean intervals and independent uniform heading refreshes are treated. No uniform simultaneous small-curvature/long-time limit, claims about correlated tumbles, rotational noise or infinite-mean intervals, or independent scientific validation are made.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
