Over every algebraically closed field of characteristic two, the singular scheme of the elementary-symmetric cubic in \(n=4m\) variables is a complete intersection of \(n-1\) quadrics consisting of exactly \(2^{n-2}\) disjoint length-two curvilinear points. Their supports are precisely the zero-one projective points of Hamming weight congruent to \(1\) modulo \(4\); the full primary decomposition and Hilbert series are explicit.
