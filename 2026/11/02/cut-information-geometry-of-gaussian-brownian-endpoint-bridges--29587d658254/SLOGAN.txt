For the complete Brownian-bridge-plus-Gaussian-endpoints family, the exact conditional mutual information between full past and future paths across a cut is an explicit scalar Gaussian expression, and its unique most-informative cut lies at \(t_*=\sqrt a/(\sqrt a+\sqrt b)\), independently of endpoint correlation. The information vanishes exactly for Markov members, remains positive and finite for nonsingular non-Markov members, and is infinite for singular non-Markov endpoint couplings.
