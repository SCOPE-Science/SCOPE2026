For Hou's characteristic-two permutation trinomial \(F_t(x)=x+t x^q+x^{2q-1}\), every cycle length is determined by the multiplicative order of a norm-one trace scalar. The number of admissible parameters exhibiting two-cycles is exactly \((q-1-\mathcal K_q)/4\), where \(\mathcal K_q\) is a binary Kloosterman sum; such parameters exist for every \(q\ge8\).
