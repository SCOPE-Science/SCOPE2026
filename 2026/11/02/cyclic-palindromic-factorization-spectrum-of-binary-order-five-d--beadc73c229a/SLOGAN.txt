Across all 2,048 binary de Bruijn cycles of order five, the smallest number of contiguous palindrome blocks attainable after choosing a circular cut ranges sharply from four to nine, with exact frequencies 48, 534, 942, 416, 106 and 2. The two hardest cycles are exchanged by reversal or bit complementation; the complete 65,536-root and minimum–maximum spectra are also determined.
