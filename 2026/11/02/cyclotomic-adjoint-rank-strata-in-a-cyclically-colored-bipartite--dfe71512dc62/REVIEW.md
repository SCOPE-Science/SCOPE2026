# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact images of adjoints are the cyclic-pencil image plus two cyclic shifts of one input vector. The proof treats zero, nonzero unit, and nonunit pencils separately and counts one-dimensional cyclic eigenlines even when characteristic divides the cycle length. Its five ranks do not coincide for \(n\ge4\), the coefficients are nonnegative, and summing them yields the full Lie-algebra size. Direct finite-field elimination checks eight complete noncentral configurations, including the extension field \(\mathbb F_4\). The printed proof, not the finite experiment, establishes all quantifiers.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected literature

Godoy Molina–Lagos treat differently labeled complete bipartite Lie algebras and **graded derivations**, not this intermediate proper-labeling adjoint rank distribution; Rossmann's polynomial class-size theorem assumes distinct, independent labels per edge; Chakrabarti–Mainkar–Swiatlowski study automorphisms on cyclically colored complete (not bipartite) graphs; Payne–Schroeder's uniform constructions require regular underlying graphs. Published findings searched under graph-Lie ranks, colored bipartite pencils, and cyclotomic nonpolynomial class strata yielded no stronger matching statement. This does not establish global priority: older general pencil-enumeration results may imply the formula after translation.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The adjoint-rank enumerator determines the centralizer-dimension distribution and element commuting probability of an explicit infinite family of class-two Lie algebras. This family is the natural intermediate coloring between one central label and a distinct label on every edge, already discussed for complete bipartite examples by Godoy Molina–Lagos. Its arithmetic fluctuation exposes the precise obstruction to extending the distinct-label graphical-group polynomial theorem to repeated disjoint edge labels. The result is structural, not a disconnected finite sample or a routine numerical improvement.

## Closest literature and limits

The independent-edge result of Rossmann is the strongest close comparator; its independent central-edge coordinates are collapsed in this construction and cannot imply the nonpolynomial counts. The complete-bipartite derivation results of Godoy Molina–Lagos have a different parameter and invariant. Results about one-factorizations or regular uniform graphs provide background, but not the claimed nonregular rank enumeration. Polynomiality failure is for fixed \(n=4\) over varying prime powers; arbitrary colorings and arbitrary rings are not classified.
