For a cyclic proper matching coloring of \(K_{2,n}\), every adjoint rank in the associated finite-field Lie algebra is enumerated exactly, with the coefficients governed by \(\gcd(n,q-1)\). Already for \(n=4\), the rank-three count \(q^7(q-1)\gcd(4,q-1)\) is not a polynomial in field size, showing an arithmetic boundary to the polynomiality of distinct-edge graphical constructions.
