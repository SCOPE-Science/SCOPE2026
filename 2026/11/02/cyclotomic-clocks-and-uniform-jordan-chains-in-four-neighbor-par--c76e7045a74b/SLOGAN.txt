For four-neighbor XOR dynamics on every \(2^a\times(m2^b)\) torus with odd \(m\), the complete Jordan chains and all temporal orbit populations are controlled by one polynomial gcd over \(\mathbb F_2\). Nonzero fixed points first appear when \(3\mid m\), whereas primitive three-cycles appear precisely when \(5\mid m\); the exact transient clock is determined only by the dyadic circumferences.
