For the literal Klein plane quartic \(X^3Y+Y^3Z+Z^3X=0\) over every odd prime field of characteristic other than \(7\), all \(28\) bitangents are defined over \(\mathbb F_{p^{\operatorname{ord}_7(p)}}\), and their Frobenius cycle profile depends only on \(p\pmod7\): respectively \(1^{28}\), \(1^4 2^{12}\), \(1^1 3^9\), or \(1^1 3^1 6^4\). The characteristic-zero bitangent splitting field is exactly \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)\).
