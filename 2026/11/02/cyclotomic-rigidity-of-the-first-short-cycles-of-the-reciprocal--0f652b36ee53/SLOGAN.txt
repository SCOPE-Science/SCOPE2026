For the reciprocal rational map \(x\mapsto x+x^{-1}\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), the complete least-period-two and least-period-three orbit counts are explicit: in odd characteristic other than three, exactly two three-cycles occur if and only if \(q\equiv1\pmod9\), and in characteristic two exactly one occurs if and only if the extension degree is divisible by three. The characteristic-zero period-three polynomial has splitting field \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_9)\), explaining the congruence criterion.
