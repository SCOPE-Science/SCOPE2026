For a priced cyclic ladder, maximizing the weighted spanning-tree count over **all independent edge conductances** has a unique globally symmetric solution, with an exact scalar price rule and sharp rung-budget bounds. When the rung price grows linearly with the cycle length, the optimal rung-to-rail conductance ratio transitions from order one to order \(n^{-2}\), with an explicit hyperbolic-cotangent limiting law and first correction.
