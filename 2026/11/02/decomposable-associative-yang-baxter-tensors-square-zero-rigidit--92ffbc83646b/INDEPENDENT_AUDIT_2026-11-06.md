# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The tensor equation for U⊗V is exactly U²⊗V⊗V−U⊗VU⊗V+U⊗U⊗V²=0. Dual tensor-functional projections force U²=λU and V²=μV; comparing associativity of (VU)U with V(U²) forces λμ=0; remaining equation yields VU=λV+μU. Conversely these equations annul AYBE, so after normalization solutions split uniquely into nil-nil, left-idempotent and right-idempotent strata. The matrix-unit tensor action gives AYBE↔weight-zero RB on End(Mn), r²=0, β²=0 and rank β=rank(U)rank(V)≤n⌊n/2⌋ (attained by I⊗square-zero B). For Fq the number Q_j=[n j]_q[n−j j]_q |GL_j| of square-zero rank-j matrices and the kernel/image subspace choices in type N, plus unique idempotent representative and q^{s(n−s)} complementary choices in L/R, give the stated rank polynomial without double-counting. Independently checked the original full AYBE coefficient tensor equation for all nonzero pairs in M2(F2) and M2(F3), finding 21 and 72 distinct nonzero tensors with N/LR counts (3,18) and (8,64), respectively.

## originality

PASS

Aguiar and later papers already establish correspondence between general AYBE solutions and RB operators and many example families. Gubarev (2024) classifies M3 characteristic not 2 or 3 quadratically closed R(1)≠0 up to equivalence, and M2 is previously classified; these must be credited as partial special-case coverage. The all-n arbitrary-field *decomposable* structure plus exact finite-field labeled operator-rank generating polynomial does not follow directly from those theorems.

## value

PASS

Complete all-field classification of a natural constant tensor-rank-one AYBE stratum and a finite-field rank-by-rank census of corresponding elementary two-sided Rota–Baxter maps, not merely a reproof of the general AYBE/RB correspondence. It separates tensor rank one from linear-operator rank one and realizes sharp rank n⌊n/2⌋; these exact enumerative invariants are independently usable within broader integrable-algebra classifications.
