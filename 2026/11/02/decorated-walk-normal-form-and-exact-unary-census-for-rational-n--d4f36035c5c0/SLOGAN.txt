Fixing rational truth constants and shifts to \(G_d\), unary non-expansive fuzzy propositional terms are exactly decorated nearest-neighbour walks of length \(d\) on \(\{0,\ldots,d\}\). Their exact number is \(\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}A_d^d\mathbf1\) for an explicit tridiagonal matrix, and it grows as \(d5^d\).
