In the ternary Hamming graph \(K_3^{\square n}\), the number of triangle-free vertex sets one below maximum is exactly \(6^n\) for every \(n\ge4\), and every such set has a unique maximum extension. The only exceptions are dimensions two and three, with respective counts \(45\) and \(270\) and exactly \(9\) and \(54\) nonextendible sets.
