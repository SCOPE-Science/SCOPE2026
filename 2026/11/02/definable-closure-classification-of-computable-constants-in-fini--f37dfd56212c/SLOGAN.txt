For every finite relational structure \(M\), the subsets of original constants that can remain computable in presentations of its Borel randomization are exactly the \(M\)-definably closed subsets, equivalently the fixed-point sets of subgroups of \(\operatorname{Aut}(M)\). Every possibility occurs even in a presentation computably isomorphic to the standard one; for a pure set of \(q\ge2\) elements, the only forbidden cardinality is \(q-1\).
