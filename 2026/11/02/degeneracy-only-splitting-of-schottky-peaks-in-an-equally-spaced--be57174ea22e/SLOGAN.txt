An equally spaced three-level canonical spectrum with degeneracies \(1,M^2,M^3\) has at least two strict heat-capacity maxima for every integer \(M\ge256\), despite having no large gap ratio. Their inverse temperatures scale as \(\log M\) and \(2\log M\), their heights diverge quadratically in \(\log M\), and a separating heat-capacity value vanishes as \(M\) grows.
