For fan graphs \(F_n=K_1\vee P_n\), \(n\ge4\), the full acyclic-orientation flip-degree enumerator has generating function \(2tz/(1-3tz+(1-t)t^2z^2)\). Exactly \(2F_{2n}\) orientations have minimum degree \(n\), and these form a connected induced subgraph—even though their fraction among all orientations decays exponentially.
