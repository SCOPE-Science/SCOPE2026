For synchronous three-color cyclic cellular automata on the line graph of any finite tree, the exact number of synchronizing initial colorings is \(3\prod_v(2^{\deg(v)}-1)\), sharply minimized by stars and maximized by paths at fixed order. For unicyclic base graphs, a one-variable Laurent constant term gives an exact polynomial-arithmetic census determined only by cycle and off-cycle degree multisets; subdividing any bridge leaves the synchronization probability unchanged.
