For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), the cardinality polynomials for induced vertex sets of uniformly even or uniformly odd degree factor into two parity-constrained binary-matrix enumerators. In the complete prisms \(K_2\square K_n\), elementary closed forms reveal a sharp residue-class obstruction: above cardinality \(n\), even-degree sets occur only at multiples of four, while odd-degree sets occur only at sizes congruent to two modulo four, with every nonzero coefficient equal to a binomial coefficient.
