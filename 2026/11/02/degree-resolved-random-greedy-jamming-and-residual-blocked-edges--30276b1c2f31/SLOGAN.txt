The finite-order degree-resolved random-greedy independent-set law on a uniform labeled tree is encoded by an exact bivariate rooted-tree generating function, not merely the known total occupancy distribution. It yields a residual blocked-edge expectation of \(\frac{1-\log2}{2}n-\frac9{16}+\frac{\log2}{8}+O(n^{-1})\) and a strictly degree-decreasing acceptance profile.
