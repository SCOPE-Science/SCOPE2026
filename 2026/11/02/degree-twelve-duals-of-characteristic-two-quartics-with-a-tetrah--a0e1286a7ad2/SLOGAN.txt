For every characteristic-two quartic \(x_1x_2x_3x_4+Q(x)^2=0\) with all four diagonal coefficients of \(Q\) nonzero, its projective dual is an irreducible degree-twelve hypersurface given explicitly by a cubic Cremona substitution. The dual has exactly six coordinate lines as its reduced singular locus, with generic line multiplicity four and coordinate-vertex multiplicity eight, uniformly across all cross-term specializations.
