The reduced rank-two quasisymmetric Grassmannian has exactly \(\binom{n-1}{4}\) extra independent quadratic equations beyond the usual Plücker and forbidden-Johnson-edge quadrics, namely \(p_{ab}p_{cd}=0\) for \(2\le a<b<c<d\le n\). For all \(n\ge4\), its homogeneous quadratic ideal has dimension \(2\binom n4\); the first additional equation occurs at \(n=5\).
