In a two-island biased-assimilation model, a community whose size advantage reaches the homophily factor eventually overturns *any* strictly complementary opposing starting opinions, while moderately balanced communities retain a polarized basin. Exactly at the demographic takeover threshold, the time needed to raise an initially tiny minority opinion \(\delta\) to any fixed level has the universal leading form \(1/(2\delta)\), with only a logarithmic correction.
