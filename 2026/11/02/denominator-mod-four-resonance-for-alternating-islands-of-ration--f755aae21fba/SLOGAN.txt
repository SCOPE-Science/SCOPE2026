For rational piecewise rotations with even denominator \(q\), the unique alternating two-cycle sits inside two congruent regular islands whose side count jumps arithmetically: \(q\) if \(4\mid q\), but only \(q/2\) if \(q\equiv2\pmod4\). Their exact area and pointwise periods follow from half-plane geometry; the \(q=10\) case yields two regular pentagons filled, away from their centers, with least-period-ten points.
