When a Johnson exchange walk observes all but \(k\) of \(n\) labels at every step, the time to see every \(s\)-subset converges to the sum of \(s\) independent complete coupon-collector times on \(k\) types, with explicit total-variation error \(O_{k,s}(1/n)\). If \(k\to\infty\) with \(k^2(\log k)^2=o(n)\), its centered, scaled fluctuations converge to a sum of \(s\) independent Gumbels (not a single Gumbel for \(s\ge2\)).
