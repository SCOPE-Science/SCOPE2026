Every sequential self-inclusive binary-parity sweep on the dense graph \(K_n\) with one edge deleted has exactly the same periodic-orbit census as some Rule 150 sweep on the sparse cycle \(C_n\). The two graphs nevertheless have sharply different scan-frequency laws: the missing-edge cyclic gap is uniform over its \(n-1\) possibilities, whereas the cycle scan classes are Eulerian; the dense recurrence order and all primitive orbit counts follow explicit gcd formulas.
