Tengely and Ulas's restricted-partition equality conjecture holds for every pair \(3\le a<b\) with nonsquare \(ab\): there are infinitely many positive solutions of \(P_{\{1,2,a\}}(X)=P_{\{1,2,b\}}(Y)\). A congruence-preserving Pell action produces these solutions explicitly, thereby settling the conjecture for a density-one family of parameter pairs.
