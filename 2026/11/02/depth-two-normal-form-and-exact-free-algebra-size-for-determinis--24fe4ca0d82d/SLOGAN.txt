For Kripke frames with at most one successor per world and weak transitivity, every pointed \(k\)-variable modal theory is one of exactly \(3\cdot4^k\) depth-two types. Every subset of these types is definable, so the free modal algebra has precisely \(2^{3\cdot4^k}\) elements; depth two and a universal model of \(3\cdot4^k\) worlds are both sharp.
