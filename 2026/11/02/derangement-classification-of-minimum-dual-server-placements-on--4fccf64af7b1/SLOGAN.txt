All minimum singleton two-colour rainbow-dominating placements on the square rook graph \(K_n\square K_n\) consist of complementary monochromatic bars and two disjoint perfect matchings on the residual square. Their exact number with named colours is \(n^2\sum_{u=0}^{n-2}(2-\mathbf 1_{u=n-2})\binom{n-1}{u}^2u!\,!u\), a derangement census beyond the previously known minimum weight \(2n-2\).
