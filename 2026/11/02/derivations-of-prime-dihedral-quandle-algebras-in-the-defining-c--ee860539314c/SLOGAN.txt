For every odd prime \(p\), derivations of the characteristic-\(p\) dihedral quandle algebra are exactly the truncated first-order operators \(a(u)+b(u)\partial_u\) with \(a,b\) supported in degrees congruent to \(1\) modulo \(\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\). Their full Lie bracket is explicit and their dimension is \(2(p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\), giving exact finite-field derivation counts and arithmetic dimension jumps.
