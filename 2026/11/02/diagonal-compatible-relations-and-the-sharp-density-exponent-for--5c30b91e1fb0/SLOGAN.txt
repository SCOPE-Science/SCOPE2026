Every compatible reflexive relation on the two-level Clifford monoid \(E_2\times G\) is determined by a nested pair of normal subgroups and two independent optional intermediate normal subgroups. This gives an exact DSC coefficient for every finite \(G\), and for \(G=(C_p)^d\) it decays as \(p^{-d^2/6+o(d^2)}\) at fixed \(p\).
