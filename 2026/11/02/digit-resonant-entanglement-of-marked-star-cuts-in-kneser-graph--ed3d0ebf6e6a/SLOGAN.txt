For pure prime-dimensional graph states on Kneser graphs, the entanglement spectrum across the union of \(s\) marked-point stars is exactly flat, with a rank determined by simultaneous subset-incidence blocks. When \(s<k\), the leading loss from the characteristic-independent rank is sharply activated precisely when a prime power exceeding \(s\) divides \(n-2k+1\); when \(s=k\), that loss disappears in every prime characteristic.
