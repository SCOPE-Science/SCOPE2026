The rational fourth and fifth homology of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_8\times K_3)\) split into distinct explicit \(D_8\times S_3\)-modules: an odd rotation-reflection/color sign line and a three-dimensional odd-rotation module \(\rho\boxtimes(\operatorname{sgn}\oplus U)\). Its full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic although \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_8\times K_3)\simeq S^4\vee\bigvee^3S^5\).
