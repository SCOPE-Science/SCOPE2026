The nearest indexed mathematical results concern wheel **discrete-Morse complexes** (a different space) or unordered two-particle spaces of individual dodecahedral, cuboctahedral and Petersen graphs. Accessible historical research claims include other graph configuration families but no wheel dihedral theorem. Potential implicit equivalence to a naturalized generic graph-braid presentation or unindexed source remains a real bibliographic risk; negative searches do not prove global uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The entire \(D_k\)-module across an infinite, canonical 3-connected planar family is a meaningful refinement of existing plain first Betti numbers. The rotation/reflection distinction gives a structurally motivated answer for symmetry-reduced two-particle motions: the rotational quotient has rational first Betti number \(2\) but the dihedral quotient has zero; two types of even-rim reflections act with traces \(-1\) and \(-3\). These features cannot be read off from a nonequivariant rank and are useful for equivariant graph-braid topology and quotient configuration spaces. The statement is not an arbitrary fixed small computation or mere substitution in a known parameterized formula.
