For unordered configurations of two particles on every wheel graph with rim size \(k\ge5\), the complete rational dihedral first-homology representation is \(\varepsilon\otimes(\mathbb Q\oplus\mathbb Q[E(C_k)])\), with all higher homology zero. Rotational quotients retain two independent rational one-cycles, while the full dihedral quotient is rationally acyclic.
