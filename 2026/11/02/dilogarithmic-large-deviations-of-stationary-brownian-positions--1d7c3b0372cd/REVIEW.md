# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
The Brownian increment over an independent exponential reset interval has a symmetric Laplace law; geometric contraction produces an exact stationary perpetuity and finite exponential moments precisely on the stated tilt domain. Locally uniform Riemann-sum limits give the dilogarithm and its derivatives. Chernoff exponential tightness, compact upper covers and directly tilted lower bounds establish the full LDP without invoking a transform theorem at a problematic domain edge. Legendre duality, local series inversion and the endpoint dilogarithm expansion supply the two geometric limits. All 19 independently implemented numerical consistency checks passed; the infinite-parameter conclusions rely on analytic proof rather than the checks.

## Originality: PASS, with publication-priority risk
The exact stationary characteristic product was published by Di Bello and collaborators, and Biroli and collaborators established fixed-factor stationary density and tail behavior. Olsen and Gupta established the weak-reset Gaussian limit; Olsen and Löwen give a related cumulant formalism with refractory periods. Those established ingredients are expressly excluded from the novelty claim. The evaluated full weak-contraction stationary-position LDP, its dilogarithmic Legendre rate and limiting nonlinear geometry were not located in the inspected primary full texts or targeted research-record searches. The closest retrieved weighted-Laplace and polylogarithmic records concern other state variables or finite-dimensional sums. A broader general geometric-sum LDP may imply this result; publication priority is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The rate characterizes rare excursions at a scale where the known Gaussian approximation loses information. Its exact central-to-linear transition, universal intercept, strict convexity and sign-independent formulation supply mathematically and physically motivated quantitative tail information for partial Brownian resetting. The claim does not extend to trajectory costs, refractory phases or arbitrary random resetting strengths.
