Under weak partial Brownian resetting, the stationary position has a universal dilogarithmic large-deviation law with rate \(I(y)=\sup_{|\theta|<1}\{\theta y-\operatorname{Li}_2(\theta^2)\}\). Its quadratic small-deviation regime crosses over to \(I(y)=|y|-\pi^2/6+o(1)\), revealing the exponential rare-event cost invisible to the already-known Gaussian bulk limit.
