For any critically sampled rectangular cyclic Gabor system generated by a nonnegative unit window, being within \(\delta\le1/10\) of Parseval in frame-operator norm forces the window within \(4\delta/3\) in \(\ell^2\) of a flat support transversal, uniformly in dimension. Exact Parseval windows are precisely those transversals, and two-tap perturbations show that the linear error order is sharp.
