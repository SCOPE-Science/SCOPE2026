For radial Itô diffusion with diffusivity \(|x|^{-\alpha}\) and resets to zero, the unique stationary spatial density switches from strictly decreasing to a single off-origin radial peak exactly when \(\alpha>d-2\). This same threshold determines the sign of the point-anchored resetting-information divergence, with a logarithm-of-logarithm transition in the planar Brownian endpoint.
