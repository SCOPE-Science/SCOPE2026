For every finite quiver in characteristic two, the reduced locus of units satisfying \(g^2=1\) in the algebra group \(1+J\) of \(kQ/J^3\) form a coordinate-subspace arrangement with components indexed by maximal independent sets of an arrow-conflict graph; the number of these units over \(\mathbb F_q\) is \(q^{p_2}I_{\Gamma_Q}(q-1)\). Its largest reduced component has dimension \(p_2+\operatorname{MaxDiCut}(Q)\), making dimension computation NP-hard even though the defining equations are monomials.
