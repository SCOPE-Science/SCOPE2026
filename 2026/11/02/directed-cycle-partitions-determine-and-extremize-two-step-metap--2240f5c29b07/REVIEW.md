# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The update rule is aligned with Kleinhans–Jonsson's exact ordered extinction–recolonization mechanism. Conditioning on the surviving state of each initially occupied patch gives the two-step event \(P_2=\mathbb E[e^{\sum_i X_i}]\). A two-state transfer matrix has trace \(e(2-e)\), determinant \(ce^2(1-e)^2\), and two distinct positive roots throughout the admissible parameter domain. Hence disjoint directed cycles contribute independent trace-of-power factors. Strict splitting, monotone convexity of \(\log(1+\rho^x)\), and the explicitly stated residue operations prove all extremizers and equality cases. Polynomial lowest-degree peeling proves response-curve reconstruction. Exact-rational independent coin enumeration and exhaustive small-partition comparisons replay successfully (12,767 assertions). Finite checks were not used as an infinite proof. A remaining mathematical risk is only misapplication to update orders or network classes outside the theorem's precise scope.

## Value — PASS

Directed cycles, asymmetry and patch-network persistence are established ecological questions. The exact theorem pinpoints **the earliest topology-sensitive update**, gives a complete structure-to-risk ordering (including within a fixed symmetric-edge count), and supplies a concrete pairwise-order obstruction to using network degree, reciprocity fraction and spectral radius alone. The recovery theorem explains what information a controlled colonization-response curve adds. These are exact natural network invariants for a standard finite-state ecological model, rather than merely a renamed transfer-matrix identity or a freely chosen numerical slice. The absolute differences in the eight-patch example are small, and the work is not an empirical management forecast.
