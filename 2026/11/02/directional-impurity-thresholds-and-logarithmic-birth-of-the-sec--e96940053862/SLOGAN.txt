For two equal positive onsite impurities in an anisotropic square tight-binding lattice, the second bound state appears at a direction-sensitive resistance threshold and rises above the band as the excess coupling divided by its logarithm. Its exact leading coefficient is determined by the anisotropically weighted squared defect separation; for hoppings \((3,1)\), nearest horizontal and vertical thresholds are \(9\) and \(6\).
