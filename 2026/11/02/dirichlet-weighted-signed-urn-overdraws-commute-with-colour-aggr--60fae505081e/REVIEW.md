# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For each finite colour set the strictly positive Dirichlet Gram matrix makes the dual basis uniquely defined. Degree elevation proves the hypergeometric match for every non-overdraw size. Independent Dirichlet aggregation plus the closure of conditional monomial expectations within degree \(m\) proves the signed naturality identity for arbitrary draw degree \(k\). The first counterexample follows from exact fourth-degree beta-orthogonal polynomial identities and an explicit five-coordinate signed mass vector. The included exact-rational checker passed 2,101 comparisons and is not used in place of the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with priority limits

Jacobs and Stein, arXiv:2312.12453, construct uniform-reference signed overdrawing and separately tabulate three- and two-colour cases. Their text does not establish colour-aggregation compatibility, use nonuniform Dirichlet weights for the dual, or present the preservation mechanism. General signed finite exchangeability in Benavoli–Facchini–Zaffalon, arXiv:2306.03869, does not provide this concrete all-degree naturality theorem. Prior classical work on dual Bernstein bases and positive Dirichlet marginalization remains relevant, and the Allen–Kirby matrix-inversion paper was checked from bibliographic material rather than full text. These are explicit residual priority risks, not evidence of independent verification.

## Value — PASS

Consistency under merging categories is fundamental for a law purported to describe coloured draws. The uniform-reference extension is already incompatible at the smallest possible full-support overdrawing example; the proposed weighted construction repairs that issue for all positive urns, all merges and all draw sizes, while retaining the pre-existing correct hypergeometric laws. The result supplies a motivated structural property and a precise boundary, not merely a numerical mismatch.

## Scope and remaining uncertainty

The construction is a family of finite signed measures; it is not a physical stochastic sampling rule beyond capacity. Intrinsic weighting only covers urns with positive counts for all represented colours, and no uniqueness among all natural signed extensions is claimed. Historical novelty and mathematical correctness have not undergone independent audit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
