A two-point, strictly convex stochastic gradient gives the Leimkuhler–Matthews sampler two disconnected almost-sure contraction windows, although its stationary variance exists only below a smaller explicit stepsize. Along a natural complete curve of exact-reset stepsizes, the stationary law is an explicit geometric Gaussian mixture with a sharp curvature-dependent moment cutoff \(p<\log 2/\log R\); the example \(R=8\) has cutoff \(1/3\).
