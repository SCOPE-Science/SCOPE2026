The origin-normalized analytic continuation of Euclidean boundary-distance Bergman kernels has a disconnected positivity set \([-3,\infty)\cup\bigcup_{j\ge2}[-(2j+1),-2j]\), with infinitely many separated positive bands. Its negative-integer endpoints are precisely finite-rank polynomial kernels, with rank \(\lfloor(k-1)/2\rfloor\) at \(t=-k\), \(k\ge3\).
