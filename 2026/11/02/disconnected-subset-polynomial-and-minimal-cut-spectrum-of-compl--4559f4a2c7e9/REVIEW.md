# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The four-case theorem follows by considering an isolated survivor or a selected induced edge in a component without isolated vertices. In the latter case, nonadjacency to both edge endpoints forces the other component into two cross-corners, and minimum degree one forces the exact four-cell rectangle. Both directions of the structural classification are proved. Disjoint-case enumeration gives the disconnected-set polynomial. Inclusion-maximal disconnected survivors are exactly full row-column crosses and four-cell rectangles, dual to the two inclusion-minimal vertex-cut families. The reliability identity includes the empty-set failure event separately. Direct graph-search checks of all 1,151,488 vertex subsets in five parameter pairs pass; they corroborate but do not prove the infinite claim.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Wang–Shan (arXiv:1105.1567, full relevant theorem and proof inspected) prove pre-existing minimum connectivity and neighborhood minimum-cut properties of Kronecker products. Soliemany–Ghasemi–Varmazyar (DOI:10.1051/ro/2022085, first publicly published 2022-08-18, relevant main results inspected) establish super-connectivity bounds and include a scalar result for a complete-graph factor of order three. These prior implications are credited. Neither inspected text supplies the complete disconnected-subset classification or coefficient-by-coefficient polynomial and all inclusion-minimal cuts. Anand et al. (DOI:10.2298/AADM111230001A) classify clique separators of direct products, not the nonclique cutsets here. The general residual reliability framework does not itself furnish this explicit product formula. OEIS A360873 concerns Cartesian rook graphs, not categorical products. Searches using direct/tensor/Kronecker product, vertex separators, complements of rook graphs, connected-induced-subset counts and reliability terminology yielded no dominating result. This is evidence of a gap, not proof of uniqueness. The inaccessible 2025 general reliability manuscript and unindexed alternative formulations remain risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete labelled disconnected-subset polynomial gives failure-state counts at every survivor cardinality and a closed node-reliability expression, substantially stronger than the known scalar vertex connectivity. Its maximal-survivor duality yields every minimal cut and proves an unbounded gap between the only two possible inclusion-minimal cut sizes. A probabilistic reliability measure and a qualitative two-tier separator structure are naturally meaningful within the studied graph-product family.

## Boundaries
The results assume factors of sizes at least three, vertex failures only, and uniformly independent survival for the reliability specialization. No theorem for factors of size two or independent publication-priority certification is asserted.
