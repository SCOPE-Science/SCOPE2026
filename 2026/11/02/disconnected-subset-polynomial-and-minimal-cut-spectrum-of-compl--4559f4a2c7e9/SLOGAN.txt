For every \(m,n\ge3\), all disconnected induced vertex sets of the direct product \(K_m\times K_n\) fall into four explicit row–column/rectangle forms, giving a closed residual node-reliability polynomial. Its inclusion-minimal vertex cuts have exactly the two sizes \((m-1)(n-1)\) and \(mn-4\), with counts \(mn\) and \(\binom m2\binom n2\) and an unbounded gap between the sizes.
