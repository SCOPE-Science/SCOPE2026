For a detuned three-site quantum chain with a lossy endpoint and pure dephasing confined to its middle site, the globally optimal dephasing strength switches from zero to positive at an explicit sharp detuning threshold. The onset is discontinuous for capture rate \(0<\kappa<32^{1/4}\), with an exact nonzero coexistence jump, but continuous for \(\kappa\geq32^{1/4}\); both regimes follow from an exact formula for mean first-capture time.
