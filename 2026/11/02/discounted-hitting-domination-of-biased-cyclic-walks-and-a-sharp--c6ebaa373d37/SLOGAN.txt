For a homogeneous biased nearest-neighbour walk on a labelled cycle, discounted hitting domination is governed exactly by a computable maximum source-free gap, giving both the minimum source count and the entire feasible-set cardinality polynomial. At fidelity \(\lambda=1/2\), the optimal four-gap bias \(p=(\sqrt{17}-1)/4\) improves its worst-vertex support from \(1/7\) to \((7-\sqrt{17})/16\), lowering the sharp source density at threshold \(1/6\) from \(1/3\) to \(1/4\).
