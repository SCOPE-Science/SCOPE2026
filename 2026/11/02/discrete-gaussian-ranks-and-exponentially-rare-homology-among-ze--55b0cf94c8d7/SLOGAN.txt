For a uniformly sampled zero-product pair \((A,B)\) of square matrices over a fixed finite field, the ranks are asymptotically complementary and their order-one imbalance has a parity-sensitive discrete-Gaussian law. The associated three-term complex is exact at its middle term with probability \(1-O_q(q^{-\lfloor n/2\rfloor})\), with an explicit sharp first-homology-defect coefficient.
