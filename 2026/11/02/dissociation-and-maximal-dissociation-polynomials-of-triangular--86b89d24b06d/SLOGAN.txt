Every dissociation set of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) is a packing of edges and two-edge paths in \(K_n\), and maximality permits exactly a full packing or a single unused point with only paths. This gives complete all-set and maximal-set polynomials, a gap-free maximal-cardinality spectrum \(\{\lceil n/2\rceil,\ldots,\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\}\), strictly log-concave nonzero maximal coefficients, and the isolated well-dissociated case \(n=7\) above order five.
