For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\), a six-step rational generating identity determines the complete dissociation-set polynomial, while maximum sets are exactly cyclic singleton-rung patterns avoiding three consecutive equal layers, plus two alternating full/empty-rung patterns at even order. Their number is \(L_n+3\mathbf1_{3\mid n}-1+2\mathbf1_{2\mid n}\), with the previously known cyclic-word enumeration duly credited.
