For symmetric conditional resets of a tight-binding quantum walker to sites \(\pm R\), the left–right memory decays with an exact positive exponential prefactor and a unique rate that strictly decreases as \(R\) increases. In the frequent-reset regime, that rate has the sharp law \(\Delta_R\sim\binom{2R}{R}\gamma^{2R}/(4^R\lambda^{2R-1})\), revealing an additional two powers of reset suppression for each step of separation.
