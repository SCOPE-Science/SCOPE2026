It exhibits a Möbius twist parity obstruction: \(m\equiv0\pmod4\) forces a one-vertex capacity deficit, while the number of extremal labelled configurations abruptly changes among quadratic, constant, and linear regimes. These are natural graph/coding invariants rather than arbitrary restricted parameter slices.
