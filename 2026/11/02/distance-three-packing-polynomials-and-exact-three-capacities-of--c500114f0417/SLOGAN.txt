For each \(m\ge5\), the entire distance-three packing polynomial of the labelled Möbius ladder \(M_{2m}\) has an explicit cyclic-gap binomial formula, with maximum packing number \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) except for the exact \(m\equiv0\pmod4\) deficit of one. The numbers of maximum packings are respectively \(m(m-2)\), \(4\), and \(2m\) for \(m\equiv0\pmod4\), \(m\equiv2\pmod4\), and odd \(m\); synchronous rotation turns every maximum packing into an optimal strong and direct distance-three tour.
