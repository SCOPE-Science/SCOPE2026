For every real coordinate-\(\ell_1\) edge space of the complete split graph \(K_a\vee\overline{K_b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\), two explicit but different invariant projections attain the exact cut-space and circulation-space projection constants. No single projection attains both minima; the orthogonal projection is optimal on the cut side only at \(a=2\) and on the circulation side only at \(a=4\).
