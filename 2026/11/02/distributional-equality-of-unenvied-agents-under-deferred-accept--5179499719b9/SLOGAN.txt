Under independent uniform school preferences, random serial dictatorship and student-proposing deferred acceptance have exactly the same **distribution**, not merely the same expected number, of students envied by nobody: both counts equal the singleton-coupon law at first complete collection. For any designated RSD picking positions \(1\le i_1<\cdots<i_r\le n\), the probability all are unenvied is exactly \(r!/\prod_{t=1}^r(n-i_t+t)\), which also yields exact fluctuations and the inherited exponential limit.
