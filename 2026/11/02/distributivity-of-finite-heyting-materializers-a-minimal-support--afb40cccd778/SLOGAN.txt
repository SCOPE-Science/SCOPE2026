For a finite Heyting algebra, the intrinsic lattice of consequents satisfying \(a\to b=\neg a\vee b\) is distributive exactly when every minimal external predecessor-support is principal; this admits a polynomial-time finite-poset test. Every Heyting algebra with at most eight elements has only intrinsically distributive materializers, whereas a unique nine-element algebra (up to isomorphism) admits a nondistributive one.
