A nonzero homomorphism between unweighted directed-cycle evolution algebras \(C_n(k)\to C_m(k)\) exists exactly when \(n\mid m\), and every such map is an embedding determined by a residue class and a \((2^m-1)\)st root of unity. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the target contains exactly \(\gcd(q-1,2^m-1)/\gcd(q-1,2^n-1)\) distinct embedded copies of \(C_n\), forming one automorphism orbit.
