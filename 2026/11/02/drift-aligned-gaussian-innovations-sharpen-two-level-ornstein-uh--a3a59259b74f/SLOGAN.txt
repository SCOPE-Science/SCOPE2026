For scalar Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Euler–Maruyama multilevel correction, replacing each coarse Brownian increment by a normalized drift-weighted combination of its two fine increments uniquely minimizes variance among independent-block, marginally correct couplings. The exact variance gain is positive at every admissible finite step; in the fixed-horizon small-step limit, the standard Brownian coupling has more than three times the variance whenever \(\lambda T>1\).
