For a smooth intersection of \(n\ge3\) quadrics containing a line, the unprojection's discriminant splits as its projective dual plus \(n-1\) copies of the hyperplane through the contracted point. The irreducible dual hypersurface has degree \((11n^2-31n+24)2^{n-1}+4n-8\), equal to \(124\) for the first K3 case.
