# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The sampling-to-positive-compositions bijection is exact for every admissible integer size. An explicit unit-reinforcement Pólya transition has uniform-composition marginals at each total and normalized-proportion martingale increments. Coordinatewise monotonicity and Jensen's inequality establish the two directions of stochastic ordering. Independent-copy covariance identities give their signed association consequences, with stated strictness boundaries. Conditioning on an internal gap of length one yields the closed-form covariance; a direct second-moment calculation yields the normalized quadratic formula. A standalone exhaustive checker passed 6,527 finite assertions without relying on previous computations. The proof, not enumeration, establishes unbounded claims.

## Originality

PASS with historical-priority qualification. Kochar and Korwar's Section 4 contains the exact joint sampling-spacing law and negative dependence of *pairs* of spacings, including their Theorems 4.1–4.4. Their result does not explicitly compare conditional gap profiles at different sample spans, does not give the normalized martingale coupling, and does not state the two simultaneous signed regression families. Flajolet, Gabarró and Pekari's urn literature supplies a classical probabilistic mechanism rather than a finite-sample span dependence theorem. Çalık's range-moment study does not involve internal-gap correlations. The main gap is a conditional dependence statement, not a new distribution for spacings or a new Pólya theorem. An earlier specialization may remain undiscovered.

## Value

PASS. The sample span, adjacent-pair count and normalized gap concentration are natural finite-population clustering measures. The structural finding shows an exact tension: larger span reduces the number of tightly clustered pairs but increases relative shape dispersion in convex order. The all-threshold and all-convex-function inequalities are stronger than a single sign calculation, and the closed-form adjacent-pair covariance gives an exact interpretable calibration under simple sampling without replacement. The result is valid for every finite population size, not only asymptotically.
