In a uniform finite-population sample, increasing the observed span makes every absolute internal gap larger in a common coupling, while simultaneously making the *normalized* gap vector larger in convex order. Thus span is negatively associated with all increasing short-gap profiles but positively associated with convex normalized-dispersion statistics; in particular, its covariance with the number of adjacent selected pairs is exactly \(-2(k-1)(N-k)/(N(k+1))\).
