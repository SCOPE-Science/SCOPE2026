For idealized duration-power-weighted transit surveys of eccentric exoplanets, response exponents \(\alpha\) and \(3-\alpha\) give exactly the same integrated yield, which is uniquely minimized at the white-noise Euclidean exponent \(\alpha=3/2\). Even there, averaging the detection weight before the duration power gives a strictly larger enhancement than applying that power to the mean duration, with a critical logarithmic singularity as eccentricity tends to one.
