For scalar sampled systems with control-linear Brownian actuator noise, the optimal mean-square contraction under any prescribed activation window depends only on the window's total duration, not its placement, and is attained by a unique exponentially shaped hold. Constant zero-order holds have a strictly smaller stabilizable noise-variance margin, by the exact factor \((a\tau/2)\coth(a\tau/2)\) for a constant unstable drift.
