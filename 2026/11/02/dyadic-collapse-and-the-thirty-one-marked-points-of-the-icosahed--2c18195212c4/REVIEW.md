## Value — PASS

The icosahedral 15-line reflection configuration is a highly symmetric and widely studied arrangement. Determining its entire exceptional-prime locus rather than sampling finite fields, its scheme-theoretic multiplicity collapse, and all 31 labeled collision paths gives a complete arithmetic boundary for this natural model. The eight-point limit has an independently reconstructed, mathematically motivated interpolation invariant, but the formerly known abstract \(7/3\) value alone is not presented as an independent achievement. The statement is stronger than a routine specialization of a precomputed table and gives useful data for further work on arithmetic line configurations and symbolic powers.
