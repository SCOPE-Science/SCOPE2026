For a natural integral model of the 15 icosahedral reflection lines, characteristic two is the sole exceptional reduction: twelve lines coalesce to one, while the original 31 marked intersections specialize to eight points with weights 1, 4, and 12 in a completely determined pattern. The reduced eight-point limit has symbolic initial degrees \(\lceil7m/3\rceil\); the abstract Waldschmidt value \(7/3\) was previously known for this collinearity type.
