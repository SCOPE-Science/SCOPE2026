For distinct primes \(p,q\), the equation \(\sigma^*(p^{2^t}q)=\sigma^{(e)}(p^{2^t}q)\) has exactly the two solutions \(20\) and \(45\); every dyadic exponent \(2^t\ge4\) is excluded by a uniform integer-remainder argument. This extends the previously published finite exponent classification to an infinite natural family.
