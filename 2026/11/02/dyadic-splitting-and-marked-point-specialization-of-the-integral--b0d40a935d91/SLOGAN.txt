For the published quadratic-integral model of Klein's twenty-one lines, every prime away from \(2\) preserves the classical \(21\)-quadruple/\(28\)-triple intersection lattice. The two split primes over \(2\) instead produce a six-line Fano deletion and a seven-line Fano arrangement, with exact nonreduced component multiplicities and an explicit specialization census of all \(49\) generic singular points.
