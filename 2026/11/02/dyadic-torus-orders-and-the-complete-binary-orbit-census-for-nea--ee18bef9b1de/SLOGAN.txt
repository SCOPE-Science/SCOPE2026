For nearest-neighbor coupled Arnol’d cat maps on a cycle of \(n=2^k\ge4\) sites, the exact permutation order on the \(2^s\)-grid torus is \(2^{k+s-2}\), while the number of fixed phases is \(2^{4s}\). On the binary torus, all primitive cycles have dyadic lengths up to \(n/2\), with a closed cycle census and an exact fraction \(1-2^{-n}\) of configurations attaining maximal period.
