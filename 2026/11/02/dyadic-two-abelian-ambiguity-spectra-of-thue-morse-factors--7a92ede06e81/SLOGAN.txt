At every dyadic length \(N=2^k\ge4\), the Thue–Morse word has exactly eight two-Abelian classes of distinct factors, but their sizes oscillate: for even \(k\) the class-size multiset is six copies of \((N-1)/3\) and two of \(N/2\), while for odd \(k\) it is four copies of \((N-2)/6\), two of \(N/2\), and two of \((2N-1)/3\). Explicit endpoint-and-adjacent-pair signatures identify every class, producing two different normalized ambiguity spectra.
