# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Counting all edge-defined connected subgraphs of a sunlet reduces to proper contiguous cycle paths, spanning paths and the full cycle. Inclusion–exclusion gives the exact number of cyclic arcs containing two prescribed attachment vertices; each other included leaf edge contributes a binary choice. The path count strictly decreases with cycle distance, both parity-specific sums yield \(L_n\), and the separate same-index/diagonal inequalities eliminate other possible minima. A standalone exhaustive original-definition check for \(3\le n\le8\) corroborates the proof but is not its substitute.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
De and Patra (arXiv:2610.19694v1, 14 October 2026) define the eccentric-subgraph index and treat extremal unicyclic families, whereas the present theorem gives all individual sunlet vertex minima and antipodal minimizing witnesses. Pandey and Patra (2021) study the sum of all connected subgraphs, a nonidentical invariant. Targeted searches for sunlet, cycle corona, paired connected-subgraph minima and the index under alternative names found no equivalent formula among inspected records. An earlier sunlet metric-mobility result counts resolving sets, not connected subgraphs. Historical priority is provisional: full coverage of older literature is not established, and the 2021 article was available only in abstract.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The sunlet is the canonical uniformly leaf-decorated cycle, providing a natural nonextremal test family for a recently introduced graph index. The result describes its complete vertex profile, eccentric-subgraph center and parity-dependent exact formula, plus its antipodal minimizing witnesses. It does not rely for value solely on computational novelty.

## Limits
This theorem does not evaluate arbitrary unicyclic graphs or longer attached branches, and independent historical and mathematical validation remain outstanding.
