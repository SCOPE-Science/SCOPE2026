For every sunlet \(S_n=C_n\circ K_1\), the eccentric-subgraph center is its entire cycle: each leaf has value \(L_n\), each cycle vertex has value \(2L_n\), and \(ES(S_n)=3nL_n\), where \(L_n=(2n-3)2^{n-2}+2^{n/2}\) for even \(n\) and \(L_n=(2n-3)2^{n-2}+3\cdot2^{(n-3)/2}\) for odd \(n\). The minimizing witnesses are exactly the leaves opposite, or as nearly opposite as possible to, the base vertex on the cycle.
