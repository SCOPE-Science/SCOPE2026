For pairwise-coprime \(a,b,c\ge2\) with \(c\) odd, the high-threshold denumerant sets for \((2a,2b,c)\) are exactly translates of \(\langle2,c\rangle\), with an effective onset and explicit Frobenius/genus formulas.
