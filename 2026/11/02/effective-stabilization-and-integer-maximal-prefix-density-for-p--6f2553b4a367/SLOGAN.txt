For every nonconstant periodic word with infinitely many palindromic prefixes, the minimum palindromic factorization length stabilizes modulo its least period \(p\) by index \(p(p-1)+1\). The ratio of running-maximum prefix positions to palindromic-prefix positions has an exact computable integer limit in \(\{1,\ldots,p-1\}\), and the upper endpoint is attained at every period.
