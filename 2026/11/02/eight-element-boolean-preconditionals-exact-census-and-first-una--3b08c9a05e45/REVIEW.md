# Review of the exact finite Boolean-preconditional census

## Correctness

**PASS.** Consequent-meet normality and conditional identity make each consequent coordinate a top-preserving meet homomorphism; finite principal-filter generators yield a unique relation on the atom carrier. The five axioms and modus ponens force the stated principal-filter restrictions, and the fifth axiom is equivalent to the explicitly quantified strong-density inclusion. Specializing to three atoms leaves precisely six bits and three four-way choices. All 4,096 choices have been compared with the original laws by the standalone checker. The 64 reduced weights independently total 1,235. Burnside's fixed-point counts yield 227, and the functional restriction yields 283, leaving 952 nonfunctional operations. No extrapolation beyond three atoms is asserted.

## Originality

**PASS within inspected evidence.** Holliday's published strong-density semantics and Chen's general preconditional axioms are prior ingredients, not claimed as new. Neither inspected source provides the three-atom unrestricted labelled and isomorphism enumeration or the functional/nonfunctional boundary. Previously published finite-chain work has different carrier order, and permutation-equivariant classifications concern a strict symmetry subfamily rather than the unrestricted class. A possible older enumeration under different terminology remains the principal historical-priority risk; a finite search cannot prove universal novelty.

## Value

**PASS.** The exact first-cardinality threshold where nondeterministic selection becomes unavoidable is motivated by the mathematical distinction between functional and relational conditional semantics. The result gives both a complete finite classification and a sharp structural boundary, including exact labelled and symmetry-quotiented counts, rather than a routine single numerical instance. The result does not imply an all-size enumeration.

## Closest literature and limitations

Holliday, *Preconditionals* (arXiv:2402.02296); Chen, *Fundamental Propositional Logic with Preconditional* (arXiv:2607.20221). The proof and checker address only the stated normal, modus-ponens class on finite Boolean carriers. The public numerical claim is limited to one, two, and three atoms, with the sharp eight-element threshold proved for all smaller finite Boolean algebras. Further expert comparison against older selection-frame census literature remains desirable.
