The antipodal quotients \(F_n\) of binary cubes have exactly eight first-order rank-four theories: six singleton dimensions \(2\) through \(7\), a shared class for dimensions \(8,9\), and one class for every dimension at least \(10\). The smallest largest representative has \(512\) vertices, and a uniform coordinate-splitting lemma proves stabilization independently of the finite exact-type computation.
