# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The polynomial \(u^6+u+1\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb F_2\). The six evaluated monomials at the displayed witness form the power basis by the identity \(t^8=t^3+t^2\). The verifier enumerates all 4096 affine pairs, all 63 nonzero binary conics and all 168 invertible binary 3-by-3 matrices, then verifies eight free orbits and Frobenius permutation \((0\ 5)(1\ 6\ 2\ 3\ 7\ 4)\). Every claimed count is finite and exhaustively checked; no experimental extrapolation is made.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The first primary source (Asgarli–Ghioca–Reichstein, arXiv:2310.10361) proves a general theorem for fields of more than two elements and leaves \(\mathbb F_2\) outside its theorem. Its Section 6 gives witness computations for fields of at least three elements and mentions binary experiments without a complete census. An earlier smooth-linear-system paper addresses a different question. Semantic record searches, exact indexed searches and recoverable preceding claims identified no covering arithmetic-projective classification. Unindexed finite-geometry tables might still contain equivalent data; this is not independent priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The smallest nontrivial missing binary planar-quadratic case of an explicit published theorem is resolved constructively, with all solutions classified under projective and arithmetic symmetry. The result is a naturally motivated complete finite classification, not an isolated unmotivated integer. It may inform future attempts on other excluded binary cases.

## Limitations
No assertion for general dimensions or degrees. Exact finite counts depend on the fully supplied verifier; only the existence witness also has an especially short symbolic proof. Independent validation has not been performed.
