Exactly 1344 points of \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_{64})\) avoid every conic defined over \(\mathbb F_2\). They form eight free \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\)-orbits, which Frobenius groups into two arithmetic-projective types of sizes 336 and 1008, giving a complete census in the first binary planar-quadratic case excluded from a general Galois-orbit theorem.
