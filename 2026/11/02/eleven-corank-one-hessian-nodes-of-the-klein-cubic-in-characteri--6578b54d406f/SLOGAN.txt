In characteristic three, the Hessian quintic of the Klein cubic has exactly eleven corank-one singularities, all ordinary nodes lying off the cubic, with explicit coordinates \([1:t:t^{-1}:t^3:t^{-5}]\) for \(t^{11}=1\). Their Frobenius splitting is \((1,5,5)\); the other Hessian rank strata are not classified here.
