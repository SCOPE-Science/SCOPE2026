# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The two-coordinate boundary Jacobian case, the at-least-three-coordinate singular lines, and the nonzero-coordinate reciprocal-gradient identity exhaust the algebraic-closure singular locus. Signed-coordinate solutions give ten old nodes plus exactly one new diagonal solution in characteristic three. At each signed point the polar quadratic form on the hyperplane has only the projective radial kernel, hence descends to a nondegenerate four-dimensional quadratic form and proves the ordinary double-point type. Exact field checks and symbolic Hessians in `verify.py` independently corroborate two primes, but the proof does not rely on finite enumeration for geometric completeness. Counting disjoint support strata proves the all-extension point formula.

## Originality
PASS, with historical-priority qualification. Hulek–Nieto–Sankaran and Hulek–Spandaw–van Geemen–van Straten report the twenty singular coordinate lines and ten signed nodes in their characteristic-zero geometric treatment, while their arithmetic arguments concern primes at least five. The first paper is a close structural source, not an implication of the extra characteristic-three diagonal node. Separate characteristic-two analysis has a different local type and does not contain this new characteristic-three result. Targeted searches did not reveal an equivalent bad-prime classification, although an unindexed paper or computational appendix could. Search absence is not a proof of global priority.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This is a complete singular-support classification at the first odd bad prime of a natural modular threefold, together with its exact all-extension arithmetic count and rigorous local type of an exceptional full-symmetry node. The change from ten to eleven isolated ordinary nodes matters for understanding the bad fiber of the standard integral model. The claim does not presume any unstated crepant resolution, cohomological consequences, or complete Jacobian scheme description.

## Limits and closest literature
The geometric models and ten ordinary nodes are classical; only their characteristic-three exception and the all-odd-prime singular-support comparison are at issue. The twenty singular lines have not been assigned transversal analytic types or a scheme-theoretic Jacobian structure. Full bibliographic priority is not established beyond the inspected sources and indexed searches.
