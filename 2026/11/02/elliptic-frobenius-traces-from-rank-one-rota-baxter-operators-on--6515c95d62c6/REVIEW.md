# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The image of the algebra multiplication is the two-dimensional annihilator; the algebra is commutative associative with cube zero. For a nonzero rank-one map \(R(x)=f(x)a\), the original Rota–Baxter identity is exactly the displayed bilinear equation in the proof. If the image line is central, this equation is automatic. Otherwise, the intersection of the image line with the annihilator is zero, forcing \(a^2=0\) and \(f(aJ)=0\) in odd characteristic. Smoothness of the two-quadric curve makes \(aJ=W\) at every nonzero square-zero noncentral image generator. This proves that there are exactly \(q^4-1\) compatible nonzero functionals per such image line. Each of the \(|C(k)|\) projective directions has \(q^2\) lifts, while central image lines contribute \((q+1)(q^6-1)\). There is no double count. The explicit elliptic quartic realization is smooth and yields the expected Weierstrass model. Standard genus-one Frobenius theory establishes the stated zeta-function recovery.

The independent-formula computational checker tests the *original* operator equation on each ordered basis pair for all \((3^6-1)^2=529984\) nonzero tensor presentations over \(\mathbb F_3\), and obtains the exact central and noncentral counts predicted. It checks eight additional prime-field/parameter combinations, including smoothness at rational points, and independently implements \(\mathbb F_9\) arithmetic for the extension point count. The finite tests are corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (priority provisional)

The algebraic central-extension framework and symmetric cocycles are established in Hegazi–Abdelwahab, *Construction of Nilpotent Jordan Algebras Over any Arbitrary Fields* (arXiv:1301.6349, first posted 2013-01-27). Its inspected theorem classifies nilpotent Jordan algebras only up to dimension four, and does not enumerate the Rota–Baxter operators of this six-dimensional genus-one family. Zheng–Guo–Rosenkranz (DOI 10.1080/00927872.2018.1552278) give order-two and order-three semigroup-algebra classifications, not this nilpotent algebra. Anghel's 2018 elliptic/Rota–Baxter presentation concerns spectral-parameter solutions on complex matrix algebras constructed from vector bundles, not rank-one operators over finite fields. A previously established rank-one tensor criterion on unrelated algebras is explicitly not treated as a new general lemma.

Published mathematical findings were searched using the algebra, operator, genus-one, Frobenius-trace and alias formulations. The nearest returned exterior-algebra Rota–Baxter count is characteristic two and dimension four; quadric/elliptic-geometry hits concern other invariants. Accessible previous operator-census claims were also checked. The combination of an exact smooth-pencil census with universal elliptic realization and recovery of all extension counts was not found in these results. A broader unpublished or differently worded rank-one enumeration could still subsume the theorem. Search non-detection is not proof of absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS

The exact number of nonzero rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter maps is a natural operator invariant of a finite-dimensional nilpotent algebra, not an arbitrary test statistic. The result determines it uniformly over a geometrically defined family and recovers an independently important invariant: the elliptic Frobenius trace and zeta function. Every elliptic curve in odd characteristic is realized, so the relation is not restricted to an accidental example. Over \(\mathbb F_5\), the curves \(y^2=x^3+\alpha x\) for \(\alpha=1,2,4\) yield respectively \(156144,124944,218544\) rank-one operators despite identical algebra dimensions and Loewy lengths. Thus the census detects genuine arithmetic variation.
