For every smooth genus-one intersection of two quadrics over an odd finite field, the nonzero rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on its associated six-dimensional nilpotent algebra number exactly \((q+1)(q^6-1)+q^2(q^4-1)|C(\mathbb F_q)|\). Every elliptic curve occurs, so a single operator count determines its Frobenius trace and all finite-extension counts.
