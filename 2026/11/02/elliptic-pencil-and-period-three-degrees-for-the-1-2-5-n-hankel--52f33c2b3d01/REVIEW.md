# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof specifies the complex rank-two Hankel variety, restricts to a nonempty generic open set, and shows that a three-observation section is birational to the explicitly smooth genus-one model. It computes both valuation orders of the fourth observed function at the two infinity points, using the exact recurrence polynomial and the first nonzero Taylor coefficient at the period-three exceptional indices. Finite points cannot be poles because the coordinate is polynomial on the affine model. The degree of the induced function-field extension is consequently the sum of the two pole orders. The accompanying exact-arithmetic elimination checks confirm seven consecutive projection dimensions at each of two parameter choices, but are not used as proof for all dimensions.
