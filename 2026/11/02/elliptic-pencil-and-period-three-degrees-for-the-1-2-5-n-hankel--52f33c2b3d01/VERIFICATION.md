# Verification record
The proof is a uniform, self-contained valuation calculation on the smooth projective completion of a quartic double cover. The standalone `verify.py` uses exact SymPy arithmetic, with \(b,c\) equal to \((2,3)\) and \((3,7)\); it checks the quartic discriminant, squarefreeness, and the degrees and squarefreeness of elimination resultants for \(6\le n\le12\). Execute with `python verify.py`.

These finite checks cannot establish an infinite theorem, non-existence of alternative descriptions, or independent mathematical validation. The displayed proof supplies the unlimited-\(n\) argument.
