For each characteristic-two Hilario–Stöhr quartic over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact distribution of rational line intersections is determined by its four-secant direction count. Away from \(t^3=1\), that count is \((\#E_t(\mathbb F_q)-6)/6\) for an explicit elliptic cubic; at \(t^3=1\) it has a separate closed formula.
