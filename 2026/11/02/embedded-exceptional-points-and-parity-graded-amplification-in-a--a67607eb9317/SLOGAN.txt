A homogeneous even ring with diametrically opposite balanced gain and loss has isolated **embedded exceptional points** at \(\gamma=2\sin(\pi k/m)\), where its spectrum remains wholly real but its propagator grows linearly. At \(\gamma=2\), the largest Jordan block is of order two or three according as the half-ring size \(m\) is odd or even, giving an exact linear-versus-quadratic amplification law.
