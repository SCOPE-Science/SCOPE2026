The line Fano scheme of the characteristic-\(p\) Hermitian cone of degree \(q+1\) is generically a \(q\)-fold nonreduced thickening of its smooth Hermitian base curve. It has exactly \(q^3+1\) embedded points, one at each \(\mathbf F_{q^2}\)-rational generator, where the completed local ring changes from \(k[[t,w]]/(w^q)\) to \(k[[t,w]]/(tw^q,w^{q+1})\).
