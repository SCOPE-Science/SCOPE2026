The non-nilpotent Leibniz family \(L_n=\mathbb F_q u\oplus\mathbb F_q v\oplus W\), with \([v,u]=u\), has a complete Gaussian-polynomial enumeration of endomorphism ranks and idempotent-retraction ranks. Its endomorphism set has exactly \(2q^{(n-1)^2}+(q-2)q^{(n-2)(n-1)}\) elements, and the rank of a random endomorphism follows the limiting square-matrix corank law in growing dimension.
