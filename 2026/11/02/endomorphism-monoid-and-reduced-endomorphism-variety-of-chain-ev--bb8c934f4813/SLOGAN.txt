For chain evolution algebras \(e_i^2=e_{i+1}\), the full unrestricted endomorphism variety over any field is a union of affine planes, with exact finite-field rank distribution \(1,q-1,q(q-1),\ldots,q(q-1)\). Every noninvertible algebra endomorphism is nilpotent under composition, and its exact nilpotency index is determined by the first nonzero coefficient of the generator image.
