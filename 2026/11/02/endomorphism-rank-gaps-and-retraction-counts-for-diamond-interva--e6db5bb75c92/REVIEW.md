# Scientific review: diamond interval-semiring endomorphisms

## Correctness — PASS

The proof classifies homomorphisms from the two operation tables. Flat addition forces distinct nonzero source elements to have distinct nonzero images, and multiplication forces the long interval either to stay nonzero (exactly simultaneous permutations) or collapse to zero (exactly injective-on-nonzero assignments into a zero-product subset). The polynomial \((1+z)(1+2z)^n\) enumerates such subsets, the falling factorial counts assignments, and fixed-image arguments determine retractions. A separate brute operation-table enumeration for the two smallest instances and a separate image-assignment check for the third reproduce all predicted rank counts. The infinite-family result relies on the explicit proof, not finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The interval-semiring construction and the bounded-height generating result belong to Gao–Zhou (arXiv:2610.06351). The equivalent directed-matching graph semirings and their three-nilpotent multiplication belong to Gao–Ren (arXiv:2506.23047); neither construction is claimed as new. Gao–Zhou's relevant Sections 3 and 5 provide variety-theoretic rather than endomorphism-rank statements. Gao–Ren's graph-semiring construction and adjacent variety results do not state the matching-family endomorphism spectrum, while targeted comparison searches under the graph and interval aliases did not identify a covering exact rank-count theorem. The originality assessment is limited by possible older general three-nilpotent semigroup endomorphism results implying this case; absence from searches is not proof of global priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The diamond family contains the canonical six-element generator for the bounded-height semiring variety \(\mathcal B_2\). A complete endomorphism classification for every number of branches determines all possible image sizes, the missing-rank interval, every rank multiplicity, all automorphisms, and all idempotent retractions. In particular, the canonical generator has a concrete rank-five obstruction and exactly 215 endomorphisms. This is a natural infinite structural classification of a recently studied algebra family, not an arbitrary isolated table entry. It does not generalize to arbitrary posets without further work.

## Closest literature and residual risks

The closest source is Gao–Zhou, *Equational Theories of Interval Semirings of Posets* (2026, arXiv:2610.06351). Gao–Ren, *The flat semirings with nilpotent multiplicative reducts* (arXiv:2506.23047), defines the equivalent directed-matching graph semirings. The correspondence between directed matching graphs and the diamond construction is acknowledged. A broader homomorphism theorem for graph semirings or three-nilpotent semigroups could subsume the result, despite no such covering theorem being located in the inspected materials. No independent validation is claimed.
