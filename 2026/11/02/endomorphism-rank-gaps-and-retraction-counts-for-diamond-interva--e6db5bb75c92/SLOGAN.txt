Diamond interval semirings have a complete endomorphism rank spectrum with a wide forbidden gap: on the \(2n+2\)-element semiring, the only ranks are \(1,\ldots,n+2\) and \(2n+2\). Their automorphism group is \(\mathfrak S_n\), and exactly \(3^n+1\) endomorphisms are idempotent; the canonical six-element example has exactly \(215\) endomorphisms.
