For the canonical \(n\)-dimensional chain evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), the entire endomorphism monoid has exactly \(q[1+(n-1)(q-1)]\) elements, with \(q(q-1)\) maps of every rank from two through \(n\). Its nonautomorphisms form a nilpotent ideal of sharp index \(n\), so the only idempotents are zero and identity, and only zero and units are regular.
