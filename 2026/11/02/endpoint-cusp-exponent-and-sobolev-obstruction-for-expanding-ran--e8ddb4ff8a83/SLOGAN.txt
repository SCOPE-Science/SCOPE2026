For two-full-branch expanding cusp maps with \(1/2<\alpha<1\), every stationary nonnegative \(BV\) density has a strictly positive explicit coefficient multiplying \(z^{(1-\alpha)/\alpha}\) at the cusp image. This forces an exact pointwise Hölder exponent and rules out \(W^{1,p}\) regularity for every \(p\ge\alpha/(2\alpha-1)\), for deterministic maps and finite random mixtures alike.
