For convex hulls of random points on a parabolic arc, fixing both endpoints changes the missing-area scale from \(n^{-1}\) with a Gamma limit to \(n^{-2}\) with independent, centered Gaussian fluctuations on scale \(n^{-5/2}\). An exact beta–Dirichlet product law gives every finite-sample area moment and explains the separation between random endpoint uncertainty and interior spacing noise.
