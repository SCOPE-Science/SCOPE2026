For a finite path with endpoint masses \(a,b>0\) and all interior masses tending equally to zero, the sharp Lipschitz gain of the uncentered weighted interval maximal operator tends to \((n-1)\max\{a,b\}/(a+b)\), with an explicit linear error bound. Equal masses instead give sharp gain \(1/2\), whereas unrestricted positive masses permit amplification arbitrarily close to the full path diameter \(n-1\).
