For a rotated qubit dephaser, every nonzero mixing rotation makes some finite repetition entanglement breaking, with an exact Chebyshev criterion at every iteration. In the weak-noise scaling \(\theta=c(1-\lambda)\), its scaled first breaking time has a sharp crossover through the exceptional point, and the shortest limiting time \(\log(1+\sqrt2)\) occurs at an infinite discrete family of resonant drive ratios.
