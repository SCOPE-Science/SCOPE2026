For a walk on \(K_{m,n}\) that resets to one fixed vertex, the entropy-maximizing reset probability has a sharp degree-imbalance crossover: it is asymptotic to \((m^3n)^{-1/4}\) at fixed \(n\), but scales as \(t_\rho/m\) when \(n/m\to\rho>0\). Both the stationary entropy formula and the leading improvement over the ordinary walk are explicit, including the necessary overlap between resetting and ordinary transitions.
