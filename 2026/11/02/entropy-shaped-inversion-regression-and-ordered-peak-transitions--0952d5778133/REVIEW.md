# Scientific self-review

**Correctness: PASS.** The finite-size claim follows from the signed full-binary-tree interpretation of the original inflation process. The inversion count is exactly the sum of sign indicators times the sizes of the left-right leaf pairs they separate. The unsigned Yule leaf-splitting tree has the random binary-search-tree split recursion, so priority conditioning and a finite harmonic integral determine every coefficient. Independent Bernoulli signs give the full conditional regression and peak covariance. Strict discrete concavity gives the ordered threshold classification, and harmonic asymptotics give the entropy profile. The original transition probabilities are independently enumerated with exact fractions by the packaged checker through seven leaves at two distinct biases, for 624 passed assertions.

**Originality: PASS (qualified).** The motivating work of Féray and Rivera-Lopez (arXiv:2306.04278, especially Proposition 1.7 and Lemma 4.8) already supplies the model and the signed-tree/total-descent facts. The new claim is not that the signs are independent, nor that the underlying trees are random BSTs. It is the position-specific harmonic conditional regression of global inversions against the *entire* descent word and the resulting strictly ordered peak-inversion covariance transitions. The 2024 uniform-separable-statistics study of Chen, Kitaev and Zhang uses a different probability measure and does not provide these identities. Closest research-record matches about uniform permutation inversion indicators, cycle counts and random-BST path lengths have different objects or observables and do not imply this regression. Direct historical priority remains uncertain because the full finite law from the motivating paper allows other, perhaps unpublished, summations.

**Value: PASS.** A global inversion statistic reacts very unequally to the location of a single adjacent descent: the exact sensitivity is logarithmic at an endpoint but linear, with binary-entropy shape, in the interior. That same nonuniformity imposes a complete and monotone family of exact covariance-sign transitions for natural interior-peak indicators. This gives a structural local/global dependence classification rather than a bare moment recomputation or an arbitrary finite case.

**Scientific limits and risks.** The theorem covers the recursive law only, for \(n\ge2\) and \(0<p<1\), and its peak statement is for \(2\le i\le n-1\). It does not establish full inversion distributions, higher conditional moments or independent validation. A more extensive historical search could uncover an equivalent formula derived from the earlier complete probability law.
