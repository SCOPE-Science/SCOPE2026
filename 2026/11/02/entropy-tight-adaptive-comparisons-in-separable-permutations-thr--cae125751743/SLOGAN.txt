The pairwise-position comparison class of separable permutations has \(\operatorname{VCdim}(\mathcal S_n)=n-1\) for all \(n\), while its exact Littlestone dimensions for \(n=1,\ldots,7\) are \(0,1,2,4,6,8,10\), each saturating the cardinality bound. A complete depth-ten certificate at seven positions gives \(\operatorname{Ldim}(\mathcal S_{7s})-\operatorname{VCdim}(\mathcal S_{7s})\ge3s+1\) for every \(s\ge1\).
