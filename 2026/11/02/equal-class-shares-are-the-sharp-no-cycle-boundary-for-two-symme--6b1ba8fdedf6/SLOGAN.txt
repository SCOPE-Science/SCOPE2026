For two populations that each obey the same symmetric increasing random-utility comparison link, exactly equal shares force a globally transitive pairwise-majority ranking by the sum of the two score vectors. Every unequal, nonzero split admits a strict three-alternative Condorcet cycle; the sharp boundary holds simultaneously for logistic Bradley–Terry–Luce and Gaussian Thurstone comparisons, and for every other link satisfying the stated symmetry and monotonicity assumptions.
