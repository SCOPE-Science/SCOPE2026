On the directed Hamiltonian tours of \(n\ge3\) labelled cities, the constant and the maximal number \(n(n-3)+1\) of independent arc-Walsh characters are obtained precisely by omitting a spanning tree of a crown graph. Every one of the \((n-1)n^{n-2}(n-2)^{n-1}\) such coordinate selections has **exactly the same Gram determinant under every strictly positive tour law**, and the uniform-law covariance spectrum has two explicit positive levels.
