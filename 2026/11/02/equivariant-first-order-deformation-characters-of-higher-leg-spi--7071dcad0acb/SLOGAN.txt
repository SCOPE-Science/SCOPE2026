For every higher-leg spider algebra with at least four legs, the complete coordinate-torus character of its embedded Hilbert tangent space and its abstract first-order deformation space has an explicit closed Laurent-polynomial formula. The Hilbert tangent excess above the smoothable-component dimension is exactly \(r(r-2)(r+1)/2\), independent of all leg lengths, and the abstract deformation dimension is \(d-1+r(r^2+r-4)/2\).
