The generalized Jacobian of each Ballico–Hefez curve is a product of nonsplit one-dimensional tori, but its entire \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\)-fixed subgroup scheme is always exactly \(\mu_2\), including the nonreduced case in characteristic two. The integral character module is a dihedrally induced signed permutation lattice; for the ten-node sextic in characteristic five its rational representation splits into irreducibles of dimensions six and four.
