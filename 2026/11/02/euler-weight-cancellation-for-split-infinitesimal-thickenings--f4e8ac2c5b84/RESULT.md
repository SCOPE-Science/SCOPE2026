# Euler-weight cancellation for split infinitesimal thickenings

## Finding

**Theorem.** Let \(Y\) be a smooth, connected, projective variety over \(\mathbb C\), let \(L\) be a line bundle on \(Y\), and let \(m\geq 2\) be an integer. Define the split infinitesimal thickening
\[
X_m=\operatorname{Spec}_Y\left(\bigoplus_{j=0}^{m-1}L^{-j}\right),
\]
where multiplication is the usual tensor multiplication when the sum of indices is less than \(m\), and zero otherwise. This is the order-\(m\) zero-section thickening inside the total space of \(L\).

The Hodge-completed *derived* Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequence
\[
E_1^{p,q}(X_m)=\mathbb H^q\bigl(X_m,\mathbf L\!\bigwedge^p\mathbb L_{X_m/\mathbb C}\bigr)
\;\Longrightarrow\;H^{p+q}(Y^{\mathrm{an}},\mathbb C)
\]
**degenerates at \(E_2\)**, and its full second page is canonically identified with the smooth reduction's first page:
\[
\boxed{E_2^{p,q}(X_m)\simeq H^q(Y,\Omega_Y^p)}
\quad\text{for all }p\geq0\text{ and }q\in\mathbb Z.
\]
The identification respects the natural pullback along the projection \(X_m\to Y\). All classes of positive fiber-scaling weight are killed by the first differential, independently of \(m\) and \(L\).

In particular, for every \(m\geq2\) the nonreduced degree-\(m\) multiple of a projective line, \(V(\ell^m)\subset\mathbb P^2_\mathbb C\), has exactly two nonzero second-page entries: \(E_2^{0,0}\cong\mathbb C\) and \(E_2^{1,1}\cong\mathbb C\). These are genuine nonreduced projective local-complete-intersection curves.

## Assumptions and scope

The ground field is \(\mathbb C\). The variety \(Y\) is smooth, projective, and connected; the line bundle is arbitrary, with no positivity condition. The thickening is **split** as an algebra over \(\mathcal O_Y\). The spectral sequence is the derived Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequence associated with the Hodge filtration on Hodge-completed derived de Rham cohomology, rather than the ordinary underived Kähler-de Rham complex. Since \(X_m\to Y\) is finite and the local defining ideal is generated by a power of one nonzerodivisor, \(X_m\) is projective and locally a hypersurface, hence local complete intersection. The underlying analytic topological space is \(Y^{\mathrm{an}}\).

## Proof

**1. A global nonnegative weight grading.** The scaling action of \(\mathbb G_m\) on fibers of \(L\) preserves the ideal defining \(X_m\). Its degree-zero algebra is \(\mathcal O_Y\), and the degree-\(j\) algebra is \(L^{-j}\) for \(0\le j<m\). The inclusion of the zero-weight part and the reduction retraction are compatible with the Hodge construction.

**2. A local differential-graded model.** On a smooth affine open \(U\subset Y\) trivializing \(L\), set \(R=\mathcal O_Y(U)\). The coordinate ring of the thickening is \(R[t]/(t^m)\). A semi-free differential-graded commutative resolution (cohomological grading) is
\[
P=(R[t,\eta],\partial),\qquad
|t|=0,\quad |\eta|=-1,\quad \partial\eta=t^m,
\]
where \(\eta\) is odd and hence exterior. The resolution is quasi-isomorphic to \(R[t]/(t^m)\). In the derived de Rham bicomplex \(\Omega^\bullet_{P/\mathbb C}\), the internal differential is induced by \(\partial\), while the horizontal differential is the universal de Rham differential \(d\). The vertical complex at horizontal degree \(p\) computes the derived exterior power \(\mathbf L\!\bigwedge^p\mathbb L_{X_m/\mathbb C}\).

Give \(t\) and \(dt\) weight \(1\); give \(\eta\) and \(d\eta\) weight \(m\); and give \(R\) and \(\Omega^\bullet_{R/\mathbb C}\) weight zero. All weights are nonnegative, and both differentials preserve weight. Coordinate changes \(t'=ut\), \(\eta'=u^m\eta\) with \(u\in\mathcal O_Y^\times(U\cap U')\) preserve the grading. The weight-zero part of the entire bicomplex is *exactly* the ordinary smooth de Rham bicomplex of \(Y\).

**3. Euler contraction cancels every positive weight at \(E_2\).** The degree-zero Euler vector field \(E\) on this semi-free model is characterized by
\[
E(t)=t,\qquad E(\eta)=m\eta,\qquad E(r)=0\;(r\in R).
\]
It commutes with the internal differential: \(\partial E(\eta)=m t^m=E\partial(\eta)\). Its contraction \(\iota_E\) on forms lowers the de Rham degree by one and commutes (with the appropriate graded sign) with the internal differential. Cartan's formula gives
\[
[d,\iota_E]=\mathcal L_E.
\]
On forms of homogeneous weight \(w\), the Lie derivative \(\mathcal L_E\) is multiplication by \(w\). Hence the first-page horizontal complex, including its global hypercohomology, has a contracting homotopy \(\iota_E/w\) in every weight \(w>0\). Indeed,
\[
(d_1\iota_E+\iota_Ed_1)/w=\mathrm{id}
\]
on the positive-weight summand. This homotopy is global because \(E\) is the infinitesimal generator of fiber scaling. Every local form is a finite sum of nonnegative homogeneous weights before Hodge completion, and the homotopy acts weightwise and continuously on the completed product of Hodge degrees; therefore the calculation survives derived global sections and Hodge completion. It follows that all positive-weight classes vanish at \(E_2\).

**4. The remaining second page and higher differentials.** The weight-zero summand is the ordinary Hodge-to-de Rham sequence for \(Y\): its first page is \(H^q(Y,\Omega_Y^p)\). Smooth projective Hodge degeneration over \(\mathbb C\) makes its \(d_1\) zero and every higher differential zero. All spectral-sequence differentials preserve weight, and the weight decomposition is a direct decomposition of the filtered complex. After Step 3 there is no positive-weight term remaining to support or receive a higher differential. Thus the displayed second-page identification holds, and all \(d_r\) with \(r\ge2\) vanish.

**5. Explicit projective multiple lines.** For a line \(Y=V(\ell)\simeq\mathbb P^1\subset\mathbb P^2\), projection from any point off \(Y\) retracts the scheme \(V(\ell^m)\) onto \(Y\). On the complement of that point, the ambient plane is the total space of \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(1)\); the order-\(m\) thickening is consequently of the form in the theorem with \(L=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(1)\). The nonzero smooth Hodge groups are \(H^0(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O)=\mathbb C\) and \(H^1(\mathbb P^1,\Omega^1)=\mathbb C\), yielding the asserted two entries.

## Verification

The local truncated-polynomial specialization admits an explicit independent check of the crucial \(d_1\) cancellation. Over \(\mathbb Q\), with \(A=\mathbb Q[t]/(t^m)\), the derived exterior power in every degree \(p\ge1\) has the two-term model, up to invertible rational factors,
\[
A\xrightarrow{\;m t^{m-1}\;}A
\]
placed in degrees \(-p\) and \(-p+1\). Its lower cohomology is the ideal \((t)\), and its upper cohomology is \(A/(t^{m-1})\). The horizontal map on these groups is represented by ordinary differentiation,
\[
(t)\longrightarrow A/(t^{m-1}),\qquad
t^j\longmapsto j t^{j-1},
\]
which is an isomorphism in characteristic zero for every \(m\ge2\). At \(p=0\), the same derivative on \(A\) has one-dimensional kernel, generated by constants. A standalone exact-rational checker named `artifacts/verify_local_e1.py` reconstructs these matrices and verifies rank identities for \(2\le m\le17\) and \(0\le p\le13\); it reports `VERIFY_OK 224 exact local E1 blocks checked`. Finite checks are diagnostics; Steps 1–4 are the uniform proof.

## Relationship to prior work

Yunfan He's *Hodge-to-de Rham degeneration and quasihomogeneous singularities of curves*, arXiv:2604.05825v1 (7 April 2026), proves an \(E_2\)-criterion for **integral** projective local-complete-intersection curves (Theorem 1.1). Section 4(1) specifically identifies **nonreduced** projective local-complete-intersection curves, including square-zero thickenings, as a further direction and asks whether nilpotents force higher differentials. The present result answers the nilpotent-forces-differentials question negatively for a substantial, geometrically canonical family and supplies a complete second page. It does not claim a criterion for all nonreduced curves.

Bhargav Bhatt's *Completions and derived de Rham cohomology*, arXiv:1207.6193v1 (26 July 2012), compares Hodge-completed derived and Hartshorne de Rham cohomology **after forgetting filtrations**. That comparison explains the abutment but does not by itself imply the specific \(E_2\)-page formula. The Euler contraction is the additional filtered calculation. A similar argument might appear implicitly in literature on equivariant derived de Rham theory; a definitive priority claim would require broader specialist review.

## Limitations

The splitting, positive fiber-weight action, and characteristic-zero division by nonzero weights are essential to this proof. No claim is made for arbitrary nonsplit ribbons, arbitrary nonreduced projective curves, positive characteristic, or nonproper smooth reductions. The theorem makes no claim that the derived \(E_1\) page equals the smooth \(E_1\) page: it generally contains infinitely many additional rows before \(d_1\) cancellation. The finite checker does not prove the global theorem. Independent verification and exhaustive historical-priority determination have not been performed.
