# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem treats precisely the split truncated symmetric algebra of the conormal line over a smooth connected projective complex variety. Locally the hypersurface is resolved by the semi-free cdga \(R[t,\eta]\) with \(\partial\eta=t^m\). Scaling supplies a globally defined Euler derivation commuting with this differential. Cartan's identity gives \(d_1\iota_E+\iota_Ed_1=w\) on weight \(w\); division by \(w>0\) contracts each positive-weight first-page complex. Weight zero is the de Rham complex of the smooth reduction and has \(E_1\)-degeneration by the smooth projective Hodge theorem. The weight splitting precludes later differentials. Global changes of trivialization preserve the Euler grading and homotopy. The checker reproduces exact local derived-exterior ranks, not the global infinite argument. No external independent proof review is claimed.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

He, arXiv:2604.05825v1, proves an \(E_2\)-criterion only for integral projective lci curves and explicitly asks about nonreduced lci curves, even square-zero thickenings, in Section 4(1). Bhatt, arXiv:1207.6193v1, establishes an unfiltered comparison of completed derived de Rham theories; this is not an \(E_2\)-criterion. No matching theorem was identified in actual published-record and research-record searches for graded nilpotent thickenings, Euler contraction, ribbons, or Hodge-to-de Rham \(E_2\)-degeneration. The contraction itself is classical, so an implicit formulation in earlier equivariant or filtered derived de Rham work remains a serious specific historical-priority risk. Unavailable older research history was not presented as checked.

## Value — PASS

The new formula computes the entire second page, not merely total Betti cohomology, for every order of a natural infinite family of projective nonreduced lci schemes. It settles a concrete boundary raised in a 2026 paper: nonreduced projective lci curves, including repeated projective lines, can retain \(E_2\)-degeneration despite a large extra derived \(E_1\) page. The mechanism distinguishes global positive-weight splitting from local quasihomogeneous singularities and identifies a useful structural criterion; it does not extrapolate to nonsplit thickenings.

## Limitations and closest literature

The setup requires a split nilpotent structure and characteristic zero. It gives no general criterion for all nonreduced lci curves. Historical priority is not independently established; the published source papers were inspected at the relevant statements rather than inferred from title searches alone. The exact-rational tests cover finite local parameter ranges only.
