For the split order-\(m\) infinitesimal thickening of any smooth projective complex variety inside a line bundle, every positive-weight derived-Hodge class dies under \(d_1\). The full \(E_2\)-page is \(H^q(Y,\Omega_Y^p)\), so the derived Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequence degenerates at \(E_2\), even for all projective multiple lines \(V(\ell^m)\subset\mathbb P^2\).
