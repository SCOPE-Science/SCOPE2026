# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The reduction identity \(E_{p+t}\equiv\Phi_p E_{t+1}\pmod p\) follows from the Eulerian recurrence and the base case \(E_p\equiv\Phi_p\). Coprime monic Hensel factors exist uniquely. Factorial valuation and Wilson's theorem give the exact Eisenstein constant-term unit \(F_{p,t}(1)/p\equiv-(t+1)^{-1}\). A principal-unit \((p-1)\)-st root then gives the stated Kummer generator. Kummer unit residues distinguish every field; standard tame extension theory proves exhaustion. The 34 small-index Hensel checks support rather than replace the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, with risk
Sharma–Straus (1974) establish relevant Eisenstein-type irreducibility for Euler–Frobenius determinants, including prime-index background, but not this blockwise local field-class realization. Cao–Pan (2006) treat Eulerian number congruences; Iijima et al. (2019) treat a different polynomial congruence modulo powers of the polynomial variable's shift. General Kummer theory provides the abstract fields but not their Eulerian indexing. An equivalent in unlocated specialist literature remains possible; no absolute uniqueness claim is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Every one of the \(p-1\) cyclic totally tame degree-\(p-1\) local extension types is realized exactly once in a single natural Eulerian prime block, with an explicit unit label \((t+1)^{-1}\). This identifies natural arithmetic structure of the full family, beyond isolated Eisenstein tests.

## Limitations and status
The theorem is local, not a claim of global irreducibility for the full Eulerian polynomials. Odd primes and one consecutive index block are the stated scope. Finite verification does not prove the infinite theorem. The reviewer has not independently validated the result.
