For every odd prime \(p\), the Eulerian polynomials \(E_p,\ldots,E_{2p-2}\) have distinguished degree-\(p-1\) local factors whose root fields realize, exactly once each, all cyclic totally tamely ramified degree-\(p-1\) extensions of \(\mathbb Q_p\). The field at index \(p+t\) is \(\mathbb Q_p(\sqrt[p-1]{p/(t+1)})\).
