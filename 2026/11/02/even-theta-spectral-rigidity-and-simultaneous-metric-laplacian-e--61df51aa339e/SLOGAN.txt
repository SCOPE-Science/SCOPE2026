For every even \(r\ge2\), the theta graph \(\Theta_{2,2,r}\) has simple ordinary and signless Laplacian spectra except that eigenvalue \(2\) is doubled when \(r\equiv2\pmod4\). Its metric dimension equals three at \(r=2,4\) and two thereafter, so both Laplacian maximum-multiplicity equalities hold simultaneously exactly for \(r\equiv2\pmod4\) with \(r\ge6\), completing the even branch omitted by the motivating odd-theta analysis.
