The stationary geometric-ensemble current transform of resetting Brownian particles and resetting run-and-tumble particles is never a genuine probability-generating function at finite positive density-to-reset-length ratio. Its coefficients eventually alternate with the exact leading law \(c_n\sim(-1)^{n+1}\lambda e^{-\lambda\pi^2/6}/(2n^2)\), even though every fixed initial configuration has a proper current distribution.
