For the eight-vertex cubic Fourier aperture, every sufficiently fine **odd** root-of-unity phase alphabet has exact minimal fourth moment \(136-64\cos(\pi/q)+16\cos^2(\pi/q)\), with exactly \(24q^4\) coefficient arrays attaining it. The unavoidable phase-quantization penalty above the continuous optimum \(88\) is asymptotic to \(16\pi^2/q^2\); the smallest admissible odd orders are not covered by the eventual theorem.
