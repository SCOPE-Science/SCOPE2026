The nondistributive pentagon \(N_5\) is the congruence lattice of a finite algebra with exclusively two-valued unary operations on **every** carrier of size at least four, and on no smaller carrier: \(\operatorname{spec}_2(N_5)=\{4,5,6,\ldots\}\). An explicit uniform construction contrasts with the other five-element nondistributive lattice, the diamond \(M_3\), whose carrier-size spectrum is the singleton \(\{3\}\).
