For every plane through the center of a regular icosidodecahedron that avoids its vertices, the section is a dodecagon; in particular, the Haar-random side count is exactly \(12\) with probability \(1\). Exact radial pairing of the four neighbors at every vertex prevents the edge-crossing count from changing with the plane orientation, while a \(\pi/5\) edge-angle calculation fixes its value.
