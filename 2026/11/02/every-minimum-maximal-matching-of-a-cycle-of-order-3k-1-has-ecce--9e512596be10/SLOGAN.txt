For every \(k\ge2\), all minimum maximal matchings of the labelled cycle \(C_{3k+1}\) have the same eccentricity under one-edge replacement: exactly \(\lfloor(3k+1)/2\rfloor\). Thus the previously described, nonregular bracelet reconfiguration graph is self-centered, and an explicit two-particle construction supplies a farthest configuration from every starting matching.
