Every maximum-block double-coalition partition of the labeled biclique \(K_{r,s}\), \(3\le r\le s\), is either a small-shore hub (possible only for shores of size at most five) or two mixed triples of types \((1,2)\) and \((2,1)\). The resulting exact number of maximizing partitions is a linear polynomial in the larger shore up to size four, becomes cubic at shore size five, and is \(9\binom r3\binom s3\) once both shores have at least six vertices.
