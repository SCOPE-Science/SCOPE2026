# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The composition representation is canonical for the stated triple avoidance class. The proposed marked words share the same length and total size, are distinct, and have no proper prefix-suffix overlaps. Replacing the first marked word is therefore a genuine size-preserving involutive bijection between first-hit classes. The layer-start and word-occurrence relations are explicitly first-order definable using the two intrinsic orders. Fibonacci prefix ratios establish convergence to the biased iid layer stream, and independent fixed disjoint windows force a marked word eventually with probability tending to one. The bundled checker reproduces finite-n equinumerosity for the specified ranges and validates the cross-bifix property for denominators through ten. Finite enumeration is supporting evidence only.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest directly inspected paper, Falgas-Ravry et al. (arXiv:2610.34328, Theorem 1.4 and §4), proves density for all small exponentially growing permutation classes, but not exact rational saturation in the Fibonacci class or the finite-n balanced-code bijections. Braunfeld--Kukla (2021/2022, §§1–4), inspected from the published eight-page primary paper, proves convergence for layered permutations but does not determine the limiting probability spectrum. Markström--Räty (arXiv:2610.22218) establishes regular-language convergence and rational generating functions; the accessible abstract and quoted theorem do not provide the proposed reverse-attainment statement. Searches for Fibonacci permutations, first-order exact rational spectra, balanced pattern races, and cross-bifix-free codes located no equivalent published theorem. An unlocated equivalent in probability/automata literature remains a real historical-priority risk, and incomplete full-text access to the Markström--Räty paper leaves a narrower inspection risk. No independent novelty certification is asserted.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The recent literature singles out the attainable limiting probability spectrum as a natural invariant and proves density in the exponential-growth regime; exact attainment of every rational is strictly stronger and is not entailed by topological density or convergence alone. The theorem provides a uniform construction for *all* denominators, a transparent first-order interpretation, and an unusually strong exact finite-n symmetry that is reusable for other weighted composition languages. This is not a one-off small computation or a trivial restatement of the density theorem.

## Boundaries
The spectrum may contain additional irrational values; the result proves only \(\mathbb Q\cap[0,1]\) inclusion. The marked patterns and formulas depend on the target rational, and historical novelty is assessed against accessible literature rather than independently certified.
