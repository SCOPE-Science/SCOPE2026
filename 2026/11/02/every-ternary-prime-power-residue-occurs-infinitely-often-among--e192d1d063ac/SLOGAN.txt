Every residue class modulo every power of three contains infinitely many Catalan numbers. Sparse ternary indices yield the exact congruence \(C_{n_{k;v,t}}\equiv3^v d_k^v c_k^t\pmod{3^k}\), where \(c_k\) generates all units modulo \(3^k\), giving a constructive negative answer to the forbidden-residue question for three-powers.
