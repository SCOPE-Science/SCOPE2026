For every \(m,n\ge3\), the 1-limited domination number of \(K_m\square C_n\) equals \(m[n-2\lfloor n/4\rfloor-\mathbf1_{n\equiv3\pmod4}]\). All minimum sets are classified by cyclic arrangements of completely omitted clique columns, with exactly three additional cycle-row solutions when \((m,n)=(3,3)\) or \((3,6)\); their exact numbers follow a four-periodic formula.
