For every nonzero even recurrence parameter \(P\), the first-kind Lucas sequence \(U_{n+2}=PU_{n+1}+U_n\) has exact \(2\)-adic image closure \(P\mathbb Z_2\sqcup(1+P^2\mathbb Z_2)\). Both constituent subsequences are surjective modulo every \(2\)-power after normalization, giving limiting residue density \(2^{-\nu_2(P)}+2^{-2\nu_2(P)}\).
