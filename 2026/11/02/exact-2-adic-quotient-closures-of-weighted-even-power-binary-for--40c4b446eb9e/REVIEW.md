# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses the exact image of the even-power map on odd \(2\)-adic units, a complete trichotomy of input valuations, and a quotient-of-clopen-cosets identity. The closure includes zero and is locally finite away from zero; all hypotheses and boundary cases are covered. The supplementary finite modular checker passed 456 value-set comparisons and 2,176 valuation-shell checks; it is not substituted for the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The most directly comparable paper is Antony--Barman--Miska, arXiv:2201.04372v1, especially Theorem 1.1: it supplies the split-versus-nonsplit density criterion but not the exact nonsplit closure. The Miska--Murru--Sanna 2018 value-set formula gives previously covered unweighted cases. Both the split branch and odd coefficients congruent to one modulo the relevant power of two are credited to existing knowledge. The genuinely additional exact answer is for coefficients outside these two residue classes. Search failures alone do not establish novelty, and implicit or unindexed coverage remains possible; a later paper was inspected only in part.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete topological classification in a natural arithmetic family, with exact \(2\)-adic valuation shells and exact surviving odd-unit congruence classes. It refines an established density theorem by specifying the entire forbidden and permitted regions, not just giving a negative answer. The nonsplit weighted classes are mathematically distinct from the unweighted unit-root-equivalent cases.
