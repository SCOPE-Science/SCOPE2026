For every odd coefficient \(c\) and even exponent \(n\ge4\), the exact \(2\)-adic quotient closure of \(x^n+c y^n\) is either all of \(\mathbb Q_2\) or a specified finite union of unit-coset balls on precisely three valuation residue classes. The nonsplit closure depends explicitly on \(\nu_2(1+c)\) and the residue classes of \(1,c,1+c\), strengthening the known density-only criterion.
