The exact conjectured 2-adic valuation of the reciprocal-binomial sum is attained at every Mersenne index \(2^e-1\) with \(e\ge4\): \(\nu_2(\sum_{k=0}^{2^e-1}\binom{2^e-1}{k}^{-1})=3e-2\). Moreover, the normalized sums converge to the nonzero 2-adic constant \(-\operatorname{Li}_3(2)\), with error valuation at least \(e-4\).
