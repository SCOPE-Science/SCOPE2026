For a specified binomial-parity run-length transform, the 2-adic valuations at Mersenne indices \(2^{10r-6}-1\) and \(2^{10r-1}-1\) are exactly \(2+\nu_2(r)\) for every \(r\ge1\). The same local rule adds across arbitrary binary words whose maximal one-runs have lengths congruent to four modulo five.
