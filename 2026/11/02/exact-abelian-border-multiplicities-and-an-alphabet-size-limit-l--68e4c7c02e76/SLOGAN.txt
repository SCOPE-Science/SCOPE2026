For uniformly sampled words over \(q\ge3\) symbols, the complete generating function for the number of proper Abelian-border lengths is explicit through multinomial-square return counts. The typical short-border count grows like \(\log m\) and has an exponential limit for \(q=3\), but converges to a finite geometric law for every \(q\ge4\), sharply contrasting with binary square-root behavior.
