For every triangular graph \(J(n,2)\) with \(n\ge5\), the adjacency–Dirac Connes distance along an edge is exactly determined by the unique minimizer of an explicit two-by-two spectral pencil. Its asymptotic length is \(2(10/3-2/\sqrt3)^{-1/2}(n-3)^{-1/2}\), with an explicit negative order-\((n-3)^{-1}\) correction caused by an avoided crossing of two symmetry modes.
