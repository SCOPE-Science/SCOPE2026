A right equilateral triangular prism has an exact elementary distribution of triangular, quadrilateral and pentagonal invariant-affine-plane sections for every aspect ratio. Pentagons are most likely at the unique half-height \(h=0.4095226703\ldots\) for a unit-circumradius base, where their probability is \(0.1126501962\ldots\)—not at the equal-edge prism.
