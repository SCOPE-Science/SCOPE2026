For the exponential-mark Poisson envelope associated with a 2026 selective-sweep model, the two-time leader persistence is exactly \(s\log(t/s)/(t-s)\), while expected actual switches number \(\tfrac12\log(t/s)\). Successive leader fitness advantages are independent \(\operatorname{Gamma}(2,1)\), giving an almost-sure log-time sweep frequency of \(1/2\); the stationary log-time switch process is not Poisson, and its unconditional residual leader lifetime has infinite mean.
