For every unweighted clique bouquet \(K_1\vee(\bigsqcup_i K_{d_i})\), all heat-kernel Wasserstein-one distances have exact closed forms, and the sharp Lipschitz norm at every time depends only on the total number of peripheral vertices and the smallest clique. In particular, when one clique is a singleton, arbitrarily repartitioning the remaining leaves into disjoint cliques leaves the entire sharp heat-norm curve identical to that of the star.
