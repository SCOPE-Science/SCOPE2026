For the unit-rate bridge-barbell graph of two \(m\)-vertex cliques, the exact all-time \(W_1\) semigroup amplification is \(A_me^{-\lambda_-t}-(A_m-1)e^{-\lambda_+t}\), uniquely maximized by the bridge edge at every positive time. Its peak occurs on a \(\log m/m\) time scale, and the maximal amplification converges to the graph diameter bound \(3\), despite the factor being \(1\) at time zero.
