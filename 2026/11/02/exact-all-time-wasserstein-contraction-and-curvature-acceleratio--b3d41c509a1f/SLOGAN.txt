For the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M\) with \(n\ge5\), unit-rate heat flow has exact Wasserstein-one contraction \(\mathcal C_n(t)=[2(n-2)e^{-(n-2)t}-(n-4)e^{-2(n-1)t}]/n\). Its sharp logarithmic decay rate grows strictly from the curvature value \(2\) to the spectral gap \(n-2\), and adjacent initial vertices realize the norm at every positive time.
