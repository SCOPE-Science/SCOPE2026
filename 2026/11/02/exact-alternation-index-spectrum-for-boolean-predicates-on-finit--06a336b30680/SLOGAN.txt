For a finite weak order with antichain levels of sizes \(n_i\), the exact number of Boolean predicates whose longest increasing \(1,0,1,\ldots\) pattern has length \(j\) is \(e_j(2^{n_1}-1,\ldots,2^{n_h}-1)\). Consequently all \(k\)-monotone predicate counts depend only on the multiset, not the order, of the level sizes.
