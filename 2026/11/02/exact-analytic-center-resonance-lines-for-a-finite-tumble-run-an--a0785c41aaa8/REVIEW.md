# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The underlying hypergeometric stationary characteristic function and inverse density are sourced to Sun–Ye–Podgornik, Section III. The resonant numerator–denominator hypergeometric parameters produce a finite differential-operator construction. A moment-by-moment calculation and compact-support determinacy prove that the construction is the stationary probability density for every \(u>0\) and integer \(n\ge0\). The coefficient of the nonsmooth branch in the published inverse density is nonzero away from precisely those rates; its residue supplies a logarithm for odd positive \(v\). No finite enumeration is used to establish the global necessity or sufficiency. 140 independent exact checks of the Fokker–Planck stationary identities and moments passed. A residual risk is an unnoticed normalization convention in the cited characteristic function, mitigated by the independent steady-state-generator computations.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge comparison)

The precise published baseline is the **complete** general hypergeometric density, not merely its title or abstract. The authors explicitly identify the single uniform point and propose generic central differentiability thresholds; they do not give the countable exceptional analytic parameter lines, the polynomial construction or the iff regularity theorem. The equal-rate three-state law (Basu et al., 2019) and the newer zero-duration tumble model (Guéneau et al., 2026) concern parameter restrictions/models not covering this claim. published-finding corpus source/aliases/equivalent-formulation searches and accessible prior claims did not reveal an equivalent rate-resonance result. A possible undiscovered special-function regularity theorem remains a residual priority risk, and no claim of independent novelty certification is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The original paper explicitly treats central cusp singularities and conjectures regularity thresholds. This result gives the sharp global exception set, reveals infinitely many experimentally meaningful rate choices with smooth central profiles, and makes their entire stationary densities elementary polynomial-times-beta expressions. It thus clarifies a physical shape-transition diagnostic rather than computing an arbitrary unmotivated parameter specialization. The result does not imply analyticity at the support boundaries or robustness to added thermal noise.
