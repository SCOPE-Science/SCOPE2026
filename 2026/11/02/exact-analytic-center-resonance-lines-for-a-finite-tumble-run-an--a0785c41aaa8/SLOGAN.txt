For a harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle with nonzero tumble durations, the stationary position density is analytic at the trap center **exactly** on the rate resonance lines \(\gamma_T=\gamma_R+(2n+1)b\), \(n\ge0\). Every such line has an explicit polynomial-times-beta stationary law, while all other positive rates retain a fractional-power or logarithmic center branch.
