On a homogeneous \(b\)-ary tree, the geometric-symbol Toeplitz operator \(\Delta M_{r^{|v|}}\nabla\) splits exactly into scaled copies of one explicit triangular operator, with branching multiplicities. This yields its complete singular-value multiset, an exact Schatten-zeta factorization with a positive pole at \(q_c=\log b/\log(1/r)\), and the sharp decay \(s_j\asymp j^{-1/q_c}\) throughout \(0<r<b^{-1/2}\).
