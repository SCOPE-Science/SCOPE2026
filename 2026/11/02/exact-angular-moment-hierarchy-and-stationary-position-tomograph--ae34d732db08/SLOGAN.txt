For shape-anisotropic planar Brownian motion with joint Poisson position–orientation resets, every even complex stationary position moment has an exact product over \(r+4\gamma j^2\). The ratio \(\mathbb EZ^4/(\mathbb EZ^2)^2\) reconstructs the hidden rotational diffusion rate, and together with two second-order position moments it gives an exact global parameter-identification and attainability theorem, up to unavoidable time-scale and isotropic degeneracies.
