Pairs of anticommuting self-inverse signed partial permutations decompose into only singleton, two-point and four-point components, with a strict even-dimensional obstruction on their common support. Their complete joint support-rank exponential generating function is \(\exp(z+2zu+z^2u^2+2zv+z^2v^2+4z^2u^2v^2+2z^4u^4v^4)\), giving \(\exp(5z+6z^2+2z^4)\) when ranks are forgotten.
