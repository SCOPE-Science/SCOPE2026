For every odd-dimensional Boolean hypercube with the adjacency-matrix Dirac operator, antipodal Connes distance equals the reciprocal-binomial sum \(\sum_{j=0}^{(n-1)/2}[(2j+1)(n-2j)]^{-1/2}\). It tends to \(\pi/2\) from below with sharp leading deficit \(-\zeta(1/2,1/2)\sqrt{2/(n+1)}\), while graph distance grows linearly.
