For every integer \(h\ge3\), the cubic generalized-Mersenne semigroups with initial generator divisible by \(h^3-h+1\) have an explicit entire Apéry set, Frobenius number, and genus. Their type is always two, with pseudo-Frobenius numbers separated by exactly \(d(h-1)^2\), even though the general higher-base greedy-weight monotonicity fails.
