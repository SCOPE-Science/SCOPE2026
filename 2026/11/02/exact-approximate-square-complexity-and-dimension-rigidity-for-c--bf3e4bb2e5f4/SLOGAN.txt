For a Bedford–McMullen survivor system with one forbidden digit pair at each column-prefix vertex, every deterministic environment has exactly \((A-1)^{\lfloor\lambda k\rfloor}B^{k-\lfloor\lambda k\rfloor}\) nonempty approximate squares at level \(k\), giving an environment-independent packing and box dimension. The published random Hausdorff dimension is strictly smaller except in one precisely characterized equal-column-survivor case.
