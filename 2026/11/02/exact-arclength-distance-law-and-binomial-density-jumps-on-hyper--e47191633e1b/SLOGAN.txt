For every dimension \(d\ge3\), the intrinsic distance between two arclength-uniform points on the wireframe of a unit \(d\)-cube has an exact binomial–triangular density, with downward jumps \(2\binom{d-1}{k-1}/(d2^{d-1})\) at every integer \(2\le k<d\). The complete law yields exact mean and variance formulas and a Gaussian high-dimensional limit.
