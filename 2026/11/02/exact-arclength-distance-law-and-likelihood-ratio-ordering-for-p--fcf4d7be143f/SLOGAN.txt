For the unit-edge point–line incidence network of every finite projective plane of order \(q\), the distance between two independent arclength-uniform points has an explicit three-piece affine density on \([0,3]\), independent of the plane's isomorphism type. As the order increases, the entire distributions increase strictly in likelihood-ratio order, with exact first two moments, a sharp near-diameter tail, and a quantitative triangular limiting law.
