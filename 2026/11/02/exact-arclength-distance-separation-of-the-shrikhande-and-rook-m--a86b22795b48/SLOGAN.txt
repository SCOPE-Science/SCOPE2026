The Shrikhande and 4×4 rook graphs share all standard strongly regular parameters and have identical intrinsic distances between arclength-random edge points up to radius \(2\), yet their cumulative distance laws differ by an exact squared tent on \((2,3)\), maximized at \(1/96\). For any diameter-at-most-two unit-edge graph, the distance tail above \(5/2\) recovers its number of induced two-edge matchings exactly.
