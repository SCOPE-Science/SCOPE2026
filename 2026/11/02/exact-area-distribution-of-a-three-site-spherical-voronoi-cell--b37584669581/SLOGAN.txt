For three independent uniform sites on the unit sphere, each labelled spherical Voronoi cell is a lune with deterministic perimeter \(2\pi\), but its area \(A\) has the exact law \(\mathbb P(A\le a)=[a(2+\cos a)-3\sin a]/(6\pi)\) for \(0\le a\le2\pi\). Its area variance is \(4\pi^2/9-10/3\), and distinct cells' areas have covariance \(5/3-2\pi^2/9\).
