# Review of the associative-subalgebra dimension spectrum

## Correctness
PASS. In the algebra of strictly upper block sizes \( (1,r,1) \), the one-dimensional product space is an annihilator. A subspace containing this product space is automatically a subalgebra. Otherwise its products must be zero, reducing classification to orthogonal pairs of projected row/column subspaces and a linear Goursat correspondence. The proof determines the Gaussian-binomial sum, shows the avoidance cutoff at dimension \(r\), and counts its exact equality case. The independent RREF checker confirms the predicted spectra for eight finite field/rank configurations. These tests are not relied upon to prove the arbitrary-field-size theorem.

## Originality
PASS subject to priority uncertainty. Woodward's upper-triangular Lie-ring zeta results (doi:10.1112/jlms/jdm068) count a different closure structure; for the three-dimensional binary example, the associative and Lie subalgebra counts are respectively 10 and 12. Eick and Moede's coclass studies (doi:10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.03.027; doi:10.1080/10586458.2016.1162229) classify algebra types or examine coclass periodicity rather than directly supplying this dimension spectrum in material inspected. Sampled chapters of Moede's 2016 thesis (https://d-nb.info/1175818445/34) discuss annihilators and extensions but were not a complete document-wide overlap check. The Goursat subspace correspondence and Gaussian counts are existing tools; originality is assessed for their completed associative subalgebra census and sharp cutoff, not for those tools individually. Missing full texts and unindexed results are explicit residual risks.

## Value
PASS. The full rank/unbounded-field family is a canonical nilpotent ideal in parabolic matrix algebras. The theorem gives a computable subalgebra-zeta dimension distribution, a non-obvious sharp bound for subalgebras disjoint from the algebra square, and an exact maximal-stratum count. It illuminates differences between associative and Lie subalgebra lattices and is not merely a sporadic low-dimensional count.

## Limits
Only nonunital \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear associative subalgebras of the strict \((1,r,1)\)-block radical are counted. Unital parabolic, Lie, integral, and general two-step associative cases are excluded. No independent audit or exhaustive historical publication-priority investigation was performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
