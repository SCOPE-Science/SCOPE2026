For the nilpotent associative radical of every three-block parabolic matrix algebra of shape \((1,r,1)\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), the complete subalgebra dimension spectrum is a finite Gaussian-binomial/Goursat sum. Subalgebras avoiding the one-dimensional square have dimension at most \(r\), and exactly \(q^r\sum_{a=0}^r \genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{r}{a}_q\) attain this sharp bound.
