On a closed rank-one matrix curve, consecutive tangent-plane projections preserve the skew mode but strictly damp both symmetric modes, with exact finite-grid spectral radius \(\cos^N(\pi/N)\). The symmetric-loop monodromy has an explicit nonnormal two-by-two formula, and its discrepancy from true parallel transport has sharp \(1/N\) coefficient \(\pi^2/2+\pi|\sin(\sqrt2\pi)|/(2\sqrt2)\).
