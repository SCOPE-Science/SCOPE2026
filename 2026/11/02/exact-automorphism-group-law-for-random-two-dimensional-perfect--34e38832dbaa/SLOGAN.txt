For a uniformly sampled invertible two-dimensional evolution-algebra structure matrix over \(\mathbb F_q\), the full automorphism group is always one of \(1,C_2,C_3,S_3\), with an explicit all-field frequency formula. The chance of a nontrivial automorphism is exactly \(1/(q+1)\) when \(q\) is even and \((q-1)/(q(q+1))\) when \(q\) is odd, with a sharp secondary distinction according to cube roots of unity.
