Balanced binary one-deletion-correcting codes of lengths \(2,4,6,8,10,12\) have exact maximum sizes \(1,2,5,14,42,132\), respectively, proved by checksum constructions and exact rational packing certificates. The fractional deletion-shadow relaxation first fails to be integral at balanced length \(8\), where an explicit feasible fractional packing has value \(14.19498>14\).
