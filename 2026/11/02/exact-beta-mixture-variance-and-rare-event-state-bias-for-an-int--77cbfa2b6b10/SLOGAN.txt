A harmonically confined Brownian particle whose trap switches between attractive and repulsive curvature has a stationary conditional variance that becomes an exact two-component beta mixture after a fractional-linear change of variables. This yields the full Gaussian-mixture position law and an explicit power-law tail coefficient, while the repulsive-state fraction among extreme displacements tends to the universal value \(a/(a+b)\), regardless of switching rates and thermal diffusivity.
