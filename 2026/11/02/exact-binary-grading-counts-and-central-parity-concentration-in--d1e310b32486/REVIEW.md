# Self-review: exact binary grading census

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem fixes the label ordering of the two homogeneous pieces and counts actual decompositions, not orbits. Its proof reconstructs all choices in the following order: intrinsic graded center and homogeneous derived line; Gaussian-binomial counts of decompositions of the center; symplectic orthogonal splittings when the derived line is even, complementary Lagrangian pairs when it is odd; and the exact linear graph-lift multiplicity. No division by two or assumption of odd characteristic is used. The standard symplectic and Lagrangian counts are reconstructed in the proof. The theta-series asymptotic includes a parity-switching involution, which removes an otherwise spurious even/odd dependence in the limit. Independently enumerated small-field decompositions and idempotent projections matched the formulas exactly; the proof, not these finite checks, supports the infinite claim.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

Calderón–Draper–Martín–Sánchez (arXiv:1405.4093, Section 3, Theorem 1) already establish homogeneous symplectic normal forms for ordinary Heisenberg algebras with one-dimensional center, treating gradings up to equivalence. The present claim is expressly restricted in its new contribution to nontrivial central stabilization, exact counts of all labeled decompositions in every finite characteristic, and an asymptotic central-parity law. It does not present the ordinary-Heisenberg qualitative result or standard symplectic-group orders as new. Eick–Moede (arXiv:2205.03155) investigate finite-field gradings on simple Lie algebras, and Hakavuori–Kivioja–Moisala–Tripaldi (arXiv:2011.06871) restrict their main enumeration to torsion-free gradings in characteristic zero. Other inspected related results concern Heisenberg subalgebra permutability and automorphism fixed-point-freeness, not the claimed binary grading enumeration. A formula implicit in earlier classical-group involution counts or in a more general symplectic-decomposition enumerator may exist; thus originality remains provisional and is not proof of publication priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Central stabilization changes the number of central homogeneous choices, the quotient lifts, and the dominant asymptotic parity. The formulas give exact distributions for every derived-line parity throughout a natural infinite family (equivalently, all finite-field two-step Lie algebras with a one-dimensional derived ideal and nondegenerate commutator quotient). The asymptotic establishes that odd-derived gradings dominate with an explicit positive rate and a nontrivial theta-series constant independent of the rank parity. This is a complete invariant for a natural counting problem, not a cosmetic specialization or an arbitrary finite-table entry.

## Closest sources and limitations

- Calderón, Draper, Martín, Sánchez, *Gradings and Symmetries on Heisenberg type algebras*, arXiv:1405.4093, Section 3 and Theorem 1: qualitatively covers the unstabilized case, not the new labeled central-stabilization counts.
- Eick and Moede, *Computing subalgebras and Z2-gradings of simple Lie algebras over finite fields*, arXiv:2205.03155, Sections 1–2: motivates finite-field enumeration, but the objects are simple rather than nilpotent.
- Hakavuori, Kivioja, Moisala, Tripaldi, *Gradings for nilpotent Lie algebras*, arXiv:2011.06871, introduction and Section 3: characteristic-zero, torsion-free gradings, not binary finite-field enumeration.

The theorem does not count equivalence classes under automorphisms and does not treat central rank growing with the symplectic rank. An implicit broader classical-group formula remains a residual novelty risk. Neither publication priority nor independent mathematical validation is certified.
