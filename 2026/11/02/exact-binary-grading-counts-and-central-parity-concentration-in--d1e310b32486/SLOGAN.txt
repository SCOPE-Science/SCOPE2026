Every ordered binary grading of a centrally stabilized finite Heisenberg Lie algebra is counted by explicit symplectic and Gaussian-binomial sums, including in characteristic two. For fixed field and central excess, the derived line is odd in a uniformly random grading with probability \(1-K_{r,q}q^{-n}+o(q^{-n})\), where \(K_{r,q}>0\) is an explicit theta-series product quotient.
