For binary non-overlapping (cross-bifix-free) codes of every length \(2\le n\le11\), the complete optimum cardinality is determined at every minimum Hamming distance \(2\le d\le n\). In particular the length-\(11\) frontier is \(42,14,10,4,2,2,2,1,1,1\), and the first strict cost of requiring distance two occurs at length \(10\).
