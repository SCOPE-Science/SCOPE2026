# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For each fixed \(n\ge3\), the nonnegative four-gap parametrization is bijective for weight-three binary words. The three linear deck coordinates determine an integral one-parameter family, and the remaining quadratic coordinate forces precisely the identity or one gap-swap involution. All three other mixed coordinates are verified explicitly, so the converse is proved, not assumed. The positive-parameter orientation is a bijection onto unordered collision pairs; its generating product and pole-order asymptotics are exact. Direct exhaustive deck enumeration through length sixteen passes but is not treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risks

The 2026 primary paper proves general deck identities and asymptotic numbers of decks; the 2020 primary paper emphasizes two-binomial equivalence; the older deck-census paper gives finite unrestricted totals. The known length-seven collision is credited to OEIS A258585. None of the inspected accessible claims states or implies without additional analysis the complete weight-three fiber involution and its all-length count. The full publisher article from 2023 was not available for material comparison, so an unobserved stronger result remains possible. No priority certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

Exact constant-weight distinguishability is a natural coding invariant of scattered-subsequence channels. The theorem classifies all fibers of the first weight-three ambiguity regime, rather than a selected finite case; it determines every restricted optimal codebook size and the limiting fraction \(7/8\). Its precise structural and enumerative outputs have direct value for benchmark construction and reconstruction-code analysis.
