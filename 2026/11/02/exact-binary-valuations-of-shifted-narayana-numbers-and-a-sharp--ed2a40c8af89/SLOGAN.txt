For Narayana's cows recurrence, the binary valuation of every shifted term \(a_n+1\) is given exactly by residue classes modulo seven and fourteen, together with the branch \(1+\nu_2((n+4)/7)\) when \(n\equiv3\pmod7\). Every valuation level has an explicit natural density, and all counting errors are bounded by the sharp constant \(9/7\), uniformly in the level and cutoff.
