Every alternating binary word \((01)^m\) has exactly \(\binom{m+2-j}{2j+1}\) minimal absent scattered subsequences of length \(m+1+j\), for \(0\le j\le\lfloor(m+1)/3\rfloor\), and none at other lengths. Their total number grows exponentially according to the rational generating function \(x(3-x+2x^2)/(1-2x+x^2-x^3)\), even though the shortest-length layer has only \(m+2\) members.
