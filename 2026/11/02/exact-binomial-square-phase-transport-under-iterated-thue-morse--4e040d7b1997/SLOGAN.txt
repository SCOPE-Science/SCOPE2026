For every infinite binary word \(\mathbf x\), every \(r\ge1\), and every \(M\ge1\), two consecutive windows of length \(M2^r\) in \(\mu^r(\mathbf x)\), where \(\mu(0)=01\) and \(\mu(1)=10\), have identical scattered-subsequence profiles through order \(r\) precisely when their common start is aligned modulo \(2^r\), or their three boundary source letters all coincide. This uniform transport law also gives the dyadic phase rule for the classical Thue–Morse word.
