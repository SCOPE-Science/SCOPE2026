For \(n\ge3\) independent uniform-arclength samples from any Euclidean triangle, the normalized expected convex-hull area has an exact formula in its three normalized side lengths, and the missing area is strictly Schur-convex in those proportions. Equilateral triangles uniquely maximize the expected fraction; degenerating thin triangles determine the sharp infimum and exhibit a distinct \(n^{-1}\) sampling-error crossover.
