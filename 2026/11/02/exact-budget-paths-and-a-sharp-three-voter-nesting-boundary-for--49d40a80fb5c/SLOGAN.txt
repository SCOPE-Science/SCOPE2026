For sunflower approval profiles, the uncompleted Method of Equal Shares has an exact nested outcome at every real budget: universal projects enter first, and each disjoint group buys its private projects at explicit proportional thresholds. The guarantee is sharp in voter count: two voters cannot lose a funded project as the budget rises, but three voters with four candidates can change the funded set from \(\{A,B,C\}\) at budget \(3\) to \(\{A,B,D\}\) at budget \(4\).
