The exact burning number of \(K_m\square P_n\) is \(1+\lceil\sqrt n\rceil\) for every \(m\ge4\), while the three-row clique chain has \(1+\max\{2,\lceil\sqrt{n-1}\rceil\}\) for \(n\ge2\), with value \(2\) at \(n=1\). The difference is sharp: three-row chains permit exactly one additional column at every burning horizon of at least three rounds, whereas all larger clique widths obey the squared-interval limit.
