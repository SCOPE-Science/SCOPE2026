For a coprime affine semidirect product \(V\rtimes H\), a subspace \(W\le V\) is c-normal precisely when \(W\cap[V,H]\) is \(H\)-invariant. Consequently, all c-normal maximal Sylow-\(p\) flags admit an exact Gaussian-binomial and eigenspace-shuffle enumeration, with existence equivalent to diagonalizability of the action over \(\mathbb F_p\) and their proportion independent of the fixed-space dimension.
