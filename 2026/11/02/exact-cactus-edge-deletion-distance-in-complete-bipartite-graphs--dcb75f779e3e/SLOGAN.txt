For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{p,q}\), the exact minimum number of edges that must be deleted to leave a connected cactus is \((p-1)(q-1)-\min\{p-1,q-1,\lfloor(p+q-1)/3\rfloor\}\). The three independent-looking cycle-capacity bounds are simultaneously sharp, witnessed by a constructive family of four-cycle cacti for all part sizes.
