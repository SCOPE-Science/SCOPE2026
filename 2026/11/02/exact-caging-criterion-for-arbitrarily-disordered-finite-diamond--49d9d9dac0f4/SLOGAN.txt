In a finite diamond chain with spatially disordered nonzero complex couplings, exact all-time confinement between distinct hub sites occurs if and only if each pair of two-hop path amplitudes cancels and the two intermediate arm energies agree. The Hamiltonian then splits into explicit two- and three-dimensional invariant cages; a path-cancellation error causes quadratic onset of adjacent-hub transfer amplitude, whereas a pure arm-energy mismatch causes cubic onset.
