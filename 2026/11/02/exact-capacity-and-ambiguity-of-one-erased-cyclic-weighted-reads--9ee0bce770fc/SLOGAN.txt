For cyclic indexed length-\(\ell\) numeric weighted reads, one known erased coordinate is entirely redundant whenever \(\gcd(n,\ell)>1\). In the coprime case, all inverse fibers are explicit integer chains and their exact number is \(q^n-\max(q-r,0)^{n-m}\max(q-\ell+r,0)^m\), where \(nr-m\ell=1\) and \(1\le r<\ell\); in particular, binary sequences remain uniquely recoverable from one erased read whenever \(\ell\ge3\).
