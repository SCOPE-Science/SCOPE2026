For binary cross-bifix-free codes whose words have exactly three ones, the maximum size at length \(n=3m+r\) is \(3m(m-1)/2\), \(m(3m-1)/2\), or \(m(3m+1)/2\) according as \(r=0,1,2\). Every optimum is one of at most two explicitly defined threshold families or its reversal; the number of labeled optima is exactly four when \(3\mid n\) and two otherwise.
