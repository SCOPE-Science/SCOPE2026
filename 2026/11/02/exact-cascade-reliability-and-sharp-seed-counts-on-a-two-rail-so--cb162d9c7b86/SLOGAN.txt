For irreversible two-neighbor adoption on a \(2\)-by-\(n\) ladder, a seven-state vacant-path transfer gives an exact rational generating function for the number of successful initial adopter sets at every size. The sharp minimum seed count is \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) for even \(n\) and \((n+3)/2\) for odd \(n\), with explicit optimal-set multiplicities, while the total number grows like \((\sqrt{21}/7)((3+\sqrt{21})/2)^n\).
