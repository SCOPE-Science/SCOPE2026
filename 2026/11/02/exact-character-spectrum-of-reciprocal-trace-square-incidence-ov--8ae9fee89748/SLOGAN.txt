The square-trace incidence of the reciprocal maps \(x+a/x\) has full column rank over every odd finite field, and its entire Gram spectrum is explicit: two levels for \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), and two exceptional levels plus Jacobi-sum character levels for \(q\equiv1\pmod4\). The proof identifies the exact quadratic-residue correlation kernel and establishes strictly positive eigenvalues without restricting to prime fields.
