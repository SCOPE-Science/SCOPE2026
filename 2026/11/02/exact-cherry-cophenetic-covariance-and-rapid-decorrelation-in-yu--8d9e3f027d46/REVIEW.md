# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The root-split branching identity, additive tree recursions, and conditional independence give an exact covariance recurrence. Cherry-mean boundary corrections are supported only at subtree sizes one and two; the resulting harmonic terms cancel algebraically. Base cases, all integer domains, and signs are explicit. Exact rational enumeration and separate moment recurrences confirm the proof's intermediate conclusions through finite ranges; they are not substituted for induction.

## Originality

PASS on inspected evidence, with acknowledged historical-priority risk. Cardona, Mir, and Rosselló (2012, arXiv:1202.6573v1) derive marginal total-cophenetic and other index moments, and a Sackin–cophenetic covariance; their inspected primary PDF does not give cherry–cophenetic covariance. Wu and Choi (2015, arXiv:1508.03139v1) derive joint cherry–pitchfork laws, rather than mixed moments involving the cophenetic index. Adjacent earlier formulas for Yule cherry–Sackin or BST leaf–path-length dependence do not identify the cophenetic sum and do not determine the stated covariance. Specialized older covariance studies could still contain equivalent coverage.

## Value

PASS. Cherry count measures local near-tip branching, while total cophenetic index measures the depth of shared ancestry across all leaf pairs. Their exact finite-size joint dependence, sign, and unusually fast normalized decorrelation are relevant to the joint interpretation of classical phylogenetic tree-shape diagnostics under the Yule null. The cancellation of logarithmic marginal-mean terms gives the covariance a simple linear asymptotic scale.
