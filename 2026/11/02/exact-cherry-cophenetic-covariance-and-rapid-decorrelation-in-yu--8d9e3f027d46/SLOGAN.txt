Under the Yule–Harding–Kingman model, cherry abundance and total cophenetic index have exact covariance \(-\frac{(n-3)(5n-8)}{9(n-1)}\) for every \(n\ge3\). Their covariance is negative from four leaves onward, but their correlation decreases at rate \(n^{-3/2}\), with exact asymptotic coefficient \(-5\sqrt{30}/3\).
