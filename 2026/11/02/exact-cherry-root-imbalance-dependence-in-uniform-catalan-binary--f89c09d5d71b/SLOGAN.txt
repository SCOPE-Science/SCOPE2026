In a uniform plane Catalan binary tree, cherry abundance is strictly negatively correlated with squared imbalance of the root split: \(\operatorname{Cov}(X_n,Q_n)=-(n-2)4^{n-1}/((2n-1)C_n)\) for every \(n\ge3\). Yet the normalized correlation decays as \(-\pi^{1/4}n^{-3/4}/\sqrt2\), revealing a strong finite-size covariance and asymptotic decorrelation.
