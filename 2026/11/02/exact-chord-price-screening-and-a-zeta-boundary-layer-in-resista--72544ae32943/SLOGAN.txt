For optimal total-effective-resistance design of a weighted path with one optional chord, the exact chord price threshold is a discrete clipped-voltage variance, strictly increasing under interval inclusion. Its continuum limit is an arcsine-variance kernel, and for the end-to-end chord the sharp next-order term is \(2\pi\zeta(-1/2)\sqrt n\) below the leading \((\pi^2/4-2)n^2\) threshold.
