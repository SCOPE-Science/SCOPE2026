For an ideal four-parameter Mogi volcano-deformation inverse model with full three-component, equally noisy stations on a common ring, the entire angular D-optimality condition is the vanishing of the first two angular Fourier moments. The unique optimal ring radius is \(r/d=\sqrt{(\sqrt{561}-11)/110}=0.339591178874808\ldots\), and every regular polygon with at least three stations attains the optimum.
