For every \(q\ge3\), every tensor power \(K_q^{\otimes n}\), and every forbidden clique order \(3\le t\le q\), the largest \(K_t\)-free induced vertex set has \((t-1)q^{n-1}\) vertices. Equality is attained *only* by restricting one coordinate to \(t-1\) symbols, yielding exactly \(n\binom q{t-1}\) maximizers; in particular, this completely solves the triangle-free maximum-Hamming-distance endpoint.
