The mutual Birkhoff–James orthograph of real \(\ell_\infty^n\) has exact clique number \(\max_{1\le k\le n}\binom nk2^{k-1}\), without restricting the vectors to a finite alphabet. Active-coordinate rounding retracts every clique into a signed-subset incomparability graph, whose width is attained by a single support-size layer and certified by a random-chain inequality.
