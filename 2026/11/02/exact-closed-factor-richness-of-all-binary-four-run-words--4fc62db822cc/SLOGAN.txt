Every binary four-run word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\) has exactly \(n+1+\min(a,c)\min(b,d)\) distinct closed factors, including the empty factor, and its length-\(n\) four-run maximum is \(n+1+\lfloor\lfloor n/2\rfloor^2/4\rfloor\). The full extremizer classification and a bivariate generating function follow from the same five-class factor decomposition.
