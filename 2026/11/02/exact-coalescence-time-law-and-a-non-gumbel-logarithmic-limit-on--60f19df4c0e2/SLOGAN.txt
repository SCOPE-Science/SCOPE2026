For coalescing rate-one random walks started at every vertex of a star with \(n\) leaves, the full coalescence time has an explicit finite Laplace product and mean \(H_n+1-(H_n+2)/(n+1)\). Centering by \(\log n\) produces a universal non-Gumbel limit of variance \(\pi^2/2-1\), refining the known logarithmic lower bound for this star process.
