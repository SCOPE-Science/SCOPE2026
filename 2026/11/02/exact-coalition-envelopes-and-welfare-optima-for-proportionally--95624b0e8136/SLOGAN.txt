For one-dimensional obnoxious facility location, every coalition-wise \(\alpha\)-proportional-fair placement lies in one of at most \(n+1\) explicitly computable intervals, obtained by prefix maxima and suffix minima. This gives exact fair-welfare optimization in \(O(n)\) time after sorting, a binding-coalition witness for every agent, and a sharp \(n+1\)-component example already at \(\alpha=2\).
