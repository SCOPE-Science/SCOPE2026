For contractive operator-valued Drury–Arveson multipliers vanishing at the origin, the exact coefficient-norm Bohr majorant at \(z\) is \(\sum_{\alpha\ne0}\sqrt{|\alpha|!/\alpha!}|z^\alpha|\), attained by a single diagonal multiplier. The sharp one-variable radius is \(1/2\), the multivariable Bohr set is non-Euclidean, and countably infinite variable dimension admits no positive Euclidean Bohr radius.
