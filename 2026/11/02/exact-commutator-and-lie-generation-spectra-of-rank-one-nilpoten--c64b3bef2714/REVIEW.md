# Assessment of the finite-field minimal-nilpotent commutator spectrum

## Correctness — PASS

Every rank-one square-zero matrix is represented by a nonzero pair of mutually orthogonal vectors modulo a free scalar action. Fixing a standard representative, two scalar contractions control the commutator, and independent linear conditions count the three disjoint rank strata. The proof verifies the rank-two eigenspaces even when the characteristic is two, counts each nonzero trace fiber exactly, and derives the precise Lie-bracket table from the sandwich identities. The enumeration program tests nine small configurations and verifies all ordered pairs for six of them. Finite experiments are not used in place of the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, residual literature risk)

The nearest inspected primary sources address commuting matrix similarity classes (Britnell–Wildon, arXiv:1001.0811), generation by nilpotent matrices with a different second-factor restriction (Chistopolskaya, arXiv:1804.09457), and spectral possibilities for complex rank-one commutators (Cheng–Liang, DOI:10.13001/ela.2020.4955). None of the portions materially inspected establish the finite-field pairwise rank/trace distribution under the two rank-one nilpotency constraints or its uniform characteristic-dependent Lie-generation count. The older Guralnick and Neubauer papers were available at abstract level only. Database queries retrieved semantically related but not mathematically dominating results. The originality assessment is not proof of worldwide uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The minimal nonzero nilpotent conjugacy class is a canonical testing ground for commutators and matrix-generated Lie subalgebras. The result computes the entire natural rank distribution and nonzero-trace fibers in arbitrary dimension, exposes a sharp two-dimensional boundary, and measures a genuine characteristic-two change from \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) to Heisenberg Lie generation. It answers a structural and enumerative question not limited to a handpicked numerical case.

## Limitations and remaining risks

The result does not compute fibers of a prescribed full matrix commutator nor the finer simultaneous-conjugacy orbital decomposition. Specialist historical results not accessible in full might contain an equivalent enumerative statement. The computational configurations are finite corroborations; external review has not been conducted.
