At either nontrivial quarter-turn resonance of the four-cycle entangling gate of a two-qubit stochastic-reset model, the unconditioned stationary concurrence is exactly \(\mathcal C(p)=p(1-p)/(1+(1-p)^2)\) for every \(0<p<1\). It is uniquely maximized by the reset probability \(p=2-\sqrt2\), with peak \((\sqrt2-1)/2\); at the intermediate half-turn the concurrence is identically zero, giving an exact operating point for the previously observed resonance-enhanced entanglement.
