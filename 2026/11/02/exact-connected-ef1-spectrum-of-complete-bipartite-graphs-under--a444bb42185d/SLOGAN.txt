For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(1\le a\le b\), connected EF1 allocation is guaranteed for **all heterogeneous monotone valuations** into \(n\) nonempty bundles exactly when \(n\le a\) or \(b\le n\le a+b\). The new high-agent proof uses greedy singleton picks and opposite-side matching, achieving the stronger \(\mathrm{EF1}_{\mathrm{outer}}\) property; a uniform additive valuation excludes every intermediate count.
