Independent bidirected percolation on a cycle admits an exact finite-size strong-connectivity formula and a complete macroscopic transition: when \(n(1-p_n)\to\lambda\), the ranked SCC sizes converge to a mixture of a single full-size component and ranked Dirichlet spacings indexed by two independent Poisson missing-arc counts. With more numerous but still rare missing arcs, the largest SCC instead obeys a sharp Gumbel law.
