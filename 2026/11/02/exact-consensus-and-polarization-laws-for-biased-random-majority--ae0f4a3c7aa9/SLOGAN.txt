On every complete bipartite social network, synchronous random majority with independent unequal community tie biases has exactly three possible long-run classes, and their blue-consensus, white-consensus, and alternating-polarization probabilities factor into explicit binomial expressions. The expected time to reach a class is at most \(22/3\) rounds uniformly in both community sizes; at exactly balanced initial opinions, opposite tie biases cause polarization with probability tending to one, with a Gaussian critical window.
