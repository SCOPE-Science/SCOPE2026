For uniformly sampled independent Boolean edge constraints on every labelled simple cycle \(C_n\), the exact consistency probability is \(s^n+t^n-4^{-n}-1/(2\cdot8^n)\), with \(s=(17+\sqrt{97})/32\) and \(t=(17-\sqrt{97})/32\). A seven-state integer transfer proof establishes the formula for every \(n\ge3\) and shows that an earlier conjectured final correction is false already at \(n=3\).
