In a fair random-walk bridge, forcing any \(k\) interior zero contacts raises the expected peak count by exactly \(k/4\), regardless of where those contacts occur. Conditioning instead on exactly \(r\) interior returns gives the strictly increasing, discretely convex regression \(\mathbb E[P\mid R=r]=(n-1)/2+r(r+2)/(4(2n-r-2))\) for \(n\ge2\).
