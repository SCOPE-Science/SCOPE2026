The exact variance-optimal Gaussian preintegration direction for every affine quadratic is determined by minimizing a single strongly convex largest-eigenvalue function, with a complete eigenspace certificate even at multiple eigenvalues. For an indefinite homogeneous quadratic, the optimal direction mixes only the most positive and most negative eigenspaces; a rank-two indefinite quadratic becomes a zero-variance integral after one preintegration, unlike the standard leading active-subspace direction.
