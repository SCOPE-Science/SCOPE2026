The closed convex hull of the Gaussian Fock coherent-state overlaps under every nontrivial planar rotation is exactly one initial logarithmic-spiral arc plus its terminal tangent chord. Its area and perimeter admit exact parameter formulas, and the hulls are strictly nested with a sharp small-angle area deficit \(\pi^2\theta+O(\theta^{3/2})\) from the full numerical-range disk along irrational rotations.
