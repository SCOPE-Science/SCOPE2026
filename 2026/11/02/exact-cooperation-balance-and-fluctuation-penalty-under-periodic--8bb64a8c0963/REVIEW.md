The published equation (9) is reduced exactly to the two-payoff representation in RESULT.md. Differentiation of an explicit interior logarithmic coordinate eliminates the cooperator payoff and yields the time-integrated identity. The special two-player payoff weight is independently derived from the published quotient formula, including the removable singularity at unit synergy. The small-forcing coefficient is verified algebraically and by numerical shooting. The proof does not extrapolate finite computations to arbitrary parameters.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2023 study explicitly motivates periodic feedback and gives numerical interior loops; the invariant time-average statistic is therefore a testable missing quantitative prediction, not a generic normalization check. The result holds for every admissible group size and every amplitude of an existing interior periodic orbit, and the pairwise specialization gives an exact finite-amplitude fluctuation penalty. The analytic stability construction makes the strict penalty nonvacuous, with an exact second-order coefficient that quantifies the suppression caused by weak environmental oscillations. There is no claim of global attraction or a novel general-purpose cycle theorem.
