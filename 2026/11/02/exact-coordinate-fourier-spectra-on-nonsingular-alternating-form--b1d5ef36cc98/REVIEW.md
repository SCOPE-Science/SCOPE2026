# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The uniform sampling measure, selected nontrivial coordinate frequencies, congruence-orbit model and parity range are fixed. The proof independently derives the two distinct finite-field character moments using random symplectic bases, decomposes the coordinate Gram by the three Johnson edge eigenspaces and a coupled two-dimensional block, proves strict positivity, and factors the exact crossing polynomial. The pure-Python finite checker reproduced 183 independently counted character moments across four prime-field cases and symbolic trace/crossover tests. These finite checks are supporting evidence, not a replacement for the all-prime-power proof. The rank-four orbit in dimension four is not part of the theorem's main domain.

## Originality — PASS under bounded comparison
The 1975 Delsarte–Goethals alternating-form association scheme provides the underlying rank-sensitive Fourier-transform framework, and its original full text remains inaccessible; this is the principal priority risk. The directly inspected published Plücker-cone result instead concerns decomposable rank-two bivectors, the symplectic-group result concerns form-preserving transformation matrices over the binary field, and the Veronese-cone result concerns symmetric rank-one forms. In even dimensions at least six, the rank-two cone is not the complement of the nonsingular alternating orbit. The de Seguins Pazzis 2015/2016 preprint was inspected in accessible HTML and studies bounded-rank affine subspaces rather than Fourier Riesz spectra. Searches did not find a theorem giving the stated full-rank Gram law and strict dimension-eight upper-mode transition; absence from searches is not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Nonsingular alternating forms make a canonical homogeneous matrix variety; the selected first-coordinate Fourier band is a natural finite sampling and stable-coefficient-recovery problem. The result gives every singular-value square and equality sector for all finite fields, identifies a uniform extremal transition from the Johnson cycle sector in dimension six to the constant-coupled sector from dimension eight, and remains invariant under nonzero per-coordinate frequency choices. It is not merely an eigenvalue table for the full association scheme and does not claim optimality for unrelated frequency masks.

## Limits
The original 1975 source could not be read completely, and no independent audit or independent mathematical validation has been performed. Inaccessible historical priority does not change the exact algebraic proof but limits the originality conclusion to the documented comparison.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
