For every even dimension \(d\ge6\) and every finite field, the constant and one additive Fourier character per off-diagonal coordinate on the nonsingular alternating-form locus have a completely explicit, strictly positive four-sector Gram spectrum. Its sharp upper Riesz mode changes from the Johnson cycle sector to the constant-coupled sector exactly at \(d=8\), independently of field size and the nonzero coordinate-frequency choices.
