In a directed three-patch recruitment network, investing in a single long return link uniquely maximizes linearized growth below a sharp cost threshold, while investing in two reciprocal links uniquely maximizes it above the threshold. Exactly at \(B_*=a c_u^3/c_r^2\), every allocation has the same Perron root, and the least budget for any target growth rate has a closed-form two-branch expression.
