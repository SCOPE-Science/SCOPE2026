For a natural Markov subfamily of tunable-mean skew tent maps, the stationary coordinate autocovariance has an exact rational generating function and a strictly positive closed-form central-limit variance. At \(t=2-\sqrt3\), three decay modes resonate into an uncancelled cubic pole with \(n^2\)-enhanced correlations, and the unique fluctuation-optimal parameter lies strictly below both the resonance and entropy optima.
