The two total-variation regimes of biased probabilistic parking meet in an exact critical window: when \(p_n=1/2+\lambda/\sqrt n\), the last car's preferred-spot law satisfies \(n\|Q_{n,p_n}-U_n\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\to[1+\sqrt{1+32\lambda^2/\pi}]/2\). A signed-measure limit separates two positive boundary atoms from a bias-dependent bulk density and explains the nonlinear crossover.
