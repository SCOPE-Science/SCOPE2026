The included checker tests independent finite examples, but is not used as proof of the infinite limit.

Their finite-product article (arXiv:2401.17520) proves sharp \(\ell^\infty\) rates, not the new sum. Semantic mathematical-record searches for the exact constant, critical fourth moment, and aligned multiple zeros found no equivalent statement; this is not an exhaustive priority proof.
