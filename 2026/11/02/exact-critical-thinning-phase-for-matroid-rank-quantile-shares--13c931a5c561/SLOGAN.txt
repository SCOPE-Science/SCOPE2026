For arbitrary finite populations with matroid-rank preferences, the exact largest feasible quantile level for an independent \(p\)-Bernoulli benchmark is \((1-p)^{n-1}\) when \(0<p\le1/n\), and zero when \(p>1/n\). An exact binomial-tail monotonicity inequality and the existing matroid-rank maximin-share theorem give the sharp boundary, attained or obstructed by uniform matroids.
