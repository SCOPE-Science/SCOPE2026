For symmetric run-and-tumble swimmers confined by sticky hard walls, the accumulated wall forces have a strictly negative cross-covariance, with asymptotic correlation increasing from \(-1\) toward \(-1/2\) as the dimensionless box width grows. The variance rate of the wall-force difference is independent of box width, while large-box and long-observation limits do not commute even though the mean force on either wall vanishes in a wide box.
