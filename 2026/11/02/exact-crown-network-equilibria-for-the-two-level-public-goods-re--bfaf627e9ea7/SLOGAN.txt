For the homogeneous strict public-goods rule "act exactly with \(0\) or \(r\) active neighbors," every pure equilibrium on a crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M_n\), \(r\ge2\), \(n\ge r+2\), belongs to one of three explicit families, giving the exact count \(2+n+\binom n{r+1}+\mathbf1_{\{n=2r\}}\binom{2r}r\). Restoring the deleted perfect matching collapses this count to exactly two, and the crown network exhibits an additional central-binomial equilibrium family precisely when \(n=2r\).
