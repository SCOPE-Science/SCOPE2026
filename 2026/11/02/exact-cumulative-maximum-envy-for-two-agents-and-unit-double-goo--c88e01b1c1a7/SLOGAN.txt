With two agents who identically value each good at either \(u\) or \(2u\), greedily assigning each arrival to the poorer bundle is globally optimal for cumulative maximum envy, in every fixed order. When item order can also be chosen, the exact optimum for \(a\) unit and \(b\) double-unit goods is \(u[b+\lceil a/2\rceil+\mathbf 1_{\{a=0,\ b\text{ odd}\}}]\); this greedy guarantee fails for the two-level alphabet \(\{u,3u\}\).
