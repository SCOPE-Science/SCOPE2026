A common Gaussian anti-Hermitian loss shared by noninteracting qubits produces an exact Curie--Weiss computational-basis law, although the *entire* quantum state remains a separable mixture of products. Connected two-qubit longitudinal correlations scale as \(N^{-1}\), \(N^{-1/2}\), or a positive constant on the three sides of the sharp noise-duration transition \(t=1\).
