Complete dyadic-uniform qubit hypergraph states have exactly \(k=2^j\) nonzero Schmidt coefficients across every cut with at least \(k-1\) qubits on each side, and their entire finite-size spectrum is determined by an explicit \(k\)-by-\(k\) residue matrix. At fixed \(k\), the large-block spectrum has a closed cosecant-square form; as \(k\) subsequently increases, its von Neumann entropy approaches the finite constant \(2.2808853887532333\ldots\) bits despite unbounded Schmidt rank.
