For any fixed number of independent uniform Cayley trees, all pairwise common-edge counts converge jointly to independent \(\operatorname{Poisson}(2)\) variables, yet at every finite size their distinct mixed cumulants vanish on index forests and are strictly positive on each simple index cycle. The exact \(\ell\)-cycle cumulant is \((n-1)/n^\ell+\tfrac{n(n-3)}2(2(n-1)/n^2)^\ell\), exposing a finite-size dependence hierarchy invisible to pair covariances.
