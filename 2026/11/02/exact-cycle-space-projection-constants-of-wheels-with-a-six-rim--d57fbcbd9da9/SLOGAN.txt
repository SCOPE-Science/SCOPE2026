The minimum real \(\ell_1\)-operator norm of a projection from a wheel graph's edge space onto its circulation space is exactly \(\min\{2-1/(n-1),9/5\}\), where \(n\ge3\) counts rim vertices. The constant rises through four rational values and then stabilizes sharply at \(9/5\) once the wheel has six rim vertices; matching circulant projections and exact dual certificates prove the plateau for every larger wheel.
