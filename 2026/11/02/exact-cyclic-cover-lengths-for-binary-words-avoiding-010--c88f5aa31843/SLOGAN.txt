The shortest circular binary word covering every length-\(n\) word avoiding `010` has length \(a_n+a_{n-2}+a_{n-5}-(n-1)\) for all \(n\ge5\), where \(a_j\) counts finite `010`-avoiding words of length \(j\); the exceptional minima are \(8\) and \(14\) at orders three and four. A sharp sink-to-source matching in the de Bruijn graph explains the exact overhead and constructs a covering cycle.
