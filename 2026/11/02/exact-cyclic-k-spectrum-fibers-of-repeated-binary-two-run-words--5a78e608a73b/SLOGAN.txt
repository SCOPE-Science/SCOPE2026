For every repeated binary two-run necklace \((0^a1^b)^t\), an exact totient-weighted binomial formula counts all circular strings with the same complete length-\(k\) substring multiset. Every inverse word is obtained by redistributing excess lengths of sufficiently long runs, and for \(t\ge2\) the exact unique-reconstruction threshold is \(k=1+\max\{a,b\}\).
