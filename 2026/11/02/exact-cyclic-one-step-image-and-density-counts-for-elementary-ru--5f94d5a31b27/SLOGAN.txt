For elementary cellular automaton Rule 18 on every binary cyclic ring, one-step reachability is equivalent to even output weight together with a nonempty alternating class of zero gaps between consecutive ones; this gives an explicit predecessor construction. A closed fixed-weight binomial count yields an all-length rational generating function and image growth rate \(\lambda\approx1.7548776662\), strictly below the full binary-state growth rate \(2\).
