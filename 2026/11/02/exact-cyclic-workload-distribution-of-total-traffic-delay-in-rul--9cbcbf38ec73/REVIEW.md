# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

A min-plus position identity follows directly by induction from the deterministic parallel Rule-184 update. It gives every car's exact cumulative stops as a cyclic maximum of prefix sums of its initial gaps. The nonpositive full-tour sum makes that maximum finite; the unique zero-containing solution of the cyclic Lindley recursion then yields a bijection with restricted workload vectors and weak compositions of the surplus holes. Rooted-car double counting supplies the site-index correction; the staircase inequality proves the sharp maximum. The result is uniform for all integers \(k\ge1\) and \(h\ge0\). Independent direct-state brute-force and gap-prefix implementations agree with the exact enumerator on 32 complete parameter pairs, and the package's `verify.py` prints `VERIFY_OK`.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority qualification

The closest 1997 and 1999 Fukś papers establish local block-preimage ballot laws, density classification and time-dependent mean flow; these are not re-attributed. Jha–Wiesenfeld–Lee–Laval (2025) provide individual-configuration Dyck-path jam-area algorithms and finite-size sampling, but do not present the fixed-particle-count distribution polynomial in the excess holes. Biham–Hertzberg Rabinovich–Katzav (2026) prove single-car stopping distributions in a different random-ensemble regime, not the joint whole-ring total. Relevant scientific records and source texts were compared at the statement/implication level, with no exact covering formula identified. The persistent risk is older queueing/Dyck-path enumerations that may be equivalent under a nonobvious re-encoding. A negative search is not a uniqueness proof.

## Value — PASS

Total integrated delay counts the full space-time area of transient traffic jams and is a motivated system-level statistic in the literature. The theorem computes its entire exact finite-size fixed-density law, not merely a single expectation or a simulation. The universal workload-vector family allows every spare-hole count to be varied by binomial coefficients without repeating the dynamical analysis. Sharp maximum and extremizer classification reveal an exact structural boundary. This is a substantive counting law even though it does not assert a new phase-transition exponent.

## Scope and remaining risks

The proof covers only indexed cyclic binary rings with the number of cars at most half the circumference and synchronous deterministic Rule 184. It does not cover stochastic driving, densities above one half under the same finite-total-delay definition, or an asymptotic limit of the distribution. Older unindexed combinatorial queueing literature could contain an equivalent law. Computational spot checks are not independent infinite verification.
