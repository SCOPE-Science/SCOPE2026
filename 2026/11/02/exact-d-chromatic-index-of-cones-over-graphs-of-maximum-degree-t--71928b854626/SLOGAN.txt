For every finite simple graph \(H\) with maximum degree at most two, the rainbow-diamond edge-coloring number of its cone is exactly \(\max\{n,3e(H),5p(H),6c(H)\}\), where \(n\) is the number of rim vertices and the indicators detect an edge, a three-vertex path, or a triangle/four-cycle component. In particular, every such cone with at least six rim vertices admits a \(D\)-coloring using exactly its maximum degree many colors; smaller cases have precisely classified obstructions.
