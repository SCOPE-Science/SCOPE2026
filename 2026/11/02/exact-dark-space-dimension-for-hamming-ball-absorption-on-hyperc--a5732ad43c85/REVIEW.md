# Scientific review: Hamming-ball quantum absorption

## Correctness — PASS
For \(d\ge6\), \(2\le r\le d\), the exact nondecaying sector is \(\bigoplus_w E_w\cap\ker\Gamma\): norm dissipation and strict positivity of the rates force every nondissipating mode to vanish at all sink sites. Walsh decomposition separates the free cube into distinct energy sectors. Triangular conversion of restricted Walsh rows to subset-incidence rows, followed by Gottlieb's characteristic-zero maximal-rank theorem, yields sector restriction rank \(\binom d{\min\{r,w,d-w\}}\). The remaining binomial summation, Catalan cases, threshold and fixed-radius asymptotics have been independently reconstructed by direct algebra. The included executable checked 280 exact sector ranks, 25 separate Krylov-rank cases, and finite closed-form identities, returning `VERIFY_OK checks=759`. These finite checks do not replace the infinite proof. The real-on-site offset and positive but nonuniform sink strengths do not alter the dark space. A possible failure would require a false inclusion-rank theorem or the wrong dissipative invariant space; both have been explicitly addressed.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, with residual historical-priority risk
Mülken–Blumen [1] and Le–Donati–Severini–Caruso [2] establish quantum trapping and dark-state concepts; Razzoli–Paris–Bordone [4] treat efficiency for a single trap. Gottlieb [3] already proves the subset-inclusion maximal-rank theorem and is explicitly credited; it is not a new lemma. The related Hamming-ball eigenoperator work of Avni–Samorodnitsky [5] concerns an induced-ball adjacency operator rather than losses on the entire cube. The present comparison concerns the whole-cube continuous-time dissipative operator with a radius-at-least-two ball of sinks, simultaneous energy-resolved restriction ranks, arbitrary positive sink strengths, exact absorption fraction and Catalan closing gap. Retrieved mathematical-research records and recoverable prior claims contained radius-zero or radius-one and nonidentical graph geometries, not this entire formula. Those negative searches are not a proof of publication priority. Earlier unindexed use of incidence ranks in quantum transport remains the main risk.

## Value — PASS
The object is the natural spatially distributed trap in a coherent quantum network; its effective transport dimension is not determined by sink count, graph connectivity or one-site results. The classification yields an exact sharp threshold for full absorption and an unexpected Catalan-dimensional residual immediately below that threshold, while at fixed radius the capture probability of an incoherently uniform initial excitation vanishes at a computable exponential rate. It quantifies robustness to arbitrary heterogeneous strictly positive sink rates and supplies an analytically tractable benchmark beyond single-site trapping. This is not a claim about noisy or many-body experiments.
