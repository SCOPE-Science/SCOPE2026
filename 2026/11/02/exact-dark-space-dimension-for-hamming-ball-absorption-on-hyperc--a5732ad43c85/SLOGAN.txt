A continuous-time quantum walker on a binary \(d\)-cube with positive losses at every site of a radius-\(r\) Hamming ball has exactly \(2^d-\sum_{w=0}^d\binom d{\min\{r,w,d-w\}}\) dark dimensions, independently of the unequal sink strengths. Absorption becomes complete precisely at radius \(\lfloor d/2\rfloor\); one radius earlier the dark dimension is a Catalan number for even \(d\) and twice a Catalan number for odd \(d\).
