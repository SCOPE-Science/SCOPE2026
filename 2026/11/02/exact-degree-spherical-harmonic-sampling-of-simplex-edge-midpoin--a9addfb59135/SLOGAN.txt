On the centered edge-midpoint set of every regular simplex with \(n\ge5\) vertices, exact-degree spherical harmonics interpolate arbitrary data in every degree \(k\ge6\). The complete exceptional rank losses occur in degrees \(2\) (all dimensions), \(3,4\) at \(n=8\), and \(5\) at \(n=16\); in particular the \(120\) vertices for \(n=16\) form a non-antipodal harmonic-index-five design.
