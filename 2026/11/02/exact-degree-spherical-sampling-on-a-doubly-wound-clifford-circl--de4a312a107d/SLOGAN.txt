On the equally spaced \((1,2)\)-winding circle in the Clifford torus of \(S^3\), exact-degree spherical-harmonic sampling has rank \(\min\{N,4\ell+1\}\) for degree three and every degree at least five; degrees one, two and four have explicit missing Fourier frequencies. Surjective sampling can fail again in degree four after succeeding in degree three (ranks \(6,5,6\) at degrees three, four, five for six samples).
