For complex homogeneous trinomials on a three-term arithmetic-progression support, a tie between the two largest coefficient magnitudes forces the coefficient Bohnenblust–Hille norm to be at most the polydisk supremum already in every degree \(m\ge3\). This degree cutoff is sharp: the equal-modulus quadratic \(z_1^2+z_1z_2-z_2^2\) violates the constant-one inequality.
