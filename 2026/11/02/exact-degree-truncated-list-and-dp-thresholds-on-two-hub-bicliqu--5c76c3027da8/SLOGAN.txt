For every \(n\ge3\), the exact degree-truncated ordinary-list and DP choice numbers of \(K_{2,n}\) are respectively \(\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor+1\) and \(\lfloor\sqrt{2n}\rfloor+1\). A degree-two neighbor forbids at most one ordinary-list hub-color pair but can exclude two disjoint DP hub-color pairs, producing a sharp asymptotic ratio of \(\sqrt2\).
