For a divisor lattice with selected maximal proper divisors deleted, the exact least integer clearing the rational power-GCD–LCM matrix quotient has a closed prime-local formula. Removing only \(N/p\) multiplies the quotient's least denominator by \(p^e+1\) when \(p^2\mid N\), but requires no multiplier when \(p\) occurs only once and \(N\) has another prime factor.
