For an infinite set of allowed base-\(g\) digit positions with infinite complement, every sum of fewer than \(g\) restricted-digit integers has density zero, while the exactly \(g\)-fold sumset has an explicit natural density given by an infinite product over consecutive allowed-position runs. That density is positive precisely when only finitely many omitted gaps have length greater than one and the exponential reciprocal run lengths are summable; it can be arbitrarily close to one.
