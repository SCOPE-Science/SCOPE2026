For uniformly mixed excitations on a complete bipartite quantum network with a single absorber and local dephasing, the exact mean capture time separates into positive \(\gamma\), inverse-\(\gamma\), and mixed \((2\gamma+\kappa)^{-1}\) terms. The unique optimal dephasing rate strictly decreases with absorber strength, independently of the initial fraction already at the absorber (provided some excitation starts elsewhere).
