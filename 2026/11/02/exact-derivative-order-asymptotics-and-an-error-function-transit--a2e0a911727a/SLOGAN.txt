For boundary power poles in the finite-interval one-diagonal Bergman trace, the sharp derivative-order energy changes from \(n^{2\sigma-7/2}\) on circular arcs to \(n^{2\sigma-3}\) on straight sides; every coefficient is explicit. At the natural circle–line junction, the \(n^{-1/2}\)-scale transition is governed by the exact factor \(e^{16(\eta_+)^2}\operatorname{erfc}(4\eta)\), identifying the sharp blind-spot window of this established one-diagonal test.
