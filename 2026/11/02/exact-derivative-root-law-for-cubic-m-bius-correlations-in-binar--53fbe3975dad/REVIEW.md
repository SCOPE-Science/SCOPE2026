# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The cubic translation \(T\mapsto T+a\) gives one free parameter and the depressed form \(T^3+uT+d\). Its squarefree boundary is exactly \(d\ne0\). The characteristic-two Pellet formula and the explicit cubic Berlekamp expression give \(\mu(T^3+uT+d)=\psi(u^3/d^2)\) when the extension degree is odd. Summation over \(u\) is exact by cubic permutation and character orthogonality; the remaining points are precisely the rational zeros of \(P'\). The bound and the two-, three-, and four-shift cases follow from the square structure of the derivative. Independent direct root-based enumerations agree in 268 shift sets, with 458264 cubic summands. The enumeration is a finite check, not the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Carmon (arXiv:1409.3694v1, 2014) supplies the discriminant formulas and a general upper bound, but the inspected full text does not state the exact all-order cubic constant-shift correlation or its derivative-root criterion. Carmon–Rudnick (arXiv:1205.1599, 2012) assumes odd characteristic. Targeted literature and mathematical-record queries did not identify an equivalent theorem. Odd-characteristic two- and three-shift evaluations do not imply the binary-field formula. Residual risk: the exact identity might occur as an unindexed corollary or in older low-degree calculations.

## Value — PASS
Exact multi-point polynomial Möbius correlations are motivated by Chowla-type cancellation. Here the entire finite-field, arbitrary-shift family becomes a critical-point count; nonnegativity and divisibility by \(q^2\) are automatic, and four shifts exhibit a sharp dichotomy controlled by their sum. These structural consequences are stronger than merely checking a small finite table or a bound.

## Limits and evidence
The proof applies only to monic cubics over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\) for odd \(m\), with distinct constant shifts. Direct testing for \(q=4\) shows the hypothesis on \(m\) cannot be removed unchanged. The literature assessment is not a declaration of historical priority, and the work has not received independent mathematical validation.
