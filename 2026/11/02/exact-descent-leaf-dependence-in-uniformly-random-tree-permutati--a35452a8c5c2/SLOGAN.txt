For a uniform permutation whose inversion graph is a tree, adjacent descents \(D\) and graph leaves \(L\) obey the exact endpoint law \(2D+L-n-1\in\{-1,0,1\}\); their complete conditional distribution is a parity-dependent one- or two-point law. Their finite correlation is \(-\sqrt{(n-3)/(n-1)}\) for \(n\ge4\) and tends to \(-1\), even though both centered statistics have fluctuations of order \(\sqrt n\).
