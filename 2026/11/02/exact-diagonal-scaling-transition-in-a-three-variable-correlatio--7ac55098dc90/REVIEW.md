# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The positive-definite domain follows from an orthogonal eigenbasis. The semidefinite sandwich \(W\preceq C\preceq K W\) rigorously symmetrizes an unrestricted diagonal scaling. The proof gives and orders all three scaled eigenvalues, establishes strict monotonicity of the two governing ratios, identifies their unique transition, and derives both optimum branches. Symbolic identities, one exact rational benchmark, one exact asymptotic limit and finite numerical minimizations from the package were checked; these finite checks are not treated as universal proof. No unproved external lemma is necessary beyond standard spectral and Loewner facts.

**Originality: PASS (qualified).** The 2020 and 2022 preconditioning papers already cover general Jacobi comparisons and SDP algorithms. The later optimality-condition paper treats general matrices, but the inspected statements do not supply this explicit two-parameter formula, eigenvalue-collision boundary or Jacobi-if-and-only-if criterion. The closed form is a special-family analytic refinement rather than a new solution framework for general preconditioning. Published-work searches and available relevant mathematical records did not locate an equivalent claim; absence is not proof of priority. The principal residual risk is an unlocated analytic worked example of this small matrix family.

**Value: PASS.** This small but natural exchangeable correlation family models a pair of equivalent variables linked to a third, and gives a complete explicit benchmark for diagonal-preconditioning algorithms. The threshold separates two mechanisms of optimal spectral balancing. The Jacobi iff equicorrelation criterion demonstrates precisely when unit diagonals can conceal a condition-number improvement; the nontrivial near-singular \(3/2\) limit quantifies it. These facts cannot be recovered by a normalization check alone and are useful for assessing and testing optimal scaling routines.

**Closest sources and limits.** Qu et al., *Optimal Diagonal Preconditioning*, arXiv:2209.00809, and Jambulapati et al., *Fast and Near-Optimal Diagonal Preconditioning*, arXiv:2008.01722; additionally Ghadimi et al., arXiv:2509.23439 (post-source-window, comparison only). The result is exact only for the positive two-parameter SPD family, positive diagonal congruences and spectral condition number. Neither solver-runtime benefits nor a universal upper bound on Jacobi loss is asserted. Independent mathematical and priority review are pending.
