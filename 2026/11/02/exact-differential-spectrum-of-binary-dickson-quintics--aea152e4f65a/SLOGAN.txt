For every \(m\ge2\), the binary Dickson quintic \(D_5(x,a)\) with \(a\ne0\) has an exact two-sum multiplicity spectrum, controlled by a trace condition on the nonzero input differences. It is APN only over \(\mathbb F_4\), and four-uniform over all larger binary extensions; its sumset size and additive energy have closed parity-dependent formulas.
