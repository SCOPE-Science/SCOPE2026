For every odd finite field, the complete product-order distribution of Miyamoto involutions indexed by the primitive axes of a nondegenerate Jordan spin factor has an explicit formula from two quadratic-shell counts. All these involutions form a three-transposition set exactly over \(\mathbb F_3\), or in dimension three over \(\mathbb F_5\) when the orthogonal complement to a unit vector is an anisotropic binary plane.
