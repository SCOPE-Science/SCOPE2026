For three independent arclength-uniform points on a hyperbolic geodesic circle of radius \(R\), the probability that their geodesic triangle is acute is exactly \(\frac14+\frac3{\pi^2}(\operatorname{Li}_2(z)-\operatorname{Li}_2(-z))\), where \(z=\sinh^2R/(2+\sinh^2R)\). This probability increases strictly from \(1/4\) to \(1\), uniquely determines the circle radius, and has explicit small-radius and exponentially sharp large-radius expansions.
