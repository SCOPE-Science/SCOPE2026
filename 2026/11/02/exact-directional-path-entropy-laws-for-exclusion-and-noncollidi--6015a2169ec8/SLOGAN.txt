The two stationary path laws of clockwise exclusion and Doob-conditioned noncolliding particles on a finite ring have exact, explicit but unequal linear-in-time relative entropies. With a fixed number of particles on a growing ring, the noncolliding-to-exclusion entropy rate is of order \(n^{-2}\) while the reverse rate is of order \(n^{-1}\); for two particles, their complete \(n^2\)-time-window divergence limits are explicit.
