# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The original fixed-point equations reduce uniquely and without approximation to occupancy classes on the peripheral pairs; the selected/unselected hub cases give necessary and sufficient floor inequalities. The occupancy multiplicities count all labelled source sets exactly once. The singleton and \(m+1\)-source analysis establishes every minimum and its multiplicity, including equality boundaries and the exceptional triangle. The finite exhaustive independent rational verifier checked the complete enumerator at 202 parameter points, including critical thresholds. Computation is supporting evidence; the unrestricted proof is symbolic.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)

The original 2026 paper by Allagan, Pereyra, and Massey was inspected throughout its mathematical definitions, spider-tree, star and complete-graph sections and its concluding scope. Those results define the invariant but do not give a friendship-graph all-set enumerator or phase diagram; complete graphs only coincide at \(m=1\). The full 2024 Guo–Lin–Wang–Wong–Lin text was inspected for its formulation and proposed pairwise/top-k hitting-probability queries; those queries do not imply minimum-cardinality uniform coverage or enumerate all optimum source sets on friendship graphs. Bibliographic searches for friendship/windmill discounted-hitting domination and related hitting-probability optimization did not identify a stronger covering theorem. Failed search alone is not proof of historical uniqueness; unindexed specialized sources remain a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The parameter was recently introduced to model minimum sets of pinned sources with network-wide discounted support. Friendship graphs are a minimal cyclic family with a high-degree articulation hub, structurally distinct from the previously solved trees and complete graphs. The exact polynomial distinguishes every possible feasible source-set size and yields two additional, strict thresholds where optimizer multiplicities change without changing the minimum size. In particular, it characterizes exactly when hub-free optimum placements exist, a practical and structural phenomenon not visible from the minimum number alone.

## Scope and limits

Only uniform simple random walk, homogeneous \(0<\lambda<1\), and positive floor \(\tau\le1\) are covered. No corresponding claim is made for heterogeneous edge weights or more complicated windmill graphs. The proof and its exact-rational verification are available; external mathematical validation has not been performed. Historical priority remains provisional against unindexed literature.
