On each friendship graph \(F_m=K_1\vee mK_2\), discounted-hitting feasible source sets admit an exact all-cardinality generating polynomial obtained from the numbers of peripheral pairs containing zero, one, or two sources. The minimum source number has sharp thresholds \(\lambda/(2-\lambda)\) and \(\lambda\), while two additional thresholds give the exact number of optimum placements and a nontrivial interval of hub-free optima.
