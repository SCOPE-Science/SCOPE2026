For uniform three-state stationary Markov flows without self-loops, the mean entropy production is exactly \(2\log(3/2)-1/2\), while fixing the stationary probabilities to \((1/3,1/3,1/3)\) raises it to \(1\). This same constraint changes the sharp exponential-moment threshold from \(3\) to \(1\), exposing a strong dependence of predicted irreversibility on the information imposed before sampling transition laws.
