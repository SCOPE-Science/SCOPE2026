For the norm-closed locus of \(k\) orthogonal positive norm-one summands in \(C([0,1])\), distance from any positive norm-one function is exactly an alternating peak–valley minimax, and a nearest point need not exist. The identity function has unattained distance \(1/2\) for every \(k\ge2\), while all such distances converge as \(k\to\infty\) to \(\max\{1/2,\min f\}\).
