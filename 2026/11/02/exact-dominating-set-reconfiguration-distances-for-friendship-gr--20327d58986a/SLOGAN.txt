At the first connected size cap for domination reconfiguration on the friendship graph \(F_r\), a three-type state model yields exact distances between every pair of dominating sets for all \(r\ge2\). The reconfiguration graph has radius \(r+2\), diameter \(2r+1\), exactly \(r+1\) central states and exactly \((r+2)2^r\) peripheral states.
