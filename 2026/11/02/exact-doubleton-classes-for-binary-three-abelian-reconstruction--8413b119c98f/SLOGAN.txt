For binary three-abelian equivalence, the number of length-\(n\) classes containing exactly two words is \(10n-14+2\mathbf1_{2\mid n}+2\mathbf1_{n\equiv1\pmod3}\) for every \(n\ge14\), with the eight smaller nonzero values completely determined. A finite 316-profile certificate, justified by a heavy-cycle reduction, explains the exact linear and periodic behavior of the first nontrivial reconstruction ambiguities.
