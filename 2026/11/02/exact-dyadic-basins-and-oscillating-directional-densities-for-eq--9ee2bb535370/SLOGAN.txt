For equilateral farthest-orthogonal projections on three complete supporting lines, every point approaches one of two three-cycles or reaches one of three midpoint fixed points, with the global basin determined by exact dyadic shell parity. Along any outward half-line, the proportion captured by a given three-cycle has distinct lower and upper asymptotic densities \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), despite convergence of every individual orbit.
