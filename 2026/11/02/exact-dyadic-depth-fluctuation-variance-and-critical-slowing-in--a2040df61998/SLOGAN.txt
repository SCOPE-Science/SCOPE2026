For the stationary Pelikan random map, the centered accumulated dyadic depth satisfies an exact Gaussian limit with variance \(\sigma_p^2=(2+5d^2+24d^3-7d^4)/(4d^4)\), where \(d=2p-1>0\). As the intermittent threshold is approached, the temporal fluctuation strength diverges as \(d^{-4}\) while the one-time depth variance grows only as \(d^{-2}\).
