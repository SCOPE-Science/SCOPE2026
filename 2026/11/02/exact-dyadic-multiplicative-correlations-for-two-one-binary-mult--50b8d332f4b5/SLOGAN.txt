For every binary multiplier \(2^k+1\) with \(k\ge2\), the mixed Thue–Morse correlation over each complete dyadic interval has the rational generating series \(1+2z^{k+2}/((1-z)(1-z^k+2z^{k+1}))\). Its first \(k+1\) nonempty dyadic correlations vanish exactly, and a signed carry-state identity determines every subsequent one.
