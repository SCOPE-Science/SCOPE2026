For every \(k\ge3\), the classical derangement numbers in one full index period modulo \(2^k\) attain each even residue once and each residue congruent to \(1\pmod8\) exactly four times, with no other residues. Their closure in \(\mathbb Z_2\) is \(2\mathbb Z_2\cup(1+8\mathbb Z_2)\), of Haar measure \(5/8\).
