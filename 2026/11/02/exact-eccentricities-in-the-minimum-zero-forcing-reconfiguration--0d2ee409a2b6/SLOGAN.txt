For every gear graph with \(m\ge4\) subdivided rim edges, the token-jumping graph on its minimum zero-forcing sets has \(5m\) states and \(11m\) moves. Its exact radius and diameter are \(\lfloor(3m+3)/4\rfloor\) and \(\lfloor(3m+6)/4\rfloor\), with the eccentricity of every configuration determined by five rotational classes.
