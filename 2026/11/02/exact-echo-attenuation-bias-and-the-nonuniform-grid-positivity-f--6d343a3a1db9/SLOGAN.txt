For free-diffusion Bloch–Torrey echoes, the two first-order Lie-splitting orders have exactly computable attenuation biases \(E\pm J\): they agree for all pulses only on uniform grids, where the echo signal error is exactly second order. On arbitrary grids, explicit tridiagonal positivity tests classify every mesh on which each order can never underattenuate, and the sharp two-step ratio safeguard fails for three steps.
