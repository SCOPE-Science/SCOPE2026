For discrete \(p\)-heat on a bouquet of cliques, individual edge-Lipschitz contraction holds exactly when \(\max_i\{N-r_i-2-(r_i-1)c_p\}\le0\), where \(c_p=1\) for \(p\le2\) and \(c_p=2^{2-p}\) for \(p\ge2\). In particular, two equally sized blades of size \(r\ge3\) have a sharp nonlinear transition at \(p=2+\log_2((r-1)/(r-2))\), beyond which normalized instantaneous edge-gradient expansion is unbounded.
