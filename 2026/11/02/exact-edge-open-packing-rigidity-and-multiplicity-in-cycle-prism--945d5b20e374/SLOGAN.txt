For the labelled cycle prism \(C_n\square K_2\), the largest edge-open packing has \(n\) edges exactly when \(6\mid n\), and otherwise has \(n-1\) edges. The numbers of optima are exactly \(6\), \(4n\), or \(2n\), according to the residue class of \(n\) modulo six, with a finite 17-state exact certificate.
