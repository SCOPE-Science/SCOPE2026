For unit-valued multistars with \(b\ge2\) leaves and \(q\ge2\) parallel goods per leaf, the exact number of all-goods-EFX edge orientations is a binomially weighted coefficient formula with an explicit finite correction. The probability that an independent random orientation is EFX decays exponentially in the number of leaves, with base exactly \(1/2\) for odd \(q\) and \(1/2+\binom{q}{q/2}/2^{q+1}\) for even \(q\).
