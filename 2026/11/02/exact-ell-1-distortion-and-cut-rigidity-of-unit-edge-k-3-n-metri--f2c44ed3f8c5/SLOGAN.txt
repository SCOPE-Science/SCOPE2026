For the unit-edge shortest-path metric on \(K_{3,n}\), \(n\ge3\), the exact least real \(\ell_1\) distortion is \(2-k(2n+1-3k)/(n(n-1))\), where \(k=\lfloor(n+1)/3\rfloor\); its values form a sharp mod-three staircase tending to \(5/3\). Every optimal noncontracting embedding has exactly the same pairwise distances: \(2\) within each part and the sharp distortion across the parts.
