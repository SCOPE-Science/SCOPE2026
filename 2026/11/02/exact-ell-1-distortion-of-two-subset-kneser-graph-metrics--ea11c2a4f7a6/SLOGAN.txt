For every \(n\ge6\), the unit graph metric of the two-subset Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\) has exact least real \(\ell_1\) distortion \(2(n-4)/(n-3)\). An orbit of Hamiltonian-cycle cuts attains the optimum, exactly saturating an incidence-eigenspace negative-type obstruction; the distortions increase from \(4/3\) toward \(2\).
