# Review of the embedding dimension computation

**Correctness — PASS.** Park–Shin's full preprint supplies the analytic construction, formal-function identification, exact graded coefficient spaces and the filtered-algebra lifting lemma. The independent contribution is a seven-generator upper bound obtained from parity-compatible formal lifts, explicit coefficients \(-qz\), and two order-raising product cancellations; the lower bound uses injectivity of \(J_2J_3\to J_5\) to exclude degree-seven cancellation. All quantifiers refer to the one displayed genus-three germ. An exact checker reproduces finite low-degree identities and semigroup cases, but does not replace the general pole-order induction or the published analytic lemmas.

**Originality — PASS with residual risk.** The motivating preprint explicitly proves only the genus-three lower bound six, whereas this result proves exact seven. Search of published published-finding corpus records using genus-three, Wahl defect, nonlinear neighborhood and embedding dimension aliases located no equivalent evaluated claim; the closest local research comparison is the genus-two exact calculation, which has a different pole semigroup and gluing exponent. A hidden general statement in Grauert/Fujiki or an unindexed treatment remains a risk; no uniqueness certification is claimed.

**Value — PASS.** Embedding dimension is the minimal ambient analytic dimension of the new non-quasihomogeneous, non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein zero-defect counterexample. Genus three is the first case where the stated source bound six is not sharp: a previously undetected degree-seven cotangent direction forces seven generators, while unexpectedly the needed two later graded monomials are generated by explicit cancellations. This supplies an exact, structurally explained invariant, not merely another lower bound.

**Scientific limitations.** The argument determines one specified analytic germ; no genus-uniform embedding-dimension formula, equations of its image, or independent review are asserted.
