For the Hecke–Kiselman monoid of an outward-directed star with \(m\) leaves, the number of identity-preserving endomorphisms has an explicit binomial-sum formula valid for every \(m\ge1\). As \(m\) grows it is asymptotic to \(3\cdot2^{m(m+1)}\), with almost every endomorphism lying in one of three equally frequent families determined by which generator images contain the center.
