For unit-rim wheel networks with any common spoke conductance \(a>0\), the symmetric electrical-gradient projection has an exact attained \(\ell^\infty\) norm given by the larger of explicit spoke and rim row formulas. A unique conductance crossover increases toward \(4/3\) with rim size, and the small-conductance/large-wheel limits disagree: \(3\) in one order and \(2\) in the other.
