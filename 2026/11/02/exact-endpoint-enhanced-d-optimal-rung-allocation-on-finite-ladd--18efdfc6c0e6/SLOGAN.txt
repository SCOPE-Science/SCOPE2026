On the finite two-rail ladder with unit rails and total rung budget \(B>0\), the unique allocation maximizing the weighted spanning-tree sum assigns \(u/2\) to each endpoint rung and \(u^2/[2(1+u)]\) to every interior rung, where \(nu^2+2(1-B)u-2B=0\) has exactly one positive root. The exact optimum is \(u(u+2)(1+u)^{n-2}/2\): every rung remains active, and each endpoint receives strictly more conductance than every interior rung.
