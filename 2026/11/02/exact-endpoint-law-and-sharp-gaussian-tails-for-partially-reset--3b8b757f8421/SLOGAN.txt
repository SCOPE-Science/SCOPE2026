For Ornstein–Uhlenbeck diffusion with Poissonian multiplicative resets, the invariant variance has an exact \(w^{\rho/(2\kappa)}\) endpoint law, forcing a Gaussian stationary density tail with a sharp power correction, while the critical Gaussian-square exponential moment is finite exactly when \(\rho>\kappa\). This differs fundamentally in the far tail from the algebraic stationary law of the frequent-weak-reset diffusion approximation.
