For continuous linear splines with alternating positive knot lengths \(a,b\), the exact \(L^\infty\) norm of the line orthoprojector is \((5(a+b)^2+12ab)/(3(a+b)^2+4ab)\), maximized uniquely by the uniform mesh at the sharp value \(2\). Every finite even cyclic mesh has strictly smaller norm, and its norms converge to this same rational envelope as the number of knots grows.
