Fairly randomized drift–kick versus kick–drift symplectic Euler has an exact two-step expected-energy multiplier, and the optimal correlation switches at \(h=\sqrt3-1\) and \(h=\sqrt3+1\). At the special step \(h=\sqrt2\), repeating the same ordering within each pair preserves oscillator energy exactly at every paired endpoint, whereas any nonresonant fair pairing yields exponential mean energy growth.
