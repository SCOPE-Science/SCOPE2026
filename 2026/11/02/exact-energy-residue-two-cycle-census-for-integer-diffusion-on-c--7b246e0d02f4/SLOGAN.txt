In synchronous integer diffusion on every labelled clique, the exact number of primitive two-cycles at conserved total \(S\) is a finite ordered-composition count depending only on \(S\bmod n\). Recurrent configurations have sharp spread \(2n-4\) for \(n\ge4\), and precisely \(n(n-1)\) two-cycles reach this spread when \(n\mid S\), with none doing so at other totals.
