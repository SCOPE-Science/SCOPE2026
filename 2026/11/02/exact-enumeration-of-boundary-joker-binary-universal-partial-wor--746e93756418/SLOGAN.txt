Binary linear universal partial words with one wildcard in the first position have exactly \(2^{2^{n-1}-n+1}\) indexed realizations for every \(n\ge2\). Moving the wildcard to the second position gives, for every \(n\ge4\), exactly \(2^{2^{n-1}-n}(1-(-1/2)^{n-2})/3\) realizations: a forced alternating prefix and a rank-three de Bruijn arborescence correction produce a sharp parity-dependent ratio tending to \(1/6\).
