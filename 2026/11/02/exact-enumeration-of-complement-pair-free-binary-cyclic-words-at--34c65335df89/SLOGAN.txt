The number of marked binary circular words of length \(n\) having no complementary pair of length-four factors is exactly \(4T_7(n)+2T_3(n)-4\), where \(T_r\) obeys a two-lag renewal recurrence. A six-graph classification also shows that both doublets \(00\) and \(11\) first coexist at length \(7\), with exactly \(28\) such marked words.
