Every labeled \(q\)-symbol alphabet with \(q\ge2\) admits a circular word listing each nonpalindromic three-letter word exactly once. The exact number of rotation classes is \(1\) at \(q=2\) and \(q^{q(q-1)/2+q-3}(q-2)^{(q-1)(q-2)/2}(q-1)^{q-1}((q-2)!)^{q^2}\) at every \(q\ge3\), via a complete overlap-graph spectrum and the BEST theorem.
