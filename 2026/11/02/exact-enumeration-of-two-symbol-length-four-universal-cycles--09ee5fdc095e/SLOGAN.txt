For a labeled \(q\)-symbol alphabet, universal cycles containing each length-four word with exactly two distinct symbols once exist for every \(q\ge2\), and their exact number modulo rotation is \(2^{(q-1)(q+2)}q^{q-2}7^{\binom{q-1}{2}}((q-2)!)^q\). A six-state pair gadget reduces the exact Euler-tour enumeration to a complete-graph matrix-tree determinant.
