The number of cyclic words that list every alphabet triple with exactly two equal symbols once and no other triples is exactly \(2^{q-1}3^{(q-1)(q-2)/2}q^{q-2}((q-2)!)^q\) for every alphabet size \(q\ge2\), with rotations identified but symbols labeled. An exact directed matrix-tree reduction exposes the factorization, and every such word uses each symbol exactly \(3(q-1)\) times.
