For strictly positive Markov-switching population matrices sharing a positive left Perron vector, exact environment-specific stochastic-growth sensitivities are determined by one finite-dimensional block Perron eigenvector, even when the matrices do not commute. In iid environments, the proposed frequency-proportionality law for all states holds exactly when every environment has the same Perron root; a rational correlated example shows that temporal correlation changes the sensitivities without changing baseline growth.
