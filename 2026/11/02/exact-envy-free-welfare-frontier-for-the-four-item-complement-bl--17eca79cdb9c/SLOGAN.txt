For the four-good complement-block family underlying the 2026 binary SPOC welfare obstruction, the exact maximum welfare under ordinary envy-freeness **equals** that under EFX: \(t+\lfloor t/n\rfloor\) versus unrestricted welfare \(2t\). Permitting deletion of up to \(\ell\) goods instead gives exactly \(t+\min\{t,\lfloor(t+(n-1)\ell)/n\rfloor\}\), so relaxing to EF1 gains exactly one welfare unit when \(n
mid t\).
