For every nontrivial finite abelian group \(G\), the full constant-free equational probability spectrum of \(G\) with an absorbing zero is an explicit three-integer family controlled by \(|G|\) and the power-image sizes \(|dG|\). Its sharp minimum is \(1/(|G|+1)\), its Cantor--Bendixson rank is three, and it separates \(C_4\) from \(C_2\times C_2\) after zero-adjoining.
