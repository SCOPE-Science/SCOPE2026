A three-site periodic cubic Nagumo ring has exactly 27, 21, 15, and finally 3 real equilibria as its diffusion crosses an explicit algebraic fold and the threshold \(d=1/3\). Its six pinned heterogeneous sinks disappear at the unique root \(d_*\approx0.1350681582114\) of \(108d^4-36d^2+12d-1=0\), leaving only the two homogeneous sinks.
