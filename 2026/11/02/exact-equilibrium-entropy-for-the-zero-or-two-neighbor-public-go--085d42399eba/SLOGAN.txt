For the strict zero-or-two-neighbor binary public-goods game on \(C_3\square C_n\), the exact equilibrium count is a trace of an explicit \(64\)-state transfer matrix with a fully factored characteristic polynomial. The count grows as \(\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\approx1.46557123\) is the positive root of \(z^3-z^2-1\): an exact entropy benchmark at the first four-regular strip where the best-response rule ceases to be parity-linear.
