For balanced diversity-seeking jump games on the odd cycle \(C_{2m+1}\) with one vacancy, every equilibrium consists exactly of alternating monochromatic runs of length at most two, with single-letter runs at the vacant vertex's two neighbors and opposite endpoint colors. The welfare spectrum is \(2k\), with exactly \(2(2m+1)\binom{k-1}{m-k}^2\) colorings at level \(2k\), yielding a sharp price of anarchy \(m/\lceil(m+1)/2\rceil\) and a parity effect absent from the published line-topology formula.
