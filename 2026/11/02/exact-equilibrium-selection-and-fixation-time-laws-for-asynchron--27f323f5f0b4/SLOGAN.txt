For asynchronous best-shot play on \(K_{a,b}\), the probability that the first part ultimately supplies the public good is exactly \(i/(i+j)\) when the parts initially contain \(i,j>0\) producers, independently of their sizes. A survivor-count law gives the exact mean number of random updates until equilibrium; from an all-producing balanced start on \(K_{m,m}\), this mean is \(4m\log m+(4\gamma-2\log 2)m+o(m)\).
