In normalized binary fair division with at least three agents, if every good is approved by at most two agents, a complete allocation simultaneously maximizes the exactly equitable common utility and satisfies EF1 (indeed full envy-freeness at even approval degree). When every good has exactly two approvers, the welfare price of exact equitability is exactly zero for even approval degree and half the number of agents for odd approval degree; efficient EF1+EQ1 nevertheless always exists.
