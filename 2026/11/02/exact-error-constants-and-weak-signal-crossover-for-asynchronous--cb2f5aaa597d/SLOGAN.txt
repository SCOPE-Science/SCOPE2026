In private-signal asynchronous majority learning on a star, the exact erroneous-consensus probability is a beta-binomial mixture controlled by the hub's relative activation rate. For fixed accuracy \(p>1/2\), its sharp leading constant is \(2\rho p(1-p)/(2p-1)^2\) divided by the number of leaves, while a \(1/\sqrt m\) accuracy advantage produces an explicit nonzero Gaussian–beta crossover; speeding up the hub strictly worsens accuracy when there are at least two leaves.
