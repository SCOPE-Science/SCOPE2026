The \(26\)-vertex point-line incidence graph of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,3)\) has exactly \(54{,}756\) minimum error-detecting open-locating-dominating sets, all formed by independently omitting a nonconcurrent triple of lines and a noncollinear triple of points. Their complete cardinality polynomial is \((234z^{10}+78z^{11}+13z^{12}+z^{13})^2\), and the minimum is \(20\), compared with \(18\) for the weaker redundant variant.
