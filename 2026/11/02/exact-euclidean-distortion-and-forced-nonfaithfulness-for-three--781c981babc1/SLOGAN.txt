For the three-subset Kneser graph \(KG(n,3)\), \(n\ge8\), the exact real-Hilbert metric distortion is \(\sqrt3\) at \(n=8\) and \(2\sqrt{1-1/\binom{n-4}{2}}\) for all \(n\ge9\). Uniquely at \(n=8\) every optimum necessarily distinguishes two types of graph-distance-two pairs, whereas at \(n=9\) both faithful and nonfaithful optima exist and for \(n\ge10\) all optimally normalized embeddings have the same pairwise distances.
