The real norm \(\max\{\|x\|_\infty,t|\sum_i x_i|\}\) has an exact all-dimensional Euclidean Banach–Mazur distortion formula for \(t\ge1\). Narrowing the symmetric diagonal slab strictly increases optimal distortion in even dimensions but strictly decreases it in odd dimensions, and the optimal permutation-invariant Hilbertian quadratic form is explicit.
