# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The proof verifies the exact cubic specialization; positive fixed points; explicit upper inverse boundary; invariant middle interval; no-prime-two factorization; and sharp existence of prime-two cycles above the endpoint. Coppel's classical interval-iteration theorem converts the absence of prime-two points to convergence of all middle-interval orbits. Instability of the lower positive fixed point rules out any exceptional middle orbit converging there. Strict monotonicity outside the middle interval proves extinction. The extinction fraction and parameter monotonicity follow from exact interval lengths and a derivative of a closed expression. Symbolic identities and finite tests are provided as reproducibility corroboration, not infinite proof.

## Originality (PASS under inspected evidence)
The full HTML text of Pires–Andrade–Herrmann's June 2026 preprint was inspected; it treats fixed points, stationary phase boundaries, temporal exponents and onset of chaos, not an exact all-initial-states basin partition and its uniform-reference-measure extinction probability. Searches for the cubic map under beta-density, Allee-map, and two-sided basin aliases found Aleixo–Rocha–Pestana (2009), which already uses the same cubic profile, and Rocha–Taha (2019), which discusses broad global Allee dynamics. Neither accessible abstract establishes the exact two-sided basin/length formula. Their full text could not be read; historical priority remains a specific risk, not a claim of global uniqueness. No claim of novelty is made for Coppel's theorem, the cubic map or elementary fixed point formulas.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
In a biologically motivated deterministic population map, a *large* initial density can also lead to extinction. The theorem gives the complete survivor interval for every initial state, an exact uniform-initial-condition extinction fraction, and the sharp boundary beyond which a nontrivial period-two orbit exists. The fraction is motivated by measuring relative sizes of the competing attraction basins, not an arbitrary numerical invariant.

## Scope and risks
The window is a proper one-parameter slice of the 2026 family; chaotic and periodic basins above the sharp two-cycle onset are not classified. The literature comparison is incomplete for the full 2009 and 2019 papers and is qualified accordingly. The uniform initial distribution is a reference measure, not a random demographic process. Finite computational checks cannot prove infinite convergence.
