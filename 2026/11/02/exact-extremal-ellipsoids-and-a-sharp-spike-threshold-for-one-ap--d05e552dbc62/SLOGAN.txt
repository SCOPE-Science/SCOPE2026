For \(K_{d,a}=\operatorname{conv}(B_2^d\cup\{ae_1\})\), the unit ball remains the unique maximum-volume inscribed ellipsoid exactly until the one-point spike reaches \(a=d\); beyond that sharp threshold its John ellipsoid shifts and stretches with explicit axial and transverse semiaxes. The unique enclosing ellipsoid also has an explicit formula in every dimension \(d\ge2\), determined by its apex and a single spherical contact latitude.
