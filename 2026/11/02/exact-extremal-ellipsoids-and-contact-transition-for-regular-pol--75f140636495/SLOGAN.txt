For the convex hull of a Euclidean unit ball and a coplanar regular \(m\)-gon with \(m\ge5\), both extremal ellipsoids are explicit in every dimension \(d\ge3\). The John ellipsoid leaves the unit-ball regime exactly when the polygon's inradius exceeds \(\sqrt{d/2}\), with a sharp \(C^1\)-but-not-\(C^2\) transition in the enclosing-to-inscribed volume ratio.
