In the monoid of order-preserving self-maps of an \(n\)-point chain, the largest possible number of semigroup inverses obeys the exact recurrence \(M_{n+3}=9M_n\) for \(n\ge4\), with all maximizing ranks and their multiplicities determined. The inverse graph also has exactly \(\binom{n+r-1}{2r-1}^{2}\) ordered edges within rank \(r\), quantifying the strong degree nonuniformity behind recent permutation-matching results.
