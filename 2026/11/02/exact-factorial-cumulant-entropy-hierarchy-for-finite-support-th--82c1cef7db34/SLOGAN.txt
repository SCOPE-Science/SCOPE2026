For every fixed finitely supported non-Poisson count law, the first nonzero factorial cumulant after the mean determines the exact power and leading coefficient of Poisson-thinning relative entropy, chi-square divergence, and total variation. Explicit finitely supported laws realize every even relative-entropy decay exponent \(n^{-2m}\) for integers \(m\ge2\), with sharp constants \(1/[2(m+1)!]\).
