In the generalized-star triples \(\langle a n(n-1)+1,a n(n+1)+1,a(n+1)(n+2)+1\rangle\) with \(a\ge4\), \(n\ge2\), and \(2n+1\mid3a+4\), every element has an explicit full arithmetic progression of factorization lengths, spaced by \(2a\). A modular two-coin formula gives the exact number of factorizations at each length, and the delta set is \(\{2a\}\).
