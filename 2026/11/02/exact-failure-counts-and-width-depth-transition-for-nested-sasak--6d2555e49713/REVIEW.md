The mutually annihilating projections \(U,V,S\) diagonalize both; their diagonal entries supply the two closed counts. The exponential scaling follows from dividing by total assignments and estimating uniformly vanishing corrections. The embedded direct-operation checker reproduced every tested full and atomic count at the stated finite parameters. Finite tests are supporting checks, not the general proof.
