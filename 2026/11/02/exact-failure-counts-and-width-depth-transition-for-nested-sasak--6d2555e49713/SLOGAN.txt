Nested Sasaki weakening on the horizontal sum of \(m/2\) four-element Boolean blocks has exactly \(m^{d+1}-m^d-m2^{d-1}\) failing full valuations, but \((m-1)^{d+1}+(-1)^d-\frac m2(1+(-1)^d)\) failing atomic valuations at depth \(d\ge1\). When \(d/m\to t\), their failure probabilities converge to \(e^{-2t}\) and \(e^{-t}\), quantifying how nesting restores almost-sure truth at fixed width while growing width undermines it at fixed depth.
