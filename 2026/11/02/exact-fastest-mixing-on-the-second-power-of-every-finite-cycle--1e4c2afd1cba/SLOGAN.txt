For every finite cycle with jumps of at most two steps, the globally fastest symmetric stochastic walk has explicit optimal holding, one-step, and two-step probabilities given by three-point discrete Chebyshev equioscillation. Its spectral gap is asymptotically \(8\pi^2/n^2\), four times that of the optimally weighted one-step cycle, while the essential one-step weight decays as \(n^{-2}\).
