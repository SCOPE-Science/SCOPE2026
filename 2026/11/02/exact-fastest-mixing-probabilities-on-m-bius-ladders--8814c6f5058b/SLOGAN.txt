For the Möbius ladder \(M_{2m}\), the globally fastest symmetric chain has exact parity-dependent rim and antipodal-rung transition probabilities, uniquely determined among rotation-invariant chains. Optimally weighting the rungs quadruples the asymptotic spectral gap over the bare cycle although each rung probability is only \(3\pi^2/(4m^2)+O(m^{-4})\).
