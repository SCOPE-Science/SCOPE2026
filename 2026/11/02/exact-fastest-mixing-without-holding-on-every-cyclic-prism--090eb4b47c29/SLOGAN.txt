For a symmetric random walk on a cyclic prism with every holding probability forbidden, the sharp fastest-mixing factor is exactly \(1\) on even prisms and \((4-\varepsilon_n)/(4+\varepsilon_n)\) on odd prisms, where \(\varepsilon_n=2-2\cos(\pi/n)\). Odd prisms have a unique symmetric edge-orbit optimizer, and their optimal spectral gap is asymptotically four times smaller than when holding is allowed.
