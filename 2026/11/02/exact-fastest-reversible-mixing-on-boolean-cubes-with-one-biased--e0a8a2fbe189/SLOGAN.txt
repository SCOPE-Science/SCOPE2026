On a Boolean cube with one Bernoulli-biased coordinate, the exact globally fastest nearest-neighbor reversible Markov chain has second-largest eigenvalue modulus \(\mu_*=[d-1-4dab+\sqrt{(d-1)^2+16dab}]/[2(d+1-2dab)]\), where \(a+b=1\) and the other \(d\) coordinates are fair. Every nonzero bias strictly improves the optimum over the uniform cube, and the optimal fair-coordinate flip probabilities depend on the state of the biased coordinate.
