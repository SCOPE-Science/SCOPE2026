For complete indexed cyclic sliding-weight reads of binary length-\(n\) words, \(d=\gcd(n,\ell)\) controls every inverse fiber: the exact zero-error capacity is \((2^{n/d}-1)^{d-1}(2^{n/d}+d-1)\), and the full fixed-weight capacity has an explicit polynomial generating function. All ambiguities arise by redistributing all-one residue classes among otherwise fixed residue-class patterns, giving exact binomial list sizes and a sharp arithmetic rate transition.
