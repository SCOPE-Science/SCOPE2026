The full image and every fiber size of \(x\mapsto x^q+a/x\) on \(\mathbb F_{q^2}^\times\) are determined for all nonzero coefficients and all prime powers. A norm-square dichotomy for extension-field coefficients yields three distinct odd-characteristic regimes and a sharp two-regime binary classification, with nonzero-target bijections occurring only at \(q=2\) or \(q=3\).
