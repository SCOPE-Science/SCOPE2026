For every odd prime-power \(q\equiv2\pmod3\), the complete histogram of the zero-, one-, two-, three- and four-preimage targets of \(x\mapsto x^4+ax\) (\(a\ne0\)) is determined by \(q\bmod24\), independent of \(a\). Exactly one target has three preimages when \(-2\) is a square, and none otherwise; the previously known prime-field value-set size is not claimed as new.
