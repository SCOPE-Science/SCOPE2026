For the natural cubic map \(x\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q}(x)\operatorname{N}_{\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q}(x)\), all fibers have size \(q\) exactly when \(3\nmid(q-1)\); otherwise their nonzero-target squared deviations sum to exactly \(2q(q-1)\). The map is surjective for every prime power except \(q=4\), where it misses precisely the value \(1\).
