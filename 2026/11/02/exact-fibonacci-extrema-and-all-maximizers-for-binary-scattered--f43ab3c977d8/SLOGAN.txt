For every binary word length, the greatest number of distinct nonempty palindromic scattered subsequences has the exact Fibonacci form \(M_{2m}=2F_{m+3}-4\), \(M_{2m+1}=F_{m+5}-4\). Odd-length maximizers are exactly the alternating words; at positive even lengths, the maximizers are exactly the alternating words and the centrally doubled alternating palindromes.
