# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The proof counts unsatisfied bonds from the down-spin induced subgraph, establishes \(U\ge n+M\) with equality precisely for six triangular-rung types satisfying a finite compatibility graph, and exhausts the intervals separated by \(|h|/J=2,4\). The critical-field transfer spectra follow by explicit eigenvalue decompositions. The odd-length zero-field kink enumeration and odd-length small-positive-field isolated-double-rung enumeration are exact. Independently implemented full-spin enumeration on four tube lengths and exact min-plus transfers on 28 lengths match all six sample fields; these finite tests corroborate, rather than replace, the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

Kawano–Takahashi (1997) concerns a *quantum Heisenberg* tube; Lajkó–Sindzingre–Penc (2011) adds plaquette projectors and four-spin exchange. Strečka et al. (2015) couple Heisenberg triangles via triangle-total-spin Ising terms, and work at zero applied field. Jurčišinová–Jurčišin (2014) concerns a zigzag, multisite ladder, and only its accessible abstract was compared. None of the examined source statements implies the exact all-field classical \(C_3\square C_n\) multiplicities; published results in narrower or differently coupled models are not represented as covering this claim. Specialized literature remains a historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The complete classification gives the exact parity-sensitive ground-state count, the one-third plateau's extensive residual entropy \((\ln 2)/3\) per spin, and two distinct critical exponential growth constants. This resolves the natural finite-circumference and magnetic-boundary counting question for a standard frustrated three-leg geometry, including cases invisible in thermodynamic-only phase sketches.

## Limits

Equal antiferromagnetic rung/leg bonds and periodicity in both directions are essential. No claims about unequal couplings, thermally excited states, transverse quantum fields or independent historical certification are made. The 2014 zigzag-ladder full text was unavailable; the accessible abstract and its different geometry were evaluated, without claiming a whole-text exclusion.
