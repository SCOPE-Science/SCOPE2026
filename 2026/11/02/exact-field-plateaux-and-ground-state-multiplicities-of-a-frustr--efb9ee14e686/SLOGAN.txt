The classical antiferromagnetic Ising tube \(C_3\square C_n\) has an exact all-field ground-state classification: a one-third-magnetization plateau with \(2^n+2(-1)^n\) labelled ground states, algebraic-exponential multiplicities at both critical fields, and an odd-length low-field degeneracy of \(n(n^2-1)/4\). All energies and multiplicities follow from an exact combinatorial proof for every \(n\ge3\).
