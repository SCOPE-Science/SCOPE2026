For the antiferromagnetic Ising model on an \(m\)-by-\(n\) rook graph with independently positive row and column couplings, the complete zero-temperature magnetic staircase has exactly \(m+n-\gcd(m,n)+1\) strict plateaux. Every ground configuration is a doubly balanced binary matrix, yielding exact critical degeneracies and a three-orbit formula for spatial correlations within each fixed-magnetization ground sector.
