The exact joint distribution of \(\nu_2\!\left(\binom{2n}{n}\right)\) and \(\nu_2\!\left(\binom{3n}{n}\right)\) over every dyadic block \(0\le n<2^L\) has a generically minimal three-state carry transfer and an explicit rational generating function. Its finite-block covariance is \(5L/12-4/9+(9+7(-1)^L)/(36\,2^L)\), including an alternating correction beyond the previously known leading asymptotics.
