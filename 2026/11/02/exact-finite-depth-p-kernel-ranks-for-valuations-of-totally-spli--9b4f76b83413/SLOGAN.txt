For monic irreducible \(Q\in\mathbb Z[X]\) of degree \(d\ge2\) that splits into distinct linear factors modulo \(p\), the rational span of the \(p\)-kernel of \(\nu_p(Q(n))\) through depth \(e\) has dimension \(1\), \(d+1\), or \(de+2\) according as \(e=0\), \(e=1\), or \(e\ge2\). This exactly quantifies an established qualitative nonregularity phenomenon and shows the depthwise rank slope equals the algebraic degree.
