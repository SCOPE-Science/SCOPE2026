For a graph evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\), a normal-basis argument converts all fixed-point and preperiod counts of the quadratic squaring map into ranks of powers of the graph's binary adjacency matrix. For complete multipartite graphs this yields explicit formulas for every cycle length and transient depth, with a sharp extension-degree parity effect: the map on \(K_{1,1}\) has four fixed points over \(\mathbb F_4\) but only two over \(\mathbb F_8\).
