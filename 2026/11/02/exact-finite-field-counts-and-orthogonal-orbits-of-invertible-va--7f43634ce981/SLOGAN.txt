For every odd finite field, invertible-value derivations of a nondegenerate spin-factor Jordan algebra are counted by anisotropic two-planes: exactly \(q-1\) maps per plane, with explicit formulas in all dimensions and \((q-1)/2\) orthogonal conjugacy orbits whenever such planes exist. The calculation also gives an explicit finite-perfect-field exception to the literal wording of Corollary 8 in arXiv:1511.00742v2; its image criterion in Lemmas 6–9 is acknowledged as prior work.
