In dimension two, exactly one-third of uniformly chosen invertible finite-field evolution-algebra structure matrices have no nonzero idempotent, for every prime power \(q\). The complete odd-characteristic idempotent-count law is \(\big(\tfrac13,\tfrac{q^2+1}{2q(q+1)},\tfrac{q-1}{q(q+1)},\tfrac{q^2-2q+3}{6q(q+1)}\big)\) for \(0,1,2,3\) nonzero idempotents, obtained by counting projective-square fibers and three-point incidences.
