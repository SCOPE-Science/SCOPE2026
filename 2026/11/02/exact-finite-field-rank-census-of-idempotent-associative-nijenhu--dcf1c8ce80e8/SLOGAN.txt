For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), idempotent associative Nijenhuis operators on \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\) have the exact rank polynomial \(1+q^2(q+1)(T+T^3)+2q(q+1)(2q+1)T^2+T^4\). This counts every splitting subalgebra projection, including the quadratic-field and characteristic-two cases, rather than merely conjugacy types.
