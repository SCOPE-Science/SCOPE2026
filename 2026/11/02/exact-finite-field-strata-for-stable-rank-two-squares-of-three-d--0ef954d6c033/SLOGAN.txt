For labeled three-dimensional evolution algebras over any finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), conditional on a two-dimensional stable square, its multiplication-kernel quadratic has an exact split/double/irreducible distribution. The stable square itself is an evolution algebra with probability \(\frac{q^4+5q^3-q^2+q}{2(q^4+2q^3-q+1)}\), tending to \(\frac12\) as \(q\to\infty\).
