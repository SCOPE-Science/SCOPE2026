In fully occupied two-color cycle Schelling swap games with binary integration utility, the exact worst equilibrium integrates \(b+\lceil b/2\rceil+1\) people and the exact optimum integrates \(\min\{b+r,3b\}\), for every minority size \(1\le b\le r\). A closed binomial-sum polynomial counts every equilibrium at every welfare level; this strengthens previously known asymptotic bounds without claiming those bounds as new.
