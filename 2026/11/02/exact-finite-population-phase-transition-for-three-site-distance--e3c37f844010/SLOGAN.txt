For Global Pair two-facility location with exactly three occupied points on a line, the sharp finite-population distortion changes form at \(n=15\): smaller populations are worst in a collapsing-gap limit, while larger populations attain their worst case at equally spaced sites with a singleton end group. The exact bound is \(\max\{3(n-2)/(n-1),\max_{a+b=n-1}4/(1+1/a+2/b)\}\) for positive integer group sizes.
