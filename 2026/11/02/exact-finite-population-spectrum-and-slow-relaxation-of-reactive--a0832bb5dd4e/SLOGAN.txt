Every noncollinear zero-mean polygon of a finite, identical Stuart–Landau ensemble with nonzero purely reactive mean-field coupling is normally attracting, with an explicit four-eigenvalue collective factor and exactly \(M-2\) neutral and \(M-2\) radial directions. The weakest collective decay vanishes universally as \(-\beta(1-q^2)/2\) near collinearity, independent of the coupling strength and ensemble size.
