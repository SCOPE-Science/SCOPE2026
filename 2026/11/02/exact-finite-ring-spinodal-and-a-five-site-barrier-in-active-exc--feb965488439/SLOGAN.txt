In the one-dimensional active-exclusion mean-field ring, the homogeneous state becomes linearly unstable at a finite activity rate if and only if the occupancy exceeds the sharp finite-size barrier \(\phi>1/[2\cos^2(\pi/N)]\). Three- and four-site rings can never cross this boundary, whereas five sites suffice: at occupancy \(4/5\) without unbiased hopping the exact threshold is \(a/r=25(2+\sqrt5)\).
