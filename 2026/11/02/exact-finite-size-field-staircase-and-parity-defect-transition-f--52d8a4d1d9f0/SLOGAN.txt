The zero-temperature antiferromagnetic Ising model on the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) has an exact all-field ground-state classification by nearly regular degrees and one parity-forced exceptional vertex. Immediately below saturation, odd \(n\) exhibits a sharp \(P_3\)-plus-matching to isolated-vertex-plus-matching switch at \(h=2J(n-3)\), whereas even \(n\) has only perfect-matching ground states in the corresponding window.
