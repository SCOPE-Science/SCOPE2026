For finite-spin \(SU(2)\) coherent-state concentration, the complete area-preserving spherical-cap shape Hessian is an explicit, strictly increasing sequence of binomial-tail Fourier multipliers. Only first harmonics are quadratic null directions; the second harmonic has the sharp smallest positive curvature \(N(N+1)(1-p)^{N-1}(2-p)/(2+(N-2)p)\), and the spectrum converges to the Gaussian disk Hessian in the high-spin limit.
