For exact cyclic best-response updates on a directed ring with heterogeneous positive strategic-substitution weights, the product gain \(W\) and ring parity determine the full sweep dynamics. Below \(W=1\) the unique Nash equilibrium attracts geometrically; at or above \(W=1\), every trajectory enters an exact fixed state or a period-two orbit in finite sweeps, with no nontrivial cycles on even rings and at most one primitive two-cycle on supercritical odd rings.
