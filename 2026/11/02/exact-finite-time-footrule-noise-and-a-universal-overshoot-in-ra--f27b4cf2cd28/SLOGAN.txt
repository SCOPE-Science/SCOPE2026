For a lazy random-transposition shuffle, Spearman-footrule fluctuations approach equilibrium from above despite monotone relaxation of their mean: an exact all-deck variance formula proves strict overshoot at every deck size \(n\ge4\). On the \(t\asymp n\) scale, the unique universal peak is \(18.488377\ldots\%\) above stationary variance, occurring at \(t\sim0.75305649\,n\), far earlier than total-variation cutoff.
