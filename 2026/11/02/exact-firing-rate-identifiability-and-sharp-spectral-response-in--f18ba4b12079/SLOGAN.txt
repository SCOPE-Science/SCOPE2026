In a weakly reinforced graph-urn process, exact long-run edge weights identify every vertex firing rate precisely when the connected graph is nonbipartite; bipartite graphs have one explicit positive ambiguity parameter. On cycles, the rate-to-limit derivative has a sharp Fourier gain crossover at \(\alpha=[1+\cos(\pi/n)]^{-1}\), with an exactly invisible alternating modulation on even cycles.
