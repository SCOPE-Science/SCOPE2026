On two cliques joined by one bridge, three-state synchronous Greenberg–Hastings excitation has exact formulas for every extinction and three-cycle first-entry layer, with a surprising mirror symmetry across the six nonrecurrent three-cycle layers. Every state reaches a fixed point or three-cycle within six updates, while the exact number of three-cycle orbits is \(3AB+A+B\), where \(A=3^{a-1}-2^a+1\) and \(B=3^{b-1}-2^b+1\).
