Uniform relocations in a deterministic clockwise scan turn first self-repetition into a circular arc-packing problem with an exact rising-factorial survival law. At relocation frequency \(q_n\asymp 1/n\), the normalized repetition time has a truncated Rayleigh-type law with a genuine atom at one full lap; above that scale it obeys a Rayleigh birthday law with coefficient \(1-q_\infty/2\).
