For the Hardy quasi-Banach space \(H^{1/2}\), the exact sharp norm of the mixed first--second Taylor coefficient functional \(a_1+\lambda a_2\) for every \(\lambda>0\) is given by the unique positive root of an explicit quartic, with one outer quartic extremizer up to phase. The resulting support curve is strictly convex and connects the known separate sharp coefficient constants while selecting their complete positive-weight tradeoff.
