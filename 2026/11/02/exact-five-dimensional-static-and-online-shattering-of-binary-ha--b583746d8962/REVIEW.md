# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The argument reduces every positive Hamming-two fiber to two- and four-set edge queries. A near-star fiber has one branch of size at most three, every two-star fiber has online dimension at most three, and the all-edge fiber has dimension at most four; therefore the full class has online dimension at most five. An explicit, separately replayed table witnesses all 32 patterns on five fixed queries in seven coordinates, extending by zero-padding to every larger dimension. The sample-fiber code is a smoke test rather than the basis of the infinite upper bound.

**Originality — PASS under accessible comparisons.** The closest original 2020 preprint and its accepted full text concern *distance-one* Hamming adjacency, with a VC ceiling of three; their description of second neighborhoods does not establish exact-distance-two neighborhood shattering. The earlier triangular-Johnson and higher-Johnson neighborhood claims use adjacency on constant-weight subsets; they do not include the four-set containment query that appears in this reduction, nor imply a five-dimensional exact-distance-two result. General online-learning closure estimates do not produce the stated sharp dimension. Searches of semantic research records and public primary literature found no covering claim, but historical priority cannot be certified by negative searches.

**Value — PASS.** The work gives a tight dimension-independent static **and** online classification for a canonical second Hamming shell with exponentially many centers, and distinguishes its behavior from ordinary Hamming adjacency. A short reusable fiber decomposition yields the bound for every ambient dimension, not merely a small finite census. This is a natural next invariant of the Hamming association scheme, with a sharp lower certificate.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Adler–Benediktsson–Macpherson (arXiv:2007.16042) provides the distance-one baseline; Ghazi–Golowich–Kumar–Manurangsi (2021) provides general Littlestone closure theory. Research records on Johnson neighborhoods also concern different predicates. A comprehensive historical priority check remains open, including terminology related to halved cubes and the binary Hamming association scheme. No extension to nonbinary alphabets or radii other than two is claimed.
