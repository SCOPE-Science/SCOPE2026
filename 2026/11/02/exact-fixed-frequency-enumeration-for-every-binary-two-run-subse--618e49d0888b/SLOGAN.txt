Every positive, attainable scattered-subsequence frequency for a binary two-run pattern \(0^a1^b\) has an exact rational length generating function with denominator \((1-z)^{a+b}\) and positive numerator at \(z=1\), so its eventual counting polynomial has the maximum possible degree \(a+b-1\). A finite nondecreasing-zero-profile formula gives that numerator for all \(a,b,k\ge1\), including the exact count \(B_{2,2}(n,5)=\binom{n-3}{3}\) for \(n\ge6\).
