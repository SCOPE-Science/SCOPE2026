For binary majority updating on the three-row torus \(C_3\square C_n\), the deterministic incumbent-tie rule has exactly \(A_n=\operatorname{tr}(T^n)\sim3^n\) fixed colorings for an explicit 64-state transfer matrix, while fair random tie-breaking has exactly \(B_n=\varphi^n+\widehat\varphi^{\,n}+2\cos(n\pi/3)\sim\varphi^n\) singleton absorbing colorings. The exponential contrast shows that even the random-tie rule retains exponentially many absorbing singletons in this narrow four-regular torus, unlike on a simple cycle.
