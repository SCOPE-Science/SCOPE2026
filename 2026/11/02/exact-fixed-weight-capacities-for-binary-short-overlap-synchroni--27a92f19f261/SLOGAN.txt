For binary fixed-weight synchronization codes forbidding overlaps of lengths one, two, and three, the exact optimum at every \(n\ge6\) is the maximum of four explicit binomial expressions, attained by one of four universal boundary templates. At fixed relative weight, the optimal template changes at the three sharp densities \((3-\sqrt5)/2\), \(1/2\), and \((\sqrt5-1)/2\).
