For every fixed positive three-periodic BDF3 step pattern, both parasitic Floquet multipliers lie strictly inside the unit circle, regardless of how extreme the step ratios are. The phenomenon has no uniform damping margin: on the pattern \((t,1,t^{-1})\) the spectral radius is \(1-16/(3t)+O(t^{-2})\), approaching the sharp unattained bound \(1\).
