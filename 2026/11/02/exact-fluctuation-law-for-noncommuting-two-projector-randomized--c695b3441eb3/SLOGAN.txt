For randomized implicit Euler switching between two noncommuting rank-one dampings, the finite-step slow-mode variance has an exact two-eigenvalue formula. Starting in the fast eigenmode, the leading numerical error becomes Gaussian entirely along the slow eigenmode, with the explicit positive variance \(V(c,T)=\frac{1-c^2}{4c}(e^{-2(1-c)T}-e^{-2(1+c)T})\), proving a sharp one-half strong-order barrier.
