For a uniformly random \(q\)-ary word, the number of distinct outputs after exactly two deletions has an explicit finite-length variance for every \(q\ge2\); the leading fluctuation variance is \((q-1)^3n^3/q^4\). Conditional on the entire adjacent-change pattern, the extra ambiguity from returning \(ABA\) triples is exactly binomial with success probability \(1/(q-1)\), revealing a nonbinary effect invisible in the deterministic binary case.
