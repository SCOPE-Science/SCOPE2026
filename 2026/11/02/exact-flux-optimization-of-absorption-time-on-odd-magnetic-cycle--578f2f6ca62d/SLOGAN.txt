For an odd coherent magnetic ring with one local Markovian absorber, the exact mean capture time is \(N/\kappa+\sigma_j/(2J\sin\phi)+\kappa j(N-j)/(8J^2\sin^2\phi)\). The globally optimal flux changes sharply at \(\kappa=2J/\max(j,N-j)\) from two interior-flux choices to a unique quarter-flux choice.
