For every homogeneous even-site magnetic quantum ring with one local absorber, the exactly optimal mean-capture flux is neither zero nor quarter flux: it is \(\phi_m=2\arctan(((m^2-1)/(3m^2))^{1/4})\) and its reflected partner, independent of absorber rate. An exact all-size spectral-gap identity gives the complete lifetime and exposes unequal inverse-square slowdowns as flux approaches the two dark-state phases.
