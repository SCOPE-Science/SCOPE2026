For every clique windmill \(K_1\vee(nK_{q-1})\) with \(q\ge3\) and \(n\ge2\), the complete polynomial counting partial \(q\)-colourings that force a proper total colouring is \(q((q-1)!)^n z^{n(q-2)}[(1+z)(q-1+z)^n-(q-1)]\). Its minimum forcing-domain size is exactly \(n(q-2)\), and the subtraction term captures the one global obstruction caused by the shared hub.
