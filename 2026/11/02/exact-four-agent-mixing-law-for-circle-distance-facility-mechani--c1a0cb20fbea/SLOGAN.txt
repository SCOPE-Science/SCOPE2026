For four agents on a circle, the precise worst-case cost ratio of \(\lambda\) Random Dictator mixed with \((1-\lambda)\) Even Proportional Circle Distance is \((5+\lambda)/4\) for every fixed \(0\le\lambda\le1\). In particular, the equal mixture has sharp ratio \(11/8\), strictly below the previously known population-uniform \(3/2\) guarantee, and the extremal four-agent shape is rigid whenever \(\lambda<1\).
