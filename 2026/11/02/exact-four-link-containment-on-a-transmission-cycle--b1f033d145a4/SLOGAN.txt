For a cyclic contact network with exactly four independently imperfect transmission links and otherwise certain transmission, the mean final size from one uniformly seeded infection is globally minimized by balanced link spacings. When the number of individuals is \(2\) modulo \(4\), the two longer gaps must be opposite rather than adjacent: adjacent placement incurs the exact strict extra mean outbreak \(2q(1-q)^2/n\), for every \(0<q<1\).
