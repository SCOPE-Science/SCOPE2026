For four students and four unit schools with independently uniform strict priorities, optimally ordered serial dictatorship has exact doubly averaged justified envy \(8641/4608\), compared with \(3\) for a uniformly random student order. The complete \(331{,}776\)-profile distribution has only \(2{,}376\) profiles where optimization cannot improve the expectation, characterized by every student pair splitting the schools two-to-two.
