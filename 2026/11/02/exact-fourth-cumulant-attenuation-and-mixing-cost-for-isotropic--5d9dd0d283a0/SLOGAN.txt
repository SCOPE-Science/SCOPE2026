Fresh Haar-direction Kaczmarz with independent non-Gaussian response noise has a unique finite-fourth-moment stationary law whose coordinate excess kurtosis is exactly \(3/(2d+1)\) times the response excess kurtosis. The same coordinates have stationary lag correlation \((1-1/d)^k\), so the exact integrated autocorrelation time grows to \(2d-1\) even as the fourth-moment non-Gaussianity is attenuated.
