For every odd prime \(p\), the complete distribution of the number of roots of a uniformly random monic cubic modulo \(p^4\) is an explicit nine-level probability law (with one level collision at \(p=3\)). Its maximum is \(3p^2\), achieved by exactly \(p^4\binom p3\) cubics, with a complete description in terms of a split cubic over \(\mathbb F_p\).
