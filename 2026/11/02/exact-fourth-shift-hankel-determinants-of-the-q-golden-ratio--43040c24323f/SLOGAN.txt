The complete fourth-shift Hankel sequence of the \(q\)-golden ratio has rational generating function \(\frac{(1-z)(1+z)^3}{(1+z^4)^2}\) and least quasi-period \(8\). An infinite symbolic Hankel-continued-fraction certificate proves the first higher-shift instance of the 2026 Han--Pedon quasi-polynomial conjecture; the initial terms had previously been tabulated.
