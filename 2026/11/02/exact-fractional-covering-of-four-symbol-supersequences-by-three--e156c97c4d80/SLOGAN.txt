The single-insertion incidence covering LP for length-three words has the exact fractional optimum \(\mathsf F_q=q(q+1)^2/4\) for every alphabet size \(q\ge3\), with the binary exception \(\mathsf F_2=4\). Explicit primal and dual rational weights prove equality and, together with known integer-covering density bounds, imply an asymptotic integrality gap strictly greater than \(4/3\).
