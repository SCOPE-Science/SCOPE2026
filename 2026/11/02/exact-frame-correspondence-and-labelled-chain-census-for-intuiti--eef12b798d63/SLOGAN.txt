The intuitionistic-monotone axiom \( (\Box\top\to\Diamond p)\to\Diamond p \) holds on a concrete constructive-neighbourhood frame exactly when every visible neighbourhood has a downward-refining witness at a future world with permanently nonempty neighbourhood support. This gives an exact labelled census on finite chains, starting \(4,241,16690673\), and proves the published continual-support condition is sufficient but not necessary.
