For a connected unicyclic graph \(G\) with an odd cycle, frustration in its canonical signed spectral line graph equals \(d_*-1\), where \(d_*\) is the smallest degree on that cycle; even-cycle graphs have frustration zero. Every optimum orientation is forced except for choosing the unique minimum-degree mixed cycle vertex, one of two alternating directions, and (when \(d_*\ge3\)) the common direction of its attached edges, giving exactly \(2t\) or \(4t\) optimizers.
