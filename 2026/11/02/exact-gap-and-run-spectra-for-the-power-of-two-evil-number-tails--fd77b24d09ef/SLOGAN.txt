For the evil-integer semigroup starting at \(2^{r+1}+1\) with \(r\ge2\), every positive gap is either below the first generator or an odious integer no larger than \(2^{r+2}\). Its genus is exactly \(3\cdot2^r\), and beyond one long initial gap run, there are precisely \((2^r+2(-1)^r)/3\) singleton runs and \((2^r-(-1)^r)/3\) doubleton runs.
