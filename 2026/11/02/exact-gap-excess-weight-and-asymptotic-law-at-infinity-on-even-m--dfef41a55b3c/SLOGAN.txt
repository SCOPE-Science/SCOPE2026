For even Ma–Wang maximal Kummer curves, the gap-excess weight at the unique point over infinity has a closed polynomial formula in \(q\) and \(q^n\); after normalization by genus squared it decreases strictly with even \(n\) and tends to an explicit positive constant. The evaluation uses a two-step Dedekind reciprocity identity intrinsic to the branch multiplicities, beyond the general Kummer gap-set description.
