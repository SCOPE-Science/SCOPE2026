For every \(g\ge13\) not divisible by three, the complete \(h\)-fold gap-sumset of the Eisenbud–Schreyer semigroup \(\langle6,9,g,g+3\rangle\) has precisely \(g-3\) missing integers within its natural interval, for every \(h\ge1\). Thus its Buchweitz deficit is exactly \(2h-3\) at all orders \(h\ge2\): every such test misses this known non-Weierstrass family.
