The two- and three-gradient stochastic Runge–Kutta Langevin samplers coincide exactly on every Gaussian target; their invariant law is **exact** at the nonzero step \(h\lambda=4/3\) for an isotropic quadratic, but their physical-time linear sampling variance is then inflated by exactly \(7/3\). At all positive stable steps this variance exceeds the continuous-time Ornstein–Uhlenbeck value, while first-order Euler–Maruyama matches that one specific variance coefficient.
