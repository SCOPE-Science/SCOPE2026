For Gaussian oblique tangent projection at a fixed rank-\(r\) matrix, every nonzero perpendicular residual leaks into the tangent space almost surely, and its \(q\)-th Frobenius moment is finite exactly when \(q<\min\{\ell_L-r+1,\ell_R-r+1\}\). When the second moment exists, the exact squared leakage factor is \((1+r/(\ell_L-r-1))(1+r/(\ell_R-r-1))-1\); a single extra sketch row beyond the rank still gives infinite mean-square error.
