# Exact Gaussian Nyström preconditioning deterioration probability on a minimal spiked system

## Finding

For any \(L>1\), let \(A_L=\operatorname{diag}(L,1,1)\). Draw a \(3\times2\) matrix \(\Omega\) with independent standard Gaussian entries, form its rank-two Nyström approximation
\[
N=A_L\Omega(\Omega^{\mathsf T}A_L\Omega)^{-1}\Omega^{\mathsf T}A_L,
\]
and write its two positive eigenvalues as \(\tau\ge1\) and \(1\), with \(u\) a unit eigenvector of \(\tau\). Use the zero-regularization Nyström spectral preconditioner of [1],
\[
P=I+(\tau-1)uu^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Let \(K_L=\operatorname{cond}_2(P^{-1/2}A_LP^{-1/2})\). Although \(\operatorname{cond}_2(A_L)=L\), preconditioning is strictly harmful with the **exact positive probability**
\[
\boxed{\Pr(K_L>L)=1-\sqrt{\frac{2L^2}{2L^2+1+\sqrt{5+4/L}}}.}
\]
This probability equals approximately \(0.17117806242279432\) when \(L=2\) and has the sharp expansion
\[
\Pr(K_L>L)=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{4L^2}+O(L^{-3})\quad(L\to\infty).
\]
Dimension three and rank two are minimal for **any** strict condition-number deterioration from this ideal Nyström spectral preconditioner: with dimension at most two, rank one leaves the matrix unpreconditioned and full rank yields condition number one.

## Assumptions and scope

All calculations are in exact real arithmetic. \(L>1\); \(\Omega\) has independent \(N(0,1)\) entries; the sketch is independent of any right-hand side. Inverses exist almost surely. The spectral preconditioner is exactly [1, Eq. (17)] with the regularization parameter \(\mu=0\), applied only to the strictly positive definite matrix \(A_L\). The source's main *expected* large-rank condition-number theorem has additional hypotheses not assumed here. The event \(K_L>L\) compares the full symmetric-preconditioning spectral condition number with the unpreconditioned matrix, **not** PCG iteration counts. No claim is made for finite-precision Nyström approximations or positive regularization.

## Proof

Let \(n\) be either unit normal to \(\operatorname{range}\Omega\), and put \(t=n_1^2\). The Gaussian sketch plane is Haar distributed, hence \(|n_1|\) is uniform on \([0,1]\) and
\[
\Pr(t\le s)=\sqrt{s},\qquad 0\le s\le1.
\]
Rotations fixing the first coordinate allow replacing \(n\), without altering the spectrum, by \((\sqrt t,\sqrt{1-t},0)\). Set
\[
 d=L(1-t)+t,\qquad e=L^2(1-t)+t,\qquad f=L^3(1-t)+t.
\]
The rank-one residual identity
\[
N=A_L-\frac{nn^{\mathsf T}}{n^{\mathsf T}A_L^{-1}n}
\]
follows from the orthogonal-projector formula for the Nyström approximation, because the nullspace of \(A_L^{1/2}\Omega\) is generated by \(A_L^{-1/2}n\). It gives the nonzero eigenvalues \(\tau=e/d\) and \(1\), and on the active first-two-coordinate plane a principal eigenvector
\[
 u=\frac{1}{\sqrt e}(-L\sqrt{1-t},\sqrt t),
 \qquad u^{\mathsf T}A_Lu=\frac fe.
\]
The remaining unit coordinate has preconditioned eigenvalue exactly one. In the active plane the preconditioner has eigenvalues \(\tau,1\); the two preconditioned eigenvalues have sum and product
\[
T=L+1-\left(1-\frac1\tau\right)\frac fe
  =L+1-\frac{L(L-1)(1-t)f}{e^2},
\qquad D=\frac L\tau=\frac{Ld}{e}.
\]
For \(0<t<1\), the rank-two difference
\[
 A_L-P=(L-1)e_1e_1^{\mathsf T}-(\tau-1)uu^{\mathsf T}
\]
is indefinite on the active plane because its two nonzero rank-one directions are linearly independent and both coefficients are positive. Congruence therefore gives one preconditioned eigenvalue above one and the other below one. The third eigenvalue is one. Consequently the full condition number \(K_L\) equals the active-plane eigenvalue ratio. The scalar \(T^2/D\) equals \(K_L+2+1/K_L\), which is strictly increasing for \(K_L>1\). Similarly \((L+1)^2/L=L+2+1/L\), so \(K_L>L\) is equivalent to \(T^2/D>(L+1)^2/L\).

Exact algebra, with no approximation or numerical root finding, gives
\[
\frac{T^2}{D}-\frac{(L+1)^2}{L}
=-\frac{(L-1)^2(1-t)}{e^3}
 \left[L^5(1-t)^2-L^3t(1-t)-(L+1)t^2\right].
\]
Write \(z=t/(1-t)>0\). The bracket divided by \((1-t)^2\) is the strictly decreasing function
\[
L^5-L^3z-(L+1)z^2.
\]
Its unique positive zero is \(z_c=2L^2/(1+\sqrt{5+4/L})\). Since the preceding prefactor is strictly negative, \(K_L>L\) holds exactly when
\[
t>t_c=\frac{2L^2}{2L^2+1+\sqrt{5+4/L}},\qquad (0<t<1).
\]
The exceptional endpoints have Haar probability zero: \(t=0\) gives \(K_L=1\), and \(t=1\) gives \(K_L=L\). The distribution of \(t\) proves the boxed probability formula. Expanding \(1-\sqrt{t_c}\) at infinity yields the stated sharp \(L^{-2}\) constant, with residual \(O(L^{-3})\).

For a concrete exact witness, take \(L=2\) and an orientation with \(t=8/9\). Then \(\tau=6/5\), \(T=25/9\), and \(D=5/3\). Solving the quadratic for the two active eigenvalues gives
\[
K_L=\frac{71+5\sqrt{85}}{54}>2.
\]
This is a deterministic orientation in an open event of Gaussian probability given above, not a finite-sample inference.

For minimality, when the problem dimension is at most two and the Nyström sketch rank is one, [1, Eq. (17)] at \(\mu=0\) has its sole positive Nyström eigenvalue divided by itself, so \(P=I\). If the sketch rank equals the dimension, a full-rank Gaussian sketch yields \(N=A\) almost surely and \(P=A/\lambda_{\min}(A)\), making the symmetrically preconditioned matrix a multiple of \(I\). The scalar dimension is trivial. Thus dimension three and a genuine intermediate rank two are necessary and sufficient for a deterioration event in this family.

## Verification

The accompanying standalone `verify_nystrom.py` checks the symbolic factor identity, unique critical root, asymptotic constant, and exact witness with SymPy. It also constructs \(160\) Gaussian Nyström sketches over five spike sizes, compares computed symmetric-preconditioned eigenvalue ratios with the analytic threshold, and tests \(20\) low-dimensional rank-one/full-rank controls with NumPy. The completed run printed: `VERIFY_OK symbolic identities=6, exact example=3, sampled Gaussian sketches=160, dimension-two cases=20`. These finite comparisons are regression checks; the exact proof above establishes the event identity for all \(L>1\).

## Relationship to prior work

Frangella, Tropp, and Udell [1] introduce the specific randomized Nyström PCG preconditioner, analyze large-sketch condition-number performance, and explicitly allow \(\mu=0\) for positive definite systems. Their Eq. (17) supplies the preconditioner here, but its expected-condition-number bounds do not state the exact \(3\)-by-\(3\), rank-two **probability of worse-than-unpreconditioned conditioning**, its sharp \(L^{-2}\) asymptotic, or minimal dimension. Martinsson and Tropp [2, §17.3] motivate randomized spectral preconditioning and approximate eigendirections without evaluating this deterioration probability. Carson and Daužickaitė [3, §3] analyze mixed-precision Nyström preconditioner error bounds and experiments rather than this exact Gaussian orientation law. Giraud and Gratton [4] study sensitivity of spectral preconditioners to eigenpair inaccuracies; their qualitative perturbation insights do not supply the specific finite-angle Gaussian Nyström probability theorem. The underlying Haar-plane distribution and elementary matrix identities are classical and not claimed as new.

## Limitations

The result is for an exact-arithmetic, zero-regularization, rank-two Nyström preconditioner on a three-dimensional one-spike diagonal matrix. Although the **probability** of worsening is exact, a larger spectral condition number does not by itself prove more PCG iterations, especially in dimension three. It does not contradict the source's expected-condition-number guarantees under appropriate positive regularization and larger sketch rank. The exact threshold may have been independently derived in specialized spectral preconditioning or spiked-matrix studies not identified in the sources inspected; mathematical publication priority needs independent checking.
