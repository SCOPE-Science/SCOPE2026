In the smallest nontrivial Gaussian Nyström preconditioner—rank two on a three-dimensional matrix with eigenvalues \(L,1,1\)—the preconditioned condition number actually exceeds \(L\) with exact probability \(1-\sqrt{2L^2/(2L^2+1+\sqrt{5+4/L})}\). The sharp bad-sketch probability scales as \((1+\sqrt5)/(4L^2)\), despite standard large-rank guarantees for Nyström preconditioning under their separate assumptions.
