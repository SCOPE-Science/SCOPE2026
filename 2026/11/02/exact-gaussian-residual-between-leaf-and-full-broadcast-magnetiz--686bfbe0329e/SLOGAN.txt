For noisy binary broadcasting on uniform recursive trees, subtracting \(\frac{1-2q}{2-2q}\) times the full spin sum from the leaf spin sum leaves an exactly centered residual with variance \(\frac{3-(1-2q)^2}{6(2-2q)^2}n\) for every \(n\ge3\). This residual has a Gaussian \(\sqrt n\)-limit, independent of the larger random broadcast mode in the superdiffusive regime \(q<1/4\).
