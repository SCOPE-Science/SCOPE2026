On the two-row identity system, using the same Gaussian-noisy residuals both to choose the largest residual and to project incurs the exact extra one-step mean-square error \(2\sigma^2\pi^{-1}e^{-\|x\|^2/(2\sigma^2)}\) compared with independently remeasuring the chosen row. This yields a sharp all-start reversal below \(\|x\|/\sigma=1.2401252666\ldots\), while the independent-remeasurement rule is never worse than uniform row choice.
