Every infinite GCD of length-\(k\) windows of cubed Fibonacci numbers is exactly a Fibonacci or Lucas number, with a factor \(3\) precisely when \(8\mid k\) and a factor \(1/2\) precisely when \(6\mid k\). The closed formula holds for every positive \(k\), settling the ordinary-Fibonacci cubic instance of a published higher-power window-GCD question.
