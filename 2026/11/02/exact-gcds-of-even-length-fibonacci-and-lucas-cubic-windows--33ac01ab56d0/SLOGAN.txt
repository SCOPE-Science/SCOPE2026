For every even length \(2m\) with odd \(m\), the GCDs of Fibonacci and Lucas cubic windows both equal \(L_m/2^{\mathbf 1_{3\mid m}}\). For even \(m\), the Fibonacci GCD is \(3^{\mathbf 1_{4\mid m}}F_m/2^{\mathbf 1_{3\mid m}}\) and the Lucas GCD is five times as large. These exact formulas resolve the natural even-length cubic-window problem and contradict a previously proposed formula at length six.
