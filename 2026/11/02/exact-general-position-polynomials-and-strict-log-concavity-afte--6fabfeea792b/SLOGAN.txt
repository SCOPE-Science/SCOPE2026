For every graph \(H\) of order \(n\ge5\) and maximum degree at most two, the full general-position polynomial of its complement is \(I(H;z)+e(H)z^2+(p(H)+t(H))z^3+q(H)z^4\), and its nonzero coefficient sequence is strictly log-concave. This covers every disjoint union of paths and cycles, although the resulting polynomials need not be real-rooted.
