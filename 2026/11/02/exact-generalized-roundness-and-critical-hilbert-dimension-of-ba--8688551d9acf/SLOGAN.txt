The exact generalized roundness of the balanced unit-edge double-star tree with \(q\) leaves at each hub is the unique \(p\in(1,2]\) solving \(q3^p=(q+1)2^p+q\). Its endpoint Hilbert snowflake embedding loses precisely one dimension for \(q\ge2\), and its exponent approaches \(1\) at rate \(2/((3\log3-2\log2)q)\), strictly below the exponent of either induced one-hub star.
