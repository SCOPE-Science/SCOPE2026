A clique with one leaf attached to each of two distinct clique vertices has an exactly calculable negative-type exponent, determined by a unique scalar root involving \(2^p\), \(3^p\), and \(4^p\). As the clique grows, the exponent decreases strictly to \(1.629581635586\ldots\), and exactly one Euclidean embedding dimension is lost at the critical snowflake exponent.
