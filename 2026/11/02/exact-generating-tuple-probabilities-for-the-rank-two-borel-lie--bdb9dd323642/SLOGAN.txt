The exact probability that \(k\) uniform elements generate the five-dimensional traceless upper-triangular Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) is \((1-q^{-k})(1-q^{1-k})^3\) except in characteristic three, where it is \((1-q^{-k})(1-q^{1-k})^2(1-q^{2-k})\). Thus the smallest generating tuple jumps sharply from two elements to three in characteristic three.
