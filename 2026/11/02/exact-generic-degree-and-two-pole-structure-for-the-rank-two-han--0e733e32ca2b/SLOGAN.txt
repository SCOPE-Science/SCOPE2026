For every \(n\ge5\), observing only coordinates \(\{1,2,4,n\}\) on the rank-two Hankel variety gives exactly \(\lfloor3(n-3)/2\rfloor\) generic complex completions. A rational recurrence parameter has just two poles, of orders \(n-4\) and \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\), explaining the uniform degree formula.
