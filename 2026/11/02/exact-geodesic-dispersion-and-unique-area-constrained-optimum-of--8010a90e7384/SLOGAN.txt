For area-uniform random pairs on every compact rectangular flat Möbius band, the expected squared intrinsic geodesic distance has an exact two-branch elementary formula and a fourth-derivative transition at half-width \(w=L/2\). At fixed area it is uniquely minimized at \(4w^2/L^2=4.3768539312\ldots\), an abstract intrinsic optimum distinct from the shortest smoothly embedded paper-band problem.
