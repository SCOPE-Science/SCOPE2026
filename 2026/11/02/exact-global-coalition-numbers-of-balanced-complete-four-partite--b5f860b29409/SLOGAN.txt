The global coalition number of the balanced four-partite graph \(K_{q,q,q,q}\) is exactly \(\max\{2q,3q-3,4q-8\}\) for every integer \(q\ge1\). A support-incidence argument proves the first balanced four-part case of the multipartite global-coalition conjecture, with phase transitions at \(q=4\) and \(q=6\).
