For every prime \(p\ge7\), the pentatope polynomial \(\binom{x}{4}\) has an explicit image modulo every \(p^k\): all but at most two attained residue classes lift freely, while the exceptional classes are precisely scaled square sets. Its exact residue counts and limiting \(p\)-adic Haar measure are controlled by the three Legendre symbols \(\chi_p(-1)\), \(\chi_p(5)\), and \(\chi_p(10)\).
