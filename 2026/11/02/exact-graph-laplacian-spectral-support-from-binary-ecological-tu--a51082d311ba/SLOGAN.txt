For a fixed predator–prey ecology, knowing which positive diffusion-rate pairs cause strict Turing instability on a connected graph determines exactly the set of distinct positive graph-Laplacian eigenvalues: any chosen eigenvalue can be isolated by an arbitrarily narrow instability window. Already at six patches, the triangular prism and the equally regular graph \(K_{3,3}\) have opposite instability outcomes, and changing only diffusion rates reverses which one is unstable.
