Completed legal total-dominating sequences of \(K_m\times K_n\) have only lengths three and four, with an explicit polynomial counting their orders and an exact positive formula for the four-step histories whose underlying sets are redundant. On \(K_q\times K_q\), uniformly sampled complete histories asymptotically have four steps, but a greedy procedure that samples each legal next move uniformly asymptotically finishes in three.
