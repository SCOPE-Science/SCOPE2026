For every generalized windmill \(W_{m,s}=K_1\vee(mK_s)\), the partial induced-graph automorphism monoid has exactly \(2\binom{m+s}{s}-s\) Green \(\mathcal D\)-classes, with explicit sizes, maximal subgroups, and a complete rank-generating polynomial. The enumeration isolates all extra local symmetries that appear when the central vertex can be mistaken for a blade vertex inside a complete induced subgraph.
