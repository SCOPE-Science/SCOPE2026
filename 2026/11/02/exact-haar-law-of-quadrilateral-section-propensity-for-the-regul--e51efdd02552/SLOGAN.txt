For a Haar-random orientation, the fraction of parallel planes cutting a regular tetrahedron into quadrilaterals has the exact survival law \(\Pr(Q>q)=\frac{12}{\pi}\bigl(\arctan\sqrt{1+2((1-q)/(1+q))^2}-\pi/4\bigr)\). Its mean is \(0.29874628557\ldots\), distinct from the width-biased probability obtained by sampling an invariant affine plane.
