For a Haar-uniform plane through the center of a regular dodecahedron, the section is almost surely a hexagon, octagon, or decagon, with exact probabilities \(10-15(a+b)/\pi\), \(15(2a+b-\pi)/\pi\), and \(6-15a/\pi\), where \(a=\arccos(1/3)\) and \(b=\arccos(\sqrt5/3)\). A complete exact spherical-arrangement classification supplies the three probabilities and an executable algebraic certificate.
