For the twenty vertices of a regular dodecahedron, exact-degree spherical-harmonic evaluation reaches its full antipodal rank ten at degrees \(6,7,9,10,11,12,13\) and every degree \(k\ge15\), but loses a dimension again at degrees \(8\) and \(14\). A signed Petersen-matrix decomposition gives the complete Gram spectrum at every degree and proves that \(15\) is the sharp permanent interpolation threshold.
