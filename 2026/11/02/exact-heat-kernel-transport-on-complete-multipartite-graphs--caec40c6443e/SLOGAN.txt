For combinatorial heat flow on every unweighted complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, every pair of heat-kernel rows has an exact graph-metric \(W_1\) distance determined solely by the sizes of its two endpoint parts and the total order. The sharp graph-Lipschitz semigroup norm depends only on the total order and largest part size, revealing a profile invariance across nonisomorphic graphs and an exact transient-amplification law.
