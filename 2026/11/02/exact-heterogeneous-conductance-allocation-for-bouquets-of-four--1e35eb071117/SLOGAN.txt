For a bouquet of arbitrarily priced four-cycles, the unique conductance allocation minimizing total pairwise effective resistance has a positive edge rate on every link: each cycle's two edge-rate classes are set by a unique positive quartic root, and cycle budgets are proportional to square roots of their exact shape costs. With equal edge-class prices, the optimal hub-to-distal rate ratio is \(\sqrt{3(3k-2)/(3k)}\), approaching \(\sqrt3\) as the number of cycles grows.
