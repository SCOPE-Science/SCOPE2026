For a finite metric consisting of two equilateral blocks of sizes \(a,b\) with internal distances \(u,w\) and cross-distance \(v\), the exact Hilbert distortion is \(\max\{1,\sqrt{(a-1)u^2/(2a)+(b-1)w^2/(2b)}/v\}\), and all optimal realizations are rigid up to similarity. In particular, the complete split graph \(K_a\vee\overline{K_b}\) has sharp distortion \(\sqrt{5/2-1/(2a)-2/b}\), with every optimizer formed by two orthogonal regular simplices sharing a centroid.
