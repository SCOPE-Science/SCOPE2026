For the real norm \(N_{n,\tau}(x)=\max\{\|x\|_1,\tau|\sum_i x_i|\}\), the exact Banach--Mazur distance to Hilbert space is determined in every dimension and at every positive slab parameter, with different explicit formulas for even and odd dimensions. A unique permutation-invariant optimal ellipsoid balances the sum direction against nearly balanced sign vectors; the squared distortion drops from \(n\) to \((n+2)/2\) (even \(n\)) or \((n+2-1/n)/2\) (odd \(n\)) as the slab narrows.
