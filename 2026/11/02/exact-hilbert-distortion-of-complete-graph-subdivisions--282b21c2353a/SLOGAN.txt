The exact squared real Hilbert distortion of the once-subdivided clique \(S(K_n)\) equals \(9/4\) at \(n=3\), \((43-8\sqrt{19})/3\) at \(n=4\), and \(4(n-2)/(n-1)\) for every \(n\geq5\) (with the path case \(n=2\) isometric). A symmetry-averaged Gram inequality and explicit two-layer coordinate embeddings give matching bounds, revealing an exceptional algebraic four-vertex clique before the distortion increases toward \(2\).
