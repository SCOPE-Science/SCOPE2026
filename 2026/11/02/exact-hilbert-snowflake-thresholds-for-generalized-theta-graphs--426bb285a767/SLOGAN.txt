For generalized theta graphs with \(m\ge3\) length-two paths and one length-three path between common poles, the exact supremal negative-type exponent is \(\log_2(t_m/(t_m-1))\), where \(t_m=(3m+2+\sqrt{29m^2+92m+4})/[2(m+4)]\). It decreases strictly toward \(\log_2((3+\sqrt{29})/(1+\sqrt{29}))\), and the critical Hilbert embedding loses exactly one affine dimension.
