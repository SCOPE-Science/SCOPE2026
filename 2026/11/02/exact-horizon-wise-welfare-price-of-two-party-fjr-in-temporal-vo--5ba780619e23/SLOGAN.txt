In two-party temporal elections, the exact worst-case utilitarian welfare price of FJR+ over all populations is a rational, explicitly computable function of the number of rounds: \(P(L)=\max_{0\le j\le\lfloor L/2\rfloor}L(L-j)/(L^2-2Lj+2j^2)\). The price oscillates as the horizon grows but converges to \((1+\sqrt2)/2\), and for every \(L\ge2\) a sharp example needs only \(L\) voters.
