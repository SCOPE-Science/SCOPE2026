Every finite-field dihedral quandle algebra of odd prime order has an explicit closed idempotent count: in its own prime characteristic the answer is \(1+q^{(p-1)/\lvert\langle-1,2\rangle\rvert}\), while in coprime characteristic a Frobenius-orbit and root-of-unity gcd formula gives the exact count. The distinction is substantive: for order \(17\), the counts over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_3\), and \(\mathbb F_{17}\) are respectively \(648\), \(36\), and \(290\).
