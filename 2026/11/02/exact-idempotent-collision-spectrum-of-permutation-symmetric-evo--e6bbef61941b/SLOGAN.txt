For every perfect complex evolution algebra with structure matrix \(aI_n+bJ_n\), \(n\ge3\), the exact number of distinct idempotents is generically \(2^n\) and, when \(b\ne0\), drops only at an explicit finite set of ratios \(a/b\). Its sharp minimum is \(2^{n-1}+1-\tfrac12\mathbf1_{2\mid n}\binom n{n/2}\), attained at \(a/b=n\), with two additional minimizers when \(n=3\).
