PASS

The coordinate Gram matrix is I+11ᵀ with determinant 4, so ambient H³ equals twice coordinate volume. On the zero-sum hyperplane the root hull is the positive-mass unit ball: each positive coordinate can be supplied from the negative ones, giving root weights of total mass at most one, with any remainder at the origin. For a point y in the open cube only shifts 0 and −1 can occur in each coordinate. For t=sum(y)<1 only the empty set and singleton shift sets qualify, and reflection gives the t>2 slab. In the middle slab, each singleton {j} qualifies for y_j≥t−1 and its complement qualifies for y_j≤t−1, so there are three shifts off the equality planes. The two extra corner simplices each have volume 1/6, giving C(0)=10/3. For integral k the Fourier coefficient of the periodization is the original coefficient, and reflection makes the corner integrals conjugate. The size-parameter Laplace integral over the simplex yields the four linear factors including the slack factor z. At simple poles the evaluated exponent is one and the coefficient is proportional to (2πi)⁻³, hence purely imaginary. At a double node r the real term is (2πi)⁻²/[(s−r)(t−r)]; two double nodes give two such terms with the same squared denominator; at a triple node the real term is −(2πi)⁻²/(s−r)². Doubling the real parts gives all stated nonzero coefficients, with the correct sign and no further cancellation. Subtracting the ordered triples of distinct nonzero values from (2N+1)³ gives 24N²+2N+1, including N=0.
