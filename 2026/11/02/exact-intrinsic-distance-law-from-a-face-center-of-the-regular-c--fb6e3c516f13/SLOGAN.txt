For a uniformly random point on the unit cube surface, the intrinsic distance \(R\) from a fixed face center has a complete elementary cumulative distribution, exact moments \(\mathbb E R^2=23/18\) and \(\mathbb E R^4=451/180\), and a sharp upper tail \(\Pr(R>2-\varepsilon)=\frac23\varepsilon^2+\frac19\varepsilon^3+O(\varepsilon^4)\). The known shortest-path unfoldings are integrated over all six faces to obtain a metric-measure law, not a new pointwise geodesic formula.
