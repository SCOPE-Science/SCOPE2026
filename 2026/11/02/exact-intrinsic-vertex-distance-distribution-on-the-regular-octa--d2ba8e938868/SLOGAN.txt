For a uniformly area-sampled point on a unit-edge regular octahedron, its intrinsic distance from a fixed vertex has an exact two-branch cumulative law: \(F(r)=\pi r^2/(3\sqrt3)\) all the way to \(r=1\), followed by an explicit circular-segment formula through the sharp endpoint \(\sqrt3\). The squared-distance mean is \(11/12\), and the terminal survival probability is asymptotic to \(\tfrac23\varepsilon^2\).
