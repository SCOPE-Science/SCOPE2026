For a uniformly random point on the surface of a unit cube, its intrinsic shortest-path distance from a fixed vertex has an exact three-piece arcsine distribution on \([0,\sqrt5]\). The wavefront-area law has distinct second-derivative jumps at radii \(1\) and \(\sqrt2\), and its terminal tail is asymptotic to \(5(\sqrt5-r)^2/12\), while the squared-distance mean is exactly \(3/2\).
