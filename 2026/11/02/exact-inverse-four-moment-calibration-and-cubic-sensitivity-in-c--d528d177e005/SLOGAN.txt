For a fixed-length cyclic Erlang-feedback phase-type model, the ratio \(\mu_2\mu_4/\mu_3^2\) uniquely determines the return probability, and the mean then uniquely determines the phase rate. This particular higher-moment-ratio inversion, unlike the simpler variance-ratio inversion, becomes cubically ill-conditioned near vanishing per-circuit absorption probability, with \(4/3-\mu_2\mu_4/\mu_3^2\asymp(1-p)^3\) as \(p\uparrow1\).
