For every odd prime \(q\), the complete inverse-totient fiber \(\{n:\varphi(n)=8q\}\) has an explicit classification: apart from the known \(q=3,5\) fibers, its elements are determined solely by the primality of \(2q+1\), \(4q+1\), and \(8q+1\), with a mod-three dichotomy. The exact nonexceptional fiber sizes are \(0,2,3,4,6\), and all individual preimages are listed by finite multiplier families.
