For the exterior algebra on \(n\ge2\) generators over an odd-order finite field, the number of actual order-two gradings has an exact Gaussian-binomial sum indexed by the negative eigenspace dimension of an automorphism. As the field grows, the dominant linear-involution types are uniformly distributed across all mixed dimensions when \(n\) is even, but only across positive even negative-eigenspace dimensions when \(n\) is odd.
