On the 24-spin antiferromagnetic Ising snub cube, the complete zero-temperature response has exactly two positive critical fields, \(h=J\) and \(h=5J\), separated by a one-third-saturation plateau. An exhaustive exact maximum-cut census shows that the lower transition has precisely 201 coexisting spin configurations; the 5302 configurations at the upper transition are independently implied by known independence-set counts.
