For the unit-shift generalized repunit semigroup of base \(b\ge2\) and embedding dimension \(n\ge3\), every isolated gap lies in one explicit, non-overlapping \((n-2)\)-dimensional digit box, so there are exactly \(b^{n-2}\) such gaps. Their quotient-stratum enumerator is \(z^{b-1}(1+z+\cdots+z^{b-1})^{n-2}\).
