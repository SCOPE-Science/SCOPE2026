Every Sturmian word with irrational \(1\)-frequency \(\alpha\) has exact \(k\)-tortoise complexity \(\max\{2,n+1-\lfloor k/\alpha\rfloor\}\) at each factor length \(n\ge1\), for every \(k\ge1\). Thus the last length of agreement with Abelian complexity is exactly \(\lfloor k/\alpha\rfloor+1\), resolving the complete slope- and iteration-dependent profile suggested by the original tortoise-complexity framework.
