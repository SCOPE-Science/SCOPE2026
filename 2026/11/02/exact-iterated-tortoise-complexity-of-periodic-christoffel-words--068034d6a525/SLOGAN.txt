For every primitive rational binary Christoffel period, every iteration count \(k\ge1\), and every factor length, the exact number of iterated tortoise images is a closed modular-interval count. The new all-iteration phase law has sharp eventual-injectivity length \(q+\lfloor(kq-1)/p\rfloor\), with periodic deviations absent from the corresponding irrational Sturmian formula; the previously known one-step case is recovered at \(k=1\).
