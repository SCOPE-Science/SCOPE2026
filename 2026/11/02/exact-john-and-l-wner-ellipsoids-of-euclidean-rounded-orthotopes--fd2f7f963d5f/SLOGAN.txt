Every Euclidean rounded orthotope with positive half-lengths has exact, unique John and Löwner ellipsoids in every dimension \(d\ge3\): the inner semiaxes are \(a_i+r\), while the outer semiaxes are \(\sqrt d(a_i+rv_i)\), where the positive vector \(v\) comes from a single scalar equation. The complete outer contact set comprises the \(2^d\) signed corners of the unique maximum-volume axis-aligned box inscribed in the rounded body, yielding a finite certificate of unrestricted optimality.
