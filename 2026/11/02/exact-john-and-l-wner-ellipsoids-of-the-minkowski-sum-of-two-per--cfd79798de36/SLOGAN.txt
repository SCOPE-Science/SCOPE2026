For the Minkowski sum of two centered perpendicular filled disks of arbitrary positive radii, both the unique John ellipsoid and the unique unrestricted Löwner ellipsoid have explicit semiaxes; the latter is certified by a finite isotropic contact distribution. The John ellipsoid occupies exactly \(2/3\) of the body's volume, while the enclosing ellipsoid occupies between \(4/3\) (equal radii) and an unattained supremum \(\sqrt3\) (extreme radius imbalance).
