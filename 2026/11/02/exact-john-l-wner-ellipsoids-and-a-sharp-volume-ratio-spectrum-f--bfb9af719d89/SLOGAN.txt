For every dimension at least three, a simplex cut parallel to a facet has explicit unique John and Löwner ellipsoids: the inner ellipsoid changes contact structure at the sharp truncation depth \((d-1)/(d+1)\), while the outer ellipsoid contacts every vertex. Their exact volume ratio decreases strictly from \(d^d\) to the sharp degenerate-frustum limit \(d^{d/2}(d-1)^{(d-1)/2}\).
