For every genuine higher-dimensional orthogonal Euclidean block bicylinder (two overlapping quadratic cylinders with block dimensions \(p+q\ge3\)), both unrestricted John and Löwner ellipsoids admit exact semiaxes and explicit contact certificates. The Löwner ellipsoid touches the full common cylinder ridge, and its volume ratio to the John ellipsoid decreases strictly with the squared radii ratio between two sharp dimension-dependent endpoints.
