For an orthogonal planar shadow of the circumradius-one regular octahedron, its area at fixed inradius occupies exactly two explicit, sharp intervals. They are separated by an impossible area band precisely when \(r>\sqrt{\sqrt2-1}\); at the threshold \(r=\sqrt{\sqrt2-1}\) the bands touch at area \(2^{3/4}\), revealing when area and inradius together determine the shadow's polygon type.
