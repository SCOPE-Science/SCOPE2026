In a uniform random mapping, conditional on exactly \(k<n\) cyclic vertices, the number of indegree-zero vertices has exactly the occupancy law of \(n-k\) specified boxes after \(n-k-1\) independent draws from \(n\) boxes. This gives an exact bivariate distribution and strictly negative covariance between terminal count and every increasing transform of cyclic count, with \(\operatorname{Cov}(C,Z)\sim-e^{-1}\sqrt{\pi n/8}\).
