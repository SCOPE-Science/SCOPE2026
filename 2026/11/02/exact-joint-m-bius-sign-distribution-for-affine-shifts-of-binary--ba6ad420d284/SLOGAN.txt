For every binary finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) and every nonconstant affine shift \(h(T)=uT+v\), the nine joint counts of the Möbius signs of monic cubics \(f\) and \(f+h\) have universal exact formulas depending only on \(q\). In particular, the entire two-point Möbius correlation is exactly \(-q\), not merely asymptotically small.
