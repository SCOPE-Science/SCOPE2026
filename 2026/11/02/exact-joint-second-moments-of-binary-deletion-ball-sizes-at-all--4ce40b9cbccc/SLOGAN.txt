The complete bivariate second-moment generating function of binary deletion-ball sizes is rational and explicitly determined for all source lengths and all pairs of deletion radii. In particular, the three-deletion-ball variance is \(\frac{(n-3)(n-2)(3n^3-12n^2-13n+94)}{768}\) for \(n\ge3\), while the variance of the total number of distinct subsequences grows at exponential rate \((5+\sqrt{17})/4\).
