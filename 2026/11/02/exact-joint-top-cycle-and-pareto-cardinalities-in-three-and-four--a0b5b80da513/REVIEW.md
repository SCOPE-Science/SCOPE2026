Direct integer majority margins and graph reachability implement the stipulated weak-majority top cycle; independent pairwise Pareto dominance tests implement the efficient set. No infinite extrapolation is made. The checker is evidence only in conjunction with the normalization argument and explicit definitions.

Full relevant primary article and closely related social-choice/matching papers were compared, and searches for aliases, exact parameters, size tables and broader reductions found no dominating statement. Publication priority remains qualified by possible unindexed or unpublished computations and tables.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The joint top-cycle/Pareto distribution measures a meaningful, otherwise unquantified selectivity/efficiency gap at the first house-market size where a rare additional top-cycle size appears. Its full-cycle probability 415/576 and the sharp exact 1/18 incidence of full-cycle versus three efficient assignments offer reproducible benchmarks for evaluating majority-based assignment rules under impartial culture. A complete natural finite classification can be valuable without a general-size theorem; the result is not just recomputing the known possible sizes.
