For every fixed number \(h\) of named ultrametric levels and every \(k\ge2\), unrestricted noncounting \(k\)-variable first-order logic sees the levelwise branching numbers only through \(\min(b_i,k)\): precisely \((k-1)^h\) theories occur, with sharp smallest-representative bound \(k^h\). Two-variable counting logic, by contrast, recovers every branching number exactly.
