On a fan with unit path weights and common positive spoke weight \(t\), all \(\varphi(2n)\) arithmetically controllable single-site path actuators have the same exact Kalman determinant magnitude \(t|q_n(-t)q_n(nt)|/n\). Exactly \(n-1\) spoke resonances destroy every single-site controller, while a complete gcd-and-divisibility rule classifies the two-site replacements and quantifies the linear loss of determinant at resonance.
