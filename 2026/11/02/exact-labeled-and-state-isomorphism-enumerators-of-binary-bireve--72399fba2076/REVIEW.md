# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof identifies bireversibility with preservation of the output-swap set by the relative transition permutation. Direct two-output inverse-reversibility checking supports this equivalence on every labeled automaton through five states. The labeled count follows by choosing the subset and its two restricted permutations; the state-isomorphism formula follows by centralizers, induced permutations of equal-length cycles, and Burnside's lemma. Both connected enumerators follow from component decompositions. The connectedness asymptotic uses explicit expectations of invariant subsets and a matching singleton lower bound. Standalone enumeration and canonicalization reproduce the numerical coefficients. The proof, not the finite experiments, establishes the infinite claims.

## Originality — PASS, with documented source-access risk

Searches considered the cycle-invariant normal form, binary bireversibility, all-state isomorphism counts, connected transducers, exact partition enumerators, and the connectedness asymptotic. The most relevant 2026 work by Curtin and Savchuk describes structural correspondences and a broader isotopism framework but does not give these count formulae in the accessible abstract or detailed review. Its full primary text could not be obtained for direct comparison. Woryna's two-state group classification and Godin–Klimann's connectedness-dependent group theory concern different invariants. The GAP package independently gives twelve labeled two-state bireversible machines, matching the formula, and eight classes under joint state-plus-alphabet relabeling, versus the ten state-only classes here. Earlier finiteness tables do not establish the all-length orbit and component series. A hidden formula in inaccessible or unindexed primary work remains the principal publication-priority risk.

## Value — PASS

This is a natural, complete all-state enumeration for a structurally distinguished class of finite transducers. The flat distribution of output-swap counts, explicit orbit polynomial, separate connected labeled and unlabelled series, and quantitative asymptotic supply reusable benchmarks for computer searches and the comparison of connected versus unrestricted bireversible automata. The result is neither an arbitrary finite slice nor a reparameterization of an existing exact table; the ordinary Burnside and exponential-formula tools are connected by a special binary structural lemma.

## Limitations

Only a fixed binary alphabet and state-only isomorphism are counted. The result does not classify minimal automata, generated groups, or broader isotopisms. The full text of the 2026 motivating paper remains uninspected, and no independent external review has occurred.
