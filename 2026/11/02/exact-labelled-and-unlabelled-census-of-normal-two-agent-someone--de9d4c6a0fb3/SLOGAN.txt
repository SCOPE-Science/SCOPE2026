For two finite \(\mathsf{S5}\) agents, the disjunction of their knowledge operators preserves all intersections precisely when their information partitions are h-equivalent. The exact labelled census has exponential generating function \(\exp(2\exp(e^z-1)-e^z-1)\), and the unlabelled census has ordinary generating function \(\prod_{m\ge1}(1-z^m)^{-(2p(m)-1)}\); four worlds, with exactly six ordered labelled pairs, are the first size where local normality holds without one globally finer agent.
