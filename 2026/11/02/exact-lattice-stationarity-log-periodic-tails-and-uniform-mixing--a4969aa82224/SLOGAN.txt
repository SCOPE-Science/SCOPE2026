For the minimal accelerated symmetric lattice walk, the complete invariant law sits on exponentially spaced sites and has an exact log-periodic survival tail with \(\liminf zS(z)=3/5\) and \(\limsup zS(z)=6/5\). Nonetheless, the walk admits a uniform four-step regeneration probability of \(1/16\), giving exponential total-variation mixing despite an infinite stationary absolute first moment.
