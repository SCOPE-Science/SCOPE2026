For binary rooted-tree partial automorphisms, every prescribed nonempty subset of terminal vertices occurs as the domain's bottom level exactly \(2^{2^n-1}\) times, irrespective of its geometric shape. The exact domain-rank polynomial is \(2^{2^n-1}(1+t)^{2^n}-1\), and a random terminal-domain subset is asymptotically indistinguishable from independent fair-coin selection.
