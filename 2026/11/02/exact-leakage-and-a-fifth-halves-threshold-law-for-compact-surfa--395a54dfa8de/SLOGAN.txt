Detuning the two equal pendant couplings of a compact surface Fano mode at attachment \(N=3m+1\) produces an exact quadratic outgoing linewidth with prefactor proportional to \((2m+1)^2/(m+1)^3\). At the background propagation threshold the linewidth instead opens as the \(5/2\) power for inward detuning, while the opposite detuning produces a genuinely bound state below the continuum.
