For positive multiplicative \(f\) with distinct consecutive prime-power values, the quotient of \(\prod_{i=0}^k f(n+i)\) by \(f(\operatorname{lcm}(n,\ldots,n+k))\) has exact least period \(\prod_{p\le k,\,p^{\lfloor\log_p k\rfloor}\nmid(k+1)}p^{\lfloor\log_p k\rfloor}\). This settles the divisor-sum instances \(f=\sigma_\alpha\) (\(\alpha\ge0\)) of a least-period question raised by Qian, Tan, and Hong.
