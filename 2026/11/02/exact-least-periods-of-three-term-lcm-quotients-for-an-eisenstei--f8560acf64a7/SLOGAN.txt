For every positive odd \(m\), the quotient of the product of three consecutive values of \(t^3+2m\) by their least common multiple has exact least period \(2\operatorname{lcm}(108m^2+1,27m^2+16)\). Its closed three-gcd factorization reveals an exceptional common factor \(7\) exactly when \(m\equiv\pm3\pmod7\).
