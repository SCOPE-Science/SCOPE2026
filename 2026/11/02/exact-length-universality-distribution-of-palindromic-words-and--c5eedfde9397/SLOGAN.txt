The complete length-by-subsequence-universality distribution of labeled palindromes over any fixed \(q\ge2\) letters has one explicit rational bivariate generating function. Its extremal coefficients classify all shortest \(k\)-universal palindromes, with length \(qk\) for even \(k\), length \(q(k+1)-1\) for odd \(k\), and exactly \((q!)^{\lceil k/2ceil}\) labeled minimizers.
