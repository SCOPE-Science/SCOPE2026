For the period-doubling word \(p=\mu^\omega(0)\) with \(\mu(0)=01\) and \(\mu(1)=00\), exactly two length-\(n\) cyclic-rotation classes have every rotation occurring as a factor when \(n=2^a\) or \(n=3\cdot2^a\), and none do at any other positive length. A parity-based desubstitution gives a bijection of the classes at lengths \(n\) and \(2n\).
