For every integer radix \(r\ge2\), separating even and odd base-\(r\) digits gives exact additive complements with counting-product limsup exactly \(2(r+1)/(r+2)\); in particular the ternary and quaternary values are \(8/5\) and \(5/3\). A simultaneous three-invariant digit induction proves a sharp global pointwise bound.
