For identical additive values with \(m\) unit goods and \(k\) value-\(r\) goods, internal transfers subject to heterogeneous individual budgets are envy-freeable exactly when two explicit sums of integer capacities cover the total value and the large-item count. This yields a two-pass constructive algorithm and an exact closed-form minimum common budget for every population size and every integer \(r\ge2\).
