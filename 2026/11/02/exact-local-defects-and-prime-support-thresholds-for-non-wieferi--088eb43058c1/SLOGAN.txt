For each fixed finite odd-prime support \(P\), the order-based non-Wieferich integers are exactly the multiples of one explicit minimum \(W(P)\) that retain support \(P\). An exact prime-by-prime valuation formula for \(\gcd(n,(2^{\operatorname{ord}_n(2)}-1)/n)\) identifies every exceptional exponent vector, even when a support prime itself is Wieferich.
