For a four-site open exclusion chain with reservoirs centered at density \(1/2\) with a single site-departure defect of rate \(t>0\), the exact near-equilibrium rectification coefficient has the strict sign of \(t-1\): a slow trap and a fast site produce opposite local current asymmetries. Four sites are the sharp minimum in the clean-boundary one-defect family, and integer-polynomial stationarity certificates prove the sign without a mean-field approximation.
