For any finite set of independently reinforced springs in an infinite equal-mass chain, the exact number of localized phonon modes is one plus the positive inertia of a weighted-path pseudoinverse matrix. For \(p\) consecutive equally reinforced springs of contrast \(\alpha>0\), it is exactly \(\left\lceil(2p/\pi)\arctan\sqrt\alpha\right\rceil\), with a sharp separate threshold for every additional mode.
