The geometric \(\ell^{8/3}\)-weighted divergence-free Matérn mixture has a positive trace increment scaling like \(r^{2/3}\) only up to a provably nonconstant log-periodic factor, with every Fourier coefficient given explicitly by a Gamma function. The sharp best-constant scaling bias vanishes like \((\log q)^{-5/6}e^{-\pi^2/\log q}\) as the scale ratio \(q\downarrow1\), while finite-scale cutoff errors admit two explicit geometric tail bounds.
