For the first three positive odd dilations of the Thue--Morse signs, the exact dyadic triple correlation is \(\sum_{n<2^r}(-1)^{s_2(n)+s_2(3n)+s_2(5n)}=-2\lfloor(r-1)/2\rfloor\) for every \(r\geq1\). A seven-state binary-carry transfer identity proves this logarithmic, parity-dependent bias and gives the exact counts of its two joint digit-parity outcomes.
