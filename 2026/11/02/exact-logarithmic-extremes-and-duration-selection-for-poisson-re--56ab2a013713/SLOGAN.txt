For fully renewed scaled Brownian motion with Poisson resets, the largest absolute-area burst among \(n\) complete cycles grows as an exact constant times \((\log n)^{(\beta+3)/2}\). Its generating reset interval has only the fraction \((\beta+2)/(\beta+3)\) of the asymptotic longest-interval duration, so the largest area and longest wait are distinct with probability tending to one as the sample grows.
