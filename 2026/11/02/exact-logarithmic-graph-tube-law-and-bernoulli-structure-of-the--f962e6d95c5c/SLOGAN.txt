For the geometric full-branch fractal tent map, the graph's Euclidean \(\varepsilon\)-tube area is \(4\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)/\log(1/q)+O_q(\varepsilon)\), so its exact box dimension is \(1\), despite published numerical values above \(1\). The same map is Lebesgue-Bernoulli, with exact entropy \(-\log((1-q)/2)-q\log q/(1-q)\).
