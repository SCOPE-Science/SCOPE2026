For identical unit-cost chores, ex-ante weighted envy-freeness can coexist with ex-post WEFX exactly when the proportional count vector lies in a simple tie-hypersimplex of Adams priority allocations. At or above one chore per agent, this requires rational entitlements; for rational weights the answer is periodic in the chore count, and for two agents with reduced denominator \(b\) the permitted counts are precisely \(m\equiv0,1\pmod b\).
