For a binary shift with \(h\) ones separated by at least \(h+1\) positions, the probabilities of exactly \(j\le h\) carries are the universal values \(\binom{h+j-1}{j}2^{-(h+j)}\), independent of the positions. Thus its digit-sum nondecrease density is exactly \(\tfrac12+\tfrac12\binom{2h}{h}4^{-h}\).
