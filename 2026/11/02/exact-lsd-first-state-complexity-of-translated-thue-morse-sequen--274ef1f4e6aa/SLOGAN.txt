For every nonnegative shift \(c\), the minimum number of LSD-first binary automaton states generating the translated Thue–Morse sequence \(t(n+c)\) has an explicit finite digit-residue formula. Among shifts \(2^k\le c<2^{k+1}\) with \(k\ge3\), the exact minimum is \(2k+3\), uniquely at \(c=2^k\), whereas the exact maximum is \(4k+1\), reached at precisely \(2^{k-3}\) shifts in the upper quarter of the block whose final two binary digits are `10`.
