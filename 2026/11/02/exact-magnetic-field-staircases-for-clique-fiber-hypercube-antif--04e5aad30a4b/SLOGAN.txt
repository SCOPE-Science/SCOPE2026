On a dimension-scalable family of frustrated Ising graphs that includes the 24-site rhombicuboctahedron, the entire zero-temperature magnetization staircase follows from an exact balanced-clique cut formula. Parity determines the first plateau, and the three-dimensional case has exact critical fields \(2J\) and \(4J\) with 1,392 and 13,875 coexisting labelled ground states.
