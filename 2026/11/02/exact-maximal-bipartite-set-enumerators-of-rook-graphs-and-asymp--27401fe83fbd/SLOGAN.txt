In rook graphs, the complete multiplicities of inclusion-maximal induced bipartite sets have an exact coefficient formula: on unequal boards every such set has size \(2\min(m,n)\), while on an \(r\)-by-\(r\) board the two counts are \(r^2A_{r-1}\) and \(A_r\), where \(A_r=(r!)^2[t^r]e^{-t/2}/\sqrt{1-t}\). Among balanced-board maximal sets, the smaller size occurs with probability \(1/2+1/(8r)+O(r^{-2})\).
