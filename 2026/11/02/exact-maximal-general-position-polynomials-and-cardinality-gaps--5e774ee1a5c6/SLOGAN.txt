For the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), every inclusion-maximal general-position configuration is either a spanning bipartite star forest or one of two precisely characterized nonspanning star coverings; an explicit exponential generating function counts all configurations by size. When \(2\le m\le n\), maximal configurations have sizes exactly \(\{m\}\cup\{n,n+1,\ldots,m+n-2\}\), leaving arbitrarily long forbidden-size intervals even though the extremal sizes were previously known.
