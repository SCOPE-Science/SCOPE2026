The geodesically convex vertex subsets of \(J(n,k)\) have exact VC dimension \(n-\min\{2,k-1,n-k-1\}\), with an explicit all-parameter enumeration of the maximum shattered supports. For interior ranks these extremizers are uniquely encoded by three-part coordinate partitions, giving exactly \(560\) maximum supports in \(J(8,4)\).
