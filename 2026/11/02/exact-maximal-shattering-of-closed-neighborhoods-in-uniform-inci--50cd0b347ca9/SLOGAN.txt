For the closed-neighborhood concept class of the bipartite incidence graph between \(n\) points and all their \(k\)-subsets, \(2\le k<n\), the exact VC dimension is \(\min\{k,n-k+2\}\), and every rank has a complete formula for the number of maximum shattered supports. In rank two, the Littlestone dimension is three from \(n=4\) onward although the VC dimension is two.
