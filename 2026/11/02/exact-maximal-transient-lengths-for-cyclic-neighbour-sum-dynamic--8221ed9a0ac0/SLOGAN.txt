The exact maximal first-cycle-entry time of every cyclic neighbour-sum map modulo \(m\) is an explicit maximum over prime-power factors of \(m\). For odd \(p\) dividing an even tuple length \(n\), it is exactly \(p^{\nu_p(n)-1}\bigl(a(p-1)+1\bigr)\) modulo \(p^a\), proving the prime-power equality conjectured in the 2024 Lewis–Tefft upper-bound theorem.
