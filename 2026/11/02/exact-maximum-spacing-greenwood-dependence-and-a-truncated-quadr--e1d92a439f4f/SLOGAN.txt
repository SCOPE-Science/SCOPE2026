For \(m-1\) uniform points on an interval, the longest gap and the Greenwood sum of squared gaps have exact positive covariance \((H_m^2+H_m^{(2)}-2H_m)/[m(m+1)(m+2)]\), yet their correlation decays as \((\sqrt6/(2\pi))(\log m)^2/\sqrt m\). A finite inclusion–exclusion formula also gives the exact expected quadratic gap energy conditional on any bound on the longest gap.
