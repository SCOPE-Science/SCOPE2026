For a scalar finite sum of positive quadratics, the sampling law \(p_i=(1+\eta a_i)/(n+\eta\sum_j a_j)\) makes a single randomly chosen proximal update match the full proximal update in conditional expectation, uniquely for all possible centers. The stationary time-averaging variance coefficient cannot decrease with step size for two components, yet strictly decreases for a natural three-component unequal-curvature family, even as its stationary single-iterate variance increases.
