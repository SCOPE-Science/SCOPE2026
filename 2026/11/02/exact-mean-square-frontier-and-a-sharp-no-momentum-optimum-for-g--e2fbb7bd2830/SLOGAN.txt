For rank-one Gaussian momentum Kaczmarz on an identity system, the exact mean-square stability condition is \(0\le\omega<1\) and \(0<\alpha<2(1-\omega^2)/(d(1+\omega)+2)\). Across all nonnegative fixed momentum and positive stepsizes, the unique fastest true mean-square rate is \((d+1)/(d+2)\), attained only without momentum at \(\alpha=1/(d+2)\).
