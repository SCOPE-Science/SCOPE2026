For a Brownian searcher covering both endpoints of a reflecting interval under fixed-rate resetting with retained visit memory, the mean-optimal reset site jumps discontinuously from the endpoints to the midpoint at \(rL^2/D=2.14832149293397\ldots\). Yet the midpoint uniquely maximizes the exponential long-time cover-completion rate at every positive reset rate, creating a sharp mean-versus-tail design conflict below that threshold.
