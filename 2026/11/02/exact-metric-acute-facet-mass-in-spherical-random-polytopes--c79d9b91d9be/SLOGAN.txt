For a Euclidean convex hull of any \(n\ge4\) uniform points on the two-sphere, the fractions of expected facet area and expected squared facet area supplied by acute triangles are exactly \(1/4+4/\pi^2\) and \(53/70\), respectively, independently of \(n\). These metric shares exceed the previously known acute fraction \(1/2\) by facet count; the complete finite-sample expectation formulas follow from an explicitly evaluated angular shape integral.
