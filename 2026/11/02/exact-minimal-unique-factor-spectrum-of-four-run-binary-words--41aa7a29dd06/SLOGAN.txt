For \(W=0^a1^b0^c1^d\), all minimal unique factors fall into five explicit run-gap families, giving the sharp count \(1+\mathbf1_{a\ne c}+\mathbf1_{b\ne d}+\mathbf1_{|a-c|\ge2}+\mathbf1_{|b-d|\ge2}\). The complete bivariate length/count generating function is \(2t((z^2+2tz^3+2t^2z^4/(1-z))/(1-z^2))^2\), so almost every long four-run binary word has exactly five minimal unique factors.
