In three dimensions, every invertible matrix sampled by \(\ell\) independent Rademacher sketch columns has normalized expected squared-Frobenius range-projection loss at most \((2^{\ell+1}-4/3)/4^\ell\), with equality precisely at scaled orthogonal matrices. The exact rank-deficiency probability is \(6/2^\ell-8/4^\ell\), so even oversampling beyond the full rank retains a quantifiable nonzero exact-recovery failure probability.
