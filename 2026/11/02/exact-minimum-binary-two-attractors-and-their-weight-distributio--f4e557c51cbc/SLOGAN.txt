Every nonconstant binary word needs either two or three positions to attract all factors of length at most two; the three-position exceptions are exactly the two explicit alternating-interior families \(0^a(10)^b1^c\) and \(1^a(01)^b0^c\), with \(a,c\ge2\) and \(b\ge1\). Their exact fixed-weight count is \(2\max\{0,\min(t-2,n-t-2)\}\), so the exceptional fraction of length-\(n\) words is exponentially small.
