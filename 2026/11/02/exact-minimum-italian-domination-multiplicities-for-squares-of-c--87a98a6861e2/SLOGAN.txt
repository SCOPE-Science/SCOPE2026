For the squared cycle \(C_n^2\), the number of minimum-weight Italian dominating functions is exactly \(3\), \(n+10\mathbf1_{n=5}\), or \(n(n+5)/6+10\mathbf1_{n=10}\) as \(n\equiv0,2,1\pmod3\), respectively. Every minimizer is a binary cyclic gap tiling except five single-\(2\) tilings at \(n=5\) and five opposite-\(2\) tilings at \(n=10\); the minimum weight itself was known previously.
