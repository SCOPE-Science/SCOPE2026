For the binary word with ones exactly at the zero-based positions \(2^j-1\), the smallest string attractor for its length-\(n\) prefix has size \(3+j\) precisely when \(6\cdot4^j\le n<24\cdot4^j\), with the initial values one for lengths one and two, and two for lengths three through five. This supplies the missing all-length matching lower bounds for the previously established explicit powers-of-four upper constructions.
