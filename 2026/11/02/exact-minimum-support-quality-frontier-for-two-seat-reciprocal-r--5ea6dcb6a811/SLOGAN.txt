For every two-seat approval election with at least \(d\ge2\) supporters per candidate, the sharp reciprocal-JR quality retention is \((3n-2d)/(4(n-d))\) below half-electorate support and exactly \(1\) from half-electorate support onward. A single candidate substitution repairs any violating quality-optimal pair; every quality-optimal pair is already feasible when \(2d>n\).
