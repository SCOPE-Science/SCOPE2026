A heterogeneous bouquet of complete cliques has an exact globally optimal resistance-minimizing conductance allocation under independent positive spoke and peripheral-edge prices. Every blade's peripheral edges are active precisely when its own price ratio is below the same universal threshold \(Q_j/P_j<2(M+1)/M\), and the optimal global budget is split in explicit square-root proportions, allowing any mixed pattern of retained and discarded clique interiors.
