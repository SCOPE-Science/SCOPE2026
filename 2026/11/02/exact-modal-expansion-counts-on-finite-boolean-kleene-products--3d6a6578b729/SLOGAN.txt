For the fixed labelled product of \(h\) Boolean two-valued and \(k\) Kleene three-valued factors, the number of modal operators satisfying the three defining Kleene-modal axioms is exactly \(2^{h^2}(2^{h+k}+3^{h+k})^k\). Every such operator has a unique atomwise finite-set normal form: classical output coordinates use one shared neighborhood, while three-valued outputs allow an inactive box or a nested pair of neighborhoods.
