Two individually stable directed shear couplings can lose finite stationary variance before their averaged response loses stability. For a Poisson-alternating, noise-driven two-coordinate system, the second-moment threshold \(q_2=4a^3/(b^2-4a^2)\) and the first-moment threshold \(q_1=a^2/(b-2a)\) enclose an exact gap of width \(a^2/(b+2a)\) whenever \(b>2a\). The critical diverging fluctuations have correlation \(2a/b\), strictly below perfect synchrony.
