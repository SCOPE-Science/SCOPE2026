An exact finite bit-vector certificate determines \(A(d)\), the longest monochromatic arithmetic progression with difference \(d\), for every \(1\le d\le1025\) in the binary fixed point \(0\mapsto0011\), \(1\mapsto1100\). It establishes the unique bounded maximizers \((d,A(d))=(65,66),(255,260),(1025,1026)\) at the first three outstanding substitution scales.
