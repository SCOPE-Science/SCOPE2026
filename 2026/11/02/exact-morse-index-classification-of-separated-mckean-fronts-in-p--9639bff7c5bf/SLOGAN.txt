The \(2^m\) widely separated McKean multi-fronts in a periodic necklace medium have a complete spectral Morse-index classification: each letter Q contributes exactly one positive transmission eigenvalue, so precisely \(\binom m k\) words have index \(k\). An exact finite Green-matrix criterion covers arbitrary simple-crossing profiles; spectral stability here does not assert nonlinear Lyapunov stability or stability against all necklace-graph perturbations.
