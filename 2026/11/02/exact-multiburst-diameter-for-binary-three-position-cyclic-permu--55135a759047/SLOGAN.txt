For binary words of length \(n\ge3\) and weight \(1\le k<n\), the greatest minimum number of sequential cyclic rotations of a contiguous three-symbol window needed to transform one word into another is exactly \(\left\lceil k(n-k)/2\right\rceil\). The extremal pair is \(1^k0^{n-k}\) and \(0^{n-k}1^k\), and a transport-duality proof shows the bound holds for every composition and length.
