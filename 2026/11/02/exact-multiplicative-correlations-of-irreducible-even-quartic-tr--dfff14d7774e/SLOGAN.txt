For odd prime powers, the exact simultaneous irreducibility count of \(X^4+aX^2+b\) and \(X^4+aX^2+ub\) is zero for incompatible square classes, equals \((q+1)/2\) on the diagonal, and equals \((q+1+a_q(E_u))/4\) at every nontrivial square ratio, where \(E_u\) is a Legendre elliptic curve. An exact sum over all such square ratios, and Hasse's bound, respectively quantify the overall correlation and guarantee common irreducible quartics at every compatible ratio.
