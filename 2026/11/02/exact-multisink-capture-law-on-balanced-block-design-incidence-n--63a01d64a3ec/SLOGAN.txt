For any balanced \(2\)-design, three or more positive-rate point absorbers capture a uniformly mixed quantum excitation with exact probability \(2(t+1)/(v+b)\), independent of their placement. Each block's absorption is \(v/b-u(s-u)/(s(\rho-\lambda))\), so a nonsymmetric incidence design admits no perfectly absorbed block site even with almost all points absorbing.
