On the incidence graph of a nontrivial balanced \(2\)-design with \(v\) points and \(b\) blocks, uniform positive hopping and \(3\le t<v\) positive-rate point absorbers give an exact capture probability \(2(t+1)/(v+b)\) from the uniformly mixed initial state, independent of absorber placement. For a block with \(u\) unmarked points, the probability is \(v/b-u(s-u)/(s(\rho-\lambda))\), where \(s=v-t\), \(\rho\) is point replication and \(\lambda\) is pair replication. No block site can be captured with certainty in a nonsymmetric design, even with almost all points absorbing.
