For equally many agents of two types, with mutual friendship \(+1\) across types and hostility \(-q\) within types, every Nash-stable coalition is exactly color-balanced; a sharp size-spread or size-cap rule then classifies and counts all equilibria. The price of anarchy has an exact parameter phase diagram, including infinite-welfare-loss resonances at \(q=k/(k-1)\) when \(k\ge2\) divides the population of either type.
