On every labeled double star \(D_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge1\), the unnormalized score-based social distance game \((1,0,-1)\) has exactly \(ab+3\) Nash-stable partitions, all explicitly classified. Optimal welfare is \(2(a+b)\), but the worst Nash equilibrium has welfare \(4\); thus the exact price of anarchy is \((a+b)/2\), unbounded even among diameter-three trees, while the price of stability equals one.
