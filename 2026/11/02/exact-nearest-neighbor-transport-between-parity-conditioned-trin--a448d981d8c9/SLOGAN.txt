The integer-conditioned and half-integer-conditioned laws of every independent sum of \(\{0,\frac12,1\}\)-valued variables admit a coupling that moves every outcome by exactly \(\frac12\). Hence their Wasserstein distances \(W_p\) equal \(\frac12\) simultaneously for all \(1\le p\le\infty\), even when the component probabilities differ and include boundary values.
