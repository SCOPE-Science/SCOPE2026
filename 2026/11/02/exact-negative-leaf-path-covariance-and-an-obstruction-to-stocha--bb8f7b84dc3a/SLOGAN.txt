For a uniform random binary search tree, the terminal-key leaf count and total internal path length have exact covariance \(-(n^2+n-6)/(9n)\), negative at every size \(n\geq3\), even though their correlation decays at rate \(n^{-1/2}\). This negative dependence does not extend to conditional stochastic ordering: at \(n=7\), the path-length distributions given three versus four leaves have crossing distribution functions.
