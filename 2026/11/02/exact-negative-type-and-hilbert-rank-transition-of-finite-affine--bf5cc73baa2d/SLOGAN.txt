For every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge2\), the unweighted incidence-graph metric has sharp generalized roundness \(\log_2(q/(q-1))\): the parallel line classes completely determine the exponent even after all point vertices are added. At the critical snowflake the Euclidean dimension drops by \(q\) for \(q\ge3\), while the order-two case has an exceptional five-dimensional equality space and embeds in \(\mathbb R^4\).
