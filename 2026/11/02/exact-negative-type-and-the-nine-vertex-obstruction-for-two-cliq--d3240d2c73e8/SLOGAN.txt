For two complete graphs glued along a nontrivial clique, the exact generalized roundness is \(\log_2\!\bigl(1+h^{-1}+\sqrt{(h^{-1}+a^{-1})(h^{-1}+b^{-1})}\bigr)\). The unit graph metric has \(1\)-negative type precisely when \((h-2)ab\le h+a+b\), and its first failures within this family occur at nine vertices in exactly two graph types.
