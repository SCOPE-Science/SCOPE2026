For every thick generalized quadrangle of order \((s,t)\), its point-line graph has an exact sharp negative-type exponent: the unique positive root of \(A2^p+B4^p-C3^p-D=0\), with explicitly given parameter coefficients. The endpoint degeneracy is always a single contrast between uniformly weighted points and lines, not a nonconstant incidence harmonic; all admissible Gaussian distance kernels are strictly positive definite at every positive scale.
