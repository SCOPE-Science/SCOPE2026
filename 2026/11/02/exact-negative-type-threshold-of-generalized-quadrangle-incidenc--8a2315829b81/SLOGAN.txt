Every finite generalized quadrangle of order \((q,q)\) has an exact supremal negative-type exponent given by the unique positive root of \(-(q+1)+q(q+1)2^p-q^2(q+1)3^p+q^3 4^p=0\), and the only critical equality direction is its point-versus-line bipartition. The exponent decays as \(1/(q^3\log(4/3))\), with an explicit second term and an exact one-dimensional loss of affine Hilbert snowflake rank at the threshold.
