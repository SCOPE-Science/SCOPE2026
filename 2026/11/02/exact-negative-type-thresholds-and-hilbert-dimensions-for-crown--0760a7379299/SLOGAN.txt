For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}\setminus M_n\), the exact supremal negative-type exponent is \(1\) at \(n=3,4\), and for \(n\ge5\) is the unique \(p\in(0,1)\) solving \((n-1)(2^p-1)=3^p\). Its critical Hilbert snowflake embedding dimension is exactly \(3\) at \(n=3,4\) and \(2n-2\) thereafter, while deleting the matching increases the exponent over \(K_{n,n}\) by a sharp asymptotic \(\frac{\log3}{(\log2)^2(n-1)^2}\).
