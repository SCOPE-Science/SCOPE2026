For each unit-edge two-level tree with \(k\ge2\) identical branches carrying \(m\ge1\) leaves, the exact supremal negative-type exponent is the unique interior root of an explicit two-by-two determinant, except that the five-vertex path has exponent two. In every nonexceptional case the critical Hilbert snowflake realization loses exactly one affine dimension, while for fixed \(k\) the excess exponent obeys \(\rho_{k,m}-1\sim1/[m(k\log(32/27)+\log(27/16))]\).
