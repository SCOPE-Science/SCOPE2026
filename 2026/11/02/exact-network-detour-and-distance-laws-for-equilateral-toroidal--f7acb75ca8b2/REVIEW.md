# Scientific review: network detour on toroidal grids

**Correctness — PASS.** The edge-route lemma is proved directly from the four endpoint paths, with the same-edge direct route treated separately. Its only strict network-versus-ambient exceptions are aligned parallel edges in different transverse levels; exact ordered-pair counting gives the exceptional mass, while a square-area calculation gives the full conditional \(\mathrm{Beta}(1,2)\) detour. Orientation conditioning gives the whole CDF with all coefficients fixed, and summation of cyclic vertex distances proves the parity mean and its complete sign classification. Independent exact-rational code reconstructed 266,256 distances and 91 polygon-integral mean/CDF comparisons. Risk: finite checks are not proof of the infinite family; the analytic route-exhaustion argument supplies that proof.

**Originality — PASS under bounded documentary evidence.** The nearest primary paper (Cheng–Qiu–Shen 2011) computes only vertex-distance generating functions and vertex means for Cartesian products of cycles. Doig 2004 studies Hamiltonian runs of the same graphs; Qiang et al. 2020 study vertex eccentricities. The general weighted metric-graph algorithm of Garijo–Márquez–Silveira 2021 does not state the complete grid CDF, universal detour distribution, or exact parity/sign classification; Baptista–Kennedy–Mugnolo 2024 study different general mean bounds. Published mathematical records searched for toroidal grid metric distances instead concern topological complexes, graph structures or other functionals. Residual risk: older unindexed mean-distance results, especially Doyle–Graver (1982), were not fully inspected and may contain a related formula. This is a documented historical-priority uncertainty, not an assertion that a negative search proves novelty.

**Value — PASS.** The exact law answers a natural geometric-probability question for an infinite family of periodic street networks. The exceptional-event detour law quantifies precisely how the network path metric differs from its ambient taxicab torus; the sharp sign reversal at the triangular grids distinguishes effects of edge sampling from actual routing detours. The classification is a family-wide geometric fact, not a choice of arbitrary isolated parameters. Scope is limited to unit edges and two cyclic factors; the comparison mean on the ambient torus uses uniform area, a different measure from edge arclength.
