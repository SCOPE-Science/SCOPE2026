For the unit-edge toroidal grid \(C_m\square C_n\), the entire distance law between independent arclength-uniform points is an explicit finite piecewise-quadratic formula. The network's excess travel over the ambient Manhattan torus is zero except with probability \((m+n-2)/(4mn)\), when its conditional value is exactly \(\mathrm{Beta}(1,2)\); the network mean lies below the area-uniform torus mean only at \((m,n)=(3,3)\), coincides only at \((3,5)\) up to order, and exceeds it otherwise.
