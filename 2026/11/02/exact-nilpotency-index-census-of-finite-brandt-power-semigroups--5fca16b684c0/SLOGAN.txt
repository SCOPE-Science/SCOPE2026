For every finite group \(G\), the nonempty-subset power semigroup of the Brandt semigroup \(B_n(G)\) has nilpotency indices exactly \(1,\ldots,n\); a closed layer-composition formula counts every index, depending on \(G\) only through \(|G|\). At the maximal index \(n\ge2\), the exact multiplicity is \(2n!(2^{|G|}-1)^{n-1}2^{|G|(n-1)(n-2)/2}\).
