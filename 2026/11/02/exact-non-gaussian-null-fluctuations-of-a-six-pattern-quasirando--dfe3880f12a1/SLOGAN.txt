The six-pattern quasirandomness statistic has an exactly vanishing first-order projection under a uniform random permutation, so its null fluctuations occur at scale \(1/n\), not \(1/\sqrt n\). The limit is a non-Gaussian centered quadratic-normal spectral law with exact variance coefficient \(\lim_{n\to\infty}n^2\operatorname{Var}(T_n)=121/1800\).
