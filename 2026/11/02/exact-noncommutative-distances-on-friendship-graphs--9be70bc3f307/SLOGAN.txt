For every friendship graph of \(m\ge2\) unweighted triangles meeting at a hub, the adjacency-Dirac Connes distance is exactly \(\sqrt{2/3}\) within each triangle and \(\sqrt{3/2}\) between leaves of distinct triangles. All distances are independent of the number of triangles, and the cross-triangle sharp value follows from an exact five-dimensional skew-commutator reduction.
