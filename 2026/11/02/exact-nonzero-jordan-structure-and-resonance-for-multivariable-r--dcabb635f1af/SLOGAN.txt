Every rapidly decaying homogeneous Rhaly operator on Drury–Arveson space has a completely explicit nonzero Jordan diagram: each transverse monomial contributes one block whose length counts the repeated diagonal coefficients above its degree. For \(a_N=(N+1)q^N\), collisions occur exactly at \(q=((n+1)/(m+1))^{1/(m-n)}\), producing \(\binom{n+d-1}{d-1}\) genuine length-two blocks and an exact dimension-dependent multiplicity jump.
