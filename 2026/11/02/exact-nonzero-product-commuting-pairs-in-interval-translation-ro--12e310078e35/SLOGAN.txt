For the interval-translation rook monoid \(M_n\), the number of ordered commuting nonzero pairs with nonzero common product is exactly \(n(n+1)(2n^3+23n^2-3n+8)/60\); opposite strictly signed shifts contribute none. Together with the mutually annihilating pairs, this gives a complete finite-size commutation polynomial and the sharpened probability \(3/10+3/(10n)+15/(8n^2)+O(n^{-3})\).
