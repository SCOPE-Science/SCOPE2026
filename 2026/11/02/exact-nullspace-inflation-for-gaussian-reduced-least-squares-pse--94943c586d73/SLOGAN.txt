For rank-deficient reduced least squares with a Gaussian variable sketch, the excess squared error from coefficient-minimal pseudoinversion is exactly a \(\chi^2_{d-r}/\chi^2_{\ell-r+1}\) ratio, while minimum physical norm within the same random subspace replaces the numerator degree by \(d-\ell\). This sharp distinction can leave an infinite coefficient-solver mean-square error even when the Gaussian sketch spans the entire variable space and the physical-minimum solution is exact.
