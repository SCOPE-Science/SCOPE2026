For the one-dimensional Bernoulli phase-fitted Helmholtz method with zero impedance data, the exact interior Green matrix has a sharp source-amplification pole proportional to \((\pi-kh)^{-1}\) at the Nyquist boundary. Its largest singular value separates from the exact Frobenius bound by the explicit relative factor \(1-(m^2-1)(2m^2+7)(\pi-kh)^2/(90m^2)+O_m((\pi-kh)^4)\), where \(m\) is the number of interior nodes.
