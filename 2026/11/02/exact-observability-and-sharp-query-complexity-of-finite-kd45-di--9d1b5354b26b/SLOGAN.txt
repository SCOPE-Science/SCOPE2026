On a labelled \(n\)-world \(\mathsf{KD45}\) frame, exactly \(2^{n-1}-1\) atomic disagreement queries are necessary and sufficient to recover the entire group-disagreement operator, even when queries are adaptive and report every world's answer. With two agents, the exact number of distinct pointed disagreement predicates is \(1+(3^n-2^{n+1}+1)/2\), with every nonzero predicate recovering one unordered pair of disjoint successor sets.
