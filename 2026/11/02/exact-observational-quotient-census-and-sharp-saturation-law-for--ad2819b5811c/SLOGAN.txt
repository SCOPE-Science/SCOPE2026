For single-agent finite \(\mathsf{S5}\) models over any fixed finite Heyting truth-value algebra with exact tests, the sizes of their observational quotients have a complete bivariate exponential generating function indexed by realized atomic support sets. The universal quotient maximum \(m2^{m-1}\) is attained with probability tending to one, and its rare failure has a sharp stretched-exponential equivalent, including an exceptional factor \(e^{-1/8}\) when \(m=2\).
