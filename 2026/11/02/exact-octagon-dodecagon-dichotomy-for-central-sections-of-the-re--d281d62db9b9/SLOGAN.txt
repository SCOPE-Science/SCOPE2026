For every generic central plane section of the regular small rhombicuboctahedron, the polygon has exactly \(8\) or \(12\) sides, never \(10\). The octagon event occurs exactly when the largest absolute plane-normal coordinate exceeds \((1+\sqrt2)\) times the middle plus the smallest, and has probability \(24\arccos(1/\sqrt{5+2\sqrt2})/\pi-9\approx0.207864\).
