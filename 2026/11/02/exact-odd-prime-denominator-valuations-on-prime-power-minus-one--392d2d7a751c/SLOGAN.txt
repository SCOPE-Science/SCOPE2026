For every odd prime \(p\) and \(1\le r<p^m\), the binomial sum \(S_r(p^m-1)\) has exact valuation \(\nu_p(r)-m\), and its first nonzero \(p\)-adic digit is explicit. Thus the reduced denominator attains the entire \(p\)-primary factor of the universal binomial denominator bound, even for unbounded \(r\).
