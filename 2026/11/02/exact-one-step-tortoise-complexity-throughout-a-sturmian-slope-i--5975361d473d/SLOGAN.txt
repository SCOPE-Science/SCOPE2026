Every characteristic Sturmian word with irrational slope \(1/3<\alpha<2/5\) has one-step tortoise complexity exactly \(n-1\) at every length \(n\ge4\). Its only nontrivial factor-equivalence classes are two explicit pairs formed by prefixing the unique left-special factors with \(01/10\) or \(001/010\), so the defect from ordinary factor complexity is exactly two.
