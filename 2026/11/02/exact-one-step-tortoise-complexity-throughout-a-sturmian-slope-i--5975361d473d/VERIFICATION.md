# Reproducibility and limits

Run `python3 verify_substitution.py` and `python3 verify_rotation.py` with standard-library Python 3. Both scripts are complete and rely on no external files.

`verify_substitution.py` uses all legal two-block windows of \(\mu^k(0)\) and \(\mu^k(1)\), where \(\mu(0)=01\) and \(\mu(1)=0\). It enlarges \(k\) until the shortest substituted block has length at least the tested factor length. Every factor then lies inside one or two consecutive complete blocks, and the three legal adjacent source pairs \(00,01,10\) all occur, so its factor sets are exhaustive for each tested length. It tests \(n=1,\ldots,180\), including the two explicit nontrivial fibers.

`verify_rotation.py` obtains certified rational upper and lower bounds on each tested irrational slope using integer-square-root enclosures, derives each required mechanical digit only where both bounds certify the same floor, and applies a direct stack simulation to all observed factors. It independently checks \(n=1,\ldots,90\) at a slope near the lower endpoint, at the Fibonacci slope, and near the upper endpoint. Each observed factor set has exactly \(n+1\) members; this certifies completeness of those finite sets from the Sturmian factor-count proof. The outputs in `verify_substitution.out` and `verify_rotation.out` were generated by running the included programs.

Finite computations by themselves would not prove the statement for all lengths or uncountably many slopes. The unrestricted conclusion rests on the analytic and combinatorial proof in RESULT.md. No formal independent audit or external validation has been performed.
