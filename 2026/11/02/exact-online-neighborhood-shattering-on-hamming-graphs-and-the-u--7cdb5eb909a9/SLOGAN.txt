The exact open-neighborhood Littlestone dimension of every Hamming graph is at most three, with a complete sharp classification over all alphabet sizes and coordinate dimensions. The ternary two-dimensional Hamming graph \(H(2,3)\) is the unique full Hamming graph for which adaptive shattering is strictly stronger than static VC shattering: \(\operatorname{VCdim}=2<3=\operatorname{Ldim}\).
