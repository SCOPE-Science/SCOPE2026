For every Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\) with \(3\le k\le n-3\), the open-neighborhood Littlestone dimension is \(5\) exactly when \(\min(k,n-k)\ge4\) and \(\max(k,n-k)\ge6\), and equals \(4\) otherwise. A uniform depth-five upper bound holds even for induced subgraphs, and a complete adaptive tree on \(J(10,4)\) makes that bound sharp.
