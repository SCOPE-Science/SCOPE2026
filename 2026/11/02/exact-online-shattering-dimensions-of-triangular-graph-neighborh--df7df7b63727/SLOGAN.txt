The open-neighborhood Littlestone dimension of the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\) is exactly \(0,1,1,3,3,4,4,\ldots\) for \(n=2,3,4,5,6,7,8,\ldots\). Its uniform upper bound of four remains valid for every induced subgraph, with matching explicit depth-three and depth-four query trees at the sharp thresholds.
