For acyclic edge orientations of the 2-vertex-connected planar biclique \(K_{2,n}\), the exact adaptive shattering dimension is \(\lceil3n/2\rceil\), while the static VC dimension is \(n+1\). A closed residual-state rank formula explains why the gap \(\lceil n/2\rceil-1\) grows without bound despite 2-vertex-connectivity.
