For the two-community partisan voter mean field, the sign of the response of stable stationary polarization to either community's separate openness is given by an exact equilibrium-state criterion. A rational stable example has \(\partial P^*/\partial\gamma_1=688/785>0\) and \(\partial P^*/\partial\gamma_2=-1220/471<0\), so reducing one community's segregation can locally increase, not decrease, opinion polarization.
