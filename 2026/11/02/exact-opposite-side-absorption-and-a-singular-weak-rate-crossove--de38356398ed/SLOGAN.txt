For a Brownian searcher reset to one side of a transparent blinking target, the opposite-side capture probability is exactly \(\frac14[1-z+z^2e^{-b x_0}/(1+z)]\), where \(z=\sqrt{r/(r+2\gamma)}\) and \(b=\sqrt{(r+2\gamma)/D}\). It increases strictly with gate switching, decreases strictly with position resetting, and has a singular joint weak-rate crossover from \(1/4\) to \(1/8\) determined solely by the ratio \(r/\gamma\).
