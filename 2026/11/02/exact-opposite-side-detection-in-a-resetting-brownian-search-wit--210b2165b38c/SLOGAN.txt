For driftless Brownian motion reset to a positive point while an independent transparent/absorbing target blinks symmetrically, the exact probability of eventual detection from the opposite side is \(\frac14[1-k/h+k^2e^{-ah}/(h(h+k))]\), with \(k=\sqrt{r/D}\) and \(h=\sqrt{(r+2\gamma)/D}\). It decreases strictly with resetting, increases strictly with gate switching, and tends to \(1/4\) as switching becomes arbitrarily fast—even though an always-active target has zero opposite-side detections.
