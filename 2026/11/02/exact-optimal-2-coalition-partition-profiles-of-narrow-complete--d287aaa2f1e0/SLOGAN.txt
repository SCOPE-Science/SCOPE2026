For unbalanced complete bipartite graphs with smaller shore of size \(3\), \(4\), or \(5\), every maximum 2-coalition partition has one of exactly seven explicit block-occupancy profiles. Their labeled counts are respectively \(3t\), \(10t\), and \(25t+15t(t-1)(t-2)\), revealing a cubic multiplicity transition at shore size five even though the already-known optimal block count is always \(t+1\).
