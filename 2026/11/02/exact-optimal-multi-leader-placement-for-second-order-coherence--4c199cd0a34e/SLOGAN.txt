For every unweighted path and every number of perfectly grounded leaders \(2\le k\le n\), all optimal second-order-noise leader placements are exactly the allocations of the cheapest explicit marginal follower-gap costs. Both endpoints are leaders in every optimum exactly when \(n\le3k-2\), and for fixed \(k\) the interior gaps approach \(15^{1/3}\) times the endpoint tails.
