Every minimum outer-independent two-rainbow labeling of \(K_m\times K_n\), except at dimensions \((2,2)\) and \((2,3)\), empties one largest coordinate fiber and assigns singleton colors to all other vertices, with each color appearing in at least two columns of the remaining matrix. The exact labeled multiplicity is \(c(s,t)(2^{(s-1)t}-2-2t(2^{s-1}-1))\) for \(s=\min\{m,n\}\), \(t=\max\{m,n\}\), where \(c(s,t)=s\) for \(s<t\) and \(2s\) for \(s=t\); the two exceptional multiplicities are \(36\) and \(48\).
