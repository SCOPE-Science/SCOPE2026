For the cycle prism \(C_m\square K_2\), the number of optimal singleton three-rainbow assignments with named colours is exactly \(12\) when \(6\mid m\), \(12m\) when \(m\) is odd, and \(18m(3m-5)\) or \(18m(3m-1)\) when \(m\equiv2\) or \(4\pmod6\), respectively. An exact finite-state identity establishes these constant, linear and quadratic regimes for every \(m\ge3\).
