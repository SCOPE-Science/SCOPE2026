The numbers of minimum singleton three-rainbow dominating functions on labelled Möbius ladders of even order \(n\ge6\) exhibit a complete residue-class trichotomy: \(12\) for \(n\equiv6\pmod{12}\), \(6n\) for \(n\equiv0,4,8\pmod{12}\), and explicit quadratic formulas for the two remaining classes. A triangle-chain decomposition of the zero supports proves these exact named-colour multiplicities.
