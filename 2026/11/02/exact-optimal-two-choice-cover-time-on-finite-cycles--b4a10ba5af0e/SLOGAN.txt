The optimal two-offer walk covers the cycle \(C_n\) in expected time \(2n-\frac92+\frac{9}{2\cdot3^{n/2}}\) for even \(n\), or \(2n-\frac92+\frac{5}{2\cdot3^{(n-1)/2}}\) for odd \(n\). An optimal policy always chooses the offered direction toward the nearer end of the currently visited arc, sharpening the known linear-order cover bound to an exact finite-size formula.
