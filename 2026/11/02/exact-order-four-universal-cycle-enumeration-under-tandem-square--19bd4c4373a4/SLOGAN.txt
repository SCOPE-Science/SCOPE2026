Deleting all four-letter tandem squares \(abab\) leaves exactly \(q^{q^3-q^2+q-4}(q-1)^{2(q-1)}(q^2-2q+2)^{(q-1)(q-2)/2}((q-2)!)^{q^2}((q-1)!)^{q^3-q^2}\) universal cycles over a labeled \(q\)-letter alphabet, counted up to rotation. The same forbidden-factor language admits universal cycles at every order through four, but at no order five or higher.
