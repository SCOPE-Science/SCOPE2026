For normalized starlike functions of order \(\alpha\), the sharp parameterized second-Hankel constant is \((1-\alpha)^2\max\{1,(3-2\alpha)[(3-2\alpha)-4\mu(2-\alpha)/3]\}\) for \(0\le\mu\le1\). The extremal construction switches exactly at \(\mu=3(1-\alpha)/(3-2\alpha)\), and the same constant is sharp for radial-type starlike maps on the unit ball of every nonzero complex Banach space.
