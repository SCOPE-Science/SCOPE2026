For prime \(p=10n+1>13\), the cyclic order-ten genus-six quintic \(y^5=x(x^2-1)(x^2-\lambda)\) is ordinary except at exactly \(3n\) simple parameter roots, where its geometric \(p\)-rank is exactly \(4\) and its Jacobian \(a\)-number is exactly \(2\). These parameters represent exactly \(\lfloor3n/2\rfloor\) geometric isomorphism classes with full automorphism group cyclic of order ten.
