The exact dyadic correlation \(\sum_{n<2^r}(-1)^{s_2(n)+s_2(3n)}\) obeys a cubic recurrence and oscillates with the sharp scale \(2^{r/2}\), while the classical marginal bias has scale \(3^{r/2}\). Together the two terms determine all four joint binary-parity counts for \(n\) and \(3n\) at every dyadic length.
