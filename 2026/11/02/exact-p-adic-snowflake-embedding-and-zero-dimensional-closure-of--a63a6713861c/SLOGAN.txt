Pascal-row integers encode the \(p\)-adic input digits at exponentially separated output positions: \(\nu_p(T_p(x)-T_p(y))=p^{\nu_p(x-y)}\) for all distinct inputs. Their closure has exactly \(p^{\lceil\log_p N\rceil}\) residue classes modulo \(p^N\) and is a perfect, uncountable, Haar-null set of Hausdorff dimension zero.
