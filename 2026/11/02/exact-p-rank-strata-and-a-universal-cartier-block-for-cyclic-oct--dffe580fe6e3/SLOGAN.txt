For the genus-six cyclic-octic plane quintics \(y^4=x^5+r x^3+x\) in every prime characteristic \(p=8h+1>13\), the geometric \(p\)-rank is exactly \(2+2\mathbf1_{T_{2h,h}(r)\ne0}+2\mathbf1_{T_{4h,2h}(r)\ne0}\), with \(T_{m,j}=[z^j](1+rz+z^2)^m\). A two-dimensional Cartier block is invertible at every smooth parameter because the determinant of its unrooted coefficient matrix is a nonzero scalar times \((r^2-4)^{2h}\).
