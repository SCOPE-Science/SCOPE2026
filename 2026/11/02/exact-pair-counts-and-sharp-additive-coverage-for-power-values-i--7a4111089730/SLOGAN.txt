In index-one Galois rings, the number of distinct two-element subsets of nonzero power values summing to any target is given by an exact valuation formula. Every target is representable precisely except for \(q=3,m=1\) in odd characteristic, or in characteristic two unless \(m\ge2\) and either \(q\ge4\) or \(m>d\); in the binary residue-field case the threshold \(m=d+1\) is sharp.
