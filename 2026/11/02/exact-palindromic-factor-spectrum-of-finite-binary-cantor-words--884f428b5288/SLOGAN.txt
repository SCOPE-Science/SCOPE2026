At each level \(k\ge1\), the finite binary Cantor word has two one-letter palindromes, three palindromes at every odd length \(3\le n\le3^{k-1}\), one at each even length up to \(3^{k-1}\), and one at each longer odd length through \(3^k\), with no others. Explicit central-factor formulas identify every palindrome, not merely the total count.
