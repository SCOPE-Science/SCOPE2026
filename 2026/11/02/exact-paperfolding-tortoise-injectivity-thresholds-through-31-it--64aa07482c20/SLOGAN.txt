For the regular paperfolding word, the first 31 eventual injectivity thresholds for iterated binary tortoise are now exact: 8, 13, 14, 25, 26, 28, 29, 49, 50, 52, 53, 56, 57, 58, 61, 97, 98, 100, 101, 104, 105, 106, 109, 112, 113, 114, 116, 117, 121, 122, 125. A finite factor-skeleton exhaustion and a prefix-inheritance lemma prove each threshold for all greater word lengths; the first five values were already reported or suggested in prior work.
