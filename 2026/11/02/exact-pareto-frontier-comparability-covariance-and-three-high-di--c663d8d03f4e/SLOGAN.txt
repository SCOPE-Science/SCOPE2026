For uniform independent points in a cube, an exact rational formula links the Pareto-frontier size to the number of comparable pairs in every sample size and dimension. At fixed sample size the covariance has three successive scales \(-n(n-1)2^{-d}\), \(-n(n-1)(n-2)3^{-d}\), and \(n(n-1)(4n-6)4^{-d}\), revealing the first higher-order corrections to the sparse-comparability regime.
