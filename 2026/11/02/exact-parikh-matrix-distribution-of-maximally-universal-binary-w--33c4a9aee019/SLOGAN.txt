The exact Gaussian-binomial fiber distribution of binary words attaining the maximum subsequence-universality index is determined whenever one letter occurs exactly \(k\) times and the other \(k+s\) times, for every \(k\ge1\) and \(s\ge0\). Their unconditional density is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{\pi}\,k^{s+1/2}/(4^s s!2^k)\) for each fixed slack \(s\).
