# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

On the open two-node Prony stratum, the recurrence with node-sum \(S\) and node-product \(P\) gives a birational identification of the three-observation fiber with the smooth conic \(s_2S^2=s_0P^2+2s_2P+s_4\). The fourth coordinate is an explicit rational function on this conic. The polynomial leading-coefficient induction has a nonvanishing \(\Delta=s_2^2-s_0s_4\) factor; hence all asserted polynomial degrees are exact. For even exponents the degree is the degree of a polynomial in \(P\) times the degree-two conic projection. For odd exponents a univariate eliminant counts a unique \(S\) over each generic \(P\). Characteristic-zero separability and removal of finitely many non-generic points justify the generic-fiber count. A separate exact-arithmetic checker reproduces all six published finite examples and additional cases, but is not substituted for the universal proof.

## Originality: PASS, qualified

Reed--Tsakiris (2026, arXiv:2610.27554v1) give finiteness and broad degree bounds plus exactly six computer-tabulated instances of this pattern. They do not formulate or prove its all-\(n\) exact formula in the inspected main results, Sections 5.1 and 5.3, or appendices. Their exact rank-two result uses three consecutive observations and does not subsume \(\{1,3,5,n\}\). Batenkov (2013, arXiv:1308.0753v1) covers decimation of equally spaced measurements and ambiguity of recovered roots, not this single off-grid sparse pattern's generic algebraic projection degree. The six tabulated counts are prior results, not claimed as new. Targeted mathematical-record and literature searches did not identify an equivalent universal theorem. Absence from search is not a proof of priority; undiscovered earlier sparse-moment formulas remain a genuine risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS

Generic coordinate-projection degrees are natural numerical invariants of Hankel determinantal varieties and control the number of rank-two completions from four observed anti-diagonals. The recent motivating work identifies them as a principal geometric problem and supplies only much larger upper bounds for this sparse index set. A uniform exact formula, including alternating repeated degrees, settles this infinite natural sample design, and the conic proof geometrically explains why two parities differ. This is a motivated infinite-family enumeration rather than an arbitrary finite slice or a recomputation of the published six values.

## Limitations and closest literature

The result is over \(\mathbb C\), only for rank two and the four-coordinate pattern \(\{1,3,5,n\}\), with generic fibers only. It does not settle arbitrary sparse patterns or the non-generic scheme structure. Priority is qualified because not every potential earlier sparse interpolation source has been inspected. Independent external mathematical audit remains unperformed.
