For every positive ring length, elementary cellular automaton Rule 30 has exactly \(2^n-(s_n+1-2\mathbf{1}_{3\mid n})/2\) distinct one-step cyclic outputs, where \(s_0=3\), \(s_1=s_2=1\), and \(s_n=s_{n-1}+2s_{n-3}\). Its finite-period unreachable-output fraction therefore decays exponentially at rate \((\rho/2)^n\), where \(\rho\) is the positive root of \(t^3-t^2-2\).
