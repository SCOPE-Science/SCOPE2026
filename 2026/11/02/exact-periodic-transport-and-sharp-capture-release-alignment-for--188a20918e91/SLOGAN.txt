Periodic reversible trapping does not require a cell problem: the exact long-time run-and-tumble diffusivity is \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}=v^2/[(\alpha+\langle\gamma\rangle)(1+\langle\gamma/\delta\rangle)]\). For fixed spatial histograms, pairing the strongest capture zones with the fastest release zones maximizes spreading, whereas opposite pairing minimizes it.
