For two stars that alternate their hub vertex every \(\tau\) neutral birth–death updates, the complete single-mutant fixation law has explicit rational formulas in two phase eigenvalues and obeys the strict hierarchy \(p_A<p_B<p_L\) for every finite \(\tau\). The second hub's fixation probability crosses from \(o(1/n)\) to order \(1/n\) when \(\tau\) reaches order \(n^2\), whereas a static aggregate incorrectly makes the hubs equivalent.
