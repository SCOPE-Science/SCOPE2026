For logistic threshold-influence networks with \(s\) autonomous leaders and an acyclic follower graph, \(\beta=4\) is the sharp global phase boundary: above it the network has precisely \(3^s\) equilibria but only \(2^s\) sinks, each with an exact leader-coordinate cylinder basin of volume \(2^{-s}\). The equilibrium counts survive small feedback-edge perturbations even though these exact basin shapes need not.
