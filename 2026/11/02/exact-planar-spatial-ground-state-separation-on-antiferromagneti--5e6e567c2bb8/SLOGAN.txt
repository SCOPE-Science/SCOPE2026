The classical XY and Heisenberg antiferromagnetic spin rings have identical field-dependent ground-state energies for every even cycle and for the triangle, but **every odd cycle of length at least five has a strict planar energy penalty at every nonsaturated positive field**. The weak-field planar-to-spatial susceptibility ratio is exactly \(1/[2\cos(\pi/N)]\), equal to the inverse golden ratio for the pentagon and tending to one-half along longer odd rings.
