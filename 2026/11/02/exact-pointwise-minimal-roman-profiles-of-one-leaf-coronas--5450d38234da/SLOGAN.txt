Pointwise-minimal Roman functions on any one-leaf corona are classified by the set \(A\) of base vertices labeled \(2\); their full weight polynomial is \(\sum_{A\subseteq V}2^{|V\setminus N[A]|}z^{2|V|-|N(A)\setminus A|}\), with sharp extremal totals \(2^{|V|+1}-1\) and \(3^{|V|}\). For the sunlet \(C_n\odot K_1\), the number of such functions is exactly \(1+(1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n\) for \(n\ge3\).
