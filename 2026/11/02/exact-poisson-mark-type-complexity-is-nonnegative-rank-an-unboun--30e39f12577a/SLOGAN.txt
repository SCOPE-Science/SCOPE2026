The minimum number of latent child types in an exact marked-Poisson branching representation with parent-independent type-emission laws is the nonnegative rank of its interaction matrix. Consequently, even strictly positive symmetric rank-\(k\) kernels for every fixed \(k\ge3\) can require at least \(R(n)=\log_2 n+\tfrac12\log_2\log_2 n+O(1)\) types, including when the associated random graphs have uniformly bounded expected degrees.
