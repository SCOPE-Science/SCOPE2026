The normalized harmonic Poisson kernels have an exactly computable Hardy-to-Bergman fourth-power ratio, strictly increasing to \(5/2\) as the poles approach the boundary. This yields \(5/2\le\mathcal C\le(3+2\sqrt2)/2\) for the previously unresolved optimal harmonic \(h^2\)-to-\(b^4\) embedding constant, without claiming the lower endpoint is globally sharp.
