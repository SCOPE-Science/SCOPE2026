For every prime \(p\ge5\) and \(N\ge2\), the scalar quotient of \(Y^p-Y=XR(X)\) for a degree-\(p^N\) linearized \(R\) has coefficient-independent infinity semigroup \(\langle p,p+d,\ldots,p+\frac{p-1}{2}d\rangle\), with \(d=(p^N-1)/(p-1)-2\). Its conductor is \(p^N-p+2\), and all one-point Riemann–Roch dimensions have an explicit Apéry formula.
