For fully occupied radius-two Schelling rings, the exact number of all-satisfied binary layouts at every fixed population balance is a closed binomial sum: only monochromatic layouts, even-ring checkerboards, and layouts with all color-runs at least three occur. The unrestricted census grows as \(\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\) is the positive root of \(\lambda^3-\lambda^2-1=0\), giving an exponentially small proportion of fully satisfied random layouts.
