For a \(k+1\)-point equilateral metric and an added center at distance \(r\in[1/2,1]\), the exact inverse Lipschitz-free \(p\)-embedding distortion on the positive Dirac cone is \(\max\{1,[r^p(1+k^{p-1})]^{-1/p}\}\). A complete tree formula proves a sharp leaf-count threshold for the onset of positive-cone distortion, distinct from the unrestricted signed-vector problem.
