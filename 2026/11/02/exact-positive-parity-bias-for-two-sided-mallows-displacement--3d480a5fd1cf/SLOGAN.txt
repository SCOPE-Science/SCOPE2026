For every \(Q>1\), the odd-versus-even size asymmetry in limiting Mallows fixed-point means is exactly \(c_o(Q)-c_e(Q)=\prod_{k\ge1}[(1-Q^{-k})/(1+Q^{-k})]^2>0\). This infinite product proves both the strict sign and strict monotonicity questions posed for these constants in He, Müller and Verstraaten's study of Mallows cycle counts.
