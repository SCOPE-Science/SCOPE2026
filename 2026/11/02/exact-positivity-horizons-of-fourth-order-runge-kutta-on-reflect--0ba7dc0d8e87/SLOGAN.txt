For the reflecting \(n\)-site heat path, classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta preserves nonnegative data exactly through the size-dependent thresholds \(\rho_2,\rho_3,2/3,4/7,1/2\), with the last value holding for every \(n\ge6\). An exact four-vertex degree-sum identity explains why RK4 gives no positivity-step advantage over forward Euler once the path has six vertices.
