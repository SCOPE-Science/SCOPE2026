The equality case forces a one-coordinate spike. Spherical homogeneity converts descendant averaging to the shifted level-mean profile, and equality at the root forces common complex phase. Integral-test asymptotics and the spectral radius follow. Supplementary reproducibility: 2,118 finite numerical checks on the accompanying checker, using the exact \(p=2\) infinite tail and several tree profiles.
