On every spherically homogeneous rooted tree, the normalized child-averaging shift on \(\operatorname{Ces}_p\), \(1<p<\infty\), has sharp power norm \(\|M^k\|^p=\zeta(p)/\zeta(p,k+1)\), independent of branching. Every nonzero extremizer is supported on level \(k\) with one common phase, and the powers grow at sharp order \(k^{1-1/p}\) despite spectral radius one.
