For two agents with identical valuations, a fixed arrival sequence of unit goods, one exceptional good, and more unit goods admits an exact, constructive one-variable formula for minimum cumulative maximum envy. When the exceptional value is integral and both unit blocks are at least that long, the optimal pre-arrival imbalance lies nearest half the exceptional good's value, and an explicit even-integer family makes always serving the currently poorer agent asymptotically twice as costly.
