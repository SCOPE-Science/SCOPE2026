For every nonzero target \(c\) modulo an odd prime \(p\ge5\), the largest set avoiding \(c\) in both its pairwise sums and products has size exactly \((p-1)/2\) or \((p-3)/2\), determined by whether \(c\) is square and an associated projective transformation has even order. The number of targets with the smaller maximum is exactly \(\big(L/2-L/2^{\nu_2(L)}-1\big)/2\), where \(L=p-(-1|p)\).
