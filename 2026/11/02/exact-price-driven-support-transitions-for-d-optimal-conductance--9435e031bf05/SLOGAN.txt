For a connected cactus with independently priced edge conductances, maximizing the weighted spanning-tree determinant assigns exactly \(B/(n-1)\) of expenditure to each bridge and \((|C|-1)B/(n-1)\) to every cycle \(C\). A cycle drops exactly its dominant-priced edge, if one exists, precisely when that price is at least the sum of all the other prices on the cycle; otherwise every cycle edge remains positive.
