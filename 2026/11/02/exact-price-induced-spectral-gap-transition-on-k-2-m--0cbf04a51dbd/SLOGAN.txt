On \(K_{2,m}\), \(m\ge3\), with unequal prices at the two hubs, the exact budget-optimal Laplacian spectral gap undergoes a sharp transition at \(\sqrt{q/p}+\sqrt{p/q}=m^2-2\). An explicit positive conductance allocation attains \(B/C_*\); its Fiedler eigenspace changes from dimension \(m\) to dimension one across that threshold.
