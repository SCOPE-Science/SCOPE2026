For every vertex- and edge-transitive core, the cost-optimal continuous-time spectral gap of its one-leaf corona has an exact two-price square-root formula, attained by uniform rates on its two edge classes. For cyclic cores, that allocation is uniquely optimal even against arbitrary asymmetric rates; at fixed budget, rim and pendant rates scale as \(n^{-1}\) and \(n^{-2}\) while the gap scales as \(n^{-3}\).
