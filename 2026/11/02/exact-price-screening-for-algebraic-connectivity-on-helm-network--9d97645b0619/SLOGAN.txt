On a helm network with independently priced spokes, rim edges and pendant edges, optimal algebraic connectivity has a sharp rim-deletion boundary at \(P/Q=1/[2-2\cos(2\pi/m)]\). With all three prices equal, every optimal network drops the rim for \(m\ge7\), retains a positive total rim weight for \(3\le m\le5\), and permits both choices at \(m=6\).
