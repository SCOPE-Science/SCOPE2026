On a one-way three-state continuous-time Markov cycle with three positive jump-rate prices \(p_i\) and total budget \(B\), the sharp asymptotic convergence exponent is \(3B/[2(\sum_i p_i-\sqrt{\sum_i p_i^2-\sum_{i<j}p_ip_j})]\). A unique cheapest link forces the unique optimal generator to have a defective repeated eigenvalue, whereas two tied cheapest links yield an unattained positive-rate supremum and fully equal prices yield a continuum of optimizers.
