The exact cost-optimal spectral gap of a weighted star is \(B/[\sum_i c_i-\Delta^2/(m+1)]\), with \(\Delta=(2\max_i\sqrt{c_i}-\sum_i\sqrt{c_i})_+\). The unique design has identical edge rates precisely when the square roots of the edge prices satisfy the polygon inequality; otherwise the unique design decreases the dominant-price edge and boosts every other edge, and for at least three leaves the spectral-gap eigenspace becomes one-dimensional.
