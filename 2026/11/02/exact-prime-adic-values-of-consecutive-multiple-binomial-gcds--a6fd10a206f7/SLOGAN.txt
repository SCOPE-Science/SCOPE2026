For every integer \(k\ge2\), the complete gcd of \(\binom{qk}{k}\) over \(2\le q\le k+1\) has the exact factorization \(\prod_{p^a\parallel k+1}p^{\max\{0,a-\lfloor\log_p((k+1)/p^a)\rfloor\}}\). This determines all previously unresolved composite-case prime exponents and supplies an explicit multiplier attaining each positive local minimum.
