For the rational kinetic map \(T_h(x)=x-hx/(1+x^2)\), every real prime-period-two orbit is classified explicitly: one symmetric orbit exists for \(h>2\), and exactly two asymmetric orbits arise for \(h>4\). The asymmetric branches have exact multiplier \(9-2h\), yielding a sharp second linear-stability boundary at \(h=5\), beyond the previously established symmetric threshold \(h=4\).
