For the quadratic critical two-cycle \(0\leftrightarrow-1\), the entire attraction-depth histogram modulo \(p^k\) for **every odd prime** is an explicit product of the first-hit polynomial modulo \(p\) and a universal valuation-shell polynomial with logarithmically many terms. The result gives sharp maximum transient lengths and complete finite-level functional-graph depth censuses at \(p=3\) and \(p=7\), while correctly restricting to the cycle basin for other primes.
