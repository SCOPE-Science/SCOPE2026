For odd primes, the common index period of second-kind Dickson polynomial functions over \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) lifts sharply by a factor of \(p\) at every prime-power level, with a different base period according to whether the parameter is a square. The exact formulas are \(p^{k-1}(p^2-1)\) for nonsquares and \(p^k(p^2-1)/2\) for squares.
