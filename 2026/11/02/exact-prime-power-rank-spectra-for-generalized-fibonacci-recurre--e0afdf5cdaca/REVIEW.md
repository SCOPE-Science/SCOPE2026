# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The prime-power statement is derived directly from a polynomial composition identity and a two-case classification. At repeated characteristic roots, a polynomial Taylor congruence at \(\pm2\) and composition yield the exact valuation \(v_p(U_n(s))=v_p(n)\). At distinct roots, the rank is the order of the square of a root in a cyclic split or norm-one group. Squaring, inverse pairing and Hensel uniqueness produce exactly \(\varphi(r)\) trace parameters per permitted rank. The total multiplicities add to \(p^k\). All assumptions, indices, and characteristic exclusions are explicit, and a separately executed exhaustive finite verifier agrees for every one of 7,129 sampled parameters.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Park's arXiv:1407.8086, Section 4, provides the finite-field rank interpretation and the weaker bound \(r(p^k)\le p^k+1\), while highlighting the coefficient-dependent prime-power question as unresolved there. Li's 2000 paper treats the prime-modulus case and supplies the basic algebraic dichotomy. The exact all-depth totient distribution, ramified frequency, and sharp \(p^k\) bound are not statements of those papers, and the implication from their hypotheses alone is not direct without the prime-power cyclic-group and ramified valuation arguments. Targeted comparisons with literature on fixed-recursion periods, initial-condition equivalence, and related published mathematical records did not identify a covering result. An unlocated equivalent special trace-count remains a residual historical risk, not a novelty certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The problem is explicitly motivated by an archival open direction: how rank modulo a prime power depends on the coefficient of a generalized Fibonacci recurrence. The result completely answers it for companion constant \(-1\), all primes at least five, and every prime-power depth. It gives every possible rank and its multiplicity, identifies exactly the exceptional coefficient locus, and improves the known general upper bound for this case. This is a natural complete classification across infinitely many rings, not an isolated finite computation.

## Limitations

No assertion is made for characteristics two or three, for arbitrary companion constants, or for composite moduli with distinct prime factors. A specialty paper giving an equivalent trace-distribution corollary might exist outside the located literature. The script is finite corroboration, not the unrestricted proof.
