For \(p\ge5\), all generalized Fibonacci ranks modulo \(p^k\), as the recurrence coefficient varies, have exact totient multiplicities indexed by nontrivial divisors of \((p\pm1)/2\), with \(2p^{k-1}\) exceptional coefficients of rank \(p^k\). The sharp maximum is \(p^k\), attained exactly when the coefficient is \(\pm2\pmod p\).
