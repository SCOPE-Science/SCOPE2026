# Review: exact prime-power two-cycle census

## Correctness — PASS
Exact identity \(F^2(X)-X=X(X^2-3)(X^2-1)^3\) separates genuinely two-periodic points from fixed points. For odd primes, the unit criterion leaves precisely the \(\pm1\) residue balls modulo \(p^{\lceil k/3\rceil}\). At two, parity and the valuation \(1+3\nu_2(x^2-1)\) yield all odds at levels two through ten and the strict threshold at level eleven. The proof covers all primes and exponents; the bundled verifier independently checks the identity and a finite selection of exact modular orbits.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
The full 2013 Diene–Salim paper on **fixed points** over finite fields, and the full 2014 Qu–Ding paper on **permutation** properties over prime-power rings, were compared to the present claim. A further 2015 valuation paper, 2017 periodic-point paper over prime fields and 2025 Dickson moment paper were checked. These inspected results do not establish the exact two-cycle census or the exponent-eleven transition. This is best-knowledge assessment, not independent certification; an unlocated equivalent historical result remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result completely classifies a natural arithmetic dynamical invariant for infinitely many prime powers. Both the explicit orbit set and its cardinality exhibit a sharp exceptional behavior at two, with scaling controlled by the triple factor at the parabolic two-cycle. The elementary factorization itself is not asserted to be new.

## Limitations
Only least period two of \(E_3(X,1)\) on \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) is determined. Other periods, degrees, parameters, and finite-field extensions remain untreated. Computer checks are finite; the written valuation argument supplies the universal proof.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
