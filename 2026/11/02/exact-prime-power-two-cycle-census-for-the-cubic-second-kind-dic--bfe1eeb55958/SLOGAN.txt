For the cubic second-kind Dickson map \(F(x)=x^3-2x\) modulo \(p^k\), all least-two points are explicit neighborhoods of \(\pm1\): for odd \(p\) the neighborhoods have radius \(p^{-\lceil k/3\rceil}\), while for \(p=2\) every odd class works from exponent two through ten and a sharp restriction begins at exponent eleven. Exact point and cycle counts follow for every prime power.
