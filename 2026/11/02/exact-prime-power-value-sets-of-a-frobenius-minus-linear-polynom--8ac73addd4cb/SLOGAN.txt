For every odd prime \(p\), the polynomial \(x\mapsto x^p-px\) has an exactly classified image at every prime-power level: for \(p\ge5\) it decomposes into a linear cylinder and translated square-map cylinders, while for \(p=3\) its image stabilizes to thirteen residue classes modulo \(81\). The exact limiting densities are \((3p+1)/(2p^2(p+1))\) for \(p\ge5\), and \(13/81\) for \(p=3\).
