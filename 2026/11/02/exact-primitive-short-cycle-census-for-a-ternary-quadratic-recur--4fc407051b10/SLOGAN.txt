The short-period sector of the ternary quadratic delay permutation has a complete primitive-cycle census: for every delay \(k\), a divisor sum of explicit binomial expressions counts every cycle whose period divides \(4k\). When \(k\) is a power of two at least four, all its \(2^k\) nonzero short-period states have the same least period \(4k\), whereas composite odd-factor delays can have genuine shorter cycles.
