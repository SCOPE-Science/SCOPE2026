In the deterministic buck-passing game on \(K_{a,b}\), prior-free equilibria are exactly the single alternating directed cycles with one-step inbound leaves, and their recurrent-cycle spectrum has the closed count \(\binom ak\binom bk k!(k-1)!k^{a+b-2k}\). On balanced \(K_{m,m}\), a uniformly selected equilibrium has recurrent fraction converging to \(1/(1+\omega)\), where \(\omega e^\omega=1\), and its maximum-cost fairness ratio converges to \(1+\omega\approx1.567143\).
