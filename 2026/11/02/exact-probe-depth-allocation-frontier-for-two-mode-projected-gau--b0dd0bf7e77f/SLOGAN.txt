On a two-dimensional positive-definite residual, one-node Gaussian projected Lanczos quadrature has less variance than exact two-node quadrature, but a fixed negative bias. When the two rules share an even projected matrix-vector-product budget \(M\), their mean-squared-error ordering switches **exactly once**, at the explicit spectral frontier \(M_*(a,b)=4(a^2-a\sqrt{ab}+ab-b\sqrt{ab}+b^2)/(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^4\).
