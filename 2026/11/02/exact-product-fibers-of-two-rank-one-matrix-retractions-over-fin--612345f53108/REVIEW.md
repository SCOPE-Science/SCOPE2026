# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof gives a bijection between factorizations of each nonzero rank-one target and pairs of a point in an affine hyperplane and a dual vector satisfying two linear equations. It treats dependent vectors in the trace-one and other-nonzero-trace cases separately; the trace-zero target has no dependent vectors. The zero fiber follows independently from image/kernel containment. The supporting verifier enumerated all fibers in eight finite-field configurations, including a genuine nonprime field. Reproducibility does not replace the general linear-algebra proof.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected material.** Nearby published results involve an idempotent times a nilpotent, or a nilpotent times an involution, neither of which determines exact counts for two rank-one idempotents without additional analysis. The complete text of Jain–Leroy [1] treats existence of products over rings and rank hypotheses but not this fiber enumerator. Fountain–Lewin [2] is related but available material establishes idempotent generation rather than the counts derived here. This finding avoids claiming that every rank-one matrix is a product of idempotents *for the first time*: the novel claim assessed is the full exact fiber distribution. Residual risk: an unlocated exact formula under incidence or semigroup terminology, and inaccessible full text of older factorization sources.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Factorization multiplicities are a natural refinement of the classical existence question for matrix idempotent products. They quantify every fiber of the multiplication map on the minimal nonzero rank stratum, including special singular targets. The exceptional trace-one fiber, zero-product contribution and dimension-two asymptotic jump demonstrate that knowing only the existence of a factorization or the target rank does not determine its multiplicity. The formula provides the precise small-rank base case against which arbitrary-rank product factorizations can be compared; no unproved downstream theorem is asserted.
