Every rank-one matrix over a finite field is a product of two rank-one idempotents, and the number of ordered factorizations depends only on whether the target is zero, nonzero nilpotent, idempotent, or has another nonzero trace. The exact fibers yield a sharp \(3/q\)-versus-\(2/q\) asymptotic transition for the probability that two rank-one retractions multiply to an idempotent, between ambient dimensions two and at least three.
