Every graph on at most six vertices with maximum degree at most two is proportionally \(3\)-choosable; in particular \(\chi_{\mathrm{pc}}(P_6)=\chi_{\mathrm{pc}}(C_6)=3\).
Complete incidence-support enumeration checks \(2{,}406{,}208\) canonical list assignments against each of four maximal graphs and, together with the known proportional \(2\)-choosability characterization, gives the exact choice number of every such graph.
