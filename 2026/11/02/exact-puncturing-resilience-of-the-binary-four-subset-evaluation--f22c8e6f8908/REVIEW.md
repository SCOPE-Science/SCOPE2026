Indexed mathematical-record and literature searches found no equivalent threshold. Absence of a search hit is not proof of priority; unindexed sources and limited visual access to the 2019 published paper remain risks.

The all-length result is exact, has nontrivial small-parameter exceptions, and supplies rank-preserving sharpness witnesses; it does not merely restate unpunctured minimality or the published weight distribution.
