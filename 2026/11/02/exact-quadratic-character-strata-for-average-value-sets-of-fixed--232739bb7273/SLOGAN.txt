For finite fields of characteristic greater than \(5\), the average image size of \(x^5+ax^4+bx^3+cx^2+dx\) over the two free coefficients \(c,d\) has an exact quadratic-character formula depending only on \(B=b-2a^2/5\). The nonzero square and nonsquare \(B\)-strata differ by \((q\chi(5)-\chi(-15))/(3q^2)\), which never vanishes.
