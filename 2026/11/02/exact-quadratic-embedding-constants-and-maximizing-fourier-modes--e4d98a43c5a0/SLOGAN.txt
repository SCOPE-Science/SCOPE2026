For every unweighted Möbius ladder on \(2n\) vertices, the exact shortest-path quadratic embedding constant is \(\csc(3\pi/(2n))\) for odd \(n\) and \(\cot(3\pi/(2n))\) for even \(n\), with the third Fourier frequency providing all maximizing directions. The positive constant excludes isometric squared-Hilbert embeddings, and its exact parity split persists at order \(1/n\) around linear growth.
