For fixed-step descent on a scalar quadratic with i.i.d. Bernoulli fresh/one-step-stale gradients, mean-square stability holds exactly when \(z+qz^2<2\), certified by an explicit positive quadratic energy identity. Independent additive noise then has exact stationary squared error \(\sigma^2(1+qz)/[z(2-z-qz^2)]\), while every nontrivial staleness probability admits steps where the mean converges but the second moment diverges.
