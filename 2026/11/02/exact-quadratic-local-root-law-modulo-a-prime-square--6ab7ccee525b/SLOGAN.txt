Choosing a quadratic coefficient vector uniformly among nonzero triples modulo \(p^2\) gives an exact five-term root-count law for every prime, including \(p=2\). Rare singular lifts force \(\mathbb E R_p^k\sim p^{k-2}\) for every fixed \(k\ge3\), while the second moments tend to \(3\) although the laws converge to equal point masses at \(0\) and \(2\).
