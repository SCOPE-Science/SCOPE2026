For every depth \(q\ge2\), finite rectangular rook graphs have exactly \(\binom q2\) first-order rank-\(q\) theories, classified solely by their two side sizes truncated at \(q\). Every rank-\(q\) graph sentence collapses on this family to a Boolean combination of clique and independent-set existence tests of rank at most \(q\), with a sharp \(q^2\)-vertex representative bound.
