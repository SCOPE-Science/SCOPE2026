For binary symplectic graphs \(S_d=\operatorname{Sp}(2d,2)\), the first-order depth needed to distinguish dimensions \(d<e\) is exactly \(d+2\) for \(d\ge2\) (and depth two for \(d=1\)). Consequently rank \(q\ge3\) has exactly \(q-1\) classes, with a sharp representative bound \(2^{2(q-1)}-1\) vertices.
