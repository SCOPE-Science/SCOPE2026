For the pure edge language of odd-order carousel tournaments, three-variable first-order logic has the exact same quantifier-rank discrimination power as unrestricted first-order logic: at rank \(q\ge2\), every order below \(2^q-1\) is separately visible, and all larger odd orders are indistinguishable. Explicit three-variable interval-threshold formulas attain the sharp cutoff, and an antipodal-circle game supplies its uniform upper bound.
