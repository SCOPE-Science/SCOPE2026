For structured orthogonal zeroth-order descent on an isotropic quadratic with fresh independent function-query noise, a single noisy baseline reused across directions yields an exact all-horizon mean-square recurrence and the same squared-error risk as separate baselines at lower query cost. Its uniquely optimal finite-difference radius is \(h_*=(8\sigma^2\ell^2/(\lambda^2d^2))^{1/4}\), while the stationary error at the fastest homogeneous contraction step is \(\sqrt2\,d\sigma/\lambda\), independent of the number of sampled directions.
