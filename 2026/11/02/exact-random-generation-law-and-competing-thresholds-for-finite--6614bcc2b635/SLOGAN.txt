For a finite three-valued MV algebra \(L_3^k\times L_2^\ell\), the exact probability that \(d\) independent uniform elements generate the whole algebra is \((3^d-2^d)_{\underline{k}}(2^d)_{\underline{\ell}}/(3^{dk}2^{d\ell})\). At the simultaneous critical scales, Boolean-only three-valued profiles and collisions of Boolean profiles give independent Poisson obstructions, so generation succeeds with probability tending to \(e^{-\lambda-\mu}\).
