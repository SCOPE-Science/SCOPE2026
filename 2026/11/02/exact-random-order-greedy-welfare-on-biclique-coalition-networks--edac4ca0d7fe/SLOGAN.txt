Uniform-random greedy coalition formation on a unit-weight complete bipartite graph has the exact expected optimal-welfare fraction \(1-1/(a+1)-1/(b+1)+2/(a+b)\), with a full finite-size distribution determined by the initial monochromatic arrival run. With a fixed part of size \(a\) and a growing other part, the welfare fraction converges to \(\operatorname{Beta}(a,1)\); when both parts grow it converges to one.
