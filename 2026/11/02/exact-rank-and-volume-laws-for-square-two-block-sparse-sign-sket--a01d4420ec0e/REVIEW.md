# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Full rank of an n=p+q square two-block signed incidence matrix requires every graph component to have exactly one cycle: with total edges=vertices, a tree component forces a multicyclic dependent component. Each unicyclic component has full rank iff its cycle parity is inconsistent (probability 1/2), with determinant magnitude 2 before column normalization. Therefore |det S|^2=4^(C-n/2) on success. Rooted-bipartite-tree equations T_L=x exp(T_R), T_R=y exp(T_L) and signed unicyclic cores yield F=(1-T_LT_R)^(-u/4), with logarithmic Jacobian 1-st under x=s exp(-t), y=t exp(-s). Coefficient extraction gives the exact polynomial and E det(S^TS)=n!/(2^n p^p q^q). Independent direct rational enumeration of hash/sign choices and exact integer determinants verified five allocations; determinant second-moment identities checked further sizes. Finite checks support, not replace, the formal all-positive-integer enumeration.

## originality

PASS

Classical graph signed-incidence rank and unicyclic labeled combinatorics are background, not new. Camaño et al. establish general SparseStack OSI guarantees and examples rather than an exact square two-block finite PGF; Jeendgar et al. model SparseStack empirically, with full original paper blocked by VPN, so its exact possible coverage is a named residual risk.
