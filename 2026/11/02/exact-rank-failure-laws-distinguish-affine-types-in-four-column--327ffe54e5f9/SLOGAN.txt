Four-coordinate Hadamard sketches have two exact, sharply different rank-failure laws determined solely by whether the four coordinates form an affine plane over \(\mathbb F_2\); all the subspaces nevertheless have identical row leverage scores. With replacement, the failure tails are asymptotic to \(4(3/4)^m\) versus \(12(1/2)^m\); without replacement, worst-case injectivity begins exactly above \(3N/4\) versus \(N/2\) sampled rows.
