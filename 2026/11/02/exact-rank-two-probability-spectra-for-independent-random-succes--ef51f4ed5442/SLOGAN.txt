For \(k\) independent random finite successor orders, the complete quantifier-rank-two two-variable probability spectrum is a finite Bernstein-coefficient set at \(e^{-1}\), containing exactly \(\prod_{j=0}^{k(k-1)}(\binom{k(k-1)}j+1)\) distinct limits. The structural mechanism is an asymptotically independent family of oriented shared-edge Poisson events; all rank-two sentences depend only on which event types occur.
