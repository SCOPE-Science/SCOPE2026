Two ordered symmetric Rayleigh candidates can reverse their ranking under a *shared* Gaussian sketch: the exact inversion probability depends only on their overlap \(\rho\) and the number of sketch rows \(k\), not on the eigenvalue gap or ambient dimension. For fixed \(0<\rho<1\), the sharp probability is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/\pi}(\rho/2)^k/\sqrt{k(1-\rho^2/2)}\), with exact one- and two-row inverse-trigonometric/elliptic formulas.
