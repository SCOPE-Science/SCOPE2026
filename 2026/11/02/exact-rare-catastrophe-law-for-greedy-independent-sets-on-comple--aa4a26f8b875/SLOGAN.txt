For a uniform-priority greedy independent set on a balanced complete bipartite graph with a regular, four-cycle-free set of missing edges, the entire output law consists of a large full-side solution or a small missing-edge star, with exact graph-independent probabilities. Rare small solutions force variance of order \(dn\), with a sharp order-\(n^{3/2}\) upper envelope attained by finite-projective-plane incidence patterns.
