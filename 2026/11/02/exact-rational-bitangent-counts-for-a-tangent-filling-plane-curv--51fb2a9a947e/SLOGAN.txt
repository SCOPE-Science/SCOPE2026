For the singular tangent-filling plane curve \(X^{q-1}+Y^{q-1}+Z^{q-1}-3(X+Y+Z)^{q-1}=0\) in characteristic greater than three, every tangent at a smooth rational point has at most two smooth rational contacts. The exact number of distinct rational tangent lines is \((q^2+4q-21)/2\) for \(q\equiv2\pmod3\) and \((q^2+6q-31)/2\) for \(q\equiv1\pmod3\), with an explicit contact-pairing involution.
