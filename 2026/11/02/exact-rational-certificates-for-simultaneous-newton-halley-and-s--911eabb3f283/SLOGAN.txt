For the specific quartic \(Q(z)=395z^4-576z^3-33z+33\), exact rational interval certificates establish a root-free attracting two-cycle for Halley's method and a root-free attracting three-cycle for Schröder's method, alongside its known superattracting Newton two-cycle.
