Binary prefix-normal words with exactly seven ones have an explicit rational length-generating function \(z^7H(z)/((1-z)(1-z^3)(1-z^4)^2(1-z^6)^3)\), where \(H(z)\) is a degree-eighteen polynomial specified in the proof. Their count is a degree-six quasipolynomial of least period twelve, resolving the first fixed density beyond the previously computed six-one cases.
