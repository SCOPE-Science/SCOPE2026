# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
The unique-descent encoding is a bijection away from the \(n+1\) subsets mapping to the identity, and the sampling discrepancy vanishes. Both directions of the first-order interpretation are explicit. Aperiodic finite automata give rational limits for fair binary words; a cyclic recurrent class of period greater than one contradicts eventual stabilization under the single-letter transition. The constructed equal-length patterns are cross-bifix-free, which makes first-hit types equiprobable by a terminal replacement bijection. The no-hit probability decreases exponentially. Finite checker tests confirm the small cases, not the infinite theorem.

## Originality: PASS, with a stated residual risk
The most relevant 2026 primary article proves convergence for all small permutation classes, and thus implies **existence** of limits here. It does not, in inspected material, state the exact spectrum for the one-descent class. The exact rational-spectrum method and cross-bifix-free first-hit device have earlier appearances for layered permutations, but their result does not imply the present claim without the different definable binary-word coding by the unique descent. The novelty claimed is the *complete spectrum for this distinct natural permutation class*, not the separate coding ingredients. No stronger prior coverage was found in the named searches. A historically equivalent derivation in specialized literature remains possible.

## Value: PASS
The value set of all limiting first-order probabilities is a natural invariant of a random permutation class. The prior convergence theorem does not determine this invariant. Here the complete classification is exact and constructive rather than a numerical example, and it distinguishes the rational-only spectrum of the one-descent class from settings with non-rational first-order limits. The result is mathematically motivated by a new general convergence theorem and its limitation to existence, rather than an arbitrary small case.

## Limits
Only uniform sampling from permutations with at most one descent and fixed first-order TOTO sentences is covered. The same mathematical model proof does not replace an independent literature review. The approach shares a general pattern-race technique with other word-encoded permutation classes.
