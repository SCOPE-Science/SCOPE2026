The smooth quadratic-character curves of Asgarli–Ghioca–Yip have exactly \(3(q-1)^2/8\) rational points when \(q\equiv1\pmod4\) and \(3(q^2-4q+11)/8\) when \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), for every prime power \(q\) of characteristic at least five. An exact Gauss-sum calculation explains both formulas and the zero-versus-one coordinate-axis intersection dichotomy.
