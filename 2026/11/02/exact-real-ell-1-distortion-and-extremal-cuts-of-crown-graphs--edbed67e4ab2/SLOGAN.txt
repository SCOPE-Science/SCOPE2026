For the unweighted crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), \(n\ge3\), the exact least real \(\ell_1\) distortion is \(2-2/\lceil n/2\rceil\). A balanced matched-pair cut construction attains the bound, and a sharp cut inequality forces every optimal metric to saturate all within-side and existing cross-edge distances.
