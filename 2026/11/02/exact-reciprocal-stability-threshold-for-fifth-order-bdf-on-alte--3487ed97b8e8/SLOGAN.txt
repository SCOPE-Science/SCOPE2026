For genuine variable-coefficient BDF5 on an alternating grid, strict parasitic zero-stability holds **exactly** when the step ratio \(r\) lies in \((R_*^{-1},R_*)\), where \(R_*=6.288402745592172\ldots\) is determined by the cubic \(4u^3-8u^2-95u-127=0\) with \(u=R_*+R_*^{-1}\). At either boundary one simple multiplier equals \(-1\); outside it exactly one real multiplier lies below \(-1\), giving a complete reciprocal threshold rather than just an isolated instability example.
