# Reproducibility

Execute `python verifier.py` with Python 3 and SymPy. NumPy supplies nine optional root-count samples but is not required for any exact claim. The script independently reconstructs the Lagrange differentiation coefficient vectors on each of the two alternating phases and verifies the characteristic quartic, Cayley coefficients, strictly positive Hurwitz-determinant expansions and reciprocal cubic uniqueness. The exact symbolic checks passed on the packaged candidate and, after embedding, must be replayed from an extracted copy of the final ledger itself.

The numerical decimal \(6.288402745592172\ldots\) approximates an **exactly** defined algebraic constant and is not used as a proof of the sign classification. The evidence establishes zero-stability only for the homogeneous test equation on fixed two-periodic grids; nonzero ODE solutions, arbitrary grids and uniform endpoint constants have not been checked. The two historical full-text priority risks are independent of the algebraic proof and remain open.
