For the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), the exchange graph of minimum zero forcing sets has exactly the metric of two distinguishable noncolliding particles on \(J(n,2)\): the sum of individual Johnson distances, except that swapping adjacent positions costs one additional step. This yields a sharp diameter of \(3\) at \(n=3\), diameter \(4\) for all \(n\ge4\), and a complete closed all-pairs distance polynomial.
