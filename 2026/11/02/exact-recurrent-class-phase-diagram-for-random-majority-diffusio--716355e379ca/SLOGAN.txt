For synchronous independently fair-tied random-majority diffusion on \(K_{a,b,c}\), the recurrent-state phase diagram is determined exactly by whether the largest part is unique, tied with one other part, or tied with both. A unique largest part supports a deterministic polarization two-cycle, exactly two largest parts support a \(2^{c+1}\)-state noisy polarized class with invariant disagreement, and three equal parts force eventual unanimity.
