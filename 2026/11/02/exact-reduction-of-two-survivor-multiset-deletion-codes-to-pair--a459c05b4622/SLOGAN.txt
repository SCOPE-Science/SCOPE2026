For every finite alphabet and length \(n\ge2\), the maximum number of unordered multisets uniquely recoverable from two surviving symbols equals the alphabet size plus the maximum \(n\)-uniform pair-packing number: \(S_q(n,n-2)=q+P(q,n,2)\). Every optimal capacity can be attained by combining all constant multisets with a maximum pair packing, giving a closed residue-class formula at length three.
