The characteristic binary word with ones at positions \(2^j-1\) has exactly \(2\lceil n/2\rceil+1\) factor classes modulo reversal for every \(n\ge4\), whereas prefixing one zero increases this count by exactly one. Its ordinary factor complexity follows a dyadic piecewise-linear law, and the ratio of reflection to ordinary complexity oscillates sharply between \(3/5\) and \(2/3\).
