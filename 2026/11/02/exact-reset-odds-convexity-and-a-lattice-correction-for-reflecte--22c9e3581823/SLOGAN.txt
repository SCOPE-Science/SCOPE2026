For geometric resetting to the midpoint of a reflected path with \(2L+1\) vertices, the exact mean cover time is \((3Z-1-2/Z)/r\), where \(Z=\mathsf T_L((1-r)^{-1})\); reset-odds convexity makes zero reset uniquely optimal for the three-vertex path but a unique positive reset rate optimal for every larger odd path. The optimally tuned mean has a precise lattice correction \(L^2f(\alpha_*)+h(\alpha_*)+O(L^{-2})\) beyond the already-known continuum limit.
