For velocity-reset telegraph particles with nonlinear drag, the stationary origin cusp has the exact universal one-sided slope \(r(g'(0)-2\lambda-r)/(2\Sigma^2)\), while each endpoint has exponent \((r+\lambda)/g'(v_*)-1\). These two independent thresholds imply that central and divergent edge peaks can coexist for shear-thinning \(g(v)=\tanh v\) at some positive rates if and only if \(\Sigma<1/\sqrt2\), and an arbitrarily small reset rate can reverse a smooth central minimum into a cusp maximum.
