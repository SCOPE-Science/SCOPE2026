Every tetrahedral residue fiber modulo every positive modulus has an exact prime-wise description: values are perfectly balanced modulo powers of three, whereas multiples of four have five times as many preimages as other residues modulo higher powers of two. For primes \(p\ge5\), quadratic critical-lift corrections give every multiplicity, and precisely \((2p+\chi_p(-3))/3\) values occur modulo \(p\).
