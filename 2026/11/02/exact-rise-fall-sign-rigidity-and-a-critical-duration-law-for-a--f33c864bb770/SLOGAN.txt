For a resonant diurnal–semidiurnal tide with amplitude ratio \(0<a<2\), the *actual total rising-water duration* is longer than the falling-water duration exactly when \(\sin(2\phi)>0\), without any amplitude-induced sign reversal; its sign is always opposite to derivative skewness. At quadrature phase, the duration admits an explicit inverse-sine formula and approaches its critical limit with a sharp square-root deficit.
