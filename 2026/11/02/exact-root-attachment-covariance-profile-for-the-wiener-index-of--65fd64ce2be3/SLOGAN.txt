In a uniform random recursive tree, attaching arrival \(j\) directly to the root has zero covariance with the final Wiener index for \(j=2,3\), but strictly negative covariance for every \(j\ge4\), with exact formula \(\operatorname{Cov}(I_j,W_n)=\frac{n(n+1)}{j(j+1)}\left(3-\frac{j+1}{j-1}H_{j-1}\right)\). Summing gives a strictly negative root-degree/Wiener covariance for every \(n\ge4\), with \(n^{-2}\operatorname{Cov}(D_n,W_n)\to\frac32-\frac{\pi^2}{6}\).
