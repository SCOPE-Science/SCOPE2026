For every pair of generators \(a=dt+1\) and \(b=a+d\) with \(d,t\ge2\), the maximal missing-integer block lengths in \(\langle a,b\rangle\) have an exact uniform frequency: each \(1\le\ell<d\) occurs \(t(t+1)\) times and each \(d,2d,\ldots,td\) once. Every corresponding exponent-gap length in the binary inclusion-exclusion polynomial occurs exactly twice as often.
