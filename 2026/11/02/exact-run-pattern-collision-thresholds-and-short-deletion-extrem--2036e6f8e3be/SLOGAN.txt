For any word, deletion patterns remain distinct exactly until the minimum cost of bridging two equal-letter runs: \(t<1+\min_{i<j,\,c_i=c_j}\sum_{h=i+1}^{j-1}\ell_h\). Consequently, if \(q\ge t+1\), balancing \(r\) run lengths and cycling the run symbols gives the exact maximum number of distinct \(t\)-deletion descendants even when \(r\nmid n\); this alphabet threshold is sharp as a uniform sufficient condition.
