For balanced binary non-overlapping codes with cyclic run length at most two, the exact maximum sizes at lengths \(4,6,8,10,12,14\) are \(1,2,3,5,11,24\). The previously published restricted Dyck construction has only \(20\) words at length \(14\), giving the first strict gap in this finite sequence; complete color-class upper certificates and matching codebooks verify the exact values.
