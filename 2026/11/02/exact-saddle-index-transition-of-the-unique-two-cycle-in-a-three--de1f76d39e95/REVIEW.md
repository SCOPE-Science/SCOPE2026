# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every real primitive two-cycle must consist of two shifted states \((u,v,u)\) and \((v,u,v)\), and the quadratic discriminant classifies all of them. The explicit monodromy polynomial is derived from the actual Jacobian; sign tests at \(\pm1\) and the excluded conjugate unit-pair parameter prove that there are no missed spectral crossings. The analytic proof fixes endpoint inequalities and shows transversality at the unique \(-1\) crossing. The included symbolic program is supplemental, not a replacement for proof.

## Originality — PASS in the inspected evidence
Muni and Banerjee (arXiv:2304.10210, Section IV) and Muni (arXiv:2406.08317, Section 2) study this exact map but their inspected material treats numerical bifurcations and higher-period cycles rather than the global primitive-two saddle-index partition. Zheng et al. (2018), Theorem 4, already establish instability of the two-cycle near its birth under the sign-reversed parameter convention. That instability is not claimed new. The precise entire-parameter census and sharp second index transition are not implied by that local result; the broader quadratic-map literature remains a possible novelty risk. The complete full text of all conceivable earlier sources was not accessible.

## Value — PASS
The exact transition from a one-dimensional to a two-dimensional unstable manifold for the unique short cycle is an intrinsic bifurcation invariant relevant to interpreting numerical hyperchaos studies of this same model. It is a complete low-period classification in a natural two-parameter family, not a selected numerical parameter check. This does not imply chaoticity, existence of an attractor, or any nonlinear period-four branch.
