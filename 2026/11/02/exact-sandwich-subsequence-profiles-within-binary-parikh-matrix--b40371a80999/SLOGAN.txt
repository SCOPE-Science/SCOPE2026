Every binary Parikh-matrix class admits an exact Gaussian-binomial generating polynomial for the number of \(b\)'s between its first and last \(a\)'s, hence exact occurrence probabilities for every scattered sandwich pattern \(ab^t a\). The possible interior counts form a full interval above one when there are at least three \(a\)'s, with a sharp modular hole at zero or one; with exactly two \(a\)'s they instead obey a parity restriction.
