For every length-\(2^k\) binary Rudin–Shapiro prefix with \(k\ge4\), the scattered-subsequence universality index is \(5\cdot2^{k-4}-\mathbf{1}_{2\mid k}\), and the number of shortest absent subsequences is exactly \(2^{2^{k-4}-\mathbf{1}_{2\mid k}}\). A stronger four-block transfer law gives \(|\operatorname{SAS}(W)|=2^{\iota(W)-|W|/4}\) for any concatenation of the four canonical length-\(16\) Rudin–Shapiro blocks.
