A single changed sign in a Walsh–Hadamard matrix of order \(N=2^d\ge8\) reduces its spectral Schur multiplier norm from \(\sqrt N\) to the exact value \(\sqrt N\,(\sqrt{N(N+2)}-2)/(2N-\sqrt{N(N+2)})\). The optimal defect-sensitive weights have two explicit levels, and the norm loss is asymptotic to \(N^{-3/2}\).
