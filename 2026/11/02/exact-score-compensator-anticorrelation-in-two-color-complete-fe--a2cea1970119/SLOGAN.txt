In balanced two-color complete-feedback card guessing, the predictable optimal-score compensator and its martingale residual have a sharp limiting correlation of \(-0.859935683747\ldots\). The exact compensator variance coefficient is \(2\log2-1/2-\pi/4\), while their covariance coefficient is \(1/2-\log2\); this large negative dependence is essential to the total score variance.
