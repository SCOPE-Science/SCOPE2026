For the rational interval map \(K_\mu(x)=\mu x(1-x)/(1+x)\), the unique primitive two-cycle loses stability at the exact second-flip threshold \(\mu_f=1+\sqrt2+\sqrt{4+2\sqrt2}\), replacing numerical estimates near \(5.03\). The bifurcation is supercritical: a locally unique attracting four-cycle emerges with two explicitly computable square-root amplitudes around the old two-cycle.
