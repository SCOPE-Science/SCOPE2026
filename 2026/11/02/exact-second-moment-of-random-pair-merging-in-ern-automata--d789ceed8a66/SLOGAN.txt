For a uniformly sampled distinct unordered pair in the \(n\)-state Černý automaton driven by independent fair input letters, the first-merging time has exact variance \((28n^6-76n^5+47n^4+68n^3-130n^2-61n+76)/45\). Its coefficient of variation converges to \(\sqrt{7/5}\), so random pair synchronization exhibits persistent order-one relative fluctuations even though the mean grows cubically.
