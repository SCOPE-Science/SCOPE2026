In one-shot two-colour antagonistic adsorption on the integer line, the first nonconsecutive pair of sites has strictly positive same-colour occupancy covariance for every nondegenerate colour bias and every positive time, with an exact double-integral law. At equal colour probabilities, this distance-two covariance is \((1-2te^{-t}-e^{-2t})/4>0\), revealing a spatial correlation across an intervening site that is not determined by the density or monochromatic-run counts alone.
