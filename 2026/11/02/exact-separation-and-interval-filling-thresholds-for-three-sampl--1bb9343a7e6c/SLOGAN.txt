For three symmetric scalar quadratic losses, constant-step random reshuffling has six first-generation interval hulls that are pairwise disjoint exactly for \(0<q<q_{\mathrm{sep}}\), where \(q_{\mathrm{sep}}\approx0.516239\), while its stationary support fills its entire convex hull exactly for \(q\ge q_{\mathrm{fill}}\approx0.657298\). Below the separation threshold the support and stationary measure have the exact Hausdorff dimension \(\log 6/(-3\log q)\); an explicit central gap remains open throughout the intervening regime.
