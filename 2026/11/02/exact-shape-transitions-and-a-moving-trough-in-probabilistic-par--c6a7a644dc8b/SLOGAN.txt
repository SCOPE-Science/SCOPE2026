In random-direction parking with \(n\) cars and \(n\) places, the conditional last-car preference law has exactly one trough or is monotone, with every change of shape controlled by explicit finite thresholds that approach \(1/(1+e)\) and \(e/(1+e)\). In the fine window \(p=1/2+t/n\), the trough migrates macroscopically across the street to fraction \(1/2+t/(\sqrt{9/4+4t^2}+3/2)\), a limit not supplied by the previously known exact mass formula.
