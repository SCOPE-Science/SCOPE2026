# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The geometric-series density, independent integer/fractional decomposition, exact stop-loss cubic, ordered endpoint derivative and logarithmic-series bound prove convex order at every real threshold. Exact MGF factorization plus the cubic cumulant lower bound establish the sharp scale at the stipulated variance. The polynomial identities are checked in a standalone script. The checker is supplementary to the proof.

## Originality
PASS (qualified). Chen's complete primary preprint establishes the generic canonical transform, M-class criterion and sharp-scale reduction, but gives examples only for gamma and bounded two-point jumps. The contribution is the natural geometric-jump specialization established by a nontrivial stop-loss comparison and its counterexample to NBUE implication. Relevant compound-geometric reliability literature [2] may contain additional overlap; only its abstract was accessible in this investigation, so historical priority is provisional. The database similarity search found no matching statement, not proof of universal absence.

## Value
PASS. Geometric positive jump sizes produce the Pólya–Aeppli/Poisson-cluster model, used for bursts and risk counts. The theorem gives the best sub-gamma scale with the actual variance as proxy, with no numerical optimization and no intensity dependence, and demonstrates an instructive distinction between convex-order and ageing-based conditions. It is not offered as a complete optimal tail bound under arbitrary proxies.

## Main residual risk
The older literature on higher-order equilibrium laws and spectral positive infinitely divisible distributions could subsume the convex-order instance without explicitly naming this geometric jump model; independent specialist review is appropriate.
