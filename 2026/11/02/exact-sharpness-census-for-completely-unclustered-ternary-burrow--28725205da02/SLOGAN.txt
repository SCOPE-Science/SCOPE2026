For ternary Burrows–Wheeler images with completely unclustered output, every length \(13\le n\le30\) attains the minimal possible cyclic input run number \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), and the exact number of attaining primitive necklaces is enumerated at all eighteen lengths. The length-\(14\) equality case is rigid: exactly four ordered ternary images, all of content \((4,6,4)\).
