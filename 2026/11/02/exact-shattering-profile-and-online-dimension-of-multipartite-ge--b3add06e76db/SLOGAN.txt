For complete multipartite graphs with at least two vertices in every part, every proper geodesically convex set is a partial transversal, and the entire VC-shattered-support polynomial is \(\prod_i(1+n_i z)+z^2\sum_i\binom{n_i}{2}\). The exact adversarial online dimension is one larger than the static dimension: \(\operatorname{VCdim}=r\) but \(\operatorname{Ldim}=r+1\) for \(r\) parts.
