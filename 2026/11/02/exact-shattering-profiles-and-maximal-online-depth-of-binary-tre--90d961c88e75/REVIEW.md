**Originality — PASS under accessible comparisons.** The specific finite-graph family is a binary-tree ancestor incidence graph, not the tree graph itself. Bousquet–Thomassé (2014) and Paszke–Pilipczuk (2020) provide broader structural VC-complexity contexts rather than this coefficientwise classification. The standard binary prefix query method is classical and is not presented as a new learning method. Searches for ancestor-incidence, equivalent laminar neighborhood and chordal-bipartite formulations found no dominating statement. A different terminology for the same laminar incidence family remains a genuine historical-priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A natural and precisely described family of diameter-four chordal bipartite graphs has a completely enumerated closed-neighborhood shattering polynomial and a simultaneously sharp entropy-bound online rank. The resulting unbounded gap between VC and adaptive shattering within this restricted graph family is structural and quantitatively exact. The exact counts also describe every maximum shattered support and distinguish finite heights that the VC-dimension scalar alone cannot distinguish.

**Limitations.** The construction is specialized to full binary trees; it does not establish analogous claims for general bipartite graphs. The finite checker cannot independently prove universal statements or historical novelty. No external mathematical validation has been performed.
