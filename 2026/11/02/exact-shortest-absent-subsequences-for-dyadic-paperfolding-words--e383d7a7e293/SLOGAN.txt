For every nonempty concatenation of the four 32-bit blocks \(110110011100100\,a\,110110001100100\,b\), the entire shortest-absent scattered-subsequence language is a fixed four-choice 12-bit language repeated once per block, with a final free bit exactly when the last block ends in 1.
