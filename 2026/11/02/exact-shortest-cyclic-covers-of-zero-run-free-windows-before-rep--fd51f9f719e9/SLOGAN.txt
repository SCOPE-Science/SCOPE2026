For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\), forbidden-zero-run length \(h\ge2\), and window size \(h\le k\le2h\), the shortest cyclic word containing every \(0^h\)-free length-\(k\) factor while itself avoiding \(0^h\) has exactly \(A_{q,h}(k)+(k-h)(q-1)(q^{k-h-1}-\mathbf1_{\{k=2h\}})\) symbols when \(k>h\), and \(A_{q,h}(h)\) symbols when \(k=h\). Every optimum is obtained by adding shortest bridges between the imbalanced overlap-graph vertices according to a perfect matching, followed by an Euler tour.
