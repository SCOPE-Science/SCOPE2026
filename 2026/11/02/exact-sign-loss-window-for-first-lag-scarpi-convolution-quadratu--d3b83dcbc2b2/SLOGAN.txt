For exponential-transition Scarpi memory, the first backward-Euler convolution weight is negative precisely on one bounded timestep interval if and only if \(2(a+b)+(a-b)\log(cb/a)<0\). On that same parameter region, the continuous integral kernel changes sign and positive-exponential Laplace approximations face a sharp nonzero uniform-error floor, with a square-root onset at the critical transition rate.
