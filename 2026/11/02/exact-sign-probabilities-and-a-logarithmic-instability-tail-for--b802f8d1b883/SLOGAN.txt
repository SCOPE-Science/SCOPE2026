For quadrature exact on quadratic polynomials at three independent uniform nodes, the chance of all-positive weights is exactly \(\frac12\log3-\frac13\), while the sum of absolute weights has the sharp tail \(\Pr(L>t)\sim2\log(t)/t\). The logarithmic collision tail makes every moment of order at least one infinite even though the exact interpolatory quadrature weights exist almost surely.
