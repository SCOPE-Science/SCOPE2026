On finite temporal Heyting frames whose Hasse diagrams are trees, simplicity forces all nonminimal worlds to be loopless, whereas subdirect irreducibility is decided by at least one minimal world's explicit downward-edge obstruction set. Both strata have complete loop-number polynomials: \(S_P(t)=(1+t)^{|\operatorname{Min}(P)|}\) and a finite inclusion-exclusion formula for \(I_P(t)\).
