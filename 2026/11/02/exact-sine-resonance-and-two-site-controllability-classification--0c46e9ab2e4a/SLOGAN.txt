For two coprime oriented cycles, exact spectral resonances occur only at the finitely many rate ratios \(\sin(2\pi r/m)/\sin(2\pi s/n)\); they create only double eigenvalues, switching the minimum number of independently controlled vertices from one to two. At resonance, every adjacent pair works, and a simple simultaneous divisibility test classifies every other two-vertex placement; equal direction rates always need just one actuator.
