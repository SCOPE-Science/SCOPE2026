For the strict public-goods response that produces exactly when one neighbor produces, every subdivided star has an explicit all-equilibria polynomial determined by its arm lengths modulo \(3\). If \(m\) arms have lengths congruent to \(2\pmod3\), the total number of pure equilibria is exactly \(2^m-m+\delta\), where \(\delta=1\) precisely when \(m=0\) and exactly one arm has length congruent to \(1\pmod3\).
