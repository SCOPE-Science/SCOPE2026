In a Brownian particle whose hidden squared-Brownian diffusivity is Poisson-reset while its position never resets, one window's displacement variance and excess kurtosis uniquely recover both the reset rate and mobility scale: the normalized kurtosis is exactly \(10(\alpha t-1+e^{-\alpha t})/(\alpha t)^2\), strictly decreasing from \(5\) to \(0\). All dimensions share that curve, and disjoint squared displacement increments reveal exponential volatility clustering even though ordinary displacement correlations vanish.
