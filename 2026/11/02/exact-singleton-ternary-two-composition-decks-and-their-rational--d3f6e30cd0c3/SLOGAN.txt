Unoriented adjacent-pair compositions of ternary words admit a complete unique-reconstruction-up-to-reversal classification: only three explicit looped-path families yield singleton decks. Their exact count has a rational generating function and grows as \(\tfrac32m^2\) for words of length \(m+1\), while all observable decks grow as \(m^5/120\); hence the singleton fraction is asymptotic to \(180/m^3\).
