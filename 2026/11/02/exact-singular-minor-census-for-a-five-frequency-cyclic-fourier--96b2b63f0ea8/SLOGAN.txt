For the five cyclic Fourier rows \(\{0,1,2,3,5\}\), every singular maximal minor has rank four and arises from precisely one of two root-of-unity geometries: a pentagon or a disjoint antipodal pair and equilateral triangle. At every order \(N\ge6\), the exact number of singular five-column selections is \(\mathbf1_{5\mid N}N/5+\mathbf1_{6\mid N}N(N-6)/6\).
