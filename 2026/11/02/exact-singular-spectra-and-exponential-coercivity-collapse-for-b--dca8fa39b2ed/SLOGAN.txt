For the boundary difference quotient on the model space of a repeated disk Blaschke factor, every singular value follows from one explicit positive tridiagonal matrix, while the highest fixed ranks grow linearly and the least singular value decays exactly like \(a^N\). Despite its single repeated eigenvalue, this natural operator has condition number asymptotic to \(2(1+a)N a^{-N}/[\pi(1-a)]\), and its sharp norm divided by the inner function's boundary derivative tends universally to \(2/\pi\).
