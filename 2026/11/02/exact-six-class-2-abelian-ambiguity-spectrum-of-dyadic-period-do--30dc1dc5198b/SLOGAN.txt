For the period-doubling word, distinct factors of every dyadic length \(N=2^k\ge8\) fall into exactly six 2-Abelian classes, and all six class sizes are given by an explicit parity-dependent formula involving \(\lfloor N/12\rfloor\) and \(\lfloor N/6\rfloor\). This determines the exact ambiguity of recovering a factor from its one- and two-letter frequency counts, sharpening class-count complexity to a full fiber spectrum.
