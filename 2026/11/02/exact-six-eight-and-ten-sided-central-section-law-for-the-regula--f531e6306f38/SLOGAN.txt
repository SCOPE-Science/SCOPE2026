For a Haar-random plane through the center of a regular dodecahedron, the section has exactly 6, 8, or 10 sides, with respective probabilities \(10-15(lpha+eta)/\pi\), \(15(2lpha+eta-\pi)/\pi\), and \(6-15lpha/\pi\), where \(lpha=rccos(1/3)\) and \(eta=rccos(\sqrt5/3)\). An exact great-circle decomposition into 20 spherical hexagons, 60 triangles, and 12 pentagons proves this distribution.
