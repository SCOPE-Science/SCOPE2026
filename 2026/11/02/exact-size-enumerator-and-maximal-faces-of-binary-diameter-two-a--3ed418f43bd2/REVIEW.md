# Review of the diameter-two binary anticode enumerator

## Correctness: PASS

For all \(n\ge3\), translating a selected vertex to zero restricts supports to sizes one and two. Pairwise intersecting two-supports form a star or triangle, giving three and only three maximal families after handling a single two-support separately. The three classes are independently proved maximal; different maximal members intersect in at most three vertices. Degree and rooted-triangle double counting recover the low-degree coefficients; unique extension above three recovers the higher-degree coefficients. A standalone separate clique search on dimensions three through seven and complete subset census on dimensions three and four reproduce the formula. The infinite conclusion is proved symbolically and does not rest on a finite computation.

## Originality: PASS, qualified

Horlemann–Ravi, arXiv:2610.31618v1, Proposition 3.4 proves an exponential-order statement for diameter two, reserving its exact formula for diameter one. Adamaszek–Adams, arXiv:2103.01040, determine the homotopy type of the same scale-two Rips complex but not its face polynomial. Briggs–Feng–Wells, arXiv:2408.01288, investigate rigid facets at general scales. Bukh–Saatashvili, arXiv:2507.10828, bound maximal fixed-diameter sets without the displayed count. A close older formula expressed using clique or simplicial-face terminology cannot be excluded solely by the searches performed. Related local Kneser-complex star/triangle structure is acknowledged as prior background.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The polynomial enumerates all cardinalities of a natural binary anticode problem left quantitatively open by the initiating 2026 study, and determines the complete maximal-facet multiset. It improves an exponential-order statement to an exact total and leading constant, and is simultaneously a complete face-vector for a widely studied hypercube simplicial complex.

## Scientific limitations

Binary alphabet only; diameter at most two; dimension at least three; labelled cube coordinates. It does not enumerate symmetry orbits, higher diameters, or nonbinary alphabets. Historical priority still needs independent assessment. Finite enumeration alone would not establish the theorem; the proof supplies all-dimension classification and counts.
