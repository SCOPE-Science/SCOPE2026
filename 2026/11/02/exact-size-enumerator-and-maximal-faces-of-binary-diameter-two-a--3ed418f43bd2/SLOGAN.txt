In the binary \(n\)-cube for \(n\geq3\), every maximal diameter-at-most-two anticode is a radius-one ball, a coordinate square, or a three-coordinate parity plane. This classification yields the exact cardinality polynomial and the total number \(1+2^{2n+1}+2^{n-2}(\binom n3-\binom n2-6n-4)\), sharpening the previously available exponential-order count.
