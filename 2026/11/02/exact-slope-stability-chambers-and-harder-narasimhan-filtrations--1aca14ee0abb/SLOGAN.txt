For every complete Segre–Veronese syzygy bundle \(M_{\mathcal O(a,b)}\) on \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\), the full slope-stability chamber is exactly \(1/(b+1)<\alpha/\beta<a+1\): the boundary is strictly semistable, and the two outer regions have explicit two-step Harder–Narasimhan filtrations by sums of line bundles. This gives an exact first-stable-power formula for every ample polarization.
