For finite Thue–Morse iterates, all smallest \(2\)- and \(3\)-attractors consist of exactly two positions and admit explicit, complete classifications by complementary centered length-three and length-five contexts. Their counts are exact parity-dependent quadratic formulas in the word length, with the number of smallest \(3\)-attractors asymptotically one quarter of the smallest \(2\)-attractors, contrasting with the known constant 32 unrestricted minima.
