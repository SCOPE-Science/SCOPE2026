For two freely sized average-distance coalitions with exactly three ordered types and any positive multiplicities, a sorted optimum always exists, and its social cost is precisely \(\min\{2ab(y-x)/(a+b-1),\,2bc(z-y)/(b+c-1)\}\). This settles the three-distinct-type case of the proposed sorted-optimum conjecture without extending the conclusion to arbitrary type support or fixed coalition sizes.
