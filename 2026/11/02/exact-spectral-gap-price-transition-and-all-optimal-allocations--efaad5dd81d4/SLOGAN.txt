For continuous-time fastest mixing on \(K_{m,m}\) with a differently priced perfect matching, the exact optimal spectral gap has a sharp matching-price threshold \(P/Q=2(m-1)/(m-2)\): every edge is uniquely equal-weighted below it, every matching edge is uniquely deleted above it, and exactly a one-parameter continuum of optimal allocations exists at equality. These conclusions hold when all individual conductances are allowed to vary independently.
