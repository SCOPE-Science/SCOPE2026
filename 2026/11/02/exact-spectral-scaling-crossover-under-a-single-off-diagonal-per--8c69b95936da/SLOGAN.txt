For a three-mode SPD system with one recycled spectral direction and an off-diagonal perturbation, the globally condition-number-optimal eigenvalue placement has an exact two-branch formula: it moves away from the lowest untouched eigenvalue by \(O(t^2)\), then locks to the other untouched eigenvalue at a sharp coupling threshold. The result quantifies the regime in which fixed-gap eigenpair perturbation expansions exclude the condition-number optimum.
