For quadratic spherical harmonics on any \(S^{d-1}\) with \(d\ge3\), concentration on the intersection of two perpendicular hemispheres has exactly \(d-1\) eigenvalues above \(1/2\), exactly two between \(1/4\) and \(1/2\), and a protected \(1/4\)-eigenspace of dimension \(d(d-3)/2\). The entire spectrum reduces to explicit blocks of size at most four, and its uniquely maximizing complex line is given by the top root of the scalar block.
