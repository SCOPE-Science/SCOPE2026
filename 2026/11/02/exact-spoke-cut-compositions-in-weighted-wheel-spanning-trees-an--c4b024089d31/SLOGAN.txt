For a conductance-weighted wheel spanning tree, conditioning on its hub degree makes the cyclic alternating spoke–cut gaps an exactly uniform weak composition, and gives a complete product-of-component-sizes law at every finite rim size. In the critical \(s/n^2\) regime this finite classification yields a Poisson-even hub degree and conditional \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(2,\ldots,2)\) component-length limit.
