Allowing a different square-root shift in the two rational-power conditions leads to a complete phase diagram: for a nonsquare integer \(m\), simultaneous rationality of \(r^2+\sqrt m\) and \(r^b+\sqrt n\) with some real \(r\) and nonsquare integer \(n\) is possible exactly when \(b=1\), \(b\) is even, or \(4\mid m\). For odd \(b\ge3\), the sharp obstruction is a unique negative \(2\)-adic valuation in a quadratic-field binomial coefficient.
