Under a common continuous two-sample null, the centered Wilcoxon rank sum is uncorrelated with the Wald–Wolfowitz run count, but its square has exact strictly negative covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(W^2,R)=-ab(ab-1)/(3(a+b))\). A position-resolved quadratic covariance profile explains the cancellation leading to this universal finite-sample law.
