For balancing, Lucas-balancing, and Lucas-cobalancing recurrences, the gcds of all consecutive \(k\)-term square sums are exactly \(\gcd(k,B_k)\), with an explicit parity halving for balancing numbers and a factor of three precisely when \(k\equiv3\pmod6\) for Lucas-cobalancing numbers. These formulas prove three cases of a published square-window gcd question; the fourth case remains open here.
