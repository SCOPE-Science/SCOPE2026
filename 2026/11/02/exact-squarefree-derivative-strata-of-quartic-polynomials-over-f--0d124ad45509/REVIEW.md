# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. A complete proof partitions derivative-squarefree quartics by the degree of their gcd with the derivative. The total derivative-squarefree count follows from the exact cubic squarefree count in characteristic greater than three, and direct derivative reductions in characteristics three and two. Rational repeated-root incidences, the twice-counted split double roots, and squares of irreducible quadratics exhaust all non-squarefree possibilities. The covariance is computed from those exact counts and the classical monic-squarefree probability. Nine finite fields were independently checked by a standalone exhaustive Euclidean-gcd verifier (94,213 quartics), including nonprime field orders. The computational check does not purport to prove infinitely many cases.

## Originality

PASS at the available literature-comparison standard, with residual historical risk. The full accessible text of Almousa--Wood (arXiv:1210.0472v1, 2012) counts multiplicities of roots of the quartic itself. A separate squarefreeness constraint on its derivative is not determined by those multiplicity partitions, as explicit squarefree quartics over \(\mathbb F_5\) with opposite derivative-squarefreeness show. Published-record and exact-formula searches did not locate the three-stratum formulas or their characteristic-three independence consequence. Ragot's related 1999 root-multiplicity article was discoverable at abstract/metadata level but not assessed in full; broad discriminant-incidence identities may exist and remain a risk. Index search is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The fixed degree is the first one where a double irreducible quadratic can contribute to the common-gcd degree; exact correction for this nonrational factor is needed to close the natural three-way singularity census. The derived independence characterization is an arithmetic feature for infinitely many finite fields, not a fitted numerical pattern: characteristic three is exactly the point at which the joint discriminant-zero correlation cancels, while characteristic two and all larger characteristics have the same positive covariance. The count gives a baseline for simultaneous polynomial/critical-point separability in this fundamental low-degree family. No higher-degree theorem is asserted.

## Closest literature and limitations

The main closest accessible source is Almousa--Wood, arXiv:1210.0472v1; it does not state the derivative-squarefree joint census, and its partition formulas do not directly determine it. The 1999 Ragot paper is a plausible less-accessible comparator. The claim is restricted to monic quartics and squarefreeness of \(f'\), not distinctness of its critical values. Independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
