For the overlapping binary `11` count \(r(n)\), the **entire nonnegative part** of the law of \(r(n+2^a+1)-r(n)\) stabilizes at the sharp threshold \(a=7\), with masses \((54,65,44,12)/256\) at \((0,1,2,3)\). Consequently, for every \(a\ge7\), the exact density of nondecreasing changes is \(175/256\).
