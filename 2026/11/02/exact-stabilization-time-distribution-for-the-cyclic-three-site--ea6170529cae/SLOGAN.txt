For synchronous three-site binary majority on a periodic ring, every nonalternating configuration becomes fixed after exactly \(\lfloor q/2\rfloor\) updates, where \(q\) is its longest cyclic run of domain walls; the alternating two-cycle is the sole exception. The number of configurations fixed by time \(t\), over every ring length simultaneously, has the exact rational generating function \(-z\,\frac{d}{dz}\log[Q_{2t+1}(1,z)Q_{2t+1}(-1,z)]\), where \(Q_L(u,z)=1-\sum_{j=0}^Lu^jz^{j+1}\).
