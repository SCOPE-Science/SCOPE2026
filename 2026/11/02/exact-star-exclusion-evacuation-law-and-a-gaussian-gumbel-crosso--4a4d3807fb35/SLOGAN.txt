For a finite exclusion process on a star with a partially absorbing hub, the full evacuation time has an exact product Laplace transform and an independent-exponential representation. Outward backscatter of size \(a/\sqrt N\) is the sharp fluctuation crossover: the centered emptying time converges to an independent Gaussian plus a weighted centered Gumbel, with pure Gaussian and pure Gumbel limits on opposite sides.
