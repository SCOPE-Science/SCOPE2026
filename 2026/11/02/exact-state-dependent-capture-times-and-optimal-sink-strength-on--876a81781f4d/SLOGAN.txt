The mean capture time from *any* initial coherent or mixed state on a uniformly coupled quantum path with one endpoint sink has an exact matrix formula \(M=F/\kappa+\kappa G/(4J^2)+C/J\). The optimal absorption rate is \(2J\sqrt{\operatorname{Tr}(\rho F)/\operatorname{Tr}(\rho G)}\) whenever the initial state is not entirely at the sink, and remarkably it is unaffected by coherences between opposite-parity sites.
