Constant-width windows have exact adaptive shattering dimensions \(\min\{\lfloor\log_2(n-k+1)\rfloor,1+\lfloor\log_2k\rfloor\}\) on paths and \(\min\{\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor,1+\lfloor\log_2\min(k,n-k)\rfloor\}\) on cycles. Their VC dimensions never exceed two, and the sharp parameter thresholds give unbounded adaptive-versus-static gaps even for path families with VC dimension one.
