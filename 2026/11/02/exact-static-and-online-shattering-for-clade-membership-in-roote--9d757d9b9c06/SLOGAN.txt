For clade-membership queries on any \(n\)-leaf rooted phylogenetic hierarchy, both the VC and Littlestone dimensions equal exactly \(n-2\), for fully binary as well as multifurcating trees. For unrooted internal-split membership they equal \(n-3\); the sharp linear online dimension contrasts with the \(\Theta(n\log n)\) number-of-hypotheses information scale and with triplet-query learning.
