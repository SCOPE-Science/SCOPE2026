On a clique windmill with fair noisy voter updates, the stationary correlations obey \(V>U>W>0\): two vertices inside one petal are more aligned than the hub and a petal vertex. When the clique size is \(m\) and the petal count grows proportionally, noise at scales \(m^{-2}\) and \(m^{-1}\) produces two distinct, explicitly solvable correlation crossovers.
