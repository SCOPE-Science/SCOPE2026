For the post-loss careless coupon collector, every stationary coupon-subset inclusion probability has an exact finite product, yielding the complete invariant law. Under per-coupon loss \(p=c/n\), the full-collection stationary mass is \(A(c)e^{-nI(c)}(1+O_c(n^{-1}))\), and the expected completion time has exact exponential rate \(I(c)>\log(1+c)\), disproving the independent-marginal rate prediction.
