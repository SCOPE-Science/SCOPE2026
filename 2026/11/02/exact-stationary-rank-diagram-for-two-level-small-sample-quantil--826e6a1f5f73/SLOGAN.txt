For the small-sample quantile Kaczmarz row-selection rule on a two-level corrupted scalar system, an exact hypergeometric two-state law determines every stationary error, optimal residual rank, and temporal correlation. Without replacement, sampling more rows than there are corruptions permits exact absorption at the truth; with replacement, every finite batch has a positive error floor, while an odd-batch median makes the states after entry into the two-state chain independent from the next update onward.
