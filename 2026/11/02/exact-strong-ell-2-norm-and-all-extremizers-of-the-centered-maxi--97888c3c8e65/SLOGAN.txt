The centered Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator on the unweighted four-cycle has exact sharp \(\ell^2\) norm \(\sqrt{7+2\sqrt{10}}/3\). Every nonzero extremizer has, up to rotation, scaling and signs, the alternating moduli \((1,3+\sqrt{10},1,3+\sqrt{10})\); an exhaustive exact-rational certificate over all \(81\) ball choices proves optimality.
