For the \(n\)-rung cycle prism, the strong odd chromatic number is \(2\) for even \(n\ge4\), \(5\) for odd \(n\ge5\), and the previously known value \(6\) at \(n=3\). Exactly \(12\mathbf1_{2\mid n}+24\mathbf1_{4\mid n}+48\mathbf1_{6\mid n}\) named four-palette colorings exist, because all such colorings have column period \(2\), \(4\), or \(6\).
