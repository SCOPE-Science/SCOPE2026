For binary linear words forbidding both \(000\) and \(111\), the complete length-by-subsequence-universality distribution has generating function \((1+2z+2z^2+uz^3)/(1-u(2z^2+z^3))\). A uniform admissible length-\(n\) word has universality index \(n/\sqrt5+O(1)\) in expectation and satisfies a central limit theorem with variance coefficient \(1-11\sqrt5/25\).
