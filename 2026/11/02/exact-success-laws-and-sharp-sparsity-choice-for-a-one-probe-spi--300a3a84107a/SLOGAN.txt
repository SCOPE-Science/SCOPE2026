For a one-spike matrix and one oblivious sparse-Rademacher range probe, the complete spectral/Frobenius error law has binomial masses, and every accuracy target below the dense-probe error has a unique strictly sparse success-optimal probe density. For each fixed near-optimal error band in growing dimension, this density is asymptotically \(\lambda_k/m\), and the maximum success probability is \(\theta_k/m\), yielding an exact independent-restart budget.
