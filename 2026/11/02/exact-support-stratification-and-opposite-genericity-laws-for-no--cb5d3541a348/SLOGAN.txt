Among equivalence relations on infinite equality atoms supported by a named \(k\)-element context, the exact number omitting \(d\) names is \(\binom{k}{d}(B_{k-d}+a_{k-d})\), where \(a_j\) counts set partitions without singleton blocks. Almost every unrestricted supported binary relation uses every name, whereas a supported equivalence relation typically omits \(\sim W(k)\) names and has full support with probability only \(\sim W(k)/k\).
