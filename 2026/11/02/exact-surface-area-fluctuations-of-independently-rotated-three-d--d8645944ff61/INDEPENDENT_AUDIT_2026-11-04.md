# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every full-dimensional compact convex K in R3, support-area identity S(K)=∫(h²-|∇h|²/2) dω follows from Gauss-map determinant, integration by parts and Bochner, with convex approximation. Harmonic deficit δ_l=(l(l+1)/2-1)||h_l||²≥0 is summable for Lipschitz support functions. Haar Schur orthogonality of inequivalent spherical harmonics makes pair terms uncorrelated and yields exact E S_n=4πn²c²-nD and Var S_n=2n(n-1)sum_l δ_l²/(2l+1). Finite-degree multivariate CLT plus uniform L1 bound 2 sum_{l>L}δ_l gives the independent chi-square series limit. Positivity proves sharp 2/5 variance and degree-two equality; support of chi-square series gives non-Gaussian skew and upper bound D. Independently checked P2 rotational correlation numerically, sample average 0.200547 vs Schur 1/5, and inspected geometric quadrature code; no universal arbitrary-K numerical proof claimed.

## originality

PASS

Schneider–Schuster develop rotation-invariant Minkowski classes and spherical harmonics, Ruan–Chirikjian analyze deterministic sums, and Coupier–Davydov analyze successive random reflections. None in their inspected portions establishes variance and non-Gaussian chi-square fluctuations for sums of iid full SO(3) rotations. Related published planar random Minkowski results concern SO(2) two-dimensional Fourier representations, not the 2l+1-dimensional SO(3) harmonic law.

PASS

Second intrinsic volume/surface area of random Minkowski sums is a natural, previously unevaluated fluctuation statistic. This result gives exact finite-n variance, universal non-Gaussian limit, and sharp extremizer/equality characterization for arbitrary nonsmooth convex bodies, with representation-theoretic structure useful beyond a special example.
