For Minkowski sums of independent uniformly rotated copies of any three-dimensional convex body, the surface area has an exact finite-sample spectral variance and a non-Gaussian limit governed by independent chi-square variables of dimensions \(2\ell+1\). A sharp variance bound in terms of the surface-area deficit is attained exactly by degree-two support harmonics, and every nonball has a one-sided limiting fluctuation law.
