For a bilateral weighted shift with constant tails \(\beta>\alpha>0\) and any nonzero complex interface weight, the exact surjectivity margin of \(T-\lambda I\) in the intervening annulus is \(\min\{\beta-|\lambda|,|\lambda|-\alpha\}\), independent of the interface. The corresponding adjoint minimum modulus and Moore--Penrose right-inverse norm are sharp but unattained, while the distance to non-surjectivity is attained by a whole-tail perturbation.
