For Satterthwaite's site-recapture seed-bank model, some partial germination gives strictly more than one expected mutant-occupied site after two years exactly when \(b>(2h^2+6h+4)/(h^2+5h+5)\), where \(h=s(1-r)/r\). This proves a sharp universal dormant-seed survival floor of \(4/5\) and forbids such short-horizon advantage at every physical survival probability if \(h\ge(\sqrt5-1)/2\); it does not assert asymptotic evolutionary invasion.
