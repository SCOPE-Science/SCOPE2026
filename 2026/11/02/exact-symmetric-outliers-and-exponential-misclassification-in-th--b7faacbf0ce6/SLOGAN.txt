For the published three-class flip voting games, the best weighted-majority surrogate that treats identical voters equally makes exactly \(\min\{\binom m3,\binom m5\}\) errors among \(2^{3m}\) coalitions, with the optimal error type switching at \(m=8\). Even without the symmetry restriction, the minimum misclassification fraction decays at the sharp exponential scale \(2^{-3m+O(\log m)}\), and at \(m=5\) exactly one mislabeled coalition is necessary and sufficient.
