For the complete quadratic Rademacher chaos on \(n\) signs, the exact real \(L^\infty\) quotient norm modulo all Walsh degrees at least three is its variance divided by the binomial absolute deviation about a median. An explicit three-valued optimizer attains that norm, and its ratio to \(n\) converges to \(1/[4\Phi^{-1}(3/4)\varphi(\Phi^{-1}(3/4))]\).
