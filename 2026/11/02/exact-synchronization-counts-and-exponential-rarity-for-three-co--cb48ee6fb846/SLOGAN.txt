For three-color cyclic cellular automata on the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), the exact synchronization-basin count has an explicit \(O(m^2n^2)\) integer-arithmetic formula. As \(\max\{m,n\}\to\infty\) with both dimensions at least two, the synchronization probability is asymptotic to \(3(2/3)^{mn}\); the general irrotationality criterion is prior work.
