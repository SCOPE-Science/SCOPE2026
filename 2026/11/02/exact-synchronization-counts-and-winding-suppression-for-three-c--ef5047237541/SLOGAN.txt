For three-color cyclic cellular automata on every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\), \(n\ge3\), the exact number of synchronizing initial states is \(3[z^0]\operatorname{tr}(T(z)^n)\) for an explicit three-state Laurent transfer matrix. The synchronization probability is asymptotic to \(3(\Lambda/9)^n/\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2n}\), where \(\Lambda=(7+\sqrt{33})/2\) and \(\sigma^2=5/4-19\sqrt{33}/132\); closing an open ladder into a prism produces an additional \(n^{-1/2}\) suppression relative to the open ladder.
