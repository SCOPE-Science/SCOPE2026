# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed domain is strong finite-trace Until on exactly \(n\ge1\) observations of two propositions. Expand Until as a monotone DNF with one distinct \(q\)-variable per prime term; enumerate its positive prime implicants and the negative prime implicants from the nested-term transversal family. Case analysis of the first \(q=1\), and of the last preceding \(p=0\), proves all subset-minimal AXp/CXp lists, including the no-\(q\) boundary. Conditioning on the last witness or last blocking \(p\)-zero gives the finite \(\alpha_{n,l}\) and \(\beta_{n,l}\) masses and the independent binomial factors. The index has a limiting geometric law, proving the displayed pair laws; the two first-event stopping times give total-variation convergence. The independently written brute-force checker verifies all minimal sets for \(n\le4\) and exact joint mass equations through \(n=7\). This is a complete proof and finite corroboration, not a reliance on experiments alone.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The 2026 finite-trace explanation paper establishes the definitions, classifier equivalence, duality and algorithms; it does not report a distribution of the two numbers for \(p\,\mathsf U\,q\). The 2026 finite-automata paper similarly provides general enumeration machinery, not this closed-form counting law. The 2015 temporal-implicant work treats diagnostic representations and algorithmic witnesses, including infinite-time settings, rather than the finite random-trace joint distribution. Searches for the exact operator, prime-implicant equivalents, geometric explanation laws and finite counting formulas in published research records and scholarly web search did not locate a dominating statement. A broader older monotone-function/certificate theorem remains a priority risk, and the relevant 2025 paper was not fully readable. This novelty judgment rests on statement/implication comparison, not on matching titles or an absence of search hits alone.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The simplest genuinely temporal connective already exhibits a structural asymmetry between satisfaction and violation: a one-step translation of the complete joint AXp/CXp law, despite identical limiting AXp marginals. Such exact explanation counts benchmark the combinatorial output of explanation-enumeration algorithms for a natural finite-trace specification, with nontrivial covariance, explicit finite-size formulas and a total-variation law. It is a motivated quantitative result, not a new name for hitting-set duality or an arbitrary small finite census.
