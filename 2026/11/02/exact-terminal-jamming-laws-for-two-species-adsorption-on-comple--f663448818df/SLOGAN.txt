Repeated two-species antagonistic adsorption on any complete multipartite graph jams either by filling the whole graph with one species or by filling precisely one part: the probabilities admit exact finite sums for every species bias. In the unbiased equal-part limit this yields the non-concentrating coverage mixture \((1-1/r)\delta_1+(1/r)\delta_{1/r}\), while on \(K_{n,n}\) the full-coverage probability is \(1/2+3/(8n)+O(n^{-2})\).
