In a uniform rooted recursive tree, the number of terminal vertices and the number of unordered terminal-sibling pairs have an **exact**, strictly positive-definite covariance matrix \(n\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1/12&1/8\\1/8&59/180\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\) for every \(n\ge5\). A closed hierarchy of terminal-child factorial moments explains the nontrivial variance and correlation created by overlapping sibling pairs.
