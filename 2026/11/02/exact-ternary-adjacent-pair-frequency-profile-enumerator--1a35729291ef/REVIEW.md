The proof partitions all realizable matrices by off-diagonal active set, separates divergence zero from the six unit divergence vectors, and uses the necessary-and-sufficient Euler-trail criterion. ## Originality — PASS, with risk

The closest prior manuscript (Jacquet–Knessl–Szpankowski, 2012, Section 2.3) explicitly studies **linear Markov transition-frequency types**, so the object is not new. It gives asymptotics and relates open-endpoint counts to balanced cyclic types without calculating the full exact ternary count. Its balanced-matrix rational function is broader as a *relaxed matrix count*, but not stronger for the connected Euler-trail-realizable **linear** profiles considered here. The inspected 2025 Euler-path realization paper establishes reconstructibility methods, not a profile-count formula. Related binary exact counts and ternary ranking questions do not imply the exact ternary formula. Priority risk remains from derivations hidden in unindexed work and possible unpublished simplifications of the 2012 formulas.
