For linear ternary words, the number of distinct ordered adjacent-pair frequency matrices of length \(n\) has an exact rational generating function with denominator \((1-z)^7(1+z)^2(1+z+z^2)\), where \(z\) counts adjacent pairs. The proof uses a complete Euler-trail classification by off-diagonal support and flow imbalance, refining the formerly asymptotic ternary Markov-type count.
