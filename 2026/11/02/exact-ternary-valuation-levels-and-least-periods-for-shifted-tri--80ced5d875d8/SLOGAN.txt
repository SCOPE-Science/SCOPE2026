For the standard Tribonacci sequence, the ternary valuation of \(T_n+1\) is completely determined by three unique \(3\)-adic roots on index classes \(3,8,10\pmod{13}\). Every positive valuation level has exactly six congruence classes of *least* period \(13\cdot3^t\), density \(6/(13\cdot3^t)\), and a uniform counting discrepancy smaller than six.
