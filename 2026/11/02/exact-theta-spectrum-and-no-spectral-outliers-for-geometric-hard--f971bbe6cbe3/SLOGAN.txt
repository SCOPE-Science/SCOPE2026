The normalized Szegő kernels along every geometric boundary chain \(r_n=1-cq^n\), \(0<c<2\), have exactly the spectral interval \([\vartheta_4(0,\sqrt q)^2,\vartheta_3(0,\sqrt q)^2]\), independent of the starting depth. An explicit positive Schur factorization rules out all spectral outliers and makes both theta-constant Riesz bounds globally sharp.
