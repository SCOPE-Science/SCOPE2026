On edge-glued books of \(k\) squares, synchronous three-state excitation has exactly \(3^k-2^{k+1}+1\) primitive three-cycles and \(2^{k+1}-2\) primitive four-cycles, with no other nontrivial periods and sharp uniform entrance bound five. Three-cycle orbits eventually outnumber four-cycle orbits, but under uniform random initial data their attraction probabilities tend to \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), respectively.
