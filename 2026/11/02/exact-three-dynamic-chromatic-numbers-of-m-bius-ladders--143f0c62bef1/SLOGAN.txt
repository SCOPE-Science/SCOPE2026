The three-dynamic chromatic number of the \(2m\)-vertex Möbius ladder equals \(4\) exactly when \(m\equiv2\pmod4\), equals \(8\) at \(m=4\), equals \(6\) at \(m=3,8\), and equals \(5\) otherwise. A twisted-period-four rigidity argument and a four-column insertion give a complete proof for every \(m\ge3\).
