# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

Capelli's criterion with the norm of a quadratic constant term reduces first-three-iterate irreducibility to three explicit quadratic-character conditions. The coordinate change from monic coefficients to \((t,x)\) is bijective in every odd finite field. The pair correlations are \(0,q,q\), the triple correlation is \(1+\chi_q(-1)\), and the character-product indicator has one and only one spurious zero contribution. Direct symbolic reconstruction therefore yields the claimed exact rational polynomial in \(q\). A standalone independent enumeration checks 14 fields, including four nonprime orders and direct polynomial-gcd irreducibility checks in four prime fields. These finite tests are supporting evidence, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with specified residual risks

The closest inspected paper, Gómez-Pérez, Nicolás, Ostafe and Sadornil, arXiv:1206.4979v1, treats the condition that all iterates are irreducible, gives general criteria, and develops estimates; it does not state the exact degree-two, finite-depth-three count, nor does its estimate imply it. The inspected author manuscript of Gómez-Pérez and Nicolás, *An estimate on the number of stable quadratic polynomials*, gives bounds for indefinite stability, including a lower bound for all-depth stability over all nonzero leading coefficients, but no monic three-step exact census. The inspected sections of Göksel, Xia and Boston study fixed-polynomial iteration factorization and associated probabilistic processes, not the enumeration over all monic quadratic coefficients. Published mathematical-record comparisons for fixed-polynomial dynamics, finite-field quadratic counts and three-stage irreducibility found no dominating prior statement. The full text of Jones and Boston, *Settled polynomials over finite fields*, was not available after ordinary open-access searches; the possibility of equivalent coverage in that or another unlocated paper remains. Failed searches are not evidence of an exhaustive novelty guarantee.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Successive irreducibility of quadratic polynomial iterates is a standard finite-field arithmetic-dynamics problem. The third stage is the first with an essential triple multiplicative-character correlation; unlike the first two elementary stages, it exhibits an exact congruence-class dependence. This complete natural cutoff and its sharp failure census give a useful finite-depth benchmark for stability questions and factorization models. It neither pretends to count polynomials stable at every depth nor claims a theorem about later stages.
