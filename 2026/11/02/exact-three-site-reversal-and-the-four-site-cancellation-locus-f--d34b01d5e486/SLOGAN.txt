In a site-disordered exclusion ring, every three-site stationary current is odd under reversal of the global drive, irrespective of rate asymmetry. The first possible rectification occurs on four sites, where the three-particle current has an exact all-drive reversal cancellation precisely when \((a-c)(b-d)(a+c-b-d)=0\), including a non-reflection alternating-sum locus.
