The superfactorials \(S(n)=\prod_{j=1}^{n}j!\) fail the three-square theorem exactly at \(n\equiv5\pmod8\) or at even indices satisfying an explicit local binary-digit predicate. Their obstruction count has density \(3/16\) and sharp square-root fluctuations, with the exact infinite identity \(E(2^{8j+5})=3\cdot2^{8j+1}+2^{4j}\).
