For a fixed-point-free golden-ratio interval map, stationary coordinate correlations vanish at every lag not divisible by three and are exactly \((5-\sqrt5)\beta^{-3k}/40\) at lag \(3k\), where \(\beta=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). The resulting Green--Kubo variance is \(\sqrt5/20\), showing how two zero short lags can conceal a persistent third-lag correlation.
