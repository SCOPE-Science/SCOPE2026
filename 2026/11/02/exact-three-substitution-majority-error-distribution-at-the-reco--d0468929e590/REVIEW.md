# Self-review

## Correctness (C): PASS

The claim reduces exactly to counting high-degree vertices in simple three-uniform hypergraphs, with one-/two-vertex sums derived by disjoint edge-type decompositions. The incidence inequalities establish the sharp error-number boundary, finite coefficient products establish the exceptional counts, and binomial inversion gives every error multiplicity. Both integer expansion directions agree for \(k=7\) through \(11\), and explicit examples attain each bound. The asymptotic probability exponent follows from the one-coordinate event sandwich and elementary binomial estimates. No unproved infinite claim rests on finite experiments.

## Originality (O): PASS, with residual risk

Papadopoulou et al. (arXiv:2610.30001v1) determine the optimal reconstruction threshold, count majority failures for two substitutions, and tabulate one aggregate count for three substitutions at \(k=7\). That aggregate is credited to them. They do not provide the complete exact-three majority-error distribution, the optimal-threshold \(k=8\) enumeration, or the all-longer-length formulas, and explicitly identify higher substitution-count enumeration as a future direction. A prior 2024 conference article describes probability estimates and lower bounds in the accessible abstract; its full text could not be inspected, leaving a definite priority risk. Relevant published research-record searches and prior claim comparisons did not find a dominating statement; negative search alone does not establish uniqueness.

## Value (V): PASS

The complete exact distribution measures a practical decoder's error severity in a channel where optimal unique reconstruction is already guaranteed, and gives an infinite classification rather than a one-off computation. The sharp four-to-three-to-two erroneous-coordinate phase transition and super-exponential reliability exponent give structural and asymptotic understanding beyond the prior isolated total.

## Scientific limitations

The alphabet is binary, the error weight is exactly three, the view set contains distinct outputs, and the observation number is the known unique-reconstruction threshold. These results concern the ordinary majority decoder, not the different weighted decoder. Independent scientific validation and comprehensive priority certification remain outstanding.
