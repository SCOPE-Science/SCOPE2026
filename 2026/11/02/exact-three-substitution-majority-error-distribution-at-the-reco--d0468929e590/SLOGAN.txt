For binary exact-three-substitution reconstruction at the optimal number of distinct views, the full distribution of coordinate errors made by ordinary majority voting admits explicit binomial-sum formulas for every blocklength \(k\ge7\). Majority voting can err in at most four coordinates at \(k=7\), three at \(k=8\), and two for all \(k\ge9\); its uniform-view failure probability has logarithm \(-\tfrac12 k^2\log k+O(k^2)\).
