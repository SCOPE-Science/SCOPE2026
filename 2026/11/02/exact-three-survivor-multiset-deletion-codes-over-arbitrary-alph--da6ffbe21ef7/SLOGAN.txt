When a multiset deletion channel leaves three symbols, the exact maximum code size for every alphabet \(q\ge2\) and every \(n\ge q+2\) is \(q+1\) up to \(n=2q\), and exactly \(q\) thereafter. This sharpens the previously known general upper bound, yields a forced one-light-plus-heavy-coordinate structure for every maximizer, and has a sharp starting threshold because \(q+2\) codewords already exist at \(n=q+1\).
