For three equally spaced liquid-democracy voters with identical independent turnout and common tolerance, the pure-equilibrium delegation graph moves exactly from isolated voting to a two-cycle with an outsider, then a two-cycle with a tail, and finally a three-cycle, at sharp algebraic thresholds. The social-welfare optimum switches to the three-cycle strictly earlier, creating an exact open interval where every equilibrium is inefficient, with a closed-form additive welfare loss.
