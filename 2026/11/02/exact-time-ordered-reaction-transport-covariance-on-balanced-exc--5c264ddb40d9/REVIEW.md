## Mathematical value — PASS
Microscopic transport measurements commonly count signed hopping events while particle reservoirs register adsorption and desorption events. Knowing product equilibrium and mean current does not quantify their joint fluctuations. The exact opposite causal-order covariance signs, equal strictly positive integrated reaction–transport covariances off half filling, and cancellation at half filling despite a nonzero mean current are a graph- and system-size-universal structural statement, not an incremental correction of a known numeric rate. The result isolates precisely which correlation information is lost by replacing the process with its mean flux.
