On a balanced complete tripartite network with \(m\) members per community, the exact cost of timed non-progressive strict-majority seeding is \(m+3\) for \(m\ge2\) (and \(3\) for \(m=1\)). Requiring every seed at the outset instead costs exactly \(\lceil 3(m+1)/2\rceil\), so timed intervention asymptotically saves one third of the static seeding budget.
