A periodic complex row modulation of the discrete Cesàro operator lies exactly \(2\) times the standard deviation of its period away from the Toeplitz operator \(C^*\)-algebra, with the same sharp distance to the essential shift commutant. Its essential norm and essential shift-commutator norm are exactly twice the respective root-mean-square coefficient and root-mean-square cyclic increment, so any nonconstant periodic modulation creates a strictly positive algebra-membership obstruction.
