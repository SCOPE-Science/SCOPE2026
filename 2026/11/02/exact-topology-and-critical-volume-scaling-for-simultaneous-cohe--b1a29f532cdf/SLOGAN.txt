For two prescribed coherent states at \(\pm a e_1\), the unique fixed-volume minimax observation set is a Gaussian-mixture superlevel set: it splits into two components precisely above separation \(a>1/\sqrt2\) and below an explicit critical volume. In \(q\) complex dimensions, that volume has a sharp \((2a^2-1)^{2q-1/2}\) onset and grows like \(2a^{2q}/q!\) at large separation.
