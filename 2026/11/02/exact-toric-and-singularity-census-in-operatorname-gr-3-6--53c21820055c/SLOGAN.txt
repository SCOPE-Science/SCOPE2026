Of the \(883\) rank-three positroid varieties in the labeled complex Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Gr}(3,6)\), precisely \(703\) are toric, with an exact dimension distribution of \((20,90,180,215,150,48)\). Exactly \(391\) of these are smooth and \(312\) singular; notably, all \(48\) maximal-dimensional toric varieties are singular, whereas exactly six toric fourfolds are smooth.
