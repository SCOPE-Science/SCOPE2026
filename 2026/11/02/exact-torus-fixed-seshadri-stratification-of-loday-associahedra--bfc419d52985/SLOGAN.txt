For the unit-weight integral Loday associahedron, every torus fixed point has Seshadri constant \(1\) or \(2\); the exact number with value \(2\) is governed by \(z(1+z)G^2-(1+z+2z^2)G+1+z=0\). In contrast, the dense-torus constant is \(n-1\), giving an explicit all-dimensional local-positivity gap.
