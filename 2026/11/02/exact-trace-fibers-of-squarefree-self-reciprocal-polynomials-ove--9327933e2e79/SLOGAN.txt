For every odd prime power \(q=p^r\), the count of squarefree monic reciprocal polynomials of degree \(2n\) with any prescribed next-to-leading coefficient differs from an explicit constant by \((-1)^n\) times the multiplicity of that coefficient in the progression \(-2n,-2n+4,\ldots,2n\). All coefficients occur equally often precisely when the field is prime and \(p\mid(n+1)\); over a proper extension the counts are never uniform.
