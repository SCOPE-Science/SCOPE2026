# Research assessment

## Correctness
**PASS.** The exact claim is the displayed two-orbit expression for the induced \(W_1\) norm on the finite two-level tree, together with the sharp maximal coefficient \(4-10/N+O((\log N)/N^2)\) along equal branching. A tree has unique signed transport flow; intertwining the vertex and edge heat semigroups reduces the full operator norm to edge row sums. Branchwise leaf contrasts, constant branch coordinates, and the orthogonal branch-standard subspace are exhaustive. Their explicitly evaluated entries give precisely the two expressions. The diagonal asymptotic follows from a uniform inner-row spectral upper bound, a matching time-dependent lower bound, and a strict uniform separation of outer-edge rows for sufficiently large branching. The finite checker supports the proof; it is not used as an infinite certificate.

## Originality
**PASS, with a historical-priority qualification.** Ollivier's general coarse-curvature Propositions 19, 20, and 29 and Münch–Wojciechowski's Theorem 3.8 establish the relevant general Markov/Wasserstein/Lipschitz equivalences. They do not supply an explicit all-time reduction to two \(2\)-by-\(2\) exponentials for this tree or the sharp coefficient in the asymptotic peak. Related exact semigroup calculations on stars and bridge-linked cliques have different distance structure and do not imply the present formula. Other published records examined for equivalent heat-gradient, Kantorovich, and depth-two tree language did not directly cover the claim. The complete text of Cheng–Li–Wu (arXiv:1907.11036) was not examined; undiscovered equivalent work remains possible. A negative search is not proof of priority.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The growing two-level family exhibits an asymptotically optimal diameter-four transient transport amplification on a vanishing time scale, while each finite tree strictly misses the barrier. The exact formula is uniform in two branching parameters and continuous time, and its matrix dimension is independent of tree size. This resolves a motivated sharp invariant for a natural new test class rather than only adding a numerical example. No broader tree-geometry or normalized-generator theorem is asserted.

## Scientific limitations and comparison
The sources most pertinent to implication are Ollivier, *Ricci curvature of Markov chains on metric spaces*, J. Funct. Anal. 256 (2009), Propositions 19–20, 29, and Münch–Wojciechowski, arXiv:1712.00875, Theorem 3.8. Cheng–Li–Wu, arXiv:1907.11036, was checked at abstract level only. The proof is self-contained for the stated finite family and uses standard Kantorovich transport duality and finite-matrix exponentiation. Extension to nonhomogeneous or deeper trees, degree-normalized generators, and separate maximizing-time classifications remains open here.
