On every two-level homogeneous tree, the exact all-time Wasserstein-one amplification of the unit-rate heat semigroup reduces from a growing edge matrix to two explicit \(2\)-by-\(2\) exponentials. When both branching numbers equal \(N\), the sharp maximum is \(4-10/N+O((\log N)/N^2)\): a diameter-four amplification barrier approached on vanishing time scales but never attained by a finite tree.
