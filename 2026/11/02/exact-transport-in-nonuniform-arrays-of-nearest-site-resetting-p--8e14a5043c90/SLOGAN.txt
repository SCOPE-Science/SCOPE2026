For Brownian motion reset at Poisson times to its nearest site in any nonuniform periodic array, the complete effective diffusion coefficient is exactly \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}=rL^2/[4(\sum_i\sinh a_i)(\sum_i\tanh a_i)]\), with \(a_i=\ell_i\sqrt{r/D}/2\). At sufficiently weak fixed resetting rate, every nonuniform array diffuses faster than the equal-spacing array of the same density, even though equal spacing retains the previously known advantage after optimizing the rate; at high rate, the largest gap controls an exponential transport bottleneck.
