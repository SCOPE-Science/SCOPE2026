For the length-\(n\) \(q\)-ary deletion-reconstruction graph whose edges indicate at least two distinct shared one-deletion reads, all three-way confusability triangles are uniquely described by two opposite alternating words and one interior phase splice. Their exact number is \(q(q-1)\sum_{\ell=4}^{n}(n-\ell+1)(\ell-3)q^{n-\ell}\), with rational generating function \(q(q-1)z^4/((1-qz)^2(1-z)^2)\).
