For fixed-width binary signed-digit representation counts, the largest mod-five excess of residue one over residue four increases by exactly four, and the smallest decreases by exactly four, every twelve additional bit positions from width eight onward. Equivalently the same sharp twelve-step law governs prefix extrema for Stern's diatomic sequence, proving a previously numerically bounded-looking residue difference actually grows in both directions at rate one-third per binary length.
