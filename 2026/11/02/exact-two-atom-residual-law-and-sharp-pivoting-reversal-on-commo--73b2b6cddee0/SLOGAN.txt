For a one-defect common-factor covariance, residual-diagonal random Cholesky has an exact two-value residual-trace law and beats largest-diagonal greedy pivoting precisely when \((a(b^{-1}+k-1)+1)(b^{-1}+k)<n-k\). Even as all pairwise correlations and the ratio of diagonal entries approach one, the one-pivot greedy-to-randomized expected trace-error ratio can diverge like \(t/2\).
