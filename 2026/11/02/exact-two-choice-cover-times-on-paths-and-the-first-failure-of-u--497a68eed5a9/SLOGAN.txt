For the two-independent-offer random walk on every path \(P_{N+1}\), the exact minimum mean cover time is \(2N-\frac32(1-3^{-N})+E_N(x)\), with a closed two-parity formula for the optimal time \(E_N(x)\) to first hit either endpoint. The optimal strategy aims first at the nearer endpoint and then at the opposite one; prioritizing an unvisited offered neighbor fails for the first time on six-vertex paths, with an exact \(4/3\)-step local continuation penalty.
