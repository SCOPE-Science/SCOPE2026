For an explicitly defined parallel, flash-entry, half-quorum oscillator on a complete graph, the equal-size two-cluster sector has exactly one synchronized cycle, \(\max(0,C-2w+1)\) coasting locked cycles, and \(\max(0,2w-C-2)\) mutually accelerated locked cycles, with exact basin sizes for every clock length and flash width. The probability of eventual exact phase synchrony is nonmonotone in flashing duration; at clock length \(10\) it is \(1/2,1,17/50\) at widths \(3,6,9\), respectively.
