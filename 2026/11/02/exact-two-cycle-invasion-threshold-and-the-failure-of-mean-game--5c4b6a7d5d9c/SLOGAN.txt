In a discrete evolutionary harvesting game, the exact cooperator-invasion multiplier of the stable all-defector resource two-cycle is a simple quadratic in the depleted- and rich-resource payoffs, identical for two different replicator updates. Averaging the fluctuating environment predicts a strictly earlier invasion threshold; an exact admissible example has mean-game growth \(81/80>1\) but true two-step growth \(1593/1600<1\).
