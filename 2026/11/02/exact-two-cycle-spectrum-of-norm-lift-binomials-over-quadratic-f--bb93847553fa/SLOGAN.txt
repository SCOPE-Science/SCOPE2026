For every non-base-field coefficient \(a\in\mathbb F_{q^2}\setminus\mathbb F_q\), the norm-lift binomial \(x\mapsto x^{q+2}+ax\) has either no two-cycles or exactly \(q+1\), with an exact trace-zero and quadratic-character criterion. Among its permutation instances, two-cycles are forbidden for \(q\equiv2\pmod3\), while in characteristic three exactly \((q-\chi_q(-1))/2\) coefficient choices have \(q+1\) two-cycles.
