The substantive new numerical claim is the full uniform distribution of all five types among ordered pairs and distinct subalgebras. Bowman–Towers–Varea (arXiv:0704.2723v1) address solvability and triangulability criteria, not this census; Kundu–Naik–Singh (arXiv:2208.07676v1) classify a two-centralizer-dimension stratum, while free class-three rank at least three has three centralizer dimensions. No dominating enumerative result was identified in the material checked. A significant risk is that Boral's relevant thesis was not readable beyond abstract and chapter-outline evidence; the literature comparison is not an independent priority certification.

The two noncentral centralizer types make the counting structure useful for further incidence and subalgebra-growth questions.
