For the free class-three nilpotent Lie algebra on \(n\ge2\) generators over \(\mathbb F_q\), every two-generated subalgebra is zero, abelian of dimension one or two, Heisenberg of dimension three, or free class-three of dimension five. Uniform exact formulas count both ordered generating pairs and distinct subalgebras of every type for all prime powers \(q\), including characteristics two and three.
