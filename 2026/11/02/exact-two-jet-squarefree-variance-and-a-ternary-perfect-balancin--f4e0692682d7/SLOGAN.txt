For monic squarefree degree-\(n\) polynomials over an odd finite field, the exact unnormalized variance across prescribed nonzero constant and linear coefficients is \(\bigl((q-3)q^{\lceil n/2\rceil}+q+1\bigr)/q\). These fibres differ pairwise by at most one for every degree precisely over \(\mathbb F_3\); outside that field perfect balancing survives only in degree two.
