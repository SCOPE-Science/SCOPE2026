# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Represent the kernel by (√a,cos x,sin x); diagonalize the two-site Gram matrix by symmetric/antisymmetric vectors. Integrated second moments are 2a²+u and 1−u, giving V=a+1−(2a²+u)/(t+2a+2u)−(1−u)/(t+2−2u), u=cos²(Δ/2). The derivative is −C/(t+2a+2u)²+t/(t+2−2u)², C=t+2a−4a²; its square-root sign witness is strictly increasing, positive at u=1, and has at most one zero. Endpoint sign at 0 yields the exact a_c(t) positive quadratic root. At a=1/2 the optimizer u=1/4; Taylor expansion at t=0 for a<1/2 gives 1−u ∼(a+1)√t/√(2a(1−2a)) and hence the quarter power. The rank-one Moore–Penrose result at exact collision exceeds the distinct-site limit by 1/2. Independent direct unsimplified 2×2 inverse and trigonometric moment checks were run at 30 positive parameter values; they corroborate, not replace, continuous global derivative proof.

## originality

PASS

General IVAR/GP design and Nyström integrated residual are established. Gorodetsky–Marzouk 2015 Section 4.2.2 uses a different infinite-rank periodic squared-exponential kernel and numerical multi-site designs; it does not state the inspected rank-three positive-ridge two-site phase boundary. Hayakawa–Oberhauser–Lyons 2023 derives general Nyström bounds but no exact two-site optimum. Classical trigonometric regression design may contain a related equivalence; Xu–Shang 2014 full text was inaccessible and retains genuine priority risk.

## value

PASS

For a rank-three periodic kernel, exactly two independently noisy landmarks form the first underdetermined nontrivial spatial design. A parameter-uniform 120° optimum and singular quarter-power collapse sharply distinguish low-noise duplicate observations from noiseless rank loss; a natural exact design and boundary result.
