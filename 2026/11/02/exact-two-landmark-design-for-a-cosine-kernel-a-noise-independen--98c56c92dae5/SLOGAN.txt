For two noisy Nyström landmarks in the rank-three circle kernel \(k_a(x,y)=a+\cos(x-y)\), the integrated posterior variance has an exact global phase boundary between antipodal and interior placements; at \(a=1/2\) the unique optimum is exactly \(120^\circ\) for every positive noise variance. Below that parameter, the optimum develops a sharp quarter-power collision law as the noise variance vanishes, while the exactly colliding noiseless design suffers a rank-loss jump of \(1/2\).
