Every simple three-arm theta graph with lengths \(2\le a\le b\le c\) is covered by three geodesics, and precisely four short-arm patterns admit two: \((a,b)=(2,2)\) or \((2,3)\) without a parity condition, \((2,4)\) with even \(c\), or \((3,3)\) with odd \(c\). The dichotomy follows from a sharp diameter bound and opposite-hub shortest-path inequalities.
