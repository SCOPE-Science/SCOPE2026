The circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) has the complete two-packing polynomial \(P_n(u)=[x^n](x+6ux^3+4u^2x^4+5u^2x^5)/(1-x-2ux^3-u^2x^4-u^2x^5)\) for every \(n\ge3\). Its maximum packings and minimum unique-response Roman functions have an exact three-regime transition by \(n\bmod4\), with optimum counts \(2n\), \(4\), or \(n(n-2)\).
