For square unit-row Kaczmarz systems whose inverse row Gram has positive complete-bipartite couplings and one negative pair within a part, the globally optimal fixed sampling probabilities are unique and explicit. A sharp transition at \(v/w=q/2\) switches the optimal correlation certificate from rank one to rank two, and the exact optimum is given by two rational formulas for every part size and every admissible inverse Gram diagonal.
