For Pascal rows indexed by \(2^r\), the exact number of entries not representable as three squares is \((2^{2\lfloor r/2\rfloor-1}+10)/3\) for \(r\ge5\), with explicit exceptions for the first four rows. The nonrepresentable proportion approaches \(1/6\) along even exponents and \(1/12\) along odd exponents.\n
