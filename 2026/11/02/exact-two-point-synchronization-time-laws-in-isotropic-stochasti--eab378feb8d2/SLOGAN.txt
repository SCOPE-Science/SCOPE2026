For isotropic first-harmonic stochastic Möbius circle flows, the common-noise two-phase approach time has an exact mean, variance, and endpoint probability in accumulated-noise units, regardless of shared angular drift or common additive noise. The first approach to angular tolerance \(\varepsilon\) has an almost-sure logarithmic scale and asymptotically Gaussian fluctuations of variance \(2\log(1/\varepsilon)\).
