On every real planar strictly convex quadratic, two exact random line minimizations using a Haar-orthogonal direction pair have exactly \(\bigl((\kappa-1)/(\kappa+1)\bigr)^2\) times the expected objective error of two independently drawn uniform directions, regardless of the initial error orientation. Orthogonal pairing solves an isotropic quadratic in two steps, and the exact relative gain for any condition number is \(4\kappa/(\kappa+1)^2\).
