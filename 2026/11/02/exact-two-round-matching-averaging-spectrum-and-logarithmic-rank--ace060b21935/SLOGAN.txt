Two independent synchronous pair-averaging rounds on \(2m\) agents lose an additional \(Z_m\) dimensions beyond the first round, with \((Z_m-\tfrac14\log m)/\sqrt{\tfrac14\log m}\Rightarrow N(0,1)\) and \(\Pr(Z_m=0)\sim2^{1/4}\sqrt\pi\,m^{-1/4}/\Gamma(1/4)\). The entire finite singular spectrum is read from alternating matching cycles, and its empirical law converges to a half-atom at zero plus a half-arcsine law.
