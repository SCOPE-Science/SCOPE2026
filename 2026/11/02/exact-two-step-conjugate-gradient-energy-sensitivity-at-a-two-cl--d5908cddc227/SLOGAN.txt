For conjugate gradients on a symmetric positive-definite matrix with exactly two eigenvalue clusters, the exact leading squared-energy error after two iterations depends only on the perturbation's anisotropy *inside* the clusters; cross-cluster couplings cancel at first order. The coefficient vanishes exactly when each active right-hand-side component is an eigenvector of its within-cluster perturbation, improving the squared error from \(\Theta(t^2)\) to \(O(t^4)\).
