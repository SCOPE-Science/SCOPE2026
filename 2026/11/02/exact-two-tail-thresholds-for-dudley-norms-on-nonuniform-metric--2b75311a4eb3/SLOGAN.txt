For every finite weighted metric star with arbitrary unequal arm lengths and a real zero-mass measure, the sum-normalized Dudley norm equals twice the unique crossing of two signed leaf-mass tail distributions with a linear threshold. Sorting at most one interval per nonzero leaf computes this norm exactly in \(O(n\log n)\) arithmetic operations and constructs an optimal bounded-Lipschitz witness, with a sharp one-sided versus two-sided cap criterion.
