The singleton count in a uniformly uncovered labeled Cayley tree has an exact, all-size **two-time** covariance from a two-marked-vertex spanning-tree determinant, extending beyond known one-time component enumeration. For \(0<s\le t<1\), its covariance divided by \(n\) converges to the strictly positive, two-product kernel \(s(1-t)e^{-t}[1-(1-s)e^{-s}+s(2-s)(1-t)e^{-s}]\).
