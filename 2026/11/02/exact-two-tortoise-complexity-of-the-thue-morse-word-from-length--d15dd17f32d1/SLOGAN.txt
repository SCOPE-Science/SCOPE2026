For every length \(n\ge18\), the loss of distinct Thue–Morse factors under two passes of the binary tortoise map is exactly the sum of its one-pass losses at lengths \(n\) and \(n-2\): \(D_2(n)=D_1(n)+D_1(n-2)\). This gives an explicit binary-digit formula with deficits \(4,6,8\) and a sharp threshold, since the identity fails at length \(17\).
