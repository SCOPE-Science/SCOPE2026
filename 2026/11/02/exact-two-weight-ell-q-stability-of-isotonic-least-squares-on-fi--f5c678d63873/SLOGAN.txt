For weighted isotonic least squares on any finite partial order, the exact \(\ell_q\) perturbation factor under independently weighted monitoring is the largest weighted-mean norm over its connected order-convex subsets; every candidate factor is attainable. For finite \(q\), nonexpansivity holds exactly when monitoring masses are proportional to fitting masses on each connected component, whereas \(\ell_\infty\) is always nonexpansive.
