For weighted isotonic least squares on a nonempty finite partial order, the exact \(\ell_q\) perturbation factor under independently weighted monitoring is the largest weighted-mean norm over connected order-convex subsets. Each block norm is attainable. For finite \(q\), nonexpansivity holds exactly when the monitoring masses are proportional to the fitting masses on each Hasse component; for \(q=\infty\) it always holds.
