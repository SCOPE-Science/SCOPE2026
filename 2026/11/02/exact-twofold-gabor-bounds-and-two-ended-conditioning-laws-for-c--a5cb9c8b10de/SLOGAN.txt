For the causal Erlang window \(g_a(t)=t e^{-at}\mathbf1_{t\ge0}\), the redundancy-two Gabor system has explicit sharp Zak-fiber frame bounds for every \(a>0\). Its optimal condition number diverges sharply as \(4a^{-4}\) when \(a\downarrow0\), but as \(e^{2a-2}/a^2\) when \(a\to\infty\), despite remaining a frame at every fixed shape parameter.
