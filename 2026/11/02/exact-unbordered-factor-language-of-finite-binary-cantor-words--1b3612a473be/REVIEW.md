# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The submitted theorem gives exact quantifiers for all finite ranks and distinct nonempty contiguous factors. Its key nonstandard lemma is the record-terminal-gap criterion. When a preceding gap at least as long as the trailing run exists, the first such gap and a 2-adic congruence construct an explicit nonempty proper border; otherwise no proper prefix can terminate with as many zeros as the final suffix. The gap valuation formula is proved inductively, and every constructed factor is realized inside the next Cantor iterate. Different parameter triples are separated by their trailing run length and one-count. Exact enumerations from an independently implemented KMP border test matched all predicted sets through rank six. This is an infinite proof supported, not replaced, by finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk
Full text of Goč–Mousavi–Shallit (arXiv:1211.1301v1) addresses unbordered factors of Thue–Morse, Rudin–Shapiro, and period-doubling sequences rather than finite Cantor iterates. Goč–Schaeffer–Shallit (arXiv:1206.5352v1) supplies general automatic-word tools and a different sparse power-of-two word. The Cantor-like permutation-complexity article (DOI:10.1016/j.tcs.2015.12.022) concerns the same substitution but not ordinary borders. Accessible earlier local-period research claims give the longest unbordered factors, not this complete set and count. Multiple actual research-record and public literature searches produced no stronger coverage. This supports provisional originality but is not an exhaustive priority guarantee.

## Value — PASS
The result completely classifies a natural word-combinatorial object on a classical non-uniformly recurrent automatic-word family, rather than only extending a short numerical table. It explains every allowed unbordered factor by a structural record-gap law and derives the surprising growth order \(\Theta(|C_k|^{\log_3 6})\), with a distinct factor count much larger than the input length but below quadratic. The classification can support exact finite-language tests and further studies of border spectra.

## Limits and residual risks
The theorem is for finite complete iterates over the binary Cantor substitution, exact contiguous borders and distinct factors. It does not determine a per-length distribution for all lengths or a generalization to arbitrary prefixes or Cantor-like substitutions. Historical-priority uncertainty remains for unindexed or inaccessible sources. The finite checker is not an infinite proof, and no independent mathematical validation was carried out.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
