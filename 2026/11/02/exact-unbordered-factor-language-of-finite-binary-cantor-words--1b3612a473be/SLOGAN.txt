For each finite binary Cantor word, every unbordered factor is a reversed pair built by adjoining a strictly record-long terminal zero run to a suffix of a smaller Cantor iterate, together with the two singleton letters. The exact number at rank \(k\ge1\) is \(\bigl(8\cdot6^{k-1}+12\bigr)/5\), and the maximum length is \(2\cdot3^{k-1}\).
