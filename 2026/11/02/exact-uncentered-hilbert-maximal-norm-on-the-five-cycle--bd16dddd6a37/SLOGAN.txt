On the unit five-cycle, the uncentered graph-ball maximal operator has exact sharp \(\ell^2\) norm \(\sqrt{\lambda_*}=1.292531070898058\ldots\), where \(81\lambda_*^3-171\lambda_*^2+62\lambda_*-4=0\). Precisely five nonnegative extremizing rays occur, each sharply peaked at one vertex with equal values on its two neighbors and equal smaller values on the remaining two vertices.
