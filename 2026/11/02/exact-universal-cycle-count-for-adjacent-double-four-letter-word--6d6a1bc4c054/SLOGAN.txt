For four-letter words with three distinct symbols and a single adjacent repeated pair, universal cycles exist exactly when the alphabet has at least four symbols. Their exact rotation-class count is \(q^{q-2}(q-2)^{3q-2}(q-3)^{q^2-3q+1}(q-1)^{q^2-3q}((q-3)!)^{2q(q-1)}\), obtained by contracting the overlap graph to a parity lift of the complete graph's non-backtracking digraph.
