For degree \(p^a\), the gcd across every finite-field size of the numbers of monic irreducible polynomials has an explicit prime-by-prime formula. For degrees \(2^a\), its odd prime divisors are exactly the Fermat primes \(2^{2^j}+1\) with \(2^j\le a-1\), giving a natural finite-field counting invariant that detects their primality.
