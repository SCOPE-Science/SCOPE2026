# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Every surviving length-n block appears exactly once in a linear word iff the residual B(2,n−1) directed graph admits an Euler trail; only active vertices count. I reconstructed the classification of three deleted arcs into balanced triangles or loop+two-cycle (four), both loops plus a nonloop (N−2), two-cycle plus remaining nonloop (N−4), one loop and an open two-arc trail (4N−16), and loop-free three-arc open trail (4N−28), giving 10N−46 degree-compatible triples for N=2^n. The published super-edge-connectivity result reduces a ≤3-arc disconnection to isolation of a constant vertex; direct examination of the two external arcs and five possible third deletions at each constant vertex gives ten genuine failures when n≥4. Thus 10N−56 successful triples. The theorem depends on the cited connectivity premise, which is not claimed as newly proved.
