For an alphabet involution with both fixed letters and swapped pairs, the entire length-by-subsequence-universality distribution of self-reverse-complement words has an explicit rational bivariate generating function. If the involution has \(f\ge1\) fixed letters and \(d\ge1\) swapped pairs, the minimum length for odd universality order \(k\) is \(k(f+2d)+f-1\), with exactly \(((f+2d)!)^{(k-1)/2}f2^d(f+d-1)!\) shortest indexed words.
