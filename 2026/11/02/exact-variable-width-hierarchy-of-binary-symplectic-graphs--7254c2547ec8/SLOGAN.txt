For every \(k\ge3\), binary symplectic graphs have exactly \(k-1\) unrestricted-depth, noncounting \(\mathrm{FO}^k\) theories: their symplectic dimensions are distinguishable precisely until dimension \(k-1\), after which all dimensions collapse to one theory. The least number of variables separating dimensions \(d<e\) is \(2\) for \(d=1\) and \(d+2\) for \(d\ge2\), with a sharp largest smallest representative of \(2^{2(k-1)}-1\) vertices.
