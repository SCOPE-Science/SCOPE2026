In the complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\), vertex-edge dominant edge metric dimension equals \(n\) for \(2\le n\le6\) and drops to \(n-1\) from \(n=7\) onward. All minimum vertex sets are classified and counted exactly: at large orders they are matching-column transversals with a single missing column and at least three selected vertices per clique layer.
