The exact uniform distance of a complete multipartite Walsh chaos from all higher-degree Walsh terms is a product of reciprocals of central-binomial probabilities: \(\prod_j 4^{\lfloor n_j/2\rfloor}/\binom{2\lfloor n_j/2\rfloor}{\lfloor n_j/2\rfloor}\). Products of sign-of-Rademacher-sum functions attain it; odd block sizes give a unique optimizer, and a weighted stability bound quantifies near-optimality.
