For a finite unit-distance star with one jump rate \(\varepsilon\le1\) and all other rates one, the entire Wasserstein-one heat-semigroup amplification is an explicit difference of two exponentials, always attained across the slow edge. Its exact peak tends to \(2-1/(r+1)\) with a sharp \(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)\) correction and an explicit logarithmic maximizing time.
