The graph-distance Wasserstein-one norm of continuous-time unit-rate heat flow on a windmill with \(r\ge2\) equal \(m\)-vertex outer cliques is exactly \(\frac{2(r-1)}r e^{-t}-\frac{r-2}r e^{-(rm+1)t}\). Except for two cliques or a three-leaf star, the norm temporarily exceeds one and has a unique explicit peak, even though the graph is finite, reversible and spectrally gapped.
