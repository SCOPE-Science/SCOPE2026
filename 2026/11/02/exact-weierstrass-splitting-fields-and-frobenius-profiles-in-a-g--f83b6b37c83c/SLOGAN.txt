The two genus-two curves in Abdellatif's degree-four isogeny family have identical unpolarized Jacobians and the same exact six-Weierstrass-point splitting field \(K(i,\sqrt{t^2+1},\sqrt t)\), yet their Galois actions on those points can differ. Over every odd finite field with \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), each admissible pair has exactly \(0\) and \(2\) rational Weierstrass points, respectively, despite identical zeta functions.
