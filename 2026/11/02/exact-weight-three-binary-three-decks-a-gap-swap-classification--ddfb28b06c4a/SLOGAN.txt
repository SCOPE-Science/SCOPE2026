Every binary weight-three word has at most one distinct word with the same full three-deck, obtained by an explicit interchange of its two successive one-position gaps. Counting those pairs proves \(\sum_{n\ge0}D_{3,3}(n)z^n=\frac{z^3}{(1-z)^4}-\frac{z^7}{(1-z)^2(1-z^2)(1-z^4)}\), the generating function proposed in Chrisnata and collaborators' Conjecture 1.
