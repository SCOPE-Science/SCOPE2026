For binary words of length \(n\ge4\) with exactly two ones, prohibiting only the two longest proper prefix–suffix overlaps has exact maximum code size \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\lfloor n/3\rfloor+\mathbf1_{\{2\mid n\}}\lfloor n/6\rfloor\). A three-spaced rotation-orbit construction attains the bound, including the exceptional antipodal orbit at even lengths.
