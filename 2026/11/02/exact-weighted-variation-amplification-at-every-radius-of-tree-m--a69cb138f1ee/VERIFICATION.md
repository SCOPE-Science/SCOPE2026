# Reproducibility and limits

Execute `python3 verify.py` with Python 3 and no external packages. The script performs 1,628 exact cut-versus-frontier comparisons over 304 randomly generated finite positively integer-weighted trees and 318,280 nontrivial indicator cuts, then checks 11,309 integer-signal variation inequalities and 12 explicit branching constructions. All random data are generated by a fixed local seed. The completed execution printed `PASS 304 weighted trees; 1628 exact cut/formula comparisons; 318280 indicator cut instances; 11309 integer-signal inequalities; 12 branching families`.

The exact analytical argument, rather than these finite checks, proves every positive real weight choice, every real input signal, all finite tree sizes, and every nonnegative integer radius. In particular, the script does not claim to enumerate infinitely many trees or to establish novelty. External independent audit has not been performed.
