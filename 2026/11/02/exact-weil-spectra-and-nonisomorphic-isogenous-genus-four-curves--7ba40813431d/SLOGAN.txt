For the genus-four reciprocal hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^9+a x^5+x\) over \(\mathbf F_{11}\) and \(\mathbf F_{13}\), every smooth member has Weil numerator \((1+bT^2+p^2T^4)^2\), with a complete explicit two-prime parameter census. In characteristic 13 exactly the two parameter pairs \(\{3,5\}\) and \(\{8,10\}\) give geometrically nonisomorphic curves whose Jacobians are isogenous over the base field.
