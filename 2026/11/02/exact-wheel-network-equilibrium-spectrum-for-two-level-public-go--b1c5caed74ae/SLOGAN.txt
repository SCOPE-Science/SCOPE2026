For the strict two-level binary public-goods response on a wheel, the exact pure-equilibrium count is bounded by eight when the second desired neighbor count is \(r=2\), but grows as the Perrin number \(\alpha^m\) for every fixed \(r\ge3\). The eight-vertex wheel with \(r=3\) is the unique one-equilibrium instance among all wheel sizes and all such response thresholds.
