A single isotropic rank-one range sketch reused along a rotating two-mode positive-definite family has an exact worst-parameter error atom of mass \(\min\{\Theta/\pi,1\}\) at relative squared error \(\varepsilon^{-2}\), although its single-parameter expected factor is only \(\varepsilon^{-1}\). Independent sketch renewal has an exact success law: equal angular intervals are best at fixed renewal count, and the optimal finite count is governed by the lower branch of Lambert \(W\).
