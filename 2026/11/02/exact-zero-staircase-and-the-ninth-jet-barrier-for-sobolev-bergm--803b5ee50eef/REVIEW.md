# Review of the jet-penalized Bergman zero staircase

## Correctness
PASS. Orthogonality changes exactly one Bergman monomial norm and yields the stated rank-one kernel formula. The boundary phase identity locates every unit-circle crossing; all crossings are strictly inward and scalar roots are simple for admissible penalties. Exact trigonometric inequalities establish the ninth-jet transition, and the small-arc count establishes the \(\sqrt m\) law. The standalone finite checker passed 1,130 assertions; no claim of infinite computer certification is made.

## Originality
PASS on the inspected literature, with historical-priority uncertainty. Miña-Díaz treats multiplicative weights and kernel representations involving origin derivatives; Perälä treats integrable radial weights; Gehlawat–Jain–Sarkar treat Gram kernels of derivative functionals. Published first-order and pure-jet disk Gram-kernel results concern kernels of functional families, not the kernel of a space whose norm includes one fixed-point derivative penalty. A previous point-evaluation penalty result addresses derivative order zero and a different variable weight parameter, and does not imply the all-positive-order staircase, ninth-order obstruction or high-order asymptotics.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The additive Sobolev point penalty is a natural rank-one regularization of a standard analytic function Hilbert space. Its kernel zeros control the failure of zero-free reproducing-kernel geometry. This work supplies a complete sharp classification in both penalty and jet order, detects an unexpected finite ninth-order barrier, and quantifies how close the high-jet spectrum comes to exhausting all eligible roots. These are properties not furnished by the elementary kernel-subtraction identity alone.

## Limits
This covers one origin jet with a nonnegative penalty in the ordinary disk Bergman space, not multiple jets or weighted/other domains. Similar unpublished or unindexed results could exist. Independent mathematical audit and validation have not been performed.
