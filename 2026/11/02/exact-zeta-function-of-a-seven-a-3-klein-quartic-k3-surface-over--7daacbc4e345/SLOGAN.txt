For the characteristic-two quartic \(w^4+x^3y+y^3z+z^3x=0\), the seven \(A_3\) singularities form one rational and two cubic Frobenius orbits, with every tangent cone nonsplit over its residue field. Their explicit resolution gives the exact zeta function of the resulting K3 surface: \(Z(T)=[(1-T)(1-4T)(1-2T)^2(1-4T^2)(1-8T^3)^2(1-64T^6)^2]^{-1}\).
