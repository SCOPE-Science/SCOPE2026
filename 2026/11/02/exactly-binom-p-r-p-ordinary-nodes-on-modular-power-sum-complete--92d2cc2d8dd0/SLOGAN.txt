For every prime \(p\ge5\) and \(1\le r<p\), the projective complete intersection cut out by the first three power sums in \(p+r\) coordinates has exactly \(\binom{p+r}{p}\) ordinary double points and no other singularities. Its reduced singular scheme consists of the \(\mathbb F_p\)-rational projective points with exactly \(p\) nonzero equal coordinates, and the complete intersection is geometrically integral and normal.
