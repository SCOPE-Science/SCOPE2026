For ideal unbiased fixed-grid stochastic rounding, repeatedly contracting an integer state by a fixed \(q\in(0,1)\) has exactly the same terminal distribution as rounding only once if and only if \(q\) is the reciprocal of an integer; successive possibly different reciprocal-integer contractions also collapse to one rounding. This yields minimum possible lattice variance and an exact geometric extinction tail, while other contraction factors can produce strictly inflated two-step variance.
