For any coherent initial qubit thermalizing under a two-jump finite-temperature Davies generator, the nonequilibrium free energies of that qubit and its unitarily equivalent population-inverted preparation cross exactly once. The decay-normalized relative-entropy difference is strictly decreasing in time, with sharp long-time coefficient \(-c\operatorname{artanh}(b)/(2b)\), where \(c\) is the initial transverse Bloch weight.
