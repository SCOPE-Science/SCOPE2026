The long-time sixth longitudinal displacement cumulant of a planar active Brownian swimmer with orientation-only Poisson resetting changes sign exactly twice as the dimensionless reset rate increases, with critical ratios \(q_-=0.25253781628\ldots\) and \(q_+=1.45152266796\ldots\). An exact sixth-order tilted-generator calculation and a two-sign-change polynomial certificate prove the global positive–negative–positive classification, unchanged by independent translational Brownian noise.
