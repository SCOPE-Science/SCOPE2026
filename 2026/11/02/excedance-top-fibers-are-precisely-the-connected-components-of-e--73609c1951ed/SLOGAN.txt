Among permutations with exactly \(k\) excedances, adjacent-position swaps preserving \(k\) connect exactly those permutations that share the same set of excedance *values*. Every \(k\)-subset of \(\{2,\ldots,n\}\) is realized, yielding precisely \(\binom{n-1}{k}\) components, one isolated permutation per Eulerian layer, and an exact Eulerian-number formula for the exchange-edge count.
