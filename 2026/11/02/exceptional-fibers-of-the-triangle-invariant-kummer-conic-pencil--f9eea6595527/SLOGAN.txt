The triangle-invariant degree-\(2(q-1)\) pencil has a complete exceptional-fiber classification: a Cremona–Fermat normalization at \(\lambda=0\), two intersecting Fermat components at \(\lambda=-1/3\) when defined, and four tangent Fermat components with \(3(q-1)\) singularities of type \(A_{q-2}\) at \(\lambda=-1/4\) when defined. Every other finite fiber is smooth, while the fiber at infinity is a doubled Fermat curve.
