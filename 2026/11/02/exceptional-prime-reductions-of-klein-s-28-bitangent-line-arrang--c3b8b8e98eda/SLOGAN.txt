For the integral model of the 28 Klein-quartic bitangent lines, the intersection arrangement changes only in characteristics 2, 3, 7, and 11. In characteristic 7 the lines become all 28 secants of a smooth eight-point conic, while in characteristic 11 they acquire exactly 84 triple points and retain 21 quadruple points; the two characteristic-two primes have distinct nonreduced four-line fibers.
