For the \(28\) geometric bitangents of the Fermat plane quartic \(x^4+y^4+z^4=0\), the reduced crossing arrangement has exceptional odd reduction primes **exactly** \(3,5,17\): its counts of double, triple, and quadruple intersections jump from \((288,0,15)\) to \((0,0,63)\), \((240,16,15)\), and \((144,48,15)\), respectively. The Frobenius orbit structures of every crossing-multiplicity stratum are completely determined by the characteristic and \(q\bmod8\), and all crossings split over at most a quadratic extension.
