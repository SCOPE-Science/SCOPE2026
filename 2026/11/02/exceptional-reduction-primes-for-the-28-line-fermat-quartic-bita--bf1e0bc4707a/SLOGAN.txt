The 28-line bitangent arrangement of the smooth Fermat plane quartic has precisely three exceptional odd reduction primes: \(3\), \(5\), and \(17\). Its geometric intersection counts are \((t_2,t_3,t_4)=(0,0,63)\), \((240,16,15)\), and \((144,48,15)\), respectively, versus the known generic \((288,0,15)\); the characteristic-three arrangement is the full Hermitian-quartic tangent configuration.
