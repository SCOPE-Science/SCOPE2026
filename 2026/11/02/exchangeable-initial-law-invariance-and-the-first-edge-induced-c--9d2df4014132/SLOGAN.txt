For four-site binary voter copying with independent rate-one clocks, the mean consensus time from two uniformly placed dissenters is universally \(4/3\) of the single-dissenter mean minus \(1/12\), regardless of network weights; hence every nontrivial exchangeable initial law orders four-site networks identically by mean consensus time. Four sites are the smallest connected simple-network size where adding one edge can strictly slow consensus, with exactly two such graph-type transitions.
