For every finite Zimin word, the entire minimal factor automaton—and, with different final states, the minimal suffix automaton—has an explicit ruler-arithmetic transition map on \(2^n\) states. Exactly \(2^{n-s}+s-1\) arcs carry the rank-\(s\) letter, so the full automaton has \(2^n-1+\binom n2\) arcs without requiring its exponential-size graph to be generated.
