For every labeled alphabet size \(n\ge5\), the directed overlap graph of distinct four-letter words has an explicit characteristic-polynomial factorization and hence a closed product formula for the exact number of cyclic universal sequences. This evaluates the next window length after the published two- and three-permutation formulas, including \(U_{6,4}=2^{171}3^{43}5^8 7^{16}\).
