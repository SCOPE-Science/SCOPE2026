For the explicit genus-\(9\) ordinary curve \((x^7+x)(z^4+z)=x^8+x^6+x^4+x^2+1\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), the exact Hasse–Weil numerator is \((1-T+2T^2)(1+T+2T^2)^2(1-T^2+4T^4)(1-2T^2+T^4-8T^6+16T^8)\). Its Jacobian has \(1024\) rational points and splits up to \(\mathbb F_2\)-isogeny into elliptic, two-dimensional simple, and four-dimensional simple factors of dimensions \(1+1+1+2+4\).
