For the least initial interval generating \((\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times\), every odd prime exponent stabilizes at \(2\) and the exponent of \(2\) stabilizes at \(3\). This reduces the invariant to finitely many exponents on any fixed prime support and yields a complete exact table for all \(2^a3^b5^c\), with sharp maximum \(17\).
