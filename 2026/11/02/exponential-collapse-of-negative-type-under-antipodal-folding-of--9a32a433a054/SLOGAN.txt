Antipodally identifying the vertices of a binary Hamming cube collapses its supremal negative-type exponent from \(1\) to \(2^{-d+o(d)}\). A weight-four Walsh character yields an obstruction for every fixed multiple above \(\sqrt{2\pi}\,d^{5/2}2^{-d}\), while a uniform perturbation bound supplies an exponential lower bound; equivalently, the isometric Hilbert-snowflake threshold collapses exponentially.
