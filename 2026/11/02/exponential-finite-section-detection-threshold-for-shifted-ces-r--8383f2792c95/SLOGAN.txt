The least leading-coordinate compression detecting nonhyponormality of a negatively shifted order-one Cesàro operator has an exact digamma–trigamma test. As the shift approaches zero from below, its dimension is asymptotic to \(e^{-1-\gamma}e^{1/\varepsilon}\), a factor \(e^2\) earlier than the published preselected finite-support witness, even though the full self-commutator has exactly one negative eigenvalue for every \(\varepsilon>0\).
