For each fixed finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), almost every perfect evolution algebra of growing dimension has only the identity automorphism, with an explicit exponentially decaying error bound. Consequently the number of its \(n\)-dimensional isomorphism classes is asymptotic to \(q^{n^2}\prod_{j\ge1}(1-q^{-j})/[n!(q-1)^n]\).
