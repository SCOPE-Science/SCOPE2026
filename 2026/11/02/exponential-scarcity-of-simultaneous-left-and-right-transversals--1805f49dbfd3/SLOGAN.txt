For a rectangular band with a two-element factor and an \(r\)-element factor, exactly \(r!(r+1)!\) permutations simultaneously make both products \(x\alpha(x)\) and \(\alpha(x)x\) bijective. They comprise only \((r+1)/2^r\) of the ordinary complete mappings, a sharp exponentially vanishing proportion, and admit a cycle-colouring parametrization.
