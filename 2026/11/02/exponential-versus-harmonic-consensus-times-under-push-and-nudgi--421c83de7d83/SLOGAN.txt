For the same complete-network external-influence process, asymmetric push requires \(\exp(N\mathcal I_{\alpha,E}+o(N))\) expected updates to reach its certain eventual consensus, whereas symmetric nudging needs only \(\Theta(N\log N)\) when a positive fraction initially opposes the field. The push exponent is an explicit positive integral, and finite nudging times satisfy sharp harmonic-order bounds.
