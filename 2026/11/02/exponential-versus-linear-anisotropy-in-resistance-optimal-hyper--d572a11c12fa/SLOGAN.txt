For total-effective-resistance-optimal conductance allocation on a priced hypercube, a fixed surcharge on one coordinate direction forces its conductance ratio to decay as \((d-1)2^{-(d-1)/2}\sqrt{Q/[4(P-Q)]}\), whereas a fixed discount makes that ratio grow linearly with dimension. Equal prices give exactly uniform conductances, and the finite-dimensional optimum is uniquely characterized by an explicit binomial-sum equation.
