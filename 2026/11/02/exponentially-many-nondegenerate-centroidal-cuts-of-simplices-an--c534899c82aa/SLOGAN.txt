Every nondegenerate \(n\)-simplex in dimension \(n\ge4\), and every sufficiently small symmetric truncation of all its vertices, has at least \(2^n-1\) distinct centroidal hyperplane cuts. These explicit cuts are nondegenerate critical directions of the oriented halfspace-volume profile, with exact beta-binomial critical values and a signed Hessian whose Morse index is determined by the vertex bipartition.
