For fixed \(0<\tau<1\), the norm of the real skew involution \(\begin{pmatrix}1&\tau\\ \tau&-1\end{pmatrix}\) on \(\ell_p^2\) exceeds the exact one-row Hölder bound by a positive correction of sharp order \(((1-\tau)/(1+\tau))^p/p\), with explicit coefficient and exactly two maximizing projective lines for all sufficiently large \(p\). This gives an exponentially sharp high-exponent law for the corresponding real \(L_p\) skew Schäffer constant.
