At the critical slope \(\nu=p/(p+q)\) of a fractional-power grazing map, infinitely many primitive periods possess attracting windows whose relative widths shrink as \(4(q/p)\nu^{M+1}/(1-q/p)\). An exact finite-return correction moves the flip away from its grazing endpoint by \(4\nu^{M+1}+O(\nu^{2M})\), with a nearby repelling doubled-period orbit on the attracting side.
