Every rectangular-section generalized Steinmetz body has exact, unique John and Löwner ellipsoids governed by one strictly monotone scalar equation; the Löwner ellipsoid touches the entire cylinder-intersection ridge. In dimension \(m+1\), the optimal outer-to-inner ellipsoid volume ratio ranges sharply from \(m^{m/2}\) to the unattained upper limit \((m+1)^{(m+1)/2}/2\).
