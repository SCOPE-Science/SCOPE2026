For every \(m\ge2\) and \(1\le k<m\), the minimum \(k\)-limited dominating sets of the depth-two Sierpiński graph \(S(2,m)\) are exactly the symmetric binary \(m\times m\) matrices with row sum \(m-k\). This proves the conjectured value \(m(m-k)\) at depth two and determines every optimum-set multiplicity by an explicit coefficient formula, with complementary-capacity duality and involution counts at both endpoints.
