A rank-\(k\) nilpotent order-preserving partial injection contraction on a finite chain attains the maximal possible index \(k+1\) exactly when it is a single monotone shift with weakly monotone gaps. All such maps have a complete partition-product rank enumerator \(\frac{2z}{(1-z)^2}(\prod_{j\ge1}(1-uz^j)^{-1}-1)\), and their total count grows as \(\frac{\sqrt3}{\pi^2}e^{\pi\sqrt{2n/3}}\), subexponentially in the chain size.
