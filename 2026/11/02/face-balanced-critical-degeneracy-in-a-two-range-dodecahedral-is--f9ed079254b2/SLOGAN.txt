In the twenty-spin dodecahedral Ising antiferromagnet, adding antiferromagnetic graph-distance-two exchange produces exactly one positive-coupling ground-state transition at \(J_2/J_1=1/2\), where every pentagonal face becomes exactly spin-balanced and the ground-state degeneracy jumps to \(9250\). The zero-temperature Curie coefficient there is exactly \(304/185\), compared with \(16/25\) and \(0\) on the two sides.
