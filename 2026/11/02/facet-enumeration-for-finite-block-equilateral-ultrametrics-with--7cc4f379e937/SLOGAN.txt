For a finite real block-equilateral ultrametric with \(k\ge2\) clusters of sizes \(m_i\) and independent internal distances strictly below the unit cross-distance, the Lipschitz-free ball has exactly \((2^k-2)\prod_i(2^{m_i}-1)\) facets. Every dual extreme potential is an independently anchored low/high two-level assignment on each cluster, so the facet count is completely insensitive to all internal positive scale changes.
