# Review of the left-regular-band and sharp-rank theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses the factual contraction's exact row map and observes that each activated content set is thereafter permanently met. This establishes chronological idempotence and left-regularity for arbitrary words, not merely individual updates. For the lower bound, evaluation on singletons forces every canonical factor representing a nonidentity \(C_A\) to contain \(A\); evaluation on the empty row (or on an excluded nonempty singleton in the serial case) forces the first factor to equal \(C_A\). Flattening arbitrary monoid generators proves indispensability among **all** generating sets, not just the canonical ones. The hub/private-marker construction separates every pair of distinct first-occurrence words, establishing the free embeddings and the exact common equational theory. Exhaustive finite tests cover the stated small carriers and embedded free bands, with no inference from enumeration to the general case.

## Originality — PASS within checked evidence
The source contraction semantics of Belardinelli–Zhang and classical free-left-regular-band theory are prior work. Two closely related published mathematical results explicitly prove individual idempotence and pairwise commutation for factual contractions; the full texts of those results were compared at statement and implication level. Neither treats the monoid generated by all factual contractions, the exact rank among arbitrary monoid generators, the free submonoid realization, or the resulting exact uniform equational theory. The pairwise criterion does not imply these assertions without new multiword arguments. General semigroup representations may offer an alternative equivalent formulation and remain a historical-priority risk; this is not an assertion of absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Factual belief contraction is designed for sequential dynamic epistemic updates, making the full composition monoid a canonical invariant. The theorem gives a complete equational calculus for updates across all finite carriers, a nontrivial exact exponential irreducibility bound for primitive-update vocabularies, and a sharp one-generator distinction under a standard seriality constraint. The free-band embedding confirms that no unrecognized universal identities are introduced by the semantics; it supplies a reusable normal form for arbitrary sequences of factual announcements. No contribution-sized generalization or computational cutoff is being assumed for value.

## Closest literature and residual limitations
The primary model-transforming operator is in Belardinelli and Zhang (2026), arXiv:2606.31861. The two closest checked published results are “Exact commutation law for factual hedged belief contractions” and “Idempotence and exact commutation of propositional hedged contractions.” Brauner–Commins–Reiner (2022), arXiv:2206.11406, treats the classical free left-regular band itself. The semigroup theorem applies to propositional content, arbitrary or merely serial rows, and sufficiently expressive valuations. It does not classify all monoid cardinalities, arbitrary modal updates, or more restrictive accessibility classes. A structurally equivalent semigroup theorem under alternate terminology may exist; historical priority has not been independently certified.
