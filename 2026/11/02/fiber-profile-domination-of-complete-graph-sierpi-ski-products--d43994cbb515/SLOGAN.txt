The domination number of every \(K_m\otimes_f K_n\) is determined exactly by the sorted fiber sizes of \(f\): non-surjective maps force \(m\) landmarks, while surjective maps reduce to an explicit minimum over at most \(n\) integer choices. For \(m\ge6\), varying \(f:[m]\to[2]\) yields exactly the values \(\{2,3,4,m\}\), creating arbitrarily long unattainable intervals.
