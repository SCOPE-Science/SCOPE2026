For the binary word formed by \(r\) alternating doubled runs \(00,11,00,\ldots\), the minimal absent scattered subsequences form at most three consecutive length layers and total exactly \(F_{r+2}\). The complete spectrum has multiplicities \(F_r,2F_{r-1},F_{r-2}\) for \(r\ge2\), proved by an exact odd-run classification and greedy embedding cost.
