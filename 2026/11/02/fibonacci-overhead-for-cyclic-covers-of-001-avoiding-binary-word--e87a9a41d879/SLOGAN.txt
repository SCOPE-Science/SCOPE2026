For every \(n\ge3\), the shortest circular binary word covering **all finite** length-\(n\) words avoiding `001` has length exactly \(F_{n+3}-1+(n-2)F_{n-1}\). The Fibonacci number \(F_{n-1}\) counts unavoidable source-to-sink transfers, each requiring exactly \(n-2\) extra windows; an Eulerian augmentation attains the bound.
