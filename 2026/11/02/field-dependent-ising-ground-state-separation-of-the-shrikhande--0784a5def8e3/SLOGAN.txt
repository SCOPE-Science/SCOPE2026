The Shrikhande Ising antiferromagnet has critical positive fields \(h/J=1,3,6\), unlike its cospectral sixteen-vertex rook mate, which has \(h/J=2,6\). A sharp adjacency-eigenvalue bound plus two exhaustive exact enumerations determine every magnetization sector and critical degeneracy, exhibiting a magnetization-four plateau unique to the Shrikhande graph.
