For every sufficiently large equal-order finite generalized hexagon, the point–line incidence metric has a sharp Hilbert snowflake threshold of order \(q^{-5}\), with exact leading constant \(1/\log(6/5)\) and an explicit next term. Its unique critical negative-type obstruction is the point-versus-line bipartition vector, and the associated minimal affine Hilbert embedding dimension drops by exactly one.
