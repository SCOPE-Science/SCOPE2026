For a fixed-point Euler decrement rounded with \(r\) random bits on a \(2^{-p}\) grid, the iterates reach zero almost surely exactly when \(r\ge p\); otherwise they stop at the positive residual \(2^{-r}-2^{-p}\). Their exact stopping time is a shifted convolution of \(2^{p-r}\) independent coupon-collector times, with a non-Gumbel finite-deficit limit and a self-averaging many-deficit regime.
