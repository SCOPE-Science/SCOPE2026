For the Poisson window \(g_w(x)=1/(x^2+w^2)\), \(w>0\), the integer-translation, half-integer-modulation Gabor frame survives deletion of **any finite number of entire translation columns**, each containing infinitely many atoms. A two-channel Fourier transfer ratio is provably nonrational, preventing finite-output defects; by contrast, deleting a whole translation half-line destroys the lower frame bound.
