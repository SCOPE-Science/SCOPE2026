For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic five, the number of ordered commuting \(m\)-tuples of restricted-nilpotent derivations of \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^5)\) is exactly \(\, (q+1)q^{2m}+(q^3-q)q^m-q^3\) for all \(m\ge0\). The resulting all-arity point-count generating function is rational, with three explicitly determined poles.
