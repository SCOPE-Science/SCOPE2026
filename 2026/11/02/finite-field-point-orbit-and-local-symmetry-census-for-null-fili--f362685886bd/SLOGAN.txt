For null-filiform Leibniz algebras over any field, two nonzero vectors are automorphism-equivalent exactly when their first nonzero indices agree and their leading coefficients differ by an appropriate power. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), this yields \(1+\sum_{i=1}^n\gcd(i,q-1)\) element orbits and an exact enumeration of every linear local automorphism via lower triangular power-subgroup diagonals.
