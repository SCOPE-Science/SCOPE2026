The reduced fifteen-line Igusa-quartic boundary has eleven arithmetic \(S_6\)-twist types but only nine finite-field zeta functions; exactly its five-cycle twists have no rational points. In the five-cycle case its sixteen-dimensional connected Picard torus is **integrally** \(\mathbb G_m\times(\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{q^5}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m)^3\), rather than merely isogenous to that product.
