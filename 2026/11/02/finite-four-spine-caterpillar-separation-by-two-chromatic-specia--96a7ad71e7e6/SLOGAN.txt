The ordinary fourth principal specialization \(\operatorname{ps}_4(X_T)\) distinguishes every four-spine caterpillar through order \(200\), a complete finite range of \(31{,}060{,}953\) nonisomorphic trees. In this range, just the residues of its evaluations at \(2\) and \(3\) modulo \(2^{64}\) suffice to separate every same-order pair.
