For a Brownian probe that resets without resetting its coupled viscoelastic bath, every finite positive bath-memory parameter admits a quantitative exponential lower bound on stationary excursion probabilities, ruling out a Gaussian-cost far tail: the probe moment-generating function diverges for \(|s|\ge\sqrt{r(g+1)}\), and its right-tail logarithmic rate is bounded below by \(-\sqrt{r(g+1)}\). The Gaussian far tails of the published frozen-bath mixture therefore cannot be extrapolated uniformly to arbitrary distances at any finite bath relaxation time.
