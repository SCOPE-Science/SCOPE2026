The finite annealed three-or-more-opinion noisy unanimity-two voter model has an exact product-form stationary count law, and its global count modes always include a one-leader, balanced-remainder profile whose maximal leader size cannot retreat as conformity rises. With six voters and three opinions, the mode jumps exactly from \((2,2,2)\) to \((4,1,1)\) to \((6,0,0)\), at two explicit radical thresholds.
