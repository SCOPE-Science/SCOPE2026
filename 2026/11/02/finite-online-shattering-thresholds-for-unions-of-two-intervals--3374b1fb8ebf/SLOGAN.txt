For unions of at most two discrete intervals, the exact Littlestone dimensions on chains of one through fourteen points are \((1,2,3,4,4,5,5,6,6,7,7,8,8,9)\), while the VC dimension is at most four. The first cardinality-bound shortfall occurs on seven points: \(\operatorname{Ldim}=5<\lfloor\log_2 99\rfloor=6\).
