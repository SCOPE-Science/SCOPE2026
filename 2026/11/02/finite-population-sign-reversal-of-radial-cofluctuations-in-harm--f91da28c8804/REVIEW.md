The disjoint RTP and ABP thresholds prove that distinct microscopic propulsion mechanisms remain identifiable using simultaneous spatial data even when their two-point orientation correlations agree. The exact finite-size boundary is natural for the harmonic collective-ring model and does not rely on an arbitrary parameter slice. Under fixed \(\mu,v,\alpha\) mean-field scaling, the signal decays as \(N^{-2}\), a relevant practical limitation.
