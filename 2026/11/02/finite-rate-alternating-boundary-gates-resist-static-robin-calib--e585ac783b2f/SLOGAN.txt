For Brownian motion between two alternating absorbing endpoints, the mean escape-time penalty is independent of starting position, while the left-exit probability remains affine. Calibrating a static radiation boundary to the mean requires \(h_{\mathrm{time}}=\sqrt{2D\kappa}\coth(L\sqrt{\kappa/(2D)})\), but calibrating it to splitting requires \(h_{\mathrm{split}}=\sqrt{2D\kappa}\tanh(L\sqrt{\kappa/(2D)})\); no finite positive switching rate permits both calibrations simultaneously.
