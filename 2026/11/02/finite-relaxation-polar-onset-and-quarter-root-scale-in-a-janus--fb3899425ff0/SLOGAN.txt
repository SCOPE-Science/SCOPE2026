In a Janus chemical slab with purely orientational phoretic response, retaining finite solute diffusion time cannot produce oscillatory linear modes: the polar–solute operator is symmetrizable, and instability occurs on exactly one finite-wavenumber band. As rotational diffusion vanishes, the critical wavenumber obeys \(q_*\asymp D_r^{1/4}\) (equivalently the selected wavelength diverges like \(D_r^{-1/4}\)), while the critical chemical gain has a square-root correction.
