On a Brownian star with one short absorbing arm and \(q\) long arms, every weak Poisson restart **increases** mean exit time, yet a fixed finite restart rate decreases it for every integer \(q\ge4\). The ratio of restarted to unrestarted means tends to zero like \(15/[2(\log4)^2q]\), giving arbitrarily large improvement despite a no-restart coefficient of variation below one.
