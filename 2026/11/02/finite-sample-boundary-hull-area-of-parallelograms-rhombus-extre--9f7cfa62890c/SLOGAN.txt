For independent arclength-uniform boundary samples from a Euclidean parallelogram, the normalized expected area of the convex hull has an exact finite-sample formula that depends on the side-length ratio but not the angle; among all parallelograms it is largest exactly for rhombi. As a side shrinks at inverse sample-count scale, the missing-area fraction undergoes a sharp crossover from fixed-shape order \(n^{-2}\) to order \(n^{-1}\).
