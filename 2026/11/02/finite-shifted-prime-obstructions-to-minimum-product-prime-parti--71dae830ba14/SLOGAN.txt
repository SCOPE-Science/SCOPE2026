For a fixed minimum-product distinct-prime set \(A\), whether adjoining any sufficiently large prime \(q\) preserves minimality is decided **exactly** by whether finitely many explicit shifts \(q+\delta\) are composite. This yields prime-relative density one for successful extensions and \(q_A(n)\sim n\log n\), resolving the asymptotic-growth question associated with Campbell's 2026 construction.
