# Three-axis scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Finite support implies all-partner commutation by a single finite-intersection identity. Nonprincipal filters fail against an ultrafilter on their own member set: the membership incidence relation has all columns large and exactly the (non-large) kernel as its large-row set. Principal filters on infinite kernels fail against a nonprincipal ultrafilter on their kernel using the complement of the diagonal. The proof explicitly invokes the ultrafilter lemma under ZFC. The finite checker corroborates 28,058 relations, not infinite generality.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence, with priority risk.** Engström's Proposition 4.5 substantially anticipates the principal-kernel case by characterizing fixed-partner dominance through intersection completeness. Goldberg covers ultrafilter pairs; recent neighborhood-product work identifies pointwise rectangularization. Neither inspected statement is the all-partner characterization of arbitrary proper filters with ultrafilter witnesses for every failure; notably the nonprincipal incidence-ultrafilter construction is not obtained by simply changing the cardinal in the principal theorem. Altman–Peterzil–Winter (2005), Corollary 8, gives a substantive countable fixed-domain scope-independence classification, including finite principal filter cases. Its full available preprint was examined, and countable consequences are not claimed as novel. The proposed extra content is the unrestricted-cardinality, mixed-carrier universal quantification and constructive ultrafilter witness for arbitrary nonprincipal filters; equivalent all-cardinality formulations remain a residual priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** Universal commutation is a natural structural invariant of filter quantifiers and local neighborhood semantics. The result gives an exact all-cardinality classification and a useful reduction: instead of testing all proper partner filters, ultrafilters suffice; finite support is the complete boundary. It is neither a numerical reparameterization nor merely the finite-carrier observation.

**Scientific limitations.** ZFC is assumed. The proof classifies universally commuting filters, not all commuting pairs, and the checker tests finite special cases only. The old literature cannot be exhaustively excluded by searches.
