In two-candidate spatial voting with independent finitely supported voting costs, every pure equilibrium has zero turnout: the complete equilibrium set is \(I^2\), where \(I=[0,1]\cap[1-c_*,c_*]\) and \(c_*\) is the smallest cost. In particular, the equally weighted positive cost grids \(\{j/(m+1):1\leq j\leq m\}\) have no pure equilibrium for any \(m\geq2\), even though their payoffs converge uniformly to a continuous-cost game with a polarized pure equilibrium.
