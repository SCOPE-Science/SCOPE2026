For finite-world closure models, model-relative global truth plus closure under modus ponens is exactly the missing criterion for a same-world Kripke representation. The number of labelled representing relations is \(\prod_{w}\prod_{T\subseteq K_w}(2^{|T|}-1)\), where \(T\) runs over complete modal types, and their minimum edge count is \(\sum_w|K_w/\sim|\).
