For four-digit simplex self-similar measures at every even integer scale \(b\), the first integer-Fourier cancellation levels have the exact generating function \(3z((b-1)b^2-z)/((b^3-z)(b^2-z))\), giving null-frequency density \(3(b^3-b^2-1)/((b^3-1)(b^2-1))\). At most two Fourier-mask levels can vanish at any integer frequency, and the density of frequencies with two cancellations is exactly \(3/((b^3-1)(b^2-1))\); odd scales have no integer Fourier zeros.
