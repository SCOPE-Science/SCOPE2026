For two symmetric run-and-tumble walkers approaching first contact on a ring, the complete collision-time Laplace transform is explicit, but a vanishing reversal rate produces a singular moment limit: typical times approach ballistic contact while the mean approaches twice that value. Every higher moment has the exact divergence coefficient \(\lim_{\alpha\downarrow0}\alpha^{m-1}\mathbb E[T_\alpha^m]=m!x/(2^m v)\) for integers \(m\ge2\).
