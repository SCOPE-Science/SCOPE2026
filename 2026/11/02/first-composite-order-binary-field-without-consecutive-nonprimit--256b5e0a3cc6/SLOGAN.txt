The first binary field with composite multiplicative-group order but no consecutive nonprimitive pair is \(\mathbb F_{2048}\): its \(111\) nonprimitive elements form an independent set under addition by \(1\), certified by four explicit polynomial Bézout identities. Consequently a general sufficient existence condition of the form \(\theta_q<c\), with only the known \(q=4,8\) exceptions, cannot use \(c>1936/2047\).
