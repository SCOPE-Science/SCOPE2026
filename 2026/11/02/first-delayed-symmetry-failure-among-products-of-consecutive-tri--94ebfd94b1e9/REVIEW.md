## Correctness — PASS

The exact domain is the family \(S_m=\langle m,m+1,m+2\rangle\), \(m\ge2\), under **multiplicative ideal powers**, only \(k=1,2,3\). Distribution of products over the generating sums gives the finite monomial-generator presentation. An elementary induction proves the bounded integer-membership recurrence. ## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge)

The close motivating paper is Chen–Hyde–Laurens–Piermarini–Simons (arXiv:2607.10778v1, Section 4.1). Its earlier two-generator power theorem does not apply to \(S_8\), which needs three generators. Its example of a symmetric semigroup with a nonsymmetric **square** is not the present delayed failure after a symmetric square. The authors' type formula in Proposition 4.7 assumes a special decomposition and does not cover the calculated type sequence \((1,1,7)\). Prior results on numerical semigroups generated by powers of three consecutive numbers use separately powered generators, not mixed generator products. The source-defined power-semigroup construction under addition of subsets is also a different operation. Targeted bibliographic and mathematical-record searches, found no stronger result that implies the exact first-occurrence statement. Priority remains a best-knowledge assessment and could change if an older general theorem is identified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A natural potential principle suggested by the new ideal-power setting is that symmetric behavior at the first two powers might persist to later powers. This explicit counterexample shows it does not, even among consecutive-triple numerical semigroups, and proves the **smallest** generator parameter where delayed loss occurs. The three exact types, complete pseudo-Frobenius set at the cubic failure, and mismatch between Frobenius and genus offer a precise structural witness, not a random numerical curiosity. This complements the previously illustrated one-step loss of symmetry at the square; the distinction between immediate and delayed loss is relevant to understanding type dynamics.
