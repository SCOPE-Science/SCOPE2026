For binary words avoiding adjacent ones, the first seven exact one-deletion code sizes are \((1,1,2,2,3,3,5)\), while the corresponding fractional shadow-packing optima are \((1,1,2,2,3,4,6)\). Thus the natural fractional bound first ceases to be exact at length six; exact rational packing and weighted conflict-clique certificates also establish the gap at length seven.
