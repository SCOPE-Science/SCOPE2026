The first VC–Littlestone gap for maximal-independent-set membership concepts occurs on five graph vertices, in exactly two graph isomorphism types, one a cograph. A single five-vertex cograph block gives connected cographs with \(\operatorname{VCdim}=m\) and \(\operatorname{Ldim}=2m\) for every \(m\ge1\), so the online advantage is unbounded even without induced four-vertex paths.
