The largest binary two-list one-deletion codes have sizes \(2,3,4,7,12,19,33\) at blocklengths \(1\) through \(7\). Length seven is the first at which no optimum decomposes into two uniquely single-deletion-correcting codes: the exact capacity is \(33\), but two uniquely decodable codes together contain at most \(32\) words. Explicit witnesses and independently checkable exact rational decision-tree certificates establish both bounds.
