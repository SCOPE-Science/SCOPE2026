For \(2\le k\le4\), the only permutation-memory Fibonacci recurrences with eventual scalar \(k\)-step collapse are rotations of \((2,3,\ldots,k+1)\); at \(k=5\), a second class generated by \((2,4,5,6,3)\) first appears. The exact successful counts are \(2,3,4,10\), and every successful multiplier is \(3\cdot2^{k-2}\).
