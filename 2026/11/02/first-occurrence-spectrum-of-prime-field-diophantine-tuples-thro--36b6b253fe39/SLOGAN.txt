The least odd primes supporting pairwise-square Diophantine tuples of sizes \(2\) through \(12\) are exactly \(3,7,11,23,37,59,83,131,181,251,353\). A complete exact clique calculation also proves \(M(353)=12\) and supplies a twelve-element witness; this is a finite first-occurrence classification, not a claim about every larger prime.
