For the matrix order \(R=\mathbb F_{q^d}+\pi M_d(\mathbb F_q[[\pi]])\), the \(q^d+3\) intermediate submodules between \(\pi S^2\) and \(S^2\) fall into exactly \(1+[2\mid d]+(q^d-q-[2\mid d](q^2-q))/(q(q^2-1))\) isomorphism classes, with an explicit multiplicity profile. Nonfree classes are precisely the \(\mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\)-orbits of nonrational points of \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_{q^d})\), distinguished by a module-theoretic integral-lifting argument.
