Among pairs of the first five normalized isotropic Gaussian–Laguerre Fourier filters, joint frequency blindness occurs only for levels \((1,4)\) in complex dimension \(6\) and levels \((2,4)\) in complex dimension \(24\), on spheres of squared radii \(12\) and \(60\). Their common nullspaces are exactly bounded/integrable Helmholtz solutions, with a sharp injectivity threshold \(p\leq 4d/(2d-1)\) in \(L^p\).
