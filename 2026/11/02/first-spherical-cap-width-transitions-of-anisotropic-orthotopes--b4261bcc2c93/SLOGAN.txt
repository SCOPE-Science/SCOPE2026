For a randomly oriented anisotropic orthotope in every dimension \(d\ge4\), the entire width-law germ at its first face crossing depends only on the longest width, shortest edge and its multiplicity, with a sharp \(d/2\)-power deficit and exact normalized coefficient. Noncongruent four-dimensional boxes can share this whole upper-tail germ; an elementary local correction is explicit in dimension four, while complete width-moment side recovery is prior work.
