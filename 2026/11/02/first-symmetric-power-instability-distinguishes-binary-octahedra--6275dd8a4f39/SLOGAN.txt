For a genus-two stable rank-two bundle of trivial determinant, the first strictly semistable symmetric power can occur at order \(4\) only with binary octahedral monodromy, and at order \(6\) only with binary icosahedral monodromy. The ruled surface's symmetric-tensor algebra is respectively the graded Kleinian \(E_7\) or \(E_8\) hypersurface ring.
