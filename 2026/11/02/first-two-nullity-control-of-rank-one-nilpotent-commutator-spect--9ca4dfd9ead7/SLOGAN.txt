For every finite field, the full moment-refined similarity spectrum of commutators \([J,X]\), where \(J\) is any fixed nilpotent matrix and \(X\) ranges over nonzero rank-one square-zero matrices, depends only on \(n\), \(\dim\ker J\), and \(\dim\ker J^2\). Explicit formulas count every moment fiber and reveal a characteristic-two split between diagonalizable and nondiagonalizable nonzero-eigenvalue commutators.
