For Boolean positional comparisons of 321-avoiding involutions, the exact VC and Littlestone dimensions through twelve letters first differ by two at eleven letters: \(\operatorname{VCdim}=5\) and \(\operatorname{Ldim}=7\). Complete adaptive trees and exhaustive comparison-support enumeration certify the transition; at eleven and twelve letters a sharp first-query minority-size obstruction prevents saturation of the cardinality bound.
