In the smooth genus-six family \(y^4=x^5+ax^2+bx+1\) over characteristic \(7\), the Jacobian \(7\)-rank attains each value \(0,1,2,3,4\), controlled exactly by two rational parameter curves and one geometrically irreducible nodal quartic whose normalization has genus \(2\). The deepest strata consist of precisely five marked rank-one fibers and one rank-zero fiber, while the \(a\)-number takes only the values \(2\) and \(3\).
