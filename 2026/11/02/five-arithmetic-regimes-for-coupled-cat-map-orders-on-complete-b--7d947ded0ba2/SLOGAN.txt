For symmetric cat-map shears coupled by the complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), the exact period modulo \(2^s\) follows a six-case arithmetic law controlled by parity and the valuations of \(ab+1\), \(ab+2\), and \(ab+3\). All states are periodic, and the maximum period is attained; even the same product \(ab\) can yield different clocks for different graph shapes.
