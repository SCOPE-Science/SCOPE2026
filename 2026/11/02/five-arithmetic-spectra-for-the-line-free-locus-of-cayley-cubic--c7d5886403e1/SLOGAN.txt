The line-free locus of every quartic étale twist of Cayley's four-nodal cubic over an odd finite field has one of exactly five rational-point polynomials, ranging from \((q-3)^2\) to \(q^2\), with a completely determined zeta function. The split twist over \(\mathbb F_3\) is the unique point-free case among all these twists over odd finite fields.
