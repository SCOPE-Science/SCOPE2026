# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For an arbitrary real skew conference matrix, the normalization \(C^2=-(N-1)I\) establishes the ETF Gram representation. For each five-element compression, the four-by-four Pfaffians take squared values one or nine; the characteristic polynomial and the mod-16 determinant divisibility force exactly two spectral possibilities. Comparing coefficients of the full conference characteristic polynomial and double-counting four-subset/five-subset incidences gives the displayed exact class multiplicities without requiring a Paley symmetry or prime-field hypothesis. The proof also checks the order-eight endpoint and strict positivity for larger permitted orders. The standalone program tests all 1,024 five-vertex skew sign patterns and every five-subset of Paley examples with \(p=7,11,19,23,31\). Numerical tests are corroborative, not a proof for arbitrarily large orders.

## Originality — PASS, bounded-literature judgment

Renes (2004, arXiv:math/0408287) proves foundational Paley ETF constructions, not five-column spectra. Hoffman–Solazzo (2011, arXiv:1107.2267) studies erasure robustness and explicitly addresses three- and four-erasure phenomena rather than a five-subset spectrum census. Haemers–Parsaei Majd (2021) consider principal spectra of *symmetric* conference matrices and explicitly exclude the skew case. King (2025, arXiv:2505.00160) characterizes high symmetry of Paley skew-conference ETFs and higher homogeneity, rather than enumerating two five-column spectral classes. A closely related published mathematical finding gives four-column Paley harmonic-frame spectra, but its stated four-column result does not provide the mod-16 five-point dichotomy and universal double-counting distribution. The scientific claim evaluated here is the matrix-independent two-class five-column spectrum with exact multiplicities and sharp restricted-isometry deviation. An equivalent treatment of five-point Pfaffian incidence statistics or five-erasure spectra under older terminology remains possible; exhaustive historical novelty is not certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Five-column restricted-isometry constants are natural deterministic frame-reconstruction invariants, refining previously understood smaller-column erasure behavior. The sharp exact norm and complete spectrum frequencies hold uniformly across all skew conference constructions, including non-Paley matrices, and the order-eight singularity is a sharp natural endpoint. The determinant parity mechanism provides a structural reason for a two-class census rather than a numerical coincidence. The finding does not claim a general restricted-isometry theorem at growing sparsity, nor a new construction of ETFs.

## Limits and closest material

A theorem about all skew conference frames does not imply existence at arbitrary orders. The result does not distinguish five-subset switching/automorphism orbits and does not classify six-column spectra. Papers considered include Renes 2004, Hoffman–Solazzo 2011, Bandeira–Fickus–Mixon–Wong 2012, Haemers–Parsaei Majd 2021, King 2025, and the related four-column published finding. The two five-point classes were tested exhaustively in small Paley cases; the universal exact formulas have a finite algebraic proof.
