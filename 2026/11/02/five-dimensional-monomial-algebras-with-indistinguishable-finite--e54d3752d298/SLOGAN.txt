Two nonisomorphic five-dimensional monomial local algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\) have the same Hilbert function \((1,2,2)\) and isomorphic abstract automorphism groups \((C_2)^4\rtimes C_2\), both of order \(32\). The pair gives the smallest two-generator monomial example where even the full finite rational automorphism group fails to recover the algebra from fixed Hilbert data.
