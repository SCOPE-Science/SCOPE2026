For the smooth genus-six curves \(y^4=x^5+ax^3+bx+c\) in characteristic \(7\), an exact Cartier calculation gives every geometric \(7\)-rank from \(0\) to \(4\) and all Jacobian \(a\)-numbers. The rank-zero locus is a punctured line, while rank one occurs on precisely two smooth punctured cuspidal curves, with closures meeting only at a singular-model boundary point.
