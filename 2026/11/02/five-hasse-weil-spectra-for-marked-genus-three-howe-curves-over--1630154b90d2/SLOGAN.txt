For the \(42\) smooth ordered normal-form genus-three hyperelliptic Howe curves \(y^2=(x^4-Ax^2+1)(x^4-Bx^2+1)\) over \(\mathbb F_9\), exactly five Hasse–Weil numerator polynomials occur, with multiplicities \(6,4,12,8,12\). The sharp rational-point maximum within this marked family is \(20\), attained by exactly six ordered coefficient pairs.
