For a smooth degree-four del Pezzo surface over a finite field whose exceptional-curve Frobenius action is a five-cycle, the connected Picard scheme of its sixteen-exceptional-curve boundary is exactly five induced quintic tori: \(\operatorname{Pic}^{0}(D)\cong(\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{q^5}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m)^5\), an integral isomorphism rather than just an isogeny. The resulting group is \(C_{q^5-1}^{5}\); the calculation also supplies an explicit motivic class and zeta function of the complement.
