The stable genus-eight curve obtained by gluing fourteen projective lines according to the Fano-plane Heawood graph has exactly five integral Picard-torus types among its \(\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\)-twists over any finite field, including a cubic norm-one torus. Its complete zeta spectrum is determined, and precisely the order-seven twists have no rational points over the ground field.
