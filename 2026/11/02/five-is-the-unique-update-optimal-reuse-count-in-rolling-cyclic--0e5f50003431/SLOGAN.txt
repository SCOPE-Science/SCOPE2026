For the exact \(q=m+1\) rolling cyclic Ritz regime, repeating each historical Ritz step precisely \(\ell=5\) times is the unique integer choice maximizing the proved doubly-exponential convergence slope per actual gradient update. The maximal slope is \(\log((7+\sqrt{41})/2)/(5m)\), and even the close competitor \(\ell=4\) is strictly slower.
