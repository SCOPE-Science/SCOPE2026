For the order-six genus-four hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\) in characteristic seven, the nonordinary locus has two disjoint components on the smooth-parameter space, one occurring twice in the Hasse divisor. The entire geometric \(7\)-rank stratification is explicit, with precisely five coefficient-marked rank-one fibers in Frobenius orbits of lengths \(1,1,3\), and none of rank zero.
