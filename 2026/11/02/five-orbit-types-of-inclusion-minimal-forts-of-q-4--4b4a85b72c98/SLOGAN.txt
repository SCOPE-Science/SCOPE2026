The \(348\) inclusion-minimal forts of the four-dimensional hypercube \(Q_4\) form exactly five automorphism orbits of sizes \(12,16,32,96,192\), with size enumerator \(28z^4+32z^6+288z^8\). Their complete orbit representatives and a reproducible exhaustive classification are supplied; the total \(348\) and the two minimum-cardinality types were already known.
