# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every labelled simple G_n=C_n square K_2, n>=5, each cubic initial force must have a black neighbor triple T (three consecutive rim vertices) or R (two adjacent rim vertices plus a rung-mate). After either first force the common 4-black H has no available continuation. A fourth original seed x must lie among four boundary vertices u_-2,u_2,v_-1,v_1 to enable a second force; outside this set neither H nor x can force. The eight choices (T/R x four) reduce exactly to families A..E, and every prototype creates two adjacent fully black rungs, which propagate around the cycle. The five disjoint symmetry classes have sizes 2n,4n,4n,2n,n and total 13n. The boundary n=5 is included and n=3,4 are excluded by theorem. Independently enumerated all 3- and 4-vertex subsets and forced closure for n=5..9; compared actual sets with exact A..E construction and confirmed no 3-seeds, all 13n minimal 4-seeds. This finite corroboration does not justify all n; the first-force obstruction and propagation do.

## originality

PASS

The numerical minimum Z=4 for generalized Petersen P(n,1) is prior art, but neither it nor general forcing polynomial inequalities gives the five actual minimum forcing set orbits and exact coefficient z(G_n;4)=13n. Best-of-knowledge originality after primary comparison, with quantified historical risks.

## value

PASS

Prisms form a canonical infinite family of connected cubic vertex-transitive graphs with an already known optimum seed size 4. The exact number of minimum seeds and complete five propagation geometries is a natural structural and combinatorial invariant useful for enumeration/reconfiguration, not given by the optimum value itself or by generic graph-operation bounds.
