All nontrivial rectangular path grids are indistinguishable by rank-two adjacency logic, but exactly five theories appear at rank three. Apart from the four exceptional grids \(P_2\square P_2\), \(P_2\square P_3\), \(P_2\square P_4\), and \(P_3\square P_3\), every such grid is rank-three equivalent to the ten-vertex ladder \(P_2\square P_5\); the representative bound ten is sharp.
