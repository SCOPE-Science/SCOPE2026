The number of indices below \(2^r\) at which a 2023 binomial-parity sum has 2-adic valuation exactly one has generating function \(t^4(1-t^5)/((1+t^5)(1-t-t^2-t^3-t^4-t^5)^2)\), while the known odd-count baseline has the corresponding pentanacci denominator to the first power. More generally, every fixed valuation \(v\ge0\) has an explicitly computable rational enumerator and count asymptotic to a positive constant times \(r^v\lambda^r\), where \(\lambda\approx1.9659482366\).
