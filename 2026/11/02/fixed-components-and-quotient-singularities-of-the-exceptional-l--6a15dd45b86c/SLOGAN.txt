For the exceptional non-natural involution on the length-two Quot scheme \(\operatorname{Quot}^{2}_{C}(\mathcal O_C^{\oplus r})\), the entire fixed locus consists of \(C^{(2)}\times\mathbb P^{r-1}\) and \(C\times\operatorname{Gr}(2,r)\). The second component always creates transverse index-two non-Gorenstein singularities in the quotient, and the involution has Lefschetz number \(r(1-g)(r+2-2g)\).
