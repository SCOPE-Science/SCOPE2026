For two nonadjacent tagged vertices in a fixed-count circle random connection graph, isolation covariance changes sign at a single spatial separation for every continuous strictly decreasing connection kernel, while its Poissonized counterpart is nonnegative. For a hard connection radius \(r\), the switching separation is \(2r(1-2r)\); among nonadjacent pairs, the positive-covariance fraction has the sharp maximum \(3-2\sqrt2\).
