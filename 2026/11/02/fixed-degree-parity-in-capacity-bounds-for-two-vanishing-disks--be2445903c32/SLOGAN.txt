For two equal disks shrinking around distinct centers, every fixed even-degree least-squares capacity bound becomes asymptotically sharp, but every fixed odd-degree bound falls short by a factor asymptotic to a known constant times \(\sqrt{r/a}\). The exact parity coefficients follow from monic Bergman-polynomial jets and the capacity law \(\operatorname{cap}(K_{a,r})\sim\sqrt{2ar}\).
