For primes \(5<p<q\) with fixed even difference \(d=q-p\), divisibility of the first \(pq\) Fibonacci sum by \(pq\) is decided exactly by the two fixed Fibonacci integers \(F_{d-3}-1\) (with value zero at \(d=2\)) and \(F_{d+3}-1\), selected by quadratic characters. The exceptional differences \(d=2,4\) allow precisely the prime pairs with \(p\equiv2\pmod5\), whereas every fixed even \(d\ge6\) has only finitely many qualifying pairs, all with \(p\le F_{d+3}-1\).
