For a one-column randomized range finder with right singular vectors aligned to the probe coordinates, iid fair signs minimize the expected squared Frobenius residual among all nonzero iid centered unit-variance probe laws for every dimension \(d\ge3\) and every nonnegative power index, with uniqueness when the singular spectrum is nonflat. On one-spike matrices, excess error decays as \(L^{-(2q+1)}\) for sign probes but only as \(L^{-(2q+1)/2}\) for Gaussian probes, with sharp constants and an exact pairwise certificate for the general gap.
