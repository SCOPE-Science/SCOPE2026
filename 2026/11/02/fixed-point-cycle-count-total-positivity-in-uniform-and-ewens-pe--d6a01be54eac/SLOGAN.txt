For a uniform or Ewens(\(\theta\)) permutation, fixed-point abundance conditioned on the total number of cycles increases in monotone likelihood-ratio order: the complete joint cycle/fixed-point count matrix is TP\(_2\), with strict cross-minors on overlapping positive support. This gives strictly positive covariance for every pair of strictly increasing transforms, uniformly in all \(n\ge2\) and \(\theta>0\).
