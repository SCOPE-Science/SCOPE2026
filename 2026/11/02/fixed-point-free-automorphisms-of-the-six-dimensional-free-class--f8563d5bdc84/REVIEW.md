# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The automorphism block form follows directly from the characteristic derived ideal and the universal exterior-square bracket. The condition for a fixed vector is reduced, with no hypothesis on characteristic, to two explicit \(3\times3\) determinant tests. Rational-canonical type enumeration covers every invertible matrix exactly once, including nonsemisimple repeated-root cases; centralizer sizes and norm-one quadratic exceptions provide the five contributions. The resulting integer formula was independently cross-checked by complete matrix enumeration for fields of sizes \(2,3,4,5\), including a nonprime field and separate rank tests on explicit Lie automorphisms. Possible limits: the enumerations check only four fields, while the all-field statement rests on the five-case proof.

## Originality: PASS (best-knowledge, priority unverified)

The rank-three reciprocal-eigenvalue restriction is not a restatement of the rank-two Heisenberg fixed-point-free count; the latter has a one-dimensional exterior square and does not imply the six-dimensional formula. The 2014 sources by Furutani–Markina–Vasil'ev and Benito–de-la-Concepción provide the free-algebra structure and classical automorphism setup, not an all-finite-field fixed-point-free census. Bryant–Papistas concerns finite-group fixed subalgebras of relatively free Lie algebras, not the present finite-rank arithmetic enumeration. Burde–Dekimpe treats general existence and structural restrictions, not this count. The source inspection and additional published-record searches found no dominating theorem. A previously unnoticed conjugacy enumeration may imply the formula, and historical publication priority is not independently certified.

## Value: PASS

This is the first rank after the familiar three-dimensional Heisenberg setting where exterior-square eigenvalues impose three distinct reciprocal-pair restrictions. The result is a complete exact census over every finite field, detects a characteristic-parity term, gives the exact acceptance density among all automorphisms, and identifies a natural full-dimensional smooth irreducible geometric locus. These are intrinsic structural and arithmetic invariants of a standard six-dimensional free nilpotent Lie algebra; they are not ad hoc parameter substitutions. The result is not claimed to generalize to arbitrary ranks or classes.
