The six-dimensional free class-two Lie algebra \(V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), with \(\dim V=3\), has exactly \(q^{12}(q^6-3q^5+q^4+4q^3+q^2-3q-8-\epsilon_q)\) fixed-point-free automorphisms, where \(\epsilon_q=1\) for odd \(q\) and zero otherwise. This gives an exact characteristic-parity correction and a smooth geometrically irreducible open locus in the automorphism group.
