For finite level-homogeneous prefix trees in the pure strict-ancestry language, unrestricted two-variable first-order logic detects height and only the first branching generation, while every width \(k\ge3\) detects the complete generation-by-generation branching sequence truncated at \(k\). This gives exactly \(h+1\) two-variable theories and \(k^h\) \(k\)-variable theories at height \(h\), with sharp smallest representatives.
