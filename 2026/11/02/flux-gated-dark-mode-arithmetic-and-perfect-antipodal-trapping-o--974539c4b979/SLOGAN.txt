A one-excitation, locally damped homogeneous quantum ring has no nondecaying modes away from zero or half flux \(\phi\equiv0,\pi\pmod{2\pi}\); at those resonances their exact dimension follows a gcd formula for any nonempty set of loss sites. For an even ring with one sink, switching from zero to half flux makes an initially antipodal excitation change from eventual certain absorption to perfect protection at every time, independently of the positive loss strength.
