# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The dissipation identity proves that perpetual survival is exactly projection onto the common nodal eigenspaces of the Hermitian hopping Hamiltonian. A direct Fourier-pair decomposition restricts all possibilities to zero or half flux; explicit sine bases yield the congruence conditions, all starting-vertex survival probabilities, the two gcd dimension formulas, and the if-and-only-if all-flux placement rule. The potentially delicate even-cycle parity split was derived explicitly. A separate finite-dimensional implementation constructs full matrices and verifies all formulas for 825 parameter/flux configurations, but finite checks are not treated as general proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, historical priority provisional
The zero-flux avoidance criterion and projection identity are **already covered** in Krapivsky, Luck and Mallick (2013/14), especially Section 5. Emary (2007) already describes magnetic-flux-controlled dark states on three-dot rings. Rudner, Levin and Levitov (2016) establish more general non-Hermitian/topological frameworks. Their examined full texts do not establish this particular arbitrary-two-trap, half-flux gcd formula, parity-switch dichotomy, every-initial-site survival law, and all-flux coprime criterion. Related indexed mathematical findings on flux-dependent band density and cycle phase laws concern different operators/observables. Similar elementary Fourier arguments could have been carried out but not separately indexed or published; priority remains uncertain.

## Value — PASS
The precise two-absorber placement problem is physically motivated: persistent coherent trapping obstructs extraction, and a controllable flux or trap separation can switch that obstruction. The exact arithmetic criterion ensures absorption for *all fluxes*, not just a generic flux, and the even-cycle paradox shows that inserting half a flux can either strengthen or eliminate trapping. Both phenomena materially guide device geometry and are not merely a routine reformulation of a zero-flux count.

## Boundaries and remaining risks
The model is restricted to coherent uniform nearest-neighbor hopping, strictly positive local losses, and the infinite-time no-jump survival probability. No finite-time speed, robustness away from resonant flux, or uniqueness against unindexed earlier literature is claimed. Independent audit has not been performed.
