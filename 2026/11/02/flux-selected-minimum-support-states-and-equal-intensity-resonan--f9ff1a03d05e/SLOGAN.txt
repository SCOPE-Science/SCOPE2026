In the magnetic Lieb optical lattice, exact compact zero modes require at least \(4\), \(6\), or \(7\) occupied waveguides depending on whether the plaquette phase equals \(1\), \(-1\), or neither. For generic flux, all minimum-support modes have equal intensity on all seven sites **only** at flux \(\phi\equiv\pm2\pi/3\pmod{2\pi}\), with inverse participation ratio \(1/7\).
