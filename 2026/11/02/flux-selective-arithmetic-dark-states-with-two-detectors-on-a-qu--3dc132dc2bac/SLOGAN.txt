Two simultaneous detectors on a magnetic quantum ring obey an exact greatest-common-divisor law for asymptotic undetected weights; continuous absorption and nonresonant projective monitoring ensure detection at every flux exactly when detector spacing is coprime to ring size. Half a flux quantum can create or destroy dark modes; for a six-site ring, suitable detector spacings change localized survival between zero and \(1/3\), or between \(1/2\) and zero.
