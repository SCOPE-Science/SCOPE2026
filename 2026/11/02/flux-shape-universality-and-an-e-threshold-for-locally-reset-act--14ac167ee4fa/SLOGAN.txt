For a broad class of locally reset active gases, the maximum stationary droplet half-width is exactly \(M/(2e\rho_m)\) and occurs when precisely \(1-1/e\) of all mass is dense, independently of the nonlinear gas-speed law. In contrast, a sharp flux-peak ratio \(F_{\max}/J=e\) decides whether this maximum occurs before or after the competing all-gas stationary branch disappears.
