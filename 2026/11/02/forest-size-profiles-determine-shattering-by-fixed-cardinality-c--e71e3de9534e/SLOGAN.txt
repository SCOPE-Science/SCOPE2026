For two-block partitions with a prescribed small block of size \(a\), an edge-query set is VC-shattered exactly when it is a forest whose component sizes pass a finite subset-sum test, independent of tree shape. This yields \(\operatorname{VCdim}\le 2a\) in every order and the exact saturation law \(\operatorname{VCdim}=2a\) for all \(n\ge3a+1\).
