The four-agent graph \(K_3\sqcup K_1\) is a strict Nash-equilibrium network in a Katz-centrality link-sponsorship game with player-specific costs arbitrarily close to equality; no non-core–periphery equilibrium graph is possible on three or fewer agents. Its exact minimum supporting cost ratio is \((1-2q)(1-2q^2)/(1-3q)\), which approaches \(1\) with attenuation \(q\downarrow0\).
