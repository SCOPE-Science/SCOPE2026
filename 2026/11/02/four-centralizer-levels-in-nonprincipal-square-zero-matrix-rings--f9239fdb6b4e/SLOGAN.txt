For the \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrix algebra over a finite split square-zero local ring with \(d\ge2\) radical generators, every element centralizer has one of exactly four sizes, and all multiplicities admit closed formulas. For fixed finite residue field \(\mathbb F_q\), the probability that two matrices commute tends to \(q^{-6}\) as \(d\to\infty\), despite unbounded ring size.
