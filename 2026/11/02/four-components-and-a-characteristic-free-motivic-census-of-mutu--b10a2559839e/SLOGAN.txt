For every field, the reduced variety of ordered mutually annihilating pairs in the split octonion algebra has exactly four geometric components, of dimensions \(9,8,8,8\). Its characteristic-free motivic class is \(\mathbb L^9+3\mathbb L^8-2\mathbb L^7+\mathbb L^5-2\mathbb L^4\), yielding an exact finite-field zero-product count and zeta function.
