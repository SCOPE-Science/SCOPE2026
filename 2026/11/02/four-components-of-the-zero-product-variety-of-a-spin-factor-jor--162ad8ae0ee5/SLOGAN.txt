For every nondegenerate spin-factor Jordan algebra \(k\oplus V\) with \(\dim V\ge2\) and \(\operatorname{char}k\ne2\), the reduced zero-product variety has exactly four geometrically irreducible components: two linear, one orthogonality hypersurface, and one Segre–quadric cone. Their dimensions show equidimensionality exactly at \(\dim V=2\); an explicit quadratic-character formula counts all ordered zero-product pairs over every odd finite field.
