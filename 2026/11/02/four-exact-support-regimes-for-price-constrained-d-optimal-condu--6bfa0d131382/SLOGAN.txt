For the \(m\)-page book network \(K_2\vee\overline{K_m}\) with three independent positive conductance-price classes and an arbitrary total budget, the unique globally D-optimal design has four explicit support regimes despite permitting each edge to vary independently. Its direct hub edge is deleted when its price reaches an exact threshold \(R_c\); at equal spoke prices \(P=Q=S\), this threshold simplifies to \(4S/(m+1)\), so even uniformly priced books with \(m\ge3\) optimally delete their hub edge.
