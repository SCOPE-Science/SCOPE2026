The higher-genus elliptic surfaces of Li and Liu have four **explicit independent sections**: \((0,1)\) and \((a_i,b_it)\) for \(a_i^3+a_i+1=0\), \(b_i^2=a_i\), with height matrix \(2I_4\); every pair meets transversely at their single smooth infinity fiber. On the rational antecedent, these same four points form an index-two orthogonal sublattice of the classically known \(D_4^*\) Mordell–Weil lattice.
