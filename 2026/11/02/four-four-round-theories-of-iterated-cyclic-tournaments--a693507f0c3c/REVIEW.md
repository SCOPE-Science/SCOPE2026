# Self-review of the four-round cyclic-tournament classification

## Correctness
PASS. The complete tuple orbit invariant has a constructive induction over ternary-prefix nodes, including repeated selected points. Two independently coded exact recursive calculations enumerate every orbit up to four choices in levels 1 through 5; their orbit counts and four-class pattern match. The independently proved lexicographic-product EF congruence propagates the verified level-4/level-5 match to all later levels, so the finite certificate is not mistaken for infinite enumeration. The proof distinguishes all four root types.

Not all historical papers or full texts were read, and a previously implicit classification remains a residual possibility.

## Value
PASS. This is a complete sharp bounded-depth classification of a natural nested family of finite regular tournaments: it identifies the exact depth at which additional lexicographic substitution becomes logically invisible while the number of vertices continues to triple. The proof supplies a reusable finite-basis/pumping method for other lexicographic tournament towers, a sharp 81-vertex representative bound, and an explicit exact certificate rather than an arbitrary finite numerical observation.
