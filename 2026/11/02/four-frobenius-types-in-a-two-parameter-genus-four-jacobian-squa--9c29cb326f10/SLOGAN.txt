For the two-parameter genus-four hyperelliptic family with Jacobian \((2,2)\)-isogenous to the square of a genus-two Jacobian, the ten Weierstrass points have only four Frobenius cycle types over any odd finite field. Their exact parameter frequencies, minimal splitting degrees, and rational Jacobian \(2\)-torsion orders follow from a quadratic-root norm decomposition and explicit character sums.
