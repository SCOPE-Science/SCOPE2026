The complete intrinsic two-point distance histogram of a unit-edge complete multipartite metric graph determines exactly the first four power sums of its part sizes, with explicit recovery from a density height, slope, jump and endpoint limit. This yields a sharp inverse threshold: all such graphs with at most four parts are distinguished, but the nonisomorphic five-partite graphs \(K_{1,5,9,17,18}\) and \(K_{2,3,11,15,19}\) have identical full histograms.
