For every binary word length \(19\le n\le128\) with exactly four \(1\)'s, the minimum number of distinct Abelian-square factors is \(\lfloor(n+2)/4\rfloor\); all minimizers are explicitly classified and number \(2,2,8,4\) according to \(n\bmod4\). The result certifies 110 consecutive lengths in the first fixed-Parikh class left open by the motivating study, without claiming an unrestricted theorem.
