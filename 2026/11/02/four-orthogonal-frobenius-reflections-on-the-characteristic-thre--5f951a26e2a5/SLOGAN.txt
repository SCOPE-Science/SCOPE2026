For the degree-two del Pezzo surface \(w^2=x^4+y^4+z^4\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), arithmetic Frobenius on the integral geometric Picard lattice is exactly the product of four orthogonal \((-2)\)-root reflections; the 56 exceptional curves form 28 conjugate pairs, the arithmetic Picard rank is four, and the cyclic Picard cohomology vanishes. Its zeta function is \(Z(T)=1/((1-T)(1-9T)(1-9T^2)^4)\).
