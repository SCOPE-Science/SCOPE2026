For synchronous three-state excitation on complete split graphs \(K_m\vee\overline{K_n}\), all clique sizes \(m\ge3\) admit an exact nine-cell first-periodic-entry census, including sharp extinction basins and primitive three-cycle counts. As the independent part grows, the **unique** nondegenerate clique size giving four equally likely oscillatory entry times \(0,1,2,3\) is \(m=3\); the previously established two-vertex-clique case instead has only three positive limiting entry times.
