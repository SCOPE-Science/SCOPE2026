For a complete multipartite graph with \(p\ge2\) parts of size \(a\) and \(q\ge2\) parts of size \(b\), the fastest symmetric loopless random walk has an exact four-phase mixing formula depending only on \(p,q,a/b\). Its unique part-symmetry-invariant optimizer deletes all edges between different size-\(a\) parts precisely when \(pa\le b\), deletes all edges between different size-\(b\) parts precisely when \(a\ge qb\), and mixes fastest at the balanced size ratio \(a=b\).
