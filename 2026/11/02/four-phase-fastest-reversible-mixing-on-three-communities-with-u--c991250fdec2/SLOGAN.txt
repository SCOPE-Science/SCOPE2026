For three complete-multipartite communities with stationary masses \(a,a,1-2a\), the globally fastest reversible mixing rate has four exact rational regimes separated by \(a=1/6,1/3,2/5\), independent of community cardinalities. Direct links between the equal-mass communities become optionally optimal at \(a>1/6\) but unavoidable only for \(a>1/4\).
