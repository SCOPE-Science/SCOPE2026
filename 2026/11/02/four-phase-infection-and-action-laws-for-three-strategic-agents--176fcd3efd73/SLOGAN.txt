In the three-agent strategic epidemic game, the immunity interval left between the existing analytic regimes has four exact outcome phases separated by three algebraic thresholds. The complete final infection law has probabilities \((0,0,1)\), \((0,1/9,8/9)\), \((1/3,1/9,5/9)\), or \((1/3,1/6,1/2)\), and each phase determines the limiting social-action profile.
