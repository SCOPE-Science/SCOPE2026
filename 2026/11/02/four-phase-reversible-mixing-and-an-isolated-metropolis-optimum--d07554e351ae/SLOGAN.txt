On balanced complete multipartite graphs with at least four parts and one exceptional stationary mass, the globally fastest reversible mixing rate has exactly four sharp mass regimes and is independent of the common part size. Every optimizer has the same distinguished-to-ordinary stationary flow, all ordinary-to-ordinary edges disappear above mass \(2/3\), and uniform-neighbor Metropolis–Hastings is optimal at precisely the single mass \(1/(2r-1)\).
