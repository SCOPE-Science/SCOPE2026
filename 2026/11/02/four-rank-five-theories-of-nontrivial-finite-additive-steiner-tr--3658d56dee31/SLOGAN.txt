In the pure ternary block language, the nontrivial finite additive Steiner triple systems have exactly four rank-five theories, represented by orders \(7,9,15,27\), despite all nontrivial Steiner triple systems sharing one rank-four theory. Two depth-five formulas—Pasch existence and an unpaired four-point cap—completely distinguish the four classes, and \(27\) is the sharp largest smallest representative.
