Over \(\mathbb F_2\), all nonzero-singular-point graphs of nonsingular even-dimensional quadratic forms have exactly four first-order quantifier-rank-four theories, represented by graphs on \(5\), \(9\), \(27\), and \(35\) vertices. The final class includes every hyperbolic rank at least three and every elliptic rank at least four; a complete quadratic-character extension count and a finite exact four-round type certificate prove this sharp collapse.
