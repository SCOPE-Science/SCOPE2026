For every binary length \(n\ge5\), uniquely reconstructible four-read sets under exactly two substitutions are precisely four labeled graph families, plus a five-star exception at length five, and their exact count per source is \(10\binom n5+180\binom n6+315\binom n7+105\binom n8+5\mathbf1_{\{n=5\}}\). The genuinely four-read cases are exactly a triangle plus a separate edge and the exceptional five-star; the latter is uniquely decodable even though coordinatewise majority fails.
