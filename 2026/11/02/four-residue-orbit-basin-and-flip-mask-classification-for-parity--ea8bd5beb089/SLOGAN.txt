Binary neighbor-XOR dynamics on the two-set Kneser graphs \(KG(n,2)\) has a sharp four-residue law: fully reversible behavior, two different one-step collapses, or complete projection, according to \(n\bmod4\). Every primitive two-cycle has an exactly classified cut-type flip mask and multiplicity: in particular, for \(n\equiv1\pmod4\) every oscillation flips every state, while for \(n\equiv2\pmod4\) only even cuts oscillate.
