In ordinary rank-four first-order graph logic, \(P_a\sqcup P_b\) is indistinguishable from a connected path \(P_c\) exactly when \(\min(a,b)\ge14\), \(\max(a,b)\ge15\), and \(c\ge16\). Among all disconnected linear forests, the unique smallest such camouflage is \(P_{14}\sqcup P_{15}\), on \(29\) vertices.
