Every infinite smooth binary word with infinitely many even-Parikh prefixes admits a self-shuffle coloring with at most \(4\) consecutive positions of either color, improving the known Kolakoski bound \(48\) to \(4\). A complete \(2764\)-word finite certificate verifies the essential four-length window \(38,40,42,44\) and proves that this is the earliest such four-run window.
