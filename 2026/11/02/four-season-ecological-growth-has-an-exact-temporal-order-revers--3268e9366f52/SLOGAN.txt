Four seasonal growth profiles in a symmetrically connected two-patch habitat have exactly three cyclic growth rankings: grouping similar habitat advantages maximizes long-run growth for dispersal \(0<m<1/2\), while alternating them maximizes growth for \(1/2<m<1\). This is the first cycle length at which temporal reordering can matter in this model, and the same profiles can switch between growth and decline solely by changing their order.
