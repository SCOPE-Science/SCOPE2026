Independently solving the 3x3 four-state reset equations yields rational M=(r²+7r+9)/(r²+8r+7), derivative (r²-4r-23)/(denom²), one positive zero 2+3√3, negative then positive; its value is 2/3+√3/6. For any graph with h(v)<=a, the killing-intensity Feynman–Kac representation bounds survival below by e^-at and hence mean >=1/a. The finite-state resolvent has f(r)=a/r+O(r^-2), so renewal M(r)=(1-f(r))/(r f(r)) tends to 1/a. The independent checker verified the algebra and derivative. No result claims homogeneous equal-weight nearest-target finite optimum.

## originality

PASS

Renewal and resolvent calculations and the hazard comparison are established methods; Hartmann–Majumdar found rapid-reset optimality on homogeneous lattices. The new useful distinction is the sharp minimal four-total-vertex boundary for heterogeneous rates (with directed impossibility at <=3) and a symmetric weighted witness. General network resetting formulae do not explicitly supply that classification.
