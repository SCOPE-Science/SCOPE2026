Among all nontrivial Kneser graphs \(\mathrm{KG}(n,k)\) with \(n\ge2k\ge4\), first-order logic of quantifier rank three sees exactly four classes, with a sharp largest least representative of \(35\) vertices. All triangle-free graphs \(\mathrm{KG}(3k-1,k)\) share the three-round theory of the Petersen graph even though their chromatic numbers \(k+1\) are unbounded.
