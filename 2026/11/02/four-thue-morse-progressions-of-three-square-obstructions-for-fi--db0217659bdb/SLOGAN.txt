For five-colored binary partitions, four entire progressions have the exact Thue–Morse law \(b_5(64k+r)\equiv16+12(-1)^{s_2(k)}\pmod{32}\) for \(r\in\{48,49,52,53\}\). Exactly half of each progression violates the three-square theorem, giving an explicit nonrepresentable set of natural density \(1/32\).
