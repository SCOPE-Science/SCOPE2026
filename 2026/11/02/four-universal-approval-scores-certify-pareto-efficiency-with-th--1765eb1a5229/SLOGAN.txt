With at most three unit-approval voters, a fixed-size committee is Pareto optimal if and only if it maximizes one of four universal weighted approval scores: \((1,1,1)\), \((2,1,1)\), \((1,2,1)\), or \((1,1,2)\). Thus integral and fractional Pareto optimality coincide on the full three-voter approval domain, with no restriction on candidate numbers or approval patterns.
