For any finite projective plane admitting an orthogonal polarity, its loop-retaining point-to-polar incidence relation has exactly four unrestricted counting-free two-variable theories: order \(2\), even order at least \(4\), order \(3\), and odd order at least \(5\). All four phases are invisible to one-variable logic, and the classification holds even for non-Desarguesian planes.
