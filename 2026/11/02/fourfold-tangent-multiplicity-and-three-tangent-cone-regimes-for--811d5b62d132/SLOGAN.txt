The projective first-four-moment variety in characteristic \(p\ge5\), with \(p+r\) coordinates and \(2\le r<p\), has exactly \(\binom{p+r}{p}\) singular points, each of multiplicity \(4\). Its tangent cone undergoes a sharp transition from nonreduced at \(r=2\), to reduced reducible at \(r=3\), to normal integral at \(r\ge4\).
