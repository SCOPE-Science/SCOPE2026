For every odd central Fourier harmonic of the two-dimensional heterochaos baker family, all odd-lag correlations vanish and the even-lag correlations are exact weighted Catalan numbers. Their integrated variance has a unique cusp at the neutral parameter \(a=1/4\), where its finite-time variance is \(3N/2-4\sqrt{2N/\pi}+4+O(N^{-1/2})\).
