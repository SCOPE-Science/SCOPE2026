For every odd prime \(p\), the additive Fourier transform of the determinant-one \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices modulo \(p^2\) vanishes exactly at frequencies whose reduction is nonzero singular or has nonsquare determinant. The associated additive convolution operator has exact rank \(p^4[1+(p^2-1)(p^2-p)/2]\), approaching one-half of the ambient dimension as \(p\) grows.
