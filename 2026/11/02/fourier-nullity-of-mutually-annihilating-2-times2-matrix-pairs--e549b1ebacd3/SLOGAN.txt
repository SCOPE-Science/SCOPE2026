For mutually annihilating \(2\times2\) matrix pairs over every finite field, every additive Fourier coefficient is an explicit integer determined by a determinant pencil, and the convolution nullity is exactly \(q(q-1)^3(q+1)^2(3q-1)+36\mathbf1_{q=2}\). The exceptional correction counts generator lines of the rank-one quadric that contribute Fourier zeros only over the two-element field.
