The finite dihedral quandles \(R_n=(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z,\;a*b=2b-a)\), viewed through the ternary operation relation, have exactly fourteen first-order theories at quantifier rank three. Apart from the six exceptional orders \(2,3,4,5,6,8\), rank-three equivalence is determined exactly by \(\gcd(n,24)\), and the sharp largest least representative has \(24\) elements.
