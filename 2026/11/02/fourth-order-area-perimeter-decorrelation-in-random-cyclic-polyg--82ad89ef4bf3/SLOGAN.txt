For random polygons formed by independent uniform points on a circle, area and semiperimeter become correlated at the sharp rate \(1-\rho_n\sim297\pi^4/(10n^4)\). Correcting the traditional four-to-one area–semiperimeter slope by \(-48\pi^2/n^2\) reduces the residual asymptotic variance to exactly \(33/113\) of the fixed-slope value.
