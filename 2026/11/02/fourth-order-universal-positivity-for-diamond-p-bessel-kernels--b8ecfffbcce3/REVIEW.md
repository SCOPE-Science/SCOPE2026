# Assessment of fourth-order universal positivity for diamond \(p\)-Bessel kernels

## Correctness — PASS

The normalization is the defining absolutely convergent diamond series in Kitajima (arXiv:2603.21072). The order-four series equals the exact divided difference of \(H_r\); coefficient equality follows from \(a+b=1\). For every positive argument, the derivative sign is controlled by \(F(t)=t(2+\cos t)-3\sin t>0\), proved by the third derivative on \((0,\pi]\) and the \(t-3\) estimate thereafter. Positivity of the beta order-raising integral extends the result to all real orders at least four. Four exact trigonometric evaluations establish negative witnesses at integer orders zero through three. ## Value — PASS

Universal sign of oscillatory generalized Bessel kernels is a natural structural positivity question. The result identifies the first universally positive integer order for a non-Euclidean polygonal p-circle, proves strictness at every angle and positive radius, and extends sufficiency to all real higher orders. The threshold cannot be improved among nonnegative integers, by four explicit witnesses. It is not merely a coefficient identity or arbitrary finite evaluation.
