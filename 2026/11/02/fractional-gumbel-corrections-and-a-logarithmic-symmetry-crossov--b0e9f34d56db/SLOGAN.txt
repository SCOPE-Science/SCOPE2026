For complete dimer coverage of an even cycle with unequal alternating edge-attempt rates, the centered Gumbel convergence has a sharp fractional-order correction \(n^{-\delta}\), where \(\delta\) is the smaller rate divided by the sum of the rates. As the rates approach equality within a \(1/\log n\) window, their two competing corrections merge into an explicit \(\cosh(\kappa/2)\) profile, with matching first-order Kolmogorov-distance and expectation coefficients.
