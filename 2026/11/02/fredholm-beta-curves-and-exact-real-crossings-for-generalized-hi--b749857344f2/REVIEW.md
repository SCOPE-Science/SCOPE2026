# Scientific scope: transverse crossings of integer Hilbert beta curves

The all-real-parameter Fredholm beta boundary is prior work: the Resultary record *The beta-curve Fredholm spectrum of generalized Hilbert operators on H²* also proves the compact comparison from which the Wiener–Hopf index follows. The boundary, comparison model and index theorem are therefore not the contribution here. Bellavita et al.'s Hausdorff family includes the operator via the measure with density \((1-t)^b\).

The narrower finding classifies every nonzero intersection for positive integer parameter, including absence of such intersections at 1 and 2. The two gamma factors yield a monotone radial profile and a strictly unwrapped phase in this case. Radius rules out collisions of unequal absolute parameters, while phase counting and the two tangent components give the real locations and transversality. These are geometric data not stated in the known beta-boundary formula, and they quantify which geometric complexities can appear as the parameter varies. No classification at 0 or at noninteger parameters is claimed; the finite checker does not establish the all-integer theorem.
