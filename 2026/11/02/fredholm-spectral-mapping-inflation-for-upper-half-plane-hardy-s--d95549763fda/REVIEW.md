## Correctness — PASS
The source's Paley–Wiener weight gives an exact unitary weighted-translation group. The logarithmic coordinate conjugates its generator to \(-d/dx+b_n(x)\) on \(H^1(\mathbb R)\), with two distinct asymptotic limits. Integrating factors show the interior generator has zero kernel and a closed codimension-one range; weakly null Weyl sequences settle the boundary. Exact group norms give the outer resolvent bounds, matched by the spectral-distance inequality. Exponentiating the countably aliased adjoint eigenvalues produces infinitely many independent adjoint eigenvectors for every point inside the fixed-time spectral annulus. The enclosing annulus follows from group power norms. Proof applies at every integer order \(n\ge1\). A finite derivative checker is supplementary, not a proof of these operator statements.
