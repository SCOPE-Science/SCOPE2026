For the variety generated by all finite bi-Heyting chains, every \(k\)-variable term is exactly an independent choice of an input value or an endpoint on each marked ordering type, giving \(F(k)\cong\prod_{r=0}^k C_{r+2}^{T_{k,r}}\). In particular the one- and two-generator free algebras have exactly \(12\) and \(62\,208\) elements, respectively.
