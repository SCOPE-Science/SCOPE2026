For a connected linear oscillator network with one Poisson-reset vertex, arbitrary frequency heterogeneity changes the entire finite-rate stationary phase law only by an explicit deterministic graph-Poisson translation: every fluctuation law depends on the frequency vector solely through its total sum. The unique invariant law has an exact covariance equation, and on a two-node edge the relative-phase variance is \(\Omega^2/[\lambda(3\kappa+\lambda)]\), vanishing precisely at zero total drive.
