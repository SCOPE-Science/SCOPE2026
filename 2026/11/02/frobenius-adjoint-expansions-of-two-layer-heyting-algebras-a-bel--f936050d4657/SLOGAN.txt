For the Heyting algebra of upsets of a two-layer complete bipartite poset with \(a\) lower and \(b\) upper points, the ordered Frobenius-adjoint operator pairs are classified by two independent set partitions and one exceptional collapsed pair. There are exactly \(1+B_aB_b\) labelled expansions and \(1+p(a)p(b)\) isomorphism types, where \(B_k\) and \(p(k)\) are Bell and integer-partition numbers.
