For the 112-line Fermat quartic over \(\mathbb F_3\), Frobenius fixes eight lines and pairs the other 104; its action on the graph critical group has fixed and coinvariant groups abstractly isomorphic to \( (\mathbb Z/28\mathbb Z)^{36}\oplus\mathbb Z/280\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/1120\mathbb Z)^{10}\). This captures the integral descent information absent from the ordinary critical-group decomposition.
