For Veniani's three quartic K3 models over \(\mathbb F_3\), the zero-parameter surface has 112 lines in 40 fixed points and 36 Frobenius pairs, while each nonzero-parameter surface has 58 lines in 10 fixed points and six Frobenius eight-cycles. The line-scheme zeta functions are \((1-T)^{-40}(1-T^2)^{-36}\) and \((1-T)^{-10}(1-T^8)^{-6}\), respectively.
