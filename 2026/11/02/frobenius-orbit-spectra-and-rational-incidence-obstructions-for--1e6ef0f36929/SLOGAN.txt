For a generic Frobenius-stable sextuple on a conic in characteristic at least seven, the 180 Pascal-line–Kirkman-point incidences have exactly eleven possible arithmetic orbit spectra, classified by the sextuple's Frobenius partition. Rational Pascal lines and rational Kirkman points exist with no rational incident pair precisely for cycle types \((6)\) and \((3,3)\); the orbit table determines the finite incidence scheme's zeta function over every extension.
