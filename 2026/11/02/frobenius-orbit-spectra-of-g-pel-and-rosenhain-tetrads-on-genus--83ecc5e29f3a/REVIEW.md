# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The finite geometric identification is standard and independently supported by the cited full-text descriptions. A direct exact computation exhausts the 720 possible six-point permutations and the 140 affine 2-planes, while an independent stable-coset kernel formula validates all eleven rational fixed-point counts. Orbit-length sums, irreducible class sizes, parity, and splitting-degree identities were checked. The all-curve statement follows because every Frobenius permutation of six branch points is one of the exhausted cases, not from numerical sampling. The precise branch-point and affine-plane normalizations are specified in RESULT.md.

## Originality
PASS, with priority reservation. Keum (1997), Ohashi (2009), and Dolgachev (2024) describe the classical six-point module and geometric 60/80 tetrad sets; the inspected relevant text does not give the complete two-scheme arithmetic Frobenius orbit spectra. González-Hernández's separate finite-field node/trope counts do not imply the tetrad cycle partition without an additional four-point calculation. The previously known affine-plane incidence and lattice framework is geometric and does not itself determine arithmetic Galois descent. Actual search comparisons found no identical complete eleven-row table; however broad group-action treatments could implicitly encode it, and no search proves absolute priority.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The complete, naturally parametrized arithmetic classification gives exact counts of rational Kummer tetrads over all finite extensions, both finite étale zeta functions, and least splitting degrees. It separates cases where Göpel and Rosenhain tetrads have different descent behavior, e.g. the six-cycle type gives one rational Göpel and zero rational Rosenhain, while a three-plus-two-plus-one type gives zero and three respectively. This is a structural computation for canonical configurations rather than an arbitrary subset of models.
