For every genus-two Jacobian Kummer quartic over an odd finite field, the complete Frobenius cycle spectra on its 60 Göpel tetrads and 80 Rosenhain tetrads are determined by the cycle partition of the six Weierstrass points. An explicit eleven-row classification gives both tetrad-scheme zeta functions and shows that each tetrad species splits over exactly the same least extension as the Weierstrass points.
