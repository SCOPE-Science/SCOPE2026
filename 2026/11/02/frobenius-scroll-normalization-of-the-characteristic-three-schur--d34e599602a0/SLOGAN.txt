In characteristic three, the displayed integral Schur quartic specializes to a nonnormal Frobenius scroll with normalization \(\mathbb F_2\), conductor the square of its singular-line ideal, and an entire \(\mathbb P^1\)-family of lines. Its complete geometric line classification gives \(q+3\) rational lines and \((q+1)^2\) rational points over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic three; this special fiber must not be confused with Schur's separately known smooth good-reduction model.
