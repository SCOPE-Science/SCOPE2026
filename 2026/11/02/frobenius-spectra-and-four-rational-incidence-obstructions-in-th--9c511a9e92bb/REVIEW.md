# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The odd-characteristic squarefree-sextic hypotheses guarantee sixteen distinct two-torsion nodes and sixteen trope planes. The symmetric-difference incidence rule reproduces all six flags through each node/trope, and the exact 720-permutation checker covers every conjugacy class and checks extension-field fixed-point counts separately. No infinite statement is extrapolated from finitely many sample fields: all possible Frobenius permutations lie in \(S_6\).

## Originality

**PASS with historical-priority qualification.** The geometric \((16,6)\) configuration and labeling by branch points are classical, established in Kumar (2007), section 4.2, and Malmendier (2009), section 2.2. The assessed distinction is the complete eleven-case arithmetic **96-flag incidence spectrum**, and precisely four rational-incidence obstructions. Equality of node and trope orbit spectra follows already from the invertible classical biplane incidence matrix and is not claimed as new. Closest accessible mathematical-record and earlier claim comparisons did not show an identical flag table; more exhaustive historical inspection could reveal implicit or explicit prior coverage.

## Value

**PASS.** Unlike merely counting nodes and tropes separately, the classification answers whether a rational trope actually passes through a rational node. Four Weierstrass splitting types exhibit an exact compatibility failure. The tables also determine every finite-extension incidence count and flag zeta function, natural arithmetic data for the canonical genus-two Kummer configuration.

## Limitations

Only reduced finite étale incidence configurations for separable degree-six hyperelliptic models in odd characteristic are considered, not the full surface zeta function or arbitrary twisted configurations. Independent validation was not performed.
