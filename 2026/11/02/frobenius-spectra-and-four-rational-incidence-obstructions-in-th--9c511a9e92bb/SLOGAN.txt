For a separable genus-two sextic over an odd finite field, the 96 node–trope incidence flags of its Jacobian Kummer quartic have exactly eleven possible Frobenius orbit spectra. Rational nodes and rational tropes exist without any rational incidence exactly for Weierstrass cycle types \((6)\), \((4,2)\), \((3,3)\), and \((2,2,2)\); all extension-field flag counts follow from the explicit spectrum.
