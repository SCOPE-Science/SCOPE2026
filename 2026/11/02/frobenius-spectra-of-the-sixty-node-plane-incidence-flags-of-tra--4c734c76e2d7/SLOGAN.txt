For every finite étale degree-six algebra over a finite field of characteristic at least seven, the ten nodes, fifteen classical Segre planes, and sixty incident node–plane flags of its trace Segre cubic have an explicit eleven-case Frobenius orbit classification. In precisely the two factor-degree patterns \((4,2)\) and \((2,1,1,1,1)\), rational nodes and rational distinguished planes exist but none of them is incident over the base field; the same table determines the exact flag-scheme zeta function and splitting-field degree.
