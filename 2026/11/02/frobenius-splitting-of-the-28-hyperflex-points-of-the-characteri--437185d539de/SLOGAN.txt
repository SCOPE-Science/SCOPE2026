For the characteristic-three Klein quartic \(x^3y+y^3z+z^3x=0\), the 28 geometric hyperflexes split under arithmetic Frobenius into cycles of lengths \(1,3,6,6,6,6\). Their reduced locus has zeta function \(1/((1-T)(1-T^3)(1-T^6)^4)\); this refines, rather than rediscovers, the classical geometric hyperflex configuration.
