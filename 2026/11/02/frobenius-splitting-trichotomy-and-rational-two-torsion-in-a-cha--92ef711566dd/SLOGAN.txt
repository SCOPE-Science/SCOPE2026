For the characteristic-three specialization of the dihedral genus-two family, the six Weierstrass points have exactly three Frobenius cycle types, \(1^6\), \(2^3\), or \(3^2\), with exact parameter frequencies \((q-3)/6\), \((q-1)/2\), and \(q/3\). These cases have minimal branch-point splitting degrees \(1,2,3\) and rational Jacobian two-torsion orders \(16,4,1\), respectively.
