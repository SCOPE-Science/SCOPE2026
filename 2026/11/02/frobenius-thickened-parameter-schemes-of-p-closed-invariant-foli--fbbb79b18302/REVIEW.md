# Scientific review: Frobenius-thickened invariant-foliation parameter schemes

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The ambient parameter space is the projective space of invariant line subbundles in the restricted Lie algebra. For the split height-one kernel, the restricted operation is componentwise Frobenius on the ordinary part and zero on the additive part. Its wedge with the tautological vector gives explicit homogeneous defining equations. On every chart where a supersingular coordinate is invertible the scheme has the complete-intersection equations \(X_i^p=0\); on every chart where an ordinary coordinate is invertible the equations reduce to \(Y_a=0\) and \(X_j^p-X_j=0\), which are reduced. These charts cover the ambient projective space and prove the disjoint decomposition without an unproved radicality assumption. The pushforward module splitting by bounded ordinary-coordinate exponents proves the polynomial and multiplicities. Finite arithmetic independently checks examples, not the infinite claim.

**Originality (O): PASS, qualified.** The closest source is Andreatta–Goren (2026), §1.10, which supplies a general identification of invariant \(p\)-closed foliations with restricted-Lie subspaces and enumerates surface examples on geometric points. This does not settle the scheme structure: in the mixed surface case the previously stated two-point set has scheme length \(p+1\), with a nontrivial nilpotent local ring. Kondō–Schröer, §1, gives p-closed-line principles and set-theoretic loci in a different restricted Lie algebra; Tziolas, Theorem 3.38, establishes the general group-scheme correspondence. Neither inspected account states the family-wide Frobenius-thickening decomposition, multiplicities, or Hilbert polynomial. Searches for equivalent formulas and a comparison with accessible prior research found no dominating claim. This is an assessed gap, not proof that no unindexed result exists; implicit coverage in the older theory of subgroup-scheme moduli remains the leading priority risk.

**Mathematical value (V): PASS.** The scheme structure of the natural parameter space detects infinitesimal deformations absent from the enumerated geometric foliations. For any ordinary rank \(r>0\) and superspecial rank \(s>0\), it gives a positive-dimensional nonreduced component with multiplicity \(p^r\), isolates the reduced ordinary directions, and computes every coefficient of the eventual Hilbert function. The sharp two-dimensional example (two points but length \(p+1\)) demonstrates why geometric-point classification is insufficient. This is an exact invariant of a natural family and a nontrivial deformation-theoretic boundary, not just an alternate point count.

**Closest literature and residual limitations.** The theorem assumes a split Frobenius kernel; nonsplit height-one kernels and nonlinear foliations are excluded. Only self-review of the proof and reproducibility calculations has occurred. Priority in additional subgroup-scheme/Dieudonné moduli literature warrants external assessment.
