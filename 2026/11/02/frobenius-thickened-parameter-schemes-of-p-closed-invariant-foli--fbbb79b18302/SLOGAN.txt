For a split Frobenius kernel \(A[F_A]\cong\mu_p^r\oplus\alpha_p^s\) with \(r,s>0\), the parameter scheme of \(p\)-closed translation-invariant line foliations is the disjoint union of \(\mathbf P^{r-1}(\mathbf F_p)\) and a Frobenius-thickened \(\mathbf P^{s-1}\) of multiplicity \(p^r\). Its full Hilbert polynomial is explicit; already for \(r=s=1\), two geometric foliations form a length-\(p+1\) scheme rather than two reduced points.
