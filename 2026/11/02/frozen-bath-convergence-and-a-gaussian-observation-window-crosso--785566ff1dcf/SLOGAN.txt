For a Brownian tracer reset without erasing a viscoelastic bath, the stationary joint law converges rigorously to a frozen-bath Gaussian mixture as bath friction grows. Stationary tracer time averages exhibit a sharp distributional crossover: they retain a nondegenerate Gaussian offset when the observation time is shorter than the bath timescale, have an explicit Gaussian variance at comparable times, and vanish when observation is much longer.
