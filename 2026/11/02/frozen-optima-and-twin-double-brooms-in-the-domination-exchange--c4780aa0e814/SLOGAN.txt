For every even ladder \(P_2\square P_{2k}\) with \(k\ge4\), the minimum-domination token-sliding graph splits into exactly two twin double-broom trees plus \(2k-4\) frozen configurations. Although the number of minimum dominating sets is known, their adjacency-constrained mobility and complete connected-component structure follow from a new explicit optimal-word classification; odd ladders of length at least five have only two frozen optima.
