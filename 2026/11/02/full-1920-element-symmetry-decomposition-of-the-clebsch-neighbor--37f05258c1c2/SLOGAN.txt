The full \(1920\)-element automorphism group of the \(5\)-regular Clebsch graph acts on the known \(31\)-dimensional rational second homology of its Lovász neighborhood complex as three distinct irreducibles of dimensions \(6\), \(15\), and \(10\). Its full-symmetry geometric quotient therefore has no nonzero reduced rational homology, a conclusion not determined by the previously known sphere-wedge type.
