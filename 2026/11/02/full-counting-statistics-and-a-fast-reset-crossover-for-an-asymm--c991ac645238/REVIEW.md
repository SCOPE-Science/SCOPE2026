# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The physical completed-cell observable is explicitly defined for independent Brownian exponential cycles. Direct Gaussian-resolvent integration gives three exact weighted cell probabilities and their absolutely convergent geometric sum. For each real counting field, strict monotonicity of the weighted cycle transform yields a unique root. Renewal tilting and the continuous key renewal theorem prove the scaled generating function; its differentiability, strict convexity and unbounded slope range establish the full Gartner–Ellis rate principle. The high-reset limit follows by dominated finite-field geometric convergence and the implicit function theorem. The third cumulant is obtained by direct differentiation. The executable verification independently integrates Gaussian conditional probabilities and checks the transform, root derivatives, boundary-layer convergence and third cumulant.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Roberts–Sezik–Lardet (arXiv:2405.10698, inspected full accessible text including appendices) calculate stationarity, current, and the instantaneous-limit mean but do not evaluate all-field winding statistics. The earlier accessible claim `e9c08adb-a980-44ea-8126-d111e288451f` already evaluates the variance rate in this exact model: the first two cumulants and the easy Fano consequence are explicitly excluded from novelty. Ghosh et al. (arXiv:2307.06202, inspected accessible full text) treat a related but different ratchet substrate/reset protocol. Meylahn et al. (arXiv:1510.02431) establish general renewal approaches to resetting large deviations, rather than the displayed evaluated geometrically resolved cycle transform. Pamulaparthy–Harris (arXiv:2602.23327) is a different direction-reversal memory ratchet; its full text was not accessible in this inspection, so subsumption by broad theorems is a residual publication risk. Searches of the accessible prior research records, published-finding corpus and primary literature found no exact all-field evaluated Brownian-cell formula. Failed searches do not prove absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS

The known current and diffusivity describe only two derivatives of transport statistics and cannot determine exponentially rare transport or skewness. The present evaluated root gives the full current rate function in an experimentally motivated infinitely strong return limit, and its high-reset scaled equation reveals an entire non-Poisson counting universality class when the ratchet apex is tuned on the diffusion length. The third cumulant is explicitly evaluable and tests features invisible to the previously known variance. This is a substantive observable/multiscale extension, not merely a restatement of the renewal theorem.

No finite-height potential theorem, no assertion about incomplete-excursion current LDP, and no independent publication-priority certification are made. A related 2026 paper was accessible at abstract level only.
