For two indistinguishable particles on the edges of the three-dimensional cube, the full \(48\)-symmetry rational second homology is exactly the natural signed coordinate representation \(V\), while first homology is \(\bigwedge^2V\oplus(\eta\otimes W)\oplus\det V\). The rotation quotient retains one rational circle class, but the full-symmetry quotient has no positive-degree rational homology.
