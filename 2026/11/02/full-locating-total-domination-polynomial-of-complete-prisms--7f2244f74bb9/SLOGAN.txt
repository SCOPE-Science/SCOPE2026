For complete prisms \(K_n\square K_2\), an exact closed polynomial counts **every** locating-total dominating set by size, and the smallest size falls from \(n\) to \(n-1\) precisely at \(n=5\). For \(n\ge5\), all minimum sets are one-per-column transversals with one column omitted and at least two choices from each clique, numbering \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\).
