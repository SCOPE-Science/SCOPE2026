The complete size-refined cop-win vertex-subset polynomial of the two-row ladder \(P_2\square P_n\) has the rational length generating function \(\sum_{n\ge1}C_n(z)t^n=tz(2+z+3tz^2)/((1-t)^2(1-tz-2t^2z^3))\). Beyond previously tabulated unweighted induced-tree counts, this determines the entire node-failure reliability curve and shows that a uniformly chosen cop-win induced vertex set has asymptotic density \(2/3\).
