The complete \(S_3\times C_2\) character on fourth rational homology of the triangular-prism Morse complex has layer-interchange eigenspace dimensions \(30\) and \(34\), and its full-symmetry quotient retains exactly three fourth-dimensional rational homology classes. The underlying 64-sphere homotopy type was known; the twelve-element symmetry representation and its quotient consequence are the additional result.
