For the persistence-conditioned uniform MA(1) chain at \(\theta=-1\) and every \(0<a<1\), the complete nonzero signed spectrum is determined by the explicit cotangent root equation \(\cot(t+\pi/4)=((1-a)/a)t\). Its exact centered \(L^2\) mixing factor is \(t_0/|t_{-1}|\), asymptotic to \(4a/\pi\) as \(a\downarrow0\); the stationary density is the square of its positive eigenfunction after normalization.
