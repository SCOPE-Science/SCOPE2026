An equivalence relation on ordered rational atoms supported by \(k\) named points is determined by choosing discrete versus collapsed gaps and partitioning the resulting blocks; its exact count is \(\sum_{j=0}^{k+1}\binom{k+1}{j}B_{k+j}\). The proportion of these relations whose least support contains every named point approaches one, with failure probability \(O((\log k)^2/k)\).
