On the two-clique barbell with mean-\(n\) intra-clique passage times and one fixed-rate exponential bridge, a nongate source floods in \(3\log n\) time with a limit combining three independent Gumbels, a logistic variable and the bridge delay. Starting at either gate instead takes \(2\log n\) to leading order, making source placement change the leading flooding constant by one-third.
