# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed normal form is derived from three intrinsic structures: the Boolean idempotent lattice, the existence of compatible rank-two joins, and the rank-one Brandt groupoid. Compatibility forces the induced permutation of block atoms to be increasing or decreasing. The rank-one composition law forces a unique shared group automorphism and uniquely normalized gauges; all higher-rank elements are recovered as joins of rank-one restrictions. The converse formula is verified directly. The numerical orders use the classical theorem for automorphisms of symmetric groups [3]. The embedded verifier checks every multiplication pair for all explicitly constructed automorphisms in three finite cases. The proof, not the finite checks, supplies the universal statement.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence

Caneco–Fernandes–Quinteiro [1], inspected in its 2019 full preprint, defines the exact same monoid and supplies rank and presentation results, without computing its abstract automorphism group. Araújo–Fernandes–Jesus–Maltcev–Mitchell [2], inspected in the full 2011 article, handles partial endomorphism automorphisms for equivalence and linear-order relations, but its spatiality theorem depends on point-valued constants absent from this monoid for nontrivial blocks. The present classification can be nonspatial for block size six, so its exceptional case is not implied by the cited spatiality theorems. The classical existence of outer automorphisms of the six-point symmetric group is credited to [3]. Related research-record matches concern different structures or invariants. Exact implicit coverage from a general Brandt/rook semigroup theorem remains a genuine risk, not eliminated merely by failed search.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The invariant is the full abstract automorphism group of a natural infinite two-parameter family generating normally ordered finite inverse semigroups. The normal form is unique, includes the small-block exception, and gives explicit orders for all parameters. It answers a structural rigidity question rather than an arbitrary census: all symmetries are spatial at every block size except six, where a well-known exceptional group symmetry propagates into a genuinely non-spatial monoid phenomenon. It provides a comparison point for automorphism questions involving colored/labelled inverse semigroups and restricted wreath products.

## Scientific limitations and priority risk

This is a same mathematical model proof review, not an independent audit or certification of publication priority. Group-labelled rook or Brandt-type automorphism theorems could subsume the normal form; no such decisive theorem was located in the sources actually inspected. The claim concerns ordered, equal-sized blocks only and does not extend automatically to nonuniform partitions.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
