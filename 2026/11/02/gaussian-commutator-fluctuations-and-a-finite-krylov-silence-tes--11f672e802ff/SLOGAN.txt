For paired without-replacement implicit splitting of symmetric positive-semidefinite matrices, centered random-order fluctuations have a sharp Gaussian scale \(h^{3/2}\) with an explicit commutator covariance. That covariance vanishes for a given initial vector exactly when \(CH^kx_0=0\) for \(0\leq k<d\), a finite Krylov-space test that can succeed even for noncommuting operators.
