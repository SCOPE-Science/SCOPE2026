For the unregularized two-point Anderson extrapolator of a scalar affine contraction, every imperfectly correlated equal-variance Gaussian oracle produces a sharp \(t^{-1}\) error tail and an infinite mean absolute error, except for a perfectly antithetic, fixed-point-centered cancellation. Perfect noise reuse instead gives a Gaussian error, and for centered trials the worst heavy-tail coefficient occurs at the unique intermediate correlation \(\rho_*=(1-b)/(1+b)\), where \(b=(1-a)^2(x_1-x_0)^2/(4\sigma^2)\).
