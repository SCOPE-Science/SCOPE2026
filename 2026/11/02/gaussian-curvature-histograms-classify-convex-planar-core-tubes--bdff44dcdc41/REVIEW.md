# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The immersion Jacobian and Gaussian-curvature formula are independently derived from the tube parametrization and coincide with the inspected literature (Saad et al., equation (3.6)). Its strict fractional monotonicity gives the extreme-curvature endpoint identities without hidden exceptional configurations. The exact area-unweighting formula yields all positive core-curvature moments. Hausdorff moment uniqueness on a compact positive interval and the turning theorem finish reconstruction. The harmonic support curves have strictly positive curvature radius and unequal symmetry orders; their weighted tangent-angle distributions coincide by a periodic change of variables. A deterministic numerical script passes supplementary checks.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, provisional).** The 2024 full article by Saad et al. and the 2011 full HTML article by Tunçer et al. establish local curvatures and Weingarten properties, not equivalence of normalized area curvature laws. Related indexed geometric work concerns curvature-function optimization, intrinsic invariants, or different inverse projection observables. The exact inverse measure equivalence and explicit noncongruent support-function family are not implied by those results. Risk remains that a previously published transformation or curvature histogram identity has the same content under other terminology.

**Value — PASS.** Curvature is a natural intrinsic scalar measured on a tube, whereas the generating core is an extrinsic geometric object. This is a complete identifiability/nonidentifiability result for an infinite-dimensional family: it identifies which physical and geometric parameters are recoverable and constructs distinct geometries that cannot be distinguished by any area-unlabeled curvature measurement. It is not merely a new notation for the local curvature formula.

**Scientific limits.** Smooth, convex, positive-curvature planar cores and constant tube radius only. Exact probability law, not finite-sample inference. The historical-priority comparison is not an independent audit.
