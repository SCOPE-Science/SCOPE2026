For fixed product length, independent uniform partial bijections have \(n^{1/4}\)-scale independent Gaussian rank deficiencies, but their overlapping missing points contribute asymptotically independent Poisson corrections with successive means \(1,2,\ldots,m-1\). An exact hypergeometric transition describes every finite product rank and yields a total Poisson correction of mean \(\binom m2\), independent of the Gaussian fluctuation.
