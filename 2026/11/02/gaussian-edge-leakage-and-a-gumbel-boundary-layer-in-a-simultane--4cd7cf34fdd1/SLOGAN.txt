The finite-population maximum of a harmonically confined Brownian gas with simultaneous Poisson resets has an exact beta-weighted Gumbel smoothing law at the apparent compact edge, with exceedance probability of order \((\log N)^{-r/(2\mu)}\). The probability of crossing the uncorrected edge \(\sqrt{2D\log N/\mu}\) instead has the sharp extra factor \((\log N)^{-1/2}\), and rare edge events select reset ages of order \(\log\log N\).
