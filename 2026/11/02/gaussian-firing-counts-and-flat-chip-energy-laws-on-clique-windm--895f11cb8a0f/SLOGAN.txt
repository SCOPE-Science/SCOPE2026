On every clique windmill \(K_1\vee(nK_m)\), primitive parallel chip-firing two-cycles have an exact firing-population polynomial, giving \(6n5^{n-1}\) distinct orbits on friendship graphs. At fixed clique width, a uniformly selected orbit has Gaussian firing-count fluctuations but a nondegenerate uniform macroscopic chip-energy law; the two limiting statistics are independent.
