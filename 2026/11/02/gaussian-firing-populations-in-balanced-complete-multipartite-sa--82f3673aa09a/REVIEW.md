# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A primitive two-cycle on a connected graph requires each vertex to fire exactly once in two steps by the Laplacian kernel. Fixing firing shore counts k_i, the permissible initial chips on each vertex have lengths d-s_i if firing and s_i if waiting, giving the stated finite count divided by two. The entropy normalized by d^(rn) equals minus a sum of binary KL divergences; its unique zero for r>=3 is x_i=1/2. The linear map M=((r-2)I+J)/(r-1) has eigenvalues 2 and (r-2)/(r-1), giving Hessian H=4M^2 and the stated covariance H^-1 and 1/(4((r-2)/(r-1))^(r-1)) orbit prefactor. Global Pinsker control prevents competing boundary saddles. Independent direct simulation of all 64, 1296 and 262144 confined chip states gave 6, 60 and 3400 orbits for (r,n)=(3,1),(4,1),(3,2), matching the independent formula; these finite tests do not prove the limit.
