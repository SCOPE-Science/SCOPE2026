# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The update law implies the Laplacian equal-firing count; a genuine temporal two-cycle forces each vertex to fire exactly once. The independently derived integer chip intervals have \(d_v-t_v\) or \(t_v\) choices and produce the exact summation. Relative-entropy nonpositivity and the linear equations for its equality cases show a unique saddle for fixed \(r\ge3\). The Hessian eigenvalues are \(16\) and \(4((r-2)/(r-1))^2\); the Gaussian integral yields the orbit constant and the inverse-Hessian covariance. Global Pinsker control excludes missed boundary saddles. A verifier independently simulated \(296{,}272\) small confined labelled configurations and compared counts. The analytic limit is proved by a uniform local Stirling / Gaussian Riemann-sum argument, not by finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS, with scope limits

The alternating two-cycle criterion itself is already implied by Jiang (2010) and stated explicitly in Scully–Zhang–Jiang (2011, Lemma 4.1); the finite sum is a specialization, and no priority is claimed for that general local criterion. The primary inspected source bodies develop period possibilities, local two-period conditions, and a later complete-bipartite density theorem. None supplied the fixed-\(r\ge3\) Gaussian firing-population law or its exact orbit-growth constant. Search comparisons included published-finding corpus semantic queries on complete multipartite two-cycle enumeration / Gaussian fluctuations and literature searches of chip-firing, period two and multipartite central limits. The earlier two-shore arcsine phenomenon does not logically imply the positive-definite \(r\ge3\) Hessian. Residual risk: an unindexed enumerative random-mapping treatment could imply the same constant or limiting distribution.

## Value — PASS

A natural invariant for fixed-energy synchronous sandpiles is the distribution of numbers of firing vertices among graph modules, under a uniform recurrent-orbit measure. The limit distinguishes the bipartite entropy ridge from the unique, nondegenerate saddle produced by a third or further shore. The explicit negative correlations and the constant-order normalized orbit growth are informative for all fixed shore counts, not a single arbitrary finite graph; the change is not a mere parameter substitution in the bipartite limit.
