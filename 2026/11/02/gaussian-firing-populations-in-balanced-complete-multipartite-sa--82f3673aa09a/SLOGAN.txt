Primitive two-cycles of synchronous chip firing on the balanced complete \(r\)-partite graph have an exact firing-set census, and for every fixed \(r\ge3\) their uniformly sampled shore firing counts obey a joint Gaussian law with explicit negative cross-shore covariance. Their orbit count is asymptotic to \(\frac14((r-1)/(r-2))^{r-1}((r-1)n)^{rn}\), with a singular entropy transition at two shores.
