For balanced Bernoulli–Laplace urns exchanging nearly half of each urn per step, every fixed time \(t\ge2\) has a sharp Gaussian worst-start total-variation profile governed by \(\sqrt n\lvert1-2k/n\rvert^t\), with the exchange-size transition window \(\lvert k-n/2\rvert\asymp n^{1-1/(2t)}\). Exactly half-urn exchange mixes in precisely two steps asymptotically, improving the previously established three-step bound.
