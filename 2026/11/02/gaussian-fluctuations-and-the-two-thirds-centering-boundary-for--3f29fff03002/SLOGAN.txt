For critical harmonic-weighted quorum collection, exact-mean-centered hitting times have Gaussian fluctuations on the \(H_NN^{\theta/2}\) scale with limiting variance \(c_\theta/2\), where \(c_\theta=e^{(\theta-2)\gamma}\). Replacing the exact center with the known first-order approximation reveals a sharp \(\theta=2/3\) bias transition: below it the limit is centered, at it the limit shifts by \(c_\theta^2\), and above it the normalized hitting time diverges to the right.
