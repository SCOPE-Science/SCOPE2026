A one-uniform-point-per-cell rule for \(\int_0^1t^{-\alpha}dt\) has a non-Gaussian power-tail limit, a critical logarithmic Gaussian limit, or an ordinary \(n^{-3/2}\) Gaussian limit depending exactly on the grading product \(p(1-\alpha)\). The unique rate-optimal power exponent is \(p=2/(1-\alpha)\); at \(\alpha=1/3\), \(p=3\), the variance is exactly \(3/(4n^3)\) at every sample size.
