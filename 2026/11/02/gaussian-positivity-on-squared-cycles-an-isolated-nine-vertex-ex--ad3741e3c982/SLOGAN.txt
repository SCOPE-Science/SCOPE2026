For the shortest-path metric of the squared cycle \(C_n^2\), every Gaussian \(e^{-t d}\) is positive definite for \(t>0\) exactly when \(n\) is even or \(n\in\{3,5,9\}\). An explicit odd-order Fourier spectrum reveals the isolated nine-vertex exception and the precise harmonic obstruction in every other odd order.
