In every finite stable linear system driven by independent Poisson-reset Brownian baths, each stationary linear readout is Gaussian exactly when it is blind to all resetting inputs; otherwise its fourth cumulant is strictly positive and every even moment beyond variance exceeds that of its covariance-matched Ornstein--Uhlenbeck surrogate. This gives a rigorous all-site, all-parameter Gaussianity obstruction for harmonic chains with resetting boundary walls, even with extra thermal noise.
