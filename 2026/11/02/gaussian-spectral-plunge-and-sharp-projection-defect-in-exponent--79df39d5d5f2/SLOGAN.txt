Radial concentration in rapidly vanishing exponential Bergman balls has a universal Gaussian eigenvalue plunge at degree \(L_\varepsilon\) on the sharp scale \(Q_\varepsilon\). Across every complex dimension, the complete projection defect has the exact leading constant \(1/\sqrt\pi\) times the homogeneous-degree density and plunge width, giving a sharp fractional-power spectral boundary law.
