A free planar swimmer with angular diffusivity \(\kappa\beta t^{\beta-1}\) has a sharp threshold at \(\beta=2\): below it the appropriately rescaled position is asymptotically isotropic Gaussian, at it spreading is logarithmic, and above it the position converges almost surely to a finite random endpoint. In the localized regime the shrinking endpoint error is itself asymptotically isotropic Gaussian after multiplication by \(t^{\beta/2-1}\), with exact variance coefficient \(v^2/[\kappa\beta(\beta-2)]\) per coordinate.
