For a Brownian particle in a harmonic trap with proportional Poisson resets, the exact stationary position law has a Gaussian far tail multiplied by a sharp \(|x|^{-r/\kappa}\) prefactor, with an explicit infinite-product amplitude. Its critical quadratic exponential moment exists exactly when the reset rate exceeds the relaxation rate; the commonly used weak-reset diffusion approximation instead has spurious algebraic tails for every fixed nonzero jump size.
