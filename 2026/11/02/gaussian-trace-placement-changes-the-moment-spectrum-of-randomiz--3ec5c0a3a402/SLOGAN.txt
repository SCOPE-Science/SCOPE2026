In generalized cross-validation, directly Gaussian-estimating \(\operatorname{tr}(I-H)\) gives a score with finite \(q\)-th moment exactly for \(q<mn/4\), whereas estimating \(\operatorname{tr}(H)\) and subtracting from \(n\) creates a denominator pole and finite moments only for \(q<1/2\), no matter how many probes are averaged. Fixed-norm isotropic probes make the two estimators coincide samplewise and eliminate this pole for strictly contractive smoothers.
