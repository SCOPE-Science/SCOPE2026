# Review: Gcd resonance of motivic local densities

## Correctness — PASS
On each monomial branch \(z\mapsto(z^a,z^A)\), the canonical measure pulls back with order \((a-1)\operatorname{ord}z\). The exact normalized ball volume is \(c_a\mathbb L^{m-a\lceil m/a\rceil}\). The two-dimensional canonical Jacobian minors separate into blocks, so the *fixed-radius* product volume factors. CRT-conditioning on residues modulo \(\gcd(a,b)\) then proves the displayed rational formula. The arithmetic proof covers all admissible integers; 676 exact finite checks support, but do not substitute for, it. The product's singularities are not isolated; only the specified embedded density is claimed.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Forey, arXiv:1512.00420, already gives the single cusp's density \(1/2\), which is not new. Ratajczak, arXiv:2610.32416v1, provides the general definitions and a resolution formula for isolated singularities, but no two-factor gcd law was found in inspected sections. Targeted mathematical-record and literature searches likewise found no equivalent Cartesian product formula. The main potential overlap risk is an implicit consequence of older motivic integration or rational Poincaré-series treatments, not a verified identical theorem.

## Value — PASS
A natural structural expectation, that independent factors give multiplicative local density, fails due to synchronized periodic averaging even while their measures factor at every fixed radius. The theorem both quantifies that failure and determines the exact coprimality boundary for an infinite family of two-dimensional singular product germs. Its motivic correction is invisible upon simply taking the rational-expression limit \(\mathbb L\to1\), making it more than a restatement of one-factor branch multiplicities.

## Limits and verification
The result assumes characteristic zero, the indicated monomial coordinates and embedding, and coprime exponent pairs for each irreducible branch. It makes no invariance claim for nonisolated singularities or generic arbitrary plane germs. An exact checker evaluated 676 combinations of factor multiplicities and motivic specializations. Independent mathematical assessment and exhaustive historical priority remain pending.
