For Cartesian products of irreducible monomial plane branches with multiplicities \(a,b\ge2\), the embedded motivic local density is \(\frac{d}{ab}\frac{\mathbb L-1}{\mathbb L+1}\frac{\mathbb L^d+1}{\mathbb L^d-1}\), where \(d=\gcd(a,b)\). Thus fixed-radius Fubini factorization does not survive periodic averaging unless the multiplicities are coprime; two cusps already give a nonzero motivic defect.
