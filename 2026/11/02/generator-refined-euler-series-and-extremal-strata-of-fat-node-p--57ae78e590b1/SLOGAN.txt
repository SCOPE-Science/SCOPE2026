For the nonreduced node \(x^uy^v=0\), the compactly supported Euler series of punctual Hilbert schemes refined by the minimal number of quotient-ring ideal generators admits an explicit \(q\)-Pochhammer formula for every \(u,v\ge1\). The maximal possible generator number is \(u+v\), and its stratum has series \(q^{\binom{u+v}{2}}/((q;q)_u(q;q)_v)\).
