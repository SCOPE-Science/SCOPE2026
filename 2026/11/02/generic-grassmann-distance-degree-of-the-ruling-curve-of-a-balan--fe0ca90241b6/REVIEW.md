# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the rational projection distance, bounds and generically attains the Wronskian degree, proves the weighted-two-square numerator map dominant by an explicit tangent-space calculation, and verifies the cut-condition avoidance on an irreducible nonempty open. Exact finite-field tests independently check examples but are not treated as a uniform proof.

## Originality
PASS with residual literature risk. The motivating primary text supplies the already-known ED count, but neither generic projector-data dominance nor exclusion of critical points from the cut locus. Comparisons include exact claims and metric definitions rather than title similarity. An unlocated general equality theorem could subsume this result; this is an acknowledged priority uncertainty.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This resolves the projection-distance enumerative invariant for every balanced rational normal scroll ruling curve, uniformly in the ruling rank. It explains why the constrained Grassmann data do not reduce the complex critical count and determines the precise cut-locus behavior.
