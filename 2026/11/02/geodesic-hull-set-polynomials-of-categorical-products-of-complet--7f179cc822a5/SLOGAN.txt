For \(m,n\ge3\), every subset of \(K_m\times K_n\) containing two vertices in one row or column has full geodesic hull, except for a sharp \(3\)-by-\(3\) obstruction: six maximal proper convex sets are complements of permutation matrices. This gives the full hull-set polynomial \(H_{m,n}(z)=(1+z)^{mn}-\sum_{k=0}^{\min\{m,n\}}\binom mk\binom nk k!z^k\) outside the exception and \(H_{3,3}(z)=6z^3+45z^4+90z^5+78z^6+36z^7+9z^8+z^9\).
