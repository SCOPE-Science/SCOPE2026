The full ordinary geodetic and inclusion-minimal geodetic cardinality polynomials of every crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus a perfect matching, \(n\ge3\), admit closed formulas, and every minimal geodetic set is either one deleted-matching pair, an entire shore, or a pair of disjoint three-index shores. In particular the minimal spectrum is \(\{2,6,n\}\) for \(n\ge7\), giving arbitrarily long missing-cardinality intervals on connected vertex-transitive bipartite graphs.
