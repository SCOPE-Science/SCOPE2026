For every prime \(p\ge5\), the ordinary curves \(y^p-y=ax+b/x+c/(x-1)\) with \(a,b,c\in\mathbb F_p^\times\) have an exact geometric isomorphism-class spectrum by full automorphism group: the generic \(C_p\) classes number \(\frac{(p-2)(p-3)}6\) or \(\frac{(p-1)(p-4)}6\) according to \(p\bmod3\), alongside \(p-2\) dihedral classes, at most one faithful order-\(3p\) class, and one sign-action order-\(6p\) class. The classification follows from a three-branch-point \(S_3\)-orbit calculation and the uniqueness of the Artin--Schreier quotient.
