For independent sign flips along a finite path with a fair starting spin, geometrically varying positive flip odds make the magnetization laws at \(2j-1\) and \(2j\) changes identical, while successive pairs have strictly decreasing absolute magnetization in likelihood-ratio order. Requiring this local parity pairing on every four-spin window characterizes geometric odds exactly, and implies strictly negative covariance between change count and every strictly increasing function of absolute magnetization.
