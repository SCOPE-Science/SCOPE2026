For a four-direction active particle with unequal reversible reorientation rates in a rotated anisotropic harmonic trap, the stationary total entropy production changes its optimal trap alignment exactly when \(\gamma_1\gamma_2=k_1k_2\). At this nondegenerate geometric-mean threshold, dissipation is independent of the trap angle and equals \(v^2/(2D)\), even though both directional mechanisms remain anisotropic.
