At the exact flip resonance \(x_{n+1}=-x_n+G(x_{\lfloor n/m\rfloor})\) with even \(m\), every trajectory is a digit-coded nonlinear orbit and its empirical measures converge in total variation to a geometric mixture of iterates. For linear feedback, boundedness and the sharp moment threshold \(|\delta|^p=2\) (with an exceptional constant orbit at \(\delta=2\)) are explicit, even though rare iterates can grow without bound.
