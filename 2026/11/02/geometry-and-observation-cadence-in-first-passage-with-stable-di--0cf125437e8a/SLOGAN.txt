Independent positive-stable diffusivity bursts give Brownian wedge survival \(\Pr(T_c>t)\sim Kt^{-\pi/(2\alpha\theta)}\), with an explicit sharp amplitude and a geometry-dependent mean threshold. At stable index \(1/2\), half-plane and quadrant survivals have exact arctangent laws, but inspecting the same freely evolving process only at refresh instants restores an infinite mean detection time even when continuous absorption has finite mean.
