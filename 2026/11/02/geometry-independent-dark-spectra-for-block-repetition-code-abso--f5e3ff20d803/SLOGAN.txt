On the binary hypercube, absorbing every word of a disjoint product of \(m\) repetition codes produces an energy-resolved bright-space polynomial \((1+z)^{m-1}\sum_{j=0}^{N-m+1}z^j\), independent of the repetition-block lengths. Its exact eventual capture fraction for a maximally mixed one-particle state is \((N-m+2)/2^{N-m+1}\), even for unequal strictly positive absorber strengths.
