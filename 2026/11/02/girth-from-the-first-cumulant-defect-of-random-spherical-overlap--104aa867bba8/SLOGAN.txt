For independent random spherical spins on a finite graph with positive edge weights, the first departure of the total edge-overlap cumulants from independent-edge behavior occurs exactly at the graph's girth, with a coefficient equal to the weighted count of shortest cycles divided by a power of the sphere dimension. For Haar-random pure qubits, one aggregate pair-fidelity distribution therefore reveals the number of edges, the girth, and the number of shortest cycles; its first five cumulants also recover triangle, quadrilateral, and pentagonal cycle counts.
