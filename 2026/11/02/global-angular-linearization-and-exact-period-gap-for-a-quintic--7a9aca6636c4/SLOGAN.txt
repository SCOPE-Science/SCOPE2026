For the quintic Llibre–Salhi flow with \(a,b>1\), a global annular radial coordinate makes the full angular Poincaré return map exactly \(\rho\mapsto e^{-2\pi\lambda/(ab)}\rho\), classifying every interior orbit as attracted to the circle, attracted to the ellipse, or periodic according to the sign of \(\lambda\). The original-time periods of the boundary circle and ellipse have the exact universal gap \(T_C-T_{\mathcal E}=\pi/(ab)\).
