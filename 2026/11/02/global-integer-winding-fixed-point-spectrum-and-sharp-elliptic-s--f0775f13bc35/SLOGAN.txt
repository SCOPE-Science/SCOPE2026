For the biquadratic nontwist map on the full cylinder, every positive integer angular winding supports exactly four fixed points, and the count inside \(|y|\le Y\) grows as \(4a\epsilon Y^4-4a(1+\epsilon)Y^2+O(1)\). Exactly two fixed points per winding can be linearly elliptic, with a sharp finite staircase whose number of elliptic winding indices has leading growth \(b^{-4/3}\) and an explicit second term as \(b\downarrow0\).
