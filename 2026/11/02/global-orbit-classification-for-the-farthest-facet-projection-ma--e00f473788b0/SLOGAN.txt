On every regular simplex of dimension \(d\ge3\), the farthest-facet orthogonal-projection map with tied maxima fixed has exactly \(d!\) attracting periodic cycles, each of least period \(d+1\), and its only other recurrent points are fixed ties. The cycles have exact rank-linear barycentric coordinates, their basins have equal surface-area weights, and all invariant probabilities are mixtures of fixed-point masses and the uniform laws on these cycles.
