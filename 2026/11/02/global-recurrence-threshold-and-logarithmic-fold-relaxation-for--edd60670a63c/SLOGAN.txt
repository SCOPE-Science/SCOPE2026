For every real generalized Hénon map in dimension \(m\ge3\) with \(0<b<1\), the fixed-point fold \(a_*=-(1-b)^2/4\) is exactly the global threshold for invariant probability measures: below it all trajectories escape, while at equality every bounded orbit converges to the unique equilibrium. Every bounded critical orbit outside the strong-stable set has the sharp coordinate expansion \(c+(m-1+b)/n-((m-1)^2-b)(\log n)/n^2+O(n^{-2})\), including an explicit dimension-dependent logarithmic correction.
