For uncorrected epsilon-outside RMSProp, the exact learning-rate boundary \(h=2\varepsilon/L\) is globally attracting on every strongly convex, globally \(L\)-smooth objective, completing the previously strict stability frontier. On the scalar quadratic, every nonterminating critical orbit obeys the memory-independent laws \(k|x_k|\to\varepsilon/(2a)\) and \(k^2v_k\to\varepsilon^2/4\), while exceptional orbits hit the minimizer in finite time.
