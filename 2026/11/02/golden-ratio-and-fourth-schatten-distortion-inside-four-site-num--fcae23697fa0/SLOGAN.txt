Among four-site nilpotent weighted shifts with the same entire Haar numerical-shadow distribution, the greatest possible operator-norm ratio is exactly the golden ratio \((1+\sqrt5)/2\), while the greatest Schatten-four norm ratio is exactly \((1+\sqrt2)^{1/4}\). Both are attained, but at different numerical-shadow fibers, and all intermediate within-fiber norm values have explicit sharp bounds.
