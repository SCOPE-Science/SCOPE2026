# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The intensity expansion of the two-exponential sector gives a rank-one Hermitian coefficient matrix. Three line traces extract the real two-dimensional interference vector through a rank-two matrix whose Gram trace equals three and determinant equals twice the squared sine of the angular mismatch. The resulting exact smaller singular value is verified by the quadratic formula; the four raw intensities invert the four Hermitian coordinates whenever the sine is nonzero, and the rank-one lifting argument proves that three arbitrary phaseless point measurements cannot suffice for all complex two-vectors. The running-minimum identity transfers the exact small-angle expansion to a Diophantine liminf; the classical sharp Hurwitz theorem and golden-ratio Fibonacci convergents prove the optimal constant. The concrete witness pair has two identical line intensities and sharply separated phase orbits. The standalone script passed 8144 supplementary finite assertions but is not a proof of any infinite limit.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk

Liehr, *Arithmetic progressions and holomorphic phase retrieval* (arXiv:2308.05722, first posted 2023-08-10), already proves the qualitative uniqueness criterion and explicitly uses two-exponential ambiguity witnesses; neither is claimed as new. The inspected paper does not provide the sectorwise interference Gram spectrum, four-site raw-intensity inverse for all resonant sectors, or the sharp global optimization of the normalized worst-frequency sensitivity via Hurwitz's constant. Wang–Xu (arXiv:1605.08034) gives general complex finite-dimensional phase-retrieval measurement theory, which supplies context but not the three-line arithmetic optimization. Grohs–Liehr (arXiv:2109.02494) studies stable Gabor retrieval on a different shift-invariant signal model. Related mathematical-record summaries concerned unrestricted three Hermitian quadratic measurements or finite cyclic Fourier frames, not this three-parallel-line entire-function family. Their full texts were not available for exact theorem-by-theorem comparison; the residual possibility of an equivalent published conditioning result remains. Known continued-fraction facts are explicitly credited.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Liehr's qualitative irrational-versus-rational geometry does not distinguish irrational sampling designs with wildly different conditioning. The exact frequency spectrum, sharp asymptotic optimum over the free line position, and strongly phase-separated near-collisions expose a natural quantitative stability obstruction. The finite four-sample inverse connects derived interference coefficients to physically measurable intensities and is accompanied by a sharp minimality argument. These are structural and motivated quantitative statements, not arbitrary parameter slices or a recomputation of a known tabulated constant.

## Scientific limits

Only the specified resonant two-exponential entire-function sectors and equal-weight extracted interference coefficients are analyzed. Frequency-dependent raw sampling sites are not a universal full-entire-function stability scheme. The golden-ratio sharpness uses established continued fractions, and historical priority for a differently formulated spectral-design result has not been independently established.
