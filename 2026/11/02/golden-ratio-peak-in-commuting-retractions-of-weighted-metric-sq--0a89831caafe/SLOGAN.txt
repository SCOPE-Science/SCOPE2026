For a four-vertex weighted Hamming square with edge lengths \(a\le b\), the optimal commuting prefix-retraction constant is exactly \(\min\{b/a,1+a/b\}\). Its largest value over all aspect ratios is the golden ratio, attained uniquely when \(b/a=(1+\sqrt5)/2\); every unequal pair of edge weights obstructs nonexpansiveness.
