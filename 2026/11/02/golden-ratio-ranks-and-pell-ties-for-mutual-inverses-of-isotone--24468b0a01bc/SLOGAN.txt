For the order-preserving transformation monoid of a finite chain, the number of ordered mutual-inverse pairs of rank \(r\) is exactly \(\binom{n+r-1}{2r-1}^2\). The most frequent rank is near \(n/\sqrt5\), with Gaussian fluctuations, and tied most-frequent ranks occur for exactly the Pell-family chain sizes beginning at \(n=72\).
