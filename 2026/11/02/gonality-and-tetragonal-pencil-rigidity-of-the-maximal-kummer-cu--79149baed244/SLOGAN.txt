The curves \(y^{q+1}=x^2(x^2+1)\) have exact geometric gonality \(2,3,4\) at \(q=3,5,\text{ and }q\ge7\), respectively. For all \(q\ge13\), their degree-four pencil given by \(y\) is unique up to target automorphisms, whereas \(q=7\) has distinct degree-four pencils given by \(y\) and \(x+y^{(q+1)/2}/x\).
