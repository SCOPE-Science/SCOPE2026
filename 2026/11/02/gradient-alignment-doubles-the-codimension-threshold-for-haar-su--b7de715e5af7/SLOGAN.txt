For a rank-one-singular quadratic, a Haar-random subspace makes the reduced Hessian invertible almost surely but can produce steps with infinite mean-square length. Kernel-orthogonal gradients have a sharp moment threshold at codimension \(d-k>2\), while any nonzero kernel-aligned component raises it to \(d-k>4\); this sharp discontinuity diagnoses the impact of inconsistent local gradients without implying failure of safeguarded methods.
