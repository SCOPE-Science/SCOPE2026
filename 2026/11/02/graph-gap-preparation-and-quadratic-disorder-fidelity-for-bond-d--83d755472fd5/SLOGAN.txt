For coherence-pumping Lindblad bonds on a connected quantum-battery graph, imperfect onsite degree compensation forces an explicit \(\Theta(\varepsilon^2)\) stationary loss of dark-state fidelity, bounded above and below using the graph gap and Hamiltonian norm. Exact compensation restores a unique maximally charged pure state with a sharp graph-gap fidelity-convergence coefficient, while incompatible bond phase cycles prevent pure stationarity.
