For the ruling-plane curve of every rational normal scroll \(S(n_1,\ldots,n_d)\) with \(d\ge2\) and \(n_i\ge2\), the Grassmann distance degree for generic rank-\(d\) projector data equals its Euclidean distance degree: \(2\deg\operatorname{lcm}_i(1+t^2+\cdots+t^{2n_i})-2\). This extends the known balanced case to arbitrary multidegrees and yields exact generic critical counts also in Grassmann ranks at least three.
