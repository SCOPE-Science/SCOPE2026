For \(n\) arclength-uniform points on a hyperbolic circle of radius \(R_n\), the random geodesic-polygon perimeter divided by the circle circumference has a strictly decreasing universal crossover controlled by \(\sinh R_n/n\), with sharp regimes \(1\), a nontrivial fraction, and \(0\). At the critical scale it has an explicit nondegenerate Gaussian fluctuation law and, for fixed radius, an exact second-order mean-deficit coefficient.
