For every fixed block size \(r\ge3\), the Markovian walk on \(r\)-subsets observes all pairs in Gumbel time \(\binom n2(\log\binom n2+G)/(r-1)\), to first-order and fluctuation precision. A finite-state avoidance expansion proves the conjectured pair-super-coupon expectation and shows that overlap among any fixed number of missing pairs does not alter their leading joint avoidance exponent.
