When the observed block in a single-swap Johnson walk grows without bound but satisfies \(r_n\log n=o(n)\), the complete collection time of fixed-order subset shadows still has a standard Gumbel limit on the exact scale \(\binom ns/\binom{r_n-1}{s-1}\), and the number of missing subsets has a Poisson limit. The mean cover time is asymptotic to \(n^s\log n/r_n^{s-1}\), extending the fixed-block picture into a growing-block regime.
