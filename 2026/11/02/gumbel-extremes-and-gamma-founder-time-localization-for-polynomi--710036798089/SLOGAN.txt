Critical geometric immigrant clans with \(\lfloor c k^\alpha\rfloor\) founders per generation, \(\alpha>0\), have a Gumbel largest-family law at scale \(n\) around \(n\lambda_n\), where \(\lambda_n^{\alpha+1}e^{\lambda_n}=c\Gamma(\alpha+1)n^\alpha\). The winning founder time is independent of the Gumbel fluctuation and has a Gamma law after rescaling its immigration time by \(\lambda_n/n\); its share of the population vanishes at the sharp rate \((\log n)/n^\alpha\).
