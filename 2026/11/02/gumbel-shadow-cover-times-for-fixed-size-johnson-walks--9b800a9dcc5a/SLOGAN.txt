For fixed \(1\le s<r\), the Johnson walk that refreshes one element of an \(r\)-set covers all \(s\)-subsets at asymptotic mean time \(\binom ns\log\binom ns/\binom{r-1}{s-1}\), and its centered time has a standard Gumbel limit. Uniform killed-chain perturbation for arbitrary fixed collections of overlapping targets proves a Poisson law for the remaining unseen subsets and resolves the fixed-parameter Markovian super-coupon cover-time conjecture in the strict regime.
