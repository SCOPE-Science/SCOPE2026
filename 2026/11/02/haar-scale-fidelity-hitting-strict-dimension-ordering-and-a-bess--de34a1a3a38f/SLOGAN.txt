For isotropic projective Brownian search, first hitting a target whose fidelity threshold is \(z/n\) has a **strictly dimension-ordered Laplace transform**, a positive explicit finite-size delay, and a universal Kummer-function limit as the Hilbert-space dimension grows. At the natural Haar scale its mean-normalized clock crosses from a Bessel-type exit law to an exponential rare-event law, with squared coefficient of variation changing from \(1/2\) to \(1\) in the two endpoint limits.
