# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The coordinate-vector test forces \(s=\sqrt n\) in every nontrivial dimension. The two exceptional positive cases are established by preservation of the full unit-ball vertex sets, not by testing a few vectors. The higher-order exclusion uses orthogonality of three arbitrary Hadamard columns to force four equally populated sign classes; the resulting gauge ratio is strictly larger than one. The source-level finite checker confirms, but does not prove, the universal combinatorial step. The hypothesis is existence of a *real* Hadamard matrix; no unproved existence assertion is needed.

## Originality
**PASS on the inspected primary and indexed comparisons, with residual bibliographic risk.** The most directly inspected 2022 paper addresses Hadamard Schur multiplier norms on Hilbert space, not linear preservation of the cube–crosspolytope intersection norm. Other published calculations concerning quadratic mixtures, endpoint cube/crosspolytope geometry, and the octagon do not entail this simultaneous all-order classification. The 2013 matrix-coding paper discusses linear images of Lee balls and cubes, but its complete text could not be inspected, so a hidden equivalence is not excluded. Familiar 24-cell symmetry is an acknowledged constituent, not asserted new by itself.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The result is a natural complete classification of orthogonal sign-matrix symmetries for a single widely used interpolation family of finite-dimensional Banach norms; it identifies exactly two exceptional geometries and a dimension-uniform obstruction, with no arbitrary parameter slicing. It distinguishes finite-order Euclidean orthogonality from preservation of a non-Euclidean absolute symmetric norm, with the first genuinely obstructing three-coordinate configuration.

## Residual risks
The full 2013 coding article was not accessible in this review, and unindexed classical isometry classifications may imply the result. The stated contribution is the full rigidity classification and quantitative obstruction rather than discovery of the regular octagon or 24-cell. The proof does not establish the actual norm of a higher-dimensional transform.
