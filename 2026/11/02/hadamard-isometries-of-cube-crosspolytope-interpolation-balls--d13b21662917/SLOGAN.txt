A normalized real Hadamard matrix preserves the cube–crosspolytope interpolation norm \(\max\{\|x\|_\infty,\|x\|_1/s\}\) in dimensions at least two only for the regular octagon \((n,s)=(2,\sqrt2)\) and the regular 24-cell \((n,s)=(4,2)\). At every higher Hadamard order a three-coordinate vector proves a quantitative failure of isometry.
