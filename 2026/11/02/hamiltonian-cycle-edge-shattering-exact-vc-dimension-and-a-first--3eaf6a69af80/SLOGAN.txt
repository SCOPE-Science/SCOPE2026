The edge-membership VC dimension of Hamiltonian cycles of \(K_n\) is exactly \(n-2\) for every \(n\ge5\), but the first strict adaptive advantage already occurs on six vertices: \(\operatorname{VCdim}=4\) and \(\operatorname{Ldim}=5\). Independent choices of Hamiltonian cycles on \(s\) prescribed six-vertex groups amplify this into the exact gap \(s\).
