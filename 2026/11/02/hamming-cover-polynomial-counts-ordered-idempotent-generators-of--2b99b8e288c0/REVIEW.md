# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The proof combines the verified Longstaff–Rosenthal main theorem (generating-set criterion for a finite incidence algebra) with a direct unipotent-orbit proof of the fiber count for every binary idempotent diagonal. The orbit stabilizer contains precisely same-color comparable coordinates. Independent elementary cover transvections yield uniform cover entry vectors, and the Longstaff–Rosenthal condition is equivalent to exclusion of the \(q\)-element span of the nonzero diagonal-difference vector. All noncover contributions are counted as residual orbit fibers; their entries are not incorrectly assumed independent. The calculation works for any characteristic, including two, and handles empty generating tuples and antichains. `verify.py` independently checked 11 exhaustive small cases. Limitation: small-field enumeration cannot prove the all-poset identity, which rests on the structural argument.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence; residual access risk
Longstaff–Rosenthal (2000) proves the generating *criterion* but does not give the claimed exact finite-field count for ordered idempotent generating tuples. Khripchenko–Novikov (2008) proves idempotent conjugacy, also not this tuple enumeration. Kolegov's article (first publicly online 5 March 2024) supplies minimum-generator numbers and the known hypercube-embedding existence classification. The formula's vanishing condition is explicitly not claimed as novel; the assessed new object is the exact weighted fiber sum, for all posets, finite fields and tuple lengths. A 2025 paper studies the probability of generating with unrestricted matrices, which is a different sample space. Semantic searches of published mathematical records returned no equivalent generator-tuple enumeration; the closest returned records concern unrelated poset tuple orbit profiles, Boolean powers, and incidence-algebra commutator statistics. The full Kolegov text was not accessible, so overlap under a differently named enumeration theorem remains an acknowledged priority risk, and search absence is not asserted to prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This supplies a natural quantitative invariant missing from existence-only classifications: the count of successful ordered idempotent generating tuples, and hence their precise finite-field probability. It isolates the exact cost of each Hasse cover relative to unconstrained idempotents and applies uniformly to arbitrary finite posets; nontrivial examples already depend on comparability and cover structure. The result is a complete structural enumeration, not a routine recasting of the minimum-generator threshold. The sum is not claimed computationally efficient in general, nor is its publication priority externally certified.
