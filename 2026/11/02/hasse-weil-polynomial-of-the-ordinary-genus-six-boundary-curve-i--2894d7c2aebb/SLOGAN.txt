For the \(\mathbb F_3\)-form \(y^3-y=x+1/(x^3-x)\) of the recently identified ordinary genus-six boundary curve, the exact Weil numerator is \(1+16T^3+118T^6+432T^9+729T^{12}\), factoring as the square of an elliptic factor times the square of a simple ordinary surface factor. Its Jacobian has \(1296\) rational points, and \(\#X(\mathbb F_{3^n})=3^n+1\) whenever \(3
mid n\).
