# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
For any finite poset, strict interval concatenation is associative and products correspond exactly to strict chains. The full power semiring is the union-and-setwise-product algebra including the empty subset. For poset height at most two its nonzero-product relational template is unary; at height three the binary template has an edge but no directed length-two walk, and is homomorphically equivalent to a single directed edge, whose obstruction is the directed two-edge path. At height at least four, each odd oriented cycle \(H_{2m+1}\) has a forced endpoint equality contradicting strictness; every proper induced substructure maps to an embedded directed length-two path of three composable intervals. Thus finite duality holds precisely through height three. The proof invokes the exact finite-duality/full-power finite-basis theorem of Wang, Yin and Wang, arXiv:2610.01329v1, Theorem 1.1, and its bounded induced-obstruction Lemma 3.3. The quantitative identity corollary invokes their Theorem 4.1. The finite checker provides additional consistency checks, not an infinite proof. One-element-zero and empty-poset cases are treated separately.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Wang, Yin and Wang supply the general criterion, the three-element-chain boundary case (Example 4.4), and a warning about non-inheritance (Example 4.5). A prior classification of index-three semigroups whose nonzero-product digraph is bipartite also covers the **entire positive height-three branch**, but neither item classifies strict intervals for **higher nilpotency indices** or settles all finite-poset chain heights. Their general theorem alone leaves the finite duality question to be resolved. Gao and Zhou, arXiv:2610.06351v1, analyze the same strict comparable-pair multiplicative reduct but equip it with flat, not powerset-union, addition. They prove finitely based flat interval semirings for chains of every height (Theorem 4.11), which is **opposite** to the eventual full-powerset result rather than implying it. Other inspected papers treat different semigroup families. Mathematical-record and accessible earlier-claim comparisons did not identify the uniform height-three classification, though absence from search is not proof of novelty. Standard digraph duality or uninspected earlier results could imply the specific threshold, a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Finite basability of full power semirings is an active algebraic classification problem. This result classifies an unrestricted natural family indexed by every finite poset, giving a sharp four-vertex-chain boundary independent of width, a transparent family of arbitrarily large obstructions, and explicit unbounded-variable identities. The contrast with flat interval semirings on the *same multiplicative semigroup* shows a substantive effect of adjoining the powerset union and is not a mere rewording of a small example. It does not settle arbitrary nilpotent semigroups or supply optimal finite identity bases.
