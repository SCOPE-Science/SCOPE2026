The six \(I_2\)-fiber locations of a rational elliptic surface with section lie exactly on the classical degree-15 Hermite involution hypersurface of binary sextics; the associated genus-two curve is bielliptic, with Jacobian on the Humbert-four locus. Generically, those six points reconstruct the elliptic surface uniquely, answering the explicit equation-and-moduli question raised in the 2026 classification.
