In a gas of independent heavy-tailed Lévy flights with random uniformly selected reset batches, the normalized probability of \(j\) simultaneous extreme displacements converges to an exact finite factor \(H_j\in[1,j!]\). The complete hierarchy \(H_2,\ldots,H_N\) uniquely determines the entire reset-batch size distribution—even though ordinary covariances of the flights do not exist.
