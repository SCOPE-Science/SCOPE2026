For the normalized two-sided exponential Gabor system at integer redundancy \(q\), the unique rectangular lattice minimizing the sharp frame-condition number has parameter \(\eta_q=2\log(2q)+O((\log q)^2/q^2)\). Its best condition number is \(1+[2\log^2(2q)+2\log(2q)+1]/q^2+O((\log q)^4/q^4)\), resolving the large-redundancy location and sharp spectral-tightness rate beyond the previously known finite-density formulas and uniqueness theorem.
