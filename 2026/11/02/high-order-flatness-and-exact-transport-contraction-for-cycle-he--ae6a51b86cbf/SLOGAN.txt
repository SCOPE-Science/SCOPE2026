On every finite unweighted cycle, the exact Wasserstein-one heat-contraction coefficient is the total absolute deviation of the heat kernel from its quarter-circumference median. Its first strict contraction term occurs at order \(\lfloor(N+2)/4\rfloor\), with an explicit coefficient depending on \(N\bmod4\), so arbitrarily many initial derivatives vanish even though contraction is strict at every positive time.
