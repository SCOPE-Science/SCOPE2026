For single-peaked, mutually beneficial swap Schelling games on a cycle with \(\Lambda>1/2\), exactly one population split has no equilibrium: three of each color on six vertices. Every stable placement avoids both alternating four-blocks and the coexistence of opposite monochromatic triples; a finite transfer matrix counts all such placements, whose fraction among fair random two-color placements decays exponentially at rate \((1.651736555\ldots)/2\).
