For an active Ornstein–Uhlenbeck searcher with complete Poisson restarts, the high-reset mean absorption time retains a finite memory of the **initial propulsion distribution**: its Brownian activation factor is divided by the exponential velocity moment \(\mathbb E[e^{-dV_0/(2D)}]\). At fixed bounded support and mean reset velocity, the uniquely fastest leading design draws only the two endpoint velocities, whereas a deterministic mean velocity is slowest.
