For the differential Hilbert operator between weighted Bergman Hilbert spaces, the leading singular value converges to a positive limit while every higher fixed-index singular value has an exact sharp power-law decay as the target weight grows, with coefficients given by finite positive Gram determinants. The naturally renormalized products of the largest \(k\) singular values decrease strictly, are log-convex in the target weight, and converge to an explicit optimal lower envelope.
