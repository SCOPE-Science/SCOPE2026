Every real two-dimensional translation-invariant subspace of the Lipschitz space over the integer line, and its canonical quotient predual, is isometric either to the Euclidean plane or to an affine-regular even-polygonal plane; every such geometry occurs. The exact distance from Hilbert geometry is \(1\) or \(\sec(\pi/N)\), and the natural cosine--sine models have arithmetic norm discontinuities precisely at rational frequencies.
