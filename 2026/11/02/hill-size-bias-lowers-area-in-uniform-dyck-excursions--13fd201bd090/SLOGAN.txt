For a uniformly random Dyck path, hill-size-biasing the law makes its entire area distribution stochastically smaller: an explicit coupling decreases area by a Catalan-distributed nonnegative split index. In particular, the number of ground-level hills and area have exact covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(H,A)=-(n-1)/2\), and their covariance against every strictly increasing area transform is negative for \(n\ge2\).
