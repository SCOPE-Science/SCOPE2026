Every hypercube geodesic of length \(k\) has exactly the same complete Hamming-distance shell polynomial, whose coefficients are ballot-number differences. Its eccentricity is \(d-\lceil k/2\rceil\), and the complete set of minimum-eccentricity geodesics has \(2^{d-1}d!\) members for odd \(d\) and \(2^d d!\) for even \(d\).
