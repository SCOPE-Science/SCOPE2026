The complete Lie-ideal dimension polynomial of the distinct-edge star-graph algebra \(L_{n,q}\) is a two-stratum Gaussian-binomial sum valid over every finite field. As \(q\to\infty\) with fixed \(n\geq4\), a random ideal has dimension \(n\) almost surely for even \(n\), while for odd \(n\) its dimension approaches the uniform distribution on \(\{n-1,n,n+1\}\).
