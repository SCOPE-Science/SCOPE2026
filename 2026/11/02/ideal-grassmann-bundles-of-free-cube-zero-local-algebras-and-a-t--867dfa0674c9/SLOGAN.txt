Every two-sided ideal in a free associative local algebra with cube-zero radical is encoded by a pair of nested Grassmannian choices and one linear lift, giving a uniform finite-field ideal census. For the two-generator seven-dimensional algebra, the reduced variety of four-dimensional ideals is the projective cone over a twisted cubic, singular only at the radical-square ideal and having \(q^2+q+1\) points over \(\mathbb F_q\).
