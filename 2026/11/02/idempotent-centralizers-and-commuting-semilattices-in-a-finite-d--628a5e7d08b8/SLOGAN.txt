Every idempotent of the finite directed-path weak-endomorphism monoid has an explicit centralizer size, and exactly \((4n-12)2^n+(n^2+9n)/2+12\) ordered commuting pairs have an idempotent first coordinate. Its idempotent-commuting graph is the full interval-intersection graph, with an exact clique polynomial and precisely \(n\) maximal commuting semilattices.
