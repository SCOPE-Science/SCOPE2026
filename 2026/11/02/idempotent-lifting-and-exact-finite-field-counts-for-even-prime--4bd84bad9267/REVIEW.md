# Scientific review: idempotents in even prime dihedral quandle algebras

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof establishes an algebra section for the natural \(2p\)-to-\(p\) quandle quotient and identifies its kernel as a right annihilator. This converts each idempotent fiber to the exact \(+1\)-eigenspace of \(Z\mapsto\iota(Z)Y\). The nonmodular proof explicitly solves the Fourier equations, checks both reciprocal coefficients and finite-field Frobenius descent, and counts all zero/nonzero orbit states. The modular proof uses the local group ring, a complete truncated-logarithm parametrization, and unit conjugation to inversion. These cover every odd characteristic, including the characteristic dividing \(p\). Independent direct multiplication enumerations in six cases agree with both formulas. Characteristic two and even orders other than \(2p\) are not claimed.

## Originality

**PASS with historical-priority limitation.** Published Fourier analysis of Alexander and Takasaki quandle algebras provides the underlying idempotent equation and proves integral idempotent theorems. Earlier quandle algebra studies cover integer-coefficient and odd-order dihedral cases; group-ring techniques cover augmentation ideals and Peirce spectra. None of the inspected statements classifies the idempotent fibers under \(R_{2p}\to R_p\) over arbitrary odd finite fields or gives the two exact counting formulas, including the coefficient-characteristic jump. The existing odd-prime finite-field census is acknowledged as a precursor and not claimed anew. The potentially closest unindexed finite-field enumeration remains a priority risk; mathematical-record and literature searches are not a proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The even-order quandle is not latin, and the quotient \(R_{2p}\to R_p\) introduces a nonzero right-annihilator kernel. Determining the lift eigenspaces gives the missing structural ingredient for composite even order, rather than simply substituting parameters into the known prime-order count. The resulting formulas distinguish Frobenius arithmetic in the nonmodular case from a uniform high-dimensional lift in characteristic \(p\). The precise idempotent fibers are relevant to coefficient-sensitive quandle algebra computations and to extending census methods beyond latin quandles. The result is deliberately confined to this natural first nonlatin doubled-prime family.

## Closest literature and limitations

Elhamdadi–Ta–Virgin, arXiv:2609.03799, develops the Fourier equation for Takasaki quandle idempotents but studies integral idempotents in its main theorems; Kaur–Singh, arXiv:2609.09183, treats odd-order integral dihedral idempotents; Sangare–Ta, arXiv:2608.01550, treats integral-domain idempotents and automorphisms of dihedral quandle algebras; Zhang, arXiv:2610.08204, treats group-ring transport, integral augmentation powers and odd-order Peirce spectra. A preceding finite-field prime-order formula does not determine the doubled-cover fibers. These comparisons establish a credible specific gap, not absolute historical priority. No claim is made for characteristic two, arbitrary even moduli, or integral quandle rings.
