# Scientific assessment

## Correctness

**PASS (self-review).** The four-phase generator determinant factors into a telegraph factor and a strictly subdominant factor for every real tilt; the finite-state Feynman–Kac theorem fixes the exact scaled cumulant-generating function. The athermal ballistic edge is treated separately using an explicit no-reversal event rather than assuming an inappropriate steepness hypothesis. The stationary autocorrelation follows from a second-order annihilating relation for the orientation vector. Integrating that correlation and applying the stable harmonic filter yields exact finite- and long-time variances; positivity follows from \(\sinh x-\sin x>0\) for \(x>0\) and positive rational denominators. The packaged executable independently checked tilted eigenvalues, characteristic polynomials, correlations, quadrature identities, and Legendre transforms at 432 deterministic tests. Numerical checks support but are not the source of proof.

## Originality

**PASS (qualified).** The new claim is the *joint* current-level large-deviation equivalence of an Erlang-2 and a Markov model, accompanied by strict exact finite-time and harmonic-fluctuation separation. Di Crescenzo (2001) and Di Crescenzo–Iuliano–Verasani (2026) already treat Erlang alternating-velocity models and some position statistics. Farago–Smith (2024) already formulate general non-Markov trapped-run motion; van Gisbergen–Redig (2019) provide standard tilted-generator large deviations. None of the inspected accessible main texts states or implies the specific calibrated pair's identical full free-current rate function **together with** both strict variance witnesses. An earlier published alternating-renewal aliasing finding concerns *matching two-time laws*, a mathematically different implication. The full texts of two plausible older sources were not available for conclusive comparison; this is a genuine publication-priority risk, not proof of novelty.

## Value

**PASS.** Interarrival law identification from only effective diffusion is notoriously underdetermined. More surprisingly, this exactly solved pair defeats *all* level-one free-current large deviations, not just diffusion calibration. It supplies explicit, strictly positive alternatives—the entire transient variance curve and harmonic confinement variance—within the same simple physical family. The meaningful limitation identifies which experimental summaries lose renewal information and which measurements retrieve it. The effect is not a parameter-renaming tautology: the processes have different non-Markovian structures and different autocorrelations while their dominant tilted spectra agree.
