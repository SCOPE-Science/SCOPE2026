For the directed Friedkin–Johnsen projection of edge-dependent weighted hypergraphs, the space of heterogeneous group roles that preserve total equilibrium opinion for every innate profile is exactly the cycle space of the vertex–hyperedge incidence graph. Incidence forests force uniform roles, while the smallest overlap of two distinct three-person groups already supports mean-neutral asymmetric influence that raises or lowers polarization relative to uniform roles, depending on innate opinions.
