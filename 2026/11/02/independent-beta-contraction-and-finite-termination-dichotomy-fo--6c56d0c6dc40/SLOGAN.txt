For rank-one Gaussian sketches in exact-line-search RCGLS, a two-column full-rank least-squares system terminates in two steps almost surely, but no system with at least three columns terminates at any finite step with positive probability. With orthonormal design columns, squared-error contraction factors are independent beta variables—one dimension-\(d\) factor followed by identical dimension-\(d-1\) factors—giving exact finite-step moments and an almost-sure exponential decay rate.
