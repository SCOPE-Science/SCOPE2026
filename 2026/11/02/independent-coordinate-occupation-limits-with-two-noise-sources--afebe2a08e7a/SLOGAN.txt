Coordinate occupation in an asynchronous hypercube elephant walk has two distinct diffusive noise sources: a classical elephant-memory fluctuation and an independent Brownian correction from randomly held spins, producing the sharp variance \(k/(3-4p_i)+k-1\) below \(p_i=3/4\). At the memory threshold the limit becomes \(\sqrt{k t}\) times one normal variable, while above the threshold a non-Gaussian elephant limit governs each coordinate; all coordinate limits are independent despite sharing the update clock.
