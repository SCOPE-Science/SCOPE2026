For all \(m,n\ge2\), every maximal independent set of the two-token graph \(F_2(K_{m,n})\) has an explicit two-subset parametrization, yielding its complete independent-dominating-set polynomial and closed independent domination number. The largest sets are exactly the two natural token-parity classes, with a tie only at consecutive triangular-number part sizes; the previously known scalar independence number is not claimed as new.
