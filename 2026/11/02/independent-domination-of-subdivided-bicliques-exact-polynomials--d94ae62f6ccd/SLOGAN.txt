For every biclique \(K_{a,b}\), subdividing all its edges gives an independent-domination polynomial \(z^{a+b}+\sum_{p=1}^{a}\sum_{q=1}^{b}\binom ap\binom bqz^{a+b-p-q+pq}\). In the balanced case its nonzero cardinality shifts are exactly the entries of a multiplication table, yielding unbounded forbidden intervals and, using Ford's known theorem, asymptotic density zero among cardinalities between the extrema.
