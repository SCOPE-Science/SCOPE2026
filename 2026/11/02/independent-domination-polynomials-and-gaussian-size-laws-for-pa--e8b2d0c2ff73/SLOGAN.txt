For every \(q\ge3\), the complete independent-domination polynomial of \(P_\ell\times K_q\) has a two-state rational generating function; the numbers of minimum sets have explicit residue-class formulas, including one exceptional configuration at \((\ell,q)=(3,3)\). A uniformly selected maximal independent set has an asymptotically Gaussian cardinality with positive linear variance, and minimum-cardinality sets form an exponentially vanishing fraction.
