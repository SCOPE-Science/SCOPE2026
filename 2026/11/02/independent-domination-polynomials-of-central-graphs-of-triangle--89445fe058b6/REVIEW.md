# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The independence and subdivision-vertex domination requirements force a unique subdivision selection for each clique of original vertices. Triangle-freeness restricts these cliques to the empty set, a singleton, or an edge. Each case is sufficient because of the minimum-degree-two condition and the absence of common neighbors of adjacent vertices. The resulting cardinality formulas are exact. Direct independent enumeration verifies the polynomial for all 560 labelled admissible graphs with four through six vertices, including two graphs whose degree sequences agree but whose polynomials and minima differ. Finite checks corroborate, but do not establish, the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The closest motivating 2026 study of central graphs focuses on minimum independent domination numbers. Its available abstract does not state a complete cardinality enumerator or an all-set bijection; the full text was not accessible, and direct priority comparison of its internal theorems remains a limitation. The 2014 independent-domination polynomial literature supplies the invariant but the searched material did not exhibit this particular triangle-free-central graph formula. Targeted published-record and mathematical-record searches for equivalent formulations, maximal-independent-set counts, degree-sum formulas and polynomial refinements revealed no covering result. An earlier equivalent theorem not indexed by those searches remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The result is a complete all-size classification for a broad graph class, with the unexpected universal total count \(1+|V|+|E|\), a structural bijection, and an exact minimum-set multiplicity. The two six-vertex examples show that even the minimum central-graph domination number is sensitive to how equal-degree original vertices are joined: the invariant sees joint degree incidences that the original degree sequence alone loses. This goes beyond merely recomputing a previously known minimum for a named small graph.

## Limits
Only triangle-free original graphs with minimum degree at least two are included. The result is not asserted for iterated central graphs. The closest 2026 primary manuscript was only available by abstract, and independent mathematical audit is still outstanding.
