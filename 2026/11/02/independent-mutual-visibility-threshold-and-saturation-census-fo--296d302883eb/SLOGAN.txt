For every \(L,m\ge2\), the independent mutual-visibility number of \(P_L\square K_m\) is \(\min\{L,2m\}\), except for a one-vertex deficit at the unique boundary \(L=2m\). Every saturated set consists of two complete permutations of the clique colors separated by an empty path layer, yielding exactly \((m!)^2\binom{L-1}{2m}\) optimal sets whenever \(L\ge2m+1\).
