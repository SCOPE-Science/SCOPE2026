In the regime \(d_n2^{-n}\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}\binom nj\to\lambda>0\), each fixed length-\(r\) binary pattern is missed independently at a Poisson rate in the limit, even though pattern misses are dependent within one input word. The number of patterns shared by all inputs becomes \(\operatorname{Binomial}(2^r,e^{-\lambda})\), and the longest common subsequence has a nondegenerate two-point limit supported on \(r-1\) and \(r\).
