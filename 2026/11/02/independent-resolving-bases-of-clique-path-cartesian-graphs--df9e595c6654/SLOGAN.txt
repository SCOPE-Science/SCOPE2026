Independent resolving bases of \(P_L\square K_q\) exist precisely for \(L\ge3\) when \(q=2,3\), and for \(L\ge q-1\) when \(q\ge4\). Their exact multiplicities are \(2\), \(12\binom L3\), and \(q!\binom L{q-1}\), respectively; the three-color case alone requires one more independent landmark than the ordinary metric dimension.
