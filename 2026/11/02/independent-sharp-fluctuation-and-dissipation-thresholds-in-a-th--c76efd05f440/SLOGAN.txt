In a stable three-coordinate skew Ornstein–Uhlenbeck chain with extra single-coordinate damping, stationary total variance is monotone exactly when \(b\leq1\), while entropy production is monotone exactly when \(q\leq2\). Above either independent threshold the corresponding observable has one finite-damping minimum, yielding all four fluctuation–dissipation response regimes in a single isotropic-noise model.
