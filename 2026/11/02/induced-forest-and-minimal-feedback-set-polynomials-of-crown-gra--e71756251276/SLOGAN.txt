For every crown graph \(C_n=K_{n,n}-M\) with \(n\ge4\), inclusion-maximal induced forests have cardinality polynomial \(n(n-1)(n-2)z^5+2nz^{n+1}\), and the polynomial of all induced forests is explicit. Thus these connected, regular, vertex-transitive bipartite graphs have maximal-forest sizes only \(5\) and \(n+1\) for \(n\ge6\), with the same cardinality gap reflected by complementary minimal feedback vertex sets.
