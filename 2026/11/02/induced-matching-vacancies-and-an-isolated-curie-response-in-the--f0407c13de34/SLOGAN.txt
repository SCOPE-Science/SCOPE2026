The twelve-site classical spin-one icosahedral antiferromagnet has exact vacancy transitions at \(D/J=1/2\) and \(D/J=1\): its intermediate ground states are precisely the \(150\) sign choices on \(75\) induced-two-edge vacancy patterns. The final degeneracy creates an isolated zero-temperature Curie coefficient \(40/117\), while all other anisotropies have zero leading Curie response.
