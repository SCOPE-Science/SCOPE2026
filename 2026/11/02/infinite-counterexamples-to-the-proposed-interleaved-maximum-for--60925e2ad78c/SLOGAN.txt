For each \(m\ge4\), a length-\(2m\) consecutive pattern has exactly \(9\cdot2^{m-4}\) two-occurrence packings with overlap \(m\), whereas the interleaved-minimal pattern proposed as extremal has only \(2^{m-1}\). The fixed \(9/8\) advantage gives an infinite family of counterexamples to the maximizing assertion in Conjecture 7.1 of Blitvić–Kammoun–Steingrímsson (2026).
