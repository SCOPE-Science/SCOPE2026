The cosine-memory logistic map has infinitely many literal primitive two-cycles, split into commensurate-memory and reflected-phase families, with exact asymptotic count \(N_2(R)=2kR/\pi+O(1)\) modulo accumulator translations. Almost all are hyperbolic repellers with return-multiplier scale \(aku^3\) at amplitude \(u\); for \(0<a<1\), they coexist with an explicit globally attracting inner strip.
