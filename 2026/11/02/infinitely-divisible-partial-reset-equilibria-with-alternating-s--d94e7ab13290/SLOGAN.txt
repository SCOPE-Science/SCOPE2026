Every finite-mean refractory partial-reset Brownian equilibrium is infinitely divisible with an explicit nonnegative Lévy density, but infinite divisibility does not guarantee self-decomposability. At partial-reset factor \(a=1/100\), the self-decomposable occupancies \(w=a^{2m}\) and the non-self-decomposable occupancies \(w=a^{2m+1}\) interlace indefinitely as \(w\downarrow0\).
