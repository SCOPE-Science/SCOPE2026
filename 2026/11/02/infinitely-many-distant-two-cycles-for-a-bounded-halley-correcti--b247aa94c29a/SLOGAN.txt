Mukasa's positive bounded Halley correction has infinitely many exact nonzero two-cycles on the strictly increasing analytic equations \(f_\delta(x)=x+\delta\sin x\), for arbitrarily small \(\delta>0\). For \(0<\delta<1/3\), ordinary Newton iteration is globally contractive on exactly those same equations, so nearly constant first derivatives alone cannot guarantee global convergence of the corrected method.
