The dual-number algebra \(\mathbb C[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\) has infinitely many pairwise nonisomorphic three-dimensional evolution envelopes, all of minimum possible dimension. An intrinsic multiplication-pencil invariant separates the explicit family: \(\mathcal I(E_m)=2(3m+2)^2/(7m^2+6m+4)\) for integers \(m\ge3\).
