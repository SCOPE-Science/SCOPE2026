For odd \(p\), prime-power degree \(n=p^a\), and \(2\le r<p\), the factorial-Hadamard polynomial \(\sum_{k=0}^n(n!/k!)^rX^k\) is locally irreducible, and its entire \(p\)-integral basis and discriminant exponent are explicit. The local discriminant exponent is \(n+r(n-1)/(p-1)-1\), so distinct allowed \(r\) realize pairwise nonisomorphic totally ramified fields.
