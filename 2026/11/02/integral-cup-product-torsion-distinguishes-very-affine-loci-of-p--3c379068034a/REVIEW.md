# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Cox equations solve exactly as x0=1-s,x1=s,y1=t,y0=1-tP(s), identifying the very affine locus with V={s(1-s)t(1-tP(s))≠0}. For T=(C\{0,1})×C* and smooth graph D={t=1/P(s)}≅C\{0,1,r1,…,rq}, the divisor is principal so its Gysin push-forward H0(D)→H2(T) vanishes. Exact integral Gysin sequences yield H1(V)=Z4, H2(V)=Z^{q+4}, all others as asserted. The meridian eta=dlog(1-tP(s))/(2πi) has residue 1, and its cups with H1(T) have residues e0,e1,-∑m_i e_ri, while H2(T) is generated by decomposables. Thus cokernel is Z^q/<(m_i)> = Z^{q-1}⊕Z/gcd(m_i) for q≥1 (0 for q0). Independent Smith normal-form calculations on restriction matrices gave factors (1,1,g) and q-1 free rank for m=(2),(5),(2,4),(2,3),(6,9,12); homogeneous family gives P(s)=(1+s)^m and fixed Betti (1,4,5). All H^j are torsion-free; only the quotient by degree-one products has torsion.

## originality

PASS

Calvo Cortes–Telen 2026 describes the exact Cox positive charts and moments, not the integral ring cokernel; Améndola et al 2017 scroll likelihood-degree theorems may cover the root-count/Euler consequence q+1, explicitly not claimed novel. Callegaro et al 2018 integral toric-arrangement ring presentations apply to character level sets, generally not a graph 1-tP(s)=0 with P vanishing at punctures. Classical Gysin is a known tool, but no inspected source gives gcd multiplicity and a fixed-Hirzebruch-surface family with equal Betti groups and different rings. Resultary search compared toric topology and nearby Schubert complements; none is the same object/invariant. Older graph-complement or arrangement ring calculations remain a material priority risk.

## value

PASS

The topology of positive toric charts depends on chart data, not just the fixed ambient fan; the claimed intrinsic torsion of the degree-two indecomposable quotient distinguishes a natural family of cohomology rings even with equal integral additive Betti numbers. The gcd formula for every polynomial with the endpoint hypothesis is a complete structural invariant for this class, not a one-off computed example. It is useful for studying chart compactification and failure of Betti-level identification.
