On the fixed Hirzebruch surface \(\mathbb F_a\), the very affine loci of a family of positive charts have identical integral Betti numbers \((1,4,5)\) but pairwise distinct cohomology rings: the degree-two cup-product cokernel is \(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\) for every \(1\le m\le a\). More generally, for an arbitrary allowed chart polynomial, the cokernel is determined exactly by the gcd of its distinct-root multiplicities, even though all cohomology groups themselves are torsion-free.
