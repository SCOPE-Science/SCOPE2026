The ordinary four-cut complexes of the strongly regular Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have different torsion-free integral homology: the former has ranks \(12,2,65\) in degrees \(9,10,11\), while the latter has ranks \(21,96\) in degrees \(10,11\). Both are pure eleven-dimensional and non-shellable despite having the same reduced Euler characteristic \(-75\).
