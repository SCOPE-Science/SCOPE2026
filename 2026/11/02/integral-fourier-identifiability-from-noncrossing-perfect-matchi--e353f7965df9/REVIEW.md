## Originality — PASS subject to stated residual risk
The inspected Asinowski proof concerns the count and convex-position extremality of noncrossing matchings, not their integral incidence-difference lattice or Fourier nullspace. The three-page Houle–Hurtado–Noy–Rivera-Campo paper proves connectivity of a matching flip graph; graph connectivity alone does not imply that its edge-difference vectors generate the full zero-marginal lattice. Paffenholz's account of Birkhoff polytopes gives the affine dimension for *all* permutation matrices, not the restricted Catalan matching set. Published semantically similar Fourier and matching results retrieved during comparison did not state a dominating integral-lattice assertion for this ensemble. The historically closest Hernando–Hurtado–Noy paper was only accessible through its abstract/bibliographic record, leaving an explicitly material originality risk; a qualified human literature review could alter the assessment.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The integral-saturation assertion establishes a full **arithmetic** tomography principle, not just a real rank calculation: every balanced integer edge perturbation is generated by differences of planar (crossing-free) measurements. It also implies exact recovery-rank stability in all characteristics and identifies all deterministic first-order Fourier relations under arbitrary full-support sampling weights. This resolves a natural identifiability question for geometric matching data despite the severe Catalan restriction, without requiring a closed spectrum or downstream theorem.
