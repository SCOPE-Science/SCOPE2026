Although convex-polygon noncrossing perfect matchings form only a Catalan-sized subset of all bipartite assignments, their incidence differences generate the entire integer lattice of matrices with zero row and column sums. Therefore centered first-degree Walsh sampling on uniformly distributed noncrossing matchings has precisely the row/column nullspace and rank \((n-1)^2\) for every \(n\ge2\), with the anchored difference rank unchanged over every field.
