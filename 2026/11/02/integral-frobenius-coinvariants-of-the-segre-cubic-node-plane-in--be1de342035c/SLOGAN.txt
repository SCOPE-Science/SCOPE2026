For the Segre cubic's ten-node/fifteen-plane integral incidence map, the cokernel is \((\mathbb Z/2)^4\oplus\mathbb Z/4\). For every finite-field Frobenius type \(\sigma\in S_6\), its coinvariants are exactly \(\mathbb Z/4\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^{c(\sigma)-1-\delta(\sigma)}\), where \(\delta\) detects any odd-length cycle.
