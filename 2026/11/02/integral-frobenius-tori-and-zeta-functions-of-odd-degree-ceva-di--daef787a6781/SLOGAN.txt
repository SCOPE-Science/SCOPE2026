For every odd \(n\ge5\) in characteristic greater than \(n\), the reduced degree-\(3n\) Ceva divisor has an explicitly split vector-unipotent generalized Jacobian and, when \(q\equiv-1\pmod n\), a torus **isomorphic** to \((\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m)^{n^2-1}\), not merely isogenous to it. Across this infinite nonsplit family the rational-point count is always \(3q+1\), independent of \(n\), and both distinguished Frobenius regimes have closed zeta functions.
