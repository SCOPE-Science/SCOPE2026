For the reduced genus-six Petersen boundary of every degree-five del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbb F_q\), the connected generalized Jacobian belongs to exactly seven explicit **integral** Frobenius-torus types; these determine its finite abelian group, not just its order. A five-cycle twist has no rational boundary point yet has Jacobian \(\mathbb F_{q^5}^{\times}\times\mathbb F_q^{\times}\), while a four-cycle twist has exactly one rational point and no rational component; the complete boundary zeta functions follow from the same Frobenius incidence classification.
