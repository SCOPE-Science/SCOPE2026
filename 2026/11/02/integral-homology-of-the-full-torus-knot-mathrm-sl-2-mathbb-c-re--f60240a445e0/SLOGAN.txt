For each coprime torus-knot group, the **full** reduced complex \(\mathrm{SL}_2\)-representation variety has integral homology \(H_2\cong(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^g\), where \(g=(p-1)(q-1)/2\), even though its singular locus has torsion-free homology. The torsion comes from gluing two quadrics along diagonal and antidiagonal two-spheres, an integral phenomenon invisible to rational Betti numbers.
