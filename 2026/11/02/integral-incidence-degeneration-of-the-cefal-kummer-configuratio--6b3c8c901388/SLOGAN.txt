The marked sixteen-node/sixteen-trope configuration of the integral Cefalù quartic has an exact arithmetic incidence scheme: twelve flat copies of \(\mu_2^3\), plus four copies of \(\mu_{2,\mathbb F_3}^2\) supported only in characteristic three. Its classical \(96\) incidences thicken into twelve length-eight points in characteristic two, while sixteen genuinely new incidences appear in characteristic three.
