For every friendship graph \(K_1\vee mK_2\) with \(m\ge2\), its two-token graph has an explicitly determined, entirely integral Laplacian spectrum despite being nonregular. An invariant incidence-space decomposition also gives the exact spanning-tree count \(2^{3m^2-3m-1}3^{m+\binom m2}m^{m-2}(m+1)^m\).
