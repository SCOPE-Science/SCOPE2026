For the \(28\) bitangents and \(63\) Steiner complexes of any smooth plane quartic in characteristic different from \(2\), the integral incidence cokernel is \((\mathbb Z/2)^{14}\oplus(\mathbb Z/4)^6\oplus\mathbb Z/12\). Every individual bitangent requires the sharp multiplier \(12\), and no smaller positive multiplier, to become an integral combination of twelve-bitangent Steiner sums.
