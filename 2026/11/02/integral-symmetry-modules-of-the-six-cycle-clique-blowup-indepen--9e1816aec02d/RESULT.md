# Integral symmetry modules of the six-cycle clique-blowup independence complexes

## Finding
For an integer \(m\ge2\), the independence complex of the six-cycle lexicographic blowup \(G_m=C_6[K_m]\) admits a complete, explicit **integral homology representation** for the full automorphism group. Write \(X_m=\operatorname{Ind}(G_m)\), let \(D_i\) denote the \(m\)-point discrete set of vertices over \(i\in\mathbb Z/6\), and put \(U_i=\widetilde H_0(D_i;\mathbb Z)=\ker(\mathbb Z[D_i]\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z)\).

The full graph automorphism group is \(W_m=(S_m)^6\rtimes D_6\). Its action on the set \(E\) of \(3m^2\) opposite-fiber vertex pairs is by ordinary permutations. Let \(\varepsilon:W_m\to\{\pm1\}\) equal \(+1\) on symmetries preserving the even and odd cycle sites separately and \(-1\) on those exchanging them. Then the following isomorphisms hold **integrally and equivariantly**:
\[
\widetilde H_1(X_m;\mathbb Z)\cong
\varepsilon\otimes\ker\bigl(\mathbb Z[E]\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z\bigr),
\qquad
\widetilde H_2(X_m;\mathbb Z)\cong
(U_0\otimes U_2\otimes U_4)\oplus(U_1\otimes U_3\otimes U_5).
\]
The second module uses graded permutations of the three reduced-degree-zero factors: interchanging two factors acts with a minus sign. All other reduced integral homology groups vanish. In particular,
\[
X_m\simeq\bigvee^{3m^2-1} S^1\vee\bigvee^{2(m-1)^3}S^2.
\]

For the cyclic subgroup \(C_6\) generated by one-site rotation, the rational Betti vector of its quotient is
\[
(b_0,b_1,b_2)(X_m/C_6;\mathbb Q)=
\left(1,\frac{m(m-1)}2,\frac{(m-1)^3+2(m-1)}3\right).
\]
The quotient by the entire \(W_m\) has rational Betti vector \((1,0,0)\). Thus rotational symmetry retains classes which disappear upon allowing all fiber permutations and reflections.

## Assumptions and scope
Here \(C_6\) is the simple undirected cycle on the residue classes \(0,\ldots,5\). The graph \(G_m=C_6[K_m]\) has vertex set \(\{(i,a):0\le i<6,\ 1\le a\le m\}\): different vertices are adjacent when they have the same site \(i\), or when their sites differ by one modulo six. The independence complex contains the independent vertex sets, including the empty simplex. These assertions hold for every integer \(m\ge2\), over the indicated integral and rational coefficient rings, without asymptotic restrictions.

A fiber is the equivalence class of vertices having identical **closed neighborhoods**; the six such classes are precisely the \(D_i\), because the three-site neighborhoods in \(C_6\) are distinct. Every automorphism permutes these six classes through the dihedral automorphism group of the quotient cycle, and arbitrary permutations within each class extend. This proves \(\operatorname{Aut}(G_m)=W_m\), including the claimed full-group scope.

## Proof
**1. Exact decomposition.** An independent set of \(C_6\) has support contained in one of the two alternating triples \(\{0,2,4\}\) and \(\{1,3,5\}\), or in one of the three opposite-site pairs \(\{0,3\}\), \(\{1,4\}\), \(\{2,5\}\). Consequently \(X_m\) consists of the two disjoint subcomplexes
\[
A_e=D_0*D_2*D_4,\qquad A_o=D_1*D_3*D_5
\]
together with precisely \(3m^2\) edges joining vertices of opposite parity, one for each pair in \(E\). No two-simplices outside \(A_e\sqcup A_o\) exist. Each \(A_\bullet\) is connected and simply connected, with
\[
\widetilde H_2(A_e;\mathbb Z)=U_0\otimes U_2\otimes U_4,
\quad \widetilde H_2(A_o;\mathbb Z)=U_1\otimes U_3\otimes U_5.
\]
This follows from the augmented simplicial chain complexes of joins; three discrete sets join to a wedge of \((m-1)^3\) two-spheres. Factor permutations carry the standard shifted-chain (Koszul) signs.

**2. Natural relative homology.** Put \(A=A_e\sqcup A_o\). The relative cellular chain groups \(C_j(X_m,A;\mathbb Z)\) vanish except for \(j=1\), where they form \(\mathbb Z[E]\). In the long exact sequence of the pair, the connecting map
\[
H_1(X_m,A;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[E]\longrightarrow
H_0(A;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\{e,o\}
\]
sends each edge, oriented from odd to even, to \(e-o\). Its kernel is the augmentation lattice \(\ker(\mathbb Z[E]\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z)\), hence naturally equals \(H_1(X_m;\mathbb Z)\). Changing a symmetry from parity-preserving to parity-swapping reverses this chosen uniform edge orientation, producing exactly the twist \(\varepsilon\). Because the relative degree-two and degree-three chains vanish, \(H_2(A;\mathbb Z)\to H_2(X_m;\mathbb Z)\) is an equivariant isomorphism. These arguments also prove absence of torsion and any other reduced homology.

**3. Homotopy type.** Collapse a spanning tree separately inside each connected \(A_e\) and \(A_o\); collapsing a contractible CW subcomplex is a homotopy equivalence. The resulting connected CW complex is a graph of two wedge-of-two-spheres spaces linked by \(3m^2\) arcs. Collapsing a spanning tree of this connecting multigraph produces \(3m^2-1\) independent circles with the two sphere wedges attached at its vertex. This establishes the displayed (nonequivariant) wedge homotopy equivalence; it is not asserted to be an equivariant wedge splitting.

**4. Rotation quotient.** Rational homology of a quotient by a finite simplicial group agrees with its invariant rational homology (subdivide equivariantly to eliminate inversions and average chains). On \(H_1\), the rotation \(r:i\mapsto i+1\) has traces \((3m^2-1,1,-1,1-3m,-1,1)\) for its six powers: nonidentity elements except the half-turn fix no opposite-pair edges; the half-turn fixes exactly the \(3m\) same-label opposite pairs and reverses edge orientation. Averaging gives \(m(m-1)/2\) invariants. On \(H_2\), odd powers exchange the two tensor summands, with trace zero; the even powers have traces \((2d^3,2d,2d)\) on the three even powers, where \(d=m-1\), because a three-factor cyclic permutation has trace \(d\). The invariant rank is \((d^3+2d)/3\).

**5. Full quotient.** Under the normal subgroup \((S_m)^6\), the second module has no invariants because each augmentation factor \(U_i\) has no \(S_m\)-fixed vectors. On \(H_1\), averaging over \((S_m)^6\) identifies three opposite-edge orbits and leaves a two-dimensional augmentation module on the three diameters, with a parity twist. The order-three rotation permutes these three coordinates cyclically, giving no nonzero fixed augmentation vector. Therefore \(\widetilde H_j(X_m;\mathbb Q)^{W_m}=0\) for all \(j\), so the full quotient is rationally acyclic.

## Verification
The accompanying standard-library checker `verify_cycle6_clique.py` reconstructs the complete simplicial chains and signed automorphism actions, computes exact modular boundary ranks as finite checks, and compares signed Lefschetz traces with the two prescribed integral modules. It was run for \(m=2,3,4\), giving ordinary Betti vectors \((1,11,2)\), \((1,26,16)\), \((1,47,54)\), respectively, and 264 action/trace comparisons in total. The proof above, rather than finite-field extrapolation, establishes the integral assertions for every \(m\ge2\).

## Relationship to prior work
Kengo Okura's 2021 paper `arXiv:2109.04181` studies independence complexes of **forest** lexicographic products, including paths and complete-graph fibers. Okura's 2023 extension `arXiv:2303.11984` treats polyhedral joins over certain complexes, with the explicit lexicographic graph-product applications framed for forests. Those source results motivate the choice of product and provide nearby homotopy techniques, but the two alternating threefold joins connected by opposite-fiber edges are a specifically cyclic six-site configuration. Neither inspected paper states this full integral automorphism representation or the six-cycle rotation-quotient formulas. These results concern lexicographic products, not the distinct categorical or Cartesian graph products. They are not claims of novelty for general graph-independent-set models or for the existing forest results.

## Limitations
The natural decomposition of the homology is integral, but the wedge-of-spheres homotopy equivalence is not claimed to be equivariant. The quotient calculations concern rational homology; they make no assertion about torsion in integral quotient homology. Numerical checks cover only \(m=2,3,4\), and the algebraic proof is what supplies the unrestricted parameter range. The comparison with indexed and inspected sources is not a guarantee that no equivalent unindexed work exists. An independent external audit has not been performed.
