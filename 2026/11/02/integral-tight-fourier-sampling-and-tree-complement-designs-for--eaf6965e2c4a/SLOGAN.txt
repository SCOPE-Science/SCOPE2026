For every odd \(n\ge3\), regular-tournament edge Walsh characters form an integral, uniformly tight Fourier sampling system of dimension \((n-1)(n-2)/2\), with exact nullities governed by omitted-edge connectivity. Under any full-support distribution, **every** maximal independent coordinate selection is the complement of a spanning tree and all \(n^{n-2}\) selections have the same positive Gram determinant; uniform sampling additionally produces a spherical \(3\)-design of exact strength three when \(n\ge5\).
