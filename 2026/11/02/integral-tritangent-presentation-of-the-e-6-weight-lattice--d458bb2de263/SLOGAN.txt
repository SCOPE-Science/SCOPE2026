For the \(27\) lines of a smooth complex cubic surface, the \(45\) tritangent-triple sums generate **every integral relation** among their \(E_6\) weights, with no finite-index defect. A specified set of \(21\) tritangents is already an integral basis of that relation lattice, giving Smith form \(\operatorname{diag}(1^{21},0^6)\).
