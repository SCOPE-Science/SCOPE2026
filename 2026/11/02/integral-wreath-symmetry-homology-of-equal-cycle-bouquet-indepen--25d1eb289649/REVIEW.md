# Review of the integral bouquet-cycle wreath-character theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The central calculation uses the **actual** vertex deletion and closed-neighborhood link, which are respectively the joins of \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{L-1})\) and \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{L-3})\). The cone attachment is an exact simplicial pushout. The fold recurrence on paths proves all stated sphere/contractible types and torsion-freeness over \(\mathbb Z\). The reflection action follows from the signed fixed-face Lefschetz formula with period-six independence-polynomial evaluations. The sign of exchanging join factors is \((-1)^{(d+1)^2}\). In the divisible-by-three case, the link sphere is strictly lower dimensional than the deletion sphere for \(m\ge2\), hence the attaching map is null-homotopic. The cofiber exact sequence gives both equivariant homology lines without requiring an equivariant wedge splitting. Rational quotient homology follows from finite-group transfer. The standalone checker independently confirms the predicted mod-two ranks and signed Lefschetz traces in ten finite cases, including the two-hexagon example. The finite computations support but do not replace the all-parameter proof.

## Originality — PASS on available evidence
The closest directly matched paper, Daundkar–Panja–Prasad, *Independence complexes of wedge of graphs* (arXiv:2303.08798, Section 3), gives general nonequivariant sphere-wedge claims and a displayed \(C_6\)-by-\(C_6\) single-sphere subcase, but not the complete integral wreath-group characters. Its displayed vertex-link path length differs from the graph-theoretic closed-neighborhood calculation; the new extra degree-two class can be seen independently from the simplicial Euler characteristic and the cofiber calculation. Adamaszek (arXiv:1106.6250, Section 3) provides the general cofiber method, which is an acknowledged ingredient rather than a covering classification. Kozlov's path computations are also acknowledged. Goyal–Shukla–Singh (arXiv:1905.06926, Sections 3–5) study different graph constructions. The available research index was queried for the equal-cycle bouquet object, homology actions, wreath characters, and two-hexagon equivalences. Closest hits involving Dutch-windmill **matching complexes** do not cover independence complexes: the bouquet graph for \(m\ge2\) contains an induced claw, so it is not the line graph of a simple graph. Accessible prior local research claims were searched for overlap; the analogous windmill record concerns a matching complex, not the present invariant. No statement implying the complete present theorem was found. Equivalent unindexed or corrected literature remains a residual risk, and no global novelty certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS
The natural full automorphism action on bouquets of equally long cycles is highly structured but is not detected by ordinary wedge homotopy types. The result classifies every integral homology line and all full-symmetry rational quotient Betti numbers for an infinite two-parameter family, including a four-class congruence pattern modulo twelve. More materially, for the classical pair-of-hexagons benchmark it identifies a second reduced homology class in degree two that is absent in the closest paper's displayed subcase. This is a motivated boundary/correction to a directly relevant published calculation, not a routine unmotivated finite sample. The proof's hypothesis \(m\ge2\) separates the two sphere dimensions and is essential to the clean integral representation statement. The theorem does not claim an equivariant homotopy splitting or integral quotient homology.
