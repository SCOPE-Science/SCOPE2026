For a bouquet of \(m\ge2\) identical cycles \(C_L\) glued at one vertex, every reduced integral homology group of its independence complex is a rank-one sign character of the full reflection-and-petal-permutation wreath group; explicit formulas cover every \(L\ge4\). The resulting rational symmetry-quotient homology survives only in four residue classes modulo twelve, and the bouquet of two hexagons has both \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z\).
