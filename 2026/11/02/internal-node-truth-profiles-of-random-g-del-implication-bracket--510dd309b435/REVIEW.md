# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The new claim distinguishes every internal implication occurrence from the top-level output. The exact marked-node series follows from one distinguished node and two surrounding branches. For any fixed fully labelled fringe pattern, contracting one or two marked occurrences yields exact first and second factorial moments; the Catalan ratios imply vanishing variance after division by the number of leaves. Truncation by fringe size is justified by almost sure extinction of the critical binary Galton--Watson process. The two-child frequencies therefore converge in probability to the product of its root-law probabilities. The order-of-limits continuum derivation is a direct Lipschitz Euler-limit argument, with explicit treatment of the nonzero top atom. The output distribution formula uses continuity away from a zero-measure diagonal. Direct finite enumeration reproduces all root and internal-pair counts in the stated range.

## Originality — PASS with stated priority uncertainty
Yildiz's full arXiv:2602.16135v2, including Part IV, derives root truth proportions and ordered pairs at the **root**, not empirical frequencies over all internal nodes; its continuum root top mass differs from the present internal-node top mass. Janson's general fringe-subtree theory implies an abstract frequency law once an appropriate score function is supplied, but does not tabulate the Gödel-specific finite marked-node series, exact internal pair profile or the derived continuum child and internal-output laws. Broutin--Devroye--Fraiman treats recursive functions on conditioned branching trees and emphasizes root evaluations. No equivalent specialized theorem was located by the recorded source and mathematical-index searches. This is provisional priority, not a claim that general results could not be combined to rederive the theorem.

## Value — PASS
The motivating paper measures only the final output of a long implication formula. Internal evaluations instead quantify the proportion of truth states processed at the intermediate computational steps. The exact size-by-size generating function, full child transition matrix, rigorous concentration and distinct continuum limiting profile answer this different natural question. For Boolean implication, the intermediate false share is \(\sqrt3-3/2\), and in the dense-chain limit the internal top mass is \(4-2\sqrt3\), quantitatively unlike the published root law. Those are interpretable invariants beyond checking a few coefficients.
