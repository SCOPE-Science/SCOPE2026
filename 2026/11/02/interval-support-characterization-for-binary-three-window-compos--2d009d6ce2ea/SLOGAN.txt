For binary length-three composition reads, a vector of four multiplicities comes from a word exactly when its positive coordinates form an interval; this gives \((m^3+11m+12)/6\) possible read multispectra from \(m\) overlapping triples and an explicit optimal zero-error codebook. The interval-support criterion fails at every read length \(h\ge4\), already for the full-support profile with weight two appearing twice; the cubic numerical sequence itself was previously recorded for a different combinatorial model.
