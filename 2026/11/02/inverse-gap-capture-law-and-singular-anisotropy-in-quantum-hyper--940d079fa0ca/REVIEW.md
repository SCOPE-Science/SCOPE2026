## Mathematical value (V): PASS

The problem is an operationally natural quantum-transport observable: the unconditional mean dwell time before removal by one local sink, with the sink rate itself tunable. The result makes exact the competition between weak absorption and overdamping for every simple flat local spectral measure, not merely for one artificial finite graph. The anisotropic hypercube shows a sharp noncommuting limit: arbitrarily small hopping asymmetry removes a large symmetry-induced dark subspace, yet the wait for certain capture diverges as the perturbation vanishes. The explicit pairwise gap invariant and its sharp rate optimization quantify two different slowdown exponents. These are structural and asymptotic statements across every cube dimension \(d\ge2\), not a routine small-graph census or a nominal reparameterization.
