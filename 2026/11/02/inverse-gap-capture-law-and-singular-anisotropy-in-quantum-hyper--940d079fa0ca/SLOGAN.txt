For a completely mixed excitation with a flat, nondegenerate absorber spectral measure, the exact mean quantum capture time is a simple inverse-square sum over every pair of energy levels, and its globally optimal loss rate is explicit. On anisotropically weighted binary hypercubes, infinitesimal bond asymmetry restores certain eventual capture but causes an exact inverse-square slowdown at fixed loss, reduced to an inverse-first-power slowdown by optimal loss-rate tuning.
