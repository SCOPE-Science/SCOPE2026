For an antithermalizing qubit battery interacting with fresh thermal ancillas, every entangling collision comes before every nonentangling one, with an exact finite lifetime and a sharp thermal cutoff. As the bath excitation probability \(p\) vanishes, the maximum number of entangling collisions is asymptotic to \(0.231960952986\ldots/p\), achievable only at weak collision strength \(\sin^2\theta\sim3.311070407\ldots p\).
