The two degree-equivalent finite groups \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/\varepsilon^2)\) and \(\mathrm{SL}_2(W_2(\mathbb F_q))\), for every even \(q\), have radically different involution geometry: the first has exactly \(q+2\) maximal elementary abelian subgroups of order \(q^3\), and the second has exactly one. A projective-line clique decomposition gives the first group's complete involution-centralizer spectrum and the exact ordered commuting-involution count \(q^7+2q^6-q^5-q^4\).
