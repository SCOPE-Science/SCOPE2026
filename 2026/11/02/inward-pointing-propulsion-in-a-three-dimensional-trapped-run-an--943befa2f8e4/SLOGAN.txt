For a three-dimensional isotropically reorienting particle in a harmonic trap, the exact stationary probability that active propulsion points inward is strictly between zero and one half at every positive tumble rate. Its slow-tumble slope is \(\log2-1/2\), and its deficit from one half at rapid tumbling is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{3/(2\pi\alpha)}\), with an exact conditional position–heading density given by last-run disintegration.
