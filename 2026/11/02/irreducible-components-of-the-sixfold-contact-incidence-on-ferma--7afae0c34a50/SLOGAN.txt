For the smooth Fermat hypersurface of degree \(d\ge6\) in \(\mathbb P^5_{\mathbb C}\), the reduced incidence of pointed lines with at least sixfold contact has exactly \(15d^3+45d^2+90d+51\) irreducible threefold components, explicitly indexed by partial partitions of the six coordinates into Fermat-zero blocks. Every such line either lies in the hypersurface or has contact order exactly \(d\), so finite contact orders strictly between five and \(d\) never occur.
