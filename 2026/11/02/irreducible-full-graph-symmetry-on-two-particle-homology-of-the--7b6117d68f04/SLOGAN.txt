For two unordered particles on \(K_{4,4}\), the full graph automorphism group \((S_4\times S_4)\rtimes C_2\) acts **irreducibly** on the twelve-dimensional rational second homology as \(\operatorname{Ind}(S^{(2,1,1)}\boxtimes S^{(2,2)})\). The action of every part-swapping graph automorphism has trace zero, and the full symmetry quotient has no positive-degree rational homology.
