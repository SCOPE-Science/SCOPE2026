For every odd prime power \(p^a\), the Ramanujan-sum polynomial has precisely the known cyclotomic factor \(1-X^{p^{a-1}}\) and one further rationally and \(p\)-adically irreducible factor. Its discriminant has a closed formula; every residual root lies outside the complex unit disk but at exact \(p\)-adic distance \(p^{-1/((p-2)p^{a-1})}\) from one, and exactly one residual root is real.
