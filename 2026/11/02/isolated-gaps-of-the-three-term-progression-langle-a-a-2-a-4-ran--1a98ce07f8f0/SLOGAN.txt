For every odd \(a\ge3\), all isolated gaps of the three-generator numerical semigroup \(\langle a,a+2,a+4\rangle\) form an explicit triangular lattice strip, and their number is exactly \(\lfloor g/2\rfloor\), where \(g\) is the genus. The smallest isolated gap is \(a+1\) and the largest is the Frobenius number.
