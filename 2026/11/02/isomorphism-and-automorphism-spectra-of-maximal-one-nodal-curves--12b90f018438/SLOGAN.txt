For a fixed maximal elliptic normalization over \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\), the unpointed maximal one-nodal curves of arithmetic genus two have an exact isomorphism-class and automorphism-order census, governed solely by the origin-fixing automorphism order \(m\in\{2,4,6\}\). The entire census comes from the action of these automorphisms on the nonzero \((p-1)\)-torsion differences of Frobenius-conjugate points.
