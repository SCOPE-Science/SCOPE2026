For monic degree-\(n\) polynomials over any odd finite field, prescribing both \(f(0)\) and \(f(1)\) yields an exact Jacobi-sum formula for the square and nonsquare nonzero-discriminant counts. The maximum signed bias is \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), but grows as \(\Theta_q(q^{n/2})\) over every fixed odd field of size \(q\ge5\).
