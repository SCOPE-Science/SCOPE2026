For split alternating quadratic pencils in characteristic two, the entire graded Hilbert series of the two-generated exterior quotient is determined by the multiplicities of the pencil eigenvalues, including every collision stratum. Over finite fields the common-annihilator dimension has sharp balanced-versus-coalesced extrema; in eight variables, four distinct eigenvalues give middle Hilbert coefficient \(17\), unlike the known complex generic coefficient \(16\).
