Under one uniformly random orthographic view of a regular tetrahedron, cube, and octahedron, their full projected wireframes have crossing counts \((1,2,2)\) with probability \(\frac{12}{\pi}\arctan(3-2\sqrt2)\), and \((0,2,6)\) otherwise. Every generic planar projection of every three-dimensional parallelepiped has exactly two crossings of disjoint original edges.
