The joint excluded-middle and double-negation probabilities of every finite Heyting algebra have an exact arithmetic spectrum, determined by connected poset factors. At a prescribed positive doubling defect \(k\), both probabilities have sharp bounds attained only by a one-top star with \(k+1\) minima, possibly times a Boolean factor: \(p\le 2/(2^{k+1}+1)\) and \(q\le 2^{k+1}/(2^{k+1}+1)\).
