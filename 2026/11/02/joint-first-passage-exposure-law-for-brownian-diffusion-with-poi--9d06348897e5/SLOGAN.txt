The three canonical stopping-time observables of a Poisson-reset Brownian search—completion time, point local time at the reset site, and residence time above it—have one explicit joint Laplace transform, not merely separate marginal formulas. The two exposure observables form an exponential-stopped subordinator pair, with reset-rate correlation \(\sqrt{\sinh(a\sqrt{r/D})/[1+\sinh(a\sqrt{r/D})]}\) increasing strictly from zero to one.
