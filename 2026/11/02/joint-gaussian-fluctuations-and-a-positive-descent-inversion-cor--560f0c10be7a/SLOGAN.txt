At critical time \(r=\lfloor cn\rfloor\), descents and inversions of random-to-top shuffled cards have a bivariate Gaussian limit with strictly positive off-diagonal covariance \(e^{-c}[e^{-c}-(1+c)e^{-2c}]/4\), in contrast to the asymptotic independence of their uniform-permutation counterparts. Their covariance is already strictly positive at every finite deck size and every positive shuffle count, with an exact formula conditional on the number of distinct touched cards.
