For Bernoulli recursive trees whose newest vertex attaches to one of its two predecessors, the conditional mean walk hitting times from oldest to newest and back have a joint Gaussian tree-to-tree fluctuation law on the \(N^{3/2}\) scale. Both have limiting variance \(4p(1-p)/(3(2-p)^3)\), and their Gaussian fluctuations have the universal correlation \(1/2\), although each conditional mean is asymptotic to \(N^2/(2-p)\).
