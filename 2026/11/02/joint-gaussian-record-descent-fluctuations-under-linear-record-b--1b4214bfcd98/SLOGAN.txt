Under a record tilt \(\theta_n=\lambda n\) with \(\lambda>0\), the number of left-to-right maxima and the number of descents in a random permutation have an explicit, nondegenerate joint Gaussian limit at scale \(\sqrt n\). Their limiting correlation is strictly negative; the new quantitative result describes joint fluctuations in the linear-record regime, beyond known fixed-bias marginal limits.
