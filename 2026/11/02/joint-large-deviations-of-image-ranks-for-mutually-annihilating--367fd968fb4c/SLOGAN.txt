Uniform ordered pairs of selfmaps with both compositions equal to one constant have an exact joint image-rank enumeration. Their ranks scaled by \(\log n/n\) obey a two-dimensional large-deviation principle with additive rate \(I(x,y)=x-1-\log x+y-1-\log y\) for positive \(x,y\); the already known typical \(n/\log n\) scale is not the novelty claim.
