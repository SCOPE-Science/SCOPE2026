# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each k≥1 indices preferring 1, reorder those cars first, remove initial k occupied slots, map remaining preferences to a parking function of m=n-k cars. Each resulting 1 has k preimages, one of which contributes v for original preference 2. Known A_m(t)=t(t+m)^(m−1) gives the exact bivariate enumerator, including n all-ones case, and conditional PGF (k−1+v)/k · ((n−1+v)/n)^(n-k-1). A common-uniform coupling proves strict conditional stochastic decrease even at k=n, and direct inverse-binomial expectation gives the covariance formula and Poisson limit. I independently enumerated all parking words of lengths 2–5 and recovered every bivariate coefficient. The all-n statement is symbolic, not from finite enumeration.
