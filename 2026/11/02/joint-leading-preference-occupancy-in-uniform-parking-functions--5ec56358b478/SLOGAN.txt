In a uniformly random classical parking function, the number of preferences for spot 2, conditional on exactly \(k\) preferences for spot 1, equals an independent \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/k)\) plus \(\operatorname{Binomial}(n-k-1,1/n)\) for \(k<n\). This yields strict negative stochastic regression and an exact covariance converging to \(2/e-1\), so dependence between the two leading preference counts persists at arbitrarily large size.
