The exact number of nondecreasing self-maps \(f:[n]\to[n]\) satisfying \(f^3=f^2\) with \(|\operatorname{im}f^2|=k\) and \(|\operatorname{im}f|=r\) is \(\binom{k+r-1}{r-k}\binom{n+k-1}{2r-1}\), so strict quasi-idempotents are exactly the cells \(r>k\). A rooted-interval decomposition yields a rational three-variable generating function and a directly enumerated correction from 52 to 53 in the previously published five-point count.
