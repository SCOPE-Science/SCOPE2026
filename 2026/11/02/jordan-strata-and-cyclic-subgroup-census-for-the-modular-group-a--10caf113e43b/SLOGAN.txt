For the regular module of \(k[C_3\times C_3]\) in characteristic three, every element of the augmentation radical has one of exactly six Jordan types; outside the usual nonzero-linear \(\pi\)-points, the transition is controlled by the quadratic discriminant \(b^2-ac\). All six strata are smooth and geometrically irreducible, and their explicit finite-field sizes give the exact five-type census of every order-three subgroup of the normalized unit group, including non-flat subgroups.
