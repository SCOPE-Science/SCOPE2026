In the radical of every Loewy-length-three cyclic Nakayama algebra, left and right regular multiplication have exactly the same Jordan type, with precisely \(\binom n2+3\) types and no type containing exactly \(n-1\) blocks of length three. A two-state transfer matrix computes every finite-field multiplicity and, after replacing field size by the affine-line class, every Jordan-stratum motive and dimension.
