For every connected cyclic graph with \(v\) anchors and exactly \(v+1\) labeled particles, the rational first-homology representation is \(\mathbf1\oplus(e-v)\operatorname{Ind}_{S_2}^{S_{v+1}}\mathbf1\oplus e\operatorname{Ind}_{S_2}^{S_{v+1}}\mathrm{sgn}\). The integral label-invariant cycles correspond to graph cycles, but their images in the unlabeled quotient have exact factorial index \(((v+1)!)^{e-v+1}\), despite the unrestricted first-homology map being onto.
