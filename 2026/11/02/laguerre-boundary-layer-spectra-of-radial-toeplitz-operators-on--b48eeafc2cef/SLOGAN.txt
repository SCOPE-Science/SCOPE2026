For radial Toeplitz symbols vanishing like \(c(1-|z|^2)^\sigma\), the full weighted polyanalytic Bergman singular-value coefficient is exactly a finite Laguerre-matrix spectral moment, with the sharp Schatten threshold \(p>1/\sigma\). At first-order vanishing it simplifies to the universal coefficient \(|c|q(q+\alpha)\) for all polyanalytic orders \(q\ge2\).
