On a complete bipartite graph, the chance that the final new vertex lies across from the start is exactly \(\Pr(C_b\ge C_a)\) for two independent ordinary coupon-collection times. Balanced parts have a sharp excess of \(1/(8n)+o(n^{-1})\) over one half, while a part-size imbalance of order \(n/\log n\) produces a logistic transition.
