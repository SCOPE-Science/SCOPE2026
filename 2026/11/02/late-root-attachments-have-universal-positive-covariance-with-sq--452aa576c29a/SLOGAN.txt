In a uniform random recursive tree with binary broadcast parameter \(\theta=2p-1\), every root attachment after the third vertex has strictly positive covariance with squared total spin, both for copying and anticopying bias. The root-degree–squared-spin covariance converges to \(1/(1-2\theta)\) for \(-1<\theta<0\), grows as \(C(\theta)n^{2\theta}\) for \(0<\theta<1\), and at \(\theta=1/2\) has the sharp coefficient \(2-\pi^2/6\).
