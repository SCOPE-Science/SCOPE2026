An overrelaxed scalar quadratic descent with ideal unbiased final-step lattice rounding has a stationary Gaussian error law at the flip boundary, with variance set by the fractional phase \(\psi(2\theta)\). Every non-half-grid minimizer has diverging single-step error variance, but the exact half-grid minimizer remains fair Bernoulli for all stable overrelaxations; alternating correlations keep long-run averaging variance finite.
