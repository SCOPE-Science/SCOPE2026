Under the fixed-content binary runs-test null, conditioning on more one-runs increases the number of singleton one-runs in the strong likelihood-ratio order, not merely in expectation. The full two-statistic count matrix is TP2, and its covariance is exactly \(2ab^2(a-1)(b+1)/[(a+b)^2(a+b-1)(a+b-2)]>0\) for \(a\ge2,b\ge1\).
