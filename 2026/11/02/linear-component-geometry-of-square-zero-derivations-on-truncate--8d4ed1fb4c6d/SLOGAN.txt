In characteristic \(2\), the square-zero derivations of \(k[t]/(t^N)\) form a reduced locus made of exactly \(\lfloor N/4\rfloor+1\) linear components of dimension \(\lfloor N/2\rfloor\), joined one hyperplane at a time. Their exact number over \(\mathbb F_q\) is \(c q^m-(c-1)q^{m-1}\), while the unreduced parameter scheme carries a genuine square-zero infinitesimal coordinate for every \(N\ge2\).
