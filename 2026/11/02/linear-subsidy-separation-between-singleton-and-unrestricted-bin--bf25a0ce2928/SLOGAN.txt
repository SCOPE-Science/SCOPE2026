On an explicit square binary-goods market with only two agent types, the least subsidy is \(r\) if bundles are unrestricted but \(2r-1\) if each of the \(2r\) agents must receive exactly one good, for every \(r\ge3\). The singleton restriction therefore incurs a sharp linear extra payment of \(r-1\), unlike the square-market coincidence known under continuously distributed random valuations.
