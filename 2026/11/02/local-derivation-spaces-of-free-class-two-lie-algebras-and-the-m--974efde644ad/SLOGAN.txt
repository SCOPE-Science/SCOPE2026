For the free class-two Lie algebra \(L=V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\) on \(n\ge3\) generators, all linear local derivations form a vector space of dimension \(2n^2+n\binom n2\), compared with \(n^2+n\binom n2\) ordinary derivations, over every field. The central block is an induced exterior-square derivation except for one independent identity direction in characteristic two; this yields exactly \(q^{n^2}\) local maps per ordinary derivation over \(\mathbb F_q\).
