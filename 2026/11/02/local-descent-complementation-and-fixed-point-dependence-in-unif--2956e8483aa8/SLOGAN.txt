In a uniform random involution, each adjacent descent is exactly independent of the complete fixed/nonfixed pattern at all other sites. A descent-complementing pairing yields the full bidiagonal fixed-site covariance matrix and shows that the fixed-point count has covariance \(-1+T_{n-1}/T_n\) with total descents, tending to \(-1\), unlike the uniform-permutation boundary-only law.
