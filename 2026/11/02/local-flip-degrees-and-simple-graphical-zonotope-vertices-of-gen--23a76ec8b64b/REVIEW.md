# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For simple generalized theta graphs k>=3 with at most one length-one arm, acyclicity means no two complete paths with opposite terminal directions. A reversed edge creates a cycle exactly when there is an alternative directed path, producing the minority-sign blocked-edge classification; the one direct terminal edge is explicitly handled. Counting the allowed path words yields the claimed all-length degree polynomial; the coefficient for exactly k-1 blocked edges is 2 sum_j prod_{i !=j} ell_i=2 tau(G), while the zonotope has dimension n-1. Independently built directed graphs, tested topological acyclicity and each flip, and reproduced full degree histograms for length lists (2,2,2), (2,3,4), (3,3,3), (1,2,3,4), (1,2,2), (2,2,2,2). The all-family proof is the combinatorial word/alternative-path argument, not a finite histogram.

## originality

PASS

The full individual vertex-degree distribution is distinct from both chromatic-polynomial evaluations giving total acyclic orientations and Grujic's global face counts of zonotopes. A global f-vector generally does not recover a vertex-degree histogram, so the latter does not imply the assessed claim. Special K_(2,k) all-length-two cases are acknowledged as prior; arbitrary heterogeneous path lengths and direct-edge boundary are the asserted new range.

## value

PASS

A complete local flip-degree distribution and number of simple vertices for a natural series-parallel graph family are structural polyhedral combinatorics; 2 tau(G) connects simplest zonotope vertices to spanning trees and the general formula resolves each degree, beyond a single benchmark count.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
