On unweighted friendship networks of maximum degree two without isolated agents, a coalition partition is Nash stable exactly when every agent has a coalition neighbor and adjacent coalitions differ in total size by at most one. On the cycle \(C_n\), the largest possible number of separated pieces of one Nash-stable coalition is \(\max\{1,\lfloor n/4\rfloor\}\), with disconnected stable coalitions first appearing at eight vertices.
