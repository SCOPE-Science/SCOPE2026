For every \(\mathrm{BMO}\) function, shrinking disk automorphisms at almost every boundary point obey the exact Garsia-norm max-law \(\lim_{r\uparrow1}\|f+c(b_{r\zeta}-r\zeta)\|_G=\max\{\|f\|_G,|c|\}\). Consequently the Garsia-norm closed unit balls of \(\mathrm{BMO}\), \(\mathrm{BMOA}\), \(\mathrm{VMO}\), and \(\mathrm{VMOA}\), modulo constants, have no strongly extreme points, even though the norm-one \(\mathrm{VMO}\) vectors are ordinary extreme points.
