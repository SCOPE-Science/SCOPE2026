Every locating-dominating set of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\) is counted by the exact polynomial \((2z+z^2)^n+n(2z+z^2)^{n-1}-2n^2z^{n-1}-2nz^n\) for \(n\ge4\), with explicit smaller boundary cases. For \(n\ge5\), there are exactly \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\) minimum sets, and independently missing sensors at scale \(1/\sqrt n\) exhibit the Poisson reliability limit \((1+c^2)e^{-c^2}\).
