In a uniform random fixed-point-free involution, conditioning on a prescribed interior nonadjacent chord *decreases* expected total descents, but an interior adjacent chord *increases* them; chords touching the two outer labels obey explicit, different affine sign laws. The complete finite covariance map gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(S,D)=(N-2)/(2(N-1))\to1/2\) between the number of short chords and ordinary descents.
