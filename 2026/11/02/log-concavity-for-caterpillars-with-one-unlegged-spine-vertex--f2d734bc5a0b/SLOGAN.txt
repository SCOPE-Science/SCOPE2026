For every caterpillar with exactly one spine vertex lacking a pendant leaf, the ordinary domination polynomial has a log-concave coefficient sequence, with no bound on the spine length or leaf multiplicities. Its exact generating polynomial factors into standard leaf-block terms and one explicitly proved log-concave correction \((1+x)U_a(x)U_c(x)-x^{a+c}\), resolving an infinite one-gap subfamily of the legless-caterpillar question.
