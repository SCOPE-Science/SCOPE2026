For Gaussian copulas mixed over correlations with \(\Pr(R>1-s)\sim cs^a\), joint tail probabilities are asymptotic to \(u\{\log(1/u)\}^{-a}\) times a universal explicit angular profile, even though the ordinary first-order tail copula vanishes. Uniform correlation mixing yields the exact profile \(\min(w_1,w_2)\{1+|\log(w_1/w_2)|/2\}\), resolving the full off-diagonal logarithmic correction.
