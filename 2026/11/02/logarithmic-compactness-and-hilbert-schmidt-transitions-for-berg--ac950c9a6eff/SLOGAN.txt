For standard Bergman-kernel Cesàro operators on \(H^2(\mathbb D)\), logarithmic damping first produces compactness at exponent \(\gamma>0\), but Hilbert–Schmidt membership only at \(\gamma>1/2\). The two-phase transition has the exact universal squared-Hilbert–Schmidt singularity \(\lim_{\gamma\downarrow1/2}(2\gamma-1)\|T_{s,\gamma}\|_{\mathcal S_2}^2=1/(2s-1)\) for every real \(s>1\).
