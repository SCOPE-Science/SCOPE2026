When the two shapes of a Beta law approach the small-sample-variance critical exponent \((n-1)/n\) on the scale \(1/\log(1/\varepsilon)\), the probability of squared deviation at most \(\varepsilon\) has an exact two-endpoint exponential-tilt crossover. Conditional on this rare event, the logarithmic sample-mean position has a tilted-uniform law and the standardized centered residual is an independent uniform Euclidean-ball vector.
