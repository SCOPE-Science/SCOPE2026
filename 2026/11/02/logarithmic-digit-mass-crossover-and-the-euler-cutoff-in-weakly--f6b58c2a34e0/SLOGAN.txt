In a weakly weighted countable affine expanding map, retaining \(\lfloor e^{\tau/c^{1/q}}\rfloor\) branches produces a universal conformal **log-digit distribution** with density \(e^{-z_\tau u}\) on \([0,\tau]\), where \(\int_0^\tau e^{-z_\tau u}\,du=1\); the full system instead has an exponential limit. The dimension crosses its convergence exponent at an exponentially large alphabet size with the sharp Euler correction \(N_*\sim e^{c^{-1/q}-\gamma}\).
