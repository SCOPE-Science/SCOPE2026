For the minimum-of-\(k\)-choices random permutation model, every fixed \(k\ge2\) creates a strict descent deficit of \(\frac{k(k-1)}{2(2k-1)}\log n+O_k(1)\) relative to a uniform permutation, even though the descent count has the same limiting Gaussian scale. A coupling to the Eulerian law has an explicit \(4kH_{n-1}\) mean-error bound and achieves the sharp order \(\Theta_k(\log n)\) in Wasserstein distance.
