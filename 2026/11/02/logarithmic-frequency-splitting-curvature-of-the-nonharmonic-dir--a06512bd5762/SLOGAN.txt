Moving one endpoint frequency of a consecutive Dirichlet exponential sum produces a strictly positive \(\varepsilon^2\log(1/|\varepsilon|)\) singularity in its fixed-circle \(L^1\) norm, with an exact finite-degree coefficient. Across even degrees, that coefficient converges to the universal value \(32(3\pi-7)/(27\pi)\), so the equally spaced spectrum has no finite frequency-Hessian in this direction.
