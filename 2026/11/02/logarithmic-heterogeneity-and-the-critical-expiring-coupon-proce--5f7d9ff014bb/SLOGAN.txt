Coupon frequencies differing from uniformity by only \(1/\log n\) already alter the entire critical-window expiring-collector law: its immigration intensity becomes \(\kappa e^{-c}\), where \(\kappa\) is the limiting average of \(e^{-\theta}\). The resulting completion-time law has an explicit Laplace transform and a strict heterogeneity penalty \(\kappa\ge1\), even though all relative sampling weights approach one.
