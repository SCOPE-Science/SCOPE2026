Minimum dominating sets of every cycle \(C_{3k+2}\) form a class of VC dimension exactly \(2\), yet their Littlestone dimension grows as \(\log_2(3k+2)+O(1)\). The first strict gap in this cycle residue class occurs at \(C_{11}\); the gap is unbounded even though every graph in the family is connected, vertex-transitive, and 2-regular.
