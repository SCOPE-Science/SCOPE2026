When the Friedman replacement probability \(\varepsilon_N\) vanishes on the scale \(1/\log N\), the final two-colour urn proportion converges to an affine-contracted Pólya beta law: \(\tfrac12+e^{-2b\lambda/c}(B-\tfrac12)\), where \(\varepsilon_N\log N\to\lambda\). This gives the exact distributional crossover between persistent beta randomness and deterministic balance, independent of the Friedman same-colour addition.
