For two generation-\(n\) site counts in planar critical branching random walk at distance \(n^{\theta+o(1)}\), with \(0\le\theta<1/2\) and bounded diffusive location, the unconditional correlation converges exactly to \(1-2\theta\). At distinct diffusive-scale sites, the covariance instead has an explicit positive Gaussian-integral limit after multiplication by \(n\), identifying the crossover from strongly shared local ancestry to macroscopic decorrelation.
