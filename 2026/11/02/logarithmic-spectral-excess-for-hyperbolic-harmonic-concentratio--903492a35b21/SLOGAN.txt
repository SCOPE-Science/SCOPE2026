For weighted hyperbolic-harmonic functions on the real four-ball, restriction of mass to a concentric subball has a complete, strictly ordered spectrum with eigenvalues \(\Lambda_m\sim C m^{s+2}T^m\) of multiplicity \((m+1)^2\). Relative to the ordinary harmonic problem, the eigenvalue-counting function acquires the universal excess \(2L^2\log L/\log(1/T)^3+O(L^2)\), where \(L=\log(1/\varepsilon)\).
