# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Uniform first-arrival order reduces winning nontrailing trajectories to Dyck words. Zero-rate separation gives the product Catalan baseline. Conditioning on the unique early slow arrival after j fast events yields a first-order probability 2l*epsilon/(2m-j); the exceptional fast paths are counted by C_r*C_{m-r} at their first depth -1, producing the harmonic sum. All remaining early-slow collisions have O(epsilon^2) fixed-size probability. For m*kappa_m/log m, fixed s=m-r weights tend to C_s/4^s and a summable majorant works on s<=m/2, with bounded remaining tail; sum_{s>=1} C_s/4^s=1. Independent exact four-count-state exponential-clock recursion with rational rates at l=2, m=1,2,3 and epsilon=1/1000 yielded slopes .665827, .702768, .661287 versus predicted 2/3,19/27,149/225, errors of order epsilon. This does not establish a simultaneous size/rate expansion.

## originality

PASS

Primary earlier work addresses two draws per discrete synchronized round, whereas here the 2(m+l) independent first-arrival clocks form one chronological word. Classical Catalan ballot identities supply the baseline, but the two-speed perturbative derivative and nested logarithmic susceptibility are not implied by Long's 2/d asymptotic. Best-of-knowledge PASS with possible earlier weighted-ballot identity risk.
