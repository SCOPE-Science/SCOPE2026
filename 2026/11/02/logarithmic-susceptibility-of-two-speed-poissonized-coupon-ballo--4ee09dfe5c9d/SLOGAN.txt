For two independent Poissonized coupon collectors with \(m\) fast and \(l\) slow equally shared coupon types, the probability that the eventual winner was never behind rises linearly away from perfect fast/slow separation, with an exact positive Catalan–harmonic derivative. At fixed \(l\), that derivative is asymptotic to \(\frac{4l}{l+1}\frac{\log m}{m}\) as \(m\to\infty\); the result is a nested derivative asymptotic, not a joint rate–size limit.
