For a two-state Markov process with unequal switching rates, the small-time minimax thermodynamic mismatch has a sharp negative \(t\log\log(1/t)\) correction, and the optimal probability assigned to the slower state is of order \(1/\log(1/t)\). A rate difference scaled as \(1/\log(1/t)\) creates an explicit continuous crossover to the exactly balanced optimal prior.
