For spatially periodic symmetric stable-like processes whose jump index has finitely many nondegenerate isolated minima, the first nonzero macroscopic limit occurs at the logarithmically corrected scale \(b_n=(n/(\log n)^{d/2})^{1/\alpha_0}\). The stable Lévy intensity is a sum of stationary-density and jump-weight contributions, each divided by the square root of the minimum's Hessian determinant.
