For symmetric random walks with Mittag-Leffler jump rates independently refreshed every period, the strong-fluctuation limit has a sharp logistic-to-geometric first-passage crossover: if \(\alpha\log(x_\alpha^2/\tau_\alpha)\to\kappa\), then the hitting time divided by the refresh period converges to a geometric law on the nonnegative integers with success probability \(1/(1+e^\kappa)\). This captures exponentially changing target distances and refresh times, beyond the previously known fixed-distance mean limit.
