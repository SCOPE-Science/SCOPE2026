# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem's state space is labeled ternary necklaces with directed, cyclic, length-two read multiplicities and without loop digrams. Conservation forces all three oriented edge imbalances equal, giving the unique parameters \(x,y,z,t\). Every admitted nonzero flow is connected on its used vertices and balanced, hence Euler-realizable. This proves the total-profile generating function without assuming that every arbitrary balanced flow on a larger digraph is connected. For individual fibers, two-edge support reduces to binary excursion necklaces; one reciprocal-edge type with positive triangle circulation reduces to cyclic weak compositions. All remaining flow types have explicit inequivalent witnesses, and the insertion operation preserves a distinguishing length-three count. The singleton generating function is an exact sum over disjoint parameter families. Coefficient and normalization checks include \(n=1\) (no allowed word), \(n=2\), rotations, and both directed triangle orientations. The independent standalone enumerator verifies all words through \(n=16\) and balanced tuples through \(n=100\). These are corroborating finite computations; the proof is valid for all positive lengths.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by residual bibliographic risk

The specific novel candidate is the **all-length singleton-fiber classification and exact singleton/profile generating functions for loopless ternary cyclic directed digrams**, not the classical balanced-flow method or the general fact that Euler tours reconstruct reads. The most direct published precursors describe general Markov-type counting or asymptotics of restricted de Bruijn profile vectors, not individual necklace-fiber uniqueness for this six-edge three-vertex constraint. The 2022 and 2023 reconstruction papers use different overlapping-read/trace channels and focus on asymptotic codes. Published records comparing binary two-read spectra and ternary profile rankings do not imply the constrained three-symbol cyclic singleton table or its period-six rational generating function. This is a statement/parameter comparison, not an inference of originality merely from lack of matching titles. Residual risk: an existing exact specialization of Markov-type enumeration or Eulerian-circuit uniqueness theory could already imply the formulation. General balanced-flow theory is explicitly credited; no claim of completely independent discovery is made.

## Value — PASS

The motivating decoding problem is whether complete frequency-only directed adjacent-pair reads determine an underlying nonrepeating ternary circle. The answer distinguishes fully reconstructible profiles from ambiguous ones for every admissible length and every profile, not just at isolated small sizes. A sharp bounded singleton spectrum versus a cubic-capacity spectrum quantifies a genuine reconstruction bottleneck. It also yields the exact finite-length zero-error code size and an efficient decision test. The three-letter loopless constraint is natural in constrained-sequence storage and the minimal strongly connected de Bruijn graph with two- and three-cycles; the exact periodicity is caused by those cycle lengths rather than an arbitrary slice. The result does not claim to solve larger-alphabet or noisy-read models.

## Closest literature and limitations

* Jacquet, Knessl and Szpankowski (2010), *Counting Markov Types*, https://dmtcs.episciences.org/articles/2768, establishes the balanced matrix/Markov-type framework and asymptotic enumeration. Its ternary example includes all diagonal edges, and there is no singleton cyclic reconstruction classification for this constraint.
* Kiah, Puleo and Milenkovic (2016), *Codes for DNA Sequence Profiles*, https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/hmkiah/docs/papers/DNA_seq_profile_ISIT.pdf, studies restricted substring profile vectors and Ehrhart growth. The inspected Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 3.3 address strongly connectedness, growth and quasipolynomials; they do not state this precise six-arc GF/fiber classification.
* Yehezkeally, Bar-Lev, Marcovich and Yaakobi (2022), *Generalized Unique Reconstruction from Substrings*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.04471, analyzes trace codes and rates under partial overlap, rather than the complete fixed length-two cyclic multiset and its singleton classes.
* Wei, Schwartz and Ge (2023), *Reconstruction from Noisy Substrings*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04790, addresses noisy trace reconstruction, not this exact deterministic read profile count.

Published neighboring records on a binary two-multispectrum classification and a ternary two-window profile-ranking extremum are materially different: the former changes alphabet and geometry, while the latter studies order/rank constraints on profile entries rather than multiplicity-fiber uniqueness. No external or independent audit has been performed. The general Euler-tour connection and its classical enumeration results remain acknowledged background.
