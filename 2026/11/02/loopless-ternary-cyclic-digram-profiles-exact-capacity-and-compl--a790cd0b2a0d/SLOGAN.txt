For ternary necklaces forbidding equal cyclic neighbors, the exact number of distinguishable directed two-gram profiles has generating function \((1+z^3)/((1-z^3)(1-z^2)^3)-1\). An explicit classification of every uniquely reconstructible profile shows that their number is eventually periodic modulo \(6\) and never exceeds \(15\), while total profiles grow as \(n^3/72\).
