For every \(n\ge8\) divisible by four, the zero-field antiferromagnetic Ising model on \(GP(n,2)\) has exactly \(4L_{n/2}\) labelled ground states and energy \(-2nJ\). An explicit bijection with cycle independent sets gives every spoke-defect multiplicity, proves zero magnetization and yields residual entropy \((\log\varphi)/4\) per spin.
