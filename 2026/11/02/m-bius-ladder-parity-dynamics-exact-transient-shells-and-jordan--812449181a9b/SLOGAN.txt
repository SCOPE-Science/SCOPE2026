For synchronous binary neighbor-XOR on every Möbius ladder, the only loss of reversibility occurs when the half-circumference is divisible by three, and every transient shell then has an exact closed count. Twisting a circular ladder preserves the periodic-state population but replaces its nilpotent Jordan chains by shorter chains in odd half-circumference and longer chains in even half-circumference, giving a sharp first-entry-time phase change.
