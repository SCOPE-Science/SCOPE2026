## Value — PASS

The Mahler product is a fundamental affine invariant, while the ball-plus-segment family is a canonical continuous interpolation from the Santaló maximizer toward a long cylinder. The exact attainable profile and inverse conditioning quantify that deformation. The distinction between a planar logarithmic tail and higher-dimensional algebraic tails is structural, as is the half-normal transition when aspect ratio scales like inverse square-root dimension. These two independent singular regimes go beyond evaluating one isolated body and give explicit quantitative benchmarks for convex-geometric asymptotics.
