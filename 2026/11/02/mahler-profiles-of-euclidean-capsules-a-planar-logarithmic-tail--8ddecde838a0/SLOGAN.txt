The Mahler volume product of a Euclidean ball thickened by a segment has an exact strictly decreasing all-dimensional profile, a sharp square-root reconstruction boundary, and a special logarithmic second-order tail only in the plane. Its large-dimensional transition takes place when segment half-length is of order \(1/\sqrt d\) and converges to an explicit half-normal Laplace profile, whereas every fixed positive aspect ratio tends to the same normalized limit \(2/\pi\).
