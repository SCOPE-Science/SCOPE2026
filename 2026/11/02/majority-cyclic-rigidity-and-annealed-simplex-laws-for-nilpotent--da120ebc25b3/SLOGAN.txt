A uniformly random nilpotent matrix has only the trivial invariant labeled \(k\)-splittings with probability greater than one half, yet its expected splitting count grows as \(n^{k-1}/((k-1)!Q_\infty(q)^{k-1})\). If a nilpotent matrix and an invariant splitting are selected uniformly **as a pair**, the normalized block dimensions converge to the uniform Dirichlet law on the simplex.
