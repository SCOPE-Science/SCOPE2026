In synchronous binary majority dynamics on a complete graph with at least four mutually disjoint edges deleted, the periodic-state population for even-order graphs is invariant, but removing a perfect matching fuses \(2^m\) balanced states into equilibria. For odd-order graphs, a near-perfect deletion instead creates \(\binom{m}{\lceil m/2\rceil}\) genuine two-cycles, and larger unmatched sets obey an exact modulo-four extinction criterion; every trajectory becomes periodic within three updates.
