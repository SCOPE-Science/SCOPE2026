At the marginal Pareto rate-tail exponent \(1/2\), the mean first-passage time of a period-resampled symmetric half-line walk is finite precisely when its rate-tail logarithmic exponent \(\beta\) exceeds \(1\). A product of \(m\) independent Pareto(\(1/2\)) rate factors gives \(\beta=m-1\), yielding a sharp threshold: three factors restore a finite mean, while one or two do not.
