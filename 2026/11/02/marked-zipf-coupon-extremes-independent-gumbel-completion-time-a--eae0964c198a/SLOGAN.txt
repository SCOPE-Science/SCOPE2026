For independent exponential clocks with power-law decreasing rates \(k^{-\alpha}\), the centered maximum has a Gumbel limit while its maximizing index lies \(n/(\alpha\log n)\) positions from the slow endpoint, with an independent exponential limit. The same joint limit identifies the last unseen coupon in the nonuniform Zipf collector and extends to iid exponential endpoint marks for each fixed number of last coupon types.
