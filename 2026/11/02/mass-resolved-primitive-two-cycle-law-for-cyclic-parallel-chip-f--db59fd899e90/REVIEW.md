# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For a primitive period-two orbit, connected-graph Laplacian conservation forces firing alternation at every site. The stated integer intervals for the first-step chip holdings are both necessary and sufficient. Their local weight products yield the displayed four-state transfer matrix; determinant expansion gives its factorization, and halving the trace correctly identifies the two temporal phases. The complementary-chip involution proves the mass symmetry, while the unique dominant analytic eigenvalue and explicit first two derivatives prove the central limit theorem. Finite independent simulation checks all 349,504 configurations with bounded chip holdings at lengths 3–9, a proven exhaustive domain for period-two states at these lengths. No enumeration is used as a substitute for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with a specific residual risk
Dall'Asta (arXiv:cond-mat/0601267v1, especially pages 3–4) already describes period-two spatial blocks and counts periodic states at the *critical* densities; none of those existence, structural, or boundary results is newly claimed here. The new claim is a single all-density orbit-mass polynomial and its quantitative Gaussian mass law. Jiang (arXiv:1003.0943, sections 1–2) develops boundedness, confinement, and allowable-period techniques but does not derive the proposed cycle mass-resolved law. Semantic searches for the precise mass polynomial, its Pell–Lucas specialization, transfer-matrix equivalents, and cyclic sandpile density enumeration did not reveal an equivalent claim. The 2006 structural classification means an equivalent generating function may still be extractable from prior block arguments, so originality is not independently settled; no earlier explicit equivalent was located in the inspected primary text.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The conserved total energy is the principal parameter of the one-dimensional deterministic fixed-energy sandpile. Resolving the complete finite-volume population of primitive temporal two-cycles **at every energy** answers a natural exact microcanonical counting question that a period-existence classification cannot answer. The explicit spectral law, exact reciprocal coefficient structure, sharp endpoint counts, and Gaussian mass fluctuations provide a motivated quantitative bridge between symbolic configurations and the thermodynamic density picture.

## Scope and comparison limits
The claim is confined to simple labelled cycles of length at least three and to initially primitive two-cycle orbits. It does not classify longer-period configurations or their attraction basins. An unindexed earlier microcanonical enumerator would alter the originality assessment. No independent audit has been performed.
