# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim's domain, classical positivity, stationary-profile requirements and constant weighted matching identity are stated explicitly. The ground-state transform, integration by parts, exact trophic cross-term cancellation, reaction coercivity, scalar logistic upper bounds, parabolic continuation and uniform long-time convergence form a complete proof for all strictly positive initial pairs. The standalone symbolic checker verifies the cancellation and exact heterogeneous example with negative intrinsic predator growth. The argument does **not** use finite computation as proof of the infinite-time theorem, and no uniform exponential convergence claim is made.

## Originality — PASS under inspected evidence
The closest motivating paper is Lou–Xin (2026, arXiv:2610.02630): its full primary HTML was inspected for the exact equations, main Hopf statements and constructions. The 2019 Ni–Shi–Wang primary text (arXiv:1909.00537) establishes weighted Lyapunov global results in the *competitive* sign regime, not the predator–prey opposing-coupling regime. Li–Wu–Dong (2019, DOI:10.1007/s00526-019-1558-4) prove stability for a saturated Crowley–Martin system under strong predator-competition hypotheses; only abstract and article excerpts were accessible. Beretta–Takeuchi (1988) concern patch diffusion with delays. Searches for equivalent matching profiles, detailed balance, entropy, and stronger global theorems in published mathematical records and bibliographic sources found no same-equation implication. This is best-knowledge originality, not historical uniqueness certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem isolates an explicit nonoscillatory structural subfamily of the recently demonstrated heterogeneous Hopf regime: the *entire* positive basin converges and the strict dissipation functional is exact for every positive pair of diffusion coefficients. The example is nonhomogeneous in both equilibrium components, with variable predator-prey exchange and negative intrinsic predator growth everywhere, so the assertion addresses an ecologically relevant and nontrivial stability boundary rather than a homogeneous special case.

## Limits and residual risks
The existence and pointwise match of a positive equilibrium are assumed; this is a sufficient structural obstruction, not a necessary characterization of global stability. More general relative-entropy frameworks might subsume the theorem, and the Crowley–Martin primary article remains partially inaccessible. The sources and comparisons are documented in AUDIT.json. Independent proof or novelty audit has not been performed.
