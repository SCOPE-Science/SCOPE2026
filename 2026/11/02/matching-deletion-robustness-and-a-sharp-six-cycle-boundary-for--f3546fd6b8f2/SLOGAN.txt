Deleting any matching from a complete bipartite graph with both parts of size at least three changes its three-colouring reconfiguration graph only by adding six four-cycle ears per missing edge, except for the six-cycle, where six frozen colourings appear. Outside that unique exception, every colouring has eccentricity \(\lfloor 3(m+n)/2\rfloor\), independent of the number of deleted matching edges.
