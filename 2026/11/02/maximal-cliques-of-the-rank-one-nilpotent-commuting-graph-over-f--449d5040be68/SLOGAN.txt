Every maximal clique in the commuting graph of nonzero rank-one square-zero \(n\times n\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\) is determined by a unique nonzero proper subspace \(W\), consisting of all rank-one maps with image in \(W\) and kernel containing \(W\). This yields exact Gaussian counts, all pairwise clique intersections, and the sharp clique number \((q^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}-1)(q^{\lceil n/2\rceil}-1)/(q-1)\).
