Every maximal commutative subsemigroup of a finite Brandt semigroup is a complete source–sink zero-product block plus maximal abelian subgroups on the unused diagonal positions, or consists entirely of such diagonal subgroups. This yields the exact maximal-clique count \((h+2)^n-2(h+1)^n+2h^n\) and a complete all-clique polynomial for arbitrary finite structure groups.
