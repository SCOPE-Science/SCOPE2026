For the full edge subdivision \(T_b=\operatorname{Sub}(K_{2,b})\), all inclusion-maximal edge-open packings have an exact four-term cardinality polynomial: \(F_b(z)=2z^{2b}+b(b-1)z^4+4bz^{b+1}+2(2^b-2b-2)z^b\) for \(b\ge3\). For \(b\ge6\) their possible sizes are exactly \(\{4,b,b+1,2b\}\), leaving an arbitrarily long gap between \(b+1\) and \(2b\) in connected, bipartite, \(2\)-degenerate graphs.
