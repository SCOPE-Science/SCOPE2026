Every cycle \(C_n\) has maximal-independent-set VC dimension exactly \(\lfloor(n+1)/3\rfloor\), but the first adaptive excess occurs at \(C_{13}\), with VC dimension four and Littlestone dimension five. Disjoint unions of thirteen-cycles yield an unbounded gap \(m\) among vertex-transitive \(2\)-regular graphs, while all smaller cycles have equality.
