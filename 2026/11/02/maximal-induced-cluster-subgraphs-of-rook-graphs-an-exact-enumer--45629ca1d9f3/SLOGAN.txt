The full size distribution of inclusion-maximal induced cluster subgraphs of every rook graph \(K_a\square K_b\) is an explicit labeled star-forest coefficient formula. For \(2\le a\le b\), attainable sizes are exactly \(\{a\}\cup\{b,b+1,\ldots,a+b-2\}\); in particular, \(\mathcal C_{2,b}(z)=bz^2+2^bz^b\) yields arbitrarily long cardinality gaps for inclusion-minimal cluster vertex-deletion sets.
