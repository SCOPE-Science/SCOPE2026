The complete inclusion-maximal induced-matching polynomials of single-edge subdivisions of \(K_n\) and \(K_{a,b}\) are exact Stirling-number surjection sums. In particular, the connected bipartite graph \(I(K_{2,b})\) has maximal induced matchings only of sizes \(2\) and \(b\), giving arbitrarily long gaps in the admissible cardinalities.
