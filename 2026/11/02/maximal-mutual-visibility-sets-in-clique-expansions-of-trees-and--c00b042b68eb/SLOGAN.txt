For a graph formed by replacing every vertex of a tree by a clique, all inclusion-maximal mutual-visibility sets correspond exactly to subtrees whose internal vertices have no neighbors outside, with one omitted vertex per internal clique. For \(P_n\circ K_m\), their complete size enumerator is \(\sum_{j=0}^{n-2}(n-j-1)m^jz^{2m+j(m-1)}\); the maximal-set cardinalities have \(m-2\) missing integers between successive attained sizes when \(m\ge3\).
