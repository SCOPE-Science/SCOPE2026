For two independent uniform upper triangular matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact probability that their product has maximal nilpotency index \(n\), conditional on being nilpotent, is \(\frac{(q-1)^{2n-2}((n+1)q-1)}{q^{n-1}(2q-1)^n}\). At fixed dimension this tends to \((n+1)/2^n\) as the field grows, separating the product-induced Jordan law from the uniform nilpotent law.
