For every prime \(p\ge5\), a quadratic recurrence with nonzero repeated characteristic root modulo \(p\) has exactly maximal period growth: \(\pi(p^k)=m p^k\) at every prime-power level. A degree-two factor of \(X^p-1\) cannot lift from \((X-1)^2\) modulo \(p\) to modulo \(p^2\), excluding a proposed unchanged-period construction; \(p=3\) is a genuine exception.
