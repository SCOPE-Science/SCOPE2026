For approval elections with one voter per edge of a regular graph and half the vertices elected, the minimum payment needed to stabilize transferable-utility CC and PAV is exactly \((n-B)/2\) and \((n-B)/4\), where \(B\) is the graph's maximum bisection and \(n\) its number of edges. Uniform one-unit payments are optimal, and no subsidy is needed precisely when the graph is bipartite.
