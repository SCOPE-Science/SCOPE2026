For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), the maximum failed zero-forcing sets are classified and counted exactly by their minimum white-fort complements. Beyond the previously known extremal cardinality, the count reveals exceptional \(2\times4\), \(3\times4\), and \(4\times4\) shapes and a three-orbit thin-board regime, while for \(3\le m\le n\) and \(n\ge5\) precisely the \(\binom{m}{2}\binom{n}{2}\) rectangular complements remain.
