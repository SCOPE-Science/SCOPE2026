Boundaries of axis-aligned ellipses are a **maximum** VC- and Littlestone-dimension-four class: for every \(n\), some \(n\)-point set has exactly all traces of size at most four, so both shatter functions equal \(\sum_{j=0}^{\min(4,n)}\binom nj\). The proof keeps all four-point conic interpolants elliptical under perturbation of a first-quadrant circle arc while eliminating every five-point incidence.
