For every finite abelian-kernel Frobenius group with a fixed-point-free cyclic complement of order \(m\ge3\), the medialized conjugation quandle remembers only the kernel size \(n\) and \(m\), and its full automorphism group is \((C_m\wr S_{(n-1)/m})\rtimes (\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times\). This collapse produces infinitely many pairs of nonisomorphic groups, with kernel exponents \(2\) and \(4\), having isomorphic medialized conjugation quandles.
