For every nonempty fixed-weight family of binary words forbidding both \(0^r\) and \(1^s\), with \(r,s\ge2\) but not simultaneously \(2\), legal adjacent swaps form a median graph. Every pair has a shortest feasible route of exactly \(\sum_i|p_i-q_i|\) swaps, so simultaneous run limits never force a detour; the excluded two-two case has frozen alternating words.
