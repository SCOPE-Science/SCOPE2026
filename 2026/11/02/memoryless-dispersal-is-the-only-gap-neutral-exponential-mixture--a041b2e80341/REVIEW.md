## Value

**PASS.** Placement of marine reserves under larval dispersal is an established mathematical management problem. The result gives an exact all-budget, all-length, all-rate-mixture criterion for whether the linear persistence indicator is sensitive to spacing, identifying the memoryless kernel as a sharp mathematical boundary and exhibiting an actual invasion-threshold crossover for an otherwise unchanged system. This is more than an arbitrary numerical slice, although it is a theoretical benchmark and cannot be used as a field-validated reserve prescription.
