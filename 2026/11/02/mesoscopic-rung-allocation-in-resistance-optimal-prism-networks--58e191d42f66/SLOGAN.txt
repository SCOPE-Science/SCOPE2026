For an independently weighted circular ladder with fixed prices, the unique conductance allocation minimizing total effective resistance puts a rung-to-ring conductance ratio of order \(n^{-2/3}\), with sharp coefficient \((18P^2/Q^2)^{1/3}\). Its optimum has an explicit \(n^{7/3}\) second-order trace term and beats equal conductances by an asymptotic one-third when the edge prices are equal.
