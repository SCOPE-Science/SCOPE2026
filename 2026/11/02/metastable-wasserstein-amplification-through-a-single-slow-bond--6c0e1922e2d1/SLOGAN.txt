For a reversible random walk on a \(2m\)-vertex unit-distance path with one central jump rate \(\varepsilon\), the peak Wasserstein-one operator norm tends to \(m\) as \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), with an explicit \(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)\) deficit and a sharp next-order coefficient. Its maximizing time is \(\lambda_m^{-1}\log(K_m/\varepsilon)+o_m(1)\); thus transient Wasserstein amplification is unbounded over finite irreducible symmetric birth–death semigroups.
