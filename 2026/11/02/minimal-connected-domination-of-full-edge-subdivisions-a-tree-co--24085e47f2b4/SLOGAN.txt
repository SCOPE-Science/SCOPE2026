Minimal connected dominating sets of a full edge subdivision correspond exactly to connected vertex covers equipped with spanning trees whose leaves see omitted vertices, plus one chosen boundary edge for each omitted vertex. This gives complete Stirling-number polynomials for subdivisions of complete bipartite graphs; in particular, the connected \(2\)-degenerate bipartite graphs \(\operatorname{Sub}(K_{2,b})\) have minimal-connected-domination cardinality spectra containing only \(b+4\) and \(2b+2\), with an arbitrarily long missing interval.
