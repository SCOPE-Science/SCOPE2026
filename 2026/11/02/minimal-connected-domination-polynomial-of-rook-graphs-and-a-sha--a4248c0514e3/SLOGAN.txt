In every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), the inclusion-minimal connected dominating sets are precisely the line-graph images of bipartite trees spanning exactly one shore, with no leaves on the other occupied shore. A Stirling-number formula counts these sets at every cardinality and exhibits an exact gap in their possible sizes whenever the larger board dimension is at least twice the smaller.
