For every power \(C_n^r\) of a cycle, inclusion-minimal dominating sets correspond exactly to cyclic gap words with gaps at most \(2r+1\) and consecutive sums at least \(2r+2\), giving a rational transfer-matrix polynomial that counts them at every cardinality. Every cardinality from \(\lceil n/(2r+1)\rceil\) through \(\lfloor n/(r+1)\rfloor\) occurs, and when \(n\) is divisible by \(r+1\), the number of upper-extremal sets is \(r+1\) or \((r+1)(2r+1)\) according to the quotient's parity.
