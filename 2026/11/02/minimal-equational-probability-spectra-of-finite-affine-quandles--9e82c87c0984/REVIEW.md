## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2026 groupoid spectrum problem naturally asks which finite quasigroup examples attain the smallest nontrivial equational probability set. The theorem completely settles the canonical finite affine-quandle family, with a non-obvious nonprojection **field** criterion that allows repeated irreducible blocks and therefore many nonsimple quandles. It supplies concrete two-variable intermediate-probability witnesses outside the classified locus, and translates a structural criterion into explicit isomorphism and labeled-operation enumerations for all orders. The result is not a claim about every quasigroup, and the enumerations use classical finite-module and irreducible-polynomial facts transparently.
