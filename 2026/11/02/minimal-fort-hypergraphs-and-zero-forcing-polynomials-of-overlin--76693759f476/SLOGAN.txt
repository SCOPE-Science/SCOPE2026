For every \(n\ge4\), the complement of the path \(P_n\) has a completely classified, two-rank minimal-fort hypergraph: its minimal forts are exactly the \(101\)-avoiding triples and the separated pairs of distance-two pairs. This yields \(\mathcal Z(\overline{P_n};z)=z^{n-3}[(n^2-6n+10)+\binom n2z+nz^2+z^3]\), including exactly \(n^2-6n+10\) minimum forcing sets.
