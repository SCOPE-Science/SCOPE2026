For the full single-edge subdivision of every \(K_n\), \(n\ge3\), minimal zero-forcing obstructions are exactly subdivided simple cycles, the original vertex set, and \(n\) specified star-cut forts. This yields a complete zero forcing polynomial from the labeled spanning-forest polynomial and shows that minimum forcing sets correspond bijectively to a chosen original vertex and a spanning tree not centered there, giving \(n(n^{n-2}-1)\) optimum seeds.
