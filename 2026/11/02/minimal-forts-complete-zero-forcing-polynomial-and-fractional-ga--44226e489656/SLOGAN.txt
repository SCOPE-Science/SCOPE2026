For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\) with \(n\ge3\), all minimal forts are exactly single-shore triples and paired-index four-sets. This yields the full zero-forcing polynomial, \(\operatorname{ft}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\), and fort-cover fractional zero forcing \(Z^*(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2n/3\); the integral-to-fractional ratio tends to three.
