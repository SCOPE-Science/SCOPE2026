For every \(n\ge3\), the complete polynomial of inclusion-minimal paired-dominating sets of the three-row rook graph \(K_3\square K_n\) has an explicit three-term formula, distinguishing four-vertex, six-vertex, and two-row spanning configurations. For \(n\ge7\), its attainable cardinalities are exactly \(\{4,6,2\lceil n/2\rceil\}\), so its upper paired-domination number grows while increasingly many even sizes are forbidden.
