# Scientific review

**Correctness:** PASS. The exact covariance follows from the bijection between fixed-weight words and uniform weak compositions and from two one-/two-gap hypergeometric probabilities. The asymptotic coefficient is obtained by first-order falling-factorial expansion, the reentrant criterion by a quadratic discriminant, and the minimal length by the inequality \(rs\ge r+s-1\). The three finite families reduce to explicit polynomial signs, confirmed by a standalone exact-arithmetic checker (1292 assertions). The proof is algebraic, not a claim based on simulated frequencies.

**Originality:** PASS within the search conducted, subject to material priority risk. Oh–Jang (2022) studies univariate exact-run counts in a geometrically varying Bernoulli scheme, not cross-length covariance under conditional fixed counts. Classical urn and run-enumeration papers by Makri–Philippou–Psillakis and Eryilmaz encompass related distributions, but the materials examined do not explicitly state the quadratic reentrant sign diagram or the \((1,9)\) minimum. The classical papers' complete articles were not fully inspected, so an earlier implicit generating-function derivation remains a real risk. Related results on transition-indicator covariance and binary adjacent triples are not stronger than this two-length run-count sign classification.

**Value:** PASS. Cross-length run-count covariance is directly relevant to the covariance matrix of a fixed-margin runs-statistics vector and to multivariate nonparametric run tests. The theorem shows that a fixed-composition constraint does *not* produce uniformly negative cross-frequency dependence: an explicit finite family has two distinct sign reversals, and the all-pairs asymptotic criterion identifies the least total pair of run lengths that can exhibit a robust positive band. This is a structural boundary rather than an arbitrary isolated calculation.

**Limitations and closest literature:** Prior work already provides exact run-distribution machinery; the novel scope asserted here is restricted to the *sign classification* and the exact three-regime witness. The sign on critical asymptotic density lines is not resolved by the leading term, nonuniform ensembles are excluded, and independent audit and full historical priority verification remain outstanding.
