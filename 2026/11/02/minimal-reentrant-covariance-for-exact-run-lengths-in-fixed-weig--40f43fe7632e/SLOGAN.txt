For fixed-weight random binary words, counts of exact success runs can pass from negative to positive and back to negative covariance as the success-to-failure ratio increases. Across all positive run lengths, \((1,9)\) is the smallest pair allowing this strict macroscopic reentrance, with boundaries \(3/2\) and \(3\); exact finite signs \((-,+,-)\) already occur for every \(t\ge10\) at success counts \(t,2t,4t\) and failure count \(t\).
