The unique minimal three-node noise law matching five standard-Gaussian moments produces, in an exact-drift Ornstein–Uhlenbeck step at \(e^{-\lambda h}=1/3\), a stationary distribution with full interval support but maximal total-variation distance from the Gaussian equilibrium. Every stationary joint polynomial moment through degree five and all lag covariances nevertheless match exactly; the first difference is the sixth moment, with deficit \((384/91)v^3\).
