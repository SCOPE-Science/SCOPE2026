# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Every total dominating set occupies every row or every column and has no isolated selected cell. The private-neighbor criterion reduces inclusion-minimality to row-degree and column-degree restrictions. The all-shores-covered exceptional cases are classified separately and reconciled with the disjoint row-complete/row-incomplete cases. Every case is bijectively enumerated, including the \(n=3\) exception and the \(n=6\) coefficient collision. Exhaustive direct subset enumeration over five orders agrees with all coefficients (2,396,667 subsets). The general theorem is proved combinatorially; computation only corroborates.

## Originality
PASS, with publication-priority uncertainty. Pan et al. (arXiv:1910.02431) study computational complexity of total edge domination and trees; their general lower-parameter results do not imply the full minimal-set size spectrum of \(K_{3,n}\). Bahadır et al. (arXiv:2010.02341) characterize well-totally-dominated graphs and use minimal-transversal duality, but that abstract framework does not determine these coefficients. Brešar et al. (arXiv:2310.07432) establish upper-total-domination/zero-forcing inequalities and analyze equality structures, not this cardinality distribution. Golovach et al. (DOI:10.1016/j.dam.2014.12.010) give enumeration algorithms for chordal bipartite graphs, not a rook-graph closed form. Relevant indexed research records and available preceding claims revealed no equivalent stronger statement. Search incompleteness and possible unindexed formulas remain residual risks.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Inclusion-minimal total domination is sensitive to internal selected-set adjacencies, unlike ordinary domination. Even at width three it admits four structurally different cardinality layers, exceptional small orders, a complete labeled polynomial, and an unbounded missing-cardinality interval as the other dimension grows. This exact classification informs extremal and enumerative total domination beyond the known minimum parameter and generic enumeration algorithms; no broad complexity or arbitrary-width result is claimed.

## Limits
Only \(K_3\square K_n\) with \(n\ge3\), labeled cells, and inclusion-minimal total dominating sets. No independent audit or publication-priority certification has been performed.
