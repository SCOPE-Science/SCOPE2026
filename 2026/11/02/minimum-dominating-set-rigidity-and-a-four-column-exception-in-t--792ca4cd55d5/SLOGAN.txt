Every minimum dominating set of the two-token graph \(F_2(K_{3,n})\) is one of \(3\binom n2\) canonical sets, except at \(n=4\), which has an additional orbit of exactly \(36\) minima. Thus the exact minimum-set count is \(3\binom n2+36\,\mathbf1_{\{n=4\}}\) for every \(n\ge3\), sharpening the previously known domination number.
