# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
The proof identifies the joint density of the minimum and its renormalized residual simplex, including the Jacobian. Uniformity makes the residual shape independent of the minimum; for other symmetric Dirichlet shapes, endpoint conditional means differ by a strictly nonzero quantity. Beta and Dirichlet exact moments give the stated regression, covariance and correlation. The standalone exact rational checker passed 1192 tests; these tests support the algebra but do not replace the general density proof.

## Originality: PASS, with a priority risk
The primary comparison is Royen (arXiv:1008.4059v1, 2010), which derives marginal quadratic Dirichlet distributions without the minimum-selected independence characterization. Sakowicz and Wesołowski (2014) study neutralities with respect to fixed index partitions, not the order-selected minimum. Pyke (1965) is a broad survey of uniform spacings and its full text was not accessible for definitive comparison. Targeted literature searches did not identify an equivalent theorem. Historical priority remains uncertain; the review does not certify uniqueness.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The result characterizes the uniform Dirichlet law using independence of only two scalar statistics and gives an exact conditional pivot for Greenwood's test after a shortest-spacing-based screening decision. This addresses a natural interaction between a global dispersion test and an extreme-spacing diagnostic; the exceptional dimension and nonuniform-shape boundary are sharp. It is not merely a recomputation of Greenwood's marginal distribution.

## Scope and residual limitations
The characterization is internal to symmetric Dirichlet distributions; no arbitrary-simplex-law characterization or alternative-hypothesis calibration is implied. The original sources not accessible in full remain a historical-priority risk. No external validation has been performed.
