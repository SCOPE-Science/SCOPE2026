For every cyclically five-edge-connected cubic graph of girth at least five, the line graph's minimum nontrivial odd-neighborhood support has size five, and every minimizer comes from a single edge, a pentagon, or (only at ten base vertices) a perfect matching. In particular, a fullerene on \(n\) vertices has exactly \(3n/2+12\) minimum-weight graph-state stabilizer elements, with the twelve additional elements corresponding to its pentagons.
