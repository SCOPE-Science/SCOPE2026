For every \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge6\), the complements of minimum three-path vertex covers in \(C_m\square K_n\) choose two labels in each cycle fiber, with neighboring fibers choosing disjoint pairs. The exact number of these covers on labelled vertices is \(\binom{n-2}{2}^m+(n-1)(3-n)^m+n(n-3)/2\), given by the trace spectrum of the two-subset Kneser graph.
