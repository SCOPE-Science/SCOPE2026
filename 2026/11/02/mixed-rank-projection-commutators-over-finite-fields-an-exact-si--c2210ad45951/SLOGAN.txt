For every finite field, the full joint product-trace and commutator-similarity distribution of a rank-one algebraic projection against a projection of any interior rank \(2\le r\le n-2\) has a seven-row closed formula. With \(r\) fixed and ambient dimension growing, the commutator rank converges to \(\operatorname{Binomial}(2,1-q^{-r})\), exposing a rank-dependent compatibility law.
