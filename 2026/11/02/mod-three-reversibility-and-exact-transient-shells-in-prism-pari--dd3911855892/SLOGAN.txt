Synchronous binary neighbor-XOR on the prism \(C_n\square K_2\) is reversible exactly when \(3\nmid n\); otherwise its complete transient-shell population is governed by the power of two dividing \(n\). On dyadic rims, a single family of rung edges turns two nilpotent rings into a reversible system with an exact, nontrivial power-of-two orbit census.
