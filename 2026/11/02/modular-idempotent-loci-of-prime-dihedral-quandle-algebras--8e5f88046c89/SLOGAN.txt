For every odd prime \(p\), the idempotent scheme of the dihedral quandle algebra over any field of characteristic \(p\) is one isolated point together with an affine space of dimension \((p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\). This yields exactly \(1+q^{(p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))}\) idempotents over \(\mathbb F_q\), with higher-dimensional families at \(p=17\) and \(p=31\).
