For every prime \(p\ge5\), every scalar latin Alexander quandle on \(\mathbb F_p^d\), and every coefficient field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic \(p\), the full quandle algebra has exactly \(1+q^{r(p^d-1)/(p-1)}\) idempotents, where \(r\) counts exponents \(j\in\{1,\ldots,p-2\}\) satisfying \(\lambda^j+(1-\lambda)^j=1\). The nonzero idempotents form a canonical product of elementary-abelian eigenspaces and, under quandle multiplication, a product of explicitly parameterized latin Alexander quandles; at \(p=7\), different scalars already yield \(8\) versus \(50\) total idempotents in dimension one.
