# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Every derivation is uniquely determined by its image of the cyclic generator. Its action is \(a_1N+\sum_{j=2}^n a_jS^{j-1}\), and the evaluation image at a vector with first nonzero index \(t\) is \(V_t\) or \(V_{t+1}\) according to whether \(t\) is invertible in the field. This proves both necessity and sufficiency of the modular diagonal conditions; a fixed cyclic generator proves nonlinear two-local rigidity. Exhaustive small finite-field checks supplement the proof.

## Originality
PASS with residual historical uncertainty. The ordinary derivation formula is previously established, and the 2026 complex-field local-derivation theorem already gives the characteristic-zero triangular space. The positive-characteristic deleted-diagonal formula and arbitrary-field two-local rigidity are not covered by those statements, nor by the associative-algebra analogue. Targeted searches found no directly equivalent general modular formula. This is a best-knowledge assessment, not a certification of global publication priority.

## Value
PASS. The classification resolves an intrinsic algebraic-reflexivity question in an unbounded family, supplies an exact finite-field census, and identifies a sharp modular degeneration at indices divisible by the characteristic. It shows that pairwise compatibility restores full derivation rigidity even when pointwise compatibility has many non-derivations. This distinction is structural rather than a fixed-dimensional numerical increment.

## Limits
The result is limited to the specified right Leibniz family. The characteristic-zero case and ordinary-derivation formula are known. Complete publication-priority verification and independent mathematical review are outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
