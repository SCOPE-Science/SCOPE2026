For every odd prime \(p\ge5\) and field \(K\) of characteristic \(p\), all nonzero idempotents of the dihedral-quandle algebra \(K[R_p]\) form exactly the connected Latin core quandle of \(K^d\), where \(d=(p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) the algebra has precisely \(q^d+1\) idempotents including zero, revealing large modular idempotent families invisible in the integral-ring classifications.
