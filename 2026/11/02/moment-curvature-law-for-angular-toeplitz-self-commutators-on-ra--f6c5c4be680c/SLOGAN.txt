For angular-winding Toeplitz operators on radial analytic Hilbert spaces, the decay of a positive self-commutator is governed exactly by the curvature of the logarithmic moment function: if \(g''(x)\sim c x^{-\gamma}\) and \(g'''(x)\sim-\gamma c x^{-\gamma-1}\), then \(s_j\sim (\gamma cm^3/4)j^{-\gamma-1}\). This gives sharp, distinct Schatten boundaries \(p>1/3\) for weighted disk Bergman spaces and \(p>1/2\) for generalized power-Fock spaces.
