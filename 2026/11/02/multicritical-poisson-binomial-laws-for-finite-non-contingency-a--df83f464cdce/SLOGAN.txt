For independent uniformly coloured equal-size \(\mathsf{S5}\) equivalence classes, the atom count of the generated non-contingency Boolean algebra has an exact support-occupancy law. With fixed \(q=2^k\) colours and both class number and class size growing, distributional convergence is equivalent to convergence of every \(m(s/q)^r\) intensity and even to convergence of the expected atom count; every nonconstant limit is an explicit shifted binomial law arising from a Poisson support threshold.
