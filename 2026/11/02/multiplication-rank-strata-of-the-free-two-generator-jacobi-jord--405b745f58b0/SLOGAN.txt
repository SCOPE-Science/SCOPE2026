For the free Jacobi–Jordan algebra on two generators in characteristic different from \(2\) and \(3\), multiplication by an element has rank only \(0,1,2,3,5\), with rank \(4\) impossible. Its complete rank strata are controlled by one binary-quadratic discriminant, yielding exact finite-field counts \(q,\ q(q^2-1),\ q^3(q^2-1),\ q^5(q-1),\ q^6(q^2-1)\) for the five possible ranks.
