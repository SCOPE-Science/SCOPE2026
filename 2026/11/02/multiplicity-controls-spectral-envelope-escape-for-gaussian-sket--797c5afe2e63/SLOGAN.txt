For a two-level symmetric matrix, a Gaussian-sketched one-vector Rayleigh quotient escapes the exact spectral interval with probability decaying as \(k^{-r/2}\), where \(r\) is the smaller eigenvalue multiplicity, while its expected overshoot decays as \(k^{-r/2-1}\) with sharp explicit constants. At any fixed sketch size \(k\), the overshoot has an exact positive \(t^{-k}\) tail, so its moments exist exactly below order \(k\).
