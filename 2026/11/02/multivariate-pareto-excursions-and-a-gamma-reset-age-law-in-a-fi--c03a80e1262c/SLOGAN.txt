A stationary Brownian gas whose first threshold hit resets every particle has fixed-population, far-left \(k\)-point tails proportional to \(R^{-(N-2)}(\sum_{i=1}^k a_i^2)^{1-N/2}\), so simultaneous extreme excursions remain strongly dependent. Conditioned on one far-left excursion, the inverse elapsed reset age converges to a Gamma law of shape \(N/2-1\), and stationary absolute moments exist exactly below order \(N-2\).
