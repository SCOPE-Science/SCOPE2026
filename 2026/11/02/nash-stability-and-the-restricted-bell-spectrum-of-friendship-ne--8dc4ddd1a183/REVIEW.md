# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The proof establishes both directions of the Nash characterization by showing that a split petal has a strictly profitable repair, and that every intact-petal partition has only two potentially beneficial deviation types. The hub's inequality gives the exact cutoff \(t\le s\), which also removes every profitable peripheral move. The restricted Stirling count is a direct labeled-choice/set-partition bijection; welfare follows by exact edge counting. The unconditional optimum and unique worst equilibrium have separate complete inequalities. A separate exact-integer partition enumerator successfully checked all \(700651\) labeled partitions for the five smallest family members. The finite tests do not certify the infinite theorem; the general argument does.

## Originality — PASS (subject to residual priority risk)

The closest inspected literature includes the 2023 block-graph welfare algorithm of Hanaka, Ikeyama and Ono and the 2018 Nash-stability analysis of Bilò and coauthors. The former describes an optimal-clique/star structural existence theorem and a dynamic program rather than all Nash-stable partitions; the latter gives general Nash existence and broad inefficiency bounds, but no inspected exact windmill enumeration. The nearest indexed mathematical records involving friendship/windmill graphs concern dissociation and fractional edge-resolution, mathematically different graph invariants. Related results on bipartite coalition blocking and double-star distance games concern different models and do not imply this theorem. Searches were conducted for both friendship/windmill aliases, the exact payoff model, stability criterion, multiplicity formula and welfare extremum. No equivalent implication was identified; absence of a search hit is not proof of novelty. Uninspected niche literature can still affect priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is an all-size Nash-spectrum theorem for a natural diameter-two family of block graphs, with an exact refined counting formula, every possible equilibrium welfare layer and tight PoA/PoS. The integer cutoff represents an interpretable competition between group size and an attractive hub. The restricted Bell spectrum captures stable but internally disconnected coalitions even in a locally clustered friendship network, and the exact welfare comparison exhibits a sharp best-versus-worst equilibrium divergence. Those statements are stronger and more informative than another isolated equilibrium or a routine numerical count.

## Scope and limits

The standard self-inclusive fractional utility, strictly profitable unilateral Nash moves, unweighted triangular petals, and unrestricted coalitions are essential. The results do not assert uniqueness of Nash equilibrium, independent review, or extension to modified fractional games or general block graphs. Source priority remains a bounded best-knowledge assessment.
