On the friendship graph \(F_m=K_1\vee(mK_2)\), every Nash-stable fractional hedonic partition consists of a hub holding \(s\) whole petals and outside blocks of at most \(s\) petals, giving an exact restricted-Bell enumeration of all equilibria. Optimal welfare is \(m+1\) and is Nash attainable, yet the grand coalition is the unique worst equilibrium, with \(\operatorname{PoA}=(m+1)(2m+1)/(6m)\).
