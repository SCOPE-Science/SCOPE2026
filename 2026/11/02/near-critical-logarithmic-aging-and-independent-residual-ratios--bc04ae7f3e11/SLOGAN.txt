In the critical window \(\beta_N=1+c/\log N\) of the Zipf disaster chain, the logarithmic age acquires the exact tilted-uniform density \(e^{-cu}/\int_0^1e^{-cs}\,ds\). Independently, the residual lifetime divided by the current age converges to a Pareto-II law with survival \(1/(1+v)\); the untilted \(c=0\) age marginal is classical.
