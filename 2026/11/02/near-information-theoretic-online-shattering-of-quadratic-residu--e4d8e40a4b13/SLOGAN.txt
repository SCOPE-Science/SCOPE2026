The Littlestone dimension of nonzero quadratic-residue translates over every odd finite field is within three of the information-theoretic maximum: \(\lfloor\log_2 q\rfloor-3\le\operatorname{Ldim}\le\lfloor\log_2 q\rfloor\). An exact quadratic-character second moment supplies almost-balanced queries for every adaptive version space, without asserting a corresponding near-maximal ordinary VC dimension.
