At the first length beyond minimal binary circular subsequence universality, admissibility is exactly the condition that the cyclic unequal-adjacency paths contain one even-length path and all remaining paths have odd length. This yields \((2k+1)2^k\) labeled words, \(2^k\) primitive necklaces, and \(2^{k-1}+2^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\) bracelets for every integer \(k\geq1\).
