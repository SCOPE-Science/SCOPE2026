# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The fixed-content condition forces a unique missing symbol for every feasible longest read, making the read-incidence graph a disjoint union of cyclic word-rotation orbits. The proof explicitly derives the vertex equations \(b_i=a_{i-1}+a_i\), the odd-cycle uniqueness, and the even-cycle alternating integer kernel with its nonnegativity interval. Canonical representatives give both exact profile and singleton generating functions; Möbius inversion supplies the primitive necklace exponents. The minimal-period argument constructs a primitive reduced-content word and proves a sharp first ambiguity threshold. Direct enumeration of \(602{,}425\) source multisets supports but does not replace these all-parameter arguments. A possible scope error would be to discard read multiplicities or allow different content vectors; neither is done.

**Originality — PASS (self-review).** The 2021 multi-strand paper formalizes the full-substring read profile and develops asymptotic codes; the fixed-content universal-cycle paper establishes the shorthand/read graph and necklace-cycle setting. Those ingredients alone do not state the image cardinality or singleton generating function for pooled repeated-source multisets. The already obtained single-marker parity law is explicitly subsumed, not attributed as new; the new general fixed-content statement involves arbitrarily many even primitive necklace cycles and their product exponents. Primary sources on prefix/suffix **compositions**, including arXiv:2110.02352 and arXiv:2411.03776, use a different observation map. Searches of related cyclic words, multistring reconstruction, graph incidence and exact profile counting did not reveal the same theorem. Residual risks include an equivalent graph-theoretic result under different terminology and results outside accessible indexed literature. Searches are not a proof of priority.

**Value — PASS.** The fixed-Parikh-vector restriction is natural for constant-composition storage and constrained multistrand sequencing. The result is a complete infinite-family classification of observational information: exact maximal zero-error code size at every pool size, exact singleton-profile count, full formula for inverse fiber cardinalities, and a sharp first ambiguous pool size with its number of ambiguous observations. The period-based obstruction is structural and varies nontrivially with repeated alphabet content; it is not an arbitrary finite slice.

**Closest literature and limits.** Yehezkeally–Marcovich–Yaakobi (arXiv:2108.11725, inspected complete six-page version) supplies the model, not these exact counts. Sawada–Williams (fixed-content universal-cycle PDF, sections on shorthand and necklace cycles) supplies cyclic background. Yehezkeally et al. (arXiv:2210.04471) develop generalized code bounds. Gabrys et al. (arXiv:2110.02352) and Yang–Chen (arXiv:2411.03776) concern composition observations, not full-string longest reads. No independent expert priority check has been made. The theorem requires all source words to have the same known content and all longest reads to be observed with their multiplicities.
