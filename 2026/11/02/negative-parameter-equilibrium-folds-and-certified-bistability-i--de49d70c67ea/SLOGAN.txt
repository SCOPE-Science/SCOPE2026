The negative-parameter ETM-3D flow has exactly two real equilibrium-fold parameters, with a complete nonzero-equilibrium census of \(4\), \(2\), and \(0\) branches across the intervening ranges (and specified collision exceptions). At \(\rho=-2\), its only two nonzero equilibria are both hyperbolic attractors, giving rigorous bistability outside the originally studied positive-parameter domain.
