For a uniformly chosen rooted plane tree with \(n\ge2\) edges, the covariance between its root's number of children and the sum of all vertex depths is exactly \(\bigl(3\cdot4^n/C_n-(2n+1)(n+3)\bigr)/(n+2)\), where \(C_n=\binom{2n}{n}/(n+1)\). It is strictly negative for every size, and equals \(-2n+3\sqrt{\pi n}-3+O(n^{-1/2})\).
