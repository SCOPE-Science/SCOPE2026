At maximal symmetric cross-rejection, the fully mobilized two-candidate map has a continuum of exactly neutral period-two states at each of two threshold exponents, \(\lambda=1\) and \(\lambda=1/2\), with all strictly interior noncritical amplitudes drawn either toward equal candidate support or toward alternating complete dominance. Near either exceptional exponent, the finite-amplitude dynamics obey an explicit slow-flow law and a sharp \(1/|\delta|\) passage-time coefficient, beyond local linear stability.
