# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Fixing one rank-one idempotent by conjugation and parametrizing the other as a normalized dyad gives the exact cubic commutator identity. The proof partitions all parameter solutions without double counting: the scalar-product cases \(a=0\), \(a=1\), and \(a\notin\{0,1\}\) determine the nilpotency strata, and all four finite-field counts sum to the independent rank-one idempotent count. The direct standalone checker passed eight exhaustive ordered-pair enumerations, including a degree-two field extension.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Britnell–Wildon (arXiv:1001.0811, primary full text inspected) classifies when matrix similarity classes commute; its theorems do not count the nilpotency index of commutators of rank-one idempotents. Radjavi–Rosenthal and Drnovšek–Radjavi–Rosenthal characterize implications and realizability of idempotent commutators, not a finite-field four-class labelled-pair census in the material obtained. An earlier finite-field classification of idempotent pairs with idempotent product is a mathematically related but different predicate, and its rank-refined counts do not directly supply the nonnilpotent or index-three commutator counts. Direct full-text checking of the two 2002 papers was not possible, and publication priority remains uncertain.

**Value — PASS.** The commutator is the natural Lie bracket inside a matrix algebra; nilpotence and its index are intrinsic invariants. The result produces a complete uniform enumeration for all matrix dimensions and all finite fields and identifies the sharp minimal dimension for cubic nilpotence. The cubic identity explains the underlying geometry; the enumeration quantifies the index-three phenomenon rather than merely asserting existence. This is not an arbitrary small-field census.

The claims concern labelled rank-one idempotent pairs, not arbitrary idempotent ranks or a conjugacy-class classification. Finite verification checks examples, not infinite quantifiers. Full historic publication priority and independent mathematical validation have not been established.
