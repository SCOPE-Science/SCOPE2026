For ordered pairs of rank-one idempotents over every finite field, the commutator has an exact four-class census: zero, nonzero square-zero, exact nilpotency index three, and nonnilpotent. The universal cubic identity \(C^3=\operatorname{tr}(PQ)(\operatorname{tr}(PQ)-1)C\) shows that index three appears sharply in matrix dimension three and higher.
