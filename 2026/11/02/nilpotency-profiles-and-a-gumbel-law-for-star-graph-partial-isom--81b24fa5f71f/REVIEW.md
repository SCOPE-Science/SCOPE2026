# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. All distance-preserving partial injections of the star fall into four disjoint cases determined by whether the center is absent, fixed, moved by a singleton, or exchanged with a leaf in a two-point domain. Exactly two center-moving cases can be nilpotent. The remaining nilpotents are directed path forests; the exponential formula gives the full joint rank/index profile. The local-limit argument proves both asymptotic conclusions with the required centering and scaling. Independent direct enumeration of partial injections and distances confirms all joint profiles through five leaves (`VERIFY_OK`). Finite cases are corroboration, not a proof of the limit law.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Nilpotency indices and ranks are intrinsic semigroup invariants, not arbitrary computed slices. This complete bivariate classification determines all nilpotent strata for every star size. It shows that center-moving nilpotents have bounded index, whereas typical nilpotent index diverges at the explicit \(\sqrt m\log m\) scale, and gives an extreme-value distribution rather than only a first moment or a finite table. The limiting nilpotent density quantifies how rare these elements become within the monoid.
