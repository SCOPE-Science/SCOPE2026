Nilpotent partial isometries of the star \(K_{1,m}\) have a complete joint rank-and-nilpotency-index formula: all but two explicit center-moving families are directed path forests on the leaves. Their density among all partial isometries is asymptotic to \(1/(2\sqrt m)\), while the nilpotency index of a uniformly chosen nilpotent has a Gumbel limit after centering by \(\tfrac12\sqrt m\log m\) and scaling by \(\sqrt m\).
