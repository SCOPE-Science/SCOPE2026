For a uniformly dissipative upper-triangular Jordan family, diagonal-Schur exponential Euler and two-stage ETD2 are positive strict contractions yet have sharp nonzero fixed-step stiff-limit defects of \(\rho^k\) and \(\rho^{2k}\), respectively, until the chain's nilpotent depth is exhausted; after ETD2 crosses that depth, the remaining error has an exact inverse-stiffness power law. The first one-step defect arises in dimension two, the second in dimension three; neither stability nor positive coefficients guarantee stiffness-uniform fast-mode annihilation.
