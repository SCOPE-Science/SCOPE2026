Over \(\mathbb F_2\), a lollipop graph and two disjoint paths have identical adjacency characteristic polynomials, yet can have different nilpotent Jordan chains and therefore different transient dynamics under synchronous neighbor-XOR. An exact all-parameter block classification shows that for a cycle and pendant length both equal to \(2^k\), the lollipop's maximum transient is \(2^{k-1}\) rather than \(2^k-1\) for its spectrally matched paths.
