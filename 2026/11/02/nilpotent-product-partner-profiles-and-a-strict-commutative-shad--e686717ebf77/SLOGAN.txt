For every finite unital ring, the full distribution of the probability that a fixed random element has a nilpotent right-product partner is an explicit Artin–Wedderburn rank statistic, unchanged by the Jacobson radical. It has the same mean as a canonical commutative shadow but is strictly smaller in convex order precisely when the semisimple quotient is noncommutative; for equal-order rings \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\) and \(\mathbb F_{q^2}[t]/(t^2)\), the propensity-variance ratio is exactly \(1/(q+1)\).
