The precise signature is equality plus a ternary C(x,y,z) with repeated triples counted collinear, and quantifier rank <=5. The recursive extension-profile T_i on ordered tuples is equivalent to a five-round Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé game, where multiplicities of extension types are irrelevant. For four-point caps only the six pair-line singleton extensions, none, and up to three opposite-pair crossings remain; the planar affine coordinates (0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(u,v) yield opposite-pair parallelities v=1,u=1,u+v=0. These agree with the dimension-splitting proof; the conclusion for arbitrary prime powers is from the coordinate identities and counting, not finite interpolation.
