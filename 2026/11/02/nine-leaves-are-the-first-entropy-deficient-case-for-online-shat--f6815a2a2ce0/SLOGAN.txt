For leaf-to-leaf paths of an arbitrary finite tree, nine is the first number of leaves for which no tree can reach the counting upper bound on Littlestone dimension: the extremal value is \(4\), rather than \(\lfloor\log_2\binom{9}{2}\rfloor=5\). Binary caterpillars attain that bound for every leaf count from two through eight, and a ten-leaf caterpillar again reaches the entropy ceiling at depth five, while the VC dimension is always two from four leaves onward.
