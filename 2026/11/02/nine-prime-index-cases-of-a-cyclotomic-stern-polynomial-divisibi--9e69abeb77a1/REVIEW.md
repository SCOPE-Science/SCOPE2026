# Review of the cyclotomic divisibility classification

## Correctness

PASS. The source recurrence agrees with the Dilcher–Stolarsky convention. The quadratic root identity gives \(\overline{A_n}(\xi)=\alpha^n+\beta^n\); absolute trace one makes the two roots conjugate and the ratio norm one. The complete prime factorizations and 22 nonzero residue certificates prove full order at all nine primes. Integer factors reduce to polynomial factors modulo two, including the negative-sign factors.

## Originality

PASS within accessible evidence. The 2014 primary paper expressly labels the necessity direction Conjecture 2; its Section 6 gives constructive sufficient families, not a necessity theorem at these nine prime parameters. Relevant later literature and mathematical-record searches do not reveal an equivalent certificate. The superficially similar 2018 divisibility article studies \(B_n\), a distinct sequence. Residual risk: a later unindexed proof of the whole conjecture, or another computation/finite-order table, may already cover the nine-parameter slice. Absence of search results is not priority proof.

## Value

PASS. The motivator is a specific published conjecture about roots of unity in a natural recursive \((0,1)\)-polynomial family. An exact finite-field test at a fixed prime extends to **every** positive polynomial index, so the result proves infinitely many nonoccurrence assertions at each of nine natural parameters. It reaches the large sharp required modulus \(2^{58}+1\) at \(p=59\), and provides an independently reusable exact certificate method. This is not an arbitrary initial-index search or a routine refactoring of the source's sufficiency theorem.

## Scope and residual risks

The nine prime values do not establish all-prime Conjecture 2. No sufficiency converse over integer polynomials is claimed. The finite computation has been rerun from the attached standalone code; independent mathematical audit is outstanding. Historic literature coverage remains incomplete.
