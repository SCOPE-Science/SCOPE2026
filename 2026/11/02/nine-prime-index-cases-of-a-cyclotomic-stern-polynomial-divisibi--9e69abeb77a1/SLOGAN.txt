For the first nine primes \(p\) with \(2\) primitive modulo \(p\), any Stern \((0,1)\)-polynomial divisible by \(\Phi_p(X)\) or \(\Phi_p(-X)\) has index divisible by \(2^{p-1}+1\). Exact characteristic-two multiplicative-order certificates establish this necessity for every positive index, not merely a tested range of indices.
