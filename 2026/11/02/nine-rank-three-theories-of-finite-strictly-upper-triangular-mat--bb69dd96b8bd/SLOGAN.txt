For the nilpotent semigroups formed by zero and the strictly upper-triangular matrix units on \(n\) ordered indices, first-order logic with relational multiplication at quantifier rank three has exactly nine theories: indices \(1\) through \(8\) are all distinct, and every \(n\ge9\) is equivalent. The sharp smallest-representative bound is \(37\) elements, despite unbounded nilpotency index.
