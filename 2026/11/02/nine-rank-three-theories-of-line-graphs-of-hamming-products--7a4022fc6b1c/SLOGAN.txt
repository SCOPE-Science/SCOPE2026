All line graphs of finite Cartesian products of nontrivial complete graphs have exactly nine first-order theories at quantifier rank three, even when factor sizes and dimension vary without bound. In particular, \(L(K_2^{\square3})\), \(L(K_3\square K_3)\), and the line graph of every product with at least three factors agree at this depth; the smallest universal representative bound is the sharp value \(16\) vertices.
