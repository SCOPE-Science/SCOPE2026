## Mathematical value — PASS

A thick partial geometry is a natural encompassing incidence-geometry class, not an arbitrary isolated finite parameter family: it contains generalized quadrangles, nets, transversal designs, affine and projective planes, and Steiner-derived examples. Exactly nine noncounting three-variable theories in the stronger named-collinearity/concurrence language give a complete, sharp classification, with quantifier-rank three already separating all classes and unrestricted quantifier depth adding nothing. The result explains an expressive-power boundary across structures with very different degrees, sizes and symmetry and relates incidence geometry to finite-variable model theory. It is stronger than a recalculation of numerical intersection parameters, while its limitations are explicit.
