The Dickson–Guralnick–Zieve curves over \(\mathbb F_q\), for \(q\in\{3,5,17,19,31,47\}\), have no \(\mathbb F_{q^7}\)-points. Frobenius must move any long automorphism orbit through seven distinct orbits because the automorphism group has no element of order seven; the resulting point-count lower bound contradicts Hasse–Weil.
