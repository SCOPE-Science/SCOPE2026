For a four-heading particle in an isotropic harmonic trap, the optimal snapshot estimate of the current propulsion direction uses only the four sectors separated by the diagonals \(x=\pm y\), unchanged by tumbling rate or independent symmetric strictly unimodal observation noise. Its exact success probability is a squared beta–Gaussian integral, and the fast-tumbling information advantage crosses sharply from order \(\beta^{-1/2}\) without thermal noise to order \(\beta^{-1}\) at fixed positive thermal noise.
