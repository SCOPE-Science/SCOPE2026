In the complete bipartite incidence algebra over a fixed finite field, the dimension of an element's commutant equals its number of equal-diagonal comparable pairs plus the number of components in a naturally associated random bipartite graph. For uniformly random elements, the centered centralizer dimension, divided by the block size, converges to a non-Gaussian sum of independent Gaussian products, with characteristic function \((1+t^2/q^2)^{-(q-1)/2}\).
