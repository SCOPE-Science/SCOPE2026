# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The stationary integral has an absolutely convergent GEM(\alpha) orientation decomposition, already established in the motivating publication. Independent isotropic directions give centered pairwise dot products with second moments precisely \(1/d\) and mutually zero covariance. The resulting exact conditional error is \(2(S^2-H_4)/d\). The beta-recursion and independent stationary generator calculations agree on all required moments. For each fixed number of directions, Gaussian normalization and the multivariate CLT give independent standard-normal limits for scaled pairwise dot products; a uniform conditional second-moment tail bound permits the infinite weighted series. The upper edge is controlled by a size-biased GEM intensity and two sharp max-weight inequalities. These prove the limits, rather than deriving them from simulation.

## Originality — PASS, qualified best-knowledge
Guéneau, Majumdar and Schehr, *Exact stationary state of a \(d\)-dimensional run-and-tumble particle in a harmonic potential* (arXiv:2602.08436; Physical Review E 114, 014144, 2026), already derive the complete stationary construction, finite-dimensional distributions and moments. Those ingredients are prior, not evaluated as new. Feng and Gao (2008) study homozygosity fluctuations in changing Poisson--Dirichlet parameters, and Stam (1982) describes classical high-dimensional sphere limits. The evaluated claim instead fixes the Poisson/tumbling parameter while increasing physical dimension: an explicitly nondegenerate random radial limit, a conditionally Gaussian but unconditionally non-Gaussian infinite-weight fluctuation limit, and a sharp limiting radial boundary probability with amplitude one. The inspected directly relevant sections and the available literature comparisons did not reveal this complete conclusion. Residual risk: a general random-weight spherical array theorem or a previously unstated implication of the original all-dimensional formulas might cover some elements, so this assessment is not an independent priority certificate.

## Value — PASS
A common physical high-dimensional shell heuristic fails even without particle interactions: the stationary radius remains random at fixed persistence. The Gaussian-mixture correction distinguishes quenched run histories from an ordinary thermal central limit, and the sharp endpoint law provides a parameter-dependent rare-near-wall prediction. This is a physically motivated asymptotic of a current solvable active-matter model, with exact finite-dimensional normalization and a reproducible finite checker; it is not an arbitrary enumeration or a re-labeling of the source's finite-dimensional formula.

## Closest literature and limitations
The motivating 2026 full text, especially the stick-breaking construction (Section III), owns the underlying stationary measure and finite-d moment relations. Classical Dirichlet-process homozygosity and spherical Gaussian normalization are likewise not novel. The novelty claim is strictly about the **joint fixed-parameter, growing-ambient-dimension radial theorem** and its conditional fluctuations and endpoint amplitude. Models with thermal diffusion, anisotropic refresh or traps, fixed-d uniform endpoint asymptotics and simultaneous persistence/dimension scaling are unproved here.
