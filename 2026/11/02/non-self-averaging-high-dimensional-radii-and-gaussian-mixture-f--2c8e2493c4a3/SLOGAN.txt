An isotropically trapped run-and-tumble particle does not concentrate on a deterministic high-dimensional radius: its squared stationary radius converges to a nondegenerate Poisson--Dirichlet concentration index. The finite-dimensional correction has an exact \(d^{-1/2}\)-scale conditional Gaussian-mixture law, while the limiting probability of lying within \(\varepsilon\) of the maximal radius is asymptotic to \(\varepsilon^{\gamma/\mu}\).
