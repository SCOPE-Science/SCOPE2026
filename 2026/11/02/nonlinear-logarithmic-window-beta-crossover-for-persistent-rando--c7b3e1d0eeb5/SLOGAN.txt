When the flip exponent approaches one as \(\alpha_N=1+\beta/\log N\), the entire persistent-walk path has an exact non-Gaussian critical-window limit with effective switching intensity \(c e^{-\beta}\) and a rescaled \(\operatorname{Beta}(c e^{-\beta},c e^{-\beta})\) endpoint. Its displacement-variance and Binder-cumulant profiles are exactly \((1+2c e^{-\beta})^{-1}\) and \(2/(3+2c e^{-\beta})\), respectively.
