# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every five-variable feedback form in the stated odd-weight family is reduced via affine transformations to three characteristic functions of affinely independent point sets. The paper gives a proof that those are the only cases and explicit five-by-five matrices of rank \(1,3,5\); their ranks exactly determine both quadratic-annihilator dimensions. Lower-degree obstructions and a dimension count determine the algebraic immunity. An independently implemented checker verifies these identities by separate degree-two evaluation matrices over all \(32{,}768\) odd components and all \(2{,}048\) one-cycle five-stage feedbacks. The computed counts do not substitute for the all-functions proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Sönmez Turan's 2012 Table 2 already gives the three maximum-period nonlinearity multiplicities \(24,1128,896\), and its Proposition 1 gives the doubling relation. These are acknowledged as prior results. The checked literature did not establish the exact quadratic-annihilator dimension law \(5-\mathrm{NL}(g)\) or infer the algebraic-immunity count and optimum criterion on this family. General low-nonlinearity implications in Carlet et al. (2006) are not equivalent to the three exact ranks. Related accessible published records and existing claims were compared; unindexed special classifications remain a genuine priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** Degree-two annihilator-space dimensions are a direct algebraic-attack parameter, while maximum-period feedback and nonlinearity are core sequence-design parameters. Their exact structural coupling gives a meaningful classification, with the first natural five-stage optimum algebraic-immunity split. No claim is made that small five-stage registers are secure, or that algebraic immunity alone captures fast attacks.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Sönmez Turan (2012), Sections 3–4 and Table 2, directly covers the feedback distribution and provides indispensable inputs. Çalık et al. (2010) covers maximal-period structure. Carlet et al. (2006) gives general immunity methods, while Boyar et al. (2016) cautions that nonlinearities need not coincide generally. The present proof is specific to five variables and odd-weight reduced feedback, not a general cryptographic equivalence. Historical priority beyond inspected primary sources and independent validation remain open.
