For every odd-weight four-variable Boolean function \(g\), the quadratic-annihilator spaces of the balanced five-variable feedback form \(f(x_0,y)=x_0\oplus g(y)\) both have dimension \(5-\tfrac12\mathrm{NL}(f)\); hence optimal algebraic immunity three occurs exactly at nonlinearity ten. Combined with a previously published order-five nonlinearity table, this gives exactly 896 maximum-period five-stage feedback functions with optimal algebraic immunity; the nonlinearity counts themselves are not new.
