For synchronous open-neighborhood NOR on every labelled rook graph \(K_a\square K_b\), periodic configurations are precisely certain full rectangles plus disjoint rook matchings, together with all partial matchings except near-perfect ones on square boards. The resulting exact orbit census includes \(2^b-1\) primitive two-cycles on every \(2\)-by-\(b\) board for \(b\ge3\); all states become periodic after at most one update.
