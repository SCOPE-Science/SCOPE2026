The functional graphs of every affine Frobenius permutation \(x\mapsto ax^{p^r}+b\) fall into exactly \(d_{\mathrm{div}}(p^{\gcd(m,r)}-1)+1\) cycle types, with explicit cycle counts from a norm-order or trace-zero invariant. Each type has an exact formula for its number of coefficient pairs, uniformly in every prime characteristic and extension degree.
