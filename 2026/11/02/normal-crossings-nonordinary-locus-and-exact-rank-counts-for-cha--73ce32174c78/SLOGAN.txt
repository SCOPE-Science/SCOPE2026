For smooth standard Ciani plane quartics in characteristic \(3\), the three nonordinary Hasse surfaces form a simple-normal-crossings divisor whose only triple point is the Fermat quartic. Over every \(\mathbb F_{3^m}\), the complete coefficient-marked Jacobian \(3\)-rank distribution is given by four explicit polynomials in \(3^m\), with exactly one rank-zero parameter.
