For equispaced boundary poles in the cubically weighted Bergman space, the sharp local stability direction is the central (Nyquist) Fourier mode, not the first harmonic. Its exact positive Hessian spectrum has a least eigenvalue \(16\pi^2/N-1728\zeta(3)/N^2+O(N^{-3})\), revealing a qualitative softening relative to the critically weighted case.
