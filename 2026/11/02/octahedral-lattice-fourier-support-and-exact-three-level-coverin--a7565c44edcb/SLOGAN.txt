The standard three-dimensional octahedron has nonzero integer-lattice Fourier coefficients exactly on the six reflection planes \(k_i=\pm k_j\), with explicit rational multiples of \(\pi^{-2}\). Its integer-radius lattice periodizations attain exactly three multiplicities, whose relative volume fractions are always \(1/6\), \(1/3\), and \(1/2\).
