The unordered two-point configuration space of the octahedral graph has rational homology modules \(H_1\cong\det(W)\otimes\mathbb Q[\{\pm1\}^3]\) and \(H_2\cong W\oplus\eta\) under all 48 signed-coordinate symmetries. Its full-symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic, whereas the rotation-only quotient retains exactly one first-homology class.
