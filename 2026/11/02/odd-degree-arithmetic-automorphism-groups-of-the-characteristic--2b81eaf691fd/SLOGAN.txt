For the characteristic-two Klein cubic threefold, the full automorphism group over every odd-degree field \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\) is \(C_5\) when \(5\nmid m\), but \(\operatorname{Sp}_4(\mathbb F_2)\cong S_6\) when \(5\mid m\). An explicit self-dual normal basis over \(\mathbb F_{32}\) converts its cyclic equation to the Fermat cubic and explains the exact jump from five to 720 automorphisms.
