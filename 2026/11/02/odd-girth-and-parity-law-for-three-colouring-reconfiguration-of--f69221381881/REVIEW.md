# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** Proper cyclic three-colourings are encoded bijectively by admissible sign words and three initial colours. A recolouring is precisely an adjacent unlike-sign exchange and, on the twisted integer height cover, a \(2\)-unit valley raise or peak lowering. The monotone interpolation argument uses mixed signs to prohibit infinite descent, proves connectivity and constructs a length-\(3n\) closed projected walk through each prescribed edge. The signed height displacement of any closed walk is an integer multiple of \(6\) at each coordinate, establishing the parity law and the sharp lower bound on odd closed walks. Commuting independent swaps give four-cycles in the remaining cases. Boundary sectors are handled separately. Two independent finite enumeration programs pass; they support but do not replace the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with explicit residual risk.** Cereceda–van den Heuvel–Johnson (2006) proves the oriented-cycle winding invariant and related untwisted height lemmas; their subsequent paths paper establishes general recolouring reachability. Johnson–Kratsch–Kratsch–Patel–Paulusma (2014) solves all-pairs shortest paths for three-colourings of arbitrary graphs. None of the reviewed corresponding statements asserts the exact odd girth \(3n\), the property that every edge lies on a shortest odd cycle, or the parity/girth classification of individual cyclic winding sectors. These are distinct from the already-known invariance or the zero-length shortest path from a colouring to itself. The 2007 paths preprint full text was not obtainable and equivalent statements under alternative cyclic-state terminology remain a meaningful novelty risk, not claimed absent.

**Value — PASS.** Odd girth and bipartiteness detect the precise obstruction to parity breaking in an otherwise natural single-site Glauber-type reconfiguration graph. The result supplies a uniform sharp integer obstruction and an edgewise constructive witness, with a clear impact on the period of the nonlazy walk, plus exact enumeration and ordinary-girth boundaries. This is an infinite-family structural classification, not a minor numerical increment.

**Limits.** No independent audit, general mixing-time bound, or equivalence classification over every unindexed paper is asserted.
