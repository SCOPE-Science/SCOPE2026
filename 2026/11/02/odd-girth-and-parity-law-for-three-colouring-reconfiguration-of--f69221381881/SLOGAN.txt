The nontrivial fixed-winding reconfiguration components of proper \(3\)-colourings of \(C_n\) are bipartite for even \(n\), whereas for odd \(n\) they have odd girth **exactly \(3n\)** and every exchange belongs to such a shortest odd cycle. Their ordinary girth is \(4\), except for the minimal-sign sectors that are precisely \(C_{3n}\).
