For every \(n\equiv2\pmod4\) with \(n\ge6\), the zero-field Ising antiferromagnet on \(GP(n,2)\) has energy \(-2(n-1)J\) and exactly \(2(n/2)^2F_{n/2}\) labelled ground states, or half as many unordered maximum cuts. Its **entire ground-state magnetization distribution** is supported on \(\{0,\pm4\}\), with exactly \(m[t^{(m-5)/2}](1-3t+t^2)^{-2}\) states at each nonzero magnetization for \(m=n/2\ge5\), and no such states at \(m=3\).
