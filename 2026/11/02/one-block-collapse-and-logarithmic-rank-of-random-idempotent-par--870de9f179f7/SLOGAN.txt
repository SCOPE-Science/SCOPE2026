For a fixed number \(r\ge2\) of equally sized blocks, almost every idempotent partition-preserving transformation collapses **all** blocks into one: the logarithmic probability of an exception is \(-m[r\log r-(r-1)\log(r-1)]+o(m)\). Its image rank nevertheless concentrates at \(rm/\log m\), and rare noncollapsed characters are asymptotically concentrated on a single \((r-1,1)\) two-root split.
