For every prime power \(q\), quadratic nonassociative cyclic division algebras and the split matrix ring \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\) have the identical commuting graph: \(q^2+q+1\) disjoint cliques of size \(q^2-q\). In the nonassociative algebras, most element commutants are not subalgebras, even though they are cliques.
