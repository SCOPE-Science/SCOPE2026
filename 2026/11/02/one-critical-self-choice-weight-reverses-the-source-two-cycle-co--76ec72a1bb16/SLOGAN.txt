In a random mapping that gives a vertex's self-image weight \(w\) and each other image weight one, the covariance between empty-indegree vertices and directed two-cycles reverses sign **exactly once**, at \(w=(\sqrt{n(n-2)}-(n-2))/2\); all cycle lengths at least three remain strictly negatively correlated for every finite \(w\ge0\). The critical weight approaches \(1/2\) with a resolved \(1/n\)-scale crossover and explicit covariance scaling.
