For every pair of equal-area centered regular polygons with \(3\le n<m\) sides, the radial distance from a uniform interior point to the center is strictly stochastically smaller for the \(m\)-gon, and their probability densities have exactly one nontrivial crossing. This crossing gives the exact, equal total-variation and Kolmogorov distances; an elementary formula also gives their one-Wasserstein separation.
