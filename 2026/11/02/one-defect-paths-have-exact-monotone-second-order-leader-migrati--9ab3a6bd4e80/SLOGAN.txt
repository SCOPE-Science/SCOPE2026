In a path with one exceptional edge, a single noise-free leader minimizing second-order grounded coherence \(\operatorname{tr}(L_{FF}^{-2})\) must lie on the larger side of that edge (or at either adjacent endpoint when the sides tie). The exact transition thresholds are \(t_k=1+[g(n-k)-g(k)]/(2h^2)\), where \(g(k)=(2k^3+k)/3\): weakening the exceptional connection moves every optimal leader monotonically toward it, with only adjacent-index ties.
