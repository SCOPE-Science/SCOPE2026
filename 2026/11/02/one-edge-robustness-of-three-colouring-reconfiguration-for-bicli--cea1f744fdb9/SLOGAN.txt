Deleting one edge from any complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\) adds exactly six proper three-colourings and twelve recolouring moves, yet changes none of the distances between pre-existing colourings. Both recolouring graphs remain self-centred with radius and diameter exactly \(\lfloor3(m+n)/2\rfloor\); the new graph is a six-hypercube necklace with six square ears.
