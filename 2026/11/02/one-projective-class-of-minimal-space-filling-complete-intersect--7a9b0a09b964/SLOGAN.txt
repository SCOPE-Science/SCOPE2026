Every smooth degree-\(12\) complete-intersection curve filling \(\mathbb P^3(\mathbb F_2)\) belongs to one projective orbit of size \(4032\), and its full rational projective stabilizer is \(C_5\). The resulting genus-\(19\) curve is a tame cyclic degree-five cover of a genus-\(3\) curve, branched at four conjugate geometric points.
