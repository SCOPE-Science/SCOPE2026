On a finite intuitionistic chain, Kaminski's two ternary persistence axioms force every row-functional binary modality to evaluate one argument at the top world: its other output moves in unit steps along an active suffix. Exactly \(2^{n+1}-n-1\) such partial-functional frames exist on the labelled \(n\)-world chain, and exactly \(2^n-1\) are total-functional.
