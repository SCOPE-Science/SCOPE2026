For the coordinatewise nearest-integer \(45^\circ\) rotation of \(\mathbb Z^2\), the exact identity \(R^3=Q\circ R\) makes every nonzero orbit enter a least-period-eight cycle after at most one step. The periodic-point density is precisely \(2\sqrt2-2\); its complement \(3-2\sqrt2\) consists of the points needing one transient step.
