For nuclear-scaled polar-gradient descent on any anisotropic two-row positive-definite matrix quadratic, the step \(1/\operatorname{tr}H\) makes every full-rank gradient either zero or exactly equal-singular-value in one iteration. Thereafter the nonlinear update is identical to optimally tuned ordinary gradient descent, with exact factor \((\lambda_{\max}-\lambda_{\min})/(\lambda_{\max}+\lambda_{\min})\); rank-one starts instead follow a different linear law.
