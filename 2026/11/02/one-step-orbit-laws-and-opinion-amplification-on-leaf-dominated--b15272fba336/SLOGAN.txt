In synchronous fair-tie random-majority dynamics, attaching more private leaves to each core vertex than its number of core neighbors forces entry into a deterministic fixed point or two-cycle after one round and yields \((4^k+2^k)/2\) exact recurrent classes for \(k\) core vertices. The complete orbit probabilities admit a binomial product formula, and independent biased initial opinions become strictly more biased in expected long-run time average whenever every core vertex has a core neighbor.
