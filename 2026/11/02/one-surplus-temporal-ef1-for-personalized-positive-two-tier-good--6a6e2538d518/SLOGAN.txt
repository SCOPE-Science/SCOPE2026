For \(n\ge2\) agents with individually chosen positive low and high values for goods, a deterministic irrevocable algorithm guarantees EF1 after every arrival of any stream containing at most \(n+1\) goods, without future-item information. Give the first \(n\) goods to previously unassigned high-valuing agents when possible, then give the sole surplus good to the earliest first-phase low-tier recipient (or anyone if none exists); that recipient's singleton is unenvied by all others.
