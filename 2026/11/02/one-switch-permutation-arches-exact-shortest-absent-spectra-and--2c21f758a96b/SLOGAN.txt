For one-switch permutation-block words \(P^aQ^b\), shortest absent scattered subsequences are exactly the patterns decreasing in \(P\)-order up to a pivot and decreasing in \(Q\)-order afterward. Their exact count has a unique minimum at \(Q=P\) and a unique maximum at \(Q=\operatorname{rev}(P)\); balancing the two phases at the maximum changes the growth degree from \(q-1\) to \(2q-2\).
