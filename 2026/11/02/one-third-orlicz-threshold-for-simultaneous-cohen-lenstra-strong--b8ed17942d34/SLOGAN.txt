For independent Cohen–Lenstra random finite abelian prime-power groups, the Orlicz test class generated by \(\Phi_\beta(t)=\int_0^t\exp((\log(e+s))^\beta)\,ds\) obeys a simultaneous strong law for every \(\beta>1/3\), while for every \(\beta<1/3\) some nonnegative integrable member has unbounded empirical averages almost surely. These classes strictly extend the previously established union of positive-moment spaces; the critical endpoint is not resolved.
