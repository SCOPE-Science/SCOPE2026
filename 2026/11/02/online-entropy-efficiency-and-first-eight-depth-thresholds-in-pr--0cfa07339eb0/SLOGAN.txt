For the prefix--sparse convex geometry \(P(k,n)\), online Littlestone dimension grows as \(\log_2 n+O_k(\log_2\log_2 n)\) although the class is Sauer-maximum of VC dimension \(k+1\); its online-depth-to-log-class-size ratio tends to \(1/(k+1)\). For \(k=1\), the first depth thresholds \(3\) through \(8\) occur exactly at ground-set sizes \(4,7,12,21,39,69\).
