# Scientific review: short-run profiles in fixed-content random circles

## Correctness — PASS

The sampling measure and all parameters are specified. A zero-run-start marking gives \(\frac{n}{r}\binom{a-1}{r-1}\binom{b-1}{r-1}\) labeled words with \(r\) runs of each color; the conditional positive-composition factorization follows exactly. Independent uniform random permutations of internal gaps couple every run-length composition by successive refinement, and splitting cannot reduce any cumulative short-run count. The total covariance identity thus establishes cross-color positive association for arbitrary increasing test functions. Strictness follows because the run count can be both one and two. Exact factorial moments of the run count establish the singleton covariance; the proof treats all small sizes through the zero convention in \(M_k\). A standalone verifier independently checked all 66 fixed-composition pairs through fourteen sites.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risks

The literature comparison distinguished an existing one-color circular Pólya enumeration (Makri, Philippou, Psillakis, 2007), a several-run-length same-color enumeration (Inoue–Aki, 2005), and the broad multicolor linear run-statistic joint law (Eryilmaz, 2008). The closest paper, Eryilmaz, Theorems 1–2, explicitly furnishes a general joint PMF and therefore covers enumerative aspects; the added claim is a cross-color monotone coupling and all-threshold positive association in the *fixed-content circular* model, not a new way to evaluate the joint distribution. The inspected relevant full-text sections did not assert the full association/order property or this strict covariance factorization. This is a qualified self-review originality assessment, not a proof of worldwide priority. A general enumerative formula could conceal a shorter route to the same law.

## Value — PASS

Simultaneously testing multiple thresholds of short-run abundance in two binary classes naturally arises in spatial clustering, runs tests, and reliability. The association theorem supplies a sign for every pair of increasing statistics of these multithreshold profiles, rather than isolated moments. It identifies the shared run count as the sole source of cross-color dependence after conditioning and provides an exact positive singleton covariance with its sharp degenerate boundary. Those structural consequences are not routine normalization or arbitrary fixed-parameter computations.

## Limits

No general within-color association or independent peer validation is claimed. Enumeration stops at fourteen sites; the all-size result is derived analytically. Older general run-statistic formulas are acknowledged and may permit reconstruction of some moments.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
