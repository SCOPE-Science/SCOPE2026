In a uniform fixed-point-free random mapping, directed two-cycles are positively correlated with the number of zero-indegree vertices, whereas every cycle length at least three is negatively correlated with that number. Exact prescribed-cycle factorial moments explain this sharp reversal and a critical three-cycle covariance that decays like \(-2/(3e n^2)\), one power of \(n\) faster than the other fixed cycle lengths.
