# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The folded-circle generator is \(D\partial_{xx}\) on a target-absorbing, antipode-reflecting half-interval; the nonreset hitting transform is the unique elementary solution of its boundary-value problem. Conditional renewal, the two distinct placements of the anchor average relative to the reciprocal, and the exact integral of \(\operatorname{sech}\) give both formulas. The strict fresh-anchor inequality and decreasing property follow from the standard positive partial-fraction expansion of \(z\coth z\). The persistent-anchor opposite inequality follows by differentiating the exact comparison and applying strict Cauchy–Schwarz. The high- and low-rate coefficients follow from explicit expansions. An independent discretization and direct quadrature passed 94 assertions; this finite evidence is not substituted for proof. The Laplace-order statement follows from a strictly concave function and Jensen's inequality.

The evaluated addition is the all-rate strict three-way uniform-ensemble ordering, opposing global optimum outcomes under persistent versus freshly redrawn anchors, exact loss of the linear rate response for the persistent protocol, exponentially separated high-rate behavior, and accompanying Laplace-transform order. Direct inspection of arXiv:2605.08414v1 (renewal framework, one-site ring section, distributed-site section, conclusions) and arXiv:2401.01125v1 (renewal section, equivalent reset-site discussion, fluctuations) did not locate that global two-protocol ensemble theorem. A stronger or equivalent unlocated resetting theorem, or a previously published global consequence of the known renewal equations, remains a substantive publication-priority risk.

A searcher's reset position may be assigned once and then remembered, or redrawn independently at each event. The exact policy distinction is physically operational despite identical marginal reset-site distributions, and it reverses the globally optimal resetting policy at every strictly positive rate.
