Two searchers can start from identically uniform positions on the same Brownian circle and reset at the same Poisson rate, yet fresh uniformly redrawn reset sites strictly improve the ensemble mean first-passage time at every positive rate while a once-chosen persistent reset anchor strictly worsens it. The former mean tends to zero with rapid resetting; the latter grows exponentially in the square root of the resetting rate, with an exact high-rate prefactor and a vanishing linear small-rate response.
