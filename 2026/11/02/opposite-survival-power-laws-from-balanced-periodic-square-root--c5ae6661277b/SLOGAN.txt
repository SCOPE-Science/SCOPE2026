A centered, periodically phased \(n^{-1/2}\) perturbation of a finite killed Markov chain has a second-order logarithmic survival exponent determined by a Poisson-response correction. An explicit four-state period-two example has survival factors \(n^{-3/160}\) and \(n^{3/32}\) from different initial cyclic classes despite the same exponential survival rate and vanishing first-order block drift.
