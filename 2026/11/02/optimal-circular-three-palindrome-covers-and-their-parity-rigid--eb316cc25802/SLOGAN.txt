The shortest cyclic \(q\)-symbol word containing every three-letter palindrome \(aba\) has length \(\frac{3q^2+q}{2}\) for odd \(q\), and exactly \(q/2\) more for even \(q\). Every shortest cover has exactly two copies of each loop-adjacent pair and three of every distinct-symbol pair, except for one extra copy along a perfect matching when \(q\) is even.
