For continuous-time quantum walks on the \(N=2^m-1\) hypercube, \(m\ge3\), any affine \(m\)-dimensional set of \(2^m\) positive-rate absorbers captures at most \((N^2-3)/2^N\) of a maximally mixed one-particle state in the long-time limit. The binary simplex code attains this sharp optimum and has exactly \(1\), \(N\), or \(N+1\) bright modes in each energy shell, yielding dark dimension \(2^N-N^2+3\).
