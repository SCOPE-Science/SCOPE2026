For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge6\), its \(2n^2+2\) minimum connected dominating sets are classified by three explicit families, and their one-vertex-exchange graph is connected with exactly \(4n(n+2)\) edges and degree polynomial \(2t^{2n}+4nt^6+4nt^5+2n(n-4)t^4\). The two full-rail configurations are exactly \(n\) exchanges apart.
