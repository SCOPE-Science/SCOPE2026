For every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge2\), its point–line incidence network needs exactly \(q^2-1\) independent vertex controls, and every minimum placement is characterized by a parallel-class coverage condition and one explicit incidence determinant. With exactly \(q\) concurrent line controls, there are precisely \(2q(q-1)^q\) optimal choices of unactuated points per pencil, giving \(2q^3(q+1)(q-1)^q\) distinct optimal configurations overall.
