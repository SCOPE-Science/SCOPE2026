For the bivariate correlated degree-two Hermite Gram block, \(\operatorname{diag}(1,\sqrt2,1)\) is the unique optimal positive diagonal congruence scaling up to a scalar for every \(|\rho|<1\). Its exact condition number is \(((1+|\rho|)/(1-|\rho|))^2\), whereas conventional unit-diagonal normalization is strictly worse at nonzero correlation and has a sharp near-degeneracy penalty of \(9/8\).
