For independent polygon-boundary samples with adjustable positive edge probabilities, the \(n^2\)-scaled missing area converges to a sum of independent products of exponential corner gaps, and the unique asymptotically optimal edge allocation has an explicit convex objective. Equal edge probabilities are optimal precisely when adjacent corner determinant sums are constant; among quadrilaterals, this characterizes parallelograms and can yield arbitrarily large improvements over uniform arclength sampling on elongated rectangles.
