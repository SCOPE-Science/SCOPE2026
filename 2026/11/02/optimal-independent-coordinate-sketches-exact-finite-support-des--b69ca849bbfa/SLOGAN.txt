For a Hilbert--Schmidt diagonal operator with squared singular values \(j^{-\rho}\), the exact optimal expected error of \(L\) independent isotropic one-coordinate sketches is a reverse-waterfilled finite-support limit of full-support laws. Its squared-error inefficiency relative to the optimal rank-\(L\) approximation converges sharply to \(\rho^\rho\) for every \(\rho>1\).
