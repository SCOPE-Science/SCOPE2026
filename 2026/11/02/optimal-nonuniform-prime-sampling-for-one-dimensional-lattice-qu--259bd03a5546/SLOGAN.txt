For one-dimensional unshifted prime-lattice integration on a periodic Korobov ball, the unique prime-sampling distribution minimizing worst-case expected absolute error is explicit and strictly favors larger primes. Uniform prime selection incurs a nonvanishing asymptotic error factor \(\sqrt{7/6}\) at smoothness \(\alpha=1\) and \(\sqrt{217/120}\) at \(\alpha=2\).
