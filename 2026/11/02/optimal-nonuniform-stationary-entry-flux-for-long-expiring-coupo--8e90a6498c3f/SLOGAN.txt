For a fixed number \(n\ge2\) of independently sampled expiring coupon types, the maximal stationary rate of new complete-window entries is asymptotic to \(C_{n-1}/M\), with an explicit sharp constant. Every asymptotically optimal sampling law concentrates almost all mass on one type while giving each of the remaining \(n-1\) types probability \(c_{n-1}/M\); equal sampling instead has exponentially small entry rate.
