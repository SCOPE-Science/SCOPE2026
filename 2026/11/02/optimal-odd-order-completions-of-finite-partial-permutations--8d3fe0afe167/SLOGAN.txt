For a finite partial bijection with no even directed cycle, the minimum additional ground-set size required to obtain an odd-order permutation is either zero or exactly one; in the one-point case there are precisely \(b!\) minimal completions, where \(b\) is the number of even-size open paths. On the original set, the complete count depends only on the numbers \(a,b\) of odd- and even-size open paths and is \(c_a(a+b-1)!/(a-1)!\) when \(a\ge1\), with \(c_a\) the classical odd-cycle permutation number.
