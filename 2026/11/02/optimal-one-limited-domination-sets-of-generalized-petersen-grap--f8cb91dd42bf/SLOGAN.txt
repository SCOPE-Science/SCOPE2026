For every \(n\ge7\), the generalized Petersen graph \(\operatorname{GP}(n,3)\) has one-limited domination number \(n\) and exactly \(2+4\mathbf1_{\{4\mid n\}}+10\mathbf1_{\{5\mid n\}}\) minimum sets on its labelled vertices. Every minimum set is determined by a constant, four-periodic, or five-periodic outer-ring sign pattern; no mixed twenty-periodic pattern is possible.
