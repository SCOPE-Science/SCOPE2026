For the labelled cycle prism \(C_n\square K_2\), proper conflict-free colouring needs exactly three named colours when \(3\mid n\) and four otherwise; the three-colour optimum has exactly twelve assignments. An explicit degree-\(36\) determinant gives the complete four-colour counting sequence and the exponential decay rate \((\rho/7)^n\) of proper colourings that satisfy the additional neighbourhood condition.
