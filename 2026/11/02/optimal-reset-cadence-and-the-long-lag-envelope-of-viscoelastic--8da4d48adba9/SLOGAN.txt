Probe positions sampled immediately before successive resets retain a strictly positive geometric event-lag memory through an unreset viscoelastic bath, but for lag \(m\ge2\) the memory is maximized at a nonzero reset rate exactly when \(g>3/[2(m-1)]\). At large lag, the optimal rate satisfies \(r_m^*\sim gm\), matching the bath relaxation time, and the maximum correlation has the sharp envelope \(\rho_m^*\sim1/(2egm)\).
