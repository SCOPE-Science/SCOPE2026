Every square-zero Schatten operator \(N\) has a single-commutator factorization whose **globally minimal** \(S_{2p}\) norm product is exactly \(2^{1/(2p)-1}\|N\|_{S_p}\), attained for every \(1\le p<\infty\). The sharp cost holds at infinite rank and at the trace-class endpoint, and follows from an orthogonal-corner commutator inequality with a matching polar-decomposition construction.
