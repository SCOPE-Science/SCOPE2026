For the geometrically regular unilateral weighted shifts \(\alpha_j=\sqrt{(b^j+N)/(b^j+D)}\), the least similarity condition number to the unweighted shift is exactly the larger of the two reciprocal infinite-product moment limits, attained by a diagonal intertwiner even among all non-diagonal similarities. As \(b\downarrow1\) it grows at an explicit dilogarithmic exponential rate, with a finite nontrivial crossover when \(N-D\) scales as \(\log b\).
