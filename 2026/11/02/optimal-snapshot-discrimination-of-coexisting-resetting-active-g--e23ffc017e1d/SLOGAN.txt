In the coexistence region of a locally reset run-and-tumble gas, one particle's radius carries an exact optimal droplet-versus-gas test: the optimal threshold is the droplet interface and its error is governed by a closed total-variation formula. Although the condensed mass rises with resetting, the phase distinguishability can have a unique interior maximum and develops a nonzero \((s-m)^{3/2}\) cusp at the gas-only fold.
