A uniformly spoke-weighted metric wheel has an exact two-mode negative-type threshold, and the spoke length maximizing its Hilbert-snowflake exponent is uniquely \(r_n^{\mathrm{opt}}=b_n^{1/\log_2(a_n/b_n)}\), with an explicit parity-dependent change in critical embedding dimension. The optimum lies in a sharp \(2/n\) boundary layer below unit spoke length, separating a global hub-versus-rim obstruction from a high-frequency cycle obstruction.
