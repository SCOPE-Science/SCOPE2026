For a connected complete multipartite graph with largest part size \(M\ge4\), every optimal sub-quorum colouring simply gives distinct colours to one largest part, with all other vertices uncoloured. At the sharp boundary \(M=3\), two additional configurations appear—one shared outsider or two matched triples—and the exact number of optimal named colourings is \(6p(3N+3p-11)\), where \(N\) is the graph order and \(p\) counts triple parts.
