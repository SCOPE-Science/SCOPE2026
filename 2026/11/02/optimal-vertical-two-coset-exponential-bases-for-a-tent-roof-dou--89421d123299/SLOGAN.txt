For a natural nonconvex planar double tile, every two-coset vertical frequency offset has exact squared Riesz bounds \((1\mp\rho(t))/2\), where \(\rho(t)=\max_{a=1,2,3}|\cos(\pi at/4)|\). The only optimal offsets are \(t\equiv1,3\pmod4\); the four forbidden offsets have three distinct sharp quadratic stability-loss coefficients.
