In a fully occupied multitype swap Schelling game on any complete bipartite graph, every maximal strict mutually profitable swap sequence has exactly \(\min(P,Q)\) moves, where \(P,Q\) are explicit initial surplus and deficit counts. This schedule-independent duration is at most \(\min(a-1,b-1)\), even on severely unbalanced bicliques where the conventional monochromatic-edge potential can decrease.
