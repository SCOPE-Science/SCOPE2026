In a harmonically trapped inertial run-and-tumble particle, the covariance of stationary potential and kinetic energies changes sign exactly once as trap stiffness increases, and this happens only deeply within the underdamped regime. Its zero lies strictly after the kinetic fourth-cumulant sign change and strictly before the potential fourth-cumulant sign change whenever that second change exists, yielding an exact ordered hierarchy of nonequilibrium fluctuation thresholds.
