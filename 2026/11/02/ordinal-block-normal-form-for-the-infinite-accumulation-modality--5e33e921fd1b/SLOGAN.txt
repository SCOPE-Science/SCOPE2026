On an ordinal with the initial-segment Alexandrov topology, infinite accumulation of a truth set begins exactly at the supremum of its first \(\omega\) members; all later iterates are explicit ordinal tails. The full-space extinction rank is exactly \(\min\{\gamma:\omega\gamma\ge\theta\}\), realizing every countable ordinal already on countable non-\(T_1\) Alexandrov chains.
