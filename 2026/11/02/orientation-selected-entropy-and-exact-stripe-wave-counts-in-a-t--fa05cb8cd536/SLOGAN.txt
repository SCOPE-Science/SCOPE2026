For the three-color cyclic automaton with Moore neighbors, the **complete unit-index-speed traveling stripe families** have sharply orientation-dependent temporal entropy: axial stripes have entropy \(\log2\) at thresholds \(1,2,3\), while diagonal stripes have entropy \(0,\log\rho,\log\varphi\) respectively, with \(\rho\) the positive Perron root of \(\lambda^4-2\lambda^3+\lambda-1\). The axial family has exactly \(2^n+2\cos(n\pi/3)\) period-dividing-\(n\) points, and both orientations have zero traveling-stripe entropy above threshold three.
