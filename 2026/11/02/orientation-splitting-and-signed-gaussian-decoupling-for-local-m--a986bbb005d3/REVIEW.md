PASS. The theorem is restricted to independent uniform noncrossing perfect matchings. Deletion of prescribed disjoint local arcs gives Catalan ratio probabilities, exact ordered placement counts give every stated factorial moment, and elementary algebra proves all three strictly negative finite-size cross-covariances. The orientation-swapping involutions commute at distinct internally paired four-vertex components and produce independent fair signs. Exhaustive small-size enumeration checks the formulas independently of the algebra, but is not substituted for the unrestricted proofs.

The result distinguishes two negatively covarying oriented shape statistics from their asymptotically independent signed difference, giving explicit dependence geometry and an exact conditional symmetry principle useful for further joint-shape analysis.
