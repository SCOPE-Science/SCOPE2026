For every regular polygon, the two widths seen at perpendicular orientations are either exactly equal or exactly countermonotone, according as the number of sides is or is not divisible by four; this yields a complete closed distribution for the area of its randomly oriented enclosing rectangle. The angular width covariance crosses zero exactly once per half-period, and its normalized many-sided limit is a universal quartic with opposite-orientation correlation \(-7/8\).
