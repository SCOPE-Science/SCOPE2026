A polynomially damped complex Gaussian Fock kernel has a sharp spectral transition on the circle \(s|1-c|=1\): inside it is all-Schatten, outside it is unbounded, and on the circle its singular values decay precisely as \(s^{1/2-\tau}j^{-\tau}\). Its critical Schatten-norm blow-up has a universal constant independent of the oscillation angle, while a quadratic phase can turn an unbounded positive-symbol operator into an all-Schatten one without altering the symbol's modulus.
