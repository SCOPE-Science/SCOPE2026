One zero per clique column implies an automatic positive neighbor for every positive vertex, so the ordinary and total feasible classes coincide. The universal bound follows from \(z\le n\), \(z\le3t\), and weight \(mn-z+t\). A direct path/cycle construction achieves the bound, with a separate proof for the three-cycle exception. Equality at \(n=3q\) forces all columns to carry zeros and all two-labeled vertices to defend exactly three, which yields the complete tiled classification. The consecutive-block proper-coloring count supplies the stated exact formulas. The included checker separately checked 936 actual graph witnesses, 20 unrestricted exact dynamic-programming minima/counts, and four directly enumerated graph labelings. Finite checks are corroboration, not infinite proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact enumeration of extremal labelings refines a studied domination invariant on two natural infinite graph-product families. Equality rigidity converts the optimization problem into a unique path-block tiling or a three-phase cyclic tiling, and the cyclic multiplicity records a chromatic-polynomial effect that a scalar optimum cannot reveal. The result describes exact extremal configurations for all clique widths and all divisible-by-three lengths; other length residues and arbitrary factor graphs remain outside its enumerative scope.
