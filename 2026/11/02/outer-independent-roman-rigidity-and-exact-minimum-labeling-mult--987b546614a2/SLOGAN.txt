On clique chains \(K_m\square P_n\) and clique cylinders \(K_m\square C_n\) with \(m\ge3\), every outer-independent Roman labeling is automatically total, and the minimum weight is \(mn-\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\), except that \(K_m\square C_3\) has minimum \(3m-1\). At lengths \(n=3q\), all minimum labelings have a forced three-column tiling, giving exactly \(m(m-1)^{2q-1}\) optimal path labelings and, for \(q\ge2\), \(3(m-1)^q((m-1)^q+(-1)^q(m-1))\) optimal cyclic labelings.
