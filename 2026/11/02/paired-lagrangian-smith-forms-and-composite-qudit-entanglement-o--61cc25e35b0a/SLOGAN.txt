For a pure \(D\)-level graph state on the binary symplectic graph, the cut formed by two complementary maximal isotropic contexts has an explicit integral Smith form and exactly \(R_{m,D}\) equal nonzero Schmidt-spectrum eigenvalues, where \(R_{m,D}=D^{-1}\gcd(D,2^{m-1})\left[\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}(D/\gcd(D,2^j))^{\binom m{j+1}}\right]^2\). Every odd local dimension has a deficit of exactly one base-\(D\) entropy unit, whereas the qubit entropy is only \(2m\) bits for \(2(2^m-1)\) qubits in the chosen subsystem.
