For independent random selection at probability \((\log N+(r-1)\log\log N+c)/N\) in the square rook graph \(K_N\square K_N\), the total \((2r-1)\)- and total \(2r\)-domination probabilities converge, respectively, to \(2e^{-\lambda}-e^{-2\lambda}\) and \(e^{-2\lambda}\), where \(\lambda=e^{-c}/(r-1)!\). Independent Poisson counts of low-degree rows and columns explain the common threshold with different odd/even laws; the limits also govern fixed-budget random placements and first-success times in a random vertex ordering.
