# Scientific review: pairwise-annihilating tuples of radical-square-zero quiver algebras

## Correctness — PASS

The path-algebra quotient has basis consisting of vertices and arrows, with all arrow–arrow products zero. For each tuple, the original ordered zero-product equations force each vertex diagonal coefficient vector to be zero or supported on one tuple coordinate. For a fixed zero-vertex set and nonzero-coloring, each arrow contributes exactly \(m\) free coordinates if both endpoints have zero vectors, and exactly one otherwise; explicit polynomial trivializations are supplied for all cases, including loops, equal colors, different colors, and characteristic two. Hence the torus–affine strata are genuine varieties over every field. Summing finite-field points proves the formula. Geometric dimension and counts of maximal-dimensional components follow from the irreducible-stratum closures; the induced-edge density gives the sharp dimension threshold. The fixed-field reconstruction follows from uniqueness of polynomial–exponential sequences with distinct positive exponential bases. A separate direct-multiplication checker exhausts 17 small configurations, including genuine \(\mathbb F_4\) arithmetic; no finite check is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, provisional

The root construction and representation varieties for radical-square-zero quiver algebras are established in Bleher–Chinburg–Huisgen-Zimmermann, arXiv:1407.3045v1, and in Nasr-Isfahani, arXiv:1603.04191. Their inspected module varieties and singular-equivalence results do not state or imply the all-arity ordered zero-product element counts, explicit tuple strata, density threshold, or fixed-field edge-polynomial recovery. Huang, arXiv:2110.15566, treats mutually annihilating unrestricted square matrices, not this quiver-family theorem. Tittmann–Averbouch–Makowsky, arXiv:0812.4147, treat a different induced-subgraph component polynomial. Published comparison records include the Kronecker-algebra commuting-probability result and the preceding commuting-orbit and tripotent computations for radical-square-zero quivers. Their conditions, invariants, and implications are strictly different. No encompassing theorem was identified in inspected primary material or available research records; undetected equivalent treatments in zero-product varieties and algebraic graph polynomials remain a nonzero historical priority risk. No uniqueness claim is inferred solely from searches.

## Value — PASS

Ordered annihilation is a natural strengthening of commutation and controls families of square-zero substructures. The theorem gives a closed count for all tuple lengths, all finite fields, loops and parallel arrows; its explicit geometric strata identify every maximal-dimensional component and a threshold controlled by induced-edge density. The full all-arity census over one fixed finite field recovers a natural graph invariant, a structural reconstruction theorem not supplied by individual pair counts. It is not a small numerical improvement or a renamed standard pair count.

## Scope and limitations

The ground field is arbitrary for the reduced geometric statements. The point-count statement is over finite fields. The theorem excludes extra individual square-zero equations, nonreduced-scheme assertions, all lower-dimensional-component classification and radical-cube-zero path algebras. Numerical checks support but do not prove uniformity. Independent verification and exhaustive historical-priority assessment have not been performed.
