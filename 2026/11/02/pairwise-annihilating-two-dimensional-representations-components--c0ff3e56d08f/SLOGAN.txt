For every field and every tuple length, the reduced variety of pairwise mutually annihilating \(2\times2\) matrices has coordinate-axis components, one four-dimensional component for each pair of indices, and—starting with triples—one common-nilpotent Segre–Veronese cone of dimension \(m+1\). This gives a sharp loss of equidimensionality at \(m=4\) and a uniform closed count over every finite field.
