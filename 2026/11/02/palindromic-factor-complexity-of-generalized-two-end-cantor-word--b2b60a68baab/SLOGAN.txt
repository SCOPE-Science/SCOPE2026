For every base \(b\ge4\), the generalized two-end Cantor word has an explicit, all-length palindrome-factor spectrum determined by the two-adic valuations of successive one-position gaps. Its number \(P_b(n)\) of distinct length-\(n\) palindromes is always at most \(3\), and all three values occur; the exact formula identifies every length where each gap scale contributes.
