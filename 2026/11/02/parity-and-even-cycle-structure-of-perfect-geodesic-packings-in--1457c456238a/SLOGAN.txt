The three-factor Hamming graph \(K_2\square K_2\square K_n\) has maximum vertex-disjoint maximal-geodesic packing size \(2\lfloor n/2\rfloor\): a parity obstruction prevents perfect packing precisely for odd \(n\). Perfect packings realize exactly the labeled loopless two-regular column-incidence multigraphs whose components are even cycles, counted by exponential generating function \(e^{z^2/4}(1-z^2)^{-1/4}\).
