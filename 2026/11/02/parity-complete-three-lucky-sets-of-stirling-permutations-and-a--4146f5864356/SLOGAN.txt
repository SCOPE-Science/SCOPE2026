For order-\(n\) Stirling permutations, every exact three-lucky set \(\{1,i,j\}\) with at least one odd index is realizable precisely when \(i\) is odd or \(i<n\); this gives a closed count of the whole parity sector. If both noninitial indices are even, \(i+j\le2n\) is an additional sufficient condition, not a claimed classification.
