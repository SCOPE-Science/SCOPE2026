For the qubit graph state of any Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\), cutting its qubits by even versus odd intersection with two distinguished coordinates gives a completely flat Schmidt spectrum of rank \(2^{\binom{n-2}{k-1}}\) when \(n\) is odd, but rank \(2^{\binom{n-2}{k-1}-\binom{n-4}{k-2}}\) when \(n\) is even. The even-size rank deficit approaches one quarter of the full binary cut rank at central Boolean layers.
