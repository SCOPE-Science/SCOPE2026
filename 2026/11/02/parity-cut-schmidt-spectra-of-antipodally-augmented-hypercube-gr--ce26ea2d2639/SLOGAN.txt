For standard qubit graph states on the \(2^n\)-vertex Boolean cube augmented by the antipodal perfect matching, every nonempty coordinate-parity cut has a completely flat Schmidt spectrum of binary entropy \(2^{n-2}\), except the odd-dimensional full-parity cut, whose entropy is \(2^{n-2}-2^{(n-3)/2}\). Unlike the ordinary hypercube, whose odd-coordinate parity cuts are maximally entangled, the antipodal edges lower their entanglement by a macroscopic, exactly determined amount.
