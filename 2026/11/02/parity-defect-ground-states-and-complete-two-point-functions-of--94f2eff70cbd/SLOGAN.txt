The antiferromagnetic Ising model on an \(n\)-antiprism has only four zero-field ground states for even \(n\), but exactly \(2n^2\) for odd \(n\); the odd states occupy only three magnetization sectors. Every ground-state two-point correlation is given by explicit parity-dependent formulas, revealing finite-size Curie response and macroscopic-distance cross-layer correlations that survive even when fixed-distance correlations vanish.
