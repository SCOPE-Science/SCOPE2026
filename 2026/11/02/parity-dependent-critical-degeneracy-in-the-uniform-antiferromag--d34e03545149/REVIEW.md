At \(x=1\), the odd rim's two hub sectors contribute \(L_n\) and \(2n\) configurations. At the even field \(x_c=n/(n-2)\), precisely two Néel positive-hub configurations and one fully positive rim/negative-hub configuration tie. At \(x=n\) there are exactly two configurations. Spin reversal treats negative fields. Exact brute-force and transfer checks are recorded separately; they are not an all-size proof.
