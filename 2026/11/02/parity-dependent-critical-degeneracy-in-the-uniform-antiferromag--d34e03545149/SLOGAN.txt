On the homogeneous antiferromagnetic Ising wheel, the first zero-temperature field crossing has \(L_n+2n\) ground states at \(|h|=J\) for odd rim length, but only three at \(|h|=nJ/(n-2)\) for even rim length. Consequently the odd critical sequence has residual entropy per spin \(\log\varphi\), whereas the even critical sequence has zero residual entropy.
