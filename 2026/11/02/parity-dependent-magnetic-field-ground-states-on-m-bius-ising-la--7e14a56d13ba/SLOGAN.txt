For the uniform antiferromagnetic Ising model on the \(2n\)-site Möbius ladder, a longitudinal-field crossing at \(|h|=J\) occurs only for even \(n\), with exactly \(4n\) ground configurations and a finite-size magnetization-\(2\) interval before saturation at \(3J\). The complete zero-temperature field energies and degeneracies are explicit for every \(n\ge3\), with established zero-field and saturation counts credited as prior work.
