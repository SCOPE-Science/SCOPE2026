Every 0-perfect forest of a complete bipartite graph is an induced odd-star partition, giving the exact bivariate enumerator \(\exp(t[x\sinh y+y\sinh x-xy])\). For \(1\le a\le b\) of equal parity, the attainable edge counts in \(K_{a,b}\) are exactly \(b,b+2,\ldots,a+b-\kappa(a)\), with \(\kappa(a)=1\) for \(a=1\), \(2\) for even \(a\), and \(3\) for odd \(a\ge3\).
