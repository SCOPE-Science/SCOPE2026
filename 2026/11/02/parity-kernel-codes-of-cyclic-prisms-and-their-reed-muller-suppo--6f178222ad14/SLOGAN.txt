Every two-layer cyclic prism has an even closed-neighborhood parity code of dimension \(\gcd(n,4)\), with exactly three possible nonzero weight spectra. When four divides the circumference, the entire code is a repeated first-order Reed–Muller \(\operatorname{RM}(1,3)\) code, yielding its full generalized support hierarchy and a sharp Lights-Out solvability fraction.
