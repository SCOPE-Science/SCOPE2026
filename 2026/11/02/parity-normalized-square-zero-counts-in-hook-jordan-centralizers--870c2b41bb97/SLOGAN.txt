For every finite field and every hook nilpotent Jordan matrix \(J_n(0)\oplus0_r\) with \(n\ge2\), the complete square-zero centralizer count factors as \(q^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) times an explicit rank-stratified polynomial that depends only on the parity of \(n\). For every nonempty singleton tail \(r\ge1\), the odd-parity normalized count is strictly larger than the even-parity count.
