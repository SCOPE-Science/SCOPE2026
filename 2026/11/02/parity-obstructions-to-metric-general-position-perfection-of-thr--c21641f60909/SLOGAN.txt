A parity obstruction on the three diameter paths of a balanced spider gives infinite counterexamples to metric general-position perfection: for every even \(k\ge4\) and odd \(q\ge3\), a three-arm tree has \(\omega_d^k=q(k-1)\) but \(\chi_d^k\ge q+1\), where \(d=q(k-1)-1\). The 13-vertex spider \(S(4,4,4)\) already has \(\omega_8^4=9\) and \(\chi_8^4=4\), answering the published tree-perfection question negatively.
