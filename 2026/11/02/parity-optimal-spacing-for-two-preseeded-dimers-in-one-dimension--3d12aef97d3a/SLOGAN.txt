On a cycle with two dimers preaccepted at edge separation \(d\), the large-cycle excess final dimer count over ordinary random-sequential adsorption has the exact strictly alternating sign \((-1)^d\), with inverse-factorial decay in \(d\). Spacing four uniquely maximizes the excess at \(2e^{-2}\); spacing three uniquely minimizes it at \((3e^{-2}-1)/2\).
