On binary words with exactly two ones, the pair consisting of the ordinary Parikh matrix and the Parikh matrix of the lexicographically least rotation has no ambiguity at all when the number of zeros is odd. For \(2h\) zeros, its fibers have size at most two, with exactly \(h(h+1)/2\) doubletons and \((h+1)^2\) singleton fibers, all classified by explicit words.
