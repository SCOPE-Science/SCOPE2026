On a complete bipartite graph, one initially absent color on one shore and a complete palette on the other create an alternating wave after exactly \(q-1\) updates when \(q\) is even, or \(q-2\) when \(q\) is odd. The least wave period is respectively \(q\) or \(q+1\), and conditioned on *any* initial palette defect in a large balanced graph, this parity-selected behavior has probability tending to one exponentially fast.
