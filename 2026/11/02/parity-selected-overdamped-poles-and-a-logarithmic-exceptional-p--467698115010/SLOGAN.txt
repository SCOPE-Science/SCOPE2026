A uniform quantum path with a single lossy endpoint has exactly one purely damped spectral mode at every positive loss strength when its length is odd, but an even path acquires two such modes through one defective eigenvalue at a sharp finite loss threshold. That threshold decreases strictly with even path length and exceeds its infinite-path limit by \([\log(4N)+1+o(1)]/(2N)\) in units of the dimensionless terminal-loss coupling.
