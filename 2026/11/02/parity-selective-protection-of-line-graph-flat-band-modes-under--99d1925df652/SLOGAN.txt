In a line graph of cycles meeting at one hub, an even cycle retains one exact flat-band mode when its onsite potential is constant, while two odd cycles retain one only when both have the same constant onsite value; an exact parity formula counts every surviving mode and determines the minimum absorber placement clearing this original band. Independent \(q\)-level onsite disorder on growing flowers of odd cycles of length \(\ell\) retains an asymptotic fraction \(q^{1-\ell}\) of the original modes, whereas continuous iid disorder almost surely retains none.
