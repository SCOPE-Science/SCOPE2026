For all simple cycles \(C_n\), the shattered triples of geodesically convex vertex subsets are exactly those with every cyclic gap at most \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\), giving \(n(n^2-1)/24\) triples for odd \(n\) and \(n(n-2)(n-4)/24\) for even \(n\). These concept families have VC dimension at most three but Littlestone dimension \(2\log_2 n+O(1)\), exhibiting an unbounded static/adaptive gap.
