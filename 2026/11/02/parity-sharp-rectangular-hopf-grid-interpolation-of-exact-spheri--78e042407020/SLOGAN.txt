On a single nonresonant Hopf torus in \(S^3\), exact-degree \(k\) spherical harmonics can be recovered from an \(N\times M\) root-of-unity grid precisely when each angular period is odd and at least \(k+1\), or even and larger than \(2k\). The smallest rectangular grid uses \((k+1)^2\) sites for even \(k\)—exactly the harmonic dimension—and \((k+2)^2\) for odd \(k\).
