For geodesically convex vertex subsets of the antihole \(\overline{C_n}\), \(n\ge7\), every proper hypothesis is an independent set of \(C_n\), giving the complete shattered-support polynomial \(I(C_n;z)+nz^2\). The online dimension is exactly \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) while VC dimension is \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\): the adaptive advantage occurs precisely at odd orders.
