Squared-shortest-path Gaussian matrices on circular ladders have a nonzero limiting last-failure bandwidth \(t_*\approx1.0378840547\), unlike the vanishing threshold on isolated cycles. Their finite-size thresholds split by parity: even ladders approach \(t_*\) at Gaussian speed, while odd ladders have an explicit \(-c_*/n^2\) correction and a two-dimensional endpoint nullspace rather than one-dimensional.
