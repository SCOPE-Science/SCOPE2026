Synchronous tie-retaining binary majority on every wheel has only two fixed configurations but admits exactly countable primitive two-cycles: a three-state cyclic-run transfer, filtered by two strict majority inequalities, gives a complete orbit formula with a parity-dependent two-orbit correction. Every nontrivial orbit flips the hub when the rim length is odd, and the two-cycle count grows at least exponentially despite the absence of non-consensus fixed points.
