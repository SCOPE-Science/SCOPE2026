For the coordinate multiplier on a two-boundary-weighted annular Hardy space, its positive selfcommutator has a completely explicit compact spectrum whose nonzero multiplicities jump from all simple to paired at precisely the integer powers of the annulus radius. The sharp operator-norm envelope oscillates between \((1-q^2)^2/[2(1+q^2)]\) and \((1-q)^2\), while its trace stays fixed at \(1-q^2\). The known Szegő-kernel zero condition for these masses coincides exactly with the odd-power spectral-maximum regime.
