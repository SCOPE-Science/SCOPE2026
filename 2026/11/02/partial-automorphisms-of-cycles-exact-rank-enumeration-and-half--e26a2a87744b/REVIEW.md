The finding is the fixed-deficiency leading term, not a new complete rank polynomial. The proof bounds the contribution of each path-forest shape and counts the dominant shapes with distinct component sizes. The exact rim rank formula in *A finite-rank hub correction for wheel partial-isometry monoids* is credited as background; the macroscopic rank estimates are included for context alongside their path counterparts. The cycle and ambient-distance monoids are not interchangeable when the cycle has diameter at least three.

The finite checker compares exact rank counts in small cycles. The asymptotic conclusion instead relies on the fixed-deficiency shape analysis, with no growth in the deficiency allowed in its error bound. The comparisons do not establish historical priority.
