For the inverse monoid of induced-subgraph partial automorphisms of every simple cycle, an explicit partition formula gives every rank coefficient; a uniformly random element has rank asymptotic to half the vertices, with macroscopic deviations bounded by \(\exp(-\epsilon n\log n+O_\epsilon(n))\). The monoid grows on the \(\exp(\tfrac n2\log n+O(n))\) scale, strictly larger than the distance-preserving cycle partial-isometry monoid once \(n\ge6\).
