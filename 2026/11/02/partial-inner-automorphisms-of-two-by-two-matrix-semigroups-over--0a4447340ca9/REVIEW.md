# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof derives all permitted fixed domains from a two-dimensional linear-algebra calculation and realizes every matrix-line arrow by explicit generators. The exact formula is supported by symbolic counting and independent enumeration over two finite prime fields. Higher-field and higher-dimension cases are not inferred from finite tests.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The explicit rank-two matrix classification is not stated or implied by the inspected 2026 primary text. The wider partial-inner construction and related open questions already appeared in the 2024 predecessor; this is specifically distinguished from the 2026 statement of the linear partial-inner problem. Nearby published records on rank-one matrix factorizations and partial isometries have different claims. Historical completeness cannot be established by search; broader matrix inverse-monoid work might contain an unrecognized equivalent result.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The result answers the two-dimensional finite-field instance of a motivated open classification problem, giving both a complete internal decomposition and a closed order formula. The nilpotent component has nontrivial local scalar groups whereas the idempotent component does not, a structural distinction that points to higher-rank questions.

## Limits and closest literature
The primary source is arXiv:2605.00041v1, especially Problem 7.9. The original precursor arXiv:2301.04252v2 (2024) is acknowledged as early work on the general construction and related problems; no novelty is claimed for those. The result is for dimension two over finite fields only and has no independent mathematical audit.
