For every finite-dimensional split basic algebra, the full scheme of ordered commuting idempotent tuples is a disjoint union of explicitly dimensioned affine spaces: the dimensions are weighted cuts of the symmetrized off-diagonal Peirce graph. Over finite fields its complete point-count census is a Potts/random-cluster evaluation, unchanged by radical multiplication whenever those Peirce dimensions stay fixed; for truncated quiver algebras the census across fields and tuple lengths recovers the path-multigraph Tutte polynomial.
