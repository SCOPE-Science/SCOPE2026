Peirce failures in a finite downset Heyting algebra are exactly the pairs \((A,B)\) for which the strict-predecessor boundary of the minimal points excluded by \(A\) is not contained in \(B\). If the underlying finite poset has a least or a greatest point and is not a singleton, there are at least \(|J(P)|-2\) ordered failures, with equality precisely for a two-level star oriented in either direction.
