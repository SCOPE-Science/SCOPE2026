For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\), the maximum length-three code forbidding only two-symbol prefix–suffix overlaps has \((q^3-q)/3\) words, and exactly \(q!(P_{q+1}-P_q)\) distinct labeled codes attain that size, where \(P_j\) are the Pell numbers. Every optimizer arises from a linearly ordered collection of singleton and paired alphabet symbols, with a unique canonical labeling of its two-symbol contexts.
