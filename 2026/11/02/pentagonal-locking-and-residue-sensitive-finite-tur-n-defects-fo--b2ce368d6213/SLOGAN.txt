For the finite cyclic Turán problem with support \(\{0,\pm1,\pm4\}\), the continuous value \(\sqrt5\) persists exactly when the modulus is divisible by five. Otherwise the mass is strictly larger, with a sharp \(N^{-2}\) defect whose leading coefficient splits into two classes according to the modulus modulo five; sufficiently large optimizers have two neighboring active Fourier constraints.
