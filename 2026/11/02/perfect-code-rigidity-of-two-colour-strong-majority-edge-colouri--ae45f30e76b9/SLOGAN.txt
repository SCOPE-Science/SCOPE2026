On a connected cubic graph, two-colour strong-majority edge colourings are exactly the two colour-swapped edge assignments induced by each efficient dominating set, giving \(b(G)=2p(G)\). Consequently, connected cubic vertex-transitive graphs of order a power of two have index two, except the Möbius-ladder family of index three; all Möbius ladders have exactly eight named two-colourings when their half-length is congruent to two modulo four, and none otherwise.
