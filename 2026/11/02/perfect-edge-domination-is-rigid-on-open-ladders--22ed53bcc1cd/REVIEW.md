# Scientific review: perfect edge domination on open ladders

**Correctness — PASS.** The definition concerns edges *outside* the selected family only, as required for perfect edge domination. All three selected-neighbor formulas are derived from the ladder incidence structure. The finite transition graph is exact for arbitrary length; its \(14\) relevant states, two components, and six-cycle prove the two-term polynomial for all \(n\ge2\). An independent edge-based exhaustive enumeration checked \(599184\) edge subsets at small lengths. Neither the graph search nor a finite length cutoff is treated as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** The 2026 work of Grippo et al. provides broad counting algorithms but no identified closed classification of the open-ladder PED polynomial. Chitra–Prabhavathi (2020) treats ladder minimum values and constructions, without counting all solutions or exhibiting the two-term polynomial; its printed \(n\equiv1\pmod3\) minimum assertion differs from direct and general verification here. Targeted literature, table, and mathematical-record searches returned no equivalent exact distribution. The unavailable full text of the 2026 algorithm paper and possible non-indexed historical results remain material priority risks.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The all-length result classifies an entire natural graph-product family at the level of *every* feasible solution, not only an optimum. The striking two-cardinality spectrum and three-periodic multiplicity identify rigidity that general counting algorithms and an ordinary domination parameter do not display. The result also supplies a concise exact finite-state certificate for reproduction and for testing generalizations.
