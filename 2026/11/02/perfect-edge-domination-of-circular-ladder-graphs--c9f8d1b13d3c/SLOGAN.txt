On the labelled prism \(C_n\square K_2\), the only perfect edge dominating set is the entire edge set unless \(6\mid n\); in that case exactly six further sets exist, each of size \(n\), obtained from the six rotations of one period-six edge pattern. Thus the entire cardinality polynomial is \(z^{3n}+6\mathbf{1}_{\{6\mid n\}}z^n\).
