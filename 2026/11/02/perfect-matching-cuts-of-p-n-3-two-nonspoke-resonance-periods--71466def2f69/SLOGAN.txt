For every \(n\ge7\), the generalized Petersen graph \(P(n,3)\) has exactly \(1+2\mathbf 1_{4\mid n}+5\mathbf 1_{5\mid n}\) perfect matching cuts: the spoke cut, two zero-spoke cuts at multiples of four, and five cuts with \(n/5\) crossing spokes at multiples of five. An exact 256-state cyclic-word reduction proves that no other cuts occur.
