# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The mathematical claim concerns the unique corner-accessible radial sector, not the entire Hilbert space's dark-state dimension. The proof constructs an explicit basis of the radial sector from normalized Walsh characters, computes the shell overlap exactly through \(K_r(k;d)\), and establishes the decay-versus-dark dichotomy by the imaginary part of the finite-dimensional dissipative Hamiltonian's eigenvalues. The quadratic identity \(2K_2(k;d)=(d-2k)^2-d\) gives the necessary and sufficient perfect-square condition without a finite search. The no-common-zero calculation for \(K_1\) and \(K_2\) handles the two-shell design. Stirling's approximation justifies the asymptotic. A standalone checker supplied independently compares direct and radial dynamics in small hypercubes and validates 1,130 finite diagnostic groups; no numerical inference is used as the infinite proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete infinite-family classification of the exact survival fraction under a physically natural whole-shell capture geometry. The previously unspecified integer-square exceptional dimensions sharply separate complete absorption from coherent indefinite survival. The exact binomial weight quantifies the effect and the positive two-shell result supplies a minimal natural robust design remedy. The limit fraction and capture-strength independence provide further structural information that is directly useful in choosing absorbing target geometry. The work does not claim a general graph theorem, an arbitrary-state statement or experimental implementation.
