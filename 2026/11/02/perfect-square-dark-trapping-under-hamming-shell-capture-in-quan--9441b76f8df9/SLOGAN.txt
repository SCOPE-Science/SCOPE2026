Uniform capture of the distance-two Hamming shell of a \(d\)-dimensional hypercube leaves an exact dark survival fraction only when \(d\ge2\) is a perfect square, in which case it is \(2^{1-d}\binom{d}{(d+\sqrt d)/2}\), independent of the positive capture rate. Monitoring the first shell as well removes every corner-accessible dark mode in all dimensions.
