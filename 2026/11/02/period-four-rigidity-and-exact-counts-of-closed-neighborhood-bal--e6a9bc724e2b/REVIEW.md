# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The closed neighborhood of a vertex of the Cartesian clique–cycle product has all clique-column vertices plus exactly two adjacent-cycle vertices of its row. Encoding two colors by signs gives the exact equation \(S_j+x_{a,j-1}+x_{a,j+1}=0\). Summation yields \(S_{j-1}+mS_j+S_{j+1}=0\). For even \(m\ge4\), the maximum-modulus argument forces all column sums to zero and then exact four-periodic antiperiod-two sign patterns. For \(m=2\), a cyclic sum-of-squares identity forces alternating column sums, and a complete dichotomy between nonzero and zero sums gives the two and four solution families. All boundary cases \(n\ge3\) are included. The verifier directly checks 10 small graphs and uses a different first-two-column generation on 40 parameter pairs. Finite computations are corroboration, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
The primary 2024 Gharibyan–Petrosyan manuscript studies decision complexity of locally balanced partitions, including the closed-neighborhood variant; it does not state the exact two-parameter enumeration. Gharibyan 2020 proves a closed-neighborhood existence criterion on rook graphs \(K_m\square K_n\), not the same graph for general cycle lengths, and supplies no corresponding counts. The 2021 paper and 2021 dissertation abstract discuss additional classes, notably open-neighborhood tori \(C_m\square C_n\). Five semantic research-record searches and direct searches for alternative equitable-partition, perfect two-coloring, and graph-product formulations found no theorem covering the stated count. These comparisons concern mathematical implications, not merely matching titles. The 2018 work on grid-like graphs was not fully inspected and could contain unindexed related material. Result is not independently priority-certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact existence pattern, all coloring structures, and labeled counts for an infinite natural Cartesian-product family refine the established existence/complexity direction of locally-balanced graph partitions. A genuine four-periodic rigidity occurs for every even clique order at least four, while the thin prism family has a distinct two-versus-six solution pattern. The binomial-square count makes all solutions explicitly constructible. Neither code execution nor being previously unindexed alone is offered as mathematical value.
