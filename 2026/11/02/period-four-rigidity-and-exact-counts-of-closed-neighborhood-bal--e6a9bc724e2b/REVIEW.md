# Scientific assessment

The theorem concerns even clique order at least four. The column-sum recurrence forces each clique column to contain equally many vertices of both colors. The remaining sign equations force a least period of four, and the two initial balanced columns can be chosen independently. This counts labelled colorings, not classes under rotations. The finite checker illustrates the classification but does not replace the recurrence proof.

The thin-prism count is a comparison, not part of the finding: the explicit parametrization in *Parity-kernel codes of cyclic prisms and their Reed–Muller support hierarchy* gives that count upon excluding monochromatic triples. Open-neighborhood balance and other graph products are outside the theorem. The literature comparisons do not establish historical priority.
