For every even \(m\ge4\), closed-neighborhood balanced two-colorings of \(K_m\square C_n\) exist precisely when \(4\mid n\), and then their exact labelled number is \(\binom{m}{m/2}^{2}\); every coloring repeats the four-column pattern \((u,v,-u,-v)\). The thin case \(m=2\) has exactly two colorings at circumferences congruent to two modulo four and six at multiples of four, and none at odd circumferences.
