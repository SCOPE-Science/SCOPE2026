Its Nash extension and Jacobian-minor valuation give multiplicity one and Mather discrepancy two, matching the published general formula exactly. Multiplication by the invertible projective-plane class followed by the rational Hodge realization yields Hodge-class equality. Finite-weight cancellation and semisimplicity give an actual rational Hodge isomorphism. Local Torelli plus countability of rational changes of marking gives countable fibers; Ehresmann's theorem fixes the homeomorphism class of the links.

Forey's prior local-density framework is broader than the elementary cone calculation. The additional statement is that the full motivic local density distinguishes uncountably many germs within one topological and Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity type, proved using preservation of full rational Hodge structures and local Torelli. Targeted searches of source text and mathematical records found no explicit equivalent. It remains possible this Hodge-separation consequence is already implicit in the combined published theories; no novelty claim is made for the source formula alone.

This result exhibits an infinite-dimensional contrast: classical multiplicity and link topology are constant while the motivic density detects countably fine rational Hodge periods. The quantified countability and distinct Picard-rank witness make this a structural boundary to topological invariance, rather than an arbitrary worked example.

**Limitations.** This is one-directional Hodge separation, not a full invariant of quartic-cone analytic types, and an independent mathematical review has not been performed.
