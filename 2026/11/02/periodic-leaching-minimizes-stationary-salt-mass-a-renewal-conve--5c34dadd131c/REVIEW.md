# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The affine post-pulse renewal recursion is contractive; the stationary calendar-time age has the standard size-biased renewal law. Direct renewal-reward and Laplace calculations yield the exact mean for every nonnegative linear uptake rate. Strict Jensen and a stationary-age stochastic domination argument establish sharp periodic optimality and the full zero-uptake increasing-convex order, respectively. The proof checks the infinite-second-moment boundary, the distinction between pulse- and calendar-time sampling, and the failure of first-order stochastic order. The companion exact algebra and deterministic-seed simulations are finite cross-checks, not substitutes for the argument.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence, subject to independent priority review.** Suweis et al. (2010) established Poisson-driven soil leaching, and Mau, Feng and Porporato (2014) investigated multiplicative Poisson jump models and their moments and crossings. Their exact assumptions do not imply a comparison across arbitrary iid renewal interarrival laws of common mean. Mau and Porporato (2015) analyze a deterministic coupled salinity–sodicity model, and Vico and Porporato (2010) analyze soil moisture rather than salt. Existing renewal-age mathematical comparisons found in topical searches concern correlations and overshoot, not the present stationary-salt inequality. The 2010 seasonal sodicity paper of van der Zee et al. could only be inspected at abstract level; this and potentially broader stochastic-order results are genuine residual priority risks.

**Value — PASS.** The exact penalty and universal zero-uptake convex-risk guarantee address a concrete, physically motivated random-timing question for soil salinization. They distinguish a useful risk guarantee from the false stronger claim of reduced exceedance probability at *every* fixed threshold. The theorem covers an entire class of renewal laws, rather than a one-case simulation or an unmotivated finite invariant.

**Scientific limits.** One-compartment, constant deposition, fixed leaching fraction, iid state-independent event times, and optional linear uptake. Positive uptake has mean-only optimality; all-convex-risk ordering is proved solely without uptake. No empirical effectiveness, nonlinear moisture feedback, salinity-threshold stochastic dominance or realistic optimization with water-cost constraints is established. An unavailable plausible source and non-exhaustive historical searches remain risk factors.
