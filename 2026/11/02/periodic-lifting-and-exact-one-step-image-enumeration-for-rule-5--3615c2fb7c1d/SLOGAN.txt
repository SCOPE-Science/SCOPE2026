For synchronous Rule 54 on an indexed binary ring, every circular word avoiding its four classical Garden-of-Eden blocks has a same-period predecessor at all lengths \(n\ge2\); the uniform-one state on the one-cell ring is the sole exception. The exact one-step image counts have generating function \(-zQ'(z)/Q(z)-z\), with \(Q(z)=(1+z^2)(1-2z+z^3-z^4)\), and grow like \(1.866760399\ldots^n\).
