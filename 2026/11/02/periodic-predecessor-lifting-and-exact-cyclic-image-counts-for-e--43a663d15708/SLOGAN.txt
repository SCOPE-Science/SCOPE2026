For binary cyclic Rule 126, `010` avoidance guarantees a predecessor after at most one period doubling. Nonconstant same-period failures occur exactly by odd parity of length-two one-runs and lift on precisely even multiples. Exception: the singleton `1` fails at length one but lifts at every length at least two, including odd lengths. A rational generating function counts every one-step indexed image and gives R_n=lambda^n-phi^n/2+O(1).
