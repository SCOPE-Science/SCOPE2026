For elementary cellular automaton Rule 126 on a binary circle, avoiding `010` gives a periodic predecessor after at most doubling the ring; the exact same-period obstruction is a parity condition on short one-runs, and doubling is sharp. The theorem also counts every one-step reachable indexed ring and gives asymptotic growth \(R_n=\lambda^n-\varphi^n/2+O(1)\) with \(\lambda\approx1.7548776662\).
