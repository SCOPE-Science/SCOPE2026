On an \(m\)-atom Boolean algebra, every permutation-equivariant fundamental preconditional is either ordinary material implication or a uniquely parameterized ternary-row operation with a degenerate negation. Two simple inter-row inequalities classify all the latter, giving exact counts \(2,4,13,55,261,1310,6802\) for \(m=2,\ldots,8\).
