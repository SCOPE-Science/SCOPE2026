## correctness

PASS

On the l_infinity-sum of normalized finite-level ell_p spaces, level-ancestor replication J_{n,m} is a genuine isometry intertwining root automorphisms. Thus the norm of any generated-algebra element is sup finite-level norms and its action on infinitely many replicated levels has a flat l_infinity subspace. Finite-rank perturbations have a nonzero kernel there; compact perturbations have arbitrarily small images on unit vectors. This proves all a_k=essential norm=norm. If A-lambda I is Fredholm, a left parametrix gives uniform lower bounds on all finite-level square matrices, forcing uniformly bounded inverses; hence it is globally invertible. Cycle roots then yield the spectral claims; finite level Hilbert permutation matrices give the p=2 chord gap. Independently enumerated all permutations through S7 and the >=sqrt(3) chord bound. No general-p Hilbert chord formula claimed.

## originality

PASS

Muthukumar–Ponnusamy and Liu–Ma develop boundedness and compactness of tree composition operators, the latter for arbitrary finite linear combinations. The source claim is not simply absence of compact cancellation: it includes flat approximation-number profiles, Fredholm spectral equality for norm-closed root automorphism algebra and exact cycle-norm gaps, not stated in the inspected Liu–Ma main compactness characterization. Broader abstract persistence results remain plausible.
