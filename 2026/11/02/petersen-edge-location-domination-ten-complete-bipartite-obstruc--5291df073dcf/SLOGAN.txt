The Petersen line graph has exactly \(246\) minimum ordinary locating-dominating codes of size \(5\), and their one-edge-exchange graph is connected with diameter \(5\). Every pair of optimum codes is joined within one exchange of the elementary set-difference lower bound; the \(90\) exceptional pairs form ten disjoint \(K_{3,3}\) graphs, one for each original Petersen vertex.
