The ordinary four-cut complex of the Petersen graph is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{26}S^5\), and its full \(S_5\)-action on fifth rational homology is \(S^{(5)}\oplus2S^{(4,1)}\oplus S^{(3,2)}\oplus2S^{(3,1,1)}\). Exactly one positive-dimensional rational homology class survives the full symmetry quotient.
