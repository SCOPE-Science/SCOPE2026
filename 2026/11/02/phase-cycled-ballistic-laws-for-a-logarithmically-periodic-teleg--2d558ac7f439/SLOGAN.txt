A telegraph walker whose reversal rate is \(r(\log(1+t))/(1+t)\) has ballistic mean-square growth, but a single rescaled displacement law exists exactly when the positive periodic function \(r\) is constant; otherwise the law cycles through a unique, explicitly constructed family of logarithmic phases. Its variance phase satisfies two closed linear equations, and harmonic rate modulation has an exact nonzero first-order oscillation amplitude.
