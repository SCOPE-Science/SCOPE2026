A \(d\)-mode coherent-state phase torus requires asymptotically \(2^{d/2}\) times as many optimal complex-linear dimensions when represented by rank-one Hilbert--Schmidt projectors rather than state vectors at the same fixed error tolerance. Exact widths arise from Poisson and Skellam Fourier masses, and a convolution-majorization argument proves nondecreasing approximation error for the phase lift of every pure state.
