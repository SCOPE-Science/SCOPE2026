## Correctness — PASS

The calculation uses the established permissible-pair classification for singular endomorphisms and verifies the red-fiber decomposition independently from all counting formulas. The core enumerative steps are bijections: rooted blue stars, unordered blue two-cycle leaf-count pairs, multisets inside red fibers, and multisets of red fibers. The decisive rank-two lemma is proved in both directions via an explicit tripotent square root exchanging two nondistinguished blue roots within one red fiber. The reduction from square roots to nonessentiality uses the product law and existence, for \(n\ge5\), of an even-type rank-three endomorphism. The integer recurrence follows from formal logarithmic differentiation, and the output counts are checked against exhaustive simultaneous-conjugacy calculations through \(n=6\). The finite checks are supportive only; the general proof is combinatorial and uses the cited structural theorems.
