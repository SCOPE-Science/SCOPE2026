For \(n\ge5\), the minimal number of generators of \(\operatorname{End}(\mathcal T_n)\) has a complete Euler-product coefficient formula: its essential rank-two orbits are counted by plane partitions minus ordinary partitions, while its rank-three orbits are the difference of two explicit unlabelled multiset transforms. The resulting generator counts begin \(35,86,207,503,1206\) for \(n=5,\ldots,9\).
