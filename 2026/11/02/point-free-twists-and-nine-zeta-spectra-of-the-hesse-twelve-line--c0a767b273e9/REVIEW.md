# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The domain is every smooth plane cubic over a finite field of characteristic \(>3\). The geometric premise is the classical Hesse configuration of nine flexes, twelve lines, nine ordinary quadruple points, and twelve ordinary double points (Artebani–Dolgachev, §§2–3). The exact Eisenstein-integer determinant-norm certificate independently excludes extra concurrence at primes greater than three. The proof labels the four distinct finite schemes (9 flexes, 12 lines, 36 quadruple branches, 24 double branches) and derives the zeta product by normalization at every extension degree. The affine action has precisely \(48\cdot9=432\) cases; `verify_hesse.py` explicitly checks all cycle signatures, the exact ten-type partition, determinant constraints, and the nine zeta signatures. The point-free criterion additionally has a direct linear-algebra proof, independent of the enumeration. The three displayed characteristic-seven cubics are checked by exact Hessian evaluation on every rational point and line; a separate split-line incidence check recovers the nine quadruple and twelve double points. Full infinitesimal singular schemes and characteristics two and three are excluded.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The genus-\(55\), geometrically reducible Hesse line curve is a canonical construction from an arbitrary smooth plane cubic. Its rational-point existence and zeta function are natural arithmetic invariants. The finding does more than recompute a textbook incidence count: it gives all extension-field counts uniformly for arbitrary plane embeddings, a necessary-and-sufficient intrinsic criterion for the entire union to be point-free, a smooth point-free example already over \(\mathbb F_7\), and a pair of twists whose identical zeta functions fail to detect distinct rational singularity strata. Those are meaningful arithmetic distinctions that cannot be inferred merely from the numbers of geometric lines and flexes. It does not claim a new geometric configuration or classify all possible twisted embeddings over every small finite field.
