For every smooth plane cubic in characteristic \(>3\), the reduced union of its twelve Hesse inflection lines has one of ten affine Frobenius incidence spectra and one of nine explicit rational zeta functions. The union has no rational point exactly when the \(3\)-torsion Frobenius is nontrivially unipotent and the plane-embedding class is not divisible by \(3\); this happens for an explicit smooth cubic over \(\mathbb F_7\).
