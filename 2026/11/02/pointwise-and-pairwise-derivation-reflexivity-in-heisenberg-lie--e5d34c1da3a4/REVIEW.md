# Review of pointwise and pairwise derivation reflexivity

## Correctness — PASS

Every derivation has a conformally symplectic quotient action with multiplier equal to its central scalar. Pointwise matching reduces to surjectivity of evaluation from the symplectic Lie algebra at each nonzero vector; the proof constructs rank-one symplectic endomorphisms spanning the evaluation image in every characteristic. Pairwise matching forces the induced alternating form to vanish on every symplectically orthogonal pair and hence be a scalar multiple of the fixed symplectic form. Conversely, independent quotient vectors allow independent central-functional interpolation, while dependent vectors allow replacement by a conformally symplectic block with the prescribed central multiplier. Explicit dimension and finite-field counts follow. The attached script checks the original Lie structure equations directly, including complete small finite-field cases; finite enumeration is not used to prove the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, with residual uncertainty

The 2015 local-derivation paper already shows nilpotent local non-derivations, including the three-dimensional Heisenberg case; that is prior knowledge, not the new claim. The 2015 two-local paper establishes existence of nonlinear two-local non-derivations for nilpotent Lie algebras, rather than the complete filtration for linear maps. The 2025 computational work covers low-dimensional local derivations, not the uniform linear two-local codimension-one description. A 2026 reflexive-hull paper studies Lie closure but not this explicit family. The relevant public texts and mathematical-index records were inspected for implication and special-case coverage; no complete stronger result was identified. Specialized earlier work remains a nonzero publication-priority risk, and no independent literature audit was performed.

## Value — PASS

The local and two-local derivation conditions are natural pointwise and pairwise algebraic reflexivity tests for a Lie-algebra derivation space. The theorem computes their entire filtration uniformly in symplectic rank and field characteristic, isolates a universal one-dimensional residual defect for pairwise matching, and gives an exact finite-field census rather than merely exhibiting examples. These invariant distinctions are meaningful even where individual low-dimensional existence witnesses were already known.

## Limits

Only linear two-local maps are characterized; no claim is made about arbitrary nonlinear two-local derivations. The classification is for Heisenberg algebras with one-dimensional center and does not settle other two-step nilpotent algebras. Publication priority remains subject to specialized historical sources; external mathematical validation is unperformed.
