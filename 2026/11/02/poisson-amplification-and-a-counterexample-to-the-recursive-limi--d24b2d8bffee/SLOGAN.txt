On complete uniformly valued \(n\)-ary trees, the number of successors passing \(k\) independent modal balance tests has exact law \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,[4^{-n}\binom{2n}{n}]^k)\), producing success-probability limits \(1\), a Poisson tail of mean \(1/\pi\), and \(0\) at \(k=1,2,\ge3\), respectively. The proposed recursive limit is always zero, so the convergent \(k=1\) instance refutes the published accumulation-point conjecture under its fixed-branching convention.
