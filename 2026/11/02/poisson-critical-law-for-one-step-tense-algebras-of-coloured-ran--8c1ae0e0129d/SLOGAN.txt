At edge density \(p_n=2^k(\log n+t)/n\), the \(2^{2k+1}\) coloured incoming/outgoing-neighbourhood defects converge to independent Poisson variables, and the first tense Boolean refinement gains a binomially distributed number of atoms. The probability that *no* modal refinement occurs, even at arbitrary modal depth, converges to \(\exp(-2^{k+1}e^{-t})\).
