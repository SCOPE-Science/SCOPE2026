# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The product stationary law at ordinary first appearance is independent of that label-based stopping time because last-appearance marks for distinct coupon types are independent; this gives exact factorization. Independent rate-one Poissonized refresh clocks and the stationary first-entry convolution provide the exact finite generating function. Uniform log-product convergence under bounded total diffuse loss and vanishing maximum loss, dominated convergence, and the classical coupon Gumbel theorem establish the weak limit. The product immigration–death/two-state construction proves the limiting transform is that of a proper hitting-time law and gives the exact mass at zero. Finite-state rational computations and numerical convergence checks reproduce 265 assertions. These experiments are not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Attrill–Garoni (2026, arXiv:2605.14206, Sections 3–4, Theorem 4.10) treat one common loss probability and identify the homogeneous critical queue. Long (2026, arXiv:2605.14511, Sections 1–4) treats fixed common clumsiness and a separate global reset. Gast (2011, arXiv:1107.3385, Sections 2–4) treats identical absorbing scanned chains. The result here has a type-dependent finite generating transform, an all-diffuse profile universality statement and a distinct mixed critical law with finitely many persistent two-state modes. Existing resolvent and Poisson-approximation tools are acknowledged, not claimed as original. Searches for heterogeneous selected-type loss, nonidentical refresh chains, coupon first-hit distributions, and finite defects did not expose equivalent complete coverage. An unindexed general hitting-time result could subsume this specialization, so historical priority needs independent checking.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The original critical coupon model has one loss parameter and hence cannot distinguish many individually negligible defects from one macroscopic unreliable coupon. The theorem identifies exactly which heterogeneity is invisible and which persists: the diffuse contribution becomes an immigration–death queue while isolated unreliable types remain two-state modes. The explicit atom \(e^{-c}\prod_j(1-q_j)\) and one-defect mixture demonstrate a nontrivial boundary of universality with direct interpretation as the remaining collection delay after every type has appeared. This is a natural critical scaling question, not an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Scientific limitations and priority risks

Only weak convergence is established. Coupon sampling is uniform; losses are independent selected-type redraws, with finitely many persistent modes and bounded diffuse total loss intensity. The homogeneous \(r=0\) queue law is known. Some broad product-chain renewal/target-hitting theorem may imply a still more general statement; no independent priority or mathematical audit has occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
