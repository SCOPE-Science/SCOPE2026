In clumsy coupon collection with type-specific loss probabilities, a diffuse cloud of vanishing losses contributes only its total intensity to the critical completion-time limit, while each of finitely many persistent-loss types survives as an independent two-state obstruction. The exact finite generating function yields an explicit Laplace transform for the limiting Gumbel-plus-product-chain hitting law, including a sharp zero-time atom that distinguishes macroscopic loss types from diffuse losses.
