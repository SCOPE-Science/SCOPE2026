In the finite-intensity window \(N(1-\alpha_N)\to\lambda\), rare failures to copy a past step produce independent Poisson component counts of means \(\lambda/[k(k+1)]\) in the associated recursive forest. The uniform two-state reinforced walk has exactly uniform odd-time positions, while its even-time total-variation distance converges sharply to \(2^{-\lambda-1}\); the root-cluster deficit converges to a heavy-tailed compound-Poisson law.
