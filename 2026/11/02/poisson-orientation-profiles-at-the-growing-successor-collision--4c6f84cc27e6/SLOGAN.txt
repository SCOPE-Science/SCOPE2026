For any fixed \(j\ge3\), \(j\)-way common adjacencies across \(k\) independent random linear-order successors undergo a sharp transition at \(k\asymp n^{1-2/j}\). At the critical scale, the orientation-resolved counts converge jointly in total variation to explicitly parameterized independent Poisson variables, yielding nontrivial limiting probabilities for two-variable existential overlap formulas.
