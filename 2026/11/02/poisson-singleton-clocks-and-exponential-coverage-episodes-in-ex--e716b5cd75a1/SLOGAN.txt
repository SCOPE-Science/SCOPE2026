After a rare entrance into complete coverage by a sliding coupon window of length \(\lfloor\alpha n\log n\rfloor\), with fixed \(0<\alpha<1\), uniquely represented coupons generate a unit-rate Poisson clock on the \(n^\alpha\) time scale. The consecutive complete-window episode lasts asymptotically \(n^\alpha\) times an exponential random variable of mean one, and its exact finite mean is a ratio of Stirling numbers.
