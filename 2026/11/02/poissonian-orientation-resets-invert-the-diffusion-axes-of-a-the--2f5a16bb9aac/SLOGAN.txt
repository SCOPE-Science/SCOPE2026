Poisson resets of only the orientation of a thermally coupled Brownian colloid have an exact long-time drift and diffusion tensor for every positive-definite body mobility. When the independent translational noise is isotropic, the major diffusion axis reverses at a universal reset-to-rotation ratio \(\rho_\star\approx0.8105357\), while longitudinal spreading is maximized at the distinct ratio \(\rho=2\).
