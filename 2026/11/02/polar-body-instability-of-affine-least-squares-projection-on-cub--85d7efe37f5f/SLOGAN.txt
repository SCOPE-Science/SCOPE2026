On the solid polar cube–cross-polytope pair, affine \(L^2\) least-squares projection has exact rational \(L^\infty\) norms, attained only at spatial vertices: on the cross-polytope the sharp norm grows as \(d/2\), but on the cube it grows as \(\sqrt{6d/\pi}\). The resulting ratio grows as \(\sqrt{\pi d/24}\), quantifying a divergent stability gap between dual bodies with the same affine target dimension.
