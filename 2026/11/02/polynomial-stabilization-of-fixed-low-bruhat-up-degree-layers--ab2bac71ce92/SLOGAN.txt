For every fixed \(r\ge1\), the number of permutations with exactly \(r\) upward strong-Bruhat covers is eventually a polynomial of degree \(r\) in the length, with leading coefficient \(1/r!\); its ordinary generating function has denominator \((1-x)^{r+1}\) and a numerator of degree at most \(2r\). Thus every fixed-width initial coefficient segment of the boxed-\(12\) distribution eventually increases strictly, without resolving global unimodality.
