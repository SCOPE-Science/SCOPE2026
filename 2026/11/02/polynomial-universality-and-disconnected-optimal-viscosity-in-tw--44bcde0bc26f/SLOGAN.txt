For any integer polynomial vanishing at \(0\) and \(1\), two equally sized rooted delegation trees can realize it exactly as the difference of their viscosity-dependent guru weights. Thus two-guru voting accuracy can have arbitrarily many disconnected optimal viscosity intervals, any preselected finite grid can miss a better interval by an accuracy gap of \(1/10\), and a six-voter star-versus-path forest is the smallest instance with a strict interior advantage over both endpoints.
