# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The removal-and-standardization coupling gives a pointwise record identity, after which a product of classical rising-factorial record polynomials gives the exact conditional probability-generating functions. Adjacent position subtraction makes every upper-tail difference an explicitly signed, nonnegative-coefficient expression. Newton coefficient unimodality justifies the total-variation identity; summing tail gaps justifies Wasserstein distance. The included Python verifier passed 193,404 exact arithmetic assertions for the specified finite range, corroborating rather than replacing the all-size proof.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority qualification
The exact record polynomial, basic fixed-point law and standard polynomial-real-rootedness inequalities are known. The new statement is the position-balancing *all-threshold* ordering of record counts conditional on \(\pi(i)=i\), with sharp adjacent tail gaps and transport/variation distances. Inspected sources on fixed points, records, mesh patterns, and strong fixed points did not give these cross-position inequalities. The possibility of an implicit result in older permutation-pattern or record literature is not excluded, and should be checked independently before asserting historical first discovery.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Conditioning a uniformly random permutation to match a prescribed position is natural in permutation statistics and exact randomization. The theorem provides a complete ordering of conditional distributions for all positions and thresholds, not merely a moment identity; it also explains why local record associations can reverse sign in interior positions despite a positive total fixed-point/record covariance. Its exact Wasserstein and total-variation laws quantify the distributional sensitivity to one-step position balancing.
