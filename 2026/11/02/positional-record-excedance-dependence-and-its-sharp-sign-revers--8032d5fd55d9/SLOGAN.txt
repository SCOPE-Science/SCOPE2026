For a uniform random permutation, each earlier excedance indicator is negatively correlated with a later record indicator, and the same-position covariance is nonnegative (strictly positive away from the final boundary), giving an exact lower-triangular mixed covariance matrix. Consequently the total record count and total excedance count change from negative covariance for \(2\le n\le4\) to positive covariance for every \(n\ge5\), approaching \(1/2\) while their correlation tends to zero.
