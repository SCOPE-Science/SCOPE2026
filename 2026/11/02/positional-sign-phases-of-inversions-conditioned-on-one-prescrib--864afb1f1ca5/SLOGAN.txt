Conditioning a uniform permutation to contain one prescribed two-cycle produces an exact positional inversion excess \(\frac12+\frac{(n-2i)(2j-n-2)}{n-2}\). In the macroscopic limit this excess is positive for pairs straddling the midpoint and negative for same-side pairs, although the aggregate covariance with the total two-cycle count is only \(1/12\).
