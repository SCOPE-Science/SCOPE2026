In a uniformly random classical parking function, the count of lucky cars and the number of distinct requested spaces are strictly positively associated under *every* pair of increasing recodings. Their exact covariance is \(n+2-\tfrac{5n+7}{2}(n/(n+1))^n\), with limiting Pearson correlation approximately \(0.630879\); a car's contribution is greatest at approximately \(56.7\%\) of the arrival order.
