For an equal-arm four-point metric tree, the sharp positive-cone distortion of the canonical Lipschitz-free \(p\)-space inclusion is achieved by the balanced two-leaf vector and one uniquely determined stem length whenever distortion exceeds one. The optimum is \(\max\{1,[2(1+h_p)^p/(2^ph_p^p+2)]^{1/p}\}\), with \(h_p=(2^p/(2-2^p))^{1/p}-1\).
