None of those inspected results directly implies the joint same-normal pair, its association inequality, or a triaxial joint density and inverse-normal formula without the new simultaneous-geometry and beta-product argument. A separately indexed classical dependence theorem may provide a route to the result, and exhaustive historical priority has not been established.
