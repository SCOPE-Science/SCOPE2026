For two-state Markov generators, a positive rate-dependent random choice of the forward and reversed Strang orders reproduces the exact transition matrix for **every positive step size**, and equal relaxation rates make the fair mixture exact even without commutation. Three states form a sharp boundary: an irreducible symmetric path split into its two edges has the identical positive trace defect \((1-e^{-h})^4/4\) for both orders, so no affine mixture is exact.
