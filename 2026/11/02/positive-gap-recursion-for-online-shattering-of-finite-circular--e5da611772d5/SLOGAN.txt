For all \(n\), the Littlestone dimension of membership queries for all cyclic intervals on \(n\) vertices is computed exactly by a positive-gap recursion with at most \(p(n)\) partition states. The first ten dimension thresholds occur at \(n=1,2,3,5,7,10,14,20,28,40\), and six vertices give the first failure of the hypothesis-counting bound: \(\operatorname{Ldim}=4<5\) despite 32 concepts; meanwhile VC dimension is three and online dimension grows as \(2\log_2n+O(1)\).
