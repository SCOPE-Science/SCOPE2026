# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim has explicit quantifiers \(k\ge1\), \(h\ge0\), \(n\ge1\), and positive integer multiplier \(M\). At an odd prime inert in \(\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)\), the Fibonacci companion matrix satisfies \(Q^p=-Q^{-1}\) and \(Q^{p^2}=Q\); these are derived from irreducibility and Frobenius, not assumed from an unverified experiment. The odd-index binomial coefficients \(\binom{2k}{j}\) are all even, eliminating the matrix sign. Thus \(F_{(p+h)^{2k}}\equiv F_{(h-1)^{2k}}\pmod p\). The fixed comparison difference \(\Delta_{h,k}>0\) is checked at \(h=1\) and \(h\ge2\) separately. Infinitely many inert primes avoid the finite divisors of \(M\Delta_{h,k}\), forcing prime-index Dold failure for every \(M\). The \(h=0\) positive assertion follows from [1, Corollary 3], and its sharp minimal multiplier from the \(n=5\) Dold condition. Executable tests verify finite instances but are not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Moss–Ward [1] proves the monomial \(h=0\) branch, with no positive-translation classification in the entire inspected preprint. Luca–Ward [2, Theorem 1] proves positive results for monomial samples of linear recurrences; it does not imply a negative theorem for a specific shifted Fibonacci sequence. Jaidee–Moss–Ward [3, Theorem 5] excludes nonmonomials only from *universal* realizability-preserving time changes, not from the weaker existential property for one particular recurrence after an arbitrary multiplier. The OEIS A054783 table [4] is a numerical sequence record, not a Dold denominator theorem. Targeted semantic research searches did not identify an equivalent claim. A later unindexed or inaccessible result could still exist; mathematical-record search is not a proof of absolute priority.

## Value — PASS

The motivating work isolates a surprising repair of Fibonacci sampling by even powers. The complete nonnegative-translation boundary is an independently meaningful rigidity question about the smallest natural polynomial deformation of that construction. An infinite simultaneous classification for every positive translation and every even power exponent, and infinitely many explicit prime-index obstructions for each one, distinguish the result from a finite example or routine parameter substitution. The result does not claim a general classification for all polynomial time changes.
