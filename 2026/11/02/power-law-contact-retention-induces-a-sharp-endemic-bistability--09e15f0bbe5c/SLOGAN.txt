For a homogeneous adaptive SIS system whose contact-breaking rate is proportional to \((1-y)^q\), the exact response-power threshold \(q_c=1+1/\omega\) separates forward endemic onset from a bistable saddle-node wedge. At the invasion threshold, the global critical extinction profile changes sharply from \(y(t)\sim C/t\) to \(y(t)\sim\sqrt{\omega/t}\), independently of contact-adjustment speed.
