For every \(r\ge2\), the least binary weight of a positive multiple of \((2^r-1)^k\) remains exactly \(r\), however large \(k\) becomes; a single bit position can be lifted through every power. Consequently the gcd of \(\binom{2n}{n}\) over all positive \(n\) divisible by \((2^r-1)^k\) is exactly \(2^r\), independently of \(k\).
