# Review: matrix-power-zero dual fibers and generic reducedness

## Correctness — PASS
Expanding \((A+\varepsilon B)^m\) gives the derivative with no characteristic assumptions. A rectangular Jordan-block space is a cyclic module for two commuting nilpotent shifts; the identity \((u-v)\sum u^jv^{m-1-j}=u^m-v^m\) makes the image one-dimensional in each surviving homogeneous degree. Directly checking the first and last surviving degree proves the exact rank \(\max(0,b+c-m)\). Nilpotent conjugacy orbit sizes provide the finite-field census. The dense partition's centralizer and tangent rank give the excess \(\min\{r,m-r\}\). The smooth dense orbit and regular-local-ring criterion establish the *generic*, not global, reducedness statement. A standalone checker verifies 612 rectangular ranks, exhaustive small dual-number counts, and 72 generic excess instances.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Weyman's 2000 paper works with additional characteristic-polynomial equations for the reduced orbit closure. Prasad–Singla–Spallone's 2012 preprint already derives the same power-map differential and a rectangular matrix-unit formula; those ingredients are credited, not claimed. Earlier square-zero chain-ring censuses already cover the full exponent-two arithmetic specialization, and a previously published one-Jordan-block index distribution covers the single-block arithmetic subcase for arbitrary exponent. Both are excluded from independent novelty. The evaluated contribution is the multi-block rank-and-count result for exponents at least three and the sharp generic-reducedness divisibility criterion for the unaugmented entry-equation scheme. Searches did not identify complete equivalent coverage. The primary residual risk is that the consequences of the older differential computations have been stated elsewhere or are regarded as immediate in the literature; publication priority needs independent assessment.

## Value — PASS
The classification quantifies first-order degeneracy in a natural nonreduced representation scheme across every rank, power and characteristic. Its sharp divisibility boundary connects orbit geometry to infinitesimal deformations, and the same rank data produce exact counts of solutions over finite dual-number rings, beyond a single-Jordan-block calculation. This is a complete, motivated family-level invariant rather than an arbitrary finite computation.

## Scientific limitations
The arithmetic census is for split dual numbers. The geometry concerns generic reducedness of a scheme defined by entry equations, not reducedness of all scheme points or the scheme formed after adding characteristic-polynomial equations. Finite enumeration is corroboration, not proof. Publication priority and independent validation remain open.
