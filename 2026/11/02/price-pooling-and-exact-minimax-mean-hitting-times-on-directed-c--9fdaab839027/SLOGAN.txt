The unique fastest worst-case mean-hitting-time design on every positively priced one-way Markov cycle pools all transitions below an endogenous price threshold, and assigns more expensive transitions rates proportional to inverse square-root price. A single scalar equation gives every optimal rate and the exact value \(M^2/B\); for three transition prices \((1,1,Q)\), pooling breaks sharply at \(Q=2\).
