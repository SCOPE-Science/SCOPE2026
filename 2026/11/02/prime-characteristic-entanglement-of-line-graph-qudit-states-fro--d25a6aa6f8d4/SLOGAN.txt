For every finite simple base graph, every partition of its edges, and every prime local dimension, the full Schmidt spectrum of the corresponding line-graph qudit state is determined by a signed overlap matrix of bipartite component indicators: \(S_p=|V|-q_A-q_B+\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}(T_A^{\mathsf T}T_B)\). On Hamilton-path cuts of complete graphs of even order, the qubit spectrum has a one-unit entropy deficit while every odd-prime qudit spectrum is maximally entangled across that cut.
