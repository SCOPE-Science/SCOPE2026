For a single absorber on \(C_p\square C_q\) with odd-prime cycle orders and rational positive hopping ratio, every starting-site capture probability is exactly \(1\), \(1/2\), or \(1/4\), except on an isotropic equal-prime square. At that unique rational resonance, a universal \(1/8\) class appears and the bright dimension drops from \((p+1)^2/4\) to \((p+1)(p+3)/8\).
