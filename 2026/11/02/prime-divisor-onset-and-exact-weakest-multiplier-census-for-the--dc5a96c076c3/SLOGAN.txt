For the twisted baker map in dimension \(M\), the smallest eigenvalue modulus among return-period-\(n\) points is exactly \(2^{\min(n,p)/M}\), where \(p\) is the smallest prime divisor of \(M\); spectral-modulus diversity first appears at length \(p+1\).
