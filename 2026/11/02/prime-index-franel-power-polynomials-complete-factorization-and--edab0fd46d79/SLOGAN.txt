For every odd prime \(p\) and every exponent \(r\ge3\), the prime-index Franel power polynomial \(\sum_{k=0}^{p}\binom pk^r X^k\) factors over \(\mathbb Q\) as \((X+1)\) times one irreducible polynomial; all its roots are simple, real, negative, and reciprocal. The nontrivial factor defines a totally ramified degree-\(p-1\) extension at \(p\), with discriminant valuation exactly \(p-2\) and unit class \((-1)^{(p-1)/2}\) modulo \(p\).
