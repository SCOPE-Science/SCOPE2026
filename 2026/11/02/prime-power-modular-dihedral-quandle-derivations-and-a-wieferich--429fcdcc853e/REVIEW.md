# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof begins from the actual quandle multiplication, reducing the Leibniz equations to \(F_{2j-i}=\sigma F_i+\tau F_j\), and then, by two substitutions and the invertibility of doubling on odd residues, to signed-doubling eigenconditions and a Jensen equation. The Jensen equation is additive on \(\mathbb Z/p^a\mathbb Z\) and yields exactly two independent parameters \(u,v\in W\). Every converse is checked, and the ordinary ring derivation \(\delta\) exists precisely because the modulus vanishes in the coefficient field. The orbit count accounts for every nonzero residue, including nonunits, and their stabilizers are compatible with the character; the exponent-lifting formula is valid for odd primes. The commutator formula follows from the explicitly displayed differential-operator normal form and respects its eigenspace conditions. Independent constraint-rank calculations for orders \(9,27,25\), with direct quandle multiplication, agree with predicted dimensions; direct operator tests cover order \(49\). Neither finite testing nor a logarithm requiring invalid denominators is used to prove the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS relative to available evidence

The closest inspected primary source, Elhamdadi–Makhlouf–Silvestrov–Zappala (arXiv:2106.08289v1), has a general linear-constraint proposition and characteristic-zero classification; its explicit positive-characteristic tables terminate at order six. Those statements do not give the modular prime-power parameterization or the multiplicative-order/Wieferich dimension formula. Previously obtained formulas for prime modulus and odd moduli coprime to field characteristic are excluded from this claim: they address neither \(p^a\) with \(a\ge2\) nor the odd-prime order-lifting transition. Search results for quandle rings, their complex representations, and later idempotent/automorphism studies do not establish the same derivation result. Residual risk: a general modular group-ring/quandle-algebra formula or equivalent unpublished treatment could yield this theorem; database non-matches alone are not uniqueness evidence.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The statement supplies a complete structural classification of a natural infinite family of nonassociative algebras whose derivations are not determined in the motivating modular literature. The dimension grows linearly with the exponent for ordinary primes but can jump at the first repeated-prime-power level when \(2\) is a Wieferich base, a genuine arithmetic boundary invisible in the prime-modulus result. It also fixes the entire Lie bracket, not just a numerical invariant, and provides exact counts over finite coefficient fields.

## Scientific limitations

The result excludes even moduli, general mixed-prime composite moduli, and internal derivation classifications. It relies on a mathematical proof, with finite computations as cross-checks only. Publication priority is not globally certified.
