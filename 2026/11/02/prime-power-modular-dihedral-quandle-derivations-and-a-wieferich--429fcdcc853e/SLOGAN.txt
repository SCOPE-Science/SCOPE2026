For dihedral quandle algebras of odd prime-power order \(p^a\), with \(a\ge2\) over characteristic \(p\), every derivation is uniquely \(M_u+M_v\delta\) for two signed-doubling eigenvectors, and their full Lie bracket is explicit. The exact dimension is \(2(p-1)/L\sum_{j=1}^a p^{\min(j-1,s-1)}\), where \(L=\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\) and \(s=\nu_p(2^{\operatorname{ord}_p(2)}-1)\); base-two Wieferich primes produce a sharp arithmetic jump.
