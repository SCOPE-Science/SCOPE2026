For every prime \(p\) and exponent \(a\ge1\), a fixed prime-power Pascal column has an explicit complete valuation-density distribution with an exact geometric tail. Each valuation threshold \(v\ge1\) has the sharp least period \(p^{a+v-1}\); in particular, \(n\mid\binom n{p^a}\) has density \(\frac{p}{p+1}(1-p^{-2a})\) and least period \(p^{2a-1}\).
