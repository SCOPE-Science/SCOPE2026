The minimum number of states needed for a binary permutation automaton to distinguish the block-swapped words \(0^a1^b\) and \(1^b0^a\) admits an exact formula from symmetric-group exponents, with explicit exceptions in degrees three and four. Exactly the prime-power state counts \(q\ge3\) can arise as minima, and for prime powers \(q\ge7\) their shortest witnesses have length \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,q-1)+1\).
