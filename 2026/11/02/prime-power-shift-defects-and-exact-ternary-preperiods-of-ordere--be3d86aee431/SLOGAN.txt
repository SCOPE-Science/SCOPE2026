For ordered Bell numbers, an explicit rational Stirling sum determines the complete defect of every odd-prime-power Carmichael index shift: \(F_{n+\varphi(p^k)}-F_n\equiv-p^nR_{p,n}\pmod{p^k}\). The least preperiod modulo \(3^k\) is \(k\) for even \(k\), \(k-1\) for odd \(k\ge3\), and one for \(k=1\).
