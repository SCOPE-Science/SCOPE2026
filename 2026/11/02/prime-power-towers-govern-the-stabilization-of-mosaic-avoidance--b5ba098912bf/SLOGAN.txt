For any fixed nonempty finite forbidden prime set, the error between finite-depth and complete prime-mosaic avoidance densities is asymptotic to a positive explicit Euler-product constant times \(r^{-M_i}\), where \(M_1\) is the least forbidden prime and \(M_{i+1}=r^{M_i}\) with \(r\) the least permitted prime. The unique first forbidden-depth witness drives the sharp iterated-exponential stabilization.
