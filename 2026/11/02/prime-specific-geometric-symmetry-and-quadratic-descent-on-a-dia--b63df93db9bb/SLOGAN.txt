On the explicit diagonal degree-four del Pezzo surface \(\sum_{i=0}^4 x_i^2=\sum_{i=0}^4 i x_i^2=0\), the geometric automorphism group jumps from order \(32\) to \(96,160,96\) precisely in characteristics \(7,11,13\), while its prime-field automorphism group always has order \(32\). Every additional geometric automorphism appears over the quadratic extension, and a complete fractional-linear interpolation and quadratic-character certificate explains the obstruction to descent.
