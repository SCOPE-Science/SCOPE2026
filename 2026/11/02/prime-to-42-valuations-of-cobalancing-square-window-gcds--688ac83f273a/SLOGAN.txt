For cobalancing numbers, every prime-power divisor of the infinite square-window GCD outside \(2,3,7\) is determined exactly by \(\gcd(5k,B_k,C_k-1)\). In particular, the valuation at \(5\) is \(1+\nu_5(k)\) precisely when \(6\mid k\), and vanishes otherwise; the three excluded primes remain unresolved.
