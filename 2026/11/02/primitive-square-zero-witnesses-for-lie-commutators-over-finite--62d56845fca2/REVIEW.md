The adjoint kernel therefore has cardinality \(q^n\), and its image is precisely the trace-orthogonal hyperplane. Parametrization by a kernel-image line and a unit scalar gives the correct count of admissible first factors. Solving the resulting primitive binary quadratic over the finite chain ring gives the stated discriminant/valuation multiplicities. The proof handles both equicharacteristic and mixed-characteristic rings when the residue characteristic is odd.
