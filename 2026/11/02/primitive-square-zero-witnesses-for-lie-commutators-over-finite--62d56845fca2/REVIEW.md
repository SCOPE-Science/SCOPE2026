# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A primitive square-zero traceless 2-by-2 operator is conjugate to E over a finite local principal ideal ring: a unimodular image/kernel line and a unit map R^2/l -> l give precisely (q^n+q^(n-1))(q^n-q^(n-1)) possible X. Because 2 is invertible, ad_E on sl2 has image RE+RH={Z:tr(EZ)=0} with exactly q^n preimages, and conjugacy preserves this trace-orthogonality condition. For a primitive isotropic line [u1:u2], trace-orthogonality is b u1^2-2c u1u2-a u2^2=0. Division by pi^s reduces to the length-(n-s) primitive binary quadratic with q^s projective lifts; the primitive discriminant 4(c0^2+a0b0) reduces locally to t^2=delta, giving N_r(delta)=q^floor(r/2) at delta=0, 2q^h for an even valuation 2h and square leading unit, zero otherwise. Independent complete Z/9 enumeration of 72 permitted first operands and all 729 Y for every 729 target Z matched every fiber, including zero and nonsplit targets. The universal claim follows from projective and valuation arguments rather than finite enumeration.

## originality

PASS

Qualitative traceless commutator surjectivity and unconstrained determinant/cross-product fibers are prior work. Stasinski does not count fibers with X forced to be primitive square zero, for which non-split target strata genuinely have zero realizations. Originality is a best-of-knowledge claim about this constrained pointwise counting formula, not the standard commutator identity or its unrestricted surjectivity.

## value

PASS

Exact constrained representation multiplicities in sl2 over arbitrary finite odd-characteristic chain rings are meaningful natural pointwise invariants; they reveal local quadratic square-class obstruction hidden by the unrestricted commutator theorem. This is an infinite-ring-family classification, not a routine reparameterization of ordinary determinant counts.
