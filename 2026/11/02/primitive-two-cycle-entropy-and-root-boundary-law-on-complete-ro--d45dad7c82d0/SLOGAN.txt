On every finite full rooted \(d\)-ary tree, the complete primitive two-cycle population of synchronous chip-firing satisfies an explicit two-variable recursion, and its per-vertex exponential growth constant is a convergent series (about \(2.0432435051\) for binary trees). A uniformly chosen two-cycle also has an exact size-biased binomial law for the number of opposite-phase root edges; despite the large orbit population, the per-vertex growth constant approaches \(1\) as \(d\to\infty\).
