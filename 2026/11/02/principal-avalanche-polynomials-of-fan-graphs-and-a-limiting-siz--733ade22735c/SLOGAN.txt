For the fan graph \(K_1\vee P_n\) with its hub as sandpile sink, the entire principal-avalanche polynomial has an exact rational generating function, and every nonzero avalanche is precisely a maximal saturated path run. As \(n\to\infty\), its avalanche-size distribution converges to an atom of mass \(1-1/\sqrt5\) at zero plus probabilities \(\ell\alpha^{-\ell}/\sqrt5\) at each positive size \(\ell\), where \(\alpha=(3+\sqrt5)/2\).
