For every finite Hamming graph \(K_{n_1}\square\cdots\square K_{n_d}\), convex vertex-set concepts have exact online dimension \(1+\sum_i(n_i-1)\), while a support is statically shattered exactly when each selected vertex possesses a private coordinate value. When at least two factors are nontrivial, the VC dimension is \(\sum_i(n_i-1)\), producing a uniform gap of one with a complete structural classification of shattered supports.
