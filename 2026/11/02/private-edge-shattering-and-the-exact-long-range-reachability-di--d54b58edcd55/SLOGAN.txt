Reachability queries on arbitrary orientations of a fixed tree are shattered exactly when their directed paths agree on shared edges and each path has a private edge. For a path with \(m=q(d+1)+r\) edges and only ordered-pair queries at undirected distance at least \(d\), the exact VC dimension is \(2q+\mathbf1_{\{r=d\}}\), with \(0\le r\le d\).
