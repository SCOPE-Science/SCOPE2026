On a scalar quadratic, averaging four fresh Gaussian zeroth-order probes can turn an almost-surely contracting step into an almost-surely expanding one even when averaging two probes contracts, at the same step \(a=107/40\). For many probes, the local pathwise-stability crossing is \(2+4/n+O(n^{-2})\), while the exact mean-square boundary is \(2-4/n+O(n^{-2})\).
