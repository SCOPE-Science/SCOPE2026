For the naturally graded non-Lie filiform Leibniz algebra \([e_i,e_1]=e_{i+1}\), the reduced variety of idempotent derivations consists of two small affine components in every odd characteristic but gains two additional components of dimensions \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) and \(1+\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) in characteristic two. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), their complete rank distribution yields exactly \(q+1\) projection derivations for odd \(q\), versus \((q+1)(q^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}+1)\) for even \(q\).
