Every idempotent derivation of a finite-poset incidence algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) lies in a unique radical-unit orbit indexed by a Boolean additive cocycle, with orbit size and derivation rank given by its support weight. In characteristic two the number of orbits is \(2^{|P|-c+\beta_1}\), and the complete rank polynomial is the cocycle support enumerator \(\sum_{\alpha}q^{w(\alpha)}T^{w(\alpha)}\).
