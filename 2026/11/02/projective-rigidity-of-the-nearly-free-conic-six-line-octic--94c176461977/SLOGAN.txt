Among nearly free complex plane arrangements of one smooth conic and six lines with only nodes, tacnodes, and ordinary triple points, there is exactly one projective-equivalence class: its singularity basket is \((3,3,6)\), and it is represented by the previously constructed \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) arrangement. The new result proves rigidity and excludes every other basket in this component type; it does not claim a new existence example.
