The geometric antipodal quotient of the scale-three Vietoris–Rips complex of the binary five-cube has precisely two integral torsion groups, \(H_1\cong H_3\cong\mathbb Z/2\), and free \(H_7\cong\mathbb Z^5\). Its degree-one mod-two covering class has nonzero fourth power but zero fifth power, revealing a projective-space cup-product tower invisible in the cover's rational sphere-wedge homology.
