For a free-semigroup endomorphism whose generator images all have length two, rank preservation is equivalent to a pseudoforest condition, and composition irreducibility is equivalent to a single spanning cycle in the associated word-edge multigraph. This gives \(n!2^{n-1}(n-1)!\) irreducible endomorphisms for \(n\ge3\), plus an exact generating function for all rank-preserving image sets.
