For the even-exponent Ma–Wang maximal curves, the natural plane Kummer model has exactly \(q^2-q\) unibranch cusps and a Gauss map of purely inseparable degree \(q\), with image function field \(k(C)^q\). Its dual plane curve has exact degree \(q(q^n+1)\), even though the original plane model has degree \(q^n+1\).
