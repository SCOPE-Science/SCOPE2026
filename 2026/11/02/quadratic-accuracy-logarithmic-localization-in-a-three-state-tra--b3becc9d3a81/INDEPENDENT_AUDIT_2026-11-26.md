## correctness

PASS

At stationarity an episode with entry position V and holding time T contributes occupation at age t with factor P(T>t), independently of V. Normalization yields the exact independent age Exp(ελ_j) and the additive entry shift −ε log D_j. Translating Exp(λ_j/b) by u changes TV by 1−exp(−λ_j|u|/b). On entrance to +, |log D_+| is dominated by h(t)+log 2 up to log 2/b and by 2e^(−bt) thereafter, with ∫h=π²/(6b). On entrance to 0, D_0=|1−exp(−b(t−t_*))| after reflection, and its negative log is bounded by h(|t−t_*|) of integral at most π²/(3b). These bounds times the previous episode's rate give both quadratic TV constants for all ε>0. For the running mean, the preceding run-end position tends to an equiprobable ±1, leading to half the integral of log(1−e^(−2bt)). For the tumble mean the previous running entry tends to 0 and t_* tends to 0. Write its integrand as h(|t−t_*|)−b(t_*−t)_+; L¹ continuity of translations of h(|·|) and the vanishing integral of the second term justify convergence near the moving logarithmic singularity. The uniform integral bound handles rare entry positions away from zero. Integration gives cπ²/(24b) and aπ²/(6b) with positive signs in the means, and the unscaled limiting mixture and calibrated rate identification follow from the exact age law and state weights.
