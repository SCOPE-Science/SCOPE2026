In a harmonically trapped three-state run-and-tumble process with rare, independently tunable run and tumble switching rates, state-conditioned logarithmic distances to their attractors are exponentially distributed up to a rigorously quadratic total-variation error. The two distinct first mean corrections are \(c\pi^2\varepsilon^2/(24b)\) for a running state and \(a\pi^2\varepsilon^2/(6b)\) for a tumble, enabling separate rate identification from sharply concentrated stationary peaks.
