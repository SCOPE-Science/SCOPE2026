For the five-element dihedral quandle algebra over every field of characteristic five, a single commutator is characterized exactly by a quadratic discriminant being a square, with only one explicitly described exceptional coordinate stratum. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), \(q=5^m\), precisely \(q(q-1)^2/2\) augmentation elements require two commutators, extending the previously computed forty-element exception over \(\mathbb F_5\) to all finite extensions of the prime field.
