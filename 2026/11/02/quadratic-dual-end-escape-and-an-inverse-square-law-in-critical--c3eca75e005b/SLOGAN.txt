For a Bernoulli mixture of two expanding square-root cusp maps, small holes at both endpoints have quadratic escape rates, but the repelling fixed endpoint leaks more slowly by the exact factor \((1-p)(1+\theta_0/2)^{-2}+p(1+\theta_1/2)^{-2}\). This inverse-square factor reflects a stationary density that vanishes linearly at the fixed point and is independent of the otherwise unknown stationary normalization.
