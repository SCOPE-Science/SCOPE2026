For a linear-affine bang-bang optimal control with simple switches, restricting the control to a shifted sampling grid creates the sharp local value-loss profile \(h^2\sum_j|\sigma'(\tau_j)|\min(r_j^2,(1-r_j)^2)+O(h^3)\). A uniformly randomized common grid phase fixes the leading mean at \(h^2\sum_j|\sigma'(\tau_j)|/12\), but the variance retains exact harmonic resonances: two symmetric switches have a sixteenfold even-versus-odd mesh variance contrast.
