# Scientific review: first even Catalan residue stratum

## Correctness — PASS

The first even slice is exactly the two-one-bit family by Legendre's binary digit formula. The factored central binomial coefficient yields an exact \(r+1\) horizontal valuation; a separate product over odd integers gives the exact \(b+2\) vertical valuation. Non-Archimedean noncancellation on a rectangular grid proves the lower bound; cutoff along both axes proves the upper bound. There are no unproved asymptotic steps or extrapolations from finite tests.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Lin's 2010 Section 5 poses the fixed-valuation even Catalan question. The strongest inspected source, Xin–Xu (2011), Theorems 8–9, already gives a vertical distinctness/truncation theorem and implies upper-type bounds; neither theorem states transverse gap separation or a uniform quadratic **lower** bound for the first even valuation layer. Liu–Yeh (2010) gives general truncated-binary congruence reductions, and Rowland–Yassawi (2013/2015) gives fixed-precision automata. No inspected statement implies the new \(\Omega(k^2)\) separated residue rectangle without a further argument. Potential equivalent consequences in digit-congruence literature remain a material historical-priority risk. Searches alone are not evidence of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result resolves the order of growth of the most accessible even Catalan valuation stratum, exactly the direction singled out by Lin. It separates a quadratic first-even residue complexity from the linear odd complexity and supplies explicit effective inequalities at every \(k\ge4\). The upper bound and fixed-scale distinctness are not presented as novel in isolation; the lower injection and new exact transverse valuation are the substantive contribution.

## Scope and limitations

Only fixed valuation one. The limiting leading constant and exact count are not determined. Computer checks are supporting evidence, not a proof of the all-exponent theorem. Historical uniqueness is not certified.
