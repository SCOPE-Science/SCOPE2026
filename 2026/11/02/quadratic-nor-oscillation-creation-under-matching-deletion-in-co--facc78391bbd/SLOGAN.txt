Deleting a matching of \(k\) edges from a complete bipartite graph creates exactly \(k\) new stationary states and \(k(k+3)/2\) new primitive two-cycles under synchronous open-neighborhood NOR, with an explicit formula for every individual attraction basin. Despite this quadratic proliferation of oscillatory attractors, the all-zero/all-one two-cycle captures an asymptotic fraction one of uniformly random initial conditions as both part sizes grow.
