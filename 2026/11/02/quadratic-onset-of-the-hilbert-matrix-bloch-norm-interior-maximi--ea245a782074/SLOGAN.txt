At the critical Bloch exponent \(\alpha=3/2\), the Hilbert-matrix norm optimizer emerges from the unit-circle boundary at the sharp quadratic distance \(1-r_\alpha\sim(\alpha-3/2)^2/2\). The excess norm over the previously known boundary expression is exactly \(\pi(\alpha-3/2)^2+o((\alpha-3/2)^2)\).
