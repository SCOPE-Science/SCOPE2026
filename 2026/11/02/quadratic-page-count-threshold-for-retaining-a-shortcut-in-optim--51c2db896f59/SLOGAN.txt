For a priced book network with \(m\ge2\) pages and independently adjustable conductances, minimizing total electrical resistance retains the direct hub edge exactly below the sharp price \(8S(m+2)/(m^3+m^2+2m+4)\). At equal prices only the two-page book retains that edge; the threshold decays as \(8S/m^2\), and the complete unrestricted optimum is explicit.
