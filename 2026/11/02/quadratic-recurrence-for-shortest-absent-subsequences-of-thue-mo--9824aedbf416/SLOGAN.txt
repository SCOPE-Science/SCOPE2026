The number \(A_m\) of distinct shortest missing scattered subsequences of the length-\(2^{m+1}\) Thue–Morse prefix obeys the exact two-variable quadratic recurrence \(A_{m+1}=2+A_mD_m\), \(D_{m+1}=D_m(A_m-D_m)\), starting at \((A_0,D_0)=(3,1)\). A complementary-pair exclusion rule and a unimodular trace identity establish the formula for every \(m\ge0\).
