For the integral elementary-symmetric Bring curve reduced modulo any odd prime other than five, geometric gonality stays three but arithmetic gonality equals three when \(p\equiv\pm1\pmod5\) and four when \(p\equiv\pm2\pmod5\). The obstruction is the square class of the unique canonical quadric, and a uniform quadratic-point-or-tangent construction provides degree-four maps whenever the two trigonal pencils are conjugate.
