For endpoint-fixed Bernstein sampling on the circle, preserving every continuous positive-definite isotropic kernel forces the unique midpoint grid in degree two, but permits exactly the symmetric one-parameter grid \((0,a,\pi-a,\pi)\), \(0\le a\le\pi/2\), in degree three. This completely classifies the first two adjustable degrees and shows that positive-definite preservation need not require uniformly spaced nodes.
