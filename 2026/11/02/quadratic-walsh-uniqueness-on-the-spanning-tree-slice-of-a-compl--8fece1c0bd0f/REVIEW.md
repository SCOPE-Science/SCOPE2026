# Self-review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The pair-inclusion matrix is shown to have full column rank for every integer \(n\ge3\) by a complete induction. The proof independently establishes the exchange lemma for first-order functions, compares all one-leaf attachments of a fixed old spanning tree, invokes the lower-order rank assertion only after rewriting a linear term into pair monomials, and eliminates all remaining coefficients through full-star and near-star trees. The constant and one-edge coordinate functions are exactly generated by pair-inclusions using the fixed number of edges. This proves rank \(\binom{\binom n2}{2}\), nullity \(1+\binom n2\), and the complete relation-space basis. The exact finite checker validates cases \(n=3,4,5,6\) but is not used in place of the infinite proof.

## Originality

**PASS under bounded primary-source comparison.** Burton and Pemantle (arXiv:math/0404048) establish general edge-inclusion probabilities through transfer-current minors; their inspected theorem yields data for each Gram entry, but does not state the complete universal rank and nullspace. Kassel and Wu (arXiv:1312.2946) establish spanning-tree pattern-field correlation results and discuss edge-count covariance constraints, not the exhaustive degree-two kernel. Filmus (arXiv:1406.0142) gives a complete basis for the *full* fixed-weight Boolean slice, and that result does not survive restriction to a proper subset without new proof. Targeted literature and indexed mathematical-result searches for matroid basis incidence rank, quadratic vanishing ideals, pair-inclusion functions and Walsh restrictions did not reveal a statement that entails this theorem. A broader historical matroid result could still exist and is a concrete residual risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The explicit degree-two rank is a natural, complete infinite-family classification for constrained Fourier sampling. It distinguishes the unavoidable fixed-cardinality dependencies from additional geometric dependencies and proves that none occur at degree two on the uniform spanning-tree support. It establishes uniqueness for all pairwise interactions even with arbitrary full-support tree weights, and identifies a sharp degree cutoff before triangle obstructions at degree three.

## Limitations and audit status

The result does not provide numerical frame constants or a degree-three classification, and does not extend to arbitrary host graphs. The source-inspection limits and originality risks are documented in the structured scientific review. The proof is the mathematical basis; small-order computations are corroboration only.
