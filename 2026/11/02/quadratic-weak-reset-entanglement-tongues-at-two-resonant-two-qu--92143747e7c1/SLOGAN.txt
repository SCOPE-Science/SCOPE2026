For the two-qubit entangling gate studied under discrete-time global stochastic resetting, the resonances at \(\theta=\pi/4\) and \(3\pi/4\) sustain entanglement at every nontrivial reset probability, yet their weak-reset entanglement windows narrow quadratically. At gate detuning \(\theta-\theta_0=cp^2\), the stationary negativity obeys \(\mathcal N/p^2\to\max\{0,1/4-4c^2\}\), so the two local boundaries are \(\theta_0\pm p^2/4+O(p^3)\).
