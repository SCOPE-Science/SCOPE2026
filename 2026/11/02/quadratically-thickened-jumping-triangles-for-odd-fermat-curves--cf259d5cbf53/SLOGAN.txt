For every smooth odd-degree Fermat plane curve of degree \(2r+1\), \(r\ge2\), the normalized logarithmic tangent bundle has precisely the three coordinate pencils as its jumping lines, with extreme splitting \(\mathcal O(r)\oplus\mathcal O(-r)\). Its scheme-theoretic determinant jumping divisor is \(V((abc)^{r^2})\): each coordinate line in the dual plane appears with exact multiplicity \(r^2\), whereas every other line has balanced splitting.
