For every fixed \(T\ge1\), the count of integers \(n\le X\) whose divisor-sum GCD ratio \(R(n)\) is at most \(T\) is \(O_T\bigl(X/\sqrt{\log\log\log X}\bigr)\). A uniform additive-function variance estimate and a growing finite-prime sieve give a quantitative strengthening of the previously qualitative density-zero result.
