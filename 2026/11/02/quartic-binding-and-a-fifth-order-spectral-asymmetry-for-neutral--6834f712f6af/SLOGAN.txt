Any nonzero finitely supported neutral onsite potential on an infinite uniform tight-binding chain creates one eigenvalue outside each spectral band edge at arbitrarily weak coupling, and at least one at each edge at every nonzero coupling. The two weak-coupling gaps share an exact positive \(\varepsilon^4\) coefficient given by squared cumulative defect charge, while a cubic distance moment controls their opposing \(\varepsilon^5\) corrections.
