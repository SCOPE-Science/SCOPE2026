Among zero-mean trigonometric perturbations of the doubling circle map using at most two harmonics, the vanishing-quadratic-entropy directions form exactly a two-dimensional space tangent to smooth conjugacies. Along those directions the first entropy loss is quartic, with exact positive coefficient \((a^2+b^2)(9a^2+5b^2)/128\) and a \(9:5\) sine-to-cosine anisotropy.
