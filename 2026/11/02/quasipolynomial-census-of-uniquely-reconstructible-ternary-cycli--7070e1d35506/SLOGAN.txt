For ternary circular words, the number of adjacent-pair count profiles admitting exactly one reconstruction up to rotation is, for every \(n\ge6\), \(U_n=n^2+9n-26+\varepsilon_{n\bmod6}\), with residue corrections \((0,7,4,3,4,7)\). An exact three-vertex Eulerian multigraph classification explains the six-periodic correction and proves the quadratic law for all lengths.
