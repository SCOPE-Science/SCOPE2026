For four-symbol multiset deletion codes with exactly three surviving symbols, the optimal code sizes are \(6\) at length \(5\), \(5\) at lengths \(6\)–\(8\), and \(4\) thereafter. The four exceptional short lengths have exactly \(288,708,16,1\) labeled optimal codebooks, falling into \(14,46,2,1\) alphabet-permutation classes, respectively.
