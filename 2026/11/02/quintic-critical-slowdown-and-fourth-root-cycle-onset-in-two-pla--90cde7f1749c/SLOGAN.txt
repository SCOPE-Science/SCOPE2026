At the asymmetric cost ratio \(c_*=3-2\sqrt2\), two-player lottery-contest best responses have a quintic rather than cubic critical return map, and deviations decay at the sharp rate \(k^{-1/4}\) over pairs of rounds. Just below this threshold, the unique nearby attracting two-cycle opens with an exact \((c_*-c)^{1/4}\) amplitude; the assertion is local, not a global convergence classification.
