For a two-mode diagonal matrix, independent Rademacher entries uniquely minimize the expected squared Frobenius error of a one-vector randomized SVD among all centered, unit-variance iid-coordinate sketches. The Gaussian-to-Rademacher expected-error ratio is exactly \((\kappa+\kappa^{-1})/2\), and is unbounded as the singular-value ratio \(\kappa\) increases.
