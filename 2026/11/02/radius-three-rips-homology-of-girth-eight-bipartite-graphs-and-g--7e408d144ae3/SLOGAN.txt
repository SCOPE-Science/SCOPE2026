For every finite connected bipartite graph of girth at least \(8\) and diameter at most \(4\), the inclusive radius-three Vietoris–Rips complex is a wedge of \(b^2\) three-spheres, where \(b\) is the graph cycle rank, and its integral third-homology symmetry module is the tensor square of the graph cycle lattice. Every finite generalized quadrangle of order \((s,t)\) therefore contributes \(s^4t^4\) three-spheres, with a nonzero rational class surviving quotient by its full automorphism group.
