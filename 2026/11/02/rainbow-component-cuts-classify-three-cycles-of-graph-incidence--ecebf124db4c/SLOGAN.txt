Primitive three-cycles of ordinary rowmotion on a connected graph's vertex–edge incidence poset are exactly independent vertex cuts whose complementary components admit three colours so that every cut vertex sees all three colours, modulo cyclic rotation. This yields a graph-wide counting formula and the exact count \(3^{a-1}+3^{b-1}-2^a-2^b+3\) for \(K_{a,b}\).
