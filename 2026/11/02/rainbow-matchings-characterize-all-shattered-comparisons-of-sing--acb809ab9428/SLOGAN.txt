For strict rankings single-peaked on a known \(n\)-point axis, a family of pairwise-comparison queries is VC-shattered exactly when its intervals form a strictly nested matching. Consequently there are \(\binom n{2d}\) shattered supports of size \(d\), the concept class is shattering-extremal with \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), while \(\operatorname{Ldim}=n-1\).
