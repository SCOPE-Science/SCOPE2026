# Scientific review: exact random generation in radical-square-zero quiver algebras

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem specifies all finite quivers with at least one vertex, finite fields, unital generation, and the radical-square-zero truncation. The proof establishes a necessary-and-sufficient criterion: distinct coordinate labels and full ranks of the arrow-corner maps after restricting to the kernel of each diagonal difference. The nonstandard step is justified with explicit lifted orthogonal idempotents, the conjugator \(g=\sum_u f_u e_u\), and a converse construction using a hyperplane in one arrow corner. Independence of uniform corner coordinates gives every factor of the exact formula. Positivity determines the generator minimum, while exact logarithmic expansion establishes the critical law. A standalone brute-force subalgebra-closure computation corroborated the result in 19 exhaustive configurations over fields of orders two, three, and four. The tests alone do not prove the infinite statement.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence

Sercombe–Shalev's general random-generation theorems and generator bounds (arXiv:2009.01115, especially Theorems 1.1, 2, 1.10) do not state an exact quiver all-tuple formula and their large-simple-factor hypotheses do not cover the many-vertex critical limit. Iovanov–Sistko (arXiv:1705.00762, especially Proposition 4.2) establish the appropriate maximal-subalgebra classification; that broader structural input is acknowledged, but it does not present the exact product probability or density-shifted birthday limit. Kravchenko–Mazur–Petrenko (arXiv:1001.2873) calculate generator probabilities in predominantly semisimple settings and explicitly identify nontrivial radical calculations as a separate direction. Mazorchuk–Zhang (arXiv:1703.08377) concerns bimodules over related quiver algebras. An existing general maximal-subalgebra description could facilitate a new derivation, so publication priority remains provisional; it is not asserted that no equivalent identity exists elsewhere.

## Value — PASS

Generating tuples are a natural finite-algebra invariant, not an arbitrary numerical slice. The complete formula handles all loop and parallel-arrow multiplicities and determines the exact minimum generator size. It shows a nontrivial orientation-insensitivity property for uniform unrestricted generators, while establishing a sharp fixed-field random threshold that differs in order from the deterministic minimal generator count. For dense simple directed quivers, arrow constraints shift the classical birthday exponent by \(q\rho/c\), a new structural contribution that measures edge density at the transition.

## Limits and remaining risk

The proof does not address higher radical layers, nonunital sampling, or idempotent-only sampling. The critical limit assumes a convergent field-size-normalized tuple parameter. The present originality assessment is limited to examined primary sources and accessible prior records; some broader exact generating-tuple theory may have escaped comparison. Numerical checks are finite and independently audited verification has not occurred.
