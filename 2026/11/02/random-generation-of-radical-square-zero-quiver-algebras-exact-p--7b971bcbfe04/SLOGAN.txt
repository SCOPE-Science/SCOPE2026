For every finite radical-square-zero quiver algebra, including loops and parallel arrows, the probability that \(k\) uniform elements generate the unital algebra factors exactly into a vertex-label birthday term and independent surjective-map factors for each arrow corner. For loopless simple directed quivers this yields a sharp random-generation threshold near \(2\log_q v\), with a density-dependent critical law \(\exp(-(1/2+q\rho)/c)\), alongside an exact deterministic generator-number formula.
