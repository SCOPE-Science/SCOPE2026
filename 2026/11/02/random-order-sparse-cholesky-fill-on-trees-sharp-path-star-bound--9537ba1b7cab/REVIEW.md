# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The pair-filling event is exactly the event that every internal vertex of the unique original tree path is eliminated before both endpoints. Its relative-order probability \(2/[d(d+1)]\) supplies the expectation by linearity. The sharp upper bound is termwise, while the sharp lower bound is proved by deleting a leaf and applying the ordered-distance inequality. A record-maxima representation yields the path probability-generating function, and conditioning on the center's rank yields the star law. All quantifiers, small-order coincidences, matrix-sign assumptions and the single-edge counting convention are specified; 258852 finite verifier assertions passed. Finite checks supplement rather than establish the infinite theorem.

## Originality

PASS with material limitations. The classical fill-path criterion and permutation-record distributions are explicit prior ingredients and are not independently attributed novelty. The relevant new combined claim is the exact tree-distance random-order mean, sharp unique path/star extremizers within all trees, and the factorization-level full-distribution benchmark on the path. The broader record-generating-function work of Jin may imply the path specialization; the RCHOL algorithm of Chen–Liang–Biros differs by sampling fill clique edges. The inspected Scott–Tůma chapter addresses deterministic symbolic Cholesky, not these random-order tree extrema. Searches of scholarly sources and available research records found no direct coverage of the full mathematical statement, but failed search does not prove worldwide priority. The original Rose–Tarjan–Lueker full text was inaccessible, and unlocated specialized ordering-probability papers are an explicit residual risk.

## Value

PASS. Even on tree sparsity patterns, uniform random elimination can have order-\(n\) expected fill on a path and order-\(n^2\) on a star, despite both admitting zero-fill orders. Exact means, uniqueness, and a path concentration law give natural reproducible benchmarks for sparse direct-solver reordering costs, rather than an arbitrary finite or merely computational statistic. These facts do not claim novel sparse Cholesky algorithms or complexity improvements.

## Limitations

The result concerns exact SPD \(M\)-matrix Cholesky and symbolic strictly triangular fill under uniform order; the numerical operation count, arbitrary SPD cancellations, general graphs, and heuristic reorderings are outside its scope. Publication priority, particularly regarding the combination of classical fill paths and existing record enumerators, requires further external assessment.
