For uniform random-order exact Cholesky on a tree-supported positive-definite \(M\)-matrix, the expected new fill edges are a universal tree-distance sum, uniquely minimized by the path and maximized by the star among trees of the same order. The path's entire fill count has a product-form Poisson-binomial law and fluctuations on the \(\sqrt{2\log n}\) scale, whereas the star has a quadratic-order mean; all trees admit zero-fill leaf orders.
