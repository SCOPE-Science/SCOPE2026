In degree-proportional preferential attachment trees, the number of unordered pairs of leaves sharing a neighbor grows like \(\tfrac14 n\log n\), but its order-\(n\) fluctuations converge almost surely and in \(L^2\) to a nondegenerate random correction. That correction is exactly one-eighth of the limiting quadratic-degree martingale, and the expected count has a closed harmonic-number formula at every tree size.
