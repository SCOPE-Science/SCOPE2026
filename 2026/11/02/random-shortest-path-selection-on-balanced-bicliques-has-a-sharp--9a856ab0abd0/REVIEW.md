This identifies two independent coupon-collection experiments. Inclusion–exclusion proves the exact reliability product; factorial moments give independent Poisson limits, Markov/second moment the first-order threshold, and deterministic midpoint capacities the optimized comparison. Searches are not proofs of absolute priority.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** For a standard graph-theoretic covering objective, the result distinguishes optimizing geodesic paths from picking shortest paths uniformly independently. It gives all-size exact route-coverage probability and a sharp two-part Poisson critical window, with a genuine factor \(\sqrt{\log N}\) between optimized and random route budgets. The mechanism is mathematical rather than computational only and its graph model is explicitly motivated.

The proof does not cover arbitrary graph classes, correlated/adaptive routing, stochastic terminal positions or uniformity in moving window parameters. Independent verification by an external mathematician has not been performed.
