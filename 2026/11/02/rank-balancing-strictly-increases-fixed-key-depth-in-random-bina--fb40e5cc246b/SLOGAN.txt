In a permutation-built random binary search tree, moving a fixed searched key one rank toward the center strictly raises every nontrivial upper tail of its depth: \(\Pr(D_{n,i+1}\ge t)>\Pr(D_{n,i}\ge t)\) for \(i<n/2\) and \(2\le t\le n-1\). The adjacent 1-Wasserstein distance is exactly \(1/(i+1)-1/(n+1-i)\), with reflection symmetry and equality at an even-size central pair.
