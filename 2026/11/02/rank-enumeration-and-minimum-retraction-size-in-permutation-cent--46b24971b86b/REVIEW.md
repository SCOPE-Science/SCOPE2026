# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
A permutation-equivariant map sends each source cycle onto one target cycle of dividing length; the number of phases is exactly the target length. Fixing selected whole cycles pointwise is both necessary and sufficient for idempotence, proving the rank-polynomial product formula. Every divisibility-minimal cycle length must occur among selected cycles; selecting one at each such length is sufficient, yielding the exact minimum and its multiplicity. Conjugation orbits of idempotents are completely classified by rooted fiber-size multisets, so rank-refined Burnside averaging yields integer partitions. A structurally distinct exhaustive enumerator tested all 66 permutation cycle-type cases for dimensions one through eight and verified the rank distribution and averaging identity. A finite check corroborates, but is not substituted for, the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, conditional on inspected evidence
Konieczny (2003) already provides the pivotal cycle-divisibility theorem and discusses idempotents inside permutation centralizers. The new assertion is **not** that theorem: it gives a complete polynomial enumerator for all cycle profiles, the exact minimum-rank fiber, and the partition-valued average. Their derivation is direct but produces quantitative invariants absent from the inspected theorem statements. Araújo–Konieczny (2013) studies broader centralizer structures, not these counts in material read. Mir–Alali (2024) studies automorphisms of centralizers of restricted-range idempotents rather than idempotents centralizing a permutation. Actual external queries also checked alternative names including equivariant retractions, cyclic-set maps, and permutation-centralizer rank enumeration. No matching stronger claim was located in the inspected sources. This is **not** a proof of global priority: an equivalent enumeration in older cyclic-set literature is the main remaining risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The idempotents of a transformation centralizer are the retractions in a natural finite cyclic group action. Formula (1) gives a previously unprovided, rank-refined counting invariant for every cycle profile; formula (2) identifies a sharp structural lower rank and its number of witnesses by a divisor-poset antichain. Formula (3) connects the entire family to integer partitions via conjugacy orbits, allowing global exact averages for random permutations. The full enumerator and minimum-profile interpretation go beyond a yes/no or simple restatement of the pre-existing cycle-divisibility criterion.
