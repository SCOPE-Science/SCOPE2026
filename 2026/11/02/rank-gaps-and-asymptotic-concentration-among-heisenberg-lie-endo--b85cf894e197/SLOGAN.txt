For every finite field, the rank polynomial of all linear Lie endomorphisms of a Heisenberg Lie algebra is determined by isotropic-subspace counts and symplectic similitudes; no rank strictly between \(m+1\) and \(2m+1\) occurs in dimension \(2m+1\). At fixed field size, the proportion of endomorphisms of rank \(m+1\) is \(1-\frac{q^2}{q^2-1}q^{-m}+o(q^{-m})\).
