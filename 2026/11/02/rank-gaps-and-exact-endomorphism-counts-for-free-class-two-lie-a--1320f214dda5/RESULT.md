# Rank gaps and exact endomorphism counts for free class-two Lie algebras

## Finding

Let \(q\) be any prime power, \(d\ge3\), \(V=\mathbb F_q^d\), and \(L_d=V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\), with bracket \([v,w]=v\wedge w\) and central second summand. Put \(C=\binom d2\) and \(N=d+C\). For integers \(m,n,j\ge0\), define the number of rank-\(j\) linear maps from an \(n\)-space to an \(m\)-space by

\[
R_{m,n,j}(q)={m\brack j}_q\prod_{i=0}^{j-1}(q^n-q^i)
\]

when \(0\le j\le\min(m,n)\), and set it to zero otherwise. Here \({m\brack j}_q\) is the Gaussian binomial coefficient; empty products equal one. Define

\[
M_{d,a}(q)={d\brack a}_q\prod_{i=0}^{a-1}(q^d-q^i).
\]

The complete image-rank generating polynomial of the endomorphism monoid is

\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
F_{d,q}(z)&=\sum_{\phi\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{Lie}}(L_d)}z^{\operatorname{rank}\phi}\\
&=\sum_{a=0}^{d}M_{d,a}(q)\,q^{Ca+\binom a2(d-a)}
 \sum_{j=0}^{d-a} R_{C-\binom a2,d-a,j}(q)\,z^{a+\binom a2+j}.
\end{aligned}}
\]

Its support is **exactly**

\[
\bigcup_{a=0}^{d}\left[\binom{a+1}{2},\ d+\binom a2\right]\cap\mathbb Z.
\]

Between the intervals indexed by \(a\) and \(a+1\), the number of excluded consecutive ranks is \(\max(0,2a-d)\). In particular, every nonautomorphism has corank at least \(d-1\), and this bound is attained: none of \(N-d+2,\ldots,N-1\) occurs as an endomorphism rank.

For fixed \(q\), if \(\phi_d\) is uniform among these endomorphisms and \(\Delta_d=N-\operatorname{rank}\phi_d\), then

\[
\frac{\Delta_d}{d}\ \xrightarrow{\mathrm d}\ T_q,\qquad
\Pr(T_q=t)=\frac{q^{-t^2}P_\infty(q)}{P_t(q)^2}\quad(t\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}),
\]

where \(P_t(q)=\prod_{i=1}^t(1-q^{-i})\) and \(P_\infty(q)=\prod_{i=1}^\infty(1-q^{-i})>0\). This is the limiting corank law for uniform square matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), transferred to endomorphisms after scaling by \(d\). For fixed \(d\), as \(q\to\infty\) through prime powers, a uniform **nonautomorphism** has rank \(N-d+1\) with probability tending to one.

## Assumptions and scope

The field has arbitrary characteristic, including \(2\); the Lie algebra is alternating, so the exterior-power description is valid. The theorem concerns all Lie algebra endomorphisms, not only idempotent maps or automorphisms. The exact rank formula actually also makes sense for \(d=2\), but the stated interval and gap conclusions are scoped to \(d\ge3\). The asymptotic assertions separately specify either fixed \(q\) with \(d\to\infty\), or fixed \(d\) with \(q\to\infty\).

## Proof

**1. Parameterization.** The exterior summand is central and generated by all brackets. Every pair of linear maps \(A:V\to V\), \(B:V\to\bigwedge^2V\) therefore defines exactly one Lie endomorphism \(\phi\), with

\[
\phi(v)=Av+Bv,\qquad\phi(v\wedge w)=Av\wedge Aw.
\]

Conversely, every endomorphism has this form, because its restriction to \(V\) uniquely determines the action on brackets. Thus the matrix of \(\phi\) in the displayed direct-sum decomposition is

\[
\begin{pmatrix} A&0\\ B&\bigwedge^2 A\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This description is valid also in characteristic \(2\) by the universal property of the alternating exterior square.

**2. Image-rank lemma.** Fix \(A\) of rank \(a\). Write \(U=\ker A\), of dimension \(t=d-a\), and \(S=\bigwedge^2(\operatorname{im}A)\subseteq\bigwedge^2V\), of dimension \(b=\binom a2\). The projection of \(\operatorname{im}\phi\) onto \(V\) has dimension \(a\). Its intersection with the center is exactly \(S+B(U)\). Indeed, a vector \(\phi(v)+\phi(z)\) has zero \(V\)-component precisely when \(v\in U\). Consequently, if \(j\) is the rank of the composite

\[
U\xrightarrow{B}\bigwedge^2V\longrightarrow(\bigwedge^2V)/S,
\]

then \(\operatorname{rank}\phi=a+b+j\).

**3. Exact enumeration.** There are \(M_{d,a}(q)\) choices of \(A\) with rank \(a\). Set \(c=C-b\). Choose a complementary \(a\)-space to \(U\); the restriction of \(B\) there has \(q^{Ca}\) possibilities. The map \(U\to(\bigwedge^2V)/S\) has exactly \(R_{c,t,j}(q)\) rank-\(j\) choices, each with \(q^{bt}\) lifts to the center. Multiplication gives \(M_{d,a}(q)q^{Ca+bt}R_{c,t,j}(q)\). For \(d\ge3\),

\[
c=\frac{(d-a)(d+a-1)}2\ge d-a=t,
\]

so every \(0\le j\le t\) occurs. Summing the counts proves the polynomial. As a normalization check, putting \(z=1\) yields \(F_{d,q}(1)=q^{d(d+C)}\), because there are \(q^{d^2}\) choices of \(A\) and \(q^{dC}\) independent choices of \(B\).

**4. Gaps and sharpness.** For fixed \(a\), the image ranks cover the whole integer interval from \(a+\binom a2=\binom{a+1}{2}\) through \(a+\binom a2+d-a=d+\binom a2\). These intervals have monotonically increasing left and right endpoints. Subtracting the right endpoint of interval \(a\) from the left endpoint of interval \(a+1\), and subtracting one, gives exactly \(2a-d\) missing integers when positive. A singular \(A\) has \(a\le d-1\), hence \(\operatorname{rank}\phi\le d+\binom{d-1}{2}=N-(d-1)\). Equality is attained by taking \(\operatorname{rank}A=d-1\) and choosing \(B\) so that its image on the one-dimensional kernel of \(A\) is nonzero modulo \(S\), a quotient of dimension \(d-1\).

**5. Fixed-field limiting corank.** For uniform \(\phi\), its \(A\) and \(B\) blocks are independent uniform matrices. Let \(T_d=d-\operatorname{rank}A\). Finite-field rank counting gives, for \(0\le t\le d\),

\[
\Pr(T_d=t)=q^{-t^2}\frac{P_d(q)^2}{P_t(q)^2P_{d-t}(q)}.
\]

For fixed \(t\), this converges to the displayed mass for \(T_q\). The tail bound \(\Pr(T_d=t)\le q^{-t^2}P_\infty(q)^{-2}\) makes the laws tight, and their limiting masses sum to one. On \(T_d=t\), the quotient \((\bigwedge^2V)/S\) has dimension \(c=t(2d-t-1)/2\). For each fixed \(t\), the random map \(U\to(\bigwedge^2V)/S\) is injective with probability

\[
\prod_{i=0}^{t-1}(1-q^{i-c})\longrightarrow1
\]

as \(d\to\infty\). On this event \(j=t\), whence

\[
\Delta_d=C-\binom{d-t}{2}=\frac{t(2d-t-1)}2,
\]

and \(\Delta_d/d\to t\). Tightness permits mixing over all \(t\), proving convergence in distribution. For fixed \(d\), as \(q\to\infty\) the conditional probability \(\Pr(T_d=1\mid T_d\ge1)\to1\) by the exact rank law, and conditional on \(T_d=1\), the rank-\(1\) map from the kernel of \(A\) into a quotient of dimension \(d-1\) is nonzero with probability \(1-q^{-(d-1)}\to1\). This proves the second asymptotic claim.

## Verification

The included standalone `verify.py` independently constructs the images of Lie generators and their brackets and computes ranks by finite-field Gaussian elimination. It exhaustively checks all \(64\) endomorphisms for \((d,q)=(2,2)\), all \(262144\) for \((3,2)\), and all \(729\) for \((2,3)\), agreeing exactly with the formula. It also checks the gap/support structure for \(3\le d\le15\). Run with `python verify.py` in Python 3; the required success marker is `VERIFY_OK`. These finite checks corroborate, but do not replace, the proof for all dimensions and fields.

For \((d,q)=(3,2)\), the exact rank multiplicities at ranks \(0,1,2,3,4,6\) are, respectively, \(1,441,8526,54264,112896,86016\); rank \(5\) does not occur. For \((d,q)=(2,3)\), the multiplicities at ranks \(0,1,2,3\) are \(1,104,192,432\).

## Relationship to prior work

Benito and Roldán-López (arXiv:1911.01897, first posted 2019-11-05), Proposition 1, describe the extension of generator maps to endomorphisms of free nilpotent Lie algebras, and characterize automorphisms by invertibility of the generator linear block. This supplies background for Step 1, but their inspected result does not count noninvertible endomorphisms by full image rank, or establish the support gaps and limits above; their paper works in characteristic zero. Cagliero, Gutiérrez Frez and Szechtman (arXiv:1703.06214) use the same free class-two Lie algebra model to study uniserial representations of a semidirect extension, not rank strata of its endomorphism monoid. Ruan, Sheng and Zhang (Journal of Algebra 586 (2021), Section 6) realize free class-two Lie algebras via 1-cyclic perfect complexes and describe their exterior-square model, but that construction alone does not enumerate these endomorphisms. The finite-field matrix-rank count is standard; the contribution is its two-block coupling through \(\bigwedge^2 A\), and the resulting full semigroup rank spectrum and corank laws.

## Limitations

This is an exact result only for the free **class-two** nilpotent Lie algebra, not arbitrary class-two quotients or free algebras of higher nilpotency class. It counts linear Lie endomorphisms, not endomorphisms up to conjugacy. Earlier equivalent rank-spectrum formulas under another terminology may exist; the literature comparison is not independent priority certification. Finite verification does not imply the infinite statements without the proof above.
