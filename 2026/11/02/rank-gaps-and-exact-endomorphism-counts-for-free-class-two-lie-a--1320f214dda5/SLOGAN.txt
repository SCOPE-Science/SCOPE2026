All endomorphism ranks of the free class-two Lie algebra on \(d\ge3\) generators over \(\mathbb F_q\) admit an explicit finite-field counting polynomial; its rank support has forbidden intervals of lengths \(\max(0,2a-d)\), and every singular endomorphism loses at least \(d-1\) dimensions. At fixed field size the normalized rank defect converges to the classical square-matrix corank law, whereas at large field size a singular endomorphism concentrates at the largest allowed singular rank.
