For deterministic samplers with the same prescribed invariant density, paired Rosenblatt quantile discrepancies have uniformly bounded baker-map sums through three binary digits, but exhibit variance \(1/32\) at exactly four of the fifteen four-bit thresholds. The underlying six-dimensional space of reversal-odd four-bit observables has a rank-one diffusion form, while an equally stationary product-tent sampler has independent half-quantile differences of variance \(1/2\).
