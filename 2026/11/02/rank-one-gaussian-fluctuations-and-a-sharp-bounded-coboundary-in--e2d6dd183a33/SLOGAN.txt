The full-branch triangular tent map has two positive expanding directions but its stationary coordinate sums converge to a rank-one Gaussian with exact Green--Kubo matrix \(\frac16\begin{pmatrix}1&1/3\\1/3&1/9\end{pmatrix}\). The unique zero-variance linear direction \(x-3y\) is a coboundary whose every orbit sum has the sharp absolute bound \(2\).
