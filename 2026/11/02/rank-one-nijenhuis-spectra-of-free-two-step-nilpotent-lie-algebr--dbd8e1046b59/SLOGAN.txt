For the free two-step nilpotent Lie algebra \(V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\), every nonzero rank-one Nijenhuis operator either factors through the abelianization or is square-zero with \(\phi(u)=\phi([u,V])=0\) in a rank-one factorization \(N=u\otimes\phi\). The resulting exact finite-field census reveals non-character solutions beginning with three generators; their proportion among rank-one Nijenhuis operators tends to one as the generator dimension grows.
