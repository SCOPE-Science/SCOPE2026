For a positive edge-weighted complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\), the two partwise ordinary-degree vectors of a Gibbs-random spanning tree are independent exactly when the edge-weight matrix has rank one. Conditional degree means given a unique maximum-degree vertex in one part recover all column-normalized edge weights, providing a constructive converse to the classical rank-one factorization.
