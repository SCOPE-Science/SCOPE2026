# Review: rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on spin factors

## Correctness — PASS

Every rank-one map factors uniquely, up to inverse scalar rescaling, as \(f\otimes w\). Polarization of the weight-zero identity and the fact that \(2\) is invertible yield \(w^2=\mu w\) and \(f\circ L_w=(\mu/2)f\); the converse follows by substitution. In the spin factor, these conditions exclude scalar \(w\), and give \(B(u,u)=a^2\), \(f(1)=f(u)=0\). Thus the rank-one locus is the punctured rank-\((n-1)\) vector bundle over the nondegenerate projective quadric. Classical additive-character evaluation produces norm-zero and norm-one fiber counts in even and odd dimensions. All boundary cases are quantified: odd finite-field characteristic, \(n\ge4\), nondegenerate symmetric \(B\), nonzero rank-one weight-zero maps. Independent direct finite checks include exhaustive rank-one operator enumeration at \(q=3,n=4\). The computations are evidence, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)

The closest direct source, V. Gubarev, *Rota–Baxter operators of weight zero on simple Jordan algebra of Clifford type* (arXiv:1710.02377), gives nilpotency bounds and a complete dimension-three classification. Its statement for higher-dimensional algebras does not give the rank-one operator count, the nilpotent/idempotent-image refinement, or the vector-bundle geometry. These are not consequences of nilpotency-index restrictions alone. Benito–Gubarev–Pozhidaev (arXiv:1801.07037) gives wider quadratic-algebra context, particularly nonzero weight, without the asserted rank-one arithmetic. Gubarev–Panasenko (arXiv:2502.18866) concerns a four-dimensional Jordan algebra, corresponding to quadratic-space dimension three and excluded. Guo–Rosenkranz–Zheng (arXiv:1402.3702) is older foundational Rota–Baxter classification for different algebras. No statement in the compared public mathematical index or inspected primary literature implies the complete pair of counts. Nevertheless, undiscovered broader literature could cover it; an independent priority audit is still needed.

## Value — PASS

Nonzero rank one is the first nontrivial stratum of a nonlinear Rota–Baxter-operator variety on a classical simple Jordan family. The exact count by intrinsic image Jordan type detects the determinant square class in **every** included dimension. In even dimensions the unrefined total is blind to that class, exhibiting a meaningful difference between raw counts and algebraically refined spectra. The smooth, geometrically irreducible punctured-bundle description explains the enumeration structurally and provides a base for subsequent higher-rank work. This is an invariant classification, not an arbitrary finite sample.

## Boundaries and remaining risks

Characteristic two and higher operator ranks are not addressed. Geometry is for the reduced rank-one locus, not for the full potentially nonreduced operator scheme. A later article might include a general rank-one theorem and subsume these formulas; historical priority remains provisional. No independent validation or external certification has been conducted.
