For every nilpotent matrix over a finite field, the rank-one square-zero part of its centralizer has an exact three-term Jordan-transition census: only \((1,1)\leftrightarrow(2)\) changes can occur, with explicit multiplicities determined by the counts of Jordan blocks of sizes one, two, and at least three. Its reduced algebraic variety is geometrically reducible exactly when the fixed matrix has one singleton Jordan block, in which case it has two components of the same dimension.
