For every branching star \(K_{1,m}\) with \(m\ge3\), the Margolis–Rhodes monoid has explicit rank polynomials for all elements and all idempotents. Its nonregular elements are exactly the injections sending a center-containing domain of size at least two into the leaves, so their complete rank distribution is \(\sum_{r=2}^{m}\binom mr\binom m{r-1}r!\,t^r\).
