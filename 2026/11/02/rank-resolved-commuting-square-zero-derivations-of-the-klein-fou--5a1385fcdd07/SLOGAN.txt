For the modular Klein-four algebra \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\) in characteristic two, the full multivariate rank distribution of every ordered commuting tuple of square-zero derivations is an explicit polynomial in \(q\) and the rank variables. Its total is \(1+(q^2+q+1)(2q^{2m}-(q+1)q^m+q-1)\) for all \(m\ge1\).
