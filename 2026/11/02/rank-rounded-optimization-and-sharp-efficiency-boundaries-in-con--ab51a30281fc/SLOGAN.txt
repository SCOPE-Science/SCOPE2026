Every continuous-type average-distance swap game on a fully occupied complete bipartite graph has a strict pair-swap potential and a directly constructible rank-rounded equilibrium; a different rank-rounding scheme computes globally minimum social cost in \(O(n\log n)\) time. Irregular bicliques can have price of stability greater than \(1\): the explicit \(K_{2,5}\) instance has ratio \(17/15\), while stars have price of stability \(1\) and sharp price of anarchy \(n-1\).
