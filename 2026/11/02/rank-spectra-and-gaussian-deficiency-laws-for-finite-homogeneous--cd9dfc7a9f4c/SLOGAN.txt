For every nonempty finite homogeneous graph, the partial-automorphism rank polynomial is explicit: the equal-clique families have a finite rook-polynomial composition, while the exceptional nine-vertex rook graph has \(34{,}498\) partial automorphisms. For a fixed number of growing equal cliques, the rank deficiency has a Gaussian limit after centering at \(t\sqrt n\) and scaling by \(n^{1/4}\).
