For the \((2m+1)\)-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), every proper nonzero retraction has an isotropic image, and the complete idempotent-endomorphism rank spectrum is an explicit product formula. In the large-field limit the typical rank is \(\lfloor(4m+4)/5\rfloor\), except when \(m\equiv4\pmod5\), when two adjacent ranks share the limiting mass equally.
