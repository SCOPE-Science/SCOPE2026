In three-block radical-ring braces, the ranks of adjacent blocks completely determine brace and circle-group centralizers, with an exact gap of \(q^{ab}\). For each central matrix \(C\), the minimal additive-product and group-commutator lengths are \(\lceil\operatorname{rank}(C)/s\rceil\) and \(\lceil\operatorname{rank}(C)/(2s)\rceil\), respectively; both commuting probabilities also have exact formulas.
