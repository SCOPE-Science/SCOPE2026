For every positive-semidefinite endpoint covariance \(C_\varepsilon\), including matrices changing rank with \(\varepsilon\), the sup-norm confinement probability of a Brownian bridge plus Gaussian affine endpoints has a universal sharp relative approximation \(\sqrt{2\pi}\varepsilon^{-1}e^{-\pi^2/(8\varepsilon^2)}H(C_\varepsilon/\varepsilon^2)(1+O(\varepsilon^2))\), with a covariance-uniform error bound. Fixed endpoint rank \(r\in\{0,1,2\}\) produces the precise polynomial prefactor \(\varepsilon^{r-1}\), and confined scaled endpoints follow an asymptotic cosine-tilted Gaussian law.
