In a Brownian gas with batch resetting, two exceptionally large positive displacements have nearly equal elapsed reset ages even when their last resets occurred at different events. Conditional on a joint extreme, the absolute age gap converges to a computable point-mass/exponential mixture; its point mass is the square of the shared-shock fraction, whereas one-at-a-time resetting produces a gap of order the square root of the excursion threshold.
