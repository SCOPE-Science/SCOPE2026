The first-order Hoeffding/ANOVA projector on any nondegenerate Bernoulli product has its sharp \(L^\infty\) amplification at the vertex of coordinatewise rarer outcomes, giving an exact weighted-Poisson-binomial norm and positivity defect. Making any coordinate fairer cannot worsen stability; the fair optimum has a parity-plateau formula, while sparse biases create a sharp Poisson transition.
