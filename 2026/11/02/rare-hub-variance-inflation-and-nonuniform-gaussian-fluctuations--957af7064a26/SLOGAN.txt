Random greedy adsorption on a wheel has a Gaussian limit of variance \(e^{-4}\) after centering and square-root scaling, but its finite-size variance per rim vertex converges to the strictly larger \(e^{-4}+(1-e^{-2})^2/4\). One rare hub-first event, of probability \(1/(n+1)\), also produces a sharp \(1/n\) lower tail and standardized skewness diverging like a negative multiple of \(\sqrt n\).
