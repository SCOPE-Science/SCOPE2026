For a Brownian particle in any nonconstant periodic landscape rocked by rare symmetric telegraph flips, the exact rescaled displacement diffusivity is \(\lim_{\gamma\downarrow0}\gamma D_{\mathrm{eff}}=[u(v;D)-u(-v;D)]^2/8\), strictly below the sharp free-particle ceiling \(v^2/2\). In a deterministically locked sawtooth ratchet, its low-temperature coefficient selects the longer arm's activated escape direction and has a distinct symmetric-apex prefactor.
