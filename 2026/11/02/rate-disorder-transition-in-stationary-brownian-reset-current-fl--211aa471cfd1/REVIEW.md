# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The stationary Laplace law, renewal decomposition, integrated Gaussian threshold kernel, Campbell covariance decomposition, Mellin evaluation, and Tauberian time-average regimes are reconstructed in RESULT.md. Two standalone numerical programs cross-check the fixed-rate Gaussian integral and spectral-edge constants; finite checks are not proofs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The paper arxiv:2302.06696 and earlier fixed-rate two-time current findings give important ingredients but do not assert a quenched continuum of particle-specific reset rates near zero, the power-law time covariance, or the sharp a=1/2 integrated-current phase transition. Actual semantic searches found no equivalent comparison. A broader general rate-mixture theorem or uninspected literature may subsume this result; priority is not independently established.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The precise low-rate spectral edge controls observably distinct temporal self-averaging classes in a motivated many-particle resetting model. The finite-rate T^-1 expansion cannot be integrated term by term across the critical mark law, and the three-regime constants are exact.

## Limits

Poisson anchors, frozen rate marks, stationary Brownian motion and a true power-law density at zero are essential assumptions. No claim of independent validation, entropy-production sign or central-limit theorem is made.
