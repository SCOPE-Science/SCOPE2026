For two competing permanent infections seeded at different leaves of \(K_{2,n}\), the probability of both types capturing a hub is \(\sqrt{\pi/(2n)}+O(n^{-1})\), with a leading coefficient **independent of their unequal transmission rates**. Winner-takes-all-but-one holds with probability tending to one, yet rare macroscopic coexistence forces the expected minority outbreak to grow on the order of \(\sqrt n\).
