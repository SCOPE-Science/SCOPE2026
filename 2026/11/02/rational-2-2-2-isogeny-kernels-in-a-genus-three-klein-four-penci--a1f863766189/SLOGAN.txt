For the genus-three hyperelliptic pencil \(y^2=(x^4+a x^2+1)(x^4+b x^2+1)\) over any odd finite field, the exact counts of rational maximal isotropic Jacobian two-division kernels are \(135\), \(15\), or \(39\), with explicit coefficient frequencies. The last two cases have equally many rational two-torsion points but different numbers of rational \((2,2,2)\)-isogeny kernels; every geometric kernel descends over the quadratic extension.
