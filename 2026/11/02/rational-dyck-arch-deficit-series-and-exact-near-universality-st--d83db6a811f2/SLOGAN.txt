The number of Dyck words of semilength \(n\) whose subsequence-universality index falls exactly \(d\) below \(n\) has a rational generating function for **every fixed** \(d\), because each lost greedy arch forces a bounded-height excursion. We give an explicit finite-matrix formula, the rational series through \(d=3\), and sharp structural sparsity; the \(d=2\) coefficient sequence already appears in OEIS under a different statistic.
