# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim is a finite, exactly specified directed Hom complex. Its complete product-cell enumeration yields \(20862\) cells; two algebraically independent mod-two rank implementations give the identical boundary ranks, and the signed cellular chain condition is checked across every cell. Universal coefficients and Euler characteristic force the stated rational Betti ranks. The complete signed Lefschetz calculation over all \(171\) affine automorphisms gives the rational character, and the four explicitly defined rational summands have the same character. Maschke semisimplicity and rational quotient invariants complete the proof. Computations do not assert unverified integral torsion-freeness.

## Originality
PASS on currently inspected evidence, with residual bibliographic risk. Dochtermann–Singh's inspected directed Hom paper covers transitive targets and general construction, not the nontransitive quadratic-residue target of order nineteen. The inspected order-nine cyclic-target calculation concerns the same Hom functor but a different graph and only the circle homotopy type; it does not imply the present rank or affine module. The inspected order-nineteen Paley directed-flag result concerns a distinct complex (transitive subtournaments), not multihomomorphism cells. Targeted published-source and indexed statement searches found no result implying the exact degree-two affine module. Unindexed and recent equivalent work remains possible.

## Value
PASS. The directed triangle is the equivariant test graph highlighted in the foundational Hom-complex paper, and the order-nineteen Paley tournament is a canonical doubly regular target. The full \(683\)-dimensional affine module and its five-dimensional quotient invariants give a substantial, object-motivated classification, rather than an isolated simplex count or a restatement of the known directed-flag result. The result offers a concrete nontransitive benchmark for graph-homomorphism topology and equivariant obstruction calculations; no downstream theorem beyond the quotient is asserted.

## Limits
Only rational homology and the natural affine action are classified. Neither odd-primary torsion nor the ordinary or equivariant homotopy type is settled. Complete uniqueness in unindexed literature is not established.
