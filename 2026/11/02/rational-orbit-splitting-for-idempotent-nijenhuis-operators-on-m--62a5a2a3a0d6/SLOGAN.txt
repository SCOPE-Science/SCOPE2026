For every odd finite field, the idempotent Nijenhuis operators on \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) form exactly eight \(\operatorname{PGL}_2\)-conjugacy orbits: two singleton orbits and, in each nontrivial rank, three orbits of sizes \(q(q+1)\), \(q(q^2-1)/2\), and \(q(q^2-1)/2\). The two equal-size semisimple orbits are genuinely distinct rational square classes, although they merge over the algebraic closure.
