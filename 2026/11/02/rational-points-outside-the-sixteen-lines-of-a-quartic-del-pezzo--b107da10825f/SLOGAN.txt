For every smooth degree-four del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbb F_q\), the count of rational points outside **all sixteen geometric exceptional lines** is an explicit quadratic determined by the signed Frobenius type in \(W(D_5)\); the full eighteen-type table is computed. The complement is empty only for a split surface over \(\mathbb F_5\) or for positive signed-cycle type \((2,2,1)\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), and both cases occur.
