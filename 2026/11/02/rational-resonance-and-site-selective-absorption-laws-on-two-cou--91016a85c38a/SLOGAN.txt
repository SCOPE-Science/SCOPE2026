For a continuous-time quantum walk on a Boolean hypercube with two classes of positive hopping rates and one absorbing corner, absorption is reduced at exactly the reduced rational coupling ratios \(\rho=u/v\) with \(u\le a\) and \(v\le b\): the bright dimension drops by \((a+1-u)(b+1-v)\). Exact Krawtchouk formulas determine absorption from every site, and at each exceptional ratio the number of perfectly captured block corners falls from four to two according to a parity test.
