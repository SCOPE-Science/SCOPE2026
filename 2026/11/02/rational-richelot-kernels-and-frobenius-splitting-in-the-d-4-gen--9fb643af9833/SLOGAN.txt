For the dihedral genus-two pencil \(y^2=c(x^2+1)(x^4+sx^2+1)\) over any odd finite field, the numbers of rational maximal isotropic two-torsion kernels are exactly \(15,3,3,7\) across four explicitly counted Frobenius strata. In particular, the two strata with \(\#J[2](\mathbb F_q)=4\) support respectively three and seven rational Richelot kernels, even though all kernels split over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\).
