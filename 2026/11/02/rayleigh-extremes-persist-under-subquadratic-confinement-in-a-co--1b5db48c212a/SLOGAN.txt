For every strictly subquadratic confining power \(1<\alpha<2\), the stationary rightmost particle of a jointly Poisson-reset Brownian gas still has the free-gas Rayleigh law after \(\sqrt{(4D/r)\log N}\) scaling. The \((\log N)^{1/\alpha}\) soft-confinement scaling proposed heuristically for this range instead collapses to zero: the missing distinction is between fixed-age far tails and long-time equilibrium tails.
