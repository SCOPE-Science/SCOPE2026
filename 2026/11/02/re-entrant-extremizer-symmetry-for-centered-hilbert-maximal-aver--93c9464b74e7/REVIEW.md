Accessible earlier mathematical claims about path variation and other finite graph families do not imply these P5/P7 constants. The three-vertex star value is known background. An unindexed equivalent calculation remains possible, and this comparison is not absolute priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The point of the classification is the nonmonotone localization of all norm-extremizing inputs along the successive odd paths: the unique center in \(P_3\), two near-boundary peaks in \(P_5\), and restoration of a unique central peak in \(P_7\). This provides an exact, reproducible boundary phenomenon on a canonical one-dimensional family, not a claim that later path orders follow the same trend.
