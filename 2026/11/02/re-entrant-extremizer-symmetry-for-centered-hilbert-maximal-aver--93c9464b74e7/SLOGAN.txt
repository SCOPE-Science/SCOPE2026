Centered graph-ball maximal averaging has exact algebraic strong \(\ell^2\) norms and completely classified extremizers on the odd paths \(P_3\), \(P_5\), and \(P_7\). The peak of every extremizer is central for three vertices, shifts one step from an endpoint for five, and returns uniquely to the center for seven, a finite re-entrance of symmetry rather than a claimed all-size pattern.
