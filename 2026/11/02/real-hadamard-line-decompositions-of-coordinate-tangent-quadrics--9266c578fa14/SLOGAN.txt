A smooth real quadric tangent to the four coordinate planes is a Hadamard product of two real lines exactly when the determinant of its zero-diagonal inverse matrix is positive; its real locus is then a torus, rather than a sphere. In the positive-sign case every real point factors and the ordered real line decompositions form exactly two reciprocal-scaling orbits; in the negative-sign case complex factors exist but no real line factors do.
