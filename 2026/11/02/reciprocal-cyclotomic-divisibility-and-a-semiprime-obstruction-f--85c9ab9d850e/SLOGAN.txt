The polynomial \(F(t)=t^2-t+1\) admits no reciprocal divisibility pair \(a\mid F(b)\), \(b\mid F(a)\) beyond \((1,1)\). A descending quotient argument consequently shows that the gcd-reciprocal sum \(\sum_{i=1}^{n}1/\gcd(n,i)\) is never integral when \(n>1\) has at most two prime factors counted with multiplicity.
