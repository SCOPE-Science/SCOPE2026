The Frobenius principal-angle cosines between finite Toeplitz and Hankel matrix spaces converge, with multiplicity two, to \(1,1/2,1/3,\ldots\) in the full \(\ell^2\) singular spectrum. After eliminating their two-dimensional common component, the sharp squared-norm stability constant for a Toeplitz-plus-Hankel decomposition tends to \(1/2\).
