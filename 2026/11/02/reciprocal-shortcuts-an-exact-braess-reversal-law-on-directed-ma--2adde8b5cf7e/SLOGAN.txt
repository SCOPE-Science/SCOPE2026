On a one-way continuous-time Markov ring, any added nonadjacent *bidirectional* link raises integrated Kemeny relaxation for \(n\ge5\), whereas the four-state antipodal link is neutral at every rate. A fixed link-rate budget is best allocated entirely to the shorter directed shortcut, and a sufficiently strong opposite link creates an exact threshold beyond which increasing the other rate paradoxically *improves* the already-augmented network.
