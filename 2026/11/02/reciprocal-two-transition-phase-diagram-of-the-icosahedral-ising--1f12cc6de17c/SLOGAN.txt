For the twelve-spin icosahedral \(J_1\)–\(J_2\) Ising antiferromagnet, the two exact frustration transitions occur at \(J_2/J_1=1/3\) and \(3\), with successive ground-state degeneracies \(60,80,20,80,60\). The intermediate phase consists exactly of three positive and three negative antipodal spin pairs; both transitions have discontinuous distance-two correlations.
