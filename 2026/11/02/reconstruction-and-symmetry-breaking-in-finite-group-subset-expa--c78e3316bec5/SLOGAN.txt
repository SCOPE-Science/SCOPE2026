For every finite group \(G\), the full Boolean subset expansion has automorphism group \(\operatorname{Hol}(G)\), while its pointed Szendrei expansion has automorphism group exactly \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\). The \(|G|\) distinct pointed copies form one orbit, whose stabilizer is the pointed automorphism group, and both expansions reconstruct \(G\) up to isomorphism.
