Conditioning a uniform permutation to have a record at any prescribed position is achieved by a constant-fibre, inversion-removing map that simultaneously decreases every convex nondecreasing separable rank-displacement cost. The footrule covariance with the record indicator at position \(i\) is exactly \(-(2i-1)/(3n)\), giving an exact negative covariance \(-(n^2-1)/(3n)\) with the total upper-record count.
