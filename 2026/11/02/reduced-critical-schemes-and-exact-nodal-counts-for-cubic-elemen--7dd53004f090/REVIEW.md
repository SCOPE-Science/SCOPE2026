The difference equation and a second-moment cancellation force both distinct nonzero values to violate \(p\nmid(n-2)\); every critical point is consequently an indicator vector with support size congruent to one modulo \(p\). Exact finite-field checks verify example instances only.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The classification determines all zero-dimensional components, multiplicity one at each, and all node counts for a natural infinite family where the motivating article explicitly leaves component completeness unresolved. The singularities also imply geometric normality for all stated parameters.
