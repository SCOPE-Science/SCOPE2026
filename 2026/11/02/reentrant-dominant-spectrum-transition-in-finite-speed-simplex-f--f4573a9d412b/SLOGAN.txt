A stationary regular-simplex flock with finite-speed communication has a sharp distance-dependent switch between real and oscillatory dominant velocity-relaxation modes. For inverse-power influence with exponent \(p>1\), increasing simplex separation crosses **two** exact spectral transition distances, returning the dominant mode to the real axis; the intervening oscillatory interval expands from scale \(\log N/N\) to \((N/\log N)^{1/(p-1)}\) as the number \(N\) of agents grows.
