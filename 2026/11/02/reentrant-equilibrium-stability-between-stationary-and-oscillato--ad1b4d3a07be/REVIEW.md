# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The common positive equilibrium \(E_h=(1,1,h,h)\) follows from the four reaction balances, with all coefficients satisfying the stated positivity and diffusion conditions. Linearization against each scalar Neumann eigenfunction yields the explicit four-by-four matrix in `RESULT.md`. Direct determinant expansion proves the shifted-quartic formula. Its second and third Hurwitz determinants reduce to two tractable expressions, and the fourth-order Routh–Hurwitz test is equivalent to positivity of the constant coefficient and third determinant. At the first spatial eigenvalue these conditions produce **two** disjoint intervals of exponential stability with exact endpoints. The higher modes are excluded analytically by a positive square-completion bound and strict monotonicity of the oscillatory threshold; the stable/unstable claims are not inferred from truncating the spectrum. Sectorial semilinear parabolic theory supplies the local nonlinear consequences. The provided `verification.py` performs exact symbolic identity checks and illustrative, explicitly nonexhaustive spectral tests. The disk spectral separation is a standard Bessel derivative-zero fact.

## Originality — PASS, subject to specialist-literature priority risk
The April 2026 motivating work derives a general quartic dispersion relation and gives numerical cross-diffusion pattern diagrams; it does not establish the exact stable–unstable–stable–unstable gain partition, the narrow recovered stability window, or the all-mode disk exclusion. An existing closely related four-component spectral calculation on a different admissible parameter slice yields an **oscillatory** first-mode instability, not real-eigenvalue loss, recovery and subsequent oscillatory loss. It neither states nor entails the present three-threshold diagram. The 2019 two-field Turing–Hopf and 2022 spatially heterogeneous WKB studies do not cover this four-field gain slice. The 2014 Brusselator discussion of reentrant *pattern branches* is a different phenomenon from restabilization of a homogeneous equilibrium. The general Hurwitz criterion and classical Turing/Hopf mechanisms are antecedents, not asserted new. An unindexed exact special-case analysis could still affect historical priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A fixed well-posed positive-parameter two-chemical predator–prey system exhibits two distinct local-instability mechanisms separated by a genuine *recovered* homogeneous stability interval, rather than the usual single monotone stability loss. The proof gives all spatial modes, explicit sharp threshold constants, precise critical eigenvalue types, and an open-set robustness check at four parameter witnesses. Such reentrance is relevant to chemical feedback design and pattern-suppression regimes. The narrowness of the regained interval does not negate its structural significance; it is not an arbitrary isolated value because the pattern persists under small coefficient perturbations. No global dynamics or nonlinear branches at the thresholds are claimed.
