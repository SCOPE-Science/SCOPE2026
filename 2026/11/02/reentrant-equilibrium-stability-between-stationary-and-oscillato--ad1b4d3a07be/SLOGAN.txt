A four-field predator–prey system with two diffusible signals has an explicitly solvable, strictly positive equilibrium that is stable, destabilizes through a real spatial eigenvalue, **restabilizes**, and finally destabilizes through an oscillatory spatial pair as signal production increases. On a smooth Neumann disk the exact three thresholds are \(\frac{11-\sqrt{17}}2\), \(\frac{11+\sqrt{17}}2\), and \(\frac{148}{\sqrt{377}}\); the recovered stability interval is nonempty and persists under small positive-parameter perturbations.
