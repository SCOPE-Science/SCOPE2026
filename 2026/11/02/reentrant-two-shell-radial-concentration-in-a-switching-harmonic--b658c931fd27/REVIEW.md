# Scientific review: reentrant two-shell radial concentration

## Correctness — PASS
The starting Gaussian scale-mixture law is independently recovered from the stationary effective-stiffness transport equations, including correct on/off occupation weights and normalization. The transformed stationary radius law follows by a strong law for Gaussian coordinate squares. Its logarithmic derivative is a rational expression with a single quadratic numerator whose discriminant and boundary value classify all limiting radial extrema. The finite-dimensional assertion is weaker than an exact finite-dimensional phase diagram: positivity of the chi-density derivative below the limiting support, combined with local \(C^1\) convergence above that support and analyticity, forces two strict local maxima and a separating local minimum for every sufficiently large dimension. Independent symbolic and numerical checks from `verify.py` confirm the normalization, algebra, and a finite-dimensional example. An explicit dimension cutoff is not established.

## Originality — PASS, with residual publication-priority risk
Frydel (2024, arXiv:2407.12213) already provides the Gaussian mixture, its arbitrary-dimensional extension and the effective-stiffness endpoint crossover at \(\alpha=2\). The novelty assessed is not those ingredients but the classification of modes of the actual Euclidean-radius histogram, its two disjoint switching-rate windows, and its provable finite-dimensional persistence. Relevant sections and appendices of the primary open-access text were examined. Biroli et al. (2023, arXiv:2312.02570) provide a more general common-variance mixture for multi-particle switching traps and analyze extremes of particle positions in a different limit. Those results do not by themselves state a single-vector high-dimensional radius-mode phase diagram. Searches for radial peaks, reentrant shell densities, Gaussian-mixture representations, and equivalent switching-trap parameters did not reveal a directly covering classification. A published mixture formula can of course be manipulated to derive the limiting law, and an unlocated prior derivation remains possible; this is not a guarantee of worldwide priority. The source's possible supplementary results and future versions remain residual risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The Euclidean radius is a physically natural concentration observable, distinct from the probability density per unit spatial volume. A single centrally peaked density per unit volume can exhibit two separated high-dimensional radial shells due to the competition of switching variance and the sphere Jacobian. The strict two-window reentrance, exact algebraic boundary values and eventual finite-dimensional persistence characterize a substantive shape phenomenon rather than a single parameter computation or a restatement of the source's effective-precision crossover.

## Scientific limitations
No finite-dimensional exact mode count or uniform dimension threshold is proved. No claim is made about unequal switching rates, a nonzero off-stiffness, nonquadratic forces, dynamical criticality or physical phase transitions. The high-dimensional and rate-critical limits are not interchanged. Numerical checks are examples, not infinite-dimensional proof. Publication-priority assessment is self-review and not independent.
