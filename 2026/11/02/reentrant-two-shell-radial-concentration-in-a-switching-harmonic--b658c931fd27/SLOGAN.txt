A passive Brownian particle in an on/off harmonic trap has a single central peak per unit spatial volume, yet its high-dimensional radial histogram develops two separated shells in exactly two disjoint switching-rate windows: \(0<\alpha<1-\sqrt3/2\) and \(1+\sqrt3/2<\alpha<2\). Both shells persist as distinct strict local radial maxima in every sufficiently large finite dimension, even though the vector density is everywhere strictly radially decreasing.
