Every refinement-closed finite family of set partitions has identical VC and Littlestone dimensions under pair-equality queries: both equal \(|V|\) minus the fewest feasible blocks, and the shattered supports are exactly feasible-component forests. For partitions into independent sets of a graph \(G\), this becomes \(|V|-\chi(G)\), with an exact analogous formula after every consistent positive/negative query history.
