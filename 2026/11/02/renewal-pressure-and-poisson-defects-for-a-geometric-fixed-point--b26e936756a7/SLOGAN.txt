For the geometric distance-to-fixed-point potential on the binary full shift, the equilibrium gaps between defect symbols are exactly independent renewals. Its pressure has the sharp low-temperature law \(P_\beta=e^{-\beta/(1-\theta)}(1+e^{-\beta}+e^{-(1+\theta)\beta}+o(e^{-(1+\theta)\beta}))\), and the stationary defects converge to a unit-rate Poisson process on the matching exponential time scale.
