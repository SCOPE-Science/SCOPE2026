Every Frobenius residue-lift graph \(\{(x,x^p+p h(x\bmod p))\}\) over \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\) has the same Fourier-zero set and the same complete family of \(p^{p^2+p}\) orthogonal exponential bases, for every odd prime and every digit perturbation \(h\). Its additive convolution rank is only \(p^3-p^2+p\), so the surviving Fourier proportion tends to zero while the number of full bases grows rapidly.
