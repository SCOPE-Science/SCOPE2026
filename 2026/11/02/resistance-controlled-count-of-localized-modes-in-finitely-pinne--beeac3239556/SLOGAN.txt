For any finite family of positive foundation pins in an infinite square mass-spring lattice, the number of localized vibrational modes above the phonon band equals one plus the positive inertia of an explicit effective-resistance form. Two adjacent equal pins support a second mode exactly above strength \(4\), diagonal pins exactly above \(\pi\), and a right-angle three-pin cluster has sharp thresholds \(\pi\) and \(3\pi/(\pi-1)\).
