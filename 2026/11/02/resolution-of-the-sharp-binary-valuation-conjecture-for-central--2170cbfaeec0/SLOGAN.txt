The sharp cubic 2-adic valuation conjecture for central Delannoy numbers holds: \(\nu_2(D_n-1)=3\nu_2(n)\) whenever \(4\mid n\), while the remaining positive indices have valuation \(1\) or \(2\) according to parity. A dyadic dilogarithm identity and an exact cancellation of two exceptional quadratic coefficients prove the all-index statement.
