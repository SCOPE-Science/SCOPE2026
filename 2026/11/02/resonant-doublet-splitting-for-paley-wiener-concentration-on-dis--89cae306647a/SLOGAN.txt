For bandlimited concentration on two fixed equal intervals separated by a large distance, the highest two sinc-kernel eigenvalues have an oscillatory \(R^{-1}\) splitting. At separations \(R=m+t/m\), their \(m^{-2}\) coefficients are explicitly \(Q_a\pm|C_a t-2Q_a|\), and the leading gap cancels at the positive detuning \(t=2Q_a/C_a\) without implying exact degeneracy.
