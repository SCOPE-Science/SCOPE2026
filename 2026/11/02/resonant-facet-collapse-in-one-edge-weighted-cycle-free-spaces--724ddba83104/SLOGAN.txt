At the nongeneric integer resonances of a cycle with one variable edge, the Lipschitz-free unit ball has exactly \(2\binom{n-1}{(n-1+t)/2}\) nonsimplicial facets, each with \(n\) vertices; outside these walls every facet is a simplex. In even cycle size, changing just one unweighted edge by an arbitrarily small amount multiplies the facet count by exactly \(n/2\).
