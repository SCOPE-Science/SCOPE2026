For every loopless finite quiver, the restricted \(p^e\)-nilpotent classes in first Hochschild cohomology of its radical-square-zero algebra are in exact bijection with tuples of \(p^e\)-nilpotent matrices indexed by parallel-arrow classes. This gives an all-prime, all-height finite-field census and an irreducible reduced nilcone whose point counts ignore the quiver's cycles, even when those cycles enlarge the ambient cohomology.
