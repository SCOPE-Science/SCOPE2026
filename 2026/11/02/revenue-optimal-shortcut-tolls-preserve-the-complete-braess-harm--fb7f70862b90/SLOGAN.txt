For the four-node Braess network, for \(0\le\kappa<1\), a profit-maximizing toll charged **only** on the added shortcut leaves the entire paradoxical-demand interval \(2(1-\kappa)/3<Q<2(1-\kappa)\) intact, although it reduces congestion harm. The theorem gives exact tolls, revenue, socially efficient shortcut flows, and sharp maximum harm for every demand and nonnegative intrinsic shortcut delay.
