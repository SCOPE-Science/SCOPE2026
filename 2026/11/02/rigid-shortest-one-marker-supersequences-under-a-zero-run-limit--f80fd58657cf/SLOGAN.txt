Every binary length-\(n\) word with exactly one \(1\) can be embedded into a host avoiding `000` in exactly \(\lceil3n/2\rceil\) symbols, and not fewer, for \(n\ge4\). Its shortest host is unique when \(n\) is even, while for \(n=2m+1\) there are exactly \(m+3\) shortest hosts, all avoiding `111` as well.
