In the five-edge Braess routing network, the sharp bounded-rationality excess-time factor \(2\) is attained at positive regret **only** when all traffic shifts to the central zigzag path, all three reference path costs tie, and three specific edge-latency intervals are flat. A nonnegative, explicitly computable plateau-and-slack certificate \(R\) strengthens the bound to \(\psi(g)\le2\varepsilon(g)-R\) in the only regime capable of attaining the factor \(2\).
