On a complete three-level finite poset, equivalence relations satisfying both modal confluence laws fall into exactly five rigid partition types. The number on levels of sizes \(a,b,c\ge1\) is \(\operatorname{Bell}(a)\operatorname{Bell}(b)\operatorname{Bell}(c)+\operatorname{Bell}(a)+\operatorname{Bell}(c)+2^b-1\).
