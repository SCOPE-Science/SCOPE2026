For the fixed two-agent \(\mathsf{S5}\) announcement \(\varphi=(p\to\Diamond_a q)\land(q\to\Diamond_b p)\), deleting exactly one world in each of \(n\) rounds forces an alternating epistemic zigzag: there are precisely \(2n!\) labelled extremal models and two isomorphism types. When the agents share an equivalence relation, the same announcement always stabilizes after one round.
