For triangular graphs \(T_n=L(K_n)\), every nonempty dual-general-position set is maximum: for \(n\ge5\) the only such sets are the \(n\) complete root-vertex stars, and \(D_n(z)=1+nz^{n-1}\). The four-root exception has eight three-vertex sets, while the known ordinary general-position number exceeds the dual number by one exactly when \(n\ge6\) is divisible by three.
