Every minimum-support \(s^2\)-dimensional subcode of an \(m\)-by-\(n\) single-parity-check product code is exactly the full subcode on a complete \((s+1)\)-by-\((s+1)\) rectangle. There are \(\binom{m}{s+1}\binom{n}{s+1}\) such subcodes over every finite field; this also gives the exact first nonzero high-rank erasure-ambiguity coefficient.
