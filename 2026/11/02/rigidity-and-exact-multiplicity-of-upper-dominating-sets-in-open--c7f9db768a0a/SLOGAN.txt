For every open ladder \(P_2\square P_n\) with \(n\ge2\), the maximum inclusion-minimal dominating sets have size \(n\) and form a rigid family of exactly four configurations when \(n\) is odd and six when \(n\) is even. The extra two even-length configurations consist of alternating empty and full rung pairs; the general bipartite upper-domination equality alone does not classify them.
