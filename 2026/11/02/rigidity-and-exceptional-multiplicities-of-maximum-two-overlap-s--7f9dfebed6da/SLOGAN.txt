For every alphabet of size \(q\ge6\) and blocklength \(n\ge4\), all maximum codes avoiding prefix–suffix overlaps of lengths one and two have just two rectangular forms and number exactly \(2\binom{q}{\lfloor(q+1)/3\rfloor}\). Only alphabets of size four and five admit genuinely mixed extremizers, producing exactly 44 and 140 labeled maximum codebooks, respectively.
