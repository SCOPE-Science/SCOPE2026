Every fastest-mixing symmetric Markov chain on the complete split graph \(K_p\vee\overline{K_q}\) uses exactly equal probabilities on all cross edges, even without any imposed symmetry; all remaining optimal designs are characterized by a precise interval for the internal clique Laplacian spectrum. The internal optimal support must be connected for \(p>q\), is optional for \(p\le q<2p\), and must disappear for \(q\ge2p\); the optimum mixing *value* itself was available from earlier clique-lifting results.
