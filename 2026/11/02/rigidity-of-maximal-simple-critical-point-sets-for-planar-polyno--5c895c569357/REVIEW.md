# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Bézout and a degree-bounded complete-intersection interpolation lemma produce a constant matrix relating the gradients of two potentials. The integrability equation is a nonzero constant-coefficient quadratic PDE. Two distinct factors force an additive separation, whereas a double factor permits at most \(d-1\) nondegenerate critical points, below the assumed \((d-1)^2\). The converse is explicit. The proof treats all \(d\ge3\) over \(\mathbb C\), and does not infer infinite conclusions from finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS, with literature risk
The 2022 paper of Arredondo and Muciño-Raymundo explicitly raises the grid-uniqueness question and treats degree three. The present result isolates and proves its maximal-simple-critical-point version for all degrees. Campillo–Olivares gives a related but distinct projective-foliation uniqueness theorem. Searches found no exact general rigidity statement; earlier unindexed equivalents remain possible.

## Value — PASS
The all-degree classification closes a natural maximal-count regime of a specifically motivated interpolation question and identifies the exceptional potential-sharing geometry as an affine Cartesian grid. It excludes every other reduced maximal configuration rather than only exhibiting examples.

## Limits
No claim about non-maximal Morse configurations, positive characteristic, or nonreduced schemes. Self-review are not independent validation. Scientific novelty requires external scrutiny before any publication.
