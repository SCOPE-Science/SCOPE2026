For a thermally diffusing run-and-tumble particle in a harmonic trap, the known mode-transition equation has exactly one thermal-variance solution for each reduced flip rate \(0<b<1\), and the critical variance decreases strictly with the flip rate. At the transition the central density becomes quartically flat and the two peaks open with the exact law \(m(B)\sim\sqrt{3(B_*-B)}\), while the full global mode count admits no competing branches.
