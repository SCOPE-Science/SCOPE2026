For a thermally noisy, harmonically trapped one-dimensional run-and-tumble particle, the known implicit shape-transition boundary is proved to be globally unique and strictly decreasing with the persistence parameter; the stationary density has exactly two modes below it and one mode on or above it. The symmetric peak locations obey the universal law \(m_\ell(t)^2=3(t_c(\ell)-t)+O_\ell((t_c(\ell)-t)^2)\) as the boundary is approached from below.
