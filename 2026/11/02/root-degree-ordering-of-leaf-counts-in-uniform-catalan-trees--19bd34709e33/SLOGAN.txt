In a uniform Catalan plane tree with \(m\ge2\) edges, the entire leaf-count distribution increases in monotone-likelihood-ratio order with root degree: its joint counting matrix is TP2, with strictly positive minors wherever all four cells occur. Yet \(\operatorname{Cov}(D,L)=(m-1)/(m+2)>0\) while \(\operatorname{Corr}(D,L)\sim\sqrt{2/m}\), so a strict finite-size stochastic dependence coexists with asymptotic decorrelation.
