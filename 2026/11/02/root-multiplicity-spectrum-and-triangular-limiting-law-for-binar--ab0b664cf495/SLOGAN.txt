For length-\(2m\) binary words with exactly two ones, the numbers with exactly \(t\) distinct shuffle roots have a closed binomial-difference formula and bivariate generating function \(uz(1+z+z^2)^2/((1-z)^2(1-uz^3))\) for \(t\ge1\). The scaled root count has a quarter-mass at zero and an explicit triangular continuous limiting law; the earlier known total number of shuffle squares in this content class is recovered, not re-claimed.
