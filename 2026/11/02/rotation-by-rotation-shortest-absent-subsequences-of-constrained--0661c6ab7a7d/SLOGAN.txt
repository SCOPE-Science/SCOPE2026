For every constrained binary circle whose successive-one gaps contain one or two zeros and never have adjacent one-zero gaps, the numbers of shortest absent scattered subsequences across all rooted linearizations have the exact spectrum \((t,2t,2(m-t))\), where \(m\) is the number of ones and \(t\) the number of double-zero gaps. The spectrum depends only on the gap counts, not their arrangement, and yields an explicit Fibonacci-number rotation law for circular Fibonacci words.
