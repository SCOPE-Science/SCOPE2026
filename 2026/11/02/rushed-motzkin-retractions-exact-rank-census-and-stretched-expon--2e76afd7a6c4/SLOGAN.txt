Weak endomorphism idempotents of a labeled cycle have an exact rank distribution given by looped-path transfer matrices. Their total count is \(3^n\exp[-c n^{1/3}+O(\log n)]\), while a uniformly sampled idempotent has rank asymptotic to \(\kappa n^{1/3}\), with explicit positive constants \(c\) and \(\kappa\).
