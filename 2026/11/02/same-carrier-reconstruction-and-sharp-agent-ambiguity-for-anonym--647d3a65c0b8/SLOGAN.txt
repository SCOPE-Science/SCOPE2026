For anonymous two-agent \(\mathsf{S5}\) knowledge with exactly two irredundant minimal supports at every finite world, same-carrier representability is equivalent to support stability and bipartiteness of a recovered incidence graph. With \(c\) connected components there are exactly \(2^c\) ordered agent decompositions, and the sharp maximum on \(n\ge4\) worlds is \(2^{\lfloor n/4\rfloor}\).
