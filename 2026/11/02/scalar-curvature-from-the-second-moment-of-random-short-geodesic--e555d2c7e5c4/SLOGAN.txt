Two independent volume-uniform points in a shrinking Riemannian geodesic ball have a mean squared intrinsic distance whose first non-Euclidean coefficient is exactly \(-\frac{n+8}{3(n+2)^2(n+4)}\operatorname{Scal}_g(p)\). Thus one natural two-point distance moment recovers scalar curvature locally, without a maximizing configuration or measuring the ball volume.
