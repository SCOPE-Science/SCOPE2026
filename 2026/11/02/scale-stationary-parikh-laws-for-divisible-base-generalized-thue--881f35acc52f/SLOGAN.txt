For generalized Thue–Morse words in any base \(B\) divisible by the alphabet size \(m\), the entire probability distribution of centered Parikh vectors of length \(B^{e+1}\) factors is **exactly the same for every exponent** \(e\ge0\). A finite rational carry formula gives every class probability, including a closed expression for the probability of perfect letter balance and the corresponding weighted additive-sum laws.
