All words formed by concatenating the sixteen-bit blocks \(1101100x1100100y\), with free bits except a final \(y=1\), have subsequence-universality index exactly six times the number of blocks and exactly \(2^{q+1}\) shortest absent subsequences for \(q\) blocks. Consequently, the regular paperfolding prefix of length \(2^k\), for \(k\ge4\), has universality index \(3\cdot2^{k-3}\) and precisely \(2^{2^{k-4}+1}\) shortest absent subsequences.
