For complete pure qubit hypergraph states with Mersenne interaction size \(k=2^j-1\) and \(j\ge3\), every bipartition with at least \(2^j-1\) qubits on each side has exact Schmidt rank \(2^j\) and a complete spectrum determined by a \(2^j\times2^j\) cyclic sign matrix. As both blocks grow and then \(j\to\infty\), a single eigenvalue approaches one, so entanglement entropy tends to zero despite unbounded Schmidt rank.
