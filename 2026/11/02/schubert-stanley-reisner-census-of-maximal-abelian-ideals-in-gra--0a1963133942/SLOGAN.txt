The maximum-dimensional abelian ideals in every graphical two-step Lie algebra admit a Schubert decomposition into Stanley–Reisner coordinate arrangements, giving an exact independence-polynomial formula for their number over every finite field. Although \(P_4\) and \(K_3\sqcup K_1\) share the same graph independence polynomial, their maximum-abelian-ideal varieties are respectively two intersecting projective lines and a projective plane, with \(2q+1\) versus \(q^2+q+1\) points.
