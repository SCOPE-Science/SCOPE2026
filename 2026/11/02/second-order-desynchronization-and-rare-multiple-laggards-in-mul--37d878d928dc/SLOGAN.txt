For \(q\ge3\) independent coupon collectors, the first loss of synchronization occurs after \(\sqrt{\pi d/(2q)}-1/(3q)+O_q(d^{-1/2})\) distinct coupons on average, and the probability that exactly two collectors lag in the first unequal round is asymptotic to \(\frac{q-1}{2}\sqrt{\pi/(2q)}d^{-1/2}\). The two-player case is exceptional: its first-break level is exactly one plus a symmetric random-walk bridge maximum, with no constant-order mean correction.
