For a simplex in any fixed dimension \(d\ge3\), the probability that a uniformly sampled daughter simplex occupies more than \(1-\varepsilon\) of its volume equals \(\frac{(d+1)!(d!)^{d+1}}{[d(d+1)]!}\varepsilon^{d(d+1)}\bigl(1+\frac{d(d+1)(d^2-1)}{2(d(d+1)+1)}\varepsilon+O_d(\varepsilon^2)\bigr)\). Conditional near-maximal samples exhibit a universal uniform-simplex law for their rescaled barycentric deviations, independently of the parent's shape.
