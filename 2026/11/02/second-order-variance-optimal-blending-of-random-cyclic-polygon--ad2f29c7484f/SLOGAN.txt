For \(n\) uniform random points on a circle, the unique minimum-variance blend of polygon semiperimeter and area shifts its classical extrapolation weight by \(16\pi^2/(3n^2)\). This reduces the sharp leading variance constant from the known \(3616\pi^{10}\) to \(1056\pi^{10}\), while the area–semiperimeter correlation differs from one by \((297/10)\pi^4n^{-4}\) to leading order.
