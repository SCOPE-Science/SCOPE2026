For the truncated polynomial algebra \(k[t]/(t^n)\) with \(n\ge4\), the reduced rank-at-most-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operator variety has exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) equidimensional Segre-cone components with explicit intersections. Over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor q^{n-1}-(q^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}-1)/(q-1)\) of these operators are nonzero.
