Hou's 2024 DSR-scoring rule refines Copeland on every strict tournament of at most six alternatives, but fails at seven: exactly \(5,040\) of the \(2^{21}\) labeled seven-alternative tournaments violate containment. All \(5,040\) obstructions are relabelings of one asymmetric tournament, giving a sharp negative answer to the original general-refinement conjecture.
