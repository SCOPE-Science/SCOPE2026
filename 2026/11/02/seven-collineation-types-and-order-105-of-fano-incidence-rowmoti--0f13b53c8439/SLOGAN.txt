The Fano plane's point–line incidence poset has exactly \(458\) order ideals, and rowmotion partitions them into \(29\) three-cycles, \(35\) five-cycles and \(28\) seven-cycles, giving permutation order \(105\). The collineation group splits the \(92\) cycles into exactly seven symmetry types, and ideal cardinality has different averages on different types.
