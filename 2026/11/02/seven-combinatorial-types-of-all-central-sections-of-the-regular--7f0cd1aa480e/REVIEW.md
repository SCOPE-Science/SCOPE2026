The polygon-incidence rule counts intersections of edges and vertices and captures the extra triangular facets lying in the cut plane in nongeneric cases. The proof explicitly treats all normal directions, not only generic sampling.

The seven-class table connects root-system diagram automorphisms with observable three-dimensional section shapes and resolves all exceptional cases, rather than repeating the generic case or selecting arbitrary examples. This is an exact, finite but exhaustive classification with explicit geometric motivation.
