For every \(k\ge3\), the \(2^k\) cyclic rotations of the finite Thue–Morse word \(T_k\) fall into exactly seven scattered-subsequence \(3\)-binomial classes, of sizes \(2^{k-2}\) and six times \(2^{k-3}\). Equality of just the two counts \(\binom{w}{01}\) and \(\binom{w}{001}\) completely determines the class among these rotations.
