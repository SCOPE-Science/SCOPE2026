# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The infinite statement follows from a tag-preserving lift of the standard four-round order-game strategy for lengths at least fifteen. The remaining cases are checked by a fully explicit, terminating rank-four type recurrence over all tuples for half-graphs of orders one through fifteen, including repeated selections. The independent direct adversarial game implementation confirms both the losing boundary at six versus seven and the winning boundary at seven versus eight. The full literal programs and their successful outputs are included; the finite computations are not used as a substitute for the infinite argument.

## Originality
PASS, with residual historical-priority risk. The inspected parts of Pikhurko–Verbitsky (arXiv:1003.4865, §2.2 and §3) supply the general tuple-type and game framework but not the exact seven-type spectrum for finite half-graphs. Malliaris–Shelah (arXiv:1102.3904, Introduction, Definition 1.2, Theorem 5.18) uses half-graphs as order-property witnesses to obtain regularity for stable graphs; it does not assert the rank-four equivalence formula here. Searches for the exact numerical claim and its half-graph, chain-graph, and Ferrers-graph formulations did not locate a covering statement. Equivalent unpublished computations or less discoverable papers remain a genuine possibility.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Half-graphs are canonical finite witnesses of the model-theoretic order property, so the exact depth-four boundary is a motivated measure of how quickly uncoloured first-order logic can resolve the length of an encoded order. The sudden stabilization at seven, against the simple length-fifteen cutoff supplied by the order interpretation, is not given by the general transfer theorem. It gives a complete exact type partition, a sharp smallest half-graph representative with fourteen vertices, and independently checked short-rank behavior. Moreover, existential-only or universal-only four-quantifier graph sentences already stabilize at size four because every four-vertex atomic pattern order-compresses into the fourth half-graph; the extra rank-four distinctions therefore genuinely use alternating quantifiers.

## Limits
This is a result only for finite uncoloured half-graphs at quantifier rank four (and contextual lower ranks); independent mathematical review is not performed. The fourteen-vertex bound is only sharp among half-graph representatives. The novelty assessment is based on inspected relevant sections and actual indexing searches, not exhaustive access to every historical work.
