The positive-root posets of type \(A_n\), viewed only with strict root order, have exactly seven first-order theories of quantifier rank three: \(\mathcal R_m\equiv_3\mathcal R_n\) exactly when \(\min(m,7)=\min(n,7)\). The sharp cutoff seven follows from exact finite type recursion and an infinite-tail Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé argument; every theory has a representative with at most 28 roots.
