In the identity-and-relational-multiplication language, the infinite dicyclic family has exactly seven first-order rank-two theories: the three exceptional groups \(\operatorname{Dic}_2,\operatorname{Dic}_3,\operatorname{Dic}_4\), followed by four classes distinguished only by divisibility of the parameter by \(3\) and \(4\). The sharp largest minimal representative is the \(48\)-element group \(\operatorname{Dic}_{12}\).
