The characteristic-two gradient-critical scheme of the six-variable elementary-symmetric quartic has a reduced \(K_7\) arrangement of \(21\) projective lines, but carries an additional length-\(8\) embedded correction at each of its seven vertices. Its exact Hilbert polynomial is \(21d+35\), and a hidden \(S_7\) projective symmetry arises because the quartic changes only by fourth powers under projective-frame transpositions.
