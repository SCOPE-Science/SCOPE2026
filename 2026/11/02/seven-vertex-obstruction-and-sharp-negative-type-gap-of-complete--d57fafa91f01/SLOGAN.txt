For every complete split graph \(K_m\vee\overline{K_n}\), the maximal metric negative-type exponent is an explicit logarithm in \(m,n\), and the sharp quadratic gap is rational in \(m,n,2^p\). Ordinary \(1\)-negative type first fails within this family at seven vertices, in precisely three parameter classes; the critical Euclidean snowflake dimension drops by one.
