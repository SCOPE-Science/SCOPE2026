For the genus-six characteristic-eleven cyclic-quartic family \(y^4=x^5+ax^3+bx+1\), the exact Jacobian \(11\)-rank ranges over \(1,2,3,4\), while the \(a\)-number is always \(2\) or \(3\). Precisely \(75\) smooth coefficient-marked parameters have rank one, forming a reduced transverse intersection scheme, and a disjoint scheme of \(70\) parameters has rank two and \(a\)-number three.
