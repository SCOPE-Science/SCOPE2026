# Review: shape ordering of kinematic box sections

## Correctness — PASS
The exact sampling disintegration is width biased in normal orientation. Every generic section vertex corresponds to one Boolean edge crossing; the proved isoperimetric lower bound and degree upper bound reduce the vertex exponent to the size of a signed-sum halfspace. Conditional exponential tilting with bounded deterministic half-lengths proves the limiting rate. Jensen proves strict shape order; the two asymptotic expansions are verified algebraically. The accompanying numerical and finite checks pass but are not used as proofs of infinite assertions.

## Originality — PASS with historical risk
Kabluchko–Prochno–Thäle, arXiv:1910.02676, establishes the homogeneous cube projection rate, which is credited, not newly claimed. Swan (2016; arXiv:1808.09482) already gives the exact expected count. The comparable indexed central-section results concern offset zero, not conditioned kinematic affine sections. The investigated new statements concern arbitrary bounded nonconstant half-length profiles, strict stochastic log-count shape order, and the sharp opposite-end coefficients. A future stronger general triangular-array result could overlap; two related indexed records and a polytope-slicing manuscript were not fully available.

## Value — PASS
The same mean count for every orthotope hides genuinely shape-sensitive, nonconcentrating exponential section complexity. A sharp cubical shape comparison and exact upper/lower tail coefficients solve natural, fully specified distributional questions beyond one special cubical calculation.

## Scientific limitations
Only bounded positive half-length profiles and invariant affine-hyperplane sampling are addressed; no finite-dimensional shape order or unrestricted geometric rigidity is asserted. Historical priority is subject to the listed incomplete full-text comparisons. The tests do not certify infinite-dimensional statements by experiment.
