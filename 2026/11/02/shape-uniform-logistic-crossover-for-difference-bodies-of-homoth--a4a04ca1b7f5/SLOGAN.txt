The difference-body volume quotient of any homothetic frustum has an exact mixed-volume integral, strict truncation monotonicity, and sharp bounds attained by symmetric and simplicial bases. Uniformly over arbitrary convex bases in growing dimension, the quotient relative to its prism endpoint has the logistic boundary-layer limit \(2/(1+e^{-\tau/2})\) when the upper-face scale is \(1-\tau/m\), with error \(O_T(1/m)\).
