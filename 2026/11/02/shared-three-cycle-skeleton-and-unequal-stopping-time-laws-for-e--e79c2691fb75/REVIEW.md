# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

For any number of triangles, the exact ordered-pair-type support reduction has at most \(3(2^9-1)=1533\) cases, and multiplicities do not influence the center update or the trajectory of a represented type. The included complete finite enumerator compares individual originally represented types at all relevant times, not only collapsed supports. It verifies the iterate identities, first-entry support-count table and equality of primitive-three periodic sets and transitions; onto-support inclusion–exclusion translates each finite certificate row into a formula valid for every positive triangle count. An independently implemented explicit-neighborhood simulator checks all labelled inputs for triangle counts one through five. The sharp witnesses arise at support size two. No claim is made that finite-size simulation alone proves the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, with material qualifications

Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff [1] gives a general 3-color cycle-circulation criterion for eventual synchronization, which **already implies the common oscillation basin** here, and its graph-diameter bound on the synchronized branch. Those inherited points are not claimed as new. Doba [2] gives another general GHM eventual-behavior criterion and a walk-based description of excitation times. Neither inspected treatment states the exact first-periodic-entry census for friendship graphs, the limiting contrast between a time-two point mass and a uniform law on times two through four, or the graph-specific common primitive-three orbit count. Comparison was by implications rather than by title alone. Residual risk: an unindexed specialization or worked-out formula may cover these exact statistics.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Exact first-periodic-entry distributions are intrinsic dynamical invariants, not arbitrary parameter slices. The two classical excitation rules are naturally compared because their asymptotic basin selection is already understood to coincide on this family. Their genuinely different and sharply computed full transient distributions reveal information absent from a synchronization-only theorem. The limit laws differ macroscopically even while the primitive-three recurrent orbit sets and their evolution agree exactly. This is a motivated all-size classification for a standard sparse graph family, with transparent reproducibility.

## Limitations and closest sources

The proof concerns threshold-one synchronous three-color dynamics only on a bouquet of triangles. General-graph cycle criteria already settle which initial states oscillate; the new part is the complete exact first-entry histogram and associated recurrent-set count. A relevant historical equivalent cannot be ruled out absolutely.
