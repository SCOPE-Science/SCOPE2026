For third-order BDF on an arbitrarily uneven but fixed alternating-step grid, both nonconstant two-step Floquet multipliers lie strictly inside the disk of radius \(1/5\), and \(1/5\) is the exact supremum over all positive step ratios. Their change between complex and negative-real modes occurs at a reciprocal pair of precisely defined step ratios near \(28.515\) and \(0.03507\).
