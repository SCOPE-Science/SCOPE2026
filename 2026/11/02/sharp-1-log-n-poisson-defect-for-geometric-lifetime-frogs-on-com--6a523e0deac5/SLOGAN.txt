For one initially active frog per loopless complete graph, with geometric mean lifetime \(\log n+c\), the unvisited-count law is a Poisson\((e^{-c})\) law except for an asymptotically \(1/\log n\) mass at complete early extinction. The total-variation error has sharp leading coefficient one, and full-coverage probability is \(e^{-e^{-c}}(1-1/(\log n+c+1))+O_c((\log n)^{-2})\).
