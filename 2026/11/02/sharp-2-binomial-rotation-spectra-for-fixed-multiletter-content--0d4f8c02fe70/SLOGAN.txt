For every fixed positive letter-content vector on at least three symbols, the number of distinct \(2\)-binomial equivalence classes among cyclic rotations attains **every integer** from the word length divided by the content gcd to the full word length. The lower endpoint is characterized by maximal repetitions of a primitive-content block, the upper endpoint by the absence of proportionally balanced proper circular segments, and adjacent swaps give constructive witnesses for the entire interval.
