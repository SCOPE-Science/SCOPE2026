For every \(m\geq1\), the formulas \((\Box\Diamond)^m\top\) and \((\Box\Diamond)^{m+1}\top\) first disagree on \(2m+2\) worlds, and every smallest separating frame is rigid and has a forced spanning directed path. Exactly \(2^{2m^2+2m+1}\) nonisomorphic minimum separators exist, with a binomial edge-count polynomial.
