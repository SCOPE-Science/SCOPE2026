Every monogenic semigroup of index \(m\geq2\) and period \(r\) embeds in the nonempty-subset power semigroup of a cyclic group of the sharp minimum order \((m+1)r\), using just a two-element subset. All minimum-order abelian realizations arise from a cyclic subgroup of order \(m+1\) with cyclic quotient of order \(r\), and noncyclic minimum hosts exist exactly when \(\gcd(m+1,r)>1\).
