On labelled reflexive \(n\)-world Kripke frames, \(n\) carefully chosen one-step knowing-whether valuations suffice and are necessary to reconstruct every edge once \(n\ge3\), even with adaptive querying. Uniformly random valuations instead exhibit a sharp coupon-collector threshold at \(2^{n-1}\log n\), with limiting success probability \(\exp(-e^{-t})\) in the standard critical window.
