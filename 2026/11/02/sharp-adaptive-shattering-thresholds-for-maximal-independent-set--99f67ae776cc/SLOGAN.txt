Maximal-independent-set membership on graphs of maximum degree two first separates static from adaptive shattering on the ten-vertex path: \(\operatorname{VCdim}=3\) but \(\operatorname{Ldim}=4\), uniquely among degree-two graphs of that order; cycles first separate at thirteen vertices with dimensions \(4\) and \(5\). Disjoint unions of \(s\) ten-vertex paths have dimensions \(3s\) and \(4s\), so the adaptive advantage is unbounded without any branching vertices.
