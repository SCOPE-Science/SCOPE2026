For the cubic graph \(C=\{(t,t^3):t\in\mathbb F_q\}\) in odd characteristic, the exact additive basis order is \(3\) for every \(q\ge11\) outside characteristic \(3\), and \(4\) for the exceptional fields of orders \(5\) and \(7\). In characteristic \(3\), the graph is an additive subgroup and has no finite covering order.
