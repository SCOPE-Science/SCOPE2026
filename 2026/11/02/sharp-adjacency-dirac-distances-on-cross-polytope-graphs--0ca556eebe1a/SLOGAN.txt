On the one-skeleton of every cross-polytope of dimension \(r\ge3\), the adjacency–Dirac Connes distance has exactly two nonzero values: antipodal distance \(1/\sqrt{r-1}\), and a smaller adjacent distance given by a unique minimizer of an explicit \(2\times2\) Gram eigenvalue. Their gap has the sharp asymptotic \(1/(8r^{3/2})+1/(128r^{5/2})+O(r^{-7/2})\), although the corresponding graph distances are two and one.
