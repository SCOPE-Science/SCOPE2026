For the adjacency–Dirac spectral metric on a book graph with \(m\) triangles sharing one edge, the two hubs are exactly \(\sqrt{2/(m+2)}\) apart and distinct outer vertices are exactly distance \(1\) apart. Hub–outer distances admit an exact attained two-variable norm formula and decrease to \(1/\sqrt2\) with sharp correction \(1/\sqrt{2(m-1)}\).
