For the optimal \(\operatorname{TTR}_2\) convolution quadrature of the exponential kernel, every lag weight is nonnegative exactly when \(h\lambda\leq0.933212708152\ldots\), the smallest positive root of an explicit quartic. The sharp endpoint has precisely one zero weight (at lag six), and the admissible time-step range is about 1.866 times that of BDF2.
