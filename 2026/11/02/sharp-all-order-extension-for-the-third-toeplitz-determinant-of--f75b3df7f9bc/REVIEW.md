# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The theorem concerns the one-variable complex-symmetric determinant, and all quantifiers are explicit: every normalized starlike function and every real order \(0\le\alpha<1\). The essential new estimate \(|6x^2+2xw-3y^2|\le7\) is proved directly from a two-step Schwarz--Pick coefficient parameterization, with the boundary cases checked and equality classified. The determinant identity and both coefficient factors are reconstructed from the logarithmic derivative, and the extremal maps attain equality at every order. The standalone symbolic checker corroborates the algebra and finite samples, without being used for infinite verification.

## Originality: PASS under inspected evidence

Panja–Majumder–Banerjee, arXiv:2610.15497v1, Theorem 2.3 and Corollary 2.2, explicitly restrict the identical sharp \(T_3(2)\) bound to \(0\le\alpha\le2/3\), and the proof on pp. 12–13 explicitly retains this range; therefore that result does not entail the new complementary \(2/3<\alpha<1\) theorem. Nearby work on other determinant indices, odd-function classes and gap-restricted starlike classes is not a stronger form of this bound. The abstracts of the related Giri and Giri–Kumar works were reviewed but their full texts could not be obtained; implicit equivalent coverage remains a residual risk, not a claim of exhaustive uniqueness. Relevant mathematical-record searches found no direct covering statement.

## Value: PASS

The same natural sharp coefficient invariant now has a complete order-parameter answer rather than a theorem truncated at \(\alpha=2/3\). The completion requires a sharp, independently proved Carathéodory three-moment estimate, not simply substitution or numerical fitting. The precise open range is identified by the statement and proof limitation in a recent primary source. No unproved generalization to higher dimensions is included.
