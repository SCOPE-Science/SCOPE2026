For every starlikeness order \(0\le\alpha<1\), the optimal third Toeplitz determinant bound is \(\frac49(1-\alpha)^3(9-7\alpha+2\alpha^2)(21-22\alpha+8\alpha^2)\), with equality only at the two imaginary Koebe rotations. The new parameter range \(2/3<\alpha<1\), absent from the 2026 bound, follows from a sharp seven-unit Carathéodory three-moment inequality.
