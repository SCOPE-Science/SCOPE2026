A Bernoulli-erased two-step deadbeat controller reaches zero in finite time almost surely at every positive success probability, yet its moment of any chosen real order can decay, persist, or grow exponentially according to an exact geometric-gap equation. For the discrete double integrator, the sharp first- and second-moment erasure thresholds are \(1/2\) and \(1/3\), leaving a genuine region of decaying mean error but exploding mean-square error.
