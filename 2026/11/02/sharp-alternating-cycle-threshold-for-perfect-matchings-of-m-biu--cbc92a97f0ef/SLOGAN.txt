For the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\), square-flip reconfiguration of perfect matchings is a Lucas cube when \(n\) is even, but has two frozen matchings when \(n\) is odd. A single alternating-cycle move of length \(n+1\) is necessary and sufficient to reconnect those frozen matchings for odd \(n\), giving an exact parity-dependent connectivity threshold of \(4\) versus \(n+1\).
