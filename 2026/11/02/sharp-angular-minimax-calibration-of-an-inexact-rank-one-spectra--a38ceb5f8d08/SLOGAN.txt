For a two-mode positive-definite system with an approximate rank-one Ritz direction uncertain by at most \(\Phi\le\pi/4\), the uniquely robust optimal spectral-preconditioner scale is \(\theta_*=1+(\kappa-1)\sin^2\Phi\), with an exact closed-form worst-case condition number. The conventional unshifted scale can worsen conditioning as soon as the angle exceeds a sharp threshold of order \(\kappa^{-1/4}\); robust recalibration needs angular accuracy of order \(\kappa^{-1/2}\) for condition numbers uniformly bounded in \(\kappa\).
