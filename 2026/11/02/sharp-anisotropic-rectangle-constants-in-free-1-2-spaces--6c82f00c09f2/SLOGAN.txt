For the canonical bilinear interpolation from any positive-sided Manhattan rectangle into its four-vertex real Lipschitz-free \(1/2\)-space, the exact Lipschitz constant is \(\bigl(2\sqrt\lambda+\sqrt{1+\lambda}\bigr)^2/(3\lambda+1)\), with \(\lambda\) the longer-to-shorter side ratio. This strictly increases from the known square value \(3/2+\sqrt2\) to the nonattained limit \(3\), and the sharp finite-ratio value is attained by an explicit interior pair.
