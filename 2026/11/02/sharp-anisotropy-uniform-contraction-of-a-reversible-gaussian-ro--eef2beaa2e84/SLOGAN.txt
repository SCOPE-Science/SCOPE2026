The exact Gaussian-rotation/Ornstein--Uhlenbeck Strang sampler is reversible and bias-free at every step, and its sharp squared-coupling factor is an explicit two-by-two Perron eigenvalue. With the optimally scaled Stratonovich perturbation, this factor is uniformly less than one even as the Gaussian target becomes arbitrarily anisotropic, with small-step decay coefficient \(3-\sqrt5\).
