On a \(d\)-dimensional hypercube with one absorber and two local dephasing sites, a pair of antipodal monitored vertices leaves \(2^d-2d\) dark dimensions, whereas any triple with no antipodal pair leaves exactly \(2^d-3d+1\). Complete eventual absorption of every initial state occurs exactly for dimension \(d=3\) with no antipodal pair, independently of all positive monitoring rates.
