For the binary indicator of positions \(b^j-1\), the maximum number of ones seen along any length-\(n\) arithmetic progression is exactly \(1+\lfloor\log_b((b-1)(n-1)+1)\rfloor\), with a unique extremal word at each jump. Moreover, the entire arithmetic-progression factor language has only \(O_b(n^2)\) length-\(n\) words, a polynomial bound derived from normalized differences of integer powers.
