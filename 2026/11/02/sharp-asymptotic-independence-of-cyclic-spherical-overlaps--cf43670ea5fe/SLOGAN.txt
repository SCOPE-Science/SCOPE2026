For independent random directions on a fixed-dimensional sphere, every proper subset of consecutive inner products around a cycle is exactly independent, but the complete edge vector retains one global dependence measured by an explicit Gegenbauer density. Its distance from independent edges decays at the sharp rate \(\frac d2\bigl[\Gamma(d/2)/(\sqrt\pi\Gamma((d+1)/2))\bigr]^m\); in particular, cyclic fidelities of Haar-random qubits have total-variation distance asymptotic to \(3/2^{m+1}\) from independent uniforms.
