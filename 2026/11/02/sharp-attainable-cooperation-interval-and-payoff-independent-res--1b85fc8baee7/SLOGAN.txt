In the finite-capacity game–environment model, even fixing any positive rich-resource game, varying positive depleted-state payoffs realizes exactly the cooperator-limit interval \((x_0N_0/K,\,1-(1-x_0)N_0/K)\). Comparing that interval with the ecological feedback threshold \(1/(1+\theta)\) gives sharp payoff-independent conditions for inevitable recovery, inevitable exhaustion, or realizability of all three long-term resource states.
