For maximal-feedback EControl on a scalar quadratic with independent Bernoulli erasures, complete mean-square stability is possible **exactly when** the transmission probability exceeds \(2/3\), and then the sharp step-size ceiling is \(\gamma_*(q)=2(6q-4)/[8-7q+\sqrt{(8-7q)^2+4(1-q)(6q-4)}]\). At or beyond this boundary, even the primal mean-square error cannot vanish from any nonzero initial state when \(0<q<1\).
