For bipartite graphs with at most \(t\) missing neighbors at every vertex, the side-size threshold \(2t+3\) forces all proper geodesically convex sets to be singletons or edges, and a four-vertex convex square shows the threshold is sharp for every \(t\ge1\). The resulting convex-set concept class has VC dimension \(2\) and Littlestone dimension \(3\).
