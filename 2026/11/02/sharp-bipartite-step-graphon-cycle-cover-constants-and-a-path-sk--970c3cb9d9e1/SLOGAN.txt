For any fixed connected bipartite step-graphon whose positive block-mass vector lies in its edge cone, the even-order spanning cycle-cover probability is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/(\pi n)}\) times a conditional Gaussian tangent-cone angle, while odd orders are impossible. For a balanced \(2r\)-block path skeleton, the angle is exactly \(1/r\), giving the sharp constant \(\sqrt{2/\pi}/r\) for every fixed \(r\ge1\).
