Centered nonregular splittings of the multiple zero of \(z^n\) exhibit an exact positive Bloch-seminorm response at the first nonzero elementary-symmetric order \(2\le k<n\), with sharp coefficient \(C_{n,k}|e_k|\) and \(C_{n,k}\sim 4k(k^2-1)/(3en^3)\) for fixed \(k\). Every intermediate flatness order is attained by an explicit roots-of-unity zero pattern.
