For every \(n\ge3\), the least possible maximum cyclic jump of a classically \(123\)-avoiding permutation of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\) is exactly \(\max\{2,\lceil(n-1)/3\rceil\}\). At the sharp tour-length boundary \(n=3m+1\), the first two and last two labels of every extremizer are forced, and explicit constructions attain the bound at every allowed length.
