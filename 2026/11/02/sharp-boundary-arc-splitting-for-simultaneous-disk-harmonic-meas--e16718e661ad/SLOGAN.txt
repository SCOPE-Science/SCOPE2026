For two points \(re^{\pm ia}\) in the unit disk, the best fixed-length boundary set for maximizing the smaller harmonic measure is uniquely a central arc or two reflected arcs, separated by the sharp threshold \(\cos a=(\sqrt{k^2+8}-k)/2\) with \(k=(r+r^{-1})/2\). The exact arc-merger length is \(\rho_c=\pi^{-1}\arccos(k\cos a+\cos^2a-1)\), and below it disconnected observation strictly outperforms every single arc.
