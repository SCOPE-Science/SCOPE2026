For every real-simple-pole resolvent approximation that matches \(e^{-tz}\) through second order at the origin, a bounded reciprocal-pole interval forces an exact minimum amount of signed-residue cancellation, attained with only two or three poles. In the symmetric window \([t-h,t+h]\), the sharp coefficient total variation is \(1+(t/h)^2\), independent of how many poles are used.
