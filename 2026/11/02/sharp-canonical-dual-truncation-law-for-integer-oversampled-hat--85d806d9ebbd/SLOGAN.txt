For every integer-oversampled hat Gabor frame, the squared \(L^2\)-mass of the canonical dual on the \(n\)-th remote unit cell is asymptotic to an explicit positive constant times \(R_q^{2n}/\sqrt n\). The sharp rate decreases from \(1/3\) to the nonzero limit \(2-\sqrt3\), and an exact leading constant governs symmetric truncation error.
