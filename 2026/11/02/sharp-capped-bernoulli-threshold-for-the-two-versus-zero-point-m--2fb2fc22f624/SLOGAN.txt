A cap of \(p\le1/2\) on individual Bernoulli probabilities creates an exact, piecewise staircase threshold for the event of two successes to overtake zero successes: the unique boundary law has as many cap-saturated trials as possible plus one fractional trial. The sharp mean interpolates between \(1\) at cap \(1/2\) and the Poisson value \(\sqrt2\) as the cap vanishes.
