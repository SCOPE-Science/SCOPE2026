Every odd-prime Heisenberg group admits injective cyclic-coset tomography from exactly \(p+1\) subgroup directions, with no central direction required; fewer directions never suffice. Assigning phase lifts \(0\) and \(1\) to complementary sets of \((p+1)/2\) and \((p-1)/2\) quotient slopes gives at least \(p\binom p{(p+1)/2}\) explicit optimal designs.
