Under the inversion-weighted Mallows law on \(S_n\), fixed-point probability strictly decreases from the edge toward the center for \(0<q<1\), and strictly increases toward the center for \(q>1\); it is \(1/n\) everywhere at \(q=1\). The finite-sample reversal follows from a strict likelihood-ratio balancing law for an explicit Gaussian-binomial cut-crossing statistic.
