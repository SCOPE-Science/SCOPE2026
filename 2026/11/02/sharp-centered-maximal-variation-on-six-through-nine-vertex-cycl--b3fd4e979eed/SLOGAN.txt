For each unweighted cycle on \(6\), \(7\), \(8\), or \(9\) vertices, the centered graph-ball maximal operator has sharp edge-total-variation factor \(1-1/n\), attained by a single-vertex indicator. Exhaustive exact-rational dual certificates close the upper bounds for the first two cycle-diameter levels beyond the previously studied five-vertex range; no general claim for larger cycles is made.
