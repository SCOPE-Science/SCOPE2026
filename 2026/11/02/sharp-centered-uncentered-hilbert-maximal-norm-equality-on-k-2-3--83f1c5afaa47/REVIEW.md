Full public preprints of Soria–Tradacete (arXiv:1410.6204), González-Riquelme–Madrid (arXiv:2005.03146 and 2011.02630), and González-Riquelme–Kovač–Madrid (arXiv:2603.12462) were inspected at relevant definitions, statements and sections. An additional 2025 fractional-maximal source had only its abstract accessible; that abstract lists complete graphs and stars as its exact Hilbert examples. Searches found no covering exact \(K_{2,3}\) Hilbert claim. Unindexed equivalent calculations and inaccessible full text remain priority risks, rather than proof of uniqueness in the literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Among two-connected bipartite graphs the first irregular example has five vertices and is necessarily \(K_{2,3}\): with a two-and-three bipartition, all three vertices in the larger part need both neighbors. Thus this is the smallest natural test of whether the off-center ball freedom changes strong Hilbert amplification beyond stars and the regular four-cycle. The exact positive-operator invariant is not a known-table recomputation in the inspected literature; it unexpectedly retains centered–uncentered equality and forces the entire extremizing profile to respect the bipartition. The proved strict spectral gap supplies a reproducible equality classification and a benchmark for the genuinely open unbalanced complete-bipartite family. This is a finite sharp invariant, not a theorem for all part sizes.
