On the first irregular two-connected bipartite graph \(K_{2,3}\), centered and uncentered graph-ball maximal operators unexpectedly share the sharp Hilbert norm \(\sqrt{(8+\sqrt{55})/9}\). Both have a unique nonnegative extremizing ray, with absolute values \((1,1,c,c,c)\) and \(c=(\sqrt{55}-7)/3\).
