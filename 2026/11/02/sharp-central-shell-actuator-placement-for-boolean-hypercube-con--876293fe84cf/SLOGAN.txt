For every Boolean hypercube \(Q_d\), exactly \(\binom d{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}\) independently actuated vertices are necessary and sufficient for full Laplacian controllability. One explicit optimal placement is the central Hamming shell when \(d\) is odd, or the two middle Hamming layers in a fixed facet when \(d\) is even; the controlled fraction tends to zero as \(d^{-1/2}\).
