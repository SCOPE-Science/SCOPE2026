For three independent arclength-uniform points on any Euclidean triangle's boundary, the probability that their convex hull contains the original area centroid is an explicit positive combination of the second and third elementary symmetric functions of normalized side lengths. It is strictly Schur-concave, uniquely maximized by the equilateral triangle at \(2/27+20\log(2)/81\), and approaches its sharp infimum \(1/12+\log(2)/9\) only as a side vanishes.
