For every \(k\ge3\), the shortest circular binary word containing every length-\(k\) factor that avoids both `000` and `111` has exact length \(2(F_{k+1}+F_{k-1})-2\), even when the covering word itself is allowed forbidden windows. An optimal circular cover avoids both forbidden triples and doubles exactly those permissible factors with unequal initial and final pairs but at least one equal internal adjacent pair.
