A conserved classical environment with \(M\) phase sectors can make at most \(M\) observation times mutually pairwise dephasing, and saturation on a regular clock forces a uniform Fourier phase spectrum. The sharp imbalance penalty is \(\big(M\sum_s p_s^2-1\big)/(M-1)\) in mean squared visibility, while a saturated \(M\)-time process retains exactly \((M-1)\ln2-H\big(\operatorname{Bin}(M-1,1/2)\big)\) nats of coherence.
