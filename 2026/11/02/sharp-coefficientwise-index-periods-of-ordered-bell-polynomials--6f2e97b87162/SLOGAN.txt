For the ordered-Bell polynomials \(G_n(X)=\sum_k k!S(n,k)X^k\) modulo any \(m\ge2\), the least eventual **coefficientwise** period in the index is exactly the Carmichael function \(\lambda(m)\), and the first index from which any period works is exactly \(\max_{p^a\parallel m}a\). Both bounds are sharp for every modulus, even though fixed integer evaluations may exhibit shorter periods.
