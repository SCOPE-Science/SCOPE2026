For the zero-prehistory BDF3 scalar Green function, **all** impulse-response coefficients are nonnegative exactly when \(0<\mu\le0.831264155297064\ldots\), an algebraic threshold at which the sixth coefficient vanishes. The full \(\ell^\infty\) convolution gain is exactly one precisely in that range; beyond it, the sixth weight becomes negative or the tail oscillates indefinitely.
