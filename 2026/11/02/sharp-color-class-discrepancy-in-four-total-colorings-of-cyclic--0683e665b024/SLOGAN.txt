Every four-total coloring of the \(m\)-rung circular ladder has color-class size discrepancy at most \(\lfloor m/3\rfloor-(m\bmod3)\), and explicit compatible blocks attain the bound whenever a four-total coloring exists. The only non-colorable simple prism is the five-rung case, yielding an exact all-order sharp imbalance law beyond earlier non-equitability examples.
