For binary \(v\)-row incidence matrices with exactly \(k\) ones in every column, the sharp Schur-multiplier norm of the complex two-level symbol \(aJ+bB\), optimized over all finite widths and supports, is \(\left|a+bk/v\right|+|b|\sqrt{k(v-k)(v-1)}/v\). Complete uniform hypergraph incidences attain the maximum, and fixed-weight sign symbols can retain norm tending to \(1+2\sqrt{k}\) despite a vanishing negative-entry density.
