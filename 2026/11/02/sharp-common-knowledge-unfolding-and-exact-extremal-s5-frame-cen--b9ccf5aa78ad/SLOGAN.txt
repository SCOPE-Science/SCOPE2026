On an \(n\)-world, \(k\)-agent finite S5 frame with \(n,k\ge2\), nested everybody-knows requires at most \(n-1\) stages to recover common knowledge for all events, and exactly the frames whose combined knowledge graph is a spanning path attain this bound. An exact recurrence counts every labelled extremizer, allowing agents to share path edges; for two agents there are \(n!\) extremizers whenever \(n\ge3\).
