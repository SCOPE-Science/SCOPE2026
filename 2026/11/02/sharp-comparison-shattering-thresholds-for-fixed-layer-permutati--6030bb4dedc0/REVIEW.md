# Scientific review: sharp comparison shattering thresholds for fixed-layer permutations

## Correctness — PASS

The permutation/cut correspondence is exact and label complementation preserves the two dimensions. Private points establish the universal bound of \(2r\). To attain that bound the all-zero, singleton and all-one traces require disjoint collections of respectively \(r\), \(2r\) and \(r\) cut positions, hence \(n\ge4r+1\); the independent four-point gadgets achieve the boundary. For three decreasing layers the lower support constructions and cardinality/counting obstructions give every finite VC value. The supplied eight-witness depth-three tree proves the first online gap, while independent threshold subblocks imply the all-parameter online growth bound. Two distinct finite implementations reproduced the boundary values. Finite checks do not replace the general proofs.

## Originality — PASS under accessible comparisons

Kabela, Kráľ, Noel and Pierron (arXiv:2110.01723, 2021) investigate layered permutation density maximization; their inspected theorems and conclusion do not determine shattering of positional comparisons. Guingona, Kolesnikov, Nierwinski and Soucy (arXiv:2109.04805, 2021) determine dimensions of zero-set families generated by all nonzero linear combinations, a different class with equal dimensions. Raz (2000) uses a different permutation-pattern shattering notion. The standard VC dimension of unions of intervals uses interval concepts queried by points, not fixed-cardinality point sets queried by intervals. A remaining priority risk concerns older dual interval-range terminology; a comprehensive historical audit was not performed.

## Value — PASS

An exact sharp threshold for every fixed number of layers, an all-length formula at three layers, and a first-size gap certificate provide structural information rather than isolated numeric examples. The static dimension stabilizes at \(2r\) while adaptive dimension grows as \(r\log_2 n\), so the distinction persists with a constant layer count and has an unbounded magnitude.

## Limits

The full general Littlestone dimension remains open in this document. Results concern precisely \(r+1\) nonempty decreasing layers and binary positional comparisons, not all layered permutations or another pattern-VC definition. Source inspection was bounded to identified relevant literature. The verification scripts check finite cases and explicit certificates only.
