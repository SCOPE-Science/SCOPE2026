For permutations with exactly \(r+1\) decreasing layers and pairwise positional-comparison queries, the VC dimension reaches \(2r\) if and only if the permutation length is at least \(4r+1\), while Littlestone dimension grows as \(r\log_2 n+O_r(1)\) for each fixed \(r\). For three layers the exact VC dimension is known at every length, and the first strict adaptive gap occurs at length six, where the dimensions are \(2\) and \(3\).
