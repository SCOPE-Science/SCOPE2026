For complex degree-\(d\) plane curves, the sharp maximum of total rational Betti number at each irreducible-degree partition has an exact, nonnegative local Milnor/intersection defect formula. Its complete finite-degree envelope changes extremizer type at degrees \(3\), \(4\), and \(5\), and for \(d\ge6\) a sharp Betti threshold detects irreducibility.
