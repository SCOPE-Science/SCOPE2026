For the uniform fragile coupon collector with fixed pre-discovery survival probability \(s\in(0,1)\), the number of repeat draws conditional on successful collection converges to the number of parts in a Boltzmann integer partition. Its total-variation error has the exact positive first-order coefficient \(C_s=\frac12\sum_{k\ge0}p_k|\alpha_s-h_k|\), rather than only an unspecified convergence rate.
