On the two-sphere at mixed radial--angular exponent four, the sharp Fourier-extension norm at every prescribed fraction \(b\) of nonconstant spherical-harmonic energy is governed by the exact profile \(1-4b/3+4b^2/5\). Every equality case, and no others, contains only constant and first-degree spherical harmonics; the proof exploits the exact identity \(\int_0^\infty J_{1/2}(r)^2J_{k+1/2}(r)^2\,dr=1/(\pi(2k+1))\).
