For the one-sided axially elongated regular cross-polytope in every dimension \(n\ge3\), both unique John and Löwner ellipsoids have explicit formulas: every facet continues to touch the John ellipsoid, while the Löwner ellipsoid loses precisely the opposite pole at the sharp spike threshold \(a=(n+1)/(n-1)\). The outer log-volume is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) there, with a computable second-derivative jump.
