# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim assumes a nonempty antichain of nonempty contract edges in finite binary-source contextual Horn abduction. Each contract edge is the span of an actual minimal obstruction. Selecting an opposite-context pair for each private coordinate yields binary difference vectors forming an identity minor, so the affine difference dimension of all contexts is at least the private-coordinate rank. The explicit construction uses exact full-assignment guards, one hypothesis atom, and heads only at the manifestation or contradiction; direct evaluation leaves precisely two singleton valid-explanation types. Each minimal obstruction is exactly a center/edge pair, giving the requested contract. With zero hypothesis atoms the only possibility is an empty contract or empty-edge contract. Equality forces chosen independent pairs to be acyclic on \(m+1\) vertices and hence a tree. Disjoint-support tree codes give a valid constructive converse. The finite checker passed, but no infinite claim depends on it.

## Originality — PASS (subject to documented historical access risk)
Yang's original Theorem 2 already gives arbitrary-contract realizability with two hypotheses and at most one more context than edges. It does not assert the one-hypothesis reduction, lower bound, sharpness or witness-tree condition. The 1992 Skowron–Rauszer discernibility literature treats pairwise decision-table distinction; relevant accessible descriptions do not establish this exact joint resource theorem. Primary-source full text was inspected for Yang; the closed 1992 chapter could not be inspected. Public literature comparisons found semantic neighbours but no covering statement. This remains best-knowledge, not a proof of global priority.

## Value — PASS
Exact representation cost is a motivated question because a canonical contract can be realizable with many possible contexts and explanatory choices. Reducing the published two-hypothesis representation to a single necessary atom is a strict optimality result, while the private-coordinate lower bound proves the general context upper bound worst-case sharp for broad, natural antichains including matchings and petals. The witness-tree consequence adds useful structural information about all context-optimal representations of that family.
