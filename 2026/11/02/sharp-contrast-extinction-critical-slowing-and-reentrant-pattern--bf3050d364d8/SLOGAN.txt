In logistic threshold-response networks with nonnegative row-stochastic weights, every trajectory converges to a homogeneous equilibrium whenever \(\beta\delta(W)\le4\), even if the homogeneous scalar equation is bistable; the sharp critical contrast law has universal upper constant \(\limsup tD(t)^2\le3/2\). A symmetric two-node family attains the constant and exhibits distinct sharp onset thresholds for heterogeneous equilibria and for their eventual local attraction.
