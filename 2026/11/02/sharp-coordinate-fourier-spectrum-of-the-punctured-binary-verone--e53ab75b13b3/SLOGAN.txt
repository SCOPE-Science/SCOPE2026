For the binary symmetric rank-one Veronese cone with the zero matrix omitted, the constant and every diagonal/off-diagonal coordinate Walsh character have a complete three-sector Gram spectrum; both sharp extremal eigenvalues always lie in one explicit three-dimensional block. The probability-normalized squared sampling condition number is asymptotic to \(d^4/16\), even though the intrinsic degree-two Walsh band on the same points becomes asymptotically tight.
