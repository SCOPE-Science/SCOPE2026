For a one-dimensional Krylov space, selecting the \(s\) most informative coordinates of its basis can completely erase the sampled matrix action at the sharp SPD condition-number threshold \((1+\sqrt{s/n})/(1-\sqrt{s/n})\). Arbitrarily close to this threshold, the exact sketched GMRES residual can exceed the ordinary GMRES residual by an unbounded factor—even as the system condition number approaches one when \(s/n\to0\).
