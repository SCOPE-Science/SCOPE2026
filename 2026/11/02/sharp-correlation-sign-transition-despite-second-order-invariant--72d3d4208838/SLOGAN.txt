For scalar Ornstein–Uhlenbeck dynamics, BDF2–Maruyama approximates the invariant variance more accurately than backward Euler at every positive step size, yet its stationary autocovariances change sign infinitely often exactly when \(\lambda h>1/2\). The full stationary covariance is explicit, and at \(\lambda h=1\) its lag-four covariance is \(-3\sigma^2h/2500\), although the exact OU covariance remains positive.
