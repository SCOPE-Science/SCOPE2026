# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The window is square-integrable for every nonzero finite positive pole measure supported away from the origin. Fourier periodization yields the exact critical Zak multiplier. The positive cross kernel has a rational form and a strictly positive derivative with respect to the cosine of the spatial coordinate. Positive weights also force strict frequency monotonicity. The sharp extrema and their normalizations are therefore established for all admissible measures, not inferred from finite sampling. The condition-number minimizer follows from an exactly optimized scalar rational function and an equality-rigid positive average. The standalone program provides 79024 supplementary tests, but is not used to justify the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority
The closest primary source is Belov, Kulikov and Lyubarskii, *Gabor frames for rational functions* (arXiv:2103.08959v1; Inventiones), which proves the frame set for finite positive Cauchy mixtures. Their later *Gabor frame operator for the Cauchy kernel* (arXiv:2212.08915v1) supplies the one-pole critical bounds, correctly reproduced as a specialization. Neither inspected paper gives the exact critical bounds for all non-atomic positive mixtures, nor the optimal universal ratio and equality classification. The general rational Zak function in the former paper is an important antecedent, and a possible implicit derivation/undiscovered equivalent remains a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Conditioning is central to stable coefficient reconstruction at the critical Gabor density. The result gives exact sharp constants for a motivated positive rational/Herglotz family extended to positive continuous mixtures, and proves that mixing poles cannot improve the sharp ratio \(17+12\sqrt2\). Its unique optimally conditioned pole depth resolves a natural quantitative design question rather than simply re-proving frame existence.

## Boundaries and evidence
The claim is confined to compactly supported positive measures on the positive pole-depth axis and critical rectangular lattices. Signed coefficients, noncritical densities, noise robustness and historical uniqueness are not covered.
