Rectangular rook graphs \(K_m\square K_n\) have exact, separately sharp real \(\ell_1\) cut- and circulation-projection constants, attained by unique symmetry-invariant projections. The orthogonal cut projection is optimal exactly for squares, whereas the orthogonal circulation projection also survives the exceptional rectangles \(K_2\square K_4\) and \(K_4\square K_2\); outside squares, no complementary pair attains both minima.
