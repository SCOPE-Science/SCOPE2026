# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Both infima are over arbitrary real linear projections in the same canonical edge-\(\ell_1\) space. The vertex-stabilizer argument proves uniqueness of equivariant cycle projections without presuming minimality. Averaging over signed edge permutations proves global minimality. The product heat kernel yields a nonnegative coordinatewise edge-gradient decomposition whose absolute sums are \(2(q-1)/q\) in the distinguished coordinate and \((q-1)/q\) in each other coordinate. The constant diagonal follows from rank and edge transitivity. Independent exact-rational reduced-Laplacian computations confirm complete projection matrices in nine finite cases. The finite assertions are not the proof of the unrestricted claim.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual priority risk.** In the primary motivating preprint arXiv:2305.12582, the general Hamming-graph uniqueness theorem is Theorem 43, while Theorem 45 and Corollary 60 give explicit norms only for binary cubes. No explicit nonbinary projection-norm formula was identified in the inspected 35-page primary document. arXiv:1807.03814 provides the older quotient/averaging framework without the present constants. Separate indexed research results on degree-two Hamming-scheme projections and Walsh projections concern different vertex-function or Lipschitz-free spectral operators, not the edge-\(\ell_1\) cut/circulation projections. Searches of available historical claims and published research records did not reveal a dominating result. Older electrical-network terminology may contain an equivalent formula; absence from search results alone is not a uniqueness proof.

## Value
**PASS.** The 2023 source motivates explicit minimal projection norms but computes only the binary Hamming cube. The exact nonbinary two-parameter family closes this natural quantitative gap, determines both complementary constants simultaneously, gives precise dimension growth, and exhibits a strict ordering of cycle and cut costs for every \(d\ge2,q\ge3\). This is not merely an isolated small-parameter computation.

## Scientific boundaries
Unweighted finite Hamming graphs, real edge-\(\ell_1\) relative projection constants only. No classification of all minimizers, no unequal alphabets, no independent validation or comprehensive historical-priority certificate.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
