For every nonbinary Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\), \(d\ge2,q\ge3\), the least norm of a real edge-\(\ell_1\) projection onto its cut space is exactly \((d+1)(q-1)/q\), and the least norm of a projection onto its circulation space is exactly \(1+(d+1)(q-1)/q-4(q^d-1)/(d q^d(q-1))\). The complementary orthogonal projections attain both optima, yielding sharp linear-in-dimension growth with alphabet-dependent slope.
