For the classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta end-step on a rate-one directed Markov cycle, the exact positivity horizon is \(1.39264678\ldots\) for two states, \(1.49337737\ldots\) for three states, and \(1\) for every larger cycle. Three states are minimal for a mass-preserving RK4 step that contracts all nonstationary spectral modes yet assigns a negative transition probability: at \(h=3/2\) its diagonal is \(-1/128\) and its nonstationary spectral radius is \(79/128\).
