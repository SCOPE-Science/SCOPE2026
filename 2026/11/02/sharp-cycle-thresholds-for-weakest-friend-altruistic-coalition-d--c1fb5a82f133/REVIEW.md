# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In a unilateral move all affected valuations of the mover's friends change via their pair with the mover, by +n or -n per affected pair. Thus the changes in the friends' sum and own value are n times the change in friend count and that quantity minus the change in enemy count, respectively. The frozen symmetric within-block pair potentials have exactly the three utility increments. For min-EQ an independent set-of-blocks partition enumerator and topological acyclicity test exhaust all 75 labeled graphs of orders 1–4, with all moves to other blocks or a singleton. It reproduced the 3068 improving mover-labeled transitions, all potential comparisons in those orders, and the cyclic counts (0,0,0,16), split 12 of type (0,1,1,2) and 4 of type (0,2,2,2). These degree types uniquely force the stated graphs. The explicit order-four path-plus-isolate cycle checks directly. In the connected family, the core personal valuation vectors in the four states A,B,C,D are (-2,0,n-1,n-1,0), (-1,-1,n,n,0), (-2,n-1,0,n-1,0), and (-1,n,-1,n,0). The friend-inclusive minima therefore give -2 to -1 for each move by 0, and -1 to 0 for each move by 3. Extra leaves are singletons and do not change those comparisons. This proves the unbounded family and, with the exhaustive small-order result, the sharp connected threshold.
