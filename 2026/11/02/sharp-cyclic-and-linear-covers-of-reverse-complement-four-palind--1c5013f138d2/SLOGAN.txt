All \(q^2\) four-letter reverse-complement palindromes over a fixed-point-free complementary alphabet of even size \(q\) fit in a shortest circular word of length \((5q^2-q)/2\), or a shortest linear word of length one more. The proof pairs targets by their unique strongest overlaps and stitches these pairs by an Euler tour; for DNA, the exact minima are \(38\) cyclic and \(39\) linear symbols.
