The minimum length of a circular \(q\)-letter word containing every palindrome \(abba\) is \((5q^2+q)/2\) for odd \(q\), and \((5q^2+2q)/2\) for even \(q\), for all \(q\ge2\). Every optimum has rigid length-two center runs and length-four constant runs, while the underlying run-transition multiplicities obey a sharp perfect-matching parity law.
