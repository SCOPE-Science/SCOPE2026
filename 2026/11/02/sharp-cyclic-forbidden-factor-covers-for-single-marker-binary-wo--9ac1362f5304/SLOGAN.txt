For a binary circular word that must contain every length-\(n\) factor except the single forbidden one-marker word \(0^i1 0^{n-1-i}\), the exact minimum length is impossible at the two end positions, \(2^n-1+(n+1)/2\) for the odd-order central marker, and \(2^n+n-1\) everywhere else. Every shortest solution has the same set of duplicated factors, determined by one unique shortest repair path in the forbidden-edge de Bruijn graph.
