For elementary Rule 110 on an indexed binary ring, every output with \(m\) ones has at most \(P_m\) one-step predecessors, where \(P_m\) is the Perrin sequence. For every ring length \(n\geq5\), the unique output with the maximum \(P_n\) predecessors is the all-one configuration; every output with exactly one one is unreachable in one step.
