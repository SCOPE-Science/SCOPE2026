For every indexed cyclic binary ring with \(n\ge8\), elementary cellular automaton Rule 54 has a unique most frequent one-step output, \(1^n\), with Perrin-number preimage count \(R_n\). The sharp runner-up count is the Padovan number \(P_{n-2}\), attained exactly by the \(n\) rotations of \(00 1^{n-2}\).
