# Sharp cyclic symbol-pair distance of the extended binary Golay code under coordinate permutations

## Finding

Let \(G_{24}\) be the binary extended Golay \([24,12,8]\) code. For any ordering \(p=(p_0,\ldots,p_{23})\) of its labeled coordinates, read each codeword through the 24 *overlapping cyclic adjacent pairs* \((c_{p_i},c_{p_{i+1}})\), with indices modulo 24. Write \(d_{\mathrm{pair}}(p)\) for the resulting minimum nonzero pair weight. The exact ordering optimum is

\[
\boxed{\max_{p\in S_{24}}d_{\mathrm{pair}}(p)=12.}
\]

Here is an explicit ordering attaining the optimum, with coordinates numbered 0 through 23:

```text
(12,7,20,15,9,14,19,11,2,3,8,23,6,18,16,21,1,0,22,10,17,4,13,5)
```

The result is a global optimum over every coordinate permutation, not merely a calculation of the pair distance in a preselected cyclic presentation.

## Assumptions and scope

The binary cyclic \([23,12,7]\) code is generated by the degree-11 polynomial

\[
g(x)=1+x+x^5+x^6+x^7+x^9+x^{11}\in\mathbb F_2[x].
\]

This is the reciprocal of the standard polynomial \(1+x^2+x^4+x^5+x^6+x^{10}+x^{11}\) displayed in the GUAVA Golay-code documentation; reversing the 23 cyclic coordinates gives the same equivalence class. For each binary message polynomial \(m(x)\) of degree less than 12, take \(g(x)m(x)\), identify its coefficients at positions 0 through 22, and append the overall parity bit in position 23. No reduction modulo \(x^{23}-1\) is necessary because the product has degree at most 22. These 4096 words form the binary extended Golay code, up to a fixed coordinate relabeling.

For an arbitrary support \(S\) in a cyclic ordering, define \(e_p(S)\) as the number of cycle edges whose two endpoints both lie in \(S\). For an arbitrary binary word \(c\) with support \(S\), inclusion-exclusion gives

\[
\operatorname{wt}_{\mathrm{pair},p}(c)=2|S|-e_p(S).
\]

The known Hamming weight distribution of \(G_{24}\) is \(1+759z^8+2576z^{12}+759z^{16}+z^{24}\); its 759 weight-eight supports (octads) form a Steiner \(S(5,8,24)\) system. Those properties can also be checked exhaustively from the generator in the accompanying verifier.

## Proof

**Universal upper bound.** Fix any permutation \(p\). Its first five consecutive coordinates are a five-element subset of the 24 labels. The Steiner property says that this subset occurs in exactly one octad \(S\). The four edges joining those five consecutive vertices are internal to \(S\); hence \(e_p(S)\ge4\). The weight-eight Golay word supported on \(S\) therefore satisfies

\[
\operatorname{wt}_{\mathrm{pair},p}(1_S)=16-e_p(S)\le12.
\]

Since \(p\) was arbitrary, \(d_{\mathrm{pair}}(p)\le12\) for every coordinate order. This is a genuine all-permutations upper bound, independent of the witness search.

**Attaining construction.** Use the explicit permutation above. For each of the 759 octads \(S\), directly count \(e_p(S)\) using only the 24 consecutive edges of this permutation. The exhaustive integer tallies are

| \(e_p(S)\) | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Number of octads | 30 | 118 | 240 | 234 | 137 |

The counts total 759, and no octad has more than four internal edges. Consequently every weight-eight word has pair weight at least \(16-4=12\), with 137 attaining equality.

For every proper, nonempty cyclic support, there is at least one run of ones, and so the pair weight is \(|S|\) plus the number of runs of ones. Every Golay word of Hamming weight 12 or 16 therefore has pair weight at least 13 or 17, respectively; the all-ones word has pair weight 24. Thus every nonzero codeword has pair weight at least 12 in the displayed order. Together with the universal upper bound, the optimum equals 12.

## Verification

The accompanying standalone `VERIFY.py` uses only Python's standard library and a seven-term integer representation of the cyclic generator. It enumerates all \(2^{12}=4096\) codewords, checks the exact Hamming weight spectrum, confirms that the 759 octads contain all \(\binom{24}{5}=42504\) five-element subsets exactly once, directly enumerates the induced-cycle-edge counts, and independently measures pair weights by examining all 24 adjacent ordered pairs.

Its exact complete pair-weight spectrum in the attaining coordinate order is

| Pair weight | Count | Pair weight | Count |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 12 | 137 |
| 13 | 236 | 14 | 246 |
| 15 | 150 | 16 | 152 |
| 17 | 480 | 18 | 884 |
| 19 | 644 | 20 | 479 |
| 21 | 292 | 22 | 246 |

These entries sum to 4096. Running `python VERIFY.py` from the directory containing the extracted file returns `VERIFY_OK`. The finite enumeration certifies the explicitly identified finite Golay code; the universal upper bound is proved separately by the design property rather than by enumerating permutations.

## Relationship to prior work

The binary Golay code, its octads, and the Witt \(S(5,8,24)\) design are classical; none of their existence or parameters are claimed as new. Chapman surveys those constructions. Cassuto and Blaum introduce overlapping symbol-pair read channels and their distance; Yaakobi, Bruck and Siegel study bounds for ordinary *cyclic* codes in that metric. Chen studies coordinate permutations in connection with covering bounds in the \(b\)-symbol metric. None of these inspected statements determines the maximal minimum cyclic pair distance of the *extended* Golay code over every coordinate permutation, or supplies the explicit order above. Their generic bounds do not imply this exact optimum because the extended 24-coordinate code is not cyclic in general, and optimal coordinate order is an additional variable.

## Limitations

This is an exact 24-coordinate result, not an asserted formula for other perfect codes, larger extended codes, higher read-window widths, or other channels. The witness uses a particular labeled generator and parity-bit convention, so an equivalent Golay realization requires applying the corresponding coordinate relabeling. The exact number of optimal coordinate orders and their orbits is not determined. The priority comparison is based on inspectable sources and research searches, and does not exclude an unindexed earlier equivalent result. No independent external audit is claimed.
