On directed trees with a unique maximum in-degree \(\Delta\), choosing that vertex for Friedkin–Johnsen opinion minimization loses a sharp factor of order \(1\), \(\log n\), or \(\,n^{\log_{\Delta-1}((\Delta-1)/2)}\), depending on whether \(\Delta=2\), \(\Delta=3\), or \(\Delta\ge4\). Exact optimal intervention on the encompassing class of directed functional networks is computable in linear arithmetic time without constructing a dense inverse.
