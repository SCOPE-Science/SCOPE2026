On finite Kripke frames with outdegree at most two, the McKinsey axiom holds exactly when every world sees an outdegree-one world. This sharp criterion gives an exact labelled-frame census and the asymptotic \(\log N_n=2n\log n-n\log\log n-n+O(n\log\log n/\log n)\); a six-world degree-three example shows the boundary cannot be raised.
