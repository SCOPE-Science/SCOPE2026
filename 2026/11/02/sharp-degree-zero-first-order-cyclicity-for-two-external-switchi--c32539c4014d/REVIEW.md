# Scientific review: sharp degree-zero cyclicity

## Correctness — PASS
For the three-zone linear-center perturbation, the radial return map is smooth in the initial radius on compact transverse-crossing subannuli. Integrating the exact first-order radial forcing over the two circular arcs gives the displayed square-root Melnikov functions, with no contribution from the common exterior constant. The quotient of the two positive arc functions has squared derivative numerator \(-2(t^2+5t-34)\); this has exactly one root for \(1<t<9\), yielding the complete zero-count classification. The exact illustrative zero equation factors as \(-(t-8)(3t-4)/4\), with nonzero radial derivatives of opposite signs. The standard implicit-function theorem gives the two claimed hyperbolic limit cycles for small positive perturbations. The provided checker reconstructed the relevant identities with rational arithmetic and compared angular integrals independently. No finite computation is used to assert an infinite zero count.

## Originality — PASS (within inspected evidence)
The October 6, 2026 source, arXiv:2610.08725, studies the same two external circles and proves high-degree lower bounds using Melnikov functions, but does not state or imply a sharp complete first-order classification in the constant-polynomial stratum by its numerical counts. The earlier arXiv:2509.01377 treats externally and internally tangent circles and parallel lines; arXiv:2604.26061 studies one circular interface; 2013 two-zone straight-line results have incompatible switching geometry. The exact polynomial stratum, coefficient-ratio threshold, and attracting/repelling pair are not among the claims inspected. A general Melnikov theorem provides the bifurcation mechanism but not the sharp zero count; the work does not claim that mechanism as new.

## Value — PASS
The degree-zero polynomials form the minimal nontrivial holomorphic perturbation class, so its complete first-order cyclicity is a mathematically natural counterpart to the motivating degree-four constructions. Two interfaces create a sharp two-cycle mechanism absent from the one-interface arc contribution, and the exact critical ratio separates existence from nonexistence of first-order crossing cycles. The result supplies both a complete classification within its stated regime and an explicit stability pairing, not an arbitrary finite parameter census.
