For every \(b\ge1\) and deletion budget \(r\ge0\), the shortest binary word whose every \(r\)-deletion descendant still contains the scattered subsequence \(0\,1^b\,0\) has exactly \(b+2+2r+\min\{r,b-1\}\) symbols. This determines the complete maximum deletion-resilience profile at each word length and a sharp transition when \(r=b-1\).
