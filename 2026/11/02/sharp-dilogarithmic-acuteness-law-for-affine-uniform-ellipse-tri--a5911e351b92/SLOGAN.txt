Under independent uniform eccentric-anomaly sampling on an ellipse, the probability that three points form an acute Euclidean triangle is exactly \(1/4-3\operatorname{Li}_2(((a^2-b^2)/(a^2+b^2))^2)/(2\pi^2)\). It decreases strictly from the circular value \(1/4\) to zero as the axis ratio diverges, with a sharp logarithmic slender-ellipse asymptotic.
