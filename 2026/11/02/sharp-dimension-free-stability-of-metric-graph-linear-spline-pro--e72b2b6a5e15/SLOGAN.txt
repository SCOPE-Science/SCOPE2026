The real \(L^2\) orthogonal projector onto continuous edgewise-affine splines on any finite positively weighted metric graph has \(L^\infty\) norm strictly below \(3\). The universal constant \(3\) is sharp even on unit-edge hypercubes without pendant vertices, where the exact rational norms have deficit \(6/d+O(d^{-2})\) in dimension \(d\).
