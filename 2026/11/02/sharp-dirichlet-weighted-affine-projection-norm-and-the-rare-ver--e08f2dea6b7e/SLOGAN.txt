For affine least-squares projection on a Dirichlet-weighted simplex, the exact endpoint norm depends only on the total concentration and the smallest coordinate parameter; precisely the vertices with that smallest parameter maximize its kernel row norm. Equal parameters uniquely minimize amplification at fixed total concentration, while concentrating the total mass in other coordinates approaches the sharp envelope \(1+2A\); symmetric high-dimensional profiles exhibit distinct linear-growth and bounded-amplification regimes.
