On every finite connected unit-edge graph, the nonconstant extreme points of the real bounded-Lipschitz **sum-norm** unit ball have exactly the possible Lipschitz slopes \(2/(k+2)\) for \(1\le k\le\Delta\), and their values lie on a corresponding parity grid. This yields an exact finite grid-maximization formula for arbitrary signed Dudley dual norms and an \(O(n^3)\) exact dynamic program on paths.
