# Mathematical assessment

**Correctness: PASS.** The theorem states precisely the one-dimensional D1Q3 model, positive interior relaxation interval, nonzero subcharacteristic Courant number, uniform Fourier-power stability, and a cap on the two *zero-frequency* nonconserved multipliers. The published stability boundary is reduced by an exact rational change of variables to \(8a^2b+4ab^2-a-b\ge0\). Writing \(p=ab\) gives a strict universal bound and a constrained quadratic inequality. The two explicit equality constructions satisfy every parameter bound and the published stability inequality, including at the crossover. The cubic logarithmic phase coefficient follows by expansion of the independently published physical eigenvalue. The finite numerical and rational tests are corroboration, not infinite proof. The limiting constant is an infimum, not an attained admissible parameter.

**Originality: PASS, subject to historical-priority limitations.** The 2026 motivating paper already gives the complete stability region, the third-order coefficient, and the qualitative impossibility of zero cubic dispersion under stability. Those statements are credited and not claimed as new. The additional precise constrained optimization, two-branch closed form, extremizer uniqueness, kinetic-damping crossover, and sharp universal quantitative floor were not stated in the inspected sections. The result is not obtained by simply choosing an already treated parameter value: the global constrained minimization and equality analysis are essential. General older two-relaxation lattice Boltzmann work examines similar accuracy-versus-stability themes, and one closely related 2008 paper's complete text could not be accessed. The historical-priority assessment is therefore provisional, not an independent certification.

**Value: PASS.** Long-wavelength dispersion and decay of nonconserved modes are natural, competing design objectives in a wave-propagation scheme. The theorem yields the exact best achievable dispersive coefficient under any specified cap on the collision relaxation multipliers, identifies when equal relaxation stops being optimal, and quantifies the permanent distance from third-order accuracy even in the marginal-damping limit. It provides a concrete design benchmark without requiring an application-scale solver theorem.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Bellotti–Caetano–Dubois–Graille (arXiv:2610.32810v1), §3, Theorem 16, equation (23), fully covers the *inputs* of the optimization but does not present the exact constrained envelope or unique optimizing designs. Servan-Camas–Tsai (2008) discusses two-relaxation advection–diffusion accuracy and stability, although its full text was not obtained. Ginzburg–d'Humières (2009) studies magic collision factors mainly in steady flow and boundaries; Lallemand–Luo (2000) studies more general dispersion. The bound on kinetic damping applies only at zero Fourier frequency, and the cubic coefficient does not imply a global solution-error optimum. These restrictions are part of the proved claim, not post-hoc excuses.
