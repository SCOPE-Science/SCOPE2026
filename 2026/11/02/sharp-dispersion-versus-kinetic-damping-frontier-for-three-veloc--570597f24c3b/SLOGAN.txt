In the stable three-velocity Lax–Wendroff lattice Boltzmann family, the least possible cubic phase error under a bound on the zero-frequency kinetic relaxation factors follows an explicit, attained, two-branch curve with a sharp crossover at \(q=5-2\sqrt6\). Removing the bound leaves a strict universal dispersion floor \(|c|(1-c^2)/24\), approached only as kinetic damping degenerates.
