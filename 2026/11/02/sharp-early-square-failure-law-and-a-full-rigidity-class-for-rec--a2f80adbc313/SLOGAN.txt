For reciprocal-sum sequences with square parameter \(h^2\), the simultaneous occurrence of nonsquare expressions at terms \(2\) and \(3\) has an exact prime-factor product distribution, determined by roots of \(t^2+1\) and \(t^4+3t^2+1\) modulo primes dividing \(h\). Moreover, no term after the second can ever fail for any starting value exactly when \(h\) has no prime factor equal to \(5\) or congruent to \(1,9\pmod{20}\), an infinite family with at most one failure even when the published general bound grows without limit.
