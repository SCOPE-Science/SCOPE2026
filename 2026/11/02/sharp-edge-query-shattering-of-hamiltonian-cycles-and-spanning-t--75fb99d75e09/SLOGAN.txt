Hamiltonian cycles and spanning two-factors on \(n\ge5\) labeled vertices both have edge-query VC dimension exactly \(n-2\), already witnessed by the edges of a path on \(n-1\) vertices. For \(3\le n\le7\) their exact Littlestone dimensions are \(0,1,3,5,6\), so the first strict adaptive-versus-static gap occurs at six vertices; the two families have distinct intermediate shattering profiles but identical dimensions through seven vertices.
