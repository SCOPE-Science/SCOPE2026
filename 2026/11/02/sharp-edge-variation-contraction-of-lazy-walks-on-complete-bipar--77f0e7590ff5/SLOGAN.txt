For every complete bipartite graph with both sides of size at least two, the sharp graph-edge-variation norm of a one-step lazy random walk depends **only** on the larger side size \(M\): it is \(\max\{1-2\alpha,1-2(1-\alpha)/M\}\). The uniquely optimal holding probability is \(1/(M+1)\), with sharp minimum \((M-1)/(M+1)\); a bipartition cut and a singleton give the competing extremal mechanisms.
