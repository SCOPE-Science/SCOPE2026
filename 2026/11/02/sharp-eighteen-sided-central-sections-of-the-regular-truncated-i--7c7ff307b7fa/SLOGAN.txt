A generic central plane section of the regular truncated icosahedron has at most eighteen sides, and the maximum occurs exactly when six pentagonal and twelve hexagonal faces are crossed. Its sharp Haar probability is \(\frac5\pi\left[2\pi-3\arccos\frac{88-\sqrt5}{109}-3\arccos\frac{25-4\sqrt5}{109}\right]\approx0.0300246408\), with the favorable orientations forming twenty congruent spherical hexagons.
