For every integer \(n\ge3\), the shortest-path metric on the categorical product \(K_n\times K_n\) has exact real \(\ell_1\) distortion \(2(n-2)/(n-1)\), increasing from one to a limit of two. Every optimally rescaled embedding has a uniquely determined three-class distance profile, and every nonzero supporting cut is the incidence matrix of a nontrivial regular bipartite graph.
