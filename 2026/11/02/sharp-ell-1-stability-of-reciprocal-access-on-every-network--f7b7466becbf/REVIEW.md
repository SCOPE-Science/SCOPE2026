# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. For every symmetric edge, the difference of its minimum contribution along a segment is a convex combination of endpoint collection differences. When these expressions enter both endpoint totals with equal weights, the associated matrix has diagonal entries exactly one larger than its column off-diagonal sums; a direct reverse-triangle calculation yields the sharp \(\ell_1\) expansion. A sorted residual-target elimination independently establishes surjectivity without presuming a fixed ordering of target coordinates, and the strictly single-peaked capped payoff equates Nash equilibrium with the access equation. Exact-rational verification checks the formula and algorithm on weighted, disconnected, and irregular graphs.

## Originality

PASS under the primary-literature and public-record comparison, with residual priority uncertainty. The closest source is Hakim, Papadimitriou, and Yannakakis (2026), Section 6 and Appendix C, which supply the unweighted graph contract and a general equilibrium construction; Appendix A.1 supplies an inverse formula only for complete interaction. They do not state sharp arbitrary-network inverse \(\ell_1\) nonexpansivity or its heterogeneous capped-equilibrium stability consequence. Earlier data-exchange models examined by abstract use different allocation/stability objectives. Prior public-record searches and accessible prior claims did not produce a covering theorem; an unindexed equivalent nonlinear-map result is still possible.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Perturbation stability is a substantive question in data-sharing networks precisely because restricting eligible exchange edges destroys the order-theoretic supermodularity used for complete networks. The exact unit total-adjustment bound, valid for all graph sizes and symmetric interaction weights, gives a strong robustness guarantee and a unique prediction for natural saturated-value agents. Connected stars exhibit unbounded coordinatewise amplification, so the special \(\ell_1\) geometry is essential and not a generic nonexpansivity statement.

## Closest literature and limitations

Hakim et al., arXiv:2602.11417, Sections 2 and 6 and Appendices A.1 and C, is the direct model predecessor. The theorem does not extend as stated to asymmetric sharing, unrestricted benefit functions, or integer actions. Independent mathematical assessment and unindexed priority checks remain outstanding.
