# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

At a residual minimum ratio z, each remaining residual target is at least its residual denominator times z. Removing that vertex subtracts w_ij z from each neighboring target and w_ij from its denominator; the inequality survives and all denominators remain at least one. This proves the assigned levels are nondecreasing, so each selected equation accounts for earlier neighbors at their fixed levels and later neighbors at its own level. Ties and zero targets are allowed. For a pair of profiles, the minimum on each edge along the segment has slopes from the two endpoint changes, yielding the asserted convex combination (if the two affine lines coincide, their slopes are equal and either assignment is valid). Each shared coefficient adds the same amount to a column's diagonal and its off-diagonal sum, so the reverse triangle inequality leaves one unit of expansion in every column. This gives injectivity and the inverse bound. On any nonempty fixed graph, increasing one strictly locally maximal positive coordinate changes no incident minimum, so sharpness is attained. The empty graph, if included, has a singleton domain and the bound but no nontrivial equality pair. At fixed neighbors, g_i is continuous and strictly increasing with increments at least the own increment. Below target the gain is strictly positive since r_i>c_i, and on/above saturation the cost makes further collection strictly worse. Thus the unique best response hits its target and all Nash profiles solve the bijective access equation. An independent enumeration of order-consistent triangular systems on a rational weighted, disconnected graph gave unique exact solutions for 82 targets including ties and boundary zeros, and checked 3321 target-pair inequalities and an equality pair. The universal result follows from the analytic proof.

## originality

PASS

The supported distinct contribution is the structural cancellation for arbitrary symmetric weighted reciprocal-min secants and the resulting sharp global inverse and equilibrium sensitivity guarantee. The reciprocal-min contract, its unweighted equilibrium construction and the matrix bound are prior. The unweighted capped existence/positive-target inversion is already a specialization of Hakim–Papadimitriou–Yannakakis and is not an originality basis; their graph algorithm is the direct predecessor of the residual elimination.
