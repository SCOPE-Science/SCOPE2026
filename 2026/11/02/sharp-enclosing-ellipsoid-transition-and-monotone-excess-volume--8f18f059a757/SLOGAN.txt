For Euclidean ball caps in every dimension at least two, the ratio of the least-volume enclosing ellipsoid to the cap volume increases strictly with truncation height; its thin-cap, fixed-positive-height high-dimensional, and Gaussian-window limits are explicit. The enclosing ellipsoid's all-height formula and its -1/d transition were already established by Todd (1982) and are used as a credited input, not claimed as new.
