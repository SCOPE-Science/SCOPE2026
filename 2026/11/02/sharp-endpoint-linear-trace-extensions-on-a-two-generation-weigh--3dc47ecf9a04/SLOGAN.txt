For the first branching binary-tree Sobolev trace problem at the total-variation endpoint, the sharp norm of a real-linear leaf extension has three exact phases: \(1+\rho/2\), \((4-\rho)/(\rho^2-3\rho+4)\), and \(3/2\). Its unique worst weight ratio is \(\rho=4-2\sqrt2\), with optimal amplification \((5+4\sqrt2)/7\); above the second transition, both internal children ignore their separate sibling averages.
