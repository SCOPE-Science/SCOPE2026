The maximum-norm amplification of the symmetrized electrical-gradient projection on every anisotropically weighted Cartesian product of complete graphs has an exact one-line edge-direction formula. Over all positive axis conductances, its sharp maximum is attained only when all coordinate Laplacian rates \(q_jc_j\) agree; in the uniform \(q\)-ary case the norm is exactly \((q-1)(d+1)/q\).
