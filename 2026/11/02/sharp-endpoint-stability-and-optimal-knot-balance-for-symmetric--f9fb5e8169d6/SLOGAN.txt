The real least-squares projection onto continuous linear splines with symmetric knots \((-1,-\varepsilon,0,\varepsilon,1)\) has an exact two-branch maximum-norm stability law. Its unique optimal knot ratio is \(\varepsilon=(\sqrt{161}-11)/4\), while clustering either the three middle knots or the outer pairs raises the limiting sharp norm to \(7/3\) or \(22/9\), respectively.
