# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem covers exactly stationary full-support binary order-three chains with uniform contiguous triple law. Row and column three-word marginal equations produce the four-parameter four-word table; both directions are checked, including positivity and existence of the stationary history chain. Pairwise reversal differences yield \(\tau=4|J|\); the order-three path factorization proves \(D_n=(n-3)D_4\) for every \(n\ge3\), without an asymptotic approximation. The explicit derivative has the sign of \(2x-h-J\), yielding the unique minimizer and the sharp lower value \(2\tau\operatorname{artanh}(2\tau)\). Divergence toward a positive-parameter boundary and continuity produce the full upper half-line at each nonzero \(\tau\). The independent finite checker tests exact rational word tables and numerical finite-word divergences, but correctness of the infinite statement rests on these analytic steps.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The baseline fact that all stationary binary three-word laws are reversible is explicitly credited to Arola-Fernández and Lacasa, *Irreversibility of symbolic time series* (2023, arXiv:2303.11868); the entropy rate as a sequence of finite-word KL increments is credited to Roldán and Parrondo (2010, arXiv:1004.2831). Sagawa (2026, arXiv:2604.07867) develops general non-Markovian stochastic thermodynamics, not the specific uniform triple stationary binary sharp feasible region. General KL-versus-total-variation convex optimization is classical; no new claim is made for its generic form. The new specific statement is the complete exact frontier, attainability and rigidity for a positive order-three binary de Bruijn flow. Targeted literature and mathematical-record searches did not surface a direct stronger theorem, but this is not proof of global uniqueness. Published special cases and source implications were compared, rather than treated as novelty on matching wording alone.

## Value — PASS

A natural experimental obstacle is local indistinguishability: all one-, two- and three-bit contiguous statistics are exactly iid-fair even though the full process can be irreversible. For this first detectable finite-memory regime, the result supplies a physically interpretable and **sharp** minimum irreversible information cost as a function of a directly measurable four-frame arrow, as well as every attainable excess cost. The exact full-range nonlinear law and its rigid optimal structure go beyond mere detection or an isolated numerical example, and make sense as an observable lower-information benchmark for stochastic time series. This does not claim the observational rate equals all microscopic physical dissipation.
