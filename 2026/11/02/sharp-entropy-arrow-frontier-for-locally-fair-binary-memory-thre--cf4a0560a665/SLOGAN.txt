For stationary positive binary memory-three processes with every consecutive triple exactly uniform, the four-word arrow-of-time total variation \(\tau\) and full observed entropy-production rate obey the sharp complete frontier \(\sigma\ge2\tau\operatorname{artanh}(2\tau)\) for \(0<\tau<1/2\), with the isolated reversible endpoint \(\tau=\sigma=0\). Every point above this positive-arrow frontier is attainable, although every observation window of length at most three is indistinguishable from fair independent bits.
