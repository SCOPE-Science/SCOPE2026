For a contracting-origin tent composition, topological entropy follows an exact increasing-slope law, develops a full \(\log2\) plateau, and then collapses discontinuously to zero at an explicit parameter. On the strict plateau \(p_*<p<p_c\), almost every orbit converges to the attracting origin, while a zero-measure Cantor repeller retains the full two-symbol shift and an exact geometric escape law; at the left endpoint \(p=p_*\), a positive-measure invariant core remains.
