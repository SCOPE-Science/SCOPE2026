For equal-singular-value rank-one summands, the optimal Schatten quasi-reverse Minkowski factor at every \(1<p<2\) is attained at a unique pair of nontrivial input/output overlaps determined by one hyperbolic equation. Near the trace norm, its sharp deficit from one is \(\kappa(p-1)^2-\kappa(p-1)^3+O((p-1)^4)\), where \(\kappa=(u_0^2-1)/2\) and \(u_0\tanh u_0=1\); the unrestricted sharp constant is not determined.
