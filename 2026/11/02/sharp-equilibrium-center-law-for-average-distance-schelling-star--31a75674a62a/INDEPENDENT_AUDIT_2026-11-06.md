# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the fully occupied star with strict bilateral improvement and real types, a hub i has cost D_i/(n-1), leaf j has cost |t_i-t_j|, and after swap these costs become the reverse choices; hence iff swap witness D_j<(n-1)|t_i-t_j|<D_i. Leaf–leaf swaps never improve both. On equal grids, D_c is quadratic and the strict swap interval reduces to integer x in (L_{n,a},U_{n,a}); continuum absolute deviations F(u)=u²-u+1/2 yield stable hubs between (sqrt3-1)/2 and (3-sqrt3)/2. Compact uniform convergence of empirical absolute deviation functions, strict-margin witnesses and boundary trimming transfer this to all weakly-uniform type profiles; the simple nonzero boundary derivative gives K_n=(2-sqrt3)n+O(1). I independently enumerated center stability for n=10,50,100,300,1000 and the five-type disconnected example; stable indices proportions approach 2-sqrt3, and [0,1,4,5,6] has stable indices [0,2,3,4].

## originality

PASS

Bilò et al. (2023) introduced and analyze exactly continuous-type average-distance Schelling swap games, mainly existence, potential and welfare on graph classes; the IJCAI full accessible HTML sections do not give the complete stable hub-index set or asymptotic (2-sqrt3) placement fraction on stars. An existence guarantee, median equilibrium or price of anarchy does not determine the equilibrium census or conditional welfare law. Discrete-type Schelling-star results have different payoffs. No matching implication was found in indexed finding queries; hidden appendix result remains risk.
