For self-essential binary anti-coordination on \(K_{a,b}\) with arbitrary nonnegative integer vertex thresholds, the possible numbers of pure equilibria are exactly \(0,1,\ldots,1+\lfloor\min(a,b)/2\rfloor\). A forbidden one-unit threshold gap separates distinct equilibria by at least two zero-action players in each part, and explicit assignments attain every permitted count.
