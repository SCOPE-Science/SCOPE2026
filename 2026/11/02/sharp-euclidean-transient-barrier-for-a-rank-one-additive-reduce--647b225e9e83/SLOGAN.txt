For a two-dimensional SPD system, optimally tuning the scalar complement of an exact one-vector additive reduced-basis inverse cannot prevent worst-orientation Euclidean one-step amplification of exactly \((\kappa-1)/(2\sqrt\kappa)\). The strict-contraction parameter window is explicit, and uniform safety ends at \(\kappa=3+2\sqrt2\), even though sufficiently small complement correction still yields spectral convergence.
