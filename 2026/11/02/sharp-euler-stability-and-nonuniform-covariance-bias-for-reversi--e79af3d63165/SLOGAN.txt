For the two-dimensional reversible Stratonovich Gaussian sampler, Euler--Maruyama has an exact, necessary-and-sufficient mean-square stability ceiling, and its stationary normalized variances are strictly inflated. At critical perturbation scaling the ceiling tends to \((3-\sqrt5)/2\); beyond that scaling it collapses as a power of the small target eigenvalue, while even a vanishing timestep at a fixed fraction of the ceiling leaves a nonzero covariance bias.
