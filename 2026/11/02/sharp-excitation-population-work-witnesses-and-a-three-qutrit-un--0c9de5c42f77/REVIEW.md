# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem concerns the whole symmetric-support state class, not selected numerical examples. The symmetric \(q\)-excitation sector has rank \(q+1\), while the unused \((q-1)\)-excitation degeneracy has rank \(2^{q-1}\binom N{q-1}-q\); the proof checks this for all \(N\ge3\) and every \(2\le q\le N\). Mutually orthogonal isometries extend to a global unitary whose energy compression is exactly the claimed operator. Sharpness follows by explicit two-eigenvalue states. For \(N=2\), the two available first-excitation directions give a sharp two-thirds bound; the maximally mixed symmetric state attains it. The fixed-unitary impossibility uses the one-dimensional ground space, a min-max energy argument and a three-into-two rank obstruction. The standalone finite-dimensional replay verifies normalization, several matrix identities and exact examples; it is not substituted for the all-\(N\) dimension proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The general ergotropy-versus-population work of Wang and Dou (arXiv:2512.21855, 2025-12-26) was read in its accessible public manuscript, including its definitions, Proposition 1 and examples. It contains the ordinary incoherent-ergotropy lower bound rather than the present state-independent V-type sector transfer. An explicit three-cell density operator with passive energy-basis dephasing shows the new positive population witness cannot follow from that bound alone. Andolina et al. (arXiv:1807.08656) treat qubit batteries and asymptotic rank/rearrangement rather than a sharp finite qutrit threshold. Grazi et al. (arXiv:2604.08151) analyze XX-qubit dissipative charging. The closest contemporary two-three-level-cell paper (arXiv:2610.05592) was found and its abstract read, but its complete text was inaccessible; no claim of full-text noncoverage is made. Published mathematical records and related claims were searched for qutrit symmetric-subspace excitation-sector isometries, rank obstructions, and equivalent population-work formulations. No found result stated this theorem; a broader unlocated rank theorem or material in the inaccessible paper remains a real residual risk, and priority is not certified by search alone.

## Value — PASS

The main object is a directly measurable excitation-population tail in a physically natural degenerate three-level battery. The theorem supplies a sharp, state-uniform finite-cell extractable-work guarantee with an explicit unitary, the exact smallest cell number supporting a state-blind discharge, and the obstruction at two cells. The passive-dephasing example shows that it detects useful work not certified by the generic incoherent-ergotropy lower bound. The exact-\(N\) distinction offers a concrete finite-size design criterion for collective quantum battery control, without assuming a thermodynamic or asymptotic limit.

## Limitations and priority risks

Controls are unrestricted global cyclic unitaries; symmetry-preserving, local, or energy-conserving operations are not covered. The two excited levels are exactly degenerate, the state is precisely permutation-symmetric, and no charging kinetics or physical control cost is modeled. The two-cell three-level study arXiv:2610.05592 could not be compared from full manuscript text; broader unlocated symmetric-subspace work-extraction principles may imply portions of the statement. Independent validation remains unperformed.
