For deterministic two-action anti-coordination Q-learning on every finite complete bipartite network, the sharp dynamical exploration threshold is \(T_c=J\sqrt{ab}/2\): above it all log-odds contract exponentially at the optimal rate, while below it two polarized attracting quantal-response equilibria coexist with a symmetric saddle. At the exact threshold, generic interior trajectories slow to a provably sharp \(t^{-1/2}\) law with an explicit unequal-part coefficient.
