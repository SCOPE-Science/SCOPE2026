For synchronous finite-field neighbor-sum dynamics on any rooted tree whose child counts vanish in the field, every configuration becomes zero at the sharp time \(2h+1\) or earlier. Regardless of tree shape, the last two extinction shells have probabilities \(q^{-1}-q^{-2}\) and \(1-q^{-1}\); complete regular trees globally extinguish exactly when the field characteristic divides their branching number.
