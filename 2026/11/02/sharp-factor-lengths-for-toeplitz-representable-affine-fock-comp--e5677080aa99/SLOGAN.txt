Affine Fock composition operators that individually have bounded Toeplitz symbols generate every nonzero strict affine contraction; for \(|\theta|<n\pi/2\), the sharp \(n\)-factor slope boundary in direction \(\theta\) is \(\cos^n(\theta/n)\), with no nonzero slopes reachable in larger angular directions. Saturating that boundary forces every factor to fix the origin, so adjoining a nonzero translation costs exactly one additional factor.
