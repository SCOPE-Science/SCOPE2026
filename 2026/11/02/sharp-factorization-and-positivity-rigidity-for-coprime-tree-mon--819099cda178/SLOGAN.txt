In the natural monomial-pole Farey-tree family, exact factorization into functions of \(x\), \(y\), and \(xy\) occurs only at exponents \(1\) and \(2\). For every higher exponent \(r\), the primitive-pair summands have a sharp positivity cutoff \(\tau_r\), with \(\tau_3=(\sqrt5-1)/2\) and \(\tau_r\downarrow1/2\), even though their total remains a positive rational function.
