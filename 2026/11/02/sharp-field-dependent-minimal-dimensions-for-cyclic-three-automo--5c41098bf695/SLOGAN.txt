The least dimension of an evolution algebra with automorphism group \(C_3\) is \(1\) over \(\mathbb F_4\), is \(2\) exactly when the field has three cube roots of unity and a noncube (excluding \(\mathbb F_4\)), and is \(3\) over all remaining fields. Restricting to perfect algebras removes the one-dimensional exception; the same cubic criterion exactly distinguishes dimensions \(2\) and \(3\).
