For every prime \(p\equiv1\pmod{11}\), a cyclic subgroup of order \(11\) modulo \(p^k\) has no positive vanishing power sum of fewer than eleven terms once \(k\ge5\); the exact sole exceptions at \(k\ge3\) are \(p=23\) with \(k=3,4\), where the minimum is nine. This makes the global stabilization exponent exactly \(5\), improving a general nonsharp cyclotomic-norm cutoff.
