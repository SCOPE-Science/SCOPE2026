In every reversible two-state Markov network, the exact globally sharp ratio of squared indicator-output lag to instantaneous entropy production is determined by a unique log-odds secant–tangent crossing. For asymmetric equilibrium this optimum strictly exceeds the integrated equilibrium memory coefficient, with a quantified \(1/[q\log(1/q)]\) amplification as the rare-state equilibrium mass \(q\) vanishes.
