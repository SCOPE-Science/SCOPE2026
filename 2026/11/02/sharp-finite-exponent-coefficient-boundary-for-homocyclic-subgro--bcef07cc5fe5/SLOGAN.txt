For every even rank \(2s\ge2\), the top \(a+1\) coefficients of the subgroup-count polynomial of \((C_{p^a})^{2s}\) agree exactly with its known infinite-exponent product series, and its first missing coefficient has the universal deficit \(2a+3\). At every odd rank at least three, each fixed top coefficient is eventually affine in the exponent, with an explicit effective threshold.
