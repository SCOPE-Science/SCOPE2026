## correctness

PASS

For all prime powers q, the universal six-dimensional natural-basis embedding of any 3-dimensional commutative algebra is an ideal. In an m-dimensional evolution envelope, pivot-coordinate normalization forces all three mixed products of any field basis into m-3 off-diagonal rank-one patterns p(a,b,c)=(ab,ac,bc). Any pair of mixed field products is independent so m>=5. If m=5, their rank is 2 and the relation plane has a fully nonzero normal; it contains precisely q-2 projective nonzero patterns (derive by scaling a=1, choosing u=b≠0,-r2/r3). q=2,3 cannot contain two independent directions, so m=6. For q>=4, choose 1,t,t/(t-1), with tz=t+z, and independent u,v∈K\{0,1}; two pattern rows and explicit diagonal-square corrections construct five-dimensional ideal envelopes. Independent enumeration of projective plane patterns with normal (1,1,1) returned counts 0,1,3,5 for q=2,3,5,7 respectively. These tests corroborate but do not prove all prime powers.
