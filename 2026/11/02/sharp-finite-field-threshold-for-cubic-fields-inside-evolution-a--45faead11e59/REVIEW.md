# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

Steps 1–4 reconstruct both upper and lower bounds without assumptions on characteristic. In an ambient evolution algebra, a pivot-normalized field basis forces the three mixed products into at most \(m-3\) off-diagonal rank-one directions. Pairwise independence of field products gives \(m\ge5\). For a rank-two mixed-product triple the unique relation has three nonzero coefficients, and its annihilating plane contains exactly \(q-2\) projective rank-one directions; this prohibits dimension five for \(q=2,3\). A universal six-dimensional ideal embedding works in all characteristics. For \(q\ge4\), the explicit basis \(1,t,t/(t-1)\), two distinct scalar parameters and specified natural squares give a five-dimensional ideal envelope. Critical finite-field cases were checked with standalone exact programs. The complete result is proved symbolically; finite enumeration is not treated as infinite proof.

The closest indexed theorem classifies split local algebras with square-zero radical in characteristic not two, rather than field extensions. Search cannot prove global uniqueness; older unindexed tensor-decomposition or evolution-algebra literature remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This determines a natural extremal embedding invariant over **all** finite fields, exhibits the smallest-degree cubic-field witnesses for a published open sharpness question, and explains the exact transition via the number of rank-one directions on a two-plane.
