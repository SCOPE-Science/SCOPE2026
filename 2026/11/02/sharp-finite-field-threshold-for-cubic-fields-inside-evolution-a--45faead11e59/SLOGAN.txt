The cubic field \(\mathbb F_{q^3}\) has a smallest evolution-algebra envelope of dimension \(6\) over \(\mathbb F_q\) precisely for \(q=2,3\), and of dimension \(5\) for every \(q\ge4\); in all cases it embeds as an ideal. The binary and ternary cubic fields therefore realize the published sharp universal \(n(n+1)/2\) envelope bound in dimension \(n=3\).
