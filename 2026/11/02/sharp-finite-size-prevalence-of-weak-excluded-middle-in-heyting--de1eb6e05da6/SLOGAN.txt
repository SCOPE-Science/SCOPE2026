For a finite Heyting algebra with \(n\ge3\) join-irreducibles that fails weak excluded middle, the largest possible fraction of elements satisfying \(\neg a\vee\neg\neg a=\top\) is exactly \((2^{n-2}+1)/(2^{n-2}+3)\). Equality holds precisely for the downset algebra of two incomparable minimal points below \(n-2\) incomparable upper points, and an exact connected-component formula counts all satisfying elements.
