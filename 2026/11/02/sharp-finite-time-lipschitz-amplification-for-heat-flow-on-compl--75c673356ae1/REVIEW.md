Spectral decomposition of the combinatorial Laplacian has within-part eigenvalues q,p and part-mean eigenvalue p+q. For an edge, the output equals z(f(ai)-f(bj))+u(f(ai)-a_-i)+v(b_-j-f(bj)) with z=e^{-(p+q)t}, u=(p-1)/p(e^{-qt}-z), v=(q-1)/q(e^{-pt}-z). Sharp network-Lipschitz upper z+2 max(u,v) attained by explicit 0,+1,-1 potentials; for p<=q, v>=u follows a monotone exponential ratio. Differentiation gives transition n-m=2 and one maximum if n-m>=3. p=q=1 separately yields exp(-2t). Independent scipy matrix exponential + linear program over graph Lipschitz polytope matched formula in 50 p,q,t cases.
