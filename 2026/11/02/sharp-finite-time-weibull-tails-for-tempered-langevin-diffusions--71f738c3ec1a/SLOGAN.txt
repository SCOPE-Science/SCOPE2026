For every subcritical tempered Langevin diffusion with a polynomial-tailed target, the fixed-time radial tail has sharp logarithmic decay constant \(c_\pi^{2\ell}/(4[1-\ell(d+r)]^2t)\) at power \(2[1-\ell(d+r)]\). This resolves the exact strict finiteness-versus-divergence time boundary for the corresponding critical exponential moment, while leaving equality at the boundary open.
