The original exponential-clock coding-tree estimator of the Riccati equation \(y'=y^2\) has a sharp finite-second-moment horizon \(t_2(\lambda)=\lambda^{-1}\log(1+\kappa\lambda^2/a^2)\), where \(\kappa=\log(3+2\sqrt2)/(2\sqrt2)\). Even with the uniquely optimal constant rate, this horizon stops at \(0.6353007524\ldots/a\), well before the solution's finite-mean blow-up time \(1/a\).
