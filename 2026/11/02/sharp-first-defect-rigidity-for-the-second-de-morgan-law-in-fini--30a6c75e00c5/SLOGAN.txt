For a finite Heyting algebra, the number of ordered failures of the second De Morgan law is at least the number of weak-excluded-middle failures, with equality outside the Stone case precisely for posets of the form \(A_2\oplus Q\) with \(Q\ne\varnothing\). Thus every non-Stone algebra of size \(h\ge5\) satisfies the binary law on at most \(1-2/h^2\) of all ordered pairs, sharply for every \(h\), and all equality structures are classified.
