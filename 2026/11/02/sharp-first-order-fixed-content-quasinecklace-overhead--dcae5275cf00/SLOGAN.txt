For binary quasinecklaces with a fixed number \(r\ge3\) of ones, the extra candidate fraction above necklaces is exactly \((r-1)/(2(n-r))+O_r((n-r)^{-2})\). An exact bounded-composition formula counts all candidates, and the first nonzero excess at three ones is \(\lfloor(n-3)/2\rfloor-\lfloor(n-3)/3\rfloor\).
