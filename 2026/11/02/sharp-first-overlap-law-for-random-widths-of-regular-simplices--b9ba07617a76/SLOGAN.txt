For every unit-edge regular simplex in dimension \(d\ge2\), the random directional width has an exact two-regime high-tail law: disjoint spherical caps above \(\sqrt3/2\), and a completely specified pair-overlap correction down to \(\sqrt{2/3}\). The first correction is sharp, with exponent \(d/2\) and coefficient \(4(d^2-1)\pi^{-1}(4/\sqrt3)^{(d-2)/2}\); the full lower-width distribution in dimensions at least three is not claimed.
