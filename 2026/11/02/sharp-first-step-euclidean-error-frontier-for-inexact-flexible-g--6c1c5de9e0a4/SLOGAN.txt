Under the inner residual criterion \(\|v-Az\|_2\le\mu\), the exact first-step Euclidean solution-error amplification of flexible GMRES on SPD systems has a sharp dimension-free profile depending only on \(\mu\) and the condition number \(\kappa\). Universal error nonexpansion holds precisely when \(\mu\le[1+(\kappa-\kappa^{-1})^2]^{-1/2}\), and two-dimensional witnesses attain every point of the sharp profile.
