For any two distinct nonorthogonal lines in the Euclidean plane, one residual-minimizing GMRES step applied to their alternating-projection fixed-point system has a strictly smaller fixed-point residual, but a strictly larger distance to the intersection, than one ordinary projection sweep from the first line. The sharp one-step Euclidean error penalty is \(\sqrt{(119+21\sqrt{21})/200}=1.0373863542\ldots\), even though full GMRES terminates in two steps.
