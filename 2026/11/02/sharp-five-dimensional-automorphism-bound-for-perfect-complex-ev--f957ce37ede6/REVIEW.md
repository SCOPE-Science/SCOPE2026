# Scientific review: sharp dimension-five evolution-algebra automorphism orders

## Correctness

PASS. An invertible structure matrix forces a directed cycle cover. The standard monomial automorphism theorem gives the exact diagonal equations and the graph-permutation upper bound. Every five-vertex graph (loops allowed) is covered by the supplied \(25\)-bit enumerator; each orbit is generated by all \(120\) vertex permutations. Every diagonal solution is encoded by a finite root tuple from the cycle cover; the modular edge test is equivalent to the complex root equation. The exhaustive integer calculation gives a unique five-cycle maximum of \(155\) and a unique two-plus-three-cycle second maximum of \(126\). Exact coefficient normalization over \(\mathbb C\) proves uniqueness of the extremal algebra isomorphism classes. A separate Hermite-normal-form checker crosschecked \(305\) graphs and the unlabeled count by Burnside's lemma. Computation proves a finite dimension-five bound only, not a general conjecture.

## Originality

PASS with priority qualification. Kaygorodov–Martín González–Viruel, arXiv:2610.22248v1, Conjectures 3 and 4, propose the value \(155\) for dimension five and explicitly report computer verification of the graph upper bound only through dimension four. Sriwongsa–Zou, arXiv:2202.01333v2, Theorem 4.1, classify the maximum diagonal subgroup but do not give the needed sharp simultaneous bound on diagonal and permutation parts. Elduque–Labra, arXiv:1902.02191v1, supplies the general exact sequence, not the five-vertex extremal enumeration. Searches for the dimension-five claim, its cycle-graph formulation, graph tables and stronger graph bounds found no prior direct proof. Historical priority outside accessible indexed sources remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The result settles the first dimension not settled in the computational evidence recorded with a current explicit conjecture, gives a sharp stronger runner-up gap, and determines both extremal complex algebra isomorphism classes. It isolates an actual structural tradeoff between diagonal and graph symmetries, not merely a restatement of the known diagonal maximum.

## Limitations

Only perfect five-dimensional complex evolution algebras, ordinary automorphism groups; the exhaustive certificate is finite and should be independently audited before external publication. It does not establish higher-dimensional conjectures.
