For the standard L-stable TR-BDF2 method applied to a unit-rate directed \(n\)-cycle, every step is positivity-preserving if \(2\le n\le4\), whereas every \(n\ge5\) has exactly one bounded interval of forbidden positive timesteps, with strictly positive endpoint matrices outside that interval. The lower unsafe boundary tends to \(1+\sqrt2\), and the final recovery boundary grows as \(3.4529561713\ldots n\); positivity is governed entirely by the diagonal entry of the endpoint matrix.
