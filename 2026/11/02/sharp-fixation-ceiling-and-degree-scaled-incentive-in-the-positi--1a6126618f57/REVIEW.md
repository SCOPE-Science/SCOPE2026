# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The published receiver-local transition rule reduces on a loopless star to exactly 2m transient lumped states. Explicit rational harmonic functions u_k and v_k satisfy the two backward recurrences and absorbing boundaries for all m>=2,r>=1; finite absorption establishes uniqueness. Substituting uniform seeding gives the rational F_m. Degree-weighted mutant mass has strictly positive drift at mixed-leaf states when r>1, proving strict advantage over leaf-only bias. Strong-bias limit differs from 9/16 by negative square -(m-3)^2/[16(m+1)^2]. Degree scaling follows directly from the exact expression. A standalone symbolic checker simplifies all critical equations to zero and independent exact Markov matrices match 28 test cases; no infinite claim rests on finite enumeration.

## Value — PASS

Star graphs isolate the hub-versus-periphery incentive placement problem in a familiar centrality extreme. Rather than a generic computation of a tiny graph, the result is an all-size closed form and sharp optimization across infinitely many sizes and arbitrarily strong bias, with a 9/16 universal ceiling and a phase-like linear resource scale r~m for positive limiting fixation from random single seeds. The result quantifies when localized influence cannot dominate diffusion despite a high-degree hub. No graph-wide or arbitrary network claim is made.

## Limits
