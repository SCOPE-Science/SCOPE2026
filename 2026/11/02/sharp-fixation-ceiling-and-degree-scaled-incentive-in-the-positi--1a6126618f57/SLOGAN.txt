On a loopless star, biasing one leaf never changes positional-voter fixation, while biasing the hub gives an exact rational fixation probability and a sharp universal one-site ceiling of \(9/16\). For large stars, a hub preference growing sublinearly in degree has vanishing success from one uniformly random mutant, whereas degree-proportional preference has limiting fixation probability \(\vartheta/(1+2\vartheta)\).
