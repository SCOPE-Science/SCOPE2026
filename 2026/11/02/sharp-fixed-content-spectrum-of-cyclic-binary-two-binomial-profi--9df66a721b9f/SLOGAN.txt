For binary words of fixed content \(p,q>0\), the number of distinct two-binomial profiles among all cyclic rotations takes **every** value from \((p+q)/\gcd(p,q)\) through \(p+q-\gcd(p,q)+1\). The minimum occurs exactly at maximal-content periodic powers, the maximum is attained by a two-run word, and equality of two rotation profiles is characterized by a population-proportional circular arc.
