For a projectively monitored coherent qubit with a prescribed mean interval between independent measurements, the exact fastest first-detection law has three phases: a zero/finite two-interval mixture, one uniquely optimal deterministic interval, and arbitrary mixtures of perfect Rabi maxima. The sharp phase boundaries are \(\Omega m=x_*\) and \(\Omega m=\pi/2\), with \(\tan x_*=2x_*\); the complete constrained optimum is \(\mathbb ET_{\min}=m/[\beta F(\Omega m)]\) for the explicitly determined three-piece function \(F\).
