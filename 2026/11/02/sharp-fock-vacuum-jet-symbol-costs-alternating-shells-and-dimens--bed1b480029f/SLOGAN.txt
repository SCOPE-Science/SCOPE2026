The least bounded-symbol norm needed to make a Gaussian Fock Toeplitz operator equal the vacuum projector through total degree \(N\) is attained by one and only one symbol: an alternating-sign function on exactly \(N+1\) concentric shells. The optimal cost grows as \(N^d\) in complex dimension \(d\), and smooth compactly supported symbols can approximate—but never attain—the minimum.
