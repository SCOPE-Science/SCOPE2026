# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The scope quantifies over every complete fixed allocation and every parameter vector in the unit cube for at most three goods. Expected-utility equality is necessary and sufficient for ex-ante envy-freeness with equal item-wise means for the two agents. The independent-agent comparison product and three-good exchange identity prove the universal assertion; the bracket is at least one for all admissible parameters. The four-good instance balances expected sums at \(7/10\) and exact rational convolution shows a strict advantage for an ex-ante unequal partition, with the quoted exact fractional probabilities. The standalone Python checker re-derives the values by independent direct enumeration and confirms the global maximizers. Finite tests supplement rather than substitute for the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk
The reviewed 2025–2026 stochastic fair-allocation papers establish different large-market, adaptivity and incentive results. The directly comparable later work *Fair Division of Lotteries* establishes a common convolution-power sufficient condition and general probability bounds, not the sharp four-item boundary for heterogeneous unit Bernoulli parameters. An accessible prior result with two-valued lottery goods of **unequal positive magnitudes** also proves ex-ante/ex-post divergence with four goods, but neither its construction nor its conclusions entail the unit-Bernoulli exchange theorem or this new positive-probability witness. No matching recorded result appeared in searches for aliases, stronger statements, exact tables and same-object formulae. This is best-knowledge originality rather than a certification that no unpublished or unindexed equivalent exists. Aloisio et al. (2024) was compared from available abstract and extended abstract; its full-length technical details were not inspected.

## Value — PASS
The question isolates the least heterogeneous payoff alphabet (realized singleton values zero or one) in precommitted fair allocation under uncertainty. The exact three-versus-four item threshold pinpoints when equal expected treatment ceases to maximize simultaneous ex-post envy-freeness even though agents' distributions remain symmetric. The exchange identity supplies a structural three-good optimizer for arbitrary heterogeneous Bernoulli parameters, rather than an isolated numerical counterexample. The magnitude of the four-good improvement is small, and the theorem does not assert a broad approximation bound or general-market classification.
