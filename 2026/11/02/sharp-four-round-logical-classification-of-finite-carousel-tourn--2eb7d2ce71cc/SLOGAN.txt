The standard odd carousel tournaments have exactly seven first-order quantifier-rank-four theories: orders \(3,5,7,9,11,13\) each stand alone, and every odd order at least \(15\) shares one theory. An antipodal-circle strategy proves the uniform upper cutoff \(2^q-1\) for every quantifier rank \(q\), while an exhaustive finite type calculation makes the four-round boundary sharp.
