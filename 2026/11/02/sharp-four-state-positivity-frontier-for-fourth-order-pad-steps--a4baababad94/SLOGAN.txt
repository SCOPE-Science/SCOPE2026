The fourth-order diagonal Padé step preserves Markov positivity on directed cycles with two or three states at every step length, but the four-state cycle has an exact cutoff at the unique positive root of \(h^4-24h^2-72h-72=0\). For every larger cycle, a group-inverse expansion forces a negative first-neighbor transition for all sufficiently large steps, despite exact mass conservation and positivity of the true semigroup.
