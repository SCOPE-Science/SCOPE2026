Every strict three-player binary game that possesses a pure Nash equilibrium has an improvement path of at most four single-player best responses from every profile, and four steps are sometimes necessary. Among the \(4096\) equally likely labeled best-response orientations, exactly \(3134\) have an equilibrium, their worst-case shortest-path radii have distribution \((258,644,1512,720)\), and exactly \(2492\) are connected in the every-nonsink-to-every-sink sense.
