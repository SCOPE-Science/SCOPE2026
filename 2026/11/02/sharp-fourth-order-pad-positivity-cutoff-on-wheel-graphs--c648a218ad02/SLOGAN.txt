For fourth-order diagonal Padé time-stepping of diffusion on a uniformly weighted wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), positivity at every timestep holds exactly for \(3\le m\le5\); every larger wheel eventually acquires a negative adjacent-rim transition. The first failing wheel, \(K_1\vee C_6\), has an exact positivity horizon given by the unique positive root of an explicit degree-six polynomial.
