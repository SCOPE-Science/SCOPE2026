The complete harmonic Fourier band with frequencies \(-2,-1,0,1,2\) has an exact sharp Poisson \(h^2\)-to-disk \(b^4\) fourth-power constant \(1.349833287676307\ldots\), the value of an explicit rational function at the unique positive root of a quartic. Every complex extremizer is, up to scalar and rotation, a single real reflection-symmetric quadratic harmonic polynomial; the result is confined to this natural five-mode cutoff and does not solve the unrestricted embedding problem.
