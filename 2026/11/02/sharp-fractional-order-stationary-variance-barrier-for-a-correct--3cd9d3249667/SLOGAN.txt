For fractional Ornstein–Uhlenbeck sampling with correlated fractional-Brownian increments, the unique quadratic-bias-cancelling step-dependent theta choice is \(\theta_h=1/2+ah/12\). Its exactly stable Padé propagator still has a sharp negative stationary-variance defect of fractional order \(h^{2H+1}\), with coefficient \(\zeta(-2H)/\Gamma(2H+1)\) after scaling by \((ah)^{2H+1}\).
