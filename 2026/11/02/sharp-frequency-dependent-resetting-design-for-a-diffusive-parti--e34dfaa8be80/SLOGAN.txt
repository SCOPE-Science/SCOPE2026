For Brownian motion with signed partial Poisson resetting \(X\mapsto aX\), the reset multiplier that minimizes stationary position noise at angular frequency \(|\omega|<r\) is uniquely \(a=(1-\sqrt{4-3(\omega/r)^2})/3\); at \(|\omega|=r\) it is \(a=0\), while above the threshold the sharp noise infimum is approached only as \(a\uparrow1\). At zero frequency, \(a=-1/3\) also uniquely minimizes the time-average central-limit variance, improving it by exactly \(5/32\) relative to complete resetting.
