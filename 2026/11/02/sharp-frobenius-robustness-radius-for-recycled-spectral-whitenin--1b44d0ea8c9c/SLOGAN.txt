For a reused exact spectral-whitening preconditioner, the sharp Frobenius perturbation radius guaranteeing spectral condition number at most \(K>1\) is \((K-1)/\sqrt{K^2/\lambda_1^2+1/\lambda_2^2}\). An arbitrarily noncommuting symmetric error cannot be worse than a commuting rank-two error supported on the two smallest eigenmodes; the guarantee is attained exactly at the boundary.
