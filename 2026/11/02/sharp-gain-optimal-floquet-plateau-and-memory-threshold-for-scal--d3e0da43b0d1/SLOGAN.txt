For one-dimensional quadratic RMSProp with epsilon outside the square root, the unique supercritical two-cycle is locally exponentially attracting at every gain, and its optimal two-step Floquet spectral radius is exactly \(\beta\). A sharp memory threshold \(\beta=1/9\) separates a bounded gain-optimal interval from an unbounded one, with an explicit reentrant deterioration in contraction for weak memory.
