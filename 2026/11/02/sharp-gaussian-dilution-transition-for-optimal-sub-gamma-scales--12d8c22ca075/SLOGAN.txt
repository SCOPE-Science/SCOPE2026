For centered Gamma-jump compound-Poisson laws with an independent Gaussian component, the optimal variance-exact right sub-gamma scale stays at the Gamma scale \(\beta\) precisely when the jump shape \(\alpha\le1\); above that threshold, its Gaussian-dilution excess has a sharp fractional-power boundary law. For Erlang-2 jumps the optimizer changes from an interior point to the origin exactly when the jump contribution reaches \(15/16\) of total variance, and the complete optimal scale is explicit.
