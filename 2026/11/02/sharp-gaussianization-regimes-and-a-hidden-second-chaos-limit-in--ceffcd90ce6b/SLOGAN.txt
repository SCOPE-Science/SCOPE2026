For fractional Brownian diffusion with a stationary algebraically correlated diffusivity, the non-Gaussian parameter has a sharp transition at \(H=\eta/4\): its decay changes from \(t^{-4H}\) to \(t^{-\eta}\log t\) and then \(t^{-\eta}\). In the subcritical regime, a positive Gaussian-square diffusivity additionally has a non-Gaussian second-chaos limit for its properly magnified conditional-variance fluctuations, even though the particle position Gaussianizes.
