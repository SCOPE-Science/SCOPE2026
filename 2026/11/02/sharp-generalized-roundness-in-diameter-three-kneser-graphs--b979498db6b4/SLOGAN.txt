For every \(k\ge8\), the shortest-path metric on the diameter-three Kneser graph \(KG(3k-2,k)\) has a sharp negative-type power threshold controlled uniquely by its second Johnson harmonic; at equality the squared-Euclidean realization loses exactly \(\binom{3k-2}{2}-(3k-2)\) dimensions. This threshold decays as \(16\sqrt\pi/(\log(3/2)\,4^k\sqrt{k})\), despite the diameter remaining three.
