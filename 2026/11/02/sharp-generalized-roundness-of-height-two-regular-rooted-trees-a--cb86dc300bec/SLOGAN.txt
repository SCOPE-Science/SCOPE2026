For every unit-edge height-two tree with \(m\ge2\) children and \(\ell\ge2\) leaves per child, the exact generalized-roundness exponent is the unique root of an explicit scalar equation below \(\log_2(2m/(m-1))\), with precisely one critical Euclidean dimension lost. For the complete \(b\)-ary depth-two family, the excess over one satisfies \(b^2(\wp-1)\to1/\log(32/27)\).
