For every fixed finite field of order \(q\ge3\), a uniformly sampled perfect evolution algebra of dimension \(n\) has a nontrivial automorphism group with sharp probability asymptotic \(\binom n2(q-1)^2q^{1-2n}\); almost every exception has exactly one involutory scaled-transposition symmetry. The same coefficient gives the first relative correction to the asymptotic number of isomorphism classes, \(|\operatorname{GL}_n(q)|/[n!(q-1)^n]\).
