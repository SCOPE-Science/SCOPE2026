For the Gaussian exponential moment \(\mathbb E[e^{aZ^2}]\), \(0<a<1/2\), Cauchy-quantile importance sampling followed by a randomly shifted periodic trapezoidal rule has RMS error \(O(e^{-c n^{2/3}})\). The \(2/3\) exponent is sharp for every faster uniform-in-\(n\) stretched-exponential envelope, because the mapped integrand has exact Gevrey order \(3/2\).
