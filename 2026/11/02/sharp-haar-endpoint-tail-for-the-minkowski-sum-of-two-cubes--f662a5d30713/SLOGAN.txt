For two side-2 cubes at Haar-uniform relative rotation, the Minkowski-sum volume lies in \([64,96]\), and its probability of exceeding \(96-\varepsilon\) is asymptotic to \(\varepsilon^{3/2}/(6\sqrt2\pi)\). The sharp coefficient yields the entrywise rotation-matrix norm moment law \(\mathbb E(\sum|R_{ij}|)^k\sim20\sqrt{10/\pi}\,5^k k^{-3/2}\), refining the previously known optimal value \(5\).
