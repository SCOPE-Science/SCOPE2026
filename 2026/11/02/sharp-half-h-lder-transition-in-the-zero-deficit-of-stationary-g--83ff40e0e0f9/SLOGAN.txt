A strictly positive spectral cusp of Hölder order \(\alpha\) gives a finite Gaussian analytic zero deficit, but its approach to that limit has a sharp transition: \((1-r)^{2\alpha}\) below \(\alpha=1/2\), \((1-r)\log(1/(1-r))\) at the threshold, and linear convergence above it. The singular and critical coefficients are explicit and local, while the supercritical coefficient is a global boundary energy.
