On every balanced double-star \(T_m\) with \(m\ge1\) leaves at each hub, the exact graph-Lipschitz heat-semigroup norm is \(\Lambda_m(t)=\big((\beta_m+2m-2)e^{-\alpha_m t}-(\alpha_m+2m-2)e^{-\beta_m t}\big)/(\beta_m-\alpha_m)\), where \(\alpha_m,\beta_m\) are the two roots of \(z^2-(m+3)z+2=0\). The unique extremizing edge-slope pattern at positive times has consistently oriented unit slopes along every leaf-to-hub-to-hub-to-leaf diameter, and for \(m\ge2\) its transient amplification peaks above one and approaches the sharp family-wide supremum \(3\) as \(m\to\infty\).
