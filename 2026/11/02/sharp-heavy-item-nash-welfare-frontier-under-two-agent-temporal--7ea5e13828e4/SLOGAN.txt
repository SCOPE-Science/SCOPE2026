For two agents with identical positive additive valuations, a largest good carrying fraction \(\delta\in[1/2,1)\) of total value forces the sharp temporal-EF1 Nash-welfare frontier \(\operatorname{NSW}/\operatorname{NSW}^*\ge\sqrt{(1+\delta)/(4\delta)}\). Every frontier point is realized by three ordered goods, so even a clairvoyant prefix-wise EF1 policy cannot improve it; over arbitrary goods the optimal uniform guarantee is the infimum \(1/\sqrt2\).
