For ideals of a finite poset of height \(h\) and width \(w\), the online shattering dimension is at most \(w\lfloor\log_2(h+1)\rfloor\), and this is the exact sharp maximum. At every dyadic height \(h+1=2^r\), equality holds only for \(w\) disjoint \(h\)-chains; in particular all height-two posets have equal VC and Littlestone dimensions, but height three permits a sharp gap of \(w\).
