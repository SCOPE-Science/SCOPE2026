The Hermitian two-body correlation projection on every qubit–qudit system has the exact trace- and spectral-operator norm \(1/2+\sqrt{(4+3c^2)/(4-c^2)}\), where \(c=(n-2)/n\). Separable states attain only \(1+c\), while an explicitly determined, generally nonmaximally entangled Schmidt-rank-two state reaches the strictly larger global norm.
