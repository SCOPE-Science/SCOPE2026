For every real Radon plane and each finite power-mean order \(t\ge1\), the sharp Birkhoff–Takahashi constant equals \(\mathcal B(t)=\bigl([1+(1+\tau_t)^t]/2\bigr)^{2/t}/(1+\tau_t^2)\), where \(\tau_t=(1-\tau_t)(1+\tau_t)^{t-1}\) has a unique solution in \([1/2,1)\); equality characterizes affine-regular hexagons. The quadratic value is \((3+\sqrt5)/4\), and the envelope rises from \(5/4\) toward \(2\), whereas the companion isosceles-orthogonal hexagon constant remains \(5/4\) at every finite order.
