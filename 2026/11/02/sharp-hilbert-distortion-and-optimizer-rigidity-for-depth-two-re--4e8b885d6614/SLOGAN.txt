The vertex metric of every unit-edge depth-two regular tree with \(r,s\ge2\) has exact squared Hilbert distortion \([a(8-2u)+2u]^2/[4a(8-2u)]\), where \(a=1-1/r\) and \(u=1-1/s\). Every optimizer is congruent to an explicit orthogonal-simplex embedding of affine dimension \(rs-1\); the distortion grows with both branching counts and tends to \(\sqrt{8/3}\).
