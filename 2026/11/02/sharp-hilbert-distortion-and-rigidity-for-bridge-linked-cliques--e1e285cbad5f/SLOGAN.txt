For two complete graphs joined by exactly one bridge, the exact least Hilbert distortion is the unique \(c>1\) solving \((\sqrt{c^2-(m-1)/(2m)}+c+\sqrt{c^2-(n-1)/(2n)})^2+(m-1)/(2m)+(n-1)/(2n)=9\). Every optimal embedding is a rigid pair of mutually orthogonal regular simplices positioned along the bridge axis, and the sharp distortion supremum over all clique sizes is \((\sqrt{38}-2\sqrt2)/3\).
