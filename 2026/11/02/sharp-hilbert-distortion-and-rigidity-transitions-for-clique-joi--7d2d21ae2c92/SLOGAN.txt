The squared least real-Hilbert distortion of \(K_h\vee(K_m\square K_n)\) is exactly \(\max\{2,[4N(M-1)+MN(1-1/h)]/(2MN+M-N)\}\), where \(M=\max\{m,n\}\), \(N=\min\{m,n\}\), \(m,n\ge2\), and \(h\ge1\). The distortion rises strictly above \(\sqrt2\) precisely when \((h-1)/(2h)>1/m+1/n\). Optimal embeddings are rigid on the threshold and throughout the unbalanced strict phase, but admit infinitely many noncongruent realizations below threshold and in the balanced strict phase.
