For weighted shortest-path products of an even cycle and any finite complete graph, with cycle edges of length \(1\) and fiber edges of length \(0<\lambda\le1\), the exact squared real-Hilbert distortion is \((m+\lambda)^2\sin^2(\pi/(2m))/(1+\lambda^2\sin^2(\pi/(2m)))\) for a cycle of length \(2m\). A regular polygon paired orthogonally with a regular simplex is optimal, and the sharp constant is independent of the number of fiber vertices; the unit-weight endpoint was already covered by earlier unweighted-product results.
