The centered graph-ball maximal operator on the Petersen graph has exact strong \(\ell^2\) norm \(\sqrt{\lambda_*}\), where \(200\lambda_*^3-355\lambda_*^2+87\lambda_*-1=0\) and \(1.484<\lambda_*<1.485\). Its only extremizers, modulo signs and scale, have three equal largest coordinates on pairs sharing a ground-set element, six equal intermediate coordinates, and one strictly smaller omitted-pair coordinate; there are exactly twenty labeled nonnegative rays.
