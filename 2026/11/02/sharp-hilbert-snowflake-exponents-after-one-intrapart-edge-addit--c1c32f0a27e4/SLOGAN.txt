For \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge2\), adding one edge inside the size-\(m\) part of \(K_{m,n}\) strictly raises its sharp Hilbert-snowflake negative-type exponent, with an exact quadratic equation for the new endpoint and a unique critical equality load. For fixed \(n\), the gain is \(\frac{2n}{(4n^2-1)\ln2}m^{-2}+O_n(m^{-3})\), while the graph still fails unsnowflaked quadratic embeddability.
