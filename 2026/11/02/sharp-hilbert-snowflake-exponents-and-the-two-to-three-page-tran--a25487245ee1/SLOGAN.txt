For an edge book of \(r\ge2\) pentagons sharing one unit edge, the sharp Hilbert-snowflake exponent is the first positive zero of an explicit two-by-two determinant. The two-page graph has an exponent above one but every graph with at least three pages has an exponent below one; the threshold decays as \(3/[r\log(32/27)]+O(r^{-2})\), with exactly one lost affine embedding dimension at criticality.
