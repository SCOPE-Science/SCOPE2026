# Reproducibility

Run `python verify.py` with a recent NumPy installation. In the completed reproducibility check, it printed `CHECK_OK 59`. The test reconstructs every distance by graph breadth-first search for selected page counts, checks the full centered powered-distance eigenspectrum on both sides of the claimed boundary, tests one-dimensional endpoint nullity and the even-sector reduction, and compares the large-page scaling. This is finite supplementary evidence only, not verification of the infinite family. The proof uses direct block algebra, the fifth-power rational enclosure for exponent \(6/5\), and the strict-interior negative-type theorem.

The exact public claim is restricted to unweighted edge books with every page a five-cycle. No independent audit has been performed.
