Removing one cross-part edge from \(K_{m,n}\), with \(m,n\ge2\) and \(m+n\ge5\), has an exactly determined sharp Hilbert-snowflake exponent: it is the unique root of a three-variable quadratic Schur complement and has a one-dimensional critical equality space. For balanced \(K_{r,r}\), the improvement over the undeleted graph is positive and exactly \(\frac{\log3}{(\log2)^2}r^{-3}+O(r^{-4})\), and the critical exponent strictly decreases with \(r\).
