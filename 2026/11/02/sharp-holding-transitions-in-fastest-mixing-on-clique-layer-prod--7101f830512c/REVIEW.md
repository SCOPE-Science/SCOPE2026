# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The unrestricted symmetric stochastic problem reduces by convex averaging to two edge classes. The complete Cartesian-product Laplacian spectrum gives an exact three-affine-function minimax, whose two independent lower bounds match a feasible explicit design. Uniqueness is asserted only inside the two-orbit class. Johnson eigenvalues are independently reconstructed, with boundary equality retained. The bundled verifier was executed successfully: 95 full-matrix spectra, 126 independent linear programs, 504 asymmetric comparisons, 91 parity checks, 20 Johnson spectra, and 90 exact-rational evaluations. All infinite quantifiers are carried by the proof, not by the tests.

## Originality: PASS (qualified)

The 2004 primary FMMC paper establishes the convex formulation and complete-bipartite example; the 2009 symmetry paper proves orbit averaging and gives Laplacian product spectra and edge-transitive and Hamming examples. Its formulas for edge-transitive whole graphs do not directly determine two independent edge probabilities on generic products. Searches for two-orbit clique-layer products, Johnson-product FMMC, and all-base holding thresholds did not uncover the displayed general expression or Johnson threshold. A prior exact solution for **zero-diagonal** cyclic clique products was compared: it can cover overlapping boundary optima but not the full holding-allowed, general-base theorem. Unknown specialized coverage remains possible.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The result solves a natural complete family of Markov-chain network designs, identifies a universal spectral balancing ratio and distinguishes a spectral bottleneck from a stochastic holding-budget bottleneck. The Johnson specialization yields a genuine two-parameter, explicitly located support transition: positive holding at \((n,s)=(4,2),(4,3),(5,2)\), and zero holding at every other admissible integer pair. The exact answer has direct use in layer-augmented symmetric mixing and is not merely an approximation of an existing formula.

## Residual limitations

No independent audit was performed; other nonsymmetric-orbit optimal matrices may exist. The claim is specific to symmetric stochastic walks, Cartesian clique layers, and vertex-and-edge-transitive bases. The prior-art investigation is substantial but not exhaustive.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
