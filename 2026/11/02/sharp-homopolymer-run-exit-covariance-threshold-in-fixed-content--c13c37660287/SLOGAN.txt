For uniformly shuffled binary words with fixed symbol counts, the count of \(000\) windows and the count of \(001\) windows switch from positive to negative covariance at the exact zero-count threshold \(a=(n+6)(n-1)/(4n-6)\). Only the nondegenerate compositions \((n,a,b)=(4,3,1)\) and \((9,4,5)\) have zero covariance, and the limiting threshold is a zero proportion of \(1/4\).
