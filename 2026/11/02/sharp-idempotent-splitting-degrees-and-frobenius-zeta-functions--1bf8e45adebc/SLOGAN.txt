For an evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_2\), the full tower of idempotent counts and the zeta function are determined by the stable invertible part of its additive squaring matrix and its Frobenius action. A perfect algebra of dimension \(n\ge2\) can have no nonzero idempotent until extension degree \(2^n-1\); this sharp delay occurs exactly for primitive-irreducible structure matrices, and then every nonzero geometric idempotent belongs to one Frobenius orbit of that maximum length.
