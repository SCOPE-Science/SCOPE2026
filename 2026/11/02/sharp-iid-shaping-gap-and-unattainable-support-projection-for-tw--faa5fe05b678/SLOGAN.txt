For the separated two-map Cantor IFS, fair IID sampling uniquely minimizes Wasserstein distance to uniform measure at the exact error \((1-2r)/4\). The best probability supported on the same Cantor set has smaller error \((1-2r)^2/[4(1-2r^2)]\), but it cannot be stationary even under state-dependent selection of the two maps; the exact multiplicative IID penalty diverges as \(r\uparrow1/2\).
