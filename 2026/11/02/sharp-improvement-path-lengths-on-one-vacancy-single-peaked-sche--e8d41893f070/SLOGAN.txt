For two-colored single-peaked Schelling jump games on a cycle with one vacant vertex, every strict improvement trajectory terminates at every peak \(0<\Lambda<1\). The exact worst-case move count jumps from \(N-3\) for peaks below one half to \(\lfloor(N-2)/2\rfloor\) for peaks at or above one half, with constructive sharpness in both regimes.
