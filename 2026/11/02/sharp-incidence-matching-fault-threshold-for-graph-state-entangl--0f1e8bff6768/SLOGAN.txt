For the graph state of *every even-order finite projective plane*, omitting any matching of at most \(q\) point-line controlled-\(Z\) gates adds exactly one bit of point-versus-line entanglement per omitted gate. At the sharp threshold of \(q+1\) omissions, exactly one bit of this gain is lost if and only if the omitted point endpoints are collinear or the omitted line endpoints form a complete point-pencil.
