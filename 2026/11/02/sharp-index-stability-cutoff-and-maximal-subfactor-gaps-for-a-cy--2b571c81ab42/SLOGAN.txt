For the normalized cyclic triple \(A=\{0,1,4\}\subset\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), maximal disjoint translates are classified by six cyclic gap lengths with two local adjacency rules. This gives the sharp index-stability set \(\{5,6,7,8,9,10,11,13\}\), an exact size-enumeration formula, and explicit unequal-size maximal packings at every larger order.
