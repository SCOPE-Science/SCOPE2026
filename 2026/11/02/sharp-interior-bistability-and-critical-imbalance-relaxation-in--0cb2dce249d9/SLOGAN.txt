In a symmetric two-species competitive model with quadratic cross-density inhibition, \(a=3/4\) exactly separates global symmetric coexistence from two competing positive coexistence attractors, even though both single-species populations remain invadable. At the transition, unequal initial populations lose their abundance imbalance only as \(t^{-1/2}\), with \(t(x-y)^2\to16/3\).
