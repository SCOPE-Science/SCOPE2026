For Tikjha's four-partition planar map \(T_{a,c}\), the exact nondegenerate inverse-fiber census has a sharp threshold at \(|a|=|c|\): above it in \(|a|\), the map is globally one-to-one, while above it in \(|c|\), the target plane splits into regions with precisely zero, two, or four preimages. The new four-sheet sector has the exact aperture \(\operatorname{atan2}(|c|,|a|)-\pi/4\), with all boundary multiplicities classified; the one-to-one case is a previously covered general inversion result.
