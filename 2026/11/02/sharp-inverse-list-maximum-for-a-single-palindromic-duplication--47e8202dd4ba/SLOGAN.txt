For a received length-\(m\) word after one length-\(k\) palindromic duplication, the sharp maximum number of distinct possible parents is \(1+\lfloor(m-2k)/s_k\rfloor\), where \(s_k=\min\{s\ge2:2s\nmid k\}\). A two-symbol periodic received word achieves this sharp bound for every alphabet size at least two, and overlapping palindrome symmetries prove the matching upper bound.
