# Scientific comparison

**Correctness.** The two deck-image distances determine the intrinsic metric. At fixed target height, maximizing their minimum over the longitudinal coordinate gives a crossing or an endpoint. The remaining one-dimensional maximization yields the eccentricity profile. Its minima occur on the core or the explicitly determined off-core circle; comparison gives the unique coexistence width and the different limiting slopes of the radius.

**Scope of the contribution.** The conditional eccentricity profile, radius, and center set are the focus. The diameter is the support endpoint of the earlier complete area-uniform distance law, and the normalized diameter inequality then follows by one-variable minimization. These diameter facts are presented with attribution as context. Related results on mean or stochastic ordering of distances do not supply the center locus. A later catalogue result also states the exact eccentricity and center transition; this is stated as overlapping content, not an independent priority determination. The historical comparison is bounded.

**Value and limitations.** A radius formula alone does not identify where to place a center. The result identifies the full optimizer set, gives a finite jump in its transverse level, and distinguishes the corresponding transition from the change of branch in diameter. The setting is intrinsic and rectangular; no conclusion is drawn for general Möbius metrics or for embeddings into Euclidean space.
