For every simple Hermitian eigenpair and every two-dimensional trial subspace of the specified perturbative form, square complex-Gaussian randomized Rayleigh–Ritz has an exact, universal *joint* complex-Cauchy law for its normalized leading eigenvalue and eigenvector errors. The simultaneous exceedance probability is \(1/(1+r^2+t^2)\), and the same coupling gives sharp angle and refined-Rayleigh-quotient limits.
