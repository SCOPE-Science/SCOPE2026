For every real codimension-one subspace of a finite maximum-norm cube, the sharp radius-to-diameter Jung constant is exactly \(\max\{1/2,1-\|w\|_\infty/\|w\|_1\}\), attained on an explicit finite simplex-like set. A dominant coefficient gives an isometric lower-dimensional cube, while equal coefficient magnitudes uniquely maximize the Jung constant in dimensions at least three.
