For complete offline temporal approval voting with exactly two alternatives and \(\ell\ge n\), temporal JR has the sharp utilitarian price \(\ell(n-1)/(\ell(n-1)-(n-2))\), and switching a single welfare-maximizing round always suffices to achieve JR. For \(n\ge3\), the only worst cases are rounds with the same lone minority voter opposing everyone else, although the majority's chosen alternative can vary over time.
