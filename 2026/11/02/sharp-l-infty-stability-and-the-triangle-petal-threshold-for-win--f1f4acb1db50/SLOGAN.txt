For windmills formed by \(k\) unit-edge cliques of size \(s+1\) sharing one vertex, the continuous affine \(L^2\)-orthogonal projector has an exact attained \(L^\infty\) norm given by an explicit six-term rational formula. Its norm exceeds \(2\) precisely for triangular petals \(s=2\) with \(k\ge9\), and the sharp unattained family supremum is \(458/225\).
