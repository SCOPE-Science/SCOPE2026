A binary relation consisting of \(r\) rectangles and a remainder with at most \(s\) neighbors per left vertex has maximum half-graph height exactly \((r+1)(s+1)-1\), attained by stable semilinear examples. At equality the rectangle breakpoints are rigid, and the mixed biclique budget for a height-\(t\) half-graph is precisely \(\lfloor t/(s+1)\rfloor\).
