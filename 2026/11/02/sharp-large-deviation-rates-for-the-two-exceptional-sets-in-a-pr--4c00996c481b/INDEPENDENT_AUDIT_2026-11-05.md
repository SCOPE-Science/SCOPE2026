# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a uniform residue mod Q_Y the CRT yields independent signed Bernoulli divisibility indicators with probabilities 1/p; for independent uniform units u,v mod Q_Y, the local event p|u²+v² has probability 2/(p-1) for p≡1(mod4) and 0 for p≡3(mod4). I independently enumerated local unit pairs and CRT classes (1155 combinations) confirming these nonstandard normalizations. Dirichlet-class Mertens supplies H_Y=loglog Y+O(1) and signed sum O(1), so per H_Y log-mgf limits are cosh(t)-1 and exp(t)-1. Exponential tilts at asinh(1/2) and -log 2, plus a bounded tilted triangular-array CLT on intervals of width H^(2/3), yield matching lower and upper rates (1/2)log(phi)+1-sqrt5/2 and (1-log2)/2. Careful strict inequality on square-unit tail shifts threshold only by O(1), immaterial asymptotically. Finite CRT checks do not prove infinite rates.

## originality

PASS

Qualified best-of-knowledge PASS for two explicit exact logarithmic exceptional-tail rates along all odd-prime cutoffs. Yan–Xu arXiv:2610.28237 defines the two sets with second-moment bounds O(1/H); its available Lemma 2.1 summary does not provide exponential asymptotics. Standard Gartner–Ellis, Cramér tilting and mod-Poisson frameworks yield the mechanism when applied and could imply the result as a corollary; specific arithmetic local Bernoulli success probabilities and sharp constants are computed here. Specialist prior deviations work may cover the specialization.

The original sieve's two natural exceptional events are quantitatively upgraded from crude Chebyshev estimates to distinct explicit sharp logarithmic large-deviation rates. Such thresholds can guide density/error analysis and exhibit a meaningful split-prime arithmetic asymmetry; no claim is made that these alone prove a new Romanoff density theorem.
