The two exceptional residue sets governing a recent prime-square Romanoff sieve have distinct sharp logarithmic decay exponents: \(\frac12\log\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}+1-\frac{\sqrt5}{2}\) and \(\frac{1-\log2}{2}\). Their proportions decay as the corresponding powers of \(\log Y\), strengthening the original second-moment bounds along the canonical cutoff of all odd primes up to \(Y\).
