Adjoining a universal apex to the binary Hamming cube collapses its graph-metric Hilbert distortion from the ordinary cube's order \(\sqrt d\) to a sharp limiting constant: \(\lim_{d\to\infty} c_2(K_1\vee Q_d)=\sqrt{8/3}\). Matching tensor-product upper bounds and universal Walsh two-shell lower bounds prove the limit, while an explicit symmetric linear program computes the finite-dimensional values.
