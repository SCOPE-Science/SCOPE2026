For every smooth plane cubic with an \(\mathbb F_q\)-rational flex and cyclic group of \(6m\) rational points, with \(m\equiv0,1\pmod4\), the exact minimum number of rational lines covering all its points is \(2m+1\). A Skolem-sequence construction attains the sharp bound forced by the unique involution; infinitely many curves \(y^2=x^3+1\) over prime fields realize it.
