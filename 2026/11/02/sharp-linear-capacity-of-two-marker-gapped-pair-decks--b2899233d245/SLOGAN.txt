Binary words containing exactly two ones have an eventually linear number of distinct minimum-gap pair-decks: for every \(s\ge3\) and \(n\ge3s-1\), the count is \((2s+2)n-3s^2-2s-3\), with a sharp one-profile excess immediately before the threshold. At gap \(s=2\), the exact count is \(5n-15\) for \(n\ge5\); these counts are also the exact maximum sizes of zero-error codes for the corresponding observation channel.
