For the unit-edge one-point union of \(r\ge2\) congruent cycles \(C_q\) with \(q\ge4\), sharp real Lipschitz variance has an exact parity correction, and every maximizing function consists of balanced signs of the rooted cycle-distance profile. Surprisingly, adjoining one more cycle strictly *decreases* the sharp constant whenever \(r\) is even, but increases it whenever \(r\) is odd.
