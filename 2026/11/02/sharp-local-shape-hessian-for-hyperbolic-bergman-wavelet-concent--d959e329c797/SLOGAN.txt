For hyperbolic-area-preserving deformations of a Bergman localization disk, the largest concentration eigenvalue has an exact, strictly ordered Fourier shape Hessian: only translations are quadratically flat, and the second angular harmonic gives the sharp nonzero curvature. This supplies a precise local operator-norm stability constant for analytic Cauchy-wavelet concentration, distinct from known global Faber–Krahn inequalities and Hilbert–Schmidt purity estimates.
