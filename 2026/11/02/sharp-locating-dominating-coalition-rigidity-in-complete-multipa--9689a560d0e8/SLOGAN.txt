For every complete multipartite graph whose parts each contain at least two vertices, the locating–dominating coalition number is exactly four if every part has size two, and exactly three otherwise. The four-class maxima in \(K_{2,\ldots,2}\) correspond precisely to auxiliary graphs \(2K_2\), \(P_4\), and \(C_4\), yielding \(2^{r-3}(4^r-5\cdot2^r+6)\) optimum partitions on \(2r\) labelled vertices.
