For every nonconstant finite Dirichlet-polynomial symbol on the Hilbert Hardy space of Dirichlet series, the Volterra singular values satisfy \(s_n(T_g)\sim \sup_{t\in\mathbb R}|g'(it)|/\log n\). The coefficient follows from a sharp arithmetic fiber construction; for \(g(s)=2^{-s}+4^{-s}-8^{-s}\) it equals \((8/\sqrt3)\log2\), not the sum of absolute derivative coefficients.
