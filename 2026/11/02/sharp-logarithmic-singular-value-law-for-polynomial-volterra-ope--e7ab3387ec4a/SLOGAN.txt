For every weighted Hilbert Bergman space of Dirichlet series and every nonconstant Dirichlet-polynomial Volterra symbol, the optimal finite-rank approximation error has the sharp law \(\log n)s_n(T_g)\to\max_{z\in\mathbb T^d}|\sum_{k\in F}a_k(\log k)z^{\nu(k)}|\). The coefficient is a coherent torus multiplier norm, independent of the Bergman measure.
