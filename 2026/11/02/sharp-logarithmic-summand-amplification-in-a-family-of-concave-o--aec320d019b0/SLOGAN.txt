For the concave Orlicz sequence gauge generated by \(F_\alpha(t)=t[\log(e^\alpha+1/t)]^\alpha\), the maximal amplification of a sum of \(n\) vectors is asymptotic to \(a_\alpha(\log n)^\alpha\), where \(F_\alpha(a_\alpha)=1\). The exact leading constant makes the logarithmic failure of normability quantitative for every \(\alpha>0\).
