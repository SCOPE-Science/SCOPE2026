For nonnegative distributed-order densities satisfying \(w(1-t)\sim ct^\alpha\) with \(\alpha>-1\), BDF2 convolution quadrature shifts backward-Euler mass from the startup coefficient into an eventually positive tail of exact size \(c\Gamma(\alpha+2)/[2n(\log n)^{\alpha+2}]\). The total difference is zero, while the cumulative BDF2 deficit remains asymptotically one-half of the backward-Euler coefficient at the same index, independent of the endpoint exponent.
