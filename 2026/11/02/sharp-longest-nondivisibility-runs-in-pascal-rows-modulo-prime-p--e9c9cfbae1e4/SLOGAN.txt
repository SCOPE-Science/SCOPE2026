For every prime \(p\), power \(p^\alpha\), and Pascal row \(n\), the longest uninterrupted interval of coefficients not divisible by \(p^\alpha\) has length exactly the least positive residue of \(n+1\) modulo \(p^{\nu_p(n+1)+\alpha}\). Both edge intervals attain it, and an explicit digitwise construction finds a divisible coefficient in every longer interval.
