For every connected vertex- and edge-transitive \(d\)-regular graph \(H\), the fastest symmetric loopless walk on \(H\square K_2\) has exact nontrivial spectral modulus \((2d-\delta)/(2d+\delta)\), where \(\delta=\min\{\lambda_2(L_H),2d-\lambda_{\max}(L_H)\}\), attained by explicit probabilities on base and matching edges. Forbidding holding is free precisely when \(\lambda_2(L_H)+\lambda_{\max}(L_H)\le2d\), with an explicit strictly positive penalty otherwise.
