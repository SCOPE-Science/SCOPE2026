For a lossy coherent-state cavity charged by exact counterdiabatic tracking, the minimum total microwave-field fluence at fixed final work and fixed cumulative cavity loss has a sharp, unique envelope that changes from hyperbolic sine to sine at normalized loss \(q=1/3\), and from sine to a saturated sine–plateau ramp at \(q=1/2\). The resulting three-regime frontier is explicit and improves the source's fixed \(\sin^2\) ramp at the same loss budget.
