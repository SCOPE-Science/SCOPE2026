On \(K_n\square P_L\), every power-dominating set of size at most \(n-2\) either meets an endpoint clique or meets two consecutive clique layers, and this cutoff is sharp. The complete low-degree power-domination polynomial is the complement of a path independence-polynomial substitution; under independent sensors of probability \(c/n\), the full-observation probability converges to \(1-e^{-cL}I_{P_{L-2}}(e^c-1)\) for fixed \(L\).
