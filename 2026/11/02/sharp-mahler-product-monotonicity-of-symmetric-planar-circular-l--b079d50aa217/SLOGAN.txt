For the intersection of two unit disks centered at \(\pm ae_1\), \(0\le a<1\), the planar Mahler volume product decreases strictly from \(\pi^2\) to the unattained sharp limit \(4(\pi+4)/3\). The limiting constant is the exact Mahler product of the full-dimensional parabolic lens \(\{(x,y):|x|+y^2\le1\}\), obtained by affine rescaling.
