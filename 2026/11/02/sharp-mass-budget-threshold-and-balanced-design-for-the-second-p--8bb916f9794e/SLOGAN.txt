Two lighter masses in an infinite harmonic chain support a second localized phonon mode exactly when \(4dab/(a+b)>1\), with a non-normalizable resonance at equality. For fixed total mass reduction \(s=a+b\), a second mode can be created precisely when \(ds>1\), and equal splitting uniquely maximizes its frequency; the weak mode detaches quadratically from the phonon-band edge.
