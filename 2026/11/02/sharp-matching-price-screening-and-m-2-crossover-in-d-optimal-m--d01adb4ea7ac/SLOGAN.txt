In weighted-spanning-tree maximization on a \(2m\)-vertex Möbius ladder, \(m\ge4\), with unit-priced cycle edges and price \(q\) on each antipodal edge, the unique unrestricted budget-optimal design deletes every antipodal edge exactly when \(q\ge m^2/(2m-1)\). Below this threshold, a single Chebyshev-root equation gives every optimal conductance, and at prices proportional to \(m\) the matching-to-cycle conductance ratio has a sharp \(m^{-2}\) crossover.
