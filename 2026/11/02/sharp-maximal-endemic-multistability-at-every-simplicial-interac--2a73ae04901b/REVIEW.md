# Scientific review: maximal endemic equilibrium count in complete simplicial SIS dynamics

## Correctness — PASS
The finding specifies a continuous-time homogeneous limiting scalar differential equation and normalizes recovery to one. After dividing out the disease-free factor, the endemic equation is a degree-\(M\) polynomial with nonzero leading coefficient, proving the total-root bound. Every pair of distinct attracting interior roots needs an intervening root, proving the sharper \(\lceil M/2\rceil\) attractor bound. The explicit positive-rate construction reduces the endemic polynomial exactly to \(-x^M+\varepsilon(1-x)Q(x)\). At rational points interleaving the roots of \(Q\), the perturbation dominates the geometric-series remainder, so the signs alternate at \(M\) points and end negative at one, forcing exactly \(M\) simple interior roots. Sign orientations and continuation of simple roots prove the stability count and robustness. Exact rational calculations for orders \(2\) through \(12\) agree with all algebraic steps. Finite checks are not used to justify the unlimited quantifier.

## Originality — PASS, subject to bibliographic risk
The closest source is Kiss–Iacopini–Simon–Georgiou, arXiv:2309.12752v1, which supplies the complete-simplicial scalar polynomial in equation (II.31), analyzes orders through three, and explicitly treats arbitrarily many endemic states at higher order as a prospect rather than a demonstrated result. Iacopini–Petri–Barrat–Latora, arXiv:1810.07031v1, treats the general-degree model but analyzes triangle and one-higher-order subfamilies, not all strictly positive simultaneous infection orders. Ferraz de Arruda–Petri–Rodriguez–Moreno, arXiv:2112.04273v1, treats multiple active branches arising in community-structured hypergraphs under a different activation rule. Liang–Cui–Liu, arXiv:2408.08602v1, treats a discrete-time network class, with sufficient stability and bistability results. Neither the compared statements nor close mathematical-record searches produced the exact sharp attainable count for the stated complete-simplicial continuous-time model. A hidden equivalent specialization may remain in literature outside the searched sources; search absence is not a priority certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The original complete-simplicial formulation raises the specific question whether increasing interaction order can produce arbitrarily many distinct endemic states, not just more than one. The finding establishes the exact optimal number of endemic equilibria and locally attracting endemic equilibria at every maximum interaction order, supplies strictly positive rates for all included interaction orders, and proves robust realizability. The result is thus a uniform structural classification, not an isolated parameter substitution or a numerical example. The fact that all normalized rates can be arbitrarily close to one shows that positivity constraints do not prevent maximal order-dependent multistability, although the constructed parameter regions can be small.

## Limits and closest comparisons
These are deterministic mean-field equilibria, not positive stationary distributions of the finite stochastic epidemic. The constructed equilibrium intervals may be very narrow. Applications beyond complete homogeneous structures or continuous-time updates are unproved.
