In the homogeneous complete-simplicial SIS mean-field model with every interaction order through \(M\geq2\), strictly positive infection rates can produce exactly \(M\) distinct endemic equilibria and \(\lceil M/2\rceil\) attracting endemic states—the largest counts mathematically possible. An explicit rational construction works at every order, even with all factorial-normalized infection rates arbitrarily close to one, and the full configuration persists under small parameter perturbations.
