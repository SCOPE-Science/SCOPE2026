For the regular rhombic dodecahedron, the largest central-plane section perimeter is exactly \(8\sqrt2\), attained only by the three coordinate planes. A uniformly oriented section has near-maximum probability \(\Pr(P>8\sqrt2-\varepsilon)\sim3(3+2\sqrt2)\varepsilon^2/(64\pi)\), governed by a four-sided linear cusp in orientation space.
