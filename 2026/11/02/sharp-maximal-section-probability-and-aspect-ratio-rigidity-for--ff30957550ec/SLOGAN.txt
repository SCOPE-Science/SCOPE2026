For a right regular \(2m\)-gonal prism with \(m\ge3\), the Haar probability that a central plane cuts the maximum \(2m+2\) sides is explicit and has exactly one optimal half-height-to-circumradius ratio, \(\sqrt{3\cos^2(\pi/(2m))-1}\). Its sharp maximum decays as \(\pi^2/(6\sqrt3\,m^2)\) with increasing base order.
