For one inserted knot in a continuous linear-spline least-squares space, the sharp maximum-norm jump from affine projection is \(12\max\{t,1-t\}/((1+2t)(3-2t))\): it is minimized uniquely by a midpoint knot at \(3/2\), yet approaches \(4\) under boundary knot collapse. The jump even exceeds the universal bound \(3\) for an individual one-dimensional linear-spline projector near either endpoint, and its nonvanishing limit persists for a fixed smooth quadratic input.
