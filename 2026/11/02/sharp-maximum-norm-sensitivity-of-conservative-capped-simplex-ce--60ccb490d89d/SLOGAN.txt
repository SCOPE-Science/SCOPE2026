For equal-volume conservative cell-average projections onto common bounds, two mass-matched inputs can have their maximum-coordinate difference amplified by the exact factor \(2-2/(\lfloor N/2\rfloor+1)\). The map is nonexpansive for two or three cells, first becomes expansive at four cells, and its sharp factor approaches two; this concerns pairwise perturbations and does not contradict its known \(L^2\)-nonexpansiveness.
