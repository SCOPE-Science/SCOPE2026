On a one-shot temporal contact path, alternating edge times uniquely minimize, and monotone edge times uniquely maximize, the uniformly seeded mean SI outbreak (apart from the short-path tie cases), with exact formulas at every transmission probability. The direct index-case reproduction mean is unchanged by the calendar, yet at probability \(q_n=1-1/n\) the optimal-to-worst mean-outbreak ratio grows as \(n/(4e)\).
