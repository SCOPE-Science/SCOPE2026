For a zero-order-held double integrator with exactly two-step-deadbeat unit-period feedback, independent equiprobable timing jitter is mean-square stable precisely below the algebraic threshold \(\delta_*=0.449460497345731\ldots\), the square root of the unique \((0,1)\)-root of \(3z^3-11z^2+12z-2=0\). In the range \(1/3<\delta<\delta_*\), random mixing stabilizes the sampled state in mean square even though the longer sampling interval alone is unstable.
