For a run-and-tumble particle escaping an interval with one sticky wall, the unique mean-, variance-, and coefficient-of-variation-optimal dimensionless tumbling rates satisfy \(b_M=\sqrt{2/3}<b_V\approx0.97533<b_Q\approx3.16897\). The exact nondominated mean–variance set is \(b\in[b_M,b_V]\), whereas the relative-uncertainty optimum is strictly worse in both mean and absolute variance than \(b_V\).
