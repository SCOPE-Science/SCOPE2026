For independent reflected uniform pairs on any partition, the exact worst-case mean-squared integration error over convex \(L\)-Lipschitz functions is \(L^2\max_i h_i^4/48\), attained only by centered V-shaped functions on longest cells. Uniform cells uniquely minimize this risk, and independent equal-variance measurement noise adds exactly \((\nu^2/2)\sum_i h_i^2\) without changing the optimal partition.
