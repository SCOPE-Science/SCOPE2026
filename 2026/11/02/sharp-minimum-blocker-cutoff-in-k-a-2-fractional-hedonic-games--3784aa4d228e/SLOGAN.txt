In simple symmetric fractional hedonic games on \(K_{a,2}\) with \(a\ge2\), every core-unstable partition has a strictly blocking coalition of size at most \(\lfloor a/2\rfloor+1\), and this universal size cutoff is sharp. Explicit worst-case partitions have only \(O(a^2)\) blocking coalitions among \(2^{a+2}-1\) nonempty subsets, so the smallest profitable deviations can be linear-sized while exponentially rare.
