The minimum lengths of overlap-free binary words that are concatenations of exactly \(k\) nonempty squares, for \(1\le k\le9\), are \((2,4,6,8,14,16,18,24,48)\); the corresponding counts of minimum-length words are \((2,2,2,2,6,4,2,8,2)\). Every minimizer has a unique such square factorization, and the only shortest nine-square words are a pair of bitwise complements of length \(48\).
