The proof uses weak Hurwitz stability of each quadratic factor, the oriented weak Hermite--Biehler theorem, a nonnegative Stieltjes partial-fraction expansion, and a coefficientwise deletion-mode lemma. In the strictly positive case, the constant polynomial part vanishes and deletion roots are positive, forcing both modal indices to be nearest neighbors. The checker carried out 125439 admissible finite tests; the all-dimensional proof does not depend on those tests.
