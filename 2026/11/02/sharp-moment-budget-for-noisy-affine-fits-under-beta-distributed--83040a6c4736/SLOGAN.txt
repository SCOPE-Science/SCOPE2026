For unweighted affine least squares with Gaussian response noise and \(m\ge4\) iid \(\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)\) nodes, the slope error has a finite absolute \(q\)-moment exactly when \(q<\min\{m-1,m\alpha,m\beta\}\). In particular, four uniform nodes have finite slope MSE, while four arcsine (Chebyshev-distributed) nodes have infinite slope MSE and five arcsine nodes restore finiteness; the four-arcsine small-range probability is asymptotic to \(48r^2/\pi^4\).
