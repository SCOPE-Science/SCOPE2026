For three uniformly random nodes forming a strictly positive degree-two exact quadrature rule, the sensitivity of its weights to perturbed monomial moments has the sharp conditional tail \(\Pr(\|V^{-1}\|_F>T\mid E)\sim[1-\pi\sqrt3/36]/[(\log3)/2-1/3]\,T^{-2}\). Its conditional second moment therefore diverges despite the positive weights.
