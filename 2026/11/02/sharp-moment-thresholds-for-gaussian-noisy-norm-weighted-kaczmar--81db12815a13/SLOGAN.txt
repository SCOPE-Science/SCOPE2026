When noisy Kaczmarz coefficient rows are freshly resampled from full-dimensional Gaussians and a row is selected with weight \(\|a_i\|^\beta\), the exact finite-time and stationary moment frontier is \(q<\min\{md,d+\beta\}\). Classical squared-norm sampling can restore finite mean-square iterates in low coordinate dimension, but no row-weighting rule of this form can suppress the singular event that all \(m\) candidate rows nearly vanish together.
