For the doubling map with moving, equally sized binary holes \(00\) and \(11\), alternating which hole is open minimizes survival at every finite time, while keeping the same hole open maximizes it; expected first-hit checks are exactly \(3\) versus \(5\). Among periodic schedules, alternation is precisely the zero-entropy, countable-survivor case, while all other nonconstant, nonalternating choices have strictly intermediate entropy.
