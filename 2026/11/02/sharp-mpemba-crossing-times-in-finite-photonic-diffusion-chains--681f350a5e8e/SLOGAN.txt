The classical incoherent limit of a photonic path has exactly one Hilbert--Schmidt Mpemba crossing between a central point mass and a distribution uniform except for one missing endpoint, for every odd number \(N\ge5\) of sites. Its crossing time is asymptotic to \(N^2\log N/(3\pi^2\omega)\), with a sharp additive correction, while the nearby relative-entropy crossing occurs earlier by \(N^{-1/3}/(6\pi^2\omega)\) to leading order.
