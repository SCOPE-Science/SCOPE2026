For a unit-reinforced multicolour Pólya urn with fixed positive initial masses and arbitrary positive quotas, the time to reach all quotas has an exact power-law tail governed by the smallest initial mass, with an explicit constant and a sharp moment threshold. Starting with one unit of every colour is the unique initial-mass configuration whose entire completion-time law depends only on the total quota: its terminal colour is independent of completion time and has probability equal to its share of the quota.
