For every \(N\ge3\), the zero-temperature center of mass of harmonically trapped active Dyson particles has exactly \(N+1\) universal caustic sites when \(\gamma/\mu<1/N\), with explicit, generally asymmetric power-law amplitudes independent of the particles' repulsive interactions. At \(\gamma/\mu=1/N\), its interior peaks become logarithmic with exact coefficients and its edge densities have finite positive one-sided limits; above this threshold its density is continuous and vanishes at the edges.
