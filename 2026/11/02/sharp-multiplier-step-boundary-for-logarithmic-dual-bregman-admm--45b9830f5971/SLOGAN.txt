For logarithmic-dual-update entropy Bregman ADMM on strictly positive optimal-transport data, column-additive costs admit a complete exact softmax trajectory and make the multiplier-step interval \(0<s<2\) sharp even for arbitrarily large penalty. At \(s=2\) the primal iterate has an exact nontrivial two-cycle; for \(s>2\) it alternates between opposite boundary supports while its column projection remains optimally feasible.
