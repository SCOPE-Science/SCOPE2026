For every \(q\ge3\) and survivor length \(k\ge4\), multiset-deletion codes have an exact \(q+1\)-word plateau above \(\lfloor(q+1)(k-1)/2\rfloor\) through \(q(k-1)\), followed by exactly \(q\) words. The first threshold is sharp, and the two upper plateau lengths have respectively \(q^2\) and one labeled maximum codebook.
