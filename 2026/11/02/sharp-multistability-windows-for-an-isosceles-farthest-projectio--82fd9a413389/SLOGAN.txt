In an acute isosceles farthest-projection triangle, two attracting three-cycles coexist with an attracting four-cycle throughout \(1<z<(1+\sqrt5)/2\), and with an attracting eight-cycle throughout \(r<z<3+2\sqrt3\), where \(z\) is squared apex height and \(r\) is the unique positive root of \(z^5-5z^4-5z^3-4z^2-2z-1\). These sharp itinerary-coexistence windows expose open-parameter multistability not captured by a single-orbit period diagram.
