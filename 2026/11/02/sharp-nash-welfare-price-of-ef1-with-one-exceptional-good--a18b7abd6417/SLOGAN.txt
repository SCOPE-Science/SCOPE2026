With one common-value good of weight \(H\ge1\) and \(L\) unit goods, all EF1 allocations admit an exact contiguous count classification, and the worst Nash-welfare product occurs at one of two explicit endpoint counts. Every such EF1 allocation retains at least \(\bigl((n-1)/n\bigr)^{(n-1)/n}\) of optimal Nash welfare for \(n\ge2\), and this uniform factor is sharp.
