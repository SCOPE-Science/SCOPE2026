The 28-vertex Coxeter graph has sharp negative-type exponent \(\alpha\approx0.5704063373\), characterized by the unique positive root of \((-1+\sqrt2)-2\sqrt2\,2^\alpha-2\,3^\alpha+(2+\sqrt2)4^\alpha=0\). Its powered-distance Gaussian kernel is positive definite at every positive scale exactly for \(0\le p\le\alpha\); the critical squared-Euclidean embedding rank drops from \(27\) to \(21\).
