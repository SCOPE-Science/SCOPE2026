For every nonsymmetric Steiner \(2\)-design with block size \(k\ge3\), the point–block incidence metric has exact maximal negative-type exponent \(\log_2(k/(k-1))\), independently of the number of points or the design. At this exponent its minimum Hilbert snowflake embedding dimension drops to \(2v-1\), and the missing \(b-v\) dimensions are precisely the block incidence dependencies.
