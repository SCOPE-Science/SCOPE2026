For every integer \(r\ge2\), attaching \(r\) leaves to each vertex of a unit five-cycle gives a graph metric whose exact supremal negative-type exponent is the unique \(p>1\) with \(r(2\cdot4^p-2^p-2\cdot3^p)=2^p\). The endpoint equality space is one-dimensional, and the thresholds decrease strictly to one with first-order excess \(0.6425936527809607\ldots/r\).
