For a hub joined to disjoint cycles, the exact supremal negative-type exponent is controlled by either an alternating even-cycle mode, the longest odd-cycle Fourier mode, or a hub-against-all-rims mode. Odd-cycle cones exhibit a sharp, arithmetically nonresonant transition at \(n=\cot^2(\pi/(2L))\), with critical Hilbert-embedding dimension drops of \(1\) or any prescribed positive even number.
