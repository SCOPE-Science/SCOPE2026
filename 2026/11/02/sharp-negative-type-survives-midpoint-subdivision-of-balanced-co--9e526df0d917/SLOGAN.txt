Replacing every edge of the balanced complete bipartite graph by a two-edge path leaves its sharp negative-type exponent exactly \(\log_2(n/(n-1))\) for all \(n\ge2\), even though the new graph has \(n^2\) additional vertices. At the endpoint the least squared-Euclidean embedding dimension is \(n^2+2n-2\) for \(n\ge3\), with an exceptional value of \(4\) at \(n=2\).
