Bouquets of \(m\ge2\) five-cycles have an exact sharp negative-type threshold determined by a single two-by-two spectral crossing, with a one-dimensional critical Hilbert-embedding collapse. Their thresholds decrease strictly to one at the sharp rate \(\wp(B_m)=1+0.712036959714\ldots/(m-1)+O(m^{-2})\), although each individual five-cycle has the larger threshold \(\log_2((3+\sqrt5)/2)\).
