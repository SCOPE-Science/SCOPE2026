For every uniformly whiskered clique \(K_n\odot\overline K_r\) with \(n\ge3\), the exact supremal negative-type exponent is the unique root of an explicit scalar equation, with a one-dimensional critical equality space and strictly decreasing exponent in both parameters. Remarkably, increasing \(n\) leaves the known ordinary quadratic embedding constant unchanged while strictly worsening the sharp Hilbert-snowflake exponent.
