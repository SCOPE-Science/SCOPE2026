For coordinatewise-maximal Lehmer codes of permutations avoiding the consecutive pattern \(321\), the inversion-number distribution is unimodal exactly for lengths \(2\le n\le10\) and \(n=12\). Explicit strict coefficient valleys prove non-unimodality at \(n=11\) and for every \(n\ge13\), refining a previously known Padovan block classification.
