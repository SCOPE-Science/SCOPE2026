In a uniformly random finite even, group-coloured hypergraph from the higher-arity parity-extension family, the first asymptotically significant vertex symmetries are transpositions: the nonrigidity probability is \(\binom n2q^{-\binom{n-3}{k-1}}(1+o(1))\). The same sharp term gives the first relative correction to the number of isomorphism classes for every fixed arity and colour rank.
