On the regular zero-order-parameter manifold of the finite discrete-time Kuramoto--Sakaguchi network, the full transverse stability boundary depends only on the second-harmonic modulus \(\rho\), and its maximum permitted gain occurs exactly at \(\rho=|\sin\alpha|\). A four-oscillator configuration at lag \(2\pi/3\) has a nonzero square-zero normal derivative at gain \(4\), canceling linear transverse errors after precisely two steps.
