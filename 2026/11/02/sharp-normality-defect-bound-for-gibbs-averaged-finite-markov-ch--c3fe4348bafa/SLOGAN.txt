For finite Markov chains, Gibbs block averaging obeys a dimension-sharp all-time squared-Frobenius inequality whose obstruction is an explicit nonnormality defect; at time two that defect is exactly half the squared commutator norm. Every normal, including nonreversible, ergodic baseline therefore improves by at least \(\|P-GPG\|_{F,\pi}^{2t}/(N-1)^{t-1}\) at time \(t\), even though its averaged chain need not be normal.
