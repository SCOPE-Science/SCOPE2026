With two opposite residuals and a fixed nugget at least as large as the latent variance, a Gaussian-process marginal likelihood never prefers an interior lengthscale: it jumps directly between zero and infinite lengthscales at the sharp squared-contrast threshold \(\kappa(h)=\frac{h(1+h)}2\log\frac{(1+h)^2}{h(h+2)}\). Contrasts satisfying \(b^2\ge1/2\) prefer zero lengthscale for every positive nugget, so arbitrarily large observation noise does not universally enforce a flat latent process.
