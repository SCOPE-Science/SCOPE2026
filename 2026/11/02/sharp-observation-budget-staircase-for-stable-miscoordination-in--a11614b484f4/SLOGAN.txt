For two-point sampling best-response learning in a symmetric coordination game, with one group taking two observations, stable persistent miscoordination is achievable exactly when the other group's sample size \(k\) exceeds \(2(u+1)\), where \(u>1\) is the payoff from successful coordination on the favored action. The sharp minimum \(k_{\min}(u)=\lfloor2(u+1)\rfloor+1\) is an unbounded staircase with real tie-boundary jumps, and an explicit rational mixing share witnesses every feasible step.
