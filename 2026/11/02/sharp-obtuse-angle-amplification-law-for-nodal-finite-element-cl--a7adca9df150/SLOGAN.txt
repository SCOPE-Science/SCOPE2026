For every planar \(P_1\) triangle with constant SPD diffusion, nodal clipping followed by reinterpolation amplifies Dirichlet energy by at most \(1\) on a nonobtuse diffusion-metric triangle and by the sharp factor \(\csc^2\Theta\) when its largest metric angle \(\Theta\) is obtuse. The \(120^\circ\) triangle achieves \(4/3\), demonstrating why pointwise clipping and nodal clipping have different energy behavior.
