On a strictly positive \(2\times2\) law of fixed odds ratio \(\theta\), the sharp supremum of the \(L^\infty\)-commutator norm of row and column conditional expectations is exactly \(2|\sqrt\theta-1|/(\sqrt\theta+1)\), twice the absolute Yule association coefficient. The bound is unattained away from independence, and an exact formula also determines the norm for every individual table.
