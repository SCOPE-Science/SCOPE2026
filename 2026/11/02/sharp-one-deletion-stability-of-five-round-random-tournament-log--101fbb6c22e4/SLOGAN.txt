For every prime-power Paley tournament \(T_q\) with \(q\equiv3\pmod4\) and \(q\ge7\), deleting **any one vertex** preserves its five-round first-order equivalence to the countable random tournament exactly when \(q\ge107\). The sharp threshold strengthens ordinary four-point extension by requiring two witnesses to every orientation pattern, proved using an exhaustive affine-orbit census and a uniform quadratic-character bound.
