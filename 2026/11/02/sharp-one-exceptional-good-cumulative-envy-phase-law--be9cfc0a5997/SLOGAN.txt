With two identical agents, \(r\) unit goods and one value-\(H\) good, the exact minimum cumulative maximum envy exhibits a parity split and universal thresholds at \(H=2\) and \(H=3\). Sorting by value is optimal for odd \(r\), but for even \(r\) its excess is the tent-shaped function \(\max\{0,\min\{H-1,3-H\}\}\), reaching one at \(H=2\).
