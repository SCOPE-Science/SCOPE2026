For interpolation-defined BDF3 on every fixed two-periodic positive time mesh applied to \(y'=0\), the parasitic two-step Floquet multipliers have modulus strictly below \(1/5\), and the uniform spectral supremum \(1/5\) is sharp even at arbitrarily unequal step lengths. This spectral robustness does not give ratio-uniform transient stability: a single-step starting perturbation can grow proportionally to the step ratio.
