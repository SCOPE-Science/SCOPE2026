For nonlinear discrete \(p\)-heat on any unit-weight complete multipartite graph, the largest edge gradient of an individual solution contracts for every \(1<p<\infty\) precisely when the largest vertex class has at most \((N+2)/2\) members. Although this threshold is independent of \(p\), the sharp instantaneous gradient-dissipation rate has an exact, genuinely exponent-dependent formula involving the two largest vertex classes.
