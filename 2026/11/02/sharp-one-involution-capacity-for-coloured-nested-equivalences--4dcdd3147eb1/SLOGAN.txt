For \(q\)-coloured finite structures with \(K\) nested equivalence relations, the complete generating polynomials for rigid structures and structures having exactly one nontrivial automorphism satisfy explicit coupled product recurrences. Rigidity is possible precisely up to \(C_{K+1}\) leaves, while a single involution remains possible precisely up to \(B_{K+1}=1+\sum_{i=1}^{K+1}C_i\), with no gaps and exactly \(q\) maximal one-involution types.
