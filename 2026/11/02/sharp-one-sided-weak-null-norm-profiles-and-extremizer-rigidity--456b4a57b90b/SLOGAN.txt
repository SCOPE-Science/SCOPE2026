On real atomless \(L^4\), the largest asymptotic one-sided norm \(\|x+t y_n\|_4\) attainable with unit weakly null perturbations is independent of the unit basepoint and obeys an exact quartic algebraic law for every \(t>0\). Every maximizing sequence is asymptotically a skew two-valued multiplier, and such maximizers exist for every basepoint.
