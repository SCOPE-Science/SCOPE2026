The one-site Fourier characters of uniformly proper \(q\)-colourings of the cross-polytope graph \(K_{2,\ldots,2}\) have a complete three-sector, exact rational Gram spectrum. They become linearly independent exactly at \(q=m+1\), where the sharp squared condition number is \(2m+1\); at fixed dimension it approaches \(1+2m/q+O_m(q^{-2})\) as the palette grows.
