For uniform periodic linear advection, standard degree-one upwind DG with SSPRK3 is one-step \(L^2\)-nonexpansive on **every** grid precisely when its Courant number does not exceed the unique positive root \(\nu_\star=0.186457280370046\ldots\) of \(18\nu^4-9\nu^3+3\nu^2+5\nu-1=0\). Above the threshold, a long-wave Fourier mode produces strictly positive one-step energy growth on a sufficiently large periodic grid, even though the semidiscretization is dissipative.
