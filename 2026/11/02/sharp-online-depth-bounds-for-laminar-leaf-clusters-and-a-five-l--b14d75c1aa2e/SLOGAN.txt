For fixed rooted hierarchies with at least two leaves, membership learning of individual leaf clusters has VC dimension two but can have arbitrarily large Littlestone dimension: on a complete \(b\)-ary height-\(h\) tree the latter is exactly \(1+\lfloor\log_2(h+1)\rfloor\). This attains a sharp online upper bound for all laminar families of the same inclusion-chain height, while five leaves are the smallest hierarchy size permitting a strict static–adaptive gap.
