For the open-neighborhood concepts of the line graph of **every** finite graph of girth at least five, \(\operatorname{VCdim}\le\operatorname{Ldim}\le\min\{3,\operatorname{VCdim}+1\}\), and maximum degree three improves the online bound to two. Both bounds are sharp: a nine-vertex tree is the smallest tree attaining online depth three, with \(\operatorname{VCdim}=2\) and \(\operatorname{Ldim}=3\).
