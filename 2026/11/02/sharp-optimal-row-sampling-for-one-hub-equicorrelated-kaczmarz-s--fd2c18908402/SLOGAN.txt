For unit-row randomized Kaczmarz systems with one hub row and \(m\ge3\) exchangeably correlated leaf rows, the globally unique fixed iid sampling probabilities have an exact two-branch formula, with hub-to-leaf ratio \(\min\{(c+ma)/(1+a),\,b(1-b)/(b-a^2)\}\) when \(b>a^2\) and just the first term otherwise. The sharp transition \(m(b-a^2)=a(1-b)\) creates a dimension-independent optimal ratio beyond its cutoff, while \(b=a^2\) retains a positive limiting hub sampling probability.
