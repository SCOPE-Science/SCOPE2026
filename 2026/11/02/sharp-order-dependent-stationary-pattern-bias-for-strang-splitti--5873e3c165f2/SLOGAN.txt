On any finite regular bipartite graph, the two exact-subflow Strang orders for odd-power Allen–Cahn reactions have different but fully explicit attracting checkerboard amplitudes: diffusion outside always underestimates the true branch, whereas reaction outside changes from over- to underestimation at the sharp diffusion rate \(\lambda=1/2\), with equality for every step at the transition. Despite identical fixed-point contraction multipliers, their physical amplitudes separate exponentially as the time step grows, with an exponent independent of the nonlinear power.
