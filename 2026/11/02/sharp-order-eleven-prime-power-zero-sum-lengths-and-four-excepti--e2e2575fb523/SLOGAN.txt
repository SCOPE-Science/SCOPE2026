For every prime \(p\equiv1\pmod{11}\), all order-eleven subgroups modulo \(p^k\) have minimum zero-sum length eleven once \(k\ge5\), and that depth is optimal. At prime squares the only shorter cases are \(p=23,67,89,683\), with respective minimum lengths \(5,8,9,9\); the complete higher-level exceptions occur only at \(p=23\) for \(k=3,4\), both with length nine.
