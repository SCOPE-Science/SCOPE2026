A strictly positive, rapidly oscillatory radial perturbation of a power-weighted disk Bergman measure preserves its entire boundary singular kernel modulo a smooth correction whose boundary trace has **exact Gevrey order two**. The correction's coefficients have an explicit sharp oscillatory envelope proportional to \(n^{(6\alpha+5)/4}e^{-\sqrt{2\eta n}}\), explaining why no smaller global Gevrey order is possible.
