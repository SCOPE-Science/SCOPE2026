For a three-dimensional positive-definite rank-one-spiked matrix with fixed spectrum \(\{L,1,1\}\), exact two-pivot randomly pivoted Cholesky has an expected trace error minimized by a coordinate-aligned spike and maximized by a spike of equal coordinate magnitudes. The sharp orientation penalty approaches \(3/2\) as \(L\to\infty\), even though the one-pivot expected trace error is independent of eigenvector orientation.
