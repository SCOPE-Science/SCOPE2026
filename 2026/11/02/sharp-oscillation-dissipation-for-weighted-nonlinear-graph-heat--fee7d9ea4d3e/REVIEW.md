Gaubert and Qu (2013, arXiv:1302.5226), Section 6 including Example 6.5, develop nonlinear Hopf-oscillation and graph \(p\)-Laplacian contraction bounds using derivatives on restricted configuration sets, without the explicit globally sharp **one-solution amplitude-homogeneous** coefficient. Hua and Mugnolo (2014, arXiv:1410.1584) address time regularity and extinction. De Pasquale, Smith, Bullo and Valcher (2022, arXiv:2201.03103) give general seminorm semicontraction theory, including the **previously established linear** adjacent/common-neighbor condition. The linear case and the diameter-two characterization as a standalone linear criterion are not claimed new. The differentiated contribution is the exact weighted nonlinear two-weight coefficient and equality witnesses for every exponent, plus the complete-versus-noncomplete high-exponent asymptotics. ## Mathematical value — PASS

Vertex oscillation is a canonical Lyapunov seminorm in nonlinear graph diffusion and consensus. The theorem computes its optimal initial dissipation constant on **every** positively weighted finite connected graph, rather than a loose convergence estimate or a selected graph census. The exact two-weight optimization yields a nonlinear dependence on masses/conductances for \(p>2\). On diameter-two graphs, the sharp \(2^{2-p}\) decay and its weighted square-root leading coefficient distinguish noncomplete graphs from complete graphs, while preserving the known low-exponent constant regime. The invariant is directly relevant for quantitative finite-time consensus upper bounds in the subquadratic regime and exposes the exact limitation of first-derivative methods for longer-diameter graphs.

## Limits and antecedents

The result addresses only one-solution *instantaneous* oscillation decay; its integrated time envelopes need not be equality curves. Zero instantaneous rate on diameter greater than two does not mean nonconvergence. Primary comparisons include Mugnolo 2012, Gaubert–Qu 2013, Hua–Mugnolo 2014, and De Pasquale–Smith–Bullo–Valcher 2022; the 2013 and 2022 results are especially relevant conceptual ancestors.
