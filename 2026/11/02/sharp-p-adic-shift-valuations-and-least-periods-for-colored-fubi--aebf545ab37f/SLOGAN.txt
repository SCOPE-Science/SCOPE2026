If \(2\) is primitive modulo \(p^2\) and \(p^s\) exactly divides \(t+1\), then colored Fubini polynomials satisfy the sharp even-shift law \(\nu_p(F_{n+q}(t)-F_n(t))=s+\nu_p(2^q-1)\) beyond an explicit index threshold. Their least eventual periods modulo \(p^a\) are exactly \(2\) for \(a\le s\) and \((p-1)p^{a-s-1}\) for \(a>s\).
