For a complete two-community diffusion network, the fourth-order \([2/2]\) Padé update preserves nonnegative probabilities at every timestep exactly when the two internal-to-cross conductance ratios satisfy \(a/b\le(p+2q)/q\) and \(c/b\le(q+2p)/p\). Outside this sharp region its positivity times form precisely one finite interval, with an explicit endpoint and a universal inverse-distance blow-up at each critical boundary.
