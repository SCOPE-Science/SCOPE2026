In four-dimensional stochastic trace estimation, fair pairwise-independent binary probes have sharp minimum variance \(4\min\{\|u-v\|_2^2,\|u+v\|_2^2\}\), achieved by a unique four-sign parity coset unless the opposing-edge correlation vanishes. One odd-parity probe exactly traces every symmetric four-cycle matrix with matched opposite edges (including every real spectral function of the unweighted cycle), and its variance under edge perturbations is exactly four times the sum of squared opposite-edge defects.
