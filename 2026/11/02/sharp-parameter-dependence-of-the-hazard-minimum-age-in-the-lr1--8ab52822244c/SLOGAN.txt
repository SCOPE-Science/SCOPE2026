For the LR1 exponential/Gamma lifetime mixture, the age \(t_\lambda\) of the lowest failure hazard decreases strictly with \(\lambda\), beginning at \(8\sqrt2\) as \(\lambda\downarrow0\) and contracting as \((4/3)(\log\lambda)/\lambda\) with an explicit log-log correction as \(\lambda\to\infty\). Yet measured in mean lifetimes, that minimum moves from asymptotically zero to \((8/3)\log\lambda\); the general qualitative bathtub-shape fact is already known and is not claimed as new.
