For a uniform quantum ring, the total magnetic flux that maximizes the **smallest Bohr-frequency gap** is exactly \(\pi/2\) or \(3\pi/2\) for odd rings, but \(N\arctan(\tan(\pi/N)/3)\) or its reflected flux for even rings. These are precisely the fluxes minimizing the sharp asymptotic Hilbert--Schmidt Cesàro dephasing-channel error envelope, with an odd-even limiting flux split of \(\pi/2\) versus \(\pi/3\).
