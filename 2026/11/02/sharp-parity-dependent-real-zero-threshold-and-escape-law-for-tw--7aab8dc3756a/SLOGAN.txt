For every degree \(n\ge2\), the real-rootedness of \(\operatorname{He}_n+b\operatorname{He}_{n-2}\) ends sharply at \(b=n-1\) for even degrees and \(b=n\) for odd degrees, after which exactly one imaginary-conjugate pair appears. Its squared imaginary radius has sharp two-sided global linear bounds, a strictly convex escape trajectory in degrees at least four, and a parity-dependent onset slope.
