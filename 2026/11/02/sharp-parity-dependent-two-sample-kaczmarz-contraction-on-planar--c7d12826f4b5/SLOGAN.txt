For a planar harmonic frame with \(m\ge3\) equally spaced unit rows, choosing the largest residual from two uniformly sampled distinct rows has an exactly computable sharp conditional mean-square progress constant, with different cotangent formulas for odd and even \(m\). The worst direction is row-aligned or halfway between rows for odd \(m\), but only halfway for even \(m\); its guaranteed progress tends to \(1/2+2/\pi^2\), strictly exceeding the uniform single-row value \(1/2\).
