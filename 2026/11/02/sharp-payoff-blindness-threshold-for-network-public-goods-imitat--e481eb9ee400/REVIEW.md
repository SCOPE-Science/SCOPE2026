# Scientific notes

The equality boundary must distinguish the zero and positive extrema. At a positive attained maximum the strict cutoff retains a nonzero payoff difference; at a zero maximum every raw difference is zero and remains so at cutoff zero. On the four-cycle at synergy 9 the shared benefit cancels the cooperator's total cost in every payoff contrast. The torus has a strictly positive maximum and does not require this exceptional branch.

The thresholding rule and voter fixation formula are prior work. The contribution is the exact statewise maximum for regular graphs, its explicit torus specialization, and their use in a sharp uniform neutrality test. It does not give a prediction of selection or fixation below a positive threshold. See `RESULT.md` for the proof and source comparison and `VERIFICATION.md` for the finite replay.
