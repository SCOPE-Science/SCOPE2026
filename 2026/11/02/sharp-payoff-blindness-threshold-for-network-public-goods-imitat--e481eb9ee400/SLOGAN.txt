For standard overlapping-neighborhood public goods on any connected regular graph, there is an exact state-independent payoff-ignorance threshold, computable from two rows of \((I+A)^2\), beyond which every finite-temperature Fermi comparison becomes a fair coin flip. On square tori of side at least six the sharp cutoff is \(\max\{5+r,|11r/5-5|\}\); the resulting uniform asynchronous process has the classical voter fixation probability.
