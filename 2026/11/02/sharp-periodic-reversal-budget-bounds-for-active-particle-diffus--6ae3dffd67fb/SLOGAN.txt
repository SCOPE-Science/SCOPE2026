For a one-dimensional velocity-flipping particle, every nonnegative periodic Poisson reversal protocol with fixed reversal budget \(\Lambda\) and period \(T\) has sharp diffusivity bounds \(v^2T/(2\Lambda)\le D<(v^2T/2)\coth\Lambda\). The homogeneous protocol uniquely minimizes spreading; a single increasingly concentrated reversal pulse per period approaches the optimal upper bound.
