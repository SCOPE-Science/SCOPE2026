For the normalized Szegő-kernel Gram matrix of two concentric regular orbits containing \(m\) and \(n\) points, the unique optimal relative phase is \(\pi/\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)\), independent of both radii. An exact cyclotomic determinant formula gives the sharp global logarithmic loss for any phase error and the arithmetic exponent \(\gcd(m,n)\).
