For every fixed alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and subsequence read length \(k\ge1\), the number of distinct length-\(k\) decks has exact polynomial order \(\Theta_{q,k}(n^{\sum_{j=1}^{k}jL_q(j)-1})\). A constructive family of equal-length positive-word commutator gadgets and base-\(2^j\) digits realizes the full exponent, giving the missing general lower bound in the 2026 distinct-deck conjecture.
