For equispaced one-dimensional Korobov kernel quadrature against \(\frac\pi2|\sin(2\pi t)|\), every optimal weight is an exact positive tent mass minus a common offset. All weights are nonnegative exactly when \(\gamma\le\Gamma_{\mathrm{pos}}(n)=n+\pi^2/3+O(n^{-1})\), whereas the rule takes the constant function to a negative number only at the much larger threshold \(\gamma\asymp n^4\), with a sharp even–odd parity effect.
