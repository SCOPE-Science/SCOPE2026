For an explicit balanced four-row Parseval frame, the population-optimal entrywise Bernoulli control-variate Gram sketch is positive definite for every sample if and only if \(K<1+12\eta/(3-2\eta)\), despite strictly reducing its Frobenius mean-squared error for every \(K\ge1\). The bound is attained by a sampling event with positive probability, and the least uniform diagonal shift to restore positive semidefiniteness is computed exactly.
