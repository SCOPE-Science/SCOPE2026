For degree-one trigonometric quadrature at three independently uniform-jittered periodic grid points, all weights remain positive almost surely through jitter fraction \(1/4\), after which their exact positivity probability is \(1-\frac32(1-1/(4\alpha))^2\). Observation-noise variance remains finite for every \(\alpha<1/2\) but diverges logarithmically with explicit coefficient \(54(2\sqrt3/\pi-1)\) as \(\alpha\uparrow1/2\).
