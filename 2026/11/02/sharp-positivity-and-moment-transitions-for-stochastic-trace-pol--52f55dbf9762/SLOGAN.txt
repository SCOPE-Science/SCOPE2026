For the unbiased spherical trace estimate substituted into the first symmetric-polynomial preconditioner on a rank-one-spiked positive matrix, \(\kappa=n\) is the exact almost-sure positivity frontier, yet at that frontier the preconditioned condition number has tail exponent \(r/2\) and infinite mean for one or two probes. Beyond the frontier, failure has a sharp incomplete-beta probability with one probe, and increasing the spike before the probe budget leaves a nonvanishing failure probability even as the number of probes grows.
