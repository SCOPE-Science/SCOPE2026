For Crank–Nicolson on every positive weighted star with at least two leaves, the sharp Markov-positivity horizon is the unique root of \(\sum_i tw_i/(1+tw_i)=1\), and negativity always first appears at the hub. At fixed total conductance \(W\), equal spoke weights uniquely give the smallest safe timestep \(2m/[(m-1)W]\), while concentrating weight on one spoke makes the safe timestep diverge like the inverse square root of the remaining spoke conductance.
