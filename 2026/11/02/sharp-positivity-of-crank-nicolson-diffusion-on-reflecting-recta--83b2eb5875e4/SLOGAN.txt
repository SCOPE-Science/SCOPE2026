On every reflecting rectangular grid \(P_m\square P_n\), Crank–Nicolson first loses entrywise positivity at the central vertex or central vertices, and its exact sharp step limit is a finite cosine-spectral root. The sharp uniform two-dimensional bound is \(\kappa\Delta t/\Delta x^2\le0.5881972836856281\ldots\), with a square-grid correction of order \(n^{-1/2}e^{-1.6502372569\ldots n}\) whose even-size coefficient is strictly larger than its odd-size coefficient.
