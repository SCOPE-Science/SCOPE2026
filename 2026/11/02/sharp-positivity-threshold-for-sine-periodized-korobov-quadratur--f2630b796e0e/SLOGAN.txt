For the complete one-dimensional sine-periodized first-order Korobov quadrature, all unconstrained optimal kernel weights are positive exactly for \(0<\gamma<\gamma_N\), where \(\gamma_N\) is an explicit even–odd cotangent expression and \(\gamma_N=N+\pi^2/3+O(N^{-1})\). At the sharp threshold a weight vanishes; above it a weight is negative, despite the positive integration density.
