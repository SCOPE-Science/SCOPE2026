The sharp Crank–Nicolson positivity threshold on a reflecting path is governed by the central resolvent diagonal, not a boundary diagonal: for every \(n\ge3\) it is a unique explicit parity-dependent algebraic root, and for \(n=2\) positivity is unconditional. The thresholds strictly decrease to \(s=3/4\), with first corrections \(4\cdot3^{-n}\) for odd \(n\) and \((20/3)\cdot3^{-n}\) for even \(n\), giving the sharp all-mesh no-flux diffusion condition \(\kappa\Delta t/\Delta x^2\le3/2\).
