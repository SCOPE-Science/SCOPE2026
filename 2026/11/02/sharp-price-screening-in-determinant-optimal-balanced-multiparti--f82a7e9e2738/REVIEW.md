# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The connected-support optimum exists on the compact budget simplex. Injectivity of the edge-weight to grounded-Laplacian map and strict log-determinant concavity prove uniqueness among all independent edge weights, not only symmetric ones. The graph automorphism orbits reduce it to two weights; the five orthogonal spectral blocks establish the exact weighted tree product. Differentiating after budget elimination gives a checked quadratic sign criterion and a sharp whole-edge-class deletion boundary including equality. The critical-window asymptotic follows from those exact coefficients. `verify.py` computes exact rational Laplacian determinants independently and checks 7,798 exact determinant, derivative, screening, quadratic-root and asymmetric-budget identities, but the general conclusion relies on the analytic proof.

**Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, with residual risk.** The complete multipartite weighted enumerator is known: Aalipour et al., arXiv:1510.00033, Theorem 1.2 and Section 4, include a class-weight specialization underlying this factorization. Khosoussi et al., arXiv:1604.01116, Sections 2 and 4.4, develop general spanning-tree log-determinant design and its continuous relaxation, but do not give the all-dimension two-price optimum and support-switch theorem stated here. Martin–Reiner, arXiv:math/0302213, study different product/threshold graph enumerators. The new mathematical content assessed here is the exact unrestricted optimization, price-screening threshold, unique explicit optimizer, and critical-window law; no claim is made to generic matrix-tree factorization or concavity. Published-search and related prior-formulation comparisons found no decisive implication, but terminology-hidden historical overlap is possible and external review remains necessary.

**Value — PASS.** This is a natural complete convex design classification with a structural edge-screening effect: a whole family of available edges becomes optimally unnecessary above a sharp dimension-dependent surcharge. The result gives a directly usable all-parameter allocation rather than merely an algorithm, and exposes a nontrivial critical scale near the screening transition. It applies even when all edges are independently controlled and allowed to vanish.

Limitations: balanced part sizes, one special part, two linear prices, one spanning-tree objective. Finite experiments do not establish historical uniqueness or substitute for the analytic proof.
