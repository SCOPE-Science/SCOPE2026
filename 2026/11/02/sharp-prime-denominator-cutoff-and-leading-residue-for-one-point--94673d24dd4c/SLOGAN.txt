For \(p\ge5\) dividing the Minkowski denominator in genus \(g\), the last one-point Hodge integral attaining its full prime-power denominator occurs exactly at the index \(i=\lfloor g/((p-1)/2)\rfloor+(g\bmod((p-1)/2))\). Its reduced prime-adic residue is explicit and nonzero, making the cutoff sharp.
