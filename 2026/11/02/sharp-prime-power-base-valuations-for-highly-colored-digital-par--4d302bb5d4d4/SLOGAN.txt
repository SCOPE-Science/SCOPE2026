For prime-power bases \(p^a\) with at least two prime factors counted with multiplicity, sufficiently divisible color multiplicities give the exact valuation \(\nu_p(S_{p^a,m}(n))=\nu_p(m)+a-(\nu_p(n)\bmod a)\), together with the first nonzero \(p\)-adic digit. This proves two conjectured \(4\)-ary partition identities of Ulas and Żmija and yields an explicit \(p\)-automatic valuation law for every prime-power base.
