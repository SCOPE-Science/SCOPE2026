For every prime \(p\) and \(a\geq1\), the Nekrasov–Okounkov series \(F_{p^{2a}}\) and the \(p^a\)-core series \(C_{p^a}\) agree coefficientwise modulo \(p^{a+1}\), sharply: the first failure modulo \(p^{a+2}\) is at degree \(p^{a-1}\). Their complete leading normalized difference is an inverse-divisor-sum series for odd primes and a square-plus-doubled-square theta correction for \(p=2\).
