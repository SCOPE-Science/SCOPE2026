In the García-Grimaldo–Campos-Cantón fixed-point-free four-branch map, the complete prime-three orbit count jumps from zero to one to two as \(r(s-1)\) crosses one; the equality case is a single discontinuity-boundary cycle. Every off-boundary prime-three orbit is repelling with a sharp universal return-multiplier gap \(rs^2>4\), and all its coordinates are explicit rational functions of the two slopes.
