In the two-cell sigmoid spiking map of Buscarino–Famoso–Fortuna, no primitive two-cycle exists at any positive sigmoid gain when the Euler step satisfies \(0<T\le2\). Every \(T>2\) nevertheless supports an explicitly localized, hyperbolically repelling primitive two-cycle for all gains \(\alpha\ge10\), with rigorous exponentially small errors from the saturated orbit.
