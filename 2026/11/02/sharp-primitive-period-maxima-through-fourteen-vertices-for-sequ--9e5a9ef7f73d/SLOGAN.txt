For permutation-sequential closed-neighborhood \((1,3)\) bi-threshold dynamics on trees, the sharp maximal primitive periods for orders \(1\) through \(14\) are \((1,1,1,1,2,3,6,10,14,22,42,58,86,106)\). Exactly one unlabelled fourteen-vertex tree attains period \(106\), and none with fewer vertices can do so.
