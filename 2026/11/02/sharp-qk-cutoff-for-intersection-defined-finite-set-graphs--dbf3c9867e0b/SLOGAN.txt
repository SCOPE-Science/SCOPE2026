For every fixed uniformity \(k\), quantifier rank \(q\), and intersection-based adjacency rule, all graphs on \(k\)-subsets of ground sets with at least \(qk\) points have identical rank-\(q\) first-order theories. The threshold is exactly sharp for Kneser graphs, where a rank-\(q\) clique sentence detects the missing \(q\)-th disjoint block.
