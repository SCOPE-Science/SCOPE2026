For quadratic complex polynomials in the Gaussian Fock space, the exact optimal square-restricted coercivity constant is \(\sqrt K=1.563357712\ldots\), where \(K\) is the root of \(7K^3+9K^2-108K+108=0\) in \((2.4440873,2.4440874)\). All nonconstant equality cases are rotations and nonzero scalar multiples of one explicit three-term polynomial \(1+\sqrt{x_*}\,z-u_*z^2\), giving a strict lower bound on the unrestricted sharp constant.
