For a three-path metric theta graph of total length \(L\), the squared intrinsic distance between two independent arclength-uniform points has the sharp expectation range \(7L^2/162\le\mathbb E[D^2]<L^2/12\): the unique minimizer has three equal arms, while the circle-degeneration supremum is unattained. An explicit quartic formula and positive-coefficient gap certificates prove both endpoints, even though the equilateral graph does not stochastically minimize all pairwise distances.
