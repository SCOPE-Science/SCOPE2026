The quadratic \(L^2\) least-squares projector on the unit metric complete graph \(K_n\) has an exact vertex-evaluation norm increasing to \(61/25+2\sqrt5/15\). For every \(n\ge6\), that vertex norm is the full attained \(L^\infty\) operator norm, with no interior norming points; the sharp positivity defect is half the norm minus one half.
