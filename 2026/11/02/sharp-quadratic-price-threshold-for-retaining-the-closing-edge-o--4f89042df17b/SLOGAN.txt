For a cycle with independently optimized edge conductances, one exceptional relative price \(r\), and a fixed linear budget, the optimal Kirchhoff-index design deletes the exceptional edge exactly at an explicit critical price \(r_c(n)\), while all ordinary edges remain active at every price. The threshold is independent of the budget and satisfies \(r_c(n)/n^2\to\pi^2/4-2\), so the price needed to remove the cycle-closing shortcut grows quadratically with network size.
