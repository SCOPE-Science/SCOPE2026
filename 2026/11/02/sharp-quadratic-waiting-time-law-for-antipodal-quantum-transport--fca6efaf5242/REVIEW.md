# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The graph's full adjacency spectrum and parity are fixed by exact distance-polynomial identities independently checked with integer arithmetic. The four-phase weighted-variance formula proves a positive lower bound on infidelity for **every** real time, while Pell algebra constructs the stated upper bound and asymptotic. The manuscript justifies all constants, time quantifiers and endpoint conditions. An independently implemented numerical matrix exponential and finite test suite corroborate the analytic formulas but are not treated as an infinite proof. Risk: no globally optimal coefficient is established; none is claimed.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, provisional
The 2007 icosahedral distance-regular study develops its spectrum and engineered perfect transfer, including non-nearest-neighbor couplings: it is the most important close predecessor. The 2012 number-theoretic paper concerns unmodulated paths, while the almost-perfect transfer chain literature provides a more general qualitative phenomenon but no identified sharp uniform icosahedral waiting-time envelope. The present two-sided \(\Theta(T^{-2})\) statement and explicit Pell witness use a different constrained Hamiltonian from the engineered transfer protocol. Searches for equivalences and stronger bounds did not find coverage; negative searches do not prove historical originality. Risk: a general Diophantine transfer theorem or inaccessible paper may imply the same bound; independent priority adjudication is outstanding.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Replacing engineered long-range interactions with uniform local hopping is physically motivated. A qualitative promise of arbitrarily good transfer is operationally incomplete without a waiting-time/fidelity tradeoff. The matching upper/lower quadratic exponent, non-attainment at finite time and constructive infinite Pell schedule give a sharp scaling classification of a canonical finite quantum network. It is not merely a parameter substitution or a fresh numerical table. The explicit constants are conservative and optimal times remain unknown.
