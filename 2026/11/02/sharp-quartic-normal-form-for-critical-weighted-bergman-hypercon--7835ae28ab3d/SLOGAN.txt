For sharp weighted Bergman \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) dilation, the constant extremizer is quartically flat in its first analytic mode: the optimal defect is \(|a|^4/[\alpha^2(\alpha+1)]\), achieved asymptotically only after the nonlinear correction \(b_2=a^2/2\). Every higher mode pays a positive, explicitly known quadratic penalty, yielding a uniform infinite-dimensional local rigidity theorem.
