For third-order two-stage Radau IIA diffusion on unweighted Boolean hypercubes, entrywise positivity holds at every step exactly through dimension three; the four-cube has one bounded forbidden step interval, and the five-cube has both a short-step and a separated intermediate-step forbidden interval with exact algebraic endpoints. In every dimension at least five, a distance-five endpoint entry starts as \(-10h^5/9\), so even arbitrarily small positive timesteps can violate positivity, while sufficiently large steps regain it.
