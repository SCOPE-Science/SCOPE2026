One stationary snapshot of an intermittently trapped Brownian particle permits an exact Bayes-optimal on/off state inference rule with at most one radial switch and a sharp critical dimension where that switch first appears. Its high-dimensional threshold satisfies \(r_d^2=2d+8(b-a)+16(1+b-a)/d+O(d^{-2})\), but an explicit strictly positive classification-error floor survives even infinitely many observed coordinates.
