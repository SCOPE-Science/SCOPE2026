For one-dimensional heat-equation finite elements on \(n-1\) independent uniform random interior knots, the sharp positivity-preserving forward-Euler step is one-half the smallest adjacent product of cell lengths. Its asymptotic law is \(2n^3\log n\,\tau_n^*\Rightarrow\operatorname{Exp}(1)\), and its reciprocal has no finite mean even at fixed mesh size.
