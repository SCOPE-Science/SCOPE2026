For a planar oscillator sampled at \(m\) independent random projective phases, the least observability-Gramian eigenvalue has a sharp lower tail of order \(\varepsilon^{(m-1)/2}\), with an exact coefficient proportional to \(\int w^m\). Gaussian-noisy least-squares state estimation has finite unconditional mean-squared error exactly at \(m\ge4\), and uniform phase sampling uniquely minimizes the leading heavy-tail constant among continuous sampling densities.
