For a minimal two-component noninterpolating quadratic, the exact stationary mean-squared error of constant-step independent-epoch reshuffling has a unique optimal order probability: it is genuinely randomized below a sharp step-size threshold and deterministic above it. At small steps, optimized biased reshuffling has error \(O(h^3)\), while uniform random reshuffling has error \(\Theta(h^2)\) whenever the two curvatures differ, despite the two orders having identical squared prefix-gradient discrepancy.
