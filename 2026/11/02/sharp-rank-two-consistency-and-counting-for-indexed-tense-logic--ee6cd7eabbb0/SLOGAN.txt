For indexed tense logic with \(r\) converse pairs and \(p\) propositional letters, depth-two modal types have an exact local consistency test and count \(m2^{r m2^{2rm}}\), where \(m=2^p\). Converse semantics cuts the number of possible second-stage profiles by a factor \(2^{r m2^{2rm}}\) compared with independent directional modalities, and every admissible profile has an explicitly bounded finite witness.
