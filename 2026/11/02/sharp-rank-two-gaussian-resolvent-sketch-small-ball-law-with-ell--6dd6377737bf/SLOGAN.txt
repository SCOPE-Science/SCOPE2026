For independent real Gaussian block probes, the probability that a fixed rank-two resolvent residue appears smaller than \(\varepsilon\) has the exact asymptotic \(C_kJ(a,c)(ac)^{-k}\varepsilon^{2k-1}\), with an explicit elliptic spectral-shape constant. The sharp exponent verifies the rank-two behavior suggested numerically in recent rational-resolvent eigensolver research and requires no logarithmic correction.
