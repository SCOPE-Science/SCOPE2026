In a uniformly random equitable red–blue colouring of the edges of \(K_n\), the probability of needing any wild edge—and the expected wild number—are both asymptotic to \((4/e)n2^{-n}\). Conditional on needing a wild edge, a single vertex isolated in one colour accounts for the failure with probability tending to one; needing two or more wild edges has probability \(O(n^2 4^{-n})\).
