In the rare-event Poisson limit of half-thinning iid centered Bernoulli summands, the retained-to-original first absolute-moment ratio is maximized **uniquely** at Poisson intensity \(\lambda=1\), with sharp value \(\sqrt e/2\). Thus any universal \(C\sqrt q\) centered-sum thinning bound requires \(C\ge\sqrt{e/2}\), even for bounded two-point summands; this is a necessary bound, not the unrestricted sharp constant.
