In a harmonically trapped particle driven by active Ornstein–Uhlenbeck noise, bounded control of the noise's correlation rate gives an exact closed interval of reachable terminal position variances for each prescribed terminal cross-covariance. The globally fastest transfer between two distinct interior stationary Gaussian states is achieved uniquely (almost everywhere) by a single switch between the two extreme admissible correlation rates, with the order set by the direction of the target change.
