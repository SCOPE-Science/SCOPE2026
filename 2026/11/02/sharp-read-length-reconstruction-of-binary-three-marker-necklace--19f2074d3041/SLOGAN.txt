Every binary circular word with exactly three marked positions is uniquely recoverable from its complete length-\(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\) read multispectrum when \(n\ge6\), and this threshold is sharp. When the three zero gaps are pairwise distinct, the exact individual identification length is \(n-M\), where \(M\) is the longest zero gap; shorter reads cannot distinguish the word from its reversal.
