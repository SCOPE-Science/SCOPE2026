For the canonical upper-half-plane Bergman Gram kernel of the real differential evaluation \(f+af'+(b/2)f''\), zero-freeness holds exactly when \(a^2\ge b+\sqrt{10/3}|b|\). Below that boundary there are exactly two simple attainable scalar zero fibres; on the boundary they merge into inaccessible double real roots, proving a sharp three-jet restoration of zero-freeness.
