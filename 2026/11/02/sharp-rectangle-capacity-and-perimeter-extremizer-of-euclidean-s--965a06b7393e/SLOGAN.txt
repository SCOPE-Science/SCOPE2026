Every Euclidean stadium with a positive straight segment has a unique largest-area inscribed rectangle, aligned with its axes, and its exact area depends algebraically on the segment-to-radius ratio. Among all stadiums with fixed perimeter, this rectangular area is maximized by the unique noncircular aspect ratio \(a/r=\pi/2-4/\pi\), while the optimal rectangle's fraction of the stadium area increases strictly from \(2/\pi\) toward \(1\).
