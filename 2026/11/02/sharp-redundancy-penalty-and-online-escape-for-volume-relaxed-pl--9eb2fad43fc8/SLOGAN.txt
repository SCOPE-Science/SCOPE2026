In globally volume-normalized over-relaxed uniform two-row Kaczmarz on \(M\) nonparallel unit planar rows, the sharp supremum of the exact mean-square error factor is \((M-2)/(M-1)\), although every ordinary full-rank block solves in one step. A running maximum initialized from the first selected block also solves immediately, whereas a precomputed global maximum can require \(\binom M2\) updates on average to hit the exact solution.
