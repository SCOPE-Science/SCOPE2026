# Assessment of the exact support transition

**Correctness.** The two branches contract in log-ratio distance and have positive weights, so there is a unique stationary law with compact invariant support. The prior reciprocal symmetry reduces the analysis to the orientation-preserving maps with parameter \(\tau\). Their support hull endpoints satisfy the stated quadratic, and its sign at \(a\) gives the exact contact threshold. Touching images already fill the hull; strict separation gives injective coding by binary addresses. The degenerate constant-map case is treated separately. Finite checks corroborate the formulas but do not prove the classification.

**Originality within the compared sources.** Morita and Yoshimura, arXiv:1207.4618, pp. 3–4, give the ratio recursion, inversion symmetry and singleton case. The equality of invariant laws at reciprocal parameters is an immediate consequence of their recursion and symmetry and is used as background, not as a novelty claim. Standard contractive IFS results cover the separation mechanism, not the specific critical migration fractions or the explicit gap endpoints. A related affine IFS support transition for a trapped swimmer (Resultary 8cb7ca113d37) uses different maps and a different parameter. The comparison does not certify historical priority.

**Value.** The exact thresholds show that a model with self-similar stationary ratios nonetheless has full interval support in two nondegenerate ranges, with a genuine gap in the intervening ranges away from the singleton. The explicit formula locates both the separations and their closures. No density or population-survival claim follows.
