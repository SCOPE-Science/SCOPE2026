In a two-habitat stochastic growth model with equally likely perfectly anticorrelated environments, stationary population-ratio support changes exactly from an interval to a Cantor set, collapses to one point, then returns through a Cantor phase to an interval as dispersal rises. The two thresholds are \(a/[a+s(a^2+a+1)]\) and \((a^2+a+1)/(a^2+a+1+as)\), and the full stationary law is identical at paired dispersal fractions \(q\) and \((1-q)/(1-q+s^2q)\).
