On every finite alternating group \(A_n\), the reflection-length kernel \(q^{n-c(g^{-1}h)}\) is positive semidefinite exactly for \(0\le q\le 1/\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) and for larger reciprocal integers \(q=1/j\); between these isolated values every intervening interval fails. Its singular endpoint rank is determined exactly by one self-conjugate Young diagram in odd order and three diagrams in even order.
