For transformations preserving two labeled blocks of \(m\) points each, the proportion of regular elements has an exact Stirling-number expression and equals \(\tfrac12+1.210700991285293\ldots\,(0.696742956572524\ldots)^m(1+o(1))\). The sharp exponential correction comes from the rare event that the images of two independent random selfmaps are comparable by inclusion.
