At fixed mean reset spacing, the maximal Gaussian endpoint Jeffreys-divergence rate for harmonic relaxation has a sharp protocol transition: deterministic waiting is uniquely optimal above a critical duration, whereas nearly simultaneous bursts of resets approach the optimum below it. The critical duration is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{c/2}/\lambda\) as the reset-to-equilibrium variance ratio \(c\) vanishes; this endpoint benchmark is not total thermodynamic entropy production.
