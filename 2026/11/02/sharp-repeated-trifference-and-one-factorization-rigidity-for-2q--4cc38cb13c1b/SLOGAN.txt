For \(2q\) codewords of length \(2q-1\) over \(q\ge3\) symbols, the sharp guaranteed triple-separation multiplicity is \(2q-4\), and equality holds exactly for labeled one-factorizations of \(K_{2q}\) encoded as coordinate symbol-pairs. At \(q=3\), this classifies and counts all \(7776\) six-word, length-five doubly trifferent ternary codebooks.
