For a sluggish diffusion restarted whenever it reaches either symmetric boundary, the complete long-time reset-count rate function is determined by an inverse confluent hypergeometric function, with exact zero-count and high-current boundary laws. After normalizing by each model's mean reset frequency, increasing sluggishness strictly raises the large-deviation cost of every above-average reset current, while its Fano factor decreases from \(2/3\) toward \(1/2\).
