For \(S=\langle n,n+1,\ldots,n+k\rangle\) and every modulus \(m\ge2\) dividing \(k\), the gap residues have an exact closed histogram and are equidistributed exactly when \(\gcd(n,m)=1\) and either \(n\equiv1\pmod m\) or \(m\mid\lceil(n-1)/k\rceil\). In the coprime regime their largest and smallest counts differ by at most two, a bound attained for every \(m\ge4\); when \(m\mid n\) the difference instead grows as \(\lceil(n-1)/k\rceil\).
