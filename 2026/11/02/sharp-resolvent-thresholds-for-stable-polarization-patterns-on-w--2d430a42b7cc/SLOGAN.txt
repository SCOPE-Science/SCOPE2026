In finite symmetric weighted opinion networks with sufficiently sharp clipped platform feedback, every attracting equilibrium is exactly a resolvent-consistent sign pattern, and all \(2^n\) patterns coexist if and only if the smallest diagonal entry of \(b(L+bI)^{-1}\) exceeds \((1+\varepsilon)/2\). This sharp local criterion distinguishes an isolated dissenting leaf from an isolated dissenting hub on the same star.
