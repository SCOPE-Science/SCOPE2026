For based evolution algebras of rank \(n\) over any finite chain ring of length \(s\), right nilpotency has the sharp bound \(ns+1\). Equality is controlled by a Hamiltonian path in the residue-unit graph and, for \(s\ge2\), one valuation-one return edge; both the complete nil-table census and the extremal-table census have explicit formulas depending only on \(n\), \(s\), and residue cardinality.
