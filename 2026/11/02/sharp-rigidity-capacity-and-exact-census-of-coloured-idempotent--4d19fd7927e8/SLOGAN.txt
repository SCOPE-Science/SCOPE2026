For \(q\) named exclusive colours and one idempotent unary function, rigidity has the sharp finite capacity \(q2^{q-1}(q+2)\), with the full unlabelled rigid census \(\prod_{j=0}^q(1+z^{j+1})^{q\binom qj}\). The same rooted-star classification distinguishes this finite rigidity spectrum from the unbounded, explicitly enumerable population of non-rigid models.
