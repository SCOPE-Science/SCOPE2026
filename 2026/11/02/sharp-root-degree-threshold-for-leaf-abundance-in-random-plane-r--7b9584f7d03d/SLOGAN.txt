In uniformly random plane-oriented increasing recursive trees, the average number of leaves given root degree \(r\) crosses its unconditional mean precisely when \(r+1\) crosses \(2\sqrt{n-1}\), with equality only at an explicit perfect-square family. The root-degree/leaf covariance grows like \(\sqrt{\pi n}/3\), although their correlation vanishes at rate \(n^{-1/2}\).
