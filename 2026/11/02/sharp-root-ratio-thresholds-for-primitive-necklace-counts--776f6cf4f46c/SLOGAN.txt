The primitive-necklace nth-root ratios become strictly decreasing at the **exact sharp thresholds** \(14,8,6,4\) for alphabet sizes \(q=2\), \(q=3\), \(q=4,5\), and \(q\ge6\), respectively. This proves the finite-field cutoffs proposed by Sun in 2012 and extends them to every integer alphabet size, by universal tail estimates and exact small-index certificates.
