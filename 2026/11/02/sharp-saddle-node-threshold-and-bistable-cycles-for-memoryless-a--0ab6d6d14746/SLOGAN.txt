For denominator-smoothed memoryless Adam on the strictly convex loss \(f_p(x)=|x|^p/p\), with \(p>2\), every real initial point converges to zero exactly when \(0<\eta<\eta_c=2(p-1)((p-2)\varepsilon)^{1/(p-1)}/(p-2)\). Above this boundary two symmetric period-two orbits emerge by a square-root collision, one repelling and one attracting, while the minimizer itself remains locally attracting.
