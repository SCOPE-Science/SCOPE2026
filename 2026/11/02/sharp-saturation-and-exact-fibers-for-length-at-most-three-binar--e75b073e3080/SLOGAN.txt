A capped-zero-gap representation gives exact inverse-fiber sizes for binary length-at-most-three substring-composition reads at every source length and fixed Hamming weight. The number of distinguishable profiles stabilizes sharply at \(n=3w+2\); for three ones its exact values are \(1,2,6,9,16,21,26,28\) at lengths \(3\) through \(10\), and \(29\) at every length \(n\ge11\).
