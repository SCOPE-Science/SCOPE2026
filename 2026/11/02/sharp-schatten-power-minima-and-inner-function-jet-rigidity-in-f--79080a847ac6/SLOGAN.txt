For every degree-\(N\) finite Blaschke product with fixed nonzero value \(B(0)=b\), the Schatten powers of its \(k\)-th compressed Hardy shift satisfy \(\operatorname{Tr}|S_B^k|^p\ge N-k+k|b|^p\) simultaneously for all finite \(p>0\). Equality is classified exactly by \(B(z)=(b+z^kC(z))/(1+\overline b\,z^kC(z))\), and a weighted sum of the initial Taylor-jet energies gives the precise Hilbert–Schmidt excess.
