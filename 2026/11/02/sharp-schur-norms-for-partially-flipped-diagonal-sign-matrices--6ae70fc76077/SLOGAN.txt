For a matrix whose only negative entries are \(k\) diagonal signs, its complex spectral Schur-multiplier norm is exactly \(1+k/(1+\sqrt{2k})\) when \(1\le k\le5\) and an unflipped coordinate is present, but \(3-4/k\) for every \(k\ge6\). This yields a sharp six-defect stabilization threshold: adding an untouched coordinate increases the multiplier norm only below that threshold, and further untouched coordinates never change it.
