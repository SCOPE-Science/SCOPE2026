For a Haar-uniform plane through the centroid of any equifacial tetrahedron, the triangular-section probability is exactly \(\frac4\pi\sum_{i=1}^3\arcsin\sqrt{p_i}-2\), where \(p_i\) are normalized squared orthogonal half-axes. This probability is strictly Schur-concave, uniquely maximized by the regular tetrahedron, and decays with a universal square-root law toward every nonvertex degenerate shape.
