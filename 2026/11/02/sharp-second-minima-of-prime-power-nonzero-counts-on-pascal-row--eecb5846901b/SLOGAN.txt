On each complete base-\(p\) length block \(p^h\le n<p^{h+1}\), the number of nonzero coefficients of the \(n\)th Pascal row modulo \(p^\alpha\), for \(\alpha\ge2\), has a unique minimum and an explicitly classified **second** minimum with every attaining row determined. The optimizers change sharply according as \(h<\alpha\), \(h=\alpha\), or \(h>\alpha\), with precisely classified binary exceptions.
