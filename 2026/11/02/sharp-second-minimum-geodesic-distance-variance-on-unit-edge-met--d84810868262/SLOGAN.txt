For any unit-edge metric tree with \(m\ge3\) edges, the variance of the distance between two independent arclength-uniform points is uniquely minimized by the star; among nonstars, the unique minimizer is the once-subdivided \((m-1)\)-arm star. The second-minimum lies exactly \(2(m-2)(3m^2-2m+12)/(3m^4)\) above the star value, by a nonnegative line-graph variance-surplus identity.
