For a run-and-tumble particle in a logarithmic trap, the origin-hitting time has finite mean but infinite variance throughout the sharp window \(v^2/2<\gamma\le3v^2/2\), provided the particle starts beyond the force-balanced turning point. Above that window the exact large-distance second moment is asymptotic to \(x^4/[4(1-D)(1-3D)]\), whereas inside the turning point every passage-time moment is finite.
