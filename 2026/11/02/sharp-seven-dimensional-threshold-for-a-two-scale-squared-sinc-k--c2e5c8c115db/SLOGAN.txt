The radial kernel \(\bigl(\sin(ar)/(ar)\bigr)^2\bigl(\sin(br)/(br)\bigr)^2\) is positive definite in Euclidean dimensions through seven precisely when \(\max(a,b)/\min(a,b)\le1+\sqrt3\); otherwise its sharp maximum dimension is five. The boundary is governed by a sign change in its seven-dimensional spherical spectral density, while an upward spectral jump forbids every eighth-dimensional lift.
