For the vertex-incidence class of all \(k\)-faces of a Boolean \(d\)-cube, \(k\ge3\), the maximum VC dimension \(k\) is attained exactly when \(d\ge2k-2\), with precisely \(2^d\binom dk\) maximal shattered supports, all of one private-coordinate shape. The Littlestone dimension never exceeds \(k+1\) and attains that value for every \(d\ge2k-1\), giving a sharp one-unit static/adaptive gap throughout that range.
