The maximum ambiguity from two deletions, among two one-deletion-distinguishable words, first reaches six at blocklength five for every \(q\ge3\), but only at length six for binary words. At the five-symbol nonbinary threshold, all \(q(q-1)(q-2)/2\) unordered extremal pairs are exactly \(\{(a,b,c,a,b),(b,a,c,b,a)\}\) with distinct symbols.
