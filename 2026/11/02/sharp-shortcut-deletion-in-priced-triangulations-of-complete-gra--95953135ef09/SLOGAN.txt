On the priced triangulation \(T(K_n)\), minimizing total effective resistance allocates zero conductance to every original clique edge exactly when \(P/Q\ge 8(n+1)/(n^2+n+2)\), despite permitting all edge weights to vary independently. At equal prices, this sharp topology transition occurs at \(n=8\), and the critical price ratio decays as \(8/n\).
