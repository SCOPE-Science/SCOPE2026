For the uniformly randomized two-stage SDIRK scheme, the chance of reversing positive scalar decay in one step jumps from zero at \(r=2\) with a sharp square-root onset and reaches one at \(r=1+\sqrt3\). When \(N\) steps operate in the window \(r=2+c/N^2\), their count of sign reversals converges to \(\mathrm{Poisson}(\sqrt c)\), despite samplewise A-stability.
