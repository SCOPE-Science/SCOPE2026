# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The statement fixes exact-length sums, distinct integer denominations, a negative member, and the progression \(h=12t+9\). The upper proof is reconstructed from the pigeonhole, residue, and parity argument and gives \(n_h^{\flat}(2)\le (h^2+6h+1)/4-1\) for all odd \(h\). The constructive lower proof uses \(A_b=\{-b,b,2b+1\}\) for even \(b\ge2\), coprime difference parameters, and explicit first/last residue representatives. Computations only cross-check the algebra.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Section 4 of van Doorn–Monticone–Nathanson (2026), arXiv:2610.19470v1, was read in full and proves the strict upper inequality, while Section 1 explicitly states that exact odd-order values remain open. Its treatment neither supplies the exhibited infinite progression of equality in the strict bound nor an implication giving the construction. Stöhr's classical nonnegative three-element optimum does not imply the signed result. Targeted bibliographic and prior-record searches found no equivalent or broader result. An independently obtained, unindexed construction or a derivation implicit in a source not inspected in full remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The exact signed odd-order optimum is explicitly left open in the source paper. This gives a sharp equality answer for an infinite and arithmetically natural progression of orders, explains the crucial coprimality parity condition, and closes the one-unit gap there. No claim is made for other odd congruence classes or uniqueness of extremizers.

**Scientific limits.** The paper's own universal upper bound is not new. The constructive lower bound and exact infinite progression are the contribution. The independent literature and priority assessment is incomplete; finite verifier results are not substituted for general proof.
