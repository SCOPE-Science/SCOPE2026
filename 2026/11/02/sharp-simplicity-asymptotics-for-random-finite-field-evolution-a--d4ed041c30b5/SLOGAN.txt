For a uniformly random invertible \(n\)-dimensional evolution-algebra structure matrix over a fixed finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the probability of nonsimplicity is asymptotic to \(2n(q-1)q^{-n}\); almost every failure comes from one vertex with no incoming or no outgoing edges to other vertices. Without conditioning on invertibility, the simplicity probability tends to the positive Euler product \(\prod_{j\ge1}(1-q^{-j})\), with an explicit first-order deficit.
