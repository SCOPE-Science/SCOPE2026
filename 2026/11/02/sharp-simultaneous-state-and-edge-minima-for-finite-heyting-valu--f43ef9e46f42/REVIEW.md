# Scientific review: simultaneous minimal resource theorem

## Correctness — PASS

For each finite Heyting algebra, characteristic formulas with exact tests separate all recursively defined modal types. The formula for modal box follows from the lattice adjunction \((\bigvee_i a_i)\to b=\bigwedge_i(a_i\to b)\), and the diamond formula from distributivity. The per-coordinate nonzero successor requirement gives the positive-edge lower bound without any assumptions on frame size or shape. Onto truncation and a section yield a type-indexed model meeting both the state and edge bounds, and equality in the edge bound proves the characterization. Independently executed checker results agree for five parameter sets including depth two and non-chain Boolean Heyting values. The proof, not finite enumeration, covers arbitrary finite Heyting algebras and all depths.

## Originality — PASS with historical risk

Lin--Liau's many-valued coalgebraic terminal-sequence and finite canonical-model methods already encompass the broad notion of stratified bounded-depth types; that foundational ingredient is explicitly credited. Their inspected primary results and the 2026 finite reduction/certificate paper do not provide the sharp joint minimum of states and positive-weight edges or classify its equality cases. The quantified optimization problem is distinct from individual-formula filtration bounds, classical S5 model quotients, and frame-validity classification. Published research record searches also found no covering exact edge-bound statement. This establishes originality relative to the materials actually inspected, not absolute chronological priority. The possibility that an older generic coalgebraic sparsification proposition implicitly entails this precise formula is a material residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact all-depth edge budget supplies a missing resource invariant for an all-type universal weighted model, and shows that the standard huge type tower can be simultaneously realized at its minimum possible vertex and nonzero-edge sizes. It is sharp in every finite Heyting truth algebra and every positive depth, with all minimizers structurally described. The quantity is natural because it counts nonzero transitions required to witness each distinguishable weighted successor-type observation, rather than an arbitrary finite search parameter. It informs the size and sparsity of finite depth-bounded semantic test models without claiming a complexity-class improvement.
