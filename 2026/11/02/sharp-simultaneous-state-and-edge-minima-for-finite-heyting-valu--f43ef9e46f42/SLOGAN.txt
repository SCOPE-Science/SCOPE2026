For arbitrary finite Heyting-valued weighted Kripke semantics with exact truth tests, a single model realizing every modal depth-\(h\) pointed type requires at least \(N_h\) states and exactly \(E_h=N_hN_{h-1}(1-1/q)\) nonzero accessibility pairs, and these two minima are simultaneously attainable. All joint minimizers have precisely one outgoing edge for each nonzero lower-type weight coordinate, with that exact weight; the underlying type stratification was already known from coalgebraic logic.
