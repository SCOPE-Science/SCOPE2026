For binary cyclic words with exactly one wildcard and length at least the target factor length, the shortest host covering every contiguous length-\(k\) word has length \(2^k-k+1\) for all \(k\ge2\), with the unique length-one exception. A de Bruijn-graph Eulerian obstruction and an explicit complement-trail construction prove sharpness, with exactly one repeated factor in every shortest host.
