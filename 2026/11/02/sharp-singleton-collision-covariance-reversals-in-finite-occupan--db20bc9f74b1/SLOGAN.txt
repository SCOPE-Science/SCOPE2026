In finite uniform occupancy, the covariance of the singleton count (Good–Turing estimator) and the matching-pair count (Simpson estimator) is negative, zero, or positive exactly according as \(n<2m\), \(n=2m\), or \(n>2m\). For two unequal category probabilities, an exact factorization gives the sharp reversal boundary \(np(1-p)=1\), extending the result to a full probability-dependent phase diagram.
