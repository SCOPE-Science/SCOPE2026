All high-threshold knowing-value observations of binary subset terms on a fixed finite world set are already determined by the singleton terms; their only posterior ambiguities are the class of all Dirac masses and swaps of unequal weights on two-point supports. The exact number of observable signatures on the denominator-\(d\) simplex grid is \(\binom{d+n-1}{n-1}-(n-1)-\binom n2\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor\), and all \(n\) singleton probes are necessary when \(n\ge3\).
