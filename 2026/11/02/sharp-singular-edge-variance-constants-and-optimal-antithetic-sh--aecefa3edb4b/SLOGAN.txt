For two uniformly shifted grids estimating a positive \(x^{-\alpha}g(x)\) integral, the exact second-moment boundary is \(\alpha<1/2\), no matter how the two shifts are coupled. Below that boundary, antithetic global shifts are asymptotically variance-optimal and reduce the leading variance by precisely \(2\sin^2(\pi\alpha/2)\) relative to two independent shifts at the same sampling cost.
