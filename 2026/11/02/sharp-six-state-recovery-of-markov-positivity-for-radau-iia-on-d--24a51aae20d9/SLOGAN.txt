Two-stage Radau IIA remains endpoint-positive at every step on uniform directed cycles of at most five states, but the six-cycle loses positivity for every step \(0<h<\rho\) and regains it exactly at \(\rho=0.208608859735\ldots\). The threshold is the unique positive root of \(h^5+15h^4+60h^3+90h^2+36h-12\).
