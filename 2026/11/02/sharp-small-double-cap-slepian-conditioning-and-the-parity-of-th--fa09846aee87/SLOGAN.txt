For two shrinking antipodal polar caps, the worst-observed spherical harmonic of fixed bandwidth \(L\) loses energy at the sharp rate \(\sin^{2L+2}\Theta\), with an explicit beta–central-binomial constant. Its azimuthal order is \(m=0\) for even \(L\) and \(|m|=1\) for odd \(L\); the optimal restriction condition number grows as \(\sin^{-L}\Theta\).
