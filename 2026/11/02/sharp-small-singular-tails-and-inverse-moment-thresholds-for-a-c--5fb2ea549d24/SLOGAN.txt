For the isotropic circular Fourier sketch restricted to two modes, the probability that its least Gram eigenvalue is below \(\varepsilon\) is asymptotic to \(\sqrt L\,\varepsilon^{(L-1)/2}/((2\pi)^{(L-1)/2}\Gamma((L+1)/2))\) for every fixed number \(L\ge2\) of samples. Its expected squared pseudoinverse norm is infinite for two or three columns and finite from four onward, unlike the three-column complex Gaussian sketch.
