For the one-dimensional tempered Langevin family with Weibull tail exponent \(\eta\), the worst-case expected time to hit zero has a sharp small-tempering trichotomy: a power divergence for \(\eta<2\), logarithmic divergence at \(\eta=2\), and a nonanalytic power cusp below a finite limit for \(\eta>2\), all with explicit universal constants. Uniform total-variation ergodicity holds at every positive tempering level \(0<\ell\le1/2\), whereas at zero it holds exactly when \(\eta>2\).
