In the cat-driven Möbius map, the first-order attracting-graph response has an exact sharp smoothness staircase: \(h_\varepsilon\in C^m\) precisely when \((1-\varepsilon)\lambda^m<1+\varepsilon\), with \(\lambda=(3+\sqrt5)/2\). Its gradient first becomes continuous at \(\varepsilon>1/\sqrt5\), where the sharp gradient norm is \(2\pi(1+\varepsilon)^2/(5\varepsilon^2-1)\).
