For every rectangular half-turn flat three-manifold, the squared shortest based-loop length at a random point has the exact law \(\min\{a^2,4h^2,h^2+U^2+V^2\}\), where \(U,V\) are independent uniforms on the transverse half-periods. Its spatial variance is at most \((a^4+b^4)/180\), with an **entire interval** of maximizing screw heights precisely when \(b/a<\sqrt2\), and a **unique** maximizing height otherwise.
