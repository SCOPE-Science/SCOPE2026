For the compact generalized Cesàro operator on \(\ell^p\), the norms of the simple-eigenvalue Riesz projections grow sharply as \(p^{-1/(2p)}q^{-1/(2q)}((1+t)/(1-t))^{m+1/p}/\sqrt{2\pi mt}\) for every fixed \(0<t<1\) and \(1\le p<\infty\). Its eigenvectors are complete and minimal, yet never form a Schauder basis in any ordering unless \(t=0\).
