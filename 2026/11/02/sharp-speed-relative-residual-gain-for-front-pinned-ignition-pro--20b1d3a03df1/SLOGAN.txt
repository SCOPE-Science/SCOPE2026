For the front-pinned ignition equation, forcing measured relative to the local orbit speed has an exact, layer-width-independent linearized response norm \(B_q\), attained uniquely *before* the front pin. Its worst-response location obeys \(q(1-y_q)\to4.300640970392\ldots\) and its sharp gain obeys \(qB_q\to1.302971455838\ldots\), whereas unweighted forcing can amplify like the inverse layer width.
