For every even phase-space dimension at least four, the exact equal-volume shape Hessian of Gaussian localization purity at a ball is diagonal in spherical harmonics, with strict stability modulo translations and a uniquely least-penalized degree-two family. Its sharp quadratic gap is a difference of adjacent modified Bessel functions, scaling like \(2\pi v_NR^{2N}\) for small balls and \((N+1)R^{N-3}/(4\pi)\) for large balls.
