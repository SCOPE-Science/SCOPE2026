For a harmonically trapped boson gas reset simultaneously at Poisson times, the exact stationary \(n\)-particle quantum purity decays as \(n^{-1/2}\) at fixed reset rate and develops a universal nontrivial limit precisely when the reset rate is proportional to \(\sqrt n\). The model remains separable across particle labels: its logarithmically growing order-two entropy combination quantifies collectively generated correlations rather than entanglement.
