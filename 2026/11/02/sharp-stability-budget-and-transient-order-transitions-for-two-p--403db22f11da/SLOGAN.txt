A two-periodic learning-rate schedule can stabilize one-step-delayed quadratic gradient descent for a normalized two-update sum approaching \(3\), whereas equal learning rates fail at \(2\). The spectrally fastest schedules undergo a sharp balancing-to-critical-damping transition at sum \(1/2\); their two orders have identical eigenvalues but different exact finite-horizon norms, and applying the smaller rate first is uniformly preferable.
