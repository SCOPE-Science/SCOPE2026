The edge-incidence class of all antipodal shortest paths in the Boolean \(d\)-cube has \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\lfloor2d/3\rfloor\) but \(\operatorname{Ldim}=d-1\), with the first gap at dimension \(4\) and an unbounded linear separation. Every shattered edge support lies on one shortest path, where it is characterized exactly by avoiding both endpoint-adjacent pairs and every three consecutive edges.
