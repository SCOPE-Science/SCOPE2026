# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Conductance parametrization makes centered symmetric similarity S-uu^T affine in edge flows, and its operator norm convex, even for reducible chains where extra eigenvalue 1 is retained. The stationary-measure-preserving edge-transitive group averages arbitrary feasible flows to a homogeneous alpha, proving unrestricted global reduction. Two-by-two Fourier/Bloch blocks yield nonstationary generator extreme eigenvalues G and 2(1+r); the exact scalar SLEM is max(1-alpha G, |1-2(1+r)alpha|). Intersection constrained by alpha<=1/2 yields piecewise alpha and r_m. Independently diagonalized full symmetric matrices and compared the formulas in 30 (m,r) regimes. Includes m=2 simple four-cycle and uniform r=1 periodic MH endpoint.
