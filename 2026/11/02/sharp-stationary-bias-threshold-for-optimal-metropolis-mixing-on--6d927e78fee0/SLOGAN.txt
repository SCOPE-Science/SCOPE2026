For a reversible nearest-neighbor chain on an even cycle with alternating stationary masses in ratio \(0<r\le1\), the usual Metropolis--Hastings walk is globally optimal in second-largest eigenvalue modulus if and only if \(r\le1-\tfrac12\sin^2(\pi/m)\), where the cycle has \(2m\) vertices. Above this sharp threshold, a strictly lazier homogeneous kernel achieves a smaller spectral radius, with an exact all-parameter formula.
