For random independent compositions of \(x\mapsto x/4\) and \(x\mapsto2x\pmod1\), the unique absolutely continuous stationary density exists throughout the positive-drift regime \(0<p<1/3\) and changes sharply from bounded to logarithmically unbounded at \(p=1/7\), then to a power singularity. As \(p\uparrow1/3\), its stationary logarithmic position has an exact mean-one exponential scaling limit.
