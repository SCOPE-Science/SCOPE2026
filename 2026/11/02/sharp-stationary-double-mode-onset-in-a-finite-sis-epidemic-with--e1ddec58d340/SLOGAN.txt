A finite SIS population with weak external infection acquires a second stationary infection-count mode precisely when pairwise transmission exceeds an exact finite-state threshold. Under external-infection scaling \(a_n=\lambda/n\), the onset occurs at \(b_c=1/n+2\sqrt{1-\lambda}/n^{3/2}+O(n^{-2})\), while its critical infected-count index is of order \(\sqrt n\).
