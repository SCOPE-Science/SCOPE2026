On the directed three-state Markov cycle with edge rates \(1:2:2\), positivity-preserving Strang exponential splitting has **smaller stationary total-variation error** than Lie splitting exactly for \(0<h<3.0237469700\ldots\), but **larger error** for every greater step; the threshold is the unique root of an explicit degree-seven polynomial under \(q=e^{-h/2}\). This reverses the expected accuracy ranking despite unconditional mass conservation and long-time convergence of both schemes.
