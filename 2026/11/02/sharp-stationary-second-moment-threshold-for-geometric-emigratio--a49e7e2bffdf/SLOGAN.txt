For Carper's geometric-emigration branching chain, the stationary second moment exists exactly when \(p>4/5\), and then equals \(6p(1-p)/[(4p-3)(5p-4)]\). The associated variance has an explicit rational form and a pole of strength \(24/25\) at the threshold, distinct from the earlier stationary-mean transition at \(p=3/4\).
