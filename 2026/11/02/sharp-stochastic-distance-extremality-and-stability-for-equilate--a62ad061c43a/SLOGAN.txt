Among all unit-edge metric trees of a fixed size, the star and path are respectively the smallest and largest in the stochastic ordering of distances between two independently arclength-uniform points, with equality in law only at those two trees. An exact near-zero CDF formula detects branching, and the optimal Wasserstein gap from the star among all nonstar trees is \(2(m-2)/m^2\), attained precisely by a once-subdivided star.
