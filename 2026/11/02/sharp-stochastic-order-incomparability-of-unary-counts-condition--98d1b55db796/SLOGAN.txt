Conditioning a uniform plane tree on successive root degrees produces *no* stochastic ordering of its nonroot unary-vertex counts except at the final deterministic boundary: for every \(1\le k\le n-2\), the upper tails reverse direction at the consecutive thresholds \(n-k-1\) and \(n-k\). The exact lower-threshold tail ratio is \((2n-k-1)/(k+1)>1\), giving a complete sharp adjacent classification.
