For a damped two-state rotation observed through one noisy scalar coordinate at \(0,h,2h\), the sharp all-parameter Fisher-determinant envelope is \(C_3(\omega/a)^2\), with \(C_3=0.2493790619\ldots\), and the bound is attained asymptotically as \(\omega/a\to0\). In that limit the D-optimal spacing is uniquely determined and obeys \(h_*=0.6312899581\ldots/a-0.2388090255\ldots\,\omega^2/a^3+O(\omega^4/a^5)\), whereas resonant nonoptimal spacings can have zero information.
