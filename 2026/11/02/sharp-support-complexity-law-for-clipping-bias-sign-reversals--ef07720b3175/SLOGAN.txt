For zero-mean scalar stochastic-quadratic gradient noise with \(m\) distinct nonzero magnitudes, the direction of the population clipping bias can reverse at most \(m-2\) times as the cap increases, and an explicit \(m\)-atom rational law attains every reversal. Those same sharp reversals occur in the unique, globally attractive population clipped-gradient equilibria; in particular, three gradient-noise magnitudes are necessary and sufficient for one direction reversal.
