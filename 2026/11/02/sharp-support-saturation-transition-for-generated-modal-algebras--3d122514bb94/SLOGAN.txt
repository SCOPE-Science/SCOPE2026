For randomly colored finite reflexive-transitive chains with \(r\) worlds per cluster and \(k\) Boolean generators, the generated modal algebra has finitely many atoms asymptotically when \(r\ge 2^k\), but has linearly many atoms when \(r<2^k\). An exact support-set reduction gives the full finite distribution and a rational linear-growth rate, including \(159/112\) for two generators and two worlds per cluster.
