For a stationary three-type expiring coupon collector, uniform sampling uniquely maximizes new-completion entry frequency at window lengths \(3,4,5\), but is a strict local minimum for every length \(M\ge6\). At the first asymmetric window \(M=6\), all global maximizers are permutations of \((a,a,1-2a)\), with \(a\in(1/5,1/4)\) the unique root of \(42a^4-40a^3+35a^2-15a+2=0\) in that interval.
