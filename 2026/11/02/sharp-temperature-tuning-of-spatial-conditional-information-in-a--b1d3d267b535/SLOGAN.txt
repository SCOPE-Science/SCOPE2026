In an inertial active Gaussian harmonic chain, adding any positive thermal noise creates conditional velocity dependence between every pair of sites, even though the zero-thermal field is nearest-neighbor Markov. For each distance \(n\ge2\), the information conditioned on all other velocities has a unique optimal thermal temperature, growing as \(T_n/m\sim (T_a/m)n/\sqrt{A^2-B^2}\), with peak partial correlation \(\sim r_0^n/(en)\).
