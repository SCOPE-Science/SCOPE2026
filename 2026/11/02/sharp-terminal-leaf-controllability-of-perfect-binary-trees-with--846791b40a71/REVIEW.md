# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-height result follows from an orthogonal radial/antisymmetric Jacobi decomposition, a verified second-order characteristic-polynomial recurrence, the exact unit-eigenspace multiplicity, and a constructive PBH argument. The leaf equation separates the unit eigenvalue from all other eigenvalues; the pair-sum map has a kernel consisting exactly of independent bottom sibling differences. Its image admits a full-rank coordinate pivot selection, and the eigenspace dimension gives the matching lower bound. The separately extracted verifier tests finite exact Kalman ranks over two prime fields for heights 1 through 6. Finite tests do not establish the unbounded statement; the proof does.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit priority risks

He–Liu–Strang already identify related four-level spectral resonances and the limiting unit-eigenspace share of a different regular rooted tree. The present spectral recurrence is not claimed as a new general phenomenon. Dai (2021) is particularly relevant: its exact Cayley-tree leader-selection work concerns a three-branch-root tree of order \(3\cdot2^g-2\), not the two-branch-root perfect binary tree of order \(2^{h+1}-1\), and it does not state the claimed leaf-only closed formula. General tree PBH/critical-set tests and cograph methods also do not directly give this all-height terminal-only minimum and construction. Published-paper and semantic-index searches found no identical or dominating inspected statement, but absence from search is not a proof of priority. Related specialist tree-leader articles may merit additional human comparison.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Exactly minimizing *terminal-only* independent actuators is a natural constrained controllability problem. The theorem gives a complete answer at all heights, exhibits surprising extra input requirements at heights congruent to three modulo four, and shows that the classical unit-eigenspace lower bound is attainable with leaf-only inputs. For example height three needs five rather than the four inputs suggested by one-per-sibling-pair symmetry breaking. The limiting input density is determined exactly.

## Scientific limits

The graph has one fixed combinatorial Laplacian with unit edge weights and a root of degree two. No all-weight or arbitrary-tree robustness is proved. The pivot construction is algorithmic, not a uniform lexical formula for selected leaf labels. Numerical checks are supporting finite evidence. The nearby root-degree-three Cayley-tree literature is a remaining specialist-priority risk.
