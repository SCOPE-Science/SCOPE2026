The exact minimum number of independent terminal-leaf controls for a unit-edge perfect binary tree of height \(h\ge1\) is \(\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor(h-1)/4\rfloor}2^{h-1-4j}+\mathbf1_{\{h\equiv3\pmod4\}}\). One leaf from each sibling pair detects all non-unit eigenmodes; a precisely counted set of extra leaf pivots resolves the four-level unit-eigenvalue resonances, giving an asymptotic optimum of \(8/15\) of the leaves.
