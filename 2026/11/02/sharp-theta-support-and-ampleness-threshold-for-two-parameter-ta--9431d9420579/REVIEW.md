# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The cocycle has positive quadratic growth and generic degree \(d\). The Fourier recursion follows from the published formula (2.4) in Kallal [1]. For each residue class modulo \(Nd\), the unique integral theta generator normalizes the two independent convex quadratic valuations; simultaneous minima give the exact image of restriction. At equality \(|e-f|=d\) this image has dimension one in every positive power, obstructing ampleness. In the open chamber, the theta generators define a morphism to projective space whose geometric fibers are embeddings (the open torus is separated by consecutive characters and the elliptic fibers by degree at least three), giving ampleness. See RESULT.md for quantifiers, argument and limits. An exact-integer verifier tested the valuation combinatorics for 2,268 parameter quadruples.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest source is Kallal, arXiv:2610.26133v1, Section 4.1, giving the degree-one wall case \((1,1,0)\) and the Fourier recursion. The proposed result computes all central restriction images and classifies ampleness for the three-integer family; that exact classification is not asserted there. The related Fourier expansion preprint provides the underlying technique and does not itself make the wall comparison. Searches for the exact numerical wall and theta-support formulation, plus mathematical-record and accessible prior-ledger comparisons, found no equivalent claim. An implicit interpretation within general Mumford/Faltings--Chai relative-ampleness or toric-model theory remains possible and warrants external priority review.

## Value — PASS
The line bundle in the motivating counterexample is a boundary point of a complete sharp chamber \(|e-f|<d\). The classification distinguishes ampleness from mere generic ampleness in every generic degree and gives a geometric explanation in terms of lost special-fiber theta characters; the all-power dimension formula supplies a concrete invariant of degeneration, not only another counterexample.

It does not establish an arbitrary-base criterion. Moret-Bailly and Lan are used through Kallal's discussion rather than independent reinspection.
