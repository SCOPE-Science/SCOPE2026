For the two-parameter Tate degeneration with period \(uv\), a generic-degree-\(d\) theta line bundle with monomial twists \(u^e\) and \(v^f\) is ample on the whole smooth semiabelian family exactly when \(|e-f|<d\). Its complete central-fiber theta-support spaces give the sharp wall and explain Kallal's boundary counterexample.
