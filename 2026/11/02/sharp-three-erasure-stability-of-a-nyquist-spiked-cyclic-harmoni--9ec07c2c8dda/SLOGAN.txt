For the cyclic Parseval frame with Fourier frequencies \(\{0,1,m\}\) on \(\mathbb Z_{2m}\), the sharp loss from erasing any three samples is \(\Lambda_m/(2m)\), where \(\Lambda_m=(9+\sqrt{9+8(1+2\cos(2\pi/m))^2})/2\). The unique worst geometric pattern, up to translation, erases three step-two sites of one parity; every three-erasure pattern is recoverable exactly when \(m\ge4\).
