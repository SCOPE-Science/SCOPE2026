For every weighted friendship network with \(m\ge2\) identical triangles, the minimum number of independent vertex actuators is exactly \(m-1\), \(m\), or \(m+1\), according to whether its two hub-spoke conductances differ, coincide without resonance, or coincide at the sharp internal-edge resonance. Every controllable vertex set is characterized, with additional exact nodal obstructions at \(b=ma\) and \(b=mc\) when the two spoke weights differ.
