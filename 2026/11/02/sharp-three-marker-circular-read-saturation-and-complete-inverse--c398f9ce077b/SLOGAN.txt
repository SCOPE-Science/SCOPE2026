For circular binary strings with exactly three ones, the complete ordered length-\(k\) factor multiset has exactly \(k+(k-1)^2-\lfloor(k-1)^2/4\rfloor\) possible profiles for every \(n\ge3k\), but exactly one fewer at \(n=3k-1\). The full inverse fibers are classified by whether the one or two short intermarker spacings are absent, unordered or ordered, with explicit sizes for every class.
