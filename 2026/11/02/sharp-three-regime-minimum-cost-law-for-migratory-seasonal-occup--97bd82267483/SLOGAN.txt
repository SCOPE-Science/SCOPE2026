For two-season migratory patch occupancy with linear colonization-rate costs, the least expense needed to meet prescribed breeding and nonbreeding occupancy targets has a unique closed-form policy in exactly three regimes: breeding target alone binds, both targets bind, or nonbreeding target alone binds. When weighted extinction costs are equal and the breeding target is \(1/2\), the two phase boundaries are \(\sqrt2-1\) and \((\sqrt5-1)/2\).
