For monomial generalized Volterra companions on the two-dimensional Bergman ball, anisotropic contractions approaching the identity have an exact three-regime Schatten norm blow-up, governed by \(q=kp/2\). The critical line \(kp=2\) produces a logarithmic factor with sharp coefficient \((1-a)/p^2\), while the subcritical and supercritical coefficients are explicit gamma-function and reciprocal constants.
