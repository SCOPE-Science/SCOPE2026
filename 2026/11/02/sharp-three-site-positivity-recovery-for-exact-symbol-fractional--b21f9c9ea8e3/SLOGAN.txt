The three-site zero-exterior restriction of the exact Fourier-symbol fractional Laplacian preserves nonnegative states at every positive time for \(0<\alpha\leq1\), but for \(1<\alpha\leq2\) it first produces a negative extreme-site response and then recovers positivity at a unique, explicitly determined sharp time. Near \(\alpha=1\), this recovery time is linear in \(\alpha-1\) and the largest negative excursion is quadratic, with exact constants.
