Every unequal binary word and its two-place cyclic rotation can be separated by a complete DFA with at most three states, and three states are necessary for precisely \((5\cdot2^{n-2}-8)/3\) words at even length \(n\ge4\) and \((5\cdot2^{n-2}-10)/3\) words at odd length \(n\ge3\). Thus the exact worst-case complexity is three from length four onward, and the proportion requiring three states tends to \(5/12\).
