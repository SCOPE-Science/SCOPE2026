# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof classifies all residue fields and all ring lengths, establishes exact exceptional targets, and is independent of the companion finite arithmetic verifier. The verification script checks sixteen sample quotient rings and does not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with substantial residual priority risk.** Gu (2026) develops general exact power-value subset-sum formulas and sufficient positivity, but does not state this exact finite ternary iff threshold. The already-known exact Li–Wan finite-field formula covers the field part. The original 2019 and 2023 Ding–Zhou texts were inaccessible during the check; their exact ring identities could imply some of the length-two instances. A general exact formula logically determines the counts, so the claimed contribution is a sharp explicit classification rather than a new method for computing all subset-sum counts.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The all-target ternary coverage question is natural for the distinct power-value set of the motivating work. A sharp global classification, the m=2 versus m=3 ternary jump, and the first available binary nonunit layer supply meaningful structural boundaries. Exact small-field exceptions explain why subset-size positivity need not be monotone.

**Closest literature.** Gu, arXiv:2610.28837v1, Sections 3, 5, 8–9 and Appendices A–B; Li and Wan, arXiv:0708.2456 (its exact field identity reproduced by Gu); Ding–Zhou, J. Number Theory 205 (2019) and 252 (2023). The latter two full texts remain an acknowledged originality-access risk.

**Limitations.** Index-one condition and prime-to-characteristic exponents are essential. No higher-subset-size criterion, independent audit, or independent priority certification is asserted.
