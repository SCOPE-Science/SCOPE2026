With at most three alternatives, any \(n\) strict preference rankings can be thinned to any \(k\le n\) of the original voters while meeting simultaneous floor/ceiling quotas for every pairwise comparison. Four alternatives are sharply different: among the \(17{,}550\) anonymous four-voter profiles, exactly \(690\) have no pairwise-proportional two-voter subsample, forming \(30\) candidate-relabeling classes.
