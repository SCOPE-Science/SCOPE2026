For an exponentially decaying energy, the exact \(|\dot E|^p\)-equidistributed grid has strictly increasing adjacent step ratios, and a strict cap \(R>1\) holds exactly when \(N>u_R(D^p-1)\). This yields a sharp minimum step count scaling like \(D\) for the energy-rate monitor and like \(\sqrt D\) for its square-root alternative, under the same stipulated ratio cap.
