For a quantum walker initially on a graph backbone, the optimal total transfer into one pendant attached per vertex is controlled by a local spectral bound and an exact parity test on frequency relations; the bound is sharp for all but countably many coupling strengths. On a three-site path, a \(2:1\) resonance cuts the achievable maximum from its spectral ceiling \(5/8\) to \(1/2\), and obstructing resonances are dense.
