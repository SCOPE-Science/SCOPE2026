For the Newton kernel-based spherical distribution, the exact total-variation error of approaching the integrable boundary cusp is \(\frac{d-2}{2}\varepsilon-(d-2)c_d\varepsilon^{3/2}+O_d(\varepsilon^2)\), with an explicit spherical cap attaining the supremum. Its first signed variation converges weakly to \(\frac{d-2}{2}(\nu_1-\delta_\mu)\), revealing a point-mass boundary layer even though the limiting probability law itself is not atomic.
