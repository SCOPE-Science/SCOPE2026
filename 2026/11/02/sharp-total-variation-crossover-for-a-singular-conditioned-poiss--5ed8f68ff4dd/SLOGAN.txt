For a conditioned \(2\times2\) Poisson table with vanishing diagonal odds, the Bessel approximation has an exact total-variation transition at \(z\asymp n^{4/3}\): its critical profile is \(2\Phi(c^{3/4}/\sqrt2)-1\). The approximation is asymptotically exact below this scale and maximally separated above it while \(z=o(n^2)\).
