When the contraction factor in Poisson partial resetting approaches one, a suitably normalized stationary symmetric \(\alpha\)-stable motion approaches its stable limit at a **sharp total-variation rate** \(\frac14\|\partial_v^2p_{\alpha,v}|_{v=1}\|_1(1-c^\alpha)/(1+c^\alpha)\), with a universal signed density correction. The Cauchy and Gaussian endpoint coefficients are explicit and positive.
