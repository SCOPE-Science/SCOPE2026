In the clean non-Hermitian diamond cage, three perfectly real flat energy bands coexist with a sharp transient coherent-amplitude gain \(G_N=Q_N+\sqrt{Q_N^2-1}\), where \(Q_N^2=\cosh^4h-\sinh^2h\min_m\cos^2(2\pi m/N)\). The exact infinite-ring bound is attained by finite rings precisely when their cell count is divisible by four; nonresonant rings have a strict, order-\(N^{-2}\) deficit.
