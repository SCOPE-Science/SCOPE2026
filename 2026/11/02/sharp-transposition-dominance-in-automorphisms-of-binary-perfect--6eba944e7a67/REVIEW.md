# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Perfectness and the independent-square relation force every automorphism to be monomial; over the binary field it is a permutation. The exact block form for matrices commuting with a single transposition gives \(2^{2n-3}|\mathrm{GL}_{n-2}(\mathbb F_2)|\) invertible centralizer elements. The permutation-module gcd formula controls the centralizer dimensions for every support size; summing support strata yields \(o(n^2 2^{-2n})\) for non-transpositions. Products of two distinct transpositions have support three or four and give a negligible second Bonferroni term. These steps together prove both the leading coefficient and the conditional cyclic-order-two conclusion. The finite direct-enumeration checker passed for \(n=2,3,4\); it is not used as a substitute for the asymptotic proof.

## Originality
PASS relative to inspected primary text and available search records, with explicitly noted priority risk. Sriwongsa–Zou's full public text proves the automorphism/graph reduction and studies prescribed automorphism groups, but the inspected introduction, Sections 2–4 and search within the complete transcription reveal no probabilistic rarity formula. Wei–Zou's 2023 abstract confirms a general Burnside-style class enumeration and explicit dimensions 2–4; this is stronger as an *exact orbit counting problem* but does not itself give the event probability or conditional stabilizer profile. Its full text was inaccessible in the checked arXiv/open routes, so an equivalent statement cannot be ruled out. Costoya–Mayorga–Viruel's publicly readable text concerns realizability of permutation representations, not random asymptotic prevalence. Searches for binary invertible digraph automorphisms, simultaneous permutation similarity and published mathematical-record aliases found no match for the exact conditional transposition-dominance conclusion. Unseen papers remain a genuine priority risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Existing existence and orbit-classification results do not quantify how frequently symmetry occurs in high-dimensional perfect evolution algebras. The coefficient \(n(n-1)\) is sensitive to invertibility conditioning, and the conditional \(C_2\) assertion identifies the overwhelmingly prevalent *type* of exceptional stabilizer. This is a natural high-dimensional structural statistic, not an arbitrary small parameter slice or an immediate consequence of a low-dimensional table. It is confined to binary fields, and the novelty of this asymptotic should be independently assessed against the full Wei–Zou treatment.
