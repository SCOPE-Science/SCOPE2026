In the fixed-point-free cosine/sine-memory map, the periodic-orbit threshold is exactly \(\beta=1\): no two-cycles occur at or below it, whereas every positive forcing above it generates unbounded towers of primitive two-cycles. Each sufficiently large even-integer resonance produces exactly two local branches, displaced by \(\pm\alpha|\cos(2n)|/(\pi n\sqrt{\beta^2-1})+O(n^{-2})\).
