On an odd periodic Fourier grid, moving one node by less than half a mesh preserves all exact interpolatory quadrature weights, but moving just two neighboring nodes toward the intervening fixed site creates exactly one negative weight past the sharp threshold \(\delta_K=1-(K/\pi)\arcsin\sqrt{1-\cos(\pi/K)}\). For every odd \(K\ge5\), the threshold increases toward \(1-1/\sqrt2\), even though the resulting absolute-weight amplification vanishes as \(K\to\infty\) for fixed displacement.
