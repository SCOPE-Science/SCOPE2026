For a harmonically trapped isotropic run-and-rest swimmer, the stationary squared radius converges in mean square as dimension grows to an explicit two-beta perpetuity. Its near-zero probability has sharp exponent \(\lambda/(2b)\), set by the resting-state exit rate, while its near-one probability has exponent \(\mu/b\), set by the running-state exit rate, with exact positive constants for all positive rates.
