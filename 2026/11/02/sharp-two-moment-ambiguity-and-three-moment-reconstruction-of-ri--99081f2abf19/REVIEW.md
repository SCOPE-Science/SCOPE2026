## Mathematical value

**PASS.** This is a complete classification for a natural smooth two-parameter family of embedded genus-one surfaces under a basic geometric-probability observable. It identifies a sharp interval where two noncongruent geometries are indistinguishable by the first two even moments, supplies an exact rational ambiguity involution, and establishes both the minimal number of *consecutive* even moments needed for reconstruction and an optimal stability coefficient. The result concerns surface-area sampling and ambient Euclidean chords; it is not a claim about arbitrary surfaces or empirical-data error bounds.
