For two Hermitian observations, the largest hidden pure-state expectation difference is exactly twice the operator-norm distance from the observed real affine span, attained by orthogonal states. Three scaled Pauli-block observations violate this rigidity sharply: the hidden oscillation is \(4r/(1+r)\) while the operator distance stays \(1\), and at \(r=0\) the hidden quantity is a nonlinear function of the observations.
