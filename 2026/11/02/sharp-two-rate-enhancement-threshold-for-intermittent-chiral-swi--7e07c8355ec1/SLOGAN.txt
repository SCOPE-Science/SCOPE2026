For chiral stop-and-go swimmers, **optimizing both run interruption and resumption rates** enhances diffusion exactly when resting angular diffusion \(d\) exceeds moving angular diffusion \(a\) and \(|\omega|>a\sqrt{(d+a)/(d-a)}\). Above this sharp threshold, the optimal diffusion is approached only by infinitely rapid switching, with a uniquely determined rate ratio and a high-chirality \(|\omega|^{2/3}\) scaling law; the underlying diffusion formula was already known.
