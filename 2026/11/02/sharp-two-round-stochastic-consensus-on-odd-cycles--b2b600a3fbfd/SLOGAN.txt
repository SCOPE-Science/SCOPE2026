On every odd cycle with at least five vertices, the fastest two-round schedule of spatially homogeneous stochastic nearest-neighbor steps uniquely combines a zero-holding round with edge probability \(1/(a+b-2)\) in the other round, where \(a,b\) are the extreme positive Laplacian eigenvalues. Its exact nonconstant contraction is \((2-a)(b-2)/[2(a+b-2)]\), strictly smaller than the squared optimal stationary one-step factor.
