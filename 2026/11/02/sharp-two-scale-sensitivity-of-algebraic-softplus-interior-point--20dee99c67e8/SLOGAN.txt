For the algebraic-softplus interior-point KKT matrix, shrinking the barrier by a fixed factor \(\sigma\) changes its Newton matrix by at most the **sharp**, barrier-independent amount \((1-\sigma^{1/3})/(2\sqrt{1+\sigma^{1/3}+\sigma^{2/3}})\); moving the constraint-state coordinate by \(h\) at fixed barrier has sharp modulus \(|h|/\sqrt{h^2+16\mu}\). These exact two-scale laws yield a sufficient frozen-factorization residual certificate and show why a vanishing barrier alone cannot guarantee small matrix changes near the transition layer.
