# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The n≥13 GP(n,2) graph has two n-vertex Fourier layers. For type-uniform conductances a,b,c the mode k is the Hermitian 2×2 block H_k=[[a e_k+c,-c],[-c,b d_k+c]], with e_k=4sin²(πk/n),d_k=4sin²(2πk/n). Its lower eigenvalue and the positive k0 mode 2c give exact λ2. A Fourier trial vector with amplitudes (1+s)ω^j on outer vertices and ω^j inner, s=sqrt(d1), gives universal upper cap d1/((1+s)²+1) because its energy depends only on orbit-averaged edge weights even for arbitrary nonuniform designs. For n≥13, e1(1+s)²<d1, giving strict inequality if outer average a>0; for a=0 even n disconnects, odd n has a strict high-frequency trial bound. Taylor expansion yields cap 8π²/n²-32π³/n³+O(n^-4). The explicit construction a=α/n²,c=2π/n,b=1-a-c with α above the even/odd threshold makes low-frequency k±1 uniquely controlling eventually; high-frequency near n/2 have limiting gap 2α (even) or 2α+2π² (odd), and intermediate escaping modes cannot beat k1. For arbitrary near-optimal designs, upper-trial deficit forces a=o(1/n); the k1 block forces nc→2π, and high-frequency trials force liminf n²a≥4π² (even) or 3π² (odd). These are two-term asymptotic, not exact finite optimization or uniqueness. Independent mode calculations for n=13,14,31,32,100,101,400,401 corroborated the leading coefficient and strict cap.

The sharp algebraic-connectivity under an unconstrained per-edge budget has a nontrivial boundary-layer design in which spoke conductance is order 1/n, stabilizing outer conductance 1/n² and an essential even/odd threshold.
