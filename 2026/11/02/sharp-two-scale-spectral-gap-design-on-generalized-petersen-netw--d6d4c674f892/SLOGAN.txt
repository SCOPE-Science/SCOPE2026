For independently weighted generalized Petersen graphs \(GP(n,2)\) with total conductance budget \(n\), the largest attainable algebraic connectivity is \(8\pi^2/n^2-32\pi^3/n^3+O(n^{-4})\), although every finite optimum lies strictly below an explicit matching two-term spectral cap. Asymptotically optimal designs require spoke conductance of scale \(2\pi/n\) while an outer-ring stabilizer of order \(n^{-2}\) suffices and its necessary lower coefficient is parity-dependent (\(4\pi^2\) for even sizes and \(3\pi^2\) for odd sizes).
