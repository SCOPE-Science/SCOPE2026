For smooth heterogeneous two-resource continuum congestion games with an interior pure cutoff, vanishing Gumbel noise changes the induced deterministic allocation at the sharp \(\sigma^2\) scale but leaves the logit choice profile at the sharp \(L^1\)-error scale \(\sigma\), with exact leading constants. A genuinely non-potential linear-impact example has cutoff shift \(-\pi^2\sigma^2/(6\sqrt{11})+o(\sigma^2)\) and a strictly decreasing cutoff at every positive noise level.
