For two initial adopters on a five-site path with spontaneous adoption rate \(a>0\) and neighbor imitation rate \(b>0\), minimizing mean full-adoption time switches exactly once: seed both endpoints when \(b/a<(11+\sqrt{249})/8\), but seed positions \(\{1,4\}\) or \(\{2,5\}\) above that threshold. No shorter path with two seeds exhibits such a reversal.
