For every uniform \(r\)-edge subdivision of \(K_n\), the least real edge-\(\ell_1\) circulation projection norm is \(3(n-2)/n\), while the complementary cut-space constant is \(1+3(n-2)/n-2(n-2)/(nr)\). For \(n\ge5\), replacing every original edge by a path of length at least two reverses which of the two projection problems is costlier.
