A sharp tensor-spectral formula yields the fastest symmetric stochastic mixing on products of edge-transitive graphs; local Fiedler-edge independence upgrades symmetry-reduced optimality to **unique unrestricted** optimality. On every mixed product of \(k\) Petersen graphs and a cycle \(C_m\), \(m\ge4\), the unique optimum has positive holding exactly when \(k=1\), and no holding for \(k\ge2\).
