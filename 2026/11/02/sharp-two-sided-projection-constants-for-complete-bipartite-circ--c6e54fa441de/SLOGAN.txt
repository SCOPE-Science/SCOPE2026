For real edge flows of every complete bipartite graph, double centering is simultaneously a least-norm projection onto circulations and a projection with the least-norm complement, for every \(\ell_p\) exponent. At both endpoints the respective exact costs are \(4(m-1)(n-1)/(mn)\) and \(1+2(m-1)(n-1)/(mn)\), with equality of the two costs only for part sizes \((3,4)\) or \((4,3)\).
