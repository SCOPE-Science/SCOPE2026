For the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\) with \(n\ge5\), the sharp relative \(\ell_1\)-projection constants of its cut and circulation spaces are \(\frac{(n-2)(3n-1)}{n(n-1)}\) and \(\frac{2(2n-5)}{n-1}\), attained simultaneously by complementary orthogonal projections. Both are uniquely label-permutation-invariant, and their norms tend to \(3\) and \(4\).
