For \(n\) independent uniform great circles on the unit sphere, the squared area of the area-weighted cell has an exact three-term asymptotic: \(\mathbb E A_n^2=64\pi^6(1-10/n+(65-28\pi^2/11)/n^2+O(n^{-3}))/(7n^4)\). The \(-28\pi^2/11\) contribution is the spherical-curvature correction beyond the planar limiting law.
