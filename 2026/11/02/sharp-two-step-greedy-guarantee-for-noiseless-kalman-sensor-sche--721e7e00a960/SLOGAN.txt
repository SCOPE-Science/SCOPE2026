In a two-state, two-step Kalman scheduling problem with exact coordinate observations, the sharp worst-case cost of greedy sensing relative to optimal lookahead is exactly \(1+\max\{a_1^2,a_2^2\}\). This yields a tight factor \(2\) for nonexpansive diagonal dynamics, but no universal factor when state growth is unrestricted, together with an explicit criterion for every strict greedy failure.
