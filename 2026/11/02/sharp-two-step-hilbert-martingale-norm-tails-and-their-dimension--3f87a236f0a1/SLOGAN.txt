The optimal terminal norm tail of any two-step Hilbert-valued martingale with difference bounds \(a,b>0\) has a complete, attained three-phase law: probability one up to \(\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), then \(2ab/[x^2-(a-b)^2]\) until \(a+b\), then zero. An asymmetric transverse second-step law attains every strictly interior sharp value, conditional symmetry reduces the upper range to probability \(1/2\), and a fixed quadratic proxy yields an attained Chebyshev bound.
