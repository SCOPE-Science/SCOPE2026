For optically thin dust with a known common emissivity index, the exact three-monochromatic-band color region at frequencies \(\nu,2\nu,4\nu\) is \(0<U<1/2\), \(U^3/(1-2U+2U^2)\le V<U/2\). Its lower boundary consists precisely of isothermal spectra, every strict interior point has an exact two-distinct-temperature realization, and two-band measurements cannot distinguish temperature mixtures.
