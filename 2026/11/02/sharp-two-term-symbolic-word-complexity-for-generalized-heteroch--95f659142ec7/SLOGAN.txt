Generalized heterochaos baker maps with \(m\ge2\) bracket pairs have an exact algebraic generating function for length-\(n\) admissible itineraries, with sharp asymptotic \(A_m(n)=(m+1)^n\bigl(\frac{(m-1)^2}{m(m+1)}n+\frac{m+1}{m}\bigr)+O_m((2\sqrt m)^n n^{-3/2})\). For the original four-symbol map this rigorously yields the previously numerical growth-ratio law \(A_2(n)/A_2(n-1)=3+3/n+O(n^{-2})\).
