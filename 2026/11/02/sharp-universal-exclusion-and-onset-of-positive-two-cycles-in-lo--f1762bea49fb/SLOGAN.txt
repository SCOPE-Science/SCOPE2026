For every \(0<\lambda\leq3\), the logistic-prey map \((x,y)\mapsto(x(\lambda-x-y),yh(x))\) has no strictly positive prime-two orbit for **any** positive nondecreasing predator recruitment \(h\). The bound is sharp: for each \(\lambda>3\), a strictly positive prime-two orbit can be constructed using affine recruitment \(h(x)=a+\gamma x\) with \(0<a<1\) and \(\gamma>0\).
