For degree-one additive Fourier sampling on the proper \(q\)-colourings of a cycle of length \(n\), the exact Gram spectrum is a rational Potts transfer-matrix transform of the cycle's spatial frequencies. Except for the singular ternary triangle, the sharp universal squared condition number of the full first Fourier band is \(\frac32(4+\sqrt5)\), attained uniquely by the ternary pentagon; the inverse spatial covariance is a cyclic nearest-neighbour operator plus a rank-one correction.
