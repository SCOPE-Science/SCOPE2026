For every subnormal unilateral weighted shift with first two squared weights \(m,s\) and squared norm at most \(R\), a single explicit two-atomic Berger measure simultaneously maximizes every subsequent squared weight, with exact formula \(\alpha_n^2\le U_n(m,s,R)\). Requiring exact norm \(\sqrt R\) removes the attainable lower endpoint at each later index, and the sharp near-flat upper extremizer develops a logarithmically delayed transition toward \(R\).
