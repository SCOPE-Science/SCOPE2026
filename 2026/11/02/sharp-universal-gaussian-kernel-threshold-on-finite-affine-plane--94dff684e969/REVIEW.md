# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness
The incidence-block formula is derived from the defining axioms of a finite affine plane. Its three invariant zero-sum sectors exhaust all dimensions. Parallel-class contrast eigenvalues change sign at the claimed exponent. The paired-sector determinant stays positive up to that threshold except for the explicitly identified binary endpoint. The global sector is bounded strictly below zero by concavity and a verified quadratic inequality. The nullspace and Euclidean embedding dimensions follow from the exact sector multiplicities. Independent small-order constructions, shortest-path reconstruction and numerical spectra corroborate, but do not replace, these arguments.

## Originality
The general finite-metric negative-type framework and Schoenberg correspondence are classical. The closest inspected primary texts give general theorems and, respectively, examples for complete bipartite graphs; neither states the affine-plane incidence threshold or its exceptional binary endpoint nullity. A related published finding about affine-plane incidence Rips homotopy has the same graph but a different invariant and does not imply this metric-kernel theorem. The review is bounded: older work on resolvable-design distance spectra could have an equivalent specialization.

## Value
This is a complete sharp all-orders positive-kernel classification for a geometrically canonical family, valid even for non-Desarguesian affine planes. It identifies the exact obstruction (parallel-line contrasts), the strict-to-degenerate transition, a binary exceptional nullity, and sharp minimal Euclidean embedding dimensions. The parameter asymptotic quantifies how kernel admissibility collapses with plane order. These results directly determine which Gaussian similarity kernels admit all-scale Hilbert-space feature representations.

## Limits
The conclusion is universal in the Gaussian scale and does not address a single chosen scale above the critical exponent. The proof presupposes a finite affine plane of the stated order. Independent audit has not been carried out.
