On the incidence graph of every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge2\), the Gaussian distance-power kernel \(\exp(-t d^p)\) is positive semidefinite at every scale \(t>0\) exactly for \(0\le p\le\log_2(q/(q-1))\). The critical squared-Euclidean embedding loses precisely \(q\) dimensions of strict negative type for \(q\ge3\), but five at the exceptional binary plane.
