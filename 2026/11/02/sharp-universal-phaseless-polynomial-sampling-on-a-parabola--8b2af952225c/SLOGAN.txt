On any nondegenerate parabola, magnitudes at any \(4n+1\) distinct locations recover every complex polynomial of degree at most \(n\) up to global phase, and this universal sample count is sharp at **every** positive degree. An explicit all-degree induction constructs two non-phase-equivalent degree-\(n\) polynomials whose magnitudes agree at \(4n\) distinct parabola points.
