For every integer \(m\ge2\), the normalized Szegő-kernel quotient of any disk selfmap vanishing to order \(m\) at the origin lies in the convex hull of \(1\) and the disk of center and radius \(1/(m+1)\); a single monomial map \(z\mapsto z^m\) makes this bound simultaneously sharp in every supporting direction. The envelope has an explicit two-tangent boundary and exact area, and its area shrinks like \(1/m\) even though the associated monomial composition operator has the closed unit disk as its closed numerical range.
