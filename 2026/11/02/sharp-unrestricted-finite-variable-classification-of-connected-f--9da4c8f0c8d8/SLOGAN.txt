For every \(k\ge3\), two connected Ferrers graphs have the same unrestricted-depth, counting-free \(k\)-variable theory exactly when their canonical staircase twin-block sizes agree after clipping at \(k\), up to exchange of the two bipartition sides. At exactly \(m\) staircase levels there are \((k^{2m}+k^m)/2\) such theories, with unique smallest representatives and a strict variable-width hierarchy.
