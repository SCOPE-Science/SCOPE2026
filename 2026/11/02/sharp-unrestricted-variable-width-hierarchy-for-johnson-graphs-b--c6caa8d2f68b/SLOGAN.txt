For every normalized Johnson-graph rank \(r\ge3\) and every variable width \(p\ge4\), ordinary counting-free \(p\)-variable first-order logic distinguishes ground-set orders below \(pr\) exactly and collapses all orders at least \(pr\), even at unrestricted quantifier depth. The resulting sharp hierarchy has \((p-2)r+1\) theories at fixed rank and a smallest stable representative with \(\binom{pr}{r}\) vertices.
