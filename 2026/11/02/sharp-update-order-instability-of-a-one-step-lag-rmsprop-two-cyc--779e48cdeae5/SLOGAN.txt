One-step staleness in a scalar epsilon-outside RMSProp preconditioner creates a sharp, memory-decay-independent stability window: the unique nonzero two-cycle attracts exactly when \(2\varepsilon/a<h<4\varepsilon/a\), becomes nonhyperbolic at the upper endpoint, and is a repeller above it. Its exact node-to-spiral crossover is \(h=16\varepsilon/[a(7+\beta)]\); no global convergence or nonlinear critical bifurcation is asserted.
