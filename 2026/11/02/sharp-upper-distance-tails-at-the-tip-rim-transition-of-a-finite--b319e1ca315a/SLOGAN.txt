For two independent intrinsic-area-uniform points on the lateral surface of a finite right circular cone, the probability of a distance within \(\varepsilon\) of the diameter has a complete four-regime sharp asymptotic law. A tip–rim/rim–rim tie at developed cone angle \(2\pi/3\) adds two distinct cubic coefficients, whereas the flat-disk limit has exponent \(5/2\) rather than \(3\).
