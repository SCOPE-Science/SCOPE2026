Symmetric infinitely divisible laws with Lévy jumps bounded by \(L\) have a sharp variance-exact sub-gamma scale no larger than \(0.1742179596517\ldots L\), with equality only for symmetric compound-Poisson jumps at \(\pm L\). When a prescribed fraction of variance comes from shorter jumps or Gaussian noise, a sharp two-magnitude envelope gives strict improvement and an explicit logarithmic dilution regime.
