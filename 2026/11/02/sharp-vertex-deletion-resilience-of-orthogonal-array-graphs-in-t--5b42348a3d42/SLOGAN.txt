For every strength-two orthogonal-array graph, the exact number of arbitrary vertex deletions that preserves its unrestricted three-variable first-order theory of the countable random graph is one less than the minimum of six explicit two-point extension counts. Optimizing over the number of array rows at prime-power order gives a sharp parity-sensitive resilience maximum: \(h(h-1)-1\) deletions at order \(2h\), and \(h(h+1)-2\) deletions at order \(2h+1\), with uniquely determined optimal row counts up to the even-order tie.
