In a closed ecological occupancy survey with at most \(k\) positive Poisson detection-rate classes, the entire binary visit-history law identifies occupancy and class rates exactly when the known visit durations have at least \(2k\) distinct nonempty subset sums. Hence \(\lceil\log_2(2k+1)\rceil\) suitably unequal visits are necessary and sufficient, compared with \(2k\) equal-duration visits, even when total observation time is held fixed.
