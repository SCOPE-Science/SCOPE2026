For independent samples from **any** moment-isotropic measure on the unit sphere, squared simplex volume is strictly negatively correlated with squared vertex-barycenter radius; the normalized covariance equals \(-(2+d^2\|\mathbb EX^{\otimes3}\|_F^2)/(d+1)^2\). Its sharp upper extremizers are exactly spherical \(3\)-design measures, while its uniquely classified lower extremizer is the uniform measure on the vertices of a regular simplex.
