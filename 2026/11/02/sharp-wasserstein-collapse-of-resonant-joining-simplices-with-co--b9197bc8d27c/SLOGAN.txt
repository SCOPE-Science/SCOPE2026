Two uniquely ergodic irrational circle rotations can have a product with continuum many limit-set stations and ergodic joinings, even though the product has exactly one station at rationally independent frequencies. At rational relative frequency \(a/q\), the entire joining simplex is Wasserstein-isometric to the probability measures on a circle of circumference \(1/q\), and its exact maximal \(W_p\) distance from independent Haar measure is \(1/[2q(p+1)^{1/p}]\) for \(1\le p<\infty\) (or \(1/(2q)\) for \(p=\infty\)).
