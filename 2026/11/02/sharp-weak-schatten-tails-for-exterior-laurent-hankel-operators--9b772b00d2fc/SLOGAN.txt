For bounded exterior Laurent symbols whose first inverse power has order \(k\ge2\), the Gaussian-Fock Hankel singular values of the conjugate symbol obey the sharp law \(s_n\sim k|a_k|n^{-(k+1)/2}\), while the original-symbol Hankel operator lies in every Schatten ideal. The critical weak-Schatten residue depends only on that first Laurent coefficient, independently of the cutoff radius and higher powers.
