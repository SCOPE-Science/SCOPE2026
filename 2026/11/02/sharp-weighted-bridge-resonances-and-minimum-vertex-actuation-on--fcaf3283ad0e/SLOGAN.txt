For a two-hub tree with \(p\) and \(q\) pendant leaves, the minimum vertex-actuator count jumps from \(p+q-2\) to \(p+q-1\) precisely at an explicit finite set of bridge conductances determined by Dirichlet path eigenvalues. All minimum actuator sets and all rational exceptional weights are classified, and nonresonant cases strictly beat the support graph's zero-forcing number whenever the hub separation is at least two edges.
