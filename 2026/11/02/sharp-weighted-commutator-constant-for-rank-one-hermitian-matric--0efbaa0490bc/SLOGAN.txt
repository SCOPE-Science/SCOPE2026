Restricting weighted Frobenius commutators to rank-one Hermitian matrices forces a new sharp constant determined by the unique solution of \((a+b)(1-2t^2)+(b-a)t^3=0\), where \(a,b\) are the two smallest eigenvalues of the weight. The optimal pair consists of two positive rank-one projections, and the constant decays like \(2/(ab)\) as the spectral ratio diverges, sharply contrasting with unrestricted rank-one commutators.
