On the complete bipartite graph \(K_{p,q}\), open-neighborhood lower-median filtering has exact, attained one-input weighted total-variation gain \(W/c_w\), where \(c_w\) is a minimum cut separating the two strict-majority labels. For unit weights the sharp gain is \(2pq/(pq+1)\) when both part sizes are odd and \(2\) otherwise; positive anisotropic weights permit unbounded gain even on \(K_{2,2}\).
