For weighted least-squares isotonic regression on a rooted star, the exact two-input edge-variation Lipschitz constant is \(\max\{1,\max_i a_i(\sum_jw_j-w_i)/[(a_0+a_i)w_i]\}\), and every candidate value has an explicit attaining pair. With uniform masses and edge weights, stability persists through three leaves and fails sharply from four leaves onward.
