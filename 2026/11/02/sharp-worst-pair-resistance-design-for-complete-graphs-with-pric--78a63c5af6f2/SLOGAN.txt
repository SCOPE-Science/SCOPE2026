For resistance-diameter minimization on a priced perfect-matching clique, there is an exact and surprisingly broad cost interval on which the globally optimal symmetric design keeps *all* conductances equal: \(2(m-1)/(2m-1)\le P/Q<2\). Above the sharp, size-independent surcharge \(P/Q=2\), every unrestricted optimum deletes every matching edge, while below that surcharge no optimum deletes them all.
