For equal-arm spider trees with \(m\ge3\), \(k\ge3\), and \(\ell\ge k+1\) satisfying \(2(\ell+1)\ge3k\), the ordinary \(k\)-th power has exact zero forcing number and maximum nullity \((m-1)k\). A polynomial in the tree adjacency matrix proves the matching lower bound through a nonnegative factorization with full required off-diagonal support.
