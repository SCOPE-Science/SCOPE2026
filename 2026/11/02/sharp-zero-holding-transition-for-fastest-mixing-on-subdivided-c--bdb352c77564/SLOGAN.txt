For edge-subdivided complete bipartite networks \(S(K_{p,q})\) with \(2\le p\le q\), every fastest-mixing symmetric stochastic chain has zero holding probability at every original vertex exactly outside \((p,q)=(2,2),(2,3)\). In that infinite range the optimal SLEM is \(\bigl(d+\sqrt{d^2+4/p}\bigr)/2\), where \(d=1-1/p-1/q\), and an optimal chain saturates both classes of original-vertex row budgets.
