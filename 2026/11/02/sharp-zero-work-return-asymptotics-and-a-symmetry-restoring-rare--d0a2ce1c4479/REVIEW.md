# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The source model's transition probabilities were independently reconstructed from its exact equations. The two eigenvalues and analytic saddle of the tilted 2-by-2 kernel were computed explicitly. The lattice period-two Fourier inversion produces the factor of two and the strict parity obstruction; the right/left Perron projection produces the precise initial-heading amplitude. Uniform separation of the other eigenmode as feedback approaches the fully lazy regime justifies the Gaussian crossover. The entropy minimization is strictly convex and its Doob-transformed kernel matches the optimizer exactly. A standalone finite-cycle checker passed 42 numerical and analytic-identity diagnostics. It is not a substitute for the asymptotic proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Bresque, Das and Roldán (arXiv:2410.18783v1) establish the two-state work process, its general exact generating functions, moments and first-passage distributions; these are acknowledged and not claimed as discoveries. Their examined main text and appendix do not explicitly give the sharp fixed-cycle return-to-zero probability, its parity-aware prefactor, the weak-feedback local crossover or the exact zero-current entropy-minimizing driven kernel. Willaert, Cleuren and Van den Broeck (arXiv:1404.3479v1) consider a different continuous-time exchange current and give no corresponding model-specific formula. Search of related mathematical research summaries and the applicable prior local research ledger did not identify a dominating claim. Generic Markov-additive local-limit and exponential-tilt theory may subsume the techniques; further publication-priority verification is warranted.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Zero net accumulated work after many cycles measures a physically meaningful finite-horizon output failure not specified by the mean, variance or upward first-passage law. Its exact exponential rarity and lattice prefactor quantify a risk without silently assuming independent increments. The singular weak-feedback window identifies when apparently persistent positive output ceases to have exponentially small work cancellation. The unique symmetric entropic driven kernel reveals how rare zero-work trajectories suppress the feedback-induced directional bias.

## Scope and remaining scientific risk

The result concerns the ideal gross-work process under the specified single-qubit protocol, not net information-processing power or a full work large-deviation principle. The fixed-parameter asymptotic is not uniform near all degenerate parameter endpoints. Standard spectral large-deviation methods provide antecedents and the original paper's full propagator could be used for alternate derivations.
