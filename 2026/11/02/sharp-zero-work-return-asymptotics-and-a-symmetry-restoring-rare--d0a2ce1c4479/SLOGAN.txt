In a lazy quantum measurement engine, the probability of zero gross work after an even number \(n\) of cycles has a sharp \(n^{-1/2}L^n\) law, including its exact amplitude; odd-cycle zero work is impossible. Near fully lazy feedback, the return probability has a Gaussian crossover on the \(n(1-q)^2\) scale, and its rare trajectories are governed by a unique symmetry-restoring entropy-minimizing two-state kernel.
