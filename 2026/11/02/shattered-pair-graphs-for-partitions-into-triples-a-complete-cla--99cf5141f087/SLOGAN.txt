For partitions of \(3m\) labeled points into unordered triples, a set of same-block pair queries is shattered exactly when its graph is a disjoint union of two-edge paths, isolated edges and isolated vertices, with a sharp two-profile exception at full block occupancy. This class has \(\operatorname{VCdim}=2m-2\), and the classification yields an explicit polynomial counting every shattered support by size.
