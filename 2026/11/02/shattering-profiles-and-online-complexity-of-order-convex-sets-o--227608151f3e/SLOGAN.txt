The order-convex subsets of the two-row product poset have an exact quartic concept count and an exact degree-four VC-shattering polynomial, yet their online Littlestone dimension grows as \(4\log_2 n+O(1)\) while VC dimension stays at four for \(n\ge3\). Complete finite recurrence certificates show nonmonotone attainment of the hypothesis-count bound across the first ten widths.
