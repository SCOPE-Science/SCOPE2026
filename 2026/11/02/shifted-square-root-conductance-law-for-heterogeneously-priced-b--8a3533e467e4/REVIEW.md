# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The full edge allocation objective N tr[(L|1⊥)^−1] is strictly convex on the connected positive domain; injectivity of the edge-to-Laplacian map yields uniqueness and right-vertex permutation symmetry forces w_ij=a_i. Connectedness forces each a_i>0. Three exact resistance types sum to (N−1)/q Σ1/a_i+N(q−1)/Σa_i. The constrained positive-definite Hessian and KKT equations imply a_i=t/(rho_i−alpha)^1/2 with the stated monotone scalar equation alpha in (0,min rho_i), which has unique solution. Budget fixes t=B/(qT), objective (N−1)T²/B. For fixed prices with unique cheapest, q²(rho1−alpha)→rho1 and T/q→sqrt(rho1), giving all limits. Independent rational weighted Laplacian pseudoinverses on K_2,2, K_2,3 and K_3,4 corroborated the reduced objective and numerical KKT stationary design. Strict convexity, not random numerical comparisons, establishes global optimality.

## originality

PASS

Ghosh–Boyd–Saberi established the general convex effective-resistance design framework and common graph examples; the row-price biclique one-dimensional closed solution and large-right-class concentration are additional mathematical statements. Chen–Yan studies weighted complete multipartite resistance distances rather than exact heterogeneously priced budget optimization; complete paper inaccessible, so risk retained.
