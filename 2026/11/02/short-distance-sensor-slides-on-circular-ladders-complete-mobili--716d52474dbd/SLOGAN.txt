For the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge8\), the token-sliding graph of minimum two-sensor power-dominating placements is connected and bipartite, has degree polynomial \(2nt^2+5nt^4+4nt^6\), and exactly \(19n\) four-cycles. The previously known short-distance criterion classifies valid pairs; the new calculation classifies their allowed movements and local square structure.
