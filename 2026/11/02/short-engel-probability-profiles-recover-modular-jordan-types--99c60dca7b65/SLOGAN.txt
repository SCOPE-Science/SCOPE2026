For every odd prime \(p\), the complete iterated Engel-word distributions on elementary-abelian-by-\(C_p\) groups with unipotent action are explicit mixtures of a point mass and uniform distributions on powers of the action's nilpotent part. Their first \(p-1\) identity probabilities, together with group order, recover every Jordan block and hence the displayed semidirect product; the cutoff \(p-1\) is sharp for distinguishing nonisomorphic groups of equal order.
