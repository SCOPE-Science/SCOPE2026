The reciprocal-logarithmic boundary sine chirp on analytic disk Bergman space produces a generalized Toeplitz operator in every Schatten class even when the symbol is not absolutely integrable. Its positive and negative eigenvalue counts obey the same sharp law \(N_\pm(e^{-L})=L^2/4+(2\beta+1)L\log L/4+O_\beta(L)\), so the subleading spectral coefficient recovers the boundary singularity order.
