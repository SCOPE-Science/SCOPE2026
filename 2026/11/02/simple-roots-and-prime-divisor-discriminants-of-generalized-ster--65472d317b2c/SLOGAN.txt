For generalized Stern polynomials at every integer parameter \(t\ge2\), every nonconstant term has simple zeros. If \(n=2^r u\) with \(u\) odd, then at every prime \(p\mid t\), \(\nu_p(\operatorname{Disc}(A_{t,n}))=r\deg(A_{t,n})\nu_p(t)\); this settles the all-index simple-root question beyond the previously known specialization \(t=2\).
