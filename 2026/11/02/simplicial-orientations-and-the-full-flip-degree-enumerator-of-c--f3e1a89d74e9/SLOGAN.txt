For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\), the minimum degree in its acyclic-orientation edge-flip graph is \(m+n-1\), and the precise number of minimum-degree orientations has a closed falling-factorial formula. The entire degree polynomial for \(K_{2,n}\) is \(2^n t^{2n}+2\sum_{j=1}^{n}\binom nj2^{n-j}t^{n+j}\), so the fraction of simple graphical-zonotope vertices in this family decays like \(\frac n2(\frac23)^n\).
