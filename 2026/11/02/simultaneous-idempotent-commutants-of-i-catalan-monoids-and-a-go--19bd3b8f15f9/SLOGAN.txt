The \(i\)-Catalan monoid has an exact idempotent-commutant spectrum: a map with \(r\) strict arrows commutes with precisely \(2^{n-r}\) idempotents, and a Narayana-derived generating function determines every simultaneous centralization count. For two or more independently chosen idempotents, the commuting-probability asymptotics are governed by a simple pole, in sharp contrast with the previously enumerated one-idempotent square-root regime; the separating continuous parameter is \((3-\sqrt5)/2\).
