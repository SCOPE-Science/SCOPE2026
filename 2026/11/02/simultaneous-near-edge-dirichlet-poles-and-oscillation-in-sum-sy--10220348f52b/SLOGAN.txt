For every fixed \(m\ge3\), all \(m-1\) real pole levels of the labelled sum-system Dirichlet series recur together near arbitrarily high vertical translates, with their explicit residues preserved asymptotically. The counting error has unavoidable two-sided fluctuations of size \(\Omega_\pm(X^{r_m-\varepsilon})\) for every \(\varepsilon>0\), so no fixed power-saving error bound is possible.
