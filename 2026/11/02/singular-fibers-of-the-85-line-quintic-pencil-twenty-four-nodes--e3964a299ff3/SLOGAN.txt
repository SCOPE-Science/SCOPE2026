For the Yu–Zhu pencil of quintic surfaces with eighty-five lines on smooth members, the two previously identified exceptional affine fibers have sharply different complete singularity baskets: twenty-four ordinary nodes at \(t=1\), versus one ordinary quadruple point with a smooth genus-three quartic tangent cone at \(t=0\). Their respective total Milnor numbers are \(24\) and \(27\), giving Euler characteristics \(31\) and \(28\).
