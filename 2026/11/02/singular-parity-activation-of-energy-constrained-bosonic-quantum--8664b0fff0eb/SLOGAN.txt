For a bosonic channel with random \(0\)- or \(\pi\)-phase kicks and fixed mean photon energy, the optimal unassisted quantum/private code has geometric photon-number statistics separately in the even and odd sectors, with a unique odd-sector weight. Near complete parity dephasing the optimal odd weight is \(q_r\sim e^{-1}\exp[-\ln(1+2/E)/(2r)]\) and its capacity gain is asymptotic to \(r q_r/\ln2\), where \(r=(1-2p)^2\): arbitrarily weak residual parity coherence yields a nonperturbatively small benefit.
