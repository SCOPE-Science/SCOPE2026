The mutual Birkhoff–James orthogonality graph of real \(\ell_1^3\) has clique number exactly six, and its unique largest clique comprises the lines \([e_i+e_j]\) and \([e_i-e_j]\) for \(1\le i<j\le3\). A sign-support argument excludes every other continuous-real configuration, not just ternary test vectors.
