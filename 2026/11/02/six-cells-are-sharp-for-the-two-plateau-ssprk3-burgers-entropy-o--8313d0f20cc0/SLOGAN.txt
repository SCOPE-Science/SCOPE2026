For the canonical two-plateau Burgers \(\mathbb P^1\)-DG/SSPRK3 update, **six** is the smallest even periodic mesh on which the published exponential-entropy obstruction occurs at every admissible positive CFL number; the earlier construction required at least twelve cells. Two- and four-cell updates in this exact family retain all cell averages between the initial plateaus, so their global cell-average entropy cannot increase for any convex entropy.
