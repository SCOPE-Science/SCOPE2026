For the classical power-sum integral model of Bring’s genus-four curve, reduction modulo \(5\) is a bouquet of exactly six rational projective lines with a single nonplanar six-branch singularity of delta invariant \(9\). The symmetric group \(S_5\) permutes the components through its exceptional degree-six action on Sylow-five subgroups, and the fiber has exactly \(6q+1\) points over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic five.
