The strong chromatic index of the six-cycle product of every star with \(r\ge2\) leaves is exactly \(\chi'_s(K_{1,r}\square C_6)=2r+5\). A saturated-clique obstruction rules out one fewer color, and a two-color leaf extension gives sharp constructions; in particular every finite simple graph \(G\) with \(\Delta(G)\ge2\) satisfies \(\chi'_s(G\square C_6)\ge2\Delta(G)+5\).
