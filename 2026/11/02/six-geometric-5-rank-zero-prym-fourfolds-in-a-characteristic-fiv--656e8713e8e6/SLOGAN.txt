The ramified double cover \(y^3=x^8+ax^4+1\to v^3=t^4+at^2+1\) in characteristic \(5\) has a Prym fourfold of constant \(a\)-number \(2\), whose geometric \(5\)-rank drops from \(2\) to \(0\) at exactly the six smooth coefficient parameters satisfying \(1+3a^6=0\). The quotient curve always has geometric \(5\)-rank \(2\), and the genus-seven covering curve has \(5\)-rank \(4\) or \(2\) accordingly.
