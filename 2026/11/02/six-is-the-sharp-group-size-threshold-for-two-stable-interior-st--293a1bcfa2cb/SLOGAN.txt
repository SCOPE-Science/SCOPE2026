In a well-mixed two-project volunteer game, six is the smallest group size that can sustain two locally stable interior volunteer frequencies when one contribution can trigger two positive strictly interior threshold goods. With thresholds \(2\) and \(5\), equal per-good reward \(b\), and cost \(5b/16<c<5b/12\), the six-person game has exactly four interior equilibria, alternating unstable and stable; no group of five or fewer permits more than two.
