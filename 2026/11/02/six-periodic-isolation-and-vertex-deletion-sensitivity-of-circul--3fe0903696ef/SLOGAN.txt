For the circular ladder \(P_n=C_n\square K_2\), isolation is exactly \(\lceil n/3\rceil+\mathbf{1}_{n\equiv3\pmod6}\), while deleting any one vertex gives \(\lceil n/3\rceil-\mathbf{1}_{n\equiv4\pmod6}\). Thus every vertex is deletion-decreasing precisely when the number of rungs is congruent to three or four modulo six; the even intact-graph value was previously known from a general bipartite-prism theorem.
