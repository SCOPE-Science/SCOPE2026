The intersection graphs of all nonempty intervals of an \(n\)-point chain have exactly six first-order theories at quantifier rank three: \(G_m\equiv_3G_n\) exactly when \(\min(m,6)=\min(n,6)\). The sharp common tail begins at \(n=6\), so every graph in this infinite family has a rank-three representative with at most \(21\) vertices.
