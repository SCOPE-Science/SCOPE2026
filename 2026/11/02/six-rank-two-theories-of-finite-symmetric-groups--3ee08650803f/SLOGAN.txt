In the named-identity, ternary-multiplication language, the symmetric groups \(S_n\) have exactly six rank-two first-order theories: singleton degrees \(1,2,3,4\), the common degree class \(5\)–\(7\), and one class for every degree at least \(8\). The sharp stabilization threshold comes from the first square-rootable element of order four, a product of two disjoint four-cycles.
