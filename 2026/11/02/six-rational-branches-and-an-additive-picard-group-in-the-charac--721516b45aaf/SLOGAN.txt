The characteristic-five power-sum model of Bring's genus-four curve splits into six \(\mathbb F_5\)-rational lines through one six-branch point of delta invariant \(9\). Its connected Picard group is the **additive group** \(\mathbb G_a^4\), and its point counts are exactly \(6\cdot5^n+1\) over \(\mathbb F_{5^n}\); this concerns the stated model rather than stable reduction.
