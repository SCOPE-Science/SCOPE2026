In characteristic five, the locus of smooth normalized Ciani quartics with Jacobian \(5\)-rank at most one consists of six rational curves crossing in eight superspecial points. The exact numbers of rank-zero and rank-one marked coefficient triples over \(\mathbb F_{5^m}\) are \(8\epsilon_m\) and \(6(5^m-3)-36\epsilon_m\), with \(\epsilon_m=1\) for even \(m\) and zero for odd \(m\).
