In characteristic \(13\), the smooth genus-five pencil \(y^2=(x^4-1)(x^8+t x^4+1)\) has exactly six geometrically distinct nonordinary parameters: the roots of \((t-6)(t^2+3t+6)(t+6)(t^2+5t-4)\). Each has \(13\)-rank three and Jacobian \(a\)-number two; every other smooth fiber is ordinary, and all six determinant zeros are double.
