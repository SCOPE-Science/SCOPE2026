Six is the sharp first even roommate-market size at which blocking-neutrality can discard a Pareto-optimal perfect pairing even though no stable perfect pairing exists: every unsolvable four-person market retains all three perfect pairings, while a six-person example retains six of nine Pareto-optimal pairings. An adjacent reversal of one agent’s two top choices causes the six-person blocking-neutral set to shrink from nine to six without changing the Pareto-optimal set or restoring stability.
