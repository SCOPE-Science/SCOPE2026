For the \(72\) exact-nine-torsion points on any smooth complex plane cubic, exactly \(1{,}928{,}772\) conics pass through six distinct chosen points: \(1{,}701{,}252\) are smooth and \(227{,}520\) split into pairs of trisecants. Their \(720\) trisecants have \(30\) through each torsion point, yielding \(141{,}771\) smooth six-secant conics through every point.
