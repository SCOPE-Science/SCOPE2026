In the pure adjacency language, the binary cubes \(Q_1,\ldots,Q_5\) have five distinct four-round first-order theories, while every cube \(Q_d\) with \(d\ge6\) shares a sixth theory. A four-coordinate triple-type reduction and an eight-coordinate pair-type reduction make the infinite classification reducible to an exact finite census, giving a sharp 64-vertex representative bound.
