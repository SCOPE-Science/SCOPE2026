Six and only six unrestricted three-variable first-order incidence theories occur among finite affine and projective point–line spaces together with complete-graph vertex–edge incidence geometries. In particular, every \(K_n\) vertex–edge incidence structure for \(n\ge6\) is three-variable equivalent to every finite affine or projective space of geometric dimension at least three; the six classes separate by quantifier rank four, and a sharp representative bound is 21 elements.
