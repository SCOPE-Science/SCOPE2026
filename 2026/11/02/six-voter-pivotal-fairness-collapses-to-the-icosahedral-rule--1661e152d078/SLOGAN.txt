For six voters, Shapley–Shubik-fairness, Banzhaf-fairness and transitive voter symmetry coincide among neutral, monotone, resolute two-candidate rules: there are exactly \(12\) labeled rules, all isomorphic to the known icosahedral rule. Fixing any voter converts the ten winning triples into a five-cycle on the other voters, giving a structural classification rather than an existence example.
