The size-enumerator of ordinary \(P_3\)-convex sets in every generalized theta graph has an exact two-terminal transfer product. For \(r\ge3\) parallel paths of length three it is \((1+rz)^2+2z((1+z)^r+rz^2(1+z)^{r-1})+z^2(1+z^2)^r\), revealing infinitely many forbidden odd cardinalities and exactly \(r\) largest proper convex sets.
