# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Every face of the Birkhoff polytope has a totally supported bipartite edge support E and its local Frobenius projector is the orthogonal projection onto row/column-zero matrices supported on E. Polyhedral piecewise affinity and a strict-complementarity point realize each projector row norm by finite input pairs. Double centering gives full-face 4(1-1/n)^2. Fresh exhaustive enumeration of all 3, 49 and 7,443 totally supported labeled edge sets at n=2,3,4 found 2,6,42 row/column orbits and exact max norms 1,16/9,9/4, uniquely full support. Exact independent incidence-Gram inversion of the 72-edge cyclic n=12 support S={0,2,3,6,8,9} gives row absolute sum 41/12, strictly exceeding 121/36 by 1/18. Results require real unrestricted inputs, Frobenius nearest-point projection, entrywise maximum perturbation norm. Checks reconstruct critical finite certificates, not extrapolation to all higher n.

## originality

PASS

The Li–Sun–Toh generalized-Jacobian formula is a broader calculus framework, but the relevant result does not state or mechanically determine the l_infinity operator row norms for all n<=4 or the explicit cyclic n=12 obstruction without substantial new support optimization. The affine full-support norm is routine and not itself new; the meaningful claims are small-order optimality and a higher-order counterexample.
