For Euclidean projection onto real doubly stochastic matrices, the sharp entrywise-maximum-norm Lipschitz constants at orders \(2,3,4\) are exactly \(1,16/9,9/4\). A six-regular cyclic support face at order \(12\) has exact local amplification \(41/12\), strictly exceeding the unrestricted affine projection norm \(121/36\); thus nonnegativity can increase worst-case coordinate sensitivity.
