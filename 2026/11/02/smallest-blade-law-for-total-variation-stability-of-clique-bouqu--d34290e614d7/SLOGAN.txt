For a graph made of cliques joined at one hub, the sharp initial heat-flow total-variation growth rate is \(K-2k_*-1\), where \(K\) is the total number of nonhub vertices and \(k_*\) the smallest blade size. Hence graph total variation decreases for every real signal and all times precisely when \(K\le2k_*+1\), a complete classification controlled solely by the weakest blade.
