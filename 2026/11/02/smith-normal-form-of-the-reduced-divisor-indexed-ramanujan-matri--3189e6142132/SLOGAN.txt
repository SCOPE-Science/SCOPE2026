The complete integral Smith form of the ordinary Ramanujan-sum matrix indexed on both axes by the nonunit divisors of \(n\) is determined for every \(n\ge2\), including all prime-power torsion and modular ranks. Beyond the previously known squarefree case, a prime \(\ell\mid n\) introduces a distinguished exponent \(\nu_\ell(n)+\nu_\ell(\varphi(n/\ell^{\nu_\ell(n)}))\).
