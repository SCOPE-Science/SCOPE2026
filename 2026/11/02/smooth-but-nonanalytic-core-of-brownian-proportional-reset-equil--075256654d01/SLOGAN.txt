For \(L=-\log|a|>0\), the stationary density under strict proportional Brownian resetting is \(C^\infty\) everywhere and real-analytic except at the reset origin, where its exact derivatives grow as \(\exp(Ln^2+O(n))\); its Taylor series consequently has radius zero and violates every finite Gevrey bound. Yet taking logarithmically many Taylor terms near the origin gives the explicit log-squared error envelope \(f(0)\exp[L-(\log(\sqrt{D/r}/|x|))^2/L]\).
