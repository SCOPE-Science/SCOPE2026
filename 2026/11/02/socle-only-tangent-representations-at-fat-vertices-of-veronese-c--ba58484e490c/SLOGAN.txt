On the degree-\(d\) Veronese surface cone, every first-order Hilbert deformation of the vertex power \(\mathfrak m^r\) maps generators only into the final socle layer, giving tangent dimension \((dr+1)(d(r-1)+1)\) and an explicit full torus character. For the \(A_1\) cone, the length-\(r^2\) fat-vertex point has tangent dimension \(4r^2-1\), so it is singular for every \(r\ge1\).
