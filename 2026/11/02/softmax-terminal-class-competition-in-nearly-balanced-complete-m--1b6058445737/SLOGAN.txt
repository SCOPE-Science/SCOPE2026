For a walk on a nearly balanced complete \(k\)-partite graph with fixed \(k\ge3\), partwise cover times converge jointly to independent shifted Gumbels, and the last-discovered part has a softmax law with weights \(e^{\theta_i/(k-1)}\). The critical part-size imbalance is \(n/\log n\); beyond it, a uniquely largest part wins with probability tending to one.
