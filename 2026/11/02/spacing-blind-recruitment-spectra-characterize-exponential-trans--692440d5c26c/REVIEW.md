# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Clockwise distances conjugate the wrapped exponential point-kernel matrix to a fixed lower-one/upper-\(q\) matrix. Every principal minor is exactly \((1-q)^{k-1}\) times the relevant site-weight product; hence all characteristic coefficients are location independent. The converse follows from two-site constant cross-products, three-site constant cycle products and a differentiable Cauchy-type equation forcing the logarithm of the kernel to be affine. For the Erlang-2 comparison, direct winding summation and the identity \(qa(a+L)=a^2\) give a strictly negative determinant and a Perron root depending strictly increasingly on \(d(L-d)\). The supplied exact-rational program passes 54 checks. A finite test is not a replacement for the quantified proof.

**Originality — PASS, with unresolved historical priority.** Direct comparisons with the full accessible Artzy-Randrup–Stone (2010), Kleinhans–Jonsson (2010) and Cecino–Treml (2021) scientific texts showed connectivity eigenvalues, directed topology, and realistic connectivity, but not the joint if-and-only-if spectral placement characterization plus the two-stage sharp reversal. Botsford–Hastings–Gaines (2001) addresses the fraction of natural larval settlement for finite reserves, whereas the present result concerns center-evaluated point-transfer spectra; its full text remained inaccessible. General semiseparable matrix theory could implicitly subsume part of the algebra, but no stronger explicit implication of the complete characterization was located. This is not a proof of publication priority.

**Value — PASS.** Spacing is a basic marine connectivity design variable. The theorem identifies a precise zero-information limit in which spacing cannot change a low-density invasion multiplier despite arbitrary site strengths, characterizes the only differentiable kernel with that property using minimal two-/three-site tests, and shows that adding a single travel stage leads to a unique opposing design rule for the smallest nontrivial network. The separation clarifies why finite-width, nonmemoryless and two-way physical features matter when applying a marine connectivity model; no unsupported real-world reserve recommendation is inferred.

The point-site approximation, one-way circular habitat, linear invasion interpretation, and restricted Erlang comparison are essential limitations. The most important remaining originality risk is an unlocated broader spectral theorem on directed semiseparable matrices, and a historically plausible reserve-design source could only be checked at abstract level.
