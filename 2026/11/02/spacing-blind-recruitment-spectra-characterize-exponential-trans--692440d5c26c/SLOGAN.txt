A one-way memoryless dispersal kernel makes every point-site recruitment spectrum on a ring independent of spacing and even of the ordering of unequal site qualities; among positive \(C^1\) kernels, invariance for all two- and three-site configurations forces the kernel to be exponential. Replacing one exponential travel stage by two destroys this indifference: two sites have a unique antipodal Perron optimum and a strictly negative secondary eigenvalue.
