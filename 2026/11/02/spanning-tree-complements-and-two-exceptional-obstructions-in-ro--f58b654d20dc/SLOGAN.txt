For the unit-weight rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), the minimum \((m-1)(n-1)\) independent vertex actuators are exactly the complements of bipartite spanning trees when \(m\ne n\). When \(m=n\), precisely two explicit families of those trees fail, leaving \(n^{2n-2}-n^2((n-1)^2+1)\) minimum controllable placements.
