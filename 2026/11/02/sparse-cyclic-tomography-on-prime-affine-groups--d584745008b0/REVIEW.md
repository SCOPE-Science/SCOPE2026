# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For p prime, q>=3 dividing p-1 and a faithful r of order q, direct left-coset calculation gives R_a f(x)=sum_{t=0}^{q-1} f(x+a(1-r^t),t). Unitary F_p Fourier transform splits into k=0 and p-1 nonzero blocks; rank at k=0 is q with P included, else one if complements exist, else zero. Nonzero blocks are submatrices of the p-prime Fourier matrix with distinct chosen row and multiplicative-action column frequencies, hence rank min(m,q) by Chebotarev prime-minor nonvanishing. Cyclotomic proof via Vandermonde leading coefficient, uniformizer ζ−1 and p-unit coefficient was checked. Summed nullity gives (p-1)max(q-m,0)+(0 if P else q-1 if m>0 else q), injectivity iff P and m>=q, independent of A. Normal blocks, Cauchy-Binet determinants and full-affine q=p-1 eigenvalues 1,p,p+(p-1)^2 follow with counting-measure normalization. Independently built 0/1 incidence matrices and checked exact modular rank in multiple small p,q cases, distinct from the bundled checker. No finite sample proves the all-p theorem.
