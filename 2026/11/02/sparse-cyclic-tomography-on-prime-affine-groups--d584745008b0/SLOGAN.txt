For every prime-affine group \(\mathbb F_p\rtimes C_q\) with \(3\le q\mid p-1\), exactly \(q+1\) maximal-cyclic coset-sum directions are necessary and sufficient for reconstruction: the translation subgroup and ANY \(q\) distinct complements. The complete nullity law is independent of which complements are chosen; in the full affine case the minimal transform has only three squared-singular-value levels and sharp mean-zero condition number \(\sqrt p\).
