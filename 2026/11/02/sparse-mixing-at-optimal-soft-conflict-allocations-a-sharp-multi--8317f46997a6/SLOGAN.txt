Every minimum-conflict EF1 allocation on a complete \(r\)-partite conflict graph with identical unit goods mixes part types in at most \(r-1\) agents' bundles, and this bound is sharp. For \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a+b=nq\), the exact minimum is \(d(q-d)\) where \(d\equiv a\pmod q\) and \(0\leq d<q\); in particular, odd \(K_{n,n}\) with \(n\) agents needs just one conflict rather than the general bound of \(n\).
