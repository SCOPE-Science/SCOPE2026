On the labelled crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), \(n\ge4\), making every distance-two pair rainbow-connected with two named edge colors needs exactly \(n-1\) red edges. Every optimum is a near-perfect red matching whose remaining permutation has no cycles of length one or two, giving exactly \(n a_{n-1}\) optimal colorings and the generating function \(\sum_m a_m z^m/m!=e^{-z-z^2/2}/(1-z)\).
