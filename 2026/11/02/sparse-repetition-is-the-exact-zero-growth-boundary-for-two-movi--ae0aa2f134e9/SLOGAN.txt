For the doubling map with its leftmost and rightmost quarter-cylinders alternately removed, the survivor language has zero exponential growth exactly when repeated consecutive choices of the same hole have density zero. The exact all-schedule bounds \(2^{1+\lceil d_n/2\rceil}\le N_n\le2(1+n/(d_n+1))^{d_n+1}\) prove this threshold, while perfect alternation gives \(N_n=n+2\) despite each fixed hole alone having Fibonacci growth.
