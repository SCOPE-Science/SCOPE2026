For primitive binary necklaces with \(k\) zeroes, ordinary Lyndon and alternating-order Galois rotations coincide in exactly the rationally enumerated families for \(k=2,3\); at every fixed \(k\ge2\), the coincidence fraction tends to \(1/2\). In contrast, if \(k/n\) tends to any strictly positive constant, the coincidence fraction tends to zero exponentially, because a shared minimum cannot coexist with adjacent zeroes.
