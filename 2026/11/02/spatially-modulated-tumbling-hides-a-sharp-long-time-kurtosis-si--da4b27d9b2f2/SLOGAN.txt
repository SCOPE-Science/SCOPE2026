For a persistent particle reversing at unequal rates in alternating equal-length spatial zones, the long-time diffusivity is exactly \(v^2/(2a)\), independent of the rate contrast \(b\). However, its fourth-cumulant growth changes from negative to positive sharply at \(L|b|=2\sqrt3\,v\), so spatial modulation reverses the sign of the leading excess-kurtosis correction without changing the Gaussian-scale diffusion.
