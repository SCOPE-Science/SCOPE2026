A Poisson-reheated granular gas obeying power-law cooling has a strictly positive continuous temperature-covariance relaxation spectrum whose edge exponent equals the cooling power \(p\): its long-lag covariance is \(\frac{pT_0}{c^p}(\partial_r\mu)e^{-rh}h^{-(p+1)}\). The covariance alone identifies both the reset rate and the cooling exponent, while the stationary mean determines the cooling scale when \(T_0\) is known.
