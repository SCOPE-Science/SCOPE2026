# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The logistic unitary makes H_1 convolution with the causal exponential of rate 1/2; its multiplier (1/2+iξ)⁻¹ has closed essential range ∂D, no positive-measure level sets, and essential supremum in modulus 2. On the nonatomic multiplier space this is also the essential norm, and every point of the range closure is non-Fredholm. The logarithmic coordinate at γ=0 instead gives the adjoint of causal convolution on the positive half-line; Laplace and Cayley transforms identify H_0 with I−S*, with index +1 inside D and no exterior spectrum. Squaring the difference kernel and integrating first over x gives, in u=1−t, (u^(1−2γ)−2u^(1−γ)+u)/(1−u). The cancellation at u=1 and the power at u=0 give the Hilbert–Schmidt threshold; the digamma differences and the pole at 2−2γ=0 give the stated formula and leading coefficient. Compact perturbation gives the subcritical essential spectrum, essential norm and indices. For λ≠0 the eigenfunction equation reduces to x^(1/λ−1) times a factor bounded and bounded away from zero, since the remaining exponent integral converges at both ends for γ<1. Its L² criterion is Re(1/λ)>1/2. The boundary flux at zero vanishes in that region and recovers the integral equation; λ=0 is injective by differentiation. The Fredholm index then gives surjectivity inside and invertibility outside. Positive-kernel domination bounds both H_γ and H_1−H_γ by the norm 2. The latter difference, on weakly null profiles concentrating at the right end, rescales to h↦∫_s^1 h(u)du/u=H_0*h, giving essential norm at least 2; on smooth profiles the same scaling gives a divergent factor δ^(1−γ) for γ>1. Dominated convergence on functions supported away from 1 and the uniform bound imply strong convergence from below.
