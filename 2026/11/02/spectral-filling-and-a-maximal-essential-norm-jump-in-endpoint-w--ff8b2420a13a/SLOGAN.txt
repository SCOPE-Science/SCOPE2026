For endpoint-power weighted Cesàro integration on \(L^2(0,1)\), every exponent \(\gamma<1\) gives a Fredholm disk filled with one-dimensional eigenvalues, but the critical normal operator at \(\gamma=1\) has only the boundary circle as spectrum. Although the operators converge strongly at this threshold, their norm and essential-norm distance remains exactly \(2\), with an accompanying change in the interior Fredholm index.
