On SPD quadratics, the two-step error floor of full-frame one-sided zeroth-order descent is simultaneously minimized in every eigendirection by reflecting the second Haar frame with the spectral signs of \(\alpha H-I\). This fixed-marginal coupling has sharp stationary variance ratio \((1-|1-\alpha\lambda|)^2/[1+(1-\alpha\lambda)^2]\) relative to independent frames, and the optimal frame sign switches at \(\alpha\lambda=1\).
