A run-and-tumble particle with smooth saturating reversal rates has an exactly solvable Pöschl–Teller relaxation spectrum: spatial bound modes appear one by one when \(g<\gamma_1/(vn)\). Its slowest density-relaxation exponent increases with gradient only until \(g=\gamma_1/v\), beyond which it remains exactly constant; even-density modes have an earlier transition at \(g=\gamma_1/(2v)\).
