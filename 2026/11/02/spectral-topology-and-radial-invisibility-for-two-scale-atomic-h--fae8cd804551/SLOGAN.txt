Three-atom Hausdorff operators undergo a sharp arithmetic spectral transition: irrational logarithmic-scale ratios fill an annulus, while rational ratios give zero-area curves, yet every centered radial symbol statistic is identical. For coprime rational degrees \(p,q\), no mixed moment below degree \(p+q\) detects the distinction, and the spectral curves approach the annulus at order at most \((p^2+q^2)^{-1/2}\).
