The intersection graph of the sixty geometric lines on the Rams–Schütt characteristic-two quartic is \(17\)-regular with an explicit integral seven-eigenvalue spectrum and a complete seven-stratum common-transversal census. Exactly \(150\) skew pairs have eight common transversals, and their three \(S_5\)-orbits have sizes \(30,60,60\), with the unique size-\(30\) orbit consisting of Frobenius-conjugate line pairs.
