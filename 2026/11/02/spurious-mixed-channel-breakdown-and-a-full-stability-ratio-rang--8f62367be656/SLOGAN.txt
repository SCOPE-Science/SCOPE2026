For inverse-leading mixed-channel extended Krylov bases, third-step breakdown despite a three-dimensional Laurent-Krylov space occurs exactly when the inverse Rayleigh quotient vanishes; such a breakdown is possible for a nonsingular real symmetric matrix exactly when it is indefinite with at least three distinct eigenvalues. On the fixed matrix \(\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,2)\), the mixed-to-split normalized third-step residual ratio takes every positive value, despite condition number \(2\).
