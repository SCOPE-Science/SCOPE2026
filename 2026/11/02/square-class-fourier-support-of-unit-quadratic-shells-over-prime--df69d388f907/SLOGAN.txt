Every unit level of the smooth quadratic shell \(a^2+bc=t\) over \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\), for odd primes \(p\), has a completely explicit Fourier-null set and convolution rank \(p^2+p(p-1)(p+\chi_p(t))(p^3+1)/2\). Changing the level from a square to a nonsquare changes that rank by exactly \(p(p-1)(p^3+1)\), while both rank fractions tend to one half.
