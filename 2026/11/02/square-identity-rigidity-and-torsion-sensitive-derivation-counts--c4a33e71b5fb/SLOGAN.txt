For every finite poset and every commutative coefficient ring, a coefficient-linear map of its incidence algebra satisfying only \(D(a^2)=D(a)a+aD(a)\) is already a derivation—even in characteristic two, without assuming the stronger triple-product identity. The exact count over finite coefficient rings detects torsion in the poset order complex: for one projective-plane face poset, coefficient rings \(\mathbb F_4\) and \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\) have derivation counts differing by a factor of two.
