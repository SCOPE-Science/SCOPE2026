For an i.i.d. biased binary source, the expected number of distinct Abelian-square scattered subsequence strings has the same exponential base \(1+\sqrt{p(1-p)}\) as all distinct subsequences, but is smaller by an asymptotic factor \(\sqrt{(1+t)/(4\pi t n)}\), where \(t=\sqrt{p(1-p)}\). A closed algebraic generating function also gives the exact expectation for every host length and every Abelian-square length.
