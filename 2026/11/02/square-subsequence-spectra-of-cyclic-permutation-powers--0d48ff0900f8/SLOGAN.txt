Every distinct concatenated square \(uu\) appearing as a scattered subsequence of \((0\,1\,\cdots\,(q-1))^k\) is recognized by a single weak-descent statistic of its root, and all counts refined by root length have a closed rational generating function. The maximum square length is \(2q\lfloor k/2\rfloor\), attained by exactly one square for positive even \(k\), or by exactly \(q\) squares for odd \(k\ge3\).
