Restrict single-edge swaps between spanning trees of \(K_{2,n}\) to fundamental four-cycles: the resulting graph has an exact wildcard-coordinate distance formula, uniform diameter \(n-1\), and Laplacian eigenvalues \(4r\) and \(2(n+r)\) with explicit multiplicities. Its spectral gap is always \(4\), and the spectrum yields a closed product for the number of spanning trees of the exchange graph.
