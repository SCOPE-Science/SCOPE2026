For every rectangular matrix sandwich algebra over a finite field, the number of square-zero elements has an exact double sum determined solely by the two matrix dimensions and the sandwich rank. At sandwich rank one, the reduced square-zero locus splits into two explicit affine linear components (when both matrix dimensions exceed one), and its point count simplifies to \(q^{(m-1)(n-1)}(q^{m-1}+q^{n-1}-1)\).
