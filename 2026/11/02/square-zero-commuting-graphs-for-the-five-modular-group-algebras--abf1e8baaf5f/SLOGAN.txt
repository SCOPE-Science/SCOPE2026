Over every finite field of characteristic two, the square-zero commuting graph of each group algebra of order eight is complete except for the dihedral algebra: there it is two \(q^5\)-vertex cliques glued along a central \(q^4\)-vertex clique. This also gives the exact ordered commuting-pair count \((2q^2-1)q^8\) and a uniform \(q^2\) conjugacy orbit size for every noncentral square-zero element.
