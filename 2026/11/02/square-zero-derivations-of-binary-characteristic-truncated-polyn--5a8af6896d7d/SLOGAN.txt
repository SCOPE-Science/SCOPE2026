For every finite field of characteristic two, the square-zero derivations of \(\mathbb F_q[x]/(x^{2M})\) have an exact rank-generating polynomial and total count \(q^M+\lfloor M/2\rfloor(q-1)q^{M-1}\). Unlike generic square-zero matrices, a uniformly chosen derivation in this locus has normalized rank \(\operatorname{rank}(D)/M\) converging to the uniform law on \([1/2,1]\), at Wasserstein-1 rate \(O_q(M^{-1})\).
