# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The statement is reconstructed for ordinary Lie derivations of \(L=V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\), with \(n\ge3\) and arbitrary finite fields, not restricted derivations. The derived ideal is invariant and forces the central action \(\rho(A)\). Squaring the block matrix gives three necessary and sufficient identities; the explicit expansion \(\rho(A)^2=2\bigwedge^2A\) when \(A^2=0\) establishes the parity restriction. A Jordan-pair basis proves the exact rank \(r(n-r-1)\) of the induced exterior-square action. Direct choice of \(B\) on the basis \(y_i,z_j,x_i\) gives the complete lifting-fiber exponent. The flag count of square-zero \(A\) completes the finite-field census without exclusions or division by the characteristic. Constant rank of the fiber equation over each nilpotent orbit gives the stated smooth reduced strata. The accompanying checker exhaustively tests quotient matrices across seven field/dimension configurations and fully enumerates every lifting matrix in three small cases; it also verifies a rank-two characteristic-two branch and its odd-characteristic obstruction. These finite calculations are corroboration, not a replacement for the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority risk

The 2011 existence theorem for square-zero restricted outer derivations does not give ordinary-derivation counts, and restricted versus ordinary conditions differ. The 2014 and 2019 free nilpotent Lie derivation descriptions provide structural antecedents but not this characteristic-sensitive square-zero census. The 2023 paper on two-step nilpotent Leibniz derivations studies different structural questions and explicitly assumes characteristic other than two. Related prior results for the *same* free class-two Lie algebra concern local-derivation spaces, rank-one Nijenhuis maps and fixed-point-free automorphisms; their statements do not imply this distribution. The special \(n=2\) Heisenberg count is already available and has been excluded from the new theorem. Actual mathematical-record and targeted literature searches did not locate an equivalent rank-stratified formula; they do not prove uniqueness or publication priority. A general unpublished or unlocated nilpotent-matrix representation count is a residual originality risk.

## Value — PASS

This is a complete exact census of a natural nilpotent-derivation invariant of the entire free class-two Lie algebra family, including separate counts and smooth geometries for every possible induced rank. The characteristic-two transition is structural, not merely a modified coefficient: only in characteristic two can the quotient rank reach \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), producing new positive-dimensional strata already at \(n=4\). The family and the invariant arise independently of the numerical test cases, and the statement distinguishes ordinary derivations from restricted ones, making the characteristic transition mathematically interpretable. A closed rank distribution and its geometric realization are potentially useful in studies of differential operators, deformation spaces and modular Lie algebra representations; no downstream theorem is claimed.
