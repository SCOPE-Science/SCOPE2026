For the free class-two Lie algebra \(V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\) on \(n\ge3\) generators over \(\mathbb F_q\), every quotient-rank stratum of square-zero ordinary derivations has an exact Gaussian-product count and is a smooth irreducible vector bundle over a nilpotent matrix orbit. In odd characteristic the induced rank is at most one, but in characteristic two it reaches \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\); the resulting formula counts every such derivation over every finite field.
