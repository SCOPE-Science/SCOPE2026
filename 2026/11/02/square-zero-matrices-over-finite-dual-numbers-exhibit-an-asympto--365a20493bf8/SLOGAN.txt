For square-zero matrices over \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), the number with reduction rank \(r\) is exactly \(q^{n^2}\Phi_n/(\Phi_r\Phi_{n-2r})\). For fixed \(q\), the normalized rank becomes uniformly distributed on \([0,1/2]\) as matrix size grows, in sharp contrast to the field-level concentration near half rank.
