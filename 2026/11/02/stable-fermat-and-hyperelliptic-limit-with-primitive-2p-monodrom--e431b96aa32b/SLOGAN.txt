At \(t=-2\), the generalized Kuribayashi--Komiya pencil of degree \(2p\) stabilizes, after \(t+2=s^{2p}\), to a Fermat curve of degree \(p\) with \(3p\) identical genus-\((p-1)/2\) hyperelliptic tails. Its Jacobian has compact-type reduction, and local monodromy has exact order \(2p\) with characteristic polynomial \((\lambda-1)^{(p-1)(p-2)}\Phi_{2p}(\lambda)^{3p}\).
