Among all deterministic-order-two explicit two-stage Runge–Kutta methods applied with independent scalar multiplicative gradient samples at each stage of a positive-definite quadratic, equal-weight Heun uniquely minimizes every finite-horizon expected squared error and has the sharpest mean-square stability step. Its admissible step approaches twice that of midpoint or stochastic Euler in the high-noise limit, while reusing one heavy-tailed sample at both stages can make the squared error infinite even when independent-stage Heun contracts.
