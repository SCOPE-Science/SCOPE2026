For normalized Hardy-space point evaluations on two different concentric regular \(k\)-gons, the unique relative rotation that maximizes the smallest sampling singular value also minimizes the largest one and the condition number: an exact half-step stagger. The squared least singular value has sharp decay \(k(1-r^2)r^{-2}(rs)^{2k}\) for fixed radii \(0<r<s<1\), quantifying the instability of dense two-layer interpolation.
