For the unit-rate continuous-time heat flow on every finite cone \(K_1\vee H\) with \(d\ge2\) peripheral vertices, the exact universal \(W_1\) amplification envelope is \(F_d(t)=2(d-1)e^{-t}/d-(d-2)e^{-(d+1)t}/d\). It is attained at every positive time **if and only if** \(H\) has an isolated vertex; the optimal peak is transient exactly when \(d\ge4\) and approaches the cone diameter two as \(d\to\infty\).
