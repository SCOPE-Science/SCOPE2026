The stationary total-position density of a harmonically confined three-state run-and-tumble particle determines **all three hidden motor-state densities** by one universal integral transform, for every pair of positive run and rest switching rates. Exactly on \(\gamma_T=\gamma_R+b\), the complete joint law is a beta-position mixture of a position-conditioned binomial motor state, with an explicit and rate-independent conditional distribution.
