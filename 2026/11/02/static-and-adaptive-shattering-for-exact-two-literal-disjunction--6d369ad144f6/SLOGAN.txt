The exact two-literal monotone-disjunction class has a universal static-shattering test by pairwise Boolean OR of coordinate columns, with sharp first five coordinate thresholds \(3,4,6,8,12\). Its VC and Littlestone dimensions are determined for every \(2\le n\le12\), including adaptive-static gaps at \(n=5,7,10,11\) and cardinality-bound failures at \(n=9,12\).
