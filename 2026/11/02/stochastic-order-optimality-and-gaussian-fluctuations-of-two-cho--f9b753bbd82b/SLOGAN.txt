For the two-choice nearest-neighbor walk on a cycle, the fixed policy that moves toward the nearer boundary of the visited arc minimizes **every** cover-time tail against all adaptive controls, not only the expected cover time. Its cover time has exact finite-stage variance formulas, asymptotic variance \(6n-129/4+O(n3^{-n/2})\), and a centered Gaussian limit on the \(\sqrt{6n}\) scale.
