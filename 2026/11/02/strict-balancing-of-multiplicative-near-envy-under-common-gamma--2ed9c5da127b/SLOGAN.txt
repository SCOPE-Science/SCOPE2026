When indivisible goods have iid Gamma-distributed values shared identically by all agents, assigning goods before their values are known maximizes the probability that everyone receives within any finite multiplicative factor \(R>1\) of everyone else **exactly when the bundle counts differ by at most one**. The guarantee is strict under all proper Gamma-shape imbalances, and the sharp small-tolerance probability has the explicit coefficient \(\Gamma(T)/(n^{T-1}\prod_i\Gamma(\theta_i))\).
