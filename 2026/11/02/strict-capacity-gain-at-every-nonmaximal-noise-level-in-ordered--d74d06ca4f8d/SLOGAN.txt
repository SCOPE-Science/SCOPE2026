Adding the mixed ordered symbol strictly increases the capacity of the two-read binary composite DNA channel over two-copy repetition for every crossover probability \(0\le p<1/2\). The advantage is asymptotic to \( (1-2p)^6/\ln 2\) bits per use at maximal noise, and the unique optimal mixed-symbol probability begins as \( (1-2p)^2-3(1-2p)^4\).
