Adding independent blind Poisson resets to one-sided smart-reset Brownian search produces a strictly convex mean first-passage time in the blind-reset rate. A unique positive optimal blind-reset rate exists exactly when the dimensionless feedback strength \(w=L\sqrt{r/D}\) lies below the root \(w_c=1.479815748755\ldots\) of \(w^3+4w^2-12=0\); above this threshold, every blind reset rate strictly worsens the mean.
