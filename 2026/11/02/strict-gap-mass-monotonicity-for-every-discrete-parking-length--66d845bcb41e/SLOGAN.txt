For every integer parking length \(k\ge2\), the limiting expected mass in gaps of length \(l\) strictly increases throughout \(0\le l\le k-1\): \((l+1)g_{k,l+1}>l g_{k,l}\). A uniform likelihood-ratio comparison and an exact interval certificate also show that the continuum gap-mass density \(\gamma f(\gamma)\) is strictly increasing on \((0,1]\).
