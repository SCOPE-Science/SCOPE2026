For the inverse-square grounded-Laplacian trace with one noise-free leader, every positively weighted tree has a unique best vertex or exactly two adjacent tied best vertices: along any simple path the signed edge-comparison brackets increase strictly, so there are no spurious local minima. Exact rerooting recurrences find the entire optimal set and its objective value in \(O(n)\) arithmetic operations, even though the numerical objective values need not be convex along paths.
