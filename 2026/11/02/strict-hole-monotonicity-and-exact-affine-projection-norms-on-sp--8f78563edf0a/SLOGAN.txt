For every real dimension \(d\ge2\), the sharp maximum norm of affine orthogonal projection on a uniform concentric spherical annulus has an explicit incomplete-beta formula and strictly decreases as the central hole grows; exactly the outer-sphere kernel rows maximize. The decrease for a small hole of radius \(r\) is a positive explicit constant times \(r^d\), while all hole sizes share the uniform high-dimensional leading norm \(\sqrt{2d/\pi}\).
