The Mahler volume product of the convex hull of a Euclidean unit ball and two antipodal axial points at distance \(a\ge1\) decreases strictly as \(a\) grows, in every dimension \(d\ge2\), from \(\kappa_d^2\) to the unattained limit \(4\kappa_{d-1}^2/d\). In dimension three its exact law is \(4\pi^2(3+2a^{-2}-a^{-4})/9\).
