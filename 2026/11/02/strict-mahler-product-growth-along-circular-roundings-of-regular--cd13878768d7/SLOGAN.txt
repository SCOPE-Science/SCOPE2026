For every regular planar \(n\)-gon with \(n\ge3\), adding a Euclidean disk of increasing radius strictly increases its Santaló/Mahler area product from \(n^2\sin^2(\pi/n)\) toward \(\pi^2\). The exact polar-support integral gives a positive, sharp inverse-square approach to the disk and a universal \(n^{-2}\) polygon-to-disk deficit at each fixed rounding radius.
