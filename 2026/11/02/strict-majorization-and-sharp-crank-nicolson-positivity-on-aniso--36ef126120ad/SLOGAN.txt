For Crank–Nicolson diffusion on a Boolean cube, directional-rate majorization strictly enlarges the exact positivity interval at fixed total conductance; isotropic rates uniquely minimize the interval and one nearly isolated direction maximizes it under a positive rate floor. The equal-rate threshold has a sharp \(1+1/(2d)+5/(8d^2)\) expansion after normalization, while the most anisotropic admissible threshold grows as the inverse square root of the rate floor.
