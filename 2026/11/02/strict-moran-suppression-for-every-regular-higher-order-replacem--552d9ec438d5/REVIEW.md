# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The model's parental fitness normalization is \(T=ri+N-i\). Uniform incidence degree \(d\) makes the probability that a particular parent--edge incidence is selected exactly fitness divided by \(Td\). For edge counts \(a,b\), only the transitions \(i\mapsto i+b\) and \(i\mapsto i-a\) contribute, yielding the displayed exact exponential drift. A geometric-sum identity proves strict positive drift for every mixed edge of cardinality at least three at all non-neutral fitness values. Connectedness supplies a mixed edge in every transient configuration. Every mixed state can reach all-mutant by monotone propagation; finiteness yields almost-sure absorption. Bounded optional stopping converts the sign of the drift into a strict Moran comparison. At neutral fitness the mutant count is a martingale. A separate rational finite-state checker verifies every nontrivial state for four example hypergraphs and checks the originating article's explicit four-vertex expression. No step uses finite experiments as a proof of the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS (subject to bibliographic uncertainty)

The full originating paper treats selected symmetric families with explicit/numerical fixation calculations and several empirical samples, not every degree-regular higher-order hypergraph at all fitnesses and initial placements. The 2024 multilayer study has a related exponential submartingale, but its model replaces individual neighbors on coupled graph layers; a theorem for that state space does not imply the hyperedge group-replacement drift identity. Pairwise isothermal results give equality for single replacements. Three semantic mathematical-record queries and targeted scientific literature searches returned no directly covering theorem. An equivalent theorem in unindexed or later literature cannot be ruled out categorically.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The originating study highlights a general suppressor phenomenon but leaves a universal analytic justification open. This result supplies an infinite structural class of provable strict suppressors, without graph symmetry, uniform hyperedge size, placement averaging or weak-selection restriction. It identifies simultaneous higher-order replacement, rather than regularity alone, as the mechanism separating strict suppression from ordinary pairwise isothermal equality. The restriction to incidence-regular networks is substantive; no result for arbitrary irregular hypergraphs is claimed.
