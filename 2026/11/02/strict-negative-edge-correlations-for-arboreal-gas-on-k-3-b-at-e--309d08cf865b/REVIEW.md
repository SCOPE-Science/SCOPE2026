For all integers \(b\ge2\) and all real \(x>0\), suppressing degree-two right vertices reduces acyclicity to three left vertices. A degree-three column precludes any other nontrivial right column; otherwise at most two degree-two columns with different left pairs are allowed. This gives the exact forest generating function and, by marking one or two specified edges, the three distinct pair-orbit inclusion polynomials. The displayed formula is only for homogeneous fugacity.
