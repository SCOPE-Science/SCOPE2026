For a one-dimensional noisy run-and-tumble particle in a harmonic trap, the already-known implicit one-peak/two-peak boundary has exactly one solution for each reversal-to-trap ratio in \((0,1)\), and its critical inverse thermal noise is strictly increasing from \(1/2\) to infinity. A self-contained Gaussian-kernel argument proves no extra peaks or re-entrant regimes exist, while faster reversal always lowers the critical diffusivity.
