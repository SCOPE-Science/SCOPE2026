Increasing the reset rate strictly concentrates the entire normalized stationary radius of a phase-separating run-and-tumble gas, even while its dense droplet half-width initially increases; all radial quantiles share a level-independent downward derivative jump at condensation, and the condensed profile strictly dominates a coexisting metastable gas. After rescaling the radius by \(\chi=\sqrt{r(r+\alpha)}/v_0\), the weak-reset law approaches \(\operatorname{Exp}(1)\) with sharp total-variation error \(q/(4\rho_m)+O(q^2)\), while the condensed law differs from \(\operatorname{Exp}(\rho_m)\) by exactly \(\Delta_{\rho_m}/q\), where \(q=M\chi/2\).
