For the clumsy coupon collector with common selected-type loss probability \(p<1\), any strict balancing of two unequal sampling weights increases \(\mathbb E[z^T]\) for every \(0<z<1\) and strictly decreases the expected time to first complete collection. An exact subset-sum transform and positive integral penalty quantify the effect; uniform sampling is the unique minimizer of the mean and maximizer of the probability-generating function.
