For quadratic-exponential weighted Fock dilations \(W_t f(z)=e^{tz^2}f(rz)\), the entire singular-value sequence of \(W_tW_s^*\) is an explicit geometric progression. At fixed nonzero squeezing magnitudes and fixed \(0<r<1\), every individual singular value increases strictly with the relative-phase cosine: perfect alignment maximizes the whole sequence and antialignment minimizes it.
