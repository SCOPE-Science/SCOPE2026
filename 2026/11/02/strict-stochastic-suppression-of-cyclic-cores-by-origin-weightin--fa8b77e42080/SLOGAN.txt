In a uniformly random finite mapping, rewarding each indegree-zero vertex by a factor \(t\ge1\) strictly decreases the entire distribution of the number of cyclic vertices as \(t\) increases, for every size and every nontrivial tail threshold. An exact positive mixture over smaller uniform mappings yields this order and a negative uniform-law covariance asymptotic to \(-e^{-1}\sqrt{\pi n/8}\).
