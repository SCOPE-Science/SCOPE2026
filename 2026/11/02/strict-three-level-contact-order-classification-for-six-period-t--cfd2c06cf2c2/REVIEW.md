# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The precise claim is local, discrete-time mean-field SIS invasion for the 20 balanced six-step schedules on a three-node path. The proof explicitly fixes the Jacobians and word convention, enumerates all dihedral classes, derives symbolic characteristic-polynomial differences and positive Perron trace gaps, and supplies exact rational certificates for the strict disease-free instability/stability example. The companion checker executes every critical algebraic identity and class enumeration. Finite checks are corroboration, not a substitute for the all-parameter argument.

**Originality — PASS within inspected literature, provisional.** Prakash et al. (2010) and Valdano et al. (2014/2015) already provide the general temporal-SIS factor-product invasion criterion; that component is acknowledged and not claimed. The reviewed contribution is the full, strict all-twenty schedule comparison and an exact threshold-separated parameter regime. A closely related six-word ordering in a distinct two-compartment continuous-time progression model has the opposite inequality; it does not imply this statement. General matrix rearrangement theorems, and uninspected follow-up material, remain possible sources of stronger earlier coverage. No independent priority certificate is claimed.

**Value — PASS.** A complete classification, including a change of the local invasion verdict with identical aggregate contacts, isolates a mathematical temporal-aggregation failure relevant to periodic contact-network assessment. The object is natural and the inequalities are rate-uniform. Scope excludes arbitrary networks and nonlinear or stochastic long-run epidemic persistence.
