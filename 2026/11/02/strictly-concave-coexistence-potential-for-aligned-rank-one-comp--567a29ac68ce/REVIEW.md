# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The force-of-infection reduction has an exact converse. The strictly negative Hessian (full-rank recovery-adjusted contact columns) proves constrained uniqueness; the two inward derivatives give the necessary-and-sufficient invasion criterion, including exclusion at equality. The positive symmetrizer and weighted Cauchy–Schwarz give strict negative definiteness, while physical Euler positivity turns the sign-conjugated discrete Jacobian nonnegative, so its Perron root is below one. The exact two-node example and the packaged reproducibility script check the nonstandard algebra. The general proof, not finite tests, establishes the theorem.

## Originality — PASS

The closest source is Gracy et al. (arXiv:2310.13853v1), whose model matches exactly but whose generic stability and exclusion theorems do not derive this rank-one log-potential criterion. Ye et al. (arXiv:2111.07507v1) analyze broad equilibrium structures, including proportional degeneracy, which is excluded here; Anderson and Ye (arXiv:2210.11044) give broader topological analyses without this specific all-n uniqueness test. Doshi et al. (arXiv:2209.07304) study different parameter restrictions. Queries covering separable mixing, potential reductions and equivalent mutual-invasion formulations found no dominating exact claim. Residual risk remains from unindexed equivalent literature and newer models whose full implications were inaccessible.

## Value — PASS

The simultaneous uniqueness, existence criterion and discrete-time stability resolve a nontrivial coexistence question in a biologically meaningful proportionate-mixing contact class. In particular, strict mutual noninvasion cannot coexist with bistable exclusion on these contact networks. Recovery heterogeneity is unrestricted except for the clear full-rank separation, so the classification is not an arbitrary low-dimensional example.

## Scientific limits

No global convergence is proved; distinct contact kernels, proportional recovery profiles, and nonphysical Euler steps are outside the statement. Finite code checks are corroborative only. Originality assessment is limited by accessible literature.
