For \(k\ge3\), the strong chromatic index of \(k\) equal-length paths between two terminals is \(2k\) for length two, \(k+2\) for length four, and \(k+1\) for every other length at least three. The strong clique number equals \(k+1\) throughout lengths at least three, so length four is the unique one-color chromatic obstruction in this entire uniform theta family.
