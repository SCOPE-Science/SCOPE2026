For friendship graphs \(F_m\) of \(m\) triangles sharing one vertex, the complete named-palette strong odd-coloring polynomial has an explicit finite parity-character formula. The minimum palette jumps from \(3\) colors for odd \(m\) to \(5\) for even \(m\), and the exact numbers of optimal colorings are respectively \(3\cdot2^m\) and \(5(12^m+3\cdot4^m)/8\).
