For the five-dimensional free nilpotent Lie algebra on two generators of class three over \(\mathbb F_q\), the numbers of Lie subalgebras of every dimension admit one explicit degree-four polynomial \(P_q(t)\), valid for every finite field. Exactly \(q^2(q+1)\) of its three-dimensional subalgebras are nonabelian Heisenberg algebras, while a uniformly chosen subalgebra has dimension one or two with asymptotic probability \(1/2\) each.
