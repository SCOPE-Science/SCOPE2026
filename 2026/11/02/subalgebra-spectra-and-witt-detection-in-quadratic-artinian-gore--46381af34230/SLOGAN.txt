For every odd finite field, the complete embedded unital-subalgebra polynomial of a quadratic socle-degree-two Gorenstein algebra is a Gaussian-Grassmannian term plus an orthogonal-isotropic term, and its dimension-two coefficient detects the even-dimensional Witt type with gap \(2q^{d/2}\). At fixed \(q\) and growing embedding dimension \(d\), a uniformly random subalgebra almost surely contains the socle and has dimension about \(d/2\); the rare socle-avoiding subalgebras have dimension about \(d/3\), with probability \(q^{-d^2/12+d/6+O_q(1)}\).
