# Review of the proper-coloring universality spectrum

## Correctness — PASS
The greedy arch construction proves the precise index equivalence, including zero and empty host. The legal repeat/new-symbol counts at each stage give independent rational-product derivations of the first arch, later arches, and incomplete suffix; the uniqueness of greedy cuts gives the formal identity. At three colors the algebra reduces to a cubic rational denominator. The uniform proper-word source admits i.i.d. renewal increments with one deterministic initial position and independent geometric waiting times. Standard renewal analysis establishes the fixed-alphabet mean, variance, and Gaussian limits, while a negative-binomial identity establishes the exact ternary binomial tails. The shortest-host count follows from boundary-compatible permutation blocks. A standard-library verifier exhaustively checks more than one million complete hosts, rational coefficients, tail identities, literal pattern membership, and shortest-boundary cases. The finite experiments support but do not prove the infinite assertions.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks
The closest primary source, Adamson et al. (arXiv:2311.10658), provides general algorithms to count universal words in regular host languages, not an explicit spectrum or a probability limit theorem for no-equal-neighbor words. Adamson (arXiv:2304.04583) counts and ranks unrestricted universal words. A publicly indexed binary universality distribution has only a two-symbol domain and does not imply the ternary law; the proper two-symbol language is deterministic. Statement-level searches for proper-coloring hosts, equivalent no-consecutive-repeat constraints, Markov coupon epochs, exact ternary bivariate denominators, and binomial survival tails did not reveal equivalent coverage. Full material comparisons were made with relevant sections of the two arXiv preprints. Residual risk: a general unindexed renewal/regular-language source could give an equivalent specialization; a 2024 dissertation was inaccessible at its host during checking. This is a best-knowledge originality assessment, not proof of global novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Proper-coloring words are a fundamental graph-coloring/regular-language constraint and the smallest non-deterministic case is ternary. The result supplies an explicit all-alphabet distribution, a strikingly simple ternary rational form, an exact finite-binomial formula for the probability of covering all target words, sharp shortest-host enumerators, and the correct Gaussian fluctuation scale. None of these quantitative data is supplied by the cited generic counting algorithms, and the combination permits direct finite-length parameter decisions without constructing automata. The central limit statement is a standard renewal consequence, not a purported new limit theorem method.
