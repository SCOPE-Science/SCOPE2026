For words over \(q\) labeled symbols with no equal neighbors, the entire scattered-subsequence universality-index distribution has an explicit rational generating function, and its typical value obeys a renewal Gaussian law with closed harmonic-number constants. On three symbols, the full bivariate generating function collapses to \(\frac{1+2z+3z^2+2uz^3}{1-z-4uz^3}\), and the probability of being \(k\)-universal is an exact fair-binomial tail.
