For the splitting graphs of all paths and all cycles of length at least four, the super domination number has an exact closed form: \(\lceil(5n-2)/4\rceil\) for paths except \(n=6\), and \(\lceil5n/4\rceil+\mathbf1_{\{n\equiv4\pmod8\}}\) for cycles. An \(81\)-state local potential proves the bounds, and tight eight-step insertions attain them.
