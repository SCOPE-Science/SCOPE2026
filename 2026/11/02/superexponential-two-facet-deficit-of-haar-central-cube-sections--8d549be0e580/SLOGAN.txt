For a Haar-uniform central hyperplane section of the \(d\)-cube, losing one opposite pair of facets is superexponentially rare: the probability is \(d e^{-1}/[\pi^{(d-1)/2}\Gamma((d+1)/2)]\) times \(1+7/[2(d-1)]+O(d^{-2})\). The sharp factor and correction come from an exact half-normal/Dirichlet rare-event representation.
