For every labelled cycle and integer \(k\ge2\), all minimum total \(\{k\}\)-dominating functions admit a complete support-refined census. A minimum can leave vertices unlabeled exactly when the cycle length is divisible by four; in that case the full support polynomial is \(4u^{n/2}+4(k-1)u^{3n/4}+(k-1)^2u^n\), whereas at all other lengths every minimum is strictly positive everywhere.
