For every complete multipartite graph with at least three parts and every surplus palette \(Q>\chi(G)\), a successful non-total forced colouring is exactly a restriction of a surjective proper colouring in which multicoloured parts are fully seeded and at most one monochromatic part is wholly unseeded; this gives an exact Stirling-number enumeration of every seed size. For balanced \(K_{t,\ldots,t}\), minimum seed sizes follow a sharp palette staircase with jumps of \(t-1\), followed by a final jump to the entire vertex set.
