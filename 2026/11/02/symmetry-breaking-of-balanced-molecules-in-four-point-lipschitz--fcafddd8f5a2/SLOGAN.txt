On the four-point equilateral metric, Euclidean-balanced signed molecules maximize the free-space-to-Euclidean ratio at \(p=1\), but instantly lose even local optimality for every \(0<p<1\). A natural two-pair family has an exact \(p\)-transport profile and a unique asymmetric maximizer characterized by one scalar equation; no unrestricted \(p<1\) optimum is claimed.
