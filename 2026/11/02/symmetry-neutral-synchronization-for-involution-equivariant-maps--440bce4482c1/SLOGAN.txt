For every involution on a finite \(N\)-point set with at least one fixed point, exactly \(1/N\) of the commuting transformations have a positive power that is constant, just as for unrestricted transformations. Consequently, exactly \(n^{n^2-2}\) of the \(n^{n^2}\) binary operations on an \(n\)-element set (\(n\geq2\)) have a constant power in the Kim–Neggers magma monoid.
