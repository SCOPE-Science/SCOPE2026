For the continuous-time, three-site photonic quantum walk with any positive dephasing rate, a pure center-localized photon relaxes toward the maximally mixed state exponentially faster than a suitably close, reflection-odd mixed state, despite starting farther from equilibrium in relative entropy. An exact parity factorization and cubic stability inequality prove a strict gap at every dephasing strength, with entropy-rate acceleration tending to \(5/4\) in weak noise and \(3\) in strong noise.
