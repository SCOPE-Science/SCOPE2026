For a complete-bipartite graph evolution algebra in characteristic two, the reduced variety of square-zero derivations is geometrically irreducible, rational, and has dimension \(\lfloor a^2/4\rfloor+\lfloor b^2/4\rfloor\). Its entire finite-field rank spectrum factors into explicit symplectic-isotropic and nonsingular-symmetric-form counts, with a distinct even-side rank-shift mechanism.
