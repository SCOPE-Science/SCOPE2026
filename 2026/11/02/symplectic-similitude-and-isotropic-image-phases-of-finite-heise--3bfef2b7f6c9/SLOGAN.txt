For center-affine, quotient-linear maps on any odd-prime finite Heisenberg group, the weight-one Rota–Baxter identity is equivalent to a symplectic-similitude equation for \(T-\mu I\) plus one uniquely determined quadratic central term and an arbitrary linear functional. The complete count exhibits a sharp transition from invertible similitudes when \(\mu\notin\{0,-1\}\) to rank-\(\le n\) maps with isotropic image at the two exceptional scalars.
