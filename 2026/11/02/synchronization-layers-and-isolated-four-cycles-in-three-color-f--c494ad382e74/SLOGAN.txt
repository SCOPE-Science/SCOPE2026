For three-color inhibitory firefly dynamics on every thick complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), all trajectories either synchronize within a sharp six updates or start on a nonsynchronized three- or four-cycle; no transient trajectory feeds an asynchronous orbit. The complete synchronization-time distribution is an explicit polynomial in \(2^a,2^b,3^a,3^b\), with balanced-shore nonsynchronization probability asymptotic to \(2(4/9)^n\) at \(a=b=n\).
