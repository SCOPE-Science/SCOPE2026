# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The teaching-set/vertex-cover equivalence has both directions proved via one-edge exchanges and the fundamental cut property. König and Hall yield the exact matching invariant. The tree-side extremum follows from the Nash-Williams–Tutte two-spanning-tree partition criterion; its converse constructs a target tree adapted to an arbitrary connected-block partition. The cotree-side extremum uses the Nash-Williams arboricity criterion in both directions. Explicit disjoint-tree and two-forest witnesses establish every complete-bipartite parameter range. An independently expressed definition-based teaching search and exhaustive finite graph enumeration corroborate the boundary cases. Finite tests are not treated as universal proofs.

The two exact global equivalences connect learning complexity to the natural pack-two-trees and cover-by-two-forests structural thresholds, while the complete-bipartite theorem supplies a sharp all-size family and an intermediate obstruction example shows the limits of both extremal laws.
