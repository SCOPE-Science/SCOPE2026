For any target spanning tree, the minimum number of positive and negative edge labels needed to teach it equals the maximum matching size of its bipartite fundamental-exchange graph. All trees meet the tree-side bound exactly when the graph packs two edge-disjoint spanning trees, and all meet the cotree-side bound exactly when its edges split into two forests; in particular every spanning tree of \(K_{p,q}\) has teaching number \(\min\{p+q-1,(p-1)(q-1)\}\).
