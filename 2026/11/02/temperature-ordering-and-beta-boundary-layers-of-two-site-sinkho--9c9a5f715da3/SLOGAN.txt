The two-site geodesic Sinkhorn length metric has exactly temperature-ordered Dirac-to-Dirac geodesics whose zero-regularization limit is the quantile curve of \(\operatorname{Beta}(3/4,3/4)\). An explicit \(\beta^{3/4}\) endpoint-time layer, with mass scale \(\beta\), and a sharp fractional interior correction quantify the singular limit rather than merely its distance growth.
