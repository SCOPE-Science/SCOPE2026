In a periodically forced higher-order Prisoner's Dilemma, every interior periodic cooperative state must spend more than one-third of its interaction budget on three-person games, and the excess equals an exact weighted squared-deviation cost. Harmonic modulation raises the sharp cooperation fold from \(1/3\) by \(3\eta^2/(32\omega^2)+O_\omega(\eta^4)\), while the resulting stable and unstable periodic states have exactly reciprocal Floquet multipliers.
