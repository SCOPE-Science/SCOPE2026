# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the stated relational language the complete atomic diagram over e,x,y is determined by equalities, the two involution tests, the two directed square tests, and the inverse test; cancellation excludes any other nontrivial product atom. Over a first x the exceptional responses are e,x,x⁻¹,x² and square roots of x. Overlaps distinguish orders 1,2,3,4 and ≥5. A generic order-three response must also avoid x² (relevant for x of order 6); at least four order-three elements suffice, since at most two order-three elements among the exceptional responses must be excluded, and an order-three square root of x is exceptional only for x of order three, where it is x⁻¹. At least two involutions suffice for a generic involution response. The presence of each resulting rooted profile is a rank-two test. For PSL₂, a semisimple lift of projective order >2 in odd characteristic has nonzero trace; a projective centralizer cannot conjugate it to its negative and hence is the cyclic torus. Trace-zero involution lifts are conjugate: their GL₂ conjugacy can be adjusted to determinant one by the surjective norm or product determinant of the lift centralizer. Their projective centralizer has size at most twice the torus order, so the conjugacy class has more than one element. In even characteristic the q−1 nontrivial translations suffice for involutions. Translations give ≥4 order-three elements in characteristic three for q≥9; otherwise the order-three torus element's conjugacy class has size |Gq| divided by its torus order, which is ≥4. Odd-order elements are squares intrinsically. For odd q the unique even torus controls the three composite-order flags and has a nonsquare generator of order ≥5 exactly when its size is ≥6, i.e. q≥11. Its order-three and order-four root flags are uniform; in characteristic three order-six elements are absent. The exceptional S₃ and A₄ groups have no order≥5 profile and differ on generic noninvolution responses to order three. The congruences leave six triples for U=1 and two complete profiles for U=0; a prime-power enumeration through 47 verifies the exact least representatives. The class with all flags set has q≥47, and q(q²−1)/2 is an increasing lower bound for the order, so its minimum is 51,888.

## originality

PASS

Both the group-structure ingredients and the logical profile method have antecedents, including a very close alternating-group comparison. The supported distinct claim is the uniform evaluation of those profiles on PSL₂ over all finite fields, including square-root uniformity, the characteristic split, the two exceptional small groups and the sharp within-family representatives.

## value

PASS

The result gives an exact small-depth invariant in a standard finite simple group family of unbounded order. It separates what the restricted relational logic actually sees—squares, roots and their small-order overlaps—from the much larger full element-order spectrum, and supplies a sharp finite representative bound within the natural family.
