In the identity-named ternary multiplication language, the groups \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\) have exactly ten first-order theories at quantifier rank two. For field sizes at least four the entire theory is determined by the existence of elements of orders four, six and eight, together with an unsquareable element of order at least five; the largest least representative is \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_{47})\), of order \(51\,888\).
