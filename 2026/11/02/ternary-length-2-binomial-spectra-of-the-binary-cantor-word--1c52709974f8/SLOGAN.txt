At every ternary length \(3^k\), the complete \(2\)-binomial profile spectrum of the binary Cantor word is a union of two explicit arithmetic progressions for each one-count, giving an exact \(2^k\)-term complexity formula. For every \(k\ge3\), at least \(2\cdot3^{k-3}+1\) distinct profiles each identify two different factors, establishing a linear family of genuine binomial collisions.
