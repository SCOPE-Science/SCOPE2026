Mohammadi–Zaffalon classify rank-two positroids and dimensions, while Machacek already establishes the unrefined ternary point count (and OEIS A001794 records the same sequence). Thus **the total is not new**; only the explicit geometric bijection and dimension-refined interpretation are advanced. The rank-two conclusions are close combinations of known facts, so earlier equivalence under a different formulation is a material historical-priority risk. Searches of the named works, relevant related literature and available mathematical research records found no explicit equivalent statement; that negative search is not a uniqueness proof.
