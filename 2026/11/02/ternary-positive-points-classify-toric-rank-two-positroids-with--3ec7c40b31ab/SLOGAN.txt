The support map gives a canonical bijection between the ternary totally nonnegative Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Gr}^{\ge0}(2,n)(\mathbb F_3)\) and the distinct toric rank-two positroid varieties in \(\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathbb C}(2,n)\). Their complex-dimension enumerator is \(\binom n2(1+z)^{n-2}+\binom n3 z^2(1+z)^{n-3}\); its unrefined total was already known from finite-field point counting.
