# Scope of the revised finding

The result treats the marked edge collisions over the full completed coefficient base and gives their simultaneous fibre strata, rather than only the generic twelve-node chamber or a paired-edge specialization. The edge Jacobian length is included for context and follows from the known ideal formula. The exact trace criterion on the all-nonzero open stratum and the generic splitting group are not asserted as new. The proof of the relative normal form works over the completed base ring, not just in separate geometric fibres.
