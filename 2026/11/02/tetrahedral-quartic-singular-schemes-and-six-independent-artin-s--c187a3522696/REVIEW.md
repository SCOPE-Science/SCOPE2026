Differentiation gives the four coordinate triple products exactly, whose common projective zero scheme is the union of six coordinate edges; vertices lie outside the surface because the relevant diagonal coefficients are units. On every edge, the surface cuts the double of a nonzero binary quadratic, furnishing a finite flat relative Cartier divisor of degree four. The six pieces are disjoint, so the total length is 24.
