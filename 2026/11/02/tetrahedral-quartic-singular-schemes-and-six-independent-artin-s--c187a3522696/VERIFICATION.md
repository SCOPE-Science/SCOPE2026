# Verification and reproducibility

The exact universal argument is in `RESULT.md`, Sections Proof Steps 1–4. The singular support is found by differentiating the given defining equation, and each relative component is the square of a nonzero binary quadratic on a coordinate edge. The local Jacobian quotient determines lengths; formal transverse splitting determines the ADE types. The six Artin–Schreier classes have distinct odd-order poles after adjoining purely inseparable diagonal square roots, so their Galois extensions are independent. Over finite fields the trace zero test is equivalent to solvability of each labeled edge quadratic.

Execute `python3 verify_tetrahedral.py` from the directory containing that standalone script. It enumerates all 64 binary-coefficient quartics over the complete projective space of 85 points over `GF(4)` and checks their singular supports against direct edge equations; it then verifies 4,028 nonzero-diagonal coefficient triples for quadratic splitting over `GF(4)`, `GF(8)`, and `GF(16)`.

Observed successful terminal marker: `TETRAHEDRAL_CHAR2_64_SURFACES_4028_TRACE_CASES_PASS`.

Finite checks cannot establish the theorem for all fields, the 24-length family scheme, or the 64-order generic Galois group. Those require the full symbolic proof. Independent external mathematical audit has not been performed.
