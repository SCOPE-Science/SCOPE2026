The previously enumerated \(3{,}872\) minimum zero-forcing sets of the five-regular Clebsch graph form a connected, self-centered exchange graph of diameter \(6\), with an exact all-pairs distance spectrum. Its exact distance from the Johnson lower bound is zero for all but \(240\) unordered pairs; each exceptional pair requires exactly one extra exchange and arises uniquely as one of three opposite-path splittings of an induced \(C_6\sqcup2K_1\), of which there are exactly \(80\).
