The smooth Fermat cubic fourfold over the unramified dyadic quadratic ring has a finite, nonflat Fano scheme of planes: exactly \(405\) sections survive from characteristic zero, while \(486\) additional reduced characteristic-two planes cannot lift even modulo four. Those \(486\) form the hexacode orbit inside the classical \(891\)-plane Hermitian configuration, and their intersection incidences with the surviving \(405\) planes are determined exactly.
