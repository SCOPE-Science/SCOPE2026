# Scope of the revised finding

The result identifies the rational complement of the 56 exceptional curves as the full double cover of an explicit 24-point axis orbit. The numerical deficit is a consequence of known Fermat bitangent incidences and the standard degree-two del Pezzo boundary formula. The proof uses that count to exhaust the complement after identifying a specific set of uncovered fibres; it does not assert novelty for the incidence table, the point counts alone, or any classification of other surfaces over the same field.
