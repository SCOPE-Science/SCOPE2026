# Review of the Fermat degree-two del Pezzo exceptional-curve complement

**Correctness (PASS).** The 28 bitangents are explicitly split over \(\mathbb F_{25}\) and exhaust the geometric bitangents. Every one of 651 projective base points is counted exactly, with its double-cover fibre and incidence to the 28 bitangent lines. The standalone verifier reconstructs all 48 uncovered points, the 24-point coordinate criterion and the symmetry orbit. This is a complete finite-field proof, not an extrapolation.

**Originality (PASS, with a historical risk).** Knecht and Reyes' study of full degree-two del Pezzo surfaces (arXiv:1510.04621) names field size 25 as unresolved in its survey. Kresch and Tschinkel (2004) already establish the bitangent and exceptional-curve formulas for diagonal quartics. Neither inspected full text gives the new exact 778/48 split or the 24-point complement locus. Older unindexed counts cannot be excluded.

**Value (PASS).** The invariant is the central fullness question for a natural symmetric split surface at a field size singled out by prior work, and its 48-point complement forms an explicit orbit rather than an arbitrary computed subset. This does not decide the existence of full surfaces of other types over the same field.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
