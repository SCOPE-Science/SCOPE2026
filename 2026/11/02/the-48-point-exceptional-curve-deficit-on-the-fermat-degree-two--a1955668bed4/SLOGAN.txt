For the split Fermat degree-two del Pezzo surface \(w^2=x^4+y^4+z^4\) over \(\mathbb F_{25}\), its 56 exceptional curves cover exactly 778 of 826 rational points. The 48-point complement lies in one symmetry orbit and projects two-to-one to 24 axis points characterized by a nonidentity cube-root fourth-power ratio.
