Among the smooth characteristic-five curves \(X^6+Y^6+Z^6+t(X^3Y^3+Y^3Z^3+Z^3X^3)=0\), the Fermat member has \(5\)-rank zero and \(a\)-number ten, while every non-Fermat member has \(5\)-rank eight and \(a\)-number two. An exact twelve-entry Hasse--Witt matrix explains why the rank-eight stratum is stable under all Frobenius iterates.
