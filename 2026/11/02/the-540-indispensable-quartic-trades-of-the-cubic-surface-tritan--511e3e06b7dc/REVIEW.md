# Review of the cubic-surface tritangent trade classification

## Correctness

PASS. Each tritangent class is anticanonical in the six-point blowup. Signature equality is exactly equality of images in the defined 27-variable monomial algebra. The standalone exact checker exhausts all 1,035 degree-two, 16,215 degree-three and 194,580 degree-four monomials and confirms the 120 cubic, 5,400 lifted-quartic and 540 coprime-quartic counts. The algebraic argument proves indispensability, not just numerical detection. Simple-root reflections reproduce the single 540-element orbit. The claim does not reach degree five.

## Originality

PASS, with priority qualification. Elsenhans--Jahnel (arXiv:1006.1474, especially Section 2) classify the 120 Steiner-trihedral pairs: these explain the cubic relations and are explicitly credited. Their subsequent Brauer-group treatment (arXiv:1110.2086, Section 3) reuses these 120 pairs, but neither inspected text supplies 540 indispensable degree-four monomial-incidence relations. Sturmfels--Xu (arXiv:0803.0892, Section 5) analyze a distinct 27-variable Cox--Nagata presentation; their 81 quadratic toric initial relations are not relations among 45 tritangent variables. Available historical claims do not imply this degree-four minimal Markov layer. Unknown classical terminology or an unpublished table might encode equivalent quartic trades.

## Value

PASS. The minimal binomial/Markov degree and generator count of a canonical 45-by-27 geometric incidence semigroup are natural, exact invariants. The classical 120 relations do not suffice: exactly 540 further quartic moves are forced, forming one geometric symmetry orbit. This gives a substantive structural obstruction to cubic generation, without requiring a complete higher-degree Markov basis.

## Limits

The result is a full degree-at-most-four characterization, not a complete generating set. It treats the incidence monomial algebra rather than the Cox ring or actual tritangent plane equations. Historical priority needs independent assessment.
