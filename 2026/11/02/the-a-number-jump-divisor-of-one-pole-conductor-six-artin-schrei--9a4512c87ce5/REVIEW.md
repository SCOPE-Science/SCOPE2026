# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The genus, pole valuations, ten-element differential basis, exact Cartier coefficient identity, and Frobenius-root normalization have been reconstructed. The rank argument treats the origin separately; away from it, columns 5–7 span one direction, column 10 contributes a new independent direction, and the intervening 2-by-2 block has determinant \(B^2+2A^3+4AC\), whose fifth power is the stated equation. The quadratic coordinate change reduces the jumping divisor to a rank-three ordinary quadric cone; its singular locus has codimension two in the hypersurface, establishing normality. Two actual standalone verification programs passed. No finite enumeration is presented as a proof over the algebraic closure.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The closest source is Abney-McPeek et al., arXiv:2003.09028, notably its characteristic-five binomial/trinomial families: it establishes lower-bound attainability rather than all-coefficient rank jumps or the normal \(A_1\) jumping locus. Farnell–Pries, arXiv:1202.4183, construct constant-rank families under different hypotheses; Zhou, arXiv:1901.08375, treats superspecial Artin–Schreier curves more broadly, but not the full varying-rank coefficient locus. The comparative search also considered genus-ten conductor-six aliases, Cartier minors, superspecial cases, and geometric singularity descriptions; none of the accessible relevant claims implies the asserted universal stratification. This is a best-knowledge priority assessment, not a proof that unindexed prior results do not exist. In particular, the known superspecial isomorphism classification and known generic minimum are not asserted as new.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The conductor-six family is the full natural normalized coefficient family for one-branch characteristic-five Artin–Schreier covers of genus ten, whose generic minimum \(a\)-number is a published benchmark. The complete exceptional-locus equation, exclusion of intermediate \(a\)-numbers, irreducible normal divisor, and transverse \(A_1\) singularity are structural moduli-stratification information, not arbitrary parameter specializations or mere reproduction of a known minimum.

## Limitations
Results are coefficient-marked and restricted to one branch and characteristic five. The individual coefficients are not asserted to parametrize pairwise nonisomorphic curves, and no full Newton-polygon or Ekedahl–Oort classification is given. Unindexed equivalent prior computations remain a residual priority risk.
