Every one-pole conductor-six Artin–Schreier curve in characteristic five has Jacobian \(a\)-number exactly \(6\), \(7\), or \(10\), controlled by the explicit equation \(b^2+2a^3+4ac=0\). Its exceptional divisor is a normal irreducible \(A_1\)-cone times a line, and the superspecial locus is exactly its singular line.
