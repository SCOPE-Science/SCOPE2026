On every indexed binary ring of length \(n\ge3\), the all-zero output is the **unique** most probable result of one Rule-22 update, with exactly \(1+t_n\) input predecessors where \(t_0=3,t_1=t_2=1\) and \(t_n=t_{n-1}+t_{n-3}\). A componentwise transfer-matrix envelope proves a strict all-length bound for every other output, not just an asymptotic preference.
