In a three-parameter central-extension family of filiform Leibniz algebras, the space of almost-inner derivations acquires exactly one non-inner direction if and only if \(\theta_3\ne0\), uniformly over every field and in every dimension \(n\ge4\). This supplies the missing parameter condition in a previously published unconditional decomposition and yields exactly \(q^{n-1}\) or \(q^n\) almost-inner derivations over \(\mathbb F_q\).
