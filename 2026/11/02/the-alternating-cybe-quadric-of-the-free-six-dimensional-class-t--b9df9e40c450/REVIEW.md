# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof classifies **all** alternating tensor solutions of the ordinary CYBE in the stated free class-two Lie algebra. Its decisive steps are the independent exterior-degree decomposition, the three coefficients proportional to the squares of the \(\bigwedge^2 V\) coordinates, the exact principal-minor identity for the remaining \(V\wedge Z\) coordinates, the rank-nine quadratic form, and injectivity of the odd-characteristic cobracket map. The finite-field count is a standard odd-dimensional nondegenerate quadratic-form count. The package checker exercises the defining tensor equation directly for full binary coefficient space and for all ternary middle blocks; arithmetic checks do not substitute for the proof. The reduced-variety and odd-characteristic boundaries are stated precisely.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk

The best algebra-specific comparison is Farinati–Jancsa, arXiv:1607.00300v1, Sections 1–3, especially Section 3 and Examples 3.2 and 3.6. It develops free class-two Lie bialgebra coboundaries, including a larger compatible parameter space, but does not solve the **ordinary alternating CYBE** for all middle matrices or give the rank-nine quadric and \(q^{11}\)/\(q^8\) counts. Since coboundary co-Jacobi permits parameters with nonzero central Schouten square, coverage of coboundaries alone is not a stronger statement covering the triangular cutoff. The other inspected papers are either general CYBE operator theory (Bai–Guo–Ni), low-dimensional three-dimensional classifications (Zhang), or the free nilpotent model and automorphism theory (Benito–de-la-Concepción). published-finding corpus searches by objects, parameter matrix, classical Yang–Baxter equation, tensor/triangular equivalents, and finite-field enumeration returned no matching or dominating statement, but a negative search result is not a uniqueness proof. An unindexed special calculation or an implicit corollary of older Lie-bialgebra tables remains possible.

## Value — PASS

The six-dimensional three-generator free class-two algebra is the smallest free class-two nilpotent algebra beyond the three-dimensional Heisenberg algebra. In a canonical \(15\)-parameter tensor space, the entire alternating CYBE locus becomes a geometrically irreducible nondegenerate quadric times affine space; this is a natural complete classification, not an arbitrary finite slice. The exact \(q^{11}\) tensor census and \(q^8\) triangular-cobracket census quantify the strict difference between unrestricted coboundary parameters and triangular solutions in a standard Lie-bialgebra family. They also provide a tractable baseline for studying orbit classes and higher free ranks. No claim is made about isomorphism orbits or arbitrary Lie bialgebra structures.
