Every alternating ordinary classical Yang–Baxter solution on the six-dimensional free class-two Lie algebra (in characteristic different from \(2\)) is a free central bivector plus a \(3\times3\) matrix \(B\) satisfying the single nondegenerate quadric equation \(\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{adj}B)=0\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) for odd \(q\), there are exactly \(q^{11}\) such tensors and \(q^8\) distinct induced triangular coboundary maps.
