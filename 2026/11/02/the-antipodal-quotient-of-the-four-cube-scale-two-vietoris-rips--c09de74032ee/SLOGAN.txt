For the sixteen vertices of the four-cube at inclusive Hamming–Rips scale two, the quotient by simultaneous complementation has integral homotopy type \(\mathbb{RP}^3\vee\bigvee^4 S^3\). A primitive nine-sphere cycle basis identifies the projective-space summand and four additional three-spheres, including the quotient’s \(\mathbb Z/2\) fundamental group.
