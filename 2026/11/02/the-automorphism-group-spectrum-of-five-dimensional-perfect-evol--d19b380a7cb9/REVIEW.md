# Scientific review: five-dimensional binary perfect evolution algebras

**Correctness — PASS.** Perfect evolution algebras have invertible structure matrices and their natural-basis changes are monomial; over the binary field these are exactly permutations. Therefore full algebra automorphism groups coincide with stabilizers under simultaneous coordinate permutations. A finite exhaustive search generates all 156 subgroups of \(S_5\), counts their invariant invertible matrices using orbital-constant binary entries and exact rank elimination, then performs subgroup Möbius inversion. The executable `verify.py` verifies all 16 group-type orbit counts, their weighted labeled-matrix totals, and a separate Burnside sum from seven permutation cycle classes. It reproduces the known lower-dimensional orbit count of 908 in dimension four.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources.** Sriwongsa--Zou (arXiv:2202.01333) discuss automorphism groups and full symmetric-group actions; Wei--Zou (arXiv:2310.12368) give the general isomorphism/Burnside framework and published computations through dimension four; Costoya et al. (arXiv:2401.05924) supply constraints on possible permutation images. The claim here is the exact *dimension-five binary stabilizer isomorphism-type distribution*, including intermediate groups and impossible types. Generic Burnside counts do not determine this distribution. Searches for binary five-vertex nonsingular adjacency matrices and for 85,411 matrix orbits found no exact table. Research-record searches and accessible ledger claims provided no equivalent implication. Originality is not a statement of exhaustive publication priority.

**Value — PASS.** The first dimension beyond the principal published explicit census is a natural next benchmark, and automorphism-group types distinguish the geometry of the isomorphism-class space more finely than the aggregate number of classes. The theorem gives the proportion of rigid classes, precise rare-symmetry strata, and three sharp nonexistence cases over an unrestricted finite collection of 9,999,360 labeled perfect multiplications.

**Limitations and risk.** The result is exact for five-dimensional perfect algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\), not general fields. A differently phrased classification of looped five-vertex directed graphs having nonsingular binary adjacency matrices could imply the result; accessible evidence did not locate one. Computation is an exhaustive finite certificate, not an infinite-field theorem. Independent review remains outstanding.
