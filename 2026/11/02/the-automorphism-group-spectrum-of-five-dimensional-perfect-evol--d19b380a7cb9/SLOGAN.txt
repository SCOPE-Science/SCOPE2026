Exactly 85,411 five-dimensional perfect evolution algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\) occur up to isomorphism, and their full automorphism groups have an exact 16-type subgroup spectrum. Among these classes, 81,408 are rigid, while none has full automorphism group \(A_4\), \(A_5\), or the Frobenius group of order 20; the count is certified by exhaustive subgroup-lattice inversion.
