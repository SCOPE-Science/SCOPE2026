On a finite \(\mathsf{S4.2}\) frame, the mere-belief operator \(\Diamond\Box p\land\neg\Box p\) recovers every nonterminal accessibility row; its only ambiguity is an arbitrary partition of terminal worlds unseen by any nonterminal world. Each fiber therefore has Bell-number size, and the number of distinct operators has EGF \(\exp(x+(e^x-1)(P(x)-1))\), where \(P(x)\) enumerates labelled preorders.
