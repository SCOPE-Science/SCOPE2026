# The boundary gluing of rank-one averaging operators on full matrix algebras

## Finding

Let \(k\) be a field and \(n\ge2\). In the affine space of \(k\)-linear endomorphisms of \(M_n(k)\), define the **reduced** variety \(\mathcal A_n\) by operator rank at most one and the two-sided averaging identities
\[
P(X)P(Y)=P(P(X)Y)=P(XP(Y))\quad(X,Y\in M_n(k)).
\]
Then \(\mathcal A_n\) has exactly \(n\) geometrically irreducible components \(\mathcal C_s\), indexed by \(1\le s\le n\), and
\[
\dim\mathcal C_s=2ns-s^2.
\]
The generic point of \(\mathcal C_s\) has image line generated by a rank-\(s\) idempotent. Every nonzero square-zero-image operator with an image generator of matrix rank \(r\) lies in \(\mathcal C_{n-r}\), by an explicit degeneration. No additional components arise from the square-zero-image locus. The zero operator lies in every component.

For every prime power \(q\), set \({n\brack s}_q\) equal to the Gaussian binomial coefficient. The **exact numbers of nonzero** operators in the idempotent-image and square-zero-image rank strata are respectively
\[
E_{n,s}(q)={n\brack s}_q q^{s(n-s)}(q^{s^2}-1),\qquad 1\le s\le n,
\]
\[
Z_{n,r}(q)=\frac{{n\brack r}_q{n-r\brack r}_q|\mathrm{GL}_r(q)|}{q-1}\bigl(q^{(n-r)^2}-1\bigr),\qquad1\le r\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor.
\]
Thus \(A_n(q)=\sum_s E_{n,s}(q)+\sum_r Z_{n,r}(q)\). For fixed \(q\),
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}q^{-n^2} A_n(q)=\frac{q\mathcal R(q^{-1})-1}{q-1},\qquad
\mathcal R(Q)=\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{Q^{j^2}}{\prod_{i=1}^j(1-Q^i)}
=\prod_{h\ge0}\frac1{(1-Q^{5h+1})(1-Q^{5h+4})}.
\]
The identity and the idempotent-only Rogers–Ramanujan law are prior ingredients; the component gluing and full-variety classification are the central conclusions.

## Assumptions and scope

Algebraic statements are over arbitrary fields, including characteristic two, and component statements are geometric (after extending to an algebraic closure). A nonzero rank-one endomorphism has the pure-tensor form \(P(X)=\operatorname{tr}(FX)U\) for nonzero matrices \(F,U\), with the sole representation ambiguity \((F,U)\sim(c^{-1}F,cU)\) for \(c\in k^\times\). The operator rank is the rank of \(P\) as an endomorphism; the **image-matrix rank** is the ordinary rank of its nonzero image generator \(U\). The two notions must not be conflated. The variety is reduced; no nonreduced scheme assertion is made.

## Proof

**1. Rank-one equations.** Substitute \(P(X)=\varphi(X)U\) with \(\varphi(X)=\operatorname{tr}(FX)\) into the identities. Because \(\varphi\ne0\), equality for all \(X,Y\) forces \(U^2=\lambda U\) and \(\varphi(UX)=\varphi(XU)=\lambda\varphi(X)\) for a scalar \(\lambda\in k\). Nondegeneracy of the trace pairing on \(M_n(k)\) turns these into \(UF=FU=\lambda F\). Conversely, these matrix conditions imply both averaging identities. If \(\lambda\ne0\), the unique idempotent generator of the image line is \(p=\lambda^{-1}U\); absorbing the scalar into \(F\) gives \(P(X)=\operatorname{tr}(FX)p\), with \(p^2=p\) and \(F=pFp\ne0\). If \(\lambda=0\), then \(U^2=0\) and \(FU=UF=0\).

**2. Irreducible non-square-zero strata.** An idempotent of matrix rank \(s\) is the projection onto an \(s\)-space along a complementary \((n-s)\)-space. Its parameter variety is geometrically irreducible, smooth and of dimension \(2s(n-s)\). Above each such \(p\), the possible \(F\) form the \(s^2\)-dimensional linear space \(pM_np\), with its origin removed. The resulting stratum \(\mathcal E_s\) is geometrically irreducible of dimension \(2s(n-s)+s^2=2ns-s^2\). The normalization \(p\) is determined by the image line, so this parametrization counts each operator exactly once.

**3. All square-zero points are boundary points.** Let \(U\ne0\), \(U^2=0\), with \(r=\operatorname{rank}U\), so \(r\le n/2\). Choose \(W=\operatorname{im}U\), a complement \(Z\) to \(W\) inside \(\ker U\), and a complement \(W'\) to \(\ker U\). Arrange the basis so \(U\) maps \(W'\) identically onto \(W\). Since \(FU=UF=0\), the corresponding block matrices have the forms
\[
U=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&I_r\\0&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
F=\begin{pmatrix}0&A&B\\0&C&D\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For the indeterminate \(t\ne0\), set
\[
p_t=\begin{pmatrix}I_r&0&t^{-1}I_r\\0&I_{n-2r}&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
F_t=\begin{pmatrix}t^2B&tA&tB\\t^2D&tC&tD\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Direct block multiplication gives \(p_t^2=p_t\) and \(p_tF_t=F_tp_t=F_t\). Over \(k(t)\), \(F_t\ne0\) because \(F\ne0\), and \(p_t\) has matrix rank \(n-r\). The entries of the *linear operator* \(P_t(X)=\operatorname{tr}(F_tX)p_t\) are polynomials in \(t\), despite the displayed negative power in \(p_t\). Evaluating at \(t=0\), only the \(t^{-1}\) upper-right block of \(p_t\) times the first-order part of \(F_t\) survives, giving precisely \(P_0(X)=\operatorname{tr}(FX)U\). Therefore the nonzero square-zero-image rank-\(r\) stratum lies in \(\overline{\mathcal E_{n-r}}\). Multiplying the functional in an arbitrary nonzero averaging map by \(t\) degenerates to the zero operator.

**4. Distinct components.** Define \(\mathcal C_s=\overline{\mathcal E_s}\) inside the reduced averaging variety. Step 3 proves that \(\mathcal A_n=\bigcup_{s=1}^n\mathcal C_s\). Distinct non-square-zero strata cannot lie in one another's closures: a family of image lines tending to a non-square-zero image line has a normalized idempotent generator tending to a nonzero idempotent, because the scalar \(\lambda\) in \(U^2=\lambda U\) tends to a nonzero scalar. Both determinantal bounds \(\operatorname{rank}p\le s\) and \(\operatorname{rank}(I-p)\le n-s\) persist in the limit, hence the limiting idempotent has rank exactly \(s\). Thus \(\mathcal E_s\cap\mathcal C_{s'}=\varnothing\) when \(s\ne s'\), and the \(n\) distinct irreducible closed sets are precisely the components. The dimensions are those of Step 2.

**5. Exact finite-field counts.** There are \({n\brack s}_q q^{s(n-s)}\) idempotents of rank \(s\) (choose an image and a complementary kernel). Each supports exactly \(q^{s^2}-1\) nonzero \(F\)'s. For square-zero \(U\) of rank \(r\), choose \(\operatorname{im}U\), then \(\ker U\), then the induced isomorphism from \(k^n/\ker U\) to \(\operatorname{im}U\); this gives \({n\brack r}_q{n-r\brack r}_q|\mathrm{GL}_r(q)|\) possible \(U\)'s. The solutions of \(FU=UF=0\) comprise a vector space of dimension \((n-r)^2\). Divide by \(q-1\) for the scaling ambiguity of nonzero pure tensors. This proves both displayed rank-stratum formulas. Furthermore \(P^2=\operatorname{tr}(FU)P\), so **every** square-zero-image operator has \(P^2=0\). In an idempotent-image stratum, the values of \(\operatorname{tr}F\in\mathbb F_q\) are equidistributed: for each nonzero scalar \(\beta\), exactly \({n\brack s}_q q^{s(n-s)+s^2-1}\) operators have \(P^2=\beta P\); exactly \({n\brack s}_q q^{s(n-s)}(q^{s^2-1}-1)\) have \(P^2=0\).

**6. Fixed-field asymptotic.** Put \(Q=q^{-1}\) and \(d_j=\prod_{i=1}^j(1-Q^i)\). For fixed \(j\), \(q^{-n^2}E_{n,n-j}(q)\to Q^{j^2}/d_j\). For fixed \(r\), \(q^{-n^2}Z_{n,r}(q)\to Q^{r^2}/((q-1)d_r)\). The corresponding normalized summands are bounded by their respective limits (using the Gaussian-product formula), and both majorant series converge supergeometrically, so dominated convergence yields \(\mathcal R(Q)+(\mathcal R(Q)-1)/(q-1)\). The last product is the classical first Rogers–Ramanujan identity.

## Verification

The standalone `averaging_rank_one_checker.py` directly enumerates all nonzero rank-one maps on \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\), for \(q=2,3,4\), evaluates the **original two-sided averaging identities** on all ordered basis pairs, and compares actual square-zero-image rank, idempotent-image rank and endomorphism-square distributions against the formulas. In dimension three over \(\mathbb F_2\) it exhausts all rank-one tensor presentations and tests their equivalent matrix conditions. Its independent finite-field implementation includes genuine \(\mathbb F_4\) arithmetic. The included `averaging_rank_one_checks.txt` is the actual run output. For \(n=2\) the totals are \(24,112,330\) for \(q=2,3,4\); for \(n=3,q=2\), the total is \(1274\). Numerical comparisons for \(q=2,3,4\) at \(n=24\) confirm convergence towards the stated infinite products. Direct tests are finite corroboration; the proof above establishes the general claim.

## Relationship to prior work

The two-sided averaging identity and foundational theory appear in Guo–Pei (2014), whose primary text was inspected, and Cao's earlier thesis, whose archived text was also inspected. The prior rank-one **idempotent averaging** classification already counts the condition \(P^2=P\), and a prior rank-one **Rota–Baxter** classification contains the same square-zero-image summand. The classical Rogers–Ramanujan identity and an earlier idempotent-only limiting corank law are also established. None of these ingredients alone describes the complete reduced full averaging variety or its degeneration of every nilpotent-image stratum onto a prescribed idempotent-image component; that geometric gluing is the central additional conclusion. The full-variety count is presented as a synthesis of independently characterized disjoint branches, not as independent discovery of already-known subcounts.

## Limitations

This result concerns operator rank at most one on full matrix algebras. It does not classify higher-rank averaging maps, arbitrary finite-dimensional associative algebras, the nonreduced defining scheme or entire conjugacy-orbit geometry. The component assertion is for the reduced variety. Historical priority remains provisional pending broader scholarly comparison, and no independent mathematical audit has been performed.
