The reduced variety of rank-at-most-one two-sided averaging maps on \(M_n(k)\) has exactly \(n\) geometrically irreducible components, of dimensions \(2ns-s^2\) for \(1\le s\le n\). Every square-zero-image map of image-matrix rank \(r\) is an explicit boundary degeneration of the component indexed by \(n-r\); all finite-field rank strata admit closed counts, yielding a Rogers–Ramanujan limiting density.
