The centre of the nonempty power monoid of partial bijections on an \(n\)-point set consists exactly of nonempty unions of fixed-rank partial-identity layers, giving \(2^{n+1}-1\) elements and \(2n+1\) idempotents. It has an explicit intersection-rank multiplication law and satisfies the sharp aperiodicity identity \(A^n=A^{n+1}\) for every central element \(A\).
