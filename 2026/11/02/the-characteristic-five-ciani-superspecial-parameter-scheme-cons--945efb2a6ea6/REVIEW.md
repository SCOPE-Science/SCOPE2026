# Assessment of the characteristic-five Ciani parameter-scheme theorem

## Correctness — PASS

The coefficient extraction from the fourth power of the plane quartic gives three diagonal Hasse polynomials and zero off-diagonals. Their differences force equal parameter squares on the smooth locus. The resulting quadratic elimination has exactly two nonsingular square parameters and four compatible sign patterns for each, with a nonzero Hasse Jacobian determinant at all eight points. The Frobenius transpositions give four degree-two closed points. A standalone finite-field verifier independently checks coefficient extraction, all \(25^3\) finite-field parameter triples and the Jacobian values; the algebraic proof, not a finite test, establishes geometric completeness.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Lercier--Ritzenthaler--Rovetta--Sijsling (2014), Sections 3 and 5, provide the Ciani family and sign symmetries, but their principal representative-family theorem assumes characteristic at least seven. Ohashi (2022), Sections 2--4, provides the superspecial quadratic-field descent criterion and the elliptic quotients, but does not evaluate the characteristic-five eight-point Hasse scheme. The closest characteristic-three and characteristic-seven classifications do not imply the characteristic-five result. Focused mathematical-record and primary-literature searches did not locate this exact classification; this absence is not a priority proof. The complete Brock thesis (1993) was not inspected; historical priority remains qualified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This computes the full arithmetic and local scheme structure of the superspecial locus of a natural normalized genus-three family at the first odd prime beyond three, rather than an arbitrary sample. Its eight reduced geometric points form four nonsplit quadratic closed points, distinguishing existence over an algebraic closure from marked-model existence over the prime field. The exact cutoff is mathematically motivated by the full three-parameter Hasse degeneracy, not by an arbitrary parameter restriction.

## Limitations

The scheme is the Hasse--Witt zero locus on the *marked normalized coefficient space*. It is not the coarse moduli space of superspecial quartics and does not count distinct geometric isomorphism classes. An uninspected historical thesis and implicit computability from general methods remain residual originality risks. Independent scientific verification has not occurred.
