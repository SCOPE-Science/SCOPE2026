In characteristic five the Hasse--Witt zero scheme of the smooth normalized three-parameter Ciani quartic family is exactly four quadratic closed points over \(\mathbb F_5\): its eight geometric parameter triples come from \(r^2-2r-2=0\) and even sign changes. All eight models are defined over \(\mathbb F_{25}\), none has normalized coefficients over \(\mathbb F_5\), and the scheme is reduced rather than a fat zero locus.
