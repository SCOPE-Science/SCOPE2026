The characteristic-five Clebsch cubic is the anticanonical contraction of the blowup of all six rational points of a smooth conic over \(\mathbb F_5\); its **full geometric automorphism group** is \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_5)\cong\mathfrak S_5\), and every automorphism is rational over \(\mathbb F_5\). Its exact point count over \(\mathbb F_{5^n}\) is \(5^{2n}+6\cdot5^n+1\) for every \(n\ge1\).
