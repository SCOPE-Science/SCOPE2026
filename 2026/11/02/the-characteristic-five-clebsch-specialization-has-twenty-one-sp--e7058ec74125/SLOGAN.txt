The characteristic-five symmetric Clebsch specialization has one ordinary node, whose resolution blows up six rational points on a smooth conic. All twenty-one geometric lines descend to \(\mathbb F_5\), and the six nodal lines realize the six-point \(S_5\cong\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_5)\) action; its zeta function is \(1/((1-T)(1-5T)^6(1-25T))\). The singular point and the abstract group action are established background; the exact arithmetic resolution and line descent are the refinement.
