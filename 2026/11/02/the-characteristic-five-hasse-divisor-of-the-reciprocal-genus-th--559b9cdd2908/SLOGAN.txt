For the smooth marked characteristic-five reciprocal genus-three Howe family \(y^2=x^8+ux^6+vx^4+ux^2+1\), the nonordinary Hasse divisor is a reduced normal-crossings union of three explicit irreducible curves. Its only double intersections are \((u,v)=(0,0),(2,0),(-2,0)\), precisely the rank-one members; no smooth member has \(5\)-rank zero.
