The characteristic-five Wiman plane sextic has six rational ordinary cusps and four rational nodes with nonsplit quadratic branches; its normalization is \(\mathbb P^1\), but its generalized Jacobian is \(\mathbb G_a^6\times(\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{25}/\mathbb F_5}^{1}\mathbb G_m)^4\). Its singular-curve point count is \(5^m+1+4(-1)^{m+1}\) over \(\mathbb F_{5^m}\), so all ten \(\mathbb F_5\)-points are singular.
