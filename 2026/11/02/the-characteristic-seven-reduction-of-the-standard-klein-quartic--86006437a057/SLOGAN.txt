For the standard integral plane equation of the Klein quartic, reduction modulo seven has one geometrically unibranch cusp with characteristic pair \((2,7)\) and delta invariant \(3\), so its normalization is \(\mathbb P^1\). This singular plane fiber still has exactly \(7^m+1\) points over every \(\mathbb F_{7^m}\), distinct from the curve's potentially good stable reduction after base change.
