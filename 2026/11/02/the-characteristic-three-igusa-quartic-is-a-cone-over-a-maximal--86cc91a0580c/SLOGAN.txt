The coefficientwise characteristic-three Igusa quartic is a cone over a fifteen-nodal quartic K3 model whose minimal resolution is maximal over \(\mathbb F_9\). Its second-cohomology Frobenius spectrum is \((+3)^{21}(-3)^1\), determining the zeta functions of the K3 resolution, the nodal base, and the original cone over every finite extension.
