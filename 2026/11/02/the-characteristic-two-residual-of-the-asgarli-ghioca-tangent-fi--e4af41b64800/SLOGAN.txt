For every \(q=2^r\) with \(r\ge3\), removing the three pairwise-equality lines from the characteristic-two specialization of the Asgarli–Ghioca equation leaves a geometrically integral degree-\(q-4\) curve with exactly \((q/2-2)(q/2-4)\) ordinary cusps and \((q-2)(q-4)\) smooth \(\mathbb F_q\)-points. Its normalization has genus \(q^2/4-5q/2+7\), and the three removed lines each touch the residual at exactly \(q/2-2\) smooth geometric points.
