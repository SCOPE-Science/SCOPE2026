The coefficientwise characteristic-two Schur quartic is a normal rational surface with a single multiplicity-three point of Tjurina length \(16\), through which pass exactly twelve geometric lines. Its singular-surface point count is \(\#S(\mathbf F_{2^m})=4^m+1+6\cdot2^m+4\cdot(-2)^m\) for every positive integer \(m\).
