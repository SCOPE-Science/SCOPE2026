The Hesse twelve-line arrangement has nine fourfold points, twelve nodes, and a length-93 intrinsic Tjurina scheme in every characteristic other than three. In characteristic two its nine fourfold Tjurina algebras keep the same Hilbert function \((1,2,3,2,1)\) as in characteristic zero but acquire two-dimensional socles, making the singular scheme non-Gorenstein and non-locally-complete-intersection exactly there.
