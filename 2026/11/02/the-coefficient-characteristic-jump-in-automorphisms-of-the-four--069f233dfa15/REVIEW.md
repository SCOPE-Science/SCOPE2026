# Scientific review of the four-element dihedral quandle algebra classification

**Correctness — PASS.** The multiplication table yields the two-dimensional intrinsic right annihilator over every field. In odd characteristic the two independent quotient characters force a block-diagonal automorphism and exactly a torus extended by its factor-swap; in characteristic two the bilinear cross-term forces matching permutation blocks while leaving four independent shear entries. Both directions are proved and the displayed normal forms have no duplicates. An independent enumerator checked every right-annihilator-preserving invertible map over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_3\), recovering 32 and 8 automorphisms respectively. The symbolic proof, not the finite test, covers arbitrary fields.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Bardakov–Passi–Singh (arXiv:2001.06843, Theorem 6.4) already proved the integral \(\mathbb Z[R_4]\) automorphism group, which is explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Sangare–Ta (arXiv:2608.01550, Section 4) establishes broader structures and partial even-dihedral embeddings but not this complete field-dependent classification; the characteristic-two branch lies outside its characteristic-not-two embedding. Targeted published-record and literature comparisons found no stronger covering result. Residual risk: an older computation over fields not indexed under dihedral quandle-algebra terminology.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Determining automorphism groups of even dihedral quandle algebras is a named active classification problem. The smallest nontrivial even case reveals two genuinely different families—multiplicative torus versus additive four-parameter shear group—with a sharp characteristic-two symmetry jump. The exact finite-field orders are structural consequences, not isolated statistics. The result is scoped to \(R_4\), not a solution of the general even-dihedral problem.
