The four-element dihedral quandle algebra has a complete but sharply characteristic-dependent automorphism group: \((K^\times)^2\rtimes C_2\) away from characteristic two, versus \((K^+)^4\rtimes C_2\) in characteristic two. Thus its automorphism order over \(\mathbb F_q\) is exactly \(2(q-1)^2\) for odd \(q\) and \(2q^4\) for even \(q\).
