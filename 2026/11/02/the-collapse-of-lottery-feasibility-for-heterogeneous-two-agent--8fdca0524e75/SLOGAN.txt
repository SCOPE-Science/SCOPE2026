For two agents dividing binary-cost chores, the presence of even one chore that is costly to exactly one agent makes an ex-ante weighted-envy-free lottery over strong WEFX and fractionally Pareto-optimal allocations possible **only when a single deterministic allocation is already exactly weighted envy-free**. The sharp feasibility interval is \([pc-(1-p)b,\ p(c+a)]\cap\{0,\ldots,c\}\), yielding a complete counting formula and universal feasibility when both one-sided chore types occur.
