The complete anti-dictionary of each finite Zimin word consists precisely of \(a_{m+1}Z_ma_{m+1}\) and \(a_iZ_ma_j\) with \(m+2\le i,j\le n\), for \(0\le m<n\). Minimal absent factors occur only at lengths \(2^m+1\), with exactly \(1+(n-m-1)^2\) at that length, giving \(n+n(n-1)(2n-1)/6\) altogether.
