For the smooth complex plane curve \(y^d=2T_d(x/2)\), every degree \(d\ge4\) has precisely \(d\) real hyperflexes and \(2d(d-2)\) ordinary flexes, all of whose \(x\)-coordinates are nonreal. The complete ordinary-flex locus is cut out by a squarefree Chebyshev Hessian factor of degree \(2d-4\), with \(d\) points above each root.
