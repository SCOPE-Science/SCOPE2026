# Review of the line-addition defect stratification

**Correctness (PASS).** Explicit quadratic syzygy, classified degree-one stabilizers, complete local singularity analysis and exact all-integer standard-basis colength give the complete Tjurina table. The global freeness-defect formula then determines the result. `verify_local_tjurina.py` and `verify_syzygy.py` passed exact rational tests; these finite checks are corroboration rather than the proof.

**Originality (PASS, qualified).** Malara–Pokora–Tutaj-Gasińska, arXiv:2007.04162 §3, first studied the precise Kummer pencil and explicitly mention its three coordinate-line additions, which are not novel. The complete stratification for every added line, the dual tangency divisor, and the generic defect-two region are separate from those observations. Dimca's arXiv:2310.08972 addition-deletion results focus on free starting curves or free unions, rather than the generic nonfree addition here. Related Măcinic–Pokora arXiv:2310.19610 was inspectable only at the abstract/metadata level: possible unpublished or more general overlap is an explicit residual risk. Relevant semantic-index results concern different arrangements; no equivalent full classification was identified by the performed comparisons.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The question is motivated by the published conic-pencil example and the addition–deletion classification program. The complete dual-plane locus is geometrically natural and its nontrivial branch through a base point requires an exact nonquasihomogeneous Tjurina computation. The theorem applies uniformly in infinitely many degrees, rather than only an isolated parameter.

**Limitations.** Only characteristic zero and coefficient-marked embeddings are considered; no full graded Betti table or independent external validation is claimed. A potentially overlapping 2024 full text remains inaccessible.

**References.** Malara–Pokora–Tutaj-Gasińska (2020/2021), arXiv:2007.04162; Dimca (2023), arXiv:2310.08972; Măcinic–Pokora (2023/2024), arXiv:2310.19610.
