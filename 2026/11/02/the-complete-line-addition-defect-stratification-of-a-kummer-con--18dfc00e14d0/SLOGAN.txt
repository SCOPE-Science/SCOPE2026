Adding any line to the nearly free Kummer pencil \((xy)^k+z^{2k}=0\) has defect \(0\), \(1\), or \(2\): the defect-one locus is a natural divisor in the dual plane minus exactly two defect-zero points, and its complement has defect two. This gives a complete, characteristic-zero line-addition phase diagram for every \(k\ge2\).
