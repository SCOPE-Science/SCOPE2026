For a repeated cyclic block \(P=a_0^{r_0}\cdots a_{q-1}^{r_{q-1}}\) on \(q\ge3\) distinct letters, every contiguous minimal absent factor of \(P^k\), \(k\ge2\), is either a forbidden directed bigram, a run overrun, an incomplete internal run, or the single endpoint obstruction \(a_{q-1}P^{k-1}a_0\). The complete antidictionary has exactly \(q(q-2)+|P|+1\) words regardless of \(k\); increasing the number of copies changes exactly one word.
