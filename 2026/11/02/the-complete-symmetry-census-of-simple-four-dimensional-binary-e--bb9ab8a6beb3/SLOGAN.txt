Among simple four-dimensional evolution algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\), exactly \(516\) isomorphism classes exist, of which \(461\) have no nontrivial automorphism. The complete automorphism-group spectrum has eight types—trivial, \(C_2\), \(C_3\), \(C_4\), \(C_2\times C_2\), \(S_3\), \(D_8\) and \(S_4\)—with respective class multiplicities \(461,45,3,2,1,2,1,1\); \(A_4\) does not occur.
