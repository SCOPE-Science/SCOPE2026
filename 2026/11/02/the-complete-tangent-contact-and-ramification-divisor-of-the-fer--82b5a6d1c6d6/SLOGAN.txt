For the smooth Fermat curve of degree \(d=q^2+q+1\) in characteristic \(p\), every tangent has contact order \(q\), \(q+1\), or \(d\), and the non-generic contact points are exactly the \(\mathbb F_{q^3}\)-rational points. The full hyperplane-series ramification divisor has \((q-2)d^2\) simple interior points and \(3d\) boundary points of multiplicity \(d-q\).
