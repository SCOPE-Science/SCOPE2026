For circular matchings on \(2n\) seats with prescribed distances only \(1\), \(2\), and \(3\), an exact construction realizes every multiplicity profile with an even number of distance-\(2\) pairs, except one distance-\(1\) pair and \(n-1\) distance-\(3\) pairs when \(3\mid n\). In particular, the prime-order seating-couples parity conjecture holds for every list supported on \(\{1,2,3\}\) and every prime \(n\ge5\).
