The two-token Petersen graph has a nontrivial critical group \((\mathbb Z/2)^8\oplus(\mathbb Z/4)^2\oplus\mathbb Z/28\oplus\mathbb Z/1596\oplus\mathbb Z/38304\oplus(\mathbb Z/191520)^3\), with exactly \(2^{36}3^9 5^3 7^6 19^5\) spanning trees. This discrete two-particle configuration graph acquires \(3\)-, \(7\)-, and \(19\)-primary torsion absent from the critical group of the Petersen graph itself.
