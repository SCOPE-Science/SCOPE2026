On the two-hub network \(K_{2,q}\), allocating \(k\ge4\) perfect leaders exhibits a sharp cubic threshold: below it every optimum grounds only peripheral vertices, and above it every optimum grounds exactly one hub. The critical network size is \((1+\sqrt2)k-3/2+O(k^{-1})\); on \(K_{2,8}\), both hubs are optimal with two leaders but neither hub is used with four.
