On the three-dimensional binary cube, the exact centered graph-ball maximal \(\ell^2\) norm is \(\sqrt{13+3\sqrt{17}}/4\); the only nonnegative norming profiles alternate between \(1\) and \(\sqrt{17}-4\) on its two parity classes. The cube's additional radius-two and radius-three balls do not improve the sharp norm over the bipartite zero/one-radius choice.
