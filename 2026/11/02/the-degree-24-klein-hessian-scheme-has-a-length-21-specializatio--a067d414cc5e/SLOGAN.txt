For the primitive integral Hessian intersection of Klein's quartic, the degree-\(24\) flex scheme stays finite flat over \(\mathbb Z_{(7)}\), but its characteristic-seven fiber consists of three simple coordinate points and a single length-\(21\) point at the quartic's unique singularity \([1:2:4]\). This is a specialization of the characteristic-zero Hessian scheme, not an intrinsic flex classification of a singular curve.
