For every \(n\geq5\), the contraction-zero variety of Baily and Danelon's four-way border-partition-rank tensor has a unique top-dimensional component of codimension \(3\), degree \(8\), and generic multiplicity one; equivalently its geometric rank is \(3\). For sufficiently large \(n\), this gives \(\operatorname{GR}(T_n)=3<\operatorname{PR}(T_n)=4\) while the border partition rank is at most \(3\).
