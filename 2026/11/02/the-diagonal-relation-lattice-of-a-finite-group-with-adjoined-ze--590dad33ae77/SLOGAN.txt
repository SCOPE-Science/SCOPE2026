For every finite group \(G\) with a new absorbing zero, diagonal subsemigroups form the lattice \(\mathcal N(G)\times\mathsf B_2\), yielding the exact DSC coefficient \(\chi(G^0)=(k+1)/(4k)\) with \(k\) normal subgroups. The coefficient equals \(3/8\) precisely for nontrivial finite simple groups, and its possible values approach \(1/4\) without reaching it.
