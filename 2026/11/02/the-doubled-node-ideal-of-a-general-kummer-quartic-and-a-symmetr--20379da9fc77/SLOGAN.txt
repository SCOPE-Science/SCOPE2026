For a general sixteen-node Kummer quartic in characteristic zero, the doubled-node ideal is \(I^{(2)}=I^2+(F)=(F)+(\nabla F)^2\), with one quartic and ten sextic minimal generators. For an explicitly certified symmetric tetrahedroid, its cyclic symbolic defect has dimensions \((1,4,10,4)\) in degrees four through seven and total length \(19\).
