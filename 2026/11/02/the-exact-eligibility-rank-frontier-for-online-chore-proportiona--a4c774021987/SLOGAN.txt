When each arriving chore can be assigned to at most \(r\) eligible agents, the exact uniform deterministic online proportionality-up-to-one-item factor is \(r\), for every integer \(r\ge1\). The lower bound already holds for unit chores on an adaptive Berge-tree of eligibility sets (an ordinary tree when \(r=2\)); the upper bound holds for every feasible assignment and every prefix.
