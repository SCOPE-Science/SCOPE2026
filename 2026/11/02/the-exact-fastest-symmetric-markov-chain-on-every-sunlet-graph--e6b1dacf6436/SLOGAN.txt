For the \(m\)-sunlet (one leaf at every vertex of a cycle), the globally fastest symmetric stochastic walk has exact second-largest eigenvalue modulus \(2(2-\delta+\delta^{3/2})/(4+\delta^2)\), where \(\delta=1-\cos(2\pi/m)\); its unique dihedrally invariant optimizer gives each rim edge weight \([4-(2-\delta)\sqrt\delta]/[2(4+\delta^2)]\) and each spoke the remaining rim probability. Its optimal spoke probability is asymptotic to \(\pi/(\sqrt2 m)\), and its spectral gap equals \(\pi^2/m^2-\sqrt2\pi^3/m^3+O(m^{-4})\).
