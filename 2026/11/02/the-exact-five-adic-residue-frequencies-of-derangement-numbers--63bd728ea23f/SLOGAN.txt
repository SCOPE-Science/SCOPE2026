For every \(k\ge1\), each residue of the derangement numbers modulo \(5^k\) occurs exactly \(2,3,1,1,3\) times per period of length \(2\cdot5^k\), according to its residue modulo five. Consequently the derangement numbers attain **every** five-adic residue class infinitely often; five is the smallest prime with full residue coverage at all prime-power levels.
