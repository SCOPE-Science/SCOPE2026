The full Krawtchouk Fourier interpolants of the sign function converge on every fixed complex compact set to a universal cardinal-sine profile with an exactly characterized, unique real overshoot. Its sharp peak is \(\Gamma\approx1.065783888266448\), attained near \(1.3769917692\); this establishes the first-lobe limiting constant, without claiming control of finite-polynomial maxima on expanding intervals.
