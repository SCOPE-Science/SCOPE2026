For \(q\equiv3\pmod8\) outside characteristic three, the trinomial \(X^{2q-1}+tX^q-X\) with \(t^2=-2\) has only zero-, one-, and three-element fibers over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\), and their exact frequencies depend solely on \(\chi_q(3)\). In particular, it has \((q^2+2S)/3\) values, where \(S=1+(q-1)(1-\chi_q(3))\).
