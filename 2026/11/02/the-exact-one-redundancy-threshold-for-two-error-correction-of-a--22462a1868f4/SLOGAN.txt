For arbitrary nonconstant binary-valued functions, **exactly 36 input bits** is the universal threshold at which a nonsystematic injective encoder can protect the function value against two bit errors using only one redundant bit: \(\kappa_1(2)=36\). A sharp 35-bit obstruction and an exact lexicographic-shadow recurrence close the gap left by the previous bound \(\kappa_1(2)\le66\).
