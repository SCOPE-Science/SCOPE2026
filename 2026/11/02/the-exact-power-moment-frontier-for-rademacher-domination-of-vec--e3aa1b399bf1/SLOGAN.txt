Rademacher sums dominate every bounded-increment Euclidean martingale in power moment exactly for \(p=2\) or \(p\ge3\). Every \(2<p<3\) admits a strictly violating three-step planar martingale, yet two steps are always safe at \(p\ge2\); below \(p=2\), two orthogonal steps suffice to break domination.
