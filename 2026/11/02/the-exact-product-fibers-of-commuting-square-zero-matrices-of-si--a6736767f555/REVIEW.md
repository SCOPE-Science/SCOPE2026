# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof explicitly handles rank-zero, rank-one and rank-two square-zero factors, computes their square-zero commuting centralizers and derives every product fiber. Centralizer equations use no characteristic-dependent divisions. An independent finite computation checked all multiplication fibers in size four over \(\mathbb F_2\), and the two fixed-rank normal forms over \(\mathbb F_3\). This is corroboration of the general argument, not substitution for proof.

## Originality
PASS at best available evidence, with residual priority uncertainty. Kokol Bukovšek, Košir, Novak and Oblak (2007) already established the possible products and the size-four existence threshold. Only the exact finite-field number of commuting square-zero factorizations of each prescribed target is assessed as new. Britnell–Wildon (2010) concerns nilpotent similarity classes that commute, rather than finite-field multiplicities of prescribed products. Closest recorded arithmetic factorization results involve different factors or dimension. An equivalent identity outside inspected literature cannot be ruled out.

## Value
PASS. This is the full arithmetic fiber census of a natural multiplication morphism at its first nonzero instance; it gives q-uniform, positive integer formulas for every target in all characteristics. The zero and nonzero strata exhibit distinct multiplicity behavior not fixed by known image classification.

## Limitations
No claims for higher sizes, orbit classification, schemes, or non-square-zero factors; no independent historical priority or mathematical certification. The first-public-date metadata refers to the precisely dated supporting preprint of Britnell–Wildon; the older 2007 background paper has only its publication month confirmed in the inspected copy.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
