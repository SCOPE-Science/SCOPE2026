The GCD of every length-\(k\) window of squared cobalancing numbers is exactly \(4\gcd(5k,B_k,C_k-1)\) for odd \(k\) and \(2\gcd(5k,B_k,C_k-1)\) for even \(k\). This completes the cobalancing-square GCD question using a reversible five-mode recurrence and exact local arguments at the exceptional primes.
