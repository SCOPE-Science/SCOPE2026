In characteristic \(7\), the smooth genus-three pencil \(y^2=x^8+t x^4+1\) becomes bicanonically nonclassical at exactly the reduced parameter \(t=0\), where its Hasse order sequence jumps from \((0,1,2,3,4,5)\) to \((0,1,2,3,4,7)\). The exceptional curve has an explicitly determined degree-\(116\) higher-Hasse-Wronskian divisor: eight branch points of weight four and eighty-four simple points.
