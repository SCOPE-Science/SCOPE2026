Under cyclic-shift-minimized Hamming distance, the optimal single centre of the weight-three binary necklace space has radius \(2\) exactly at lengths \(5\)–\(12\) and \(14\), but radius \(4\) at \(13\) and every length at least \(15\); lengths \(3,4\) have radius \(0\). At length \(14\), precisely two optimal centres survive, represented by the one-position sets \(\{0,2,6\}\) and \(\{0,2,10\}\), with the sharp boundary explained by Mantel's theorem and the prime-thirteen obstruction by Cauchy–Davenport.
