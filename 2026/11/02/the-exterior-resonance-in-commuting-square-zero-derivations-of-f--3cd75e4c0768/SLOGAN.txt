Commuting square-zero ordinary derivations of the four-dimensional quantum complete intersection are mutually annihilating in every odd characteristic. The exterior parameter \(\lambda=-1\) is a sharp resonance: for tuples of length \(m\ge2\), the reduced locus changes from affine \(2m\)-space to two components of dimensions \(2m\) and \(2m+1\), with exact joint-rank polynomial \((Q+2)\prod_i(1+(Q^2-1)T_i)-(Q+1)\prod_i(1+(Q-1)T_i)\) over \(\mathbb F_Q\).
