The one-flag exchange graph of maximum induced matchings of the Heawood graph is a connected six-regular, three-part, \(84\)-vertex graph in which every edge belongs to exactly one triangle. Its radius and diameter are \(4\) and \(5\), and any two optimum flag systems can be connected with at most two exchanges beyond their symmetric-difference lower bound; these are new metric assertions, not a new claim for the known matching number or the previously enumerated \(84\) triples.
