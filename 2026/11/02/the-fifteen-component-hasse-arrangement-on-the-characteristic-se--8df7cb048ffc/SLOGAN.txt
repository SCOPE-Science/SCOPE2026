For the genus-five Humbert--Edge family in characteristic seven, the only Jacobian \((7\text{-rank},a)\) pairs are \((5,0),(4,1),(2,3)\). The twenty maximally nonordinary marked curves are precisely \(M_{0,5}(\mathbb F_7)\), where fifteen elliptic-Hasse components form a reduced \((15_4,20_3)\) triple-point configuration.
