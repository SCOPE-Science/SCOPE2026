# Review of the fifth-order velocity-reset drag fingerprint

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns a stationary, symmetric, velocity-only-reset telegraph process with fixed positive forcing and switching rate. The precise generator identities, moment bounds and exact first-reset decomposition are sufficient for the asymptotic \(-6\kappa a^4/(\lambda r^5)\) coefficient. The no-reset remainder is \(O(r^{-5})\) before division by \(r+\gamma\); the cubic Taylor expansion is controlled by exponential reset-age moments. The accompanying exact-arithmetic polynomial-generator test is a supplementary separate algebraic calculation, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified best knowledge)
The 2026 source establishes only the leading rapid-reset \(r^{-2}\) law; the specified fifth-order drag fingerprint was not found in its full-text analysis. The zero-reset 2025 nonlinear-drag quadratures and the fully renewing 2024 velocity-reset framework do not directly contain the statement because the propulsive state is retained across resets. Relevant claim-level searches of published mathematical research records found no equivalent. An unlocated general nonrenewal perturbation theorem or an unindexed source could still affect publication priority.

## Value — PASS
The result gives the first order at which a measurable diffusivity can discriminate nonlinear shear-thickening and shear-thinning drag despite the exact equality of their earlier asymptotic coefficients. The order is structurally forced and the signed coefficient has a clear microscopic origin: two equally contributing terms from stationary force–velocity response and reset-age covariance.

## Closest literature and limitations
The model and leading scaling belong to Howlader–Mondal–Das (arXiv:2606.00560). Earlier nonlinear-drag no-reset theory appears in arXiv:2507.12313 and arXiv:2504.11683; the partially renewing velocity-reset theory appears in arXiv:2401.12685. The result fixes all other parameters as the reset rate grows and assumes smooth odd dissipative drag with bounded accessible speed. It does not cover vanishing flipping rates, asymmetric forcing or arbitrary renewal clocks.
