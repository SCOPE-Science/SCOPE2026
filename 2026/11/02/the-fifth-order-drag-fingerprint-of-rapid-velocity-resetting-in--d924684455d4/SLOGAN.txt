For an inertial run-and-tumble particle whose velocity is frequently reset but whose propulsion orientation is not, the first non-Newtonian correction to diffusivity is exactly \(-6\kappa a^4/(\lambda r^5)+O(r^{-6})\), where \(\kappa\) is the cubic coefficient of the drag law. Thus shear-thickening and shear-thinning media can share the entire diffusion expansion through \(r^{-4}\) yet differ by a signed fifth-order term.
