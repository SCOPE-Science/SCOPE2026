Inclusion-minimal dominating sets of a path first separate static and adaptive vertex shattering on eleven vertices: \(\operatorname{VCdim}=4\) but \(\operatorname{Ldim}=5\). Joining copies of this path at the neighbors of pendant leaves preserves the Cartesian-product concept structure, producing connected bounded-degree trees with exact dimensions \(4s\) and \(5s\) for every \(s\ge1\).
