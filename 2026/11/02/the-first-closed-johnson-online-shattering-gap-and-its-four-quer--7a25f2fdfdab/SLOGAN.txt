On the twenty-vertex Johnson graph \(J(6,3)\), closed neighborhoods have VC dimension \(3\) but Littlestone dimension \(4\), whereas open neighborhoods have both dimensions \(4\). Every four-query closed-neighborhood support realizes exactly \(4\), \(8\), or \(14\) traces, with respective frequencies \(45\), \(1680\), and \(3120\); this is the smallest Johnson graph with such a closed-neighborhood adaptive gap.
