For binary words correcting exactly two adjacent deletions, the maximum code sizes at lengths \(2\) through \(8\) are \(1,2,4,4,8,13,20\). Length \(8\) is the first strict gap in the standard fractional deletion-shadow packing bound: its optimum is \(21\), while an exact rational conflict-clique certificate proves the integral optimum is \(20\).
