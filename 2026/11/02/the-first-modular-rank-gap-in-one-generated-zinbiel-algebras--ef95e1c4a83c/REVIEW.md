# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The statement is about a precisely defined positive-characteristic quotient of the free one-generated Zinbiel algebra, with positive degree basis and integral binomial multiplication. The Zinbiel relation follows from the integer binomial shuffle identity, so there is no hidden division by a characteristic-zero integer. For \(n\le p\), the leading entries of every positive power of either multiplication operator have nonzero binomial coefficients, proving the full power-rank sequence of \(J_n^d\). At \(n=p+1\), \(e_1\circ e_p=e_{p+1}\) is the unique nonzero top-degree basis product. That yields the exact left/right power-rank cases, all Jordan types, and annihilator asymmetry. A disjoint first-nonzero-coordinate count yields the joint finite-field polynomial, whose coefficients sum to \(q^n\). The full case \(p=2\) is included. An independent standalone routine evaluates the raw multiplication matrices, their power ranks, and finite-field distributions in 11 exhaustive plus 3 reproducibly sampled configurations. Finite testing is supporting evidence only.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)

The closest primary source is Adashev–Omirov–Khudoyberdiyev, *Classifications of some classes of Zinbiel algebras* (arXiv:math/0702809v1), Theorem 2.1. It **already publishes the exact complex binomial multiplication table**; its classification and the table are excluded from the claim of originality. Its full original text (null-filiform theorem, proofs, filiform results, dimension-four classification, references) was inspected, and it does not give positive-characteristic two-handed Jordan multiplicities or rank gaps. Adashev et al. (arXiv:1303.5881v1) and Adashev–Ladra–Omirov (arXiv:1410.7549v1) classify distinct complex graded families using selected left-multiplication characteristic sequences; neither provides the all-element positive-characteristic joint census. Sage's shuffle model implies the structural coefficients, not the claimed modular classification.

Mathematical literature searches tested 'null-filiform', 'one-generated Zinbiel', both multiplication hands, rank gaps, nilpotent Jordan types, binomial coefficients, extension fields, and equivalently phrased ring/operator questions. A relevant mathematical finding on Jordan lifts over dual numbers concerns a different coefficient ring and does not address Zinbiel multiplication. Other mathematical results on Jordan-type distributions concern Leibniz derivations and Jacobi–Jordan multiplication, not this Zinbiel product and its two-handed invariant. None of these stronger or analogous statements implies the new exact polynomial. Turemuratova (2026), Section 4.4, explicitly observes a three-dimensional characteristic-two example with unequal handed annihilators, covering that narrow observation already; the novelty claim does not include that p=2 example in isolation. The full six-page article, however, neither states all-prime first-cutoff Jordan types nor finite-field joint rank polynomials. Alvarez–Fehlberg Júnior–Kaygorodov (2022) provides a complex five-dimensional classification with a distinct scope, which was compared at theorem level. An unnoticed earlier equivalent result, or an unexamined consequence of a broader theorem on divided-power algebra operators, remains possible. This PASS is self-review best-knowledge comparison, not an independent priority certificate.

## Value — PASS

The first modular cutoff \(n=p+1\) is forced by the first potentially vanishing internal binomial coefficient \(\binom p j\), rather than an arbitrary computational slice. The result gives a **complete** classification of the Jordan forms of both multiplication hands for every element at and before this boundary, together with an exact joint distribution over all finite extensions, the one-sided annihilator jump, a genuinely missing left rank, and the geometry of every rank stratum. This is a natural refined structural invariant of a canonical Zinbiel family and exposes behavior invisible to the classical complex classification or the usual generic characteristic sequence. The proof and data identify the mechanism rather than merely tabulating small cases. No general classification beyond the first modular dimension is claimed.

## Scope and unresolved risks

The result is restricted to the explicit truncated one-generator Zinbiel algebra, not all positive-characteristic Zinbiel algebras; the geometry is reduced set-theoretic geometry, not scheme structure. The original binomial multiplication table and the isolated dimension-three characteristic-two handed-annihilator example are published. Priority may be affected by an equivalent previously published modular normal-form result under different terminology; independent mathematical and historical audits have not been performed.
