For the divisibility poset of triangular numbers, a multiplicative-order criterion identifies \(T(43)=946\) as the first nontrivial triangular number coprime to three dividing some \(T(2^k)\) with odd \(k\), and this happens precisely at \(k\equiv35\pmod{70}\). This yields the first nonzero value after the initial exception on the odd-dyadic Möbius subsequence: \(\mu_T(2^k)=0\) for odd \(3\le k\le33\), but \(\mu_T(2^{35})=1\).
