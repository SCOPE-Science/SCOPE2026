The eight-vertex spider with arm lengths \(1,2,4\) is the unique smallest forest whose maximal-independent-set membership concepts have unequal VC and Littlestone dimensions: they are \(2\) and \(3\). Disjoint unions of \(s\) copies have dimensions \(2s\) and \(3s\), giving an arbitrarily large adaptive-versus-static shattering gap within forests.
