In the Gaussian pluriharmonic Fock space, evaluation kernels first become orthogonal at the sharp ball radius \(\sqrt{\alpha\log2}\), at antipodal points. On growing one-dimensional equal-radius circles the least orthogonality angle decreases smoothly, starts with a square-root departure from antipodality, and produces orthogonal kernels at chord length asymptotic to \(\pi\sqrt\alpha/(2\sqrt t)\).
