Three Hermitian measurements in complex dimension three are the first setting where exact information about a target observable on pure states can fail maximally for mixed states, despite identical measurement data. For the explicit qutrit Pauli-block example, the sharp pure-state uncertainty is \(2\min\{1,\|\boldsymbol\varepsilon\|_2\}\), whereas the mixed-state uncertainty is always \(2\); no such gap is possible with two or fewer observables or in dimension at most two.
