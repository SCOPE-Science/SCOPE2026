In the dihedral genus-three pencil \(y^2=x(x^6+a x^3+1)\), characteristic \(13\) is the first tame odd prime admitting a smooth Jacobian of geometric \(p\)-rank one: exactly the two marked parameters \(a=\pm6\) have this rank and \(a\)-number two. Six other geometric parameters have rank two, while all remaining smooth members are ordinary, with Newton polygons determined by two elliptic Hasse polynomials.
