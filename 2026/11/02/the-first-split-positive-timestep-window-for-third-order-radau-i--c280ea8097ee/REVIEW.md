The denominator is strictly positive, and all relevant numerator signs are resolved by positive coefficient tests, Sturm root counts, Descartes' rule and disjoint rational isolating intervals. A publicly indexed eight-state positivity barrier concerns a fourth-order approximation on an absorbing chain, not this third-order Radau map. The matching objects and implications were compared, rather than titles alone. Specialist prior analysis of fixed cyclic Radau steps could still exist and independent validation is outstanding.

The criterion is exact and directly testable, although positivity does not imply accuracy.
