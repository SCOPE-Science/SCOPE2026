For the one-pointed linear Hodge integrals \(H_{g,i}=\int_{\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,1}}\psi_1^{2g-2+i}\lambda_{g-i}\), the smallest number of denominators needed to reach the universal Minkowski denominator is \((1,1,2,2,1,2,2,2,2,3)\) in genera \(1\) through \(10\). Genus \(10\) is the first requiring three: the full \(3^{14}\), \(5^6\), and \(19\) denominator powers occur at pairwise-disjoint coefficient-index sets, while indices \(\{0,1,3\}\) attain all prime powers together.
