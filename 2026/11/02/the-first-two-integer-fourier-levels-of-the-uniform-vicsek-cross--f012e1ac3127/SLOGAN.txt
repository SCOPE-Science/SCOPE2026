For the uniform five-map cross-form Vicsek probability measure, the two largest nonzero integer Fourier-multiplier moduli occur exactly at the \(3\)-power dilates of coordinate frequencies of sizes \(1\) and \(2\). All other nonzero frequencies have modulus strictly below \(6/25\), which is itself below the second extremal level, giving a sharp two-stage spectral separation for convolution on the torus.
