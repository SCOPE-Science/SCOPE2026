For the genus-\(55\) generalized Fermat curves of type \((3,4)\) in characteristic \(2\), the Jacobian has constant \(a=15\) and exactly three possible \(2\)-ranks: \(40\), \(38\), and \(30\). The five rank-drop divisors meet only at two \(\mathbb F_4\)-marked configurations, where all five tangent directions form \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_4)\).
