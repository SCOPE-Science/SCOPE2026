# Scientific review: minimum Italian functions on open ladders

## Correctness — PASS

The stated claim is an all-length theorem for the open \(2\)-row ladder with distinct row and column positions and Italian labels \(0,1,2\). The finite potential matrix is explicit. Its \(729\) middle-triple tests and both families of boundary inequalities imply the lower bound \(\gamma_I(L_n)\ge n\) by a telescoping argument. Alternating columns attain the bound. Tight initial/interior/final inequalities reduce all equality cases to a twelve-state directed graph with exactly the sixteen listed arcs, whose three alternating connector excursions have lengths \(1,2,5\). Hence the composition bijection, rational generating function and recurrence follow for every \(n\ge2\). The standalone verification program checks the finite certificate against the original feasibility rule and independently checks small graph labellings. Its finite tests are not used as infinite extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk

The direct motivating 2026 paper of Shao and Zhao counts Italian functions on paths, trees and cycles, not the minimum functions on open two-row Cartesian ladders. Gardner's 2018 thesis establishes ladder minimum weights; the available abstract does not state a complete extremal enumeration, but the inaccessible full thesis remains a material historical-priority risk. Accessible published findings on ordinary convexity, forts, minimal domination and perfect edge domination of ladders have different defining properties and do not imply the Italian-function recurrence. Records about Italian or Roman functions on complete multipartite graphs or complete prisms concern different adjacency structures. No equivalent claim was found through the indicated search comparisons; absence in a search index does not prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The contribution is a complete structural classification of every minimum Italian labelling, not a repeat of its known minimum weight. The genuinely nonbinary five-column excursion changes the exact generating function from the two shorter excursions alone and produces a different growth rate. This closes a motivated extremal counting question for a standard two-row grid family, in direct relation to the new Italian-domination-polynomial programme.
