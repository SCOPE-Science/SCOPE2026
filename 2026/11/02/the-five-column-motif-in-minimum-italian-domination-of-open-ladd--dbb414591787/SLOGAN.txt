Minimum Italian dominating functions on the open two-row ladder admit an exact tiling classification: choose one of two row orientations, then compose the remaining \(n-2\) columns into tiles of lengths \(1,2,5\). Consequently their number is \(2[x^{n-2}](1-x-x^2-x^5)^{-1}\), revealing a genuine five-column extremal motif.
