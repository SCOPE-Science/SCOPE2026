For the three-bilinear-equation Nash discriminant over an algebraically closed field of characteristic \(2\), the reduced discriminant is a cubic whose singular locus has four irreducible components: one degree-\(14\) fivefold and three linear \(\mathbb P^3\)'s, each meeting the fivefold in a quadric surface. The reduction of the classical sextic to the square of that cubic follows already from the known determinant formula and is not claimed as a separate novelty.
