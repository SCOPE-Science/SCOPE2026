For the two-parameter generalized Wiman–Edge sextic in every characteristic at least five, the exact geometrically integral nodal-cubic region \((a+b+3)(a^2+ab+b^2)\ne0\) splits along the explicit cubic discriminant: away from it the sextic has four nodes and genus six, while on it the normalization is rational with ten nodes, except for one four-node, six-cusp member at \((a,b)=(3,3)\).
