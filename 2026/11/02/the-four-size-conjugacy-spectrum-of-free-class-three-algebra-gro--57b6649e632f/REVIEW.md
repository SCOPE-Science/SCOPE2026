# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The group-centralizer equation is exactly \(uv=vu\), not a linearized approximation. The free-word lemmas prove the complete kernels of the degree-one and degree-two concatenation commutator maps over arbitrary fields, including characteristic two. The four ranks are derived with exact quantifiers, all parameters in the group are partitioned uniquely, and the pure-square count uses projective lines without double counting. The symbolic proof covers every finite field and \(d\ge2\), independently of the five finite-field computer checks. Remaining correctness risk: transcription in the formulas or checker, mitigated by direct coefficient comparison and standalone exact rank enumeration.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, provisional historical priority)

The closest same-object publication is Hansmann's full-text study of circle groups of free nilpotent algebras, but its principal results concern the subgroup generated by free generators and its relative-freeness, not the full algebra group's centralizer stratification. Soffer and Pak–Soffer concern unitriangular-pattern groups and class-number methods; their cited results do not imply the free-tensor four-size spectrum. Diaconis–Isaacs supplies general algebra-group superclass theory but was not available for complete full-text comparison, so this is an explicitly recorded residual publication-priority risk. Indexed-claim and accessible earlier research overlaps were tested by mathematical implication, not title similarity. An unlocated older result could cover the claim; search absence alone does not establish novelty.

## Value — PASS

Free nilpotent associative algebras and their algebra groups are natural universal objects. For *every* generator rank and finite field, the complete conjugacy-class-size distribution is a stronger invariant than the total class number, supplies a closed commuting probability, and shows a small, field-independent rank stratification controlled by a tensor-geometric pure-square condition. This is a coherent structural family theorem, not an isolated small-field census or routine renaming of a known matrix-family formula.

## Closest literature and limits

Hansmann (2019, based on a thesis from 2015) studies relative-freeness of a distinguished subgroup; Soffer (2014), Pak–Soffer (2015) and Diaconis–Isaacs (2008) address related algebra-group conjugacy theory. Only direct freely truncated degree-three algebra groups are covered here; no generalization to arbitrary nilpotent associative algebras or higher truncation is asserted.
