For nondegenerate bilinear radical braces of order \(q^3\), the three one-sided or involutive-flip probabilities all equal \((q^2+q-1)/q^3\), but the two-sided flip probability is controlled by eigenspaces of the bilinear-form cosquare. Across every labeled invertible \(2\times2\) form over an odd finite field there are exactly three probability strata at \(q=3\) and four at \(q\ge5\), with explicit exact matrix multiplicities.
