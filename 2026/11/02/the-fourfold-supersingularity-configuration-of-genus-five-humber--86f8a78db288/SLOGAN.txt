In characteristic \(23\), the genus-five Humbert family has exactly \(60\) marked points where four of its five elliptic quotients are supersingular; these constitute one branch-equivalence class, and no smooth member has five supersingular quotients. The complete geometric incidence and all-extension \(23\)-rank counts are explicit, and every marked member over \(\mathbb F_{23}\) is nonordinary.
