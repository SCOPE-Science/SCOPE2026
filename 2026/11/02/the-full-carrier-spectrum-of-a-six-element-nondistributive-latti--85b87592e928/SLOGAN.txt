The six-element nondistributive lattice \(L_{6,1}^{d}\) is the complete congruence lattice of a two-valued unary algebra on **every** carrier of size at least four, and on no smaller carrier. A two-valued interval-union construction gives uniform explicit representations and proves the sharp spectrum \(\operatorname{spec}_2(L_{6,1}^{d})=\{4,5,6,\ldots\}\).
