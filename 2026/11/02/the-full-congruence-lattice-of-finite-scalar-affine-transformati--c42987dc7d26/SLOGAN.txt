Every two-sided congruence of the scalar-affine transformation monoid on \(\mathbb F_q^r\) is determined by a nested pair of vector subspaces, or by a subgroup of \(\mathbb F_q^\times\), except for the universal congruence. This gives a closed Gaussian-binomial count of the whole lattice and a sharp \(q=2\) exception for its least commutative quotient.
