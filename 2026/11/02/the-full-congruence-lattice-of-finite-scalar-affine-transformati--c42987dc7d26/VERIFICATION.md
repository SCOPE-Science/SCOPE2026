# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A relation joining a unit and a constant collapses all constants by right multiplication, and then all elements by translating the unit to the identity. Otherwise the constant partition is affine invariant; the class difference at zero is an additive group stable under all nonzero scalars, hence an F_q-subspace W. Unit classes come from a normal subgroup N of the unit group; right multiplication by constants means N is contained in the kernel of the action on V/W. This condition is also sufficient, by considering unit/unit, unit/constant and constant/anything products. For W proper the kernel consists of translations in W; scalar conjugation makes N a subspace T. For W=V, any element of N with nonidentity scalar produces all translations by commutation, so the only extra kernels are V semidirect H. Inclusion gives the stated ordered sum. The dimension sum bounds strict flag steps by 2r, attained by growing W then T; the cyclic scalar interval contributes Omega(q-1) and the universal relation one more. Gaussian flag counts and cyclic subgroup counts give the displayed formula including q=2. Commutativity requires all constants equal and the unit derived subgroup in N; that subgroup is V for q>2 and trivial for q=2. Both indicated quotients are indeed commutative. This is a field-size-two boundary, not a general characteristic-two exception: q=4 belongs to q>2. Independently, python3 /workspace/reproductions/affine_congruences.py takes the partition generated by all two-sided contexts of each pair and exhausts joins of those principal congruences, so it neither assumes the flag classification nor needs Bell-partition enumeration. For (q,r)=(2,3),(3,2),(4,2),(5,1) the observed (congruence count, strict height, least commutative quotient size) is respectively (67,7,9),(17,6,3),(20,6,4),(6,5,5); the computed least commutative relations also matched pairwise. These checks do not substitute for the arbitrary-field-and-rank proof.
