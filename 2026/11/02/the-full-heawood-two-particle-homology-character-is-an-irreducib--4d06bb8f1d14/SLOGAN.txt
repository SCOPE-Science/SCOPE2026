The rational homology of two unordered particles on the Heawood graph carries an irreducible eight-dimensional graph-cycle representation in degree one and its direct sum with a distinct irreducible six-dimensional representation in degree two, under the full \(336\)-element graph automorphism group. The complete character makes the full symmetry quotient rationally acyclic, although a sevenfold-rotation quotient retains two classes in each of degrees one and two.
