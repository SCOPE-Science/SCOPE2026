## Originality — PASS against inspected prior work
An–Drummond-Cole–Knudsen (arXiv:1708.02351) gives general graph-braid machinery and first-homology results; An–Knudsen (arXiv:2008.10371) concerns planar generation of second homology, which does not apply to this nonplanar graph. Farber–Hanbury (arXiv:1005.2300) concerns ordered configurations and nonequivariant formulas for mature graphs, not the present explicit ordinary unordered symmetry character. Bibby–Chan–Gadish–Yun (arXiv:2109.03302) concerns one-point compactifications and particle-label actions rather than graph automorphisms. None of these inspected claims supplies the complete \(S_5\times C_2\) multiplicities here. The closest related computations involve the Petersen, dodecahedral and cuboctahedral graphs rather than this twenty-vertex incidence double cover. Unindexed equivalent calculations are a residual risk; an index search by itself is not a proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The Desargues graph is the canonical bipartite incidence double cover of the Petersen graph, with the central complement duality and an unusually large automorphism group. The finding resolves how the duality partitions all thirty-five degree-two classes and determines the full seven-constituent degree-two Specht signature. The contrast between the acyclic full-symmetric quotient and the nontrivial complement-only quotient is an intrinsic topological distinction that cannot be inferred from ordinary Betti numbers or a graph cycle count. The fact is natural to graph braid symmetry, not an arbitrary restricted numerical slice.
