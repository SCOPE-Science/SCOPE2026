For unordered two-point configurations on the Desargues graph, the complete \(S_5\times C_2\)-action splits rational first homology into dimensions \(6+5\), and second homology into central-even and central-odd parts of dimensions \(15+20\), with explicit multiplicity-free Specht constituents in each part. The central quotient retains rational Betti vector \((1,6,15)\), whereas quotienting by \(S_5\) already eliminates all positive-degree rational homology.
