# Scientific review: Gauss noninjectivity curves on the Fermat quartic

## Correctness — PASS
The Gauss map is \([x_i]\mapsto[x_i^3]\), so equal images have the same zero-coordinate support and differ on that support by cube-root phases. Modulo the scalar phase there are exactly 27 patterns, partitioned as 1 scalar, 8 of type \((3,1)\), 6 of type \((2,2)\), and 12 of type \((2,1,1)\). The 3+1 pattern gives only a fixed point on its support. The 2+2 pattern gives exactly 48 lines; the 2+1+1 pattern gives exactly 48 distinct smooth plane quartics. The proof explicitly handles the omitted coordinate-boundary points and does not confuse closure with the pointwise noninjectivity set. Generic fibres have cardinalities three and two respectively; intersections may be larger. An exact independent finite-field enumeration confirms the phase criterion over \(\mathbb F_{13},\mathbb F_{37},\mathbb F_{73}\), including cardinality-five special fibres. The finite check is not used to deduce the infinite-field theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with historical priority risk
Corvaja–Zucconi (2021) determine the Gauss-double geometry for very general quartic surfaces, not the highly symmetric Fermat quartic; their irreducible degree-320 general double divisor cannot be substituted for this 96-component reduced locus. Veniani (2017, 2021) provides the known 48-line baseline and symmetry context, but its inspected results do not state the additional 48 genus-three Gauss-double components or the exact contact condition. Searches of available mathematical records and primary-literature titles did not yield a statement implying the claimed classification. The possibility of unindexed older computational work remains, so the review is not an independent priority certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The self-overlap of a Gauss map is a natural enumerative problem in projective duality. Very general quartics have qualitatively different double-contact curves. This explicit Fermat specialization explains the degeneration into 48 rational lines of generic triple contact and 48 smooth genus-three curves of generic double contact, an exact structural classification beyond the familiar line count. It also identifies why the noninjectivity set is not closed and separates generic and special Gauss-fibre cardinalities.

## Limitations
All statements are geometric, over characteristic zero (with elementary extension away from 2,3), set-theoretic, and about marked components. Multiplicities and scheme structure of the double-contact divisor, as well as complete special-fibre intersection classification, are not asserted. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
