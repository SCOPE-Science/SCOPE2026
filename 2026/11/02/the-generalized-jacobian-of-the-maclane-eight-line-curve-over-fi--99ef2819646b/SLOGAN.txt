For the MacLane eight-line plane curve in every characteristic other than three, the connected Picard group has eight additive dimensions and a thirteen-dimensional torus whose nonsplit quadratic Frobenius lattice is **integrally** one sign module plus six regular modules. Consequently its finite-field rational-point group is \((\mathbb F_q,+)^8\times C_{q-1}^{13}\) when \(q\equiv1\pmod3\), and \((\mathbb F_q,+)^8\times C_{q+1}\times C_{q^2-1}^{6}\) when \(q\equiv2\pmod3\).
