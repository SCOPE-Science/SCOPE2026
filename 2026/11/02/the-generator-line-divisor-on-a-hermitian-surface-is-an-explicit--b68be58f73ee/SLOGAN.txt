On the Hermitian surface \(\sum_{i=0}^3 X_i^{q+1}=0\), the reduced union of all \((q+1)(q^3+1)\) generator lines has saturated ideal generated by this equation and \(\sum_{i=0}^3 X_i^{q^3+1}\). Thus its minimal additional equation has degree \(q^3+1\), and its singular points are exactly the rational Hermitian points, each supporting \(q+1\) transverse branches.
