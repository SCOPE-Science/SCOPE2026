The reduced union of all 48 geometric lines on the Fermat quartic in characteristic at least five has arithmetic genus 289 and a generalized Jacobian with a 72-dimensional unipotent part and an explicitly split-or-induced 217-dimensional torus. Its entire rational-point group is determined integrally for every finite field, and the line union has no rational point precisely when the field size is not congruent to one modulo eight.
