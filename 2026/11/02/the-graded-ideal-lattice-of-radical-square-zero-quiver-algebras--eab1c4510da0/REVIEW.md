# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** For any ideal, a nonzero coefficient of a primitive vertex idempotent produces an invertible element in that local corner, forcing the idempotent itself into the ideal. Products with orthogonal vertex idempotents separate all arrow spaces; the radical-square-zero relation leaves arbitrary independent subspaces in the surviving corners. This establishes a bijection, the order relation, the exact weighted count and all boundary cases, including loops and empty arrow sets. Gaussian coefficient leading terms give the asymptotic after checking which survivor subsets retain every positive degree contribution. The isolated prime-field computations are corroborating evidence, not the proof of the unrestricted theorem.
