For every finite quiver, the full quotient-dimension distribution of two-sided ideals in \(\mathbb F_qQ/J^2\) is an explicit sum of products of Gaussian subspace enumerators, valid for loops and parallel arrows. As the field grows, ideal counts concentrate on survivor sets containing every endpoint of a parallel-arrow class, with a computable limiting quotient-dimension law; multiplicity two is the exact threshold for field-size dependence.
