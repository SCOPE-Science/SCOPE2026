For cyclic two-approval elections, every committee is Hare-core stable when \(2k<n\), but at \(n=2k\) stability holds exactly when there are no three consecutive unselected candidates. There are precisely \(2[z^k](1+z+z^2)^k\) stable committees, an asymptotic fraction \(\sqrt3(3/4)^k\) of all size-\(k\) committees.
