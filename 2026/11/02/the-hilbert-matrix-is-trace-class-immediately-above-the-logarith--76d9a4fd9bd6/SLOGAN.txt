The classical Hilbert matrix from the logarithmically weighted Bergman space \(A^2_{\log^\alpha}\) into \(A^2\) is trace class precisely when \(\alpha>2\); at \(\alpha=2\) it is bounded but noncompact, and below \(2\) it is unbounded. A dyadic rank-one expansion resolves nuclearity at the same threshold where boundedness first becomes strict.
