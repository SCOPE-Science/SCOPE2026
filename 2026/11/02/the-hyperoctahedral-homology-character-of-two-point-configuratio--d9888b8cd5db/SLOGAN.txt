For two unordered particles on the 4-dimensional cross-polytope **graph**, the complete 20-class hyperoctahedral rational homology character has Betti numbers \((1,17,56)\). The full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic, but the axis-permutation quotient retains two second-homology classes and the independent-sign-flip quotient has Betti numbers \((1,3,3)\).
