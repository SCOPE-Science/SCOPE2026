For the two-generated free class-three Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_{q^m}\), considered over \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(m\ge2\), all ideals form three explicitly glued subspace-lattice strata, while all abelian subalgebras form \(q^{2m}+q^m+1\) subspace lattices glued along the center. Exactly one maximal abelian subalgebra is an ideal; the others form a second automorphism orbit, and both dimension enumerators are closed Gaussian-binomial formulas.
