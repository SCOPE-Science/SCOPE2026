In a uniform random binary search tree, the covariance between total internal path length and the leaf indicator of a key changes sign sharply with its inorder rank: for every \(n\ge6\), only ranks \(2\) and \(n-1\) have positive covariance. The complete finite-sample rank profile has a closed rational formula, and summing it yields \(\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,P_n)=-(n-2)(n+3)/(9n)\).
