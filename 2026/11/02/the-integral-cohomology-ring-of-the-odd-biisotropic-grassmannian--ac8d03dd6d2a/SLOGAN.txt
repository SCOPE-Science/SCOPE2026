For every \(n\ge2\), the complete **integral cup-product ring** of the odd biisotropic Grassmannian of lines \(\operatorname{BIGr}(2;2n+1)\) has an explicit two-generator, two-relation complete-intersection presentation. Its additive Betti numbers match \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,2n)\), but its integral cohomology ring does not: the Plücker top powers are different Catalan numbers.
