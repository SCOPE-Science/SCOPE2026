Over \(\mathbb Z_{(5)}\), the Clebsch cubic's relative scheme of lines is exactly fifteen sections plus six quadratic branches \(\operatorname{Spec}(R[T]/(T^2+T-1))\). At the singular characteristic-five fiber, twenty-seven generic lines specialize to fifteen reduced lines and six doubled node-lines, with the fifteen ordinary lines forming the edges of a natural \(K_6\) on the six node-lines.
