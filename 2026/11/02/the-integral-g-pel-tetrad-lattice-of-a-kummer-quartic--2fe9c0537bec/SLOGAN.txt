The sixty Göpel tetrads on the sixteen nodes of a genus-two Kummer quartic span exactly the integer node-weight vectors whose total is divisible by four and whose four linear plus one quadratic symplectic moments vanish modulo two. The quotient is \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^5\), giving a complete congruence test for membership.
