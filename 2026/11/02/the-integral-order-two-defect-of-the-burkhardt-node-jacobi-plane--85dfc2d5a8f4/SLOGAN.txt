For the characteristic-two Burkhardt quartic, the integral incidence map from its \(40\) Jacobi planes to its \(45\) nodes has nonzero Smith factors \(1^{15},2^{10}\). Its cokernel is \(\mathbb Z^{20}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^{10}\), exhibiting ten independent order-two obstructions to saturation of the plane-incidence lattice.
