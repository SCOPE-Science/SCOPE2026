The full integral \(S_m\)-homology of the independence complex of the two-subset Kneser graph is the hook lattice \(\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^m\to\mathbb Z)\), not merely an unspecified wedge of spheres. Its full symmetric and alternating quotients have trivial positive rational homology, while cyclic rotation quotients have an explicit, usually positive second Betti number.
