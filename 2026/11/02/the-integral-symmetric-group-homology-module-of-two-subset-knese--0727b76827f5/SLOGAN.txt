The known wedge-of-spheres homology of two-subset Kneser independence complexes carries a canonical integral symmetry module: for every \(n\ge4\), its only nonzero reduced homology is \(\widetilde H_2\cong\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^{[n]}\to\mathbb Z)\) as a \(\mathbb Z[\mathfrak S_n]\)-module. An explicit equivariant star-to-graph retraction identifies its attaching map with augmentation contraction and determines the complete symmetric-group character.
