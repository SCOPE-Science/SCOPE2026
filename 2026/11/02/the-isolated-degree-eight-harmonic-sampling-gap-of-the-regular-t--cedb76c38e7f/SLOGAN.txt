The regular truncated octahedron has exact-degree spherical-harmonic rank \(1,3,5,6,9,9,10,12,11\) in degrees zero through eight and the maximal parity-compatible rank \(12\) in every degree at least nine. Its isolated degree-eight loss is a unique alternating-permutation moment relation between two twelve-vertex alternating-permutation classes, rather than a uniform spherical-design identity.
