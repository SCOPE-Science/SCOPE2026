The backbite reconfiguration graph of the 504 undirected Hamiltonian paths in the Heawood graph is obtained from the cubic Klein graph by truncating each vertex to a triangle, doubling intertriangle edges, and subdividing every resulting edge once. This graph is connected and bipartite, has radius 24 and diameter 25, and reveals the 56-vertex Klein graph by a canonical suppression-and-contraction procedure.
