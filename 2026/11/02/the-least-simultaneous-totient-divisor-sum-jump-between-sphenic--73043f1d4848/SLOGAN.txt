The least positive shift \(h\) admitting simultaneous \(\varphi\)- and \(\sigma\)-jumps of size \(h\) between two products of three distinct primes is \(320\), attained by \(7337=11\cdot23\cdot29\) and \(7657=13\cdot19\cdot31\). An exact \(37\)-shape discriminant census and a modulo-\(3\) obstruction exclude every smaller shift.
