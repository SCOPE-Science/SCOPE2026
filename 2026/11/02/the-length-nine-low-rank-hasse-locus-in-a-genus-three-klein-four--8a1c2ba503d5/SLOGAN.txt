For the smooth reciprocal genus-three family \(y^2=x^8+a x^6+b x^4+a x^2+1\) in characteristic \(7\), the low-\(7\)-rank Hasse locus has exactly six reduced rank-one parameter points and one superspecial point with nonreduced length three. Its determinantal scheme has total length nine, so no other rank-at-most-one points occur over the algebraic closure.
