The 240 projective line-packings of \(\operatorname{PG}(3,2)\) admit no one- or two-spread trades, but their smallest legal three-spread trades form a connected, bipartite, 7-regular graph of diameter five. All shortest-path layers have sizes \((1,7,42,105,77,8)\), and every minimal trade exchanges the missing symbol of an eight-labelled Fano plane with one of its seven used symbols.
