For the genus-five cyclic-cover family \(z^4=(w^2-1)^2(w^2-t)(w^2-u)\) in characteristic seven, the Jacobian \(a\)-number is exactly \(2\), \(3\), or \(5\), never \(4\). The superspecial locus consists of the two reduced ordered parameter points \((3,5)\) and \((5,3)\), with all other parameters classified by three explicit lines.
