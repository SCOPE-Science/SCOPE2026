The 49 rational singular points of the characteristic-seven Klein 21-line arrangement have homogeneous ideal generated by three degree-eight Frobenius minors and exactly one additional degree-nine separator \(xyz(x^2-y^2)(y^2-z^2)(z^2-x^2)\). Their minimal ideal resolution has syzygy shifts \(9,11,13\), completing the missing-generator case noted by Seceleanu.
