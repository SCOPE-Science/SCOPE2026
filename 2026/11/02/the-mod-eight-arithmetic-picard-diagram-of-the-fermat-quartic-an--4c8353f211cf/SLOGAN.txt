For the Fermat quartic over every odd prime field, the arithmetic Picard rank is \(20,12,8,12\) as \(p\) runs through residues \(1,3,5,7\pmod8\). The 48 classical lines account for \(20,11,8,11\) invariant divisor directions respectively; at every supersingular prime their two-dimensional missing quotient splits into one Frobenius-fixed and one Frobenius-negated class.
