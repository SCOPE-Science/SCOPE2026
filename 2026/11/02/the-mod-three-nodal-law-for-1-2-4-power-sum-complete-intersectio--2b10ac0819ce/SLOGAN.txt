The complex power-sum complete intersection \(p_1=p_2=p_4=0\) in \(\mathbb P^{n-1}\), for every \(n\ge4\), is smooth exactly when \(3\nmid n\). When \(n=3a\), its singularities are exactly \(n!/[3(a!)^3]\) ordinary nodes at equally repeated cubic roots of unity; the size-six root configuration is classical, while the theorem classifies every dimension.
