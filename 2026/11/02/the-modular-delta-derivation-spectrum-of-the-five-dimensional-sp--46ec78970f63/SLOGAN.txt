For the five-dimensional Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{sl}_2(k)\ltimes k^2\) over any field of characteristic other than two, every \(\delta\)-derivation space has an explicit dimension given by a single formula. Characteristic three merges ordinary and \((-2)\)-derivations into dimension eight, while characteristic five merges the \((-2)\)- and half-derivation strata into dimension five; the full claim follows from an exact symbolic unit-pivot reduction.
