The reduced two-dimensional representation variety of the Jordan plane \(xy-yx=y^2\) has exactly two geometric components over every field, but it is equidimensional only outside characteristic two: in characteristic two their dimensions are \(4\) and \(5\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), its exact labeled point count jumps from \(2q^4-q^2\) for odd \(q\) to \(q^5+q^4-q^3\) for even \(q\).
