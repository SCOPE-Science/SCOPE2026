For the genus-four cyclic-quintic hyperelliptic pencil \(y^2=(x^5-1)(x^5-\lambda)\), the geometric \(p\)-rank is either \(0\) or \(4\) whenever \(p\equiv2,3\pmod5\). In characteristics \(3\) and \(7\), the full nonordinary Cartier divisor is respectively \(2[-1]\) and \(2[-1]+2[\lambda_+]+2[\lambda_-]\), and every exceptional curve has \(a\)-number \(2\).
