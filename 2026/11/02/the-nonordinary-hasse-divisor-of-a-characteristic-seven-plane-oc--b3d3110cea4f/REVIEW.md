# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** For the smooth characteristic-seven octic pencil, the proof derives the singular-parameter exclusion, the order-seven quotient and its genus-three Hasse ranks, then calculates the full parent Hasse matrix by the exact coefficient formula for residue forms. Eleven explicit row/column blocks have determinants whose product is \(-\lambda^{21}(\lambda^2-2)^7\); their ranks at the exceptional roots give the parent \(a\)-numbers. Deuring–Shafarevich gives the separately determined parent geometric \(7\)-ranks. The two included standalone programs reproduce the finite symbolic coefficient and determinant identities, rather than relying on sample computations.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to priority risk.** Borges, Korchmáros and Speziali (arXiv:2202.05765v1, Sections 3, 4.8 and 8.1) supply the octic pencil, the group action, the quotient and generic ordinarity. Novoselov (arXiv:1902.05992v1, Section 2) supplies broad quotient-type hyperelliptic Cartier formulas. Both are credited. The parent pencil's complete nonordinary scheme multiplicities and all its \(a\)-numbers are not stated in these inspected results, and do not follow from the quotient's \(7\)-rank alone. Targeted literature and mathematical-record searches did not yield covering statements, but an unindexed or uninspected equivalent calculation remains possible.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The exact \(21[0]+7[3]+7[-3]\) Hasse divisor and parent \(a\)-numbers refine a published generic-ordinarity theorem into a complete exceptional-locus classification for a natural symmetric wild-cover family. They distinguish a superspecial degeneration from two rank-seven Cartier defects and give precise Hasse-section multiplicities. This information is not supplied by geometric \(7\)-rank or quotient information alone and is directly relevant to explicit stratification in characteristic \(7\).

## Limits and closest literature
The result is limited to smooth characteristic-seven coefficient-marked curves, not moduli or isomorphism-class counts. The two primary sources above are the nearest relevant literature; all singular parameters and full Ekedahl–Oort types are outside the claim. No independent external audit is claimed.
