For the smooth characteristic-seven invariant plane-octic pencil \(C_\lambda:Y^8-X^7Z-XZ^7-\lambda(Y^2-2XZ)^4=0\), the exact Hasse determinant is \(-\lambda^{21}(\lambda^2-2)^7\), and the only nonordinary parameters are \(0\) and \(\pm3\). Their geometric \(7\)-ranks and \(a\)-numbers are respectively \((0,21)\) and \((14,7)\); all remaining smooth parameters are ordinary.
