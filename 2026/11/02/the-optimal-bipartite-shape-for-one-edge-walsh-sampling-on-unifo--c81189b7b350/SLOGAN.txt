The complete one-edge Walsh Gram spectrum of uniformly sampled spanning trees of \(K_{a,b}\) has exactly one deterministic null direction and four explicit positive spectral sectors. At fixed vertex order, the optimally conditioned bipartition is balanced only at small orders, is \((3,n-3)\) for \(10\le n\le14\), and switches permanently to \((2,n-2)\) for \(n\ge15\), with exact squared condition number \(n-3\).
