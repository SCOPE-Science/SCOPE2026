For a smooth cubic surface, the formal sums of the twelve lines in its \(36\) Schläfli double-sixes span an index-\(65536\) sublattice of the primitive anticanonical-relation lattice on its \(27\) lines. The quotient is \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^{14}\oplus\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\), making multiplication by \(4\) the sharp universal integral reconstruction multiplier.
