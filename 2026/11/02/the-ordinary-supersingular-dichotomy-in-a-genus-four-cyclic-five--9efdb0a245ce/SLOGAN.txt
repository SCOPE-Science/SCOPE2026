In characteristic \(7\), the order-five hyperelliptic genus-four pencil \(y^2=x^{10}+ax^5+1\) has exactly three smooth nonordinary parameters, characterized by \(a(a^2+1)=0\). Their Jacobians are supersingular with \(a\)-number \(2\); every other smooth fiber is ordinary, and the dichotomy is explained by two genus-two quotients.
