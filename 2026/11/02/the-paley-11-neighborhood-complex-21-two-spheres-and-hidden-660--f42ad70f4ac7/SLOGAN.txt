The out-neighborhood complex of the Paley tournament on \(11\) vertices is a wedge of \(21\) two-spheres, yet its full simplicial symmetry group has \(660\) elements, rather than the tournament's natural \(55\)-element affine group. Exactly one rational homology class survives the affine quotient, but none survives the full-symmetry quotient; the full degree-two character splits into distinct irreducibles of dimensions \(10\) and \(11\).
