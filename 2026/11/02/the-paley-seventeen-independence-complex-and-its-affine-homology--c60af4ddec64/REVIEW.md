# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Exact full-face enumeration for the quadratic-residue graph gives 17 vertices, 68 independent edges, 68 independent triangles, and no independent sets of size four. A complete deterministic matching of 68 face pairs leaves a single zero-cell and sixteen two-cells; a full topological sort of all 340 oriented Hasse covers proves acyclicity, yielding the sphere wedge integrally. Rational Lefschetz traces of all 136 affine automorphisms agree with the vertex-permutation augmentation character; equality of finite-group rational characters proves the representation statement. The quotient consequence follows from exact averaging on rational finite-group chains. `checker.py` replays all finite checks. No infinite extrapolation is made.

## Originality
**PASS on examined records; priority remains provisional.** The inspected full texts of Deshpande–Singh (arXiv:2001.05448) and Berghoff (arXiv:2008.06267) contain methods but neither the exact Paley-17 result nor its affine character. Indexed public records on Paley-17 **neighborhood** topology and Paley-29 **independence** topology concern different complexes or orders and neither mathematically covers the present claim. Searches included clique/independence synonyms and Paley self-complementarity, as well as the affine augmentation character. The highly related indexed neighborhood-complex record was inspected at summary level only; its full text was not available, leaving residual bibliographic risk. Finding no indexed coverage is not a proof of universal novelty.

## Value
**PASS.** The Paley graph of order seventeen is the canonical extremal 17-vertex Ramsey graph without a clique or independent set of size four. Its resulting two-dimensional independence complex is therefore a natural topological object, not an arbitrarily chosen finite slice. The integral wedge decomposition and *full affine symmetry module* identify meaningful information about this extremal graph; character values and rational quotient acyclicity are not supplied by the face vector or ordinary Betti ranks. This is a complete finite classification, not a general theorem over Paley orders.

## Scientific limitations
The affine subgroup is explicitly specified, but the representation under any larger full automorphism group is not claimed. Rational quotient acyclicity leaves integral quotient torsion undetermined. The computer-assisted acyclic matching is finite and completely replayable. Unindexed identical or stronger prior results remain possible.
