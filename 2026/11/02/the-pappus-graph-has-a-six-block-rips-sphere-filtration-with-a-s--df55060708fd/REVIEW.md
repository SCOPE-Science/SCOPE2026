# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The graph is explicitly specified by a standard LCF cycle/chord prescription. The independent face enumeration yields the exact scale-two face vector \((18,81,108,18)\). The 99 deterministic Morse pairs have precisely one vertex and 26 two-cells left critical, and the **full** oriented Hasse graph on 225 faces has a verified topological ordering. Hence the integral wedge-of-spheres statement does not rest on Betti ranks alone. At scale three the exact missing-edge graph consists of six disconnected triangles, making the Rips complex the join of six three-point discrete complexes. The integral join formula and Koszul sign produce the asserted 64-generator lattice and group character. All 216 automorphism actions are computed and checked by independent signed fixed-simplex traces. All dimensions, exact scale endpoints, coefficient rings, and quotient hypotheses were checked.

## Originality (PASS against inspected evidence)
Adamaszek–Adams 2021 calculates graph-metric Rips topology for hypercubes, not the eighteen-vertex Pappus incidence graph. Feng 2023 supplies a hypercube scale-three theorem, not a covering general statement for distance-regular incidence graphs. Adamaszek's graph-power paper concentrates on stability hypotheses and cycles; its no-induced-six-cycle sufficient hypothesis fails on Pappus, and its later specific graph constructions do not imply the computed homotopy/character. Mathematical-index searches for Pappus, its Levi and Bouwer aliases, graph-square homology, the numbers 26 and 64, and six-block automorphism modules did not return a covering Rips result. The closest indexed Pappus computation is for the distinct intrinsic **Čech** nerve, with a different stated Betti degree and multiplicity. Other Pappus matching and independence complexes are also different objects. An unindexed direct computation or a general theorem not retrieved remains possible; failed searches do not establish global uniqueness.

## Value (PASS)
This completes a natural all-scale topological classification for a canonical 18-vertex distance-regular incidence graph rather than an arbitrary subgraph or artificially tuned parameter. The change from 26 degree-two spheres to a six-block join of 64 degree-five spheres is structurally explained. More decisively, the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic at scale two but retains an invariant degree-five class at scale three, which ordinary Betti numbers cannot determine. The complete tensor-character formula gives further usable information about every automorphism, not merely a single number or superficial homology-rank recomputation. The result is confined to this graph and does not claim a general graph-family theorem.
