The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of the Pappus graph passes through wedges of \(10\) circles, \(26\) two-spheres, and \(64\) five-spheres before becoming contractible. Its scale-three top homology is the signed tensor product of six rank-two augmentation lattices, and exactly one rational fifth-dimensional class survives the full \(216\)-element automorphism quotient, while no positive-degree class survives at scale two.
