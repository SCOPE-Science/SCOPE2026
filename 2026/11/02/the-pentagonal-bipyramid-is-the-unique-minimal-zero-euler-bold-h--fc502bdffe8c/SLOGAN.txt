Among all connected simple graphs on at most seven vertices, the pentagonal bipyramid \(C_5*\overline{K_2}\) is the unique isomorphism class with \(D_c(G;-1)=0\) and nonzero mod-two bold homology; seven is the sharp minimum order. The witness itself was already covered by a published suspension formula: the new result is the exhaustive minimality and uniqueness classification.
