The \(120\) named three-colourings of the Petersen graph form a six-regular Kempe-change graph whose colour-permutation quotient is five \(K_4\) cliques linked by the reversal edges of \(K_5\). Every colouring has exactly \(6,25,61,27\) neighbours at distances \(1,2,3,4\), while elementary single-vertex recolouring instead leaves sixty isolated two-state components.
