For any integrable integer-valued increasing-failure-rate law, adjacent mass dominance \(\Pr(X=m+1)\ge\Pr(X=m)>0\) at or below the mean forces \(\Pr(X\ge m+1)\ge\rho-1=0.3247179572\ldots\), where \(\rho^3=\rho+1\). The constant is sharp, and an explicit one-sided geometric-tail law is its unique equality case up to translation.
