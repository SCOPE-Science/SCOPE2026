# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS (self-review).** The unique singular point and unique tangent line reduce every projective automorphism to an affine action on the normalization parameter. Comparing coefficients of \(t^{q+2},t^q,t^2,t,1\) yields the exact geometric stabilizers, including the exceptional cancellation at \(q=2\). Direct differentiation of the defining homogeneous polynomial gives the complete three-dimensional Lie algebra modulo scalar matrices. A displayed finite subgroup scheme is verified by Frobenius and its coordinate-ring length is exact. The independent sparse-polynomial checker verifies small characteristic-two cases, but the proof does not rely on extrapolation from them.

## Originality

**PASS, with historical uncertainty.** Kollár's source defines these very curves and discusses their unique cusp, but the inspected full text does not state their projective symmetry groups, Lie algebras or explicit nonreduced stabilizer subgroup. Search comparisons considered characteristic-two strange curves, exact equation and parametrization aliases, general projective stabilizers and non-smooth automorphism schemes. The closest accessible mathematical records concern different curves or even different algebraic objects; they neither imply the sharp degree-four transition nor the explicit length formula. Full historical exhaustiveness is impossible, and a differently worded older classification remains a risk.

## Value

**PASS.** This is a complete geometric projective-symmetry classification for an infinite family of arbitrarily high-degree rational cuspidal curves, plus a quantitative group-scheme discrepancy. The exceptional additive symmetry in degree four and the persistent three-dimensional tangent space even when the reduced stabilizer is finite give structural information relevant to positive-characteristic equivariant geometry and stabilizer behavior. The result does not claim a classification of all abstract curve automorphisms or of the entire nonreduced stabilizer scheme.

The original paper's curve construction and singularity analysis are prior work; the exact projective-symmetry and infinitesimal-stabilizer formulas are the assessed addition. Independent mathematical validation and exhaustive historical-priority review remain outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
