The rational cuspidal plane curves \(Z^2Y^q+X^{q+2}+XZ^{q+1}=0\) in characteristic two have projective automorphism group \(\mu_{q+1}(k)\) for \(q\ge4\), while the degree-four member has \(\mathbb G_a(k)\rtimes\mu_3(k)\). Despite these reduced symmetries, every member has a three-dimensional infinitesimal projective stabilizer and an explicit nonreduced finite subgroup scheme of length \(q^3(q+1)\).
