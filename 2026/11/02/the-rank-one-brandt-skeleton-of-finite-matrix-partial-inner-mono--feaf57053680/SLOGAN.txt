For every \(n\ge3\) and prime power \(q\), the rank-one partial-inner maps of \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\) form exactly two orthogonal minimal nonzero ideals: \(B_{E_n}(1)\) and \(B_{Z_n}(\mathbb F_q^\times)\), with \(E_n=q^{n-1}(q^n-1)/(q-1)\) and \(Z_n=(q^n-1)(q^{n-1}-1)/(q-1)^2\). Together with \(\operatorname{PGL}_n(q)\), they form a proper submonoid of order \(1+|\operatorname{PGL}_n(q)|+E_n^2+(q-1)Z_n^2\).
