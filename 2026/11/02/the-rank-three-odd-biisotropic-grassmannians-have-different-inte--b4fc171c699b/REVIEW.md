# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
For the general pencil the section of \((\wedge^2S^\vee)^{\oplus2}\) has regular zero locus of expected codimension six by the cited smoothness theorem. The Grassmannian coefficient extraction computes the top Plücker degree. The key auxiliary polynomial identity is verified exactly over the integer polynomial ring, while torus localization independently checks multiple specializations. A standard-form coordinate four-space supplies a Plücker line, proving that the degree-two classes on both manifolds are primitive. Top intersection inequality then rigorously excludes a graded integral-ring isomorphism.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority qualified)
Fonarev--Kuznetsov identify the graded abelian groups, explicitly isolating cup-product structure as a further problem. Neither the even-dimensional constructions of Benedetti or Fatighenti--Mongardi nor the rank-two equivariant ring calculations imply the rank-three comparison. Source and mathematical-record searches found no identical theorem. A manuscript on the smallest odd rank-three example is cited as in preparation, and unlocated older equivalent calculations remain a residual risk. This is not a claim of a complete cohomology-ring presentation.

## Value — PASS
The comparison is the natural first higher-rank test of whether the recently discovered additive coincidence reflects an isomorphism of cohomology rings. A uniform top-intersection obstruction proves that it does not, and the closed Plücker degree gives a natural enumerative invariant for an infinite family of smooth Fano varieties. The exact contrast is already visible as \(61\) against \(5\) in the smallest case.

## Limits
Only smooth general complex odd biisotropic three-plane varieties with \(n\ge3\) are considered; no general-rank statement, complete ring presentation, or expert audit is asserted. Priority remains provisional where in-preparation literature is inaccessible.
