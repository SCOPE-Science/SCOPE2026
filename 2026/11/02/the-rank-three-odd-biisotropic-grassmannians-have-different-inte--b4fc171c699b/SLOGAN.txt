For every \(n\ge3\), the odd biisotropic Grassmannian \(\operatorname{BIGr}(3,2n+1)\) and \(\operatorname{Gr}(3,2n-1)\) have identical integral graded cohomology groups but nonisomorphic graded cup-product rings. The obstruction is a strict discrepancy between the top self-intersection numbers of their primitive Plücker classes (already \(61\) versus \(5\) when \(n=3\)).
