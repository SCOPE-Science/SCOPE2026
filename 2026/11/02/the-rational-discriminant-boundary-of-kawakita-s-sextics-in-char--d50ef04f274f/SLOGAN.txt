On the nonempty discriminant boundary \((ab)^2=(a+b)^3\) with \(a+b\ne0\) and \(a\ne b\), Kawakita's characteristic-three sextic is geometrically integral and rational, with exactly four ordinary cusps and six ordinary nodes. The six nodes occur in pairs on the three coordinate axes where the cuspidal cubic quotient acquires an order-two axis tangency.
