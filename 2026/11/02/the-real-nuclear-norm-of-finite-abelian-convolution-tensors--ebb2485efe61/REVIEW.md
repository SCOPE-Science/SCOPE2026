# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim fixes the standard orthonormal counting-measure basis, the threefold projective norm, scalar field, and every finite abelian group including the trivial case. Real Fourier/character decomposition gives exactly one one-dimensional real block per real character and one two-dimensional real complex-multiplication block per nonreal conjugate pair. Finite abelian duality gives exactly \(r=|G[2]|\) real characters. The convolution normalization gives the scalar coefficient \(\sqrt n\) and the complex-block coefficient \(\sqrt{n/2}\). The real \(\mathbb C\)-multiplication tensor has four coefficients of absolute value one, with injective norm one; projective/injective duality proves that its projective norm is exactly four. A block-diagonal injective witness of norm at most one matches the explicit rank-one cost. Complex Fourier diagonalization and a normalized tensor witness give the known comparison value \(n^{3/2}\). The proof quantifies over all groups, not merely the finite examples. Executing the embedded numerical script passed all 13 listed groups, but these finite tests are only supplementary.

## Originality — PASS, best-knowledge assessment

Prochno's October 2026 cyclic tensor is the same object up to factor permutation, but the cited proposition computes its injective trilinear norm and Hilbert–Schmidt norm, not its real projective norm. Derksen's group-algebra theorem computes the **complex-field** nuclear norm, including the entire complex companion in this result; the complex formula must not be presented as new. Dai–Lim compute the real tensor nuclear norm of multiplication of a *single pair of complex numbers*. Their statement does not cover an arbitrary real finite abelian group, the count of its real Fourier blocks, the exact group-wide norm, or the sharp real/complex ratio. The complete real group classification is a new conjunction of a structural real Fourier decomposition and sharp block-diagonal projective duality, rather than a restatement of the complex-field theorem. Searches of relevant public mathematical records and accessible research claims located no exact group-wide real formula. The 2016 real Wedderburn perspective and the existing block facts make implicit earlier coverage a meaningful residual publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The projective tensor norm is the minimum sum of lengths in a rank-one decomposition and measures the optimal growth factor of bilinear convolution algorithms. The exact real value is a natural invariant missing from the explicitly complex group-algebra computation. The sharp ratio detects \(2\)-torsion and distinguishes the cyclic four-group from the Klein four-group at equal group order, where the complex norm cannot distinguish them. This is a complete natural classification for every finite abelian group, not a substitution or parameter-level numerical improvement.
