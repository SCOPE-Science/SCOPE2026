For every prime \(p\equiv5\pmod6\), the marked genus-three bielliptic Picard family \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+b\) has exactly a degree-\((p-5)/6\) reduced superspecial Hasse divisor: its Jacobian has \((p\text{-rank},a\text{-number})=(0,3)\) on the divisor and \((2,1)\) everywhere else in the smooth family. There are precisely \(\lceil(p-5)/12\rceil\) marked superspecial geometric classes, counted by the roots of an explicit coefficient polynomial modulo the scaling \(a\sim-a\).
