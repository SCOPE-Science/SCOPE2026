For all total orders on \(n\) labeled items queried by pairwise comparisons, the exact Littlestone dimensions through \(n=7\) are \(0,1,2,4,6,9,11\): seven is the first size where the cardinality bound \(\lfloor\log_2(n!)\rfloor\) cannot be attained. Complete finite decision-tree certificates and an exhaustive seven-item obstruction prove the threshold, while six-item block concatenation yields an unbounded, linearly growing gap over the known VC dimension.
