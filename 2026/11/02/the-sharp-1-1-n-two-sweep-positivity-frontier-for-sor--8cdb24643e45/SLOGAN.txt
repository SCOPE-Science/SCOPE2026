For every dimension \(n\ge2\), the largest relaxation parameter that guarantees a nonnegative **second forward SOR sweep from zero** for all nonsingular \(M\)-matrices and all nonnegative right-hand sides is exactly \(1+1/n\). An ordering with lower-triangular nilpotency index \(q\) is safe through \(1+1/q\), while arbitrarily weakly coupled symmetric positive-definite paths prove the dimension-wide bound sharp.
