In the Möbius ladder on \(2n\) vertices with \(n\ge4\), the shortest permitted alternating-cycle flip length that connects all perfect matchings is \(4\) for even \(n\) but exactly \(n+1\) for odd \(n\). In the odd case, each of two previously square-flip-frozen perimeter matchings gains exactly \(n\) one-chord neighbours at that sharp threshold.
