For every power-Fock weight \(e^{-|z|^\alpha}\), the logarithmically normalized total projection leakage of an angular \(L^2\) symbol converges exactly to \(\alpha^{-1}\sum_m m^2|a_m|^2\), including the value \(+\infty\). Finite leakage coefficient is equivalent to the circle Sobolev condition \(a\in H^1(\mathbb T)\); unimodular symbols give a sharp Toeplitz spectral-defect trace law.
