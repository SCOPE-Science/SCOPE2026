# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The only centered ball radii on \(K_{3,3}\) are zero, one, and two. The strong \(\ell^2\) norm is exactly the largest singular value among 729 nonnegative six-by-six row-selection matrices. The automorphism reduction gives 55 representatives. For 54 choices the exact, reproduced Sylvester minors of \(39I/25-A^{\mathsf T}A\) are positive; the remaining spectrum is elementary and gives \((13+3\sqrt{17})/16\) as the leading squared singular value. Its strictly active, positive eigenvector accounts for every equality case. The lower formula for all \(K_{r,r}\) follows from explicit two-color constant inputs, and its limit and monotonicity are proved without assuming all-order sharpness. The exact checker is complete and independent of floating-point tests.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Soria–Tradacete (arXiv:1410.6204) supplies foundational graph-maximal theory and low-exponent results, while González-Riquelme–Madrid (arXiv:2005.03146; arXiv:2011.02630) computes sharp strong norms for complete graphs and stars. The inspected primary statements do not imply the cubic-biclique value. Previously determined exact strong norms for four- and five-cycles are different cases; the \(r=2\) lower-formula specialization is explicitly acknowledged as known. Targeted checks found no exact prior \(K_{3,3}\) norm, but earlier unindexed equivalent computations remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The six-vertex cubic biclique is the smallest cubic bipartite graph and a canonical test of strong-type maximal regularity beyond complete/star geometries. The exact constant and rigid two-class extremizers show the precise nontrivial competition of local and global balls. The derived all-order balanced-biclique bound is attained within a canonical invariant subspace and grows strictly toward \(\sqrt2\), giving a structural infinite-family comparison rather than an isolated numeric evaluation.

## Limits

The norm of \(M_{K_{r,r}}\) is not determined for \(r\ge4\); the proved \(B_r\) there is only a lower bound. Other graph measures, unbalanced sizes, and exponents are untreated. Publication priority has not been independently verified.
