The centered graph-ball maximal operator on the smallest cubic bipartite graph has exact sharp strong \(\ell^2\) norm \(\sqrt{13+3\sqrt{17}}/4\), attained only by two part-constant nonnegative rays up to signs and automorphisms. On every balanced biclique \(K_{r,r}\), an explicit attained lower bound rises strictly to \(\sqrt2\); equality with the full norm is proved here at \(r=3\), not at larger orders.
