The projective complete intersection cut out by the first \(m\ge3\) power sums in \(n\) variables over characteristic \(p>m\) is smooth exactly for \(n<p\). At the sharp boundary \(n=p\), translation identifies its graded ring with a polynomial extension in one variable, forcing a unique singular vertex of Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity \(m!\) and a precise fat-point/line-arrangement/normal-cone trichotomy.
