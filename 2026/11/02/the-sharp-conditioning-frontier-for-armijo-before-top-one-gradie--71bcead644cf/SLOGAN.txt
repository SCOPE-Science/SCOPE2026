For a two-dimensional positive-definite quadratic, the largest Armijo-acceptable full-gradient step followed by top-one compression decreases the objective universally exactly when \(a(1-\sigma)R(\kappa)\le1\), with the sharp explicit orientation factor \(R\). Without extra scaling, the standard half-Armijo choice has the unexpected exact condition-number boundary \((3+\sqrt5)/2\): beyond it, dropping the smaller gradient coordinate can reverse the line search's descent guarantee even on the first step.
