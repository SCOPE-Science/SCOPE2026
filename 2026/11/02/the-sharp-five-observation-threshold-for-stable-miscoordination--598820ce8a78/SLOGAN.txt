In a two-action coordination game with heterogeneous best-response sampling, observation sizes at most four cannot support stable interior miscoordination when the favored coordination payoff exceeds one and small-sample ties are excluded. Five observations are enough: combining sample sizes two and five produces stable miscoordination exactly when the size-two fraction lies in \((1/2,(343-126\sqrt7)/19)\) for \(1<u<3/2\).
