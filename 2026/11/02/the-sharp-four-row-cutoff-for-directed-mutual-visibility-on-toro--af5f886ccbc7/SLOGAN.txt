For the consistently directed toroidal product \(\overrightarrow C_4\square\overrightarrow C_n\), directed mutual visibility equals \(5\) at \(n=4\), \(7\) at \(n=5\), and \(8\) for every \(n\ge6\). From \(n\ge8\), this is exactly four less than the mutual-visibility number of the underlying undirected torus.
