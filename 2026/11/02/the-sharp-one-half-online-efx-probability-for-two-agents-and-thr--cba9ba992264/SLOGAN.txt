With two agents and three irrevocably arriving indivisible goods, the optimal worst-case probability of an exact EFX allocation is precisely \(1/2\) for every nonnegative additive valuation domain. A two-coin adaptive allocation policy attains the bound, closing the gap between the \(1/3\) constructive guarantee and \(1/2\) impossibility bound previously known in this smallest nontrivial online setting.
