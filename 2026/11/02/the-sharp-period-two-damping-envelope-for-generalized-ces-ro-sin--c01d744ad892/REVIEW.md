# Scientific assessment

## Correctness

The blocked operator is a trace-class perturbation of the nonnegative-frequency compression of a classical order-minus-one matrix-symbol operator on the circle. The Birman–Solomyak theorem gives the fixed-parameter singular-value coefficient with only one cotangent direction contributing. At the endpoint the elliptic term supplies the exact logarithmic subtraction; after rationalization the remaining integrand is uniformly bounded, giving the finite part by dominated convergence. The proof includes zero rows and distinguishes the two successive limits.

## Originality

The fixed-parameter coefficient itself is a consequence of Birman–Solomyak, as presented in Ponge, *Connes’ integration and Weyl’s laws*, Theorem 6.1. The sharp budget extrema and small-parameter expansion are included as direct context from the fibre integral. The assessed contribution is the exact endpoint finite part at general nonnegative period-two row weights. Inspected scalar results do not give the full two-weight finite part, and the related period-two weighted-space result uses a different weighting and does not evaluate this endpoint. Priority beyond the inspected sources is not assumed.

## Mathematical value

The leading logarithmic coefficient only fixes the growth scale. The finite term identifies what remains after that scale is removed and separates mixed profiles from pure-parity profiles, with the same normalization across the whole contrast range. The numerical checks are supplementary and do not establish any asymptotic by themselves.
