Two-periodic row damping of a compact generalized Cesàro operator has an exact weak-trace-class singular-value coefficient given by a two-channel Fourier integral. At any fixed total damping and \(0<t<1\), equal weights uniquely minimize that coefficient while one-parity damping maximizes it, and the sharp anisotropy ratio approaches \(\sqrt2\) at the endpoint; an explicit finite-part integral resolves the next asymptotic term.
