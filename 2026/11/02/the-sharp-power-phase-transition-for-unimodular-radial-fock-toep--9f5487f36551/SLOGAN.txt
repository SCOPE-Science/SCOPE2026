For the unimodular Fock symbol \(e^{i(|z|^2)^\alpha}\), Gaussian radial averaging has a sharp transition at \(\alpha=1/2\): the Toeplitz essential spectrum is a full circle of radius \(1\) below the threshold, a full circle of radius \(e^{-1/8}\) at the threshold, and only \(\{0\}\) above it. Above the threshold the nonzero operator lies in every Schatten class, and the multiplicity-weighted eigenvalues converge to Haar measure on the corresponding circles below or at the threshold.
