# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The Hermite basis is orthonormal in Gaussian measure; every radial operator is diagonal. The normalized Berezin series is exactly an entire second-order differential operator on the diagonal exponential generating series. Its unique null line has Laguerre diagonal, and the DLMF fixed-argument expansion together with explicit square-root-phase equidistribution proves the sharp sorted singular-value constant and ideal classification. The computational checker passed 130 finite assertions; it is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality

PASS, with historical-priority risk. The strongest known comparison, Fulsche–Hagger (arXiv:2308.11292, Theorems 3.5, 3.8–3.9), describes general Berezin noninjectivity via Weyl operators and shows trace-class injectivity. Maximenko–Tellería-Romero (arXiv:1905.00978, Theorem 1.2) supplies radial diagonalization. Accordingly, the one-dimensional radial kernel is not claimed independently new. A related Toeplitz symbol-quantization nullspace result has a planar symbol-integrability threshold at exponent four; this is a different map, and no implication to radial operator Schatten membership or the ordered Laguerre coefficient was found. The precise Berezin operator-ideal threshold and ordered constant are absent from the inspected relevant primary statements. DLMF's fixed-argument Laguerre asymptotic is acknowledged as known. Wider indirect equivalences remain possible.

## Value

PASS. This resolves a mathematically natural, formerly unquantified middle regime between trace-class injectivity and bounded-operator noninjectivity, with an exact spectral-order coefficient and logarithmic critical mean. The object and invariant arise directly from coherent-state measurements and radial symmetry, rather than arbitrary numerical slices.
