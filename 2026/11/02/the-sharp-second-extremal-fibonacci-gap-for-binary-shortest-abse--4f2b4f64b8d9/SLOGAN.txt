Among binary words of length \(2m\), for every \(m\ge5\), the first strict drop below the known maximum \(F_{m+3}\) for the number of shortest absent subsequences is exactly \(F_{m-2}\). Exactly four words achieve the second-highest value, obtained by inserting one equal adjacent two-letter-arch boundary at one of two specific positions in an otherwise alternating sequence of \(01\) and \(10\) arches.
