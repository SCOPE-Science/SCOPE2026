For every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph with uniform edge conductance, the sixth-order Gauss–Legendre/\([3/3]\) Padé diffusion update retains **strictly positive off-diagonal entries at every finite positive timestep**, yet its diagonal entries cross zero together at one uniquely determined finite timestep. Unlike the fourth-order update, sixth-order positivity always has a sharp finite horizon—even on graphs where fourth-order positivity holds indefinitely.
