When only \(M_n=n^{\alpha+o(1)}=o(n)\) preference labels are visible in a uniform parking-function experiment, the optimal critical-scale testing distance is \(2\Phi(\sqrt{\lambda\alpha/\pi})-1\). Revealing labels from the two ends of the preference range attains this information limit, so even a vanishing fraction \(M_n=n/\log n\) retains the entire uncensored Gaussian testing profile.
