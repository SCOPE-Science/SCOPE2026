# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof first compares every alternative committee with the proposed committee; there are only one-candidate replacements because the candidate set has exactly one more member than the committee size. It then reduces all voter coalitions to the three type counts and shows that any coalition containing a competing-block voter has a strictly sufficient payment witness. The remaining groups yield a monotone two-parameter expression, so their largest subgroup gives the exact condition. Finally AM–GM and a monotone one-variable function prove the below-twelve impossibility, while the explicit all-size construction gives a strict violation. The included exact-rational verifier independently recomputes all committee scores and all distinct coalition signatures for the main 41-voter instance, and checks 810 smaller parameter combinations. No extrapolation from finite tests is used.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual priority risk

Skowron, *Robust Proportionality Axioms for Additive Utilities* (arXiv:2610.15516, October 11, 2026), Sections 3 and 5, supplies the axiom and a core-versus-strong separation, but not this PAV-optimality counterexample or exact three-bloc threshold. Its PAV discussion concerns a weaker requirement. Targeted searches on PAV, strong EJR+, payment-based proportionality, and three-bloc counterexamples did not yield a dominating theorem or published exact table. Relevant earlier results on standard EJR+ or PAV do not establish the new stronger-axiom behavior. An unindexed earlier instance, or a general implication hidden in another source, cannot be ruled out without independent literature review.

## Value — PASS

The result answers a substantive rule-versus-axiom compatibility question newly suggested by payment-based strong representation. It separates a uniquely PAV-optimal and core-stable committee from the stronger axiom, avoiding reliance on a tie, and explains the transition in a motivated shared-project/competing-bloc model by a closed-form structural classification. The 12-seat bound is sharp for this natural model only; the finding does not claim a global optimality threshold.
