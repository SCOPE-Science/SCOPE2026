For every genuinely two-sided imaginary-axis interval \(i[-S,R]\) with \(R,S>0\), a first-order complex quadratic integrator is contractive throughout exactly when \(R+S\le2\); on equality the coefficient is uniquely \(c=(2+i(R-S))/(R^2+S^2)\). This sharp mixed-sign frontier quantifies the maximal gain over real-coefficient two-stage methods for unbalanced oscillatory spectra.
