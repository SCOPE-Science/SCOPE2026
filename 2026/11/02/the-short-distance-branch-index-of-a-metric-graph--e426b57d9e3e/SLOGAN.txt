The unlabeled intrinsic-distance law of two independent arclength-uniform points on any finite simple metric graph has an exact short-range quadratic coefficient equal to the subdivision-invariant branch index \(B=\sum_v\binom{\deg(v)}2-|E|\). It determines the precise number of trivalent branch points of any subcubic metric tree, irrespective of its positive edge lengths, and has sharp path/star extrema among trees with fixed edge count.
