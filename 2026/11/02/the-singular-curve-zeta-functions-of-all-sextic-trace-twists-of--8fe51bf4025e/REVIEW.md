# Review of the trace Igusa singular-curve zeta classification

**Correctness (PASS).** The singular-locus proof uses the constrained gradient cubic: its three distinct roots force coordinate multiplicities two by a Vandermonde system; its double-root case gives precisely the fifteen triple intersection points. Incidence is proved by equality constraints on pairs of matchings. A complete permutation enumeration of \(S_6\), with an independent fixed-point test, verifies every orbit spectrum. The normalization–branch point identity proves the zeta factorization, and explicit split-field projective computations test the geometric algebra over two primes. No infinite claim depends on sampling alone.

**Originality (PASS, qualified).** The defining quartic, its fifteen singular lines, their classical \(S_6\)-incidence, and the theory of twists are known; they are not claimed as new. The inspected work of Hulek–Sankaran, Rito, and Bruin–Kulkarni provides those geometric foundations but not the precise eleven-case zeta functions of the reduced singular curve. A related arithmetic classification of sixty Segre-cubic node–plane flags concerns a different finite incidence scheme; its line-orbit information is acknowledged as overlapping geometric input, not the complete normalization correction. Neither web searches nor searched mathematical-record results identified the full claimed formula, but searches do not establish absolute novelty. The Dolgachev–Ortland monograph was bibliographically identified but not examined completely; implicit or unindexed prior coverage remains a risk.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The reduced singular curve is a distinguished boundary invariant of the classical Igusa modular threefold. Its entire zeta function over every sextic trace twist is computed, not just a few small-field counts; the result distinguishes a rational singular point from the existence of a rational irreducible component and identifies a genuinely point-free splitting type. This is a natural complete arithmetic classification of a nonnormal geometrically reducible curve. No broader ambient zeta or nonreduced singular structure is claimed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
