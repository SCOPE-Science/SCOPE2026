For every degree-six finite étale algebra over a finite field of characteristic at least seven, the reduced singular curve of its trace Igusa quartic has a complete eleven-case zeta-function classification determined by the Frobenius cycle partition on six embeddings. In the cycle type \((3,1,1,1)\), this singular curve has three rational points despite having no rational irreducible component, whereas cycle type \((5,1)\) has no rational points.
