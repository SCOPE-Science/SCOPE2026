For graphic-matroid hyperplane (bond-complement) concepts, the house graph is the unique connected simple graph with at most six edges where adaptive shattering exceeds VC shattering: its dimensions are \(\operatorname{VCdim}=2\) and \(\operatorname{Ldim}=3\). More generally, every simple three-path theta graph has a complete all-length dimension classification, with a strict gap exactly when one path is a direct edge and the other two have lengths at least two, not both two.
