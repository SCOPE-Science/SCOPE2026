For \(y^3=x(x-1)(x-a)(x-b)\) in characteristic \(5\), the Jacobian has \(5\)-rank \(0\) exactly on an explicit smooth geometrically irreducible divisor in the ordered two-parameter family, and \(5\)-rank \(2\) elsewhere. Every smooth member has \(a\)-number \(1\); there is no \(5\)-rank-one stratum.
