In characteristic \(3\), the classical Wiman plane sextic becomes a smooth conic tangent to four lines, with six split nodes and four split tacnodes. Its reduced special fiber has \(\operatorname{Pic}^0\cong\mathbb G_m^6\times\mathbb G_a^4\) and exactly \(5q-5\) points over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic \(3\).
