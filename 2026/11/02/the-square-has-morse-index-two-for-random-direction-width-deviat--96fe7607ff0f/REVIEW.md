Cauchy's projection identity and the exact angular absolute-product kernel reduce the random-width functional to a finite edge-pair sum. Expansions at anti-parallel and perpendicular edge pairs are controlled through cubic order, yielding a twice differentiable functional. The exact Hessian and its eigenvalues are independently reproduced by symbolic differentiation of the derived jet; direct numerical angular integration checks the sign and coefficient scale along distinct deformation directions. The result is local and the proof does not infer a global optimum from finite checks.

Independent discovery of an equivalent Hessian calculation remains possible; primary and database searches did not return one. **Value — PASS.** The exact two-positive/two-negative decomposition is structural local information for the specifically identified power-of-two quadrilateral case left outside the known sharp width-deviation classification. It gives a verifiable finite-dimensional obstruction to square local optimality and identifies the two independent symmetry-breaking mechanisms, while retaining strict stability along central-symmetry-preserving directions. This is not a substitute for solving the global minimization problem.
