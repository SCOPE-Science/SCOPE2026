For independent uniform \(r\)-set drawings, the covariance of two missed \(s\)-supercoupons changes sign at most once as their overlap increases, and overlapping targets can have negative covariance. In the proportional-block, square-root-target-size regime the exact phase boundary is \(j\log(1/\rho)=c^2(1-\rho)/\rho\); at \(\rho=1/2\), \(c=1\), a fraction \(2/e\) of all target pairs have negative covariance, including a majority of the pairs that intersect.
