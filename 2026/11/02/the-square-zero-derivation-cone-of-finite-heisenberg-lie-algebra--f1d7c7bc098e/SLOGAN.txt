The reduced square-zero derivation variety of every Heisenberg Lie algebra \(\mathfrak h_{2r+1}\) is geometrically irreducible, rational, and has dimension \(r(r+2)\), over every field. Its complete labelled finite-field rank census follows from isotropic planes, nonsingular symmetric forms, and a central-functional lifting count; in dimension three its projectivization is the cubic scroll \(S(1,2)\).
