Removing one vertex from a crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\) admits an exact strong-domination polynomial for every \(n\ge3\), counting all eligible vertex sets by cardinality. In total there are exactly \(4^{n-1}+1-(n-1)2^{n-2}\) such sets. The minimum rises from two to three exactly when \(n\ge4\), with \(\binom n2+\mathbf1_{n=4}\) optimal residual sets; the complete enumeration reveals the degree-asymmetric structure behind this change.
