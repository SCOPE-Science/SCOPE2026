The elementary-symmetric integral Segre model \(V(e_1,e_3)\) has a characteristic-two fiber that is a cone with one triple vertex and a length-\(16\) Jacobian singular scheme. Its reduced plane-Fano scheme is exactly \(15\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb F_2)\sqcup6\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb F_4)\), distinguishing this bad reduction from the usual ten-node characteristic-two model.
