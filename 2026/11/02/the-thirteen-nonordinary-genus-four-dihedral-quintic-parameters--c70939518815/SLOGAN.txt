In characteristic \(23\), the cyclic-quintic genus-four hyperelliptic pencil has exactly thirteen smooth nonordinary marked parameters, all with Jacobian \(23\)-rank zero and \(a\)-number two, forming seven geometric isomorphism classes. The exceptional classes are the seven roots of an explicit squarefree polynomial in the invariant \(t=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}\); the general-prime construction gives exact \(a\)-number strata from two hypergeometric coefficient polynomials.
