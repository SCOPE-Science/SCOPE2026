# Scientific scope of the extremal classification

The order-thirteen sorted bound and the modular-translation profiles are known from Yihan Wang, *On Periodic Points in Covering Systems*, arXiv:2306.10523v1 (Lemma 1.6 and Example 3.6(1)). The extremal classification does not claim a new case of an open inequality. It asks which cycles saturate the known sharp bound, and which are one below it.

The exact enumeration treats every labeled single cycle on thirteen sites, by its unique orbit sequence rooted at the first site. The interval-graph shortest-return computation and direct convex-hull replay test the same return invariant. At sum 78 the twelve translations exhaust the equality locus. The next sum, 77, occurs only for the two stated witnesses, with their common stated profile.

The inspected prior theorem and examples give neither this exclusion of all other order-thirteen maximizers nor the next-level count. The result gives rigidity data for a natural sharp inequality at a particular order, not a general structure theorem or a claim that the all-order inequality remains open.
