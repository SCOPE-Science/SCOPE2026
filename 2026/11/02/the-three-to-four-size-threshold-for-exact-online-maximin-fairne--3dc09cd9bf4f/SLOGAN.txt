With two agents valuing every arriving good identically at either \(1\) or an integer \(r\ge2\), exact maximin-share fairness and item-by-item nonworsening reassignments can coexist after every arrival precisely for \(r=2,3\). A constant-time rule moves at most one earlier unit good per arrival in those cases, whereas four unit goods followed by one good of size \(r\ge4\) make the requirements incompatible.
