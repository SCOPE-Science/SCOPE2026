A Haar-uniform central plane cuts a right equilateral triangular prism into a triangle, quadrilateral, or pentagon with three explicit inverse-sine probabilities. The pentagon probability is uniquely maximal at height-to-base-edge ratio \(0.6136881767\ldots\), where it equals \(0.3851558057\ldots\); in the thin-prism limit it grows linearly while the triangle probability grows quadratically.
