For every real \(\ell^p\) space with \(1\le p<\infty\) and \(p\ne2\), the space of isometric embeddings from dimension \(n-1\) to \(n\) is a connected finite graph homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{1+2^{n-1}(n-2)n!}S^1\). The result completely determines its topology through the way signed disjoint-support patterns meet when one coefficient vanishes.
