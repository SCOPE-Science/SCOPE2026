The Fermat quartic's line Fano scheme over \(\mathbb Z_3[i]\) is exactly 48 horizontal sections plus 64 reduced points confined to characteristic three: every extra Hermitian line is obstructed already modulo nine. Over \(\mathbb Z_3\), the complete scheme descends to 24 unramified quadratic horizontal points, eight rational vertical points, and 28 quadratic vertical points.
