For the middle-thirds Cantor probability measure, the two largest nonzero integer Fourier-coefficient moduli occur exactly at frequencies \(n=\pm3^a\) and \(n=\pm4\cdot3^a\), respectively. Every other nonzero mode lies strictly below the second value by a uniform explicit gap, giving the first two sharp \(L^2\) convolution multiplier norms under natural frequency exclusions.
