For any separable quintic in characteristic three, the moving-sixth-Weierstrass-point genus-two family has either exactly two simple smooth nonordinary parameters or none: all two Hasse zeros can coalesce only at a singular branch collision. The quadratic Hasse discriminant equals the resultant of the quintic with its third Hasse derivative, and smooth nonordinary fibers always have \(a\)-number one even though their \(3\)-ranks can differ.
