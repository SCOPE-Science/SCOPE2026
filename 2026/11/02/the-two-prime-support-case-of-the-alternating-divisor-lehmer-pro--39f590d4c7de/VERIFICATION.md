The algebraic proof verifies the multiplicative prime-power formula, strict size bounds, the necessary parity of the odd-prime exponent in even two-prime inputs, and the final contradiction modulo eight. The included `verify.py` independently evaluates the Liouville divisor convolution and compares all integers in the range from 2 to 50000, with a further 420 parameter pairs. A successful finite verification does not prove the universal claim. No independent audit has been performed.
