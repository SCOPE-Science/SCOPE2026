For the twelve vertices of a regular truncated tetrahedron, exact-degree spherical-harmonic sampling has ranks \((1,3,5,7,9,11,9)\) through degree six and full rank at every degree from seven onward. Despite permanent interpolation, degree seventeen is uniquely worst-conditioned: its smallest Legendre Gram eigenvalue is exactly \(462143232/4177248169415651\), less than \(10^{-6}\), whereas every other full-rank degree has smallest eigenvalue exceeding \(10^{-4}\).
