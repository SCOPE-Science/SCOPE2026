# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The characteristic restriction is essential: it makes the tangent quadratic criterion equivalent to precisely eighteen reduced two-coordinate Eckardt points. The six-line Stanley--Reisner ideal is the rank-two uniform matroid; an explicit monomial argument establishes its second symbolic power. Varbaro's Cohen--Macaulay theorem, together with regularity of the diagonal cubic and the multiplication exact sequence, proves saturation of the proposed doubled-point ideal. The Hilbert series follows from independently checked monomial counts. Finite-field interpolation over three primes checks the predicted low-degree spaces. No finite sample is used as the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The literature provides the geometric Eckardt configuration and general machinery for matroid symbolic powers, but inspected texts did not state the exact doubled-Eckardt ideal \(I^{(2)}=I^2+(Q)\), its one-generator symbolic defect, or the seventy-two-length Hilbert function. Dardanelli--van Geemen (arXiv:math/0409322) is about the cubic surface Hessian and Eckardt geometry. Varbaro (arXiv:1003.2912) proves the Cohen--Macaulay ingredient. Nagel--Seceleanu (arXiv:1509.04977) concerns a different plane point ideal. Mantero (arXiv:1907.08172) covers full star configurations, not this partial cubic section. The new exact evaluation still has a residual possibility of appearing as an unnamed application of general fat-point formulas; no historical uniqueness is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The eighteen Eckardt points are an extremal and geometrically canonical configuration on smooth diagonal cubic surfaces. Their uniformly doubled ideal exhibits a unique degree-four surface where eighteen general double points impose far too many conditions for any quartic to exist. Computing the full symbolic square, minimal generators, regularity, and Hilbert function gives structural interpolation information rather than a formal restatement of the known eighteen-point count. The result applies to all diagonal coefficients and nonsplit ground fields in the stated characteristics.
