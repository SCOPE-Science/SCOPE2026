For every smooth diagonal cubic surface in characteristic different from \(2\) and \(3\), the eighteen Eckardt points have symbolic-square ideal \(I^{(2)}=I^2+(x_0x_1x_2x_3)\): there is a unique quartic vanishing doubly at all eighteen points. The complete doubled-point Hilbert function stabilizes at length \(72\) in degree \(7\), with exactly one symbolic generator beyond the ordinary square.
