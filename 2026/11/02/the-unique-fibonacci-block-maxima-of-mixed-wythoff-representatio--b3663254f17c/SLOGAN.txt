For every Fibonacci block \([F_m,F_{m+1})\) with \(m\ge4\), the number of positive mixed lower-plus-upper Wythoff representations has exact maximum \(F_{m-1}-1\), attained uniquely at \(F_{m+1}-2\). An elementary carry-and-coprimality argument proves that this extremal integer is never a sum of two upper Wythoff numbers.
