In characteristic seven, the smooth genus-four cyclic trigonal pencil \(y^3=x^5+a x+1\) is ordinary at every parameter except \(a=0\), where the Jacobian has \(7\)-rank zero and Newton slopes \(1/4\) and \(3/4\), each four times. The Hasse determinant is \(4a^2\) times the quintic discriminant, and the exceptional fiber has zeta numerator \(1-77T^4+2401T^8\).
