For a symmetric \(m\)-colour Pólya urn sampled exactly \(m+1\) times, the singleton indicators for two named colours are always negatively correlated when \(m\le4\), but for every \(m\ge5\) their covariance changes sign exactly once as the initial mass \(\alpha\) increases. Strict monotonicity follows from a convexity identity, giving the complete threshold classification across all numbers of colours.
