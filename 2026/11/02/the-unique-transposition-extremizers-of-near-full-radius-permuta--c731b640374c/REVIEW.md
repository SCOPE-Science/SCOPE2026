# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The Hamming-ball complement is exactly the derangement class after normalizing one center. The event-count formula for one or two prescribed off-diagonal permutation entries follows from forbidden-cell inclusion-exclusion; its three possible two-event counts are explicitly checked against direct permutation enumeration. A transposition has exactly two moved-position events, whereas every other nonidentity center difference admits three events. The second Bonferroni inequality bounds the latter, and the strictly positive derangement gap in equations (7)–(8) proves uniqueness for every integer \(n\ge5\). Direct enumeration of all relative conjugacy types through \(n=8\) and a separate restriction-count checker agree. These finite runs are corroboration; the proof supplies the infinite quantifier.

## Originality — PASS, with acknowledged priority risk

The relevant 2024/2025 predecessor already computed prescribed-distance-two intersections and formulated a general transposition optimality conjecture. The January 2026 primary paper proves it for fixed radii \(5,6,7\), develops a character sum for arbitrary parameters, and lists finite checks through radius \(14\). None of these inspected statements establishes strict transposition optimality on the full unbounded diagonal \(r=n-1\). In particular, a maximum over conjugacy classes is not a theorem identifying its unique maximizing class. Searches under Hamming-ball, derangement, relative-cycle and rook-theoretic formulations yielded no equivalent prior result in accessible mathematical records. The earlier 2024/2025 full article was unavailable, leaving a real risk of an implicit identity or a stronger result not visible in its available preview; novelty is not independently certified.

## Value — PASS

This proves a natural infinite boundary case of an explicitly stated permutation-reconstruction conjecture for radii of unbounded size, not a fixed-radius evaluation. The exact closed expression determines the minimum number of distinct noisy copies needed to guarantee reconstruction for this regime, and strict uniqueness identifies precisely the extremizing center types. The proof reduces a factorial state-space problem to sharp, low-order event inequalities rather than exhaustive class character computations.

## Scientific limitations

Only radius \(n-1\) and \(n\ge5\) are covered. The two lower-length exceptions are described accurately; nothing about the full open conjecture at intermediate radii is asserted. The scientific and priority review is not independent.
