For every odd prime \(p\) and polynomial \(f\) of degree \(d\ge2\) with \(p\nmid d-1\), the non-Galois curve \(uv^p-v=f(u)\) has a unique point at infinity with Weierstrass semigroup exactly \(\langle p,p+(d-1),\ldots,p+(p-1)(d-1)\rangle\). Its minimal generator count is \(p\), the genus is \((p-1)d/2\), and the complete gap and pseudo-Frobenius structure is independent of the lower coefficients of \(f\).
