# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For the full coefficient and exponent domain, affine smoothness, the unique totally ramified infinity and the pole valuations are established directly from the additive equation. The tame scalar action diagonalizes the complete affine coordinate ring, and its invariant monomials have *pairwise distinct* pole orders. That exhausts all permissible functions and yields the Apéry set, minimal generators, gaps and every Riemann--Roch space. Exact integer checks for 14 distinct parameter pairs independently corroborate the identities, but do not substitute for the general proof. Risk: conclusions apply only in odd characteristic with the even-support condition and nonzero leading/trailing coefficients.

**Originality — PASS, with historical caveat.** The new assertion is the complete Weierstrass semigroup and all one-point Riemann--Roch bases *on the scalar quotient*, not the covering-curve semigroup or abstract Apéry arithmetic. Bartoli--Borges--Timpanella--Zini, arXiv:2610.35788v1, constructs and studies the quotient's genus, gonality, automorphisms and maximality; its inspected geometric construction and relevant sections do not give these formulas, and full-document text searches found no Weierstrass-semigroup discussion. Zhang--Zhao, arXiv:2605.29311v1, gives general formulas for **Galois** linearized extensions, inapplicable as stated to the automorphism-free degree-\(r\) quotient. Related Kummer and other maximal-curve semigroup literature concerns different covers. Actual published mathematical-record searches found no equivalent claim. This is evidence against immediate coverage, not proof of universal novelty. Risk: an older general theorem on invariant rings or tame scalar quotients might imply the result without stating the same formulas.

**Value — PASS.** On a recently constructed natural class of maximal automorphism-free curves, the theorem determines every one-point gap and pole basis uniformly in the coefficients, not merely its known genus. The unexpected maximal embedding dimension, large pseudo-Frobenius type and almost-symmetric gap structure provide new geometric information for studying rational-point semigroups and one-point algebraic-geometry codes; no new code-distance theorem is asserted. Its uniformity over a whole cover class and complete classification distinguish it from an isolated routine computation.

**Limitations.** Pointwise semigroups at other places, multi-point gaps, code-distance improvements and smaller-base-field descent remain open in this result. Historical priority and independent mathematical checking remain outstanding.
