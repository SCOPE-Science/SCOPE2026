For every even-support additive cover \(Y^r-Y=XR(X)\) in odd characteristic, the scalar quotient has one-point Weierstrass semigroup \(\langle r,r+L,\ldots,r+(r-1)L\rangle\) at its unique infinite point, where \(L=(r^h-2r+1)/(2(r-1))\). The result supplies all pole-order Riemann--Roch bases and proves maximal embedding dimension and type \(r-1\), including for the recently constructed automorphism-free maximal curves.
