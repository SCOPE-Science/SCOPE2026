For every finite tree, the entire VC-shattering polynomial of its geodesically convex halfspaces is determined by the vertex count and the Wiener index: the number of shattered triples is \(\binom n3+\binom n2-W(T)\), and there are no shattered supports of size four or more. A triple is shattered exactly when none of its vertices lies between the other two; among trees of fixed order, paths minimize and stars maximize the triple count.
