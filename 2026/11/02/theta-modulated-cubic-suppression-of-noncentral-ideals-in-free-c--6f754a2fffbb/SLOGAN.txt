For a uniformly chosen ideal of the free class-two Lie algebra on \(d\) generators over a fixed finite field, the probability of not lying in the center is asymptotic to an explicit four-periodic theta factor times \(q^{-d^3/4+d^2-d/2}\). Conditional on this exceptionally rare event, the ideal's projection to the generating space is one-dimensional with probability tending to one.
