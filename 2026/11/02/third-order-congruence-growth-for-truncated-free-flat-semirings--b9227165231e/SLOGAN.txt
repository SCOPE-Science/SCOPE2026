For the truncated free flat semiring on \(n\) letters and words of length at most a fixed \(k\ge3\), the number of congruences is \(2^{n^k}\) times \(1+(n^{k-1}+n)2^{-2n}+[\binom{n^{k-1}}2+2n(n^{k-1}-1)+3n(n-1)]2^{-4n}+O_k(n^{3(k-1)}2^{-6n})\). The third-order coefficient records loops, directed adjacency and reciprocal two-cycles in a de Bruijn graph, while the length-two family admits an exact binomial sum.
