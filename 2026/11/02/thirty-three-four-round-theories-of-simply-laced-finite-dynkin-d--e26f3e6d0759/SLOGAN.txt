In the pure adjacency language, the finite simply laced Dynkin diagrams have exactly 33 first-order theories through quantifier rank four: the \(A\)-series stabilizes at \(A_{16}\), the \(D\)-series at \(D_{17}\), and \(E_6,E_7,E_8\) remain three separate classes. Every diagram has a rank-four equivalent representative on at most 17 vertices, and that bound is sharp within the family.
