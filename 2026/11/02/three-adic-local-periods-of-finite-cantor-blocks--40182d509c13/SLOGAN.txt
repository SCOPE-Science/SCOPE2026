Every cyclic cut of the finite Cantor block \(C_r0^{3^r}\) has local period exactly \(1\), \(2\cdot3^{\nu_3(i)}\), or the full length \(2\cdot3^r\), according to an explicit three-adic transition rule. The law gives the complete multiplicity spectrum and identifies exactly two longest unbordered factors of \(C_{r+1}\).
