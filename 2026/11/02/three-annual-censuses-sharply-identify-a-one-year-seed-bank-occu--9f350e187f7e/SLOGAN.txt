In the one-year seed-bank occupancy model with perfect annual detection, the full population-level law of three consecutive censuses uniquely recovers immigrant colonization, immediate and delayed recruitment, and disturbance probabilities, while any stationary two-census law leaves a two-dimensional parameter ambiguity. An explicit rational inverse and a sharp feasible-transition-table test make the three-census horizon exact, without implying that three finite observations suffice for reliable estimation.
