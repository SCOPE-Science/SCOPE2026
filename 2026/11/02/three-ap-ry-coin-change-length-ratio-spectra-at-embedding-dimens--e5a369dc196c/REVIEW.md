**Correctness — PASS.** Exactly three nonzero composite Apéry minima give a sharp length bound of four. Distinct atoms in a length-four factorization would force four distinct composite minima; thus its summands all coincide, and a length-three representation at that value is impossible. Three explicit integer generator families realize the three options at all multiplicities \(m\ge7\). Shortest-residue computations and every atomic length are replayed for 279 instances by `verify.py`. The proof, not this finite replay, establishes the unbounded statement.

Their examples may already exhibit individual spectra, so the novel content is the complete universal classification and one family of realizations for every \(m\ge7\). Unindexed or implicit coverage remains possible.

It isolates a structural prohibition on simultaneous length ratios and supplies uniform constructions, pertinent to minimal coin-change representations and factorization lengths.

**Limitations.** Multiplicity at least seven; Apéry minima only, not the full semigroup. Formal independent checking has not been performed.
