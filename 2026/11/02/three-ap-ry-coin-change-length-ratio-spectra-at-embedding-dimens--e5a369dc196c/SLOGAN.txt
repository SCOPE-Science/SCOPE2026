For coin-change semigroups with multiplicity \(m\ge7\) and exactly \(m-3\) minimal denominations, the ratios of longest to shortest representations of residue-class minimum amounts form exactly one of \(\{1\}\), \(\{1,3/2\}\), or \(\{1,2\}\). Each possibility occurs for every such \(m\), and no other ratios are possible.
