For the sparse Euler integrand \((1+t+\epsilon t^q)^s t^\nu\), every smooth nonzero coefficient gives full geometric \(S_q\)-monodromy as the exponent ratio varies. Its finite collision parameters form a rational three-branch-value Belyi cover with passport \((q)\), \((q-1,1)\), \((2,1^{q-2})\), uniformly for \(q\ge3\).
