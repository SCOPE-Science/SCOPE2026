In characteristic \(7\), the smooth genus-four family \(y^3=x^5+a x^2+1\) has constant Jacobian \(a\)-number \(2\), but its \(7\)-rank is respectively \(0\), \(1\), or \(2\) at \(a=0\), the five solutions of \(a^5=1\), or all other smooth parameters. The rank-one locus is one explicit fifth-root scaling orbit.
