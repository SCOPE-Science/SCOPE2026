In finite three-edge-colorable cubic graphs, a spin-1 antiferromagnet on the line graph has exact vacancy-ordering boundaries at \(D=0\) and \(D=J\): its intermediate ground states are precisely proper three-edge colorings, and its upper-boundary states are alternating even-cycle packings. For the cuboctahedron the five corresponding ground-state multiplicities are exactly \(450,3054,24,69,1\), with a sharp, occupancy-dependent magnetic response.
