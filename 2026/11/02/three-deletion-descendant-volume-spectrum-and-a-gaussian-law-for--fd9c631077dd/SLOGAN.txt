For uniformly chosen size-\(n\) multisets on \(q\) symbols, exactly-\(t\)-deletion descendant counts admit exact first and second moments, and exactly three deletions have an explicit complete volume histogram. At fixed \(t\) with \(n/q\to\lambda>0\), their centered fluctuations are Gaussian on the scale \(q^{t-1/2}\), with an explicit positive variance constant.
