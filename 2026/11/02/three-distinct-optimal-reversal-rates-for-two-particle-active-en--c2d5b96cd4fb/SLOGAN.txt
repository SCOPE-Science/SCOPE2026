For two independent equal-speed run-and-tumble particles meeting on a ring, the rates minimizing mean first-encounter time, its variance, and its squared coefficient of variation are universally different: \(\sqrt3<1.920764515\ldots<2.406782794\ldots\) in units of speed divided by circumference. An exact second-moment formula proves this risk-versus-speed tradeoff and an intermediate regime of squared coefficient of variation below one.
