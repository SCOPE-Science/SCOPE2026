For a binary tree with Bernoulli-\(p\) marks and dyadically discounted path scores, the probability of coming within \(\varepsilon\) of the maximum has three sharp phases: power-law decay below \(p=1/2\), the exact critical rate \(2\log2/\log(1/\varepsilon)\), and convergence to a positive endpoint atom above \(p=1/2\) with a second sharp power-law correction. The noncritical laws possess finite positive dyadic-scale amplitudes determined by one exact quadratic recursion.
