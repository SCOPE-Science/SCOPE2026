For a habitat patch with environment-dependent colonization and extinction, accelerating a two-state Markov environment can only change stationary occupancy monotonically (or not at all). Three environmental regimes are the minimum for a strict interior optimum: an exact symmetric three-regime example has identical slow- and fast-switching occupancy \(5/8\), but reaches \((27-\sqrt7)/38\) at the unique positive speed \(2\sqrt7/3\).
