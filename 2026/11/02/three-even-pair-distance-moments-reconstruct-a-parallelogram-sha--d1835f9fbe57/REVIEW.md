# Scientific review of three-moment parallelogram reconstruction

## Correctness — PASS
The statement is quantified over every compact nondegenerate planar parallelogram, using independent normalized area-uniform endpoints and ordinary Euclidean distance. Its reduction to two independent triangular coordinate differences is exact. The three even pair-distance moments in equation (1) follow from the second, fourth, and sixth triangular moments. The determinant of the two-by-two inversion matrix is \(-41/75600\), so the recovered Gram invariants are unique. The exact inequalities \(s>0\), \(0<p\le s^2/4\), and \(0\le q<4p\) are both necessary and sufficient for a positive-definite Gram realization. Generator exchange and reversal account for all congruence ambiguities, including rectangles and rhombi. The explicit noncongruent pair with the same first two moments proves optimality. The checker independently integrates polynomials in four original uniform variables and passed 62 rational Gram cases plus the counterexample checks. No finite test is used as a proof for all parallelograms.

## Originality — PASS (self-review with residual priority risk)
Sheng (1985) already supplies elementary-function formulas for all integer-order *forward* random-pair-distance moments of arbitrary parallelograms, directly covering the moment calculations as forward facts. The claimed remaining gap is the exact minimal three-moment *inverse* characterization and feasibility region; that is an additional invertibility/minimality assertion not present in the available abstract, but his inaccessible full text creates substantial priority risk. An exact PDF for two uniform points in a special rhombus appears in Tong–Ahmadi–Pan (2013), and square moments appear in Mathai–Moschopoulos–Pederzoli (1999); neither accessible statement is an all-parallelogram inverse theorem. Gates (1982) recognizes quadrilaterals from a *complete chord-length density derivative*, not from three even moments of an interior-pair-distance law. The full Gates text was inaccessible and remains a named risk. Pure–Durrani–Tong–Pan (2019) provide a forward PDF construction for arbitrary polygons, which can generate moments but does not establish the sharp finite-moment inverse cutoff. The most relevant accessible published research records concern reconstruction from three *direction-unlabeled widths* and fourth-power *boundary/interior chord amplification*, distinct from the present three interior-pair-distance moments. Their RESULT.md statements and proofs were read. The available own claim titles and recoverable claim fields showed no pair-distance parallelogram inversion. These comparisons are about implication, not matching words; unsuccessful search is not a proof of global historical novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This solves a natural shape-identification problem using a very small list of accessible integral-geometric statistics rather than a full probability density. It supplies (i) exact reconstruction for **every** parallelogram, including symmetric special cases, (ii) a complete algebraic description of feasible moment triples, and (iii) an explicit and locally continuous two-moment ambiguity showing the cutoff of three is sharp. These are nontrivial inverse results for an elementary but geometrically meaningful family, independent of a hypothetical downstream theorem. The result does not claim finite-sample stability, general-quadrilateral rigidity, or a new formula for complete distance distributions.
