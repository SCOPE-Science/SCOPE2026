For every \(r\ge2\), the image of the first-kind Dickson polynomial \(D_{2^r}(X,1)\) over the 2-adic integers is exactly three disjoint, explicitly centered residue balls with radii \(2^{-(r+2)}\), \(2^{-(2r+3)}\), and \(2^{-(2r+5)}\). Their reductions give the exact image cardinality modulo every power of two and shrink toward precisely the two fixed values \(-1\) and \(2\).
