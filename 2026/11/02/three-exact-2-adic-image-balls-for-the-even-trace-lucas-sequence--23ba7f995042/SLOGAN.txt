For the Lucas recurrence \(U_{n+2}=P U_{n+1}-U_n\) with nonzero \(4\mid P\), the 2-adic image closure is exactly three disjoint balls: \(2^h\mathbb Z_2\) and \(\pm1+2^{2h}\mathbb Z_2\), where \(h=\nu_2(P)\). Exact index isometries give every attained residue class modulo \(2^N\) and the limiting density \(2^{-h}+2^{1-2h}\).
