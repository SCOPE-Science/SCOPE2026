For a complete graph with one attached leaf and three independently priced edge classes, the unique global fastest continuous-time mixing allocation has an exact three-phase formula: all clique edges active, only the hub-centered star active, or a pendant-price-dominated star. The switching boundaries are \(R=k^2P\) and \(Q=2(k^2P-R)/(k(k-1))\), and the theorem proves uniqueness even among completely asymmetric edge allocations.
