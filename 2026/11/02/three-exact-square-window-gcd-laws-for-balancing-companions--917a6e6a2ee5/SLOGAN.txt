For the balancing, Lucas-balancing, and Lucas-cobalancing sequences, the GCDs of every length-\(k\) window of squared terms are all determined by \(h(k)=\gcd(k,B_k)\): the balancing case has a factor \(1/2\) for even \(k\), and the Lucas-cobalancing case gains a factor \(3\) exactly when \(k\equiv3\pmod6\). This proves two previously experimental relations and reduces three square-window problems to one finite index GCD.
