For every prime characteristic \(p\ge13\), the third-canonical ramification divisor of the Klein quartic has exactly \(276\) distinct points except at \(p=79,139\), where it has \(164\), and at \(p=277\), where it has \(108\). An exact integral Wronskian normalization shows that the free \(168\)-orbit specializes onto the tame \(56\)- or \(84\)-point branch orbit precisely at these three primes.
