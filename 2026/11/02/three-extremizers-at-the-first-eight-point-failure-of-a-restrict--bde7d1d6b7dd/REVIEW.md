# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The normalized finite domain is exactly enumerated: six strictly increasing interior points between the endpoints. The direct combination support and a separate polynomial-coefficient bitset support are checked for every primitive instance for every diameter from seven through sixteen. There are exactly 4998 primitive instances when the diameter is sixteen; all three asserted extremizers are verified and all other instances excluded. Both the first-failure assertion and the equality classification follow from exhaustive finite proof, with no appeal to heuristic extrapolation.

## Originality
PASS, with priority risk. Wang's arXiv:2610.25073v1 provides the eight-point witness {0,1,2,3,13,14,15,16} as a counterexample to the nine-element hypothesis, but does not state the exact three-extremizer classification. Manna--Mohan--Pandey arXiv:2505.07415 and Mohan--Pandey's 2024 work address different cardinality or dimension hypotheses (fourfold characterizations requiring at least twelve elements). Searches for parameter aliases, exact sets, stronger coverage and sequence tables did not find an equivalent census. Unindexed or implicit prior results remain possible.

## Value
PASS. The exceptional diameter sixteen is the *first* possible failure of the tempting eight-point extension of a sharp new diameter-dependent restricted-sumset bound. Classifying all three extremizers rather than one example elucidates its true sharpness boundary and different optimal structural configurations. This is an exact natural finite inverse problem, not an arbitrary unmotivated numerical sample.

## Literature and limitations
Original witness and general theorem: Wang, arXiv:2610.25073v1 (2026-10-18). Related inverse theorems: Manna--Mohan--Pandey, arXiv:2505.07415, and Mohan--Pandey, doi:10.4134/BKMS.b230631. The result covers only eight-element integer sets of diameter at most sixteen and sums of four distinct elements. No claim is made that the first witness is novel, or that previous literature could not have implicitly supplied the full result. No independent audit has been undertaken.
