For a geometric infinite alphabet, uniform empirical averages over the unit ball of \(L^q\) exhibit three sharp regimes: a Gaussian-sequence limit for \(q>2\), an exact \(\sqrt{\log n/n}\) law at \(q=2\), and log-lattice Poisson subsequential limits for \(1<q<2\). At and below the critical exponent, the corresponding discrepancy powers have infinite expectations even for finite samples.
