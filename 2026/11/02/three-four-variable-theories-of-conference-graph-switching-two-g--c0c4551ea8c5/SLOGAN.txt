For every finite conference graph, its odd-edge-parity ternary switching reduct has one unrestricted three-variable theory, while unrestricted four-variable logic has exactly three theories according to whether the graph has \(5\), \(9\), or at least \(13\) vertices. The sharp universal number of deletions preserving the large-order four-variable theory is \((v-13)/4\), certified by an exact fourth-point extension formula.
