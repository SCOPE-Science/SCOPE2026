For a generic complex Ciani–Kuribayashi quartic, all 300 third-order Weierstrass points are simple: 36 sextactic points lie on involution axes, and the remaining 264 dectactic points form eleven free orbits of the natural \(S_4\)-action. Exact modular Wronskian certificates establish the required generic transversality and exclude overlap with second-order Weierstrass points.
