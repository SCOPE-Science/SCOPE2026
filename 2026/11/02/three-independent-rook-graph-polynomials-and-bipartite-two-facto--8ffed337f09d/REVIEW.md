# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The edge-to-vertex identification \(K_m\square K_n\cong L(K_{m,n})\) gives the exact local degree condition \(d_H(u)+d_H(v)\le4\). The proof derives a necessary-and-sufficient four-type component classification: isolated vertices, alternating paths, even cycles of length at least four, and three-stars. The bipartite labelled exponential formula enumerates them without overlap. Equality in the edge-versus-vertex inequality forces spanning cycle covers, yielding the square optimum and exact count; a direct neighborhood argument proves the total-domination complement threshold. Independently executed graph-subset enumeration, component recursion and exact-rational series expansion agree with the infinite formulas. The computational checks are corroborative, not the proof of the infinite statements.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Mao et al. (DOI 10.26493/2590-9770.1216.6d2) give general two-independence bounds for Cartesian products, without the present complete rook polynomials or exact square multiplicities. Cabrera-Martínez et al. (DOI 10.3390/math8020194) motivate the total outer parameter, without a covering rook-product formula. The full 2022 Mortosa thesis reprises bounds and treats path-grid questions. Existing enumeration of bipartite linear forests counts path components only; it does not entail the new cycle and star contributions or the two-factor classification. Broad bibliographic, equivalence and research-record searches did not reveal an exact prior result. The 2024 paper DOI 10.1051/ro/2024098 was only inspected at abstract level and could contain unexamined relevant material. Related unindexed degree-constrained bipartite enumeration is a genuine residual priority risk; the review is not independent certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This completes a size distribution for a natural two-parameter graph family whose maximum three-independence number previously had coarser general bounds. The sharp square parameter, its exact extremal multiplicity, the two-factor structural bijection, and the complete minimum total outer three-independent domination enumeration are consequences that address motivated finite-graph questions rather than arbitrary parameter substitutions. It differentiates three-independence from the previously enumerated induced-forest case by allowing cycle and star witnesses.

## Limits

The polynomial counts maximum-induced-degree-two sets only; smaller-board total outer equivalence is unproved. The 2024 literature source was not fully accessible, and publication priority remains subject to external comparison.
