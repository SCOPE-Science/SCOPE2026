# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The arbitrary-field proof explicitly lists all 4-by-8-by-4 cochain maps, computes their ranks by coefficients, and checks exact cup representatives. All derivations are valid in characteristic two because the derivatives of the defining squares vanish. The rank trichotomy and explicit representative products settle every cohomology group and the full graded ring. The direct checker exhausts all brackets in three small finite fields and tests products, but does not replace the general derivation.

Existing classification of the underlying Poisson brackets and existing calculations of their centres are explicitly treated as prior ingredients rather than findings here: neither includes the positive-degree cohomology maps or cup products. Searches for modular Poisson cohomology, equivalent normalizations, and complete cohomology-ring tables found no decisive covering theorem. A general reference not located in the literature remains a residual priority risk.

## Value — PASS
Poisson cohomology governs derivations modulo Hamiltonian derivations and infinitesimal Poisson deformations; the cup ring is a finer invariant than the Poisson centre or the bracket's isomorphism type alone. The classification is complete for every bracket on the first nontrivial nonreduced two-variable plane, uniform in all characteristic-two fields. It reveals an eight-dimensional modular cohomology ring for the log-canonical bracket, contrasting with the five-dimensional complex case, and an exact jump to an exterior ring in the generic stratum.
