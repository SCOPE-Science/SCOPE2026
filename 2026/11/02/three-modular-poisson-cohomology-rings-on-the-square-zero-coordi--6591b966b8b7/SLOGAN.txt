The complete alternating-multiderivation Poisson-cohomology ring of \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\) in characteristic \(2\) takes exactly three forms, with Betti triples \((4,8,4)\), \((2,4,2)\), or \((1,2,1)\), as the bracket moves through the zero, punctured-socle, and complementary strata. In the punctured-socle case the four-dimensional first cohomology has a nondegenerate cup pairing into a one-dimensional subspace of degree-two cohomology; outside the socle line the entire ring is an exterior algebra on two degree-one classes.
