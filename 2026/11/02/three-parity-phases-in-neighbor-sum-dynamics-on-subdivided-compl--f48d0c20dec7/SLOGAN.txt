Synchronous neighbor-sum dynamics over fields of characteristic two on the one-subdivision of a complete bipartite graph have exactly three arithmetic Jordan regimes: extinction in four steps for even-even shores, only periods one and two for odd-odd shores, and genuine period-four orbits for mixed parity. The full Jordan blocks give every transient-entry and primitive-orbit count, and in mixed parity the subdivision creates recurrent states even though the unsubdivided update is nilpotent.
