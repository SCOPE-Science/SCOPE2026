The real Lipschitz-free space of a four-cycle with edge lengths \(1,1,1,r\) has an exact three-phase numerical index for \(1\le r\le3\), with transitions at \(5/3\) and \(1+\sqrt2\). Its unique minimum is \(5/9\) at \(r=5/3\), attained by an explicit rank-one operator; the index returns to one at both endpoint geometries.
