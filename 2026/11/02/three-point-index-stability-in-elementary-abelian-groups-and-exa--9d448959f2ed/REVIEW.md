# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The defining two conditions for maximal disjoint-translate sets, \((B-B)\cap(A-A)=\{0\}\) and \(B+(A-A)=G\), are necessary and sufficient in finite abelian groups. The binary and ternary affine-coset arguments classify every possible subfactor without sampling. Negative cases are established by fully specified rank-two maximal witnesses, a symbolic direct-product lift, and cyclic-gap constructions covering every prime at least thirteen. The only small-prime step requiring finite computation is exhaustively checked for every three-subset and every possible packing subset of the cyclic groups of orders five, seven, and eleven. The checker directly recomputes the maximality conditions and ends with `VERIFY_OK`. Scope boundaries include the vacuous size-two group.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The motivating 2026 work explicitly asks for three-subset extensions; its stated solution is for two-point cyclic supports. The comprehensive 2023 treatment computes and publishes certain nonstable examples and the classification of groups stable for *all* subsets, neither of which entails three-index stability of elementary abelian groups in every rank. Its recorded \(C_7^2\) three-point counterexample and \(C_5^2\) known instability are openly credited. Actual searches of published research records for three-index stability, elementary abelian groups, affine-space subfactors, and exact binary–ternary packing counts did not identify the full theorem or a stronger result implying it. This is a qualified self-review, not an independent priority certification. Residual risks include implicit equivalences with older well-covered Cayley-graph theorems and unreviewed publisher-only full text.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The full classification answers a natural fixed-support-size group-theoretic question over an infinite class of groups, sharply separating prime characteristics and low-dimensional exceptions. The positive cases include an exact structural list of every maximal packing and their numbers, far beyond the existence of a single example. The negative cases provide explicit witnesses uniform in rank. This yields an infinite-dimensional stabilization phenomenon in ternary vector spaces even though some of those groups fail stability at larger support sizes.
