For a balanced double star with independently budgeted symmetric transition rates, the unique minimizer of the worst **oriented mean first-hitting time** has exactly three price phases: equal spoke rates, one-direction crossing dominance, and a tie between within-class and crossing hitting times. The first phase boundary is the sharp bridge-price-independent ratio \(H/L=2m+1\), and the second is \(H/L=(2m+1)(1+\sqrt{R(m+1)/(mL)})^2\); all optimal rates and values are explicit.
