For a rational weighted Mersenne-cycle evolution algebra, all odd-prime reductions preserve perfection, but the rational automorphism group and nonzero idempotents jump together through exactly three finite-field strata. Their automorphism–idempotent pairs have explicit densities \((\ell-2)/(\ell-1)\), \(1/(\ell(\ell-1))\), and \(1/\ell\), where \(\ell=2^n-1\) is prime and \(n\ge3\).
