Finite symmetric \(2\)-\((v,k,2)\) biplanes with \(k\ge3\) have exactly three first-order rank-four theories, distinguished by \(k=3\), \(k=4\), and \(k\ge5\). Every such biplane has a sharp rank-four representative with at most \(11\) points and \(11\) blocks, regardless of its isomorphism type or automorphism group.
