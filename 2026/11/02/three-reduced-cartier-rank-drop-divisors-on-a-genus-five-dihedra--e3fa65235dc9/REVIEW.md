# Review of the Legendre--Cartier stratification

## Correctness — PASS

The genus and excluded parameters follow from a squarefreeness calculation. The coefficient extraction in the canonical holomorphic basis reduces the Cartier matrix to three scalar orbit types. The generating-series identity gives the exact Legendre factors for all primes in scope, and the orbit argument proves stable Cartier rank rather than merely one-step matrix rank. The Legendre differential equation proves each root simple away from the excluded boundary. The standalone verifier separately reconstructs the finite coefficient matrices, verifies complete prime-field parameter sweeps for 44 primes below 200, and computes exact small-prime gcds. No finite test is used to prove the infinite statements.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The exact motivating family, elliptic Jacobian decomposition, and superspeciality criterion are in Ohashi--Kudo, arXiv:2603.21569v1, Section 4.3. Their paper does not state the normalized all-parameter geometric \(p\)-rank formula, the three *reduced* Legendre root divisors with individual degrees and multiplicities, or the explicit rank-two intersection at characteristic 19. These are the only asserted refinements. Its elliptic isogeny and standard Hasse theory give a method to derive related results, and an as-yet-unexamined generalized Legendre-form manuscript could cover them; this is the principal historical-priority risk. Absence from searches is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The natural \(p\)-rank stratification answers a geometrically motivated question about all ordinary and nonordinary members, not merely the superspecial ones targeted by the source. It identifies the reduced parameter divisors, their exact sizes for every characteristic, determinant multiplicities, and how coincident zeros combine to lower rank. These are intrinsic invariants of a widely symmetric genus-five family, rather than arbitrary parameter slices.
