For finitely many attractive impurities receding proportionally from the boundary of a fractional Dirichlet lattice half-line, the sharp positivity threshold has three phases: independent sites below \(\alpha=1/2\), logarithmic decoupling at \(\alpha=1/2\), and an explicit interacting fractional Green matrix above \(\alpha=1/2\). Throughout \(1/2<\alpha<3/2\), two individually nonbinding impurities can jointly produce a negative eigenvalue, with an exact leading-order criterion.
