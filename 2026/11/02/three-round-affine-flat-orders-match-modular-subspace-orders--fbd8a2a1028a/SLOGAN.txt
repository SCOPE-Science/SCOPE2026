The pure inclusion poset of affine flats in every finite dimension \(d\ge2\) has the same three-round first-order theory as the modular lattice of linear subspaces in dimension \(d+1\), over arbitrary and unrelated fields. Exactly six theories occur among all positive-dimensional affine spaces, and every dimension \(d\ge5\) belongs to one stable class whose sharp smallest finite representative contains \(2452\) flats.
