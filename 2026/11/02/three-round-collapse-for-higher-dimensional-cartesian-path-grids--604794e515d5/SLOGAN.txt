Among Cartesian products of at least three nontrivial finite paths, all graphs except the three-cube \(Q_3\) have exactly the same rank-three first-order adjacency theory as the odd seven-cycle \(C_7\), regardless of dimension or side lengths. The exceptional cube is distinguished by a three-quantifier dominating-pair sentence; the nonexceptional class first appears at twelve vertices within this path-product family.
