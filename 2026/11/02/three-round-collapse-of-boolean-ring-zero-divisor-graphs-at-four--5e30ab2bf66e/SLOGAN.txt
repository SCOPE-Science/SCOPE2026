In the pure adjacency language, the zero-divisor graphs of the Boolean rings \(\mathbb F_2^n\), \(n\ge2\), have exactly three first-order quantifier-rank-three theories: \(n=2\), \(n=3\), and all \(n\ge4\). The sharp largest smallest representative has only \(14\) vertices, even though the full graphs distinguish every factor count.
