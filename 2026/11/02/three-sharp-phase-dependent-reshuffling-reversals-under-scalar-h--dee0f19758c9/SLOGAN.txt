For two-sample quadratic heavy-ball SGD, reshuffling can yield *lower* stationary error at epoch boundaries but *higher* error after the first update of each epoch, with exact rational witness \(V_{\rm E}=1/3<V_{\rm I}=3/2<V_{\rm O}=5/3\). Positive momentum creates two additional sharp within-epoch reversal thresholds \(1+\beta\pm\sqrt{\beta(1-\beta^2)}\) not detectable from a time-averaged error alone.
