Three independent square complex-Gaussian randomized Ritz compressions have infinite individual squared-error risk when the subspace residual is nonzero, but taking entrywise real-and-imaginary medians produces the exact finite risk \(m\|(I-WW^*)AW\|_F^2\). Remarkably, that equals the risk of one ordinary least-squares Ritz sketch using exactly one extra Gaussian test row.
