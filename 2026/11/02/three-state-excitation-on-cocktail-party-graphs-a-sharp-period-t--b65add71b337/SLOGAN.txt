Deleting a perfect matching from a complete graph creates a sharp Greenberg–Hastings oscillation transition: the four-vertex graph has exactly two primitive four-cycles, but every graph with at least six vertices has only three-cycles, numbering \(3^{2m-1}-(2m+4)2^{2m-2}+2m+1\). Almost every three-color configuration on the larger graphs is periodic already at time zero.
