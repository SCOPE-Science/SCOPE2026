For the linearized two-stage SEIR epidemic with exactly three high- and three low-contact blocks per period, the six-block invasion multiplier has exactly three strictly ordered calendar levels: \(\rho_\mathrm{C}<\rho_\mathrm{M}<\rho_\mathrm{A}\) (clustered, intermediate, alternating). The ordering is universal for all positive stage rates and fixed block durations, and it yields three distinct epidemic-invasion contact thresholds when the low-contact regime is subcritical.
