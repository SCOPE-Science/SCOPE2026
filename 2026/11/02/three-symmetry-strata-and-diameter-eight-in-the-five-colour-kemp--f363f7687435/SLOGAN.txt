For fully labelled optimal five-edge-colourings of \(K_{3,5}\), whole-bichromatic-component Kempe swaps form a connected graph on \(66{,}240\) states with radius \(7\) and diameter \(8\). The natural row–column–colour symmetry splits the states into precisely three orbits, of sizes \(8{,}640\), \(14{,}400\), and \(43{,}200\), with degrees \(15\), \(16\), and \(17\) respectively.
