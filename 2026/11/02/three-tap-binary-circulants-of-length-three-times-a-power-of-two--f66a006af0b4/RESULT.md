# Three-tap binary circulants of length three times a power of two: exact rank census and kernel support hierarchy

## Finding
Fix an integer \(k\ge0\), put \(m=2^k\) and \(n=3m\), and choose an unordered set \(S=\{a,b,c\}\subset\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\) of three distinct positions. Let \(T_S\) be cyclic convolution by the binary polynomial \(f_S(x)=x^a+x^b+x^c\) on \(R=\mathbb F_2[x]/(x^n-1)\). These \(\binom n3\) operators are exactly the order-\(n\) binary circulant matrices with three ones in a fixed column, and equivalently, up to index inversion, with three ones in a fixed row.

**Rank census.** Exactly \(\binom{3m}{3}-m^3\) of these matrices are invertible. For each \(0\le s<k\), precisely
\[
N_s=\frac{3m^3}{4^{s+1}}
\]
have nullity \(2^{s+1}\), and precisely \(N_k=m\) have nullity \(2^{k+1}=2m\). There are no other nullities.

**Kernel identities and coding invariants.** Define \(d=2^s\), \(r=m/d\). The kernel for every matrix in the \(s\)-th singular stratum is the *same actual cyclic subcode*
\[
K_s=\left\langle\frac{x^{3m}-1}{1+x^d+x^{2d}}\right\rangle\subset R.
\]
The nonzero kernels form a strictly nested chain \(K_0\subset K_1\subset\cdots\subset K_k\). As a binary linear code of length \(3m\), \(K_s\) is permutation-equivalent to \(r\)-fold repeated blocks from the direct sum of \(d\) copies of the binary even-weight length-three code \([3,2,2]_2\). In particular, it has dimension \(2d\), minimum distance \(2r\), complete Hamming weight enumerator
\[
W_{K_s}(z)=(1+3z^{2r})^d,
\]
and generalized Hamming weights, for every integer \(1\le j\le2d\),
\[
d_j(K_s)=r\left(3\left\lfloor\frac j2\right\rfloor+2(j\bmod2)\right).
\]
For nonsingular matrices the kernel is zero, with weight enumerator \(1\). All counts refer to labelled support sets, without quotienting by rotation or reversal.

## Assumptions and scope
All fields are exactly \(\mathbb F_2\). All matrices are cyclic convolution operators of size \(n=3\cdot2^k\) with exactly three nonzero (unit) coefficients at distinct positions. The convention of first **column** support specifies multiplication by \(f_S\). Mapping to first-row support replaces the three exponents by their negatives modulo \(n\), a bijection on triples that preserves the congruence strata. Since \(g(x)=1+x+x^2\) and all its powers are self-reciprocal, it also preserves the actual kernel ideal \(K_s\) and the stated code invariants. The parameter \(k=0\), where every such matrix has nullity two, is included. For a linear code \(U\subseteq\mathbb F_2^n\), the generalized Hamming weight \(d_j(U)\) is the minimum size of the union of supports over its \(j\)-dimensional linear subcodes.

## Proof
### 1. Polynomial rank and all possible singularities
Multiplication by \(f_S\) in \(R\) has nullity \(\deg\gcd(f_S,x^n-1)\), because the kernel is the ideal generated by \((x^n-1)/\gcd(f_S,x^n-1)\). In characteristic two,
\[
x^{3m}-1=(x^3+1)^m=(x+1)^m(x^2+x+1)^m.
\]
The polynomial \(f_S\) has odd coefficient weight, so \(f_S(1)=1\) and the factor \(x+1\) never divides it. Let \(g(x)=x^2+x+1\), and fix a root \(\omega\) of \(g\) in \(\mathbb F_4\). Writing \(e_i\in\mathbb F_2\) for the parity of how many elements of \(S\) are congruent to \(i\pmod3\), the identity \(1+\omega+\omega^2=0\) implies \(f_S(\omega)=0\) exactly when \(e_0=e_1=e_2\). Because \(|S|=3\), the only possibility is one element of \(S\) in each residue class modulo three. Hence a matrix is singular if and only if this distinct-residue condition holds.

For a singular \(S=\{a,b,c\}\) with \(a<b<c\), set
\[
s=\max\{t\in\{0,\ldots,k\}:a\equiv b\equiv c\pmod{2^t}\},\quad d=2^s.
\]
Then \(f_S(x)=x^a\big(1+x^{b-a}+x^{c-a}\big)\) is a \(d\)-th power of a polynomial \(h(x)\) after removing the invertible monomial factor, by Frobenius. Its two nonconstant exponent differences after dividing by \(d\) are not both even (also when \(s=k\), since \(a,b,c\) occupy distinct residues modulo three and are distinct modulo \(3m\)). They are distinct modulo three. The derivative of \(h\) is nonzero at each root of \(g\): if exactly one exponent difference is odd the derivative consists of a single nonzero term; if both are odd, the two terms evaluate to distinct powers of \(\omega\) and cannot cancel. Thus each root of \(g\) has multiplicity exactly \(d\) in \(f_S\). Since \(d\le m\),
\[
\gcd(f_S,x^n-1)=g(x)^d=1+x^d+x^{2d}.
\]
This proves the possible nullities, ranks, and kernel ideal identities. The ideals are strictly nested because their dimensions are \(2^{s+1}\) and the divisibility of their generators is monotone as \(s\) increases.

### 2. Counts in each singularity stratum
The number of supports with one element from each residue class modulo three is exactly \(m^3\). Among these, let \(B_t\) count those whose positions all agree modulo \(2^t\), for \(0\le t\le k\). By the Chinese remainder theorem, each of the \(2^t\) residue classes modulo \(2^t\) contains exactly \(m/2^t\) choices from **each** residue class modulo three. Therefore
\[
B_t=2^t(m/2^t)^3=m^3/4^t.
\]
For \(s<k\), the number whose *maximal* shared-power exponent is \(s\) equals \(B_s-B_{s+1}=3m^3/4^{s+1}\). At \(s=k\), it equals \(B_k=m\). The nonsingular count is the total minus \(B_0=m^3\). These classes are disjoint and exhaustive.

### 3. Explicit code structure
Put \(d=2^s\) and \(r=m/d\). Polynomial division in \(\mathbb F_2[x]\) gives
\[
\frac{x^{3m}-1}{1+x^d+x^{2d}}=(1+x^d)\sum_{t=0}^{r-1}x^{3dt}.
\]
The kernel ideal has a unique expression \(u(x)Q(x)\) with \(\deg u<2d\), where \(Q\) is this quotient; such products already have degree less than \(3m\). The length-\(3d\) seed block \(u(x)(1+x^d)\) has, for each \(i\in\{0,\ldots,d-1\}\), three coordinates
\[
(u_i,\ u_i+u_{i+d},\ u_{i+d}),
\]
whose binary sum is zero; every even-parity triple occurs. The displayed quotient repeats this entire block \(r\) times. Thus \(K_s\) consists of \(d\) independent even-parity triples, with every coordinate of the triple repeated \(r\) times. Each triple gives either its zero word or one of three words of weight \(2r\), proving \(W_{K_s}(z)=(1+3z^{2r})^d\) and minimum distance \(2r\).

### 4. Generalized Hamming-weight hierarchy
For a \(j\)-dimensional subcode \(U\subset K_s\), project it onto each of the \(d\) independent even-parity triples, and let the projection dimensions be \(a_i\in\{0,1,2\}\). The map into the direct product of these projections is injective, hence \(j\le\sum_i a_i\). Their union-support sizes are at least \(0,2r,3r\), respectively. The least possible total with \(\sum_i a_i\ge j\) is obtained by taking \(\lfloor j/2\rfloor\) projections of dimension two and, if \(j\) is odd, one projection of dimension one. Its support is \(r[3\lfloor j/2\rfloor+2(j\bmod2)]\). Selecting the direct sum of these component subspaces attains the bound, proving every generalized weight.

## Verification
The standalone dependency-free `verify.py` checks all support triples for \(k=0,1,2,3,4\) by exact binary polynomial gcd, testing predicted nullities and all stratum counts, **19,561 circulant matrices in total**. It separately generates every vector of each kernel for all \(k\le3\), checks annihilation by the relevant divisor, and compares the entire computed weight distribution with the formula: **66,112 codeword cases** in total. The complete proof above is not an extrapolation from those finite checks.

To reproduce: run `python3 verify.py` with a standard Python 3 interpreter. The final line must read `VERIFY_OK three_tap_matrices 19561 kernel_words 66112`.

## Relationship to prior work
The polynomial-gcd rank criterion for circulant matrices is standard; Choi and Hur (2026) review that criterion alongside new finite-field invertibility criteria. Wei and Zou enumerate finite-field circulants by rank without imposing an exact weight-three support constraint. The older work of Charpin, Tietäväinen and Zinoviev treats binary cyclic weight-three words and trinomial properties with a different length regime and different invariants. General cyclic-code principles also make the even-parity triple block construction elementary once its precise ideal is identified. The proposed contribution is their joint **fixed-sparsity, mixed repeated-root infinite rank census** together with exact kernel identities, complete weight enumerators, and generalized support hierarchies. The search performed found no matching published statement; the absence of a match is not an independence or global-novelty certificate.

## Limitations
The theorem requires characteristic two, exactly three nonzero coefficients, and length \(3\cdot2^k\). It does not classify larger odd factors, other weights, or circulants over other fields. Distinct kernels are counted as distinct subspaces with labelled coordinates; multiplicities are not orbits under cyclic symmetry. Priority relative to all older repeated-root coding literature is not completely resolved.
