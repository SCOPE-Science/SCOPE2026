For all integers \(n\ge2k\ge4\), the line graphs \(L(J(n,k))\) have exactly three quantifier-rank-three first-order adjacency theories: the isolated cases \((n,k)=(4,2)\) and \((5,2)\), and the single common class \(n\ge6\). The sharp largest minimum representative has \(60\) vertices, even though the stable class includes arbitrary Johnson-graph dimensions and orders.
