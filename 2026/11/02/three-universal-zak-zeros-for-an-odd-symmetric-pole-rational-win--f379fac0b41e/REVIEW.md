# Scientific assessment: Three universal Zak zeros for an odd symmetric-pole rational window

## Correctness — PASS
The Fourier transform follows from the two-term partial-fraction identity and the absolutely convergent difference of two exponential Fourier multipliers. The Zak Poisson formula is valid at the frequency boundary as well as in its interior. The nonzero denominator factors reduce zero classification to a cubic; all polynomial identities are independently recomputable in the accompanying symbolic checker. A nonreal unimodular root forces an exact coefficient condition that factors with the single admissible factor \(x^2-q\). At its only possible parameter \(x=\sqrt q\), a strictly positive quadratic excludes the nonreal pair. The two real circle points and the \(\xi=0\) boundary are exhausted by exact identities. The remaining inequalities use \(0<q<1\), so there is no omitted dilation regime.

## Originality — PASS under inspected evidence
Ghosh [1, Sections 1–4, Appendix] studies the identical rational window, derives the Zak cubic and a reflection/anti-self-reciprocal relation, and proves parameter-dependent frame theorems through polynomial-matrix ranks. Those results neither claim the complete scalar zero census nor exclude nonreal circle roots at all interior frequencies. Belov–Kulikov–Lyubarskii [2] address Herglotz and irrational-density frame properties, not this zero classification. Janssen [3] and Kloos–Stöckler [4] concern different nonnegative/totally positive window classes. The closest indexed studies located by targeted mathematical searching involve either a different even rational window, odd spline zero patterns, or discrete two-tap windows, with no implication to this exact result. Searches cannot prove historical novelty; an implicit older equivalent remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS
A natural, explicitly studied sign-changing rational Gabor generator has a cubic Zak numerator yet no previously identified all-dilation complete zero set in the inspected primary literature. Knowing its exact three-point scalar cancellation locus is a structural invariant and a useful boundary datum for subsequent Zibulski–Zeevi matrix analyses, not a numerical sample or arbitrary parameter specialization. It does not by itself establish a new frame region, which is stated explicitly as a limitation.
