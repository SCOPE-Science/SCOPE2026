For the odd rational Gabor window \(g(s)=s/((s^2+1)(s^2+4))\), the Zak transform of \(g(s/\beta)\) has exactly three zeros, \((0,0)\), \((1/2,0)\), and \((0,1/2)\), in one fundamental square for every \(\beta>0\). An exact real-cubic coefficient obstruction rules out every other zero, including nonsymmetry zeros.
