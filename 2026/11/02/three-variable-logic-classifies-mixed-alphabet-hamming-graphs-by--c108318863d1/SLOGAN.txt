For every finite Cartesian product of complete graphs with nontrivial factors, unrestricted noncounting three-variable first-order logic detects precisely the number of binary factors and the number of factors of size at least three, but none of their larger alphabet sizes. The class indexed by \((b,s)\) has sharp smallest representative \(K_2^{\square b}\square K_3^{\square s}\) with \(2^b3^s\) vertices, and four variables already separate some members that three variables cannot distinguish.
