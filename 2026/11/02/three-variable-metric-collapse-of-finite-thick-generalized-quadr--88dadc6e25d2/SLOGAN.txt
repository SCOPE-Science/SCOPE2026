All finite thick generalized quadrangles have the same unrestricted non-counting \(\mathrm{FO}^3\) theory of their point–line incidence graphs, even when the two sorts and all graph-distance relations are named; the smallest representative has \(30\) vertices. Four variables are necessary and sufficient to distinguish the order-\((2,2)\) and order-\((3,3)\) symplectic examples in this enriched language.
