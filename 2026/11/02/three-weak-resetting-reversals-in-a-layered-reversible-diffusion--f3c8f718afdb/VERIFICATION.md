The moment identities are derived from the reflecting/absorbing backward boundary equations. Polynomial coefficients and four sign witnesses were independently obtained by rational layer integration. A separate transfer-matrix solution for the Laplace transform of first-hit time reproduced the exact first two moments at all four rational witnesses. `verify.py` reproduces the two approaches from this package.

Proof-dependent limits: finite arithmetic assertions, decimal root approximations, and transfer-series truncation are not proofs of root uniqueness or arbitrary smooth stability. The rigorous proofs use exact polynomial derivative signs and uniform convergence of the integral moment functionals, respectively. The three-layer model has exactly three interior roots; sufficiently close smooth approximations have at least three. The relative sizes of globally optimal reset rates are not established.
