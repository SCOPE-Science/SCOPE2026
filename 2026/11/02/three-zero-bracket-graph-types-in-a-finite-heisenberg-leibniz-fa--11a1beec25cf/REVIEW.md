# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The central extension is a two-step nilpotent left and right Leibniz algebra because all triple brackets vanish. Both-sided bracket vanishing reduces exactly to two explicit linear equations on the two-dimensional quotient. The three projective-line cases are exhaustive for all nonzero parameters in odd characteristic. Clique polynomial expressions follow from the stated component graph decompositions. Ordered tuple formulas explicitly handle repeated entries with nonzero self-bracket, so the potential loop/tuple mismatch is addressed. The algebra isomorphism criterion follows from the eigenvalues of the bilinear asymmetry operator and is completed by the coordinate swap. Independent brute-force construction of all vertices and all ordered brackets over three prime fields confirms ten parameter cases; four independently represented extension-field cases over \(\mathbb F_9\) confirm the component and pair/triple formulas and the pair/triple phenomena. The general theorem is symbolic, not extrapolated from finite data.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected literature.** La Rosa–Mancini, arXiv:2106.12904 (2021), already describes the bilinear-form classification and the three-dimensional algebra family; neither contribution is presented as new. La Rosa–Mancini–Nagy, arXiv:2302.08306 (2023), gives isotopism classifications but not the element-level two-sided vanishing graph or its all-arity enumerators; isotopy's separate first and second argument maps do not preserve that graph. Barreiro–Calderón–Lopes–Sánchez, DOI 10.1080/03081087.2022.2092048 (2022), defines a directed graph on a multiplicative basis for ideal decomposition, not a graph on every field-valued algebra element. Targeted searches of related published records found Lie/group commuting-graph and subalgebra-commutativity statements but not an implication of the full three-case graph decomposition and tuple formulas. An unlocated general theorem of nonsymmetric bilinear orthogonality may still imply the classification: publication priority is not asserted to be certain.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result gives a complete graph-isomorphism classification and clique-counting polynomial for a natural one-parameter family in the established classification of nilpotent Leibniz algebras. It exhibits an explicit failure of algebra reconstruction from an element-level zero-bracket graph: over \(\mathbb F_7\), nonisomorphic \(L_2,L_3\) have literally identical graphs. It also shows that identical ordered commuting-pair counts need not imply identical triple counts, with a strict, exact gap \(q^3(q-1)^4\) between the symmetric and alternating endpoints for every odd field. These are structural limitations of graph and pair-count invariants, rather than arbitrary small-size improvements.

**Scope and risks.** No statement is made for characteristic two, degenerate bilinear parameters, or higher-dimensional Leibniz algebras. The odd-characteristic formula holds for all prime powers by proof; verifier tests cover three prime-field orders and one extension-field order. Historical overlap checks used recoverable claim fields, not a re-review of older work. Published priority and independent mathematical review remain open.
