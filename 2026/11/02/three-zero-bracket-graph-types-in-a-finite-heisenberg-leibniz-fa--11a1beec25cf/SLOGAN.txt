For the three-dimensional nilpotent Leibniz family \([X,Y]=Z\), \([Y,X]=cZ\) over odd finite fields, the complete two-sided zero-bracket graph has exactly three possible shapes, with closed formulas for every clique size and ordered commuting-tuple length. All generic parameters \(c^2\ne1\) have identical graphs even though they represent \((q-3)/2\) nonisomorphic algebras, while the symmetric and alternating endpoints have identical commuting-pair counts but differ by exactly \(q^3(q-1)^4\) commuting triples.
