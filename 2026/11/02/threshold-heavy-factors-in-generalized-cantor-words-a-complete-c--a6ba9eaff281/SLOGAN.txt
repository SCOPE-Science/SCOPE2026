For every generalized Cantor word in integer base \(b\ge4\), all factors at the first length admitting \(k\) ones can be listed by a carry-free binary-index rule, and their number is exactly \(2^{\lceil\log_2 k\rceil-s_2(k-1)}\). This creates unbounded jumps in the diversity of maximally dense factors even though the prefix always achieves the maximum weight.
