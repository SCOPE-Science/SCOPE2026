Degree-proportional irreversible contagion on the sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\) has exact complete contagious-set polynomials for all three threshold regimes. The minimum source size is \(1\), \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), or \(n\), and the corresponding numbers of optimal source sets are \(2n\), then \(2\) for even \(n\) or \(3n\) for odd \(n\), and finally \(F_{n-1}+F_{n+1}\).
