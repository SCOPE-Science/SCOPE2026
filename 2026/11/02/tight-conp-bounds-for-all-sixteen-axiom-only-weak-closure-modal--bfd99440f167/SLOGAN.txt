For each of the sixteen weak modal logics obtained by independently adding \(\mathsf M,\mathsf C,\mathsf N,\mathsf K\) but no modal inference rules, satisfiability is \(\mathrm{NP}\)-complete and validity is \(\mathrm{coNP}\)-complete. A finite Horn closure over the boxed subformulas gives polynomial certificates, sharpening the known primitive-recursive decision bound.
