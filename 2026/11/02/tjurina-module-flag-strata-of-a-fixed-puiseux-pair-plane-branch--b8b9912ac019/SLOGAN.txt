A natural \(q\)-parameter, one-Puiseux-pair family has exactly \(q+1\) Tjurina-number strata, determined solely by its first nonzero coefficient. The entire \(\mathbb C[[x]]\)-module structure of each noncentral Tjurina algebra is \(\mathbb C[[x]]/(x^{mq+1})\oplus\big(\mathbb C[[x]]/(x^{(m-1)q+j-1})\big)^{m-2}\), revealing a positive nonlinear defect even for the highest tangent-trivial perturbation.
