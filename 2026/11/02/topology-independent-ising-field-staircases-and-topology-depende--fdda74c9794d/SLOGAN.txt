For every finite biregular bipartite graph, the two-coupling antiferromagnetic Ising model on its line graph has exactly \(r+s-\gcd(r,s)+1\) zero-temperature magnetization plateaux, with a fixed-sector energy independent of graph topology. Yet the saturation-field ground-state degeneracy equals the total number of matchings of the underlying graph; connected cubic examples with identical staircases have \(1104\) and \(1092\) ground states.
