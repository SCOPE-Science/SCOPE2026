For a fixed finite symmetric-exclusion branch coupled to one dense reservoir, the empty-to-empty excursion has a geometry-independent exact mean and an asymptotically exponential lifetime, but its slow spectral rate carries an explicit graph-dependent correction \(T_G\). An endpoint-rooted path has \(T_G=F_{2N-1}/F_{2N-2}\), whereas a center-rooted star has \(T_G=2/(N-1)\), despite identical vertex count and average lifetime.
