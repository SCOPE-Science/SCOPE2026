For the translated cyclic covers of \(E:y^2=x^3-1\), every full orbit of the distinguished order-three subgroup forces a factor of \(m\) in the number of normalization components, while pairwise torsion collisions change the genus even without disconnecting the fiber product. For odd \(m\ge5\) prime to three, the exact component count is \(m^t\) and each component has genus \(1+\frac{3c}{2}m^{r-t-1}(m-1)\), where \(c\) counts occupied torsion cosets and \(t\) counts complete triples.
