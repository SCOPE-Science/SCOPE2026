The full Mordell–Weil torsion of Suluyer's four-conic–line-fiber cubic pencil is trivial for every \(a
e0,1\) except at the two roots of \(a^2-a+1=0\), where it is exactly \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\). The jump is detected by a new rational branch point of the genus-one double cover, while fiber component groups and Shioda's height exclude every other torsion order.
