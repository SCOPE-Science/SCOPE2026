Edge-triangulating any isolate-free graph turns total domination into an exact vertex-cover product enumerator that distinguishes original from added vertices. For the edge-triangulated complete bipartite graph \(R(K_{a,b})\), all inclusion-minimal total dominating sets belong to four classes, with polynomial \(b z^{a+1}+a z^{b+1}+b^a z^{2a}+a^b z^{2b}\); in the \(R(K_{2,b})\) subfamily, their sizes have arbitrarily long gaps and almost all minimal sets are largest as \(b\to\infty\).
