For every \(2\le m\le n\), the entire total-domination polynomial of the two-token graph \(F_2(K_{m,n})\) factors into a row-column occupancy polynomial and a complete-graph edge-cover polynomial. Its minimum is \(n-1+\lceil m/2\rceil\), and the number of minimum sets has an exact Stirling-number formula, distinguishing total domination from the recently determined ordinary domination number.
