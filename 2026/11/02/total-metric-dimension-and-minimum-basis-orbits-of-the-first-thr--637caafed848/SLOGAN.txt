For the first three dimensions left open by the total-resolvability hypercube problem, the exact values are \(\dim_t(Q_4)=\dim_t(Q_5)=\dim_t(Q_6)=6\). The four-cube has exactly \(1600\) minimum total resolving sets in \(11\) automorphism orbits; a complete finite certificate verifies both the minimum and the orbit census.
