For the cyclic graph generated by all odd jumps \(\pm1,\pm3,\ldots,\pm(2r-1)\), \(r\ge2\), every total perfect code is forced to repeat modulo \(4r\). Such codes exist exactly when \(4r\mid n\); then there are exactly \((2r)^2\), one for each choice of an even and an odd residue class, no matter how large the cyclic lift is.
