For every bounded antilinear Hilbert-space operator in dimension at least two, its normalized \(k\)-frame trace numerical range is a centered disk of radius equal to the average of the first \(k\) generalized singular values of its self-adjoint antilinear part. An exact spectral-multiplicity test determines boundary attainment, and successive disk radii recover the complete generalized singular-value sequence, including compactness and Schatten-ideal membership.
