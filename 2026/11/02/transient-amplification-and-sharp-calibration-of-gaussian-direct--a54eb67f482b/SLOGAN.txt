On an isotropic quadratic, Gaussian direction-hint control variates improve the sharp asymptotic mean-square rate exactly for \(0<\beta<2\), yet never improve the worst-case first step at the same stepsize. With only \(r\) directions unguided, dimension-free long-run convergence requires calibration error \(O(d^{-1/2})\), and perfect hints can still create an arbitrarily large initial mean-square transient.
