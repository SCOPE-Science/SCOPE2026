For the graph-metric scale-two Rips complex of every Desarguesian affine plane of prime-power order \(q\ge3\), translations act on degree-two rational homology through \((q-1)\) augmentation-regular modules and on degree-\(q\) homology through an explicit parallel-class tensor representation. The entire translation quotient is rationally acyclic in positive degrees at order three, but retains \(18\) degree-four classes at order four; a closed formula determines the quotient rank in every order.
