A smooth characteristic-two Picard cover \(Y^3=X^4+aX^3+bX^2+cX+d\) has \(2\)-rank either zero or two, never one, and always has \(a\)-number one. The rank-zero locus is exactly \(a=0\), where a Klein-four translation symmetry splits its Jacobian, up to isogeny, into three supersingular elliptic curves; both Newton polygons and the counts of marked models are explicit.
