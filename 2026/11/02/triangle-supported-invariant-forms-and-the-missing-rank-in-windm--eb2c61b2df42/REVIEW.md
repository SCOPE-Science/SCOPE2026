# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The full claim has one algebraic proof: ad-invariance forces the mixed vertex–edge pairing to be a graph-supported alternating 3-form, with one independent coordinate for each triangle. Its symmetry and invariance converse is checked on basis triples and needs no characteristic restriction. The matrix block reduction of rank uses an invertible \(2r\)-block with zero top-left corner in its inverse, so the restriction to the kernel controls the remaining rank even in characteristic two. In the windmill case, exactly \(k\) nonzero triangle coefficients give mixed-block rank \(0\) if \(k=0\), otherwise \(2k+1\). Multiplying independent symmetric-matrix restriction fibers by standard nonsingular symmetric-form counts proves the complete enumerator. At the top two ranks the stated multiplicities and one-rank gap follow directly. The standard finite-field formula for symmetric nonsingular matrices was checked via classical-group-order derivations and finite examples; no computational sample is treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by source coverage
Mainkar, arXiv:1310.3414, gives the graphical Lie-algebra construction and isomorphism classification, not the invariant-form rank census. Del Barco and Ovando, arXiv:1104.4773, classify free nilpotent algebras admitting nondegenerate invariant metrics, not arbitrary degenerate graph-supported forms. Del Barco, arXiv:1602.08286, Section 3 and Theorem 3.4, **already classifies which graphs give nondegenerate invariant metrics**, so that conclusion is explicitly prior work. Benito, de-la-Concepción and Laliena, arXiv:1604.02923, frame quadratic nilpotent quotients through invariant bilinear forms and alternating 3-form theory, and may facilitate derivation of our general graph parameterization, but their inspected abstract, introductory statements and relevant section framing do not assert the all-rank finite-field windmill formula, the rank gap or the two top-rank multiplicities. Published published-finding corpus and own-history meaning searches found no matching implication; this is negative retrieval evidence, not a proof of novelty. Residual risk remains that the special finite-field rank census is implicit in older metric-algebra work or an unlocated table.

## Value — PASS
Prior metric-existence criteria identify when the radical can vanish, but do not quantify the full degeneracy spectrum in non-metric graphical Lie algebras. Windmill graphs form a canonical infinite connected family built by gluing triangular building blocks. The exact rank enumerator captures every degenerate invariant form over every finite field, and the sharp missing rank between the maximal and second maximal attainable strata, with their multiplicities, gives a structural quantitative refinement of the known nonexistence of a nondegenerate metric. The triangle-count dimension and common-radical characterization also apply uniformly to arbitrary graphs. This is a naturally motivated invariant and a complete classification in the stated family, not an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Remaining risks and boundaries
The publication-priority assessment is best-knowledge rather than exhaustive. The broad alternating 3-form framework is known, and a specialized formula could already be derivable from less accessible literature; these risks have been retained instead of suppressing them. Nondegenerate metric existence is not claimed to be new. No isometry-orbit enumeration, all-graph rank enumeration or independent mathematical validation is asserted.
