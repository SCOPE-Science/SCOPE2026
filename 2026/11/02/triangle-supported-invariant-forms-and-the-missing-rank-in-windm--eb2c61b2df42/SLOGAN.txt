Symmetric ad-invariant forms on a graphical two-step Lie algebra are parametrized exactly by an arbitrary symmetric vertex form and one scalar per graph triangle. For windmills of \(t\ge2\) triangles, the full finite-field rank distribution is explicit: its maximum rank is \(4t+2\), rank \(4t+1\) is impossible, and the top two attainable ranks occur \((q-1)^tq^{\binom{2t+2}{2}}\) and \(t(q-1)^tq^{\binom{2t+2}{2}-1}\) times, respectively.
