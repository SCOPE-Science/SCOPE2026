The full semigroup algebra of order-, fence-, and parity-preserving partial injections on \([n]\) decomposes over any commutative unital ring into explicit matrix blocks whose sizes are binomial counts of parity-and-gap signatures. Its number of simple modules over every field follows the Tribonacci recurrence \(D_n=D_{n-1}+D_{n-2}+D_{n-3}\), beginning \(1,2,4\) at \(n=0,1,2\).
