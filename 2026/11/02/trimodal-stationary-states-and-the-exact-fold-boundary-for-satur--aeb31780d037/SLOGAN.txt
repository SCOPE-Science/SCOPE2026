A stationary two-state run-and-tumble particle with saturating speed and reversal rates admits **three distinct positional density maxima**, contrary to a previously assumed two-maximum bound. On the commensurate slice \(g=3u\), its trimodal region is exactly \(B<5C\) and \(A_f<A<B+3C\), with an algebraic fold boundary and an explicit sixth-order critical peak.
