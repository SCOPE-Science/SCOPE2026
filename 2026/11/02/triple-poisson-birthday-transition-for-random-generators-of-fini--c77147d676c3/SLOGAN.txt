For random \(k\)-generator tuples in growing powers of finite commutative local algebras, Frobenius residue collisions, proper-subfield residues, and cotangent-rank failures have a sharp joint birthday-scale phase diagram. Exactly when the residue degree and tuple length are even and the cotangent dimension is \(k/2+1\), all three survive as independent Poisson obstructions and the generation probability tends to \(\exp(-2c-fc^2/2)\).
