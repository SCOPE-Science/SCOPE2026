For any fixed variable width \(k\), two finite commuting permutations have the same unrestricted-depth counting-free theory exactly when the multiplicity of each \(\mathbb Z^2\) period-lattice orbit agrees after truncation at \(k\). The corresponding theories by smallest-representative size have generating series \(\prod_{h\geq1}(1+z^h+\cdots+z^{kh})^{\sigma_1(h)}\), while one-variable logic with counting recovers every orbit multiplicity.
