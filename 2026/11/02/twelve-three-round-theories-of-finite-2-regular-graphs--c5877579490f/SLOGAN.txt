Among nonempty finite simple \(2\)-regular graphs, first-order logic of quantifier rank three has exactly twelve theories: five small exceptions and, from seven vertices onward, seven classes determined only by whether \(3\)-cycles, \(4\)-cycles, and cycles of length at least \(5\) occur. Every class has a representative of at most \(12\) vertices, sharply attained by \(C_3\sqcup C_4\sqcup C_5\).
