# Scientific review of the twelve-class affine-quandle result

## Correctness
PASS. For two distinct fixed points the six ordered atomic quandle equations reduce to the anharmonic orbit of the scalar parameter. Every orbit value yields exactly one third point. Equality and repeated arguments are handled separately. Equality of extension-type supports gives a genuine indefinitely maintainable three-pebble partial-isomorphism strategy. Three-variable existential mask formulas distinguish any different supports. The stabilizer equations leave exactly the orbit-size possibilities one, two, three, and six, with three labeled harmonic patterns. Two finite programs check the masks and independently compute bounded-variable logical refinements. The computations corroborate, rather than replace, the infinite argument.

## Originality
PASS, with historical-priority qualification. Nelson (2002) classifies finite Alexander quandles up to isomorphism; Ferman–Nowik–Teicher (2008) determine structural automorphisms; Elhamdadi (2012) surveys quandle algebra; Grohe–Otto (2012) develops general pebble methods. The mathematical conclusions in the inspected materials do not directly state the exact twelve-type unrestricted \(\mathrm{FO}^3\) quotient or sharp nine-element representatives. The three named database quandle hits address different algebraic invariants. A general published theorem about three-variable types of relational Latin squares remains a plausible indirect-priority risk.

## Value
PASS. The finite-field scalar Alexander construction is a motivated, widely studied family, and replacing isomorphism by bounded-variable indistinguishability produces an exact natural classification. The representative bound is sharp, the individual harmonic orientations stay separated, and the four-variable example shows the width restriction has nontrivial content. No new general quandle structural theorem is claimed.
