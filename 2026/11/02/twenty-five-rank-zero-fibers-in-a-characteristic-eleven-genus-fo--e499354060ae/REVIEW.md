# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A finite multiple root requires x nonzero, a=-(5/2)x^3 and x^5=2/3, giving exactly a^5=6 in characteristic 11; each such a admits that simultaneous solution because the relevant exponents 3 and 5 are coprime. Off that locus, tame total ramification at five finite roots and infinity gives genus four. The four differentials are regular at the finite ramification points (dx has order 2 and y order 1 there) and at infinity; the pole orders distinguish them within their Cartier source spaces. Exact multinomial coefficient extraction reproduces A and B as displayed. Both are nonzero, so Cartier rank is two. On the dual space let Frobenius be H composed with the eleventh power. Its image is the direct sum of the top line and the lower line spanned by B. On those lines it alternates via nonzero scalar B in the lower direction and scalar A^t B^(11)=T in the upper direction. If T is nonzero the image is stable of dimension two; if T is zero the ranks descend 2,1,0. Independently checked the quartic remainder and gcd, and obtained the two distinct irreducible factors by exact finite-field factorization. The Kummer proof is valid: the base fifth powers are ±1; the norm of the quartic generator is -2, so the generator is not a fifth power either. Both factors miss the excluded locus. Orbit lengths and extension counts follow from their degrees; x↦ζx establishes the stated marked-parameter symmetry, not an isomorphism count.
