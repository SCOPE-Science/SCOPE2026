For the smooth characteristic-eleven genus-four pencil \(y^3=x^5+ax^2+1\), the Jacobian has constant \(a\)-number \(2\), but its \(11\)-rank drops from \(2\) to \(0\) at exactly \(25\) marked parameters. Those parameters form two Frobenius orbits of lengths \(5\) and \(20\), determined by the separable polynomial \(2a^{25}-3a^5-3\).
