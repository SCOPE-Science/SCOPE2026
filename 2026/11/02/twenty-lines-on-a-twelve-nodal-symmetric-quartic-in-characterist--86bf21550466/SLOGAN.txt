The symmetric characteristic-two quartic \(\sigma_4+(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2)^2=0\), whose twelve nodes arise from the classical twelve-node construction, contains exactly twenty geometric lines: eight coordinate-plane lines and twelve paired-coordinate lines. Their reduced Fano scheme consists of ten degree-two points over \(\mathbb F_2\), and the twenty lines have seventy-two nodal pair-intersections and twenty-two distinct smooth crossings.
