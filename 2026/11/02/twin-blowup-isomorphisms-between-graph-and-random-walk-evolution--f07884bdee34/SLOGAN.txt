For every characteristic-zero field, the graph and random-walk evolution algebras of a connected independent-set blowup of a nonsingular base graph are isomorphic exactly when the blowup is regular, or when it is bipartite biregular and its degree product \(D_X^2D_Y\) is a field cube. A bilinear-pencil determinant recovers all twin blocks intrinsically, yielding a complete parametrization of isomorphisms and the real/complex singular-graph conjecture for this infinite family.
