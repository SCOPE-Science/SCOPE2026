For Mallows permutations, the asymptotic variance per vertex of the longest descent-first alternating subsequence is uniquely maximized at two reciprocal inversion biases, \(q\approx0.2254456654\) and \(q\approx4.43565858\). Uniform sampling \(q=1\) instead makes this variance coefficient an interior local minimum, even though it maximizes the leading expected subsequence length.
