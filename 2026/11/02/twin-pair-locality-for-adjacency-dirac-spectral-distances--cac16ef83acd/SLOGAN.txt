For vertices with identical weighted external adjacency profiles, the adjacency-Dirac Connes distance is exactly \(\bigl(D_{xy}^{2}+\frac12\sum_{z\ne x,y}D_{xz}^{2}\bigr)^{-1/2}\), regardless of all edges among other vertices. In particular, false twins with \(k>0\) common neighbors have distance \(\sqrt{2/k}\), while adding their mutual edge changes it sharply to \(\sqrt{2/(k+2)}\).
