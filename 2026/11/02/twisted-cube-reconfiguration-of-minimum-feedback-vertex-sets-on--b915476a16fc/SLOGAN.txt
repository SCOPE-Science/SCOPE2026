For odd prisms \(C_{2k+1}\square K_2\) with \(k\ge2\), all \((2k+1)2^k\) minimum feedback vertex sets form an explicit cyclic twisted-cube exchange graph of degree \(k+1\). The graph is bipartite and has exactly \((2k+1)2^{k-2}(\binom{k-1}{2}+2(k-2))\) four-cycles; the previously known optimum size and general connectivity are not claimed as new.
