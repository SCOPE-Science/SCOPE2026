For a symmetric tensor product of disk analytic kernel spaces, twisted-diagonal restriction has an exact Gegenbauer spectrum. Away from two degenerate angles, its centered positive trace operator has sharp ordered singular-value decay \(s_k\sim \beta_{r,\theta}M_{r,\theta}^{r}k^{-r}\), with a rational-versus-irrational angle dependence and an exact Schatten threshold \(p>1/r\).
