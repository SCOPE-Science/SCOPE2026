For uniform random pairs on a flat rectangular Möbius band, the intrinsic geodesic distance is stochastically no greater than on the same-size untwisted cylinder. The exact squared-distance improvement has a polynomial-to-logarithmic transition at equal width and circumference, is \(C^3\) but not \(C^4\) there, and grows from zero to three quarters of the cylinder mean square in relative terms.
