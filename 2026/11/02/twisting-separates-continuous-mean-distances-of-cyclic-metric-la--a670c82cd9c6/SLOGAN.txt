For equal-edge cyclic ladders with \(n\ge3\) rungs, a Möbius twist reduces the mean intrinsic distance between arclength-uniform points by exactly \(1/(6n)\) for even \(n\), and \(4/(27n)\) for odd \(n\). In the odd case the corresponding vertex-mean distance does not change at all: continuous sampling detects a twist that vertex averaging misses.
