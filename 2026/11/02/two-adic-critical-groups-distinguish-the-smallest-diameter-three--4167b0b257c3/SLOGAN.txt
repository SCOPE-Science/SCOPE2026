The 64-vertex Doob graph \(\mathrm{Sh}\square K_4\) and Hamming graph \(H(3,4)\) have identical Laplacian spectra, spanning-tree counts and prime-field Laplacian ranks, but different two-primary sandpile groups. In particular, the numbers of chip-firing classes annihilated by four are \(2^{67}\) and \(2^{69}\), respectively, as certified by complete prime-power Smith reductions.
