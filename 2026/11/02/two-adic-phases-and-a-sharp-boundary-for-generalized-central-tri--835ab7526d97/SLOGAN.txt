For every nonzero integer coefficient pair outside the sharp boundary \(\nu_2(c)=2\nu_2(b)-1\), generalized central trinomial coefficients have an exact all-index two-adic valuation formula, with a normalized odd-unit correction modulo four. On the boundary, fixed coefficient valuations allow arbitrarily large valuations already at index two; for coprime pairs the noncritical formulas yield an exact binomial histogram at each dyadic depth.
