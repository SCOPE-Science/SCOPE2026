For two distinct lower-quota blocks, ex-ante proportional and quota-respecting apportionment is envy-free only when the two block-count laws are exact reflections, a measure-zero condition in any nontrivial continuous quota chamber. In the four-party two-by-two case, a unique lottery minimizes both expected envy and the chance of any envy, with common sharp value \(\lvert b+c-1\rvert\).
