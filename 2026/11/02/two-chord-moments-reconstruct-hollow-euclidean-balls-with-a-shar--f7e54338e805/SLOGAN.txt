For two independent volume-uniform points in an unknown concentric hollow \(d\)-ball, the second and fourth Euclidean distance moments recover both radii whenever \(d\ge2\) is given. Even with dimension unknown, these moments identify every shell in dimensions \(2\) through \(5\), but precisely from \(d=6\) onward there are moment-identical shells in adjacent dimensions; recovery of the inner-radius ratio has sharp boundary exponents \(1/d\) and \(1/2\).
