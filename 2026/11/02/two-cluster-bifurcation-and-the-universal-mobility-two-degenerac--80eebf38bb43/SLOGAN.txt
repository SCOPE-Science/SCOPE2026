On every finite complete-graph logistic Keller–Segel network, each fixed two-cluster partition has a locally explicit symmetry-breaking equilibrium diagram; unbalanced partitions ordinarily cross linearly, but at the universal mobility ratio \(\eta_n/r=2\) all partitions instead exhibit the same \(\sqrt{3(\tau-1)}\) leading gap. Balanced partitions show square-root onset at every positive mobility ratio; these are local stationary—not stability or full-equilibrium-census—claims.
