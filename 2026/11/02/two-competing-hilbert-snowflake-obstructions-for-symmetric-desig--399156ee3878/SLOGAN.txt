For every symmetric \(2\)-design with \(k-\lambda\ge2\), the sharp Hilbert-snowflake exponent of its incidence graph has two competing spectral obstructions whose winner is determined by the explicit inequality \(2^{p_s}\lessgtr(k+\sqrt{k-\lambda})/(k-1)\). Incidence graphs of projective planes have exponent of order \(q^{-1/2}\), but complementary incidence graphs have exponent of order \(q^{-2}\) from order \(q=3\) onward, with a simultaneous change in the critical Euclidean embedding dimension.
