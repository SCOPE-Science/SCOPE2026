For the smooth characteristic-seven Picard curves \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+bx+c\), Jacobian \(7\)-rank vanishes exactly on two weighted-scaling orbits: \(a=c=0,b\ne0\) or \(a=0,c^3=2b^4,b\ne0\). Both have \(a\)-number \(2\), and every other smooth parameter with \(a\ne0\) is ordinary; the only smooth Cartier ranks are \(1\) and \(3\).
