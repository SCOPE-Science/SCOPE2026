On the modular cyclic-four group algebra in characteristic two, all weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators form two affine four-spaces meeting in an affine three-space, with an exact rank and nilpotency census. Every linearly compatible family is confined to one component, so over \(\mathbb F_q\) there are exactly \(2q^{4m}-q^{3m}\) ordered compatible families of length \(m\), and a complete joint-rank polynomial is explicit.
