Over every field of characteristic different from two, the reduced projectivized rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter locus of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) is a Veronese \(\mathbb P^2\) united with \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) along a smooth conic. Over every odd finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), it yields exactly \(2q^3-q-1\) nonzero rank-one operators in \(2q+1\) automorphism orbits with completely explicit sizes.
