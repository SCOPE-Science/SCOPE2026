For unscaled \(\mathrm{Beta}(2,b)\) adoption thresholds in locally tree-like regular social networks, vulnerable branching is exactly unimodal in connectivity and its supercritical degrees form one interval. As \(b\to\infty\), the most vulnerable degree is \(0.5576367\ldots b\), but supercritical propagation remains possible up to \(b^2/2-b/6+O(1)\) neighbors.
