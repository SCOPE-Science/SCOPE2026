For weighted recursive trees with attachment fractions \(p_j=cj^{-\gamma}\), \(0<c<1,\ 0<\gamma<1\), total path-length variance has sharp transitions at \(\gamma=1/2\) and \(\gamma=2/3\), with an explicit five-regime asymptotic law. Beyond the latter threshold the centered path length divided by \(n\) converges almost surely and in \(L^2\) to a nondegenerate random limit, extending the known linear-scale regime to stretched-exponential cumulative weights.
