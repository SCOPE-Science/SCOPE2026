The sixth cyclotomic polynomial \(\Phi_6(z)=z^2-z+1\) has no nontrivial mutual-divisibility pairs of positive integers: \(a\mid\Phi_6(b)\) and \(b\mid\Phi_6(a)\) force \(a=b=1\). This rules out all squarefree semiprimes from the integer-valued reciprocal-gcd sums studied by De Koninck and Letendre.
