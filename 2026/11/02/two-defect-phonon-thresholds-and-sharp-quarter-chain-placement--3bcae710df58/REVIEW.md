# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The domain and free/free endpoint convention are fixed unambiguously in the finding. The generalized normal-mode pencil has positive mass matrix for every \(0<\delta<1\). Congruence converts its count above the infinite homogeneous band edge into the positive inertia of a rank-two perturbation. The exact Green function is verified by the finite-difference equation and endpoint conditions. Its two-site compression gives the count at both strict and critical inequalities; an auxiliary positive two-dimensional principal Laplacian block proves that both contrast thresholds lie below one. The Rayleigh quotient proves the sharp site-independent second threshold and its equality conditions, and a symmetric quarter-neighbor construction proves the finite-size approximation. These steps are analytical for every \(N\ge3\). The independent standard-library exact-rational diagnostic passed 2,104 Green entries, 6,544 geometry checks, 39,264 inertia comparisons, and 25 equality witnesses (no skips). Numerical diagnostics do not constitute an exhaustive proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence

The exact one-defect open-chain threshold in Luo (arXiv:1607.06946, Sec. 3.2) is explicitly acknowledged and recovered as a rank-one special case, not rebranded. Theocharis et al. (arXiv:0906.4094, Sec. IV.1) already establish qualitatively that two nearby granular defects can support one or two localized modes as radius and separation vary. Boechler et al. (arXiv:1103.2483, Sec. V) give a four-bead approximation for nearby pairs and numerical eigenanalysis in a different boundary and variable-contact model. The reviewed sources do not contain the all-position, constant-spring free/free two-mode phase diagram or sharp quarter-site optimum. Semantic research-index comparisons for alternate names including double isotope, phonon defect, threshold, Green resolvent and spectral count returned no clear equivalent; that is evidence only to the extent of those actual searches, not a uniqueness proof. A full historical census (including early surface-defect S-matrix work) is not claimed; equivalent unpublished or differently normalized treatments remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Earlier experiments and harmonic calculations make the one-versus-two localized-mode transition a genuine physical question. The result replaces a site-by-site eigenvalue scan or few-bead approximation with one explicit two-by-two formula covering every admissible finite chain, position and contrast. It also solves the geometric design question of how weak two identical impurities can be while producing two modes beyond the host band: the sharp \(2/N\) threshold, exceptional exact attainability at \(N\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(O(N^{-3})\) approximation on other lengths are substantive coupled-defect information, not mere recomputation of the rank-one result. The result does not treat variable contact springs or nonlinear granular crystals, and makes no quantitative localization-length claim.
