Two equal light-mass defects in a free finite harmonic chain have exactly computable one- and two-mode thresholds above the homogeneous phonon band, for every chain length and pair of sites. The least contrast permitting both modes is at least \(2/N\), with equality exactly when the chain has \(N\equiv2\pmod4\) sites and the defects occupy the two quarter-chain sites; nearly optimal placements exist for every length.
