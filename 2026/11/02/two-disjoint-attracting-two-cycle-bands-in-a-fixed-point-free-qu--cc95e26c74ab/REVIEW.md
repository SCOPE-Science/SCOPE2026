# Research review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is restricted to the exact real-plane map with \((a,b,d)=(1/100,1/10,1/10)\) and \(c>0\). Exhaustiveness follows because a primitive two-cycle necessarily alternates vertical coordinates \(t,-t\) with \(t>0\), its horizontal midpoint must be \(2/(1+b)\), and both return conditions reduce to a single quadratic \(Lt^2-2at+K=0\). Exact discriminant signs give all four orbit-count regimes, including the distant branch diverging at \(c=21/40\). The derivative at the positive/negative points is evaluated on the correct smooth side of the absolute-value term. The two-step trace and determinant identities yield three exact Jury inequalities. A quartic with exactly two positive roots (by Descartes plus disjoint rational sign brackets) and a strictly decreasing rational function with two isolated unit-modulus crossings give precisely two hyperbolic sink intervals. The standalone exact-rational checker passed directly against the packaged source.

The proof does not infer a global classification from trajectories or from a finite scan. At the four spectral boundary parameters, the theorem deliberately asserts multiplier conditions but no nonlinear attraction classification.

## Originality — PASS within examined sources

The exact map and parameter slice appear in Huynh et al., *Entropy* 21 (2019), 279 (DOI 10.3390/e21030279), whose complete readable article was compared, especially Sec. 2.1's numerical period-doubling diagram. It does not state the global prime-two census, the two separate stable windows, the algebraic unit-circle crossings, or the escape-to-infinity transition. Panahi, Sprott and Jafari, *International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos* 28 (2018), 1850144 (DOI 10.1142/S0218127418501444), have the \(a=0\) quadratic predecessor. Their full manuscript describes the construction, numerical Lyapunov/bifurcation diagrams and basins, without proving the positive-\(a\) four-boundary claim. A 2022 review (DOI 10.1007/978-3-031-02472-6_8) is a remaining access risk, because only its abstract was available; this does not establish noncoverage of its full text. Search results for several unrelated exact period-two studies of Duffing, Bogdanov, and Rulkov maps were compared at the mathematical-object level: they do not imply the theorem for this different absolute-value planar map.

An undiscovered identical derivation remains a publication-priority risk, and no uniqueness beyond the searched evidence is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2019 defining paper explicitly uses the \(c\)-sweep to display the onset of period-two dynamics and period doubling. The exact orbit count and the unexpected **two disconnected windows of exponential attraction** in precisely that published slice are intrinsic global/local dynamical invariants, not arbitrary test points or merely reproduced chaos diagnostics. The count exhibits a fold followed by loss of a distinct cycle at infinity, while its one finite survivor repeatedly gains and loses spectral stability. The exact algebraic boundaries permit reproducible phase-diagram classification and distinguish loss by a \(-1\) multiplier from loss by a nonreal unit-modulus pair. Their mathematical relevance does not depend on a claimed global attractor classification or on speculative applications.

## Closest literature and limits

The predecessor with \(a=0\) is explicitly acknowledged rather than presented as a newly constructed map. The 2019 paper's numerical bifurcation does not determine the exact thresholds. The 2022 comparative chapter is uninspected in full. The classification concerns prime-two orbit geometry and strict local exponential attraction only; higher periods, nonlinear behavior at critical points and global basins remain open within this result.
