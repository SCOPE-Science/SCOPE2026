For the critical clumsy-coupon collector with loss probability \(p=c/m\), the squared coefficient of variation of successive full-coverage entrance cycles has an explicit limit \(V(c)\) with exactly one minimum, one maximum, and one crossing of the Poisson-dispersion level \(1\). This yields a sharp sub-Poisson to super-Poisson transition at \(c=1.502861\ldots\), despite both extreme-load limits approaching Poisson dispersion.
