The 120 proper named three-edge-colourings of the Möbius–Kantor graph split under complete alternating-cycle Kempe switches into unequal 24- and 96-state components. The large sector is a bipartite six-regular Cayley graph with diameter four; the small sector has diameter three and odd girth five, with the two sectors sharply distinguished by their bichromatic two-factor cycle types.
