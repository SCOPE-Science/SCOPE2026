## Mathematical value — PASS

When a physical variable is coupled to a hidden nonequilibrium medium, it matters whether a vanishing observed hidden-position correlation means true marginal screening or instead conditional screening after controlling a measured coordinate. The result gives a natural complete classification of these two information-theoretic relations over an entire physical parameter family, not merely a specially chosen zero. The strict non-overlapping intervals \((1/2,2/3)\) and \((4/5,1)\) distinguish two experimentally meaningful information-screening regimes and show they cannot coincide below the instability. Their exact formulas, uniqueness, monotone tuning and rational separation witness provide reproducible benchmarks for hidden-coordinate modeling. The result does not assert causality, vanishing entropy production, general unequal-diffusion behavior, or dynamical independence.
