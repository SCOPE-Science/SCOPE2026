In a stationary three-coordinate nonreciprocal Brownian trap, positive shear makes the hidden coordinate \(Z\) independent of physical position \(X\) at precisely \(g=(k^2+2)/(2k^2+3)\), while independence conditioned on the second physical coordinate \(Y\) occurs at a different explicitly solvable shear. These two information-screening points are unique in the stable interval and obey the universal strict separation \(1/2<g_{\mathrm{marginal}}<2/3<4/5<g_{\mathrm{conditional}}<1\) for every positive hidden-coupling ratio \(k\).
