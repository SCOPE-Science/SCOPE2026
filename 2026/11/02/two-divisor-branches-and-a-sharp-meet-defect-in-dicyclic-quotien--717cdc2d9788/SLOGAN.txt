The quotient-isomorphism poset of every nonabelian dicyclic group consists of two reversed divisor branches joined at the \(C_2\)-quotient, with a greatest trivial quotient. It has exactly \(\tau(2m)+1\) elements—the same number as the dihedral group of equal order—but, unlike the dihedral lattice, is a join-semilattice with exactly \(\tau(u)(\tau(m)-1)\) unordered pairs lacking a meet, where \(m=2^s u\) and \(u\) is odd.
