In a strictly convex eye-shaped billiard, the entire parameter window where two nonaxial two-bounce trajectories exist has two hyperbolic axial diameters but two linearly elliptic diagonal diameters, except at exactly two explicit \(-1\) multiplier resonances. The diagonal two-collision trace is an exact rational function of \(u=\sin^2\theta\), with the sharp ellipticity bound proved by a positive factorization.
