With Pareto-timed Brownian exploration and noisy harmonic first-hit returns, the two stationary phases have distinct exact spatial-tail exponents: \(2(\delta-1)\) for free exploration and \(2\delta\) for trapped return, with explicit amplitudes and matching sharp moment cutoffs. Thus a positive recurrent system can have infinite global variance while its phase-weighted harmonic energy retains the previously known equilibrium value.
