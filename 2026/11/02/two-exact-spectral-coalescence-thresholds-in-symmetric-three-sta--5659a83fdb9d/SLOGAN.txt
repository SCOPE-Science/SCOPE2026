A symmetric three-state stochastic frequency exchange produces exactly three, then two, then one spectral peaks as the exchange-to-splitting ratio increases. Its two critical ratios are the square roots of the positive roots of \(81y^3-81y^2-5y+1=0\), approximately \(0.29547238\) and \(1.02355844\); general three-state spectral formulas were already known.
