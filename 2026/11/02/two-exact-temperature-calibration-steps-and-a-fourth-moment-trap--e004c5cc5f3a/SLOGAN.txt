For one-step unadjusted HMC using the triangular randomized rRKN 2.5 integrator on a harmonic Gaussian target, the invariant position variance matches its target exactly at two nonzero squared steps \(z=4\pm2\sqrt2\), even though the invariant law is never the Gaussian target. At the larger calibration step the invariant fourth moment is infinite despite a perfectly calibrated finite variance; the exact finite-variance stability cutoff is \(z\approx7.036784618\).
