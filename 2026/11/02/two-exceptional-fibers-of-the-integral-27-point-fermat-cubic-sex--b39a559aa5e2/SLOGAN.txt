The integral complete intersection of the Fermat cubic with its classical sextactic divisor is finite flat of degree \(27\) and geometrically reduced away from characteristics \(2\) and \(3\). In characteristic \(2\) it becomes the triple nine-flex divisor \(E[6]-E[3]\) on a supersingular smooth cubic, whereas in characteristic \(3\) it becomes a single length-\(27\) complete intersection with local algebra \(k[[u,v]]/(u^3,v^9)\).
