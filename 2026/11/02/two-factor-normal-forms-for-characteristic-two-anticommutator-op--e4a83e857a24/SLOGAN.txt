Over every characteristic-two field, all products of unnormalized anticommutator maps \(X\mapsto AX+XA\) on \(M_2\) collapse to products of at most two factors, with explicit disjoint normal forms. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) for even \(q\), the generated unital monoid has exactly \((q-1)q^4+2q^3+q^2-q\) elements, and no operators of rank three.
