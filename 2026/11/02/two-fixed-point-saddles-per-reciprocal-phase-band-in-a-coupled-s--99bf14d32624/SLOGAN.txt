For the coupled sine-square map with a reciprocal-coordinate singularity, every sufficiently high phase band contains exactly two nearby fixed points, and both are saddles of opposite Jacobian orientation. Their separation is \(2a^{3/2}/(\pi n^{5/2})+O(n^{-7/2})\), while their unstable and stable multipliers grow as \(n^{3/2}\) and decay as \(n^{-1}\), respectively.
