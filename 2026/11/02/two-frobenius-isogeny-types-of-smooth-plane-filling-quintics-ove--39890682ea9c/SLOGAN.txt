All smooth plane-filling quintics over \(\mathbb F_3\) have only two Hasse--Weil numerators, represented by a \(144\)-equation class and a \(432\)-equation class. Their ordinary genus-six Jacobians are respectively the cube of a simple abelian surface up to isogeny and a simple abelian sixfold, despite identical point counts over \(\mathbb F_3\) and \(\mathbb F_9\).
