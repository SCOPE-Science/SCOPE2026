For symmetric Bernoulli threshold events, the BK gap first switches on at threshold fraction \(p/2\), while failure of independent disjoint testimony has a distinct Gaussian crossover at \((2p-p^2)/2\). At the second window the symmetric-pivot bound has the correct constant order, the shared-open-coordinate overlap acquires an explicit conditional Gaussian shift, and rare product-space defects admit a sharp binomial large-deviation equivalent.
