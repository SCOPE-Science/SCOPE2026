When two equal groups of uniformly sampled coupons require \(m\) and \(m+1\) copies per type, the completion time has a universal Gumbel window for every \(m\to\infty\). Which group finishes last has a sharp transition at \(m\asymp\log n\): the extra-copy group wins with limiting probability \(1\), \(r/(1+r)\), or \(1/2\), in the three regimes, with \(r-1-\log r=\lim (\log n)/m\) in the intermediate regime.
