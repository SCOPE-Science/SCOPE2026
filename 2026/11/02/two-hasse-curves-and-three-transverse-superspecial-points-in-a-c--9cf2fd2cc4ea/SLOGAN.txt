For the genus-two bielliptic family \(y^2=x^6+ax^4+bx^2+1\) in characteristic seven, the two geometrically irreducible Hasse curves meet transversely at exactly three smooth superspecial parameters but have three doubled intersections on the singular-fiber boundary. Over every \(\mathbb F_{7^r}\), the numbers of smooth coefficient-marked fibers of ranks \(0,1,2\) are exactly \(3,\,2\cdot7^r-14,\,7^{2r}-3\cdot7^r+12\).
