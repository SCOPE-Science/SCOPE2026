In Arapura--Greer--Zhang's integral invariant-cycle counterexample, the exceptional elliptic K3 has transcendental lattice \(A_2(4)\), and its index-four transcendental specialization image is exactly \(2A_2\subset A_2\). Thus the missing transcendental classes form \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2\), not a cyclic group of order four.
