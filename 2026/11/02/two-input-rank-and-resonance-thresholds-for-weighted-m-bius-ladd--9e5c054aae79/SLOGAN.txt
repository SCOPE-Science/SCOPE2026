For weighted Möbius-ladder consensus networks, the exact reachable rank from two adjacent controls is \(2n-2C_4-C_3\), where \(C_4\) and \(C_3\) count explicitly prescribed cosine resonances; two, three, or four consecutive controls are minimally sufficient at every coupling. At the first resonance, two-input reachability loses one dimension for even half-cycle size, two for the six-vertex case, and four for every larger odd half-cycle size.
