For two noise-free leaders on a friendship graph whose hub spokes have conductance one and whose triangle-closing edges have conductance \(t>0\), optimal leader placement changes from hub-plus-peripheral to two different triangles at the exact threshold \(t=1/3\) for two triangles and \(t=3\) for three triangles. With four or more triangles, hub-plus-peripheral is optimal for every \(t>0\), and same-triangle peripheral leaders are never optimal.
