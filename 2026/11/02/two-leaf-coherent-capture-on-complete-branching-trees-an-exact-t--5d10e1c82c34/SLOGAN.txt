On complete rooted \(b\)-ary trees with \(b\geq3\), two absorbing leaves at distance \(2h\) have exactly \(D(L)+D(h-1)\) coherent bright modes, where \(D(m)=\sum_{q=2}^{m+2}\varphi(q)\). Thus the ideal eventual capture probability from a maximally mixed excitation grows strictly with leaf separation, and opposite root branches are optimal for every positive pair of absorption rates.
