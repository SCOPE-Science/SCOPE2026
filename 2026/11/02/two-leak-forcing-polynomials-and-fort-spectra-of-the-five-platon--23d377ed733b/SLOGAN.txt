Across the five Platonic graph skeletons, the exact two-leak forcing numbers are \((3,6,5,9,8)\), with \((4,16,6,2360,240)\) minimum-size placements. The dodecahedron has \(1205\) inclusion-minimal two-leak forts spanning five sizes, whereas all \(255\) minimal two-leak forts of the icosahedron have size four; complete forcing-set and fort polynomials are established by exhaustive finite certificates.
