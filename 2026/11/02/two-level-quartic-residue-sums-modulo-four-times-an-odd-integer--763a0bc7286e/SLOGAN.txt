For every odd \(q>1\), cyclic order-four subgroups modulo \(4q\) whose square is \(2q-1\) exist exactly when all prime divisors of \(q\) are \(1\pmod4\). Their exact residue sums split evenly between \(4q\) and \(8q\), with \(2^{\omega(q)-1}\) groups at each level; the centered half never contains \(-1\).
