For every finite dihedral group \(D_{2n}\) with \(n\ge3\), two-local automorphisms are exactly an independent unit multiplier on rotations and a gcd-distance-preserving permutation of reflections. Their group is a product of the unit group and finite rooted-tree automorphism groups, with exact order \(\varphi(n)\prod_{p^e\parallel n}(p!)^{(p^e-1)/(p-1)}\); all ordinary automorphisms form a proper subgroup.
