At the leading marginal adaptive Brownian resetting rate \(2dD/\rho^2\), logarithmic and iterated-logarithmic corrections determine whether a stationary probability exists: the exact thresholds are \(b>d+2\), or \(b=d+2\) with \(c>d+2\). The killed occupation density acquires corresponding logarithmic powers, although every model has the same leading inverse-square rate.
