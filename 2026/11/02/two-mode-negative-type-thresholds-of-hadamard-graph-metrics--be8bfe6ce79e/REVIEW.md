# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The distance-regular intersection array and full centered powered-distance spectrum reduce the universal inequality to four scalar eigenvalues. The two nontrivial positive-root candidates are shown unique by strict monotonicity and concavity, the remaining sectors have fixed signs, and the endpoint Hilbert dimensions follow from the centered Gram matrix rank. A separate independent graph-construction checker passes for Sylvester and Paley examples. The finite computations are not used as proof; the core recurrence, root uniqueness and limiting estimates are analytical. Order \(4\), endpoint ranks, all positive exponents and existence restrictions are stated explicitly.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk

The closest direct Hadamard paper, Cioabă–Gupta–Ihringer–Kurihara (2021, Section 3.3.1), calculates graph-distortion quantities and gives the intersection array, but not the continuous distance-power conditional-negative-type roots. Hirasaka–Kim–Yu (2014) classify/refine related association schemes; Robertson (2023) treats distorted negative type and other graph examples. General Schoenberg and Li–Weston results give the framework but no specific Hadamard threshold or inverse-order law. Targeted searches for Hadamard graph generalized roundness, negative type, graph snowflakes, powered distance matrices and equivalent association-scheme formulations did not identify an exact covering theorem. This is evidence for noncoverage among checked sources, not a proof of global priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result supplies a natural exact Banach/metric-geometric invariant for every Hadamard graph, reveals competing geometric obstructions at orders \(8\) and \(12\), determines sharp endpoint embedding dimension from spectral multiplicity, and proves a nontrivial \(1/k\) decay unlike the \(1/\sqrt{k}\) scale of the other obstruction. This distinguishes the result from merely tabulating one graph's spectrum. It also gives sharp snowflake thresholds for non-isomorphic graphs with identical Hadamard parameters.

## Limits

No assertion is made for nonexistent Hadamard orders or for weighted metrics. Equality of the two roots at some hypothetical continuous parameter is not excluded, and the conditional endpoint-rank statement covers it. Earlier equivalent analysis under a different association-scheme name remains possible. The cited negative-type and distortion papers were checked in their relevant full-text portions; independent mathematical audit has not occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
