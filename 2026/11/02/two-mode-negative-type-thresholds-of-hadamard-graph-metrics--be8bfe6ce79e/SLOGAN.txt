The graph metric of every Hadamard matrix of order \(k\ge8\) has an exact Hilbert-snowflake cutoff determined by the first of two uniquely defined scalar eigenmode roots, independent of the chosen matrix. The obstruction switches between orders \(8\) and \(12\), and the maximal negative-type exponent decays as \(1/(k\log(4/3))\) along existing orders.
