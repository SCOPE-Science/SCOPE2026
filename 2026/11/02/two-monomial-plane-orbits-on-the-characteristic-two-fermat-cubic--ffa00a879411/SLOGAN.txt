The characteristic-two Fermat cubic fourfold has \(891\) geometric planes in two coordinate-monomial orbits of \(405\) pairing and \(486\) hexacode-type planes. Their respective line-, point-, and disjointness-incidence quotient matrices are \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}24&18\\15&27\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}156&180\\150&186\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), and \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}224&288\\240&272\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
