For primes \(p\equiv5\pmod8\) with primitive root \(2\), the minimum cyclic runs of a necklace with completely unclustered Burrows–Wheeler output is exactly \((p+3)/2\) at **both** new lengths \(p+2\) and \(p+3\), over every alphabet of size at least three. Two explicit ternary last-column patterns create the required single BWT cycle by a short splice of a modular-doubling permutation.
