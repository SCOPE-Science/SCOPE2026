For the smooth characteristic-\(23\) genus-two pencil \(y^2=x^5+x+t\), the only nonordinary fibers are the superspecial center \(t=0\) (multiplicity four in the Hasse divisor) and four mutually isomorphic \(23\)-rank-one fibers \(t^4=-2\); every other smooth fiber is ordinary. The four rank-one parameters occur over \(\mathbb F_{23^2}\), not \(\mathbb F_{23}\).
