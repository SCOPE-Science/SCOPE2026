For a positive operator with a single spiked eigenvalue in its second input, the near-spectral geometric endpoint has at most two outlying spectral values in every Hilbert-space dimension, and both are determined by the single scalar \(\langle Au,u\rangle\langle A^{-1}u,u\rangle\). The sharp possible spectral amplification and its attainment are governed exactly by the endpoint eigenspaces of the first input, including when continuous spectrum prevents attainment.
