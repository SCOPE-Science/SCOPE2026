The two periodic binary inputs \((0^p1)^n\) and \((1^p0)^n\), for all \(p,n\ge2\), have exactly \(\binom{2((p-1)n+1)}{(p-1)n+1}\) shortest common supersequences, each of length \(2pn+1\). A forced-step bijection with balanced words proves that this nontrivially overlapping pair family saturates the universal binomial counting bound.
