In the first three Hermite modes, two narrow symmetric observation windows have exactly two optimizer-parity reversals as their separation grows: even, then odd, then even. The squared switching centers are \(\frac12+\frac{\varepsilon^2}{3}+O(\varepsilon^4)\) and \(\frac32+\frac{\varepsilon^2}{3}+O(\varepsilon^4)\), revealing the same leading finite-width drift at both parity transitions.
