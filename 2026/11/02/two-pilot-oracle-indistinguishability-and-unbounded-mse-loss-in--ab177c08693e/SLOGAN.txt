Two strictly positive matrix spectra can agree exactly at both Frobenius-error pilot ranks yet require opposite rank-versus-probe allocations for preconditioned Gaussian log-determinant estimation. Even with \(\kappa(I+A)<5/4\) and exact three-step Lanczos quadratic forms, every deterministic rule using only those pilots has an unbounded ideal mean-square-error competitive ratio; this is not a claim about the implemented randomized Nyström detector.
