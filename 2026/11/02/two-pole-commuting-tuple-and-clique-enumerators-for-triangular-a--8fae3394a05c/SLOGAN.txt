For every finite commutative chain ring with residue-field size \(q\) and length \(\ell\), the entire ordered pairwise-commuting-tuple sequence of its two-by-two upper triangular algebra is \(S_\ell(q)q^{2\ell k}-(S_\ell(q)-1)q^{(2\ell-1)k}\), where \(S_\ell(q)=1+q+\cdots+q^\ell\). The complete clique polynomial is \(S_\ell(q)(1+z)^{q^{2\ell}}-(S_\ell(q)-1)(1+z)^{q^{2\ell-1}}\), uniformly across chain rings of different characteristics with the same \((q,\ell)\).
