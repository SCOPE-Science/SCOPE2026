For every even polygonal Danielewski family from the 2026 recursive construction, the complete extension-field point counts and local zeta function have explicit closed forms determined by the polygonal index modulo eight. The zeta function has only the poles \(q^{-1}\) and \(q^{-2}\), and the extension-degree counts are generally not obtained by substituting \(q^r\) into the original polygonal-number formula.
