For a two-temperature Brownian gyrator completely reset to an off-axis point, the stationary full-path signed area (including the reset chords) can reverse direction twice as reset rate grows. There is an exact necessary-and-sufficient threshold for the two reversals and a universal product law \(r_-r_+=1-u^2\); for \(u=3/5\), a concrete example reverses at \(2/5\) and \(8/5\).
