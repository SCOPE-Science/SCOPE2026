For an explicitly prescribed alternation of two leader-rooted follower trees, a four-state herding filter can be Schur stable in both frozen networks while the two-step switched network has an exponentially growing real mode. The exact PAT parameter window \(75/32<\beta<25/8\) gives a structural warning about extrapolating frozen-tree stability to unconstrained time-dependent predecessor histories, without making a claim about the original geometric selection rule.
