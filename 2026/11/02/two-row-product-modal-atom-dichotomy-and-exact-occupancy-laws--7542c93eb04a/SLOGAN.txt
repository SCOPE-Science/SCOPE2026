On a \(2\times m\) product of complete equivalence frames, the generated bimodal Boolean algebra has exactly \(|E|\) atoms if its occupied column-colour support is transpose-invariant and \(2|E|\) otherwise; modal depth two always suffices. This dichotomy gives the sharp maximum \(2\min(m,q^2-1)\), a complete Stirling-number occupancy law under independent uniform colours, and explicit examples where adding a column decreases the number of atoms.
