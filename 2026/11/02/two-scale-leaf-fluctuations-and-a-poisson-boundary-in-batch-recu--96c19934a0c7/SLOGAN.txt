In uniform batch-recursive trees, the leaf count and the number of still-intact birth cohorts obey a joint Gaussian law with correlation \(\sqrt{3(m+2)}/(2m+1)\) for fixed batch size \(m\). As batch size grows, the two counts become Gaussian on different independent scales while the exceptional internal-recruitment count undergoes a sharp Poisson crossover when \(m\) is proportional to the number of rounds.
