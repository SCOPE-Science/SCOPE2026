In the minimal open exclusion chain with reversible particle pauses, the two-site exit-count Fano factor lies below, then above, then below its one-site counterpart as pausing increases, with exactly two crossings whenever \(0<u\le1/10\). In the slow-unpausing limit the crossings scale as \(k_-/u^2\to7/15\) and \(k_+/u\to1.113211611\ldots\), while the globally optimal two-site noise is asymptotic to \(0.23046896985\ldots/u\).
