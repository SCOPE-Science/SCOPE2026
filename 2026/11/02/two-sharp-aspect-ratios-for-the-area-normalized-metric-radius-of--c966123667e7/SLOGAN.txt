Among flat Klein bottles, the sharp area-normalized minimax geodesic radius satisfies \(R^2/A\ge 2/(3\sqrt3)\), with equality at exactly two nonisometric aspect ratios \(a/b=1/(2\sqrt3)\) and \(a/b=\sqrt3/2\). The complete pointwise farthest-distance profile has an exact switching boundary and a single minimizing center curve.
