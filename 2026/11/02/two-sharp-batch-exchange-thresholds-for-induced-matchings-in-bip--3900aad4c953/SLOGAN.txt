For every \(m,n\ge3\), the \(mn\) maximum induced matchings of the once-subdivided \(K_{m,n}\) form a rook graph only when simultaneous exchanges can replace at least \(\max\{m,n\}\) edges; smaller budgets leave either isolated configurations or multiple complete components. This gives unbounded sharp batch-mobility thresholds in bipartite girth-eight graphs of average degree below four.
