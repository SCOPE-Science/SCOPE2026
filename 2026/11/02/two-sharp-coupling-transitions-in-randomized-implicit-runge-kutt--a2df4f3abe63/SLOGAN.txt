For two uniform-randomized implicit Runge–Kutta steps, the best mean-square coupling pairs opposite ranks of squared amplification, not necessarily opposite stage values. Ordinary stage antithetic coupling is exactly optimal for nondimensional decay \(r\le1\) and strictly loses for every \(r>1\); at \(r=2\) the exact optimal two-step squared factor is about \(0.00187992\), nearly ten times below the antithetic factor.
