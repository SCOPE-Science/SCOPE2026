For the regular cross-polytope symmetrically clipped by \(|x_1|\le t\), both unique extremal-volume ellipsoids have explicit semiaxes but undergo **different** contact transitions. Their enclosing-to-inscribed volume ratio has one sharp interior minimum, attained at the unique solution of \((d-1)t^2(2-t)=(1-t)^2(1+t)\), and the minimum divided by the uncut ratio is asymptotic to \((2ed)^{-1/4}\).
