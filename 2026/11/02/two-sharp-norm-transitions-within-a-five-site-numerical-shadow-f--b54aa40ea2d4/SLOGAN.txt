Among strictly positive five-site nilpotent weighted shifts having exactly the same complete numerical shadow, the sharp operator-norm infimum is an explicit three-branch function of the two positive eigenvalues of the Hermitian part. The threshold \(q_*=(7-3\sqrt5)/2\) separates nonattainment from two distinct optimizers, while \(q=1/3\) is a second transition to a continuous family of optimizers.
