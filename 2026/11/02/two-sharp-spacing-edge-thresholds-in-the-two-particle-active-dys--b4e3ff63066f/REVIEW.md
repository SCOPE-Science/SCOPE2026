# Scientific review: two-particle active Dyson spacing edges

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem gives the exact stationary support and sharp asymptotic tail laws under the stated positive-parameter, zero-thermal-noise assumptions. Its proof is reconstructed from the gap drift, generator and conservative mode-transport equations. At either endpoint, positivity of the incoming flux and the strictly inward velocity of the other modes bounds their tail masses by one and two extra powers of the boundary distance. The attracting-mode boundary tail then solves a scalar first-order logarithmic asymptotic equation with an integrable error, yielding a finite strictly positive leading constant, not just an exponent. Boundary equality cases, conditional mode population ratios and the two sharp thresholds follow without division by quantities that can vanish at isolated parameters. Exact symbolic identities and independent finite-volume generator diagnostics passed. Finite tests supplement rather than replace the all-parameter proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Touzo, Le Doussal and Schehr (2023, arXiv:2302.02937) establish the two-particle drift and fixed-point endpoints, and study singularities of *particle positions*; the new theorem instead describes the *interparticle spacing*. Le Doussal, Majumdar and Schehr (2021, arXiv:2105.11838) already write down general relative-coordinate stationary equations, show a repulsive inverse-distance example in their force diagram, and solve an attractive harmonic example; the present result does not claim those established methods or examples as new. The 2023 same-author follow-up focuses on position covariances, while Dhar and colleagues (2019) study a distinct one-particle two-state confining problem. No examined result states or directly establishes the exact two different log-repulsive gap-edge exponents, the sharp three-regime classification and the state-resolved endpoint asymptotics. The general 2021 equations are the closest possible implicit coverage, so priority remains qualified pending independent subject-specialist comparison.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Particle spacing is a natural microscopic statistic of repulsive particle systems. Two distinct geometry-dependent edge thresholds distinguish singular close spacing from depleted wide spacing and supply a complete exact classification, rather than a small numerical gain. The reciprocal threshold sum and conditional mode hierarchy are structural quantities with direct implications for how orientation-conditioned observations concentrate near extreme gaps.

## Limits and nearest literature

The proof applies to two noncrossing particles in the zero-temperature repulsive model, not a large-particle gas or a generic multi-particle extension. It establishes precise powers but not elementary formulas for their positive amplitudes. Especially relevant existing work is arXiv:2105.11838 (general two-particle stationary differential system) and arXiv:2302.02937 (the same active-Dyson dynamics, but different marginal observables). The possibility that a broader earlier PDMP edge theorem implicitly covers these formulas is an unresolved bibliographic risk, not a correctness uncertainty.
