In the two-particle repulsive active Dyson model, the stationary spacing density has unequal endpoint exponents \(\beta_\pm=\gamma(R\pm v)/(\mu R)\), where \(R=\sqrt{v^2+2\mu g}\). Its two sharp rate thresholds obey \(\gamma_+<\mu<\gamma_-\) and \(1/\gamma_++1/\gamma_-=2/\mu\), leaving a genuine interval where close-packing fluctuations are singular but wide-spacing fluctuations vanish.
