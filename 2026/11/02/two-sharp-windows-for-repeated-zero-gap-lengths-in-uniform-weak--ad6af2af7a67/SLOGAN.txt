Uniform weak compositions exhibit two distinct transitions for equal-length zero gaps: the first repeated pair emerges around mass \(n^{1/3}\), repeated gaps persist with high probability throughout \(n^{1/3}\ll m\ll n^2\), and repetitions disappear around \(n^2\). The initial window has a Poisson collision count, whereas the terminal window has the law of unordered pairs drawn from a Poisson number of isolated zero gaps.
