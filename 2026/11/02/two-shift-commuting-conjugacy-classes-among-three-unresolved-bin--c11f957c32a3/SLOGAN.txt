The three non-bipermutive two-to-one binary cellular automata singled out in a recent five-site census form exactly two shift-commuting topological conjugacy classes: the first two are \(PD\) and \(DP\) for a reversible four-site map \(P\) and the binary difference map \(D\), while a fifth-iterate periodic-point count separates the third. Hence the three open locally-eventually-onto decisions require at most two independent answers; no decision itself is settled.
