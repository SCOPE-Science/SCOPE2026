For a harmonically confined Brownian gas undergoing simultaneous Poisson resetting, the stationary fraction of particles inside a fixed central interval has an exact compact-support law with a reset-rate-dependent lower-edge exponent. At finite population, the probability of falling below that equilibrium edge decays as \(N^{-r/(4\mu)}\), whereas the probability that **all** particles are inside decays only as \(ra^2/(4D\log N)\).
