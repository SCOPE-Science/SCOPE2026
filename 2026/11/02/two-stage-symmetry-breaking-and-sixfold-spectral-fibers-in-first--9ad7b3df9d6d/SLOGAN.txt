For the normalized first Hermite STFT, concentration on the unbounded strip \([-a,a]\times\mathbb R\) is exactly a multiplication operator whose sharp norm undergoes a discontinuous change of maximizing locations at a unique half-width \(a_*\approx1.0776636283762369\), after the center changes to a local maximum for \(a>1\). Its purely absolutely continuous spectrum has an explicit two-, four-, or sixfold multiplicity profile according to the strip width and spectral level.
