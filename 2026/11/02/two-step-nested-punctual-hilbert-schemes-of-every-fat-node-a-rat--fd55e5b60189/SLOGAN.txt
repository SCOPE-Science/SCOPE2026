For every fat node \(x^uy^v=0\) over \(\mathbb C\), the Euler series of two-step nested punctual Hilbert schemes with colengths \(n\) and \(n+2\) has an explicit finite rational formula with denominator dividing \((q;q)_u(q;q)_v\). The nonreduced symmetric node \(x^2y^2=0\) gives \(\bigl(2-q+2q^3+q^4+q^5+3q^6+2q^8\bigr)/\bigl((1-q)^2(1-q^2)^2\bigr)\), and the general coefficient growth is of degree \(u+v-1\).
