For three-color cyclic cellular automata on every cocktail-party graph with at least six vertices, all trajectories enter a fixed point or primitive three-cycle after at most two updates, and the six first-entry populations have explicit formulas. Uniform random initial colorings are already periodic with probability \(1-O(n(4/9)^n)\), while the exact consensus probability is \((3\cdot4^n+6n-3)/9^n\).
