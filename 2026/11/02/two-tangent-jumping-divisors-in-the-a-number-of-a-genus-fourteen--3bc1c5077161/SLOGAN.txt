For the genus-fourteen characteristic-five Artin–Schreier family \(y^5-y=x^8+bx^6+ax^4\), the Jacobian \(a\)-number is \(8\) generically, rises to \(9\) on two tangent parameter divisors, and is \(10\) only at their common origin. The genus-six involution quotient has constant \(a\)-number \(4\), so the entire jump occurs in an eight-dimensional Prym factor.
