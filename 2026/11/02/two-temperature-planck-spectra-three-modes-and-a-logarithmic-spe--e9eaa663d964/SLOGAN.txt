For two exact Planck frequency spectra whose temperatures differ by a factor \(R\) and whose hot-to-cold emitting-area ratio is \(R^{-2}\), sufficiently large \(R\) creates exactly two nondegenerate spectral peaks separated by one valley. The valley lies at \(\log R+2\log\log R-\log2+o(1)\) in cold-temperature frequency units and has spectral height asymptotic to \((\log R)^2/R\).
