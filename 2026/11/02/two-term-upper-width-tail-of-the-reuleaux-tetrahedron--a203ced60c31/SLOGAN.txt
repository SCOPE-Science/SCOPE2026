For a uniformly oriented caliper direction of the unit Reuleaux tetrahedron, the probability of being within \(\varepsilon\) of the classical largest width is \(6\varepsilon/(\sqrt3-\sqrt2)+3\sqrt3\,\varepsilon^2/[2(\sqrt3-\sqrt2)^3]+O(\varepsilon^3)\). The sharp two-term tail follows from the quadratic geometry of the six opposite-edge maximizing directions, not from the previously known maximum alone.
