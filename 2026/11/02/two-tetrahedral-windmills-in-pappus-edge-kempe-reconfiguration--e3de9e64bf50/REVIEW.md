# Scientific review: Pappus edge-Kempe windmills

## Correctness — PASS
Complete finite coverage is established twice using unrelated state generators: lexicographic edge-colour backtracking in `verify_lcf.py`, and recursive perfect-matching enumeration from nine incidence triples in `verify_incidence.py`. The graph has 42 perfect matchings, 20 unlabelled one-factorizations and 120 named colourings; both programs reconstruct the exact 324-switch adjacency certificate and all-source breadth-first metrics. The quotient is checked as two copies of a universal vertex joined to three disjoint triangles. The explicit base-graph automorphism exchanges the components. An independent mathematical argument derives bipartiteness from the odd half-length of every permitted alternating cycle.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Belcastro and Haas (arXiv:1209.1730, 2012) developed class counting; Goedgebeur and Östergård (arXiv:2105.01363, 2021) exhaustively tabulated possible class counts for cubic graphs, including the Pappus order, so the two-class count is **not** isolated as a novel observation. The directly relevant 2017 follow-up by Belcastro and Haas (*Edge-Kempe-equivalence graphs of class-1 regular graphs*, Australas. J. Combin. 69(2), 197–214) defines the exact same reconfiguration graph and derives structural theorems; its publicly available full PDF was inspected, including an exact-title and Pappus search and the worked cyclic-prism example. It does not state a Pappus metric or quotient theorem. The 2012 and 2021 accessible full texts were similarly checked and found not to cover the particular windmill theorem. Public mathematical record searches for Pappus, F018A, labelled/unlabelled Tait colourings, Kempe switching, the two-windmill quotient and four-step eccentricities did not identify an equivalent theorem. Closest accessible prior results concern counts of equivalence classes, not the exact quotient adjacency or metric of a component. The residual risk is an unindexed historical calculation or a stronger unpublished database export, and priority is not certified.

## Value — PASS
The Pappus graph is the unique order-eighteen cubic symmetric graph and the Levi graph of the classical \((9_3)\) configuration. The result determines the full local transformation geometry of its optimal three-edge-colourings: two disconnected sectors, a very small and explicit nontrivial quotient, and unexpectedly bipartite metric lifts of quotients containing triangles. The ten-state windmill identification makes the decomposition usable beyond an opaque census of the number of Kempe classes. This is a deliberately finite, self-contained structural classification rather than a claim of an infinite family.

## Scope and residual risks
All numerical assertions are exhaustively checked over 120 named states; no independence of scholarly review is claimed. Prior literature can contain equivalent unindexed finite computations. Every certificate is finite and describes precisely the specified graph and full alternating-cycle move convention. References: arXiv:1209.1730, Belcastro–Haas (Australas. J. Combin. 69(2), 2017), arXiv:2105.01363, and the standard Pappus-graph LCF representation.
