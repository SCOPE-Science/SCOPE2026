For a periodically replenished susceptible population vaccinated by fixed fractions at each campaign, two campaigns uniquely minimize mean invasion potential when evenly spaced—even if their strengths differ—but unequal strengths shift the unique schedule minimizing peak instantaneous growth. Three unequal-strength campaigns break the two-pulse rule: equally spaced campaigns are not even stationary for mean invasion potential, and equal spacing is stationary exactly when all three pulse strengths coincide.
