The multipath complexes of the fully bidirected complete bipartite graphs \(K_{2,4}\) and \(K_{2,5}\) contain an integral mod-two Moore-space summand, with precise homotopy types \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^7S^2\) and \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{31}S^3\). Both digraphs are single unstable dynamical modules, giving explicit counterexamples to the proposed stability condition for multipath-complex torsion.
