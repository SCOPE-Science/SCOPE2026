**PASS.** The line scheme of this smooth quartic threefold is a natural exceptional example in Fano geometry. Its forty generic doubled components leave the special punctual geometry unsettled: the 240 reduced nodes and 240 additional embedded points of the intrinsic singular scheme determine exactly what happens where two nonreduced components meet.
