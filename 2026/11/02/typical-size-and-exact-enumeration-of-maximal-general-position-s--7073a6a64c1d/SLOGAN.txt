For the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), the full size polynomial of inclusion-maximal general-position sets follows from labeled star-and-triangle partitions, with an exceptional one-uncovered-vertex class. A uniformly sampled maximal set has \(n-n/\log n\cdot(1+o_{\Pr}(1))\) vertices: its deficit is sharply of order \(n/\log n\), although the minimum maximal size is only of order \(n/2\).
