For a uniformly selected inclusion-maximal null subsemigroup of the full transformation monoid on \(n\) labeled points, the rank of its zero is asymptotic to \(n/\log n\), while the logarithm of the subsemigroup's order is asymptotic to \(n\log\log n/2\), both in probability. A marked exponential formula yields these laws and the logarithmic total-count asymptotic \(n\log n-n\log\log n+(\log2-1)n+o(n)\), building on the published maximal-null decomposition.
