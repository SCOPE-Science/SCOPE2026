When every cyclic block has length at least half the circle up to the one-point boundary, the full transversal-size distribution is ultra-log-concave, with exact polynomial \((1+z)^n-1-nz(1+z)^{n-q-1}\). This proves Galvin--Zhang's ultra-log-concavity conjecture for all cycle powers \(C_n^\ell\) satisfying \(n\le4\ell+3\).
