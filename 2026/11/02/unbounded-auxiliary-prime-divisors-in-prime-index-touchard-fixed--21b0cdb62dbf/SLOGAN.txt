For every prime \(p\), the universal fixed divisor of \(T_p(z)-2z\) contains exactly one factor of \(p\), but its quotient by \(p\) is unbounded along the primes. Every auxiliary prime \(q\) divides that quotient for infinitely many prime indices \(p\equiv q\pmod{q^q-1}\), and the extra factors \(2\) and \(3\) admit sharp index-congruence criteria.
