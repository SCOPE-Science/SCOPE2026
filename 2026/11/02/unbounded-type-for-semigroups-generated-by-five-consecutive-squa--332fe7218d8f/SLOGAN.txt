Five consecutive-square generators support unbounded numerical-semigroup type: for every \(k\ge4\), the semigroup generated by \((12k)^2,(12k+1)^2,\ldots,(12k+4)^2\) has at least \(2k\) explicitly constructed pseudo-Frobenius numbers. A uniform coefficient-and-moment obstruction proves that each proposed number lies outside the semigroup while all five generator translates lie inside.
