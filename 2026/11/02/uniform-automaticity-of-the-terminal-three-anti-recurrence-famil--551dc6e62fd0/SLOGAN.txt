For every integer \(a\ge2\), the anti-recurrence generated by the complementary-pair form \(aB_{2n-1}+3B_{2n}\) has exactly one term in each interval of length \(2a+7\), and its normalized difference sequence is \((a+3)\)-automatic. An explicit substitution with at most \(2a+4\) states proves a full infinite family beyond the previously established terminal-coefficient-at-most-two theorem.
