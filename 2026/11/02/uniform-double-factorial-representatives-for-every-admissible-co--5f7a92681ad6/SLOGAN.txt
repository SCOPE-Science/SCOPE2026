For every overlap \(m\ge3\) and length \(K>2m\), the explicit consecutive pattern \((1,\ldots,m-1,K-1,K,m,\ldots,K-2)\) has exactly \((2j-1)!!\) prescribed-overlap packings with \(j\) copies. A separator-chain argument gives a uniform proof and resolves an open construction question from Blitvić–Kammoun–Steingrímsson (2026).
