For every even \(k\ge4\), the exact number of residue classes modulo \(2^n\) expressible as sums of at most \(t\) \(k\)-th powers has an explicit geometric recurrence with an exactly periodic correction. The \(2\)-adic minimal-length law assigns the same probability to each of the first \(2^{\nu_2(k)+2}-1\) lengths, with a separately determined final atom.
