For every normalized Pell-type trace \(T_{n+2}=2uT_{n+1}+T_n\) with \(u\geq1\), the primes for which the local \(p\)-part fails the Dold congruences are exactly the odd primes satisfying \(p\equiv3\pmod4\) and \(\bigl(\frac{u^2+1}{p}\bigr)=-1\), a set of density \(1/4\). All local \(p\)-parts admit explicit optimal repair multipliers, and the sole dyadic exception is even \(u\).
