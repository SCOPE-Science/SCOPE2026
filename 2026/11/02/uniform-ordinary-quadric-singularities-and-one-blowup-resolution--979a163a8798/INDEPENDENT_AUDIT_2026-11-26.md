## correctness

PASS

The two independent quadrics form a regular sequence; on the nonzero-u locus the two linear equations in v are independent, giving an irreducible vector-bundle chart family over projective u-space. The all-v linear subspace has codimension r−2, so cannot add a component when r≥3. A dependent Jacobian row combination forces u=0 by full row rank of each block. Its remaining equations give the geometric progressions parametrizing the scroll (the pencil parameter is recovered from a nonzero block). On the chart normalizing a last v coordinate, eliminating the corresponding u gives the bilinear equation with coordinates v_{i,j}−αv_{i,j+1}; the triangular inverse keeps α and the other block tips free. The reversed chart covers the opposite endpoint; a nonzero block on the scroll has an eligible tip in one of these two directions. These are polynomial product charts, so the Jacobian ideal on them is precisely all transverse coordinates, proving reducedness scheme-theoretically. Cohen–Macaulayness and regularity in codimension one give normality; complete-intersection adjunction gives Gorenstein and Fano. The blowup is locally the product of the affine factor with the total space of O_Q(−1) over the smooth projective quadric Q of dimension 2r−4. The smooth irreducible fibers and irreducible scroll give one exceptional divisor. The ambient blowup contribution 2r−3 minus multiplicity 2 gives discrepancy 2r−5; positivity on the smooth resolution gives terminality. At r=2 the transverse equation is a crossing of two axes, as claimed separately. The supplied script was inspected, not executed; finite symbolic identities or F3 point counts are not scheme or discrepancy proofs.
