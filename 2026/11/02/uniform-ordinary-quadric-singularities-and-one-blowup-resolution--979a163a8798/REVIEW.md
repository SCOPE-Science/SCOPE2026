# Scientific review: uniform singularities of constant-rank pencil base loci

## Correctness — PASS

For every partition into positive blocks, the two canonical quadrics form a complete intersection. Their Jacobian drops rank exactly on the known kernel scroll. On either the top- or bottom-coordinate chart covering every point of that scroll, eliminating one variable gives an **exact polynomial coordinate isomorphism** to a smooth \(h\)-dimensional factor times \(\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}U_jV_j=0\). The inverse coordinate transformations are triangular, so no formal-approximation or genericity gap remains. These charts prove the Jacobian singular *scheme* is reduced, and allow the blowup, discrepancy \(2r-5\), terminality and Fano arguments. A standard-library checker verified 432 symbolic elimination/inverse identities and complete small-field Jacobian-support checks for seven partition examples. Computational checks corroborate but do not replace the general proof. The characteristic-zero and \(r\ge3\) limits are explicit.

## Originality — PASS, subject to priority limitations

Boralevi--Mezzetti (arXiv:2206.00503v1, Sections 2--4) classify the pencils, supply canonical block equations and identify the scroll as the *support* of the singular locus; these are not claimed as new. The complete accessible HTML text was examined for claims of a reduced singular scheme, a Zariski-local quadratic product normal form, blowup resolution, exceptional quadric bundle and discrepancy, none of which it states. Wang's regular pencil study (arXiv:1302.2385, abstract and introductory hypotheses) requires at least one nonsingular quadric, unlike the constant-rank pencils here. Searches for the local equivalent formulations, blowup and terminal structure produced no implication-equivalent result, and no identified published result covers this theorem. The remaining risk is an unindexed or older singular-pencil treatment that derives the same normal form implicitly. The absence of a search result is not a proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The singular scroll was already known, but support alone does not determine transverse analytic type, reducedness of the singular scheme, whether a blowup resolves, or the discrepancy. A single partition-independent local equation settles all four questions uniformly for the full natural classification, proving terminal Gorenstein Fano singularities for every rank \(2r\ge6\) and a sharp contrasting rank-four boundary. The explicit resolution supplies exceptional-fibre dimensions and discrepancies usable in higher-dimensional birational geometry; this is not a cosmetic rephrasing of the published support calculation.

## Closest work and limitations

The closest source is Boralevi--Mezzetti, *Pencils of singular quadrics of constant rank and their orbits*, arXiv:2206.00503v1 (2022-06-01), Sections 2--4. Comparison literature includes Wang, *Maximal linear spaces contained in the base loci of pencils of quadrics*, arXiv:1302.2385. Classification of singular pencils is a classical subject; older literature not exhaustively available could affect priority. No claims are made for characteristic two, pencils with fixed common kernel, or arbitrary rank-varying families. Formal independent assessment has not occurred.
