Every nondegenerate complex constant-rank \(2r\) quadric pencil with \(r\ge3\) has a base intersection whose entire singular scheme is a reduced smooth scroll, and whose transverse singularity is the same ordinary quadratic cone of dimension \(2r-3\), regardless of the partition class. Blowing up that scroll once resolves the intersection with smooth quadric exceptional fibres and discrepancy \(2r-5\).
