For every odd modulus \(m\), successive Stern-polynomial values \((B_n(-2),B_{n+1}(-2))\) are uniformly distributed over the \(J_2(m)\) primitive residue pairs, with all other pairs impossible. In particular, the exact natural density of zeros modulo \(m\) is \(\bigl(m\prod_{p\mid m}(1+p^{-1})\bigr)^{-1}\).
