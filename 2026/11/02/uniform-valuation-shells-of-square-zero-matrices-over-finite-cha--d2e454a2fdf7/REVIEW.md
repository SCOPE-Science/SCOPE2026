# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The square-zero condition is invariant under conjugacy. A primitive square-zero matrix over any finite commutative chain ring is conjugate to the standard size-two nilpotent Jordan block: choose a vector with primitive image, and its image and preimage give a basis. The invertible centralizer count is \((q^k-q^{k-1})q^k\) at level \(k\), so the primitive orbit size is \((q^2-1)q^{2k-2}\). For entry valuation \(s\) with \(2s<d\), passage from length \(d\) to \(d-2s\) imposes the square-zero equation while creating \(q^{4s}\) lifts. The exponents cancel to give the same \((q^2-1)q^{2d-2}\) in every shallow shell. Deeper matrices square to zero automatically. The limit law follows from the bounded relative deep mass. Twenty independent small-ring brute-force instances agree.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected literature

Bogdanić–Đurić–Koljančić–Oblak–Šivic (arXiv:2212.08425) identify the relevant equal-Jordan-block centralizer and classify attainable nilpotent Jordan partitions; the inspected main theorem and proof do not supply the valuation-shell counts. Carnevale–Shechter–Voll (arXiv:1709.02717) count matrices satisfying the linear trace condition by image invariants, rather than the nonlinear square-zero condition. Cheong–Liang–Strand (arXiv:2209.03626) require square-free reduction of their matrix polynomial, which fails here. Brawley's older work on Drazin indices might imply the aggregated square-zero total for some coefficient rings, but the nilpotency index is constant across the nonzero square-zero locus and does not imply the individual valuation-shell counts. No equivalent shell law was established by the checked searches. A complete priority guarantee is not claimed: the full text of Brawley's work was not available, and earlier valuation refinements could exist under other terminology.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The valuation depth is a canonical invariant on matrices over a local principal ring, not an arbitrary parameter. In the two-equal-Jordan-block centralizer it records how far a commuting matrix lies inside the radical filtration. Equal cardinalities for all shallow square-zero layers are a structural cancellation, not implied by existence of the possible Jordan types. The formula gives a complete ring-independent count and a nontrivial normalized limiting law across all residue characteristics and both mixed- and equal-characteristic chain rings. No higher-dimensional conclusion is inferred.

## Closest literature and limitations

The main motivating source is arXiv:2212.08425, first posted December 16, 2022. The principal risk is an older unlocated shell-refined count; the 1982 Drazin-index article was only assessed from its abstract and bibliographic information. The finding concerns only size-two matrices over commutative chain rings and resolves the deepest ideal only as a single block.
