For every finite commutative chain ring of length \(d\) and residue size \(q\), the number of square-zero \(2\times2\) matrices in each valuation shell below \(\lceil d/2\rceil\) is the same: \((q^2-1)q^{2d-2}\). This uniformity yields an exact ring-independent total and a universal uniform limit law for normalized valuations.
