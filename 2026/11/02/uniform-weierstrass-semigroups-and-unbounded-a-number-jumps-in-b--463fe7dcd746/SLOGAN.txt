For characteristic-two biquadratic Artin–Schreier covers \(y^2+y=x^M\), \(z^2+z=\alpha x^M+\lambda x^D\) with odd \(M/2<D<M\), the complete pole semigroup jumps from \(\langle4,M\rangle\) at \(\lambda=0\) to \(\langle4,2D,3M-2D\rangle\) elsewhere. Along \(M=8s+5\), \(D=6s+3\), this creates an unbounded \(a\)-number jump of \(s+1\) while all three intermediate quadratic quotients have unchanged genera and ramification breaks.
