For uniformly spaced subsequences over a \(q\)-letter alphabet, a greedy spacing-aware arch decomposition gives an exact rational generating function for the entire universality-index distribution. Every shortest host for all length-\(k\) patterns is a concatenation of \(k\) permutations separated by arbitrary blocks of length \(s-1\), and there are exactly \((q!)^kq^{(s-1)(k-1)}\) such hosts.
