Descartes' rule, endpoint positivity, and the positive activity-root construction prove one unique fold and exactly two simple positive confinement crossovers above it. ## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Shee's arXiv:2409.06920v1/v2 supplies the full forward moments and numerically illustrates two trapping zero crossings for a selected example. Those are not claimed as new. Chaudhuri and Dhar's arXiv:2005.14234 studies the non-reset harmonic ABP, so its qualitative reentrant crossover does not settle the positive-reset classification. The present contribution is the exhaustive root count, unique activity onset for each positive reset rate, square-root double-zero unfolding, and two limiting scaling laws. Actual source texts, publicly available research records, semantic aliases, and previously accessible findings were compared. A broader unlocated theorem or treatment of these results as implicit in the forward formula remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result converts a few observed sign changes in the original active-matter study into a complete, experimentally meaningful parameter-space boundary and supplies physically useful scaling in weak and strong resetting. The classification is specific but natural: it predicts whether changing harmonic confinement can ever make the stationary radial kurtosis negative and how transitions are created. It concerns a moment statistic, not Gaussianity or a thermodynamic nonanalytic phase transition.
