For the scalar Gaussian-target multiscale derivative-free Langevin step, Gaussian OU probe persistence creates a unique sharp mean-square instability threshold even when fresh-probe stepping is stable. The principal-eigenvalue frontier is strictly increasing with temporal correlation for every finite probe budget; in particular, \(J=1\), \(ah=1/2\) is stable for independent probes but already unstable at correlation \(\rho=1/2\), witnessed by the exact bound \(25/24>1\).
