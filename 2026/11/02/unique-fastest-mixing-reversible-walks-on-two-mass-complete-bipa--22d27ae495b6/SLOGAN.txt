For a complete bipartite network whose stationary law is uniform separately on each side, the reversible fastest-mixing Markov kernel is globally **unique**, even when every cross-edge flow may vary independently. Its optimal SLEM depends only on the smaller side's total stationary mass \(a\), with a sharp transition at \(a=1/3\): ordinary Metropolis--Hastings is optimal exactly when \(a\leq1/3\).
