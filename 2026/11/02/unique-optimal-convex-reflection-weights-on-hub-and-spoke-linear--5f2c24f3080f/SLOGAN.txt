For a hub-and-spoke family of \(n\) hyperplanes, the unique best positive probabilities for a simultaneous convex reflection average assign mass \([1+(n-2)c]/[n+(n-2)c]\) to the hub and equal masses \(1/[n+(n-2)c]\) to the spokes. For each fixed overlap \(0<c<1\), these weights improve the sharp Euclidean contraction gap from order \(n^{-2}\) under uniform masses to order \(n^{-1}\).
