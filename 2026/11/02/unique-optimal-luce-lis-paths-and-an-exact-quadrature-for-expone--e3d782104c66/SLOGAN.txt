For Luce permutations with exponential weights \(e^{-\beta(k-1)/n}\), the limiting longest-increasing-subsequence constant and its **unique** optimal continuum path have an explicit one-parameter quadrature formula valid against all right-continuous nondecreasing competitors. The constant is \(1+\beta/8+O(\beta^2)\) for small \(\beta\), and grows as \(\sqrt{\beta/e}\) for large \(\beta\).
