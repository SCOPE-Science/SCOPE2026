For a phase-rotated qubit with independent Bernoulli resetting to \(|+\rangle\), the stationary **unconditioned** phase quantum Fisher information has exactly one globally optimal reset probability for every nonresonant gate phase. The optimum is \(2/3\) at a half-turn and approaches \(\sqrt{(1+\sqrt3)/2}\) times the angular detuning near an identity-gate resonance, where the optimized information grows as \((2\sqrt3/9)\) times inverse squared detuning.
