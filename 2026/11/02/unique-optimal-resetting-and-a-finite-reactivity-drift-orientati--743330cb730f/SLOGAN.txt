For a Brownian particle reset toward a finite-reactivity point trap, the mean absorption time has exactly one global optimal reset frequency for every constant drift; reversing a nonzero drift always makes the optimal rate strictly larger when it points away from the target. In the strong-drift limit, both optimal rates have leading term \(v/\ell\), but their difference converges to the nonzero constant \(\kappa/(e\ell)\).
