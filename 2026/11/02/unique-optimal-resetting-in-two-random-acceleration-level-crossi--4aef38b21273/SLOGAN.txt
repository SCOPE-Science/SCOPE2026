For a completely Poisson-reset random-acceleration process, each of two natural level-crossing experiments—crossing a displaced level from rest and crossing zero from a nonzero reset velocity—has exactly one globally optimal resetting rate. The dimensionless optima are approximately \(0.520327689493271\) and \(0.505943004050176\), with uniqueness proved by model-specific Kac--Rice kernel sign inequalities and a strict Laplace-transform ordering argument.
