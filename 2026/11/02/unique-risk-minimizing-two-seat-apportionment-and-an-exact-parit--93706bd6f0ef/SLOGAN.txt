For \(n\ge3\) equal-entitlement parties dividing exactly two residual seats, the unique proportional lottery minimizing fixed-order envy variance has variance \(0\) for even \(n\) and \(2/n\) for odd \(n\), realized by mirror pairs with a central triangle only in the odd case. An anonymous lottery necessarily has variance \((n+1)(n-2)/6\), revealing a sharp parity-dependent symmetry-versus-risk tradeoff.
