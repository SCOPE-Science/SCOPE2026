For the half-integer weighted modified-Bessel endpoint quotient, every exponential tilt \(0<\gamma<1\) has exactly one sharp maximizing argument. The optimizer starts at \(10\gamma+O(\gamma^3)\) and, near critical tilt, develops the double-logarithmic scale \((1-\gamma)x_\gamma=\log(1/(1-\gamma))+2\log\log(1/(1-\gamma))+o(1)\); the best constant exceeds \(1/(1-\gamma)\) by a sharp logarithmic correction.
