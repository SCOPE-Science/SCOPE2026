For a harmonically confined active Brownian particle with simultaneous position-and-orientation Poisson resetting and matched trap/rotational relaxation rates, the stationary longitudinal skewness changes from negative to positive at exactly one resetting rate for every positive activity. The critical rate increases strictly with activity and always lies between \(\sqrt2-1\) and the unique positive root (approximately \(1.92291776319\)) of an explicit seventh-degree polynomial.
