# Reproducibility and limits

Execute `python verify.py` after installing the dependency in `requirements.txt`. The checks are exact symbolic identities in the rational function field of the positive rate and activity variables, with separate generator-recursion and reset-age Gaussian-correlation derivations. The certificate also checks the derivative of the activity boundary, the polynomial root-sign structure, the global positivity bound and representative parameter signs. The checker is intended to reveal transcription, denominator, normalization and algebra mistakes; the proof in `RESULT.md` establishes the uncountable-parameter claim analytically.

Domain: positive activity and resetting rate, unit translational diffusion, unit angular diffusion, unit trap relaxation, joint resetting to the origin with fixed orientation. No independent scientific validation or publication-priority certification is implied.
