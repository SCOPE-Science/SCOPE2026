In a positive-feedback rational planar map with no fixed point, one unique initial condition separates upward and downward ballistic escape, yet the separating trajectory itself grows as \(y_n\sim(9ad/2)^{1/3}n^{2/3}\). The exact \(2/3\) exponent and \(n^{-1/3}\) transverse correction quantify the otherwise hidden critical boundary between escape basins.
