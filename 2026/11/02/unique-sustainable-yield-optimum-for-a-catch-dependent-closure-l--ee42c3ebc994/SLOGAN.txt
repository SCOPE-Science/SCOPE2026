For logistic pulse fishing with a closed-season duration proportional to the gap between capacity-scaled post-harvest stock and current post-harvest stock, there is exactly one effort maximizing sustainable catch per unit time whenever the dimensionless feedback slope satisfies \(0<A<4\). This maximum strictly decreases with \(A\), remains below \(rK/4\), and has the sharp small-feedback deficit \(rKA^2/768+O(A^3)\).
