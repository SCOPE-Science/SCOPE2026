Every unital \(\mathbb F_q\)-subalgebra of the free cube-zero local ring \(\mathbb F_q[X_1,\ldots,X_d]/(X_1,\ldots,X_d)^3\) is classified by a first-order subspace, a quadratic space containing its symmetric square, and a linear graph map, giving an exact all-dimensions Gaussian-binomial rank polynomial. For fixed field size, the fraction whose first-order image is not a line is asymptotic to \(1/{d\brack1}_q\) as \(d\to\infty\).
