For every pair of nonzero integers \(a,b\), the quotients of values of the binary cubic \(ax^3+by^3\) at integer points are dense in \(\mathbb Q_3\); with \(3\)-adic integer inputs, every element of \(\mathbb Q_3\) is attained exactly as a quotient. This includes both \(x^3+3y^3\) and \(x^3+2y^3\), counterexamples to the non-density alternatives stated in the inspected 2021 cubic-form preprint.
