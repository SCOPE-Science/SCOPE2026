For every monic polynomial \(P\), its nested lemniscate shell \(r\le|P|\le R\) has an exact degree-\(dk-1\) area-Bergman polynomial \(P'P^{k-1}/d\), independent of its topology. Ransford's capacity lower bound has the universal exact shell ratio \(\sqrt{1-(r/R)^{2k}}\) at each multiple degree \(dk\), quantifying an exponentially small effect of the interior lake.
