For fixed-count iid sampling on a line, the inverse of an exponential Gaussian-process kernel matrix has the exact Pareto tail \(\Pr\{\|K^{-1}\|_2>t\}\sim[n(n-1)/\gamma]\int w^2/t\), so its finite moments stop sharply at order one. Removing a positive nugget makes expected inverse norm diverge logarithmically and higher moments by explicit powers, while uniform location sampling uniquely minimizes the leading tail constant.
