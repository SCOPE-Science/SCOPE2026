For arclength-uniform samples on a parallelogram approaching a rectangle at rate \(n|\cos\theta_n|\to\tau\), the normalized sample-diameter loss converges to \(\min\{G_1,\tau+G_2\}\) for independent \(\operatorname{Gamma}(2,1)\) variables. This shape-independent crossover interpolates between mean loss \(5/4\) at an exact rectangle and mean loss \(2\) when one diagonal dominates.
