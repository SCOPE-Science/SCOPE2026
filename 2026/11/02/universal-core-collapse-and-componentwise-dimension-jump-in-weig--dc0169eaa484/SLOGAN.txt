For convex mixtures of internal trust and minimum external trust on any nonzero weighted directed network, the coalition core is empty below coefficient \(1/2\), unique at \(1/2\), and has affine dimension \(n-c\) above \(1/2\), where \(c\) counts positive-weight weak components. Thus a single universal mixing threshold governs existence while the network topology governs the dimension of the stable-allocation family.
