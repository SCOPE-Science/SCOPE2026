**Correctness: PASS.** The invariant set \(S+E+I\le1\) and bounded fomite dynamics supply compactness. The exact dissipative infected-mass identity forces \(E,I,F\to0\); logistic comparison forces \(S\to1\). The infection Jacobian has a simple zero Perron eigenvalue; its stable complement is \(o(W)\) since the forcing is \(o(W)\) and \(W\) varies subexponentially. The susceptible deficit is similarly slaved to the slow mass. This yields \((1/W)'\to\mu K/D^2\) and all four explicit limits. Both the all-seeded quantifier and the zero-direct-transmission boundary are covered.

**Originality: PASS (qualified).** The main 2024 SEIF paper specifies the reproduction threshold and qualitative extinction but not the all-stage \(t^{-1}\) amplitudes. The closely related 2019 environmental-transmission model has different vital dynamics. Generic multistage epidemic results give threshold stability under conditions but do not by themselves supply this exact four-component amplitude. The closest indexed critical-rate results concern different models (date-palm life stages, multiple-exposure SIRS, predator/epidemic systems). Unindexed general center-manifold formulae and an independently derived model-specific specialization remain possible.

**Value: PASS.** The precise amplitude matters for the persistence of subdetectable host infection and fomites near the critical control boundary. It distinguishes environmental clearance's contribution \(\beta_f\rho/\omega^2\) to critical duration from its contribution \(\beta_f\rho/\omega\) to the reproduction threshold. Explicit initial-state-independent limits for four coupled compartments are more informative than simply knowing extinction at threshold; the theorem is not a generic statement that critical systems relax slowly.

**Limitations.** Deterministic homogeneous continuous ODE; strict positivity of specified rates, exact reproduction equality and susceptible seed; no stochastic extinction time or near-threshold crossover formula. The proof and computation apply only to the printed model.
