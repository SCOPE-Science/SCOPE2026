At the critical transmission threshold in a susceptible–exposed–infectious–fomite epidemic, every biologically feasible seeded trajectory with initially positive susceptibles has the same explicit \(t^{-1}\) extinction profile. Its infected-host amplitude is \(D/(\mu K)\), where \(D=1+\mu/\gamma+\beta_f\rho/\omega^2\) and \(K=1+\mu/\gamma+\mu/r\); the environmental reservoir shares the slow decay rather than disappearing exponentially.
