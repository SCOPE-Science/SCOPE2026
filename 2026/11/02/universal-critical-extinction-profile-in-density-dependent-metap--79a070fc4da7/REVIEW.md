# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof establishes strict decay of the Perron population functional for every nonzero initial distribution under density suppression, uses positivity for boundedness and zero limit, then makes a second-order expansion and proves projective Perron alignment using the contracting subdominant invariant space. A reciprocal population functional yields the universal constant. All critical constants and normalizations are explicit. The exact two-patch example's rational coefficient was checked by an executed standalone program. Simulations illustrate, rather than establish, infinite-time behavior.

## Originality

PASS under best-knowledge comparisons, not independently certified. The originating full-text model paper establishes extinction for a strict subcritical Perron eigenvalue and a positive equilibrium above the threshold, but has no statement of the critical algebraic profile and no derivative-split coefficient. Published population-turnover and persistence/extinction work supplies broader threshold context. The accessible abstracts of those general results did not state an equivalent exact coefficient; full-text coverage of all possible generalizations is not established. An equivalent source using nonlinear Perron theory remains a genuine risk.

## Value

PASS. The neutral invasion boundary is ecologically different from exponential decline: any finite nonzero starting population has an asymptotically predictable universal slow-relaxation spatial signature. The coefficient distinguishes the influence of the recruitment curve and of density-dependent emigration. The scope includes arbitrarily many heterogeneous connected patches with bounded Kolmogorov recruitment, while explicitly excluding non-monotone recruitment, time dependence, and stochastic extinction.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
