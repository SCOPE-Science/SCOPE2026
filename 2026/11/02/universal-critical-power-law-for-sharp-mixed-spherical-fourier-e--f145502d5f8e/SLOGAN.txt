In every fixed dimension \(d\ge3\), the sharp global stability coefficient for mixed spherical Fourier extension vanishes at its lower integrability threshold with the universal fractional exponent \(1/d\), even as the optimal operator norm diverges. Its strictly positive exact leading coefficient is a conditionally convergent adjacent-Bessel finite-part integral; after normalization by the squared extension norm, the stability gap vanishes linearly with an explicit positive slope.
