For smooth symmetric confining search/return diffusions, the mean target time approaches the known return-only limit at the sharp rate \(r^{-3/2}\), with coefficient \(4[b_2'(0)-b_1'(0)]\int_0^1\exp(-\int_0^z b_2(y)\,dy)\,dz\). Only the search trap's local slope, not its global shape, enters the leading correction.
