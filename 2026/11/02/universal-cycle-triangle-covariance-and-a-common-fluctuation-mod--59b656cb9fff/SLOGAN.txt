In a uniformly random tournament, the covariance between the cyclic-triangle count and directed \(k\)-cycle count is exactly \((n)_{\underline{k}}/2^{k+2}\), for every \(n\ge k\ge3\). All fixed-length directed-cycle counts have the same leading quadratic edge-fluctuation mode and become perfectly positively correlated as \(n\to\infty\).
