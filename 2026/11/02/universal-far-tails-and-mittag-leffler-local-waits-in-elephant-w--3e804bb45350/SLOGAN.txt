The forward wait to the next nonzero step in a full-memory elephant walk with stops has a Pareto far tail of exponent \(\alpha=1-r\), with the exact same leading constant at every finite observation time and an infinite mean. On its natural scale \(n^{1-\alpha}\), the wait instead converges jointly with the activity count to an exponential variable divided by a Mittag-Leffler variable, yielding a distinct exponent-one tail after the large-time limit.
