Attaching one private leaf to each vertex of a connected vertex- and edge-transitive graph yields a fastest-mixing symmetric Markov chain governed only by the normalized algebraic connectivity \(\alpha/d\): the optimum has a closed radical formula, saturates every core row, and puts positive holding only on leaves. For spectrally slow bases the optimal leaf transition scales as \(\tfrac12\sqrt{\alpha/d}\), while the optimal mixing gap scales as \(\alpha/(2d)\).
