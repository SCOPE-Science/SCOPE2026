A finite partition with sorted block sizes \(a_1,\ldots,a_b\) refines a conjugate of every partition into at most \(k\) blocks exactly when \((k-1)(a_j-1)\leq\sum_{i<j}a_i\) for all \(j\). The sharp maximal ground-set size for \(b\) support blocks follows \(G_k(b+1)=G_k(b)+1+\lfloor G_k(b)/(k-1)\rfloor\), giving an exact minimum support-block count for every finite ground-set size.
