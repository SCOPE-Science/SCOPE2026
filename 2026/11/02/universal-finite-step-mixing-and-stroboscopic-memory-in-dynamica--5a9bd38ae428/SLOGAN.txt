For every connected finite graph with independently refreshed, strictly nondegenerate edge percolation, a fixed-step Laplacian quantum walk converges to an explicit geometry-independent two-block density channel whenever the step duration avoids \(\pi\mathbb Z\). That exclusion is sharp: on the three-site path the asymptotic return probability jumps from \(1/3\) to \(5/9\) at odd multiples of \(\pi\), and to \(1\) at even multiples.
