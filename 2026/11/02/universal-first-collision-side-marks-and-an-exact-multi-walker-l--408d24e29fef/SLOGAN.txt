For any number of independent rate-one walkers on \(K_{m,n}\), the side of their first collision is an independent Bernoulli mark: the part of size \(m\) wins with probability \(n/(m+n)\), regardless of the colliding pair, elapsed time, or starting positions. An exact \((k+1)\)-state birth-death reduction gives the entire collision-time law, with fixed-\(k\) asymptotic rate \(k(k-1)(m^{-1}+n^{-1})/4\).
