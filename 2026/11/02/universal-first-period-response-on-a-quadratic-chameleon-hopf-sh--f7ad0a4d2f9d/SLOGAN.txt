For the quadratic chameleon flow with damping \(b>0\), the Hopf bifurcation is subcritical throughout the entire positive damping range, and its first Lyapunov coefficient equals \(5/(b^2+4)\). The small repelling cycles have a damping-dependent amplitude but the universal leading period law \(T=2\pi(1+\alpha/4)+O_b(\alpha^2)\) as \(\alpha\uparrow0\).
