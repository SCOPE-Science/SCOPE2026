Two harmonically confined particles can have exactly the same stationary position autocovariance but different long-time fluctuations of accumulated squared position: resetting Brownian forcing gives a strictly larger variance rate than covariance-matched Gaussian OU forcing. The enhancement is exactly \(\mathcal R(z)=(z^3+26z^2+31z+10)/((z+2)(z^2+3z+1))\), decreasing strictly from \(5\) to \(1\) as the reset-to-confinement rate ratio \(z\) increases.
