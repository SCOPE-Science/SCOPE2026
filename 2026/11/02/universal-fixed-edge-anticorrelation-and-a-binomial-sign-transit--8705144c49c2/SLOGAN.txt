In \(G(n,m)\), triangle count and induced-open-wedge count are strictly negatively correlated in covariance at every nondegenerate edge count \(3\le m\le\binom n2-2\), with an exact closed factorization. In \(G(n,p)\), the same covariance is instead positive below \(p=2(n-3)/(3n-8)\), exposing a sharp difference between fixed-edge and independent-edge graph nulls.
