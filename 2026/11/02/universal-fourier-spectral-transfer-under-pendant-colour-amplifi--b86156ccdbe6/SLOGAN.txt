For uniformly sampled proper \(q\)-colourings of any graph, adding \(s\) leaves at each vertex transforms every one-site Fourier Gram eigenvalue by one explicit quadratic pair, without changing its nullity. Whenever the core sampling system is independent, decoration strictly worsens its sharp condition number, with squared conditioning growing as \(\frac{b}{(q-1)^4[1-(q-1)^{-2}]}s^2\), where \(b\) is the largest core Gram eigenvalue.
