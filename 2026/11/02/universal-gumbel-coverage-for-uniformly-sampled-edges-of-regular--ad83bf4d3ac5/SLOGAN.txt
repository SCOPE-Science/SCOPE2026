For independent uniformly sampled edges, every sequence of simple regular graphs with degree at least two has the same standard Gumbel vertex-coverage limit and the same order-\(n\) expectation correction, regardless of topology or connectivity. A graph-uniform \(O_x(n^{-1/2})\) Poisson approximation controls the unmarked-vertex count; perfect matchings are the sharp degree-one exception, shifting the cover-time limit by \(\log2\).
