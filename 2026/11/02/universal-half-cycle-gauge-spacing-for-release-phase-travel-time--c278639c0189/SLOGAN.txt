In a positive one-way periodically varying current with even, half-wave-antisymmetric modulation, the spread of tracer arrival times over release phase is uniquely maximized at every odd half-cycle of mean advective distance, independently of waveform details. Its exact maximum is \(4\delta\), where \(\delta=\int_0^{\pi/2-\delta}f(t)\,dt\); full-cycle separations make release phase completely unobservable and exhibit a sharp linear cusp.
