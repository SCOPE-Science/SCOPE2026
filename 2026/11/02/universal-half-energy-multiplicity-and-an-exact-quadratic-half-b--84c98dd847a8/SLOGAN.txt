For every reflection-symmetric positive-weight polynomial space, concentrating degree-\(L\) polynomials on one half of the domain produces exactly \(\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor L/2\rfloor}\binom{d+L-2j-2}{L-2j}\) eigenvalues at one-half, with every other eigenvalue paired across that midpoint. On the uniformly weighted \(d\)-ball, the complete quadratic-band spectrum has just two noncentral singular-value levels with explicit gamma-function formulas, giving a sharp, unique best-concentrated quadratic polynomial in every \(d\ge2\).
