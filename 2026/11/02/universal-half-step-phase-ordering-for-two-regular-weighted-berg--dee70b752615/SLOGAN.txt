For every positive integer Bergman kernel power \(p\ge2\), every regular orbit size \(d\ge2\), and all interior radii, the Gram determinant of two concentric equal-sized regular kernel orbits decreases strictly with \(1+\cos(d\theta)\). The unique determinant-optimal relative rotation is the half-step \(\theta=\pi/d\), with a fully explicit finite product for the sharp determinant.
