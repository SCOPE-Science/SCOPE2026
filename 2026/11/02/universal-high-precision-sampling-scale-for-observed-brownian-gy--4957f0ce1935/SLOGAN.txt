In a noisy two-frame observation of the stationary Brownian gyrator, the time lag maximizing forward/reverse pairwise Gaussian relative entropy per unit time is asymptotically \(\sqrt{h/\kappa}\) as proportional sensor-noise strength \(h\) vanishes. The optimal distinguishability rate loses the universal fraction \(2\sqrt{\kappa h}+o(\sqrt h)\) of its ideal continuous-time value, independently of fixed nonzero coupling and bath-temperature contrast.
