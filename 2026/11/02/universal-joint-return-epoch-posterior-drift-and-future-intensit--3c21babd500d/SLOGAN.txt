Conditioning an arbitrary fixed-initial-mass Pólya urn walk on at least \(k\) returns forces a universal joint limit for its hidden drift, \(k\)-th return time, and remaining return count: exponentially scaled drift, an inverse-exponential \(k^2\)-time law, and an independent exponential future-return mark conditional on drift. The result gives a joint conditional coupling beyond previously known marginal tie-duration and tie-intensity laws.
