The original lag-repetition statistic of the synchronous noisy voter model has variance at least \(\varepsilon^2L/4\) after \(L\) observations, on every locally finite graph and for every positive lag. Conditioning on copying ancestries reveals nonnegative squared Rademacher-character multiplicities; this rules out the formerly unresolved sublinear-variance case and activates the published unthinned Gaussian limit.
