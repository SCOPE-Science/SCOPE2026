For nonlinear \(p\)-heat on any finite star with positive vertex masses, conductances and independently weighted edge variation, the sharp infinitesimal variation-growth coefficient is \(\max_i[(W-2w_i)/m_0-w_i/m_i]\), simultaneously for all \(p>1\). Some positive variation gauge is nonexpansive for all real initial states and times exactly when \(\sum_i(2+m_0/m_i)^{-1}\le1\), so sufficiently high central mass stabilizes stars of arbitrarily large degree.
