Every fixed nondegenerate randomization of Störmer–Verlet step lengths within the deterministically stable range makes the harmonic oscillator's expected energy grow exponentially, despite each step being symplectic and individually stable. For bounded centered step perturbations of amplitude \(\varepsilon\), the sharp leading per-step growth factor is \(1+\sigma^2h^4\varepsilon^2/[2(4-h^2)]+O_h(\varepsilon^3)\).
