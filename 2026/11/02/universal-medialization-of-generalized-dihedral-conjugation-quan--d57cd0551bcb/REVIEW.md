# Scientific review: universal medialization of generalized-dihedral conjugation quandles

## Correctness — PASS

The group operation gives the two-type affine conjugation formula. The proposed relation is checked to be a quandle congruence, and a complete four-variable expansion shows every medial defect is zero on rotations and a multiple of \(4A\) on reflections. One explicit quadruple forces every identification by \(4A\) in any medial quotient. Those two inclusions prove the least congruence exactly. Finite independent checks reconstruct the group multiplication and close all medial defects on twelve test groups, but the unrestricted theorem is proved symbolically.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)

Bardakov–Sangare (arXiv:2610.18676v1, Theorem 5.4) give mediality criteria, universal lower bounds, and the known \(S_3\) example, not the reflection-versus-rotation quotient for general \(A\). Fan (arXiv:2609.13326v1, Sections 3 and 10) computes the componentwise conjugation formula for link-coloring purposes, not the least medial congruence. The general affine-mesh representation of Jedlička and collaborators (arXiv:1409.8396v2) describes medial outputs but does not determine this reflector on nonmedial inputs. Targeted mathematical-record searches did not locate a stronger covering result. Residual risk: a differently named result in older quandle/congruence literature might subsume the statement; this review is not a proof of historical priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a natural exact universal quotient, rather than a finite table or reformulation of a known criterion. It resolves the least medial congruence for an entire group family and every abelian kernel, gives complete operation laws, and proves that the universal quotient retains every rotation while collapsing only the reflections modulo \(4A\). It explicitly refines a recent general lower bound and separates quandle medialization from the generally smaller quotient induced by a group lower-central-series quotient.

## Boundaries

No conclusion is made for other group families, and an independent mathematical audit has not been performed. Algebraic finite checking is corroborative only; the general proof does not depend on enumeration.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
