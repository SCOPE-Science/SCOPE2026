For every abelian group \(A\), the universal medial quotient of the conjugation quandle of \(A\rtimes C_2\), with inversion action, preserves all rotations separately and identifies reflections exactly modulo \(4A\). Its underlying set is \(A\sqcup(A/4A)\); for the dihedral group of order \(2n\), its size is exactly \(n+\gcd(n,4)\).
