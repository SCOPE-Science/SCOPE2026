For every finite triangular ring with finite-field diagonal blocks and a nonzero finite unital bimodule, the exact \(k\)-factor product probability at every target depends only on the cardinalities of its two fields and its bimodule, for every \(k\ge1\). Consequently two nonisomorphic rings of order \(256\) have identical product distributions for all word lengths but centers of cardinalities \(4\) and \(2\); in equal-field sizes, the surviving nonzero products concentrate uniformly on the nonzero square-zero radical.
