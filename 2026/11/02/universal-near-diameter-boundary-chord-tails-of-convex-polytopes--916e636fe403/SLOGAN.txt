For independent surface-area-uniform points on any full-dimensional Euclidean convex polytope in dimension \(d\ge3\), the probability of a chord within \(\varepsilon\) of the diameter is \(C(P)\varepsilon^{2d-2}+O_P(\varepsilon^{2d-1})\), with an explicit positive coefficient from the incident facet cones at ordered diametral vertices. The coefficients are closed-form for regular simplices and cubes in every dimension.
