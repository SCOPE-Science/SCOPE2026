Under every finite Dirichlet law with at least two positive concentrations, Shannon entropy and Simpson concentration have a strictly negative covariance, with an exact decomposition into concentration-overlap and digamma-dispersion terms. At fixed total concentration two, the asymmetric two-category Dirichlet prior \((1/2,3/2)\) has covariance \(-1/24\), more negative than the symmetric prior's \(-1/36\).
