# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exchange decomposition into triangle frustration and bridge frustration is exact. The fixed-magnetization lower bounds follow from separate inequalities for monochromatic triangles and for equal-sign bridges; the perfect-matching complement supplies explicit spin configurations attaining both inequalities in every magnetization sector. Spin reversal and the lower convex envelope establish all fields and critical coexistence sectors. Equalities give actual bijections with constrained partial edge orientations, so counting is exhaustive rather than sampling. The cube's 13 counts have two independent computational derivations (a three-state-per-edge enumeration and an exhaustive spin enumeration), and the general formula is also tested on two smaller, structurally distinct cubic graphs. The theorem for arbitrary \(n\) relies on its combinatorial proof, not extrapolation from these computations.

## Originality — PASS on currently inspected evidence, provisional
The full accessible 2015 paper by Strečka, Karľová and Madaras analyzes five Platonic polyhedra and does not state the universal triangle-expansion theorem. The full accessible manuscript by Yu studies periodic Archimedean lattices without field and does not assert the fixed-magnetization all-cubic-graph formula. A 1995 star-lattice correlation paper may have related decorations, but only its abstract was available: its full contents remain a historical-priority risk. Queries about Fisher/triangle expansions, one-third plateaux, cube matching counts, and exact critical fields returned no identified dominating statement in the examined records. The novelty claim is limited to the combined all-graph fixed-magnetization theorem, matching/orientation equality classification, and truncated-cube exact critical degeneracies; it does not claim invention of standard Ising methods, graph decoration, or the general occurrence of one-third magnetization plateaux. Failure of a search alone does not establish originality, and independent priority verification remains outstanding.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The graph family is natural: triangle expansion is a standard way of introducing geometrical frustration into arbitrary cubic networks, and the perfect-matching hypothesis encompasses an infinite collection of graph topologies. The universal exact field thresholds and one-third plateau are physically interpretable, while ground-state degeneracies distinguish network topologies through an incidence-matching enumeration. The truncated cube is a canonical 24-site Archimedean solid with nontrivial and directly reproducible critical degeneracies, including \(11682\) and \(26441\) microstates. This is not an arbitrary slice of a numerical table: the finite numbers instantiate a graph-family theorem and are natural entropy data at field-driven coexistence.

## Scope and remaining risk
All exchange bonds must have the same positive strength; spin variables are classical and binary, temperature is zero, and the original cubic graph must have a perfect matching. No finite-temperature, thermodynamic-limit, or quantum generalization is claimed. Priority relative to uninspected older Fisher/star-lattice literature is not independently certified. Public numerical checks corroborate but do not replace the complete combinatorial proof.
