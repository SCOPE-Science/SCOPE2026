Every simple cubic graph with a perfect matching yields, after triangle expansion, a classical Ising antiferromagnet with the exact zero-temperature magnetization staircase \(0\to 1/3\to1\) at universal fields \(h/J=1,3\), irrespective of the original graph's shape. The ground-state degeneracies are encoded by incidence matchings and constrained partial edge orientations; for the truncated cube the zero-field, one-third-plateau, and two critical-field multiplicities are exactly \(450\), \(828\), \(11682\), and \(26441\).
