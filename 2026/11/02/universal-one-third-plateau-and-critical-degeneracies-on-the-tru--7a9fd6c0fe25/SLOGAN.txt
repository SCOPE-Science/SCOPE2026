On the twelve-spin truncated-tetrahedral Ising antiferromagnet with positive but arbitrary intra-triangle and inter-triangle couplings \(J,K\), the exact zero-temperature field curve has a universal \(1/3\) magnetization plateau precisely for \(K<h<K+2J\). Its two field transitions carry exactly 114 and 163 coexisting ground states, with explicit local spin rules and no thermodynamic-limit assumptions.
