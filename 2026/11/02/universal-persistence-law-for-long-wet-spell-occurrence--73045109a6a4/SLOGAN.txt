For a stationary wet/dry Markov model with half its days wet, every wet-spell length \(k\ge3\) has a unique persistence \(q_k\) maximizing the long-window exponential rate of observing that spell. As spell length grows, \((k-1)(1-q_k)\to0.75430126082451\), a universal high-persistence scaling law rather than a fixed-window forecast.
