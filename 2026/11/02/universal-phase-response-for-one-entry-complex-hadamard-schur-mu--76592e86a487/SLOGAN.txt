For every complex Hadamard matrix, changing one entry by a unit phase produces a spectral Schur multiplier whose norm has a universal closed formula depending only on the order and phase. The norm decreases strictly from \(\sqrt n\) at zero phase to the known real sign-flip value, with sharp local quadratic deficit \((n-1)\theta^2/(4n^{5/2})+O_n(\theta^4)\).
