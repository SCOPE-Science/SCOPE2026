Applying Aitken \(\Delta^2\) extrapolation to nested \(1,2,4\)-probe Gaussian trace means creates a universal \(t^{-1}\) absolute tail, with infinite mean absolute error for every nonzero positive-semidefinite input matrix. For \(A=I_2\), the tail constant is exactly \(27/128\), and a smooth denominator ridge still has mean-squared error asymptotic to \(243\pi/(512\delta)\) as \(\delta\downarrow0\).
