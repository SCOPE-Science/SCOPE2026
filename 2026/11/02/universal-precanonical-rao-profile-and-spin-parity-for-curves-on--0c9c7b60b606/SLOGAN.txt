For curves \(C\in|m(2H+E)|\) on a line-containing \((2,2,2)\) K3 surface, the precanonical Rao dimensions are universally \(\lfloor t^2/4\rfloor\) for \(2\le t<2m\), with a one-unit correction at \(t=2m\). The natural theta characteristic \(\mathcal O_C(mH)\) has exactly \(4m^2+2+\lfloor m^2/4\rfloor\) sections and is odd precisely when \(m\equiv2\pmod4\).
