# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The critical reduction is an exact algebraic identity, not a formal approximation. Both faces of the uniform thin trapping tube have strictly inward flow for fixed sufficiently small parameters. This yields a uniform relative \(O(u)\) error in the scalar decay rate. Integrating the inverse speed, rescaling, and applying an integrable majorant proves the universal profile; the two fixed-parameter decay laws follow from elementary derivative asymptotics. The packaged symbolic checker confirms the exact identities and supplies non-proof numerical corroboration. The result is local, and its quantifiers are stated precisely.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The main source, Xia–Chen–Srivastava–Parshad (2023, arXiv:2310.10558), derives critical equilibrium and bifurcation structure, but its inspected body and appendices do not state the rescaled two-parameter extinction-time limit or the implicit function \(\Theta(U)=1/U+\log[U/(1+U)]\). The result is not offered as a new center-manifold normal form; its claimed increment is an exact, locally uniform singular trajectory profile with explicit clock/density scales. Related additive-Allee linear dispersal and discrete-time nonlinear-dispersal work are mathematically inapplicable to this continuous-time singular limit without additional derivation. Semantic research records include mathematically analogous but different ecological/epidemic extinction laws. An unindexed analogue remains a risk; a prior theorem that explicitly covers the same nonlinear ODE and singular limit would defeat originality.

## Value — PASS

Population extinction at a degenerate invasion threshold produces long transients. Knowing separate \(t^{-1/2}\) and \(t^{-1}\) endpoints does not predict the intermediate transition time and density; the full profile supplies an experimentally testable response curve and scales for parameter sweeps in a directly motivated published ecology model. The local result is a natural finite-dimensional asymptotic classification, not a loose numerical observation or arbitrary parameter slice.

## Boundaries and outstanding checks

The theorem does not prove global convergence, statistical power, robustness to demographic noise, or whole-half-line uniformity. Scientific comparisons are based on specifically inspected primary sources and accessible mathematical records, not exhaustive indexing. The symbolic and numerical checker is secondary to the presented analytic argument.
