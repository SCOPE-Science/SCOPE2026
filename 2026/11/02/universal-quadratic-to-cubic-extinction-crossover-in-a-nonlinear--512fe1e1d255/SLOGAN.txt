A two-patch Allee model has a universal extinction crossover when its quadratic decay term vanishes: after rescaling density by \(\sigma/H^2\) and time by \(H^2/\sigma^2\), the trajectory converges to the unique profile satisfying \(\tau=1/U+\log(U/(1+U))\). This links \(t^{-1/2}\) critical relaxation to \(t^{-1}\) off-critical relaxation in an explicit near-extinction basin.
