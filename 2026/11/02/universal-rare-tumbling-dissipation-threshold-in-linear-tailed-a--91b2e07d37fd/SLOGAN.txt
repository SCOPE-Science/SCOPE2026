For a thermally diffusing run-and-tumble particle in any one-dimensional trap with fixed linear restoring tails of slope \(b\), rare orientation flips have a universal sharp dissipation boundary: the stationary entropy production tends to \((v^2-b^2)_+/T\) as the flip rate vanishes. Below \(v=b\), the position converges to a mixture of frozen-direction equilibria; at and above \(v=b\), its stationary probability escapes every bounded region, even though each positive flip rate has a unique stationary law.
