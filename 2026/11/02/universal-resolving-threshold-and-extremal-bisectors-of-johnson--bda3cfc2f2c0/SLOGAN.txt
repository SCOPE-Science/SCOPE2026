For every Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\) with \(n\ge2k\ge6\), the universal resolving threshold is \(\binom{n-2}{k}+\binom{n-2}{k-2}+1\), except that \(J(8,4)\) has threshold 37. Every largest nonresolving landmark set is an explicitly classified distance bisector; the exact number of such sets is \(\binom n2\), except 30 for \(J(6,3)\) and 35 for \(J(8,4)\).
