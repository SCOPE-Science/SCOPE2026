The known finite-size thresholds for exactly linear mean and covariance of gap counts between successive valleys on a random cyclic permutation are sharp for every gap length: \(\mathbb E D_{i,i+3}=(i+4)a_i\) and the first subthreshold covariance defect is the strictly positive trace \(\operatorname{tr}(A_iBA_jB)\), where \(B\) is the Brownian-bridge covariance kernel. The proof replaces finite numerical sharpness checks by a uniform all-parameter argument.
