Every \(q\)-ary de Bruijn cycle of order \(n\) has the same number \((q-1)P_q(p)q^{n-2p-1}\) of circular maximal repetitions of least period \(p\) whenever \(2p+1\le n\). At the first nonrigid binary order-five boundary, all 2,048 cycles have exactly 12, 13, or 14 circular runs, with respective multiplicities 1,008, 736, and 304 and exactly six possible period profiles.
