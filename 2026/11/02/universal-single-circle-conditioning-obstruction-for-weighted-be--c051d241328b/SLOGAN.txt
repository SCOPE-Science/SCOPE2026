For degree-\(N\) weighted Bergman polynomials, no choice of angular nodes, positive weights, or angular oversampling on one circle can beat the exact squared sampling condition number \(\max_{0\le k\le N}((\alpha+2)_k/k!)((\alpha+2)_N/N!)^{-k/N}\). Equality fixes one radius and requires the first \(N\) angular Fourier moments to vanish; with exactly \(N+1\) nodes this forces a uniformly weighted regular polygon. The optimal squared condition number grows sharply as \((N/\log N)^{\alpha+1}\), quantifying the unavoidable loss from single-radius observation.
