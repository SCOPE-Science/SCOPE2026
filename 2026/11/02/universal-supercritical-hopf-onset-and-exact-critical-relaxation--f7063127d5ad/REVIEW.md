# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The object is the published smooth polynomial vector field, not a rescaled or truncated surrogate. The exact Routh–Hurwitz cubic \(\lambda^3+a\lambda^2+b\lambda+2b\) yields the full equilibrium spectral partition at positive parameters. At \(a=2\) the eigenvalues are \(-2,\pm i\sqrt b\) and the crossing speed is \(-b/[2(b+4)]\). The symmetric second- and third-derivative tensors are differentiated explicitly in RESULT.md; exact symbolic evaluation gives \(\operatorname{Re}G=-2(b+1)/(b+4)\), always strictly negative. The periodic amplitude and first period correction follow from the radial Hopf equation and imaginary coefficient, and are separately reproduced by Poincare–Lindstedt harmonic balance. The critical radial decay follows from \(r'=-c r^3+O(r^5)\) and stable-foliation theory. The limits are local, fixing positive \(b\), and make no claims about remote attractors. The embedded verification program checks the decisive nonstandard algebra, but the infinite-time conclusions use the written normal-form proof, not finite sampling.

## Value — PASS
The paper's bifurcation diagrams and multistability examples vary \(a\) and \(b\), but do not provide a nonnumerical local boundary with predictable cycle amplitude and oscillation period. The exact all-positive-\(b\) onset formula fixes whether the newborn cycles attract, gives a physical coordinate amplitude and period for a natural precision benchmark, and quantifies the nonlinear slowing at criticality. The structural distinction from mere spectral crossing is the global-in-\(b\) negativity of the nonlinear Hopf coefficient and its derivative-level observable consequences.
