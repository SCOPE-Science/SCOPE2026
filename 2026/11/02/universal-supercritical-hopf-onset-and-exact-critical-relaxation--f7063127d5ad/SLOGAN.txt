For the three-cubic-term jerk flow \(\dot x=y,\ \dot y=z,\ \dot z=-az-x(x^2+xy+y^2-b)\), every \(b>0\) gives simultaneous nondegenerate supercritical Hopf bifurcations at the exact threshold \(a=2\), with an explicit negative Lyapunov coefficient and leading cycle-amplitude and period laws. At threshold, typical nearby trajectories decay algebraically with an explicit \(t^{-1/2}\) complex-amplitude constant.
