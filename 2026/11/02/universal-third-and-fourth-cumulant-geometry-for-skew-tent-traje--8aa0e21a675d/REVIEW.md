# Scientific review: skew tent cumulant geometry

## Correctness
**PASS.** For each fixed \(0<a<1\), the two branches are expanding and onto, the Perron operator is exact and has an isolated simple eigenvalue at \(1\) on bounded variation, and the analytic tilted eigenvalue controls stationary cumulants. Explicit polynomial zero-mean eigenfunction derivatives through degree three were substituted into all perturbation recurrences and symbolically checked for general \(a\). The cumulant formulas, normalization, signs, standardized curve and special symmetric-map identities follow. Exact-rational finite-horizon recurrence tests independently corroborate the analytic calculation. They do not replace the spectral argument.

## Originality
**PASS within checked evidence.** Smith's 2022 main text and supplement explicitly derive the symmetric \(a=1/2\) cubic and quartic generating coefficients and the relevant general technique; those symmetric coefficients are credited and excluded from the novelty claim. The parameter-wide formulas, their exact normalized curve and universal strict sign classification were not stated in the inspected Smith sections or in the retrieved skew-tent bifurcation abstract. Public research-index comparison returned only scientifically different dynamics/statistics. A broader treatment of twisted full-branch operators might imply these expressions without matching names; publication-priority uncertainty remains.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** These are natural fluctuation invariants for a canonical full-branch parameter family motivated by large-deviation calculations. The exact rigidity relation gives a continuum-wide diagnostic stronger than isolated parameter evaluations. Negative temporal skewness and positive temporal excess kurtosis contrast with the symmetric one-time uniform marginal; at \(a=1/2\), absence of every positive-lag pair correlation fails to remove higher-order temporal dependence. These are concrete structural features relevant to non-Gaussian finite-time statistics rather than an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Closest literature and limitations
Smith (arXiv:2204.04652) is the strongest comparator; its symmetric-map formulas and method are not new. Lindström--Thunberg (2008) concerns skew-map bifurcations; only its abstract was available. The current result concerns a fixed parameter, one coordinate observable and cumulants of orders at most four, and does not prove an all-parameter large-deviation principle or uniform endpoint estimates. Independent priority verification is needed before external novelty claims.
