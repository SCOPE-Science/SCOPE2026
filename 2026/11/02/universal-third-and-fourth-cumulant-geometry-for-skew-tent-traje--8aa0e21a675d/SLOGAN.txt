The full-branch skew tent family has exactly computable second-, third- and fourth-order cumulant growth rates for coordinate sums, and its asymptotic standardized skewness and excess kurtosis lie on the rigid curve \(H=6/5+(19/15)G^2\). Every parameter has negative temporal skewness and positive excess kurtosis, even though each individual coordinate is uniform; at the symmetric tent map all nonzero-lag pair correlations vanish while higher temporal cumulants remain nonzero.
