The harmonic Brownian gyrator's integrated angular probability current obeys an exact single-rate response equation under arbitrary positive, time-dependent bath temperatures, even from non-Gaussian initial states. Starting from a steady temperature imbalance and allowing the new imbalance no lower than \(-B\), the fastest reversal takes \(\log(1+\Delta_0/B)/(a+b)\); swapping the two baths gives the universal time \(\log2/(a+b)\).
