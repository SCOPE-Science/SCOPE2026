For periodic honeycomb qubit graph states with Mersenne width \(L=2^k-1\ge7\), omitting any two controlled-\(Z\) bonds with four distinct endpoints increases the full sublattice entanglement entropy by exactly two bits, from \(L^2-L+1\) to \(L^2-L+3\). The result is sharp: a third disjoint bond cannot universally add a third bit.
