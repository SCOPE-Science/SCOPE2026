For any finite reversible Markov propulsion process in a weak harmonic trap, the entropy production averaged over physical times of order \(1/\kappa\) has a universal crossover: a persistent propulsion-variance contribution plus a mean-drift contribution proportional to \(e^{-2\kappa t}\). A quantitative \(O(\kappa)\) error bound resolves the otherwise singular free-versus-confined stationary limit, including asymmetric two-state run-and-tumble motion.
