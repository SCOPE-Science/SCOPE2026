For Brownian search interrupted at any fixed positive rate and restarted only after a stochastic harmonic return, the \(n\)th completion-time moment diverges as \(c_n k^{-(n-1/2)}\) as the return stiffness \(k\) vanishes, with exact constants \(c_n=n\sqrt{2/\pi}\int_0^{\pi/2}(\log\sec\theta)^{n-1}d\theta\) independent of the interruption rate. In particular the variance grows as \(\sqrt{2\pi}\log2\,k^{-3/2}\), faster than the square of the mean.
