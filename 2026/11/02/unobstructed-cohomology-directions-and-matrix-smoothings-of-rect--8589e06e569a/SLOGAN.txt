Every rectangular-band algebra \(k[B_{m,n}]\) has at least \((m-1)(n-1)\) linearly independent, simultaneously integrable Hochschild \(2\)-classes arising from sandwich-matrix perturbations. In the square case these directions yield a flat deformation from a radical-cube-zero algebra with radical dimension \(r^2-1\) to the full matrix algebra \(M_r\) over the generic field.
