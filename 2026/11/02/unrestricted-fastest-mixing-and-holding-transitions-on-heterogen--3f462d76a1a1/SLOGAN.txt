On every Cartesian product of at least three complete graphs with unequal alphabet sizes, the fastest symmetric stochastic chain is unique even when individual edge probabilities are unconstrained: every coordinate direction receives the same spectral decrement, and its edge probabilities are inversely proportional to alphabet size. Its holding probability switches off exactly at the reciprocal-size threshold \(\sum_i1/n_i=(d-1)/2\), with heterogeneous three-factor critical triples \((2,3,6)\) and \((2,4,4)\).
