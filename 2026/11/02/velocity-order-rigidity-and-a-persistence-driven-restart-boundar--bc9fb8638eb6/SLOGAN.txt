For active Ornstein–Uhlenbeck search with complete velocity resetting, the absolute absorption time is pathwise increasing in the reset velocity, at every activity and reset rate. Yet the weak-activity shift of the critical position for *beneficial resetting* reverses with propulsion persistence: its coefficient is asymptotic to \(-\tau\) for short persistence and tends to \(4L^2/(125D)\) for long persistence, where \(L\) is interval length and \(D\) thermal diffusivity.
