For even alphabet size \(q=2d\), Watson–Crick reverse-complement palindromes have an exact rational length-by-subsequence-universality-index generating function governed by which involution pairs appear in the half-word's arch remainder. Every shortest \(k\)-universal such word has length \(qk\), and the exact number is \((q!)^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\) times \(2^d d!\) for odd \(k\), or times \(1\) for even \(k\).
