An antisymmetric dark state of a monitored three-site quantum ring becomes detectable on the \(\alpha^{-2}\) attempt scale under a weak magnetic phase \(\alpha\). The rescaled first-detection time has an exact exponential limit, whose rate jumps from \(9t^2/2\) to \(t^2\) at every fixed positive full-revival sampling interval \(t=2\pi k/3\).
