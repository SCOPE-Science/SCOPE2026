For periodic binary words avoiding every antisquare of order at least two, the Hamming-weight-refined zeta function is \(\bigl[(1-z-uz^4-u^2z^6)(1-uz-u^3z^4-u^4z^6)\bigr]^{-1}\). It determines all indexed and primitive-necklace weight spectra and reveals a sharp forbidden density interval \((1/3,2/3)\).
