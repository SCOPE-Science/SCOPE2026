For two unequal exchange-coupled charger qubits, the pure states preserving a fully excited battery are exactly the span of the doubly excited charger and a weighted dark state; every such state has deterministic conditional charging \(e_n=1-(1-e_0)(1-r)^n\) under suitable local adaptive readout. The concurrence and rate obey \(r=q(1-\mathcal C_D\mathcal C)\), and a dark charger inert at equal couplings is activated with a rate quadratic in coupling imbalance.
