Every finite positively weighted electrical cycle has an effective-resistance metric that is **itself** a full-dimensional Euclidean simplex metric, not merely a squared Euclidean metric. An explicit Fourier embedding gives strict \(2\)-negative type on the entire resistance circle, while every finite cycle has a supercritical exponent above \(2\) and the regular-cycle exponents converge to the sharp continuum boundary \(2\); the regular simplices also admit exact spectral and volume formulas.
