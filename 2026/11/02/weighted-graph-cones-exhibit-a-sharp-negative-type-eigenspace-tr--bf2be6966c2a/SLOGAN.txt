A weighted apex over any noncomplete regular graph with least adjacency eigenvalue at most \(-2\) has an exact two-branch negative-type threshold: one branch is a global apex--base imbalance and the other is the least graph eigenmode. Their unique, explicitly located crossover changes the nullspace and the minimum isometric Hilbert-snowflake embedding dimension; for \(q\)-ary Hamming graphs the dimensional loss is \((q-1)^d\).
