On weighted trees, nonlinear \(p\)-heat preserves the Lipschitz seminorm of each input exactly when a finite edge-by-edge dominance test holds, and a compatible choice of edge lengths exists precisely at a Perron–Frobenius threshold. But for every \(p\ne2\), no tree containing two adjacent edges can make the same evolution nonexpansive **between two inputs** in Lipschitz distance—even when every individual input contracts.
