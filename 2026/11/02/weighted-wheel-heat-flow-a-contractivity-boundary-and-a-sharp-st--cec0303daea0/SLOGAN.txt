For a wheel with unit rim conductances and uniform spoke conductance \(r\), unnormalized heat is graph-Lipschitz contractive exactly through rim size nine at the sharp thresholds \(r\le2/(m-5)\) for \(6\le m\le9\), but no positive spoke conductance works once \(m\ge10\). Across all spoke conductances, its exact fixed-size heat-Lipschitz amplification envelope comes from a star limit, with a sharp universal supremum of \(2\) that is never attained on a finite wheel.
