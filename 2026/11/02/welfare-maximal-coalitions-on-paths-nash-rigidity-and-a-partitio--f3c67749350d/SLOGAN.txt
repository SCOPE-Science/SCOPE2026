For non-normalized induced-distance coalition games on every path with positive reward \(p\) for neighbors and penalty \(-q\) for all farther connected partners, all welfare-maximal partitions are Nash stable and an exact binomial formula counts them. At critical ratios \(p/q=(L(L+1)-2)/2\), this count grows exponentially rather than polynomially, with generating function \(1/(1-z^L-z^{L+1})\) for all sufficiently large path orders.
