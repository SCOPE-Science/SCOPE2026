In two-color Schelling swap games on equal-part complete multipartite networks, **every** equilibrium is globally welfare-optimal, and the exact number of equilibrium colorings is \(\binom ms\binom{k}{q+1}^{s}\binom{k}{q}^{m-s}\) for \(R=mq+s\). When every part has two vertices, the number of strictly improving exchanges is order-independent and has an explicit finite-population distribution under uniformly random initial colorings.
