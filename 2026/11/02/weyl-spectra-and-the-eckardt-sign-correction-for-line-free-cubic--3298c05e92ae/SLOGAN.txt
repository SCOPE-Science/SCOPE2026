For every smooth cubic surface over a finite field, the zeta function of the complement of all 27 geometric lines is an explicit \(W(E_6)\)-orbit baseline multiplied by one signed factor for every closed Eckardt point. Exhaustive computation evaluates all 25 Weyl classes; two split cubics over \(\mathbb F_{17}\) show that the Weyl class alone does not determine the open zeta function.
