On every wheel, arboreal-gas spokes have an explicit all-order cyclic-gap inclusion law at every fugacity \(x>0\). As the rim grows, their local law is simultaneously a stationary renewal process and a determinantal point process with kernel \(K_x(i,j)=\rho q^{|i-j|/2}\); finite spoke pairs are strictly negatively correlated, with limiting covariance \(-\rho^2q^r\) at separation \(r\).
