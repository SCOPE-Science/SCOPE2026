On every labelled wheel with \(n\ge3\) rim vertices, synchronous parallel chip-firing has exactly \(n a_n\) primitive two-cycle orbits, where \(a_3=20,a_4=69,a_5=225\) and \(a_n=2a_{n-1}+4a_{n-2}+a_{n-3}\). The exact count grows like \(\left(2/3-\sqrt{13}/39\right)n\left((3+\sqrt{13})/2\right)^n\) and retains an explicit alternating correction.
