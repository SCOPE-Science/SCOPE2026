For every wheel with \(m\ge3\) rim vertices, the unrestricted spanning-tree exchange graph has radius \(\lceil2m/3\rceil\) and diameter \(m\), with each tree's eccentricity determined exactly by the cycle rank of its edge complement. Exactly \(2^{m+1}-4\) spanning trees are peripheral, equivalently the two trees in each of \(2^m-2\) complementary spanning-tree pairs.
