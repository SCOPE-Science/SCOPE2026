For the token-jump reconfiguration graph on minimum connected dominating sets of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\), each solution's degree is exactly the Wiener index of its associated \((n-1)\)-vertex tree plus \((n-2)\) times one less than its leaf count. The entire exchange graph is connected, has a closed edge-count sum over tree splits, and is regular only for \(n=3,4\); double brooms expose a persistent four-move advantage over paths despite smaller Wiener index.
