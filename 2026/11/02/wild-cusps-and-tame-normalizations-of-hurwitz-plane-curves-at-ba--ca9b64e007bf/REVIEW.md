# Review of wild Hurwitz degeneration

## Correctness — PASS
The torus relation \(x^N(1-t)=(-1)^{n+1}t^n\), the simple critical parameter \(t=n/(n-1)\), the \(M\) geometric roots, and the local substitution \(u=x^M-x_0^M\) prove the exact \(v^2=u^{p^e}\) singularity types. The Frobenius identification of function fields, tame Riemann--Hurwitz, and geometrically bijective normalization establish all genus and zeta assertions. Exact finite-field enumeration independently checks selected cases, but is not used to prove universal statements.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The general classical Kummer transformation was already reported by Bennama and Carbonne (1997); its authorship is retained. Montanucci--Speziali (2016) and Bisogno et al. (2018) treat smooth characteristic regimes rather than the wild local structure at \(p\mid n^2-n+1\). Targeted mathematical-record searches and the accessible earlier claims found only unrelated varieties and a prior \(n=3,p=7\) special case. The 1997 original full text was unavailable: its precise unquoted coverage is the principal residual priority risk, not evidence of noncoverage.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete all-parameter classification of bad-fiber singularities, genus collapse, and zeta stability has a natural application to arithmetic degeneration and explicit curve models. For \(n=19,p=7\) a plane curve of arithmetic genus \(171\) becomes rational with a single wild cusp; for \(n=5,p=7\) the normalization is elliptic. This is a substantive structural boundary statement, not a finite table or a generic textbook consequence.
