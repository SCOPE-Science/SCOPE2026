At every prime \(p\mid n^2-n+1\), the degree-\(n+1\) Hurwitz curve has exactly \(M=(n^2-n+1)/p^{v_p(n^2-n+1)}\) wild cusps, each formally \(v^2=u^{p^{v_p(n^2-n+1)}}\), and its normalization has genus \((M-1)/2\). The singular curve and its tame Kummer normalization have identical finite-field zeta functions.
