In characteristic two the Steiner Roman quartic has three wild Artin–Schreier double-line covers: its normalization's differential drops rank at three doubled points rather than six reduced points. Over every \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the surface has \(q^2+q+2\) rational points, but exactly \(3q/2\) lie outside the image of its canonical quadratic normalization parametrization, characterized by an absolute-trace obstruction.
