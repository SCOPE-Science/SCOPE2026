All six single length-three pattern-avoidance classes have the same Catalan number of permutations and VC dimension \(n-1\), but their exact pair-comparison Littlestone dimensions first differ at \(n=5\): monotone forbidden patterns give \(4\), while nonmonotone forbidden patterns give \(5\). Complete adaptive trees and exhaustive upper-bound programs determine both symmetry classes through \(n=8\), with another strict difference at \(n=7\).
