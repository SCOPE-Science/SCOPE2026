For the finite-field evolution algebra \(xy=B(x,y)u\) with nondegenerate symmetric \(B\), the commuting graph of rank-one algebra retractions is an explicit singular-line/hyperplane incidence graph, and its vertex and edge counts have closed finite-orthogonal-space formulas. In odd dimensions, anisotropic choices of \(u\) of opposite norm square class yield the same vertex count but edge counts differing by exactly \(q^{(5n-15)/2}\).
