A three-state run-and-rest drive and a covariance-matched two-state telegraph drive yield identical second-order position statistics and mean active work in the same harmonic trap, but different long-time mechanical-work noise. Their variance ratio is exactly \((k+g)/(2k)\): resting reduces fluctuations for \(g<k\) and increases them for \(g>k\), with both work observables obeying a central limit theorem.
