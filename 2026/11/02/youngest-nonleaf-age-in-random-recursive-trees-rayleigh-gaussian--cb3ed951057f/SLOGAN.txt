In a uniform random recursive tree, the number of final birth labels that never acquire a child is asymptotically Rayleigh on the \(\sqrt n\) scale, independently of Gaussian leaf-count fluctuations. Nevertheless, this age extreme has strictly positive finite-size covariance with the number of leaves, with \(\operatorname{Cov}(H_n,L_n)/\sqrt n\to\sqrt{\pi/2}/4\), and an exact conditional regression formula explains the residual dependence.
