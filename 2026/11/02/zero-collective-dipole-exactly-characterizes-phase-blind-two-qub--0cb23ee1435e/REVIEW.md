# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The complete claim is tied to an explicit number-conserving three-qubit Hamiltonian, arbitrary complex normalized pure charger amplitudes, detuning, and a nonexceptional interaction time. Exact exponentiation of both two-dimensional bright sectors yields an excited-population formula whose sole incoming-battery phase dependence is \(\operatorname{Re}[e^{-i\gamma}\bar uv(\bar a b+\bar b d)]\). This proves both necessity and sufficiency when \(uv\ne0\). A separate Pauli-tensor linear-algebra construction gives **local** measurement axes that equalize all nonempty conditional branches without a joint entangling measurement. The population recurrence and the coherent separable example then follow exactly. Full three-qubit exponentiation and local measurement simulation pass 4,600 consistency assertions. Finite tests are supporting evidence; the proof is analytical.

## Originality — PASS, qualified best-knowledge
The motivating 2026 two-ancilla collision paper supplies Bell-like phase-blind examples and the adaptive energy-equalization premise, but its available main-body sections do not provide a necessary-and-sufficient complex collective-polarization criterion, nor the explicit opposite-polarization separable product and resulting closed recurrence. The present general condition does not follow from the more restricted characterization of chargers that leave an *already fully excited battery* unchanged: the latter is a different fixed-point question and does not entail classification of all phase-blind chargers or nontrivial half-charging trajectories. The earlier one-ancilla paper concerns a different Hilbert space. The published abstract of the related coherent-ancilla antiphase study concerns multilevel charging and ergotropy oscillations, not the present ideal three-qubit local-measurement statement. Historical records, published searches, and Results database comparisons found no claim whose stated hypotheses and conclusion imply this exact classification. Search absence is not a uniqueness proof. A relevant full paper was inaccessible; its potential implicit coverage is a residual risk requiring human priority review.

## Value — PASS
The condition \(\langle J_-\rangle=0\) is a physically interpretable design invariant and completely classifies a continuous family of pure charger states rather than isolated examples. It reveals that coherent yet **separable** chargers can be tailored to eliminate *trajectory-to-trajectory variation in conditional mean energy* under adaptive local measurement, without restricting the charger to computational-basis or Bell-like states. The exact recurrence supplies a realizable nontrivial half-filling benchmark and distinguishes phase-blindness from fixed-point full charging. It does not claim that quantum energy variance vanishes.

## Scientific limitations
Exact equal real couplings, pure charger and battery states, ideal adaptive local measurements, fixed collision duration, and no inter-collision memory. No claim about individual quantum energy-measurement outcomes, extraction work, robustness to noise or unequal couplings. At \(uv=0\), pulse degeneracy removes the necessity of collective-dipole cancellation.
