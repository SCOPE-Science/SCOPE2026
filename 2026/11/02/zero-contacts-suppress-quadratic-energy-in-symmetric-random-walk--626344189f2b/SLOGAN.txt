For a uniform \(2n\)-step symmetric walk bridge, the number of interior returns to zero and the sum of squared heights have exact covariance \(-n(n-1)4^n/(6\binom{2n}{n})<0\). Their correlation tends to the nonzero universal constant \(-\sqrt{5\pi}/(8\sqrt{4-\pi})\); every prescribed zero contact also reduces expected squared-height energy by an explicit segment-length product.
