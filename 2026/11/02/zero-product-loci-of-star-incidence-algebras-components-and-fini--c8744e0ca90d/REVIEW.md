# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** Matrix multiplication gives exactly the equations \(a_0b_0=0\), \(a_ib_i=0\), \(a_0\beta_i+\alpha_i b_i=0\). The two exhaustive slices \(a_0=0\) and \(a_0\ne0\) produce the stated component candidates. The nonstandard point is the closure of each open component: a height-equals-number-of-generators complete intersection is unmixed, and its \(a_0=0\) fiber has dimension one below that of the irreducible open slice. Thus there is no extra boundary component. Witnesses separate all proposed components. The disjoint scalar count gives the motivic and finite-field formulas; nine direct finite-field enumerations pass. Remaining mathematical boundaries are nonreduced scheme structure and general posets.

## Originality

**PASS, provisionally on the inspected evidence.** The nearest available earlier exact enumeration addresses zero-product pairs in the *full* upper triangular matrix algebra and gives no component classification for the sparse star algebra: restricting both factors changes the ambient multiplication locus and does not follow by parameter substitution. Other full-text material treats either the homology of radical-square-zero path algebras (Lanzilotta–Marcos–Mata), a factorization property of bilinear maps vanishing on zero products (Brice–Huang), or pairs of full nilpotent matrices annihilating in both directions (Schröer). Those statements do not imply the enumeration and component classification of the one-sided zero-product locus in the star incidence algebra. The closest accessible triangular-algebra article (Ghahramani) concerns zero-product-determined maps; only its abstract was accessible, and the uninspected body is a named residual risk. Independent historical priority verification remains outstanding.

## Value

**PASS.** The set of annihilating ordered pairs is an intrinsic multiplication-geometric invariant, relevant to zero-product preservers, annihilator stratification and arithmetic representation varieties. In this unbounded natural family it has exponentially many geometrically irreducible components with an explicit non-equidimensional dimension profile, yet a single finite-field counting polynomial and motivic class. The joint geometric/arithmetic determination is a structural classification rather than a small numerical refinement.
