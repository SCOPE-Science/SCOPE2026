For the incidence algebra of an outward star with \(r\ge1\) leaves, the reduced variety of ordered zero-product pairs has exactly \(2^{r+1}\) geometrically irreducible components, with explicitly classified dimensions from \(2r+1\) through \(3r+1\). Over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact number of pairs is \((q-1)q^r(2q-1)^r+q^{r+1}(q^2+q-1)^r\), and the full Grothendieck class is explicit.
