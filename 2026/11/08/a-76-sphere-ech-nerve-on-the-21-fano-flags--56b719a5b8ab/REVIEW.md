The object, unit-edge intrinsic metric and closed-ball convention are fully specified by Fano incidence coordinates. Adams–Shukla–Singh [1, Lemma 2.2] identifies the exact 42 seven-vertex generating facets for scale three. A standalone checker enumerates 3,395 nonempty faces by generating subsets and independently tests all 2,097,151 nonempty vertex masks for membership. It constructs two different 1,659-pair acyclic Morse matchings, topologically sorting every directed Hasse incidence (13,755) in each. The only critical cells are one vertex and 76 two-cells; therefore the integral homotopy type is \(\bigvee^{76}S^2\). Independent mod-two ranks agree. No infinite extrapolation or unspecified computational certificate is used.

## Value — PASS

The 21-vertex Fano flag graph is a canonical 4-regular graph arising from the smallest projective plane, not an arbitrary example. Its scale-two Čech nerve retains eight loop classes, while the next integer scale instead has 76 independent integral two-sphere classes. The pure six-dimensional but non-shellable scale-three complex supplies a reproducible structural benchmark beyond the known line-graph scale-two regime. The exact homotopy information is stronger than a Betti-number table and does not claim a family-wide result.

## Limits

Only one graph and one interval of radii are proved; not a classification of arbitrary projective-plane line graphs or of persistence maps. Inaccessible or unindexed equivalents cannot be excluded with absolute certainty.
