The uniform-vertex covariance of every complete-bipartite stable-set polytope has an explicit four-class Euclidean spectrum. Balance forces its least-variance direction to be the global all-ones line, while any unequal bipartition makes the least eigenspace exactly the smaller-part contrast space; fixing one part and enlarging the other makes the covariance condition number grow asymptotically like \(2^{|p-q|}\).
