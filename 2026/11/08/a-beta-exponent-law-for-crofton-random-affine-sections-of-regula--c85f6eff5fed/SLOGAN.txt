For a Crofton-random affine hyperplane meeting a regular simplex with \(n\) vertices, the complete section-type law is given by successive Gaussian-order-statistic spacings. Unlike centroidal sections, the number \(V_n\) of section vertices has a random polynomial-growth exponent: \(\log(V_n)/\log(n)-1\) converges to \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,1/2)\).
