For an even helm with \(2k\) rim vertices, minimum zero forcing is encoded by one doubled \(000\)-defect and one doubled \(111\)-defect in an otherwise alternating cyclic defect sequence. This gives exactly \(6\) minimum sets at \(k=3\) and \(k^2 2^{k-4}\) at \(k\ge4\), all isolated under token sliding.
