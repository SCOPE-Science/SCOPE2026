For \(5\times5\) strictly upper-triangular matrices, every finite-field-linear map satisfying \([f(X),X^2]=0\) has the characteristic-zero standard form unless the field is exactly \(\mathbb F_2\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\) four explicit extra directions raise the solution-space dimension from \(41\) to \(45\), whereas every larger finite field retains dimension \(41\).
