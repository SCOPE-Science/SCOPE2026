# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For every odd \(3\le m<2^n\), \(n\ge3\), the published Apéry theorem gives \(N_j=aw(j)-j\), and the published greedy-weight lemma gives monotonicity of \(w\). The new binary-splitting proof identifies the endpoint minimum coin count \(2t\), proves the exact last weight plateau has length \(\delta+1\), and locates its first index as the unique Frobenius maximizer. The nonstandard borrow inequality also excludes shorter coin representations that use powers below the binary block boundary. Domain limits and extrema are explicit. Independent residue-graph Dijkstra calculations tested all 119 pairs through \(n=7\) and 599039 residues; 2115 additional pairs checked the digital parameters. Finite checks do not prove the infinite result.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** Srivastava--Thakkar's full manuscript proves a closed formula only for the \(m=2^r+1\) subfamily. Liu--Xin--Ye--Yin's available revision v4 contains the general Apéry description, but its stated closed formulas cover only first- or second-digit cases and explicitly leave longer zero-prefix cases such as the parameter \(63\) outside them. A preceding isolated-gap result gives Frobenius values for a nonperfect subfamily, not the perfect cases with correction at least two. Here the explicit Hamming-weight formula includes all such cases and gives unbounded corrections. Targeted mathematical-record and primary-literature searches did not find a covering formula. A published equivalent in unindexed literature remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is a complete resolution of a named natural Frobenius invariant for all odd Proth coefficients in the standard domain, not a table recomputation or a substitution. The correction \(\delta\) measures the length of a structurally forced terminal Apéry-weight plateau, can be arbitrarily large, and is calculated solely from binary Hamming weights. It moves beyond the two earlier special regimes using a new extremal localization argument.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Liu, Xin, Ye, Yin, arXiv:2306.03459v4, Theorems 5.2, 5.4, 5.5; Srivastava and Thakkar, arXiv:2311.12462v1, Theorem 1. The proof is conditional only on explicitly cited published lemmas, which were read in the primary PDF. It does not address genus, type, pseudo-Frobenius sets, or even coefficients. Priority beyond accessible sources is not certified.
