For every odd \(3\le m<2^n\) with \(n\ge3\), the Proth-semigroup Frobenius number is an explicit function of \(m2^n\) and the binary Hamming weight \(s_2(m)\), with a correction determined by the first integer \(t\) satisfying \(2t-s_2(t)\ge s_2(m)\). The formula settles all previously untreated longer zero-prefix cases in the greedy Mersenne representation and exhibits an unbounded correction beyond the two earlier closed-form regimes.
