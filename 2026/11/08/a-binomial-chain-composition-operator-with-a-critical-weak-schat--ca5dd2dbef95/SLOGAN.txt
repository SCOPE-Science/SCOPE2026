An explicit binomially weighted Hardy space makes the noncompact subnormal composition operator \(f(z)\mapsto f(z^2)\) essentially normal, while its compact positive self-commutator has the sharp counting law \(t^2N(t)\to1/30\). The commutator belongs to \(\mathcal S_p\) exactly for \(p>2\), providing a natural two-dimensional weak-Schatten boundary from dyadic coefficient orbits.
