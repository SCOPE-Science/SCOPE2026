For any permutation \(P\) on \(q\ge2\) letters and any positive exponents \(k,\ell\), the shortest supersequences of \(P^k\) and \((\overleftarrow P)^\ell\) have length \(\max\{qk,q\ell,(q-1)(k+\ell)+1\}\). Writing \(A=(q-1)k-\ell+1\) and \(B=(q-1)\ell-k+1\), their number is exactly \(\binom{A+B}{A}\) when \(A,B\ge0\), and otherwise is one; a forced-match skeleton bijectively encodes all the optima by binary solo-step schedules.
