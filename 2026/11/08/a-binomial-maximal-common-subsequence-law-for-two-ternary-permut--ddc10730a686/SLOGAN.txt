For every positive \(r,s\), all inclusion-maximal common subsequences of \((012)^r\) and \((021)^s\) are already longest; their exact number is \(\binom{r+s}{2\min(r,s)-\max(r,s)}\) when the repetition counts differ by at most a factor of two, and otherwise is one. The binomial phase includes \(\binom{2n}{n}\) distinct maximal words for equal powers and collapses sharply to a unique word outside the factor-two boundary.
