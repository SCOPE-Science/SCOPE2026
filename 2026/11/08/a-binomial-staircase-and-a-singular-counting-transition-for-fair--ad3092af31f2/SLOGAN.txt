For two agents with \(t\) chores of each of two types and symmetric disutility ratio \(r>1\), the number of jointly EFX and fractionally Pareto-optimal labeled allocations is exactly \(2\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor t/2-(t-1)/(2r)\rfloor}\binom tj-1\), with discrete rational jump thresholds. At \(r=1\) it instead equals \(\binom{2t}{t}\), producing a sharp transition from polynomial to exponentially small probability under a uniformly random allocation.
