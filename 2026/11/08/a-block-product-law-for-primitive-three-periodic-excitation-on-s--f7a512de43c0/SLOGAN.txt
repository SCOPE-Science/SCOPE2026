For three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on a connected cactus with exactly one cycle of length divisible by three, the number of initial primitive three-periodic configurations is \(6\prod_{C\ne C_*}(2\lfloor |C|/3\rfloor+1)\). Arbitrary tree attachments and rearrangements of the other cycle blocks leave the count unchanged: each extra four- or five-cycle multiplies it by three.
