## Originality — PASS (with historical risk)

The focal Keshavarzi–Wu paper establishes Schatten membership criteria for general weighted Bergman symbols, not exact high-monomial-degree operator norm asymptotics. Bourass and coauthors establish a fixed-symbol singular-index asymptotic, and their Section 8 gives the degree-one Hankel moment-difference formula. The elementary diagonalization and degree-one behavior are therefore not claimed new. The present claim is the uniform-in-parameter *classification of fixed-weight large-symbol-degree optimizers* and its essential-singularity transition as the weight approaches zero. Literature and published-finding corpus comparisons did not uncover a theorem implying that package. Prior monomial examples may nevertheless contain an equivalent implicit statement; historical priority is not independently certified.
