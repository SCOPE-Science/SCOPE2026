For weighted Bergman big Hankel operators with symbols \(\overline z^{\,m}\), the norm-attaining input degree undergoes a sharp transition at weight exponent \(\alpha=0\): it scales as \(m\) below the threshold but as \(t_\alpha m\) with \(t_\alpha>1\) above it. The excess obeys the nonperturbative law \(t_\alpha-1\sim\tfrac12 4^{-1/\alpha}\) as \(\alpha\downarrow0\), and at the unweighted point the norm is exactly \(1/\sqrt2\) at every symbol degree.
