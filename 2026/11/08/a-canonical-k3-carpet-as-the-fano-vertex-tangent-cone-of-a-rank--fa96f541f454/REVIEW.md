## Correctness — PASS
The Grassmannian graph chart parametrizes exactly the lines near the vertex; coefficientwise restriction of the quadric yields three homogeneous quadratic equations and hence the entire tangent cone. Its reduction is the degree-four balanced scroll because a nonsingular conic contains no line; codimension three makes the three equations a regular sequence. The explicit \(a_0\)-chart gives a square-zero double structure. The other charts follow from coordinate and row symmetries. Adjunction, finite duality and the exact sequence with the scroll identify the nilradical with the canonical bundle, and calculate \(h^1=0\). The complete-intersection Hilbert series gives local multiplicity eight. Symbolic reductions and Hilbert-function computations replay successfully in the included script.

Neither prior result is claimed new. Ilten–Zotine (2016) study toric Fano schemes but reserve their complete nonreduced-structure theorem for \(k=\dim(X)-1\), excluding lines on this threefold. The result assessed here is specifically the identification of the K3 carpet as the tangent cone at the vertex line of the rank-three quadric, and the induced exact Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity. Relevant accessible primary text and mathematical-record searches disclosed no statement that already makes that Fano-scheme identification. A further historical treatment might still contain or imply it; priority is not independently established.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a natural singularity of the line Hilbert scheme on a classical rank-three quadric, not an arbitrary polynomial example. The deformation directions at the contained vertex line unexpectedly organize into a Gorenstein, globally nonreduced K3 carpet, relating Fano deformation geometry and established doubled-scroll geometry. The result supplies the exact local ring, intrinsic infinitesimal conormal bundle, and multiplicity eight, all of which would be relevant in degenerations of Fano schemes of quadrics. It goes beyond tangent-space dimension or reduced-support calculation, although it is a focused structural observation rather than a global Fano classification.
