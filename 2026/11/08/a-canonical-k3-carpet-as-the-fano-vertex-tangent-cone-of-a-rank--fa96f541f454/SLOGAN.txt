For the rank-three quadric threefold \(x_0x_2=x_1^2\), the projectivized tangent cone of its line Fano scheme at the vertex line is the canonical K3 carpet on \(S(2,2)\), in every characteristic. Its square-zero nilradical is \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1}(-2,-2)\), and the Fano singularity has Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity eight.
