For the positive-multiplier one-step predictive stabilization of a generalized tent map, the exact nonconvergent set is a two-branch Cantor repeller with Hausdorff dimension \(\log 2/\log s\), and survival inside its natural interval has geometric tail \((2/s)^k\). At the endpoint gain, almost every orbit reaches zero in *finite* time with an explicit distribution, whereas at every strictly smaller stabilizing gain finite-time capture occurs only for countably many initial conditions.
