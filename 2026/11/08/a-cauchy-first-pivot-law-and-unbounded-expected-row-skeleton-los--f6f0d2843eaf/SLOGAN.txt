In a nonnegative \(3\times2\) row-skeleton benchmark, the first Gaussian-sketch LU pivot chooses the optimal row with probability tending to one while its optimal refitted rank-one squared-Frobenius error grows like \(t/\pi\), against a constant optimal error. The exact Cauchy pivot law has a sharp visibility threshold \(t=1/2\) and is unchanged by adding sketch columns before selecting the first pivot.
