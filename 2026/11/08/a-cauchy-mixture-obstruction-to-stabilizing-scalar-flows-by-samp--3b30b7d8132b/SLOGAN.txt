For every symmetric stable Lévy driver with index \(0<\alpha\le1\), sampled multiplicative noise feedback has logarithmic growth rate at least the uncontrolled scalar drift, strictly larger at nonzero gain; it cannot stabilize a nonnegative drift. A positive-stable/Cauchy mixture gives the exact pathwise upper exponent and the unique destabilization threshold for negative drift, explicit at \(\alpha=1\).
