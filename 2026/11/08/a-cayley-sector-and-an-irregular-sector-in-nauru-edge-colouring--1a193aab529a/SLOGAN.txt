The named three-edge-colouring Kempe graph of the Nauru graph \(GP(12,5)\) splits into a 144-state bipartite six-involution Cayley sector of diameter four and a 360-state irregular sector of radius five and diameter six. A ten-cycle in any bichromatic two-factor characterizes the Cayley sector exactly; the irregular sector has no such ten-cycle and has odd girth five.
