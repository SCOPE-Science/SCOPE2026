For a squarefree cyclic group with \(r\ge5\) prime factors, every disjoint union of cyclic chains of proper subgroup factorizations covers at most \(2^r-2-\lceil\frac12\binom{r}{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}\rceil\) subgroups. The obstruction comes from the middle Boolean level, is sharp for cycle-cover coverage at \(r=5,6,7\), and leaves the exact longest-cycle problem at larger ranks open.
