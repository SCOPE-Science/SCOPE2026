For the quartic \(Q(z)=395z^4-576z^3-33z+33\), exact rational trapping intervals prove that Newton, Halley, and Chebyshev–Schröder iterations each have an attracting nonroot cycle, of periods two, two, and three respectively. This turns a previously numerical three-method failure example into a rigorous degree-four obstruction, without asserting any common failure basin or minimality across polynomial degrees.
