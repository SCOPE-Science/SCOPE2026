# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For a one-dimensional graph, the descending link is zero-dimensional; it is a simplicial cone exactly when the later vertex has a single earlier neighbor. The first vertex in either bipartition class therefore controls all arrows from the opposite class. A case split on which class appears first exhausts the generating stars and flaps, and explicit vertex orders realize each one. Each mixed simplex lies in a unique flap; deleting its non-pivot faces from larger to smaller cardinality yields genuine free-face collapses without touching stars. The remainder consists of \(m+n\) disjoint full simplices connected by exactly \(2mn\) edges. Quotienting these contractible CW subcomplexes gives the connected doubled complete-bipartite multigraph and exactly \(2mn-m-n+1\) independent circles. Boundaries \(m=n=2\) and arbitrarily large sizes are included; parts of size one are excluded because the nonempty-facet condition changes. Full executable checks corroborate this proof at seven parameter pairs but are not the justification for all parameters.

## Originality — PASS with documented risk
Direct searches targeted the full strong complex, bipartite family, doubled multigraph reduction, and exact circle formula. The recent Knudson–Owens-White introduction (arXiv:2609.06844, 2026-09-06) reports computations on paths, stars, simplices, and cycles, not the arbitrary \(K_{m,n}\) classification in its available indexed abstract. The fully accessible Fernández paper (arXiv:2504.15729) develops the formal matching machinery but does not compute the present complex. Work on ordinary Morse complexes concerns a larger complex of arbitrary acyclic matchings and does not imply the strong result. The previously obtained \(m=2\) special case is strictly narrower: it leaves every \(m\ge3\) open and does not supply the current uniform star/flap decomposition. The motivating paper's complete manuscript could not be read, so an equivalent result in its full text, or in unindexed literature, is a residual risk; the abstract's stated scope supplies positive but non-exhaustive differentiation.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This gives a complete homotopy classification of a naturally symmetric two-parameter family, for which the full simplicial complexes have unbounded dimension, yet all their homotopy concentrates in degree one. The collapse onto the graph \(2K_{m,n}\) identifies an unexpectedly simple structural model and provides exact integral Betti numbers for every bipartition size, rather than adding a routine finite case. The assertion does not rely on a tiny computational improvement or a renamed invariant.

## Limits
The result does not classify the ordinary or pure strong complexes, and makes no claim about equivariant homotopy. The motivating preprint's full text was inaccessible. The reported review is not an independent audit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
