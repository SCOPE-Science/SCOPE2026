Although the Shrikhande graph and the \(4\times4\) rook graph share the strongly regular parameters \((16,6,2,2)\) and both have closed-neighborhood VC dimension \(3\), their complete closed-neighborhood shattering polynomials are \(1+16z+120z^2+224z^3\) and \(1+16z+120z^2+288z^3\), respectively. Their corresponding open-neighborhood polynomials coincide.
