# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The result is a complete statement for all positive hoppings and all normalized two-site pure states. The gapped case follows from smooth two-band Fourier moment convergence and an explicit angular Jacobian identity, while the gap-closing homogeneous case is treated separately. Checks establish the sign of the odd coherence term, nonnegative density, normalization, all displayed moments, and the directional-extremizer bound. The standalone finite checker passes 198 assertions, but the proof rather than the finite experiments supports weak convergence.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualification

Machida (arXiv:2304.06464, Theorem 1) treats only a delta-localized initial state and obtains a symmetric arcsine limit. Boutet de Monvel and Sabri (arXiv:2202.00940, Theorem 5.5) give the prior general arbitrary-initial-state *velocity-pushforward mechanism*, so this result cannot claim that underlying existence theorem. Its narrower contribution is a closed probability density for the coherent two-site dimer, the cancellation that makes the entire density depend on one scalar, and an exact class of directional extrema. The 2018 Harter–Saxena–Joglekar paper covers a distinct lossy-system mean displacement, not the present full unitary density. The general 2022 pushforward theorem implies an integral description, so the size of the explicit-evaluation novelty, and possible unindexed exact antecedents, are publication-priority risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a natural complete classification of asymptotic quantum transport in the smallest nontrivial coherent preparation of the alternating-bond chain studied by Machida: a phase can change directional ballistic bias without changing the full even-moment hierarchy or propagation endpoint. The formula supplies an explicit nonnegative density, CDF and sharp attainable directional imbalance, not merely a formal substitution into a pre-existing quadrature or an unmotivated numerical parameter. These give directly testable signatures of intersite coherence.

## Limits and inspection

The claim is restricted to positive alternating bonds, two-site pure initial data and the weak ballistic observation law. Finite-time local density estimates are not asserted. Relevant full-text sections were read in Machida 2023, Boutet de Monvel–Sabri 2022 and Harter–Saxena–Joglekar 2018. Prior art may exist outside indexed searches.
