# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The scope fixes the two words and their aligned zero-based indices. The established upper-Wythoff indexing of the Fibonacci \(1\)-positions and the two copies of each \(1\) in the Lucas inflation yield exact Beatty identities. The Beatty floor-difference criterion then produces both simultaneous rotation cutoffs. Their interval intersections have the four claimed lengths; all are positive and sum to one. The continued fraction \([0;3,1,1,\ldots]\) supplies bounded-type rotation block estimates and proves the logarithmic discrepancy, also for any fixed finite joined word. The integer Beatty, two independent morphic constructions, and high-precision rotation encoding agree in the first 50,000 positions. These tests are not substituted for the infinite proof.

Ashburn (arXiv:2610.04842v1, Section 5) proves gap-set conjectures for the corresponding OEIS position sequences, not the simultaneous rotation formula, four exact joint densities, or arbitrary joined-block frequency field and logarithmic discrepancy. OEIS A383423–A383427 name the exact joint letter sets and list gap questions; none of the inspected entries states these density formulas. The general Fibonacci mechanical word, Beatty and irrational-rotation tools are prior art and not claimed as new. Targeted equivalent-formulation, coverage and table searches, accessible previous mathematical claims, and published research results found no statement implying this package's joint theorem. An unindexed earlier equivalent computation or unpublished notes remain a genuine priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The exact letter-pair proportions answer an intrinsic quantitative question for four naturally defined integer sets in the motivating 2026 work. Explicit positive covariance establishes dependence that cannot be inferred by multiplying the marginal densities. The common rotation law supplies effective densities and logarithmic counting error for every finite aligned pattern, extending the result from four constants to the entire joined word. The result is thus structural and directly usable in studying joint occurrence statistics, not an arbitrary numerical slice.
