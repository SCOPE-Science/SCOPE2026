In a two-coordinate viscoelastic Brownian model where a Poisson clock resets the probe but leaves its bath unreset, both stationary coordinates share the exact exponential-moment cutoff \(\sqrt{r(\gamma+1)}\) and logarithmic tail slope \(-\sqrt{r(\gamma+1)}\) for every finite \(r,\gamma>0\). Thus the strong-memory Gaussian-looking profile cannot describe the arbitrarily distant tails at any finite bath-memory parameter.
