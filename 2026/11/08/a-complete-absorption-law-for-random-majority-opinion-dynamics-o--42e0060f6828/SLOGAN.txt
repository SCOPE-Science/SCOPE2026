In synchronous random-majority dynamics on the nonbipartite triangular book with two adjacent hubs and an even number \(m\) of common leaves, every trajectory reaches one of two consensus states or a deterministic polarization two-cycle, with exact outcome and entrance-time laws from every initial coloring. When the hubs disagree, the eventual oscillation probability is \(\bigl(1-2^{-m}\binom{m}{m/2}\bigr)/2\) regardless of initial leaf opinions, and tends to one half as the book grows.
