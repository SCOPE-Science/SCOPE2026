In the full reduced one-pole genus-six Artin–Schreier family \(y^3-y=x^7+a x^5+b x^4+c x^2+d x\) over an algebraically closed field of characteristic \(3\), the Jacobian \(a\)-number is exactly \(3\) for \(a\ne0\) and \(4\) on the entire smooth hyperplane \(a=0\). The complete Cartier-iterate ranks are respectively \((3,1,0,\ldots)\) and \((2,1,0,\ldots)\), despite the common genus \(6\) and \(3\)-rank zero.
