For the classical Černý automaton on \(n\ge3\) labeled states, the exact shortest word reaching each prescribed image of size \(n-2\) is given by a four-case formula in its two omitted states. The graded distance spectrum is \(\sum_{\ell=4}^{n+1}(\ell-3)z^\ell+\sum_{\ell=n+2}^{2n}z^\ell\): every length occurs, with a unique longest-distance target.
