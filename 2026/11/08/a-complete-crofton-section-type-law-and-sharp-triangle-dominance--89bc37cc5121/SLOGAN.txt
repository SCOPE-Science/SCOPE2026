For stationary isotropic affine planes conditioned to meet any right rectangular pyramid, the exact probabilities of triangular, quadrilateral and pentagonal sections are given by one elementary one-dimensional quadrature and an explicit dihedral-angle normalizer. Triangles are **always** more likely than pentagons, while the expected section size grows strictly with apex height and lies in the sharp interval \(2+\sqrt2<\mathbb EN<4\).
