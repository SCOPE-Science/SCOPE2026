# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each prime factor p of n with phi(n)=8q, p-1 divides 8q. The only primes with p-1 of this form are 2,3,5 and conditional 2q+1,4q+1,8q+1. A mixed prime is greater than q and cannot occur squared, hence supplies q exactly once. Absent 3^2/5^2, solving alpha(u)+e+2f=3-a for a=1,2,3 yields precisely the four A, three B, two C preimages; q=3 permits 3^2 and q=5 permits 5^2, each giving exactly three additional preimages. Divisibility excludes higher powers and other factors; disjointness and congruence cases follow. Independent totient sieve checked all 34 odd primes q<=149 within bound 6000 (10 values at q=3, 9 at q=5), not used for unrestricted theorem.
