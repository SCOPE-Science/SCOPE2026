For every odd prime \(q\), the full fiber \(\varphi^{-1}(8q)\) is an explicit disjoint union of three prime-conditional sets, completed by known exceptional fibers at \(q=3,5\). The uniform classification settles the first open mirror exponent in He's 2026 problem and determines its parameter-dependent multiplicities; its no-singleton consequence was already implied by Grosswald's theorem.
