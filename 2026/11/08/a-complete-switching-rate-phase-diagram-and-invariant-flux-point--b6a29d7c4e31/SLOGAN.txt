A two-state frequency-jumping quantum emitter has exactly classified switching-rate response: depending on detuning and jump size, faster switching can strictly increase fluorescence, strictly decrease it, or produce one unique interior extremum, with two disconnected enhancement regions at large detuning. At the unique nontrivial invariant point, the integrated intensity remains fixed at one half of its resonant reference while the elastic component strictly gains the intensity lost by the inelastic component.
