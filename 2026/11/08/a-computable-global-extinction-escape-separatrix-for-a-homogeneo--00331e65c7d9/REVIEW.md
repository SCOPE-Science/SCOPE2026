# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The direct polynomial identity yields an exact projective map and a scalar squaring recurrence. Relative to the interior fixed composition, each coordinate ratio moves strictly toward one before renormalization, forcing a decreasing projective spread and compactness away from the simplex boundary; the subsequence argument proves global convergence. The radix-two logarithmic product converges uniformly because its factors have explicit positive bounds. The displayed finite-time log-radius identity then proves extinction, escape, and the critical fixed-point limit, not just a numerical approximation. For equal parameters the simplex norm bounds and the derivative at the balanced composition give strict extrema and the computed quadratic coefficient. The finite checker was run and passed, but is corroborative only.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** The original Shoyimardonov–Rozikov preprint (arXiv:2409.13450v1, Sections 1–4) and 2025 journal article (global-dynamics section and stable-curve discussion) were compared at the level of their claims. They present the map, fixed points, a qualitative two-dimensional separating curve, a two-dimensional polynomial approximation, higher-dimensional invariant subsets, and an invariant unstable line. They do not contain a globally valid, uniformly certified radial basin graph in arbitrary dimension or the sharp balanced-versus-vertex threshold geometry. The convergence component alone overlaps the general nonlinear Perron–Frobenius theory treated by Lins (arXiv:2207.14098); it is explicitly not presented as the isolated novelty. Search results for the map, its aliases and the product-boundary representation did not identify a stronger result that implies the entire theorem. Unindexed and later equivalent formulations remain a publication-priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The originating work explicitly treats the exact shape of its stable separating curve as difficult and supplies a polynomial approximation in dimension two; in dimensions greater than two it establishes only sufficient invariant regions. An all-dimensional exact threshold with a uniform \(2^{-N}\) log-error certificate fills that naturally stated geometric gap, separates every positive starting state, and computes the nonflat shape of the critical boundary. The result is meaningful even though it does not cover parameter chambers where the positive equilibrium is absent. General order-preserving theory supports rather than obviates this specific computable classification.

**Limitations and closest sources.** The 2024 arXiv source and 2025 journal version are the nearest exact-model sources. Lins (2022) is the nearest methodological covering theorem for projective convergence, but does not give the parameter-specific radial product, certified basin boundary, or symmetric curvature. Claims are restricted to the positive-fixed-point chamber and strictly positive starting coordinates. The analytical proof is self-contained; calculations and self-review do not constitute independent validation.
