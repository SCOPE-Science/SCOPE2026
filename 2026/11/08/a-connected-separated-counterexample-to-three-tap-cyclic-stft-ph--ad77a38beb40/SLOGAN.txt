The flat three-tap STFT on every cyclic group of order divisible by three identifies the connected, one-zero-separated signals repeating \((a,b,0)\) and \((b,a,0)\), even when \(a\ne b>0\). In the complex three-periodic two-occupied-site family, every nondegenerate spectrogram has exactly two global-phase classes, disproving an unrestricted half-window connectedness characterization.
