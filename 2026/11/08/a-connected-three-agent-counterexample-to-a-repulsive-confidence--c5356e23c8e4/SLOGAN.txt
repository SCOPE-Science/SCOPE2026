The diameter inequality proposed in Kann–Feng's conjectural Theorem 4.11 fails on a connected three-agent signed path: \((0,1/4,7/4)\) freezes after one step at \((-3/8,5/8,7/4)\), with width \(17/8>2\). An open parameter family gives a positive excess, approaching \(c/2\), while preserving immediate convergence.
