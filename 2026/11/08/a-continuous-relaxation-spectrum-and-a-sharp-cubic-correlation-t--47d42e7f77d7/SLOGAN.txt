In the exactly renewed Haff-cooling model, stationary granular-temperature memory has a strictly positive continuous relaxation spectrum beginning at the reset rate, even though the age dynamics is not reversible. Its covariance has the sharp law \(C(t)\sim qT_0\tau^q m'(r)e^{-rt}t^{-(q+1)}\), giving a cubic correction for physical Haff cooling \(q=2\) and a strictly decreasing effective decay rate.
