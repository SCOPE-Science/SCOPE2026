For a random conductance-weighted spanning tree of \(K_{n,n}\) whose perfect-matching edges have common weight \(t\), the rooted degree is exactly an independent binomial–Bernoulli sum. Its distribution is strictly maximized in convex order at the unbiased weight \(t=1\), while a conductance scale \(t\asymp n\) produces a non-Poisson Poisson–Bernoulli crossover and a sharp leaf-density law.
