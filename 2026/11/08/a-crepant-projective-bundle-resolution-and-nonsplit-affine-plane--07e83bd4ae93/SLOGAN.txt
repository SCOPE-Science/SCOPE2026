The characteristic-two cubic of singular plane conics has a smooth crepant resolution that is a projective-plane bundle over \(\mathbb P^2\), but its smooth complement is a genuinely **nonsplit** affine-plane torsor whose obstruction is the missing monomial \(DEF\) in a Frobenius–Euler sequence. Nonetheless its motivic class is \((1+\mathbb L+\mathbb L^2)(1+\mathbb L^2)\), giving \(\#D(\mathbb F_q)=(q^2+q+1)(q^2+1)\) for every even prime power \(q\).
