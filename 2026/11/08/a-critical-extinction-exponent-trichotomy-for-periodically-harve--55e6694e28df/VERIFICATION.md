# Reproducibility and evidentiary limits

The one-cycle power identity follows algebraically from logarithmic derivatives and the specified pulse factors. The proof uses the exact scalar ODE, uniform comparison for small post-pulse states, and a reciprocal-power telescoping limit. The special case \(\kappa=1\) admits an exact finite-cycle expression.

The numerical script `verify.py` checks the power identity in a direct two-dimensional trajectory and finite-time closeness to the six limiting amplitude formulas at \(\kappa=1/2,1,2\). These checks cannot establish the universal conclusion or historical priority. A separate independent audit has not been performed.
