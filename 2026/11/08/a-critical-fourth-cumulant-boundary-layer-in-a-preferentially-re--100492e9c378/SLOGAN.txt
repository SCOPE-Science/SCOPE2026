In a one-dimensional telegraph-driven particle that resets its position and velocity to a uniformly chosen earlier state, the leading fourth cumulant changes sign at reset rate \(r=2\gamma\), but the cumulant there has a strictly positive finite limit. The unique sufficiently-late fourth-cumulant zero in \([3\gamma/2,5\gamma/2]\) sits above \(2\gamma\) by the sharp amount \(\gamma(3-\pi^2/6)/\log(\gamma t)\), with an explicit universal near-critical excess-kurtosis profile.
