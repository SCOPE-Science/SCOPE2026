For an active particle that resets its position and velocity to a uniformly chosen past state, the fourth cumulant has exactly one zero in the reset-rate interval [3 gamma/2, 5 gamma/2] at sufficiently large observation times. The zero is shifted above 2 gamma by gamma (3 - pi^2/6)/log(gamma t), and the moving-rate excess-kurtosis profile is explicit. The fixed-rate critical cumulant constant was established earlier; the contribution is the uniform finite-time threshold theorem.
