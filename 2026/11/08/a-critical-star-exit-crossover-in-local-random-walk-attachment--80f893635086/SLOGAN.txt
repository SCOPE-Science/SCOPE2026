In local one-edge random-walk attachment started from a star, one-step walks produce an almost-sure but infinite-mean delay before the first nonstar graph, with exact survival \(s/(s+t)\). An arbitrarily small probability of zero-step attachment makes the mean finite, and the crossover at \(q\asymp1/s\) has limiting survival \(e^{-\lambda x}/(1+x)\).
