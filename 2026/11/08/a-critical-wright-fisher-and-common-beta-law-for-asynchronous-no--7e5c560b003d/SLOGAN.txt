For continuous-time noisy voting on \(K_{a,b}\), both diverging part populations synchronize at critical refresh noise, and their common stationary opinion density is exactly \(\operatorname{Beta}(2\lambda,2\lambda)\) when \(\varepsilon/(a^{-1}+b^{-1})\to\lambda>0\). The full rescaled density process converges to a Wright–Fisher diffusion, uniformly over arbitrarily imbalanced part sizes; exact finite-size correlations describe the crossover to fair consensus and deterministic coexistence.
