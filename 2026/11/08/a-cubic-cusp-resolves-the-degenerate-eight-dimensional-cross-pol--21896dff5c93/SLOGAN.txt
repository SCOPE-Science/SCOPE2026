At the quadratically degenerate sparse normal of the eight-dimensional facet-barycenter constrained cross-polytope, relative section volume increases by \(1001\sqrt3\,|s|^3/3072+O(s^4)\) along the null-Hessian geodesic, but decreases by \(3s^2/2+o(s^2)\) along another feasible geodesic. Therefore this normal is a genuine saddle, settling the previously unresolved dimension-eight local type.
