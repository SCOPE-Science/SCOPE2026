For three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on the open range-two chain \(P_n^2\), the number of initially primitive three-periodic configurations has the unexpectedly short generating function \(6/(1-3z+2z^2-2z^3)\). Its exponential growth constant is \(2.5213797068\ldots\), so the fraction of such states among all configurations decays exponentially with chain length.
