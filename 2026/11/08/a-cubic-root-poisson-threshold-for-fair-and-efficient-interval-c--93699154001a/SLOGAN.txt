For adjacent-pair approval elections, Pareto efficiency and justified representation reduce to endpoint and forbidden-run conditions, giving an exact four-state count of jointly fair and efficient committees. If a uniform committee omits \(t\sim\lambda m^{2/3}\) out of \(m\) candidates, its probability of satisfying both Pareto optimality and EJR tends to \(e^{-\lambda^3}\); this sharp window follows from rare three-candidate gaps.
