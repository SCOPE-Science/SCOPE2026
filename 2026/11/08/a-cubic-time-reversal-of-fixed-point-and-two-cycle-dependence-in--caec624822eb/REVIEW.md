# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The fixed-point, two-cycle, and mixed-product character expansions were reconstructed using Frobenius characteristics, Pieri rules, and standard character eigenvalues for the lazy transposition kernel. The centered four-mode formula was checked directly and by exact Markov-state enumeration. The one-switch proof uses a strictly convex normalized forward difference (monotone for the smallest permitted order); the asymptotic sign constant follows from an explicit separated spectral-scale limit. Tests are supplementary, not substitutes for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest primary works are Diaconis–Shahshahani on the shuffle and mixing, Berestycki on early giant-cycle transitions, Berestycki–Kozma on exact single-cycle expectations, and Arcona on powers of individual cycle counts and their mixing. The published claims inspected do not state the same mixed count covariance or its cubic-time reversal. The closest database Fourier sampling finding treats static conjugacy-class spectra rather than this transient law. The 1980 report's full text was unavailable; general character theory makes implicit earlier coverage a genuine priority risk.

## Value — PASS
The pairing is a canonical mixed dependence question under a classical random walk. A unique sign reversal governed by a cubic rather than mixing-scale clock is structural information not apparent from either marginal distribution. The covariance at reversal is very small, so significance is mathematical/spectral rather than an empirical detection claim.

## Limitations
The law is restricted to the ordered-pair lazy shuffle from the identity for \(n\ge6\). Extension to other generators or other cycle sizes is unproved. Independent priority or proof review has not occurred.
