For every \(m\ge3\), the \((m-1)\)-token sliding graph of independent sets on a uniformly once-subdivided \(m\)-arm star comprises \(m\) isometric \((m-1)\)-cubes connected by a degree-two bridge for each edge of \(K_m\). The exact diameter is \(2m\), achieved by configurations differing in just two occupied leaf vertices; a closed all-pairs distance formula is supplied.
