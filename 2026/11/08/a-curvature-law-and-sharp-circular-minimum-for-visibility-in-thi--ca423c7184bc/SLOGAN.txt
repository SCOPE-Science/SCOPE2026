For every positively curved smooth planar convex body, the Beer visibility probability of a uniformly sampled Euclidean inner-parallel shell is asymptotic to \(\frac{8\sqrt2}{3P^2}\sqrt\varepsilon\int_{\partial K}\kappa^{-1/2}\,ds\). At fixed perimeter the leading coefficient is minimized uniquely by disks, with sharp value \(8/(3\sqrt{\pi P})\).
