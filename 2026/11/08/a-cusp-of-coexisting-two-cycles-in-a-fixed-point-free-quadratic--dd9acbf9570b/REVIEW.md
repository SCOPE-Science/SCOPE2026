# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The strict branch inequalities establish two-step alternation. The exact return map is increasing, so every forward orbit converges to a return fixed point, with no longer cycles. The cubic tangency equation and exact fold ordering give the full root census. The rational coexistence example and cusp expansion are checked with exact symbolic arithmetic. The discontinuity at the switching point causes no unaddressed orbit because the left branch owns that endpoint.

## Originality

PASS within inspected evidence. Lawnik–Moysis–Volos (2023, Section 4.1) introduced the power map and its no-fixed-point criterion, and plotted nonchaotic parameter regions; it did not derive the strict-alternation global two-cycle census, algebraic fold surface, or cusp rate. The related 2024 paper has a different construction in its abstract; its full accepted manuscript was inaccessible in the available route and remains a residual risk. The closest research-record result concerns fixed points for \(0<d<1\), not two-cycles for \(d=2\). No assertion of absolute novelty is made.

## Mathematical value

PASS. A natural region of a proposed chaotic-map family is completely nonchaotic but admits distinct coexisting attracting cycles. The exact cusp and global basin threshold provide useful structure beyond sample trajectories, especially in view of the originating motivation to avoid periodic behavior in pseudorandom applications. This is not a security proof, and the classification does not extend to the chaotic parameter regions.
