A fixed-point-free piecewise-quadratic interval map can have two stable two-cycles separated by a repelling two-cycle: its strictly alternating parameter region has an exact three-phase fold diagram with a cusp at \(q=k=3/4\). All orbits approach some two-cycle, and the cusp approaches at the sharp rate \(|t_n-1/3|\sim 2/(3\sqrt n)\).
