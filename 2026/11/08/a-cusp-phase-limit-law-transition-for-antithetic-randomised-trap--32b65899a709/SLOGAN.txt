For complementary randomized trapezoidal quadrature of the centered cusp \(|x-1/2|^\gamma\), the error changes from parity-sensitive non-Gaussian infinite-series limits for \(0<\gamma<3/2\), through a logarithmically normalized Gaussian limit at \(\gamma=3/2\), to a universal \(n^{-5/2}\) Gaussian scale for \(3/2<\gamma\le2\). At the absolute-value cusp \(\gamma=1\), every even grid is exactly integrated while every odd grid has a uniformly distributed error after \(n^2\) scaling.
