The cyclotomic order-two recurrence \(f_{n+2}=-qf_{n+1}-q^2f_n\) with \(f_0=-(t+1)\) and \(f_1=q\) is binomid exactly when \(t\mid q^4\), but is an index-divisibility sequence only when \(t\mid q^2\). Thus every \(q\ge2\) admits a growing, nondivisible binomid recurrence (take \(t=q^3\)), answering the binomid implication in Shapiro's 2023 order-two question negatively.
