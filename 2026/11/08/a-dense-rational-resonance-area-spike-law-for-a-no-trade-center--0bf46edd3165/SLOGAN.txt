On the conservative center line of a three-partition stock-market map, the maximal no-trade region has an exactly computable area: an ellipse value at irrational rotation numbers and a strictly larger, denominator-controlled regular-polygon value at every rational rotation number. Its area is continuous at every irrational parameter and has dense rational spikes with relative height asymptotic to \(\pi^2/(3L^2)\) for \(L\) polygon sides.
