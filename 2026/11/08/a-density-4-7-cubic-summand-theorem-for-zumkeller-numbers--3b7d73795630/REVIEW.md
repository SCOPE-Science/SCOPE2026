# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The explicit half-divisor partition of \(2^kp\) is valid for every odd prime \(p\le2^{k+1}-1\). The congruence construction uses cube-power automorphisms on odd units modulo powers of two and on units modulo \(p^2\) for primes \(p\equiv2\pmod3\). For any \(\nu_2(N)=3j\), the valuation of \(N-2^kp\) is exactly \(3j\) when \(k>3j\); CRT yields a positive cube root, and the growth bound makes its complementary summand positive and Zumkeller. Taking finitely many independent prime obstructions and then increasing their number establishes relative density one in each stratum. The sum of these disjoint stratum densities is \(4/7\). The exact modular tests passed for seven choices but are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The full relevant 2023 paper (Sections 2–5) explicitly suggests studying cube summands but establishes other additive representations. The known two-prime-support criterion is acknowledged and is merely an input, not the asserted new result. Relevant published-finding corpus searches for cube summands and corresponding \(2\)-adic-density statements, together with open-web searches, did not identify an implication-covering theorem. Existing prime- and square-summand density theorems do not imply the cubic statement because primes, squares, and cubes have different local image sets. Unindexed equivalent arguments remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The authors of the motivating paper identify cube-summand representations as a future direction. The new theorem gives a uniform almost-everywhere assertion on every natural cubic-valuation stratum, including the entire odd stratum, and produces the non-arbitrary lower density \(4/7\) from the exact geometric series of those strata. This is a global density result for a natural additive problem, not a routine finite threshold or an arbitrary slice. It does not resolve universal representability or determine the full density.
