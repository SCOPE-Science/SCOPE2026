When exact leaf Gaussian quadrature posteriors are repeatedly recombined by treating child posterior means as new independent pseudo-observations with noise equal to their posterior variances, a balanced tree can exhibit an exact depth-dependent miscalibration. A correctly specified independent-cell Gaussian construction makes the actual error variance exceed the reported variance by \(\frac{1+(h+1)^2v}{1+(h+1)v}\), and every fixed-normal-width credible interval has coverage tending to zero like \(h^{-1/2}\).
