For independent fixed-size coupon batches, the fully uniform sampler minimizes every fixed-time factorial moment of the unseen-count, and equality at rank \(r\) is equivalent to weighted block-design strength \(r\). Uniform projective-plane line sampling matches the full sampler's mean and variance at every time but has a strictly larger third factorial moment after every \(t\ge2\) draws, with an exact two-round gap for every existing plane order.
