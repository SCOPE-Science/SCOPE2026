For every odd prime \(p\ge3\), the complete cyclic \(b\)-symbol weight distribution of lexicographically digit-ordered first-order \(p\)-ary Reed–Muller codes has an exact finite product-and-coefficient formula for all window lengths. Its multiplicities arise from a superincreasing base-\(p\) carry polynomial, because every nonconstant affine word has cyclic zero-runs of only two adjacent power-of-\(p\) lengths.
