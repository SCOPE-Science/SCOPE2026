# Scientific review: full power-semigroup automorphisms for the cyclic group of order four

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses the intrinsic unit group to reduce every automorphism to one fixing the four singleton units. Translation-orbit sizes and the square-to-absorber property then identify the remaining four-, two-, and one-element orbits. The displayed complete sumset multiplication table forces the two-element orbit to be fixed and the co-singleton shift to equal twice the adjacent-pair shift. All four resulting maps preserve every product, and adjoining the two base-group automorphisms gives exactly eight maps with dihedral multiplication. The reduced-submonoid stabilizer is determined by its intersection with the adjacent-pair orbit. A standalone script verifies all 225 ordered products for each candidate and directly enumerates all 32 normalized equivariant orbit-preserving permutations.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest 2026 source is Tringali–Wen, arXiv:2604.26901, in which Conjecture 5.2 asks for reduced-submonoid invariance for all cancellative monoids. The present theorem is an explicit exact classification for the *full* power monoid of the even cyclic group of order four. Rago, arXiv:2510.17533, Theorem 13 classifies only the reduced monoid; it does not determine the full automorphism group or its reduced stabilizer. Antoniou–Tringali, arXiv:1804.10913, Section 5 studies minimal factorization lengths for cyclic-group power monoids, not automorphisms. Earlier available findings include non-characteristic reduced monoids for other finite groups and a classification for order three; none provides or implies the order-four dihedral result, whose additional two-element coset orbit requires a separate argument. No equivalent claim was found in the stated source/record searches; that absence does not establish historical uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The natural next case with even cyclic torsion has a genuinely different orbit decomposition and automorphism group from the previously classified order-three case. The complete \(D_8\) group calculation provides a sharp description of which full-power symmetries extend reduced-power symmetries and shows explicitly that the additional four symmetries violate the general preservation conjecture. A complete structural classification of the first cyclic power monoid with a proper nontrivial subgroup is a motivated exact algebraic invariant, not merely a numeric test.

## Closest literature and limitations
Primary references: Tringali–Wen, arXiv:2604.26901, Sections 2 and 5; Rago, arXiv:2510.17533, Theorem 13; Antoniou–Tringali, arXiv:1804.10913, Sections 3–5. This does not classify larger cyclic orders or arbitrary power monoids. A historically equivalent computation in uncatalogued older semigroup literature remains a priority risk.
