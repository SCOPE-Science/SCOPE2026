The fifteen-element nonempty-subset sum monoid of \(C_4\) has automorphism group \(C_4\rtimes C_2\cong D_8\), rather than merely the two automorphisms of its underlying group. Its reduced submonoid has stabilizer \(C_2\): an explicit symmetry sends \(\{0,1\}\) to \(\{1,2\}\), providing a complete even-order cyclic instance of non-rigidity.
