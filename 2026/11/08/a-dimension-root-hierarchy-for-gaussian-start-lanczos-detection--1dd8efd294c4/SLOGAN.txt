For a Gaussian-start Lanczos probe of a Hessian with one negative eigenvalue and \(q\) distinct positive spectral bands, the critical negative-spike magnitude for detecting negative curvature in \(\ell\le q\) steps is \(n^{1/(2\ell-1)}\), with an exact chi-square transition law. After \(q+1\) steps the negative mode is detected with probability one, regardless of its magnitude.
