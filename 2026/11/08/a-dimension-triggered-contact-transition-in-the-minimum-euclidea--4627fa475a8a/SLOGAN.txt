For Ferroni's sparse box-Cayley polytopes \(P_{d;\alpha,\beta}\), the unique minimum enclosing Euclidean sphere and every contacting vertex admit exact formulas for all integers \(d\ge3\), \(\alpha,\beta\ge1\). When \(\beta>\alpha\), a single cube-apex vertex is screened out in small dimensions but reappears at the sharp algebraic dimension threshold \(d_+(\alpha,\beta)\); the number of sphere-contacting vertices jumps from \(d\) to \(d+1\).
