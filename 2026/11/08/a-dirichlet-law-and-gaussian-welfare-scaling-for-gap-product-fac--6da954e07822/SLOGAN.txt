For uniformly sampled agent locations, product-of-consecutive-gaps facility selection has a Dirichlet \((1,2,\ldots,2,1)\) spacing limit and exact expected approximation ratio \((3k+1)/(2k+1)\), improving strictly on uniformly chosen facilities. Its realized welfare has a nonzero limiting variance for every fixed \(k\), but its optimally normalized ratio has Gaussian fluctuations of variance \(3/(4k)\) in the subsequent high-facility limit.
