For the Hardy composition operator \(f(z)\mapsto f(z^m)\) with integer \(m\ge2\), the closed convex hull of its Berezin range is exactly the convex hull of \(1\) and the disk \(\{w:|w-1/(m+1)|\le1/(m+1)\}\). An AM–GM inequality gives the sharp disk envelope, whose boundary, support function, perimeter, and area are explicit; the original Berezin range meets that envelope's boundary only at \(1\).
