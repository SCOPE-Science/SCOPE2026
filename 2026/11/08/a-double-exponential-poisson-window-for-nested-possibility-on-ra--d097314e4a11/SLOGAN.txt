On two independent uniform \(\mathsf{S5}\) equivalence frames, globally satisfying the nested possibility formula \(\Diamond_R\Diamond_S p\) has a sharp critical window at mark probability \(p_n\sim\log\log n/\log n\). When \(p_nW(n)-\log W(n)\to c\), the obstruction count is asymptotically Poisson with mean \(\exp(e^{-c})-1\), giving the exact limiting satisfaction probability \(\exp(1-\exp(e^{-c}))\).
