# Scientific self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For every squarefree quintic \(f(x)=x^5+ax^2+bx+c\) over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_7\), the tame cyclic triple cover has six total branch points and genus four. The four explicit holomorphic differentials lead by Cartier extraction from \(f^2\) and \(f^4\) to a block matrix with scalar \(2b\) and an explicit \(3\)-by-\(3\) block whose determinant is \(2b\Delta\). The quintic discriminant is independently \(\Delta\), proving the all-parameter identity \(\det H=4b^2\Delta\). On the smooth \(b=0\) locus Cartier has rank two; the exact semilinear iterates, not merely the first matrix, yield geometric ranks zero at \(a=0\), one at \(a^5=c^3\), and two otherwise. Smooth \(b\ne0\) members are ordinary. Three standalone programs independently check symbolic extraction, Frobenius iterate polynomial arithmetic, and the \(\mathbb F_7\)/\(\mathbb F_{49}\) normalized census. The latter finite checks are corroborating, not substitutes for the universal algebraic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Borges–Gonçalves, arXiv:2212.03987 (first public 2022-12-07), study Fermat-type curves \(y^m=x^n+1\), a strict special case; their theorem does not imply this mixed-parameter discriminant identity and complete characteristic-seven rank stratification. Baldwin–Eilbeck–Gibbons–Ônishi, arXiv:math/0612654, study the complex geometry and Abelian functions of general genus-four cyclic trigonal curves, not Cartier rank strata. Lin–Mantovan–Singhal, arXiv:2303.13350 (Theorem 1.1 and Remark 8.4), establish generic p-ordinariness for selected branched abelian covers but do not determine the present six-branch characteristic-seven locus or the four strata. Kudo–Harashita, arXiv:1607.01114, exclude superspecial genus-four curves in characteristic seven but do not determine the present coefficient loci. A separate genus-three Picard family has similar-looking phenomena, but a rank theorem in genus three does not logically imply this genus-four rank classification. An older unindexed or differently parameterized computation could exist; this is a historical-priority risk, not claimed to be eliminated by negative searches.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The nonordinary locus in this natural smooth three-dimensional coefficient family is exactly \(b=0\) with multiplicity two in the Hasse determinant, yet it exhibits three different geometric ranks, and rank three is excluded. The complete rank-zero and rank-one equations and the a-number rigidity distinguish this geometry from generic determinant rank-drop exercises. The statement is a closed classification on a motivated genus-four family, not an arbitrary finite sample. This may inform arithmetic deformations and future Newton-polygon stratifications; the latter is not claimed here.

## Boundaries and verification status

Full Newton polygons, all genus-four cyclic covers, geometric isomorphism-class counts, and independent mathematical validation remain outside the claim. Primary full-text material was compared in sections dealing with relevant objects, methods and results; archival equivalence is not ruled out.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
