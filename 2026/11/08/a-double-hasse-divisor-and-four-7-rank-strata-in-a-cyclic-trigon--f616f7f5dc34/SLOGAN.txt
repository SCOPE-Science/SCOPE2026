In characteristic \(7\), the genus-four cyclic-trigonal family \(y^3=x^5+a x^2+b x+c\) has Hasse–Witt determinant \(4b^2\operatorname{Disc}(x^5+a x^2+b x+c)\), so the nonordinary divisor is a double hyperplane section. On its smooth locus the geometric \(7\)-rank and Cartier \(a\)-number are exactly \((4,0),(2,2),(1,2),(0,2)\), with explicit coefficient equations for all four strata.
