On the cocktail-party graph \(K_{2m}\) with one perfect matching deleted, driving the unique nonneighbor of the sink creates a maximal avalanche of \(2m\) individual topplings with exact probability \(\frac{(2m-3)^{m-1}(2m-1)^{m-2}}{(2m-2)^m(2m)^{m-2}}\). The unique double-toppling site is the drive, and these rare system-spanning events carry asymptotically \(1/e\) of all mean toppling activity.
