For uniform circular gaps, the covariance between the largest gap and the number exceeding a threshold has a universal signed crossover: at \(t=(\log\log n+\log\log\log n+z)/n\), its value multiplied by \(n\) converges to \(1-e^{-z}\). In particular the unique sign-change threshold is \((\log\log n+\log\log\log n+o(1))/n\), far below the typical largest gap.
