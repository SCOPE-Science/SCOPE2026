Weighted recursive trees with extra attachment mass \(w_{2^k}=2^{\beta k}\) at dyadic hubs have a sharp linear-fluctuation threshold: for \(0\le\beta<1\), centered total path length divided by tree size converges almost surely and in \(L^2\), while for \(\beta\ge1\) its variance grows at least logarithmically. The boundary identifies when sparse but persistent attachment hubs destroy the finite-variance linear-scale regime.
