# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The completed-turn observable is regenerative: reflection separates its fair signed marks from the independent waiting times. The piecewise backward equation gives an exact turn-duration Laplace transform. Exponential tilting of the completed-turn renewal equation proves the scaled cumulant generating function, and its explicit positive-rate large-argument asymptotic gives both the factor-four high-current coefficient and the logarithmic correction. The Gärtner–Ellis conclusion uses full-domain differentiability and steepness. An independently discretized boundary-value calculation and root tests passed 105 finite numerical assertions; those tests are checks, not proofs.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, qualified)

The no-reset winding large deviations of Kundu–Comtet–Majumdar, the mean and variance for geodesic ring resetting described by Grange, the stationary ring-current protocol analysis of Tucci and collaborators, and the general reset additive-functional machinery of den Hollander and collaborators were compared by mathematical implication. Those publicly inspected statements do not give the stated positive-reset rare-winding coefficient, its logarithmic correction, or the singular order-of-limits assertion. Broad renewal methodology is known and is not claimed as new. The closest Grange primary full text was inaccessible; only the abstract and first-page publisher preview were readable. Later uninspected results may affect priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Rare winding currents are a natural nonequilibrium observable for transport on a closed geometry. The factor-four change holds for every nonzero return rate, however small, and is not visible from Grange's mean/variance alone. It quantitatively distinguishes the physical shortest-arc return protocol from reset-free Brownian winding in an experimentally meaningful large-deviation regime. The statement does not assert a comparable factor for ordinary diffusivity or other resetting rules.

## Boundaries and outstanding risks

The model counts full geodesic reset-induced turns, not unfinished winding angle; returns have zero physical duration. The paper closest to the model could not be checked beyond its accessible preview. The findings are analytical, backed by finite diagnostics, but are not independently certified.
