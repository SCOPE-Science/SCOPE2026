For Brownian motion on a ring whose Poisson resets traverse the shortest return arc, every positive reset rate reduces the high-winding large-deviation cost to one quarter of the no-reset value: \(I_r(j)\sim L^2j^2/(16D)\), versus \(I_0(j)=L^2j^2/(4D)\). The effect is singular because fixed-tilt statistics nevertheless converge continuously as the reset rate vanishes.
