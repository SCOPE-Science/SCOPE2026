For a uniform perfect matching of \(2n\) circularly arranged endpoints, the total shorter-arc span has covariance \(-n^2(n-1)^2/[6(2n-3)(2n-1)]\) with nested-pair count, but its covariance with the marginally equidistributed crossing-pair count is exactly \(n\) times larger and positive. Their correlations split asymptotically: \(\operatorname{Corr}(S_n,X_n)\to\sqrt{15}/4\), while \(n\operatorname{Corr}(S_n,N_n)\to-\sqrt{15}/4\).
