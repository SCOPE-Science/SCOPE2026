For two eight-point affine simplices over \(\mathbb F_2\) differing in one vertex, a common orthogonal Walsh basis exists precisely when the six-coordinate displacement has Hamming weight zero, three, or four. The exact numbers of common unordered spectra are respectively \(480\), \(96\), and zero in dimension seven, multiplied by \(2^{8(d-7)}\) in ambient dimension \(d\ge7\); Fano-plane incidence explains the sharp incompatibility.
