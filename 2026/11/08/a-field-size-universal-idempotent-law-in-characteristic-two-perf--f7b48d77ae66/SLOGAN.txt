For a uniformly random perfect evolution algebra of dimension \(n\) over **any** finite field of characteristic two, the number of nonzero idempotents has exactly the distribution of \(2^D-1\), where \(D\) is the fixed-space dimension of a uniform \(\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb F_2)\) matrix. The law is independent of field extension degree, and the idempotent-free probability tends to \(\prod_{j\ge1}(1-2^{-j})\approx0.2887880951\) as \(n\to\infty\).
