For the smooth Klein quartic threefold, its 30-dimensional intermediate Jacobian is isogenous over \(\mathbb C\) to the fifth power of a simple CM abelian sixfold. Its rational endomorphism algebra is \(M_5(L)\), where \(L=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{61})^{\langle-3\rangle}\) has degree \(12\); the result is an unpolarized isogeny classification.
