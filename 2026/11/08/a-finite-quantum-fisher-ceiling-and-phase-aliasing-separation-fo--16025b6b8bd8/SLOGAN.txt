In an ideal globally reset Rabi-spin ensemble, a single stationary snapshot has a finite, exactly computable quantum Fisher information ceiling for the Poisson reset rate, no matter how many spins are measured. Two local measurement bases attain the ceiling as the ensemble grows, whereas measuring only \(\sigma_z\) loses nearly all rate information at weak resetting: its limiting information vanishes quadratically while the unrestricted limit approaches \(\pi^2/(12\Omega^2)\).
