At the quarter-turn neutral boundary of a zero-determinant planar border-collision map, an entire unit-area square consists of exact period-four orbits and absorbs every trajectory in finite time precisely for \(0\le a\le1\). Beyond this sharp threshold a unique exterior period-three saddle appears at \(p=a/(a-1)\), separating finite capture from exponential escape; exact capture counts change from logarithmic through linear back to logarithmic near the saddle.
