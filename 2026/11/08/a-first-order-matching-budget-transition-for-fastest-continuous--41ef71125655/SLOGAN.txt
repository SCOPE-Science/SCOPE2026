For continuous-time mixing on \(K_{n,n}\), independently optimizing all edge conductances under a budget gives an abrupt matching-investment transition at the exact relative price \(c_*=2(n-1)/(n-2)\). Below the cutoff every optimal edge is equally weighted, above it the entire matching is omitted, and exactly at the cutoff an explicit interval of equally optimal designs appears; no asymmetric optimizer escapes this classification.
