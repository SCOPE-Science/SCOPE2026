# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The derived ideal is exactly the central line, forcing the block form of all endomorphisms. The bracket-preservation, projection, and central-functional identities are explicitly derived. Isotropic frame counts divided by general-linear frame counts produce the exact finite-rank formula. The exponent is completed to a square and the finite-field correction factors are uniformly bounded by the reciprocal Euler product. Dominated convergence proves the residue-dependent total and total-variation laws. The maximal-rank product has a different, explicitly tracked limit. Direct finite-field enumeration was performed by the packaged checker and reproduced the predicted rank spectra, including characteristic two. The finite checks do not constitute proof of the limits.

## Originality — PASS, subject to publication-priority uncertainty

The closest source is Anil Kumar and Pradhan, arXiv:1908.00331, whose full-text Theorem Ω and Theorems 5.2–5.3 classify and count endomorphisms of the odd-prime Heisenberg group, including isotropic-subspace enumeration. The linear algebra and the isotropic product are therefore not claimed independently new; this exact idempotent formula is a supporting specialization. The assessed new claim is the sharply normalized five-residue discrete-Gaussian retraction-rank law, its four-fifths center, and its maximal-rank tail. These are not stated in the inspected primary text and require a separate centered asymptotic and boundary analysis. arXiv:2310.00624 studies a different operation (transposed Poisson structures), and the 2022 isotropic inclusion-graph paper studies graph invariants. Searches of mathematical records and available earlier claims found no exact covering result. A broader unlocated symplectic-projection asymptotic is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The image ranks of retracts are a natural semigroup invariant of a fundamental nonabelian nilpotent Lie family. The distribution exhibits a nontrivial entropy-vs-projection tradeoff: despite maximal isotropic subspaces of dimension \(m\), a typical retraction has rank approximately \(4m/5\). The exact periodic discrete-Gaussian law and quadratically exponentially small maximal-rank probability provide informative structural information not captured by a total endomorphism count. This is an infinite-family result with a sharp leading factor, not an arbitrary finite slice.

## Limitations and closest literature

The result assumes a nondegenerate alternating form, a one-dimensional derived center, and fixed field order. The odd-prime group classification already supplies much of its algebraic substrate. The 2022 isotropic inclusion-graph publication was inspected at abstract/scope level only, not characterized by an unsupported whole-document negative. Historical records predating the available current-week ledger could not all be retrieved. No statement of independent validation or absolute priority is made.
