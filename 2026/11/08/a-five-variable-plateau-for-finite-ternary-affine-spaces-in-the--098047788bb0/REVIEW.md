# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The classification is restricted to widths one through five in the pure four-ary parallelogram language. A full reconstruction checks that every minimal affine dependency on at most four positions over \(\mathbb F_3\) is represented by equality, a collinear triple, or one of three four-point parallelogram pairings. Explicit formulas detect affine dimensions zero, one, two, and three within the corresponding variable budgets. Permanent pebble strategies prove equivalence above each threshold; the five-variable strategy treats affine-independent four-survivor configurations by replacing an invisible full-support dependency with a four-term affine sum. Direct enumeration and an independent tuple-orbit bisimulation algorithm reproduce the predicted widths two through five. Finite enumerations are corroboration, not substitutes for these proofs.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem supplies a sharp complete classification of a natural unbounded-dimension family at all variable widths through five, including an unexpected stagnation of expressive power from four to five variables. The plateau is structural—four-term atomic equations cannot expose a dependency spanning all five pebble positions—rather than a small numerical observation. Its distinction from the characteristic-two affine setting motivates further work on field-sensitive finite-variable hierarchies. No claim for wider fragments is made.
