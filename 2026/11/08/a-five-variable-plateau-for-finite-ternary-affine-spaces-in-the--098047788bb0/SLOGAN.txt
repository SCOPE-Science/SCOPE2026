Finite ternary affine spaces in the unpointed parallelogram language have exactly \(1,2,3,4,4\) counting-free finite-variable theories at widths one through five. In particular, \(\mathbb F_3^3\) and \(\mathbb F_3^4\) are indistinguishable with five variables even at unlimited quantifier depth, while four variables already distinguish affine dimensions two and three.
