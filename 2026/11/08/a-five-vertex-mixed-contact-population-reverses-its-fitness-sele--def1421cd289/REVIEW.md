# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The object is a specified connected five-vertex mixed-size hypergraph with reproduction proportional to fitness and uniform choice of incident group, followed by full-group copying. The transition probabilities in `RESULT.md` sum to one, all mixed states reach absorption with positive probability, and the \(30\)-dimensional backward equations have a unique solution. An independent orbit-lumped \(22\)-dimensional system reproduces the full singleton fixation vector at each of the three rational test fitnesses. Exact substitution verifies all recorded fractions. Analytic differentiation at neutrality gives \(\rho_H'(1)-\rho_M'(1)=10/993\), and exact opposite signs at \(3/2\) and \(2\) establish a crossover by continuity. No inference of uniqueness is made.

## Originality — PASS (subject to external priority review)

The primary full text of Liu and Masuda (2023) was examined in its model and results sections, especially §2, §3.1 and §3.3. It introduces the update rule and proves neutral uniform initialization but calculates selected all-3-uniform families and reports numerical suppression in four empirical hypergraphs; it does not cover the four-edge mixed-group object or these exact sign comparisons. The full-text graph regime work of Alcalde Cuesta et al. (2018) already proves the qualitative reversal on ordinary graphs; the full available arXiv text of Bhaumik and Masuda (2024) similarly contains transient amplifiers on weighted ordinary graphs. Both are acknowledged as prior art for the phenomenon, but neither imposes a simultaneous multi-site copying event and neither entails the new numerical identities. Research index queries returned no directly covering result. The inaccessible/unindexed literature risk persists; failed search alone is not a novelty proof.

## Value — PASS

The motivating hypergraph work explicitly highlights suppression in its examined complete, cycle, star and empirical networks, and calls for analyses of general hypergraph dynamics and weak-selection expansion. A compact mixed-contact population shows that the selection direction depends on fitness, with a rigorous positive infinitesimal selection coefficient yet opposite outcome at fitness two. The five-site witness is smaller than the seven-site onset reported for unweighted ordinary graph amplifier-to-suppressor examples in the 2018 exact graph census, without asserting minimality among hypergraphs. This is a substantive boundary to broad suppression extrapolations and clarifies why coupling dyadic and triadic events can matter. It is not merely a different notation for a known graph, because one reproduction can change two other individuals at once.

## Scope and residual risk

This proves only one five-vertex witness and a crossover existence, not a classification, nor the location or uniqueness of any crossing. The finite-state exact-rational certificate is independently reproducible, but external priority and independent mathematical audit remain outstanding.
