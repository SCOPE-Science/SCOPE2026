For strictly ultra-log-concave, full-support laws with fixed mean one, the constant-birth indicator Stein sup-norm factor can stay near \(1/w\) even as the standard deviation vanishes. Explicitly, for an independent \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1-\varepsilon)+\operatorname{Poisson}(\varepsilon)\) sum with \(0<\varepsilon\le1/2\), the exact factor is \((1-\varepsilon e^{-\varepsilon})/w\), disproving the conjectured universal \(C\sigma/w\) bound.
