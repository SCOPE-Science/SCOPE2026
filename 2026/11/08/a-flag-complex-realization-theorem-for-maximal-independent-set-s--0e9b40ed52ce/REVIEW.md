Counting module choices gives the full polynomial, while selecting the core-positive branch down any complete online query tree proves the matching adaptive upper bound. The star example and induced flag-complex realization follow exactly; independently exhaustive finite tests support, but do not replace, these unrestricted arguments.

Baker, Vander Meulen and Van Tuyl (arXiv:1606.04447, sections 1–3) supply the established pure vertex-decomposability of fully whiskered graphs, but not the support-polynomial, flag realization, online dimension, or nonpurity contrast for the *shattering* complex. Searches for whiskered VC dimension, maximal-independent-set shattering, independence-polynomial substitution, and general flag-complex realizations found no covering theorem. Previously established maximal-independent-set shattering criteria for forests and cographs cover restricted instances, but do not give the arbitrary-core realization. The related known graph-construction facts are explicitly attributed, and the possibility of a derivation or earlier formulation under different terminology is not excluded.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** A universal structural transfer identifies every query support for an infinite class of graphs, recovers every coefficient and both learning dimensions, transfers arbitrary finite flag complexes to a well-covered graph's query structure, and gives an unbounded failure of purity even when the graph's own independence complex is pure vertex-decomposable. The graph-family restriction and elementary nature of the transfer are made explicit.
