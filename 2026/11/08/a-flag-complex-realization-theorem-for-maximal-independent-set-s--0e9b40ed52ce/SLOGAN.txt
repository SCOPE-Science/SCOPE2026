The shattered-support complex of the maximal independent sets of a graph with \(t\) private leaves per vertex is exactly the clique-blow-up independence complex of its core graph, with polynomial \(I_G((t+1)z)\) and both VC and Littlestone dimensions equal to \(\alpha(G)\). At one leaf per vertex, even a pure vertex-decomposable well-covered graph can have an arbitrarily nonpure shattering complex, and every finite flag complex appears on a set of its queries.
