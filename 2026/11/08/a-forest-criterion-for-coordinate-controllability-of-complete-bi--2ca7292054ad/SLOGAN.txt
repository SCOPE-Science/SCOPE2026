For the unit-weight Laplacian on the once-subdivided complete bipartite graph \(S(K_{p,q})\), independently driven vertices give full controllability exactly when the unactuated inserted vertices form a forest, with at least one original vertex also controlled if and only if \(p=q\). The minimum actuator count is \((p-1)(q-1)+\mathbf1_{p=q}\), and every minimum placement is the complement of a labeled bipartite spanning tree among inserted vertices, together with an arbitrary original vertex in the balanced case.
