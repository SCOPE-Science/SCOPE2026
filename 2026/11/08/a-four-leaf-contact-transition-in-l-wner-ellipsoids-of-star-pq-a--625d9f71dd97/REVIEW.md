# Scientific assessment: star PQ-adjacency Löwner ellipsoids

## Correctness — PASS
The result reconstructs the complete \(3m+1\)-vertex set of the PQ-type adjacency polytope of \(K_{1,m}\) as a union of coordinate-square vertices. The proposed nonnegative weights have unit total mass and an exact center and positive-definite covariance. Substitution into the three complete vertex-orbit quadratic-form expressions gives \(2m\), \(2m\), and either \(2m\) (for \(m\le4\)) or \(8\) (for \(m\ge4\)). The weighted-covariance trace and determinant AM--GM argument proves minimum enclosing volume and uniqueness against nonsymmetric or off-center competitors. An exact-rational standalone verifier independently reconstructs the covariance for \(m=1,\ldots,9\). The intrinsic metric and volume Jacobian are explicitly accounted for. The finite calculation is supporting evidence, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk
Ohsugi--Tsuchiya (arXiv:2103.15045v1, Sections 1 and 4) give the PQ construction and its Ehrhart and normalized-volume formulas; the star specialization has normalized volume \(2^m\). They do not derive an enclosing ellipsoid, its four intrinsic axis classes, or the four-leaf tangent-orbit transition. The closest located Löwner result for PQ polytopes concerns **balanced** \(K_{r,r}\) with \(r\ge2\); its hypotheses do not include a star \(K_{1,m}\), and it has all vertices tangent, unlike the new star regime \(m\ge5\). The root-polytope lattice equivalence is not an implication of an intrinsic Euclidean ellipsoid formula. Direct published-finding corpus and literature searches for the PQ star and free sums of orthogonal squares did not locate equivalent coverage. An unindexed general formula for free sums or D-optimal designs could nevertheless imply the result and remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Stars form the natural smallest connected complete-bipartite join class in a studied family of adjacency polytopes. The theorem gives a closed form for the unique Löwner ellipsoid in every intrinsic dimension \(2m\), its four squared-semiaxis classes, exact intrinsic volume, and *all* contacts. The exceptional leaf threshold \(m=4\), after which the hub-diagonal vertex loses contact while the other three orbits remain tangent, is a nontrivial phase distinction. For \(m\ge4\), two axis classes stabilize independently of dimension. These metric geometric conclusions are not implied by existing normalized-volume formulas, and give exact benchmarks for ellipsoidal rounding of graph-root polytopes.

## Limits
The theorem applies to the canonical PQ realization for star graphs and not to arbitrary complete bipartite graphs or alternative coordinate metrics. Published-record overlap checks cannot prove historical uniqueness. The all-dimensional proof is self-contained, but an independent mathematical audit has not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
