In the unit tube simplex-sum stellohedron of a star with \(m\ge2\) leaves, the covariance of uniformly sampled vertices has exactly two intrinsic eigenvalue classes, given by explicit truncated-exponential expressions. Its dominant axis is hub-versus-leaves for \(m=2,3\) and leaf-contrast for every \(m\ge4\); the leaf-contrast variance grows as \(4^m/(3m)\), while the hub-axis variance converges to \(e^3-e^2\).
