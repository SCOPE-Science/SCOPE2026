For parity neighbor-sum dynamics on the distance-three graph of every binary \(n\)-cube, the full rank and orbit census follows a four-phase law in \(n\bmod4\): the update is either square-zero or an involution. In the two exceptional square-zero phases, exact rank deficits are controlled by powers of two of order \(2^{n/2}\), and the resulting counts determine every extinction-time or primitive two-cycle class.
