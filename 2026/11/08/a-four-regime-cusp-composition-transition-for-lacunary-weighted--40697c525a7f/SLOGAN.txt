For the fractional-cusp composition symbol \(\psi_\theta(z)=1-(1-z)^\theta\), geometric lacunary coefficient weights in a Hilbert Hardy space exhibit exactly four regimes: Hilbert–Schmidt, compact but non-Hilbert–Schmidt, bounded noncompact, and unbounded. The sharp power boundary is \(\alpha=1/\theta\); its logarithmic thresholds are \(\gamma=-1\) and \(\gamma=0\), with exact Hilbert–Schmidt divergence residues \(A_\theta/\log b\).
