For the real affine least-squares projector on the unit metric graph \(K_{m,n,1}\), with \(m,n\ge2\), the sharp maximum-norm amplification has a five-term rational kernel formula and is attained only at vertices in a largest nonsingleton part. Its value is below two exactly when \(\max(m,n)\le3\), and exceeds two exactly when \(\max(m,n)\ge4\), despite the independently variable size of the other part.
