For four disjoint congruent tetrahedral spherical caps, the pure degree-three harmonic concentration operator has three explicit eigenvalue branches with multiplicities \(1,3,3\). Its maximizing eigenspace jumps from dimension three to dimension one at the exact cap height \(c=\sqrt{(9+\sqrt{33})/24}\), where a four-dimensional top eigenspace forms; the complete analytic spectrum and all equality modes are determined.
