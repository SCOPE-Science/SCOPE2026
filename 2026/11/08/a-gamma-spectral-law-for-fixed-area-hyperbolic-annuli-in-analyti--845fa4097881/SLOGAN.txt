In analytic-wavelet localization to a centered annulus of fixed hyperbolic area escaping toward the disk boundary, the maximally concentrated Bergman monomial degree follows an exact one-step staircase, while the rescaled eigenvalues converge to the sharp gamma profile \(h x^\rho e^{-x}/\Gamma(\rho)\). The trace remains exactly \(\rho h\), but the operator norm decays as \(u^{-1}\) and every positive Schatten moment has an explicit sharp constant; the effective spectral rank grows linearly in \(u\), with a leading coefficient independent of the annulus area.
