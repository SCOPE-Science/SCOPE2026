# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim includes explicit Gaussian normalization, the exact diagonal Poisson-tail formula for every singular value, and the moving-circle parameterization. Polar integration directly verifies eigenvalues; opposite Poisson-tail products are positive and summable. A uniform-in-compact-sets Poisson CLT together with Chernoff control of two far tails yields full Schatten-p moments for each fixed positive p, not just integer moments. A strictly decreasing even Gaussian product gives the operator-norm and counting limits. The exact trace formula is a nonnegative finite-count identity followed by Tonelli; the Gaussian positive-part integral yields the stated coefficient. The included checker tested 225 integer pairs and 12 finite-parameter comparisons. The finite tests do not establish the infinite-dimensional theorem.

## Originality — PASS (qualified best-knowledge)
The closest motivating paper of Qin treats the criterion for vanishing Schatten semi-commutators for each fixed bounded second symbol and does not explicitly supply a two-boundary microscopic-displacement singular-value law in its accessible abstract. Apredoaei treats single fixed indicator domains and traces of functions of one Toeplitz operator; Fenzl–Lambert treat disk-count fluctuations and could imply the p=1 covariance coefficient. Our full arbitrary-positive-p spectral moment and singular-counting law is a joint moving-domain statement and is not established by the available inspected statements. Prior general theorems may still imply it under a different formulation, and full-text access was unavailable for these three plausible sources. This is a qualified best-knowledge assessment, not priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The boundary width of Fock indicator quantization is proportional to \(\sqrt t\). The exact one-parameter crossover quantitatively determines how two close geometric interfaces interact, including all Schatten moments (and the quasi-norm range), the full limiting singular distribution and an exact finite-scale Poisson/Skellam trace representation. It interpolates between the coincident-disk boundary defect and suppressed interaction at separated interfaces, and directly refines qualitative convergence tests for this natural two-domain family. The result is not an arbitrary parameter evaluation.
