For the compact Hardy operator \(f\mapsto(1+c z^m)f^{(d)}(rz)\) with fixed positive derivative order, the first correction to its scaled norm is a sharp Gaussian-oscillator term proportional to the tap separation \(m\). This produces an exact sign transition at \(m=4\) when \(d=1\) and \(|c|=1\), a phenomenon absent from the undifferentiated boundary-edge model.
