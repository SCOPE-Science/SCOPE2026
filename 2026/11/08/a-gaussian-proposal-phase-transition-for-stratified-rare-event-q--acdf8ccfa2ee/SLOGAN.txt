For stratified Gaussian importance sampling of a fixed normal tail probability, narrowing the proposal below unit variance creates a non-Gaussian endpoint-driven error with sharp \(n^{-s}(\log n)^{-(1-s)/2}\) normalization and a Pareto limit; variance is infinite for \(s\le1/2\). At or above unit proposal variance, the leading error instead comes from the threshold-straddling cell and follows an arithmetic-phase Bernoulli law.
