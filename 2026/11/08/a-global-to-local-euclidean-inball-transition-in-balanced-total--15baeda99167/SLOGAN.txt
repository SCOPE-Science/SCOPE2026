The exact Euclidean inradius of the total-matching polytope of \(K_{n,n}\) is \(\bigl[\max\{n+2+\sqrt{1+2/n},n+1+\sqrt{n+1}\}\bigr]^{-1}\), with a unique all-equal-coordinate incenter. Its tangent facets switch sharply from the full balanced-biclique facet at \(n=2,3\) to all \(2n\) degree facets at \(n\ge4\), in addition to every coordinate facet.
