For Everett's farthest-orthogonal-projection map on the three complete supporting lines of an isosceles triangle with half-base 1 and height h>0, the complete nonvertex primitive four-cycle count is exactly one for h^2<(1+sqrt(5))/2 and zero otherwise. This all-height census rules out every alternative four-step itinerary; the golden-ratio coexistence window and base-repeated four-cycle for h>1 had already been published and are not claimed as new.
