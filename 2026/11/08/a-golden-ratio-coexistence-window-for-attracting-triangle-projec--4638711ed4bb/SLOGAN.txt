For the farthest-orthogonal-projection map on the complete supporting lines of an isosceles triangle, precisely one attracting primitive four-cycle exists if and only if the height satisfies \(h^2<(1+\sqrt5)/2\). This yields an exact coexistence window \(1<h<\sqrt{(1+\sqrt5)/2}\) with two attracting primitive three-cycles and one attracting primitive four-cycle, while no other cycles of these two periods exist.
