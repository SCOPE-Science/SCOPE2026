For the algebra groups of four-vertex shortcut quivers with any number of parallel \(1\to3\) arrows, the complete conjugacy-class-size distribution is explicit over every finite field. The number of classes is \(q^m(2q^{m+2}+2q^3-q^m-q^2-2q+1)\), and an arbitrarily long gap of absent class sizes occurs as \(m\) grows.
