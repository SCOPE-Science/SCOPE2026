PASS. Euclidean mean width is the first intrinsic-volume-type size parameter for a polytope and measures its average directional extent. The exact all-parameter expression supplies this natural missing metric for a well-studied family. The complete proportional-rank profile detects a geometrically meaningful threshold at half density: beyond it the positive coordinates in a typical direction, rather than the label budget, constrain support. The sharp nonanalytic third-order transition is not a mere table value and gives an interpretable asymptotic shape classification. No result for arbitrary generalized permutahedra is claimed.
