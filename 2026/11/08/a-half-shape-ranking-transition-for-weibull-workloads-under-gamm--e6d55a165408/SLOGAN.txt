For independent Weibull-workload jobs whose actual durations are sums of one exponential service time per workload unit, the largest-workload and longest-duration jobs coincide asymptotically with probability \(1\) for shape \(\beta<1/2\), with probability \(0\) for \(\beta>1/2\), and with an explicit nontrivial probability \(p_\star\approx0.744414083\) exactly at \(\beta=1/2\). The boundary arises when the spacing of extreme Weibull workloads matches the square-root fluctuations of Gamma service time.
