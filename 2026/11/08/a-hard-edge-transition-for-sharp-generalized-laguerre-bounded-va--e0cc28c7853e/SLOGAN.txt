For generalized Laguerre coefficients of bounded-variation functions, the sharp norm undergoes a hard-edge transition at \(\alpha=-1/2\): below that value the unique worst jump is at the first polynomial zero and the norm decays as \(n^{-1}\); above it the worst jump localizes at \(\alpha+1/2\) with an explicit \(n^{\alpha/2-3/4}\) constant. An exact Sonin envelope also reduces every finite-degree maximization to one or two candidate zeros.
