In bipartite discrete Huygens networks with interaction \(\sin t+\kappa\sin(3t)\), the exact threshold \(\kappa=-1/27\) separates attracting supercritical and unstable subcritical two-cycles near phase opposition. At the threshold the first nonzero nonlinear saturation is fifth order, and the attracting cycle grows with the fourth root of excess coupling rather than the usual square root.
