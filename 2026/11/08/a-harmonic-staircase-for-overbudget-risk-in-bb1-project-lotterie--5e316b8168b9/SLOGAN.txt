For a budget of \(k\) unit-cost projects and one higher-cost project of cost \(c=m+f\), the optimal probability of exceeding budget under marginal-preserving BB1 lotteries falls along the sharp harmonic staircase \(q(1-f)/(j+1)\) as the permitted maximum overrun passes \(f+j\). No lottery is feasible below the first threshold \(f\), and each attainable optimal lottery uses at most \(2k+1\) outcomes.
