In a fitness-dependent locally reset zero-range mean-field model, co-occupied sites develop a universal pair-crowding singularity already at reset-vanishing exponent \(\nu=1/2\), although mean density remains finite throughout \(0<\nu<1\). The integrated ordered-pair intensity is finite below that threshold, grows as \(4C^{-2}\log(1/\zeta)\) at it, and has a sharp \(\zeta^{1/\nu-2}\) divergence above it.
