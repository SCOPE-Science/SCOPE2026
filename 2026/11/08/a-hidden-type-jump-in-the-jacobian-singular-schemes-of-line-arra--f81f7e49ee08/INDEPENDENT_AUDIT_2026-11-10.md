## correctness

PASS

Unit invariance reduces the local curve to a squarefree homogeneous binary product F of m distinct linear factors. In tame characteristic, Euler gives F in (Fu,Fv), and no common projective zero of derivatives makes them a complete intersection of degrees m-1. When p|m>=3, Euler forces Fu=vH,Fv=-uH with H degree m-2. Squarefreeness gives gcd(F,H)=1; (F,uH,vH) has height two. Explicit Hilbert–Burch matrix with maximal minors F,uH,vH yields minimal syzygy shifts m and 2m-2, common Hilbert series (1-t^(m-1))²/(1-t)², length (m-1)², socle degrees m-2 and 2m-4. The m=2,p=2 node has algebra k and type one. Independent finite characteristic-three m=3 F=uv(u-v) confirms Hilbert [1,2,1], length4, socle dimension2; finite example is not general proof.
