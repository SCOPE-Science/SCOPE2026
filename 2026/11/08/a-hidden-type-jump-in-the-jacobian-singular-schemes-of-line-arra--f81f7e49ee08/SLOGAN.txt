For every reduced plane line arrangement, an ordinary \(m\)-fold intersection has local Tjurina length \((m-1)^2\) and the same Hilbert function in every characteristic, but its Jacobian singular scheme is Gorenstein exactly when the characteristic does not divide \(m\), or \(m=2\). When the characteristic divides \(m\ge3\), its socle has dimension two and its entire minimal graded resolution is explicit.
