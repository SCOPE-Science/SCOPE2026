For a friendship graph built from \(r\ge2\) triangles meeting at one hub, any pebble configuration of size at least \(4r-1\) with an occupied hub can be cleared to a single pebble, so \(\operatorname{stack}(F_r)\le\operatorname{clear}(F_r)\le4r\). Exhaustive exact analysis shows both inequalities attain the bound for the two- and three-triangle friendship graphs; sharpness for arbitrary \(r\) is not asserted.
