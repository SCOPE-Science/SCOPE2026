For second-order point-mass Cesàro operators on \(H^2\), the complete singular-value sequence, multiplied by \((-\log r)^2\), approaches the reciprocal square roots of positive zeros of an explicit entire hypergeometric Wronskian, with an \(\ell^2\)-error of order \(O(-\log r)\). Exact second and fourth Schatten moments independently fix the limiting normalization.
