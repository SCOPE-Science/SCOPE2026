# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof starts from a conditional uniform relative ordering of the currently unenvied assigned schools and the remaining schools. The transition probability \(r(s)_{\underline{k}}/(s+r)_{\underline{k+1}}\) is a successive-sampling probability and sums to one. First-choice event inclusion in the zero-destruction case gives the joint transition. Conditional survival yields the exact cross-moments, with all boundary cases including \(n=1\) stated. The auxiliary exact-rational checker agrees with independent exhaustive enumeration of complete preference profiles through four students and tests the closed moments to seven. The finite test is not used as a substitute for a universal proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk

The full text of Ortega et al. (2026), Section 3, supplies separate means but no bivariate distribution or cross-moment; the companion Ortega et al. (2024/2025), inspected through its main and appendix sections, studies DA envy digraphs rather than the RSD joint statistic. Myers–Wilf's singleton-coupon count gives a marginal, not the dependence on RSD first-choice assignments. Published research-record searches of random-priority inefficiency and marginal-law work found no covering statement, and the previous marginal result does not imply the bivariate coupling. A differently phrased coupon-collector joint law or unindexed matching analysis may have anticipated part of the theorem, which remains the main originality risk. No absence-of-hit is treated as proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

The two forms of envy-free status are introduced and compared separately in the motivating 2026 market-design paper. Their overlap and dependence are necessary to quantify how many *distinct* students satisfy at least one, rather than double-counting the two known expectations. The theorem determines the complete finite joint distribution and an exact all-market union mean; this is a natural mathematical gap with potential use for finite-market fairness benchmarking. The bivariate recurrence and union calculation are not a restatement of either single-variable expectation.

## Scientific limits

The theorem applies to complete, independent, uniform, strict preference lists and square one-seat serial dictatorship. Its union is only a union of two specified envy categories, not an assertion about all Pareto-unimprovable agents. Exhaustive verification ends at four students; higher-dimensional statements rest on analytic proof. Residual risk of equivalent coupon formulations warrants independent checking.
