For a uniform partition of \(n\), the difference between the total sizes of its odd and even parts has an exact product-form distribution and a Gaussian lattice-local limit on the scale \(n^{3/4}\). Its limiting variance coefficient is \(2\sqrt6/\pi\), and exact parity-mass balance occurs with probability asymptotic to a computable constant times \(n^{-3/4}\) only when \(4\mid n\).
