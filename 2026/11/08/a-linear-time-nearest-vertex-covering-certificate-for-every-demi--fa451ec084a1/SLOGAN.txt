For every even-parity demihypercube in dimension \(n\ge4\), **every Euclidean-nearest vertex** \(v\) to any point \(x\) yields an optimal convexification witness \((nx-v)/(n-1)\) inside the same polytope; the vertex can be chosen in linear time by parity-corrected coordinate signs. The exact Schneider index is \(n-1\), with sharpness witnessed by simplex facets, although that numerical index also follows from earlier general boundary criteria.
