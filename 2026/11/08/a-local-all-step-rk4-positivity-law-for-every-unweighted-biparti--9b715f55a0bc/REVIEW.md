# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The argument covers every finite connected simple unweighted bipartite graph. Signless-Laplacian walk expansion yields exact formulas for all distances through four. Distance-two summands are positive by a strictly negative quadratic discriminant, edge-cubic derivatives have a strictly negative discriminant bounded by \(-s^4/72\), and all diagonal entries are positive by the scalar RK4 integral formula and positive-semidefinite spectral calculus. Exact integer/rational replay covers 3,249 labelled connected bipartite graphs of orders two through six and a seven-vertex edge-controlled witness. The infinite-size conclusion follows from the algebraic proof, not the finite enumeration.

**Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, with residual search risk.** The primary full texts of Gottlieb–Shu–Tadmor (2001) and Horváth–Song–Terlaky (2014) were inspected at the method and positivity-threshold sections. The former is a general SSP framework; the latter permits polynomial-zero tests and explicitly acknowledges that the first zero need not give global all-step behavior. Neither supplies the bipartite graph's exact distance-class sign law or edge-cubic discriminant proof. Earlier regular-bipartite and directed-cycle specializations were compared by implication; the regular-bipartite result is subsumed but does not cover arbitrary irregular bipartite graphs. Named semantic research searches and targeted literature searches found no exact equivalent, but unindexed specialist coverage remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A broad and natural class of graph heat systems acquires an exact, local and globally sharp positivity law without any global spectrum computation. The nontrivial permanent positivity of distance-two entries, the no-recovery property, and two independent negative-entry mechanisms are useful to select admissible timesteps. The seven-vertex irregular double-star witness proves that simply using a distance-three bound would be false; the six-vertex path provides the opposite active mechanism. This is not a mere graph-size substitution or a table lookup.

**Limits.** Unweighted undirected bipartite graphs and completed RK4 endpoints only; no weighted or nonbipartite extension, no internal-stage guarantee, and no claim of exhaustive publication-priority verification. The code supplies finite consistency checks, not an infinite proof.
