# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The weighted Bergman monomial basis and radial beta integral give exact eigenvalues. A quantitative beta-to-gamma scaling estimate uniform in the logarithmic phase handles the discontinuous binary indicator. The harmonic multiplier series yields a smooth nonconstant response; the logarithmic sample phases fill its entire range. Ordinary and logarithmically weighted spectral measures follow from separate, explicitly justified Riemann sums. The small-weight expansion is uniform because nonzero Gamma Fourier modes decay exponentially, and the large-weight limit follows from Gamma concentration. The standalone numerical checker agrees with incomplete-beta annulus integration and checks representative rates; numerical assertions are not treated as proofs.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
The radial eigenvalue integral, log-metric slowly oscillating operator algebra, and single-frequency Gamma attenuation are all known and expressly acknowledged. The inspected papers by Rozenblum–Vasilevski, Suárez, Grudsky–Maximenko–Vasilevski, Looi, and Taskinen do not assert a complete phase-parametrized ordinary eigenvalue empirical distribution for a binary self-similar annular observation region, nor its logarithmic restoration and weight-collapse coefficient. The closest available research finding concerns a logarithmic spectral filter for Fock-space Dixmier traces, which is a different operator/statistic. A more general historical Mellin-convolution theorem might imply this specialization; source searching cannot establish uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The self-similar annuli are a natural observation mask, with a noncompact concentration operator whose *entire essential spectrum* is a strict interval inside \((0,1)\), permitting uniform sampling of both the mask and its complement. The cutoff eigenvalue distributions are genuinely nonconvergent and admit a complete continuum classification, whereas a canonical logarithmic weighting gives a unique limit. Their spectral interval collapses at an explicit positive linear rate in the degenerating Bergman weight and opens to the full unit interval in the opposite regime. These are structural statements, not isolated table values.

## Limitations and literature comparisons
The theorem is restricted to the weighted analytic disk, fixed log-periodic binary mask, and fixed-weight cutoff limits. It does not establish a universal nonradial statement or finite-index optimal concentration ratios. Papers in the references have broadly related radial Toeplitz spectral characterizations, and historical-priority risk remains. The theoretical proof is internal, while the numerical checks provide independent computations of only finite instances.
