For a two-state Markov chain with unequal positive jump rates \(a>b\), the minimax mismatch entropy acquires a sharp negative \(at\log\log(1/t)\) correction: its exact small-time form is \(at[L_t-\log L_t]+o(t)\), where \(L_t=\log(1/((a-b)t))\). Its unique optimal input prior places only \(a/((a-b)L_t)(1+o(1))\) mass on the slower-escaping state, whereas equal rates retain a fair optimal prior and have no log–log correction.
