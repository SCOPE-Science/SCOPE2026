Three boundary-arclength-uniform points of a \(L\)-by-\(1\) rectangle form an acute triangle with probability \((3\log L-3/2)/L^2+O((\log L)/L^3)\) as \(L\to\infty\). The logarithm comes precisely from configurations with two points on one long edge and one on the opposite long edge; all other configurations contribute only a lower-order probability.
