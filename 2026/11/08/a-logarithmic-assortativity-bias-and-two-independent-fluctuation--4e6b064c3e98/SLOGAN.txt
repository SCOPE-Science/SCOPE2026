In symmetric soft two-community uniform attachment, the predictable cross-community edge count has a sharp logarithmic bias \(2q(1-q)(1-2q)\log n\) whose sign reverses at neutral affinity. Its \(\sqrt{\log n}\)-scale Gaussian fluctuations are asymptotically independent of the \(\sqrt n\)-scale edge-choice noise, whereas hard community-choice attachment has exactly binomial cross-edge counts.
