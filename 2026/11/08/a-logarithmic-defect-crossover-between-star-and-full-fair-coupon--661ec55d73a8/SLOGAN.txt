In fair coupon collection, the star mechanism and the full uniform mechanism exhibit a one-round expectation reversal when the center deficit satisfies \(c=\log m-\tfrac12\log\log m+x+o(1)\). The full mechanism has a Bernoulli last-round crossover with mean \(1-\exp(-e^x/\sqrt{2\pi})\), whereas star completion occurs in \(c+1\) rounds with probability tending to one.
