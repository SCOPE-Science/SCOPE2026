A clique with one sparsely connected vertex exhibits a sharp \(N/\log N\) degree window for random-greedy dimer jamming: if \(d_N\log N/N\to c\), exactly two vertices remain uncovered with limiting probability \(e^{-c}\). The graph has a perfect matching as soon as \(d_N\ge1\), but the deposition process can still miss it with asymptotically positive probability.
