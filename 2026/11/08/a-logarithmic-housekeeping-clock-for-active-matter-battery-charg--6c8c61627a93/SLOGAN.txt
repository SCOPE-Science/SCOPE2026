For a two-state active-matter battery, weak housekeeping selects an optimal charging time growing as \(\lambda t_* =\log(1/h)+\log[(1-\rho)/\rho]+o(1)\), while the best fuel efficiency loses a sharp \(\rho h\log(1/h)\) term. At weak active drive the complete optimal-time curve is unique and strictly decreases with housekeeping, tending to the maximum-power clock at high cost.
