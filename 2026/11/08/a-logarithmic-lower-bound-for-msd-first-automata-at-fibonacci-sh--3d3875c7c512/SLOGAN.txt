For every Fibonacci shift \(c=F_k\) with \(k\ge4\), the first difference between the Fibonacci word and its shift occurs exactly at \(F_{k+1}-2\). This forces every most-significant-digit-first Zeckendorf automaton for the shifted word to have at least \(\lceil k/8\rceil\) states, making the published \(O(\log c)\) upper bound asymptotically sharp on this infinite family.
