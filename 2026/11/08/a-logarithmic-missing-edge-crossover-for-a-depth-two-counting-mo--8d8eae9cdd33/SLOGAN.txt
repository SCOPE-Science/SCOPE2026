The counting-modal formula \(\#(\#\top=2\#p)\ge\#(\#\top=2\#p-1)\), whose probabilities oscillate on regular rooted trees, has an exact logarithmic missing-edge transition on near-complete random directed graphs. With absent-edge probability \(\varepsilon_n\to0\), its root truth probability is \(3/2-\Phi(\sqrt{\varepsilon_n\log n})+o(1)\), ranging from \(1\) to \(1/2\).
