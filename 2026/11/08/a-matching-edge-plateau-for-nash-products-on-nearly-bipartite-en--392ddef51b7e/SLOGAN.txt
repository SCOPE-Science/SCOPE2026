For symmetric aversion-to-enemies games whose hostility graph is \(K_{a,b}\) plus \(t\) disjoint edges within one part, the exact maximum Nash product, all optimal coalition sizes, and the number of maximizing partitions admit closed formulas. The first added enemy edge forces a third coalition and causes an asymptotically quadratic product loss, while adding a second disjoint enemy edge leaves the optimal product unchanged but changes its optimizer multiplicity.
