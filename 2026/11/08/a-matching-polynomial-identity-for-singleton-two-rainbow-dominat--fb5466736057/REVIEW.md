# Scientific review: singleton two-rainbow domination on cycles

## Correctness — PASS

A zero-labeled vertex forces an ordered pair of unlike colors on its neighbors. Hence zeros form an independent set. For every nonalternating independent zero set of cardinality \(k\), the induced color-difference constraints form a forest on \(n-k\) colored positions with exactly \(k\) edges, giving exactly \(2^{n-2k}\) colorings. The standard cyclic-gap count is \(\tfrac n{n-k}\binom{n-k}k\). The alternating case is dealt with separately and is consistent exactly when four divides \(n\), with four colorings. A cycle-matching trace identity proves the algebraic form, including its sign. In particular, the endpoint correction cannot be dropped. The independent finite checks cover direct labeled functions through \(n=12\), a separately constructed nine-state transfer recurrence through \(n=60\), and a monomer-dimer recurrence through \(n=150\); finite calculations are corroboration, not the unrestricted proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)

In arXiv:2610.06906v1, Lange's Proposition 12 and Section 5.1 give minimum *weights* for cycles, not the number of minimum functions or the complete weight polynomial. The accessible arXiv:2608.14690v1 addresses dual-server domination on splitting graphs and other graph operations, not these labeled-cycle enumerators. The 2018 parameter-comparison literature concerns definitions, bounds and reductions. The closest indexed comparable record on complete multipartite rainbow domination concerns a different graph class and is not a stronger covering theorem. Searches using singleton two-rainbow, dual-server, matching-polynomial and exact mod-four count descriptions found no direct covering statement. A full-text comparison with Chellali–Haynes–Hedetniemi (2026) was not possible; that is an explicit residual priority risk, not evidence of absence. Nor does a failed search prove absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2026 cycle minimum-weight result leaves a natural counting refinement: how many distinct placements achieve the known bound, and what is the entire size distribution? The theorem resolves both, connects the entire enumerator to cyclic monomer-dimer matchings, and isolates the topological parity obstruction to a closing alternating color constraint. Its minimum layer has a sharp constant/linear/quadratic residue-class transition. This is a self-contained family-wide classification, not an arbitrary one-off parameter evaluation. No general theorem for other graph families is asserted.

## Remaining limitations

Ordinary simple labelled cycles and singleton two-color assignments only; colors are not identified under interchange. The complete 2026 foundational dual-server source was not read, and other unindexed enumerations may exist. Independent mathematical validation is outstanding.
