# Scientific review — single-resonant-cycle chip-firing census

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses the connected graph Laplacian to enforce a uniform three-step firing count, derives the exact local admissible interval of length equal to the number of preceding-phase neighbors, applies the bounded complement involution to exchange the two nonfixed firing densities, and counts the resulting selected-neighbor maps as directed cycles plus rooted forests. Uniqueness of the only length-divisible-by-three simple cycle forces a single directed feedback cycle; the remaining choices are exactly spanning trees after contraction. This chain is logically infinite and independent of numerical interpolation. Direct dynamics and independent graph-wide enumeration support the precise normalization (factor twelve), including non-cactus examples. Risks: none identified in the proof after checking the threshold inequalities, the graph contraction with parallel edges, and the distinction between simple undirected cycles and spurious directed two-cycles.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest primary text is Jiang–Scully–Zhang (arXiv:1211.6786), sections 3–4 and 6–7: it proves general firing-pattern restrictions and propagation on tree-like attachments, and explicitly identifies graphs with at most one cycle per vertex as a direction for further work; it does not count the present immediate three-cycles on single-resonance graphs. Dall'Asta (arXiv:cond-mat/0601267), pages 1–4, solves the one-dimensional ring model rather than arbitrary graphs with nonresonant extra cycles and contraction multiplicities. Backman's spanning-tree correspondence (arXiv:1207.6175) is for a sink-based, hereditary model and not this fixed-energy synchronous map. Jiang (arXiv:1003.0943) considers different graph families. Searches for periodic states, spanning-tree contractions, functional digraphs and unicyclic/chip-firing phase classifications produced no dominating theorem. A differently phrased literature result may still exist; search failure alone is not proof of novelty. A previously known excitation-coloring census counts a different update rule and does not entail the chip-firing phase weights; on triangle-plus-square the two censuses are 18 and 48 respectively.

## Value — PASS
This is an exact recurrent-state invariant on an infinite and structurally motivated family: networks with one viable three-phase feedback cycle. It identifies the precise influence of *arbitrary nonresonant detours* through a standard graph invariant, not just the existence of a wave. The matrix-tree formula handles non-cactus theta graphs, and the cactus product makes the effect of each extra cycle transparent. The result directly connects temporal dynamics with rooted spanning forests and provides a tractable classification beyond the previously solved ring case.

## Boundaries and reproducibility
The hypothesis cannot be weakened to several resonant cycles without additional terms; the result does not address basins, arbitrary primitive periods or priority beyond inspected literature. Two included Python checkers and captured outputs reproduce the finite tests; they require Python 3, networkx 3.6.1 and sympy 1.14.0. No external expert audit has occurred.
