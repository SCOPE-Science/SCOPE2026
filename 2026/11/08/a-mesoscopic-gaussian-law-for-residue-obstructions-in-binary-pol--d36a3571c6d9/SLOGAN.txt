For binary polynomials of degree \(d\), the differential-residue obstructions at every irreducible of degree up to \(\log_2d-O(\log\log d)\) jointly approximate independent Bernoulli bits with total-variation error at most \(7d^{-2}\). Their number obeys a Gaussian law, and the probability of no such obstructions is asymptotic to \(1.4303595418685\ldots/\sqrt{\log_2d}\); this makes no claim about positive orthogonal multiplicity.
