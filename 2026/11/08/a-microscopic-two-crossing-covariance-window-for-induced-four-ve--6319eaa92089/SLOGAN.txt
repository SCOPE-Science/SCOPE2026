At edge density \(p=1/2\), induced four-vertex claws and paths have strictly negative covariance even though their dominant three-vertex fluctuations are orthogonal. In \(G(n,1/2+\lambda/n)\), their covariance divided by \(n^4\) converges to \((12\lambda^2-8\lambda-1)/2048\), revealing two asymmetric sign crossings inside a width-\(n^{-1}\) window.
