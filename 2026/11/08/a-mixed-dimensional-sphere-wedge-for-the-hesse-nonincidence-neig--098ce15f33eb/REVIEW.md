# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The nine-point affine plane and its twelve lines are built from explicit field coordinates. Two independently expressed membership tests agree on every point and line subset. A complete staged discrete-Morse matching on each component has respectively 163 and 766 pairs; both leave one zero-cell, eleven three-cells and one four-cell. Every one of 1260 or 7008 nonempty-face incidences is included in a directed-acyclicity check, and both reverse-pivot matching checks agree on the critical dimensions. Exact integer signed boundary reduction yields the zero attaching-degree vector for the four-cell on each component. Since \(\pi_3(\bigvee^{11}S^3)\cong H_3(\bigvee^{11}S^3;\mathbb Z)\), this establishes the claimed integral homotopy wedges. Independently generated mod-two chain ranks corroborate the Betti numbers but are not used to assert the integral attaching map.

## Originality — PASS on the homotopy claim, against inspected evidence
Lew's 2021 thesis calculates exactly the integral homology ranks of the nine-point affine-plane **line-free** complex, and by combinatorial Alexander duality these are the same already-known integral Betti numbers for the present point-side noncover complex. The equal homotopy types of its point and line Dowker partners are also a standard general fact. Those facts are **credited**, not represented as original. The new statement is a concrete integral **homotopy** classification with an explicit full acyclic Morse matching and zero integral four-cell attachment. The thesis' Chapter 7 and introductory statement treat homology, not this homotopy decomposition. Kim and Kim's noncover theory, the thirteen-point projective nonincidence calculation, and the affine incidence-graph Rips and Steiner-incidence neighborhood results do not supply that attaching-map conclusion. Residual risk remains of an unindexed or differently named homotopy calculation.

## Value — PASS
The Hesse configuration is a canonical symmetric nine-point affine plane. Its nonincidence graph is a natural object whose two connected neighborhood-complex components have different facet dimensions (five versus seven), yet identical mixed-dimensional integral sphere-wedge homotopy types. Both are non-shellable, while the corresponding incidence-graph topology is very different. The Betti numbers were known, so the value here is strictly the full explicit integral homotopy enhancement and its reproducible complete gradient certificate. The first nontrivial mixed-dimensional small affine case provides a useful exact comparison and reproducible test for noncover and Dowker-complex constructions; no general-order classification is asserted.

## Limits
Only the finite order-three graph is classified. No equivariant splitting, higher-order plane theorem, or independent external audit is supplied. Unindexed equivalent prior work remains possible.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
