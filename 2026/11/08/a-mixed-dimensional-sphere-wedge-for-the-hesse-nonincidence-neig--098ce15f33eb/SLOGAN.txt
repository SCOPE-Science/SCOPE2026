The nine-point affine plane has a nonincidence Lovász neighborhood complex with two components of different simplicial dimensions but identical integral homotopy type, \(\bigvee^{11}S^3\vee S^4\). Explicit acyclic discrete-Morse matchings and zero integral attaching degrees establish both sphere wedges.
 The integral Betti numbers are known from line-free-complex duality; the new result is the explicit full homotopy splitting.
