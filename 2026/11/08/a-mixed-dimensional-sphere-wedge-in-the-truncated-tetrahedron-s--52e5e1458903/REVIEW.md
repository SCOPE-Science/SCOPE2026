# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The graph is given by an unambiguous twelve-vertex ordered-pair model. All nonempty masks are classified, giving 863 faces. The explicitly prescribed 428-pair matching is checked for cycles on every one of the 3432 face-poset covers. Its seven critical cells have dimensions \(0,3,3,3,4,4,4\). Exact signed integer gradient-path reduction produces a zero three-by-three boundary from dimension four to dimension three. Since the three-skeleton is a wedge of three three-spheres and its third homotopy group is \(\mathbb Z^3\), the vanishing boundary entries make each four-cell attachment nullhomotopic. An independently constructed mod-two boundary calculation reproduces Betti ranks, but the integral Morse boundary is the essential homotopy evidence.

## Originality
**PASS relative to inspected evidence.** Taylan (2015) defines the relevant three-vertex-path-devoid complexes and proves classifications for paths, cycles, and forests. Deshpande–Singh (2020) covers complete multipartite, fully whiskered, cycle, and perfect rooted-tree families, not this graph. Abdelmalek et al. (2021) treat chordal-graph-related families, whereas the truncated tetrahedron has induced six-cycles. Indexed results on the same graph concern ordinary independence, edge matching, cut, or metric nerve complexes, whose claims do not determine the second vertex-independence complex. An earlier ordinary-independence computation uses the same ordered-pair graph but a different forbidden-subgraph condition. Residual risk: the equivalent \(P_3\)-devoid or dissociation terminology could occur in unindexed work, and one related public record for the graph's edge matching complex was available only as a summary.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The truncated tetrahedron is a canonical Archimedean polyhedral graph. This exact nonflag simplicial invariant is a motivated test case beyond cycles, trees, and multipartite graphs. The result displays homology simultaneously in consecutive dimensions three and four, with a genuine attaching-map issue that a Betti-number table does not settle. Its complete finite homotopy calculation can serve as a benchmark for conjectures about higher-independence complexes under graph truncation; no unproved generalization is asserted.
