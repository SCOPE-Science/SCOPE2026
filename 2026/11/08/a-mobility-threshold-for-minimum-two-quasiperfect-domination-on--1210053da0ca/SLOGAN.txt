For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(3\le m<n\), the minimum two-quasiperfect dominating sets consist of \(n\) immobile full columns and a mobile arrangement component of \((n)_m\) nonattacking rook placements, whose exact diameter is \(m+\lfloor m/2\rfloor\). On square boards \(K_n\square K_n\) with \(n\ge3\), all \(n!+2n\) minimum configurations are immobile under a single replacement.
