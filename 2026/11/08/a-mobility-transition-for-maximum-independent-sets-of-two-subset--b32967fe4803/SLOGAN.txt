The maximum-independent-set reconfiguration graph of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\square K_2\) is a cube at order three, eight isolated vertices at order four, a connected eighty-vertex graph at order five, and exactly \(n(n-1)\) isolated edges at every order \(n\ge6\). The exceptional five-label triangle families unlock mobility that disappears permanently from the sixth label onward; token sliding and token jumping coincide.
