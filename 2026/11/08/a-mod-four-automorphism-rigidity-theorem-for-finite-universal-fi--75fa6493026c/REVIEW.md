# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The pair-successor action is one cycle of length \(n^2\); its cyclic centralizer bounds algebra automorphisms. A fixed symbol would fix the pair \((a,a)\), so nonidentity automorphisms act semiregularly. Writing their quotient directed arcs as \((A,B,h)\) and counting phase increments yields the congruence \(1\equiv m^2d(d-1)/2\pmod d\), whose only nontrivial solution is \(d=2\) with odd \(m\). The inverse Euler-tour lift at these cardinalities gives a full de Bruijn cycle. The nonperiodic case uses Klimov's uniqueness theorem, not an unproved assumption. ## Originality — PASS, qualified

The foundational classification in Klimov, arXiv:2604.05754v2, is not claimed as new, and its strict-preperiodicity uniqueness already implies the rigid corollary. The new claim is the sharp group-of-order-at-most-two bound for periodic universal Fibonacci groupoids, with the exact congruence for realizability. An ambient de Bruijn graph automorphism theorem does not classify stabilizers of individual Euler cycles.
