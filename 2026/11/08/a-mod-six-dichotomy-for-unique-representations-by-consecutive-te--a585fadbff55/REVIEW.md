# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The classification reduces to a general, proved equality between the multiple-representation ideal and the union of published Betti translates. Polynomial difference identities exhibit containment in the three positive residues. For the three negative residues, exact Frobenius-complement certificates prove a Betti difference is a gap; the initial cases are checked separately. The symmetry, Apéry and genus consequences follow without extrapolation. Independent finite denumerant and reachability arrays check all integers through the natural cutoff in 27 examples. The strongest scientific dependency is the accurately cited published freeness/Betti/Frobenius table; it has been inspected in full context.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** The motivating 2017 paper calculates the classical Frobenius number, free structure and Betti table, but does not state or analyze the collapse of the nonunique-representation ideal, the all-uniquely-representable set, or the associated first generalized invariants. The 2022 generalized Frobenius study is restricted to triangular triples; the 2023 arithmetic-progression study has different generators. Targeted exact-expression, representation-count, structural and research-index comparisons revealed no matching statement. The result is a mathematically derived consequence of the published Betti data plus arithmetic identities, so an unindexed equivalent deduction or a later table remains a residual originality risk. An absence of search hits has not been treated as a proof of global novelty.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The problem identifies precisely when *all* four-generator tetrahedral representations are already nonunique on a single translated ideal. The sharp six-class boundary yields a complete finite Apéry-set characterization of unique representations and simultaneous exact first generalized Frobenius/genus formulas in half the congruence classes. This addresses the first representation-sensitive step beyond the classical Frobenius problem, which is explicitly central in the cited literature. It is a natural structural classification, not a new notation for the original Betti or Frobenius formulas. The complementary three generalized-invariant formulas remain open in this result.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Robles-Pérez–Rosales, arXiv:1706.04378, Proposition 12, Proposition 32 and Corollary 34, give the original arithmetic inputs. Komatsu, arXiv:2206.13054, concerns triangular triples. Komatsu–Ying, Symmetry 15 (2023), 1328, concerns arithmetic-progression triples. A possible equivalent deduction from the old explicit Betti table is the most serious unresolved risk. No independent audit has been performed; the included verification is finite evidence supporting, not replacing, the infinite proof.
