# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
The proof treats the exact Gallo--Videla brackets, separates the triangular \(C_3\) case, and derives the mixed Kirillov block and its support-cycle determinant. Invertibility of this block is necessary and sufficient for the Kirillov radical to be precisely the center. When invertibility is impossible, one-dimensional mixed corank witnesses and the even-rank property of alternating forms prove the exact index, with no assumption on characteristic. The finite-field functional count follows by counting zero and nonzero products in three disjoint blocks. Exact finite-field enumeration and independent rank computations corroborate the result but do not substitute for the determinant proof.

## Originality: PASS, qualified
The closest primary preprint, Gallo--Videla (arXiv:2610.33963v1), introduces the class-three cycle Lie algebra and derives its graded dimensions and center but not the Kirillov index or count. Burde--Dekimpe (arXiv:2505.08331, Section 4) applies a graph matching number to a distinct two-step Lie algebra. The existing complete-bipartite index computation covers the special case \(C_4=K_{2,2}\) but not the remaining infinite cycle family or the complete finite-field enumerator. Searches for the alternative language of coadjoint rank, matching, and cyclic graph Lie algebras yielded no dominating statement. An unindexed or independently discovered result remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The Kirillov index measures minimal coadjoint stabilizer dimension and is a standard invariant of nilpotent Lie algebras. The theorem completely identifies the center-attainment boundary for a canonical infinite graph family from a recent construction, including a nontrivial mod-three obstruction and an exceptional smallest-field effect even within characteristic two. The count refines this qualitative index into the exact abundance of center-minimal functionals over finite fields.

## Scope and limitations
This concerns the Gallo--Videla class-three construction rather than all graph Lie algebras. Finite experiments are illustrative only. The count is for functionals, not orbit classes; the previously known four-cycle index is not reclaimed as new. Historical priority remains subject to discovery of unindexed equivalent work.
