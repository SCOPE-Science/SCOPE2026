# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
The class-three cycle graph bracket relations imply a vertex-edge mixed Kirillov matrix with a support graph splitting into gcd(n,3) bipartite cycles. The determinant vanishes for 3∤n and factors as the three residue-product differences when 3|n. The triangle is separately treated using its original three path-bracket differences. The rank of the full alternating block is 2n if the mixed block is invertible; otherwise alternation bounds it by 2n−2, and explicit corank-one witnesses attain that bound over every field, including characteristic two. The counting formula weights zero and nonzero products in three disjoint parameter blocks and multiplies by the unrestricted 2n noncentral coordinates.

An independent symbolic reconstruction verified these determinant identities at n=3–9. Direct F4 mixed-rank enumeration confirmed 24 and 1,296 full-rank central assignments at n=3 and 6, respectively; finite counts over other prime fields also agreed. The written proof, not these finite experiments, supports the unrestricted theorem.

## Originality: PASS, qualified
Gallo and Videla (arXiv:2610.33963v1) supply the source brackets, dimensions and centers; their construction does not determine the Kirillov index or full-rank-functional count. Burde and Dekimpe (arXiv:2505.08331v1, Section 4) give a matching-number formula for the different two-step graphical Lie algebra. A separate three-step complete-bipartite index theorem already covers the C4=K2,2 index (six), and receives full credit for that specialization. Results on three-step paths and subdivided stars involve different graphs and do not imply the cycle closure determinant or finite-field count. Available record and primary-literature searches found no stronger covering theorem. An unindexed equivalent calculation remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The index is a standard coadjoint invariant of a natural infinite family. The mod-three threshold and the distinction between F2 and F4 reveal a genuine field-cardinality phenomenon even at fixed characteristic, while the exact number of functionals with center-only radical refines the mere generic index. The theorem is not a relabeling of a known two-step formula, a single-instance table entry, or a promise about full orbit distributions.

## Scope and limitations
The statement concerns only the Gallo–Videla three-step construction, with the triangle handled separately. The finite-field count counts individual dual functionals, not coadjoint orbits. Finite checks cannot prove the arbitrary-field result. The independent mathematical assessment is best-of-knowledge rather than a claim of formal verification or exhaustive historical priority.
