For the three-step Lie algebra of a cycle of length \(n\ge3\), the Kirillov index is \(n\) exactly when \(3\mid n\) and the field has more than two elements; otherwise it is \(n+2\). An explicit support-cycle determinant also gives the exact number of minimum-stabilizer linear functionals over every finite field, exhibiting a genuine difference between \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\).
