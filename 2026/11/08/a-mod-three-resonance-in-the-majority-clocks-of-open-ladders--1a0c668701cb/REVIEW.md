# Scientific review: a mod-three resonance in the majority clocks of open ladders

## Correctness — PASS
For every integer \(n\ge2\), the graph is the open-ended \(P_n\square K_2\), with binary synchronous strict **open-neighbor** majority and retention of the old bit on ties. The proof of eventual period at most two uses a symmetric adjacency-plus-small-diagonal matrix and a strictly decreasing two-time energy whenever states two steps apart differ. Fixed configurations reduce to two persistent mixed-rung words and \(2F_{n+1}\) homogeneous-rung words with no consecutive changes. Full flipping forces every rung to be mixed, yielding \(2F_{n-1}\) configurations. The critical two-periodic count follows from an explicit \(256\)-state transfer and the exact coordinate identity \((T^5-3T^4+T^3-T^2+3T-I)r=0\), whose eight row patterns and multiplicities are documented. Five exact initial values determine the Lucas-plus-period-three formula for all \(n\ge2\). Independent direct state simulation and transfer counts agree in all tested sizes. The certificate proves an infinite recurrence; the finite checks alone would not.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The claim is not that symmetric threshold dynamics has eventual period at most two; this is classical Goles–Olivos. Kaaser, Mallmann-Trenn and Natale treat convergence rates of this update rule rather than exact open-ladder periodic counts. Agur–Fraenkel–Klein treat self-inclusive one-dimensional cyclic majority, not the present ladder's open-neighborhood rule. Turau's characterizations are for trees. A related exact census on the same open ladder under *additive characteristic-two neighbor summation* concerns a linear map, not nonlinear majority and does not imply its periodic populations. OEIS A395029 considers a closed-neighborhood majority on the same ladder and does not imply these counts. Searches for equivalent formulas, reduction to stronger theorems and exact tabulations found no known implication, including in accessible mathematical-result records. This is bounded evidence of noncoverage, not proof of universal historical uniqueness. Original full text for Goles–Olivos was unavailable and any unindexed earlier equivalent remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The ladder is the canonical finite-width two-dimensional graph, with endpoints of different degrees from its interior. A complete, all-length closed census distinguishes fixed points, all two-periodic configurations and fully flipping orbits, uncovering a \(3\)-divisibility correction to a Lucas-number leading term. The result also quantifies the exponentially vanishing relative size of fully synchronous flips. It addresses the structure of recurrence on the entire natural family, rather than one isolated size or a renaming of an existing one-dimensional count.

## Limitations and related results
The formulas are for initially periodic populations; they do not classify basins or transient times. The published general period bound and one-dimensional results are separately identified.
