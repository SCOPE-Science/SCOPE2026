Synchronous open-neighbor majority on the \(n\)-rung ladder has exactly \(L_{2n}+1+3\mathbf1_{3\mid n}\) two-step periodic states but only \(2(F_{n+1}+1)\) fixed states, so its primitive two-cycle count has a sharp three-divisibility resonance. Only \(2F_{n-1}\) states flip at every vertex, an exponentially vanishing proportion of its period-two population.
