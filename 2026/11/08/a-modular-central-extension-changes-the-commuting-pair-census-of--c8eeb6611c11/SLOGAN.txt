For odd prime \(p\), the derivation Lie algebra of \(\mathbb F_q[x]/(x^{p+2})\) has a complete order-stratified centralizer census and exactly \(q^{p+3}+\frac{p-1}{2}(q^2-1)q^p\) ordered commuting pairs. Its central one-dimensional quotient extension is nonsplit, and its commuting-pair count differs sharply from that of a split central stabilization.
