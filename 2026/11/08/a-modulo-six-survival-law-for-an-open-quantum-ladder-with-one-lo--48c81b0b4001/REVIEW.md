# Scientific review: modulo-six survival on an open quantum ladder

## Correctness
PASS. The stated dynamical model and normalization are explicit, the two-band spectrum is exact, the rational-angle lemma is proved via cyclotomic Galois conjugates and trace identities, and the source-to-dark-mode projection accounts for parity. Dissipative spectral separation establishes the full-time limit for every positive loss strength. The independent executable checker passes 93 exact-polynomial, matrix-projector and evolution assertions. Finite numerical checks are not substituted for the all-size proof.

## Originality
PASS on the inspected evidence, not an independent priority determination. Full accessible texts of arXiv:1008.0085, arXiv:1102.3994, arXiv:1908.10173 and arXiv:2205.13188 were compared for graph, Hamiltonian, sink, source and claimed limit. The closest ladder studies use discrete-time coined walks with directed-edge Hilbert spaces; they do not supply the continuous-time site-adjacency modulo-six law. An earlier graph-family study of periodic circular quantum ladders and absorbing-site cardinalities concerns a different graph and different endpoint question. Broad, unpublished or unindexed coverage remains possible.

## Value
PASS. The exact arithmetic switch between complete eventual absorption and a protected survival probability is a physical transport-efficiency observable across an infinite natural family of open networks. It distinguishes existence of a dark state from accessibility by the opposite-corner initial state, a nontrivial distinction for quantum transport design. The result is not a bulk phase transition or a noise-robust statement.

## Closest limitations
The result requires uniform hopping, a single positive corner absorber, and a fixed localized diagonally opposite initial excitation. Published work on Grover ladders is not directly comparable at the level of evolution operators. Historical priority and physical robustness have not been independently assessed.
