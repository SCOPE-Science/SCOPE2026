On a finite open two-leg quantum ladder with one absorbing corner, a particle launched at the opposite corner survives indefinitely with probability exactly \(3/[2(n+1)]\) when the number of rungs is \(n\equiv5\pmod6\), and with probability zero otherwise, regardless of the positive absorption rate. The arithmetic exception comes from a unique zero-energy degeneracy whose dark eigenmode is invisible to the source for the other \(n\equiv2\pmod3\) rungs.
