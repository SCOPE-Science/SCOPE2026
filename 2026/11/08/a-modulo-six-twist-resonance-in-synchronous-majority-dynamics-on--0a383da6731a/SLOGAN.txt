For synchronous three-neighbor majority on prisms and Möbius ladders, every primitive orbit has length at most two and all fixed, periodic, and fully flipping state counts have explicit Lucas-number formulas. Twisting the closing rung increases the number of immediately periodic states exactly for circumferences coprime to six, and decreases it otherwise; this arithmetic sign reversal has size on the order of \(\varphi^n\) even though both families have the same leading periodic population \(\varphi^{2n}\).
