The sharp affine least-squares projector on the continuous perimeter of every regular \(n\)-gon has its norm exactly at the vertices and admits a closed sine-quotient formula. Its convergence to the circle constant \(\frac13+\frac{2\sqrt3}{\pi}\) has a positive quadratic correction and a signed cubic correction depending on \(n\bmod3\), arising from two kernel zero-crossing edges.
