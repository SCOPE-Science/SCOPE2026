For every odd-characteristic Fermat surface of degree \(d\ge3\) with characteristic prime to \(d\), the standard \(3d^2\)-line divisor has a uniform motivic class \([B_d]=3\mathbb L[E^2]-3[E^2]+6[E]-3[E^3]\), where \(E\) is the finite étale scheme of roots of \(t^d=-1\). This identifies every reduced multiple-point stratum and gives the rational-point incidence spectrum and Hasse–Weil zeta function over all finite extensions.
