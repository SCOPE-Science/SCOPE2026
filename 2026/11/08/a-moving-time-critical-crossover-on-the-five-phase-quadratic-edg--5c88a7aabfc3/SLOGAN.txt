On an invariant edge of the five-phase permuted-Volterra model, the five-step return collapses to five iterates of a single quadratic map, and the singular endpoint has double-exponential \(32^n\) attraction. When the parameter gap is tuned to \(1-p_n=(1-s)^{32^n}/c\), the deficit after any further fixed number \(\ell\ge1\) of five-step returns has the sharp law \((1-S_{5(n+\ell)}^{(n)})/(1-p_n)^{5\ell+1}\to c+c^2\), explicitly resolving the moving-time crossover to geometric relaxation.
