# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is a rational \(S_n\)-module isomorphism for the exact range \(n\ge6\), \(3\le k\le n-3\). The proof determines the maximal star/top cliques, supplies an explicit free-face collapse to a two-dimensional complex, and establishes simple connectivity via the hypersimplex two-skeleton. It then obtains an exact equality in the rational representation ring by equivariant Euler inclusion–exclusion. Stabilizers of signed oriented Johnson edges yield \(h_{k-1}h_{n-k-1}e_2\); this sign is essential. Finally, a four-row Jacobi–Trudi determinant telescopes to the complete character and proves the multiplicity-one Specht decomposition. These steps prove unbounded parameters independently of the finite computation. The standalone checker agrees on 130 conjugacy-class traces in six explicit complexes and on full mod-two homology for four cases. There is no claim of an integral equivariant splitting.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

The closest published result, *Sphere-wedge collapse of scale-one Johnson-graph Rips complexes* (2026), proves the nonequivariant integral homotopy type for exactly the same central Johnson graph parameters, but its full result and proof do not give any \(S_n\)-character or representation decomposition. Shuldiner–Oldford (2022, arXiv:2208.12710) classify the cliques, not the homology. Iglesias–Natale (2019, arXiv:1912.09243) decompose the vertex-function representation into two-row Specht modules, not the four-row modules found in homology. The line-graph topology theorem of Goyal–Shukla–Singh (2020) cannot cover this central range because these Johnson graphs have induced claws. Targeted searches on Johnson clique homology, hypersimplex flag complexes, cycle-type formulae and Specht modules did not locate an equivalent or stronger prior conclusion. The exact known nonequivariant theorem is expressly treated as prior; no originality claim is made for it. Unindexed or inaccessible equivalent literature is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The group \(S_n\) is the defining natural symmetry of the entire Johnson scheme. The theorem gives, for all central ranks simultaneously, a sharp and uniform irreducible characterization of the actual second homology—not just its rank—showing an unexpected multiplicity-free decomposition into precisely specified four-row Specht modules. It refines the earlier sphere count, distinguishes this topological module from the classical two-row vertex-function module, and immediately determines all symmetry traces and the vanishing of global invariants. The exact representation can support symmetry-sensitive topology or homology calculations, while no speculative downstream theorem is assumed. It is a structural family-wide theorem, not a small-case census or trivial substitution.

## Closest literature and limits

Primary literature inspected: Shuldiner–Oldford, *The Clique Structure of Johnson Graphs* (arXiv:2208.12710); Goyal–Shukla–Singh, *Topology of Clique Complexes of Line Graphs* (arXiv:2009.12130); Iglesias–Natale, *A Fast Fourier Transform for the Johnson graph* (arXiv:1912.09243); and the complete published 2026 Johnson sphere-wedge result. The finding is rational equivariant homology at scale one. It does not imply integral module splitting or an equivariant sphere-wedge equivalence. Search incompleteness and possible uncatalogued equivalent work remain limits.
