For all \(n\ge6\) and \(3\le k\le n-3\), the rational second homology of the scale-one Johnson-graph clique complex is the multiplicity-free \(S_n\)-module \(\bigoplus_{j=1}^{\min(k,n-k)-1}S^{(n-j-2,j,1,1)}\). This determines its full symmetry character and shows that it contains no nonzero class invariant under every ground-set permutation; the previously known sphere count alone does not determine this representation.
