In a fixed-edge uniformly random bipartite graph on two equal \(n\)-vertex parts, the covariance between the numbers of degree-one vertices on opposite sides is asymptotically negative at every fixed mean degree except two. Near two edges per vertex, a narrow \(\sqrt n\)-scale window reverses the sign: if \((k-2n)/\sqrt n\to x\), then \(n\operatorname{Cov}(L,R)\to e^{-4}(8/3-2x^2)\), so positive covariance occurs for \(|x|<2/\sqrt3\).
