For a two-atom Sinkhorn problem whose off-diagonal kernel weight is \(\delta\) and whose two marginals differ by \(\kappa\delta\), the entire projective iteration and the small cross-fluxes converge on the \(n\delta\) timescale to an explicit Riccati trajectory. An exact Möbius conjugacy yields the sharp contraction constant and a joint crossover from marginal-mismatch domination to singular-kernel slowdown.
