The Grundy total domination number of a rectangular rook graph has a sharp adjacent-width exception: for \(2\le m\le n\), it equals \(2m-2\) if \(n=m\), \(2m\) if \(n=m+1\), and \(m+n-2\) if \(n\ge m+2\). A selected-edge forest argument proves the formula for every board size and, by standard duality, gives the exact skew zero-forcing number.
