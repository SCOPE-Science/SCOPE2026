For every finite graph \(H\), the complete weight polynomial of pointwise-minimal Roman dominating functions on its one-leaf corona is \(\sum_{X\subseteq V(H)}2^{|V(H)\setminus N_H[X]|}z^{|V(H)|+|X|+|V(H)\setminus N_H[X]|}\). Its total count equals the independent-set count of an associated bipartite neighborhood double, while its smallest and largest weights recover \(|V(H)|+\gamma(H)\) and a product over component orders.
