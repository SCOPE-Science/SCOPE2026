For all interleavings of two internally ordered four-element chains, binary cross-comparison queries have Littlestone dimension exactly \(5\), one below the cardinality ceiling \(6\). This is the first balanced-square failure of the information ceiling, and it occurs despite four perfectly balanced first-query splits: a nested \(35\)–\(17+18\)–\(9+9\) obstruction rules out depth six.
