For a symmetric metric double star with \(d\) unit pendant edges at each hub and a variable connecting edge, the sharp real \(L^\infty\) norm of the continuous affine \(L^2\) projector has a unique finite bridge-length maximum **exactly when \(d\ge9\)**; below that threshold the norm increases monotonically. This first two-hub family passes above the sharp \(7/3\) envelope for metric stars, and every worst-case evaluation occurs at an outer leaf.
