# Mathematical review: harmonic-polytope vertex covariance

## Correctness — PASS
The polytope's exact distinct-vertex parametrization and its dimension are supplied by Ardila--Escobar (2020), Propositions 3.4--3.6. The new calculation starts from that source and derives the marked-record probability \(1/t\), the full size-biased normalization \(h_n\), two rank--mark moments, and one conditional product-rank moment. All covariance blocks follow from these moments and the exact coordinate-sum constraints. Every nonzero covariance eigenspace and its multiplicity follow by simultaneous-label symmetry. The inequalities for the exact phase transition use rational checks at \(2\le n\le9\) and a separate monotonicity argument for **all** \(n\ge6\); the asymptotic statement follows from the harmonic-number expansion. The standard-library verifier independently enumerates \(34152\) distinct vertices in dimensions \(n=2,3,4,5\) and checks every full covariance entry exactly, plus \(299\) exact parameter profiles. These experiments corroborate but are not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (subject to historical-priority limits)
The closest source on the identical family, Ardila--Escobar's *The harmonic polytope*, provides vertex and face descriptions and a volume formula, but not the covariance tensor or its symmetric--antisymmetric eigenspace ordering. The related bipermutahedron has different vertices and does not imply the harmonic-polytope vertex moments. Closest published mathematical findings on the same family compute radial vertex shells, inballs and vertex degrees; none gives the two cross-block second-moment parameters. Shell radii constrain the trace only and do not determine the cross-block term \(c_n\) or its unique sign reversal. Direct comparisons were made against accessible complete RESULT texts as well as topical, alias and implication searches. An earlier equivalent covariance formula under records, Pareto maxima or size-biased permutation-pair terminology could still exist; searched literature does not prove absolute priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete exact tensor/eigenspace classification for uniformly sampled vertices of a natural high-dimensional polytope used in matroidal geometry, not a single-coordinate moment. The unexpected strict change of dominant geometric direction at exactly nine labels is a structural phenomenon not obtained from the existing radial or facial classifications. The theorem also identifies the sharp rate \(1+6/(n\log n)+o(1/(n\log n))\) of asymptotic relative isotropy, while the absolute eigenvalue gap diverges. These properties explain how the two naturally coupled permutohedral blocks exchange dominance under discrete vertex sampling.

## Limitations
The result depends on the standard Euclidean realization and equal weights on *distinct vertices*. It assumes the known vertex description and makes no statement for uniform volume sampling. The possible equivalent permutation-record formulation is the outstanding historical-priority risk. Numerical checking is finite; the general statement is supported by the symbolic proof.
