Uniform vertices of the Ardila–Escobar harmonic polytope have exactly two nonzero covariance eigenvalues, each repeated \(n-1\) times. The principal fluctuation mode switches sharply from symmetric to antisymmetric block contrasts at \(n=9\), while the intrinsic covariance condition number approaches \(1\) as \(1+6/(n\log n)+o(1/(n\log n))\).
