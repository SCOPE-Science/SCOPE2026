A proper KC tree shift can **decrease** the expected size of a random-order greedy independent set: an explicit nine-vertex example has expectations \(73/15\) before and \(34/7\) after, a drop of \(1/105\). Exhaustive exact Prüfer-code verification shows that no smaller tree has such a reversal, refuting the proposed KC-monotonicity while preserving the known global path-minimum theorem.
