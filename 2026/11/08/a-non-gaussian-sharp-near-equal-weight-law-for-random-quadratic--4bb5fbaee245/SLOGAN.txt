For independent uniform random quadrature nodes, the smallest maximum relative correction to equal weights required for exact quadratic integration has an explicit non-Gaussian \(m^{-1/2}\)-scale limit: \(2(|G_2|+\sqrt{G_1^2+G_2^2})\) for two independent Gaussian moment fluctuations. The resulting one-dimensional integral gives asymptotically exact confidence levels for existence of strictly positive weights in a prescribed narrow band around \(1/m\).
