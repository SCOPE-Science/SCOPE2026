# A non-shellable Möbius-ladder dissociation complex with the homotopy type of a four-sphere

## Finding
Let \(M_{14}\) be the cubic Möbius-ladder graph with vertex set \(\mathbb Z/14\mathbb Z\), cycle edges \(\{i,i+1\}\), and antipodal edges \(\{i,i+7\}\), with subscripts modulo 14. Let \(K=\operatorname{Ind}_2(M_{14})\) be the simplicial complex of vertex sets inducing graphs with no connected component on more than two vertices. Then
\[
K\simeq S^4,\qquad
\widetilde H_j(K;\mathbb Z)=\begin{cases}\mathbb Z&j=4,\\0&j\ne4.\end{cases}
\]
However, \(K\) is **not shellable**, even under the Björner–Wachs nonpure definition: its 149 facets all have dimension five or six, while \(\widetilde H_4(K;\mathbb Z)\) is nonzero. The complex itself is six-dimensional; the conclusion is a homotopy equivalence, not a PL-sphere or homeomorphism assertion.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph is simple and undirected. Its edges are exactly the 14 cycle edges and seven antipodal chords. A selected subset is a face precisely when every selected vertex has at most one selected neighbor. Equivalently, the selected induced graph contains no (not necessarily induced) path with three vertices. Thus \(K\) is also the \(P_3\)-devoid or dissociation complex of this graph. All homology and homotopy claims concern the full finite complex on 14 vertices and integral coefficients.

A face is represented in the proof and verifier by the integer bit mask \(s=\sum_{i\in F}2^i\), with vertex labels \(0,\ldots,13\). The independent exhaustive enumeration yields the face vector, by dimension zero through six,
\[
(f_0,\ldots,f_6)=(14,91,322,644,658,287,30),
\]
with 2,046 nonempty faces. Of the 149 facets, 119 are five-dimensional and 30 are six-dimensional.

## Proof
**Construction of an acyclic matching.** Process vertices in the fixed order
\[
(7,9,0,1,5,12,3,11,2,13,8,4,10,6).
\]
At the step for vertex \(v\), inspect remaining nonempty faces in increasing bit-mask order. Whenever a remaining face \(F\) avoids \(v\) and \(F\cup\{v\}\) is also remaining, match these two faces and remove both permanently from subsequent steps. This deterministic procedure constructs exactly 1,021 pairs. The only four unmatched faces have masks \(128,3141,4373,9556\), corresponding respectively to
\[
\{7\},\quad\{0,2,6,10,11\},\quad\{0,2,4,8,12\},\quad\{2,4,6,8,10,13\}.
\]
They have dimensions zero, four, four and five.

To check acyclicity independently of any unproved matching-tree heuristic, orient every covering relation in the full Hasse diagram downward, except that each matched relation is oriented upward. There are exactly 8,946 covering relations. The standalone verifier constructs all these directed edges and performs exhaustive topological sorting; all 2,046 faces are removed from the zero-indegree queue. Therefore the matching is acyclic and discrete Morse theory gives a homotopy-equivalent CW complex with one zero-cell, two four-cells and one five-cell.

**Integral attaching degree.** Give each simplex its increasing-vertex orientation. For a face \(s\) matched upward with \(b\), the algebraic Morse reduction expresses \(s\) by the other codimension-one faces of \(b\), using the unit coefficient \([\partial b:s]=\pm1\). Inductively, the projection \(R(s)\) of an oriented four-face to critical four-cells is zero for a face matched downward, is its basis vector if critical, and in an upward pair is
\[
R(s)=-[\partial b:s]^{-1}\!\sum_{t\lessdot b,\ t\ne s}[\partial b:t]R(t).
\]
Acyclicity guarantees this recursion terminates. Applying it to the oriented boundary of the critical five-face (mask 9556), in the ordered critical four-face basis (masks 3141, 4373), gives the **exact integral** Morse column
\[
\partial^{\mathrm{Morse}}_5=\binom{-1}{-1}.
\]
This is a primitive nonzero attaching vector. The four-skeleton is \(S^4\vee S^4\), whose fourth homotopy group is \(\mathbb Z^2\) by the Hurewicz theorem. Hence the cellular boundary vector determines the attaching-map homotopy class. A unimodular change of the two four-cell generators carries \((-1,-1)\) to \((1,0)\); the five-cell cancels one four-cell, leaving precisely \(S^4\) up to homotopy. The calculation does not confuse a mod-two homology computation with an integral homotopy proof.

**Failure of nonpure shellability.** Theorem 4.1 of Björner–Wachs identifies the reduced homology of a shellable nonpure complex as generated by its homology facets, whose dimensions are the homological degrees. Since every facet of \(K\) has dimension five or six, a shellable \(K\) would have \(\widetilde H_4(K;\mathbb Z)=0\), contradicting the integral homotopy classification. Therefore \(K\) is not shellable.

## Verification
Run the complete standalone checker with `python3 verify.py` from the unpacked file directory. It uses only the Python standard library. It independently compares induced-degree and forbidden-triple face tests, enumerates all faces and facets, constructs and verifies the entire directed Hasse diagram, computes the signed Morse boundary, and separately calculates mod-two homology using the original simplicial boundary matrices. The respective mod-two boundary ranks in dimensions one through six are
\[
(13,78,244,400,257,30),
\]
which yield ordinary mod-two Betti numbers \((1,0,0,0,1,0,0)\) in degrees zero through six. These field checks corroborate, but do not replace, the integral Morse attaching calculation. The recorded output is supplied in `verification_output.txt`. The checker must finish with `ALL_CHECKS_PASS`.

## Relationship to prior work
Taylan's first publicly available 2015 work develops matching-tree and splitting methods for devoid complexes, giving exact results for paths, cycles and forests. The present object is the \(P_3\)-devoid complex of a *non-forest* Möbius ladder, outside those family classifications. Björner and Wachs establish the nonpure shellability obstruction used here, not the homotopy type of this graph complex. Agarwal and Basak's study of Möbius and circular ladders determines the **ordinary** independence and perfect-matching complexes; these have different faces from \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(M_{14})\) and do not determine the claimed attaching degree. Relevant named graph-complex research records were checked for the aliases Möbius ladder, circulant graph, dissociation complex, higher independence and \(P_3\)-devoid complex. No inspected source gave a theorem implying the asserted integral homotopy type for this particular complex.

## Limitations
This is a finite exact classification for the specified 14-vertex graph, not a recurrence or uniform theorem for all Möbius ladders. An equivalent calculation could exist outside indexed sources, so historical priority is not guaranteed. No equivariant decomposition, homeomorphism type, or classification of arbitrary higher-independence complexes is claimed. The finite checker proves the enumerated combinatorial and integer identities only together with the stated standard discrete-Morse and Hurewicz deductions.
