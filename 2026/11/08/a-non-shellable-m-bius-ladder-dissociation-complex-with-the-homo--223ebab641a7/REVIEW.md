# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The precise finite complex is defined by induced maximum degree at most one on the circulant graph with steps \(\pm1\) and 7 modulo 14. Two independent face tests give 2,046 nonempty faces. An explicit deterministic matching has 1,021 pairs, and full topological sorting of all 8,946 oriented Hasse incidences proves acyclicity. The four critical cells have dimensions \(0,4,4,5\); the signed Morse boundary column is \((-1,-1)\), primitive over \(\mathbb Z\). Since \(\pi_4(S^4\vee S^4)\cong\mathbb Z^2\), one four-cell and the five-cell cancel by a unimodular basis change. The resulting homotopy type is \(S^4\). A separate mod-two rank calculation corroborates the Betti numbers. Facet enumeration and Björner–Wachs Theorem 4.1 prove nonpure non-shellability. The checker and its actual output are embedded alongside the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The same-object aliases \(M_{14}\), the 7-Möbius ladder, and the circulant graph \(\mathrm{Ci}_{14}(1,7)\) were compared, as were the equivalent names two-independence complex, dissociation complex and \(P_3\)-devoid complex. Taylan's 2015 full text treats path/cycle/forest devoid complexes and provides general methods but no Möbius-ladder instance. Agarwal–Basak's 2026 full text classifies ordinary independence and perfect-matching complexes of Möbius ladders, not this larger first higher-independence complex; it contains no such 14-vertex conclusion. Published work on total three-cut complexes or matching complexes does not imply the target homotopy and non-shellability. Relevant mathematical-record searches and accessible earlier claims found no covering result. Publication priority is not independently established; unindexed calculations remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a natural canonical cubic graph, not an arbitrary induced subgraph or parameter slice. First higher independence is a recognized graph-topology construction. The 14-vertex example features a nontrivial primitive Morse attachment (two critical four-cells and a critical five-cell do **not** leave a two-dimension sphere wedge) and gives a concrete nonpure non-shellability obstruction despite sphere homotopy. It provides an exact reference case for extending existing ordinary-independence and devoid-complex classifications to cyclic graphs with antipodal chords. The claim is an exact finite theorem, not a claimed infinite-family law.
