Its three quadrics form a regular sequence because their Veronese support has codimension three. Three explicit affine chart rings show the only extra infinitesimal is one square-zero direction, and Gorenstein duality identifies its line bundle with the canonical line bundle. Its global obstruction, canonical ideal, and Hilbert polynomial distinguish it from merely reporting derivative vanishing or local dual numbers.
