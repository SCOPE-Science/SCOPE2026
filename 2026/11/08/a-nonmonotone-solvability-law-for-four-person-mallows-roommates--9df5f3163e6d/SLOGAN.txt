In a four-person stable-roommates market with independent full-order Mallows draws followed by self-deletion, the exact probability of having no stable matching is \(q^2(3q^8+q^7+3q^6-2q^5+6q^4-2q^3+3q^2+q+3)/[(1+q)^2(1+q^2)^2(1+q+q^2)^3]\). It has a unique maximum at \(q\approx0.379464\), where unsolvability is roughly \(1.688\) times as likely as under uniform preferences, and its low-noise onset is quadratic rather than linear.
