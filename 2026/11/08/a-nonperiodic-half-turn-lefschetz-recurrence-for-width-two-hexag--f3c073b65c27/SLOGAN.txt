For the width-two linear hexagonal strip, the geometric half-turn fixed-point independence complex has reduced Euler series \(\sum_{n\ge1}e_nz^n=z^2(z-1)/(1+z+z^4)\). This gives the exact nonperiodic recurrence \(e_n=-e_{n-1}-e_{n-4}\) and determines the half-turn's reduced rational homological Lefschetz number for every strip length.
