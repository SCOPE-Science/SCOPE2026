# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The diagonal involutions commute projectively but their matrix lifts anticommute. Their tensor-square lifts commute, giving an honest linearization of the bundle used to define the ruled threefold. The displayed three-parameter bidegree \(2,2\) equation is invariant. Its quadratic discriminant is a binary quartic with distinct zeros under the stated open conditions, and the coefficient triple has no common zero; this proves the center smooth of genus one. The published Mori–Mukai description identifies the blow-up as a smooth Fano threefold with the stated Picard basis. The exceptional divisor, tautological bundle and \(\mathcal O(1,1)\) are linearized. Restriction to the preserved disjoint section proves that \(\mathcal O(1,0)\) is not. Consequently the complete Amitsur obstruction group, not merely a lower bound, has order two. Exact polynomial and matrix checks replay successfully.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Sharma's Lemma 9.2 gives only upper bounds for family \(4.2\), and Remark 1.2 specifically leaves the order-four case unresolved. Its parent toric family \(3.31\) can have an order-two obstruction; neither that known fact nor equivariant blow-up invariance produces a smooth invariant elliptic center without the new construction. The geometric Mori–Mukai references describe the family but not this exact equivariant obstruction on a nonempty two-parameter locus. Searches for the equation, diagonal quaternion/Klein-four lifts, Amitsur group and family identifier found no equivalent statement. A more obscure explicit example might exist, so historical originality is conditional on the inspected accessible literature rather than exhaustive certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The principal cited paper determines a general upper bound of four but leaves sharpness open. Demonstrating an explicit nonzero value of two on a positive-dimensional family supplies a lower bound and identifies the exact group and its geometric obstruction. This is not merely a numerical recomputation: smooth equivariant elliptic blow-up centers must be constructed and checked, and the order-two class is shown to survive the blow-up. The result does not resolve attainability of the full bound.

## Closest literature and limitations

Sharma, *Amitsur groups of Imprimitive Fano Threefolds*, arXiv:2610.38963v1, especially §9.2, gives the geometry, Picard generators and upper bound; the stated example and exact subgroup are not supplied there. De Biase et al., *Fano 3-folds from homogeneous vector bundles over Grassmannians*, §4.50, records the Fano model. The toric parent obstruction and invariant degree-two line bundles are classical ingredients, not claimed as new. No assertion is made about full sharpness, quotient geometry, or distinctness of all family members. Independent verification has not been undertaken.
