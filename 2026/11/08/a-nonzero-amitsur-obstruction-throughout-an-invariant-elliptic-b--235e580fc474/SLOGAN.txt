A nonempty two-parameter family of smooth Mori–Mukai \(4.2\) Fano threefolds admits faithful Klein-four actions with exactly one nontrivial Amitsur obstruction: \(\operatorname{Am}(X,G)\cong\mathbb Z/2\). The construction realizes a nonzero case below the known order-four upper bound, without resolving whether that bound is sharp.
