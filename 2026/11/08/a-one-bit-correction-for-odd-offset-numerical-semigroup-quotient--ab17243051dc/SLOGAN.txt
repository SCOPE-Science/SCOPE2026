For odd-offset numerical semigroup quotients \(\langle a,ha+d,ha+3d,\ldots,ha+(2k+1)d\rangle/p\) with \(p\mid a\), the Frobenius number is the last-residue candidate plus a single explicit parity correction. The correction is positive precisely when \(p\) and the terminal odd-coin remainder are odd, that remainder exceeds \(p\), and \(d<ha/p\), completing the unresolved odd-remainder branch of a 2023 formula.
