For a bipartite graph with both shores of size at least two, the induced-forest complex has nonzero second homology only in the complete bipartite case: deleting any cross-edge makes it \(2\)-connected, and deleting exactly one makes it contractible. The bound is sharp, since deleting two disjoint edges from \(K_{3,3}\) yields a complex homotopy equivalent to \(S^3\).
