For zero-inclusive EFX orientations of a simple cycle with common positive edge values, changing exactly one unit-weight good to any unequal positive weight reduces the exact fair-orientation count from \(L_n+2\cos(n\pi/3)\) to \(F_{n+1}+\delta_n\), independent of the perturbation's size or direction. Even an arbitrarily small perturbation removes a fraction approaching \(1-\varphi/\sqrt5\) of all formerly fair orientations.
