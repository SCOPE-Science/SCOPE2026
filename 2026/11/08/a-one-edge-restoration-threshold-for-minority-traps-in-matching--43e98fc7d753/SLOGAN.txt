Deleting the entire perfect matching from a balanced complete bipartite graph creates \(\binom{n}{n/2}\) suboptimal absorbing states of asynchronous closed-neighborhood minority dynamics when \(n\) is even, each cutting only \(n^2/2\) edges. Restoring just one of those edges eliminates **all** suboptimal recurrent classes, and for odd \(n\) they never occur.
