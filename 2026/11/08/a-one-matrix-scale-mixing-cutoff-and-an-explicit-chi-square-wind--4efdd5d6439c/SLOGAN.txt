The additive walk on traceless \(n\)-by-\(n\) finite-field matrices, driven by uniformly chosen nonzero rank-one nilpotents, has a sharp total-variation cutoff at \(n\) steps for every fixed field order. Its chi-square divergence at time \(n+s\) tends to the explicit convergent series \(\sum_{c\ge1}q^{-c^2-2cs}/\prod_{j=1}^c(1-q^{-j})\), exposing an entire hierarchy of exceptional Fourier eigenspaces.
