For a rank-\(k\) flat-spectrum positive matrix, exchangeable Gaussian Nyström trace estimation with exactly \(k\) probes has variance \(a^2(k+3)/(2k)\), but its customary squared-error proxy has mean only \(3a^2/(2k)\); one extra probe makes the estimate exact. The discrepancy comes from a fixed \(1/4\) pairwise correlation of leave-one-out estimates, not from loss of unbiasedness.
