When a uniformly sampled group copies one random member's opinion, a complete population with at most two agents left outside each group has an exact one-rate geometric consensus-time tail after its first event. For \(n\) agents and initial opinion split \(x,n-x\), the two-holdout survival probability at event \(t\ge1\) is \(\frac{3x(n-x)}{n(n-1)}\bigl(\frac{4n-6}{n(n-1)}\bigr)^{t-1}\), whereas a three-holdout example already loses this geometric law.
