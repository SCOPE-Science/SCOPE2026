For a symmetric lattice search with bounded independently resampled jump rates, heterogeneity always delays absorption from the site adjacent to the target, but from every farther site it accelerates early absorption and retards late absorption. The late survival penalty is exactly asymptotic to \(3m\sqrt{2/\pi}\,v\tau\,t^{-3/2}/(8\mu^{5/2})\), and the weak-disorder sign reversal occurs at one uniquely defined time independent of the rate-resampling period.
