For \(\mathbb F_q[x]/(x^N)\) in characteristic two, square-zero derivations at lengths \(2m\) and \(2m+1\) have exactly the same rank polynomial, with \(q^m+\lfloor m/2\rfloor(q-1)q^{m-1}\) operators. At fixed \(q\), a uniformly chosen such derivation has normalized rank converging to the uniform distribution on \([1/2,1]\), rather than concentrating at one rank.
