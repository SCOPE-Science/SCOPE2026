Perfect binary evolution algebras supported on a path with optional loops have exactly \(\frac{a_n+a_{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}}2\) isomorphism classes, where \(a_j=(2^{j+1}+(-1)^j)/3\). Precisely \(a_{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) labeled perfect members have a nontrivial automorphism, so reflection symmetry has probability asymptotic to \(2^{-\lceil n/2\rceil}\).
