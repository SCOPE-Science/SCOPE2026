For a unicyclic graph, the minimum enclosing sphere of its in-in zonotope detects whether the cycle is even: even cycles give exactly two antipodal contacts, while odd cycles give exactly \(4k\), where \(k\) is the number of cycle vertices of minimum graph degree. The squared-radius deficit from the coordinatewise degree bound is exactly \(4(\delta+t-1)\) in the odd case, where \(\delta\) is the minimum cycle degree and \(t>0\) the uniform coordinate-segment half-length.
