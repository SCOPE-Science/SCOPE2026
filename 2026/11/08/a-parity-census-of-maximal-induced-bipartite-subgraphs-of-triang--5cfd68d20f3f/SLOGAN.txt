In the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), every inclusion-maximal induced bipartite subgraph has \(n-1\) vertices when \(n\) is odd; for even \(n\ge6\), its order is either \(n-1\) or \(n\), with an exact rational-coefficient enumerator. The proportion of the larger class tends to \(2/3\), and complementary sets give the full inclusion-minimal odd-cycle-transversal census.
