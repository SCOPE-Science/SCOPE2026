# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is a universal feasibility statement for all positive integer part sizes and all prescribed odd-indegree subsets. Necessity follows from the sum of indegrees and existence of a directed acyclic source. For sufficiency, a parity calculation guarantees a potential sink. The safe-source lemma proves that an eligible source can always be removed while retaining both a potential source and a potential sink; all four parity cases and the unique-potential-sink boundary are treated explicitly. A source removal preserves the parity congruence, and the reduced graph stays complete bipartite. The star base case is verified directly. The exact signature count follows by subtracting the sole admissible but unrealizable parity vector when both part sizes are even. This argument proves the theorem without any finite-enumeration assumption. The verification script independently exhausts every orientation for part sizes through four and reproduces all 4,032 canonical parity-profile witnesses through twelve.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
The motivating 2026 work by Gravier, Petiteau, and Sivignon defines the general parity-constrained acyclic-orientation problem, studies parity/source/sink conditions and advertises classifications for Cartesian grids, cylinders and tori, as well as complete graphs. The accessible abstract, introduction and section outline do not supply this complete-bipartite classification, but the entire text was not retrievable; this is explicitly a priority limitation. West (1995) was read in full and characterizes possible numbers of independent edges as well as all acyclic orientations by alternating blocks; it does not state or derive the feasibility of every prescribed odd-indegree set. Das et al. (2021) concern acyclic bitournament structure and isomorphism, not the image of the indegree-parity map. Relevant published research records about orientations on complete bipartite graphs were compared: the nearest concerns non-separating out-branchings in \(K_{3,n}\), which does not entail constraints on every vertex's indegree parity. This is an actual mathematical distinction rather than title matching; historical priority is not certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete constructive classification for an infinite two-parameter family, resolving a natural restricted instance of the 2026 prescribed-parity orientation problem. Even though the individual graph has a standard orientation encoding, deciding whether an arbitrary **vector** of degree parities is simultaneously achievable while retaining acyclicity is stronger than the classical count of orientations or independent edges. The classification proves that the elementary edge-sum parity obstruction and the existence of a source are the **only** obstructions, including a sharp parity-dependent exceptional target. It also enumerates all feasible labelled signatures and constructs witnesses in time polynomial in the output graph. It does not assert hardness or a general-graph theorem.
