Every prescribed odd-indegree set \(T\) on a complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) has an acyclic realization exactly when \(|T|\equiv mn\pmod2\) and at least one vertex is prescribed even. Consequently the number of feasible parity signatures is \(2^{m+n-1}-1\) when both part sizes are even and \(2^{m+n-1}\) otherwise, with a constructive polynomial-time witness.
