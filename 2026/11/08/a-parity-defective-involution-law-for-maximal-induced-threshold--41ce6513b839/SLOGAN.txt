For triangular graphs \(T_n=L(K_n)\), the cardinality polynomial of inclusion-maximal induced threshold subgraphs is a weighted involution polynomial on \(n-3\) labels with one parity-dependent perfect-matching correction. It has contiguous support, strictly log-concave positive coefficients for \(n\ge7\), and bivariate exponential generating function \(z^3t^3(e^{zt+zt^2/2}/2-e^{zt^2/2}/3)\).
