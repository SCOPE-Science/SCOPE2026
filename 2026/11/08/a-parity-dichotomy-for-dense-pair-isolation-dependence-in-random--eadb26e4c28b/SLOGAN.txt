Conditioning \(G(n,p)\) on all degrees being even makes two vertices' isolation indicators positively correlated at \(p=1/2\), but in the dense limit their covariance is positive for every odd \(n\ge3\) and negative for every even \(n\ge4\). Thus every even order has at least one sign reversal; for four vertices the crossing is exactly \(p=2/3\).
