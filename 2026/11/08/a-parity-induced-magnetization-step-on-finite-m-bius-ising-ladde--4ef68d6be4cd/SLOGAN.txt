A twisted cubic Ising ladder has a parity-sensitive finite-size magnetization step: for even rung count the uniform antiferromagnet jumps from zero magnetization to exactly two aligned net spins at \(|h|=J\), before saturation at \(|h|=3J\); for odd rung count the intermediate step disappears. At saturation coexistence the exact degeneracy is \((1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n-(-1)^n\), the number of independent sets of the \(2n\)-vertex Möbius ladder.
