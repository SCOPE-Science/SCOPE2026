A positive, vertex-transitive direction-weighted hypercube can have every Laplacian eigenspace of dimension at most \(2\) yet require exactly \(3\) dedicated vertex actuators. The weights \((1,3,5,7,9,20)\) give an exact parity-obstruction certificate and the optimal placement \(\{0,e_1,e_2\}\); superincreasing extra direction weights preserve the gap in every higher dimension.
