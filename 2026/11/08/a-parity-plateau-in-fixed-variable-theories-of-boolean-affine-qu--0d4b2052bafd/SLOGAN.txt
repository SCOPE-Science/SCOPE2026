In Boolean affine spaces whose only predicate says when four points have zero XOR, counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^k\) sees dimension only up to an exact, parity-dependent cutoff: \(k-1\) for odd widths \(k\ge5\), but \(k-2\) for even widths \(k\ge6\). Thus the full \(5\)- and \(6\)-variable theories coincide on every such space, whereas seven variables separate dimensions four and five; the same classification applies to Boolean Steiner quadruple systems.
