For irreversible dimers on two dense cliques joined by one weighted bridge, the bridge's occupancy probability has an exact finite Laplace law, a weak-rate parity singularity, and the unequal-clique asymptotic \(P_{a,b}(\lambda)\sim\lambda\log(b/a)/(b-a)\) when both clique sizes grow at comparable rates, interpreted as \(\lambda/a\) for equal sizes. A single occupied bridge improves the jammed matching by one dimer for two odd cliques but worsens it by one for two even cliques.
