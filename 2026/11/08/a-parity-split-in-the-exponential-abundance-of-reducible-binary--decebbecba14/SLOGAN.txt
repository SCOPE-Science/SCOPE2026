For rank-preserving binary free-semigroup endomorphisms whose two generator images both have length \(n\), reducible maps are exponentially rare, but their sharp exponential growth rate depends on parity: \(2^{3/2}\) for even \(n\) and \(\varphi^2\) for odd \(n\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). This split comes from pure-power factorizations at even lengths versus Fibonacci-weighted factorizations at odd lengths.
