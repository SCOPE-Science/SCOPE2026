For a uniform labeled tree, the joint law of off-path and marked-endpoint leaves refines the previously known off-path distribution into an exact probability-generating polynomial for the **total** number of leaves conditional on two-point distance. The conditional expected leaf count is nonmonotone: its maximizing distance is asymptotic to \((2n)^{1/3}\), although typical distances are of order \(\sqrt n\); the leaf-count/distance covariance is asymptotic to \(-\sqrt{\pi/2}\,\sqrt n/(2e)\).
