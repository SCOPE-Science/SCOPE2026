# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The statement concerns only finite paths and membership queries on *minimum-cardinality* independent dominating sets. With \(r=\lceil n/3\rceil\), all minimum solutions are bijective with short gap positions, in number \(3r-n\), and chosen vertices occur at \(3j-1-|H\cap[j]|\). This proves the exact classification of hypotheses. For two gaps, all vertex queries reduce to three-state equality predicates on a nondecreasing word; the proof classifies every shattered pair and excludes triples by a complete case table. For one gap, membership queries are binary thresholds on a linearly ordered parameter; balanced binary search and the cardinality bound give identical online depths. The bundled standard-library checker independently enumerates solutions, all supports of size at most three through 43 vertices, exact online depth through 32 vertices, and state-pair patterns through 15 selected vertices. Finite calculations support the universal proof but are not substituted for it.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to priority limits.** Samanmoo, Trakultraipruk, and Ananchuen (2019, full path discussion and Theorems 1–3) classify reconfiguration graphs for these *same* minimum-cardinality independent dominating sets, and Brewster, Mynhardt, and Teshima (2023, Section 2) describe their gaps and counts. Thus the construction's solution parametrization is not presented as novel. Neither inspected primary source states the vertex-membership shattered-support polynomial, its quadratic coefficient, nor the exact online depth and unbounded VC–Littlestone separation. The problem is also distinct from membership shattering for **all** inclusion-maximal independent sets, because the smaller minimum-solution family has different trace geometry. Equivalent formulations involving graph-solution traces or decision-tree identification remain a residual historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The exact polynomial counts *every* shattered support for every path length, not only the maximum dimension. It exposes a period-three phase change and a nontrivial quadratic support count in one residue class. More importantly, the exact formula \(\operatorname{Ldim}(\mathcal I_{3k+2})=\lfloor\log_2(k+2)\rfloor\) while \(\operatorname{VCdim}(\mathcal I_{3k+2})=1\) supplies an unbounded adaptive-minus-static gap on connected paths for a natural minimum-solution concept class. Such separation is not implied by the known reconfiguration graph being a path: that graph does not encode vertex-membership query traces.
