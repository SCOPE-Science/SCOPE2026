# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact face rule is determined by the shared-edge geometry. Splitting into the private-block \(S_2(m)\) join and the two cone extensions over the \(S_1(m)\) join gives a cellular attaching decomposition, with a genuinely additional connecting edge. Integral Koszul chains prove the skeleton homology modules; the connectivity inequality nullhomotopes the attaching inclusion; homotopy cofibers give two, not one, suspended copies of the link, plus the fundamental circle. The integral representation follows from natural exterior chains, join orientation signs, and permutation of the two cone tips. Exact mod-2 and mod-3 homology, and signed Lefschetz checks for three automorphism generators corroborate the proof. Finite computations are not used as a proof for unbounded parameters.

## Originality — PASS with residual literature uncertainty
The motivating arXiv:2602.09623 theorem covers shellability under a diamond-free condition, whereas the present edge-glued graph has diamonds, and the inspected full article contains no comparable topological or equivariant classification. A previously studied vertex-glued windmill has no persistent fundamental-circle class and has one copy, not two, of the low-dimensional sphere family: its formulas cannot imply the present edge-glued theorem. Searches for the K2 clique-sum, clique-free books, Alexander-dual expressions, exact homology and symmetry module found no directly equivalent or stronger treatment. Search limitations do not establish uniqueness. Equivalence under an unindexed alternate terminology remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Edge overlap is the first structural obstruction to the diamond-free hypothesis in the motivating shellability study. The result gives an infinite, natural family with a persistent fundamental-circle generator plus two separable sphere families and full integral symmetry data. The distinction from a vertex-overlap book is substantive: adding a common edge changes both connectivity and representation type. The rational acyclicity of the full symmetry quotient is an additional topological consequence. The result does not claim a general chordal-graph classification.

## Limitations
Only equal-size complete-graph pages sharing exactly an edge, triangle avoidance, and the indicated automorphism group; no homotopy-equivalent equivariant wedge is claimed. Full-quotient integral homology, torsion and contractibility are not settled.
