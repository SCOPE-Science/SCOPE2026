For \(k\ge2\) complete graphs \(K_q\) sharing exactly one edge, \(q\ge4\), the triangle-free vertex-subset complex has integral homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^{2(q-3)^k}S^k\vee\bigvee^{\binom{q-3}{2}^k}S^{2k-1}\). Its full automorphism action is determined on integral homology; unlike vertex-glued clique books, the edge-glued complex has a persistent fundamental circle, while its full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
