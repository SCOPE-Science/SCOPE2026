# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** For every potential isolated chord, the two complementary arcs contain independent uniform matchings. This yields the exact conditional difference in expected crossing count, the finite covariance sum, and strict negativity. The geometric bound on split probabilities gives the stated three-term asymptotic. The isolated-minus-simple count vanishes in \(L^2\), establishing isolated-count variance convergence; the crossing variance is given in Flajolet and Noy's Theorem 3. Exact finite assertions also check the identities independently.

**Originality: PASS (with residual priority risk).** Flajolet–Noy (2000, sections 1–2) gives separate marginal component and crossing results, Acan (2015, sections 2–3) gives a marked-chord degree analysis and monolithic structure, and Paguyo (2021/2023, sections 2–3) gives separate crossing and simple-chord approximations. A previous simple-chord/crossing joint enumerator already supplies the simple-chord covariance and sharp correlation decay, so those are not claimed new. The inspected statements and accessible records do not include the **all-isolated-chord** covariance or the persistent \(-1/3\) covariance contribution from rare non-simple isolated chords. General chord-diagram enumerators could conceivably encode this mixed moment implicitly; that remains a priority risk and no unique-priority claim is made.

**Value: PASS.** The result resolves the dependence between two natural but differently scaled statistics of the same circle graph. Its conditional decomposition and exact all-size sign show that marginal Poisson and normal laws conceal a negative finite-size dependence; the three-term asymptotic proves that a class of rare chords, despite vanishing in \(L^2\), retains a nonzero mixed covariance. The sharp correlation constant is a consequence, not the independent novelty claim. It is not an arbitrary parameter slice or a mere re-enumeration of a known table.

Scientific limitations: no joint weak convergence is claimed; the use of exact finite checks is supplementary to the written infinite proof; older implicit joint enumerations remain a possible overlap. Relevant public primary texts: https://algo.inria.fr/flajolet/Publications/FlNo00.pdf ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.01489 ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.01134 .
