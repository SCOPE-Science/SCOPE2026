In uniform random chord diagrams, isolated chords and total crossings have strictly negative covariance with exact split-sum formula and expansion \(-n/3-1/6-5/(6n)+O(n^{-2})\). Although **non-simple** isolated chords disappear in \(L^2\), their covariance with total crossings converges to the nonzero value \(-1/3\), revealing a persistent rare-event correction beyond the previously known simple-chord dependence.
