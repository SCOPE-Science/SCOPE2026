For a length-\(N\) cyclic Gabor basis formed from two translations and every even modulation, a phase-only window exists precisely when the translation step has even order; a binary alphabet suffices when that order is divisible by four, while order two modulo four requires a quadrature alphabet. For every admissible \(q\)-phase alphabet the exact number of windows is \(2^{\gcd(N,k)}q^{N/2}\), and the unrestricted phase-only solution space is a disjoint union of \(2^{\gcd(N,k)}\) tori.
