For every odd prime \(p\ge5\), a unimodular signal is either a quadratic chirp with exactly \(p\) nonzero periodic auto-ambiguity samples, or its auto-ambiguity support has at least \(4p-3\) samples. This phase-only forbidden support interval is sharp at \(p=5\), and every nonzero delay of a nonchirp contains at least four Doppler frequencies.
