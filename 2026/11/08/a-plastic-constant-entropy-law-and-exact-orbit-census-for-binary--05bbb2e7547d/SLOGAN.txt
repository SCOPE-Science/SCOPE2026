The binary \((1,3)\)-pseudoperiodic shift has exactly the dynamical zeta function \(1/((1-z)^2(1-z^2-z^3)(1+z^2-z^3))\), with entropy the logarithm of the plastic constant. Its nonconstant periodic words are precisely the cyclic words whose runs have length one or two without adjacent singleton runs; the zeta function gives every periodic-point and primitive-orbit count.
