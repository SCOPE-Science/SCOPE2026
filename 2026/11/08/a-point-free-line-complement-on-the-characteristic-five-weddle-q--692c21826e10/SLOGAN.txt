For the Weddle quartic attached to all six points of \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_5)\), the twenty-five \(\mathbb F_5\)-rational lines contain every one of its 66 rational points. The line-complement has no \(\mathbb F_5\)-points but has exactly 460 \(\mathbb F_{25}\)-points, with an explicit Hasse–Weil zeta function.
