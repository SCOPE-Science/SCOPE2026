For the constant-rate zero-range process on \(K_{r,n}\) with \(r\ge3\) fixed hubs and finitely many pendant edges, the non-hub mass converges to an independent \(\operatorname{Poisson}(r)\) variable while the hub occupation fractions converge to an exponentially tilted uniform law on the \((r-1)\)-simplex. Even one added pendant edge creates a nonvanishing bias in the macroscopic mass sharing.
