At the double-critical scale of \(\mu\) expected broken rim edges and \(\lambda\) expected open spokes in a percolated wheel, the hub-connected rim fraction converges to a Poisson-cut, Poisson-seed arc law with nonzero interior mass. Its full-connectivity probability has the explicit crossover \(2e^{-(\lambda+\mu)}[e^{\lambda/2}\cosh(\sqrt{\lambda^2+4\lambda\mu}/2)-1]\), backed by an exact two-type finite-wheel transfer identity.
