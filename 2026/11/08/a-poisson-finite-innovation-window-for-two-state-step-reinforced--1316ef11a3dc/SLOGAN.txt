For a fair binary step-reinforced walk with rare independent fresh increments, the even-horizon return bias converges to \(2^{-\lambda}\) when the expected number of fresh increments after the first converges to \(\lambda\). Its mechanism is a Poisson collection of finite recursive-tree components with intensity \(\lambda/[k(k+1)]\) at size \(k\), which yields an exact critical window between perfect reinforcement and mixing.
