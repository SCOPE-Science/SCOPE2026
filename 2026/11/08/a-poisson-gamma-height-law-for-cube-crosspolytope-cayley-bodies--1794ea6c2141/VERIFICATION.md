The theorem is based on an exact volume partition of cube-plus-crosspolytope sections into orthant simplices, followed by beta integrals. The finite mixture weights are normalized exactly by the truncated exponential, and weak convergence follows by fixed-index beta-to-gamma convergence plus a Poisson tail argument. The first two moments follow by exact conditional beta moments and dominated convergence.

The standalone `verify.py` performs exact-rational tests of normalization and moments and numerical convergence tests for several fixed ratios. These computations corroborate the proof but do not certify infinite-dimensional assertions. Historical-priority coverage is bounded and independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
