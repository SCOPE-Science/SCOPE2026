For independent uniform agent locations on a line, the strategyproof Inverse-Square mechanism with one fewer facility has a conditional expected-cost approximation ratio of order \(\log n\), with \(R_n/\log n\xrightarrow{d}(T_1\sum_{j\ge1}T_j^{-2})^{-1}\) for rate-one Poisson arrival times \(T_j\). In particular, its approximation ratio diverges in probability even though the mechanism has a square-root-order worst-case guarantee on arbitrary metrics.
