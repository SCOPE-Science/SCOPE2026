For every fixed \(k\ge3\), the common-edge count of \(k\) independent uniform spanning trees of \(K_{a,b}\) has a sharp threshold at \(b\asymp a^{k-1}\): when \(b/a^{k-1}\to\theta\in(0,\infty)\), it converges in total variation to \(\operatorname{Pois}(\theta)\). At this threshold the common edges form, with high probability, a uniformly distributed bipartite matching of Poisson size.
