In binary graphical fair division, the minimum number of envious agents equals the number of nontrivial tree components, even when welfare maximization is required. At random edge density \(p_n=(\log n+\log\log n+c)/(2n)\), this minimum converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(e^{-c}/4)\), yielding a sharp zero-envy probability of \(\exp(-e^{-c}/4)\).
