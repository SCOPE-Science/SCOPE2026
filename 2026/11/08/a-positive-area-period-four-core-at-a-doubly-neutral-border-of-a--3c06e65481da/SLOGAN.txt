At the joint neutral boundary \(a=0,c=1\) of a four-partition absolute-value map, every real-plane orbit reaches a compact periodic core after finitely many steps, and that core has exact area \(1/2\) with almost every point of least period four. For \(a=0\) and \(0<c<1\), each fixed base two-cycle instead attracts a unique fiber two-cycle; the limiting selected graph develops a jump filled by the critical period-two vertical segment.
