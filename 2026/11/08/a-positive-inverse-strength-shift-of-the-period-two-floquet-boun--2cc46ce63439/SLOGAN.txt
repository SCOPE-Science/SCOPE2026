For the two-predator–one-prey logarithmic return map, the Floquet multiplier \(-1\) boundary of the unique period-two orbit approaches the classical Ricker threshold \(p_*\approx2.52646772585\) from above, with the exact first correction \(p_{\mathrm{flip}}(q)=p_*+6.74559154467/q+O(q^{-2})\). Thus the finite-strength orbit remains locally attracting at the limiting Ricker threshold for all sufficiently large finite \(q\), even though its limiting multiplier is neutral.
