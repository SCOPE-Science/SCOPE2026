The number of \(2\)-spheres in the clique complex of the one-cell-transfer graph of partitions of any positive integer equals a positive sum over conjugate partitions: each contributes \(\binom{s-2}{2}\) for at least four distinct column heights, plus one for each interior height gap of size at least two. This solves the exact sphere-rank enumeration in a partition-statistic form and yields an explicit formal generating function.
