An unweighted rooted tree with only one or two children per vertex can realize *any* prescribed critical exponent \(\rho>1\): the peripheral circle of the backward shift is absent from its \(\ell^p\) point spectrum for \(p\le\rho\) and entirely present for \(p>\rho\). The full and Fredholm essential spectra remain exact disks, with infinite-dimensional eigenspaces throughout the point spectrum.
