Two derivations of \(K[x,y]\) can each satisfy \(D^p=E^p=0\) while generating an infinite-dimensional associative operator algebra, so local finiteness of the generators alone cannot replace local finiteness of every commutator. For the explicit Frobenius-twisted pair, the generated Lie algebra is infinite-dimensional in odd characteristic but is three-dimensional in characteristic two, where the associative envelope is exactly a rank-four Clifford-type algebra over a polynomial ring.
