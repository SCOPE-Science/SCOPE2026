# Review of the Petersen-boundary integral lattice result

## Correctness
PASS. The graph is the known ten-component, fifteen-node Petersen boundary of a degree-five del Pezzo surface. Normalization gluing identifies the connected Picard torus's character lattice with integral graph cycles; the orientation signs are essential and are included explicitly. The displayed six-by-six map is integral, with determinant negative two and Smith invariants \((1,1,1,1,1,2)\). Exact checks of all 120 permutations establish equivariance. An exact modular rank-35 certificate for the complete 72-by-36 intertwiner matrix, combined with the exhibited solution, proves uniqueness over the rationals; the unit matrix entry proves integral primitivity. The duality of tori and Galois lattices gives the \(\mu_2\)-kernel even in characteristic two. These steps form an actual proof, not a numerical experiment or a claim inferred from failed enumeration.

## Originality
PASS, with historical-priority qualification. The 2010 degree-five del Pezzo literature supplies the ten-line configuration and its arithmetic twists. Dolgachev–Farb–Looijenga (2018) and Farb–Looijenga (2019/2020) identify the Petersen degeneration with the full \(S_5\)-action. Those results imply the graph and support rational representation comparisons, but the specifically tested integral index-two embedding, complete equivariant Hom group, and \(\mu_2\)-isogeny were not located in the material inspected. Ren–Shaw–Sturmfels study the tropical complement, not this Jacobian's integral character comparison. Results about homology of other Petersen-derived simplicial complexes are not implications for the ordinary graph-cycle lattice. Specialist integral representation tables remain an identifiable priority risk. Absence from a search is not proof of historical novelty.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The divisor is the canonical geometric boundary of degree-five del Pezzo surfaces and their \(\overline{M}_{0,5}\) interpretation. Its generalized Jacobian governs gluing of line bundles on a maximally nodal genus-six curve. The exact index two distinguishes integral descent and algebraic group schemes despite identical rational \(S_5\) characters; in characteristic two its kernel is infinitesimal. The theorem gives a complete equivariant map classification, not an arbitrary selected statistic or a restatement of a known complex representation.

## Scope and risks
The nonisomorphism is \(S_5\)-equivariant, not a blanket assertion about every twisted torus; in particular, split tori are abstractly isomorphic. The result does not include new point-count profiles and has not received independent mathematical validation.
