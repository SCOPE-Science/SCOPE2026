For the full ten-line boundary of a smooth degree-five del Pezzo surface, the Petersen-graph cycle lattice is rationally but not integrally the exterior square of the \(A_4\) root lattice: their unique primitive \(S_5\)-equivariant inclusion has index two. This gives a degree-two \(\mu_2\)-isogeny from the six-dimensional generalized Jacobian to the naturally associated exterior-square torus, including a nonreduced kernel in characteristic two.
