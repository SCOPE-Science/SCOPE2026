# A primitive invariant in the Pappus graph's degree-five Rips homology

## Finding
Let \(P\) be the eighteen-vertex Pappus point–line incidence graph, with the shortest-path metric, let \(X=\operatorname{VR}_{\leq3}(P)\), and let \(G=\operatorname{Aut}(P)\). The complete **integral** action of \(G\), of order \(216\), on \(\widetilde H_5(X;\mathbb Z)\) is the signed permutation-of-factors action on the rank-\(64\) lattice
\[
\widetilde H_5(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\bigotimes_{i=1}^{6}\widetilde H_0(B_i;\mathbb Z),
\]
where \(B_i\) are the six natural three-vertex classes of mutually distance-four vertices. The word “signed” means that permuting the six augmented degree-zero factors contributes the sign of the factor permutation.

For \(g\in G\), let \(\pi_g\in\mathfrak S_6\) be its permutation of the classes. For each cycle \(C\) of \(\pi_g\), of length \(\ell\), choose one class \(B_C\) on that cycle and let \(f_C(g)\) be the number of vertices of \(B_C\) fixed by \(g^\ell\). Its degree-five rational character is exactly
\[
\chi_5(g)=\operatorname{sgn}(\pi_g)\prod_{C\text{ cycle of }\pi_g}\bigl(f_C(g)-1\bigr).
\]
Among the \(216\) group elements, the trace distribution \((\text{trace}:\text{multiplicity})\) is
\[
(-8:18),\quad(-2:36),\quad(0:54),\quad(1:56),\quad(4:42),\quad(16:9),\quad(64:1).
\]
In particular the fixed integral lattice \(\widetilde H_5(X;\mathbb Z)^G\) is **rank one**, generated by the explicit primitive class below. The subgroup preserving the point–line bipartition has fixed rank three. Furthermore,
\[
\widetilde H_j(X/G;\mathbb Q)\cong
\begin{cases}\mathbb Q,&j=5,\\0,&j\ne5.\end{cases}
\]
This character, primitive invariant, and quotient conclusion—not the previously determined nonequivariant sphere count—are the asserted finding.

## Assumptions and scope
Work over the field \(\mathbf F_3\). Label nine points \(p_{x,y}\), with \(x,y\in\mathbf F_3\), and nine lines in the three classes \(x=c\), \(y=c\), and \(y-x=c\), with \(c\in\mathbf F_3\). Edges encode incidence. For a reproducible integer labeling put \(p_{x,y}=3x+y\), and label the lines \(9+3t+c\) with \(t=0,1,2\) for those three respective families. This graph is isomorphic, by the explicit permutation supplied in `verify_character.py`, to the standard Pappus LCF graph \([5,7,-7,7,-7,-5]^3\). Its diameter is four.

The inclusive radius-three Rips complex contains every nonempty vertex subset whose pairwise graph distances are at most three. Its automorphism action is the natural simplicial action; homology and fixed lattices here are unreduced in degree zero only when explicitly indicated, and otherwise reduced. The claimed quotient homology has rational coefficients; no statement of integral quotient homology is made.

## Proof
**Distance partition and join.** Exhaustive breadth-first search on the above eighteen explicitly given vertices finds that the graph whose edges join pairs at distance four is a disjoint union of six triangles. In the stated labeling the triangles are
\[
B_1=(0,5,7),\ B_2=(1,3,8),\ B_3=(2,4,6),\ B_4=(9,10,11),\ B_5=(12,13,14),\ B_6=(15,16,17).
\]
The certificate checks all \(153\) unordered vertex pairs, including absence of interclass distance-four pairs. A face at radius three therefore chooses at most one vertex from each class. Hence, as an **actual simplicial isomorphism**, not just a homotopy equivalence,
\[
X=B_1*B_2*B_3*B_4*B_5*B_6,
\]
where each \(B_i\) is discrete on three vertices. The reduced integral join–Künneth formula gives a free rank-\(2^6=64\) homology lattice in degree five and zero other reduced homology.

**Signed equivariance.** Each automorphism preserves graph distance and therefore permutes the six classes. Choosing the ordered class list above identifies the top homology lattice with the tensor product of their augmentation lattices. Under \(g\), the resulting map rearranges the six factors while applying the induced bijections of their vertices. The join-chain suspension grading gives \((-1)^{(0+1)(0+1)}=-1\) for every exchange of two degree-zero factors; thus the factor-reordering sign is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\pi_g)\), not the trivial sign. On a cycle of \(\ell\) tensor factors, the trace is that of the composite endomorphism of one factor. For a permutation of a three-point set, the augmentation-lattice trace equals its number of fixed points minus one. Multiplication over block cycles proves the displayed formula for all \(g\).

**Completeness of the group and character computation.** A distance-preserving permutation is a graph automorphism, and conversely every automorphism preserves graph distance. The certificate enumerates all such permutations by recursive injection construction: at every step, all unused vertex images consistent with *every already fixed pairwise distance* are tried. The search has exactly \(3313\) recursion nodes and finds \(216\) distinct complete permutations. The resulting trace histogram is computed from the formula above and, separately, from the exact alternating oriented-face Lefschetz trace on all \(4095\) nonempty faces of \(X\). The two methods agree **element by element** on all \(216\) permutations. The trace sum is \(216\), so the rational invariant dimension is \(216/216=1\). For the \(108\) point–line-preserving elements the trace sum is \(324\), hence their rational fixed dimension is \(3\).

**An integral primitive generator.** Write each ordered class as \(B_i=(b_{i0},b_{i1},b_{i2})\), put \(u_i=[b_{i1}]-[b_{i0}]\in\widetilde H_0(B_i;\mathbb Z)\), and let \(u=u_1\otimes\cdots\otimes u_6\). The explicitly defined orbit sum satisfies
\[
z=\frac1{12}\sum_{g\in G}g(u)\ \in\ \bigotimes_{i=1}^6\widetilde H_0(B_i;\mathbb Z).
\]
The exact integral expansion checked by the standalone certificate has precisely \(42\) nonzero tensor-basis coefficients, each equal to \(+1\) or \(-1\); all coefficients of the undivided orbit sum are divisible by \(12\). The vector \(z\) is fixed by every automorphism, is nonzero, and is primitive because a coefficient is a unit. Since the fixed rational subspace is one-dimensional, \(z\) generates the entire integral fixed sublattice.

**Quotient.** For a finite simplicial group action, pass to the barycentric subdivision and use rational averaging/transfer to identify homology of the quotient with the invariant rational homology of the original complex. Here all reduced rational homology outside degree five vanishes, while the degree-five invariant space is one-dimensional. This establishes the displayed quotient homology. It does not assert an integral or homotopy classification of the quotient.

## Relationship to prior work
Adamaszek's *Clique complexes and graph powers*, first publicly posted April 3, 2011, develops the graph-power Rips construction, including a girth-collapse theorem and graph-square stability criteria. Its explicit cycle theorems and criteria do not provide the stated Pappus integral automorphism character. A previously available nonequivariant classification of the Rips filtration for affine-plane incidence graphs with one parallel class removed **does** cover the Pappus graph's rank-\(64\) fifth homology; this rank and the join topology are not claimed as original here. The new scope is the **integral signed group action**, its exact full-group character, the primitive invariant generator, and the quotient's rational homology. Known equivariant Rips computations for other graph families and homology representations of different Pappus graph complexes are not implication-equivalent to this statement.

## Limitations
No integral homology or homotopy type of \(X/G\) is established. The formula determines the full character on all group elements but does not tabulate irreducible rational constituent multiplicities. It concerns only the canonical Pappus graph at inclusive graph-distance scale three and does not assert the same character for general affine planes or other filtration stages. Publication priority remains subject to broader external review; no independent mathematical audit has been performed.
