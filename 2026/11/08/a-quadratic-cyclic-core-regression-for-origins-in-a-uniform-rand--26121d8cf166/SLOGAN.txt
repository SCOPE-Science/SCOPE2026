In a uniform random mapping, conditioning on \(m\) cyclic vertices makes the expected number of indegree-zero vertices strictly decrease with \(m\), but on the natural \(m\asymp\sqrt n\) window the dependence is quadratic rather than linear: its order-one correction is \((1-m^2/n)/(2e)\). This yields an exact negative covariance identity between cyclic-core and origin counts, with sharp asymptotic \(-\sqrt{\pi/8}\,\sqrt n/e\).
