The mathematical proof is in `RESULT.md`. With Python 3, run `python3 verify.py`; it enumerates all \(n^n\) functions for \(1\le n\le6\) and performs 200 integer/rational comparisons. Exact finite matching is a consistency check only. The all-size conditional factorial-moment law follows from the forest deletion-code bijection; the covariance sign and asymptotics follow from conditional restriction and Rayleigh tails. The proof is not a formal computer proof. The strongest residual uncertainty concerns implicit prior coverage by historical multivariate generating-function literature. No independent audit was performed.
