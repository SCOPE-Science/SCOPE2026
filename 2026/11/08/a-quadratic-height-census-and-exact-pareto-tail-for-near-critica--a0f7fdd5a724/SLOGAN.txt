Primitive unordered four-runner speed sets with maximum speed at most \(H\) and loneliness below \(1/4\) number \(H^2/\pi^2+O(H\log(2H))\). Their scaled gap \(H(1/4-\operatorname{ML})\) has an explicit eight-sector limiting law whose tail is exactly \(1/(24z^2)\) for every \(z\geq1/2\), so the limiting law has finite moments precisely below order two.
