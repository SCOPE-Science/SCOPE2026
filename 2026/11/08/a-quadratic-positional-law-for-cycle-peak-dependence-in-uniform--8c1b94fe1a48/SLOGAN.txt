For a uniform permutation, the covariance between its total cycle count and a peak comparison across any three ordered positions has an exact quadratic numerator independent of permutation size. Adjacent peaks have negative covariance, but suitably separated comparisons have positive covariance starting sharply at five letters; the dependence scale jumps from order \(n^{-3}\) for adjacent peaks to order \(n^{-1}\) for widely separated positions.
