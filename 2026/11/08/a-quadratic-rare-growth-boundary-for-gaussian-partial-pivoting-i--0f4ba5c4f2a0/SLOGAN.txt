For exact \(2\times2\) Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting, the entire upper-tail probability is given by an explicit two-dimensional integral and the probability of growth exceeding \(2-\varepsilon\) is \(\varepsilon^2/(2\pi^2)+o(\varepsilon^2)\). The quadratic exponent extends to independent continuous symmetric entries with exponential tails, but its sharp coefficient depends on the entry distribution.
