For a rate-one random walk resetting to its initial leaf on a two-hub tree, a positive reset rate shortens mean time to a neighboring target leaf exactly when \(a<4b^2+3b-2\), with a unique optimum and a strictly convex mean-time curve. With fixed local branching \(a\) and a remote trap growing as \(b\to\infty\), optimal resetting keeps mean search time bounded even while the no-reset mean diverges.
