For clique-joined odd-cycle stable-set polytopes, the exact Euclidean inball changes its supporting facets at the quadratic threshold \(t=a^2-2a-1\). Exactly at that threshold (for \(a\ge3\)), a single ball of radius \(1/[a(a-1)]\) touches **every facet**; away from it, either the lifted cycle-edge facets or the global odd-hole facet ceases to touch.
