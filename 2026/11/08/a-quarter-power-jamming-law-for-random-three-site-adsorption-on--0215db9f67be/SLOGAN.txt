Equal-rate irreversible three-site path adsorption on balanced complete bipartite graphs leaves a random number of sites of order \(m^{1/4}\), with exact limit \(|Z|^{3/2}\) after normalization; an initial \(\sqrt m\)-scale imbalance shifts the Gaussian before the power. For a fixed relative imbalance \(\delta\ne0\), the vacancy fraction instead approaches \(|\delta|^{3/2}\).
