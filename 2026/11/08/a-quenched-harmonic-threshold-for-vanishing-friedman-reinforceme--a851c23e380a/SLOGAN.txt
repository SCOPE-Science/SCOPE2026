In a balanced two-colour Pólya–Friedman urn, even a vanishing frequency of opposite-colour reinforcements forces the limiting proportion to be exactly one half if and only if their harmonic weight diverges. If that weight is finite, the limit remains genuinely random even after conditioning on the entire sequence of reinforcement choices; independent intervention probabilities \(p_n\asymp(\log n)^{-\theta}\) have a sharp transition at \(\theta=1\).
