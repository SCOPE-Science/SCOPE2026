For positive radial Toeplitz probes on uniformly convex weighted Fock spaces, a Weyl constant exists exactly when the radial density \(\Phi'(r)/r\) tends to a limit. More generally the entire spectral-counting cluster interval is precisely the \(d\)-th-power image of the radial-density cluster interval, divided by \(d!\); a smooth uniformly convex oscillatory weight realizes a nontrivial interval.
