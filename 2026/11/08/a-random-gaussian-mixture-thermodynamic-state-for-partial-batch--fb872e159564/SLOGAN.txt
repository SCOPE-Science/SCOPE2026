At every fixed strictly partial batch fraction \(p\in(0,1)\), a many-particle Brownian gas under uniform Poisson batch resetting has a genuinely random thermodynamic position density: a geometric-weighted mixture of Gaussians with successive shared Poisson ages. Its collective mean-square displacement converges to \(2D\sum_{j\ge0}(1-p)^jE_j\), with an exact infinite-product Laplace transform and an exponential right tail whose sharp amplitude is \(\prod_{j\ge1}[1-(1-p)^j]^{-1}\).
