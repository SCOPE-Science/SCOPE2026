For the complete two-level poset, enriched order and enriched chain polytopes have equal volume, but their uniform-interior covariance matrices differ by an exactly computable positive rank-one matrix. Their affine-invariant isotropic constants satisfy \((L(\mathcal O^{(e)})/L(\mathcal C^{(e)}))^{2(m+n)}=1+3mn/((m+n+1)(n+2))\), whose strict universal supremum is \(4\).
