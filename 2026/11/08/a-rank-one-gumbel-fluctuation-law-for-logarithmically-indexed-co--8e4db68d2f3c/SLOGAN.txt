At the first completion of a uniform coupon collection, the empirical empty-album profile for sibling indices \(j=\lfloor\log N+x\sqrt{\log N}\rfloor\) has a universal rank-one Gumbel fluctuation: after exact Poisson centering and multiplication by \(\sqrt{\log N}\), its whole compact-index profile converges to \(-\phi(x)G\). This growing-index law is qualitatively different from the fixed-sibling exponential limit, and its leading covariance kernel is \((\pi^2/6)\phi(x)\phi(y)\).
