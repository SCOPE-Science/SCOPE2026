# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The independent exponential-order-statistic representation reduces ranked gap lengths and Greenwood's quadratic index to linear and quadratic forms of a uniform Dirichlet vector. Third moments give the exact all-size covariance; the double harmonic identity is elementary and exact. Monotonicity of the single-step covariance difference establishes one sign transition. First- and second-order Riemann-sum limits together with exact variance formulas establish the rank-fraction correlation profile. The standard-library checker supplies exact finite validation, not a substitute for the all-size proof. Correlations are only asserted for strictly interior rank fractions.

## Originality
PASS with residual historical priority risk. Holst (1980), inspected in full, gives the exponential representation and studies rank marginals and linear-combination asymptotics, not the mixed Greenwood covariance or \(e^{-2}\) cutoff. Schlemm (2014), inspected by HTML full text, establishes maximum-gap moments, not the joint concentration profile. Selected Ghosh–Jammalamadaka (1998) sections provide Greenwood marginal approximations; Erdmann-Pham et al. (2022), inspected in HTML, study two-sample rank-spacing counts rather than sorted Dirichlet arc lengths. Known historical mixed moment methods may in principle be specialized to the formula; they are not themselves a reported complete rank sign/correlation phase law. The inaccessible remainder of some older broad survey material is a residual risk, not evidence of disjointness. Database and historical claim searches did not identify the same ranked covariance profile. The previously recorded maximum-gap/long-gap-count and threshold/power-concentration laws determine aggregate threshold covariances, not the covariance of every individual ranked spacing with Greenwood's statistic; no claim of database uniqueness follows from that observation.

## Value
PASS. The exact sign change across the ordered gap profile identifies which gap ranks reinforce or oppose the quadratic concentration statistic in a canonical null model for spacing tests. The \(e^{-2}\) crossover and continuous nontrivial correlation limit are structural information not evident from marginal ordered-gap moments or Greenwood's variance, and characterize every interior rank rather than an arbitrary hand-picked case. The claims are finite/exact and asymptotic; no unproved practical testing-power improvement is asserted.

## Limitations
The uniform Dirichlet model, interior-fraction asymptotics, possible implicit general mixed-moment coverage, and incomplete historic priority are explicit limits. Numerical experiments and checks do not establish uniqueness against unsearched literature.
