Conditioning a uniform involution on exactly \(k\) fixed points gives every pair of nonoverlapping descent indicators the same covariance, with a single sign transition at \(k\asymp 2^{-1/3}n^{2/3}\). The transition has explicit \(n^{1/3}\) and constant corrections; the fixed-point-free specialization is classical, while the rare-conditioning phase boundary is the contribution.
