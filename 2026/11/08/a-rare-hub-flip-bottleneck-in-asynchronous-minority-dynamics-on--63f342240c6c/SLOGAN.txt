On the friendship graph of \(m\) triangles, self-inclusive asynchronous minority dynamics has one \(2\cdot3^m\)-state recurrent class, yet hub switches occur at stationary rate only \((2/3)^m/(2m+1)\). This gives an explicit exponential lower bound \(t_{\mathrm{mix}}(1/4)\ge\lceil (2m+1)(3/2)^m/4\rceil\) even after the dynamics has entered its recurrent class.
