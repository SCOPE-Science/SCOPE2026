For three-species sampling, equalizing the abundances of the two common species always hastens full detection, but it *increases* the variance of detection time when the rare species has frequency below the unique root \(a_*\approx0.1479022673\) of \(3a^5-12a^4+56a^3-38a^2+45a-6=0\). The variance response switches sign sharply at that frequency, where its first nonzero imbalance term is positive and quartic; the effect is impossible with fewer than three species.
