For two integer expanding circle maps chosen independently with a horizon-dependent, exponentially rare slow-branch probability, the stationary Poisson-kernel autocovariance is governed by an infinite staircase of optimal counts of faster-branch visits. Each transition has a universal \((\log n)/a^n\) shift and an explicit two-itinerary crossover factor \(1+e^{\xi}/(j+1)\), rather than a sharp switch at the unshifted exponential-cost equality.
