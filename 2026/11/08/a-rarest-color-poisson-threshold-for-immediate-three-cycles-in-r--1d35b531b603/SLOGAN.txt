For three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on an independently colored Erdős–Rényi graph, the precise onset of three-periodic motion from time zero is controlled by the rarest initial color: at \(p=(\log n+t)/(n\min_i\pi_i)\), the number of vertices lacking a successor-colored neighbor converges to a Poisson law of mean \(e^{-t}\sum_{i:\pi_{i+1}=\min_j\pi_j}\pi_i\). The graph is connected with high probability in this window, so the probability of an immediate primitive three-cycle tends to the corresponding Poisson zero-mass.
