The natural density of integers whose unitary-divisor sum exceeds their exponential-divisor sum is rigorously greater than \(77\%\). An exhaustive ten-prime valuation certificate and an elementary reciprocal-square tail bound yield \(\delta\ge 397N/(400Q)=0.770292125386622\ldots\), improving the previous published \(0.753659844\ldots\) lower bound without claiming to determine the exact density.
