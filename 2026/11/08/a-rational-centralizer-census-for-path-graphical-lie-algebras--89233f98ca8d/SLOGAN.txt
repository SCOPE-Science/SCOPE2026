Distinct element-centralizer subalgebras of the finite-field two-step Lie algebra of a path have a universal rational length-generating function, \(\frac{1+(1-q)u+qu^2-qu^3+u^4}{1-qu+(q-2)u^2+u^4-u^5}\). A seven-state deterministic automaton counts kernels without confusing equality of centralizers with equality of their dimensions.
