The Gaussian cross-covariances of every fixed pair of outdegree counts in two uniformly grown trees with independently sprinkled attachment alignment have a single rational bivariate generating function, interpolating exactly between independence and Janson's one-tree covariance series. In particular the outdegree-one cross covariance is \(\alpha(1+\alpha)(5\alpha^2-18\alpha+297)/[432(3-\alpha)^3]\), strictly increasing over \(0\leq\alpha\leq1\).
