For every labeled cycle, paired-disjunctive dominating vertex sets have an exact rational cardinality generating function: their cyclic words consist of even selected runs separated by one to three omitted vertices, apart from the even full cycle. The resulting complete census has a five-residue formula for the number of minimum sets and total count asymptotic to \(\rho^{-n}\), where \(\rho^2+\rho^3+\rho^4+\rho^5=1\), while the minimum cardinality itself was previously known.
