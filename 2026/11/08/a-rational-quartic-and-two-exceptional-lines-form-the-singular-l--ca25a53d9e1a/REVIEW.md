# Review of the reduced singular locus of the exponent-seven swept threefold

## Correctness — PASS

The two-point determinant and infinity chart establish that the projective-plane fibers are pairwise disjoint. The scroll map from a smooth projective bundle is thus a finite universally injective normalization. The Stacks Project's proper-unramified-universally-injective criterion identifies its noncritical image with the smooth locus of the reduced target. The proof gives exact critical ideals on two charts covering the base and explicitly proves that both are reduced. The standalone exact symbolic checker reproduces all critical algebraic identities. No finite experiment is substituted for a general argument.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualification

The closest source is Li–Li–Russo, arXiv:2610.38184v1: it constructs exactly this model, computes its determinant, and determines normalization and degree. It treats singular loci explicitly for rank-two swept surfaces, not for this rank-three example. Perkinson's older differential-rank theory supplies general conceptual tools but no matching explicit component statement was identified in the accessible text. Published mathematical research-record searches with descriptions of the same frame, singular-locus formulation, and equivalent normalization-critical formulation found only unrelated singular varieties. Implicit prior coverage in older scroll work remains possible and was not ruled out by an exhaustive historical survey.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The first simple rank-three, exponent-seven Grassmannian curve gives a natural and geometrically motivated threefold. Its reduced singular scheme has three components rather than the single singular curve of the rank-two tangent-developable prototype: a rational normal quartic and two exceptional lines with a specific incidence pattern. This supplies previously unspecified local-geometry input for the concrete model and distinguishes boundary degenerations not visible from degree or diagonal exponent.

## Boundaries

The result is about this exact swept threefold, its *reduced* singular locus and the critical locus of normalization. It does not compute the embedded Jacobian ideal, analytic singularity types, or conductor multiplicities. Independent verification and comprehensive historical priority review remain outstanding.
