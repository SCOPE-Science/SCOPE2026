Uniform-frontier-edge growth rooted in the two-vertex part of \(K_{2,b}\) has an exact root-degree distribution and a non-Gaussian \((R+Z)/2\) scaling limit, with independent Rayleigh \(R\) and standard Gaussian \(Z\). This growth law stochastically dominates the corresponding uniform-spanning-tree root degree, with an expectation gap asymptotic to \(\sqrt{\pi b/8}\).
