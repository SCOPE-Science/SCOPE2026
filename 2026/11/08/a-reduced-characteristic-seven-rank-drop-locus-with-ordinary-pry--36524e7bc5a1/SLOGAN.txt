The genus-four cyclic trigonal family \(y^3=x^5+tx^3+x\) in characteristic seven has an everywhere-ordinary Prym, even where its genus-two quotient becomes superspecial. The rank-at-most-two Cartier degeneracy scheme is the reduced pair \(t^2+2=0\), representing one geometric isomorphism class, while the full Hasse determinant vanishes to order two at both points.
