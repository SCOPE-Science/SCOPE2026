# Scientific review: reduced superspecial locus of a genus-four trigonal family

## Correctness — PASS
The characteristic assumption \(p\equiv5\pmod6\) makes \(q=(p-2)/3\) odd and \(m=2q+1<p\). The six finite branch points of \(y^3=x^6+t x^3+1\) are simple exactly when \(t^2\ne4\), and the three infinity points are unramified, giving genus four. The explicit four-differential basis is regular. Cartier extraction gives just the two arrows with coefficients \(H_m(t)^{1/p}\) and \(H_q(t)^{1/p}\), with the other two basis vectors killed. The monic central-trinomial polynomial ODE and its unique coefficient recurrence prove \(H_m=(T^2-4)^{(q+1)/2}H_q\) in characteristic \(p\). Thus both arrows vanish together on the smooth locus, and the surviving two-cycle is semilinearly bijective, proving the asserted stable \(p\)-ranks rather than confusing them with one-step Cartier ranks. The ODE, the nonvanishing at \(T=\pm2\), and the nonzero \(q\)-th derivative prove that the degree-\(q\) polynomial has exactly \(q\) simple roots on the smooth base. This is an infinite-prime argument, not an extrapolation from finite tests.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Borges and Gonçalves (arXiv:2212.03987, first public 2022-12-07) establish formulas for fixed Fermat-type curves \(y^m=x^n+1\), including the distinguished central specialization. Kudo (arXiv:1804.09063) exhibits a characteristic-dependent superspecial genus-four curve, rather than enumerating the moving parameters of the displayed family. Montanucci and Speziali (arXiv:1603.03604) analyze \(a\)-numbers for fixed Fermat and Hurwitz curves. Their inspected statements and pertinent sections do not imply the complete rank-stratification and degree-\(q\) reduced superspecial locus for this deformation. Searches using the exact equation, cyclic-trigonal terminology, Cartier/Hasse invariants, and relevant broader families revealed no dominating published theorem. The coefficient extraction, ODE and superspecial equivalence are established methods, not claimed as independently new. A differently normalized or unindexed general theorem of cyclic Kummer covers could still implicitly contain the result; independent priority verification remains necessary.

## Value — PASS
The precise cardinality \((p-2)/3\) of the reduced superspecial parameter locus in infinitely many characteristics is a natural finite-intersection invariant, and the rank dichotomy excludes five otherwise conceivable arithmetic strata in a family of genus-four curves with extra automorphisms. The coincidence of the two Cartier vanishing conditions is governed by a structural central-trinomial polynomial identity, not a single small-prime search. Complete arithmetic stratification and transverse reducedness in a motivated family can be mathematically useful even without a downstream theorem or isomorphism-class census.
