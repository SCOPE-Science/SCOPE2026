For every prime \(p\equiv5\pmod6\), the smooth genus-four deformation \(y^3=x^6+t x^3+1\) has exactly \((p-2)/3\) distinct reduced superspecial coefficient parameters, given by a central trinomial polynomial. Every other smooth member has Jacobian \(p\)-rank two and \(a\)-number two; no intermediate arithmetic strata occur.
