# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The complement on even coordinates gives a genuine Euclidean isometry of the zigzag order and chain polytopes. The path independent-set recurrence exactly evaluates every Gaussian support value. The coupled sign-reset chain is uniquely stationary, admits iid regeneration blocks of mean length four, and has sufficient moment bounds for a bivariate renewal CLT. A finite-dimensional Gaussian integration-by-parts identity and exact Gaussian radial independence justify the correction \(\sigma^2=\tau^2-2c^2\) and convergence of variance, not only weak convergence. Nonconstant two-step cycle rewards prove strict positive variance. The independent checker passed the stated tests.

## Originality

PASS with historical-priority risk. Coons–Sullivant (arxiv:1901.07443) give zigzag Ehrhart shellings; Petersen–Zhuang (arxiv:2403.07181) give zigzag Eulerian recurrences. Gamarnik–Nowicki–Świrszcz (arxiv:math/0309441) give existing random independent-set fixed-point/expectation technology, and Foss–Konstantopoulos–Logachov–Mogulski (arxiv:2011.09981) prove renewal limits in a different independent edge-weight maximal-path model. Kabluchko–Litvak–Zaporozhets (arxiv:1511.08479) handle regular-polytope widths. Neither the elementary dynamic program nor the general regenerative CLT is proposed as original. Checked implications do not cover the specific normalized zigzag Gaussian width law and its positive cycle-variance constant. A parallel result in path-optimization probability literature remains possible.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The finding identifies a natural non-regular family of high-dimensional metric polytopes, exact finite-size expected-width control and a nontrivial Gaussian projection-width fluctuation constant, distinguishing radial fluctuation from the Gaussian numerator. The positive variance has an explicit reproducible renewal characterization. No unsupported wider universality or elementary numerical evaluation is claimed.

## Limitations

One-dimensional Haar projections only, alternating zigzag path posets and ambient Euclidean metric. A closed-form elementary variance expression and a uniform quantitative CLT error bound are not established. Numerical approximations are illustrative. Historical originality and mathematical correctness have not received independent audit.
