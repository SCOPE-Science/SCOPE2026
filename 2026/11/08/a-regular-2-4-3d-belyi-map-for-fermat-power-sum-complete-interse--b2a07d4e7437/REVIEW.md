# Review of the explicit regular Belyi quotient

## Correctness — PASS
The map is the quotient of the smooth complete-intersection curve \(\sum x_i^d=\sum x_i^{2d}=0\) by its diagonal \((\mu_d)^3\) symmetry followed by the permutation \(\mathfrak S_4\) symmetry of the resulting conic. The coordinate-powering map has degree \(d^3\), and the conic quotient has degree \(24\). On the conic, \(e_3\), \(e_4\), and the quartic discriminant have disjoint reduced zero divisors of degrees \(6,8,12\). Their associated indices are \(4,3,2\); the coordinate cover increases the pole index to \(3d\). Riemann–Hurwitz reproduces adjunction and there is no unaccounted ramification. The full deck-group identification uses Theorem 3.6 of Duque Franco–Liendo–Palomino in its applicable degree and smoothness range. ## Originality — PASS, qualified
Duque Franco–Liendo–Palomino explicitly prove the complete-intersection automorphism group, not this Belyi function, its geometric branch divisor, or its regular-dessin passport. Kontogeorgis (2002) treats Fermat-like automorphism groups without a corresponding Belyi formula in its inspected main sections. The classical octahedral quotient and quartic discriminant are used as known inputs, not represented as discoveries. Searches for aliases, exact branch signature, Fermat power-sum equations, and group extensions found no equivalent published result in the searched material. The older Terasoma (1988) full text could not be inspected; a stronger historical equivalence in classical quasiplatonic-curve literature remains a risk. A search miss is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The explicit uniform, full-automorphism regular Belyi quotient turns a recently studied geometric family into an infinite family of three-point covers with exact degree, genus, monodromy group, geometric passport and branch-point formulas. Neither knowing only that a finite group acts nor an abstract quotient curve supplies the branch signature: the interaction of coordinate ramification with permutation ramification determines the non-obvious \(3d\) entry and sharp all-degree form. It is a natural moduli/dessin invariant, not an arbitrary small numerical slice.
