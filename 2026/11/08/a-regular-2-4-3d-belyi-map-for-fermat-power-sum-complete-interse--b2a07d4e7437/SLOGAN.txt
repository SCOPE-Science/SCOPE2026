For each integer \(d\ge3\) with \(d\ne4\), the smooth Fermat power-sum complete-intersection curve \(\sum x_i^d=\sum x_i^{2d}=0\) has an explicit regular Belyi quotient \(27e_3(x_i^d)^4/(256e_4(x_i^d)^3)\) of passport \((2,4,3d)\), degree \(24d^3\), and deck group equal to its full automorphism group \((\mathbf Z/d\mathbf Z)^3\rtimes\mathfrak S_4\). These give an infinite quasiplatonic family with genus \(1+d^2(3d-4)\).
