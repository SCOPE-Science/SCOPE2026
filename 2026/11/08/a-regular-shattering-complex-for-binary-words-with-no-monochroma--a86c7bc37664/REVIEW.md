# Scientific review: no-monochromatic-triple shattering

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exact theorem is about the finite language avoiding both \(000\) and \(111\), with independent completion allowed for every query labeling. The necessity proof explicitly constructs an impossible labeling for either obstruction: three consecutive queried coordinates, or two queried adjacent pairs connected through isolated queries. The sufficiency proof reduces along two consecutive free coordinates, uses an explicit one-double completion, and joins fragments through a two-or-more-position bridge. All cases include empty and short words. A six-state automaton gives the rational generating function; a run-count argument gives the exact maximal support size. The standalone checker exhaustively compares the forbidden-factor criterion with direct trace enumeration through length 15 and verifies the polynomial coefficients independently.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual historical-priority risk.** The key quantifier is existential completion separately for *each* label assignment. Louidor–Marcus–Pavlov's multi-choice subshift instead requires that every choice on free positions work with a fixed outside word, a strictly stronger condition. Johnson's VC dimension for an individual binary word is generated by its substrings, not by the full forbidden-word language. Chornomaz treats general shattering-extremal systems rather than the exact coefficientwise profile of this particular constrained language. Targeted published-source searches and semantic-database searches did not reveal the same forbidden-support theorem or rational function. The possibility of an earlier equivalent result in constrained coding or symbolic dynamics has not been eliminated by independent historical verification.

## Value

**PASS.** A natural shift-of-finite-type constraint has a complete, explicit shattered-support complex despite a nontrivial infinite family of minimal forbidden support factors. The rational enumerator gives every coefficient for every length, while the sharp \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) VC law exposes a linear gap from fixed-background independence (\(\lceil n/3\rceil\)). This matters for coordinate-puncturing freedom with label-dependent versus predetermined parity positions, and for distinguishing notions of symbolic independence. The result is not merely a finite census, a rephrasing of the standard run-length count, or a generic regular-language decidability statement.
