The coordinate-shattered supports of binary words avoiding \(000\) and \(111\) are exactly the indicator words with no \(111\) and no two \(11\) factors uninterrupted by \(00\). Their full counting series is \(\bigl(1+tz-t^2z+t^2z^2\bigr)/\bigl(1-t-2t^2z+t^3z\bigr)\), giving VC dimension \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) for every positive length, compared with only \(\lceil n/3\rceil\) positions possible under one fixed background.
