The minimum uniform projection norms for the real axial first Fourier shell on ternary and quaternary product groups and on continuous tori share the leading order \(2\sqrt{d/\pi}\), but have three different exact second-order laws. In particular, the ternary-to-continuous norm difference switches sign with \(d\bmod3\), with sharp rescaled limits \(-17/24\) and \(7/24\), while the quaternary-to-continuous difference has limit \(-3/8\).
