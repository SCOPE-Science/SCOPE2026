A single diagonal rank-one defect of the unilateral shift has an exact numerical-range boundary transition at strength \(1/2\). The defect’s finite compressions cross the essential numerical-range edge exactly at \((n+1)/(2n)\), and a universal Robin transcendental profile describes their \(n^{-2}\) radial and \(n^{-1/2}\) angular critical window, with a sharp exponential limit above threshold.
