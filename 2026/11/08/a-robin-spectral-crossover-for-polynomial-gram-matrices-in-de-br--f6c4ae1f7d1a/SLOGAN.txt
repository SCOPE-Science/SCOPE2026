The monomial Gram matrices of \(\mathcal H(c(1+z)/2)\) have an exact reflected-AR(1) form and determinant, with a sharp change of conditioning at \(1-c^2\asymp n^{-2}\). At that scale their entire fixed-index upper spectrum converges to the Dirichlet–Robin Green-operator spectrum, whose principal eigenvalue gives the exact condition-number crossover.
