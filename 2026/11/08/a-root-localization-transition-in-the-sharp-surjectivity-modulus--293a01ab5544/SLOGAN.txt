For a rooted tree with one child at the root and four children at every later vertex, the exact sharp surjectivity modulus of the backward sum shift has a localized-root branch up to \(r=6/7\) and a bulk branch \(2-r\) thereafter. Surprisingly, its optimal surjectivity margin initially increases from \(1\) to \(2/\sqrt3\) at \(r=\sqrt{2/3}\), even though the spectrum is the disk of radius \(2\).
