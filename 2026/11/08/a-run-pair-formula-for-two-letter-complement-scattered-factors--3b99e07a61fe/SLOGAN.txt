For every finite word and any two distinct letters \(a,b\), the number of distinct remainders after deleting a scattered \(ab\) equals the number of ordered \(a\)-run/\(b\)-run pairs minus the number of contiguous \(abab\) factors. All collisions arise from local \(a^r b a b^s\) swaps, giving a linear-time count for every alphabet and input word.
