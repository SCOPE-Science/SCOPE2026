A bounded continuous scalar filter \(F\) with \(F(0)=1\) preserves entrywise nonnegativity on every complete multipartite graph heat system for every step size precisely when \(0\le F\le1\) and \((1-F(x))/x\) is nonincreasing. Thus the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre Padé end-step is strictly positive and stochastic on every connected complete multipartite graph at all positive step sizes, despite failing positivity on a four-vertex path.
