For a uniform random mapping, the covariance between total component count and the number of components of size \(r\) changes sign at a unique size cutoff. The cutoff is \((1-e^{-2})n+\frac23\sqrt{\pi/2}\,e^{-2}(e-1)\sqrt n+o(\sqrt n)\): the nonzero second-order displacement distinguishes this dependence transition from the usual largest-component scale.
