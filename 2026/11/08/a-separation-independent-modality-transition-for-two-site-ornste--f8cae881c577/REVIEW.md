# Scientific assessment: symmetric two-site Ornstein–Uhlenbeck resetting

**Correctness: PASS.** The renewal integral is a strictly positive normalized invariant density; Poisson coupling ensures uniqueness. The distributional stationary equation gives the exact integrating-factor derivative identity, which fixes the sign of the derivative throughout the source-free interval. Independently, positivity of every Gaussian derivative outside the extreme reset site fixes the exterior derivative sign. At the critical rate the ODE and source jump produce the normalized piecewise complementary-Gaussian density. Equation (4) rigorously proves strict monotonicity and both limits of plateau mass. A standalone finite numerical checker returned 291 assertions; the infinite theorem uses the analytical steps, not numerical extrapolation.

**Originality: PASS, qualified.** Olsen, arXiv:2310.04840, is particularly close because it gives the exact stationary renewal equation and all moments for arbitrary OU reset distributions. Its analyzed full text develops moment calculations and a Gaussian-reset example; it does not assert the symmetric two-atom critical shape classification. Trajanovski et al., arXiv:2301.12304, analyze OU resetting to one point and generalizations, but not two competing sites. Search comparisons across aliases including distributed resetting, OU multimodality, symmetric two-site reset points, and Green resolvents, as well as published research-record semantic search, found no direct covering statement. A general unpublished or unindexed one-dimensional Green-function mode theorem could imply this result, and an external priority review remains appropriate.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** Mode count is an interpretable qualitative invariant of a nonequilibrium stationary law. The exact flat interval at a single rate, with a threshold independent of both noise scale and reset-point separation, resolves a sharp global modality phase diagram that is not specified by moment formulas. The critical plateau mass also gives an explicit experimentally meaningful concentration statistic; the fact is not an arbitrary parameter slice or a moment substitution.

**Limits.** The theorem is confined to one-dimensional OU dynamics, positive parameters, independent Poisson reset times, and equally weighted symmetric point sources. The finite diagnostics are not exhaustive. Publication priority and independent validation remain unverified.
