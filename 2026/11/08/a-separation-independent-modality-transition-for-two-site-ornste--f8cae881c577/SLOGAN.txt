For an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle reset equally to \(\pm b\), the stationary density has a unique central mode for \(r<\kappa\), an exact flat plateau on \([-b,b]\) for \(r=\kappa\), and precisely two modes at the reset sites for \(r>\kappa\), independently of separation and diffusivity. At the critical resetting rate, the entire stationary density and the strictly increasing plateau mass are explicit.
