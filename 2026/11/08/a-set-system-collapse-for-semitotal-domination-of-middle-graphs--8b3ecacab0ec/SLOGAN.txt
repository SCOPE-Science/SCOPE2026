Every dominating set of the middle graph of a connected simple graph on at least three vertices is automatically semitotal dominating, and an explicit edge-subset formula counts every such set by cardinality. The minimum semitotal dominating sets of middle graphs of paths and cycles are completely classified: their counts are \(1\) or \(n\) for paths and \(2\) or \(2n\) for cycles, according to parity.
