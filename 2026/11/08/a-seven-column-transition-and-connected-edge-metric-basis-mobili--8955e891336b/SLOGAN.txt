The complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\) has edge metric dimension \(n\) through \(n=6\), dropping to \(n-1\) at \(n=7\). From that threshold onward every basis omits one matching column and has at least three landmarks in each clique layer, giving exactly \(n\sum_{k=3}^{n-4}\binom{n-1}{k}\) bases; all are mutually reachable by single-edge landmark slides.
