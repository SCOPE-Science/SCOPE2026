Partial alternating-sign-matrix polytopes and the much smaller partial-permutation matching polytopes share the exact Frobenius inradius \(1/(\max\{m,n\}+\sqrt{\max\{m,n\}})\). The matching incenter is unique, but the partial-ASM incenter set has dimension \(mn-\max\{m,n\}\) off the square and \((n-1)^2\) on the square, with a complete classification of its forced entries and tangent facets.
