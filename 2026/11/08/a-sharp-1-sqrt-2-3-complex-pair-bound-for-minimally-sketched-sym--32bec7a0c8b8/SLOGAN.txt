For every real symmetric \(3\times3\) matrix and every fixed two-plane, a two-row Gaussian sketched Rayleigh–Ritz compression produces a nonreal conjugate pair with probability at most \(1-\sqrt{2/3}\). Equality is attainable by positive-definite matrices exactly when the two residual couplings to the plane's normal have magnitudes equal to the ordinary Ritz spectral gap.
