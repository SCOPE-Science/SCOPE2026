The Fourier projection onto the union of the two opposite parabolas in \(\mathbb F_p^2\) has exact finite-prime norm determined by a one-dimensional signed Gauss sum and sharp asymptotic \((4/\pi)\sqrt p\). Relative to projection onto one parabola, the endpoint amplification tends to the universal factor \(4/\pi\); the indicator's Fourier ratio is \((2\sqrt2/\pi)p+O(\log p)\).
