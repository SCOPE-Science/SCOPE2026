For the reflected five-site weighted diffusion chain with edge conductances \((1,t,t,1)\), the largest positivity-preserving Crank–Nicolson step changes its controlling diagonal at the exact ratio \(t=3/4\): shoulders control below it, and the center controls above it. The weak-coupling horizon diverges sharply as \(2/\sqrt{t}-\sqrt{t}+O(t)\), even though every positive \(t\) gives a finite cutoff.
