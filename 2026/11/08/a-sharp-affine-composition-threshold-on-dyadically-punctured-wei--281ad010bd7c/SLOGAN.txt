For the affine boundary-contact maps \(\varphi_t(z)=1-t+tz\), \(0<t<1\), on Hardy spaces whose dyadic coefficient weights are \(\beta_{2^k}=2^{-\alpha k}\) and all other weights are one, composition is bounded exactly when \(\alpha\le1/2\). Its lacunary-input restriction is in every Schatten class below the threshold and, at equality, is a scaled isometry modulo trace class with exact essential norm \([4\pi t(1-t)]^{-1/4}\).
