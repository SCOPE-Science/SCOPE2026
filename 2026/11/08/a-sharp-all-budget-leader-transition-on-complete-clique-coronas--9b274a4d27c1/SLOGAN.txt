For every complete-clique one-leaf corona \(K_n\circ K_1\), optimal noise-free leader locations are determined at every budget: below \(n\), use either one core and the remaining leaders on other pairs' leaves or use leaves only, separated by the sharp sign of \(nk^2+(2n^2+3n+2)k-2n(n+1)^2\). At budgets at least \(n\), select every leaf and any additional cores, while the core-abandonment transition occurs near \((\sqrt3-1)n\).
