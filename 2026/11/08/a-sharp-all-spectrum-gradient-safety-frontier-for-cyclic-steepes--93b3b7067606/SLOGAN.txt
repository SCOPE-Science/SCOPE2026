A cycle of \(m\ge2\) Cauchy-initialized repeated gradient steps is uniformly gradient-norm nonexpansive on every finite-dimensional positive-definite quadratic exactly up to a unique sharp condition number \(\kappa_m\). The worst case always lives on the extreme two eigenspaces, and the thresholds decrease to \(2\) with \(\kappa_m-2\sim[\log m+\log(4e)]/(2m)\).
