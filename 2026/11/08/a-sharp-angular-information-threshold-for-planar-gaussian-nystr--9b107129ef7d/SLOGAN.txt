For one-column Gaussian Nyström in two dimensions, the expected trace error has an exact covariance-dependent trace-and-determinant formula. If the dominant eigendirection is known only within angle \(\vartheta\), the optimal covariance design switches sharply at \(\vartheta_c=\arctan((a/b)^{1/4})\): below it extreme anisotropy is best in the limiting sense; above it isotropy is uniquely minimax. The near-perfect alignment risk has competing \(r^{-1/2}\) and \(\theta^2\) error scales.
