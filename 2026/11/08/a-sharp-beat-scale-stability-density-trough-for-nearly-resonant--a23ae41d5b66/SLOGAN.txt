For two nearly resonant rotating modes under a common zero-order-hold negative state feedback, the fraction of stabilizing sampling periods has an exact beat-scale limit with a unique global trough \((\sqrt{10}-3)/4\) at rescaled horizon \(\pi\sqrt{10}/2\). This fraction drops below both the resonant density \(1/4\) and the small-detuning irrational long-horizon density \(1/16\), quantifying a sharp noncommutation of time and resonance limits.
