For a Bell pair repeatedly reinitialized at Poisson times while undergoing symmetric independent amplitude damping and dephasing, stationary CHSH violation begins at exactly one positive reset rate, with a sharp switch between two optimal Bell-correlation mechanisms. When dephasing dominates, this onset scales as \(r_{\mathrm{CHSH}}\sim4\gamma^{1/3}\kappa^{2/3}\), parametrically faster than the \(\kappa^{1/2}\) entanglement-onset scale.
