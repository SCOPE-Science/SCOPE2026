Adding one edge inside the size-\(a\) part of \(K_{a,b}\) increases the expected leaf count of a uniform spanning tree exactly when \(b\ge a\); it decreases the count for \(b<a\), apart from the single equality case \((a,b)=(2,1)\). The result follows from an exact weighted spanning-tree perturbation polynomial, giving a sharp and nonmonotone edge-addition law across all part sizes.
