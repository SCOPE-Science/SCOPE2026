Binary words with exactly two ones have a complete, explicit longest-border spectrum: their longest proper border is governed by which of the three zero gaps is largest, with sharp maximum \(\lfloor(2n-1)/3\rfloor\). Under uniform fixed-weight sampling, the normalized longest-border length has a nontrivial piecewise-linear density, an exact rational bivariate enumerator, and limiting mean \(7/27\), sharply contrasting unrestricted random words.
