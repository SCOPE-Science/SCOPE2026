The unilateral weighted shift with weights \((t,u,b,a,b,a,\ldots)\) has an exactly determined open-to-closed numerical-range transition along \(t^2+(1+b/a)u^2=(a+b)^2\). Above this boundary the radius is an explicit root of a quadratic equation and grows quadratically from the two-periodic bulk edge; at and below it the extremal boundary is not attained.
