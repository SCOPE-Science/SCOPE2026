In a Kendall-Mallows random permutation, two neighboring fixed points at the first two positions switch from attraction to repulsion at the unique bias \(q>1\) satisfying \(q^{n-1}=1+q\), while the first and last fixed points remain positively correlated. In the weak-bias regime \(q=e^{\beta/n}\), the sharp transition is \(\beta=\log2\), with an explicit fourth-order critical crossover.
