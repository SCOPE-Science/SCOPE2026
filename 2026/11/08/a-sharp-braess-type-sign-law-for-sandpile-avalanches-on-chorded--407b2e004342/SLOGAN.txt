For a uniformly recurrent one-sink Abelian sandpile on \(C_n\), adding a nonsink chord \(\{i,j\}\) changes the mean toppling count by exactly \(\frac{(j-i)^2(n-i-j)}{2[n+(j-i)(n-j+i)]}\), so its sign is determined by \(n-i-j\). A chord incident to the injection site can almost double this mean as \(n\to\infty\), despite introducing an extra edge.
