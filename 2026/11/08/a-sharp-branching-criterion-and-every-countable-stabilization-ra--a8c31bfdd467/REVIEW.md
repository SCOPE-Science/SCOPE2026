# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The upward Alexandrov topology has least neighbourhood T_s, so Ω_T(A) is exactly the nodes with infinitely many marked descendants. Ω_T(A) is prefix-closed and Ω²_T(A)⊆Ω_T(A) even for arbitrary A, because any descendant in Ω_T(A) witnesses infinity within the ancestral cone. For finitely branching T, an infinite A∩T_s cannot be exhausted by finitely many finite child cones; iterated finite pigeonholing gives an infinite chain of Ω_T(A) nodes below s, hence Ω_T(A)⊆Ω²_T(A). Conversely an infinitely branching node s and A equal to its infinitely many immediate children produce Ω_T(A)=the finite set of ancestors of s, so Ω²_T(A)=∅ and idempotence fails. For the ordinal claim, prefix-closed A implies Ω_X(A)⊆A; successor operations and limit intersections preserve locality within each child cone. Recursively attached B_β copies under infinitely many siblings prove stage-α root survival and stage-(α+1) extinction: at α=β+1 all sibling copies end exactly when the parent survives; at countable nonzero limit α, a cofinal sequence β_n makes infinitely many child roots survive at every preceding stage, but none at α. No infinite branch arises in B_α because any branch enters one well-founded copy. For λ a nonzero countable limit, a perpetual 0-spine and side copies B_{α_n} for cofinal α_n<λ leave the spine at exactly stage λ and it is already Ω-fixed; a side root remains at every earlier stage, ruling out earlier stabilization. The empty valuation gives rank 0; if all countable successor steps strictly decreased, they would remove uncountably many distinct nodes from the countable carrier, impossible. These arguments cover every α<ω₁, with supremum ω₁ unattained. The finite-rank and infinite fan examples were separately reconstructed by hand; no bounded test was substituted for the transfinite induction.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge originality of the **joint exact prefix-tree statement**, not of ω-accumulation, König's lemma, all-countable classical tree ranks, or the generic Cantor–Bendixson decomposition. The full 2026 Montacute primary paper supplies the ω-accumulation semantics, closedness, universal Ω²⊆Ω and existence of a transfinite perfect kernel, but not this global finite-branching iff or the full valuation-dependent ordinal spectrum on one *fixed* countable prefix space. Published Resultary 2026-11-02 ordinal-chain normal form realizes each countable rank by varying the underlying ordinal carrier X_θ, with downward-open initial segments, not all ranks in one carrier with upward prefix cones. Clote 1985 defines a different height-based derivative D(T)={s:ht(s)>ω} for recursive trees, whereas the present Ω removes finite descendant cones; an infinitely many-terminal-child root separates them. Idziaszek–Skrzypczak–Bojańczyk 2015 use derivatives based on finitely many branches or single-branch cores of finite-alphabet forests, not the same infinite-node-cone threshold. These checks do not establish exhaustive priority: a historical tree-pruning formulation could still directly cover the construction, and basic pieces admit classical reductions.

## value

PASS

The natural question is how an ω-accumulation modality on prefix-order Alexandrov spaces behaves when changing only the tree branching: exactly finite branching forces immediate idempotence for **every** valuation, while the single canonical countable infinitely branching carrier admits all countable stabilization ordinals for prefix-closed valuations. The sharp quantified contrast is a motivated structural classification and an exact maximal rank spectrum, not a parameter relabeling, arbitrary numerical slice or code-only exercise. The necessary finite-fan witness and the cofinal-copy and spine constructions identify precise mechanisms. Standard König/tree-rank ingredients reduce conceptual novelty, so the value is a self-contained specialist lemma/classification rather than a new general ordinal theory.
