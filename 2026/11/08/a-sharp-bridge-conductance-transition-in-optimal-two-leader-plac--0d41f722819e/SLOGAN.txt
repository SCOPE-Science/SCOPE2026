On two unequal unit-edge cliques linked by one bridge of conductance \(t\), the exact best two noise-free leaders switch at \(t=p^2(q+2)/(4(q-p))\): below it one internal vertex in each clique is optimal, while above it the larger clique's bridge gateway replaces its internal leader. The boundary produces exactly two optimal placement types, and equal-sized cliques never switch.
