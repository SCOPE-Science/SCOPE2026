In a book of \(m\ge2\) parallel three-edge routes sharing one direct hub-to-hub bridge, the unique resistance-optimal budget allocation eliminates the bridge exactly when its unit price reaches an explicit critical value \(d_m^*\). The critical price is independent of budget and satisfies \(d_m^*\sim(3+3\sqrt3/2)/m\), while every non-bridge edge class remains strictly funded.
