## Scientific limitations

The statement is a stylized three-patch continuous-time binary-occupancy model. Positive-rate connected-network restrictions exclude the attained concentrated boundary; then the optimal value for small budgets is approached as one rate tends to zero. Nothing is claimed for population sizes, nonlinear density dependence, fixed-horizon survival probabilities, higher graph orders, or field efficacy. The full 2001 and 2010 management articles could not be obtained through available access, although an earlier full conference exposition was inspected; priority remains provisional.
