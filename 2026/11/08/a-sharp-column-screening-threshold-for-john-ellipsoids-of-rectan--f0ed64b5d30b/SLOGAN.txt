For a rectangular partial-permutation (complete-bipartite matching) polytope with \(m\) rows and \(n\) columns, its John ellipsoid equals the exactly computed row-simplex-product ellipsoid if and only if \(n+1\ge m+\sqrt m\). The column facets join the tangent set exactly at the arithmetic boundary \((m,n)=(s^2,s^2+s-1)\), while all coordinate and row facets always touch throughout this regime.
