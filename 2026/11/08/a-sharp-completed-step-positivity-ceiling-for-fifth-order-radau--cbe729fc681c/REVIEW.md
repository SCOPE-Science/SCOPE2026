## Value — PASS
This gives a sharp timestep/exitrate rule for a standard fifth-order stiff solver on **any** finite directed Markov generator, rather than a numerical cutoff on one graph. The all-timestep converse makes the rate threshold operationally sharp even in arbitrarily high dimension. The three-state reentrant result explains why testing a small network at a large step can falsely suggest an unconditional method-wide positivity guarantee. The concrete residue inequality, rather than a mere parameter substitution, closes the gap between the known general pole upper bound and an attained positivity radius for the canonical Radau scheme.
