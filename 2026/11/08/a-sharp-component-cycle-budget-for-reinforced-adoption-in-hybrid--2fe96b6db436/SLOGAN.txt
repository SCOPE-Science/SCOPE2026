Each reinforced adoption requires extra pre-existing active connectivity, and a finite network can supply that resource only by merging initial seed components or consuming independent cycles. Specifically, \(h\) nonseed adoptions requiring at least \(r\) neighbors obey \((r-1)h\le |S|-1+\beta(G)\), with equality on explicit connected fan networks for every \(r\ge2\); the result applies almost surely to positive-rate irreversible hybrid imitation-and-threshold contagion.
