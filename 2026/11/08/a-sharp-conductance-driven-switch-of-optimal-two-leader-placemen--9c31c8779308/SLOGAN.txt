For a complete bipartite network with one positive-weight edge inserted inside its smaller part, every optimal two-leader set uses an endpoint of the inserted edge. The second leader switches from the same part to the opposite part at a single explicit conductance threshold **only** for size regimes \(p=3,q\ge8\), \(p=4,q\ge13\), or \(p=5,q\ge31\); otherwise the same-part placement remains optimal for every positive conductance.
