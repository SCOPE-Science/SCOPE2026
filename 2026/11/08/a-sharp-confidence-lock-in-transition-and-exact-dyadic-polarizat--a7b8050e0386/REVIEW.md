# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The directed random-time update law is explicitly specified with strict inequalities and independent Poisson source clocks. The proof constructs the marked fair-bit embedding, demonstrates permanent absorption in the one-source region, and proves almost-sure finiteness of entry into that region. The exact bit-pattern exit criterion is checked at the crucial equality boundaries, including \(m=2\); the first-run recurrence and its special initial-condition correction give the expectation. The time-change uses the independent rate-\(2\lambda\) Poisson waiting times. The absence of almost-sure limits when both sources stay active is established separately from weak convergence to the uniform stationary distribution. The finite exact-rational checker supports the algebra but is not substituted for the general proof.

## Originality

PASS on inspected literature and available research-index comparisons. The initiating paper by Chu and Porter specifies the model family but does not state an exact absorbing-edge waiting-time law for two opposing stubborn sources. The always-active disagreement theory of Acemoglu et al. and the halfway Bernoulli stationary law of Cao and Cortez cover distinct components of this argument and are not claimed anew. Discrete-time adaptive-confidence models and a known deterministic seven-agent nonfreezing switching calculation do not imply this three-agent absorbing Poisson clock. The classical consecutive-run identity is also credited explicitly. The exact strict-boundary \(-3\) correction and inverse-gap lock-in delay are the asserted new combined claim. Publication priority cannot be proven by search alone; a broader unindexed stochastic-BCM treatment remains a residual risk.

## Value

PASS. A three-agent network is the minimal setting for unresolved competition between two fixed, opposed social influences. An exact expected time to irreversible loss of one influence is a directly interpretable social-dynamical observable, especially because it diverges near the confidence value at which the system stops polarizing and starts fluctuating. The special dyadic confidence levels are selected by the natural binary-halving update, not by arbitrary parameter slicing. The calculation resolves a full infinite family of exact exit times and a sharp critical scaling, not merely a finite simulation or a textbook run expectation in isolation.

## Closest literature and scope

The model uses Chu and Porter's multiple-renewal directed update with exponential times. Acemoglu et al. explain persistent random interactions with stubborn sources without confidence rejection; Cao and Cortez supply Bernoulli-convolution stationary analysis. The exact formula relies on symmetric clocks, midpoint averaging, a centered initial opinion, and a strict threshold. General graphs, unequal clock rates, and nondyadic exact waiting-time laws are not proved. Literature indexing is incomplete and independent expert assessment is pending.
