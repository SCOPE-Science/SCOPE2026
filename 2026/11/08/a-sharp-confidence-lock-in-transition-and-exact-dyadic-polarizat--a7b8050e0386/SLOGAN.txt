In a three-agent random-time opinion model with two fixed opposing influences, every confidence \(1/2<c<1\) eventually silences one source, but at \(c\ge1\) the central opinion fluctuates forever. At the dyadic bounds \(c=1-2^{-m}\), the exact expected time until permanent silencing is \((2^m-3)/(2\lambda)\), diverging at the critical confidence threshold.
