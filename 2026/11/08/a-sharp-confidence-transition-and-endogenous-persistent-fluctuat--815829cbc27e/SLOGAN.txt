For a pure neighborhood Deffuant–Weisbuch star with at least three leaves and both binary opinions present, every leaf stays permanently unreceptive exactly when the strict confidence radius is at most the smaller leaf-population fraction. Its randomly updating hub then has a biased Bernoulli-perpetuity stationary law, singular with full support at half compromise whenever the leaf populations are unequal. The known three-vertex threshold and classical perpetuity results are expressly excluded from novelty claims.
