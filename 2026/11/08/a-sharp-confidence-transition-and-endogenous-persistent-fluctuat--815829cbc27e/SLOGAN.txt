In pure neighborhood Deffuant–Weisbuch dynamics, two initially opposing leaves on a three-vertex path become endogenously unresponsive exactly when \(\varepsilon\le1/2\), leaving a forever-fluctuating center; for \(\varepsilon>1/2\), all three opinions converge to consensus. On binary stars, the frozen-leaf regime yields exact perpetuity moments and, at half compromise, singular biased-digit stationary measures whenever the leaf opinions are unbalanced.
