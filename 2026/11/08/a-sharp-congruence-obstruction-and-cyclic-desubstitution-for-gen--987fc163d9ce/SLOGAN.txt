For the generalized Mephisto–Waltz fixed points \(0\mapsto0^{b-1}1\), \(1\mapsto1^{b-1}0\) with \(b\ge3\), every rotation-complete factor of length \(n\ge b+2\) has \(b\mid n\). At surviving lengths \(bm\), a unique circular desubstitution gives an exact bijection with the length-\(m\) cyclic words whose periodic windows of length \(m+1\) all occur, and the congruence threshold is sharp.
