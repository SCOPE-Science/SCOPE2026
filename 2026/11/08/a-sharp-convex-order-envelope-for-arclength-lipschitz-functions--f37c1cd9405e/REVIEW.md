# Scientific review: sharp convex-order envelope for bridgeless metric graphs

## Correctness
**PASS.** The two-edge-disjoint-path lemma yields the exact lower mass density of every intermediate value interval, including edge interiors; inverse distribution estimates yield the quantile Lipschitz bound. The centered quantile differs from the proposed uniform quantile by a nonincreasing mean-zero function, which proves integrated-quantile dominance and hence convex order. Strict convexity determines the only equality law. The equality classification uses total path length, the diameter bound, exhaustion by two geodesics and the resulting distance functions. No finite search is used to infer an all-graph statement. Exact-arithmetic checks independently corroborate small cases and the bridge boundary.

**PASS.** The result determines all convex costs of the centered Lipschitz distribution at once on an infinite, topologically characterized family of compact length spaces. The exact universal constants, graph-dependent attainment criterion and complete strictly-convex optimizer classification distinguish cycles and diameter-saturating networks from theta-like graphs. The bridge counterexample demonstrates the structural necessity of two paths. This is not a mere relabeling of a previously computed discrete variance constant.
