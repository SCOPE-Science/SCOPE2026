The minimum real Fourier-projection norms of the two neighboring symmetric-group Specht coefficient spaces \((n-2,1,1)\) and \((n-2,2)\) reverse order at small dimensions but satisfy \(\Lambda_{\mathrm t}(n)>\Lambda_{\mathrm h}(n)\) for every \(n\ge8\), despite their irreducible degrees differing by one. Their normalized norm gap tends to a strictly positive independent-Poisson absolute-character difference between \(1/40\) and \(1/38\).
