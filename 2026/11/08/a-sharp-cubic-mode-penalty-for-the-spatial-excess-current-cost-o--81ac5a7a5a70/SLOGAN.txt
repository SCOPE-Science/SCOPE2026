For harmonic boundary forcing of open-exclusion correlations, the correctly integrated spatial excess-current cost produces a sharp cubic, rather than merely qualitative, high-mode penalty: the maximal normalized lag-to-cost ratio is \(0.1585838965\ldots n^3+0.0897766134\ldots n^2+O(n)\). At every sufficiently high mode the maximizing frequency is unique, and its ratio to the diffusive mode eigenvalue converges to \(2.5291930138\ldots\).
