For uniform spanning trees of every complete multipartite graph on \(N\) vertices, the two-degree covariance is never positive and is at least \(-((N-3)/4+N^{-2})\), with equality only for the two singleton vertices of \(K_{1,1,N-2}\). At that extremal host the correlation approaches \(-1\) with sharp gap \(1+\rho_N\sim4/N^2\).
