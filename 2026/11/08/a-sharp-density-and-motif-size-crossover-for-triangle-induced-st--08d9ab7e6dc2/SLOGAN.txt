In \(G(n,p)\), the triangle count and the induced \(r\)-star count have a single sharp covariance sign switch for every \(r\ge3\) when \(n\ge r+2\), at \(p=6(n-r-1)/((r+1)(3n-2r-4))\); on \(r+1\) vertices their covariance is always negative. This yields a monotone motif-size/density phase diagram and the joint scaling law \(rp_*\to6(1-\rho)/(3-2\rho)\) when \(r/n\to\rho\in(0,1)\).
