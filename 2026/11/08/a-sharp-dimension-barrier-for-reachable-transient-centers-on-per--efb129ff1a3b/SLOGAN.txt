A nonstationary periodic orbit of any complete smooth flow on a two-dimensional manifold can never be a reachable observable-speed transient center: the monotone local return map always traps one time direction. The bound is sharp: a complete smooth three-dimensional saddle-cycle model makes every point of its unit-circle orbit reachable, with both forward and backward threshold-exit times equal to \(\log(1/\varepsilon)+O(1)\) and explicit distinct additive constants.
