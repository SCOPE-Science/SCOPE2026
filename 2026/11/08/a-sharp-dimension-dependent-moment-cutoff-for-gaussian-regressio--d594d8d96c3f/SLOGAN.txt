For same-sample OLS control-variate integration of every nonzero quadratic under \(d\ge2\) independent Gaussian coordinates, the absolute integration-error moment of order \(q>0\) exists exactly when \(q<n-d\). In particular, the fitted-intercept estimator has no ordinary expectation with just \(d+1\) observations and infinite mean squared error with \(d+2\), even though ordinary Monte Carlo has moments of every order.
