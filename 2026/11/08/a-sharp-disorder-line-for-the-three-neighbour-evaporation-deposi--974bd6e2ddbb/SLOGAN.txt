In the exactly solvable three-neighbour evaporation–deposition automaton, the stationary spatial covariance changes sharply from damped sign-changing oscillations to an eventually positive exponential tail on one explicit algebraic curve \(q=q_c(p)\). At that curve the asymptotic gains an \(r\)-prefactor, while the oscillation wavelength diverges as \((q_c-q)^{-1/2}\); size two cannot exhibit the nonreal spatial modes responsible for this phenomenon.
