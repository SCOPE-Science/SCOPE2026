On the loopless wheel \(W_n=K_1\vee C_n\), where one hub follows minority opinion with tie retention and all rim vertices follow strict majority, a primitive eight-cycle exists exactly when \(n\) is even and \(n\ge12\). The first example has exactly \(288\) eight-periodic configurations, and a single cyclic block of \(n/2-4\) rim vertices in opinion one constructs an eight-cycle at every admissible size.
