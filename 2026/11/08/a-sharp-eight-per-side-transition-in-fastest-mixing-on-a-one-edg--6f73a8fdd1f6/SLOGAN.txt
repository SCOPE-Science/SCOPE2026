Deleting just one crossing edge from the balanced biclique produces an exactly solvable fastest-mixing problem with a sharp transition at eight vertices per side: the optimal SLEM is \(1/\sqrt n\) for \(3\le n\le7\), but a different radical formula holds for all \(n\ge8\). An explicit two-orbit stochastic kernel attains the global minimum, and it is the unique optimum among automorphism-invariant kernels.
