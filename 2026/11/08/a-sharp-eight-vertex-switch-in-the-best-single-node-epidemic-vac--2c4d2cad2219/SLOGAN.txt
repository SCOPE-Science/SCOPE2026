In the independent-edge SIR model on trees, every network with at most seven vertices has a maximum-degree vertex that is an optimal single-vaccination target for every transmissibility \(0<p<1\). On an explicit eight-vertex broom, the optimum instead moves uniquely from its degree-three hub to the first handle vertex when \(p\) passes the root of \(p^2+p^3=1\), proving eight is the first tree order where that switch is possible.
