On a three-patch colonization–extinction chain with unit extinction and a unit external-immigration gateway, placing the gateway at the center always maximizes the stationary chance of at least one occupied patch. Yet simultaneous occupancy of all three patches uniquely favors the center when \(0<c<c_*\) and an endpoint when \(c>c_*\), with the one sharp crossover \(c_*\approx3.637678\).
