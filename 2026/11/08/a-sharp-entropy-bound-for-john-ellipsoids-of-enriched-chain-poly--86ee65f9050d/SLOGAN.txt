For the enriched chain polytope of every finite poset, height–width imbalance forces a quantified, strictly larger John ellipsoid than the maximal Euclidean ball: the ellipsoid-volume ratio is at least \(\exp(\tfrac n2 D(w/n\|1/h))\). The bound is attained by layered posets, and the John ellipsoid is round exactly when \(n=h w\).
