On an anti-coordination wheel with unit rim weights and common spoke weight \(\lambda\), the exact pure Nash equilibrium census changes at \(\lambda=2\) from twice the Perrin number to twice the Lucas number, then to just two profiles. The same classification yields a sharp \(3/2\) universal price-of-anarchy supremum and proves that every equilibrium is welfare-optimal at and above the threshold; the underlying Perrin count of cycle maximal independent sets is known.
