The standard Euclidean stable-set polytope of every odd antihole has a unique uniform incenter; its exact inscribed radius is the smaller of \(2/(2a+1+\sqrt{2a+1})\) and \(1/(a+\sqrt a)\) in dimension \(2a+1\). Tangency switches from the odd-antihole rank facet to maximum-clique facets, with a unique tie at dimension nine, and all remaining facet supporting-hyperplane distances have explicit binomial multiplicities.
