For every even Taylor order \(p\), a dominating clique joined to an arbitrary unweighted residual graph has the exact heat-step positivity cutoff \(\beta_p/n\) whenever \(n\ge\beta_p(k+\Delta)\), where \(\beta_p\) is the positive root of \(F_p(\beta_p)=1\). A rank-one correction and a positive shifted-adjacency expansion prove sufficiency, while every clique-to-core entry turns negative beyond the cutoff.
