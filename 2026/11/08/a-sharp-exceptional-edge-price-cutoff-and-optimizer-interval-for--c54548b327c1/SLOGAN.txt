On every \(n\)-cycle with \(n\ge5\), when one edge costs \(c\) times as much as each other edge, every optimal continuous-time mixing design deletes it exactly when \(c>(n-1)^2\); at equality, the entire optimizer set is a nontrivial one-parameter interval. The five-cycle has an explicit radical endpoint \(\zeta_5=(10-\sqrt{10})B/200\), and below the cutoff every optimizer instead funds the costly edge.
