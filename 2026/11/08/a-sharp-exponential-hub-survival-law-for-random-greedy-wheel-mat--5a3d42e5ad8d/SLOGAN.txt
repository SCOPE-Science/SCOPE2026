A hub of a wheel can survive a random greedy edge-matching process only when the rim has even length, and its exact survival law follows a quadratic convolution for the exposed-hub probability on fans. With \(2m\) rim vertices the probability is \(2\rho^{-m}+O(R^{-m})\) for some \(R>\rho\), where the explicit entire-function root satisfies \(3.47547<\rho<3.47548\).
