In complete-substrate random sequential adsorption with growing block size \(k\), the ratio between remaining vacant blocks and block size determines the entire near-saturation fluctuation phase diagram: exponential for \(u/k\to0\), a geometric-exponential sum for \(u/k\to\theta\in(0,\infty)\), and Gaussian for \(u/k\to\infty\). The critical law has an explicit infinite-product Laplace transform, and the Gaussian regime has Kolmogorov error \(O(\sqrt{k/u})\).
