For the Gaussian and first-Hermite ambiguity multipliers, the exact worst-radius noise margin undergoes a sharp exposure-allocation transition at squared phase-space radius \(T=2\): Gaussian-only below, a whole half-interval of optimal splits at the threshold, and a unique first-Hermite-majority split above. At large radius the optimal squared margin improves on Gaussian-only by a factor asymptotic to \((T-1)^2\), with the two active worst-case radii exactly balanced.
