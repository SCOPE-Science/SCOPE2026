For classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta on the stable planar Jordan family \(A_\gamma=-I+\gamma N\), the exact Euclidean one-step contraction region acquires a detached intermediate-step interval precisely when the continuous system becomes locally expansive (\(\gamma>2\)). Its contraction interval collapses toward a universal stationary point of the RK4 stability polynomial at rate \(\gamma^{-1}\), while the exact flow there grows without bound with the shear.
