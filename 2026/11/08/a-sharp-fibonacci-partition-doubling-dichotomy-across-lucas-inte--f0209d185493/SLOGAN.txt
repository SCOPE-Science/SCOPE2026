For every integer \(N\ge3\), the number of distinct-Fibonacci partitions of \(F_{r(N)+4}N\) is at least twice that of \(F_{r(N)+2}N\), where \(-r(N)\) is the last Bergman base-\(\varphi\) exponent. Equality holds exactly on odd Lucas intervals, while the ratio is unbounded on the even-interval endpoints.
