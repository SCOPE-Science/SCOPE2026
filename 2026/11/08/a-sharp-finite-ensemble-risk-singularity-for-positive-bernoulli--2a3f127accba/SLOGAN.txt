For every fixed ensemble size \(\nu\ge2\), uniformly positive Bernoulli Gram sketches can have control matrices converging to their targets while same-ensemble fitted control-variate correction has mean-square error asymptotic to \(C_\nu/\varepsilon\), even though the fixed-weight control error is \(2\varepsilon/(9\nu)\). The corrected scalar Gram becomes negative with an exact positive limiting probability, despite positive uncorrected averages on all responsible events.
