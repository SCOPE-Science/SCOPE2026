For \(A_m=\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(m,m),(m+1,m+1)\}\), the holes of the lattice polygon dilation \(h\operatorname{conv}(A_m)\) have a complete diagonal-residue classification. For every integer \(m\ge2\), their number stabilizes sharply at \(h=2m-2\) to the exact constant \(m(m-1)(2m-1)/3\), while the preceding degree has exactly one fewer hole.
