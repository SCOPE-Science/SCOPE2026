For \(m\ge3\), the geometric fixed spaces of rotation by \(2\pi/m\) on nonempty subsets of \(S^2\) with fewer than \(2m\) points are classified integrally: they change from discrete sets to \(S^2\sqcup\{\mathrm{pt}\}\), then \(S^1\vee\bigvee^3S^2\), and finally \(\bigvee^2S^1\vee\bigvee^4S^2\). The first circle is forced exactly at subset size \(m+1\), and a second appears at size \(m+2\).
