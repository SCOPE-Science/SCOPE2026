A single dissenting hub in the nonlinear voter model can convert every opponent without ever changing its own opinion; on a star with \(m\) leaves, that event has exact probability \(\prod_{j=1}^{m}(1+j^{q-1}/m^q)^{-1}\). Its large-star probability is \(e^{-1/q}\), yielding a sharp one-half leader-survival threshold at \(q=1/\log2\), and the full first-reversal location has an explicit atomic-continuous limit law.
