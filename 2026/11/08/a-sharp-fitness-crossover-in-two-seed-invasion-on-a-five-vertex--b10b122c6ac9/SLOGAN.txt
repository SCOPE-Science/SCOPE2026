In a five-vertex bipartite population under death–Birth updating, the exact optimal placement of two mutants switches once: seed both degree-three vertices when \(r<r_*\) and one vertex in each part when \(r>r_*\), with \(r_*=2.0514402663\ldots\). Two seeds confined to the degree-two part are never optimal; the optimum is tied only at the threshold.
