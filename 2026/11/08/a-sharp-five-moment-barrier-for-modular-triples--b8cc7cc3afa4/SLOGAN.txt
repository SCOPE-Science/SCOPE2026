For triples of residues modulo \(N\), the minimum number of initial power sums that determines every later power sum is exactly \(1\) at \(N=2\), \(2\) at \(N\in\{3,6\}\), \(5\) when \(9\mid N\), and \(3\) otherwise. The exceptional fifth-moment threshold follows from a sharp Hensel-lifted cubic obstruction at powers of three.
