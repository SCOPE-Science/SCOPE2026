For simple unit-edge graph coverage games, Shapley ranking first provably loses optimal inclusion area at five players: the sole obstruction type is \(K_3\sqcup K_2\). Among disjoint unions of cliques, Shapley ranking is optimal exactly when the nontrivial cliques all have one common size, and on \(kK_3\sqcup qK_2\) even the best tie-breaking loses exactly \(kq\) inclusion-area units, permitting a persistent relative loss up to \(1/(7+\sqrt{39})\).
