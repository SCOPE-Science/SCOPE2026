Among all simple graphs on at most five vertices, exactly the three-page triangular book \(K_{1,1,3}\) has integral torsion in the multipath complex of its fully bidirected digraph. Its only nonzero reduced integral homology is \(\widetilde H_1\cong\mathbb Z/2\) and \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z^7\), establishing the sharp five-vertex torsion threshold.
