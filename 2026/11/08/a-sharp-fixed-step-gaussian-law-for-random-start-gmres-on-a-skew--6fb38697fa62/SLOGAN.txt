For fixed \(q\ge1\), exact GMRES on the real skew tridiagonal Toeplitz stencil with a Haar-random right-hand side stagnates at every odd iteration and has residual \(R_{N,2q}\to(2q+1)^{-1/2}\) as even dimension grows. Its sharply centered fluctuations obey \(\sqrt N[R_{N,2q}-(2q+1)^{-1/2}]\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,(4q+1)(2q)/(6(2q+1)^2))\), an explicit numerical-reliability law absent from ordinary worst-case residual bounds.
