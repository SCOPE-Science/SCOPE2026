On a four-state Markov pure-birth cascade activated in reverse causal order, the two-term Magnus exponential has exactly one negative entry for every positive integrated rate below the sharp threshold \(h_*=5.2022837279006018\ldots\), yet becomes entrywise nonnegative at and above it. For arbitrary positive pulse weights on every longer reverse cascade, the forbidden transition begins with the universal negative term \(-a_1a_2a_3h^3/3\), whereas shorter equal-rate cascades stay nonnegative.
