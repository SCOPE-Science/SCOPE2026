The complex right Peirce spectrum of a finite abelian core-quandle algebra is exactly \(\{1\}\), \(\{0,1,-1\}\), or all of \(\mathbb C\): the jump to every complex eigenvalue occurs precisely when the group has four-torsion. Every right Peirce operator is nevertheless diagonalizable, and a one-parameter family of explicit idempotents realizes the full-plane case.
