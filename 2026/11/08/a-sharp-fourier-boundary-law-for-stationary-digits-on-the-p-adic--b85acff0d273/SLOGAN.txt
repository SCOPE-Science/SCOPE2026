For every stationary base-\(p\) digit process, its additive \(p\)-adic Fourier coefficients along \(kp^{-n}\) converge to a canonical reversed-digit circle Fourier coefficient at the sharp universal rate \(2\pi|k|p^{-n}\). Consequently, Haar probability is the only digit-shift-invariant probability on \(\mathbb Z_p\) with any positive Fourier dimension; all other such probabilities have dimension zero.
