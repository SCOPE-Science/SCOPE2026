For real first harmonics on even-dimensional orthogonal Grassmannians, the sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) norm switches from a one-spike traceless spectrum to a balanced positive/negative spectrum precisely when \(k(n-k)\) crosses \(n(n^2+n+2)/(5n+6)\). The transition approaches the density thresholds \((1\pm1/\sqrt5)/2\), and all equality spectra are classified.
