For fixed-gain zero-order-held proportional–derivative control of a double integrator, **every** alternation of two individually Schur-stable sampling intervals remains stable exactly when the dimensionless gain ratio \(k_p/k_d^2\) lies in \((0,1/2]\cup[2,\infty)\). In the intervening gain window, two perfectly stable fixed-step controllers can combine to give an exponentially unstable two-period Floquet map.
