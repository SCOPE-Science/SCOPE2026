For fourth-order Gauss–Legendre heat stepping on unweighted windmills \(K_1\vee(mK_{r-1})\), stars and complete graphs remain positive for every timestep, but every windmill with at least two nontrivial cliques has a unique sharp loss-of-positivity time. The exact boundary is the positive root of an explicit quartic; beyond it precisely the within-blade off-diagonal transitions turn negative.
