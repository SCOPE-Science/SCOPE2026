For independent fair forward/reverse Lie splitting of arbitrary fixed real matrices, the individual trajectory error has a sharp \(h^{3/2}\) Gaussian limit with covariance determined by the propagated commutator, although the mean propagator has \(O(h^2)\) error. The leading squared-error constant is positive exactly for noncommuting generators; for the harmonic-oscillator shear splitting over unit time it equals \(1/2\).
