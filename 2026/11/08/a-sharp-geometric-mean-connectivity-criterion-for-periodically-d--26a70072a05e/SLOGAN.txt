For any periodically shortcut-decorated \((2,2)\)-flower, a geometric mean of shortcut nonoccupation at most \(27/32\) forces complete limiting two-root connectivity, except at the sharp homogeneous critical profile. Any nonconstant modulation at the critical average occupation \(5/32\) therefore restores full connectivity; even below that average, the mean-matched profiles \((3/10,0)\) and \((3/20,3/20)\) have different connectivity phases.
