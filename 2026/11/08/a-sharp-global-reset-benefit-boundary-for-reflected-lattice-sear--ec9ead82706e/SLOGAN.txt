A symmetric random walk on a finite path with deterministic reflection at the far endpoint is globally helped by geometric resetting to its initial site if and only if \(5(n-k)^2>n^2+5\), where \(0\) is absorbing, \(n\) is reflecting, and \(k\) is the start. Outside that sharp region, every positive resetting probability strictly increases the mean hitting time, not merely its initial slope.
