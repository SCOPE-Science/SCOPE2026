On a six-candidate triangle of pairwise-overlapping approval blocs, every sequential-PAV tie-break gives a suboptimal four-member committee exactly when the third bloc-to-first bloc weight ratio lies strictly between \(4/3\) and \(3/2\); the precise harmonic-score loss is \((3y-4x)/12\). Among all three-bloc integer profiles of this form, irrespective of symmetry, \(17\) voters is the sharp minimum for such tie-break-independent failure, with the sole minimal multiplicity pattern \((5,5,7)\) up to relabeling.
