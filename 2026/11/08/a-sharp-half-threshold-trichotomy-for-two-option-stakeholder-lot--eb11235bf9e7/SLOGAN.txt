With two alternatives and a known finite grid of stakeholder acceptability thresholds in \([a,b]\), the exact deterministic worst-case extra-rejection cost of consulting only \(K\) of \(n\) agents is zero when \(b\le1/2\), \(\max\{0,n-K-(K\bmod2)\}\) when \(a>1/2\), and \(n-K\) when \(a\le1/2<b\). The half-threshold crossing, not the number of alternatives alone, decides whether the full information-theoretic obstruction survives in this smallest lottery dimension.
