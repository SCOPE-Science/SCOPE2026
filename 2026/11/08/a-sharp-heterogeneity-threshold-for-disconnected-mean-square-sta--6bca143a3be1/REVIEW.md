# Scientific assessment of the corrected claim

**Correctness.** For a symmetric anti-persistent chain the signed second eigenvalue of the nonnegative moment transfer is nonpositive. The sign of the transfer determinant at one therefore gives the exact strict stability test. The first discriminant classifies simple roots; the multiple-root factorization distinguishes a puncture from loss of the second window. The second discriminant, the endpoint signs, and continuity give the sharp all-contrast threshold, with a triple learning-rate root at equality. The exact symbolic companion checks algebraic certificates; it is not a substitute for the parameter-wide sign argument.

**Scope of originality.** An earlier Resultary result, *A reentrant mean-square stability window for Markov-sampled quadratic descent* (October 12, 2026), already proves reentrant mean-square stability for the same sampling dynamics with curvature ratio six, using the same determinant and cubic and an exact persistence boundary for that ratio. The general two-state moment machinery is also not new. The contribution here is the sharp onset across variation in the curvature contrast, the two-parameter classification on the anti-persistent domain, and the distinction between a puncture and a disappearing window there. This is a comparison with the examined works, not a guarantee of absolute priority.

**Value and limits.** The threshold distinguishes contrasts at which reentrance can occur from those at which it cannot occur for any permitted chain. The puncture clarifies why a slow scan or an approximate root test may miss a loss of strict stability. These results concern moment stability for centered scalar quadratics, not pathwise divergence, additive-noise stationary variance, or a tuning guarantee for general stochastic optimization.
