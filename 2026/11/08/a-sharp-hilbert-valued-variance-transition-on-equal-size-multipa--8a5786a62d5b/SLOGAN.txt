For \(r\ge3\) equal-size metric clusters with intracluster distance \(t\) and cross-distance one, the sharp variance of Hilbert-valued one-Lipschitz maps equals \(\min\{1/2,(r-1)/(2r)+(m-1)t^2/(2mr)\}\). Its equality geometry changes exactly at \(t=\sqrt{m/(m-1)}\), with a collapse of isometric embedding dimension and a graph-endpoint parity law for the smallest maximizing Euclidean dimension.
