# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The claim specifies known, distinct positive real decay rates, two unknown amplitudes, three independent equal-variance readings at \(0,t,T\), and a Fisher-determinant objective. Cauchy–Binet gives the exact determinant. Its derivative in the exponential coordinate is a strictly convex quadratic with explicitly controlled endpoint signs. The three possible sign patterns yield the complete global maximum classification; no numerical grid is used to prove globality. The bound \(t_*<T_c\) follows from the strict negative derivative of that quadratic at \(1/r\). Standard Taylor expansion and the separated limiting quadratic establish the transition and asymptotic claims. The standalone verifier passed 56,063 independent rational and floating-point assertions, with the finite-versus-infinite distinction stated in the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with publication-priority risk
The closest directly motivating primary article, Reci et al. (2018), was examined in its lawful full manuscript. It optimizes a four-parameter CRLB-derived percentage-error objective numerically over 32-point sampling families. It does not determine the exact three-event Fisher determinant, the two-known-rate endpoint/interior transition, the quadratic optimizer, or the exponentially diverging gain. Krauss et al. (2022) address rank observability under discrete-time sparse measurements rather than D-optimal timing. Kazma et al. (2023) address selection of sensors from a network rather than continuous measurement-time placement. Dette et al. (2004) already obtain the same logarithmic-mean expression as the robust maximin-optimal sampling point when the rate of a single exponential regression is unknown within a prescribed interval. That is not this exact pinned three-event design for two *known distinct* decays, so the logarithmic mean itself is prior art while the complete bifurcation and finite-rate optimizer remain the asserted contribution. A previously established equal-eigenvalue Jordan case provides the confluent limit but not this finite-separation theorem. A related arithmetic-spectrum observation-horizon result concerns different multiparameter modes and equally spaced designs; its published summary does not imply the pinned-endpoint classification. The remaining risk is specialized exact experimental-design literature that was not exhaustively available or searched.

## Value — PASS
For two-mode decay experiments, the given three-event budget and compulsory first/terminal readings are a natural minimal sensing design. The result identifies precisely when repeating a compulsory time is efficient and when a genuinely new reading is necessary; the transition depends on the logarithmic mean of the known rates, not an arbitrary tuned constant. The strict nonmonotonicity of optimal interior timing and an unbounded exponential information ratio provide interpretable structural consequences. This does not solve the four-unknown-rate bi-exponential problem or more general sensor-budget designs.
