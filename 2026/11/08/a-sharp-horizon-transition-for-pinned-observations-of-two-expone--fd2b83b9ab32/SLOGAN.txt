For every pair of distinct positive decay rates, three scalar measurements with the first and last times fixed have an exact D-optimal transition at the logarithmic-mean horizon \(T_c=\log(b/a)/(b-a)\): below it, repeat an endpoint; above it, sample at a unique interior time. The interior time lies strictly before \(T_c\) and is nonmonotone in the horizon, while its information advantage over endpoint replication grows exponentially for long horizons.
