For an asymmetric two-species Ricker competition map with positive coexistence equilibrium \((X,Y)\) satisfying \(0<X,Y\le2\), the entire positive prime-two-cycle census is exact: there is one in-phase orbit precisely when \(\alpha\beta>(2/X-1)(2/Y-1)\), and none otherwise, provided \(\alpha\beta\ne1\). The same sharp inequality is the negative sign of the equilibrium's \(-1\) characteristic factor, yet no global attraction claim is made.
