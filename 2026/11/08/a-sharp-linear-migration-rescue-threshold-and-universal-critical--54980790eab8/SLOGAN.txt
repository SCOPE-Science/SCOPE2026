In the linear-migration two-patch Allee--logistic model, the severe-Allee regime has a sharp global rescue/extinction threshold \(\delta_c=es/(e-s)\) when \(e>s\), with one globally attracting positive equilibrium below it and extinction above it. At equality every nonzero nonnegative population approaches extinction with the universal exact law \(t(u,v)\to(a,1)/k\), and the positive equilibrium below threshold has an explicit linear emergence amplitude.
