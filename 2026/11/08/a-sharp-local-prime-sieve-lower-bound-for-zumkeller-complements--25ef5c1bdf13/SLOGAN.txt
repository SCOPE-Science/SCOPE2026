For every sufficiently large odd integer \(N\), the number of prime-plus-Zumkeller representations is at least \(cN/(\log N)^3\), improving the previous fifth-power logarithmic denominator by two powers. An explicit local prime sieve retains asymptotically a proportion \(1-1/q\) of a reduced residue class, where \(q\) is the least odd prime missing from \(N\).
