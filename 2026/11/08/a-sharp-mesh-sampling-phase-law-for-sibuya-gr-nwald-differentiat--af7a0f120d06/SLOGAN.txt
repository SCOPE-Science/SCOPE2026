For Sibuya-sampled Grünwald differentiation of a one-sided polynomial past tail, the exact first-order mesh bias and sharp variance constant together imply a fine-mesh sampling trade-off of order \(N^{-2/(2+\alpha)}\). At the natural power-growth threshold \(2\beta=\alpha\), the unmodified estimator changes from finite to infinite mean-squared error even though the target Marchaud derivative stays finite.
