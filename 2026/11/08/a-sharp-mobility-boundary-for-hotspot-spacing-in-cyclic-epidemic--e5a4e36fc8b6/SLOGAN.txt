In the symmetric SIR-network model, two equally elevated transmission patches on any cycle have strictly greater invasion potential when closer together for mobility fraction \(0<h<2/5\), but the ordering reverses on a five-cycle for \(h>2/5\). Exactly at \(h=2/5\), the five-cycle reproduction number becomes invariant under every permutation of arbitrary positive patch transmission rates, and exact rational parameters turn this into an outbreak-threshold reversal.
