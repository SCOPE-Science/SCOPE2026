On even periodic Fourier-collocation grids, exact heat evolution does not regain positivity on the usual \(N^{-2}\) diffusion scale: its eventual entrywise-nonnegative horizon obeys \(NT_N\to\pi\), with strict positivity uniformly for all later times beyond \(c/N\) when \(c>\pi\). Negative propagation entries persist at time \(c/N\) for each fixed \(0<c<\pi\) on all sufficiently fine even grids.
