For renewal-reset walks with instantaneous continuous jumps, a fixed nonzero point cannot be reached by exact landing; a small target interval has a sharp \(1/\varepsilon\) mean-capture law and an exponential scaled capture-time limit, with coefficient set by the stationary occupation density. With competing Pareto clocks and Gaussian jumps, the mean first interval-arrival time is finite exactly when the sum of the two clock exponents exceeds one, even when each clock separately has infinite mean.
