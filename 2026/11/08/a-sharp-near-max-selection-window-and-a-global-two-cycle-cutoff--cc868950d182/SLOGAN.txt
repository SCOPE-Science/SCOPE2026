On every finite periodic \(H\)-by-\(W\) grid with \(H,W\ge4\), the normalized separable-field automaton with coincident birth and survival intervals \([1-\varepsilon,1]\) has an exact maximum-field update for the sharp window \(0\le\varepsilon<1/(3\max\{H,W\})\). In that positive-width window every trajectory becomes rectangular after one step and settles into a fixed point or two-cycle within a quadratic-size bound; genuine two-cycles and linearly long transients occur.
