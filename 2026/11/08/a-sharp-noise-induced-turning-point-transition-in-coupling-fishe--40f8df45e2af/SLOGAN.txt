In a stable two-coordinate Langevin particle, stationary snapshot information about the harmonic coupling is strictly increasing with coupling below one exact added-noise threshold \(d_\star=33.219537333\ldots\), but above it develops exactly one local maximum and one local minimum. The extrema emerge by a square-root splitting and separate at large noise, with the first coupling scale \(2/\sqrt{3d}\) and the second tending to \(\sqrt{(9-\sqrt{57})/4}\); this classifies a previously reported qualitative nonmonotonicity rather than replacing the known Fisher-information formula.
