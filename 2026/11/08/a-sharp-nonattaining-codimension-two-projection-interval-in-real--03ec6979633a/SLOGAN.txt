For the real codimension-two subspace of continuous functions whose means on the first two thirds agree and whose mean on the final third is \(t\) times that common mean, the sharp projection constant is \(2+t/(1+t+t^2)\), and no minimizing projection exists for any \(0<t\le1\). The values fill the entire sharp interval \((2,7/3]\), settling the codimension-two case of the fixed-codimension nonattainment question posed by Kania and Lewicki.
