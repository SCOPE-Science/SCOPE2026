For a weighted five-cycle, the exact minimum two-probe Hutchinson variance under arbitrary coupling of uniform Rademacher marginals is \(4t^2\) for \(t\le1\), \(3+(t-2)^2\) for \(1\le t\le3\), and \(4\) for \(t\ge3\). Nonlinear coupling strictly improves every fixed sign-flip coupling exactly for \(1<t<3\), with a sharp 25% relative variance reduction at \(t=2\); no such separation occurs on simple cycles with three or four vertices.
