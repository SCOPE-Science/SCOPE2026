In a fully separable odd-cycle charger–battery state, every fixed local product-projective charger readout has a sharp, strictly positive variance of conditional battery energy: \((2^n\delta)^2\min\{h^2,\tfrac{nJ^2}{2}[1-\cos(\pi/n)]\}\). The optimum switches exactly from one vertical readout axis to a uniformly twisted equatorial pattern, while even-cycle graphs permit complete branch-energy equalization.
