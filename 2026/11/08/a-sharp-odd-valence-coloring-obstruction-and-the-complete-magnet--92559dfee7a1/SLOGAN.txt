Every three-coloring of a closed triangulated surface incurs at least \(\lceil o/4\rceil\) monochromatic bonds when there are \(o\) odd-valence vertices, and the icosahedron attains this sharp obstruction with three bonds, twenty defect patterns and 120 ground states. Its three-state Potts antiferromagnet has an exact asymmetric seven-plateau magnetic response, with six field crossings and fully enumerated coexistence multiplicities.
