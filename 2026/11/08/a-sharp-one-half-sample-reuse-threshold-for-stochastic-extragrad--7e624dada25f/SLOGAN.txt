On scalar bilinear games, equal-step stochastic extragradient contracts in mean square at some positive step precisely when \(2\mathbb E[AB]>\mathbb E[S^2]\), where the two oracle calls have common marginal \(S\). Reusing one oracle sample on at least half the iterations is a sharp distribution-free guarantee of a nonempty stable step-size interval: any smaller reuse fraction can fail even for a strictly positive two-point law.
