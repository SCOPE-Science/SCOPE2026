For the scalar Allen–Cahn double well, stabilized ETDRK2 decreases the original quartic energy in one step for every time step and every input in \([-1,1]\) exactly when the splitting stabilizer satisfies \(\alpha\ge1\). The sharp threshold follows from an exact rational Bernstein-positivity certificate and a repelling-well fixed-point argument; it does not imply interval preservation or energy descent for indefinitely repeated steps.
