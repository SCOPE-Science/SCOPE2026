For every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph, the two-stage Gauss–Legendre graph-heat update is entrywise nonnegative at all timesteps exactly when \(N\le2k-\lambda+\mu\); otherwise a closed-form positive quadratic root is the unique sharp cutoff. After this cutoff every adjacent-vertex transition becomes negative, while every diagonal and nonadjacent transition remains strictly positive at every positive timestep.
