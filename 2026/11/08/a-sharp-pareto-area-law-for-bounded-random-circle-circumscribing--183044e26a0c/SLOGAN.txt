Even after a random circle-circumscribing polygon is conditioned to be bounded, its area has the sharp survival law \(\Pr(A_n>t\mid\mathrm{bounded})\sim 2n(n-1)/[\pi(2^{n-1}-n)t]\) for every fixed \(n\ge3\). Consequently every such polygon has infinite conditional mean area, while all positive fractional moments of order below one are finite.
