The two-step IMEX delay integrator is stable for every fixed integer delay and the underlying scalar DDE is stable for every bounded variable delay, yet the three-phase grid delay \((1,2,3)\) makes the numerical method unstable exactly when \(-1<\mu<-10773/13315\). The threshold comes from an exact cubic Floquet polynomial; at equality a nondecaying \(-1\) multiplier remains.
