For noisy scalar quadratic optimization with two equally frequent stable learning rates, the exact stationary noise floor depends sharply on whether the learning-rate sequence alternates or persists. A symmetric Markov schedule beats the constant mean learning rate exactly when \(t=h^2(1-\delta^2)>2\) and its repeat probability satisfies \(p<(t-2)/(2(t-1))\), while independent random choices always lose.
