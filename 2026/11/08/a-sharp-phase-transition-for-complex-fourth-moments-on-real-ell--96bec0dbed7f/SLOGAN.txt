For complex linear functionals on uniform real \(\ell_p\) balls, the sharp fourth-moment minimizer changes at \(p=2\): it is phase-balanced with all coefficients nonzero below two, but exactly two equally weighted quadrature-phase coefficients above two. The exact gamma-function endpoints, all equality cases, positive deficit identities, and optimal complex Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance hold in every dimension \(n\ge2\).
