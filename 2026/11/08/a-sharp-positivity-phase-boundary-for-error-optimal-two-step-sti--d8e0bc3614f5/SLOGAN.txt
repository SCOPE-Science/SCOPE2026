The error-optimal A-stable two-step stiff-solver family has an exact backward-Euler-started positivity boundary: \(h\lambda\le(1+\kappa)/(2(1-\kappa))\) until damping \(\kappa=1/2\), then \(h\lambda\le1/\kappa-\kappa\). Its uniquely most positivity-tolerant member has \(\kappa=1/2\) and threshold \(3/2\), three times BDF2's \(1/2\), while retaining a smaller published local error constant.
