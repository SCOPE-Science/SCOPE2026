Uniform recursive trees which forbid attachment to the first \(\lfloor m^\gamma\rfloor-1\) existing vertices exhibit a sharp hub transition at \(\gamma_*=1-1/(2\log2)\): the maximum number of children retains its unrestricted \((\log n)/(\log2)\) growth below this threshold, but has a strictly smaller, explicitly determined coefficient above it. The logarithmic label exponent of the maximizing vertex moves from \(\gamma_*\) to \(\gamma\) at the same transition.
