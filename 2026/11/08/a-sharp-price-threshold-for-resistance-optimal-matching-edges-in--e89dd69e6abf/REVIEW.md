## Value (PASS)

A matched interlayer link is an economically natural candidate in paired bipartite networks. The theorem provides a **complete, globally unique edge-allocation rule**, a sharp criterion for removing an entire edge orbit without disconnecting the network, and a non-obvious scaling law: even a \(2/n\)-order relative price premium suffices for deletion in a large balanced network. This is not a bare fixed-weight index computation or a generic KKT restatement: identifying the closed rational threshold and the full price phase diagram resolves the edge-support decision for a parametrized family.
