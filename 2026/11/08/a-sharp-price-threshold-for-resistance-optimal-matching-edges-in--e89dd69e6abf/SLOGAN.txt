When the \(n\) matching edges of \(K_{n,n}\) are priced at \(c\) times the other edges, the unique allocation minimizing total effective resistance deletes **every** matching edge exactly when \(c\ge c_n\), where \(c_n=1+2/n+O(n^{-2})\). Below this threshold, the unrestricted global optimum has two explicit conductance classes determined by one uniquely solvable scalar equation.
