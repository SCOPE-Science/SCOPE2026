The continuous-time fastest mixing rate on \(K_p\vee\overline{K_q}\) with separately priced crossing and clique edges has an exact closed form and a sharp clique-edge extinction threshold at \(C/A=2p/(p-1)\) when \(q<p\). Across the threshold, optimal class-symmetric designs switch between positive and zero clique rates; at equality a continuum of rate allocations is optimal, and cheap clique edges can improve the spectral gap by a factor approaching \(p/q\).
