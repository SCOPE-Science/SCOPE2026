For a prime-length quadratic chirp with any two symbols erased, the entire root-dependent periodic sidelobe-energy spectrum is independent of erasure spacing after a square-index permutation. The optimum is \(4p-30-8\sqrt p+o(1)\) for primes \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), but \(4p-30-4\sqrt p+o(1)\) for primes \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), with an explicit \(O((\log p)^2/\sqrt p)\) error.
