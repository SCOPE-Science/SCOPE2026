For a uniform random recursive tree conditioned to have no leaf within \(h\) edges of its root, the expected leaf count equals \((n-h+1)/2\) at protection depths \(h=1,2\), but for every \(h\ge3\) this equality ends sharply at \(n=h+4\) and the mean is strictly smaller thereafter. The first strict case is depth three on seven vertices, with conditional mean \(65/27\) instead of \(5/2\).
