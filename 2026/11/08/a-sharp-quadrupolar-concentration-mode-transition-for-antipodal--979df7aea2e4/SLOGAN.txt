For pure quadratic spherical harmonics concentrated on a pair of antipodal polar caps in every dimension \(d\ge3\), the unique sharp cap threshold \(a=1/\sqrt d\) switches the optimally concentrated modes from a \((d-1)\)-dimensional mixed sector to a one-dimensional zonal sector. The complete three-eigenvalue spectrum, all multiplicities, and exact spectral-gap identities follow from normalized spherical moments.
