For every connected complete multipartite graph, the squared norm of the centered embedding of its real Lipschitz space into uniform \(L_2\) is \(N^{-2}\max\{\lfloor N^2/4\rfloor,Nm-(m\bmod2)\}\), where \(m\) is the largest part size. The clique-level spread persists until a sharp one-quarter largest-part threshold, with balanced binary and polarized largest-part extremizers coexisting exactly at the divisible-by-eight boundary.
