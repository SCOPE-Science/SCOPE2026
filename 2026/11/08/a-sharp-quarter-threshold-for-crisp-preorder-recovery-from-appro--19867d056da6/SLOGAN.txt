For every finite real-valued Łukasiewicz Kripke frame, the exact worst-case failures of modal normality, reflexivity and transitivity determine its distance from genuine crisp S4 frames whenever their maximum is below \(1/4\). Rounding the accessibility weights at \(1/2\) then recovers the unique nearest crisp preorder with no loss, and a three-world example proves the strict quarter-threshold sharp.
