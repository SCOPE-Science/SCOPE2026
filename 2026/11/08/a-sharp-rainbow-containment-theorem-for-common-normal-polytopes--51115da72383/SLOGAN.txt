For \(m\) color families of polytopes sharing \(m\) fixed facet normals, there is one rainbow selection whose intersection is contained in the **entire intersection of one color**; a maximum-weight assignment constructs it. This lossless, simultaneous-for-all-monotone-functionals containment has a sharp \(m\)-color threshold whenever all \(m\) facet constraints are essential (in particular, \(2d\) for axis-parallel boxes in \(\mathbb R^d\)).
