For two inconsistent strongly convex scalar quadratic components, independent random ordering of one proximal call per component has an exact stationary risk whose unique optimum switches from biased random reshuffling to a deterministic curvature-last order at an explicit positive stepsize threshold. Choosing the curvature-weighted order probability removes the leading \(h^2\) stationary squared-error term of uniform random reshuffling, leaving a sharp \(h^3\) term.
