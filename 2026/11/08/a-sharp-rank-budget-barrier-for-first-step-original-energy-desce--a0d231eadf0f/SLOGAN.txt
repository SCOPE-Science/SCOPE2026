For a coherently initialized scalar-auxiliary-variable step on an SPD quadratic, universal first-step descent of the original objective holds exactly when \(h(H-2L)\preceq2I\), and any violation causes an objective increase from arbitrarily small inputs. Among all positive-semidefinite rank-\(r\) curvature operators with \(r<d\), the sharp largest universally safe step is \(2/\lambda_{r+1}(H)\).
