For drifted Brownian motion reset repeatedly to the origin, with two partially absorbing point traps at \(\pm a\), the optimal fixed-sum reaction budget undergoes an exact threshold: below \(K_c\), all killing strength belongs downstream; above \(K_c\), a unique optimum splits it across both traps. The proof combines an exact two-point resolvent with strict concavity from the targets' occupation local times.
