# Mathematical review

## Correctness
PASS. The killed occupancy-count generator and Laplace resolvent are given explicitly. An exact resultant identifies the sole positive-rate pole cancellation at \(c=1\); spectral continuity and the initial coefficients at \(c=0\) prove the sign trichotomy. The hazard derivative has at most one zero in the subcritical interval and is negative throughout the other interval. The two-site result is an exact two-exponential identity. A separately extracted checker replayed symbolic equalities and \(56\) independent labelled-state versus count-state calculations (\(168\) state probability entries).

## Originality
PASS within inspected evidence, with documented residual historical-priority risk. Cavanaugh et al. (2014) motivates dangers of patch aggregation; Drake (2014) addresses extinction-time empirical tails. Dickman and Vidigal (2002) cover classical quasi-stationarity. Navarro et al. (2009) already study bathtub hazard mixtures and mixed reliability systems, so those general observations are not claimed new. The claimed distinction is the sharp global interaction-rate switch and habitat-number minimum for a recolonizing Markov ecological network. The complete Navarro paper and full publisher material for Cavanaugh and Drake were not accessible, so an unrecognized older coverage risk remains.

## Value
PASS. Unlike a descriptive finite counterexample, the theorem gives an exact parameter phase boundary, equality case, unique-minimum classification, and minimal landscape size. It isolates a qualitative flaw in identifying regional extinction risk from aggregate occupancy ages, while explicitly restricting itself to the stylized stochastic model.

## Scientific limitations
The conclusion holds for one stipulated initial mixture on a homogeneous three-habitat complete graph, independent exponential patch lifetimes, and edge-additive recolonization without immigration after regional extinction. It neither generalizes automatically to larger heterogeneous landscapes nor estimates empirical hazard rates. Finite replay is not an infinite proof; the analytic argument supplies the quantifiers.
