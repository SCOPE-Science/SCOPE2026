In a three-site, boundary-driven exclusion channel with departure rates \((t,1,2t)\), the leading rectification current changes sign exactly once, at the positive root \(t_*=15.6669537561\ldots\) of an explicit degree-eight polynomial, although the spatial rate ordering never changes. An exact two-site comparison proves that this reversal cannot occur under common rate scaling in a unit-hopping-multiplier chain of length two.
