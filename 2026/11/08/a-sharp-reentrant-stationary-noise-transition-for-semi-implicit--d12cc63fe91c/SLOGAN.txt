For fixed-damping semi-implicit NAG-GS on noisy quadratic modes, the stationary variance develops two step-size turning points exactly when the curvature ratio lies between \((4+12\sqrt3)/13\) and \(2\), even though the iteration remains Schur stable for all positive steps. An explicit eleven-dimensional quadratic has decreasing–increasing–decreasing total stationary mean-square error entirely within this unlimited stability regime.
