# Scientific review: a sharp refinability criterion for cycle barbell graphs

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem quantifies over every integer \(m,n\ge3\), \(d\ge1\). In the universal cover, the hubs have incident compressed path-length multisets \(\{m,m,d\}\) and \(\{n,n,d\}\). Equal rooted types correspond to matching weighted segment lengths, endpoint types, and root positions, up to permitted reversals. Distinct hub signatures when \(m\ne n\) force orbit equality; equal signatures with distinct connector length \(m=n\ne d\) still separate connector versus cycle positions; when \(m=n=d\), the homogeneous subdivided trivalent cover collapses precisely one bridge orbit with one cycle orbit for each distance class. The finite automorphism group is generated by the two cycle reflections and, when the cycle lengths agree, simultaneous exchange of both cycles and connector reversal. This proves both directions and all class counts, including \(d=1\) and odd/even lengths. Direct execution of verify_barbell.py confirmed 2,760 cases, and an independent exact automorphism enumerator checked 70 small cases. The finite tests are corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

KV14, arXiv:1407.3175 §2.3 Lemma 2.6 proves the general universal-cover/color-refinement correspondence, an explicitly acknowledged ingredient rather than the claimed novelty. AKRV15, arXiv:1502.01255 §2/§8 defines refinable graphs and studies general graph classes; KSS15, arXiv:1503.08792 studies absolute identification by counting logic. The inspected sections do not imply a complete three-parameter classification without a new weighted-cover analysis. The previously established eight-vertex example \(B(3,3,3)\) is NOT new; it is a special case, not a proof of the iff for all parameters. published-finding corpus, relevant primary sources and accessible prior research were searched for aliases and stronger cactus-level coverage. No decisive covering claim was found. An unindexed earlier graph-specific classification remains a real possibility; independent priority review has not occurred.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The family includes every pair of disjoint simple cycles connected by one path, a canonical two-cycle cactus class. Refinability and automorphism-orbit recovery are natural in descriptive complexity, graph isomorphism and symmetry detection. The sharp diagonal criterion \(m=n=d\) distinguishes true orbit recovery from a systematic blind spot of 1-WL; the precise number of merged orbit pairs grows unboundedly. The theorem simultaneously supplies a complete positive rigidity classification off the diagonal, not merely another small negative example. The effect is structural, motivated by universal-cover indistinguishability, and holds at all orders.

## Limits and literature risk

No claim is made about iteration-depth formulas, arbitrary cactus graphs, colored graphs, or absolute identification. This is not independent validation. Stronger unpublished or differently named work on equitable partitions or graph coverings may already contain the classification.
