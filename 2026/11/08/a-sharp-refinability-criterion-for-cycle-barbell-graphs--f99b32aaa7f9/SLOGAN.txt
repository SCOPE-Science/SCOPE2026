For a graph formed by joining two cycles of lengths \(m,n\ge3\) by a path of length \(d\ge1\), stable 1-WL colors coincide with automorphism orbits **except precisely when** \(m=n=d\). On that diagonal, refinement merges the distinct cycle and bridge orbits at each of \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) distances, leaving exactly \(1+\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) stable color classes.
