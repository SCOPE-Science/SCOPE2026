For a detuned quantum dimer with continuous capture and fixed sink rate \(2J\), the first-absorption-time coefficient of variation is uniquely minimized by positive dephasing precisely when \(|\Delta|/J>\sqrt{2\sqrt{21}-9}\). Whenever this fluctuation optimum is positive it lies strictly above the mean-time-optimal dephasing, and their dimensionless difference tends to \(2\) at large detuning.
