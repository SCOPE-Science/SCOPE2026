For any two nonorthogonal independent rows, the sharp worst-case two-step mean-square contraction under fixed relaxed Kaczmarz and uniform row marginals is the maximum of two explicit affine functions of the repeat/alternate coupling. At sufficiently strong overrelaxation, the unique optimal policy deliberately repeats a row with positive probability; for overlap \(c=1/2\) and relaxation \(h=3/2\), the optimal switching probability is \(20/27\) rather than \(1\).
