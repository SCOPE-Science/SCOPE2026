For a unit-edge-rate continuous-time walk on a clique with a pendant path, Poisson resetting to the gateway speeds arrival at the path endpoint only when the path has at most two edges: the two-edge case has the exact optimal rate \(\sqrt{m-1}-1\) when the clique size \(m\ge3\). Every path of three or more edges is instead strictly harmed by any positive reset rate, regardless of the clique size.
