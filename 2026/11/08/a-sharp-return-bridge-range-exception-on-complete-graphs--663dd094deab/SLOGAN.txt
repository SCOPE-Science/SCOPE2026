On every complete graph \(K_n\) with \(n\ge3\), conditioning a simple random walk to return at time \(t\ge2\) reduces its expected visited-vertex count—except on the triangle at odd times, when the mean increases by exactly \(2^{1-t}\). For odd \(t\) and \(n\ge t+1\), neither the open nor the closed range stochastically dominates the other.
