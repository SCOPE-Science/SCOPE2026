For the quadratic-reformulated, positive-root SAV-BE scheme, the auxiliary variable can stay positive and its modified energy can decay while the numerical state converges away from the unique minimizer of a strongly convex quadratic. The permanent first-sign-reversal branch has the exact unshifted threshold \(a\tau=(11+3\sqrt{17})/4\), and a nonsingular counterexample persists for every \(0<C\le1/2\) at \(a=\phi_0=1\) and \(\tau=100\).
