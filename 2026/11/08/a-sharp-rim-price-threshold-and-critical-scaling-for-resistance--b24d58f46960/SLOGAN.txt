For a resistance-optimal wheel with independently adjustable edges, equal spoke prices and rim price \(c\), every rim edge is unfunded exactly when \(c\ge2+2/m\), where \(m\) is the number of rim vertices. The sharp switch has a nontrivial finite-size boundary layer: when \(c=2+\kappa/m\), the optimized Kirchhoff-index gain over the no-rim design, rescaled by \(m^2\), tends to \((2-\kappa)_+^2/8\).
