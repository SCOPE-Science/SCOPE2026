# Review of the exact noisy-voter law on complete bipartite graphs

## Correctness
PASS. The finite connected stochastic chain has a unique stationary distribution at every positive resetting rate. Symmetry reduces the exact stationary two-spin generator to a solvable linear system, including correct partner-copy contributions on cross-part pairs; its solution yields the stated rational variance. Strict differentiation establishes finite-size optimal partitions, while the harmonic effective-size bounds give the if-and-only-if self-averaging criterion. The standalone independent count-process calculation verifies 48 exact small cases and 714 rational assertions. Neither a finite experiment nor a log is substituted for the analytical infinite-family proof.

## Originality
PASS with residual priority risk. The 2023 motivating article studies switching all-to-all noisy-voter dynamics; the 2016 work contains the known general moment equations and an annealed approximation; the 2018 work uses pair approximations; the 2020 complete-bipartite paper treats noise-free quasi-stationarity; and the 2021 asymptotic work uses a degree-weighted rather than unweighted observable. The compared results do not state the all-size exact bipartite variance together with the sharp bounded-core versus growing-core criterion. The broad general moment equations are acknowledged; deriving those equations is not claimed to be novel. An unindexed equivalent or a stronger previously published exact theorem remains a genuine priority risk.

## Value
PASS. The exact behavior resolves whether a growing population averages away individual noise when all communication passes between two groups. It identifies the precise structural condition: the smaller group, not total population size, must grow. At any fixed positive noise rate, the star and other bounded-core networks retain macroscopic global-opinion variance as the total population diverges, in contrast to balanced large networks. The exact pair-correlation contrast additionally quantifies how network architecture redistributes statistical dependence. These are natural, reproducible benchmark statements rather than arbitrary parameter slices.

## Limits and closest literature
The theorem is only about two-opinion continuous-time copying plus unbiased resetting, fixed positive noise, complete bipartite graphs and unweighted global magnetization. It does not assert a limiting stationary distribution or a modality transition. Relevant inspected sources: Caligiuri–Galla (arXiv:2305.19096), Carro–Toral–San Miguel (arXiv:1602.06935), Peralta et al. (arXiv:1807.04615), Ben-Ari et al. (arXiv:2004.10187), and Pymar–Rivera (arXiv:2112.01478).
