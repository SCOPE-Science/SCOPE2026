When every ecological function needs two surviving species and at least two of three functions must persist, seven species are the first budget for which the globally best allocation changes with survival probability: \((1,3,3)\) below \(p=1/2\), and \((2,2,3)\) above it. For every larger species budget, a two-effective-guild arrangement beats a balanced three-guild arrangement at low survival probability, but loses to it near certain survival.
