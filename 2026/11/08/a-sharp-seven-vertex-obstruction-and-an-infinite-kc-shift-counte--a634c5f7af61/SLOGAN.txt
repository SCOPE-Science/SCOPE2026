A proper Kelmans–Csikvári shift from the path \(P_{L+5}\) to the three-arm spider \(S(2,2,L)\) strictly **raises** the expected random greedy matching size for every \(L\ge2\), and the first such increase occurs at seven vertices with exact gap \(1/9\). Other proper shifts strictly lower the same expectation, so neither monotonic direction holds on the tree-shift poset.
