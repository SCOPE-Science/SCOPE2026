For a two-hub network with heterogeneous prices on its mirrored two-edge routes, the globally unique Kirchhoff-index optimizer installs the direct hub bridge exactly when its unit-conductance price falls below the explicit budget-independent threshold \(d_{\mathrm{crit}}=4(m+2)/\rho\). The active regime has closed radical conductances and an exact resistance optimum; with equal route prices, the bridge's critical price relative to a spoke price decays as \(8/m^2\).
