# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact 15-state even-degree stationary moment recurrence through order four follows from the generator of a stable linear oscillator coupled to a symmetric two-state Markov driver. Symbolic elimination yields the displayed rational variance and fourth moment; subtraction produces a single explicit sign polynomial with a positive denominator. The quadratic-root argument establishes the sharp boundary for all strictly positive parameters, including equality. The accompanying checker reproduces the symbolic identities and independently compares them with 160 direct full-generator numerical solves. Finite checks supplement, but do not replace, the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualification

Dutta et al (arXiv:2406.06120) provide this exact inertial run-and-tumble model and second moments, not the stationary position fourth-kurtosis sign diagram. Caprini and Marconi (arXiv:2009.14032) study the Gaussian colored-force oscillator and its two-time covariance; this cannot determine the telegraph fourth cumulant. Patel and Chaudhuri (arXiv:2404.01107) compute fourth moments for a different multi-dimensional active Brownian orientation process. Pawula (Phys. Rev. A 35, 3102, 1987) describes general moments of dichotomous-noise-driven oscillators, but only its abstract was accessible; a specialized equivalent theorem could exist. Literature searches and accessible research-record comparisons revealed no exact covering sign classification. This is not proof of publication priority.

## Value — PASS

A sharp fourth-cumulant boundary is a natural diagnostic of stationary non-Gaussianity in an experimentally motivated inertial active-matter model. The universal necessary activity threshold, explicit stiffness crossover and exact positive-kurtosis examples distinguish telegraph and Gaussian active forcing beyond all second-order statistics. A zero fourth cumulant does not turn the bounded stationary law into a Gaussian. This provides a mathematically exact test of the interpretation of trapped-particle position fluctuations, not a routine restatement of known second moments.

## Limitations

One-dimensional, symmetric telegraph forcing, positive finite parameters, no additive thermal noise, stationary one-time fourth cumulant. No inference about finite-sample estimation or full density modality. Generic dichotomous-oscillator moment literature has an unresolved full-text comparison.
