In an exactly specified finite chain-binomial SIR epidemic, the first two new-case counts switch from positive to negative covariance at one sharp transmission threshold. With \(N=n+1\) people, the threshold obeys \(np_*^2=\tfrac12(\log n+\log\log n-\log2)+o(1)\), revealing a depletion-driven dependence reversal absent from the fixed-contact-intensity branching limit.
