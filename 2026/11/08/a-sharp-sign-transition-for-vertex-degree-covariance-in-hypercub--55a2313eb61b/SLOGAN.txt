In a uniform spanning tree of the binary hypercube, degrees of **adjacent** vertices have positive covariance in every dimension \(d\ge3\), while degrees at Hamming distance at least two always have negative covariance. The adjacent covariance is exactly \(\frac{(2^d-2d)(2^d(d-4)+2d+4)}{4^d d(d-1)(d-2)}\), revealing a sharp local reversal of dependence in the degree field.
