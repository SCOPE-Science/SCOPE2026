For a Cayley sum of coordinate boxes over a \(d\)-simplex, one explicit row-by-row inequality determines exactly when its Euclidean inradius attains the simplex-projection bound \(1/(d+\sqrt d)\), and in that case all optimal centers form an explicit box. Every entrywise-positive integral box-Cayley polytope with \(d\ge2\) has this same radius and a full-dimensional fiber-center box, while Ferroni's sparse two-parameter family has a unique incenter only at the sharp point \((\alpha,\beta)=(1,1)\).
