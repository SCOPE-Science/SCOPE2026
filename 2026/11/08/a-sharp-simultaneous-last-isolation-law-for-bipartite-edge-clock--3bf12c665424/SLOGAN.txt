When every potential bipartite edge has its own exponential arrival clock, the last isolated vertices on the two sides disappear simultaneously with probability asymptotic to \(1/[4n\cosh^2(\kappa/2)]\) in the critical imbalance window \((a-b)\log n/n\to\kappa\). Their ordinary completion times have competing independent Gumbel limits, but a synchronized finish has a distinct conditional gamma law.
