Gaussian-weighted Fock dilations \(f(z)\mapsto e^{tz^2/2}f(az)\) exhibit a sharp compact-to-noncompact transition at \(t=1-a^2\): their full subcritical singular spectrum is geometric, and the critical operator has exact essential norm \(a^{-1/2}\). As the threshold is approached, a \((1-a^2-t)^{-1/2}\)-width singular-value layer has an explicit locally uniform exponential profile and sharp counting constant.
