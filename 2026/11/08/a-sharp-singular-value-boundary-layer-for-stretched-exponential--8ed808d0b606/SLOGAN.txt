For a compact unilateral shift with weights \(e^{-c(j+1)^\alpha}\), generalized Aluthge iteration has a sharp normalized singular-value transition at \(\alpha=1\): the largest singular value asymptotically carries all Schatten mass above the threshold, a fixed fraction at it, and a vanishing fraction below it. In the stretched regime \(0<\alpha<1\), the effective number of leading singular values grows at the exact rate \(t^{1-\alpha}n^{1-\alpha}/(pc\alpha)\), with an exponential boundary-layer profile.
