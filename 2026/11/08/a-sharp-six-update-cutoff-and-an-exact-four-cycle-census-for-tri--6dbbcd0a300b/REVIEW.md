# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact four-state rule gives a multiplicity-independent support map on 3375 triples. The finite certificate checks every triple for eventual four-step support return and four-step identity transport on every occupied color, implying the all-size identity \(F^{10}=F^6\). The same certificate obtains all 16 periodic-support coefficients and proves the absence of primitive-two cycles. Surjection counting is justified by inclusion–exclusion, not extrapolation. A separate direct-state implementation checks 592704 configurations across 27 shore-size triples and agrees exactly. The all-zero fixed point is unique. The six-step example on \(K_{1,1,2}\) confirms sharpness.

## Originality — PASS, with historical qualification

The most relevant full-text theoretical source, Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff (2016), addresses three-color excitable dynamics on arbitrary graphs and does not supply the four-color tripartite exact orbit polynomial. Fisch–Gravner (1995) treats one-dimensional neighborhoods and stable periodic objects, not complete tripartite dynamics. Related finite-graph results on three-state tripartite or four-state bipartite systems do not imply the current three-shore, four-state coefficients. The comparisons included equivalent names and stronger-coverage possibilities, not only title matches. The 1993 Durrett–Griffeath source was available at abstract level only; older unindexed equivalences remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact infinite-family orbit census and sharp global stabilization bound distinguish three-community excitation from two-community and three-state systems. The symmetric onto-coloring polynomial is a complete structural invariant of recurrent dynamics under this natural local rule, useful as a benchmark for changes in coupling or refractory length. No assertion is made for other numbers of colors, other thresholds, or arbitrary graph families.
