For three independent points sampled from a smooth strictly convex plane curve, the probability of triangle area at most \(t\) is asymptotic to \(3\,4^{2/3}B(1/3,1/3)t^{2/3}\int f^3\kappa^{-2/3}\,ds\). The unique density minimizing this rare-degeneracy coefficient is normalized affine arclength \(f\propto\kappa^{1/3}\); among uniform-arclength samples of fixed-perimeter curves, the circle is the unique minimizer.
