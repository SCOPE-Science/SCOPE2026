For every spatial period \(n\ge5\), the symmetric Nagumo lattice has a heterogeneous stationary pattern of least period exactly \(n\) precisely when \(0<d<1/[16\sin^2(\pi/n)]\); at and above this threshold all \(n\)-periodic equilibria are constant. The result gives an exact diffusion boundary for primitive spatial patterns without asserting their stability or uniqueness.
