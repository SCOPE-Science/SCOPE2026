For the exact logistic Game of Life, all phase-inverting two-level oscillations are characterized by an explicit Moore-neighbor cut-degree condition. The homogeneous oscillation occupies \(2/9\le\lambda\le6/11\), while stable nonuniform two-wide stripe oscillations begin sharply at \(\lambda>2/3\) on compatible periodic tori; at \(\lambda>6/7\), every such oscillation is encoded by a \(\{\pm1\}\)-valued adjacency eigenvector with eigenvalue \(2\).
