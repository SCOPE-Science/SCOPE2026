For the hyper-permutahedron formed by summing every \(k\)-coordinate simplex in \(n\) variables, the exact minimum Euclidean width is a normalized minimum mixed-hyperedge cut, and every minimizing normal has only two coordinate levels. If \(k/\sqrt n\to c>0\), the normalized width tends to \(\min\{c,2\}\): singleton cuts are eventually the unique minimizing type for \(c<2\), while balanced cuts are eventually the unique type for \(c>2\).
