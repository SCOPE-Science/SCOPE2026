Uniform-point intrinsic distances on a flat Möbius band are strictly stochastically smaller than on the cylinder made from the same rectangle, although both have identical area, total boundary length, and flat local geometry. Their distance CDFs agree exactly through radius \(a/2\), then separate with the sharp \((r-a/2)^{3/2}\) onset coefficient \(4/(3b\sqrt a)\), and agree again only once both are saturated.
