# Review: a sharp synchrony--rescue threshold for joint occupancy

## Correctness — PASS

The finite-state generator follows from independent immigration, ordinary local extinction, donor-preserving rescue and a convex interpolation of independent and common catastrophe clocks at *fixed per-site catastrophe hazard*. Solving the two independent stationary balance equations and normalizing gives the displayed rational law; its denominator is strictly positive at both synchronization endpoints and hence throughout the interval. Exact differentiation proves the three monotonicity and sharp-threshold statements, including all equalities. The independent exact-rational four-state verifier passed 5,000 diagnostic assertions; those tests are corroboration and are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)

Closest comparisons: Johst and Drechsler (2003) analyze disturbance correlation and mean time to irreversible extinction in spatially explicit dynamic landscapes, rather than stationary joint occupancy with continuing immigration. Wilcox, Cairns and Possingham (2006) analyze dynamic habitat disturbance and quasistationarity, not a reviewed exact fixed-hazard three-statistic classification. Keeling (2000), inspected in full, uses population-size moment closure and dispersal coupling. Cornell and Ovaskainen (2008), inspected in its author manuscript, analyze correlated creation of habitat and explicitly omit spatially correlated extinctions. These inspected statements do not imply the new fixed-hazard threshold. Searches for equivalent formulations, broader exact coverage, exact tabulations and implication dominance were performed; failed searches alone are not novelty proof. The complete older Johst and Wilcox papers were unavailable, so potential implicit coverage and broader historical priority remain risks.

## Value — PASS

The distinction between the stationary probability of at least one occupied habitat and the probability of both occupied is biologically meaningful. A universal decline in the former coexists with a regime-dependent improvement in the latter; the threshold is sharp and natural in the rescue/immigration/extinction rates. This is a complete analytic benchmark for two patches, not a case-specific numerical gain or a claim about general landscapes.

## Scientific limitations

The result assumes two identical binary patches, constant positive immigration and memoryless clearing catastrophes. Closed metapopulation extinction times, more patches, delayed or partial disturbance and field inference are outside scope. Independent historical-priority and mathematical audit have not occurred.
