In a two-patch metapopulation with a fixed per-patch catastrophe hazard, synchronized disasters always reduce the stationary chance that at least one patch is occupied, yet they **increase** the chance both are occupied when rescue colonization \(c<\mu+\kappa+\lambda\), and **decrease** it when \(c>\mu+\kappa+\lambda\). At equality, joint occupancy is exactly invariant under every degree of catastrophe synchronization.
