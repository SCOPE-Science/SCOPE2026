Ten vertices are necessary and sufficient for a rooted-tree parking process whose best destination root is not an endpoint of a diameter: with four cars the unique witness is a double hub carrying a central pendant, whose success count is \(9290\) versus \(9263\) at any diameter endpoint. More generally, for a two-hub tree with \(a,b\ge3\) outer leaves at its hubs, the central pendant is the unique best root for four cars, by exact polynomial gaps including \(27(a-2)\) and \(27(b-2)\) against the diameter endpoints.
