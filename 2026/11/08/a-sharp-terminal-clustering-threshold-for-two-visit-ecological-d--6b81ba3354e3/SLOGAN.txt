Two independent imperfect ecological surveys of a site initially empty should both occur at the deadline if the deadline is short, but a longer horizon has a unique strictly better staggered plan: the second survey stays terminal and the first moves to \((H+H_c)/2\), where \(H_c=(c+e)^{-1}\log((1-da)/(d(1-a)))\) and \(a=c/(c+e)\). The transition is exact for a reversible two-state colonization–extinction process, and the extra detection probability above terminal clustering is an explicit positive-part square.
