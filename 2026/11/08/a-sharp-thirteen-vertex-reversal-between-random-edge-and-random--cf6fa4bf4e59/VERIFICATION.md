# Reproducibility and limits

Run from the directory containing `verify.py`:

```bash
python3 verify.py
```

No external libraries, downloads, random seeds or network access are needed. Exact rational first-step recurrences are implemented with `fractions.Fraction`. Unlabeled trees are generated by appending one leaf to all representatives from the preceding size and deduplicating using the canonical tree-center code. Both bipartition orientations are evaluated.

The checked numbers of nonisomorphic trees for sizes one through thirteen are \(1,1,1,2,3,6,11,23,47,106,235,551,1301\). Of 4,576 oriented cases through thirteen vertices, all 1,974 cases through twelve have nonpositive random-edge-minus-random-vertex expectation, and precisely one of the 2,602 order-thirteen orientations has positive gap, \(1/6480\). The program also verifies the exact spider identities for the first six arm counts and independently enumerates all edge priority permutations at arm counts one through three (including 362,880 orders at three arms) and all job/local-neighbor priorities at arm counts one through four (including 46,080 priority profiles at four arms). These are exact finite arithmetic computations, not simulation.

**Analytic checks:** every peripheral job has a private leaf; failure of the center under job-order priorities requires the center to be last and every peripheral job to choose its inner neighbor; failure under edge-order priorities requires the internal edge of every three-edge arm to be the earliest of its three edges. Independence is across disjoint arm edge-priority lists, not across already accepted edges. Thus the law holds for all arm counts, whereas the enumerated minimality only concerns trees of order at most thirteen.

The optional independent cross-check against another unlabeled-tree generator was performed through thirteen vertices and reproduced all counts and canonical isomorphism classes. It is not a premise of the standalone certificate. This verification supplies reproducibility, not an independent mathematical audit.
