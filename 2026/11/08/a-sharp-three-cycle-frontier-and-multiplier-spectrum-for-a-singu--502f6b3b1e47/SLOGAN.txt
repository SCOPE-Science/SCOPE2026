For the singular logistic branch \(f_\mu(x)=\mu x(1-x)/(\mu-x)\) with \(\mu<-1/3\), the exact first appearance of real three-cycles occurs at \(\mu=-1/2\): no three-cycle exists below it, one neutral three-cycle exists at the threshold, and precisely two three-cycles exist above it. Their closed-form multipliers give a unique narrow attracting window, and the onset is the sharp threshold for the existence of every positive least period on an invariant real interval.
