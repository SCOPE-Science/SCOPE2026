For localization on growing symmetric phase-space annuli with the first Hermite Gabor window, the maximizing Hermite mode undergoes a unique sharp transition at annular half-width \(b_*=1.524046518925874\ldots\): two detached asymptotic modes dominate below the threshold, three modes tie at the limiting threshold, and the centered mode dominates above it. The transition and optimal energy are determined exactly by a Gaussian second-moment interval integral and a unique scalar transcendental root.
