For uniform-feasible-edge greedy matching on the cocktail-party graph \(K_{2n}\setminus M_n\), the exact probability of leaving a forbidden vertex pair unmatched satisfies \(p_n=3/(4n)+O((\log n)/n^2)\), with a rigorous all-\(n\) error bound and an exact finite recurrence. The \(3/4\) constant gives a \(3/2\)-fold amplification over the forbidden-pair probability of an unbiased uniformly chosen pair.
