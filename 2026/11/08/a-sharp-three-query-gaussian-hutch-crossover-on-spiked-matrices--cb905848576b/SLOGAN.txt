For the original three-query Gaussian Hutch++ estimator on a one-spike positive-definite matrix, the variance grows only linearly in the spike ratio, with an exact dimension-dependent coefficient. In the first dimension where three generic queries do not span the matrix, a unique sharp spike ratio \(\kappa_*=4.56842704056585\ldots\) separates superiority of three-probe Gaussian Hutchinson from superiority of Hutch++.
