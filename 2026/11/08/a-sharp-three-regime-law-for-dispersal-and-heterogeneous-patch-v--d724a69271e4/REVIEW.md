# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The drift eigenvalues are strictly negative, so a unique stationary law exists. Sum and difference coordinates yield three exact stationary second moments without approximation. Their combination proves the local-variance formula. Its derivative reduces to a strictly increasing one-dimensional ratio, which covers all parameter boundaries and forces the unique interior optimum when it exists. The additional isolated-variance crossover follows from a factored rational expression. The packaged symbolic and exact-rational verifier reproduced all covariance identities and classified representative admissible cases. The theorem is about a linear Gaussian fluctuation model, not population extinction.

**Originality (PASS within examined evidence; historical priority provisional).** Abbott (2011) analyzes dispersal-induced stability with common environmental marginal variance in an AR(1) setting. Wang, Haegeman, and Loreau (2015) use the directly relevant continuous-time Lyapunov method and also impose the same environmental marginal variance in every patch (their Methods, model and covariance matrix assumptions). Their general discussion of heterogeneous growth, capacity and directional dispersal does not itself imply a sharp classification with heterogeneous *noise strengths*. The contribution assessed is the all-parameter environmental-contrast and cross-correlation phase diagram, not the underlying OU method or the known equal-noise monotonic case. An ecological linear-model equivalent might have escaped the searches; the 2015 supplementary appendix could not be read in full.

**Value (PASS).** Spatially unequal environmental volatility is an ordinary and motivated ecological feature, and within-patch variability is an explicit stability proxy in the prior literature. The theorem gives a complete, exact decision rule for when dispersal initially stabilizes a low-noise patch but ultimately destabilizes it, together with a unique finite stabilizing rate and two sharp environmental-noise thresholds. It distinguishes local and aggregate variance without presenting fluctuation variance as an extinction-probability theorem. The result is a closed-form benchmark for population-fluctuation theory, not a policy prescription.
