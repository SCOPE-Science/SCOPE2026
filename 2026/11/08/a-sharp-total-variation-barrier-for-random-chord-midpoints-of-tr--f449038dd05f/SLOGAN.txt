For a chord joining two independent arclength-uniform points of an arbitrary triangle, its midpoint law has a singular boundary component and a four-step area density, and the exact total-variation distance from uniform area is \(3/4-(ab+ac+bc)/(a+b+c)^2\). This distance is at least \(5/12\), with equality only for equilateral triangles, and approaches but never reaches \(1/2\) in a thin-triangle limit.
