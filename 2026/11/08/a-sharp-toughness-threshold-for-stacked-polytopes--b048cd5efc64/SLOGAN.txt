Every stacked convex polytope of dimension \(d\ge4\) has graph toughness strictly exceeding \(1/(d-1)\), and this threshold is the sharp unattained infimum across all stacked polytopes of that dimension. A monotone exposed-facet budget proves the stronger vertex-cut inequality \(\omega(G-S)\le(d-1)|S|-(d-2)\), while Kleetopes of large stacked polytopes asymptotically attain the toughness threshold.
