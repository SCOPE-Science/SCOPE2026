For the exactly unbiased spherical directional-curvature Hessian estimate, orthogonal frames can increase Frobenius risk on traceless saddle quadratics in dimensions two and three, by as much as \(4/3\) and \(45/44\) in risk ratio; from dimension four onward they strictly improve risk for every nonzero symmetric Hessian. The exact reversal is determined by \((\operatorname{tr}H)^2/\|H\|_F^2\), with sharp boundaries \(2/9\) and \(1/42\).
