Summing the exact pair integrals yields the cubic expression (1); the power-sum identity yields a strictly positive balancing certificate (2). The scalar derivative (3), endpoints, and sign comparison (4) prove the all-integer minimization and nonattainment. The limiting-sequence conclusion follows by compactness and positivity of the same identities. A separate exact-rational polygon-clipping implementation independently integrated 85 small configurations and matched the cubic; a symbolic derivative check verified the endpoint difference. These tests are not substituted for the general proof.
