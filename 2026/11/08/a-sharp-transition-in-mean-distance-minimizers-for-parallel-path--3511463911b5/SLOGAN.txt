For two-pole metric networks with \(m\) positive-length parallel paths and fixed total length \(L\), the smallest possible arclength-uniform mean distance undergoes a sharp transition: at \(m=3\) it is uniquely attained by three equal paths, but for every \(m\ge4\) it is an unattained infimum approached only when one path collapses and the other \(m-1\) paths equalize. The exact values are \(5L/27\) and \((2m-3)L/[4(m-1)^2]\), respectively.
