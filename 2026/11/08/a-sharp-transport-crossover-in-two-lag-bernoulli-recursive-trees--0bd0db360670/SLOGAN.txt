For Bernoulli two-lag recursive trees, the finite-walk Kemeny constant divided by \(N^2\) tends to \(1/[3(2-p)]\) at fixed positive one-lag probability \(p\), but switches between \(1/6\) and \(1/3\) across the sparse scale \(Np\asymp1\). Exactly one rare one-lag attachment produces an explicit three-arm penalty \(2abc/(N-1)\), proving that the critical window cannot concentrate at any deterministic value.
