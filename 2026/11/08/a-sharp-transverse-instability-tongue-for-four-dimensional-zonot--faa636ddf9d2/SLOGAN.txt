The volume-log-submodularity failure of a four-dimensional zonotope persists under a fourth-coordinate tilt \(z\) and generator scale \(t\) exactly in the explicit bounded tongue \(-1<z<1/3\), \(0<t<T(z)\), with unique maximum \(T(0)=2\). At unit scale the sharp asymmetric tilt interval is \(2-3\sqrt{2}/2<z<1/13\).
