In the triangle-only two-community social-contagion mean-field model, hyperbolically stable unequal-prevalence equilibria exist at some positive transmission strength precisely when triangle community preference exceeds \(\frac{9+24\sqrt3}{61}\approx0.8290035964\). Below this sharp preference threshold, every unequal-prevalence equilibrium is a saddle, and the boundary itself has no hyperbolic unequal-prevalence sink.
