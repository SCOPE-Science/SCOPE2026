On a star-shaped social network with partially averaging opinions and two stubborn camps, the covariance of any two flexible agents switches from decreasing to increasing under addition of flexible leaves at the unique trust value \(\theta_h=(h+2-\sqrt{h^2+4})/h\), depending only on the total number \(h\) of stubborn leaves. Individual variances decrease at partial trust and leaf-to-leaf correlation increases at every trust level, so more correlation need not mean more covariance.
