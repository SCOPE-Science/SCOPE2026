In a two-component bilinear game, the optimal dependence between two unbiased stochastic-extragradient oracle calls switches sharply from shared samples to antithetic samples at squared stepsize \(2/(a+b)^2\). Antithetic pairing allows a strictly larger mean-square-stable step exactly below the sharp component ratio \((1+\sqrt5+\sqrt{2+2\sqrt5})/2\), although shared sampling always achieves the globally fastest contraction when the step is tuned.
