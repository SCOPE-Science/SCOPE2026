For skew-isometric monotone variational inequalities, no fixed-step golden-ratio mixing parameter beats the asymptotic contraction factor \(1/\sqrt2\); the unique rate-optimal choice is \(c=2\), \(\lambda L=1\). At this choice, the exact error norm is \((k+1)2^{-k/2}\) times its starting value, so the fastest asymptotic tuning initially amplifies error by a sharp factor \(3/2\).
