For two monochromatic tones measured by Gaussian-window spectrogram intensities on two symmetric frequency lines, the globally best linear-lifting stability has a sharp transition at \(a_*=\sqrt{\operatorname{arsinh}(1/\sqrt2)/(4\pi\sqrt3)}\). The unique optimal frequency offset switches from a hyperbolic stationary point to an explicit line-crossing position, and two bins are necessary for universal phaseless identification in this model.
