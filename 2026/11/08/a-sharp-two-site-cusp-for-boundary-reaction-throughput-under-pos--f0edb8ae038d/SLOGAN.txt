Retaining boundary local time across Poisson relocations makes the surface-contact throughput of a reflected Brownian particle exactly \(\sqrt{Dr}\,\mathbb E[e^{-R\sqrt{r/D}}]\). For two reset sites, the ratio \(b/a=3+2\sqrt2\) is a sharp cusp threshold: above it, a unique mixing probability creates two coexisting globally optimal reset rates, with a discontinuous switch as the reset-site weight changes.
