Volume-normalized overrelaxation never improves conditional one-step expected squared error over unit projections, yet can strictly improve the expected two-step error for some initial conditions. For two uniformly sampled nonorthogonal rows, the exact sharp crossover is \(\tau^4+\tau(\tau+2)(1-\rho^2)<1\), where \(\tau\) is the overrelaxation excess and \(\rho\) is the normalized row inner product.
