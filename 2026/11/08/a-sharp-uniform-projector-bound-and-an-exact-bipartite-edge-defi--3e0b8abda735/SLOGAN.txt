The real \(L^2\) projection onto continuous piecewise-affine functions on any finite weighted metric graph has \(L^\infty\) norm strictly below \(3\), with \(3\) the optimal universal supremum even across arbitrary branching and cycles. For bipartite graphs, an exact formula gives the deficit from \(3\) as a positive sum of vertex-atom and edge-sign-cancellation terms, and geometrically graded paths show sharpness.
