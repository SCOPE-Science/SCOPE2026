In a two-orientation run-and-tumble model with bounded, spatially saturating speeds and switching rates, making the switching gradient three times steeper creates a sharp unimodal–trimodal–bimodal stationary transition at \(u=25/4\) and \(u=7\). The three-peak phase is impossible when the switching gradient is no steeper than the speed gradient; this resolves a mode-count assumption left open by the original stationary formula.
