For the two-strategy replicator map with any periodically varying equilibrium target, normalized learning intensity \(0<a\le8\) guarantees one globally attracting periodic response whose mean equals the mean target. At the sharp endpoint, every nonconstant periodic target is exponentially attracting; only the constant half-target produces the explicit \(n^{-1/2}\) relaxation law, while all uniformly interior aperiodic targets still synchronize.
