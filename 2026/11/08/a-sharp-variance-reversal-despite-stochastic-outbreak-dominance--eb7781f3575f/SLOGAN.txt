The two connected cubic contact networks on six vertices are the triangular prism and \(K_{3,3}\). Under independent transmission, the latter has a stochastically larger outbreak for every \(0<p<1\), yet the variance difference changes sign exactly once, at \(p=0.479245294778868\ldots\); this is the sharp first cubic-network comparison where larger outbreaks and smaller outbreak variance can coexist.
