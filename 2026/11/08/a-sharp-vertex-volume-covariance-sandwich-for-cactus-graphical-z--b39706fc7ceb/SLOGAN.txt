For every unit-incidence cactus graphical zonotope, uniform-vertex and uniform-volume covariance are explicit cycle-weighted graph Laplacians satisfying the sharp Loewner sandwich \(\frac{16}{7}C_{\mathrm{vol}}\preccurlyeq C_{\mathrm{vert}}\preccurlyeq 3C_{\mathrm{vol}}\). Equality occurs at the lower constant precisely for bridge-free cacti built from four- and five-cycles, and at the upper constant precisely for trees; the ratio of the positive covariance determinants depends only on cycle lengths and bridge count.
