For exact all-but-one-coordinate quadratic minimization, a nonnegative signed inverse-Hessian Gram with positive inverse row sums has a unique explicit distribution minimizing the sharp expected one-step energy retention. On every nondegenerate three-variable star, determinant-proportional volume sampling is strictly worse than uniform pair sampling; the optimal law improves the near-singular expected energy decrement by a sharp factor tending to \(4/3\) relative to volume sampling.
