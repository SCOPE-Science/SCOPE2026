On a single rootless quasi-Brownian tree, analytic expansive weighted shifts arbitrarily close to the same isometry in every Schatten \(\mathcal S_p\)-norm with \(p>2\) can either have or fail the wandering-subspace property, with the exact switch at \(\beta=1\). The perturbations have the sharp ideal threshold \(p>2\) and obey \(\|S_{N,\beta}-U\|_{\mathcal S_p}^{p}\sim 4N^{1-p/2}/(p-2)\).
