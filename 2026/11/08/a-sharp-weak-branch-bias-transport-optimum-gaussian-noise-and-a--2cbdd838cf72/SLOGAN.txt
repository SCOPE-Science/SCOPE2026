In a ring exclusion process with one side path of length proportional to the ring, scaling the path bias as \(q_L=e^{\lambda/L}\) produces a unique finite current-maximizing bias precisely in the density window \(1/2<\rho<c+1/2\). At that optimum, Gaussian canonical fluctuations generate a strictly positive \(L^{-1}\) excess over the limiting maximum, while distinct backbone sites have an explicit negative covariance of order \(L^{-1}\).
