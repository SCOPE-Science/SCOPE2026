# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

Direct beta-moment integration determines every monomial norm in the specified normalized-area Hilbert space. A shifted generalized binomial series then derives the exact kernel, including its removable value at zero. Since \(\operatorname{Re}(1-s)>0\), the logarithm has a unique branch, so the phase equation yields exactly the stated discrete conjugate root set. The strict radius test gives the ceiling formula at every boundary equality, and the numerator derivative proves simplicity. A standalone standard-library script verifies 659 representative coefficient, root, and threshold cases. Finite checks supplement rather than replace the proof.

Miña-Díaz's 2022 Theorem 1.1 treats Blaschke-factor weights, not the independent Jacobi boundary factor here. Published mathematical-record comparisons for lower fixed Jacobi orders and for the actual Euclidean boundary-distance weight have different hypotheses or do not quantify arbitrary real boundary orders and fixed-basepoint thresholds. The principal historical uncertainty is whether an unlocated general formula for an origin-weighted standard Bergman space already implies the same zero-count staircase. Search noncoverage is not a proof of priority; independent confirmation remains desirable.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Zero-freeness and the number of zeros of each fixed reproducing-kernel section are natural Lu Qi-Keng invariants. This result solves that classification for a continuous unbounded one-parameter family, identifies a sharp global boundary exponent \(4\), and gives every later six-step transition and a sharp zero-free basepoint radius. Unlike merely producing arbitrarily many zeros, it determines every location and multiplicity, including exact boundary exclusions. The formulas are short, but they provide the full all-parameter phase diagram rather than an arbitrary finite instance.

The claim does not extend to general origin powers or arbitrary radial weights. The 2017 and 2022 comparisons establish noncoverage of their inspected stated results, not mathematical independence from every prior source. There is no independent audit.
