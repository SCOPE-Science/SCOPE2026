For the disk Bergman weight \(|z|^2(1-|z|^2)^\beta\), \(\beta>-1\), the kernel is globally zero-free exactly through \(\beta=4\), and every later threshold \(\beta=6j-2\) adds one conjugate pair of simple scalar zeros. More precisely, a kernel section at a point of modulus \(r\) has exactly \(2\max\{0,\lceil(\beta+2)\arcsin(r/2)/\pi\rceil-1\}\) zeros, with explicit locations and sharp basepoint thresholds.
