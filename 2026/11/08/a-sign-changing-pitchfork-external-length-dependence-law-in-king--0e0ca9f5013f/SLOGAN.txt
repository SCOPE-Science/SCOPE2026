In the standard Kingman coalescent, the number of rooted three-leaf pitchforks has a stagewise covariance with singleton ancestral-lineage counts that changes sign exactly at half the ancestral sample size. Its covariance with total external branch length is exactly \(-1/18\) for every \(n\ge5\), in sharp contrast with the positive value \(2/9\) at \(n=4\).
