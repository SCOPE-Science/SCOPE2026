For fixed-grid unbiased stochastic rounding, positive and negative affine contractions with the same fixed point and absolute contraction factor have radically different stationary errors: the positive branch is exactly Bernoulli, while the negative branch has variance asymptotic to \(\{2\theta\}(1-\{2\theta\})/(1-r^2)\) whenever \(2\theta\notin\mathbb Z\). The normalized negative-branch stationary error converges to a Gaussian, even though every unscaled state is integer-valued.
