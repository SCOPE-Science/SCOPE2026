For all \(m\ge3\), \(k\ge3\), and \(\ell\ge2k-1\), the product of the first \(k\) path-eigenvalue factors, evaluated at the **unweighted adjacency matrix** of an equal-arm spider, is an optimal-rank real symmetric matrix whose off-diagonal sign at tree distance \(d\le k\) is exactly \((-1)^{k-d}\). This gives a canonical sign-regular spectral certificate, not a new claim about the previously known scalar zero-forcing or maximum-nullity value.
