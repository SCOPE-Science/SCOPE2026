For independently biased orientations of \(K_{2,n}\), the rooted reachable-vertex count is a binomial law plus an exact signed difference of two other binomial laws, with one sign change and sharp total-variation error \((1-p(1-p))^n(1+o(1))\) for fixed interior bias. At fair orientation the reachable count is stochastically smaller than its binomial proxy yet has strictly greater variance, quantifying a rare global reachability bottleneck.
