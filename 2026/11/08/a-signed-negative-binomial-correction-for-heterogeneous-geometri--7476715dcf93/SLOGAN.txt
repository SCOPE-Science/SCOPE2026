For sums of independent heterogeneous geometric counts matched to \(\mathrm{NB}(c,w)\) at the arithmetic-mean parameter, a quadratic signed correction is exactly a scaled difference between a two-step-shifted \(\mathrm{NB}(c+2,w)\) law and \(\mathrm{NB}(c,w)\). The remaining total variation error has an explicit global cubic-plus-quartic bound, and the uncorrected quadratic onset has an exact negative-binomial-tail coefficient.
