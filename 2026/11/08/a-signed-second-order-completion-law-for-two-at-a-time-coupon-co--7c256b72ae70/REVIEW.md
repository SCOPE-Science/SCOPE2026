# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The exact inclusion-exclusion identity follows from missing a specified set; a uniform factorial-tail truncation justifies summing the second-order term. Three alternating exponential identities evaluate the coefficient; at the exceptional center the logarithmic coefficient cancels. An independent finite-state missing-count recurrence agrees on all tested small instances. The proof, not the finite checks, establishes the asymptotic domain.

**Originality (O): PASS, with historical-priority risk.** Schilling–Henze (2021) give the general group-drawing Poisson limit; Betken–Thäle (2022/2023) and earlier Barbour–Holst–Janson give order \((\log n)/n\) approximation bounds, not an evaluated signed coefficient for the zero-missing event nor a corresponding unique cancellation. Pair-group completion, coupon/occupancy terminology and exact inclusion-exclusion equivalents were compared. A relevant earlier paper is accessible only in abstract form; old literature might contain a particular expansion.

**Value (V): PASS.** Completion is the natural event defining the stopping-time distribution. The signed correction gives the direction and magnitude of finite-size bias and reveals a unique centering at which the usual logarithmic error disappears. This is a concrete refinement not provided by a general total-variation bound, without claiming an improvement for the entire count distribution.

**Limitations.** Fixed group size two, bounded Gumbel window, canonical rather than moment-matched Poisson centering. Independent historical priority verification remains open.
