For two-option temporal voting with \(a\) exclusive supporters of one option, \(b\) of the other, and \(c\) flexible supporters, PJR, EJR and EJR+ are satisfied by an independent stationary lottery exactly when its binomial selection count lies between two explicit Hare-style quotas. Without flexible supporters the best success probability vanishes like \(L^{-1/2}\), but with just one flexible supporter and balanced blocs it has the sharp joint limit \(2\Phi(\sqrt\tau)-1\) at horizon \(L/n^2\to\tau\), with exact optimal coin bias \(1/2\).
