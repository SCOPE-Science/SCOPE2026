Adding the single edge between the hubs of \(K_{2,n}\) creates exactly \(6n\,2^n\) immediate primitive-three parallel chip-firing configurations, with an explicit two-band binomial mass polynomial; without that edge there are none. Conditioned on these configurations, chip mass divided by \(n\) converges to an equally weighted mixture of the uniforms on \([5/2,3]\) and \([4,9/2]\).
