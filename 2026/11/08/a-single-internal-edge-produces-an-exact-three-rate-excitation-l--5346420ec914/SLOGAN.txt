On a complete bipartite graph with one internal edge, three-colour cyclic excitation has exactly the asymptotic activity levels \(0\), \(3/4\), and \(1\), with explicit counts for every pair of shore sizes; Greenberg–Hastings excitation has exactly one-third those rates. When the added edge joins the only two vertices of its shore, it removes the intermediate activity phase entirely, upgrading all previously oscillating configurations to maximal activity and activating exactly six additional configurations.
