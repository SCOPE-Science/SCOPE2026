Adding a single irreversible dimer-deposition edge inside one part of \(K_{a,b}\) paradoxically reduces the final dimer count when \(a\le b\), leaves it unchanged at \(a=b+1\), and increases it when \(a\ge b+2\). Its exact occupation probability has a macroscopic population-imbalance transition and a sharp square-root critical window: if \((a-b)/\sqrt a\to c\), then \(aP_{a,b}(\lambda)\to\lambda+(\max\{c,0\})^2\) at every fixed \(\lambda>0\).
