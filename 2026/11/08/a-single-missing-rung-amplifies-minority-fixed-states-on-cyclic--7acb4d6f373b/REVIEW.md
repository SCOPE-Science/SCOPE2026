# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The defective graph has exactly two degree-two vertices and otherwise degree three. Normal-rung fixedness and the deleted-rung tie-retention condition are different, and both are explicitly represented in two exact 16-state local transfer matrices. The trace identities correctly impose either a normal or row-swapped seam. The exact integer matrix annihilator proves an order-eight recurrence for all lengths from three onward, and eight exact initial values prove the Fibonacci-plus-period-six formulas without relying on finite extrapolation. Binet asymptotics and previously established intact-ladder full-flip counts then yield the common amplification constant. Two separate checkers reproduce the matrix identity and exhaustively simulate 699008 labelled spin assignments.

## Originality

PASS, qualified. The old intact-ladder majority full-flip formulas, including their Lucas and twisted no-triples counts, were compared at the implication level and are explicitly treated as prior facts. They do not apply to the changed degree-two local constraint created by deleting one rung. Targeted searches for missing-rung ladders, antiferromagnetic single-bond defects, minority fixed states and one-spin-flip metastability did not identify the stated two exact perturbed traces or their universal amplification. Takata et al. (2016) and Cen et al. (2022) concern intact Ising ladder benchmarks and ground-state searches; Jacobs and Coram (1987) concern random-bond ferromagnetic metastability, not the exact deterministic missing-rung count. The complete 2016 main-paper PDF was inaccessible, and historical equivalence is a residual risk; searches are not proof of absolute priority.

## Value

PASS. The result answers a natural defect-susceptibility question: the effect of removing just one edge on the number of local anti-coordination equilibria in an infinite sequence of physically motivated ladder networks. The limiting factor \(3/\sqrt5\) is strictly greater than one and does not vanish with system size, while the two distinct global seam topologies have explicit bounded residue corrections. The local defect transfer is genuinely different from the known intact graph and the exact statement is not simply a new presentation of its old Lucas formula. The result is a complete all-length local-stability invariant, independently motivated by robustness of metastable states under a one-edge perturbation.

## Closest literature and limitations

The intact cubic-ladder majority full-flip census is a previously known baseline; the new claim concerns the perturbed graph with tie-retaining degree-two endpoints. Readable sections of the open-access 2022 benchmark paper address both seam topologies but not this single-edge fixed-state susceptibility. The 2016 source is accessible via its dated arXiv record and indexed physical study, with partial full-text limitations. Random-bond Ising metastability papers do not establish the present exact labelled finite-size population. No basin or optimization-algorithm probability statement is implied. Independent validation is outstanding.
