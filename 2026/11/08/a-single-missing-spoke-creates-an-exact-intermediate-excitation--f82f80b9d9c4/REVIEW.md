# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The domain is every labelled one-spoke-deleted wheel with at least four rim vertices, simultaneous three-colour threshold-one CCA and GHM rules. The proof imports exactly the established finite-graph cycle-integral criterion and activity formula of Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff, constructs a complete integer cycle-space basis of surviving triangles plus the one new quadrilateral, and derives all possible cycle means. A direct six-pair verification of the edge coboundary identity shows the CCA/GHM factor-three rate relation. The quadrilateral table and the three-state transfer matrix are explicit; their matrix powers give integer-valued counts. No infinite-family period or transient bound is inferred from numerical tests. Independent exhaustive transition and simple-cycle implementations agree for all stated finite test cases.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The 2016 published preprint and its 2018 journal version are a decisive general theorem about cycle-integral activity and conservativity; those statements are expressly not new. They do not supply the missing-spoke three-rate support or the three exact initial-state populations. A pre-existing exact extinction census for the **intact** wheel concerns a different graph; it does not imply the middle-rate population after deleting an edge. Doba's 2018 finite-graph paper contains another broad maximum-cycle-density result, but its full text was inaccessible; the abstract does not justify claiming that it excludes all specialized enumerations. Search comparisons included wheel, one-missing-spoke wheel, incomplete wheel, cyclic cellular automata, Greenberg–Hastings, four-cycle activity, and transfer-matrix synonyms. No explicit earlier covering population formula was identified. Historical priority risk persists, and absence from search results is not claimed as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A single deleted hub–rim edge creates a genuinely new allowed asymptotic rate, rather than only changing the number of states in pre-existing classes. Both nonequivalent three-state automata have **the same partition of initial colourings** but their activities scale by three. The intermediate phase has an exact positive-size integer census for every order and a limiting conditional prevalence of approximately 27.6% among states without rainbow surviving triangles. This is a natural quantitative structural perturbation and an exact initial-condition invariant relevant to network-based excitable dynamics; it is not a disconnected finite computation or a routine count of known attractor states.

## Closest literature and limitations
Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff (arXiv:1610.07320v1, Theorem 3) is mathematically necessary and supplies the general dynamical reduction. Doba (2018, DOI:10.25088/ComplexSystems.27.2.101) may contain relevant further discussion; only its abstract could be inspected. Detailed independent historical-priority work and independent scientific validation remain outstanding. No claims are made about primitive periods or time to enter periodic states for arbitrary sizes.
