Every geometrically smooth Frobenius-nonclassical plane sextic over \(\mathbb F_4\) is \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb F_4)\)-equivalent to \(XYZ(X^3+Y^3+Z^3)+X^6+\alpha Y^6+(\alpha+1)Z^6=0\), with \(\alpha^2+\alpha+1=0\). Exactly 960 normalized smooth equations occur, forming one projective orbit with rational stabilizer order 63.
