Adding just one degree-one vertex to a Boolean cube makes neighbor-parity dynamics reach its eventual fixed point or two-cycle within exactly three updates in the worst case, and a fraction \(1-q^{-1}\) of all states needs all three updates over \(\mathbb F_q\) in characteristic two. The unmodified odd-dimensional cube is reversible and the unmodified even-dimensional cube is square-zero, yet this single local defect gives both parity classes the same sharp transient clock and completely different recurrent-space sizes.
