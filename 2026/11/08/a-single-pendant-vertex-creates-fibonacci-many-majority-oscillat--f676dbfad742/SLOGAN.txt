On a binary majority cycle, adding exactly one private leaf leaves all fixed states unchanged but creates \(2f_{n-2}+c_{n\bmod6}\) primitive period-two states, where \(f_j\) is Fibonacci and \(c_j\) has period six with residues \((4,2,2,-2,0,0)\). Oscillating states grow at rate \(\varphi^n\), although the undecorated cycle has at most two.
