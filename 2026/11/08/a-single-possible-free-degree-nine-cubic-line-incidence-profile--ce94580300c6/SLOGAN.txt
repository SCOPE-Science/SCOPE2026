For free complex degree-nine arrangements of smooth cubics and lines admitting only \(A_1\), \(D_4\), and \(A_5\) singularities, the sole possible component/incidence profile is two cubics plus three lines with \((n_2,n_3,t_5)=(3,0,9)\). Thus the one-cubic/six-line case is impossible and three of four previously open numerical profiles are excluded; existence of the remaining profile is not asserted.
