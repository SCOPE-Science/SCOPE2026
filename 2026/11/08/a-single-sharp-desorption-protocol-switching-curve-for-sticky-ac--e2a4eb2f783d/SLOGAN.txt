For a sticky-wall run-and-tumble searcher, the better of two desorption protocols is determined exactly by a single universal curve \((\alpha x_0/v,r x_0/v)=(\theta/\sinh\theta,\theta\tanh(\theta/2))\), independently of the bound-state waiting law and the common permanent-absorption probability. Starting outward always favors launch from the wall, whereas starting inward admits precisely one preference reversal as either bulk reset rate or tumble rate varies.
