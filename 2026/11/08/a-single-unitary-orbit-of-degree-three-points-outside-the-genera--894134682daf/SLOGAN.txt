For the Hermitian surface over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) with every geometric generator removed, the first points occur in extension degree three: they form exactly one \(\operatorname{PGU}(4,q)\)-orbit with cyclic stabilizer of order \(q^3+1\). Their unique Frobenius-span planes partition the orbit into smooth Hermitian-curve sections and give \(q^6(q^2-1)(q^4-1)\) points over \(\mathbb F_{q^6}\).
