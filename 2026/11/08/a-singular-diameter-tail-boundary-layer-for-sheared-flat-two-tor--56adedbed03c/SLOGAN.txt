For unit-area sheared flat two-tori, the probability of geodesic distance within \(\varepsilon\) of the intrinsic diameter is asymptotic to \(C_\delta\varepsilon^2\), with an exact coefficient \(C_\delta=2(\delta^{-1}+(1-\delta)^{-1}+(1-\delta+\delta^2)^{-1})\) that diverges as the shear vanishes, although the square torus has coefficient \(4\). The singularity has an exact nontrivial crossover when \(\varepsilon=t\delta^2\), with probabilities of order \(\delta^3\).
