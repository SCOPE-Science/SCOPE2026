For an equilateral three-site base and one axial site, the Voronoi nerve of the product ball \(\bigl(\|u\|_2^p+|w|^p\bigr)^{1/p}\le1\) is complete for every \(p>1\), but the bicone endpoint \(p=1\) loses exactly the base triangle and the full tetrahedron when \(0<h<R\). The unique fourfold Voronoi point escapes to infinity at rate \(\frac{R}{e}(R/h)^{1/(p-1)}\), and an explicit four-translate configuration at the endpoint has a provably positive failure of Voronoi-nerve inclusion-exclusion.
