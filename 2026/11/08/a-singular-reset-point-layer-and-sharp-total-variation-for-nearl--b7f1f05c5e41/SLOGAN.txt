For Poisson Brownian resetting that retains a fraction \(c\) of position, the invariant law differs from the complete-reset Laplace law by exactly \(c^2(1+o(1))\) in total variation as \(c\downarrow0\). The density has a universal reset-point layer of width \(c\), a unique sign crossing at distance asymptotic to \(c\log(1/c)/\sqrt{r/D}\), and a signed probability deficit that concentrates at the reset point.
