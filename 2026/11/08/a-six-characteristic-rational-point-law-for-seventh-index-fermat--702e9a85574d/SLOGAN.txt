For the smooth Fermat surface \(X_0^{(q-1)/7}+\cdots+X_3^{(q-1)/7}=0\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), with \(7\mid q-1\), rational points exist in exactly six characteristics: \(2,3,29,43,71,547\). An exact cyclotomic-norm certificate gives a polynomial in \(d=(q-1)/7\) for the number of rational points in each exceptional characteristic and zero everywhere else.
