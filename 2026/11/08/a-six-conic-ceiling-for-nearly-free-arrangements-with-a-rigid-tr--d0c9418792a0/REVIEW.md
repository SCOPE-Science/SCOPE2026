# Scientific review: nearly free smooth-conic arrangements

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact hypotheses of the two external theorems match the smooth complex conic arrangements under consideration: the reduced divisor has exclusively the specified quasihomogeneous singularities. Pokora's near-free Tjurina identity and Dimca–Sernesi syzygy lower bound give \(r=k\) for \(5\le k\le8\). Bézout and local Tjurina numbers yield \(n_2=t_5+2t_7-n_3\) and \(t_3=k(k-1)-n_3-2t_5-3t_7\). Substitution into Zieliński's published orbifold inequality gives the linear restriction used in the proof. The paper proves the eight-conic and seven-conic exclusions by an exact bound and an integer-divisibility argument; it proves the triple-free boundary by a separate sharp linear optimization. The triangle-free contact graph argument uses the full intersection multiplicity four for two conics at an \(A_7\) point. The four-conic witness has exact polynomial intersection identities and a verified minimal Jacobian syzygy degree three. Both supplied checkers completed successfully on the supplied files. Risk: the conclusion depends on the published orbifold inequality and the general near-free characterization, which are cited but are not independently reproved here.

**Originality — PASS under the examined literature; historical priority not certified.** Pokora, arXiv:2303.08584, Remark 2.7, explicitly reports only the bound \(k\le8\) and unknown constructions for \(k\in\{5,6,7,8\}\). Zieliński (DOI 10.1007/s12215-023-00890-8, Theorem 1.1) proves the general incidence inequality but does not apply the nearly free identity to exclude seven/eight components or isolate the unique five-component incidence profile. The published 2026 plus-one-generated conic paper gives a distinct general incidence inequality and different classification results. The independent combination of inequalities, the integer obstruction at seven, the five-component rigidity and its \(K_{2,3}\) contact graph do not follow from these sources as stated. The four-conic sharpness example is a verified witness of a previously studied construction, not claimed as novel. A differently phrased or unpublished earlier combination of these constraints remains a priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A structural, independently quantified near-free existence question raised explicitly in the motivating literature is reduced from eight candidate components to six, with six impossible without ordinary triple points. The five-conic no-triples boundary has exactly one admissible weak-combinatorial type and a forced extremal contact graph; this is a substantive reduction of the geometric realization problem. The strict nodes-plus-full-contact bound is attained by an explicit near-free arrangement. No broad realization theorem for six conics or for the hypothetical five-conic boundary is asserted.
