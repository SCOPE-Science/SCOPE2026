# Scientific review: triple-free near-free smooth-conic arrangements

## Correctness

The corrected theorem concerns only the triple-free class. For five and six components, the near-free syzygy bound forces minimal relation degree equal to the component count. Pairwise Bézout and local Tjurina contributions give the displayed elimination identities. The published orbifold inequality bounds the remaining contact counts and excludes a six-component triple-free arrangement. Exact integer arithmetic forces the unique five-component basket (13,0,0,1,6). A projective normal form for two full order-four contacts to one smooth conic excludes a triangle in the A7 contact graph; the equality case of Mantel forces K2,3. The four-component witness and its Jacobian relation degree are an already-published sharpness example. The unrepaired broader six-conic bound is true but was not original.

## Originality

The universal six-conic ceiling, including the seven- and eight-component exclusion, is established in the earlier 2 November theorem *A six-component ceiling for nearly free smooth-conic arrangements*. The four-conic witness and minimal-Jacobian-syzygy data are also previously published. Those statements receive no new priority claim. The corrected surviving result is restricted to the strict triple-free five-conic boundary, its uniquely necessary singularity basket, the forced K2,3 contact graph, and the resulting sharp nodes-plus-A7 component maximum. The inspected source theorems give neither the full triple-free count nor the contact-graph necessity. The 2023 Pokora and Zieliński papers supply the near-free and orbifold inequalities as premises. Earlier unindexed five-conic realization or contact-graph literature remains a priority risk.

## Value

The known six-conic bound leaves the smallest new realizability candidates at five and six components. Imposing the natural no-three-branch constraint completely excludes six and forces a uniquely specified five-component weak type and contact graph. These are substantial structural necessary conditions on the open realizability problem, not a numerical restatement of the already-known ceiling. Existence for the five-component type is not asserted.

## Limitations

The restrictions rely on published near-free and orbifold inequalities. The K2,3 contact graph and numerical basket are only necessary, not sufficient for geometric realization. The four-conic example is prior. No classification with ordinary triple points is asserted, and the corrected claim does not imply a sharp overall six-conic bound. Finite necessary-condition enumeration corroborates, but does not replace, the proof.
