A nearly free arrangement of smooth complex plane conics with only \(A_1,A_3,D_4,A_5,A_7\) singularities has at most six components, improving the previously established ceiling of eight. Without ordinary triples there are at most five, and the five-conic boundary would require exactly 13 nodes, one \(A_5\), six \(A_7\) points and the contact graph \(K_{2,3}\); with only nodes and \(A_7\) points, the sharp maximum is four.
