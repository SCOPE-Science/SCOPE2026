Without ordinary triple points, a nearly free arrangement of smooth conics with only A1, A3, D4, A5 and A7 singularities has at most five components. The five-conic boundary forces 13 nodes, one A5 contact, six A7 contacts, and the K2,3 contact graph. For nodes and A7 alone the sharp bound is four. The previously published general six-conic ceiling is not claimed as new.
