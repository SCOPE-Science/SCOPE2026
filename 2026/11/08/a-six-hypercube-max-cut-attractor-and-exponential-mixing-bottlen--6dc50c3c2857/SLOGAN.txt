On the dense graph \(K_{n,n,n}\), asynchronous closed-neighborhood minority dynamics reaches one recurrent class consisting of exactly \(6(2^n-1)\) maximum-cut configurations arranged as six joined hypercubes. The class has frequent local flips, but its mixing time is greater than \(\lfloor 3\cdot2^n/4\rfloor\): a connected attractor can still exhibit an exponential phase bottleneck.
