The Cayley–Hamilton matrix embedding proves cubic nilpotence for every radical element, including characteristic two, not merely for the degree-one pencil. The degree-filtration rank argument and explicit degree-one-to-degree-three matrix give precisely six possible pairs of ranks \((\operatorname{rank}L_f,\operatorname{rank}L_f^2)\); these determine all Jordan partitions. The nonsingular projective conic count yields the exact rank-three stratum, and cyclic right-ideal generator counts from Nakayama yield the proper principal right-ideal census. The full radical was independently enumerated over \(\mathbb F_4\), with a separate exhaustive \(J^2\) check over \(\mathbb F_7\). Risks are limited to unchecked larger-field numerical samples and scheme-theoretic multiplicities not claimed here.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
Actual searches compared six-stratum Jordan multiplication, principal right-ideal counts, radical square-zero quadrics and quantum complete intersection rank varieties against published research records, the accessible prior claim record, and three relevant primary papers. Benson–Erdmann–Holloway (2007) and Bergh–Erdmann–Jorgensen (2019) concern restriction along nonzero linear generator combinations, not the complete radical, while Bergh–Erdmann (2007) concerns cohomology. A distinct earlier commutative characteristic-three group-algebra census has the same numerical distribution, but does not logically entail the result for this noncommutative algebra in characteristic different from three. The 2007 article was materially inspected in its accessible indexed text, but full-page inspection of all appendices was not completed. Broader unnoticed rank-variety/ideal-lattice formulas remain a genuine priority risk; publication priority is not certified.

The complete distribution, irreducible square-zero cone, and exact count of distinct principal right ideals supply three natural geometric and arithmetic invariants uniformly for all admissible finite fields. The unexpected numerical match with the commutative modular characteristic-three model motivates comparing deformation phenomena without asserting an unsupported equivalence.
