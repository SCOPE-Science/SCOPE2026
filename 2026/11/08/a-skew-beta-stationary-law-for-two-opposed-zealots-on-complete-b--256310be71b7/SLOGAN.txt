With one frozen positive voter in the first part and one frozen negative voter in the second, the stationary positive fractions on \(K_{m,n}\) converge jointly to a common \(\operatorname{Beta}(2\theta,2(1-\theta))\) variable when \(n/(m+n)\to\theta\in(0,1)\). Balanced parts give a uniform limit, while unequal parts yield a skew, non-self-averaging stationary opinion distribution despite equal numbers of opposite zealots.
