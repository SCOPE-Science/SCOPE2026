# Scientific review: rational tangent lines and the dual quartic

## Correctness — PASS
All hypotheses and quantifiers are explicit: odd prime powers, parameters for which \(T^7+kT^3-1\) has no rational root, and tangencies at *rational* smooth points only. The tangent-vector base locus is exactly the rational root condition. Restricting the degree-\(q+3\) equation to each rational line produces the Frobenius binary form of degree \(q+1\) times a nonzero binary quadratic, bounding rational tangency multiplicities by two. Its determinant is the stated quartic polynomial. The quartic's geometric smoothness is established uniformly including characteristics three and seven by an elementary derivative calculation, independent of numerical tests; genus three follows from the degree-genus formula. Double counting proves the exact spectrum and Hasse–Weil gives the claimed deviation. The accompanying verifier independently enumerates all prime-field instances for 41 root-free parameters up to characteristic 29; it is corroboration, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Asgarli–Ghioca (arXiv:2307.03072, original July 2023) already supply the plane-filling pencil, rational-smoothness criterion, no-rational-line-component theorem, and conjectural geometric smoothness. Their original article's statements and Sections 2–4 were compared against the actual new theorem: they do not identify the rational tangent-line spectrum, the precise discriminant quartic, its geometric smoothness, or a genus-three Hasse bound for the tangent spectrum. Their separate tangent-filling article (arXiv:2302.13420) analyzes coverage of *points* by tangent lines, a different invariant from the number of *lines* with 0, 1, or 2 rational contacts. Homma's 2020 article classifies components of minimum-degree \(q+2\) plane-filling curves, not this \(q+3\) pencil or its quartic line-discriminant. Exact-claim and alias searches yielded no identified dominating prior result. Relevant historical claims on singular-pencil parameters were compared by implication, not just wording: rational singularity moments do not imply the quartic's point counts or geometric smoothness. A possibly older equivalent discriminant calculation remains a priority risk, so this is not an independent historical-uniqueness certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The new invariant is natural: the distribution of rational lines by rational tangent contact for an extremal plane-filling curve. The classification across every odd prime power is unexpectedly governed by a geometrically smooth genus-three quartic, even though the original curves have degrees growing with \(q\). The exact equality of uncovered lines and double-tangent lines, and its \(O(\sqrt q)\) refinement with explicit constant, are structural information absent from rational-smoothness and parameter-existence results. The construction does not merely vary a known finite-field parameter or recompute a source table.
