For the Asgarli–Ghioca degree-\(q+3\) plane-filling pencil over every odd \(\mathbb F_q\) with rationally smooth parameter, the rational lines tangent at exactly one rational point are precisely the points of an explicit smooth genus-three quartic in the dual plane. Consequently the numbers of lines with zero and two rational tangency points coincide exactly and differ from \(q^2/2\) by at most \(3\sqrt q\).
