For smooth, globally Lipschitz sample losses, reusing a random gradient sample in both stages of stochastic Heun leaves an exact first-order weak bias against its proposed noise-only modified diffusion: \(\lim_{n\to\infty}n(\mathbb E X_n-\mathbb E Z_1)=1/(2e)\). This sharply separates the shared-sample integrator's stated weak-order-two guarantee from the behavior of the separate adaptive optimization algorithm.
