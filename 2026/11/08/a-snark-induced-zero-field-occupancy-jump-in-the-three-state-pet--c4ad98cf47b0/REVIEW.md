# Scientific review of the Petersen-line three-state Potts ground-state diagram

## Correctness — PASS

The local triangle energy inequalities give three global affine lower bounds on the number of equal-color edge pairs. The sole exceptional preferred-color occupancy, five, is settled by the Petersen graph's odd two-factor obstruction and the parity argument excluding a single defective triangle. Sixteen explicit color strings meet all sector lower bounds. Two independent exact programs, one ternary depth-first and one by disjoint subset partitions, traverse all 14,348,907 states and agree on all minimum energies and exact multiplicities. The field envelope is then a finite affine minimization. The proof and computations are for a single finite graph, not an infinite-system assertion.

## Originality — PASS, provisional

The graph-coloring/Potts correspondence (Boettcher–Percus, arXiv:cond-mat/0402282), arbitrary-graph field-dependent partition functions (Shrock–Xu, arXiv:1009.1182; Ellis-Monaghan–Moffatt, arXiv:1005.5470), and the Petersen snark's two odd five-cycle obstruction (Candráková–Lukoťka, arXiv:1311.0512) are established antecedents. The closest inspected Petersen Potts study colors the original ten-vertex graph, not its fifteen-vertex line graph. No examined source gives the explicit sixteen-sector minimum/degeneracy table or the singular favored-color occupancy selection, but an unindexed graph-specific evaluation is possible. General partition-function formulas are fully credited and are not represented as novel here.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The calculation resolves the natural three-state field response of a minimal non-three-edge-colorable cubic graph. The exactly calculated occupancy jump, critical coexistence degeneracies, and two equal-size types of zero-field minimizing colorings provide a useful sharp benchmark for frustration and graph coloring at finite size. No large-system nonanalyticity or finite-temperature claim is inferred.

## Scope and limitations

Homogeneous positive coupling, uniform real field selecting one labeled color, a fixed finite 15-vertex line graph, and zero temperature only. Publication priority is subject to unindexed or inaccessible evaluations; independent external scientific confirmation has not occurred.
