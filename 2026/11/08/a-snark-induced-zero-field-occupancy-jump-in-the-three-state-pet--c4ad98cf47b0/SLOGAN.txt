The three-state Potts antiferromagnet on the \(15\)-site Petersen line graph has a sharp zero-field occupancy jump: an arbitrarily weak negative field selects four preferred-color sites, while an arbitrarily weak positive field selects six. Its exact all-field ground energy has four crossings at \(h/J=-2,0,2,4\), with all sector minima and ground-state degeneracies determined by a local edge-coloring obstruction and exhaustive finite counts.
