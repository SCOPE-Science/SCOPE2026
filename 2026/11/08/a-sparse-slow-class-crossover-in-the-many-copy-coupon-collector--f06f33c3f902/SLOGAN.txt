In a many-copy coupon collector with \(n\) common types and a sparse class of rarer types, the sharp number of rare types needed to compete for the final coupon is \(n^\delta(\log n)^\eta\), with explicit exponents. At that boundary, the centered completion time has a non-Gumbel two-exponential extreme-value law, and the last coupon has a nondegenerate class label described by an independent, two-color Poisson extremal process.
