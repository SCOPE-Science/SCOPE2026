# Review of the semi-Markov spectral discriminator

**Correctness — PASS.** For positive harmonic stiffness, the stationary OU variance and time covariance are uniformly integrable. Solving the two birth-time variance recursions, integrating future attenuation by a fresh-sojourn renewal recursion, and averaging the stationary within-sojourn reward yields the exact formula. The 4-point example uses exact rational arithmetic for all Laplace transforms and gives a strictly positive rational multiple of \(1/\log 2\). Independent Markov-resolvent and constant-stiffness tests agree. The one-sided covariance integral is not a bare finite-sampling estimate.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Frydel (2026) proves the stationary and thermodynamic formulas for precisely this alternating trap; its full-text sections on moments and energetics do not state a two-time stationary covariance or zero-frequency spectral separation. Behme–Di Tella–Sideris derive autocovariance for finite-state Markov modulation, which covers exponential sojourns but not nonexponential semi-Markov sojourns without an additional age state and a different analysis. The 2025 Gupta–Klapp stiffness is continuous OU, not alternating renewal; Biroli et al. evaluate interparticle stationary correlations for memoryless switching. Published-result and accessible preceding-claim searches found no statement implying the displayed arbitrary-renewal formula or the matching-statics, separated-spectrum witness. A general renewal-response treatment may contain an implicit route to the answer, so undiscovered implicit coverage remains a priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** Stationary second moments and mean work are the central measurable observables in the motivating paper. The theorem proves that these observables fail to determine the slow-frequency position fluctuations, even if measured state by state, and identifies precisely the additional holding-time information required. The finite four-point witness isolates this effect while controlling mean residence, second-rate Laplace transforms, energy and work exactly. This is a motivated physical inverse-observation boundary rather than an arbitrary numeric slice.

Both stiffnesses must remain strictly positive, and the laws have finite means. Only the zero-frequency covariance integral is established.
