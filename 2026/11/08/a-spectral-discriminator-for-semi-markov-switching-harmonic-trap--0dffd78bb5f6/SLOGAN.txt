For a Brownian particle in a semi-Markov two-stiffness harmonic trap, the exact zero-frequency position spectrum depends on residence-time Laplace transforms at both \(\kappa_i\) and \(2\kappa_i\), whereas stationary second moments and average switching work depend only on the latter. Two explicit protocols have identical occupation fractions, state-conditioned variances, potential energy and work yet differ in integrated covariance by exactly \(341004/(919602257\log 2)\).
