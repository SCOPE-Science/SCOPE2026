For every norm-continuous pairwise commuting normal field, the closed joint \(q\)-numerical range is exactly a compact convex slice of complex spectral measures with prescribed mass \(q\) and total variation at most one; finite-dimensional fields admit an exact coefficient formula without closure. Every interior-overlap supporting direction reduces to a tilted smallest-enclosing-circle problem, and at \(q=0\) to the ordinary minimum enclosing circle of the spectral image.
