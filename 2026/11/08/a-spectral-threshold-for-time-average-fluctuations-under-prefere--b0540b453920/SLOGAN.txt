A uniformly history-resetting confined particle can retain its Gibbs one-time law at every instant yet have unusually persistent time-average fluctuations. For any equilibrium eigenobservable, the finite-time variance is an exact Beta expectation, with a sharp transition from \(t^{-1}\) to \(t^{-1}\log t\) to \(t^{-2\lambda/(r+\lambda)}\) as the relocation rate crosses the eigenvalue.
