For a uniformly random orientation of unit-valued parallel goods on a multistar, the probability of zero-inclusive EFX has an exact binomial formula and a sharp square-root transition when one spoke dominates: if the other spokes have total multiplicity asymptotic to \(\gamma\sqrt m\), the probability tends to \(2^{-(k-1)}(\Phi(\gamma/2)-1/2)\). Below this window the success probability is asymptotic to \(2^{-(k-1)}R/(2\sqrt{2\pi m})\); above it, the probability approaches \(2^{-k}\).
