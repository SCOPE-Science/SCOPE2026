For three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on an open path with \(n\) vertices, the number of initial configurations that first become all-rest after the sharp maximum \(n+1\) updates has generating function \(\sqrt{(1+z)/(1-3z)}\). The resulting probability is asymptotic to \(2/(3\sqrt{3\pi n})\); the same coefficient sequence was already known combinatorially, but its dynamical maximal-extinction interpretation is the point of the result.
