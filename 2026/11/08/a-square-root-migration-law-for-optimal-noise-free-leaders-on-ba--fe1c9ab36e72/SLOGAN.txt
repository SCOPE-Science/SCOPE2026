For noise-free leader placement on the balanced split graph \(K_n\vee\overline{K_n}\), the optimal number of clique leaders changes on the sharp budget scale \(k\sim c\sqrt n\): its ratio to \(\sqrt n\) tends to \(c\), \(2-c\), or \(0\) as \(c\le1\), \(1<c<2\), or \(c\ge2\). A finite exact witness proves that optimal four- and seven-leader sets on the same graph need not be nested.
