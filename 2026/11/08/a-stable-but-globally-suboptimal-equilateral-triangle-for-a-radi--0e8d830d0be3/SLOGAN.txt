An equilateral triangle inscribed in the unit disk is a strict local maximizer of \(\int_T\|x\|^{p}\,dx\) for every \(0\le p<2\), yet at \(p=7/4\) an explicitly specified isosceles triangle has greater radial weighted area by more than \(0.00011\). Therefore a nonempty open interval of exponents exhibits geometrically stable but globally suboptimal regular triangles.
