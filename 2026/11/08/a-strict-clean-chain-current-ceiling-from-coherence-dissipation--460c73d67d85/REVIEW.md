# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The exact \(N^2\)-equation, not a truncated covariance ansatz, implies a positive-definite dissipative Hilbert–Schmidt balance. Boundary conservation gives the normalized bond current and endpoint diagonal values. Subtracting the unavoidable imaginary nearest-neighbor coherences leaves the nonnegative residual form \(\mathcal E(X)\), and \(\mathcal E(X)=j(1-j/j_0)\). Vanishing current contradicts the boundary conditions; vanishing excess energy forces spatially constant onsite potential. A directly constructed constant-profile stationary matrix attains the ceiling. Nine exact rational and 120 randomized finite tests were run successfully. They are checks of algebra, not general proof.

## Originality

PASS, with priority uncertainty. Jacob et al., arXiv:2407.21715, derive the source model, exact tiny-chain responses and the clean baseline, and use a tridiagonal approximation for larger tilted systems. Žnidarič and Horvat, arXiv:1207.5952, supply the more general disorder response equations, which are acknowledged as existing premises; Žnidarič, arXiv:1005.1271, supplies the homogeneous-chain solution. The new assessed statement is the exact nonnegative residual-coherence decomposition and strict all-size clean-current ceiling at fixed dephasing. An equivalent theorem under another formulation has not been excluded with independent certainty; no title-based search is treated as a novelty proof. The earlier four-site dephasing optimum studies varying the *noise rate* at fixed field and does not supersede the claim varying the *field* at fixed noise rate.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The inequality and iff equality characterization give a universal nonperturbative benchmark for Wannier–Stark dephasing transport beyond the published small-chain exact results. The positive residual-coherence identity connects a transport decrement directly to observable stationary off-diagonal correlations. This constrains physically motivated transport approximations, rather than reporting a small arbitrary numerical example.

## Limitations

Only the specified finite, noninteracting, symmetric-reservoir, uniformly dephased nearest-neighbor chain is covered. There is no conclusion about whether increasing dephasing improves current at nonzero tilt, nor about zero dephasing, nonlinear interactions, long-range hopping or unequal boundary rates. The constant-profile formula is prior work, and formal external validation has not occurred. A different published general coercivity theorem could affect priority.
