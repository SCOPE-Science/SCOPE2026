At the upper synchronized two-cycle stability boundary \(a_*=(11-4\sqrt6)/5\) of a quadratic Chebyshev lattice, a subcritical checkerboard pitchfork produces exactly two distinct unstable spatially alternating two-cycles on the lower-coupling side of every fixed even ring. Their squared checkerboard amplitude satisfies \(d_0^2=1.10076997586\ldots\,(a_*-a)+O((a_*-a)^2)\), while their unique expanding return multiplier is \(1+16\sqrt6\,(a_*-a)+O((a_*-a)^2)\).
