For smooth characteristic-three genus-three curves \(y^2=x^7+a x^5+b x^3+c x\), an order-four automorphism forces exactly two Jacobian Newton polygons: almost ordinary when \(a^3c+b^3\ne0\), and supersingular (never superspecial) when \(a^3c+b^3=0\). The supersingular locus is a smooth irreducible divisor with an explicit punctured-line normal-form parametrization, while the \(a\)-number is uniformly one.
