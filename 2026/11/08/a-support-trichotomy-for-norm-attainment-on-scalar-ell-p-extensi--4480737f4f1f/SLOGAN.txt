For every finite \(1\le p<\infty\), a natural real operator on \(c_0\oplus_p\mathbb R\) has norm \((2^p+1)^{1/p}\) and a three-way support classification: finite support makes the operator attain, infinite proper support makes only its second adjoint attain, and full support makes neither attain. The spaces are \(2^{1/p}\)-equivalent to classical \(c_0\), so universal second-adjoint norm attainment can fail under arbitrarily small renormings even though it holds at the \(p=\infty\) endpoint.
