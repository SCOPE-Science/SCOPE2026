# Self-review

## Correctness (PASS)

The exact logarithmic correspondence between antiphase primitive two-cycles and solutions of \(A_L(u)=cw\), \(A_L(w)=cu\) was reconstructed and checked in both directions. The threshold root is unique and lies strictly inside the existence region. The fixed equal-amplitude branch crosses the reduced derivative \(-1\) there. For every lower coupling, the positive-to-negative sign change of \(f(f(v))-v\), with an explicit first-exit fallback, supplies an unequal two-cycle; the species-exchanged image gives a third original-map cycle. Two 65-digit examples reproduce all three pairs. Numerical examples are not the proof of the infinite-parameter theorem.

## Originality (PASS within inspected evidence)

Goldar et al. (2026, DOI 10.1155/ddns/7255117) discuss period-2 solutions and individual stability but do not provide this quantitative three-antiphase-orbit guarantee. Kulakov, Neverova and Frisman (2022, DOI 10.3390/math10071076) give substantial prior work on phase multistability and pitchfork scenarios, largely using numerical nullcline intersection, without the implicit analytic threshold or all-\(L\) interval in the inspected passages. Prior exact antiphase existence conditions yield one orbit, not a lower bound of three. An older unlocated analytic multiplicity theorem remains a priority risk; searches cannot establish uniqueness of the result.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The amplitude symmetry breaking changes the count of **distinct orbits**, not merely the phase labels on one orbit. The theorem guarantees a physically meaningful multiple-cycle configuration for every positive coupling below a determined nonlinear critical curve, rather than at a chosen numerical parameter point. It provides a nontrivial exact benchmark for phase multistability and subsequent basin analysis.

## Limitations

Only a lower bound of three is proved. Stability, complete orbit census, asymmetric growth or coupling, and nonexistence above the scalar multiplier crossing are open. The result has undergone this review only, with no independent audit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
