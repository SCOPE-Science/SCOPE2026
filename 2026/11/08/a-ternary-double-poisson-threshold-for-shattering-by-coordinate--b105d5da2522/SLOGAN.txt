For random \(m\)-point supports, positive coordinate-equality concepts have a sharp shattering transition whose limiting law is \(\exp(-e^{-c}-\tfrac12e^{-2c})\) for the ternary alphabet, but \(\exp(-e^{-c})\) for binary and every fixed alphabet of size at least four under their respective critical dimension scalings. The exceptional ternary factor comes from two asymptotically independent kinds of missing near-full traces.
