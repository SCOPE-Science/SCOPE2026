For a maximal ranked poset with layer sizes \(m_1,\ldots,m_k\), the excess centered Euclidean inertia of its order polytope over its Ehrhart-equivalent chain polytope is exactly \(e_3(m_1,\ldots,m_k)/((N+1)^2(N+2))\), where \(N=\sum m_i\). The gap is invariant under reordering layers, vanishes precisely at height at most two, and is sharply maximized at fixed \(N,k\) by balanced layers.
