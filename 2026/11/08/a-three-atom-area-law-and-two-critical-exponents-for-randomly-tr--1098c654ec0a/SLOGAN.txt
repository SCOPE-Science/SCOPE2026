For a uniformly translated unit square lattice, the area of the convex hull of lattice points inside a disk of radius \(1/\sqrt2<R<1\) has an exact three-atom law on \(\{0,\tfrac12,1\}\), determined by a diagonal circle-lens area and a fourfold disk-intersection area. At the first nondegenerate radius, triangular hulls emerge with exponent \(3/2\), whereas square hulls emerge with exponent \(2\); the complete probabilities and moments are explicit.
