For a Haar-random central section of the unit cube, its polygon type is controlled sharply by the width measured in the same normal direction: widths below \(\sqrt2\) force quadrilaterals, widths above \(\sqrt{8/3}\) force hexagons, and the intermediate conditional quadrilateral probability is exactly \(\frac3\pi\arccos\bigl(s/(2\sqrt{6-2s^2})\bigr)\). This yields the complete joint width–vertex-count law, not just either previously known marginal.
