Two opposed populations can each have a strict Condorcet winner while a robust maximal lottery, under full shift ambiguity, has exactly one-, two-, or three-alternative support according to an explicit margin trichotomy. More sharply, an intermediate increase in population-weight uncertainty can make the pooled electorate's strict Condorcet loser the unique robust choice, with an exact critical radius \(r_c=(v-w)/(u-v-w)\).
