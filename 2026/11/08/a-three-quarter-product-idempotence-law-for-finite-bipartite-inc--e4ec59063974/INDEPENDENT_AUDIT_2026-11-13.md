# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Every diagonal of an idempotent is a bit. For each comparable bipartite pair, independent free off-diagonal entries occur precisely when the corresponding endpoint diagonal bits differ. Local idempotency of EF requires (ur+vs-1)(u y+x s)=0; FE adds (ur+vs-1)(r x+y v)=0. Their linear ranks over arbitrary finite-field characteristic yield the printed 4×4 W and W_both; independent edge multiplication and multinomial left-state counts prove both exact finite-field Phi formulas. For balanced n+n, the two opposite bit patterns have q^{n²} choices each; any other pattern with nearest Hamming distance d<=n loses at least nd/2 free coordinates, giving error O(nq^{-n/2}). Conditional on dominant orientations the two product probabilities are (3+q^{-n²})/4 and (1+q^{-n²})/2. Independent literal small algebra enumeration and direct rank-table construction over q=2,3,5 corroborate; no arbitrary-height posets or varying-q limit proved.
