In the full incidence monoid of a complete bipartite poset over any finite field, a four-state enumerator counts exactly which ordered pairs of idempotents have idempotent products in one or both multiplication orders. For independent uniform idempotents on balanced parts of size \(n\), these probabilities tend respectively to \(3/4\) and \(1/2\), with error \(O(nq^{-n/2})\) at each fixed field order \(q\).
