For a Gaussian rank-\(k\) range finder on a matrix with one singular-value spike, the squared Frobenius excess over optimal rank-\(k\) approximation has an exact beta-mixture distribution. As the spike grows, its expected excess is linear, logarithmic, or bounded at one, two, or at least three sketch columns without power iteration; each power iteration gives exact polynomial improvements with a persistent two-column logarithmic factor.
