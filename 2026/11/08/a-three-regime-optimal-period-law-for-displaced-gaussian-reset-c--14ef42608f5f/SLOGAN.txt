For a periodically restarted harmonic diffusion with a displaced Gaussian reset state, optimizing the symmetric endpoint-divergence rate exhibits a sharp threshold at squared normalized displacement \(c=2\). As the reset variance ratio \(q\downarrow0\), every optimal dimensionless reset interval scales as \(\sqrt q\) below threshold, as \(q^{1/3}\) at threshold, and converges to a positive constant above it; this concerns endpoint information cost, not total physical dissipation.
