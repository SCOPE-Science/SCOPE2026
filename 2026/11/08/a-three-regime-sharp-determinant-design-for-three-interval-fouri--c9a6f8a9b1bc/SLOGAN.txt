For three equally long intervals starting at \(0\), \(1+\delta\), and \(2+2\delta\), three equally spaced Fourier cosets maximize the worst-fiber Gram determinant at a quarter shift for \(0<\delta<1/2\), an explicit irrational shift at \(\delta=1/2\), and a fifth shift for \(1/2<\delta<1\). The respective sharp determinant values are \(16\), \((9748+1360\sqrt{10})/729\), and \((25+5\sqrt5)/2\); this optimizes the determinant, not the condition number.
