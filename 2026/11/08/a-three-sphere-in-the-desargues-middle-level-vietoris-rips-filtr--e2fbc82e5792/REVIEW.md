# Scientific review: A three-sphere in the Desargues middle-level Vietoris–Rips filtration

## Correctness — PASS

The statement is exact for the labeled 20-vertex graph and all real radius thresholds. For radius three, the complete face poset has \(3200\) nonempty simplices and the verifier replays a deterministic 1599-pair matching leaving precisely one vertex and one three-simplex. Exhaustive orientation of every Hasse cover and topological sorting confirm that the matching is acyclic. The discrete Morse CW theorem therefore proves \(S^3\) integrally (not merely ranks over a finite field). ## Value — PASS

The Desargues graph is the canonical 20-vertex middle-two-level graph of the five-dimensional Boolean cube and a familiar symmetric \(10_3\) incidence configuration. The adjacent-level scale-two classification provides a direct literature-based reason to determine what happens at scale three. The new integral \(S^3\) transition explains where nineteen two-dimensional classes die and a new three-dimensional class appears, while the later nine-sphere stage completes the natural finite persistence problem. It is an exact motivated invariant, not a relabeled exercise, and the exhaustive matching provides structural proof beyond a Betti table.
