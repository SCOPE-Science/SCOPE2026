No exact matching classification was found in the checked index or literature. A differently named or unindexed equivalent theorem remains a priority risk. Lack of a search hit is not proof of novelty.

The exact threshold separates two spatially distributed leaders from common-hub leadership, and an unexpected hub-plus-*any*-other-vertex tie occurs for all larger sizes. The result offers a fully evaluated design rule, not just a numerical sample or a rephrasing of the general optimization criterion.
