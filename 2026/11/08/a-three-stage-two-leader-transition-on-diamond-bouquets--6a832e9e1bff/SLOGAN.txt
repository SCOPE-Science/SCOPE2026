For the diamond bouquet \(K_1\vee(mP_3)\), the optimal noise-free two-leader pair moves through three strict regimes: the two tips of the lone diamond at \(m=1\), the centers of different diamonds at \(m=2\), and the common hub plus any other vertex at every \(m\ge3\). All optimal placements, their counts and exact grounded-Laplacian inverse traces follow from six explicit rational symmetry-class formulas.
