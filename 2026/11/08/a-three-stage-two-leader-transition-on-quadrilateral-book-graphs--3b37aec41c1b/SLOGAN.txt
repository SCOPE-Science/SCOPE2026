Two perfect leaders on the unweighted quadrilateral book \(K_{1,m}\square K_2\) change optimal placement twice: cross-page nonincident outer vertices at \(m=2\), one hub with a nonadjacent outer vertex at \(m=3\), and both common-edge hubs at every \(m\ge4\). Exact six-orbit grounded-Laplacian inverse traces prove these are all minimizers and yield optimum values \(46/21\), \(95/24\), and \(4m/3\), respectively.
