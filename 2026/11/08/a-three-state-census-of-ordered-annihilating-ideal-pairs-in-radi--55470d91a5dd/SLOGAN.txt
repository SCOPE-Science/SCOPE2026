The ordered zero-product two-sided ideal census \(\sum_{IK=0}X^{\dim I}Y^{\dim K}\) of every finite-field radical-square-zero quiver algebra is an explicit three-state vertex partition function with Gaussian-binomial arrow weights. It distinguishes a source fork from its opposite quiver even though their ordinary ideal-dimension polynomials coincide, and counts all directed zero-product pairs rather than only ideals individually.
