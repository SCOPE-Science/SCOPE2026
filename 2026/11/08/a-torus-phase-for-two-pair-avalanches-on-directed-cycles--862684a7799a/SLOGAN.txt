Four chips arranged in two separated adjacent pairs on a forward directed cycle give a three-dimensional avalanche complex that simplicially collapses onto a torus whenever \(d\ge3\) and \(n\ge2d+3\), where the occupied vertices are \(\{0,1,d,d+1\}\). The immediately preceding case \(n=2d+2\) instead has Euler characteristic \(d+1\), giving a sharp one-sided transition.
