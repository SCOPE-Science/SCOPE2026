For a uniform random permutation, the covariance between a record at position \(i\) and an excedance at position \(j\) is strictly negative when \(j<i\), positive on the interior diagonal, and zero when \(j>i\); the complete matrix has explicit rational entries. Summing its rows or columns gives sharp positional sign classifications, while the total record–excedance covariance changes sign exactly between sizes four and five.
