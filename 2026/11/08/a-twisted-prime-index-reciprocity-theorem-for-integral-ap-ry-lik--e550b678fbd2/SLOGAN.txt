For every integral symmetric quadratic or cubic Apéry-like recurrence with nonzero parameter \(c\), the coefficients modulo any odd prime \(p\nmid c\) satisfy \(c^nu_{p-1-n}\equiv u_{p-1}u_n\pmod p\) and \(u_{p-1}^2\equiv1\pmod p\). This proves a previously experimental zero-reflection property for the Almkvist–Zudilin families and gives a full twisted reciprocal-polynomial identity.
