For the unbounded-genus Dickson hyperelliptic family whose geometric automorphism group has order two, the full rational Jacobian group over \(\mathbb F_q\)—not merely its order—is determined: it is \((\mathbb Z/(q+1)\mathbb Z)^g\) when \(q\equiv1\pmod4\), and \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^g\oplus(\mathbb Z/((q+1)/2)\mathbb Z)^g\) when \(q\equiv3\pmod4\). The distinction comes from Weierstrass-point Frobenius orbits and integral \(2\)-adic elementary divisors.
