For all nonnegative integers \(n\) and positive integers \(h\), the reciprocal-binomial sum \(f(n)=\sum_{j=0}^{n}\binom{n}{j}^{-1}\) satisfies \(\nu_2(f(n+h)-f(n))\ge\nu_2(h)-s_2(n)-s_2(n+h)-1\). This proves Davis's 2013 binary-digit-sum Conjecture 1.2 for every dyadic shift and makes its conditional two-adic definability consequence unconditional.
