# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof first reduces every initially periodic configuration to one of the only admissible hub-transition types. For equal flipping hubs, cyclic erosion followed by dilation gives the necessary and sufficient three-one-run condition and the exact two density inequalities; for opposite flipping hubs, the radius-one majority characterization gives balanced fixed words and two alternating words. The marked-run composition formula accounts for every labelled cycle word exactly once. All boundary cases \(n\ge3\), the sharp odd/even existence criterion and the length-five exponential construction are explicit. Two separately written programs reproduce the enumeration on all configurations through \(n=16\); those finite checks are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality

PASS, qualified. Available published-result searches, accessible related mathematical statements and primary-source comparisons did not identify a theorem that entails the exact \(K_2\vee C_n\) population formula and its odd/even onset. Kaaser–Mallmann-Trenn–Natale (arXiv:1508.03519) treats voting-time bounds and contraction of graph symmetries, not initial two-cycle counts. Tosic–Agha (2004) and Nakar–Ron (arXiv:2205.08972) concern one-dimensional cellular automata; their ring-periodicity descriptions supply only one ingredient. Existing one-hub wheel calculations have one fewer universal-neighbor input at each rim vertex and therefore do not entail the three-site opening census. The Goles–Olivos original full text could not be compared, and any unindexed equivalent remains a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The first two adjacent dominating vertices change the dynamical input rule at every rim vertex and introduce two different oscillator mechanisms, not a parameter substitution of the one-hub wheel. An exact census, a sharp delayed odd-size onset at nine, only two fixed states, and an explicit exponentially large periodic subfamily together explain a natural graph-perturbation effect.

## Scope and limitations

These are counts of immediately periodic configurations, not basin sizes, transient durations or a determined all-length entropy. Finite exhaustive checks cover \(3\le n\le16\), whereas the proof covers all \(n\ge3\). Historical priority and external scientific evaluation remain open.
