For the incidence algebra of three fully comparable antichains of equal size over a field of characteristic two, square-one units are exactly the triples of matrices with \(AB=0\), and their first-block rank has an explicit finite-field spectrum. At fixed field size, the centered rank converges to one of two parity-dependent discrete-Gaussian laws, while the associated uniformly sampled three-term complex has vanishing middle homology with probability tending exponentially to one.
