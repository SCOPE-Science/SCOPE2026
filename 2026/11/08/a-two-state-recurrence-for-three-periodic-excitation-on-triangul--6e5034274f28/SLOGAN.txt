For three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on a chain of \(m\) vertex-glued triangles, initially primitive three-periodic states satisfy \(N_1=6\), \(N_2=24\), and \(N_m=4N_{m-1}+N_{m-2}\) for every \(m\geq3\). Their exact generating function is \(6/(1-4z-z^2)\), with growth factor \(2+\sqrt5\) per triangle and \(N_m/3\) distinct three-cycles.
