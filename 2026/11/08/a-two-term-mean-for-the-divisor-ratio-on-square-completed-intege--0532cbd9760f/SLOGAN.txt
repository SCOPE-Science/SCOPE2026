The mean of the divisor-to-unitary-divisor ratio at the least perfect square at or above each integer has an explicit two-term expansion \(K_0X\sqrt{\log X}+K_1X/\sqrt{\log X}+O(X/(\log X)^{3/2})\), where \(K_0>0\), \(K_1<0\), and both constants are given by a convergent Euler product and its derivative. The square-root-logarithmic growth replaces a logarithmic-order formula asserted in the motivating paper.
