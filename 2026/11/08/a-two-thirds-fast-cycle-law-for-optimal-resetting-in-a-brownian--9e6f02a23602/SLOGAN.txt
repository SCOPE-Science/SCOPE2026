For a two-temperature overdamped Brownian Otto protocol with instantaneous origin resetting, every reset rate maximizing the mechanical work per fast cycle grows as \(r_*\sim c_*\tau^{-2/3}\), with an explicit all-parameter coefficient. This noninteger optimal-control law follows from a rigorously optimized competition between incomplete thermal relaxation and excessive reset suppression; reset-actuator work is not included.
