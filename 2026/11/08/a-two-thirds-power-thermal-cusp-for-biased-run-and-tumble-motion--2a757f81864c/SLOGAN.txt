Biasing the two tumble directions of a harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle by \(\varepsilon\) shifts its unique thermal two-peak-to-one-peak transition downward by \(A_\ell^2|\varepsilon|^{2/3}+o(|\varepsilon|^{2/3})\), with a disappearing-peak location scaling as \(A_\ell\operatorname{sgn}(\varepsilon)|\varepsilon|^{1/3}\). The symmetric transition, stationary beta law and Gaussian-convolution representation are known; the asymmetric global classification and explicit cusp coefficient are the assessed extension.
