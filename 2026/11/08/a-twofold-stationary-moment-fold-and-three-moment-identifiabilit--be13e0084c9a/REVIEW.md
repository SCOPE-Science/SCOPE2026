## Mathematical value — PASS

Run and tumble rates govern two physically distinct residence times, but are typically latent when only stationary positions are measured. The theorem rigorously specifies which low-order snapshot data are compatible with the model, shows exactly when variance-plus-kurtosis calibration is intrinsically ambiguous, and proves that a single additional observable—the sixth moment—restores global uniqueness. The exact twofold witness and sharp fold boundary make the distinction operationally meaningful, not an arbitrary moment computation. This is an algebraic identifiability result, not an assertion of finite-sample inference quality or broad statistical optimality.
