For independent exponential first-passage percolation on \(m\) cliques of size \(k+1\) sharing one hub, the hub-rooted flooding time has a standard Gumbel limit after the universal centering \(kT-2\log(k-1)-\log m-\log\log m\), uniformly for every growth regime \(k\ge2\). The extremal petal times, jointly with the independent uniform split between their first and last exploration waits, converge to a marked Poisson point process; a single-edge petal has no double-log correction.
