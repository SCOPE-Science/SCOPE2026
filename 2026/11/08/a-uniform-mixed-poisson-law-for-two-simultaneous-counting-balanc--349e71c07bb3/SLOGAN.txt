For a fixed root of a uniformly colored dense random directed graph, the number of neighbors satisfying two independent exact counting balances converges not to an ordinary Poisson law but to a Poisson law with a uniformly random intensity in \([0,2/\pi]\). Consequently the depth-two counting-modal existence sentence has limiting probability \(1-\frac\pi2(1-e^{-2/\pi})\).
