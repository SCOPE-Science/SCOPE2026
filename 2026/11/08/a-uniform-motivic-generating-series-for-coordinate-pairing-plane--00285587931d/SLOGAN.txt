For every smooth odd-characteristic Fermat cubic with \(2m\) homogeneous coordinates, the reduced union of its coordinate-pairing \((m-1)\)-planes has an exact, dimension-uniform Grothendieck class determined by a formal \(\cosh\)-and-Bessel generating series. This yields the rational-point counts and Hasse--Weil zeta functions of these canonical arrangements over every finite field of characteristic at least five, without asserting that the arrangements include all planes on the cubics.
