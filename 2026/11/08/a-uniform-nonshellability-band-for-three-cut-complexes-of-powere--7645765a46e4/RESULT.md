# A uniform nonshellability band for three-cut complexes of powered cycles

## Finding

**Theorem.** For every pair of integers \(p\ge2\) and \(n\) satisfying
\[
\left\lceil\frac{8(p+1)}3\right\rceil\le n\le4p,
\]
the ordinary three-cut complex \(\Delta_3(C_n^p)\) is **not shellable**. More precisely, the cyclically ordered set
\[
S=\left\{\left\lfloor\frac{jn}{8}\right\rfloor:0\le j\le7\right\}
\]
induces the squared eight-cycle \(C_8^2\) inside the powered cycle \(C_n^p\); its three-cut complex is the link of the face \(V(C_n^p)\setminus S\) in \(\Delta_3(C_n^p)\). The two bounds on \(n\) are also jointly **necessary** for any induced copy of \(C_8^2\) whose vertex cyclic order agrees with that of the host cycle. No claim is made for arbitrarily reordered induced copies.

This gives a uniform infinite subrange of the nonshellability prediction in Conjecture 4.2(i) of Chauhan–Shukla–Vinayak (2024/2025). The known nonshellability of the eight-vertex seed and the general graph-link principle are acknowledged as prior mathematical ingredients; the parameter-uniform induced embedding and resulting infinite-range conclusion are the new assertion.

## Assumptions and scope

All graphs are finite, simple and undirected. The cycle \(C_n\) has vertices \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), and \(C_n^p\) joins distinct vertices when their cycle distance is at most \(p\). The ordinary three-cut complex \(\Delta_3(G)\) is generated by complements of disconnected induced three-vertex subgraphs of \(G\); equivalently, a subset \(F\subseteq V(G)\) is a face exactly if some disconnected induced three-vertex subgraph is disjoint from \(F\). Shellability means ordinary (pure) simplicial shellability. In the stated parameter band, \(n\ge8\) and the complex is nonvoid and pure of dimension \(n-4\).

## Proof

**1. The induced eight-vertex obstruction.** Fix the specified \(n,p\), and write \(v_j=\lfloor jn/8\rfloor\) for \(0\le j\le7\), extended by \(v_{j+8}=v_j+n\). The successive gaps are positive, because \(n\ge8\). For every \(j\), elementary floor inequalities give
\[
v_{j+2}-v_j\le\lceil n/4\rceil\le p,
\qquad
v_{j+3}-v_j\ge\lfloor3n/8\rfloor\ge p+1.
\]
For an index separation of one or two, one arc between the selected vertices therefore has at most \(p\) host-cycle edges, so the vertices are adjacent in \(C_n^p\). For an index separation of three or four, each of the two arcs contains at least three consecutive selected gaps. Their lengths are at least \(p+1\), so the vertices are nonadjacent. Thus the induced graph on \(S\), in cyclic order, is exactly \(C_8^2\).

**2. Exact threshold for cyclic-order-preserving copies.** Conversely, let eight cyclically ordered vertices induce \(C_8^2\), and let \(g_0,\ldots,g_7\) be their positive successive host-cycle gaps, summing to \(n\). Because selected vertices three steps apart are nonadjacent, every sum of three successive gaps is at least \(p+1\). Summing the eight inequalities gives \(3n\ge8(p+1)\). Now take selected vertices two steps apart. Their complementary arc contains six successive gaps, divisible into two three-gap sums, and hence has length at least \(2(p+1)>p\). Their adjacency must therefore use the two-gap arc, so each two-successive-gap sum is at most \(p\). Summing gives \(2n\le8p\). These are precisely the stated lower and upper bounds (with integrality). Step 1 proves sufficiency. The assertion of necessity is restricted to copies respecting cyclic order.

**3. Transferring nonshellability through links.** For any finite graph \(G\) and nonempty induced vertex set \(S\) such that \(\Delta_3(G[S])\) is nonvoid, put \(F=V(G)\setminus S\). Since a disconnected triple exists inside \(S\), \(F\) is a face of \(\Delta_3(G)\). For every \(T\subseteq S\), the membership condition for \(T\) in the link of \(F\) is precisely the existence of a disconnected induced triple in \(S\setminus T\). Thus
\[
\operatorname{lk}_{\Delta_3(G)}(F)=\Delta_3(G[S]).
\]
Links of faces of shellable pure simplicial complexes are shellable. Hence if the three-cut complex of an induced subgraph is nonshellable, so is the three-cut complex of its host.

**4. Verifying the seed obstruction.** The complex \(\Delta_3(C_8^2)\) is pure of dimension four: its facets are five-vertex complements of disconnected triples. An exact computation over \(\mathbb F_2\) gives its augmented face counts, ordered by face cardinality zero through five,
\[
(1,8,28,56,60,24),
\]
and augmented simplicial boundary ranks
\[
(1,7,21,35,24),
\]
for the successive boundary maps out of face cardinalities one through five. Therefore its third reduced Betti number equals \(60-35-24=1\); in particular,
\[
\widetilde H_3(\Delta_3(C_8^2);\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2.
\]
This seed behavior was already present in the earlier paper's homology table and is here independently reproduced by the bundled exact finite computation. A pure shellable four-dimensional complex has reduced homology only in dimension four (over any field); the nonzero group in dimension three therefore obstructs shellability. Steps 1 and 3 transfer this obstruction to all claimed powered cycles. This completes the proof.

## Verification

The bundled `VERIFY.py` uses only the standard Python library. It enumerates the seed simplices and independently computes boundary ranks of the three-cut complex and of its Alexander dual over \(\mathbb F_2\), checks all cyclically spaced embeddings in the stated band for \(2\le p\le80\), and separately verifies the induced-link correspondence for small host graphs. The exact all-parameter conclusion is proved in Steps 1–3; finite checks are supplementary and do not constitute an infinite proof. Run `python3 VERIFY.py` from a directory containing the bundled script; compare with `VERIFY_OUTPUT.txt`.

## Relationship to prior work

Chauhan, Shukla and Vinayak's paper *Shellability of 3-Cut Complexes of Squared Cycle Graphs* (first released June 4, 2024; revised January 22, 2025), Conjecture 4.2(i), predicts nonshellability in the wider band \(2p+3\le n\le4p\), and its Table 2 records the nonshellable eight-vertex squared-cycle seed. Chauhan and Shukla's *Shellability of 3-cut complexes of powers of cycle graphs* (first publicly released March 24, 2026), Theorem 1.4, proves shellability for \(n\ge6p-3\). The theorem above is an infinite-range partial confirmation of the **non**shellability part of the earlier conjecture. General link descriptions for induced subgraphs also appear in the foundational paper by Bayer and coauthors; Step 3 is included explicitly to make the deduction self-contained. Neither the previously tabulated finite seed nor the published large-cycle shellability theorem gives the new uniform embedding or its precise cyclic-order threshold.

## Limitations

The theorem does not resolve the remaining proposed nonshellable interval \(2p+3\le n<\lceil8(p+1)/3\rceil\), nor the conjectured homotopy types. The optimality statement concerns eight-vertex induced squared cycles **in the cyclic vertex order**, not all possible graph embeddings. Only the nonshellability consequence is claimed; no full Betti-number or homotopy classification is asserted for the host graph.
