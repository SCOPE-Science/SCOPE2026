# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim uses finite simple undirected powered cycles and ordinary three-cut complexes. The key inequalities for floors hold at both endpoints: two-step host gaps are at most the ceiling of one-quarter of the cycle length, and three-step gaps are at least the floor of three-eighths. Both arcs separating nonadjacent selected vertices contain at least three gaps. The converse cyclic-order threshold follows by summing eight three-gap and eight two-gap inequalities. The link identity follows directly from the cut-face definition; non-shellability follows from the seed's non-top-dimensional mod-two homology and the shellability inheritance of links. The seed ranks and embedding checks were independently recomputed by the supplied standalone exact-arithmetic script. No infinite statement relies on finite sampling.

## Originality — PASS, subject to indexing limits

Closest primary sources: Chauhan–Shukla–Vinayak, arXiv:2406.01979, Table 2 and Conjecture 4.2(i); Chauhan–Shukla, arXiv:2603.23155, Theorem 1.4 and conclusion; Bayer et al., *Topology of Cut Complexes of Graphs*, on cut-complex links. The eight-vertex homology obstruction, the induced-link principle, and the conjecture are acknowledged as prior. A conjecture is not a previous proof. The new result is a parameter-uniform, necessary-and-sufficient cyclic embedding criterion and its resulting proof of an infinite subrange of nonshellability. Available indexed comparable results concern shellability above a larger threshold, isolated small powered cycles, or different graph families; none located implies the all-parameter band. Residual risk: a prior induced-subgraph obstruction may have been stated elsewhere in a stronger unindexed theorem.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem confirms a natural, unbounded subrange of a specifically published open nonshellability conjecture. The obstruction is structural: an exactly characterized eight-point cyclic pattern survives as a nonshellable link for infinitely many cycle powers. The endpoint inequalities delimit the mechanism and expose where a different obstruction is needed, without claiming the rest of the conjecture or a homotopy classification.

## Limitations

The method does not cover smaller cycle lengths in the conjectured nonshellable band and does not prove anything about induced squared-eight-cycles with a different cyclic ordering. Independent audit has not been performed.
