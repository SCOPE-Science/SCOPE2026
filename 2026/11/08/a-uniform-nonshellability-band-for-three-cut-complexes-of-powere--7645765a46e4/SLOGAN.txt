For every \(p\ge2\), the three-cut complex of \(C_n^p\) is nonshellable throughout \(\lceil8(p+1)/3\rceil\le n\le4p\), because eight evenly spaced vertices induce a squared eight-cycle carrying a lower-dimensional homology obstruction. This proves an infinite subrange of a published nonshellability conjecture, with sharp bounds for cyclic-order-preserving copies of the obstruction.
