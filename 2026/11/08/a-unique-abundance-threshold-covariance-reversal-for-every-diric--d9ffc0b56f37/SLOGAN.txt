For every finite symmetric Dirichlet abundance vector and every real power \(q>1\), the covariance of the number of components above threshold \(t\) with \(\sum_i P_i^q\) changes sign exactly once, from negative to positive. At Simpson order the threshold is exactly \((a+1)/(ma+2)\); as power tends to infinity, the crossing approaches the unique threshold at which one component is expected to exceed it.
