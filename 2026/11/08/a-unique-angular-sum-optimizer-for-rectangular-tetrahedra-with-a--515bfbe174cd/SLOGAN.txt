Among right-corner tetrahedra with fixed longest-to-shortest perpendicular-arm ratio \(R>1\), the total normalized vertex solid angle has a unique, nonboundary minimizer. Its intermediate arm is asymptotic to \((2R)^{1/3}\), and the optimal angular sum has a sharp \(R^{-5/3}\) correction to its leading \(R^{-1}\) deficit.
