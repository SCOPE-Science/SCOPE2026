On rectangular rook networks \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m\ne n\), a single quantum absorber has an exact all-source efficiency law that is constant across positive hopping ratios except at \(am=bn\), where all three nontrap source classes incur an explicit loss. The diagonal-source resonance deficit is strictly positive for rectangles, contrasting with the known square strongly regular case where that deficit vanishes.
