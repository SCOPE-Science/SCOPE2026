For independent uniformly positioned equal-length arcs on a circle, the covariance of total uncovered length with the number of uncovered gaps crosses zero exactly once as arc length grows: it is negative first and positive afterward. With \(n\) arcs, the unique threshold satisfies \(na_n\to1.7932821329007556\ldots\), the positive root of \(e^c=1+c+c^2\).
