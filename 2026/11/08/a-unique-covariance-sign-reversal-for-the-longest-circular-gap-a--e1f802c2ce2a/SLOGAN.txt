For \(n\ge2\) uniform random points on a circle, the covariance between the longest spacing and the number of spacings exceeding \(t\) changes sign exactly once: negative below a unique threshold and positive above it. A convex conditional-maximum regression proves uniqueness for every \(n\), while simplex inclusion-exclusion yields an exact finite formula; the transition is at \(t_2=1/2\) and strictly below \(1/2\) for all larger sample sizes.
