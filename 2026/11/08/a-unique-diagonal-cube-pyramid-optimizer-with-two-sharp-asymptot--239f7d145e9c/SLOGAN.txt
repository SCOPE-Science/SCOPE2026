For a pyramid inside the \(n\)-cube whose base is a section perpendicular to a body diagonal, the optimal section is unique and lies asymptotically a coordinate-sum distance \(1/6\) beyond the center. Its maximal volume exceeds the centered diagonal-section pyramid volume by the sharp relative factor \(1+1/(6n)+7/(120n^2)+O(n^{-3})\); no unrestricted-orientation optimum is asserted.
