In a continuous-time irreversible contagion on a path, two initial sources maximize the probability of infecting every vertex by a deadline by merging three explicit decreasing Poisson-tail marginal sequences. For every path with at least five vertices, there is exactly one deadline threshold above which the two endpoints—and only the endpoints—are optimal; the exact threshold is the unique positive solution of \(Q_{n-2}(2s)=Q_{n-3}(2s)(1-e^{-s})\).
