# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The scope is exactly the fixed four-dimensional six-generator zonotope, its nonnegative scale parameter, all unordered distinct pairs from the ten elementary directions, and positive lengths for both added segments. Minkowski multilinearity for segment generators gives the signed defect as \(-srF_{u,v}(t)\). The classical maximal-minor volume identity makes each of the four underlying volumes affine in \(t\), so each \(F_{u,v}\) is quadratic and the coefficient table determines its sign for the *entire* continuum \(t\ge0\). The table covers all \(45\) pairs, has only one row with a negative coefficient, and shows \(\gamma>0\) and \(\alpha,\beta\ge0\) for each of the other \(44\) rows. The exception has \(F=4t(t-2)\); at \(t=0\), exactly eight pairs have zero constant term. `verify.py` was executed against the embedded TSV, and a separate exact symbolic determinant implementation cross-checked the four volume expressions at both anchor parameters for every pair (360 comparisons). The finite certificate enumerates the finite pair universe; it is not being used to extrapolate the real parameter signs.

## Originality — PASS, with bounded historical risk
Skorupinski, arXiv:2608.07702v1, gives the base matrix and one bad segment pair at \(t=1\), but not the complete sign graph over the ten ordinary template columns. The published scalar phase note `75844497da3f` gives the factor \(4t(2-t)\) for the *same* exceptional pair: it is already known and is not asserted as a separate new finding. The published directional phase note `f4edcfe36776` varies the special generator with fixed added directions and hence does not imply the signs of the remaining \(44\) choices of the added pair. The general Rayleigh formulation and rank/codegree threshold of Fradelizi and collaborators do not decide this codegree-four enumeration; Averkov, von Dichter and Soprunov's 2026 inequality is a distinct formulation. Actual semantic mathematical-record searches for the template, all-pair classification, aliases, and Rayleigh sign graph did not identify a covering claim. The principal residual risk is inaccessible full text of a related 2026 preprint and potentially unindexed exact computations.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The ten directions are not a contrived numerical slice: they are the entire elementary column set \(I_4\cup D_4\) of the canonical \(2\)-modular matrix template that generated an influential counterexample to volume log-submodularity. The new classification proves that the previously known obstruction is **unique among all 45 elementary direction pairs**, independently of positive segment lengths, and gives strictness and equality transitions for all other pairs. This identifies how localized the failure is within a standard rank-four 2-modular configuration, provides a precise Rayleigh sign graph, and yields a self-contained exact benchmark for mixed-volume/Rayleigh investigations. The result does not assert anything about arbitrary zonotopes, so its mathematical interest is the complete natural-template classification, not a global theorem.
