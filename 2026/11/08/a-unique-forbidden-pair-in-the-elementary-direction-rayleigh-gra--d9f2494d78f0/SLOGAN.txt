Within the ten elementary directions of the canonical four-dimensional \(2\)-modular zonotope template, exactly one unordered pair can violate volume log-submodularity when the exceptional generator is scaled positively. That pair is \(\{d_{14},d_{23}\}\); its defect is exactly \(4sr\,t(2-t)\), while all other \(44\) pairs satisfy the inequality strictly for every \(t>0\) and all positive segment lengths.
