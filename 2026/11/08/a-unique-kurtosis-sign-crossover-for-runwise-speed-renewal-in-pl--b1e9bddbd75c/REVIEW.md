## Value — PASS

Speed fluctuations and angular redistribution compete in active-particle non-Gaussian displacement statistics; their relative effect is explicitly motivated in Shee and Chaudhuri. In the joint-speed-and-heading reset model the full speed law collapses to a single experimentally interpretable fourth-to-second moment ratio, with sharp universal cutoffs, at most one temporal crossover, and an invariant sign under added translational diffusion. This classifies a natural complete model family instead of a selected numerical slice. The explicit stop/go example identifies three regimes in terms of the moving fraction. The exact critical boundary has an improved negative \(t^{-2}\) relaxation rather than the generic \(t^{-1}\) fourth-moment normalization.

## Limitations and closest literature

This does not describe correlated speed/angle updates, non-Poisson run durations, interactions, confined transport, full displacement densities or thermodynamic phase transitions. Mori et al. is closest by model identity; its main published claims concern different observables. General formulas elsewhere may allow alternative derivations, and a publication-priority check by independent subject-matter experts is outstanding. The process is stationary only in its velocity marks; the freely moving position has no stationary distribution.
