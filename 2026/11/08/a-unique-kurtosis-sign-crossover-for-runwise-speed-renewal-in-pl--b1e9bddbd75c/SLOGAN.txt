In a planar run-and-tumble particle whose heading and random speed are jointly refreshed at Poisson times, the displacement kurtosis can change sign at most once: it stays negative for speed-moment ratio \(\rho\leq4/3\), stays positive for \(\rho\geq2\), and crosses uniquely from negative to positive for \(4/3<\rho<2\). The crossover time is fixed by a universal strictly decreasing integral threshold and is independent of translational thermal diffusivity.
