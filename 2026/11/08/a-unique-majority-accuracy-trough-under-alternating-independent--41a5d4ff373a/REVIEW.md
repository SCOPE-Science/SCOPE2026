## Value — PASS

The all-size theorem supplies a sharp decision criterion for a natural recruitment pattern that permanently adds one expert and one weaker-than-chance assessor. It identifies a structurally unavoidable short-run accuracy loss, an exact regime where the loss persists forever, and a complementary regime in which the same sequence eventually recovers but can do so arbitrarily late. These conclusions constrain when individual-average competence can justify enlarging independent juries; they go beyond a one-off negative example or routine numerical calculation.
