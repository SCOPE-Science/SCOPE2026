In the tandem exclusion process, the unique density maximizing stationary current decreases and then increases as the follower-recovery rate grows, with its single reversal at the positive root of \(7q^4+12q^3+94q^2+20q-53=0\). Along the large-recovery optimal-density path, the current deficit and the vacancy density scale as \(q^{-1/3}\), while the optimal correlation length scales as \(q^{1/3}\), not the fixed-density \(q^{1/2}\).
