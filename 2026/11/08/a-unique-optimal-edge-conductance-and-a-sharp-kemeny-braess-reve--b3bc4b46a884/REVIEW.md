# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof covers every integer \(a\ge3\), \(b\ge1\), and real \(w>0\). The stated transition normalization and zero-at-self Kemeny convention are explicit. The equitable three-class quotient, exceptional antisymmetric eigenvalue and \(n-4\) null eigenvalues exhaust the transition spectrum. Their trace formula yields the exact rational conductance response. Its derivative has precisely one positive zero, proving unique minimization; 175 exact rational full-matrix tests and 252 additional scaling identities independently cross-check the algebra. No finite checks are used as infinite proofs.

## Originality

**PASS, with priority risk.** The single unweighted edge insertion case and its sign criterion belong to Hu and Kirkland (2019) and are expressly excluded from the novelty claim. The examined primary texts by Hu–Kirkland, Altafini et al. (2022), Faught et al. (2021), and Breen et al. (2026) do not state an arbitrary-conductance \(K_{a,b}\) response, its exact universal sign-reversal parameter or the globally optimal strength. General matrix methods and symmetry machinery make the derivation accessible, so independent priority review remains warranted. The claim is a continuous parameter phase diagram, not a relabeling of the published \(w=1\) result.

## Value

**PASS.** In a natural reversible Markov-chain network-design problem, adding more conductance initially improves mean stationary-target passage time and eventually makes it worse *on the same fixed graph topology*. A sharp all-size threshold, unique global optimal weight and conductance rescaling collapse quantify how much intra-part connectivity is best. The old unweighted sign formula does not answer this optimization question.

## Closest literature and boundaries

Hu–Kirkland 2019 supplies the exact unit-conductance special case; Altafini et al. 2022 treat general edge-centrality changes using different matrix methods; Breen et al. 2026 treat unweighted clique insertions. The result does not cover arbitrary base weights, multiple added edges, or other graphs. Independent historical priority remains unverified.
