For the conductance-proportional random walk on \(K_{a,b}\), a new weighted edge between two vertices of the \(a\)-part first *improves* and later *worsens* Kemeny's constant as its conductance increases, with the exact reversal at \(w=3b/[4(a-2)]\) for \(a\ge3\). The unique optimal conductance is \(b\) times a positive root depending only on \(a\), giving an exact normalized network-design law beyond the previously known unit-weight case.
