For a one-scale Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle independently redrawn from a finite-width Gaussian at Poisson times, the conditional Gaussian endpoint-Jeffreys-divergence reward per unit time has exactly one optimal reset frequency for every width ratio \(0<q<1\). The frequency obeys \(r_*/(2\lambda)\to\sqrt2\) as \(q\uparrow1\) but grows like \(q^{-1/2}\) as \(q\downarrow0\), with a sharp two-term peak-value expansion; this endpoint diagnostic is not a universal physical heat or work cost.
