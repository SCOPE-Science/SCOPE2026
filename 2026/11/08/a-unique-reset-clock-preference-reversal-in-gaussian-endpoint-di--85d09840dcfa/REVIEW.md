# Scientific review: unique reset-clock preference reversal

## Correctness — PASS
The proof is fully analytical. It fixes a precise independent Gaussian reset law and matches physical frequencies to the dimensionless clock mean. The symmetric Gaussian endpoint KL expression follows from the two variances; its second derivative is an explicit rational polynomial in \(1-e^{-x}\) with exactly one positive-to-negative curvature change. The Gaussian renewal reward is exactly an expectation under an exponential unit-mean variable, not an approximation. The nonnegative stop-loss kernel is strictly ordered across scales: its logarithmic elasticity is decreasing on each side of its single downward jump. This makes the negative-curvature contribution grow strictly relative to the positive-curvature contribution. Small-mean and large-mean estimates have opposite signs and are proved with uniform integrability. Consequently there exists exactly one crossing, with strict signs on each side. The additional optimized-clock comparison follows from renewal-reward averaging and attainment of both finite maxima. No finite experiment is treated as a proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Farago, arXiv:2605.28445v1, already supplies the Gaussian endpoint-divergence framework and approximate periodic/Poisson rate formulas for very narrow resets. These ingredients are explicitly excluded from the claimed novelty. The current theorem establishes the exact finite-width **equal-mean** protocol difference with a single global sign crossing. The 2023 Mori–Olsen–Krishnamurthy work computes pathwise stochastic entropy and the 2024 Olsen–Gupta–Mori–Krishnamurthy work studies thermodynamic work for finite-time switching; neither visible result states this exact endpoint-metric crossing. General total-positivity and variation-diminishing techniques may contain an abstract lemma that would imply it, but the application to the full finite-width Gaussian cycle has not been identified in examined published statements. A prior result on separately optimized periodic or Poisson endpoint-divergence rates does not imply a unique crossing between the two at every matched mean. Independent priority verification remains desirable.

## Value — PASS
At fixed mean reset frequency, randomizing the clock is neither always beneficial nor always detrimental for the finite-width endpoint-information cost. The exact unique reversal gives a meaningful protocol choice boundary that disappears from the narrow-reset approximation. Its proof explains why the reversal is unique, not merely observable in a finite parameter scan, and specifies what is and is not being measured. For example, at half-equilibrium reset width, the dimensionless equality occurs at a mean reset interval about \(0.42891636051\), or dimensionless frequency about \(2.3314568808\). This is a natural complete classification for an experimentally motivated contrast, rather than an arbitrary narrow parameter slice or a restatement of Gaussian KL algebra.
