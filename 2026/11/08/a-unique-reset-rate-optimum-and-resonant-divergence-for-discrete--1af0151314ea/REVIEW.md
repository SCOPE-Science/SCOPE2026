For nonresonant phases it has full rank; the standard qubit QFI formula reduces exactly to the displayed rational function. An explicit positive change of variable turns the derivative into a polynomial whose coefficients exhibit one Descartes sign change, proving unique global optimality for all allowed parameters. The small-detuning balance and the endpoint result follow algebraically. A separately implemented density-matrix SLD checker, geometric-series comparison and symbolic derivative identities passed all 171 check groups.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** The 2024 discrete-time quantum-reset paper supplies the stationary mixture and resonance mechanism but not the phase QFI maximization or the universal resonant constant. The 2018 quantum-reset spectral framework addresses continuous-time generators. The 2026 dissipative metrology work concerns local QFI response in a different continuous-time open system and was accessible only through summary/abstract for this comparison. General metrology formulas alone require a model-specific global polynomial sign proof. Search results did not produce an equivalent global classification; this is limited evidence, not a guarantee of priority.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Tuning reset frequency to optimize information in a quantum phase sensor is an interpretable mathematical design problem, and the result gives an exact global optimizer for every nonresonant phase plus the sharp singular regime near gate resonance. It distinguishes an actual optimal measurement-information question from merely computing another stationary-state moment.

The near-resonance divergence assumes uncharged waiting/gate resources, ideal complete resets, known reset probability, and single-snapshot local QFI. Exact resonances are rank-changing and excluded. The recent general reset-metrology paper arXiv:2610.31249 was not fully inspected, so some implicit-coverage risk remains.
