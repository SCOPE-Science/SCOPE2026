In a planar harmonic active-Brownian swimmer whose position and heading are jointly reset, the covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y^2)\) changes from negative to positive at exactly one positive reset rate for every rotational diffusivity and isotropic thermal-noise strength. More thermal noise strictly lowers that threshold, from a sharp athermal value toward the universal lower bound \(\sqrt{1+D_r/\kappa}-1\) in units of trap relaxation rate.
