For a basal resource supporting \(m\) independently arriving specialists that each contribute resource-extinction rate \(k\), stationary expected biodiversity increases, stays exactly constant, or decreases with \(m\) according as \(k<c+e\), \(k=c+e\), or \(k>c+e\), regardless of consumer colonization and disappearance rates. The richness converges to \(c/k\) with an explicit \(m^{-1/2}\) correction whenever \(k\ne c+e\).
