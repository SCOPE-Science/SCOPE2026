For three independent uniform directions on a high-dimensional sphere, both the largest and smallest geodesic side-length cdfs differ from their independent-Gaussian approximations by the same sharp positive correction \(\phi(t)^3/\sqrt d\), uniformly on bounded normalized thresholds. An explicit cubic expansion of the three-side Gram density controls the remaining error by \(O(d^{-1})\); earlier work supplied qualitative and order-sharp triangle excesses but not this coefficient profile.
