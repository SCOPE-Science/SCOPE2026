In a three-plant, capacity-one mutualist network with \(d\ge3\) possible partners per plant and exactly \(3d-3\) active pollinators, the unique design maximizing the chance of assigning a distinct survivor to every plant is a triangle of pair-shared partners with \(d-2\) private partners per plant. The optimum is strict for every independent pollinator failure probability \(0<q<1\), with an explicit reliability polynomial and an exact four-architecture proof.
