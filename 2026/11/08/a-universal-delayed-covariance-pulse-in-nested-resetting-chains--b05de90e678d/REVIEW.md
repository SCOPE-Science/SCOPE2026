# Scientific review: directed covariance transport in nested resetting

## Correctness — PASS

The process is defined for every finite chain of independent equal-rate reset clocks and centered square-integrable Lévy driving noises, with a pinned root. Its stationary construction has finite backward ancestry and finite second moments. Generator calculations establish the exact stationary first-row covariance and a triangular closed conditional-mean system. Differentiation reduces the forward cross-covariance to a polynomial with exactly one positive derivative zero; the ratio of its falling-factorial terms is strictly decreasing. A uniform geometric-tail expansion and Stirling's formula give the peak location and amplitude, while a finite gamma integral gives the integrated directional asymmetry. All critical quantifiers, initial and late-time limits, and the normalizing first-site variance are explicit. The standalone verifier passed 1,499 checks; these checks are supporting evidence, not a proof of the all-depth claims.

## Originality — PASS, with publication-priority risk

The closest primary source is Alston–Britton–Bertrand, arXiv:2502.03225, particularly the full paper's static two-point correlation section and supplement. It already covers the equal-time formula and the model's generator. The 2026 follow-up, arXiv:2609.25252, establishes the progression of one-time marginal densities and accumulation times, but not the stationary time-lagged pair covariance. General conditional-mean propagation is elementary and not asserted here as an independent novel contribution. The substantive comparison concerns the unique optimal time-lag, sharp linear-depth delay, algebraic maximum versus exponential simultaneous attenuation, finite nonzero integrated directional signal, and distribution-free downstream noise universality. The available direct sources and indexed research claims did not explicitly establish those combined statements. Broader queueing/linear-network response theory or later manuscripts could still contain equivalents.

## Value — PASS

Static correlation length and one-time relaxation alone do not quantify when an upstream fluctuation is most observable downstream. The exact temporal covariance gives an operational delay calibration for hierarchical signal transmission and establishes a sharp asymmetry between causal and reverse observations. The exponential-to-algebraic attenuation contrast is a nontrivial scaling boundary, and the nondecaying integrated transfer is an exact conservation-like statement for the signal response. Independence of downstream noise laws makes these conclusions useful well beyond Gaussian Brownian particles. The result is structural and physically motivated, not merely a numerical evaluation of one special parameter set.

## Limits and closest sources

Only equal independent reset rates, a fixed root and root-to-descendant covariances are covered. Nonlinear feedback, general pair indices, correlated Lévy noises, infinite-chain stationarity, and publication priority outside the inspected materials remain open. Source inspections were of the 2025 arXiv main text and relevant supplement, the 2026 arXiv HTML main sections and references; additional batch-resetting literature was compared by abstract and protocol. This review provides no independent scientific validation.
