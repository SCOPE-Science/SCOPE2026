Under independent species extinctions, distributing redundant species more evenly always lowers uncertainty in surviving ecological functions up to the sharp extinction probability \(\rho\approx0.465571\), where \(\rho(1+\rho)^2=1\). Above this boundary, redistributing even a single species can increase or decrease functional-richness variance, although balancing always improves its expectation; an exact transfer identity classifies every such change.
