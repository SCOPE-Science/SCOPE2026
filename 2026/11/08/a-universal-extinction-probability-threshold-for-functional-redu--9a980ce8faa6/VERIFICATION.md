# Verification

The mathematical proof is in `RESULT.md`. With Python 3 and no third-party packages, execute `python3 CHECK.py` in the directory containing the files.

An exact integer-coefficient polynomial calculation checks the identity for 370 transfer parameter pairs. Independently, all \(2^S\) species-loss states are enumerated for six sample allocations with \(S\le9\), and the exact first two moments are compared to the disjoint-group Bernoulli formulas. Two rational variance differences at nine species are checked, along with a rational enclosure for the unique root. Another 1,234 exact-rational comparisons corroborate the low-extinction balancing inequality for selected small sizes and rational probabilities.

The universal theorem is established by the analytic factorization and a finite balancing argument, not by those computations. The checks do not cover correlated losses, multifunctional species, or fixed-count sampling. This is not an independent scientific audit.
