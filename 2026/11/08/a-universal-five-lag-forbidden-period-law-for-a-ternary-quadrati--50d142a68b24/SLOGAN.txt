For every memory length \(k\ge2\), the ternary quadratic permutation \(T_k(w_0,\ldots,w_{k-1})=(w_1,\ldots,w_{k-1},w_0+w_{k-1}^{2})\) has exactly one state fixed by \(T_k^{5k}\): zero. A five-row zero-mask argument excludes every nonzero cycle whose length divides \(5k\), sharply contrasting with the known abundance of \(4k\)-periodic states.
