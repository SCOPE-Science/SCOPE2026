In the resonant two-site Lindblad failure model with unequal local loss rates, the time-to-failure coefficient of variation has a unique global minimum at exchange \(J_*=(\gamma_1+\gamma_2)/(2\sqrt2)\), always beyond the dissipative exceptional point. Every stronger exchange has both shorter mean lifetime and greater relative timing uncertainty, while equal local losses make the entire failure-time law independent of exchange.
