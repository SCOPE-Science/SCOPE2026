In a fixed-edge Erdős–Rényi graph, the covariance between isolated-vertex count and induced copies of any connected motif depends on the motif only through its vertex and edge counts, and for noncliques has at most one sign reversal as the edge budget rises. At large order, the transition density is \(p_*=(2h-k)/[k(k-2)]\), exactly \(1/k\) for every induced tree on \(k\) vertices; in the independent-edge model the same covariance is always negative.
