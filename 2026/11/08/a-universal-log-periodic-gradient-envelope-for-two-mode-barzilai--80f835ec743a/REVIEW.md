For the real strictly convex quadratic with two eigenvalues and matched BB1 initialization, exact spectral factors yield the two-step ratio recurrence. Its logarithmic characteristic roots have modulus \(\sqrt2\). Summation of the two component logarithms, via a uniform soft-plus approximation, yields the explicit infinite-series phase law with a controlled \(O(n2^{-n/2})\) error. Density of the irrational phase rotation gives attained liminf and limsup, and the signed-series identity establishes nonconstancy and a factor-two separation. The standalone checker replays exact rational and numerical consistency tests. Infinite conclusions follow from proof rather than computations.

A general R-superlinear classification does not determine how rapidly the residual gradient decreases between individual late iterations. This characterizes a minimal but nontrivial spectral dynamical system without asserting practical floating-point speedups or higher-dimensional generality.
