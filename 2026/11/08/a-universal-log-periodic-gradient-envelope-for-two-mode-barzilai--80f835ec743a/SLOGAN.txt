For matched BB1 descent on a strictly convex quadratic with exactly two distinct eigenvalues, every nonbalanced initial spectral gradient ratio yields a universal nonconstant phase profile for the scaled negative log gradient norm at scale \(2^{n/2}\). Its positive oscillating coefficient is independent of the eigenvalues, while a balanced initial gradient retains the known geometric trajectory; the ratio recurrence and qualitative superlinear convergence are prior results.
