# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For every edge, its current selection count gives an exact geometric decay factor; a persistent receptive edge bounds the total weight away from zero. Uniform conditional-hazard bounds make the inter-contact waiting times lie between geometric scales times polynomial factors almost surely, by summable-tail Borel--Cantelli estimates. Inverting these clocks gives the universal logarithmic coefficient. Positive-edge frequencies and the endpoint cases follow from the bounded martingale strong law and direct update identities. In the three-node path, the contact intervals are independent geometric variables, yielding exact moment formulas. A standard-library checker passed 137 rational tests; finite tests do not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The full relevant model and theoretical-result sections of [1] were compared by mathematical implication, not titles: its Lemma 1 establishes infinitely many edge selections, Theorem 2 proves limiting zero/one weights, and Theorem 3 gives eventual graph stabilization. None states a logarithmic sampling coefficient or the finite-contact geometric clock. The closest related published-finding corpus hit, the seven-agent logarithmic switching-lifetime theorem, studies switching of a bounded-confidence proximity graph rather than downweighted-edge contact counts. Classical randomly reinforced urn and strong-reinforcement papers [2,3] involve different weight evolution; only their abstracts were available for comparison. Risk: an older general self-inhibiting-urn theorem could subsume this special case without matching its terminology.

**Value — PASS.** Infinite sampling of incompatible edges despite their weights tending to zero is a surprising feature already recognized in the motivating model. The exact \(\log t\) coefficient turns that qualitative observation into a universal network-independent resource/communication cost; the two-edge clock specifies finite expected time to suppress a bad tie, and the three endpoint regimes show why the decay parameter must be kept away from zero and one when extrapolating simulations. These are motivated quantitative questions, not arbitrary numerical slices.

**Limits.** Fixed (or eventually fixed) edge classifications, positive initial weights, a surviving receptive edge, a finite network, and the precise normalized multiplicative rule are essential. No guarantee of global opinion convergence speed, no assertion for all-incompatible graphs, and no independent expert assessment are claimed.
