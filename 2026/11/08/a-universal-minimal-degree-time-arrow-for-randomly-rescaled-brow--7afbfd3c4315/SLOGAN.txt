Every stationary Brownian diffusion subjected to independent Poissonian random strict rescalings has a universal, strictly negative fourth-order time-reversal difference \(\mathbb E[X_0^3X_t]-\mathbb E[X_0X_t^3]\) at each positive lag, even though all lower-degree polynomial time differences vanish. Its magnitude has exactly one positive-time maximum, given explicitly by the first- and third-moment relaxation rates, including the degenerate-rate case.
