For a passive planar colloid with isotropic translational mobility but nonzero thermal translation–rotation coupling, Poisson orientation resets produce a strictly larger long-time diffusion rate along the reset-induced drift than across it. The anisotropy has a unique, shape-independent maximum when the reset frequency is exactly twice the rotational diffusion rate, with covariance-rate gap \(10D\|c\|^2/(27b)\).
