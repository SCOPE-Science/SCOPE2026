At the critical point of the active spherical Debye model, the stationary correlation on the force-persistence clock is \(e^{-s}\) plus a strictly positive, universal correction of order \(\tau^{-(d-4)/2}\) for \(4<d<6\). Its exact scaling function crosses between \(s^{(6-d)/2}\) and \(s^{-(d-2)/2}\), with an error-function formula in dimension five.
