For uniform allocation of balls to \(m\) boxes, the counts of \(j^2\)-occupied and \((j+1)^2\)-occupied boxes have a positive order-one covariance at exactly \(j(j+1)\) balls per box, despite a vanishing ordinary leading covariance. In the \(m^{-1/2}\) load window its limiting covariance is an explicit downward parabola, changing sign at the two square-root offsets.
