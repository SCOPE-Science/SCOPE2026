At a typical zero of a white-noise Hermite spectrogram, the local real Jacobian is almost surely invertible, yet its condition number has an exact quadratic tail: its mean is finite and its second moment is infinite at every Hermite order at least one. As the order grows, the condition-number survival law approaches \(4k^2/(k^2+1)^2\) for \(k\geq1\).
