For irreversible dimer adsorption on a complete graph with a triangle-free bounded-degree graph of forbidden bonds, the probability of leaving a pair vacant is universally \(3|E(H_N)|/(N^2-1)\) up to relative error \(O_\Delta(\log N/N)\). In particular, deleting an even Hamiltonian cycle gives a rare-jamming probability asymptotic to \(3/N\), independent of local cycle placement or other topology within the bounded-degree class.
