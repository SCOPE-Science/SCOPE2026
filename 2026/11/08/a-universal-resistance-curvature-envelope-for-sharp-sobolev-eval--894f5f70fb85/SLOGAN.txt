For every finite connected metric graph with arbitrary positive edge lengths, the exact real mean-zero \(H^1\)-to-\(L^\infty\) constant and all norming points come from finitely many effective-resistance quadratic edge profiles. The profiles obey the topological curvature law \(\sum_e c_e/\ell_e=\beta_1(G)-1\), and all equality functions are centered Green representers.
