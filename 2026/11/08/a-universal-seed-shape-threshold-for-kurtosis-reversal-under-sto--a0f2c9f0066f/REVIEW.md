The closest 2023 resetting-noise paper introduces the physical model and treats origin-resetting Brownian noise in detail. The present claim explicitly concerns a nondegenerate random restart distribution of the *noise* and an exact global kurtosis-sign classification; the origin-reset limit does not imply the two-dimensional seed-shape phase boundary. Olsen's random-reset distribution results concern direct resetting of the *particle's position*, not its noise. Shee's non-Gaussian trapped active Brownian particle has angular propulsion and position-orientation resets, not the generator here. Comparisons against published sources and earlier documented scientific claims did not uncover an equivalent or stronger statement. They do not prove absolute novelty; unindexed randomized-noise seed-moment formulas are a material priority risk.

The object is a canonical extension of resetting Brownian driving noise to experimentally meaningful uncertain restart amplitudes. This is a stationary fourth-moment classification, not a density or tail theorem.
