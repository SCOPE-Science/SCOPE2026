For every \(n\ge4\), replacing the deepest four-leaf fork of a rooted unit-edge phylogenetic caterpillar changes the variance ranking of future diversity at exactly one common extinction probability, even though the unmodified caterpillar has strictly larger expected diversity for every nontrivial probability. The crossing threshold decreases with \(n\), from about \(0.510595\) toward the algebraic limit \(0.491144\); four taxa are the sharp minimum for this phenomenon.
