Two noninteracting bosons subject to collision loss on a positively weighted star have an exactly computable protected subspace of rank \(q(q-3)/2\), appearing sharply at four leaves, with a full closed-form projector valid for arbitrary positive loss rates. From a symmetrized distinct-leaf pair on a uniformly coupled star, the eventual loss probability is exactly \(2/(q-1)\), so greater branching protects rather than exposes the pair.
