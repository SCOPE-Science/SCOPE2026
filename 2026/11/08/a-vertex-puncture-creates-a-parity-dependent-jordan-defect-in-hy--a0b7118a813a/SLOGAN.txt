Deleting one vertex from a Boolean hypercube changes characteristic-two neighbor-sum dynamics sharply: in even dimension it creates exactly one size-three nilpotent block and forces extinction at time three, while in odd dimension it introduces one transient direction into an otherwise involutory system. The complete Jordan decompositions give the exact number of configurations at each first-entry time and every primitive two-cycle count over \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\).
