# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The genus-six assertion follows from a squarefree degree-seven Kummer cover and Riemann--Hurwitz; the exact smooth parameter divisor is \(\lambda^7=2\). All six regular differentials are justified at ramification points and infinity. The explicit Cartier matrix follows from a completely specified coefficient extraction; its nonzero entries and zero entries were independently reconstructed from the polynomial expansion. The semilinear-chain argument establishes each rank for *all* algebraically closed smooth parameters, with no finite-to-infinite extrapolation. The standalone verifier checks the symbolic coefficient matrix and all 24 smooth cases over \(\mathbb F_{25}\), including nonprime-field Frobenius twists.

## Originality: PASS, with residual risk

Borges--Gonçalves (arXiv:2212.03987, Theorem 3.1 and its proof) classify \(p\)-ranks for undeformed Fermat-type curves; their statement and calculations do not give the nonzero-parameter stratum. Pries--Ulmer (arXiv:2010.15160, Sections 6--9) give broader group-scheme information for Fermat curves and quotients, so the undeformed fiber cannot be claimed original. Their result does not imply a universal Cartier profile under the \(\lambda x\) perturbation. Nourozi--Rahmati study quartic-right-hand-side Picard curves (arXiv:2306.07823), while Montanucci--Speziali compute invariants of Fermat and Hurwitz curves (arXiv:1603.03604). Direct searches for characteristic-five cyclic trigonal deformations and the exact septic equation, combined with comparison to accessible research records, found no covering statement. An unindexed or differently parameterized trigonal computation could cover this and remains a historical-priority risk; absence of search matches is not a proof of global novelty.

## Mathematical value: PASS

This is an exact positive-dimensional smooth family confined to the zero \(5\)-rank locus, with a sharp and complete \(a\)-number jump and distinct Cartier nilpotence chains. Because stable \(p\)-rank is identically zero, the special-versus-generic distinction is invisible to the invariant computed in the motivating Fermat-type paper. The explicit formula describes a natural single-monomial deformation of the rigid Fermat-type source and locates precisely the excluded singular parameters, rather than tabulating arbitrary finite-field samples. The two Cartier chains offer finer geometric control than the mere existence of a zero-\(p\)-rank example; the claim is limited to this family and does not assert any stronger global consequence.
