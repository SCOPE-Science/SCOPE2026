For the smooth genus-six cyclic trigonal curves \(y^3=x^7+\lambda x+1\) in characteristic \(5\), every Jacobian has \(5\)-rank zero, but the \(a\)-number jumps from \(2\) at every nonzero smooth parameter to \(4\) at \(\lambda=0\). The complete Cartier-rank profile changes from \((6,4,3,2,1,0,0)\) to \((6,2,1,0,0,0,0)\), with nilpotence indices five and three.
