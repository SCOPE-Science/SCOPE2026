For the strictly subdoubling sequence \(s_n=2^n+1\), the scalings \(\alpha\) whose floored sequence represents every nonnegative integer by distinct-index subset sums form a Lebesgue-null but infinite set containing every \(2^{-k}\). For almost every \(0<\alpha<1/2\), the missing-integer gaps at consecutive levels have asymptotic length \((1/2-\alpha)n\), separating critical-threshold existence from the positive-measure phenomenon below uniform growth ratio two.
