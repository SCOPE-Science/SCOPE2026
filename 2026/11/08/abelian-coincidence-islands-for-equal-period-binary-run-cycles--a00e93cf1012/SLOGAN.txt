For two binary two-run periodic words of equal total period but unequal zero densities, common Abelian-factor lengths form explicitly bounded, increasingly separated integer intervals; their exact shared-Parikh-class count is also given by a piecewise-linear formula. The longest matching length is \((T+1)(b+c)\), where \(T=\lfloor\min(b,c)/(a-c)\rfloor\) after orienting the run profiles so that \(a>c\).
