For binary words with exactly three ones, distinct Abelian-cube factors admit an explicit zero-gap formula and a rational all-length aggregate generating function. Under uniform fixed-weight sampling, the count divided by \(n^2\) converges to an explicit Dirichlet functional with mean \(1/270\) and an atom of size \(2/9\) at zero; the probability of any cube using all three ones tends to \(7/9\).
