Every binary word \(b^i a^3 b^j a b^k\) has an explicitly classified set of distinct Abelian-square factors, with a closed formula covering even the genuine five-run cases. The sharp minimum at every length \(n\ge5\) within this triple-plus-singleton family is \(\lfloor(n+2)/4\rfloor\); this is not an unrestricted four-marker theorem.
