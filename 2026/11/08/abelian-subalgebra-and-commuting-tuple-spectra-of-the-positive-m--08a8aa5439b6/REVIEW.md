# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact domain is the nilradical \(x^2k[x]/(x^p)\partial_x\) of the modular Witt Borel, for prime \(p>3\), with finite-field parameter \(q=p^e\). The proof derives the needed low-valuation centralizers directly using Laurent quotients, proves they are abelian, classifies all abelian subspaces by their lowest degree, and counts normalized cosets modulo their intersection with the tail. The ordered-tuple formula is independently derived by first-pivot stratification. Exhaustive tests were run at \(p=5\), with a second-prime commutator-rank census at \(p=7\). No finite test is used as a substitute for the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)
Yao–Chang (2013, arXiv:1301.5667, Lemma 2.3) already contains the relevant element-centralizer structures, and this input is explicitly credited. Their paper addresses commuting pairs and geometric irreducible components, not the displayed dimension-by-dimension finite-field subalgebra census or all-arity arithmetic generating data. The closest competing work, Yao (2015, DOI 10.1016/j.jpaa.2015.02.005), analyzes exactly the higher nilpotent commuting varieties, including subalgebras, but its full text could not be inspected; the accessible abstract speaks about components and their dimensions. The 2021 Yao–Zhang paper on full-Witt commuting tuples has a similar access limitation. Both are genuine residual priority risks, not declared noncovering based on snippets. The proof develops the additional Gaussian quotient-subspace counts and leading-pivot enumeration needed for the claimed numerical invariants. Neither a failed search nor a slot partition proves priority.

## Value — PASS
Counting actual abelian subalgebras by dimension is a natural finite-Lie-algebra invariant distinct from a component count. The formula supplies the entire sequence of subalgebra Grassmannian point counts across arbitrary prime characteristics \(p>3\) and finite extensions. The classification of all maximum-dimensional abelian subalgebras by \(\mathbb P^1(k)\), and the exact all-arity commuting-tuple polynomial including its large-arity limit, provide interpretable structure that is not just a small parameter table. The precise scope is motivated by modular Witt cohomology/commuting-variety literature. Priority of the numerical formulas remains subject to further full-text comparison.

The checker samples prime fields only; the proof covers finite extensions. Independent validation is not claimed.
