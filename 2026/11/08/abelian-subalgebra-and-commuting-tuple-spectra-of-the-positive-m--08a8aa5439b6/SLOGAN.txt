The positive nilradical of the characteristic-\(p>3\) Witt algebra has an exact Gaussian-binomial census of abelian subalgebras in every dimension, with its maximum-dimensional members indexed by \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_q)\). A matching closed formula counts ordered pairwise-commuting \(r\)-tuples for every finite extension \(\mathbb F_q\) and every \(r\ge1\), interpolating all clique sizes rather than only commuting pairs.
