# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The local update is determined solely by the total number of ones and each part occupancy. Mixed parts cannot be re-created after becoming uniform, so mixed parts of a two-cycle must tie at both phases. This yields a common mixed occupancy and the necessary-and-sufficient identity \((c-1)(j-q/2)=0\). The count distinguishes one mixed part from multiple half-filled parts, and separately handles fully uniform parts. A symmetric two-time integer energy proves that every orbit eventually has period at most two. This proves the complete formula over all integers \(r,q\geq2\), without extrapolating from finite tests. Two independent exact programs checked the claimed fixed and activity-resolved two-cycle counts across 21 and 34 parameter cases, respectively.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The Goles–Olivos eventual-period theorem is prior art and is not reasserted as a discovery. The relevant open original text of Adams et al. (arXiv:1102.5361), especially its first two sections and main complete-multipartite theorems, concerns irreversible activation and minimum conversion sets. The relevant open original text of Kaaser–Mallmann-Trenn–Natale (arXiv:1508.03519), notably the Turán-graph discussion, symmetry contraction and appendices, concerns voting-time bounds, not an exact recurrent-state profile. Aledo et al. (doi:10.1155/2020/9708347) treat OR/AND-type maxterms and minterms rather than majority. Searches for reversible complete-multipartite majority fixed-point counts, two-cycle activity spectra, and synonymous balanced Turán-graph formulations did not locate equivalent published formulas. Nearby research-index results concerned parity-rule automata or static majority colorings, neither of which logically implies a majority-dynamics cycle count. Search nonappearance is not proof of priority; a previously unindexed result or an inaccessible older paper remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The family \(K_{q,\ldots,q}\) is the equal-part Turán graph, a central dense, highly symmetric graph model for clustered networks. The result is a full two-parameter natural periodic-orbit classification and, beyond aggregate counts, an exact activity spectrum with a parity-dependent change in qualitative behavior. When both part count and part size are odd, a two-cycle freezes exactly one mixed part; with even part size, several half-filled parts can remain stationary. This has use for benchmarking symmetric threshold dynamics and comparing otherwise similar dense graphs. The theorem does not classify all transient basins or uneven multipartite graphs.

## Closest work and limitations
The closest full-text material includes the explicit equal-part graph family in the 2015 voting-time work and the complete-multipartite activation problem in the 2011 irreversible work. Neither directly provides the present activity-resolved orbit census. A related Boolean-network counting paper has a different update function, so its statements are not dominating results for strict majority. The published research-index summaries were checked for equivalence and stronger implications, but some full underlying documents could not be inspected. Independent checking and broader historical-priority evaluation remain outstanding.
