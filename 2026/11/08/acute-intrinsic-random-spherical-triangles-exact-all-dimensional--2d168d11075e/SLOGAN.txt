For three independent uniform points on \(S^{d-1}\), the probability that all three *intrinsic spherical geodesic* angles are acute has an exact two-dimensional beta-integral formula and approaches \(1/8\) from below with sharp deficit \(1/(\sqrt2\pi^{3/2}\sqrt d)+O(d^{-3/2})\). Effective finite-dimensional bounds control the entire approach for \(d\ge5\).
