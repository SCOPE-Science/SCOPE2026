For every finite commutative chain ring of nilpotency length \(\ell\ge3\) and residue-field order \(q=p^f\), the prime-field adjacency code of its simple zero-divisor graph has exact parameters \([q^{\ell-1}-1,q^{\lfloor\ell/2\rfloor},1]_p\), dual minimum distance two, and a one-dimensional Euclidean hull. The complete rowspace and dual are explicit, and weight-one words occur exactly at socle coordinates and, for even \(\ell\), also at the middle valuation layer.
