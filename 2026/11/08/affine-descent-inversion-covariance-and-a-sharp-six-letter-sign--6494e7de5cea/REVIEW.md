# Review of affine descent–inversion covariance

## Correctness — PASS
All claims are exact for uniformly permuted fixed multiplicities. The swap involution, reversal symmetry, triple-sampling expansion and cancellation give a complete algebraic proof of the positional profile and its aggregate covariance. The six-letter sharpness follows from the bound on each signed multiplicity gap and the unique extremal profile. Actual independent exact enumeration is provided by `CHECK.py` (161 small multiplicity profiles, 2,356 arithmetic and symmetry assertions and additional two-type tests). A finite check is not substituted for proof. Assumptions and the size-two boundary are explicit.

## Originality — PASS with qualified priority
The exact position-resolved affine profile and the strict sign-transition/minimal-six boundary were not found in the inspected primary work. Conger and Viswanath (arXiv:math/0508242) give joint probabilities of inversion-pair indicators, and Thiel (arXiv:1302.6708) gives an exact bivariate inv/maj recurrence and asymptotic mixed correlation. Both are genuine stronger underlying moment resources: some unweighted or weighted sums are derivable, and neither the moment-generating setup nor known marginal distributions are claimed as new. The point of the proposed result is the *closed positional law and sharp qualitative sign classification*, not a newly introduced statistic. The possibility of implicit full coverage in a prior specialized calculation is the principal residual priority risk; the review does not constitute independent historical certification.

## Value — PASS
The same multiset gives different dependence signs at different descent positions, with at most one sign crossing. A negative covariance between two classical equidistributed Mahonian statistics first becomes possible at the exact smallest length six, in one strictly ordered composition. Such sharp finite-sample dependence information is motivated by run/inversion statistics for shuffled sequences with repeated keys, and is not a routine new table entry. The affine law supplies all positions and is used to prove a universal sharp boundary.

## Limitations
Uniform fixed-multiset law only. No claim of novelty for MacMahon equidistribution, the raw mixed generating function, ordinary inversion moments, or asymptotic bivariate normality. There is no independent validation or priority adjudication.
