For a uniformly shuffled ordered multiset, the covariance between the inversion count and a descent at a given position is exactly affine in the position, with slope determined by a signed cubic multiplicity statistic. This gives a sharp sign boundary: the major index and inversion number have nonnegative covariance for every word of length at most five, but a profile of five smaller and one larger letter has covariance \(-5/12\) at length six.
