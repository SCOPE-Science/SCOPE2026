For \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge3\), deleting any \(t\) independent cross-edges changes the entire hop-total-Roman weight polynomial by exactly \(-t z^2(H_a(z)+H_b(z))+t z^4\), where \(H_s(z)=(z+z^2)(1+z+z^2)^{s-1}-z(1+z)^{s-1}+z^s\). Thus every coefficient responds affinely to the number of deleted matching edges; the complete set of weight-four optimal functions has size \(ab-t\).
