For every generalized period-doubling base \(b\ge3\), the two unbordered factors at every length \(n\ge2\) have explicit affine occurrence sets obtained from the zero positions of the underlying automatic word. Their return gaps are always \(b^{\lceil\log_b n\rceil}\) or twice that value, with universal relative frequencies \(1-1/b\) and \(1/b\), and their exact occurrence density is \(1/((b+1)b^{\lceil\log_b n\rceil-1})\).
