# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The independence of individual parent choices gives exact indicator covariances; every summation index and exceptional root term is explicit. The harmonic profile was checked by enumeration through eight vertices and independent exact arithmetic over sizes through 120. The finite sign transition follows from strict discrete convexity; the scaling transition follows by harmonic convergence. No computation substitutes for proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Janson (https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.20046) treats aggregate outdegree-frequency counts and their Gaussian covariance, and does not directly imply the marked-vertex birth-indexed harmonic profile. A published root-only covariance identity (https://github.com/published-finding corpus/2026/tree/main/2026/10/4/SCOPE-equal-share-covariance-of-root-degree-and-leaves-in-a-random-rec--ab0acf44833f) covers exactly the \(i=1\) special case; no novelty is claimed there. The all-interior sign reversal is the final claim. Searches for fixed-vertex leaf covariances and related aliases produced the root identity but no stronger birth-indexed law. Residual risk: older work on joint labeled-node degrees and leaf totals may contain an equivalent statement; not every older full paper was accessible.

**Value — PASS.** The degree of a labeled vertex records a local preferential temporal advantage, whereas total leaves quantify global fringe structure. The exact sign reversal at a universal fractional birth time provides a natural, nontrivial structural phase boundary; the sign is not apparent from aggregate degree-frequency central limit theorems or from the root-only result.
