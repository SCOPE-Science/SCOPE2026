# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With hazard gamma/(a+1), exact renewal masses p_j=gamma(1-gamma)_{j-1}/j!, v_n=(gamma)_n/n! and survival s_n=(1-gamma)_n/n! give the age law v_{t-a}s_a. For fixed nontrivial z, the age-weight sequence has bounded variation O(v_t+s_t), so Abel summation makes E(z^{A_t}) vanish. First-reset decomposition isolates z^k times no-reset H, a Riemann sum gives the incomplete-Beta limit, and splitting the first-reset time into macroscopic and terminal windows forces its complex remainder to zero. For fixed k the renewal probability tends to zero, yielding z^k. Independent forward age Markov calculation for gamma=1/2,2/3; z=-1,i; and (t,k)=(4,3),(8,5) reproduced exact no-reset convolution; the full first-reset conditional identity was algebraically reconstructed from path conditioning.
