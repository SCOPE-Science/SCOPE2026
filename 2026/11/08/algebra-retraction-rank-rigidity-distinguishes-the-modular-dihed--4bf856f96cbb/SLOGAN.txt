Over every characteristic-two finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the dihedral group algebra \(\mathbb F_q[D_8]\) has exactly \(2q^5\) nontrivial algebra retractions, all onto two-dimensional dual-number subalgebras, whereas the quaternion group algebra \(\mathbb F_q[Q_8]\) has none. Their complete idempotent endomorphism rank polynomials are \(1+2q^5z^2+z^8\) and \(1+z^8\), respectively.
