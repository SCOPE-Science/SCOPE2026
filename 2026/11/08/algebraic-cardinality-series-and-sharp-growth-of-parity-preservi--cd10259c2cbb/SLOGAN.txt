The order-, fence-, and parity-preserving inverse submonoid of partial injections on a finite chain has an explicit algebraic cardinality generating function, with coefficients \(1,2,4,10,22,52,120,\ldots\). Its order grows as \(C\rho^{-n}/\sqrt n\), where \(\rho\) is the positive root of \(1-3z+2z^2-2z^3\) and \(\rho^{-1}=2.5213797068\ldots\), making this monoid exponentially sparse among all increasing partial injections.
