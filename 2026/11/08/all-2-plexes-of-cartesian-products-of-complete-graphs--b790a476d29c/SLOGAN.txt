Every \(2\)-plex in a Cartesian product of complete graphs is built from coordinate-line cliques, three-corner squares, full squares, or sets of at most two vertices; the complete all-set polynomial follows. Its inclusion-maximal \(2\)-plexes are exactly distant pairs, full coordinate lines, and squares, yielding an exact polynomial and arbitrarily long gaps in maximal sizes for homogeneous Hamming graphs.
