For the \(3\times4\) chessboard torus, quotients by all thirty subgroups of the four-column permutation group fall into exactly six homeomorphism types: torus, sphere, Klein bottle, Möbius band, annulus, and disk. A complete barycentric-incidence census distinguishes all eleven subgroup conjugacy classes; in particular, the full symmetric quotient is a disk and the alternating quotient is a sphere.
