In the binary-digit-sum word modulo any \(N\ge3\), a factor of the form \(X\sigma(X)\sigma^2(X)\), with \(\sigma\) the cyclic letter shift, exists exactly when the root length \(|X|\) is a power of two. Every starting position is described by explicit intervals indexed by binary valuations, and the first such factor for root length \(2^e\) begins at \(2^e(2^N-2)\).
