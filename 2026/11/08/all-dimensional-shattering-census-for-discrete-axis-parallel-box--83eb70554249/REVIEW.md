# Scientific review: all-dimensional shattering census for discrete axis-parallel boxes

**Correctness — PASS.** Every nonempty support of size at least three is shattered exactly when each point is a strict minimum or maximum in some coordinate. The converse uses bounding boxes of nonempty chosen sub-supports and a distinct singleton witness for the empty labeling, justified by the finite grid bound \(N\geq1+K\). The local inclusion–exclusion counts the unique extrema of specified rows, accounts for opposite extreme pairs via \(E_m(n)\), and excludes all triple intersections. A second inclusion–exclusion and division by \(m!\) give the exact coefficients. The upper bound, witness construction and top-coefficient factorization are proved. The singleton-domain and pair exceptions are explicit. Direct independent trace enumeration succeeded on all stated finite tests.

**Originality — PASS for the higher-dimensional theorem, subject to historical-priority risk.** An accessible earlier calculation for geodesically convex subsets of \(P_m\square P_n\) already gives the exact polynomial for two chain factors (including the empty hypothesis), so the planar count is not novel; small-domain low-degree conventions differ when the empty hypothesis is omitted. The earlier formula has degree at most four; it does not imply the new formula in arbitrarily many factors, its degree growing with the number and lengths of factors, or the factorized top coefficient. Gillibert–Lachmann–Müllner (arXiv:2004.13861, Introduction and Sections 2–4) state the familiar nonperiodic dimension bound and study periodic toroidal boxes, not the coefficientwise census for every finite chain product. Moran–Rashtchian (arXiv:1511.08245, Sections 1–2) treat algebraic bounds for abstract shattered systems rather than discrete-box enumeration. Existing anchored-orthant results use one-sided private extrema and do not imply the simultaneous minimum/maximum local counts or the alphabet-dependent leading coefficient. Exact-equivalence, broader-coverage and alternative-terminology searches found no direct covering statement; that is a qualified assessment, not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** The all-parameter counting polynomial is a natural finite invariant for a standard definable range space, not merely a dimension restatement. The coefficientwise formula handles arbitrary chain lengths and coordinate ties; its top coefficient factorizes despite the dependent role assignments, and it gives exact random-support shattering probabilities. Grids with equal VC dimension can have markedly different full profiles, such as a ternary square and a four-dimensional Boolean cube. This is an unbounded-dimensional, all-coefficient extension beyond the known planar census, rather than an incremental planar numeric improvement.

Limitations: no result for periodic, equal-side-length or fixed-volume boxes. Broader historical searches and independent mathematical review remain worthwhile.
