A smooth quartic del Pezzo surface over a finite field can have every rational point on its full exceptional-curve boundary exactly when the field has 3 or 5 elements. The split 5-element-field example is classical; a concrete new nonsplit example over the 3-element field is the blow-up of one rational point and two conjugate degree-two points in the projective plane. The earlier published general exceptional-boundary zeta formula is credited as prior work.
