For the complement of all sixteen geometric exceptional curves on any smooth quartic del Pezzo surface over a finite field, the full Hasse–Weil zeta function is determined explicitly from the eighteen signed Weyl-cycle types, accounting for conjugate branches at rational nodes. Beyond the classical split \(q=5\) case, a concrete nonsplit quartic del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbb F_3\) has every rational point on its exceptional boundary; these are the only two field sizes admitting such a surface.
