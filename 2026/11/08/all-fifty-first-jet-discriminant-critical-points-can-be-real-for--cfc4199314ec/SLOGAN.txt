The first-jet Bombieri–Weyl discriminant-distance function of the real ternary cubic \(x^3-3xy^2-\varepsilon z(x^2+y^2)+2z^3\) has at least fifty distinct nondegenerate real critical points on the unit sphere for every sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\). Consequently a nonempty open set of generic ternary cubics attains the full complex count of fifty, answering the three-variable cubic case of a 2026 all-real sharpness problem.
