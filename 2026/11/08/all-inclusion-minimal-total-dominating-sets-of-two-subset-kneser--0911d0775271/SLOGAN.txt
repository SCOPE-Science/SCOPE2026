For every \(n\ge4\), all inclusion-minimal total dominating sets of \(KG(n,2)\) arise from only four auxiliary edge-graph types: \(3K_2\), \(K_3\sqcup K_2\), \(C_5\), and \(K_4\). Their exact size polynomial is \(15\binom{n}{6}z^3+10\binom{n}{5}z^4+12\binom{n}{5}z^5+\binom{n}{4}z^6\); in particular, the upper total domination number is \(6\) for every \(n\ge4\).
