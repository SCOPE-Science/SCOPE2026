# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof separates the two-token graph into two within-part layers and a mixed layer. Because these layers have no internal edges, maximality forces the entire residual mixed rectangle. Where it is nonempty, the selected within-part pairs must be complete on their endpoint supports. These conditions are both necessary and sufficient, giving a bijection and a coefficient-wise polynomial. The integer identity in the minimum-size argument is exact, including the boundary cases \(m=n=2\), \(m=2<n\), and \(3\le m\le n\). The standard-library verifier independently enumerates all maximal independent sets by complement cliques for \(2\le m,n\le5\) and checks the formulas on larger parameters. No finite experiment is used as proof of an infinite statement.

## Originality

PASS (historical priority provisional). The nearest October 2026 article by Acosta Troncoso, Caldera Caldera and Rivera provides a necessary-and-sufficient description of *all dominating sets* in the two-token biclique. This has substantial overlap in the incidence structure, but does **not** enumerate or explicitly classify the *independent* dominating sets and their cardinalities; the new rigidity from simultaneous independence and maximality, the closed polynomial and the sharp minimum-set census require an additional argument. Chinman and collaborators also establish ordinary domination numbers, a weaker/different invariant. De Alba and collaborators determine the largest independent-set size; Abdelmalek and collaborators determine when token graphs are well-covered. Neither provides the complete maximal-independent-set enumerator. Search comparisons against alternate terminology (independent domination, independence complex facets, symmetric squares, double vertex graphs), candidate coefficient sequences, and related mathematical result records found no covering enumeration. Full text of one highly relevant preprint was unavailable and leaves a real residual risk; no claim of uniqueness is based on failed searching alone.

## Mathematical value

PASS. A maximum independent-set size or an ordinary domination number cannot determine how many maximal independent sets exist at each size. The theorem completely enumerates the facets of the independence complex in a natural two-parameter family of token graphs, gives a product formula for their total number and exact independent domination numbers and minimum-set counts. The rigidity collapses a graph with \(\binom{m+n}{2}\) token-configurations to two subsets of the original bipartition. These are motivated full-family invariants, not a parameter substitution, a small numerical improvement, or a finite census with an arbitrary cutoff.

## Closest literature and residual limits

The 2026 general dominating-set characterization is the closest potential implication and is expressly distinguished from the present complete independence restriction and enumerator. The known maximum independent-set size and known non-well-coveredness are compatible with, but insufficient to imply, the coefficients. The accessible scholarly rendering of the 2026 complete-domination paper was inspected in its introduction, dominating-set characterization and concluding sections; its mathematical formulas in parts of the rendering were imperfect. Full text of arXiv:2610.28936 remained unavailable. The result is for ordinary two-token biclique graphs only. Independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
