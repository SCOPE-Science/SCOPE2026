# Scientific review: all maximizers of two length-two palindromic duplication spheres

## Correctness — PASS
The all-parameter proof partitions two-step histories into five overlapping local types and commuting disjoint-block types. It counts the canonical histories, identifies the first equality edge for every source with unequal neighbors, and supplies a complete table of adjacent-source-letter witnesses distinguishing every canonical pair. A repeated adjacent source letter collapses two specific otherwise-distinct local histories. Direct finite tests cover 17,337 source words and symbolic canonical comparisons through length 27, independently corroborating but not replacing the proof.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence
Zabokritskiy (arXiv:2609.00779, Theorem 5.1, 2026-09-01) proves the maximum two-duplication sphere size and attains it by alternating words. The inspected theorem does not classify all equality cases. Here all adjacent-unequal words are proved maximal, including nonalternating examples over alphabets with at least three symbols, and all maximizers are counted. Lenz--Wachter-Zeh--Yaakobi (arXiv:1707.00052, Section 3.3) treat a single duplication, not two. Related finite binary code-size records involve packing sets rather than maximizer words. Unindexed or implicit coverage remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result settles a natural complete equality classification for a sharp extremal coding-channel invariant. The precise count of extremizers and the associated probability for uniformly sampled sources distinguish the typical from the worst-case two-step channel behavior and add information not present in the prior maximum-value theorem.

## Scientific limitations
The theorem concerns exactly two sequential length-two palindromic duplications. It neither derives all nonmaximal sphere sizes nor treats longer blocks or optimal coding rates. Symbolic and exhaustive computations are consistency checks, not the basis for the general proof. Independent review and definitive priority verification have not occurred.
