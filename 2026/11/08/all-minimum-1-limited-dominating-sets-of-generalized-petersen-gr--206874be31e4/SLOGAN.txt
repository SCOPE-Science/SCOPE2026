Every generalized Petersen graph \(GP(n,2)\), for \(n\ge5\), has exactly \(2+10\mathbf1_{5\mid n}\) minimum \(1\)-limited dominating sets: the two full shores and, precisely when \(5\mid n\), ten additional sets determined by two period-five column patterns. Equivalently, it has exactly \(1+5\mathbf1_{5\mid n}\) unordered vertex cuts whose crossing edges form a perfect matching.
