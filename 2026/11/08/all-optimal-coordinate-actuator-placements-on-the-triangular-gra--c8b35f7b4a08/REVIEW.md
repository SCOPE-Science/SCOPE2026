# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The identities \(A=B^\top B-2I\) and \(BB^\top=(n-2)I+J\) yield all eigenspaces and multiplicities without numerical approximations. The PBH condition makes restriction to selected edge coordinates the exact control criterion. For the largest eigenspace, it is equivalent to independence of columns of the unsigned incidence matrix of the unselected graph; an elementary sign-alternation proof characterizes invertibility exactly by odd-unicyclic components. The smaller eigenspace is ruled out by a complement-edge cut bound except for the balanced \(K_{3,3}\) selected graph, occurring precisely for ten complements at order six. Small cases and labelled counts are proved separately. An exact checker enumerates all minimum-cardinality selections through seven original-graph vertices. The proof covers all integers in the stated domain; the finite check is corroboration only.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature coverage risks

The spectral multiplicity lower bound itself is not claimed as new: it follows the general framework of Yuan et al. (2013). The all-position criterion by odd-unicyclic complements, the unique six-vertex exception, and the labelled optimal-placement generating function were not found in the searched related literature. Yuan's full text addresses generic minimum driver numbers and column operations but does not give those graph-specific combinatorial statements. Dai's full text addresses general critical-set theory and selected tree networks, not \(J(n,2)\). Godsil's single characteristic-vector actuator is not equivalent to independent coordinate actuation; Trefois–Delvenne address strong structural rather than fixed-weight control. The accessible Farrugia–Sciriha repository abstract is relevant but its full text was restricted, leaving a nonzero publication-priority risk. Related indexed material using unsigned incidence for a representation-theoretic sampling problem concerns another observable and cannot imply this exact all-eigenspace input criterion without additional work. Database searches are not an exhaustive uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A spectral-multiplicity number alone does not tell which physical nodes to actuate. The theorem gives a deterministic necessary-and-sufficient graphical test for every optimal placement in an infinite, classical, dense graph family. Its odd-cycle pseudoforest description is structurally surprising because it constrains **unactuated** pairs, and the balanced \(K_{3,3}\) failure shows why the dominant eigenspace test alone is insufficient. The generating function counts every optimal selection; the six-vertex correction changes an otherwise valid incidence enumeration. These address exact input placement and design diversity rather than a textbook restatement of the PBH criterion.

## Limits and comparison

Unit-weight full-state Laplacian; distinct independent coordinate inputs only. The multiplicity lower bound is prior work. General weighted or single-broadcast control is not addressed. The full Farrugia–Sciriha document could not be examined from the accessible repository, and nonindexed specialist treatments remain a residual priority risk.
