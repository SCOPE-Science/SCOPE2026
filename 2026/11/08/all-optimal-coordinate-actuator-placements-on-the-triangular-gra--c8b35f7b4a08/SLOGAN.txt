For the unweighted triangular graph \(J(n,2)=L(K_n)\), all minimum independent coordinate-actuator sets are characterized by odd-unicyclic spanning complements, with exactly ten additional failures at \(n=6\). The resulting optimal-placement enumeration has an exact rooted-tree generating function; the minimum number of actuators is \(\binom n2-n\) for every \(n\ge5\).
