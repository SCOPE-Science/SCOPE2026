For complete multipartite social networks, every minimum-envy assignment of strictly ordered houses is obtained by sorting the parts' descending priorities \(n_i-2k-1\), with unrestricted tie-breaking only between different parts at a common priority. This gives a value-gap-independent classification, an exact minimum-envy formula, and the exact labeled optimizer count \(\bigl(\prod_i n_i!\bigr)\bigl(\prod_w m_w!\bigr)\).
