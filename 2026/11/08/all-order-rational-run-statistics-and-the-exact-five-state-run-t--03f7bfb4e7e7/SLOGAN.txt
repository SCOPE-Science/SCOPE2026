The maximal runs of the half-scaled base-\(3/2\) Thue–Morse word have lengths only \(1\) through \(5\); every finite consecutive-run pattern has an effectively computable rational frequency. Their exact adjacent-run distribution is a symmetric five-by-five matrix with eight forbidden ordered transitions and marginals \((1/4,1/4,1/3,1/12,1/12)\).
