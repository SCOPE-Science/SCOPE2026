Standard variable-step BDF2 on any exactly alternating pair of positive step lengths is A-stable for every nonzero scalar mode in the closed left half-plane, even when adjacent-step ratios are arbitrarily large. Yet at fixed total-period decay \(q\), the extreme-ratio monodromy approaches the trapezoidal multiplier \((2-q)/(2+q)\) plus a zero mode, so its stiff-decay and ratio limits do not commute.
