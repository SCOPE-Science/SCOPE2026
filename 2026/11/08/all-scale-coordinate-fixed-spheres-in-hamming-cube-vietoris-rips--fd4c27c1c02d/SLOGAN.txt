For every binary \(n\)-cube and every inclusive Hamming radius \(0\leq r<n\), the geometric coordinate-permutation fixed space is a single sphere \(S^{\lfloor\lfloor r/2\rfloor/(n-r)\rfloor}\). An explicit variable-band graph collapse proves the all-scale formula, and simultaneous bit complementation acts freely with top-homology sign \((-1)^{1+\lfloor\lfloor r/2\rfloor/(n-r)\rfloor}\).
