# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The domain is fully quantified: two positive run lengths and an integer number of complete periods at least two, with one-based linear factors. Necessity is forced by the single letters and the two transition factors. The periodic central-position lemma, proved using overlapping occurrence intervals for length-at-least-period factors, and the exhaustive short-factor transition classification establish sufficiency in all three run-length cases. A standalone direct enumeration compares all candidate pairs and every factor; 360 cases passed. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic risk

The general power bound of Mantaci et al. (2019), Proposition 4, permits optimal sizes two or three and does not classify all positions; their simple-run construction gives an example rather than the all-pair census. Restivo et al. (2022) prove qualitative boundedness and study span/leftmost complexity but do not give these counts. Prior public work already reports the alternating subcase \((01)^k\) with \(k^2-1\) pairs; that special case is credited and is not separately claimed. Searches of related research records and the accessible literature did not find the formula for arbitrary positive run lengths. Unindexed older results remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The natural number of *all* optimal attractor sets distinguishes repeated words whose minimum attractor size is identically two. The complete classification reveals a sharp stability phenomenon (independence of both run lengths once each exceeds one) and a quadratic regime change when either run has length one; it additionally proves a length-two testing criterion that is not valid for arbitrary periodic words. This is an infinite-parameter structural enumeration, not a fixed-size census or a routine normalization.

## Limitations

No independent validation has been performed. Prior-result accessibility was incomplete for one public repository record, whose known conclusion was only used to identify already covered alternating subcases. The present result is confined to linear complete powers of two-run binary words.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
