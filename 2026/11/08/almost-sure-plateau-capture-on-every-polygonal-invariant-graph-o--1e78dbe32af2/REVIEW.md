# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The graph construction and its four quadrant tangent multipliers are explicit in [1], Lemmas 21–22. The standalone exact-rational script checks all 16 possibilities: the only collapsed tangent pairs are the two plateau directions, and every other branch expands arclength by \(\sqrt2\) or \(2\). The proof divides finite four-step surviving branches into affine intervals, derives a strictly contracting BV estimate \(\operatorname{Var}(P^4g)\le(3/4)\operatorname{Var}(g)+C\|g\|_1\), obtains bounded-variation compactness for surviving densities, and rules out a positive survivor mass using the published open-dense preimage theorem. The almost-sure result is for canonical finite graphs and arclength; degenerate singleton graphs have zero arclength. No finite calculation is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS on inspected evidence.** [1], Proposition 23, proves the capture set is open dense, not full measure; its Theorem C classifies the limiting orbits on that open dense set. [2], Section 5 and Theorem 3, explicitly states that full measure is established only for special parameter regions, not the entire family. The exact all-parameter statement goes beyond these results. Related surviving-set and Lasota–Yorke theory [3] gives general methods but no identified theorem immediately covering the stated canonical graphs and every parameter without the additional BV and density argument. Semantic-record searches and accessible prior claims were compared for implication, including special-parameter variants. An unindexed equivalent application of general theory remains a research-priority risk.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The conclusion resolves a natural explicit full-measure question from the source literature, strengthening topological genericity to probabilistic typicality for every parameter, including graphs with positive entropy. The absence of absolutely continuous graph-invariant probabilities is a meaningful dynamical consequence. This is not presented as a new graph construction, a planar-area result, or a classification of the surviving exceptional fractal set.
