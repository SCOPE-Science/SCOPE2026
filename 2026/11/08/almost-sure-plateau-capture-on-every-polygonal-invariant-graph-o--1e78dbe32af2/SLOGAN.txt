For the polygonal invariant graphs of the Cima–Gasull–Mañosa–Mañosas piecewise-affine plane map with negative first parameter, the open dense set of plateau-preimages actually has full Euclidean arclength measure at **every** parameter. This rules out absolutely continuous invariant probabilities on every nondegenerate graph, including chaotic parameter regimes not covered by earlier special-case arguments.
