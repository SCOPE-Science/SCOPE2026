For four-capped Hamming metrics on growing nonbinary alphabets, the sharp negative-type threshold undergoes a discrete-Charlier phase transition at alphabet/dimension ratio \(q/n=1/2\). Below that ratio the critical Fourier layer eventually stabilizes **exactly** at full weight, with an alphabet-independent exponent; above it another layer becomes critical, while at fixed dimension the exponent decays as \(q^{-2}\).
