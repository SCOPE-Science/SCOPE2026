For classical RK4 diffusion on every nonbinary Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\) with \(d\ge3\), entrywise positivity holds exactly up to an explicit sharp timestep: Hamming-distance-three entries control it for all \(d\ge4\) and for \(d=3,q\le12\), but neighboring entries control it from \(d=3,q=13\) onward. This identifies a strict stability--positivity gap and a dimension-specific alphabet threshold, without claiming intermediate-stage positivity.
