# Scientific review — alphabet-relabeling symmetry of de Bruijn cycles

## Correctness
PASS. An invariant primitive cyclic de Bruijn word forces a freely acting symbol permutation whose cycles have one common order. Quotienting the de Bruijn graph gives a voltage obstruction: total monodromy is zero for all orders \(n\ge3\), while at order two it generates the deck group only for an involution with an odd number of symbol pairs. BEST and the directed matrix-tree theorem count all quotient Euler tours, and elementary orbit indexing gives the order-one count. Direct small-alphabet checks and independent exact quotient Euler-tour enumeration corroborate boundary cases; neither is substituted for the general proof.

## Originality
PASS on a best-knowledge, qualified comparison. Binary complement/self-dual cases are known and are not the novelty asserted. The contribution is the uniform classification for **every** nonidentity alphabet permutation and **every** order, plus an exact cyclic count in the sole positive order-two regime. Burris–Motta–Shipman investigate reversal/unoriented blocks, rather than symbolwise permutations. Kari–Xu's pseudo sequences also concern an antimorphic variant. Etzion's 2025 discussion covers a broader notion of self-dual cyclic sequences but does not in the inspected portions imply this ordinary complete de Bruijn fixed-cycle enumerator. The 1987 Etzion primary article was not available as full text; its possible implications, especially for nonbinary cases, are a material priority risk. No negative literature search is treated as proof of priority.

## Value
PASS. Complete all-length classification and explicit enumerator resolve a nontrivial symmetry feasibility problem for universal cycles; the voltage mechanism pinpoints exactly why a superficially natural extension from order two fails at all higher orders. The formula counts actual labeled-word designs, not a cosmetic reformulation of a known optimum. This can inform enumeration and sampling of relabeling-constrained universal cycles. The general graph-Euler correspondence and known binary consequences alone are not new contributions.

## Closest antecedents and limitations

The result excludes reversal and reverse-complement, pseudo-de Bruijn covers and cyclic classes under all alphabet permutations.
