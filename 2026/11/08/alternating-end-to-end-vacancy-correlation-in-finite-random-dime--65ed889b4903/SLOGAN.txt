In length-two random dimer parking on a finite line, the two boundary vacancy indicators are negatively correlated at every odd size and positively correlated at every even size: \((-1)^n\operatorname{Cov}(U_n,V_n)>0\) for \(n\ge3\). Their correlation signal is sharply superfactorial: \(\operatorname{Cov}(U_n,V_n)\sim(-1)^n2^{n-2}/(n-1)!\).
