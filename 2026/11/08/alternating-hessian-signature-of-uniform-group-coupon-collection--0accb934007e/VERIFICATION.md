The exact-rational standalone checker `verify.py` uses Python's standard library. Run `python3 verify.py` from the extracted file directory. It computes the proper-subset Hessian action separately from the proposed Johnson-level eigenvalue formula, checks alternating signs for small instances, and tests actual expected coverage times at perturbed package distributions. The general sign conclusion follows from the analytical forward-difference argument, not from finite enumeration. The full text of Schilling (2021) was unavailable for direct priority comparison; no independent validation is claimed.
