# Correctness

**PASS.** The two-variable finite generator has leaf transitions at rates \((n-k)(a+hz)\) and \(k(a+h(1-z))\) and hub flips at rates \(a+hk/n\) or \(a+h(1-k/n)\). Uniform generator convergence on the compact state space gives a stationary limit process. After normalization, its deterministic speeds are \(-y\) and \(1-y\), and switching rates are \(lpha+eta y\) and \(lpha+eta(1-y)\) with \(2lpha+eta=1\). Zero stationary current and the forward equation imply the unique integrable density \(y^{lpha-1}(1-y)^{lpha-1}/B(lpha,lpha)\), with conditional hub probability \(y\). Unique stationary law fixes the full sequence, not merely subsequences. Independent finite generator construction and symbolic moment checks run successfully from the included source.

# Mathematical value

**PASS.** Star networks are a canonical degree-heterogeneous setting where one highly connected endogenous agent couples many conditionally noisy leaves. The exact limiting stationary measure, joint hub coupling and finite-n variance reveal persistent, non-Gaussian macroscopic polarization under every fixed positive noise rate, an effect not visible from mean-field self-averaging and qualitatively unlike dense regular-network concentration. This is a natural kinetic invariant rather than an arbitrary finite parameter slice.

# Limitations

The result is restricted to a star with fixed \(a,h>0\) and concerns stationary states only. Its continuous stationary law is a limit and does not assert a finite-n beta law. The original full 1995 paper could not be inspected; publication priority and independent verification remain open.
