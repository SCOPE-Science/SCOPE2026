For a noisy voter process on a growing star, the stationary fraction of positive leaves converges to an explicit affine \(\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\alpha)\) law with \(\alpha=2a(a+h)/(2a+h)^2\), rather than concentrating at one half. Even at fixed positive spontaneous-flip rate, a single hub preserves strictly positive macroscopic polarization variance, with an exact formula at every finite star size.
