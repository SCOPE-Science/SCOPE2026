For the natural compact survivors of an explicit mixing infinite-entropy interval map, the exact finite-core entropy is controlled by a tridiagonal Perron matrix, and its sharp boundary correction is an Airy eigenvalue. Specifically, \(h_K=\log(6K)-(a_1 72^{1/3}/6)K^{-2/3}+o(K^{-2/3})\), with \(a_1\) the first positive Airy-zero magnitude.
