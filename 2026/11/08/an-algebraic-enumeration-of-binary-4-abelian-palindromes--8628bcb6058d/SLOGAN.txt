The generating function for binary \(4\)-abelian palindromes is algebraic and explicit: \(2z+4z^3+(2z^2-4z^3+4z^4-8z^6)/\sqrt{(1-2z)(1-2z+4z^6)}\). Their proportion among binary length-\(n\) words is asymptotic to \(1/(2\sqrt{\pi n})\); the exact recognition test requires a mirrored three-symbol boundary and equal counts of `0011` and `1100`.
