For the published Haar-random simplex-gradient expected-decrease model with \(c\) parallel evaluation cores and ambient dimension \(d\ge2\), the optimal subspace size is exactly \(p=1\) for one core and \(p=\min(c,d)\) for at least two cores. The only tie is \(p=2\) and \(p=4\) with two cores and \(d\ge4\); a Gamma-ratio inequality proves the complete all-integer statement previously supported only by finite numerical inspection.
