The cospectral sixteen-vertex rook and Shrikhande graphs have strictly ordered zero-field Ising partition functions at every nonzero real coupling, in both ferro- and antiferromagnetic regimes: \(Z_R(K)>Z_S(K)\). Their cut-polynomial difference factors exactly as \(16q^{12}(q^2-1)^8(3q^4+2q^2+1)\), and their even-subgraph expansions first diverge at order eight by \(96\).
