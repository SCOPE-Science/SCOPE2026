Every smooth symmetric minimal bidegree-\((4,4)\) filling curve on \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) has a genus-nine Jacobian of \(3\)-rank eight and \(a\)-number one. The factor-swap quotient is an ordinary genus-three plane quartic, so its six-dimensional Prym is almost ordinary, with \(3\)-rank five and \(a\)-number one.
