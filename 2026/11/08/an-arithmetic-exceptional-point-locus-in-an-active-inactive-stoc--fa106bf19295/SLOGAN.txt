A particle that alternates between clockwise motion and immobility on a finite stochastic ring has a completely classified set of positive-rate exceptional points: exactly \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) for an \(n\)-position ring, all lying on one parabolic rate curve. At each point, the Markov generator develops two conjugate size-two Jordan blocks and an exactly observable, linearly enhanced damped Fourier oscillation.
