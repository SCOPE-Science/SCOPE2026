For independently jittered integration of the triangle \(0\le y<\alpha x\), the sharp \(n^{-3}\) variance coefficient is a smooth cubic at every irrational slope but has a strictly positive arithmetic spike at every rational slope. The exact spike at reduced slope \(a/b\) is \(1/(60ab^3)\), so the coefficient is discontinuous at every rational orientation and continuous at every irrational one.
