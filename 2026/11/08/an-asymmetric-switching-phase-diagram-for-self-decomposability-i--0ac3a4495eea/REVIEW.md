## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete natural two-rate phase diagram, not a single isolated parameter example. The classification establishes which stochastic trap laws can be represented by isotropic Lévy noise in an ordinary OU trap, exhibits distinct scalar and joint obstructions, and shows the surprising disappearance of the on-to-off rate from the multivariate threshold. The explicit independent-jump realization makes the classification constructive. This is useful for distinguishing stationary distribution equivalence from temporal mechanistic equivalence.

## Scientific limits and nearby work
The result assumes two Markovian switching states, isotropic thermal diffusion, a fully inactive off state, and linear confinement when on. It concerns invariant spatial laws only, not process-level equivalence, dissipation, or temporal correlations. Classical polar decomposition and the Lévy-driven OU representation are standard ingredients, and the equal-rate slice is prior coverage. Relevant primary full texts: Alston–Cocconi–Bertrand, arXiv:2202.02630v1, especially Section 4.2.1 and Appendix A; Frydel, arXiv:2407.12213v2, especially Sections III.1 and V; and classical class-\(L\) theory in Sato (1999).
