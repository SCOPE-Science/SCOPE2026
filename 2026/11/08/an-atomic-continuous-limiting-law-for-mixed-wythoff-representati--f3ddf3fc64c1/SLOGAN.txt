The mixed lower-plus-upper Wythoff representation count has a uniform phase profile: \(r(n)=(n-1)L(\{n/\varphi\})+O(\log(n+1))\). After normalization by \(n\), the counts converge to a mixture of density \(2\) on \((0,\varphi^{-2})\) and an atom of mass \(\varphi^{-3}\) at \(\varphi^{-2}\), with quantitative Lipschitz-observable error \(O(\log^2(N+1)/N)\).
