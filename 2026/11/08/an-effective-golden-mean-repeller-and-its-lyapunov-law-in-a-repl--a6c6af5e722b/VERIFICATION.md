The standalone `VERIFY.py` reproduces the rational bounds and tests 25 selected endpoint parameter pairs, nine nontrivial period-two branch itineraries, and their derivative asymptotics; it is not a substitute for the proof. Run `python3 VERIFY.py` from the directory containing this file and the script.

Its edge frequencies are exact, and the uniform Lyapunov expansion is derived analytically from the map recurrence.
