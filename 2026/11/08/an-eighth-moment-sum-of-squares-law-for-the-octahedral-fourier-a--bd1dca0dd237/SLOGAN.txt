The six-frequency octahedral Fourier polynomial has the exact eighth-moment identity \(\int|P|^8=6390+3360|S|^2+36|T+4Q|^2\), so its sharp range is \([6390,44730]\). The minimum is achieved precisely when the three antipodal coefficient products form a rotated cubic-root triple; it can be achieved with \(q\)-th-root coefficients exactly when \(3\mid q\), with \(2q^4\) minimizing arrays.
