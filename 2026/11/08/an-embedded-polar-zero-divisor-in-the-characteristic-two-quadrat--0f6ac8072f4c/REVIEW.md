The binary-quadratic resultant is the smallest nontrivial universal resultant hypersurface whose singular support has positive dimension. The complete scheme structure reveals a geometrically intrinsic embedded associated divisor, a failure of Cohen–Macaulayness and a normal-slice length jump \(1\to3\).
