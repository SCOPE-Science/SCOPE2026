The independence complex of the categorical product \(K_m\times K_n\) admits a canonical symmetry-equivariant strong deformation retraction onto the subdivided complete bipartite graph. Consequently every subgroup quotient has free fundamental group with rank given by vertex- and edge-orbit counts, every subgroup fixed space is classified by fixed grid labels, and the integral first-homology representation is the tensor product of two augmentation lattices with a negative tensor flip under coordinate interchange.
