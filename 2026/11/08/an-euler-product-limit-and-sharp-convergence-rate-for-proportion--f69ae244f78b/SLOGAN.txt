Offering a fixed positive fraction of all coupon types each round, but keeping only one previously unseen type, changes the completion-time excess \(T_N-N\) from a Gumbel-scale phenomenon to a tight Euler \(q\)-product law with explicit probabilities and moments. The approximation is sharp at order \(N^{-1}\) in total variation, with a strictly positive first-order correction to the probability of requiring no extra rounds.
