# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-signing equivalence is proved by a prime cyclotomic zero lemma for nonzero modulations and an exact induced-path construction for the zero modulation. The support properness and prime-order assumptions are used essentially: they supply a zero Fourier coefficient and make the step graph a single cycle whose proper induced subgraphs are paths. The unique zero-delay boundary, singular-value normalization, and sharp counting lower bound were independently walked through. The exact checker exhausts every proper support and signing at primes 3, 5, and 7; numerical tests are not used as an infinite-family proof.

## Originality — PASS under bounded evidence

Bojarovska–Flinth (2015) proves the general nonvanishing ambiguity criterion and the unsigned difference-set examples; the published positive-rational window classification proves a support-only rank law **under positivity**, which cannot imply the present universal adversarial-sign property. The nearest three-tap ambiguity paper treats a fixed progression and support-size law rather than every sign assignment. Searches for signed difference-set Gabor phase retrieval, odd intersection counts, and aliases found no covering theorem. The novelty claim is only the full odd-overlap equivalence, failure-sign construction, and derived robust sharp-sparsity characterization; it does not claim invention of the cyclotomic theorem, Weyl singular values, Singer constructions, or unsigned Gabor phase retrieval.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Support design for recoverability under arbitrary binary calibration/sign errors is a natural adversarial measurement problem. The criterion is a complete necessary and sufficient structural test and is exactly tight at the information-theoretic difference-count support threshold for projective-plane examples. The infinite Paley congruence family supplies structurally nontrivial robust examples beyond a one-off exhaustive census.

## Limits and closest literature

The theorem applies only to nonempty proper supports and signs in odd prime dimension. Arbitrary complex phases can destroy the parity protection; no uniform conditioning bound is asserted. Historical priority remains subject to independent comparison with possibly broader unpublished or differently phrased sign-robustness results.
