For any nonempty proper support \(S\subset\mathbb Z_p\) at odd prime order, **every** binary signing of its indicator gives complete Gabor tomography if and only if every nontrivial cyclic overlap \(|S\cap(S-u)|\) is odd. This criterion has sharp \(k(k-1)\ge p-1\) sparsity and is attained by planar difference sets, including explicit four-site windows at \(p=13\) that tolerate all sign flips.
