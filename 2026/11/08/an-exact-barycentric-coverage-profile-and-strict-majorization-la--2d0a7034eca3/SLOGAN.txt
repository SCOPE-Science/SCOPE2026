For one independent arclength-uniform point on each side of any Euclidean triangle, the probability that their triangle contains a target with barycentric coordinates \(a,b,c\) is exactly \(\sum_{t\in\{a,b,c\}}t(1-t)\log((1-t)/t)\). It is strictly Schur-concave on interior barycentric triples, uniquely maximal at the centroid with value \(2\log2/3\), and zero along the original boundary.\n
