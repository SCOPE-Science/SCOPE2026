For two-seat approval voting, adverse justified-representation tie-breaking is possible only through complementary half-electorates with a duplicated support pattern. Under independent Bernoulli approvals, its exact probability for any number of candidates is a complementary-mask occupancy coefficient; for three candidates it is \(3\binom{n}{n/2}[p(1-p)]^{3n/2}\) when \(n\) is even and zero otherwise.
