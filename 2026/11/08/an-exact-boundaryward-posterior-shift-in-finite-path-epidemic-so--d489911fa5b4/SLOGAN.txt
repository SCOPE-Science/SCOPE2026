For an SI epidemic observed at a known age with precisely the first \(k\) vertices of a finite path infected, the posterior source location shifts strictly toward the physical boundary in monotone-likelihood-ratio order as the hypothetical age increases. Under a uniform prior its MAP source traverses from the center to the boundary in exactly \(\lfloor(k-1)/2\rfloor\) ordered single-vertex transitions, at explicit unique Poisson-tail thresholds.
