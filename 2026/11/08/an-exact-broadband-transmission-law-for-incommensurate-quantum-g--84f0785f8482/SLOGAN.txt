For an incommensurate chain of quantum-graph side resonators with symmetric channel couplings, the high-wavenumber mean transmission has an explicit nonzero limit as the number of resonators grows. Its weak-coupling loss scales as \((2b/\pi)\log(2/b)\), unlike the linear losses of one- and two-resonator arrays, and at \(b=1\) the long-array mean is exactly \(1-2/\pi\).
