For ordinary labelled cycles, open locating-dominating codes are precisely complements of independent sets in the distance-two/four circulant, which is one cycle-square when the order is odd and two cycle-squares when it is even. This gives every code-size coefficient and shows that the extra unit proposed in a 2021 cycle conjecture occurs for lengths congruent to four modulo six, but not for odd lengths congruent to one modulo six; the four-cycle has no such code.
