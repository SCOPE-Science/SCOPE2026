For the three-step Lie algebra of a star \(K_{1,m}\) over every finite field of characteristic two, the exact probability that two uniformly sampled elements commute is \(2(q+1)q^{-2m}-(q+1)^2q^{-3m}+q^{2-4m}\). At fixed field size this probability vanishes as the number of leaves grows, even though the corresponding two-step quotient retains a positive limiting probability \(q^{-2}\).
