On a directed cycle, one ordered sweep of exact single-edge Markov steps has a stationary law controlled exactly by the cyclic ascents of its update order, with invariant-measure error \(u m(n-m)/[n(n-u m)]\) and sharp all-dimension envelope \(\tanh(h/4)\). Independently randomizing the cyclic starting position restores the exact uniform stationary distribution at every positive step size without additional edge evaluations.
