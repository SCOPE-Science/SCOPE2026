For a fixed Hermitian interior eigenvalue, the square complex-Gaussian randomized Rayleigh–Ritz refinement has an exact finite-angle distribution, with expected absolute error \(8\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)+O(\varepsilon^2)\) but root-mean-square error \(2\varepsilon+o(\varepsilon)\). On the same trial plane, deterministic Rayleigh–Ritz has error \(2\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4)\), demonstrating a precise unconditional moment cost not visible in high-probability quadratic-rate guarantees.
