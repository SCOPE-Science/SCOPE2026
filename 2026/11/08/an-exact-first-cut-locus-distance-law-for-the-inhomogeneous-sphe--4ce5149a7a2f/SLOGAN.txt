For the inhomogeneous round spherical lens space \(L(5,2)\), the pairwise geodesic-distance CDF admits an exact elementary formula up to \(\frac12\arccos(-1/4)\). Its first departure from the spherical-ball law is \(\frac{10(\sqrt5-1)}{3\pi}(r-\pi/5)^3\), revealing a cubic rather than quadratic first cut-locus transition.
