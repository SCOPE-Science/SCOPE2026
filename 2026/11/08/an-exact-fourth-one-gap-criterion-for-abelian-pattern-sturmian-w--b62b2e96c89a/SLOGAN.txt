An Abelian pattern Sturmian binary word can begin with \(1\)'s at exactly \(0,1,M,M+t\) precisely when \(t\ge4\) and \(M\bmod t
otin\{0,1,2\}\), for \(M\ge3\). This complete fourth-gap obstruction follows from a residue-class decomposition of the relevant incidence forest and is realized by explicit infinite continuations.
