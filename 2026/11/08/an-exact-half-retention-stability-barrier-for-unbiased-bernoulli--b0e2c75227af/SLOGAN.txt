Classical error feedback with the unbiased Bernoulli compressor \(z\mapsto\xi z/p\) has a sharp scalar-quadratic mean-square stability window \(p>1/2\) and \(0<\eta a<(4p-2)/(3-2p)\). Without error feedback, the identical compressor converges for \(0<\eta a<2p\) at every positive retention probability, so error-feedback memory can destroy all fixed-step mean-square stability at or below half retention.
