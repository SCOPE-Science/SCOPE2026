For a Brownian gas undergoing common weak partial resets, the exact and Gaussian-approximated stationary second moments agree, yet their finite higher-moment thresholds differ strictly. At fixed drift ratio \(c\in(0,1)\), the number of moment orders falsely classified as divergent by the diffusion approximation grows as \([z_c-(1-c)]/s+O(1)\), where \((1-e^{-2z_c})/(2z_c)=c\).
