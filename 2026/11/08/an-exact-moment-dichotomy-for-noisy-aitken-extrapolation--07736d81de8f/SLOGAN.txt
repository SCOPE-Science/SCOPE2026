For Aitken extrapolation of three geometrically converging values with arbitrary jointly Gaussian observation noise, the output has every absolute moment only on two precisely characterized covariance-degeneracy classes; otherwise both tails decay as an explicit constant divided by \(t\), and even its absolute first moment diverges. Exchangeable Gaussian errors retain this heavy tail at every imperfect correlation, while perfectly shared noise recovers an ordinary Gaussian output.
