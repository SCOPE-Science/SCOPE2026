In a uniformly random fixed-edge \(r\)-uniform hypergraph, the covariance between isolated-vertex count and the number of hyperedge pairs overlapping in exactly \(t\) vertices has a strict sign boundary at \(nt=r^2\): negative below, positive at and above, regardless of the number of selected edges in the nondegenerate regime. The boundary is sharp for every \(r\ge3\) and \(1\le t\le r-2\), with no zero-covariance order.
