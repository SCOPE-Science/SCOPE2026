# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For the independently and uniformly attached recursive-tree model, the updates of the depth generating polynomial and the leaf generating polynomial are exact conditional identities. They yield a closed three-moment recursion, an explicit product for the entire covariance polynomial, and a Poisson-binomial representation. Strict log-concavity of the Poisson-binomial mass function proves one positive-to-negative sign transition at all sizes. The standalone program verifies 4,687 exact equalities, including all \((n-1)!\) recursive trees through size ten and independent symbolic recurrences through size 65. The universal result is based on the analytic argument; finite tests are only corroboration.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk

Janson's depth-congruence work (arXiv:math/0509471), the exact level–level covariance work by van der Hofstad, Hooghiemstra and Van Mieghem, and Drmota–Hwang's profile correlation study were compared by mathematical observables and statements. Existing outdegree-count work and selected-vertex joint laws were checked for broader coverage. They do not assert the complete leaf-number/depth-profile covariance polynomial or its sign switch. Two projections, root degree against leaves and total path length against leaves, have been established independently, so **neither projection is claimed novel**. The inaccessible complete text of some older degree-profile work and the possibility of a hidden bivariate generating-function specialization remain meaningful historical-priority risks. Search failure alone is not a novelty proof.

## Value — PASS

Leaf number and depth histogram are canonical global descriptors of recursive tree shape. Their mixed covariance has genuine depth-dependent structure: a single positive-to-negative transition despite a positive root-degree covariance and negative total path-length covariance. The exact finite-sample classification, not a routine substitution into those two known moments, is the substantive contribution.

## Limits

Only uniform recursive trees are covered; the result concerns mixed second moments and not joint limiting laws. The polynomial's exact coefficient identity and sign theorem have been checked directly. No independent mathematical audit has occurred.
