For \(q=2t+1\) with \(t\equiv4,5\pmod7\) and \(e=(q^3-1)/14\), the shortest nonempty zero sum of powers of \(q\) modulo \(e\) has exactly \(2t=2\gcd(e,q-1)\) terms. Its reduced exponent-count vector has exactly three cyclically related possibilities, furnishing an infinite sharpness family for the cubic-range bound proposed by Jacob and Külshammer.
