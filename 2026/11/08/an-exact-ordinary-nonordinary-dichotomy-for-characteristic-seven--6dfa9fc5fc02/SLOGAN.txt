For the smooth characteristic-seven Picard family \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+bx+1\), the Jacobian is ordinary exactly when \(a\ne0\); otherwise its \(a\)-number is always \(2\), with \(7\)-rank dropping from \(1\) to \(0\) exactly at \(b^4=4\). No smooth curve in this family has \(7\)-rank \(2\) or \(a\)-number \(1\).
