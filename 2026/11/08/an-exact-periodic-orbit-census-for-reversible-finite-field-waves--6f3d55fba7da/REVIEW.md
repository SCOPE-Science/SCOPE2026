**Correctness — PASS.** The proof fixes the full parameter range and shows rank identities over the algebraic closure; the local resonance blocks and their sizes are derived explicitly, and the counting formula and least global period follow from all local factors. Two standard-library programs reproduced all asserted finite checks: 1,235 exact matrix-rank cases and 103,788 directly enumerated state configurations. Finite checks corroborate but are not the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Targeted full-statement comparisons with accessible historical claims and published-finding corpus searches did not locate the exact finite-cycle, all-field, all-iterate formula. Steeb–Hardy's inspected article covers the reversible construction and a different wave rule; Macfarlane's 2004 abstract concerns weights of seed-generated patterns for three binary rules, rather than fixed populations of every iterate from all initial pairs. The complete Macfarlane article was inaccessible, and a hidden equivalent finite-ring result or differently formulated wave identity remains a substantial residual risk. The conclusion is best-knowledge originality, not exhaustive certification.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** An exact dynamical zeta input (all fixed-point counts) and complete primitive-period census across every ring length and every finite field are natural invariants of this standard reversible wave. The characteristic-two even-ring exception sharply separates two distinct recurrent clocks and follows from a structural Jordan-block change, rather than a bare finite-table observation.
