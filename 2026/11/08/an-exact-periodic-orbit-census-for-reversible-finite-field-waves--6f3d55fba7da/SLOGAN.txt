For the reversible finite-field wave \(T(u,v)=((S+S^{-1})u-v,u)\) on every simple cycle, every iterate has exactly \(q^{2\gcd(n,k)-\delta_p(n,k)}\) fixed configurations, with an explicit arithmetic resonance defect. Its exact global period is \(pn\) for odd characteristic, \(2n\) for odd cycles in characteristic two, and \(n\) for even cycles in characteristic two; Möbius inversion gives the entire primitive-orbit census.
