Every pair of conservative two-state Markov generators admits an exact, positivity-preserving three-exponential split with total time \(h\) per generator and a unique, explicitly computable positive allocation \(0<\vartheta(h)<1/2\) when the generators do not commute. The allocation decreases strictly with step size; no fixed Strang allocation achieves this exactness, and its noncommuting cubic error is explicit.
