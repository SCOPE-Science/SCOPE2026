# Scientific review: exact clearing criterion for triangular friendship graphs

## Correctness — PASS
The five defect cases are disjoint and exhaustive, including the exceptional pair of single pebbles. Their three move inequalities establish a nonincreasing integer potential. Any successful clearing from more than one pebble must pass through a two-pebble source with potential at least one, giving necessity. Every configuration of potential at least one can be reduced to the potential's exact number of pebbles at the common vertex, using explicit blade-local extraction sequences and a provable reserve condition. The central three-pebble exception is genuine; for any other configuration of potential three an explicit sequence first reduces potential to one or two. This gives sufficiency. The defect sum yields the threshold and a single automorphism orbit of critical obstructions. Independent labelled-graph enumeration confirms the criterion through size twelve on three triangles. The mathematical proof does not extrapolate from those tests.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualification
Csernák and Soukup, arXiv:2604.22341v1 (first public 2026-04-24), define the clearing number and prove general existence/bounds and special-family values. Their complete-multipartite theorem does not include friendship graphs with two or more blades. The result for the single triangle is known and excluded from novelty. Their general framework does not supply the additive, complete configuration-by-configuration criterion, exact \(4m\) threshold, or critical labelled census. The directed follow-up, arXiv:2606.04659v1, addresses different arc-directed moves. Previously studied cover and edge pebbling numbers on friendship graphs have different success predicates, so their numerical formulas cannot establish these assertions by a statement-level implication. Public research-record searches found related zero-forcing and resolver results for the same graph family, but not clearing; absence of a search hit is not conclusive proof of priority. Some older cover-pebbling sources were available only at abstract level, leaving residual historical risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This result gives a genuinely new exact dynamic reachability criterion for *every* nonnegative integer configuration on an infinite family of connected non-bipartite graphs. The potential is a linear-time mathematical decision procedure, and the sharp threshold replaces a much larger general diameter-based bound. The classification of the unique critical obstruction orbit and its \(2m\) labelled representatives explains the sharpness, rather than merely giving an extremal number. The calculation is not a renaming of the known ordinary, cover, or edge pebbling number.

## Scope and limitations
The theorem is specific to triangle bouquets sharing a common vertex, assumes ordinary undirected pebbling moves of weight two-to-one, and distinguishes size-one initial configurations from larger ones. The exact potential does not automatically extend to general cactus or generalized windmill graphs. Prior-art priority is provisional. Finite computations verify only the explicitly tested ranges. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
