For every friendship graph \(F_m=K_1\vee(mK_2)\), a five-case, additive integer potential decides whether any pebble configuration can be cleared to a single pebble, apart from a uniquely specified central three-pebble exception. Consequently \(\operatorname{clear}(F_m)=4m\), and for \(m\ge2\) the only nonclearable configurations with \(4m-1\) pebbles are the \(2m\) labelled versions of one explicit outer-vertex concentration pattern.
