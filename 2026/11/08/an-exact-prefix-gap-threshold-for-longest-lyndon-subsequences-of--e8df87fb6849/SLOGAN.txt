For binary marker words with positive nondecreasing gaps, the minimum deletions needed to obtain a Lyndon scattered subsequence are exactly the first prefix-gap index \(t\) whose total zero length exceeds the largest gap (capped at the final index). Thus the longest Lyndon subsequence of \(1\,0^{g_1}1\cdots0^{g_k}1\) has length \(|W|-t\), with an explicit optimum formed by deleting the first \(t\) markers.
