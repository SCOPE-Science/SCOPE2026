For a four-site, two-particle exclusion ring with adjacent trap contrasts \((a,b,1,1)\), the quadratic bias-rectification coefficient has the exact factor \((a-1)(a-b)(b-1)(ab-3)\). Reflection-symmetric rates have identically zero even current, but the nonsymmetric cancellation curve \(ab=3\) has a universally nonzero quartic leading even response, with an explicit coefficient and sign classification; the existence of some nonsymmetric quadratic-cancellation witnesses was already known.
