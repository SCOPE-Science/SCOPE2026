For independent square-grid jittered quadrature of an oblique half-plane indicator with a fixed primitive integer normal, the exact variance reduces to a finite lattice-count sum, and its scaled leading coefficient is a universal quartic function of the grid phase \(\{nc\}\). Irrational intercepts produce an entire sharp interval of variance constants rather than a single asymptotic constant, with the diagonal case ranging from \(7c/32\) to \(c/4\).
