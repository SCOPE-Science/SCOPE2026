Uniformly random weighted-permutation evolution algebras over \(\mathbb F_q\) have an exact multiplicative cycle-count law controlled by \(\gcd(2^\ell-1,q-1)\), with expected number of nonzero idempotents equal to the dimension for every field size. Over \(\mathbb F_4\), the probability of having no nonzero idempotent is zero in odd dimension and exactly \((1/3)_m/m!\) in dimension \(2m\), while the variance grows as \(n^4/48\).
