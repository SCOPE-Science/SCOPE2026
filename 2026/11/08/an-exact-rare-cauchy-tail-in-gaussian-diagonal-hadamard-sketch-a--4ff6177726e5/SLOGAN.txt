For a one-column least-squares problem, replacing the standard Hadamard sketch's sign diagonal by a shared Gaussian diagonal creates an exact standard-Cauchy error component of probability \(2^{1-m}\) for every finite row budget \(m\). The resulting estimator is almost surely unique but has infinite absolute first moment and infinite mean-square error; its tail above \(m/(2\sqrt{m-1})\) is exactly \(2^{2-m}\arctan(1/t)/\pi\) for \(m\ge2\).
