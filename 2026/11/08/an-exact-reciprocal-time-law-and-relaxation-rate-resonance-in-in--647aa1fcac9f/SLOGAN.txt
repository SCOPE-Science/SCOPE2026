In an inhibitory SIS–activity system at its invasion threshold, the modified reciprocal prevalence \((1+s y)/x\) grows **exactly linearly in time** if and only if activity and recovery rates match, and this is the unique affine-in-activity reciprocal observable with constant derivative. For arbitrary activity relaxation speed, prevalence still decays as \(1/[\zeta(1+s k)t]\), but its reciprocal acquires the sharp logarithmic term \(sk(\zeta/\kappa-1)\log t\), whose sign reverses at the exact linearization resonance.
