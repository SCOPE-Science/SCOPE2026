For every \(n\ge3\), the root-to-maximum-key path in a random binary search tree has positive covariance with leaf and two-child counts, but negative covariance with one-child count: \(\operatorname{Cov}(R_n,N_{n,d})=c_n(1,-2,1)_d\), where \(c_n=(n-2)(n+1)/(12n(n-1))\). The profile approaches \((1/12,-1/6,1/12)\), while the covariances vanish for \(n=1,2\).
