Deleting an alternating subsequence \((01)^k\) from \((01)^{k+r}\) produces exactly the paired words in \(\{01,10\}^r\) with at most \(k\) runs of \(10\) blocks. Their exact number is \(\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{r+1}{2j}\) (with out-of-range binomial coefficients zero), giving the sharp full-spectrum cutoff \(r\leq2k\).
