In a fixed-size uniform vertex sample from \(r\ge2\) disjoint cliques of common size \(m\ge3\), induced-edge count and isolated-vertex count have negative covariance at sample sizes \(2\le k<2r\), positive covariance at \(2r\le k\le(r-1)m+1\), and zero covariance outside these ranges. Independent vertex retention in the same graph always gives strictly negative covariance, so fixing sample size can reverse the dependence sign.
