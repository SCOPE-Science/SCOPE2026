An independent-column sparse sign sketch can report a uniformly well-conditioned two-column truncated-Arnoldi basis even when the true basis condition number diverges and the positive-definite generating matrix tends to the identity. For every admissible sparsity and sketch size, the exact false-safe probability is \((M-2)/M^2\), where \(M=2^\zeta\binom{s}{\zeta}\); it is a rare collision event, not a typical-case failure.
