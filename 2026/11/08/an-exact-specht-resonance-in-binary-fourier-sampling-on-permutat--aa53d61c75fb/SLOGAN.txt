Binary Fourier self-sampling on the permutation matrices has a fixed, explicitly identified Specht-sector kernel in every dimension \(n\ge7\): its nullity is at least \([n(n-1)(n-3)(n-6)/8]^2\). An exact factorization of the corresponding fixed-point convolution multiplier explains the characteristic-two resonance and gives the precise rank \(4599\) at \(n=7\).
