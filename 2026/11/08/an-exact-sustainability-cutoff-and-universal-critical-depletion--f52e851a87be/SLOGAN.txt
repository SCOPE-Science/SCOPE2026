For the logistic-response common-pool-resource game, sustainability begins at the exact sensitivity \(k_c=c^{-1}\log[(d-1)/(1-b)]\); every initially positive resource is depleted at or below this boundary and is globally sustained above it. At criticality, resource abundance follows the universal law \(tR(t)\to[1+(d-b)k_cq(1-q)]^{-1}\), and a strict integral Lyapunov function proves the sustainable side globally.
