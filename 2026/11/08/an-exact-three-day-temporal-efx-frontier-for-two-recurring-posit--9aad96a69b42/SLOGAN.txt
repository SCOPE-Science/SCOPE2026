For two identical agents splitting a recurring pair of positive goods valued \(H\ge L>0\), exact cumulative EFX is possible through all days exactly when \(H\le2L\). At the first obstructed horizon of three days, the optimal approximate EFX factor is \(\max\{(H+2L)/(2H),H/(H+L)\}\) for \(H>2L\), with a sharp uniform minimum \((\sqrt{17}-1)/4\).
