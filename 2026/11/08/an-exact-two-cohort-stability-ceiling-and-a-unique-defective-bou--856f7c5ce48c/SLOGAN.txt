In the two-cohort recurrence \(x_{t+1}=gF(x_t)+(1-g)F(x_{t-1})\), no mixture can make the equilibrium a hyperbolic sink when the recruitment slope is at most \(-3\), while every slope strictly between \(-3\) and \(1\) admits a stabilizing mixture. The stability window collapses uniquely at \(g=2/3\), where the linearized system has a defective double multiplier \(-1\) and exhibits linear transient amplification.
