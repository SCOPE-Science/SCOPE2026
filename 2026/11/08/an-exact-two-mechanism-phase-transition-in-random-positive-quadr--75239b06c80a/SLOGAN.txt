For independent uniform nodes on \([-1,1]\), positive exact quadrature for the first three moments undergoes a sharp reliability phase transition at target variance \(c_*=3-2\sqrt2\): failure is governed by an overlarge central gap below the threshold and insufficient extreme-node span above it. The full finite-sample feasibility probability is given by two one-dimensional integrals, with \(P_3(c)=\frac{3c}{2}(\log(1/c)-1+c)\) and sharp exponential prefactors on both sides of the transition.
