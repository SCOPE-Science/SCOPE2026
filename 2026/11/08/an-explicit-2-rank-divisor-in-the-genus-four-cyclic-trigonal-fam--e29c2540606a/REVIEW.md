# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The genus-four differential basis follows from tame Kummer valuations at all six branch points. In the displayed basis, the exact Cartier transformations are \(U\mapsto V\), \(W\mapsto U\), \(H,J\mapsto0\), and \(V\mapsto a^{1/4}U\); semilinearity then proves every iterate rank over any algebraically closed characteristic-two field. The nonempty squarefree locus and affine-coordinate transformation are checked algebraically. Exact finite-field matrix and zeta tests independently corroborate the identities; they are not substituted for the infinite-field argument.

**Originality (PASS with historical risk).** Elkin already establishes the ramification-type determination of the \(a\)-number, and Özman–Pries–Weir already establish dimension and irreducibility of the abstract rank-zero moduli stratum. Those facts are explicitly treated as background. The checked primary results do not give the particular branch-coefficient hypersurface \(a=0\), the two complete Cartier-iterate sequences, or their invariant transformation law. The direct Hasse–Witt formulas are a known method and the refinement is modest. Specialized historical tables or an uninspected source might still cover it; no absolute priority certification is claimed.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** Exact equations for Newton/p-rank jumping strata of classical cyclic covers make abstract special-Shimura strata effective in branch coordinates. This calculation gives a natural complete classification on the entire squarefree quintic family, rather than a hand-picked finite example: the single second elementary symmetric function of the five finite branch points precisely detects the rank-zero locus despite the constant \(a\)-number. It also distinguishes the two possible nilpotence profiles. It neither claims a new general Cartier formalism nor asserts full EO or Newton classifications.

**Limitations and closest literature.** The precise moduli existence and irreducibility statements are already in Özman–Pries–Weir (2022); constant \(a\)-number is in Elkin (2007/2010). Baldwin et al. (2006) study the same curve model over characteristic zero. Xarles (2020) treats finite \(\mathbb F_2\) census. Bouw (2001) was available only at abstract level; historical priority remains provisional. No independent validation has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
