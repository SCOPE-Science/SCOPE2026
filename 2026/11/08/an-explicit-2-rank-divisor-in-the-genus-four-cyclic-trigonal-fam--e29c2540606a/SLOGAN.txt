For smooth genus-four characteristic-two cyclic trigonal curves \(y^3=x^5+d x^4+a x^3+e x^2+b x+c\), the entire Cartier-iterate rank profile is \((2,2,2,\ldots)\) for \(a\ne0\) and \((2,1,0,\ldots)\) for \(a=0\). Thus an explicit affine-invariant coefficient hyperplane cuts out precisely the Jacobian \(2\)-rank-zero locus; the previously known constant \(a\)-number is \(2\).
