# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The radial Toeplitz moment computation is a gamma-integral identity, not an asymptotic heuristic: \(G_{m,t}\) has the precise binomial diagonal, with geometric trace sum. The resulting parity-projection \(S_1\) error is bounded by \(2N^{-2}\). The endpoint peak is a direct Gaussian integral. Gaussian envelopes give the stated weighted summability and separation estimates for the fixed centers. Trace-ideal convergence and the coherent-state domain argument identify the assembled symbol's Toeplitz extension. The rational checker independently evaluates the finite algebra; infinite quantifiers are supported by analytic bounds.

## Originality

PASS at best-knowledge level for the **explicit all-\(p>1\) endpoint assembly**, not for the already-known abstract existence result or the earlier Gaussian–Laguerre block identities. Virtanen (arXiv:2609.11853v3) states the all-\(p>1\) existence result through Baire category and calls its examples nonconstructive. Looi (arXiv:2601.20859v1) constructs a bounded-operator counterexample by a separate Weyl/Schwartz mechanism, with no simultaneous all-\(p>1\) Schatten conclusion. Looi (arXiv:2601.10711v2) constructs explicit smooth heat-threshold symbols without the present ideal classification. Searches of published mathematical findings, source aliases, Laguerre projector terminology, and operator-ideal implications found no covering explicit construction. Esmeral García–Maximenko (arXiv:2503.23276v1) already gives the individual exact Gaussian–Laguerre symbols and binomial diagonal (Definition 3, Lemma 4.2), so these are credited, not claimed novel. Its uniform approximation problem does not entail the present trace-norm signed parity assembly and endpoint heat divergence. Earlier complete endpoint constructions remain a publication-priority risk.

## Value

PASS. The sharp endpoint gap was known to exist but lacked a named, reproducible all-\(p>1\) symbol. Here all coefficients, widths, centers, and trace-norm parity-block approximation errors are explicit, using known Laguerre eigenvalue blocks. This provides a concrete test case for borderline Berezin/Toeplitz estimates and a direct constructive realization of the trace versus supertrace Schatten separation. The finite-rank approximation is quantitatively sharp enough to make simultaneous membership transparent, unlike mere operator-norm blocks.

## Limitations

No independent certification of novelty or proof has been performed. The symbol is not asserted bounded, the example is one-dimensional, and no quantitative minimization of the extremely large centers is claimed. The existential portion is covered by Virtanen; novelty rests only on the explicit and quantitatively specified realization. The finite checks do not independently prove convergence.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
