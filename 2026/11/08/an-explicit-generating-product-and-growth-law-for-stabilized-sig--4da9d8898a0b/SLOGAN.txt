The stabilized correction in the signed Fibonacci Tribonacci recurrence is exactly a shifted bounded-digit Fibonacci partition function, with generating series \(z\prod_{j\ge1}(1+z^{F_j}+z^{2F_j})\) and an explicit effective stabilization threshold. Its cumulative count grows on the sharp scale \(X^{\log 3/\log\varphi}\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\).
