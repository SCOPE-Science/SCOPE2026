The exact universal closed-point threshold for smooth genus-four curves over \(\mathbb F_4\) is \(\rho(4,4)=4\). The explicit Artin–Schreier curve \(y^2+y=x^9+\omega\) has only its infinity point over \(\mathbb F_{64}\), so it has no degree-three closed points; a uniform Weil-bound argument guarantees all degrees at least four for every genus-four curve over \(\mathbb F_4\).
