The two non-lattice-equivalent six-point polygons \(P_6^{(4)}\) and \(P_6^{(5)}\) from Luo–Yau–Zhang–Zuo define monomially equivalent \([49,6]\) toric surface codes over \(\mathbb F_8\): the involution \(f(x,y)\mapsto x^3f(x^{-1},x^{-3}y)\) reverses the four polynomial coefficients and uses \(x^7=1\) to match the remaining Laurent monomial. This proves their explicitly stated Conjecture 1.1 and completes the one unresolved equivalence in their dimension-six classification.
