The diagonal-quotient Jacobians of the Artin–Mumford curve form an explicit isogeny whose norm–pullback compositions both equal multiplication by \(p\); the correspondence has degree \(p^{(p-1)^2}\) and pulls the canonical principal polarization back to \(p\) times the product polarization. Over \(\mathbb F_p\), the quotients collapse into two nonisogenous square-class representatives, yielding a two-factor Frobenius-polynomial decomposition.
