The ideal regularized three-block Stokes–Darcy preconditioner admits explicit residual polynomials \(p_m(t)=(1-t^2)(1-t)^{m-2}\), with a nonnormal GMRES bound \(\|r_m\|/\|r_0\|\le M_\alpha[\alpha/(\mu+\alpha)]^{m-1}\) that also controls restarted cycles. A three-dimensional exact example proves that its \(O(\alpha)\) two-step decay order is unimprovable in general.
