On the compact product of cyclic groups of order four, an explicit antipodal-free Fourier spectrum is strongly small-2 but not Riesz. A reversible sixteen-point flat sign yields a singular full-spectrum measure whose convolution square has the exact finite squared \(L^2\)-norm \(\prod_{j\ge1}(1+a^4/16^j)\) for every \(0<a\le1/2\).
