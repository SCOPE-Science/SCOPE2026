For the sparse Hardy Hankel symbol \(\psi(z)=z+t z^m\), the normalized-kernel Garsia test remains maximized at the origin until an explicit amplitude \(T_m\sim2^{(m-1)/2}\) for degrees \(m\ge4\), when an entire nonzero-radius orbit ties the origin and then takes over. The exact phase diagram exposes a discontinuous localization transition and a quadratic-versus-quartic small-perturbation gap between the Hankel norm and kernel testing.
