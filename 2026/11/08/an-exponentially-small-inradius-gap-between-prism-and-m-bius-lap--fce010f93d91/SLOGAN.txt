For every \(m\ge3\), the canonical Euclidean Laplacian simplices of the cubic prism and Möbius ladder on \(2m\) vertices share the same circumradius, yet the Möbius ladder has strictly larger inradius. Their exact relative inradius gap decays as \(120(2-\sqrt3)^m/m^2\), revealing exponentially small sensitivity to the periodic versus twisted boundary condition.
