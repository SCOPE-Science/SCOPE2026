The four-spine caterpillars \(C_{(h+2,h+1,h+3,3)}\) and \(C_{(h+3,h+1,h+2,3)}\) are nonisomorphic for every \(h\geq0\), yet their complete three-color \(t\)-chromatic principal specializations coincide. Their fourth specializations differ already in the coefficient of \(q^8\) after setting \(t=1\), by \(14\), \(4\), or \(2\) as \(h=0\), \(h=1\), or \(h\geq2\), respectively.
