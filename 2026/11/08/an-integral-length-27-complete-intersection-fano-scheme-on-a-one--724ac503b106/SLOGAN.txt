For the integer cubic \(xy^2+yt^2+z^3=0\), the whole scheme of lines is a finite-flat rank-\(27\) complete intersection given by two explicit weighted-homogeneous equations in two variables, even in characteristics \(2\) and \(3\). All \(27\) infinitesimal lines coalesce at one geometric line, and in characteristic \(3\) its local algebra is exactly \(\mathbb F_3[c,d]/(c^3,d^9)\); the classical length-\(27\) count is prior art, not a claimed discovery.
