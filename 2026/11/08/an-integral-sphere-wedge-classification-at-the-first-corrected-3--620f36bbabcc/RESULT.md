# An integral sphere-wedge classification at the first corrected \(3\times9\) grid width

## Finding
Let \(G=P_3\square P_9\) be the Cartesian product of paths with 3 and 9 vertices. Its ordinary matching complex \(\mathcal M(G)\) has one simplicial vertex for each graph edge, and its faces are the pairwise vertex-disjoint sets of those edges. The complete **integral homotopy type** is
\[
\mathcal M(P_3\square P_9)\simeq\bigvee^{163}S^8.
\]
Consequently \(\widetilde H_8(\mathcal M(G);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{163}\) and every other reduced integral homology group is zero. This is not a general formula for \(3\times n\) grid graphs.

## Assumptions and scope
The grid has vertices \((r,c)\), where \(r\in\{0,1,2\}\) and \(c\in\{0,\ldots,8\}\). Horizontal edges connect \((r,c)\) to \((r,c+1)\), and vertical edges connect \((r,c)\) to \((r+1,c)\), whenever both endpoints exist. There are 27 vertices and 42 edges. A face is a nonempty matching of this graph; the empty face is not included in the non-augmented face poset used for the acyclic matching. All chain coefficients are integers, and each simplex is oriented by increasing graph-edge index in the verifier's explicit edge order.

## Proof
**Exhaustive face poset and acyclic reduction.** The complete set of 7,594,360 nonempty faces is generated by an include/exclude search on the graph edges, rejecting any selected edge whose endpoint has already been used. In increasing graph-edge index order, the matching procedure pairs any as-yet-unmatched face not containing edge \(e\) with its face union \(\{e\}\), provided both are still unmatched; it removes the two faces before the next edge index. Every pair is a codimension-one face/coface. A separate full directed-Hasse-graph check reverses each paired cover relation and leaves each unpaired cover relation directed from coface to face. All 61,837,314 cover relations are traversed and Kahn's topological sort visits all 7,594,360 faces, establishing global acyclicity (not merely a local test). The matching has 3,797,049 pairs and exactly 262 critical faces: one zero-cell, 212 eight-cells, and 49 nine-cells, with no others. Discrete Morse theory therefore gives a CW model with precisely those cells.

**Integral attaching matrix.** The signed cellular differential from critical nine-cells to critical eight-cells is computed by the acyclic matching recursion: a critical eight-face maps to its corresponding basis vector; an eight-face paired downward to a seven-face maps to zero; an eight-face \(f\) paired upward to a nine-face \(h\) maps to the negative of the signed projections of all other eight-faces of \(h\), divided by the incidence sign \(\langle\partial h,f\rangle\in\{\pm1\}\). Recursion terminates by global acyclicity. Taking oriented boundaries of the 49 critical nine-faces gives an exact \(212\times49\) integer matrix with 615 nonzero entries and maximum absolute entry 3. The included code performs 49 consecutive pivot steps with pivot \(\pm1\) in the remaining submatrix; every pivot uses integral elementary row/column operations and produces an integral identity block of size 49, leaving a zero block with 163 rows. Integer entries in this elimination grow to absolute value at most 4. Hence the matrix has 49 unit invariant factors and no other nonzero Smith invariant.

**From degrees to homotopy.** The Morse CW complex has one vertex and cells only in dimensions 8 and 9. Its eight-skeleton is \(\bigvee^{212}S^8\). Every nine-cell is attached by a map \(S^8\to\bigvee^{212}S^8\), whose homotopy class is uniquely determined by its integral degree vector because the Hurewicz map \(\pi_8(\bigvee^{212}S^8)\to H_8(\bigvee^{212}S^8;\mathbb Z)\) is an isomorphism. Integral elementary basis changes are realized by homotopy self-equivalences of this sphere wedge and changes of the nine-cell generators. The 49 unit degrees cancel 49 eight-/nine-cell pairs homotopically; the resulting CW type has one vertex and 163 eight-cells. Thus it is the claimed wedge of 163 eight-spheres. The argument does not infer an infinite theorem from finite enumeration.

## Verification
The supplied standalone C++17 program `verify.cpp` constructs the graph and every matching from first principles, checks every matched incidence, proves global acyclicity using the full Hasse graph, computes the exact integer Morse differential by the indicated recursive formula, and performs an all-unit integer elimination. It also checks the independent Euler-characteristic prediction \(\chi=164\). To reproduce: compile with `g++ -O3 -std=c++17 verify.cpp -o verify`; run `./verify 9`. Its recorded output is in `verify_output.txt` and ends in `ALL_VERIFY_OK`. The included comparison run `./verify 8` reconstructs the known adjacent width, without claiming any new result for that width. Full width-9 verification requires approximately 800 MB peak resident memory on the recorded run.

The width-9 face vector, in dimensions 0 through 12, is \((42,767,8022,53292,235800,708881,1450678,1993990,1786876,991849,313290,48319,2554)\). The alternating sum is 164, agreeing with one plus the number of eight-spheres. These checks are mathematical computations, not independent certification.

## Relationship to prior work
Goyal, Shukla and Singh (2021) stated a general wedge formula based on recurrences, but the authors' 2025 corrigendum withdraws the relevant argument because non-induced subgraphs invalidate the proposed splitting. The corrigendum explicitly points out that the nine-column example has homology only in dimension eight, contrary to the old two-degree prediction. Its published table determines smaller widths through eight, not this corrected nine-column integral homotopy type. The 2026 study of Chauhan, Sharma and Shukla establishes \((n-2)\)-connectivity and nonzero homology in dimension \(n-1\); those bounds do not by themselves imply this exact 163-fold integral homotopy equivalence. Public metadata and abstract were available for this recent paper, but its full text could not be accessed; overlapping unindexed results remain a risk.

## Limitations
This is one exact finite classification for the specified grid, not a uniform recurrence, a general theorem for all widths, an equivariant decomposition, or a proof of shellability. The acyclic matching is computationally exhaustive; it is not a short structural formula. The comparison with the inaccessible full text of the 2026 manuscript is necessarily qualified. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
