# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The graph and ordinary cut-complex conventions are fixed. The dual face characterization follows from the elementary dichotomy for intersecting edge families, with any disjoint edge-pair forcing four-vertex support. The support map used in the matching is monotone and matching arrows never change support. The nonexceptional fibers split into elementary cone matchings, and the unique thirty-seven-face relative four-vertex pattern has eighteen acyclic pairs and one critical three-cell; finite directed-Hasse checks independently verify this local acyclicity. Its incidence with positive-star faces only has unmatched arrows from nonstar to star. The global matching is therefore acyclic by the order-preserving patchwork lemma. Morse theory gives one critical zero-cell and \(\binom{n-1}{4}\) critical three-cells, whence the dual is a wedge of three-spheres. The Alexander duality shift \(i\mapsto N-i-3\), integral free rank, full lower skeleton and simple-connectivity argument yield the claimed homotopy type by Hurewicz and Whitehead. The nonshellability conclusion follows from pure dimension and lower-dimensional nonzero homology. The verifier exhausts dual membership for the first two cases and acyclicity for five cases, not for infinitely many cases.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

The foundational primary articles arXiv:2304.13675 and arXiv:2406.01979 were inspected for definitions, family theorems and the full-paper presence of triangular or Johnson graphs. The principal indexed Johnson/triangular result examined treats the fourth cut parameter on \(J(5,2)\), not the third parameter for all \(n\ge5\). Another treats ordinary clique or scale-one Vietoris–Rips complexes, which do not encode connected induced triples as the dual does. Broader three-cut wedge theorems require absence of triangles or four-cycles, whereas \(J(n,2)\) has both. Exact-phrase and alias searches of indexed records found no stronger covering statement. The conclusion is a documented best-knowledge originality assessment, not a guarantee that all literature is indexed; an unindexed equivalent theorem remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS

Triangular graphs are canonical highly symmetric line graphs of complete graphs. This uniform integral classification covers infinitely many new pure nonshellable cut complexes with explicitly computable, rapidly growing sphere ranks. The method resolves the natural four-vertex incidence obstruction to applying existing triangle-free/four-cycle-free cut-complex theorems; it is neither a parameter renaming nor a numerical table of small examples. There is a clear structural reason for the rank \(\binom{n-1}{4}\): precisely one dual critical three-cell for each four-label support away from a distinguished original vertex.

## Scientific limitations and residual risk

The scope is \(J(n,2)\) and ordinary three-cut complexes, not all Johnson graphs or higher cut levels. The verifier covers \(5\le n\le9\) while the formal proof covers every \(n\ge5\). No independent audit or formal proof assistant is claimed. Equivalent obscure sources may exist despite the inspected full papers and indexed searches.
