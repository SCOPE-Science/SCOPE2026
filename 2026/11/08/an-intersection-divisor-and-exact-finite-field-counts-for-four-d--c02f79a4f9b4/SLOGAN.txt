The four-dimensional representation variety of the exterior algebra on two generators has a regular-module component and a mutually annihilating component, each twelve-dimensional; they meet in an eleven-dimensional locus governed generically by a \(2\times2\) discriminant. An all-characteristic exact point-count formula is \(q^6(2q^6+q^5+q^4-q^2-q-1)\), with precisely \(|\mathrm{GL}_4(\mathbb F_q)|/(q^3(q-1))\) nonzero-product pairs.
