For the unit Laplacian of a balanced complete-multipartite line graph with at least three parts of size at least two, every minimum coordinate-actuator placement is exactly the complement of a spanning graph whose components are odd-unicyclic, except for four forbidden two-triangle complements in the six-vertex base graph. The minimum number is \(\frac{rk((r-1)k-2)}2\), and the exceptional line graph has exactly 496 minimum placements.
