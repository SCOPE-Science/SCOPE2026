Two disjoint opposite tails of a synchronously reset Brownian gas switch from negative to positive count covariance as the population grows: the exact finite-population threshold has a logarithmic small-distance singularity. At tail distance \(a_N=\sqrt{D/r}\sqrt{c/(N\log N)}\), the covariance divided by \(N\) tends to \((c-\pi)/(4\pi)\), giving a sharp mesoscopic sign threshold at \(c=\pi\).
