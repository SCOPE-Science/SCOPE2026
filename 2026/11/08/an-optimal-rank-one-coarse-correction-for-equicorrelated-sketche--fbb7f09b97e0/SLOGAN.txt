For an equicorrelated Nyström kernel with replicated training centers, full-block-sketch block Jacobi leaves the original condition number unchanged, but one positive rank-one global correction achieves the sharp optimum \(d_c/d_a\) among **all** positive semidefinite rank-one denominator updates. At fixed block width this optimum remains bounded as the number of blocks grows, and its complete scalar tuning interval is explicit.
