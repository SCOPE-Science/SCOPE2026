In a uniform random mapping, the number of fixed points and the size of the cyclic core have covariance exactly zero, yet they are not independent. Their mutual information is asymptotic to \(\mathcal J/n\) nats, where \(\mathcal J=\sum_{m\ge1}mD_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_m\|\operatorname{Pois}(1))\approx3.69292623898\), and \(Q_m\) is the rencontres law for a uniform permutation of size \(m\).
