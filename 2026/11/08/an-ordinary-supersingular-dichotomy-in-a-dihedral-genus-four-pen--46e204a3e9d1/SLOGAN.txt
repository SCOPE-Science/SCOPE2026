For odd primes \(p\equiv2,3\pmod5\), the genus-four dihedral pencil \(y^2=x^{10}+ax^5+1\) has no intermediate Newton polygons: every smooth Jacobian is either ordinary or supersingular. In characteristic seven precisely the three smooth coefficient parameters \(a=0\) or \(a^2=-1\) are supersingular, all with Cartier \(a\)-number two.
