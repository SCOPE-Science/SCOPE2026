At any positive rotational diffusivity, an active Brownian particle reset to a fixed heading in a harmonic trap has identical leading and first subleading stationary extreme-displacement tails in every direction, even though its bulk distribution remembers that heading. At exactly zero rotational diffusion the first subleading coefficient retains a cosine angular factor, and a zero-kurtosis crossover never makes the positive-diffusivity stationary law exactly Gaussian.
