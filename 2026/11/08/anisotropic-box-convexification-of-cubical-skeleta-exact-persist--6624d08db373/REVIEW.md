# Scientific review: cubical skeleton offsets

## Correctness — PASS
The pointwise membership criterion follows by selecting at least \(d-r\) coordinate faces, each within the prescribed axis-parallel dilation box. It proves the necessary and sufficient convexification threshold, including ties. Coordinatewise clamping is a strong deformation retraction onto a smaller cubical skeleton, so the cell structure yields the full wedge-of-spheres homotopy type. A coordinate-face section proves **surjectivity of all persistence maps**, rather than inferring it from Betti ranks. An exact gap/endpoint partition gives the missing-volume formula; lowest-degree terms at the last threshold give a strictly positive vanishing coefficient and exponent. The standard-library checker reproduced 50,114 exact assertions, including cubical boundary calculations through six dimensions. Finite checks alone do not prove the all-dimensional statement.

## Originality — PASS, with a documented priority risk
Meyer, *Finite convexification by Minkowski summation* (arXiv:2610.26744v1, October 19, 2026), supplies the broad finite-convexification framework, but the inspected main theorems and special-case analyses do not give an explicit cubical-skeleton box-offset filtration, persistence barcode, or volume-loss order at tied side lengths. Rowlands, *Topology of complements of skeletons* (arXiv:2209.08434), studies topological complements and links without the anisotropic Minkowski scale filtration; only its abstract was inspected here. Hu, *MMPersistence* (arXiv:2602.15502), supplies software infrastructure for general cubical persistence, not the particular exact formulas; only its abstract was inspected. Existing quantitative results for product-of-simplices skeletons under homothetic self-dilation already cover the **isotropic** convexification threshold as a special case, and an orthotope-skeleton result covers repeated equal-weight Minkowski averaging. Neither determines anisotropic width-dependent persistence maps, the offset void-volume polynomial, or the positive tie-sensitive vanishing coefficient. Published mathematical-record matches for simplex-skeleton self-Minkowski sums and cubical graph Rips/Čech complexes likewise do not imply those new outputs. A classical but unindexed equivalent statement remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The entire **anisotropic** offset filtration of a natural family of polytopal skeleta admits a nontrivial explicit homotopy classification, exact persistence maps and barcode, and a finite polynomial formula for every intermediate missing volume. Tied coordinate widths cause rigorously classified higher-order extinction, including the isotropic exponent \(r+1\). These joint geometric and topological conclusions extend beyond the known bare topology of a cubical skeleton and beyond existence-only convexification criteria. No general claim for arbitrary bodies is made.

## Limitations
Axis alignment is essential. The homotopy equivalence does not assert a literal cubical cell decomposition of the offset. Historical originality remains provisional; independent verification has not been performed.
