For a Poisson resetting protocol that uniformly samples its entire history, the unwrapped effective time has a full **annealed** logarithmic-speed large-deviation law with sharp rate \(I(y)=(\sqrt y-1)^2\) for \(y\ge0\). The clock also has Gaussian fluctuations of standard deviation asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2\log(rt)}/r\), refining its known mean effective time \(\log(rt)/r\).
