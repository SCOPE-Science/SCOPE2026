PASS. The source supplies the exact blow-up resolution and contraction dictionary. The calculation \(K_S=-3H+\sum E\), the quotient discrepancy of the contracted strict transform of infinity, and the classes of successive exceptional strict transforms yield the two canonical self-intersections. The infinitely-near line Bézout inequality proves nonnegativity on *all* other curves; separate exceptional and infinity intersections establish the sharp nef/ample conditions. For singular pencils with maximal-length sum at least three, the exact orthogonal decomposition supplies the Zariski positive and negative parts. The regular maximal-block core pulls back anticanonical class to the same nef class, proving semiampleness and the asserted canonical contraction. The standalone exact checker tests finite instances, not the general curve argument.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Anticanonical positivity governs the birational classification and contraction geometry of these natural singular rational surfaces. The theorem detects a genuine boundary at total maximal block sum three, distinguishes weak from strict anticanonical positivity for regular pencils with mixed Jordan sizes, and identifies a common ample core even when the singular-pencil anticanonical square becomes negative. The structural formula is unbounded in matrix size and removes dependence on all nonmaximal Jordan block sizes at the anticanonical-model level.
