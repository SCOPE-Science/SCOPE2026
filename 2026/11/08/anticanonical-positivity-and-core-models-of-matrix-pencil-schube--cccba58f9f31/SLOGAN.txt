For normal Schubert surfaces of complex matrix pencils, regular pencils always have nef and big anticanonical class, ample exactly when each eigenvalue has one distinct Jordan-block size; singular pencils have nef anticanonical class precisely when the sum of the maximal block sizes is at most three. The positive anticanonical model is the Schubert surface of the regular pencil retaining only one maximal block per eigenvalue, while the singular higher-sum case has an explicit one-curve Zariski negative part.
