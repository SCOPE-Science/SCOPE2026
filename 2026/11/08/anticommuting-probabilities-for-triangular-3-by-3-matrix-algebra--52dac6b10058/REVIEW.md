# Scientific review: zero Jordan-product pairs in triangular matrices

**Correctness (PASS).** The six matrix-entry equations are obtained by literal multiplication. The proof partitions diagonal coefficients into disjoint cases and counts free off-diagonal variables exactly, with separate cases for odd and even characteristic. The odd-characteristic calculation explicitly handles the two one-constraint exceptions and the all-zero case, while the even-characteristic calculation uses proportional vectors and the alternating cross-term. The partitions sum to the two stated polynomials for all finite fields. Exact rank-of-linear-map enumeration over six fields and literal pair multiplication over two fields agree. The proof, not the finite tests, carries the general quantifiers.

**Originality (PASS, provisional).** Buckley–MacHale–Zelenyuk (2014), DOI 10.12785/amis/080102, develop the invariant and a classification only at high probabilities, but do not give a closed all-field count for this algebra; their averaging identity alone requires a separate, nontrivial rank calculation here. Ghahramani (online 2012), DOI 10.1080/03081087.2012.703193, studies a universal zero-Jordan-product *factorization property*, not a cardinality; only its abstract could be checked. Kaur–Sharma–Singh, arXiv:2006.01493, study ordinary commuting in the group of invertible upper-triangular matrices, rather than zero Jordan products in the full ring. A previous radical-square-zero commutator formula does not apply because the radical of this algebra has nonzero square. Targeted semantic and text searches found no direct or dominating formula; absence of a result is not a uniqueness proof.

**Value (PASS).** Anticommuting probability is an established numerical invariant of finite rings. The triangular algebra of order three is the first canonical triangular example with nontrivial multiplication between radical elements, so it is a natural exact benchmark beyond the radical-square-zero regime. The result measures a large, sharp characteristic distinction: \(q^{-3}\) rather than \(22q^{-6}\) probability decay. This exact small-algebra value is a natural datum for studies of the low-probability range and for comparisons with the large-value classification. No general triangular-dimension theorem is claimed or needed for this precise motivated invariant.

**Risks and limits.** An undiscovered enumeration theorem might cover the precise formulas. The functional-identity article was available only by abstract, but its stated mathematical question is not enumeration. Nothing here determines the full higher-dimensional sequence or orbit structure. The verification is finite and not independent mathematical validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
