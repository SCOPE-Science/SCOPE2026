For the first upper-triangular matrix algebra with nonzero radical-square, the ordered pairs with zero Jordan product have the exact count \(q^3(2q-1)(11q^2-15q+5)\) in odd characteristic and \(q^5(q^4+3q^3-2q^2-2q+1)\) in characteristic two. Their anticommuting probability therefore decays as \(22q^{-6}\) in odd characteristic, but as \(q^{-3}\) along characteristic-two extensions.
