On the dodecahedral graph \(D=GP(10,2)\), the fifteen minimum disjunctive totally dominating sets are precisely the four-vertex antipodal lifts of the edges of the Petersen-graph quotient; in particular, \(\gamma_t^d(D)=4\). Every optimum is frozen under one-vertex replacement, whereas allowing two simultaneous replacements produces the connected line graph of the Petersen graph, with diameter three.
