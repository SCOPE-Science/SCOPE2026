# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

On the Boolean cube the conditional law on the antipodal pair is exchangeable and invariant under global sign. Its distinct-coordinate second and fourth moments are derived from the pointwise identity \(X^2=s^2\) and Newton identities. These moments determine all degree-\(\le2\) Walsh Gram entries. Odd and even characters decouple; degree-one indices give one radial eigenvalue and a standard contrast eigenvalue. Degree-two indices split into a radial line, a standard sector and a zero-row-sum quadratic sector. The radial two-by-two block has exactly one null direction, and direct adjacency identities yield the remaining two eigenvalues. The five positive eigenvalues exhaust the quotient dimension for all stipulated parameters, with strict positivity at the endpoints of the allowed range. The square-dimensional optimizer follows by evaluating all five branches at \(s=\sqrt n\), then using parity to exclude all other offsets by strict comparisons of two explicit branches. Three-wise independence follows from odd symmetry and the second moment. The standalone checker independently constructs actual conditional Walsh Gram matrices for all admissible shells in dimensions 5–11, tests ranks and multiplicities, and checks exact arithmetic for square dimensions 9 through 250000. It passes 3566 assertions. Finite computation supports but does not replace the analytic proof. Scientific limitations: the quotient condition number is not invertibility of the full operator, and only antipodal full-shell observation is optimized.

## Originality (PASS, provisional priority)

The strongest directly comparable published result, *Square-dimension spectral resonances for quadratic-band Walsh concentration on Hamming shells* (published-finding corpus 2026), treats one Hamming shell and has a much larger nullspace of dimension \(n+1\). Its three shell eigenvalues and Johnson-module technique do not assert the two-shell five-band Gram spectrum, the restored standard components, or minimization over antipodal shell radii; notably its arithmetic resonance dimension is one greater than a square, rather than the square-dimensional optimality here. Rao (arXiv:2111.03213v1, 2021) studies the Fourier transform of functions defined on a slice and their coordinate restrictions. Filmus (arXiv:1406.0142v1, 2014) supplies a general orthogonal Johnson-slice basis, so the underlying decomposition is not claimed original. Bannai–Bannai–Bannai (arXiv:1304.5760v1, 2013) concern tight relative designs supported on one or two shells, but their notion of tightness is not equal nonzero singular values for the full quadratic Walsh sampling operator. The nonbinary first-degree Hamming-shell result published by published-finding corpus in 2026 concerns a different frequency band and one shell. Targeted overlap searches found no published statement implying the paired-shell sharp global optimum. Implicit equivalence through a classical general Terwilliger-algebra decomposition remains an acknowledged priority risk; a negative search is not a proof of global novelty.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

A single interior Hamming shell loses a whole first-order family of quadratic Walsh coefficients, whereas the paired complementary shells restore all but one explicit radial ambiguity. Choosing the two measurement radii by the exact formula \(s=\sqrt n\), possible in every square dimension, gives an asymptotically tight frame on the identifiable quotient and a unique optimum in the family. The exact sharp condition ratio, five spectral levels and three-wise independent sampling law are structural design information, not an arbitrary isolated computation, and distinguish spectral design from combinatorial relative-design cardinality. The cost is only two full layers; sparse or asymmetric designs are not covered.

## Remaining risks and restrictions

The broad representation-theoretic methods could implicitly give the five coefficients, and earlier work might encode the radius optimum under different language. No claim of priority beyond the inspected sources. The theorem is deterministic and continuous over each finite shell, not a finite subsample or a noise-model guarantee outside Hilbert-space quotient conditioning.
