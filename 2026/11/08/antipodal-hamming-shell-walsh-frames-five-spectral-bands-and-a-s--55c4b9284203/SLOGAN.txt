Quadratic Walsh measurements on two antipodal Hamming shells have five explicit positive spectral bands and just one radial blind direction. In every square dimension \(n=m^2\ge9\), the unique best-conditioned complementary-shell pair has signed coordinate sums \(\pm m\), is three-wise independent, and has sharp squared condition number \(m^2(m^2-3)/((m^2-1)(m^2-4))\), approaching one as dimension grows.
