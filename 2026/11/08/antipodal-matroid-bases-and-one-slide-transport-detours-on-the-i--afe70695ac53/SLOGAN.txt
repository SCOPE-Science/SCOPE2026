The \(160\) minimum resolving sets of the icosahedral graph are exactly the triples from three different antipodal vertex pairs, forming a rank-\(3\) truncated partition matroid. Their token-slide reconfiguration graph is connected and \(9\)-regular with radius \(4\) and diameter \(6\); exactly \(610\) of its \(12720\) unordered configuration pairs require one slide beyond optimal unrestricted three-token transport.
