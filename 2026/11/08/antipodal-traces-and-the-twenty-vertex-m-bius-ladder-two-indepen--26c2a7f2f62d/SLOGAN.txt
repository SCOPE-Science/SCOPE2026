The twenty-vertex Möbius ladder has two-independence complex \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(M_{20})\simeq\bigvee^{23}S^7\), and its antipodal half-turn has rational degree-seven eigenspace dimensions eleven and twelve. For every Möbius-ladder length \(n\ge3\), the reduced antipodal Lefschetz number of \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(M_{2n})\) is \(-2\cos(n\pi/3)\).
