For every prescribed total observation length, two equal arcs on the circle uniquely give the strongest worst-case energy observation of the Fourier band \(\{-1,0,1\}\) when their centers are antipodal. The sharp lower bound is \((2\beta-\sin(2\beta))/\pi\), with an exact positive separation-loss formula and a \(\beta^{-2}\) small-arc stability advantage over one contiguous interval.
