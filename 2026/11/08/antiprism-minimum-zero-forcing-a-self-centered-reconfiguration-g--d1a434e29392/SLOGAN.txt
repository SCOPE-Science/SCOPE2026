Every minimum zero-forcing set of an \(n\)-antiprism, \(n\ge4\), belongs to one of three explicit cyclic families; there are exactly \(6n\) sets, with only two propagation times, \(n-2\) and \(n-1\). Their token-jumping reconfiguration graph is nonregular yet self-centered: it has \(6n\) vertices, \(18n\) edges, and every vertex has eccentricity \(n\).
