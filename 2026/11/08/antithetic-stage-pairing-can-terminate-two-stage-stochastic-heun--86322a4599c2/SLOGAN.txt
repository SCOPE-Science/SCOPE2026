For a two-component interpolating quadratic, antithetic pairing of the two oracle calls in a Heun-form stochastic gradient step uniquely minimizes the mean-square amplification factor over every coupling with uniform stage marginals, at every positive stepsize. There exists a positive stepsize at which it exactly reaches the minimizer in one update if and only if the relative curvature contrast is at least \(1/\sqrt2\); a rational example terminates even while independent stages and full-gradient Heun are unstable at the same stepsize.
