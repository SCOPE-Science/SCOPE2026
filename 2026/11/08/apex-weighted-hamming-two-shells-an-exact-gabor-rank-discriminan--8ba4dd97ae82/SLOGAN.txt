An arbitrary complex apex coefficient added to the Boolean Hamming weight-two shell yields an exact Gabor matrix-lifting rank discriminant: defects occur only on a real axis, finitely many arithmetic vertical lines, and concentric circles, with exactly additive multiplicities. The maximum rank is \(2^n(1+\binom n2+\tfrac12\binom n4)\), while the positive singular values divided by \(2^{n/2}\) converge for every parameter sequence to \(\tfrac34\delta_2+\tfrac14\delta_6\).
