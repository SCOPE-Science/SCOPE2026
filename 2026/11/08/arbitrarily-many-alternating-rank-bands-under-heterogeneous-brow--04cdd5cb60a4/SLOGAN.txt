Heterogeneous noninteracting Brownian searchers can exhibit arbitrarily many alternating rank fractions where infinitesimal Poisson resetting helps or hinders completion. For any prescribed number \(K\) of positive-weight starting-distance classes, sufficiently separated distances force at least \(2K-1\) distinct reversals of the zero-rate completion-quantile response, despite each searcher resetting only to its own initial position.
