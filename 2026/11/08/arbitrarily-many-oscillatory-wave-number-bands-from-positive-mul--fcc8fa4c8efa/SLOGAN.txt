In the Gaussian density–polarization theory of interacting active Brownian particles, positive Gaussian-core repulsions with \(K\) separated interaction lengths can create exactly \(2K+1\) disconnected wave-number intervals of damped oscillatory relaxation. Thus no finite universal bound on oscillatory-band complexity exists within this closure, even for smooth positive pair potentials with positive Fourier transform; the claim is not a microscopic high-wave-number prediction.
