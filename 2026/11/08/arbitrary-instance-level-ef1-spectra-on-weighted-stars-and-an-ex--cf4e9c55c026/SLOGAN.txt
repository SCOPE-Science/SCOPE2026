With a fixed common additive valuation on a star, connected ordinary EF1 feasibility can follow **any prescribed finite yes/no pattern** as the number of agents changes, even with strictly positive, strictly increasing leaf values. A prefix-sum criterion decides feasibility, and with a dominant hub each feasible population size has exactly \(n!\) labeled allocations; five vertices already permit a feasible two-agent allocation, no three-agent allocation, and feasible four-agent allocations.
