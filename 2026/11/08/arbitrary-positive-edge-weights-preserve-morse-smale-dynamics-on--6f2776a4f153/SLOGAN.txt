The discrete repulsive three-clock path is Morse–Smale for **every** positive pair of edge weights, not just nearly equal weights: its two saddle unstable manifolds always feed the unique antiphase sink when \(0<h<[a+b+\sqrt{a^2-ab+b^2}]^{-1}\). A forward-invariant triangle rules out all saddle-to-saddle connections and shows the sharp diffeomorphic step range.
