# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For all integers \(b,q\ge2\) and product-Haar initial states, integer toral matrices prove invariance and pairwise uniformity, and the unimodular coordinate \(Z=\omega-(b-1)x\) proves the conditional expanding-fiber structure. When \(q\mid b\), each interval image covers the whole circle exactly \(b/q\) times and induction yields conditional i.i.d. labels. For all parameters, fiber mixing yields the covariance limit as the variance of the conditional two-letter cylinder probability. The Fourier coefficients of that probability give the positive convergent series and the fourth-Bernoulli closed form; the first harmonic proves positivity exactly when \(q\nmid b\). The stated exponential approach is bounded via grid-quadrature for bounded-variation functions. An independently implemented exact-rational overlap integration agrees with the Bernoulli formula across the checker’s stated finite range. No unproved extension is presented as a theorem.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The closest primary source, Giulietti–Marmi–Tanzi (arXiv:2104.06434v1), develops approximate decay of correlations for deterministic forcing, with additive forcing in Example 2.2. Its Theorem 2.1 concerns a one-observable two-time correlation and does not give the present all-\(b,q\) arithmetic i.i.d. dichotomy or persistent two-symbol covariance. Direct searches for the precise torus matrix, the divisibility condition and the Bernoulli-polynomial correlation formula revealed no equivalent claim in the checked corpus. Parry (1995) and Siboni (1994) study related toral skew-product systems, accessible here at abstract level only; their full texts were not inspected and might contain relevant special cases. Hence priority is provisional, not certified unique.

## Value — PASS

The result supplies a natural complete classification of when uniform-resolution coarse observations of a simple deterministically driven circle system are genuinely Bernoulli, rather than merely pairwise independent. The alternative regime retains an exact positive long-memory two-letter correlation even though all single-time pairs are independent. The arithmetic criterion and exact rational covariance, including the sharp binary even/odd transition, address statistical observability and clarify the gap between low-order decorrelation and mixing. This is a substantive structural boundary, not a finite table or isolated numerical gain.

## Closest literature and limits

Giulietti, Marmi and Tanzi, *Random-like properties of chaotic forcing* (2021 preprint; 2022 journal) is the motivating general system. Parry, *Ergodic properties of a one-parameter family of skew-products* (1995), and Siboni, *Ergodic properties of a class of skew-systems* (1994), provide additional general context, but only abstract material was available for those two articles. Standard circle-expansion mixing, Haar homomorphism properties and the fourth-Bernoulli Fourier expansion are classical and explicitly used as prior methods. The proof treats one fixed integer expansion, equal-length cells, Haar stationarity and two-letter cylinder correlations; other partitions or nonlinear forcing are not classified. Publication-priority risk remains for equivalent identities hidden in older group-extension or symbolic-partition literature.
