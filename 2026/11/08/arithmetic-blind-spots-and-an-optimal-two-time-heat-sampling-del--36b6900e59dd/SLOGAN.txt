On an even cycle, observing every other vertex before and after heat diffusion recovers the complete signal exactly when the cycle length is \(2\pmod4\); multiples of four have permanent Fourier blind spots. The sharp recovery frame bound has an explicitly computable optimum whose sampling delay tends to \(0.5544287764\ldots\), while its best possible magnitude decays like \(1.1913354676\ldots/N^2\).
