# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The displayed degree-two del Pezzo surface is smooth in characteristic three. The Hermitian tangent identity restricts the quartic to a scalar fourth power on each of its 28 rational tangent lines over \(\mathbb F_9\), yielding all 56 geometric exceptional components. On each of the four \(\mathbb F_3\)-rational tangent lines the scalar is the nonsquare 2, so its two exceptional branches are conjugate; the other 24 tangent lines themselves are nonrational. Exactly 63 anisotropic base points each lie on four distinct tangents, yielding 126 ordinary fourfold points of the boundary, while its 28 branch points are tangencies of two components. Every such singular support point is \(\mathbb F_9\)-rational. These facts prove the boundary formulas uniformly for all finite extensions, without extrapolation from enumeration. Rank-eight Picard cohomology and the ten-point base count give the surface zeta function. The normalization sequence and localization long exact sequence give the precise compactly supported eigenvalue decomposition; the key degree-two cohomology injection follows from the exceptional curves spanning the nondegenerate Picard lattice. Exact finite-field enumeration corroborates the F9 incidence and F27 point counts. All quantifiers, branches, sheaf normalizations, and exceptional characteristics are accounted for.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Kaplan (2013, Proposition 70) already determines this exact model over \(\mathbb F_9\) and its maximal point count. Knecht and Reyes (2015, Theorem 4.1) already establish fullness, the 28 hyperflexes, and nine generalized Eckardt points per bitangent. The present finding does not claim those results as new, nor independently claim the lack of \(\mathbb F_3\)- or \(\mathbb F_9\)-rational complement points as novel. The additional assertion is the arithmetic descent to \(\mathbb F_3\): no individually rational exceptional curve, yet exactly ten boundary points throughout **every odd-degree extension**, with the full Frobenius spectrum of the complete exceptional complement. The inspected relevant primary statements do not give this conclusion or the nonsquare tangent-splitting obstruction, and targeted published mathematical-record searches did not identify an equivalent claim; this does **not** prove uniqueness or historical priority. Specialist treatments of Hermitian configurations and open del Pezzo motives remain a substantive residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The reduced union of every exceptional curve is the most natural distinguished divisor on this del Pezzo surface. Its arithmetic behavior is not captured by geometric fullness at the quadratic extension: none of its components descends, yet its rational points stabilize throughout all odd extensions. The complete cohomology decomposition of its open complement determines motivic invariants that are finer than the known small-field point count and geometric hyperflex configuration. The exact structural result combines a geometric Galois descent obstruction with boundary cohomology, rather than an arbitrary parameter slice or one additional finite-field enumeration. The characteristic-three setting and the exceptional boundary are intrinsically motivated.

## Closest literature and limitations

Kaplan, *Rational Point Counts for del Pezzo Surfaces over Finite Fields and Coding Theory* (2013), Proposition 70, includes the same split Fermat double plane. Knecht–Reyes, *Full Degree Two del Pezzo Surfaces over Small Finite Fields* (arXiv:1510.04621, posted 2015), Theorem 4.1, specifies its 28-hyperflex and fullness structure over F9. They cover multiple important antecedents and, combined with standard incidence counting, imply the even-extension boundary count. They do not directly settle the nontrivial F3 branch descent or full compactly supported Frobenius spectrum in the inspected portions. The work is not a complete classification of twists, and an independent literature audit remains necessary to establish historical novelty.
