For the complete \(56\)-exceptional-curve boundary of the Fermat degree-two del Pezzo surface \(w^2=x^4+y^4+z^4\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), no component descends individually to \(\mathbb F_3\), yet the boundary has exactly ten points over every odd-degree extension. The precise even-degree growth and the full compactly supported Frobenius spectrum of the complement are determined by the Hermitian incidence and the branch-descent obstruction.
