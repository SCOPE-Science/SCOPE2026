For every fixed gap \(k\ge2\), rational-Hartogs Bergman numerator zeros approaching the nontrivial \(k\)-th roots of unity admit exact first-order radial escape constants determined by the modular inverse of the numerator modulo \(k\). At gap five, the rate already splits into distinct arithmetic residue classes, beyond the previously known unquantified all-gap localization and sharp gap-three case.
