For odd dihedral quandle algebras in characteristic coprime to the quandle order, every derivation is circulant, and its Lie algebra is abelian with dimension given explicitly by signed doubling orbits. Unexpected nonzero derivations occur in cross-characteristic, including quandle order \(11\) in characteristic \(3\) and order \(13\) in characteristic \(5\), although all odd-order derivations vanish in characteristic zero.
