On every classical power-weighted analytic Bergman space, the cross-adjoint commutator of composition maps \(z\mapsto z^m\) and \(z\mapsto z^n\), for integers \(m,n\ge2\), is compact exactly when the exponents are coprime. In that case it has an exact positive \(1/N\) singular-value coefficient and is weak trace class but not trace class; otherwise its essential norm is explicitly \((mn)^{-(\alpha+1)/2}\).
