For the smooth genus-nine curve \((x^7+x)(z^4+z)=x^8+x^6+x^4+x^2+1\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), exactly eight of its 32 geometric automorphisms descend to \(\mathbb F_2\), forming \(C_4\times C_2\). Every geometric automorphism is already defined over \(\mathbb F_4\), and the full automorphism group over \(\mathbb F_{2^r}\) alternates between these two groups according to the parity of \(r\).
