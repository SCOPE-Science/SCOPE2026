The Fermat cubic has 8244 geometric conics through six distinct sextactic points; over finite fields of characteristic at least five, the number of geometrically smooth rational such conics is exactly \(5976\), \(0\), or \(22\) according to the splitting of its two- and three-torsion. The complete Frobenius cycle types of the conics, the 27 sextactic points and their 81 trisecants are determined; notably nine trisecants can be rational even when no sextactic point is rational.
