For every odd prime \(p\), the nonzero idempotents of the characteristic-\(p\) dihedral-quandle algebra \(k[R_p]\) form an explicit affine core quandle of dimension \((p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact number of all idempotents is \(1+q^{(p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))}\), giving \(290\) at \(p=q=17\).
