For the fully chaotic logistic map, the exact number of positive-measure edges between \(m\) equal amplitude bins is \(3m-m/R_m-(m\bmod2)\), where \(R_m\) is the least positive integer whose square is divisible by \(m/\gcd(m,4)\). Its finite-resolution deficit has sharp order \(\sqrt m\), and almost every initial state eventually exhibits every such edge infinitely often.
