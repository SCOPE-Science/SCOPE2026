For two distinct monomial slant Toeplitz operators of order \(k\ge2\) on the Bergman space, the commutator has essential norm exactly \(2\) when the monomial degrees coincide modulo \(k^2\), and exactly \(\sqrt2\) otherwise. Its absolute-value essential spectrum jumps from the full interval \([0,2]\) to the two-point set \(\{0,\sqrt2\}\).
