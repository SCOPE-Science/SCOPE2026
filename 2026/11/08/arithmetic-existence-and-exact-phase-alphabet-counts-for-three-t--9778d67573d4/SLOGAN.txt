For a flat cyclic Gabor window in even dimension, three arithmetic-progression translations and all even modulations form a tight frame exactly when the translation step has order congruent to three modulo six. A finite \(q\)-phase alphabet permits this precisely when \(3\mid q\); the exact number of indexed tight windows is \(q^p(2q)^{d/2}\), where \(N=2p\) and \(d=\gcd(N,s)\).
