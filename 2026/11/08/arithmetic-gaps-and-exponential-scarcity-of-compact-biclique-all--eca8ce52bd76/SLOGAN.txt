For identical unit-value goods on a complete bipartite graph, every labeled envy-free compact allocation admits an exact factorial-coefficient census, and radius-one feasibility occupies an arithmetic progression inside the full radius-two range. At the smallest gap-forming bundle size of four, balanced instances exclude radius-one allocations for odd agent counts and have the sharp conditional fraction \(\sim(4/\sqrt7)(4/7)^n\) for even counts; the elementary star-factor existence condition was already known.
