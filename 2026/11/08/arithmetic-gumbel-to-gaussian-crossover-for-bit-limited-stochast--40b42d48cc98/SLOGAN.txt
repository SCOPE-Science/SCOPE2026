For a scalar descent step with downward-truncated \(r\)-bit stochastic rounding and decrement \(\varepsilon=(q2^r)^{-1}\), extinction is an exact pure-death chain with rates \(\lceil j/q\rceil/2^r\). At high guard-bit resolution its scaled termination time has a \(q\)-fold averaged Gumbel limit for fixed integer \(q\), whereas diverging \(q\) yields Gaussian standardized fluctuations; finite means, variances and the full probability generating function are explicit.
