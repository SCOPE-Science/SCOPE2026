For the Clebsch diagonal cubic over every finite field of characteristic greater than five, the numbers of points lying on zero, one, two or three of its geometric 27 lines are explicit polynomials determined only by the quadratic character of \(5\). The resulting full-extension zeta function of the line complement shows that precisely \(\mathbb F_7\) and \(\mathbb F_{11}\) have no rational points off those lines.
