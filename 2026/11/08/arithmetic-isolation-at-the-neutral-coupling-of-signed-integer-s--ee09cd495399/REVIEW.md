# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Subtracting the two coupled updates gives the exact congruence \(q(x'-y')\equiv\pm q(x-y)\pmod1\), valid at branch boundaries. This proves both first integrals, the permanent separation, and the invariant-level singularity argument. The uniform gap law follows from Haar-uniform residues of independent initial coordinates; conditioning an invariant density on nontrivial invariant levels rules out a unique absolutely continuous invariant probability. The exact-rational checker reproduces the algebra for finite samples but the proof does not depend on sampling.

## Originality
**PASS within inspected evidence.** Zhang–Wang [1] proves synchronization and intermittent synchronization only on strict coupling intervals, leaving the ternary neutral equalities uncovered. The present arithmetic foliation forces a genuinely different critical dynamical behavior; it is not implied by the sign of the transverse exponent alone. Li–Lin–Wang–Yau [2] addresses a continuous tent map, and Keller–Künzle–Nowicki [3] addresses slope two rather than the claimed slopes three and above. Comparison was made at the level of asserted implications and models, not keywords alone. The full text of [3] was inaccessible and remains a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The known ternary lattice has sharply distinguished intermittent and synchronized regimes but leaves both transition points untreated. The theorem resolves exactly those boundary cases and demonstrates a third, quantitatively distinct regime with uniform-in-time desynchronization, an exact uniform isolation-radius law, and an obstruction to ergodic invariant densities. It also clarifies why vanishing transverse Lyapunov exponent cannot settle global synchronization for discontinuous branches.

## Scope and limitations
The formula is for two nodes, exact integer slopes, and the two neutral couplings; it does not classify all invariant measures or assert that an absolutely continuous invariant probability exists. The probabilistic bound is for guaranteed isolation, not an equality for the actual minimum along trajectories. Historical priority needs independent checking.
