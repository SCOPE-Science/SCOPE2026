# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof covers all \(n\ge2\) and all finite fields \(\mathbb F_q\) with nonzero cycle weights. It invokes the previously established monomial-isomorphism criterion for perfect evolution algebras, verifies that the only graph permutations are rotations, and derives the exact transformation law for the weighted product invariant. The converse scalar recursion supplies existence, not just necessity. Kernel/image counting and Möbius inversion give the automorphism-order and class multiplicity formulas. Over \(\mathbb F_4\), explicit generators give both complete group isomorphism types; direct computation of the generator power establishes the nonsplit criterion. There is no assumption of algebraic closure. The independent finite enumerator and explicit automorphism checker passed (45 orbit comparisons, 50 even-dimensional checks, and all 180 invertible two-dimensional linear maps over the four-element field). These finite checks corroborate, rather than replace, the proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected work
Wei--Zou, *Isomorphism Classes of Idempotent Evolution Algebras* (arXiv:2310.12368), already prove the general monomial-orbit framework; that criterion is not claimed. Sriwongsa--Zou (arXiv:2202.01333) prove maximal diagonal-group results and a cyclic semidirect product in algebraically closed characteristic zero; the finite-field twist spectrum and nonsplit groups are outside that assertion. Alshatnawi--Costoya--Viruel (arXiv:2607.15154), Remark 9.2, give the normalization to one weighted cycle parameter, which is prior work. The assessed step is the arithmetic classification by doubling orbits together with full automorphism-order spectrum and, over \(\mathbb F_4\), explicit abstract group structures for both twists. Kaygorodov--Martin González--Viruel (arXiv:2610.22248) exhibit a different nonsplit six-dimensional example; existence of such extensions in isolation is prior work and not asserted as novel. Searches for equivalent cycle-specific all-dimensional spectra and this infinite simple nonsplit family yielded no covering theorem. Unidentified broader automorphism-group or orbit-census results remain a publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Weighted directed cycles are natural, indecomposable, simple perfect evolution algebras. Their arithmetic descent from algebraically closed fields produces a genuinely field-sensitive isomorphism problem. Exact classification and automorphism spectra answer intrinsic, motivated questions for every finite field, and the even-dimensional \(\mathbb F_4\) dichotomy proves a concrete infinite family of nonsplit group extensions, not merely an arbitrary low-dimensional example; it supplies an explicit solution on one natural family to Question 33 of the 2026 survey about splitting of the diagonal-automorphism sequence. These statements offer reusable test cases for theories of automorphism extensions and field-dependent classification.

## Limits and evidence
This is a proof for directed single cycles with nonzero weights; it does not determine arbitrary graph-supported evolution algebras or the abstract automorphism groups for arbitrary \(q\). The explicit \(\mathbb F_4\) group calculation is special to that field. A previous paper proves the normalization to one weight, and a survey proves a different nonsplit example. The verifier enumerates finitely many cases. Independent scientific audit and final comprehensive priority determination are outstanding.
