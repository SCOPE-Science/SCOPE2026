Weighted directed-cycle evolution algebras over \(\mathbb F_q\) are classified in every dimension by doubling orbits on the cyclic group of order \(\gcd(q-1,2^n-1)\), with an exact automorphism-order spectrum. Over \(\mathbb F_4\), each even dimension has precisely two simple isomorphism types, whose automorphism groups are respectively \(C_3\rtimes C_n\) and \(C_{3n/2}\); every dimension divisible by six yields a nonsplit diagonal-automorphism extension in this explicit infinite family.
