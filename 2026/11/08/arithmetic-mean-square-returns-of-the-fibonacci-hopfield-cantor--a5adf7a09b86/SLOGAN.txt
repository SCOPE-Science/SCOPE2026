On the chaotic Cantor attractor of a Fibonacci-driven Hopfield network, the stationary mean-square return at every time is an exact weighted arithmetic sum of irrational-rotation distances. At Fibonacci times the return is \(\frac43\varphi^{-j}\) and falls below that leading value by a strictly positive relative amount of order \(2^{-F_{j+1}}\), exponentially small in the Fibonacci denominator.
