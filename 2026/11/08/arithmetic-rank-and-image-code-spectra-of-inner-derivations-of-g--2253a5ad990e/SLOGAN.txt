For every odd-order finite abelian kernel \(H\), the complete rank and distinct-image-space spectra of inner derivations of \(\mathbb F_q[H\rtimes C_2]\) factor according to the paired Frobenius/inversion orbits of characters. The formulas give exact rank multiplicities, exact numbers of realized image codes, and an arithmetic criterion for rank-two inner derivations, including in characteristic two.
