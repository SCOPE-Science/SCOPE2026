The complete singular spectrum of self-Fourier sampling on the nonzero two-dimensional Veronese image is an explicit multiplicative-character Gauss-period decomposition over every odd finite field. Every prime-field instance is invertible, but quadratic extensions with \(p\equiv1\pmod4\) have an explicit growing kernel for the canonical trace character; the \(\mathbb F_{25}\) operator has exact nullity \(52\).
