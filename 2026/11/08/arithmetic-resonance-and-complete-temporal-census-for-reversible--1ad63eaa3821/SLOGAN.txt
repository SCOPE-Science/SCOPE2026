For reversible characteristic-two neighbor-sum waves on a wheel with a rim of length \(n\ge5\), every state is periodic, and the complete fixed-point and primitive-orbit populations have explicit formulas in \(\gcd(n,t)\), divisibility by three, and the powers of two dividing \(n\) and \(t\). Adding the hub changes the exact global clock to \(\operatorname{lcm}(6,n)\) for odd \(n\), \(4n\) for \(2\parallel n\), \(2n\) for \(4\parallel n\), and \(n\) for \(8\mid n\).
