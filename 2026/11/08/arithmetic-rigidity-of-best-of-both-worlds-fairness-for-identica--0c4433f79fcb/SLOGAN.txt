For identical unit chores, exact weighted envy-freeness in expectation together with ex-post WEF1 is feasible on a continuum of entitlements below the one-chore-per-agent threshold, but at and above that threshold it is possible only for finitely many rational weight vectors. At exactly one more chore than agents, the feasible vectors are classified by the \(2^n-1\) nonempty subsets of agents; for two agents and at least two chores, exactly \(2m-3\) distinct entitlement splits work.
