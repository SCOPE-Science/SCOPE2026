In characteristic seven, the genus-five Howe pencil obtained from \(y^2=x^4-1\) and \(z^2=x^4-a\) has an exact geometric Jacobian rank stratification, with a unique superspecial parameter \(a=-1\). All five rational parameters have explicitly determined Hasse--Weil zeta numerators; the \(a=-1\) member attains the genus-five Weil upper bound of 120 points over \(\mathbb F_{49}\), uniquely within this pencil.
