# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The literal fixed-point equation on each weighted permutation cycle yields a zero-or-positive solution event with exact probability \(1-1/\gcd(2^\ell-1,q-1)\); the complement events factor over disjoint cycles. The permutation exponential formula, Möbius inversion, positivity-based support characterization, and finite-root singularity analysis establish the stated exact series, exact support, and sharp limiting constant, including the essential multiplicity factor \(D\). The proof specifies the exceptional odd-part-one fields and fixed-field asymptotic quantification. The standalone program independently solves finite-field idempotence equations and verifies exact coefficient identities; it is corroborative, not the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with a substantial prior-implication caveat
The existing cycle-factor law and full integer moments for the same ensemble, as well as the \(q=4\) probability, are explicitly treated as prior and excluded from the new contribution. The submitted theorem instead derives the **general all-field arithmetic support semigroup, full cyclotomic factorization, and sharp exponent and leading constant**; these require supplementary root-of-unity analysis not stated in that prior calculation. The particular \(q=64\) two-generator support and exponent \(100/189\) illustrate the extra content. Wei–Zou study isomorphism-class enumeration over all invertible structure matrices, and Narkuziyev studies permutation-associated evolution algebras and particular idempotent equations. Neither inspected source contains the claimed all-field tail law. An exact finite-dimensional cycle law determines the probability sequence in principle, so priority and the degree of independence from the previous result remain real risks. A generic weighted-cycle singularity theorem might also encompass the analytic step. No unverified independence or exhaustive-literature claim is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The existence of a nonzero idempotent is a natural property of a nonassociative algebra and a discrete fixed-point question for its quadratic evolution map. Exact arithmetic non-occurrence dimensions and the uniform-field power-law decay with explicit constants answer a natural rare-event question; the transition from even-only support over \(\mathbb F_{16}\) to every dimension at least two over \(\mathbb F_{64}\) demonstrates a meaningful field-arithmetic effect. This is a motivated sharp asymptotic refinement, not a new general classification or an asserted paper-sized contribution.

## Closest sources, coverage, and remaining limitations
The closest previously derived result in the same sampling ensemble supplies the cycle-factor law, its all-moment generating functions and the complete \(q=4\) special case. Wei–Zou, arXiv:2310.12368, Sections 1–2, concern isomorphism classes of all nonsingular evolution algebras. Narkuziyev, arXiv:2005.05793, Sections 4–5, studies particular idempotent systems and permutation-associated algebras. Robles–Zeindler, arXiv:1806.04700, develops singularity tools for a different logarithmic cycle-weight model. Residual risks include a stronger general periodic-cycle theorem, as well as the earlier cycle law's logical implication of finite probabilities. The sampling convention is labeled monomial matrices, not uniform invertibles or isomorphism classes. Numerical agreement is finite only.
