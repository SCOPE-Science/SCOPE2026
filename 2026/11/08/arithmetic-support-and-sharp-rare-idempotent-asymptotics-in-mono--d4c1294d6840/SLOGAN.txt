For uniformly random monomial evolution algebras over any fixed finite field, the probability that zero is the only idempotent has a complete arithmetic support criterion and an explicit sharp power-law asymptotic. Its leading coefficient detects all equally dominant roots of unity; over \(\mathbb F_{64}\) the probability is positive in every dimension at least two and decays like a constant times \(n^{-89/189}\), while over \(\mathbb F_{16}\) it vanishes in every odd dimension.
