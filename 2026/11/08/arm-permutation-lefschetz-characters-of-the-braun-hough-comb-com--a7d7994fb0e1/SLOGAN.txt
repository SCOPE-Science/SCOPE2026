Arm permutations of the Braun–Hough comb independence complexes have a complete reduced Lefschetz character depending only on the parity of the permutation's number of arm cycles; every nontrivial irreducible beyond the sign representation cancels in the alternating rational homology. In the sphere case \(n=2\), the entire top integral homology is precisely the sign lattice \(\mathbb Z_{\operatorname{sgn}}\).
