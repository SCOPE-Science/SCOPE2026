For the two-species Ricker map at intrinsic growth \(2\), all weak but unequal competition strengths synchronize the equilibrium-normalized populations exponentially even though convergence to coexistence may be only algebraic. Every non-exponentially relaxing orbit has the same sharp critical clock, \(z_{2m}^{-2}=\frac83m+\frac85\log m+C+o(1)\), for both species with one common constant \(C\), independently of the asymmetric couplings. A codimension-one asymmetric resonance \(1-2\alpha-2\beta+3\alpha\beta=0\) upgrades interspecies synchronization from geometric to supergeometric.
