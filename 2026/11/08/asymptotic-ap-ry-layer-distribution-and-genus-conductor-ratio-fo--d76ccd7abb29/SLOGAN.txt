For the shifted-square numerical semigroups \(S_a=\langle a,a+j^2:j\ge1\rangle\), asymptotically one-sixth of nonzero Apéry residues have height four and five-sixths have height three. Their genus is \(19a/6+O(a/\sqrt{\log a})\), their conductor is \(4a+O(a/\sqrt{\log a})\), and the gap-to-conductor ratio tends to \(19/24\).
