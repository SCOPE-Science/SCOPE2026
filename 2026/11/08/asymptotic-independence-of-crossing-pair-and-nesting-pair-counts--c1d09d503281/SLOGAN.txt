The numbers of crossing and nesting edge pairs in a uniform random perfect matching are negatively correlated at every finite size, with exact correlation \(-5/[2(n+3)]\), but their centered \(n^{3/2}\)-scaled counts converge jointly to **independent** Gaussians of variance \(1/45\). The key mechanism is the exact orthogonality of their first conditional chord projections; previously known joint generating functions already determine the finite moments.
