For every fixed finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\ge3\), almost every labeled simple evolution algebra has no nonidentity automorphisms, and its isomorphism classes obey \(N_{n,q}\sim\bigl(\prod_{j\ge1}(1-q^{-j})\bigr)q^{n^2}/((q-1)^n n!)\). This yields an asymptotic census of simple types from their monomial natural-basis orbits, without claiming a census of all non-perfect algebras.
