For finite complete directed simplicial cups, the full three-valued modal face quotient is as small as its atomic signatures exactly when every atomic source type has uniform zero/nonzero access to each target type; its labeled census is an explicit product. On balanced randomly directed cups, this semantic collapse has two separated sharp critical windows, at edge densities \(c/n^2\) and \((\log n+c)/n\), and disappears between them.
