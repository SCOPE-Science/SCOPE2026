For the \(m\)-arrow Kronecker path algebra in characteristic \(p\), every derivation satisfying \(D^p=0\) belongs to an automorphism orbit indexed by a nilpotent Jordan partition and either zero or one of its occurring block lengths below \(p\). The exact orbit-generating series and finite-field rank and orbit-size formulas follow from the centralizer action on \(\ker(T^{p-1})/TV\); in characteristic two there are exactly \(m+1\) conjugacy classes.
