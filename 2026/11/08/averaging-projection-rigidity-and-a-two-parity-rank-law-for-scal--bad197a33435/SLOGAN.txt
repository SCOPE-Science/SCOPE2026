For the scalar-affine Lie algebra \(\mathbb F_q e\ltimes\mathbb F_q^m\), every idempotent averaging operator is unexpectedly a Lie-algebra retraction, with exact rank polynomial \(1+\sum_{r=0}^{m}{m\brack r}_q q^{(m-r)(r+1)}z^{r+1}\). The centered rank has two reflected discrete-Gaussian theta limits according to the parity of \(m\), with total-variation error \(O_q(q^{-m/4})\).
