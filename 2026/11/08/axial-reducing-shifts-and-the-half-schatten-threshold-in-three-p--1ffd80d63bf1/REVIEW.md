# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The boundary measure densities are normalized by the exact beta moments \(h_j=\binom{2j}{j}/4^j\). The independent torus characters prove complete orthogonality of axial monomials to the \((s,p)\)-ideal, so the axial subspaces reduce the individual first-coordinate multiplier. The shift weights follow by consecutive moment ratios. Direct matrix action gives the full positive diagonal self-commutator and trace telescope, while elementary comparison with \(k^{-2}\) gives the sharp all-\(q>0\) Schatten boundary. The kernel follows from normalized basis evaluation and its closed form from an explicitly solved generating-function equation. None of these analytic conclusions depends on a truncated computation.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge comparison)

The closest primary work is Su–Tomar (arXiv:2610.17729), fully inspected, especially its Section 2 fibre decomposition, Section 4 joint irreducibility and Section 5 three weighted measures. Those give the setting but do not explicitly identify the simultaneous axial tail models and their restricted self-commutator threshold or axial arcsine kernel. Joint irreducibility is for three multipliers and does not imply irreducibility for one multiplier. Pal–Tomar (arXiv:2606.00850) treats Toeplitz corona criteria using different operators and general kernels, not the exact axial spectral problem. Searches included equivalent phrases for pentablock weighted shifts, axial kernels, trace and Schatten boundaries. No covering statement was found; standard shift and beta identities are not presented as novel. Remaining priority risk: an older calculation in weighted Bergman/Hardy-shift language may imply part of the statement without pentablock terminology.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The sharp \(1/2\) cutoff and leading constants are natural quantitative invariants of a coordinate multiplier on three geometrically motivated, genuinely different Hardy spaces. The fact that the axial restrictions are tail-equivalent while the ambient spaces are not equivalent is a structural relation, and the explicit restricted kernels also settle their boundary evaluation-growth rates. This is not a claim about the full multiplier's self-commutator or all coordinates.
