Across the three natural pentablock Hardy-space measures, the first-coordinate multiplier has the same explicit one-variable weighted-shift structure up to successive tails. Its axial self-commutator belongs precisely to the Schatten ideals \(S_q\) for \(q>1/2\), and all three axial reproducing kernels have an exact arcsine form with \((1-r^2)^{-3/2}\) boundary growth.
