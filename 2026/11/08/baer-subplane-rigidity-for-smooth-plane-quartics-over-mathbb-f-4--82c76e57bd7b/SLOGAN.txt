Over \(\mathbb F_4\), a smooth plane quartic whose rational points are exactly a seven-point Baer subplane must descend, up to scalar and coordinates, to \(\mathbb F_2\). Its seven forced tangent-line incidences form a Fano perfect matching: exactly 24 curves occur per Baer subplane and 8640 in all, forming one projective orbit with stabilizer order 7.
