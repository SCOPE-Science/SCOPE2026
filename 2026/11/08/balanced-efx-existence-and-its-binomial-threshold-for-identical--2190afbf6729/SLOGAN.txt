For agents with identical values \(H>L>0\) and exactly \(r\) goods each, balanced EFX exists precisely when \(H\le2L\), the high-good count is divisible by the agent count, or every agent can receive at most one low good. Above the contrast boundary, this yields exact allocation counts and a Gaussian random-feasibility threshold at high-good probability \((r-1)/r\), despite unconditional balanced EF1 existence.
