For complex quadratic polynomials, exactly four uniformly spaced intensity samples on each of two concentric circles recover every coefficient vector up to phase precisely when the radii differ and the two quadrature grids are angularly staggered by an angle \(\phi\) with \(\sin(2\phi)\ne0\). The eight measurements are count-minimal, and the criterion yields an equally budgeted two-Gaussian-channel, three-tone phase-retrieval design.
