For any odd-order finite abelian group, every balanced four-point convolution on its generalized dihedral extension has an exact arithmetic nullity and complete singular spectrum. Over \(\mathbb F_p^d\), the two pair-differences yield exactly three kernel strata; independent differences have smallest positive singular value \(2(1-\cos(\pi/p))\), so their sharp recoverable-space condition number grows quadratically with \(p\).
