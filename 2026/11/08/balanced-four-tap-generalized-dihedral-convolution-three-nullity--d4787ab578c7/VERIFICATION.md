The theorem is proved by an exact unitary two-coset character decomposition, inclusion–exclusion of annihilator hyperplanes, and elementary optimization of root-of-unity moduli. Run `python3 dihedral_four_tap_check.py` from the extracted directory with NumPy. Its direct integer convolution matrices, modular-rank checks, and numerical singular-value comparisons cover multiple elementary and cyclic odd-order groups. It also enumerates all two-pair support configurations for the indicated two small vector spaces. Finite checks are not a proof of the infinite families; no independent mathematical audit has been performed.
