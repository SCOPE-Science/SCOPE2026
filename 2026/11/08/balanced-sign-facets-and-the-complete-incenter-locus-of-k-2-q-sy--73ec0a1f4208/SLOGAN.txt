For every \(q\ge3\), the canonical symmetric-edge polytope of \(K_{2,q}\) has inradius \(\bigl(q-(q\bmod2)/(q+2)\bigr)^{-1/2}\). Its entire family of maximum-ball centers is an explicit segment when \(q\) is odd and an explicit rectangle when \(q\) is even, with every tangent facet classified by balanced sign vectors.
