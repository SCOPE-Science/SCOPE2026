For every Stewart word, including every nonperiodic directive and either completion of a persistent hole, the number of distinct Parikh vectors of length \(n\) is exactly \(1+H_3(n)\), where \(H_3(n)\) is the number of nonzero balanced-ternary digits of \(n\). The formula gives an identical Parikh spectrum for the entire uncountable family and logarithmically growing peaks in an otherwise linear-factor-complexity class.
