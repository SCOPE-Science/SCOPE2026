For every degree bound \(d\), \(4d\) intensity measurements at two sets of \(2d\) positive radii on distinct rays determine a complex polynomial up to phase whenever the radial products differ and \(\sin(m\theta)\ne0\) for all \(1\le m\le d\). The same data recover both complete radial intensity polynomials by an explicit linear interpolation formula; angular resonance has concrete ambiguity witnesses, and \(4d\) is sharp for linear reconstruction of these whole profiles.
