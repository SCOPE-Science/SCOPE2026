For every \(a,b\ge1\), the entire distinct-scattered-subsequence length spectrum of \((0^a1^b)^m\) has an explicit rational generating function in the subsequence length and number of periods. Among all positive two-run periods of fixed length, the balanced split uniquely maximizes the total for every \(m\ge1\), and its exponential growth base is governed by the product \(ab\).
