For a chiral swimmer that turns through a fixed angle after arbitrary independent finite-mean run durations, the run-time phase characteristic function separates bounded trajectories, strictly positive mean-square diffusion, and exact ballistic resonance. Two-point timing jitter of amplitude \(\sigma\) changes resonant ballistic motion to diffusion with the sharp singularity \(D_{\mathrm{MSD}}\sim4/(\pi\sigma^2)\).
