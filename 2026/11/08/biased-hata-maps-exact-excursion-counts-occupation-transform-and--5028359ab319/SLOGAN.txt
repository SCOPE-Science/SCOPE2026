For the Hata random interval map with unequal map-selection probabilities, the number of additional visits to the disfavored half is exactly geometric, and its total occupation time has an explicit algebraic generating function. Near equal selection, excursion count scales as \(\delta^{-1}\) but occupation time scales as \(\delta^{-2}\), with distinct explicit limiting laws; off criticality the trajectory almost surely converges to the favored endpoint.
