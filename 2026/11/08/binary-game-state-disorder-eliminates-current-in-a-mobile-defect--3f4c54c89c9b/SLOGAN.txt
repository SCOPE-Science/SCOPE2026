In a two-synergy frozen public-goods game on a cycle, a single cooperative block produces a permanently mobile one- or two-defector boundary whenever \(1<r_L<r_H<1+r_L/2\); its exact stationary Markov chain is reversible for every binary edge arrangement. This universal reversibility breaks at three synergy levels, while a homogeneous environment has exactly \(2/r\) defectors on average at stationarity.
