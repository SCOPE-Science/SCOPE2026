The cospectral Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have sharply different synchronous binary-majority clocks: the rook graph has 302 fixed states and 1,197 primitive two-cycles, versus 142 and 685 for the Shrikhande graph. Their sharp transient depths are three and five, respectively, and the first graph has eight four-occupied fixed configurations while the second has none.
