Binary palstars with exactly four ones admit a complete five-gap membership criterion and a rational content-refined generating function. Their number at length \(2m\), for \(m\ge2\), is \(\bigl(4m^4-4m^3+2m^2-8m+3-3(-1)^m\bigr)/24\), so their asymptotic density among weight-four binary words is exactly \(1/4\).
