For uniformly random \(q\)-ary strings, deleting one observation from the unpadded unordered-composition \(\ell\)-read vector has exactly \(1+\operatorname{Bin}(n-\ell,(q-1)/q)\) distinct outcomes. Ambiguity counts for different window lengths are pairwise uncorrelated but not generally independent, with all underlying position-equality indicators mutually independent exactly on forests.
