# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The independent-parent construction makes final leaf status equivalent to excluding one parent choice at each subsequent arrival. The resulting conditional path-length recurrence has an exact harmonic solution, with all boundary indices checked. The sum over indices and the unimodality limit are derived analytically. Exact finite enumeration and symbolic recurrence checks independently reproduce the identity but are not invoked as infinite proofs.

## Originality

**PASS (qualified).** The unit of novelty is the *birth-indexed leaf-event/total-path-length covariance and conditional regression profile*, not the marginal path-length law or global leaf distribution. The 1999 primary path-length study was read in full; its moment and fixed-point theorems are not statements about leaf status at label \(j\). A 2026 full-text study of leaves and path lengths uses global markers, not indexed labels, so its generating functions may imply an aggregate special case but do not automatically specify the full age-indexed formula. A closely related published root-attachment/path-length profile involves an attachment-to-root event and does not determine the high-order no-attachment event through individual second moments. Residual risk: earlier implicit age-marked calculations and one inaccessible 2014 dissertation are not conclusively excluded.

## Value

**PASS.** Birth age controls both remaining exposure to future attachments and total search cost. The exact conditional mean reveals how these mechanisms combine; the finite concavity and asymptotic \(e^{-1}\) maximum locate the age with strongest local dependence, while the global covariance follows without needing a high-dimensional generating-function computation. These are natural statistics in a canonical growing-tree model, not arbitrarily selected parameters.
