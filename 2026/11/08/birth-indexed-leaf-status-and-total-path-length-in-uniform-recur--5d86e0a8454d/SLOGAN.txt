For a uniform recursive tree, conditioning the vertex born at time \(j\) to remain a leaf reduces the expected total path length by exactly \(H_{n-1}-H_{j-1}\). The resulting negative leaf-status covariance has a birth-age profile with maximal asymptotic magnitude \(e^{-1}\) at relative birth time \(e^{-1}\), and its sum across vertices equals \(-(n-2)/4\).
