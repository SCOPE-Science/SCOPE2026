Sequential internal DLA on \(K_{a,b}\), started in its \(a\)-vertex class, has an exact geometric-repeat/coupon-collector clock for the moment that class fills. For fixed \(a\), its \(B\)-occupation level scaled by \(\sqrt b\) is the maximum of \(a-1\) independent Rayleigh variables; when \(a\to\infty\) and \(a(\log a)^3=o(b)\), the transformed level \(J_{a,b}^2/(2ab)-\log(a-1)\) is asymptotically standard Gumbel.
