# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The inner-derivation reduction and formula \(\operatorname{ad}_A^2=\operatorname{ad}_{A^2}\) are direct matrix identities in characteristic two. The lemma that commuting idempotent inner derivations have *commuting*, not merely scalar-commuting, implementers follows since an element of the image of an idempotent cannot be a nonzero member of its kernel. The four conditional counts are checked by choosing a fixed root of \(t^2+t+c\), counting split projections or irreducible quadratic embeddings, and dividing by precisely \(q\) scalar implementations of the same derivation. The nonsplit centralizer case uses both embeddings of \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\), giving the correct \(n^2/2\) rank. Exhaustive small-field bivariate checks reproduce the theorem's coefficients, including the extension-field and balanced-rank cases. These finite checks are supplementary to the six-step proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)
Caenepeel–Dăscălescu–Năstăsescu's 2001 general descent classification and Diniz–Pellegrino's 2020 grading-isomorphism enumeration are close, substantial earlier frameworks; neither inspected text gives the labeled, bivariate finite-field rank polynomial. Khazal–Boboc–Dăscălescu's complete \(M_2\) grading classification, including its characteristic-two Klein-four case, is an essential predecessor and already covers low-dimensional grading *types*. The distinct claimed assertion is the all-dimensional, simultaneously rank-refined **labeled ordered-pair census**. Ayupov–Arzikulov's matrix-ring derivation theory supplies background but no such counts. The inspected characteristic-zero Bahturin–Zaicev abstract does not address characteristic two; a newer abstract assumes a cyclic-subgroup condition excluding the Klein four group. The complete published research record on complex quadratic inner-derivation resonance has a different scope. Searches did not locate a result that implies the entire bivariate formula; absence of a match does not establish uniqueness. A broader grading/orbit enumeration theorem may still imply the answer and is the main remaining priority risk.

## Value — PASS
An idempotent derivation gives a degree-zero/degree-one decomposition of an associative algebra; a commuting ordered pair specifies the joint eigenspace architecture of a \((C_2)^2\)-grading in characteristic two. Exact **joint** rank statistics are not recoverable from the single-derivation rank polynomial, since the conditional distributions depend essentially on split versus irreducible quadratic centralizers. The theorem supplies a natural full-dimensional arithmetic invariant for every finite full matrix algebra and isolates a genuine characteristic dependence; it is not a mere numerical table or a restatement of an equivalence-class classification.
