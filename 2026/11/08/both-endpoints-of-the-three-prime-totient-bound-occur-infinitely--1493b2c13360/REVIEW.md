## Correctness — PASS

The exact transfer identity counts admissible odd lifts and removes precisely two forbidden lifts per permitted base residue. The congruence modulo \(2pq\) forces every forbidden lift's parity to persist for all sufficiently large primes in that progression. Complete finite base-residue certificates give correction values \(-13\) and \(+11\), confirmed independently by direct gcd counting. Dirichlet supplies infinitely many prime witnesses. The proof's \(r>pq\) domain and the difference between finite computations and the infinitude theorem are explicit.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Both-sided sharpness of a natural error inequality in the first nontrivial three-distinct-prime case is a motivated structural question. Infinite, explicit extremal families rule out reducing either error constant uniformly on all three-prime supports. This is a meaningful endpoint phenomenon, not merely a finite table. No exhaustive classification or independent assessment is claimed.
