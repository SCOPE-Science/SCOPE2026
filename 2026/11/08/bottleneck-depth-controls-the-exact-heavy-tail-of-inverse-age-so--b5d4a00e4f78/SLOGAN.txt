For progressive threshold contagion with common inverse-age adoption rate \(a/(t+2)\), the exact late cascade-completion tail is \(t^{-a}\) times a graph-determined power of \(\log t\). That logarithmic power counts the longest feasible sequence of single-eligible-agent bottlenecks, and the completion-time moment of order \(r\) is finite exactly when \(r<a\); paths and stars realize sharply different bottleneck depths.
