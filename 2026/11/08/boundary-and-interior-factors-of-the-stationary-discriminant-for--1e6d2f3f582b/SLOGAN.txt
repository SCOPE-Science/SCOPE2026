For the ruling-plane curve of every rational normal scroll with block degrees at least two, the stationary-polynomial discriminant for unrestricted symmetric Euclidean-distance data has exactly \(m\) linear pole components and one geometrically irreducible interior component of degree \(3m-6\), where \(m\) is the degree of the least common denominator. A generic interior degeneration is one ordinary collision of two finite stationary roots.
