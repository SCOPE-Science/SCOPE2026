# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** Formula (1) bijects each output with a feasible zero-sum difference vector, and the exact second-moment formula counts simultaneous feasibility by coordinatewise lower bounds and stars-and-bars. The rare-boundary asymptotic follows from counting centers with exactly one deficient coordinate; the probability of two such coordinates is of order \(n^{-2}\). The ternary spectrum uses all possible patterns of coordinates smaller than two and the sole overlap of forbidden differences for two zero coordinates. Direct-distance checks, distinct from the difference-vector evaluator, passed on 4,451 test centers; the ternary tables and exact rational moments were also checked for 35 lengths. Finite tests are corroboration and not the proof.

**Originality — PASS against inspected material, with priority risk.** The closest paper (Kreindel–Essayag–Zabokritskiy, arXiv:2603.18322) already has individual ball sizes, maxima, means and the corresponding difference-vector representation; those are not new. Its full Sections 4 and 5 do not state second moments, concentration on specific boundary deficits, the conditional uniform layer law, or the six-bin all-length ternary distribution. The exact mean does not imply these second-order and frequency conclusions. Kovačević–Tan provide the underlying multiset metric and ideal difference sets. Prior work on ordered-word deletion-sphere variance concerns different ambient spaces. Searches did not find a dominating result; unindexed or implicit coverage remains a risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Center-volume nonhomogeneity is identified in the motivating work as the obstacle for volume-based multiset coding arguments. The theorem quantifies it with an exact general second moment and first-order dispersion law, including the unexpected equidistribution over distinct boundary-volume deficits. The complete ternary radius-two distribution is the natural smallest two-dimensional nontrivial multiset-ball census, useful for testing finite-length bounds. It is not offered as a new capacity or optimal-code-size theorem.

**Scope and risks.** All asymptotics keep the alphabet size and radius fixed, and uniformity is over multisets, not ordered words. Independent proof review and comprehensive historical-priority certification remain outstanding.
