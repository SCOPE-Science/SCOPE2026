For a uniform random permutation, conditioning on the length of the cycle containing \(1\) changes only the first individual descent probability: all later descent indicators still have mean \(1/2\), and the first has an exact four-regime law. Consequently the tagged-cycle length and total number of descents have positive covariance \((n^2-3n+4)/(4n(n-1))\to1/4\), even as their correlation decays like \(3n^{-3/2}\).
