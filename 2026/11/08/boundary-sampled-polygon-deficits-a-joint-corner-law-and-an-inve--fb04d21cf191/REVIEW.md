Pardon and Groeneboom draw samples from the interior rather than the boundary. ## Mathematical value — PASS
The result distinguishes two natural polygon approximation errors through an explicit joint non-Gaussian distribution, rather than only an expectation or a worst-vertex metric. More significantly, a single asymptotic area-deficit law determines the multiset of angular sines, and for triangles it becomes a full similarity invariant. The sharp equiangular mean extremum and exponentially accurate finite-sample expectation connect the limit to usable geometric shape comparison.

## Scope and limitations
Fixed positive-edge-length polygons only, independent uniform arclength samples, and no joint polygon-order/sample-size limit. The scalar law determines unordered angle sines, not all geometry of polygons with four or more vertices. The comparison does not establish independent mathematical validation or absolute publication priority.
