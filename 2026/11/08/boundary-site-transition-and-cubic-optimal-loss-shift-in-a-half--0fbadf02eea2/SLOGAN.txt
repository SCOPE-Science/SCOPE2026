In a half-line quantum walk with a Lindblad boundary sink, eventual absorption falls strictly with the initial distance, and the strongest possible loss is optimal only in the boundary-site limit. At every other fixed site an optimal finite loss-to-hopping ratio exceeds one, while sufficiently distant sites have a unique optimum \(1+3/(4n^3)+O(n^{-4})\), quantifying the calibration shift beyond the known bulk duality.
