For random-order greedy independent sets, adding one leaf near the end of an increasingly long path has an exactly computable, oscillatory excess over an equal-size path: among fixed positive attachment depths, distance two uniquely minimizes the excess, at \((1-5e^{-2})/2\). When the branch point moves far from both ends, the excess instead tends to \((1-e^{-2})/4\), with factorially small error.
