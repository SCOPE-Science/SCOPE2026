For bounded-branching rooted trees, the normalized backward shifts can all be unitarily equivalent while the logarithmic singular-value means of their commutators with the same depth-decay rule have different behavior. An exact level-population criterion gives both a measurable weak-trace-class example and a nonmeasurable one, with identical exponential branching rate and sharp limiting coefficients, even when nonbinary branching occurs on a set of levels of density zero.
