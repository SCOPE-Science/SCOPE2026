# Scientific review: stationary risk law for a discontinuous hysteresis map

## Correctness — PASS

The proposition specifies a genuine zero-reset map, an open two-branch parameter domain, an explicit section measure and its physical-time lift. Branch identities, the second-stage reset condition, image tiling and the sign reversal are reconstructed directly; the pushforward law integrates to one, and parameter inversion is algebraic. The exact return-time coding telescopes to a strict unit bound on full-time reset-count discrepancy at every horizon, without irrationality. The irrational-return assertion uses an ergodic two-sheet rotation extension. Rational exact tests and an actual-time orbit check agree. No finite test is treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The closest primary work is Kryzhevich et al., arXiv:2104.11825, especially Theorem 3 and Section 8.3. It gives the rotation-return geometry and existence of a non-atomic invariant measure but not the exact original-time unit reset discrepancy, the physically time-weighted signed risk law and two-parameter identification. Arnold et al., arXiv:1605.00584, address a different continuous clipping map. Published research records, primary literature and accessible earlier mathematical claims were compared for aliases, equivalent formulations, stronger theorems and exact tables; no identical or dominating result was found. Standard return-tower induction remains prior mathematics and may reduce the incremental novelty. Unindexed equivalent work is an unresolved publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The risk variable is the directly meaningful observable of the model. Its distribution is singular at zero yet exactly log-uniform off zero; the physical-time reset fraction differs from the return-section fraction, and the count has a strict uniform unit discrepancy at every horizon. The same one-dimensional stationary law determines both map parameters, and the irrational case has an original-time orbit interpretation. This is a concrete quantitative characterization, not merely the already-known existence of a stationary measure.

## Scope and independence

The result concerns the explicitly constructed non-equilibrium invariant probability in the two-branch regime. It is not a global stationary-measure uniqueness theorem. Irrational return ratios are required for the typical-orbit conclusion; rational parameters can exhibit distinct periodic invariant laws. Independent external mathematical validation is not claimed.
