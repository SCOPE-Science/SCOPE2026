For the \(q\)-ary graph of words confusable from two distinct single-deletion reads, every triangle has a unique pivot and two adjacent alternating swap intervals joined at an equal-symbol boundary. This gives the exact triangle count \(q(q-1)\sum_{L=4}^{n}(n-L+1)(L-3)q^{n-L}\) for all \(q\ge2\) and \(n\ge1\).
