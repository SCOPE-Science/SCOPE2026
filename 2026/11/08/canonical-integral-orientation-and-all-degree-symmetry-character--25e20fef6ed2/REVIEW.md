## Correctness — PASS
The one-cochain assigning \(+1\) to sink-to-source flex edges is closed because the two flippable vertices of any square are nonadjacent, so each opposite-edge pair has the same source/sink status and cancels in the cellular boundary. Graph isomorphisms preserve the cochain exactly. Kozlov's Theorem 6.1 and Proposition 6.7 give a generating \(n\)-edge core circle of all positive sink flips in every noncontractible connected component; thus the period is exactly \(n\), canonically selecting an integral positive generator. Integral Künneth and the Eilenberg–Zilber graded sign prove the exterior-power automorphism action for products, including its boundary cases. Finally the cycle-factor determinant identity and rational finite-group transfer prove the quotient formula. The finite all-cube/symmetry/cocycle verifier passed; it is corroborative rather than an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
Full open text of Kozlov's 2026 paper was inspected at its definition, ordinary torus classification, core-cycle theorem and sink-flip construction. Its known circular cores imply ordinary homotopy, but the paper does not give a canonical natural integral cohomology generator or any \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\)-module and quotient representation formula. The closest published published-finding corpus complete-graph finding already describes the sink-flip positive orientation for the **special case \(K_n\)** and computes complete-graph rational characters; that special case is not claimed as new. The separate general-graph cycle-anchor finding computes reachability and distances, not homological characters or exterior signs. The new statement uniformly determines the integral all-degree automorphism representation for arbitrary finite graphs without isolated vertices and all subgroup-quotient rational Betti numbers. An unindexed all-graph equivariant treatment remains possible, and the novelty claim does not erase source theorems.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The integral orientation eliminates otherwise nontrivial sign ambiguity under graph symmetries simultaneously across all circular components. In disconnected graphs, permuting torus factors introduces degree-dependent Koszul signs: the exterior-power formula captures them without enumerating the enormous cubical complexes. The result supplies a structural theorem for every connected and disconnected finite graph in the domain, and an exact all-degree rational quotient formula for every subgroup, extending beyond ordinary homotopy ranks or a numerical special family. The component permutation must still be determined combinatorially.
