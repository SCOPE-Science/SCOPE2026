In the symmetric genus-\(17\) Humbert–Edge family with six branch values \(\{\infty,0,\pm1,\pm t\}\), the \(32\) points above \(0\) and \(\infty\) have canonical Weierstrass weight \(23\), except at the twelve distinct zeros of two explicit reciprocal polynomials, where it jumps exactly to \(27\). Exact canonical vanishing sequences and symbolic jet determinants establish the complete two-fiber classification.
