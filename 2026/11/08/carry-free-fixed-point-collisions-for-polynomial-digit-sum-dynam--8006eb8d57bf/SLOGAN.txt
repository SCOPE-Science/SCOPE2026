Every nonnegative-coefficient integer polynomial of degree at least two has infinitely many bases in which two distinct fixed points of its digit-sum dynamics occupy the same residue class modulo \(b-1\). For the square map, this obstruction occurs throughout the base classes \(b\equiv4,7\pmod9\), apart from the initial base \(4\), so at least \(2/9\) of bases fail the residue-class basin property.
