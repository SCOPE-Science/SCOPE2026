In the genus-\((p-1)/2\) smooth family \(y^2=x^{p+1}+a x^{(p+1)/2}+1\), for every prime \(p\ge11\), the geometric \(p\)-rank equals the Cartier rank at every parameter and is given by explicit odd coefficients of \((T^2+aT+1)^{(p-1)/2}\). The superspecial fiber \(a=0\) is the unique zero-rank fiber, while all nonzero smooth parameters have rank at least two and generic members are ordinary.
