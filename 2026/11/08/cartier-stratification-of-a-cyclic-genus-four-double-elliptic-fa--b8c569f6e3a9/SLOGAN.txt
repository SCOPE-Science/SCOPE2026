For the smooth cyclic double-elliptic genus-four family \(y^2z=x^3+z^3\), \(w^2=x(y+tz)\), the complete Jacobian and three-dimensional Prym \(p\)-rank and \(a\)-number strata are explicit in characteristics five and seven. In characteristic seven the Prym is superspecial precisely at \(t=0\), even though its elliptic quotient is ordinary and its genus-four Jacobian is not superspecial.
