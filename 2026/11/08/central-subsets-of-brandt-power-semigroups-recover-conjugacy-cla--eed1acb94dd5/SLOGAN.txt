For a finite Brandt semigroup over a group \(G\), the center of its nonempty-subset power semigroup consists exactly of diagonal copies of conjugacy-invariant group subsets, optionally containing zero. Its cardinality is \(2^{k(G)+1}-1\); for a fixed group, the center multiplication and idempotent count distinguish one matrix index from multiple indices, while the idempotent count recovers the number of normal subgroups once the index regime is known.
