For principal binary Walsh matrices indexed by a fixed-weight Johnson slice, exactly half the eigenvalues vanish in dimension \(2k\), while one and two extra coordinates both restore invertibility. The sharp condition number grows like \(\sqrt{\pi k/2}\) in dimension \(2k+1\), but like \(\sqrt\pi\,k^{3/2}/(2\sqrt2)\) in dimension \(2k+2\), with exact formulas governed by central binomial and Catalan numbers.
