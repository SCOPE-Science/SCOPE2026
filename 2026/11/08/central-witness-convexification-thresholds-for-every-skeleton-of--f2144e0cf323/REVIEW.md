# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The precise domain is a product of nondegenerate simplices, a closed face skeleton, and a nonnegative homothetic polytope convexifier. In each factor a point of the dilated product has a nonnegative barycentric capacity vector totaling \(1+t\). A supported unit barycentric row exists exactly when the selected-coordinate capacities add to at least one; the independent greedy construction proves sufficiency. The sum of the largest \(s\) entries is always at least \(s\) times the mean and is equal to the mean bound at the centroid. This proves both universal sufficiency and the center obstruction with exact weak inequalities at breakpoints. The optimized finite rational formula follows directly from these blockwise inequalities. The \(t=0\), \(k=0\), and facet-skeleton endpoints are addressed explicitly; an exact rational verifier supplements, but does not prove, the general theorem.
