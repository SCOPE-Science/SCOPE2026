For perfect evolution algebras over any finite field, the exact labeled distribution of centroid dimensions and indecomposable-ideal sizes is determined by a recursion involving counts of invertible matrices with connected support. As the dimension grows over a fixed field, the probability of a decomposable algebra is asymptotic to \(n(q-1)/(q^{n-1}(q^n-1))\), with singleton summands accounting for the leading term.
