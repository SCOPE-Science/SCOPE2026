The contribution is the explicit neutral-voter-diffusion hypergeometric transform together with a fully specified, certified local threshold and model-specific coefficient. The 2012 Wright–Fisher study treats the no-reset process. The accessible full scientific material and current research-record search returned no statement implying these exact constants for this reset problem. Possible unindexed diffusion/reset evaluations remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The concrete threshold explains which opinion imbalances make rare exogenous shocks locally beneficial for time to consensus and establishes an exact first-order onset coefficient in the natural population-diffusion scaling. The result addresses an explicitly motivated open quantitative calculation rather than merely renaming a general restart lemma, and a self-contained rational proof certificate makes its delicate local nondegeneracy reproducible.

## Limitations

The published 2019 generic mechanism and classical restart identity are not novel. The current result is local (not global) in reset intensity, confined to the diffusion limit and complete-graph symmetry, and neither resolves uniqueness outside the certified bracket nor extends to arbitrary networks.
