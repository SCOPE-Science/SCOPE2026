For unscaled finite-field Wishart walks \(S_{n,t}=\sum_{j=1}^t v_jv_j^{\mathsf T}\), an exact Fourier calculation gives distinct odd- and even-characteristic chi-square profiles throughout the bounded window around \(t=n\). In every fixed field, the walk has total-variation cutoff at \(n\), while its full finite-dimensional chi-square divergence is an explicit rank sum.
