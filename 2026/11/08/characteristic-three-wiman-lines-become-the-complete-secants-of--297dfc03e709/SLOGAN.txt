Under the explicit characteristic-three specialization of the Wiman arrangement, its 45 lines become precisely all secants of the ten-point conic \(x^2+y^2+z^2=0\) over \(\mathbb F_9\). Their crossings fill the entire rational projective plane, and the descended line-complement has no points over \(\mathbb F_3\) or \(\mathbb F_9\), with an explicit zeta function.
