# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** At each dense-torus point the image of the degree-two Hasse jet map is explicitly spanned by translated monomials; independent linear and pure-square coefficients give the universal lower bound, and the identity \(h_{ij}-g_i-f_j=-1+2u_iu_j\) gives the precise characteristic split. Linear independence of all translated degree-at-most-three monomials proves the order-three rank. In characteristic two the exact second fundamental system consists of independent coordinate squares, whose function-field extension has purely inseparable degree \(2^{n-1}\). Finite exact arithmetic confirms all claimed ranks in several characteristics. The proof is independent of factorials and valid over arbitrary fields.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to historical-priority risk.** The monomial toric family and its characteristic-zero one-defect osculation are not new (Mezzetti–Miró-Roig–Ottaviani, arXiv:1110.5239, Section 4). Michałek–Miró-Roig (arXiv:1310.2529) studies smooth monomial Togliatti systems in characteristic zero. The specific all-dimension characteristic-two rank collapse and its Frobenius second fundamental map are not implied by the stated characteristic-zero theorems. Perkinson's 1996 complex toric principal-parts results supply a framework but not this positive-characteristic conclusion. The full 2000 Perkinson inflection paper was not inspectable, so an unrecognized historical overlap remains possible; the claim of originality is best-knowledge rather than absolute priority. A search of relevant mathematical research records did not identify an equivalent statement.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The family is the prototypical smooth monomial Togliatti system for an unbounded range of dimensions. Its previously known single Laplace defect becomes an explicitly growing defect in characteristic two, and the pure-square second fundamental system has a concrete inseparability degree. The distinction between Hasse second jets and vanishing ordinary second derivatives is geometrically meaningful in positive characteristic. The result supplies exact jet ranks and the first nondefective higher jet for every dimension, not an arbitrary one-off calculation.

## Scope and residual risks
Only smooth dense-torus points are treated. The full inflection stratification at the boundary and historical completeness of the comparison with Perkinson (2000) remain open. No independent verification has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
