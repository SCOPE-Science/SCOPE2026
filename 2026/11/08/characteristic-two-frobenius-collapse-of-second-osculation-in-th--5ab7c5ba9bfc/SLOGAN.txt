For the classical truncated-simplex cubic toric family, characteristic two collapses the generic second Hasse-osculating dimension from \(2n+\binom n2-1\) to \(2n\), creating \(\binom n2\) independent Laplace defects. Its quadratic second fundamental map becomes a purely inseparable Frobenius map of degree \(2^{n-1}\), even though every third osculating space still fills the ambient projective space.
