# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A derivation of the free class-two Lie algebra is uniquely determined by a generator-endomorphism block and a central-shear block. The induced central block acts by the scalars \(2,1,0\) on the exterior square of the image, mixed exterior factors, and the exterior square of the kernel of the generator projection. Idempotency then imposes sharply different conditions in odd and even characteristics. The coefficient counts are exact Gaussian projection counts times free central-shear parameters; the rank computation and maximum-rank support follow. The explicit exponent gap yields fixed-field concentration. The independent enumeration in `verify.py` confirms four exhaustive cases including the first rank-four characteristic-two example.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected records and literature.** Benito–Roldán-López (2019) describe derivations of free nilpotent Lie algebras through their generator maps, but do not enumerate idempotent derivations or a finite-field characteristic-two rank polynomial. La Rosa–Mancini (2023) treat a different derivation-algebra classification problem, in a setting excluding characteristic two. Eick–Moede (2022) study gradings of small simple Lie algebras. The closest accessible research records count idempotent *endomorphisms* of free class-two Lie algebras and characteristic-sensitive idempotent derivations of *different* Lie families; neither implies these counts. Searches for equivalent formulations, tables, and broader coverage found no direct rank distribution. Publication priority remains provisional and is not established by the absence of search hits.

**Value — PASS.** Idempotent derivations encode two-eigenspace linear structures compatible with a Lie bracket, so the possible image dimensions and their multiplicities are natural invariants. The theorem exhibits a structural characteristic boundary: odd-characteristic maximal rank is linear in \(n\), but characteristic two permits ranks quadratic in \(n\), while an exponentially dominant family remains rank \(n\). The full rank polynomial over an infinite family is more than a small or arbitrary finite census. This is not an assertion about all two-step nilpotent Lie algebras.

Limits: Prime-field exhaustive tests are finite evidence; general finite-field correctness relies on the proof. The motivating 2019 derivation paper assumes characteristic zero, so finite-field extension of its structural observation is independently proved here. Unlocated broader classification results could affect originality. No independent audit was performed.
