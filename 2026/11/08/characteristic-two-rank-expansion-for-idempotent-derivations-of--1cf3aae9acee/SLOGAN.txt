In the free class-two nilpotent Lie algebra on \(n\) generators, idempotent derivations have only ranks \(0,n\) in odd characteristic, but in characteristic two attain every rank \(r(n-r+1)\), reaching \(\lfloor(n+1)^2/4\rfloor\). Exact finite-field multiplicities and a sharp concentration theorem show that nearly all characteristic-two idempotent derivations nevertheless have rank \(n\).
