In characteristic two, weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on the local algebra \(k\oplus V\) with \(V^2=0\) can have nilpotence index three, unlike the characteristic-zero case. They admit exact finite-field counts by square-zero matrix rank and exactly \(2m\) or \(2m+1\) conjugacy classes, according to the parity of \(m=\dim V\).
