For every even screened cycle, the alternating checkerboard is the unique (up to color exchange) minimum-variance partition among all two-color Rademacher trace-probing schemes, regardless of the screening strength. Its exact variance is strictly smaller than that of two independent full Hutchinson probes at the same two-solve cost, and the large-cycle variance ratio is \(2r^2/(1+r^2)\), with \(r+r^{-1}=\mu+2\).
