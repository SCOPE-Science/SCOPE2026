For independent uniformly oriented chiral active Brownian particles, no two-time particle-count covariance can reveal clockwise versus counterclockwise propulsion, regardless of observation-window geometry. A signed three-time count-cumulant moment equals \(v^2\operatorname{Im}[(1-e^{-(D_r-i\omega)t})(1-e^{-(D_r-i\omega)s})/(D_r-i\omega)^2]\), and together with pair counts uniquely determines propulsion speed, both diffusivities, and signed angular drift without tracking individuals.
