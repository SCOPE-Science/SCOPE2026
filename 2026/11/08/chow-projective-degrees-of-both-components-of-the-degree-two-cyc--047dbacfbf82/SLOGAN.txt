The two components of the reduced complex degree-two Chow variety have explicit, computable Chow-projective degrees for every \(n\ge3\) and \(1\le p\le n-2\). Their dimensions cross at \(2n=3p+3\), where both degrees contribute: the previously known \((p,n)=(1,3)\) case gives \(232\), while the higher equal-dimensional case \((3,6)\) gives \(295115130128\).
