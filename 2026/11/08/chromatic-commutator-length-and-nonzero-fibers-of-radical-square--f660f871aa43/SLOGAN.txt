For radical-square-zero algebras of finite loopless quivers over \(\mathbb F_q\), the minimal number of additive commutators needed for a radical target is exactly \(\lceil\log_{q^2}\chi(G[S])\rceil\), where \(S\) is its nonzero arrow support. Nonzero commutator fibers admit an exact contracted-graph chromatic-polynomial formula, and over \(\mathbb F_2\) the complete five-vertex quiver has exactly one radical target requiring two commutators.
