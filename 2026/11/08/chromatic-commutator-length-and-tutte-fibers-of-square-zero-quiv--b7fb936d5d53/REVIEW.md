# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The coordinate formula for the associative additive commutator is derived directly from the square-zero multiplication rule. Each supported edge requires a distinct endpoint label in the \(2\ell\)-component diagonal-vector coloring, and a proper coloring conversely provides arrow coefficients solving every edge independently. The empty-support and edgeless cases yield zero length. The complete fiber formula distinguishes equal from unequal endpoint labels and expands as a known multivariate Tutte coloring identity. Its large-field exponent follows from positive leading terms and the classical forest-partition criterion. Three scripts enumerate direct products/commutators over prime and extension fields and check the formulas, but finite cases are not substituted for proof.

## Value — PASS

Commutator width and full commutator fiber populations are natural associative-algebra invariants. The results give exact nontrivial obstructions from chromatic number, unbounded widths even in simple quiver families, and an arboricity transition in large-field commuting probability. They are structural statements for all loopless quivers, not an arbitrary small census.
