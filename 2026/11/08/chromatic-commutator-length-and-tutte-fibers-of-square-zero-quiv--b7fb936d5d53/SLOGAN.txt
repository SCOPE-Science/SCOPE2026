For every finite loopless quiver with square-zero radical over \(\mathbb F_q\), the minimum number of additive commutators needed to express a radical element is exactly \(\lceil\log_{q^2}\chi(G_S)\rceil\), where \(G_S\) is the support graph of that element. All commutator fibers have an exact chromatic-polynomial formula, and the large-field commuting probability detects whether the underlying graph decomposes into two forests.
