For a disk Bergman weight jumping by \(a^{-m}\) inside \(|z|^2<a\), every fixed scalar radius \(a<r<1\) eventually encloses exactly \(m-1\) kernel zeros, and these bulk zeros equidistribute on \(|s|=a\). A bilateral-product limit, argument-principle winding and subharmonic zero measures give the exact count and condensation.
