For rooted phylogenetic-tree clade queries, every binary labeling of a support is realizable exactly when its proposed clades are laminar. This characterizes the entire shattering complex, identifies its exact face coefficients with singleton-free partition counts, and sharpens the previously known unconditional capacity to the exact conditional VC and Littlestone value \(n-2-|H|\) after any compatible positive history \(H\).
