For every nonempty finite simple graph with \(\chi(G)=\omega(G)=k\), its remanent signed-walk language consists exactly of words avoiding \(+^k\) and \(-^k\), and its minimum complete DFA has \(2k\) states. Thus every nonempty perfect graph has a remanent language and exact length enumerator depending only on its chromatic number, regardless of its size.
