Adding a clique of \(t\) universal vertices raises the preference-stable chromatic number of *every* nonempty graph by exactly \(t\): \(\chi_{\mathrm{stable}}(K_t\vee G)=t+\chi_{\mathrm{stable}}(G)\). The proof lifts any obstructing preference profile across one new universal vertex, so clique coning also preserves the excess over ordinary chromatic number.
