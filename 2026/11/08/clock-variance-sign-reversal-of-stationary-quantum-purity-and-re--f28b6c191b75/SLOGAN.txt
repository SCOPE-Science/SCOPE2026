For a time-independent finite-dimensional Hamiltonian repeatedly reset on a two-stage Coxian renewal clock, stationary purity lies above or below overlap with the reset state exactly according as the clock's squared coefficient of variation is below or above one, provided the reset state does not commute with the Hamiltonian. The crossover is sharply classified by \(q=1-b/a\) when \(a>b\), and its strongest balanced-qubit witness occurs at energy gap \(\sqrt{ab}\).
