# Review: closed Johnson-three ball shattering

**Correctness — PASS.** The inclusive closed-ball predicate is intersection size at least two, not open adjacency. The five-support obstruction applies to all ambient orders, since any pair of shattered queries would need eight distinct jointly positive centers, forcing every pair of triple queries to share two symbols; the resulting five-member star cannot realize exactly three positives. Four-support classification reduces to the 66 unlabeled 3-uniform hypergraphs on four edges, exhaustively generated by `check.py` using a complete vertex-incidence invariant. Every possible center trace uses at most three symbols outside the twelve in the queries, so the 15-symbol test is exhaustive for every ambient order, not just a finite sample. Exactly one support type is shattered, with seven distinct symbols. The formula counts this type using its unique central vertex and the two repeated partners, and low orders have independently checked boundary witnesses. The certificate was executed and rechecked against the claims in the result.

**Originality — PASS within accessible comparisons.** Adler–Benediktsson–Macpherson (arXiv:2007.16042v1) studies the Johnson *open* neighborhood edge relation and bounds the *closed* convention, but its inspected arguments do not classify all four-query inclusive-closed shattered supports or count their unique orbit. Coudert–Csikós–Ducoffe–Viennot (arXiv:2405.07588v1) develops general closed-neighborhood dimension computation, not this structural formula. An earlier finite closed-neighborhood result at \(J(6,3)\) shows dimension three there; it cannot establish the all-\(n\) orbit classification or the exact coefficients for \(n\ge7\). Related Johnson-scheme and neighborhood-homology results study different invariants. An unlocated equivalent result under radius-one Johnson-ball trace terminology remains a genuine priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** This identifies the first ambient order at which the maximum possible four-query stratum appears and gives a full structural classification and exact count, rather than only a bounded dimension. The seven-symbol obstruction supplies a geometric restriction on query intersections, and the orbit count provides a reproducible combinatorial benchmark for learning on constant-weight slices. Neither finite data alone nor a general VC dimension bound would imply this coefficientwise law.

**Scope and risk.** No online dimension, higher Johnson rank, or complete sub-four support polynomial is asserted. The finite certificate is proof-critical, so an independent audit should reproduce or replace the 66-type exhaustive lemma. Historical priority is not certified.
