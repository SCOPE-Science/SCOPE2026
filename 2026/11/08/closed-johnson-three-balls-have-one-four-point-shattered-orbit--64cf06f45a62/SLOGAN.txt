For inclusive radius-one neighborhoods of the Johnson graph on triples, every VC-shattered four-query support has the same seven-symbol structure, and the exact number of such supports is \(n\binom{n-1}{2}\binom{n-3}{2}\binom{n-5}{2}\) for \(n\ge7\). The exact VC dimension is \(0,0,2,3\) for \(n=3,4,5,6\), respectively, and \(4\) thereafter.
