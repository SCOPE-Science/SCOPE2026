For every cycle-of-cliques graph \(C_n[K_m]\), \(n\ge3\) and \(m\ge2\), an initial set zero forces exactly when it leaves at most one white vertex per clique and has two completely blue cliques within cyclic distance two. This yields a closed zero-forcing polynomial by subtracting the square-cycle independence polynomial and proves every inclusion-minimal forcing set is minimum, with exactly \(|E(C_n^2)|m^{n-2}\) minimum sets.
