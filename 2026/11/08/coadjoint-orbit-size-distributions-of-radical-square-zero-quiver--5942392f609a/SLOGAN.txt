For every finite radical-square-zero quiver algebra \(A=\mathbb F_qQ/J^2\), the full unit group's coadjoint orbits on the commutator Lie dual have sizes \([q(q-1)]^j\), and their exact size enumerator is \(q^c z^{n-c}T_\Gamma(1+q/z,q)\), where \(\Gamma\) is the underlying multigraph and \(c\) its component count. In particular, the total number of coadjoint orbits is \(q^cT_\Gamma(q+1,q)\), independent of arrow orientations and valid with loops and parallel arrows.
