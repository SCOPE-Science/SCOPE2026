In calibrated ecological presence–absence surveys approaching near-universal occupancy, keeping only an “ever detected” flag has a sharp detection-quality transition: optimal repeat visits stay bounded for \(p\le2f-f^2\) but grow logarithmically for \(p>2f-f^2\). Keeping the complete visit history instead makes optimal replication diverge for every \(0<f<p<1\); the one-bit design also has an exact integer-phase asymptotic.
