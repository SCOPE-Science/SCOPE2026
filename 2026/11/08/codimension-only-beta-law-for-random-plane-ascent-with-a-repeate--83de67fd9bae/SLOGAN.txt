For exact random-plane Rayleigh–Ritz ascent on a real two-level matrix with a repeated largest eigenvalue, the small-error contraction multiplier converges to \(\operatorname{Beta}((m-1)/2,1/2)\), where \(m\ge2\) is only the codimension of the top eigenspace. Every fixed finite collection of successive multipliers becomes asymptotically independent, yielding the sharp mean squared-error factor \(((m-1)/m)^k\) after \(k\) steps.
