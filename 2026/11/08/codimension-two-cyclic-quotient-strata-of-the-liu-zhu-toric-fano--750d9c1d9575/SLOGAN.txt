For every \(n\ge3\), the codimension-two singular components of the Liu–Zhu K-polystable toric Fano variety are exactly \(\binom{n+1}{2}\) orbit closures, each with transverse cyclic quotient singularity \(\frac1{4n}(1,2n-1)\). Every such quotient has canonical index \(2\), transverse minimal log discrepancy \(1/2\), and resolution chain \([3,2,\ldots,2,3]\) with \(n-2\) interior twos.
