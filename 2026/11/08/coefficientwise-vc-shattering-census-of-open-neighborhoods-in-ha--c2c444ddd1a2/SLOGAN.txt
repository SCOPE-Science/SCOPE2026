The open-neighborhood concepts of every q-ary Hamming graph have a complete VC-shattering polynomial of degree at most three, with exact coefficients counting all shattered pairs and triples. The cubic term splits into three explicit geometric types with different sharp parameter thresholds; for example, \(H(2,4)\) has exactly \(320\) shattered triples, while \(H(3,3)\) has \(216\).
