For two equal, widely separated Gaussian time-frequency localization disks of fixed area \(R\), the two largest eigenvalues split by \(2(1-e^{-R})e^{-\pi d^2/2}(1+o(1))\). Their optimizing signals are asymptotically the even and odd combinations of the two local coherent states, while all remaining eigenvalues stay below a fixed spectral gap.
