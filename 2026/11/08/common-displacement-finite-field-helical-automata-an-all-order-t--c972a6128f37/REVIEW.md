# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof constructs the quotient \(V/\{0,d\}\) and shows local interaction indicators never change under allowed translations. The exact global update is consequently a fixed translation of order at most two. The fixed-state count follows by independent site choices conditioned on the carrier; all remaining states are two-cycles. The functional equation and rule-count enumeration are proved symbolically. The standard-library checker independently passed 1,686,656 exact finite checks for three field orders. Finite testing is corroboration, not a substitute for the all-order proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
Araoka–Tokihiro (2026) define the model and prove period results for fields of orders four and eight, including single-transposition and additive-rule cases. Their broader claim is still stated as a conjecture. The present result covers all orders and every mask of cosets with the same displacement, produces the exact periodic-orbit census, and counts all such rules. A public affine helical cycle census is closer than generic Yang–Baxter work but cannot cover the nonaffine single-transposition examples for larger fields. Gombor–Pozsgay (2022) use a different brickwork global update. Specialized older constructions remain a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem extracts an explicit all-order solvable stratum of a recent open dynamics problem, with exact observable periodic-orbit counts and a closed zeta function. The family includes nonlinear rules satisfying the published functional identity and thus is not merely the already-classified affine slice. The exact census and map count describe the size and dynamics of the entire naturally defined common-displacement class.

## Closest literature and limits
The 2026 work already contains small-field special cases and the local functional equation; neither is claimed as novel. The result does not settle the conjecture for rules with several distinct nonzero displacements. The site-and-carrier update is the precise object counted, rather than the site projection. Earlier specialized results could alter historical priority. Independent validation has not occurred.
