Every helical automaton derived from a bijection of a characteristic-two finite field whose nonzero displacements all equal one fixed amount has an exactly involutive full site-and-carrier dynamics, even when the rule is nonaffine. For field order \(q\), support size \(s\), and \(L\) sites, there are exactly \(q(q-s)^L\) fixed states and \((q^{L+1}-q(q-s)^L)/2\) two-cycles, for a complete family of \(1+(q-1)(2^{q/2}-1)\) distinct rules.
