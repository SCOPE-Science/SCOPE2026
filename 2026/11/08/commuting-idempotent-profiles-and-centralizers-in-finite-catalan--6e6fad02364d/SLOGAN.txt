Commuting pairs of idempotents in the finite Catalan transformation monoid admit a unique two-color gap decomposition, yielding an exact trivariate fixed-point enumerator \(F=B/(1-wB)\) and explicit centralizer counts for every idempotent. In particular, their commutation probability is asymptotic to \(\sqrt2((2+\sqrt2)/4)^n\), although the unrefined count sequence is already recorded in OEIS under other interpretations.
