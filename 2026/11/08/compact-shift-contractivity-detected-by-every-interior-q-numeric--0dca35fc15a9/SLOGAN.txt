For every compact perturbation \(S+K\) of the unilateral shift, each interior \(q\)-numerical radius detects contractivity exactly: \(w_q(S+K)=1\) if and only if \(\|S+K\|\le1\), for every \(0<q<1\). At \(q=1\), a one-edge shift can have norm strictly above one while its ordinary numerical radius remains one, up to the sharp weight threshold \(\sqrt2\).
