# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** Each compact Kraus tuple induces norm limits of finite-rank superoperators on the operator-norm Banach space. The finite-dimensional adjoint relation yields conjugate nonzero spectra with matching algebraic multiplicities, which persist under compact-operator norm convergence through Riesz projection perturbation. Positivity gives the spectral-radius identities and compact Perron eigenoperators. Length-m compact words yield a compact tuple with completely positive maps equal to the m-th powers. The full Fock-space example is explicit. The 1,584 numerical tests are supportive finite diagnostics, not an infinite-dimensional proof.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** Scherer–Shalit–Shamovich (2026), Example 5.2 and Remark 5.3, already prove the finite-matrix row/column radius identity and give the full Fock counterexample. The narrower present contribution is the conjugate nonzero spectral-multiset theorem for compact Kraus tuples and its compact-word/Perron consequences. Tornes (2026) studies distinguishing arbitrary operator-space structures and does not provide this compactness classification. Searches for compact Kraus, compact-word row/column radii, and equivalent completely positive map formulations identified no directly covering result. The main residual risk is a general theorem about spectra of elementary maps with compact coefficients that subsumes the classification without these terms.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result bridges a precise finite-dimensional equality and a known infinite-dimensional separation by a structurally natural compact-word hypothesis, and strengthens equality to the full nonzero spectral multiset plus positive compact eigenoperators. It supplies a finite-section approximation principle even though ordinary spectral radius need not be norm-continuous on general bounded-operator families. No assertion is made for arbitrary operator-space quantizations or for zero-radius positive eigenvectors.
