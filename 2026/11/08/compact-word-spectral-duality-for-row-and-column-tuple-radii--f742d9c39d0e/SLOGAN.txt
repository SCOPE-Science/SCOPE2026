For a finite tuple of compact Hilbert-space operators, the associated row and column completely positive maps have conjugate nonzero spectra with matching algebraic multiplicities, and their joint radii coincide with positive compact eigenoperators at every nonzero radius. The radius equality persists even when only all operator words of one fixed length are compact, whereas the familiar Fock-space isometries show that unrestricted infinite-dimensional tuples can separate the radii.
