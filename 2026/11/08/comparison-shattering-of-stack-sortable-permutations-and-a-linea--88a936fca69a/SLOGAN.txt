For permutations avoiding 231, exactly those sets of positional pair-comparison queries with at most one edge from each smaller position are VC-shattered. These supports form a partition matroid, with complete enumerator ∏_{j=1}^{n−1}(1+jz), Stirling-number coefficients, and (n−1)! facets. Previously established finite online dimensions and their direct-sum corollaries are not claimed as new.
