The complement involution on the intrinsic radius-two Čech complex of the twenty three-subsets of a six-set acts freely, with quotient fundamental group \(\mathbb Z/2\) and rational reduced Betti numbers \(\widetilde\beta_3=6\), \(\widetilde\beta_4=15\). On the original complex the involution has rational homology traces \(+1\) in degree three and \(0\) in degree four.
