In every three-coordinate chainlink polytope with \(2\ell\leq\min_i a_i\), complementary coordinate-sum sections have identical Euclidean perimeters at **all real heights**, and even their coordinate-facet and difference-facet edge-length totals agree separately. Explicit piecewise-linear length profiles prove the equality despite noncongruent complementary hexagons; the general statement fails beyond the half-size link threshold.
