Among all integral two-parameter Raney sequences \(a_n=\frac{R}{Pn+R}\binom{Pn+R}{n}\), with \(P\geq1\) and \(R\geq1\), only the constant sequence at \((P,R)=(1,1)\) is almost realizable as periodic-point counts. The off-diagonal obstruction occurs at prime indices, while the entire diagonal \(R=P\geq2\) is ruled out at twice-prime indices.
