On a complete bipartite conflict graph \(K_{a,b}\), every maximal EF1 schedule of unit-cost chores either assigns all chores or discards precisely one entire bipartition side; all such labeled schedules admit exact counting formulas. The best maximal EF1 schedule always assigns at least half the chores, and the bound is asymptotically sharp already with four agents on \(K_{2h-1,2h+1}\).
