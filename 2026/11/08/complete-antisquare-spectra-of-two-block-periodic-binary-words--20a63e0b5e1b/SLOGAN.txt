For the two-run periodic binary word \((0^a1^b)^\omega\), the complete distinct-antisquare spectrum depends only on \(s=\min(a,b)\) and \(t=\max(a,b)\): an unequal pair has exactly \(3s-1+\max(0,2s-t+1)\) antisquares, while an equal pair has infinitely many orders spaced by \(2s\). The phase transition and every order-specific count follow from the geometry of two translated circular run intervals.
