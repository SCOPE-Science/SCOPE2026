On every regular Euclidean simplex of dimension at least three, repeatedly project a point in one facet to its farthest other facet, fixing ties: there are exactly \(d!\) attracting prime-\((d+1)\) cycles, all with equally spaced barycentric coordinates and equally sized area basins. Every strictly ranked initial point has a cyclic facet itinerary from the start, while tied coordinates cause finite-time arrest at a fixed point.
