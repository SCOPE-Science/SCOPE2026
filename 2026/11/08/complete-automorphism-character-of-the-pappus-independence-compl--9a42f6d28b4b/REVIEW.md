# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns the exact thirteen-class character of a specific 216-element automorphism group. The graph comes from the standard eighteen-vertex LCF description, with 27 edges. An exhaustive enumeration produces 2,597 nonempty independent faces. An explicitly defined 1,293-pair matching is verified acyclic over all 11,430 nontrivial face-poset covers and leaves one critical vertex and ten critical four-cells. Hence only reduced fourth homology is present, integrally, with rank ten. Complete adjacency-preserving permutation backtracking finds exactly 216 automorphisms; identity, inverse and closure are checked, as are the thirteen conjugation orbits. The reduced Lefschetz character formula \(\chi(g)=-I(Q_g,-1)\) is proved using cycle orientations, and computed for every automorphism. An independent direct signed-chain-trace program checks the thirteen representatives and independently computes the mod-two Betti vector. The group-average and norm statements follow by explicit class-weighted sums and character orthogonality. Finite verification is exhaustive for the fully specified finite object; no claim about an infinite graph family is made.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
A publicly indexed 2026 result, *Order-three symmetry on Pappus independence homology*, already states both the sphere-wedge homotopy equivalence and a single order-three subgroup character; neither is claimed new here. It expressly does not give the full automorphism-group representation. The new 216-element character, thirteen conjugacy classes, vanishing global invariants, and multiplicity-free two-constituent conclusion are not implied by a ten-dimensional Betti number or by the three-element subgroup character. Two distinct order-three classes with the same vertex-cycle type but different homology traces provide a concrete implication distinction. Relevant published graph-independence and incidence-graph literature was inspected: no matching full-character theorem was found. An unindexed or inaccessible equivalent source is a continuing priority risk, not evidence of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The Pappus graph is the canonical Levi graph of the Pappus configuration and has a sizeable, nontrivial symmetry group. Full equivariant homology describes information invisible to the known nonequivariant sphere wedge. A natural order-three subgroup had already been analyzed, making the full symmetry calculation motivated rather than an arbitrary slice. The character determines the absence of invariant rational four-cycles and a nontrivial multiplicity-free complex representation. The split between two order-three classes sharing cycle type demonstrates that coarse permutation data cannot replace the equivariant topological calculation. This exact classification is a useful finite benchmark for topological representations of incidence configurations without purporting to solve a larger family.

## Limitations and nearest literature
The result classifies one finite graph only, and does not identify the degrees of the irreducible constituents, integral representation lattices or equivariant homotopy types. The nearest result already computes the Pappus sphere-wedge and the period-six order-three subgroup trace. Przytycki–Silvero (2016), Tsukuda (2017) and Berghoff (2020/2022) concern broader independence-complex methods but do not state the full thirteen-class Pappus character. A fully independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
