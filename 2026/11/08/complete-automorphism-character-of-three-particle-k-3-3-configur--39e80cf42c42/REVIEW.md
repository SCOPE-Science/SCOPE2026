**Correctness — PASS.** The weight-three reduced Świątkowski complex has integral chain ranks \((165,540,540,160)\) and explicitly verified zero boundary compositions. Exact unit-pivot reductions give nonzero Smith invariant factors \(\partial_1:1^{164}\), \(\partial_2:1^{371},2\), and \(\partial_3:1^{160}\). All \(72\) graph automorphisms act by integral chain maps. Image traces are recovered over characteristic \(10007\), whose rank behavior is certified by the integral Smith factors; bounded integer traces lift uniquely to rational characteristic values. Nine class traces and the nine-irreducible character inner products produce the stated two representation decompositions. A separately generated subdivided Abrams cubical complex agrees on Betti numbers over two fields and all \(72\) Lefschetz numbers.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources.** The closest published study is the full-text Section 5.5 of *Non-abelian Quantum Statistics on Graphs* (2019): it already proves \(\beta_2=8\) for three unordered particles on precisely \(K_{3,3}\), and its higher-configuration results also cover larger particle counts. The rank and product cycles are not claimed as discoveries here. Ko–Park (2011) already establishes integral first homology. The new claim is a complete graph-automorphism representation in each positive rational homology degree; neither source states the character or the two four-dimensional irreducible summands. The general Świątkowski comparison theorem is a method, not this calculation; Lütgehetmann's stability treats a distinct action. Relevant published-finding corpus records concern different constructions on the same graph or different graphs. There is residual priority risk from unindexed equivalent computations.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** \(K_{3,3}\) is a fundamental Kuratowski obstruction to graph planarity with an unusually natural \(72\)-element automorphism group. The two inequivalent rational four-dimensional summands, the signed flip in degree one, and absence of globally invariant positive-degree homology are structural invariants not determined by known nonequivariant Betti numbers. This exact equivariant refinement is mathematically motivated independent of a general-family theorem; it is not a recomputation of an existing rank table.
