# Scientific review: Complete automorphism characters of the Möbius–Kantor independence complex

## Correctness — PASS

The result applies to the specified 16-vertex graph, not to an extrapolated family. Exact rational simplicial boundary ranks \((15,81,191,181,58,14,2)\) prove that reduced homology is nonzero only in degrees three and four, with ranks four and one. The independent symmetry checker enumerates 96 graph automorphisms and all 13 conjugacy classes, verifies a degree-four cohomological sign for each map over \(\mathbb F_{101}\), and evaluates exact signed simplicial Lefschetz traces. Rational and mod-101 degree-four dimensions coincide; hence the computed top sign identifies the rational top character. Independent source programs and outputs are included, with no unsupported infinite generalization.

The complete character is a natural group-topological invariant for a classical cubic symmetric graph. It detects the failure of global invariants and the two-constituent character norm of degree-three homology, information unavailable from ordinary Betti numbers and the already recorded cyclic restriction. This is a substantive finite exact character classification, not a choice of an arbitrary numerical slice.
