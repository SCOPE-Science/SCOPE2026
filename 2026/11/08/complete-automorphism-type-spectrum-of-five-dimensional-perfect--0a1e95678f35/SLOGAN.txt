Among five-dimensional perfect evolution algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\), exactly \(85{,}411\) isomorphism classes split into thirteen distinct abstract automorphism-group types. Exactly \(81{,}408\) classes are rigid, while the nonrigid classes include distinct \(C_4\), \(C_2\times C_2\), \(C_6\), and \(S_3\) symmetry strata; the complete distribution is established by two exhaustive orbit algorithms and an independent Burnside check.
