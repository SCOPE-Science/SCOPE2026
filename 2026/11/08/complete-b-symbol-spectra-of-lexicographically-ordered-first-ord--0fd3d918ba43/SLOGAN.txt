For lexicographically ordered binary first-order Reed–Muller codes of length \(n=2^m\), every cyclic \(b\)-symbol weight distribution is explicit: for \(b\ge2\), each positive weight below \(n\) occurs exactly four times, and the remaining nonzero codewords have weight \(n\). The key is that the complete cyclic zero-run multiset of an affine truth table depends only on its number of runs, whose attainable values have a two-to-one coefficient-vector distribution.
