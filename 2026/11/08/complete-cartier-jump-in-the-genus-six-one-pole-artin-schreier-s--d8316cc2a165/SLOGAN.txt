Every genus-six \(3\)-rank-zero Artin–Schreier curve in characteristic \(3\) has a model \(y^3-y=x^7+a x^5+b x^4+c x^2\): its Cartier ranks are \((6,3,1,0)\) when \(a\ne0\), and \((6,2,1,0)\) when \(a=0\). Thus the full natural normal-form family has precisely two \(a\)-number strata, \(3\) and \(4\), separated by one explicit coefficient hyperplane.
