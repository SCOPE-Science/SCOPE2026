The genus-three Dickson quotient \(Y^2=(X+2)D_7(X)\) has a complete characteristic-wise Cartier rank classification: ordinary reduction when \(p\equiv1\pmod{14}\), nilpotent rank sequences \((3,2,1,0)\) and \((3,1,0,0)\) in four nonmaximal residue classes, and zero Cartier at \(p\equiv13\pmod{14}\). The last zero-Cartier case was already implied by published maximality results; the new information lies in the full rank profile, particularly the distinct nonmaximal nilpotent types.
