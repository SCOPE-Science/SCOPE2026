The exhaustive search accounts for all \(40^2\cdot4^2=25{,}600\) RC-invariant equality candidates and independently counts maximal compatible families of 44 allowable RC-orbits by graph clique enumeration. Both programs agree exactly on all sixteen labeled maxima. Their direct assertions check the palindrome counts, five symmetry orbit sizes, 512 oriented selections, and distance spectra. All algorithmic inputs are the unmodified definitions, and the full solution list is included.

Searches for the exact census and equivalently formulated DNA/Hamming/GC/RC conditions in available published research records did not identify a covering claim. Aboluion–Smith–Perkins (2012) is a close plausible source but was available only at abstract level, creating residual priority risk. The finding is an exhaustive classification, not an asserted improvement in maximum code size.
