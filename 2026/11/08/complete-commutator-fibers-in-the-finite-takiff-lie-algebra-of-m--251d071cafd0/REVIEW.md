# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The trace-form and centralizer lemmas hold in every odd characteristic, including characteristic three and the nilpotent-adjoint cases. Every coefficient equation, unique-projective-line decomposition and solution multiplicity can be reconstructed from the proof. The finite-field checker fully reproduces all target fibers at \(q=3\) and checks representative fibers and all adjoint ranks at \(q=5\). A distinct checker directly multiplies all \(3^{12}\) ordered pairs over \(\mathbb F_3\), matching every target count. A finite test does not substitute for the proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)
The closest inspected archival papers are Panyushev (arXiv:math/0506579, first posted 2005-06-28), concerning invariant theory and quotient morphisms of Takiff algebras, and Greenstein--Mazorchuk (arXiv:1505.05471, first posted 2015-05-20), concerning Koszul duality. Their relevant full-text sections give neither this labeled bracket-fiber census nor a stronger theorem immediately implying it. Standard \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) centralizer facts and the Takiff construction are credited, and closest published comparisons address different algebras (Kronecker path, maximal-class filiform). Search absence does not prove uniqueness: a broader unidentified finite-field commutator-morphism theorem could subsume the result. An original Takiff paper and a later commuting-varieties paper were not exhaustively read.

## Value — PASS
The complete distribution of bracket fibers is a natural structural statistic, finer than the commuting probability alone. It identifies a sharp three-stratum effect of the abelian Takiff ideal, gives exact counts at every odd prime power, and determines the full adjoint-rank distribution. These are independent mathematical invariants of the first Takiff extension, not numerical tests chosen after seeing a result.

## Limits
The claim concerns the first Takiff extension of split \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) over odd finite fields. Neither extension to characteristic two/higher nilpotent order nor classification up to conjugacy is proved. No independent priority or correctness certification has been performed.
