Every commutator fiber of the six-dimensional Takiff Lie algebra of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb F_q)\), for odd \(q\), has one of exactly three explicit sizes, determined only by whether the constant bracket coefficient is nonzero, the constant coefficient vanishes but the nilpotent coefficient is nonzero, or the whole target vanishes. The complete formula gives the commuting probability \(q^{-4}+q^{-5}+q^{-6}-q^{-7}-q^{-8}\) and an exact three-rank adjoint census.
