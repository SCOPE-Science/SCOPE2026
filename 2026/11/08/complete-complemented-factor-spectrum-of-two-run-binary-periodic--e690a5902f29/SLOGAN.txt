For unequal positive run lengths \(a,b\), the complemented factors of \((0^a1^b)^\omega\) are exactly the unary words and one-transition words whose run lengths are at most \(m=\min(a,b)\); their complete length enumerator is \(2z(1-z^m)(1-z^{m+1})/(1-z)^2\), with exactly \(2m(m+1)\) distinct nonempty words. Equality \(a=b\) reverses the behavior sharply: every factor is complemented, and the complemented-factor language is infinite.
