For every connected girth-at-least-five base graph \(H\) and clique width \(q\ge3\), all connected mutually visible sets in \(H\square K_q\) lie in one fiber or in two adjacent fibers sharing exactly one color, yielding \(C_X(z)=|V(H)|((1+z)^q-1)+|E(H)|qz^2(1+2z)^{q-1}\). Every inclusion-maximal such set is maximum of size \(q+1\), and exactly \(|E(H)|q2^{q-1}\) maximum labelled sets occur.
