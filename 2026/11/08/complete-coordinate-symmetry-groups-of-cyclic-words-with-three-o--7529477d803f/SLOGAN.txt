For a cyclic word made of at least three distinct monochromatic runs, all coordinate symmetries of length-\(n\) factors, for \(n\) at least one period, are within-residue permutations, with an additional cyclic permutation of residue blocks **exactly** when \(n\) is divisible by the period. The full groups are wreath products \(S_m\wr C_p\) at \(n=mp\) and direct products \((S_{m+1})^r\times(S_m)^{p-r}\) at \(n=mp+r\), \(1\le r<p\).
