For every odd prime \(p\), the family of all single subgroup cosets in the dihedral group of order \(2p\) has shattered-support polynomial \(1+2pz+\binom{2p}{2}z^2+p^2(p-1)z^3\). Precisely the mixed 2-plus-1 triples are shattered, giving VC dimension three and a complete nonabelian coset-incidence benchmark.
