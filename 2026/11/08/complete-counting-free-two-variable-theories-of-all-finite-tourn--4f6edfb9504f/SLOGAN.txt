For nontransitive finite tournaments, counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{2}\) remembers exactly the lengths of the successively removable universal-winner and universal-loser chains, but nothing about the source-free, sink-free core; transitive tournaments are instead distinguished by their orders. Accordingly, \(n\)-vertex tournaments realize exactly \(1+\binom{n-1}{2}\) two-variable theories, with smallest nontransitive representatives formed by a directed triangle between two transitive chains.
