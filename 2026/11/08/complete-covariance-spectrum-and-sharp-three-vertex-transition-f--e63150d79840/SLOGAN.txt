Uniform intrinsic-volume covariance of the polar symmetric edge polytope of \(K_{p,q}\) has three exact eigenspaces, with eigenvalues \((p+1)(p+2)/(3n(n+1))\), \((q+1)(q+2)/(3n(n+1))\), and \((pq+2)/(3n(n+1))\) for \(n=p+q\). For \(2\le p\le q\), the least-variance direction switches sharply from the between-block mode to first-block contrasts at \(q=p+3\).
