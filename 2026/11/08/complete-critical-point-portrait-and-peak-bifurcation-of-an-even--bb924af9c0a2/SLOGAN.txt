For the Gaussian-weighted Fock intensity \(H_a(z)=e^{-|z|^2}|\cosh(az)|^2\), every critical point lies on a coordinate axis: simple imaginary-axis zeros are minima, a unique saddle separates each consecutive pair, and a sharp peak bifurcation occurs at \(a=1\). Above the threshold the central maximum becomes a saddle and exactly two nondegenerate global peaks emerge; the full classification holds at every separation.
