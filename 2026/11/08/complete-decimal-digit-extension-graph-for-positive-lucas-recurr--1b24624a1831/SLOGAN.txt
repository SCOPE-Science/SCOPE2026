The complete decimal right-digit-extension graph of \(U_{n+2}=aU_{n+1}+U_n\), \(a\ge2\), is determined for every coefficient: three-vertex walks occur exactly when \(a=3\) or \(a=10\), and no longer walk exists. A quadratic-norm bound gives a fully effective finite search for the graph; general Lucas-walk termination was already known.
