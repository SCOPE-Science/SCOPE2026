# Complete dihedral homology characters of the 25-vertex Schrijver independence complex

## Finding
For the graph \(SG_{4,2}\) of stable four-subsets of a cyclic ten-set, let \(X=\operatorname{Ind}(SG_{4,2})\) and let \(D_{10}=\langle r,s\mid r^{10}=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle\) act by \(r(i)=i+1\) and \(s(i)=-i\) on the ten labels. Then \(\widetilde H_3(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\), \(\widetilde H_4(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^2\), and all other reduced integral homology vanishes. Over \(\mathbb Q\), the degree-three module is the reflection-sign character \(\eta\) given by \(\eta(r)=1,\eta(s)=-1\). The degree-four module is \(\mathbf1\oplus\rho\), where \(\rho(r)=-1,\rho(s)=1\). Thus \(\chi_3(r^a)=1\), \(\chi_3(r^as)=-1\), \(\chi_4(r^a)=\chi_4(r^as)=1+(-1)^a\), for every \(0\le a<10\). In particular the degree-four homology contains exactly one globally dihedral-invariant rational line and degree-three homology contains none.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph has one vertex for every four-element subset of \(\mathbb Z/10\mathbb Z\) containing no cyclically consecutive labels. Two such vertices are adjacent precisely when the underlying subsets are disjoint. The independence complex consists of pairwise intersecting collections of these stable four-subsets. The acting dihedral group consists of the ten label rotations and ten reflections; the claim determines its rational character on each nonzero reduced homology degree. No statement about the full graph automorphism group is necessary.

## Proof
Lexicographically list the twenty-five stable four-subsets. Enumerate all independent sets by bitmask; there are exactly 19,907 including the empty face, with 60 edges in the graph. Sequentially pair every currently unpaired face \(F\) missing a prescribed vertex \(v\) with \(F\cup\{{v\}}\) when both are present. The prescribed order is supplied in `verify.py`. The directed Hasse graph, with reversed matched incidences, is acyclic: topological sorting visits all 19,907 faces. Precisely three critical nonempty cells remain: one of dimension three and two of dimension four. Consequently reduced integral homology vanishes outside degrees three and four, and any possible differential is from degree four to degree three.

To prove that differential vanishes integrally, use signed simplicial boundaries with increasing vertex order. Starting from the critical three-face with coefficient one, eliminate the boundaries of all two-faces matched upward to three-faces, obtaining an explicit sixteen-term integral three-cycle \(z\). Independently, eliminate the incidence columns of four-faces paired downward to three-faces. The resulting critical-three coefficient defines an integer-valued cocycle: the coefficient is zero on the boundary of **every** one of the 5,079 four-faces. Its value on \(z\) is one. Thus the degree-three integral class is primitive and nonzero, the possible Morse differential is zero, and the free homology ranks are one and two.

Under the two generators \(r\) and \(s\), the critical-three coefficient of the images of \(z\) is respectively \(+1\) and \(-1\). Since this coefficient descends to an integral homology functional with value one on \(z\), these signs are the actual degree-three homology actions.

For each of the twenty dihedral elements, compute the signed reduced simplicial Lefschetz trace \(L(g)=\sum_{F:\,gF=F}(-1)^{|F|-1}\operatorname{sgn}(g|_F)\), including the empty face with sign \(-1\). Direct complete enumeration gives \(L(r^a)=(-1)^a\) and \(L(r^as)=3\) for even \(a\), \(1\) for odd \(a\). Because only degrees three and four carry reduced rational homology, \(L=-\chi_3+\chi_4\). Substitution yields the two character formulas in the finding. Their character values identify the stated one-dimensional constituents uniquely over \(\mathbb Q\).

## Verification
The standalone `verify.py` uses only Python's standard library. It enumerates all simplices, checks the full directed Hasse graph for acyclicity, constructs the integral cycle and cocycle, verifies the cocycle on every four-face boundary, evaluates all twenty dihedral Lefschetz traces, and asserts the formulas. The recorded stdout is `verification_output.txt`. The finite exhaustive computations are the proof certificates for this explicitly fixed finite complex; no conclusion is inferred from bounded sampling.

## Relationship to prior work
Braun's *Independence Complexes of Stable Kneser Graphs* (first posted 2009-12-03, arXiv:0912.0720) treats related stable Kneser independence complexes and distinguishes the Schrijver family. A separate indexed result determines the nonequivariant homotopy type of this exact 25-vertex complex as \(S^3\vee S^4\vee S^4\); that result **already supplies the Betti numbers**, not the full graded dihedral character. Indexed dihedral characters for \(SG_{2,k}\) concern the stable two-subset family, a different graph and degree. Schultz's equivariant stable-Kneser topology concerns the box/graph-homomorphism complex \(\operatorname{{Hom}}(K_2,SG)\), not this independence complex. The present contribution is the symmetry decomposition across the two nonzero homology degrees.

## Limitations
The decomposition is rational in degree four, not a classification of its integral representation lattice. It does not classify equivariant homotopy types or the action of automorphisms outside the indicated dihedral group. Equivalent calculations in literature not indexed or materially accessible remain a priority risk; independent audit has not been performed.
