# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS (self-review).** The projective critical support is exhaustively classified by the common-root equation \(x_i^2+E_1x_i+E_2=0\), including the case \(E_1=0\). Height and the linear syzygy produce an \(n-1\)-quadric complete intersection. Each explicit saturated primary ideal contains the Jacobian ideal; equal projective lengths force equality after saturation. The Hessian and quadratic-radical computation establishes a smooth tangent cone even in characteristic two. Exact finite checks at \(n=4,8,12\) agree but are not relied on for the general claim.

## Originality
**PASS (provisional).** Xie, arXiv:2610.00259v1, proves the critical-locus dimension and multiplicity along coordinate components but expressly leaves smaller-dimensional and embedded components and primary decomposition open. The full family of \(2^{n-2}\) nontrivially thickened points, including supports of size above one, is not covered by the published local-coordinate statement. Orzel's dimension bounds and Cafure–Matera–Privitelli's partition-space bounds do not imply the complete primary ideals. Search comparisons of equivalent formulations, dominant results, and current accessible research records found no equivalent all-parameter formula. Some historical records outside the available set were inaccessible; historical priority and independent verification remain open.

## Value
**PASS.** This determines an explicitly motivated previously unclassified singular scheme of a natural invariant hypersurface family, including exponentially many points, their exact primary ideals, their infinitesimal thickness, and ordinary-double-point tangent cones. It distinguishes support, scheme length, and characteristic-two polar degeneracy, resolving a meaningful full-primary-decomposition subproblem rather than a small unmotivated numerical instance.

## Residual limitations
The proof applies only to the unit-coefficient elementary symmetric cubic with \(4\mid n\) in characteristic two. The complete cited literature does not guarantee historical uniqueness, and no independent audit was conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
