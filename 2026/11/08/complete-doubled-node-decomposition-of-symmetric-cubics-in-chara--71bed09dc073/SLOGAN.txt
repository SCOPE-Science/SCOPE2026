For the elementary symmetric cubic \(e_3\) in \(n=4r\) variables over any algebraically closed field of characteristic two, the full projective singular scheme is exactly \(2^{n-2}\) curvilinear double points, indexed by subsets of cardinality \(1\pmod4\). Its saturated Jacobian ideal has an explicit primary decomposition, and every underlying hypersurface singularity has a smooth quadratic tangent cone.
