For the normalized discrete Fourier matrix restricted to odd residue indices modulo \(2^k\), all complex eigenvalues and orthogonal eigenspace projectors are explicit: the phases split exceptionally at \(4\) and \(8\), then each of \(1,-1,i,-i\) occurs equally often from \(16\) onward alongside a half-dimensional kernel. A Chinese-remainder factorization completes the complex spectrum, exact rank, and reconstruction condition numbers for every even modulus.
