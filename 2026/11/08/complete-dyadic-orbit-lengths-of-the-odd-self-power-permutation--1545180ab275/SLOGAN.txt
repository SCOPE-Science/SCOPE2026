On odd residues modulo every power of two, the self-power map \(x\mapsto x^x\) is a permutation whose **entire cycle decomposition** is determined by \(\nu_2(x-1)\) or \(\nu_2(x+1)\). The two branches have different exact period-doubling laws; in particular, for \(k\ge6\) there are exactly two cycles of maximal length \(2^{k-4}\).
