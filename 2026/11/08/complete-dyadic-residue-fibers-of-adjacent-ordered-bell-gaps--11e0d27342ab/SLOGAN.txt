The adjacent differences of the ordered Bell numbers have an exact two-adic residue spectrum: for every \(k\ge7\), each residue \(110\) or \(114\pmod{128}\) occurs exactly once per least gap period \(2^{k-6}\) modulo \(2^k\), from the sharp index \(k-1\). Their two-adic closure is precisely two balls plus six isolated initial values.
