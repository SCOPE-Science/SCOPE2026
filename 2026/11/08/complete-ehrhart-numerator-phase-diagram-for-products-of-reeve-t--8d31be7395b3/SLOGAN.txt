The Ehrhart \(h^*\)-polynomial of every Cartesian product \(R_m\times R_n\) of two Reeve tetrahedra is unimodal, even though each nonunimodular factor has an internal coefficient zero. Its coefficients are log-concave exactly when \((m-1)(n-1)+10(m+n-2)\le72\), and all its roots are real exactly for \((m,n)=(1,1),(1,2),(2,1)\).
