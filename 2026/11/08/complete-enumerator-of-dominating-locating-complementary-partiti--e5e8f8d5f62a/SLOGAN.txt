For every finite simple base graph \(H\) and every clique attachment size \(r\ge2\), all complementary locating-dominating/dominating bipartitions of \(H\circ K_r\) admit a complete local classification and an exact subset-sum cardinality polynomial. The smallest locating-dominating class has size exactly \((r-1)|V(H)|+\gamma(H)\), and its multiplicity is explicit.
