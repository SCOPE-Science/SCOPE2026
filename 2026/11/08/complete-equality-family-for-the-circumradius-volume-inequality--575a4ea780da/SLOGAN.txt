All equality cases in the sharp three-dimensional four-generator zonotope volume–circumradius inequality \(V\le 2R^3\) form an explicitly described three-dimensional family of common shifts of regular-tetrahedron generators. Most are nonregular rhombic dodecahedra, so regularity is not necessary for minimal circumradius at fixed volume; the entire equality locus is specified by four linear and five quadratic inequalities.
