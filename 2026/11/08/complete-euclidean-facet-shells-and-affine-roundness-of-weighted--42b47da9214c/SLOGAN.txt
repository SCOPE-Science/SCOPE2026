The difference body of every positively weighted staircase simplex has exactly \(2(2^d-1)\) facets with explicit subset-indexed Euclidean distances, but only two facets contact its centered largest inball; the full incenter set nevertheless has dimension \(d-1\). Its Euclidean radius ratio is uniquely minimized by equal weights, while its optimal affine ellipsoid containment factor depends only on dimension.
