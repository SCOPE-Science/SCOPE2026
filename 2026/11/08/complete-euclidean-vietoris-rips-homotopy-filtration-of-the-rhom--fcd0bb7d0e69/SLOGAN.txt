The fourteen Euclidean vertices of the regular rhombic dodecahedron have a complete Vietoris–Rips filtration with successive integral homotopy types fourteen points, \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\), \(S^2\), \(S^3\), \(S^6\), and a point. An explicit 80-pair simplicial collapse explains the otherwise nonobvious \(S^3\) stage, and all positive-degree persistence intervals are determined exactly.
