The binary hypercube \(Q_d\) admits a Fair and Tolerant \(k\)-coloring with off-color neighbor proportion \(\alpha\) exactly when \(k=2^r\), \(\alpha=t/d\), and \((k-1)t\le d\) for positive integers \(r,t\). Consequently, \(\chi^{\mathrm{FAT}}(Q_d)=2^{\lfloor\log_2(d+1)\rfloor}\); a repeated-syndrome construction realizes every permissible tolerance, including the exact proper-coloring boundary.
