For the five-generator exterior algebra over any odd finite field, the complete ordinary-commutator fiber distribution depends only on whether the degree-two target is zero, nonzero decomposable, or nondecomposable, and on whether the degree-four target vanishes in the first case. Its additive commutator subspace is \(\bigwedge^2V\oplus\bigwedge^4V\), and the sharp commutator width is \(2\).
