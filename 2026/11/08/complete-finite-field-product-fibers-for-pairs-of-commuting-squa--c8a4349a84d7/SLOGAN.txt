For every finite field and matrix size, the number of ordered commuting square-zero factorizations of any prescribed product is given by an explicit Gaussian-binomial formula. For a nonzero square-zero target of rank \(t\), the fiber is nonempty exactly when \(4t\le n\), and its cardinality is an explicit prefactor times the zero-product pair count in dimension \(n-4t\); the previously known four-dimensional case is recovered.
