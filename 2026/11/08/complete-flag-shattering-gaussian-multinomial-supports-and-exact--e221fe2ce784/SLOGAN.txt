For complete flags of \(\mathbb F_q^n\) queried by subspace membership, the VC-shattered supports are exactly chains of proper nonzero subspaces, so their complete shattering polynomial is a Gaussian-multinomial face enumerator. Both VC and Littlestone dimensions equal \(n-1\), and the shattering complex is the classical spherical building of type \(A_{n-1}\).
