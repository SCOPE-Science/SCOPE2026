For each cyclic order \(8\le N\le17\), all four-point subsets minimizing the unnormalized Fourier Wiener sum form exactly one invertible-affine orbit: subgroup cosets when \(4\mid N\), antipodal rectangles when \(N\equiv2\pmod4\), and four-term progressions at the five odd orders. An exhaustive rational-interval certificate proves the classification, including global optimality at \(N=17\), where a previously known nonglobal single-exchange local minimum obstructs greedy search.
