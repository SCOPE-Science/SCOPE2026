Convolution by the normalized rank-one affine slice \(\{(1,w)(1,w)^{\mathsf T}:w\in\mathbb F_q^d\}\) has a completely explicit positive Fourier singular spectrum in every finite characteristic, with only even polar-rank bands surviving in characteristic two. The asymptotic fraction of recoverable modes is \(q/(q+1)\) for odd \(q\), but has two different dimension-parity limits given by Euler products for even \(q\).
