For every odd finite field of size \(q\ge5\), the additive Cayley graph on trace-zero symmetric three-by-three matrices generated by nonzero rank-one nilpotents has exactly six eigenvalues, with a complete binomial-inclusion-exclusion multiplicity law. Its sharp convolution condition number is \(q^2-1\), and its sharp nonconstant normalized spectral radius is \((3q-1)/(q^2-1)\).
