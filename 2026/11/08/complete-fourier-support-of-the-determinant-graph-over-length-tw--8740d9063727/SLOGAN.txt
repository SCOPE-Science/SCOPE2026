For the determinant graph of \(3\)-by-\(3\) matrices over every odd finite chain ring of length two, additive Fourier support is governed exactly by cofactor rank and a square-class condition, yielding a closed convolution-rank formula. Among invertible determinant frequencies, every rank-two matrix frequency vanishes and exactly half of full-rank residue frequencies survive, with the supported fraction tending to \(1/2\) as the residue field grows.
