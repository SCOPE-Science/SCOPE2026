# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
For \(2\le m\le n\), the proof supplies a bidirectional identification of general-position sets with bipartite edge-star forests. It proves all three cases of inclusion maximality by testing every possible missing edge, derives exact labeled exponential generating functions, constructs the full spectrum, and counts the extremal cases. The exceptional \(2\)-by-\(2\) board is checked separately. Independent, directly tested distance-triples agree with the exact generating-function coefficients for eight boards; these finite tests support but do not replace the unrestricted proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional publication priority)
The closest paper is Di Stefano et al., *Lower General Position Sets in Graphs*, arXiv:2306.09965, Theorem 5.3; it gives the minimum size of a maximal set on rook graphs. Klavžar et al., *On General Position Sets in Cartesian Products* (DOI:10.1007/s00025-021-01438-x), give the known maximum size. Iršič et al., *General Position Polynomials*, arXiv:2401.05696, introduce all-set polynomial enumeration but do not present this rook-graph census in inspected text. Anand et al., arXiv:1812.08460, contain the known general structural characterization, which is not claimed as new. The exact two-polynomial rook census, maximality cases, interior cardinality gaps and extremal counts were not found in the inspected publications or related record searches. Searches cannot establish global novelty: unindexed equivalent results are a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result answers the natural refinement of the lower and upper general-position problem: not only the extrema but every possible cardinality of an inclusion-maximal configuration and its multiplicity. The infinite family of two-row rook graphs has an arbitrarily long missing interval between possible maximal-set sizes. The weighted enumerators are complete on a classical graph family, with independent structural and computational checks, and carry information not implied by the previously known scalar formulas.

## Scientific limits
The claim is for labeled rook graphs with each factor of order at least two. The older minimum, maximum and general clique-based criterion are explicitly credited. Finite checks are not proofs for all parameters; the full combinatorial proof supplies the unrestricted claim. Global publication priority and independent mathematical audit remain open.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
