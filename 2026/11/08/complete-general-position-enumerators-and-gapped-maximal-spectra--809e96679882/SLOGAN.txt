For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(2\le m\le n\), general-position sets are precisely the edge-star forests of \(K_{m,n}\), giving complete labeled generating functions for both all and inclusion-maximal sets. Maximal sets have exactly the cardinalities \(\{m\}\cup\{n,\ldots,m+n-2\}\), so the missing interval can grow arbitrarily long; in particular \(M_{2,n}(z)=nz^2+2^nz^n\).
