## Correctness — PASS

The graph is explicitly defined by three types of indexed edges, and the package verifies a second incidence-configuration presentation. Its exact all-pairs shortest-path metric is checked against exhaustive clique enumeration by two algorithms plus all 65,536 subset predicates. The radius-two face vector is \((16,72,112,48)\). Two different vertex orders produce 119 augmented Morse pairs each; full topological-sort tests of 688 Hasse incidences certify both matchings acyclic. Both leave nine critical two-cells and two critical three-cells after removing the augmented empty/vertex pair. Integer unit-pivot elimination, independently run in both cancellation directions, gives the zero \(9\times2\) differential. Since \(\pi_2(\bigvee^9 S^2)\cong\mathbb Z^9\), zero integral attaching degrees prove the exact wedge homotopy type, not just ranks. Unreduced mod-two, mod-three and mod-five boundary ranks \((15,57,46)\) are independent checks. Radius three is a cross-polytope boundary by its exact eight disjoint nonedges; radius four is a simplex. The only algorithmic risk is an implementation error shared across the two discrete-Morse orders and finite-field methods, mitigated by separate face predicates, exact integral elimination and full replay from embedded `verify.py`.

## Originality — PASS against checked sources

The closest papers were actually inspected in full available text. Adamaszek–Adams proves hypercube/Hamming-metric statements, not the graph metric of the cubic Möbius–Kantor graph. Adamaszek's general graph-square result needs larger girth; the published hexagon-packing result needs pairwise edge-disjoint six-cycles, whereas this graph has 24 six-cycles, up to six sharing one edge. The finite-affine/projective-plane incidence results have different graph families. Feng–Nukala treats selected Boolean-layer symmetric-difference metrics. Knill's complete 2026 same-graph text covers group geometry and geodesics but not this Rips filtration. Public research-record searches covering the graph name, generalized-Petersen alias, incidence description, Rips/square-clique equivalents and stronger graph families found no implication of the mixed-dimensional homotopy statement. Same-graph Čech, matching and independence complexes are not this Rips complex. Residual risk: unindexed work or a stronger inaccessible theorem could cover the same graph and scale; absence from search is not a proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The sixteen-vertex Möbius–Kantor graph is a canonical symmetric cubic graph and the Levi graph of the \((8_3)\) configuration. Its first genuinely nontrivial intermediate Rips stage is a mixed-dimensional integral wedge of nine two-spheres and two three-spheres, despite the graph having 24 overlapping hexagons and lying precisely outside the edge-disjoint-hexagon theorem. The result supplies the **complete** homotopy filtration and integral barcode for a natural finite graph metric. It distinguishes a full homotopy invariant from a face count, mod-prime experiment or isolated Betti rank and may serve as a benchmark for graph-power topology beyond high-girth classifications. No broad family theorem is needed to make the exact natural invariant meaningful; claims are explicitly limited to this graph.
