The scale-three graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of the McGee graph is \(S^5\): its distance-four graph splits into six four-cycles, so the Rips complex is a sixfold join of zero-spheres. The full reduced integral barcode has 23 components on \([0,1)\), 13 loops on \([1,3)\), and one five-dimensional class on \([3,4)\).
