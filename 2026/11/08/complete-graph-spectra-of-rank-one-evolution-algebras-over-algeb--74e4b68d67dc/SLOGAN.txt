For every evolution algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from two whose square is one-dimensional, the directed graphs of *all* natural bases are classified exactly by the annihilator dimension and by whether the square generator lies in the annihilator, is isotropic, or is anisotropic. Every admissible graph pattern occurs in the same algebra, yielding exact labeled/unlabeled graph counts and a simultaneous-realization criterion for the entire rank-one-square family.
