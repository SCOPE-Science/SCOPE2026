For a normalized genus-\(14\) Artin–Schreier family in characteristic \(5\), the ranks of every Cartier power fall into exactly six coefficient strata. The first-rank jumping divisor is a quadric cone, but a disjoint Frobenius-twisted codimension-two locus has generic \(a\)-number and exceptional second Cartier rank: \(\operatorname{rk}\mathcal C=6\), \(\operatorname{rk}\mathcal C^2=1\).
