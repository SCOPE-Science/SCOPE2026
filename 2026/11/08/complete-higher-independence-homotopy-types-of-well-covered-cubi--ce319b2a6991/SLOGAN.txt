Among the four well-covered cubic polyhedral graphs, the first higher-independence complexes have homotopy types \(\bigvee^3S^1\), \(\bigvee^4S^1\), \(\bigvee^{11}S^3\), and \(\bigvee^3S^3\vee S^4\), respectively. The Dürer case has a primitive, nonzero signed Morse attaching map that forces its mixed-dimensional integral wedge decomposition.
