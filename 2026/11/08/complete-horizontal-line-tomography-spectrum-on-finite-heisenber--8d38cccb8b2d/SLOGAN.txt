The counting-norm X-ray transform along **all** distinct horizontal lines of a finite odd-characteristic Heisenberg group has a complete four-level squared-singular-value spectrum \(\{c+q^{2n},c,c+q^n,c-q^n\}\), with exact multiplicities. It is noninjective in rank \(n=1\) but injective for every \(n\ge2\), with a sharp condition number and explicit central-Fourier inverse.
