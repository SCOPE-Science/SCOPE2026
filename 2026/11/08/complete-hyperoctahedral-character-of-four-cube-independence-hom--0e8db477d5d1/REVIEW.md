# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The graph and automorphism action are defined exactly on sixteen binary vectors. An exhaustive pivot matching on all \(742\) nonempty independent faces gives \(367\) pairs and exactly one zero- and seven three-dimensional critical faces. Reconstructing all \(2816\) Hasse covers and completing a topological sort establishes acyclicity, so the integral homology is free of rank seven in degree three. Direct signed simplicial Lefschetz traces over all \(384\) automorphisms reproduce the stated character; Fourier projections and character theory identify two rational irreducible summands. The replayed standalone script checks the full finite domain, not samples. The matching is not equivariant, but the homology-action calculation is independently equivariant by chain traces.

## Value — PASS

The sixteen-vertex four-cube is simultaneously the smallest nontrivial four-dimensional hypercube and a natural fully periodic four-by-four square grid in hard-particle topology. Its previously known rank-seven homology does not say how the 384 geometric symmetries act. The new decomposition into irreducible rational modules of dimensions three and four, and the exact weight-three translation Fourier content, provide a complete natural invariant of this finite object and a useful equivariant test case for lattice-state models. The result is not presented as a new nonequivariant wedge theorem or a generic graph calculation.
