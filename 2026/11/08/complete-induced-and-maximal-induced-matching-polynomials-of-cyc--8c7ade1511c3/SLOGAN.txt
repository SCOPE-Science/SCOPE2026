For every \(r\ge1\) and \(n\ge2r+1\), a cyclic block decomposition determines the complete induced- and maximal-induced-matching cardinality polynomials of the cycle power \(C_n^r\). Inclusion-maximal induced matchings occur at every size from \(\lceil n/(3r+2)\rceil\) to \(\lfloor n/(r+2)\rfloor\), with exact extremal multiplicities at the corresponding divisibility boundaries.
