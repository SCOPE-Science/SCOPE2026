# Scientific review: complete infinity Weierstrass semigroups

**Correctness — PASS.** The argument starts from an explicit tame scalar quotient of a smooth Artin–Schreier curve of degree \(p\). It independently identifies the *whole* ring of functions regular away from infinity as the invariant ring \(\bigoplus_{b=0}^{p-1} k[U]\Phi_b\). Exact poles \(p\) and \(p+bL\) have pairwise different residues modulo \(p\), precluding cancellation. This proves equality of the semigroup with the claimed Apéry rays, not merely containment. The genus and gap-weight calculations follow by summing residue-class progressions; the pseudo-Frobenius and minimal-generator assertions follow from the same Apéry set. A standalone verifier enumerates all gaps and bounded invariant monomials in six substantial finite cases and agrees with the symbolic formulas. It does not replace the general proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** Bartoli–Borges–Timpanella–Zini [1] construct the curve, establish maximality and trivial automorphisms, and give the quotient representation and unique \(p\)-gonal pencil. Their inspected construction and main results do not state the complete distinguished-point Weierstrass semigroup, Apéry set, type, and weight. Published work [2] determines Weierstrass semigroups for a different maximal family. Yang–Hu [3] discusses other Kummer-cover situations, and Ito [4] analyzes \(L\)-polynomials and quotient maps, principally in characteristic two. General Apéry and almost-symmetric criteria [5] provide the algebraic interpretation once the geometric semigroup is computed, but do not by themselves identify this semigroup on these curves. Searches for the given curve name, scalar quotient, generator sequence, equivalent gap formula, and weight formula did not uncover a stated equivalent or stronger result. A broader implicit theorem for cyclic quotient semigroups remains a nonzero historical-priority risk; absence from a search index is not uniqueness proof.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result classifies an entire geometric Weierstrass invariant, including minimal generators, Apéry and pseudo-Frobenius sets, and exact weight, at a canonical distinguished rational point in an infinite, unbounded-genus, maximal and geometrically automorphism-free family. These invariants are not determined by the known genus, Frobenius maximality, gonality or automorphism group. The associated explicit formula for every one-point Riemann–Roch dimension supplies immediate input to one-point algebraic-geometric code constructions, without claiming distance bounds. It is a natural, fully quantified structural classification rather than a parameter-specific or decorative numerical evaluation.

**Closest literature and limitations.** The closest source is [1], whose scalar-cover construction is an essential premise and whose maximality/trivial-automorphism claims are not repeated as discoveries. The exact distinguished-point semigroup is the new assertion. Results [2]–[4] concern related but different semigroup or Artin–Schreier questions. The proof is over an algebraic closure and concerns only \(P_\infty\). No complete census of other Weierstrass points, AG-code minimum distances, independent audit or exhaustive priority determination is asserted.
