The distinguished point at infinity of every odd-characteristic Bartoli–Borges–Timpanella–Zini maximal curve has the explicit Weierstrass semigroup \(\langle p,p+L,\ldots,p+(p-1)L\rangle\), where \(L=(p^{2n}+1-2p)/(2(p-1))\). Its embedding dimension and multiplicity both equal \(p\), its type is \(p-1\), and its Weierstrass weight is \((p^2-1)(L^2-1)/24\).
