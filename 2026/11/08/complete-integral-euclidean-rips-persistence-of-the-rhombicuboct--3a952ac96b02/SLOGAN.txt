The regular Euclidean rhombicuboctahedron has an exactly determined integral Vietoris–Rips filtration: after seventeen circles, its geometrically marked boundary sphere persists across three strict Euclidean distance shells, then gives way to seven three-spheres and a single eleven-sphere. Exact collapse certificates, a unimodular boundary-rank witness, and a seventy-nine-tetrahedron filling determine all integer persistence intervals.
