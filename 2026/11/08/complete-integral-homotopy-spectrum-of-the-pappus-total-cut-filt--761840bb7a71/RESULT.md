# Complete integral homotopy spectrum of the Pappus total-cut filtration

## Finding

Let \(P\) be the Pappus graph on 18 vertices. For \(1\le k\le9\), let \(\Delta_k^{t}(P)\) be the simplicial complex generated by the complements of independent vertex sets of cardinality \(k\). There is an **integral homotopy equivalence for every level**
\[
\Delta_k^{t}(P)\simeq \bigvee^{b_k}S^{18-2k},\qquad
(b_1,b_2,\ldots,b_9)=(1,10,55,202,481,688,460,64,1).
\]
At \(k=9\), the wedge notation means \(S^0\), a two-point space. For integers \(k>9\) there are no independent \(k\)-sets, hence the total-cut complex is void. All homotopy equivalences above are integral, not merely rational or homological. Each level is pure of dimension \(17-k\); consequently all nonvoid levels with \(k\ge2\) are non-shellable. The new computational classifications are principally the five interior levels \(4\le k\le8\); the endpoint and low-level cases include consequences of known general results.

## Assumptions and scope

Use the canonical labeled Pappus graph on \(\{0,1,\ldots,17\}\): include all cyclic edges \(\{i,i+1\}\) with indices modulo 18, and each edge \(\{i,i+\ell_i\}\) where \((\ell_0,\ldots,\ell_{17})=(5,7,-7,7,-7,-5,5,7,-7,7,-7,-5,5,7,-7,7,-7,-5)\). Duplicate undirected edges are removed. This is the LCF description \([5,7,-7,7,-7,-5]^3\) of the Pappus graph. The graph has 18 vertices, 27 edges, two bipartition classes of nine vertices and independence number nine. For every positive integer \(k\), a face \(F\) of \(\Delta_k^{t}(P)\) is exactly a subset whose complement contains an independent \(k\)-set. All spaces are ordinary geometric realizations of finite abstract simplicial complexes, and all stated homology is integral.

## Proof

**Face test.** Encode a vertex set by an 18-bit integer \(m\), with the full-set mask \(U=2^{18}-1\). For a nonempty mask choose its least indexed vertex \(v\), and let \(R=m\setminus\{v\}\). The maximum independent-set cardinality satisfies the elementary exact recurrence
\[
a(0)=0,\qquad a(m)=\max\{a(R),1+a(R\setminus N_P(v))\}.
\]
This follows by partitioning independent subsets into those not using \(v\) and those using \(v\). Thus \(F\) is a face at level \(k\) precisely if \(a(U\setminus F)\ge k\). The attached standalone verifier exhausts all \(2^{18}\) masks and cross-checks this criterion with a second exact recurrence for existence of an independent \(k\)-set, as well as direct independent-set enumeration for selected cardinalities.

**Discrete-Morse matching.** At each level \(k\), start with the augmented face poset including the empty face, ordered by single-vertex inclusion. Sweep the pivot vertices in the exact order \(0,1,\ldots,17\). At pivot \(v\), pair every currently unmatched face \(F\) omitting \(v\) to its currently unmatched coface \(F\cup\{v\}\) whenever both are faces; the stand-alone verifier specifies the exact finite iteration order. Each face participates in at most one pair. For every covering pair \(F\subset F\cup\{v\}\), direct its Hasse arrow upward if matched and downward if unmatched. Exhaustive construction of all directed incidences followed by topological sorting visits **every** face at each of the nine levels: the matching is acyclic, and this is a complete finite certificate rather than a statistical test.

In each run, the augmented empty face is paired to a singleton vertex. Delete this one pair and the empty face when passing to the ordinary nonempty face poset. This leaves one critical vertex, together with the displayed \(b_k\) critical cells of dimension \(18-2k\). The remaining matching is acyclic since it is a restriction of an acyclic matching. By Forman's discrete-Morse theorem, the geometric realization has a CW model with precisely those cells. For \(1\le k\le8\), the higher critical-cell dimension is at least two; with no intermediate positive-dimensional cells, every attaching map has a point as its target. The model is therefore \(\bigvee^{b_k}S^{18-2k}\). For \(k=9\), the model has two zero-cells and is \(S^0\). A separate independence-number certificate excludes \(k>9\). Since complements of independent \(k\)-sets have uniform size \(18-k\), every nonvoid level is pure of dimension \(17-k\). For \(k\ge2\), its nonzero reduced homology is strictly below the pure top dimension; a pure shellable complex cannot have such homology, establishing the non-shellability conclusion.

Its checks cover two independently implemented independent-set recurrences on all \(262144\) masks, the nine exact face sets, direct spot enumeration of independent subsets, critical-cell dimensions and counts, reduced Euler characteristics, all face-cover incidences, and directed-cycle absence via topological sorting. No claimed infinite-family result is inferred from this finite enumeration. The table is a finite complete certificate because the vertex set, all levels, all faces and all incidence arrows are exhaustively covered.

## Relationship to prior work

The definition and topological framework for total cut complexes are due to Bayer, Denker, Jelić Milutinović, Rowlands, Sundaram and Xue (first public preprint 2022-09-27). They classify complete bipartite, multipartite, cycle and other specified families, not the nine-level filtration of the Pappus Levi graph. Carnero Bravo (2026) develops Alexander-dual and connectivity methods with exact families of disconnected graphs, complete multipartite graphs, cycle powers and certain Cartesian products; these hypotheses do not identify the present full sequence. Public research on three-robust clique complexes of connected bipartite graphs, together with Alexander duality, already covers the \(k=3\) homology/homotopy rank in this example, namely \(55\), and the universal \(k=1\) and classical \(k=2\) cases have previously available explanations. Those components are included only for **completeness of the single full-filtration statement**. The substantive additional classification is the exact \(k=4,5,6,7,8\) homotopy sequence \((202,481,688,460,64)\) in dimensions \((10,8,6,4,2)\). The ordinary Pappus cut complex, based on complements of disconnected induced subsets, is a distinct complex: its prior five-cut calculation does not cover the total five-cut complex based on *independent* subsets.

## Limitations

This is a classification of one canonical graph, not an assertion for all cubic bipartite graphs or all incidence graphs. It does not determine integral automorphism modules, equivariant homotopy types, persistence at the chain level, or complete classifications for other graphs. All sphere decompositions are ordinary nonequivariant homotopy equivalences. The low-level \(k=1,2,3\) values should not be considered independent discoveries. Novelty is assessed against inspected sources and searchable indexed records, so unindexed equivalent work remains possible. Computation checks a finite universe exactly but does not constitute independent external validation.
