The complete total-cut filtration of the eighteen-vertex Pappus graph has nine nonvoid stages, each an integral wedge of spheres in dimension \(18-2k\), with multiplicities \((1,10,55,202,481,688,460,64,1)\) for \(1\le k\le9\). The five interior stages \(4\le k\le8\) give the principal new classifications, and all eight nontrivial later stages are non-shellable.
