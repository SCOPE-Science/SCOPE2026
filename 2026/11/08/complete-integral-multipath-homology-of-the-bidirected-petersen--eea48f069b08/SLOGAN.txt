The fully bidirected Petersen graph has multipath-complex reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{12}\), \(\mathbb Z\), \(\mathbb Z^{44}\), and \(\mathbb Z^{94}\) in degrees \(4,5,6,7\) respectively, and zero otherwise. Two exhaustive acyclic reductions show that every nonzero integral boundary invariant factor is a unit, so no torsion occurs.
