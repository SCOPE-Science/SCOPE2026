The twenty-vertex Desargues incidence graph has an exact integral vertex-Rips filtration transitioning through \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{19}S^2\), \(S^3\), and \(S^9\) at the four consecutive positive integer thresholds before becoming contractible. Two complete acyclic Morse matchings certify the exceptional middle stages, while the final sphere is an antipodal cross-polytope boundary.
