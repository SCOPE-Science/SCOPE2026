For every alphabet \(q\ge2\), ordered read width \(\ell\ge2\), and source length \(n\ge\ell+1\), the number of candidate source words after one deleted overlapping read is exactly determined by the read-overlap defect and the long internal constant runs of its compatible reconstruction. Its complete finite-length distribution has a rational generating function, and its sharp worst-case list size is \(2q+\lfloor(n-3)/(\ell-1)\rfloor\).
