For the four-quadrant planar map \(T_{a,c}(x,y)=(|x|+ay-1,x+c|y|-1)\) with \(a,c>0\), the complete inverse-fiber phase diagram has a homeomorphism for \(c<a\), a sharply bounded region of two- and four-fold inverse multiplicities for \(c>a\), and exactly specified continuum-sized fibers at \(c=a\). The classification includes every critical ray and every target, not only generic values.
