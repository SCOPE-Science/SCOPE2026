# Complete isolated-gap classification for a threefold quotient of consecutive-generator semigroups

## Finding
For every integer \(k\ge 2\), let \(a=3k+1\), and define the numerical semigroup
\[
Q_k=\{n\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}:3n\in\langle a,a+1\rangle\}.
\]
Call a positive integer \(g\) an *isolated gap* of a numerical semigroup \(S\) if \(g\notin S\) but \(g-1,g+1\in S\). Then
\[
\boxed{Q_k=\langle2k+1,3k+1,3k+2\rangle,\qquad
I(Q_k)=\{3k^2-2k-2,3k^2-k-1,3k^2-1\}.}
\]
All three generators are minimal, so \(Q_k\) has embedding dimension three. Its Frobenius number is \(3k^2-1\) and its genus is \(k(3k+1)/2\). In particular, exactly three gaps are isolated at every parameter \(k\ge2\). For the excluded boundary \(k=1\), \(Q_1=\langle3,4,5\rangle\) has no isolated gaps.

## Assumptions and scope
Here \(\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\) includes zero; \(\langle A\rangle\) denotes the additive submonoid generated by \(A\), and a numerical semigroup has finite complement in \(\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\). The assertion covers *every* integer \(k\ge2\), with no primality or parity restriction. The isolated-gap definition uses **both** immediate neighbors; it is distinct from an arbitrary gap or a pseudo-Frobenius number. The Frobenius number is the largest gap; genus is the number of gaps.

## Proof
**1. Quotient generators.** Set \(a=3k+1\). A representation \(3n=xa+y(a+1)\) with \(x,y\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\) satisfies \(x+2y\equiv0\pmod3\), equivalently \(x\equiv y\pmod3\). Writing \(x=3r+t\), \(y=3s+t\), with \(r,s\ge0\) and \(t\in\{0,1,2\}\), gives
\[
n=ra+s(a+1)+t(2a+1)/3.
\]
The converse is immediate, so \(Q_k=\langle m,u,v\rangle\), where \(m=2k+1\), \(u=3k+1\), \(v=3k+2\). For \(k\ge2\), \(m<u<v<2m\), so these distinct generators are all minimal. Also \(\gcd(m,u)=\gcd(2k+1,k)=1\), hence the complement is finite.

**2. An exact Apéry set.** Since \(u+v=3m\) and \(u\equiv k\), \(v\equiv-k\pmod m\), any nonnegative combination \(ru+sv\) with \(r,s>0\) can have one copy of each replaced by \(3m\), preserving value and residue. To minimize a residue representative, eliminate all such pairs and all positive copies of \(m\). Thus the least \(Q_k\)-element in the residue class \(jk\pmod m\), for \(0\le j\le 2k\), is
\[
W_j=\min\{ju,(m-j)v\}
=\begin{cases}ju,&0\le j\le k,\\(m-j)v,&k+1\le j\le2k.\end{cases}
\]
Indeed \(ju-(m-j)v=m(3j-v)\), whose sign changes between \(j=k\) and \(j=k+1\). Each nonnegative integer of residue \(jk\pmod m\) belongs to \(Q_k\) exactly when it is at least \(W_j\). Every gap in that class therefore has form \(W_j-hm\), for an integer \(h\ge1\) for which the result is nonnegative.

**3. Neighbor criterion.** The congruence \(-2k\equiv1\pmod m\) implies that \(n-1\) has Apéry index \(j+2\pmod m\) and \(n+1\) has index \(j-2\pmod m\), when \(n\) has index \(j\). Therefore \(n=W_j-hm\) is isolated precisely when
\[
W_j-hm-1\ge W_{j+2\bmod m},\qquad W_j-hm+1\ge W_{j-2\bmod m}.
\]
For \(0\le j\le k-1\), the first inequality fails because \(W_{j+2}>W_j\) (including the crossover at \(j=k-1\)). At \(j=k+2\), the second fails since \(W_k>W_{k+2}\). For \(k+3\le j\le2k\), the second fails since \(W_{j-2}>W_j\). Thus only \(j=k\) and \(j=k+1\) remain.

At \(j=k\), the exact differences are
\[
W_k-W_{k+2}=ku-(k-1)v=m+1,\qquad W_k-W_{k-2}=2u=3m-1.
\]
The inequalities reduce to \(hm\le m\) and \(hm\le3m\), giving \(h=1\). This gap is \(ku-m=3k^2-k-1\).

At \(j=k+1\), the exact differences are
\[
W_{k+1}-W_{k-1}=kv-(k-1)u=2m-1,\qquad W_{k+1}-W_{k+3\bmod m}=2v=3m+1.
\]
The latter formula includes the wrap-around \(k=2\), where \(k+3=m\) and \(W_0=0\). The inequalities become \(hm\le2m\) and \(hm\le3m\), giving precisely \(h=1,2\). The resulting isolated gaps are \(kv-m=3k^2-1\) and \(kv-2m=3k^2-2k-2\). They are positive for \(k\ge2\). This completes the exact classification.

**4. Standard numerical-semigroup invariants.** The largest Apéry representative is \(W_{k+1}=kv\), so the Frobenius number is \(kv-m=3k^2-1\). Since
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{2k}W_j=\frac{k(k+1)}2(u+v)=\frac{3mk(k+1)}2,
\]
the usual residue-class gap count gives genus \(\sum_j W_j/m-(m-1)/2=k(3k+1)/2\). At \(k=1\), direct inspection of \(\langle3,4,5\rangle\) gives gaps \(1,2\), neither isolated.

## Verification
The standalone dependency-free `checker.py` computes Apéry sets using Dijkstra shortest paths on residue classes, independently of the displayed formula. It compares exact Apéry sets and isolated gaps for all \(2\le k\le120\), tests the quotient representation against direct enumeration for all \(2\le k\le24\), and checks the excluded boundary \(k=1\). The generated `checker_output.txt` records 119 Apéry checks, 119 gap checks, and 23 quotient checks. These are finite cross-checks; the preceding derivation supplies the proof for all parameters.

Run with `python3 checker.py` from the directory containing the files.

## Relationship to prior work
The quotient construction is in the subject of Cabanillas's *Quotients of numerical semigroups generated by two numbers* (arXiv:1904.08240, initially released 2019-04-17), which develops general generators and arithmetic invariants for these quotients. The definition and general Apéry approach to isolated gaps appear in Smith's *On isolated gaps in numerical semigroups* (2022). Singh and Ram's *On Isolated Gaps of Numerical Semigroups of embedding dimension two* (arXiv:2406.09848, 2024-06-14) gives a full classification for two-minimal-generator semigroups; the present quotients have three minimal generators. The statement here is the exact three-isolated-gap classification for the specified quotient family; the general quotient machinery and the general isolated-gap criterion are not claimed as new.

## Limitations
This result does not classify all three-generated numerical semigroups, all quotients, or all possible isolated-gap counts. Published general formulas for quotients may offer alternative derivations of the Frobenius number and genus, which are included here for context. The exact isolated-gap classification was not identified in the checked literature, but inaccessible full text and unindexed equivalent results remain a literature-priority risk. Independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
