Every threefold quotient \(Q_k=\langle3k+1,3k+2\rangle/3\), for \(k\ge2\), has embedding dimension three but exactly three isolated gaps, namely \(3k^2-2k-2\), \(3k^2-k-1\), and \(3k^2-1\). The last is also its Frobenius number; a complete Apéry-set calculation proves the classification for all parameters.
