For every field, the complete Jordan partition of multiplication by any nilpotent element \(a(y)+xb(y)\) in \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^m)\) depends only on the \(y\)-adic valuations of its two coefficients, with an explicit rank-of-powers formula valid in every characteristic. Over each finite field, a closed weighted sum determines the exact frequency of every Jordan partition among all nilpotent elements.
