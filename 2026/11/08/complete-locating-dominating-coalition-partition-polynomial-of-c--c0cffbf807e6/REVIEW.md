# Self-review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The false-twin characterization is both necessary and sufficient: for nontrivial complete bipartite graphs, locating-dominating sets are exactly complements of transversals with at most one vertex on each side. Coalition partners correspond to complementary transversal blocks. The four-block exclusion for at least five vertices is an explicit contradiction among three singleton side assignments. For three blocks, the necessary-and-sufficient two-transversal criterion gives exhaustive types, including all four small-order boundary graphs. The formulas for unordered partitions are derived without hidden symmetry divisions. Direct graph-based exhaustive checking through ten vertices and checks of additional constructed instances support the proof, without being used as an infinite certificate.

## Originality

**PASS, historically provisional.** The 2026 initiating manuscript, arXiv:2602.18760, was inspected across its definitions, general bounds, paths and cycles, order-extremal results, complexity results and conclusions. It gives the general bound \(C_L\le N-\gamma_L+2\) and includes \(K_{2,2}\cong C_4\) as a known extremal case; it does not state the all-partition counts for nontrivial complete bipartite graphs. The 2025 locating-dominating-partition manuscript arXiv:2506.12933 asks for disjoint classes that are each LD, a logically different condition. Targeted mathematical-record and literature searches found related locating-domination and different coalition variants, but no equivalent all-parameter enumerator. Failure to find coverage is not proof of priority; unindexed or differently phrased literature is a residual risk.

## Value

**PASS.** The complete natural partition-counting polynomial is stronger than the numerical coalition maximum for an infinite two-parameter family, with an exact classification of every maximizing partition and the exceptional triangular coalition graphs. The contrast between exponentially many two-class partitions and only polynomially many three-class optimizers is structural and usable for further enumeration. The result is not presented as a general graph theorem or a resolution of complexity questions.
