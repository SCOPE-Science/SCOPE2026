Every locating-dominating coalition partition of a labelled complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\) is classified and counted by an explicit polynomial with no terms beyond degree three, except for the four-cycle \(K_{2,2}\). From seven vertices onward, all maximum partitions arise from two small transversal blocks of three possible forms, while triangular coalition graphs appear only in two exceptional graph sizes.
