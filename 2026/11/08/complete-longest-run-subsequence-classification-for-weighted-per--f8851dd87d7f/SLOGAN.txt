For a finite power \(W=(c_0^{a_0}\cdots c_{q-1}^{a_{q-1}})^k\) with distinct letters and positive run lengths, the longest subsequence having at most one run of each letter has length \(\sum_i a_i+(k-1)\max_i a_i\). Every longest subsequence preserves the natural letter order, and there are exactly \(\binom{k+r-2}{r-1}\) distinct optimum strings, where \(r\) is the number of tied longest runs in one period.
