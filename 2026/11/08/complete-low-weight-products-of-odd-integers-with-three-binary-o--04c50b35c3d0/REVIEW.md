# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For odd factors with three one-bits, s2(ab)=1 is impossible. Kaneko–Stoll Theorems 2.1 and 2.2 bound possible products by 2^14 at product-weight 2 and 2^23 at weight 3. Consequently there are only 13 and 231 odd candidate output numbers of these weights. An independently written product-first enumeration of their potential odd 3-bit divisors recovered exactly (25,41,1025) and the six listed 3-bit-product pairs. A separate assigned factor-first checker uses 231 factors, 3672 unordered products below cutoff. Exhaustive finite enumeration combined with cited global bounds proves exactly seven pairs and maximum 1029. The code alone does not prove the infinite bound.
