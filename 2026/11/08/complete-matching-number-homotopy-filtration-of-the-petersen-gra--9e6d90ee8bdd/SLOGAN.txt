For the Petersen graph \(P\), the complete nonmatching filtration has \(\operatorname{NM}_2(P)\simeq\bigvee^6S^1\), \(\operatorname{NM}_3(P)\simeq\bigvee^9S^3\), \(\operatorname{NM}_4(P)\simeq\bigvee^4S^5\), and \(\operatorname{NM}_5(P)\simeq\bigvee^5S^9\), becoming contractible at \(k=6\). Each nonzero integral homology class survives for exactly one successive matching threshold.
