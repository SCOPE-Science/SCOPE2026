For every monotone directed two-row ladder, every root order \(N\ge2\), and every Mayer index \(1\le q<N\), the unreduced Mayer path Betti numbers are given by explicit piecewise formulas and all groups above degree two vanish. At orders \(N\ge4\), exactly the indices \(q\ge3\) produce nonzero degree-two homology, of rank equal to the number of directed squares.
