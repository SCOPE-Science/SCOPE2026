For every binary word \(W=0^a1^b0^c1^d0^e\) with positive run lengths, the complete antichain of minimal absent scattered subsequences consists of exactly seven unconditional and at most six explicitly classified conditional patterns. Its exact size is \(7+x+y+z+xy+yz+xyz\), where \(x=[b\ge2]\), \(y=[c\ge2]\), and \(z=[d\ge2]\); the only possible sizes are \(7,8,9,10,13\).
