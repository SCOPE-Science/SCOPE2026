# Scientific review

The proof explicitly derives and verifies the seven allowed trigrams, proves morphism-boundary synchronization of `01`, desubstitutes all right-special prefixes, uses the published three-left-extensions and affine-complexity theorems to eliminate every other left-special factor, and transports the complete joint left/right context graph through two bijections. The only nonstandard steps are the synchronization and context-transport lemmas, each checked by tracing both directions of the morphism and by independently testing all predicted MAWs against literal factor languages through length 40. Infinite correctness rests on these arguments, not on stabilization in two finite substitution iterates. Risks: the proof depends on the stated primitive-substitution and older special-factor premises; they have been checked against their original accessible texts, not independently re-proved in full.

Unindexed literature and inaccessible source pages remain a historical-priority limitation.

## Value (V): PASS
Minimal absent words are the natural finite obstructions that characterize a factorial language. This gives a complete infinite antidictionary for a canonical non-Arnoux–Rauzy ternary morphic word, with sparse exact exceptional lengths and constructive enumeration of all forbidden contexts. That is more informative than the known affine factor complexity, and can be used directly to decide membership in the factor language by checking minimal forbidden factors. The result does not claim a general theorem for all Parry substitutions.
