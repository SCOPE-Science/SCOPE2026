The infinite Narayana word has exactly four minimal absent words of length two and exactly two at each length \(|u_j|+2\), where \(u_1=0\) and \(u_{j+1}=\nu(u_j)\) or \(\nu(u_j)0\) according as \(j\) is odd or even. An explicit two-state extension-graph recurrence generates every absent word and proves that no other lengths occur.
