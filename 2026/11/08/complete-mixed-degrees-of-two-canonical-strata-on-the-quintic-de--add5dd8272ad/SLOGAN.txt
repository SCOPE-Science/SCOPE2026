The canonical intersection locus and its singular stratum in the degree-five moduli geometry of the quintic del Pezzo threefold have complete mixed \(H\)-Pluecker degree sequences \((504,448,210,45)\) and \((110,52,20,5)\), respectively. Their Segre degrees in the natural product embedding are \(15876\) and \(620\), with explicit integral Chow-class formulas.
