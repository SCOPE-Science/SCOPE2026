For an explicit annihilator-free real evolution-algebra family with two-dimensional square, every natural-basis graph is a complete-split graph and every such graph with at least two dominating vertices occurs. The entire edge-Cheeger spectrum is exact: its minimum alternates between \(2\) and \(2-2/n\) with dimension parity, while its maximum is \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), giving unbounded basis dependence.
