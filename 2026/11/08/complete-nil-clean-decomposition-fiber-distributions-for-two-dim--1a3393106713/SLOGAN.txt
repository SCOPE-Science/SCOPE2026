For every finite field, the number of ways to write a two-by-two matrix as an idempotent plus a nilpotent is determined exactly by its trace, determinant and rational eigenlines. Its complete fiber histogram has a uniform characteristic-dependent formula, with maximal multiplicity \(2q-1\) and precisely \(q^3+2q^2-\mathbf1_{\{q\text{ odd}\}}\) nil-clean matrices.
