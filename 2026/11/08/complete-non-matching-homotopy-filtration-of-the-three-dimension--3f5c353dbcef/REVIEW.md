# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The definitions use the twelve cube edges and all \(4096\) edge subsets. The matching-number recurrence is exact by the choose-or-exclude argument for an edge, and a separate exhaustive disjoint-edge-family enumerator agrees on all inputs. The three explicit full face-poset pairings have respectively \(19\), \(295\), and \(1331\) ordinary matched pairs and respectively \(1+5\), \(1+9\), and \(1+1\) critical cells in dimensions \((0,1)\), \((0,3)\), and \((0,5)\). Their entire directed face-poset cover graphs pass acyclicity checks. Forman's theorem thus gives one vertex and only one positive cell dimension in each case, forcing the stated integral sphere wedges. A separate vertex-based enumerator and mod-two chain-complex reduction agree, but the homotopy proof is the Morse certificate, not field homology. A four-edge perfect matching settles all remaining thresholds. The persistence barcode follows from degree separation.

## Originality — PASS, subject to publication-priority uncertainty

Linusson–Shareshian–Welker (arXiv:math/0410345, Sections 1 and 4.2) compute complete bipartite non-matching complexes, with sphere ranks \(9,9,1\) for \(K_{4,4}\), not its twelve-edge proper spanning subgraph, the cube. Holmsen–Lee (arXiv:2003.11270, Theorems 1.1–1.2 and Section 4.3) prove homology vanishing bounds, not the exact cube homotopy types. Shan–Wang (arXiv:2609.02621, Sections 1–3) prove top-homology injectivity and cycle nonvanishing, but not exact cube sphere ranks and attaching data. Targeted indexed comparisons to cube matching, two-matching and cut complexes have different face conditions. Residual uncertainty is possible equivalent unindexed work; neither an absence of search results nor a different title constitutes proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The cube is the smallest nontrivial three-dimensional hypercube and the four-crown \(K_{4,4}\) minus a perfect matching. Determining all of its non-matching homotopy stages is a natural complete finite classification in the exact graph-complex family studied by the archived foundational paper. It shows that matching deletion changes the first sphere multiplicity from nine to five while preserving the next two multiplicities and that the bipartite top-degree bound is attained at every stage. The result includes explicit reusable finite certificates and a complete integral barcode; it is not claimed to classify general crowns or to yield a new global bound.

## Literature and residual limits

Full relevant mathematical statements and methods were inspected in the three cited arXiv HTML texts. The most closely indexed cube graph results concern *ordinary matching*, *bounded-degree matching*, and *cut* complexes, which are not the complexes defined here. General bounds and the top-degree injection theorem do not imply this complete homotopy filtration. GitHub full text of some indexed records was inaccessible; only search-index summaries were used for those records, and no whole-document negative claim is made about them. Unindexed equivalences and independent mathematical validation remain open risks.
