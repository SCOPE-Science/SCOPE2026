The non-matching complexes of the three-dimensional cube form a complete sphere-wedge filtration: \(\operatorname{NM}_2(Q_3)\simeq\bigvee^5S^1\), \(\operatorname{NM}_3(Q_3)\simeq\bigvee^9S^3\), and \(\operatorname{NM}_4(Q_3)\simeq S^5\), followed by a simplex. All three nontrivial stages attain the top-dimensional bipartite non-matching homology bound, and explicit acyclic Morse matchings certify the integral homotopy types.
