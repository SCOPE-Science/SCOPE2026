For the zero-field Ising model on the Brownian-covariance graphon \(W(x,y)=\min\{x,y\}\), every nonzero stationary profile belongs to an exactly enumerable pair of nodal branches born at \(\beta_j=\pi^2(2j+1)^2/4\), with nodal positions independent of temperature. Above the fundamental inverse-temperature threshold \(\beta_0=\pi^2/4\), only the two nodeless branches minimize free energy; all sign-changing branches are non-global stationary solutions.
