The complete one-limited domination polynomial of every labelled circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) is the \(u^n\)-coefficient of an explicit rational logarithmic derivative. Every minimum set has \(n\) vertices, with exactly two minimizers unless \(4\mid n\), when there are six; the total count grows as \(\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\approx2.2923702494\).
