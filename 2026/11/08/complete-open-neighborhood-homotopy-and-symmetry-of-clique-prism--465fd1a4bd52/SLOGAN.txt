For every \(n\ge4\), the open neighborhood complex of two \(n\)-cliques joined by a perfect matching is \(S^{n-2}\vee S^{n-2}\vee\bigvee^{n^2-3n+1}S^2\). For \(n\ge5\), its full \(S_n\times C_2\) homology action is given by a zero-row/column-sum matrix lattice and a pair of sign-twisted sphere generators; the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
