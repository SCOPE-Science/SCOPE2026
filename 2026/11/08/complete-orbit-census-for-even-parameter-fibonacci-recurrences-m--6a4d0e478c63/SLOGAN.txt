For every \(K\equiv2\pmod4\), all \(K\)-Fibonacci recurrences with arbitrary starting values modulo \(2^r\) collectively have exactly \(2^{r+1}-2\) distinct cycles when \(r\ge2\), independent of \(K\). Their primitive lengths are precisely \(1,2,4,\ldots,2^r\), with explicit multiplicities obtained from the two-adic Smith forms of their companion-matrix powers.
