# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof computes both integral Smith cokernels using cyclotomic factorizations and reduction modulo the *other* prime. Every fixed-point cardinality follows from those cokernels. Exact orbit counts follow by inclusion–exclusion. The direct exhaustive verifier checks 932,979 modular states across five cases; these tests corroborate but do not replace the infinite-domain proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest primary source is Pudelko, arXiv:2510.24882, especially its prime and prime-power cyclotomic recurrence analyses. The separately established prime-power-index orbit theorem assumes a single prime factor, so it does not imply this distinct-odd-prime-index classification. General orbit-count inversion does not compute the required Smith invariants. Targeted searches for fixed-point, cyclotomic quotient, and semiprime-index formulas found no covering source; unindexed prior equivalents remain possible.

## Value — PASS
The result completely resolves a first natural squarefree composite-index family and explains the two independent sources of shorter modular periods. Exact symbolic counts hold for arbitrary moduli, including those divisible by neither, one, or both index primes. It does not classify indices with more than two prime factors or repeated prime factors.

## Evidence and limits
The five finite checks are exhaustive only over their listed finite state spaces. Analytical proof is complete; priority and the proof have not received an independent mathematical audit.
