For the cyclotomic recurrence of squarefree index \(pq\), where \(p<q\) are distinct odd primes, all modular states have cycles of lengths only \(1,p,q,pq\), with an explicit full census at every modulus. The short cycles are controlled by elementary Smith factors \(p\) and \(q\), the first two-odd-prime case beyond prime-power indices.
