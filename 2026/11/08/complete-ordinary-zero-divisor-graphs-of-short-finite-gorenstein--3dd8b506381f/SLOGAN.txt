For every finite local Gorenstein ring with radical-cube zero and two-dimensional radical quotient, the entire ordinary zero-divisor graph is determined by its residue-field order and the isotropic-line count of the multiplication form: a universal socle clique joined to an explicit disjoint collection of cliques and bicliques. The classification yields exact chromatic, spectral, and graph-automorphism formulas and shows that even distinct ring characteristics can produce the same graph.
