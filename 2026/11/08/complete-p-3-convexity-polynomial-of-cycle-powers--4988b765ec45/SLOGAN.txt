For every integer \(r\ge2\), the proper \(P_3\)-convex vertex sets of a cycle power \(C_n^r\) are exactly the cyclic packings whose selected vertices are pairwise at least \(2r+1\) apart in the original cycle. This yields the complete two-parameter polynomial \(1+nz+z^n+\sum_{k=2}^{\lfloor n/(2r+1)\rfloor}\frac{n}{k}\binom{n-2rk-1}{k-1}z^k\).
