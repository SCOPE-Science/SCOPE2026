The complete additive pair-sum multiplicity spectrum of the inverse hyperbola in \((\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z)^2\), for odd \(p\), is determined exactly by valuation strata and quadratic roots. Its unweighted two-frequency Kloosterman fourth moment, normalized by the number of frequencies, equals \(\varphi(p^k)^2\left(3k-3/(p-1)\right)\); the representation spectrum and moments extend multiplicatively to every odd modulus.
