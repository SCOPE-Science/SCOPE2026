Every factor of the binary period-doubling word of length \(n\ge1\) is a concatenation of two palindromes exactly when \(n\) is \(2^j\), \(3\cdot2^j\), or \(5\cdot2^j\) for some \(j\ge0\). All length-\(7\cdot2^j\) factors are palindromic periodicities, but an explicit morphic factor at each such length is not a palindrome pair.
