# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The stated domain is all odd finite fields and unrestricted ordered two-by-two matrices. The proof partitions the first matrix into the zero, nonzero trace-zero, singular nonzero-trace, and invertible nonzero-trace cases, obtains corresponding ranks 0, 2, 3, and 4, and counts admissible projections by rational eigenlines of the target. The seven output strata sum to all targets, and their weighted fibers sum to all ordered pairs. Literal direct product enumeration over F3 and F5 and a separate F9 finite-field solver corroborate the proof; they are not an infinite proof. No characteristic-two or higher-size conclusion is made.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)
Bourgeois's 2012 full preprint studies the same Sylvester map but determinant/rank/solvability rather than prescribed finite-field output multiplicities. Kaylor--Offner 2014 establishes finite-field similarity-class counts that are an ingredient, not the target-fiber theorem. A 2021 MathOverflow discussion gives the **previously known** all-size anticommuting (zero-target) count; this component is credited, not claimed new. Claim-level comparisons to accessible previous research records found no same seven-target distribution; related factorization counts restrict their input pairs and use different maps. Broader general results might imply the nonzero target counts, and exhaustive historical priority is not claimed.

## Value — PASS
The exact count for every prescribed output is a natural and meaningful refinement of a zero-anticommutator count and generic Sylvester solvability. Five different nonscalar target classes have distinct fiber populations controlled by rational eigenspace geometry; the theorem also enumerates the number of targets in each class, yielding a complete distribution over all fields in the stated family. This is not a claim of extension to larger matrices or of an independent certificate.
