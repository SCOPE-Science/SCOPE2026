For every odd finite field, the prescribed anticommutator fibers \(\#\{(A,B)\in M_2(\mathbb F_q)^2:AB+BA=C\}\) are determined exactly by whether \(C\) is scalar and by its rational eigenlines and zero-eigenvalue lines. The five nonscalar target strata have explicit, unequal populations, refining the previously studied zero-target anticommuting-pair count.
