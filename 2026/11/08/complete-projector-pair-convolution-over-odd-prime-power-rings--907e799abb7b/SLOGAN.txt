Over every odd prime-power ring \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) with \(k\ge2\), the complete ordered sum of two rank-one \(2\times2\) idempotents has an exact valuation-sensitive fiber formula and covers the entire trace-two hyperplane. Its sharp mixing floor is independent of depth, and its unweighted fourth Fourier moment is exactly \(p^{5k-3}(p^3+7p^2+5p-1)-3p^{4k-2}(p+1)\).
