The half-quotient of every odd-base interval numerical semigroup \(\langle a,a+1,\ldots,a+k\rangle\), with \(3\le k\le a-2\), has an explicit disjoint-block gapset and a complete pseudo-Frobenius spectrum: its final gap block plus at most one parity-governed extra gap. The resulting genus, Frobenius number, and type are exact for the entire two-parameter family.
