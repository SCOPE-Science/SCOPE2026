For the union of all \(\mathbb F_q\)-rational hyperplanes in projective space, the local F-pure threshold depends only on the \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear rank \(r\) of a point's coordinates: it equals \(q^{-(N-r-1)}\) on the divisor. Every Frobenius-level exponent and the number of points with each threshold over every finite extension have closed formulas.
