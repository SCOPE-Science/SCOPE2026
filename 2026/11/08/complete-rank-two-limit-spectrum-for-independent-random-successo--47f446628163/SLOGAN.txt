For a fixed number \(r\) of independent uniform linear-order successor relations, all \(r(r-1)\) oriented pairwise overlap counts converge to independent Poisson-one variables. The complete quantifier-rank-two first-order limit-probability spectrum in the pure successor language is \(\{\sum_{k=0}^{N}a_k(1-e^{-1})^ke^{-(N-k)}:0\le a_k\le\binom Nk\}\) with \(N=r(r-1)\), and it has exactly \(\prod_{k=0}^{N}(1+\binom Nk)\) distinct values.
