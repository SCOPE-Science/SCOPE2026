The full dihedral group of the eleven-vertex Grötzsch graph acts on the third rational homology of its ordinary matching complex as \(2\mathbb Q[D_5]\oplus\mathbb Q(\zeta_5)\), with the cyclotomic factor carrying reflection by complex conjugation. This determines every symmetry trace and shows that its full dihedral quotient has exactly two independent degree-three rational homology classes and no other positive-degree rational homology.
