# Scientific review: rational icosahedral matching-homology character

## Correctness — PASS
The graph, matching-complex faces, full group, orientation character, signed simplicial action, and all numerical ranks are specified explicitly. An exhaustive exact verifier independently enumerates 5,887 nonempty faces and certifies mod-two boundary ranks \((29,286,1114,1357,125)\). The rational ranks cannot exceed these values because the augmented chain dimensions and \(\partial^2=0\) force equality, establishing that only reduced rational degree-three homology survives, of dimension \(64\). Face propagation generates all 120 graph automorphisms, and the verifier independently computes their signed Lefschetz character, as well as 2,775,600 signed boundary-equivariance checks. The central involution has trace \(16\); complete character inner products yield \(40\) antipodal-even and \(24\) antipodal-odd dimensions with the stated irreducible multiplicities. The finite-group averaging argument for rational orbit cohomology then applies. No integral torsion or quotient-space homotopy type is inferred from rational data.

## Originality — PASS relative to the evidence inspected
The closest same-object icosahedral matching-complex record (`28565ad1ea1a`, inspected full `RESULT.md` and certificate methodology) proves a nonequivariant integral homotopy type, but gives neither the homology action of \(A_5\times C_2\) nor the antipodal trace. None of these statements implies the finite icosahedral action. The public research-index searches of synonyms, representation parameters, dual descriptions and stronger generality returned no precise covering statement. An equivalent unindexed calculation remains possible, so publication priority is not certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The icosahedral matching complex is a particularly motivated torsion-bearing example, and its full \(120\)-element symmetry group is a canonical test of equivariant topological information invisible to the previously known nonequivariant homotopy type. The explicit full rational module distinguishes the even and odd antipodal summands (dimensions \(40\) and \(24\)), gives the exact multiplicities of three irreducible rational rotation modules, and proves that both the rotation and full-symmetry quotients have trivial positive-degree rational cohomology. The claim resolves a natural missing symmetry invariant of a specific prominent example rather than a cosmetic numerical variation. It does not purport to classify integral representation lattices, torsion-module actions or general families.
