The icosahedral graph matching complex has a \(64\)-dimensional third rational homology module whose antipodal-even and antipodal-odd parts are \(2V_4\oplus4V_5\oplus2W_6\) and \(2V_4\oplus2V_5\oplus W_6\), respectively, under the rotation group \(A_5\). Neither part has rotational invariants, so the full icosahedral symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic despite the original complex's nonzero homology.
