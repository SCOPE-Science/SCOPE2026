For the 24-vertex Nauru graph, the full \(S_4\times S_3\) action on the scale-three Vietoris–Rips complex has explicitly decomposed rational homology in degrees \(3,4,5\), including one globally fixed degree-three class and a top-degree sign character. The classification refines the previously established nonequivariant join-of-two-tori homotopy description.
