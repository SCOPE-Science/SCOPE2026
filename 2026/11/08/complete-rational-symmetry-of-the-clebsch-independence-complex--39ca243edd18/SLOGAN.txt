For the five-regular Clebsch graph, the full \(1920\)-element symmetry action on the nine-dimensional rational third homology of its independence complex splits as an irreducible four-dimensional sign-twisted standard module and an irreducible five-dimensional sign-twisted signed-direction module. An exact character formula identifies every automorphism's trace and shows that no nonzero homology class is invariant under all symmetries; the previously known nine-sphere homotopy type is not claimed as new.
