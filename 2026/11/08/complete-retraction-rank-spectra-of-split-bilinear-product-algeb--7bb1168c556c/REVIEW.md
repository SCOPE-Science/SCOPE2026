# Scientific review

## Correctness
The multiplication identities reduce every retraction to either the identity or a projection onto a totally isotropic subspace of the full nonsymmetric bilinear form, with a central graph shift. For an \(r\)-subspace the number of maps is exactly \(q^{r(2n-r+1)}\). The generic case uses both orderings of the pairing to force orthogonality of the two coordinate projections, followed by the vector-space Goursat lemma. The two degenerate parameter regimes use symplectic and hyperbolic-orthogonal frame counts. Direct subspace and map enumerations over four small fields match the formulas. Correctness: PASS.

Anil Kumar–Pradhan, arXiv:1908.00331 (2019-08-01), counts symplectic spaces and group endomorphisms, covering the familiar alternating/Heisenberg geometry but not nonsymmetric bilinear-product associative retractions. The previously studied Heisenberg Lie retraction spectrum also covers the odd-characteristic alternating subcase and is not claimed to be new. Generic nonsymmetric and all-phase associative distribution were not identified in the inspected literature or mathematical-results comparisons. Priority risk remains if a broader published bilinear-form enumeration dominates the formula. Originality: PASS relative to inspected sources.
