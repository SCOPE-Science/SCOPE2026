# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness

For the split nonunital algebra \(A=V\oplus kz\) with multiplication \(uv=B_\lambda(u,v)z\), the square ideal is \(kz\). Every algebra endomorphism has the block form \(P(v+sz)=Tv+(\ell(v)+\alpha s)z\), satisfying the two-sided bilinear homomorphism equation, \(T^2=T\), \(\alpha^2=\alpha\) and \(\ell T+\alpha\ell=\ell\). If \(\alpha=1\), nondegeneracy forces the identity. Otherwise, \(T\) projects onto a totally \(B_\lambda\)-isotropic \(r\)-plane and \(\ell\) factors through \(T\). Each such plane gives exactly \(q^{r(2n-r+1)}\) retractions.

For \(\lambda^2\ne1\), both ordered products enforce perpendicularity of the two coordinate projections, and vector-space Goursat data enumerate their common quotient. The exceptional cases are the standard hyperbolic symmetric form when \(q\) is odd and \(\lambda=1\), and the symplectic form when \(\lambda=-1\), including characteristic two. This establishes the stated rank gap and all-phase formulas for every prime-power field. Independent complete small-dimensional RREF and direct matrix-map checks over \(\mathbb F_3,\mathbb F_4,\mathbb F_5,\mathbb F_7\) reproduce the counts; finite tests are corroboration, not the all-dimension proof.

## Relationship to earlier mathematics

Mainellis, *Associative Algebras with Small Derived Ideal* (arXiv:2212.01425, Theorem 3.1.2), already classifies the relevant extra-special associative central sums. Anil Kumar and Pradhan (arXiv:1908.00331, Theorems Ω and 5.2) supply classical symplectic endomorphism and isotropic-space counts in the related extra-special-group setting. The previously published *Heisenberg Lie retractions: isotropic affine strata and a rank-dominance transition* proves the same numerical retraction formula for the alternating phase, while *Endomorphism rank spectra and an automorphism-density transition in alternating two-step algebras* studies the same alternating associative form. These components are not claimed as new.

Facchini and Heidari Zadeh, *Algebras with a bilinear form, and Idempotent endomorphisms* (arXiv:2210.08230), establish categorical equivalences for unital weakly augmented algebras, not the present complete nonunital rank distribution. Gluesing-Luerssen and Ravagnani (arXiv:2212.07777) count isotropic subspaces of symmetric finite-field forms, but not the generic nonsymmetric associative retractions. The previously published one-dimensional-square evolution-algebra retraction theorem assumes a different multiplication with the square generator inside its nondegenerate bilinear space. The distinctive surviving content is the explicit generic two-sided Goursat rank spectrum together with an exact phase comparison. Equivalently formulated older results may exist, so priority is best of knowledge.

## Mathematical interest and limits

The full rank spectrum of algebra retracts is a natural invariant. It identifies a forbidden interval of proper ranks and distinguishes generic nonsymmetric, symmetric, and alternating algebra structures over all finite fields. This is a complete calculation for one coherent central-sum family, not a result about all extra-special associative algebras or the orbit structure of retractions. Known special cases and classical enumerative ingredients retain their original priority. No formal proof-assistant check, human expert attestation or external certification is asserted.
