For split two-step associative algebras defined by a nondegenerate bilinear pairing, the complete idempotent-endomorphism rank spectrum has three explicit finite-field phases: generic nonsymmetric, split symmetric, and alternating. Proper retracts are always square-zero of rank at most half the noncentral dimension, and the generic maximum-rank count is governed by a sum of Gaussian binomials rather than the symplectic product.
