For categorical subcubes on any finite product of alphabets, a support of size at least three is shattered exactly when every point is a unique coordinate outlier; this gives an explicit all-degree shattering polynomial and a maximal-support count of \(\prod_i q_i(q_i-1)\) in dimension at least three. Uniform random samples obey a universal \(\exp(-e^{-c})\) critical shattering law even when coordinate alphabet sizes vary arbitrarily.
