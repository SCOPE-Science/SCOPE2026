# Mathematical review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof reduces arbitrary labeling extension to an independent set of local positive or neighboring witnesses, proves the sharp forbidden cyclic-gap obstruction by forced domination, and constructs compatible witnesses blockwise using long gaps as separators. It then counts all labeled supports by rooting long gaps and accounting for cyclic overcount. Finite direct enumeration through length eighteen checks every support and every coefficient but is not the infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS under accessible comparisons.** The full primary text of Bisdorff–Marichal treats Perrin counts and dihedral/orbit enumeration of maximal independent sets, not traces on arbitrary queried vertex subsets. Guingona–Kolesnikov–Nierwinski–Soucy treat zero sets of linear combinations. The general bounded-cycle maximal-independent-set extremal paper counts concepts rather than shattered supports. Semantic searches of available finding records and deduplication against accessible earlier claims did not identify the present cyclic forbidden-gap support characterization or its complete polynomial. This is evidence of noncoverage in searched records, not a guarantee of worldwide novelty.

## Value
**PASS.** The result identifies every shattered face of a natural nonhereditary concept class, expresses all coefficient counts for arbitrary cycle length, and determines its top-dimensional multiplicity in a sharp congruence class. The distinction between concept counting (Perrin sequence) and trace counting is substantive; a cycle endpoint closure changes the witness constraints relative to a path. The full classification also allows direct computations beyond just the VC dimension.

## Closest literature and risks
The 2007 Bisdorff–Marichal paper is closest at the level of objects and the length-two/length-three gap representation. Earlier path maximal-independent-set shattering results concern a different concept class. A priority risk remains under independent domination, cyclic constraint-satisfaction, or binary trace nomenclature. This review is not independent validation.
