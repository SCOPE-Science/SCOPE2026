The maximal-independent-set concept class of a labeled cycle shatters a vertex support exactly when its cyclic gaps avoid adjacency and all runs \(2,3,\ldots,3,2\). This yields a complete binomial-sum shattering polynomial, \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\lfloor(n+1)/3\rfloor\), and exactly \(n\) maximum supports when \(n=3k-1\).
