For every positive integer \(m\), the harmonic Bergman Toeplitz self-commutator \([T_{\overline z^{\,m}},T_{z^m}]\) has an explicit, paired positive/negative eigenvalue list, exact norm \(m^2/((m+1)(2m+1))\), and exact trace norm \(m(5m+1)/(3(m+1))\). Its singular values satisfy \(s_n\sim4m^2/n^2\), giving the sharp Schatten threshold \(p>1/2\) and a weak but not strong endpoint at \(p=1/2\).
