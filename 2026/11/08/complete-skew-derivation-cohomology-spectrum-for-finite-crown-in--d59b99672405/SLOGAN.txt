For every finite-field incidence algebra of a crown with \(m\ge3\), the dimension of outer automorphism-twisted derivations is exactly \(0\), \(1\), \(2\), or \(m\): adjacent rotations contribute \(m\), reflections contribute \(2\), and identity twists are controlled by one multiplicative holonomy. A random automorphism has cohomology dimension converging in law to equal masses at \(0\) and \(2\), but its expected dimension tends to \(2\), not \(1\), because rare adjacent rotations have unbounded cohomology dimension.
