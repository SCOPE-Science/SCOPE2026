For every prime \(p\) and every depth \(k\ge3\), the inner-rank-one Cayley graph on \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices over \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) has an explicit complete Fourier spectrum with Smith-type multiplicities. Its adjacency operator is always invertible, and its smallest absolute eigenvalue is exactly \(p+1\), independent of depth; the extremal frequency characters are precisely the matrices invertible modulo \(p\).
