For the fair cyclic random Fibonacci tiling in every fixed dimension \(d\ge2\), the full family of subsequential laws of the centered point-covering time \(T(a)-B(a)\) is classified by admissible integer phase offsets and independently chosen parameters \(\kappa_r\in[1/2,1]\). Every member occurs, and all dyadic interior parameter families can even be realized exactly at infinitely many increasing target distances.
