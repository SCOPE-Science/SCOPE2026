For the positive-area wandering rectangles in the Nakano–Soma–Yamamoto expanding planar maps, the empirical distribution of successive normalized finite-time Lyapunov exponents has an explicit one-parameter family of subsequential probability laws, rather than a unique limit. At completed geometric blocks its CDF is \(4\gamma t/[(\gamma^2-1)(1-t^2)]\) for \(0\leq t\leq(\gamma-1)/(\gamma+1)\), whereas a natural intra-block phase has a strictly different law.
