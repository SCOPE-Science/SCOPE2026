Over \(\mathbb F_3\), exactly three projective classes of smooth plane cubics are tangent-filling, with \(4\), \(6\), and \(7\) rational points and respective equation-orbit sizes \(936\), \(1872\), \(936\). These exhaust \(3744\) of \(16848\) smooth cubic equations, an exact proportion \(2/9\), and settle the minimum tangent-filling degree as \(3\).
