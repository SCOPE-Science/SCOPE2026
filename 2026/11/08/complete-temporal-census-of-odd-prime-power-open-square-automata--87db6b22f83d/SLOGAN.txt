On the open \((p^r-1)\times(p^r-1)\) square grid in odd characteristic \(p\), synchronous neighbor summation has exactly the Jordan blocks \(J_{2j-1}(4)\), \(J_{2j-1}(-4)\), and two copies of \(J_{2j-1}(0)\) for \(1\le j\le(p^r-1)/2\). This yields closed counts for every first-periodic-entry time and primitive orbit period, a sharp maximal entry time \(p^r-2\), and an attained maximal temporal period controlled by the orders of \(4\) and \(-4\) modulo \(p\).
