# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The trace identity follows from the cubic minimal polynomial and first three Perrin terms. The exact identity \(\alpha^{13}=7+12\alpha+9\alpha^2\) and the nonzero unit \(\beta=(\alpha^{13}-1)/3\bmod3\) imply exact order \(13\cdot3^{k-1}\). The discriminant \(-23\) makes the trace pairing invertible modulo \(3^k\), so sequence period equals element order. The displayed thirteen rows give all regular and singular branches; nonzero first differences prove 3-adic isometries; the singular branches are fixed modulo nine. These facts prove both universal coverage and optimal conditional minima. The standalone exact finite checker corroborates every arithmetic seed and small exponents, but is not invoked as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical-priority risk

The inspected Yilmaz–Taskara full paper (arXiv:1502.07919, initial public posting February 27, 2015) studies Perrin matrix binomial transformations rather than 3-adic residue-fiber minima. Jacobsen (2016) treats primality signatures and cubic traces. OEIS A104217 records some least periods, which are explicitly not novel here. The Bundschuh–Bundschuh 2011 and Lang–Lang 2013 residue-completeness investigations concern second-order Fibonacci/Lucas sequences, not the cubic Perrin trace orbit. Targeted mathematical research-index searches, including the explicit terms 'Perrin sequence modulo 3^k surjectivity congruence R_n=a count residue fibers', returned unrelated nearest results; separate published web searches for the same sequence and powers of three found no covering theorem. Search absence cannot establish priority; obscure cubic-recursion literature remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Perrin numbers have longstanding primality and modular-period applications. A value-level distribution theorem answers a different natural question than their period: does every residue occur, and how many times must each occur? The all-exponent, exact residue-conditioned minimum theorem supplies meaningful uniform arithmetic information. Its 3-adic branches also distinguish regular and singular behavior and suggest a path to full fiber distributions, which is not obtained here.
