For the classical Perrin recurrence, every residue modulo \(3^k\) appears in one least period of length \(13\cdot3^{k-1}\). From \(k=2\) onward, the exact minimum number of occurrences among residues congruent to \(0,1,2\pmod3\) is respectively \(3,2,4\), explained by nine isometric 3-adic branches and four singular branches.
