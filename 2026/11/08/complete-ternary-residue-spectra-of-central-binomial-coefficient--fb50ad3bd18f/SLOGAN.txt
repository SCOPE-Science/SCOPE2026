For \(3\nmid b\), the residues modulo three of \(\binom{2(bn+\beta)}{bn+\beta}\) as \(n\ge1\) range over all three classes exactly when \(b\) is odd; for even \(b\), exactly zero and the unique nonzero class \((-1)^\beta\) occur, each infinitely often. This supplies a complete progression classification and infinite prime-three counterexamples to Guo–Krattenthaler Conjecture 7.1.
