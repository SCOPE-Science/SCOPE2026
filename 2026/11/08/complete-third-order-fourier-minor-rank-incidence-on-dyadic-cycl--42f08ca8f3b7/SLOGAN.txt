Every third-order Fourier minor on \(\mathbb Z/2^k\mathbb Z\) has rank determined by just four dyadic valuations of its row and column triples. This yields exact all-\(k\) rank multiplicities and shows that a random three-by-three minor is singular with probability \(3/2^k+O(k^2/4^k)\), with rarer rank-one defects beginning at order sixteen.
