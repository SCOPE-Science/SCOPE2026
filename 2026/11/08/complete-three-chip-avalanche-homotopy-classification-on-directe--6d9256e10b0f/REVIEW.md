# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** Binary parallel firing shifts the whole initial support by one on a directed cycle. The proof turns the resulting orbit of three-subsets into a structural classification. Progression cases reduce to filled triangles, tetrahedral boundaries, or cycles. All other three-subsets have three distinct edge-difference classes and at least two free-edge classes; exactly one may be antipodal. Removing one full free-edge orbit leaves connected graphs with explicitly calculated cycle ranks. The proof handles all cycle sizes, disconnected cases and boundary cases. The accompanying exact standard-library verifier checks 9,139 normalized supports and separately checks dynamics and mod-two chain ranks.

**Originality — PASS, with residual uncertainty.** Riihimäki and Smith (arXiv:2606.26786v2) treat adjacent-chip support in Proposition 3.34 and consecutive support in Theorem 3.32; those are acknowledged as known. The genuinely new content here is the general nonadjacent, nonprogression classification and the unified gcd–antipodal structural dichotomy. Adamaszek et al. (arXiv:1410.4336) cover circular-arc nerves (the progression subfamily) but not generic larger-rank circle bouquets. Searches of indexed mathematical results and accessible previous claims found no full classification. Equivalent unindexed work is an unresolved priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is the complete first nontrivial three-grain stratum of the recent avalanche homology construction, motivated directly by Question 3.36 of the introducing paper. The sharp change from isolated points and two-spheres to high-rank circle bouquets is not a routine numerical restatement; it records the topology for every placement of three grains and has integral, all-parameter content.

**Limitations.** No classification is claimed for four or more grains, other graph orientations, or persistence barcodes. Finite tests are corroborative only. Independent mathematical validation has not occurred.

**Primary sources.** Riihimäki–Smith, arXiv:2606.26786v1 and v2; Adamaszek–Adams–Frick–Peterson–Previte-Johnson, arXiv:1410.4336. A separate structured review record describes equivalent formulations, broader implications, and residual novelty uncertainty.
