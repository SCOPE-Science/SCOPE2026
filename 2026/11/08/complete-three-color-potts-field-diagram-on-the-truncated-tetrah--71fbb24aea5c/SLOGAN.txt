The three-color antiferromagnetic Potts model on the twelve-vertex truncated tetrahedron has exactly four color-selective critical fields, \(h/J=-1,1,2,3\), with critical ground-state degeneracies 1320, 984, 1824, and 1273. The result is an exhaustive exact finite-field classification, including a zero-field population of four favored-color spins and 120 proper three-colorings; it does not assert a thermodynamic phase transition.
