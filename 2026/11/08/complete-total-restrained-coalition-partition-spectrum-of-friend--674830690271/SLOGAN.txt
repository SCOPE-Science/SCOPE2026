Every total restrained coalition partition of the friendship graph \(F_m=K_1\vee(mK_2)\) with at least three blocks arises by partitioning whole triangular blades around the singleton apex, so its coalition graph is a star. The exact block-count census is \(c_m(2)=4^m-2^m+1\) and \(c_m(k)=S(m,k-1)\) for \(3\le k\le m+1\), giving a unique maximum partition when \(m\ge2\) and a total of \(4^m-2^m+B_m\) partitions.
