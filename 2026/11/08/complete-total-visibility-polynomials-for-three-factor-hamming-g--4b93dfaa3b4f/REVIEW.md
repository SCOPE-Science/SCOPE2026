# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The exact-distance-two criterion is reconstructed using Cartesian-square necessity and an interval-cube geodesic induction. The layer classification gives a disjoint, exhaustive decomposition: one occupied layer, one binary group across several layers, or two binary groups classified by their layer multiplicities. Every counting factor and its boundary ranges are derived explicitly. Exhaustive distance-two independent-set enumeration matches all coefficients for eleven dimension pairs, including boundary consistency tests, and literal shortest-path testing agrees for all 4,352 subsets in the two smallest profiles. Finite tests are not used to claim the general theorem.

**Originality (PASS, provisional).** The closely related 2023 Hamming-graph theorem already establishes the distance-two criterion and the maximum cardinality, so neither is claimed as new. The 2024 preprint introducing visibility polynomials defines the total polynomial and treats other graph families; the inspected full text contains no Hamming-family coefficient formula. The accessible product literature treats bounds and extremal numbers rather than the complete enumeration here. The disjoint-layer decomposition and exact maximum-set multiplicities in the genuinely two-free-factor regime \(b,n\ge3\) were not found in source and claim comparisons. The known \(K_2\square K_2\square K_n\) complete enumerator is excluded from the novelty claim. Residual risk: an equivalent independence polynomial of the distance-two Hamming graph may appear in older coding-theory or graph-enumeration literature.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** Hamming total visibility is tied to forbidden-distance codes and a published extremal problem. The theorem refines a scalar extremal value to all coefficients for a two-parameter infinite family, gives a constructive classification of all feasible sets, and identifies exact maximum-set multiplicities for both large factor dimensions. This is a natural enumeration question rather than an arbitrary parameter slice: the three-factor Hamming products with one binary factor and two independently varying dimensions support an exact, symmetric, all-cardinality solution.

**Closest results and limits.** Bujtás--Klavžar--Tian, *Total mutual-visibility in Hamming graphs*, Theorems 1, 6, 9; Bujtás--Klavžar--Tian, *Visibility polynomials, dual visibility spectrum, and characterization of total mutual-visibility sets*, Section 3; Kuziak--Rodríguez-Velázquez, *Total Mutual-Visibility in Graphs with Emphasis on Lexicographic and Cartesian Products*, Section 5. This work does not claim those earlier feasibility or extremal results, does not classify all maximal sets, and has not received independent audit. Search noncoverage is not novelty certification.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
