For all \(b,n\ge3\), the entire total-visibility polynomial of \(K_2\square K_b\square K_n\) has an explicit binomial expression obtained by classifying occupied layers and their disjoint coordinate supports. The maximum cardinality \(b+n-2\) was known, but the complete coefficient distribution and the exact count \(2bn\) of maximum sets refine it; the binary--binary-factor boundary is not claimed as new.
