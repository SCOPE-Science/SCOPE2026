Among the \(2925\) unordered triples of the Fermat cubic's \(27\) hyperosculating conics, exactly \(27\) have common points: nine tangent triples and eighteen transverse triples; no other triple and no fourfold intersection occurs. Their complete union has \(1242\) nodes, \(36\) ordinary triple points and \(9\) common-tangent triple points, certified by exhaustive symmetry-orbit analysis and two exact finite-characteristic exclusions.
