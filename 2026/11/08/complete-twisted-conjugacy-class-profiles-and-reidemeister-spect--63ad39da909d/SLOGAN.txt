For every odd prime \(p\) and \(d\ge2\), all Reidemeister classes of every automorphism of \(\mathbb F_p^d\rtimes C_2\) have an explicit two-coset size profile. Every allowable pair of \(\pm1\)-eigenspace dimensions occurs, yielding \(\operatorname{Spec}_R(G)=\{1+(p^a+p^c)/2:a+c\le d\}\) with precisely \(\lfloor(d+2)^2/4\rfloor\) distinct values.
