For every binary field \(\mathbb F_q\), the ordered sum of two rank-one Hermitian projectors has exactly three convolution values: \(q(q-1)\) at \(0\) and \(I_2\), \(q\) at each nonscalar trace-zero matrix for which both \(S\) and \(S+I_2\) are invertible, and zero elsewhere. Consequently its sumset has precisely \(q^3-2q^2-q+4\) elements, with exact additive energy \(q^2(q^3-5q+4)\).
