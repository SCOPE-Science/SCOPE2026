For spanning-tree frequencies of every \(K_{2,n}\), the Walsh transform has an explicit three-case formula, exponentially sparse support, and a sharp Fourier projection norm \(\frac n2+\frac{n}{2^n}\binom{n-1}{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\). The norm has a square-root correction to its linear growth and an exact parity-dependent increment law.
