The classical antiferromagnetic Ising model on the 30-site icosidodecahedron has exactly \(3{,}251{,}250\) zero-field ground states, with a complete eleven-sector magnetization polynomial, compared with the previously known \(36\) perfect-matching states throughout \(0<|h|<4J\). At the sharp saturation field \(|h|=4J\), every dodecahedral matching is allowed, giving exactly \(111{,}376\) ground states and a matching-size-resolved magnetization polynomial.
