The first verifier enumerates all ten-site inner colorings and applies a complete min-plus recurrence along the outer ten-cycle, retaining exact tie multiplicities. The ground-energy envelope follows from the exact finite sector minimum, with no infinite-size extrapolation.

PASS on inspected evidence, subject to historical-priority risk. The 2015 regular-polyhedra reference addresses two-state Ising spins, rather than the three-state model. An unindexed prior graph-specific numerical or transfer-matrix table cannot be excluded.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The exact one-color-field response on a canonical Platonic spin cluster provides a natural finite-geometry comparison between proper-coloring phases and field-driven improper configurations. Its asymmetric negative and positive field thresholds, occupancy plateaux, and exceptionally large critical coexistence multiplicities are not determined by the already-tabulated zero-field chromatic polynomial. The result is a complete natural classification for the fixed model, without asserting an infinite-family theorem.

## Limitations
This is a zero-temperature, fixed-size classification for three labeled colors and homogeneous couplings. It does not compute finite-temperature partition functions or establish thermodynamic-limit phase transitions.
