# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS
A diagonal affine normalization reduces arbitrary positive box half-lengths to the cube. The key noncentral three-cube plane-section lemma is proved using unique edge directions and the pairing of opposite support edges under central symmetry; boundedness forces two active coordinates, and two active coordinate pairs force a dominating remaining coefficient. Applying this lemma to the largest-coordinate facet proves the full-support necessity. Dominance gives an explicit affine-cube parameterization, while a zero normal coordinate yields a segment product of a centrally symmetric planar polygon. This handles strict inequality, equality, and all zero-coordinate cases. The classical implication from zonoid to centrally symmetric exposed facets is sufficient; no unproved converse is needed for necessity. The exact-rational checker corroborates 1295 finite cases but is not the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority
The primary predecessor (Anantharaman, 2019, DOI 10.32219/isms.81.3_315) openly asks for complete characterization of which central cube slices are zonoids, supplies partial examples, and specifically leaves a six-sided-face case unresolved. The present equivalence fully characterizes all central sections of four-boxes, including support degeneracies. A distinct public research result gives the generic two-point face-vector law; it does not characterize central symmetry of the facets, and its eight-facet value also occurs for zonotopal hexagonal prisms. Neither comparison implies this theorem. Searches for dominance, cubical-section zonotopes, and alternate facet-symmetry formulations found no covering theorem. The historical risk is an unindexed general result making the geometric classification immediate; full archival priority is not established.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result closes a concrete, explicitly stated convex-geometric classification gap, not an arbitrary finite exercise. It gives a closed-form necessary-and-sufficient test for every section normal, identifies the exact transition from parallelotopes to non-zonoids in the full-support regime, and resolves the otherwise ambiguous six-sided-facet cases. Its complete boundary treatment is needed because vanishing normal coordinates produce genuinely zonotopal hexagonal prisms even without a dominant coefficient.

## Scope and risk
Only central hyperplane sections of four-dimensional boxes are covered; the proof uses positivity of the three coefficients in the obstructing planar facet. Earlier equivalent statements in nonindexed polytope or Banach-space literature remain possible. The checker verifies exact rational samples, not all real parameters.
