## correctness

PASS

In any strict vertex order, the first two distinct parts A,B suffice for every uniquely-earlier-neighbor gradient arc. After an earlier vertex of a third part C appears, a later vertex in each part has at least two earlier neighbors across different parts, so no arc from C or later A/B can occur. Conversely extend any order on A union B by appending all other vertices, which have >=4 earlier neighbors and introduce no arcs. Each pair subcomplex equals full SM(K_{ni,nj}); pair vertex sets consist of different directed graph arcs and are disjoint except the abstract empty face. The known bipartite star/flap collapsing model is a doubled complete bipartite graph with 2ni nj edges and ni+nj vertices, wedge rank 2ni nj-ni-nj+1. Components indexed by unordered part pairs give H0 augmentation kernel under graph automorphisms. Fresh enumeration of all orders in sizes (2,2,2), (2,2,3), and (2,2,2,2) reproduced exact pair support, counts and Euler ranks; the unrestricted proof is structural.
