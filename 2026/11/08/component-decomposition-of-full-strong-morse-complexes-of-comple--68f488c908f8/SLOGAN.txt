For all complete multipartite graphs with at least three parts of size at least two, the full strong discrete-Morse matching complex splits into exactly one connected component for every unordered pair of parts. Each component is a wedge of \(2n_i n_j-n_i-n_j+1\) circles, so a connected graph gives a disconnected strong matching complex with a canonical integral permutation-augmentation action on its reduced zero-dimensional homology.
