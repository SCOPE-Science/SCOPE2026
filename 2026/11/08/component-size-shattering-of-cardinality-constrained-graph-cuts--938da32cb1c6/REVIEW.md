Title or keyword differences alone are not treated as proof of novelty; the possibility of earlier equivalent results under terminology such as balanced cuts or restricted cut vectors remains a risk.

**Value — PASS.** Exact all-support shattering for a natural constrained binary concept family is more informative than a scalar VC dimension. The result compresses arbitrary forest shapes to integer partitions and therefore counts every shattered face, while the stable-range theorem determines the exact capacity for infinitely many \(n,k\). The small-forest result explains which combinatorial query sets are universally feasible under a global shore-size constraint. It is not presented as an optimal running-time result or as a complete closed form for all intermediate VC dimensions.
