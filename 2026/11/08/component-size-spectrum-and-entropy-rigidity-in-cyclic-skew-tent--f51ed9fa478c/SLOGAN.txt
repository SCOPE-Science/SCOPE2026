For the cyclic product of \(n\) skew-tent maps with \(k\) chaotic bands, a Möbius–necklace law determines every trapping-component length and its exact stationary selection mass. Even though these masses and lengths can differ, every stationary component has the same entropy and all \(n\) Lyapunov exponents coincide; shorter components have exponentially small total mass in the natural high-dimensional regime.
