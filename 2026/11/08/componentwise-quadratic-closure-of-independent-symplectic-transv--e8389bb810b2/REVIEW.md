# Scientific review: Componentwise quadratic closure of independent symplectic transvections

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof identifies symplectic Lie endomorphisms with the quadratic tensors \(\operatorname{Sym}^2 V\) in characteristic different from two. Its explicit bracket formula builds every mixed tensor along each edge of a connected nonorthogonality graph. Orthogonal graph components commute and yield linearly independent tensor subspaces, giving the exact all-component dimension law. For a symplectic vector basis, nondegeneracy forces even component sizes, and explicit nondegenerate path blocks realize every even partition. Direct matrix-commutator tests independently verify 3,744 cases. The proof covers all allowed fields; computations do not certify infinite quantifiers.

**Originality — qualified PASS.** In 't Panhuis, Postma and Roozemond already establish extremal generators associated with one connected path for the symplectic Lie algebra; their path construction and minimal generator count are not presented as new. Cuypers–Fleischmann establish the rank-one extremal framework and analyze a degenerate three-space. Draisma–in 't Panhuis classify families of graph-parametrized extremal Lie algebras for selected Dynkin diagrams. Chistopolskaya handles unrestricted nilpotent generators, and Choudhary–Kishnani–Singh address abstract two-generation. The all-independent-family tensor-closure identity, exact component dimension formula, and full-basis dimension spectrum were not identified in those examined results or in relevant searches. Risk remains of a historical equivalent formulation under extremal/infinitesimal transvection or quadratic Hamiltonian terminology; the newest 2026 motivating full paper could not be inspected.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result supplies a complete dimension and structural classification for every independent system of constrained, rank-one nilpotent symplectic generators, including degenerate subspaces. It gives a sharp connectedness test for maximal generation, and a complete attainable dimension spectrum for symplectic bases uniformly over odd-characteristic finite fields and other fields of characteristic different from two. These are natural generation invariants in the context of recent finite-field Lie-algebra generation work, not an arbitrary small instance.

Limitations: characteristic two and dependent vector families are excluded; no discrete transvection-group result or priority certification is asserted. Earlier graph-extremal literature is a genuine originality caveat. Independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
