For a symmetric run-and-tumble diffusion, the polarity marks on the *first eventual visits to opposite sides* are exactly independent after fixing the initial polarity, despite being generated by one trajectory. For an unbiased initial polarity and equally distant targets, their raw signed correlation changes sign exactly once at \(\ell_*=(D/\sqrt{v^2+D\alpha})\log(1+\sqrt{v^2+D\alpha}/v)\), while the ordinary covariance remains positive.
