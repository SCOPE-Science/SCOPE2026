# Scientific review: nonbinary two-deletion spheres

**Correctness — PASS.** Every ordered distinct \(n-2\)-subsequence is assigned a unique right-greedy embedding. Counting its four possible two-deletion shapes establishes the pointwise identity for all \(n\ge3\), and the empty-output case covers \(n=2\). Uniform independent alphabet symbols induce independent uniform increments in \(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\); conditional on the transition pattern, all eligible adjacent return indicators are independent Bernoulli with parameter \(1/(q-1)\). The exact conditional binomial law follows. Orthogonal centered Bernoulli monomials yield the stated mean and variance, with boundary and binary specialization checked algebraically. Exact exhaustive enumeration compared 9,240 words, 18 finite laws and 38 binary specializations with the formulas. Residual correctness risk is primarily a missed argument in the right-greedy characterization, rather than sampling error; the proof supplies a direct case split and exhaustive small-case cross-check.

**Originality — PASS against inspected evidence.** The 2026 nonbinary deletion-cardinality paper treats extremal and run-restricted balls, not second moments of uniform random inputs. The 2008 paper on explicit small-deletion cardinality formulas is a particularly close *pointwise* antecedent and must not be mistaken for novelty of the local counting identity. The published binary two-deletion variance is a proper specialization of the new all-alphabet variance; it does not imply the nonbinary orientation-return term or exact conditional binomial mixture without additional analysis. The 2018 paper on random distinct subsequences treats the total count of all lengths and expectations, not this fixed-length random-variable distribution. The multiset-deletion paper uses unordered receipts. Searches of published research records for equivalent claims, aliases and dominating formulas did not identify the nonbinary conditional law. Residual priority risks are an implicit stochastic calculation in the older 2008 paper (full text unavailable) or an unindexed related result.

**Value — PASS.** The all-parameter exact distribution (not just a single extra table value) determines every finite moment and all tail probabilities of the number of distinct received words after exactly two deletions. The variance gives a quantitative dispersion benchmark for uniformly generated nonbinary deletion-channel inputs. Crucially the nonbinary conditional randomness is absent at the binary alphabet and explains the new additive term in the variance. No downstream theorem beyond this self-contained natural channel statistic is asserted.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Li–Wang–Fu, arXiv:2601.06493v1 (2026), treats extremal nonbinary deletion cardinalities; Mercier–Khabbazian–Bhargava, DOI:10.1109/TIT.2008.924730 (2008), gives prior small-deletion formulas; Biers-Ariel–Godbole–Kelley, arXiv:1704.08661, studies overall random subsequence counts; Kreindel–Essayag–Zabokritskiy, arXiv:2603.18322v1, treats multiset receipts. The theorem does not cover biased or dependent source symbols, more than two deletions, or unique parsing. Earlier implicit priority cannot be excluded; independent validation is unperformed.
