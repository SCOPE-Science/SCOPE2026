In uniform random recursive trees, the empirical number of leaf children conditioned on surviving at least \(h\ge2\) leaf-peeling rounds converges almost surely to an explicit Yule–Poisson mixture. As the fixed layer threshold subsequently tends to infinity, the mixture approaches \(\operatorname{Pois}(1)\) in total variation, so a deep-layer vertex has rank one with limiting probability \(1-e^{-1}\), rather than the unconditional \(e^{-1}\).
