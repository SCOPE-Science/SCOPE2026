For every rational Feres angle parameter \(\alpha=\pi/N\) with \(N\ge7\), a fixed-angle point-source circular billiard has a boundary position that equidistributes weakly exactly when \(\theta/\pi\) is irrational, although the full rational-parameter billiard is not mixing. The angular chain has an explicit sine-weighted stationary law and a sharp odd/even period dichotomy; each fixed nonzero spatial Fourier mode contracts exponentially.
