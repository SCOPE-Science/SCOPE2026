The dual hypersurface of every rational normal scroll \(S(1,n)\) has product normalization \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^n\) and conductor exactly the \((n-1)\)-st power of its singular linear-space ideal. When the characteristic divides \(n\), its generic transverse polar map is degree one rather than the prime-to-\(n\) generic degree \(n-1\) (a strict drop when \(n\ge3\)), while the full Jacobian algebra retains an explicit graded length.
