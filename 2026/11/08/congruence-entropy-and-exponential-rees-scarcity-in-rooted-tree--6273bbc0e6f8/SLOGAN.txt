Every finite outward rooted tree has an exact bivariate recurrence enumerating all graph inverse semigroup congruences by collapsed vertices and non-Rees choices. On complete \(b\)-ary trees with \(b\ge2\), the fraction of Rees congruences is \(\exp(-\kappa_b b^h+o(1))\) for an explicitly computable constant \(\kappa_b>0\), so Rees congruences are exponentially scarce in the number of vertices.
