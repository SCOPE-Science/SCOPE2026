For indicator windows of norm-one circles in \(\mathbb F_p^2\), the complete complex Gabor lifting rank is \(p^2(1+(p-\chi(-1))^2/2)\). The positive singular values, rescaled by \(p\), converge in distribution to the arcsine law on \((0,2)\), with the explicit two-point-intersection band carrying asymptotically all spectral mass.
