For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the probability that two random elements of \(\operatorname{UT}_4(\mathbb F_q)\) have commuting conjugates is exactly \((2q^5-3q^4+3q^3+2q^2-4q+1)/q^7\). It exceeds ordinary commuting probability by \((q-1)^3(q+1)(2q-1)/q^7\), exhibiting a characteristic-uniform strict gap at the first class-three unitriangular group.
