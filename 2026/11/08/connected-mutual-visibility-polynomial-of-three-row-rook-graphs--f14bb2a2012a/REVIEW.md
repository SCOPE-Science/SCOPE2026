# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The exact shortest-path geometry gives the all-four-corners forbidden rectangle criterion, and the induced graph is the line graph of the chosen incidence edges. At most one multirow column exists per row pair; hence a complete case split consists of zero, one, two, or three two-row columns, together with the disjoint three-row-column case. For each case the connectivity condition is explicitly proved necessary and sufficient. There is no finite-to-infinite extrapolation: the formula follows from unrestricted symbolic counting. The leading coefficient follows by maximizing selected cells in each case. Exhaustive vertex-subset checks for column counts one through five and further coefficient checks corroborate the proof.

## Originality (PASS, provisional priority)
The ordinary rook-graph mutual-visibility/no-rectangle equivalence is existing mathematics (Cicerone–Di Stefano–Klavžar and the 2026 Builder-Blocker article) and is used as a starting point. The complete cardinality polynomial requires additional connected-incidence classification not supplied by the published ordinary-visibility theorem, the 2026 game result, or related total-mutual-visibility work. The 2026 initiating connected-visibility preprint's public abstract lists general bounds, extremal characterizations, block formulas, and complexity, but not this enumerator; the full paper could not be accessed, so unrecognized overlap remains possible. Targeted web and mathematical-record searches returned no stronger covering claim. Absence of a search hit alone does not certify novelty.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
The exact distribution of sizes of connected mutual-visibility sets refines the newly introduced maximum parameter for a standard nontrivial two-factor Hamming graph family. A structural five-type theorem explains the generating function and the extremal multiplicity, and the result remains uniform for arbitrary column count. It is useful for quantitative comparisons between connected and ordinary mutual-visibility configurations, and for small-rank Hamming graph enumeration. This is a natural infinite enumeration, not an arbitrary finite table.
