Every complex symplectic restriction-rank locus \(X_{k,s}\subset\operatorname{Gr}(k,2m)\), where \(k\le m\) and \(\operatorname{rank}(\omega|_L)\le2s<k-1\), has conormal rank \(2\lfloor(k-2s)/2\rfloor\): its conormal matrices are precisely alternating forms on the radical of the restricted form. The first nonmaximal conormal rank locus is a Pfaffian divisor of degree \((k-2s)/2\) when the radical dimension is even at least four, but has codimension three and degree \((k-2s)((k-2s)^2-1)/24\) when that dimension is odd at least five.
