For the Parseval cyclic harmonic frame built from three consecutive Fourier characters, removing any three coefficients leaves a lower frame bound at least \(1-\frac3N[1+(b_N+\sqrt{b_N^2+8a_N^2})/2]\), with \(a_N=(1+2\cos(2\pi/N))/3\) and \(b_N=(1+2\cos(4\pi/N))/3\). Exactly the \(N\) cyclically consecutive three-position erasure masks attain this sharp bound for every \(N\ge6\).
