For a one-sink chain of \(k\ge2\) triangles, adding one grain at the distal tip yields the global maximum \(2k^2+k-1\) individual topplings with **exact probability \(1/9\)** under the uniform recurrent measure, independently of \(k\). The maximizing avalanche is characterized by just the last two triangular recurrence codes, while the exact mean is \((2k^2+k)/3\).
