The compact differential Hilbert operator between weighted Bergman Hilbert spaces admits explicit rank-\(O(L^2)\) approximants with error \(O(e^{-cL})\), hence \(s_N\le Ce^{-c\sqrt N}\). This yields membership in every \(S_p\) for \(p>0\), extending the known \(p\ge1\) criterion to all quasi-Schatten exponents.
