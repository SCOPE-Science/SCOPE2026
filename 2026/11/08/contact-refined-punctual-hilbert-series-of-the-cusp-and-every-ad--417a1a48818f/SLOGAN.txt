The contact-refined punctual Hilbert series of the cusp \(y^2=x^3\) is \(1+Qz+(Q^2+Q^3)(\mathbb Lz+z^2)+(1+\mathbb L)Q^4z^2/(1-Q)\). Together with the general fat-branch modification identity, this gives an explicit full contact-refined motivic series for every thickened union \(x^u(y^2-x^3)=0\), uniformly for all \(u\ge0\).
