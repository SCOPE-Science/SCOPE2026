At completed epochs of a scalar-affine SGD model, fresh reshuffling has lower risk than shuffle-once for **every convex loss** when the common sweep multiplier satisfies \(0<A<1\), but the complete convex-order comparison reverses when \(-1<A<0\). The sign switch is sharp, holds at every finite epoch and at stationarity, and can occur while all individual quadratic-gradient steps and the epoch map remain stable.
