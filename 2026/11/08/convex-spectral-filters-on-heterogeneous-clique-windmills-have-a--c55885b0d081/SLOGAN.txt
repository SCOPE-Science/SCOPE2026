For any nonnegative convex spectral filter \(f\) with \(f(0)=1\), a graph formed by joining one apex to an arbitrary disjoint union of cliques has entrywise nonnegative \(f(hL)\) if and only if \(f(nh)\le1\), where \(n\) is the vertex count. Every even-order Taylor graph-heat update consequently has the exact, clique-size-independent positivity horizon \(\beta_p/n\), with \(F_p(\beta_p)=1\).
