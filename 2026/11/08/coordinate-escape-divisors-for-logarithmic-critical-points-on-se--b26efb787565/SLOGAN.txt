For every Segre–Veronese rectangle \(\{0,1\}\times\{0,\ldots,m\}\) with \(m\geq2\), the two critical-point coordinate eliminants of a generic Euler integrand have explicit degree \(2m\), with exact escape factors. The \(x\)-escape divisors detect discriminants of the two degree-\(m\) edge polynomials, while the \(y\)-escape divisors depend only on their endpoint coefficients and resonance; the resonance multiplicities differ by a factor of \(m\).
