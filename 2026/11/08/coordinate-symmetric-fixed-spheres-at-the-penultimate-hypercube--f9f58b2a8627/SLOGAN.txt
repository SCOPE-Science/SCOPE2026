At Hamming scale \(n-2\), the geometric fixed-point space of the full coordinate-permutation action on the binary \(n\)-cube's Vietoris--Rips complex collapses onto a cross-polytope boundary and is homotopy equivalent to \(S^{\lfloor(n-2)/4\rfloor}\) for every \(n\ge2\). Global bit complementation acts freely and has integral top-homology sign \((-1)^{\lceil\lfloor n/2\rfloor/2\rceil}\).
