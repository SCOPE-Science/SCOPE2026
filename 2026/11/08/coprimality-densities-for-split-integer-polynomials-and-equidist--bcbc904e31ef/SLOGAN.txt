For any fixed product of distinct integer shifts and totally homogeneously equidistributed integer-part coordinates, the coprimality density is an explicit Euler product counting shift roots modulo each prime, provided one coordinate is a polynomial with an irrational nonconstant coefficient. In particular, \(\gcd(n(n+1),\lfloor\alpha n+\beta\rfloor)=1\) has density \(\prod_p(1-2/p^2)\) for every irrational \(\alpha\).
