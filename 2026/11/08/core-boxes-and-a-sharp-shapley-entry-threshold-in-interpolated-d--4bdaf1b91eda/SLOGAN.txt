For every positively weighted directed in-star, interpolating internal and external trust produces an empty core below \(\alpha=1/2\), a singleton at equality, and an explicit weighted box above it. The Shapley value enters that core at a sharp weight-dependent threshold, minimized by equal weights at \(2m/(3m+1)\) and arbitrarily close to one under severe weight asymmetry, even though the games remain non-supermodular until \(\alpha=1\).
