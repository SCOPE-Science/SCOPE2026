For \(N\) independent arclength-uniform samples from a fixed convex polygon, the scaled missed area of the sample hull converges to a sum of independent products of exponential corner gaps, with a universal \((N+1)(N+2)\) correction to the mean. For triangles of known perimeter, the mean and variance of this limiting area loss are the smallest sufficient collection of initial moments to reconstruct all three sides up to congruence.
