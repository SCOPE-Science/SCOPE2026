# Scientific review: diameter graphs of cosmological polytopes

## Correctness (PASS)
The proof identifies all source vertices and their exact integer Gram products in the standard orthonormal embedding. Squared diametral distance is eight, with edge adjacency precisely equal to source-edge-local triangle pairs or opposite signs on the unique shared graph node. Its count has no duplicate incidence contributions in a simple graph. The four-clique obstruction follows from a zero-norm sum incompatible with the affine coordinate-sum-one hyperplane. Every diameter triangle uses three colours; opposite-sign cross edges force a consistent node colour, and the inverse assignment from a proper source colouring respects all diameter edges. This gives an exact bijection, not just a reduction in one direction. The complexity corollary follows from the classic NP-completeness of connected 3-colourability and a polynomial-sized coordinate realization. The standalone exact checker passed 2,627,773 tests across 771 connected labelled graphs of at most five vertices; arbitrary graph size is handled by proof, not finite enumeration.

## Originality (PASS, provisional historical priority)
The motivating Donzelmann–Juhnke–Rednoß–Thäle 2026 source (arXiv:2605.23371v1) studies ordinary polytope and triangulation edge counts. Kühne–Monin (arXiv:2209.08069v2) analyze the ordinary face lattice; Birkemeyer–Donzelmann–Fink–Juhnke (arXiv:2603.03894v1) analyze duals, tubings and canonical forms. None determines the maximum-Euclidean-distance graph or its colourings. The particularly close public published-finding corpus theorem on a graph-independent enclosing sphere for the same cosmological polytope does not imply pairwise distances; its own limitations leave the pairwise spectrum undetermined. Searches for diameter graph, farthest-point pairs, chromatic polynomial, Euclidean metric and covering graph-polytope statements, plus a claim comparison with accessible prior records, found no covering result. An unindexed equivalent metric-colouring treatment, particularly under alternate physics terminology, remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
The result classifies the exact farthest-pair graph for every finite connected simple source graph and shows that a complete proper three-colouring problem is faithfully encoded by that Euclidean metric graph. In particular, the graph has uniform clique number three while three-colourability remains NP-complete. The exact edge count and colour-bijection capture structures not determined by the previously known circumradius or counts of one-dimensional faces. The contribution is structural and family-wide, not an arbitrary small-graph computation.

## Limitations
The results refer to the standard Euclidean coordinate realization only, concern proper labelled three-colourings only, and assume connected simple source graphs with at least one edge. The verifier checks finite cases; the general theorem has an independent elementary mathematical proof. Historical priority remains provisional. No independent mathematical audit or validation was performed.
