For every finite connected simple graph \(G\) with an edge, its standard cosmological polytope has Euclidean diameter \(2\sqrt2\), and the proper labelled three-colourings of its maximum-distance graph correspond bijectively to those of \(G\). The diameter graph has \(3|E|+4\sum_v\binom{\deg(v)}2\) edges and no \(K_4\), yet its three-colourability is NP-complete over this natural polytope family.
