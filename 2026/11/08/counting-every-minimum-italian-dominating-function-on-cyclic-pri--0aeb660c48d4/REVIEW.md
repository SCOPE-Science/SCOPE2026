# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

A necessary-and-sufficient local triple rule represents all Italian functions as closed walks on 81 states. A stated integer potential makes every permitted reduced transition nonnegative, proving the known lower bound without assuming the literature theorem. A circular binary witness attains the bound for all lengths. The complete 161-edge zero-cost graph has exactly three cyclic components of orders 12, 4 and 2, listed in the proof; exact determinants yield the rational generating function. Strong connectivity and length-two/length-three cycles give the Perron asymptotic. The included standalone checker independently validates critical arithmetic, component structure, determinants and small-graph enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Gardner (2018, Theorem 3.13) establishes the minimum cardinality \(\gamma_I(\Pi_n)=n\), not the enumeration of all minimizers or their exponential growth. Shao–Zhao (2026, arXiv:2610.00108v1) investigates Italian weight enumeration on paths, trees and cycles; the inspected complete HTML (Sections 1–9) does not treat the prism count. Comparisons against published findings about Roman-\(\{3\}\) complete prisms, other multipartite Italian variants and dominating matchings on cyclic prisms show that their hypotheses or counted objects differ. Searches found no exact same count or larger theorem implying it. The full HTML of the motivating 2026 paper and the 2018 thesis prism section were inspected. Novelty is provisional, not a claim of exhaustive historical uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

For a standard 3-regular graph family with already-known Italian domination number, the exact multiplicity of optimal defenses is a natural unrecovered enumerative invariant. The finite automaton collapses to three cyclic components and gives a low-degree rational series with algebraic exponential rate \(1.704902776\ldots\); this is more than a routine minimum-weight recalculation or an isolated finite table. The theorem counts all labelled optima and supplies a reusable structural critical-graph description for studying configurations rather than only their minimum weight.
