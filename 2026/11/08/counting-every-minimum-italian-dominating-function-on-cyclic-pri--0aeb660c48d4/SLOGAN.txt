For the cyclic prism \(C_n\square K_2\), the known minimum Italian domination weight \(n\) is attained by exactly \(-[z^n]zP'(z)/P(z)\) labelled functions, where \(P(z)=(1+z)(1+z^2-z^3+z^4)(1-z-z^2-z^5)(1-z^4)(1-z^2)\). Their number grows as \(\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\approx1.704902776\) is the positive root of \(x^5-x^4-x^3-1\).
