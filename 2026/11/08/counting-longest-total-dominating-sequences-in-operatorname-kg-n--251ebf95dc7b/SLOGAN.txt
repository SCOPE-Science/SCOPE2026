The number of ordered maximum-length total-dominating sequences in the Kneser graph \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) is exactly \(720\binom n4+33120\binom n5+257040\binom n6+655200\binom n7+624960\binom n8+181440\binom n9\) for every \(n\ge5\). Although the optimal length is the previously known constant six, such sequences use at most nine ground elements and occur with probability asymptotic to \(32/n^3\) among random ordered six-tuples of distinct graph vertices.
