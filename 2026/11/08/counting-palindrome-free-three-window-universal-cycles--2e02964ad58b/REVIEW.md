# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The overlap digraph contains each admissible triple as exactly one labeled directed edge, is balanced and strongly connected, and admits a cycle/word bijection modulo rotation. The adjacency identity \(B=UV-R\), the two matrix contractions \(VU=J_q\) and \(VRU=qI_q\), and the determinant-lemma factorization provide all nonstandard algebraic steps explicitly. The directed matrix-tree formula gives an exact integer arborescence count; BEST then counts rotational Euler circuits with a distinguished edge. The exceptional alphabet size \(q=2\) is evaluated separately. Exact integer Laplacian minors through \(q=6\), direct Euler-circuit enumeration for \(q=2,3\), and a separate outgoing-edge-ordering enumerator for \(q=2,3\) agree with the theorem. Computations supplement but do not replace the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Leitner–Godbole (arXiv:0808.1309) establish several restricted-word universal-cycle existence theorems; Chen–Kitaev–Sun (arXiv:1908.01116) address existence probabilities for deleted words; Sawada–Gabrić (arXiv:2510.16545) provide random generation via arborescences. Inspected parts of their public full texts include the relevant construction and enumeration sections. They provide applicable graph-theoretic ideas but no identical closed count or evaluated characteristic polynomial for the explicitly excluded length-three palindromes. A general nonbacktracking matrix reduction might yield this exact specialized formula by a short additional computation, and an unindexed earlier enumeration may exist. No proof of absolute historical uniqueness is asserted.

## Value — PASS

The exact cardinality of all nonrepeating cyclic test sequences for the natural local restriction \(abc\) with \(a\ne c\) is an invariant distinct from mere existence. The result supplies a nontrivial product at every alphabet size, a visibly structured transition spectrum, and exact reproducible small instances. The fixed three-window restriction is motivated independently by forbidding immediate two-step returns and palindromic local patterns. It does not depend on a chosen arbitrary finite cutoff.

## Limitations and closest literature

No general longer-window formula or quotient by reversal and relabeling is obtained. Previously established matrix-tree and BEST counting facts are used, not claimed. The remaining historical-priority risks are equivalent nonbacktracking-operator calculations, records not captured by indexed literature, and unpublished enumerations.
