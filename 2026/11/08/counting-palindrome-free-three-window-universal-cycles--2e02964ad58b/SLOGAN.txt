For every labeled alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), the number of rotationally distinct cyclic words containing each nonpalindromic length-three word exactly once is \(q^{(q+3)(q-2)/2}(q-1)^{q-1}(q-2)^{(q-1)(q-2)/2}((q-2)!)^{q^2}\), while for \(q=2\) the count is one. A rank-\(q\) determinant reduction of the palindrome-free overlap graph produces this exact all-alphabet count.
