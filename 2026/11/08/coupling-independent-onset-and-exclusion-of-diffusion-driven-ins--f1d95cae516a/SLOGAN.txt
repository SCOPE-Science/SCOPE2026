For ideal-storage oscillators with symmetric stochastic rate averaging, coupling cannot destabilize an otherwise stable homogeneous equilibrium: the complete network is locally Schur stable exactly when \(5c(1-r)<1\), with \(r=(1+\exp(C_r-5c))^{-1}\). The exact threshold has two branches for \(C_r>2\), and on any connected genuinely coupled network all nonuniform modes remain strictly damped at onset.
