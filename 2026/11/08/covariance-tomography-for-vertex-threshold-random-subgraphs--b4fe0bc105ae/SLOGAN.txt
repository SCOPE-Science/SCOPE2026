In the vertex-threshold random-subgraph model, the covariance of the observed degree vector is exactly \(a_\tau Q+c_\tau Q^2\), a strictly invertible quadratic function of the hidden host's signless Laplacian for every \(0<\tau<2\). Thus repeated degree-only observations reconstruct any labelled host graph, with an explicit finite-sample guarantee and a covariance nullity that counts its bipartite components.
