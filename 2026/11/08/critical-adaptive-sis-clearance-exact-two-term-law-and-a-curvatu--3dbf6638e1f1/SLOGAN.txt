At the exact reproduction threshold, strictly positive smooth monotone adaptive SIS link responses always clear infection with \(1/y(t)=Kt+B\log t+C+o(1)\), where both coefficients are explicit functions of the link-response derivatives. For \(b(y)=1+y+\kappa y^2\), the leading coefficient remains \(3/2\) while the logarithmic correction changes sign at \(\kappa=3/4\).
