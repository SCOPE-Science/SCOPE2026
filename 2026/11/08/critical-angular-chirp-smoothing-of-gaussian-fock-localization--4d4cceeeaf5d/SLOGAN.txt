Gaussian Fock localization with a rotating angular profile \(f(\theta-\beta r^\gamma)\) has an exact three-regime classification modulo compact operators: unsmoothed Hardy Toeplitz for \(\gamma<1\), circle-heat-smoothed Hardy Toeplitz at \(\gamma=1\), and the scalar angular mean for \(\gamma>1\). For nonconstant continuous profiles, centering becomes compact exactly above the critical exponent, with explicit essential spectra and Fredholm indices in every regime.
