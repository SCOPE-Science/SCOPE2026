For every unweighted discrete Huygens ring with \(4\nmid n\), the exact spectral endpoint \(h=2/\Lambda_n\) still forces every phase-quotient orbit to a fixed point and almost every orbit to a twisted minimum. On rings with \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), antiphase remains attracting at its critical multiplier \(-1\), and the generic alternating error obeys \(k a_k^2\to3/2\) rather than exponential relaxation.
