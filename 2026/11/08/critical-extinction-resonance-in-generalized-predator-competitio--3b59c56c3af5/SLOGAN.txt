At the exact predator-invasion threshold of an additional-food competition model, all positive trajectories approach predator extinction, but fractional competition \(1<p<2\) produces a universal \(t^{-1/(p-1)}\) predator-decay law. The first correction switches from a finite shift to a logarithm at \(p=3/2\), and then to a growing fractional power; quadratic competition \(p=2\) changes even the leading coefficient.
