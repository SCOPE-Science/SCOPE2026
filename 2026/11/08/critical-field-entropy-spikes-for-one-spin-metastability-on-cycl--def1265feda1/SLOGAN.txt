The uniform antiferromagnetic Ising model on the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) has exactly computable one-spin-stable counts for every field, with isolated exponential-entropy spikes at \(|h|/J=1\) and \(|h|/J=3\). Away from these critical fields the count grows at the Lucas-number rate below saturation, while at the two critical fields the growth bases are respectively \(2.33935824388750\ldots\) and \(1+\sqrt2\); the latter independent-set endpoint is previously known.
