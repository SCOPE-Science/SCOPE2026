For square consecutive-frequency Fourier interpolation at independent uniform random nodes, the inverse-Frobenius noise gain has a sharp tail of order \((\log T)^{n-2}/T\), and its squared gain has finite \(s\)-th moment exactly for \(s<1/2\). The dimension-dependent logarithmic degree comes from products of reciprocal chord distances, whose exact Mellin transforms and critical truncation constants are also determined.
