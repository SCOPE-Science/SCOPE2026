For the post-loss careless coupon collector at erasure probability \(p=c/n\), the mean time to possess all \(n\) types grows with sharp exponential rate \(\int_0^1\log(1+(e^{cx}-1)/x)\,dx\), strictly above the stationary-marginal independence prediction \(\log(1+c)\). An exact finite product for the stationary all-present probability, combined with monotone coupling and quantitative mixing, proves the rate.
