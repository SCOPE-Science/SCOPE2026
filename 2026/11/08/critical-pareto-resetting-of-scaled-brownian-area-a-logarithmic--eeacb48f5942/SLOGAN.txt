At the previously unresolved critical Pareto exponent \(\alpha=1+\beta/2\), the absolute area of a reset scaled Brownian particle grows like \(\frac{4\beta}{(2+\beta)^2\sqrt\pi}t\log t\) in probability and has nondegenerate one-stable fluctuations of order \(t\). Yet the same normalized trajectory area has infinite almost-sure limsup, separating typical logarithmic growth from rare extremely long excursions.
