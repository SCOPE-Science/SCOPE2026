# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The section dynamics follow exactly from two elementary integrals of the vertical field. Upper and lower half-flights alternate and have radii \(a^{\kappa^r}\); their physical times are sums of consecutive radii because the horizontal speed is \(1\) in modulus. This proves the three regimes, the precise cycle multiplier and convergence to the singular origin. The \(E_1\) time law is obtained by a half-line Euler--Maclaurin expansion with an integrable smooth kernel. Its constant terms cancel algebraically. At the repelling-cycle edge, shifting the lacunary series index gives a uniformly convergent periodization and an \(O_\kappa(L)\) tail estimate. Fourier-unfolding gives nonzero Gamma coefficients, proving that the ripple is nonconstant. All quantifiers, section and real-exponent restrictions are given in RESULT.md. The standalone checker supports the derivation but is not the proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Glendinning et al. (2023), DOI: 10.1007/s00332-023-09910-4, Theorem 3 and equations (13)--(15), already obtain finite-time convergence of a monomial sewed focus and the exact lacunary sum for the particular exponent ratio \(2\). That existence and power-return recurrence are credited, not claimed as novel. Its inspected open-access full text does not state the sharp exponent-coalescence law involving \(E_1\), cancellation of the constant term, or the log-periodic physical-time boundary law and its nonzero Gamma Fourier coefficients for a two-exponent ratio family. The author's arXiv HTML version was inspected independently for the same comparisons. Targeted mathematical record and literature searches found no covering theorem. This is a best-knowledge originality assessment, not proof that no equivalent formula has ever been published.

A related paper, *Classification of Filippov Type 3 Singular Points in Planar Bimodal Piecewise Smooth Systems* (DOI: 10.1137/23M1622842), establishes topological classifications, not the quantitative physical-time asymptotics inspected here; only its publicly accessible abstract was checked. General results on lacunary sums, Mellin summation or Euler--Maclaurin could contain equivalent asymptotics; that publication-priority risk remains.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Finite physical-time attainment of a sewed singularity is a mathematically unusual phenomenon established in the originating paper. Determining the exact divergence of this time when the asymmetric exponent ratio approaches the center threshold, including its cancellation of a constant-order term, resolves a natural quantitative singular-limit question. The separate boundary law distinguishes the repelling cycle's logarithmically diverging delay from an ordinary smooth correction, giving an exact nonconstant discrete-scale signature even when numerically tiny. These are precise invariants of physical time for a natural family, not an arbitrary parameter table or a restatement of the source's existence theorem.

## Evidence and limitations

The source paper's original 2023 construction and first-return formulas were read in full from the lawful open-access journal HTML, especially Section 3, Theorem 3 and equations (13)--(16); other sections were inspected for stronger coverage. The arXiv v1 HTML was also read, including its analytic case and general sewed-focus classification. No published full-text theorem inspected gives the proposed sharp two-limit time laws. A 2024/2025 type-3 classification paper was inspected only at abstract level; no claim of exhaustive inspection is made. The present results do not control generic perturbations or simultaneous two-parameter limits, and singular-origin continuation is outside scope.
