At the predator-Allee collision \(m=c\) in a fear-sensitive Leslie–Gower model, a whole positive-data wedge has \(y(t)\sim c/\sqrt{2st}\), but prey decays with the sharper law \(x(t)\sim L t^{\sigma}\exp[-\mu t-rkc\sqrt{2t/s}]\), with \(L>0\) and \(\sigma=rkc(kc-1)/(2s)\). The fear parameter changes both subexponential factors even though the ordinary exponential extinction rate does not depend on fear.
