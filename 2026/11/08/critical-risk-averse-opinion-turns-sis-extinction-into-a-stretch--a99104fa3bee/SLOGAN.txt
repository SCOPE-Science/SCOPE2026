At the simultaneous disease and opinion thresholds of a nonlinear opinion–SIS system, every initially infected risk-averse trajectory has \(p(t)\sim P_\infty e^{-\beta\sqrt{2\tau_x/(1/3-k_x)}\sqrt t}\), while \((-x(t))^{-2}\) has an exact signed logarithmic correction. Exactly neutral initial opinion instead gives the ordinary \(1/t\) SIS law, so initial risk aversion changes the class of critical extinction rather than merely its coefficient.
