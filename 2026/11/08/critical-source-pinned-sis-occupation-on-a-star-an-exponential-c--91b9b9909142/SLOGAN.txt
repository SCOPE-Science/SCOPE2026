On a star with one permanently infected leaf and infection rate \(\lambda_n=c/\sqrt n\), the stationary hub infection probability is asymptotic to \((e^{c^2}-1)/(c\sqrt n)\), while the expected number of infected ordinary leaves converges to \(e^{c^2}-1\). The nonzero leaf burden despite vanishing hub prevalence follows from exact graphical duality and a critical-window occupation potential.
