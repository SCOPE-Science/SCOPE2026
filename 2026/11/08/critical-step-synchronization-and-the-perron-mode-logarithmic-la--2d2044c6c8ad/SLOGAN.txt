At the critical noninvertible step \(h=2/\lambda_{\max}(K)\), every orbit of a sine-coupled unweighted tree of phase oscillators converges to a fixed configuration and almost every orbit synchronizes in antiphase. Its slowest mode follows an explicit graph-dependent inverse-square law with a logarithmic correction; the first nonuniform four-vertex path has coefficient \((17-\sqrt2)/96\).
