On the \(R\)-null recurrent boundary of the open Pelikan random map, survival from every sufficiently light-tailed initial distribution has an exact \(\lambda^n/\sqrt n\) law with a computable initial-law amplitude. For uniform initial interval data the prefactor collapses to the simple value \(2\sqrt{2/\pi}/\varepsilon\), revealing a critical correction invisible to the exponential escape rate alone.
