Independently reset Brownian coordinates in one shared, separately reset stochastic-diffusivity environment develop squared-displacement cross-correlations whose ratio to the same-coordinate lag covariance increases and tends to one exactly when the coordinate reset rate is at least the environment reset rate. At matched rates the ratio's deficit is exactly \(7/(8+\alpha t)\), while the normalized collective squared radius retains nonzero fluctuations in arbitrarily high dimension.
