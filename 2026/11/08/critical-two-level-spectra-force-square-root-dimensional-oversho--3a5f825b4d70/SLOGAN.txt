For the unsafeguarded nonnegative first-degree Frobenius-fit of a Newton–Schulz matrix-sign step, an explicit two-level positive spectrum makes the **unique optimal coefficient** halve the squared Frobenius residual while causing a spectral-residual spike of \((1/2+o(1))\sqrt n\) and a matrix-sign approximation error of order \(n^{1/4}\). The \(\sqrt n\) growth order is universally maximal, and the prefactor \(1/2\) is exact within the specified critical two-level scaling family, not claimed as a universal sharp prefactor.
