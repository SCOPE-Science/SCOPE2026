Triangles and induced chordless four-cycles in \(G(n,p)\) change covariance sign near density \(2/3\), but their correlation switches across the wider window \(p=2/3+\lambda/\sqrt n\) with exact profile \(-\lambda/\sqrt{\lambda^2+10/27}\). A first-edge projection cancels at the critical density, exposing a lower-order graph-factor variance and creating the crossover.
