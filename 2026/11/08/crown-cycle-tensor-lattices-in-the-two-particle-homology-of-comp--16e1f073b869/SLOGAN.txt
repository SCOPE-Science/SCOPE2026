For every \(p,q\ge3\), the integral second homology of the ordinary two-particle configuration space of \(K_{p,q}\) is canonically the tensor product of the cycle lattices of the two crown graphs, with particle exchange acting by a negative simultaneous transposition. This uniform identification determines the entire integral graph-automorphism action, including the bipartition swap when \(p=q\), and its complete rational character.
