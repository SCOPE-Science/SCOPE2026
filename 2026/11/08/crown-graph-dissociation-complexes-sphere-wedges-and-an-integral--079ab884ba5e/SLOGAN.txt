The second-independence complex of every crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M\), for \(n\geq3\), is a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}{2}\) two-spheres. Its full integral \(S_n\times C_2\)-homology representation is \(\bigwedge^2\ker(\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z)\) twisted by the sign of interchange of the two bipartition classes.
