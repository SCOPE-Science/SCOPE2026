In the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), power domination is exactly the union of mixed-shore selections and one-shore selections of at least \(n-2\) vertices; this yields complete all-set and inclusion-minimal-set polynomials. For every \(n\ge6\), the token-addition/removal reconfiguration graph is connected at budget \(3\), disconnected at budget \(n-2\), and connected again from budget \(n-1\) onward, with an exact component count at every budget.
