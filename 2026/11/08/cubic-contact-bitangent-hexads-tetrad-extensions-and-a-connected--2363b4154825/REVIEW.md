# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

A theta characteristic differs from a fixed even theta characteristic by a vector in a six-dimensional quadratic space over \(\mathbb F_2\). Adjunction gives \(2D_L\sim K_C\), and surjectivity of plane cubic sections identifies the cubic-contact condition with the zero-sum condition on six odd theta vectors. Conic contact of triple and tetrad sets translates into quadratic-parity tests. The attached standalone exact checker enumerates the entire finite model: \(376740\) candidate six-sets, \(20475\) tetrads and every retained ordered hexad pair. It also independently reconstructs the exchange graph from tetrad containment and proves its connectivity via full breadth-first traversal. The proof's geometric prerequisites are standard, and the computational claims are full finite censuses rather than experiments on a sample.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The two cubic-contact families encode distinct topological arrangement types. The extension spectrum determines precisely which four prescribed bitangents extend to each family, and the exchange graph gives a complete, connected combinatorial navigation between the two kinds of six-set while preserving the cubic-contact condition after each exchange. This is a natural exact classification and a structural connectivity theorem on the full classical incidence configuration, not an arbitrary chosen parameter slice or numerical recomputation of the pre-existing \(1008/5040\) totals. Connectivity is not claimed to induce a deformation of arrangements.
