For cubic-contact six-bitangent sets on a very general complex plane quartic, every four-bitangent subset has exactly one of three extension profiles \((0,0)\), \((3,3)\), or \((0,4)\), occurring \(315\), \(5040\), and \(15120\) times. The two known topologically distinct hexad families form a connected bipartite graph under exchanges of two bitangents, with \(45360\) cross-family edges and degrees \(45\) and \(9\).
