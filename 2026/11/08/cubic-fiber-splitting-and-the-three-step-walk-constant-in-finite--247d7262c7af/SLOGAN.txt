For the quartic finite-field surface \(S_q=\{(x,y,xy^3):x,y\in\mathbb F_q\}\), the entire Fourier-kernel magnitude distribution and sharp endpoint projection norm are determined by whether cubing is bijective and, otherwise, by a one- or two-dimensional trace-phase law. Along split fields of increasing characteristic the normalized norm approaches one third of the classical three-step planar random-walk mean, whereas bijective-cube fields have normalized limit one.
