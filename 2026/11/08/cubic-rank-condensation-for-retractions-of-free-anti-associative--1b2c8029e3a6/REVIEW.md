# Scientific review: cubic-rank condensation for free anti-associative retractions

## Correctness — PASS
The free odd-characteristic anti-associative algebra has homogeneous degrees one, two, and three, and dimensions \(n,n^2,n^3\). A retraction on the degree-one quotient is a linear projection \(P\). Every retraction is conjugate by a unique-up-to-centralizer automorphism that is the identity modulo the square to the homogeneous extension of \(P\). Counting those conjugates gives, for each rank \(r\), the exact number \({n\brack r}_q q^{r(B(n)-B(r))+(n-r)d(r)}\), with \(B(t)=t^2+t^3\) and \(d(r)=r+B(r)\). Orbit-stabilizer independently gives embedded-image counts and fixed fibers. Uniform boundedness of the Gaussian-binomial correction and strict curvature at the unique interior maximum of the quartic exponent imply exponentially sharp two-point concentration. The finite-field checker reproduces the canonical rank-one fixed-projection fiber over \(\mathbb F_3\) and checks additional small cases; these finite tests do not replace the proof. Risks: an implicit characteristic-two use would be invalid; all arguments and counts explicitly assume odd characteristic. The concentration statement applies only to fixed odd field order and sufficiently large \(n\).

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
Remm, *Anti-associative algebras* (arXiv:2202.10812, section 2.3), supplies the free algebra and its three graded layers, but not these retraction fibers or their rank condensation. Cerqua and Facchini, *Pre-Lie algebras, their multiplicative lattice, and idempotent endomorphisms* (arXiv:2301.02627, section 6), discuss general retraction/splitting principles, but do not give this finite-field rank census. Earlier finite-field counts for two-step free associative and truncated symmetric algebras concern inequivalent multiplication tensors and cannot imply the cubic layer count by specialization. The exact embedded images, constant retraction fibers, and irrational limiting rank fraction were not located in consulted literature or mathematical-record searches. A generic operadic or free-object Grassmannian theorem may subsume aspects of the result; publication priority requires further human scrutiny.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Retractions correspond to algebraic direct-factor decompositions and have natural linear image ranks. This classification simultaneously determines every embedded retract image and its multiplicity over every odd finite field, and identifies a nontrivial large-rank condensation phenomenon. The irrational asymptotic fraction \(\alpha^3\) follows from the presence of a genuine cubic layer and differs structurally from quadratic free-object analogues. This is a family-wide structural invariant, not merely a low-dimensional computation or arbitrary finite slice.
