Retractions of the free anti-associative algebra on \(n\) generators over every odd finite field have exactly computable embedded-image multiplicities, and each \(r\)-generator image has exactly \(q^{(n-r)(r+r^2+r^3)}\) retractions. The generator rank of a uniformly chosen retraction concentrates exponentially sharply on two adjacent integers near \(\alpha n\), where \(\alpha\approx0.6610498029\) is the irrational root of \(8\alpha^3-3\alpha^2-1=0\).
