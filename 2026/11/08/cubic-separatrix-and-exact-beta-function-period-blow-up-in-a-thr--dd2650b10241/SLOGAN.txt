For three quadrature-coupled identical phase oscillators, both phase-difference period annuli terminate on a cubic three-loop separatrix, and their original-time periods obey the exact nonlogarithmic law \(T(h)\sim2^{2/3}B(1/3,1/3)|h|^{-1/3}\). This gives a closed beta-function constant for the slowdown of the smallest conservative nearest-neighbor phase ring, rather than an attractor-transient or continuum-wave approximation.
