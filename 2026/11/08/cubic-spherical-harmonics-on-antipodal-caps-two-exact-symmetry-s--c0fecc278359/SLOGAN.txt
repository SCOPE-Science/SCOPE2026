On antipodal spherical caps in every dimension \(d\ge3\), the optimal pure cubic spherical harmonic switches symmetry exactly at squared cap cutoffs \(1/(d+2)\) and \(3/(d+2)\). The first transition unites three branches into an eigenspace of dimension \((d-1)(d+2)/2\), while the least-observable cubic sector never changes; the thin-cap spectral condition number has a sharp cubic blow-up.
