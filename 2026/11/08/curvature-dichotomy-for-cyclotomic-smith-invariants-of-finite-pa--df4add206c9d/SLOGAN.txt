For every odd prime, Fourier samples between two prime-field parabolas determine every signal uniquely. The complete cyclotomic-local Smith exponents are \(0,1,1,\ldots,(p-1)/2,(p-1)/2\) when both parabolas have nonzero curvature, but \(0,1,\ldots,p-1\) when either is flat: curvature exactly halves the largest local reconstruction-denominator exponent.
