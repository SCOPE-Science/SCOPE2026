For a rational Feres reflection parameter, every fixed incoming angle—even a rational multiple of \(\pi\)—has a countable exceptional set of geodesic-circle radii outside which its one-time collision position converges weakly to uniform arclength, on both hyperbolic and spherical disks. Two positive return loops acquire a curvature-dependent irrational phase difference, eliminating the flat billiard's rational-angle spatial locking without implying global mixing.
