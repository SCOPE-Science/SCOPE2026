For two independent hyperbolic-area-uniform points in any fixed ideal hyperbolic polygon, the probability of distance exceeding \(R\) is \(C(P)R e^{-R}+O_P(e^{-R})\), with an explicit cusp-pair coefficient sensitive to the polygon's shape. Among ideal quadrilaterals, this coefficient is at least \(8/\pi^2\), with equality only for the regular ideal quadrilateral.
