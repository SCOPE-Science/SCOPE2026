For the Fermat curve of degree \(d=2p^e+1\) in odd characteristic, the reduced dual is a degree-\(2d\) curve with exactly \(3d\) unibranch cusps of analytic type \(v^2=4u^d\), and its normalization is the original Fermat curve. Its bidual has the unexpectedly larger degree \(d(d-2)\), making nonreflexivity explicit.
