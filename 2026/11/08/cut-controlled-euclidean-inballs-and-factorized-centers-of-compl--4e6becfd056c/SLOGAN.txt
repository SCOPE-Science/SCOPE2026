For complete directed flow polytopes with positive prefix netflows, every intrinsic Euclidean inball is controlled by a smallest normalized prefix cut, and all optimal centers split as independent flows between the tight cuts. For the unit-source/unit-sink realization of the Chan–Robbins–Yuen family, the inradius is \(\sqrt{n/(n-2)}/\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\), while the incenter locus has dimension \((\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1)(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-2)\).
