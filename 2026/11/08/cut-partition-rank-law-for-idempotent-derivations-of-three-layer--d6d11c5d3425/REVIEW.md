# Scientific review: Cut-partition rank law for idempotent derivations of three-layer incidence algebras

## Correctness — PASS
For the connected three-level incidence algebra in characteristic two, every linear derivation is inner: the usual diagonal-idempotent normalization leaves an arrow cocycle, and each strict pair occurs in a bottom–middle–top chain, forcing the cocycle to be a potential difference. The center is exactly the scalar identity, so \(D^2=D\) is equivalent to \(X^2+X\) being scalar, and subtracting a diagonal root produces an idempotent generator. Each derivation has precisely two such generators, differing by the identity. Fixed-diagonal idempotents are a single unipotent conjugacy orbit of cardinality \(q^{r(\epsilon)}\), where \(r(\epsilon)\) is the cut size of the coloring; this equals the rank of the associated inner derivation. The full generating polynomial, unique zero-rank operator, and maximum-rank multiplicity follow. Compactness away from maximum-cut configurations and a low-defect binomial estimate prove exponential concentration, not merely a finite-size numerical trend. The standalone standard-library enumeration explicitly checked \(n\le2\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(n=1\) over \(\mathbb F_4\), and the maximum-cut claim through \(n=10\); it ended with `VERIFY_OK`. Boundaries and fixed-field asymptotics are explicit. Risk: the concentration proof's entropy estimate depends on the fixed \(\eta=1/100\) numerical inequality, which is verified algebraically for every \(q\ge2\) because its left side is largest at \(q=2\).

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
Fornaroli–Khrypchenko (arXiv:2307.08439, 2023), Theorem 2.8 and Corollary 3.9, and Krylov–Tuganbaev (arXiv:2305.02984, 2023), provide general descriptions of derivations of incidence algebras and their additive cocycles. Santulo–Souza–Yasumura (arXiv:1905.08391, 2019), Lemma 7 and classification results, together with Dos Santos–Yasumura (arXiv:2406.19329, 2024), Theorems 6.1–6.2, already establish diagonal conjugacy of idempotents and classification of abelian-group gradings up to isomorphism. These ingredients, including the equivalence with characteristic-two \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\)-gradings, are **not claimed as new**. However, a classification up to graded isomorphism does not give the number of distinct derivation operators at each rank: the concrete unipotent-fiber weights, three-level cut polynomial, full rank coefficients, and asymptotic maximal-rank concentration require further arguments. No inspected source reports these quantitative statements. published-finding corpus semantic searches and the accessible own current-week ledger did not reveal the same or a stronger count. This is best-knowledge originality, **not publication-priority certification**; the 2003 Jones dissertation was only accessible in abstract, and broader gradings enumerations could subsume the rank formula.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The invariant is the operator-rank spectrum of all idempotent derivations of a natural infinite family of incidence algebras, not an arbitrary exceptional case or mere rewording of grading classification. It refines an operator's induced \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\)-grading by counting distinct embedded derivations, revealing a weighted tripartite-cut structure and an exponentially dominant rank stratum. The exact degeneracy and quantitative concentration give nontrivial information that a classification up to isomorphism leaves undetermined. Mathematical scope is restricted to equal-size complete three-level posets and finite characteristic-two fields, and independence has not been established.
