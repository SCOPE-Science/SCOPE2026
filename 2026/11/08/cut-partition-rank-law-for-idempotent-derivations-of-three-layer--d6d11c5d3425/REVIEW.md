# Scientific review: extremal projection-derivation ranks in balanced three-level incidence algebras

## Correctness — PASS for the narrowed final theorem
The general rank-weight formula is a previously published theorem about Boolean additive cocycles; the final result now uses it as input rather than purporting to prove it first. Counting binary color patterns with two opposite monochromatic levels, correcting the six doubly represented all-pure patterns and dividing by global color complement yields exactly \(3(2^n-1)q^{2n^2}\) maximal-rank operators. The entropy bound isolates six near-ground-state regions and shows their total nonmaximal weight is \(O_q(\kappa_q^n)\) with \(\kappa_q<2\); far configurations are suppressed by \(q^{-\delta n^2}\). The conclusion is for \(n\ge1\), fields \(q=2^e\), and fixed \(q\) in the limit. Finite checks of small \(n\) complement rather than prove the limit.

## Mathematical value — PASS for the narrowed final theorem
The maximum-rank stratum of a growing naturally parametrized incidence-algebra family, its exact multiplicity and the probability of occupying it are mathematically motivated operator statistics. The concentration estimate quantifies a sharp difference between an exponentially degenerate ground-state family and the potentially much larger set of all color patterns. It is more than an arbitrary small numerical slice of a known table, although it depends on the previously established general rank formula.
