For the incidence algebra of three equal complete antichain levels over \(\mathbb F_{2^e}\), every idempotent derivation is a two-color cut operator up to unipotent conjugacy, and its complete rank polynomial is a weighted cut polynomial of \(K_{n,n,n}\). Exactly \(3(2^n-1)q^{2n^2}\) such operators attain maximal rank \(2n^2\), and their share tends to one exponentially for every fixed field size.
