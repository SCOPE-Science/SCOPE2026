For binary tie-retaining minority updates on a wheel, the exact cut-size polynomial of fixed states is a cyclic-run enumeration; locally stable but globally suboptimal cuts exist precisely for rim lengths \(n=6\) and \(n\ge8\). A uniformly selected fixed state has cut size asymptotically normal with mean \(1.41149558866\,n\) and variance \(0.01707962906\,n\), while global optima comprise an exponentially vanishing fraction of fixed states.
