The uniform last-exit unicycle walk on the looped complete bipartite graph with parts \(a_n,b_n\) of bounded aspect ratio has a worst-case total-variation cutoff at \(2\max(a_n,b_n)\log\max(a_n,b_n)\). Full-memory coupling proves the upper bound, while untouched arrows concentrating on one opposite-part vertex force the lower bound.
