Under Ewens cycle weighting with \(\theta\ne1\), a fixed-point indicator has a nonzero, position-independent covariance with every nonadjacent descent indicator, and its sign is that of \(1-\theta\); this long-range background vanishes at \(\theta=1\). For every \(n\ge3\), total fixed points and circular one-line descents have strictly positive covariance for \(0<\theta<1\), zero covariance for \(\theta=1\), and strictly negative covariance for \(\theta>1\).
