A connected, locally finite graph can have every vertex on a triangle and still allow an exponentially directed-edge-reinforced ant walk to escape with probability at least \(\frac23e^{-3/4}\). More generally, for a rooted tree decorated with private cycles, the strictly outward escape event has an exact cycle-length-independent product law and occurs with positive probability exactly when \(\sum_{i\ge1}1/b_i<\infty\).
