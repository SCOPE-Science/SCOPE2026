# Mathematical review

## Correctness

**PASS.** Each of the four disjoint root-row cases is proved necessary and sufficient using the quantified confluence axioms. For any leaf permutation, source-cycle representatives are independently invariant under its corresponding power, and that power has \(\gcd\)-many orbits on each target cycle; summing the case-wise fixed counts and applying Burnside proves the formula for every integer \(m\ge2\). A standalone checker independently tests confluence, all four modal operators on all upsets, exact orbit canonicalization and fixed-permutation counts through three leaves; no finite sample is treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality

**PASS under the inspected evidence.** The 2026 four-connective paper provides the semantics and confluence definitions; earlier general labelled enumeration covers the same family as a labelled special case. Neither establishes leaf-permutation fixed counts or the exact unlabelled orbit series. The closest checked public chain-census articles concern finite linear orders and are insufficient to imply this branching orbit count. Searches for equivalent Burnside and cycle-index formulas and the initial sequence found no covering statement. These comparisons do not establish uniqueness across all literature; prior unpublished orbit counts remain a risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** A rooted star is the simplest naturally branching intuitionistic poset. Isomorphism classes are the appropriate units for canonical finite-frame generation without repeated renamings. The all-size cycle-index formula and finite exact values measure the nontrivial stabilizers ignored by labelled counts. It is a complete natural classification of a semantically motivated finite-frame family, not an arbitrary small-order sample.

The result does not classify other posets or prove independent historical priority.
