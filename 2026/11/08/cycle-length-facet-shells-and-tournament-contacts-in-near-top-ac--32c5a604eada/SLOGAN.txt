The near-top acyclohedron \(\mathcal A_{n,n-3}\) is a scaled orthogonal projection of the complete-graph edge cube onto its cycle space: its facets lie in exact cycle-length shells, with inradius \(\sqrt{3n}/2\). Its minimum enclosing sphere has squared radius \(n(\binom n2-\epsilon_n)/4\), where \(\epsilon_n=0\) for odd \(n\) and \(1\) for even \(n\); the contacts are exactly regular or almost-regular tournaments, and both extremal volume ellipsoids are Euclidean balls.
