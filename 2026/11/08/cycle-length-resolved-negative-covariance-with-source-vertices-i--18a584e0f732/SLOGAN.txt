For a uniformly random finite map, the number of indegree-zero vertices is uncorrelated with fixed-point count but negatively correlated with every directed cycle count of length at least two. The exact cycle-length covariance has a nontrivial \(\sqrt n\)-scale profile \(-xe^{-x^2/2}/(2e)\), yielding explicit limiting covariances with component count and the number of cyclic vertices.
