# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The fixed-point count reduces exactly to a centralizer sum. The wreath-product model supplies independent uniform block shifts and a uniformly distributed block-cycle length. The congruence \((k/\ell)S\equiv1\pmod d\) has a solution exactly in the coprime case; after summing over the cyclic blocks, this proves the central formula. Möbius inversion recovers all cycle multiplicities, while a separate double count recovers the fixed-pair probability. A standalone checker directly iterates right translations and confirms 230 fixed-power instances and 11 complete finite profiles. Finite computation is corroborative, not the proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with historical-priority risk.** Kai's first public September 2026 action-quandle paper introduces the object and gives the multiplication and inner-symmetry structure, but the inspected relevant text does not give centralizer-wreath fixed-point enumerations or translation cycle profiles. Nelson's generalized quandle polynomials define the general fixed-power statistic, not this family's explicit evaluation. Lages–Lopes–Vojtěchovský analyze general cycle profiles as hypotheses for latinness, not the formula here. Higashitani–Kurihara study a distinct homogeneous-quandle construction. Searches for alternative names (matrix rack, action quandle, universal symmetric quandle, fixed points, cyclic profiles, cycle index) and indexed research-record analogues did not produce an implication covering the result; absence from search results alone is not proof of priority.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The target is a canonical universal finite quandle for each cardinality, making its right-translation profile a natural invariant of an important new family. The formula determines every length and multiplicity in the profile, for all degrees and right operands, and yields an exact partition-theoretic fixed-pair probability. It connects quandle cycle-profile questions to symmetric-group centralizer wreath products, making an otherwise factorial-sized invariant computable from integer partitions. This is an exact family-wide structural result, not a selected small-case census.

## Literature and limitations

Compared full accessible relevant sections of Ryoya Kai, arXiv:2609.17438 (September 2026); Sam Nelson, arXiv:0801.2979; Lages–Lopes–Vojtěchovský, arXiv:2104.10199; and the distinct symmetric-group homogeneous-quandle construction in Higashitani–Kurihara, arXiv:2005.12057. The classical symmetric-group centralizer structure and generic Möbius inversion are prior tools, not independent novelty claims. An earlier formula using alternate terminology may remain. The complete two-variable quandle polynomial is not claimed. Independent audit is absent.
