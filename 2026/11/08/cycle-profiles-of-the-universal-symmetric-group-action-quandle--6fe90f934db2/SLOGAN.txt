The complete cycle profile of every right translation in the universal symmetric-group action quandle is determined by a divisor-and-coprimality sum over the cycle multiplicities of \(h^k\). In particular, its probability of a random fixed pair is \(\bigl(\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}p(j)\bigr)/(n\,n!)\), and every right translation has the same exact order \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,n)\) when \(n\ge3\).
