For a uniform random permutation, the conditional mean of ordinary inversions given the full cycle type depends only on fixed points and 2-cycles. The covariance with the number of \(j\)-cycles vanishes for every \(3\le j<n\), while the \(j=1,2,n\) contributions are exactly \(-(n+1)/6\), \(1/12\), and \(1/6\), respectively.
