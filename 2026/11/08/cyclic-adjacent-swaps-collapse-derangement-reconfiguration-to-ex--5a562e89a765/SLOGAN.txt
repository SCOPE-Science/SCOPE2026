Cyclic adjacent-position swaps of derangements preserve the excedance count, and this is the *only* obstruction to connectivity: for every \(n\ge3\), precisely \(n-1\) components occur, with classical derangement Eulerian sizes and exactly two isolated cyclic shifts. Permitting just the first-last-position swap collapses the \(2^{n-2}\) components of ordinary linear adjacent swaps to these \(n-1\) classes.
