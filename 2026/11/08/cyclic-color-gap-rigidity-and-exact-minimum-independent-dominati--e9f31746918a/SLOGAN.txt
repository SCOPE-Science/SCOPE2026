Every minimum independent dominating set of the direct product \(C_n\times K_q\), for \(n\ge6\) and \(q\ge3\), is a cyclic sequence of \(\lfloor n/3\rfloor\) empty layers alternating with monochromatic runs of at least two singleton layers, and neighboring runs have different colors. Their exact number is \(\frac{n}{k}\binom{k+r-1}{r}((q-1)^k+(-1)^k(q-1))\), where \(k=\lfloor n/3\rfloor\) and \(r=n-3k\).
