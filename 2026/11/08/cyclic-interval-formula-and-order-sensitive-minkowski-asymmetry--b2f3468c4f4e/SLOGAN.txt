Minkowski asymmetry of every chainlink polytope in the positive-link regime \(2\ell\le\min_i a_i\) is the maximum of explicit cyclic-interval ratios and one full-cycle ratio; all minimizing centers form a concrete difference-constraints polytope. Two cyclic orderings of the same lengths, \((4,4,20,20)\) and \((4,20,4,20)\) at \(\ell=2\), have different asymmetries \(4/3\) and \(6/5\).
