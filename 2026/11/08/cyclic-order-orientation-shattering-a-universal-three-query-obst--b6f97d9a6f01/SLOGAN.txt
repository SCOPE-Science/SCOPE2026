Three cyclic triple-orientation questions are jointly free exactly when they are not three faces of one four-label set; this holds on arbitrarily many labels. On six labels, the complete shattering complex has face polynomial \(1+20z+190z^2+1080z^3+3420z^4+1680z^5+30z^6\), VC dimension \(6\), and ten symmetry orbits of facets across three distinct sizes.
