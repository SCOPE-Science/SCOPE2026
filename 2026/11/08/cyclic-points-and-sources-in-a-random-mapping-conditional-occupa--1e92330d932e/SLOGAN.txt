In a uniform random mapping, conditioning on exactly \(k\) cyclic vertices makes the zero-indegree count exactly an empty-box count for \(n-k-1\) throws into \(n\) boxes, measured on \(n-k\) designated boxes. The conditional expected count strictly decreases with \(k\), yet the normalized cyclic-point and zero-indegree counts converge to independent Rayleigh and Gaussian limits.
