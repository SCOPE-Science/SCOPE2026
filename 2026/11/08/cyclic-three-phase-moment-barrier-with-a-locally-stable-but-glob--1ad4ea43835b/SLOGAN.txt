A directed three-phase exponential feedback chain obeys the strict Erlang fourth-moment-ratio floor \(\mu_2\mu_4/\mu_3^2\ge6/5\), with equality only without feedback and with equal holding times. Equal holding times remain locally optimal at every fixed return probability, yet sufficiently strong feedback admits finite heterogeneous rate choices with a strictly smaller ratio; the two specified benchmark designs cross at the unique root in \((0,1)\) of \(p^4+48p^3+148p^2+16p-60=0\).
