The coordinate cycle on the smooth Klein cubic \(\sum_{i\bmod p}X_i^2X_{i+1}=0\), for every prime \(p\ge5\), has integral fixed scheme \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z_{(p)}[\lambda]/\Phi_p(\lambda)\). Its \(p-1\) distinct generic fixed points collide in characteristic \(p\) to one curvilinear fixed point of length \(p-1\), while the ambient cubic remains smooth.
