The nine-dimensional two-generator quantum complete intersection over every finite field has an exact, parameter-dependent count of radical elements of each nilpotency index from one through five. Cyclotomic resonances cause sharp gaps: under \(1-\lambda+\lambda^2=0\) with \(1+\lambda+\lambda^2\ne0\), index four disappears even while index five occurs, whereas \(1+\lambda+\lambda^2=0\) makes every radical element cube zero despite a nonzero fourth radical power.
