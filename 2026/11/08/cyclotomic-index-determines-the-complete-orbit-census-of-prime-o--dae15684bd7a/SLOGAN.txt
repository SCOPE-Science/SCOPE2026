On every prime-order generalized Paley graph with cyclotomic index \(k\ge3\) and even degree \(d=(p-1)/k\), the characteristic-\(p\) neighbor-sum map has one Jordan block of size \(k+1\) and \(d-1\) blocks of size \(k\), all at eigenvalue \(d\). Every configuration is already periodic, and the complete temporal-orbit census consists of one fixed state and two explicitly counted nontrivial period classes.
