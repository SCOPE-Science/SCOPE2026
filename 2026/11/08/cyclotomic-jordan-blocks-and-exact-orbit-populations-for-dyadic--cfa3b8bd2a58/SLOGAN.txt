For every open ladder with \(2^r-1\) rungs, \(r\ge2\), the reversible characteristic-two neighbor-sum wave has exactly two cyclotomic primary blocks of lengths \(2^r\) and \(2^r-2\), forcing sharp global period \(3\cdot2^r\). Its complete fixed-point and primitive-orbit populations follow from an explicit two-block formula over every finite field of characteristic two.
