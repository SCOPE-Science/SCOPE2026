For two prime-field hyperbolas, cross-Fourier sampling is invertible exactly when their parameter product is a square; otherwise a single quadratic-character mode is lost. Every cyclotomic-local Smith factor is explicit, with finite valuations \(0,1,1,\ldots,m-1,m-1,m\) in the invertible case and the last factor zero in the nonsquare case, where \(m=(p-1)/2\).
