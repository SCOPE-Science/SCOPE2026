For the affine plane Chebyshev arrangement \(T_b(x)=T_a(y)\) with a line at infinity and coprime \(2\le a<b\), degree-one winding classes generate the full chamber-function algebra **only** when \((a,b)=(2,3)\). Otherwise the ungenerated dimension is at least \(\frac{(a-1)(b-1)}2-a+1\), because a degree-\(a\) vertical projection permits at most \(a+1\) winding levels.
