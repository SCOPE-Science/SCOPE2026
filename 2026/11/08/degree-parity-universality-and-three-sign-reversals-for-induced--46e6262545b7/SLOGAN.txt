In \(G(n,p)\), the covariance between the count of any induced \(k\)-vertex motif and the number of odd-degree vertices depends, after division by the motif’s mean count, only on how many vertices of that motif have odd degree. For the induced five-vertex path and every even graph order at least six, this dependence changes sign three times, exactly at \((1-5^{-1/4})/2\), \(1/2\), and \((1+5^{-1/4})/2\).
