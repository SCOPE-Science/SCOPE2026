Among finite equicharacteristic Gorenstein local rings of Hilbert function \((1,n,1)\) with \(n\ge2\), the ordinary simple zero-divisor graph's degree multiset determines the entire unital ring-isomorphism type. Its three degree strata are controlled by the number of isotropic vectors of a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form, distinguishing the even-dimensional Witt types in odd characteristic and alternating versus nonalternating forms in characteristic two.
