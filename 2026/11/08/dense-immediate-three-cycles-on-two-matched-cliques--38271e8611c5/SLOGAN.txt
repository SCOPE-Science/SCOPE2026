For three-state synchronous excitation on two matched \(n\)-cliques, the number of configurations already in primitive three-cycles is exactly \(9^n-6\cdot6^n+15\cdot4^n-12\cdot3^n+3\). Consequently a uniformly chosen initial state is immediately three-periodic with probability tending to one at deficit \(6(2/3)^n+O((4/9)^n)\), even though no three-cycle exists for \(n<3\).
