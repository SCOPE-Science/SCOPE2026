For cyclic traffic Rule 184, a factorized two-variable rational generating function counts every pair of inputs that collide after one update, separately at each conserved particle number. The unconditional collision probability decays as \((\lambda/4)^n\) with \(\lambda=\tfrac12+\sqrt{\tfrac54+\sqrt3}\), while conditioning both inputs to exact half filling multiplies the leading rate by an explicit factor of order \(\sqrt n\).
