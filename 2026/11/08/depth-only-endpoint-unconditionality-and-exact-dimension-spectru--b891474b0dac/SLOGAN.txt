Every finite fair dyadic Haar tree has sharp real signed \(L^\infty\) unconditionality norm determined solely by its deepest leaf, regardless of how many other branches have been pruned. At fixed \(N\)-leaf dimension, the complete spectrum is \(\{F_h:\lceil\log_2N\rceil\le h\le N-1\}\), with shallow trees minimizing and comb trees maximizing, where \(F_h=2h/3+5/9+(4/9)(-1/2)^h\).
