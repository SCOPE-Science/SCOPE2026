Every optimal rich-neighbor edge-colouring of \(K_{2,n}\), for \(n\ge3\), has one unique two-edge rich column and is otherwise determined by a derangement of \(n-1\) colours. There are exactly \(n^2(n+1)(n-1)!\,!(n-1)\) named optimum colourings and \(p_{\ge2}(n-1)\) equivalence classes under vertex and colour relabelling.
