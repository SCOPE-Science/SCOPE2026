For every connected non-dihedral Alexander quandle of prime order \(p\) over a field of characteristic \(p\), its entire derivation Lie algebra splits into explicitly enumerated ordinary multiplication operators and truncated polynomial vector fields, with an exact bracket table. The derivation dimension is \(|\{1\le d<p:a^d+(1-a)^d=1\}|+(p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(\operatorname{ord}_p(a),\operatorname{ord}_p(1-a))\), taking the distinct values \(2,3,4\) already for three non-dihedral quandle parameters of order seven.
