For every three-block rank-two toric positroid with parallel-class sizes \((a,b,1)\), \(2\le a\le b\), the projective dual in its Plücker linear span is the rank-at-most-\(a\) locus of one augmented matrix. Its dual defect is \(b-a\), its degree is \(\binom{b+1}{a}\), and balanced classes have a single irreducible adjugate equation.
