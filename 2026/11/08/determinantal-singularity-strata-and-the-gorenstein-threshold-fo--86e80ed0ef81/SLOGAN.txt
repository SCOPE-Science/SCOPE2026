For every rank \(r\ge3\), parallel extensions of \(U_{r,r+1}\) with arbitrary consecutive block sizes have exactly the Segre singular strata predicted by their nontrivial blocks, with transverse rank-one \(a_i\times r\) determinantal cones. They are \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein precisely when they are Gorenstein, namely when each block has size \(1\) or \(r\); the local multiplicity is \(\binom{a_i+r-2}{a_i-1}\).
