Every maximal component of the rank-two quasisymmetric Grassmannian has a complete explicit singular-locus classification: one or two disjoint projective spaces, with transverse rank-one \(2\times s\) determinantal cones. The singular multiplicity is \(s\), and the transverse germ is an ordinary threefold node at \(s=2\) but is not \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein at \(s\ge3\).
