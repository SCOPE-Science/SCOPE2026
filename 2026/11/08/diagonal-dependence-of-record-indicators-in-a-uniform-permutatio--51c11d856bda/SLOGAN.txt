For a uniform random permutation, a prefix-record indicator and an inverse-permutation prefix-record indicator are independent at distinct indices; their entire cross-covariance matrix is diagonal, with entry \((i-1)/(i^2\binom{n}{i})\) at index \(i\). The exceptional dependence occurs exactly when the first \(i\) positions contain the first \(i\) values, giving a direct combinatorial mechanism.
