For every convex quadrilateral, Minkowski asymmetry is exactly \(2-\min\{\alpha,\beta\}/(1-\max\{\alpha,\beta\})\), where \(\alpha,\beta\) are the shorter-part fractions of its two diagonals. Applied to any \(\lambda\)-Steiner symmetral of a triangle, this yields the complete sharp directional range \(2-2\lambda\le s\le2\), with precise equality cases and a formula at every intermediate direction.
