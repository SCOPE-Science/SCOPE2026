Independent-set token sliding preserves diamond-freeness, so the clique complex of every such reconfiguration graph collapses to a graph. For \(k\) nonattacking rooks on an \(m\)-by-\(n\) board, the complete clique polynomial gives an explicit circle-wedge rank \(1-V_k+B_k[A(B-1)+B(A-1)]\), and the girth has sharp phases \(3,4,6,\infty\) according to the number of vacant rows and columns.
