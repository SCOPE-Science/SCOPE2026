Among graph-indicator windows built from the entire Maiorana–McFarland bent family \(x\cdot y+h(y)\), full Gabor matrix-lifting rank is determined exactly by the Walsh support sizes of the derivatives of \(h\). Quadratic perturbations uniquely minimize this rank, while a cubic perturbation increases it by \(21D/4\) with \(D=2^{2m}\), despite preserving perfect bent Walsh flatness.
