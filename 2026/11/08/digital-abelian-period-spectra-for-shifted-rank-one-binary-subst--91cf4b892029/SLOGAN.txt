For \(\varphi(0)=0^a1^b\), \(\varphi(1)=1^b0^a\), the exact substitution-power Abelian periods of every shifted fixed-point suffix are determined by whether the shift's base-\((a+b)\) digits meet the substitution's coincidence columns. At scale \(L^t\), precisely \(L^t-(L-1)(2\min(a,b))^{t-1}\) of the first \(L^t\) shifts have that pure Abelian period, whereas aligned shifts have exactly all positive multiples of \(L=a+b\).
