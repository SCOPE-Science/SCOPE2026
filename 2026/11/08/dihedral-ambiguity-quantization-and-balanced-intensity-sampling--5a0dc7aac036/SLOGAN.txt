For polynomial magnitudes on two transversely intersecting circles, the first possible nontrivial ambiguity degree is exactly the reduced denominator of the intersection angle divided by \(\pi\); every ambiguity quotient is a rational all-pass function of the corresponding dihedral invariant. Independently, exactly \(4d\) scalar intensity values reconstruct the entire degree-\(d\) intensity profile linearly, and a balanced \(2d+2d\) sampling design is invertible exactly when an explicit two-crossing sine-product determinant is nonzero.
