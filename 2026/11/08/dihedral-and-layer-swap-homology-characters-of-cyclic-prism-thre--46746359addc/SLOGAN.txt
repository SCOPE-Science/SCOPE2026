For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge5\), the complete rational \(D_n\times C_2\)-character of its ordinary three-cut homology is determined, distinguishing rotations, both reflection axes, and layer interchange. The invariant homology dimension is \((n-3)/2\) for odd \(n\), and zero for even \(n\); the previously established sphere-wedge rank is not claimed as new.
