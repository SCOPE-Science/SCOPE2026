For all \(t\ge3\) and \(n\ge2t\), cycle nonmatching complexes admit a concrete dihedral-equivariant harmonic-sphere model that determines every subgroup's geometric fixed-point type and an arithmetic family of lens-space rotation quotients. Remarkably, for \(t=3\) the *full* dihedral quotient is homeomorphic to \(S^3\) for every \(n\ge6\), even when the rotation action has fixed points.
