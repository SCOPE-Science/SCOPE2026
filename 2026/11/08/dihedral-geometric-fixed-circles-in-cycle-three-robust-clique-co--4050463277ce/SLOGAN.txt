For every \(n\ge6\), each reflection of the three-robust clique complex of \(C_n\) has a geometric fixed space PL-homeomorphic to a genuine unfilled polygon, and the antipodal rotation does too when \(n\) is even; all other nonidentity rotations have empty fixed space. The exact number of polygon vertices distinguishes the two reflection axes in even cycles, although all nonempty fixed spaces are topological circles.
