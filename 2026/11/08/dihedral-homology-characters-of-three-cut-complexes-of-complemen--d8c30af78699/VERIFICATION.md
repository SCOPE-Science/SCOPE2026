The standalone `verify.py` is executable with Python 3 standard library. `verification_output.txt` records the execution on this finalized scientific calculation. For every \(5\le n\le15\), all \(2n\) dihedral permutations are tested by explicit signed simplicial Lefschetz sums against the character formula; for \(5\le n\le10\) independent mod-two boundary ranks are also computed.

The all-parameter proof is in RESULT.md and relies on Alilooee--Faridi's Theorem 4.6 for the preexisting nonequivariant homology concentration. The finite computations do not prove that theorem or extrapolate to arbitrary \(n\). This check is not an independent external audit, and no integral representation assertion is tested.
