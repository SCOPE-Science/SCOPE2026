The complex moment-angle manifold of every polygon has explicit, complete dihedral symmetry characters in cohomology degrees three and four; its third cohomology is the permutation module on polygon nonedges. The dihedral quotient has rational Betti numbers \(b_3=\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\) and \(b_4=((n-2)(n-4)-3\mathbf1_{2\nmid n}-2\mathbf1_{3\mid n})/6\).
