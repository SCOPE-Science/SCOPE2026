# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The classification quantifies over all cycle lengths and all subgroups of their natural dihedral groups. Geometric fixed points are modeled using complete vertex orbits, not solely fixed vertices. Rotation quotient cases include the exceptional one-orbit and two-orbit cases. Reflection quotients give the explicitly defined forbidden-run interval complex, and a relative free-face pairing collapses every such complex onto a cross-polytope boundary. The published ordinary cycle homotopy theorem supplies the rotation-only homotopy types. The standalone verifier performs direct independent finite orbit comparisons, matching checks, DAG acyclicity and mod-two homology calculations. Limitation: finite verification is corroboration, not the unlimited proof; the latter uses the explicit combinatorial argument.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The 2020 cycle classification of Deshpande and Singh determines ordinary homotopy types, not pointwise geometric fixed spaces. Sawant's 2026 tree theorem determines vertex decomposability in a different graph family. Related dihedral character and robust-clique results concern different complexes or representations and do not imply these actual subgroup fixed-space homotopy types. Statement-level searches found no matching dihedral subgroup fixed-space classification. Risk remains from unindexed or differently named equivariant higher-independence results; indexed absence is not a novelty certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result gives an exact, motivated, infinite-family classification for every subgroup rather than a few isolated symmetries; distinguishes geometric fixed spaces from homology characters; captures the axis parity and nontrivial exceptional cases; and provides explicit collapses that explain why reflection-containing fixed spaces always have exactly one spherical homotopy summand even where ordinary cycle complexes have three. This supplies new structural information beyond previously available ordinary cycle homotopy types.
