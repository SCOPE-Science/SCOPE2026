Three equiaffine-arclength-uniform points on an ellipse form an acute Euclidean triangle with probability \(\frac14-\frac{3}{2\pi^2}\operatorname{Li}_2(\eta^2)\), where \(\eta=|a^2-b^2|/(a^2+b^2)\). The probability uniquely determines the ellipse's semiaxis ratio, with sharp global anisotropy bounds, and coincides exactly with a previously known but geometrically different circular point-enclosure probability.
