The model is an explicitly defined stationary isotropic no-thermal-noise run-and-tumble trap. Its stationary path-integral representation gives independent GEM interval weights and sphere directions. The direct Markov-generator balances yield the exact second and fourth moments, including the dimension-one boundary; spherical fourth-moment identities give the sharp excess-kurtosis formula. ## Value — PASS
For bounded-speed particles, a negative-versus-positive fourth cumulant is a direct, measurable shape diagnostic. The sharp crossing at dimension five separates a permanently platykurtic class from dimensions that become leptokurtic as the reorientation-to-relaxation ratio increases. More surprisingly, concentration of individual direction pairs as dimension grows does not imply concentration of the stationary particle's radius: temporal run durations leave a non-degenerate GEM concentration statistic. The conditional radial fluctuation theorem determines the next scale and prevents an incorrect ordinary-Gaussian approximation; the scaled coordinate limit exhibits a persistent normal variance mixture. These facts concern an experimentally meaningful isotropic active-particle model and a natural limit, not an arbitrarily selected finite graph or isolated numeric moment.
