For an isotropically harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle, the stationary coordinate's excess kurtosis is negative in dimensions at most four but changes sign at the sharp rate ratio \(\alpha=6/(d-4)\) in every dimension \(d\ge5\). In high dimensions the radius remains random with a GEM/Poisson–Dirichlet concentration law, while its angular fluctuations have a conditional Gaussian limit and fixed scaled coordinates approach a non-Gaussian normal variance mixture.
