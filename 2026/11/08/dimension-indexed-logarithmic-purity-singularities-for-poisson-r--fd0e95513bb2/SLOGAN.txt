For a continuous-time quantum walker on \(\mathbb Z^d\) fully reset by a Poisson clock, the stationary ensemble purity has its first nonanalytic weak-reset correction exactly at order \(r^d\log(1/r)\), with the explicit coefficient \((-1)^{d-1}\binom{2d}{d}/[4^d(\pi\kappa)^d(d-1)!]\). Its second Rényi entropy has a distinctive \(-\log\log(\kappa/r)\) term in dimension one, absent in every higher fixed dimension.
