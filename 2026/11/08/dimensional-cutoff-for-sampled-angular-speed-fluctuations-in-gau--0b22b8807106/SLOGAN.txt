The sampled angular-speed magnitude of every full-rank stationary Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck diffusion in spatial dimension \(d\ge2\) has a strictly positive \(w^{-d}\) exceedance tail, with an exact prefactor determined by the stationary density at the origin and the \(d\)-th innovation norm moment. Its second moment diverges in two dimensions but is finite in three or more dimensions, including an explicit circulating three-dimensional example with nonzero mean axial angular speed.
