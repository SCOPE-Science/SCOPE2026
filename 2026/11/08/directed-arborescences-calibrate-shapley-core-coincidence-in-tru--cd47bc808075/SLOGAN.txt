For directed trust games with at least two incoming peers per vertex and equal scores from those peers, the Shapley value can equal the unique core allocation under positive score calibration exactly when the graph has no edges between distinct strongly connected components. Every such calibration is proportional, within each component, to its rooted out-arborescence counts times the factor \((d_i+1)/(d_i-1)\), and is unique up to one scale per component.
