For a normalized real Gaussian–first-Hermite STFT window with first-Hermite power fraction \(s\), the coincidence limit of its white-noise spectrogram-zero pair intensity is \(2s^3(4+7s-8s^2)/(1+2s)^2\) along time separations but \(6s^4/(1-2s+4s^2)^2\) along frequency separations. The resulting cubic versus quartic small-admixture onset reveals strongly anisotropic opposite-charge zero clustering, although the pure Gaussian and pure first-Hermite endpoint contact limits are isotropic.
