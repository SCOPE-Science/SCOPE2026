# Review of directional fourth-cumulant phases

## Correctness — PASS

The four-state orientation generator has a unique uniform stationary law for every nonnegative rate triple with positive total quarter-turn rate. The tilted-generator characteristic polynomial is obtained directly, and its simple leading eigenvalue yields the long-time cumulant rates. The fourth-order coefficient was checked both symbolically and using a separate finite-time moment-system matrix exponential. Strict positivity of \(F+Q^2\) proves the angular-sector classification; differentiating the explicit axial threshold establishes the interior maximum and the reentrant example. These exact arguments cover all stated parameter boundaries. The finite-time numerical comparisons are supplementary and do not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS with publication-priority qualification

The closest source is Mallikarjun and Pal, arXiv:2209.05912 (2022), Sections II.1–II.3 and Appendices A–B. It already gives the four-state chiral model, the Fourier-Laplace propagator, the long-time variance and the leading order \(t^2\) fourth raw moment. Those objects are explicitly credited. Its inspected treatment does not state the subleading fourth cumulant's angular sign sectors, the sharp turning-reversal frontier, or the nonmonotone reentrant boundary. The generic known propagator can in principle be differentiated to obtain the result; the novelty assessment concerns this explicit nontrivial sharp phase analysis, not a new generator. The published summary of Loewe et al., arXiv:2305.03663 (2023), treats 90-degree tumble-turn anisotropy rather than a general left/right/half-turn chirality phase diagram. Targeted published-record searches and scientific comparison with related results did not disclose an equivalent or stronger classification. Unindexed prior results and more general cumulant calculations remain risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This gives an exact physically interpretable boundary separating opposite long-time non-Gaussian signatures in different observation directions despite identical diffusive variance. It also proves a genuinely reentrant response when reversal frequency alone is changed, including a sharp algebraic maximum of the chirality threshold. It is a structural characterization of the source's full natural three-rate model rather than an arbitrary finite numerical slice. The practical inference of such signs from noisy data is not established.
