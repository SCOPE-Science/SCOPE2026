# Scientific assessment

**Correctness (PASS).** The transient generator is explicitly normalized with unit hopping, center detuning, a single sink jump at site 3 and a single dephasing jump at site 2. The stationary resolvent equations yield an exact mean absorption time; a rational sign reduction establishes the phase boundary and its first-order/continuous regimes. Strict convexity for the strong-sink regime and the unique sign change of the second-derivative cubic for weak sink prove the uniqueness of the positive global optimum. A nine-variable exact-rational generator constructed separately verifies the exact resolvent for 294 parameter triples. Its numerical cases are checks, not infinite proofs.

**Originality (PASS, qualified).** The main three-site transport predecessors are Jacob et al., arXiv:2407.21715 (bulk-dephased boundary-driven chain with a linear potential), and Contreras-Pulido et al., arXiv:1407.0924 (uniformly dephased triple dots with source reinjection and exact steady current). The latter is particularly close in detuning and sink geometry, but its dephasing acts on all sites, whereas here it acts only on the middle site, which changes the end-to-end coherence dynamics and the resulting enhancement surface. Coates et al., arXiv:2106.12567, and Lawrence et al., arXiv:2604.23005, examine localization and nonuniform noise but do not state the exact mean-first-absorption jump/coexistence classification in this model. Published finding searches and available prior-claim comparison did not expose the same result; that does not prove universal novelty. General resolvent theory and unindexed broader sources remain substantial priority qualifications.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The phase diagram answers a natural physical optimization question, with a precise discontinuous-to-continuous crossover and a nonzero two-minimizer coexistence curve. This global bifurcation is not established by a local derivative at zero noise and distinguishes whether an experimentally tunable noise rate should be added in a finite absorbing network. The sharp boundary at the fourth root of 32 and the closed-form positive coexistence rate are exact and structurally motivated.

The result is restricted to one excitation, three sites, one detuned and dephased central site, equal unit hoppings, a Markovian sink and no reinjection. It does not determine experimental validity for arbitrarily strong dephasing, and the publication-priority risks noted above remain. No independent external audit has been conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
