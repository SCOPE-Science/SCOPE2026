For every even-degree complex Fermat surface, projection from a non-Galois quasi-Galois center has a reduced branch curve consisting of smooth Fermat curves with exactly \(d\) common order-\(d\) tangencies. Its full geometric monodromy group is the hyperoctahedral group \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^m\rtimes S_m\), much larger than its order-two deck group.
