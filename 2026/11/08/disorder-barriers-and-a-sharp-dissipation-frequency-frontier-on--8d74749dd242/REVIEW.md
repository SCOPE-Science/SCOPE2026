The proof treats all positive edge activities and all physically admissible circulations, including the reversible diabolic exception. ## Originality — PASS, qualified

The recent photonic sources (Huang et al. 2026, arXiv:2606.15935; Longhi 2025, DOI 10.1002/qute.202500539) concern discrete-time Floquet/unistochastic maps rather than the parameterized continuous-time generator. Kolchinsky--Ohga--Ito 2024 and 2026 give broad EPR and affinity-based spectral bounds; Xu--Meng 2026 provides a particularly relevant geometric-mean comparison bound for nonuniform Markov cycles. Those results do not give the exact three-site disorder no-oscillation threshold or the fixed-total-symmetric-rate, fixed-EPR sharp optimum. In particular, a positive upper frequency bound in Xu--Meng cannot imply exact nonoscillation for the (1,1,3) profile. Targeted published-finding and literature comparisons found no covering equivalent; absence is not proof of uniqueness. Earlier unindexed specializations remain possible.

## Value — PASS

The explicit barrier separates broken detailed balance from actual oscillatory relaxation. In the natural three-state minimal ring, strong edge disorder can block oscillations even as the required driving becomes unbounded. At the same total microscopic activity and thermodynamic budget, the unique optimal design is homogeneous, with a sharp nonlinear entropy-frequency relation. These are structural classification and design statements, not merely eigenvalue calculations at sampled parameters.

## Scientific limitations

The result is about strictly positive rates, a uniform stationary distribution, a three-state continuous-time generator, and exact stationary entropy production. It does not predict the coherence of the discrete-time quantum walks that motivate the problem, does not classify arbitrary larger cycles, and has not undergone independent audit.
