On a uniformly stationary, three-site continuous-time Markov ring, unequal symmetric edge activities can forbid oscillatory relaxation even when entropy production becomes arbitrarily large: the sharp feasibility condition is \(\Delta^2<3(\min_i g_i)^2\). At fixed total symmetric rate and entropy-production budget, the unique maximizer of relaxation frequency (up to orientation) is the uniform ring, and its exact frequency is given by an invertible nonlinear entropy equation.
