For triangular graphs \(T_n=L(K_n)\), both the all-set dissociation polynomial and the inclusion-maximal dissociation polynomial have explicit exponential generating functions arising from disjoint edges and two-edge paths on \(n\) labels. Inclusion-maximal dissociation sets attain every size from \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) through \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\), without gaps.
