For the crown graph \(C_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the dissociation (\(2\)-independence) complex has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{2}}S^2\), in sharp contrast to the one-dimensional sphere wedge for \(K_{n,n}\). Its entire face polynomial and all inclusion-maximal dissociation sets are classified: the facets are two complete shores and \(\binom n2\) four-vertex matched-pair configurations.
