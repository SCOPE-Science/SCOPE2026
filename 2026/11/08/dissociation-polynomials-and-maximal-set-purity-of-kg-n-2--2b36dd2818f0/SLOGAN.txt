Every dissociation set in the two-subset Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\) is governed by an intersecting-edge family or a six-edge four-set, yielding a complete cardinality polynomial. Its inclusion-maximal dissociation sets are precisely full stars and full four-set families (with a small-order exception), so uniform dissociation occurs exactly at \(n=4\) and \(n=7\), and the maximal-cardinality spectrum has arbitrarily long gaps.
