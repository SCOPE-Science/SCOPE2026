For every finite weighted cactus metric graph, the intrinsic distance density of two independent arclength-uniform points jumps downward exactly at half the circumferences of its cycle blocks, with jump size \(2c\,m(c)/L^2\). Consequently this single scalar distance distribution determines the complete multiset of cycle circumferences, the graph's total length, and its first Betti number, even with coincident cycle lengths and arbitrary bridge attachments.
