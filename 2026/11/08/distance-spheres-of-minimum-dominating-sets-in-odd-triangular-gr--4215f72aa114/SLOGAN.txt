The minimum dominating sets of an odd triangular graph are near-perfect matchings, and their complete one-exchange distance profile has exponential generating function \(\exp((1-x^2)z)(1-2xz)^{-1-x/2}\). This determines every distance-sphere cardinality and a parity-dependent exact number of diametral configurations; the underlying shortest-path formula is already implicit in earlier flip-distance work.
