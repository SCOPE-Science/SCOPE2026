For every \(n\ge3\), von Mises weighting pins two different reflection-symmetric circular Lloyd fixed-codebook branches near the regular \(n\)-gon: their exact first-order intrinsic and chordal deformations have distinct coefficients \(B_{\mathrm C}(\pi/n)>B_{\mathrm I}(\pi/n)>0\). In fact no regularly spaced codebook, in any orientation, is fixed by either map at any positive concentration, although both near-uniform branches attract within the reflection-symmetric subspace.
