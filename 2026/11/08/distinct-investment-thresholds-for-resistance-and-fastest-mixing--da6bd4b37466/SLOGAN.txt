On the complete network with two equal communities and a common surcharge for edges within each community, resistance minimization stops buying intracommunity edges at \(c_R=4m/(4m-3)\), but fastest continuous-time mixing stops only at \(c_M=m/(m-1)\). The resulting nonempty price window forces opposite edge-investment decisions, and its rescaled boundaries converge to \(3/4\) and \(1\), respectively.
