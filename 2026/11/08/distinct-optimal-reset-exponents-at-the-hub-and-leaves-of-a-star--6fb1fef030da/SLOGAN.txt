For a reset random walk on the star \(K_{1,m}\), center-reset entropy is maximized at a probability asymptotic to \(m^{-1/2}\), while **every** leaf-reset entropy optimizer is asymptotic to \(m^{-3/4}\). The corresponding entropy gains above the no-reset baseline are asymptotic to \(m^{-1/2}/2\) and \(m^{-3/4}\), quantifying a sharp root-location effect on stochastic transport entropy.
