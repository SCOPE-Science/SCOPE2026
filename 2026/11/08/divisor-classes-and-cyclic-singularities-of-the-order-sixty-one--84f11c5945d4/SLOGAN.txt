For the canonical order-\(61\) action on the Klein quartic threefold, the quotient has five identical noncanonical singularities of type \(\frac1{61}(8,19,33)\), but its global Weil classes realize only a two-dimensional subspace of their five local divisor-class groups. Explicitly \(\operatorname{Cl}(Y)=\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/61\), \(\operatorname{Pic}(Y)=61\mathbb Z\), and every local minimal log discrepancy is \(22/61\).
