On finite permutation Kripke frames, the cycle lengths validating any basic modal formula are either all positive integers or a finite divisor-closed set \(A\), and every such set is attainable with minimum modal depth \(\max A\). For a uniform \(n\)-point permutation frame, finite nonempty \(A\) gives the exact validity probability \([z^n]\exp(\sum_{a\in A}z^a/a)\) and a sharp decay exponent \(1/\max A\).
