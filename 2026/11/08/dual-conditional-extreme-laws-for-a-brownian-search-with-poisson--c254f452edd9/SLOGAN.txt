For Brownian search stopped at first passage, an exact joint law of the path maximum and the number of Poisson resets yields two different conditional extreme phenomena: Gumbel growth after many resets and a size-biased geometric reset count on exceptionally high excursions. A sharp low-rate crossover makes the latter count Bernoulli, with zero-reset probability \(e^{-u}\) when the extreme threshold is scaled by the reset diffusion length.
