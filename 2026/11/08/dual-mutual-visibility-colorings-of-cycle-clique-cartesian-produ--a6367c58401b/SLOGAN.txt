For \(n\ge5\), dual mutual-visibility colorings of \(C_n\square K_m\) exist exactly at circumference \(n=6\), where the chromatic number is \(3\lceil m/2\rceil\). The only nonempty dual sets are single horizontal adjacent pairs and, when \(n=6\), antipodally shifted pairs in two distinct layers; this also yields the complete dual visibility polynomial and an exact factorial formula for every optimal partition.
