In the nine-point affine plane, the simplicial complex of point sets containing no full affine line is homotopy equivalent to \(S^2\vee\bigvee^{11}S^3\), while its Alexander-dual theta complex is \(\bigvee^{11}S^3\vee S^4\). Explicit acyclic matchings and zero integral attaching degrees upgrade previously known homology ranks to full integral homotopy classifications.
