# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the 12-vertex grid, exhaustive independent-quartet enumeration constructs precisely the two prescribed simplicial complexes. The reproducible program checks all 1,415 and 2,679 nonempty faces, each directed Hasse incidence, every vertex-toggle pair, and the entire topological sort of both reversed-matching digraphs. Its counts show exactly one critical zero-cell and 20 critical five- or four-cells, giving the two integral homotopy types by the standard acyclic discrete Morse theorem. Signed simplicial Lefschetz traces independently produce the character on all four reflection-group elements, and direct fixed-triple counts prove the rational representation comparison. No finite tests are being extrapolated to larger grids.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with residual risk)
The first-public 2026 work of Filakovský classifies \(k=2,3\) rectangular grids and arbitrary \(k\) for *edge-only* square attachment; it explicitly proposes the corner-gluing case at \(k>3\) as a conjecture. The present \(k=4\), three-by-four case requires corner gluing. The 2024 work of Chandrakar and collaborators treats total \(k\)-cut complexes on two-row grids and total three-cut complexes on three-row grids, not total four-cut on the present three-row grid. Adjacent finite graph cases and \(k=3\) equivariant results do not imply this homotopy and rational character pair. A source-by-source comparison and searches under dual aliases, graph parameters, homotopy and reflection characters found no dominating theorem. Unindexed or independently tabulated work remains a real risk; search absence is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The case is the next non-square, twelve-vertex three-row rectangular grid at the first conjectural threshold \(k=4\). It determines both Alexander-dual **integral homotopy types**, rather than Betti numbers alone, and identifies nontrivial reflection traces as a concrete twenty-dimensional group representation. It provides a finite, independently replayable boundary test for the unresolved corner-gluing extension, and the square-triple representation exhibits the expected geometry of the six grid cells. This does not assert a general theorem.

## Closest literature and limits
Filakovský (2026), Theorem 1, Corollary 3 and Conjecture 5; Chandrakar–Hazra–Rout–Singh (2024 preprint; 2026 journal), Theorem 1.1. Differences in \(k\), the number of rows, and the attachment condition prevent these results from implying the present one. Integral equivariance and the general \(3\times n\) case remain open here; literature completeness is not guaranteed.
