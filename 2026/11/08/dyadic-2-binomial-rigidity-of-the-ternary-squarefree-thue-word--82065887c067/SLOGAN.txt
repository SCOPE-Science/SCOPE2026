At every dyadic length \(n=2^j\) with \(j\ge2\), all \(3n\) factors of the ternary squarefree Thue word remain distinct under 2-binomial equivalence: \(b_{x,2}(n)=3n\). Just four letter-count and position-sum statistics distinguish every factor, contrasting sharply with bounded 2-binomial complexity of the binary Thue–Morse word.
