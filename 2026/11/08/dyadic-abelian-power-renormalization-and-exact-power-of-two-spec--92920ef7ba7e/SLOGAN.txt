In every generalized period-doubling word, the starting positions of Abelian powers at period \(2^k d\) are exactly \(2^k\)-fold expansions of those at period \(d\). At every power-of-two period, squares and cubes occur with respective exact densities \(1/(2^m-1)\) and \(1/[2(2^m-1)]\), while no Abelian fourth power occurs; a common-prefix substitution identity proves these claims for every alphabet size \(m\ge2\).
