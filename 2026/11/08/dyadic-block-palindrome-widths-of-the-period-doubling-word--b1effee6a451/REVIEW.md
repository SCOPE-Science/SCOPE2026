# Scientific review: dyadic block-palindrome widths

**Correctness — PASS.** The shortest-border lemma is proved directly. For every even dyadic exponent, the paired-boundary valuation dichotomy partitions all allowed intervals and leaves center \(10\); exact counting gives \(L_{2m}=(2\cdot4^m+1)/3\). The injective morphism doubles border lengths while excluding odd-length borders, so \(L_{2m+1}=L_{2m}\). Independent brute-force maximization of finite examples and the full standalone verifier support, but do not replace, the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** Goto et al. (arXiv:1806.00198) prove a general greedy algorithm; Gabrić and Shallit (arXiv:2302.13147) count general widths and prove bounded random-word expectation; Barro et al. (Australas. J. Combin. 83, 2022) classify sequential palindromic Ziv–Lempel/Crochemore factorizations of the same fixed point. None determines these exact dyadic mirrored-equal-block widths. Matched source texts and public mathematical-record queries were compared by claim implication, not merely keyword; unindexed equivalents cannot be excluded. The same-word 2022 full-text material was available as extracted text but not as a served screenshot.

**Value — PASS.** A natural maximal factorization invariant has a complete all-scale classification in a canonical automatic word, with explicit unique optimal block structure and a nonconvergent normalized two-cycle \(2/3,1/3\). The 2023 literature instead finds bounded expected width for random binary words. The contribution is not a finite-table increment or a trivial restatement of the generic algorithm.

**Scope.** The theorem concerns prefixes of lengths \(2^k\) only, and its numerical checks are finite diagnostics. A separate independent scientific review has not occurred.
