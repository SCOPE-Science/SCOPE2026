For the period-doubling word, the largest block-palindrome widths of the prefixes of lengths \(4^m\) and \(2\cdot4^m\) are equal, both exactly \((2\cdot4^m+1)/3\) for every \(m\geq0\). Their normalized widths have distinct subsequential limits \(2/3\) and \(1/3\), with the optimal factorization explicitly determined by two-adic valuations.
