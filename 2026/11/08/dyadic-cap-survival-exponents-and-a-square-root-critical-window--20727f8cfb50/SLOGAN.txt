For normalized translations of a round sphere, cap nonentrance has an exact inverse-angle integral; as translation strength approaches one, rare-survivor probabilities exhibit a dyadic staircase of powers of \(1-t\). The angular resonances \(\varepsilon=\pi/2^m\) have half-order exponents and a universal square-root crossover \(\bigl(\sqrt{z^2+2}-z\bigr)^d\).
