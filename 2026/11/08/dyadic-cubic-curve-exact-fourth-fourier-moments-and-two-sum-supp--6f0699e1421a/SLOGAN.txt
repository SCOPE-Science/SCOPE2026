For the cubic graph in \((\mathbb Z/2^k\mathbb Z)^2\), the complete unweighted fourth Fourier moment is exactly \(2^{2k}(k^2-k+8)/4\), and the twofold sumset has cardinality \((5\cdot2^{2k}+13\cdot2^k-10)/18\) for odd \(k\), or \((5\cdot2^{2k}+14\cdot2^k-10)/18\) for even \(k\). Both laws follow from a valuation-stratified description of the ordered two-sum fibers, and the sumset occupies asymptotically \(5/18\) of the ambient group.
