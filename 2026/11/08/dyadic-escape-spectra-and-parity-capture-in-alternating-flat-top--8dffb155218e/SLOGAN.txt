For the alternating flat-top/full-tent pattern at every dyadic height \(u_m=1-2^{-m}\), the full first-capture law and the dimension of the exceptional survivor set come from one explicit finite Markov matrix, with Perron root satisfying \(\lambda_m^{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}(4-\lambda_m)=2\) for even \(m\) and \(=1\) for odd \(m\). The escape correction has an exact \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) coefficient, while almost all trajectories are ultimately captured by either a superstable cycle or a repelling fixed point according to parity.
