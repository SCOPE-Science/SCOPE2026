At each perturbation \(\delta=2^{-(k+2)}\) with integer \(k\ge2\), the generalized Hata–Yano induced core has an explicit invariant \(2k\)-point reflected random walk, with stationary weights and a complete cosine spectrum; these infinitely many cases lie below the previously treated deterministic-core threshold. On the full two-sided dyadic lattice, the **original** random maps are exactly a simple symmetric random walk for every \(0<\delta\le1/6\), yielding a pointwise arcsine occupation law independently of any induced-core limit theorem.
