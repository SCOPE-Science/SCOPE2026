The ordered threefold convolution of the dyadic parabola \(\{(x,x^2):x\in\mathbb Z/2^k\mathbb Z\}\) takes only zero, \(3\cdot2^k\), and one parity-dependent exceptional positive value, with explicit multiplicities for every exponent. Its sumset occupies asymptotically one-third of the ambient ring plane, yet its complete unweighted sixth Fourier moment has the uniform formula \(N^3(3N-2)\), where \(N=2^k\).
