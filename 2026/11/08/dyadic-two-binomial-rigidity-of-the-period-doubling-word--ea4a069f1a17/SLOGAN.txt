For the binary period-doubling word, every factor of length \(2^k\) is uniquely reconstructed from its number of ones and its scattered `01` count: its two-binomial complexity equals its factor complexity \(3\cdot2^{k-1}\), while its Abelian complexity is only two, for every \(k\ge1\). Distinct factors of length five already collide, so this exact rigidity is special to the dyadic scales.
