The two-read single-deletion ambiguity graph restricted to Dyck words has a canonical marked-positive-vertex edge bijection, and its unique maximum-degree vertex for \(n\ge3\) is \(1(10)^{n-1}0\), of degree \((n-1)^2\). Its edge count \(\sum_{m=1}^{n-1}m(2m+1)C_m/(m+2)\) matches a previously known Catalan-peaks sequence, giving that sequence a deletion-reconstruction interpretation rather than a new numerical evaluation.
