Among planar unit disks with an inner radius-\(a\) circular hole, concentric placement is a strict local minimizer of the two-point Beer visibility probability for every \(0<a<1\). Moving the hole by a small vector \(z\) raises the probability by exactly \(5a\lVert z\rVert^2/[\pi(1-a^2)^{3/2}]+O_a(\lVert z\rVert^4)\), and the sharp Hessian diverges as the annulus becomes thin.
