Every edge-open packing of the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) is a set of disjoint horizontal edges, vertical edges, and perpendicular two-edge paths with nonoverlapping row and column supports. This gives a complete two-sort exponential generating function and the sharp parity law \(\rho_e^o(K_m\square K_n)=\min(m,n)-\mathbf{1}_{\{m=n\text{ odd}\}}\), together with exact counts of all maximum packings.
