For every Mersenne Stirling column \(k=2^m-1\) with \(m\ge4\), all fixed multiplier pairs \(a>b\ge1\) eventually satisfy \(\nu_2(S(a2^n,k)-S(b2^n,k))=n+1+\nu_2(a-b)\), with an explicit sufficient cutoff. A uniform normalized divided-difference congruence transfers the previously known doubling-case valuation to arbitrary multiplier pairs.
