The exact distribution of prime-local obstructions to the universal integer-square invariant in reciprocal-sum sequences is an explicit independent Bernoulli product: \(q_2=1/48\) and \(q_p=1/[2p(p+1)]\) for odd primes. The entire empirical defect-count distribution converges to that product law in total variation at the effective rate \(O((\log\log X)/\log X)\), yielding universal-square parameter density approximately \(0.901295\).
