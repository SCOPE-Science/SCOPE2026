For binary chores comprising agent-specific zero-cost tasks and universally costly tasks, all efficient EFX allocations obey an exact multinomial occupancy window. For two agents starting with only common chores, adding one zero-cost-to-its-recipient task halves that count when the common-chore number is odd and increases it when even; in symmetric populations the share of efficient allocations satisfying EFX transitions sharply when private-task multiplicity reaches order \(\sqrt{u}\).
