For the normalized nearest-neighbor evolution on every prime square torus \(C_p\square C_p\) with \(p\ge5\), a fixed contiguous \(4\times2\) rectangle of eight sensors reconstructs every complex signal using \((p+1)(p+3)/8\) consecutive time samples. Seven sensors can never suffice, because an eight-dimensional eigenspace makes any smaller observation set invisible to some nonzero signal.
