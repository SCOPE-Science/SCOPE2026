In the truncated-octahedral graph, the nine rational scale-two Vietoris–Rips two-sphere classes split into eight local hexagon classes permuted without signs by octahedral symmetry, plus one determinant-character line. All eight local classes die at scale three, while the determinant line lives for \(2\le r<4\) and dies at scale four; thus the degree-two symmetry barcode is \(\mathbb Q[\{\pm1\}^3]\otimes\mathbf I_{[2,3)}\oplus\mathbb Q_{\det}\otimes\mathbf I_{[2,4)}\).
