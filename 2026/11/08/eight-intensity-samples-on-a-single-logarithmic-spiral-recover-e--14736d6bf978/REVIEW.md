## Value — PASS
The sample count eight is the absolute sharp minimum for complex three-dimensional phase retrieval, yet every measurement is of the constrained form \(|p(z)|^2\) at a point of a single fixed logarithmic spiral. The construction separates rank-one recovery from nine-dimensional linear intensity recovery and shows every arbitrarily short spiral arc supports locally generic cardinal-minimal sampling. This is a natural structured measurement problem, not a free choice of arbitrary frame vectors or an arbitrary numerical catalogue. It makes no global stability claim.

## Verification and limitations
The proof uses exact Gaussian-integer arithmetic, an abstract rank-two Hermitian difference criterion, an elementary odd-determinant argument, and real-analytic nonvanishing. The checker verifies the numerical certificate, not an infinite collection of curves or test signals. The scope is degree at most two; higher degree and uniform conditioning remain open.
