Eight squared-modulus samples of any complex quadratic polynomial at the Gaussian-integer spiral points \((1+i)^j\), \(0\le j\le7\), recover the polynomial up to global phase, and seven samples never suffice. The eight-sample Hermitian measurement kernel is one-dimensional but generated by a full-rank matrix, so arbitrary short subarcs of the same logarithmic spiral admit generic cardinal-minimal phase retrieval.
