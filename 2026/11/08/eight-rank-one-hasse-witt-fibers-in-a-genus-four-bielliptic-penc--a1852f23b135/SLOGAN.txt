In characteristic \(19\), the smooth genus-four double covers \(y^2z=x^3-xz^2\), \(w^2=xy+tz^2\) have Jacobian invariants \((19\text{-rank},a)=(1,3)\) exactly at the eight geometric parameters with \(t^4\in\{6,7\}\), and \((2,2)\) everywhere else on the smooth pencil. These eight parameters form two four-element symmetry orbits.
