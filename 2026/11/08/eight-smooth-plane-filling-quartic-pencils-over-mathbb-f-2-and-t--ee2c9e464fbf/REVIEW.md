# Review of smooth binary plane-filling quartic pencils

## Correctness (C): PASS
The degree-four vanishing space is enumerated by seven independent evaluation constraints. Every candidate is checked at all binary rational points; all 24 survivors are independently proved geometrically smooth by unit Jacobian ideals on three affine charts. The exact 8-pencil partition, projective orbit/stabilizer and matrix-field/Singer correspondence are verified by two different enumerations. For the base scheme, the explicit ideal decomposition, 13-dimensional affine quotient, three infinity points, transversality at seven rational points and local triple lengths account for the full Bézout length 16. Limits: no claims about all geometric parameter values or larger fields.

## Originality (O): PASS, with residual priority risk
The 2009 Homma--Kim classification covers individual minimum-degree filling quartics and the matrix criterion; neither its inspected pencil-free statements nor its automorphism section assert the eight-line decomposition of the smooth subset or the scheme-theoretic multiplicity-three residual base orbit. The 2022 Asgarli--Ghioca work poses smooth-linear-system questions but its inspected exact examples address conics and quadric surfaces, and its general existence bound does not classify binary plane-filling quartic pencils. The 2022 Asgarli--Ghioca--Reichstein general existence theorem (arXiv:2207.00918, Theorem 2) constructs degree-four smooth *nets* in the unrestricted space of plane quartics over \(\mathbb F_2\), whereas our sharper filling-specific constraint excludes any smooth net: these statements are compatible, and the general result does not cover the eight-pencil enumeration or base schemes. Earlier accessible work on smooth quartic rational-incidence conditions and smooth cubic pencils concerns different objects. Targeted published-result and literature searches did not reveal an equivalent pencil/base-scheme theorem. However, the older matrix representation makes this result a potentially discoverable refinement; an earlier unnoticed computation could subsume it.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS
The theorem solves the complete small-field case of a natural intersection of two motivated geometric conditions: extremal minimum-degree plane-filling curves and linear systems whose every ground-field member is smooth. The one-orbit classification, sharp maximal linear-subspace dimension, Singer-group parameterization and precise nonreduced base scheme reveal structure not captured by a count of individual curves. In particular, the triple base points matter for understanding the blowup resolving the pencils; they are not a routine decorative numerical invariant.
