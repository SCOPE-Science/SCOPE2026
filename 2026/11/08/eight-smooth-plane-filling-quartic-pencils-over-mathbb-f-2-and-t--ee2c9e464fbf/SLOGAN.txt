Over \(\mathbb F_2\), the \(24\) smooth minimum-degree plane-filling quartics partition into exactly eight three-member pencils, one for each Singer subgroup of \(\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\); no larger all-rationally-smooth linear system exists in their filling space. Each pencil has a nonreduced degree-\(16\) base scheme comprising the seven rational points and a degree-three closed point of geometric multiplicity three.
