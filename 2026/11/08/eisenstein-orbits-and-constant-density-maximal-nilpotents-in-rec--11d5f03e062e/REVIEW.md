# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Identify the rectangular Jordan centralizer with the full matrix algebra over a truncated-polynomial ring. Nilpotence is detected in the residue field; the Fine–Herstein finite-field count supplies \(q^{r^2-r}\) nilpotent residues. Smith normal form proves that one ground-field Jordan block is equivalent to a regular nilpotent residue and determinant of valuation one. A nonzero determinant derivative then gives the exact number of lifts for every residue. The orbit theorem follows from lifting a cyclic vector and identifying the maximal-Jordan centralizer with a single polynomial algebra. These steps apply in all finite characteristics, including the edge case \(r=1\). Two independently written exhaustive checkers verify finite cases, with no claim that finite computation proves all cases.

## Originality — PASS, with priority uncertainty
The 2022 primary work on two equal Jordan blocks classifies *possible* Jordan types, not the enumerated finite-field stratum. The 2010 finite-field commuting-classes paper is likewise an existence/classification study. A 2026 publicly available calculation proves the maximal-index distribution for lifts of one **fixed** Jordan reduction over dual numbers; this is a genuinely covered case and is excluded from the claimed increment. The additional uniform formula for all truncation lengths and the Eisenstein-characteristic-polynomial orbit classification are not statements identified in the inspected works or available mathematical-record searches. An unlocated broader chain-ring matrix conjugacy theorem might imply them; that is residual publication-priority risk, not a proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The single-maximal-Jordan-block stratum is a canonical geometrically open and algebraically natural part of the nilpotent commuting variety. The exact density, unexpectedly independent of the common block length, refines the known existence-oriented Jordan-type classification and is useful for random commuting nilpotents and finite matrix conjugacy. The uniform rational orbit classification supplies a stronger structural explanation and exact finite enumeration, rather than an arbitrary small-dimensional statistic. It is meaningful for every multiplicity \(r\) and arbitrarily large truncation length.

## Scope and limitations
Only equal-length blocks and the maximal nilpotency stratum are classified; the quoted prior fixed-dual-lift special case is not claimed as novel. All audits here are internal scientific checks; third-party mathematical validation and complete publication-priority assurance are not asserted.
