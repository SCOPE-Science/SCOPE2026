Among nilpotent matrices commuting with \(r\) identical Jordan blocks of length \(n\ge2\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), the fraction having one maximal Jordan block is exactly \(\prod_{i=1}^{r}(1-q^{-i})\), independent of \(n\). Their conjugacy classes under the invertible centralizer correspond bijectively to Eisenstein characteristic polynomials, and every class has the same explicit size.
