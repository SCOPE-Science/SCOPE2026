For the Narayana-cows recurrence \(u_0=u_1=0\), \(u_2=1\), \(u_{n+3}=u_{n+2}+u_n\), exactly eleven terms are divisible by \(2^k\) in every least period of length \(7\cdot2^{k-1}\), for all \(k\ge6\). A singular two-adic branch supplies eight zero residues while two nonsingular branches supply three, independent of the modulus exponent.
