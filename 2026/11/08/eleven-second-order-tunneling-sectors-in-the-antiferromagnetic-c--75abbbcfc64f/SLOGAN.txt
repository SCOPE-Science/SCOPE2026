For the \(60\)-site antiferromagnetic buckminsterfullerene Ising model, the graph of ground states connected by two-spin flips has exactly eleven components: one on \(15{,}360\) states and ten six-dimensional cubes on \(64\) states each. In uniform transverse-field perturbation theory, the giant component alone selects the leading second-order ground-manifold eigenvector because its tunneling spectral radius is at least \(29/4\), whereas each small cube has radius \(6\).
