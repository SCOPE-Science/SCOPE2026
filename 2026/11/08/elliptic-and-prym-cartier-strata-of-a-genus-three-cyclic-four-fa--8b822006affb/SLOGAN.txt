For the smooth genus-three cyclic-four family \(z^2=w^8+c w^4+1\), the elliptic-quotient and Prym Cartier loci are completely determined in characteristics \(7\) and \(11\): the Prym is superspecial only at \(c=0\) in characteristic \(7\), or at the two points \(c^2=5\) in characteristic \(11\). At the characteristic-eleven superspecial-Prym points the elliptic quotient is ordinary, and the Hasse determinant has a double zero even though the deeper rank-at-most-one determinantal locus is reduced.
