The additive energy of the inverse-square graph \(\{(x,x^{-2}):x\in\mathbb F_q^\times\}\) over every finite field has an exact formula: polynomial terms in characteristics two and three, but an elliptic Frobenius-trace correction in every characteristic at least five. The trace of the fixed elliptic curve \(v^2=(1-4z)(1-4z^2)\) also determines the complete two-frequency fourth moment and all extension-field energies by a second-order recurrence.
