# Review of the finite-field inverse-hyperbola three-step theorem

## Correctness

PASS. The sum-of-two discriminant and its normalization, the elliptic character-sum count for generic nonzero products, the singular product cases, and the axial/zero cases are reconstructed in the proof. The squarefreeness discriminant and the two rational infinity points yield the exact genus-one formula. Hasse's bound proves positive third-step counts for all nonzero targets when \(q\ge13\); the axes force the sharp lower graph-diameter bound. The finite-field checker exhausts the remaining \(q=3,5,7,9,11\) cases and verifies the formulas in additional odd extension and prime fields. Its finite checks are not substitutes for Hasse's infinite-family bound.

## Originality

PASS under bounded inspection. Shparlinski's 2011 survey supplies earlier hyperbola/Kloosterman context. Forey–Fresán–Kowalski–Wigderson establish spectral results for this exact generating set, including the ordinary Cayley variant; those known results are cited rather than rediscovered. Their Fourier eigenvalues determine a formal inversion for any convolution power, but the inspected theorem does not provide the explicit genus-one evaluation or the full odd-field diameter and exceptional-product table. A published odd-characteristic norm-one-torus three-sum classification is for the anisotropic torus rather than the present split torus. Historical priority is not independently established and full coverage by undiscovered earlier results cannot be excluded.

## Mathematical value

PASS. An exact graph-diameter classification for all odd field orders is a natural structural objective. The exceptional orders \(5,7,9\), the distinct exact-length omission at \(11\), and the elliptic-curve mechanism for larger fields distinguish the answer from a routine low-order computation. The full convolution formula supplies exact heat-kernel information beyond diameter.

## Scope and reproducibility

The field has odd prime-power order; results do not extend as stated to characteristic two. The checker is independent enumerative confirmation over finite selected fields; the general result uses Hasse's bound. The closest comparison is the ordinary Cayley variant of arXiv:2511.21351; historical priority remains a residual scientific risk. Independent external audit has not been carried out.
