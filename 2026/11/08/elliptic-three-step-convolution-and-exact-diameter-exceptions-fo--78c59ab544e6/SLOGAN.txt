For the odd-characteristic finite-field inverse hyperbola \(S=\{(t,t^{-1})\}\), the entire three-step additive convolution is an explicit elliptic-curve point count with elementary exceptional cases. Its Cayley graph is disconnected at \(q=3\), has diameter four exactly at \(q=5,7,9\), and diameter three for every odd prime power \(q\ge11\).
