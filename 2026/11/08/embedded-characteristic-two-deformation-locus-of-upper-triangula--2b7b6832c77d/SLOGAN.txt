For even \(n=2m\) in characteristic two, the reversal-inner involution parameter scheme of the upper-triangular matrix algebra is a smooth \((m^2+m-1)\)-dimensional reduction with an embedded square-zero direction supported in codimension \(m\). This infinitesimal direction is obstructed at second order, whereas the corresponding odd-dimensional scheme is smooth; exact finite-field counts hold in every characteristic.
