The commuting square-zero equation scheme inside the three-dimensional nilpotent centralizer of a rank-one \(3\times3\) nilpotent matrix has two reduced affine branches but, from two variables onward, an embedded nilpotent layer. For \(m\) commuting elements its transverse nilradical has length \((m-2)2^{m-1}+1\), with every graded piece and nilpotent power explicitly classified over arbitrary fields.
