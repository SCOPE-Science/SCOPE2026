On every equilateral metric ladder with at least three rungs, the sharp mean-zero first-order Sobolev evaluation norm is attained at exactly two points: the midpoints of its end rungs. Its squared norm is the rational function \((18n^{3}-18n^{2}-9n+10)/(12(3n-2)^{2})\), and all evaluations off the open end rungs fall below the optimum by an explicit positive gap.
