In odd characteristic, the direct-sum decomposition \(A_{r+1}=A_r\oplus x_{r+1}A_r\) produces a necessary-and-sufficient annihilator-weighted recursion. The complete four-stratum enumeration of square-zero elements in \(A_3\) has counts \(1\), \(q-1\), \(q(q^3-1)\), and \(3(q-1)q^3\) and multiplication ranks \(0,1,2,4\), giving the stated polynomial without extrapolation. The monomial cotangent-matrix argument classifies all odd-characteristic automorphisms. Frobenius and invertibility of the cotangent map handle all characteristic-two fields. Finite enumeration over \(\mathbb F_2,\mathbb F_3,\mathbb F_5\) verifies critical cases, but it is not the infinite-field argument.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
For a canonical family of monomial Artinian complete intersections, counting the full endomorphism monoid is a natural finite-ring invariant not determined by automorphism-group structure alone. Rank four is the first genuinely new threshold where square-zero elements can have interacting disjoint quadratic supports and the automorphism density falls to zero in odd characteristic. In characteristic two every radical element is square-zero and the density instead tends to one. These are sharp structural contrasts across an infinite family of finite fields, not a chosen numerical example. The theorem makes no unsupported claims for ranks beyond four.
