For the \(16\)-dimensional tensor product of four dual-number algebras over an odd finite field, the exact number of unital endomorphisms is \(q^{28}(q^3+5q-5)^4\), while the automorphism fraction is \(24(q-1)^4/(q^3+5q-5)^4\sim24q^{-8}\). This is the first rank through four where the fraction vanishes as the field grows; in characteristic two the corresponding automorphism fraction instead tends to one.
