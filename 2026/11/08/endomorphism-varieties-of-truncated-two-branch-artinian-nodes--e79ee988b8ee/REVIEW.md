# Scientific review: endomorphism varieties of truncated two-branch Artinian nodes

## Correctness — PASS
The defining generator-image equations split into two independent one-variable truncated-ring pair loci. The valuation proof gives an exhaustive irredundant union of linear subspaces and its count is verified by classifying zero and nonzero possible orders. Their product gives the full reduced geometric component decomposition; finite-field counts follow exactly. The tangent-space Nakayama criterion and independence of the two quadratic directions yield the invertible-versus-noninvertible component distinction. The source-level program `verify.py` exhaustively enumerates original equation solutions in thirteen small field/ring settings; `verification_output.txt` gives the reproducible results. Computation supports rather than replaces the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (subject to historical-priority limits)
The archival fiber-product treatment by Ananthnarayan–Avramov–Moore (arXiv:1005.1304v1), published work of Díaz and coauthors (arXiv:2408.02197; arXiv:2409.15081), and the public complete proof *Automorphisms of truncated coordinate-cross algebras over arbitrary fields* were compared on the same objects and all relevant parameters. The automorphism subgroup and its count were already addressed and are credited. None of the inspected materials states the complete all-(m,n) reduced endomorphism-component decomposition or its all-q point count. The precise polynomial and fiber-product aliases were separately searched in the mathematical research index. Residual risk is an unlocated general result about endomorphism varieties of monomial Artinian rings, so this review is not an independent or exhaustive priority certificate.

## Value — PASS
The endomorphism variety is a natural moduli invariant for the finite nodal algebra. The result gives its complete component count and equidimensionality, and separately exact finite-field rational-point counts. In equal branch lengths it demonstrates that most of the full-dimensional components are entirely noninvertible while the automorphism locus is confined to exactly two components. This is mathematically structural information beyond a simple reformulation of the known automorphism group.

## Limits and sources
The geometric claim is restricted to the reduced support: scheme nilpotents, local multiplicities, component intersections, conjugacy orbits and composition laws are not calculated. Principal sources are arXiv:1005.1304v1, arXiv:2408.02197, arXiv:2409.15081, and the public all-field automorphism classification noted in RESULT.md.
