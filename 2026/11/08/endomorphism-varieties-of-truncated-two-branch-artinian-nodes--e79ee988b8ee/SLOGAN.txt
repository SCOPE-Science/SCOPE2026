Every truncated two-branch Artinian node \(k[x,y]/(xy,x^m,y^n)\) has an equidimensional reduced endomorphism variety whose components are affine spaces; the precise component count and finite-field endomorphism count have closed formulas for all \(m,n\ge2\). In the equal-length family, only two components generically contain automorphisms once the branch length exceeds two.
