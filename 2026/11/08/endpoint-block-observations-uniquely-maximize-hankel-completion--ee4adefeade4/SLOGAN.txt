Among all ways of observing exactly \(2r\) anti-diagonals of a complex rank-at-most-\(r\) Hankel matrix of length \(n\ge2r+1\), the first \(r\) and last \(r\) uniquely maximize generic completion ambiguity. Their exact complex completion degree is \(\binom{n-r}{r}\); every other observation pattern has strictly smaller degree.
