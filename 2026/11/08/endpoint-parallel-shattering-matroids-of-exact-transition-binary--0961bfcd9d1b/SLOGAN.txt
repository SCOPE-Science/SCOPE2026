For length-\(n\) binary words with exactly \(k\) adjacent bit switches, the coordinate-shattering complex is a rank-\(\min\{k+1,n-k\}\) matroid with only the endpoint pair as an extra forbidden circuit: every smaller support not containing both endpoints is shattered. Its exact all-order polynomial is \(\sum_{j=0}^{\min(k+1,n-k)}[\binom{n}{j}-\binom{n-2}{j-2}]z^j\), while for fixed positive \(k\) the online dimension grows as \(k\log_2n+O_k(1)\) despite constant eventual VC dimension.
