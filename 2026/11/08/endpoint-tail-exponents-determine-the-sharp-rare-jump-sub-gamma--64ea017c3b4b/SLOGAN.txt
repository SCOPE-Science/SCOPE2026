For Gaussian-smoothed rare compound-Poisson laws whose jump tail decays with exponent \(\beta>0\) at a finite upper endpoint, the exact optimal variance-proxy sub-gamma pole obeys \(b/c_\lambda=L+(\beta+3)\log L+1-\log K+o(1)\). The endpoint exponent, invisible in the leading inverse-logarithmic decay, determines the first logarithmic correction for every global optimizer.
