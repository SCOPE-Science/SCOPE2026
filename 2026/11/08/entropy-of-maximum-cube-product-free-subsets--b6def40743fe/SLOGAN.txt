For sets of maximum size in \(\{1,\ldots,N\}\) containing no two distinct integers with a cubic product, the number of extremizers is exactly \(\lfloor N^{1/3}\rfloor 2^{H(N)}\), with an explicit finite squarefree-kernel tie count \(H(N)\). Its entropy has the sharp positive law \(\log\mathcal M(N)\sim \tfrac12(\log2)(\pi^2-9)\prod_p(1-3/p^2+2/p^3)\,N^{2/3}\).
