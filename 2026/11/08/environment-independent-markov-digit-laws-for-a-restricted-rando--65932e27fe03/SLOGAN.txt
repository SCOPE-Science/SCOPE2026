For Kalle–Maggioni's restricted two-map Lüroth system, every predetermined sequence of standard/alternating map selections produces exactly the same stationary digit law: a three-state Markov factor with an explicit common transition matrix. The digit-\(3\) autocovariance is \(\frac3{256}(-\frac13)^n\) at every positive lag, and the counting central limit theorem has variance \(75/512\), independently of the switching schedule.
