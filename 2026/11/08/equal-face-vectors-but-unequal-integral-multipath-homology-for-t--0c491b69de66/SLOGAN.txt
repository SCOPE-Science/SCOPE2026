The eight-vertex Wagner and cube graphs have fully bidirected multipath complexes with exactly the same face vector \((24,216,928,2016,2160,1008,144)\), yet different reduced integral homology. The Wagner ranks in dimensions three through six are \((4,41,32,10)\), versus \((6,45,36,12)\) for the cube; all these homology groups are torsion-free.
