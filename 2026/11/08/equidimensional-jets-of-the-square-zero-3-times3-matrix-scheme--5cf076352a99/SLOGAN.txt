For the scheme defined by \(X^2=0\) on \(3\times3\) matrices, every reduced jet space of length \(m\) has exactly \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor+1\) geometrically irreducible components, all of dimension \(4m+\lfloor m/2\rfloor\), in every characteristic. The number of square-zero matrices over any finite chain ring depends only on its length and residue-field size, with an explicit rational generating function.
