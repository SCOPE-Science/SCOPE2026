# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The equilibrium characterization follows from two incompatible no-improvement inequalities when the fully occupied opposite part has both colors; the converse checks every possible jump. Four disjoint monochromatic-side cases give the binomial census. The balanced incidence and the two welfare classes of \(K_{a,2}\) are exact deductions; Stirling's formula supplies the asymptotic. A separately coded direct move-payoff checker tested 15,136 configurations in 80 small parameter cases and additional welfare families. Finite checking corroborates but does not prove the infinite results.

## Originality — PASS, with bounded publication-priority risk

The closest primary reference is Narayanan–Sabbagh–Voudouris, arXiv:2305.17757, especially Theorems 6.1 and 6.3. Theorem 6.1 supplies a generic bound and Theorem 6.3 a star construction with a singleton minority; neither gives a biclique equilibrium classification, binomial census, balanced rarity result, or the exact two-connected two-minority inefficiency family. Agarwal et al. 2021 optimize same-color proportions rather than the current opposite-color proportions. Narayanan et al. 2025 study variety rather than fraction-of-opposite-color utilities. Searches for the exact biclique one-vacancy problem, its aliases, and welfare ratios revealed no covering theorem. An unindexed equivalent remains a residual risk; absence of a search hit is not novelty proof.

## Value — PASS

The exact equilibrium classification goes beyond mere equilibrium existence: it gives a census and sharply different efficiency regimes on a canonical family of diameter-two graphs. In particular, a linearly unbounded price of anarchy persists in 2-connected graphs with minimum degree two and at least two agents of each color, unlike the previously published star lower-bound example that relies on one minority agent. Exponentially rare equilibria can simultaneously be socially optimal in the balanced case. These are substantive structural and economic boundaries, not arbitrary small enumerations.

## Limits and closest source

The work assumes a unique vacancy, two colors, no immobile agents, and fraction-of-occupied-neighbors utility. Primary comparison: https://arxiv.org/html/2305.17757v3 (definition of utility and equilibrium, Theorems 6.1 and 6.3). Other inspected context: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2021.103576 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10005. Any claim of publication priority remains provisional pending independent literature review.
