# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every automorphism permutes the unique nonadjacency classes. The affine formulas \(\pi(x)_i=\sum_{v\in V_i}x_v\), \(\sigma(y)_v=y_i/n_i\), and \(F_s=(1-s)\mathrm{id}+s\sigma\pi\) remain in the clique-free complex, are equivariant, and give a strong deformation retraction simultaneously for all thresholds. In fixed points, orbit barycenters give precisely weighted quota faces. The classical quota wedge argument is reproduced with all boundary and empty-fixed-space cases; 50 independently reconstructed finite cases corroborate it.

**Originality — PASS, with a stated risk.** The 2026 source studies shellability and clique attachments, not equivariant compression or fixed-point topology of complete multipartite clique-free complexes. The 2013 quota-complex theorem covers the last wedge step, but cannot by itself identify these geometric fixed-point spaces or produce the filtration-natural \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\)-equivariant retraction. A 2026 multipartite path-free study has a different forbidden pattern. Searches for multipartite clique-free fixed spaces, vertex-inflation equivalents, and equivariant polyhedral joins found no directly covering claim; unindexed equivariant generalizations are a residual risk. Accessible prior recorded claims concern other graph complexes or generalized windmills, not this all-subgroup result.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem compresses an entire family of potentially large complexes equivariantly and determines the exact homotopy of every subgroup-fixed space. Orbit weights can create disconnected or mixed-degree sphere wedges, even though the ordinary complex has spheres concentrated in one degree. For instance, the action generated by a disjoint two-cycle and three-cycle on \(K_8\) has fixed-space type \(S^2\vee S^1\vee S^1\) at clique threshold five, showing why the full subgroup-fixed classification is not read off from ordinary Betti numbers. The formula is natural for equivariant calculations and is not an arbitrary finite slice.

**Limits.** No assertion of novelty is made for the general scalar quota wedge theorem. The finite program does not independently prove the unrestricted result; that rests on the symbolic deformation and quota argument. Prior literature on general equivariant polyhedral joins could contain an abstraction implying this specific construction.
