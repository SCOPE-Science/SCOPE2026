# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The dual-face characterization follows from the connected induced triples in a matching-deleted bipartite graph. Its subcomplex is precisely the two-row multiple chessboard complex of capacities \((2,2)\), to which Jojić–Vrećica–Živaljević Theorem 4.4 applies because the number of columns is at least five. The integral homology and free-rank formula follow by the relative cellular exact sequence, Euler characteristic and integral combinatorial Alexander duality. The equivariant sign twist is determined by the ambient vertex orientation. The full trace character is derived from a complete fixed-simplex enumeration, checked independently in the executable program. In particular, the nonstandard general premise is a cited theorem, not an extrapolation of finite data. Risks: no homotopy type or lower integral lattice decomposition is claimed.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

The complete-bipartite and clique-prism theorems in Bayer et al. concern different graphs and do not give the matching-deleted crown character. The published chessboard shellability theorem already determines the homology of the *auxiliary* chessboard complex, which is expressly acknowledged and excluded from the novelty claim. The new conclusion instead gives the complete integral homology and full rational \(S_n\times C_2\) character of the Alexander-dual crown cut complex. Its top-degree part-flip sign and the four-cycle dependence of its lower character are not contained in the inspected sources or indexed closest results. Risks: the 2026 chessboard source could only be compared at abstract level, and unindexed equivalent calculations may exist.

## Value — PASS

The ordinary cut-complex literature explicitly investigates homology and symmetry modules of natural graph families. Matching-deleted bicliques are a canonical dense square-rich family just outside the complete-bipartite case; the results identify separated nonzero homology degrees, a sharp connectivity bound, universal nonshellability, and a complete symmetry character with a nontrivial parity twist. The calculation isolates exactly how the missing perfect matching creates the extra high-dimensional permutation module while the chessboard component controls the other degree. It is not merely an Euler-characteristic lookup of a preclassified auxiliary complex; the equivariant relative-cell and Alexander-duality calculations determine new invariants of a distinct graph complex.
