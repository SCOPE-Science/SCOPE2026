For every \(n\ge5\), the ordinary three-cut complex of the matching-deleted complete bipartite crown graph has free reduced integral homology only in degrees \(2n-6\) and \(2n-4\), with ranks \(1+n(n-5)(n-3)(n-2)/4\) and \(n\), making it a sharply connected non-shellable complex. Its full rational \(S_n\times C_2\) homology character is determined by explicit formulas involving only fixed points, transpositions and four-cycles, while the integral top-degree lattice is a parity-twisted index permutation module.
