Every essential-maximal numerical range of a coordinatewise real-linear operator on complex \(\ell^2\) is the convex hull of a compact subset of its essential-norm circle, and every such convex hull occurs. Adding an arbitrarily small complex-linear component to coordinate conjugation collapses its essential-maximal unit disk to a singleton, with the largest possible Hausdorff jump \(2\), even while the ordinary essential numerical range varies continuously. For this diagonal class, the essential-maximal range is exactly the convex hull of the essential-norm-circle contact points of its ordinary essential numerical range.
