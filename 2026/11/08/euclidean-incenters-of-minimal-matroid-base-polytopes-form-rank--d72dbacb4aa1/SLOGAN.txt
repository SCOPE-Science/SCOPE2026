In the canonical Euclidean realization, the minimum-base-count connected matroid of rank \(k\) and corank \(r\) has inradius \(\sqrt{k+r}/(\max\{k,r\}\sqrt{k+r-1}+\sqrt{kr})\). Its entire incenter locus is a regular simplex of dimension \(\min\{k,r\}-1\) whenever \(k\ne r\), but collapses to one point exactly when rank equals corank.
