For the natural 14-vertex interpolation from the tetrakis hexahedron to the rhombic dodecahedron, the entire integral Euclidean Vietoris–Rips barcode is exact for every \(3/2\le t\le2\): it passes through eleven circles, a two-sphere, a three-sphere, and a six-sphere. The six-sphere dies by axial-antipodal edges when \(t<\sqrt3\), but by cube-corner antipodes when \(t>\sqrt3\), with both events simultaneous at equality.
