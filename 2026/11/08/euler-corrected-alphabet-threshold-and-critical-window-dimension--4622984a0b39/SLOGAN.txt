For a Zipf-normalized countable expanding map, the smallest retained alphabet whose survivor dimension exceeds the branch-convergence exponent has an exact exponential threshold with an Euler–Mascheroni polynomial prefactor. The critical-window dimension moves by \(2a^2t/q\), and the formerly unresolved unshifted exponential boundary lies strictly above the exponent by \(2\lambda\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)\) to leading order.
