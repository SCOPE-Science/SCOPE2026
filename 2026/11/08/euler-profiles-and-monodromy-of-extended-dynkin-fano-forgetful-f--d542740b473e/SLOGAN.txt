For the five Fano forgetful families built from small extended-Dynkin quivers, every fibre's Euler characteristic is a product of factors \(m+1\) at ordinary roots and \(2m+1\) at exceptional roots. In all dimensions at least six, the reduced non-smooth divisor is the binary discriminant plus the exceptional-root hyperplanes, while cohomological monodromy permutes the \(k\) projective-line factors and is invisible around the exceptional hyperplanes.
