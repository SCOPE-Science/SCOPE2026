For irreversible dimer adsorption on complete tripartite graphs with classes \((n,n,2n)\) and weak \(AB\)-edge weight \(w_n\), the terminal vacancy count converges to twice a Poisson variable of mean \(\lambda/2\) whenever \(nw_n\to\lambda<\infty\). In particular, the probability of complete coverage tends to \(e^{-\lambda/2}\), revealing a nondegenerate inverse-size weak-channel jamming crossover.
