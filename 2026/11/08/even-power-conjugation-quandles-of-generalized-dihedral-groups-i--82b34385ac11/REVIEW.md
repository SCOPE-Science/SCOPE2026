# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For even integer m, every reflection of Dih(A) has m-th power identity, while rotation R_b^m=R_mb. Thus operation fixes every rotation left input and every reflection right input, and F_a*R_b=F_(a-2mb). Right translations act as H=(2m)A on reflection cosets and fix rotations, so Inn(Q)≈H. For H>1, rotations are exactly points fixed by all inner translations; automorphisms split into bijections f,g on the two layers. Equation g(a-2mb)=g(a)-2m f(b) forces a unique group automorphism alpha:H→H with pi∘f=alpha∘pi and g alpha-equivariant. Counting f gives (h!)^d, counting g gives h!d^h, times |Aut(H)|; abstract semidirect product follows after choosing fiber/coset labels. Iso if and only if |A| and H≅H′; H trivial is trivial quandle and entire symmetric group. Independently reconstructed group operation for cyclic groups of orders 3..6 at exponents -2,0,2; source verifier supplies additional finite configurations but is not all-order proof.

PASS. Existing power-conjugation quandle papers furnish general constructions and subgroups of automorphisms, while Takasaki (core) quandle families classify a different operation and ordinary conjugation is exponent 1. The present even-power generalized-dihedral operation has nontrivial fixed-point right translations, unlike any nontrivial Alexander quandle, so Hou-style Alexander automorphism theorems do not immediately dominate the classification. The exact all-finite-abelian-A isomorphism criterion and full automorphism count remain distinct in inspected records; general quandle affine-mesh literature might imply them after specialization and remains a priority risk.

## value

PASS

A complete isomorphism and full automorphism classification for a natural family of finite nonconnected power-conjugation quandles is a useful structural invariant: nonisomorphic groups can yield isomorphic quandles, and the full automorphism group contains many symmetries not induced from group automorphisms. The all-order formula and trivial-image boundary are mathematically motivated, rather than a tiny instance count.
