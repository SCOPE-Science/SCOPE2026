For any finite abelian group \(A\), every even-power conjugation quandle of \(\operatorname{Dih}(A)\) is classified up to isomorphism by \(|A|\) and \((2m)A\). Its full automorphism group has order \(|\operatorname{Aut}(H)|(h!)^{d+1}d^h\) when \(d=|H|>1\), with \(H=(2m)A\) and \(h=|A|/d\); the trivial case has automorphism group \(S_{2|A|}\).
