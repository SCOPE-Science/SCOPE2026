For every odd prime \(p\), the pairs of neighboring Fermat quotients \((Q_p(n),Q_p(n+1))\) modulo \(p^k\) have, for all explicitly bounded large exponents, exactly \(\binom{p-2}{2}\) double fibers and no higher fibers. Their exact image size is \((p-2)p^k-\binom{p-2}{2}\), and the same profile holds for every shift prime to \(p\).
