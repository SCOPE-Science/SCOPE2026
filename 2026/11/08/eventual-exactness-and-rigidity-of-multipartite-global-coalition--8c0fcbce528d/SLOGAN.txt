For a complete \(r\)-partite graph with \(r\ge4\) and every part of size at least \(3r-6\), the global coalition number is exactly \(N-r(r-2)\). Once each part has at least \(3r-4\) vertices, every maximum partition consists of the \(r\) transversal-minus-one-part classes and the remaining singletons, yielding exactly \(\prod_i(n_i)_{r-1}\) labelled-vertex partitions.
