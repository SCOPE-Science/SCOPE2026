Every three-dimensional integral lattice prism over an arbitrary planar lattice polygon has a real-rooted Ehrhart \(h^*\)-polynomial, even when the polygon's own numerator has nonreal roots. A single unit segment therefore restores real-rootedness in all three exceptional planar cases \(h_P^*(t)=1+kt+kt^2\), \(k\in\{1,2,3\}\).
