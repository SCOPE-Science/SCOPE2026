For the harmonic oscillator, **every** non-point-mass independent velocity-Verlet timestep law supported inside the stable range \((0,2)\) has a strictly positive almost-sure top Lyapunov exponent, whereas every constant-step law has exponent zero. An exact hyperbolic-commutator identity for any two distinct steps proves the classification without exceptional distributions, even though a deterministic alternation between those steps can be stable.
