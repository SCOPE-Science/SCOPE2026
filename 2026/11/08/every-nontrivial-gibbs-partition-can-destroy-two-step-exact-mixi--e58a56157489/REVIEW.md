# Scientific assessment: universal obstruction to finite-step mixing under Gibbs averaging

## Correctness — PASS

The Hilbert-space splitting exists precisely when the partition has at least two blocks and at least one nonsingleton block. Unit centered orthogonal vectors in the block-constant and within-block fluctuation subspaces produce centered orthogonal perturbation directions. The rank-one kernel is stochastic and stationary because both directions are centered, strictly positive for sufficiently small perturbation, and nonreversible because its centered part is non-self-adjoint. Its centered square is zero, giving exact mixing in two steps. Gibbs compression creates a nonzero orthogonal projection eigenmode, yielding exact power and Frobenius norm formulas at every positive integer time. Both trivial partition endpoints are checked separately. The exact rational checker passed 13,899 assertions, including all nontrivial partitions for three through six states under two rational stationary laws. This finite testing does not establish infinite quantifiers; the construction does.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

Theorem 4.1 of Choi–Wang (arXiv:2610.39239v1) makes the all-time inequality under reversibility and uses the self-adjoint spectral-power argument. Choi–Wang (arXiv:2509.02996v1, Section 7.1.6) already supplies a different nonreversible **one-sided** averaging slowdown, so novelty is not assigned to generic nonreversible slowdown. Choi–Lim–Wang (arXiv:2512.13067v1, Proposition 6.2) identifies the spectrum of the Gibbs-projected chain but gives no partition-uniform existence construction coupled to exact two-step mixing of the baseline. The present claim is the sharp characterization of all fixed partitions preserving universal timewise dominance, with the stronger exactness-destruction property for every nontrivial partition. The closest primary papers and mathematical research records were compared by claims and implications. An unlocated equivalent linear-algebraic or lumpability formulation remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Finite-time exact mixing is a natural boundary condition for transition-kernel acceleration, and the motivating reversible theorem invites a precise determination of its necessity. The answer is complete across all partitions and all positive target distributions; it shows that no intermediate partition is universally safe. The example is primitive, as close to equilibrium as desired, and minimally three-dimensional, so the obstruction is not caused by reducibility or periodicity. The stark change from exact two-step convergence to a perpetual geometric tail is mathematically explanatory, rather than an arbitrary small constant gain.

## Scope and risks

The result does not compare all total-variation mixing thresholds or claim that every nonreversible kernel slows under averaging. Its novelty assessment is not an exhaustive priority proof, and independent proof verification has not been performed. The rational certificate is a diagnostic finite replay, not an automated proof of the general theorem.
