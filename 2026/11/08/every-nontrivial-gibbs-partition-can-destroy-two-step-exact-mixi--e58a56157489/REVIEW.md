# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every positive π and nontrivial block partition with 2≤k≤N−1, conditional Gibbs G is a self-adjoint projection on ℓ²(π) fixing constants and Π. There are centered orthogonal unit vectors a∈ranG, b∈kerG; putting u=(a+b)/sqrt2 and v=(a−b)/sqrt2 makes ⟨u,v⟩=0 and Gu=Gv=a/sqrt2. For sufficiently small positive ε, P=Π+ε(u⊗v) has all entries positive, preserves rows and π, is nonreversible, and P²=Π because (u⊗v)²=0. But GPG=Π+(ε/2)(a⊗a); since a⊗a is an orthogonal projection orthogonal to Π, (GPG)^t=Π+(ε/2)^t(a⊗a) for every t≥1, and its weighted Hilbert–Schmidt norm is (ε/2)^t>0. At k=1 G=Π; at k=N G=I, so universal Frobenius improvement holds exactly at endpoints. For N=2 intermediate partitions do not exist, so N=3 is sharp. Independently exact-rational calculation of the stated 3×3 P,G reproduces P²=Π, the 1/8 eigenvalue and all displayed entries. Infinite N/t proof is the rank-one argument, not the finite verifier.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge originality resides in the universal if-and-only-if partition classification and constructive arbitrary-positive-π nilpotent Markov witness with destroyed two-step exact mixing and a precise nonzero compressed mode. Earlier Choi–Wang work establishes reversible improvement; other work establishes one-sided group-averaging slowdowns or a defect-corrected inequality for general nonnormal chains, but none of the inspected mathematical statements constructs these positive P²=Π examples for EVERY intermediate partition. The source-specific subspace construction is not a routine renaming of a normal-baseline result.

## value

PASS

The theorem identifies the exact structural boundary of an appealing all-time monotonicity principle for two-sided Gibbs averaging, not merely an isolated numerical slowdown. Arbitrary stationary laws and partition classes, positivity, and a sharp three-state witness make the obstruction a useful design warning for nonreversible chains.
