For every strictly positive stationary distribution, every genuinely intermediate state partition admits a strictly positive nonreversible Markov kernel with \(P^2=\Pi\), yet its two-sided Gibbs average \(GPG\) never equals \(\Pi\) at any finite time. Thus only the one-block and singleton-block partitions guarantee that Gibbs averaging never worsens weighted-Frobenius distance to stationarity at every integer time for all stationary kernels.
