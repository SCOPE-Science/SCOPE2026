For every \(k\ge1\), the central trinomial coefficients \(T_n=[x^n](1+x+x^2)^n\) attain every odd residue modulo \(2^k\) infinitely often, and no even residue. Their set of values is therefore dense in the full \(2\)-adic unit group; separated binary blocks and a generator congruent to \(3\pmod8\) give constructive witnesses.
