# Scientific review: root-cluster continua of finite sums of weighted shifts

## Correctness

The diagonal entries of the positive root of each shift power are exponential moving averages of bounded logarithmic weights. For each fixed coordinate, comparison with the prefix average has error at most 2B(k-1)/n. Uniform boundedness gives strong convergence whenever the prefix vector has a limit; conversely weak convergence determines that vector through the first diagonal entries. Bounded vector Cesaro sequences have vanishing consecutive jumps, forcing nonempty compact connected cluster sets.

For a prescribed compact continuum K in logarithmic coordinates, finite epsilon-chain polygonal tours of shrinking nets produce a bounded Lipschitz curve with omega-limit K even when K is not path connected. Sampling at times log n retains that omega-limit. The inverse Cesaro formula b_n=n c_n-(n-1)c_(n-1) gives bounded logarithmic weights and hence strictly positive, bounded shift weights. The asymptotic numerical-range formula follows from scalar expectations of the block-scalar limits and connectedness of K times the probability simplex. Finite rational and diagonal tests corroborate, but do not establish, the infinite theorem.

## Originality

Ji and Yang (arXiv:2608.21553) already establish general necessary connectedness and the scalar one-shift interval classification. Nayak and Shekhawat (arXiv:2410.16318) establish a uniform criterion for norm convergence, rather than realization of arbitrary joint nonconvergent WOT cluster continua. A published sparse-shift cluster-universality construction uses weights lacking a positive lower bound and produces different operator limits. Neither materially inspected primary theorem supplies the complete positive-continuum realization for finite bounded-below direct sums. Classical summability results, especially Barone (1939), could cover the underlying vector Cesaro lemma and remain a genuine priority risk because the complete original article was unavailable. This is a best-of-knowledge assessment, not an assertion of exhaustive historical priority.

## Mathematical value

The result completely determines which joint operator-root cluster shapes are possible for a basic class of weighted shifts. In one summand only scalar intervals occur, while two summands realize any positive planar compact continuum. The exact asymptotic numerical-range formula identifies which geometric distinctions this scalar invariant cannot detect.

## Scope

The statement is restricted to finitely many uniformly bounded-below positive unilateral shifts. It does not classify arbitrary operators, infinite direct sums or fixed universal weight bounds. No formal proof or external expert attestation is claimed.
