For any permutation word \(P\) on \(q\ge3\) distinct symbols and \(k\ge2\), every smallest string attractor of \(P^k\) chooses one position of each symbol, except that the two extreme word positions cannot both be chosen. Exactly \(k^{q-2}(k^2-1)\) smallest attractors exist, and among one-per-symbol selections the single junction factor comprising the last and first symbols of \(P\) tests the entire attractor condition.
