Every positive-integer generalized parking-function polytope has a unique Euclidean incenter and an explicit inradius given by a minimum over subset cardinalities. Unlike the restricted two-parameter case, **every cardinality** \(k=1,\ldots,n\) can be the unique upper-facet contact rank, giving \(n+\binom nk\) tangent facets even within a single fixed combinatorial type.
