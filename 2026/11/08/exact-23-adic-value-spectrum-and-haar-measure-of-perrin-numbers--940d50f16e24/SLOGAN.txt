The image of the Perrin sequence modulo every power of the ramified prime \(23\) has an exact formula: \(|I_2|=211\) and \(|I_k|=198\cdot23^{k-2}+13S_{k-3}\) for \(k\ge3\), where \(S_m\) counts squares modulo \(23^m\). Its \(23\)-adic closure is exactly \(198\) complete prime-square balls plus \(13\) quadratic critical images, with Haar measure \(9517/25392\).
