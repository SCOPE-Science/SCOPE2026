On two pairs of Boolean variables, the three-way lax splitting formula \(\operatorname{dep}(X,Y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(X,Y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(Y,X)\) has exactly 292 inclusion-minimal false teams, with size polynomial \(48z^{11}+4z^{12}+240z^{13}\). A capacitated Hall criterion classifies every minimal failure as one of three explicit occupied-cell patterns in a \(4\)-by-\(4\) grid.
