For smooth genus-three hyperelliptic curves \(y^2=x^8+a x^6+b x^4+c x^2+1\) in characteristic \(3\), the marked extra-involution family has no 3-rank-zero members; its nonordinary locus splits into two disjoint smooth divisors, with 3-rank one occurring on four punctured lines. The numbers of smooth marked coefficient triples in every 3-rank stratum over each \(\mathbb F_{3^n}\) are explicit polynomials in \(3^n\), with a parity-dependent four-root correction.
