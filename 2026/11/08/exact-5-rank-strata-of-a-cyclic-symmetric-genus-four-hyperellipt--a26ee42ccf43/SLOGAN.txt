In characteristic five, the cyclic-symmetric genus-four hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\) has every smooth member nonordinary, but all four possible ranks \(0,1,2,3\) occur. Its complete stable-Cartier stratification has exactly one smooth \(5\)-rank-zero coefficient pair, \((a,b)=(-1,2)\), and this isolated intersection is reduced.
