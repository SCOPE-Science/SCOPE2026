For every periodic word built from at least three pairwise distinct monochromatic runs, the full Abelian factor-complexity profile depends only on the multiset of run lengths: for \(1\le r<p\) it equals \(p-\sum_i((a_i-r)_++(a_i-p+r)_+)\). The profile is symmetric and concave, and its exact maximum and plateau are determined solely by the longest run.
