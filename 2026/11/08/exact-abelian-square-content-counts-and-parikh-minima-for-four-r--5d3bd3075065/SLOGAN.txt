The distinct Abelian-square contents of every positive four-run binary word \(a^p b^q a^r b^s\) admit an exact parity-sensitive closed formula. For every fixed pair of letter counts \(A,B\ge4\), the least count among all four-run words is exactly \(\lfloor(A+2)/4\rfloor+\lfloor(B+2)/4\rfloor\), proving optimality of the previously known effective construction within this family, but not among arbitrary binary words.
