For the Thue–Morse word, Abelian-square starting-position density at every block length \(n\ge1\) has the exact formula \((1-\eta(n))/4\) for odd \(n\) and \((5+3\eta(n/2))/8\) for even \(n\), where \(\eta\) is its recursively computable two-point correlation. The unique global minimum is \(1/6\) at \(n=3\), and the global maximum is \(3/4\) precisely at lengths \(n=6\cdot2^k\) for integers \(k\ge0\).
