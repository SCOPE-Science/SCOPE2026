On every finite uniform tight-binding path with a single Lindblad absorber at one endpoint, the exact time-integrated survival operator has an elementary parity-structured kernel. A walker starting at site \(j\ge2\) has mean absorption time \((N-j+1)[j/\kappa+(j-1)\kappa/\Omega^2]\), with a unique optimal loss rate \(\kappa=\Omega\sqrt{j/(j-1)}\) independent of the path length.
