For the Gaussian norm-one points modulo \(2^k\), every odd–odd vector is a sum of two points, all odd-parity vectors are exactly the threefold sumset, and all even-parity vectors are exactly the fourfold sumset. Their additive Cayley graph has exact diameter \(2\) for \(k\le2\) and \(4\) for \(k\ge3\), with the sharp obstruction already visible modulo eight.
