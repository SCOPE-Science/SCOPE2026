For the modular inverse graph \(H_q=\{(u,u^{-1}):u\in(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)^\times\}\) at every odd modulus, its ordered additive energy has the exact Euler product \(\mathcal E(q)=\prod_{p^k\parallel q}3p^{2k-2}(p-1)(k(p-1)-1)\). For prime powers, its energy divided by the square of the unit count is precisely \(3k-3/(p-1)\), yielding the complete two-frequency Kloosterman fourth moment.
