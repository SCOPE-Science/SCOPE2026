For the quartic graph \(\{(x,x^4)\}\) over every residue ring \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) with \(p\ge5\), its exact additive energy obeys an explicit four-step recurrence controlled only by \(p\) and \(\left(\frac{-3}{p}\right)\). This extends the known finite-field energy formula to every such prime power and, by the Chinese remainder theorem, to all moduli coprime to six.
