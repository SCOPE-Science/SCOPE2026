The joint Fermat-quotient vectors \((q_p(n+h_0),q_p(n+h_1))\) over a complete eligible period modulo \(p^2\) have a three-line Fourier zero set, constant nonzero Fourier amplitude \(p\), and an exact pointwise formula for every additive convolution power. For every odd prime \(p\ge5\), their image is a proper subset of \(\mathbb F_p^2\) but is an additive basis of order exactly two, with sharp minimum two-sum multiplicity \((p-2)(p-3)\); all even additive energies are explicit.
