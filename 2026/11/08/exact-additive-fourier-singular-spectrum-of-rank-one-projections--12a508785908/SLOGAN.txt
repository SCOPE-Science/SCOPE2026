For the rank-one idempotents in \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\), additive convolution has a complete, explicit singular-value spectrum controlled by rational eigenlines and the absolute field trace. Its least positive singular value is \(q\) in characteristic two or three, but is exactly \(2q\sin(\pi/(2p))\) in every prime characteristic \(p\ge5\), with exact multiplicities and kernel dimension in all cases.
