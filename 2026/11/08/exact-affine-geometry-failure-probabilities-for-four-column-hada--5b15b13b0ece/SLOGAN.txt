For a four-column Walsh–Hadamard sketch sampled uniformly without replacement, exact rank failure obeys one of two closed binomial laws depending only on whether the four frequency indices form an affine plane. All such coordinate subspaces have identical leverage scores, yet their failure probabilities differ by a factor \(9/2\) at \(N=8,m=4\), and by an exponentially growing factor when \(m\to\infty\) with \(m^2/N\to0\).
