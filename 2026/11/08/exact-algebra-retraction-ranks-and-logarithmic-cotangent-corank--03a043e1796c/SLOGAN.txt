For the characteristic-two truncated symmetric algebra \(\mathbb F_q[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_i^2)\), every unital algebra retraction is conjugate to a coordinate deletion, and every attainable image dimension \(2^r\) has an exact Gaussian-binomial multiplicity. A uniformly chosen retraction typically keeps \(n-\log_2 n+O(1)\) generators, so its linear image dimension is \(\Theta(2^n/n)\), with a superexponentially small probability of deviations outside a fixed five-point corank window.
