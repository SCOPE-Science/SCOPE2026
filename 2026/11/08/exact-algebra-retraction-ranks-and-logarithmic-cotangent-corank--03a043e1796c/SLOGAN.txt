For a uniformly random unital retraction of the characteristic-two algebra \(\mathbb F_q[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_i^2)\), the cotangent corank lies within two integers of \(\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor\) with superexponentially high probability, so the image dimension is typically \(\Theta(2^n/n)\). This new concentration result is derived from, and explicitly credits, the previously published exact rank enumerator; those finite-rank counts are not claimed as novel.
