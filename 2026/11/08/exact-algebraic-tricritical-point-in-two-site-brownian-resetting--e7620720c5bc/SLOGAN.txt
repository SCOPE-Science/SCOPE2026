For Brownian search on a circle restarted at an antipode and one closer site, the previously numerical zero-rate tricritical parameter is exactly the unique physical root of \(b^3+15b^2-16b+4=0\), with reset odds \(m=(5b-2)/(3b^3-5b+2)\). Its first nonzero rate correction is strictly cubic, and a transverse local control path produces a positive minimizing reset rate with a square-root onset.
