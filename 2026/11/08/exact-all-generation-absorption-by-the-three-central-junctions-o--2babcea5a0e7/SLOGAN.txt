For a finite level-\(g\) Sierpinski gasket with absorbing loss at its three internal junctions, the exact number of accessible Laplacian quantum-walk modes is \(13\cdot2^{g-2}+1\) for every \(g\ge2\), rather than merely the values previously tabulated at a few generations. Thus the maximally mixed eventual-capture probability is \((13\cdot2^{g-2}+1)/((3^{g+1}+3)/2)\), asymptotic to \((13/6)(2/3)^g\), regardless of the three positive loss rates.
