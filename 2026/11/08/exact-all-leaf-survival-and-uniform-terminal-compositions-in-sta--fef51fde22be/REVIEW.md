# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The productive-step transition probabilities follow directly from compatible-pair counting; conditional on the aggregate walk, all-positive composition probabilities remain equal by explicit up/down predecessor sums. The bounded product of the leaf counts is a martingale; finite gambler's ruin and optional stopping normalize their common terminal probability. The positive-composition weighted sum is exactly the coefficient of \(z^n\) in \(z^r(1-z)^{-2r}\). These steps prove the result for all permitted finite \(n,r\); the exact-rational eight-case checker gives corroborating, not exhaustive, finite validation.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The recent opinion-graph voter source establishes stabilization and individual survival properties, but the inspected star-graph and survival sections do not state this joint all-leaf terminal mass or its conditional uniformity. Classical constrained-voter papers concern three opinions, corresponding only to \(r=2\). No covering claim was identified in targeted research-record and primary-literature searches; formal novelty cannot be guaranteed and equivalent neutral branching/gambler's-ruin descriptions may exist outside indexed material. The two-leaf case and elementary supporting tools are not claimed as new.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Simultaneous survival of all initially present incompatible opinions is an intrinsic diversity observable of star opinion graphs, complementing known marginal opinion survival bounds. The exact \(n^{-r}\) rare-event order and complete conditional composition law are new quantitative information for every \(r\ge3\), including all finite population sizes; they are not a free reformulation of the expected stabilization time or an arbitrary small-parameter table.

## Closest literature and limitations
Berenbrink et al., arXiv:2610.16684, is the model-defining and closest current source. Vazquez–Redner (2004) and Mobilia (2011) study the three-opinion classical predecessor. The theorem is for a complete interaction graph, fair copying, initially one agent per leaf; it does not cover unequal initial counts or full terminal laws with extinct leaf types. Possible unindexed stronger neutral-population theory remains a genuine residual originality risk.
