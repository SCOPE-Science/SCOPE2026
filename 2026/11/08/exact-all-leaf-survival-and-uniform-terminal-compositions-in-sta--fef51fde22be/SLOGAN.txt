In a symmetric star-opinion voter model starting with one supporter of each of \(r\ge3\) incompatible leaf opinions, every positive terminal allocation of \(n\) agents has the same exact probability \(1/\binom{n+r-1}{2r-1}\). The probability all \(r\) opinions survive is \(\binom{n-1}{r-1}/\binom{n+r-1}{2r-1}\), and their terminal sizes are uniform over positive compositions conditional on simultaneous survival.
