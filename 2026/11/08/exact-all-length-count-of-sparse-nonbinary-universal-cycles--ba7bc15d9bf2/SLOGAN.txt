Every cyclic word containing each \(q\)-ary length-\(n\) word with at most two nonzero positions exactly once can be counted explicitly for \(q\ge3\) and \(n\ge3\). The total is \(((q-1)!)^{1+(n-1)(q-1)}q^{(n-1)(q-2)+\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\), obtained from a factorized sparse-de-Bruijn arborescence determinant.
