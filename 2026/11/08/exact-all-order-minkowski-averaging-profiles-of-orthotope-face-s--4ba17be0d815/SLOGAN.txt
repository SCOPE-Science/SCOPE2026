Every equal-weight Minkowski average of the closed \(k\)-skeleton of an orthotope is exactly a finite coordinate-grid stratum, and its Hausdorff distance to the box is \(\bigl(\sum_{j=1}^{\max(n-Nk,0)}\ell_{[j]}^p\bigr)^{1/p}/(2N)\) in each finite coordinate \(\ell_p\) norm. This yields the full all-order distance profile, all deep holes for the unit cube, and the sharp finite filling threshold \(Nk\ge n\).
