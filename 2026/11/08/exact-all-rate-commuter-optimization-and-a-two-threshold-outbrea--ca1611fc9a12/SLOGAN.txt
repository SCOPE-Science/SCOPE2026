In a two-patch SIR commuter model, the exact reproduction number has a unique global commuter-allocation optimum for every positive recovery and travel rate, with no fast-mixing assumption. A rational example has a sharp subcritical window \((7-\sqrt{33})/8<x<(7+\sqrt{33})/8\) in the safer patch's commuter population, while both zero and full participation remain supercritical.
