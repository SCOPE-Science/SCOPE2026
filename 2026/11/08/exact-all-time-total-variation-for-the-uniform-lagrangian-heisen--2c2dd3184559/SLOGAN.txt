Uniformly averaging all Lagrangian subgroups of an odd-characteristic finite Heisenberg group yields an exactly solvable random walk: at every positive step \(t\), its total-variation distance from Haar measure is \(\frac{q-1}{q}(\frac{2}{q^m+1})^{t-1}\). The distance stays large after one step but tends to zero after exactly two steps as \(q^m\to\infty\), with an explicit full convolution spectrum and pointwise distribution at every time.
