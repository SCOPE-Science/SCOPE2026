On every finite two-agent \(\mathsf{S5}\) frame, all pure nestings of the two knowledge operators form two strictly growing alternating chains meeting exactly at common knowledge; their lengths are given by shortest-path diameters of the agents' bipartite class-incidence graph. For \(N\ge3\) worlds the exact maximum number of distinct operations is \(2N\) for odd \(N\) and \(2N-1\) for even \(N\), achieved by overlapping-pair path frames.
