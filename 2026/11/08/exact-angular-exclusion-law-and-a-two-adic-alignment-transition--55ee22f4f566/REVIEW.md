# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The formula follows from the mixed-area support identity, a period and reflection argument for the regular-polygon support sum, exact angular averaging to determine its cosine amplitude, and an integer parity calculation for the aligned orientation. For every real angle, every integer side count at least three, and all positive radii, continuity and the midpoint/cusp analysis supply both extrema and their equality classes. The accompanying standard-library verifier independently checks 1008 finite hull calculations; these are corroboration rather than the proof of infinitely many cases.

## Originality — PASS, provisional priority
The main source, Abbasi Moud (arXiv:2606.21812), gives fixed-orientation mixed-species entries and same-species parity, but not the all-angle lcm-controlled profile or two-adic phase classification. Geigenfeind and de las Heras (arXiv:1902.05961) study the angle dependence primarily for identical particles and selected regular polygons, and the CGAL Minkowski-sum manual gives generic algorithms. The numerical table values and standard mixed-area identity are acknowledged as existing work, not claimed to be new. Dedicated mathematical record searches and accessible prior claims revealed no complete implication. Older exact formulas, including poorly indexed sources, remain a priority risk. The related 2023 aligned-RSA article was checked only at abstract level.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The problem is an explicitly used physical-geometric pair-interaction invariant rather than an arbitrary polygonal calculation. A full classification of optimal orientations for any two regular polygon orders, together with the exact scale-free lcm oscillation and the unexpectedly binary arithmetic alignment rule, supplies structural information absent from the fixed-angle numerical table. The work does not infer actual packing/jamming density from excluded area alone.

## Limitations and closest literature
The theorem assumes two centered regular Euclidean polygons and positive circumradii. Its novelty is the complete parametric angular result, not previously tabulated individual values.
