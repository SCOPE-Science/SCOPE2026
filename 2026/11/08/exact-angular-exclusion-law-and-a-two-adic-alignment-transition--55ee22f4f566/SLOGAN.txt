For two centered regular polygons with \(n,m\ge3\) sides, the entire relative-rotation excluded-area function is a periodic cosine arc whose exact period is \(2\pi/\operatorname{lcm}(n,m)\). In the common-vertex orientation, exclusion is minimized exactly when the two side counts have the same positive two-adic valuation; the normalized peak-to-trough amplitude depends only on their least common multiple.
