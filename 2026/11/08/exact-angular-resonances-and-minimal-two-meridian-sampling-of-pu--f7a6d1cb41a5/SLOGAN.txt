For pure spherical harmonics of every degree \(\ell\), \(2\ell+1\) equally spaced samples on two meridians have determinant exactly proportional to \(\prod_{m=1}^{\ell}\sin(m\theta)\). Reconstruction is therefore sharp and complete unless the meridian angle has a rational multiple of \(\pi\) with denominator at most \(\ell\); every resonance has an explicit nullspace and exact vanishing order.
