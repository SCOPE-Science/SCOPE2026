For any affine prism over a convex \(n\)-gon with \(p\) opposite-parallel edge pairs, the antipodal vertex-pair count is \(n^2+2n+2p\), the antipodal edge-midpoint-pair count is \(n^2+3n+7p\), and the defect is exactly \(3n-1+5p\). Each opposite-parallel base-edge pair contributes exactly five to the defect, giving the same explicit rank for the integral second homology of the antipodal square complex by a previously established homology theorem.
