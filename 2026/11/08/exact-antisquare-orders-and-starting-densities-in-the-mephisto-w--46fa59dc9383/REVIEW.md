# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The 3-uniform substitution gives the letter recursion \(w(3a)=w(3a+1)=w(a)\), \(w(3a+2)=1-w(a)\), and its length-\(3^t\) concatenation law. Every antisquare order at least four not divisible by three contradicts the equal/unequal adjacent-pair pattern in one of three residue classes. Dividing an antisquare order divisible by three produces a shorter antisquare; complement-commuting substitution provides the converse. Orders one and two have explicit witnesses. At the allowed orders, the quotient-and-residue comparison is equivalent to the stated derivative tests, and the derivative recursion proves their exact simplification. A logarithmically bounded derivative discrepancy proves the frequencies needed for the starting-position densities. All quantifiers include the boundary case \(t=0\). The independent finite diagnostic checker successfully compared 2,097,152 ordered pairs of order/start choices, but finite tests are not the proof of an infinite claim.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The direct prior antisquare study [1] classifies and avoids antisquares in general binary words and does not state the exact Mephisto Waltz order spectrum, position predicate or natural densities. The same-word pseudoperiodic analysis [2] studies membership in a covering collection of same-letter shifts, not bitwise-complement adjacency at a fixed shift. Complement-avoidance analysis [3] excludes simultaneous occurrences of two complementary factors anywhere, not necessarily adjacent consecutive factors. The sequence catalogue [4] gives substitution and fourth-power avoidance, not antisquare starting sets. An earlier local finding about Abelian cubes in this same word concerns equal Parikh vectors of three blocks and does not imply the present position-by-position antisquare criterion. Available mathematical records did not reveal an equivalent claim. These comparisons establish no absolute priority: equivalent results in unindexed sources remain a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A canonical 3-automatic word receives a complete, infinite classification of all antisquare orders and a position-by-position decision rule, with sharp asymptotic densities for both lacunary families of allowed orders. The positive limiting densities along arbitrarily large orders distinguish the result from a merely finite forbidden-pattern census. The exact density statements can guide further work on complement repetitions and morphic dynamics without asserting such downstream theorems are already proved.
