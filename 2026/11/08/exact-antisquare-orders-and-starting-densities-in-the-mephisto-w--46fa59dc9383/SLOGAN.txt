In the Mephisto Waltz fixed point, antisquares occur at exactly the orders \(3^t\) and \(2\cdot3^t\), for \(t\ge0\), with a complete position-by-position criterion. Their natural starting densities are precisely \(1/6+3^{-(t+1)}\) and \(1/9+2\cdot3^{-(t+2)}\), so arbitrarily large antisquares retain positive density along both order families.
