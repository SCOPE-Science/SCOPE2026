Deleting intermediate repunit generators leaves a rigid three-generator family: for every base \(b\geq2\) and odd index \(n\geq3\), the semigroup generated by \(R_n,R_{n+2},R_{n+6}\) has an explicit coin-cost Apéry set and exact pseudo-Frobenius, Frobenius, and genus formulas. Its only symmetric case is \(n=3\); every odd \(n\geq5\) has exactly two pseudo-Frobenius numbers.
