For three independent area-uniform sites on the unit two-sphere, the ordinary area \(A\) of a labelled spherical Voronoi cell has the exact distribution \(\Pr(A\le a)=[a(2+\cos a)-3\sin a]/(6\pi)\) for \(0\le a\le2\pi\). Its variance is \(4\pi^2/9-10/3\), and its small-area probability has sharp fifth-order decay \(a^5/(360\pi)\).
