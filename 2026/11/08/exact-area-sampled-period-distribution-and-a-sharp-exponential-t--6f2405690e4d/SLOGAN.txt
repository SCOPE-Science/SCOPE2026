For the symmetric two-population zero-sum replicator center, the least orbital period under uniformly sampled initial strategies has an exact distribution in complete elliptic integrals. Its survival tail is \(\Pr(\mathcal T>t)=(2t+4)e^{-t/2}+O(t^2e^{-t})\), with the exact exponential-moment threshold \(s=1/2\).
