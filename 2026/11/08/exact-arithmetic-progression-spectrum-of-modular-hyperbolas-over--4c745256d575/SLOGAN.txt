For every modular hyperbola \(xy\equiv a\pmod N\) with \(a\) invertible, all affine three-term steps form one explicitly computable additive ideal, and every such triple extends to a cyclic affine progression. The complete cycle-length spectrum consists of the divisors of an explicit \(Q\), with exactly \(\varphi(N)\varphi(d)\) rooted cycles of length \(d\).
