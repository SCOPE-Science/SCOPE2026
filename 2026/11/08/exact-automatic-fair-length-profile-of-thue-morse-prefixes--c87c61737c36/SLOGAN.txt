The fair length of every nonempty Thue–Morse prefix is exactly \(1\), \(2\), or \(3\) according to its length modulo four, with the \(4k+3\) case determined by the Thue–Morse bit \(t_k\). Each prefix of fair length \(2\) has a unique optimal cut, and the resulting bounded fair-length sequence is aperiodic with natural densities \(1/4,3/8,3/8\).
