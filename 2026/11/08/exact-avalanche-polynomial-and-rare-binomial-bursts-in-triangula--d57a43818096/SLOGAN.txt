For the one-sink Abelian sandpile on a triangular book \(K_{1,1,n}\), the complete principal-avalanche polynomial has a closed product formula, giving an exact two-scale distribution: mostly zero or one toppling, but an event of probability of order \(1/n\) produces \(n/2\)-scale binomial bursts. Every fixed nonsink source has mean avalanche size exactly one, while its variance grows linearly in \(n\).
