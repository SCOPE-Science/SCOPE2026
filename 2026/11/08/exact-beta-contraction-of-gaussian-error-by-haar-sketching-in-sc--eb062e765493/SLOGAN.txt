For one-column least-squares sketch-and-solve, the exact excess squared residual under a Haar-orthonormal sketch is an **independent beta multiple** of the Gaussian-sketch excess residual, for every real or complex dimension and every nontrivial sketch size. This proves strict all-threshold stochastic improvement when the sketch has more than one column, while both methods retain the same sharp power-law moment threshold and a computable tail-constant ratio.
