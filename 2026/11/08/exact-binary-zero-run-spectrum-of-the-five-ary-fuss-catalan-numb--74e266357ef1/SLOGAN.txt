The five-ary Fuss–Catalan numbers are odd precisely at binary indices with no two set bits two places apart. Their entire positive even-run spectrum consists of \(\lfloor 2^j/5\rfloor\) and \(\lfloor 3\cdot2^j/5\rfloor\), and every distinct length first appears as a new record; an exact Fibonacci-product formula counts the odd indices below powers of two.
