For every prime \(p\), exponent \(a\ge1\), and one-digit positive cofactor \(m<p\) with \(mp^a\ge3\), the greatest common divisor of all \(\binom{q(mp^a-1)}{mp^a-1}\) for \(2\le q\le mp^a\) is exactly \(p^a\). A uniform Kummer-carry argument supplies the full prime-power valuation, refining the previously known criterion that detected only the presence of a prime factor.
