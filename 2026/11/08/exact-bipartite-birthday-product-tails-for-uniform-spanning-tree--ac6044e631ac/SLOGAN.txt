For a uniform spanning tree of the complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), every marked-pair distance has an exact parity-resolved birthday-product tail: across parts, \(\Pr(D>2k+1)=(a-1)_{\underline{k+1}}(b-1)_{\underline{k+1}}/(ab)^{k+1}\); within the \(a\)-part, \(\Pr(D>2k)=(a-2)_{\underline{k}}(b-1)_{\underline{k}}/(ab)^k\). These finite laws hold even for highly unbalanced parts and yield a Rayleigh distance limit whenever both part sizes diverge.
