Two distinct interior occupation local times before first absorption in Poisson-reset Brownian motion have the exact transform \(\mathbb E e^{-sL_a-tL_b}=[1+As+Bt+\eta ABst]^{-1}\). Their correlation rises strictly from \(a/b\) to one with the reset rate and their entire joint law is independent of the reset location whenever \(R\ge b\).
