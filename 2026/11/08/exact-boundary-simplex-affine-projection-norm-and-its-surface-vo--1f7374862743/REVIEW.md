## Mathematical value — PASS
This is not a change of notation for a solid-simplex formula: the boundary sampling measure is singular and changes both Gram normalization and the one-coordinate marginal by adding an atom at zero. The result determines the exact sharp projector norm, all norming locations, and the positivity defect for a natural boundary-only least-squares discretization. Its strict stability improvement in every dimension, together with a limiting nonzero gap of \(2/e\), compares two canonical observation measures on the same family of simplices. These are mathematically motivated and apply to an entire dimension-indexed class, rather than to an arbitrary numerical instance.
