For two independent random finite monadic structures with \(r\) unary predicates, the probability of agreeing on every first-order sentence of fixed quantifier rank has an explicit bivariate coefficient formula and a sharp two-dimensional saddle asymptotic as the number \(2^r\) of Boolean types grows. At rank one and domain size \(\lfloor 2^r\log2\rfloor\), the collision probability is asymptotic to \(2^{-2^r}/\sqrt{1-(\log2)^2}\), with a nontrivial exact prefactor.
