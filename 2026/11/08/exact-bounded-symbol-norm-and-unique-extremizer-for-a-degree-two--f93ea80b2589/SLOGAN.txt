For \(\theta_r(z)=(r-z^2)/(1-rz^2)\), every \(0<r<1\) admits an exact sharp bounded-symbol norm \(\beta_{\theta_r}(R_0^{\theta_r})=1/F_r\), where \(F_r\) is an explicit Poisson-weighted circular-distance integral with unique geometric median \(2r/(1+r^2)\). The optimal symbol exists, is continuous with constant modulus, and is unique up to null sets.
