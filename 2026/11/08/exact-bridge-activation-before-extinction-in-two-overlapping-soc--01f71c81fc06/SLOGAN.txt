For two overlapping social hyperedges with independent recoveries and whole-group threshold reactivation, the exact probability of activating the target group before extinction reduces to a finite hypergeometric order-statistic sum. If the overlap reaches the target threshold, the failure probability as source reactivation \(\alpha\to\infty\) is asymptotic to \(a!\delta^a/(\beta\alpha^{a-1})\), with a leading coefficient independent of the overlap size and target threshold.
