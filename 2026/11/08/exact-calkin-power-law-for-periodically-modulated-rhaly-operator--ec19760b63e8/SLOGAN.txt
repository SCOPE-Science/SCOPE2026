For every complex periodic Rhaly modulation, the essential norm of the \(p\)-th power is exactly \(2^p\sqrt\mu\,|m|^{p-1}\), where \(m\) is the coefficient mean and \(\mu\) is the mean squared modulus. At zero mean the operator stays noncompact but its square becomes Hilbert–Schmidt, yielding a nonzero square-zero element of the Calkin algebra even when its Gram operator agrees exactly with that of the ordinary Cesàro operator.
