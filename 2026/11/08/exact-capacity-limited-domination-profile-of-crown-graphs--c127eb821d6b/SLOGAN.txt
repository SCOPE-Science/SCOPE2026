All capacity-limited dominating sets of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\) admit an exact two-shore classification by whether their chosen matching indices overlap, yielding the complete bivariate enumerator for every \(n\ge3\) and \(1\le k\le n-2\). Deleting the matching lowers the minimum from \(2(n-k)\) by exactly two only in the sharp intermediate regime \(n-1-k\ge2\) and \(n\ge2(n-1-k)\); all minimum sets are counted explicitly.
