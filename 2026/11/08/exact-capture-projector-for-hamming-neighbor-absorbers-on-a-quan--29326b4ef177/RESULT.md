# Exact capture projector for Hamming-neighbor absorbers on a quantum hypercube

## Finding
For every integer \(d\ge2\), consider a one-particle continuous-time quantum walk on the binary hypercube \(Q_d\), with Hamiltonian
\[
H_{\mathrm{eff}}=JA(Q_d)+\epsilon I-i\sum_{i=1}^{d}\gamma_i|e_i\rangle\langle e_i|,
\qquad J>0,\quad\epsilon\in\mathbb R,\quad\gamma_i>0.
\]
Here \(e_i\) is the Boolean vertex of Hamming weight one supported at coordinate \(i\). Let \(P_B\) denote the orthogonal projector onto the entire space of initially accessible (ultimately decaying) states. For Boolean vertices \(x,y\subseteq[d]\), set \(k=|x|\), \(\ell=|y|\), and \(q=|x\cap y|\). Write
\[
K_j(k;d)=\sum_{s=0}^{j}(-1)^s\binom{k}{s}\binom{d-k}{j-s}
\]
with out-of-range binomial coefficients zero. The **complete capture-projector kernel**, including all off-diagonal coherences, is
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\langle x|P_B|y\rangle={}&\mathbf1_{k=\ell}\left[
\frac1{\binom dk}+
\mathbf1_{1\le k\le d-1}\frac{d(d-1)}{\binom dk\,k(d-k)}
\left(q-\frac{k^2}{d}\right)\right]\\
&-\mathbf1_{2\mid d}
\frac{K_{d/2}(k;d)K_{d/2}(\ell;d)}{2^d\binom d{d/2}}.
\end{aligned}}
\]
The final term is absent for odd \(d\). For **every** initially normalized pure state \(\psi\), eventual capture is \(\langle\psi,P_B\psi\rangle\); for every initial density matrix \(\rho\), it is \(\operatorname{tr}(P_B\rho)\). The projector is independent of the positive individual loss rates.

In particular, for a localized initial vertex of Hamming weight \(k\),
\[
\boxed{\eta_d(k)=\frac{1+(d-1)\mathbf1_{1\le k\le d-1}}{\binom dk}
-\mathbf1_{2\mid d}\frac{K_{d/2}(k;d)^2}{2^d\binom d{d/2}}.}
\]
For odd \(d\), capture coherences between **different Hamming-weight shells** vanish exactly; for even \(d\), all such cross-shell matrix elements are generated by one rank-one central Krawtchouk mode. Within-shell coherences can matter in both parities.

As a concrete interference consequence, when \(d\) is even, let \(\psi_\pm=(|0^d\rangle\pm|1^d\rangle)/\sqrt2\) and \(b_d=2^{-d}\binom d{d/2}\). Then
\[
\eta(\psi_\pm)=1-b_d\left[1\pm(-1)^{d/2}\right].
\]
One of these two antipodal superpositions is captured with certainty, while the other has capture probability \(1-2b_d\). For \(d=4\), the plus superposition is captured with probability \(1/4\), and the minus superposition with probability \(1\), although either individual corner has capture probability \(5/8\). This is an exact preparation-dependent change with the same absorber placement and rates.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph vertices are the subsets of \([d]\), with nearest-neighbor adjacency given by toggling one coordinate. The Hilbert-space basis \(|x\rangle\) is orthonormal in unnormalized counting measure. The loss matrix \(\Gamma=\sum_i\gamma_i|e_i\rangle\langle e_i|\) is positive at **all** \(d\) weight-one vertices and zero elsewhere. Loss rates need not be equal, but all must be strictly positive. Coherent hopping \(J\) is equal and nonzero on all coordinate edges; \(\epsilon\) is a scalar offset. An initially normalized state has survival probability \(\|e^{-iH_{\mathrm{eff}}t}\psi\|^2\), so eventual capture is one minus its limit. The result is about eventual capture, not finite-time decay rates.

## Proof
**1. Why one orthogonal spectral projector controls eventual capture.** Let \(A=A(Q_d)\). The largest \(A\)-invariant subspace of \(\ker\Gamma\) is the dark space \(D\), equivalently the sum of eigenspaces of \(A\) consisting of vectors vanishing at every absorbing vertex. Its orthogonal complement \(B=D^\perp\) is the minimal \(A\)-invariant subspace containing all \(|e_i\rangle\). Because \(A\) and \(\Gamma\) are Hermitian and \(\operatorname{ran}\Gamma\subseteq B\), both subspaces reduce \(H_{\mathrm{eff}}\). On \(D\), evolution is unitary. On \(B\), every eigenvalue of \(H_{\mathrm{eff}}\) has strictly negative imaginary part: if an eigenvector had a real eigenvalue, the imaginary part of its Rayleigh quotient would give \(\langle\psi,\Gamma\psi\rangle=0\), hence \(\psi\in\ker\Gamma\), and its eigenvalue equation would make it an \(A\)-eigenvector in the excluded dark subspace. Thus the finite-dimensional evolution on \(B\) decays exponentially up to polynomial factors, while the \(D\) norm persists. Therefore eventual capture equals the orthogonal \(B\)-weight of the initial state, with no dependence on the numerical positive loss rates.

**2. Decompose the cyclic absorber space into radial and first-harmonic pieces.** For each weight \(0\le k\le d\), let \(R_k\) be the normalized constant function on the Hamming sphere \(S_k=\{x:|x|=k\}\). The radial space \(R=\operatorname{span}\{R_0,\ldots,R_d\}\) has dimension \(d+1\). For each \(1\le k\le d-1\) and coefficient vector \(a\in\mathbb C^d\) with \(\sum_i a_i=0\), define
\[
f_{k,a}(x)=\mathbf1_{|x|=k}\sum_{i\in x}a_i.
\]
The \((d-1)\)-dimensional space \(W_k=\{f_{k,a}:\sum_i a_i=0\}\) is orthogonal to \(R\) and to all other \(W_\ell\). Injectivity follows because every coordinate occurs in some but not all weight-\(k\) subsets. Directly summing neighbors gives
\[
Af_{k,a}=k f_{k+1,a}+(d-k)f_{k-1,a},\qquad f_{0,a}=f_{d,a}=0.
\]
The absorber source space \(\operatorname{span}\{|e_i\rangle\}\) splits into the radial vector \(\sum_i|e_i\rangle\) and its coefficient-sum-zero component \(W_1\). Repeatedly applying \(A\), using the nonzero nearest-shell raising coefficients, generates **every** \(W_k\); conversely their direct sum is \(A\)-invariant. Hence the nonradial bright space is exactly \(\bigoplus_{k=1}^{d-1}W_k\).

**3. The sole possible radial dark mode.** The orthonormal radial eigenvectors are
\[
\phi_j(x)=\frac{K_j(|x|;d)}{\sqrt{2^d\binom dj}},\qquad
A\phi_j=(d-2j)\phi_j,\qquad 0\le j\le d.
\]
This follows by summing the standard Walsh characters of fixed Fourier weight \(j\). The overlap of \(\phi_j\) with the radial source \(\sum_i|e_i\rangle\) is proportional to
\[
K_j(1;d)=\binom dj\left(1-\frac{2j}{d}\right).
\]
Since the radial eigenvalues \(d-2j\) are all distinct, the radial source cyclically generates every radial eigendirection except, when \(d\) is even, precisely \(\phi_{d/2}\). Thus
\[
B=\left(R\oplus\bigoplus_{k=1}^{d-1}W_k\right)\ominus
\begin{cases}\operatorname{span}\{\phi_{d/2}\},&2\mid d,\\\{0\},&2\nmid d.\end{cases}
\]
This identifies the entire accessible projector, not just its dimension. The total bright dimension \(d(d-1)+2-\mathbf1_{2\mid d}\) is a consistency check with earlier Hamming-sphere spectral-rank results and is **not asserted here as a new dimension theorem**.

**4. Exact matrix elements of its three pieces.** The radial projector is
\[
\langle x|P_R|y\rangle=\mathbf1_{|x|=|y|}/\binom d{|x|}.
\]
To project onto \(W_k\), regard its values as the centered subset-incidence vectors \(v_x=\mathbf1_x-(k/d)\mathbf1\in\mathbf1^\perp\). Permutation symmetry (or direct counting of pairs of subsets) gives the exact frame operator identity
\[
\sum_{|x|=k}v_xv_x^*=\frac{\binom dk\,k(d-k)}{d(d-1)}I_{\mathbf1^\perp}.
\]
Therefore the orthogonal projector onto the range of the incidence map has entries
\[
\langle x|P_{W_k}|y\rangle=
\mathbf1_{|x|=|y|=k}\frac{d(d-1)}{\binom dk\,k(d-k)}
\left(|x\cap y|-\frac{k^2}{d}\right).
\]
Finally, when \(d\) is even, subtracting \(|\phi_{d/2}\rangle\langle\phi_{d/2}|\) yields exactly the boxed kernel. The diagonal formula follows by setting \(x=y\). On the two antipodal corners the radial projector has no off-diagonal entry, while the omitted mode contributes \((-1)^{d/2}b_d\) to the dark overlap; the displayed coherent-superposition formula follows.

## Verification
The included standard-library script `artifacts/verify.py` independently forms the spectral Gram pseudoinverse of each distinct Walsh eigenspace restricted to the complete absorber set, and compares its entries as exact rational numbers against the displayed direct formula. It checks **21,840 ordered projector matrix elements** across all dimensions \(2\le d\le7\). It additionally computes the adjacency Krylov rank over a large prime in each dimension and checks the projector trace, reporting `VERIFY_OK exact_projector_entries=21840 modular_krylov=6`. The finite calculations are corroboration only; the arbitrary-dimension proof is Steps 1–4 above. To reproduce, run `python3 artifacts/verify.py` in the package root; only Python 3's standard library is required.

## Relationship to prior work
Moore and Russell (2001) introduce and analyze the coherent hypercube walk and its Walsh-spectrum dynamics, without the local absorber capture kernel. Krovi and Brun (2006) develop the dark-eigenspace mechanism for infinite quantum hitting times, including hypercube walks, but do not give this complete continuous-time shell-absorber kernel. Agliari, Mülken and Blumen (2009) give the non-Hermitian local-trap formalism and examples on rings, not this Hamming-shell capture effect operator.

General Hamming-shell spectral-rank computations already determine the size of the bright subspace and particular localized initial-state survival values; the contribution here is **the entire orthogonal projector in the position basis**, hence the eventual capture functional for arbitrary coherent initial states. Rank or one-site survival data alone do not specify its off-diagonal entries or the antipodal phase-controlled capture law. Related Boolean-Hamming-ball first-Walsh-band concentration spectra and nonbinary single-shell Fourier sampling spectra concern different compressed operators and do not state the all-energy absorbing-walk projector. The literature comparison is finite; an equivalent historical formula in association-scheme or quantum-network control literature remains possible.

## Limitations
Uniform coherent hopping, one-particle dynamics, the **complete** weight-one absorbing layer, and strictly positive absorber rates at every site are essential assumptions. The claim is not about unequal coherent edge couplings, missing absorber sites, finite-time decay scales, dephasing, interactions, or thermodynamic phases. Although the formula determines all initial-state probabilities, it does not optimize a preparation under physical resource constraints. Earlier general bright-dimension and corner-survival results are acknowledged rather than repackaged as novelty. The mathematical comparison does not constitute independent external verification or a proof of historical priority.
