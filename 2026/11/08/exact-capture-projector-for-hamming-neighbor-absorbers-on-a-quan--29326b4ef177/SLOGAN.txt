The complete eventual-capture projector of a uniform quantum walk on \(Q_d\), with arbitrary positive absorbers at every weight-one vertex, has an explicit two-shell-incidence formula plus a single parity-dependent rank-one correction. In even dimensions, opposite-corner coherent superpositions can have radically different capture probabilities despite identical individual-corner probabilities; for \(d=4\), the two relative signs give capture \(1\) and \(1/4\).
