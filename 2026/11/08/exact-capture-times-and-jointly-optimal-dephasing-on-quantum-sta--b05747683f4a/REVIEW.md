# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The site-basis Lindblad dynamics and its off-diagonal dephasing normalization are explicit. The Hilbert–Schmidt dissipation identity forces the only invariant dark state for positive dephasing to be zero. Symmetry/linearity allow a localized leaf to be represented by an **incoherent** uniform leaf mixture for the survival functional; this is not the coherent peripheral preparation used in the closely related literature. A four-variable ODE yields exact time integrals. Strict convexity in each of the two positive rates proves the unique global optimizer. The zero-dephasing bright/dark decomposition handles the boundary separately. Independent exact-rational and unrestricted Liouvillian verifiers agree with the formulas. The finite tests are independent corroboration rather than the infinite-family proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, provisional

Multiple semantic searches of published mathematical claims and primary scientific literature found no exact duplicate. Rebentrost et al. (arXiv:0807.0929) focus on FMO and binary trees; Kassal–Aspuru-Guzik (arXiv:1201.5202) on ordered chains and capture *efficiency*; Alterman–Berman–Strauch (arXiv:2309.00164) on complete-graph hopping. The closest model, Pepe–Pouthier–Yalouz (arXiv:2308.15857), includes a trapped/dephased extended star but studies a coherent peripheral superposition, a 50% capture quantile and energy-defect tuning. The localized-leaf unconditional mean, its exact all-size formula and joint trap/dephasing minimizer are not stated or directly implied by those examined results. The full 2004 Gaab–Bardeen Haken–Strobl optimization paper could not be read, although its abstract confirms close conceptual overlap; this limits historical-priority confidence. No independent priority audit was performed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact result links three motivated effects in a single family: dark-state blockade when coherent, finite-noise release from a localized leaf, and suppression under very strong dephasing. It identifies two independent sharp optimal control rates with \(\sqrt m\)-scaling and an unexpected hub-initial discontinuity. These are quantities directly relevant to transport design and are not merely an arbitrary small-system enumeration.

## Scope and remaining risks

The calculation does not cover disordered hopping, correlated or site-dependent noise, interacting excitations, other trap locations, finite-time quantiles or conditional capture at zero dephasing. The publication priority of the exact identity is provisional, especially against the inaccessible full 2004 article.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
