For every labelled cycle \(C_n\), the number of unordered maximum three-block total outer-independent coalition partitions is \(N_n=(a_n+b_n-f_n)/2-2\mathbf1_{3\mid n}\), where the three short linear recurrences are given in the result. Exactly one partition has a triangular coalition graph when \(3\mid n\); all others have a three-vertex path, and \(N_n\sim(2.065994892\ldots)^n/2\).
