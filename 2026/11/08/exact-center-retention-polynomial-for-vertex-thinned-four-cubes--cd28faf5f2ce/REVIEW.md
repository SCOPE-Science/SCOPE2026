# Scientific review: center retention in the four-cube

## Correctness: PASS

Strict separation gives an exact equivalence between origin-avoiding subsets and subsets of positive antipodal transversals. Every one of the 256 sign patterns is either obstructed by one of the twenty exact rational circuits or realized by an explicitly enumerated normal with integer coordinates in the range from minus two to two. The resulting 104 transversals give the histogram exactly. A genuinely independent proof uses Carathéodory and all 48 minimal positive dependencies of sizes two, four and five. Both exact enumerations in the standalone checker agree on all 65,536 masks. Zero retained points and all probability endpoints are handled explicitly. The finite loops constitute an exhaustive finite proof, not numerical evidence for an unverified asymptotic statement.

## Originality: PASS, historical priority provisional

The 104 three-variable threshold-function count is classical and not claimed. The published cut-complex classification concerns separable hyperplane cuts, while the precise overlapping down-closure of all positive antipodal transversals, its vertex-cardinality histogram, and the corresponding Bernoulli center-reliability polynomial are distinct enumerative objects. The inspected treatment by Emamy and Meléndez Ríos describes separable cut types rather than these counts; Newman's vertex-thinning and Dyer–Füredi–McDiarmid's random-volume theorems have different conclusions. Direct searches of relevant records and published sources found no equivalent polynomial. The complete 2011 cut-complex paper was not accessible, so a prior equivalent computation remains a genuine risk. An exact polynomial is not a claim that the underlying threshold classification is new.

## Mathematical value: PASS

Center retention is a natural geometric coverage property of Bernoulli random vertex polytopes. Dimension four is the first small Boolean-cube case in which nontrivial five-vertex minimal positive dependencies coexist with opposite-pair and four-vertex witnesses. The full sixteen-cardinality law supplies exact without-replacement probabilities as well as the entire Bernoulli reliability polynomial, which is a useful finite benchmark for random 0/1-polytope models. Overlapping threshold-classifier down-closures mean the coefficients do not follow from merely citing the known number 104.

## Scientific limitations and literature risk

No arbitrary-dimensional extension, interior-containment law, or asymptotic threshold is proved. Historic cut enumerations could imply this specific histogram after additional computation; historical priority is provisional. The 2011 article could not be inspected in full because licensed access was unavailable, although its abstract and a closely related author manuscript were reviewed.
