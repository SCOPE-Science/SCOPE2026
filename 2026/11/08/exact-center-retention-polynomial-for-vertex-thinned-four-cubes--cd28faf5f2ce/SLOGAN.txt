Independent vertex retention in a four-dimensional cube has an exact center-containment polynomial: the numbers of center-avoiding subsets of cardinalities zero through eight are \(1,16,112,448,1096,1584,1312,576,104\), and no larger subset avoids the center. Two independent exact certificates, one through 104 strict separating transversals and one through 48 minimal positive dependencies, prove the complete law.
