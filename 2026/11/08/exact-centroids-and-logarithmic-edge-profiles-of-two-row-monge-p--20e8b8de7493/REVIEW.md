# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The Monge inequalities are exactly the ordering of independent exponential-pair differences. Normalizing independent exponentials induces uniform affine volume on the simplex, and the ordering event has probability \(1/q!\). Conditional exponential splitting gives the coordinate centroid as a positive-part Laplace order-statistic expectation, integrated exactly on a rational interval. The strict convexity proof uses the positive distributional second derivative of the Laplace quantile's positive part; the boundary logarithm uses the exact harmonic-sum endpoint. Interior asymptotics use beta order-statistic concentration and uniform integrability. This covers every integer \(q\ge1\), and finite tests are not used as an infinite proof. `verify_centroid.py` reported 4036 passing exact-rational assertions for \(1\le q\le36\), including an independently derived conditional-Beta computation for \(q=2\).

**Originality — PASS, with priority limitations.** Erickson–Kretschmann (arXiv:2311.07522v2) develops vertices/facets and the exact two-row normalized volume, but its relevant full-text sections do not state the centroid coordinates or edge asymptotics. Erickson–Jones (arXiv:2609.09508v1) gives an all-dimension volume formula, Ehrhart series and explicit volume table, not integrated first moments. These statements do not imply the new moment evaluation through a direct substitution; the centroid cannot be recovered from the scalar normalized volume alone. Targeted arXiv/web queries and semantic mathematical-record searches for centroid/barycenter, mean matrix entries, Laplace-order-statistic equivalence, and log-edge profiles found no covering theorem; the closest record concerns Euclidean **incenters** of square Monge polytopes, not their volume centroid. The comparison is bounded, not exhaustive, and independent priority assessment remains outstanding.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The normalized Monge polytope is a canonical family of cost matrices in discrete optimization. Its volume and combinatorial face structure are studied, but those scalar/combinatorial invariants do not describe its first-moment geometry. The full exact centroid and strict U-shaped column profile show an order-induced nonuniform mass distribution, with a logarithmically enhanced edge despite a constant average row mass. Both the all-parameter rational coordinates and asymptotic shape provide reusable benchmarks for integral geometry and sampling of Monge cost arrays, rather than an arbitrary finite parameter substitution.

**Closest literature and residual limits.** Erickson–Kretschmann (2023), Sections 1 and 5.2; Erickson–Jones (2026), introduction, Theorems A/B description, volume table and Stanley decomposition discussion. A more specialized unpublished centroid calculation could still exist. The theorem is restricted to two rows and does not establish stochastic concentration, centroid formulas for three rows, or independent scholarly validation.
