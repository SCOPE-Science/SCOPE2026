Primitive two-cycles of parallel chip-firing on every finite ring have an exact chip-mass generating polynomial, whose total is the Pell–Lucas expression \((1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n+2(-1)^n\). Their allowed energies have a parity-dependent sharp boundary, and a uniformly sampled primitive-two configuration has asymptotically Gaussian chip mass with mean \(3n/2\) and variance \(n\sqrt2/8\).
