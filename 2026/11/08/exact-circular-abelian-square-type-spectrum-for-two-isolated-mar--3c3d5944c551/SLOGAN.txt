Every circular binary word \(a^pba^qb\) has an exact two-threshold formula for its non-equivalent Abelian-square types: \(z_m=\mathbf1_{\{2m\le\max(p,q)\}}+\mathbf1_{\{m\ge\lceil(\min(p,q)+2)/2\rceil\}}\). In odd length, every allowed half-length supports a square type, and the total is exactly \((n-1)/2+(|p-q|-1)/2\), attaining the conjectured minimum precisely when the two gaps differ by one.
