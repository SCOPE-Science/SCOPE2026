Every finite signed profile on the iterated classicality ladder \(p,\circ p,\circ^2p,\ldots\) in minimal evidence logic is globally realizable exactly when a positive classicality flag excludes inconsistent or incomplete evidence one level below, giving \(4\cdot3^{k-1}\) depth-\(k\) profiles. Positive combinations of the signed literals form precisely the upset lattice of an explicit punctured profile poset (83 and 70,806 classes at depths two and three), while positive combinations of the unsigned ladder form a free distributive lattice.
