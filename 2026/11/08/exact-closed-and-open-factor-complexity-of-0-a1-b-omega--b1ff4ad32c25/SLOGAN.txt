For the two-block periodic binary word \((0^a1^b)^\omega\), the number of distinct closed factors at every length has an explicit parity-sensitive two-parameter formula, and open complexity is obtained by subtraction from \(\min(n,a)+\min(n,b)\). Every factor is closed at all lengths from \(a+b+\max(a,b)\) onward, while an open factor still exists one length earlier.
