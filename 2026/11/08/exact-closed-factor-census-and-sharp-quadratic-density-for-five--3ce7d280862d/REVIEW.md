# Review of the five-run closed-factor census

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed exact count is obtained by a disjoint classification of distinct closed factors into zero through five runs. The one-run and two-run cases are immediate; the three-run count has one overlap correction when the two inner one-block lengths coincide; the four-run count uses the only possible mixed-symbol border; and the five-run count distinguishes mixed-symbol returns from long pure-zero returns. The elementary run-length identities collapse the count to the displayed formula. The quadratic upper bound is a direct constrained product inequality and the five-run construction realizes the limiting constant. The independent finite enumerator passed all stated cases. The infinite conclusion depends on the symbolic argument, not finite tests.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The 2013 Badkobeh–Fici–Lipták source classifies CR-poor (bitonic) binary words and supplies a quadratic four-run construction, without a five-run all-parameter census. The later Parshina–Puzynina source proves the unrestricted leading coefficient \(1/6\), and uses a special five-run family as an example, but does not derive the five-run exponent formula or its sharp leading coefficient \(1/8\). A published result on unrestricted finite maxima at lengths 25–27 is not implication-equivalent to an all-length fixed-five-run formula; its full package was inaccessible during comparison. Exhaustive historical priority is not claimed, and the finite-source inspection gap remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS

The count of distinct complete first returns is an established string-combinatorial invariant, and the five-run family is a natural low-complexity extension beyond the three-run CR-poor boundary where non-palindromic returns can contribute quadratically. The theorem provides the complete closed-factor count for every choice of five run lengths and a sharp asymptotic capacity under the run constraint. The correction that appears exactly when the inner run lengths agree is structurally meaningful, not a numerical table. No unrestricted extremal or higher-run theorem is inferred.

## Scientific limitations

Binary, linear, exactly five nonempty runs; empty factor counted; exact finite-length maximizer among all five-run words is not classified. The global unrestricted sharp constant remains the existing \(1/6\) result. The finding has not been independently audited.
