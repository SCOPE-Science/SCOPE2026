For every five-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d0^e\), the number of distinct closed factors is exactly \(n+1+\min(b,d)(\min(a,c)+\min(c,e))+\mathbf1_{b=d}(\min(a,c)\min(c,e)-\min(a,c,e))\). The maximum over five-run words of length \(n\) has sharp leading term \(n^2/8\), contrasting with the unrestricted \(n^2/6\) asymptotic.
