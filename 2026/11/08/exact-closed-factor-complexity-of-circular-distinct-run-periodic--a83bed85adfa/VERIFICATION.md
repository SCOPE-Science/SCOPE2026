This package contains a self-contained proof and `closed_verify.py` with direct factor enumeration. Exhaustive comparisons: 35,920 profiles/lengths for alphabet run counts 2–5 and run lengths 1–4, and 2,000 additional seeded tests over larger instances. Every comparison passed. The finite tests do not certify arbitrary integers; the paper proof supplies the general statement. Review was performed on the exact claim presented, not by an independent reviewer. The construction is limited to infinite periodic words with pairwise distinct run labels.
