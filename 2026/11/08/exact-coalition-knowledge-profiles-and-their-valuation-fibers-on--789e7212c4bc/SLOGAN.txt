On a full finite \(\mathsf{S5}\) product, the coalitions failing to know one proposition at a point realize exactly the Dedekind-number family of order ideals, with an explicit product formula for every valuation fiber. The extreme profile where only the grand coalition knows has probability \(t\prod_i(1-t^{n_i-1})\) and a sharp balanced threshold at \(k t^{n-1}\asymp1\).
