In the exactly solvable four-direction slit model, the criterion \(2(1-\alpha)-(\alpha)\eta\tau_r=0\) separates continuous and discontinuous loss of the finite run-time diffusivity optimum. The discontinuous regime has explicit coexistence and spinodal speeds with a metastable finite optimum, while the tricritical boundary changes the optimizer divergence from inverse-linear to inverse-square-root.
