Two noninteracting bosons on a Boolean hypercube with irreversible loss at every collision have exactly \(2^{d-1}(2^d-d-1)\) protected states, for every \(d\ge2\) and all strictly positive site-dependent collision-loss rates. For initially separated particles the eventual loss depends only on Hamming distance \(s\), by the exact law \(\eta_d(s)=2\sum_{j=s}^{d}\binom dj/(2^d\binom ds)\), strictly decreasing from nearest neighbors to antipodes.
