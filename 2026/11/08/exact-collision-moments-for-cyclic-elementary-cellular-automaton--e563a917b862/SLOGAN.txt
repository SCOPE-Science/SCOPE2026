For cyclic elementary cellular automaton Rule 4, the number of ordered input pairs that collapse to the same one-step output grows asymptotically as \(\Lambda^n\), with leading constant one and \(\Lambda=3.138990099335421\ldots\) the largest root of \(t^6-3t^5-t^3-2t^2+2t+2\). An exact \(16\)-state transfer matrix and a separate weighted-gap formula determine the collision count at every ring length \(n\ge3\).
