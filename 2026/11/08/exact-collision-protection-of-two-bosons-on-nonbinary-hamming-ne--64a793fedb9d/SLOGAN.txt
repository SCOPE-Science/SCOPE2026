On every nonbinary Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\), two noninteracting bosons subject to loss at every collision site have exactly \(q^d(q^d+1)/2-q^{d-1}((d+1)q+d)+d\) permanently protected states. A finite rational coefficient formula gives their ultimate capture probability at every initial Hamming separation, independent of all strictly positive collision-loss rates; on \(H(2,q)\) the two distance classes have capture probabilities \((q^2+2q-2)/(q^2(q-1))\) and \(2/(q-1)^2\).
