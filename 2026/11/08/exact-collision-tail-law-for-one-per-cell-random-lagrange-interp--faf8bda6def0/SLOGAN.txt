With one uniform random interpolation node in each equal-width cell, the Euclidean norm of fixed-point Lagrange weights has an exact \(t^{-2}\) tail, with a positive coefficient except at the two-node central evaluation point. Thus full-cell jitter yields almost-sure interpolation solvability but infinite unconditional variance from independent evaluation noise for every degree at least two; at three nodes and the midpoint the sharp coefficient is \(9/16-(\log2)/2\).
