For any spider tree, the number of strict color-refinement rounds is \(\lceil L/2\rceil\) if all arms have equal length and otherwise \(\max\{\lceil L/2\rceil,B+1\}\), where \(L\) and \(B\) are the largest and second-largest **distinct** arm lengths. All refinement classes at each round are truncated center/leaf distance classes, and the stable classes are exactly the automorphism orbits.
