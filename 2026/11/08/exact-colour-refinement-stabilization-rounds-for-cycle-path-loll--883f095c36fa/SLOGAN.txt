For a cycle of length \(m\ge3\) with a pendant path of \(\ell\ge1\) edges, uniform-start colour refinement stabilizes after exactly \(\max\{\lfloor m/2\rfloor,\min(m,\ell),\lceil\ell/2\rceil\}\) strict rounds. Its final colours are precisely the automorphism orbits, and the three terms correspond to cycle symmetry, cycle–tail ambiguity, and unresolved middle-tail vertices.
