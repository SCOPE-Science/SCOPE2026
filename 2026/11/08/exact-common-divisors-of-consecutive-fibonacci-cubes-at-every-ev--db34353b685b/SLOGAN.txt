For every \(m\ge1\), the gcd of all sums of \(2m\) consecutive Fibonacci cubes is exactly \(3^{\mathbf1_{4\mid m}}U_m/2^{\mathbf1_{3\mid m}}\), with \(U_m=F_m\) for even \(m\) and \(U_m=L_m\) for odd \(m\). The factor-of-two correction occurs precisely when \(3\mid m\), giving a complete corrected answer to the even-length Fibonacci-cube gcd question.
