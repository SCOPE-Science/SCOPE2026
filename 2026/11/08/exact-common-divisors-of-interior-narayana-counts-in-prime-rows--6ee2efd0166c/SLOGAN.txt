For an odd prime \(p\), the gcd of all nonendpoint Narayana numbers in row \(p\) is exactly \(p\), except when \(p\) is a Fermat prime other than \(3\): it is \(2p\) at \(p=5\) and \(4p\) at every larger Fermat prime. Thus a common divisor of Dyck-path counts with an interior number of peaks detects the Fermat-prime exception without relying on numerical tables.
