For every odd finite field, the number of ordered split-octonion pairs with prescribed commutator \(z\) has a complete four-stratum formula: beyond zero, it depends only on whether the trace-zero quadratic norm \(Q(z)\) vanishes or is a square or nonsquare. The nonzero square/nonsquare fiber gap is exactly \(2(q^8-q^6)\); the known image and zero-fiber phenomena are not claimed as new.
