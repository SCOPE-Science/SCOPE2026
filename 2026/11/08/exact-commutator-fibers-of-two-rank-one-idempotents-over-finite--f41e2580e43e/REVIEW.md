# Scientific review: exact commutator fibers of two rank-one idempotents

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem specifies the exact finite-field domain, ordered rank-one projector constraints, additive commutator orientation, target classes, characteristic restrictions and the rank-one/trace-one normal form. For each first projector \(P\), the second projector \(Q\) is determined by the off-diagonal entries and by a root of \(a(1-a)=\det C\), making the scalar-root factor exact. Compatibility of the first projector is counted by invariant lines for invertible traceless targets, by two kernel/image families for nonzero nilpotents, and by exclusions for scalars. The odd-field image cardinality follows from the finite-field quadratic Jacobi sum; the even-field image cardinality follows from the absolute trace. A standalone checker enumerated all ordered rank-one projector pairs and all target matrices over eight fields, including three extension fields, and compared every fiber and the image count. The source and the output are embedded. The proof, not the finite sample, establishes all prime powers.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
The 2010 determinant identity already entails \(\det[P,Q]=\operatorname{tr}(PQ)(1-\operatorname{tr}(PQ))\) for rank-one projectors; that necessary invariant is expressly not claimed original. The 2002 characterization (inaccessible full text), 2018 ring-theoretic invertibility criteria, and 2023 projector square-root existence theorem address whether commutators occur but do not, in the inspected material, give the complete prescribed finite-field fiber distribution or the closed image-cardinality law. The 2009 result on sums of idempotents concerns a different operation. Similarity-type and alternative-parameter queries were compared against relevant accessible records, without using search absence to assert priority. The historical-priority assessment is provisional because a full-text check of the 2002 source and a comprehensive independent literature audit are still missing.

## Value — PASS
This is a natural complete preimage-size invariant of an algebraic commutator restricted to a conjugacy class of rank-one idempotents. It distinguishes individual traceless targets with identical determinant by rational eigenline incidence, gives complete characteristic-two Artin--Schreier obstructions, and yields an exact image size with a nontrivial congruence dependence for odd field order. It is substantially more informative than mere existence or a determinant relation; all natural targets and all field orders are classified rather than choosing an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Closest literature, assumptions and limitations
The primary 2010 determinant relation is acknowledged as providing the scalar support equation. The 2018 and 2023 idempotent-commutator existence criteria do not imply a target-fiber count without a further enumeration of compatible decompositions. The 2002 characterization is highly relevant but was accessible only through its abstract/review, so historical coverage cannot be definitively ruled out. No statement is made for arbitrary idempotent ranks, higher dimensions, rings with zero divisors, or similarity orbits of witnesses.
