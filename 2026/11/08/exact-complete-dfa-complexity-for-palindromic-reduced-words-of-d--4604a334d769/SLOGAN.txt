For a product of finitely many standard dihedral Coxeter systems, the language of nonempty palindromic reduced words has an exact minimal complete-DFA size of \(2+4|J|+2\sum_{i\in F}(M_i-1)+\mathbf{1}_{F\ne\varnothing}\), where \(M_i\) is the largest odd integer no greater than the finite dihedral exponent. This sharp quotient count incorporates a single shared terminal state across finite factors and gives six necessary states already for one infinite-dihedral factor.
