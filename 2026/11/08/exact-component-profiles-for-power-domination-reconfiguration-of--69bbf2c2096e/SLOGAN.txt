For every integer \(n\ge4\), the minimum-budget power-domination TAR graph of \(T_n=L(K_n)\) is discrete; at every larger budget it consists of one connected component and exactly the isolated inclusion-minimal sets of the permitted maximum size. Those sets are enumerated by labeled spanning star forests and odd-order near-perfect matchings, and \(n-2\) is the exact first connected budget.
