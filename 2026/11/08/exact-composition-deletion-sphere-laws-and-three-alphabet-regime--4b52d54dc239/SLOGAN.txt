Deleting one occurrence from the full prefix/suffix composition multiset of a uniform \(q\)-ary word has an exact finite-length sphere-size distribution governed by the returns of a \((q-1)\)-dimensional lattice walk. Its lost distinctness has a half-normal \(\sqrt n\) scale for \(q=2\), an exponential \(\log n\) scale for \(q=3\), and a tight geometric limit for \(q\ge4\).
