For every prime \(q\ge7\) with \(q\equiv1\pmod6\), the conductors of Abhyankar–Moh semigroups of degree \(3q\) are exactly \(0\), \(3q-1\), and all integers congruent to \(0\) or \(2\) modulo six from \(6q\) through \((3q-1)(3q-2)\). Thus an infinite family of odd squarefree composite degrees has an explicit closed conductor spectrum of size \(3q^2-5q+4\).
