For a squarefree cyclic group with \(r\ge2^t\) prime factors, restricting its factorization graph to subgroups with at least \(t\ge2\) prime divisors gives vertex- and edge-connectivity exactly \(2^t-1\). Every minimum vertex cut isolates exactly one subgroup of order divisible by precisely \(t\) primes, and there are exactly \(\binom rt\) such cuts.
