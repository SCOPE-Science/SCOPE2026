On a nearest-neighbor social opinion ring, the number of asymptotic closed influence classes is exactly the maximum of one and the number of clockwise R-to-L transitions among strictly direction-biased median agents, so universal consensus holds from every initial opinion precisely when there is at most one such transition. An exact law for the full number of persistent opinion classes under independent random median/averaging types yields closed consensus-reliability probabilities, a sparse-type scaling transition, and a sharp exact-half-weight boundary.
