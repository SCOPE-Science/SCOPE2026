On a triangular book (two adjacent hubs and any number of shared leaves), uniform asynchronous majority with current-opinion tie retention always reaches consensus, and its entire initial-state consensus-selection probability has a closed form. Two initially one-valued hubs against an arbitrarily large zero-valued population still win with probability approaching \(1/4\); adding the hub edge changes that probability by exactly \((s+3)/((s+1)(s+2))\) for odd leaf count \(s\), and by zero for even \(s\).
