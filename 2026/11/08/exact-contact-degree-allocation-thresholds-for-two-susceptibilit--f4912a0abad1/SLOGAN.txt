In a two-susceptibility configuration-model SIR epidemic with equal class sizes and a fixed integer mean contact degree, the exactly optimal integer degree assignment moves through a sequence of adjacent allocations at explicit susceptibility ratios. Once the high-risk class is sufficiently more susceptible, its optimal degree is one and the invasion threshold becomes independent of its susceptibility; a concrete allocation change shifts the reproduction number from \(21/20\) to \(1/6\) without changing mean degree.
