For normalized rank-one matrices over any finite field, convolution has an explicit rank-stratified Fourier spectrum: every positive singular value is a power of the field size, with exact multiplicities and a nontrivial kernel. In large rectangular dimensions, the fraction of recoverable Fourier modes decays asymptotically with the rectangular excess, while the positive spectral condition number is exactly \(q^{\min(m-1,n-1)}\).
