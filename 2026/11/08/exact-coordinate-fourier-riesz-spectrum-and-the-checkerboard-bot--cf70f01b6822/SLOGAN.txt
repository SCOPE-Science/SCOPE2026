All constant and one-coordinate Fourier characters on the affine finite-field \(2\times2\times2\) Segre cone form a Riesz family whose complete spectrum has an explicit Hamming-cube block decomposition. Its unique weakest mode is the coordinate checkerboard, with exact squared lower bound \(q/(q^3+2q^2-2)\) and optimal squared condition number asymptotic to \(12q^2\).
