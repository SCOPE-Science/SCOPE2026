On every finite connected nonregular network, the sharp maximum degree–attribute correlation compatible with failure of the node-averaged generalized friendship paradox is the supremum \(\rho_G=\sqrt{1-\operatorname{Corr}(d,\delta)^2}\), where \(\delta_i=\sum_{j\sim i}1/d_j\). An exact two-sided gap envelope holds for every prescribed correlation, and every extremum has an explicit bounded attribute assignment.
