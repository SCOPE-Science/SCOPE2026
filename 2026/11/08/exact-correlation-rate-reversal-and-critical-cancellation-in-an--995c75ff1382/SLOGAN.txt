In a harmonic Brownian trap with an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck fluctuating stiffness, the instantaneous decay rate of stationary position correlations can cross its eventual relaxation rate **at most once**; an exact tilted-beta threshold determines whether it crosses. At the critical noise-strength boundary, the leading \(e^{-\mu t}\) correction cancels and a strictly positive \(e^{-2\mu t}\) correction governs the late-time approach.
