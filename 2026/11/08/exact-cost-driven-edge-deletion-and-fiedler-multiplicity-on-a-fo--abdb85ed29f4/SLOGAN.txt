On a four-cycle with three unit edge prices and one price \(d^2\), the maximum continuous-time spectral gap is \(4B/(d^2+2d+5)\) until \(d=3\), then \(B/5\). Below the threshold the unique optimum uses every edge and has a double Fiedler eigenvalue; at the threshold a continuum of optima appears, and above it the expensive edge is absent from every optimum.
