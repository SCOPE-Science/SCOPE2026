For uniform points on an interval, the number \(R_n\) of mutual nearest-neighbor pairs and the total length \(W_n\) of these pairs have the exact positive covariance \((64n+105)/(2700(n+1))\) for \(n\ge5\). Their jointly Gaussian fluctuations have a nonzero limiting Pearson correlation \(8/(3\sqrt{22})\), despite the count growing linearly and the total length remaining bounded.
