The full number \(C_n\) of maximum-length no-adjacent-`11` binary de Bruijn cycles modulo rotation is an explicit directed-tree determinant on \(F_{n-1}\) branch vertices, for every \(n\ge2\). The maximum length and optimal factor support were already known; the contribution here is enumeration of **all** optimal cycles, with independently reproduced exact values through \(n=15\).
