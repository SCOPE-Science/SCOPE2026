For every alphabet with at least three symbols, the number of cyclic universal sequences containing each ordered triple with at least two nonzero entries exactly once is \(q^{q(q-2)}(q-1)^{2(q-2)}((q-2)!)^{2(q-1)}((q-1)!)^{(q-1)^2}\). The count comes from a complete Laplacian factorization of the weight-restricted de Bruijn graph, giving \(1728\) cycles for the ternary alphabet.
