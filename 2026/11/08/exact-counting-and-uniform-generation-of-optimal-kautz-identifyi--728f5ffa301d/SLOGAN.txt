Every minimum radius-one incoming identifying code of the ternary Kautz digraph \(K(2,n)\), \(n\ge2\), is a two-cycle-free directed cycle cover one order lower. There are exactly \(2\), \(4\), and \(27\cdot2^{3\cdot2^{n-3}-6}\) labeled optimum codes for \(n=2\), \(n=3\), and \(n\ge4\), respectively, and a rejection-free uniform sampler follows from three independent local obstructions.
