In the ternary relational language for elementary abelian two-groups, the exact counting-free \(k\)-variable theory sees dimension only up to \(k\) for \(k\leq3\) and up to \(k-1\) for \(k\geq4\). In particular, the nonisomorphic groups \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^4\) and \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^5\) are indistinguishable by every five-variable first-order sentence, contradicting a published unconditional five-variable distinguishability claim, while six variables separate them.
