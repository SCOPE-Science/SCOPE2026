Linear binary universal partial words with one wildcard have exactly \(2^{2^{n-1}-n+1}\) realizations when the wildcard is first. When it is second, the count for \(n\ge4\) is \(\frac{2^{n-2}-(-1)^n}{3}2^{2^{n-1}-2n+2}\), with small exceptions \(N_2(2)=0\) and \(N_2(3)=2\); a three-by-three directed-tree Green determinant explains the parity correction.
