For two stopped walks estimating a single-edge graph's Green diagonal, global inverse-CDF antithetic lengths are exactly variance-optimal, whereas stepwise antithetic termination is strictly suboptimal at every stopping probability below \(1/2\). A second sharp boundary at \(1-1/\sqrt2\) permits complete elimination of paired revisits, and in the rare-stopping limit the optimal-to-stepwise variance ratio is \(2-\pi^2/6\approx0.3551\).
