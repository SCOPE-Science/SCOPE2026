For the planar rotation Lyapunov equation with a rank-one noise source, second-order integrating-factor SSPRK loses strict covariance positivity exactly at half-turn steps, while the third-order method loses it only at full-turn steps. Their smallest-eigenvalue ordering switches exactly at \(\cos(\omega h)=-1/2\), with an explicit all-step spectral formula showing when higher order restores covariance rank but need not improve conditioning.
