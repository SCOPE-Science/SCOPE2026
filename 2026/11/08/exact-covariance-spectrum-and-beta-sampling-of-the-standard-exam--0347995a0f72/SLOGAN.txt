The standard-example order polytope admits a two-beta-variable exact sampler, giving every interior-volume polynomial moment and an explicit four-branch covariance spectrum in every dimension \(2n\), \(n\ge2\). Distinct coordinates are strictly positively correlated for \(n\ge3\), nonmatching lower–upper pairs correlate more than matching pairs by an explicit positive rational gap, and the isotropic constant tends to the cube constant.
