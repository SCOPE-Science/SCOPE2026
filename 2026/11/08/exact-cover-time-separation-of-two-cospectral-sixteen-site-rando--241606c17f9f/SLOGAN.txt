Two sixteen-site, six-regular graphs with identical adjacency spectra have provably different exact mean random-walk cover times: the Shrikhande graph exceeds the \(4\times4\) rook graph by a positive rational number between \(0.00040429484\) and \(0.00040429485\) jumps. Their exact subset-hitting expansions first differ at four retained vertices, demonstrating a higher-order transport distinction invisible to the common strongly regular parameters.
