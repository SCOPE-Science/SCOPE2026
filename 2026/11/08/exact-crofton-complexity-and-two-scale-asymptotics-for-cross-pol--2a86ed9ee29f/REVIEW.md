# Mathematical scope and comparison — sharp Crofton tails

## Correctness

The finite-dimensional conditioned affine-Crofton face law and the mean formula are known from the October 18, 2026 published joint face-vector theorem and are used as inputs, not rediscovered. The proved remainder is the sharp quadratic-scale probability and the complete fixed-positive-moment asymptotics. The empirical half-normal quantile converges in L1 by a uniform second-moment bound; the moment argument has an integrable Gaussian-tail majorant for all s>0, including fractional exponents. Neither argument relies on finite numerical sampling for an infinite limit.

## Originality and limitation

The earlier Resultary joint face-vector theorem already supplies the same finite vertex-count law, expected value and beta logarithmic exponent. Those statements are explicitly credited and excluded from the new claim. Its inspected full RESULT.md does not state the specific inverse-error-function quadratic tail coefficient or the family of positive moment limits; the latter require a further quantile analysis and uniform domination. Search comparisons did not find a stronger source proving these refinements, but this is a best-of-knowledge judgment, not a priority certificate.

## Value

The precise tails and positive moment constants show that rare sections with quadratic-size vertex sets govern all annealed moments even as typical normalized complexity tends to zero. The constant for any threshold x in (0,1) is geometrically intrinsic to the natural conditioned Crofton ensemble. This refined two-scale description is useful independently of the already-known finite law and mean.

## Scope

Unit regular cross-polytopes, invariantly sampled translated codimension-one hyperplanes, fixed quadratic threshold x in (0,1), and fixed positive moment order s. No claim extends uniformly to endpoint thresholds or to changing s; unindexed earlier sharp-tail results remain a priority risk.
