Every closed face skeleton of a cross-polytope has an exact, all-parameter \(\ell_1\) Hausdorff deficit under Minkowski addition of a scaled cross-polytope: \(\max\{0,1-\lambda,2[1-(k+1)(1+\lambda)/n]\}\). The sharp convexification parameter is \(\max\{1,n/(k+1)-1\}\), with a geometric phase transition from uniform-coordinate boundary holes to a unique central hole when the skeleton crosses half the coordinate directions.
