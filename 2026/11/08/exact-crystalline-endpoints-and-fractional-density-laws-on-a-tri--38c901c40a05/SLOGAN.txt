On the two-row, one-diagonal triangular maximal hard-core strip, the exact minimum and maximum occupations are \(\lceil2n/5\rceil\) and \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\), with unique five- and three-periodic ground-state cycles. The pressure is analytic for every positive activity, but its low- and high-activity densities have sharp fractional-power corrections of orders \(z^{1/5}\) and \(z^{-1/3}\), respectively.
