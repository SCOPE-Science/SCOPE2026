Circular words over an ordered \(q\)-letter alphabet avoiding every cyclic nondecreasing triple have exact all-length generating function \(-z\Delta_q'(z)/\Delta_q(z)\), where \(\Delta_q(z)=1-\sum_{j=2}^q c_{j-2}\binom qj z^j\) and \(c_j\) repeats \((1,1,0,-1,-1,0)\). Möbius and Burnside inversion give all primitive and unrestricted necklace counts, while admissible cycles exist at all lengths at least two for \(q\ge3\).
