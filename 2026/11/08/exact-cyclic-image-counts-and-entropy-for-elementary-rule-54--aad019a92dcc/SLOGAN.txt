The number of indexed cyclic binary outputs reachable in one step under elementary cellular automaton Rule 54 has an exact all-length rational generating function with denominator \((1+z^2)(1-2z+z^3-z^4)\). Its leading growth is precisely \(\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda=(1+\sqrt{3+2\sqrt5})/2\), so the reachable fraction decays like \((\lambda/2)^n\).
