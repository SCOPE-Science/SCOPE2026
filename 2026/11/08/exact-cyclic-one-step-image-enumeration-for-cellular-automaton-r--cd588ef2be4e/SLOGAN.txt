Rule 110 has an exact rational generating function counting distinct one-step outputs on every indexed binary cyclic ring. Its image count grows as \(\rho^{-n}+O((3/2)^n)\), where \(\rho\) is the positive root of \(1-2z+z^2-z^3-z^4\), giving spatial image entropy \(\log_2(1/\rho)\approx0.9238561093\) bits per site.
