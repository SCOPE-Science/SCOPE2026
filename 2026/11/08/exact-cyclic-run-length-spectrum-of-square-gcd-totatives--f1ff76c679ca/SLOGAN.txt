For every modulus \(n\ge2\), the counts of all cyclic blocks of consecutive residues whose gcd with \(n\) is a square factor exactly over prime powers and obey an explicit two-piece local formula. This determines every maximal-run multiplicity and gives the sharp longest-run length \(\min_{p^a\parallel n}(p-1\text{ if }a=1,\;2p-1\text{ if }a\ge2)\).
