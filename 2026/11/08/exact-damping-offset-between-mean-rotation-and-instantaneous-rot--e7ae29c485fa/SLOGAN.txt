For a stable two-temperature inertial Brownian gyrator, the unique damping that maximizes the stationary single-snapshot angular-momentum signal-to-noise ratio is strictly larger than the damping that maximizes mean rotation. Their ratio is exactly \(\left[(T_h+T_c)^2/(4T_hT_c+(u/k)^2(T_h-T_c)^2)\right]^{1/4}\), always between \(1\) and \(\sqrt{k/u}\).
