On every unweighted Dutch windmill \(D_{\ell}^{(m)}\) with \(m\ge2\) cycles of length \(\ell\ge3\), the exact minimum dedicated Laplacian-control budget is \(m\): one hub neighbor from each cycle suffices and every optimum uses one vertex in every cycle but not the hub. Every minimum control placement is stalled by zero forcing, whose previously known budget is \(m+1\).
