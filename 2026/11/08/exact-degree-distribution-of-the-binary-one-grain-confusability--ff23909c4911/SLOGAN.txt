For the binary one-grain overwrite channel, the complete all-length confusability-graph degree spectrum is an explicit binomial run-composition polynomial, refining the familiar output-ball size to the actual number of competing source words. The maximum degree is \(n-2+\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\) for \(n\ge3\), achieved only by the specified monotone binary derivatives, and the graph has exactly \(2^{n-3}(n-1)(n+2)\) edges for \(n\ge2\).
