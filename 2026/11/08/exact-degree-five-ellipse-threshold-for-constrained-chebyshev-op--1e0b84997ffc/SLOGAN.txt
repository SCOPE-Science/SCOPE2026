The degree-five ellipse Chebyshev threshold previously tabulated numerically as \(r^*(5)\approx2.6492\) is exactly \(r_*=\sqrt{1+v_*}+\sqrt{v_*}\), where \(v_*\) is the unique positive root of an explicit quartic over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\). At and below this threshold the normalized Chebyshev polynomial is optimal for every real exterior normalization point, while above it the polynomial fails for all points sufficiently close to the ellipse.
