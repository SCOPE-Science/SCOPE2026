# Scientific review: exact affine correlations for twisted baker maps

## Correctness — PASS
The original-time map and normalized volume are explicitly fixed. Each inverse branch has Jacobian modulus \(1/2\), yielding an exact transfer action on centered affine coordinates and the rational resolvent. The first negative covariance follows from the unique shortest signed cycle of length \(M\); a Jensen equality argument excludes unit-circle transfer eigenvalues and justifies absolute convergence. The piecewise quantity \(d=f+H-H\circ F\) has \(Pd=0\), uniform \([-1,1]\) distribution, and bounded conditional variance, so a reverse martingale CLT proves the exact long-run variance \(1/3\). The checker validates finite rational cases but does not substitute for these arguments.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

A full-text read of their article finds no Euclidean-coordinate covariance transform or exact variance. The closest published same-map research record classifies derivative eigenvalues at iterate-fixed points; its statement cannot imply the transfer-matrix covariance formula. Accessible historical claims for this map concern periodic orbit derivatives and do not match the volume-sampled observable. Classical martingale CLTs are explicitly credited. ## Mathematical value — PASS
The first negative lag is exactly the ambient dimension, yet the integrated positive-lag covariance vanishes and the asymptotic orbit-sum variance stays constant for every dimension. Both the delayed sign reversal and cancellation are quantitative dynamical phenomena not determined by qualitative Bernoulli mixing. The explicit rational law also gives exact finite-time covariances and their sharp exponential rate on the affine observable subspace.
