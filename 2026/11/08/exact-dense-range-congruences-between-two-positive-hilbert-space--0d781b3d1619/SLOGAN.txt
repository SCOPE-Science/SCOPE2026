The two positive frame operators \(P=I\oplus\operatorname{diag}(1/n)\) and \(Q=\bigoplus_{j\ge0}2^{-j}I_{\ell^2}\) admit exact pullbacks in both directions by injective dense-range contractions, although no invertible comparison is possible. The explicit diagonal-permutation construction answers the dense-range question for this pair and proves that dense-range and invertible comparison differ even for nonclosed-range Bessel families.
