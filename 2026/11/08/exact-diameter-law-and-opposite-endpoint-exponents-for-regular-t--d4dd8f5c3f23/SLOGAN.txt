For the Haar-random planar shadow of a regular tetrahedron, the Euclidean diameter has an exact one-dimensional distribution formula and the sharp range \([\sqrt{12/5},\sqrt{8/3}]\) in unit-circumradius normalization. Its lower and upper endpoint probabilities scale respectively as \(\frac{225\sqrt5}{4\pi}\varepsilon^2\) and \(3\sqrt2\,6^{1/4}\sqrt\varepsilon\), with the smallest shadows occurring in twelve precisely classified unoriented directions.
