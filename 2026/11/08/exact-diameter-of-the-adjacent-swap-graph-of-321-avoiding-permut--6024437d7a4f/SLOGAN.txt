The graph of all \(321\)-avoiding permutations, joined only by avoidance-preserving adjacent swaps, has sharp diameter \((n^2-1)/2\) for odd \(n\) and \(n^2/2-1\) for even \(n\). A forced double crossing of large and small letters proves sharpness; at every odd order at least three exactly one unordered pair attains the diameter.
