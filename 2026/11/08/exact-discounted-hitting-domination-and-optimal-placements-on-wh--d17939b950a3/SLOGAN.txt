For discounted first-hitting support on every wheel \(W_{n+1}=K_1\vee C_n\), the worst-vertex value of any source set is an explicit function of its cyclic rim gaps and whether it contains the hub; among hub-free placements of fixed size, the unique optimal gap profile is as balanced as possible, while with the hub every gap profile with minimal maximum gap is optimal. The resulting exact minimum-source formula works for all \(n\ge4\), \(0<\lambda<1\), and \(0<\tau\le1\), and a five-vertex wheel has a sharp threshold at which both optimum sets exclude the hub.
