## correctness

PASS

For m=n−1, the size-d root set is the sign group modulo simultaneous reversal. In the discriminant, unordered root pairs with fixed sign-class difference form a matching of d/2 pairs. The difference determines an unordered nontrivial bipartition S,T; local sign classes on S and T independently parametrize the matching. The squared difference is 4(1−r_S²)(1−r_T²), e.g. by expanding Laurent cosines. Each local S-class occurs 2^(|T|−1) times, and the scalar is 4^(d(d−1)/2). The singleton product is 1−x_i²; pairing opposite remaining phases for a fixed coordinate gives (x_i²−r²)², hence (x_i²−x_j²)² for a pair and (Γ_S Γ_S^−)² for larger S by the monic root formula. Only proper subsets contribute. This checks the exact scalar and multiplicities, including 4 and no pair component at n=3, and 4096 at n=4. Lower-cycle factors are irreducible by the transitive sign action on their distinct Laurent roots; each uses all of its variables, and freezing the others at 1 distinguishes the two odd-sign classes by (X−1)^d_S and (X+1)^d_S. Parity of linear-factor valuations gives the asserted square criterion. The radicals x_i²−1 are independent by valuations at x_i=1; the root stabilizer is precisely simultaneous inversion by independence of Laurent monomials. Taking the torus ring invariants by simultaneous inversion yields a finite normal ring in the monogenic model's fraction field and thus its normalization. The image of each one-coordinate inertia subgroup has order two in the quotient since m≥2; off those hyperplanes the multiquadratic cover and its quotient are étale.
