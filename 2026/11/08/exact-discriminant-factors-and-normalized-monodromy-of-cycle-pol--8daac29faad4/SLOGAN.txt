The monic \(n\)-cycle polynomial has an exact all-dimensional discriminant factoring into coordinate and pair hyperplanes together with signed lower-cycle polynomials, with explicit multiplicities. Its normalized coordinate projection is a regular \((\mathbb Z/2)^{n-2}\)-Galois cover ramified in codimension one only at \(x_i=\pm1\), even though the discriminant has many additional collision components.
