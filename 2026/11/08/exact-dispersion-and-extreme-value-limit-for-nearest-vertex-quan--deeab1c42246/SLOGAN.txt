For uniform points in a regular simplex, the squared distance to the nearest vertex has an exact harmonic-number variance in every dimension; the key mixed-moment calculation is a strictly positive covariance between the largest barycentric weight and the sum of squared weights. After the precise high-dimensional centering, the quantization error has a reflected-and-scaled Gumbel limit, while the two controlling barycentric statistics become asymptotically uncorrelated.
