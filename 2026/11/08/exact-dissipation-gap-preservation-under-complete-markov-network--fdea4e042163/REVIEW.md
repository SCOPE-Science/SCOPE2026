## Originality — PASS, with qualification

Ray and Boyd, arXiv:2507.10476, prove a three-state disagreement and describe near-minimality in large randomly sampled CTMCs; their full accessible presentation does not state an arbitrary-size complete-network construction preserving the **minimum over all distributions**, nor an all-distributions conditional-KL equality. Teza and Stella, arXiv:2003.08674, preserve the stationary EPR and its fluctuations under decimation of particular networks but do not address exact preservation of the global minimum in a fully connected refinement. Puglisi et al., arXiv:1002.4520, study coarse-graining and loop currents, not this optimization landscape. A relevant published mathematical-finding index and accessible earlier claims were searched for state splitting, MEPS, lumpability, and complete-network EPR gaps without finding a matching stronger statement. The general coarse-graining EPR decomposition is classical (Esposito 2012), and the construction retains a block-lumpable structure; priority may exist under another naming convention. The originality claim is limited to the joint exact refinement/decomposition/dissipation-gap theorem, not its ingredients or general failure of universal MINEP.

## Value — PASS

The finite three-state counterexample of Ray and Boyd by itself leaves open whether a growing and **complete** state network can maintain a nonvanishing scaled NESS/MEPS discrepancy. The theorem gives a constructive boundary even with nonuniform microscopic stationary weights and a derived all-distributions information-theoretic decomposition. Esposito (arXiv:1112.5410, Sec. IV.2) already gives a general positive decomposition of coarse-grained entropy production; the mathematical novelty is not the general KL decomposition but the complete-network exact MEPS-preserving refinement and quantitative growing-size boundary. It isolates the missing notion of effective independent mesoscopic complexity: high vertex count, full connectivity, and order-one jump exit rates do not suffice. This addresses an explicit motivated interpretation issue raised in their large-network investigation, without claiming to refute its random-sample trend.
