Complete finite-state Markov networks of arbitrarily large size can retain a fixed positive gap between stationary and minimum entropy production: a fully connected destination-weighted clone refinement (including nonuniform microscopic stationary occupations) preserves both values exactly, with an explicit gap exceeding \(9/50\) of the minimum. An exact two-sided relative-entropy identity quantifies every microscopic deviation from block-uniform occupation; the construction does not challenge random-ensemble typicality claims.
