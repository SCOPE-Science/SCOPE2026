For an asymmetric Brownian reset ratchet, the exact long-time diffusivity contains a strictly negative drift–clock covariance correction absent in the symmetric nearest-site model. Every fixed asymmetry produces the sharp weak-resetting enhancement \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}/D=1+[1/24+(\delta-1/2)^2/2]rL^2/D+O(r^2)\), followed by suppression at high resetting rates.
