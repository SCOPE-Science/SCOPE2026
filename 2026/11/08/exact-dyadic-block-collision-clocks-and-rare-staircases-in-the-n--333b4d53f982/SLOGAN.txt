In the polygonal necktie symplectic billiard, the collision-iteration clock for \(2^k\) consecutive first returns has the exact law \(8\cdot2^k-4+4G\), where \(\Pr(G=j)=2^{-(j+1)}\); its variance is always \(32\), however large the block. Deep staircase visits occur exactly once per complete dyadic block of the appropriate length, with a non-Poisson finite-window count law.
