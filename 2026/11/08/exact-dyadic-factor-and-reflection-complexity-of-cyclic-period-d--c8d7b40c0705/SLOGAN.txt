The cyclic \(m\)-letter period-doubling word has exactly \((2m-1)2^{k-1}\) distinct factors of length \(2^k\), with an explicit classification of every collision among their arithmetic templates. For \(k\ge2\) none of these factors is palindromic and exactly half as many reversal classes remain; the new full-modulus enumeration concerns \(m\ge3\), while \(m=2\) is a known special case.
