Polygonal numbers on the first \(2^k\) indices have a complete dyadic fiber trichotomy: orders congruent to two modulo four permute the residues, orders divisible by four have the translated-square spectrum, and odd orders have precisely \(d\) double fibers and \(d\) holes, where \(d=\min(r-1,2^k+1-r)\) and \(r\equiv(s-2)^{-1}\pmod{2^k}\). Every odd-order collision is an explicit reflection pair, yielding exact permutation and twofold-cover criteria.
