The ones in the Baum–Sweet word have an exact, sparse spectrum of consecutive-position gaps: only \(1\), \(2\), \(2^{2j+1}+1\), and \(2^{2j+2}+2\) occur. Every binary-length layer has an explicit Fibonacci multiplicity for each gap type, and the dyadic empirical gap distribution converges to a geometric law with mass \(\varphi^{-1}\) at gap \(3\).
