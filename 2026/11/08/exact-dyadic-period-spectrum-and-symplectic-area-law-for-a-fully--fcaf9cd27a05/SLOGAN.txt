For the fully periodic square–rhombus symplectic billiard, every regular phase point has least period \(8(m+n+\varepsilon)+4\), with dyadic edge depths \(m,n\) and an edge-parity bit \(\varepsilon\), so the exact period spectrum is \(4,12,20,\ldots\). Under normalized invariant symplectic area, \(\Pr(P=8k+4)=(2k+1)/(3\,2^{k+1})\), giving an exact geometric-polynomial period tail and mean \(68/3\).
