For rank-one matrix projectors over \(\mathbb Z/2^k\mathbb Z\), the twofold additive convolution misses exactly \((8^{k-1}-1)/7\) trace-two matrices, characterized by a primitive dyadic scalar obstruction. Its sumset fills an asymptotic fraction \(55/56\) of the trace-two hyperplane, and every attained target has at least \(2^{k+1}\) representations, sharply.
