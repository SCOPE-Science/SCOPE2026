The fixed column \(\binom{n}{3}\) is surjective modulo every power of two but is never equidistributed: in its minimal period, every multiple-of-four residue occurs exactly five times and every other residue once. Its exact two-adic pushforward density is \(\tfrac12+2\mathbf 1_{4\mathbb Z_2}\), the first nonuniform example among the lower binomial columns one, two, and three.
