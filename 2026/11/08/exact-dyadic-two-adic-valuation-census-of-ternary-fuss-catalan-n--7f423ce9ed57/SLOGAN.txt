The exact two-adic valuation histogram of the ternary Fuss–Catalan numbers over every dyadic index block has the rational generating function \(\sum_{r\ge0}P_r(t)z^r=(1+z)(1-tz)/(1-(1+t)z-(t^2-t+1)z^2+(t^2+t)z^3)\). Every positive valuation is at least two, the valuations in a block of length \(2^r\) are exactly \(0,2,\ldots,r\) for \(r\ge2\), and precisely \(F_{r+2}\) entries are odd.
