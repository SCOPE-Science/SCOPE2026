Deleting \(k\) edges from a clique all at one vertex gives a polar symmetric edge polytope with exact Ehrhart polynomial \((m+1)^n-m^n+2((m+1)^k-m^k)\sum_{s=1}^{m}s^{n-k-1}\) and intrinsic volume \((n+2k/(n-k))/\sqrt n\). Whenever an equal-sized deleted matching exists and \(k\ge2\), concentrating those deletions at one vertex strictly increases the polar lattice-point count at every positive dilation and strictly increases its volume.
