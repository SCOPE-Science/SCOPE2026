# Exact eight-secant and exterior-hole geometry of the rational BKS octic over \(\mathbb F_{343}\)

## Finding

Let \(k=\mathbb F_{7^3}\). Let \(C\subset\mathbb P^2_k\) be the degree-eight rational BKS curve
\[
Y^8-X^7Z-XZ^7+(Y^2-2XZ)^4=0,
\]
and let \(\Omega=C(k)\), counting distinct projective points, including its singular points. Then \(|\Omega|=365\), and the number \(L_i\) of \(k\)-rational lines meeting \(\Omega\) in precisely \(i\) distinct points is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrr}
i&0&1&2&3&4&5&8\\\hline
L_i&48048&20000&46032&3528&21&28&336.
\end{array}
\]
For all other \(i\), \(L_i=0\), including \(i=6,7\).

For \(P\in\mathbb P^2(k)\), let \(s(P)\) be the number of the 336 eight-secants through \(P\). The entire point-concurrence distribution is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
s&0&1&2&8\\\hline
\#\{P:s(P)=s\}&51633&19152&46872&336.
\end{array}
\]
The 29 points of \(\Omega\cap\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_7)\) all have \(s(P)=0\), and the other 336 points of \(\Omega\) have \(s(P)=8\). Thus precisely **51604 exterior points** lie on no eight-secant, so \(\Omega\) is an incomplete \((365,8)\)-arc in \(\operatorname{PG}(2,343)\). The 336 eight-secants have 336 eightfold intersection points on \(\Omega\), 46872 double intersections off \(\Omega\), and no exterior triple concurrence.

Equivalently, the projective linear code obtained by taking the 365 curve-point homogeneous coordinates as columns of a \(3\times365\) generator matrix has parameters \([365,3,357]_{343}\), and its Hamming-weight enumerator is \(1+342\sum_i L_i z^{365-i}\), with the \(L_i\) above. This coding restatement is an immediate consequence, not a separate novelty claim.

## Assumptions and scope

All arithmetic is in the fixed finite field of 343 elements of characteristic seven. Rational line means a line over \(k\), and intersection size is the cardinality of the set of distinct \(k\)-points, **not** the scheme-theoretic intersection multiplicity. The polynomial is the \(\lambda=-1\) member of the \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_7)\)-invariant pencil of Borges–Korchmáros–Speziali (2022); geometrically it is a rational nodal octic. The theorem is a finite exact classification for \(q=7\), extension exponent three, not a result for every odd prime power or every exponent.

## Proof

**Finite-field models.** Since the cubes in \(\mathbb F_7\) are \(0,1,-1\), \(u^3+2\) has no root, hence is irreducible. Thus \(k\simeq\mathbb F_7[u]/(u^3+2)\). Independently, \(v^3+v+1\) has no root in \(\mathbb F_7\) (direct substitution of the seven residues), so \(k\simeq\mathbb F_7[v]/(v^3+v+1)\) is a second valid presentation. In each representation arithmetic is encoded in the coefficient basis \(1,u,u^2\) or \(1,v,v^2\), with all operations reduced modulo seven and the cubic relation.

**Point enumeration.** In the affine chart \(Z=1\) the equation is
\[
f(x,y)=y^8-(x^7+x)+(y^2-2x)^4=0.
\]
The homogenized equation has at infinity \(2Y^8=0\), so the unique point at infinity is \([1:0:0]\). The script `verify_direct.py` tests the defining equality for every one of the \(343^2=117649\) ordered affine pairs, and appends that unique infinite point. It obtains exactly 365 different curve points. This computation does not assume the parametrization. For an independent enumeration, the source parametrization
\[
P(t)=\bigl(2(t+1)^8:2+t+t^7:1\bigr),\qquad t\in k,
\]
with its point \([1:0:0]\) at infinity, gives 344 points; adding the 21 affine \(\mathbb F_7\)-rational internal conic points \(y^2-2x\in\{3,5,6\}\) gives exactly the same total of 365 in `verify_pairlines.py`. The field-subfield intersection consists of 29 points (the eight conic points and 21 nodes).

**Complete line enumeration.** Every projective line over \(k\) is uniquely normalized as one of
\[
X+bY+cZ=0\ (b,c\in k),\qquad Y+cZ=0\ (c\in k),\qquad Z=0.
\]
These are \(343^2+343+1=117993\) lines, with no duplicates or omissions. In `verify_direct.py` each line is evaluated against all 365 directly computed points using exact finite-field multiplication and addition. Counting zero evaluations yields precisely the displayed seven nonzero \(L_i\) values. In particular the maximum line intersection is eight and is attained by 336 lines.

**Independent pair-line reconstruction.** In `verify_pairlines.py`, all \(\binom{365}{2}=66430\) unordered pairs of distinct curve points are formed using a different irreducible cubic. Their projective cross products, normalized to a common scalar convention, determine each line meeting at least two points. A line with \(r\) curve points is generated by exactly \(\binom r2\) unordered pairs, so the number of pairs identifying a line determines \(r\) uniquely. The program obtains 49945 distinct lines meeting at least two points, with the same counts for \(r\ge2\). The counts for zero and one follow from the projective identities
\[
\sum_i L_i=117993,\qquad
\sum_i iL_i=365\cdot344=125560,\qquad
\sum_i\binom i2L_i=\binom{365}{2}=66430.
\]
All are asserted in the program. This method does not rely on the direct complete line scan.

**All point-concurrences.** Each eight-secant meets exactly 344 projective points. The scripts generate these points explicitly by solving its linear equation (and accounting for the one point at infinity), accumulating the eight-secant multiplicity at every projective point. The resulting counts are \(51633,19152,46872,336\) at multiplicities \(0,1,2,8\). They satisfy the independent checks
\[
\sum_Ps(P)=336\cdot344=115584,\qquad
\sum_P\binom{s(P)}2=\binom{336}{2}=56280.
\]
A separate membership check finds 29 zero-multiplicity points on \(C(k)\), all defined over \(\mathbb F_7\), and 336 eightfold points on the curve. Hence \(51633-29=51604\) points are exterior and uncovered. As all off-curve points have \(s(P)\le2\), no exterior point lies on three eight-secants. Since \(C\) has degree eight and no line component (from the cited geometric construction), no rational line can contain more than eight distinct curve points, and the 51604 uncovered exterior points certify incompleteness.

The two separate finite-field representations, independent point-generation mechanisms, direct complete line scan versus pair-line counting, and agreement of all incidence identities constitute the exact finite proof. The code paths cited here are included completely in this package.

## Verification

Run, from the directory containing the scripts,

```sh
python3 verify_pairlines.py
python3 verify_direct.py
```

Both scripts require Python's standard library only. On the included implementations, the successful final lines are `PAIRLINE_VERIFY_OK` and `DIRECT_VERIFY_OK`. Each script contains assertions for every count and the relevant incidence identities; neither uses random sampling or a precomputed external table. The direct verifier scans all 117993 rational lines, while the pair verifier reconstructs them from 66430 point pairs plus line-incidence identities. Their field presentations are different. The calculation is exhaustive **only for \(\mathbb F_{343}\)**.

## Relationship to prior work

Borges, Korchmáros, and Speziali (first public February 11, 2022) construct the \(\operatorname{PGL}_2\)-invariant pencil and identify the rational nodal member and its singularities. Korchmáros, Nagy, and Szőnyi (first public February 20, 2023) study its secant characters and completeness through Galois-theoretic arguments. Their broad completeness theorem treats extension exponents at least five, and explicitly leaves the exponent-three BKS case open; their separate theorem about covering points **on the curve** in smaller exponents is prior work. The known geometric parametrization and the on-curve covering statement are not presented as independent discoveries here.

The contribution proved here is the **entire exact rational-line spectrum and all-projective-point eight-secant multiplicity spectrum** at the unaddressed \(q=7,r=3\) specialization. In particular, the 51604 exterior uncovered points, the exact nonmaximal secant counts, and the exterior concurrence classification are not obtained merely by substituting parameters into the cited general bounds. An earlier exact equivalent table in less indexed literature remains possible, so this is a self-review, not a priority certificate.

## Limitations

The 336 eight-secants and which curve points they cover can partly be inferred from earlier general point-on-curve arguments; the new substance is the complete exact distribution, especially off the curve. No claim is made about \(q\ne7\), finite extension degrees other than three, arbitrary curves of degree eight, automorphism classification of the entire line configuration, or scheme-theoretic intersections. No independent mathematical audit or historical-priority certification has been performed.
