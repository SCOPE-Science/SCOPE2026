On every two-row \(\mathsf{S5}\) grid with \(m\) columns, Boolean formulas in one variable and the sole bundle “some agent knows whether” have exactly \(4\), \(64\), or \(256\) equivalence classes as \(m=1\), \(m=2\), or \(m\ge3\), respectively. Three columns give the sharp stabilization threshold, and modal depth two is sufficient for all widths and necessary once there are two columns.
