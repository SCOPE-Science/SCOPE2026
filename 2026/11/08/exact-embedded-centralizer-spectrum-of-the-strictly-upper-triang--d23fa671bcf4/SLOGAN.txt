In the strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) algebra over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the element centralizers form exactly \(2q^3+q^2+3\) distinct embedded subalgebras, with a complete size-and-fiber distribution. A single exceptional codimension-two centralizer has \(q^2\) times the ordinary fiber multiplicity, and the same spectrum governs the associated unitriangular group.
