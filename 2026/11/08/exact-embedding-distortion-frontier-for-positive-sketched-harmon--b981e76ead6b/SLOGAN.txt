For every symmetric positive-definite matrix of condition number \(\kappa\), the sharp universal squared-norm sketch distortion protecting **all** sketched harmonic Ritz values is \(\varepsilon<2\sqrt\kappa/(\kappa+1)\). At the boundary the reduced Gram factor of a two-dimensional harmonic pencil can become singular, and above it a negative Ritz value appears, even with just one Krylov step.
