The smallest Euclidean enclosing sphere of the fractionally capped cube \(\{x\in[0,1]^n:\sum_i x_i\le\tau\}\) has an exact median-formula center and radius for every real \(0<\tau\le n\), together with a complete classification of contacting vertices. For every \(n\ge5\), along the one-integer cap family \(\tau=1+s\), Boolean-only contacts give way to a mixed-contact phase and then fractional-only contacts; the first phase has a constant circumradius despite strict growth of the polytope.
