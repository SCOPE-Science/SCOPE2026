# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The parity decomposition of the symmetric Lyapunov equation rigorously forces the mean endpoint lifetime to have only inverse and first powers of loss strength. Weak- and strong-loss perturbation identify both coefficients exactly by reflection symmetry and a bipartite zero-mode calculation. The packaged verifier reproduces 150 finite-dimensional Lyapunov checks and 56 independent spectral checks. Finite checks are not the all-size proof.

**Originality: PASS on inspected evidence, provisional.** The closest fully inspected SSH first-passage source, Menczel et al. (2026), uses two periodically coupled cells and a collective dissipator, not a single endpoint loss. Wang and Zhang (2026) study a closer single-loss SSH geometry, but the available abstract reports Liouvillian gaps rather than this initial-condition-dependent exact mean or its optimized rate; the full paper could not be checked. Exact general weighted-chain trapping identities, if previously published, could imply this formula. No such dominating theorem was found in the examined sources.

**Value: PASS.** A natural quantitative transport observable on the standard dimerized chain has an all-length closed form and a nontrivial loss-optimization law. The leading asymptotic switches from linear to exponential at the coupling crossover. Eigenvalue-gap results alone do not determine the initial-state-weighted mean lifetime or optimal absorber strength.

The conclusion is restricted to the stated one-particle finite open chain. Priority awaits independent historical review.
