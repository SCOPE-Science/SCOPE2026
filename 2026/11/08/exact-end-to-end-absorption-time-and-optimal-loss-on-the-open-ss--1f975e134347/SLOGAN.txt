The open \(2n\)-site SSH chain with absorption at one end and a particle initialized at the other has exact mean lifetime \(2n/\kappa+\kappa((n-1)/(2w^2)+w^{2n-2}/(4v^{2n}))\). Optimizing the absorber strength yields linear-time transport for \(w\le v\) but exponentially large mean capture time for \(w>v\).
