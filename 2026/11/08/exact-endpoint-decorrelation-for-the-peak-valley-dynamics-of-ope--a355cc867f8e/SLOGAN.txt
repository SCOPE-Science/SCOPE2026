For the peak/valley/free-terminal walk dynamics of open meandric systems, the stationary endpoint autocorrelation has an exact half-integer cosine-mode expansion, an exact integrated value \(m(2m^2+1)/3\), and a universal nontrivial profile on the \(m^3\) time scale. This determines the endpoint-decorrelation exponent as \(3\), while leaving the crossing-event exponent open.
