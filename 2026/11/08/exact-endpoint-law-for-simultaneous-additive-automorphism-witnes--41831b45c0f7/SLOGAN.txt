For a finite abelian \(p\)-group, the chance that \(p-1\) independent uniform automorphisms share an additive automorphism witness is exactly \(\bigl(1-(p-1)!/(p-1)^{p-1}\bigr)^r\), where \(r\) counts cyclic-exponent blocks of multiplicity one. A universal linear witness exists at this sharp endpoint precisely when \(r=0\); the statement does not decide existence of arbitrary permutation witnesses.
