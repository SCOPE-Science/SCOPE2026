The full second Fourier level of \(S_n\), containing both Specht blocks \((n-2,2)\) and \((n-2,1,1)\), has a sharp \(\ell^1/\ell^\infty\) projection norm given by one truncated exponential plus a factorial correction. Its degree-normalized norm tends to \(3/(2e)\), because an exact cancellation makes the degree-weighted character negative precisely on permutations with one or two fixed points.
