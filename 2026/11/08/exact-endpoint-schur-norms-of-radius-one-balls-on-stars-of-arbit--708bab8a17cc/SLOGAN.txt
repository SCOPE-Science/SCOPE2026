For the radius-one distance mask on the star with \(n\) leaves, the exact operator and completely bounded Schur projection norms are \((5n-1)/(3n+1)\), increasing to \(5/3\) for the countably infinite star. The previously known two-leaf value \(9/7\) is recovered; the arbitrary-degree formula and infinite endpoint follow from matching positive-kernel decompositions and trace-norm witnesses.
