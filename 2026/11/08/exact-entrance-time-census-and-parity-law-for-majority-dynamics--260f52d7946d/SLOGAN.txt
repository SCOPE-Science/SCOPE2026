For strict synchronous binary majority on the triangular book with \(n\) pages, the exact first-periodic-entry census has no entries beyond time two. The full two-cycle basin has size \(2^n+inom{n}{n/2}\) for even \(n\), but \(2^n-2inom{n}{(n-1)/2}\) for odd \(n\ge3\); both represent asymptotically one quarter of all initial states, even though immediate two-cycles have sharply different parity laws.
