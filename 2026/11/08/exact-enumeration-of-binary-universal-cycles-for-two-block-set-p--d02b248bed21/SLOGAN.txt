Binary universal cycles for all nontrivial two-block partitions of an \(n\)-set are in bijection, modulo rotation and global bit complementation, with binary de Bruijn cycles of order \(n-1\). Their exact number is \(2^{2^{n-2}-n+1}\) for every \(n\ge3\), through cyclic XOR differences and insertion of the missing all-zero window.
