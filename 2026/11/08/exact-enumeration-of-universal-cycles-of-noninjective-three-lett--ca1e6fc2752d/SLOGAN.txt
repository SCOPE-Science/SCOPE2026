For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\), the number of rotationally distinct universal cycles containing each noninjective three-letter word exactly once is \(2^{q-1}3^{(q-1)(q-2)/2}q^{q-2}((q-1)!)^q\). The exact product follows by contracting the off-diagonal states of a directed overlap-graph Laplacian and applying the BEST theorem; for three letters on three symbols there are exactly \(288\) such cycles.
