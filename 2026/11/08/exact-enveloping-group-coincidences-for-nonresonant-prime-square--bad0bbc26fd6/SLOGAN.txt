For every odd prime \(p\), the connected two-dimensional Alexander quandles whose multiplier has determinant different from one form exactly \(p^2-2p-1\) quandle-isomorphism classes but only \((p^2-2p-1)/2\) enveloping-group isomorphism classes. In contrast, equal inner automorphism groups need not determine equal enveloping groups, as an explicit order-\(25\) pair demonstrates.
