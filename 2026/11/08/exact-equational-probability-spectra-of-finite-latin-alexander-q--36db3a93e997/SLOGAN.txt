For every finite latin Alexander quandle \(x*y=Tx+(I-T)y\) over a prime field, its complete equational probability spectrum is exactly \(\{|rV|^{-1}:r\in\mathbb F_p[T]\}\), and every value has a two-variable term witness. The elementary divisors of \(T\) give a closed formula for all these probabilities, refining general 2026 finite-spectrum results for affine algebras.
