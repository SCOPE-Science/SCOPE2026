# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The closed-neighborhood fractions available on a degree-two ring are \(1/3\), \(2/3\), and \(1\). Symmetry about the half-peak makes the first two utilities equal and positive and the last zero. Consequently a strict mutually beneficial swap exists exactly when both colors have a segregated run interior. The equilibrium characterization follows. A marked-run composition argument proves the labeled-ring count; run-boundary contributions and explicit extremal arrangements prove the integration extrema. Direct pairwise-swap enumeration of 35,103 colorings across 55 population instances agrees with the general proof, count and welfare formulas. This is not presented as an infinite computational certification.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The directly relevant Bilò–Bilò–Lenzner–Molitor single-peaked swap paper (arXiv:2204.12599) proves a general necessary segregation condition, existence/convergence, and asymptotically tight ring price-of-anarchy bounds; it does not state the all-population exact census or exact finite-size welfare ratio. Its ring witness is one case subsumed by the formula and is credited. The earlier *Topological Influence and Locality in Swap Schelling Games* has an exact cycle price of anarchy for a different monotone payoff model and different welfare quantity. The *Single-Peaked Jump Schelling Games* paper concerns vacancy-based moves, not swaps. Targeted literature and mathematical-record searches revealed no equivalent theorem; absence from such searches does not prove worldwide priority.

## Value — PASS

The object is natural because the initiating paper explicitly studies ring segregation, swap equilibrium, the number of integrated players, and price of anarchy. Its asymptotic lower construction leaves the precise dependence on the minority population and exact equilibrium abundance undetermined. The current theorem fills both gaps with an explicit closed-form equilibrium census and the sharp finite-population welfare ratio, including exact parity corrections for all population sizes rather than a small arbitrary case.

## Limits and comparisons

The statement is specific to a fully occupied ring, two colors, symmetric peak \(1/2\), and mutually profitable nonlocal swaps; it is not a statement about monotone preferences or jump dynamics. General source lemmas are credited and retained as context, and an equivalent result in unindexed literature remains a residual risk. Independent external audit has not been performed.
