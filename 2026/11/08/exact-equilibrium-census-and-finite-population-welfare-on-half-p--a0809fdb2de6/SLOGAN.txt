For half-peaked single-peaked swap Schelling games on a fully occupied two-color ring, stability means that at least one color has no run longer than two. Every fixed population has an exact equilibrium count and a sharp integration price of anarchy \(\min\{b+r,3b\}/(b+\min\{r,\lceil b/2\rceil+1\})\), refining the previously known limiting bound of two.
