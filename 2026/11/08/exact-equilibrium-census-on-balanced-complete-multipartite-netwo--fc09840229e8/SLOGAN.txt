Binary anti-coordination on every balanced complete multipartite network has a complete arithmetic classification and an exact sum for the number of pure Nash configurations. On \(K_{n,n,n}\), odd \(n\) makes every equilibrium welfare-optimal, while even \(n\) adds \(\binom n{n/2}^3\) inefficient equilibria with price of anarchy \(4/3\); as even \(n\) grows, the efficient equilibria become exponentially rare among all equilibria.
