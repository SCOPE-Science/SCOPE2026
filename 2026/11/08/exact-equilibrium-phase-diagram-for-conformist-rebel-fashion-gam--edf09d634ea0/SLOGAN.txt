Binary conformist–rebel fashion games on wheels admit an exact four-case equilibrium classification: all-rebel rims give Perrin counts for rebel hubs and four-or-zero parity counts for conformist hubs, while replacing just one rim rebel by a conformist changes the counts to ordered two-/three-step compositions for rebel hubs and zero for conformist hubs. The theorem also classifies every strict equilibrium, exposing a sharp disappearance of all equilibria after one local type change.
