For the two-locus quadratic stochastic map with a uniformly sampled initial probability simplex, the selected equilibrium coordinate has an exact rational density depending only on the asymmetry \(\kappa=(a-b)^2/(ab)\). It interpolates from \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,2)\) to uniform selection while developing an explicit \(1/\kappa\) boundary layer with cumulative profile \(w^2/(1+w)\).
