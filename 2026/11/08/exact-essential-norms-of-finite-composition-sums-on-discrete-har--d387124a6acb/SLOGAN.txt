For finite sums of bounded composition operators on discrete tree Hardy spaces, the exact essential norm is a tail limit of finite-dimensional dual block-matrix norms; for a single symbol it reduces to an explicit levelwise fibre-count formula. Compactness is independent of the Hardy exponent in this setting, but the essential-norm value need not be: a natural cyclic-difference operator has norms \(2\) and \(\sqrt3\) on \(\mathbb T_1\) and \(\mathbb T_2\), respectively.
