In every even dimension, the top Laplacian polytope of a simplex boundary is intrinsically an orthogonal free sum of two congruent regular simplices. This gives its exact Santaló volume product, spherical John and Löwner ellipsoids, and scalar uniform-volume covariance; its Mahler-product ratio to an equal-dimensional simplex grows like \(\frac{e}{2\sqrt\pi}\sqrt{k}\).
