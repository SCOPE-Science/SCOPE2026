For every center and every positive radius, Hilbert balls in a Euclidean square have an exact Euclidean area given by a projected rectangle minus four explicit, disjoint corner triangles. The square center is the unique maximal-area center, with sharp deficit \(6t^2(1-t^2)\|c\|^2+O(\|c\|^3)\), where \(t=\tanh(r/2)\) for the unhalved cross-ratio metric.
