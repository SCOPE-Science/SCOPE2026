Every cyclic adjacent-block polytope with \(m\ge4\) equal blocks has squared Euclidean diameter \(2\lfloor m/2\rfloor\), and its diametral endpoints are exactly disjointly colored maximum independent sets of the underlying cycle. The exact number of unordered diameter pairs is \(a^{2r}+[a(a-1)]^r\) for \(m=2r\), and \((2r+1)a^{r+1}(a^r-(a-1)^r)+(2r+1)[a(a-1)]^r/2\) for \(m=2r+1\); every half-integral odd-cycle vertex is excluded.
