The standard Euclidean diameter of the preorder polytope for an ordinal sum of an \(m\)-element antichain below an \(n\)-element antichain is exactly \(\sqrt{2(m+1)^2+n-2}\) for all \(m\ge1\) and \(n\ge2\). Its complete set of unordered diametral pairs has \(\binom n2 2^{n-2}\) members: two distinct upper-coordinate peaks at height \(m+1\), with complementary zero–one backgrounds, independently of the lower-block size.
