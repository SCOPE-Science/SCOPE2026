Every standard-coordinate Pitman–Stanley polytope with positive increments has Euclidean diameter \(\sqrt{B_n^2+B_{n-1}^2}\), attained by exactly one pair of single-coordinate vertices, with its value depending only on the final two cumulative capacities. For unit increments, the next-largest distinct squared vertex distance is \(n^2+(n-2)^2+1\), with an exact gap of \(2(n-2)\) and at most two realizing pairs.
