For the cyclic Minkowski sum of edge-supported coordinate diamonds, the John and Löwner ellipsoids are exactly concentric Euclidean balls with squared radii \(\min\{n,4\}\) and \(n+2\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). The full contact classification exhibits an inball facet-orbit transition at \(n=4\) and a parity-dependent exact count of farthest vertices.
