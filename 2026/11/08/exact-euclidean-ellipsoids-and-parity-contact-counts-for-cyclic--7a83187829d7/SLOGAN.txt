The John ellipsoid of the unit cyclic edge-diamond Minkowski sum is the centered Euclidean ball with squared radius \(\min\{n,4\}\). Its tangent facets form exactly the \(2^n\) full-support sign orbit for \(n=3\), both that orbit and \(2n\) coordinate facets for \(n=4\), and only the \(2n\) coordinate facets for \(n\ge5\). Previously published outer-sphere and farthest-vertex formulas are expressly credited, not claimed as new.
