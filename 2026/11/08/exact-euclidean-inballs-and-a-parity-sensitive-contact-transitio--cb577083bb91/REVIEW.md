# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** For complete multipartite graphs with at least three parts, the proof derives the complete dichotomy of facet potentials from tight-edge spanning connectivity, computes projected squared normal lengths, and constructs maximizing potentials of both kinds. Central symmetry proves that the origin-centered ball is globally optimal. The contact-facet count, strict-largest-part selection, and parity-only coexistence follow from exact normal-norm calculations. The finite checker enumerates facet potentials and tests norms and facet counts on 67 complete multipartite graphs with at most nine vertices; computation is not substituted for the infinite proof.
