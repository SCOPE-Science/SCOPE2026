For every star graph, its standard PQ-type adjacency polytope has a unique Euclidean incenter and an explicit inradius \(r_m=\sqrt{m+1}/(m\sqrt m+\sqrt{m+2\lfloor m^2/4\rfloor})\). Exactly the leaf nonnegativity facets and the balanced-selection upper facets touch the maximal ball, giving a parity-sensitive count among exponentially many facets.
