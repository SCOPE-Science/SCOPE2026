For every \(n\ge2\), a generalized parking-function polytope with arbitrary positive integer parameters has a unique Euclidean inball of radius \(\min_{1\le k\le n}\bigl(\sum_{j=n-k+1}^n(S_j-1)\bigr)/(k+\sqrt k)\); the minimizing ranks are exactly the touching upper-facet orbits. Every rank \(1,\ldots,n\) occurs as the unique active upper rank for some positive integer parameter vector, in contrast to the two endpoint phases of the classical two-parameter subfamily.
