For every path cosmological polytope on \(n\ge4\) vertices, the exact intrinsic Euclidean inradius is \(1/[c_2+c_3+(n-3)c_4/2]\), where \(c_j=\sqrt{j(2n-1-j)/(2n-1)}\). Its entire set of optimal ball centers is an explicit \((n-4)\)-dimensional affine cube, so centers cease to be unique as soon as \(n=5\).
