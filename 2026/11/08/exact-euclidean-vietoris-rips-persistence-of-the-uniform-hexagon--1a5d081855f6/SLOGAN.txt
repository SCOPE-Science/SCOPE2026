The twelve Euclidean vertices of the unit-edge hexagonal antiprism have an exact Vietoris–Rips filtration passing from a persistent circle to \(\bigvee^3S^2\), then \(\bigvee^2S^3\), before becoming contractible. All five critical radii and all integral persistence maps are determined, including the nontrivial isomorphism across the first circle-to-circle transition.
