For real quadratic spherical harmonics on \(\mathbb S^2\), every normalized even-moment ratio is an explicit polynomial with strictly positive nonconstant coefficients in the squared spectral cubic invariant. The fourth moment is completely flat, but from the sixth moment onward the sharp minimum is attained exactly by the \((a,0,-a)\) eigenvalue orbit and the sharp maximum by the zonal \((2a,-a,-a)\) orbit, with exact constants at every even order.
