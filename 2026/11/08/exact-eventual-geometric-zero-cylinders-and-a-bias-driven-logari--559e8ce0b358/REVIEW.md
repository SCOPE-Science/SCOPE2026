# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The map branches and zero-digit convention were reconstructed from equations (11)--(12) in Dajani--Kalle--Maggioni. The stationary density plateau holds for matching parameters by Lemma 3.8; their Theorem 3.5 and the \(L^1\)-continuity established inside the proof of Lemma 3.11 extend the plateau to every \(1<\alpha<3/2\), and the source treats the remaining range. Restricting a length-\(n\) zero cylinder to \(|x|\leq2^{-n}\) puts it in this plateau at the asserted threshold. A rescaling and exact finite geometric sum prove the cylinder, normalization, threshold and endpoint formulas. The provided independent exact-rational partition test checks 423 instances. Quantifiers: all \(1<\alpha\leq2\), all \(0<p<1\), all integer \(n\geq\max\{1,\lceil\log_2(1/(\alpha-1))\rceil\}\). The balanced and biased asymptotic error bounds are for fixed \(p\), and only as \(\alpha\downarrow1\).

## Originality

**PASS on inspected evidence.** The closest originating paper proves one-digit frequency, density matching and continuity, but its complete Sections 3.1--3.4 and final remarks do not state the exact stationary all-zero cylinders, finite parameter-dependent cutoff or the balanced-versus-biased edge exponents and log-periodicity. The deterministic 2017 paper studies different objects, including finite zero blocks in a distinguished kneading sequence, not random stationary cylinder probabilities. The 2018 density paper supplies general invariant-density methods, which do not themselves state this claim. Semantic mathematical records and targeted literature searches did not surface a statement implying the combined result. Residual risk: the formula could be an unstated corollary or already derived in unindexed symbolic or word-statistics literature. Priority is not independently established.

## Value

**PASS.** The known invariant-density and single-zero-frequency theory leaves the lengths and dependence of clusters of zero digits unresolved. The exact geometric cutoff and its transition at \(\alpha=3/2\) classify a natural family of block observables, while the balanced logarithm versus biased power law and log-periodic amplitude reveal a nontrivial singular response to selection bias. These are useful structural descriptors for signed representations and random expanding dynamical systems, not an arbitrary parameter slice or a renamed frequency identity.

## Boundaries and external verification

The full zero-digit process is not proved to be independent or renewal; the geometric law is a conditional tail after a finite cutoff. The small-parameter asymptotics exclude simultaneous vanishing of the environmental bias. Matching and density-continuity results are imported with explicit attribution. Finite exact tests are not infinite proofs. Independent replication and publication-priority audit have not occurred.
