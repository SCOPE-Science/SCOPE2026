For the three-state Greenberg–Hastings map on the truncated tetrahedral graph, the only primitive periods are \(1,3,4,6,8\), and every configuration reaches its cycle within ten steps. Each of the nontrivial long-period classes \(4\), \(6\), and \(8\) forms a single tetrahedral-symmetry orbit; the four- and eight-cycles share excitation rate \(1/4\) per vertex despite having different least periods.
