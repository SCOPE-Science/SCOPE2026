For three-state excitation on a graph made of several internally disjoint paths between two vertices, the entire finite-size distribution of long-run activity is an explicit residue-restricted product of trinomial coefficients, and the two classical update rules have activity rates in an exact ratio of three. As the path lengths grow proportionally, activity has a Gaussian-range limit while the probability of eventual synchronization decays with the sharp exponent \((r-1)/2\), where \(r\) is the number of parallel paths.
