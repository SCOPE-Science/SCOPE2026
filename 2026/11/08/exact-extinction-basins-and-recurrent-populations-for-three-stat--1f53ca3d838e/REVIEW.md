# Scientific review of the extinction-basin theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The exact synchronizing-versus-oscillating dichotomy is supplied by the established graph-circulation theorem of Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff (2018, Theorem 3). For a complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, chordless four-cycles decompose as integer sums of triangles using a third part, hence all edge-circulations vanish exactly in the absence of a rainbow triangle. Under this GHM rule synchronization reaches all zero. Hall's marriage theorem shows that if all three colors occur and a rainbow triangle does not, two colors must appear in a single matched pair while all other pairs bear the third color. These disjoint exceptions number 6r; inclusion–exclusion adds 3·4^r−3 colorings missing at least one color. The result follows for every r≥4 without extrapolation. Fresh direct enumeration gives extinction counts 207, 789, 3099 for r=3,4,5 (the r=3 boundary is not claimed as new).

## Originality — PASS for the corrected claim, with explicit exclusions
The previously published November 2 theorem *Three-state excitation on cocktail-party graphs: a sharp period transition and exact orbit count* already establishes the primitive period-three structure and the exact periodic-point and temporal-orbit counts for the very same family. The original broad claim cannot count those as new. The corrected theorem limits its contribution to the extinction basin, its 6r Hall-obstruction correction, and complementary basin probability, including transient configurations. The prior temporal-census paper expressly does not compute exact basin sizes. An October 26 paper gives a complete extinction-time histogram for three-partite graphs, not arbitrary r≥4 matched pairs. The general circulation theorem implies qualitative extinction criteria but does not count the labelled family-specific colorings. These full-statement comparisons replace an erroneous blanket originality assertion. Remaining historical risk: an unindexed earlier exact Hall count for cocktail-party colorings.

## Value — PASS
The cocktail-party graphs are a canonical, vertex-transitive, unbounded graph family. The extinction probability is a natural global dynamical reliability observable, distinct from the previously counted periodic configurations; it cannot be determined from those counts without the distribution of transient basins. The exact small 6r correction distinguishes three-color obstructions from simple missing-color events and yields a sharp exponentially small extinction asymptotic. It is an independently useful exact family classification, not a cosmetic reformulation of the prior temporal-cycle count.

## Scope and integrity

No independent claims of new primitive periods, immediate-cycle numbers, complete entry-time histograms, stronger graph generality, or full historical priority. Finite programs corroborate but do not prove the all-r argument. The mathematical change narrows and corrects the previous description; original evidence remains available in repository history.
