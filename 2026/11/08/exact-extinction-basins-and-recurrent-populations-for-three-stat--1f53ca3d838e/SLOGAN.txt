For three-state excitation on the complement of a perfect matching with \(r\ge4\) matched pairs, exactly \(3\cdot4^r-3+6r\) initial colourings go extinct, while every other colouring reaches a primitive three-cycle. There are exactly \((9^r-3\cdot4^r+3-6r(4^{r-1}-1))/3\) such labelled temporal cycles; the formulas arise from a rainbow-triangle matching obstruction and a disjoint singleton-mate obstruction.
