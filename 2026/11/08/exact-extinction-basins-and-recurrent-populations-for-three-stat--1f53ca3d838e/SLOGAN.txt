For three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on the cocktail-party graph with r≥4 matched pairs, the exact number of labelled initial colorings that eventually go extinct is 3·4^r−3+6r. The additional 6r are precisely the three-color Hall obstructions concentrated in one matched pair; thus the extinction probability is 3(4/9)^r+(6r−3)/9^r. Primitive-period and immediate-cycle counts belong to earlier published results, not to this claim.
