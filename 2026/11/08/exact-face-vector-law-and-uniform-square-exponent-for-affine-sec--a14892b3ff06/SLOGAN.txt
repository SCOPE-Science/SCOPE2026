For a regular simplex, the entire face-vector distribution of an invariantly sampled intersecting affine hyperplane section is given exactly by Gaussian order-statistic gaps and products of smaller simplices. Although the mean number of section vertices is of order \(n^2/\sqrt{\log n}\), its typical logarithmic exponent converges to \(2-U^2\), with \(U\) uniform on \([-1,1]\); the same random exponent shift governs every fixed face dimension.
