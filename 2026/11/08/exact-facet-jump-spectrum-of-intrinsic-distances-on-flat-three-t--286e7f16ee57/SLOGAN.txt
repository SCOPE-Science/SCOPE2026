For every Euclidean flat three-torus, each Voronoi facet contributes a negative jump of exactly \(2\pi r/V\) to the second derivative of the two-point geodesic-distance CDF at its half-lattice-vector length \(r\), and no edge or vertex event creates a false jump. The distance histogram therefore determines the entire multiset of Voronoi-relevant vector lengths, with multiplicities, even when critical radii coincide.
