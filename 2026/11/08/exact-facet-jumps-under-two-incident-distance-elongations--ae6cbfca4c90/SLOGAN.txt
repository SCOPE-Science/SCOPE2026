Elongating two *incident* distances in an otherwise equilateral \(N\)-point metric produces exactly \(7\cdot2^{N-2}-10\) facets of the real Lipschitz-free unit ball for every \(1<t<2\). The facet count jumps from \(2^N-2\) at \(t=1\) to that higher value, then drops to \(2^N-4\) at \(t=2\); all dual extreme potentials admit an explicit two-family classification.
