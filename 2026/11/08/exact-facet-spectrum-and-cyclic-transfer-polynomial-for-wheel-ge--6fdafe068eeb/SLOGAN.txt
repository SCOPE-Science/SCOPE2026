For wheels \(W_m=K_1\vee C_m\), all inclusion-maximal ordinary general-position sets have an exact cyclic-run classification and facet polynomial \(M_m(z)=mz^3-m[u^m]\log(1-zu^2-z^2u^3-z^2u^4+z^3u^6)\) for \(m\ge5\). Their attained cardinalities are exactly \(\{3\}\cup[\lceil m/2\rceil,\lfloor2m/3\rfloor]\cap\mathbb Z\), so only the complete four-vertex wheel and the five-rim wheel have pure general-position complexes.
