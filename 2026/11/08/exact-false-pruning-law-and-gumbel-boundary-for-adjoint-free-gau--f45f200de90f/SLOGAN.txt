For the adjoint-free Gaussian Frobenius range finder on a rank-\(b\) flat-spectrum matrix, the second-lookahead residual is exactly zero, yet final sketch-based pruning violates tolerance with an explicit chi-square probability. As \(b\) grows and the squared tolerance approaches one, the failure probability has a sharp threshold at gap \(2\sqrt{\log b/b}\) and a centered Gumbel limit.
