For every cycle size \(n\ge12\) divisible by six, the fastest symmetric walk allowing jumps of one, two or three edges has exactly equal holding, one-hop and two-hop probabilities, with an explicit different three-hop weight. Its optimal spectral gap is \(18\pi^2/n^2+O(n^{-4})\), a ninefold asymptotic improvement over the optimally tuned nearest-neighbor cycle.
