For every sunlet graph \(C_m\circ K_1\) with \(m\ge3\), the globally fastest symmetric stochastic walk has exact second-largest eigenvalue modulus \(2(z+1)/(z^2+2z+2)\), where \(z=\sqrt2\sin(\pi/m)\). Its core vertices never hold, and its optimal leaf-edge probability decays as \(\pi/(\sqrt2\,m)\); global optimality holds even against noninvariant edge weights.
