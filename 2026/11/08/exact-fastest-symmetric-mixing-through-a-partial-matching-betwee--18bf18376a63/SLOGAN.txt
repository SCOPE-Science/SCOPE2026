For two \(m\)-vertex cliques linked by a matching of \(k\) edges, \(2\le k\le m/2\), the globally fastest symmetric stochastic chain has exact second-largest eigenvalue modulus \(1-(6-4\sqrt2)k/[m+(2-2\sqrt2)k]\). An explicit optimal weighting leaves the gateway-to-gateway edges within each clique unused, while saturating each gateway's outbound probability.
