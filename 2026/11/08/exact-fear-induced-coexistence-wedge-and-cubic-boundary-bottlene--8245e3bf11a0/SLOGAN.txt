In a rational fear-modified two-competitor model, an explicit fold curve \(k_f(b)\) creates a sharp coexistence wedge of width \(2(b-3/2)^2+O((b-3/2)^3)\) against the boundary-invasion threshold \(k=1\). At the wedge endpoint the boundary center flow has a strictly positive cubic coefficient, while along the adjacent attracting critical boundary the disappearing competitor has an exact \(1/t\) relaxation constant, with a general positive-parameter formula.
