For length-\(n\) binary words with exactly four ones, every class under equality of the maximum-one-factor profile has an explicit cardinality \((r+1)H(p,q)\), yielding a complete class-size spectrum and a sharp largest class of size \(4(n-6)\) for \(n\ge10\). Two independent uniform four-one words share that one-sided prefix-normal profile with probability \(32n^{-3}+O(n^{-4})\); equality of full factor-Parikh sets is a different, stronger condition.
