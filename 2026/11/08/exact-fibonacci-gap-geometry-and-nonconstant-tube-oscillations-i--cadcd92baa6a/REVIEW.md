# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

The claim fixes the original six-piece affine Lorenz map, the exact four survivor intervals, and Euclidean metric. Direct symbolic substitution proves the four core branch images with common expansion \(\beta=2\sqrt{2/3}\). The two initial outer gaps have the identical exact length \(\delta\), the center gap length \(\kappa\), and each of the two interior states creates one first-level inverse gap. The four-state transition matrix has inverse-gap count \(2F_{m+1}\) at depth \(m\). Strong expansion and disjointness exclude missing complementary gaps, and the total gap-length identity independently matches the convex hull. Summation gives the geometric zeta function. Exact gap clipping gives the tube identity and Binet's formula yields the explicit nonconstant periodic profile with error \(O(\varepsilon)\). A symbolic checker verifies the map and recurrence identities, and high-precision numerical checks test the tube limit. Standard separated graph-directed theory gives the dimension, but is not novel to this claim. The exact proof, not a finite experiment, supports the infinite result.

## Originality (PASS, qualified)

Cholewa and Oprocha, *On α-Limit Sets in Lorenz Maps*, Entropy 23 (2021) 1153, DOI 10.3390/e23091153, Sections 3–4, supply the Lorenz map, invariant Cantor set, symbolic coding, Fibonacci-type entropy and topological conclusions. Their complete open-access text was inspected for metric gap lengths, geometric zeta function and tube amplitude, which were not stated. Their related article, *Renormalization in Lorenz Maps—Completely Invariant Sets and Periodic Orbits*, arXiv:2104.00110, was inspected in full-text PDF (particularly introduction and later invariant-set results) and likewise does not give this example's metric gap census. General fractal-string references (Lapidus–Pearse–Winter, arXiv:1006.3807; Lapidus–Radunović–Žubrinić, arXiv:1604.08014) supply methods, not the exact original-coordinate gaps of this map. Published mathematical-record searches for aliases involving Lorenz alpha-limits, Fibonacci gaps and tube formulas returned related but differently scoped results, none of which covers the concrete constants or precise tube function. The dimension/entropy quotient and Fibonacci denominator alone are known or routine; they are not claimed as independent novelty. Unindexed or alternate-language equivalences remain a genuine publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The original dynamical construction gives a natural Cantor alpha-limit but does not answer the metric questions of *all* gap lengths or the Lebesgue size of thin neighborhoods in original phase coordinates. Their answer matters for quantitative distinguishability of this near-full-dimensional exceptional orbit set, not an arbitrarily chosen discretization. The exact single central gap, Fibonacci multiplicities, zeta function and nonconstant tube amplitude characterize this specifically embedded invariant set. The oscillation shows its near-one-dimensional dimension cannot be replaced by a single Minkowski content constant. Neither unproved downstream applications nor a new general tube theorem is alleged.

## Limits and closest literature

Only the fixed map is covered. Generic lattice fractal-string oscillations, symbolic entropy and the abstract Fibonacci recurrence are pre-existing concepts. Earlier exact example-specific gap calculations in unindexed work cannot be ruled out. The analysis assumes Euclidean length, not a symbolic metric. The checker cannot certify infinite proofs or originality.
