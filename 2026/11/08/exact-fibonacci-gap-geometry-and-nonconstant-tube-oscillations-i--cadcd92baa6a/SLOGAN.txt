The Cantor alpha-limit set in an explicit six-piece expanding Lorenz map has one gap of length \(\kappa\) and \(2F_{m+1}\) gaps of length \(\delta\beta^{-m}\) for each \(m\ge0\), giving an exact Fibonacci geometric zeta function. Its Euclidean tube volume has an explicit nonconstant log-periodic leading coefficient, so this near-full-dimensional invariant set is not Minkowski measurable.
