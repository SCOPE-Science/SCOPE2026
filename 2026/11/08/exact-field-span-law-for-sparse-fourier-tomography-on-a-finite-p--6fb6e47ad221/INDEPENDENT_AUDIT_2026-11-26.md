# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Nontrivial ψ makes (s,y)↦ψ(sy) a nondegenerate character pairing, even over extension fields: for nonzero y multiplication by y is a bijection. Invert in s; equality of quadratic labels factors as (t−u)(t+u−c)=0. The fiber equations give antisymmetry under t↦c−t, including zero at c/2. Composing with the c₀ reflection produces all translations generated by c−c₀; over characteristic p that additive subgroup is exactly H_C, independently of c₀. The converse follows by factoring each reflection using the c₀ reflection and a translation. On F/H_C the shifted reflection has one fixed point and (q/h−1)/2 opposite pairs, proving the claimed nullity and rank. Each active pair contributes exactly 2h support coordinates, with equality on a single pair; the two indicators then attain failure at sparsity h. The full-span case is injective without a sparsity restriction. Full span requires affine dimension m, with equality in view count exactly for an affine basis, and the distinct lines intersect only at the origin.

PASS

Pass on the extension-field affine-span law for arbitrary complete nonhorizontal view sets and the exact rank, minimum null support and design consequences together. ## value

PASS

A view count by itself can be misleading in extension fields: over F₉ three offsets {0,1,2} leave only a one-dimensional span and admit a collision on two 3-site cosets, whereas {0,1,ω} for ω outside F₃ is injective. The exact span invariant explains the difference and supplies an immediately usable test for rank, worst-case sparse identifiability, and how many complete views to acquire. The prescribed-line restriction is integral to the problem, not a claim about optimal arbitrary samples.
