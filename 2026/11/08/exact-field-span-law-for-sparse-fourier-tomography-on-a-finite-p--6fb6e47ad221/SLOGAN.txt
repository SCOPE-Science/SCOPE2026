On the finite parabola over \(\mathbb F_{p^m}\), complete nonhorizontal Fourier-line views have a kernel determined exactly by the prime-field affine span of their slopes: if that span has size \(h\), the nullity is \((p^m/h-1)/2\) and the smallest invisible support is \(2h\). Full linear reconstruction needs precisely \(m+1\) affinely independent views, whereas a smaller collection still identifies every signal of sparsity below \(h\).
