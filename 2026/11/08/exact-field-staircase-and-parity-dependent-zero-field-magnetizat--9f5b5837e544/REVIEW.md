# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The fixed-population cut formula is proved by exact decomposition into row and column clique cuts, integer concavity and constructive balancing of both degree classes. The discrete first differences give all transition fields, including repeated plateaus, and an inclusion-exclusion count of multiples supplies the exact gcd transition count. Zero-field equality constraints yield the parity-dependent magnetization spectrum. The packaged exhaustive checker independently tests eight small boards, 323 larger energy envelopes and 361 gcd counts. Finite enumeration is not the infinite proof; the symbolic argument covers all finite dimensions and arbitrary real fields.

## Originality — PASS (examined evidence; provisional priority)

The comparison explicitly acknowledges older max-cut results on line graphs, including Mohar–Poljak's even-by-even rook-graph subfamily (1990, Proposition 4.3), as established prior work. Arbib and Guruswami give more general line-graph cut methods; Dyer and coauthors analyze generic line-graph Ising partition functions; Betre–Lewis analyze a different base-graph family. Those results are not presented as novel. The specific exact all-field staircase, gcd count and parity-dependent zero-field magnetization sector classification have not been located as a statement or direct corollary in the accessible material. There is residual historical-priority risk from inaccessible older optimization papers, alternative balanced-degree treatments, and unindexed work. Search nonfindings alone do not establish priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This solves the full zero-temperature field phase diagram of a canonical high-degree frustrated lattice family, identifies the arithmetic number of magnetic transitions across all finite aspect ratios, and distinguishes an odd-by-odd field-induced magnetization jump from the even-side plateau. The joint structural classification of all ground states, not the elementary special-case max-cut number, is the motivated contribution. It supplies exactly solved benchmarks for degeneracy and finite-size magnetic response.
