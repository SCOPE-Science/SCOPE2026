The antiferromagnetic Ising model on every rectangular rook graph \(K_a\square K_b\) has an exact finite-field ground-state staircase with \(a+b-\gcd(a,b)\) distinct transition fields and completely classified balanced-row-and-column ground configurations. When both sides are odd, the magnetization jumps by \(2\min(a,b)\) across zero field, whereas a graph with either side even has a zero-magnetization plateau.
