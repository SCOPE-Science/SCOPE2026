For the antiferromagnetic Ising model on the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), every ground state in every uniform field is a near-regular positive-edge subgraph of \(K_n\), and all critical fields have explicit parity-dependent arithmetic formulas. Even orders exhibit \(n/2+1\) distinct zero-field magnetization sectors and an order-\(n\) total-magnetization jump under an infinitesimal field, unlike odd orders.
