For every odd prime \(p\ne5\), the fifth-power graph over \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) has an explicit ordered additive-energy recurrence for all \(k\), with a five-step singular-origin contribution governed by \(\chi_p(-1)\). This evaluates the prime-power collision counts beyond the known finite-field case and extends multiplicatively to all odd moduli coprime to five.
