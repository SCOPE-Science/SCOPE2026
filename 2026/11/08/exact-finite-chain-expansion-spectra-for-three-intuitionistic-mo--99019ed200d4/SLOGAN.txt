On every finite Heyting chain, the \(\mathsf{CM}\), \(\mathsf{WM}\), and \(\mathsf{IM}\) modal expansion classes have explicit all-size binomial-sum censuses governed by a forbidden band in the possible values of \(\Diamond\). As the chain grows, precisely \(1/4\) of \(\mathsf{CM}\) expansions survive the \(\mathsf{WM}\) axiom asymptotically, and precisely \(2/3\) of those survive the additional \(\mathsf{IM}\) axiom.
