On a finite \(N\)-cycle, the exact one-sided ergodic halo constant is \(C_N(\alpha)=\max_{1\le k\le N}\min\{N,\lceil k/\alpha\rceil-1\}/k\), and a single consecutive block always attains the optimum at each size. Unlike the continuous-time value \(1/\alpha\), the finite constant equals one throughout \(\alpha\ge1-1/N\), has a sharp jump at that threshold, and increases to \(1/\alpha\) as \(N\to\infty\).
