The complete rational line spectrum of the projective torus \(\{[1:x:y:(xy)^{-1}]\}\) on \(X_1X_2X_3=X_0^3\) has exactly \((q-1)^2((q-2)(q-5)+2\rho_q)/6\) three-secants, where \(\rho_q\) counts roots of \(t^2+t+1\) over \(\mathbb F_q\). Thus the exceptional-triangle toric code has second generalized weight \((q-1)^2-2\) exactly for \(q=3,5\), and \((q-1)^2-3\) for every other prime power \(q\ge3\); the formula also counts every optimizing two-dimensional subcode.
