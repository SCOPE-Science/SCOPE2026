Anchored lower orthants on any finite product of chains shatter exactly the supports in which every point owns a strictly maximal coordinate, giving a complete all-parameter inclusion–exclusion shattering polynomial. For uniformly chosen supports, the sharp shattering transition follows a universal coupon-collector law with an alphabet-dependent threshold ranging from roughly \(2^m\log m\) in Boolean cubes to \(m\log m\) for sufficiently large alphabets.
