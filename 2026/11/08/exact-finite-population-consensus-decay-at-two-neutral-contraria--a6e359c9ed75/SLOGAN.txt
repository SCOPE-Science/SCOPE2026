At two neutral corners of a three-agent contrarian opinion model, the deterministic density either stays fixed or reverses each round, but a finite randomly reshuffled population reaches unanimity almost surely. Its expected heterozygosity decays exactly as \(\left(1-2/[3(N-1)]\right)^t\), with a sharp matching consensus-time tail exponent and initial-share fixation (or alternating-phase) probabilities.
