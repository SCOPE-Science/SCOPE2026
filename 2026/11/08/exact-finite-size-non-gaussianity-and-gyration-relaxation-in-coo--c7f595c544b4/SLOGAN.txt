In constant-rate cooperative Brownian pair averaging, the stationary centered single-particle and pair-separation excess kurtoses decrease exactly from \(3\) to the distinct nonzero limits \(6/7\) and \(3/7\), while the global squared radius self-averages at rate \(26/(7N)\). Its stationary autocovariance is exactly a single exponential with decay rate \(\nu N/2\); all formulas hold for every integer \(N\ge2\) and every positive diffusion and pair-reset rate.
