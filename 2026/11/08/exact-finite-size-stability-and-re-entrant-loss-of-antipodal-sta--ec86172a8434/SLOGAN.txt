For the complete-graph Kuramoto flow with attractive pairwise and distinct-triad coupling, a nontrivial antipodal cluster is exponentially stable exactly when its squared population imbalance exceeds the network size and the triadic gain lies in an explicit sharp interval. Some clusters lose stability again at high triadic gain: on seven nodes, increasing \(\delta/\sigma\) from \(2\) to \(8\) removes exactly \(21\) attracting antipodal equilibrium circles while preserving \(8\) others including synchrony.
