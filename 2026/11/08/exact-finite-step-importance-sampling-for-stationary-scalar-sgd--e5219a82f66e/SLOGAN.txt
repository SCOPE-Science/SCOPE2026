For unaveraged importance-weighted SGD on scalar strongly convex finite-sum quadratics, the unique fixed sampling law minimizing **exact finite-step stationary mean-square error** has probabilities proportional to \(\sqrt{r_i^2+V_*a_i^2}\), where a one-dimensional monotone equation determines \(V_*\). Even a component with zero gradient at the minimizer may optimally dominate the sample budget: an explicit three-component example achieves exactly five times smaller stationary error than uniform sampling.
