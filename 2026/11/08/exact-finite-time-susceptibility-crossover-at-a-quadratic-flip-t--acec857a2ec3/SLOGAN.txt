For the quadratic flip normal form, the entire finite-time parameter-susceptibility crossover has an explicit universal integral profile, with sharp value \(\mathcal S(0)=1/16\) and positive-side slope \(\mathcal S'(0)=1/72\), beyond its previously known exponent. The critical one-step Lyapunov exponent also has the exact logarithmic coefficient \(-3/2\).
