For a symmetric searcher on a comb with reflecting teeth, the Poisson-reset mean search time has an exact finite-size formula, and tooth height \(H\sim\kappa L^2\) yields a nontrivial \(L^4\) crossover: \(G_\kappa(u)=[\exp(u^{1/4}\sqrt{\tanh(\kappa\sqrt u)})-1]/u\). Its two limiting profiles connect anomalous infinite-tooth search and normal finite-tooth diffusion without imposing a fictitious sharp thermodynamic transition.
