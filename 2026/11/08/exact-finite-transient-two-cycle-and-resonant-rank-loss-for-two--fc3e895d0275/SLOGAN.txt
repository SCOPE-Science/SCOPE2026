For the two-row isotropic quadratic, nuclear-scaled polar descent at any fixed normalized step \(0<\alpha<2\), \(\alpha\ne1\), has an exact singular-contrast map whose generic trajectories enter a two-step scalar orbit \(E_{t+2}=(1-\alpha)E_t\) after finitely many updates. Exactly the exceptional initial contrasts \(|1-\alpha|^k\), \(k\ge1\), instead cause rank loss at iteration \(k\) and switch to a different rank-one rate.
