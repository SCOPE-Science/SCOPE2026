For the two-row isotropic quadratic at a fixed normalized step \(0<\alpha<2\), \(\alpha\ne1\), nuclear-scaled polar descent has an exact orbit classification: outside the equal-singular and resonant starts it eventually obeys \(E_{t+2}=(1-\alpha)E_t\), while precisely the initial contrasts \(|1-\alpha|^k\), \(k\ge1\), lose rank at update \(k\) and then obey a rank-one scalar law.
