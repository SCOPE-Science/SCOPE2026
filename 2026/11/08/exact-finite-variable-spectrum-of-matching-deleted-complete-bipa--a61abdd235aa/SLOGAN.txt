For complete bipartite graphs with both sides of size at least three, deleting an arbitrary matching leaves an exact counting-free \(k\)-variable theory determined by the matching size and the two unmatched-side sizes after truncation at \(k\), with a separate zero-matching case. All members share one two-variable theory, whereas the number of unrestricted-depth \(k\)-variable theories for \(k\ge3\) is exactly \((k^3+4k^2-7k-2)/2\).
