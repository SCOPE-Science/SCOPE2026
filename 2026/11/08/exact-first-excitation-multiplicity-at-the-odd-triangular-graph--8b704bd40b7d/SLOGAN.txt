For the antiferromagnetic Ising model on \(L(K_n)\), odd \(n\ge5\), at the parity field \(h=2J(n-3)\), the first excited energy lies exactly \(4J\) above the ground state and has an explicit closed degeneracy formula obtained from three-degree-defect paths and triangles. The first finite-temperature correction is enhanced by an exact degeneracy ratio growing like \(n^3/24\); the already-known ground-state staircase is not the claimed contribution.
