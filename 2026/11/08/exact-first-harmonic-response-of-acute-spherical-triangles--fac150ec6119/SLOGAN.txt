For three uniform points on the two-sphere, the cosine of any one side, weighted by the event that all three geodesic angles are acute, has exact mean \((\pi-2)/64\). This yields the entire acute-triangle probability \(1/(2\pi)-1/8+(\pi-2)\varepsilon^2/64\) for independent points sampled from any dipole density \(1+\varepsilon u\cdot x\), and the exact local slope \((\pi-2)/32\) in the pinned von Mises–Fisher model.
