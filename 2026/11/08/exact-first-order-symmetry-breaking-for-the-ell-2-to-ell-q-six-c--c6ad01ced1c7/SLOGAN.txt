The six-cycle incidence matrix has an exact first-order \(\ell_2\)-to-\(\ell_q\) norm symmetry-breaking threshold at \(q_*=\log4/\log(4/3)\): the uniform vector is uniquely extremal below it, and at the threshold it ties exactly with the nonuniform orbit of \((1,2,2)/3\). A complete one-variable norm formula holds for every finite \(q\ge2\), and the symmetry breaks strictly above the threshold.
