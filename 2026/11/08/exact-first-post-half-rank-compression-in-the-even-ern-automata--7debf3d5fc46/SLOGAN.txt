For the even \(2m\)-state Černý automaton, the first rank threshold below half is exactly \(\lambda_{2m}(m+1)=3m+3\) for every \(m\ge3\). A wraparound obstruction forces two extra letters beyond the preceding linear deficiency rule, and the explicit word \(d r^3d(r^2d)^{m-2}r^3d\) attains the bound.
