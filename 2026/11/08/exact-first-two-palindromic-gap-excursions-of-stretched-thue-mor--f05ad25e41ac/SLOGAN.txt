For the stretched Thue–Morse word \(h_M(0)=a\), \(h_M(1)=a^M b\), the first nonzero local palindromic gap persists for exactly \(2M+4\) lengths, vanishes for the next \(M+2\), and then returns at \(5M+9\). An exact finite gap-context calculation proves the entire initial gap spectrum for every \(M\ge1\).
