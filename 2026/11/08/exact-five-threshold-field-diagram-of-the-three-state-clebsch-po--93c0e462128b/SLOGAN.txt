The 16-site folded-cube Clebsch graph has an exactly five-threshold three-color Potts antiferromagnetic field diagram, with transition fields \(h/J=-5/4,0,1,11/3,5\) and 720 zero-field ground states of precisely three frustrated bonds. At saturation \(h=5J\), six favored-population sectors coexist with total labeled degeneracy \(I_C(2)=4065\).
