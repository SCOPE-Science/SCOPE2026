For synchronous binary minority versus majority dynamics on the same squared cycle \(C_n^2\), the exact fixed-state counts have degree-five and degree-three spectral formulas, respectively. Their counts grow as \(1.6663019373\ldots^n\) and \(1.4655712319\ldots^n\), so local anti-coordination has exponentially more fixed states than local coordination under the same tie-retention rule.
