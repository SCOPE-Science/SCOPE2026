For finite disjoint unions of strict chains, counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{k}\) equivalence for \(k\ge2\) is precisely equality of each chain-length multiplicity truncated at \(k\), even at unlimited quantifier depth. The number of theories by minimum representative order has the generating function \(\prod_{(k+1)\nmid r}(1-x^r)^{-1}\), connecting sharp logical width to partitions avoiding multiples of \(k+1\).
