For the clique bouquet \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_i K_{a_i}\), the entire forced-colouring polynomial at its chromatic palette \(s+1\), where \(s=\max_i a_i\), factors into a simple product with one inclusion-exclusion correction. Every smaller blade must be seeded completely, while a maximum blade may leave just one initially blank vertex; the formula gives all coefficients and the exact minimum number of seeds.
