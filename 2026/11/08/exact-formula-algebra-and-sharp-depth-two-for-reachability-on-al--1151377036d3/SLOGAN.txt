On a finite rooted Alexandrov star with \(m\) leaves, topological path reachability together with the Cantor derivative sees precisely the root's atomic type, the set of atomic types realized by leaves, and the pointed leaf's type: multiplicities are invisible. For \(k\) variables this yields exactly \(2^{Q\sum_{j=1}^{\min(m,Q)}\binom Qj(j+1)}\) formula classes, where \(Q=2^k\), with a sharp primitive-language modal-depth bound of two when \(k\ge1\) and \(m\ge2\).
