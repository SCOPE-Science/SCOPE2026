On the product of the reflexive two-point chain and a finite complete \(\mathsf{S5}\) cluster, each pointed modal theory is determined exactly by one paired lower/upper propositional color and the set of pairs occurring among columns. For \(k\) variables and \(m\) columns this yields precisely \(2^{\sum_{s=1}^{\min(m,4^k)}s\binom{4^k}{s}}\) inequivalent formulas, with sharp depth two for \(k\ge1,m\ge2\) and exact stabilization at \(m=4^k\).
