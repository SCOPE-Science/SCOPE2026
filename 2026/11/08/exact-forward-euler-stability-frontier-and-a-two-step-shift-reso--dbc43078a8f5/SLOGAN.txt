For two-node exponential FSBP advection, the sharp all-Fourier-mode forward-Euler CFL is \(\frac{2\tanh(\beta/2)}{\beta}\min\{1,\sinh^2(\beta/2)\}\), while the polynomial endpoint has no positive grid-uniform forward-Euler step. At the uniquely optimal shape \(\beta=2\operatorname{arsinh}1\), two Euler steps exactly shift every nodal value backward by one cell, with no two-step damping.
