For the real Paley harmonic frame at every prime \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), all four-column Gram matrices have exactly three spectra, and each spectral type has a closed-form occurrence count. The sharp four-column restricted-isometry deviation is \(1\) for \(p=5\) and \(3/\sqrt p\) thereafter, with all extremizing sets characterized by a triangle-sign test.
