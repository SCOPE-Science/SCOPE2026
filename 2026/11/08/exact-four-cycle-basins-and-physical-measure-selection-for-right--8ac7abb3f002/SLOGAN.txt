For Everett's farthest-projection dynamics on the complete supporting lines of a right-isosceles triangle, exactly one attracting primitive four-cycle competes with five fixed points: its basin is a finite union of explicit intervals with total arclength \(14(2-\sqrt2)\). Its exact share of the triangle perimeter is \(6-4\sqrt2\), and only its uniform orbit measure and the opposite-hypotenuse midpoint atom are physical for one-dimensional arclength.
