In the finite-step max-plus negative-feedback map of Yamazaki and Ohmori, the global number of primitive four-cycles jumps from zero to one to two at the exact locking ratio \(T/B=5/3\), throughout \(3/2<T/B<2\). Above this border collision the two cycles have exact fourth-return spectra \(\{0,0\}\) and \(\{0,4\}\), distinguishing a superattractor from a saddle-type orbit.
