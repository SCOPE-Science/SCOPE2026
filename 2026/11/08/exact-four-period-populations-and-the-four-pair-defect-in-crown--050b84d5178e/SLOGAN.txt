For threshold-one three-state Greenberg--Hastings excitation on the labelled crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), the exact population of primitive four-period states is \(2(2^n-2)(2^n-2-2n)+24\mathbf{1}_{n=4}\) for every \(n\ge3\). The isolated extra \(24\) states at \(n=4\) are precisely the phase permutations whose deleted matched pairs carry opposite four-phase labels.
