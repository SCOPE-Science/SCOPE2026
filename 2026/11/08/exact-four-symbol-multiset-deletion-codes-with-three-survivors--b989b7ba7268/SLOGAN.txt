For four-symbol multisets, the exact maximum number of codewords distinguishable after leaving only three symbols is \(20,11,6,5,5,5\) at source lengths \(3,4,5,6,7,8\), and exactly \(4\) at every length \(n\geq9\). Explicit optimal constructions and exhaustive finite upper-bound certificates meet a pigeonhole proof for the infinite tail.
