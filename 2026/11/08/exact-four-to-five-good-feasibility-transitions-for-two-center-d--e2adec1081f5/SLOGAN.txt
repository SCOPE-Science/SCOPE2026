With two centers of two agents, joint center-EFX and global agent-EF1 feasibility for four goods is equivalent to \(a\le b+c\), while five goods require \(a\le b+c+e\) in descending order. Adding one good can rescue or destroy feasibility; its complete four-to-five transition probabilities are exactly \((p_{00},p_{01},p_{10},p_{11})=(3/40,7/40,1/120,89/120)\) for uniform values and \((41/105,22/105,4/105,38/105)\) for exponential values.
