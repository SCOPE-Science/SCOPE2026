Shortest ternary \(k\)-subsequence-universal words, for any \(k\ge1\), admit exactly four types of single-trigram contiguous avoidance, classified by equality patterns `000`, `001`, `010`, and `012`. Their exact rational generating functions and distinct exponential growth bases follow from a six-permutation transfer argument; the result concerns this restricted enumeration, not the novelty of the underlying OEIS sequences.
