At the critical fourth-component window for \(L^\infty\) random torus graphs, every one of the six four-vertex connected types has an explicit all-dimension Poisson intensity, obtained by exact coordinate-order integration. The induced four-cycle has limiting first-order existence probability \(0\) in dimension one but \(1-e^{-a/9}\) in dimension two; in large fixed dimensions, the normalized four-component intensity is asymptotically \(3/4\) paths and \(1/4\) claws.
