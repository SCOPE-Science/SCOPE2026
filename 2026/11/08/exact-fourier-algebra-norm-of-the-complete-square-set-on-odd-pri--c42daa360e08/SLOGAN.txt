For every odd prime, the Fourier projection onto **all** square residue classes modulo \(p^k\), including nonunit squares, has an explicit sharp endpoint norm obtained from one Gauss-sum pair at each valuation level. Its Fourier-algebra norm grows exactly as \(\sigma_p k+O_p(1)\) with an explicit arithmetic two-branch slope, unlike the depth-independent norm of the unit-square projection.
