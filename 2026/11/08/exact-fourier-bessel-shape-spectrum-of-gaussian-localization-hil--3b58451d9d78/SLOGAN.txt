For Gaussian short-time Fourier localization, the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm at a disk has an exact area-preserving Fourier–Bessel shape Hessian: translations are the only quadratic zero modes, and elliptic boundary oscillations are uniquely the least costly nontranslation modes. The sharp quadratic coefficient is \(e^{-2A}[I_1(2A)-I_2(2A)]\), with an explicit small- and large-area law.
