In a prime-power cyclic group, moving fewer than \(p\) points of a complete-residue transversal into already occupied residue classes leaves exactly \(p^V-1\) Fourier zeros, where \(V\) is the optimal \(p\)-adic matching depth of the departed and receiving residues. Every nonempty permitted defect destroys at least \((p-1)p^{b-1}\) original zeros; the threshold of fewer than \(p\) moves is sharp.
