For Gaussian time-frequency localization, every smooth area-preserving deformation of a disk has an exact diagonal Fourier shape Hessian: degree one is flat by translation symmetry, and degree two is the unique least-stable genuine shape mode. At disk area \(R\), the sharp quadratic deficit relative to the squared-radius boundary-profile norm has coefficient \(e^{-R}/(R+2)\), and the maximizing signal has an explicit first-order Hermite response.
