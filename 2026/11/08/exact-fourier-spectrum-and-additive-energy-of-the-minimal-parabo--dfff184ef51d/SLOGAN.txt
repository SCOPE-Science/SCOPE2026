The minimum-size odd-order parabolic Kakeya set has a completely explicit Fourier transform, and its exact fourth Fourier moment splits according to \(q\bmod4\) and, in one branch, the quadratic character of \(2\). Its **uniform** probability measure satisfies \(|\widehat\mu(\xi)|\le 2/q\) for every nonzero frequency, a stronger specification than existence of an unspecified Fourier-decaying measure.
