For the Moore-coupled ideal-storage lattice, every Fourier mode has an exact stability interval \(-1/d-r/(2-r)<1-p+p\mu<(d^{-1}-r)/(1-r)\), so diffusion never destabilizes a strictly stable homogeneous fixed point. Yet a transverse stripe mode can regain and lose stability as coupling grows: at \(r=1/4\), \(d=3\) its sharp interval is \(16/27<p<62/63\).
