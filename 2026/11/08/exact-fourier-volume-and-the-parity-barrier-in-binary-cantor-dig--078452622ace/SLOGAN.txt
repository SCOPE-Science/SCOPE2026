The entire finite integer-digit Fourier tower on a two-digit Cantor grid has exact normalized determinant \(\prod_j|\sin(\pi d t_j/b)|^{2^{n-1}}\); optimizing its volume gives a parity-dependent, completely classified family of digit steps. When \(b/\gcd(b,d)\) is odd, even the best of these square digit-tower frames has a least squared singular value at most \(\cos^n(\pi/(2q))\) with \(q=b/\gcd(b,d)\), while even quotient bases permit exact unitarity.
