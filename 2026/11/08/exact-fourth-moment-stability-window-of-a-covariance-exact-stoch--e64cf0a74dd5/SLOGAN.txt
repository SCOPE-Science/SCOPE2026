For a scalar Itô diffusion with independent additive and multiplicative Brownian noises, drift-midpoint theta-Maruyama matches stationary variance at every positive step size but can create finite fourth moments where the exact invariant law has an infinite fourth moment. The complete nonmonotone stability window is governed by the sharp noise ratio \(2/3<b^2/a<2/\sqrt3\), and every finite true fourth moment is strictly underestimated at positive step size.
