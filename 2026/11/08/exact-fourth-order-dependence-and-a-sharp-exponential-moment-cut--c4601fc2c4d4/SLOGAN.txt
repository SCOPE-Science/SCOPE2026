For an isotropic Gaussian target, a scalar-noise-calibrated, full-noise random-coordinate Langevin scheme has the exact target stationary covariance but strictly positive coordinate kurtosis and strictly negative dependence between distinct squared coordinates at every stable positive step. At the natural complete-reset step, each marginal is an explicit geometric Gaussian scale mixture whose moment-generating function is finite on only a bounded interval, proving that covariance accuracy does not restore Gaussian tails.
