Two equal-strength Cole–Cole dielectric relaxation processes have one exact loss peak if and only if their relaxation-time ratio \(r\) is at most \(r_*(\alpha)=\exp\{2\operatorname{arcosh}[(c+\sqrt{c^2+8})/2]/\alpha\}\), with \(c=\cos(\pi\alpha/2)\), and otherwise have exactly two peaks. The threshold rises from \(3+2\sqrt2\) in the Debye limit to exponentially large ratios as fractional broadening increases; all peak positions and the intervening trough height are explicit.
