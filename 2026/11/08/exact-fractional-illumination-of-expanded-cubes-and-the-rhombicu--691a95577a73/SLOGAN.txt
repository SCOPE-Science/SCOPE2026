The full 24-vertex expanded-cube family has exact fractional directional illumination number \(24/7\), including the regular rhombicuboctahedron; four ordinary directions suffice, yielding gap \(7/6\). As soon as the cubic parameter increases from \(a=1\) to any \(a>1\), the fractional value drops from eight to \(24/7\), and an exact seven-vertex-per-direction bound proves this value sharp.
