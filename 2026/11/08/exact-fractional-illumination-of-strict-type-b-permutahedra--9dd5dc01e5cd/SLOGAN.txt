Every strict signed-permutation permutahedron in dimension \(n\) has fractional illumination number exactly \(4^n/\binom{2n}{n}\), and every generic direction illuminates precisely \((2n-1)!!\) vertices. In particular, the regular truncated cuboctahedron has fractional illumination number \(16/5\), and the all-dimensional scale grows as \(\sqrt{\pi n}\).
