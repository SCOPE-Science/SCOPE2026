The six possible gaps between consecutive indices where \(b(2n)\) is not a sum of three squares have exact frequencies \((1/4,1/4,1/16,3/16,3/16,1/16)\) for gap lengths \((6,8,10,16,18,24)\), respectively. Each empirical gap count has logarithmic discrepancy, derived from adjacent Thue–Morse parity correlations.
