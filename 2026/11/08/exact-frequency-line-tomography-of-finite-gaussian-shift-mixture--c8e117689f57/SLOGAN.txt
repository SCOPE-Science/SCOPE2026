The exact minimum number of parallel frequency scans needed to linearly reconstruct the full Hermitian coefficient matrix of a finite Gaussian translate mixture is \(\max_s(a_s+2b_s)\), where \(a_s\) and \(b_s\) count diagonal and off-diagonal shift-pair sums. Sidon shift supports need precisely two frequency lines and admit an explicit optimal \(m^2\)-sample design, whereas \(m\) equally spaced shifts need \(m\) frequency lines; these optimality claims concern linear lifting, not arbitrary nonlinear phase retrieval.
