In a percolated random recursive tree, the number of deleted edges touching the retained root component has an exact bivariate generating function with the component size, and at the first time the component reaches size \(k\) its frontier is negative binomial with \(k-1\) successes. At a fixed total tree size the frontier is asymptotic to \((1-p)/p\) times the root-component size, with an exact centered second moment and a Gaussian fluctuation of variance \((1-p)/p^2\) after dividing by the square root of the observed root-component size.
