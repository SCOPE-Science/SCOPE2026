# Exact Gabor lifting ranks and composite-alphabet obstructions for cyclotomic phase masks

## Finding

Fix an odd prime \(p\ge5\), an integer \(m\ge1\) with \(\gcd(p,m)=1\), and a nonempty proper subset \(S\subsetneq\mathbb F_p\). Let \(\mu_m\) denote the complex \(m\)-th roots of unity and let \(g:\mathbb F_p\to\mathbb C\) be zero outside \(S\), with \(g(t)\in\mu_m\) on \(S\). For \(u\in\mathbb F_p\), set
\[
E_u=\{t\in S:t+u\in S\},\qquad \lambda(u)=|E_u|,\qquad D=S-S.
\]
Define
\[
c_g(u)=\sum_{t\in E_u}g(t+u)\overline{g(t)},\qquad
Z(g)=|\{u\in D\setminus\{0\}:c_g(u)=0\}|.
\]
The complex linear span of all \(p^2\) Gabor rank-one intensity projectors generated by \(g\) has dimension exactly
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}\mathcal L_g=p|D|-Z(g).}
\]
Every zero of the Gabor ambiguity function at a *nonzero* modulation frequency is forced only by a missing support difference; for \(u\in D\), any ambiguity zero can occur solely at modulation zero.

Let \(\mathscr W(m)=\{\sum_{\ell\mid m,\ \ell\text{ prime}}n_\ell\ell:n_\ell\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\}\), with \(\mathscr W(1)=\{0\}\). Then a complete universal robustness criterion holds:
\[
\boxed{
\text{Every phase assignment }g|_S:S\to\mu_m\text{ yields }\operatorname{rank}\mathcal L_g=p^2
\iff
\lambda(u)\notin\mathscr W(m)\quad\text{for all }u\ne0.
}
\]
For a support with \(k=|S|\), universal robustness implies \(k(k-1)\ge p-1\).

The criterion detects a genuine *composite-alphabet* obstruction. For \(p=31\), let \(S\) be the fifteen nonzero quadratic residues. Every nonzero overlap equals \(7\). Consequently every signing from \(\mu_2\) is complete, every phase assignment from \(\mu_3\) is complete, yet some phase assignments from \(\mu_6\) are incomplete because \(7=2+2+3\). An explicit \(\mu_6\) assignment has lifting rank \(959\), instead of \(961\).

## Assumptions and scope

All statements concern complex matrix-level informational completeness of the complete set of cyclic time-frequency Gabor *intensity projectors*, not just injectivity on rank-one signal matrices. Specifically, set \(\omega=e^{2\pi i/p}\), let \((T_ag)(t)=g(t-a)\), \((M_bg)(t)=\omega^{bt}g(t)\), and define \(P_{a,b}=(M_bT_ag)(M_bT_ag)^*\). The lifting map is \(\mathcal L_g:X\mapsto(\operatorname{tr}(XP_{a,b}))_{a,b\in\mathbb F_p}\) on \(M_p(\mathbb C)\). Its rank equals the complex dimension of the projector span. No normalization of \(g\) is needed for rank.

The coprimality assumption \(\gcd(p,m)=1\) is essential to the stated cyclotomic-field argument; no theorem is claimed when \(p\mid m\). Proper support is also essential: it creates a missing coefficient in each cyclotomic polynomial and makes the shift graph acyclic. Singular lifting does not, by itself, establish failure of ordinary pure-signal phase retrieval.

## Proof

**Step 1: Weyl-diagonal rank formula.** The Weyl operators \(M_vT_u\) form an orthogonal basis of \(M_p(\mathbb C)\). Conjugation by a Weyl operator acts on each Weyl basis vector by a unit complex character. Taking the two-dimensional discrete Fourier transform of the orbit \(\{P_{a,b}\}\) therefore isolates, up to a nonzero scalar, the coefficient of \(gg^*\) along each Weyl basis vector. Thus
\[
\operatorname{rank}\mathcal L_g
=|\{(u,v)\in\mathbb F_p^2:A_g(u,v)\ne0\}|,
\quad A_g(u,v)=\sum_{t\in E_u}g(t+u)\overline{g(t)}\omega^{vt}.
\]
This is the same Weyl-character decomposition underlying the standard Gabor matrix-recovery criterion.

**Step 2: No spurious nonzero-modulation cancellation.** Let \(K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)\), where \(\zeta_m=e^{2\pi i/m}\). Because \(\gcd(p,m)=1\), the cyclotomic fields \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)\) and \(K\) intersect in \(\mathbb Q\); hence \(\Phi_p(X)=1+X+\cdots+X^{p-1}\) is irreducible over \(K\). If \(E_u\ne\varnothing\) and \(v\ne0\), the polynomial
\[
F_{u,v}(X)=\sum_{t\in E_u}g(t+u)\overline{g(t)}X^{vt\bmod p}\in K[X]
\]
has degree at most \(p-1\), is nonzero, and has at least one zero coefficient because \(E_u\subseteq S\subsetneq\mathbb F_p\). If \(F_{u,v}(\zeta_p)=0\), irreducibility forces \(F_{u,v}\) to be a scalar multiple of \(\Phi_p\); its zero coefficient would force that scalar to vanish, a contradiction. For \(u=0\), all coefficients are exactly \(1\) on \(S\), so the same argument applies. At \((u,v)=(0,0)\), the ambiguity value is \(k>0\).

Consequently, shifts outside \(D\) contribute zero for every modulation, shifts in \(D\) contribute nonzero coefficients at the \(p-1\) nonzero modulations, and at modulation zero contribute exactly when \(c_g(u)\ne0\). The identity \(c_g(0)=k\) proves \(\operatorname{rank}\mathcal L_g=p|D|-Z(g)\).

**Step 3: Universality and constructive converse.** For any nonzero \(u\), the directed graph with vertices \(S\) and edges \(t\to t+u\) for \(t\in E_u\) is a disjoint union of directed paths: the full shift permutation is one cycle of length \(p\), and the proper support omits at least one vertex. Given *arbitrary* labels \(h_t\in\mu_m\) on these edges, set one starting vertex phase to \(1\) on each path and propagate \(g(t+u)=h_tg(t)\). This realizes every prescribed list of \(\lambda(u)\) edge products as an actual phase mask. Therefore a mask satisfying \(c_g(u)=0\) exists if and only if some \(\lambda(u)\) many \(m\)-th roots of unity sum to zero. Lam and Leung's vanishing-sum theorem gives exactly the weight semigroup \(\mathscr W(m)\), including \(0\) for an empty overlap. This gives both directions of the universal criterion, with an explicit construction whenever it fails. If it holds, all \(p-1\) nonzero differences occur, and counting the \(k(k-1)\) ordered distinct-element differences yields \(k(k-1)\ge p-1\).

**Step 4: Composite-phase certificate.** For \(p=31\), the quadratic residues are
\[
S=\{1,2,4,5,7,8,9,10,14,16,18,19,20,25,28\},
\]
and direct residue counting gives \(\lambda(u)=7\) for every \(u\ne0\). Let \(\xi=e^{2\pi i/6}\), and assign \(g(t)=\xi^{a_t}\) with exponents, listed in increasing order of \(t\in S\),
\[
(a_t)=(0,0,0,3,0,0,3,3,0,0,0,2,0,0,0).
\]
For \(u=1\), the ordered edge-product exponents on edges beginning at \(t\in\{1,4,7,8,9,18,19\}\) are \((0,3,0,3,0,2,4)\). Thus
\[
c_g(1)=(1-1)+(1-1)+(1+\xi^2+\xi^4)=0.
\]
The opposite shift also vanishes by conjugation, and exact enumeration of the remaining zero-modulation sums gives \(Z(g)=2\), hence rank \(31^2-2=959\). By contrast, \(7\notin2\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\) and \(7\notin3\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\), so all binary and all ternary phase assignments are matrix-informationally complete. This is not an implication of either single-prime alphabet alone.

## Verification

A standalone Python checker (`checker.py`) implements exact arithmetic in \(\mathbb Z[e^{2\pi i/3}]\) for all ambiguity sums at modulation zero; it exhausts \(1898\) mask assignments across the indicated prime and support cases, and verifies the complete \(p=31\) destructive certificate. Sample direct complex ambiguity calculations test the separately proved nonzero-modulation claim, but floating-point observations are not used as its proof. Run `python checker.py`; the expected marker is `EXACT_CYCLOTOMIC_ALPHABET_GABOR_PASS`.

## Relationship to prior work

Bojarovska and Flinth, *Phase Retrieval from Gabor Measurements* (first public preprint March 19, 2015), prove the nonvanishing-ambiguity Gabor reconstruction criterion and a sufficient difference-set construction for unweighted indicator windows (Theorem 2.2, Theorem 2.3 and Proposition 2.2). Lam and Leung, *On vanishing sums for roots of unity* (first public preprint November 13, 1995), establish the exact semigroup of attainable zero-sum lengths. Both are explicitly used here as prior ingredients. The binary-sign criterion and positive-rational-support formula are special cases and are not asserted to be independently new. The present claim combines cyclotomic disjointness, an exact rank expression, and realization of *every* edge-product multiset on proper prime cycles to obtain an all-alphabet necessary-and-sufficient classification. In particular, it reveals mixed-prime cancellation even when every constituent prime alphabet is individually safe.

## Limitations

This is a theorem on prime cyclic groups, proper supports, unit-magnitude \(m\)-th root coefficients, and \(\gcd(p,m)=1\). It does not classify windows with arbitrary complex amplitudes, full supports, nonprime cyclic dimensions, or phase alphabets divisible by \(p\). The matrix-lifting rank formula is not a classification of ambiguous pairs of pure signals. The claim's priority beyond the cited sources is bounded by the literature comparison; no independent mathematical audit is claimed.
