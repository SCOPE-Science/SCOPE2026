# Scientific review: Exact Gabor lifting ranks and composite-alphabet obstructions for cyclotomic phase masks

## Correctness

**PASS.** Weyl-character diagonalization establishes the projector-span rank as the ambiguity support cardinality. Coprime cyclotomic irreducibility removes nonzero-modulation cancellations, and the shift-induced path forest gives an exact constructive converse to the Lam–Leung zero-sum weight criterion. The explicit \(p=31\) mask is verified by exact algebra. The included checker exhausts 1,898 finite assignments; these checks corroborate rather than replace the all-prime argument.

## Originality

**PASS under bounded literature comparison.** Lam and Leung's 1995 preprint supplies the roots-of-unity weight theorem, not Gabor rank. Bojarovska and Flinth's 2015 preprint supplies the general Weyl-ambiguity rank criterion and a sufficient unweighted difference-set construction, not the arbitrary-alphabet necessity construction. Binary-sign and positive-rational statements are treated as prior special cases; the all-order semigroup threshold, exact rank correction, and composite-alphabet contrast are the surviving new claim. Relevant search-index entries were compared by implication, although a positive-rational result was available only at abstract level. Residual possibility of an unlocated stronger theorem remains.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The question of full matrix reconstruction under every alphabet-constrained phase code is naturally motivated by Gabor measurement design. An exact rank formula and a necessary-and-sufficient overlap filter provide a complete, efficient structural characterization. In \(\mathbb F_31\), safe binary and safe ternary codebooks combine into an unsafe six-phase codebook, which cannot be inferred from either prime-alphabet robustness statement alone.

## Boundaries and sources

The proof is only for odd prime cyclic groups, proper supports, unit-magnitude coefficients, and phase-alphabet order coprime to the prime. Matrix lifting failure is not pure-state phase-retrieval failure. Main sources: Lam–Leung, arXiv:math/9511209v1 (Main Theorem); Bojarovska–Flinth, arXiv:1503.05800v2 (Theorems 2.2–2.3, Proposition 2.2).
