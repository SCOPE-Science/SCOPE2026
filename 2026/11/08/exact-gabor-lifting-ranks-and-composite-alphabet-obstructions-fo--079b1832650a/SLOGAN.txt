For a proper support on a prime cycle, arbitrary coprime cyclotomic phase coding can destroy Gabor matrix tomography only at zero modulation, and the exact lifting rank is \(p|S-S|-Z(g)\). Every phase mask remains informationally complete exactly when no nonzero support-overlap count belongs to the additive semigroup generated by the prime divisors of the phase-alphabet order; a 31-point example is robust for binary and ternary codes separately but not for their six-phase combination.
