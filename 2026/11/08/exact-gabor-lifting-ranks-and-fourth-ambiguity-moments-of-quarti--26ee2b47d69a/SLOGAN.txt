For the unit quartic chirp \(g_p(x)=p^{-1/2}e^{2\pi i x^4/p}\) at every prime \(p\ge5\), the complete Gabor lifting rank is \(p^2-p+1\) when \(p\equiv1\pmod3\), but drops to \(p^2-2p+2\) when \(p\equiv2\pmod3\). Its normalized fourth ambiguity moment is exactly \(3-3/p+1/p^2\), exceeding the optimal constant-modulus fourth moment by \((1-1/p)^2\).
