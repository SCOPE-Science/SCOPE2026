For a strictly positive-definite quadratic Hamiltonian with an exactly stable two-dimensional symplectic reduction, Gaussian least-squares sketched assembly followed by symmetrization produces exponentially unstable reduced trajectories with the explicit probability \(p_k(\gamma)=\pi^{-1}\int_0^1(\gamma^2c^2/(1+\gamma^2c^2))^{(k-1)/2}dc/\sqrt{c(1-c)}\). This probability is positive at every finite sketch size and has a sharp \(k^{-1/2}(\gamma^2/(1+\gamma^2))^{(k-1)/2}\) large-sketch law, distinguishing preserved Hamiltonian form from preserved positive energy.
