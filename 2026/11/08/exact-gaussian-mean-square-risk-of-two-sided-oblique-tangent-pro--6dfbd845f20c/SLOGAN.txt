For a fixed matrix velocity, two independent Gaussian sketches give a locally unbiased oblique dynamical low-rank tangent projection once the sketch has at least one extra row, but its exact squared-error factor on the normal residual is \(2r/(\ell-r-1)+r^2/(\ell-r-1)^2\), infinite when \(\ell\le r+1\). At \(\ell=2r\), this factor tends to \(3\) even for rank-one normal residuals, so proportional oversampling does not remove instantaneous mean-square perturbations.
