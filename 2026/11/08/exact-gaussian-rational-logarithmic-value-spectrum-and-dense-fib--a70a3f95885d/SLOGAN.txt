For Kovič's polar arithmetic derivative on \(\mathbb Q(i)^\times\), the exact logarithmic-value spectrum consists of reduced rationals with a factor of at most \(4\) at the prime \(2\) and a squarefree odd denominator; every nonempty value class is dense in \(\mathbb C\). Thus \(1/4\) occurs but \(1/8\) does not.
