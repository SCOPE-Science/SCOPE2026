For the strict-future modal algebra of \((\mathbb N,<)\), finitely many Boolean generators create exactly one atom for every position up to the last finitely occurring color and one atom for every recurrent tail color; therefore the exact generated-algebra size is \(2^{N+|I|}\). This partition is the unique coarsest tuned refinement of the original color partition, and even one singleton generator \(\{m\}\) produces \(2^{m+2}\) elements.
