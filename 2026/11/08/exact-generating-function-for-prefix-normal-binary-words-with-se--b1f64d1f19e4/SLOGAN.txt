For binary prefix-normal words with exactly seven ones, the complete length generating function is \(z^7N(z)/((1-z)(1-z^3)(1-z^4)^2(1-z^6)^3)\), with an explicit symmetric degree-eighteen numerator. This closes the first fixed-density case beyond the published formulas through six ones and gives \(p_7(n)\sim (289/2488320)n^6\).
