For every crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\) with \(n\ge4\), all inclusion-minimal geodesic transversals fall into three explicit structural families, with exact cardinality polynomial \(2nz^{n-1}+(2^n-2n-2)z^n+\binom n2z^{2n-4}\). Their spectrum has unbounded gaps, and the matching geodesic-packing number is \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).
