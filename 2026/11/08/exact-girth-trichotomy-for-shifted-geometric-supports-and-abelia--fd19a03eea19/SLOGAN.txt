For binary words marking shifted geometric powers \(\{b^j+c:j\ge0\}\), the associated sum-incidence graph is a forest exactly when \(c<b(b-2)\), and its finite girth equals \(2b+2\) until \(c=b^2-b-1\), above which it is \(6\) (a strict drop for \(b\ge3\)). Via the published forest criterion, this is also an exact two-parameter classification of which such words have the minimum possible Abelian maximal pattern complexity at every length.
