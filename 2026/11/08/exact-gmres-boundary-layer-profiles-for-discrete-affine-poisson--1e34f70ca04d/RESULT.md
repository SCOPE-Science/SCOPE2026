# Exact GMRES boundary-layer profiles for discrete-affine Poisson data

## Finding
For every integer \(n\ge 2\), take the symmetric positive-definite Dirichlet matrix \(A_n=\operatorname{tridiag}(-1,2,-1)\) and the discrete-affine right-hand side \(b_j=L+(R-L)j/(n+1)\), with real \((L,R)\ne(0,0)\). Starting unrestarted Euclidean GMRES at \(x_0=0\), for every integer \(0\le k\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), its exact squared residual is
\[
\|r_k\|_2^2=\sum_{j=k+1}^{n-k}\left(L+\frac{(R-L)j}{n+1}\right)^2+\frac{(R^2-L^2)^2}{(L^2+R^2)(n+1)^2}\sum_{j=1}^k j^2.
\]
The minimal polynomial degree of \(A_n\) relative to \(b\), hence the exact GMRES termination step, is \(n\) if \(L\ne R\) and \(L\ne -R\); \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) if \(L=R\ne0\); and \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) if \(L=-R\ne0\).

For constant data \(L=R=1\), the entire residual curve is \(\|r_k\|_2^2=\max\{n-2k,0\}\). For the ramp \(L=0,R=1\), the entire residual curve is
\[
\frac{\|r_k\|_2^2}{\|b\|_2^2}=\frac{(n-k)(n-k+1)(2(n-k)+1)}{n(n+1)(2n+1)},\quad 0\le k\le n.
\]
Thus for any fixed target relative residual \(\tau\in(0,1)\), the minimal iteration counts \(k_n(\tau)\) satisfy \(k_n(\tau)/n\to(1-\tau^2)/2\) for constant data and \(k_n(\tau)/n\to1-\tau^{2/3}\) for ramp data, despite identical matrices.

## Assumptions and scope
All statements concern exact-arithmetic, unrestarted GMRES minimizing the Euclidean residual over \(x_0+\mathcal K_k(A_n,b)\), where \(x_0=0\). The matrix is the dimensionless second-difference Dirichlet operator with diagonal entries \(2\), neighboring off-diagonals \(-1\); uniform positive scaling of this operator does not change the GMRES residual curves. The domain includes arbitrary real boundary parameters \(L,R\) not both zero, and every integer \(n\ge2\). No preconditioning, truncation, numerical roundoff, or higher-dimensional grids are asserted.

The vector \(b\) is the unique discrete harmonic interpolant with exterior boundary values \(L\) and \(R\): \(b_0=L\), \(b_{n+1}=R\) and \(2b_j-b_{j-1}-b_{j+1}=0\) at interior indices.

## Proof
**1. Localized images and the first-half Krylov space.** The discrete harmonicity identity gives exactly \(A_n b=L e_1+R e_n\). Define reflected endpoint vectors \(w_j=L e_j+R e_{n+1-j}\). Since \(A_n\) is tridiagonal, for \(1\le j\le \lfloor n/2\rfloor\) the expansion of \(A_n^{j}b\) has the new outermost inward entries \((-1)^{j-1}L\) at position \(j\) and \((-1)^{j-1}R\) at position \(n+1-j\), plus previously encountered reflected endpoint pairs. Equivalently, \(A_n^j b=(-1)^{j-1}w_j+\sum_{i<j}\alpha_{i,j}w_i\). This follows by induction from the tridiagonal stencil: no shorter propagation reaches the pair at depth \(j\), and the pair is disjoint for these indices. The vectors \(w_1,\dots,w_k\) are mutually orthogonal and nonzero because their supports are distinct and \(L^2+R^2>0\). Consequently
\[
A_n\mathcal K_k(A_n,b)=\operatorname{span}\{w_1,\dots,w_k\},\qquad 0\le k\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor.
\]

**2. Exact projection.** GMRES residuals are the orthogonal residuals after projecting \(b\) onto \(A_n\mathcal K_k\). The central coordinates \(k+1\le j\le n-k\) are unchanged. For each reflected pair \(j,n+1-j\) with \(j\le k\), the orthogonal projection onto the line generated by \((L,R)\) leaves squared norm \((Rb_j-Lb_{n+1-j})^2/(L^2+R^2)\). Because \(b_{n+1-j}=R+(L-R)j/(n+1)\), its determinant is \((R^2-L^2)j/(n+1)\) (up to an immaterial sign). The pair residuals and the untouched central coordinates are orthogonal, giving the stated squared-norm identity exactly, including \(k=0\) and the even-dimensional central-empty case.

**3. Exact termination degree.** The Dirichlet matrix has simple positive eigenvalues \(\lambda_s=2-2\cos(\pi s/(n+1))\) and unnormalized eigenvectors \(v^{(s)}_j=\sin(\pi s j/(n+1))\), indexed by \(1\le s\le n\). As \(A_nb=L e_1+R e_n\), symmetry gives
\[
\lambda_s\langle v^{(s)},b\rangle=\sin\left(\frac{\pi s}{n+1}\right)\bigl(L+(-1)^{s+1}R\bigr).
\]
Every sine and eigenvalue factor here is nonzero. Thus every odd spectral mode survives unless \(L=-R\), and every even mode survives unless \(L=R\). Counting the odd and even indices proves the three grades, and the relative minimal polynomial of a symmetric matrix with distinct eigenvalues has degree equal to the number of active eigenmodes. Since \(A_n\) is invertible, unrestarted GMRES terminates at precisely this degree, and not earlier.

**4. The two full profiles and mesh-scale iteration laws.** When \(L=R=1\), the boundary-pair projection removes both entries exactly at each step through \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). The residual squared is the number of remaining central ones, \(n-2k\). In odd dimension, the central eigenmode is eliminated by the final step \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), proving the asserted full profile. When \(L=0,R=1\), the image \(A_nb=e_n\) generates in each successive step one new unit basis direction moving left; hence \(A_n\mathcal K_k=\operatorname{span}\{e_{n-k+1},\dots,e_n\}\) for every \(0\le k\le n\). The residual has precisely its first \(n-k\) ramp coordinates, whose squared sum is \((n+1)^{-2}\sum_{j=1}^{n-k}j^2\). Divide by the initial sum and use \(\sum_{j=1}^m j^2=m(m+1)(2m+1)/6\) to obtain the full formula. Uniform normalized limits as \(n\to\infty\), together with monotone residual decay and continuity/strict monotonicity of the limiting functions on the interior, yield the stated limits for \(k_n(\tau)/n\).

## Relationship to prior work
Chen and Soodhalter (2026, arXiv:2609.24751) examine attainable GMRES behavior under constrained tridiagonal Toeplitz structure, emphasizing nonsymmetric settings and discussing structured discretization right-hand sides as further scope. The present result is not a general attainable-curve classification: it explicitly solves the fixed symmetric Dirichlet Poisson operator for its entire natural two-parameter discrete-harmonic right-hand-side family. Liesen and Strakoš (2004) supply general tridiagonal/Jordan GMRES residual analysis and orthogonality methodology, but their stated estimates do not give the above exact projection formula, grade trichotomy, or two sharp tolerance laws. Zhang's 2007 thesis includes Toeplitz GMRES bounds and canonical endpoint-vector right-hand sides; it does not state this combined affine-interpolant classification in the inspected sections. Earlier work on convection-diffusion models and right-hand-side-weighted spectral bounds is acknowledged as covering the general need for right-hand-side-sensitive GMRES analysis, not the particular exact conclusions here.

## Limitations
The theorem is confined to a fixed one-dimensional SPD Toeplitz Poisson matrix and a structured two-dimensional affine right-hand-side family. The finite-step identity is stated only through \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) for general \(L,R\); full curves beyond this are given only for constant and one-sided ramp data. Asymptotic rates are exact-arithmetic iteration counts for the specified Euclidean residual tolerance, not predictions of floating-point, restarted, preconditioned or higher-dimensional solvers. Historical-priority uncertainty remains for unindexed material and alternative equivalent derivations.
