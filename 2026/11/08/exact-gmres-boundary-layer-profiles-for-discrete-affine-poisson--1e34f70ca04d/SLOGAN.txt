For unrestarted GMRES on the one-dimensional Dirichlet Poisson matrix, every discrete-affine right-hand side has an explicit squared-residual law through half the grid and an exact spectral termination degree. Constant forcing reaches a fixed relative tolerance after asymptotically \((1-\tau^2)n/2\) steps, while a one-sided harmonic ramp requires \((1-\tau^{2/3})n\) steps on the same matrix.
