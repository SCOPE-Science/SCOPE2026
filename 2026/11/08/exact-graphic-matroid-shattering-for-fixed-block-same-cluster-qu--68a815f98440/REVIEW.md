# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** A signed trace is realizable precisely when its required-equality contraction has at least \(q\) vertices and the quotient negative graph has chromatic number at most \(q\), including loop obstructions. Contracting a subset of edges in a forest preserves acyclicity of the complementary negative edges, while cycles and too many all-positive edges each force a missing labeling. This proves the complete shattering characterization, and graphic-matroid truncation and Tutte's subset expansion give the enumerators. The standard three-coloring reduction proves NP-completeness for specified traces. The standalone verifier checks 9,916 partition/support cases, finite traces and exchange; no finite test is treated as an infinite proof.

**Originality (PASS within examined material).** The 2018 *Semi-supervised clustering for de-duplication* paper models clusterings as binary pair queries and gives general VC bounds, but its inspected Section 5.1 and Appendix C.1 do not give the fixed-block forest/cardinality criterion. Ailon et al. (2017) and Saha–Subramanian (2019) study approximation and query complexity, not all-support shattering. Related mathematical results address upper-bounded block sizes, ordered/noncrossing blocks, or a prescribed unequal bipartition. Their claims do not imply the unrestricted exactly-\(q\)-block result. The forest, matroid, Tutte, Cayley and 3-coloring inputs are standard, not claimed as new. Unlocated earlier equivalent statements remain possible.

**Value (PASS).** For the natural fixed-number-of-clusters family, the result gives a complete structural classification across arbitrary allowed query graphs, exact capacity and face enumeration, and a matroid basis-exchange structure. The contrast between NP-complete existence of a single trace and linear-time recognition of shattering is a meaningful consequence rather than a routine numerical increment. It can sharpen VC-based sample estimates where the class is exactly all \(q\)-block partitions.

**Limits and comparison risk.** The theorem does not analyze adaptive Littlestone dimension, balanced partitions, geometric restrictions or clustering approximation algorithms; priority remains subject to unsearched vocabulary. No independent external check was performed.
