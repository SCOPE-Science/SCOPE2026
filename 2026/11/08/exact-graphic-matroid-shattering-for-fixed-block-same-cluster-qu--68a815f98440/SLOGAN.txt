For pairwise same-block queries on arbitrary allowed edges, partitions into exactly \(q\ge2\) nonempty clusters shatter precisely the forests with at most \(n-q\) edges: the shattering complex is a truncated graphic matroid and has exact VC dimension \(\min\{n-q,n-c(H)\}\). Individual signed-trace feasibility is governed by chromatic number and is NP-complete already for three clusters, despite linear-time testing of universal shattering.
