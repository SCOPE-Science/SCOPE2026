For every \(d\)-regular four-cycle-free missing-edge graph on two \(n\)-vertex parts, a random greedy maximal independent set in the remaining bipartite graph has an exact law determined by \(n,d\) alone: its \(2n\) mixed terminal sets each have probability \(d/[2(n^2-(d-1)^2)]\). For fixed \(d\), this greedy law and the uniform law on maximal independent sets have total-variation distance tending to one.
