The canonical online greedy attractor for the binary characteristic word of positive powers of two marks exactly the zero-based positions \(\{0\}\cup\{3\cdot2^k-1:k\ge0\}\). Its exact length-\(N\) cost is \(1\) for \(N\le2\) and \(2+\lfloor\log_2(N/3)\rfloor\) otherwise, so the asymptotic coefficient of \(\log_2 N\) is exactly one.
