For the uniform zero-field classical Ising antiferromagnet on the regular \(24\)-cell graph, eight edge-disjoint octahedra give an exact frustration rule and ground energy \(-32J\). The complete magnetization-resolved ground-state polynomial is \(948+672(z^2+z^{-2})+264(z^4+z^{-4})+48(z^6+z^{-6})+3(z^8+z^{-8})\), totaling exactly \(2922\) states.
