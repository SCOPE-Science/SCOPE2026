The uniformly oriented central section of a regular truncated octahedron is almost surely a hexagon, octagon, or decagon, with exact probabilities \(1-12(U+V+W)/\pi\), \(12(U+W)/\pi\), and \(12V/\pi\), where \(U,V,W\) are three explicit spherical-triangle angles. Its signed-coordinate symmetries reduce the complete probability law to five normal chambers with just three elementary solid-angle evaluations.
