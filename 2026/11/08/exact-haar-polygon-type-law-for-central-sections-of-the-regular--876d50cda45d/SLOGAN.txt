A Haar-random plane through the center of a regular truncated icosahedron cuts a polygon with exactly \(10,12,14,16\) or \(18\) sides, and each type has an explicit positive probability given by finite algebraic spherical-angle sums. An exact sign-chamber enumeration resolves the complete five-point polygon-type law, with an 18-gon slightly more likely than a 16-gon.
