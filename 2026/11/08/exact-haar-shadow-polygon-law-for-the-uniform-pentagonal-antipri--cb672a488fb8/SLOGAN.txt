For uniformly oriented orthogonal shadows of the uniform pentagonal antiprism, the number of projected polygon vertices is almost surely \(6\), \(8\), or \(10\), with exact probabilities determined by a complete 32-chamber spherical normal arrangement. Decagonal shadows occur with probability \((5\arccos(-1/3)-3\pi)/(2\pi)\approx0.02043362\), a quantity not determined by the previously known expected silhouette count.
