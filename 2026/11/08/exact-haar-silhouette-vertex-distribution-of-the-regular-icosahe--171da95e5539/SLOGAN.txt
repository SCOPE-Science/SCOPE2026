The silhouette of a Haar-random orthogonal projection of a regular icosahedron has exactly \(6\), \(8\), or \(10\) corners, with a complete exact three-point probability law. Its ten facet-normal great circles form \(20\) hexagonal, \(60\) triangular, and \(12\) pentagonal visibility chambers, yielding \(\mathbb E[N]=30\arccos(\sqrt5/3)/\pi\).
