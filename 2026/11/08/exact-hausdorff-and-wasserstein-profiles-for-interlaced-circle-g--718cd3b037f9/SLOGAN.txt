For a two-interlaced circular grid with alternating mass imbalance \(\varepsilon\), the exact Steiner-centered polygonal Hausdorff error is \(1-(\pi/N)\cot(\pi/N)+|\varepsilon|(\pi/N)\tan(\pi/N)\), and its geodesic Wasserstein discrepancy is \(\pi(1+\varepsilon^2)/(2N)\). Fixed nonzero alternating imbalance moves the normal measure \(\Theta(N^{-1})\) from the equal grid while preserving the reconstructed body's \(\Theta(N^{-2})\) convergence to the disk.
