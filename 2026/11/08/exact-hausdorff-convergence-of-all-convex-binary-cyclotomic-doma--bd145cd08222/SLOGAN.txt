Across all convex binary cyclotomic domains, the exact Euclidean Hausdorff distance to \([-1,1]^2\) is \(\sqrt2(1-p^{-1/(p-1)})\) for indices \(p\) and \(2p\) with \(p\) odd prime, and \(\sqrt2(1-2^{-1/2^{k-1}})\) for index \(2^k\). These distances give sharp, distinct prime and dyadic convergence rates.
