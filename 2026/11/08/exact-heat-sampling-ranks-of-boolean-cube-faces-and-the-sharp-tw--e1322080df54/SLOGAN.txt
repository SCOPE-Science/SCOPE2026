For heat evolution on a Boolean cube, the full space-time sampling rank of every coordinate face and low-pass Walsh band has an exact binomial–Vandermonde formula. Among proper coordinate faces, only codimension one permits recovery of nonconstant low-pass bands from repeated fixed-face observations; two full-band snapshots recover everything, and their unique determinant-optimal separation is \(\frac d2\log\frac{d+1}{d-1}\).
