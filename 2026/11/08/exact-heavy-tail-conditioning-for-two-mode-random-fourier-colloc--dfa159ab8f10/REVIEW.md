# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The two-column Gram matrix has eigenvalues \(m\pm|S|\). The singular torus set is exactly coincident phases; the positive quadratic form \(B=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^\mathsf T/m\) has determinant \(1/m\). Uniform local expansion and ellipsoid-volume squeezing prove the sharp all-index tail; tail integration gives the if-and-only-if moments. The exact two-point formula independently checks normalization. The included script supplies reproducible rational and deterministic numerical checks, not a substitute for the analytic infinite-limit proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Ming–Yu's 2026 primary article treats large, multi-feature PDE random-feature matrices and gives fast singular-value decay / lower condition-number bounds, not the specified two-column random-location distribution. Tucci–Whiting analyze primarily square and large random Vandermonde ensembles, while Kunis–Nagel prove deterministic near-collision conditioning estimates. Accessible mathematical-record searches found no direct all-\(m\) formula or moment cutoff for the present matrix. Unindexed Steinhaus random-walk small-ball results may imply the local asymptotic, and no exhaustive priority certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS
The smallest two-mode Fourier least-squares problem is a natural exact test case for numerical conditioning under random collocation. The sharp constant and every-moment cutoff reveal a sample-count design transition: \(m=2\) has divergent mean conditioning, while \(m=3\) has divergent second moment and \(m=4\) finite second moment. This quantifies a reliability feature obscured by almost-sure full rank; no extension to general random features is asserted.
