For two distinct Fourier characters sampled at \(m\ge2\) independent uniform circle points, the collocation-matrix condition number has the sharp heavy tail \(\Pr(\kappa>t)\sim m^{m/2}t^{-(m-1)}/[\pi^{(m-1)/2}\Gamma((m+1)/2)]\), independently of the integer frequency gap. Its \(q\)-th moment exists precisely when \(q<m-1\), so increasing the sample count changes the exact integrability threshold.
