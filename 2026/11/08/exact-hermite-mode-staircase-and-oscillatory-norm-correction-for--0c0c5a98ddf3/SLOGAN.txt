For Gaussian time-frequency localization on a circular annulus of area \(L>0\) and positive inner enclosed area \(s>0\), the optimal Hermite order is \(\lfloor L/\log(1+L/s)\rfloor\) when the logarithmic mean is nonintegral; at an integral value \(j\), exactly the two modes \(j-1\) and \(j\) tie, while the zero-inner-area disk uniquely selects the Gaussian. All mode-switch radii and norm derivative jumps are explicit, and the scaled second asymptotic coefficient has a nondegenerate interval of accumulation values.
