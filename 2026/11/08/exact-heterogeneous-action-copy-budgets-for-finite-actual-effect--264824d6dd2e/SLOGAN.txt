For \(k\) agents and at most \(r\) local outcomes, unequal action-copy budgets \((m_1,\ldots,m_k)\) suffice for every finite actual-effectivity frame exactly when the product of the \(k-1\) smallest budgets is at least \(r\). A mixed-modulus construction realizes every feasible budget vector, completing the tradeoff beyond the previously known equal-budget bound.
