# Scientific assessment: exact heterogeneous-price bipartite pinning

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem has a direct all-parameter analytic proof. The positive-semidefinite constraint for the complete-bipartite pinned Laplacian is *equivalent*, with no relaxation, to the product-of-reciprocal-sums inequality. A strictly positive Hessian for the logarithmic constraint establishes uniqueness of the target-cost optimizer. The KKT equations reduce it to a unique scalar root. A second monotone scalar inversion exactly recovers the budget-optimal rate. The price-prefix, first-actuator, terminal affine and two-price threshold statements are all verified from the same formulas. The standalone script passes 1,962 finite tests across spectral, optimization, support, high-rate, special-case and exact-rational checks; the calculations corroborate but do not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS on the inspected record, with priority limits

Zhou et al., arXiv:1511.02012v1 (2015), supply a relevant linear-matrix-inequality budget framework, but their full preprint derives an analytic solution only for its uniform-control-weight case and uses numerical optimization for heterogeneous cases. The new claim specifies every optimal independently priced actuator on complete bipartite graphs, with a unique two-root construction, exact support order, an explicit high-budget affine law and a closed two-price activation transition. Orouskhani, Jalili and Yu (2016) optimize different eigenratio-based pinning objectives using numerical heuristics; no such exact result is present in the inspected article sections. Jalili, Sichani and Yu (2015) is relevant to pinning placement but was only available at abstract level and remains a priority risk. Jafarizadeh (2022) provides a general uniform-price pinning-budget algorithm and exact formulas for complete bipartite networks when one whole part is pinned and the other part is free; these earlier special cases are not treated as novel. The present arbitrary-positive-independent-price, two-sided activation law does not follow from those uniform-price formulas. General convexity/KKT/Schur-complement tools and general optimization problem statements are explicitly acknowledged as prior work. The classified bipartite model is not a complete graph or a star, and the formula is not an implication of their specialized gain laws without substantial new analysis.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Heterogeneous actuator cost is intrinsic to feedback allocation, and complete bipartite networks model two-group coupling. The classification holds for all graph sizes and all independent positive vertex prices, avoids an unrestricted semidefinite numerical solve, proves unique price-ordered actuator activation within each group, identifies the first activated vertex under a strict cheapest-price condition, and supplies an exact eventual affine rate/budget law. The two-uniform-price specialization has a genuine, sharp switch from controlling one entire side to both sides. These are structural all-budget facts, not isolated numerical records or routine parameter substitutions.
