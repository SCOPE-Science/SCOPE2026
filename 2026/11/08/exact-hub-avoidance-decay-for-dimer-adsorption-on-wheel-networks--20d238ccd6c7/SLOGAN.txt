For equal-rate irreversible dimer adsorption on an even-rim wheel, the probability that the hub remains vacant is exactly twice the trace of a cyclic integral operator and decays as \(2\lambda_*^m\), where \(m\) is half the rim length and \(\lambda_*=0.287730645241419\ldots\) is determined by a modified-Bessel root. On odd-rim wheels the hub-vacancy event is impossible.
