For the ordinary voter model on a bouquet of private cliques, initially only the common hub holds its opinion, and the probability that it never reverses has an exact product-of-tridiagonal-resolvents formula. When blade sizes remain bounded, this probability equals \(\exp[-\sum_i r_i^2/\sum_i r_i+O(1/\sum_i r_i)]\), so equal-size blades of size \(r\) yield the sharp limit \(e^{-r}\), in contrast to the classical eventual-fixation probability \(1/(r+1)\).
