For every finite field and every upper triangular matrix algebra, ordered tuples of idempotent algebra generators have an exact weighted-hypercube count: injective binary diagonal labels contribute a product of adjacent factors \(q^{h}-q\) and nonadjacent powers of \(q\). The formula refines the established minimal-number-of-idempotent-generators theorem by determining every positive fiber of the counting problem, with no restriction on field characteristic.
