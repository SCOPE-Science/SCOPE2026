For every dimension \(n\ge2\), a uniformly sampled perfect evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) has exactly unit idempotent-count variance in characteristic two, but in odd characteristic its variance is \(1-\frac{(q-1)((2q-1)^n-2q^n+1)}{2(q^n-1)(q^n-q)}\). This exact two-point law strengthens the previously known unit mean and quantifies the finite-field correction to the predicted symmetric-group fixed-point behavior.
