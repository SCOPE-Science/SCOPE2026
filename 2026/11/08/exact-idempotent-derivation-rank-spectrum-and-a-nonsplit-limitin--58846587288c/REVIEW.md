# Review of the idempotent-derivation rank spectrum

## Correctness (PASS)

The theorem is reduced to matrix generators modulo scalars by a matrix-unit derivation identity. In characteristic two, the operator equation becomes a quadratic matrix equation with two Artin–Schreier classes; the split class is represented by complementary projectors and the nonsplit class by an irreducible quadratic field structure. This yields the stated exact multiplicities by counting complements and using the centralizer \(\mathrm{GL}_m(q^2)\). The derivation ranks are obtained from commutant dimensions. Odd characteristic has only the zero operator by the nondegenerate trace form. For the asymptotic claim, the exact Gaussian-binomial factorization has a uniform summable majorant, and the nonsplit orbit has an explicit finite-product ratio. Exhaustive direct checks confirm the small-field histograms. A finite computation alone is not used to prove any infinite claim.

## Originality (PASS within the inspected published material)

Eick–Moede's work supplies the notion and relation to Lie-algebra gradings, and Ayupov–Arzikulov supplies background on matrix derivations. Crucially, Caenepeel–Dăscălescu–Năstăsescu already classify cyclic gradings on matrix algebras, including the characteristic-two nonsplit type, so the *existence and structural classification* of these types are not new. Diniz–Pellegrino count grading isomorphism classes; the rank-by-rank number of distinct operators on each fixed matrix algebra and the asymptotic nonsplit sampling share are different claims and are not stated by those inspected sources. Relevant record searches found no identical or dominating quantitative theorem. This does not establish publication priority. The inaccessible Diniz et al. 2026 full text remains a particular risk, together with possible implicit consequences of a broader classified and enumerated grading theory.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The full rank census is a natural graded-algebra invariant: each idempotent derivation is a concrete \(\mathbb Z_2\)-grading projection, so its rank is the dimension of the odd graded component. Exact counts identify both the finite-dimensional distribution and a nonvanishing nonsplit population that is invisible if one counts only isomorphism types. The asymptotic theta-mixture is precise, not a routine restatement of classification, and quantifies the statistical effect of quadratic descent.

## Limitations

The statement is limited to the fixed full matrix algebra and \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear derivations. The asymptotic keeps \(q\) fixed and matrix size even. No priority or independent verification certificate is claimed. The 2026 paper cited in the result was not available in full text.
