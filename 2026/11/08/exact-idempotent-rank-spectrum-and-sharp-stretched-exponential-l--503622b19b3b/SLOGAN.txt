Idempotent endomorphisms of an even cycle have an exact rank distribution given by endpoint-to-endpoint walks on finite paths. Their total number is asymptotic to \(B2^n n^{1/6}e^{-c n^{1/3}}\), with explicit \(B,c>0\), and the image rank has Gaussian fluctuations of order \(n^{1/6}\) around a constant multiple of \(n^{1/3}\).
