For every finite commutative chain ring with nonzero square-zero maximal ideal and residue field of size \(q\), the complete independence polynomial of its two-by-two triangular commuting graph is \((1+q^2(q^2-q)z)^{q(q+1)}+q^2(q^2-1)z(1+q^2(q^2-q)z)^{q^2}\). The count, including the \((q^2(q^2-q))^{q(q+1)}\) maximum independent sets, depends only on the residue-field order, even for nonisomorphic coefficient rings.
