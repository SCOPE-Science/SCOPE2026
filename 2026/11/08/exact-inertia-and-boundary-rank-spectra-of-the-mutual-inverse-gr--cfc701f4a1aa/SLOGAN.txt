The looped mutual-inverse graph on the rank-\(r\) Green class of all order-preserving transformations of an \(n\)-chain has exactly \(\binom{p+1}{2}\) positive, \(\binom p2\) negative, and \(p(q-p)\) zero eigenvalues, where \(p=\binom{n-1}{r-1}\) and \(q=\binom nr\). Its entire spectrum reduces to singular values of the transversal incidence matrix, with explicit trigonometric eigenvalue lists at ranks \(2\) and \(n-1\).
