For unpreconditioned conjugate gradients on \(AX+XA=bb^\mathsf T\), every early matrix iterate \(X_k\), through the number of active eigenvalues, has exactly \(\lceil k/2\rceil\) positive and \(\lfloor k/2\rfloor\) negative eigenvalues. Thus the rank grows by exactly one per step, and all early iterates after the first are indefinite even though the exact Lyapunov solution is positive semidefinite.
