The ordinary matching complex of the fan \(K_1\vee P_n\) is always a wedge of spheres in exactly one degree, with sphere multiplicities \(k+1\), \(k\), and \(2k+2\) for path lengths \(n=3k\), \(3k+1\), and \(3k+2\), respectively. A spoke-cone decomposition explains the entire three-residue pattern integrally.
