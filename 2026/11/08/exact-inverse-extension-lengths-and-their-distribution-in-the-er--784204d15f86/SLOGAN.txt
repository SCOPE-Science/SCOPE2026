In every \(n\)-state Černý automaton, the shortest word increasing the inverse image of a nonempty proper subset \(S\) is uniquely \(b a^{r}\), where \(r\) is its earliest cyclic run start; the exact length is \(r+1\). The number of subsets requiring each length \(1\leq t<n\) is \(t2^{n-t-1}\), while exactly \(n-1\) subsets require length \(n\).
