Every planar stable Gaussian diffusion with fixed stationary law \(N(0,I_2)\) and drift eigenvalues \(-a\pm b\) lies on one exact entropy-production versus noise-condition-number curve, with a sharp attainable anisotropy-budget optimum. Its entropy production is unbounded even though the stationary Gaussian, relaxation eigenvalues, total noise power, and each fixed-lag antisymmetric covariance remain bounded or unchanged.
