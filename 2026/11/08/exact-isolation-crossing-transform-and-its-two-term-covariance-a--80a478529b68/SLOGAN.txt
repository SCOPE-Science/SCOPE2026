In a uniform random chord diagram, the complete isolation-weighted crossing polynomial is a convolution of the classical crossing polynomials for the two arcs on either side of an isolated chord. The number of isolated chords and the total crossing count have strictly negative covariance for every \(n\ge2\), with the sharp two-term law \(\operatorname{Cov}(J_n,X_n)=-n/3-1/6+O(n^{-1})\).
