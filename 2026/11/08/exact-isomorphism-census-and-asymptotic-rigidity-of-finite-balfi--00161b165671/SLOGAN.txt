Finite Boolean algebras with LFI operators admit an exact Burnside isomorphism census: on \(k\) atoms there are \(6^{k2^{k-1}}\) labeled expansions, with \(6,666,362937456\) isomorphism classes at \(k=1,2,3\). Almost all such algebras have no nonidentity automorphisms, so the number of classes is asymptotic to \(6^{k2^{k-1}}/k!\).
