For an active Brownian particle in an absorbing disk with a uniformly random initial heading, the first nonzero weak-swimming change in mean exit time is an explicitly negative modified-Bessel coefficient that increases strictly toward zero with rotational diffusivity. At high rotational diffusivity, this response develops a sharp \(\beta^{-1/2}\)-wide edge layer with universal profile \(-(z-1+e^{-z})/4\), distinct from the bulk scaling.
