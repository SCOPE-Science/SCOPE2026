For the intersection of two congruent Euclidean balls in every dimension, both the maximum-volume inscribed and the minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoids have explicit semiaxes and complete contact sets. The John contacts lie at normalized axial latitude \(1/\sqrt n\), and in fixed lens geometry its axial semiaxis shrinks at rate \(n^{-1/2}\) while the transverse semiaxis approaches the equatorial radius.
