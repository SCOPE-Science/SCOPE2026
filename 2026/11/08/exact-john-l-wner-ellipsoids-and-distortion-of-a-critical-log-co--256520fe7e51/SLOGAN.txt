The critical log-cosh facet body of every centered volume-one parallelotope has exact John radius \(\sqrt d\,\operatorname{arcosh}(e^{\beta/d})\) and Löwner radius \(\operatorname{arcosh}(e^\beta)\) in affine cube coordinates, giving their full contact orbits and sharp Banach–Mazur distance. That distance rises strictly with both level and dimension, connecting the Euclidean and cross-polytope limits while staying bounded as dimension grows at fixed level.
