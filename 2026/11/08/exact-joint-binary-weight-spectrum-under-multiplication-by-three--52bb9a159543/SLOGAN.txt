For every positive binary length \(r\), the pairs \((s_2(n),s_2(3n))\) with \(n<2^r\) occupy exactly \(\binom r2+2\) positions, with an explicit classification and shortest witness for each position. Equivalently, this completely determines the attainable joint 2-adic valuations of \(\binom{2n}{n}\) and \(\binom{6n}{3n}\) in every dyadic index interval.
