For binary words with exactly three ones, the full set of contiguous-factor Parikh vectors determines the word up to reversal except when both unequal interior zero-gaps are at least as large as the larger of two unequal exterior gaps. Every exceptional class has exactly four words, and the number of exceptions at length \(n\) is the number of partitions of \(n-7\) into parts of sizes at most four, giving a limiting ambiguity probability of \(1/6\) under uniform weight-three sampling.
