For every complete multipartite graph and every \(4\le k\le |V(G)|\), the cardinality polynomials of all \(P_k\)-free induced vertex sets and their inclusion-maximal members have explicit formulas governed by a longest-path occupancy threshold. In \(K_{m,m}\), even \(k\) gives a single maximal-set cardinality, while odd \(k\) gives two classes, arbitrarily long gaps, and a precise polynomially vanishing proportion of the larger sets.
