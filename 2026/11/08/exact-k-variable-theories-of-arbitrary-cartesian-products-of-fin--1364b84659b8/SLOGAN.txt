For every \(k\ge3\), two Cartesian products of arbitrarily many finite nontrivial cliques satisfy the same counting-free \(k\)-variable first-order sentences of unrestricted depth exactly when their multisets of factor sizes, truncated at \(k\), agree. The factor-size multiplicities are recovered intrinsically by clique-defined edge colours and bounded-variable path formulas, and the least width identifying a given product in this family is one more than its largest factor size.
