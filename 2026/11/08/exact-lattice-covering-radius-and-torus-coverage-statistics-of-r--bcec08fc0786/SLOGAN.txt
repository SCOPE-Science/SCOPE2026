For every Reeve tetrahedron \(R_q=\operatorname{conv}\{0,e_1,e_2,(1,1,q)\}\) with integer \(q\geq1\), the standard-lattice covering radius is exactly \(1+2/q\), and the pointwise entry scale has a closed fractional-part formula. The unscaled translates leave precisely \(5/6\) of a unit torus uncovered for \(q=1\), or \(2/q-4/(3q^2)\) for \(q\geq2\), while the mean entry scale is \(2/3+1/q-1/(6q^2)\).
