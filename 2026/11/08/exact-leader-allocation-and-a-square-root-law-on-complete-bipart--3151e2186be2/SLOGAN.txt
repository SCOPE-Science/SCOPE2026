On the unweighted complete bipartite graph \(K_{p,q}\), every fixed-budget noise-free leader placement reduces to an explicit one-variable grounded inverse-trace formula; balanced parts have endpoint allocations, while for fixed \(p,k\) as \(q\to\infty\) its optimal small-part leader count is \(\min(k,r_p)\), up to a possible adjacent tie, where \(r_p\sim\sqrt p\). The strictly optimal two- and three-leader sets on \(K_{2,3}\) are disjoint, so optimal leader sets need not be nested.
