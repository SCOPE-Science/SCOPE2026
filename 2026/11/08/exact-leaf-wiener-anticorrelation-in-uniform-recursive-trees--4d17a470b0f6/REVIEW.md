# Scientific review: exact leaf–Wiener anticorrelation

## Correctness — PASS
The definitions fix rooted leaves and unordered-pair Wiener normalization. The proof independently derives an exact leaf-transmission update and the total-covariance recurrence under a uniform independent parent choice. Eliminating the auxiliary expectation gives a rational first-order recurrence, telescoped to the exact harmonic-number expression and its sharp negative sign. The attached checker enumerates all recursive trees through nine vertices and separately verifies 500 rational recurrence transitions. The infinite result is proved analytically; numerical or exhaustive finite checks are not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
Neininger's 2002 Wiener-index paper gives a Wiener/internal-path-length joint theorem rather than a leaf/Wiener joint theorem. Its accessible author manuscript was materially inspected. Holmgren–Janson (2015) gives limit results for local fringe counts but the accessible abstract does not discuss cross-covariance with Wiener distance; the complete article was inaccessible during examination. Existing public mathematical results comparing root degree with leaf count, root attachments with total root-path length, and fixed-label degrees with total root-path length do not state or imply the aggregate rooted-leaf versus all-pairs-distance result. Exact equivalent-formulation and stronger-coverage searches located no decisive precedent, but they cannot establish historical uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
Rooted leaf count is a canonical local branching statistic and the Wiener index is the canonical total all-pairs distance statistic. Their **exact finite-size** negative covariance, quadratic leading coefficient, and leaf-transmission excess give a quantitative local–global dependence law beyond separate marginal moments. The full all-size classification and normalization are naturally useful for checking approximations and joint fluctuation models of recursive trees. This is a motivated pair of invariants, not an arbitrarily chosen numerical parameter slice.

## Limits
This result applies only to uniform recursive trees. It does not prove conditional stochastic monotonicity, independence, or priority over inaccessible full-text literature. The correlation refinement uses Neininger's established Wiener-index variance asymptotic. External independent verification and historical-priority assessment have not been performed.
