For a uniform random recursive tree on \(n\ge2\) vertices, rooted leaf count and the Wiener index have exact covariance \(nH_n-n(n+5)/4+1/2\): it is zero only at \(n=2,3\), then strictly negative and asymptotic to \(-n^2/4\). The proof identifies a sharp expected leaf-transmission excess of \((n-1)(n-2)/4\) over the Wiener index.
