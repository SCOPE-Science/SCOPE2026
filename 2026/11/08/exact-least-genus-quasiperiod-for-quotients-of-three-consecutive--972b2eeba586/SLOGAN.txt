For the quotient \(\langle a,a+1,a+2\rangle/p\), the genus is an explicit quadratic quasipolynomial with **least period \(2p\)** for every \(p\ge3\), versus period \(2\) at \(p=2\). Its linear coefficient is \([2-\gcd(p,a)-\gcd(p,a+2)]/(4p)\), revealing a sharp arithmetic obstruction to period collapse.
