Every shifted generalized Thue–Morse digit-sum word \(n\mapsto s_2(n+c)\bmod m\) has an explicitly classified minimal binary least-significant-digit-first automaton, with its exact state count obtained by a finite union of binary popcount intervals. For power-of-two shifts \(c=2^k\), the exact count is \(m+\sum_{j=1}^{k+1}\min(m,j)\), exhibiting both triangular growth and eventual linear growth as the shift depth increases.
