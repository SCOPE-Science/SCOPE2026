For the summatory parity of adjacent \(00\) blocks in unpadded binary expansions, every positive level occurs finitely often, with an exact sawtooth occupation law: within each shell \(2u-1\le K\le4u-2\) for a power of two \(u\), multiplicities are first \(u\), then descend to \(1\), and jump back to \(u\) at the endpoint.
