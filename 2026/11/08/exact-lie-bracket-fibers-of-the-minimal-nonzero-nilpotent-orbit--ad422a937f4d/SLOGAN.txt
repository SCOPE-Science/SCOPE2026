For the minimal nonzero nilpotent orbit in \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\), the additive commutator has a complete prescribed-target fiber law: outputs are zero, rank-one square-zero, or rank-two split semisimple, and no other matrices occur. Every rank-two target has exactly \(2(q-1)\) ordered witnesses in odd characteristic but \(q(q^2-1)\) in characteristic two, with explicit formulas for the zero and rank-one fibers.
