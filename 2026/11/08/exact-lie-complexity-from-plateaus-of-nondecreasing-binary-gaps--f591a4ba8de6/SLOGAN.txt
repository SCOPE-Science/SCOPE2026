For an infinite binary word whose positive zero gaps increase weakly and without bound, every completely present cyclic factor class is either all zeros, contains one marker, or is a power of a single equal-gap marker block. The exact Lie complexity at length \(n\) is \(2\) plus one class for each gap size \(a\) whose plateau has at least \(n/(a+1)\ge2\) copies and whose block length \(a+1\) divides \(n\); the same construction yields both constant and unbounded Lie complexity.
