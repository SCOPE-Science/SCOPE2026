For the affine scalar equation \(A(s)x=1\) with \(A(t)=1+ct\), exact companion GMRES has physical residual \(1-(1+cs)\widetilde{x}_m=(1-c/s)R_m^2\), where \(R_m\) is its minimized lifted residual. When \(c=s>1\), every recovered iterate is already the exact scalar solution even though the lifted residual remains bounded away from zero as the Krylov dimension increases.
