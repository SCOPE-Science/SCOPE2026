For identical additive values with goods of sizes \(1\) and \(h\), individually capped and budget-balanced transfers allow exact envy-freeness precisely when aggregate capacity covers total value and the sum of per-agent \(h\)-item slots covers all large goods. The minimum total integer budget is \(\max\{r,(h-s)\Delta\}\), revealing a sharp indivisibility premium and a residue-driven phase transition for random one-unit budgets.
