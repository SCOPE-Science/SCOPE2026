# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof derives the entire derivation space by restricting a derivation to the one-dimensional derived ideal and imposing the conformal symplectic identity. The crucial nonstandard pointwise evaluation lemma explicitly constructs an infinitesimal symplectic operator with any prescribed image on a nonzero vector; the construction uses alternating bilinear identities and is valid in characteristic two. It then builds a matching ordinary derivation at every noncentral and central vector. Dimension and finite-field counts are direct consequences, with a separate rank computation verifying the infinitesimal symplectic parameter count. The independent code solves derivation linear equations and checks all input elements in six small prime-field cases, including characteristic two, but its finite tests are not a general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk

The principal 2026 paper establishes existence of proper local derivations for nilpotent complex Lie algebras but not exact full-space classification for all Heisenberg ranks and fields. The 2024 paper gives an explicit Heisenberg example rather than necessary and sufficient conditions on every linear map; the 2015 paper likewise gives existence examples. The conformal Galilei study treats larger semidirect Lie algebras, and its Heisenberg-radical terminology does not cover the standalone Lie algebra. A previous exact local-automorphism result concerns different witnessing maps and does not imply the local-derivation classification, particularly over the smallest finite field. Search and full-text comparisons did not identify an equivalent complete result; an implicit earlier reflexive-hull classification remains possible.

## Value — PASS

The finding upgrades qualitative existence results to an exact complete classification of a natural operator space in every symplectic rank and characteristic. It identifies the failure of local-to-global rigidity precisely as a codimension \(m(2m-1)\), supplies exact finite-field densities, and explains a characteristic-two contrast in which the smallest Heisenberg case can exhibit local-automorphism rigidity while local-derivation rigidity fails. The pointwise symplectic evaluation lemma is an explicit structural mechanism rather than a tabulation of small examples. The scope is the whole Heisenberg family but not all nilpotent algebras.
