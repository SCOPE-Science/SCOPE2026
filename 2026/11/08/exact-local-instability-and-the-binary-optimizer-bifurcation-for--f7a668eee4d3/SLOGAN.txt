The exact tangent-space Hessian of stationary new-completion frequency in a finite-window expiring coupon collector is a finite occupancy-count expression and becomes positive at the uniform sampling law for every fixed alphabet when the window is long. For two coupon types the full finite-window optimizer classification has a sharp bifurcation: uniform sampling uniquely maximizes entry frequency at lengths \(M=2,3\), but two unequal, explicitly characterized laws are the only maxima for each \(M\ge4\).
