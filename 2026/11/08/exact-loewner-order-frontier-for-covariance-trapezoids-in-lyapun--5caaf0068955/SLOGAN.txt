For a constant-input differential Lyapunov equation, the integrating-factor SSPRK2 covariance step upper-bounds the exact covariance for every positive-semidefinite input and positive step precisely when the drift obeys \(A^2=aI+bA\) for real \(a,b\) with \(4a\ge b^2\). Among Hurwitz drifts, this universal one-sided guarantee holds only for scalar multiples of the identity; any nonscalar stable drift admits rank-one forcing that defeats the upper bound at arbitrarily small steps.
