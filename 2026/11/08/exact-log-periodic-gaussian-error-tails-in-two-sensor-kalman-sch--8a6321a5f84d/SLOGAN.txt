In a two-sensor Gaussian Kalman filter with symmetric Markov switching, the actual stationary estimation-error tail has an explicit \(z^{-2\beta}\) law with a **nonconstant log-periodic multiplier** whose every Fourier coefficient is available in closed gamma-function form. An exact price frontier identifies when switching every step, rather than allowing longer sensor runs, is optimal for mean-square error plus a switch cost.
