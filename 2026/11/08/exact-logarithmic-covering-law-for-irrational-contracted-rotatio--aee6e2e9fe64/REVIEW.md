# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The known Sturmian language gives precisely \(n+1\) occupied atoms; affine contraction bounds their diameters, and the first-step gap \(1-\lambda\) survives as a lower gap \((1-\lambda)\lambda^{n-1}\) by induction. This provides two-sided estimates for the minimum interval covering number and the exact leading logarithmic coefficient. The checker is a finite corroboration, not a substitute for the proof.

**Originality: PASS within inspected evidence.** Bugeaud–Kim–Laurent–Nogueira (2020) describe the contracted rotations and Sturmian coding; Calderón–Villar-Sepúlveda (2023) already prove vanishing Hausdorff and lower box dimensions and the atom upper estimate. The new lower separation estimate is essential for the stated two-sided logarithmic law. Laurent–Nogueira (2018) was checked at abstract level only, so an equivalent earlier computation remains possible.

**Value: PASS.** For a classical piecewise contracting Cantor attractor, standard dimensions vanish and fail to measure the sharp resolution cost. The coefficient \(1/\log(1/\lambda)\) gives a natural nontrivial finer invariant and is independent of irrational rotation number.

**Scientific limitations:** No limiting additive constant is claimed; rational rotations and general non-affine piecewise contractions are outside scope. Literature checks cannot certify priority.
