For any fixed index restriction \(m\ge2\) and prime \(p\nmid m\), the rows whose restricted binomial gcd is divisible by \(p\) have an exact digit-cutoff quasipolynomial of degree \(m\), with leading coefficient \(1/(m!\operatorname{ord}_m(p)^{m-1})\). Thus their ordinary count through multiplier \(X\) grows like \((\log_p X)^m/(m!\operatorname{ord}_m(p)^{m-1})\), with a power lower logarithmic error.
