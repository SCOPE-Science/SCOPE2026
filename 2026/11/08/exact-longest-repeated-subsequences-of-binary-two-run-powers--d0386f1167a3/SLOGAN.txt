For the finite binary run power \(W=(0^a1^b)^k\), the longest scattered exact \(e\)-th power has length \(e\max\{\lfloor k/e\rfloor(a+b),\lfloor ka/e\rfloor,\lfloor kb/e\rfloor\}\) for every \(a,b,k\ge1\) and \(e\ge2\). A mixed-symbol root never beats repeating whole run periods, and a pure-symbol root wins precisely when the leftover partial periods provide enough copies of the longer run.
