For every binary word with exactly three ones, six explicit gap candidates determine the longest unbordered factor, and the sharp smallest possible length at \(n\ge5\) is \(1+\lfloor n/4\rfloor\) with all minimizers classified. Under uniform fixed-content sampling, the longest-factor length divided by \(n\) converges to a nonconstant Dirichlet functional with mean \(157/192\) and variance \(2483/184320\).
