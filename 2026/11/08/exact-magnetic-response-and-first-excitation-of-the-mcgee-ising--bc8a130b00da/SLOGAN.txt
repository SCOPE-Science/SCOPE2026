For the unique cubic girth-seven McGee cage, the classical antiferromagnetic Ising model has exactly two zero-field ground states and its only positive-field ground-state crossings occur at \(h=J\) and \(h=3J\), with degeneracies \(18\) and \(34{,}305\), respectively. The first excitation gap is \(2J\), with exactly sixteen states, and the complete ground-state magnetization and degeneracy are determined at every field.
