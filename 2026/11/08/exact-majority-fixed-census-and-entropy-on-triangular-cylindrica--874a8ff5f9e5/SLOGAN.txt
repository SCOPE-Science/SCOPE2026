On the triangular cylindrical graph \(C_n\square K_3\), tie-retaining synchronous binary majority dynamics has an exact spectral formula for every one-step fixed-state population, obtained from four explicit polynomials of degrees three and four. The number of fixed configurations is \(3^n+O(2^n)\), so the fixed-state fraction is asymptotic to \( (3/8)^n\) even as their population grows exponentially.
