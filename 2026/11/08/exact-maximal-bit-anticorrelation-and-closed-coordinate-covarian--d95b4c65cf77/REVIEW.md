# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The stated object is the equal-endpoint, exponent-one subfamily of the Lawnik–Moysis–Volos map, defined on a quotient circle. The two affine branches have the same external fixed center. Taking the logarithm of the distance to that center gives a genuine orientation-preserving conjugacy to rigid rotation, including the limiting translation case. The pullback invariant measure is normalized. Under its uniform phase, the coordinate's Fourier coefficients are exactly the reciprocals of the logarithmic modulus minus a Fourier frequency. Parseval and the periodic Helmholtz Green function then yield the stated all-lag hyperbolic covariance, with the Bernoulli-polynomial limit at zero modulus. The bit cylinder is exactly a rotation interval of length equal to the rotation number; elementary circle-overlap geometry gives the all-lag bit covariance. Its lag-one value is the Fréchet lower bound for equal Bernoulli marginals. The mechanical-word telescope and standard irrational-rotation coding prove discrepancy and factor complexity. All endpoints, rational and irrational cases, and the circle/interval boundary convention are specified. The executable finite checks compare direct numerical integration to analytical formulas; they are not substituted for infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The source paper (Lawnik–Moysis–Volos, 2023, equation (15) and Figures 4–5) provides the underlying map and numerical chaotic/nonchaotic regimes, but does not state these exact stationary covariance identities, bit joint probabilities, or maximal one-step anticorrelation. The most serious broader source, Laurent–Nogueira (2021), already computes the logarithmic rotation number of a broad two-interval affine circle-homeomorphism family. The present work does **not** claim that rotation-number formula or circle conjugacy as new. It adds observable-specific all-lag covariance kernels in closed form, including the exact threshold-bit law. Gaivão–Laurent–Nogueira (online 2024) likewise analyzes general rotation numbers, not these stationary observables. The prior 2018 break-point invariant-measure results are qualitative. Accessible related research records concerning exponent-two maps, contracted rotations and Sturmian coding concern other maps or different claims. General Fourier and symbolic-rotation methods make the result accessible, but no identical or stronger statement computing both laws for this source family was located. An unindexed general observable-covariance calculation remains a residual priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The exact observables are not arbitrary: the real coordinate is the sequence used by a proposed no-fixed-point chaotic-map family, and the branch indicator is its natural threshold bit. The formulas show that the entire equal-endpoint affine parameter surface has an unusually strong output defect, not merely that a Lyapunov exponent vanishes. Adjacent bits have the mathematically most negative correlation compatible with their common marginal, no binary word has more than minimal Sturmian complexity in the irrational case, and all-lag analog and bit covariances are explicit and recurrent rather than mixing. This is useful for rigorously assessing numerical chaos claims and cryptographic bit-generation feasibility. The precise covariance kernels and sharp extremality matter independently of the qualitative circle-map classification.
