On the equal-endpoint affine surface of a fixed-point-free map, the stationary coordinate has an exact hyperbolic-cosine covariance at every lag, while the natural branch bit attains the maximally negative Bernoulli covariance already at lag one. Irrational-rotation bit sequences have exactly \(N+1\) length-\(N\) patterns and recurrent, nondecaying correlations, exposing a precise obstruction to using this subfamily as a decorrelated output generator.
