For every binary four-run word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\), every maximal closed substring occurrence has an explicit run-boundary endpoint description, and its total count is \(5+\min(a,c)+\min(b,d)-\mathbf1_{c\le a}-\mathbf1_{b\le d}\). At each length \(n\ge5\), the attainable counts are precisely all integers from \(5\) to \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+4\) (only \(5\) at length four).
