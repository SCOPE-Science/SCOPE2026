For every integer base \(q\ge2\), the largest gap between successive \(n\) having \(s_q(2n)\equiv j\pmod q\) is exactly \(2q-1\) for even \(q\), and \((3q^2-1)/2\) for odd \(q\), independent of \(j\). Each largest gap occurs infinitely often; the linear-to-quadratic parity jump follows from explicit carry constructions and a two-block digit-covering argument.
