For the incidence algebra of an ordinal sum of antichains, a Gaussian-binomial rank transfer counts every element of the radical by the rank of its highest potentially nonzero power, thereby exactly counting maximal nilpotency index. In width two the survival probability is \(\frac{q+1}{q}(1-q^{-2})^{h-1}-\frac1q((1-q^{-1})(1-q^{-2}))^{h-1}\), and survivors have rank-one top power with asymptotic probability one.
