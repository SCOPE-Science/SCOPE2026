For two-state probabilistic automata whose transition matrices preserve the order on state probabilities, the uniquely most probable words are exactly the words whose constant runs have pairwise distinct symbols. Every such word has a rational strictly positive two-state witness, and the exact number of length-\(n\) words over \(q\) symbols is \(\sum_{m=1}^{\min(q,n)}q!\binom{n-1}{m-1}/(q-m)!\).
