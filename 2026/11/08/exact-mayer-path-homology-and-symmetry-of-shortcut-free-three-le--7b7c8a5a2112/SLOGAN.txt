Every shortcut-free complete three-level directed network has an explicit, all-order, all-index Mayer path homology decomposition into symmetric-group augmentation modules. The degree-two groups pass through three distinct representation stages, from the three-way tensor \(U_A\otimes U_B\otimes U_C\) to \(U_B\otimes(U_A\oplus U_C\oplus U_A\otimes U_C)\) and then \(P_A\otimes U_B\otimes P_C\).
