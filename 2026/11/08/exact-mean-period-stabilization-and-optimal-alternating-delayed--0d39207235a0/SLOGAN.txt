In an integrator feedback loop whose hold uses the previous sample, alternating two sampling intervals can stabilize every fixed average \(1\le m<3/2\), while uniform sampling at the same average is unstable or marginal. The sharp stable frontier is \(2m-2<ab<1\); the unique fastest unordered pair is \(m\pm\sqrt{4m-1}/2\), with two-update spectral factor \(m-1/2\).
