Independently changing a symplectic-Euler stepsize inside its pointwise stable interval forces exponential expected-energy growth on a harmonic oscillator whenever the stepsize law is nonconstant. For equally likely steps \(h\pm\varepsilon\), the sharp small-jitter factor is \(\rho=1+2h^2\varepsilon^2/(4-h^2)+O_h(\varepsilon^4)\), although either fixed step separately preserves a positive quadratic energy.
