On every positive-definite quadratic, independent common multiplicative stepsize noise destroys geometric mean-square stability of classical heavy-ball iteration exactly at the explicit largest-eigenvalue threshold \(s^2=2(1-\beta)/(\alpha L)-(1-\beta)/(1+\beta)\). At deterministic Chebyshev-optimal tuning its variance tolerance is \(2/[\sqrt\kappa(\kappa+1)]\), strictly below the \(1/\kappa\) tolerance of minimax gradient descent for every \(\kappa>1\).
