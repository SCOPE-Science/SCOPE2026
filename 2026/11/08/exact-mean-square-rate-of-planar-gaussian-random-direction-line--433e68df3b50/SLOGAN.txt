For planar positive-definite quadratics, Gaussian random-direction exact line search has an exact two-state second-moment recurrence and the sharp expected-objective rate \(\lambda_+=(t^2+t+1+\sqrt{t^4-t^2+1})/[2(t+1)^2]\), where \(t\) is the square root of the Hessian condition number. At high condition numbers its true asymptotic contraction gap is \(3/(2t)\), rather than the \(1/t\) gap supplied by the tight conditional one-step bound.
