For every positive-length metric cactus, the exact sharp mean-zero Sobolev point-evaluation norm is a finite maximum of explicit quadratic cycle envelopes and leaf values, with a closed blockwise integrated-resistance correction. Every norming point is a leaf or a unique interior critical point on an attachment-free cycle arc (except that a pure circle is everywhere norming); a three-circle cactus has a unique, non-antipodal norming point and sharp constant \(1777/1500\).
