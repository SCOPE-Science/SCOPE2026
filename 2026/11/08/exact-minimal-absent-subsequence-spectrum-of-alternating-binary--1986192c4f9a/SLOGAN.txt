For the canonical alternating binary words \(W_k=(01)^k\), minimal absent scattered subsequences are exactly the words with \(k\) weak descents, descending end-pairs, and no contiguous \(010\) or \(101\). Their complete length spectrum has rational generating function \(\frac{y^2z(3-2yz+y^2z+2y^3z^2)}{1-2yz+y^2z^2-y^4z^3}\), yielding an exponential growth constant \(1.754877666\ldots\) although only \(k+2\) shortest absent subsequences exist.
