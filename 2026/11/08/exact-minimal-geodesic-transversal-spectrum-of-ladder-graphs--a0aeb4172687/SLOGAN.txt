For every two-column ladder \(P_h\square K_2\), all inclusion-minimal geodesic transversals have two or three vertices: exactly \(3h\) have size two, and exactly \(h(h-4)(h-5)/3\) have size three for \(h\ge4\), with no triples for \(h=2,3\). Three-vertex irredundant transversals first appear at six rungs, despite the minimum transversal number remaining two.
