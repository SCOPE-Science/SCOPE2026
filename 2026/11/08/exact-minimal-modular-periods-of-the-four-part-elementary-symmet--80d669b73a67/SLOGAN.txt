The weighted count of partitions into exactly four positive parts has a purely periodic residue sequence modulo every positive integer, with an explicit least period at every prime power. The period is \(3\cdot2^{r+3}\) modulo \(2^r\), \(54\) modulo \(3\), \(4\cdot3^{r+2}\) modulo \(3^r\) for \(r\ge2\), \(12\cdot5^{r+1}\) modulo \(5^r\), and \(12p^r\) modulo prime powers \(p^r\) for \(p\ge7\); composite moduli use least common multiples.
