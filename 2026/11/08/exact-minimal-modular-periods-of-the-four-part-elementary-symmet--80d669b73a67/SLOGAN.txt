For the pair-product sum over partitions into exactly four positive parts, the least period modulo \(2^r\) is \(48\cdot 2^{r-1}\). Modulo 3 it is 54, but from modulus 9 onward the least period modulo \(3^r\) is \(4\cdot 3^{r+2}\). In particular the least period modulo 18 is 1296, not the conjectural 1246.
