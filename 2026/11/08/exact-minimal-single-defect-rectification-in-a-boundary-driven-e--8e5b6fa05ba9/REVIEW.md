# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claim is an exact stationary response theorem for a specified irreducible four-site continuous-time exclusion chain. A normalized equilibrium law, two finite Poisson equations, and a symbolic rational-function identity recover the full quadratic coefficient. Positivity of the explicit polynomial coefficients proves the universal sign; reflection proves the minimal length. The bundled checker replays the crucial computation. Finite spot checks are supplementary rather than proof of the universal statement.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Sakai–Akimoto (arXiv:2609.03560, 2026-09-03) introduced the boundary-driven trap-disordered model and obtained approximate nonlinear current response with a necessary symmetry condition, not the exact all-contrast finite-size sufficiency theorem established here. The companion external-field model (arXiv:2512.03316) and Chou's pore transport (cond-mat/9708223) were compared at the available material level. Broader exact finite-pore treatments remain a possible priority risk; later sections of the 2026 primary text and Chou's full paper were not exhaustively available. Published-record searches did not reveal an exact equivalent; this does not certify uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** The first possible single-trap rectifier with symmetric clean boundaries is a natural finite statistical-mechanics system. Establishing strict nonlinear current asymmetry for every trap contrast, together with a universal sign and the minimum system size, resolves a motivated finite-state mechanism without relying on a mean-field closure. The result is deliberately restricted to half-filled reservoirs, equal exchange rates, and one defect.

**Scientific limits.** No claim about arbitrary disorder configurations, arbitrary mean density, global finite-gradient sign, or thermodynamic-size persistence. No independent validation or publication-priority audit.
