For unequal positive run lengths \(a\ne b\), every deletion-minimal common supersequence of \(0^a1^b\) and \(1^b0^a\) has one of two explicitly counted central-run forms. Their full length spectrum has a one-branch/two-branch threshold, exactly one shortest word, and \(2\binom{a+b-2}{a-1}^2\) longest words.
