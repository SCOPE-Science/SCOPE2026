# Review of the exact three-Hermite ambiguity design

## Correctness

PASS. The result reduces the normalized ambiguity functions to the first three Laguerre polynomials. A single algebraic contact radius gives the universal upper bound; the strict inequality for the middle polynomial excludes every positive middle weight. The exact positive sum-of-squares factorization proves the all-radius lower bound, and the contact derivative forces the unique optimizer. The checker verifies this identity in the cubic quotient ring and tests the isolation and sign arguments. No numerical mesh is used as an infinite-domain proof.

An unidentified stronger result in weighted polynomial minimax theory remains possible.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Window selection is essential in Hermite-based multiwindow phase retrieval because each ambiguity function vanishes on circles. The present exact optimization quantifies a natural, smallest consecutive three-window design without a Gaussian order-zero window; it proves that spending any exposure on the intermediate order is strictly suboptimal, identifies the unique optimal active pair, and gives an exact sharp stability margin and bottleneck circle. The statement is narrower than general inverse stability but is a complete natural design fact.

## Boundaries

The mathematical constant controls the ambiguity multiplier relative to the Gaussian envelope, not an unweighted inversion bound. The exposure simplex permits a zero weight, and the optimum is not attained when all three weights must be strictly positive. More general Hermite families and finite-radius optimization remain outside scope. Bibliographic priority remains provisional pending broader independent review.
