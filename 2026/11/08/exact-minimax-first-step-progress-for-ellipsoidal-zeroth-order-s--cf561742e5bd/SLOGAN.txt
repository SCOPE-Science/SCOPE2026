For the first normalized POEM-ES step with covariance eigenvalues \(1,\delta\) on a two-dimensional unit-slope linear objective, the worst expected objective decrease is asymptotic to \(2\sqrt2\,\sqrt{\delta\log(1/\delta)}/\pi\). The least-favorable direction has strong-eigenvector component asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/\log(1/\delta)}\), rather than being exactly a weak eigenvector.
