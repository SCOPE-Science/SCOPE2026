For an exact two-coordinate block descent on a frustrated three-variable quadratic, the globally minimax omitted-coordinate probabilities have the closed form \(p^*\propto(d(1+d),c^2+d,c^2+d)\). Standard determinant-proportional pair sampling is uniform yet strictly inferior whenever \(c<d\), with a sharp limiting \(4/3\) factor in worst-direction expected energy retention.
