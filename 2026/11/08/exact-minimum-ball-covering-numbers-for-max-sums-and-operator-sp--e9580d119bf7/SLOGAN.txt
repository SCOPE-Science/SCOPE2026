The smallest number of origin-avoiding balls covering the unit sphere of a finite real max-sum is exactly the sum of the component minima, and every optimal covering is forced to localize to individual coordinate spheres. Consequently, \(\kappa(\mathcal L(\ell_1^m(\mathbb R),\ell_2^n(\mathbb R)))=m(n+1)\), an exact excess of \(m-1\) over the dimension-only lower bound.
