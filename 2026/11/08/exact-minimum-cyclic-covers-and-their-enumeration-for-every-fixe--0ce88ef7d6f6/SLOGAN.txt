For every \(q\ge2\), \(n\ge2\), and \(1\le k\le n\), the minimum number of circular tracks covering all length-\(n\) words with exactly \(k\) nonzero coordinates is the number \(N(n,k)\) of binary weight-\(k\) necklaces. The exact number of optimal covers is \(s^{s^{k-1}\binom{n-1}{k-1}-kN(n,k)}((s-1)!)^{s^{k-1}\binom{n-1}{k-1}}\), where \(s=q-1\), derived from an exact arborescence count in each support component.
