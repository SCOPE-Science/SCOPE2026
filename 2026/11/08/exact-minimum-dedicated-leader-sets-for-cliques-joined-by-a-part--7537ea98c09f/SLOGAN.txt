For two \(m\)-vertex cliques linked by \(k\) independent matching edges, every minimum dedicated-leader set for the fixed Laplacian is classified and counted exactly when \(m\ge3\) and \(2\le k\le m\). Remarkably, completing the matching leaves both the minimum number of leaders and the total number of optimal placements unchanged, while most optimal sets in some instances are not zero-forcing sets.
