# Scientific assessment and corrected priority boundary

## Correctness — PASS

For a cycle labeling with t twos, o ones, and z zeros, z<=2t gives w>=ceil(2n/3). At equality, elementary integer bounds leave one defender/one/zero-count triple in residue classes 0 and 1, and exactly two in residue 2. Saturation enforces cyclic gap lengths 3/4/5 with at most two extra ones; the all-zero-internal alternative has one gap of length 2. The complementary-defense argument establishes that every ordinary Roman minimizer at n>=6 is globally Roman, not merely that some global configurations attain the lower bound. Two exceptional length-four gaps yield floor(m/2) distinct dihedral orbits. The small cycles 3,4,5 are checked separately. An fresh direct enumeration of all ternary labelings through n=12 and all their dihedral images confirms the stated minima, labelled counts and orbits. Finite tests do not prove the unbounded result.

## Originality — PASS for the narrowed claim, with an essential correction

The **labelled minimum counts** of Roman functions on cycles were published by Deepak G., Alwardi and Indiramma in 2021, Theorem 2.4(ii)–(iv). Since the displayed proof establishes equality of the ordinary and global minimum sets for n>=6, those formulae are prior coverage despite the distinct terminology. They must not be advertised as new. The surviving result is the complete cyclic gap and **dihedral orbit** classification, including its floor(m/2) symmetry parameter, the small global exceptions, and the ordinary/global optimizer-set comparison. This distinction is substantive: a polynomial coefficient or total number does not by itself determine the group orbit decomposition. The 2021 paper's inspected complete parsed text, its counting formulas and proof, and its table do not state the complete orbit theorem. Roman Census treats coordinatewise-minimal Roman functions; Shi–Koh gives graph-general algorithms; Reddy and colleagues study global Roman complexity. None of the inspected primary results implies the dihedral formula without this new structural analysis. Older unindexed cyclic bracelet classifications remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The objects are all minimum graph-and-complement Roman defenses on the fundamental family of cycles; quotienting by graph automorphisms is a natural exact invariant. A complete classification distinguishes one- and two-exception mechanisms and identifies the exact number of symmetry-inequivalent optimizing deployment shapes for all lengths, not just sample counts. Though elementary, the family-wide orbit classification is not a numerical recomputation of the known labelled coefficient.
