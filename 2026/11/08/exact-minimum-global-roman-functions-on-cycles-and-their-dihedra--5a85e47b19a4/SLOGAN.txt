Minimum global Roman functions on simple cycles have a complete dihedral gap classification: for n=3m+2>=8, two single-exception types and floor(m/2) two-exception types yield exactly 2+floor(m/2) symmetry orbits. The ordinary-cycle labelled minimum counts are previously known (Deepak G., Alwardi and Indiramma, 2021); the contribution here is the gap/dihedral classification and complement-feasibility comparison.
