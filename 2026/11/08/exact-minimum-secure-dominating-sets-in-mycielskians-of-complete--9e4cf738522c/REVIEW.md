# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses exact true-twin class occupancies for the Mycielskian of a complete bipartite graph. Its domination clauses and admissible guard exchanges are equivalent to the vertex-level definition. The necessity argument analyzes all secure sets with at most five guards, rules out occupancy four in one class, separates apex-present and apex-absent cases, and derives exactly six universal and up to two size-four boundary profiles. Every remaining profile is checked against all five guard-attack classes. Binomial choices yield the stated labelled multiplicity, and the argument independently proves there is no solution of size four or less. All parameters have the stated ranges.

## Originality — PASS, provisional priority
Bhutani, Christiana, and Ulrickson, *On the Secure Domination of Mycielskian Graphs* (arXiv:2603.17897, 2026-03-18), Theorem 3.3, already determine the scalar secure-domination number five for two bipartition classes of size at least four. The present claim explicitly excludes that scalar fact and establishes instead every optimal guard-placement profile and its labelled multiplicity. Related secure-domination polynomials for complete multipartite graphs do not cover this graph: every such Mycielskian contains an induced five-cycle. Related chain-graph results concern bipartite graphs; this graph is not bipartite. Searches of the accessible mathematical literature did not identify the exact classification, but complete historical priority has not been independently established. Some older source full texts were not inspected.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete classification covers the entire natural regime in which the initiating paper's minimum is five, not an arbitrarily selected finite slice. It explains six universal classes of optimal guard placements and two genuinely exceptional classes occurring exactly at a shore size of four. The explicit counts quantify redundancy of optimum defensive placements and distinguish configurations invisible to the minimum-value theorem alone.

## Limitations
Ordinary Mycielskians, two shores each of order at least four, and minimum secure dominating sets only. No result is asserted for other graph operators, smaller shores, or all-cardinality secure-domination polynomials. Historical priority remains provisional and mathematical review is not independent.
