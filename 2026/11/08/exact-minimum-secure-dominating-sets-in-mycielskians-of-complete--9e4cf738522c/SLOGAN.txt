Every minimum secure dominating set of the ordinary Mycielskian of \(K_{a,b}\), for \(a,b\ge4\), has one of six universal five-class guard profiles, plus one exceptional profile when either shore has size four. This yields an exact labelled count \(a^2b^2+a^2\binom b2+b^2\binom a2+(a+b+1)\binom a2\binom b2+\mathbf1_{\{a=4\}}b\binom a3+\mathbf1_{\{b=4\}}a\binom b3\), refining the known minimum size of five.
