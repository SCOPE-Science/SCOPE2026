For every integer base \(b\ge3\) and depth \(k\ge1\), the finite generalized Cantor word \(\mu_b^k(1)\), where \(\mu_b(1)=10^{b-2}1\) and \(\mu_b(0)=0^b\), has minimum string-attractor size exactly \(\max\{2,k\}\). An explicit optimal set of zero-based positions is given, with matching disjoint-occurrence lower-bound witnesses at every depth.
