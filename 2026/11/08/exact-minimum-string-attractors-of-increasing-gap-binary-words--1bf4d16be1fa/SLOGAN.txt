For every finite binary word \(W=1\,0^{g_1}1\cdots0^{g_k}1\) with strictly increasing positive zero gaps, the exact smallest string-attractor size is \(\gamma(W)=\lceil k/2\rceil+1\), regardless of the gap lengths. Explicit optimal positions and a sharp parity-sensitive lower bound prove this for every \(k\ge1\).
