On the nonbipartite triangular cylinder \(C_n\square K_3\), the number of minority-rule fixed states has a complete exact spectral formula for every \(n\ge3\). These locally stable antiferromagnetic configurations grow as \(\lambda^n+O(3^n)\), where \(\lambda\approx3.6690790883\) is the unique root greater than three of \(X^3-3X^2-3X+2\), giving exact entropy per vertex \(\log(\lambda)/3\).
