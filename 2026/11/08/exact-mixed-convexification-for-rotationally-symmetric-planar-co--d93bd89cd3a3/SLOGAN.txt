For the vertices of a regular \(n\)-gon, every \(n\)-fold rotationally invariant planar convexifier has an exact finite-convexification threshold given by the larger of a central gauge obstruction and a support-edge length ratio; without the requisite flat face, no finite dilation works. For two regular polygons this gives a sharp divisibility-and-rotation resonance and explicit thresholds, with arbitrarily small angular misalignment changing the answer from finite to infinite.
