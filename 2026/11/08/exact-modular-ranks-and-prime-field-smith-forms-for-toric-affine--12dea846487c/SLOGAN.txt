A toric finite-field line X-ray matrix is invertible over \(\mathbb Q\) for every \(q\ge3\), yet its rank modulo the field characteristic is exactly \(\bigl(p(p+1)/2\bigr)^f-2\) for odd \(p\) and one lower for \(p=2\). For prime \(p\ge5\), its complete integer Smith diagonal consists only of \(1\), \(p\), and a final \(p(p-2)\), separating exact rational recovery from arithmetic information loss.
