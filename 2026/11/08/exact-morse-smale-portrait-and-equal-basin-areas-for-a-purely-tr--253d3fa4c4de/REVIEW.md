The full quotient vector field is reconstructed in angular variables. The map from the quotient to the three-phase constraint torus has determinant \(3\), and all common-sine triples on that target torus are enumerated. Their exact Jacobian eigenvalues are strictly away from zero. The Lyapunov identity yields global convergence; equality of all saddle potential levels excludes saddle connections; the order-three deck symmetry and the measure-zero stable manifolds give exactly \(4\pi^2/3\) area for each sink basin. The standalone checker verifies every stationary lift and spectrum. Finite computations are not used to prove infinite-time behavior.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete portrait of the simplest symmetric pure-triadic three-clock motif distinguishes synchronized and two splay attractors. In addition to hyperbolic index counts and absence of periodic recurrence, the theorem provides an exact nontrivial basin area, relevant as a rigorous finite-size reference for interaction-driven synchronization and basin studies. Neither the classification nor exact basin fraction is claimed to extend to general hypergraphs.
