For every radix \(b\ge2\), modulus \(m\ge2\), and shift \(c\ge0\), the exact minimal MSD-first automaton size of \(n\mapsto\nu_b(n+c+1)\bmod m\) is the cardinality of an explicit finite set of valuation signatures generated by a short radix trie of \(\lceil(c+1)/b^j\rceil\). This yields a constructive minimal automaton and an \(O((\log(c+2))^2)\) bound for fixed \(b,m\), without enumerating infinite input words.
