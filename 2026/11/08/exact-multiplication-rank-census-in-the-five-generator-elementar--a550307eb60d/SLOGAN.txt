In the group algebra \(k[C_2^5]\) over any characteristic-two field, principal multiplication has exactly the ten ranks \(\{0,1,2,4,6,8,10,12,16,32\}\), with a closed counting polynomial for every finite field. The first genuinely inhomogeneous rank jump occurs when a decomposable quadratic term \(ab\) acquires the disjoint cubic term \(cde\), increasing its rank from \(8\) to \(10\).
