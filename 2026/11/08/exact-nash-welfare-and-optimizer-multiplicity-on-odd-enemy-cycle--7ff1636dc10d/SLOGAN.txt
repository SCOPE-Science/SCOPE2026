For symmetric enemies-aversion coalition games whose enemy graph is the odd cycle \(C_{2k+1}\), the Nash optimum vanishes on three and five agents, but for every \(k\ge3\) it is attained exactly by independent coalitions of sizes \((k,k-1,2)\). The full product formula and exact count of maximizing partitions follow from sharp majorization and the structure of maximum independent sets on an odd cycle.
