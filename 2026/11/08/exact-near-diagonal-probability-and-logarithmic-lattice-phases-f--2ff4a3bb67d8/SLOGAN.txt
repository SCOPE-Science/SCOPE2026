For the explicit stationary two-point law of a randomly driven affine interval map, the probability of separation below \(\varepsilon\) has an exact finite-scale formula and a sharp trichotomy: linear decay, a critical \(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)\) law, or a power law with a nonconstant log-periodic amplitude. The formula resolves the leading scale, critical constant, and all normalized subsequential limits without assuming uniqueness of the stationary law.
