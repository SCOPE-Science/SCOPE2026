For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and positive universality index \(k\), the complete necklace and primitive-orbit census of shortest circular scattered-subsequence-universal words is given by an explicit composition inclusion–exclusion and two divisor sums. The number of binary circular classes equals the ordinary binary length-\(k\) necklace count minus one, while all-alphabet class counts grow like \((q!)^k/k\).
