# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The two-prescribed-image completion count is proved by inclusion-exclusion on the common remaining domain/codomain labels. Splitting the joint-excedance event according to whether the first image equals the second position yields a closed two-point formula; the one-point conjugation symmetry and subfactorial recurrence then give the exact negative covariance. Both boundary determinisms and positivity of the coefficient are covered. An independent brute-force count through nine labels and exact rational identities through eighty labels passed 579 assertions. The universal result rests on the proof, not the bounded computations.

## Originality — PASS (with priority risk)
The closest inspected work includes Ehrenborg–Steingrímsson (2000, unrestricted full excedance sets, Sections 2 and 6) and the total derangement excedance polynomial (Kim–Zeng, author preprint, pp. 1–6), constrained descent sets (Eriksen–Freij–Wästlund, arXiv:0811.1925, introductory sections and the correlation theorem), and aggregate derangement exceedance expansions (Shin–Zeng, arXiv:1411.6184, introduction, Theorem 2 and connected sections). Their displayed results do not entail the positional two-event covariance from the stated aggregate parameters alone. Searches also checked published records and analogous unrestricted-permutation claims. An unlocated exact multivariate excedance-set enumeration could imply this claim, so historical priority is not certified.

## Value — PASS
The result supplies an exact dependence matrix for the full family of positional excedance indicators under a natural constrained-permutation null. Its universal strict negative sign and exact negative Brownian-bridge shape give more information than the familiar scalar derangement Eulerian moments and provide a reusable covariance input for linear positional statistics and asymptotic fluctuation analysis.

## Scope and limitations
The proof is for pairwise covariance and its fixed interior-pair asymptotics, not negative association or a process limit. Literature checks cannot establish global uniqueness.
