For a uniform \(2n\)-step simple random-walk bridge, the number of returns to zero is strictly negatively correlated with the squared positive-occupation imbalance for every \(n\ge2\), with exact covariance \(-n(n-1)4^n/(12\binom{2n}{n})\). The correlation approaches the nonzero constant \(-\sqrt{5\pi}/(8\sqrt{4-\pi})\), revealing a persistent bridge dependence missed by the uniform Chung–Feller occupation marginal.
