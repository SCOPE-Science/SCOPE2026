For every odd prime power \(q\), the rank metric on three-by-three symmetric matrices has generalized roundness exactly the root of \(q^2(3^t-2^t)=1\). At this exponent the optimal Euclidean embedding dimension falls from \(q^6-1\) to \((q^3-1)(q^2+1)\), with the entire rank-three Fourier sector and no other sector becoming singular.
