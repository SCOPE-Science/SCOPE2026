# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reconstructs every Fourier coefficient from two exact incidence counts on two-planes, not from a guessed table. The proof explicitly treats characteristic two, verifies the size of each frequency orbit, and proves the unique positive crossing by derivative signs rather than numerical sampling. Schoenberg double centering gives the exact embedding dimension. A self-contained checker performs exact Walsh transforms at **every** frequency on the 32768-point binary example and verifies the general prime-power polynomial formulas numerically for six field sizes. Remaining risk is normal same-author review without a separate expert audit; enumeration does not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS under bounded source inspection

A recoverable claim-level search of earlier local results identified rectangular rank-distance thresholds and Fourier analysis of alternating-form random walks, but none has the same alternating-six-form metric and threshold. Semantic searches of published mathematical records were compared to actual statements. The classical Delsarte–Goethals association scheme already contains related alternating-form harmonic analysis and may yield the spectral inputs as a corollary; the new claim is the exact sharp negative-type crossing, endpoint embedding rank, and large-field asymptotic, not that older eigenmatrix. General lower bounds of Weston and the arbitrary finite-metric algorithm of Sánchez do not state the closed alternating-form result. The complete 1975 Delsarte–Goethals paper was not accessible and could explicitly subsume it: no claim to exhaustive historical priority is made. The all-dimensions alternating scheme is not by itself asserted to prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This determines the exact generalized roundness of the first full three-distance alternating matrix metric across *every* finite field, a natural test case for spectral harmonic analysis and finite negative-type geometry. The surprising \(q^{-7}\) threshold and exact endpoint Hilbert dimension quantify a natural loss of Euclidean representability. Neither is an arbitrary slice parameter or just an isolated computation. The proof's three coherent Fourier branches explain why rank four rather than rank two or rank six governs the threshold.

## Limitations and references
