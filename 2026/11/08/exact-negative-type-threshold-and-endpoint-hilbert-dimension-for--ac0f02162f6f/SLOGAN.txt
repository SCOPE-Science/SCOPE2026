The rank metric on alternating \(6\times6\) matrices over every finite field has an exact, uniquely determined negative-type threshold \(\beta_q\), with \(\beta_q\sim(q^7\log(3/2))^{-1}\). At the threshold its minimal squared-Euclidean embedding dimension drops by exactly the number of rank-four alternating frequencies, and a complete three-sector Fourier calculation proves the result.
