In the finite partisan voter model, the neutral degree-weighted consensus probability survives any strict nonzero partisan bias for every initial opinion state **if and only if** every contact joins opposite intrinsic preferences; this is also exactly when any nonconstant linear opinion martingale can exist. An arbitrarily large star with one initially positive hub then reaches positive consensus with probability \(1/2\) even as the expected time to the first opinion change diverges when bias approaches its frozen limit.
