For every nonbinary cyclic alphabet and dimension at least four, the sharp negative-type exponent of the Hamming metric capped at three is the unique zero of a single Fourier multiplier at character weight \(k=\lfloor((q-1)n+3)/q\rfloor\); at the endpoint, that entire harmonic layer and no other disappears from the Euclidean embedding. The exponent has distinct sharp large-dimension and large-alphabet scaling laws.
