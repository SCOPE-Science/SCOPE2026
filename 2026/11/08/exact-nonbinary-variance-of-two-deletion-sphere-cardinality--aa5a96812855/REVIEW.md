# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The theorem fixes uniform independent \(q\)-ary input and exact two-deletion output **sets**. The recurrence for appending a symbol proves the pointwise cardinality formula and explicitly handles singleton and repeated terminal runs. Independent modular differences produce iid Bernoulli boundaries, while conditional nonzero increment choices make the backtracking indicators independent. Orthogonality of the centered linear and quadratic terms proves the exact variance for every \(q\ge3,n\ge2\); separately enumerated raw deletion sets verify finite cases and the \(n=2\) boundary. The proof is not dependent on numerical interpolation.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The closest public mathematical research record already gives the \(q=2\) variance, which is explicitly excluded from the new claim. Swart–Ferreira (2003) and Mercier–Khabbazian–Bhargava (2008) treat deterministic deletion counts, and Liron–Langberg (2012) and Li–Wang–Fu (2026) examine deletion-ball extrema or run-structured bounds. Their inspected material and the actual research searches did not supply the evaluated variance for a uniform alphabet of size \(q\ge3\). The 2008 article's complete text was inaccessible; its deterministic nonbinary formulas are assumed as a strong antecedent, not dismissed. Novelty is best-knowledge, not independently certified.

**Value — PASS.** Nonbinary deletion channels, particularly \(q=4\) for DNA sequences, have nonuniform numbers of possible outputs even at a fixed input length. The exact variance gives the previously unavailable finite-length fluctuation and leading cubic coefficient throughout the nonbinary range, beyond extremal bounds and first moments. It is a natural second moment of a standard synchronization-channel statistic; it does not claim an immediate rate improvement or full sphere-size distribution.

**Closest work and limits.** Liron–Langberg, arXiv:1202.1644 (2012-02-08); Swart–Ferreira, DOI 10.1109/TIT.2002.806155 (2003); Mercier–Khabbazian–Bhargava, DOI 10.1109/TIT.2008.924730 (2008); Li–Wang–Fu, arXiv:2601.06493 (2026); the 2026 binary two-deletion variance research note. Full-text inspection of Mercier et al. was prevented by institutional-access availability. Biased sources, more deletions, and higher moments remain outside the proof.
