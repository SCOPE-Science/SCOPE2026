For a uniform \(q\)-ary word with \(q\ge3\), the exact variance of the number of distinct words surviving two deletions is an explicit cubic polynomial in word length, with leading term \((q-1)^3n^3/q^4\). A run-boundary decomposition and independent conditional reversal indicators turn the nonbinary calculation into orthogonal Bernoulli moments; the previously known binary formula is recovered without being claimed as new.
