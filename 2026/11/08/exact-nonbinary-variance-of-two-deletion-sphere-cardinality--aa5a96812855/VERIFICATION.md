The primary argument is an induction for the exact two-deletion cardinality, followed by a conditional binomial law and an orthogonal expansion of centered run-boundary indicators. The variable \(q\) ranges over every integer at least three and \(n\) ranges over every integer at least two. Exact enumeration verifies pointwise identities and moment formulas on finite nonbinary alphabets and lengths, but is not used to extrapolate the theorem.

Run `python3 verify_deletion_moments.py` and `python3 verify_conditional.py` in the folder containing the extracted files. Both use only the Python standard library, with rational arithmetic for moment comparisons. External human verification and historical-priority checks have not been performed. The full text of the relevant 2008 Mercier–Khabbazian–Bhargava article was not accessible for inspection.
