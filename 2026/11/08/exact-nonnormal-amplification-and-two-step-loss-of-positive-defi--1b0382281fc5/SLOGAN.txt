For exact frozen-Sylvester square-root refinement on a two-by-two positive-definite family, the true fixed-point Jacobian always has spectral radius \(t<1\) while its Frobenius operator norm and the first-step square-root error amplification diverge with anisotropy. Even as the initial relative root error tends to zero, the second iterate is indefinite once \(K>2\sqrt2/t^2\).
