For the minimal-embedding smooth-normalization toric family, the conductor is determined by the maximal remaining numerical-semigroup gap distance, and the exact normalization defect is a gap-weighted binomial sum. When the coprime generators are consecutive, it collapses to \(\delta=(\prod_i\lambda_i)\binom{n+s-1}{s+1}\), refining the explicit two-sided bounds in the closest earlier study of these monomial maps.
