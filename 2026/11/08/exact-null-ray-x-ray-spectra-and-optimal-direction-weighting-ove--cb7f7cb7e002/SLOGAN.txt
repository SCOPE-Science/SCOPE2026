Affine X-ray measurements restricted to null directions of a nondegenerate quadratic form over every odd finite field have an exact, at-most-four-level Fourier singular spectrum and lose information precisely in dimension \(3\). In dimension \(4\), nonsplit geometry has condition number \(\sqrt{q^2+1}\), far worse than the split value \(\sqrt{q+1}\), and no nonnegative scalar weighting constant along each direction improves either sharp bound.
