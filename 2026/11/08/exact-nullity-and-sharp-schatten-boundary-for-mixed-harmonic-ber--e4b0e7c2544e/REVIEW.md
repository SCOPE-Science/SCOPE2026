# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The statement assumes \(\alpha>-1\) and distinct positive integer degrees. Projection of monomials gives the complete three-case bilateral weight formula; adjunction turns the commutator into a single weighted shift. The moment sequence is strictly log-convex for every permitted weight. Pairwise majorization then proves strict positivity of both upper coefficient regimes and an exact zero interval, while the known reflection symmetry supplies the negative tail. This settles the exact kernel dimension and paired singular-value multiplicity. A second-order gamma expansion produces the positive tail constant \((\alpha+1)mn\); a two-tail counting function, rather than finite enumeration, proves the sorted singular-value and Schatten endpoint assertions. Diagnostic code checks selected finite instances but does not certify the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The source of September 2026 proves commutativity rigidity for analytic/co-analytic Toeplitz operators, not the quantitative Schatten and kernel theorem. The 2012/2013 quasihomogeneous paper already provides zero-reflection symmetry, which is credited. The 2014 finite-rank classification does not give a sharp infinite-rank singular-value law; the 2021 weighted harmonic Bergman paper characterizes commutation, semicommutation, and normality, not these mixed-monomial singular numbers. Accessible related research records on weighted Bergman composition commutators and unweighted **equal-degree** harmonic Bergman selfcommutators are different objects/parameter regimes and do not imply the unequal-degree theorem as stated. Complete older preprint and journal material, not snippets alone, was inspected for the closest primary works. Residual risk remains from broader classical commutator asymptotic results, especially if an index theorem supplies the same nullity; search failure is not proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2026 noncommutativity rigidity question motivates quantitative information for its simplest nontrivial unequal-degree pairs. Exact finite kernel and co-kernel dimensions are operator invariants absent from merely noncommuting conclusions; the symmetric paired singular spectrum and its sharp coefficient determine optimal approximation rates, the critical weak-Schatten class, and the logarithmic spectral sum for all power weights. This is a natural complete family classification, not an arbitrary finite numerical slice. It does not resolve general analytic-symbol commutators or claim a uniform asymptotic remainder.
