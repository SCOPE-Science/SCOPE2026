For unequal positive monomial degrees \(m,n\), the co-analytic/analytic Toeplitz commutator on every weighted harmonic Bergman space \(h^2_\alpha\), \(\alpha>-1\), has kernel dimension exactly \(|m-n|+1\) and paired nonzero singular values with \(N^2s_N\to4(\alpha+1)mn\). Consequently its Schatten membership has the exact cutoff \(p>1/2\), with a sharp critical logarithmic coefficient \(2\sqrt{(\alpha+1)mn}\).
