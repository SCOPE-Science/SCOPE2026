# Mathematical review

**Correctness: PASS.** The supporting quadratic form is reduced exactly to an integral of positive two-frequency Hermitian matrices. Long paired-frequency blocks achieve the corresponding largest eigenvalues asymptotically, and elementary strict concavity gives the sharp switching criterion. The commuting-shift argument identifies essential and ordinary closures. Strict uniqueness of the frequency maximizer excludes attainment; published convexity then gives the open interior. The finite-section checker passes 390 finite diagnostic assertions but is not substituted for these proofs.

**Originality: PASS (qualified).** The closest full-text sources [1] and [2] establish general geometry and convexity, but no explicit support formula for this shift-plus-coordinate-conjugation operator. The purely antilinear theorem [3] yields disk geometry and does not imply the two-component support law. The related finite-dimensional and compact-antilinear literature was checked at abstract level where applicable; more general real-linear Toeplitz results remain a publication-priority risk. No claim of independently certified uniqueness is made.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** This gives an all-parameter closed-form convex-body classification for the most elementary genuinely mixed shift/conjugation example, with a sharp angular transition, circular and noncircular boundary regimes, and a complete open-versus-essential boundary-attainment distinction. In particular the shape is neither the disk of the pure shift nor the disk of pure conjugation. The result is not a generic real-linear operator theorem.
