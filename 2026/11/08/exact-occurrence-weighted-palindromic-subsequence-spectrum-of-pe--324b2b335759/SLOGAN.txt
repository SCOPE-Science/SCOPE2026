For every permutation word repeated \(k\) times, the number of index-set occurrences of palindromic subsequences at every length has an exact binomial formula and a rational generating function \(\frac{qzt(1-t+zt)}{(1-t)((1-t)^2-z^2t(q-1+t))}\). The longest such subsequences have length \(2k-1\), and precisely \(q(q-1)^{k-1}\) positional embeddings attain it, each yielding a different word.
