For the real affine least-squares projector on the unit sphere with one added point mass, the exact sharp \(L^\infty\) norm reduces to at most six real roots of an explicit polynomial. The antipode becomes a strict local normer at mass weight \(1/3\) but is not globally norming there; at a distinct later weight it coexists with an off-antipodal global normer before becoming uniquely globally norming near weight one.
