Among length-\(n\) palindromes over a \(q\)-symbol alphabet, a one-deletion-correcting code has sharp maximum size \(q^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) for every \(n\ge1\); for even lengths all palindromes work, while for odd lengths fixed-center families attain the bound. At odd length \(2m+1\), the full confusability graph has exactly \(q(q^{m+1}-1)/2\) edges and a recursive decomposition into \(q\) smaller copies joined only at alternating-palindrome pairs.
