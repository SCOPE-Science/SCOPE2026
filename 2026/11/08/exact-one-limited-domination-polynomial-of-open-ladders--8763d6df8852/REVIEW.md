# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The sixteen-state transfer gives a bijection between admissible labelled subsets and boundary-tested column sequences. The standalone exact polynomial certificate checks an annihilating matrix polynomial of degree sixteen, the sixteen residuals that force the order-five recurrence, and every coefficient of the rational numerator. This proves the unrestricted polynomial identity. A distinct perfect-matching-crossing argument proves the minimizer classification; the simple strictly dominant pole gives the asymptotic. Brute-force subset enumerations at small sizes corroborate but do not establish infinite validity.

## Originality — PASS, provisional priority
The numerical minimum \(\gamma_1^{\mathrm L}(P_2\square P_n)=n\) follows already from A. Tepeh's 2026 Cartesian-product grid lemma and is not claimed new. Božović et al. study the parameter and general bounds but not the complete ladder weight polynomial. The full Cartesian-product preprint and relevant alternate-terminology references were compared at the level of implication, not just titles. Earlier work on circular ladders involves a trace and does not cover open-end boundary-vector coefficients. The exact bivariate rational enumerator has not been identified in inspected records; an equivalent older result under a different name remains a historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete polynomial gives every feasible cardinality count, not merely the minimum, for a natural family of Cartesian grid graphs. A short order-five rational denominator and exact total-count growth constant describe the full distribution. The separate minimizer proof classifies all optimum labelled sets, with an endpoint-parity phenomenon absent from a bare numerical bound.
