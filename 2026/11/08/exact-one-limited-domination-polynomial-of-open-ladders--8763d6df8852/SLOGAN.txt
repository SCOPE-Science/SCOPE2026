For the open ladder \(P_2\square P_n\), the weight enumerator of **all** one-limited dominating sets has an explicit rational generating function with a degree-five denominator, an exact sixteen-state algebraic certificate, and asymptotic total count \(8.17943774264\ldots(2.29237024940\ldots)^{n-2}\). Its minimum sets are precisely two whole-rail selections plus two additional alternating whole-column selections when \(n\) is even; the previously known numerical minimum is \(n\).
