# Scientific review: exact bias reversal in a one-vacancy exclusion ring

## Correctness — PASS

The stationary finite-volume claim follows from an explicit vacancy-to-nearest-neighbor-walk reduction, a directed spanning-tree normalization and the unicyclic flux identity. The numerator's antisymmetry and the independence of the degree-\(L-1\) Bernstein monomials yield an if-and-only-if coefficient palindrome. The four-site coefficient difference factors exactly into three rate equalities; denominator positivity proves the global sign of the even current. A separate exact generator computation matches the general identity on multiple small ring lengths and all claimed rational examples. This is an analytic result; its finite checks are not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The general periodic hopping drift is classical (Derrida, 1983) and excluded from the novelty claim. Sakai–Akimoto (2025) provide the site-disordered exclusion model and geometric sufficiency for bias-reversal symmetry. Their discussion expects a generic quadratic rectification outside the sufficient conditions, whereas the all-distinct-rate four-site one-vacancy profile here has exact symmetry for all biases without inversion or uniform bond bias. Earlier explicit four-site **two-particle** results cover a different occupancy sector and do not imply this four-configuration vacancy classification. The complete coefficient-palindrome criterion, all-bias factorization and sign profile were not located in these sources; publication priority remains subject to potentially unindexed work.

## Value — PASS

The result gives a necessary-and-sufficient diagnostic for when quenched trap disorder can or cannot rectify a nearly full ring. It exhibits a sharp three-to-four-site threshold and a nongeometric cancellation surface, a motivated counterexample to an emerging transport conjecture rather than an arbitrary finite arithmetic instance. The sign formula on the complementary four-site parameter domains supplies an exact natural classification.
