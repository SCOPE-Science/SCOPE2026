In the weighted Friedkin–Johnsen model, polarization plus disagreement is an exact strictly convex quadratic of \(t/(1+gt)\) when one individual's stubbornness \(t\) varies, so the global optimizer and all monotonicity regimes admit closed forms for every connected undirected network with other stubbornness levels positive. A three-node path has a strict interior optimum at \(t=1/3\) with objective \(9/28\), disproving a boundary-only rule for this quadratic social objective.
