The exact adaptive (Littlestone) dimensions of open and closed vertex-neighborhood classes are classified for every Cartesian product of finite complete graphs, including unequal coordinate alphabets. For every \(q\ge4\), the heterogeneous rook graph \(K_2\square K_q\) has open online dimension three but closed online dimension two; the sole full-product gap between previously known static VC dimension and adaptive dimension occurs at \(K_3\square K_3\).
