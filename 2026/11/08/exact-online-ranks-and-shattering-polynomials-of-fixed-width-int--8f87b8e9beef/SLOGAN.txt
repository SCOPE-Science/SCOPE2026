For exactly length-\(r\) intervals on an \(n\)-point chain, with \(m=n-r+1\) possible starts, the exact online ranks without and with the null concept are \(\min\{\lfloor\log_2m\rfloor,1+\lfloor\log_2r\rfloor\}\) and \(\min\{\lfloor\log_2(m+1)\rfloor,1+\lfloor\log_2r\rfloor\}\), and both shattering polynomials have explicit quadratic coefficients. Adding the null concept raises the online rank exactly at a feasible power-of-two boundary; at \(n=2r\) the non-null class has VC dimension one while its online rank grows logarithmically with \(r\).
