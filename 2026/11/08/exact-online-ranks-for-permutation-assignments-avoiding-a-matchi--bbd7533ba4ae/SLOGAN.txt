For edge-membership learning from perfect matchings of a complete bipartite graph with any forbidden matching, the Littlestone dimension is exactly \(n-1\) for every \(n\ge5\), even when all \(n\) diagonal edges are forbidden. At this last deletion, static VC dimension drops from \(n-1\) to \(n-2\) while the adaptive rank stays fixed, with a complete exceptional profile at smaller orders.
