The family of all nonempty balls in a full finite prefix ultrametric of height \(h\) has exact Littlestone dimension \(1+\lfloor\log_2(h+1)\rfloor\), except for the singleton and the binary height-one cases, while its VC dimension never exceeds two. The same formula gives a sharp depth threshold for stability of the ball-membership formula in ultraproducts, independent of branching.
