# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each support edge (i,j), an open-neighborhood center (a,b) selects the XOR of row and column incidences, equivalently δ_F({r_a,c_b}); overlap at (a,b) cancels. Enumerating the three edge-shapes of size two and four relevant size-three types gives the stated binomial support coefficients and sharp m,n boundary conditions, including (m,n)=(1,1),(2,2),(2,4). A connected 3-edge path and 3-edge matching cannot yield the full trace; all qualified star/2-star-plus-edge shapes have explicit eight trace witnesses. The universal no-four-points step uses Adler–Benediktsson–Macpherson Theorem 4.6 VC≤3 for open neighborhoods of Hamming graphs and the induced-subgraph trace embedding, not finite enumerations. Independent direct trace enumeration on fifteen small boards (27,810 supports) agrees; finite checks do not prove the all-order bound.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge the full bivariate labeled support-count polynomial and four-type triple classification for unequal rook graphs are not established by the existing open-neighborhood VC-bound or equal-alphabet Hamming census. Novelty is the coefficientwise rectangular trace classification, not the VC upper bound.
