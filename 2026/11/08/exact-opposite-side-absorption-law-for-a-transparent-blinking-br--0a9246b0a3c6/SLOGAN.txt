For a Brownian particle reset to the positive side of a transparent blinking target, the exact probability of eventual absorption from the negative side is \(\frac{\pi_I}{2}(1-\sqrt{r/(r+\lambda)})(1+(r/\lambda)e^{-x_0\sqrt{(r+\lambda)/D}})\). At fixed gate occupancy it increases strictly with switching speed, decreases strictly with reset rate, and approaches the nonzero fraction \(\pi_I/2\) under infinitely fast switching, despite ordinary absorption-time statistics approaching an always-active target.
