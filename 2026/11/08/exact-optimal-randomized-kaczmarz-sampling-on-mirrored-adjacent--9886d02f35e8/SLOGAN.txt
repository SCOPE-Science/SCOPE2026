For the signed adjacent-coordinate path frame, the sharp worst-case one-step randomized Kaczmarz sampling optimum has an explicit geometric edge-weight profile with value \(\big[1/c+n-2+c(1-(-c/(1-c))^{n-2})\big]^{-1}\), where \(c\) is the smaller squared row amplitude. Every unbalanced optimal sampler is unique and uses all signed rows, while the balanced case admits a sparse minimum-edge-cover optimum; optimized sampling can improve the guaranteed decrease over uniform sampling by a factor approaching \(n-1\).
