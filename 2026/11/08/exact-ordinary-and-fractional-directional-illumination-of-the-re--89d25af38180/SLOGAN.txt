The regular icosidodecahedron has ordinary directional illumination number \(4\) but fractional directional illumination number \(3\), a sharp integrality gap of \(4/3\). The fractional optimum assigns weight \(1/4\) to each of twelve icosahedral vertex-axis directions, while a sixty-four-transversal exact certificate rules out improvement or ordinary illumination by three directions.
