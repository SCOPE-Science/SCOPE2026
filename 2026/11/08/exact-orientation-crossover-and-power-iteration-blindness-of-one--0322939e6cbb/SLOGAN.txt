For one-vector, two-column randomized range finding with any number of power iterations, Gaussian and Rademacher sketches exchange expected-error advantage at the exact orientation boundary \(\sin^2(2\theta)=(p^2+1)/(p+1)^2\), where \(p=(\sigma_1/\sigma_2)^{2q+1}\). Unlike Gaussian sketches, a diagonal-coordinate sign sketch retains a positive error excess no matter how many power iterations are applied.
