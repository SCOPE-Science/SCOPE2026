For the max-plus negative-feedback oscillator, square-uniform initial states have an exact, parity-asymmetric first-capture distribution, with mean \((4m-1)/(m-1)\) in the original discrete clock. At the critical feedback threshold \(m\downarrow1\), the scaled capture time converges to the maximum of two independent exponentials, sharply contrasting with the continuum of neutral four-cycles at \(m=1\).
