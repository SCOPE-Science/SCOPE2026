For every \(n\ge1\), distinct shuffles of two copies of \((01)^n\) are exactly the balanced binary paths in the height strip \([0,2]\), numbering \(2^{2n-1}\). Shuffling \((01)^n\) with \((10)^n\) instead gives every concatenation of \(2n\) blocks from \(\{01,10\}\), numbering \(2^{2n}\); the two families intersect only in \((01)^{2n}\).
