The complete ordered two-point convolution of the inverse graph over \(\mathbb Z/2^k\mathbb Z\), for \(k\ge4\), is classified by dyadic quadratic-root fibers. Its exact Minkowski-sum image has \((q^2+40q+256)/96\) points for even \(k\) and \((q^2+32q+256)/96\) for odd \(k\), approaching density \(1/96\).
