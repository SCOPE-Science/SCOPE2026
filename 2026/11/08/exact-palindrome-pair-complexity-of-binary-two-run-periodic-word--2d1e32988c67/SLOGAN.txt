For the periodic binary word \(W=(0^a1^b)^\omega\), every factor needs at most three palindromes, and the length-\(n\) factors that need three are counted exactly by a convolution of the two strictly interior run-length ranges. All factors are palindrome pairs precisely when \(\min(a,b)=1\); otherwise the non-pair count is periodic in \(n\) after a sharp initial cutoff.
