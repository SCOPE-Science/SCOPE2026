For every binary circular word with exactly three \(1\)'s, its number of distinct nonempty palindromic factors of length at most its circumference is determined explicitly by the three zero-gap lengths. Among all such words of length \(n\ge14\), the maximum is \(2n-\lceil n/3\rceil-2\), and all optimizers are classified, with one dihedral type for \(n\equiv0,2\pmod3\) and two for \(n\equiv1\pmod3\).
