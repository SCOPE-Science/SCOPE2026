For every binary word with at most six alternating constant-letter runs, an explicit two-term formula gives its exact palindromic defect and a local-additivity law. All minimal non-rich words in this class form a three-parameter five-run family with length enumerator \(4z^8/((1-z)^2(1+z)(1-z^3))\); a seven-run minimal obstruction shows the boundary is sharp.
