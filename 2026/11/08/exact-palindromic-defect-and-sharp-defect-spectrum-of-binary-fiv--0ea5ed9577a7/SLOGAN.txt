For every five-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d0^e\), its palindromic defect is zero when \(b=d\) and is \(\max\{0,\min\{a,e\}-c\}\) otherwise. At fixed length \(n\ge5\), the attainable defects are exactly all integers from zero through \(\max\{0,\lfloor(n-6)/2\rfloor\}\), giving the first non-rich five-run example at length eight.
