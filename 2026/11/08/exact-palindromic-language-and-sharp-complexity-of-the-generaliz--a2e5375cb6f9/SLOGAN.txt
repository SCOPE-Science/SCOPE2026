The generalized base-\(b\) Cantor word (all digits \(0\) or \(b-1\), with \(b\ge3\)) has an explicit complete palindromic factor language: zero blocks, bounded single-marker palindromes, and centered factors of one palindromic block per dyadic valuation level. Its palindromic complexity satisfies \(P_b(n)\le3\) at every length, sharply infinitely often, with an exact all-length formula and complete maximal-palindrome classification.
