In binary Parikh-matrix classes, avoiding a scattered pattern \(a b^r a\) has an exact Gaussian-polynomial enumerator for every run length \(r\ge1\). For the first nontrivial case \(abbba\), each class contains at most \(1+\lfloor A/2\rfloor\) avoiding words when there are \(A\) occurrences of \(a\), and this sharp bound is attained for every \(A,B\ge2\) at the class with \(t=A\) scattered \(ab\)-pairs.
