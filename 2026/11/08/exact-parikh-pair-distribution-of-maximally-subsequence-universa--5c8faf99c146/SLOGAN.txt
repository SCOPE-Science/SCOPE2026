When one letter occurs exactly \(k\) times, the entire distribution of scattered \(ab\) counts among binary words attaining universality index \(k\) is given by \(q^{\binom{k}{2}}[z^r](1-z)^{-1}\prod_{j=1}^{k}(1+q/(1-zq^j))\), where the other letter occurs \(k+r\) times. This yields exact counts inside each binary Parikh-matrix class, a reciprocal distribution, and explicit first-edge coefficients for every \(k\ge4\) and \(r\ge0\).
