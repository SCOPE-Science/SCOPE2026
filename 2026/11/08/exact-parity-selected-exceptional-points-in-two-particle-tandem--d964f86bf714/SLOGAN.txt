The two-particle active tandem exclusion process is exactly an alternating-rate directed cycle whose generator has a size-two Jordan block if and only if the ring is even and \(\beta/\alpha=3\pm2\sqrt2\). Its staggered adjacent-pair signal crosses from infinitely many damped oscillations to at most one sign change at these sharp thresholds, even though the stationary spatial clustering crossover occurs at \(\beta/\alpha=1\).
