For the zero-field antiferromagnetic Ising model on the Mycielskian of a cycle, the exact ground-state multiplicity is \(4\) for even cycle length \(n\ge4\) but \(2n\) for odd length \(n\ge5\); all ground states have total magnetization \(\pm1\). The parity split identifies a unique movable frustration defect on triangle-free four-chromatic Mycielskian cycles, with exact energy \(-3(n-1)J\) in the odd case versus \(-3nJ\) in the even case.
