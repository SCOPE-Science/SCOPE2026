# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The exact identity expressing the Hamiltonian as the sum of row quadratics and four times the number of doubly-down reverse pairs follows by partitioning all graph edges. The parity-restricted quadratic minimum produces each proposed breakpoint. Regular-graph plus Eulerian-orientation constructions attain every positive plateau; a perfect-matching modification attains the even-order zero plateau. All equality cases at positive critical fields follow directly from this identity. Exhaustive enumeration for \(n=3,4,5\) separately confirms the proposed piecewise energy and off-critical magnetization. These finite enumerations do not replace the all-order proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence
The full-text-accessible Coffey–Trugman 1992 preprint concerns a truncated-tetrahedral quantum/classical Heisenberg model with varying bond couplings and correlation observables; its examined Hamiltonian and classical sections are materially different from the proposed uniform classical Ising field classification. The full arXiv HTML of Strečka–Karľová–Madaras 2015 treats the five Platonic solids and does not state the all-order truncated-simplex theorem. Searches for truncated-complete-graph Ising magnetization, Eulerian orientations and related family equivalences returned no published statement implying this theorem in the consulted research index; the closest research-index records concerned other graph families or observables. However, an unindexed graph-polytope or historical truncation paper could still dominate the result, so independent priority assessment is needed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Truncated simplices provide an infinite canonical family of frustrated magnetic networks with a growing number of spin sites and row cliques. The exact parity shift of the first field threshold, the full number and heights of plateaux, and the complete critical-field ground-state equality conditions are natural field-response information not available from single-cluster counts. A constructive graph-theoretic proof connects spin ground states to Eulerian orientations and regular subgraphs, which is useful for future entropy or coupling-perturbation questions. This is not a claim of finite-temperature bulk critical behavior.
