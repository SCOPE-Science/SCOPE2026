The uniform classical Ising antiferromagnet on the clique truncation of \(K_n\) has an exact zero-temperature magnetization staircase for every \(n\ge3\): transition fields are \(h/J=2,4,\ldots,n-1\) for odd \(n\) and \(h/J=1,3,\ldots,n-1\) for even \(n\). A row-sum energy identity and Eulerian-orientation constructions prove all the ground-state sectors and characterize the states at each transition.
