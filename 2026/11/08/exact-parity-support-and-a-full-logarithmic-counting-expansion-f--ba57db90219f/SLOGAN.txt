The odd values of the \(16\)-regular partition function occur exactly when \(8n+5=p^{4a+1}u^2\) for a uniquely determined prime \(p\equiv5\pmod8\) and odd \(u\) coprime to \(p\). Their count up to \(X\) has a complete logarithmic asymptotic expansion and, in particular, is asymptotic to \((\pi^2/4)X/\log X\).
