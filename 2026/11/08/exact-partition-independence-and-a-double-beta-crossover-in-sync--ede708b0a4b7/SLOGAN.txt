In a synchronous noisy voter system, the two sides of every finite bipartite graph have **exactly independent stationary configurations**, despite copying each other's opinions every round. On \(K_{a,b}\), the critical reset scale \(a^{-1}+b^{-1}\) yields two independent \(\operatorname{Beta}(2\lambda,2\lambda)\) opinion shares, separating independent fair consensus coins at lower noise from deterministic half-occupancy at higher noise.
