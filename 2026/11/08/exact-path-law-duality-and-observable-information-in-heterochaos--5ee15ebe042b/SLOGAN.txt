The stationary center coordinate of generalized heterochaos baker maps has a parameter-independent uniform marginal, but consecutive observations recover every independent branch decision and therefore distinguish different parameters with exact Bernoulli likelihoods. Reversing the observed path replaces the contraction probability \(p\) by \(1-p\); the center process is reversible exactly at the zero-Lyapunov threshold, with an explicit binomial entropy-production law.
