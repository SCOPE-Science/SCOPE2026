For the centrally punctured \(3\times3\) Fourier aperture, exact enumeration determines the sharp aperiodic peak-sidelobe level and every phase-modulation optimizer for phase alphabets of sizes two through eight. The universal sidelobe floor \(1\) is first attained with six phases, but fails again for seven and eight; the five-phase peak optimum has 240 normalized optimizers, in contrast with the much more rigid fourth-moment problem.
