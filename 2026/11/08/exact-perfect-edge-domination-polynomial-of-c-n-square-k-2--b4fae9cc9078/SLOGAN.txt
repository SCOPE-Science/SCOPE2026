The complete perfect-edge-domination polynomial of the labelled circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) is \(z^{3n}+6\mathbf{1}_{6\mid n}z^n\): apart from the full edge set, there are exactly six perfect edge dominating sets when \(6\mid n\), and none otherwise. Every nontrivial set follows one forced six-column pattern, a rigidity proved by an exact finite-state reduction.
