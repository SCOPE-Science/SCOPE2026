For independent area-uniform vertices on a hemisphere, every labelled spherical geodesic cycle of length \(n\ge3\) has exactly linear perimeter variance \(n\sigma_H^2\), where \(\sigma_H^2=0.4332351609628\ldots\) has a rapidly convergent central-binomial series with a rigorous tail bound. The same constant governs a Gaussian cycle-perimeter limit, and the spherical triangle has variance \(1.29970548288849\ldots\), strictly larger than the independent-edge prediction because its sides share a positive covariance.
