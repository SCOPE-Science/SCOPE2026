For the three-block cyclic adjacent-sum polytope with blocks of any equal size \(a\ge2\), the Ehrhart quasipolynomial has **minimum period exactly two**, and its alternating contribution has degree \(3a-3\) with leading coefficient \(1/(2^{3a+1}((a-1)!)^3)\). An exact minimum-layer decomposition proves the full reduced generating-function denominator \((1-z)^{3a+1}(1+z)^{3a-2}\).
