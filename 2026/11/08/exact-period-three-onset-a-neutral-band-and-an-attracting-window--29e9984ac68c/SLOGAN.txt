For the quarter-breakpoint Parrondo composite \(g_{t,1/4}\circ f_1\), primitive three-cycles first appear at \(t=(3+\sqrt{21})/2\) as a continuum of neutral cycles, then split into exactly two cycles for every larger admissible parameter. One of these is attracting only until the distinct branch-collision threshold \(t^2(4-t)=1\); both are repelling afterward.
