Among the five rotations by integer fifths composed with the von Neumann–Kakutani map, exactly the \(2/5\)- and \(4/5\)-rotations have periodic points; every such point has least period five. Their periodic supports consist of five explicit intervals, with Lebesgue masses \(1/4\) and \(1/2\), while the other three rotations are globally aperiodic.
