For synchronous majority updating with tie retention on every wheel \(K_1*C_n\), \(n\ge3\), there are exactly two fixed colorings, while all strict two-cycles admit an explicit all-size binomial-coefficient census determined by the one- or two-component distance-two rim structure. In particular, even-rim wheels have two additional constant-hub two-cycle orbits, and the number of hub-switching oscillators grows exponentially on the subsequence \(n=6t+3\).
