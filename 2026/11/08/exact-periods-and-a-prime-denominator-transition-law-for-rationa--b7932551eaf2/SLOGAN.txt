For every prime denominator \(p\) and rational monomial \(an^k/p\) with \(k\ge2\), the coprimality indicator \(\mathbf 1_{\gcd(n,\lfloor an^k/p\rfloor)=1}\) has an exact least period and rational density determined by the finite power-residue coset of \(a\). As \(p\) grows, all such densities uniformly approach \(1/\zeta(2)\) at the character-sum rate \(O_k((\log p)^2/\sqrt p)\).
