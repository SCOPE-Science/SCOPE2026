For a standard Brownian bridge with an independent centered Gaussian endpoint vector, the probability of staying nonnegative has an exact formula for every positive-semidefinite endpoint covariance. As the endpoint variance grows, its deficit is \((\log r)/(4\pi r\sqrt{\det C})\) for fixed positive-definite covariance, but only order \(r^{-1/2}\) for positively aligned rank-one covariance; negatively aligned rank-one endpoints give zero persistence.
