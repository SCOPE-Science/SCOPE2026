For the Petersen graph, four-colour single-vertex recolourings form a connected graph of radius \(13\) and sharp diameter \(14\), with \(44{,}400\) diametral unordered pairs and exactly eleven symmetry orbits. Its three-colour recolouring graph is \(60K_2\), whereas the four-colour graph has only two possible vertex eccentricities, \(13\) and \(14\).
