For every \(q\ge3\), the exact phase and distinct-factor counts of Abelian \(q\)-powers in \((0^a1^b)^\omega\) are determined by the shorter residue \(s=\min(k\bmod(a+b),a+b-(k\bmod(a+b)))\), with a unique two-phase half-period resonance when both run lengths are even. For nonzero block-length residues smaller than half a period, all surviving equal-count configurations must arise from consecutive monochromatic residual windows; multiples of the period allow every phase.
