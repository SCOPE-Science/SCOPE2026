# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a>0, omega>0 the bounded two-tone signal gives an absolutely convergent STFT. Explicit Fourier transformation of exp(-a|s|) produces P(y)=(8/a^2)N(t)/D(t)^2, t=y^2>=0, N=(1+c)(t+1+q)^2+4(1-c)qt, D=(t+1+q)^2-4qt>0, c=cos(2 omega x), q=(omega/a)^2. Its derivative has the stated cubic H; its t^2 coefficient is strictly negative, leading coefficient nonpositive and constant 2(1+q)^2((5+c)q-(1+c)). For q<=q*(c)=(1+c)/(5+c) a direct coefficient inequality makes H(t)<0 for t>0. For q>q* Descartes' sign rule gives exactly one positive H root: a unique symmetric pair of global maxima with a central local minimum. At q=q*>0 the first nonvanishing y derivative is negative quartic. The special c=-1 has q*=0. Uniform phase gives the exact arccosine beat occupancy, with small-q square-root law. Independent symbolic reconstruction of N,D,H and direct 30-case root classification corroborates but does not substitute for this continuous analytic proof.
