A two-tone signal analyzed with the non-Gaussian Laplace STFT window has either exactly one or exactly two frequency ridges, never three: at beat phase \(c\) the sharp split-ridge condition is \((\omega/a)^2>(1+c)/(5+c)\). The exact fraction of each beat period with two ridges is an explicit arccosine law and shrinks like \(2\sqrt2\,\omega/(\pi a)\) for closely spaced tones.
