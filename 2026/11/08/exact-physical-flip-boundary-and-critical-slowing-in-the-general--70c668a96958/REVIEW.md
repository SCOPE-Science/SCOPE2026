# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The positive-fixed-point flip is reduced to a one-positive-root quadratic; the root is bounded between \(2/3\) and \(3/4\). Exact differentiation gives the nonzero transverse multiplier slope \(-D\), and exact cubic composition gives the two-step coefficient \(-2K\) with \(K>0\). The local implicit-function argument establishes the cycle and its multiplier. Reciprocal-square summation establishes the critical coefficient; a convex decomposition proves the full interval self-map condition. The standalone symbolic and numerical verifier executes successfully, but is not itself an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS within the inspected evidence. The original 2026 map paper supplies the object, fixed points, extinction thresholds, and numerical period-doubling behavior, but not the all-parameter nonzero-equilibrium flip formula, its normal-form sign, the physical self-map guarantee, or the critical asymptotic constant. Historical claim comparisons and searches for formulas, normal-form equivalents, and stronger generic classifications did not show implication. The 2019 Rocha–Taha paper concerns generalized logistic maps and is a substantial remaining priority uncertainty: the available abstract was read but the full work was not accessible. Generic flip theory alone does not supply the sign-uniform coefficient for this family.

## Value
PASS. The source emphasizes that the Allee effect changes the period-doubling route but gives numerical regime diagrams rather than the threshold at which the active population first loses local fixed-point stability. The theorem closes that parameter-space gap with an exact two-parameter boundary, excludes subcritical flips across the biologically nonnegative wedge, and quantifies critical slowing by an explicit coefficient. This matters for separating stable population equilibrium from nascent oscillation, though it does not classify distant attractors or the strong-Allee wedge.
