For the algebraic limit cycle of a 2023 polynomial oscillator, original-time phase sampling gives an exact Cauchy tangent law and an explicit non-arcsine position density. Its period diverges as \((4-w)^{-1/2}\), while a fixed squared-position moment concentrates at rate \((4-w)^{1/2}\) and the transverse Floquet multiplier tends to zero at an explicit exponential rate.
