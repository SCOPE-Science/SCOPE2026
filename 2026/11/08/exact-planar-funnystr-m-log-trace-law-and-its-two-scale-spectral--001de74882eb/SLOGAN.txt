For a one-column Gaussian Nyström approximation of a two-eigenvalue positive matrix, applying the matrix logarithm *after* sketching has an exact Cauchy-derived error law: at large spectral contrast its typical excess above the optimal rank-one trace error is order \(a^{-1}\), but its mean excess is asymptotic to \(2\sqrt{b/a}\). Sketching the logarithm directly has a limiting nonintegrable error distribution and a mean error that diverges like \(\sqrt{\log(a)\log(1+b)}\), revealing a sharp difference between typical and average numerical reliability.
