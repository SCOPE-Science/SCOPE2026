For basic unnormalized two-probe Gaussian XNysTrace on every real positive-definite \(2\times2\) matrix, both the actual variance and the expectation of the reported squared error have exact closed spectral formulas. The reported squared error underestimates true mean-square error by a sharp factor of at most \(5/3\), while trace-estimation variance is at most \(5/8\) of the corresponding two-probe Gaussian Girard–Hutchinson variance.
