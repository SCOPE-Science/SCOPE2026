# Mathematical review

## Correctness

PASS. For each alphabet, the nonconstant character space splits orthogonally into one radial point-indicator mode and a \((q_j-2)\)-dimensional marked-point-vanishing contrast space. In each tensor block indexed by a set of contrasts, multiplication by the rectangular unobserved indicator compresses to a rank-one operator. Its unique nonzero eigenvalue is the indicated Poisson-binomial cumulative probability. Complementary projection therefore gives the entire singular spectrum, including exact multiplicities. Strict event containment establishes uniqueness of the lowest level, and the explicit low-degree expansion gives its eigenvector. The packaged checker independently constructs and diagonalizes actual finite Fourier sampling matrices in twelve heterogeneous cases, with maximum discrepancy below \(4\times10^{-15}\). The all-dimensional claim is established by proof, not extrapolation from tests.

## Originality

PASS under bounded comparison. Comparisons with related mathematical statements identified homogeneous-alphabet Hamming-sphere spectral formulas and a homogeneous first-band Hamming-ball theorem as nearest neighbors. Their statements concern a single fixed-weight shell or first-degree band and do not imply the complete heterogeneous multi-degree coordinate-hyperplane spectrum as stated without an additional tensor-product argument. Filmus (arXiv:1406.0142) develops binary Hamming-slice harmonic analysis; Vasil'eva (arXiv:1412.3622) studies reconstruction from homogeneous q-ary spheres. Their inspected article texts do not state the present exact Poisson-binomial tail condition number for unequal alphabet factors. The rank-one product-mask calculation is classical and not asserted independently new. Residual priority risk: general operator-compression literature may already contain a theorem that implies this specialization; the comparison is not exhaustive.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The observation set is a natural deterministic missing-data pattern in multivariate categorical Fourier inference: at least one coordinate must take a prescribed reference value. The result provides an exact singular spectrum for every interaction band and every heterogeneous product alphabet, a simple minimum singular value even when many other singular values coincide, an explicit worst-recoverable signal, and sharp regimes based on Poisson-binomial tails. These facts measure actual worst-case reconstruction stability for multilevel interaction models, rather than only verifying formal invertibility or a small numerical example.

## Boundaries

The theorem requires a rectangular unobserved set, fixed marked coordinate points and Fourier interaction degree less than the number of factors. Finite numerical checks are not a proof and no independent mathematical audit has been performed. Historical equivalence with very general tensor-compression results is a residual risk.
