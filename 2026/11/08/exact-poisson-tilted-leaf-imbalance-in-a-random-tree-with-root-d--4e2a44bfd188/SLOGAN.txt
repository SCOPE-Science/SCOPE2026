For a continuous-time random recursive tree with root doublings, the joint finite-time transform of the leaf imbalance \(I_t=2L_t-N_t\) and the Poisson root-doubling count is explicit. In the natural three-vertex initial state, \(\operatorname{Cov}(I_t,D_t)=-t^2\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(I_t)=3e^{2t}+4e^t-7-8t-4t^2\), quantifying dependence that is invisible in limiting degree proportions.
