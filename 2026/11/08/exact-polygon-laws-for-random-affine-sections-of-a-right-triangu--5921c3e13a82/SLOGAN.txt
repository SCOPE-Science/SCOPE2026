For a right triangular bipyramid with equilateral base circumradius one and apices at \(\pm h\), the probabilities that an isotropic uniformly random intersecting affine plane cuts out a triangle, quadrilateral, pentagon or hexagon are four explicit arctangent functions of \(h>0\). Pentagonal sections are most frequent at the unique height \(h\approx1.211448413583\), with maximal probability \(\approx0.462970871315\), whereas the hexagonal-section probability decreases strictly with height.
