For backward-Euler-started variable-step BDF2 on positive scalar decay with alternating normalized steps \(x,y>0\), every iterate is positive and strictly decreasing exactly when \(x+y<2\) and \((x-y)^2\geq4(x+y-1)\). Strong step alternation can nearly double the allowed mean normalized step relative to the equal-grid positivity frontier, while the required step ratio diverges quadratically near the limiting total step.
