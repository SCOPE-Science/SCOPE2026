For the stiff two-stage GIRK stability function \(F(z)=(1-z)/(1-2z+2z^2)\), the unit-rate absorbing birth-chain update \(F(hQ_n)\) is nonnegative for all steps in dimensions two and three, but dimensions four and five have sharp positivity-recovery thresholds \((\sqrt3-1)/2\) and \(1/\sqrt2\), respectively. On the normalized stiff window \(h\ge1/2\), five states are the first in this chain family to exhibit a negative probability, exactly \(-7/625\) at \(h=1/2\).
