The reduced \(256\)-node set of the \(3\times3\) magic-square-of-squares surface has Hilbert function \((1,9,39,109,206,256,256,\ldots)\); quintics first interpolate arbitrary values at its nodes, and its saturated ideal is \(6\)-regular. The first equations additional to the surface's quadrics are exactly two cubic classes represented by \(BMG\) and \(DME\).
