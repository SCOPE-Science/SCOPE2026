Averaging P'/P and N'/N on a strictly positive periodic orbit gives the locked prey mean N* and the covariance formula. Independently, averaging P' gives ⟨NP⟩=N*⟨P⟩. Subtracting N* times the averaged logarithmic prey equation from the averaged prey equation then gives ⟨ρ(N−N*)(1−N/K)⟩=0. Expanding this gives the weighted-variance identity. It vanishes only for N constant at N*, which makes P and ρ constant. The factor is at most one, with equality exactly at ω=Ω. The benchmark frequencies use Ω and √(Ω²−γ²/4), respectively, and are distinct for the stated parameters.
