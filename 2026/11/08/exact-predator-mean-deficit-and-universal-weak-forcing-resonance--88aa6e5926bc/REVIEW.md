# Scientific review: exact predator-mean deficit and weak-forcing resonance

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem uses the specified coexistence-region equations and positive periodic solutions. Averaging the logarithmic predator equation fixes the prey mean; averaging the logarithmic prey equation ties the predator-mean deficit to forcing–prey covariance. An exact Volterra coboundary differentiates to a positive, forcing-weighted prey variance over a full period. For nonconstant positive forcing, a vanishing variance would force the entire state and the forcing to be constant, proving strictness. The unforced Jacobian is Hurwitz for every \(0<N_*<K\), providing a unique locally attracting small-forcing periodic branch at each fixed positive frequency. Its harmonic response yields the displayed quadratic coefficient, half-period symmetry excludes odd terms, and explicit differentiation gives the unique optimum. The embedded checker passes two symbolic identities and 16 numerical shooting/integration cases. The proof does not mistake finite sampling for universal verification.

## Originality — PASS, subject to documented priority risk
The motivating full primary text (Gao et al., arXiv:2610.39113v1, including Section 2, Methods 4.1 and Appendix C) provides the autonomous Jacobian and Volterra function and shows frequency-dependent tipping, but does not give either the exact *forced* periodic mean/covariance/weighted-variance law or the universal optimal quadratic *mean-predator* response. The result is a distinct observable, not a restatement of the paper's peak/trough resonance and threshold crossings. Closely related published mean-defect results in nonlinear-Allee, delayed additive-predation, and maturation-delay systems were read and compared at the equation/implication level: those systems do not contain the same time-periodic growth factor or yield this covariance identity and resonance law without new deductions. The 2013 Taylor–Sherratt–White seasonal-forcing paper is a genuine unresolved historical-priority risk: its abstract emphasizes simulation and bifurcation rather than exact means, but complete primary text could not be inspected under current access. Search absence alone is not asserted to prove novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
For an actively studied threshold-tipping model, the finding identifies a global-in-amplitude **sign constraint** for every periodic coexistence trajectory, quantifies the precise predator cost of environmental fluctuations, and separates the unique quadratic mean-loss resonance at the undamped frequency from the paper's damped eigenfrequency and from tipping thresholds. The value is not merely numerical reproducibility or a routine substitution: the exact dissipative balance controls a biologically meaningful population mean for arbitrary positive periodic waveforms, while the universal normalized peak furnishes a sharp, parameter-independent small-forcing benchmark.
