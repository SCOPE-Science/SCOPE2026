In a periodically forced logistic predator–prey coexistence region, every positive locked cycle has exactly the unforced prey mean but a strictly lower predator mean, with the loss given by a positive forcing-weighted prey variance. For weak sinusoidal forcing, the leading fractional predator-mean loss peaks uniquely at the undamped frequency \(\Omega\), with universal peak coefficient \(1/(2r_0^2)\), without claiming any tipping threshold or finite-amplitude resonance location.
