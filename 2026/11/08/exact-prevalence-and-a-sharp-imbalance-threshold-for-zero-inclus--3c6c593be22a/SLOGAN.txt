In unit-edge graphical fair division on a labeled biclique \(K_{a,b}\), the exact number of zero-inclusive EFX orientations is \((2^b-1)^a+(2^a-1)^b-2^{ab}+a+b\). A uniformly random orientation is fair with asymptotic probability \(e^{-\lambda}\) when the larger side has size \(b\sim\lambda2^a\) and the smaller side grows, producing a sharp imbalance transition despite unconditional fair-orientation existence.
