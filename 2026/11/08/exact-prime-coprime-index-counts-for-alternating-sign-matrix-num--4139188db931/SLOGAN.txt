For every prime \(p\ge5\), the number of indices below \(p^r\) at which the alternating sign matrix count is coprime to \(p\) has an explicit two-branch formula: pure powers for \(p\equiv1\pmod6\), and the same power law with a two-endpoint correction for \(p\equiv5\pmod6\). The corresponding closed set of \(p\)-adic indices has exact box-counting dimension \(\log_p b_p\) and the integer indices have natural density zero.
