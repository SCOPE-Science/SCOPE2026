# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The over-rotation crossing statistic is identified using the sign of displacement on the two sides of the nonzero fixed point. For the full tent map, the three-state transition matrix has determinant polynomial \(t(t^2-t-2z)\); its weighted trace is an explicit Lucas polynomial. Each nonboundary periodic itinerary is unique because inverse branches contract, and the only trace defects are the all-left fixed point and two alternating boundary codes when the iterate is even. These are subtracted explicitly. Repeat-factor Möbius inversion gives the primitive-orbit counts. The Perron eigenvalue has logarithmic first and second derivatives \(1/3\) and \(2/27\); proper-divisor-period contributions are exponentially negligible, yielding the stated central limit law. The proof handles all integers \(n\ge2\), all admissible crossing indices, and fixed-point boundary exceptions. Exact-rational checks across periods two through twelve agree, but they are not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Bhattacharya's unfolding-number theorem (arXiv:2403.16549, Corollary 4.11) provides identification with the classical unimodal over-rotation statistic; it does not count cycles of the tent map. Blokh–Misiurewicz (1997) gives the forcing order and realizable over-rotation pairs, including discussion of truncated tent maps. Blokh–Snider (2013) gives unimodal rotation intervals/twist-map structure. Han et al. (2026) establish existence thresholds for particular over-rotation pairs in expanding unimodal maps. The Artin–Mazur interval-map zeta-function literature concerns principally unmarked periodic-point series, which does not imply these coefficients without a crossing-weight refinement. None of the inspected source statements gives the explicit \(N_{n,k}\) formula and its Gaussian limit; analogous dynamical zeta results for different maps do not dominate the claim. This is a qualified source comparison, not a universal novelty certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS

For a canonical full-shift interval map, possible over-rotation/unfolding pairs alone do not determine how many primitive cycles realize each pair. The exact joint multiplicity is a natural cycle invariant, and the formula resolves it for all periods and every admissible fraction, including the structural corollary of exactly two minimal-crossing cycles at each period at least three. The accompanying limit theorem quantifies the typical distribution of a recently introduced invariant over primitive orbits. The explicit boundary correction is essential: the uncorrected Markov trace would give wrong primitive counts at half-rotation.

A hidden equivalent marked-zeta calculation in older symbolic-dynamics literature cannot be excluded. The theorem is not generalized to non-full or differently sloped tent maps, nor is a local limit theorem shown. The identification with unfolding number relies on the published unimodal equality theorem, not a separately reproved invariance theorem. Independent specialist examination remains desirable.
