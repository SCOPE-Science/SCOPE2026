For the full tent map, the exact number of primitive period-\(n\) cycles of unfolding number \(k/n\) is \(\frac1n\sum_{d\mid\gcd(n,k)}\mu(d)[\frac{n/d}{n/d-k/d}\binom{n/d-k/d}{k/d}2^{k/d}-2\mathbf1_{\{n=2k\}}]\). Uniformly selected primitive cycles have a Gaussian crossing-count fluctuation: \((K_n-n/3)/\sqrt n\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,2/27)\).
