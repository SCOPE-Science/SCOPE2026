The proof isolates the common prime-power factor, proves a full if-and-only-if vanishing criterion and nonzero absolute value for primitive quadratic Gauss sums using a bijective change of variables in odd characteristic, and handles the terminal zero-quadratic-coefficient stratum separately. Numerical checks independently corroborate ten instances and the complete lifting matrix at \(N=9\), but are not used as universal proof.
