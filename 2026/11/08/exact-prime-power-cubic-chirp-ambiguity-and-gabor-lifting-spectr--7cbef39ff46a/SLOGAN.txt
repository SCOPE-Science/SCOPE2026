For the cyclic cubic chirp \(g(x)=e^{2\pi i x^3/p^k}\) at every odd prime power, the full Gabor ambiguity zero pattern, all nonzero magnitudes, and all singular values of complex intensity lifting follow exact valuation strata. The lifted rank is \((p^{2k+1}+1)/(p+1)\) for \(p\ge5\) but only \((3^{2k}+3)/4\) for \(p=3\), a sharp characteristic-three anomaly.
