# Reproducibility and exact versus numerical evidence

The mathematical proof is by exact finite additive-character orthogonality and a unit-coefficient quadratic Gauss sum, for every odd prime and every positive prime-power exponent. No finite enumeration is claimed to prove the universal quantifier.

Run `python3 check.py` (requires NumPy) to check every ambiguity frequency for ten stated cyclic groups and the complete singular spectrum of the complex lifting operator for \(N=9\). For these finite checks, the checker compares floating-point complex exponential values against **exact predicted magnitudes**, with explicit absolute-error bounds; it checks the exact predicted cardinalities and the Parseval identity numerically. It prints `CUBIC_CHIRP_AMBIGUITY_STRATA_TESTS_OK` on completion.

The raw window is unimodular and unnormalized. The result is about complex-linear operator lifting; nonlinear phase retrieval and independent review are not established.
