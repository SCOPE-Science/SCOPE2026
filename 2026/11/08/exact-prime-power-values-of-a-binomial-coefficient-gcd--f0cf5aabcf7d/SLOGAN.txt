The finite gcd \(D(k)=\gcd_{2\le q\le k+1}\binom{qk}{k}\) has an explicit complete prime factorization: for \(p^a\parallel k+1\), the exponent is \(\max\{0,a-\lfloor\log_p((k+1)/p^a)\rfloor\}\). Consequently the gcd is always \(1\) or a single prime power, and the proof gives an explicit binomial coefficient attaining each nonzero valuation.
