For the unperturbed cubic-chirp Gabor window on \(\mathbb Z_{p^2}\), every signal occupying at least three residue classes modulo \(p\) is uniquely determined up to global phase by its full spectrogram. On exactly two occupied classes, a second phaseless partner exists if and only if both blocks have perfect periodic autocorrelation and unequal energies; the partner uniquely exchanges the two block energies.
