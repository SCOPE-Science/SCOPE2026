For the normalized generalized Fibonacci recurrence, fixing coefficients modulo an odd prime leaves exactly one period-preserving lift modulo its square when the characteristic discriminant is nonzero. Repeated-root fibers have no such lifts for primes at least five, but modulo nine there are exactly three per fiber, characterized by \(a^2+4b\equiv6\pmod9\); the total number is \((p-1)^2\) for \(p\ge5\) and \(10\) for \(p=3\).
