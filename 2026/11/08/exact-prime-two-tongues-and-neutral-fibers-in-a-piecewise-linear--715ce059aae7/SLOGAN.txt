The piecewise-linear doubled circle map has precisely two disjoint regular attracting prime-two tongues, whose exact width is \((4b^2-3)/(2(3+2b))\) for \(b>\sqrt3/2\) and whose combined parameter area is \(-11/4+3\sqrt3/2+3\log(5/(3+\sqrt3))\). At the sharp threshold each tongue collapses to one parameter supporting a whole interval of neutral prime-two cycles, rather than an isolated neutral orbit.
