For every integer \(n\ge1\), the exact proportion of primitive arithmetic progressions of length \(\tau(n)\) that admit a coprime matching with its divisors is a positive rational Euler product with local factor \(1-\min\{p,(\nu_p(n)+1-p)_+\tau(n/p^{\nu_p(n)})\}/(p+1)\). The density decreases under divisibility and has a sharp three-phase profile on prime powers, reaching arbitrarily small positive values.
