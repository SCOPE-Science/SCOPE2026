For synchronous parallel chip-firing on the simple complete graph \(K_n\) with \(n\ge3\), every initially primitive three-cycle state has total chip number in exactly two explicit integer bands separated by the strict middle-stair gap. A multinomial generating polynomial counts every state by chip mass, and the two middle-stair endpoints each support exactly \(3^n-3\cdot2^n+3\) such states.
