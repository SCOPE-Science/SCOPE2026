For synchronous parallel chip-firing on every fan \(K_1\vee P_n\) with \(n\ge2\), initially primitive-three states occur at exactly the two mass bands \([2n-1,3n-3]\) and \([4n-2,5n-4]\), with every integer mass attained. Their total count has rational generating function \(12(1-s)/((1-3s+s^2)^2(1+s+s^2))\), and a fixed nine-state transfer matrix gives every individual chip-mass coefficient.
