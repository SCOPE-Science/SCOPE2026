For undissipated synchronous chip-firing on a labelled wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), \(m\ge3\), the complete mass-generating polynomial of configurations already on primitive two-cycles has a four-state transfer formula and no holes between chip totals \(3m-\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) and \(4m+\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1\). Both endpoint coefficients are \(4\) for even rim length and \(2m\) for odd rim length, with exact reflection symmetry about total mass \((7m-1)/2\).
