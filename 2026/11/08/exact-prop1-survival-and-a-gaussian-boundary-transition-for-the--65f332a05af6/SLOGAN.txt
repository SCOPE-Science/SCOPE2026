In the two-agent Like rule with independent Bernoulli approvals, joint realized PROP1 has an exact trinomial-tail formula and converges to probability one exponentially fast whenever both approval rates are strictly below one. At the fully approving boundary the limiting probability is zero, with a sharp Gaussian crossover \(\Phi(d_1)+\Phi(d_2)-1\) when the two nonapproval rates scale as \(d_i/\sqrt m\).
