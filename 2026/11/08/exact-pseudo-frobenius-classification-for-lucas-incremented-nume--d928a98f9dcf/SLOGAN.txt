For \(S_a=\langle L_a,L_a+L_0,\ldots,L_a+L_{a-1}\rangle\), every pseudo-Frobenius number is classified by a Lucas-Zeckendorf index set whose successive gaps are \(2\) or \(3\), plus precisely two exceptional sets when \(a\) is odd. Consequently its type is \(c(a-2)+c(a-3)+c(a-4)+c(a-5)+2\mathbf 1_{a\text{ odd}}\), where \(c\) counts compositions into \(2\) and \(3\).
