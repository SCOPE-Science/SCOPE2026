On every finite linear horizon of \(n\geq2\) times, pure nesting of strict-past and strict-future necessity induces exactly \(2(n-1)(2n-1)\) different operators over any nontrivial complete lattice. Every composition reduces to the empty relation, identity, a strict directional power, or a proper prefix/suffix rectangle, with an equivalent word of length at most \(2n-1\).
