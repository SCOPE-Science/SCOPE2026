For every conjugate-linear scaled isometry on \(\mathbb C^2\), its entire \(q\)-numerical range is a closed annulus whose two radii are explicit functions of the ordinary numerical radius and the operator norm. The central hole disappears at \(|q|=\sqrt{1-(w(A)/\|A\|)^2}\), while the outer radius remains maximal exactly through \(|q|=w(A)/\|A\|\); these two thresholds need not occur in the same order.
