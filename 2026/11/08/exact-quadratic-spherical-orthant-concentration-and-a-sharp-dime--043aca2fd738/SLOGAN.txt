Positive-orthant restriction of pure quadratic spherical harmonics has a complete four-sector spectrum in every dimension \(d\ge3\), with a uniquely most-concentrated symmetric mode and a \((d-1)\)-dimensional least-concentrated sector. Its sharp concentration condition number grows as \(\frac{2(\pi-2)}{\pi^2(\pi-3)}d^2\), whereas the corresponding degree-one condition number grows only linearly in \(d\).
