# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The Walsh Gram entry \(a_{|I\triangle J|}\) follows by an exact binomial-sign enumeration. Orthogonal decomposition into the radial, two standard, and quadratic row-sum-zero sectors proves the \(3+2+1\) block spectrum and its complete multiplicities at every radius. At radius two the square evaluation map is a triangular Boolean subset-incidence matrix after a Walsh-to-monomial change of basis, giving determinant \(2^{-nD+2n+4\binom n2}\) and the exact rank threshold. Direct expansion yields the radial cubic, standard quadratic and constant eigenvalue. A Rayleigh lower bound from the radial pair diagonal, a trace upper bound, the second elementary symmetric sum and the cubic product prove the \(n^8/256\) sharp spectral ratio, with no numerical extrapolation. The standalone checker executed 80,228 exact tests; all-dimensional claims are analytical.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
Hogan and Lakey (arXiv:1812.08905v1, full PDF, 2018) establish the general hypercube concentration setting and an eigenspace-reduction procedure but do not explicitly state the degree-two all-ball \(3+2+1\) formulas or the minimal-ball \(n^8/256\) condition asymptotic. Their 2021 article is a highly relevant continuation, but only its publisher preview was obtainable; an equivalent embedded result is a residual risk. Two closely related publicly available results give the complete first-band ball spectrum and the quadratic-band **shell** spectrum. The former lacks quadratic modes; the latter has individual-shell zero modes and cannot yield the sum-of-shell concentration spectrum by scalar eigenvalue summation because the eigenspaces vary. A general Terwilliger formula might imply the theorem by specialization, so historical priority is not established absolutely. Originality is assigned to the full explicit special case with its sharp instability constant, not the established representation-theoretic reduction.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The Hamming ball of radius two contains exactly \(1+n+\binom n2\) vertices, the information-theoretic minimum for sampling all quadratic Walsh coefficients. The exact determinant and sharp \(n^8\) spectral instability quantify a meaningful tradeoff: unique reconstruction at the minimal cardinality is severely ill-conditioned as dimension increases. The all-radius spectral blocks make nearby sampling geometries analyzable without diagonalizing a matrix of dimension quadratic in \(n\). This is a naturally specified sampling threshold with a nontrivial quantified stability penalty, not a renamed covariance computation.

## Limitations and sources
The theorem does not give an alternative noise-stable reconstruction, optimize other sampling sets, or treat bandwidth beyond two. Historical-priority uncertainty remains for the full 2021 Hogan–Lakey continuation and possibly classical Terwilliger representations. Scientifically distinct comparisons include arXiv:1812.08905v1 (full text), DOI 10.1007/s00041-021-09845-y (preview only), and the two published finite-cube concentration results linked in the references of RESULT.md.
