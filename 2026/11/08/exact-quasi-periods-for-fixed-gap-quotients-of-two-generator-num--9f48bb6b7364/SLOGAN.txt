For every fixed quotient modulus \(p\ge2\) and every fixed coprime generator gap \(h\ge2\), the Frobenius numbers of \(\langle a,a+h\rangle/p\), as \(a\) varies through sufficiently large admissible integers, follow explicit quadratic residue-class polynomials whose **least** quasi-period is exactly \(p\). No period collapse occurs, even when the admissibility condition \(\gcd(a,h)=1\) removes some integers.
