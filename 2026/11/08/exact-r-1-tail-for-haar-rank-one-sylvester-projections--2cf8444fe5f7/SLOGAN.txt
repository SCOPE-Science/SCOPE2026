Independent Haar rank-one trial spaces for a uniformly separated, exactly rank-one coupled Sylvester system produce an almost-surely finite projected solution with \(\Pr(\|\widetilde X\|_F>R)\sim 2\operatorname{arsinh}(1)/(\pi^2R)\). Every positive moment of the projected norm or Frobenius error of order \(p\ge1\) diverges, whereas all orders \(0<p<1\) remain finite; this statement concerns generic random trial spaces, not structured Krylov iterations.
