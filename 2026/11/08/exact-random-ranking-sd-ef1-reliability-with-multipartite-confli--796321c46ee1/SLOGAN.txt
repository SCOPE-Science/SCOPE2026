With more than half as many agents as goods, complete multipartite item-conflict graphs admit a full, common-ranking SD-EF1 allocation exactly when each graph part contributes at most half its goods to the lowest-ranked tail; all labeled allocations have a closed falling-factorial count. For balanced bipartite conflicts, uniform random rankings exhibit an error-function success transition with a \(\sqrt m\) agent surplus, far below the known worst-case \(\lceil3m/2\rceil\)-agent requirement.
