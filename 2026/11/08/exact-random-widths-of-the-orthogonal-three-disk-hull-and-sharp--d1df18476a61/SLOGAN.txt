The convex hull of three centered mutually perpendicular unit disks has an elementary, exact directional-width distribution, obtained from the minimum absolute coordinate of a uniform point on the sphere. For the corresponding coordinate-hyperdisk hulls in every dimension, the narrow-width tail has a sharp \(d-1\) power, the wide-width tail has a sharp square-root power, and the rescaled high-dimensional width deficit converges to \((\pi/2)E^2\) for a unit exponential \(E\).
