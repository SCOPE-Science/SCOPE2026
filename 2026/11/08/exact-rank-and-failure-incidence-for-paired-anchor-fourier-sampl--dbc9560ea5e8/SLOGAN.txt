In a natural four-sample, two-channel Fourier design on every cyclic group of twice an odd prime, interpolation fails exactly when the two row-pair midpoints coincide and either the pairs coincide or the off-anchor sampling frequencies are symmetric. Every failure has rank three, and the exact fraction of failing designs is \((3p-7)/(p(p-1)(p-2))\), decaying like \(3/p^2\).
