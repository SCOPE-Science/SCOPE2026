Idempotent weight-\(-1\) Rota–Baxter operators on every finite-field Heisenberg Lie algebra of dimension at least five have an exact rank polynomial obtained by counting complementary isotropic and central subalgebras. For fixed field order, their rank concentrates in two equally weighted packets near \(4n/5\) and \(6n/5\), with five residue-dependent discrete-Gaussian limits indexed by \(n\bmod5\).
