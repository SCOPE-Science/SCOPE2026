# Review of the quadratic-product Fourier rank theorem

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses multiplicative-character decomposition of the ring into unit and nonunit sectors, exact summation over the principal-unit subgroup, two-root cyclotomic noncancellation, nonzero tame Gauss couplings, and an explicit determinant \(-p^3\) for the constant block. All coefficients are units and the conclusion is for all odd primes. Exact arithmetic stationarity checks (140) and full numerical singular-value checks (20) corroborate the argument without being used as its universal proof.

## Originality
PASS under bounded search. Kelmer (arXiv:0801.4162, Section 2) establishes analogous stationary-phase evaluations for twisted inverse-phase Kloosterman sums but not the present product-polynomial Fourier matrix or its full kernel. Iosevich--Koh (arXiv:0804.4505) concerns finite-field restriction, while the closest published prime-square matrix-minor and parabola results address different operators and invariants. Searches for quadratic-product sampling, prime-square polynomial Fourier rank, character aliases, and stationary discriminants did not identify a theorem with stronger coverage. This is not proof of exhaustive priority; older general exponential-sum formulas remain a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. Quadratic chirping of an ordinary invertible discrete Fourier transform is a natural nonlinear measurement construction. At the first nonfield cyclic modulus, its critical sampling map loses a positive asymptotic fraction of dimensions; the exact rank and explicit lost-character subspace supply an interpretable reconstruction boundary, independent of both unit parameters. The finding is a complete infinite-family rank classification, not a one-off numerical calculation or a routine rescaling of the ordinary Fourier matrix.

## Limits
No higher prime-power theorem, nonunit coefficient classification, nonzero singular-value spectrum, or complete historical-priority certificate is asserted. Independent validation is outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
