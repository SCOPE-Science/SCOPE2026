For every odd prime, the quadratic-product Fourier matrix \(\bigl(e_{p^2}(a st+b(st)^2)\bigr)_{s,t\in\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z}\), with both coefficients units, loses exactly \((p-1)^2/2\) signal directions. Its entire kernel is the multiplicative-character sector whose quadratic stationary discriminant is nonsquare, giving an explicit orthogonal reconstruction obstruction.
