For every positive power and every window length, divisibility by the first rank-four balancing prime \(17\) is completely classified: odd powers require lengths divisible by \(8\), while even powers require lengths divisible by \(4\) together with either a factor \(17\) in the block count or exponent congruent to \(10\pmod{16}\). The exceptional exponent \(10\) is the first even power for which every four-term window sum acquires this prime divisor.
