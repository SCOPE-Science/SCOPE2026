# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The inverse-image condition is reduced to disjoint fiber cases, each counted with disjoint choices. The idempotent classification follows from the fixed-image criterion; regularity is equivalent to existence of a continuous fiberwise section, yielding an exact forbidden pattern. The edge and singleton cases include the complete graph boundary with one leaf. The standalone checker exhaustively and independently compares all ranks, idempotents and regularity for the five smallest stars, including 12,365 qualifying partial maps.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The 2026 Margolis–Rhodes paper defines the monoid and proves a general qualitative nonregularity criterion, while explicitly enumerating paths and cycles rather than stars. Its general structural decomposition does not state the present closed star-rank formulas, idempotent counts, or exact ranks of the nonregular elements. The 2020 foundational preprint gives general structure and examples of empty and complete graphs; the 2024 star-endomorphism paper treats edge-preserving maps, not inverse-image continuous maps. Exact phrasing, structural aliases and related calculations were checked in scholarly literature and published research search records. Residual priority risk: an unindexed prior star enumeration, or an unstated derivation by applying the general inverse-fiber decomposition.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Stars are the first branching tree family beyond paths and cycles in this study. The theorem determines three canonical semigroup statistics simultaneously, and upgrades qualitative nonregularity to a complete rankwise obstruction count. In particular, the idempotent profile is explicitly binomial whereas the full rank profile includes partial-injection numbers; this quantifies structural differences that a general continuity criterion alone does not compute.

## Limits and closest literature
The relevant broadest source is arXiv:2609.39370 (Sections 3, 4 and 8); its graph-independent decomposition and nonregularity theorem are prior work. arXiv:2005.02606 establishes the monoid's foundations. arXiv:2401.11741 treats a different map direction and contains cardinalities for other monoids. A 2023 complexity/examples paper had only accessible abstract and bibliographic material in this check; it is a residual priority uncertainty. Finite replay is evidence for small cases, not a proof of the infinite family.
