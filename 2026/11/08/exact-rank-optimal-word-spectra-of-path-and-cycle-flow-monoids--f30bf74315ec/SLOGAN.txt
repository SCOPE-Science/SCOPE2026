The number of length-\(m\) rank-optimal edge-collapse words in a path flow monoid is exactly \(2^m(n-m)^m\); on a cycle it is \(2^m n(n-m)^{m-1}\) for positive \(m\). In particular the cycle/path multiplicity ratio at every nontrivial rank is \(n/(n-m)\), and the shortest reset-word counts are \(2^{n-1}\) and \(n2^{n-1}\), respectively.
