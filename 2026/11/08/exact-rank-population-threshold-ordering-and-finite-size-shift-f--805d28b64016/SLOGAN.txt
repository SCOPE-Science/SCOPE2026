For independent Brownian searchers, the sign of the infinitesimal-resetting benefit follows a strict two-axis staircase in both arrival rank and population size. Its large-population switching rank is \(k_{\mathrm{crit}}(N)=0.4123101754587\ldots N-2.0763670879430\ldots+O(N^{-1})\), revealing a universal finite-population shift of more than two arrival ranks.
