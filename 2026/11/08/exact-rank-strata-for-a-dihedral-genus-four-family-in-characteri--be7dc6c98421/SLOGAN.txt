In characteristic seven, the dihedrally symmetric genus-four family \(y^2=x^9+ax^7+bx^5+ax^3+x\) has Jacobian \(7\)-ranks only \(4,2,0\), with \(a\)-numbers respectively \(0,2,2\). Its rank-zero locus contains exactly 20 distinct smooth geometric coefficient pairs, characterized by an explicit degree-22 elimination polynomial with 20 distinct roots.
