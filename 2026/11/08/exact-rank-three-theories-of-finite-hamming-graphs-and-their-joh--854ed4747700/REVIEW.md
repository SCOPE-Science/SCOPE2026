# Scientific review: exact rank-three Hamming graph theories

## Correctness — PASS

The central lemma says that any two finite nonempty vertex-transitive graphs are rank-three equivalent if and only if the sets of two-vertex extension masks match. Both directions are proved by elementary rank-three formulas and an explicit three-move Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé strategy, including equality and singleton cases. For Hamming graphs, common-neighbor counts at distances one, two and at least three are respectively \(q-2\), 2 and 0, and the exclusive/no-adjacency counts are derived from the degree and total order. Strict monotonicity of the three potentially zero quantities proves all parameter boundaries without extrapolating finite data. Johnson common-neighbor counts and stated binomial lower bounds prove the two cross-family equivalences. The checker directly evaluates 116 finite graph instances and all eight analytic profiles; the uniform result rests on the proof, not finite extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The October 2026 Johnson-graph model-theory preprint proves results for a different, overlapping family and proposes Hamming first-order limit theories as further research. The earlier VC-dimension paper and Leeds thesis discuss Hamming graph definability and its prospects, but the relevant inspected portions do not state the exact eight rank-three classes or the Hamming–Johnson three-round coincidences. Searches under three-round Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé equivalence, exact quantifier-rank profiles, Hamming graph parameter regimes and Johnson equivalence did not produce a covering statement. This is evidence for novelty, not proof of worldwide historical priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete, explicitly bounded logical classification for a natural two-parameter graph family whose higher-rank limit theory is actively investigated. The sharp binary versus nonbinary thresholds, minimal 27-vertex representative of the generic Hamming rank-three class and unexpected matches with Johnson families provide concrete structural information and a natural starting point for higher-rank study. Neither the result's interest nor its correctness depends on claiming an all-rank limit theorem.

- Adler, Benediktsson and Macpherson, *Vapnik–Chervonenkis dimension and density on Johnson and Hamming graphs*, Discrete Applied Mathematics 312 (2022), DOI:10.1016/j.dam.2021.09.017.
- Benediktsson, *Model-theoretic methods for algorithmically tame graph classes*, University of Leeds doctoral thesis (2022), accessible abstract and relevant sections.

The result concerns pure graph-language sentences of rank at most three, with no coordinate predicates or counting quantifiers. The 27-vertex optimality claim is restricted to Hamming graphs, not to all graphs. The model-theoretic and enumerative literature has not been searched exhaustively: a general theorem about three-round games on association schemes could subsume portions, and the earlier thesis and article were not read in full page by page. Independent validation has not been performed.
