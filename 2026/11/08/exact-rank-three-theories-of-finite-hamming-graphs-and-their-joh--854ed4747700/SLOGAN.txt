The pure first-order rank-three theories of all finite Hamming graphs fall into exactly eight classes, with the common large-parameter theory attained sharply at \(H(3,3)\) on 27 vertices. In the same rank-three language, nonbinary two-dimensional Hamming graphs match large triangular Johnson graphs, while Hamming graphs with both parameters at least three match Johnson graphs with both subset and complement sizes at least three.
