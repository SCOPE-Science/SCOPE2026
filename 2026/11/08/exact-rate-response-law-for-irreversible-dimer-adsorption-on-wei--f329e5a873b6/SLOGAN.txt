In irreversible dimer adsorption on a fan with spoke rate \(\lambda>0\), the probability that the hub survives empty has an exact Catalan-type recurrence and a tunable exponential decay \(q_m(\lambda)\sim 2(1+\lambda)\rho(\lambda)^{-m-1}\). The dominant root is strictly increasing with the spoke rate and yields an exact \(m^{-1}\)-scale rate-response law for this rare jamming event.
