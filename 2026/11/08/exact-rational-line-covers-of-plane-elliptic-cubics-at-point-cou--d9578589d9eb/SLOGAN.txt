For any smooth plane elliptic cubic with a rational flex and \(3\mid N=|E(\mathbb F_q)|\), the minimum number of rational lines covering its rational points is exactly \(N/3\), except when the elliptic-curve group has a unique involution, when it is exactly \(N/3+1\). A zero-sum partition theorem and a Cayley-table near transversal explain both sharp cases.
