For the rational BKS quartic \(2Y^4-X^3Z-XZ^3-XY^2Z+X^2Z^2=0\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), the complete rational line-section and four-secant point-incidence distributions are determined over \(\mathbb F_{27}\) and \(\mathbb F_{81}\). Respectively \(282\) and \(108\) points outside the curve lie on no four-secant, so neither short-extension rational point set is a complete \((k,4)\)-arc.
