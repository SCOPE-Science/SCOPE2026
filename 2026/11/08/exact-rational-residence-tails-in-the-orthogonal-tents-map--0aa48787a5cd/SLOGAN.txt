The orthogonal tents map has an exact rational \(2m\)-step residence area \(\mu(S_{2m})=(2m-1)/[4(4m-3)(4m-1)]\) inside its parabolic upper-left branch for every integer \(m\ge2\). Uniform initial points in that branch escape almost surely, but the first-exit survival tail is \(\Pr(\tau>n)\sim 1/(4n)\), so the mean residence time diverges.
