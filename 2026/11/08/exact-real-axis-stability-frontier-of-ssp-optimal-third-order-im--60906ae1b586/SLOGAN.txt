For the uniquely SSP-optimal third-order implicit Runge–Kutta method with \(s\ge2\) stages, the exact scalar-decay stability interval has an explicit parity-dependent endpoint of size \(4s^2+O(1)\). Yet its arbitrarily-stiff amplification approaches \(+e\) along even stage counts and \(-e\) along odd ones: increasing the stage count does not produce stiff damping.
