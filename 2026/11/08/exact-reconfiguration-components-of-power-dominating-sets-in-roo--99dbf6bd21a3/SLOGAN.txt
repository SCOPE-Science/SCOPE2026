For rook graphs \(K_m\square K_n\), the complete power-domination TAR component sequence is determined by row/column occupancy, with an exact Stirling-number count of newly isolated configurations at budget \(n-1\). For \(3\le m\) and \(n\ge m+2\), connectivity disappears and returns as the budget increases, and the gap between first and permanent connectedness grows without bound.
