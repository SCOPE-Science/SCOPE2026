For simultaneous three-person majority updates on a connected loose hyperstar with \(k\) petals, there are exactly \(2^k-2\) permanently polarized two-state recurrent classes, despite connectivity. Starting from unbiased independent opinions, the probability of eventual global consensus is exactly \((3/4)^{k-1}\), while the expected time to freeze the \(m\) initially mixed leaf pairs is the harmonic number \(H_m\).
