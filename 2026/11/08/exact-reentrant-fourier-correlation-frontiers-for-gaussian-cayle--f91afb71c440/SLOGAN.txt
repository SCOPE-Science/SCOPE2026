A Gaussian Cayley step preserves the unit circle and its uniform invariant distribution while its second Fourier correlation changes sign twice, with sharp stepsize thresholds \(h\approx1.489832571435\) and \(h\approx36.820960093978\). An exponential-polynomial zero-count argument bounds the number of positive sign thresholds of the \(m\)-th Fourier mode by \(m\), and an exact inequality proves the intermediate negative interval without relying on numerical experiments.
