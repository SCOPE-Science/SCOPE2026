# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The reflection-class census is reconstructed from unique gaps between consecutive powers, the exact union of feasible multi-marker window starts and the pairwise reversal conditions. The only nonpalindromic reflected collisions come from two-marker factors with compatible padding, including the isolated binary short-length exception. The resulting piecewise formula has the claimed sharp liminf, limsup and continuous interval of subsequential normalized limits. Independent finite enumeration over bases 2–10 and lengths 0–150, plus separate binary simplification and endpoint samples, corroborates the all-length symbolic proof without replacing it.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Allouche–Campbell–Li–Shallit–Stipulanti prove general regularity and study different automatic words, not the specific geometric-marker closed form. Gheorghiciuc's increasing-gap theory counts ordinary factors, while related same-word studies count palindromes, minimal absent factors or binomial-equivalence classes. Neither such totals nor general reflection regularity determines the nonpalindromic reversal-pair correction without new work. The sharp normalized oscillation interval for all bases was not found in the inspected primary results or published mathematical findings. A published mirror of this identical result is not an independent predecessor; an unindexed equivalent is a real residual priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A natural and much-studied factor invariant is evaluated at every length for all geometric-support automatic words, with a sharp base-two versus higher-base asymptotic dichotomy. Its structural reflected-pair classification fills a gap not supplied by ordinary or palindromic complexity alone.

**Scope.** One-sided binary word, markers precisely at positive powers of an integer base at least two, reversal equivalence only. All finite checks are diagnostic. No assertion is made about complement or arbitrary antimorphism quotients or all automatic words.

**Relevant sources.** Allouche et al., *The reflection complexity of sequences over finite alphabets*, arXiv:2406.09302; Dvořáková–Pelantová, *Reflection on the Reflection Complexity*, DOI:10.1007/s00224-026-10278-7; Blažek et al., *Generalized reflection complexity*, arXiv:2610.14637; Gheorghiciuc, *The subword complexity of a class of infinite binary words*, arXiv:math/0512256. Published results for the same B-power marker words concerning palindromes, 2-binomial complexity and minimal absent factors were also compared.
