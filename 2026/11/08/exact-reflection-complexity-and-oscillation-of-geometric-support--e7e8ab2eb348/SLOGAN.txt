For the binary word marked exactly at positions \(B^j\), the number of distinct length-\(n\) factors up to reversal has an explicit exact formula for every integer \(B\ge2\) and \(n\ge0\). Its normalized lower and upper accumulation values are \(\frac12+\frac1{B(B-1)}\) and \(\frac12+\frac B{(B-1)(B+2)}\), respectively: they coincide only at base \(B=2\), where \(R_2(n)=2\lceil n/2\rceil+2\) for all \(n\ge4\).
