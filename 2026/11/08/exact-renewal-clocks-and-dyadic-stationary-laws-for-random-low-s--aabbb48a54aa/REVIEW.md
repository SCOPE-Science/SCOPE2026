# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof calculates the conditional distribution of the return position explicitly on every dyadic leading-zero interval. Conditional uniformity and the independence of future random choices yield iid excursion records. Transfer-operator equations are checked on every dyadic shell using the exact tail difference identity; Tonelli justifies the mass computation even at infinite mean. The stationary covariance is conditional return probability minus stationary mean. The standalone rational checker tests 6 finite reset distributions, transfer identities, renewal coefficients and pair-digit independence. These tests do not replace the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
Inoue–Toyokawa (2022 and 2023) already introduce the maps and obtain Lebesgue-equivalent sigma-finite measures with shell-comparability estimates. The precise new assertion is full independent-return encoding and its explicit all-law renewal and stationary-correlation formulas, with equalities for all shell masses. Inspected versions of the exact family do not provide those formulas. General induced maps, interval maps and regenerative processes are known in the literature; the novelty claim is for this mathematical realization and its exact consequences, not the existence of renewal theory itself. An inaccessible 2020 first-return paper and non-indexed equivalent treatments remain meaningful priority risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The transition between finite and infinite invariant measure is central to the source example; an exact iid return law supplies the entire finite-horizon visiting probabilities and yields an explicit observable covariance sequence. It upgrades an asymptotic comparability statement to a fully normalized invariant density for arbitrary countable reset laws, including heavy tails. This is a structural statistical description of a natural previously studied random intermittent system rather than a chosen numerical slice.

## Limits and source chronology
The proven result is annealed, for iid map choices; only the absolutely continuous invariant component is characterized, not singular measures. Covariance requires finite mean reset. The 2023 arXiv source first appeared on 2023-03-20, whereas an earlier proceedings treatment of the same model is dated only 2022-04. General first-return theory may contain a hidden equivalent and was not fully accessible.
