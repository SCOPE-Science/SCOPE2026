# Mathematical assessment: Exact renewal pressure and entropy separation in a degenerate border-collision map

## Correctness
PASS. For each integer q>=3 the exact q-cell Markov partition gives a primitive 0-to-j uniform transition chain; the stationary density solves all Frobenius-Perron equations. An induced renewal process with iid uniform delays proves the counting CLT variance exactly. Perron-Frobenius of the tilted q-state matrix gives the full pressure equation, and the generating Markov partition gives topological entropy. Root bracketing and expansion establish both large-q limits. Exact rational checks on q=3..9 and pressure checks q=10,20,40,80 corroborate but do not replace this proof.

## Originality
PASS within inspected evidence, subject to historical-priority risk. The motivating 2026 Simpson map contains the slice, but the same one-dimensional map is EXACTLY conjugate under u=-2x to the 2012 Botella-Soler et al. family beta=2,s=q. The 2012 full manuscript gives q=2 invariant densities, Lyapunov 2 log 2/3 and golden-ratio entropy; none is presented as original. For q>=3 the new evaluated all-parameter iid uniform return law, exact CLT variance, tilted pressure and asymptotic contrast/softplus crossover were not located in inspected relevant manuscripts or published-finding corpus claims. General Markov-renewal methods are prior art; uninspected literature may still imply some parts. Distinct algebraic/combinatorial content is the natural parameter-indexed exact identification and its singular pressure limit, not a claim to invent renewal theory.

Closest material: Simpson, arXiv:2604.06570, Section 4; Botella-Soler et al., arXiv:1205.5360, especially equation (5), Sections 3 and 4, full exact affine conjugacy; Simpson arXiv:2408.04790 and arXiv:2606.08901. A 2012 paper's exact \(q=2\) results are previous knowledge, not claimed here. General renewal/finite Markov symbolic tools may already derive part of the result after specialization.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This is a structurally motivated postcritically finite sequence in a recent rank-one border-collision model, not an arbitrary isolated parameter sample. The physical metric entropy vanishes while compact topological entropy tends to log 2, and an exact rare-event pressure crossover remains nontrivial. These different statistical limits show that a singular parameter family can retain exponentially many itineraries while typical expansion disappears, with quantitative CLT and pressure usable for finite-time observations.

## Scientific limitations
Restricted to integer \(q\ge3\), the degenerate determinant-zero slice and the compact invariant interval. The CLT uses the unique absolutely continuous stationary law; generic deterministic convergence and robustness under off-slice perturbations are not claimed. Independent validation is not performed; exhaustive historical priority remains unresolved.
