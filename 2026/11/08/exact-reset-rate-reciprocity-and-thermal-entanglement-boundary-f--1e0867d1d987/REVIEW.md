# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The joint replacement reset generator r(tr(rho)rho_*−rho) has a unique stationary resolvent; the pair Hamiltonian changes only even-parity population imbalance z and imaginary coherence y. Solving 0=−4Jy+r(Delta−z), 0=Jz−(r+Gamma)y gives z=r(r+Gamma)Delta/D and y=JrDelta/D. Wootters X-state formula gives concurrence 2[J|Delta|r/D−sqrt(bc)]_+. Differentiation shows a unique positive-coherence maximum at r=2J when nonzero; quadratic positivity has roots with product 4J², with correct h=0, Delta=0, tangency cases. Substituting thermal a=(1−s)^2,b=c=s(1−s),d=s² gives the given sharp s_c(η). Independently assembled complex 16×16 Liouvillians were solved for 16 parameter/rate cases and concurrence evaluated from the full spin-flip eigenvalues, matching the formula within 10^(−9). This corroborates rather than proves unrestricted parameters; analytical equations do.

## originality

PASS

The general Poisson reset resolvent and nonmonotone resetting-induced entanglement were already known (Rose et al 2018; Kulkarni–Majumdar 2023), and earlier local-reset/dephased two-qubit studies (Altintas 2016, Hartmann et al 2007) investigate qualitatively adjacent effects. However, inspected Altintas full original shows single-qubit local |+> resets and Ising ZZ Hamiltonian, not this global joint diagonal separable re-preparation and pair-conversion Hamiltonian. No inspected source yields the exact three-regime concurrence classification, universal 2J optimum independent of dephasing/preparation, reciprocal root law or sharp locally thermal s_c(η). Best-of-knowledge qualified original model-specific exact boundary.

PASS

A natural experimentally motivated design variable is the rate of whole-pair re-preparation under independent dephasing. This is not mere stationary resolvent rewriting, whose general principle is already credited.
