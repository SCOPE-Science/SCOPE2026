For a pair-converting two-qubit Hamiltonian subject to separate local dephasing and global Poisson resets to any diagonal separable preparation, the exact stationary concurrence has either no positive region, one finite positive reset-rate interval with reciprocal endpoints \(r_-r_+=4J^2\), or positivity for every positive rate. Whenever entanglement is attainable, the unique optimal rate is \(r=2J\), independent of dephasing and preparation, and identical local thermal preparations have a sharp explicit excitation-population threshold.
