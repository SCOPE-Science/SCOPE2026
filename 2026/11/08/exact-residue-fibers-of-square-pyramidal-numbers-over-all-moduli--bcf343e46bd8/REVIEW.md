# Review of exact square-pyramidal residue fibers

## Correctness — PASS
The proof gives exact units for index increments at 2 and 3, proves each residue branch is a p-adic isometry, and checks minimal periods using the short base sequences. At primes at least five it passes to the bijective affine normalization of the Dickson cubic, counts all field fibers via the conic character sum and repeated roots, and lifts the critical branches using an explicit square-root change of variable. The fiber counts add to the least-period domain sizes. CRT makes the composite formula exact. The standalone script exhaustively verifies 66 prime powers and all moduli through 300. Computations are supporting checks, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified historical priority
The 2015 Küçüksakallı paper provides the established finite-field Dickson value set formula, which is not counted as a new result here. The new claim is the all-modulus *multiplicity histogram*, exact least periods, and exceptional critical-branch counts, including small-characteristic branches where the figurate-number polynomial has denominators. A 2023 general paper provides extremal bounds for polynomial value-set sizes but no such complete classification. Bremser–Gómez-Calderon (1991) concerns Dickson preimages over Galois rings and is the most material unresolved priority risk: only its abstract was accessible. No claim of absolute historical priority is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete local-to-global fiber distribution is a natural arithmetic invariant of a classical figurate sequence and of degree-three modular polynomial maps. The exceptional ramified multiplicities grow with the prime-power exponent; these are not visible from the known finite-field image cardinality. The classification supplies exact representability and frequency information for every modulus and pinpoints why characteristic two and three behave uniformly.

## Limitations
No claims about other power-sum degrees. The result is mathematically proved here, not independently audited; the 1991 full-text unavailability leaves residual historical-priority risk.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
