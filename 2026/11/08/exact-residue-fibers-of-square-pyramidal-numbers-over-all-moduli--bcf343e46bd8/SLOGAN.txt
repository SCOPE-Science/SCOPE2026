Square-pyramidal numbers have exact least period \(m\gcd(m,6)\) modulo \(m\), and their complete residue-fiber multiplicity spectrum is determined by prime-local formulas. Singular cubic branches create explicit exceptional multiplicities \(1+2p^j\), while the image-size product is \(m\prod_{p\mid m,\,p\ge5}(2p+(\frac{-3}{p}))/(3p)\).
