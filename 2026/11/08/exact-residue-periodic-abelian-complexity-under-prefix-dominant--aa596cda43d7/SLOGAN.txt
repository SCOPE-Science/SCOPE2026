For every aperiodic binary fixed point of a uniform Parikh-constant substitution whose image-prefix one counts are pointwise ordered, the abelian complexity is exactly periodic from the image length onward, with an explicit finite residue formula. The family \(0\mapsto0^p1^q,\ 1\mapsto1^q0^p\) is aperiodic for all positive \(p,q\) and has \(2\min(p,q)+1\) abelian classes at every positive multiple of \(p+q\).
