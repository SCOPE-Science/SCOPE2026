# Research assessment: weighted circular-ladder coordinate control

## Correctness (PASS)

The proof defines the graph, positive rung weight, coordinate-input model, Fourier frequency set, mode multiplicities and all resonance boundaries. Direct sums of the two vertical parity bands yield the exact eigenvalue multiplicities. The real symmetric PBH criterion proves their lower bound. Explicit restrictions on the nested sensor sets have determinants respectively \(\sin(2\pi j/m)\), twice this sine, or a product of two such nonzero sines. Singleton–singleton collisions are separately covered, including the even-cycle \(t=2\) exception. There is no missing case of three modes at one eigenvalue because each band's reduced cycle spectrum is strictly ordered. The included reproducible checker passed 550 numeric and 10 exact-rational parameter instances. Numerical experiments are not claimed to prove the infinite theorem.

## Originality (PASS with specialist-priority risk)

The closest primary sources are Parlangeli–Notarstefano (2011), which treats cycle reachability including adjacent two-node sets, and Anđelić–Brunetti–Stanić (2020), which provides product eigenspaces and **single binary-input** graph controllability conditions. The latter's complete accessible manuscript was checked including its Cartesian-product section and Theorem 5. Neither source states nor implies a formula for the minimum of independently actuated vertex coordinates of a uniformly rung-weighted prism for all weights; in particular neither classifies the scalar–double and double–double resonance requirements or supplies the nested optimal sets. General PBH and symmetry foundations are acknowledged and not presented as new. Queries of the indexed mathematical findings for prism/circular-ladder control, collision aliases and weighted actuator counts found different graph invariants or sensor-bandwidth questions, without an equivalent or stronger statement. Absence from indexed search is not proof of uniqueness; uncatalogued specialist papers remain a risk.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The result provides a complete sharp all-size, all-positive-weight placement theorem for a canonical two-layer network, without a numerical control-Gramian search. The finite resonant values can double the minimum hardware count, and the result distinguishes harmless scalar–scalar collisions from high-multiplicity collisions. Crucially, the same few geometrically localized actuator positions are optimal for every size and coupling level; this is a directly reusable structural design fact rather than an arbitrary small-graph census.

## Limitations

Only exact full-state controllability with independent coordinate inputs and uniform rung weights is covered. No energy, numerical conditioning or heterogeneous-rung claim is made. A publication-priority risk remains for unindexed literature; the open specialist product manuscript is substantively inspected. The result has not received an external independent mathematical audit.
