For every uniformly rung-weighted circular ladder \(C_m\square K_2\), \(m\ge3\), the exact minimum number of independent coordinate actuators is the largest of \(2\) and the dimensions created by cross-band Fourier spectral collisions. A fixed nested family of two, three or four adjacent-layer control vertices always attains this minimum; notably the even-ladder collision at rung weight \(t=2\) needs only two actuators, while double–double collisions require four.
