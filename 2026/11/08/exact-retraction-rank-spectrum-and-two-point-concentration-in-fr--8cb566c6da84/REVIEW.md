# Scientific review: finite-field class-two free Lie retractions

## Correctness — PASS

The proof determines every Lie homomorphism of \(V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\) by its generator components \(A\) and \(B\), derives both idempotency conditions exactly, and resolves the \(B\)-constraints on the image/kernel splitting of \(A\). The central-shear conjugation gives one orbit for each abelianized rank, and counting complements and independent central maps gives the stated multiplicity. The sharpened saddle argument uses the exact real cubic exponent and a uniform bound on Gaussian-binomial corrections; it supplies an explicit exponential tail, not just a heuristic. Independently constructed small-field homomorphism matrices were composed and ranked exhaustively by `verify.py`; all tested histograms agreed. Boundary cases \(r=0,n\), characteristic two, the rank/whole-image distinction, and fixed-field asymptotic quantifiers were checked. Infinite statements rest on the proof, not finite tests.

## Originality — PASS relative to identified prior statements

Cagliero–Gutiérrez Frez–Szechtman (arXiv:1703.06214) identify the free two-step Lie object and investigate uniserial representations. Benito–Roldán-López (arXiv:1911.01897) give general endomorphism extension and automorphism descriptions. Ruan–Sheng–Zhang (arXiv:1705.07307) construct free two-step Lie examples via Hall algebras. The known linear-projection Gaussian count and the three-dimensional Heisenberg specialization are prior mathematics. Neither these papers nor the relevant mathematical-record searches supplied a conjugacy-resolved all-rank finite-field idempotent count combined with the irrational two-neighbor rank concentration. Existing general endomorphism descriptions imply the starting normal form but do not, without additional idempotency solving, counting and saddle analysis, assert the displayed conclusion. Residual priority risk: a broader enumeration of free-nilpotent retracts may have gone unidentified, especially in terminology other than 'idempotent endomorphism'.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Retracts and idempotent endomorphisms are canonical structural invariants; the free class-two family has dimension growing quadratically with the number of generators. The all-rank census and single-orbit property determine its entire retraction spectrum over every finite field. The limiting rank proportion \(\frac{1+\sqrt7}{6}\), rather than the midpoint from ordinary linear projections, and the exponentially accurate concentration on two integers identify a substantive Lie-specific phenomenon across an infinite family. The exact exponent of total retraction growth is an additional consequence. This is more than a table of small ranks or a renaming of ordinary projection counts.

## Comparison limits

The full HTML text of arXiv:1703.06214 and arXiv:1911.01897 was examined in the portions pertaining to the Lie model, universal property, endomorphism extension, automorphism structure and primary results. Searches within those full texts found no idempotent census. The arXiv:1705.07307 abstract and available source description were examined for the separate Hall-Lie construction; that paper was not treated as a full-text exclusion. A relevant 2017 quadratic-Lie classification available in article text discusses generator maps but not the claimed rank spectrum; its general framework remains an implication risk. Mathematical-record searches are evidence of no observed duplicate, not proof of global uniqueness. No independent mathematical audit has occurred.
