Idempotent endomorphisms of the free class-two Lie algebra \(V\oplus\bigwedge^2V\) over \(\mathbb F_q\) have an exact Gaussian-binomial rank census, with one automorphism-conjugacy orbit at each generator rank. At fixed \(q\) a uniformly random retraction concentrates, up to exponentially small error, on the two integers adjacent to \(\frac{n-2+\sqrt{7n^2+8n+4}}6\), giving the irrational limiting generator-rank fraction \(\frac{1+\sqrt7}{6}\).
