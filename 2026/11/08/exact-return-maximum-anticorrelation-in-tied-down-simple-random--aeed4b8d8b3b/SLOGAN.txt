For a uniform length-\(2n\) simple symmetric bridge, the number of completed returns and the one-sided maximum have the exact covariance \(\bigl(3n+1-(4^n/\binom{2n}{n})^2\bigr)/2\), strictly negative for every \(n\ge2\). Their correlation converges to \((3-\pi)/(4-\pi)\), despite their closely related marginal means.
