## Value

**PASS.** Reflection equivalence is a recently studied natural invariant, and the 2025 source classifies this concrete morphic word by its ordinary factor growth. The exact finite spectrum, complete palindrome list, and sharp permanent cutoff \(n=10\) describe qualitatively different behavior from reversal-closed Rote families. They provide a reusable boundary-case example for reflection complexity and a finite-language mechanism for recognizing when no long reverse pairs can occur. This is more than a change of notation or an arbitrarily chosen finite length.
