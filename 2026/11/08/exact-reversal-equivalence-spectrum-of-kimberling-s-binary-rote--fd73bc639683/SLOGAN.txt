For Kimberling’s binary Rote word, no factor of length at least \(10\) has a reverse occurrence, and precisely \(15\) nonempty palindrome factors exist. The complete reversal-pair counts at lengths \(1\)–\(9\) are \((0,1,2,2,2,3,2,1,0)\), so reflection complexity reaches its maximum \(2n\) for every \(n\ge10\).
