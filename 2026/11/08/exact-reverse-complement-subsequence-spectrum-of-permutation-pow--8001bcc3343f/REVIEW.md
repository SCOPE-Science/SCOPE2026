# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The domain fixes a positive integer half-alphabet size, a positive integer period count, an order-reversing fixed-point-free complement and **distinct output strings**. The midpoint-reflection lemma is proved from arbitrary mirrored embeddings by taking coordinatewise minima; it is also independently enumerated for 466 symmetric hosts. The weak-descent criterion gives an if-and-only-if embedding condition on exact increasing permutation powers; the central θ-pair correction and all transfer identities are derived explicitly without a singular substitution. The rational formal series and every first-three-period coefficient follow from these identities. A separate exhaustive mask enumerator and polynomial coefficient implementation agree for all 18 cases tested (751,506 candidate masks), with no discrepancies. Finite tests are not substituted for infinite reasoning.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The principal literature compared was Müllner–Ryzhikov (2019), about maximal palindrome/antipalindrome subsequence **lengths**, and Mahalingam–Pandoh (2021), about counts of distinct ordinary palindromic **scattered** subsequences. The factor-based antimorphism literature of Ambrož–Masáková–Pelantová (2019) concerns contiguous antipalindromes. Their materially inspected results did not imply this reverse-complement bivariate spectrum; neither an extremal-length theorem nor reversal-fixed palindrome counts determines the present fixed-point-free-antimorphism counts. Published mathematical-record searches on the explicit rational form, aliases, periodic permutation host and distinct scattered reverse-complement words did not identify a dominating equivalent theorem. The accessible historical claims were compared as statements, particularly ordinary-palindrome counts of permutation prefixes; no one supplied this exact θ-involutive distribution. This remains best-knowledge originality, not a uniqueness or priority certificate. An equivalent result in unindexed literature, appendices, or another formal-language presentation remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem completely determines an entire two-dimensional length spectrum for infinitely many alphabet sizes and repetition counts. The problem is motivated by reverse-complement symmetry in symbolic sequences, rather than a randomly chosen small numeric slice. Its universal half-word reduction yields a reusable exact structural simplification, while the parameter-uniform rational form is stronger than a collection of fixed-instance dynamic-programming outputs. The first genuinely nontrivial period gives an explicit correction to simple independence-type counts. No claims of optimality outside the named family or practical biological performance are made.
