For every even alphabet size \(2m\) and positive repetition count, the distinct reverse-complement-palindromic scattered subsequences of \((0\,1\cdots(2m-1))^k\) have the exact half-length generating function \(z((1+t(1-z^2))^m-1)/((1-z)(1-z(1+t(1-z^2))^m))\). A symmetry reduction and weak-descent transfer recurrence explain why the first nontrivial counting collision appears at the third repeated period.
