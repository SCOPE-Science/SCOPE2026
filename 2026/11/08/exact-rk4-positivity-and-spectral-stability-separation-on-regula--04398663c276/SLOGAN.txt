For classical RK4 on every finite connected \(d\)-regular bipartite diffusion network, spectral stability lasts to \(dh=\rho\approx1.39264678\), but matrix positivity lasts only to \(dh=1\) unless the graph is exactly \(K_{d,d}\). The complete bipartite case alone preserves positivity throughout the entire stable interval, and a distance-three parity witness gives an explicit negative matrix entry immediately beyond the smaller threshold.
