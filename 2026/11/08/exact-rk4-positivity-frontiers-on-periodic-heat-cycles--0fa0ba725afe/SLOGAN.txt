For classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta on the periodic nearest-neighbor heat equation, the exact nonnegativity cutoff is fully classified for every cycle size: exceptional sharp thresholds occur at three, four, five, and seven grid points, while every cycle of eight or more points has the forward-Euler positivity cutoff. The positive-step domain is always one interval, and from five points onward it is strictly smaller than the spectral-stability interval.
