For every directed cycle with at least five states and arbitrary positive transition rates, the unmodified fourth-order Runge–Kutta update has an exact, non-reentrant positivity interval determined only by adjacent-rate cubics and four-rate sums. Strict improvement over the forward-Euler limit occurs precisely when no four consecutive rates attain their common maximum, and its sharp possible gain approaches \(\alpha=2.7852935634052818\ldots\) with a single high-rate defect.
