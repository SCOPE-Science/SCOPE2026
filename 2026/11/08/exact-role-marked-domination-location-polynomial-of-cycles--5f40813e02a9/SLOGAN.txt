Every role-marked partition of a labelled cycle \(C_n\), \(n\ge5\), into a locating-dominating set and a complementary dominating set is exactly a cyclic binary word avoiding \(000\), \(111\), and \(00100\). This gives a six-term exact counting-polynomial recurrence, sharp class-size bounds \(\lceil2n/5\rceil\) and \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\), and total growth asymptotic to \(\rho^n\) for \(\rho\approx1.5701473122\).
