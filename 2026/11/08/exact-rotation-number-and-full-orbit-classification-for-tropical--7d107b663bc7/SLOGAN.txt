Every nonzero orbit of the tropical rank-two mutation with \(0<pq<4\) lies on an invariant elliptic oval and is exactly conjugate, by sector area, to a rotation of fraction \(2\arccos(\sqrt{pq}/2)/(\pi+2\arccos(\sqrt{pq}/2))\). This yields the complete rational-angle prime-period formula \((b+2a)/\gcd(2,b)\), and dense uniquely ergodic orbits at irrational angles.
