For binary length-\(n\) words with exactly three ones, the circular Burrows–Wheeler transform has precisely \(2\), \(4\), or \(6\) runs, with exact all-length counts split only by whether \(3\mid n\). The four-run class is determined by the ordering and spacing of the three cyclic zero gaps; six runs occur with probability \(1-6/n+O(n^{-2})\).
