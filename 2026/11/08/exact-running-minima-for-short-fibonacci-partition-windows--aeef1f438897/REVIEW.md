# Review of the exact Fibonacci-partition running-minimum theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof establishes a complement involution for short offsets, an all-index characterization of unique partitions, an exact two-representation classification, and the published two-index descent identity. Their combination yields a lower bound and **all** equality cases in the stated window; the endpoint is handled separately. Exact-integer finite verification checks all windows for indices 7–21, but is not substituted for the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (subject to historical-priority uncertainty).** The 2026 motivating article proves short-average estimates and a useful descent identity, not the optimization statement. Robbins, Weinstein, and Chow–Slattery give representation recurrences, count-level information and full evaluational formulas; these are acknowledged, not republished as novel. Search and substantive source comparison did not locate the explicit running-minimum staircase with its complete extremizer set. General formulas for evaluating each coefficient do not explicitly supply this moving-window optimality theorem. A possible unindexed earlier formulation remains a genuine risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Exact minima and all attaining offsets across every positive window in the first Fibonacci block answer a natural extremal counterpart to the new short-average theorem. The result describes a structural staircase and gives the precise logarithmic extremal scale in power-length windows, rather than a single numerical example or routine parameter substitution. It is a concise elementary theorem, not an independent solution to a larger conjecture.

**Scope and risks.** This is a theorem about distinct Fibonacci subset-sum representations, not unrestricted partitions. It only covers \(1\le H\le F_{m-1}-1\) and \(m\ge7\); no global distribution, maximum, or independent verification is claimed. Prior representation formulas and singleton facts are credited. Earlier unindexed literature might already have the optimization interpretation.
