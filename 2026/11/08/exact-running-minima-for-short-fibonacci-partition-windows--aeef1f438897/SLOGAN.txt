For every initial interval immediately after a Fibonacci number, the minimum number of distinct-Fibonacci partitions follows an exact Fibonacci-index staircase, with all attaining offsets explicitly given by at most two neighboring Fibonacci numbers minus one. In power-length windows the minimum grows as \(\frac{1-\alpha}{2\log\varphi}\log F_m+O_\alpha(1)\), complementing known short-average asymptotics.
