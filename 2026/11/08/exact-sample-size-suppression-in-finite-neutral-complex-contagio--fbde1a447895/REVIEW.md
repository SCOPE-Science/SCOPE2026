# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The specified with-replacement binomial model gives exact interior birth--death rates from a second-moment identity; solving the harmonic equation yields the Gamma-weight formula. Reflection and monotonicity follow from finite positive products, and a strictly concave saddlepoint phase with correct Stirling factors proves the exponential rate and prefactor. A standalone exact-rational verifier checked the unsimplified binomial mechanism, recurrence and independent absorption linear systems; the large-population claim is analytical, not extrapolated from finite tests. Boundary cases \(N=2\), \(m=1\) and finite \(m\ge2\) are explicitly separated.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with priority risk).** Direct comparison with Chiba-Okabe–Plotkin's stochastic framework and the two principal nonlinear \(q\)-voter studies finds the microscopic rule and qualitative ordering behavior already known. The latter studies use a mean-field transition law and diffusion approximation, rather than the exact finite sampling-normalization formula, its strict sample-size ordering, and its explicit \(N^{-1/2}\) rare-mutant asymptotic with a specified rate. Searches of relevant research records and accessible prior findings did not identify a theorem with these conclusions. This is not a proof of worldwide priority: the broad nonlinear-voter literature may contain an equivalent finite-state treatment.

**Value — PASS.** Finite contact sampling is part of the motivating social-learning mechanism. The theorem quantifies a sharp finite-sample distinction between one contact (neutral \(1/N\) survival) and any fixed number of at least two contacts (exponential survival suppression), with an exact exposure-size-dependent rate and prefactor. Those constants are useful as finite-population invasion benchmarks and distinguish contact-sampling effects from payoff selection. The work is narrower than a general game-theoretic or network model, but resolves a natural exact invariant not stated in the inspected primary analyses.

**Closest literature and scope.** Chiba-Okabe and Plotkin (2024) give the underlying update and continuum analysis. Castellano, Muñoz and Pastor-Satorras (2009) and Moretti et al. (2013) already describe the equivalent mean-field polynomial for \(q=2\), so the present claim does not assert a new contagion rule or qualitative ferromagnetic transition. Their Fokker--Planck analyses do not directly cover the exact Gamma-weight fixation census or its fixed-encounter large-deviation prefactor. The sampling convention, neutrality, and fixed-encounter asymptotic restrictions are essential.
