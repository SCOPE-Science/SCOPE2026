A finite neutral quadratic social-contagion process has exact fixation probabilities given by symmetric Gamma-product weights, and its one-mutant success probability strictly falls with every increase in the number of encounters. Just two independently sampled contacts produce a sharp \(N^{-1/2}e^{-NI_2}\) rarity law, with \(I_2=\log(32/27)\), instead of the ordinary voter law \(1/N\).
