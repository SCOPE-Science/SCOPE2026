Two concentric regular \(n\)-gons of unequal scale have a complete, elementary rotation-overlap profile: once their apothem ratio exceeds \(\cos(\pi/n)\), half-step rotation uniquely minimizes overlap, while a precisely quantified alignment plateau maximizes it. The first loss past containment is quadratic at unequal scales but becomes linear when the scales coincide, and radial weighted overlaps obey the same angular order.
