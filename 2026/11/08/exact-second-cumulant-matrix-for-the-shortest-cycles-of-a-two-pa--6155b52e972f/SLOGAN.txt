In the two-parent uniform-attachment recursive multigraph, the complete covariance matrix of parallel-edge counts and edge-labeled triangle counts has exact finite harmonic-number formulas, with nonzero zeta-valued second-cumulant corrections. For every \(n\ge4\), the triangle count is strictly underdispersed relative to its Poisson approximation, and its covariance with the double-edge count is strictly negative despite asymptotic joint Poisson approximation.
