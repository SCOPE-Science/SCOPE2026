The classification is obtained by determining the anti-involution action on the diagonal quotient and the square-zero radical. Every basis arrow must go to its reversed edge multiplied by a nonzero scalar. The involution equations pair scalars along edge orbits: fixed edges contribute \(q+1\) norm-one possibilities and two-edge orbits contribute \(q^2-1\). The image family of primitive idempotents determines a unique radical twist; squaring forces that twist into the \(q^m\)-element fixed-radical space. Edge rescaling plus additive Hilbert 90 establishes conjugacy. The proof works for all prime powers, including characteristic two. Three independently organized finite verification scripts passed their stated exhaustive domains; the general claim is proved symbolically rather than inferred from those domains.
