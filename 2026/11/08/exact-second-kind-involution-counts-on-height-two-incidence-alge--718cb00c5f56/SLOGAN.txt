Every connected height-two incidence algebra over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) has an exact second-kind-involution census: an order-reversing poset involution fixing \(f\) of the \(m\) edges contributes precisely \(q^m(q+1)^f(q^2-1)^{(m-f)/2}\) anti-involutions. Despite their potentially different counts, all maps inducing the same poset involution are algebra-conjugate, even in characteristic two.
