# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The radical is square zero; reduction modulo it and the nonzero edge bimodules force the induced permutation to be an order-reversing involution and the restriction to each edge to be the claimed Frobenius-semilinear map. In a lifted complete idempotent family, an edge coordinate may occur only at its endpoints, and the two coordinates are negatives. It is therefore obtained by a unique Ad(1+v), v in J; translations central in J are zero since they commute with all vertex idempotents. After this change, each fixed edge has q+1 norm-one coefficients and each paired orbit has q^2−1 choices; the square of the twisted reversal is Ad(1+v−rho_c(v)). Its fixed-twist space has q choices on a fixed edge (rescale via finite-field Hilbert 90) and q^2 on a pair. Independent edge scaling is multiplicative because J^2=0; it normalizes the coefficients. The remaining conjugation equation x+rho_c(x)=v is surjective onto the fixed space by field trace on each fixed edge and the one-free-coordinate construction on each pair. In characteristic two the trace is still nonzero: the kernel of x+x^q is exactly F_q. An automorphism permutes the quotient vertices preserving the same edge bimodules, and every poset automorphism lifts, giving the claimed orbit quotient. For the six-vertex crown, label the missing edges by equal indices. A duality is then an involution of the three indices: the identity fixes no actual edges, and a transposition fixes two; conjugation is ordinary S_3 conjugation. For the complete bipartite poset, all matchings are admissible, fix one edge per lower vertex and are conjugate.
