For uniformly random length-\(2m\) binary words with \(2h\) ones and \(\gcd(m,h)=1\), the number of distinct 2-binomial signatures across cyclic rotations has explicit finite first and second moments. When \(h/m\to p\in(0,1)\), its collision deficit has mean asymptotic to \(\sqrt{m/(\pi p(1-p))}\) and variance asymptotic to \(m(\pi-2)/(2\pi p(1-p))\).
