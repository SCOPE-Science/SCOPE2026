For uniformly random \(q\)-ary inputs, the logarithm of the radius-one limited-permutation output-sphere size obeys an explicit positive-variance Gaussian law, while the sphere's exact finite-length second moment is a power of a three-state matrix. The Perron growth rate of that second moment strictly exceeds the square of the mean growth rate for every fixed \(q\ge2\), so relative variance increases exponentially with block length.
