For stationary walks on a possibly lazy complete graph, the union of \(k\) independent ranges with total length \(T\) has exactly the same **labeled-set law** as one length-\(T\) walk plus \(k-1\) independent uniform vertices, regardless of the individual lifespans. This yields a set-inclusion coupling, exact finite-size distribution and collaboration gap, giving complete-graph answers to the stochastic-domination and quantitative-gap questions of Dey, Kim and Terlov.
