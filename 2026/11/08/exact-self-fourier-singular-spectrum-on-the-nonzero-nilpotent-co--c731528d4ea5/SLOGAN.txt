The complex self-Fourier matrix on all nonzero square-zero \(2\times2\) matrices over any finite field is invertible, and its complete singular spectrum has only two levels in even characteristic and four in odd characteristic. Its sharp Euclidean condition number is \(q\) for even \(q\) and \(q^{3/2}\) for odd \(q\), with no restriction on the nontrivial additive character.
