For the diagonal entire-function composition \(Tf(z)=c f(\lambda_1z_1,\ldots,\lambda_dz_d)\), two-sided shadowing holds exactly when all coordinate dilations strictly contract or all strictly expand, and no Taylor monomial has multiplier of modulus one. Mixed expanding–contracting directions destroy shadowing even without any resonant eigenmonomial, because small polynomial pseudotrajectory errors can force a tracing vector with non-entire Taylor growth.
