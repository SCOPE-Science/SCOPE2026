For rooted out-arborescences in a complete bidirected graph, the shattered directed-arc supports are precisely the cycle-free, indegree-at-most-one arc sets of size at most \(n-2\). The complete support polynomial is \(1+\sum_{s=1}^{n-2}\binom{n-1}{s}(n-s)n^{s-1}z^s\), giving exact VC dimension \(n-2\) and a pure shattering complex.
