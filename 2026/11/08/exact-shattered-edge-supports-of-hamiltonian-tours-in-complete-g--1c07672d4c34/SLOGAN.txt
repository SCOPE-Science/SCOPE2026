For the edge-incidence family of all Hamiltonian cycles of \(K_n\), the shattered supports are exactly the linear forests with at most \(n-3\) edges, together with certain two-component spanning forests of size \(n-2\). At the maximum size, two nontrivial paths survive precisely when each has at least three edges, yielding an exact all-degree shattering polynomial and \(\operatorname{VCdim}=n-2\) for \(n\ge5\).
