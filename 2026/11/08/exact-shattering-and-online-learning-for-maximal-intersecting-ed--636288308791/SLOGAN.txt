The shattered query supports of all maximal intersecting families of edges of \(K_n\) are precisely the size-at-most-three cliques in its line graph, with complete polynomial \(1+\binom n2z+3\binom n3z^2+(n-2)\binom n3z^3\). For every \(n\ge4\), both VC and Littlestone dimensions equal \(3\); the shattering complex is a wedge of \(\binom n2+(n-5)\binom n3-1\) two-spheres.
