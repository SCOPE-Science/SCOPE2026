Cuts of a complete graph whose selected shore has exactly two vertices have an exactly classified shattering complex: its maximal shattered edge supports have four edges, and its full face polynomial is given by ten small graph motifs. The VC dimension is \(1\) for \(n=3,4\), \(2\) for \(n=5\), \(3\) for \(n=6\), and \(4\) for all \(n\ge7\).
