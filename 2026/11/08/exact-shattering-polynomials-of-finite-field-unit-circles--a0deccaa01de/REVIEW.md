# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The zero-dimensional coefficient, singleton and pair trace criteria, antipodal-center reflection, and the triple trace construction are proved for arbitrary odd finite fields and nonzero radii. Nonzero conics have exactly \(k=q-\chi_q(-1)\) points. Distinct fixed-radius circles intersect in at most two points, including when isotropic directions exist; the proof explicitly rules out an affine line component of a nonzero circle. The inequalities \(q^2>2k\) for \(q\ge3\), \(q^2>3k\) for \(q\ge7\), and \(k\ge8\) for \(q\ge7\) are checked. The two exceptional fields have \(k=4\) and lack any antipode-free triple. Each coefficient follows from the multiplicity of circle centers. Independent finite incidence-bitset checks include all radii for \(q=3,5,7,9\) and radius one for \(q=11,13\). The exact proof, not just these checks, establishes the infinite family.

Earlier exact count results for related Hamming balls are metric-incompatible rather than covering.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This is a complete face-enumerator of a canonical finite-field distance-graph neighborhood concept class. It gives the exact entire shattering complex for a nontrivial infinite family, separates the two small-field exceptions, and reveals precise dependence on \(\chi_q(-1)\). This is stronger in structure than the previously studied asymptotic VC scalar. Pair and triple counts also yield exact incidence densities for learning-theoretic tests.
