For every odd prime power \(q\) and every nonzero radius parameter, the complete shattered-support polynomial of the finite-field plane's circle-membership class is \(1+q^2z+q^2k^2z^2/4+q^2k(k-2)(k-4)z^3/6\), with \(k=q-\chi_q(-1)\). Shattered triples are exactly concyclic triples containing no antipodal pair, and the VC dimension is two only for \(q=3,5\), otherwise three.
