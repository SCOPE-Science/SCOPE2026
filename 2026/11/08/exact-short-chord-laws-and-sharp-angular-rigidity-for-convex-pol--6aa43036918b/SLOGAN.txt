For two independent arclength-uniform points on any convex polygon boundary, the short-chord probability is exactly \(2r/P+B(Q)r^2/P^2\) at sufficiently small radius, where \(B(Q)=\sum_i(\theta_i/\sin\theta_i-1)\) depends only on exterior angles. Its excess above the equiangular value controls the squared angular variance with the globally optimal, side-count-independent constant \(1/6\).
