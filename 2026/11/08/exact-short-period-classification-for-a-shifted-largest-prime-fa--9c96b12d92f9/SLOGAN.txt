For the prime-shift/largest-prime-divisor map \(\varphi_k\), every primitive cycle of length at most four has an exact arithmetic classification: three-cycles occur precisely at Fermat-prime parameters \(k=2^{2^s}-1\), while four-cycles split into a two-prime smoothness family \(k=tpq-p-q\) and an exceptional three-prime powers-of-three family. The classification is necessary and sufficient for every positive shift, without claiming to classify longer periods.
