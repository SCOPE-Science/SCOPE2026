For a two-block fully independent SparseStack restricted to a fixed coordinate subspace, full rank is governed exactly by unbalanced signed cycles in a bipartite multigraph. If the subspace dimension divided by the number of buckets per block tends to \(c\), the full-rank probability tends to \((1-c^2)^{1/4}\) for \(0\le c<1\), and to zero for \(c\ge1\); this is a rank-only guarantee, not a lower singular-value bound.
