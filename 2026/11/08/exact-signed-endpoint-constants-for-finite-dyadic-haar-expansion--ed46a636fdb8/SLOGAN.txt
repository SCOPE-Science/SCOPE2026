The signed unconditionality constant of the full depth-\(d\) dyadic Haar system in \(L^\infty\) is exactly \(\frac{2d}{3}+\frac59+\frac49(-\frac12)^d\), giving sharp linear endpoint growth with an alternating exponentially small correction. Every maximizing transform is described along a norming dyadic path by a two-state sign rule, and a row-kernel sign function attains the sharp norm.
