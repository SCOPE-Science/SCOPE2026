At the degenerate fixed point of the reduced Chialvo neuron map, every nonnegative trajectory converges, but all nonexceptional trajectories do so at the critical \(n^{-1/2}\) scale. The exact reciprocal-square relaxation has a side-dependent \(\sqrt n\) correction and a universal logarithmic correction \(\bigl(59/160+\sqrt2/5\bigr)\log n\), with a finite renormalized limit for each initial condition.
