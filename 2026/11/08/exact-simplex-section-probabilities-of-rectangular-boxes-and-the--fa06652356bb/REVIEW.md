# Mathematical review: higher-dimensional Crofton simplex sections

## Correctness — PASS
For \(d\ge4\), an affine hyperplane section of a rectangular box is a simplex exactly when its intercept lies in one of the two outermost projected-vertex gaps. This follows from the minimum-edge-cut theorem and singleton equality classification for a hypercube. Integrating the two gap lengths against the invariant width-weighted hyperplane law gives the ratio of expected minimum to expected width. Replacing Haar directions by normalized Gaussian vectors gives the stated erfc integral with its exact normalization. Symmetric concavity and positive-measure branch switching prove strict Schur concavity, and the halfnormal density at zero gives the \(\pi/d^2\) asymptotic. The finite checker corroborates, but does not prove, unrestricted statements.
