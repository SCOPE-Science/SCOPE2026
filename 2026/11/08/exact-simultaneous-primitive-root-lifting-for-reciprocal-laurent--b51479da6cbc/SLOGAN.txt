For every admissible primitive pair \(a,a\pm a^{-1}\) modulo an odd prime \(p\ge7\), exactly \(p^{k-2}(p-2+\delta)\) lifts give two primitive roots modulo \(p^k\), where \(\delta\in\{0,1\}\) is an explicit Fermat-quotient coincidence test. This proves the prime-power existence cases of Sun's Conjecture 4.10 and identifies the complete exceptional prime sets.
