A single input at the first cycle vertex of a unit-weight cycle with one positively weighted pendant leaf is fully controllable at every edge weight except an explicit finite resonance set, with rank loss at most two. At leaf conductance \(t=1\) the loss occurs exactly for \(n\equiv6\pmod8\), and at \(t=\tfrac12\) exactly for \(n\equiv7\pmod{10}\), despite the input vertex never forming a singleton zero-forcing set.
