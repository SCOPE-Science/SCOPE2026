Inserting exactly one bit into a linear binary word creates at most six distinct adjacent-pair-frequency profiles, with the exact count determined by five simple occurrence and endpoint indicators. The complete all-length profile-ball distribution has explicit Fibonacci–Lucas formulas; for a uniformly random long word, five and six profiles each occur with limiting probability \(1/2\). Six distinct deletion profiles always imply six distinct insertion profiles, but the two channels have different saturation laws.
