The profile-to-run-statistics map is invertible. Every fixed run-statistics fiber is a product of one-color run-composition sets under diagonal cyclic rotation, and the one-color transitivity criterion follows from the explicit binomial vector count and orbit-size bound. The paired-orbit criterion is exact for all run counts, including two. Summing the resulting disjoint cases proves the rational function and the asymptotic. Direct word enumeration through length 18 and independent run-composition and rational recurrence checks support, but do not replace, this all-length proof.
