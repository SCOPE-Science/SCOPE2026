# Scientific review — Exact singular and arithmetic strata in a Frobenius-twisted cubic linear system

## Correctness (C): PASS

For every finite field of order \(q\), the \(q^3+q^2+q+1\) projective parameters are partitioned by the nonzero Frobenius coefficient \(a\) and the equation \(d^3+27\operatorname{N}(a)=0\). The torus derivative matrix has determinant nine in characteristic other than three; in characteristic three the unique point follows from injectivity of the cube map over an algebraic closure. Complete local equations \(s^2-st+t^2+s^2t\) and \(r^2+s^2r-s^3\) prove the node or cusp type. The odd-degree splitting test and genus-zero normalization give the point counts. Both independent implementation routes pass: exact prime-field family enumeration and symbolic local identities. The general theorem is not inferred from its four finite-field examples.

## Originality (O): PASS

Asgarli–Ghioca (arXiv:2407.18104, Lemma 8 and Theorem 5) construct the specific family and establish its all-but-one geometric irreducibility; these facts are acknowledged as prior results. The complete smooth/nodal/cuspidal partition, norm singularity criterion, split-node test and exact rational point counts do not follow from those claims. Earlier work on hypersurfaces through Galois orbits (arXiv:2310.10361) and related later work (arXiv:2501.01906) concern irreducibility/existence rather than this quantitative classification. Indexed binary all-smooth cubic-net results and a different Frobenius cyclic hypersurface-system result are not covering. Targeted searches for alternative names, norm equations, exact tables and stronger coverage returned no dominating statement.

Residual risk: an earlier unindexed or unexamined source could present an equivalent norm-discriminant calculation, possibly without using this source's terminology. Such priority is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS

This determines natural discriminant and arithmetic invariants of every rational member of a motivated extremal family, including a characteristic-three cusp transition and two arithmetic node regimes. Unlike a routine special-case count, the result gives exact uniform formulas and a full natural classification for all prime powers, with an explicit norm equation identifying singular members.

## Limitations and references

Only the specified four-dimensional linear system is treated, and its generic smooth members do not have a uniform point count. The parameter equation is set-theoretic for rational members, not a discriminant scheme statement. Sources: arXiv:2407.18104v1 (first public July 25, 2024), arXiv:2310.10361v1 and arXiv:2501.01906v1. Verification is local symbolic identity and exhaustive rational-member checks at four prime fields; the universal conclusion uses the algebraic proof.
