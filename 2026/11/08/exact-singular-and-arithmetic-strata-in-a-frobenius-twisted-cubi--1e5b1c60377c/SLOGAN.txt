In the explicit Frobenius-descended four-dimensional plane-cubic system of Asgarli–Ghioca, exactly \(q^3-1\) rational members are smooth, exactly \(q^2+q+1\) are geometrically integral with one node or cusp, and one is a conjugate-line triangle. The singular integral members satisfy the norm equation \(d^3+27\operatorname{N}(a)=0\); their rational point counts are exactly \(q\), \(q+2\), or \(q+1\), according to the field characteristic and node splitting.
