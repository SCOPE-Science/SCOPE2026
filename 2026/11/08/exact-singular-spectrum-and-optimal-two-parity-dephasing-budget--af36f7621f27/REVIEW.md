The recent tunable coherent/classical quantum-walk interpolation makes finite-time and strength-allocation questions natural. The result gives an exact dimension-independent worst-case mixing law for every nonstationary schedule and the complete resource-optimal allocation; it identifies that splitting a low budget into just two opposite-parity impulses is necessary and sufficient.
