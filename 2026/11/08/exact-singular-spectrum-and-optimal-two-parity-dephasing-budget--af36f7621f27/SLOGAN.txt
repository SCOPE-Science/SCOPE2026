For every complex-Hadamard unitary whose square is monomial, the full finite-time Hilbert--Schmidt singular spectrum of an arbitrarily dephased unitary-channel sequence depends only on the products of the residual coherence factors at odd and even steps. Under any fixed total dephasing-probability budget \(B\), the exact minimum worst-case mixing error is \(\max\{0,1-B/2\}\): when \(0<B<2\), every optimal schedule concentrates its dephasing into exactly one odd and one even pulse.
