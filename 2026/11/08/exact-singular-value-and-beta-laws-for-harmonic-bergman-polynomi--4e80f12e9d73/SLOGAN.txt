For every analytic polynomial of degree \(d\ge2\), the Toeplitz functional-calculus defect \(T_p-p(T_z)\) on harmonic Bergman space is square-zero of exact rank \(d-1\). For the monomial sequence \(p(z)=z^m\), every singular value is explicit and the empirical singular spectra converge to the beta-derived density \(4s/\sqrt{1-4s^2}\) on \((0,1/2)\).
