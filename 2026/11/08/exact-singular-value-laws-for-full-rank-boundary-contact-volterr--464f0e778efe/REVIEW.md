# Scientific review

## Correctness
The normalized ball monomials are orthogonal and the radial companion maps each monomial to a unique transverse-shifted monomial. The exact coefficient follows from the normalized Bergman monomial norm. For each fixed damped multi-index, the weights decay as a negative power of undamped degree; the number of undamped monomials has an exact binomial multiplicity. The convergent damping-weighted series dominates the rescaled counting functions, making the interchange of asymptotics and the infinite transverse sum legitimate. The counting inversion proves the all-index singular asymptotic and the Schatten/weak-Schatten boundary. Exact rational finite tests passed, but they are corroborative only.

## Originality
The nearest motivating paper is Dong–Xu (arXiv:2609.27584), whose Section 3.3 treats a rank-deficient projection and records a power-law order, while Proposition 4.8 applies only when the image is relatively compact. The present family is full rank with nonzero strict transverse contraction, retains infinitely many transverse modes, and gives the exact asymptotic coefficient depending on every contraction. Li–Long–Wang (arXiv:2609.22908) treats ordinary weighted compositions/Toeplitz forms, and Mengestie (arXiv:1507.03536) concerns Fock spaces. Comparison of actual statements, accessible historical claims, and relevant research records found no covering theorem. A broader historical weighted-shift asymptotic remains a genuine priority risk.

## Mathematical value
The result classifies a natural family with boundary contact but compact companions, including sharp Schatten thresholds and critical coefficients. The geometric contact dimension and symbol vanishing order control the exponent; the damped coordinates control the exact coefficient. This refines the qualitative and order-only results in nearby literature and includes full-rank self-maps not covered by the compact-range theorem.

## Limitations
The statement is restricted to strict transverse diagonal contractions and monomial symbols on unweighted ball Bergman space. It does not assert general small-exponent derivative-Carleson criteria or general Weyl laws for arbitrary convex domains. The collapsed-projection threshold already appears in the initiating literature; no novelty is claimed for that isolated exponent.
