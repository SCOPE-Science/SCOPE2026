A full-rank diagonal self-map of the complex unit ball can touch a positive-dimensional boundary sphere and still produce a compact generalized Volterra companion when its transverse monomial symbol vanishes to positive order. The complete singular multiset and a sharp Weyl coefficient show that the Schatten threshold is exactly \(p=2h/k\), where \(h\) counts undamped coordinates and \(k\) is the transverse vanishing order; the coefficient measures every strict transverse contraction.
