For every existing finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\), exactly \(q^2+q\) independently controlled vertices suffice for Laplacian controllability, and a single square incidence determinant classifies every minimum placement. For its fourteen-vertex Heawood incidence graph, exactly \(1890\) of the \(3003\) six-vertex choices work, arranged into \(14\) symmetry orbits under the full \(336\)-element graph automorphism group.
