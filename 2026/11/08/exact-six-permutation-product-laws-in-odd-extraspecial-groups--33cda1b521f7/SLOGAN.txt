For any odd prime \(p\), the complete six-outcome distribution of permuted products of three independent uniform elements is explicit and identical for both extraspecial group types of order \(p^{2m+1}\). Exactly three cardinalities occur at \(p=3\), five at \(p=5\), and all six at \(p\ge7\), with probabilities controlled by a uniform nonzero symplectic Gram triple.
