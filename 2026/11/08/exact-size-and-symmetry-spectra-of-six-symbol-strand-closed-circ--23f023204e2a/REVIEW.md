# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is an exact finite classification, including the declared empty-code convention. The directed-cycle equivalence is derived from the two possible pairwise frames. The reversal-complement source/sink lemma constructs symmetric topological orders and proves that all valid codebooks occur among the 48 maximal tournaments. The nine undirected-pair complement orbits give precisely three choices each, so the direct 19,683-case search is exhaustive. A separate enumeration of all 48 symmetric orders and 512 strand-closed subsets per order yields the same size histogram; Burnside's lemma reproduces all 16 symmetry-class counts. The 220 representative codeword lists reconstruct the 8,383 labeled sets under the specified group. There is no sampling or extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Michel–Pirillo (2013) directly establishes the four-letter case (24 maximum codes, eight strand-closed) and explicitly identifies expansion to larger alphabets as a natural research direction. Pellegrini–Pirillo (2014) treats maxima for arbitrary alphabet sizes, not the complete six-symbol code-size enumerator. Michel–Pellegrini–Pirillo (2015) studies four-letter maximal circular codes, and Fimmel–Michel–Strüngmann (2016) gives the directed-acyclic graph criterion that is used here. The new information assessed is the full 16-coefficient six-letter enumerator and the 220-class size-refined symmetry quotient with explicit representatives. Searches for a six-letter strand-closed circular-code census, equivalent acyclic anti-invariant digraphs, and type-B signed partial-order histograms did not find this result. Historical-priority risk remains for unindexed enumerations, and a full-text view of the 2014 all-alphabet maximum-codes article was unavailable.

## Value — PASS

The first even alphabet beyond four letters introduces three complement pairs and a nontrivial 9-orbit choice space. The result describes every feasible strand-closed circular dinucleotide code, including sizes below the familiar maximum, instead of only exhibiting a largest construction. The symmetry quotient determines 220 inequivalent design types, and the complete representative list makes the classification reusable. This is a natural finite benchmark for complement-invariant synchronization codebooks; biological performance is not inferred.

## Relationship to earlier results and residual risks

The general digraph criterion and maximum-tournament construction are not claimed as new. The comparison addresses implications: a theorem determining only maximal codebooks cannot determine the entire enumerator or orbit statistics at each smaller size, whereas the 2013 biological-alphabet result has only four symbols. The full 2014 article was not available on the examined access routes; a hidden complete six-letter enumeration remains possible. Source metadata alone does not certify historical priority. No claim of a general all-alphabet counting formula is made.
