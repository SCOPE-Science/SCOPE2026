# Review of the exact Jacobsthal companion sliding-square GCD formulas

**Correctness — PASS.** The two Binet formulas yield explicit integer window expressions. A third-order monic recurrence reduces the infinite gcd to three windows; a \(432\)-determinant argument handles all primes at least five. The remaining primes are resolved exactly by modulo-16 squares and a checked 3-adic binomial congruence. The companion correction agrees with direct exact-integer checks for 700 lengths and both sequences. This is a complete proof rather than an extrapolation.

The full-text 2022 sliding-GCD work of Mbirika–Spilker is for the Fibonacci recurrence and the 2023 full-text Pell/balancing work treats other recurrences. Indexed-literature searches and available prior claimed results did not locate a dominating theorem. **Mathematical value — PASS.** This closes the full sliding-square GCD determination simultaneously for a natural Lucas companion pair, including a precisely bounded two-part, an exact exceptional three-factor, and all larger prime valuations from a single length-dependent gcd. The comparison is motivated by established sliding-GCD studies and earlier Jacobsthal square-sum identities; it is neither a one-off numerical instance nor a routine rewording of a known finite sum formula.
