The sliding-square GCDs of the Jacobsthal and Jacobsthal–Lucas companion sequences have complete formulas: both equal \(2^{\min\{\nu_2(k),3\}}\gcd(k,2^k-(-1)^k)\), except that the Jacobsthal value is divided by \(3\) when \(3\mid k\). Thus the companion ratio is exactly \(3\) at multiples of three and \(1\) elsewhere, while the common two-adic valuation saturates at three.
