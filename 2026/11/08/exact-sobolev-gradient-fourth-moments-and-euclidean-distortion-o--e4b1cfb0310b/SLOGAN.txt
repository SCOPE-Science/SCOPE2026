For real degree-two spherical harmonics in every dimension, the tangential-gradient \(L^4/L^2\) ratio has exact parity-dependent sharp endpoints: balanced spectra minimize, and one-outlier spectra maximize. The resulting gradient \(L^4\) norm is Hilbertian only on the circle and two-sphere, while its optimal Euclidean Banach--Mazur distortion is an explicit rational fourth root approaching \(3^{1/4}\).
