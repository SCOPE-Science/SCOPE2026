The auxiliary piecewise-affine comparison map for Fibonacci bimodal dynamics has exact Lebesgue-sampled first-return and maximum-height laws for every \(0<\lambda<1\). Near the recurrence transition, return times and excursion heights have distinct parameter-window widths, \(N^{-1/2}\) and \(L^{-1}\), with explicit nontrivial crossover profiles.
