A Brownian particle in a randomly switched on/off harmonic trap has a sharp asymmetric-rate threshold where its entire stationary covariance is exactly that of an equilibrium Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle, although its excess kurtosis is strictly positive. Across the threshold, the fast relaxation mode switches from a negative to a positive covariance coefficient, separating non-completely-monotone from completely-monotone temporal correlations.
