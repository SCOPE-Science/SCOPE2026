For arbitrary sparse Fourier frequencies observed through \(q\) equally spaced, shrinking arcs, the complete concentration spectrum has an exact arithmetic staircase: each residue class modulo \(q\) contributes eigenvalues \(c_j(q\alpha)^{2j+1}\), where every \(c_j\) is an explicit positive ratio of integer moment determinants and Legendre norms. The worst residue-class multiplicity determines the sharp conditioning exponent, while collision-free residue classes give a perfectly tight restriction frame at every admissible arc width.
