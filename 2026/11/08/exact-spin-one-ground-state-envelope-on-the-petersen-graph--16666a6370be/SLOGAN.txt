The classical spin-one antiferromagnet on the Petersen graph has an exact seven-branch zero-temperature energy envelope in the nonnegative-field half-plane, yielding twelve open chambers for arbitrary real magnetic and crystal fields. At zero magnetic field, crystal-field tuning drives a sharp occupancy-and-magnetization cascade \((K,M)=(10,\pm2)\to(6,0)\to(0,0)\), with exact degeneracies \(30\) and \(21\) at the two coexistence points.
