Every four-run binary circular word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\) has a closed exact count of its distinct ordinary circular square factors: its mixed-letter contribution is \(1\), \(\min(a,c)+1\), \(\min(b,d)+1\), or \(a+b\), according to which opposite run pairs agree. This yields the sharp maximum \(\lfloor3n/4\rfloor\) for lengths \(n\ge5\), except \(n\equiv3\pmod4\), where it is one less; at \(n=4\) the maximum is two.
