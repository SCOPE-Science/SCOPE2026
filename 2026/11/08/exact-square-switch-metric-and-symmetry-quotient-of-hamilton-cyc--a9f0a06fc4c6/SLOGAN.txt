For cyclic Gray codes on the four-cube \(Q_4\), every nonfrozen Hamilton cycle is reachable from every other by coordinate-square inversions, in at most twelve steps; the unique mobile component has \(1104\) codes, radius \(10\), and diameter \(12\). A complete symmetry quotient gives the local exchange structure, while the \(240\) frozen codes and the inversion operation themselves are acknowledged as previously studied.
