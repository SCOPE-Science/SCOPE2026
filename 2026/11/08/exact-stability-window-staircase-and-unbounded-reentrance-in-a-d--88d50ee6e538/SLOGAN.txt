For the Holling-II predator–prey model with delayed prey regulation and instantaneous predator conversion, the complete linear-stability set is an exact two-frequency staircase, with the number of reentrant stable windows given by \(\max\{0,\lceil T\rceil-1\}\). A positive-parameter family realizes arbitrarily many such windows as intrinsic prey growth decreases, with a sharp \(r^{-1/2}\) count law.
