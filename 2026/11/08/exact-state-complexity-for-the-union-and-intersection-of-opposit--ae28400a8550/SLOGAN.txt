For the binary subsequence ideals generated by \(0^a1^b\) and \(1^b0^a\), the exact minimal complete DFA sizes of their intersection and union are respectively \(\frac{(a+b)(a+b+3)}2+ab\) and \(\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+ab\) for every \(a,b\ge1\). The entire difference of \(a+b\) states is the accepting-sink collapse; all other reachable product states are pairwise distinguishable.
