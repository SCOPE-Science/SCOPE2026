For every alphabet size \(m\ge2\) and shift \(c\ge0\), the shifted generalized period-doubling word has **exactly** \(\lceil\log_2(c+1)\rceil+2m\) states in its minimal binary least-significant-digit-first DFAO. The same formula counts its distinct binary \(2\)-kernel sequences, with an explicit residual-state classification proving minimality.
