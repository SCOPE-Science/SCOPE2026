For the binary language in which the scattered occurrence count of \(0^d1\) lies in a prescribed residue class modulo \(m\), the minimal complete DFA has exactly \(m\prod_{p^a\parallel m}p^{a+\lfloor\log_p d\rfloor}\) states. An explicit counting automaton attains this number, and a subgroup argument proves that every state is Myhill–Nerode distinguishable.
