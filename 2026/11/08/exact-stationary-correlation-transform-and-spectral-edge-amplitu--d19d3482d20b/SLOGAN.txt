The Pelikan random doubling/halving map has an exact algebraic generating function for **every stationary position-covariance lag**, with no commuting-map approximation. Its correlations cross from square-root spectral-edge behavior to a dominant pole at \(p=4/5\): the precise critical law is \(C_n\sim 2(4/5)^n/[9\sqrt{2\pi n}]\), while above the threshold the exact leading exponential amplitude is \((5p-4)/[12(2p-1)]\).
