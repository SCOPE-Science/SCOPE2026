In an all-to-all Bell-measurement-only quantum circuit, the pairing dynamics is exactly reversible with a finite-size monomer–dimer stationary law, yielding the entire stationary cut-entanglement distribution by a single counting polynomial. Every macroscopic partition has a sharp asymptotically sub-Poisson entropy Fano ratio, at most \(1/(2-u)<1\), with the maximal ratio at an equal-half cut.
