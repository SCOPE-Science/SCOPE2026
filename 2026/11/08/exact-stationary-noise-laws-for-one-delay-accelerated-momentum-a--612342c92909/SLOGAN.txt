For the deterministically rate-optimal one-delay DREAM tuning on a two-eigenvalue quadratic, iid additive gradient noise produces stationary iterate mean-square error asymptotic to \(\sigma^2\kappa^{3/2}/(8L^2)\). Optimally rate-tuned one-delay gradient descent instead has error \(5\sigma^2\kappa/(6L^2)\), giving a precise \((3/20)\sqrt\kappa\) variance penalty in exchange for the square-root improvement in deterministic spectral gap.
