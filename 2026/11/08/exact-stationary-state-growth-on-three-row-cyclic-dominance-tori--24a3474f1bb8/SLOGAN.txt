For the three-color threshold-four cyclic automaton on every periodic \(3\times N\) Moore lattice with \(N\ge3\), the exact number of frozen configurations is a fixed combination of power sums of two explicit degree-18 polynomials and \(6^N\). Their growth is asymptotic to \(\lambda^N\), where \(8.6245<\lambda<8.6246\), giving an exact stationary-state entropy for the full infinite three-row strip.
