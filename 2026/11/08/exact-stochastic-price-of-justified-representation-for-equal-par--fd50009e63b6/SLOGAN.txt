On equal-sized party-list approval profiles, justified representation forces one candidate per party; under iid candidate quality, the optimality cost has an exact finite expectation and a dependent-block Gaussian limit. For \(q\ge2\) uniform-quality candidates per party and \(k\) parties, the expected welfare loss is exactly \(k(k-1)(q-1)/[2(kq+1)(q+1)]\), and for \(q=2\) the fair-to-unconstrained welfare ratio tends to \(8/9\).
