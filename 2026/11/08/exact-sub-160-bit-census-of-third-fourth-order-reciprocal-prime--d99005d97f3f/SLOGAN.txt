There are exactly six ordered prime pairs with \(\operatorname{ord}_q(p)=3\), \(\operatorname{ord}_p(q)=4\), and \(\max\{p,q\}\le 2^{160}\). A complete quadratic-descent enumeration reduces the huge finite search to \(169\) integer pairs, with the nonprime candidates excluded by parity and exact Fermat witnesses.
