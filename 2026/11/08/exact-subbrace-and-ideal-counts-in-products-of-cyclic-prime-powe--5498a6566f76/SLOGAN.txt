For direct products of two cyclic \(p\)-power radical-ring braces, every subbrace and ideal has a unique congruence parametrization, yielding exact formulas for both counts and for the defect \(S-I\). The product of two individually Dedekind factors can fail to be Dedekind; the precise boundary is \((k>\ell,\ m=\ell+1)\) or its factor-symmetric counterpart.
