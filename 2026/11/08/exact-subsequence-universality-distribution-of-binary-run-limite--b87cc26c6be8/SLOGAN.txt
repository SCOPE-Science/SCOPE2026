For binary words forbidding \(r+1\) consecutive ones, the complete length–subsequence-universality distribution is \(\bigl(1+z-z^{r+1}-uz^{r+2}\bigr)/\bigl(1-z-2uz^2+uz^{r+2}+u^2z^{r+3}\bigr)\) for every integer \(r\ge1\). At the shortest possible length \(2k\), the number of \(k\)-universal words drops sharply from \(2^k\) for \(r\ge2\) to \(k+1\) for \(r=1\), and the same generating function gives exact typical universality rates.
