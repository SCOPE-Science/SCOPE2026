For every even alphabet with a fixed-point-free complementation, the entire subsequence-universality distribution of reverse-complement symmetric words has an explicit rational generating function, including \(\frac{1-2z-5z^2+6z^3+8uz^2}{1-6z+11z^2-6z^3-24u^2z^4}\) for four-letter DNA-style complementation (where \(z\) records half-length). The shortest symmetric words that contain every length-\(j\) subsequence have length exactly \(qj\), and their number is \((q!)^{j/2}\) for even \(j\) or \((q!)^{(j-1)/2}2^{q/2}(q/2)!\) for odd \(j\).
