For the binary fixed points of \(0\mapsto0^{b-1}1\), \(1\mapsto1^{b-1}0\) with every integer \(b\ge3\), the exact Abelian complexity at length \(b^k\) is \(2\sum_{j=0}^k(b-2)^j-(b-2)^k+1\). It grows linearly in \(k\) for \(b=3\) but exponentially in \(k\) for every \(b\ge4\), with both extremal factor weights explicitly realized.
