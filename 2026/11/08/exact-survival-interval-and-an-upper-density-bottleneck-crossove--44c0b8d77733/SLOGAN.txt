At the generalized Allee-logistic fold, the positive population survives precisely from the fold density \(\rho\) to an explicit upper crowding cutoff \(s\), while all larger densities collapse to extinction. Just below the fold, the extinction delay develops an exact arctangent crossover when initial density differs from \(s\) by order \(\sqrt\varepsilon\), unifying full, half, and absent bottleneck passage.
