In binary friendship games on even cycles, fixed-size pair coalitions are swap stable exactly when their unhappy vertices are nonadjacent and no unhappy pair closes an alternating three-edge path. An exact inclusion-exclusion formula counts every stable pairing, and the sharp price of anarchy is \(1\) for \(n=6,8\) and \(n/(n-2\lfloor n/6\rfloor)\) for even \(n\ge10\), never exceeding \(3/2\).
