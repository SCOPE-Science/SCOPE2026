For uniform randomized Osborne updates of any positive-weight directed triangle, the logarithmic balancing error is halved exactly every time the chosen coordinate changes; those changes are independent Bernoulli\((2/3)\), yielding an exact binomial error law and negative-binomial stopping time. Under the generic nontermination condition, the original normalized \(\ell_1\) balancing time has mean asymptotic to \(3\log(1/\varepsilon)/(2\log2)\) and an explicit Gaussian fluctuation law.
