The published spin-exchange Lindblad opinion equation has exact, correlation-independent first-moment dynamics governed by a symmetric graph Laplacian, and it equals the claimed directed Friedkin–Johnsen reduction only when every exchange rate vanishes. On a three-agent directed network, the full model converges to opinion \(-1/3\) while the stated reduction converges to \(-3/5\); a second exact anchored example also separates their equilibria.
