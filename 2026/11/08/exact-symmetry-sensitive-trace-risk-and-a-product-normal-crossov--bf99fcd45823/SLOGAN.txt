For one-sided Gaussian diagonal recovery, the trace error has an exact variance decomposition: the symmetric off-diagonal part contributes at order \(q^{-1}\), while skew-symmetric coupling contributes only at order \(q^{-2}\). At a two-dimensional near-skew crossover the properly scaled error converges to \(Z(\kappa+2aW)\), a generally non-Gaussian product of independent normal variables.
