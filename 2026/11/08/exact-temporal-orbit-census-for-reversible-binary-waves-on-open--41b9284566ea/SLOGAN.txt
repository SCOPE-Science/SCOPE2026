For the reversible binary wave automaton on an open path of \(n\ge2\) sites, every paired-row state is periodic and the exact global order is \(2(n+1)\). The count of states fixed after any \(t\) updates is \(2^{D_n(t)}\), with an explicit two-adic gcd formula for \(D_n(t)\), yielding every primitive temporal-orbit count by Möbius inversion.
