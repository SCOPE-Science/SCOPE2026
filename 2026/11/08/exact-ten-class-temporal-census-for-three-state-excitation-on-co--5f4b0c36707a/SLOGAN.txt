On every complete tripartite graph, three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation has an exact ten-class census of first periodic-entry times and periods, with \(F^7=F^4\) and a sharp four-step cutoff except on the triangle. On balanced shores, nonperiodic initial states become exponentially rare as \(9(4/9)^n\), and their entry times converge conditionally to equal probabilities at steps one, two and three.
