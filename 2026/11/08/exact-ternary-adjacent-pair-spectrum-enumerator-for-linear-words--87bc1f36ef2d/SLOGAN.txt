For linear ternary words, the number of distinct nine-entry adjacent-pair multiplicity spectra has an exact rational generating function, beginning \(1,9,24,65,156,342,683,\ldots\) at zero through six edges. Accounting separately for all six endpoint imbalances and disconnected-support exclusions gives \(a_m\sim7m^6/8640\), unlike the previously enumerated balanced cyclic Markov types.
