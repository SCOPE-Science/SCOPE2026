For one arbitrary four-position substring permutation over a ternary alphabet, the sharp output-sphere maximum is exactly \(8n-18\), \(8n-20\), or \(8n-19\) according as \(n\equiv0,1,2\pmod3\), for every \(n\ge4\). The number of maximizing source words is also exact: respectively \(6\), \(3(t+2)(t+3)\), or \(6(t+2)\), where \(n=3t\), \(3t+1\), or \(3t+2\).
