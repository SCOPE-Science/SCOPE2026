For every integer \(a\ge1\), the affine Wang–Sun sum at modulus \(2\cdot3^a\) has exact reduced denominator \(3^{3^a}\) and reduced numerator congruent to \(1\pmod3\). A unique lowest \(3\)-adic layer from the two primitive cube roots survives the affine-divisor decomposition, extending finite examples and complementing the known prime-denominator theorem for primes greater than three.
