# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof uses the exact convergent falling-factorial interpolation for derangements, evaluates the first two relevant derivatives modulo three, locates the critical point by Hensel lifting, and applies an explicit odd-prime analytic square-coordinate change. Both image branches and their exact intersection are proved, yielding the all-exponent count and normalized Haar measure. Independent finite recurrence enumeration through exponent eight and derivative checks corroborate, but do not replace, the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual historical risk.** Sun–Wu–Zhuang (arXiv:1009.2929, Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 3.2) concerns finite-field congruences. Miska (arXiv:1508.01987, Sections 4.1 and 6.2) proves periodicity, presents the p-adic analytic interpolation, and examines valuations, but not the exact ternary residue-image spectrum or Haar measure. O'Desky–Richman (arXiv:2012.04615, Sections 3–5) develops p-adic interpolation and congruence techniques, not an exact image decomposition. The new claim is the exact image and its measure, not the already-studied period or interpolation machinery. A more obscure equivalent classification may exist; search absence is not a novelty proof.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The image size and Haar measure are natural arithmetic distribution invariants of a classical counting sequence. Their nontrivial structure arises from one 3-adic quadratic critical branch and a precise overlap of two translated square images; they are not a mere finite tabulation or a renaming of the interpolation results.

**Limitations.** This does not determine multiplicities, images at primes other than three, or a general theory for all combinatorial counting sequences. The reviewed proof is not independently certified.
