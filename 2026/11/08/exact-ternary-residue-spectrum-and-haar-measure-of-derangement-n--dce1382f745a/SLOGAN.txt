The residues of the derangement numbers modulo \(3^k\) have the exact cardinality \(2\cdot3^{k-1}+2S_{k-2}-3^{\max(k-5,0)}\) for every \(k\ge2\), where \(S_m\) counts square residues modulo \(3^m\); the value at \(k=1\) is three. Their \(3\)-adic closure is a union of two full unit balls and two overlapping quadratic critical images, with exact Haar measure \(725/972\).
