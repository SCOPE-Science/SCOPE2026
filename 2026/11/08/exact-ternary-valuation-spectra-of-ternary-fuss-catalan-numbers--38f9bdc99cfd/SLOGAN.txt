The exact \(3\)-adic valuation histogram of the ternary Fuss–Catalan numbers over every index interval \(0\le n<3^r\) is encoded by \(\sum_{r\ge0}P_r(t)z^r=(1-tz)/(1-2(1+t)z+(1+t+t^2)z^2)\). The valuations have explicitly determined mean and variance and, after normalization, converge to a Gaussian law.
