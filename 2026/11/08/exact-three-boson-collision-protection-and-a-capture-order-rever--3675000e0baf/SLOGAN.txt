For three noninteracting bosons with local triple-occupancy loss on the complete network \(K_n\), the exact loss-free subspace has dimension \(\binom{n+2}{3}-3n\), and eventual capture is independent of all strictly positive site-specific loss rates. The capture ordering of initially separated triples versus an initially co-located pair reverses sharply between \(n=5\) and \(n=6\), with explicit rational capture laws valid for every \(n\ge3\).
