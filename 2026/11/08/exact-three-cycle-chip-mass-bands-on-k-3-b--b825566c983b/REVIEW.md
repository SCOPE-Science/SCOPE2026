# Scientific review: exact three-cycle chip-mass bands

## Correctness — PASS
The all-parameter proof derives exact necessary-and-sufficient threshold intervals for a vertex's specified one-in-three firing phase, then proves that all three phases appear exactly once on the three-vertex side. The multinomial count and inclusion–exclusion give the full generating function. The chip-complement involution gives the upper band and proves exhaustiveness. Independent brute-force iteration of all confined states for \(b=3,4\) and a separate integer polynomial test through \(b=30\) reproduced the exact counts; neither finite test replaces the proof. The statement explicitly counts initially periodic labelled states, not transient basins.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, qualified)
Jiang's arXiv:1003.0943 establishes attainable period lengths and examples, not an energy-resolved population count. Jiang–Scully–Zhang's arXiv:1211.6786 concerns attainable firing words, not their chip assignments and labelled populations. Ji–Li–Wang arXiv:2408.10508 and Jung arXiv:2608.01350 establish exclusion or period two for a *strict open* middle mass interval, not the exact positive endpoint multiplicities. Related primitive-four counts on \(K_{2,b}\) do not imply primitive-three populations on \(K_{3,b}\). Searches for equivalent formulas and aliases did not locate a covering count; this is evidence, not proof of world-wide priority. An unindexed earlier formula remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The smallest complete-bipartite side on which primitive three-cycles are possible is three. On this natural boundary family the exact count yields two separated complete mass bands, a positive explicit count at both endpoints of the middle-stair period-two theorem, and exponential growth of the three-cycle orbit population. Those sharp boundary multiplicities supply a meaningful dynamical invariant and a rigorous benchmark for fixed-energy sandpile state enumeration.
