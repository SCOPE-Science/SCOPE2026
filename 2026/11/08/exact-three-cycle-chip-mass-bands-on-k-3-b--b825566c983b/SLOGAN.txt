On every \(K_{3,b}\), \(b\ge3\), parallel chip-firing has a complete, chip-mass-resolved primitive-three-cycle census: such configurations occur exactly in the two disjoint integer bands \([4b,5b-3]\) and \([6b,7b-3]\), with generating polynomial \((z^{4b}+z^{6b})Q_b(z)\). Both endpoints of the known strict middle-stair period-two interval admit exactly \(6(3^b-3\cdot2^b+3)\) primitive-three configurations, and the total number of distinct three-cycle orbits is \(4b(b-1)(b-2)3^{b-3}\).
