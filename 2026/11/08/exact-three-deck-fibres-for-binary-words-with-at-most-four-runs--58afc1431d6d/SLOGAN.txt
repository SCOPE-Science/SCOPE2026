Within binary words having at most four constant runs, every ambiguous scattered three-deck fibre consists of exactly two reversed words \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\) and \(1^d0^c1^b0^a\), where \(2bc=(a+c)(b+d)\). This gives an explicit divisor-sum census of all restricted fibres and a sharp uniform reconstruction order of four; the previously known four-deck upper bound is not claimed as new.
