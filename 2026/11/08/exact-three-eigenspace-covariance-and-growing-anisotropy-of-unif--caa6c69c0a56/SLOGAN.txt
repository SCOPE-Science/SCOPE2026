The uniform vertex distribution of the complete-bipartite matching polytope has three exactly computable Frobenius covariance eigenvalues, strictly ordered in every dimension. Their scalar, row/column and interaction scales are respectively \(1/(2n^{3/2})\), \(1/n^{3/2}\), and \(1/n\), so its covariance condition number is asymptotic to \(2\sqrt n\).
