For \(F(n)=\binom n5\), the least period modulo \(2^k\) is \(2^{k+2}\), and for every \(k\ge3\) each residue \(y\) has exactly \(2+2\mathbf1_{2\mid y}+8\mathbf1_{8\mid y}\) preimages per period. The resulting 2-adic output distribution has three sharply distinct density levels, the first such behavior among the five smallest positive fixed binomial columns.
