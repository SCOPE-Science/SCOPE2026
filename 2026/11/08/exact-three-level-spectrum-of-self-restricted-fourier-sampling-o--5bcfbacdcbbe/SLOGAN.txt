The additive Fourier matrix restricted on both axes to all invertible \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\) is invertible for every prime power \(q\), with exact singular values \(q\), \(q^{3/2}\) when \(q>2\), and \(q^2\), and explicit multiplicities. Its sharp singular-value condition number is \(q\); the three levels arise from an exact Gram identity involving the nonzero-vector permutation character of \(\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\).
