For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and length \(n\ge8\), the contiguous three-mer multisets of words with exactly two nonzero positions have precisely \(18(q-1)+37\binom{q-1}{2}\) equivalence classes, independent of length. The entire ambiguity-class-size distribution is explicit, including exactly \(12(q-1)^2-2(q-1)\) uniquely reconstructible words within this sparse family for \(n\ge9\).
