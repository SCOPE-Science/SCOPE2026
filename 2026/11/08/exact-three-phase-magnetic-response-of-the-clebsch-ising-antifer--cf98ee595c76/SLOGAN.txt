The 16-spin antiferromagnetic Ising model on the folded 5-cube has an exact field-induced magnetization jump from zero to \(3/8\) of saturation at \(h=7/3\), followed by saturation at \(h=5\). The first coexistence field has exactly 26 ground states, and the second has 393; a sharp induced-edge obstruction rules out all intermediate plateaux.
