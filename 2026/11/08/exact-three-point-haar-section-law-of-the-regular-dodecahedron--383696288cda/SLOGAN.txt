A Haar-uniform central plane section of a regular dodecahedron is a hexagon, octagon or decagon with exact probabilities \(\frac5\pi(2\pi-3\alpha-3\beta)\), \(\frac{15}\pi(2\alpha+\beta-\pi)\), and \(\frac3\pi(2\pi-5\alpha)\), where \(\alpha=\arccos(1/3)\) and \(\beta=\arccos(\sqrt5/3)\). The same law describes the vertex count of a Haar-uniform orthogonal silhouette of a regular icosahedron.
