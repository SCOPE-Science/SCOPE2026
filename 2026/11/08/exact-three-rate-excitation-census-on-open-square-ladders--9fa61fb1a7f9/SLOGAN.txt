On every open two-row ladder, three-state cyclic excitation has only the long-term rates \(0\), \(3/4\), and \(1\); the Greenberg–Hastings rates for the same initial coloring are exactly one third as large. Two adjacent equal nonzero square circulations force the maximal rate, and explicit cubic and quadratic recurrences count the initial colorings of all three phases, with the maximal phase approaching probability one exponentially in ladder length.
