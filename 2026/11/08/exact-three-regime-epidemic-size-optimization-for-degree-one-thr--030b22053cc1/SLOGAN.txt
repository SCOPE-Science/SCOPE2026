For independent epidemic transmission on configuration networks whose degrees are restricted to \(1,3,5\) with fixed mean \(3\), the optimal degree heterogeneity has an exact three-regime phase diagram: maximal spread for \(0.3<T\le0.503512762\ldots\), a unique interior optimum for \(0.503512762\ldots<T<0.642387381\ldots\), and a homogeneous degree-three network for \(T\ge0.642387381\ldots\). Below \(T=0.3\), no permitted network has a macroscopic outbreak.
