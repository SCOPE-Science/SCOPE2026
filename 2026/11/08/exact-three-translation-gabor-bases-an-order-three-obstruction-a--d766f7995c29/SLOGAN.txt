For a critical cyclic Gabor family with exactly three translations and all modulations divisible by three, flat \(q\)-phase orthonormal windows exist exactly when the translation step has order divisible by three and \(3\mid q\). Their exact number is \(6^d q^M\), where \(N=3M\) and \(d=\gcd(N,k)\); without phase quantization the window space consists of \(6^d\) disjoint \(M\)-tori.
