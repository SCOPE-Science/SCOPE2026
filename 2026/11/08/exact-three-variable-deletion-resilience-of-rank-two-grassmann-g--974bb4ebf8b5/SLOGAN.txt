Every rank-two Grassmann graph \(J_q(n,2)\) with \(n\ge4\) has the same counting-free three-variable theory as the random graph, and this survives deletion of any fewer than \((q+1)^2\) vertices, except that \(J_2(4,2)\) tolerates only five arbitrary deletions. All minimum destructive deletion sets are explicitly classified by a unique bottleneck adjacency pattern on a disjoint pair of two-spaces (the representing pair need not be unique).
