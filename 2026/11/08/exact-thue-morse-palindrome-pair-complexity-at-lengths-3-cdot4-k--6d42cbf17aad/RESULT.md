# Exact Thue–Morse palindrome-pair complexity at lengths \(3\cdot 4^k\)

## Finding
Let \(\mathbf t=0110100110010110\cdots\) be the Thue–Morse word, the fixed point of \(\mu(0)=01,\ \mu(1)=10\). A palindrome pair is a finite word expressible as a concatenation of two palindromes, with either part allowed to be empty. Write \(\mathrm{PP}_{\mathbf t}(n)\) for the number of distinct length-\(n\) factors of \(\mathbf t\) that are palindrome pairs. For every integer \(k\ge0\), set \(n=3\cdot4^k\), \(U_k=\mu^{2k}(010)\), and \(V_k=\mu^{2k}(101)\). Then

\[
\{w\in\mathrm{Fac}_n(\mathbf t): w\text{ is a palindrome pair}\}
=\mathrm{Conj}(U_k)\mathbin{\dot\cup}\mathrm{Conj}(V_k),
\qquad \mathrm{PP}_{\mathbf t}(3\cdot4^k)=6\cdot4^k=2n.
\]
Here \(\mathrm{Conj}(u)\) means the set of distinct cyclic rotations, not the set of arbitrary substrings of \(u\). Both rotation classes have precisely \(n\) elements.

## Assumptions and scope
Words are binary, finite factors are linear contiguous words, the infinite fixed point starts with \(0\), and rotations are regarded as distinct labeled words unless literally equal. The claim holds for every nonnegative integer \(k\), including \(k=0\). The underlying classification of palindrome pairs is that a word is such a pair if and only if its reversal is a cyclic rotation of the word: one direction follows by reversing a factorization into two palindromes; the converse follows from the reflection axis of the associated circular word, which divides the cycle into two palindromic arcs.

## Proof
Write \(\phi=\mu^2\), so \(B_0=\phi(0)=0110\) and \(B_1=\phi(1)=1001\) are palindromes.

**Lemma 1 (reversal symmetry of a local spectrum).** For a circular binary word \(u\), the cyclic length-three factor frequencies of \(u\) are invariant under reversal: the only non-palindromic reversed pairs are \((001,100)\) and \((011,110)\), whose frequencies agree by in/out-degree balance at the two vertices \(00\) and \(11\) of the order-two de Bruijn graph. Each cyclic length-six factor of \(\phi(u)\) uses at most three consecutive letters of \(u\). As \(\phi\) commutes with reversal, the cyclic length-six factor-frequency vector of \(\phi(u)\) is therefore reversal invariant.

**Lemma 2 (a mismatched cyclic seam obstructs a palindrome pair).** Consider a cyclic word built from at least one full \(B\)-block and exactly one exceptional block \(C\) selected from
\(\{1110,0001,1010,0101,1000,0111\}\). Such a cyclic word is not a palindrome pair. Indeed a palindrome pair has reversal-invariant cyclic length-six factor frequencies. Replace \(C\) by \(B_0\): the resulting all-good block word has a reversal-invariant length-six spectrum by Lemma 1. Any changed length-six factor has a start within five positions before or three positions after the exceptional block, so for at least four good blocks the discrepancy is determined by the two neighboring good blocks on each side. Exhaustive inspection of all sixteen choices of these four binary block labels gives strictly positive values for the half-\(\ell^1\) reversal discrepancy. For \(C=1110,0001,1000,0111\), the minimum is \(4\) and maximum \(7\); for \(C=1010,0101\), the minimum is \(4\) and maximum \(12\). The cases with one, two or three good blocks are checked directly (respectively \(2,4,8\) choices of the good blocks per exceptional block); their discrepancies are likewise positive. The standalone `proof_checks.py` reproduces all the local case tables. The comparison with an all-good word shows that unaffected interior blocks cannot change the discrepancy, so this is an all-length argument, not an inference from finitely many word lengths.

**Lemma 3 (descent under the four-uniform morphism).** For every binary word \(u\) of positive length, \(\phi(u)\) is a palindrome pair if and only if \(u\) is one. The forward direction follows from the uniqueness of the \(B\)-block decomposition of a nonconstant cyclic \(B\)-word: a shift by one or three positions cannot give a full \(B\)-decomposition, while a shift by two requires consecutive source symbols to agree everywhere, hence the source to be constant. Because \(B_0,B_1\) are individual palindromes, reversal of \(\phi(u)\) is \(\phi(u^R)\). Thus reflection symmetry of the four-uniform cyclic word induces reflection symmetry of the source circle. Constant words are immediate. The backward implication follows by applying \(\phi\) to each palindromic part.

More generally, let \(w\) be a length-\(4m\) factor of \(\phi(\mathbf t)=\mathbf t\), where \(m\ge2\). If its start aligns with a block boundary, it is \(\phi(u)\) for a length-\(m\) factor \(u\). Otherwise let the start be at phase \(r\in\{1,2,3\}\) inside \(B_a\), and let \(B_b\) be the final partial block. If \(a=b\), the circular word \(w\) is a rotation of \(\phi(u)\), where \(u\) is a length-\(m\) factor. If \(a\ne b\), the cyclic seam combines the prefix of \(B_b\) and suffix of \(B_a\) into one exceptional block \(C\): for \(a=0\), phases \(1,2,3\) give \(1110,1010,1000\), respectively, and for \(a=1\) they give their complements. Lemma 2 excludes a palindrome pair. Consequently **every length-\(4m\) palindrome-pair factor is a rotation of \(\phi(u)\) for some length-\(m\) palindrome-pair factor \(u\)**.

**Induction.** The six factors of \(\mathbf t\) of length three are the six nonconstant binary triples. They are all palindrome pairs and form exactly the disjoint rotation classes of \(010\) and \(101\). Suppose the classification holds at \(m=3\cdot4^k\). By Lemma 3 every palindrome-pair factor at length \(4m\) lies in one of the two rotation classes of \(\phi(U_k)=U_{k+1}\) and \(\phi(V_k)=V_{k+1}\). Conversely, both squares \(010010\) and \(101101\) occur in \(\mathbf t\) (at zero-indexed starts \(15\) and \(11\), respectively). The morphism maps factors to factors, so the squares \(U_kU_k\) and \(V_kV_k\) occur for every \(k\). Every rotation of \(U_k\) or \(V_k\) therefore occurs and is a palindrome pair, because each \(U_k,V_k\) is itself a palindrome. Finally the unique cyclic block decoding from Lemma 3 proves recursively that these words are primitive, and that their two rotation classes are disjoint: any equality of rotations would descend to an equality of rotations of \(010\) and \(101\), which is impossible because their symbol counts differ. Both classes therefore have \(m\) elements. This proves equality at every level. \(\square\)

## Verification
The independently written `proof_checks.py` evaluates the complete local exceptional-block tables, checks good-block six-factor symmetry and cyclic phase uniqueness for finite stress-test ranges, and independently checks every mismatched seam up to source lengths eleven. The program `independent_counts.py` directly enumerates distinct Thue–Morse factors at lengths \(3,12,48,192\), tests the palindrome-pair property from its definition, and compares the resulting sets with the two asserted rotation classes. It reports counts \(6,24,96,384\). The finite calculations verify the displayed local cases and small instances; the proof of every length is the preceding symbolic descent and induction.

## Relationship to prior work
Borchert and Rampersad (arXiv:1509.05396v1, Proposition 11) proved the matching **lower bound** \(\mathrm{PP}_{\mathbf t}(3\cdot4^k)\ge 6\cdot4^k\) for \(k\ge1\) by constructing these two rotation classes. They explicitly posed exact palindrome-pair complexity formulas for the Thue–Morse word as a problem. The result here establishes the reverse inequality and the absence of any additional partial conjugacy classes at those lengths. Bell and Shallit's later Lie-complexity framework counts full conjugacy classes that occur in an infinite word; that invariant does not, by itself, exclude additional palindrome-pair factors belonging to incomplete conjugacy classes. The all-length morphic exclusion lemma supplies that extra step.

## Limitations
Only the subsequence of lengths \(3\cdot4^k\) is resolved. No formula is asserted for arbitrary lengths, the Fibonacci word, or larger alphabets. The count concerns distinct linear factors (not factor occurrences). Historical priority beyond the inspected literature and independent review remain open.
