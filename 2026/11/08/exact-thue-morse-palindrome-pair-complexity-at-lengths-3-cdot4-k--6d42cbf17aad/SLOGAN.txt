For the Thue–Morse word, the number of distinct palindrome-pair factors of length \(n=3\cdot4^k\) is exactly \(2n\) for every \(k\ge0\). They are precisely the two complete cyclic rotation classes of \(\mu^{2k}(010)\) and \(\mu^{2k}(101)\), closing a previously known lower bound at infinitely many lengths.
