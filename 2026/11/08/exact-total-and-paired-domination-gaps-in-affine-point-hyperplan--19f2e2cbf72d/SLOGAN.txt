In the full point–hyperplane incidence graph of every finite affine space \(\operatorname{AG}(d,q)\) with \(d\ge2\), total domination requires \(d(q-1)+q+1\) vertices, while paired domination requires \(2d(q-1)+2\). Their exact gap \((d-1)(q-1)\) is attained by coordinate-axis points and hyperplanes with an explicit backup matching and grows unboundedly in either parameter.
