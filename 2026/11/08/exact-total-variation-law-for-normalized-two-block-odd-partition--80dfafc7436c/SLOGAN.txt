The normalized signed two-block odd-partition coefficients have a discrete limiting law with masses \(2^{-t-1}\) at explicitly determined dyadic values. At **every** cutoff \(N\), the exact total-variation distance to this law equals the proportion of positive integers up to \(N\) representable as two squares, hence tends to zero at the sharp Landau--Ramanujan rate \(K/\sqrt{\log N}\).
