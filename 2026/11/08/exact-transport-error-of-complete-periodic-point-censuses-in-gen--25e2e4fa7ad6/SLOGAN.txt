For the slope-\(H\) generalized tent repeller, the uniform census of all \(2^n\) points fixed by the \(n\)-th iterate approximates the natural Bernoulli measure in Euclidean \(W_1\) with the exact error \(\frac{H-1}{(2H-1)H^n}(1+\frac1{2(H^{2n}-1)})\). The formula reveals a sharp \(H^{-n}\) rate and a positive \(H^{-3n}\) correction, while the Kolmogorov discrepancy is exactly \(2^{-n}\); this concerns the complete periodic-point census, not a single orbit.
