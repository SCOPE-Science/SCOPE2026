For the rectangular group \(S_{r,s}=[r]\times C_2\times[s]\), the exact number of complete mappings is a single balanced coefficient of a product of explicit four-term polynomials, times \((r!)^{2s}(s!)^{2r}\). The formula is symmetric in \(r,s\) and gives a closed binomial sum for the two-row family, refining a previously established existence criterion.
