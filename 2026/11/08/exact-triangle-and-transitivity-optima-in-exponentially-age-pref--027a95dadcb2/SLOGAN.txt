In the two-parent exponentially age-preferential growth graph, the almost-sure triangle density and global transitivity of its simple-graph projection are explicit rational functions of \(q=e^{-\beta}\). Both have unique interior maxima, but the triangle density is greatest at \(q\approx0.420413\), whereas the closed-triplet proportion is greatest at \(q\approx0.216931\): two natural notions of clustering favor provably different age biases.
