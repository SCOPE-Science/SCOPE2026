The five-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra over every finite field has an exact closed formula for the number of triangles in its subalgebra comaximal graph. The count includes a nontrivial contribution from triples of complementary Lagrangian planes, governed by invertible symmetric 2-by-2 matrices; over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_3\), the counts are \(42105\) and \(8121580\), respectively.
