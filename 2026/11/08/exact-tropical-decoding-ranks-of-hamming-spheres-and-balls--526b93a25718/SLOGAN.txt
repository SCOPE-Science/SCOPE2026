The conditional Hamming-decoding matrices of both a constant-weight sphere and its corresponding Hamming ball have exactly the same tropical and min-plus factor rank, \(1+\min\{\ell,t,r,\ell+t-r\}\), for every alphabet size, cut, and radius. These natural nonlinear constraints achieve rank \(3\) already on four binary coordinates, in contrast with the power-of-two rank theorem for binary linear codes.
