At the two extreme controls of the parity-hybrid discrete skew tent permutation on \(2^m\) states, *every* trajectory has period at most four: \(C_1\) has exactly two two-cycles and \((2^m-4)/4\) four-cycles, while \(C_{2^m-1}\) consists only of \(2^{m-2}\) four-cycles. These exact cycle spectra show that the source's asymptotically maximal discrete Lyapunov exponent does not, by itself, guarantee long raw state periods.
