For both Pell and associated Pell numbers, the fourth-power consecutive-window GCD has exactly \(3^{1+\nu_3(k)}\) in its three-primary part if \(4\mid k\), and no factor of \(3\) otherwise. Its two-primary part is \(2^{\nu_2(k)-1}\) for Pell when \(4\mid k\) (otherwise trivial), versus \(2^{\nu_2(k)}\) for associated Pell at every length.
