In the classical \(n\)-state Černý automaton, the shortest word reaching any specified \((n-2)\)-state image has an exact four-case formula in the two missing indices. Every distance \(4,5,\ldots,2n\) occurs, with multiplicity \(t-3\) at distance \(t\le n+1\) and multiplicity one at each larger distance through \(2n\).
