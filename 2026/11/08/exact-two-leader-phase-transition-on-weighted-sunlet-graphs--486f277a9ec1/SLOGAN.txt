Two noise-free leaders on a sunlet network with unit cycle edges and uniform positive pendant conductance \(t\) always occupy diametrally separated core indices: they are a core–leaf pair below the exact threshold \(3/[1+2(n-3)\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/n]\), a core–core pair above it, and both types tie at equality. All optimizer multiplicities and inverse-trace minima are explicit for every \(n\ge3\), and leaf–leaf leader pairs are never optimal.
