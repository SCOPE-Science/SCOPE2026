# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For Markov operators C(Y)->C(X), variation of each fiber measure gives norm 2(1-inf_x overlap). A continuous map X->Y lands in one connected covering component. Degree-one components admit unique inverse covering sections; nontrivial connected degree>=2 component admits no section by the clopen image argument. For any two maps involving a nontrivial component, pick a fiber miss of one: the other can overlap at most one atom, bounded by max(t1,u). For two degree-one maps overlap bounded by t1+t2 or by the equal-weight capped expression. Two-map continuous coverage construction uses a fixed sheet on alternating transition subintervals to connect arbitrary vertex lifts in connected Y_i; at least one of two maps is a sheet section locally at every edge position. This attains u with equal weights. The degree-one sections attain the other maxima. Independent exact rational examples verify the corollary: masses (3/4,1/4-epsilon,epsilon), degrees (1,1,2) yield errors 2epsilon and 1/2 for epsilon=.1,.01,.001. Frozen verify_examples.py only checks arithmetic, not the topological gluing.

## originality

PASS

Duru-Ilter-Bilgin 2026 proves sharp m-term distances for a single connected covering with one uniform degree; when m=2 gives 2(1-1/d) for both choices, matching the connected specialization. The audited new case has disconnected covering and heterogeneous degree-one and higher-degree component weights, two different piecewise sharp formulas, and unbounded ratio; this is not a corollary of one-degree connected theorem without the component-sensitive dichotomy.
