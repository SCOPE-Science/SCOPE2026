For the nonconcave planar parallelogram pair of Codenotti–Freyer–Krivokuća, the inverse lattice covering radius is exactly \(\min\{1+s/14,7/6-s/2\}\), where \(s=\min\{t,1-t\}\). Its two and only two strict interior peaks occur at \(t=7/24\) and \(t=17/24\), both of height \(49/48\), separated by a central trough of height \(11/12\).
