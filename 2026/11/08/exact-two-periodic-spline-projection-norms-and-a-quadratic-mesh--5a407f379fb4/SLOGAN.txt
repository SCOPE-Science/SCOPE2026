For periodic continuous linear splines with alternating interval lengths \(a,b>0\), the exact \(L^\infty\) norm of the orthogonal projector is a finite explicit Green-kernel sum, attained only at knots. As the number of periods grows, its sharp norm approaches \(2-(a-b)^2/((3a+b)(a+3b))\): any fixed nonzero alternating contrast strictly *reduces* the uniform-mesh limit, with a matching exact worst-case positivity defect.
