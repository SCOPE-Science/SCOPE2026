# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The primal volume has an exhaustive exact-arithmetic oriented-matroid chamber certificate: every five-point affine determinant polynomial, its positive roots, and exact supporting triangulations at one point per chamber. An explicit one-dimensional polar integral gives independent rational formulas; exact symbolic checking proves continuity and the two-piece product identity. Descartes' rule plus verified rational sign certificates proves exactly two local maxima, one local minimum, and the uniquely larger global maximum. The certificate is finite but exhaustive for the finite vertex family; it does not extrapolate from random sampling. The origin is interior by cubical containment, and all parameter endpoints and overlaps are handled.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk
Shen and Dubovski's primary full text (arXiv:2610.11014, Section 3, Corollary 4) explicitly defines the identical family and asserts only a lower bound for heights up to \(12/7\). The complete exact volume-product function, its phase change at \(2\), the non-unimodal two-peak classification, and asymptotic approach at infinity are not stated there. The 2026 manuscript *The symmetric Mahler conjecture and its equality cases* gives a more general Mahler lower bound but not the precise value as a function of height or its stationary points. Public mathematical-record searches for exact cube-apex products, polar cap overlaps, Mahler two-peak profiles and parameter equivalents did not find covering formulas. Related cube–crosspolytope intersection and cube–Hanner interpolation results concern different bodies. Automated and unindexed sources may have been missed; no independence or definitive priority is asserted.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The motivating paper singles out this two-apex family as lying outside its sufficient hypotheses. Its complete Mahler profile resolves the natural beyond-threshold question, and yields the structurally unexpected two local peaks with a sharp separating notch, a unique global maximizing height, and a second approach to the cubical extremal constant at infinite apex height. These are full-family geometric facts with precise optimization and asymptotics, not a single numerical example or a restatement of the universal inequality.

## Limits
The all-dimensional Mahler inequality, where cited, is prior literature, not re-proved. The main primal-volume calculation is a reproducible finite exact certificate; there is no claim for arbitrary apex arrays. Formal independent audit, exhaustive historical priority and independent human review remain outstanding.
