The least number of marked sites whose uncentered two-sided maximal orbit average exceeds \(2/3\) at every point of an \(N\)-cycle is exactly \(\lfloor N/2\rfloor+1+\mathbf1_{N\equiv3\pmod4}\). A consecutive block always achieves this bound, while the congruence-class penalty follows from an exact two-cover obstruction; for even cycles the alternating half-density configuration saturates just below, but never at, the threshold.
