For two independent iid samples from the same two-species community, expected incidence Jaccard autosimilarity is nondecreasing with sample size exactly when the dominant species has frequency at most \((1+\sqrt{\sqrt{41}-6})/2\); above that threshold it rises, falls, and rises again, with precisely one trough. Extreme dominance permits an interim drop approaching one quarter, despite eventual convergence of expected similarity to one.
