For synchronous tie-retaining binary majority on the balanced complete tripartite graph \(K_{n,n,n}\), every configuration reaches a fixed point or a two-cycle within two updates; the entire two-cycle basin has an exact binomial-sum census. Under unbiased random initial opinions, the two-cycle probability is asymptotic to \(6\arctan(1/\sqrt2)/(\pi^{3/2}\sqrt n)\), even though initially periodic configurations form an exponentially small fraction.
