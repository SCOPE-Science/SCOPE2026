For two determinant-one matrices over \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\), odd \(p\) and \(k\ge2\), every possible matrix sum has a completely explicit valuation–discriminant representation count. The least count is exactly \(p^{2k-1}(p-1)\), attained for a precisely enumerated target family, and the two-step additive convolution dominates uniform measure by a sharp factor \(p^3/((p-1)(p+1)^2)\) independent of depth.
