Two labelled cards in a \(2^d\)-position Thorp shuffle retain an exactly solvable memory: the two-card sweep kernel has only the eigenvalues \(1\), \(2^{-d}\), and \(0\). After two coordinate sweeps its worst-case total-variation distance from a uniform ordered pair is exactly \((2^d-2)/[2^{d+1}(2^d-1)]\), although mixing of the entire deck is a different problem.
