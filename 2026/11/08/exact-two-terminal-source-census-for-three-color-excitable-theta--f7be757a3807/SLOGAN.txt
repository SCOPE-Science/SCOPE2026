For three-color Greenberg–Hastings excitation on generalized theta graphs, an exact bivariate polynomial counts initially primitive-three states by how many distinct paths sustain each of the two terminals, and attached trees do not change any coefficient. Equal arm lengths \(3h\) yield precisely \(3[(2h+1)^m-2(2h)^m+(2h-1)^m]\) such states, whereas equal lengths not divisible by three yield none.
