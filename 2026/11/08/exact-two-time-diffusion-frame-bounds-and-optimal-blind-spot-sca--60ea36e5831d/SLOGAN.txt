Two heat snapshots taken only at the even vertices of the integer lattice have exact sharp frame bounds on the spectral subspace \(|\cos\theta|\ge c>0\); removing the spectral notch destroys stability at every finite observation horizon. As the notch shrinks, the best possible two-snapshot lower bound is asymptotic to \(0.12070751969725028\,c^2\), achieved at times converging to the unique positive solution of \(t=1+e^{-2t}\).
