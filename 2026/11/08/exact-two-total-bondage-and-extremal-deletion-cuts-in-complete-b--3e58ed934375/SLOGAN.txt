For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(2\le a\le b\), the minimum edge-deletion cost to raise total domination from two to at least four is \(b\) when \(a=2\) and \(\min\{b,2a\}\) otherwise. Every optimal cut is a labelled surjective column-cover or a three-column triangle pattern, yielding exact Stirling-number formulas for the number of optimizers and a sharp change of structure at \(b=2a\).
