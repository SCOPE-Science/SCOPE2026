## Correctness: PASS

The stationary generator yields \(w_1=D/\kappa\), \(w_0=D/\kappa+D/\nu\). An exact two-component lag regression closes with a matrix whose characteristic polynomial is \(z^2+\Sigma z+\Pi\). The recovered right derivatives of its covariance determine only \(\Sigma\), \(\Pi\), and \(D\), and the positive roots of \(\kappa^2-\Sigma\kappa+2\Pi=0\) give all parameter aliases. A separate fourth-moment generator identity is strictly affine in the candidate stiffness on the alias fiber. Reconstructed symbolic identities, an independent stationary-characteristic-function expansion, and a standalone matrix-exponential checker support each algebraic step. Finite numerical checks supplement but do not prove the all-parameter result.

Frydel (2024), arxiv:2407.12213, provides its exact stationary second and fourth moments and Gaussian-mixture stationary law. Those forward results are not claimed as new. ## Value: PASS

Even a perfect observation of every stationary autocovariance lag cannot distinguish a trap with rates \((\kappa,\nu)\) from a different trap with rates \((2\nu,\kappa/2)\). The theorem determines the exact twofold ambiguity, its self-dual transition, and the lowest-order extra stationary position moment sufficient to resolve it. This is a meaningful identifiability boundary for random confinement, distinct from recomputing an existing one-time moment formula.

## Scientific limitations

Exactly symmetric switching, zero off-state stiffness, stationary operation, and homogeneous Brownian forcing are required. Full-covariance and fourth-moment measurements are treated as exact population data; no estimator or sampling error is analyzed. The stationary source papers give one-point formulas that underlie the new inverse relation.
