For a Brownian particle in a harmonic trap that flips on and off, the entire stationary position autocovariance is unchanged by exchanging the trap relaxation rate \(\kappa\) with twice the switching rate \(2\nu\). Except on the self-dual line \(\kappa=2\nu\), exactly two distinct rate pairs share the same autocovariance, and the stationary fourth moment uniquely separates them.
