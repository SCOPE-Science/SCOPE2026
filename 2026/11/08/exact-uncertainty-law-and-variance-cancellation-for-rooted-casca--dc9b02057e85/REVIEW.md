# Review of the exact rooted-cascade opinion law

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is made only for one cascade on a finite unique-parent outward tree with independent node-level broadcast coins and deterministic initial states. Every exposed vertex sees its already-updated unique parent, so induction gives the deterministic conditional opinion recursion. This yields a weighted random-subtree representation for the total change. Conditioning on a vertex's own broadcast coin and the independent descendant subtrees proves the full moment-generating recursion and its sum-of-squares variance formula. The nonnegative decomposition proves both directions of the zero-variance criterion. The covariance formula counts exactly the strict-ancestor broadcast coins required for joint exposure. Boundary cases of zero and unit resharing probability are handled without division. Exact-rational exhaustive simulation independently agreed with dynamic convolution and covariance formulas for 360 parameterized trees of sizes two through nine, as well as a cancellation example. The finite checker is supporting evidence, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with a substantial clearly identified remaining risk

The motivating paper, arXiv:2610.19867 (first publicly submitted October 14, 2026), derives a conditional layer-wise expectation recursion and uses a mean-field decoupling and tree reduction. The inspected model and analytic sections (especially Eq. 6 and the exact-versus-approximate discussion of Section IV-D) do not specify the full generating function of signed opinion changes or a zero-variance characterization on a genuine unique-parent tree. The original 2025 FJC work emphasizes simulations rather than these exact distributional laws. Generic independent-cascade reachability, weighted tree branch recursions, and conditional-variance decompositions are already known and are expressly excluded from any novelty claim. The distinction is the exact deterministic-conditional-opinion reduction for the coupled FJC updates, which supplies the model-specific weights and enables an exact full opinion law rather than merely a reachability count. Targeted primary-literature searches and mathematical research-record searches yielded no result explicitly establishing this model-specific complete law. However, the earlier source was not fully inspected, and an unindexed general weighted-cascade theorem may imply the result immediately after the FJC reduction; originality requires external expert checking before treating it as established fact.

## Value — PASS

The source paper emphasizes the statistical coupling of cascade realization and opinion update, which prevents generic unconditional mean-field closure and motivates approximate computations. A unique-parent directed tree is a natural, mathematically identifiable subclass of the very exposure mechanism under study, not an arbitrary parameter slice. The exact generating function provides every aggregate moment and an exact response distribution, while the linear-time nonnegative variance decomposition identifies all aggregate-cancellation cases. This answers a motivated uncertainty question unavailable from a mean curve alone. The four-node example gives a concrete nontrivial phenomenon: two individually random leaf opinions have negative covariance and exactly deterministic total opinion. The result can serve as a rigorous tree baseline for diagnosing independence approximations in larger networks.

## Closest literature and limitations

Sources reviewed: Biondi et al., arXiv:2610.19867v1, especially the full-text definitions and analytic development; Biondi et al., arXiv:2506.16302v1, original-model abstract/history; Abbe, Kulkarni, and Lee, arXiv:1706.05295, influence-bounds abstract; and targeted public mathematical research records. The available full-text FJC inspection covered the update rule and layer recursions but not every supplementary page. The result says nothing about general multilayer graphs with multiple parents or full multi-post trajectories. Its basic probabilistic recursion relies on classical tree independence rather than introducing a new generating-function technique.
