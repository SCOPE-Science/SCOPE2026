For negative doubling with hole \((2/5,3/5)\), Lebesgue survival through \(n\) observations is exactly \(4(n+1)/(5\,2^n)\), and the conditional time of switching from the outer two-cycle channel into an inner fixed-point channel is exactly uniform on \(\{0,\ldots,n\}\). This creates an explicit time-dependent atomic conditional profile and a distinct nonatomic terminal limit, whereas the neighboring natural boundary hole \((1/3,2/3)\) has a pure exponential survival law.
