Conditioned on surviving n observations of negative doubling with the hole (2/5,3/5), the first transition from an outer alternating channel into an inner fixed-point channel is exactly uniform over times 0,...,n. The conditional position law has an explicit atomic profile at each earlier macroscopic time fraction but a nonatomic uniform-interval terminal limit, approached in total variation at exactly 1/(n+1). The previously published escape tail is used only as normalization, not claimed anew.
