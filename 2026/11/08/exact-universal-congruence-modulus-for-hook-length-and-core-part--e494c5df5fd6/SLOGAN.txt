The greatest common divisor of all coefficient differences between the Nekrasov--Okounkov hook-length series at \(t^2\) and the \(t\)-core partition series is exactly \(p^{a+1}\) when \(t=p^a\), and exactly \(1\) otherwise. For prime powers the first failure of the stronger congruence modulo \(p^{a+2}\) occurs precisely at degree \(p^{a-1}\), with difference congruent to \(-p^{a+1}\).
