For the blow-up of \(\mathbb P^2\) at any \(n\ge3\) distinct collinear points, every integer from \(1\) through \(n-1\) occurs as the reduced denominator of a Zariski negative-part coefficient on an explicit big integral divisor. Consequently the least single multiple that makes **every** integral-divisor Zariski decomposition integral is exactly \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,n-1)\), refining the earlier known sharp bound \(n-1\) for an individual denominator.
