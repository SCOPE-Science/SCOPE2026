A connected unweighted tree can have arbitrarily many circular gaps in the surjectivity set of its backward-shift translates while retaining Rota universality throughout the entire open spectral disk. In the finite star of regular subtrees with branch degrees \(b_1<\cdots<b_k\), the non-surjective circles are exactly \(|\lambda|=\sqrt{b_j}\), but every \(|\lambda|<\sqrt{b_k}\) is Rota-universal.
