# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness (PASS)
For every integer \(q\ge2\), the standard greedy arch factorization gives the exact arch enumerator \(A_q(z)\), incomplete-remainder enumerator \(R_q(z)\), and \(q-1\)-letter remainder enumerator \(S_q(z)=A_q(z)/z\). The previously published palindrome midpoint lemma implies index \(2m\) for an even palindrome with \(m\) half-arches and index \(2m+1\) for an odd palindrome only when the center finishes the half's deficient remainder. Counting those two disjoint cases gives both rational bivariate series. The probabilistic interpretation is an exact discrete renewal identity with independent coupon-collection arch lengths; its aperiodicity and rational probability generating function prove the exponential convergence to \(1/(qH_q)\). Boundary cases \(h=0\), missing alphabet letters, and both even/odd index parities are included. The standalone verifier passed 52,710 enumerated palindromes, including 630 independently tested direct subsequence-set computations. The finite enumeration is corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality (PASS, with residual risk)
The primary source Barker et al. (arXiv:2003.04629v1), Proposition 15, already gives the palindrome midpoint structural lemma, so that is excluded from the claim of novelty. Adamson (arXiv:2304.04583v1), Sections 2–3, treats unconstrained-word arch counting and ranking, not an ordinary-palindrome-restricted all-length distribution. Fleischmann, Germann and Nowotka (arXiv:2104.09063v1) concern the universality remainder and repetitions, not these palindrome generating series. The closest semantically indexed claim is the complete binary distribution for **all** words, with a different bivariate function and without symmetry restriction. Known results concerning adjacency-distinct palindromes or reverse-complement symmetry do not imply the unrestricted ordinary-palindrome count. An equivalent derivation not indexed in the inspected literature remains possible; the status expresses the supported self-review, not a global uniqueness certificate.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
The exact joint length-index counts of palindromes are natural constrained-language invariants: the palindrome class is central to word combinatorics and universal-subsequence measurements. Their distribution is not obtained by simply halving unrestricted word counts, because the reversal-fixed midpoint can create a new arch. The parity-renewal law identifies the limiting probability \(1/(qH_q)\) for every alphabet and gives explicit oscillatory finite-length correction for the binary case. This resolves both an all-length exact enumeration and its natural asymptotic statistic. The statement does not assert new midpoint theory or new coupon-collector facts, and does not address growing alphabets or independent auditing.
