For every labeled alphabet of size \(q\ge2\), the complete length-and-scattered-subsequence-universality distributions of ordinary even and odd palindromes have explicit rational generating functions. The probability that a uniformly sampled odd-length palindrome has odd universality index converges exponentially to \(1/(qH_q)\), where \(H_q=\sum_{j=1}^q1/j\), and the finite binary probability is exactly \((1-(-1/2)^h)/3\) at length \(2h+1\).
