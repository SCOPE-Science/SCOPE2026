For two named commuting S5 equivalence relations, the exact isomorphism-class generating function is the multiset transform of a positive-integer-matrix orbit series given explicitly by Burnside cycle types. The resulting unlabelled counts begin \(1,4,9,25,54,136,294\) on one through seven worlds, and the vertex-transitive types on \(n\) worlds number exactly \(d_4(n)\), the ordered fourfold divisor function.
