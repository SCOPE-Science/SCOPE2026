## Mathematical value

PASS. The icosahedron is a smallest highly symmetric triangularly frustrated spin cluster relevant to molecular and quasicrystalline magnetic models. The classical spin-one vacancy cost creates a nontrivial two-parameter exact classification with 19 open regions, and a sharp double occupancy jump on the physically natural zero-field line. The first transition additionally hosts a boundary-only ten-site sector; the second hosts six- and seven-site sectors, generating precisely 462 and 351 coexisting ground assignments. These structured multiphase and boundary degeneracies are not a renaming of a familiar lattice rule or a single arbitrary test value. The result is intrinsically finite, with no asserted bulk transition.
