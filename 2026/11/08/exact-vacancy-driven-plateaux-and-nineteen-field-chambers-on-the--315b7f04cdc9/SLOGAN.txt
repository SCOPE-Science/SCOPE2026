The twelve-vertex icosahedral spin-one Blume–Capel antiferromagnet has exactly nineteen open zero-temperature field/crystal-field sectors, proved by exhaustive integer enumeration and rational lower-envelope certificates. At zero magnetic field it jumps from twelve occupied sites to eight at \(D/J=1/2\), and then to the all-vacant state at \(D/J=1\), with exact coexistence degeneracies \(462\) and \(351\).
