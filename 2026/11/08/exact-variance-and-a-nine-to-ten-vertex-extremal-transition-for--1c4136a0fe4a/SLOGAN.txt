For two independent uniform spanning trees of \(K_{a,b}\), their number of shared edges has variance \(\frac{(a-1)(b-1)((a+b)^2-5(a+b)+7)}{a^2b^2}\). Among complete bipartite graphs of fixed order \(n\), the unique maximum switches from \(K_{3,n-3}\) at \(6\le n\le9\) to \(K_{2,n-2}\) at \(n\ge10\) (and is \(K_{2,n-2}\) at \(n=4,5\)).
