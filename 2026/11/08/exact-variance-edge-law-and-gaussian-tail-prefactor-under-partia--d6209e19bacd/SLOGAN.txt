A harmonically confined Brownian particle with Poissonian proportional resetting has a stationary Gaussian far tail multiplied by the exact algebraic depletion \(|x|^{-r/\gamma}\), independent of the reset contraction factor \(0\le q<1\). The prefactor is computable from a convergent stationary-moment series and increases strictly, diverging as \(q\uparrow1\), while the variance deficit has an exact power-law density near zero.
