For odd-state Černý automata driven by independent fair letters, the most difficult pair's coalescence time has exact variance \(n(n-1)(4n^4-8n^3+3n^2+7n-14)/6\) for every odd \(n\ge3\). Its coefficient of variation tends to \(\sqrt{2/3}\), revealing order-one relative fluctuations beyond the previously known cubic mean.
