For proper three-colourings of a finite cycle, binary tests of whether a vertex has a specified colour have exact VC dimension \(\lfloor3n/4\rfloor\) and Littlestone dimension \(n-1\) for every \(n\ge3\). The closing edge leaves a linear adaptive-versus-static gap \(\lceil n/4\rceil-1\), first positive on the five-cycle.
