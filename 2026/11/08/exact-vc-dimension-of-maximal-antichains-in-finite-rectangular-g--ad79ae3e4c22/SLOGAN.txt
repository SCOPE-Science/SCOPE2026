For maximal antichains of the coordinatewise grid \([m]\times[n]\), the exact binary membership VC dimension is \(\min\{m,n,\lfloor2(m+n)/5\rfloor\}\). A local slack-coordinate obstruction gives the upper bound, and anti-diagonally concatenated \(2\times3\) and \(1\times2\) shattering gadgets attain it for every rectangle.
