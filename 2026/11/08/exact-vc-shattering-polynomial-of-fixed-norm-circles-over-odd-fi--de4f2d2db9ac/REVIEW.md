# Review: Exact VC-shattering polynomial of fixed-norm circles over odd finite fields

## Correctness — PASS
The circle-size character sum, the two-circle intersection lemma, explicit two-center pair geometry and the antipodal obstruction are proven for all odd prime powers, not inferred from experiments. Counting once- and twice-centered pairs gives \(s_2=q^2S^2/4\); choosing three antipodal pairs and signs gives \(s_3=q^2S(S-2)(S-4)/6\). Three points on one circle determine its center, excluding larger shattered supports. The low-field and empty-trace cases are addressed. An independent finite enumeration of traces checked fifteen field/radius instances.

## Originality — PASS within inspected sources
Fitzpatrick–Iosevich–McDonald–Wyman (arXiv:2108.13231, 2021) study exactly these circle classifiers but provide dimension/existence bounds and constructed witnesses, rather than all-support coefficient counts or the antipodal classification. The related distance-chain result (arXiv:2210.03058, 2022) and pseudorandom-graph result (arXiv:2303.07878, 2023) likewise do not entail the formulas. Published mathematical-record searches and accessible earlier claim lists produced no equivalent theorem; unindexed or differently worded priority remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The enumerator refines the known scalar VC bound for a canonical finite-field metric-incidence system. It gives a complete geometric classification, an exact small-field threshold and a quantitative difference between random pair and random triple shattering. These are intrinsic invariants of the motivating range family, not arbitrary parameters.

## Limitations
The result concerns odd characteristic, fixed nonzero quadratic norm and the full plane. An exhaustive literature priority certificate and independent external mathematical validation are not available. Computer checks corroborate, but do not replace, the stated proof.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
