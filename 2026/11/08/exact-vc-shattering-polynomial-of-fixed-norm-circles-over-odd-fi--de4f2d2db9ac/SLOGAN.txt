For every odd prime power \(q\ge3\), the radius-\(t\) quadratic-circle classifiers in \(\mathbb F_q^2\) have the exact shattering polynomial \(1+q^2z+q^2S^2z^2/4+q^2S(S-2)(S-4)z^3/6\), where \(S=q-\chi(-1)\) and \(t\ne0\). Shattered triples are exactly the concyclic triples with no antipodal pair; the VC dimension is two at \(q=3,5\) and three at every \(q\ge7\).
