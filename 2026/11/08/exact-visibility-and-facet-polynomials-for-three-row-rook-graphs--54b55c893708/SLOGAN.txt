For every \(n\ge2\), ordinary mutual-visibility sets in the three-row rook graph \(K_3\square K_n\) have an explicit complete size-generating polynomial. Its inclusion-maximal sets fall into exactly three column-support types, with facet sizes \(n+2\) and, when \(n\ge3\), \(n+3\); the exact labelled multiplicities are \(n3^{n-1}+3n(n-1)\) and \(n(n-1)(n-2)3^{n-3}\), respectively.
