For every labelled cycle \(C_n\), the complete weight enumerator of pointwise-minimal Roman dominating functions is rational in cycle length and weight, with a cyclic private-neighbor gap classification and every weight from \(\lceil2n/3\rceil\) to \(n\) attainable. The unweighted numerical sequence coincides with a known 1992 Fielder sequence, while the bivariate minimal-only distribution distinguishes the finding from prior enumerations.
