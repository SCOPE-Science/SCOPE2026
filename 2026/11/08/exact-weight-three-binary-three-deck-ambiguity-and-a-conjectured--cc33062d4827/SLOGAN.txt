Every binary word with exactly three ones has at most one distinct partner sharing its complete scattered three-deck; a simple involution swaps the two inner zero gaps and adjusts the outer gaps. Counting its two-element classes proves the all-length generating function conjectured by Chrisnata and collaborators and gives a sharp fixed-weight three-deck code size of \(\binom n3-c_n\), where \(\sum_{n\ge3}c_nz^n=z^7/((1-z)^2(1-z^2)(1-z^4))\).
