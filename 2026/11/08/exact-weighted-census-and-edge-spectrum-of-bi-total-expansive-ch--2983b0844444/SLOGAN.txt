Bi-total expansive correspondences between two finite reflexive chains have a complete weighted normal form and every edge count from \(\max(m,n)\) through \(mn\). Their number is \(2^{(m-1)(n-1)+O(\log(m+n))}\), and, in the square case, occupy a \(2^{-2n+O(\log n)}\) fraction of all binary relations.
