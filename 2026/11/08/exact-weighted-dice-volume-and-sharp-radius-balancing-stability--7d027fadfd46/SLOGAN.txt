For three centered, pairwise perpendicular circular disks of radii \(a,b,c\), the Minkowski sum has volume \(2\pi(a^2b+a^2c+b^2a+b^2c+c^2a+c^2b)+Cabc\), where \(C=\sqrt2\pi^2{}_3F_2(-1/4,1/4,1/2;1,1;1)\). At fixed total radius \(S\), equal radii uniquely maximize volume; the sharp global deficit is at least \(S(2C-3\pi)/9\) times the squared distance from equal radii, with equality also at the three two-disk boundary midpoints.
