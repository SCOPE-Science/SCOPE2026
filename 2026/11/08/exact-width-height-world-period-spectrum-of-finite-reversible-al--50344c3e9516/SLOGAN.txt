On finite reversible Alexandrov spaces, the exact existence of a temporal period under simultaneous world-count, width and height caps is equivalent to packing the period's maximal prime-power factors into antichain layers. For period \(27720\), exactly \(40\) worlds are necessary, while the minimal height falls from \(5\) to \(4\) as the permitted width rises from \(11\) to \(12\).
