For the inverse-dependence disjunction \(\operatorname{dep}(x,y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(y,x)\), the number of irredundant splitting witnesses is a product of tree-component orders times two per unicyclic component. On a \(d\)-by-\(d\) alphabet its sharp maximum grows as \(3^{2d/3}\) up to an exact residue-dependent factor, and an inclusion-exclusion identity counts every witness over every possible team.
