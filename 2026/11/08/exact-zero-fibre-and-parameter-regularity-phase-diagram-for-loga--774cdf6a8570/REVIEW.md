# Scientific self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The precise domain is the punctured unit disk with \(t>-1\) and integer logarithmic order \(q\ge1\). The norm of each Laurent monomial is evaluated exactly by a gamma integral, fixing which negative exponents belong to the Hilbert space. The rational numerator is rebuilt by the differential generating operator. The all-orders real-rootedness proof uses strict sign interlacing; reciprocity reduces the physical disk count to crossings at \(-1\); and the Euler-polynomial derivative/symmetry induction proves that the only interior parameter crossing occurs at the half-residue of an odd order. The \(C^{q-1}\), non-\(C^q\) defect comes from one explicit mode whose coefficient is switched on at an integer parameter. The accompanying checker passed 293 tests, with exact Sturm counts and symbolic identities. Finite checks supplement rather than replace the proof. The limitation is to these weights, off-diagonal points and integer logarithmic exponents.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected literature

The closest directly relevant work is the explicit Poincaré cusp model by Auvray, Ma and Marinescu (2021, Section 3.1), which has an integral-power monomial sum and studies high tensor-power asymptotics, not the full fractional-residue zero-fibre law. Perälä (2017, Section 3) proves existence of radial weights with prescribed finite numbers of zeros and a general finiteness bound, not the family-wide count and differentiability threshold. Blaschke (2012, main theorems) treats logarithmic singularities at the **outer boundary**, not the interior puncture. Marinescu and Xing (2026) establish real-analytic dependence for Euclidean-boundary-distance weights on smooth pseudoconvex domains: the puncture-mode jump in the present theorem cannot be deduced from that different smooth-domain result. Searches also considered Euler–Frobenius polynomial reductions and matching exact zero counts. The underlying Euler-polynomial algebra is classical and not presented as new. Risk remains that an equivalent weighted-kernel application appears in older spline or punctured-domain literature; the complete 2026 preprint and Schempp's historical paper were not available for full reading. Failed searches do not establish publication priority.

## Value — PASS

The model is a natural explicit singular-weight test case for parameter dependence of reproducing kernels and the Lu Qi-Keng zero problem. The theorem gives a complete and sharp integer/half-integer parameter phase diagram at arbitrary logarithmic order. Its exact \(q\)-th derivative jump supplies a structural boundary to analytic-in-weight results, and the root count separates zero-free logarithmic orders from arbitrarily many root fibres. These are not claimed to extend to arbitrary radial weights or noninteger log exponents.
