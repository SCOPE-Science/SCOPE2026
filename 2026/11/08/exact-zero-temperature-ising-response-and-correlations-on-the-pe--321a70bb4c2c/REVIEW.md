# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The local triangle decomposition exactly converts the Hamiltonian into a count of all-equal triangles, and the degree-sum identity proves the global energy bounds and their equality cases for every real field. Exact inclusion-exclusion over all 1024 selected constraint sets establishes the zero-field polynomial and each of 105 pair correlations; a second exhaustive enumerator based on a Kneser model independently checks all 32768 configurations. The computed integer numerators and ground counts agree. The known matching polynomial and an independent deletion recursion agree on 332 total matchings, six of them perfect. The proof is fixed finite-system mathematics, not an extrapolation from partial experiments.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, with residual risk

The closest line-graph Ising work of Dyer et al. explicitly supplies the general Holant expression and approximation/sampling algorithms, not the specialized fifteen-spin zero-field magnetization coefficients or the three distance-resolved correlations. Loh et al. treat an infinite periodic triangular-kagome lattice, not this finite Petersen-derived cluster, and are credited for the triangle/frustration-to-dimer context. The Sage graph-theory manual already tabulates the Petersen matching polynomial: the matching coefficients and their sum are not claimed as original. No equivalent target polynomial or correlation spectrum was located in the targeted published-record and literature searches. Unindexed older graph-cut/NAE enumeration tables remain a genuine historical-priority risk, so the finding is not represented as independently established publication priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The canonical smallest snark gives a nonarbitrary finite system of ten coupled frustrated triangles, with 15 spins and the full symmetry of a distance-regular line graph. The exact field response resolves the physical one-third plateau and saturation boundary, while the complete zero-field magnetization distribution and shell-by-shell two-point correlations quantify frustration beyond the standard matching correspondence. The nonlocal negative distance-three correlation and sign-changing profile are informative exact finite-size observables, not arbitrary slices of a large table. No downstream bulk-phase claim is needed for this finite classification.

## Closest literature and limitations

The arXiv full texts 0711.3471 and 2005.07944 were inspected in relevant methodological and claims sections; the latter's Holant formula was explicitly compared to the new evaluated coefficients. The published Sage manual's Petersen matching-polynomial example was read and recognized as covering that subcount. Earlier potentially unindexed Ising enumerations remain possible. The result is restricted to zero temperature, one fifteen-spin graph, uniform positive coupling, and uniform longitudinal field; it has no independent external audit.
