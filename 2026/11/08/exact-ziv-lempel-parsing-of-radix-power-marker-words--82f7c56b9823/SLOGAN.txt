For the binary word marked exactly at positions \(b^j-1\), the entire incremental Ziv–Lempel parsing is explicit in every base \(b\ge2\). Its phrase-start count is \(\log_2 N+O(1)\) in base two but \(2\log_b N+O_b(1)\) in every higher base, because higher-base gaps produce alternating new zero-runs and first-marker-ending factors.
