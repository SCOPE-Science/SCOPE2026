The squarefree integers with exactly four prime factors and \(\sigma(n)=2\varphi(n)\) are precisely \(24871,29029,50065,58435,64285,87685,137885,140335\). Exact monotone rational bounds turn the unrestricted classification into twelve possible three-prime prefixes and eight surviving prime completions; all eight terms were previously listed numerically, but the result proves global exhaustion.
