For every \(J\ge9\), the five consecutive step-two integers \(L_J+23,L_J+25,L_J+27,L_J+29,L_J+31\) are all Lucas–Niven exactly when \(J\equiv7,13\pmod{24}\), and then both immediate step-two neighbors fail the Lucas–Niven test. These maximal five-term runs form an infinite explicitly counted family with \(\log X/(12\log\varphi)+O(1)\) starting points at most \(X\).
