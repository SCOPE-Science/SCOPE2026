Every period-doubling word \(D_k\) for \(k\geq3\) has exactly four minimal unique substrings, all of length \(2^{k-2}+1\), with explicitly classified zero-based starting positions \(2^{k-2}-1\), \(3\cdot2^{k-3}-1\), \(2^{k-1}-1\), and \(3\cdot2^{k-2}-1\). A four-block decomposition and exact companion-prefix divergences prove that no other minimal unique substrings occur.
