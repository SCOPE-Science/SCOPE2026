# Scientific review: consecutive pentagonal triple symmetry

## Correctness — PASS
The result is the exact equivalence \(S_n\) symmetric if and only if \(n\in\{2,3,5,40\}\), for every integer \(n\ge2\), together with the four listed Frobenius numbers and genera. The proof establishes the pairwise gcd identities; separates the two-generator cases; applies the classical, necessary-and-sufficient three-generator gluing criterion only to minimal triples; eliminates both adjacent-pair gluing families algebraically; and uses \(\gcd(P_n,P_{n+2})\mid35\) to prove no gluing for \(n\ge142\). The remaining \(140\) indices are handled exhaustively with two independently written exact tests, not sampling. The \(44\) nonadjacent-gcd cases are explicitly certified in `finite_residue_certificate.csv`. An independently written Apéry shortest-path implementation verifies the exceptional Frobenius and genus values. The checker is finite evidence attached to a logically complete infinite proof, not a substitute for its cutoff.

## Originality — PASS within available literature
The most directly related public data are OEIS A069757, which gives the Frobenius numbers of exactly the same three-pentagonal-generator family and tabulates \(258529\) at index \(40\). Those preexisting Frobenius values are not newly claimed. The new conclusion is a necessary-and-sufficient **symmetry/complete-intersection classification for every index**, plus the exceptional genera obtained through standard formulas. For example, index \(4\) has the preexisting odd Frobenius number \(287\) but genus \(145\), disproving an inference from odd Frobenius number to symmetry. Robles-Pérez–Rosales (arXiv:1706.04378) prove telescopic structures for triangular and tetrahedral sequences, which are different generator sequences, and their manuscript contains no treatment of the ordinary pentagonal family. García-Sánchez–González–Planas-Vilanova (arXiv:2404.06358) study semigroups generated by \(a,a+1,a+2\), not the quadratic pentagonal triple. The general gluing criterion itself is classical and explicitly credited. Targeted web, full-text and mathematical-record searches did not identify a covering classification. Unindexed work remains a residual originality risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Three consecutive ordinary pentagonal numbers define a natural numerical semigroup family already catalogued by its Frobenius numbers. The symmetry predicate is a structural invariant inaccessible from a Frobenius-only sequence table. An exact global classification reveals a scarce, nonobvious three-generator exception at index \(40\), controlled by the prime factors of \(35\), and demonstrates a sharp contrast with the all-telescopic consecutive triangular triples. The result is a motivated full classification of an infinite family, not a fabricated slice or simply a small numerical improvement. No broader classification for all polygonal orders is claimed.

## Source comparisons and limitations
The exact theorem is not implied by the published triangular/tetrahedral results, by the general consecutive-integer factorization-length paper, or by the prior OEIS Frobenius list. The method uses classical three-generator theory. Its finite part is backed by an independently re-evaluable standard-library program and explicit finite certificate. Potential non-indexed equivalent work is not excluded. No independent scientific audit has occurred.
