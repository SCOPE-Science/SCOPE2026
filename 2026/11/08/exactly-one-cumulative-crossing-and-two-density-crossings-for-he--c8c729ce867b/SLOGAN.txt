For quenched Brownian diffusivities \(D_\varepsilon=\mu(1+\varepsilon Z)\) with bounded centered nonconstant \(Z\), the two first-passage-density crossings and the cumulative crossing converge as \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) to the universal times \(x^2/[2\mu(3+\sqrt6)]\), \(x^2/(6\mu)\), and \(x^2/[2\mu(3-\sqrt6)]\). The previously established global crossing-count and bracketing theorems are credited, not claimed as new.
