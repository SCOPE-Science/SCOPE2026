Uniform-edge irreversible dimer adsorption on a cycle with one leaf at every vertex has exact mean jamming density \(\rho=\frac12+\frac{\sqrt\pi}{4e}\operatorname{erfi}(1)=0.769039753\ldots\). The finite-ring expectation differs from \(n\rho\) by a signed term asymptotic to \((-1)^{n-1}2^{n-3}/(2n-1)!!\): odd/even rings alternate around the thermodynamic limit with superfactorially small corrections.
