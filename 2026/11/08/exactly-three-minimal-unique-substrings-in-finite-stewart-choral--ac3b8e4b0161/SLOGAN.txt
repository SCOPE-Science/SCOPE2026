Every finite substitution iterate \(W_k=\varphi^k(0)\), with \(\varphi(0)=001\), \(\varphi(1)=011\) and \(k\ge2\), has exactly three minimal unique substrings, with explicit positions and lengths depending linearly on \(3^{k-1}\). A four-interval repetition cover and occurrence-preserving trimmed images prove this rigid infinite-family classification.
