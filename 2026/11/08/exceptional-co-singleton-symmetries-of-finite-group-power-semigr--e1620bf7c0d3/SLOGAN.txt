Among all finite groups, the only nontrivial automorphisms of a nonempty-subset power semigroup that fix every subset except possibly the co-singletons occur for \(C_3\) and \(C_2\times C_2\). Their full power-semigroup automorphism groups are respectively \(S_3\) and \(S_4\), demonstrating a precise failure of augmentation rigidity despite singleton rigidity.
