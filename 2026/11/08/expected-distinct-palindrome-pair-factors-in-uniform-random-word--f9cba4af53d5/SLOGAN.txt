For a uniformly random \(q\)-ary word of length \(N\), with fixed \(q\ge2\), the expected number of **distinct factors expressible as products of two palindromes** is \(\Theta_q(\sqrt N\log_q N)\). This has an extra logarithmic factor compared with the classical expectation \(\Theta_q(\sqrt N)\) for factors that are themselves palindromes.
