In a two-resource nonatomic game with a continuum of pure equilibria, every positive amount of Gumbel noise uniquely selects a population share, but arbitrarily small cost biases can redirect its zero-noise limit to **any** pure equilibrium. The entire selection is controlled by the exponent \(-\sigma\log|\delta_\sigma|\), with an explicit inverse-hyperbolic-sine formula for the noisy equilibrium.
