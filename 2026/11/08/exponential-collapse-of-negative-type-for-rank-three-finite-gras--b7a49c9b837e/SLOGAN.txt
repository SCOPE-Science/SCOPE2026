On all rank-three finite Grassmann graphs over any field of order \(q\), the exchange distance has a unique sharp negative-type exponent \(\beta_{q,n}\in(0,1)\), determined by an explicit three-term equation, and only harmonic degree two becomes critical. For fixed \(q\), the threshold collapses exponentially: \(q^{n-2}\beta_{q,n}\to(q-1)/(q\log3-(q+1)\log2)\).
