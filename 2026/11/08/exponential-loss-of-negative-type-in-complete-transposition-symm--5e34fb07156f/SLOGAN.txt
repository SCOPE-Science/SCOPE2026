The generalized roundness \(\beta_n\) of the complete-transposition Cayley metric on \(S_n\) decays exponentially, with \(\limsup_{n\to\infty}\beta_n^{1/n}\le3/4\). Exact initial values \(\beta_3,\beta_4,\beta_5\) are determined by character equations, including the sharp Euclidean embedding dimensions at their thresholds.
