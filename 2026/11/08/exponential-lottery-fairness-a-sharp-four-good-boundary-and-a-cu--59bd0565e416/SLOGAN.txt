With independent exponential good values, ex-ante envy-free division between two agents always maximizes ex-post envy-freeness probability through three goods, but can fail at four. For four means \((1,2,t,t+3)\), the optimal split switches exactly at the unique positive root of \(3t^3+2t^2-46t-33=0\) from a fair singleton-versus-triple division to an unequal pair-versus-pair division.
