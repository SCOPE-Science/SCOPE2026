For antipodal quotients of Boolean cubes in every dimension \(n=4m\ge12\), the maximal exponent of conditional negative type has explicit lower and upper bounds of the form \(2^{-n}\) times subexponential factors. Its exact exponential decay base is \(\lim_{m\to\infty}\beta_{4m}^{1/(4m)}=1/2\), proved using a parity-character Fourier obstruction and a uniform small-power perturbation bound.
