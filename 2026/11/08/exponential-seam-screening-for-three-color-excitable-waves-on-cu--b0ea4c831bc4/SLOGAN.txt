On circular two-rail and Möbius two-rail graphs, the exact difference between counts of initially primitive-three Greenberg–Hastings configurations is \(2(1+(-1)^n)+4s_n\), with \(s_n=-5s_{n-2}+3s_{n-3}\) and \((s_0,s_1,s_2)=(3,0,-10)\). Both counts grow like \(\bigl((3+\sqrt{17})/2\bigr)^n\), while their relative discrepancy decreases exponentially.
