# Scientific review: subcritical complete-window spells

## Correctness — PASS

The claim applies to independent uniform coupon draws conditioned on a stationary *new entrance* into full coverage, with \(M_n=\lfloor\alpha n\log n\rfloor\), fixed \(0<\alpha<1\). A direct counting argument gives the uniform-on-surjections law of the preceding length-\(M_n-1\) word; the departing symbol introduces an independent constant factor. Exact singleton-prefix factorial moments are Stirling-number ratios, whose \(n^{-\alpha r}\) asymptotic follows from the zero-truncated-Poisson occupancy representation and a lattice local limit for an \(O(\log n)\) off-center shift. The first initial singleton creates the first failed window unless it is replenished; conversely a failure with no initial singleton requires an initially repeated color to expire entirely. Both discrepancies are bounded by \(O(n^{\alpha-1})\). The Poisson limit therefore yields exponential normalized spell duration. The separate exact mean is the stationary complete-window mass divided by the entrance flux and asymptotically equals \(n^\alpha\). The checker independently verified 29 exact finite configurations, including factorial moments and complete future-draw enumeration, but finite enumeration does not establish an infinite limit. The critical nonstandard step is the triangular-array local limit, supported by a characteristic-function argument and the inspected appendix of Long (2026).

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority

Long's arXiv:2604.26298 establishes entrance probability and the exponential *waiting time until entry*, and supplies the occupancy representation, but the inspected full text does not give the entry-conditioned distribution of contiguous *spell lengths*. Its mass/flux ratio implies the exact mean and is duly credited, not claimed new. The 2012 motivating question discusses the global waiting time rather than contiguous conditional duration; the general rare-set return-time literature is methodologically relevant but has not been shown to imply the model-specific \(n^\alpha\) exponential spell law. Accessible research records were searched with complete-window duration, arrival-conditioned spell, singleton-position Poisson, and related equivalent expressions; no covering result surfaced. The most material residual risks are an unindexed theorem on clump-size laws and a stronger result in general scan statistics; neither was asserted absent from inaccessible sources. Uniqueness cannot be inferred solely from these searches.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Because complete sliding windows arrive in rare bursts, the time until first completion and the length of a burst answer genuinely different reliability questions. The new all-\(\alpha\) exponential spell law identifies a natural exact asymptotic within-spell distribution from the microscopic singleton mechanism. The statement is neither a mere reparameterization of the published inter-entry exponential law nor an arbitrary finite slice. The source's stationary mass and entry flux yield only the average spell duration without its distribution. Endpoints, heterogeneity, and the size-biased stationary-window law remain outside the claim.
