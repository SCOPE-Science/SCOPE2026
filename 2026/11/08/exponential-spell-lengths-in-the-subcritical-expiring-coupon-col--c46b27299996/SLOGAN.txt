When a subcritical expiring coupon collector first reaches complete coverage, the ensuing consecutive spell of complete sliding windows lasts on scale \(n^\alpha\), with a mean-one exponential limit after division by \(n^\alpha\) for every fixed \(0<\alpha<1\). The mechanism is a Poisson cloud of initial singleton coupons about to leave the window, distinct from the exponentially long waiting time to the next entrance.
