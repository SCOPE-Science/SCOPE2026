For a fixed positive-definite two-mode sketched Rayleigh–Ritz model, nonreal Ritz pairs have an exact Gaussian probability at every sketch size and a sharp oversampling law \(p_s\sim(1+h^2)^{1-s/2}/(h\sqrt{3\pi s})\). The risk remains strictly positive at every finite sketch size even in genuine high-dimensional sketching.
