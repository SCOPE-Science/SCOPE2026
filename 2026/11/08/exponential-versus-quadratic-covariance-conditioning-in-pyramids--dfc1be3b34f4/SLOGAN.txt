For a pyramid over the \(m\)-fold Cartesian power of any fixed full-dimensional polytope with \(v\) vertices, uniform-vertex covariance has condition number asymptotic to \(v^m\) times the base vertex-covariance top eigenvalue, whereas uniform-volume covariance has condition number asymptotic to \((mr)^2\) times the base volume-covariance top eigenvalue. This gives a universal sharp exponential-versus-quadratic separation, with exact ratio constant \(q+2\) after natural normalization for the clique-windmill stable-set family.
