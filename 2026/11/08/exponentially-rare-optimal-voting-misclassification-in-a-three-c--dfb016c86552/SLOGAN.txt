For the established three-class flip voting games \(\widetilde G_m\), the smallest possible fraction of coalitions misclassified by **any** weighted-majority rule is \(\Theta(m^3/8^m)\), even though their known normalized weight-excess is only \(1/(47m)\). Among class-symmetric weighted rules the exact error fraction is \(\binom{m}{3}/8^m\) for every \(m\geq9\); an explicit balanced-trade argument lower-bounds the unrestricted error within a factor of 20.
