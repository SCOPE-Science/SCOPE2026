For any fixed pair of Hermite windows with disjoint Laguerre root circles, the unique high-radius optimal exposure allocation places only \(b(T)/c+O(b(T)^2)\) of its weight on the lower order, where \(b(T)=e^{-T}L_n(T)^2\) and \(c\) is the least lower-window response on a higher-window zero circle. The sharp two-channel ambiguity floor is \(b(T)-b(T)^2/c+o(b(T)^2)\): a precise second-order price for repairing the higher-order window's radial zeros.
