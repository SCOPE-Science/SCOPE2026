# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The normal-basis coordinate argument gives an explicit bijection between the idempotent elements over each binary extension and the fixed space of the corresponding structure-matrix power; it handles singular and invertible matrices, all dimensions, and all positive extension degrees. The Fitting decomposition proves the exact least period and attainment criterion. Elementary-divisor valuations prove the block formula. Independently programmed exhaustive small-field checks directly enumerate idempotents, including all binary matrices up to dimension three in the tested fields; the script ends in `VERIFY_OK`. Computational examples do not replace the unrestricted proof.

## Originality
PASS relative to checked sources. The closest evolution-algebra references study graph structure and derivations (Elduque–Labra 2019; Reis–Cadavid 2020), classification of perfect/idempotent algebras (Wei–Zou 2023), or existence of idempotent elements (García-Martínez–Pérez-Rodríguez 2026). None of the materially inspected primary results contains the all-binary-matrix field-extension count and recovery of its exact least period. The basic idempotent equation, normal bases, Fitting theory, and primary-decomposition counts are earlier results and are not claimed new. Full text of Wei–Zou was inaccessible and a broader semilinear fixed-point theorem might subsume the application: publication priority remains provisional.

## Value
PASS. For graph evolution algebras, existence and number of individual idempotent elements across field extensions are natural nonassociative invariants. An exact formula for all binary structures, a full elementary-divisor resolution, a guaranteed least period that reconstructs the stable adjacency order, and a degree-seven first-appearance example provide a complete structural answer rather than a small table or arbitrary slice. The result is restricted to binary structure coefficients and finite fields of characteristic two.
