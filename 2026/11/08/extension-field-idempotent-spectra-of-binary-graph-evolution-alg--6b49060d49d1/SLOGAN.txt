For any evolution algebra defined by a binary directed-graph structure matrix \(A\), the exact number of idempotent elements over \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\) is \(2^{\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\ker(A^f-I)}\). Its extension-degree count has least period exactly the order of \(A\) on its stable image, with an explicit elementary-divisor formula and delayed-appearance examples.
