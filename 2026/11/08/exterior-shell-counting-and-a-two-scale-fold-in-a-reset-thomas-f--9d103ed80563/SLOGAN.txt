In a Poisson-reset Thomas–Fermi condensate, the particle fraction in a two-sided exterior shell has an exact two-scale stationary law with a geometry-only upper cutoff, a zero atom and sharp \(k^{-1/2}\) or \(k^{-3/4}\) lower-edge singularities. Near the maximal shell count, two distinct cloud-expansion scales become equally likely under conditional inference, producing a universal fold singularity.
