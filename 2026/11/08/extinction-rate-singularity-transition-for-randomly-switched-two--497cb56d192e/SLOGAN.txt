Independently switching two legitimate two-sex quadratic stochastic maps produces double-exponential extinction, but the normalized logarithmic extinction rate has an exact affine dyadic-Bernoulli law. Balanced switching makes that rate uniformly distributed; every biased switch makes it singular continuous with dimension \(h(p)/\log2\), where \(h(p)=-p\log p-(1-p)\log(1-p)\), and different biases give mutually singular laws with identical full interval support.
