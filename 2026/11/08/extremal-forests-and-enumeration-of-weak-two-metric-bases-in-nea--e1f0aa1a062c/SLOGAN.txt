For \(8\le n\le m\le2n-5\), every minimum weak two-metric landmark set in \(K_n\square K_m\) is exactly a spanning bipartite forest with \(\lfloor(n+m)/3\rfloor\) components, all trees of size three except at most two small surplus components. The complete labelled minimum-basis count is a bivariate coefficient of explicit order-three, order-four and order-five tree polynomials, sharpening the already-known numerical dimension formula.
