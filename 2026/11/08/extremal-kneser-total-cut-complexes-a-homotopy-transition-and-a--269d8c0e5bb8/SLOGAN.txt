The total maximum-independent-set cut complex of the Kneser graph \(\operatorname{KG}(n,k)\) changes from a single high-dimensional sphere at \(n=2k\) to a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}{k-1}\) spheres at \(n>2k\). Above the boundary its top integral homology is a hook exterior-power lattice, while at the boundary the sphere orientation is the sign representation exactly when \(k-1\) is a power of two.
