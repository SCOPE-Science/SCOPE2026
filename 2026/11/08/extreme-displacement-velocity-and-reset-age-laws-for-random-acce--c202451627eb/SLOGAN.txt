For a stationary randomly accelerated particle with simultaneous Poisson resets of position and velocity, conditioning on an extreme displacement produces an exact oriented-velocity large-deviation rate and a wrong-way exponent \(4(\sqrt2-1)/3\) at the natural speed \(r\tau_x\). Reset age and velocity then have a coupled Gaussian fluctuation limit with universal correlation \(-\operatorname{sgn}(x)/\sqrt3\), separating ordinary stationary observations from crossing-weighted observations.
