For an equilateral finite metric with four consecutive edges of a distinguished cycle shortened to \(t\), the real Lipschitz-free unit ball has exactly \(38\cdot2^{N-4}-22\) facets for \(\tfrac12<t<1\). At \(t=\tfrac12\) and \(t=1\), the count collapses to distinct explicit values, yielding two sharp boundary losses in every dimension \(N\ge5\).
