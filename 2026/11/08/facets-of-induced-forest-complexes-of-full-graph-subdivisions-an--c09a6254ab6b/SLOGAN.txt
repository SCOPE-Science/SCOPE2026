Every inclusion-maximal induced forest of a full graph subdivision is determined by a selected original-vertex set whose omitted vertices have two neighbors in one selected component, together with spanning trees of those components. For \(S(K_n)\), this gives the exact facet polynomial \(\sum_{a=2}^n\binom na a^{a-2}z^{\binom n2+2a-1-\binom a2}\), and all inclusion-minimal feedback vertex set sizes and multiplicities.
