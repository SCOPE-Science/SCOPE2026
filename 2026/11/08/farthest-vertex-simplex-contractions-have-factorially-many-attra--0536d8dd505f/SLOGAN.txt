For every regular Euclidean \(d\)-simplex with \(d\ge3\), contracting toward the farthest vertex by any fixed fraction in \((0,1)\) yields exactly \(d!\) attracting \((d+1)\)-cycles, with geometrically spaced barycentric coordinates. Every initial condition eventually selects all vertices in cyclic order, and the \(d!\) basins have equal normalized volume.
