For a complete graph with \(b\ge2\) edges deleted at one vertex, the globally fastest symmetric Markov chain admits an exact three-regime SLEM and a constructive stochastic kernel. Sharp transitions at \(b=a^2\) and \(4b=2a^2+ab+a\), where \(a\) counts the vertex's remaining neighbors, distinguish universal lower-bound attainment, balanced spectral saturation, and one-sided gap limitation.
