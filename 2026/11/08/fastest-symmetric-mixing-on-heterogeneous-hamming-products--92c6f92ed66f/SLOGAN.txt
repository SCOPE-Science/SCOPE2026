On every heterogeneous Cartesian product of complete graphs, the fastest symmetric stochastic walk equalizes the nontrivial spectral relaxation rate of every coordinate, assigning edge probability \(\theta/q_i\) in direction \(i\), with \(\theta=\min\{2/(d+1),1/\sum_i(1-1/q_i)\}\). This gives an exact dimension–alphabet threshold for when optimal holding vanishes and keeps the mixing contraction at \(1/3\) on \(K_2\square K_Q\), while the best single-edge-weight design deteriorates toward \(1\).
