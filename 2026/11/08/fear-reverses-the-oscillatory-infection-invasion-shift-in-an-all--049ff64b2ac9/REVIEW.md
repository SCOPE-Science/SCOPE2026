# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The model is the positive-parameter specialization of [1], equation (2.2). The explicit Jacobian, analytic nondegenerate Hopf reduction, standard first Lyapunov formula, period-integrated log identities and exact rational witnesses establish the claim. The accompanying verifier computes both rational cases exactly and checks attracting orbit averages. It does not substitute finite sampling for the analytic \(\varepsilon\to0^+\) argument.

## Originality — PASS (best available evidence)
Al Amri, Khan and Greenhalgh establish the ecological Hopf crossing in the same model, not the exact cycle-mean or infection-invasion comparison. Bate and Hilker already establish the general averaged-invasion approach and show opposite signs in a different multi-infection ecological setting; that general fact is not presented as new. The two model-specific rational fear-floor examples with identical \(\alpha\) and opposite predator-mean shifts are not implied by their analysis. Public mathematical records concerning fear–cooperation cycles, two-prey models, or discrete fear–Allee maps have different equations. A 2019 fear–infection paper was available only through abstract-level material, which limits the publication-priority comparison; its explicit fear-dependent transmission mechanism differs. Unindexed equivalent model-specific work is a residual risk.

## Value — PASS
A biological effect can reverse the reliability of a prevalence-invasion calculation on an unstable ecological equilibrium: the susceptible-prey mean stays fixed, but predator-density curvature produces a controlled signed correction. The exact contrasting rational parameter cases demonstrate an actual local model regime change rather than a qualitative possibility or trivial substitution.

## Limitations
The theorem is local near an ecological Hopf crossing for an undelayed deterministic three-dimensional model; the positive ecological cycle is only asserted locally attracting within the disease-free plane. It makes no claim about endemic attractors, global population basins, stochastic persistence or general monotonicity as fear parameters change. Independent external evaluation has not occurred.
