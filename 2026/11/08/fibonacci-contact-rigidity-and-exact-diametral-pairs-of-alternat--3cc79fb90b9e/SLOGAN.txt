The alternating adjacent-sum polytopes have explicit unique Euclidean minimum enclosing balls, but their full sphere-contact sets change for \(d\ge4\) at \(s=2\): from \(3^r\) or \(5\cdot3^{r-1}\) contacts to exactly \(F_{d+2}\) weighted path-independent-set vertices. The number of unordered diametral pairs simultaneously changes from \(2^{r-1}\) to one, in either parity of the dimension.
