For two-type diversity-seeking jump games on a cycle with one vacancy, all pure equilibria are classified by isolated opposite-color agents or alternating runs of lengths one and two, giving an exact composition census. At equal type populations, a uniformly random placement is an equilibrium with exact exponential rate \(\frac{3+\sqrt5}{8}\) per red-blue pair, while the all-composition type-word count has a Fibonacci formula.
