For the Abelian sandpile on a fan graph with its hub as sink, the exact probability of any prescribed nonempty interval of topplings factorizes into two Fibonacci weights, making the left and right avalanche reaches conditionally independent at every finite size. Deep inside large fans, positive avalanches have independent geometric reaches and the complete size law \(\mathbb P(T=t)\to t\varphi^{-2t}/\sqrt5\) for \(t\ge1\), with \(\mathbb P(T=0)\to1-1/\sqrt5\).
