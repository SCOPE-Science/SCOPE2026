On every wheel graph \(W_n\) with rim length \(n\ge4\), the number of initially primitive-three states of synchronous parallel chip-firing is exactly \(3n(F_{2n+1}+\delta_n)\), where \(\delta_n\) depends only on \(n\bmod3\). Their conserved chip masses form exactly two parity-dependent integer bands, with outer-edge multiplicities \(6\) or \(12n\) and an explicit nine-state transfer formula for every coefficient.
