On the antiferromagnetic complete tripartite network \(K_{n,n,n}\), all weak single-spin minima admit an exact all-field classification, and isolated fields \(|h|/J=2k\) generate binomial-count spikes with parity-alternating configurational complexity. All extra nonground weak minima escape by a zero-cost flip followed by an energy-decreasing flip, so these spikes are not dynamically absorbing states.
