For a uniform invertible triangular evolution-algebra matrix over every binary finite field, the number of nonzero idempotents has an exact law independent of field size: it equals \(2^{D_n}-1\), with \(\Pr(D_{j+1}=D_j+1\mid D_j=d)=2^{-d}\). Its mean is \(n\) and its variance is \(\binom n2\), sharply contrasting the mean-one law for uniformly random unrestricted invertible matrices.
