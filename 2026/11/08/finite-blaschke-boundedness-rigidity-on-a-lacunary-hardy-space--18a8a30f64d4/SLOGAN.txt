A sparse weighted Hardy space can have exactly the same Hilbert–Schmidt composition-symbol class as classical Hardy space, yet among all nonconstant finite Blaschke products only the rotations of the powers \(z^{q^j}\) induce bounded composition operators. Every excluded finite Blaschke product has a universal sharp normalized-test growth \(\|C_B e_k\|\sim(k+1)^{\gamma/2}\), independent of its zeros.
