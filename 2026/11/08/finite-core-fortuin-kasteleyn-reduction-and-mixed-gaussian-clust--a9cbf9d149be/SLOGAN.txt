At edge scale \(n^{-1/2}\), the fixed hubs of a \(q\)-random-cluster model on \(K_{r,n}\) converge to a genuinely dependent finite Fortuin–Kasteleyn graph, while the attached ordinary population has independent Gaussian fluctuations at scale \(n^{1/4}\). For \(q\ne1\), its effective hub edges retain component-weight correlations that cannot be reproduced by independent bond thinning.
