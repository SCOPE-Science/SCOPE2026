For unitaries with at most \(n\) distinct antipodal eigenphase classes, the \(2n\) even Cartesian radii \(\rho_2,\rho_4,\ldots,\rho_{4n}\) determine exactly the unordered multiset of projective cyclic spectral gap lengths and their multiplicities. The entire \(L^p\)-radius profile cannot recover their cyclic ordering, as an explicit four-point pair demonstrates.
