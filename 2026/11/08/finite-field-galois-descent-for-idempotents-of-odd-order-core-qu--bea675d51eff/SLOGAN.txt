For every odd-order finite abelian group and every nonmodular finite coefficient field, the core-quandle algebra's complete idempotent Fourier-support distribution is an explicit product over Frobenius–signed-doubling character orbits. Frobenius descent both merges orbits and restricts allowable roots: the same dihedral quandle of order \(11\) has exactly \(68\) idempotents over \(\mathbb F_2\) but only \(24\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), including zero.
