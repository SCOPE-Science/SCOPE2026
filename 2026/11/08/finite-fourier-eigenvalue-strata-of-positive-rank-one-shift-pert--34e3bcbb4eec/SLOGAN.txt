Every conjugation-symmetric subset of the nonidentity \(m\)-th roots of unity occurs as the **entire point spectrum** of an explicitly positive rank-one perturbation of a shift-plus-permutation isometry on \(\ell_1\), even with arbitrarily small perturbation norm. All such operators still have the closed unit disk as their spectrum and the unit circle as their essential spectrum; their eigenvalue patterns form exact Fourier-linear strata and their powers grow at a computable linear rate.
