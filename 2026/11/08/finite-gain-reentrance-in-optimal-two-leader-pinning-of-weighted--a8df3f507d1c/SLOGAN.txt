For identical finite-gain leaders on weighted friendship networks, the exact two-leader optimum switches between hub–outer pairs and outer pairs in different triangles only for \(m=2,3\); at \(m\ge4\), a hub–outer pair always wins. With two triangles and within-triangle conductance \(r=1/4\), increasing the common pinning gain makes the optimum switch hub–outer, then outer–outer, then back to hub–outer.
