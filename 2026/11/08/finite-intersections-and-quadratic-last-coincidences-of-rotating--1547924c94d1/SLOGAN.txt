Distinct rotating-memory Fibonacci periods have only finitely many common positive values, and every common value occurs at a synchronized index (apart from the duplicated initial one). Their complete intersection-count spectrum is explicit: for neighboring periods \(k,k+1\) with \(k\ge4\), the count is \(k(k-1)/2+k-2\) and the final shared index is exactly \(k^2-1\).
