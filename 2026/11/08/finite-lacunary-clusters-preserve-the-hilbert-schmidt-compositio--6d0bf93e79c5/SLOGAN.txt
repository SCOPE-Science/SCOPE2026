For weighted Hardy spaces whose small coefficients occur at any fixed pattern of at least two offsets from Hadamard-lacunary indices, the Hilbert–Schmidt composition-symbol class equals that of classical Hardy space exactly when \(\inf_{n\in\Lambda_F}n\beta_n>0\), for every lacunarity ratio \(q>1\). A finite invertible Toeplitz block and compact cross-cluster interactions recover missing coefficient energy, even for adjacent exceptional indices.
