For the stationary first-passage resetting Brownian gas with \(N\ge3\), any fixed number of simultaneous negative particle excursions shares the exact power-law exponent \(N-2\), with a sharp threshold-dependent coefficient and an inverse-gamma limiting reset age. All positive mixed distance moments exist exactly when their total degree is less than \(N-2\), separating existence of a stationary law from existence of its physically useful moments.
