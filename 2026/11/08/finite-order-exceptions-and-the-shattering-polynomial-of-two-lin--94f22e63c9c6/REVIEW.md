# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every existing finite projective plane of order q>=2, a shattered S with four selected collinear points cannot isolate all but one; two line unions cannot shatter six because both covering lines must carry a triple and deleting a point forces the original line. A shattered five-set therefore has one or two 3-point lines, and the private-line/disjoint-line isolation construction proves sufficiency for all q>=4, and only two-triple bow-ties when q=3. Counting line intersection multiplicities n_0=q²-4q+6-t gives the empty trace. Double counting collinear triples yields B_q=v*C(q+1,2)*C(q,2)² and T_q=v*C(q+1,3)*C(q²,2), with s5=T_q-B_q for q>=4 and s5=B_3=702. The q=2 and lower-degree strata are checked separately; this proof uses only incidence axioms, not a claim that plane exists at arbitrary q. Independently enumerated all two-line traces on every support k<=5 in Singer models for q=2 and q=3, obtaining exactly [1,7,21,28,0,0] and [1,13,78,286,702,702]. Those finite models do not by themselves prove non-Desarguesian cases.

## originality

PASS

Eleftheriou–Papadopoulos–Westhead (2025) already characterize two five-point incidence types and VC dimension five for real Euclidean two-line unions; those facts are not new. Their real affine setting cannot establish the sharp q=3 projective obstruction, q=2 lower cutoff or all-order exact coefficients for every finite projective plane. A separate later affine-plane nonparallel-lines result concerns a strictly smaller, different concept class. The finite-plane type counts and order-exception shattering polynomial survived statement-level comparison to full primary mathematics; priority is best knowledge.

## value

PASS

The number of shattered supports in a natural finite incidence range space is a meaningful sample-complexity invariant. This result gives the entire shattering polynomial uniformly for all finite projective planes (including non-Desarguesian), a sharp order-two/order-three/order-four transition, explicit counts of two maximal incidence configurations, and an asymptotic 10/q rarity of maximal shattered supports. It supplies strictly more information than a mere generic VC dimension and is not an arbitrary finite instance.
