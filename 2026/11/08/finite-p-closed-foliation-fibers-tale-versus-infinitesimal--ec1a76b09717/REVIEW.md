# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
For the precisely defined split surface and coefficient-scheme structure, the restricted-power identity reduces \(p\)-closure to the polynomial \(T(f)=f^p+\delta^{p-1}(f)-hf\). This is an equality of derivations and remains meaningful over coefficient algebras, rather than merely a comparison of geometric points. Riemann--Roch and the residue theorem give the correct dimension of the ambient polar-divisor fiber. Surjectivity of multiplication by \(p\) on an elliptic Picard variety constructs one logarithmic solution for every \(\mathbb F_p\)-valued zero-sum residue vector. The explicitly independent functions \(1,f_1^p,\ldots,f_{d-1}^p\) prove equality of defining ideals. Restriction to nonzero residues yields the asserted disjoint union of finite schemes; the two possible coordinate equations have length \(p\) but respectively \(p\) and one geometric point. The included independent finite residue census and characteristic-two calculations replayed successfully. The proof handles all primes, all reduced divisors and both possible types of \(Y\).

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Andreatta--Goren (arXiv:2610.32975v1) identify the fixed-pole-parameter space and pointwise \(p\)-closure criteria, with existence conclusions. The present statement gives the exact defining equations and the distinction between finite étale fibers and infinitesimal \(\alpha_p\) fibers of the natural restricted-Lie subscheme for a split ordinary-first-factor family. Direct searches for equivalent statements and stronger results in the research index, recent primary literature and older Cartier/foliation literature did not identify that scheme-level classification. Standard generalized-Jacobian and Cartier theory explains the residue and torsion ingredients but does not, without additional construction, give the stated scheme of embedded turbulent foliations. Further historical checking of niche foliation-moduli literature could affect priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The \(p\)-closed locus is an explicitly introduced closed subset of a natural foliation parameter space. Its scheme fibers, rather than simply their nonemptiness, are a natural local moduli invariant: the answer distinguishes reduced finite étale points from nonreduced height-one group schemes, and exhibits a length-versus-geometric-point discontinuity when the elliptic quotient changes from ordinary to supersingular. The result is uniform over arbitrary configurations of distinct polar points, not an arbitrary finite enumeration or a known example rewritten. The fixed divisor is geometrically motivated by the moduli map; the invariant is the complete fiber scheme at a generic divisor and can inform investigations of the global \(p\)-closed locus and its degeneration across the ordinary/supersingular boundary.

## Closest literature and limitations

Goren, *Foliations on abelian surfaces in positive characteristic*, arXiv:2610.32975v1 (first public 2026-10-23), especially their pointwise \(p\)-closure criterion and the parameter scheme over \(\operatorname{Sym}^d Y\). E. Z. Goren and E. de Shalit, arXiv:2205.00702, concern Shimura varieties rather than this fixed-divisor elliptic product situation. The proof also uses classical Riemann--Roch, Picard-division and Jacobson formulas. The new theorem is confined to the specified natural restricted-Lie scheme over reduced pole divisors; it does not classify nonsplit extensions, multiple poles, all moduli scheme structures or equivalence classes of foliations under automorphisms.
