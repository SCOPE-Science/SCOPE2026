For the split surface \(E\times Y\) with ordinary \(E\), the natural scheme of \(p\)-closed turbulent foliations having any fixed reduced degree-\(d\) pole divisor has \(N_p(d)=((p-1)^d+(p-1)(-1)^d)/p\) components, each a copy of \(\mathbb Z/p\) when \(Y\) is ordinary and of \(\alpha_p\) when it is supersingular. The scheme length is unchanged across the two cases, but its number of geometric points drops by a factor of \(p\).
