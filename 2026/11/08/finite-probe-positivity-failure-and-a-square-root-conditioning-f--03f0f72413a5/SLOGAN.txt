In Gaussian projected Lanczos covariance completion, exact quadrature and exact boundary correction can still produce a **negative** Monte Carlo bulk variance, with exact law \(\widehat\omega_N\stackrel{d}{=}(\chi_N^2+\kappa^{-1}\widetilde\chi_N^2)/N-1\). The failure probability vanishes in joint probe/conditioning limits exactly when \(N/\kappa^2\to\infty\), and the phenomenon persists for irreducible positive-definite tridiagonal matrices.
