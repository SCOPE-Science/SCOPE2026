# Verification scope and limits

The mathematical proof is self-contained after the cited 2026 published nonvanishing theorem and its explicit kernel representation. The essential checks are: (1) the kernel-amplitude derivative bound from joint exponential type; (2) the coefficient identity for the Borel transform of an exponential times an exponential-type entire function; (3) nonremovable singularity inside each possible Borel disk for any nonzero fiber; (4) gluing/identity-theorem continuation under a hypothetical linear dependence; and (5) existence of arbitrarily many separated parameters whose fibers are nonzero.

The exact-arithmetic script `verification.py` was executed on the actual packaged content. It tests a polynomial two-factor example and 240 coefficient identities (plus three assertions about a nonsingular jet matrix), reporting `FINITE_FOCK_BOREL_JET_CHECKS_OK 243`. This finite calculation checks algebra and conventions, not the universal infinite-rank claim.

The full cited initiating manuscript was read for Theorem 1.2, Lemma 2.1, Proposition 3.1, and equations (4.2)–(4.3). An independent mathematical audit, external reproduction, and publication-priority certification have not been performed. The general theorem does not assert boundedness, noncompactness, or higher-dimensional extension.
