For periodically refreshed Mittag-Leffler jump rates, a finite absorbing-reflecting lattice rounds the half-line first-passage transition at \(\alpha=1/2\) into an exact logarithmic crossover: the mean time stays bounded below the threshold, grows as \(\log N\) at it, and as \(N^{2\alpha-1}\) above it. A finite spectral renewal formula proves the sharp amplitudes and the universal \(\alpha=1/2+u/\log N\) scaling profile, with a physical-time correction uniformly bounded in the chain length.
