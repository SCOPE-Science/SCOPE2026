For square column index \(b=c^2\ge4\), all square Narayana entries are parametrized by proper divisor pairs of one explicit square, giving the exact count \((\tau(h^2)-3)/2\) of nontrivial squares and a sharp maximal row. In particular, \(b=4\) and \(b=9\) have none, correcting the published assertion that every fixed Narayana column contains infinitely many nontrivial squares; infinitude holds precisely for nonsquare \(b\).
