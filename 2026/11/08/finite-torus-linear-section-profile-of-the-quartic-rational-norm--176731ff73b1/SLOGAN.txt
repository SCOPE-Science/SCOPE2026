The complete rational-torus linear-section profile of the quartic scroll \(S(1,3)\) is \((3t,2t,t+1,t,1,0)\) for every prime power \(q\ge5\), where \(t=q-1\). Equivalently its six-dimensional toric code has generalized Hamming weights \((t^2-3t,t^2-2t,t^2-t-1,t^2-t,t^2-1,t^2)\), with the third optimum realized by one ruling fiber and one isolated off-fiber torus point.
