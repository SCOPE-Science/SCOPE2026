Nonempty finite permutation digraphs have exactly three counting-free one-variable theories and exactly 26 two-variable theories, distinguished by small-cycle multiplicities and whether the long-cycle part is a single triangle. From three variables onward, unrestricted-depth \(\mathrm{FO}^k\) equivalence is exactly equality of every cycle-length multiplicity truncated at \(k\), giving canonical minimum representatives and a restricted-partition Euler product.
