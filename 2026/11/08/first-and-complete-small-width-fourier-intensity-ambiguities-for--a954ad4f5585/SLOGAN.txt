Among full-support binary two-row arrays, complete continuous Fourier magnitude first fails to identify the array (up to sign and central reversal) at width \(6\), with exactly four ambiguous double fibers; surprisingly width \(7\) is unambiguous, while width \(8\) has twelve double fibers. The classification is exhaustive for widths \(1\) through \(8\) and does not claim a new mechanism for the classical reciprocal-polynomial factor flip.
