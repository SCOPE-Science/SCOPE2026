For every dyadic factor length \(2^k\) with \(k\ge2\), the first Rudin–Shapiro factor attaining the global maximal signed sum starts at \((5\cdot2^k+1)/3\) for even \(k\) and \((10\cdot2^k+1)/3\) for odd \(k\); the corresponding first minima start at \((35\cdot2^k+1)/3\) and \((16\cdot2^k+1)/3\). These are exact first-occurrence locations, beyond the previously known extrema of the factor-sum spectrum.
