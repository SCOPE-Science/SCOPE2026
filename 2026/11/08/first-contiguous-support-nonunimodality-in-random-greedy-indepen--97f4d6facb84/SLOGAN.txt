The smallest tree whose random-order greedy independent-set size has a genuinely nonunimodal law with no gaps in its support is the six-vertex spider \(S(1,1,3)\): its three consecutive probabilities are \(19/72,16/72,37/72\). An exhaustive Prüfer-and-permutation certificate shows that it is the unique six-vertex shape with this property and that no smaller tree has it.
