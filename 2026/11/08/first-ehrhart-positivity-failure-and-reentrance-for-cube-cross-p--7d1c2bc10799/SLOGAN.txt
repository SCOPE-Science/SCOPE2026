The canonical lattice join of a unit \(p\)-cube and a \(q\)-cross-polytope is Ehrhart-positive in every dimension at most \(17\), and at dimension \(18\) its only two failures are \((p,q)=(14,3)\) and \((16,1)\). At fixed \(p=14\), positivity disappears for \(q=3,4\) but returns at \(q=5\), as proved by exact integer coefficient certificates.
