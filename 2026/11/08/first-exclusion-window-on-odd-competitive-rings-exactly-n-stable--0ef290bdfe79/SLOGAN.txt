On an odd ring of \(n\ge5\) mutually competing species, increasing nearest-neighbor competition through the exact Fourier threshold \(\alpha=(2\cos(\pi/n))^{-1}\) replaces one stable coexistence state with exactly \(n\) stable species-exclusion states throughout the sharp next interval. Every new state loses two species yet has greater total biomass than the now-unstable homogeneous reference state.
