## Checks carried out

The mathematical proof specifies exact Fourier, path-eigenvalue, potential and trigonometric identities. The standalone `verify.py` exhaustively traverses the nonempty supports at each of 18 rational test parameters on odd cycles of sizes 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15. Candidate path-matrix positive definiteness is tested by exact rational leading principal minors; positive equilibria and invasion signs are computed with rational elimination. Exactly 131,022 nonempty support candidates were inspected and the only stable patterns were the expected n rotated supports at every parameter.

## Verification limits

Finite supports and a finite collection of rational parameters do not establish the theorem for every odd n or continuous interaction strength. Those universal assertions depend on the symbolic proof in RESULT.md. The source publication date and the limitations of accessible comparison literature were checked separately. No independent external audit or validation has been conducted.
