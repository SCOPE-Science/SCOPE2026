Miska's cyclotomic binomial congruences are not monotone in the root-of-unity filter order: the least failing modulus is \(d=36\), with \(C_{15}(36)\) true but \(C_{16}(36)\) false. In fact, at this modulus the valid orders are exactly \(15\) and every integer at least \(17\), while all smaller moduli have upward-closed valid-order sets.
