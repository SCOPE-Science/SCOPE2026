For the noisy cubic pitchfork, the classical Gaussian crossover of mean first-passage times near an unstable threshold has an explicit and strictly positive finite-barrier correction \((2\beta^2+1)e^{-2\beta^2}/(2\sqrt{2\pi}\,r)\), uniform for bounded saddle-scaled offsets \(\beta\). This source-specific correction calibrates detection thresholds beyond the known leading error-function law.
