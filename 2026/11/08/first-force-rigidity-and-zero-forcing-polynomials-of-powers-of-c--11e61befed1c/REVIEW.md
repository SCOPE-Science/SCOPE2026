(2023) similarly address well-forced families. Crucially, Abiad et al. (2026) give the scalar minimum \(2k\) and a consecutive-set witness, so neither is new here. Inspection of these primary articles, related formulations for consecutive circulants, and relevant research-record searches did not disclose the exact all-set trigger, complete cycle-power polynomial, or minimum multiplicity trichotomy. Failure to find an equivalent statement does not prove priority, and unpublished, incompletely indexed or generically implicit descriptions remain risks.
