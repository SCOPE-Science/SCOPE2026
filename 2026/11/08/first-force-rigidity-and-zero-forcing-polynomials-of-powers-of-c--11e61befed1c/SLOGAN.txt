Powers of cycles have a rigid zero-forcing rule: one initially blue vertex with at most one white neighbor guarantees complete forcing. Every inclusion-minimal set has size \(2k\), their exact number is \(n\), \(kn\), or \((2k-1)n\) according to the three sharp cycle-length regimes, and the full polynomial is given by a finite cyclic-window transfer matrix. The scalar minimum was previously known.
