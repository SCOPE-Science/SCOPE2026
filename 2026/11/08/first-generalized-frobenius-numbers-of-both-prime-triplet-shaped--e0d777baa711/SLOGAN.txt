The first generalized Frobenius number, allowing at most one nonnegative coefficient-vector representation, is exactly \(12k^2+40k+23\) for \((6k+5,6k+7,6k+11)\) and \(12k^2+50k+56\) for \((6k+7,6k+11,6k+13)\), for every integer \(k\ge1\). These formulas answer the first nonclassical representation threshold for both standard prime-triplet-shaped families, whether or not the entries are prime.
