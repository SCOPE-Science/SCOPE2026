The first two tautological Chern classes of \(\operatorname{Gr}(3,\mathbb C^{3+\ell})\) integrally generate the top class for every \(1\leq\ell\leq12\), fail by exact index \(3\) for \(\ell=13,14,15\), and recover index \(1\) at \(\ell=16\). The obstruction is invisible to rational cohomology and is certified by two independent exact top-intersection computations.
