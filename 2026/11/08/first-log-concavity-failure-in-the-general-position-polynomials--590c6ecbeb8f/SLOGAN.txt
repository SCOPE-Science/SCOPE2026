The general-position polynomials of the triangular graphs \(T_n=J(n,2)\) are log-concave for every \(3\le n\le29\), but \(T_{30}\) is the first failure: its coefficient at degree \(29\) violates the log-concavity inequality. An exact labeled-component recurrence, a separate generating-function calculation, and an integer certificate establish the finite cutoff; no statement is made for all larger orders.
