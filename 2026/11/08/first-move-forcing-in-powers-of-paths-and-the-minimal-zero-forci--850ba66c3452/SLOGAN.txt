On every power of a path, a proper blue set zero forces all vertices exactly when an ordinary force is already possible at time zero. For the square \(P_n^2\), all inclusion-minimal sets are explicit local patterns: for \(n\ge8\) their cardinality polynomial is \(4z^2+2z^3+3(n-7)z^4\), with exactly \(3n-15\) labelled solutions.
