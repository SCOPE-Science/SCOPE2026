For full-support binary \(n\times n\) masks with \(n\le4\), complete continuous-torus Fourier magnitudes determine the mask up to global sign and central reversal except for one double fiber at \(n=3\) and twelve at \(n=4\). All ambiguity at side three is separable; at side four exactly eight double fibers consist of genuinely nonseparable masks, giving the first such binary-square obstruction.
