For two degree-two matrix groups over length-two rings with residue field \(\mathbb F_5\), exactly \(300000\) ordered involution pairs over \(\mathbb Z/25\mathbb Z\) have product of order \(25\) or \(50\), but none do over \(\mathbb F_5[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\). Each group nevertheless has exactly \(752\) involutive elements and \(4504\) commuting ordered involution pairs; the full exact product-order census is certified by exhaustive matrix arithmetic.
