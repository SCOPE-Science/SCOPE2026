For unequal permutations \(P,Q\) of a \(q\)-letter alphabet, the first scattered-subsequence disagreement between \(P^r\) and \(Q^r\) occurs exactly at length \(r+1\), and its entire size spectrum is a binomial transform of the independence polynomial of their inversion graph. The spectrum recovers every common-order increasing-subsequence count, with sharp minima at adjacent swaps and maxima at complete reversal.
