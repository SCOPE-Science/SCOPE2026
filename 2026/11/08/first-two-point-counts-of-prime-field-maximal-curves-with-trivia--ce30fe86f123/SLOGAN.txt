For the odd-characteristic maximal curves with trivial geometric automorphism group constructed by Bartoli–Borges–Timpanella–Zini, the first two point counts are exactly \(p+1+p\delta\epsilon\) and \(p^2+1+p(p-1)\delta/2\), where \(\delta=[p\mid 2n+1]\) and \(\epsilon=[p\equiv3\pmod4]\). Thus their first two Hasse–Weil coefficients exhibit a sharp arithmetic jump even though maximality is known over the much larger field \(\mathbb F_{p^{4n+2}}\).
