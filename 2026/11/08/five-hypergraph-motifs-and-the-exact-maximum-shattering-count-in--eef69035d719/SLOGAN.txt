Among the vertices of the rank-three Johnson graph \(J(n,3)\), exactly five three-uniform hypergraph motifs give four-point VC-shattered supports for open neighborhoods. Their count is zero through \(n=5\), is \(720\) at \(n=6\), and equals \((n)_5(6n^2-17n-41)/48\) from \(n=7\) onward, so a random four-support is shattered with probability asymptotic to \(3888/n^5\).
