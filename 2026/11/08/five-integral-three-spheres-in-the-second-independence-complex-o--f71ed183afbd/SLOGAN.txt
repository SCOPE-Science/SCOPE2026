The second independence complex of the fifteen-vertex Kneser graph \(\mathrm{KG}(6,2)\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to exactly five three-spheres: \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(\mathrm{KG}(6,2))\simeq\bigvee^5 S^3\). An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching proves this first triangle-bearing two-subset Kneser case.
