For every complete multipartite graph with at least three nonempty parts and at least five vertices, its complex of vertex subsets containing no simple five-vertex path is an integral homotopy wedge of three-spheres. The exact number of spheres is \(\binom{N-1}{4}-\sum_i[\binom{n_i-1}{4}+(N-n_i)\binom{n_i-1}{3}]\), settling the five-path case of a recent multipartite path-free wedge conjecture.
