For ordered pairs of matrices satisfying \(P^2=P\), \(N^2=0\), and \(PN=0\), every rank stratum has an exact Gaussian-binomial count and is smooth and geometrically irreducible. The reduced pair variety has dimension \(\lfloor4n^2/5\rfloor\), while its number of top-dimensional irreducible components follows a nonconstant five-periodic pattern (with an exception at \(n=1\)).
