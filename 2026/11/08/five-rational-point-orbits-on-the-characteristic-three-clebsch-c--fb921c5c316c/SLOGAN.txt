The \(145\) rational points of the characteristic-three Clebsch cubic over \(\mathbb F_9\) form exactly five \(S_5\)-orbits of sizes \(10,15,30,30,60\), with point stabilizers \(S_3\times C_2,D_8,C_4,C_2^2,C_2\). Every rational point has a nontrivial involution symmetry; the two size-\(30\) orbits are distinguished by stabilizer type and by their incidences with the \(27\) lines.
