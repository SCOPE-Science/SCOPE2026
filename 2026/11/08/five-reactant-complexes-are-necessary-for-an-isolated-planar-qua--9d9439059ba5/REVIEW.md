# Scientific review: five-reactant-complex lower bound

**Correctness (C): PASS.** For a degree-at-most-two vector field, a full zero Jacobian at a positive equilibrium leaves three vector-valued quadratic Taylor coefficients. The six monomial coefficient rows are pairwise nonproportional; two missing monomials make the entire field vector-proportional to one homogeneous scalar quadratic. An isolated root then requires a definite quadratic, whose translation back to the positive equilibrium unavoidably has at least five nonzero monomial coefficients. Each reaction supplies exactly one reactant-monomial vector, proving the lower bound. The five-reaction witness is checked directly. The symbolic checker verifies the finite row-rank assertions and the witness; it does not substitute for the definiteness argument.

**Originality (O): PASS against inspected sources, with historical-priority risk.** The closest complete source, Yeung–Erban, arXiv:2610.06130v1 (Section 4), already gives a *five-reaction example*. Its published upper bound does not imply that five reactions are *necessary*, and it explicitly discusses minimum reaction counts as a question. Banaji–Boros–Hofbauer (2024), DOI 10.1007/s11071-024-10068-1, contains extensive small-network classifications and a *five-reaction threshold for an unfolded cusp*, but that refers to a simple-zero eigenvalue and parameter unfoldings, not an isolated equilibrium with identically zero Jacobian. Neither inspected complete text states or implies the new universal monomial-support obstruction. General older results in sparse quadratic vector-field theory could nonetheless imply it; historical priority has not been independently certified.

**Value (V): PASS.** Reaction number is a standard, explicitly motivated complexity measure for chemically realizable local dynamics. The result determines an exact global minimum, not an arbitrary numerical instance or routine recomputation. Its proof works across all phase-portrait classes and does not depend on enumerating or choosing chemical stoichiometry. The matching five-reaction construction closes the necessity–sufficiency question for *existence* of any isolated positive equilibrium with zero full Jacobian. Individual geometric classes, weak reversibility and product-molecularity restrictions remain open or require separate investigation.

**Closest literature and limitations.** The existence of a five-reaction witness is not claimed as novel. The statement does not cover generic cusp unfoldings, rank-one nilpotent Jacobians, nonisolated zero sets, nonpositive equilibria, or reaction order three and higher. The two cited full texts were inspected in the relevant sections. Independent novelty review has not been performed.
