# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The secure-set lemma treats both cases of the center's membership and supplies defending swaps; an omitted peripheral pair forces a center swap, giving necessity. The quotient formalization is exact because each peripheral pair contributes one edge, possibly a loop. The center singleton is not itself secure for \(m\ge2\), so it must also have a coalition partner; the test imposes this condition without an invalid universal-singleton exemption. The proof's upper-bound dichotomy covers both disjoint and pairwise-intersecting quotient-edge supports; the counting identity bounds non-center classes by four. The five-class construction works for every integer \(m\ge2\). Direct secure-domination and partition enumeration independently corroborate the small instances.

## Originality
PASS, with priority risk. The initiating full-text manuscript, Shetty–Udupa–Rakshith (arXiv:2511.21170, first public 2025-11-26), introduces the invariant, proves general upper and lower bounds, and treats special extremal values and trees. It does not establish this constant-five infinite-family result or the block-incidence multigraph argument. The published general bound is \(\operatorname{SEC}(F_m)\le m+3\), which does not imply the new sharp bound for \(m\ge3\). The 2020 ordinary-coalition paper has a different predicate. Searches of equivalent terminology, the graph family's aliases, indexed research records, and related general theorems identified no stronger covering claim. Negative search results do not certify priority.

## Mathematical value
PASS. A security-based graph partition invariant on an unbounded family of connected graphs is shown to have an unexpectedly constant sharp maximum, improving the existing growth-dependent general bound for the entire nontrivial regime. The quotient reduction supplies a new structural obstruction and a usable exact criterion for every proposed optimum, not only its cardinality. It is a natural family and a natural parameter, without an arbitrary numerical cutoff.

## Limitations
Only the triangular windmill family is covered. General windmills with larger blade cliques and a closed count of optimal partitions remain outside the claim. Independent mathematical examination and complete historical priority certification have not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
