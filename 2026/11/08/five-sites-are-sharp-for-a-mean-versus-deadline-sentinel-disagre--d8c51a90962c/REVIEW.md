# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The first-arrival decomposition follows from independent exponential edge passages along a path. The closed-form first-passage survival probabilities cover all six reflection classes of two sentinels on five vertices. Strict dominance eliminates three classes, and the remaining two analytic sign changes are uniquely determined by their exponential equations. All equality boundaries and all smaller path sizes are handled. An independently implemented finite-state first-event calculation reproduced 80 exact Laplace-transform values and every rational mean class. The finite checks corroborate, but do not replace, the all-time analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected material

Leskovec et al. (2007) supply the general outbreak-detection optimization framework, and Heavey et al. (2022) concern approximation for mean detection delay. The four-site path deadline switch follows from the smaller-path formulas, and the new result explicitly does not claim novelty for that simpler behavior. The strictly later emergence of a mean-versus-deadline conflict at five sites and a third deadline phase are not entailed by that earlier result or the inspected literature. The potentially pertinent Zhou et al. (2015) mobile-network paper was accessible as an abstract only; its full text remains a priority risk. Unindexed mathematically equivalent Erlang placement analyses remain possible.

## Value — PASS

The exact smallest conflict and its complete time-threshold phase diagram show why minimizing expected first detection can be a different surveillance design goal from minimizing the chance of missing a specific deadline. The example is a small rigorous benchmark, not a claim of a broad application guarantee.

## Scientific limitations

Only rate-one homogeneous irreversible SI infection on finite paths, uniform initial source, two perfect sentinels and unweighted edges are covered. No recovery, heterogeneous sources, noisy detection or other graph topologies are considered. Independent validation and full historical-priority certification have not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
