For a uniformly seeded continuous-time SI epidemic on a path with two perfect sentinels, five vertices are the sharp minimum at which a deadline-optimal placement is strictly suboptimal for mean detection delay. On the five-site path, the optimal sentinel layout has three exact deadline regimes, switching at the unique positive roots of \(e^t=1+2t\) and \(e^t=1+2t+2t^2\); the final endpoint layout has mean delay \(3/5\) rather than the best \(1/2\).
