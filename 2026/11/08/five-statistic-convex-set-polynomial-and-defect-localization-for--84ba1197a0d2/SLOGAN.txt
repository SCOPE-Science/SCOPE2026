The complete five-variable convex-set polynomial of the dimension-\(n\) standard-example poset \(S_n\) has an explicit binomial formula for every \(n\ge3\). Although \(S_n\) is obtained by deleting \(n\) cross-relations from a complete height-two poset, the five statistics of a labelled convex subset remain unchanged for exactly \((2^n-n)^2\) of the \(4^n\) subsets.
