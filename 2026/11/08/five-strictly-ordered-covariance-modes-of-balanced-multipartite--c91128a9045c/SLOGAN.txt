For the spanning-tree polytope of every balanced complete \(k\)-partite graph with \(k\ge3\) parts of size \(n\ge2\), uniform vertex sampling has an explicitly diagonalized covariance with four positive eigenspace types for \(k=3\) and five for \(k\ge4\), all strictly ordered. Its intrinsic covariance condition number is exactly \(2\) for three parts and \(2+2/[k(kn-2)]\) for four or more parts, remaining uniformly bounded as the dimension grows.
