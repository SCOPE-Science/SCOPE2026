For a single arbitrary permutation of a five-bit window, the largest number of distinct binary outputs is \(16\lfloor n/3\rfloor-16,-10,\text{ or }-6\) according as \(n\bmod3\) is \(0,1,\text{ or }2\), for every \(n\ge5\). The numbers of maximizing words eventually repeat \(4,2,10\) with period three, proved for all lengths by an exact sixteen-state recurrence rather than finite extrapolation.
