For every cyclic prism \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge5\), all minimum standard zero-forcing sets form exactly five symmetry classes, totaling \(13n\), and their complete synchronous propagation-time distribution is an explicit three-term polynomial depending only on the parity of \(n\). The classification determines all fastest and slowest minimum starting sets without extending the claim to larger starting sets.
