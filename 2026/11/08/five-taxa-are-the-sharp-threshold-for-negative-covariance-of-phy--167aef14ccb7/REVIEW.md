# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The two-leaf affine identity and near-complete-sampling pendant/distance-sum covariance identity reduce the entire four-taxon statement to two rooted binary shapes. The balanced topology gives positive covariance directly and the caterpillar's ordered distance sums give nonnegative covariance through a pairwise-difference identity; contractions follow by continuity. The five-taxon phase law is a direct exact covariance calculation, independently reproduced by enumerating all four-of-five samples. All witness lengths are positive and all five outcomes, variances, covariance and correlation are rationally checked. The checker is supplemental and does not stand in for the all-trees argument.

## Originality

**PASS, qualified to inspected evidence.** Nipperess–Matsen's 2012 preprint develops the exact rarefaction framework for the **variance of PD**, not the covariance with MPD or the smallest possible negative sign. Mazel et al. (2015 online) already show that PD and MPD can disagree in ecological pattern inference; only the sharp finite-taxon covariance threshold and topology-specific sign formula are assessed as new. Tucker et al. review connections and divergences of phylogenetic metrics without furnishing these exact statements. Related indexed results on species discovery covariance and PD rarefaction variance do not logically imply the joint PD–MPD claim. Some specialized historical literature or supplementary material may contain equivalent results; the novelty finding is provisional and not an independence claim.

## Value

**PASS.** Faith diversity and mean pairwise distance are natural, widely used community phylogenetic descriptors. Their joint response under equal-richness random subsets matters when interpreting several metrics on the same survey. The exact minimal-taxonomic-complexity threshold and an open negative-covariance phase distinguish an intrinsic effect from an isolated numerical coincidence. The large-magnitude negative correlation provides a concrete, structurally motivated warning against assuming positive co-fluctuation. No larger-sample or universal methodological benefit is asserted.

## Limits and closest literature

The result is restricted to ultrametric rooted trees and equal-probability sampling without replacement; not a result on weighted community prevalence, causal ecological effects or all five-leaf topologies. Closest material is Nipperess–Matsen, arXiv:1208.6552; Mazel et al., doi:10.1111/ecog.01694; Tucker et al., doi:10.1111/brv.12252. Literature coverage is finite and historical priority remains provisional.
