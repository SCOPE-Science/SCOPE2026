# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For k=2 ultrametricity gives PD=H+MPD/2, implying nonnegative covariance. For k=n both constants. When k=n-1, dropping taxon i gives PD=PD(all)-pendant_i and MPD=(total pair distance - distance-sum_i)/C(n-1,2), so covariance is Cov(pendant_i,distance-sum_i)/C(n-1,2). For n=4, balanced two-cherry and caterpillar ultrametric shapes have similarly ordered pendant and distance-sum vectors; their covariance is >=0, and zero branch limits cover multifurcations. For five-tip ((0,1),(2,(3,4))) exact polynomial 2/75(3Hx-Hy-2Hz+3x²-5xy-4xz+3y²+3z²) is a correct sign test. Direct edge-by-edge enumeration of all five four-leaf samples with H=1,x=1/10,y=3/10,z=1/4 yields covariance -7/1000, squared correlation 45/49 with negative sign.

## originality

PASS

Nipperess–Matsen established rooted PD mean and variance under rarefaction; Mazel and Tucker establish PD/MPD comparison and qualitative discordance. Those statements do not determine the joint PD–MPD covariance sign at fixed richness, its sharp five-taxon boundary or exact polynomial phase region.
