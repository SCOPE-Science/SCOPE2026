For uniform fixed-size sampling from a rooted ultrametric phylogeny, rooted Faith diversity and mean pairwise distance always have nonnegative covariance with at most four taxa, but a strictly positive five-taxon tree has covariance \(-7/1000\) and correlation \(-3\sqrt5/7\). A quadratic expression gives the exact open region of negative covariance within a natural three-parameter five-taxon family.
