# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof classifies every one-point extension over a stationary triple using unique pair completions. For a nonblock stationary triple the three completion points are distinct and outside the triple, leaving exactly \(v-6\ge1\) generic points; for a block triple every fresh point is generic. This is a permanent four-pebble winning invariant, not a finite-depth claim. The displayed Pasch sentence reuses the first variable name under a nested quantifier and has exactly five available names. Pair uniqueness forces all six witness points to be distinct. The Fano and ternary affine constructions are checked algebraically and by standalone finite enumeration.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** Grohe–Otto provide the pebble-game methodology, not this Steiner-system completion catalogue. Barbina–Casanovas address model completion and model theory of the function-symbol Steiner quasigroup, not this finite relational width result. Ling–Colbourn–Grannell–Griggs and Grannell–Griggs–Whitehead prove anti-Pasch constructions, not bounded-variable definability. Heinlein–Östergård study algorithmic configuration generation and counting; three *generating points* are not three logical variable names. A direct search of mathematical records, relevant primary pages, and accessible prior findings found no statement implying the combination of universal four-variable equivalence and Pasch's exact five-variable threshold. The full text of one older anti-Pasch construction paper was not accessible, and a stronger unpublished or unlocated general theorem remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The Pasch configuration is a standard forbidden subconfiguration in triple-design theory. The result determines the smallest first-order variable resource capable of recognizing its presence; moreover, it settles the entire four-variable theory of every nontrivial finite Steiner triple system, not just one example or one quantifier rank. The Fano/affine-nine pair supplies a sharp structural obstruction with different size, characteristic, and Pasch status, yet no four-variable distinction.

**Limits.** This is neither a classification of complete five-variable theories nor a result for the functional quasigroup language. Exact historical priority and mathematical verification have not been independently audited.
