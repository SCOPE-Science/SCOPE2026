All nontrivial finite Steiner triple systems have exactly the same counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{4}\) theory in the pure ternary block language, irrespective of order or design. A Pasch configuration is expressible with five reusable variables but not four: the Fano system contains one while the affine system of order nine does not, and those systems are \(\mathrm{FO}^{4}\)-equivalent.
