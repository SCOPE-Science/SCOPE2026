The L-stable second-order Padé \([0/2]\) graph-heat step can have negative entries at arbitrarily small positive times whenever two vertices are distance four apart. Among all simple graphs with at most five vertices, the five-vertex path is the sole exception to unconditional entrywise positivity, and its exact recovery threshold is the positive root of \(5s^4+40s^3+42s^2-4=0\).
