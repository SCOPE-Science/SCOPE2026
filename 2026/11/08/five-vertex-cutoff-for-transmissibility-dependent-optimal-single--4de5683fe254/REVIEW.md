# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The exact endpoint selection, source normalization, and independently retained undirected-edge law are explicitly specified. The identity between mean outbreak size and squared component-size expectation is exact. For at most four original vertices, every residual graph is on at most three vertices and its isomorphism class is determined by its number of edges; a coupling proves the maximum-degree choice uniformly optimal. In the five-vertex witness, a triangle-plus-isolate and a four-vertex path give the exact difference \(p^2(1-4p)/2\). The other two possible vaccine targets leave proper supergraphs of the path and therefore cannot optimize when \(0<p<1\). A standard-library exact checker verifies 75 lower-order labelled graph cases and all five witness polynomials.

## Originality

PASS within inspected material, with residual historical-priority risk. Newman's bond-percolation model (arXiv:cond-mat/0205009), Kenah and Miller's 2011 intervention framework (DOI 10.1155/2011/543520), and Altarelli et al.'s 2013–14 immunization optimization work (arXiv:1309.2805) provide broader modelling/optimization context, not an identified statement of the five-vertex minimum or exact \(p=1/4\) change of optimal vaccine recipient. The Kenah–Robins 2006–07 corrective framework warns against extending undirected bond equivalence to arbitrary SIR laws. A tree-only vaccination-order result does not imply the cyclic-graph witness. Searches by formulation, source model, and exact switching parameters did not identify dominating published coverage; this is not a claim of exhaustive worldwide novelty.

## Value

PASS. The minimal graph size at which parameter-independent single-dose target selection fails is a natural structural benchmark for outbreak-control heuristics. The witness isolates a subtlety that raw degree alone cannot resolve: three equally high-degree potential targets are not equally good once transmission strength is specified. This supplies a complete, exact parameter threshold in the smallest possible connected instance. Its mathematical value is independent of empirical effectiveness and does not rely on a claim that the improvement is large.

## Closest literature and limitations

The cited works establish that epidemic percolation and vaccine-node optimization are well motivated but do not, in the inspected portions, resolve this exact small-network classification. The result does not cover multiple doses, nonuniform initial infections, arbitrary time-dependent SIR transmission, correlated/directed edges, or other outbreak objectives. Independent historical-priority checking has not been performed.
