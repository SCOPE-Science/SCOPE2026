# Scientific assessment

## Correctness (PASS)
The model, objectives, quantifiers, boundary cases and finite enumeration are explicit. On at most four vertices, a spanning-subgraph chain of the four three-vertex graph types establishes a universal stochastic minimizer. The two exceptional five-vertex graph pairs have exact probability and mean polynomial factorizations giving distinct argmin sets only for \(1/4<p<3/4\). A complete permutation-canonicalized enumeration of \(2^{10}\) five-vertex graphs produces \(21\) connected isomorphism classes; exact root isolation proves dominance for all \(p\in[0,1]\). Two structurally different component formulas agree at all \(105\) deletions. The verifier and exact output were replayed from this package, without simulation-based inference.

## Originality (PASS, with residual priority risk)
Kenah and Miller (2011) consider epidemic probability versus attack rate in broader heterogeneous populations; they do not derive this homogeneous fixed-graph finite classification. Keeling and Shattock (2012) optimize expected final case count, not the joint risk criteria. Holme (2017) performs an exact small-network census but compares mean-vaccination performance to influence maximization and sentinel surveillance, not the three-case tail for the same vaccination decision. A previous result establishes the mean-optimal switching law for the particular first witness graph and is explicitly acknowledged; it does not imply the new tail-optimal switching law or the two-class complete census. Semantic research searches and accessible earlier findings disclosed no stronger covering statement. Residual risk: unlocated graph-reliability or vaccination-tail literature may contain a logically equivalent classification.

## Value (PASS)
The distinction between mean cases and the probability of an outbreak exceeding a specified harm threshold is a motivated choice in stochastic epidemic control, not an arbitrary graph invariant. The theorem gives the exact first order at which these policy objectives must conflict, the complete two-type minimal obstruction, and a clean interval where the difference is unavoidable. This supplies a finite, exactly reproducible benchmark for comparing risk-neutral and risk-sensitive node interventions, without asserting empirical clinical implications or an asymptotic theorem.

## Limits
The model is independent undirected bond percolation with a uniformly selected source after one perfect vaccination. The threshold is at least three infected individuals. Other disease processes, weights, importation laws, or thresholds are not covered. The status is self-review only.
