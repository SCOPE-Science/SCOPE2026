In characteristic seven, the genus-four hyperelliptic \(C_6\)-family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\) has a reduced Prym nonordinary parabola, with exactly five geometric coefficient parameters where the Prym has \(7\)-rank zero; elsewhere on that parabola its rank is one, and off it the Prym is ordinary. The full Jacobian \(7\)-rank and Cartier \(a\)-number stratification is explicit, and the Hasse divisor has multiplicity two along the reduced rank-two Cartier locus.
