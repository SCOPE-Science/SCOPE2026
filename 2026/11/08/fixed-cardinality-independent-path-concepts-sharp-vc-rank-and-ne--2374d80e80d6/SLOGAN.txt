For independent \(k\)-vertex subsets of a path on \(n\) vertices, coordinate membership has the exact VC dimension \(\min\{k,n-2k+1\}\). With fixed packing defect \(r=n-2k+1\geq1\), its Littlestone dimension instead grows as \(r\log_2 k+O_r(1)\), and for \(n=2k\) it equals \(\lfloor\log_2(k+1)\rfloor\) exactly.
