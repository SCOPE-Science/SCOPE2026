For the raw Twin-Step rule, any fixed positive common damping can increase both quadratic objective values without bound in one step while the two descent paths move strictly closer together—even for a fixed, mildly anisotropic Hessian. On a condition-two family, objective safety has the sharp limiting damping threshold \(\eta/\varepsilon=(20+2\sqrt{19})/9\), equivalent to a square-root angular-gap scale.
