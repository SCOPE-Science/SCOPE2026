For a self-propelled Brownian swimmer whose heading alone is renewed at fixed mean interval \(\mu\), deterministic orientational resets uniquely minimize the transverse long-time diffusivity, and the sharp attainable range has upper endpoint \(D+v^2/(2d)\) that is never reached. The very same mean-reset budget permits arbitrarily large longitudinal diffusivity while transverse diffusivity approaches its minimum, reversing the deterministic anisotropy.
