For weakly increasing maps of finite chains that stabilize at the second iterate but not the first, an exact bivariate rational generating function counts maps by degree and fixed points. The number of fixed points is asymptotically Gaussian, with linear mean \(0.3014166091\ldots n\) and variance \(0.1935077065\ldots n\), even though these maps form an exponentially vanishing fraction of all order-preserving transformations.
