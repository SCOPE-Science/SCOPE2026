For the eight-dimensional class-five right Leibniz algebra introduced in arXiv:2610.40348, the exact number of fixed-point-free automorphisms over \(\mathbb F_q\) (characteristic greater than three) is \(q^5\bigl((q-1)^2-(6+g)(q-1)+4+6g+2\varepsilon\bigr)\), where \(g=\gcd(q-1,3)\) and \(\varepsilon=1\) exactly when \(4\mid(q-1)\). There are none over \(\mathbb F_5\), but some over every admissible larger field; a corrected automorphism normal form and an eight-weight torus count give the proof.
