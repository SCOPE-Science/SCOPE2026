Among binary affine partial bijections on \(\mathbb F_2^d\) that are their own partial inverses, the joint domain-size and fixed-point spectrum has an exact Gaussian-binomial formula, and the proportion with no fixed points tends to \(1/2\). At each full affine-domain dimension the probability instead has distinct even/odd limits \(1/2\mp0.060562104\ldots\); a limiting affine-domain codimension distribution balances them exactly.
