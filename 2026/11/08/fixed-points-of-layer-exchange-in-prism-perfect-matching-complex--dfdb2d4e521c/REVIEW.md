# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The fixed-point space is a tile complex because each invariant simplex consists of rung singletons and whole horizontal edge-pairs. Every tile set extends to a prism perfect matching by inserting unused rungs. A monomer-toggle matching based on a fixed minimum free vertex is globally acyclic: dimers never increase on arrows, and within a constant dimer fiber a selected monomer can be added but cannot be removed. The only unmatched nonempty faces are one rung vertex and one dimer-only face per base-graph perfect matching. The resulting CW model immediately proves the stated integral wedge type, including odd order and the dimension-zero case. Both constructions and global acyclicity were checked exhaustively in the bundled finite tests.

## Originality

PASS on accessible evidence, with material uncertainty. Bayer–Jelić Milutinović–Vega (2023) treat the original perfect-matching complexes of honeycomb graphs. Agarwal–Basak (2026) treat ordinary homotopy types for Möbius and circular ladders. Neither accessible statement implies the arbitrary-base-graph fixed-point theorem. Semantic searches for monomer-dimer complexes, equivalent tile-conflict independence complexes, graph-prism involutions, and stronger coverage returned no covering claim, but non-detection does not certify novelty. The expanded 2026 manuscript could not be inspected in full. There is a residual risk of an equivalent result within that paper or another source.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The invariant is natural: the involution is the canonical layer exchange on a Cartesian prism, and perfect-matching complexes are established topological graph invariants. The theorem computes an entire integral fixed-point homotopy type for arbitrary finite bases, revealing an exact and potentially large Betti multiplicity equal to the perfect-matching count. It yields nontrivial spherical fixed spaces even when the full complex has more complicated nonequivariant topology, and gives a direct equivariant Lefschetz formula. These are structural consequences beyond finite tabulation or a mere Euler-characteristic calculation.

## Scope and limitations

The result does not classify the entire prism perfect-matching complex or other automorphisms. The principal 2026 expanded paper was not available for full-text comparison. Finite verification complements rather than replaces the uniform proof. Independent audit remains outstanding.
