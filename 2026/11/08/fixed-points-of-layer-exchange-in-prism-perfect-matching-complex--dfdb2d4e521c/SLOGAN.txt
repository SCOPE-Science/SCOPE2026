For every finite graph \(H\), the pointwise fixed set of layer interchange on the perfect-matching complex of \(H\square K_2\) is a wedge of exactly \(p(H)\) spheres of dimension \(|V(H)|/2-1\) when the vertex count is even, and is contractible when it is odd; here \(p(H)\) counts perfect matchings of \(H\). In particular, the fixed-set integral Betti rank recovers the perfect-matching count, including two spheres for every even circular ladder.
