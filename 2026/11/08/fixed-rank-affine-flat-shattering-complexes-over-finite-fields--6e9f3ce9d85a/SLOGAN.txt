For the incidence concepts of all affine \(k\)-flats in \(\mathbb F_q^d\), where \(0\le k<d\), the VC-shattered point sets are exactly the affinely independent supports of size at most \(k+1\). This identifies the entire shattering complex with a truncated affine-geometry matroid, gives its exact coefficientwise polynomial, and yields \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\operatorname{Ldim}=k+1\) with a one-unit online-rank drop for each new affinely independent positive point.
