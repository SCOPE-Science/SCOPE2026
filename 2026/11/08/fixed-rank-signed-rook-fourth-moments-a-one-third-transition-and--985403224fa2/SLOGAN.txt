For uniform fixed-rank signed rook matrices, the sharp real \(L^4/L^2\) ratio undergoes an exact rank-sparsity transition at \(n=3k-2\): flat coefficient magnitudes minimize on one side, complete row/column concentration on the other, and an additive row-column family at the critical ranks. The full sharp range, every extremizer, and the exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur distortion are determined in every dimension.
