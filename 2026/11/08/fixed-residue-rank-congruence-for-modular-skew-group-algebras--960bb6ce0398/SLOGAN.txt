A fixed residue point forces every finite projective module over a modular \(p\)-group skew algebra of a connected commutative ring to have underlying rank divisible by \(|G|\). Consequently \(k[D_\infty]\) has only \(0\) and \(1\) as idempotents in characteristic \(2\); removing the fixed-point condition admits explicit rank-one idempotents from Artin–Schreier translations.
