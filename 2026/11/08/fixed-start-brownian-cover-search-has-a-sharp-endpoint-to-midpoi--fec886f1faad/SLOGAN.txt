For a reflecting Brownian searcher initially at the midpoint of an interval, optimizing the site of subsequent Poisson resets produces an exact, discontinuous switch: either endpoint is optimal below a unique reset-rate threshold, while the midpoint alone is optimal above it. The threshold is \(r_\star=4D\operatorname{arcosh}^2(c_\star)/L^2\), where \(4c_\star^3-6c_\star-3=0\) and \(c_\star>1\); no asymmetric interior site is ever optimal.
