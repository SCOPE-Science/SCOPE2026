The family of exactly \(k\) independent vertices of a cycle of length \(2k+r\) has VC dimension \(\min\{k,r+1\}\), while its Littlestone dimension is \(r\log_2 k+O_r(1)\) for every fixed positive defect \(r\). Closing the corresponding path preserves VC dimension but removes one full \(\log_2 k\) term from the online-learning complexity.
