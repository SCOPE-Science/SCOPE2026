# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof provides explicit definitions and a constructive witness for every assignment to any independent support of the conflict graph. Comparable vertices and a leaf in opposition to a node in the adjacent branch are the only forbidden pairs. Replacing leaf queries by parents yields the internal-tree width formula. Balanced threshold queries and concept counting establish the exact comb Littlestone rank; the complete-regular-tree recursion follows from the pair characterization. The standalone finite verifier independently checks direct traces, including all rooted-tree shapes through eight vertices and exact small comb ranks. It corroborates, rather than proves, the general theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with bounded historical priority
The closest inspected primary work, Misanantenaina–Wagner (2018, arXiv:1803.09623), counts maximal antichain concepts and cutsets. Its Theorem 2.8 is a relevant starting decomposition, not an enumeration of shattered membership-query supports. Darnstädt–Ries–Simon (ALT 2015, TCS 2018) uses the same frontier objects for minimum-disagreement data structures; available article passages do not provide a directly dominating statement. Prior grid-maximal-antichain and ancestor-ideal learning claims concern different set systems. Exact-source, alias and broader-implication checks yielded no covering theorem, but omitted historical terminology and incomplete publisher access remain risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Pairwise obstructions across arbitrary rooted trees give a complete natural classification and an exact VC parameter. The same theorem produces all coefficients for complete regular trees and exposes a fixed-VC, logarithmically diverging adaptive-learning complexity on unbalanced comb trees. These results directly distinguish expressiveness of hierarchical frontiers from adaptive identification of which frontier is present.

## Scope and limitations
The claim does not give the exact Littlestone dimension of every rooted tree and is not asserted for arbitrary posets. The one-vertex case is stated separately. Computational checks are finite. Full historical-priority clearance and an independent scientific audit remain outstanding.
