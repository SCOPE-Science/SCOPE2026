For maximal-antichain membership concepts on every nontrivial rooted tree, all shattering obstructions are pairs: the shattering complex is explicitly a flag independence complex, and the VC dimension counts terminal internal vertices. On binary comb trees, this VC dimension stays at one while the Littlestone dimension is exactly \(\lfloor\log_2(m+1)\rfloor\), exhibiting an unbounded adaptive-learning gap.
