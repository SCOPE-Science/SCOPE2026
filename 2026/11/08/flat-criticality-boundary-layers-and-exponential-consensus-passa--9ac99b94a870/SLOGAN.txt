With one zealot of each opinion, a finite complete-graph collective-decision model has an exactly uniform stationary opinion-count law at \(\alpha=1\), geometric boundary layers for \(\alpha<1\), and Gaussian central fluctuations for \(\alpha>1\). The expected time to reach an all-A susceptible state from all B is exactly \(m(m+1)H_m\) at criticality but grows exponentially with an explicit barrier for every fixed \(\alpha\ne1\).
