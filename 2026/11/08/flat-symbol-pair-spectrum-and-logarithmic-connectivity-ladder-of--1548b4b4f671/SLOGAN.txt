When first-order binary Reed–Muller evaluations are read in ordinary binary numerical order, their cyclic symbol-pair spectrum is unexpectedly flat: exactly four codewords occur at every weight strictly between \(2^{m-1}\) and \(2^m\). The span of the low-pair-weight words grows in logarithmic jumps, and the corresponding distance graph becomes connected at the sharp threshold \(3\cdot 2^{m-2}\).
