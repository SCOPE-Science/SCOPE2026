For a complete multipartite graph with at least three nonempty parts, adding one available colour beyond its chromatic number forces a rigid choice: apart from already-total monochromatic colourings, exactly one part must be fully assigned two colours, and all but at most one other part must be seeded. This gives the complete forcing-domain polynomial and, whenever a nonsingleton part exists, the exact minimum seed size \(s+r-2\) and its labelled multiplicity, with \(s\) the smallest nonsingleton part size and \(r\) the number of parts.
