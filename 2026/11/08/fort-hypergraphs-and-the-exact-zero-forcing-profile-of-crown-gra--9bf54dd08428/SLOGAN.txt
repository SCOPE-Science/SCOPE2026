The minimal forts of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M_n\) are exactly the within-part triples and matching-aligned four-sets, giving a complete zero-forcing polynomial and showing that all minimal zero-forcing sets have size \(2n-4\). Its fort number is \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\), its fractional zero-forcing number is \(2n/3\), and its ordinary-to-fractional forcing ratio tends to \(3\).
