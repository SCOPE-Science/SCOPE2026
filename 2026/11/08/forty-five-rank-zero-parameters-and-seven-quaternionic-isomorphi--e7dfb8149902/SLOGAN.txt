In characteristic \(11\), the quaternionic genus-four pencil \(y^2=x(x^4-1)(x^4+a x^2+1)\) has constant Jacobian \(a\)-number \(2\), yet its geometric \(11\)-rank drops from \(2\) to \(0\) at exactly \(45\) smooth coefficient parameters. Those rank-zero parameters represent precisely seven geometric isomorphism classes with full automorphism group \(Q_8\), plus one enhanced-automorphism class; none is superspecial.
