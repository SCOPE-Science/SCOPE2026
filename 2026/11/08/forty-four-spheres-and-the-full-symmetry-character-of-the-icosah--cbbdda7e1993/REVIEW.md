# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Both disjoint-set and independent component-count tests classify all \(4096\) vertex masks, yielding \(983\) nonempty forest faces. The \(471\) matching pairs are disjoint. The verifier orients **all** \(3972\) face-poset cover relations and proves acyclicity by a complete topological sort, leaving one zero-cell and forty four-cells; Forman's theorem gives the integral wedge. Independent mod-two reductions corroborate the Betti ranks. Exhaustive constrained backtracking finds the complete 120-element automorphism group. Both signed chain traces and a separately coded orbit-subset Lefschetz formula agree on every group element, and the \(A_5\) character table verifies the irreducible decomposition.

## Originality
PASS on the inspected evidence, not as a claim of absolute publication priority. Taylan (arXiv:1501.06754, 2015) provides general matching-tree and devoid-complex techniques, but classifies different families; Carnero Bravo (arXiv:2307.13271, 2023) defines the same forest filtration and classifies paths, cycles, cactus graphs, double stars, joins and specific graph products, not the regular icosahedron. Direct examination of published icosahedral neighborhood-complex and three-cut-complex results confirms materially different face criteria and no theorem implying this forest classification. Exact-phrase and synonym searches yielded no matching complete induced-forest homotopy or automorphism-character calculation. Unindexed exact computations are a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This computes a natural invariant of a canonical five-regular Platonic triangulation that is not determined by its existing neighborhood or cut complexes. The exact integral homotopy result is stronger than an Euler characteristic or single-field Betti calculation. The full ten-class \(A_5\times C_2\) character and antipodal eigenspace decomposition add nontrivial symmetry information inaccessible from the forty-sphere homotopy type alone. The graph-specific scope is explicit, and no general-family assertion is made.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest direct source is Carnero Bravo's *The Forest Filtration of a Graph*, whose Section 4 theorems concern other graph families. Related icosahedral open-neighborhood and three-cut complexes have distinct simplex definitions and homology dimensions. The integral \(G\)-lattice and equivariant homotopy type are not calculated. Equivalence to an unindexed earlier result cannot be ruled out absolutely.
