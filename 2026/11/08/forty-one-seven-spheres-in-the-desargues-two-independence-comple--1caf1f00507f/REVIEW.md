# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The graph is explicitly defined as \(\operatorname{GP}(10,3)\), and a verified vertex permutation identifies it with the incidence graph between two- and three-subsets of a five-element set. The criterion of induced degree at most one is precisely the component-size-at-most-two condition. Two independent full face tests agree on all \(2^{20}\) masks, with \(62{,}048\) nonempty faces and the listed face vector. Two deterministic matchings, with vertex orders \(7j\bmod20\) and \(3j\bmod20\), each have \(31{,}003\) pairs, one critical zero-cell and forty-one critical seven-cells. Each of the two complete reoriented Hasse graphs has \(400{,}760\) edges and admits a full \(62{,}048\)-vertex topological ordering. These are exact finite checks rather than sampled numerical evidence. Forman's theorem gives the claimed integral wedge directly, since each seven-cell attaches to a point.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
The nearest early topological source is Taylan's 2015 full preprint on arbitrary simplicial-complex matching trees and \(P_3\)-devoid complexes, explicitly specializing its homotopy formulas to paths, cycles, and forests; no Desargues or general cubic-distance-transitive classification is present in the inspected relevant theorems. The full 2020 higher-independence text of Deshpande–Singh covers complete multipartite graphs, fully whiskered graphs, paths, cycles and perfect rooted trees. The later 2021 chordal treatment concerns trees/chordal families, not this nonchordal graph. Publicly indexed Desargues findings on ordinary independence and edge matching have different underlying complexes and neither imply the second independence result. published-finding corpus comparisons and targeted literature queries under the aliases \(\operatorname{GP}(10,3)\), \(H(5,2)\), three-vertex-path-free/devoid, higher independence, and dissociation complex did not identify an equivalent or stronger theorem. Searches are not proofs of priority; equivalent unindexed work remains a live risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The Desargues graph is the canonical twenty-vertex cubic symmetric graph represented both as \(\operatorname{GP}(10,3)\) and as a two-subset/three-subset incidence graph. The second independence complex is the first nontrivial enlargement of its ordinary independence complex and the same natural \(P_3\)-avoiding object appearing in the 2015 matching-tree framework. The complete topological classification supplies an exact, motivated test case beyond the established forest/cycle formulas, not merely a numerical count. The complex has maximal faces of cardinalities eight, nine, ten and twelve, yet collapses homotopically to forty-one equidimensional seven-spheres. The exact integral homotopy type and multiplicity are not implied by the published neighboring graph-complex calculations.
