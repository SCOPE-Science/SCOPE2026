The minimum-degree smooth filling curves of bidegree \((4,3)\) on \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) number exactly \(66\), forming four ruling-preserving projective classes of sizes \(6,18,36,6\). Their genus-six Jacobians have four distinct Weil polynomials and are therefore pairwise nonisogenous over \(\mathbb F_2\).
