Weakly imported finite SIS contagion can have three separated strict stationary infection-count peaks only when interaction groups of at least four people are permitted: groups of size at most three allow no more than two. Four-person contagion realizes exactly the peaks \(0,2,N\) with all transmission channels positive for **every** population size \(N\ge4\), and a polynomial-degree argument proves three is the maximum when groups have size at most four.
