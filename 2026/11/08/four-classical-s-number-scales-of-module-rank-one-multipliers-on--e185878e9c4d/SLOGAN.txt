Every left multiplier of a \(c_0\)-sum of compact-operator algebras has a one-term Hilbert-module elementary representation, yet its four classical Banach-space s-number sequences equal the finite-block singular values repeated by matrix row dimension, subject to a flat floor from every nonzero infinite-dimensional block. Classical compactness holds exactly when that floor vanishes; for \(a_j=j^{-\sigma}I_j\) on \(M_j\), the common s-numbers decay like \((3n)^{-\sigma/3}\).
