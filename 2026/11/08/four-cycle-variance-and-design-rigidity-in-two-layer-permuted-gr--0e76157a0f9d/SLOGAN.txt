For a binary \(r\)-regular directed graph, the variance of two-layer path-pair collisions under independent uniform interlayer permutations is an explicit positive multiple of the squared deviations of pairwise out-codegrees from \(r(r-1)/(d-1)\). This fluctuation vanishes precisely for incidence matrices of symmetric \(2\)-designs, and the whole graph dependence is determined by the number of bipartite four-cycles.
