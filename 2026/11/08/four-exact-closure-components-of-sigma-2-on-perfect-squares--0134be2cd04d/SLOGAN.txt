For \(F(n)=\sum_{d\mid n^2}d^{-2}\), the closure of its values has exactly four components: \([1,\pi^2/9]\), \([91/81,\pi^2/8]\), \([21/16,4\pi^2/27]\), and \([637/432,\pi^2/6]\). Their order is determined solely by whether \(2\) and \(3\) divide \(n\); the prime tail \(p\ge5\) fills each interval.
