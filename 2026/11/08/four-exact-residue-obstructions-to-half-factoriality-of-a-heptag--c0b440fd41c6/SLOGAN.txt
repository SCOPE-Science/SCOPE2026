For the heptagonal polygonal-number self-map \(E_{7,x}\), unequal irreducible-composition lengths occur exactly when its prime-residue multiplicities \((a,b,c)\) for residues \((3,7,9)\pmod{10}\) dominate one of \((2,2,2)\), \((2,4,1)\), \((4,2,1)\), or \((4,4,0)\). The least such ratio is \(75411=81\cdot931=21^2\cdot171\), with irreducible-composition lengths \(2\) and \(3\).
