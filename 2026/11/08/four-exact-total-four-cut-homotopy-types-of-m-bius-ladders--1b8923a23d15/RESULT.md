# Four exact total-four-cut homotopy types of Möbius ladders

## Finding
For each \(n\in\{6,7,8,9\}\), let \(M_{2n}\) be the Möbius ladder with vertex set \(\mathbb Z/(2n)\), edges \(\{i,i+1\}\) and \(\{i,i+n\}\) (indices modulo \(2n\)). The **total four-cut complex** \(X_n=\Delta^t_4(M_{2n})\) is the simplicial complex generated by the complements of the independent four-element vertex subsets. Then
\[
X_6\simeq\bigvee^{30}S^4,\qquad
X_7\simeq\bigvee^{64}S^6,\qquad
X_8\simeq\bigvee^{93}S^8,\qquad
X_9\simeq\bigvee^{130}S^{10}.
\]
These are **integral homotopy equivalences**, not assertions based only on field-valued Betti numbers. In particular all reduced integral homology groups vanish outside degree \(2n-8\), where the homology is free abelian of the indicated rank. Each \(X_n\) is pure of dimension \(2n-5\) and is not shellable.

## Assumptions and scope
The four values of \(n\) are an exhaustive finite domain, not samples claimed to establish an unrestricted formula. A vertex set \(\sigma\) is a face exactly when there exists an independent four-set \(I\subseteq V(M_{2n})\) with \(\sigma\cap I=\varnothing\). Every facet therefore has \(2n-4\) vertices. The width \(n=5\) is deliberately excluded: its independent four-sets coincide with those of the complete bipartite graph \(K_{5,5}\), so its cut complex is covered by established complete-multipartite results. The four displayed widths are the first four after that elementary coincidence.

## Proof
The following finite, exhaustive discrete-Morse argument establishes all four cases; its full implementation is supplied as `verify.py`.

**1. Exact face generation.** For each prescribed \(n\), enumerate every four-element subset of \(\mathbb Z/(2n)\) in lexicographic order and retain it exactly when none of its six vertex pairs is an edge. A four-set is independent if and only if it passes these six tests. For every retained independent set, enumerate *every* subset of its complement by the bitmask recurrence `s = (s - 1) & facet`, beginning at the complement mask \(f\). The union, together with the empty face, is exactly \(X_n\). Thus no candidate facet or face can be missed.

**2. Explicit matching.** Initially every nonempty face is unmatched. For each vertex \(v=0,1,\ldots,2n-1\) in order, examine the still-unmatched faces \(s\), ordered first by decreasing cardinality and then by increasing bitmask. Whenever \(v\notin s\) and both \(s\) and \(s\cup\{v\}\) are still unmatched, pair these two faces and remove both from the unmatched pool. Every pair is a distinct codimension-one incidence. The complete matching is deterministic.

**3. Certified acyclicity.** Form the directed Hasse graph on *every nonempty face*. Direct an incidence from a larger face to its immediate subface unless the incidence belongs to the matching, in which case reverse it. Enumerate each incidence by clearing each set bit. Run Kahn's integer topological sort. It removes **every** face in every case, so the complete directed graph has no cycle. This is a sufficient exact certificate for an acyclic discrete-Morse matching. The vertex and incidence counts are not estimates: every face and incidence is explicitly visited.

**4. Critical-cell enumeration.** The exact resulting critical cells are one vertex and, respectively, \(30\), \(64\), \(93\), or \(130\) cells in dimension \(2n-8\), with no others. The verified counts are:

| \(n\) | Independent four-sets | Nonempty faces | Hasse incidences | Matched pairs | Critical cells |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 6 | 48 | 2,545 | 12,876 | 1,257 | one 0-cell; 30 four-cells |
| 7 | 154 | 13,463 | 87,038 | 6,699 | one 0-cell; 64 six-cells |
| 8 | 384 | 60,578 | 467,728 | 30,242 | one 0-cell; 93 eight-cells |
| 9 | 810 | 254,369 | 2,255,346 | 127,119 | one 0-cell; 130 ten-cells |

By the acyclic-matching theorem of discrete Morse theory, the geometric realization is homotopy equivalent to a CW complex with precisely those cells. A CW complex consisting of a single zero-cell and cells all of one dimension \(d\ge2\) is a wedge of \(d\)-spheres: each attaching map is constant because its target is a point. This proves the four homotopy equivalences *integrally*, without an integral boundary-matrix assumption.

**5. Failure of shellability.** The independent four-sets define distinct, equal-size complement facets, so \(X_n\) is pure of dimension \(2n-5\). The established nonzero reduced homology is in dimension \(2n-8<2n-5\). A pure shellable complex has reduced homology only possibly in its top dimension, so none of the four complexes can be shellable.

## Verification
Run `python3 verify.py` using only the Python standard library. The program independently computes the reduced simplicial boundary ranks over \(\mathbb F_2\); these agree with the Morse-derived ranks for every homological degree. It also verifies the complete face vector, pairwise disjointness and completeness of matching pairs, absence of directed cycles on all Hasse incidences, critical-cell dimensions, and matching fingerprints. The exact recorded program output is `verification_output.txt`, ending in `ALL_VERIFY_OK`. The finite proof is exhaustive over the stated four widths; no extrapolation to larger widths is used.

## Relationship to prior work
The motivating work of Agarwal and Basak (first submitted August 31, 2026; later revised) classifies independence and perfect-matching complexes of Möbius and circular ladders and explicitly poses the investigation of their cut and total-cut complexes as a further direction. Its full nineteen-page version was examined, including its future-directions section. The present finding answers the total-four-cut question **only for four specified Möbius ladders**, not for the whole family.

The established total-three-cut Möbius-ladder classification concerns different facets and does not imply these four total-four-cut equivalences. Earlier general treatments of total cuts include complete multipartite graphs, cycle powers and several grids; they supply definitions and tools but do not state these four Möbius-ladder classifications. The complete-multipartite theory does cover the simpler \(M_{10}\) total-four-cut case and that case is not part of this finding.

## Limitations
No unrestricted formula for \(n\ge10\) is proved or inferred. The matching itself is not claimed equivariant, and no automorphism representation or homotopy decomposition compatible with graph symmetries is calculated. The program constitutes a reproducible finite certificate and was reviewed within this analysis; no independent mathematical audit has been performed. The exact results depend on the explicitly given graph and face conventions.
