The total four-cut complexes of the Möbius ladders on 12, 14, 16 and 18 vertices have exact integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^{30}S^4\), \(\bigvee^{64}S^6\), \(\bigvee^{93}S^8\) and \(\bigvee^{130}S^{10}\), respectively. Complete reproducible acyclic Morse matchings establish all four sphere wedges and show that these pure complexes are not shellable.
