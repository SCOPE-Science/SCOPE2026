Every subset of positions in a binary two-run power \((0^a1^b)^t\), for \(a,b\ge1\) and \(t\ge2\), is a string attractor exactly when it covers four specified factors; the criterion reduces to three factors if one run length is one. All optimal two-position attractors are classified and counted by the exact formulas \(2t(t-1)\), \((t-1)(bt+1)\), or \((t-1)(at+1)\), according to the run lengths.
