On clique–cycle joins, choosing the lowest-degree vertex among \(q\) uniformly sampled candidate pivots gives an exact sparse-Cholesky fill law with excess expected fill dropping from quadratic to linear, logarithmic, then bounded as \(q\) increases from one to four. For fixed \(q\ge2\), the limiting excess nevertheless has a sharp power-law tail with moment cutoff \((q-1)/2\); sampling more candidates than hubs makes the fill exactly minimal.
