For every odd \(m\ge3\), the uniform point measure on the explicit quadratic-tangent Kakeya set over \(\mathbb F_{3^m}^2\) has exactly four distinct nontrivial Fourier-magnitude levels, with fully determined multiplicities and no Fourier zeros. Its exact maximal nontrivial Fourier coefficient is \(\sqrt{(q+1)(q+1+\sqrt{3q})}/(q^2+2q-1)\) for \(q=3^m\), giving sharp asymptotic \(1/q\) decay for the uniform measure on this particular half-density set.
