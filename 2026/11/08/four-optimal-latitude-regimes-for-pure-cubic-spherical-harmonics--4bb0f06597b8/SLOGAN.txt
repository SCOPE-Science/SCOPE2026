The unique latitude maximizing the lower continuous-sampling stability of pure cubic spherical harmonics obeys four different eigenvalue-crossing laws in dimensions \(d=3\), \(d=4\), \(5\le d\le11\), and \(d\ge12\). In high dimensions its squared coordinate is asymptotic to \(4.195823345445944\ldots/d\), and the sharp squared lower frame bound tends to one.
