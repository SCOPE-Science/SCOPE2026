# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof establishes the equivalence between the four zero-product equations and \(\operatorname{im}A+\operatorname{im}B\subseteq\ker A\cap\ker B\), counts the two image subspaces with prescribed sum, independently counts the common kernel, and obtains the final factor by Möbius inversion over the subspace lattice. The injectivity/surjectivity construction proves sharp feasibility over arbitrary fields. All boundaries, including the zero pair and dimension zero source quotients, are included. The standalone enumerator independently checks every coefficient in five small prime-field configurations; the all-dimensional theorem is mathematical, not experimentally inferred.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority limits

Morrison (1980) concerns the very same algebra and its representation-scheme geometry, and Schröer (2002, arXiv:math/0211293) concerns the same matrix variety. Their known structural decomposition is acknowledged as prior. Huang (2021, arXiv:2110.15566) computes coarser annihilating-pair generating functions; those unrefined totals do not determine the simultaneous rank/radical/socle profile. Kinser–Lőrincz (2018, arXiv:1810.10997) treats general radical-square-zero representation-variety components, not the displayed labeled four-index finite-field coefficients. Ram (2015, arXiv:1509.08053) counts an iterative observable-kernel problem rather than two independent surjections with joint zero kernel. No inspected predecessor explicitly establishes or implies the full specified coefficient formula without an additional nontrivial flag and incidence enumeration. The residual risk is an unlocated Hall-type formula already specializing to the same four-index distribution; the assessment is not independent priority certification.

## Value — PASS

The four indices are natural representation-theoretic invariants: the two action-map ranks and the radical and socle dimensions for modules over a basic three-dimensional local algebra. The exact simultaneous distribution is stronger than either a total point count or a two-rank distribution: over \(\mathbb F_2\) in dimension three, the same individual ranks \((1,1)\) split into three genuinely different radical/socle profiles with multiplicities \(42,21,42\). The formula describes all feasible profiles for every dimension and field and yields explicit probabilities over every finite field, addressing the arithmetic refinement of a known representation variety rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limits

The result does not classify isomorphism types, indecomposables or component geometry. Finite experiments support but do not replace the proof. Priority against an unidentified general count remains a research risk.
