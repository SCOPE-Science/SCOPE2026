Ordered pairs of square-zero, mutually annihilating \(n\)-dimensional operators over \(\mathbb F_q\) have an exact closed four-parameter census by their individual ranks and the radical and socle dimensions of the corresponding \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x,y)^2\)-module. A Gaussian flag count combined with subspace Möbius inversion gives every labeled multiplicity, its sharp feasible support over arbitrary fields, and an independently checkable total.
