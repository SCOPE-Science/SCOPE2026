Every four-point translate family on an abelian group of order at least eight has Littlestone dimension one, two or three, decided exactly by the translation stabilizer and the existence of a nonzero shift with two-point overlap. For primes at least eleven, dimension two is equivalent to the seed being Sidon; exactly \(52\) of the \(715\) four-subsets of \(\mathbb Z/13\mathbb Z\) attain it.
