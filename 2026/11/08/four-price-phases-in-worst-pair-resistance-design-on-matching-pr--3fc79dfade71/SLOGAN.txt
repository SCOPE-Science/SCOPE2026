For matching-priced \(K_{m,m}\), minimizing the *largest* effective resistance under a conductance budget has four exact price regimes: same-part bottlenecks, a balanced bottleneck, an interior matching-dominated phase, and complete deletion of the expensive matching in a global optimal design. The interior phase has an asymptotically sharp width \(1/(2m^2)\), and the resistance criterion drops matching links earlier than spectral-gap optimization.
