The nonzero finite Boolean compatibility frames \(C_n=(\mathcal P([n])\setminus\{\varnothing\},R)\), with \(R(X,Y)\) meaning nonempty intersection, have exactly four first-order quantifier-rank-three theories: one for each \(n=1,2,3\), and one shared by every \(n\ge4\). The sharp stable representative is the fifteen-world frame \(C_4\), despite the distinct modal-hierarchy behavior of the same Boolean frame family.
