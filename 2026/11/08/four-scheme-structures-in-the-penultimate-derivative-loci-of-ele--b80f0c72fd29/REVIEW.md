# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is an equality of homogeneous ideals, not merely a support calculation. The quadratic generators follow by differentiating a multilinear symmetric form. Four-cycle combinations reduce the original inclusion-matrix span to an \(n\)-dimensional quotient, whose two row types have determinant \(-Lc\). Its four ranks exactly match the proposed four candidate ideals. The equal-pair quotient is proven reduced via a graded spanning argument and evaluation on the coordinate axes and diagonal, and the exceptional characteristic-two algebra is monic quadratic over a reduced one-dimensional Cohen–Macaulay ring, so is Cohen–Macaulay without embedded associated primes. Both the projective lengths and the explicit equations follow. Exact independent-formula modular span checks cover 112 finite parameter instances and support enumeration covers 20 small fields; these are finite corroboration, not the infinite proof. Residual correctness risk is the standard fallibility of an unaudited written proof, not an acknowledged missing lemma.

## Originality — PASS, provisional against historical priority

The motivating original paper (Ying Xie, arXiv:2610.00259v1) was read in HTML, including its derivative-locus definition, Theorems 1.1–1.4, proof Sections 3–5, and open questions in Section 7. Its exact statements give dimensions, extremal partition components, and generic coordinate local rings, but do not identify the global ideals, added diagonal component, or complete embedded-prime answer in this quadratic-depth slice. Section 7 expressly leaves smaller-dimensional and embedded components, primary decompositions, and global reducedness open. Orzel arXiv:2509.05009v3, Sections 1–2, handles order-two vanishing (the polynomial and first derivatives), not the complete ideal of order-\(d-2\) derivatives for \(d\ge4\). The nearest accessible prior research involving \(e_3\) concerns a characteristic-three cubic hypersurface's reduced singular support and has neither the same degree nor derivative order. published-finding corpus searches tested source, derivative-order, radical, diagonal, and characteristic-two nilpotent synonyms; the nearest returned studies of other singular schemes do not imply this claim. Publication and independent priority checks remain pending, and a mathematically equivalent older result may exist.

## Value — PASS

The family is a natural first unsolved global-scheme stratum of the new higher-derivative geometry: order \(d-2\) makes every equation quadratic while leaving \(n\) and \(d\) unbounded. The result answers the **complete scheme**, not just a dimension or example, resolving reducedness and absence of embedded components and uncovering a sharp four-way arithmetic phase transition. The nonreduced length-two case and the additional-diagonal case have different degrees despite potentially identical dimension, which cannot be recovered from the prior dimension formula; this provides concrete structural input for primary decomposition and degenerations. It does not claim the full higher-order classification or any consequence in algebraic complexity beyond this complete natural slice.
