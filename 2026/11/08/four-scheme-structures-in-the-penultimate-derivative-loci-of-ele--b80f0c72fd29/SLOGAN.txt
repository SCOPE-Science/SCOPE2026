For every \(n\ge d\ge4\) in characteristic \(p>0\), the ideal of all order-\(d-2\) derivatives of \(e_d\) has an explicit four-case quadratic presentation: reduced coordinate axes, those axes plus a diagonal, doubled coordinate axes, or reduced all-but-one-equal planes. The four possibilities have projective degrees \(n\), \(n+1\), \(2n\), and \(n\), respectively, and none has embedded components.
