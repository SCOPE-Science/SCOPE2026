Four species are the sharp minimum for two reversals of the Hill-diversity ordering as its order changes: no pair with at most three species can do so. The four-species abundance profiles \((1,9,12,18)/40\) and \((3,5,16,16)/40\) reverse rankings once between orders \(2\) and \(3\), and again between orders \(3\) and \(4\), with exactly these two crossings.
