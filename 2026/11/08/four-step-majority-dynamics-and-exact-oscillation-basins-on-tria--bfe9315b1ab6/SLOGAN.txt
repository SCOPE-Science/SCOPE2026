On the graph made of any number of triangles sharing a hub, synchronous binary neighbor-majority dynamics always reaches a fixed point or a two-cycle within four updates, and this is sharp from three triangles onward. Exact multinomial formulas count every first-entry/period class and both two-cycle phases and their basins; the primitive two-cycle count is asymptotic to \(5^r\) for \(r\) triangles.
