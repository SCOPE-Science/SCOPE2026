For the neutral birth–death Moran process, four vertices are the smallest connected unweighted graph size at which two initial mutant configurations of equal size and equal fixation probability can have different conditional fixation times. Exactly the path \(P_4\) and cycle \(C_4\) do so among the six four-vertex graph classes: an adjacent versus separated mutant pair changes conditional fixation time from \(20\) to \(15\) on the path while fixation probability remains \(1/2\).
