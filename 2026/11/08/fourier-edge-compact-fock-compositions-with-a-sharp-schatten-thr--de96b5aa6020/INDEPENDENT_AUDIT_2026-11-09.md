# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With beta=1-lambda^2, the weighted kernel profile is |g_a(x+iy)| exp(-beta y²); uniform strip Riemann–Lebesgue and Gaussian domination imply C0, permitting compact truncations. Du (2011) Theorem 2.3 (the full 2011 PDF text and its theorem page were inspected) gives S_p iff ∫|g_a|^p e^{-p beta y²} dA<∞ for any p>0, once boundedness is established. The endpoint Mellin asymptotic g_a(x+iy)=Gamma(1-a)e^{sign(x)iπ(1-a)/2}|x|^{a-1}+O_Y(|x|^-1), combined with a Gaussian-dominated global bound, yields integrability iff p(1-a)>1 including divergence at equality. For p=2 Plancherel with ∫e^{-2beta y²-2ty}dy=sqrt(pi/(2beta))e^{t²/(2beta)} gives the stated norm. Integrating the |x|^{2a-2} asymptotic over [-R,R] verifies both log coefficient at a=1/2 and power constant at a>1/2. These are analytic integrals, not finite sampling. The window is spatial, not singular-value partial sum.
