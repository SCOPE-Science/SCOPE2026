Compactness need not imply Hilbert--Schmidt membership even for weighted composition operators on the classical Gaussian Fock space. An explicit Fourier-edge family has the sharp Schatten threshold \(W_{\lambda,a}\in S_p\iff p>1/(1-a)\), with an exact Hilbert--Schmidt norm below the transition and logarithmic/power divergence of a natural spatial integral at and above it.
