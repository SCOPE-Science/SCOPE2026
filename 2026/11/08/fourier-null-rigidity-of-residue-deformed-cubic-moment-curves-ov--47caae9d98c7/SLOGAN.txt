Every prime-square cubic moment curve \(x\mapsto(x,x^2,x^3)\) retains exactly the same Fourier-zero set after arbitrary residue-dependent perturbations of all three coordinates by multiples of \(p\). The complete all-prime nullity formula has small-prime exceptions at \(p=2,3\), while its ambient convolution rank approaches one-half of the space dimension for growing primes.
