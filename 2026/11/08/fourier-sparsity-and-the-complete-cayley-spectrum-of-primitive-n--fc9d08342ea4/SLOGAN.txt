The Cayley operator generated by primitive square-zero \(2\times2\) matrices over every odd prime-power residue ring has a complete integral Fourier spectrum governed by the discriminant of a binary quadratic form. At depth two, exactly a fraction \(1-1/p\) of all eigenvalues vanish, and at depth \(k\) the surviving Fourier fraction is of order \(p^{-k}\) for each fixed odd prime \(p\).
