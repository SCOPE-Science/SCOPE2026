On additive matrix spaces over every prime field, convolution by the rank-one idempotents has an explicit Fourier multiplier determined exactly by its frequency matrix's rational eigenspace dimensions, with a zero multiplier precisely when those dimensions agree. A finite Gaussian-binomial inversion counts all zero modes, while the normalized full complex spectrum converges to an explicit independent-eigenspace law whose zero atom is \(Q_\infty(p)^p\sum_{k\ge0}p^{-pk^2}/Q_k(p)^{2p}\).
