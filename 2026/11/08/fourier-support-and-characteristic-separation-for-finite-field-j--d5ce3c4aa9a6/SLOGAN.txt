The additive Fourier support of the finite-field Jordan-product graph \(\{(X,Y,XY+YX)\}\) is controlled exactly by the image of one Sylvester operator, and a coupled rational-canonical Euler product gives every convolution singular-value multiplicity. At fixed matrix dimension its supported frequency density approaches one in odd characteristic but zero in characteristic two, with sharp condition numbers \(q^{n^2}\) and \(q^{n^2-n}\), respectively.
