The additive Fourier transform of the graph \(\{(X,Y,XY-YX)\}\) has a nonzero coefficient exactly when both matrix frequencies lie in the image of the same inner derivation. Its complete singular spectrum in every matrix dimension is encoded by a centralizer Euler product; for \(3\times3\) matrices it collapses to exactly three positive levels, with a sharp all-dimensional condition number \(q^{n^2-n}\).
