For commuting pairs of \(2\times2\) matrices over any finite commutative chain ring, the additive Fourier transform vanishes exactly outside the trace-zero frequencies whose associated \(2\times3\) matrix has all maximal minors zero one ring-length lower. The resulting convolution rank is \((q^2+q+1)q^{4k}-(q+1)q^{3k+1}\), independent of the characteristic and ring presentation.
