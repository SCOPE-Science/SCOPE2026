# Research assessment

## Correctness
**PASS.** The explicit classification of negative curves follows from nefness of the elliptic fiber, adjunction, the published extremal-fiber and order-three Mordell--Weil classifications, and the torsion height formula. The complete \(13\)-curve intersection matrix is then fixed. Exhaustive exact negative-definiteness tests of all \(8{,}192\) subsets, independent rational and integer algorithms, explicit minimal-obstruction checks, and a separate inclusion--exclusion sum agree coefficient by coefficient. Reproduction code and output are embedded.

## Originality
**PASS, with provisional priority.** Miranda--Persson's classification and the general support-counting theorem are prior facts. The published works inspected do not contain the complete \(14\)-obstruction classification or the exact \(4{,}712\)-chamber distribution for these two three-torsion fibrations. An earlier related computation for a \(II^*\) pair has a different graph, only one torsion section, three forbidden supports, and \(1{,}021\) chambers; it does not imply this result. The originality search cannot exclude an unindexed specialist article or unpublished enumeration.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The negative-curve support stratification is a natural birational invariant of classical extremal elliptic surfaces, and the classification identifies the complete minimal isotropic obstruction set. The equality of the two distributions has a geometric explanation through their common marked intersection graph despite distinct fiber types. Exact support-size coefficients are informative beyond the total count and applicable to Zariski decomposition and stable-base-locus comparisons.

No complete Mori-chamber classification or positive-characteristic extension is asserted. The work includes no independent validation, and historical priority is provisional.
