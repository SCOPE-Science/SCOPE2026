The two classical three-torsion extremal rational elliptic surfaces with fibers \(IV^*+IV\) and \(IV^*+I_3+I_1\) each have exactly \(4{,}712\) Zariski chambers. All chamber supports are characterized by avoiding \(14\) inclusion-minimal isotropic configurations among their \(13\) negative curves, giving the complete support-size distribution.
