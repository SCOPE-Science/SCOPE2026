# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem quantifies over arbitrary finite network sizes, arbitrary four sites and arbitrary real observation times. It assumes an entrywise nonnegative active input matrix, a stable Metzler drift, independent symmetric positive-rate telegraph sources, and independent additive Gaussian noise. Stationarity follows from the exponentially decaying semigroup. The exactly calculated four-time telegraph cumulant is strictly negative for every ordered quadruple; Fubini and cumulant multilinearity give an absolutely convergent fourfold integral. Nonnegative matrix exponential coefficients make each source's transfer kernel either identically zero or everywhere positive on positive times, with the distinction exactly the directed reachability relation. This proves strict negativity if and only if the intersection of four source-ancestry sets is nonempty. The Gaussian comparator has exactly matching two-time correlations, yet all its connected fourth correlations vanish. The proof also checks the sharp boundary by an explicit stable non-Metzler counterexample. `verify.py` passed 895 deterministic finite diagnostics, independent of the analytic quantifiers.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, qualified)
The closest original research is Paul–Dhar–Chaudhuri (arXiv:2402.11358), whose equation (1) contains the fixed-end nearest-neighbor overdamped active chain and whose calculations include stationary two-point functions and transient displacement kurtosis. It does not state the exact common-source support equivalence for stationary mixed fourth position cumulants or their rigidity across all time lags and arbitrary cooperative directed networks. Sarkar–Santra–Basu (arXiv:2210.09035) compare OU and RTP active reservoirs but in an inertial chain with potentially signed response kernels, not this sign/support theorem. Lutes (1986) offers broad linear-response cumulant methods, not verified as covering the particular telegraph positive-semigroup reachability equivalence. An earlier single-particle beta law is credited, not claimed new. Searches of primary literature, related fourth-order antecedents, and published mathematical results returned no stronger matching theorem after implication comparison. Remaining risk: an unindexed specialized fourth-cumulant/positive-systems theorem could subsume the result. A failed search does not certify absolute publication novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an if-and-only-if structural classification for **all four-site tuples and all time lags**, not a lone fourth-moment computation. The exact zeros and strict negative entries encode which observed sites can share a non-Gaussian active ancestor despite arbitrarily correlated Gaussian noise and even when all covariance matrices are matched by a Gaussian OU forcing model. The contrast with transient displacement kurtosis (which may be positive) and the explicit anti-cooperative sign reversal sharply delimit the statement. The result answers a natural physical identification question: what four-point stationary measurements can reveal that equilibrium-style two-point measurements cannot, without promising recovery of a unique graph.
