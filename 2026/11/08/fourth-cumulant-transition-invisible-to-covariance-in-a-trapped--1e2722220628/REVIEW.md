## Correctness — PASS
The model specifies every transition probability, parameter range, and normalization. A stationary stochastic convolution exists uniquely because the internal finite-state continuous-time chain is irreducible for \(-1\le\rho<1\), and harmonic relaxation is strict. The Poisson no-event decomposition proves the entire two-time active-force covariance without assuming independence of speed labels. Seven closed stationary moment equations, obtained by applying the full joint generator, reconstruct the fourth cumulant exactly; adding independent Brownian forcing changes the second but not the fourth cumulant. The rational witness and boundary classification follow by direct substitution. An independent four-state exact-rational generator solver passed 1,347 deterministic assertions; this corroborates but does not replace the parameter-uniform proof. Critical risks checked: heading update is independent at *each* Poisson event, \(\rho=1\) is not assumed ergodic, and Gaussian thermal noise is independent of the marks.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The February 9, 2026 preprint by Guéneau, Majumdar, and Schehr provides the exact *independent-post-tumble-velocity* harmonic-trap stationary law and arbitrary-velocity moments; the present \(\rho=0\) case lies under their broader formula. Their finite-velocity Dirichlet mixture does not encode a Markov correlation between successive speed labels. The full relevant Sections III and VI and Appendix B were inspected. Frydel (2023), Sun–Ye–Podgornik (2024/2025), Sun–Ye–Chen (2026), and Sevilla–Meléndez Mora (2026) solve fixed-speed or finite-tumble-time variants but do not assert a speed-memory fourth-cumulant sign boundary. The 2020 study of actively driven harmonic traps already demonstrates qualitatively that forces sharing an autocorrelation may have different higher-order statistics; that broader idea is not new. The finite-state generator itself is standard; the assessed narrower claim is the exact Markov-speed identity and the sharp realizable sign boundary. A systematic literature and research-index search found no dominating formulation, but a general colored-noise theorem not found by the searches remains a genuine priority risk.

## Value — PASS
Correlated run-to-run propulsion amplitudes are physically motivated in heterogeneous active-particle populations. A second-order description is blind to this memory by construction. The exact formula reveals that increasing the same hidden memory can change the *sign* of the stationary fourth cumulant at a sharp, dimensionless parameter boundary, even though the entire velocity covariance and stationary variance remain unchanged. The threshold is attainable for explicitly given finite parameters, is unaffected by independent thermal noise, and differentiates near-independent speed renewal from persistent run-speed heterogeneity. The fact is a precise new structural boundary for the minimal four-state model, not merely a generic moment calculation or trivial reparameterization.
