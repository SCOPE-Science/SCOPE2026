For the five abelian groups of order sixteen, an exhaustive exact census of every \(4\times4\) Fourier restriction gives five distinct rank distributions. In particular, the number of nonsingular restrictions identifies the group isomorphism type, with the three intermediate groups having exactly \(2{,}565{,}888\), \(2{,}297{,}856\), and \(2{,}052{,}608\) nonsingular restrictions, respectively.
