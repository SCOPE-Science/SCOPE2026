On the smooth Fermat surface of degree \(d=(q-1)/4\ge2\) over an odd field \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\equiv1\pmod4\), the \(3d^2\) coordinate-pairing lines cover all rational points precisely in characteristics at least seven. The exact off-line count is \(4d^2\) in characteristic three and \(4d^3+12d^2\) in characteristic five.
