The Euclidean inradius of every canonical Hansen polytope is exactly \((1+4\mu(\overline G))^{-1/2}\), where \(\mu\) is the largest squared norm in the complement's clique-constrained fractional stable-set polytope; all tangent facets arise from its quadratic maximizers. Unlike the known perfect-graph clique formula, the formula can differ from the clique-number prediction by an unbounded multiplicative factor, with a strict finite witness on \(35\) vertices.
