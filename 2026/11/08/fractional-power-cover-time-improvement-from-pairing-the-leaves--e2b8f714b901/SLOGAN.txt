For a simple random walk started at the hub, pairing the \(2m\) leaves of a star into \(m\) triangles reduces expected vertex-cover time by the sharp amount \(2^{2/3}\Gamma(4/3)m^{2/3}+o(m^{2/3})\). An exact finite-graph integral follows from independent Poissonized petal excursions; the fractional-power gain is a nontrivial subleading transport effect despite identical \(4m\log(2m)\) leading terms.
