The reduced union of the \(28\) bitangents of the Fermat plane quartic has a complete finite-field point-count and zeta-function classification: its complex intersection lattice changes in odd characteristic exactly at \(3,5,17\). Surprisingly, this union covers every rational point of the projective plane exactly over \(\mathbb F_3\), \(\mathbb F_5\), and \(\mathbb F_9\), while some rational singularities in other fields lie on no rational component line.
