The Chilean twelve-conic divisor has an exact Frobenius-equivariant motivic class and zeta formula determined by five finite incidence sets on the affine plane \(\mathbb F_3^2\). Among its nondegenerate finite-field realizations, one divisor over \(\mathbb F_5\) has exactly one rational point despite having no rational conic, while a divisor over \(\mathbb F_7\) has no rational point at all.
