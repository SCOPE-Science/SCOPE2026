The complete \(112\)-line boundary of the Fermat quartic over \(\mathbb F_3\) has a \(729\)-dimensional first étale cohomology with Frobenius signs \(+1\) and \(-1\) of multiplicities \(369\) and \(360\). Its open complement has no points over precisely \(\mathbb F_3\), \(\mathbb F_9\), and \(\mathbb F_{81}\), while an exact normalization-and-branch identity determines its zeta function over every extension.
