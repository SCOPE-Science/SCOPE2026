A Frobenius-corner congruence gives exact local F-pure thresholds \(1/(p^r-1)\) throughout an infinite class of formal hypersurface equations. For \(\sum_i x_i^{p^r+1}+\lambda\prod_i x_i^{p^r-1}\), the threshold jumps from \(1/p^r\) to \(1/(p^r-1)\) when \(\lambda\) becomes nonzero, although every germ has the same smooth projective tangent cone and Milnor number \(p^{rn}\).
