On the smooth Fermat cubic over every finite field of characteristic different from three, the 72 geometric sixers descend in exactly 72 or 2 cases according as \(q\equiv1\) or \(2\pmod3\). In the nonsplit case four unordered double-sixes descend, but three have their two sixer halves exchanged by Frobenius, and the full marking zeta functions are explicit.
