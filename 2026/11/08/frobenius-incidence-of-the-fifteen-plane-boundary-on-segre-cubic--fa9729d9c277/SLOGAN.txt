The fifteen-plane boundary of every finite-field \(S_6\)-twist of the Segre cubic has an explicit, uniform eleven-type Frobenius incidence spectrum. In the twist with a five-cycle, that entire geometrically nonempty boundary has no rational points until extension degree \(5\), and its zeta function is \(\frac{(1-q^5T^5)^6}{(1-q^{10}T^5)^3(1-T^5)^5}\).
