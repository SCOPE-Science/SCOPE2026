For every \(n\ge4\) in characteristic \(2\), the dual of the generic symmetric-determinant hypersurface collapses from the quadratic Veronese to a linear \(\mathbb P^{n-1}\) through Frobenius on the kernel map. Its Jacobian singular scheme is generically a square-zero double of the corank-at-least-two locus, with nilpotent line \(\det(\mathcal K)\otimes\mathcal O(-1)\); for \(4\times4\) matrices its degree is \(20\).
