Even when every equation is unit-normalized, a Frobenius-optimal choice of two exact constraints can have sharp projected-Kaczmarz progress \(s^2\), while a different unique choice achieves \(1/2\), an unbounded \(1/(2s^2)\) gap. Exact expected-error trajectories show the gap for the same consistent system and initialization rule, both with and without rejection of zero-residual samples.
