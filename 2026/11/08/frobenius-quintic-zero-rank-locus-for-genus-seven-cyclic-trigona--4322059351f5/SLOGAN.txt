For all smooth genus-seven cyclic trigonal curves \(y^3=f(x)\) with monic squarefree octic \(f\) in characteristic two, the Jacobian \(2\)-rank is exactly \(0\), \(2\), or \(4\), determined by a \(2\times2\) coefficient matrix and its Frobenius-twisted square. The complete rank-zero locus is an irreducible codimension-two cone over a smooth rational quintic, and its singular vertex distinguishes two different nilpotence profiles of the Cartier operator.
