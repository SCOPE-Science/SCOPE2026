The coefficientwise characteristic-three Schur quartic is a nonnormal Frobenius scroll, not the alternative smooth reduction: its normalization is \(\mathbb F_2\), its exact conductor is the square of the singular-line ideal, and its geometric lines are two directrices plus a single ruling pencil. Its Jacobian singular scheme has degree \(9\) and genus \(10\), while \(\#S(\mathbb F_{q^m})=(q^m+1)^2\) for every finite characteristic-three field and every \(m\ge1\).
