For weighted rational curve cones of type \((d,e)\), the normalization conductor is precisely the monomials of \(s\)-exponent at least \(e-1\), and the nonnormal divisor normalizes by the degree-\(e\) map \(u\mapsto u^e\). The rings are seminormal exactly for \(e\le2\), and in characteristic \(p>0\) are Frobenius split exactly for \(e=1\) or \(e=2\) with odd \(p\).
