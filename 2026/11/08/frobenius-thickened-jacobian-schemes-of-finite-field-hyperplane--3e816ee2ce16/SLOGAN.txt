For the complete arrangement of \(\mathbb F_q\)-rational hyperplanes in \(\mathbb P^{N-1}\), \(N\ge4\), the full Jacobian ideal is exactly the \(q\)-th Frobenius power of the radical ideal of its codimension-two incidence locus. The resulting scheme has \({N\brack2}_q\) components, each generically of length \(q^2\), and an explicit minimal graded resolution.
