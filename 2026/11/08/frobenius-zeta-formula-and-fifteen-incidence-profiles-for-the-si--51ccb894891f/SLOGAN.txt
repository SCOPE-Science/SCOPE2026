Removing the sixteen geometric exceptional curves from a smooth quartic del Pezzo surface produces an open surface whose rational-point counts and full zeta function are determined by a signed Frobenius action on the sixteen-vertex, forty-edge intersection graph. Exactly fifteen Weyl-group incidence profiles occur, yielding fourteen distinct ground-field counting polynomials, with the previously known split value \((q-5)^2\) as one case.
