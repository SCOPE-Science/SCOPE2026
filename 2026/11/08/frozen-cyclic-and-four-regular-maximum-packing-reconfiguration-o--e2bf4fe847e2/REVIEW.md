# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The domain and the allowed edges are specified exactly: \(N\ge6\), maximum independent sets of \(C_N^2\), and one legal token slide of cyclic length one or two. The cyclic gap map is a bijection after accounting for the exactly \(m\) choices of a designated token. Its slack-transfer rule is equivalent in both directions to the graph's token-slide rule. The proof establishes freezing, the one-slack cycle, degree four, connectivity by coalescing defects, and the triangle count by the Johnson-graph triangle dichotomy. The unique fixed packing of the available interval has been checked algebraically. The exceptional smallest two-slack case \(N=8\) is addressed separately for triangle incidence. Complete finite enumerations are reproducible through the two embedded programs but are not used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The closest directly inspected prior text is Bonsma–Kamiński–Wrochna (arXiv:1403.0359v1), especially Proposition 1 and Theorem 16, giving adjacency equivalence for maximum sets and polynomial reachability on claw-free graphs. It does not compute the exact \(\mathcal R_N\) graph for squares of cycles. Avis–Hoang (arXiv:2203.16861), Section 5 and its structural results for base paths and cycles, does not imply the square-of-cycle result: squaring changes both feasible sets and allowable slide edges. Bonamy–Bousquet (arXiv:1605.00442) addresses chordal/interval reachability; Hoang (arXiv:2208.07199) concerns distance-three constraints with slide edges of the underlying graph, which differ here. No equivalent formula was recovered from actual relevant mathematical database searches. Searches cannot prove global novelty; incomplete indexing and unpublished results remain risk.

## Value — PASS

The objects are natural densest distance-three packings of a cycle. The number of unused cyclic sites \(N-3\lfloor N/3\rfloor\) changes the connectedness and local move geometry sharply: frozen states, a lone cycle, or a connected four-regular graph. The latter has an exact triangle-incidence stratification, distinguishing combinatorial types that degrees and counts alone cannot. This determines complete structural invariants uniformly for an infinite family, not a routine re-expression of its independence number.

## Scope and residual risk

There is no claim for smaller independent sets or a closed formula for distances in the four-regular case. Historically priority remains qualified, and the separate literature comparison did not access every possible unpublished computation. Results have not received independent scholarly audit.
