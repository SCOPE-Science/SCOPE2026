Maximum independent-set token sliding on squared cycles exhibits a three-way modular transition: three frozen states when \(N\equiv0\pmod3\), one cycle of \(N\) states when \(N\equiv1\pmod3\), and a connected four-regular graph with exactly \(N\) triangles when \(N\equiv2\pmod3\). For \(N\equiv2\pmod3\) and \(N\ge11\), the four-regular graphs are nevertheless non-vertex-transitive, with an exact three-level triangle-incidence distribution.
