For a threshold predator–prey model with a stable-focus coexistence state, there is an explicit nonempty \(2/e\)-separated threshold window in which **every frozen growth rate** from an arbitrarily weak sinusoidal forcing stays coexistent from the same initial state, yet the resonant periodically forced system has a Filippov trajectory that becomes extinct. The prey-response gap is universal and sharp in the critically damped limit; the theorem is an existence statement, not a claim of uniqueness at grazing contacts.
