For square-plaquette quantum dimers on binary hypercubes, the four-cube has eight frozen coverings and one connected 264-state resonance sector, while the five-cube has 120 frozen coverings. A layerwise construction from four pairwise edge-disjoint frozen base matchings proves at least \(8\,3^{2^{d-5}}\) isolated coverings in each dimension \(d\ge5\).
