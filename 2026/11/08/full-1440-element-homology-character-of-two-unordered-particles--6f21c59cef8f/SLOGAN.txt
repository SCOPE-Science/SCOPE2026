For two unordered points on the Tutte–Coxeter graph, integral second homology has rank \(90\), and its full \(1440\)-element rational symmetry character is \(90\) at identity, \(-6\) at color-preserving involutions, \(2\) at color-preserving elements of order four, and zero elsewhere. On the natural \(S_6\) subgroup this is twice the sum of four specified Specht modules, with no globally invariant class.
