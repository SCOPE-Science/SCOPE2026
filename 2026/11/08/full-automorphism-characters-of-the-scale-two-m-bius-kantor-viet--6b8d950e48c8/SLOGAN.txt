The full \(96\)-element automorphism group of the scale-two Möbius–Kantor Rips complex acts on degree-three integral homology by permuting its two bipartition spheres, but its nine-dimensional degree-two rational homology has no invariants and is a multiplicity-free sum of three complex irreducibles. The quotient has exactly one nonzero reduced rational homology group, of rank one in degree three; the nonequivariant homotopy type was known previously.
