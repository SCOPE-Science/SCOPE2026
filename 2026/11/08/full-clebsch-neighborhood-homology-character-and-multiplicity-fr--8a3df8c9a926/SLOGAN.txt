The full \(1920\)-element automorphism group of the five-regular Clebsch graph acts on the second rational homology of its open neighborhood complex as a multiplicity-free sum of irreducibles of dimensions \(6\), \(10\), and \(15\). An exact character formula extends the previously known \(384\)-element four-cube Čech action to all Clebsch symmetries; the known nonequivariant homotopy type is not the new contribution.
