For the labelled sunlet graph \(S_n=C_n\odot K_1\), a nine-state transfer matrix gives its entire Italian-domination polynomial. Every minimum-weight labelling has a four-column local form, and the exact multiplicity is \(3\cdot2^k\), \(n(k+2)2^{k-1}\), or \(n2^k\) according as \(n\equiv0,1,2\pmod3\), where \(k=\lceil n/3\rceil\); the scalar minimum weight itself was known previously.
