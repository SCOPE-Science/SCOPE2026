The 24-vertex three-dimensional permutahedron has a four-dimensional fifth-homology module, but its full 48-element symmetry acts as the sum of a flip-parity line and a permutation-sign-twisted irreducible signed-coordinate three-space. The entire action is determined by \(\chi_5(\pi,\varepsilon)=\prod_i\varepsilon_i+\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)\sum_{\pi(i)=i}\varepsilon_i\), so central inversion is \(-I_4\) and no nonzero rational homology class is fixed by every symmetry.
