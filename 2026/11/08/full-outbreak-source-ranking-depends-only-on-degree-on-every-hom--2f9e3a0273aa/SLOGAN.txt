For a homogeneous single-source epidemic on any finite tree, the probability that **every vertex is eventually infected** depends on the source only through its degree, even when infectious lifetimes are iid and arbitrarily distributed. The risk ratio between a degree-two source and a leaf equals \(1+\operatorname{Var}(X)/(\mathbb E X)^2\), exactly isolating individual-transmissibility heterogeneity from tree topology.
