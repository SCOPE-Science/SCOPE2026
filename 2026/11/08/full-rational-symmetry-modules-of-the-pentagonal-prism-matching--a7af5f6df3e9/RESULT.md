# Full rational symmetry modules of the pentagonal-prism matching complex

## Finding
Let \(P=C_5\square K_2\) be the pentagonal-prism graph, \(X=\mathcal M(P)\) its **ordinary** matching complex, and \(G=\operatorname{Aut}(P)\cong D_5\times C_2\). Let \(\rho\) denote rotation of the pentagon, \(\tau\) a reflection, and \(\sigma\) the interchange of the two pentagonal layers. Define rational one-dimensional characters \(\eta,\lambda:G\to\{\pm1\}\) by
\[
\eta(\rho)=1,\quad\eta(\tau)=-1,\quad\eta(\sigma)=1;\qquad
\lambda(\rho)=1,\quad\lambda(\tau)=1,\quad\lambda(\sigma)=-1.
\]
Then the complete **reduced rational homology representation** is
\[
\widetilde H_j(X;\mathbb Q)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Q_{\eta}\oplus\mathbb Q_{\lambda},&j=2,\\
\mathbb Q\oplus\mathbb Q_{\eta\lambda},&j=3,\\
0,&j\ne2,3.
\end{cases}
\]
In particular, the full finite-symmetry quotient is rationally cohomologically a three-sphere:
\[
H^*(|X|/G;\mathbb Q)\cong H^*(S^3;\mathbb Q)
\]
as graded rings. No homotopy equivalence with a sphere is asserted.

## Assumptions and scope
The ten vertices of \(P\) are \((a,i)\), where \(a\in\{0,1\}\) and \(i\in\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z\). Its fifteen edges are the ten rim edges \(\{(a,i),(a,i+1)\}\) and five spokes \(\{(0,i),(1,i)\}\). The matching complex \(X\) has these fifteen graph edges as vertices; a \(k\)-element matching is a \((k-1)\)-simplex. The group acts by \(\rho(a,i)=(a,i+1)\), \(\tau(a,i)=(a,-i)\), \(\sigma(a,i)=(1-a,i)\). Coefficients in the representation and quotient claims are \(\mathbb Q\), not \(\mathbb Z\).

This is the closed, odd five-rung circular ladder, not the open \(2\times5\) grid, and \(X\) is not the independence complex of \(P\) nor its perfect-matching subcomplex. All candidate matchings of sizes zero through five are enumerated; matchings with six edges are impossible on ten graph vertices.

## Proof
**Full automorphism group.** There are exactly five four-cycles in \(P\), namely its side squares. Each spoke lies on two four-cycles; each rim edge lies on one. Hence every graph automorphism preserves the spoke set and the pair of five-cycles formed by deleting spokes. It consequently induces an automorphism of a pentagon and must either preserve both layers or interchange both; these operations produce all \(20\) automorphisms. Thus \(G=D_5\times C_2\).

**Augmented cellular ranks.** Order the fifteen graph edges lexicographically by their endpoint pairs. Enumerate all subsets, retain precisely those with disjoint endpoints, and orient each simplex by its increasing edge-index order. Let \(C[k]\) denote the augmented reduced chain group in dimension \(k-1\), so \(C[0]=\mathbb Q\) is generated by the empty simplex. For a matching \((e_1<\cdots<e_k)\), define
\[
\partial_k(e_1,\ldots,e_k)=\sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^{i-1}(e_1,\ldots,\widehat e_i,\ldots,e_k).
\]
The complete enumeration and **exact rational** boundary elimination yield

| \(k\) | \(0\) | \(1\) | \(2\) | \(3\) | \(4\) | \(5\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(\dim C[k]\) | 1 | 15 | 75 | 145 | 95 | 11 |
| \(\operatorname{rank}\partial_k\) | — | 1 | 14 | 61 | 82 | 11 |

Consequently \(\dim\widetilde H_2=145-61-82=2\), \(\dim\widetilde H_3=95-82-11=2\), and every other reduced rational Betti number vanishes. Exhaustive signed-face cancellation verifies \(\partial_{k-1}\partial_k=0\).

**Involution eigenspaces.** For any of the commuting involutions \(u=\tau,\sigma,\tau\sigma\), form explicit \(\pm1\) chain eigenbases. On a two-element orbit of oriented simplices \(b_i\mapsto s b_j\) (where \(s\in\{\pm1\}\)), use \(b_i\pm s b_j\); a fixed oriented simplex contributes only to its own signed eigenspace. The following are ranks of the induced boundaries in \(\pm\) eigencomplexes, ordered \(\partial_1,\ldots,\partial_5\):

| \(u\) | ranks for \(+1\) | ranks for \(-1\) | \((\dim H_2^+,\dim H_2^-)\) | \((\dim H_3^+,\dim H_3^-)\) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| \(\tau\) | (1,8,29,39,6) | (0,6,32,43,5) | (1,1) | (1,1) |
| \(\sigma\) | (1,9,31,38,6) | (0,5,30,44,5) | (1,1) | (1,1) |
| \(\tau\sigma\) | (1,7,27,43,7) | (0,7,34,39,4) | (0,2) | (2,0) |

These eigen-ranks are certified over \(\mathbb Q\), rather than inferred from reduction modulo a prime: each modular Gaussian elimination supplies a lower bound on the corresponding rational rank, and the two bounds sum to the independently computed exact rational rank of the unrestricted boundary. As each rational boundary splits into its \(\pm\) eigenspace boundaries, both lower bounds must be equalities. Euler's kernel-minus-image formula in each eigencomplex gives the last two columns. The embedded checker constructs the actual signed matrices, checks every face and all \(20\) group actions, and reproduces the table.

**Character identification.** Since every nontrivial irreducible rational representation of \(C_5\) has dimension four, the fivefold rotation acts trivially on each two-dimensional homology group. The commuting involutions \(\tau,\sigma\) can be simultaneously diagonalized over \(\mathbb Q\). In degree two each has trace zero but their product has trace \(-2\), forcing the two characters \(\eta\) and \(\lambda\). In degree three the individual traces are zero and the product trace is \(+2\), forcing \(1\) and \(\eta\lambda\). These are the asserted full \(D_5\times C_2\) modules.

**Orbit-space cohomology.** For a finite group acting simplicially, characteristic-zero transfer (equivalently, the rationally acyclic stabilizer fibers of the Borel-to-orbit map) identifies \(H^*(|X|/G;\mathbb Q)\) with \(H^*(X;\mathbb Q)^G\) as rings. The representation formula shows invariants only in degrees zero and three, one-dimensional in each. Their graded product structure is necessarily that of \(H^*(S^3;\mathbb Q)\).

## Relationship to prior work
Braun and Hough's June 3, 2016 preprint studies matching complexes of **open** two-row grids and develops discrete-Morse machinery; those graphs omit the closing pair of rim edges in the prism and their results do not determine this full automorphism representation. Matsushita's 2018 preprint gives homotopy decompositions for the same open-grid family, but not for this circular ladder's ordinary matching complex with its symmetry. Agarwal and Basak's September 2026 revision concerns independence and **perfect** matching complexes of circular ladders, not the ordinary matching complex (the complete revised text was not available for line-by-line comparison). Earlier indexed results on circular-ladder independence and cut complexes address different simplicial objects. No inspected source was found to imply the stated two-module decomposition or quotient cohomology; non-detection is not a universal novelty proof.

## Limitations
This is an exact finite calculation for one natural ten-vertex graph. It does not classify circular ladders of all lengths, integral homology lattices, torsion, equivariant homotopy types, or homotopy types of the quotient. The modular eigen-rank checks are used only in conjunction with exact rational total ranks; modular ranks alone would not suffice. A complete publication-priority search, especially for unindexed calculations or inaccessible portions of relevant works, remains outstanding. No independent mathematical audit is claimed.
