# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For arbitrary \(d\ge2\) and \(n_i\ge2\), the determinant \(\det(A(t)B)\) is precisely the Schubert-type cut equation because the projection kernel is \(\ker B^{\mathsf T}\). A real diagonal specialization supplies \(N\) simple roots and nondegenerate Gram data. Independent two-point evaluations make the ordered-pair incidence irreducible, which gives \(2\)-transitivity. A one-parameter quadratic collision inside the nondegenerate-frame open set provides a transposition, hence full \(\mathfrak S_N\). Root permutations descend from frames to Grassmannian planes. Exact arithmetic verifies representative determinant, interpolation, discriminant and projector identities. No finite sample is treated as proof of the universal assertion.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The closest directly relevant paper, Friedman–Hoşten–Rosana, arXiv:2610.02018, Example 2.1 and Sections 2 and 4, studies the same scroll ruling curve, but counts *distance-critical* points and defines the cut locus without computing the present \(N\)-sheet cut-cover monodromy. The earlier line-distance paper arXiv:2601.22843 concerns optimization on lines; its Sections 1, 3 and 4 were inspected and do not state the present cut-root monodromy. The general support-generic root-monodromy theorem of Esterov–Lang, arXiv:2204.14235, does not directly apply to the restricted coefficient locus of determinants of row-degree-bounded polynomial matrices. Accessible research records concerning scroll ruling ED/GD degrees are not equivalent to the present cut-divisor Galois group. A specialized implicit reference may remain; historical priority is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The cut divisor is a canonical geometric obstruction in Grassmann distance problems, not an arbitrary polynomial slice. Its degree alone does not predict root permutations. The theorem completely identifies the generic cut-cover Galois group for all scroll block types, establishes maximal irreducibility of labeling, and gives a concrete Abel–Ruffini barrier for degrees \(N\ge5\). This is a new structural statement distinct from ED/GD critical-point counts.

## Limits

A published restricted-determinantal-family monodromy classification could potentially dominate the statement and warrants specialist review.
