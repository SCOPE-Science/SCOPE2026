For the stationary Abelian sandpile on \(K_{a,b}\), adding a chip at a specified neighbor of the sink causes every nonsink vertex to topple with exact probability \(b^{-1}(1-1/a)^{b-1}\), because maximizing recurrent states correspond bijectively to spanning trees of \(K_{a-1,b}\). On balanced graphs these rare avalanches occur with probability asymptotic to \(1/(en)\) yet contribute a fraction tending to \(1/e\) of the expected total toppling activity.
