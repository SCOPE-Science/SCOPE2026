In exponentially age-biased recursive trees with every fixed \(a>1\), the total leaf count satisfies an explicit nondegenerate functional Gaussian limit, with strictly negative leaf-indicator covariances at every positive separation. As \(a\downarrow1\) after tree size diverges, its leaf density tends to \(e^{-1}\) and variance per vertex to \(e^{-1}-2e^{-2}\), rather than the corresponding uniform-attachment values.
