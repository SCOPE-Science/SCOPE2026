In the scalar inhibitory hybrid equation \(\dot x=-ax-g\tanh(x(\lfloor t\rfloor+\theta))\), advancing the within-cell sampling phase past the exact threshold \(\theta_*(a)=-a^{-1}\log((1+e^{-a})/2)\) stabilizes zero exponentially at **every** feedback gain. Below this phase, a sharp gain threshold creates a unique symmetric stable anti-periodic orbit, while the critical equilibrium attracts algebraically at the rate \(|x(k)|\sim(2Kk)^{-1/2}\) except for finite-hitting initial states.
