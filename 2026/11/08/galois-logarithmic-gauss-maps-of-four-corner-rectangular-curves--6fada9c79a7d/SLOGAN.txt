For every \(m,n\ge2\) and \(\lambda\in\mathbb C^*\setminus\{1\}\), the four-term curve \(1+x^m+y^n+\lambda x^my^n=0\) has a Galois logarithmic Gauss map of degree \(2mn\), with generalized-dihedral deck group \((\mu_m\times\mu_n)\rtimes C_2\). Its four branch directions have respective ramification partitions \(m^{2n}\), \(n^{2m}\), \(2^{mn}\), and \(2^{mn}\), giving explicit regular four-point covers of every genus \((m-1)(n-1)\) in this family.
