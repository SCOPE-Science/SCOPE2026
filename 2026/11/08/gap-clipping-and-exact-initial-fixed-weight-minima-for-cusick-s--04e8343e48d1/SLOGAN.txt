Every fixed-weight binary digit-sum shift problem has an exact finite search domain: gaps between set bits can be capped at \(k+1\), so the fixed-Hamming-weight Cusick infimum is attained below \(2^{k^2}\). Exact enumeration gives all minima and equality shifts through weight eight, including \(\min_{s_2(t)=8}c_t=8839/16384\) attained precisely at \(t=951\cdot2^a\) for integers \(a\ge0\).
