In a symmetric two-phase run-and-tumble continuum with local resetting, fixed matched interfacial flux makes the dense-droplet width and mass completely independent of the dilute-gas motility law. For a dense-phase flux proportional to \(\rho^p\) with \(0<p\leq1\), the unique optimal resetting scale obeys \(\Gamma_*/\Gamma_c=p^{p/(p-1)}\) for \(p<1\), continuously reaching \(e\) at \(p=1\); this gives a sharp, nonuniversal control law beyond the constant-core-speed model.
