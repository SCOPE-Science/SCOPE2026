# Scientific review: three-symbol permutation-burst sphere fluctuations

## Correctness — PASS
The claim has three separable parts grounded in one exact identity. First, the changed-coordinate support of a nonidentity three-position permutation uniquely classifies outputs as adjacent swaps, endpoint swaps, or one of two three-coordinate derangements. This yields the deficit identity with overlap correction for all-equal triples, without double-counting. Second, each equality event has an exact uniform-alphabet probability, any two distinct pair-equality events are independent, and the relevant pair/triple covariances give the displayed exact variance. Third, a two-dependent blocking argument proves the fixed-alphabet Gaussian limit, while joint mixed factorial moments of local edge-equality counts prove the sparse-collision independent-Poisson limit. The packaged direct-channel checker replay passed 195,317 words and exact rational moment tests in 16 parameter profiles. These tests check finite steps only; the limit proofs are analytical.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence; residual priority risk
Chen and Milenkovic (arXiv:2610.15701v1, October 11, 2026) study the binary three-window and general permutation-burst model, present an exact binary mean and concentration bounds, and explicitly defer larger alphabets. Related pre-existing deterministic exact-volume results include the general-alphabet volume decomposition and the binary three-window enumerator, so those are not claimed as discoveries. The new theorem instead identifies an exact nonbinary variance and two qualitatively different probabilistic limits, with independent rare-collision types of respective deficit weights three and five. Statement-level searches of the primary text and related published research results revealed no equivalent variance-and-compound-Poisson statement. The finite variance is obtainable from the known deterministic model with additional covariance work; the compound-Poisson law requires a joint rare-collision argument and is not an immediate specialization of any inspected bound. An unindexed source could still cover these laws; independent priority certification has not occurred.

## Value — PASS
A source model with a large alphabet can have essentially maximal individual error spheres, yet a finite random number of local repetitions creates nontrivial ambiguity losses. The exact weights three and five isolate two geometrically different mechanisms, giving a sharp limiting distribution when the alphabet is comparable to block length, and a contrasting Gaussian fluctuation regime at fixed alphabet size. This supplies a quantitative probabilistic refinement of the sphere-count information used in permutation-burst redundancy arguments, rather than a numerical small-length example or a restatement of the maximum.

## Scope and limitations
Uniform independent symbols; one length-three non-wrapping permutation; one output set, not multiple bursts; \(q\ge3\). No higher-window, nonuniform-source or coding-rate theorem is asserted. The verification program is finite and independent mathematical and historical audits remain outstanding.
