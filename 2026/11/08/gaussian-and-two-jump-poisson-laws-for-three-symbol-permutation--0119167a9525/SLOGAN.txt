For uniform \(q\)-ary words, a single three-symbol permutation burst has an exact variance and a Gaussian sphere-size law at every fixed \(q\ge3\). When the alphabet grows with \(n/q\to\lambda\), the shortfall from the maximum instead converges to a two-jump compound-Poisson law \(5P_\lambda+3Q_\lambda\) with independent mean-\(\lambda\) Poisson counts.
