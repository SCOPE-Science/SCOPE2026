# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With m unmatched vertices all proposals choose distinct neighbors independently. Ordered disjoint mutual-pair patterns give E(C_m)_r=(m)_{2r}/[2^r(m−1)^{2r}], hence mean removed m/(m−1) and variance 2m(m−2)^2/(m−1)^3. The uniform probability of at least one pair controls geometric state residence. The exact stopped-martingale identity N_n=T_n+A_n+M_T, where A_n=O_L2(log n), gives E T_n=n+O(log n), Var T_n=2n+O(sqrt n log n), and a martingale CLT yields (T_n−n)/sqrt(2n)⇒N(0,1). The bounded-away-from-terminal quadratic variation gives the functional Brownian law. Independent exhaustive enumeration of each loop-free proposal mapping m=2..6 recovered the exact mean/variance. Martingale infinite limits rely on the analytic bounds.

## originality

PASS

The 2003 rendezvous/handshake result proves Poisson(1/2) one-round mutual pairs on the complete graph, and recent sparse-random-graph mutual-proposal matching results study a different regime. Neither directly determines a cumulative absorption time centered at n with variance 2n or the path functional CLT. Some related handshake thesis chapters remain inaccessible.
