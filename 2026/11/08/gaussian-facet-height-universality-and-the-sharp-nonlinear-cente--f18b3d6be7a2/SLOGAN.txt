For a labeled candidate facet of a high-dimensional spherical random polytope conditioned to be a facet, the signed offset has a sharp total-variation Gaussian law throughout the finite \((n-d)/d\) regime, with explicit shrinking variance and a matching Gaussian-polytope offset law. The naive linear centering fails at the precise scale \(n-d\asymp d^{3/4}\), while the nonlinear saddlepoint centering remains valid; finite-dimensional heights also satisfy a universal strong-log-concavity concentration bound.
