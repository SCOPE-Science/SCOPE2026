Every left ideal of an upper-triangular matrix algebra is uniquely a nested flag of constrained row subspaces, giving an exact Gaussian-binomial formula for the number of ideals of each dimension over every finite field. The fixed-dimension-vector strata are smooth iterated Grassmann bundles, and the resulting formal field-size polynomial specializes at \(q=0\) to the Catalan number \(C_{n+1}\).
