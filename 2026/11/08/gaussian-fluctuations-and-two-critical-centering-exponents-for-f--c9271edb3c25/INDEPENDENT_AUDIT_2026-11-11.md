# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed F_N from independent Bernoulli clocks, positive derivative L and variance V, Lyapunov third-moment O(N^-1/2), and inversion through P(W_N<=u)=P(M_N(u)>theta). Verified generalized harmonic-sum Euler–Maclaurin, uniform endpoint-smoothed Riemann sums, D_s(a)=(J_s(a)-e^-a)/s, unique cancellation root, B2 sign and exact theta ranges. Independent quadrature and root-finding at s=.3,.5,.7 and theta=.4 gave scaled finite drifts .33072,.40645,.44426 versus predicted .31821,.40576,.43335 at N=16000, consistent with finite corrections; they do not prove the CLT. No assertion at s=0,1 or moving parameters.

## originality

PASS

Müller–Šebek Theorem 5.3 gives the aligned diffuse model first-order N-linear limit; Theorem 5.5 concerns a small-s deformation of that limit, not sampling-noise Gaussian fluctuations nor the finite harmonic normalization B1/B2 with cancellation threshold. Chebunin–Zuyev and Karlin analyze fixed infinite-frequency urns; other published SCOPE quorums use homogeneous clocks or s=1, not this finite aligned s<1 case. Primary arXiv full-text §5 and theorem compared, so best-of-knowledge PASS with broader inverse-CLT risks.
