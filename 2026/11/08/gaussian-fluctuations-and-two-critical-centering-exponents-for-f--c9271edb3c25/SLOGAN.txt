For finite aligned-Zipf coupon quorums with fixed \(0<s<1\), the exact-mean-centered hitting time has a \(\sqrt N\)-Gaussian law, but centering at its first-order limit changes character at \(s=1/2\). A unique quorum threshold cancels the leading harmonic-normalization bias and shifts the critical exponent to \(s=3/4\), with an explicit negative second-order correction.
