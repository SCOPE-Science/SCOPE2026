A uniformly random idempotent in the graph-endomorphism monoid of the loopless path \(P_n\) typically retracts it onto a subpath of \(cn^{1/3}\) vertices, where \(c=(\pi^2/\log 2)^{1/3}\). Its image rank has a Gaussian fluctuation on the finer \(n^{1/6}\) scale with limiting variance \(c/(3\log 2)\), and an exact adjacency-walk convolution counts every finite rank.
