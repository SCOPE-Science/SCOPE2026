For positive-drift biased simple walks, the duration rank of the deepest completed drawdown has a second-order Gaussian fluctuation: \((\log R_n-(1-\rho)\log n)/\sqrt{\log n}\) converges to a centered normal law with variance \(8pq\beta^2/(\delta^3\alpha)\). The sharper relation \(\log R_n=\log n-\beta L_{I_n}-\frac32\log L_{I_n}+O_{\mathbb P}(1)\) explains how the selected excursion's duration transfers its fluctuation to its rank.
