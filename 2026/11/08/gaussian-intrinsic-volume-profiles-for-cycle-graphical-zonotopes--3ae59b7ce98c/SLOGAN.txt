The intrinsic-volume generating function of the standard graphical zonotope of a cycle reduces exactly to a logarithm of the polylogarithm \(\operatorname{Li}_{-1/2}\). For every fixed positive intrinsic-volume weight, its normalized index has a fully explicit Gaussian limit with linearly growing mean and variance; at unit weight the asymptotic center is \(0.5294005567\ldots\) times the cycle order.
