In leaf-neighbor-driven preferential-attachment trees, the global leaf count satisfies a Gaussian central limit theorem with explicit variance \(V(a)=r(a)^3(1-r(a))/(3r(a)^2-4r(a)+2)\). This variance has a unique sharp maximum at \(a=0.4447336345\ldots\), before the leaf-degree tail transition at \(a=1\), and crosses the uniform recursive-tree benchmark \(1/12\) at an explicit smaller parameter.
