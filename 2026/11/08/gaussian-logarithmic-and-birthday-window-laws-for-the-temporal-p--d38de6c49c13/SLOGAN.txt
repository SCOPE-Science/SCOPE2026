For static singleton-party approvals with \(an\) temporal decisions, PJR, EJR and EJR+ have exactly the same welfare price \(n\max_jB_{n,j}/\sum_jB_{n,j}^2\). With independent uniformly chosen parties, this price passes from a Gaussian fixed-party regime to logarithmic growth, then to sharp birthday-window plateaus and eventually back to one.
