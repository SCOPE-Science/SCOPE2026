For the periodic twin-candidate indicator obtained by sieving through a prime \(P\), every additive Fourier coefficient is nonzero, and its log-magnitude over uniform frequencies obeys a quantitative Gaussian law with variance \((\pi^2/12)\pi(P)+O((\log P)^3)\). The associated integer circulant has the exact negative determinant \(-\prod_{3\le p\le P}(p-2)^{P^{\#}/p}\).
