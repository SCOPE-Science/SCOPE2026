Refreshing an equilibrium Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle at Poisson times from its own Gaussian stationary law preserves reversibility, every one-time Gaussian marginal, and the complete covariance function of a slower Gaussian OU comparator, yet creates a strictly positive two-time fourth cumulant and extra mutual information. Its fourth-order witness has exactly one optimal lag, \(r^{-1}\log[2(r+\lambda)/(r+2\lambda)]\), whose peak height increases strictly with refresh rate.
