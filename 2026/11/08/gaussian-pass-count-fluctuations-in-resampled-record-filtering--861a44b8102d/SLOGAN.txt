Resampled record filtering takes \(n/\log n\) passes rather than the \(\sqrt n\)-scale of the non-resampled version. Its pass count has a Gaussian limit with fluctuations of size \(\sqrt n/\log n\), centered by the exact harmonic sum \(\sum_{m\le n}1/H_m\), whose error relative to the true mean is only \(O(\log n)\).
