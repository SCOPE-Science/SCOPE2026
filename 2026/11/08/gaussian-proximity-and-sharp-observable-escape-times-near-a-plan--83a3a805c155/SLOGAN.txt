For the planar transient-center flow \(\dot x=-xy,\ \dot y=x-1\), the first exit from a fixed observable-speed band occurs after \(\sqrt{2\log(1/\varepsilon)}\) time units, with an explicit logarithmic correction and a sharp error estimate. Achieving a long residence time \(\tau\) requires an initial distance from the invariant line of order \(\exp(-\tau^2/2)/\tau\), with an exact coefficient determined by the initial vertical coordinate and speed threshold.
