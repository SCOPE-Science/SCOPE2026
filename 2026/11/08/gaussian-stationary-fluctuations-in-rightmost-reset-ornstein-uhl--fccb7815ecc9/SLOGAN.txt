For \(N\ge2\) fixed, a group of Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particles collectively reset at rate \(r\) to its rightmost member has stationary reset-location fluctuations \(R_r-c_N\sqrt r\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,V_N)\), where \(V_N=(D/k)(
u_N-\pi\mu_N^2/4)\) and \(\mu_N,
u_N\) are moments of the maximum of \(N\) standard Gaussians. All particle coordinates share the same limiting Gaussian fluctuation, and for \(N=2\) its variance is \(3D/(4k)\).
