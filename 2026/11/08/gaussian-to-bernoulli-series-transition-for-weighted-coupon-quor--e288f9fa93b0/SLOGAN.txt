For equal-rate discovery clocks, diffuse weighted coupon quorums have a universal first-order time but a sharp second-order transition: Zipf quorum weights with exponent \(\rho\le1/2\) yield Gaussian fluctuations, whereas \(1/2<\rho\le1\) yield an explicit non-Gaussian Bernoulli-series law. At the harmonic endpoint and half-weight quorum, the limiting standardized excess kurtosis is exactly \(-4/5\).
