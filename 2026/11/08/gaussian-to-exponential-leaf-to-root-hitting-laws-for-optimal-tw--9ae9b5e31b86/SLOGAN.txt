In a complete rooted \(b\)-ary tree, always taking an offered parent is optimal for every root-hitting deadline under two independent random neighbor offers. Starting from a leaf, the optimal time has Gaussian fluctuations around \(9h\) for \(b=2\), but for every fixed \(b\ge3\) it has an exponentially large mean in \(h\) and converges after mean-normalization to an exponential law.
