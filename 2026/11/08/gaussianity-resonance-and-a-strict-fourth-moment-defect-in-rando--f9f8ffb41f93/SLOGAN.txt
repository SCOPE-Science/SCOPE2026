For independently repartitioned harmonic random-batch Euler–Langevin sampling, the unique stationary law is Gaussian at exactly the reflection step \(h_*=2/(2\alpha+\kappa p/(p-1))\), even though every other mean-square-stable step produces a strictly positive fourth-moment defect in a particle contrast. The theorem also gives the sharp mean-square stability boundary and an explicit batch-size-dependent lower bound for non-Gaussianity.
