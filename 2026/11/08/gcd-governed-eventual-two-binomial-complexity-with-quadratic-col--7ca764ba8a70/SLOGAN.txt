For the binary periodic word \((0^a1^b)^\omega\), two-binomial complexity eventually attains its full period \(a+b\) at every nonperiod-multiple length, but equals \(a+b-\gcd(a,b)+1\) at period multiples. This exact gcd deficit coexists with transient collisions persisting for quadratically many complete periods, so no subquadratic uniform stabilization threshold is possible.
