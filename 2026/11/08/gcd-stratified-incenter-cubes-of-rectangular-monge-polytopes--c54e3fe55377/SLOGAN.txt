The intrinsic Frobenius inradius of every unequal \(p\)-by-\(q\) normalized Monge polytope has a sharp closed formula with a \(\gcd(p,q)^2\) correction. Its entire incenter set is an affine cube of dimension \(\gcd(p,q)-1\), so coprime rectangles have a unique incenter while noncoprime rectangles have a positive-dimensional family.
