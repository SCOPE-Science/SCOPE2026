# Mathematical review

The unrestricted generator criterion of Longstaff–Rosenthal is reconstructed for each covering corner and applied to pairwise distinct diagonal words. Height two ensures radical square zero and independent idempotency constraints, giving the precise \(q^h-q\) edge factor. The star generating function follows from elementary-symmetric enumeration of labeled distinct leaves; the two relevant obstruction probabilities and all their intersections are bounded explicitly, establishing both asymptotic regimes. Full direct algebra-generation enumeration and an independent weighted enumeration reproduce small counts. Finite checks are not presented as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to investigated literature
Longstaff–Rosenthal (2000) proves the generator criterion but does not count idempotent tuples. Kolegov (2024) states an exact minimum number of idempotent generators and a hypercube criterion, so that threshold is excluded from the new claim. Kolegov (2025) counts unrestricted random generator tuples, and cannot by itself supply the conditional idempotent distribution: it requires different per-edge factors and a weighted binary-code assignment. The new complete height-two enumeration and star field-size crossover were not found in the accessible sources or searched mathematical records. ## Mathematical value — PASS
Counting random idempotent generators is a natural refinement of generation rank for incidence algebras. The exact law supplies probabilities at every tuple length and every prime-power field for an infinite family, and identifies a genuine change in the leading failure mechanism between the binary field and larger fields. Neither the minimum-generator threshold nor a generic unrestricted-generation count gives the precise crossover law.
