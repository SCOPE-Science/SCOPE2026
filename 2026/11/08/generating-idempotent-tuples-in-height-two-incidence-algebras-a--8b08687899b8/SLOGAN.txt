For every finite height-two poset over \(\mathbb F_q\), ordered idempotent generating tuples admit an exact weighted binary-Hamming embedding formula, and star posets have a closed coefficient enumerator. Among random idempotent tuples in a fixed star, failure is dominated by a single defective edge over \(\mathbb F_2\), but by a collision of two leaf diagonal words for every fixed \(q\ge3\).
