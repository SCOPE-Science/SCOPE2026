Every generic central hyperplane through a type-\(D_d\) root polytope, for \(d\ge4\), yields the same combinatorial section, with \(4d(d-1)(d-2)/3\) vertices and \(2^d+2d-6\) facets. The regular \(24\)-cell consequently has a generic central section combinatorially equivalent to the chamfered cube: six quadrilaterals, twelve hexagons, and \(32\) vertices.
