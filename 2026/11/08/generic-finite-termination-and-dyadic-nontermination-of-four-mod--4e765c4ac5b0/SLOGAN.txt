For nuclear-scaled polar descent on a \(4\times4\) isotropic quadratic at step \(1/4\), almost every initial matrix reaches the exact minimizer in finite time, despite the method being analyzed previously mainly through linear rates. The full-rank spectra \((3,1+t,1,1-t)\) exhibit an exact logarithmic stopping-time law with nonterminating resonances at inverse powers of two.
