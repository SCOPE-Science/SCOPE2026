For a Zariski-general genus-nine hyperelliptic étale Klein cover of a genus-three hyperelliptic curve in the five-parameter type-I.1 family, the full geometric automorphism group is exactly \((\mathbb Z/2)^3\). An exhaustive characteristic-719 branch-stabilizer certificate proves this on a rational specialization and hence generically, recovering the fixed-point-free deck group intrinsically from the top curve.
