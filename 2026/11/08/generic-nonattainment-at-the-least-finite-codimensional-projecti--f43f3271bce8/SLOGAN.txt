For any finite collection of pairwise singular probability measures on a perfect compact space, the common kernel in continuous functions has projection constant exactly \(2\), but a minimizer exists precisely when their closed supports are disjoint. At every codimension at least two, attaining and nonattaining tuples are both dense while nonattainment is generic; a concrete interval example additionally has the optimal inverse-tolerance order of Lipschitz blow-up for near-minimal projections.
