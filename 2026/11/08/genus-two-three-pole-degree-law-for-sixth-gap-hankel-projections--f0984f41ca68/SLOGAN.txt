For every \(n\ge7\), the generic rank-two Hankel completion degree from anti-diagonals \(\{1,2,6,n\}\) is \(2n-7+\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor-\mathbf1_{\{n\equiv2\pmod4\}}\). Its first three observations define a genus-two curve and the final coordinate has three explicit poles, with a period-four leading-term cancellation at one of them.
