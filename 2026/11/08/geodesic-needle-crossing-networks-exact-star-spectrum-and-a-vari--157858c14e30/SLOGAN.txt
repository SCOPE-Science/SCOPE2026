Independent isotropic shorter great-circle arcs have an exact crossing-degree mixture and full star spectrum determined by their length law. Their crossing-count variance scales as \(n^3\) precisely when arc lengths have positive variance, but as \(n^2\) for deterministic length; uniform-endpoint arcs produce the explicit limiting degree density \(2\pi\sin(4\pi x)\) on \((0,1/4)\).
