Every family of vertex-disjoint maximal geodesics of a complete multipartite graph has an exact directed-array enumerator, and maximal families are identified solely from the unused part sizes. For parts of size two, the maximal-packing sizes form the complete interval \(\lceil r/2\rceil\) through \(\lfloor2r/3\rfloor\), making the geodesic-packing complex pure exactly for \(r\in\{2,3,4,5,7\}\).
