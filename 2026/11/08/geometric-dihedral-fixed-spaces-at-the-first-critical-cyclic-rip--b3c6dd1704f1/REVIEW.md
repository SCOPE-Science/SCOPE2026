# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The quotient-barycenter identification is a homeomorphism for geometric fixed points. Every admissible orbit is enumerated exactly; the even and odd reflection cases are proven using sharp graph-distance bounds, and the rotation cases follow from orbit diameters. Each resulting flag complex has its claimed explicit homotopy type. The rational character is obtained from the finite-order simplicial Lefschetz formula and the established ordinary homotopy calculation. Executable finite checks reconstruct all dihedral elements for 23 parameter choices.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Adamaszek's original paper determines the ordinary cyclic-power clique homotopy type; Park's recent full arXiv paper studies random circle-sample homotopy distributions, not geometric fixed spaces. The closed scale convention is treated separately from the almost-sure strict-scale random theorem. A relevant earlier scale-two stable regime does not imply the critical \(n=3r\) all-parameter distinction. published-finding corpus and historical-claim searches found no equivalent dihedral fixed-space classification, but unindexed equivariant literature is still possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This completely classifies fixed spaces of all dihedral elements on the first non-circle critical Rips family, including a loop occurring as a fixed space of an ambient two-sphere wedge. It detects reflection-axis geometry invisible to ordinary homotopy and upgrades the known sphere rank to the full rational dihedral character. This is a natural infinite critical family, not a finite parameter increment.

**Limitations.** No full equivariant homotopy type, all-subgroup fixed-space system, or integral module decomposition is claimed; first public 2026 source motivates the critical-circle setting, while ordinary sphere-wedge classification is credited to older literature. Finite computations are not the proof.
