At the first critical circle scale, every dihedral element of the cyclic Rips complex has an explicitly determined geometric fixed space: order-three rotations fix isolated points, while reflections yield a circle, two points, or a contractible complex according to parity and axis. These fixed spaces also determine the complete rational dihedral character on the second homology of the known sphere wedge \(\bigvee^{r-1}S^2\).
