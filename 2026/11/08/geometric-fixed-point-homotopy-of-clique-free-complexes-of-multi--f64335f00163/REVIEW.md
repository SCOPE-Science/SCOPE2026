# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The clique condition is exactly the count of occupied multipartition classes. For a finite group action the barycentric-coordinate fixed-point characterization gives the vertex-orbit complex, including points centered on nontrivial orbits. Every orbit meets all classes of its part orbit and no others. The simplicial contiguity collapse retains exactly one orbit per class-orbit and produces the weighted threshold complex. The minimum-weight star is contractible; every remaining simplex has its entire boundary in that star, and the quotient is a bouquet with explicitly indexed dimensions and multiplicities. This is an integral homotopy proof, not an inference from sampled Betti numbers. The standalone 124-case computation independently corroborates the claim, including empty and mixed-dimensional cases.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations

The February 2026 primary work of Ghosh–Selvaraja is a paper on shellability/decomposability of clique-free complexes, not arbitrary subgroup fixed-point homotopy types. Its abstract, introduction, relevant theorem statements and sections on chordal graphs, clique attachments and ideals were inspected; targeted text searches found no multipartite equivariant theorem. Standard nonequivariant skeleton topology, contiguity, and elementary star/quotient arguments are acknowledged rather than claimed original. Search comparisons found no equivalent or dominating result for **all** automorphism subgroups, especially those interchanging equal-sized graph parts. An unindexed treatment of weighted threshold complexes or graph fixed loci is a residual risk, and a failed search is not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The formula provides a complete invariant of the actual geometric fixed spaces over all subgroups of a natural infinite class. It is useful specifically because taking geometric fixed points does not equal simply taking fixed graph vertices; within-part permutations and part permutations affect topology in very different ways. The precise mixed-dimensional instance \(S^1\vee2S^2\) inside an ambient \(15S^3\) gives a substantive topological contrast, while the same theorem determines emptiness, contractibility and integral homology for all parameter values. It is not merely an increment in a single small numerical invariant.

## Closest literature and limits

R. Ghosh and S Selvaraja, *Shellability in Clique-Free Complexes of Graphs*, arXiv:2602.09623v1, 2026-02-10. Classical nonequivariant simplex-skeleton homotopy is recovered but not newly asserted; the retained contribution is the all-subgroup geometric fixed-point classification. Results do not supply a compatible equivariant system of wedge decompositions or any normalizer action. The mathematical record may be incomplete.
