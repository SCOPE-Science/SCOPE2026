# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The geometric fixed-point orbit homeomorphism is derived from barycentric coordinates, including forbidden orbits containing graph edges. Reflection orbit models are determined by explicit axis and layer parities. The open-ladder homotopy recurrence uses a contractible vertex link and a valid graph fold; its base cases fix the integer sphere dimensions. The two half-turn models are respectively the ordinary circular and Möbius ladders, whose homotopy types are taken from Agarwal--Basak, Theorems 12 and 15. Finite checker results confirm the quotient graph *edge sets* and all mod-two Betti numbers in the stated ranges, without replacing the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The motivating paper is A. Agarwal and B. Basak, arXiv:2608.30601v2 (first circular-ladder version 2026-09-14); the closest public research record is *Complete dihedral and layer-swap homology representations of circular ladders* (2026-10-11). The first determines nonequivariant homotopy types; the second determines rational homology traces using signed face counts and explicitly describes a plain-reflection path of column orbits. Neither supplies the complete homotopy types of the geometric fixed spaces, including the layer-flipping reflections and the two half-turns. Most importantly, the published rational character cannot distinguish the \(S^3\) and \(S^1\) fixed spaces of the two reflection axes at \(n=12\). Targeted equivalence and stronger-coverage searches found no matching all-involution result. Residual risks are unindexed equivariant graph-topology work or implicit derivation in a stronger source not retrieved. The elementary general orbit lemma and previously published ordinary homotopy classifications are explicitly credited.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The involution-fixed spaces are a natural equivariant invariant of a symmetric family newly classified nonequivariantly in 2026. The table covers every conjugacy type of order-two symmetry for infinitely many graph sizes, gives exact integral homotopy types and includes all boundary cases. The contrast between same rational trace and different fixed-point dimensions supplies concrete mathematical information not recoverable from the available full homology character; it is not merely an Euler-characteristic recomputation.

## Limits
No classification of fixed spaces of higher-order subgroups, full equivariant homotopy equivalence or compatible subgroup restriction diagram is claimed. The two half-turn homotopy types depend on the published ordinary ladder results; the standalone finite verifier supports, but does not replace, the structural proof. Independent mathematical validation is unperformed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
