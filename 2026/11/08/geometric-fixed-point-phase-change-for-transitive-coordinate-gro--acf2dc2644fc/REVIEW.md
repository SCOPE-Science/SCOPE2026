# Scientific assessment: transitive-coordinate fixed spaces of hypercube Rips complexes

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is an exact PL classification for every integer \(n\ge1\) and every subgroup of \(S_n\) transitive on coordinate positions. The proof passes from actual geometric fixed points to orbit barycenters by uniqueness of nonnegative barycentric coordinates. Every admissible orbit is forced to lie in weight zero, one, \(n-1\), or \(n\). In intermediate weights, the full weight-preserving orbit would be a clique of a Johnson graph; the elementary star/top lemma and the fact that a transitive group has no proper nonempty invariant coordinate subset make this impossible. Exact Hamming-distance comparisons give a filled segment, filled triangle, four-cycle, or two disjoint filled edges. The resulting integral homology follows directly from the explicit complexes.

A separate exact enumerator tested 32 generated transitive groups in dimensions \(1\) through \(8\), computed orbit-face complexes and mod-two Betti numbers, and matched their face counts and predicted types. This is corroboration, not the proof for all groups and dimensions. No unsupported equivariant homotopy or torsion conclusion is asserted.

## Originality — PASS, with stated risk

The closest primary full-text results for this exact ambient object are Galetto–Montaño–Wellner, arXiv:2606.20784, whose Theorem 5.4 describes rational hyperoctahedral representations of **ambient homology**, and Bendersky–Elia–Grbić, arXiv:2605.00705, on **ambient cohomology and connectivity**. Adamaszek–Adams, arXiv:2103.01040, gives the **ordinary** scale-two Rips sphere-wedge type. No such ambient invariant implies the PL types of all transitive-subgroup fixed-point spaces. The proof also uses a classical Johnson-clique star/top lemma, without claiming that lemma as new.

Previously available fixed-point classifications for translations and individual coordinate swaps concern different subgroup actions. In particular, the coordinate-transposition subgroup in degree greater than two is not coordinate-transitive, and a nonzero translation is not a pure coordinate permutation. Searches of the relevant published mathematical record did not reveal the stated uniform four-case PL classification. Search absence is not a mathematical uniqueness proof. Residual risk is an equivalent, unindexed classical observation involving Johnson cliques and orbit barycenters.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The scale-two cube Rips complexes are a concrete, actively studied family with ambient homotopy concentrated in degree three, yet their coordinate-transitive geometric fixed spaces are circles in the smallest nontrivial case and disconnected from dimension four onward. The classification holds uniformly for every transitive permutation group, including imprimitive groups, and identifies the sharp boundary between \(n=3\) and \(n=4\). Unlike a homology-character calculation, it determines the entire PL type and all integral fixed-space homology. Such fixed spaces are natural basic invariants in equivariant topology, and their independence of the particular transitive subgroup is a structural statement rather than an arbitrary finite table entry.

## Scope, closest literature, and limitations

The result is restricted to inclusive scale two and coordinate permutations. The argument does not handle coordinate sign flips/translations or larger Rips scales, and no quotient-space or complete equivariant homotopy classification is claimed. The finite verifier checks selected groups only; unindexed equivalent formulations cannot be ruled out. Independent mathematical review has not been performed.
