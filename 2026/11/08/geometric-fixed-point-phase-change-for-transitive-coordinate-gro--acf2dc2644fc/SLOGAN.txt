Every coordinate-transitive subgroup of \(S_n\) has the same geometric fixed-point space on the inclusive scale-two Rips complex of the binary \(n\)-cube: a segment for \(n=1\), a filled triangle for \(n=2\), a circle for \(n=3\), and two disjoint segments for every \(n\ge4\). The sharp circle-to-disconnected transition is a PL classification, not an inference from ordinary Rips homology or homological symmetry characters.
