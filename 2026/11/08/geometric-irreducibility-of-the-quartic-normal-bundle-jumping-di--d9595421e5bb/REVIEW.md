# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The normal-bundle condition is the exact sixteen-by-sixteen determinant of Iwasaki's cohomology map in characteristic zero. Three explicit determinant evaluations rule out identically vanishing quartic-coefficient fibers over the coprime quadratic-pencil locus. A geometrically integral plane-quartic section, certified by an exact finite-field Jacobian computation, gives an integral fiber and hence an integral generic fiber. The vertical-component argument then proves a single reduced component on the honest-map parameter space; restriction to the nonempty unramified locus preserves integrality. The published determinant construction, openness and nonemptiness are explicitly cited inputs.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** Proposition 6.7 and Appendix A of Iwasaki, arXiv:2609.39309v1, establish a nonempty Cartier divisor, its class and one smooth point, but do not establish global geometric irreducibility. Cela–Lian, arXiv:2607.01432, treat general normal-bundle/interpolation properties, rather than this jumping divisor's irreducible components. Comparison searches found no equivalent or stronger statement. Cela–Lian's complete manuscript could not be inspected, so an indirect implication remains a specific historical-priority risk, not evidence of coverage.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Irreducibility and reducedness decide whether the exceptional normal-bundle wall is a single geometric degeneration family or several components of the same computed class. This resolves a structural aspect not established by nonemptiness, a class formula, or the existence of one smooth point, and matters in studying the boundary and intersection theory of this explicitly motivated family.

The result is restricted to characteristic zero and the unramified fixed-source family, not the quasimap boundary. The exact computational checks replayed successfully; the generic geometric argument is independent of finite sampling.
