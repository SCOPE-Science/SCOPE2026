# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. Stationary-entry conditioning makes the common middle word uniformly onto the other \(n-1\) types. Exact Stirling ratios give all fixed joint singleton-event probabilities. A fixed-parameter zero-truncated Poisson local limit sends these ratios to powers of \(e^{-\tau_a}\). The first finitely many outgoing symbols are distinct with probability tending to one; fresh-symbol rescues are negligible for fixed shifts. Consequently the cluster survival function tends to the geometric survival function. The mean follows separately from the exact stationary mass/flux identity and the same Stirling ratio asymptotic. The standalone finite checker passed all 134 assertions. The local-limit theorem, rather than enumeration, controls the infinite parameter range.

## Originality

PASS on available evidence, pending independent priority assessment. Long (arXiv:2604.26298v1) explicitly develops entry flux, rare first-entrance times and occupancy local-limit estimates, but not the Palm distribution of consecutive complete-window run lengths for linear windows. The subcritical logarithmic-window cluster law does not imply the fixed-linear-window geometric law: both the normalization and limiting family differ. Targeted searches for onto-window run durations, surjection clusters, and linear expiring collectors yielded no directly covering statement. The main residual risk is older unindexed scan-statistics or occupancy-run literature.

## Value

PASS. Cluster lengths are the natural local counterpart to the paper's stationary entry flux: two models with the same entry frequency need not have the same temporal organization of visits. The result completely identifies the limiting Palm run-length distribution for every fixed linear window ratio \(a>1\), including its parameter dependence through the standard surjection saddle point; it is not just a known-table calculation or a finite special case. The exact mean serves as a consistency check, not the primary contribution.

## Scope and limitations

The theorem is for uniform iid coupon draws and deterministic windows \(\lfloor an\rfloor\) with fixed \(a>1\). It concerns stationary entrance-conditioned runs, not time-stationary size-biased runs, not the distribution of the first hitting time, and not arbitrary growing \(a\). The primary paper is publicly available. Historical priority beyond the sources and indexed searches inspected remains open.
