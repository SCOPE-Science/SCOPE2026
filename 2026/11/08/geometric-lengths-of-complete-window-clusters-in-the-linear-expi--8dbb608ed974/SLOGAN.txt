For the expiring coupon collector with window length \(\lfloor an\rfloor\), \(a>1\), a fresh run of fully covered windows has an asymptotically geometric duration with parameter \(e^{-\tau_a}\), where \(\tau_a/(1-e^{-\tau_a})=a\). This gives the local clump-duration law complementing the known exponentially long first-arrival time.
