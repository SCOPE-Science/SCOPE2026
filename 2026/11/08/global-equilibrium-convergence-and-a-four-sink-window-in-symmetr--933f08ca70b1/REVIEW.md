The model was introduced to study changes from cyclic competition to fixed-point and periodic behavior. On the equal-competition slice, an all-initial-data convergence theorem is stronger than local equilibrium stability or selected trajectories: it rules out every oscillatory or non-equilibrium recurrent outcome at any positive mutation rate, including nonhyperbolic parameter boundaries. The exact four-sink window is biologically interpretable coexistence of four alternative stationary population compositions rather than an arbitrary parameter sample, and global convergence guarantees that the competing attractors are actual long-term endpoints rather than only local linearization artifacts.
