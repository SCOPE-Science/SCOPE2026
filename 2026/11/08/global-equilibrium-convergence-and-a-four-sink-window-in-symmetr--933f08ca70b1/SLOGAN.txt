For the three-species May–Leonard flow with equal cross-competition \(a>0\) and symmetric positive mutation \(\mu>0\), every nonzero nonnegative trajectory converges to one equilibrium; no sustained recurrence survives on this symmetric slice. When \(a>1\), the exact interval \(\frac{a-1}{3(2a+1)}<\mu<\frac{a-1}{4a+2\sqrt{2(a+1)}}\) contains exactly four hyperbolic sinks, and the global theorem ensures all trajectories still settle at a fixed point.
