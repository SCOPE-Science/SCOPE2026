The discrete linear-response plankton map \((x,y)\mapsto(x(2-x-y),\,y(1-r+\gamma x))\) has no biologically nonnegative prime-two cycle for **any** \(r,\gamma>0\), even beyond the positive equilibrium's Neimark–Sacker instability threshold. An exact two-step prey ordering forces predator abundance to rise, whereas the predator multipliers force it to fall.
