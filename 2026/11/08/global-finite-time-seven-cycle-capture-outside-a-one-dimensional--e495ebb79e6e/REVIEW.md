## Mathematical value — PASS

Exact full-plane basin measure and the sharp Hausdorff dimension of noncaptured starting points are natural dynamical invariants for this published invariant-graph example. The theorem supplies a complete answer on an uncountable arithmetic class, with a geometric half-line accounting for the nonzero exceptional dimension, and does not merely renormalize the graphwise periodic-point census. It distinguishes planar Lebesgue-generic behavior from graphwise Lebesgue-generic behavior, an issue raised by the original papers. The remaining rational denominator-divisibility cases, uniform capture-time tails, and geometry of individual preimage components are unresolved.
