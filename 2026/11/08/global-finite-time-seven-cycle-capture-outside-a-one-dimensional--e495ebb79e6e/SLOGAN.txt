For the dissipative planar map \(F_b(x,y)=(|x|-y-1,x-|y|+b)\), almost every starting point reaches its attracting seven-cycle in finite time whenever \(b\le-2\) is irrational or has reduced rational denominator not divisible by \(15\). The exact noncaptured set is the fixed point and all preimages of the known repelling seven-cycle, and its Hausdorff dimension is exactly \(1\).
