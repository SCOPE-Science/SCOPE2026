For the positive rational negative-feedback limit map \(F(x,y)=(y,b/(1+x^m))\), every positive orbit converges to the fixed point when \(m\le1\) or \(b\le m(m-1)^{-1-1/m}\) with \(m>1\); above that threshold there is exactly one attracting and one saddle four-cycle, with explicit basins and invariant measures. At equality, all positive trajectories still converge and satisfy an exact signed four-phase \(n^{-1/2}\) law.
