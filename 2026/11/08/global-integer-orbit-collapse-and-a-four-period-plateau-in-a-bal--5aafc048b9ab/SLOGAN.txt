For the balanced integer-parameter ultradiscrete predator–prey map \(F_c(x,y)=(1+x-\max\{0,x,y\},\ y+\max\{0,c+x\}-c)\), every integer orbit except the isolated fixed state \((0,1)\) reaches a single primitive cycle of period \(18,19,22,24\) as \(c=1,2,3,\ge4\). The period and complete cycle stabilize for all \(c\ge4\), while at \(c=1\) the hitting time from \((0,N)\) is exactly \(N^2+2N+2\).
