At the singular parity transition of a four-species competitive cycle, every strictly positive orbit converges exponentially to its uniquely conserved-level-selected coexistence state when \(1<a<\rho\), where \(\rho\approx1.574743073887\). The unique positive diagonal relative-entropy certificate, up to scale, exists exactly for \(1<a\le\rho\); this sharp certificate boundary is not asserted to be a dynamical instability threshold.
