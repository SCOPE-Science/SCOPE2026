For the fear–refuge predator–prey flow with saturating prey recruitment and quadratic predator self-interference, the predator invasion threshold gives the entire global phase portrait with no extra parameter inequality. At the exact threshold the predator always disappears as \(v(t)\sim 1/(Ct)\), and the prey's deficit has the universal amplitude \(U-u(t)\sim B/(ACt)\), with explicit positive model-dependent \(A,B,C\).
