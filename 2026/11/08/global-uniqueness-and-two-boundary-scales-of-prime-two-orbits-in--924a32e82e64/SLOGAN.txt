For every nonnegative-coefficient generalized Allee-logistic cubic, the first primitive two-cycle is the only positive primitive two-cycle at **every** subsequent growth rate, irrespective of any later higher-period bifurcations. Its approach to the two state-space endpoints changes from powers \(r^{-1},r^{-2}\) to \(r^{-1/2},r^{-3/2}\) precisely when the linear-growth coefficient vanishes; high-growth limits describe the polynomial, not necessarily a globally invariant population interval.
