In the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\), every globally minimal defensive alliance is either a disjoint equal-shore pair of size \(t=\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\), or an equal-shore pair of size \(t+1\) with at least three common indices. Locally minimal alliances admit exactly one additional intersection threshold (two), yielding complete two-term counting polynomials and a precise family of configurations that are locally but not globally minimal.
