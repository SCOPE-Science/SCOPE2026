Every cyclotomic pole is generically simple and uncanceled. A two-block independent coefficient-variation argument makes the universal derivative incidence smooth and hence generic roots simple; a separate two-equation incidence excludes cut-locus roots. Riemann–Hurwitz forces the sharp count and Zariski openness transfers it to general projector data. The exact SymPy verifier checks five genuine projector witnesses. The proof handles all n_i>=2, d>=2 including shared poles, and finite checks are not treated as proof.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The GD degree is a natural enumerative distance-complexity invariant for an algebraic model inside a Grassmannian. The exact all-parameter formula settles its behavior for the full family of rational-normal-scroll ruling curves, including unequal block sizes and nontrivial cyclotomic overlap; equality is not automatic for arbitrary Grassmannian subvarieties, as the motivating paper's GD/ED contrasts demonstrate. The proof isolates why the constraint to genuine idempotent projectors produces no critical-point loss, a structural fact beyond numerical restatement of ED.
