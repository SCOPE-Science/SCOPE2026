For every rational-normal-scroll ruling curve with at least two blocks of degree at least two, restricting distance data to genuine rank-matched orthogonal projectors causes no loss of complex stationary points away from the algebraic cut locus. Its Grassmann distance degree equals the known Euclidean distance degree, \(2\deg\operatorname{lcm}_i(1+t^2+\cdots+t^{2n_i})-2\), even for unequal block degrees with overlapping cyclotomic factors.
