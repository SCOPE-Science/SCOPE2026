Every balanced rational-normal-scroll ruling curve \(\mathcal R_{n,d}\subset\operatorname{Gr}(d,d(n+1))\), for \(n,d\ge2\), has Grassmann distance degree exactly \(4n-2\), equal to its previously known Euclidean distance degree. A full-rank Jacobian calculation for a two-block projector family shows why restricting the data to the Grassmannian causes no critical-point loss in this family.
