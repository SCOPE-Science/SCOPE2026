In Page–Rényi dimer parking on a line, the last successful arrival time, centered by \(\log n-2\), converges to a standard Gumbel law. The total number of attempted placements, divided by \(n\) and centered the same way, has the identical limit because the number of still-available dimers at time \(\log n+x\) converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(e^{-2-x})\).
