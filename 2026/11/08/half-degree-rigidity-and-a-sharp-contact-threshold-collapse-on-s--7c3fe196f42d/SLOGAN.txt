At the half-degree contact threshold, every initially primitive-three Greenberg–Hastings state on a connected even-regular graph is a globally rotating, balanced proper three-coloring, equivalently a cube-root-valued adjacency eigenvector. On squared cycles, threshold two leaves exactly six three-periodic configurations when the circumference is divisible by three and none otherwise, whereas threshold one supports at least \(3\cdot2^{n/3}\) when \(3\mid n\) and \(n\ge6\): an exact contact-threshold collapse of periodic-wave population.
