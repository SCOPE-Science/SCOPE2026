For any half-sized subset of \(\mathbb F_2^r\) with \(r\ge5\), four distinct elements fail to realize some target sum if and only if the subset is an affine hyperplane. At \(r=4\), exactly \(840\) additional nonhyperplane examples occur, each omitting one target and arising as the full lift of an affinely independent four-set in a three-dimensional quotient.
