A power-law reset clock with infinite mean dephases every nonresonant coherence of a discrete quantum gate at the sharp rate \(t^{-\min\{\alpha,1-\alpha\}}\), with two competing renewal-age contributions meeting at \(\alpha=1/2\). For a phase-flip qubit, the coherence alternates in sign at late times exactly when \(\alpha\le1/2\), even though the averaged state approaches the maximally mixed state.
