When Friedman reinforcement is applied with probability \(\kappa/(\log n)^\theta\), \(1/2\le\theta\le1\), the two-colour urn converges to equal proportions but retains a nondegenerate random amplitude after an explicit deterministic rescaling. At the sharp boundary \(\theta=1/2\), unequal totals of the two replacement rules create an additional \(\log\log n\) correction absent above that boundary.
