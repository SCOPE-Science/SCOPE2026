In a driven underdamped harmonic trap restricted to two finite position jumps, the first oscillatory half-period uniquely optimizes both mean energy extraction and the chance of extraction for every fixed extracting amplitude. As friction vanishes, the mean-optimal pulse approaches the unrestricted work benchmark, its variance stays within the universal factor \(\pi^2/8\) of the ideal-protocol variance, and non-extraction becomes exponentially rare with a sharp prefactor.
