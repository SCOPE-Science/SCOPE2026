# Review of the half-rank Cartier statement

## Correctness
**PASS (self-review).** A tame quotient identifies global invariant differentials. The binomial-support Cartier matrix is an exact partial permutation; the residue involution induced by \(p^2\equiv-1\pmod{2n}\) pairs its active and inactive rows, and passage to quotient invariants halves the count. Therefore the first Cartier rank and Jacobian \(a\)-number both equal \((n-1)/4\). Cartier square-zero follows from the same congruence, while exact modular computations independently verify six example pairs. Computation is supplementary to the uniform proof.

## Originality
**PASS with historical-priority qualification.** Borges–Reis (2026) already proves maximality and the geometric automorphism classification for the same family. Tafazolian–Top (2019) already constructs the Dickson quotient. Garcia–Tafazolian (2008) already implies square-zero Cartier action for maximal curves over \(\mathbb F_{p^4}\). Those statements are not claimed as new. The identified addition is the precise first rank and \(a\)-number, not implied merely by square-zero or supersingularity. The closest 2019 Cartier computations concern another defining family, while a 2025 Chebyshev paper treats curves without the extra branch factor. Same-object sources were inspected, but a related 2025 publisher full text was not inspected, leaving a residual priority risk.

## Value
**PASS.** The \(a\)-number is a natural local group-scheme invariant that is not determined by maximality or by a Newton polygon of slope one-half. The uniform formula supplies a previously unrecorded p-torsion invariant for geometrically low-symmetry maximal curves, with explicit nonsuperspecial genus-six examples in infinitely many prime characteristics.

## Limitations
Only the stated congruence family and first Cartier rank are calculated; no complete Ekedahl–Oort classification or independent mathematical certification is asserted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
