For each odd \(n>5\) and prime \(p>3\) with \(p^2\equiv-1\pmod{2n}\), the maximal Dickson curve \(y^2=(x+2)D_n(x)\) has first Cartier rank and Jacobian \(a\)-number both equal to \((n-1)/4\), while its Cartier square vanishes. For \(n=13\), this gives maximal genus-six curves with \(a=3\) and geometric automorphism group \(C_2\) in infinitely many prime characteristics; the maximality and automorphism statements themselves were already known.
