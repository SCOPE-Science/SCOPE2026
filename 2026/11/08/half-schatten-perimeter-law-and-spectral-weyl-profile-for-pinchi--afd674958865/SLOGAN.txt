For any fixed nonconstant Laurent polynomial \(\phi\), the positive self-commutator of multiplication by \(\phi\) on a pinching annular Hardy space has a universal hyperbolic-secant spectral profile whose angular factor is \(|\phi'|^2\). At the critical Schatten exponent \(p=\tfrac12\), its limiting half-power trace equals **half the parametrized length of the boundary curve** \(\phi(e^{i\theta})\); all other fixed exponents follow an exact gamma-function law.
