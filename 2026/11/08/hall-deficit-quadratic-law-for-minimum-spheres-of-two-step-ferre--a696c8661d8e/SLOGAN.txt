For two-step Ferrers root polytopes obtained by deleting one biclique, the squared radius of the minimum Euclidean sphere drops by an explicit quadratic Hall-deficit term \(h(\max\{0,a/p+b/q-1\})^2\). Exactly at \(a/p+b/q=1\), the contact pattern changes from every edge root touching to only the two cross-edge classes touching, with an explicit positive clearance for the remaining vertices.
