A binary Fourier pupil in a Schur-preconditioned Tikhonov saddle system creates an idempotent transformed operator: standard GMRES reaches an exact, often nonzero residual plateau after one step, and permanently mask-restricted flexible updates cannot eliminate the discarded-mode residual. Any strictly positive transmission in every omitted mode restores two-step exact-arithmetic termination, at the cost of condition numbers that diverge inversely with the transmission.
