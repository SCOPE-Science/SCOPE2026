For every prime characteristic \(p\ge7\), the middle multiplication map by the cube of \(xy(x-y)(x-\lambda y)\) in \(k[x,y]/(x^p,y^p)\) has generic rank \(p-6\), but exactly rank \(p-7\) at each harmonic cross-ratio \(\lambda=-1,2,1/2\). Its determinant defines a nontrivial parameter divisor of exact degree \(3(p-6)\), so the nilpotent Jordan type of quartic multiplication varies even among quartics with four distinct linear roots.
