For uniformly sampled nonzero nilpotents in the consecutive-diagonal rook inverse monoid, the nilpotency index converges to the heavy-tailed law \(\Pr(K=j)=1/[j(j-1)]\) for \(j\ge2\). Its finite mean is \(\log n+2\gamma-5/6+O(n^{-1/2})\), while the variance grows as \(\pi^2 n/12\); exact finite spectra and rare linear-scale tails follow from one divisor-weighted count.
