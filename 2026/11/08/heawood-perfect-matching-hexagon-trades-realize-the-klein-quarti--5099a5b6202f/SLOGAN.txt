The 24 perfect matchings of the Heawood graph, joined by trades around any alternating six-cycle, form the 24-vertex distance-regular Klein quartic graph, with a canonical bijection to Coxeter seven-cycles respecting their common-two-path adjacency. Restricting trades to the seven faces of the natural toroidal embedding instead gives exactly \(3K_1\sqcup 3C_7\), exposing how the 21 nonfacial hexagons restore connectivity.
