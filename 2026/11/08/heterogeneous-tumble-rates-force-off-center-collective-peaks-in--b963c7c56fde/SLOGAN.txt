For two logarithmically repelling run-and-tumble particles in a harmonic well, unequal orientation-reversal rates create off-center collective-density peaks even when the total tumble rate is large. In the full twice-differentiable regime \(a+b>3\), an individual rate below trap relaxation makes the center a strict local minimum, whereas both rates at least as large make it the unique global maximum; the threshold is independent of repulsive strength.
