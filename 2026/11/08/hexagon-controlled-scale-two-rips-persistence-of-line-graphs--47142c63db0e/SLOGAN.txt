At inclusive Rips scale two, the line graph of a girth-six graph with edge-disjoint hexagons loses one primitive circle and gains one two-sphere for every hexagon. If \(b\) is the original graph's cycle rank and \(q\) its number of hexagons, the exact integral homotopy type is \(\bigvee^{b-q}S^1\vee\bigvee^qS^2\), with the change explained by precisely two new Rips triangles per hexagon.
