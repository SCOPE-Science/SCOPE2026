For every finite connected graph of girth at least six, the graph-metric scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex of its line graph has a two-cell CW model: attach two disks along every source six-cycle, even when hexagons overlap arbitrarily. Equivalently it is the graph with one disk per hexagon, wedged with one additional two-sphere per hexagon; the complete integral first- and second-homology formulas follow from the six-cycle lattice.
