The ordinary matching complex of the hexagonal prism \(C_6\square K_2\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{12}S^3\), and its complete rational \(D_6\times C_2\) homology representation has exactly one invariant dimension. Consequently, its full symmetry quotient has the rational cohomology ring of \(S^3\), despite not being asserted homotopy equivalent to a sphere.
