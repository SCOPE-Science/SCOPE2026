On a three-color prism automaton, vanishing circulation around every square does not guarantee synchronization: longitudinal winding can sustain excitation without any locally charged square. Among square-flat initial colorings on an \(n\)-rung prism, the synchronization fraction is asymptotic to \(3/\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2n}\), where \(\sigma^2=5/4-19\sqrt{33}/132\), and typical excitation activity is of order \(n^{-1/2}\).
