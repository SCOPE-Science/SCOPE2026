# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

Each commuting pair of nonzero rank-one square-zero matrices has both ordered products zero: equality of two nonzero rank-one products would force equal image lines, contradicting square-zero. Consequently all tuple maps factor through the common quotient by their joint image span. This is a bijective, not merely surjective, Grassmannian parameterization. Inclusion-exclusion correctly excludes zero image vectors in a surjective coordinate matrix; dividing by independent scaling accounts for unique rank-one operators. The geometric strata are relative open subvarieties in products of smooth nonzero rank-one loci over the Grassmannian, and the degree calculations match the exact finite-field count. Exhaustive small-field matrix multiplication and span-rank checks agree. Infinite-family assertions use the proof, not finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS, provisional

The closest earlier results concern which similarity classes commute (Britnell–Wildon), restricted commuting varieties in low dimension (Ngo), simultaneous similarity orbit counts rather than labeled tuples (Sharma), and dimension bounds for broader mutual-annihilation incidence varieties (Levy–Ngo–Šivic, especially Lemma 3.3). The latter provides an important common-span technique, so no claim to that technique itself is made. Mutually annihilating matrix-pair research (Huang) and an earlier labeled pair-count within a broader rank spectrum do not imply the stated exact higher-tuple count without additional simultaneous image-span arguments. Published record and full-text inspection did not yield the exact formula, but an unidentified general tuple generating series could subsume it; historical priority is provisional, not certified. The full text of Huang's paper was unavailable in this review, so no full-text exclusion is asserted.

## Value — PASS

Counting commuting ordered tuples constrained to the minimal nilpotent orbit is a natural higher-commutativity probability. The result yields an exact distribution by intrinsic joint image-span rank for every finite field and every tuple length at least three, plus a geometric explanation of the parity-dependent top-dimensional component multiplicity. This sharpens existence/dimension and pair-only statements. The mathematical contribution lies in the uniform refined count and top-component transition, not in rebranding a routine special case of a known closed formula.
