# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every n≥6 the squarefree Koszul multigraded cellular cochain model of Z_{C_n} decomposes on a proper k-support I, 2≤k≤n−2, into an exact-ended three-group complex whose H^{k+1} is reduced H⁰(C_n[I]) with the determinant twist. The signed stable-support Lefschetz trace δ_I(g)(f_I(g)−1−Σ_{setwise fixed e} ε_e(g)) follows from alternating cochain traces. Fixed subset counts give rotations of order d: −(−1)^{(d−1)k/d}binom(n/d,k/d) when d|k and zero otherwise, and the three explicit reflection generating polynomials from the vertex/pair orbits. Direct independent enumeration of connected components and signed fixed supports matched these closed character formulas for 260 (n,g,k) instances n=6..10; this is corroboration, not proof for all n. Rational transfer averages the characters. The top Hochster summand ∼ H¹(C_n) carries sign(permutation)×polygon orientation degree, trivial on D_n iff n≡3 mod4. Equivariant Poincaré pairing then gives quotient rational duality exactly in that congruence class. The proof derives the uniform n formulas algebraically; finite tests do not substitute.

## originality

PASS

The Hochster decomposition and polygon Betti numbers are classical, while a separately published lower-degree dihedral computation covers degrees 3 and 4 only. This final claim begins at degree 5 and computes all higher trace coefficients, rational orbit Betti numbers and top orientation/Poincaré criterion. Li Yu and Panov study torus/vertex-partition quotients, distinct from ordinary finite dihedral coordinate permutation quotients. The most plausible 2022 Lü–Zhang article concerns polygon quotient cohomology but its exact finite-group action and implications could not be checked beyond an abstract: best-knowledge O PASS with a material, explicit risk, not a claim that the unseen full paper is noncovering.
