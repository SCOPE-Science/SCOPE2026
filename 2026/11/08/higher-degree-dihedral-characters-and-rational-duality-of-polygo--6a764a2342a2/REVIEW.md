# Scientific review of higher-degree polygonal dihedral characters

## Correctness
**PASS.** The squarefree Koszul model is natural under coordinate permutations, including its exterior reordering signs. Every proper induced subgraph of the polygon is a forest, so each fixed-support cochain block has cohomology only in the middle degree, where its trace is the alternating trace of the three cochain modules. The rotation and reflection counts are algebraically exact. The top-degree orientation character follows from the full-support Hochster summand. Rational transfer and invariant Poincaré pairing establish the quotient conclusions. The standalone verifier reproduces the closed trace formulas by independent component permutations and cochain traces, tests differential equivariance, and checks arithmetic averages. It does not purport to exhaust all cycle lengths.

## Originality
**PASS relative to the evidence inspected, with a material residual priority risk.** Buchstaber--Panov (arXiv:math/9912199, complete HTML full text, especially Theorem 3.3 and Example 3.11) establishes the general model and ordinary Betti numbers, not the higher-degree dihedral traces. Li Yu (arXiv:1406.7392, full HTML text, Theorem 1.2 and Section 3) treats torus-subgroup quotients from vertex partitions, not the finite coordinate-permutation quotient. Panov (arXiv:1506.06875) likewise describes free subtorus quotients. The novelty claim starts at degree five and does not reassert the already established low-degree character computations. A semantically related 2022 paper of Lü--Zhang (DOI:10.1007/s10473-022-0602-1) concerns certain quotients of polygonal moment-angle manifolds, but only its abstract was accessible; it cannot be ruled out on the basis of an abstract, and a full-text priority check is still needed. Generic derivability from Hochster's model should not be confused with publication of the explicit closed all-higher-degree character and transfer formulas.

## Value
**PASS.** The formulas give an all-degree finite-group representation classification on a natural infinite family, not a handpicked instance or minor numerical increment. They identify arithmetic rotation-order corrections, three distinct reflection patterns, exact higher-degree quotient Betti numbers, and a sharp mod-four threshold for rational Poincaré duality of the quotient. For instance, the quotient in dimension nine for a heptagon has nonzero top cohomology, whereas the octagon case does not. These distinctions cannot be inferred from the ordinary polygon Betti numbers or the existing degree-three and degree-four character values alone.

## Limitations
Full comparison with the Lü--Zhang 2022 text is pending authorized access. No integral quotient-homology, ring presentation, or equivariant homotopy classification is established. Correctness remains a self-review supported by exact computations, not an independent certification.
