For every finite coprime semidirect product of two abelian groups, all right Engel words of depth at least two have the same *complete value distribution*, a mixture of uniform distributions on the commutator images \((v-1)A\). Its Fourier coefficients are character stabilizer ratios, and its exact identity probability equals the number of complement orbits on the kernel divided by the kernel order; all depth-two Engelizers also have explicit conjugate-subgroup descriptions.
