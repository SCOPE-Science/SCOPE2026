# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The key Lucas congruence holds for every prime power \(m=p^e\) because lower-digit binomial coefficients decouple. It gives every osculating coordinate as \(z^a w_r\), and the invertible triangular change of incidence parameters proves equality with the scroll's dense chart, not merely containment. The standard prime determinantal description and the function-field extension \(t^m=z\) establish all dimensions, degree, smoothness, and precise inseparability. The exact finite tests algorithmically confirm the coefficient formulas and minor vanishings in several small parameter ranges.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
Gmainer–Havlicek compute *intersections* (nuclei) of osculating spaces and provide the Hasse derivative matrix, but not the image geometry, determinantal ideal, or incidence degree. Aprodu et al. prove the characteristic-two first tangent scroll and, for other characteristics, containment of first tangents in a scroll. Those statements concern order one and do not imply identification of the \((p^e-1)\)-osculating union, with \(e\ge2\), as that scroll or the Frobenius-power degree. Targeted bibliographic searches and semantic finding comparisons found no equivalent or dominating theorem. Room's 1932 historical paper on configurations of osculating spaces was not accessible in full; this is an explicit priority uncertainty, not assumed noncoverage.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem simultaneously classifies dimension, smoothness, degree, defining equations, and inseparable incidence for an unbounded prime-power family of higher osculating loci. At the first admissible degree it identifies entire smooth Segre varieties; the Frobenius multisection gives a geometric explanation for the nonreduced curve contact. These are natural fundamental projective invariants, not an arbitrary finite enumeration.
