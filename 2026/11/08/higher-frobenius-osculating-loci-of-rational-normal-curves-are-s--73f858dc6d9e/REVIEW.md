Every 2x2 scroll minor vanishes, and conversely on dense chart z,w the triangular equations in lambda_j invert after choosing an m-th root t, proving equality of reduced image with smooth balanced scroll S(floor((d-r)/m)). Standard scroll ideal prime, dimension m degree d-m+1, for d>=2m-1 all directrices positive. The theorem does not cover special incidence fibers or low-degree cone boundary.
