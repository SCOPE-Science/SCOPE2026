Over characteristic \(p\), every order-\((p^e-1)\) osculating locus of a rational normal curve with \(e\ge2\) and \(d\ge2p^e-1\) is a smooth balanced rational normal scroll, with explicit quadratic determinantal equations and degree \(d-p^e+1\). The natural incidence map has purely inseparable generic degree \(p^e\), and each general ruling meets the curve in one length-\(p^e\) point.
