For a star with \(k\ge3\) two-edge arms, every higher independence complex is either contractible or an explicit binomial wedge of equidimensional spheres, with a sharp contractibility interval. When the spheres occur, arm permutations act on integral homology as \(\mathrm{sgn}\otimes\bigwedge^{r-k-1}U_k\), where \(U_k\) is the rank-\(k-1\) augmentation lattice.
