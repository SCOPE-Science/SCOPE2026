For \(k[x]/(x^{2m})\) and \(k[x]/(x^{2m+1})\) in characteristic two, restricted nilpotence \(D^{2^e}=0\) at every height \(e\geq2\) has an explicit finite union of linear components and the same closed rank-counting formula in both parities. Already at height four, the locus can have unequal-dimensional components, with a sharp general criterion for equidimensionality.
