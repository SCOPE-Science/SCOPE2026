# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof gives a full infinite-dimensional argument. For fixed positive shift \(\ell\), off-diagonal matrix entries are \(a_{N_j+\ell-N_i}\): the lower triangle has finite support, and each positive gap \(t=N_j-N_i\) has multiplicity bounded solely in terms of the lacunarity ratio. Consequently the deviation from \(a_\ell I\) is Hilbert–Schmidt, and it is uniformly small on a sufficiently late tail. A nonconstant analytic disk self-map has some \(a_\ell\ne0\) with \(\ell\ge1\); multiplication by it is contractive in classical \(H^2\). This yields the square-summable lacunary components of its powers from the square-summable complementary components. The latter are dominated by the weighted Hilbert–Schmidt series. Low fixed coefficients of the powers decay geometrically, completing the inclusion without a sum-free hypothesis. The reverse inclusion is the monotone lacunary-block argument, valid for every \(q>1\); necessity uses the classical Paley projection and an outer boundary-modulus example with a sparse subsequence. All quantifiers and the disk-analyticity root condition are explicit. The finite checker is supplementary and not a substitute for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge assessment)
The closest primary source, Cruz-Zamorano–Dellepiane–Seco, arXiv:2610.40148v1, Theorem 1.3(b), assumes \(q>2\) and uses a sum-free lacunary projection in Proposition 4.5. The current statement covers \(1<q\le2\), where the original lemma is false (notably for powers of two). The alternative compact-compression lemma identifies a genuinely different route to the missing inclusion. The original paper's lacunary-block and Paley construction portions already largely cover the other implications and are not claimed as independent new discoveries. The Lefèvre–Li–Queffélec–Rodríguez-Piazza characterization is for universal boundedness, not Hilbert–Schmidt symbols. Targeted semantic research-record queries and recovered historical claims show no dominating equivalence. That search does not establish absolute novelty; publication priority and hidden equivalent formulations remain risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The ratio \(q=2\) is a genuine mathematical boundary of the previously used sum-free argument and includes the canonical dyadic lacunary set. Removing the artificial threshold solves the precise remaining gap in a sharp if-and-only-if classification for all Hadamard sets, with no additional weight regularity. The new compact-multiplier lemma explains why additive collisions do not obstruct Hilbert–Schmidt symbol-class equality. This is a structural result rather than a numerical increment or an arbitrary special case.

## Scope and risks
No claim is made of uniform Hilbert–Schmidt norm equivalence for \(q\le2\), Schatten-class equivalences for exponents other than two, or the full classification for arbitrary nonlacunary weights. Paley's inequality and the source's outer-function lower construction remain prior input. An equivalent unpublished or differently formulated theorem may exist; independent audit was not undertaken.
