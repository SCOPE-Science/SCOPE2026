For analytic weighted Hardy spaces whose exceptional monomial weights lie on **any** Hadamard-lacunary set with ratio \(q>1\), the Hilbert–Schmidt composition symbols coincide with those for ordinary \(H^2\) exactly when \(\inf_jN_j\beta_{N_j}>0\). A shifted lacunary multiplier becomes a nonzero scalar plus a Hilbert–Schmidt operator, removing the previously necessary assumption \(q>2\) and covering dyadic exceptional weights.
