# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The equation \(D^2=0\) reduces to \(A^2=AB+BA=0\); contractible cancellation leaves the induced map on \(\ker A/\operatorname{im}A\). Direct cycle/boundary calculations show \(H\cong R^{h-s-c}\oplus k^{2c}\). An intersection-of-subspaces count proves every coefficient, including edge cases \(r=0\), \(s=0\), and \(h=0\). Exhaustive tests use original equations and independently calculated quotient homology, not the classification formula.

## Originality

PASS subject to historical-priority limitations. Avramov–Buchweitz–Iyengar (arXiv:math/0602344), Schröer (arXiv:math/0211293), and Moreau–Yu (arXiv:1411.6427) were materially inspected. None of the inspected results supplies the claimed labeled finite-field homology-type distribution. Existing rank/Smith censuses are not dominating: the two explicit size-two examples have identical Smith forms and total ranks but nonisomorphic homology. An unlocated equivalent general counting theorem remains a residual risk.

## Value

PASS. Homology is the central invariant of differential modules; its free/torsion decomposition is more informative than operator rank. The exact all-dimensional distribution and the even-torsion restriction identify natural obstructions and give a finite-field baseline for differential modules over singular local rings. The distinction is substantive even when the differential's elementary divisors agree.

## Limitations

No conjugacy classification, higher-length local rings or general underlying modules. Finite computations corroborate, but the proof supplies the universal quantifiers. No independent mathematical or priority certification.
