For square-zero differentials on free modules over \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), homology always has the form \(R^u\oplus\mathbb F_q^{2c}\); its torsion multiplicity is even. An exact all-dimensional Gaussian flag formula counts each homology type jointly with reduction rank and total linear rank, distinguishing differentials indistinguishable by Smith invariants.
