There are noncongruent planar unions of \(6m\) identical disjoint disks with exactly the same **full, direction-sensitive two-point displacement distribution** and covariogram, yet at every offset radius \(11/4<r<13/4\), one enlarged union is connected while the other has \(m+1\) components. Thus even perfect two-point geometric data can conceal a topological discrepancy growing linearly with the number of constituent disks.
