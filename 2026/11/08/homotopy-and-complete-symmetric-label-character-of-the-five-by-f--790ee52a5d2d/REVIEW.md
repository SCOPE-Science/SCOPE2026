# Scientific review of corrected claim

## Correctness
The five-crown matching complex has 779 nonempty faces. The specified vertex-toggle matching has 382 pairs and leaves a zero-cell, a two-cell and thirteen three-cells. Exhaustive directed-cover topological sorting establishes acyclicity. Exact rational homology ranks force the integral cellular differential between dimensions three and two to vanish. Every attaching map of a three-cell to the unique two-sphere therefore has degree zero and is nullhomotopic, establishing the ordinary wedge decomposition.

## Originality and prior coverage
The complete \(S_5\times C_2\) rational character and all integral homology ranks were already calculated in the 18 October and 1 November 2026 five-crown results. They are credited as background and are not part of the corrected novelty claim. Shareshian–Wachs's earlier rank table is likewise credited. The surviving statement is the ordinary homotopy splitting via an explicit Morse CW model, which does not follow from character formulas or Betti numbers alone without controlling attaching maps. A full historical Björner–Welker text comparison remains inaccessible; priority is qualified.

## Mathematical value and limits
The first mixed-dimensional example in a canonical directed matching-complex family has a natural homotopy-type question. The explicit nullity of all its attaching maps determines its ordinary integral homotopy beyond the existing homology and symmetry data. This is a finite exact classification, not a theorem about other orders, integral group-action lattices, or equivariant homotopy. No external certification is asserted.
