The matching complex of the once-subdivided tetrahedral graph is a wedge of five two-spheres, and its natural \(S_4\)-action on rational second homology is \(\mathbf1\oplus(\operatorname{sgn}\otimes\mathbb Q^4)\). Every subgroup quotient is simply connected, and its reduced rational second Betti number is given by averaging \(1+\operatorname{sgn}(g)\#\operatorname{Fix}(g)\) over the subgroup.
