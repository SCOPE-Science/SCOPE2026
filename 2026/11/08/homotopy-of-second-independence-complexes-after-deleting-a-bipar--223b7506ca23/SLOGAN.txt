For every complete bipartite graph with a matching of \(t\geq1\) edges removed, its second independence complex is a wedge of \((a-t)(b-t)\) circles and \(\binom{t-1}{2}\) two-spheres. Thus deleting the full perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) changes the homotopy type from \(\bigvee^{n^2-1}S^1\) to \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{2}}S^2\).
