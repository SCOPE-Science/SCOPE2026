On the Boolean hypercube, every even-stage Gauss–Legendre heat step eventually acquires a negative adjacent entry in dimension three or higher despite positive eigenvalues and exact mass conservation. For two stages, the three-cube has the sharp positivity cutoff \(h=\rho\), the unique positive root of \(h^4-6h^3-20h^2-18h-6\), while cubes of dimension one and two are positive for every step size.
