Unit shortcuts from the origin to triples of vertices of a Boolean cube preserve its sharp Lipschitz variance \(d/4\) exactly when those triples form a two-colorable hypergraph; every preserving optimizer is a signed coordinate sum. Non-two-colorability forces a certified loss of at least \(2^{-d-1}\), making preservation NP-complete to recognize from the compact chord list although any single shortcut is invisible.
