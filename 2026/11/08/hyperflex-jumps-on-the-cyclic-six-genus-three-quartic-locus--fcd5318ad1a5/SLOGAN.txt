The smooth complex quartics \(X^3Z+Y^4+rY^2Z^2+Z^4=0\) have exactly \(1\), \(4\), or \(7\) hyperflexes, with the jumps occurring precisely at \(r=0\) and \(11r^4-1080r^2+3888=0\). Among the curves whose full automorphism group is \(C_6\), exactly two complex isomorphism classes have seven hyperflexes, and their parameters are explicit.
