Conditioned on exactly \(k<n\) zero-to-zero excursions in a uniform symmetric walk bridge of length \(2n\), the number of two-step excursions is exactly hypergeometric with population \(2n-k-1\), marked count \(k-1\), and sample size \(n\). At typical \(k\asymp\sqrt n\), the remaining \(n^{1/4}\)-scale fluctuations form a Rayleigh-mixed Gaussian, and the return-count/short-excursion correlation approaches one with an explicit square-root correction.
