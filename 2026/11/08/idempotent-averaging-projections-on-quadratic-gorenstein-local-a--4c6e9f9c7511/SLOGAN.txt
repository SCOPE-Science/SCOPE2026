On each nondegenerate quadratic-socle local algebra \(k1\oplus V\oplus kz\), every idempotent averaging projection belongs to an explicit orthogonal, isotropic, or exceptional rank-two family. Its complete finite-field rank polynomial is determined by nondegenerate and totally isotropic subspace counts, and non-radical-preserving projections occur only in ranks \(1\) and \(2\).
