# Scientific review of finite Heisenberg idempotent derivation ranks

## Correctness — PASS

The direct decomposition \(D(v)=Av+f(v)z\) reduces derivation and idempotency conditions to the conformal-symplectic equation, \(A^2=A\), and a linear condition on \(f\). Evaluating the bilinear condition on the image and kernel separates complementary Lagrangian projections (all characteristics) from orthogonal symplectic projections (only characteristic two). Stabilizer counts yield every coefficient, with no missing functional parameters. The \(\Theta_q(q^{-n})\) bound and two conditional limits follow from explicit quadratic exponents and uniformly bounded symplectic product factors. A standalone exhaustive checker covers four small dimension/field cases, including five-dimensional Heisenberg algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\). Finite checks are not substituted for the proof.

La Rosa–Mancini, arXiv:2301.11058 (January 26, 2023), studies derivation algebras of two-step nilpotent Lie and Leibniz algebras, with a restriction away from characteristic two; inspected sections do not enumerate these idempotent derivations. The dimension-three specialization agrees with the earlier free class-two result and is excluded from the new scope. Additional literature and recorded-claim searches for conformal-symplectic idempotents, broader classification, rank tables and Heisenberg endomorphism analogues identified no equivalent statement for \(n\ge2\). A broader unpublished or unlocated theorem is still possible; no global novelty certificate is asserted.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The image rank of a Lie derivation is an intrinsic invariant, and idempotent derivations encode compatible eigenspace splittings. The result provides a natural complete finite-field classification by rank, a sharp characteristic-two geometric branch, a quantitative rarity law, and a parity-sensitive conditional rank distribution. The branch geometry distinguishes it from routine projection counts and from previously studied Lie-algebra retractions.

## Limitations and close sources

The exact formulas apply to standard finite Heisenberg Lie algebras of dimension at least five, not arbitrary two-step algebras or Lie retractions. The asymptotics fix \(q\). The source's derivation normal forms and classical symplectic orbit counts are not original components. The proof includes the extension fields, while the direct checker enumerates only small prime fields. Close literature: La Rosa–Mancini (arXiv:2301.11058); Mancini–La Rosa (arXiv:2106.12904); prior free class-two derivation ranks (dimension-three boundary).
