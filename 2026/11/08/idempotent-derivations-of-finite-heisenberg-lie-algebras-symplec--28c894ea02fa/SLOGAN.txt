Idempotent derivations of higher finite Heisenberg Lie algebras have an exact symplectic rank polynomial: in odd characteristic, only ranks \(0\) and \(n+1\) occur, while characteristic two adds even-rank fibers indexed by nondegenerate symplectic subspaces. The additional branch has probability \(\Theta_q(q^{-n})\) and, when conditioned on, exhibits two reflected discrete-Gaussian rank laws.
