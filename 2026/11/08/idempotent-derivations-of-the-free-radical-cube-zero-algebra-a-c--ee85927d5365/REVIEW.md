# Review of idempotent derivations of the free radical-cube-zero algebra

## Correctness — PASS
The algebra, rank parameter, and derivation equations are explicit. Every derivation kills the scalar image of generators, because applying Leibniz to a mixed length-three zero word separates three independent degree-two monomials. The induced map on quadratic words is necessarily \(L_P=P\otimes1+1\otimes P\). The two equations for \(D^2=D\) are computed on both degree-one and degree-two pieces. For characteristic different from two, \(L_P^2-L_P=2P\otimes P\) forces \(P=0\) and then \(Q=0\). In characteristic two, the projection eigenspace decomposition gives the precise fiber dimension \(r^3+3r(m-r)^2\), the full derivation rank \(r+2r(m-r)\), and the explicit shear that removes every quadratic term under conjugation. Smoothness and irreducibility follow from the projection homogeneous spaces and constant-rank linear fibers. Both standalone verifiers ran successfully: complete generator-image enumeration over \(\mathbb F_2\) at two generators, independent finite-field linear systems through three generators, and direct automorphism-orbit enumeration at two generators. No step infers a universal theorem from the finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, with priority limitations
The full text of Sánchez-Flores (arXiv:1004.2820) was inspected in the relevant introduction and radical-square-zero derivation/cohomology sections. Its multiple-loop example has **radical square zero**, while the algebra here retains all quadratic words; the cited results concern Lie structures of outer derivations rather than the idempotent locus or its rank/orbit census. Hoffmann–Kowalski (arXiv:1212.5788) studies integration of iterative higher derivations, particularly on fields; those theorems do not count derivations or components of the specified finite noncommutative algebra. Redondo–Román (arXiv:1504.02495) treats quadratic string algebras rather than the multiloop cubic monomial truncation. Searches for exact and equivalent parity-grading formulations, the object, equations, fibers and rank formula did not reveal comparable coverage. Idempotent derivations of Heisenberg and free class-two **Lie** algebras involve alternating multiplication and do not subsume this associative tensor-algebra theorem. A broad classification of parity gradings or truncated-free-algebra automorphism actions could potentially imply the result; independent priority verification has not been performed, and search absence alone is not evidence of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
An idempotent derivation in characteristic two is the parity operator of a \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\)-grading. The question therefore asks for the natural grading moduli of the first nontrivial noncommutative truncated free algebra, not an arbitrary numerical slice. The theorem simultaneously classifies all conjugacy classes, admissible full linear ranks, smooth geometric components, dimensions, and exact finite-field cardinalities, and proves a sharp characteristic-two versus non-two obstruction. This is a structural and infinitely parametrized result that connects associative multiplication and grading geometry rather than a finite table alone.

## Sources and limitations
The closest sources are Sánchez-Flores, *On the semisimplicity of the outer derivations of monomial algebras* (arXiv:1004.2820, 2010); Hoffmann and Kowalski, *Integrating Hasse–Schmidt derivations* (arXiv:1212.5788, 2012); and Redondo and Román, *Gerstenhaber algebra structure on the Hochschild cohomology of quadratic string algebras* (arXiv:1504.02495, 2015). The projection parameter and shear argument are established here; classical results on projections, their Gaussian counts, and parity gradings are not presented as new. The underlying **reduced** variety alone is classified. Independent historical priority and mathematical validation are outstanding.
