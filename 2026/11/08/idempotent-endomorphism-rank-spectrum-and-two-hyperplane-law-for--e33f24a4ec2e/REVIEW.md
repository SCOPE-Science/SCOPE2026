## Correctness

**PASS.** The homomorphism identity reduces to the single rank-one bracket \([x,y]=\beta(x)\alpha(y)e_1\). For a map that does not kill \(e_1\), the identity forces an explicit nonabelian retraction normal form. For a map that kills \(e_1\), the image is abelian and lies in one of two intrinsic hyperplanes. Counting projections, graph images and their intersection proves each rank coefficient. A uniform Gaussian-binomial estimate yields the factor of two, the small nonabelian tail and the parity-dependent theta limit. The attached verifier separately checks rank counts and image types against complete small-dimensional enumeration.

**PASS.** Retracts and idempotents of endomorphism monoids are intrinsic structural invariants. The rank law distinguishes this algebraic invariant from idempotent linear maps, while quantifying exactly how the latter govern its typical size.
