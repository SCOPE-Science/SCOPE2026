For the solvable Leibniz algebra with sole nonzero basic bracket \([e_2,e_1]=e_1\), the rank spectrum of idempotent algebra endomorphisms has an exact Gaussian-binomial formula in every finite-field size. As the dimension grows over a fixed field, almost all retractions have abelian image, split equally between two intrinsic maximal abelian hyperplanes, and exhibit a parity-dependent discrete-Gaussian rank law.
