Independent and common Poisson reset clocks in a Brownian stochastic-diffusivity tracer yield exactly the same joint second-order stationary time-series statistics at fixed marginal reset rates \(R,Q\), but distinct fourth moments. The ordinary tracer kurtosis has the sharp formula \(K=6(6R+Q)/(R+Q-c)\), uniquely identifying the common-clock intensity \(c\). At equal marginal rates, complete independence gives \(K=21\) while full synchronization gives \(K=42\).
