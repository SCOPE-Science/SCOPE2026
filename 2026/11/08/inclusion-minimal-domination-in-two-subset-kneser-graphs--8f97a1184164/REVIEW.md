# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The root-edge/cover criterion handles selected versus unselected Kneser vertices exactly. The cover-one and cover-two regimes admit a direct argument, including the shared-leaf obstruction. The cover-three regime reduces to at most six edges by a random-order set-pair inequality; an exhaustively generated, isomorphism-checked finite census then gives the only possibilities. The provided standard-library checker independently confirms the finite census and checks every selected subset for orders four through seven. Its outputs are corroborations of a universal proof, not finite extrapolation.

## Originality

PASS, subject to the documented residual bibliographic risk. Ivančo–Zelinka (1993) establish the minimum size and dominating triples, not the full inclusion-minimal-set structure. Cornet–Torres (arXiv:2308.15603, 2023) study \(k\)-tuple domination and incidence constraints; their results on minimum sizes do not imply a classification or count of all inclusion-minimal ordinary dominating sets. The 2007 Gorodezky thesis concerns domination minima and designs, and the 2014 general enumeration work is algorithmic rather than a closed formula for this family. Searches for alternative terms (minimal domination, upper domination, two-element set systems, and vertex-cover-critical root graphs) did not locate a directly covering result. General literature searches are not proof of absolute priority.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The exact classification and labeled enumerator refine the known minimum domination number for a standard graph family, yield the upper domination number, characterize the full minimal-size spectrum, and exhibit unbounded gaps. The proof connects domination to a bounded root-edge criticality problem via a uniform set-pair inequality; the structured result is not a mere parameter substitution or a recomputation of minimum sizes.

## Limitations and closest sources

The strongest directly related primary sources are Ivančo–Zelinka (1993) and Cornet–Torres (2023; revised 2024), both inspected for relevant statements. Gorodezky's 2007 thesis was partially inspected for its domination discussion. Possible unindexed reformulations in maximal irredundant families or vertex-cover-critical graph classifications remain a residual originality risk. The classification is proved for ordinary domination of \(KG(n,2)\) only. Independent verification is not claimed.
