For discrete-time quantum renewal resetting with strictly interior age hazards, nondecreasing reset hazard is **equivalent** to CP-divisibility of the averaged channels uniformly over every finite-dimensional CPTP step map and reset state. Each strict hazard drop has an explicit cyclic-unitary witness of dimension \(t+1\) with a negative intermediate-channel matrix entry of magnitude \(r_1S_{t-1}(r_t-r_{t+1})\), although a particular fixed quantum system need not become indivisible.
