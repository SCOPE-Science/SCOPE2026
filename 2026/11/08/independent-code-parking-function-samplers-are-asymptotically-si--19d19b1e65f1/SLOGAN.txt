Any parking-function sampler formed by independently choosing one value from each range \(\{1,\ldots,j\}\) and then uniformly reshuffling is asymptotically singular in total variation from the uniform law, uniformly over all choices of the coordinate distributions. In particular, the Mallows-parameter family has total-variation distance tending to one for **every** positive parameter sequence, answering Pinsky's full-law approximation question.
