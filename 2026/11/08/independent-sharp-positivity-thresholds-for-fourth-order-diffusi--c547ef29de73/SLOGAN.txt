For a complete network split into arbitrarily many internally homogeneous communities, the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre heat update preserves positive states at **every** timestep exactly when each community's internal conductance obeys \(a_i/b\le2+n_i/(N-n_i)\). These sharp thresholds are fully independent across communities: exceeding one causes a unique within-community sign crossing, regardless of all the other community weights.
