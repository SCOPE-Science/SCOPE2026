For every clique size \(m\ge2\), the induced-forest facets of \(C_n[K_m]\) have an exact cyclic gap polynomial, with smallest size \(\min\{n-1,2\lceil n/3\rceil\}\) and largest size \(n\) or \(n-1\) according to parity. Every facet has the same cardinality **exactly for** \(n=5,7\); all other cycle lengths \(n\ge4\) give nonpure complexes.
