For the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), \(n\ge4\), every inclusion-maximal induced forest is either a whole shore plus one opposite vertex or an induced five-vertex path. The complete forest and maximal-forest polynomials are explicit; in particular, minimal feedback vertex sets occur only at cardinalities \(n-1\) and \(2n-5\), with exactly \(2n\) and \(n(n-1)(n-2)\) configurations of the respective forms (combine counts at \(n=4\)).
