For every finite graph \(G\), induced matchings in its clique blowup \(G[K_m]\) correspond exactly to weighted dissociation sets of \(G\), and maximal ones correspond exactly to the dominating dissociation sets. For cyclic bases, this gives a closed polynomial for every maximal-matching size and proves that the induced-matching complex is pure precisely for cycles of lengths \(3\) and \(5\), for every \(m\ge2\).
