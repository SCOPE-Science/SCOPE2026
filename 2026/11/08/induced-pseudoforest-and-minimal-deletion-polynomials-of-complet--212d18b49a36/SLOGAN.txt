For every complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with \(r\ge3\), all inclusion-maximal induced pseudoforests belong to four explicitly counted structural families, yielding an exact cardinality polynomial and the dual polynomial for minimal pseudoforest-deletion sets. On the vertex-transitive graphs \(K_{m,m,m}\), their only cardinalities are \(3\), \(4\), and \(m+1\) for \(m\ge5\), and a uniformly chosen maximal set has size four with probability \(1-4/(3m)+O(m^{-2})\).
