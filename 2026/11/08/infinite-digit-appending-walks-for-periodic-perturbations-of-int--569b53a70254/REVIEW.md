# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The dependent-base proof first forces resonance uniformly over all positive digit and index increments using an integer-power gap of at least one half, then converts the digit condition into an exact floor relation for periodic offsets. Positive offsets double; the shifted magnitude of negative offsets at most \(-2\) doubles. The terminal offsets \(0\) and \(-1\) have explicit infinitely repeatable resonant steps. The supplied finite exact-arithmetic checks corroborate the algebra; they are not substituted for it.

## Originality — PASS, with priority caveat
Mosahab and collaborators' Theorem 1.2 assumes a digit-length cap, their Theorem 1.3 concerns independent roots, and their Examples 1.1 and 3.3 provide two constant-offset instances. These do not state the unrestricted-digit dependent-root periodic-offset classification, all-start residue criterion, or sharp length cap. The Kominers work addresses bounded-digit Fibonacci/Lucas walks, not this class. Banks–Luca study concatenating entire terms of a binary recurrence, rather than arbitrary appended digit strings. Searches of scholarly sources and semantic mathematical records found no coverage, but such searches cannot establish historical uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result completes the dependent-root half of the recent existence-versus-termination dichotomy for a broad, natural family of integer linear recurrences. It classifies *all* sufficiently late initial indices, gives their exact natural density, and supplies a period-independent, appended-length-independent sharp worst-case termination bound. The structural doubling obstruction is informative beyond the two published constant-offset examples.

## Limitations and closest sources
The scope is integer-power sequences with bounded periodic integer offsets and sufficiently late indices. Independent-base existence is entirely prior work. The full text of arXiv:2610.18919v1 was consulted; arXiv:2512.06446 was compared from its abstract and publication description, not an independently obtained full text. Previously unindexed equivalent results remain a residual priority risk.
