For any finite-height Gaussian sketch, one-step sketched FOM on a nontrivial positive-definite linear system has infinite expected absolute solution error because its reciprocal coefficient has a smooth Student-law density at zero. With the same sketch, one-step sketched GMRES has finite \(p\)-th error moment exactly for \(p<s\) and finite mean-square error as soon as \(s>2\).
