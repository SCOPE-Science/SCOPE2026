For the order-one generalized Cesàro matrix with complex shift \(\alpha\) and \(\operatorname{Re}\alpha>-1\), its compact self-commutator has negative index zero for nonnegative real shifts, exactly one for negative real shifts, and **infinite** for every nonreal shift. Hence an arbitrarily small imaginary perturbation creates infinitely many negative eigenvalues, a phenomenon absent from the established real-shift hyponormality classification.
