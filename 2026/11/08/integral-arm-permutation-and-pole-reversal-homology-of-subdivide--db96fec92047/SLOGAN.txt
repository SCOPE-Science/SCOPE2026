For \(p\ge3\), the independence complex of \(p\) internally disjoint length-three paths between two poles is \(S^1\vee S^{p-1}\); its integral circle class carries pole-reversal sign, while its top class carries arm-permutation sign and pole-reversal parity \((-1)^p\). In particular, this resolves the full integral automorphism characters of the independence complexes of Kronecker double covers of friendship graphs with at least two petals.
