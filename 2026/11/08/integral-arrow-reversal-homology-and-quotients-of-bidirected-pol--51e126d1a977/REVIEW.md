# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The arrow-reversal action is geometrically free because no simplex contains an arrow and its reverse. The augmented-chain Lefschetz number is therefore \(-1\). The top homology is generated integrally by the boundaries of the two prohibited directed full-cycle simplices; reversing arrows exchanges these cycles, while reflecting vertex labels exchanges them with the explicit permutation sign. The independent weighted transfer-matrix computation has eigenvalues \(-1,i,-i\) and yields the complete rotation character in the lower degree. The nonequivariant ranks and concentration are cited from the full text of Caputi–Collari–Smith, Theorem 2.10. Commutation and the decomposition into a line and the rational cyclotomic plane determine the reversal operator, and torsion-free homology upgrades rational operator equality to integral equality. Transfer and the published connectivity theorem give quotient rational homology and the fundamental group. The executable independently enumerates faces and signed traces for six lengths and computes quotient-chain homology over two odd primes for four lengths. No finite test is presented as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit residual risk
The primary paper proves homology ranks and connectivity, but does not consider reversal of every arrow as a separate involution or quotient. The closest published dihedral homology calculation gives the rational representation of vertex rotations/reflections, not the independent global arrow-reversal action; its cyclic character is rederived in the proof and not claimed as new. Direct comparison of implications shows that existing dihedral representations alone do not fix the action of a separate central involution. The free-action observation combined with character theory provides the **additional integral operator identity** and the exceptional quotient classification. Dedicated searches for arrow-reversal/unoriented multipath quotients did not find a stronger covering result. This negative search cannot prove absolute uniqueness. An equivalent unindexed involution computation is the principal residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Passing from oriented multipaths to configurations identified under reversing **all** arrow directions is a natural geometric operation, not a cherry-picked parameter slice. The theorem determines its actual covering structure, its integral action on all nonzero graded homology, its full rational quotient Betti numbers for every cycle length, and the quotient fundamental group. The exceptional \(4\mid m\) lower homology arises as a two-dimensional cyclotomic invariant subspace rather than from a numerical coincidence. These data are invisible to nonequivariant ranks, and the integral relation to two-step rotation makes the result usable in later equivariant calculations. The work does not claim a full integral lattice classification.
