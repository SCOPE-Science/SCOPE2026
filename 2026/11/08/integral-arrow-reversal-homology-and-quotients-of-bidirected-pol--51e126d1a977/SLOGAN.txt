For bidirected cycle multipath complexes, reversing every arrow acts integrally as the negative identity on lower homology, except at cycle lengths divisible by four, when it equals minus the square of one-step rotation. This free involution has a twofold quotient whose rational homology has an exceptional two-dimensional lower summand exactly at those multiples of four, and whose fundamental group is cyclic of order two for all cycle lengths at least six.
