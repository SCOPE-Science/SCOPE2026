# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The invariant subcomplex of faces omitting a core vertex is contractible by the full-simplex nerve of deleted-core complexes; each intersection is a cone on an isolated pendant leaf. The complement relative chain complex is exactly the determinant line of the non-leaf set tensored with the truncated augmented simplex chains of the leaf set. The top kernel is the exterior power of the *integral* leaf augmentation lattice, not merely its rationalization. Naturality of the chain construction and the long exact sequence establish the full graph-automorphism module, including the degree-zero and boundary cases. Eleven independently constructed graph profiles gave 712 homology/character comparisons and 819 deletion-cone checks. The finite tests are supportive rather than infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to literature risk.** Deshpande and Singh, Theorem 3.10 of arXiv:2001.05448, already establish *all* stated nonequivariant sphere counts and wedge homotopy types for this graph family. These are prior results and are not the novel claim. The full-group **integral homology representation and cycle-type character** are not given by that theorem, its choice-dependent discrete Morse matching, Sawant's 2026 vertex-decomposition theorem, or the inspected chordal-complex literature. Targeted indexed-record searches found analogous integral representation calculations for unrelated graph complexes but no statement implying the exact module here. Failure to find an equivalent unindexed article is not a uniqueness proof; that risk remains.

**Value — PASS.** Previous sphere counts cannot distinguish the natural actions of non-leaf and leaf permutations on homology. The exact integral lattice determines every trace under arbitrary graph automorphisms without separately computing chain matrices, works for arbitrarily large non-tree cores, and separates core orientation from the exterior leaf augmentation. It resolves a natural equivariant refinement of a previously classified family; the new claim is not mere reparameterization or a numerical increment.

**Scope and limitations.** Connected graphs whose every non-leaf has a pendant neighbor; ordinary integral homology modules only. No equivariant homotopy equivalence, geometric fixed-point classification, or independent validation is claimed. For an automorphism the sign on non-leaves and cycle lengths on leaves determine its trace; no additional structure is assumed. The relative-chain proof does not rely on a canonical selection of an individual leaf.

**Sources.** Deshpande–Singh, arXiv:2001.05448, §§3.8–3.10; Sawant, arXiv:2605.25150v1, Theorem 3.6; Abdelmalek et al., arXiv:2106.10863, main statements; related indexed results on different Morse and Mycielskian complexes. An unindexed equivalent remains possible.
