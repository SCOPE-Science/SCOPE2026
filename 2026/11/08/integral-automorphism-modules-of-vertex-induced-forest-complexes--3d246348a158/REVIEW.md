The argument is integral and uniform. Face characterization excludes three occupied parts by a triangle and two multiply occupied parts by a square; the converse is a star/independent set. Relative chains split by the unique unordered support pair. Each pair is the union of two contractible joins intersecting in the connected graph \(K_{n_i,n_j}\), so reduced Mayer–Vietoris identifies its second homology with the bipartite cycle lattice. The connecting sequence for the relative pair gives a part-graph edge, and the global pair sequence gives the part-graph cycle lattice. The two signs incurred by a part swap (edge-orientation reversal and cover-order reversal) cancel, so tensor-factor interchange is positive. Explicit character formulas follow from the actual module action. Exact signed face traces and boundary reductions in `verify.py` independently corroborate cases but are not used as infinite proofs.
