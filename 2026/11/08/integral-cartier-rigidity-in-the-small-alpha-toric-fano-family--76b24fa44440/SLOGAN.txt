For the Liu--Zhu toric small-alpha Fano varieties in every dimension \(n\ge3\), all global Cartier classes lie on one primitive polarization, even though the Weil-class rank is \(n^2\). The exact global Weil--Cartier defect is \(\mathbb Z^{n^2-1}\oplus\mathbb Z/(4n-2)\mathbb Z\); the class-group and Gorenstein-index components appearing in this formula were previously known, while the integral Cartier lattice and Picard generator are identified here.
