For every \(n\ge2\), the complete integral cohomology ring of the two-plane odd biisotropic symplectic Grassmannian is the explicit ordinary-Grassmannian Schubert ring modulo the annihilator of the squared Plücker class. A unimodular tridiagonal middle pairing and unit-diagonal higher pairings prove integral generation and distinguish this ring from that of the ordinary Grassmannian with the same Betti numbers.
