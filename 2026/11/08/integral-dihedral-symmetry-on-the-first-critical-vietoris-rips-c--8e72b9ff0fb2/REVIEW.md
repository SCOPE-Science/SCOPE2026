# Scientific self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** For every integer \(k\ge2\), the maximal-clique dichotomy is proved directly: every clique is contained in a consecutive length-\(k\) arc or is an exceptional three-step triangle. The arc-generated subcomplex is a nerve of one-third-circle arcs with contractible nonempty intersections, so it has the homotopy type of a circle, with the stated dihedral orientation action. The \(k\) remaining two-cells each attach with integer degree \(+1\) under an explicit normalized-root map; the relative long exact sequence therefore identifies integral \(H_2\) exactly with the augmentation kernel and rules out torsion. The orientation sign under reflection is checked at the chain level. A standard-library-only exhaustive test independently confirms the clique classification, relative Euler characteristics, and every signed Lefschetz character for \(2\le k\le8\); this finite evidence does not substitute for the uniform proof.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources.** Adamaszek (2011, Corollary 6.7) proves the nonequivariant sphere-wedge type. Adamaszek–Adams–Frick–Peterson–Previte-Johnson (2014, Section 8, especially Example 8.3) provides the analogous attachment picture for nine vertices, and Section 7 studies dihedral actions on a different **nerve** complex. The map in its Theorem 8.5 relates different numbers of vertices and is not equivariant for the full source dihedral group. The present uniform integral \(D_{3k}\)-module and its reflection character are not statements in these inspected results. Related indexed records concern different simplicial constructions. It remains possible that an equivalent derivation appears in unindexed literature or is obtained by combining prior propositions with extra arguments.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The critical one-third-radius stage is the natural first even-sphere phase transition in a classical Vietoris–Rips family. The canonical integral dihedral lattice, order-three kernel and parity-dependent reflection traces are invisible in the known homotopy type and supply symmetry data relevant to equivariant persistence and quotient homology. The entire infinite family is covered by a structural relative-cell proof, not by an unmotivated finite-instance calculation.

**Closest literature and scientific limitations.** The 2011 and 2014 articles are the closest original sources; their nonequivariant results and the nine-vertex example are excluded from the novelty claim. No equivariant homotopy classification or radius-general result is asserted. The finite checks end at eight triangles, while the proof applies to all \(k\ge2\). Independent originality and proof review may uncover an equivalent formulation not captured by the inspected record.
