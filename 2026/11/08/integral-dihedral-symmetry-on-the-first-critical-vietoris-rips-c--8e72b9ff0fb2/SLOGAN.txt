At the first critical one-third-radius stage, the integral second homology of the cycle-power Vietoris–Rips complex is an orientation-twisted augmentation lattice on \(k\) exceptional triangles, not merely the previously known wedge of \(k-1\) spheres. Its complete dihedral character has rotation traces \(k-1\) or \(-1\) and parity-sensitive reflection traces, while an order-three rotation acts trivially.
