For every complete split graph \(K_m\vee\overline K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), the \(t\)-clique-free complex for \(3\le t\le m+1\) is equivariantly a wedge of \(\binom m{t-1}\) spheres \(S^{t-2}\). Its full integral automorphism homology is the exterior-power lattice \(\bigwedge^{t-1}\mathbb Z^m\), independent of \(n\), although the complex dimension increases with \(n\).
