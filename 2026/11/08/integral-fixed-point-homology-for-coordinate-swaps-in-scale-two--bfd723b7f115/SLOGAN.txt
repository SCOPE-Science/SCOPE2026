For the inclusive scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex of the binary \(n\)-cube, the geometric fixed space of a swap of two coordinates is simply connected for \(n\ge3\). Its only nonzero reduced integral homology groups lie in dimensions two and three, with torsion-free ranks \((n-3)2^{n-2}+1\) and twice the known three-sphere count for the \((n-2)\)-cube, respectively; the two-dimensional fixed-point homology is invisible in the ambient complex.
