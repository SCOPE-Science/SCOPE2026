# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** The once-bisected cubical model satisfies the sufficient subdivision conditions for three unordered particles. Exhaustive incidence checking removes all 2,880 cubes by free square–cube pairs. The deformation retract is two-dimensional. The nonzero second homology proves that this dimension is minimal.

**Originality.** The earlier Petersen symmetry computations already determine the rational Betti vector, including the second Betti number 25. They compute ranks in a chain model and do not give the cubical free-face collapse. Ko–Park supplies the first integral group; the earlier particle-number tables and distinct-graph cases do not give this spine. This distinction is based on the sources inspected, not a claim of absolute priority.

**Value.** An actual deformation retract, rather than just a dimension bound on homology, gives a concrete minimal-dimensional model for a canonical nonplanar graph configuration space. It also certifies integral second-homology freeness without extrapolation from field computations.
