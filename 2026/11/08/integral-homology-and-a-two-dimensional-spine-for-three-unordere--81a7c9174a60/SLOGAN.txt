The unordered three-point configuration space of the Petersen graph collapses to a two-dimensional CW complex and has \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{25}\). Its complete integral homology is \(H_0\cong\mathbb Z\), \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z^6\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\), and zero in higher degrees; the first-homology contribution was already covered by general graph braid results.
