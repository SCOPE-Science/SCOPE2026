The Paley-23 out-neighborhood complex has a previously known integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{209}S^3\vee S^4\). Its natural order-253 affine group acts on third rational homology as the inflated regular \(C_{11}\)-module plus nine copies of the reduced 23-point permutation module; fourth homology is trivial as a representation. The ordinary affine quotient therefore retains exactly one rational homology class in each of degrees three and four.
