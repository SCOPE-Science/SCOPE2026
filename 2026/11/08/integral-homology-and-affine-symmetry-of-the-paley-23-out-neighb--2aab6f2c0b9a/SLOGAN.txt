For the irreflexive out-neighborhood Dowker complex of the Paley tournament on \(23\) vertices, the complete reduced integral homology is \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{209}\) and \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z\), with no other nonzero groups. The natural affine group \(\mathbb F_{23}\rtimes C_{11}\) acts on rational degree-three homology as nine copies of its reduced \(23\)-point permutation module plus the inflated regular \(C_{11}\)-module, giving one rational homology class in each of degrees \(3\) and \(4\) in the quotient.
