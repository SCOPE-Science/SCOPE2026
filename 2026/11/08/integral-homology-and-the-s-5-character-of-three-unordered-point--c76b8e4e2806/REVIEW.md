# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
A fully specified unordered Abrams cubical chain model on the once-subdivided complete graph has 455, 1560, 1650 and 520 integral generators. The subdivision meets the three-particle configuration-space deformation-retract criterion. Every signed boundary composition and every generator of the symmetric-group chain action was checked. Integer unit elimination of the second boundary leaves a 23-by-37 residual whose Smith factors are 1 and 2, and the third boundary has 520 primitive unit pivots. This proves the claimed complete integral homology. The ten geometrically supported triangle loops prove the six-dimensional first rational homology identification. All 120 signed cubical Euler traces and Murnaghan–Nakayama character multiplicities were checked directly by the accompanying executable. The character values and decomposition agree.

## Originality — PASS relative to examined published work
The 2026 Ramos–Yun paper is a substantial exact-object comparison: it determines the *ordered* three-particle configuration-space representation for the same complete graph, but Table 8 does not specify the commuting particle-label action necessary to pass to the *unordered* representation, nor the integral Smith form here. The 2019 Maciążek–Sawicki full text addresses neighboring graph families and universal-cycle theory, but not these characters. Ko–Park proves first-homology statements, which are not presented as the new discovery. Additional exact-object, permutation-character, alias and published research-record searches found no implication of the complete claim. An unindexed joint-action calculation could still supersede this claim; the available comparison is not a universal priority certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete five-vertex graph is a Kuratowski-minimal nonplanar network with all five vertex permutations acting. Its three-point configuration lies at the first nonplanar stage of the complete-graph family, a regime singled out in Ramos–Yun's 2026 comparison with higher homology and intrinsic graph properties. An exact free second homology of rank thirty, coupled with a sharply supported nontrivial character, distinguishes the unordered configuration-space structure from an ordered representation or from Betti numbers. This is a naturally selected complete-graph benchmark, not an arbitrary parametrized slice or a routine transfer of the known first-homology theorem.

## Scope and residual risks
The result is finite and does not classify all complete graphs, integral symmetry-module extensions or equivariant homotopy splittings. Published sources and semantic retrieval are not exhaustive; additional equivalent results might exist outside inspected material. Verification is executable and exact, but independent validation has not been performed.
