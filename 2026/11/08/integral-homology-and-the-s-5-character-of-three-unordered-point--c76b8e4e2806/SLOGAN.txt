For three unordered collision-free particles on the geometric \(K_5\), the integral second homology is free of rank \(30\), while the first homology is \(\mathbb Z^6\oplus\mathbb Z/2\). The full \(S_5\)-character of second rational homology is \(30\) at the identity, \(-2\) on double transpositions, and zero elsewhere, giving five nontrivial Specht constituents and no invariant class.
