The dominance complex of the sixteen-vertex four-cube has complete torsion-free reduced integral homology \(\widetilde H_7\cong\mathbb Z^{10}\) and \(\widetilde H_9\cong\mathbb Z\), with no other nonzero groups. A fully checked acyclic matching reduces its \(45{,}706\) nonempty simplices to one vertex, ten seven-cells, and one nine-cell; the nine-cell attaching homotopy class is not determined.
