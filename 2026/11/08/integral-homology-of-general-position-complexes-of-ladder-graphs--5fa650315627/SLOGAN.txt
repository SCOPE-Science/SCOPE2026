For the ladder \(P_n\square K_2\), the simplicial complex of all graph-geodesic general-position sets has torsion-free integral homology with \(\operatorname{rank}\widetilde H_1=3n+1\) for \(n\ge4\) and \(\operatorname{rank}\widetilde H_2=n(n-4)(n-5)/3\) for \(n\ge6\). A uniform boundary-matrix calculation shows that degree-two homology appears for the first time at \(n=6\), with rank four; the exceptional small orders \(n=2,3\) are handled separately.
