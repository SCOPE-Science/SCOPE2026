The directed-linear-forest complex of the cyclically oriented octahedral graph is simply connected and has torsion-free integral homology in three consecutive positive dimensions: \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z\), \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^4\), and \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z^4\). A complete \(134\)-pair acyclic discrete-Morse certificate gives a ten-cell CW model with zero integral cellular differentials; its higher attaching maps are not classified.
