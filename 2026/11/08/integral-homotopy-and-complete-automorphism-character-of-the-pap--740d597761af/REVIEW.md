# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact LCF graph, face predicate and full range of \(2^{18}\) vertex subsets are specified; union-find and the independent edge-versus-component test agree on every subset. The augmented stagewise toggle matching has \(102{,}223\) pairs, leaving \(43\) critical ten-faces and \(3\) critical eleven-faces; a complete topological sort of \(1{,}704{,}456\) oriented covering relations verifies acyclicity. After restoring a critical vertex, the resulting cellular attaching matrix has the explicit determinant-\(-1\) minor on columns \(6,9,11\). The standard homotopy calculation on a wedge of ten-spheres gives \(\bigvee^{40}S^{10}\). An independent signed-chain computation on each conjugacy-class representative agrees with the orbit formula, and class-weighted sums check the character consequences. The matching and character scripts are exact finite certificates, not numerical approximations. The uniform graph statement is a *single fixed finite graph*, so exhaustive face and automorphism enumeration is logically exhaustive in this case.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
Taylan's primary preprint (arXiv:1501.06754v1, initial 2015-01-27) introduces general devoid-complex methods and treats path/cycle/forest forbidden-pattern cases, not this Pappus forest complex. Carnero Bravo's primary full paper (arXiv:2307.13271v3) directly studies induced-forest vertex complexes, but the inspected introductory scope, explicit graph-family classification and product sections treat graphs outside this Pappus configuration. Publicly indexed matching-complex and Čech-nerve results on the same graph do not cover the present forest complex, even though the matching complex independently happens to have sphere multiplicity \(40\). An inspected Pappus matching-complex result defines faces as disjoint **edges** and proves a wedge in dimension five, so it neither implies the ten-sphere forest result nor the induced-forest character. Pappus ordinary independence-homology symmetry results concern a proper subcomplex with different homology. Matching counts, vertex-cycle types and other coincidental parameters do not constitute implication. Residual risk: a differently named or unindexed finite computation may exist; no claim of absolute bibliographic uniqueness is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The Pappus graph is a canonical cubic symmetric incidence graph, and vertex-induced forest complexes encode complements of decycling sets. Its complete integral homotopy classification is not determined by decycling numbers, face counts or rational Betti ranks. Computing the **full** symmetry character of a 40-dimensional homology group adds information invisible in the homotopy type; the inequivalence of two six-three-cycle order-three actions provides a concrete representation-theoretic distinction. The precise object and the two complementary invariants were motivated by the existing forest-filtration literature, not chosen as arbitrary small subcases. The calculation is a valuable fixed-graph benchmark without asserting an infinite-family theorem.
