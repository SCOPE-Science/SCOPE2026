For every wheel \(W_n\) with \(n\ge5\), the independence complexes of all generalized Mycielskians \(\mu_\ell(W_n)\) have explicit integral sphere-wedge homotopy types, with explicit dimensions and multiplicities as functions of \(n\) and \(\ell\), organized by their residue classes modulo six and three. A universal cone-cover splitting \(\operatorname{Ind}((K_1\vee H)\times K_2)\simeq\operatorname{Ind}(H\times K_2)\vee S^1\) yields an additional circle, reversed in homology by exchanging the two sheets.
