# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The complex is exactly specified by independent four-vertex sets in a sixteen-vertex graph. The executable checks all \(2^{16}\) candidate faces independently of facet generation, checks 30,319 actual disjoint matching pairs, and checks global acyclicity using **every** one of 469,288 nonempty face covers. One critical zero-cell and 84 critical eight-cells imply the integral sphere wedge by discrete Morse theory. Exact signed fixed-simplex Lefschetz sums and independent fixed-square-triple counts agree for all eight dihedral elements. Character equality implies rational (not integral) module isomorphism.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated risks
Bayer et al., arXiv:2209.13503, Table 3 already contain the numerical degree-eight Betti rank 84. This known rank is excluded from the originality claim. Their grid theorem establishes total two-cut homotopy, not total four-cut homotopy. Chandrakar et al., arXiv:2408.07646, treat narrower two-row and three-row grid regimes; Filakovský, arXiv:2602.11365, gives general-grid results at thresholds two and three, but not the present threshold-four case. Carnero Bravo, arXiv:2602.21427, does not supply this exact higher-cut rectangular-grid classification. The closest accessible published results on total three-cut complexes of four-by-four grids involve a distinct complex and cannot imply the claimed degree-eight homotopy or character. No equivalent stronger result was located in the targeted mathematical-result searches. Residual risk: unindexed or inaccessible stronger work may exist; no publication-level priority is certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This natural, previously tabulated but not homotopically classified high-dimensional complex supplies a concrete case in the higher-threshold grid problem. An explicit acyclic matching upgrades the already-known rank to **integral homotopy type**, while the full rational symmetry character distinguishes orientation-sensitive topology invisible in the Betti table. The value lies in those two structural refinements, not in recomputing the rank.

## Limitations
The result concerns one finite graph, not the conjectural all-grid family. It supplies a rational character, not an integral equivariant decomposition or equivariant homotopy equivalence. The evidence consists of an exhaustive transparent finite calculation and self-review, not an independent audit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
