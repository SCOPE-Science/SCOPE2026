The independence complex of the categorical product \(C_8\times K_3\) has integral homotopy type \(S^4\vee\bigvee^3 S^5\), resolving the torsion ambiguity for one example singled out in the literature. Its full natural \(D_8\times S_3\) action has \(\widetilde H_4\cong\alpha\beta\operatorname{sgn}\) and \(\widetilde H_5\cong\alpha(\operatorname{sgn}\oplus\operatorname{Std})\) over \(\mathbb Q\), with no global invariant in either degree.
