The four-row multiple chessboard complexes with capacities \(1,1,1,2\) admit a complete integral homotopy classification for every number of columns. The newly determined four-, five- and six-column cases include the mixed-dimensional \(\bigvee^{53}S^3\vee S^4\) at six columns, established by exhaustive acyclic Morse matchings and exact signed boundaries; the seven-column case is separately corroborated but already follows from published homological theorems.
