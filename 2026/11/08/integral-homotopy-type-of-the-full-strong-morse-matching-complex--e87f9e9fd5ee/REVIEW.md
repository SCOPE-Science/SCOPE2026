# Scientific review: full strong Morse matching complex of the nine-cycle

## Correctness — PASS
The unfilled graph and full (not pure) matching complex are defined using all injective vertex filtrations. Exactly one prior neighboring vertex is equivalent to a descending-dominated vertex in a one-dimensional nine-cycle, so exhaustive enumeration of all 362,880 vertex orders generates every strong gradient. Its full downward closure has 2,634 nonempty faces. Every one of the 1,314 proposed face--coface pairings is checked, and the complete reversed Hasse graph (2,634 vertices, 11,538 incidences) is topologically sorted. The six remaining critical cells are one vertex and five four-cells, so Forman's theorem proves the wedge of five four-spheres. The full original mod-two chain computation agrees independently; it is not substituted for an integral homotopy argument.

## Originality — PASS, with limits
Knudson--Owens-White, arXiv:2609.06844v1, fully defines the objects and gives integral homology-type data for the full nine-cycle complex, but proves cycle homotopy theorems only for the pure subcomplex. Its Table 1 is a homology calculation rather than the required integral homotopy classification. A published rotation-character result likewise treats the pure object. The already known integral homotopy type of the **full eight-cycle** is a different graph parameter; it does not imply the nine-cycle theorem. Searches of equivalent phrases, other matching-complex conventions, public mathematical records, and accessible prior claims revealed no proof supplying the all-incidence acyclic matching or the full nine-cycle homotopy type. Equivalence in unindexed literature is not ruled out.

## Value — PASS
The motivating 2026 source explicitly identifies full strong-cycle homotopy classification as difficult despite substantial homology tables. At the first nine-vertex instance of its observed three-step double-suspension pattern, the result certifies the *actual homotopy type* and supplies a perfect discrete-Morse vector field. The full and pure complexes differ by only 36 faces, yet the known pure type \(S^2\vee\bigvee^4S^4\) contrasts with the proved full type \(\bigvee^5S^4\). The topological change and exact optimal certificate make this a motivated natural classification, not merely another Betti-number computation.
