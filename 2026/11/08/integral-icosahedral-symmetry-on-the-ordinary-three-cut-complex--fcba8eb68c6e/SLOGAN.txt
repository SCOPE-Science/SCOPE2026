For the icosahedral graph, the ordinary three-cut complex has **integral**, not merely rational, symmetry modules: degree-seven homology is the sphere-orientation sign line and degree-eight homology is the signed permutation lattice on six antipodal vertex pairs. Under rotations the latter becomes the irreducible six-dimensional rational representation of \(A_5\), refining a previously known nonequivariant sphere-wedge calculation.
