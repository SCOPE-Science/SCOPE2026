## Correctness — PASS

The graph is fixed explicitly (two length-eight cycles and eight spokes). The certificate generates the entire augmented face set of 11,396 simplices, pairs 11,384 faces, and checks all 55,296 codimension-one incidences for directed-cycle freedom. Its four critical four-simplices and eight critical five-simplices yield a finite Morse CW model. A signed integer gradient-path computation gives rank-one boundary generated by a primitive vector \((0,1,-1,-1)\); the resulting Smith factor is one. Because the four-skeleton is a wedge of four four-spheres, Hurewicz identifies degree vectors with all five-cell attaching maps; elementary cell slides and cancellation yield \(\bigvee^3 S^4\vee\bigvee^7 S^5\). Independently generated simplicial mod-two homology agrees. Risk: an implementation error is possible in principle, mitigated by full reproducible combinatorial checks and the separate field calculation; there is no claim of external verification.
