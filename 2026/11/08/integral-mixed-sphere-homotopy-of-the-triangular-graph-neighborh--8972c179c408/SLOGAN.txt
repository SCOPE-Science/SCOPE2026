The open Lovász neighborhood complex of the triangular graph \(T_7=L(K_7)\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{246}S^3\vee\bigvee^{7}S^4\). An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching with 254 critical cells and a zero integral four-to-three Morse differential upgrades previously known homology to a complete mixed-sphere homotopy classification.
