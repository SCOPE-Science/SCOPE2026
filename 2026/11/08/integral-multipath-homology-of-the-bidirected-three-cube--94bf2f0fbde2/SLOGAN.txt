The multipath complex of the fully bidirected three-cube has torsion-free reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^6,\mathbb Z^{45},\mathbb Z^{36},\mathbb Z^{12}\) in four consecutive degrees \(3,4,5,6\), and zero elsewhere. Exact acyclic matchings and integral unit-pivot reductions determine the full homology; no sphere-wedge homotopy claim is made.
