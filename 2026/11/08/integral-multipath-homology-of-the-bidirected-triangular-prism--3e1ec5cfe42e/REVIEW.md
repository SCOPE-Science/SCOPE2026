# Scientific review: integral multipath homology of the bidirected triangular prism

## Correctness: PASS
The object is fixed by a six-vertex, nine-edge graph and the complete set of eighteen opposite directed arcs. The two predicates agree on all \(262144\) possible arc subsets and find \(687\) faces including empty. Both exact signed reductions match \(323\) pairs and check acyclicity on all \(2274\) Hasse incidences. Each leaves critical ranks \((9,26,6)\) in degrees two to four, with degree-three boundary rank four and degree-four boundary zero. A maximal-rank integral minor has determinant \(-1\), so no integral torsion occurs. Direct mod-two and mod-three simplicial calculations agree. This does **not** prove a homotopy wedge.

## Originality: PASS relative to inspected evidence
The 2022 multipath-complex foundational source [1] does not establish the bidirected triangular-prism calculation; the 2026 bidirectional-polygon study [2] treats maximum-degree-two underlying graphs; the no-oriented-cycles hypothesis of the matching comparison [3] fails for a bidirected graph. The complete directed chessboard complex in [4] does not yield the homology of this restricted arc set. Research-record and literature queries for arc multipaths, cycle-free partial injections and the precise graph found no covering result. A prior computation of the bidirected \(K_{3,3}\) multipath complex is explicitly **excluded** from the new finding. The graph's separately studied discrete Morse complex is not the present construction. Residual risk: an unindexed or inaccessible equivalent computation may exist.

## Value: PASS
The two connected simple cubic graphs on six vertices are exactly the triangular prism and \(K_{3,3}\), by complementing the two possible two-regular graphs on six vertices. Their bidirected multipath homology differs already in degree two. The new prism calculation completes this small natural topological comparison, providing a finite benchmark beyond the 2022 foundational families. No broader theorem or unverified homotopy decomposition is claimed.
