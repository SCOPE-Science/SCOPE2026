The multipath complex of the bidirected triangular prism has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^5\), \(\mathbb Z^{22}\) and \(\mathbb Z^6\) in degrees two, three and four, and zero elsewhere. Its degree-two classes distinguish it from the already-known bidirected \(K_{3,3}\) instance, the only other connected cubic graph on six vertices; no sphere-wedge homotopy type is asserted.
