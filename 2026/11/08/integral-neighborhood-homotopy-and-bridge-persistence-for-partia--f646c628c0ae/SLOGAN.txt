When two \(m\)-cliques are connected by matching edges one at a time, their open-neighborhood complexes move from two disjoint spheres to a circle wedge and then to a sphere wedge with \(t^2-3t+1\) new two-dimensional classes. For every \(m\ge4\), the complete integral persistence barcode has one transient circle, two permanent \((m-2)\)-sphere classes, and exactly \(2t-4\) additional permanent two-dimensional classes at each stage \(t\ge4\).
