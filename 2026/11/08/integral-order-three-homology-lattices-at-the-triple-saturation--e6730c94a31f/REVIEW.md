# Review

**Correctness: PASS.** The proof classifies the complete two-dimensional simplicial structure and gives an exact integral group-ring chain complex. Cancellation of the \(m-1\) tree edges and the injectivity of multiplication by \(g-1\) on \(R/(N)\) prove the infinite-family decomposition. The standalone checker validates independent finite cellular models and mod-three homology action for \(2\le m\le8\); it is not substituted for the algebraic proof.

**Originality: PASS relative to inspected evidence.** The principal direct antecedent is Adamaszek (2011), arXiv:1106.6250, whose full-text Section 6 treats nonequivariant homotopy splitting of cycle-power independence complexes with the main theorem for \(n\ge5r+4\). The present case is \(n=3r+3\); none of its inspected results identifies this integral order-three lattice. Related indexed rotation representations of cut complexes and other graph complexes have different chains or group actions. An unindexed equivalent integral calculation remains possible.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** Triple saturation is the first parameter at which three pairwise circularly separated vertices fit exactly, producing a geometrically intrinsic cyclic symmetry. The uniform mixed regular/Eisenstein lattice and mod-three Jordan invariants are not determined by nonequivariant sphere counts and may be used as a concrete equivariant benchmark.

The theorem is limited to one order-three symmetry subgroup; a full dihedral or equivariant-homotopy analysis is not asserted. The finite script corroborates, but does not independently certify, the uniform argument. Publication priority is not conclusively established.
