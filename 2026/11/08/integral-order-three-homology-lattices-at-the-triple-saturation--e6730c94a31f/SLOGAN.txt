At the triple-saturation threshold \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3m}^{m-1})\), rotation by one third of the cycle acts on integral first homology as \(\mathbb Z[C_3]^{\binom{m-1}{2}}\oplus\mathbb Z[\zeta_3]^{m-1}\) for every \(m\ge2\). The decomposition determines both the rational trace and the characteristic-three Jordan-block sizes, which ordinary sphere counts cannot recover.
