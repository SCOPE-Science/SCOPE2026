For two labeled, collision-free particles on an \(m\)-armed equal-length star, the first integral homology has a canonical crown-graph cycle lattice whose particle-exchange eigensublattices fail to split integrally by exactly \((\mathbb Z/2)^{m(m-3)/2}\). Its complete joint \(S_m\times C_2\) rational representation is the positive-exchange exterior square of the standard module plus the negative-exchange two-row Specht module.
