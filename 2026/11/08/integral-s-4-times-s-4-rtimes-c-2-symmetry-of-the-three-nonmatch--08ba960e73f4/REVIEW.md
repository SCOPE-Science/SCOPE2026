# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The domain consists of all edge subsets of \(K_{4,4}\) with matching number less than three, encoded by the exact 96 forbidden three-matchings. Exhaustive face enumeration yields 3017 augmented faces. The full 13504-incidence Hasse digraph under the explicit 1504-pair matching is acyclic and has nine critical 3-faces. Every cancellation is a unit over \(\mathbb Z\), hence the integral reduced homology is free and concentrated in degree three. The nine explicitly oriented six-cycle join spheres are actual integral cycles. Their critical-coordinate matrix is a negative signed permutation matrix of determinant \(-1\). This supplies integral primitivity, not a numerical guess. All 576 product symmetries, factor exchange and 1152 independent signed Lefschetz traces reproduce the stated action. The result is finite and exact; no unproved extension to arbitrary board sizes is used.

## Originality — PASS relative to checked sources
Theorem 1.4 of Linusson–Shareshian–Welker gives only the ordinary wedge-of-nine-three-spheres homotopy type for this board. Its Conjecture 1.14 **predicts** the rational hook tensor but does not prove it; the verified statement gives an actual integral lattice and the extra bipartition-swap action. Holmsen–Lee's homological and Leray vanishing theorem has a different conclusion. Indexed degree-bounded two-matching and Morse complexes have different face definitions. The closest checked earlier ledger claims concern nonmatching complexes on other graphs or the \(k=2\) complete-graph case. No checked record determines this integral \((S_4\times S_4)\rtimes C_2\)-module. A possibly unindexed equivalent source remains a genuine risk; this is not a priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The case \((4,4,3)\) is the first balanced three-nonmatching board with homology rank greater than one, making it a natural minimal nonscalar test of an explicitly stated 2004 representation conjecture. The integral tensor lattice, oriented generating cycles, and precise positive factor exchange carry strictly more mathematical structure than the previously known rank nine and ordinary sphere wedge. The claim does not require a general conjecture resolution to be useful, and it does not present a routine re-tabulation of the known Betti number.
