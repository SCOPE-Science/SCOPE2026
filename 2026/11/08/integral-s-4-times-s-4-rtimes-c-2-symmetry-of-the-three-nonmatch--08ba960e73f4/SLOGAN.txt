The nine integral third-homology generators of the three-nonmatching complex of \(K_{4,4}\) form \(\bigwedge^2L_4\otimes\bigwedge^2L_4\) under the full independent row-and-column symmetries. Exchanging the bipartition parts acts by the positive tensor flip, providing an integral and enlarged-symmetry refinement of a concrete case of the 2004 hook-representation conjecture.
