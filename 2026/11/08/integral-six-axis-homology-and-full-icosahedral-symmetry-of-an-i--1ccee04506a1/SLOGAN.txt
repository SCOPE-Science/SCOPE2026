The ordinary independence complex of the icosahedral graph is a canonical triangulated two-sphere with six antipodal edges attached, giving a nonequivariant homotopy type \(S^2\vee\bigvee^6 S^1\). Its full integral \(A_5\times C_2\) homology action is the signed permutation lattice on the six axes in degree one and the inversion-sign representation in degree two; first rational homology is a six-dimensional irreducible rational icosahedral module with negative central inversion.
