# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The physical legal-move graph and the Sierpiński swap-presentation graph are constructed separately; the full 27-vertex isomorphism determined by distance-to-corner triples is checked on all 39 edges and shown to commute with all six peg permutations. Thus computations on the swap graph apply to the classical legal-move graph, rather than silently equating two labeled adjacency rules. Exhaustive integer enumeration of all 108,144 independent sets, with the empty face excluded from the ordinary Morse matching, yields 54,065 matched pairs and exactly 13 critical faces. An explicit directed topological sort of all 108,143 nonempty faces certifies acyclicity, not merely a failure to discover a cycle. Signed integral Morse cancellation gives an exactly zero \(6\)-by-\(6\) differential in degrees seven-to-six. The CW complex has a single zero-cell and six cells each in dimensions six and seven; since \(\pi_6(\bigvee^6 S^6)\cong\mathbb Z^6\), these zero-degree attachments are nullhomotopic. This proves the ordinary wedge homotopy type, including the absence of torsion.

Independently lifted exact integer cycles from critical masks `18911508` and `72370452` have identically zero signed simplicial boundary. Their peg-permutation orbit matrices have integer determinants \(-1\) and \(+1\), proving the group translates are primitive **integral** homology bases and yield two regular group-ring lattices. All six signed Lefschetz traces provide an additional check. Exact code and reproducible actual output are included.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence
The relevant exact-object sources were examined as complete open texts, not only snippets. Park (arXiv:0809.1179, 2008) proves the full peg-permutation automorphism group. Chen, Wu, Huang and Deng (2016 online/2017 journal) establish independence-set recurrence/counts, including 108,144 faces for the graph represented here (with a shifted stage index). Sreeja, Vinodkumar and Ramkumar (2021) likewise count independent sets and give independence-polynomial statements. None of these primary results determines the homotopy or integral homology actions. Searches across graph-aliases, exact ranks, and homotopy/representation statements found indexed results on other independence complexes, not a stronger same-object theorem. The nearby truncated-cube, Coxeter-graph, and generalized-Mycielskian statements involve different adjacency relations; they cannot imply the specific Hanoi graph classification merely from matching dimensions. This search is not a proof of global novelty, and unindexed theses or disguised equivalent graph identifications remain risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The three-disk Hanoi state graph is a canonical 27-vertex self-similar finite graph rather than a parameter chosen solely to produce a favorable number. Its independence polynomial and number of independent sets were specifically studied in the mathematical literature, but those data do not determine its topology. The first exact mixed-dimensional sphere-wedge classification together with primitive integral regular homology lattices supplies a natural structural target for recursive calculations at higher disk numbers, and distinguishes group actions that ordinary Betti ranks cannot. A finite exact result is appropriate here: it resolves the complete natural invariants for this explicitly motivated stage, rather than asserting a routine count or a small numerical gain.

## Closest literature and limitations
The main exact-object comparisons are Park's automorphism theorem and the 2017/2021 independent-set papers. Other searched homotopy papers on named graph complexes cover nonisomorphic graphs. The theorem does not generalize to more disks and gives no equivariant homotopy splitting. Possible equivalent results under different naming and absent independent validation are residual limitations.
