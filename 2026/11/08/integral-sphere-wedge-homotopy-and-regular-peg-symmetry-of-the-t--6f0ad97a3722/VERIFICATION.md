# Computational verification and limits

The bundled program `verify_hanoi.py` reproduces the exact statements below on Python 3.10+ using only the standard library. All arithmetic is over \(\mathbb Z\); no approximate ranks or untested infinite extrapolations are involved.

- The legal-move graph and swap-presentation graph each have 27 vertices and 39 edges; the graph isomorphism defined by the injective distance triples to the three pure states is checked on every vertex, every edge, and every peg permutation. The complete complex contains 108,143 nonempty faces and 672,537 nonempty Hasse cover incidences. The explicit matching has 54,065 pairs; a complete topological sort proves no closed alternating path.
- The 13 critical cells occur in dimensions zero, six and seven, with multiplicities one, six and six. Signed integral Morse projection checks all 36 seven-to-six boundary coefficients are zero.
- Two critical masks `18911508` and `72370452` lift to exact integral cycles with 200 and 256 signed terms. Their boundaries are independently recomputed. The six group translates project to integer matrices of determinants \(-1\) and \(+1\), respectively; hence both degree-six and degree-seven homology modules are the integral regular module.
- Each of six permutations of the three pegs preserves all 39 graph edges, and the direct signed Lefschetz trace is 1 for every group element.

The standalone output file `verification_output.txt` is generated by executing the packaged Python file. The certificate establishes a finite theorem only; it does not test a family of larger Hanoi graphs or prove equivariant homotopy equivalence. Independent audit is not performed.
