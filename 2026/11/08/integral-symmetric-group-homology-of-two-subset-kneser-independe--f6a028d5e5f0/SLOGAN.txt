For every \(n\ge4\), the full label-permutation action on the second **integral** homology of the two-subset Kneser independence complex is the exterior cube of the standard augmentation lattice, \(\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z)\). It is rationally the irreducible hook representation \(S^{(n-3,1,1,1)}\), refining the known sphere-wedge homotopy type.
