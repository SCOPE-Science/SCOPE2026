For every \(n\ge2\), the integral first homology of the full strong Morse matching complex of \(K_{2,n}\) is the signed augmentation kernel on three bridge edges per bipartition vertex. Its \(S_2\times S_n\) rational character is \(3f-1\) without class swapping and \(1-f\) with swapping, where \(f\) counts fixed vertices in the \(n\)-element class.
