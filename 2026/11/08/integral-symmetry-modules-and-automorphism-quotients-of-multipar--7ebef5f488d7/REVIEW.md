The 2026 tree-vertex-decomposability preprint offers current context, not evidence for these equivariant claims. Unindexed or differently formulated results remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** The known ordinary Betti rank loses the group action and says nothing about rational homology of the orbit space. Here a natural uniform two-level invariant is determined for all part sizes and all thresholds: an integral representation including orientation-sensitive character values, and an automorphism quotient whose rank depends only on the number of **distinct** sizes. In particular identical-part graphs can have large nontrivial ordinary homology yet rationally acyclic symmetry quotients. This distinction is a motivated structural equivariant topological invariant, not a routine numerical extension.

**Important limits:** No integral torsion or homotopy classification for the orbit space, no general equivariant homotopy equivalence, and no claim for the already analyzed two-part case.
