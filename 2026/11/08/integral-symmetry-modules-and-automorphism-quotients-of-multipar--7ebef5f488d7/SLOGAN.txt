For complete multipartite graphs with at least three parts, every higher independence complex has a canonical torsion-free integral full-automorphism homology module given by an exterior augmentation quotient. The rational homology of its full automorphism quotient depends only on the number of distinct part sizes: its sole possible reduced Betti rank is \(\binom{t-1}{r}\) in degree \(r-1\), despite arbitrarily large ordinary homology ranks.
