For the higher-independence complex of every two-ended subdivided double star, both possible reduced integral homology groups are explicitly determined as natural automorphism modules: a hub-orientation-twisted exterior power of the full leaf augmentation lattice and a tensor product of the two leaf-block exterior powers. Interchanging the end hubs introduces the additional graded sign \((-1)^r\) in the upper degree, information not encoded by the already known sphere multiplicities.
