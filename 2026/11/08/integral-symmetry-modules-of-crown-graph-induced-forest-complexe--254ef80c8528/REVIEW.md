# Review of integral crown-forest symmetry modules

**Correctness — PASS.** The statement concerns precisely the integral \(S_n\times C_2\)-actions on reduced homology for \(n\geq3\), not a new sphere count. The established inclusion \(\mathcal F_2(C_n)\hookrightarrow\mathcal F_\infty(C_n)\) induces an integral homology isomorphism [2, Lemma 40]. The six-cycle sphere cover, contractible nonempty multiple intersections, and its nerve were reconstructed independently. The natural integral Mayer–Vietoris cover spectral sequence is nonzero only at \((0,4)\) and \((3,0)\) on its second page, so the filtration has no differentials or extension ambiguity. Its degree-four generators are the oriented boundaries of six-vertex cycle simplices; permutations act with positive triple-permutation sign and side exchange with negative sign. Its degree-three module is the degree-two homology of the triangle-skeleton nerve, which is \(\bigwedge^3 W_n\), with the negative suspension-tip-exchange sign. An independent program checked graph acyclicity, mod-two boundary ranks, explicit integral top cycles, and all 1,740 group elements for \(3\leq n\leq6\). Limits: the infinite statement is established by the integral cover argument, not finite enumeration; no equivariant homotopy statement follows.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected results, with an explicit proximity risk.** Taylan's first public 2015 paper defines the encompassing forbidden-subgraph construction [1]. Carnero Bravo's Proposition 41 already states the **complete nonequivariant** sphere-wedge homotopy type for this exact crown-graph family, and its proof already builds the sphere-and-simplex cover [2, PDF pp. 27–29]. Neither cited theorem states the complete integral homology actions or the negative layer-switch action in both degrees. This result tracks the cover spectrally as a functor of the group action, identifies its skeletal nerve homology as an augmentation exterior power, and computes the signed six-cycle generators. The group-module statements are strictly more structured than the stated homotopy type; the original proof's natural cover makes the refinement close, and priority from implicit unpublished extensions is not certified. Searches also compared related public record entries on graph configuration spaces and Morse complexes; they have different chain objects and do not imply this claim.

**Value — PASS.** Natural \(S_n\times C_2\) symmetry is central for crown graphs; a nonequivariant wedge of \(3\)- and \(4\)-spheres does not determine the associated integral representations. The theorem gives a natural full integer lattice, not just a rational dimension or selected cyclic trace, in both nonzero degrees. The shared negative action of exchanging the bipartition and the exterior cube of the augmentation lattice distinguish these actions for an infinite family. Such actions matter when taking invariants, coinvariants or symmetry-equivariant constructions. The mathematical motivation is the previously classified forest-filtration family, rather than an arbitrary parameter slice.
