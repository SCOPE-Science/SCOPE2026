For every crown graph \(K_2\times K_n\) with \(n\geq3\), its induced-forest complex has integral homology representations \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z[\binom{[n]}3]\otimes\varepsilon\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^n\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z)\otimes\varepsilon\), where \(\varepsilon\) is the sign of layer exchange. The previously known sphere multiplicities do not specify these full natural symmetry actions.
