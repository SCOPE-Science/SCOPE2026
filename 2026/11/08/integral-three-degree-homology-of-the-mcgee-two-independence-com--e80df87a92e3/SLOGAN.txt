The first higher-independence complex of the 24-vertex McGee cage has exactly \(\widetilde H_7\cong\mathbb Z^5\), \(\widetilde H_8\cong\mathbb Z\), and \(\widetilde H_9\cong\mathbb Z^2\), with no other reduced integral homology or torsion. Two exhaustive acyclic-Morse reductions and exact integer Smith forms establish the classification; no sphere-wedge homotopy type is asserted.
