The unordered three-point configuration space of the octahedral graph has torsion-free integral homology of ranks \(1,8,47\) in degrees \(0,1,2\), vanishing above degree two. The full \(48\)-element octahedral symmetry group acts on rational first and second homology by explicitly computed characters, with no globally fixed positive-degree class and a \(23+24\) eigenspace split under central inversion in second homology.
