For bidirected triangular books with three through six pages, exhaustive integral discrete-Morse reductions give \(\mathbb{RP}^2\vee\bigvee^7 S^2\), \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{17} S^2\), \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{22} S^2\vee\bigvee^{31}S^3\), and \(\bigvee^{38} S^2\vee\bigvee^{169} S^3\), respectively. Two-torsion occurs in degree one, then degree two, and is absent in the last tested case; no assertion is made for larger books.
