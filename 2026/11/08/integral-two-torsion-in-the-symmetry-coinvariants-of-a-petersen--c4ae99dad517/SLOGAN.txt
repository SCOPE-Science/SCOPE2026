For the Lovász neighborhood complex of the Petersen graph's line graph, the natural \(S_5\)-action has integral first-homology coinvariants \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\), even though its first homology is free of rank 31 and has no rational invariant line. The complete rational symmetry character is determined; the nonequivariant circle-wedge homotopy type is prior knowledge, not the novelty claim.
