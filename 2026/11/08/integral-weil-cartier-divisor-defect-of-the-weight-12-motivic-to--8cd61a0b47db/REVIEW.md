# Review of the integral divisor-group claim
**Correctness — PASS.** The polynomial equations define the normal toric base supplied by the primary source. The saturated character lattice, six primitive rays, explicit Smith minors, and local Cartier-support constraints are sufficient for the claimed global class and Picard groups. The witness \(2[D]=[H]\) is verified over the integers. Finite checks verify every arithmetic step, not an unproved generalization.

**Originality — PASS, with bibliographic risk.** Burmester–Charlton–Kidambi–Lotter–Preiß (2026), Example 3.8, gives the object, its equations, and polytope but not the global integral divisor groups. A same-object local singularity analysis does not compute the global Weil–Cartier quotient. The canonical toric divisor sequence is existing theory; only the explicit evaluated integral invariant is offered as new. The risk of earlier equivalent toric calculations remains.

**Value — PASS.** A complete integral divisor classification for the first nonsmooth motivic-MZV toric base isolates a free \(\mathbb Z^2\) obstruction and a less apparent order-two obstruction. It sharpens available geometric descriptions and is usable in subsequent divisor/Cartier and resolution analyses.

**Limits.** The result concerns only the normal projective base, not the larger cone or actual numerical multiple-zeta-value relations. The publication priority and independent proof audit have not been established.
