In a Bernoulli-generated one-ended recursive comb tree, the random conditional mean time for simple walk to reach backbone level \(m\) has a deterministic leading term \((1+q)m^2\) and Gaussian environment fluctuations of order \(m^{3/2}\) with exact asymptotic variance \(4pq/3\). The whole profile converges to integrated Brownian motion because the branch length and backbone advance form a jointly regenerative pair.
