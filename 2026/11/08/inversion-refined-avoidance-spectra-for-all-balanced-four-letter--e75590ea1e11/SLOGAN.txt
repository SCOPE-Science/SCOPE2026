For binary words with fixed numbers of zeros and ones, all six balanced four-letter scattered patterns have the same total number of avoiders, \(AB+1\), but their distributions across fixed \(01\)-subsequence counts split into a uniform, a divisor-sum, and a piecewise-linear type. The piecewise-linear type gives the sharp threshold \(C\ge A+B\) for universal occurrence of \(0011\) when both letter counts are at least two.
