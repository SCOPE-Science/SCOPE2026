For every \(n\ge4\), the projective second-hypersimplex toric variety has exactly \(\binom n2\) isolated singularities, each a rank-one \(2\times(n-2)\) determinantal cone, and its integral Weil–Cartier defect is \(\mathbb Z^n\). The local singularities are ordinary threefold nodes exactly at \(n=4\); for every \(n\ge5\) none is \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein.
