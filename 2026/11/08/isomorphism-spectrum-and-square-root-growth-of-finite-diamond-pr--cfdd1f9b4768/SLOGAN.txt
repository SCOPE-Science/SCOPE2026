Finite diamond preconditionals have an exact isomorphism spectrum governed by a four-colored functional-digraph quotient, with \(24,66,186,533\) classes for two through five atoms. Unlike their labeled count, their unlabelled count has the sharp Otter-rooted growth law \(U_m\sim K\rho^{-m}m^{-1/2}\), with an explicit positive constant \(K\).
