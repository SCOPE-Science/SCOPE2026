For a cyclic flat-plus-one-defect Gabor window with subgroup modulations, an erasure of entire translation fibers preserves a frame exactly when each residue class loses at most one translation; all surviving masks of equal size have identical, explicitly computed spectra. With \(m\) residue classes of \(q\) translations each, exactly \(\binom me q^e\) masks survive \(e\le m\) erasures; except for a four-dimensional endpoint, any nonempty admissible erasure destroys tightness.
