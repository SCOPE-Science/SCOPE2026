For every four-dimensional split commutative local algebra with radical Hilbert dimensions \((1,2,1)\), the exact rank distribution of idempotent weight-minus-one Rota–Baxter operators over \(\mathbb F_q\) is controlled by the number \(h\) of isotropic projective lines of its multiplication form: \(1+(1+hq^2)T+2hq^2T^2+(1+hq^2)T^3+T^4\). Nontrivial splittings exist precisely when the binary multiplication form is isotropic; characteristic two distinguishes alternating from nonalternating forms by growth orders \(q^3\) and \(q^2\).
