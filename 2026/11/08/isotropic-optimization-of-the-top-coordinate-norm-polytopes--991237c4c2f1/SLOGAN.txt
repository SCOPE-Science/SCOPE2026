The convex-hull top-coordinate norm balls \(B_d(k)=\operatorname{conv}(B_1^d\cup k^{-1}B_\infty^d)\) have an exact squared isotropic constant in every dimension; their global minimizer is found among at most two explicit algebraic candidates. As dimension grows, all minimizing parameters satisfy \(k/d\to(\sqrt3-1)/2\), and the optimal squared isotropic constant tends to \(e^{1-\sqrt3}/8\), strictly below either endpoint's limiting value.
