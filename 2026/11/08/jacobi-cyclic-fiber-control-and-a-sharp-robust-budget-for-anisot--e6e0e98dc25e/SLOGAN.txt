For a Jacobi path factor coupled to any symmetric network with a cyclic root, the exact minimum number of coordinate actuators equals the largest product-spectrum multiplicity, and that many consecutive vertices on one root fiber always attain it. Consequently, an anisotropic \(n\times n\) square grid with an unknown common positive coupling in one direction needs exactly \(n-1\) fixed boundary actuators for controllability at every coupling, for all \(n\ge3\).
