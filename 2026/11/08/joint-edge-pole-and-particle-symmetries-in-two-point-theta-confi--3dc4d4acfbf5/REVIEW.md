# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The finite deleted-product model is natural for every graph automorphism and particle swap. The all-parameter proof counts signed fixed cells in all four pole/particle cases and uses the known connected-graph homotopy type for two labeled particles on banana graphs. The resulting first-homology character is checked independently by full cellular enumeration for all 3,480 symmetry elements at arm counts three through six. The stated rational representation is determined by character equality in characteristic zero. Pole reversal is \(-I\) on the integral first homology because the graph-retract result makes the group free abelian. The finite checks are supplementary rather than a proof for unbounded \(m\).

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence

Barnett–Farber (2009) already gives the planar deleted-product and homology machinery; Lütgehetmann–Recio-Mitter (2018) already gives the graph-homotopy type of the same two-particle banana configurations. Those established portions are expressly credited. Neither inspected paper states a joint \(S_m\times C_2\times C_2\) character, its explicit signed ordered-pair quotient, or the integral negative pole action. The graph-braid presentation treatment of An–Maciążek (2021) focuses on presentations and theta examples with different particle counts. The precise joint representation was also compared against indexed mathematics records for theta/banana, all-vertex-sink two-particle configurations, and three-particle theta relations. The main residual priority risk is an unindexed equivalent symmetry calculation or an implicit derivation from older graph-braid generators.

## Value — PASS

The generalized theta/banana family is a canonical two-vertex, multicycle graph family, and two-particle configurations are the first collision-sensitive case. A uniform three-factor symmetry character, with Specht decomposition and an integral scalar-action theorem, distinguishes natural particle interchange from pole interchange and captures topology invisible in the established Betti rank and graph-retract statements. The formula is not a specialization of the adjacent star or sink-collision problems.

## Limits

The integral signed ordered-pair quotient lattice is not identified; only the rational representation and the integral pole-involution action are proved. Independent verification and definitive publication-priority determination are outstanding.
