For two ordered particles on an \(m\)-arm theta graph, \(m\ge3\), the complete joint action of arm permutations, pole reversal, and particle exchange on rational first homology is a signed ordered-arm-pair permutation module with its constant line removed. Pole reversal is exactly \(-I\) even on integral first homology, while the particle-exchange character depends only on the number of two-cycles of the arm permutation.
