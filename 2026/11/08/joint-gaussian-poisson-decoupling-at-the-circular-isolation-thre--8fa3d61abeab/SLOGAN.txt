In a circular one-dimensional hard random geometric graph at the isolation window \(r_n=(\log n+x)/(2n)\), the centered number of connected components has Gaussian fluctuations of order \(n^{1/4}\), while the isolated-vertex count has a Poisson limit; their two limits are independent. Yet their unscaled covariance converges to the strictly positive constant \(2e^{-x}\).
