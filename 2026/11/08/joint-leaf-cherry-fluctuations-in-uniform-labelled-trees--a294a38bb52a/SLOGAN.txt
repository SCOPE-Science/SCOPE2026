In uniform labelled trees, the number of leaves and the number of degree-three cherries have an exact strictly positive covariance at every order \(n\ge5\), and their centered counts converge jointly to a nondegenerate Gaussian law. Their limiting correlation is \(\sqrt{2(e-2)/(e^3-3)}\approx0.289967\): local symmetry-generating cherry counts retain positive fluctuations with leaves even though degree-one and degree-three counts alone asymptotically decorrelate.
