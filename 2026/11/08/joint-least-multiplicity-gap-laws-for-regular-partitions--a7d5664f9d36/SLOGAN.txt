For uniform \(\ell\)-regular partitions, all fixed least-multiplicity-gap levels have an exact simultaneous finite-size counting formula. After scaling squared gaps by \(\sqrt n\), their joint law converges to successive running minima of independent rate-\(\pi(1-1/\ell)^{3/2}/(2\sqrt6)\) exponentials, a dependence law not captured by the known one-threshold Rayleigh marginals.
