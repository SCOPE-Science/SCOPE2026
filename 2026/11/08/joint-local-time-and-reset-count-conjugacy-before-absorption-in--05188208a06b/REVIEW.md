# Scientific review: joint local-time and reset-count conjugacy

**Correctness (PASS).** The fixed-point occupation local time has a well-defined Kac ordered-time expansion for the stated class of killed regular diffusions. Conditioning on either terminal event of a single exponentially stopped cycle gives \(m!p_jG^m\), where the last visit contributes the same terminal probability \(p_j\) by the strong Markov property and reset-clock memorylessness. These moments force an exponential local-time law independent of terminal success or failure. Geometric concatenation proves the complete joint transform, conditional gamma and Poisson distributions and correlation. The occupation-density normalization matches the original source's delta-function convention. Numerical checks from `verify.py` passed 462 diagnostic assertions, but no finite checks are used as infinite proofs.

**Originality (PASS, qualified).** Dubey and Pal, arXiv:2304.05226 (2023), Sections 2–3, analyze first-passage Ornstein–Uhlenbeck local-time marginals and expectations under Poisson resetting, not reset-count-conditioned local time. Singh and Pal, arXiv:2202.02715 (2022), Sections 2–3, treat corresponding Brownian local-time marginals. Pal et al., arXiv:1902.00907 (2019), Sections III–IV, treat fixed-observation-time local time, sometimes conditioned on survival, not absorption-stopped bivariate statistics. Toledo-Marin and Boyer, arXiv:2206.14387 (2022), treat mean reset counts with random reset positions. Existing local work on joint first-passage maximum/reset count studies a distinct path statistic and does not imply the local-time law. The bivariate conditional distributions are not established by any marginal alone. The argument uses classical Markov and Kac identities, so a more general excursion-theory theorem may cover it; that remains a publication-priority risk.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The exact Poisson law of reset effort conditional on accumulated occupation at the reset site is a new structural answer to a natural stochastic-search intervention-cost question, not a recomputation of a mean. It yields a fully resolved count/exposure coupling, all conditional moments, a drift-independent correlation formula at fixed one-cycle survival probability, and an explicit Ornstein–Uhlenbeck physical application. It separates experimentally measurable local exposure from the number of control interventions and gives sharp inference over the entire distribution rather than a numerical approximation.

**Remaining scope and scientific limitations.** Reset position must coincide with the occupation-density point, the reset clock must be independent exponential, the initial location must equal the reset location, and the killed diagonal resolvent must be finite. The given model does not establish analogous conjugacy for renewal resets, distributed reset positions, or non-Markov environments. The cited primary manuscripts were read in publicly accessible full-text form for the relevant propositions; no independent expert verification or priority guarantee is represented.
