For a one-dimensional diffusion reset at a Poisson rate to the point where its occupation local time is measured, the reset count given the total pre-absorption local time is exactly Poisson with intensity \((1-h)L/G\). Equivalently, local time given \(n\) resets is Gamma with shape \(n+1\), and the universal count/local-time correlation is \(\sqrt{1-h}\), where \(h\) is the success probability of one reset-free cycle.
