For finite Alexander-quandle algebras over splitting finite fields, the complete joint distribution of left- and right-multiplication ranks is a closed bivariate polynomial controlled by \(\operatorname{rank}(I-\varphi)\) and \(\operatorname{rank}((I-\varphi)^2)\). The separate rank distributions lose the second invariant, but the joint census detects it and distinguishes explicit quandle actions with identical marginals.
