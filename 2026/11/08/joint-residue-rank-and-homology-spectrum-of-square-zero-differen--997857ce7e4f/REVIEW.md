# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** For each fixed square-zero residue matrix, the anti-commutator equations force an explicit block form. The restricted residual blocks are unrestricted and contribute a known power of \(q\). The only block affecting the underlying-field rank is the vertically stacked \(ds\times s\) matrix on the residual homology space. The kernel-counting argument proves \(\operatorname{rank}_kX=(d+1)r+t\). Gaussian binomial enumeration then proves all fibers, and rank-nullity proves the homology and sharp-gap conclusions. Exact exhaustive examples in the provided standalone verifier agree. A finite computation is not substituted for the general proof.

## Originality
**PASS, provisional priority.** The nearest primary source on finite-ring polynomial matrix equations, Cheong–Liang–Strand (arXiv:2209.03626v1, first public 2022-09-08), assumes a square-free/irreducible reduced polynomial and is not applicable to this repeated-root equation over a square-zero multi-parameter local algebra. D'Alesio (DOI:10.1007/s00029-021-00724-4) identifies the square-zero matrix representation variety but does not supply the joint rank-counting result in the inspected examples and representation-functor discussion. A coarser count only by residue rank is subsumed and is not claimed to be new. Searches of rank-refined, homology, local-ring, lifting, dual-number, and matrix-square-zero formulations found no covering result. Stronger unlocated matrix-lifting or representation-variety formulas remain a genuine publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** For a square-zero differential on a finite free module, the dimension of homology is a canonical invariant, controlling exactness of the associated periodic complex; the residue-rank marginal does not determine it. The theorem gives all its finite-field multiplicities for every size and embedding dimension. It also exposes the sharp acyclicity boundary for non-Gorenstein square-zero local rings and noncontiguous rank spectra already for free modules of rank two. These properties are natural structural data rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limits
The result treats split commutative local algebras with radical square zero. It does not classify the module structure of homology, conjugacy classes, or higher-radical-power rings. Priority has not been independently certified.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
