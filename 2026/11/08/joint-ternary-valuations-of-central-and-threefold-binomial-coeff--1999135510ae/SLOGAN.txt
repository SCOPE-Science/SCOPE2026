The valuations \(\nu_3\!\left(\binom{2n}{n}\right)\) and \(\nu_3\!\left(\binom{3n}{n}\right)\), over a full ternary interval, have an explicit rational bivariate generating function and a complete \(r^2+r+1\)-point support. Their covariance is strictly positive but remains bounded (tending to \(3/16\)), while the centered, square-root-scaled pair converges to independent Gaussians.
