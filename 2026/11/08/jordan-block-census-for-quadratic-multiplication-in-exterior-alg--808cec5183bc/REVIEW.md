# Scientific assessment: quadratic exterior-multiplication Jordan census

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem follows from the alternating normal form, the four-dimensional local exterior-module decomposition \(J_2\oplus2J_1\), the explicit modular rule for the additive tensor of \(J_s\) with \(J_2\), and the transfer recurrence \(T_p\). The proof separately justifies the maximal-block count by dimension, the exact Gaussian/symplectic count of quadratic forms of each alternating rank, and the spectral-rate estimate. Characteristic two, zero alternating rank, and the boundary block \(J_p\) were handled explicitly. Independent finite cases were checked by full exterior-multiplication matrices and an unrelated subset-incidence rank formula. Computation is corroboration, not the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The closest literature is Nguyen, *Koszul hypersurfaces over the exterior algebras* (arXiv:1406.0489): it explicitly uses the same alternating normal form but addresses Koszulness of the quotient rather than counts of modular Jordan types. Glasby, Praeger and Xia, *Decomposing modular tensor products* (arXiv:1403.4685), and earlier Green-ring results already cover modular tensor-block decompositions. Their formulas imply an algorithm once an exterior-factorization is supplied, but their displayed conclusions do not give the asserted path-graph closed transfer, finite-field populations, or asymptotic saturation for all quadratic exterior forms. Those component facts are acknowledged, not claimed original. Context on Lefschetz ranks (Cook, arXiv:1111.4979), Jordan types (Iarrobino–Macias Marques–McDaniel, arXiv:1802.07383), and elementary abelian group modules (Carlson–Friedlander–Pevtsova, arXiv:1106.4474) was also compared. Searches of an external mathematical-results index using exact objects, aliases and Jordan-rank parameters found nearby but materially different claims, such as a characteristic-two exterior-algebra Rota–Baxter operator count and Jordan blocks over dual numbers. The accessible existing research claims were compared for overlap, including the previous five-variable characteristic-two group-algebra multiplication example. The mathematical contributions assessed here are an arbitrary-rank, arbitrary-characteristic population theorem and the sharp spectral saturation. A general theorem not yet located on symplectic modular Lefschetz modules or induced Green rings could subsume them, and the literature comparison is not a priority certificate.

## Value — PASS
The elementary alternating rank classification determines only the equivalence classes of quadratic forms; it does not specify the nilpotent operator's modular Jordan partition. The result gives a natural complete population census of a linear operator invariant across every dimension, field size and characteristic. The tiny path-transfer matrix is independent of ambient dimension and exposes a precise phenomenon: a generic high-alternating-rank multiplication operator becomes almost entirely free over \(k[t]/(t^p)\), at a calculable characteristic-dependent exponential rate. These exact, reusable invariants strengthen isolated Koszul/non-Koszul and single-power rank tests. Further results on graded Jordan data would be valuable but are not required for this theorem.

## Verification limits
The direct brute-force checks cover finite examples only. Algebraic completeness rests on the elementary proof and not on extrapolation. The standalone checkers do not perform an independent mathematical audit. Overlap checks compare statements and implications and cannot exclude unknown stronger papers. The original article establishing one general subset-incidence rank theorem was not necessary for the proof and was not relied on for scientific completeness.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
