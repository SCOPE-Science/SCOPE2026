For a quadratic two-form of alternating rank \(2r\), multiplication on an exterior algebra in characteristic \(p\) has nonmaximal Jordan-block multiplicities given by the first column of \((2I+A(P_{p-1}))^r\), with the size-\(p\) multiplicity fixed by dimension. Exact finite-field alternating-form counts turn this into a complete Jordan-type population formula, and the proportion of the module in maximal blocks approaches one with an explicit exponential rate as \(r\) grows.
