For the positive-degree nilradical of the rank-one Witt Lie algebra in characteristic \(p>3\), the complete Jordan partition of every nilpotent adjoint action depends only on the first nonzero exponent of its coefficient polynomial; every power rank and finite-field multiplicity is explicit. This rigidity fails in the ambient Witt algebra: \(x^2\partial_x\) and \((x^2+x^3)\partial_x\) have ambient adjoint partitions \(\operatorname{sort}(p-3,3)\) and \((p-1,1)\), respectively.
