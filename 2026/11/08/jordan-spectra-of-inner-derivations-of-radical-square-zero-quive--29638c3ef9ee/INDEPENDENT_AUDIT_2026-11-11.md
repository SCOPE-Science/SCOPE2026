## originality

PASS

Two older published theorems already give pointwise commutator rank, simple-graph matroid rank distribution and forest rank polynomial, and must be credited. However they explicitly exclude parallel/opposite arrows on the same unordered pair, and do not classify all zero/nonzero eigenvalue Jordan blocks or the bivariate (ρ,h) law. Their univariate rank polynomial does not determine its bivariate refinement. The current complete multigraph spectral classification is a proper extension, though close to the same incidence calculation.
