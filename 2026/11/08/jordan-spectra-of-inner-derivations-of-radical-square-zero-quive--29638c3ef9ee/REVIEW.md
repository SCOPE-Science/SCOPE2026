# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The claimed operator is reconstructed directly from the radical-square-zero multiplication table. A block shear eliminates the nonzero-eigenvalue incidence coupling, leaving an invertible diagonal block and a square-zero weighted-incidence block. The incidence-matrix kernel consists of colorings constant on nonzero-weight edge components, giving exactly the zero Jordan block multiplicities. Every field characteristic is included because the spanning-forest incidence-rank argument is characteristic-free. Formula (1) partitions the entire finite algebra into disjoint color/support cases, formula (2) follows from a two-term edge expansion, and formula (3) follows from independent forest edges. Two executable checkers confirm 11 small complete algebra element censuses and four bivariate state-sum comparisons. These finite calculations do not replace the proof.

## Originality

**PASS, subject to historical-priority risk.** Strametz, Sanchez-Flores, and Guo--Li treat the derivation/cohomology structure of monomial or quiver algebras. Their inspected relevant portions do not give this all-element Jordan classification with its two-variable finite-field distribution. The strongest same-algebra comparison is an existing commutator-fiber and commuting-probability formula: its commuting probability equals one evaluation of the present polynomial but it does not state the zero-Jordan block populations or tree binomial law. Earlier incidence rank and Tutte/Potts transformations are used and credited; they are not claimed as innovations. The possibility of an overlooked stronger paper giving the precise distribution, or an equivalent pre-existing graph polynomial that makes the state sum routine, remains a residual risk.

## Value

**PASS.** Jordan type of an inner derivation, and the associated centralizer dimension, are canonical invariants of associative algebra elements. The result determines their complete paired distribution for all loopless radical-square-zero quiver algebras over all finite fields. In particular, all orientations and shapes of a fixed forest have the same exact rank law, while existence of a maximal-rank square-zero inner derivation detects precisely the forest condition. This is a natural complete classification rather than an arbitrary small parameter slice. It also exposes the graph-matroid structure of operator ranks, not just their average commuting probability.

## Limits and nearby work

The claim concerns labeled elements, not distinct derivation operators; it does not extend to quivers with loops or higher radical powers. The older commutator-fiber theorem is not repackaged as new. Published literature checked includes the full-text Strametz monomial-algebra preprint, the Guo--Li path-algebra derivation paper, and the Sanchez-Flores radical-square-zero cohomology study. Their different purposes do not themselves prove novelty, and broader unpublished or inaccessible treatments may exist.
