For every loopless radical-square-zero quiver algebra, the Jordan form of each inner derivation is controlled by the monochromatic nonzero-edge incidence forest: its only nontrivial blocks are zero-eigenvalue blocks of size two. The exact joint Jordan-profile enumerator is an orientation-independent graphic-matroid state sum; for any forest it collapses to \(q^c(1+(q-1)s+q(q-1)t)^m\), giving a topology-independent binomial rank law.
