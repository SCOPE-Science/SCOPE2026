For the Gallo–Videla three-step Lie algebra of every path \(P_n\) with \(n\ge2\), over any field, the Kirillov index is exactly \(n-1\). For an explicit maximal functional, the one-dimensional radical modulo the length-two-path span is supported on vertices when \(n\not\equiv2\pmod3\), but on every third edge when \(n\equiv2\pmod3\).
