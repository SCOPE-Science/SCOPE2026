For the three-step graph Lie algebra of the once-subdivided \(m\)-arm star, the Kirillov index over every field is exactly \(m(m+1)/2+2\lceil m/2\rceil-1\). The difference between index and center dimension is \(2\lceil m/2\rceil-1\), revealing an unbounded generic coadjoint-rank defect within a natural tree family.
