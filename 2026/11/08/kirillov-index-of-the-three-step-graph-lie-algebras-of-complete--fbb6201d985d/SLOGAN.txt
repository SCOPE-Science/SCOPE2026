For the Gallo–Videla three-step Lie algebra of every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\), the Kirillov index over any field is \(b\binom a2+a\binom b2+ab-a-b\), except at the four-cycle \(K_{2,2}\), where it is \(6\). A unimodular mixed-bracket minor proves generic maximal Kirillov rank, while the exceptional four-cycle has an identically singular mixed block.
