In a near-mass-lumped one-dimensional finite-element heat discretization, the influence between the two outermost interior nodes changes sign **exactly \(n-2\) times** for each fixed \(n\ge3\) and sufficiently small nonzero consistent-mass fraction. All crossing times have leading terms given by the positive zeros of \(L_{n-2}^{(1)}\), while the three-node problem admits a sharp all-fractions positivity-restoration threshold.
