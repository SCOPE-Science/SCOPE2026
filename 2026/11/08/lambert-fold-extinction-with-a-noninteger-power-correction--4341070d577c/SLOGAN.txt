At the collision of two Lambert-W extinction directions in an exponential ratio-dependent predator–prey model, an open wedge of positive populations obeys \(v/u-z_*\sim 1/(\kappa t)\) but \((u,v)\sim C e^{-\mu t}t^{-2q/(1-q)}(1,z_*)\). A nearby exceptional stable curve has pure exponential decay, and the critical extinction wedge can coexist with a stable prey-only state.
