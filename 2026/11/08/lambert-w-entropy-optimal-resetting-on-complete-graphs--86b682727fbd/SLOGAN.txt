For an observed resetting random walk on the \(n\)-vertex complete graph, every entropy-maximizing reset probability satisfies \((n-1)^2\gamma_{\mathrm{opt}}=W(n-1)+o(1)\), not an order-\(1/n\) reset law. The maximum entropy gain above ordinary random walk is \([W(n-1)^2/2+W(n-1)+o(1)]/(n-1)^3\), because reset moves overlapping ordinary moves must be merged before computing path entropy.
