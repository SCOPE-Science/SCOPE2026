For the involution exchanging layers of the prism \(H\square K_2\), every robust clique fixed-point complex is a wedge of \((k-2)\)-spheres when \(H\) has at most one odd cycle. If its sole odd cycle has length \(\ell\), the rank is exactly \(\binom{N-1}{k-1}-\binom{N-\ell}{k-\ell}\), showing an arithmetic change invisible to the ordinary simplex skeleton.
