# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof couples orders of original edges and new leaf edges by shared continuous priorities and follows one alternating discrepancy. It proves core-edge count is nonincreasing and therefore total matching size nondecreasing. The first-edge path decomposition establishes an exact finite rational recurrence, while an entire-function representation establishes the mean boundary constant and remainder. Replaying the included standard-library checker gave `VERIFY_OK 541040 exact and seeded checks`, including independent permutation enumeration and priority-coupling comparisons. These finite checks do not replace the analytic proof.

## Value

**PASS.** Strict first-order stochastic improvement under private-leaf augmentation is structurally useful for all finite core graphs, and the decorated-path distribution yields exact tails, an attainable-size classification and unusually sharp finite-size means. Its motivation is random-greedy matching under controlled graph growth, not a cosmetic variant or a finite table. The method does not assert arbitrary graph edits or new general RSA theory.
