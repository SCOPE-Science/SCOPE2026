Attaching additional private leaves to every vertex of a finite graph stochastically increases the size of its random-order greedy edge matching, and strictly so if the original graph has an edge. For a path core the full finite-size distribution satisfies an explicit Riccati generating equation, and its mean has an exact boundary constant with a remainder of order \(O_{q,R}(R^{-n})\) for every \(R>1\).
