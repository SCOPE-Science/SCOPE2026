The complex of subsets of the thirteen points of \(\mathrm{PG}(2,3)\) containing no whole four-point line has integral homotopy type \(S^5\vee\bigvee^{39}S^6\). The full projective collineation group acts without invariant classes on the 39-dimensional degree-six rational homology, so the symmetry quotient has reduced rational homology \(\mathbb Q\) only in degree five.
