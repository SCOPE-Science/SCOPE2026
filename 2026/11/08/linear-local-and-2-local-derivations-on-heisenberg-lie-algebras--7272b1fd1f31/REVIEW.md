# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reconstructs every ordinary derivation from the symplectic bracket. The crucial surjectivity of evaluation on a nonzero vector is proved through arbitrary symmetric bilinear forms, valid also in characteristic two. The simultaneous witness construction treats independent and dependent quotient projections separately, while an isotropic-pair test forces exactly the conformal-symplectic equation. The dimension and Gaussian rank counts follow algebraically. Original equations and witness existence were checked by the embedded `verify.py`; exhaustive finite checks are corroboration, not infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS (subject to independent priority review)
The archival Ayupov–Kudaybergenov 2015 preprint proves existence of nonglobal local derivations on filiform algebras (including the three-dimensional Heisenberg algebra), but does not state the uniform hierarchy, the linear 2-local space, or a rank census in its examined Sections 1 and 4. A related 2015 2-local paper proves general existence, but its full text was not accessible; abstract-level comparison does not establish noncoverage. Relevant published and searchable records were compared by mathematical implication, including related Heisenberg centralizer/automorphism/census papers, which do not imply this classification. The main residual risk is an unlocated general theorem about local operator spaces or conformal-symplectic reflexive hulls, or a statement in the inaccessible 2015 full text.

## Value — PASS
Local-to-global rigidity and two-point witnesses are central questions for algebra derivations. The complete three-step hierarchy across all dimensions and characteristics distinguishes pairwise compatibility from pointwise compatibility; it identifies the first strict dimension threshold and gives exact field-uniform counts and ranks. The finite-field rank law is natural as a structural population statistic; the claim is not limited to a small arbitrary slice. It does not claim to classify nonlinear 2-local maps.
