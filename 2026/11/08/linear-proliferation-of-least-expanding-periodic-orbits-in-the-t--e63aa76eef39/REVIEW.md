# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The three-dimensional Jacobian cocycle is signed monomial. For three-divisible right-visit counts the exponent vector is explicit in the three cyclic gap totals; otherwise every eigenvalue has modulus \(2^{n/3}\). The lower bound follows from the integer winding count, and the exact \(n(n-3)\) census follows from a marked-right-symbol count of weak compositions with an empty component. A proper-power argument proves the claimed least-period property. The checker explicitly solves real rational periodic points for all words up to period nine, but finite data are not treated as the all-period proof.

## Originality
**PASS within inspected evidence.** The original twisted-baker paper proves qualitative nonuniform expansion. The indexed real-spectrum-residue result gives the characteristic-polynomial structure but no sharp minimizer census; a prior planar fine-spectrum result covers dimension two only. The dimension-three zero-gap classification and linear orbit count require a separate combinatorial analysis and are not statements implied by the existing two-dimensional formulas. The nearby heterochaos literature concerns different systems. There remains uncertainty about equivalent counts in unindexed sources.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The three-dimensional twisted baker map was introduced as a canonical model of nonuniform periodic expansion. An exact finite-period extremal multiplier and a complete count of primitive orbits achieving it clarify how weak periodic repulsion is realized geometrically. The contrast between a single weakest orbit in the planar model and \(n-3\) weakest orbits in dimension three distinguishes a structural dimension effect, rather than merely recomputing a small-period table.

The result is restricted to the specified dimension-three family; the entire higher-dimensional spectral distribution remains open to separate investigation. The symbolic correspondence is taken from the primary source. Independent audit and publication-priority certification have not been performed.
