In the three-dimensional twisted baker map, the smallest normalized multiplier among nontrivial period-dividing-\(n\) points equals \(2^{1/n}\) for every \(n\ge4\). Exactly \(n(n-3)\) points attain it, forming \(n-3\) primitive \(n\)-cycles characterized by three right-branch visits with at least one empty cyclic gap.
