For the concept class of all maximal independent sets of a connected path, the exact VC dimension is \(\lfloor(n+1)/3\rfloor\), but a complete depth-four online decision tree already appears at ten vertices, where VC dimension is three. Repeating that ten-vertex gadget across one-vertex separators gives paths on \(11k-1\) vertices whose Littlestone dimension exceeds their VC dimension by at least \(\lceil k/3\rceil\).
