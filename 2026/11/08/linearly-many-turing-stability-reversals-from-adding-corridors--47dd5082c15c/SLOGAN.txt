For a single fixed two-species ecological diffusion model, adding only new unit-weight habitat corridors can alternate local linear stability and instability after every addition, for \(2\lfloor(n-1)/3\rfloor\) consecutive additions on \(n\) connected habitats. A spectral monotonicity bound proves that the largest possible number of such reversals is of linear order in \(n\), even though every added corridor increases the graph Laplacian.
