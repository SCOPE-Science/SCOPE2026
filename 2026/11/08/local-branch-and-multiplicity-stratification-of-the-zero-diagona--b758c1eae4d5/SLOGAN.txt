Every nonzero point of the zero-diagonal rank-one minimal-nilpotent fiber has a completed local ring given by a smooth factor times \(c\) independent nodal crossings, where \(c\) is the number of indices missing from both row and column supports. Consequently it has exactly \(2^c\) branches and multiplicity \(2^c\), and its reduced singular locus has \(m(2^{m-1}-2)\) irreducible divisors.
