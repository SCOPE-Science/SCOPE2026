For the free two-step nilpotent Lie algebra on \(n\ge3\) generators over any field of characteristic different from \(2\), every linear local derivation is an independent pair of endomorphisms on the generators and the exterior-square commutator ideal, together with an arbitrary central shear. The complete local-derivation space has dimension \(2n^2+n\binom n2\), exceeding ordinary derivations by exactly \(n^2\) dimensions.
