For every integer \(P\ge2\), the prime-part sequences of \(U_{n+2}=PU_{n+1}+U_n\) are locally Dold and realizable exactly at the odd primes where \(P^2+4\) is a nonzero quadratic residue; these primes have density \(1/2\). Exact repair factors at every bad prime are determined, and all local prime-part sequences are almost realizable.
