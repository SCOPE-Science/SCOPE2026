On a star of indivisible goods with identical nonnegative additive values and nonempty connected bundles, an allocation is locally PMMS-fair exactly when it is leximin; all fair allocations and their labeled counts admit a closed-form sorted-leaf/zero-center classification. With distinct positive leaf values and a zero-valued center, increasing the number of agents from \(k-1\) to \(k\) changes the fair fraction from \(1/\binom{k}{2}\) to \(1\), while every positive center value leaves only a \(1/k\) fraction fair at \(k\) agents.
