For every odd prime \(p\), the Fourier matrix restricted to the norm-one conic \(\{(x,y)\in\mathbb F_p^2:x^2+y^2=1\}\) is invertible, and its complete local cyclotomic Smith exponents are \(0,1,1,2,2,\ldots,m-1,m-1,m\), where \(m\) is half the conic's size. Its determinant has exact uniformizer valuation \(m^2\), giving a sharp arithmetic profile of finite-conic Fourier reconstruction.
