For a connected strongly regular graph with fixed parameters, the degree law at one vertex in a uniform spanning tree determines, and is determined by, the adjacency spectrum of that vertex's neighborhood. Consequently the sixteen-vertex Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have identical first two tree-degree moments but different third factorial moments, with generating-function difference \(z(z-1)^3(5z+27)^2/2^{23}\).
