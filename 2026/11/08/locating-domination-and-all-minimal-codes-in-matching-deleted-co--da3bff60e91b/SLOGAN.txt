For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), the entire ordinary locating-domination polynomial is \(z^n(2+z)^n+nz^{n-1}(2+z)^{n-1}-2n^2z^{n-1}-2nz^n\), valid for every \(n\ge4\). When \(n\ge5\), all inclusion-minimal codes have size \(n-1\) or \(n\), with exact multiplicities \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\) and \(2n(n-1)+2\), respectively.
