In the Bernoulli-driven fractional telegraph with holding-clock rate proportional to the number of visits to each direction, the expected positive residence fraction after any fixed number of completed intervals is **exactly independent of the fractional index**. Its event-count limit is a Lamperti stable ratio that ignores any fixed direction bias; polynomially rare directions change the limiting law, while a sharp \(\log(p/(1-p))/\log n\) correction recovers the hidden bias.
