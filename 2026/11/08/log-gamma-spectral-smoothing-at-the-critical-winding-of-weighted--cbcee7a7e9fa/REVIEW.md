# Scientific assessment: Log-Gamma spectral smoothing at the critical winding of weighted Bergman boundary spirals

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is an equality modulo compact operators for fixed \(\alpha>-1\), \(\beta\ne0\), \(\gamma>0\) and all continuous circle symbols. The proof is anchored by an exact Beta-integral formula for each Toeplitz matrix diagonal. The scaled radial densities converge in \(L^1\) to a Gamma density. The phase has a constant limit for \(0<\gamma<1\), the explicit nonzero Gamma characteristic function at \(\gamma=1\), and a nonstationary oscillatory limit for \(\gamma>1\). The explicitly given gauge cancels the rotating scalar phase, after which finite-band matrix remainders are compact. Uniform polynomial approximation extends to arbitrary continuous angular profiles. The continuous-symbol Hardy Toeplitz theorem supplies the stated Calkin, norm, and index consequences. The finite numerical checker passed 15 comparisons; its calculations are corroborating evidence, not a substitute for proof.

## Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority

The closest primary works are Quiroga-Barranco (arXiv:2106.01980, formulae of Section 7), Esmeral–Maximenko–Vasilevski (2015, angular upper-half-plane Toeplitz spectral algebra), Hagger (arXiv:1705.04553, abstract limit-operator Fredholm description), and Taskinen (2025, periodic domains). Their inspected constructions differ in symbol class or space, or give an abstract framework without the concrete winding-critical Mellin coefficient and full three-regime spectrum. A related Gaussian Fock radial-shell winding problem has a different (Gaussian) limiting law and is not a reduction of the weighted Bergman boundary-layer result. Actual database searches for logarithmic spirals, Gamma/Mellin smoothing, diagonal gauges, and Fredholm essential spectra returned no identical or stronger claim. This negative search result is not a proof of absolute novelty; a more general, unlocated boundary-symbol calculus could still subsume this exact case.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A bounded spiral symbol with no continuous boundary trace has a natural question: does increasing its radial winding erase angular spectral information? The theorem gives the sharp dividing exponent, a nontrivial asymmetric log-Gamma filter exactly at the boundary, a complete compactness dichotomy, and computable Fredholm essential spectra and indices across all real Bergman weights. The critical angular character has the explicit sharp gain \(\sqrt{\pi|\beta|/\sinh(\pi|\beta|)}\) for \(\alpha=0\); this is more informative than a generic limit-operator reduction. No unproved numerical-to-infinite inference is made.

## Source comparisons and limitations

The 2021 quasi-homogeneous symbol formulas give relevant matrix-integral technology but not this asymptotic spectral theorem. The 2015 article's angular symbols are invariant under dilations of the upper half-plane, whereas the present symbols rotate with radius in the disk. The 2017 general Fredholm theory is compatible but does not state the exact effective symbol. The 2025 periodic-domain Floquet result covers a different geometry. The main remaining priority risk is an earlier explicit treatment of spiralling disk symbols under a general oscillatory Toeplitz calculus. Mathematical limitations are continuous angular profiles, fixed nonzero winding strength, and essential rather than full spectrum.
