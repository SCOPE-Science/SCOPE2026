For weighted Bergman Toeplitz symbols whose angular pattern winds at speed \([1+\log(1/(1-r^2))]^\gamma\), the exact spectral boundary is \(\gamma=1\): slow winding preserves the circle symbol modulo a diagonal gauge, critical winding applies an explicit log-Gamma convolution, and fast winding leaves only the angular mean modulo compacts. The classification gives sharp essential spectra, norms, and Fredholm indices, with compactness of every nonconstant angular component precisely in the fast-winding regime.
