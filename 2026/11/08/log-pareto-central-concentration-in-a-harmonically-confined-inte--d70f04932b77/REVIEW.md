# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The uniform contraction of run-start states constructs a unique stationary chain; the finite-mean equilibrium alternating-renewal formula gives exact phase occupation. Because every inactive episode begins from a position in \( [a,L] \), its survival factor at the spatial log-distance is squeezed between two Lomax survival values whose ratios converge to one. This proves both the stationary density and cumulative small-ball constants, the absence of an origin atom and the divergence of negative moments. The code checks algebraic bounds and recurrence behavior on three nondegenerate parameter choices; it does not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, not certified)
The most relevant full-text comparisons are Basu et al. (2019) for the Markovian three-state trapped particle, Datta et al. (2024) for arbitrary waiting times in free transport, Farago–Smith (2024) for non-Markovian trapped **two-state** telegraph orientation, and Sun–Ye–Podgornik (2024) for a trapped zero-speed tumble state with **exponential** dwell times. These sources do not assert the same stationary distribution or imply its exact log-Pareto constant without analyzing a different zero-propulsion heavy-tailed rest mechanism. Literature and published-result searches used multiple claim aliases and potential broader coverage, not just title matching. Relevant unindexed prior work may still exist.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result connects a finite-mean heavy-tail waiting-time exponent to an unusual integrable density singularity near the trap center, with an exact physical parameter coefficient, a corresponding small-ball law and a sharp inverse-moment failure. It separates the observable effect of long inactivity from memoryless three-state central cusps. No universality under thermal noise, sign-changing propulsion or arbitrary run-time laws is claimed.

## Closest literature and limitations
See the cited primary papers in RESULT.md. The non-Markovian two-velocity confined model of Farago–Smith is particularly close in the confinement/waiting-law dimensions, but it does not contain zero-velocity rest. The Sun–Ye–Podgornik finite-tumble trapped model contains zero velocity but uses exponential switching; neither contains the stated model or exact log-Pareto asymptotics. Full-text inspection and the documented published-finding corpus queries do not establish absolute novelty. All assertions are conditional on fixed positive run duration, strictly positive directed propulsion, iid Lomax inactive times with exponent greater than one, harmonic confinement and absence of Brownian translational noise.
