For any nonconstant finite real log-periodic radial profile on a power-weighted complex-ball Bergman space, the commutator with coordinate multiplication lies in \(\mathcal S_p\) precisely when \(p>d\) and has a measurable critical \(d\)-th-power singular trace. Its exact logarithmic residue is a period average of a Mellin–Gamma filtered phase derivative, with a closed gamma-function constant for one cosine.
