In a uniform composition of \(n\), the number of singleton parts and the largest part have negative covariance with a sharp order-\(\log n\) law: \(\operatorname{Cov}(S_n,M_n)=-\tfrac14(\log_2 n)P(\{\log_2 n\})-\tfrac14Q(\{\log_2 n\})+o(1)\). The leading profile \(P\) is genuinely log-periodic, and the bounded second-order oscillation is resolved explicitly.
