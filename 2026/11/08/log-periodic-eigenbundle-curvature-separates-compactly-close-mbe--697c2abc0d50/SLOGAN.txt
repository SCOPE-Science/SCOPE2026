Exponential-tail Mbekhta-type bilateral shifts have identical spectra and quasinilpotent parts, and their pairwise differences lie in every Schatten class, but the shifts are mutually nonsimilar. Their rank-one eigenbundle curvatures develop nonconstant discrete-Gaussian oscillations with an exact logarithmic period and mean \(1/(2h)\), recovering the damping parameter \(h\).
