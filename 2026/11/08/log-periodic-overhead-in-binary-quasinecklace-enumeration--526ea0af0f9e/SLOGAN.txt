The number of binary quasinecklace candidates per ordinary necklace has the sharp asymptotic \(\Psi(\{\log_2 n\})+o(1)\), with mean \(1/\log 2\) and a nonzero, extraordinarily small logarithmic oscillation. The exact phase function is \(\Psi(\theta)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}2^{\theta-j-2}e^{-2^{\theta-j-2}}\), so the overhead ratio never converges to a single constant.
