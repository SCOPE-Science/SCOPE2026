# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The model is the four-edge cycle with nonnegative integer occupation at each labelled vertex and strictly positive fixed couplings. The energy reduces exactly to \(\kappa AB+\mu(A+B)\), with multiplicity \((a+1)(b+1)\), and the conditional negative-binomial formula is normalized. The one-dimensional partition sum has a logarithmic \(1/b\) window extending from order one to order \(1/\beta\); both endpoints contribute only \(O(1)\) after multiplying by \(\beta^2\). The conditional Laplace transform converges uniformly on interior logarithmic windows to \((1+s)^{-2}\), yielding an independent shape-two gamma energy and complementary uniform occupation exponents. Conditional energy bounded by two and a chemical-energy expectation bound imply the entropy constant. All quantifiers and boundary exclusions are stated in RESULT.md. Exact finite-cap grouping and positive-series high-temperature diagnostics were executed from the accompanying verifier. These finite checks are supportive, not infinite-limit proofs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Dhochak, Kikuchi and Singh, arXiv:2608.19605v1, derive the finite-temperature \(2\times2\) partition series (with directed bonds doubling four physical edges) and plot high-temperature entropy; that formula is prior and supplies the starting identity, not a stated joint logarithmic scale/gamma-energy limit. Andriolo, Nguyen, Richards and Sulejmanpasic, arXiv:2604.09768v2, establish the high-temperature power and logarithmic orders and relevant fractional-independent-set mechanism, which cover the leading *order* but not the distributional refinement or exact amplitude for this four-site Gibbs state. Huang and collaborators, arXiv:2512.07980v2, develop the model and its entropic-order motivation, not these exact small-cluster fluctuations. Targeted primary-literature searches and published-claim searches found no stronger covering theorem or exact tabulated values. Closest indexed records concern unrelated finite-dimensional Gibbs expansions, mean-field alphabet criticality and Widom–Rowlinson counts. Search failure is not a uniqueness proof. A general, potentially unindexed marginal-manifold distributional theorem is the main remaining priority risk.

## Value — PASS

At marginal logarithmic degeneracy, thermodynamic power counting alone does not determine how the occupation mass divides between two sublattices. Here every interior logarithmic scale receives equal probability, while interaction energy approaches a nondegenerate independent gamma law; the result also determines the exact amplitude and the otherwise unknown entropy constant. This gives a sharply defined finite-cluster benchmark for high-temperature entropic-order models and resolves a natural distributional question motivated by the fractional-independent-set picture, rather than an arbitrary numerical slice or another derivation of the known partition series.
