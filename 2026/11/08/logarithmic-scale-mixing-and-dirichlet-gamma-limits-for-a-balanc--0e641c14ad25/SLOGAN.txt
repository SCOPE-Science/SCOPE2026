On every balanced complete bipartite arithmetic Ising graph \(K_{k,k}\), the infinite-temperature partition function has an exact \(\beta^{-k}\log(1/\beta)\) coefficient and the full occupations exhibit independent log-uniform scale, gamma interaction-energy, and Dirichlet site-share limits. This identifies the probability law along the fractional-independent-set flat direction, rather than only its previously known logarithmic weight.
