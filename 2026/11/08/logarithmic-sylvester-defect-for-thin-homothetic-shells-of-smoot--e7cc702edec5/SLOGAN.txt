For four area-uniform points in the thin homothetic shell of any smooth positively curved planar convex body, the probability of failing convex position is asymptotic to \(\delta\log(1/\delta)\) times the affine-invariant coefficient \(\operatorname{area}(K)^{-3}\int_{\partial K}\langle x,n(x)\rangle^4/\kappa(x)\,ds\). The coefficient equals \(2/\pi^2\) for every origin-centered ellipse and varies nontrivially for smooth nonelliptical shapes.
