For directed cyclic nearest-neighbor many-loci quadratic dynamics, uniform-simplex initial data select equilibria with a critical \(\sqrt{\log m/m}\) fluctuation scale and exact Gaussian constant \(1/24\); symmetric all-to-all mixing has the smaller \(m^{-1/2}\) scale. The transition arises from inverse fiber masses, whose second moment is infinite precisely at the uniform-simplex boundary of this Dirichlet regime.
