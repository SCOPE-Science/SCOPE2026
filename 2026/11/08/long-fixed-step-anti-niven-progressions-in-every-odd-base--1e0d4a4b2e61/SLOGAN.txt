For every odd base \(b\ge3\), including composite bases, infinitely many arithmetic progressions of exactly \(2b+1\) terms with fixed difference \(b-1\) consist entirely of integers coprime to their base-\(b\) digit sums. A two-level digit-sum construction and simultaneous CRT roughness extend the previously published odd-prime-base lower bound without asserting sharpness for odd bases.
