For a binary word \(w\), the expected fraction of ones observed up to the first occurrence of \(w\) is exactly \(1/2\) if and only if every nonempty border of \(w\) is balanced. Otherwise its deviation from \(1/2\) has precisely the sign of the longest imbalanced border, resolving the first-hit-proportion clause of Bóna–Maga–Richey's 2026 conjecture.
