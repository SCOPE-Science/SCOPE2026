On a finite parallel-path metric graph, the sharp mean-zero \(H^1\)-to-\(C\) embedding constant depends only on total length, the cubic sum of path lengths, and the longest path; every nonzero extremizer is a centered Green function at the midpoint of a longest path. At fixed total length and number of paths, equal lengths uniquely minimize the squared norm, while the circle constant is a strict but approachable supremum when at least three paths are present.
