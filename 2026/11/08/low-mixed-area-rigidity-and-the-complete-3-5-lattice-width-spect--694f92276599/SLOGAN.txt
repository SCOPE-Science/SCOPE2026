For coprime positive integers \(a<b\), fixed directional widths \(ad\) and \(bd\) force every positive normalized mixed area below \(\min(d,b)\) to be divisible by \(a\), with the smaller-width polytope necessarily a segment; every allowed multiple occurs. The entire \(3:5\) width-ratio spectrum is classified for all \(d\ge1\), with a sharp persistent gap at mixed area \(4\) beginning at \(d=5\).
