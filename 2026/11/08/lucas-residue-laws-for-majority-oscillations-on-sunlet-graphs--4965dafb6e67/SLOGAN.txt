One pendant memory vertex per site turns at most one majority two-cycle on the bare ring into exponentially many on its sunlet: the exact count is a Lucas-number formula with a six-periodic correction. Almost every sunlet two-cycle has some vertices fixed across phases, while an asymptotically \(\varphi^{-n}\) fraction of period-two states oscillates at all vertices, where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\).
