For antiferromagnetic Ising wheels, the number of one-spin-stable configurations changes from Perrin-exponential for \(0<h<J\) to polynomial just above \(h=J\), with a critical entropy spike to \(\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)\) exactly at the field threshold. Exact parity-sensitive counts also describe the \(h=2J\), \(h=3J\) and \(h=nJ\) transitions for every rim size \(n\ge5\).
