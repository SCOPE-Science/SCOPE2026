Clipping a cube by the symmetric diagonal slab \(\lvert\sum_i x_i\rvert\le t\) has an exact all-dimensional Mahler product with a strict odd/even reversal as the slab becomes thin. In dimension three the product is explicitly piecewise algebraic, has minimum \(32/3\) only at two different widths, has a unique interior secondary peak, and approaches its unattained sharp supremum \(12\) at zero width.
