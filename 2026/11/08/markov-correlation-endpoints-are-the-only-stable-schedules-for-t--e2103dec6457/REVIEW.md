# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The conditional covariance recursion is derived directly from the newly selected mode, with no assumption of independent successive steps. The two-step cone map is strongly positive because distinct admissible symplectic-Euler matrices send every real line to distinct lines. Strict \(\log\det\) concavity and the unique positive-definite invariant form at each fixed step force the Perron radius strictly above one for every interior switching probability. The endpoints are checked by the exact elliptic traces. An independent exact-rational script reproduces the six-dimensional determinant identity. The proof covers all positive times steps \(0<a<b<2\), not merely the rational example.

## Originality — PASS, with residual publication-precedence risk

The 2018 random-time-step integration source establishes symplecticity and finite-horizon energy control, rather than an exact fixed-distribution Markov-correlation stability classification. The 2018/2019 random shear product analysis treats independent choices and different matrix families; the general 2018 Markov jump system stability framework provides generic conditions rather than this sharp dichotomy. Related prior investigations of iid random symplectic-Euler time steps and bounded perfect alternation establish the two separate endpoint/independence facts, but do not imply the universal all-interior Markov instability, which needs the two-block matrix-cone argument. Unexamined specializations of general Markov matrix-product theory remain a publication-precedence risk.

## Value — PASS

The two-endpoint contrast answers a natural numerical design question: can small random failures of a perfectly periodic, energy-stable variable-step timetable be mean-square harmless? The exact answer is no, for every positive transition imperfection probability, while zero imperfection and completely frozen mode selection are bounded. This sharp infinite-horizon difference complements finite-horizon geometric preservation, is not just another numerical stability example, and is supported by a general mechanism and a polynomial certificate for a concrete test case.

## Limitations and nearest literature

This is a two-dimensional harmonic model using one specified symplectic-Euler ordering; it asserts mean-square and not almost-sure instability. General Markov jump spectral criteria already exist, and a complete independent specialist audit of potential historical equivalents has not occurred.
