Two symplectic-Euler step lengths can be individually stable and perfectly alternating or perfectly fixed schedules can be bounded, yet every genuinely stochastic symmetric Markov switching schedule between them has exponentially growing mean-square energy. For the exact pair \(a=1/2\), \(b=3/2\), the obstruction holds for every interior stay probability \(0<s<1\), certified by \(\det(I-\mathcal L_s)=-9s(1-s)^2/4\).
