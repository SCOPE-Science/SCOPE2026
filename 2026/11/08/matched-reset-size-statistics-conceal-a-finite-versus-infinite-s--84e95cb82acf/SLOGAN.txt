Two independently randomized partial-reset protocols have the same mean and variance of reset amplitude, the same exact stationary position variance, and the same weak-reset Gaussian model, yet at \(s=1/4\) one has an infinite fourth spatial moment while the other has \(\mathbb E X^4=12288D^2/(31\lambda^2)\). Their finite-moment thresholds are strictly ordered for every \(0<s<1/3\), with a nonvanishing \(1/s\)-scale separation as the resets become weaker.
