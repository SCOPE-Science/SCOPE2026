At every nonmaximal singular-value gap of an iterate of the two-dimensional twisted baker map, the numbers of real-spectrum and complex-spectrum periodic points are exactly equal; only one excluded fixed-face itinerary breaks the equality at the maximal gap. The joint normalized eigenvalue-modulus and singular-value gaps converge to \((B|Z|,|Z|)\), with an independent fair bit \(B\) and standard Gaussian \(Z\).
