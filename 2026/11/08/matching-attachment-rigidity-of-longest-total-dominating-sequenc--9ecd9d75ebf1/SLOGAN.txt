For the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), the longest open-neighborhood total dominating sequences have length \(n-1\) for odd \(n\) and \(n-2\) for even \(n\). When \(n=2m+1\), every optimum begins with an ordered near-perfect matching and then attaches its matched pairs one by one; the exact number of labelled, ordered optima is \((2m+1)!(2m)!/2^m\), and their unordered supports are precisely spanning trees admitting a near-perfect matching.
