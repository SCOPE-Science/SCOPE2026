Deleting a matching of \(k\ge2\) edges from a complete graph changes the polar symmetric-edge-polytope Ehrhart numerator to \(E_n(z)+2kzE_{n-2}(z)\), which remains \(\gamma\)-nonnegative. Even when the deleted-pair distances are independently relaxed, the intrinsic polar volume is exactly additive in their individual contributions, with no cross terms.
