Deleting any proper matching from \(K_{n,n}\) with \(n\ge4\) leaves its connected mutual-visibility maximum equal to \(2n-2\), but the exact number of maximizing sets drops quadratically to a unique one when a single undeleted matching edge remains. Deleting the final matching edge reduces that maximum to \(2n-3\) and produces \(2n(n-1)\) maximizing sets; all maximizers are classified, including an exceptional extra configuration after exactly one deletion.
