For every non-Frobenius cyclotomic map on \(\mathbb F_{p^n}\) of multiplicative index at most \(p\), the additive index is exactly its maximum \(p^n\), upgrading the known lower bound \(p\). This uniform threshold is sharp for odd \(p\): a two-term linearized permutation on \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\) first breaks maximal-index rigidity at multiplicative index \(p+1\).
