For every finite field, the largest possible rank of an associative multiplication pairing on the upper-triangular algebra \(T_n\) is \(\lfloor(n+1)^2/4\rfloor\), and the exact proportion attaining it has two distinct formulas according to the parity of \(n\). The submaximal locus is a hypersurface for odd \(n\) but has codimension two for even \(n\), producing a persistent parity gap as dimension grows.
