For two powers of permutations differing by one adjacent swap, every maximal common subsequence is a uniquely segmented chain of alternating swapped-letter blocks; its lengths form the complete spectrum \(2k+(q-3)r\), \(1\le r\le k\). Although maximal subsequences can be arbitrarily shorter than a longest one, their total number is always \(\binom{2k}{k}\), independent of the alphabet size and swap position.
