For every permutation \(P\) of \(q\ge2\) distinct letters, **all** maximal common scattered subsequences of \(P^k\) and its reversed-order power are longest, of length \(2k-1\): they are exactly the adjacent-distinct words with equally many rises and falls in the order of \(P\). Their exact number is a central coefficient of the known Smirnov-word descent generating function, while exactly \(q(q-1)^{k-1}\) of them are palindromes.
