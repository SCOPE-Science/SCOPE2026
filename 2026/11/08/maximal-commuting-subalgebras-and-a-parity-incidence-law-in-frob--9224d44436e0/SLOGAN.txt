For the nilpotent radical of the fourth-order Frobenius-skew polynomial quotient \(\mathbb F_{q^m}[t;\sigma]/(t^4)\), all maximal commuting subalgebras are explicitly classified and counted by \(1+q^{m-1}(q^m-1)/(q-1)\). Their intersection geometry changes sharply with the parity of \(m\) because the projective \((q+1)\)-power map is bijective in odd degree and \((q+1)\)-to-one in even degree; the complete centralizer-size distribution and commuting probability also have exact formulas.
