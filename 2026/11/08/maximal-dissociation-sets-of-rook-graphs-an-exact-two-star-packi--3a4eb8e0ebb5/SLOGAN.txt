Every maximal dissociation set of a rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) corresponds to a vertex-disjoint collection of single edges and two-edge stars in \(K_{m,n}\), with an exact coverage-and-saturation criterion that gives its complete cardinality polynomial. When \(n\ge2m\), maximal sets have exactly the even sizes \(2\lceil m/2\rceil,2\lceil m/2\rceil+2,\ldots,2m\), exhibiting an unbounded lower-to-upper cardinality gap.
