On every tree, inclusion-maximal edge-general-position sets are exactly the leaf-edge sets of connected subtrees whose nonleaf vertices retain all of their original neighbors. This gives both complete cardinality polynomials by rooted recurrences, the sharp lower parameter \(\operatorname{gp}_{e}^{-}(T)=\min_{\deg_T(v)\ge2}\deg_T(v)\), and a path-or-star characterization of trees whose maximal sets are equicardinal.
