PASS. The subdivision-incidence bridge criterion explicitly distinguishes an allowed shared source or reciprocal pair from a forbidden nonreciprocal directed two-step walk. That criterion gives an exhaustive structural decomposition. The proof tests every form of possible one-edge extension, including the exceptional single-source/single-target reverse arc, and counts disjoint labeled families with their precise factorial normalization. The universal upper and lower bounds and parity property follow algebraically; the independent finite test checks the raw graph definition and the complete predicted families.

PASS. The complete classification supplies an exact full labeled distribution and size spectrum of maximal feasible edge selections, not merely the best size. The classification is reusable for exhaustive benchmarks of maximal edge-open-packings and injective edge-color-class construction.
