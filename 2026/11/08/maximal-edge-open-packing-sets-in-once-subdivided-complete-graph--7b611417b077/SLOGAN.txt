Every inclusion-maximal edge-open-packing set of a once-subdivided complete graph is a disjoint union of reciprocal incidence pairs and a complete directed source-to-target block, or a perfect/near-perfect reciprocal pairing. This gives the entire labeled cardinality polynomial, a sharp range from \(n-1\) to \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\), and an even-cardinality spectrum whenever \(n\) is odd.
