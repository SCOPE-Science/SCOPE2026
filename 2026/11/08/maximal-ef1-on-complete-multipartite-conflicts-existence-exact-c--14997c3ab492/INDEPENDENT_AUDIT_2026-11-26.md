## correctness

With identical unit values EF1 is exactly bundle-size spread at most one, even with empty bundles. A nonempty bundle is confined to one part; any used part must be fully assigned, and leaving a whole part unassigned requires all agents to be nonempty. These conditions suffice for maximality. When the total goods fit into singleton bundles or the largest part can supply every agent, the construction is immediate. Otherwise the shortest descending prefix reaching n has sum S and last size c with S−n≤c−1; its first and last parts are distinct and supply at least floor(a₁/2)+floor(c/2)≥c−1 disjoint pairs. Splitting into S−n pairs and 2n−S singleton bundles yields maximal EF1 in the stated sort-and-scan bound. For two parts, complete assignments use the balanced sizes fixed by quotient and remainder; selecting the left labels and the larger bundle labels yields F_n, while nonfull assignments are balanced surjections from one shore and yield the two D_n terms. The injection and single-agent boundaries are included. If full fairness is impossible, existence implies at least one side has enough goods for a balanced surjection; hence the larger side does too. In the odd family the full balance would require pairs on both odd shores; the only fair maximal assignments are the 2t! side bijections. Disjoint label sets and side surjections give T_t. Keeping the (i,j)=((t−1)/2,(t+1)/2) term and counting the specified partitions gives the stated log-probability upper bound, not a claimed matching asymptotic.
