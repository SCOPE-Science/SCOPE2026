With identical unit-valued goods, every complete multipartite conflict graph admits a maximal EF1 allocation for any number of agents; complete bipartite graphs additionally admit an exact formula counting all such allocations. For odd \(t\geq3\), the graph \(K_{t,t}\) with \(t\) agents forces every maximal EF1 allocation to discard half the goods, and fair allocations form a superexponentially small fraction of all maximal feasible allocations.
