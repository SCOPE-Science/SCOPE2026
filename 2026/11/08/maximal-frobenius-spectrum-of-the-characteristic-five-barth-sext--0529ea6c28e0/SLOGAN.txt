The ramified characteristic-five reduction of Barth's \(65\)-nodal sextic retains precisely \(65\) ordinary nodes, all rational over \(\mathbb F_5\). Its minimal resolution has weight-two Frobenius spectrum \(92\) copies of \(+5\) and \(14\) copies of \(-5\), yielding the exact zeta function \(Z(X,T)=((1-T)(1-25T)(1-5T)^{27}(1+5T)^{14})^{-1}\).
