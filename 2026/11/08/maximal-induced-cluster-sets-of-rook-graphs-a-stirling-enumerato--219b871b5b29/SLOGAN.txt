Every inclusion-maximal induced cluster set of a rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) is encoded by either a vertex-covering star forest of \(K_{m,n}\) or a one-sided collection of nontrivial stars covering the opposite shore; this yields a complete Stirling-number cardinality polynomial. For \(2\le m\) and \(n\ge2m\), the only attainable maximal-set sizes are \(m\) and \(n,n+1,\ldots,n+m-2\), giving arbitrarily long gaps.
