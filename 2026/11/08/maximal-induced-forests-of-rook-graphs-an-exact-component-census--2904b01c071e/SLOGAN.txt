Every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), for integers \(2\le m\le n\), has all its inclusion-maximal induced forests of the same order, and their complete labeled counts by connected components admit a closed factorial–binomial formula. Square boards have \((m!)^2\) connected maximal forests, whereas strict rectangles admit exactly \(1,\ldots,\min\{m,n-m\}\) components.
