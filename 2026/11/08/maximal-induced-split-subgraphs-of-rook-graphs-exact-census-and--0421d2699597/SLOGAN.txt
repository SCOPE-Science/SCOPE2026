For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), the inclusion-maximal induced split-graph subsets have an exact two-orientation falling-factorial census and, away from almost-square boards, only the cardinalities \(2m\) and \(m+n-1\). Their two size classes exhibit a sharp square-root aspect-ratio crossover: for \(n-m=d\ge2\), the larger-set probability is exactly \(m/(m+d(d+1))\).
