# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The bipartite line-coloring equivalence reduces the graph-coloring property to maximum degree three. The full low-row/low-column block is forced by inclusion-maximality, its dimensions are at most two, and four explicit deficit patterns exhaust the possibilities; comparing shore degree sums yields every boundary and every coefficient without overlap. The coefficient-extraction normalizations are labeled and independently tested against exhaustive enumeration on six boards and direct graph coloring on two. The infinite statement follows from the structural proof rather than from the finite tests.

**Originality — PASS (provisional priority).** The 2022 paper *Well-Hued Graphs*, Lemma 6, already proves the precise qualitative uniform-cardinality criterion for rook graphs at any color bound. It does not state or count the distinct defect families or their labeled cardinality polynomial, and the present claim explicitly does not assert the known criterion as new. The earlier 2019 well-bicovered article concerns the two-colorable case, and OEIS A001501 already gives the regular three-margin sequence. The new formula adds the mixed-margin strata and class multiplicities, which none of these inspected sources implies individually. Semantic overlap searches for three-colorable rook graphs, bounded-degree subgraph facets, 3-matchings, and matrix-margin enumeration found no matching full census; this is not proof of world-wide priority. Residual risk: unindexed enumerations of maximal bounded-degree bipartite subgraphs could cover it.

**Value — PASS.** The source literature raises the natural problem of maximal induced colorable subgraphs on Cartesian products of complete graphs and identifies when their orders are uniform. Exact labeled distributions at the first nontrivial bound beyond bipartiteness refine that result and expose a sharp width-one defect transition, including two distinct configurations contributing to the smallest square-board cardinality. This is not merely a new value of a scalar invariant or an automatic color-bound substitution: the three-color case requires a distinct two-sided margin-defect classification. The formulas are reusable for counting facets of bounded-degree incidence-set systems and inclusion-minimal three-colorability deletion sets.

**Closest prior work and limitations.** Goddard–Kuenzel–Melville (2019 and 2022) establish the concepts and the qualitative rook-graph boundary; OEIS A001501 tabulates the regular-matrix term. The full result is limited to three colors and complete rectangular rook graphs. The novelty search cannot exclude every inaccessible or unindexed work, and no independent audit is claimed.
