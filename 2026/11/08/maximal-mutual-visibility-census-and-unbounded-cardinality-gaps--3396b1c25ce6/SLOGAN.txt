For every \(n\ge4\), maximal mutual-visibility sets in the near-crown graph \(K_{n+1,n+1}-M_n\) have an exact six-term cardinality enumerator with minimum size \(n+1\) and unique maximum size \(2n\). For \(n\ge8\), their possible sizes omit every integer from \(n+3\) to \(2n-5\), an arbitrarily long gap.
