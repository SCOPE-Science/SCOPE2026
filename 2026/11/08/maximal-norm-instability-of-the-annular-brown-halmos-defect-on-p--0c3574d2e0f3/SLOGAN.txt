On each annular Hardy space, the exact quadratic Brown–Halmos test admits positive finite-rank norm-one operators at the **maximum possible distance** \(1/2\) from all Toeplitz operators while their test defect tends to zero. A triangular diagonal family has the exact defect norm \(N^{-1}[r^2+(1+r^{2N+2})/(1+r^{2N-2})]\) and disproves every uniform operator-norm error modulus for this characterization.
