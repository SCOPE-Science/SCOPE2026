# Maximal shattering faces of noncrossing equivalence relations

## Finding
Fix an integer \(n\ge 2\), and arrange \([n]\) in convex cyclic order. A *noncrossing partition* is a partition with no four cyclically alternating vertices \(a,b,c,d\) for which \(a,c\) belong to one block and \(b,d\) belong to a distinct block. Let \(\mathcal N_n\) be the concept class indexed by all noncrossing partitions \(\pi\) of \([n]\), with query domain \(\binom{[n]}2\) and binary response \(h_\pi(\{u,v\})=1\) exactly when \(u,v\) lie in the same block.

The shattering complex \(\mathsf{Sh}(\mathcal N_n)\), whose faces are shattered sets of pair queries, has the following properties.

1. A support \(S\subseteq\binom{[n]}2\) is shattered if and only if, for each \(e\in S\), there is a noncrossing partition whose trace on \(S\) is exactly \(S\setminus\{e\}\). Thus every proposed support has an exact \(|S|\)-witness test; the empty support is shattered.
2. \(\operatorname{VCdim}(\mathcal N_n)=n-1\). The supports of cardinality \(n-1\) are **exactly** the noncrossing spanning trees drawn with straight chords on the convex \(n\)-gon. Their exact number is the established noncrossing-tree number
   \[\frac{1}{2n-1}\binom{3n-3}{n-1}.\]
3. For every even \(n=2m\ge4\), the \(m\) pairwise crossing diametral chords \(\{\{i,i+m\}:1\le i\le m\}\) form an inclusion-maximal shattered support of size \(m\). Since noncrossing spanning trees are maximal shattered supports of size \(2m-1\), the shattering complex is nonpure, with two maximal-face sizes separated by \(m-1\). In particular, it is not a matroid independence complex.

The identification of maximum shattered supports and the nonpurity phenomenon are the contributions; the enumeration of noncrossing spanning trees is a previously known formula.

## Assumptions and scope
All vertices are distinct and labeled by cyclic positions. Each query is an unordered pair of distinct vertices, and the hypothesis answers whether the endpoints occupy one block; hypotheses are ALL noncrossing partitions, with no restriction on block count or block sizes. A support is shattered when each \(0/1\) labeling of its queried pairs is realized by some hypothesis. A maximal face means maximal by inclusion, not maximum by cardinality. The numeric face count applies to the fixed labeled convex cyclic order, not to arbitrary unlabeled trees or a quotient by symmetries.

## Proof
**Meet lemma.** Given noncrossing partitions \(\pi,\rho\), let \(\pi\wedge\rho\) have blocks the nonempty intersections of their blocks. It is noncrossing: if its blocks \(A\) and \(B\) crossed, then their containing blocks in \(\pi\) would have to coincide, since \(\pi\) is noncrossing. The same holds in \(\rho\), forcing \(A=B\), a contradiction. Thus the concept family is closed under coordinatewise intersections of its positive-answer sets.

**Deletion criterion.** Necessity is immediate from shattering. For sufficiency, suppose each \(e\in S\) has a witness \(\pi_e\) that is positive on all of \(S\setminus\{e\}\) and negative on \(e\). Given an arbitrary desired positive subfamily \(F\subseteq S\), take the meet of the witnesses \(\pi_e\) for \(e\in S\setminus F\). Every pair in \(F\) remains positive, and each excluded pair is negative in at least one witness and stays negative under refinement. The meet is noncrossing by the lemma. If no exclusions exist, use the single-block partition; for the empty support, use any partition. This proves the exact equivalence.

**Upper bound and largest faces.** The undirected graph whose edge set is a shattered support cannot contain a cycle: selecting all other edges of the cycle as positive would force the endpoints of the remaining negative edge to be equivalent. Hence every shattered support is a forest and has at most \(n-1\) edges.

If \(T\) is a noncrossing straight-chord spanning tree, then for any positive edge subset \(F\subseteq E(T)\), put the vertices of each connected component of \(([n],F)\) into one block. This partition is noncrossing: two components with alternating boundary vertices would contain two connecting paths that must cross in the disk, contradicting the embedded forest's noncrossing edges. Distinct components cannot contain the endpoints of any edge of \(T\setminus F\), since \(T\) is a tree. Hence \(E(T)\) is shattered.

Conversely, suppose a spanning tree \(T\) has two crossing chords \(ac\) and \(bd\), with cyclic order \(a,b,c,d\). Remove an edge \(e\) on the unique tree path joining these two disjoint chords. The two components of \(T-e\) contain the endpoints \(a,c\) and \(b,d\), respectively. Any partition positive on all edges of \(T-e\) must keep these two component vertex sets intact; to give \(e\) a negative response they must lie in distinct blocks. Those two blocks cross, violating noncrossingness. Thus \(T\) is not shattered. Therefore the cardinality-\(n-1\) faces are precisely the noncrossing spanning trees. Such trees exist, for example the fan from vertex \(1\), proving the claimed VC dimension. The displayed face count is the classical count of noncrossing trees on a convexally ordered labeled vertex set, equivalently the \(k=1\) specialization of Okoth–Wagner's noncrossing-tree enumeration (their Corollary 3.3), algebraically simplifying to the displayed ternary-Catalan form.

**Linearly separated maximal faces.** Let \(n=2m\ge4\), and take the diameters \(d_i=\{i,i+m\}\), \(1\le i\le m\). For any positive subfamily \(F\), form one block consisting of all endpoints of positive diameters; put every remaining vertex in its own singleton block; when \(F=arnothing\), use the all-singleton partition. A partition with at most one nonsingleton block is noncrossing and realizes exactly the prescribed diameter labels, so the matching \(D=\{d_1,\ldots,d_m\}\) is shattered.

Every two diameters cross, so in any noncrossing partition in which all diameters are positive, the blocks containing them must coincide. They exhaust all vertices, forcing the one-block partition. For every additional pair query \(g\notin D\), the labeling making all diameters positive but \(g\) negative is therefore impossible. The support \(D\) is inclusion-maximal. A noncrossing spanning tree is also inclusion-maximal because no shattered support has more than \(n-1\) edges. These maximal faces have unequal sizes \(m\) and \(2m-1\); the nonpure gap is \(m-1\), which grows with \(m\). A matroid's inclusion-maximal independent sets all have the same size, so the complex is not a matroid independence complex.

## Verification
Run the included Python 3 program `verify_noncrossing.py` without third-party packages. It generates all set partitions independently using restricted-growth strings, filters the noncrossing ones by checking all cyclic quadruples, represents each hypothesis by its complete pair-equality mask, and independently checks every support on \(n=2,3,4,5,6\) against both direct trace shattering and the deletion criterion. On every support of size \(n-1\) it separately tests the tree and crossing criteria. It also exhausts all \(54,264\) size-six supports for \(n=7\), obtaining exactly \(1,428\) maximum faces. The check additionally confirms the diametral constructions' graph properties through \(n=14\). Recorded output appears in `verification_output.txt`.

For \(n=2,3,4,5,6\), the respective counts of ALL shattered supports are \(2,7,34,201,1357\); the maximum-stratum counts are \(1,3,12,55,273\). The program verifies the noncrossing-partition concept counts \(2,5,14,42,132\). These are finite cross-checks, not substitutes for the proofs for arbitrary \(n\).

## Relationship to prior work
Aichholzer et al. (2022, arXiv:2206.03879) study noncrossing spanning-tree reconfiguration and its geometric constraints, not pair-equality query shattering of noncrossing partitions. Okoth and Wagner (2023, DOI:10.1007/s00026-023-00642-6) prove refined enumerations of noncrossing trees, including the classical number used here; that enumeration is *not* claimed to be original. Kleer and Simon (2021, arXiv:2108.10037) study general primal/dual combinatorial dimensions, not this noncrossing equivalence-relation concept. These results do not directly imply the maximal-face classification, the deletion-witness criterion for these hypotheses, or the nonpure matching construction. The familiar graphic-matroid description for all unrestricted set partitions is a useful contrast: forbidding crossing blocks changes the support complex while leaving the maximum dimension unchanged.

## Limitations
No complete count of shattered supports below cardinality \(n-1\) is asserted for arbitrary \(n\), and no classification of *all* inclusion-maximal faces is claimed. The known noncrossing-tree counting formula is cited, not rediscovered. The computational checks are finite; the universal results rest on the written proofs. Historical priority under alternative terminology (e.g. closure-independent chord sets, noncrossing-equivalence teaching sets) may require broader specialist review.
