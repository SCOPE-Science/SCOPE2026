# Scientific review: maximal shattering faces of noncrossing equivalence relations

## Correctness — PASS
The statement concerns all \(n\ge2\), not just tested sizes. The meet of two noncrossing equivalence relations is noncrossing, which proves the deletion-witness criterion. Cycle obstruction bounds a shattered support by \(n-1\) edges. Noncrossing trees shatter by connected-component partitions of arbitrary positive subforests. Conversely, for any crossing chords in a spanning tree, deleting a connecting-path edge gives the forbidden alternating-block partition. Two mutually crossing diametral chord classes force all vertices into a block when simultaneously positive, proving an inclusion-maximal support with \(n/2\) edges. The classical exact tree enumeration has an explicitly cited source. Boundary cases \(n=2,3\) and empty positive labelings are addressed. A standalone checker brute-forces every possible support through \(n=6\), all top-sized supports for \(n=7\), and construction properties through \(n=14\). Finite tests corroborate but are not used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, with historical risk
The prior literature inspected discusses noncrossing-tree reconfiguration (Aichholzer et al., arXiv:2206.03879), enumeration of noncrossing trees (Okoth–Wagner, DOI:10.1007/s00026-023-00642-6), and broad primal–dual learning dimensions (Kleer–Simon, arXiv:2108.10037). The first two treat the geometric trees whose established counting formula is used here, but do not characterize pair-equality shattering by all noncrossing partitions. The third does not specialize to this family. The nearest earlier partition-query result for a fixed-size bipartition and the general matroid-base dimension theorem concern different concept families and do not imply nonpure facets here. A noncrossing tree count by itself is not a new contribution. Searches of connected mathematical records and public literature found no theorem implying both the support/facet classification and the linearly nonpure shattering complex. Some unindexed literature under closure independence or shattering complexes of noncrossing equivalence relations could remain, so this is not a claim of exhaustive priority.

## Value — PASS
This is a natural learning-theoretic invariant of the Catalan family of noncrossing partitions. The exact maximum shattered-support classification explains why the VC dimension remains \(n-1\) but only noncrossing trees attain it, gives the full top coefficient through a known enumeration, and exposes a global difference from the unrestricted partition/graphic-matroid paradigm: inclusion-maximal shattered sets can be as small as half the ground-set order while other maximal sets are nearly twice as large. The gap grows without bound, and the deletion criterion gives a precise test applicable to every support, not merely maximum ones. This is not presented as a new count of noncrossing trees or a full census of all lower-dimensional faces.

## Limits and closest prior work
The lower strata of the shattering complex are not fully classified; the construction only proves existence of two distinct maximal-face sizes. Alternative terminology in the partition-lattice literature may contain undiscovered overlap. A separate mathematical audit and broader specialist priority review would be useful.
