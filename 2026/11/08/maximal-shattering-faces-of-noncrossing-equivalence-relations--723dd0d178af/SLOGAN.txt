For pair-equality queries on noncrossing partitions of \([n]\), the maximum shattered supports are precisely the noncrossing spanning trees, giving \(\operatorname{VCdim}=n-1\) and exactly \(\binom{3n-3}{n-1}/(2n-1)\) maximum faces. Yet when \(n=2m\ge4\), the mutually crossing diameters form an inclusion-maximal shattered support of size only \(m\), so the shattering complex is nonpure with a linearly growing facet-size gap.
