# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For a connected graph, conservation across two updates makes the firing-count vector harmonic. Primitive two-periodicity forces exactly one firing at every vertex; the first firing set then determines independent, sharp chip intervals of length equal to each vertex's cross-cut degree. Every admissible cut gives a contiguous mass band and every maximum cut is admissible. Nested bands prove the full universal support. The endpoint count and NP-complete decision reduction are explicit. Direct exhaustive dynamics checked all 771 labelled connected graphs with two through five vertices, while a separate constructive checker tested 139 further connected graphs. Both programs completed successfully; the proof is not a finite extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The closest primary full text is Jiang's 2010 work (Proposition 2.5: equal firing totals imply exact return) together with Jiang, Scully and Zhang's 2012/2015 nonclumpiness analysis. These give useful prerequisites but do not state the maximum-cut identity for the complete chip-mass support or the extremal coefficient formula. Available previous exact mass claims address cycles, wheels, split graphs and tree extremal totals, not this arbitrary-connected-graph theorem. Targeted published-finding corpus and literature searches for period-two firing cuts, degree products, MAX-CUT and minimum chip mass found no covering statement. The original 1992 Bitar–Goles and 2026 general middle-stair papers could not be inspected in full, which leaves a nontrivial priority risk. No independent validation or global novelty certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

Energy bands of period-two parallel chip-firing states are natural fixed-energy sandpile invariants. The theorem makes their entire graph-universal support exactly computable in terms of the MAX-CUT value, identifies how many states occupy the two boundaries, and proves an NP-complete bounded-energy periodic-state decision problem. This is a substantive graph–dynamics connection and explains, uniformly, why the chip-mass spectra seen in previous families have no gaps.

## Scientific limitations

Only ordinary synchronous chip-firing on finite connected simple undirected graphs with at least two vertices is treated. The result does not classify longer periods, eventual basins, or all interior coefficients. The priority assessment is qualified by unavailable full texts and unindexed terminology.
