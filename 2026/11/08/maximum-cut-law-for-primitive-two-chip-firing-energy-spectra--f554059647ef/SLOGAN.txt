For synchronous parallel chip-firing on any finite connected simple graph, the chip totals of initially primitive two-cycles fill exactly \([2|E|-\operatorname{MaxCut}(G),\,2|E|+\operatorname{MaxCut}(G)-|V|]\) with no missing integers. The two endpoint populations each equal twice the number of unordered maximum cuts, and bounded-energy primitive-two existence is NP-complete.
