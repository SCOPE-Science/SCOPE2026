On every wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), one exact spoke conductance \(w_m\) produces the unique maximum-entropy random spanning-tree law among all distributions minimizing expected overlap; every edge then appears with probability \(1/2\). The calibrations increase to \(4/3\) with sharp error \(3^m(4/3-w_m)\to64/9\), while ordinary uniform spanning trees incur a positive, asymptotically linear overlap penalty for \(m\ge4\).
