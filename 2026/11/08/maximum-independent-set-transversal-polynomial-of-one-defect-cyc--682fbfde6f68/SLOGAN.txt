For \(q,k\ge2\), the maximum independent sets of the distance-power cycle \(C_{kq+1}^{q-1}\) become exactly the length-\(k\) circular intervals under a step-\(q\) relabelling. Consequently, all transversals have an exact bounded-gap polynomial, their minimum size is \(q+1\), and exactly \(\frac{kq+1}{q+1}\binom{k+q-1}{q}\) attain the minimum.
