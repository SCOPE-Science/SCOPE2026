For every connected bipartite graph, the Euclidean inradius of its type-B root polytope is \(1/\sqrt{\alpha(G)}\), and the entire incenter locus is governed by inclusion-maximal independent sets; its dimension counts the vertices lying in no maximum independent set. This yields a unique incenter for even paths, an explicit crosspolytope of incenters for odd paths, and the sharp tree-wide dimension maximum \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\).
