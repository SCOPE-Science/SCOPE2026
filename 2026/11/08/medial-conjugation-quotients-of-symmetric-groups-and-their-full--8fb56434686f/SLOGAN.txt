The universal medial quotient of the conjugation quandle of \(S_n\), for every \(n\ge3\), has \(p(n)+s(n)\) elements, where \(s(n)\) counts partitions into distinct odd parts; its operation is controlled solely by operand parity and a simultaneous swap of split alternating-group conjugacy classes. Its full automorphism group is \((C_2\wr S_{s(n)})\times S_{e(n)-s(n)}\times S_{o(n)}\), with \(e(n)\) and \(o(n)\) the counts of even and odd cycle types.
