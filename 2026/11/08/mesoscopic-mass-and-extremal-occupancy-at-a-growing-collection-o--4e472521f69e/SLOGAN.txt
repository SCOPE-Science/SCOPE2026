At critical slow-site rate, a growing but subextensive collection of defects in a conserving zero-range process stores \(\sigma\sqrt{Mr_M}\) particles, with \(\sqrt M\)-Gaussian fluctuations while each individual defect has an asymptotically exponential occupation on the \(\sigma\sqrt{M/r_M}\) scale. Under \(r_M(\log r_M)^2=o(M)\), the largest defect occupation has a Gumbel limit after subtracting \(\log r_M\).
