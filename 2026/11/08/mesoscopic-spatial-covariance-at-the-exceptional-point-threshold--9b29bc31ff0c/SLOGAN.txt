A spectrally stable but maximally nonnormal noisy directed chain develops stationary fluctuations of order \(N^{1/4}\) and a spatial coherence scale of order \(\sqrt N\) when its damping is tuned within order \(N^{-1}\) of unit coupling. Its full finite-size covariance crossover is an explicit Gaussian-integral kernel, and at criticality its normalized correlation has a closed complementary-error-function profile.
