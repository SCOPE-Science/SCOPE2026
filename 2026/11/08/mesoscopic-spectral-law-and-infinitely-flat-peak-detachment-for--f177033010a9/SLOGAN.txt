The positive self-commutators of high powers of weighted Bergman shifts have a complete continuum singular-value profile, with a sharp transition in the location of the dominant spectral defect at the classical Bergman weight. Just above this transition the peak moves by \(\frac12\exp(-2\log2/\varepsilon)\) and its height increases by only \(\frac\varepsilon8\exp(-2\log2/\varepsilon)\), a nonanalytic effect invisible to algebraic perturbation.
