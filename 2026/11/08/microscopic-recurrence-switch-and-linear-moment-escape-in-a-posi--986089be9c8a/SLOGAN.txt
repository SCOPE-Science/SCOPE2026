At the smallest spatial threshold of a position-feedback random walk, raising the integer acceleration parameter from \(r=1\) to \(r=2\) extinguishes stationary occupation and recurrence at the origin, while preserving unique, uniformly geometrically mixing, heavy-tailed equilibrium. The exact lattice stationary law has oscillating reciprocal-distance tail constants, and \(\mathbb E|X_t|\) still grows linearly—at slope \(3/5\) for \(r=1\), and slope \(r\) for every \(r\ge2\)—despite convergence in total variation.
