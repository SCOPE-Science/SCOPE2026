On a metric star with \(d\ge2\) equal arms, inserting one midpoint into each arm changes the sharp real \(L^\infty\) norm of the continuous affine \(L^2\) projector from \((7d+3)/(3(d+1))\) to \((1552d^2+928d+19)/(9(4d+3)(20d+1))\). The norm increases under this refinement for \(d=2,3\), decreases for all \(d\ge4\), and is attained exactly at the outer leaves.
