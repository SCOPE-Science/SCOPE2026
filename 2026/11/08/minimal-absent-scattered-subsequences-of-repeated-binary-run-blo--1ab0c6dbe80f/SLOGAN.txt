For the repeated binary-run word \((0^a1^b)^r\), every minimal absent scattered subsequence has an explicit run-length criterion. Its total number is \(F_{2r+2}\) when \(a,b\ge2\), \(3\cdot2^{r-1}\) when exactly one run length is one, and the coefficient of \(z^r\) in \(z(3-z+2z^2)/(1-2z+z^2-z^3)\) when both lengths are one.
