For the graph obtained by subdividing every edge of a labelled \(K_n\) once, every inclusion-minimal connected dominating set consists of all but one original vertex, a subdivided spanning tree on the survivors, and one additional subdivision vertex incident to the omitted original. Thus all such sets have size \(2n-2\), and exactly \(n(n-1)^{n-2}\) exist for every \(n\ge3\).
