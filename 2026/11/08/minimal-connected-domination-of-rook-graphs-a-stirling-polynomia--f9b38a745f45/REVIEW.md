# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The cell-to-edge identification converts rook-graph connectivity into connectivity of a bipartite incidence graph, and domination into saturation of at least one shore. Minimality excludes cycles and leaves on the incompletely represented shore. Conversely the classified trees have no removable edge: internal edges disconnect, and leaf edges expose an undominated cell across an already omitted opposite-shore vertex. The proof addresses arbitrary proper subsets, derives the bipartite weighted tree identity by the matrix-tree theorem, and counts all configurations by surjective words. Direct exhaustive tests on twelve small boards agree with every coefficient.

**Originality: PASS, provisionally.** The closest established exact parameter is the connected edge domination minimum of \(K_{m,n}\) (Kulli–Sigarkanti, cited in Li–Liu–Liu, Theorem 2(ii)), which proves only the smallest size. Abu-Khzam et al. give general enumeration algorithms but no closed-form minimal connected domination distribution for rook graphs. OEIS A360875/A289196 count all connected dominating subsets, not the inclusion-minimal subfamily. The adjacent maximal-induced-cluster, maximal-induced-forest, and bipartite-subgraph rook classifications are neither equivalent nor stronger because they impose unrelated induced-subgraph properties. The identified 1988 and 2009 texts were not completely accessible; an equivalent unindexed enumerator remains possible. The claim is mathematical novelty on best-available comparisons, not a priority guarantee.

**Value: PASS.** The gap is structural: a closed cardinality enumerator and a two-family bijective classification for a standard two-parameter family, including a new exact upper inclusion-minimal connected-domination number and unbounded missing-size intervals. It extends a known minimum result to a complete distribution and exhibits behavior that cannot be inferred from that minimum or the overall connected domination counts. This supports study of minimal-solution size spectra and connected edge domination.

**Critical limits.** The finite verifier is corroboration only, and the proof applies to complete rectangular boards with both dimensions at least two. Neither broader graph classes nor historical publication priority is certified.
