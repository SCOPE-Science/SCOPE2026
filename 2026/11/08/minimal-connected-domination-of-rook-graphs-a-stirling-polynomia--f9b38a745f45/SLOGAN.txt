For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), all inclusion-minimal connected dominating sets correspond exactly to bipartite trees covering one entire shore, missing at least one vertex on the other shore, and having no leaves there; a Stirling-number polynomial counts them at every size. For \(2\le m\le n\), their sizes form \(\{m,\ldots,2m-2\}\cup\{n,\ldots,n+m-2\}\), yielding arbitrarily long gaps when \(n\ge2m\).
