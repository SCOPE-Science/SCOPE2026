For every connected graph of minimum degree at least two, inclusion-minimal connected vertex covers of its full edge subdivision correspond exactly to spanning trees of connected induced vertex covers, yielding an independent-set weighted spanning-tree polynomial. On subdivided \(K_{2,q}\), the polynomial is \(q3^{q-1}z^{2q+3}+2z^{3q+1}\), with arbitrarily long missing intervals between possible cardinalities.
