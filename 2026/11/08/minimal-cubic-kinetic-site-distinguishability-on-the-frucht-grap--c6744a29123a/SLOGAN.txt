On the twelve-vertex Frucht graph, uniform random sequential monomer adsorption assigns twelve pairwise distinct terminal occupation probabilities to vertices that all have degree three. Twelve is the sharp minimum order for this phenomenon among cubic graphs; the exact terminal cardinality law is \((\Pr(X=3),\Pr(X=4),\Pr(X=5))=(1/64,169/192,5/48)\).
