All inclusion-minimal dominating sets of \(K_m\times K_n\), for \(m,n\ge2\), are complete rows, complete columns, three-corner rectangles, three-cell matchings, or four-cell two-row/two-column configurations with disjoint opposite coordinates. Their exact enumerator yields the spectrum \(\{3,4,n\}\) for \(K_3\times K_n\) when \(n\ge6\), with \(n-5\) missing intermediate sizes.
