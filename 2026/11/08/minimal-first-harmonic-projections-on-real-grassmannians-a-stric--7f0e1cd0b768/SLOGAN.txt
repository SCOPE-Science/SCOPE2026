The minimum-norm first-harmonic projector on the real rank-\(k\) Grassmannian has an exact Haar absolute-deviation formula; at fixed rank, its sharp norm grows like \(n c_k\), with \(c_1<c_2<\cdots\uparrow1/\sqrt\pi\). For every growing sparse rank \(k=o(\sqrt n)\), the normalized sharp norm converges to \(1/\sqrt\pi\), revealing a strict finite-rank hierarchy followed by a universal Gaussian regime.
