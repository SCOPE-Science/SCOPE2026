Every inclusion-minimal fort of a rook graph is a corrected spanning star cover, a one-sided incidence forest with degree-two centers, or a nonspanning alternating cycle, giving an exact two-parameter generating function. On the two-row rook graph \(K_2\square K_n\), the only minimal-fort sizes are \(4\) and \(n\) for \(n\ge5\), leaving arbitrarily long gaps.
