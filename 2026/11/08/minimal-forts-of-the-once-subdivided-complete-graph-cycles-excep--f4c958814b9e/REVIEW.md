# Review of the minimal-fort classification

**Correctness — PASS.** The core equivalence \(\delta(X)\subseteq Y\) with \(\deg_Y(v)\ne1\) for outside original vertices follows from the two vertex types in the edge subdivision. The proof checks separately \(|X|=0\), \(|X|=1\), and \(|X|\ge2\); inclusion-minimality is tested against every proper fort subset, not just immediate deletions. The case \(2\le|X|\le n-2\) is ruled out by an actual four-cycle fort contained in the proposed set, and the exceptional star-complement forts are shown to have no proper fort subset. Exhaustive small cases use the original fort condition and full-subset minimality.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** The 2024 minimal-forts study establishes counts for other families and product constructions; the 2023 fort-hypergraph paper gives the general framework; and the 2014 complete-subdivision theorem calculates zero forcing and maximum nullity, not all inclusion-minimal forts. Their conclusions do not determine this polynomial or the gap spectrum. Searches for subdivision-specific formulations and record-level overlap found no equivalent result. A possible unindexed equivalent classification remains a publication-priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The complete hypergraph of minimal zero-forcing obstructions is needed for fort-cover formulations, which a single zero forcing number cannot reconstruct. The classification on a canonical complete-subdivision family exhibits two structurally different kinds of obstructions (cycles and original-vertex configurations), an unbounded size gap, and factorial growth with an explicit cardinality enumerator. The result is not a mere normalization of the 2014 scalar parameter.

The finite checker establishes only its explicitly tested instances; the unrestricted claim rests on the stated proof. Publication priority has not been independently certified.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
