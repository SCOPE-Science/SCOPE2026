In the graph obtained by subdividing every edge of \(K_n\) once, every inclusion-minimal fort is either the subdivision vertices of a simple original cycle, all original vertices, or all but one original vertex together with that exception's incident subdivision vertices. The resulting fort-size polynomial has an unbounded interval of missing cardinalities, even though its total number of minimal forts grows asymptotically as \(\frac e2(n-1)!\).
