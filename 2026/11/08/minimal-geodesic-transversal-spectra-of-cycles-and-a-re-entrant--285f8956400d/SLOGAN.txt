Every inclusion-minimal geodesic transversal of the labelled cycle \(C_n\) has two or three vertices, with exact counts \(b_n\) and \(c_n\) given by a parity-sensitive closed formula. The associated cyclic-geodesic avoidance complex is pure for \(3\le n\le8\), nonpure at \(n=9\), pure again at \(n=10\), and nonpure forever after; the new three-point minimal transversals grow cubically.
