For \(n\ge3,m\ge2\), every inclusion-minimal geodetic set of the clique fan \(K_1\vee(P_n[K_m])\) is encoded by an exact three-state local word, yielding a nine-state transfer matrix for the entire labelled cardinality polynomial. The complementary simplicial complex is pure precisely at \(n=3\), or when \(m=2\) and \(n\in\{4,6\}\); the six-layer exception is genuine.
