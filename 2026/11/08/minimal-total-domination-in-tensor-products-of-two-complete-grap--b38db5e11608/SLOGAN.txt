The minimal total dominating sets of \(K_m\times K_n\), for \(m,n\ge2\), are exactly three disjoint-coordinate cells or four cells forming two pairs in distinct rows or columns. Their complete polynomial is \(6\binom m3\binom n3 z^3+\binom m2\binom n2(\binom m2+\binom n2-1)z^4\); in particular the upper total domination number is always \(4\), settling the \(k=1\) case of Kazemi’s direct-product question.
