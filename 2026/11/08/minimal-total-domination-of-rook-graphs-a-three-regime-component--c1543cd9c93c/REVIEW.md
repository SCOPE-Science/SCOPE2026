# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses the exact selected-rook adjacency count on the row-column incidence graph. With one empty shore it proves necessity and sufficiency of three types of vertex-disjoint incidence components using indispensable edge deletions; with both shores occupied, minimality reduces the selected rook graph to disjoint \(K_2\) components. The labeled-component EGF counts every configuration once, including rectangular boundaries. The companion verifier reconstructs open neighborhoods, independently confirms component types, and exhaustively compares all subsets on eleven boards.

## Originality — PASS (provisional priority)
Velammal and Arumugam (2012) already determine the minimum total edge domination number of complete bipartite graphs (equivalently the minimum total domination number of their line graphs), and that prior minimum is acknowledged. Mertens (arXiv:2401.00716) counts all **ordinary** dominating rook sets and does not enforce open-neighborhood coverage of selected cells or inclusion-minimality. Pahlavsay, Palezzato and Torielli (arXiv:1807.08104) determine the minimum \(3\)-tuple total-domination number, not the complete distribution of inclusion-minimal \(1\)-tuple total dominating sets. The published research search checked the line-graph, rook, all-parameter, enumerator, and total-domination terminology; no statement shown by the consulted sources implies the component classification. Absence of an indexed source is not a proof of publication priority. Related ordinary connected-domination classifications are distinct because they impose connectedness and use closed neighborhoods.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result gives a natural all-parameter minimal-total-domination classification and a complete labeled-set enumerator for a classical graph product, beyond the known minimum or the ordinary domination polynomial. The three separate coverage regimes and nonconsecutive cardinality spectrum on a square board make the distribution structurally informative rather than an isolated numerical example.

## Risks and scope
Publication priority remains provisional because unindexed or differently formulated exact formulas may exist. Higher-tuple total domination, arbitrary line graphs, and products of three or more factors are not covered. Finite verification corroborates but does not replace the unrestricted proof.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
