For every equilateral complete multipartite metric graph, the exact real mean-zero \(H^1\)-to-supremum embedding constant and all equality cases are explicit: minimum vertex degree at least three forces every maximizing evaluation point into the interiors of edges joining the two largest parts. At minimum degree at most two, maximizing points instead lie at vertices of a largest part, except that the triangle and four-cycle have constant evaluation norm along the whole circle.
