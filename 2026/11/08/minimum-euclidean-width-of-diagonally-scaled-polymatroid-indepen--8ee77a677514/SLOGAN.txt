The minimum Euclidean width of any diagonally scaled polymatroid independence polytope is exactly \(\min_{S\ne\varnothing}f(S)/\sqrt{\sum_{e\in S}s_e^{-2}}\), and every minimizing direction is a signed, inverse-scaled indicator of a minimizing support. For uniform matroids, the normal changes sharply from a coordinate axis to the all-ones axis at \(n=r^2\).
