# Correctness, originality and mathematical value

## Correctness — PASS
The color-class estimate \(\sum_{v\in A_j}|u_v|\le W_K(u)\) proves \(W_K(u)\ge1/\sqrt q\) for any unit direction, and every maximum-clique indicator attains equality. Equality in the classwise square inequalities forces exactly one nonzero in every class; stable-pair and opposite-support obstructions force a common sign and a clique. A quantitative defect decomposition and the same pair obstruction yield the claimed stability estimate in its exact domain. The proof treats \(q=1\) explicitly. The bundled checker independently enumerates stable sets and clique contacts and verifies the formula and bound on 810 finite graphs. Finite tests are supporting evidence, not the proof.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)
The inspected full-text presentations of Ahmad--Fourier--Joswig (arXiv:2309.01626) and Freij-Hollanti--Lundström (arXiv:2312.13890) study face structures, combinatorial operations, and face-number comparisons. Hu--Laurent (DOI:10.1016/j.ejc.2018.02.014) treats extension complexity and clique inequalities. None of these identified results provides the exact minimum directional width, its complete set of minimizing signed normals, or the all-graph-order near-minimizer estimate. Related exact inradius and Minkowski-asymmetry results do not imply a minimum-width formula. Specific semantic searches of other published mathematical findings located no dominating theorem. Residual risks include an earlier equivalent thickness statement and a stronger unindexed result, particularly in older convex geometry literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete quantitative metric classification on a large natural class of canonical graph polytopes. It recovers the graph's maximum clique size from one Euclidean invariant, recovers every maximum clique from the minimizing directions, and gives a graph-order-independent stability modulus identifying near-minimizers. For poset chain polytopes it recovers all longest chains geometrically. The nontrivial classification and stability provide information that inradius or facial counts alone do not provide; neither a narrow numerical recomputation nor a mere restatement of the clique inequalities is claimed.

## Limits
The graph must satisfy \(\chi(G)=\omega(G)\). The stability threshold is sufficient and not claimed optimal; unrestricted graphs are outside the exact theorem. Historical priority needs independent review.
