# Scientific review

**Correctness.** Color classes bound the width and the sum of their squared-mass deficits is \(\delta=qw^2-1\). The mass in a color class away from its largest coordinate has \(\ell_1\) norm at most that coordinate's deficit from \(w\), giving an \(O(\delta)\) bound. Opposite signs or same-sign nonedges among the chosen coordinates contradict the width under the stated threshold. The one-color case needs no threshold. Complete graphs (and an edgeless two-vertex graph for \(q=1\)) show that the linear order cannot be improved in general. Finite tests in the original checker cover only the weaker estimate and baseline, not the new analytic refinement.

**Originality.** The baseline width and all its exact minimizing directions follow from the Hansen-polar description in Resultary 79ea53f863b0; the ordinary chain case also follows from the enriched-chain inradius and contacts in db93f92ff100. The theorem retained as a contribution is the graph-order-independent near-minimizer estimate with a linear deficit rate. Neither of those comparisons supplies such an estimate. This is a bounded comparison, not a historical-priority guarantee.

**Value.** The estimate recovers a signed maximum clique from a direction described only by a small width excess. A bound uniform in the number of vertices is not a consequence of compactness for one fixed polytope. The linear rate quantifies this recovery and is sharp in order of magnitude.
