The smallest finite-dimensional scalar Hardy model permitting a rank-\(r\) truncated Toeplitz perturbation whose kernel has the maximal nearly backward-shift defect \(2r+1\) has dimension exactly \(4r+2\), for every \(r\geq1\). A sparse two-monomial Toeplitz compression and rank-\(r\) coordinate coupling attain that dimension, with the kernel and its backward-shift image forming complementary subspaces.
