For every \(n\ge5\), the minimum isolating triples in the Kneser graph \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) are exactly all three-edge subgraphs of \(K_n\) other than paths \(P_4\), giving \(\binom{\binom n2}{3}-12\binom n4\) configurations. Their one-replacement reconfiguration graph is connected with diameter exactly three, but is not isometric in the ambient Johnson graph; a complete twelve-endpoint certificate proves the diameter bound for all \(n\).
