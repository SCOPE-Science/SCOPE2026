Every minimum \(k\)-limited dominating set of the two-level Sierpiński graph \(S(2,m)\) is exactly the complement of a symmetric binary matrix with constant row sum \(k\), for all \(1\le k<m\). This classifies and counts every optimum through a coefficient extractor, with involutions counting the \(k=1\) and \(k=m-1\) cases and looped paths and cycles counting the \(k=2\) case.
