For every cycle power \(C_n^k\) with \(k\ge2\) and \(n\ge2k+3\), the \(2k\)-vertex minimum zero forcing sets are exactly the closed cyclic neighborhoods with one neighbor deleted: there are \((2k-1)n\) of them, falling into exactly \(k\) dihedral orbits. The two shorter-order boundary counts are \(n\) and \(kn\), as already implied by classical coefficients.
