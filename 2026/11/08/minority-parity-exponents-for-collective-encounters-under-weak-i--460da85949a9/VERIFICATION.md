## correctness

PASS

Product killed kernel gives exact discounted success p_eps=(1-gamma)e_r^T(I-(1-gamma)Q_eps)^(-1)b_eps. Bipartition parity shows a successful path requires >=k=min(m,S-m) individual holds; explicit same-length paths with exactly k holds yield a strictly positive k-th Taylor coefficient a_j. Analyticity gives p_eps=a_j eps^k+O(eps^(k+1)); exact renewal gives E T=(1-p)/(gamma p), and geometric cycles with uniformly bounded second moments yield scaled Exp(1). For reset hub/leaf in any star, exact 3-state Markov equations yield given rational mean, and the derivative quartic has one positive root by Descartes signs.
