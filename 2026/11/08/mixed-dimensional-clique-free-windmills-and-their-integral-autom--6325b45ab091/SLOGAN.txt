The \(t\)-clique-free complex of a generalized windmill with \(k\ge2\) clique blades is either contractible or a wedge of spheres in the two explicit dimensions \(k(t-1)-1\) and \(k(t-2)\). Its entire integral homology action under vertex permutations and blade exchanges is an explicit graded tensor product of exterior powers of augmentation lattices, with a closed character formula.
