For every \(n\ge5\), the vertex-centered closed-neighborhood complex of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus a perfect matching is \(\bigvee^{n^2-3n+1}S^2\vee S^{n-2}\vee S^{n-2}\). Deleting the perfect matching therefore creates two high-dimensional spheres absent from the corresponding complete bipartite graph's closed-neighborhood complex.
