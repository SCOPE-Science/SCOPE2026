# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact selected-degree inequalities force every mixed face to have at most two selected vertices on either shore. They give the closed two polynomials and the explicit square-complex quotient. Contracting the two shore simplices is justified by CW-cofibration homotopy equivalence. The quotient is the unreduced suspension of a concrete graph-with-squares, and each square removes precisely one of the two parallel subdivided paths corresponding to a deleted-edge pair. The residual core is \(K_t\), with \((a-t)(b-t)\) other isolated points. This establishes the **integral** homotopy type; the finite mod-two program only checks small cases.

## Originality — PASS, provisional priority

The foundational full texts arXiv:2103.01402 and arXiv:2307.13271 were inspected for matched objects, parameter ranges, and stronger implications. Tu–Li–Du count global extremal maximal dissociation sets, not this parametrized topology. Carnero Bravo identifies \(\mathcal F_1\) and treats \(K_{a,b}\) and categorical products, but not the intermediate family with a proper nonempty deleted matching. Earlier complete-deletion and empty-deletion endpoint results were compared and not relabeled as novel. Targeted searches using dissociation, \(2\)-independence, partial matching deletion, and forest filtration did not find a broader formula entailing the mixed circle/sphere ranks. Residual risk: an unindexed theorem for a differently named restricted graph complex could subsume this result; search absence alone cannot certify priority.

## Value — PASS

The formula settles a natural interpolation between two previously separately understood homotopy regimes. For \(3\le t<\min\{a,b\}\), both degrees one and two occur *integrally and simultaneously*, with exact independent ranks. The homotopy model also supplies complete feasibility and inclusion-maximal cardinality distributions and an explicit mechanism for the dimensional transition, rather than merely a routine extremal count.
