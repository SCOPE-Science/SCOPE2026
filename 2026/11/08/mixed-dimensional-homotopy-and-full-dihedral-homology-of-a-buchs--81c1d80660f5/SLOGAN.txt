The Buchsbaum circulant \(C_{16}(1,2,6)\) has an independence complex of exact homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^4S^3\), and its full dihedral group acts on rational homology by a reflection-sign line in degree one and a four-dimensional cyclotomic module in degree three. Both positive-degree invariant spaces vanish, so the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
