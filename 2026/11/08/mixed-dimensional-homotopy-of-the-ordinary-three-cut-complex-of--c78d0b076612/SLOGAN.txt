The ordinary three-cut complex of the first triangle-bearing two-subset Kneser graph has the integral homotopy type \(\Delta_3(KG(6,2))\simeq\bigvee^{10}S^9\vee\bigvee^{24}S^{10}\). Ten naturally indexed three-sphere blocks in its Alexander dual explain the two adjacent homology degrees and yield a structural, torsion-free proof.
