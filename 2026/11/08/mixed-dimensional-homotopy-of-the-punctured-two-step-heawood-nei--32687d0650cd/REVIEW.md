Critical cells occur only in dimensions zero, four and five, with counts one, twenty-nine and two. Searches using both distance-neighborhood and squared-graph formulations and comparisons against nearby known published mathematical claims found no covering result. Unindexed literature and the possibility of a stronger published uniform theorem remain explicit risks; a search is not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** The exact-distance-one-or-two rule is a canonical radius expansion of the Fano incidence graph, equivalently the open neighborhood complex of its graph square, not an arbitrarily selected induced subcomplex. The simultaneous four- and five-dimensional sphere summands in a pure eight-dimensional complex, and the vanishing integral attaching matrix, reveal structural topology not supplied by known ordinary graph-degree results or simple face counting. This is a natural finite classification; no all-orders extension is claimed.

**Limitations and prior work.** The theorem fixes the fourteen-vertex Fano incidence graph and distance set \(\{1,2\}\). It does not characterize automorphism modules, all distance subsets or higher-order finite planes.
