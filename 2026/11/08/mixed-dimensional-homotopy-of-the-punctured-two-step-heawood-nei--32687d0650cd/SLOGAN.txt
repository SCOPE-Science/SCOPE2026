The Fano-plane incidence graph's punctured two-step distance-neighborhood complex, generated by vertices at graph distances one or two from a common center, has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{29}S^4\vee\bigvee^2S^5\). An exact acyclic matching and zero integral Morse attaching degrees identify this mixed-dimensional sphere wedge, even though the simplicial complex is pure eight-dimensional.
