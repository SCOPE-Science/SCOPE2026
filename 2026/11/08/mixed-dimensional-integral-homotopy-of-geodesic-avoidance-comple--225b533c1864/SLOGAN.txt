For a bouquet of any number of peripheral cliques, the simplicial complex of vertex sets avoiding all maximal geodesics is an integral wedge of \(N-1\) circles and \(\prod_i(m_i-1)\) spheres of dimension \(r-1\). Its entire face and inclusion-minimal-transversal polynomials are explicit, and genuine mixed-dimensional homology appears as soon as at least three petals have size at least two.
