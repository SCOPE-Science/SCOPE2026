# Scientific review: mixed-dimensional four-cube \(P_3\)-devoid complex

**Correctness — PASS.** The two independent face predicates agree for all 65,536 subsets. The entire nonempty face poset has 3,054 cells and 13,568 cover relations. A starting acyclic matching (1,513 pairs) is transformed via four unique 5-to-4 gradient-path cancellations and one unique 4-to-3 cancellation. Each intermediate matching is fully rechecked for acyclicity. The final 1,518 pairs leave one zero-cell, sixteen three-cells and one four-cell. The signed **integral** Morse boundary of the four-cell is exactly zero; the Hurewicz isomorphism then makes its attaching map null. The full homotopy statement follows. An independent mod-two chain reduction yields Betti ranks 16 and 1 in the predicted degrees. Risks: finite scripts require replay; mod-two checks alone would not prove an integral wedge, hence the signed integral computation is essential.

**Originality — PASS on inspected evidence.** The primary archived source, Taylan (arXiv:1501.06754v1, 2015-01-27), computes the \(P_3\)-devoid complexes of paths, forests and cycles, not the four-dimensional cube. Deshpande–Singh (arXiv:2001.05448, submitted 2020-01-15), describes higher independence for complete multipartite, fully whiskered, path, cycle and rooted-tree families, not this four-regular Cartesian cube. Previously recorded ordinary four-cube independence, matching, cut and Čech complexes use different face predicates and their stated formulas neither imply nor cover this particular second-independence complex. A targeted mathematical-record search for exact graph aliases and the proposed sphere ranks found no exact result. Risks: indexing is incomplete and an equivalent source may be unindexed or unpublished. The searchable result statements for dissimilar constructions were not treated as a full-text exclusion of every possible assertion.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The four-cube is a canonical regular graph, and forbidding induced three-vertex paths is the first nonflag higher-independence condition. The mixed-dimensional integral wedge, involving both three- and four-spheres, is qualitatively different from the equidimensional sphere wedges of the main archived path/forest/cycle calculations. The nullity of the integral four-cell attaching map is genuine structural information not forced by a face vector or mod-two Betti numbers. Risks: no extension to arbitrary-dimensional cubes is supplied, so this is a benchmark exact case, not a general classification.
