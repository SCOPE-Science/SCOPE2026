# Mixed-dimensional matching-complex homotopy on the pentagonal three-row cylinder \(P_3\square C_5\)

## Finding
Let \(G=P_3\square C_5\) be the Cartesian product of the three-vertex path and the five-cycle. The matching complex \(\mathcal M(G)\) is the abstract simplicial complex whose vertices are the edges of \(G\) and whose faces are pairwise vertex-disjoint edge sets. There is an integral homotopy equivalence
\[\mathcal M(G)\simeq\bigvee^{24}S^4\vee\bigvee^3S^5.\]
Thus \(\widetilde H_4(\mathcal M(G);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{24}\) and \(\widetilde H_5(\mathcal M(G);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^3\), with all other reduced integral homology zero. The space is 3-connected. This is an exact mixed-dimensional result for the first nontriangular odd-circumference three-row cylinder, not an infinite-width assertion.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph is **simple**, with fifteen vertices \((i,j)\) for \(i\in\{0,1,2\}\) and \(j\in\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z\). Its edges are the ten vertical pairs \(\{(i,j),(i+1,j)\}\), \(i=0,1\), and fifteen horizontal pairs \(\{(i,j),(i,j+1)\}\), \(i=0,1,2\), with residues modulo five. Exactly 25 distinct edges are present. There are no diagonals, parallel edges, or wrap-around edges in the path coordinate. A face of \(\mathcal M(G)\) is an arbitrary matching, including the empty face in augmented chain conventions. The result concerns ordinary non-equivariant geometric realization and integer coefficients.

## Proof
Order the 25 graph edges by increasing row, then increasing residue, listing the vertical edge at each position first where it exists and the horizontal edge second. Identify a face with the 25-bit integer recording its selected graph edges. Enumerate faces by branching on exclusion/inclusion of each edge, allowing inclusion only when its two endpoints are unused. This is exhaustive by induction on the 25 edge decisions. The number of faces at cardinalities \(0,1,\ldots,7\) is exactly
\[(1,25,240,1125,2710,3227,1645,240),\]
for a total of 9,213 augmented faces and 42,405 covering incidences.

Construct an acyclic matching on the **augmented** face poset by processing edge indices \(p=0,1,\ldots,24\). At stage \(p\), pair every still-unmatched face \(\sigma\) not containing \(p\) with \(\sigma\cup\{p\}\) whenever both remain, and remove both from subsequent stages. Exactly 4,586 disjoint pairs result. The complete Hasse graph, oriented from lower to higher face for nonmatched covers and reversed on matched covers, has all 9,213 vertices consumed by an exact topological sort. Hence the matching is acyclic; this checks *every* covering incidence, not only sampled paths. The unpaired augmented faces consist of 31 four-simplices and ten five-simplices. The empty face is paired with one ordinary vertex; dropping that augmented pair yields a valid ordinary acyclic matching with one critical vertex, 31 critical four-cells and ten critical five-cells.

Fix the orientation of every simplex by the chosen increasing edge order. For a lower face \(s\) paired with an upper face \(v\), write the unit incidence \(\epsilon=[\partial v:s]\in\{\pm1\}\). In the algebraic Morse reduction, the projection of \(s\) into the critical chains of the same dimension is defined recursively by
\[R(s)=-\epsilon^{-1}\!\sum_{t\prec v,\ t\ne s}[\partial v:t]R(t).\]
Set \(R(c)=c\) for critical faces and \(R(s)=0\) for an upper face paired downward. Each term is well founded along the verified acyclic gradient graph. For each critical five-face \(f\), the integral Morse boundary is \(\sum_{s\prec f}[\partial f:s]R(s)\). The resulting complete signed matrix \(B\), with 31 rows and ten columns, is included as `proof/morse_boundary_31x10.csv`. Its exact integer Smith normal form has seven nonzero diagonal entries, all \(+1\), and three zeros. Thus its rank is seven and its image is a primitive direct summand of \(\mathbb Z^{31}\).

The discrete Morse CW model is 3-connected, since it has no 1-, 2- or 3-cells. Its 4-skeleton is a wedge of 31 four-spheres. The attaching maps of the ten five-cells lie in \(\pi_4(\bigvee^{31}S^4)\cong\mathbb Z^{31}\) by the Hurewicz theorem. Integer row and column basis changes correspond to homotopy equivalences of the four-sphere bouquet and cellular changes of five-cell generators. The seven unit Smith factors therefore cancel seven four/five cell pairs; the other three five-cell attaching maps are null. The remaining cells give exactly 24 four-spheres and three five-spheres. This establishes the **integral homotopy type**, not just field-valued Betti numbers.

## Verification
Run `python3 proof/verify_cylinder.py` with Python 3 and SymPy (checked with SymPy 1.14.0). The verifier enumerates all matching faces, constructs the matched pairs from the specified ordering, tests the entire oriented Hasse graph for acyclicity, computes all signed integral Morse path contributions, and obtains exact Smith factors. It separately calculates the ordinary simplicial boundary ranks over \(\mathbb F_2\) using original simplices rather than the Morse complex. The verified mod-two reduced Betti ranks in dimensions one through six are \((0,0,0,24,3,0)\), corroborating the integer result; these finite-field checks are not used to infer absence of integral torsion. The entire executable output and the 31-by-10 signed boundary matrix are included.

## Relationship to prior work
Chauhan–Sharma–Shukla (arXiv:2609.27366, first public September 23, 2026) study matching complexes of **rectangular** graphs \(P_3\square P_n\), proving connectivity and homology-vanishing statements. The cited accessible abstract does not cover periodic graph \(P_3\square C_5\); its full preprint was not available for a complete appendix comparison, which remains a priority risk. Goyal–Shukla–Singh (2021, DOI 10.37236/10496), whose paper was subsequently retracted, explicitly define their graphs as rectangular grids without periodic edges, so the claimed planar result would not imply this cylindrical computation. Work on the independence complexes of **underlying** cylindrical grid graphs (e.g. arXiv:2204.05629) uses \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_m\square C_n)\), not the matching complex \(\operatorname{Ind}(L(P_m\square C_n))\). Targeted published-record and mathematical-results searches located no same-graph integral homotopy classification; absence from searches is not evidence of uniqueness.

## Limitations
Only the graph \(P_3\square C_5\), with the precise edge convention above, is classified. No periodic family theorem, equivariant homotopy decomposition, ring structure, or independent mathematical audit is claimed. The homotopy conclusion uses standard discrete Morse theory and elementary CW cancellation together with a finite exact computation; its reproducibility depends on the stated deterministic edge ordering and signed reduction conventions. Unindexed equivalent work and the uninspected full text of the 2026 motivating paper remain originality risks.
