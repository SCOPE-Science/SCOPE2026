# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The definition fixes the fifteen graph vertices and all twenty-five distinct edges of \(P_3\square C_5\). Exhaustive matching enumeration yields 9,213 augmented faces and a complete 42,405-incidence Hasse graph; acyclicity is checked by consuming all face nodes. The matched-face counts are 4,586 pairs, leaving 31 critical 4-faces and ten 5-faces, plus one ordinary critical vertex on deleting the empty-face pair. The exact 31-by-10 signed Morse differential has seven nonzero Smith entries, each a unit. Since all other positive Morse dimensions vanish, cellular attaching maps live in \(\pi_4\) of a four-sphere bouquet and the integer Smith reduction yields the stated homotopy wedge. An independent original-complex mod-two boundary calculation recovers Betti ranks 24 and 3; only the integral Morse matrix establishes torsion-freeness. Risk: a finite executable is part of the proof and must be replayed as supplied; no infinite-size inference is made.

## Originality — PASS, residual risk
The closest motivating preprint is about \(P_3\square P_n\), not the periodic product \(P_3\square C_5\), and the accessible abstract states only vanishing/connectivity for rectangular grids. A full-text arXiv attempt, alternate lawful OA attempts and authorized institutional attempt did not yield the complete 2026 text. A relevant 2021 full-text paper and its corrigendum were inspected: the graph is explicitly the *rectangular* grid, so its claimed (subsequently retracted) theorem does not imply this cylindrical claim. Work on ordinary independence complexes of cylindrical square grids does not apply to the line graph without a further theorem. Targeted mathematical-record and historical-claim searches for the exact cylinder, its line-graph alias and its integral homotopy numbers gave no dominating result; analogous sphere-wedge calculations on other graphs are not the same claim. Risks: unindexed equivalent work and an uninspected appendix of the recent preprint; search failure is not novelty proof.

## Value — PASS
Closing the column boundary is a canonical variation of the actively studied three-row grid matching-complex problem. The five-cycle is the smallest *nontriangular odd* circumference, so the graph is a natural boundary-condition test rather than an arbitrary edge selection. Unlike a purely same-dimensional wedge, it exhibits 24 four-spheres **and** three five-spheres. The homotopy splitting and integral torsion exclusion are mathematically finer than an Euler characteristic or a mod-two census. The finding does not claim a general cylinder classification, but it supplies a verified exact initial odd-periodic case suitable for detecting the dimensions and attaching-map patterns in that broader question.

No independent audit, all-width theorem or equivariant splitting is claimed.
