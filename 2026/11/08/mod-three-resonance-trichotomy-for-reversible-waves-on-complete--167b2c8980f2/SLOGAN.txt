For second-order neighbor-sum waves on \(K_{a,b}\) over any finite field of characteristic three, the residue of \(ab\) modulo three determines a sharp global period of eight or twelve and the exact number of configurations fixed at every time. A resonance at \(ab\equiv1\pmod3\) creates precisely the extra primitive periods two, three, and six, while complete orbit counts follow from the fixed-space formulas.
