For neighbor-sum dynamics on the open ladder \(P_n\square K_2\) over any characteristic-two finite field, reversibility holds exactly when \(n\not\equiv2\pmod3\). At the exceptional lengths the zero-primary part consists of two equal Jordan chains of length \(2^{\nu_2(n+1)+1}\), giving an exact entrance-time census in which \(1-q^{-2}\) of states require the maximum transient.
