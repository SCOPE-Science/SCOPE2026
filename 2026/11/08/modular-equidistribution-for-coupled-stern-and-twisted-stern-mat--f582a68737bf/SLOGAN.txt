For every odd modulus, the dyadic-sign-normalized matrix of adjacent Stern and twisted-Stern values is uniformly distributed over all matrices of determinant \(-2\). The unnormalized pair \((s(n),t(n))\) is uniformly distributed over primitive residue pairs, whereas an individual unnormalized coupled matrix has distinct lower and upper frequencies \(1/(3G_m)\) and \(2/(3G_m)\).
