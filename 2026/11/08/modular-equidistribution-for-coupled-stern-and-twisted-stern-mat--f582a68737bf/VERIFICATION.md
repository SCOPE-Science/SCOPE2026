# Verification

The finite exact-integer checker `verify.py` independently generates the two sequences from their recurrences and computes normalized coupled matrices modulo \(3,5,7,9,11,15\). It enumerates the Cayley subgroup generated by the two elementary matrices for each modulus, checks its cardinality against the exact \(\operatorname{SL}_2\) formula, and compares every tested binary-word matrix product with the independent integer sequence generation. It tests determinant identities and residue supports on all complete binary blocks from depth \(1\) through \(16\), and verifies the recurrences independently for thousands of inputs.

The finite output can show substantial deviation from uniformity for larger moduli; this does not contradict the asymptotic theorem. It is not an infinite proof and cannot establish historical novelty. The mathematical proof relies on the finite-group irreducibility/aperiodicity argument and the uniform binary-cylinder discrepancy estimate. No independent audit was carried out.
