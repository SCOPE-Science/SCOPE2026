# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem uses an explicit product transport, a nilpotent augmentation ideal, the exponent-\(p\) principal-unit group, and an equivariant filtration. The splitting step is valid because \(\operatorname{ord}_p(2)\) is prime to \(p\); hence each doubling cycle contributes exactly one eigenline. The induced nonzero-idempotent operation is the midpoint operation on the eigen-subgroup. The proof covers all odd primes, positive ranks, and coefficient fields of characteristic \(p\). Exhaustive finite-field calculations corroborate the stated small examples, but do not replace the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest published result classifies \(R_3\) in characteristic three and explicitly covers the special case \(p=3,r=1\); that case is excluded from the novelty argument. Bardakov–Elhamdadi (arXiv:2601.07057, Proposition 5.5) treats a different coefficient-characteristic regime. Zhang (arXiv:2610.08204) supplies the transported product and integral idempotent results, and Churchill et al. (arXiv:2608.09149) study smaller or different examples. The general modular all-rank eigenspace count and induced idempotent-quandle classification were not located and do not follow directly from those statements without the new principal-unit argument. The full text of Elhamdadi–Ta–Virgin (arXiv:2609.03799) was unavailable; absolute priority is not certified. Published-record searches and source comparisons are documented in `AUDIT.json`.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This exact structural classification answers a natural coefficient-sensitive idempotent problem for all elementary abelian groups and all extension degrees, not just for a chosen small example. It identifies the characteristic-specific explosive growth of the idempotent quandle, for example nine basis elements versus eighty-one nonzero idempotents at \(p=3,r=2,K=\mathbb F_3\), and isolates the arithmetic control by \(\operatorname{ord}_p(2)\). The result is relevant to algebraic enhancements of quandle-coloring invariants and modular quandle algebra structure.

## Closest work and limitations
The result does not cover non-elementary groups, characteristic two, or different coefficient characteristic. The earlier three-element case is covered rather than repeated as an original finding, and one relevant recent preprint was only inspectable to abstract level. No independent external audit is represented.
