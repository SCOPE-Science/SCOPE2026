For the midpoint quandle on \((\mathbb F_p)^r\) in its own odd characteristic, the nonzero idempotents in the quandle algebra form another midpoint quandle, of \(\mathbb F_p\)-dimension \((p^r-1)\dim_{\mathbb F_p}K/\operatorname{ord}_p(2)\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), their exact number is \(q^{(p^r-1)/\operatorname{ord}_p(2)}\), with one additional zero idempotent.
