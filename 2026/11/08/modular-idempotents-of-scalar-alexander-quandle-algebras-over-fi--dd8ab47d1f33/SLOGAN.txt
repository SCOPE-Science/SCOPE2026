For every scalar Alexander quandle on the additive group of a finite field, modular idempotents form an explicit affine quandle and their exact number is \(1+|K|^d\), where \(d\) counts solutions of \(\alpha^k+(1-\alpha)^k=1\) among the multiplicative character exponents. In characteristic two over \(\mathbb F_{16}\), different nontrivial scalars give \(17\) or \(1025\) idempotents with \(\mathbb F_2\) coefficients.
