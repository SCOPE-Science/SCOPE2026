For every nilpotent matrix \(N\) satisfying \(N^d=0\) over a positive-characteristic field, the differential of the power map has only Jordan blocks of lengths one, two and three, with explicit multiplicities from the Jordan partition of \(N\). When the characteristic divides \(d\), **all** length-three blocks disappear and the differential becomes square-zero; otherwise their number is exactly the square of the number of length-\(d\) blocks of \(N\).
