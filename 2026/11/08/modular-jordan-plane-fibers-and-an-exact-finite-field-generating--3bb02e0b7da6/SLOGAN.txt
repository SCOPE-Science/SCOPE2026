For finite-field Jordan-plane representations satisfying \([X,Y]=Y^2\), a matrix \(Y\) admits a partner precisely when all its nonzero-eigenvalue elementary-divisor exponents are divisible by the field characteristic. The resulting affine fibers yield a characteristic-dependent infinite product that counts every labeled representation in every dimension; the first nonnilpotent contribution occurs exactly in dimension \(p\) and equals \(q|\operatorname{GL}_p(\mathbb F_q)|\).
