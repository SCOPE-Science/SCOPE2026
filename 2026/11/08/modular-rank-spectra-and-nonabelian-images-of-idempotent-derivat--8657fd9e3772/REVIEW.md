# Review of modular rank spectra of free class-two idempotent derivations

## Correctness — PASS
The full derivation space is parameterized by a quotient block on the free generators and an arbitrary central block. Squaring its explicit matrix gives three necessary and sufficient equations. The induced action on the three exterior-square summands has eigenvalues zero, one and two; the condition that two be idempotent is exactly the characteristic-two fork. The free-block solution spaces, full linear ranks, geometric component dimensions and Gaussian-binomial finite-field counts follow by direct linear algebra. The included checker reconstructs and tests the *original* identities in four exhaustive small cases; the infinite theorem relies on the proof rather than those samples. Potentially nonreduced scheme structure has not been determined.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)
Bai, Gao and Li (2015) work over the complex numbers and infer an abelian image by cancelling two; their valid theorem therefore does not cover the characteristic-two nonabelian images. Benito and Roldán-López (arXiv:1911.01897) provide a general characteristic-zero description of free-nilpotent derivations that is credited as structural background, not a rank census. Enomoto and Satoh (arXiv:1012.2169) and Milentyeva (arXiv:1408.0871) address different free-Lie derivation or class-two geometry questions. The earlier Heisenberg result includes the d=2 case; novelty is restricted to d>=3. An earlier free-class-two *square-zero* derivation census uses the incompatible identity \(D^2=0\). No inspected primary source or published-record search provided the characteristic-two idempotent rank formula and component theorem. A yet-unidentified covering enumeration is still possible; failed searches are not priority proof.

## Value — PASS
The finding supplies a complete rank-resolved invariant and component geometry uniformly in the generator count, and it displays an unbounded modular proliferation of components as well as nonabelian-image projections excluded by the usual complex-field theorem. The exact rank polynomials also distinguish subtle full-operator rank collisions across quotient ranks. These are natural structural and arithmetic invariants; the contribution is not merely computational correctness.

## Verification, scope and risk
The theorem treats free two-step nilpotent Lie algebras with at least three generators, over all fields; finite-field counts are for labeled operators. Reduced component geometry is established but unreduced schemes and conjugacy classes are not. Arithmetic outputs labeled FORMULA in the checker are evaluations, not exhaustively enumerated configurations. The relationship to earlier structural work and the still-provisional priority claim are spelled out in the result and structured audit.
