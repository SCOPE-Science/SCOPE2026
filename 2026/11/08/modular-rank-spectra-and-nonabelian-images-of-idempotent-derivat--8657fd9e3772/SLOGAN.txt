For the free class-two Lie algebra on at least three generators, idempotent derivations have only ranks \(0\) and \(d\) outside characteristic two, but in characteristic two their possible ranks are exactly \(r(d+1-r)\), with a complete Gaussian-binomial finite-field census and \(d+1\) smooth reduced components. Every component indexed by \(r\ge2\) has nonabelian, non-ideal image, sharply exposing the characteristic restriction in the classical abelian-image theorem.
