In complex \(c_0\), bounded closed convex bodies with no nonzero modulus-support functional occur arbitrarily close to the ordinary complex unit ball. Each body is the image of that ball under an invertible real-linear map \(R_\tau=I+\tau A\circ\mathrm{conj}\), where the perturbation is real-nuclear and has exact norm \(\tau/3\); it can be made arbitrarily small.
