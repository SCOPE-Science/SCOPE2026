On a nonzero harmonic level \(\operatorname{Im}(z^n)=c\), every polynomial phase ambiguity is a reflection quotient in \(z^n\), so its degree is a positive multiple of \(n\); two distinct levels eliminate all ambiguities. With degree cutoff \(d\), the exact complete-intensity dimension across \(L\) levels is \((d+1)^2-\max\{0,d-nL+1\}^2\), giving sharp finite linear sampling allocations and a distinct threshold for full Hermitian lifting.
