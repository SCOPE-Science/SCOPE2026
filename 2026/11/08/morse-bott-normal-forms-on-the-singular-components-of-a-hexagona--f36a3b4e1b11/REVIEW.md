PASS (same-model). The support argument considers all seven possible support sizes from zero through six and uses two explicit dihedral normalizations for the four-support case. Every displayed component is annihilated identically by the gradient, not merely at sampled points. The Hessian-rank upper bounds follow from the tangent cones of linear singular strata, and five rational witnesses provide matching nonzero minors on every symmetry orbit. The holomorphic Morse–Bott lemma therefore establishes the claimed analytic normal forms on dense open subsets. The exact standalone arithmetic checker reproduces all twenty linear-space identities and rank bounds. The theorem does **not** extrapolate finite tests to infinitely many parameter values.
