Every finite-field degree-five del Pezzo surface with a pentagon of exceptional curves has an open complement of one of just two exact Grothendieck classes: \(\mathbb L^2+1\) for every rotation and \(\mathbb L^2-1+[\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_{q^2}]\) for a reflection. Four marked arithmetic types thus collapse to two characteristic-free motivic and Frobenius types, with point counts \(q^{2n}+1\) or \(q^{2n}+(-1)^n\) over every extension.
