# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** If \(f^2=e_S\), extensivity forces \(f\) to fix \(S\), and injectivity plus order preservation prohibit any other fixed point, directed two-step chain, or moving arrow that touches/crosses \(S\). Independent gaps are precisely Motzkin words under domain/image/unused encoding. Their ordered matching yields a bijection, not just a recurrence. Summing over all gap lengths gives \(M(z)/(1-uzM(z))\). At its unique dominant singularity \(1/3\), the denominator has a square-root zero; transfer on factorial-moment derivatives yields the stated Rayleigh moments. Carleman moment determinacy converts moments into weak convergence. The Catalan cardinality yields the rarity constant. An exhaustive finite check through length nine covered 23,713 total maps and every one of 1,023 prescribed square-root fibers, plus coefficient identities to degree 29. **Risk:** Computations are finite; the infinite theorem relies on the written bijection and analytic proof. Algebraic singularity transfer uses the standard isolated positive branch point of the explicit radical.

**Originality — PASS relative to checked evidence, with residual risk.** The closest source, Al-Kharousi–Umar–Zubairu (arXiv:2501.00285), defines quasi-idempotents and classifies specific shift-one generators but does not give an exact all-idempotent square-fiber decomposition or conditional fixed-point limit in its relevant sections. Gusev–Sapir–Volkov (arXiv:2301.12426) introduces the monoid in the finite-basis context, not this enumeration. OEIS A005773 already contains the exact unweighted Motzkin INVERT generating function and unweighted asymptotics: **those numerical identities are excluded from priority claims**. The contribution is the complete, idempotent-resolved semigroup equation and its probability law. Searches of related transformation-semigroup literature and mathematical claim records found no directly covering statement. **Risks:** A 1992 thesis discusses quasi-idempotents of a larger decreasing-injective monoid but could not be reliably read in its relevant chapters (repository PDF later returned access denial); the full 2015 Laradji–Umar article was not available, and its abstract focuses on nilpotents and other refinement parameters. A broadly formulated Motzkin renewal theorem could imply the probabilistic corollary after the structural identification. Priority is therefore provisional.

**Value — PASS.** Counting solutions of the natural internal equation \(f^2=e_S\) uniformly for every idempotent distinguishes maps with the same fixed-point rank but differently spaced fixed points: on a four-element chain, singletons at endpoints have four roots, whereas singletons in the interior have two. This is a full structural fiber classification rather than a restatement of the known scalar sequence. The conditioned Rayleigh scale \(\sqrt n\), together with the exponentially rare probability of the qualifying semigroup event, describes the typical quasi-idempotent in a way rank totals do not. These exact fibers may be used to analyze power maps and idempotent-root statistics in finite Catalan-type monoids. **Risk:** No downstream theorem beyond the stated classification and limit is claimed.
