# Scientific review

**Correctness.** If the square of an injective, order-preserving, extensive partial map equals the partial identity on a prescribed set, every prescribed point is fixed. Moving arrows cannot touch/cross those points and cannot continue for a second step; the resulting disjoint interval gaps carry exactly nonnegative Motzkin paths under the ordered domain/image/unused encoding. Hence the count of roots is the product of Motzkin numbers of the gap lengths for every nonnegative chain length, including the empty case. Summation by the number of prescribed fixed points gives F(z,u)=M(z)/(1-uzM(z)); the explicit Motzkin square-root singularity at z=1/3, factorial-moment transfer and moment determinacy yield the conditional Rayleigh law with scale sqrt(2/3), its mean constant sqrt(pi/3), and the Catalan-normalized rare-event estimate. A separate direct census of 23,713 partial injections through n=9 checked all 1,023 idempotent fiber cases; this is corroboration, not an infinite proof.

**Originality, best of knowledge.** OEIS A005773 already records the total unweighted coefficients 1, 2, 5, 13, 35, 96, ... and their Motzkin-INVERT generating function and scalar asymptotic; these are **not** presented as new. The complete primary text of Al-Kharousi, Umar, and Zubairu (arXiv:2501.00285) covers quasi-idempotent terminology, shift-one generation and ranks rather than individual prescribed square fibers or a conditioned Rayleigh distribution. The original full 2004 Laradji–Umar paper and the 2013/2016 lattice-path treatment enumerate related noninjective partial-transformation families. The full earlier mathematical results on i-Catalan idempotent commutants, total-map stable rank, and commuting Catalan idempotent product fibers concern distinct equations or ambient monoids. They do not imply the exact Motzkin fiber assignment. The full 2015 Laradji–Umar paper and Umar's 1992 thesis chapters could not be read; an unindexed equivalent theorem remains a serious historical-priority risk. No worldwide priority guarantee is made.

**Mathematical value.** The all-idempotent square-fiber formula is a natural exact power-map classification on an infinite Catalan-type semigroup family, discriminating fixed-point location rather than only rank. The conditioned fixed-point Rayleigh scale and its exponentially rare event describe a natural subpopulation not recovered from scalar totals. In particular, at n=4 the root fiber over an endpoint singleton has size 4 whereas over an interior singleton it has size 2. No generalization to noninjective or arbitrary partial transformations is asserted.
