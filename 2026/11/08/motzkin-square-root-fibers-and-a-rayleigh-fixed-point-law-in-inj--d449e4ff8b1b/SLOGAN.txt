In the injective partial Catalan monoid, the number of solutions of \(f^2=e_S\) is exactly the product of Motzkin numbers of the gaps cut out by the fixed points of \(e_S\). Conditioned on \(f^2\) being idempotent, the number of fixed points divided by \(\sqrt n\) converges to a Rayleigh law of scale \(\sqrt{2/3}\); the known unweighted Motzkin INVERT sequence is not claimed as new.
