For the Gaussian-weighted modulus of a polynomial whose zeros are \(k\) concentric regular \(n\)-gons, there are at most \(kn+1\) local maxima, all off-center maxima lying on the opposite symmetry rays. For every \(k\), the bound is attained by an explicit configuration with nondegenerate maxima whenever \(n\ge\lceil8k\log(8k)\rceil\).
