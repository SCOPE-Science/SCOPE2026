# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the fixed N,m,k, r,D domain, stationary tagged ages have exact Marshall–Olkin survival exp(-r sum d_j t_[j]), with d_j=C(N-j,m-1)/C(N,m)>0 and k<=N-m+1. Positive marks avoiding older tags give local lower bounds, Gaussian mixing yields inf_{u>0}[r sum d_j u_[j]+sum a_i^2/(4Du_i)], and strict convexity plus ordered rearrangement yields decreasing weighted PAV. Four k=3 PAV profiles were independently recalculated for (N,m)=(5,2): singleton, first pair, last pair, full pool; the d values 2/5,3/10,1/5 were checked. Distinct-age event forcing and tied-age approximation cover boundaries. Finite tests cannot establish R→infinity, so the exact Poisson/Laplace proof is essential.
