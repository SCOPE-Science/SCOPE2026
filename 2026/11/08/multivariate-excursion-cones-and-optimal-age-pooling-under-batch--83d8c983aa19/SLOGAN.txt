Uniform partial-batch resetting produces a sharp multivariate far-tail rate for any \(k\ge3\) tagged Brownian particles in the stated admissible range: the rate is \(\sqrt{r/D}\sum_B\sqrt{d_BS_B}\), where blocks are selected by weighted isotonic pooling of the excursion thresholds. Unlike two-particle data alone, three-particle extreme excursions distinguish four different optimal reset-age clustering geometries and exhibit genuine second-derivative transition boundaries.
