For unit-edge rooted binary phylogenies, the two near-complete rarefaction variances \(\operatorname{Var}Y_2\) and \(\operatorname{Var}Y_3\) uniquely recover the numbers of cherry and pitchfork clades. At every size \(6m\), \(m\ge1\), an explicit pair of trees reverses its variance ranking between exactly two and exactly three lost taxa, despite equal total branch length and identical sampling budgets.
