For \(n\ge3\) random radius-\(\pi/n+\varepsilon\) caps on \(S^m\), the probability of covering a prescribed great circle has a sharp explicit gamma-function coefficient times \(\varepsilon^{n(m+1)/2-1}\). Given this rare event, scaled transverse displacements approach a spherical-direction/Beta law, while normalized cyclic overlap slacks are exactly independent \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,\ldots,1)\) for every sufficiently small positive \(\varepsilon\).
