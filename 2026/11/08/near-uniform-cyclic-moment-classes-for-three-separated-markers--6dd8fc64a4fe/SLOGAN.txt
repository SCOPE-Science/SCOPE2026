For every indexed binary circular word of weight three with at least \(h-1\ge1\) zeros between successive ones, the checksum \(\sum_i i w_i\pmod n\) distributes perfectly uniformly when \(3\nmid n\) and with an exact one-word excess in precisely the residues divisible by three when \(3\mid n\). An explicit all-length formula gives every bucket size, including the boundary \(n=3h\).
