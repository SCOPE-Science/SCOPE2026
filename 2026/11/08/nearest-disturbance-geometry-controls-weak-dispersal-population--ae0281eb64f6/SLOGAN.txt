In a connected habitat network with localized independent environmental disturbances, stationary cross-correlation under weak migration has an exact positive leading coefficient and an integer decay exponent determined by shortest paths to disturbance sources. With one source, the limiting Pearson correlation is \(\frac{(d_i+d_j)!}{\sqrt{(2d_i)!(2d_j)!}}\), independent of migration edge weights, damping, and disturbance intensity; patches at equal source distance become perfectly correlated even though their fluctuation amplitudes vanish.
