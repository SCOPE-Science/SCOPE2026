For every fixed \(a\ge1\) and \(\Delta\ge0\), the negative-one partial-Stirling values at indices \(a2^e+\Delta\) converge in \(\mathbb Z_2\) to \(2^{s_2(a)}P_\Delta(-1)/U_a\), where \(U_a=\lim_{e\to\infty}U((a2^e)!)\). This resolves the \(-1\) input throughout an all-shift family beyond the established nonnegative-input limit formula, without claiming the general periodic-input conjecture.
