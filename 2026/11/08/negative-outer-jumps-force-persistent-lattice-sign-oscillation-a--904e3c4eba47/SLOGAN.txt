For a spectrally stable symmetric finite-range heat stencil with a negative outermost jump, the infinite lattice propagator has infinitely many coefficients of **both signs at every positive time**, even though each finite periodic propagator eventually becomes strictly positive. For the classical fourth-order five-point stencil the positivity-recovery time on an \(n\)-point cycle obeys \(\liminf_{n\to\infty}H_n/n\ge1/500\) and \(H_n\le n^2/16\), revealing a quantitative dimension-dependent delay.
