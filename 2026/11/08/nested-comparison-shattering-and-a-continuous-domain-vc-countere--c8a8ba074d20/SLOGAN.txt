Pairwise-comparison supports of strict single-peaked votes are shattered exactly when their queried pairs are strictly nested, giving \(\Phi_n(z)=\sum_k\binom n{2k}z^k\), \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), and \(\operatorname{Ldim}=n-1\). The same nested pairs show that unrestricted continuous real-line single-peaked preferences have infinite VC dimension, contrary to a published illustrative finite-dimensional claim.
