# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the unital square-zero algebra, evaluating the defining quadratic operator identity on radical-radical pairs eliminates the radical-to-scalar functional over **every field**; evaluating at the unit leaves exactly the marked-eigenvector equation. The resulting normal form was reconstructed from definitions and gives the raw count by counting kernels of all linear maps. The automorphism group is exactly \(\operatorname{GL}(V)\). Fixed-similarity-type orbits are classified by the smallest Jordan block length appearing in an eigenvector; a direct cyclic-module proof establishes that there is one nonzero orbit per distinct primary-block size. The Euler product follows from the rational canonical partition classification and unique factorization of monic polynomials. The included exhaustive prime-field computations check the identity directly, the normal form, and actual conjugacy orbit counts, but are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources
The closest source, Zou–Gao–Kang–Lü (arXiv:2308.12121, Theorem 4.1), already covers parameter families for the three-dimensional complex case; that portion is not claimed new. The full text says it does not carry out automorphism-orbit analysis. Achar–Henderson (arXiv:0712.1079, §2) gives much broader framed nilpotent-matrix orbit theory, which supplies antecedents to the key marked-vector step; matrix similarity classification and its Euler product are also established. These do not directly provide the arbitrary-dimensional associative Nijenhuis normal form together with the exact finite-field automorphism-orbit series. Searches of the title, algebra aliases, marked-eigenvector formulation, generating series, and broader classifications did not reveal an equivalent statement; this is bounded evidence, not a guarantee of publication priority. The Ma–Long bialgebra paper was only compared at abstract/bibliography level; stronger inaccessible or unlocated classification is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Classification up to algebra automorphism is the natural moduli problem for Nijenhuis structures on a fixed algebra, avoiding the excessive labeled parametrizations. The theorem handles every radical dimension and field characteristic, determines both labeled and isomorphism-type counts, and explains why one marked eigenvector changes the classical matrix-class Euler product by exactly \((1-z)^{-1}\). The result relates operated-algebra moduli to rational canonical modules, rather than just redoing one small example; it also isolates the precise square-zero boundary of the argument.

## Closest literature and limits
Three-dimensional complex examples in arXiv:2308.12121 overlap with the base case of the normal form. General vector-nilpotent matrix orbits in arXiv:0712.1079 underpin a proof ingredient; neither is a newly discovered lemma. The classification does not extend automatically to non-split radicals or to radical cube nonzero. Proof validity was checked for the stated square-zero algebra only. Literature searches were bounded and may miss alternate nomenclature.
