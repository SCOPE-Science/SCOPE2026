# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The decomposition into a partial injection of chain indices and a residual matching is a bijection, with ranks adding after a shift by twice the chain-index rank. Nilpotency is exactly absence of cycles. A direct directed-path enumeration gives the acyclic index factor, and a signed permutation cycle sieve cancels every marked subset of size at least two, yielding the precise residual polynomial. All boundary cases \(r=0\) and \(s=0\) are treated explicitly. The independent exhaustive partial-map checker passes every coefficient for all fifteen square-zero conjugacy types through six vertices.

**Originality — PASS, provisional and qualified.** Konieczny's full centralizer membership characterization is not new and is credited. Gomes–Howie's nilpotent-chain classification is not new and is credited. The candidate contribution is the simultaneous, parameter-uniform *nilpotent rank distribution inside* square-zero centralizers, including the cycle-cancellation formula and the top-rank equality. The 2012 membership theorem and 1987 chain theorem make these counts in principle computable but do not directly state the evaluated two-parameter enumerator. A 2020 paper on finite centralizer structure and Lipscomb's monograph were not fully inspected; they present explicit historical-priority risk. Searches for a dominating rank-distribution formula did not locate one; absence of a search hit is not proof of novelty.

**Value — PASS.** Finite symmetric inverse monoids are canonical finite inverse semigroups; centralizers and nilpotency are both natural and substantially studied invariants. The theorem supplies an exact infinite two-parameter intersection enumeration, not just a small example, with a compact cancellation law for cycles on an overlapping source/target set. It gives every rank count, totals, and a sharp highest-rank consequence, providing a reusable enumerative specialization of the known centralizer structure.

**Scope and risks.** Only partial injections commuting with a square-zero partial injection are counted; nilpotency indices and arbitrary centralizer types are not resolved. Primary papers' full relevant sections were inspected, but not all historical literature has been recovered. No independent audit or historical priority certification was performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
