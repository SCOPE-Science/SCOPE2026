For a square-zero partial injection of rank \(r\) on \(2r+s\) points, the complete rank distribution of nilpotent elements in its symmetric-inverse-monoid centralizer is an explicit two-parameter sum of rook polynomials involving the cancellation \(P_m(t)-s t P_{m-1}(t)\). In particular, when \(r\ge1\), exactly \(r!s!\) commuting nilpotent elements have the highest possible rank \(2r+s-1\), matching the number of full-rank centralizer elements.
