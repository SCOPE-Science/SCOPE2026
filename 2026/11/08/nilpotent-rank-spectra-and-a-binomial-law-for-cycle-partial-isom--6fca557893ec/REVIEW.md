# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof reduces nilpotency of each permutation restriction to the absence of whole permutation cycles, uses the exact dihedral permutation cycle types, and corrects every nonunique extension. The only duplicated nilpotent maps have ranks zero, one, and (for even cycles) two. A uniform random rotation and vertex subset yield a binomial rank; an order-divisor cutoff makes the nonnilpotent rotations negligible, and reflection restrictions are exponentially fewer. Exact literal partial-map enumeration for every size from three through eleven checks the full rank coefficient arrays and total monoid sizes. Computation is corroboration, not the general proof.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence.** Fernandes–Paulista establishes the dihedral-restriction characterization, monoid cardinality, ranks, and presentations, but the inspected full text does not contain a nilpotent enumeration. Dimitrova–Fernandes–Koppitz–Quinteiro examines three related submonoids and their presentations/cardinalities, not the nilpotent rank polynomial. Multiple semantic searches of published mathematical results and web literature gave no exact statement or a dominating result. Residual risk: a general restriction-orbit/cycle-index theorem may imply the polynomial, or an unlocated paper may study nilpotent cycle isometries. Thus absence of coverage in inspected evidence is not proof of global priority.

**Value — PASS.** Nilpotency, domain rank, and their joint enumeration are intrinsic semigroup invariants, and the nontrivial duplicate-extension corrections distinguish this monoid from a naive group-action count. The polynomial resolves all finite ranks, while the half-density and total-variation binomial limit describe the dominant nilpotent behavior uniformly through an infinite family. These properties are not implied by the known total cardinality alone.

**Closest prior work and limitations.** The structural characterization and total cardinality are explicitly credited to Fernandes–Paulista (arXiv:2205.02196). The related submonoids work is arXiv:2301.01519. The claimed novelty is restricted to the nilpotent strata and their asymptotics. The bound on total variation may not be sharp; this work does not treat arbitrary graphs or nilpotency-index distributions.
