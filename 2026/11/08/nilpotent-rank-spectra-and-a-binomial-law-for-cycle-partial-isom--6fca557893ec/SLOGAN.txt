For the inverse monoid of partial isometries of an \(n\)-cycle, an explicit divisor-sum polynomial counts every nilpotent by domain rank after correcting the rare duplicate dihedral extensions. Exactly half the monoid is asymptotically nilpotent, and the rank of a uniform nilpotent differs from \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,1/2)\) by total variation \(O((\log n)^2/n)\).
