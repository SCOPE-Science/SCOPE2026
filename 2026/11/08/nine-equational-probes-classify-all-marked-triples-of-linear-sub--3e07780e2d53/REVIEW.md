The scope is the marked, bounded sublattice generated by a triple, not the ambient lattice. The classical decomposition into eight one-dimensional membership patterns plus the two-dimensional three-line pattern is valid over every field. The two displayed sides of each Boolean probe agree on all blocks except its designated one-dimensional block; the distributivity probe agrees on all Boolean blocks and fails on the two-dimensional block. Componentwise interpretation of **all nested terms** proves completeness. Direct sums realize each of the 512 independent support patterns. The fixed-dimensional formula accounts for the crucial parity exception when only the two-dimensional block is supported, and treats the zero-dimensional all-true pattern separately. There are 512 distinguished types, so nine binary tests are necessary and the explicit nine are sufficient. The standalone programs' actual outputs corroborate the finite checks.

The exact 512-type classification and per-dimension formula give a usable, natural invariant of marked subspace configurations and identify the exceptional parity obstruction. This is a structural classification, not a small benchmark gain or an arbitrary parameter slice.
