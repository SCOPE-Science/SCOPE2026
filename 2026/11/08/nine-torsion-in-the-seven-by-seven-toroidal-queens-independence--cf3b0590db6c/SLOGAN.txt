The independence complex of the seven-by-seven toroidal queens graph has \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{120}\), with all other reduced integral homology zero. Exact integral boundary reduction exposes a single Smith invariant nine, which field-only computations would not distinguish from smaller three-primary torsion.
