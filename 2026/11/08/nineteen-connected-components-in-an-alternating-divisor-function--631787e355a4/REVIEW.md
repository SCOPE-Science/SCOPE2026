## Originality

**PASS with residual historical-priority risk.** Defant's complete accessible 2015 paper introduces the same alternating function and asks for the connected-component count, but proves only the criterion for a single interval. The closest inspected public exact claim gives two intervals at \(r=2\), and explicitly excludes other parameters. Neither it nor Defant's threshold implies the nineteen components for \(r=3\). Sanna's and Zubrilina's closely related results concern the ordinary divisor function \(\sigma_{-r}\), whose local factors have no alternating signs, and do not state the present endpoint table. Search of the mathematical research index and primary literature did not identify any r=3 exact nineteen-interval theorem. An unindexed equivalent prior calculation is possible; searches alone do not establish global novelty.
