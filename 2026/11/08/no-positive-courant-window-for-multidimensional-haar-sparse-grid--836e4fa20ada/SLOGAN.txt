For the unmodified periodic piecewise-constant Haar sparse-grid upwind transport scheme, every dimension \(d\ge2\) and sparse level \(N\ge2\) has **no positive forward-Euler time step** that preserves all nonnegative fine-cell values, even inside its sharp \(L^2\)-stable CFL range. By contrast, level \(N=1\) preserves nonnegativity exactly when each directional Courant number is at most one, giving a complete sharp boundary between order preservation and its failure.
