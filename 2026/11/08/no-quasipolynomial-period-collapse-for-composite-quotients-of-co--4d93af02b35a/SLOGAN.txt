For every composite modulus \(p\ge4\), the Frobenius number of \(\langle a,a+1\rangle/p\), as \(a>p\) varies, has **least quasipolynomial period exactly \(p\)**, sharpening a known period-divides-\(p\) formula. The key is that its residue-zero branch never coincides with the branch at any proper divisor of \(p\).
