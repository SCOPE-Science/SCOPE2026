# Self-review

## Correctness

**PASS.** For every graph state, the squared edge increment and two-star increment follow by exact uniform-parent averaging. The resulting closed moment recurrences have explicit polynomial solutions verified by substitution. A bounded centered edge-count martingale yields almost-sure and \(L^2\) convergence of the normalized density, and the exact second moment yields the positive limiting variance. A standalone exact-rational enumerator corroborates all conditional identities and finite recurrences (1,940 assertions), but is not substituted for the martingale argument.

## Originality

**PASS, provisional historical priority.** The 2025 motivating random-cograph study has a distinct uniform-cograph law, and the closely related 2026 source paper restricts neighbor-copying probability to \(0<p<1\) and studies degrees and rooted local neighborhoods; its parameter and output do not cover the \(p=1\) edge-density limit with a parent-link coin. Hermann–Pfaffelhuber's full copying does not include the independent parent link. Uniform random cograph limits are for different laws: the published Brownian co-graphon variance at equal bias is \(1/30\), as opposed to \(1/18\) here. Semantic records and searches for identical and equivalent statements revealed no direct covering theorem. A general earlier twin-copying result not indexed by these sources remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The extreme inheritance parameter changes the model's asymptotic behavior from vanishing edge density to a macroscopic non-self-averaging density. The exact variance quantifies the persistent shared ancestral randomness and distinguishes the sequential law from uniform random cographs. This is a natural endpoint characterization, not a routine restatement of martingale convergence.
