For Haar-random fixed-distance sections of a regular simplex, all fixed face counts undergo a sharp fluctuation transition at centroid distance \(t\asymp n^{-1}\): the common critical limit is noncentral \(\chi^2_1\), while larger shrinking offsets produce a common Gaussian limit. The full intermediate and \(t=c/\sqrt n\) laws have explicit coefficients and a variance correction forced by Gaussian studentization.
