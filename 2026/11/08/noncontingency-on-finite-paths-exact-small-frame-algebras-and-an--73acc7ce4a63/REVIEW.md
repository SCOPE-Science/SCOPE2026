## Correctness
The modal-type recursion follows from structural induction and definability of each union of finitely many depth-bounded types. On a degree-two path vertex, truth of every noncontingency test is determined by the unordered pair of successor types, and singleton tests recover that pair when distinct. Exact full-state enumeration yields the finite profiles; a different singleton-test implementation checks them and a separate truth-vector implementation checks all \(64\) pointed states on the four-world path. The infinite strict-depth result is proved by radius locality and the unique length-\(d\) path contribution in the mod-two adjacency matrix. All four checkers execute with the stated outputs. Risks: finite counts depend on correct frame convention and exact enumeration, both expressly specified and independently cross-checked.

## Mathematical value
The quantified language has an endpoint-blind nonnormal modality, so an exact finite-path expressive-power profile is not a generic normal-modal depth calculation. The result identifies the smallest path exhibiting a genuinely depth-three distinction, determines its full finite formula algebra, and, in sharp contrast, proves an unbounded hierarchy over the simplest connected degree-two frame family. The refinement gives a reusable exact equivalence procedure; the infinite family has an explicit linear-size witness rather than a nonconstructive separation. The finite census is motivated by the structural expressivity boundary, not by an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Scope and limits
The theorem concerns one atom, Boolean truth, undirected irreflexive paths, and the sole noncontingency modality. A uniform closed formula for general path type counts and optimal world-count lower bounds for arbitrary-depth separations are not claimed. The numerical table has exact exhaustive certificates; the all-depth hierarchy has a separate proof.
