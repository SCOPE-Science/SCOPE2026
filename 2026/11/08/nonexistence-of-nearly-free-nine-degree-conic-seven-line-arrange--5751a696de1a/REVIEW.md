# Proof correction and scope

The previous argument omitted the line-only double points on the conic. Each of the \(v\) transverse conic–line–line triples remains a double point after the conic is removed. Consequently Hirzebruch's inequality bounds \(u\le6\), not \(9u+4v\le56\). The numerical contradiction in that version is invalid. The corrected proof uses geometry of the secants and tangents to exclude each of the surviving profiles, without enlarging the claim beyond one smooth conic and seven distinct lines with the three stated singularity types.
