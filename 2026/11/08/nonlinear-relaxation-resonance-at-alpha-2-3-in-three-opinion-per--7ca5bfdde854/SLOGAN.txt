In the three-opinion iterative persuasion–polarization model, every nonconsensus initial state reaches a unique interior coexistence distribution for each \(0<\alpha<1\). A sharp nonlinear resonance at \(\alpha=2/3\) changes the relaxation of the moderate-opinion share from an imbalance-squared exponential to \(t e^{-(1-\alpha)t}\), then to the ordinary exponential mode.
