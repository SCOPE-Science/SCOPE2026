For two uniform-attachment trees whose parent selections are copied with probability \(\alpha\) and otherwise made independently, the limiting covariance of their centered average root-path lengths is exactly \(\sum_{j\ge2}\alpha/[j^2(j-\alpha)]\). Its strictly convex dependence on \(\alpha\) makes the macroscopic path-length correlation strictly smaller than the microscopic copying parameter for every \(0<\alpha<1\).
