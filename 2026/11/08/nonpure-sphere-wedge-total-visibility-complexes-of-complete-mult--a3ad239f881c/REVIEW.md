**Correctness — PASS.** In complete multipartite graphs all nonadjacent pairs occur inside a non-singleton part and have the complement of that part as their common neighborhood. The necessity-and-sufficiency test follows immediately, and the inclusion-exclusion polynomial has only one multi-family intersection. Facets are minimal complements: singleton vertices or cross-class pairs. Without singleton classes the complex is the independence complex of the dual of a rank-two partition matroid; matroid shellability and the reduced Euler characteristic give the claimed integral spheres. With singleton classes the exact union of two cones suspends the base complex once per singleton. Boundary cases with zero or one non-singleton part are cones or simplices. The proof establishes every quantifier and boundary used in the statement; the standalone exhaustive verifier supplies separate finite corroboration.
