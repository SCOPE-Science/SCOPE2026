For every connected complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, the full total mutual-visibility complex has an explicit all-face and facet polynomial, and is either contractible or an integral wedge of \(a-1\) spheres of dimension \(N-3\), where \(a\) is the number of non-singleton classes. Adding a singleton class destroys matroidality and purity exactly when at least two non-singleton classes remain, but preserves the sphere-wedge multiplicity through suspension.
