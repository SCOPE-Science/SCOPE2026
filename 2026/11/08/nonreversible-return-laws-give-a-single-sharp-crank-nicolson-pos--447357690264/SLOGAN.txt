For every finite continuous-time Markov chain, including nonreversible and reducible ones, Crank–Nicolson's end-step is entrywise nonnegative on exactly one interval of stepsizes: a first-return generating function forces each nonabsorbing resolvent diagonal to decrease strictly. All-step positivity holds exactly for disjoint unions of absorbing singleton states and balanced two-state chains; the directed three-cycle loses positivity at the sharp step \(1+\sqrt5\).
