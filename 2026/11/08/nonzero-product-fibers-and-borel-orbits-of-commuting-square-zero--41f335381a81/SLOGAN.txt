For the first nonzero-product case of ordered commuting square-zero strictly triangular pairs, every prescribed nonzero product fiber is \(\operatorname{GL}_2\times\mathbb A^2\), over every field. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the full nonzero-product locus splits into exactly \(q+1\) simultaneous upper-triangular conjugacy orbits of equal size \(q^3(q-1)^3\), indexed by a projective line of ideals in \(k[s,t]/(s^2,t^2)\).
