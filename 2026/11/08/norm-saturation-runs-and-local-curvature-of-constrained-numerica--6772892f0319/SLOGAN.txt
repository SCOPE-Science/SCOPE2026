For every nonzero finite nilpotent weighted shift \(T\), let \(M\) be its largest weight modulus and \(L\) the longest consecutive run of weights of modulus \(M\). Its constrained numerical radius satisfies \(w_q(T)=M\) exactly for \(0\le q\le\cos(\pi/(L+1))\). If the largest weight is unique, the sharp quadratic defect at \(q=0\) is \(w_q(T)=M-[(M^2-\ell^2)(M^2-r^2)/(2M(2M^2-\ell^2-r^2))]q^2+o(q^2)\), where \(\ell,r\) are precisely the neighboring weight moduli (zero beyond the endpoints).
