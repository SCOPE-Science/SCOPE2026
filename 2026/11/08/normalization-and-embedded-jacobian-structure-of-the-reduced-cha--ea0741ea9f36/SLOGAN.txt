The reduced discriminant of characteristic-two monic quintics has the exact affine equation \(U^2+bUV+dV^2=0\): its singular locus is a smooth threefold but its Jacobian scheme has an embedded stratum. Its normalization is affine four-space with conductor \((U,V)\), and the normalization's degree-two conductor cover changes from separable to inseparable on \(b=0\).
