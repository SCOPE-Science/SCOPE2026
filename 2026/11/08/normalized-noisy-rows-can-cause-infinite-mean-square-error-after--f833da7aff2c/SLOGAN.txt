Averaging independently observed, unit-norm coefficient rows before a Kaczmarz projection can increase first-step mean-square risk from the finite value \(a^{-2}-1\) with one observation to infinity with every finite batch of size at least two, even with a well-conditioned positive-definite true system and arbitrarily small noise variance. The same first step converges almost surely as the batch grows, while a standard ridge denominator restores an \(O(N^{-1})\) mean-square bound on this example.
