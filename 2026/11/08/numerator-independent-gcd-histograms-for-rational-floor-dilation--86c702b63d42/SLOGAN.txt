For every reduced positive rational slope \(p/q\), the full histogram of \(\gcd(n,\lfloor pn/q\rfloor)\) on each consecutive block of length \(q\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,q-1)\) is exactly independent of \(p\), with a closed formula for every gcd value. The limiting distribution has mass \((q-1)/q\), with the missing \(1/q\) escaping along multiples of \(q\), and approaches the classical \(1/(\zeta(2)d^2)\) law at each fixed value \(d\) as \(q\to\infty\).
