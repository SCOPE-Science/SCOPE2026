For a Poisson-count sampler built from a one-periodic arrival intensity and a one-unit sliding window, constant intensity uniquely minimizes the physical-time asymptotic variance of every nonlinear square-integrable observable, while all affine observables are modulation-invariant. The second factorial moment has an exact extra variance \(\frac{2\lambda^2}{\pi^2}\sum_{k\ne0}|\widehat\eta_k|^2/k^2\), quantifying the penalty for oscillatory sampling.
