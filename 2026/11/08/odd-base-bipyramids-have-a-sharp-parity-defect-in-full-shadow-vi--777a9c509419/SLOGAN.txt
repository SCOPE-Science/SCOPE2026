For a right bipyramid over an odd regular \(n\)-gon, the chance that a uniformly random planar shadow retains all \(n+2\) vertices has an exact four-arcsine expression. Its optimal probability is asymptotic to \(\pi^2/(3\sqrt3\,n^2)\), exactly half the even-base leading coefficient, and every optimal apex height tends to \(1/\sqrt2\) times the base circumradius.
