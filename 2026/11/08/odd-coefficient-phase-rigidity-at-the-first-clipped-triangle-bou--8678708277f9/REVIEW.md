The claim applies for all integers \(N\ge3\) and to competitors supported in the same known clipped triangle. Two uniquely realized autocorrelation displacements force the four Newton vertices. Laurent polynomial factor-reversal and the Minkowski geometry of the one-pair-parallel trapezoid restrict a nontrivial mate to reversal of a factor depending on the vertical variable alone. The top two adjacent columns have lengths two and three with exclusively odd coefficients; a shared vertical root would require an odd integer to vanish. This proves uniqueness for all \(N\), without relying on finite search. The boundary pair is explicitly certified by \(|1+2w|=|2+w|\).
