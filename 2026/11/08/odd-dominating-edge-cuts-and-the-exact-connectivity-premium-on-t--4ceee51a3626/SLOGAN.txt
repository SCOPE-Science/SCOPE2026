For the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), every odd-dominating set is a union of the internal edges of two complementary vertex groups, with one group of even size when \(n\) is odd; for even \(n\), only the full vertex set works. This gives the complete weight enumerator and shows that for odd \(n\ge5\), demanding induced connectivity raises the optimum by exactly \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor-(n-1)\), with precisely \(n+1\) connected solutions.
