For every odd generalized Petersen graph \(GP(n,2)\), the uniform zero-field antiferromagnetic Ising model has exact minimum energy \(-(2n-1)J\) and \(2nF_{(n-1)/2}\) ground states when \(n\equiv1\pmod4\), or \(2nF_{(n+1)/2}\) when \(n\equiv3\pmod4\). This gives a sharp odd-rim frustration count and residual entropy \(\frac14\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)\) per spin.
