# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The fixed definition uses three complete supporting lines, ties fixed, h>=2, and excludes fixed/even cycles. Any odd periodic line itinerary contains a leg-to-leg step; perpendicular projection sends it to O, hence at most one nonfixed odd cycle. Iterates of O alternate between L2 and L1 with hypotenuse coordinate b^j until first b^k<=d; compare b^k to c to distinguish hypotenuse tie, vertical-leg tie, or strict return after 2k+1 steps. If b^k<c, b^k>bd>1/2 forces the final horizontal-to-vertical return. Strict branch choices and the constant perpendicular last projection give finite-time local capture. Derivative inequalities prove rho,sigma strictly increasing, rho<sigma<rho+1 and disjoint interlaced integer crossings, with rho=h-1/2+O(1/h). My independent Fraction-based implementation of actual farther-line projection gives least periods 5,7,11,21 for h=2,3,5,10, independently of the source CHECK.py. Such four finite cases are not a proof of the all-real result.

PASS

Best-of-knowledge distinct from Everett’s qualitative periodicity and from earlier arbitrary-triangle period-three or isosceles-only cycle theorems: the exact unique high-aspect right-triangle *all odd least periods* and the two parameter sequences are not implied by the inspected work.
