For right triangles with leg ratio \(h\ge2\), every nonconstant odd cycle of Everett's farthest-projection map passes through the right-angle vertex, so there is at most one; away from two interlaced sequences of tie parameters, its exact period is \(2\lfloor\log(q/(q+1))/\log(h^2/(1+h^2))\rfloor+3\) and it captures a neighborhood in finite time. Every odd period at least five occurs, with sharp isolated disappearance parameters and \(P(h)=2h+O(1)\).
