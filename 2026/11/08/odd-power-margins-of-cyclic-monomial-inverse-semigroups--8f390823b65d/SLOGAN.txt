For every odd exponent \(n\ge3\), two cyclic-phase partial monomial matrices are marginally equivalent for \(x^n\) exactly when they differ by one global \(n\)-th root of unity; the marginal relation is already a congruence, with an explicit margin and exact quotient count. At exponent four, nonuniform diagonal phase changes can instead be marginally invisible, showing a sharp parity obstruction to this scalar-orbit classification.
