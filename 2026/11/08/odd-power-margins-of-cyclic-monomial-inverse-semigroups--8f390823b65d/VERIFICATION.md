# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For odd power n=2k+1, rank-one test X=E_{ji} makes (AX)^n a nonzero diagonal projector precisely for an occupied arrow of A; equality of power signatures forces A and B to have identical partial-permutation supports. On two occupied arrows the multiplier producing a 2-cycle gives ratios delta_u^(k+1)delta_v^k and delta_v^(k+1)delta_u^k; dividing implies delta_u=delta_v. Conversely a global n-th root scalar vanishes from every nth power and is compatible with products and inverses. Marginal centralization of rank-one idempotents forces diagonal matrices; equivalence to support idempotent is exactly zeta P_I, a normal inverse subsemigroup. Free scalar orbits give count 1+sum_{r=1}^m C(m,r)^2*r!*n^(r-1), margin 1+n(2^m-1). A complete rank-based enumeration independently produced 31 matrices/11 quotient classes at (m,n)=(2,3) and 71/15 at (2,5). The n=4,m=2 diagonal counterexample is covered by explicit full- and lower-rank multipliers. Infinite-parameter claim relies on the proof, not enumeration.

## originality

PASS

The 2025 primary inverse-semigroup paper explicitly asks in Problem 11 when power-word marginal equivalence is a congruence; its centralization lemmas are acknowledged. Brookes’s partial-wreath-product framework classifies possible congruences, not which are induced by this power-word equality. This result selects precisely scalar/projective classes for the nontrivial all-m odd cyclic-phase family, derives exact margin/quotient and demonstrates an even obstruction. Historical projective rook-monoid presentations may still predate it.
