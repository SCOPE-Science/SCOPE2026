For every positive integer \(m\), the norm-two Pell equation \(X^2-m^{2r+1}Z^2=2\) is solvable for any \(r\ge1\) exactly when \(m\) is odd and \(x^2-my^2=2\) is solvable; a precise prime-adic lifting law constructs solutions at every odd-power coefficient. The first two squarefree multipliers are \(7\) and \(23\), giving explicit infinite families of three-term powerful-number progressions without asserting that their elements are consecutive powerful numbers.
