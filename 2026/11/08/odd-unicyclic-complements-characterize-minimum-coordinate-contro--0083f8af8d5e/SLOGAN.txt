On the two-subset Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\), exactly \(\binom n2-n\) independently actuated vertices suffice for full Laplacian control: their unactuated complement must form an odd-unicyclic pseudoforest on \(n\) labels, apart from the exceptional pair of disjoint triangles when \(n=6\). The resulting exact enumeration gives \(162\) optimum placements for the Petersen graph and \(2520\) for \(KG(6,2)\).
