For affine composition on the square-summable Dirichlet-series Hardy space, every positive-translation symbol \(s\mapsto ms+b\) with integer \(m\ge2\) has an exact disk-and-point numerical range, and the support of its entire closed convexified Davis–Wielandt shell is determined by one compact Jacobi matrix built from the chain starting at \(2\). A negative-parameter Schur-complement comparison proves that no other multiplicative chain can enlarge any supporting face, with super-exponentially accurate finite truncations; the nonconvex shell itself is not classified.
