# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The reflection decomposition reduces the odd sector to an explicitly normalized irreducible Jacobi matrix with diagonal entries \((4,2,\ldots,2,3)\). Strict rank-one eigenvalue interlacing separates odd and even modes. For even modes, the parity argument excludes nodal zeros at integer vertices. For odd modes, a hypothetical interior zero gives a common eigenvalue of two disjoint Jacobi blocks; their Chebyshev recurrences and an odd-order cyclotomic divisibility contradiction exclude it. PBH then gives the exact all-order and all-position classification. An extracted standalone exact-modular Krylov check passed 320 positions in odd sizes \(5\) through \(35\). Finite checks are supporting, not the all-order proof.

## Originality: PASS on inspected evidence

The 2014 primary source treats qualitative-matrix zero forcing and ordinary cycles, while the present result concerns the exact fixed Laplacian of a chorded cycle, with successful controls that are not zero forcing. A 2019 path-interconnection paper treats a different graph construction. Searches of indexed research claims and accessible historical statements found no equivalent single-input chorded-cycle theorem, no stronger result that implies it, and no table answering the all-order placement question. An unindexed or inaccessible equivalent mathematical theorem remains possible; publication-priority confirmation would require broader expert literature scrutiny.

## Value: PASS

This is a natural minimal graph modification of a cycle where an ordinary unweighted cycle needs two dedicated input vertices. The complete sharp classification pinpoints the only uncontrollable singleton after one chord is added, using a nontrivial zero-free Jacobi-eigenvector lemma rather than an arbitrary finite census. It also gives a concrete distinction between fixed-Laplacian controllability and qualitative zero-forcing sufficient conditions.

## Scientific limitations

Only odd orders \(n\ge5\), precisely one distance-two chord of weight one, and the ordinary fixed Laplacian are covered. No general claim is made for perturbed weights, other chords, even order, or quantitative control cost. Independent review and exhaustive publication-priority confirmation have not occurred.
