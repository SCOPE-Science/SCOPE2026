In an alternating free-diffusion/harmonic-trap process, the full stationary position distribution observed during release determines either unknown residence-time law when the other clock and the required calibration data are known: explicit triangular formulas recover all Laplace or power moments. Yet for every finite number of even position moments, two distinct strictly positive finite-support confinement clocks with the same mean can be observationally indistinguishable, and exactly \(K+3\) distinct possible confinement durations are necessary and sufficient to hide \(K\) measured even moments.
