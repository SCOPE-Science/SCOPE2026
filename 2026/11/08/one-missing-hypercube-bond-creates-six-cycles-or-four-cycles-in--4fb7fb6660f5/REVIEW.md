# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The proof uses the exact cube identity \(A^2=dI\) in characteristic two and separately analyzes the even and odd bipartite adjacency blocks. In even dimensions, an explicit four-dimensional invariant component has squared cyclotomic minimal polynomial and exact order six; the complement has only size-two zero Jordan blocks. In odd dimensions, rank-one block perturbation, the characteristic-two square structure, and an explicit nonzero third-power witness force one size-four unipotent block, size-two unipotent blocks, and two simple zero blocks. The Fitting direct sum makes the exact joint entry-time/primitive-period counts multiplicative. Both bundled exact-arithmetic checkers passed; small-state enumeration is not substituted for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The classical 1984 algebraic cellular-automata framework treats regular lattices, while the 2017 full-text study of deleted edges of hypercubes optimizes a *real* spectral radius after deleting two, three or four edges. Neither gives the specific characteristic-two dynamical Jordan partition for a single deleted edge. The published distance-two cube parity census and an earlier classification of the cube with an appended pendant vertex concern different operators; neither stronger theorem subsumes the statement here on its described domain. A genuinely equivalent unindexed specialization remains possible and cannot be ruled out by searches alone.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Deleting a single existing graph edge is the elementary bond-fault perturbation for a highly symmetric finite network. The exact defect transforms all-extinguishing dynamics into primitive six-cycles in even cube dimensions and doubles the maximum primitive period in odd cube dimensions. This infinite-family boundary sensitivity, plus an exact distribution for *every* initial state, makes the invariant natural and the result substantially stronger than isolated nullity or small-size calculations.
