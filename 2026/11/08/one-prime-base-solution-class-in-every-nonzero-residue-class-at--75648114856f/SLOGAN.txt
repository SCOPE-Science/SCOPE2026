For each prime-power modulus \(p^a\), the prime-base binomial congruence \(\binom{qp^a}{p^a}\equiv q^{p^a}\pmod{p^a}\) has exactly one admissible base class above each residue modulo \(p\). Its prime solutions therefore have relative natural density \(1/p^{a-1}\) among primes, including the cases \(p=2,3\).
