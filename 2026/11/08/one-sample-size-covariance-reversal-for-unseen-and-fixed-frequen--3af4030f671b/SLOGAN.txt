For symmetric Pólya sampling with \(m\ge3\) categories, the covariance between the number of unseen categories and the number observed exactly \(r\ge2\) times changes from positive to negative at one uniquely determined sample size. For the uniform weak-composition law its exact real crossing is \((m(r-1)+2)/2\), while the independent-multinomial limit crosses at roughly twice that scale when \(m\) is large.
