For divisors \(\{1,p,q,pq\}\) of any semiprime with distinct primes \(p,q\ge5\), a single coprime assignment across \(k\) independently chosen primitive four-term arithmetic progressions has an exact all-order density \(\mathcal D_{p,q}(k)=U_p(k)U_q(k)-12W_p(k)W_q(k)\), where the factors are explicit exponential polynomials. In particular, the two-progression failure probability is \(48/((p+1)^2(q+1)^2)\), and the success density decays with sharp constant \(24\) as \(k\to\infty\) for fixed primes.
