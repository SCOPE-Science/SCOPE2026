For a vacuum-initialized, infinitely frequently monitored noisy oscillator, the Laplace transform of the mean-normalized energy-threshold first-passage time decreases strictly as the integer threshold grows, always lying above its squared-Bessel diffusion limit. The positive discrepancy has an explicit nonasymptotic bound and a sharp uniform \(B^{-1}\) correction \(sI_2(2\sqrt{s})/[2B I_0(2\sqrt{s})^2]\), with \(B\) the threshold and \(s\) the Laplace argument.
