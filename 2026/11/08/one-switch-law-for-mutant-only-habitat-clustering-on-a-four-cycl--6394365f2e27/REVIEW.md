Its proper/non-proper cycle plots concern shared background variation between types and so do not yield the present mutant-only sharp threshold. The 2015 Markov-chain computation source supplies general tools, not the comparison. Semantic literature and research-record searches yielded no implication-covering result. These searches are not proof of novelty; older or incompletely indexed material remains a risk.

## Value (PASS)
Four vertices form the minimal even cycle with two favorable and two unfavorable sites and two distinct site-arrangement classes. The exact phase boundary is therefore a natural complete finite classification of an ecological-genetic question, not a cherry-picked subset. Under otherwise identical resource multiset and dispersal graph, optimal structure first favors clustering and eventually favors alternation, a distinction not inferable from counts, mean fitness, or the general prior result about symmetrical background heterogeneity. The precise threshold can serve as a test case for more general environmental evolutionary graph theory. Applicability is confined to mutant-only fitness, fixed habitats, birth–death updating and a uniformly chosen source.
