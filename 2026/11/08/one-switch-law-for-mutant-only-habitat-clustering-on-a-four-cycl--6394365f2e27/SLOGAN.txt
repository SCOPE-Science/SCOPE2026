For a mutant-only two-quality Moran process on the four-cycle, clustered and alternating high-fitness habitats exchange fixation-probability optimality at one exact algebraic threshold when the lower fecundity is below \(3.22069281998732\). At or above that lower-fecundity threshold, alternating habitats win for every nonzero contrast; the result exhausts all four-site arrangements, not larger networks.
