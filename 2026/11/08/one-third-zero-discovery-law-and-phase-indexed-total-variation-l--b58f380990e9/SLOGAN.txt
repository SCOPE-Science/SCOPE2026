For iid categories of probabilities \(2^{-j}\), the number newly seen between draw \(n\) and draw \(2n\) has a phase-indexed, non-Poisson Bernoulli-sum limit in total variation, with a uniform \(O(n^{-1/3})\) error. The probability of *no* newly seen category converges to exactly \(1/3\), independent of dyadic phase, even though the expected new count tends to one.
