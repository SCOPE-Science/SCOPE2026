For every \(m\ge1\), the minimum paired-dominating sets of the triangular graph \(T_{3m+1}=L(K_{3m+1})\) form a connected, vertex-transitive, \(4m\)-regular graph under single-vertex token slides, with \((3m+1)!/(2^m m!)\) configurations, exactly \(2m(3m+1)!/(2^m m!)\) edges and exactly \(2m(3m+1)!/(3\cdot2^m m!)\) triangles. Its connectivity is realized by swaps on a bouquet of four-cycles, using only one selected vertex replacement per move rather than a whole matched-pair replacement.
