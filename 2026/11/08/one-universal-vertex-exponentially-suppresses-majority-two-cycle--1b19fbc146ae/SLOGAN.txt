For synchronous binary majority, adding a single universal vertex to the \(2n\)-vertex cocktail-party graph cuts the probability of an immediate two-cycle by an asymptotic factor \(\sqrt2\,2^{-n}\). Exact labelled primitive-two populations are \(\binom{2n}{n}-2^n\) before the addition and \(2\binom{n}{\lceil n/2\rceil}\) afterward; every state becomes periodic in at most two updates.
