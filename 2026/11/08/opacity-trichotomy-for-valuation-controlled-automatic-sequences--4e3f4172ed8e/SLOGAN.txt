For every ultimately periodic controller \(f\), the valuation word \(f(\nu_b(n+1))\) has exactly \(a+p\) intrinsic automaton states, where \(a\) and \(p\) are its least preperiod and eventual period. Its normalized prefix-metric opacity is always \(1\) in bases \(b\ge3\), but in binary is \(0\) for a genuine preperiod and \(2^{1-p}\) for a purely periodic controller.
