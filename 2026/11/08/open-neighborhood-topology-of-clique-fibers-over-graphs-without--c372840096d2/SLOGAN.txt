For every connected graph \(H\) without triangles or four-cycles and every \(m\ge4\), the open-neighborhood complex of \(H\mathbin\square K_m\) is homotopy equivalent to \(H\vee\bigvee^{|V(H)|}S^{m-2}\vee\bigvee^{|E(H)|(m^2-3m+1)}S^2\). The formula extends the known cycle-base case to arbitrary branching and multiple-cycle bases through explicit free-face collapses and integral CW cancellations.
