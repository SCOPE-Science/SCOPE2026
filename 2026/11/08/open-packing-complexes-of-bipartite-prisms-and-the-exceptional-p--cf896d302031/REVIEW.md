# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The explicit parity relabeling gives \(\mathcal N(G\square K_2)\cong G^2\sqcup G^2\) by checking all possible common-neighbor pairs, without connectedness assumptions. Independent sets and maximal independent sets factor uniquely across the two copies. On \(P_n\), index shifting proves the all-set binomial formula and radius-two coverage proves the maximal-gap generating function. The gap parameter intervals prove the entire facet spectrum for all \(n\ge1\), including the isolated purity exception \(n=6\). The standalone code checks 427 bipartite labelled graphs of at most five vertices and paths through twelve vertices.

## Originality — PASS, provisional

Primary comparison: Brešar–Klavžar–Rall (arXiv:2309.04963v1) proved \(\rho^o(G\square K_2)=2\rho_2(G)\), a maximum-cardinality identity, not all-set or all-maximal-set factorization. The independent-set/common-neighbor equivalence and distance-two coloring definitions in Brešar–Samadi–Yero (arXiv:2106.09823) and Samadi–Soltankhah–Yero (arXiv:2306.01488) are acknowledged prior knowledge. The broader graph-product work of Mojdeh et al. (arXiv:1901.06813) provides scalar bounds. Targeted research searches and accessible previously published mathematical claims found no theorem encompassing the full bipartite-prism complex and ladder facet spectrum. The 2015 Song–Yue Cartesian injective-coloring paper was not accessible beyond metadata and abstract, so overlap with the non-focal coloring consequence remains a specific risk. The absence of search hits is not itself proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

The claim upgrades an extremal identity to an exact simplicial-join isomorphism, two coefficientwise counting identities, and the complete irredundant cardinality spectrum for ladders. The exceptional purity of the six-rung ladder after nonpure smaller cases reflects genuinely nonmonotone structural behavior invisible to the maximum packing number. The classification applies to arbitrary finite bipartite graphs, including disconnected and isolated-vertex cases.

## Limitations

This proof does not cover nonbipartite base graphs; an odd-cycle prism fails the decomposition. The status is a self-review, not external validation, and priority remains provisional where inaccessible or unindexed literature may have stronger coverage.
