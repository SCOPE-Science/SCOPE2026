The full open-packing complex of every bipartite prism \(G\square K_2\) is the join of two independence complexes of \(G^2\), so both its all-set and inclusion-maximal cardinality polynomials are exact squares. For the \(n\)-rung ladder this gives closed enumerators and every maximal-face size, with the exceptional purity pattern \(n\in\{1,2,3,6\}\).
