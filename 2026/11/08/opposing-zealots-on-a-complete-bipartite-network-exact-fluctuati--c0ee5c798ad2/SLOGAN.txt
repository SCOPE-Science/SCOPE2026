With a permanently opinion-1 zealot on one side and a permanently opinion-0 zealot on the other side of \(K_{a,b}\), random neighbor-copying has exact stationary opinion-1 variance \((a-1)(b-1)/[(a+b-1)(3a+3b-4)]\). As the two side sizes grow in proportions \(\alpha\) and \(1-\alpha\), the stationary opinion-1 fraction converges to \(\operatorname{Beta}(2(1-\alpha),2\alpha)\), retaining macroscopic fluctuations despite an increasing population.
