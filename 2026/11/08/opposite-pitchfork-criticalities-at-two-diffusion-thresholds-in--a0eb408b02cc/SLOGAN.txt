In an explicit six-patch weak-Allee predator–prey network, two exact stationary Turing crossings have **opposite nonlinear criticality**: a locally unstable patterned pair exists just below the lower diffusion threshold, while a locally stable positive two-community patterned pair exists just below the upper threshold. Exact rational cubic coefficients establish the distinction, and the stable patterned branch has smaller mean prey and predator densities than the homogeneous state.
