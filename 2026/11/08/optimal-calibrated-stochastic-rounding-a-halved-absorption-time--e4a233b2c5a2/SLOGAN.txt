A fixed total-variation budget \(B\) for descent-biased stochastic rounding on the integer-grid scalar quadratic is optimally spent by equalizing the small-state downward transition probabilities at a water-filling threshold. For fixed positive \(B\), this halves the leading \(M\log M\) expected absorption time and replaces the unbiased coupon collector's order-\(M\) Gumbel fluctuations with a Gaussian limit at order \(M^{3/4}\).
