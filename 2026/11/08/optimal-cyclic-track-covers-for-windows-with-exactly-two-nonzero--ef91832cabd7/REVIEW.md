# Mathematical review of the optimal exact-two-nonzero cyclic cover theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim specifies full-length, exact-weight-two windows, labeled symbols, rotation equivalence, and the minimum number of circular tracks. The proof derives the invariant shorter cyclic support gap, reduces each gap component by suppressing forced paths to either the bidirected complete bipartite graph or the complete directed graph with loops, then obtains the exact Euler-tour multiplicities with BEST and the directed Matrix-Tree theorem. The \(s=1\) and even-antipodal cases are separately normalized. The exact Python checker reconstructed 31 original unsuppressed component graphs, tested directed integer cofactors and factorial factors, and independently enumerated fixed-edge Euler tours in small cases. These finite tests do not substitute for the infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS on currently inspected evidence, with a recorded historical-priority limit.** The 2012 Ruskey–Sawada–Williams manuscript explicitly identifies the disconnected exact-weight binary graph but solves a different problem using the shorthand representation and adjacent weight range. The March 2026 Campbell–Janik-Jones–Sawada and Gabrić–Imam–Janik-Jones–Sawada manuscripts concern bounded-weight constructions or decoding, not the multiplicity of minimal full-length fixed-weight-two track covers. A July 2026 Mitchell–Wild paper extends weight-range constructions to nonbinary alphabets; its full manuscript could not be inspected. Standard graph-counting formulas, the existence obstruction, and the support-gap concept are prior art. The claimed new implication is the complete family of optimal exact-weight-two multitrack counts with gap-dependent factors. Searches of the published semantic index and the accessible matching literature found no statement implying that exact family. Search failure alone does not establish uniqueness; independent bibliographic priority checking is still warranted.

## Value
**PASS.** The full-length fixed-weight-two language loses a one-track universal cycle at \(n=4\); hence determining the least number of tracks is the natural replacement for the existence problem. The result determines both this optimum and all optimal cyclic designs, exhibiting the antipodal/non-antipodal distinction and an exact alphabet-dependent factorization valid for every length. This is a complete natural classification rather than a chosen finite table, a renamed standard construction, or a count of merely one example. It provides all possible minimum-size circular indexes for these constrained two-active-position windows.

## Limits and sources
The strongest directly relevant sources inspected were Ruskey–Sawada–Williams (2012), DOI 10.1137/100808782; Campbell–Janik-Jones–Sawada (arXiv:2603.11954v1); and Gabrić–Imam–Janik-Jones–Sawada (arXiv:2603.11934v1). Mitchell–Wild (arXiv:2607.22350) was checked from its abstract but not fully read because the open manuscript download returned access denial. The prior exact-two-nonzero shift-register memory problem and two bounded/dense universal-cycle enumerations do not imply an enumeration of the fixed-weight-two component tours. The theorem makes no claim for higher exact weights, arbitrary symbol weights, or quotienting by reversal and alphabet permutations. Independent validation has not occurred.
