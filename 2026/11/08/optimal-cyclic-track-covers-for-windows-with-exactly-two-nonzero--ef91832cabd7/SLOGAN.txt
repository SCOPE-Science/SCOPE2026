The complete language of length-\(n\) words with exactly two nonzero positions has an optimal cover by precisely \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) circular tracks. For every \(q\ge2\) and \(n\ge3\), the number of such minimum covers is \(A_{q-1}^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}B_{q-1}^{\mathbf 1_{\{2\mid n\}}}\), with the explicit complete-bipartite and complete-digraph factors proved in the accompanying result.
