For a directionally biased run-and-tumble particle on every ring of size \(n\ge4\), the globally optimal switching rate is uniquely determined by \(t^3+t^2-t=\delta^{-2}\), and optimizes the **full Markov spectral gap**. Bias rounds the known symmetric exceptional-point optimum by a new nonanalytic \(\delta^{2/3}\) law; the symmetric zero-bias optimum itself is established prior work.
