For homogeneous finite peer epochs on a fixed reversible opinion network, exact consensus in finitely many rounds is possible precisely when every nonconstant transition eigenvalue is negative; the fewest outer rounds equal the number of distinct such eigenvalues, and longer peer horizons cannot improve that count. A missing network edge rules out exact finite-round consensus entirely, while a complete unweighted network achieves it in one odd-horizon round at a uniquely tuned susceptibility.
