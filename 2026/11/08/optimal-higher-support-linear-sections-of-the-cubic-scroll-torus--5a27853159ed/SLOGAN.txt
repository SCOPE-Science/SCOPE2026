For the cubic scroll's \(\mathbb F_q\)-rational dense torus, the projective planes containing the maximum \(q\) torus points are exactly those containing a ruling fiber and one extra point: there are \((q-1)^2(q-2)\) of them. Exactly \(q-1\) projective lines attain the maximum \(q-1\) points, and they are the dense-torus rulings; the theorem classifies and counts optimizing linear sections in every codimension.
