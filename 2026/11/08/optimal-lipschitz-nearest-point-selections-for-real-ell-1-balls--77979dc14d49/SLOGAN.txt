Among every possible choice of nearest points onto the unit ball of real \(\ell_1^n\), the least global Lipschitz constant is exactly \(2-2/n\) for \(n\ge2\); no nonlinear or asymmetric selection can do better than soft thresholding. In infinite-dimensional real \(\ell_1\), the sharp optimum is \(2\), independent of the ball radius.
