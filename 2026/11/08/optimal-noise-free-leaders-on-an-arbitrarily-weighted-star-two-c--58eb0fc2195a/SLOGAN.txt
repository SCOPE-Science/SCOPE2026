On any positively weighted star, the exact optimal noise-free leader set for each budget is one of just two sets: the \(k\) weakest-conductance leaves, or the center plus the \(k-1\) weakest-conductance leaves; the center is excluded precisely when \(\sum_{i=1}^k w_i>(m-k+1)w_k\), with both patterns optimal at equality. This yields all-budget optimization after one sort and shows that optimal center leadership can disappear, reappear and disappear as the budget increases.
