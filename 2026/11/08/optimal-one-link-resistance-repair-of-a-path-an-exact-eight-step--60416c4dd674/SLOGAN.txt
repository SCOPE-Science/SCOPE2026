For a unit-resistance path \(P_n\), the unique best single unit-resistance shortcut for minimizing the total effective resistance joins vertices \(k+1\) and \(n-k\), where \(k=\lfloor(n-2)/8\rfloor\). The optimal attachment points approach one-eighth and seven-eighths of the path and asymptotically remove \(9/16\) of its original Kirchhoff index.
