Correlating consecutive uniform random stages in an explicit randomized Runge–Kutta method can expand the scalar mean-square stability ceiling from \(1.5960716\ldots\) to the sharp value \(2.0563299\ldots\). The optimal dependence pairs opposite quantiles of the **absolute** stage multiplier, not necessarily opposite uniform stage values; at \(z=2\) it contracts squared error by a factor \(4/5\) every two steps.
