For every binary word \(0^a1\,0^b1\,0^c1\,0^d\) requiring four palindrome blocks, exactly \(b+c+2\) distinct minimum-cut factorizations exist. Their complete bivariate generating function is \(2z^8u^3/((1-z)^2(1-z^3u)(1-z^4u^2))\), and the normalized multiplicity has an explicit nondegenerate conditional limit law.
