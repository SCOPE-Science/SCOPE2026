In the fully adaptive semi-random graph process, the best achievable chance of completing a triangle by \(\lfloor x\sqrt n\rfloor\) rounds converges to \(1-\exp(-2x^2/3)\), with a uniform \(O(n^{-1/2})\) error on bounded normalized horizons. Disjoint four-vertex paths attain the sharp \(4/3\) closable-vertices-per-edge extremum and generate an asymptotically optimal Rayleigh first-triangle law.
