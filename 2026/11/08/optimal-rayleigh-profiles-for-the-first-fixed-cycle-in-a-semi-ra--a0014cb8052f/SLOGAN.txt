For every fixed simple cycle length \(\ell\ge3\), the optimally accelerated first appearance time in the full-choice semi-random graph process has limiting survival \(\exp[-(\ell-1)x^2/(2\ell-3)]\) at horizon \(x\sqrt n\). The universal sharp exposure inequality \(a_\ell(G)\le(2\ell-2)|E(G)|/(2\ell-3)\) and an online disjoint-path construction determine the exact constant for all cycle lengths.
