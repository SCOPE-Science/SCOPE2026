For fixed integers \(p\ge q\ge2\), the exact first strict crossing of a reciprocal Lucas partial sum is \(\lfloor(3-\log_Aq)k+b\rfloor+1\) for all odd indices outside a zero-density set, where \(b\) is an explicit Binet-corrected logarithm. The proportions of true crossings at one and two steps above the previously known lower cutoff are exactly \(1-\vartheta\) and \(\vartheta\), with an explicit positive frequency of nonoptimal published upper bounds when \(p=q\).
