For an \(n\)-species food chain already containing a basal-to-apex feeding shortcut, the optimal one additional forward link to reduce trophic incoherence always skips exactly \(\lfloor(2n-2)/5\rfloor\) species and never ends at the apex. Every position with that span ties, and the optimal squared incoherence is asymptotically \(4/5\) of its original value.
