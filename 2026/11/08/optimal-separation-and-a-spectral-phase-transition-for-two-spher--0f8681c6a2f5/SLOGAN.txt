For stable reconstruction of real quadratic spherical harmonics from two equal, non-overlapping spherical caps, the unique optimal center separation is \(\max\{2\rho,\pi/2\}\): orthogonal whenever possible, otherwise tangent. The least concentration eigenvalue has an exact algebraic formula and, at orthogonal placement, its worst-mode multiplicity jumps from one to three to two as \(\cos\rho\) passes through \(\sqrt5/3\).
