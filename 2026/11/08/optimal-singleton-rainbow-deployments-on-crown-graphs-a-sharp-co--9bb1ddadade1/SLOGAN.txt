On a crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), every minimum singleton rainbow \(k\)-dominating function, for \(n>2k\ge4\), colors precisely \(k\) matched index pairs and uses every color once on each side; there are exactly \(\binom nk(k!)^2\) such named assignments. The count changes sharply at \(n=2k\), where additional one-side-full minima appear, and removing the matching suppresses the optimal-assignment count of \(K_{n,n}\) by the factor \(\binom nk\) without changing its minimum weight.
