For a pairwise star with independent three-person interactions among its leaves, the sharp mean-degree-normalized local synchronization gap is \(n/(n-1)\), attained exactly at mixing weight \(\alpha=(n-2)/n\) by pair-balanced triple designs. A seven-triple Fano system on eight vertices exactly matches the entire linearized spectrum of all \(35\) possible leaf triples at every mixing weight and doubles the star-only relaxation rate.
