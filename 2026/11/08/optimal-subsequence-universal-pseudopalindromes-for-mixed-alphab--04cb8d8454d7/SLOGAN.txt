For an alphabet involution with both swapped letter-pairs and fixed letters, the shortest \(k\)-subsequence-universal pseudopalindrome has length \(qk\) for even \(k\) and \(qk+f-1\) for odd \(k\), where \(q=2p+f\). Every shortest witness is an explicit symmetric arrangement of permutation blocks around a central orbit transversal, giving exactly \((q!)^{(k-1)/2}f2^p(p+f-1)!\) witnesses for odd \(k\).
