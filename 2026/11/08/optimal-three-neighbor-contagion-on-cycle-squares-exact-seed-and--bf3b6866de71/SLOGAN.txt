For three-neighbor bootstrap percolation on the square of every cycle of order \(n\ge5\), the smallest seed has \(\lceil(n+2)/3\rceil\) vertices; all optimal seeds and their synchronous completion times are counted exactly by the residue of \(n\) modulo three. In particular, at orders \(n=3k+2\) equally small seeds can take anywhere from \(\lceil k/2\rceil\) to \(k\) rounds, while the other two residue classes have a single optimal-seed completion time.
