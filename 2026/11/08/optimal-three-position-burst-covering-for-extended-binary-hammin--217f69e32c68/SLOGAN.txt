For every extended binary Hamming code of length \(2^m\), \(m\ge2\), a finite-field ordering makes three-position bursts sufficient to represent every syndrome, and no coordinate ordering can make two-position bursts sufficient. The same ordering reaches the sharp optimum of \(2^{m+1}-1\) obtainable syndromes with two-position bursts, both cyclically and noncyclically.
