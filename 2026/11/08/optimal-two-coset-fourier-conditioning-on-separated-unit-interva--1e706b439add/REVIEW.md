# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Pinned proof fiberizes the two disjoint unit intervals against integer translates: for fractional coordinates 0<=y<delta, n(y)=m+1; otherwise n(y)=m. Fiber Gram matrices have sharp eigenvalues 2±2|cos(pi n tau)|, with both types on positive measure. Invertibility and completeness give the exact two-coset Riesz basis criterion. Simultaneous distance-to-half-integer inequalities prove minimum max(|cos(pi m tau)|, |cos(pi(m+1)tau)|) = sin(pi/(4m+2)) uniquely at tau=±1/(2m+1) mod1, with squared-bound ratio (1+s)/(1-s); at integer gap there is a single orthogonal fiber. Independent grid checks for m=1..8 matched candidate optima, not substitutes for all-tau proof.
