For two disjoint unit intervals separated by a noninteger distance \(m+\delta\), the entire optimal two-coset exponential Riesz-basis conditioning is independent of \(\delta\): the unique shifts modulo conjugation are \(\pm1/(2m+1)\), and the optimal ratio of squared Riesz bounds is \((1+\sin(\pi/(4m+2)))/(1-\sin(\pi/(4m+2)))\). At integral separation the same family admits an orthogonal basis, giving an exact jump in best conditioning.
