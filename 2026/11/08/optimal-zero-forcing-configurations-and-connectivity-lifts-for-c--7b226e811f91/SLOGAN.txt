For every connected graph \(G\), attaching a clique of size \(r\ge2\) to each vertex multiplies the number of minimum classical zero-forcing sets by \(r^{|V(G)|-Z(G)}(r+1)^{Z(G)}\). The token-jump reconfiguration graph of those sets has exactly the same number of connected components as that of \(G\), and every component grows by this precise factor.
