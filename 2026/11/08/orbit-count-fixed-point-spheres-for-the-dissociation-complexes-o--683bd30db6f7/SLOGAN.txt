For a tree with \(m\) identical three-edge arms, the geometric fixed space of its dissociation complex under any arm-permutation subgroup \(H\) is a sphere of dimension \(2o(H)-1\), where \(o(H)\) counts the orbits on arms. An explicit equivariant homotopy equivalence realizes all fixed spheres simultaneously, even though every permutation acts trivially on the top integral homology.
