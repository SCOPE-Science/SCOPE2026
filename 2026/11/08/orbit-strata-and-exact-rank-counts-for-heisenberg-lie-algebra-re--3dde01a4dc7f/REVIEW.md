# Scientific assessment

**Correctness: PASS.** For an arbitrary field, the homomorphism and idempotence equations are solved without characteristic exceptions. The central-image alternative forces either the identity or an isotropic projection with a free functional on its image. The full automorphism conjugation removes that functional and reduces the orbit problem to a marked pair of complementary subspaces; alternating-form congruence is the complete orbit invariant. The finite-field formula follows by bijections between isotropic frames, graph complements, alternating forms and functionals. The reduced geometric strata follow from smooth affine bundles over isotropic Grassmannians. An exhaustive original-equation check over two prime fields in dimension three and a direct matrix check in dimension five over the binary field agree exactly with every predicted orbit-rank multiplicity.

**Originality: PASS (provisional).** Carlson–Friedlander–Pevtsova (`arXiv:1207.5898`; inspected expanded version `arXiv:1408.3913`, Proposition 2.2) classify relevant elementary subalgebra varieties, but not complementary kernels or idempotent endomorphism orbits. Cerqua–Facchini (`arXiv:2301.02627`, Proposition 15) state a generic idempotent-decomposition principle for pre-Lie algebras, but not Heisenberg orbit strata, exact labeled counts or their characteristic-independent geometry. Rojas (`arXiv:1206.5867`) develops the Heisenberg presentation in a faithful-representation problem. Published mathematical-record searches reveal related Heisenberg subalgebra commutativity counts and fixed-point-free automorphism counts, not the claimed classification of retractions. A previously unknown broader retraction theorem remains a genuine priority risk; absence from searches is not a proof of novelty.

**Value: PASS.** Isotropic image subspaces alone do not determine retractions up to automorphism: their complementary kernels split into different orbits according to an alternating restriction. In the five-dimensional binary Heisenberg algebra, two distinct rank-two orbit strata contain exactly 480 labeled projections each. The theorem determines this natural kernel-sensitive invariant, all rank gaps, every orbit multiplicity and smooth component geometry uniformly in the dimension and characteristic. This strengthens a basic image-subalgebra description by nontrivial, reproducible arithmetic and geometric information.

**Closest comparisons and limitations.** The isotropic Grassmannian and standard alternating-form count are established ingredients, not independently claimed as new. The proposed synthesis could be subsumed by a broader symplectic retraction classification not located here. The statements concern ordinary Lie homomorphisms and reduced varieties only. The finite-field check covers dimensions three and five and does not certify infinite families. Historical priority and correctness have not been independently audited.
