# Review of order-three arithmetic resonance in fixed-weight periodic autocorrelation

## Correctness — PASS

For every integer \(N\ge6\), every fixed occupied count \(0\le k\le N\), and every nonantipodal cyclic distance, the distance graph is two-regular with exactly \(N\) edges. An exact classification of triples of edges, including triangles when the shift order is three, gives the first three raw moments. The identity \(p_3-3p_4+3p_5-p_6=(k)_3(N-k)_3/(N)_6\) proves the resonant excess. Exact enumeration checked all cases through \(N=12\), with 827 exact comparisons; the infinite family follows from the counting proof, not from this experiment.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Mercer's aperiodic iid-sign moments (2006), Willms's deterministic run identities (arXiv:1206.4572), Zhong–Zhou–Jiang's triple-symbol correlations (2013), and Caullery–Érard–Rodier's iid periodic extremes (arXiv:2410.11347) were compared materially. A published fixed-composition transition-dependence formula gives second moments only; it does not imply a third-order triangular excess. published-finding corpus semantic searches covered periodic-autocorrelation aliases, fixed composition, third moments, and order-three resonances; no dominating claim was located. Older circular-run enumerations may contain an equivalent formula not indexed with this terminology. Search absence alone does not establish absolute priority.

## Value — PASS

The exact law identifies a natural and sharp arithmetic effect invisible to mean and variance: for balanced fixed-weight cyclic words, the third centered moment flips sign exactly at distance \(N/3\) when \(6\mid N\). The result motivates higher-order null calibration of periodic autocorrelations and explains its special status through a triangle-motif identity. Full distributions and higher moments remain outside scope.
