The rational \(\operatorname{MH}_{3,4}\) of the ternary projective-plane incidence graph is the transitive \(2808\)-point permutation representation on ordered noncollinear point and nonconcurrent line triangles, under collineations and polarity. The projective collineation character equals \(72\) on each of \(117\) involutions and vanishes at every other nonidentity element; the polarity-extended action fixes a single rational homology line.
