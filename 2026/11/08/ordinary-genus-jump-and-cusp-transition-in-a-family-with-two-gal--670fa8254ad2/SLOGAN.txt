For the natural two-Galois-point plane family \((x^p-x)^2-(y^p-cy)^2+1=0\) in odd characteristic, the normalization is always ordinary, but its genus jumps from \((p-1)^2\) to \(p^2-1\) precisely when \(c\ne1\). This jump is governed by an explicit order-\(p\) to order-\(p^2\) inertia transition and by two ordinary multiple points becoming unibranch cusps.
