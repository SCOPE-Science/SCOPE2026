For every \(n\ge4\), the ordinary three-cut complex of the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{2n(n-4)+1}S^{2n-4}\). An explicit uniform collapse of its Alexander dual to a connected graph resolves this parameter family and yields the same sphere ranks as untwisted circular ladders despite their opposite bipartiteness.
