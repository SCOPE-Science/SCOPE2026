For every odd finite field, the complete two-plane Fourier sampling spectrum on a nonzero ternary quadratic sphere is determined solely by whether the annihilator line is isotropic and, otherwise, one quadratic-character sign. Exactly \(q+1\) isotropic directions maximize recoverable rank, with a single \(q=3\) tie exception; the optimal-rank system becomes tight on its identifiable subspace for one radius sign.
