Orthogonal normalized descent directions can make two distance-minimizing gradient rays meet exactly while both objective values increase: for equal initial energies the sharp SPD condition-number threshold is \(\kappa=4/\eta-1\). Without that energy balance, for every fixed \(\kappa>1\) and damping \(\eta>0\), one path's quadratic objective amplification is unbounded although the inter-path distance contracts by \(1-\eta\).
