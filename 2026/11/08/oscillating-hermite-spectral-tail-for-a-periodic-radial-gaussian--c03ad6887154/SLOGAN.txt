For a Gaussian short-time Fourier localization mask consisting of infinitely many concentric radial bands with period \(T\) and width \(s\), the Gaussian is the uniquely isolated maximizer, with exact norm \((1-e^{-s})/(1-e^{-T})\). The other Hermite eigenvalues oscillate infinitely often across their nonzero essential-spectrum limit \(s/T\), at the explicit exponential rate \((1+4\pi^2/T^2)^{-(k+1)/2}\).
