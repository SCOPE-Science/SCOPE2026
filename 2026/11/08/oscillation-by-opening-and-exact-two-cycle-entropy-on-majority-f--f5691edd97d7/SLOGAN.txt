In synchronous binary majority dynamics on a fan graph, every nontrivial immediate two-cycle reverses the universal hub and corresponds exactly to a cyclic binary word without isolated ones satisfying two strict density inequalities. The exact run-binomial census has a sharp exception at four rim vertices and grows as \(2\lambda^n\) states, where \(\lambda\) is the Perron root of \(z^3-2z^2+z-1\).
