For a uniform noncrossing partition, the covariance of its number of outer blocks with its number of size-\(r\) blocks has an exact Catalan-binomial formula and a complete sign classification: singletons are positive, sizes at least three are negative, and size two changes sign at \(n=4\). The cross-moment gives an exact size-biased regression law, \(\mathbb E[R_n C_{n,r}]/\mathbb E[C_{n,r}]=3-2r/n\).
