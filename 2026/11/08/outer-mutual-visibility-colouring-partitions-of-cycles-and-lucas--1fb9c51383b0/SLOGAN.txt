Outer mutual-visibility colourings of every labelled cycle reduce exactly to matchings in a cycle or a cubic ladder: the complete partition polynomial has an explicit cyclic binomial formula in odd order and a three-state transfer-matrix formula in even order. The number of minimum-class partitions is \(n\) for odd \(n\ge5\) and the Lucas number \(L_{n/2}\) for even \(n\ge4\).
