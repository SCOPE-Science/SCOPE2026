The Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have identical total-domination polynomials, but their paired-domination polynomials differ by exactly \(32z^4+80z^6+96z^8\). Among \(80\) optimal total-dominating four-sets in each graph, exactly \(32\) Shrikhande sets induce an unmatchable claw, whereas all rook sets admit perfect matchings.
