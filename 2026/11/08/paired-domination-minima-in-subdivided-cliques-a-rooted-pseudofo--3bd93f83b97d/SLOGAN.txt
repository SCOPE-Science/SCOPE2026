Minimum paired dominating sets of the once-subdivided clique \(S(K_n)\) are in bijection with spanning pseudoforests having one nontrivial root tree and otherwise unicyclic components. Their exact matching-multiplicity distribution is \(A_n(y)=n(n-1)![x^{n-1}](e^{T(x)}-1)e^{yU(x)}\), and precisely \(n^{n-1}\) minimum sets have a unique induced perfect matching.
