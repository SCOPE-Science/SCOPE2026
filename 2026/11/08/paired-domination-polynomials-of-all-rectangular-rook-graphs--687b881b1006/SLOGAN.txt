Paired-dominating placements on every rectangular rook graph admit an exact two-variable labelled square-root generating function: retain only bipartite incidence-graph components containing an even number of selected edges, then require every row or every column to be occupied. The formula determines every cardinality count and yields explicit parity-dependent minimum-placement counts, including a nine-configuration overlap correction on the \(3\)-by-\(3\) board.
