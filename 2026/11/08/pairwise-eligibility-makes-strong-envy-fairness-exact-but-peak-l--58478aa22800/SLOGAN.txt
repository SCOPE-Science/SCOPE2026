When every task is eligible for at most two agents, assigning it to the currently less-burdened endpoint **for that pair** maintains strong EF1 online, and unit tasks even maintain strong EFX. For unit tasks, an exact induced-subgraph density formula gives the minimum fair makespan and an integral-flow algorithm attains it, although a star family has a fairness cost of \((n-1)/2\) relative to the unrestricted optimum.
