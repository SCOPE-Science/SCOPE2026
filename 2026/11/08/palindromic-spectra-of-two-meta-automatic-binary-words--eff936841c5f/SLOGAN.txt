The first meta-automatic word \(M_1\) of Campbell and Cloitre has exactly \(28\) distinct nonempty palindromic factors, with maximum length \(16\), and its complete palindromic complexity is determined by a finite exhaustive two-block certificate. In contrast, their second word \(M_2\) has a palindromic prefix of length \(8^r\) for every \(r\ge0\), giving unbounded palindromic factors.
