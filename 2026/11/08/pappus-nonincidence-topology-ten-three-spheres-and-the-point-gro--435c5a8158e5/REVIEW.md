# Scientific review: Pappus nonincidence topology and symmetry

## Correctness
PASS. The explicitly specified nine-triple Pappus incidence structure is cross-matched with the published model. Two independent face tests produce 298 faces per component. Two fully checked Morse matchings per component each have 143 matched pairs and exactly one critical vertex and ten critical three-simplices. All 1,080 face-cover relations per component are checked in the directed graph, and exhaustive topological sorting proves acyclicity. Thus the discrete-Morse CW complex has only one zero-cell and ten three-cells, giving the **integral** wedge decomposition. Mod-two boundary ranks provide an independent corroboration. All 108 point-incidence automorphisms are enumerated, and the character is computed from exact oriented face traces by the Lefschetz formula. Risk: the finite verifier and proof have not received independent external audit.

## Originality
PASS against located evidence, with residual bibliographic risk. Closest full texts were Kim--Kim (arXiv:2101.01850), Santhanam--Shukla--Singh (arXiv:2211.10607), and Abdallah--Schenck (arXiv:2208.01557, Example 3.5). The first two give bounds and exact results for other hypergraph families, not this configuration. The last studies the Pappus arrangement and graded algebraic Betti tables of a different monomial ideal; its Pappus line triples are acknowledged as established, but it states neither the present noncover homotopy type nor the incidence automorphism character. Comparisons also include the neighborhood-complex terminology, the noncover Alexander dual, and nearby indexed Pappus and incidence results. Searches cannot eliminate the possibility of unindexed equivalent work.

## Value
PASS. The Pappus configuration is a canonical self-dual \((9_3)\) point-line configuration with a large symmetry group. A pure five-dimensional natural noncover complex with a full ten-dimensional homology group two dimensions below its facets is a motivated finite classification, not an arbitrary induced restriction. The exact character—including different trace values for automorphisms of the same point-cycle type—adds new structure beyond a Betti table. The result supplies a precise topological and equivariant test case for studying how incidence geometry influences Alexander-dual hypergraph complexes; no general classification is asserted.

## Scientific limitations and closest risks
The result does not determine an integral equivariant decomposition or an all-configuration family theorem. The published Pappus algebraic ideal and its Alexander dual must not be conflated with this noncover complex. Possible equivalent nonindexed computations remain the principal originality risk.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
