# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The parabolic coordinate identity \(h_\gamma^{\circ n}(s)=(s^{-\gamma}+n)^{-1/\gamma}\) is proved by induction; the displayed choice \(f=\varphi=g=h_\gamma\) checks all defining inequalities of an \(F\)-stochastic operator. The row transition probabilities follow from the original construction. The common-uniform coupling has a summable state-uniform discrepancy bound and gives almost-sure finite disagreement. Equivalence, which does not follow merely from coupling, has a separate two-direction uniformly \(L^2\)-bounded likelihood proof. Singularity uses mutually exclusive occupation-frequency events. Almost-sure count limits, the central limit theorem, and the triangular critical-window law use independent bounded variables with their hypotheses checked. The embedded checker passes algebraic and finite asymptotic sanity tests. Finite testing is not the source of the infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The closest sources are Souissi–Mukhamedov (2024), which defines the class and path construction, and Jamilov et al. (2024), which supplies a general equivalence criterion and a sufficient summability theorem but explicitly raises the nonsummable comparison problem. A general criterion could be specialized to the fixed-exponent equivalence result, so no novelty is claimed for that abstract method. The identified model-specific combination goes further: full equivalence/singularity classes even across parameter values, eventual finite-defect reduction to an independent product, an almost-sure rare-occupation threshold, and an explicit critical \(1/\log N\) normal crossover. Focused literature and research-record searches found no equivalent combined statement; failure to locate one is not a proof of world-first priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The gap is structural rather than a routine normalization: all the deterministic dynamics converge to the same point, but some associated path laws have only finitely many visits to the vanishing state, others infinitely many, and their entire path measures divide into explicitly determined equivalence classes. Distinct seed data give equivalent measures despite nonsummability, directly probing the boundary question in Jamilov et al. The finite-defect coupling supplies an exact probabilistic reduction and makes a sharp logarithmic critical window calculable. The result is confined to a natural parabolic family and does not resolve the broad classification for arbitrary nonlinear stochastic operators.

## Closest literature and limitations

The two 2024 full-text sources are inspected at their definitions, matrix constructions, theorems on absolute continuity, examples, and the nonsummability question. The classical Hellinger/Kakutani criterion and Bernoulli normal limits are acknowledged as existing tools. Possible alternative presentations as direct consequences of general nonhomogeneous-chain equivalence principles, or unindexed equivalent model calculations, remain publication-priority risks. Zero row seeds and degenerate initial measures are outside the theorem.
