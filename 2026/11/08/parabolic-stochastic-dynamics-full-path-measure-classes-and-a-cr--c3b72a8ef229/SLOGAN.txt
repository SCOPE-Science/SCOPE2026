A parabolic \(F\)-stochastic operator converges to a single deterministic equilibrium for every \(\gamma>0\), yet its associated Markov laws split into one common equivalence class for \(0<\gamma<1\) and one mutually singular class for each \(\gamma\ge1\). Every path law is equivalent to an explicit independent Bernoulli product and couples to it after finitely many coordinate discrepancies, yielding an exact \(1/\log N\) critical-window Gaussian rare-visit profile.
