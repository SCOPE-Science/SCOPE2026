In a random piecewise-convex interval map with Pareto-distributed flatness, the first-return survival probability to the expanding section has the exact stationary formula \(\Pr(R>n)=n^{-\kappa}\mathbb E[\min(Z,n)^\kappa]\) and a \(C_\kappa n^{-\kappa}+O(2^{-n}/n)\) tail. Conditioned on unusually long excursions, the initial logarithmic entrance depth is \(Z^\kappa\)-size-biased and the scaled excursion length converges independently to a Pareto law; stationary mass is finite precisely for \(\kappa>1\).
