A Brownian search with Poisson resetting and a target drawn once from an exponential distance law has a Pareto absorption-time tail of exponent \(a=\beta/\sqrt{r/D}\), even though every fixed-target tail is light. Conditioned on unusually long searches, the hidden target acquires a Gamma-selected logarithmic position and the reset count becomes asymptotically identical to elapsed time in Poisson-clock units.
