In a regular-base bipyramid whose two apices share a lateral displacement, every Haar-random central section has exactly \(n+2\) vertices when \(n\) is even, but odd orders exhibit an explicit three-point law on \(n+1,n+2,n+3\). The lower exceptional count is always more probable, with a sharp shear-asymmetry of order \(\lambda^n\), and the middle probability uniquely recovers the shear-to-height ratio.
