On almost-complete, vertex-transitive cycle-complement graphs, synchronous binary majority has a sharp parity contrast: every odd-order configuration reaches one of exactly \(2N+2\) fixed points within two updates, whereas even order \(N=2m\) admits explicit initial states requiring \(m-2\) updates before a two-cycle. The slow even trajectories are balanced minority waves, so diameter two and degree \(N-3\) do not force uniformly fast worst-case settling.
