For real two-monomial Müntz spaces on the symmetric interval, opposite-parity powers have a sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) transition: both powers are required when \(h\le5\ell+1\), but only the higher power is optimal when \(h\ge5\ell+3\). The transition has an exact norm and coefficient-energy ratio and converges to a degree-ratio-five boundary, while every same-parity pair necessarily has mixed extremizers.
