For synchronous binary majority on the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) with its diagonal perfect matching deleted, even \(n\) gives a one-step periodicization clock and polynomially many initially periodic states, whereas odd \(n\) gives a sharp three-step clock (from \(n\ge5\)) and exponentially rare initial recurrence. Exact labelled entry-time populations and both eventual basin sizes follow from explicit binomial formulas, and only odd \(n\) has a unique primitive two-cycle.
