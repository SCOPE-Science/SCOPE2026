# Scientific assessment: parity bifurcation on clique coronas

## Correctness
PASS. The leaf-copy identity reduces the two-period relation to two explicit hub-majority tests; the even-order rule is a complete three-case partition and the odd-order classification lists all four local ordered types. The parity-separated counts follow by disjoint labelled choices. A symmetric threshold energy proves that no longer recurrent orbits are omitted. Independent graph-based exhaustive checks cover all 21,840 states for orders 2 through 7, while a distinct multinomial enumeration covers orders 2 through 60. These finite checks corroborate, not replace, the all-order proof.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The most relevant full-text primary source, Kaaser–Mallmann-Trenn–Natale (arXiv:1508.03519v1, 2015), formalizes the same tie-retaining majority rule and proves voting-time bounds and module reductions, not a recurrent-population formula for the clique corona. Benjamini and colleagues (arXiv:1405.2486v1, 2014) concern infinite/random-graph convergence. A public exact formula for general-position subsets of the same corona graphs is a different invariant. Prior exact majoritarian results for pineapple, split, and multipartite graphs do not imply the matched private-leaf dynamics: their hub neighborhoods are different. Targeted corpus and web searches found no same-object formula, but coverage of unindexed papers and other names for clique corona remains a priority risk.

## Value
PASS. The one-leaf-per-hub clique corona is a canonical dense-core/pendant-memory network. Its recurrent state census demonstrates a sharp structural parity effect: the proportion of immediately primitive-two states decays as \(\Theta(n^{-1})\) on even orders, versus \(\Theta(\sqrt n\,2^{-n})\) on odd orders. Exact fixed-state and two-cycle counts, with a proof of completeness, make the comparison a natural exact invariant, not an arbitrary finite slice or numerical extrapolation.

## Limitations
Only strict synchronous open-neighbor binary majority with tie retention is covered; no transient basin census is claimed. The independent audit remains outstanding. Novelty against inaccessible or unindexed equivalent formulations is not guaranteed.
