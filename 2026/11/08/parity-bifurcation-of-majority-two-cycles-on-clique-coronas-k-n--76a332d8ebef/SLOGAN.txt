On clique coronas \(K_n\odot K_1\), synchronous majority has an exact binomial census of immediately primitive two-cycles, with separate even- and odd-order formulas. The uniform initial oscillation probability is asymptotic to \(2/(\pi n)\) for even \(n\), but to \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}\,2^{-n}\) for odd \(n\): changing the clique parity switches polynomial rarity to exponential rarity.
