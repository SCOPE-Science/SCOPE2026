For a complete graph with any matching deleted, every **odd-defect** chromatic polynomial is identical to that of the complete graph. At even defect \(d=2h\), the entire difference is enumerated by colour classes consisting of \(h+1\) deleted pairs; it first appears exactly when the matching has at least \(h+1\) edges, with leading coefficient \(\binom{t}{h+1}\) in degree \(n-d-1\).
