In the unit-weight binary anti-coordination game on \(K_c\vee\overline K_m\), all pure Nash equilibria and their exact labeled count admit a closed occupancy classification. Odd \(c\) makes every equilibrium socially optimal, whereas even \(c\) permits inefficient tie equilibria for \(m\ge2\), with price of anarchy approaching 2 as \(m\to\infty\).
