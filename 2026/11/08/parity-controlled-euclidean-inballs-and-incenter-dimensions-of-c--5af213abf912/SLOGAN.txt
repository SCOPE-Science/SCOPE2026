For every genuine complete-bipartite symmetric-edge polytope \(P_{p,q}\) with \(2\le p\le q\), the intrinsic Euclidean inradius is exactly \(\bigl(q-(q\bmod2)/(p+q)\bigr)^{-1/2}\). The full incenter family has dimension \(p-(q\bmod2)\) when \(p<q\), but for equal parts it is a segment in even size and a singleton in odd size; exact facet-contact counts accompany this parity transition.
