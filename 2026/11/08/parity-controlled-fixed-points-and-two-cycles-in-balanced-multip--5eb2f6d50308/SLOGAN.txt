On a balanced complete multipartite network with at least four parts, binary synchronous majority has an exact fixed-state and primitive-two census controlled by the parity of both the number of parts and their size. For even part size, two reversing uniform parts can coexist with arbitrarily many independently balanced frozen parts, creating \(r(r-1)\binom{n}{n/2}^{r-2}\) asymptotic oscillating configurations, whereas odd part size destroys those mixed-part families.
