A weighted folded-hypercube Ising antiferromagnet has an exact parity-dependent frustration-relocation threshold at \((-1)^dK=\min_iJ_i\). Its complete ground-state configurations are only the full-parity Walsh character and the characters omitting one weakest direction, yielding multiplicities \(2\), \(2m\), and \(2(m+1)\) on the two sides and at coexistence.
