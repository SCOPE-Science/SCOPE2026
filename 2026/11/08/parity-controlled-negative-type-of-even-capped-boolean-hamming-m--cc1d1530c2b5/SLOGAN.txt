For each fixed even cap \(r\), the supremal negative-type exponent of the Boolean Hamming metric truncated at \(r\) decays as \((r-1)!/[n^{r-1}\log(r/(r-1))]\), with parity producing the sharp obstruction. For cap four in every dimension \(n\ge8\), an explicit one-variable equation gives the exact threshold, and the endpoint loses exactly one Euclidean embedding dimension.
