# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The edge variables are independent, and the proof explicitly separates equal and distinct vertex labels. Conditional on an isolated vertex, the degree of another vertex has exactly \(\mathrm{Bin}(n-2,p)\) distribution, giving the mixed covariance without a limiting approximation. The resulting factor has strictly determined signs for even orders and one strictly monotone zero for odd orders. A logarithmic expansion supplies the critical-window limit; exhaustive exact-rational checks through six vertices and independent numerical window checks support the algebra. The proof, not enumeration, establishes all sizes.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Related published primary work determines marginal isolated-vertex properties and fixed-degree count approximations, and later works address numerical degree correlations or parity properties at a fixed edge density. None of the inspected material evaluated this exact mixed nonlinear covariance and its unique parity-dependent crossing near the complementary \(\log(n)/n\) scale. A fully general joint-degree generating function might imply the formula after specialization; this remains a priority risk and is not ruled out by a title-level search. ## Value — PASS

Isolation and degree parity are two canonical, sharply different vertex statistics. Their relationship distinguishes odd and even graph orders, with an exact finite-size threshold and a nontrivial logarithmic critical window. The reversal has a transparent combinatorial explanation: on isolating one vertex in a nearly complete graph, the degree parity of all others flips in opposite directions depending on order parity. The effect is very small in absolute covariance, limiting direct empirical detection, but the exact structural sign classification has intrinsic probabilistic value.
