At zero field, the antiferromagnetic spin-one model on every wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\) has an exact parity-dependent vacancy phase diagram: odd rims have a two-vacancy plateau for \(1/2<D/J<(n-2)/(n-1)\), whereas even rims lose only the hub at \(D=0\) before emptying at \(D=J\). The complete ground-state multiplicities, including all coexistence boundaries and the exceptional \(n=3\) case, follow from a nonnegative local bond identity and an odd-path parity obstruction.
