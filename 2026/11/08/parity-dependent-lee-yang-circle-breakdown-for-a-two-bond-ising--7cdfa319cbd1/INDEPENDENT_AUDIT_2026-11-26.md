The Hamiltonian gives exactly tr(T_L² T^(N−2)); T_L=cT+dσ_x and Cayley–Hamilton reproduce the three terms in (1). In q,u notation their coefficients are u(1−q²), u^−1−2q+u q² and 2q(1−u q), respectively. At x=0 their even signed combination is u−u^−1; the signed odd derivative is Nu−(N−2)/u−2q, giving the stated unique positive threshold below 1. For L≤0 the endpoint x=1 has positive value and the strictly positive interior nodes cos(jπ/(N−1)) have signs (−1)^j. On the positive side j=0,…,m−1 gives m−1 sign-change intervals for either N=2m or 2m+1. Parity gives their negatives and, when odd, x=0, hence N−2 distinct real zeros before testing the central gap. For N=3 the closest positive node is the endpoint 1: use (3), not the interior-node formula. The positive signed odd derivative yields the two additional central real roots; at either threshold the central multiplicity and the distinct outer roots exhaust the degree, hence the multiplicities are exactly two and three. For the fugacity map write F_N(x)=x^εR(x²), ε=N mod 2, and x²=(z+1)²/(4(1−q²)z). A nonzero pair gives a quadratic in z with product 1; real |x|<1 gives two simple circle zeros, imaginary x gives negative distinct reciprocal zeros. Each factor x² raises the order at z=−1 by two and x^ε by ε. This removes branch ambiguity. The L≥0 circle property uses the classical ferromagnetic theorem. The result concerns the common L changing sign, not varying one edge independently.

## originality

PASS

The exact two-bond impurity partition model and its transfer evaluation are prior art; the nonnegative-coupling circle result is also prior. A distinct single-frustrated-bond result anticipates the qualitative parity and collision pattern very closely, but has another bond geometry and different critical formulas. The surviving result is the sharp two-equal-bond threshold at negative coupling, with exhaustive off-circle count, real location and odd triple collision for this topology.
