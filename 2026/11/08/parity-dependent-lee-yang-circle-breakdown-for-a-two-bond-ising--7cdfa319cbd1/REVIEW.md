## Value

**PASS.** The classical Lee–Yang circle property is a central structural diagnostic of complex-field statistical mechanics. The model is a physically motivated finite Ising chain with a single atom attached by two equal impurity bonds. A sharp parity threshold settles precisely when antiferromagnetism can coexist with the circle property, and the triple \(z=-1\) collision distinguishes odd-size finite clusters from even ones.
