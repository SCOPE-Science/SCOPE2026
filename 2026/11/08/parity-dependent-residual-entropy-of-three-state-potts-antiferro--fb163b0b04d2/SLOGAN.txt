For three-state zero-field Potts antiferromagnets on Mycielskians of all cycles, the exact ground-state multiplicity follows two parity-dependent cubic recurrences: even cycles are unfrustrated, while odd cycles have exactly one unavoidable bad bond and a linear-in-length degeneracy prefactor. Both parities have the exact per-vertex logarithmic growth rate \(\tfrac14\log(3.24697960371747\ldots)\).
