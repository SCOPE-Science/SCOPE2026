For normalized adjacent-difference equations on a path with two absolute endpoint measurements, the exact best fixed randomized-Kaczmarz worst-direction one-step rate is \(1/\sum_i[1+\sqrt2\min\{i-1,n-i\}]^2\). Every even-order optimum uniquely discards the middle comparison, while every odd-order optimum belongs to an explicit one-parameter segment, with all rows active in the segment's interior.
