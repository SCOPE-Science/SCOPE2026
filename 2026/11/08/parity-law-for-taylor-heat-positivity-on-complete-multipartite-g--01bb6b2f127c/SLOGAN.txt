For Taylor heat updates on every unweighted complete multipartite graph, every even degree has the same sharp normalized positivity horizon, independent of all part sizes, whereas every odd degree has finitely many unique, permanent sign-change obstructions. At degree three the entire partition dependence collapses to the smallest nonsingleton part and the presence of singleton parts; at degree four the universal horizon is \(nh=2.785293563405\ldots\).
