For synchronous binary majority on two cliques of orders \(m,n\ge3\) connected by one bridge, primitive two-cycles exist exactly when both orders are even, and their number is \(\binom{m-1}{m/2}\binom{n-1}{n/2}\); none attracts a transient state. The sharp first periodic-entry time is two, four, or five updates according as both orders are odd, both are even, or their parities differ.
