In characteristic two, the depth-one order-two locus in the degree-\(N\) truncated Nottingham group is the reduced smooth variety \(\mathbb G_m\times\mathbb A^{\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor}\). Every such involution lifts to a formal involution when \(N\) is even, but at odd \(N\) exactly one in \(q\) lifts over \(\mathbb F_q\), because the next even-degree coefficient is the unique remaining obstruction.
