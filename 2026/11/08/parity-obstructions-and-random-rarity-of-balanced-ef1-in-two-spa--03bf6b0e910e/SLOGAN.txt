For fixed two-group EF1 sharing with Eulerian two-sparse binary valuations, the exact minimum item-count imbalance is the number of odd-edge connected components, and each extra neutral good removes one unit of the obstruction until parity is reached. In a uniformly random labeled 2-regular support graph, balanced EF1 becomes rare with probability asymptotic to \(C n^{-1/4}\) on even orders and \(C n^{-1/4}\log n/4\) on odd orders, where \(C=e^{1/2}2^{3/4}\sqrt{\pi}/\Gamma(1/4)\).
