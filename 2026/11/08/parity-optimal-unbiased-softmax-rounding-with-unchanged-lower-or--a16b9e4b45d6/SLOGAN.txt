For any number of independently margined stochastic-rounded logits, preserving every proper-subset joint law still leaves a higher-order dependence that changes exact softmax cross-entropy; its unique optimal coupling is an explicit parity endpoint and is the same for every true label. At fair rounding probabilities the optimum is uniform even parity, and its loss advantage over independent rounding grows as \(h/2^n+O(1)\) with a common bin width \(h\), despite identical means and covariances.
