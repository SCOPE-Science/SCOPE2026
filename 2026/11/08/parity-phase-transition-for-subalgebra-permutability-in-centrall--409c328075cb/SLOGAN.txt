For \(L_r(q)=\mathfrak{aff}_1(\mathbb F_q)\oplus\mathbb F_q^r\) with \(r\ge3\), the number of Lie subalgebras and the number of ordered nonpermuting subalgebra pairs have explicit Gaussian-binomial formulas. At fixed central rank, the probability that two uniformly chosen subalgebras permute converges to \(0\) for odd \(r\), but to \(1/4\) for even \(r\).
