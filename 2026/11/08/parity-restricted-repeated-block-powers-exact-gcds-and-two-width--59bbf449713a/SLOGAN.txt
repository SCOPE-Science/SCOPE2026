Restricting repeated-block powers to odd block widths changes their exact common divisor to \(\frac{b^k-1}{b^t-1}\gcd(t,\frac{b^t-1}{b-1})\), where \(t\) is the odd part of \(k\); block widths \(1\) and \(t\) already certify the value. For binary repetition counts that are powers of two, this creates an exponentially large congruence obstruction, whereas even widths retain the unrestricted base-four formula.
