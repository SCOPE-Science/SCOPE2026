For any alphabet with at least three symbols, a two-state probabilistic automaton whose letter transitions all reverse the probability order can uniquely maximize only words whose odd-position and even-position subsequences each have no recurrent symbol-runs. This forces at most \(O_q(n^{2q-2})\) possible length-\(n\) winners for fixed alphabet size \(q\), exponentially fewer than all \(q^n\) words.
