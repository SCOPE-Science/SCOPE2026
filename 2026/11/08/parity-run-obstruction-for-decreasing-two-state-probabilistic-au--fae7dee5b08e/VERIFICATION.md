The proof is a self-contained monotone-envelope argument after the two-state affine representation. Run `python3 checker.py` with Python 3 standard library. The deterministic exact rational test covers 460 parameter instances (including some binary comparison instances), checks all words at tested sizes, and confirms the stated combinatorial cardinality of the necessary family. It does not prove realizability of all such words or establish historical uniqueness. The theoretical result is for all finite alphabets of cardinality at least three and every positive integer input length. No independent audit has been performed.
