In uniform occupancy, the number of boxes hit exactly \(r\) times has strictly negative covariance with the number of odd-load boxes for every even \(r\), but for odd \(r\ge3\) the covariance reverses sign exactly once as sample size increases. The transition occurs near \(n=c_rm\) in \(m\) boxes, where \(e^{2c_r}+2c_r=2r-1\), equivalently describing the dependence between coordinate-flip frequencies and Hamming displacement in a hypercube walk.
