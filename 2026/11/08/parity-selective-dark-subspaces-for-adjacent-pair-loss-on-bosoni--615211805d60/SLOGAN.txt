For two noninteracting bosons on a ring with strictly positive pair loss on every edge, permanently protected states exist exactly on even rings: their dimension is \(n-1\) when \(4\mid n\) and \(n-2\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\). All protected states have zero energy, and an exact Fourier projector gives distance-resolved eventual capture probabilities independent of the individual positive loss rates.
