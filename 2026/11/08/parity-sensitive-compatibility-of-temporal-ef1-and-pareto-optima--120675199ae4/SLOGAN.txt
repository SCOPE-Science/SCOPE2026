With reciprocal positive values and \(p\ge2\) items of one type arriving before \(q\ge1\) of the other, temporal EF1 and final Pareto optimality are jointly possible exactly when \(q\beta\le\alpha(1+(p\bmod2))\), for \(\alpha>\beta>0\). When possible, exactly \(2^{\lfloor p/2\rfloor}\) labeled-item allocations work; an odd first-phase length doubles the allowable second-phase capacity relative to an even one.
