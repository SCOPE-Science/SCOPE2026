For isotropically sampled initial states of a higher-dimensional quasiperiodic Stuart–Landau oscillator, the intrinsic volume of the asymptotic \(N\)-torus has an exact independent-beta product factorization and a Dirichlet-product Mellin law. Passing from ambient dimension \(2N\) to \(2N+1\) adds one logarithm to the sharp small-volume tail and an extra half-power to the near-maximal-volume tail, with explicit constants for every \(N\ge3\).
