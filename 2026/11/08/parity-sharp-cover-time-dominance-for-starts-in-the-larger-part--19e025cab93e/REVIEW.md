# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Uniform complete-bipartite transitions yield two independent coupon streams with a one-step offset. Their completion-time maximum gives exact CDFs, including the odd/even boundaries and both starting sides. A self-contained Rolle induction establishes distinct negative roots of ordered-Bell polynomials, hence the strict successive-coupon ratio comparison. The proof checks the cases of equal parts, a singleton part, and the earliest possible even-time cover separately. The cover-time gap is identified on a common coupling, so its strict range and star equality follow. Exact rational finite-state computation independently confirms 850 CDF instances through time 16 and 60 strict even-time cases; these finite checks are not used to infer the general result.

**Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk.** The primary full-text comparisons concern heterogeneous cover-time universality (Dong et al., arXiv:2210.05122v1), random-regular cover laws (Tishby et al., arXiv:2110.13592v1), general cover-time transfer matrices (Zlatanov–Kocarev, arXiv:0910.3545), and independent structured coupon concentration (Falgas-Ravry et al., arXiv:1601.04455v1). None of the examined statements provides the strict all-parameter part-start order with sharp parity equality; a general transfer matrix can compute individual distributions but does not imply the required sign comparison without a new inequality. The nearest separately retrieved exact complete-bipartite discounted-hitting result addresses source-set optimization, not full simple-walk coverage. A classical or harder-to-find direct formula could predate this work; no exhaustive priority guarantee is claimed.

**Value — PASS.** Start-vertex dependence is a substantive concern in cover-time optimization on irregular graphs. The theorem gives a complete stochastic, rather than merely expectation, comparison for every unbalanced biclique, with sharp exceptional parity, the exact first gap, and a surprising uniform one-step upper bound on the expectation advantage. The full-law order is stronger than an isolated coupon-collector formula or finite table and is motivated by how bipartite periodicity affects exploration.

## Closest literature and limits
Dong et al. (2022) investigate heterogeneous Gumbel universality, not fixed-graph start-side ordering. Tishby et al. (2021) study random regular graphs. Zlatanov–Kocarev (2009) supply universally applicable distribution formulas, a genuine background method but not this comparison inequality. Falgas-Ravry et al. (2016) provide structured coupon concentration. Limits: uniform, unweighted, no holding, complete bipartite only. The polynomial real-rootedness lemma is not presented as an original research finding.
