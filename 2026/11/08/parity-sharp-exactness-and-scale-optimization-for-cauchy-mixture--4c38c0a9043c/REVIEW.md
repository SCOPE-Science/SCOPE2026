## Value — PASS

The 2026 polynomial-weight extension specifically identifies the Cauchy weight as a meaningful heavy-tailed quadrature target. A Cauchy mixture is the first natural class in which no single Möbius scale flattens all components. Exact normalization of a probability density is a prerequisite for reliable weighted integration; the result yields a sharp parity obstruction, a complete tuning rule, and the cost of insisting on even counts. The finite-node formulas and large-node limit explicitly identify which component scales matter, giving a useful calibration benchmark for heavy-tailed transformed quadrature. It is not asserted to imply new convergence guarantees for arbitrary test functions.
