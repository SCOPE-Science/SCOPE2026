For a nontrivial positive mixture of centered Cauchy densities, scaled Möbius trapezoidal normalization has exactly one error-free scale at every odd node count, but strictly positive unavoidable bias and one unique minimizing scale at every even count. Both optimal scales approach the geometric mean of the extreme component scales, and the even minimum has exact leading size \(4\sqrt{p_-p_+}\rho^n\); equal-weight two-scale mixtures exhibit an exact geometric-mean optimum at every even count and strictly decreasing odd exact scales.
