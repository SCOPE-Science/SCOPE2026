# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

All singular values follow from orthogonal Weyl character columns, whose lengths are \(\sqrt N|A(a,b)|\). The complete polar matrix of the derivative of the symmetric elementary cubic was derived entrywise. Its kernel has dimensions zero, \(n-2\), one, or two in the stated classes, with the parity-dependence treated exactly. The elementary quadratic Walsh support lemma proves absolute values and support sizes without an unproved character-sum assumption. Summing shift classes gives every multiplicity, rank, kernel dimension and condition number. The embedded integer checker confirms all shift/frequency coefficients and ranks for \(3\le n\le9\), and separate direct small-matrix singular-value checks fix normalization. The infinite theorem rests on the proof, not finite enumeration.

## Originality — PASS under bounded primary comparison

The precise complete cubic symmetric hypergraph state is studied in Lyons et al., arXiv:1609.01306 (inspected in full, including Theorems 1–4, applications and appendix). Its classification of *maximal* local Pauli stabilizers implies the presence of an extra maximal ambiguity coefficient for even \(n\), but does not determine the other positive coefficients or rank and conditioning. This covered observation is explicitly excluded from novelty. Bojarovska–Flinth, arXiv:1503.05800 (inspected), provide the Weyl/ambiguity lifting mechanism but no claim about this cubic state. Nöller–Gühne–Gachechiladze, arXiv:2302.01695 (full text inspected), discuss the same complete hypergraph state, local stabilizers, Bell nonlocality and entanglement, not this complete singular spectrum. Broad source and published-finding corpus searches for all-Pauli correlation tables, symmetric cubic Boolean derivative spectra and Weyl support laws found no dominating explicit result; absence in search results is not absolute priority proof. A historical all-Pauli table remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The complete three-uniform hypergraph phase is a canonical nonquadratic Boolean phase with applications in symmetric multi-qubit states. Its full complex-linear time-frequency lifting has a genuinely nontrivial rank-density parity transition and exact conditioning. These global Fourier and tomography invariants are not given by knowing only local Pauli stabilizers, nor by the standard quadratic Walsh lemma without the cubic derivative classification. The result is an infinite all-dimension structural theorem, not a small finite computation or an arbitrary parameter slice.
