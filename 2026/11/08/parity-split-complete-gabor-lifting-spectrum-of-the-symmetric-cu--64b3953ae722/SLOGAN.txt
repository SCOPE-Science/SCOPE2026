For the complete symmetric cubic Boolean phase on \(\mathbb F_2^n\), the full Gabor projector lift has three exact positive singular levels and a parity jump: recoverable complex matrix-space density tends to \(1/4\) for odd \(n\), but \(1/8\) for even \(n\). Its exact positive-subspace condition number is \(2^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\).
