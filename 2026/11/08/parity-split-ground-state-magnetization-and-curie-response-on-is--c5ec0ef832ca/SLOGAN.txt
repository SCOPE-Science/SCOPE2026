For a uniformly antiferromagnetic Ising model on the \(n\)-gonal antiprism, the complete zero-field ground-state magnetization polynomial jumps from \(4\) for even \(n\) to \(n(n+1)+\tfrac{n(n-1)}2(y^{-2}+y^2)\) for odd \(n\). This yields a sharp parity-dependent zero-temperature Curie coefficient: \(0\) for even \(n\) and \(2(n-1)/n\) for odd \(n\).
