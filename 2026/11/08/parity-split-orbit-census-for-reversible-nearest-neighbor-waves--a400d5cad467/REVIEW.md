# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

The claim specifies a simultaneous map on paired rows over every finite characteristic-two field, all ring lengths at least three, and every positive time. The inverse map is explicit. For odd circumference, the sum-zero subspace is complementary to constants and an invertible traveling-wave substitution conjugates the nonconstant dynamics to two shifts; the constants carry one size-two unipotent block. For even circumference, a local factorization over each odd-order shift root proves the two regular-shift elementary-divisor copies, including the nonsemisimple unit-root case. This establishes the fixed-space dimensions, group order, Möbius inversion and the odd-circumference population relation. The independent matrix/tuple/Möbius checkers confirm the predictions in substantial finite ranges. Infinite claims rest on the proof, not the checkers.

## Originality (PASS, historical uncertainty)

Macfarlane (2004) provides foundational second-order linear updates and explicit single-seed weight calculations but uses different chosen rules and does not treat the present Rule-90R finite-ring orbit census. Schiff (2007) describes exactly this local Rule-90R equation under periodic boundaries, so the rule and its reversibility are expressly prior art; the inspected Chapter 3.7 does not give the even/odd similarity, all-time fixed-point census or primitive-period relation. The general second-order lattice-gas reduction of Toffoli, Capobianco and Mentrasti could imply some structural features, but no covering specialization was identified from available material and its complete text was not read. A separate related open-path census deals with different boundary operators and does not imply this cyclic result. Published research-record comparisons with affine traveling-CA clock factors and finite-ring reversible first-order rule classifications address distinct maps. No proof of global historical uniqueness is claimed.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The Rule-90R model is a standard reversible counterpart of a classical cellular automaton. Its global finite-volume dynamics exhibits a sharp boundary-parity dichotomy: two freely rotating symbolic rows in even volume versus an unavoidable Jordan defect in odd volume. The all-time fixed-point formula and its Möbius inversion determine every primitive temporal orbit of every ring size, while the odd-circumference relation forces a non-obvious exact correspondence between distinct periods. This is an infinite-family natural classification beyond basic invertibility or a single initial-state picture.
