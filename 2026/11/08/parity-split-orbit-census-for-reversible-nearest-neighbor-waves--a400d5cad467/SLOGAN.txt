A reversible nearest-neighbor wave automaton on a characteristic-two cyclic ring is conjugate to two independent cyclic shifts when the circumference is even, but acquires one nontrivial size-two unit Jordan block when it is odd. This parity defect yields the complete fixed-point and primitive-period census, including \(P_n(2\ell)=(Q-1)P_n(\ell)\) for every divisor \(\ell\) of odd \(n\).
