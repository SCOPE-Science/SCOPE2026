For two independent arclength-uniform points on a regular polygon approaching a circle, the probability of being within \(\pi^2t/n^2\) of diameter has two different parity-dependent limits after multiplication by \(n\). The even limit begins like \(t^2/2\), while the odd limit has an exact gap up to \(t=1/4\) and opens like \(16(t-1/4)^2/9\); both reach the circular square-root envelope at large \(t\).
