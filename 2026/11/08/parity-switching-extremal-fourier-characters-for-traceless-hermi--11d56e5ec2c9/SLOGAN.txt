The traceless Hermitian rank-one nilpotent Cayley walk has a sharp nontrivial absolute Fourier eigenvalue, with an exact parity switch in every maximizing character: rank-one square-zero Hermitian frequencies for odd matrix size, versus rank-one nonzero-trace Hermitian frequencies for even size. Every maximizing eigenvalue is negative, and the resulting sharp normalized \(\ell^2\) contraction factor is explicit for every prime power and matrix size at least three.
