In asynchronous closed-neighborhood minority dynamics on cocktail-party graphs, even numbers of pairs have only absorbing maximum cuts, while odd numbers have one nonabsorbing recurrent class with \((m+3)\binom m{(m-1)/2}\) states. For odd \(m\), the recurrent chain is a doubled-edge subdivision of a middle-level inclusion graph and has exact spectral gap \(\bigl(d+1-\sqrt{(d+1)^2-2}\bigr)/(2m)\), where \(d=(m+1)/2\).
