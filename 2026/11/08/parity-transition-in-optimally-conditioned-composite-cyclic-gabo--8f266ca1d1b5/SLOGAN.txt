For every composite cyclic order \(N\ge6\), the optimally conditioned constant-plus-one-impulse Gabor tomography window has a sharp parity transition: the unique odd-order optimizer is \(z=-\sqrt N\), while the even-order optimizers are \(z=-\sqrt N e^{\pm i\pi/(2N)}\). The exact optimal condition numbers are \((\sqrt N-1)/\sin(\pi/(2N))\) and \((\sqrt N-\cos(\pi/(2N)))/\sin(\pi/(2N))\), respectively, and the complete equality cases are classified.
