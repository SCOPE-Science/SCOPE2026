For finite-field affine Lagrangian graphs with invertible symmetric slopes, the counting-measure Radon transform has exactly determined Fourier singular values and kernel \(\{g(x)+h(y):\sum g=\sum h=0\}\). On its recoverable nonconstant component it is perfectly tight in odd dimension, whereas in even dimension its sharp condition number is \((1-q^{1-d})^{-1/2}\).
