For the strict 0-or-3-neighbors public-goods game on a cubic prism \(C_n\square K_2\), every non-all-active equilibrium is exactly an even-parity cyclic tiling by gaps of lengths \(2\) and \(3\), with two forced layer colorings. The prism with \(n=5\) alone has a unique equilibrium; for large \(n\), the fraction of equilibrium profiles is asymptotic to \((\rho/4)^n\), where \(\rho^3-\rho-1=0\) and \(\rho>1\).
