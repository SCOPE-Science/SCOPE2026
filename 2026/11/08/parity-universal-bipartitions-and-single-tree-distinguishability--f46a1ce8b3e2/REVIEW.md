# Mathematical review

## Correctness

PASS. Every edge traversal flips bipartition color; this supplies the exact conditional attachment probabilities under every allowed adaptive walk. The odd case is the elementary two-color Pólya recursion and the even case the Eulerian insertion recursion, both with the correct size-two initial condition. The second-moment recurrence is exact for the even law and the uniform integer law bounds the odd small-imbalance event, giving the explicit total-variation separation on unrooted unlabeled trees. Rational enumeration of each tree history for walk lengths zero through three and orders up to six passed 612 finite checks; finite experiments are not substituted for proof.

Classical two-color urn and Eulerian formulas cover the projected count processes once the graph-to-urn reduction is made; those ingredients are explicitly credited and not asserted new. Targeted mathematical-literature and research-record comparisons returned no covering graph-level statement, although a comprehensive historical-priority audit has not occurred. A broader random-tree bipartition theorem not accessed here remains a residual risk.

## Value

PASS. The exact formula is a natural complete classification of a graph isomorphism invariant for an entire, topology-sensitive family of attachment rules, not an arbitrary finite slice. The observable is invariant under all walk lengths and adaptive neighbor biases of one parity despite potentially striking differences in degree statistics. The classification produces a quantitative and uniform statistical distinction using one unlabelled tree, with an explicit worst-case misclassification bound. This bridges work on anomalous network growth and classical urn recursions without claiming a new urn limit theorem.

## Scope and evidence

The claims concern two-vertex initial trees; fixed edge-traversal parity at each arrival; uniform starting vertex; arbitrary adaptive nearest-neighbor walk transition kernels; and every integer order. Exact-variance and finite-tree enumeration evidence are provided in the main result and standalone script. Sources: arXiv:2604.01540v1; arXiv:2403.20185; arXiv:2605.26669; DOI 10.1002/rsa.70082. Remaining concerns are historical coverage of similar graph parity lemmas and interpretation of older color-class results, not unproved analytic steps.
