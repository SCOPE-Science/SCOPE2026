For every finite completely zero-simple Rees semigroup, the monoid of partial inner automorphisms is the identity-adjoined Brandt semigroup indexed by the nonzero sandwich entries and with structure group \(G/Z(G)\), except for the one-entry case where the identity is already present. Thus two such monoids are isomorphic exactly when the sandwich matrices have the same number of nonzero entries and the structure groups have isomorphic central quotients.
