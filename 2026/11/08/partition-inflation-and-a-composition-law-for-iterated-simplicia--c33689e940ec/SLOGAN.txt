Two successive simplicial \(r\)-tupling operations compose not to a single larger tupling, but to a uniform inflation: \(D_r(D_sX)\cong(D_{rs}X)^{[\frac{(rs)!}{r!(s!)^r}]}\). The partition model yields an integral homotopy decomposition through the classical vertex-inflation theorem and shows that reversing the two parameters can change even the number of connected components.
