For all complete split graphs \(K_r\vee\overline K_n\) and path lengths \(t\geq5\), the path-free complex is an explicit wedge of spheres of one dimension, with an integral leaf-stabilizer module and a cycle-type formula for the full automorphism character. Despite potentially many sphere generators, none of its positive-degree homology is fixed by the entire part-preserving symmetry group.
