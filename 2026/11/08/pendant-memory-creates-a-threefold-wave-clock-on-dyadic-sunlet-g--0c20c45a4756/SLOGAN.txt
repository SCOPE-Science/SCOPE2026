For the reversible characteristic-two graph wave on a cycle with one private leaf at every vertex, dyadic cycle lengths \(n=2^r\ge4\) force an exact threefold clock: the update has order \(3n\), and every nonzero state has primitive period \(3\cdot2^j\) for some \(0\le j\le r\). The full labelled-state populations are \(P_3=q^4-1\) and \(P_{3\cdot2^j}=q^{4\cdot2^j}-q^{4\cdot2^{j-1}}\) for \(1\le j\le r\), with zero as the only fixed point.
