For every central-character pair of \(UT_3(\mathbb F_7)\), the projective secant varieties of the equivariant bilinear channel model are nondefective at every order \(1\le r\le16\), with dimension \(145r-1\). The sixteenth secant has dimension \(2319\) and codimension \(81\) in \(\mathbb P^{2400}\); filling at the next order is not established.
