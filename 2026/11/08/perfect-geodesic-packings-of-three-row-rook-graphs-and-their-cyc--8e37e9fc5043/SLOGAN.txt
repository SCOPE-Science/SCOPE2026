For every \(n\ge3\), the three-row rook graph \(K_3\square K_n\) has geodesic packing number \(n\), and all maximum packings correspond bijectively to fixed-point-free column permutations with properly three-colored cycles. Their cycle-count generating function is \(\sum_{n\ge0}A_n(y)t^n/n!=(1-2t)^{-y}(1+t)^{-2y}\); this identifies a graph-geodesic interpretation of a previously known integer triangle (OEIS A137946).
