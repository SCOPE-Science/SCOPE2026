The perfect-matching complex of the four-dimensional cube (equivalently, the four-by-four toroidal square grid) is a pure seven-dimensional complex with integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{54}S^3\vee\bigvee^{21}S^4\). Exact acyclic Morse reductions and unimodular boundary elimination prove the mixed-dimensional sphere wedge and its non-shellability.
