For the Boolean incidence family of all fixed-point-free permutations, every support of at most \(n-2\) off-diagonal matrix cells realizes exactly its internal matchings as traces. Consequently the shattered supports are precisely the partial derangements of size at most \(n-2\), with an explicit rook-number polynomial and an \(e^{-c^2}\) critical sampling law at \(m\sim c\sqrt n\).
