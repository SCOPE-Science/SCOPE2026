Transpose-fixed spaces of symmetric rook boards with every diagonal square are contractible on odd orders and wedges of \(p(G)\) spheres in dimension \(k-1\) on even orders \(2k\), where \(p(G)\) counts perfect matchings of the underlying graph. Their nonzero reduced integral homology is the signed perfect-matching permutation module, giving \((2k-1)!!\) spheres on full square boards.
