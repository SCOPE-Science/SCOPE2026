# Scientific review: perfect-square survival under Hamming-sphere absorption

## Correctness — PASS
The finite-dimensional contractive generator has undamped modes precisely on adjacency eigenspaces that vanish on all positively lossy sites. Its stable complement decays. The coordinate-permutation invariant line in each Walsh band is either entirely loss-free or has zero projection onto loss-free modes, leading to an exact zero test by the Krawtchouk polynomial. The degree-two polynomial has roots if and only if the dimension is a square. The exact formula, normalizations, rates, integer boundaries, and Gaussian asymptotic were checked. The accompanying standalone full-band/nullspace and full-generator numerical computations both passed; finite tests are not offered as infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence
Moore–Russell (2001) provides untrapped hypercube coherent dynamics, Krovi–Brun (2006) provides general dark-state/hitting-time mechanisms, and Feinsilver–Kocik (2007) provides symmetric Hadamard–Krawtchouk identities. None of the examined relevant sections states the arbitrary-positive-rate Hamming-shell survival law or the second-shell perfect-square detector effect. Published research searches on shell restrictions and hypercube quantum networks brought up related spectral results but not this absorption statement. This result is explicitly an application of known general spectral principles and could be historically covered by a less easily found paper; its originality is provisional rather than certified uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact law solves a natural network-detection question for an infinite graph family and every full Hamming detection shell, showing that placing a detector at all sites two graph steps from the source still permits permanent coherent escape in exactly the perfect-square dimensions. The nonzero probability is rate-invariant even for unequal positive losses, admits a closed binomial expression, and has a quantified sharp size law. This is a structural arithmetic boundary for physical monitoring, not a small tabulated parameter gain.

## Scope and residual risks
There is no claim concerning arbitrary initial states, arbitrary partial absorber sets, finite-time capture rates, dephasing, or disordered hopping. The existing dark-space principle and Krawtchouk transform are both acknowledged; an independently located source combining them for precisely these absorbing shells could defeat originality. A single unindexed paper cannot be excluded by database retrieval alone.
