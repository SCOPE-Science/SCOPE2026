A quantum particle launched at one corner of a uniformly coupled binary hypercube, with arbitrary positive irreversible losses on every vertex at a fixed Hamming distance, has an exact Krawtchouk-zero formula for its never-captured probability. For loss at distance two, that probability is positive exactly when the hypercube dimension is a perfect square; at dimension \(d=s^2\) it equals \(2^{1-d}\binom{d}{(d-s)/2}\) and decays as \(\sqrt{8/(\pi e)}/s\).
