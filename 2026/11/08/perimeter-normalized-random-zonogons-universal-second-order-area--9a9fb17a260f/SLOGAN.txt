For independently uniform segment directions, the area deficit of a perimeter-normalized random zonogon has a universal non-Gaussian second-order law whose only dependence on diffuse segment lengths is the ratio \(\mathbb E L^2/(\mathbb E L)^2\). A single segment of central-limit scale changes the limiting spectral law even when that ratio's deterministic analogue has the same limit, sharply exposing the boundary of universality.
