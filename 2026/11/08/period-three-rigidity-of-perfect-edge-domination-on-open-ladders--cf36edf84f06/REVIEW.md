# Scientific review: open-ladder perfect edge domination

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim has the exact domain \(L_n=P_2\square P_n\), \(n\ge2\), and explicitly distinguishes perfect edge domination from efficient edge domination. The three vertex-state rules follow from the local identity \(d_F(u)+d_F(v)=1\) on each omitted edge, and their converse reconstructs the chosen edges uniquely. The two-column automaton tests the true degree-two endpoint constraints and degree-three interior constraints. Its complete reachable/co-reachable reduction has only a six-cycle and an all-black self-loop. The permitted start/end phases give counts \(4,2,2\) in the three residue classes; the selected-edge weight is \(n\) in every nontrivial path, using six-periodic extension with an exact increment of six. Full-edge weight is \(3n-2\). The included program independently exhausts the edge subsets for \(2\le n\le7\) and checks the finite automaton directly. Unrestricted validity depends on the finite-state reduction, not on finite-length extrapolation.

## Originality
**PASS, provisional priority.** Lin, Lozin, Moyano and Szwarcfiter's accessible 2017 full text (section 2) states the three-state representation and general complexity results, not the open-ladder census. Grippo, Lin, Moyano and Vera (first public 2026-07-04) give algorithms for general series-parallel graphs; this provides computational coverage but does not state or imply the exact periodic closed polynomial without applying and analyzing the algorithm on this family. An earlier exact census for cyclic ladders concerns a different edge set and different boundary conditions, and cannot be converted into the present open classification by mere parameter substitution. Semantic and literal searches of the literature and mathematical research records found no equivalent open-ladder statement. The closest 2026 preprint was accessible only through its abstract and secondary descriptive material; the possibility of a hidden special-case example in its inaccessible full text is a real residual risk, not claimed absent. This review does not establish definitive historical priority.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** A natural infinite graph family has an exact complete enumeration and structural classification of *all* perfect edge dominating sets, not only an algorithm or a minimum parameter. Their multiplicity remains bounded but jumps exactly with length modulo three, and every proper set is minimum while every such set fails the stronger dominating-induced-matching condition. The endpoint-dependence is substantive: forbidding the two closing edges permits optimal configurations at every length, in contrast to the cyclic case's stricter divisibility. These facts are not stated by general algorithms and provide a sharp exact boundary phenomenon.

## Limitations and outstanding checks
The proof does not cover wider grids, twisted or cyclic closures, weighted enumeration, or arbitrary generalized series-parallel graphs. Historical priority is provisional because one highly relevant 2026 preprint's full text and some unindexed literature were not available. The embedded verifier is fully reproducible, but mathematical assessment here is not independent validation.
