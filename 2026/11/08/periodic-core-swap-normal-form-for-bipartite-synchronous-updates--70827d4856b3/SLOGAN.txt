Any finite cross-coupled synchronous system \(F(x,y)=(f(y),g(x))\) has a periodic subsystem conjugate to a reversible swap wave determined by \(f\circ g\), with all odd/even periodic-point counts explicit. In particular, \(K\) fixed configurations force exactly \(K(K-1)\) immediately primitive two-periodic configurations; this counts mixed majority two-cycles on all odd-degree bipartite graphs.
