For any fixed separation \(h\ge1\), arithmetic-periodic sets with gaps greater than \(h\) can capture an arbitrarily large fraction of the leading \(X\log X\) divisor mass, although no single period captures all of it. An explicit primorial construction has missing mass \(O_h((\log Y)^{-(3-2\sqrt2)})\), distinguishing periodic structural approximation from the already known unrestricted extremal asymptotic.
