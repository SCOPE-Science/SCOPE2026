For synchronous integer diffusion on a labelled complete graph, the exact number of already-periodic configurations modulo common translation has exponential generating function \((1-e^{-z})/(1-z+z^2-ze^z)\). All but the constant configuration occur in primitive two-cycles, and the periodic-state count grows like \(0.3309832060\,n!\,(2.1297213573)^n\).
