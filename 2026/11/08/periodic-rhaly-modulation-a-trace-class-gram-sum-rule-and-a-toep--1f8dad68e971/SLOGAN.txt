For every bounded periodic modulation of the discrete Cesàro operator, the squared modulus differs from its period-mean Cesàro counterpart by a **trace-class** operator with an exact harmonic trace, and its essential singular spectrum is \([0,2\sqrt\mu]\). Nonconstant periodic modulations lie outside the Hardy Toeplitz algebra even though their positive moduli belong to its commutator ideal; alternating unit-modulus phases give an exact same-modulus counterexample.
