For an \(s\)-coordinate cyclically coupled interval map, every iterate has exactly \(a_{m/\gcd(m,s)}^{\gcd(m,s)}\) fixed points, where \(a_n\) counts the periodic points of the one-coordinate return map. A finite trace model transfers to an explicit tensor-determinant zeta function, with an exterior-square factor in the two-coordinate case; this proves rationality for Cournot maps whenever the return-map zeta function is rational.
