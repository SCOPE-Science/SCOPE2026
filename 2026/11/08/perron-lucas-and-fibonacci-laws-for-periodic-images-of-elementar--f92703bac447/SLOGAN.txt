For binary elementary Rule 126 on indexed periodic rings, the number of distinct one-step outputs has an exact 45-state rational generating function and a Perron–Lucas minus half-Fibonacci–Lucas closed form. Its sharp two-term asymptotic is \(a_n=\lambda^n-\frac12\varphi^n+O(1)\), with \(\lambda=1.7548776662\ldots\).
