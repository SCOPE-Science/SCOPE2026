For an irreducible coordinatewise Lipschitz bound of Perron radius \(1\), all fixed-point distance vectors are synchronized along one positive Perron ray; in strictly convex coordinate spaces the fixed set becomes an affine isometric graph. Over real scalar coordinates it is exactly a possibly degenerate signed Perron interval, and every prescribed sign and compact interval is realized on a full-dimensional box; an explicit resolvent bound controls approximate synchronization.
