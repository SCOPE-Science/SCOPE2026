When Poisson resets erase a particle's position but not its Brownian stochastic diffusivity, its position escapes stationarity yet has an exact normal-times-Laplace \(\sqrt t\)-scaled limit with a logarithmic core and stretched-exponential tails. At proportional observation times \(t\) and \(ct\), ordinary position memory disappears but squared-displacement correlation converges to the universal value \(2/(17c)\) for every fixed \(c>1\).
