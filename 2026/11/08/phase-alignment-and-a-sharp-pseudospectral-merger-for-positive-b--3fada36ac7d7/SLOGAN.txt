For an ergodic Bernoulli-shift composition operator with any constant positive bidiagonal matrix weight, the full infinite-dimensional resolvent norm is exactly the inverse norm of a finite real matrix evaluated at the spectral radius. For a two-dimensional weight, two distinct spectral circles merge into a connected open pseudospectrum precisely above \(\big(\sqrt{(b-a)^2+\gamma^2}-\gamma\big)/2\).
