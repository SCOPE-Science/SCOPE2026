At a neutral invasion threshold in a two-species Lotka–Volterra system with staggered periodic pulses, the invasion multiplier does not depend on pulse timing, but the universal critical extinction law is \(ny(nT^+)\to[(f-ec/b)\mathcal I_\phi]^{-1}\). The exact derivative \(\mathcal I_\phi'=(1-m_y)E(\phi)\) shows that the critical extinction amplitude changes strictly monotonically with the relative pulse phase whenever \(m_y\ne1\).
