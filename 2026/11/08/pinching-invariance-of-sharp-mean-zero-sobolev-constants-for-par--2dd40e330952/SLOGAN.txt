Identifying the two endpoints of a parallel-edge metric graph creates a rose bouquet with a different cycle rank but **exactly the same** sharp arclength-mean-zero Sobolev embedding constant \(\sum_i L_i^3/(12T^2)+M(T-M)/(4T)\), where \(T=\sum_iL_i\) and \(M=\max_iL_i\). For at least three edges, every norming point is a midpoint of a longest edge and even the corresponding Green extremizers survive pinching unchanged; the two-edge case instead has every point norming on the original circle.
