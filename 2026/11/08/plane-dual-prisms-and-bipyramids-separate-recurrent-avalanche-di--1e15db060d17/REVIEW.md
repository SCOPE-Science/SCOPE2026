# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The prism recurrent state follows from an explicit Dhar burning order and yields a single toppling away from its sink. In the dual bipyramid, the initial burning test requires a rim site at its maximum stable height; every toppling at the other apex then forces a rim toppling and therefore sink loss. Plane duality gives group isomorphism. Exact \(n=4\) counts arise from exhaustive burning enumeration independently cross-checked by a full addition-orbit census, both supplied as executable source.

**Originality — PASS on examined evidence.** The accessible full paper of Chan et al. proves the sandpile group and rotor-routing duality but not a comparison of zero-loss principal avalanches. The accessible full paper of Austin et al. defines and enumerates multivariate avalanche polynomials for trees, cycles, wheels and complete graphs but not the primal–dual prism/bipyramid separation. Actual published-result searches for avalanche, planar duality, cube, octahedron, prism and bipyramid returned no explicit equivalent statement. A historical-priority risk remains for unindexed local sandpile studies and the unavailable full text of Cori–Rossin (2000); absence in a search is not a proof of global priority.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The infinite-family constructive countercomparison separates a highly natural, dynamical absorption observable from the critical-group invariant under planar duality. The cubical example exhibits a strictly positive exactly enumerated burst-free probability versus zero on the group-equivalent dual, a motivated boundary of group-based avalanche characterization rather than an arbitrary isolated statistic.

The result concerns finite graphs, specified sinks and single-grain principal additions. The comparison does not establish a thermodynamic critical exponent or results for other sink choices.
