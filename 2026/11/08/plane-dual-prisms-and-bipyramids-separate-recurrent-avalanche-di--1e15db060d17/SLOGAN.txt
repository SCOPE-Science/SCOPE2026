For every \(n\ge4\), planar-dual \(n\)-prisms and \(n\)-bipyramids have isomorphic sandpile groups but opposite behavior for positive-toppling, zero-loss avalanches with specified rim and apex sinks. The cube admits exactly \(64\) such events out of \(2688\) equally weighted recurrent additions, whereas its octahedral dual admits none out of \(1920\).
