# Review

**Correctness — PASS.** The geometric-lag coupling is exact for the independent parent law. The proof verifies the finite-start product, finite-dependence approximation and exact overlapping-window pair law. The anti-clustering bound together with the classical local-dependence Poisson estimate supplies the infinite-dimensional convergence; finite checker assertions independently test algebraic identities and do not constitute the proof. The stationary process exists on a two-sided iid lag space, and its full cut event is a measurable limit of finite windows.

**Originality — PASS (subject to historical priority).** The source model of Aguech–Mahmoud–Mohamed–Yang (2025, DOI 10.1017/nws.2025.3) concerns degrees. Tang–Wang–Wu (arXiv:2611.02329, publicly dated November 2, 2026) already defines the same terminal cuts and proves fixed-bias renewal and recurrence, so none of those assertions is claimed new. The new statements are the \(\beta\downarrow0\) Poisson process and exact activation asymptotic; searched research-index and ledger records did not yield a covering result. A general rare-renewal theorem could eventually establish priority coverage.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem identifies the exceptionally long typical gap to the first terminal bottleneck as age bias vanishes and shows it has a memoryless Poisson scaling limit, quantifying a global connectivity threshold not captured by degree profiles or existence of fixed-bias cuts. The eta prefactor is necessary for the unit-rate normalization.

**Limitations.** Fixed parent multiplicity and independent exponential-age choices. No claims about random-walk scaling, degree statistics, or variable parent multiplicity. Historical priority and independent verification remain open.
