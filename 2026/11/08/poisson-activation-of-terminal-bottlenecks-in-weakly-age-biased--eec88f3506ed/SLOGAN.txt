For fixed parent multiplicity, rare terminal cuts in weakly age-biased recursive trees become a unit-rate Poisson point process after rescaling birth time by \(\rho_\beta=[(1-e^{-\beta})\prod_{h\ge1}(1-e^{-\beta h})]^m\). This identifies an essential-exponential first-bottleneck scale \(\rho_\beta\sim(2\pi\beta)^{m/2}e^{-m\pi^2/(6\beta)}\), beyond the previously known fixed-parameter renewal description.
