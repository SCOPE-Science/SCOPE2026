For proposition-free one-step counting logic on a sparse random pointed directed graph, all limiting truth probabilities form a countable dense subset of a zero-dimensional Poisson-subsums Cantor set. The number of precision-\(\varepsilon\) intervals needed to cover that set has logarithm asymptotic to \((\log2)\log(1/\varepsilon)/\log\log(1/\varepsilon)\), and the finite-model probability spectra approach it at rate at most \((\lambda+\lambda^2)/n\).
