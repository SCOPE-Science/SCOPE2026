Under independent approvals, a committee meeting distinctive representation is, with exponentially small error, exactly a choice of \(k\) candidates having the Hare-quota number of supporters. At the critical scale of \(\sqrt n\) candidates and approval propensity \(1/k+\beta/\sqrt n\), the number of qualifying committees converges to \(\binom{P_\mu}{k}\) for an explicit Poisson variable \(P_\mu\).
