For a regular cross-polytope projected onto a Haar-random subspace of any fixed codimension \(q\), the losses of the outermost antipodal-pair radii form a Poisson endpoint spectrum on the scale \(n^{-(1+2/q)}\). Its outer radius obeys an explicit Weibull law with convergence of all positive moments; in three dimensions the planar octahedron-shadow circumradius also has an exact elementary CDF.
