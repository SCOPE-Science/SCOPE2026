For Luce rankings with any fixed finite number of positive constant-weight blocks of asymptotically positive proportions, the blockwise fixed-point counts converge jointly in total variation to independent Poisson laws, with exact intensities \(\lambda_j=e^{-w_jt_{j-1}}-e^{-w_jt_j}\). Their sum realizes the diagonal-density Poisson prediction for this natural class, and a two-block bias produces a strictly monotone mean fixed-point count.
