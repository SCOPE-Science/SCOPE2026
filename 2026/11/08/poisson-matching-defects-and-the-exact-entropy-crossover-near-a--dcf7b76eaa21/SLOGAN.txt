When the connection range of a uniform geometric circle graph approaches its maximal value as \(r_n=1/2-\lambda/(2n^2)\), the missing-edge graph is asymptotically a Poisson-size random matching and converges in total variation to an independent-edge graph. Despite dependent geometric edges, its Shannon entropy agrees even at constant order: \(H(G_n)=\lambda\log_2 n+(\lambda/2)\log_2(e/\lambda)+o(1)\).
