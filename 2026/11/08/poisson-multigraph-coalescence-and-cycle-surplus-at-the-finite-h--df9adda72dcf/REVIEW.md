**Correctness (PASS).** For each exact hub pair the rare-category occupancy probabilities are \(a^2/n+O(n^{-3/2})\); one-trial Poissonization with fixed number of categories gives a total-variation estimate. Right vertices of degree at least three occur with probability \(O(n^{-1/2})\), and the Euler cycle-rank identity gives exactly \(\sum_eN_e-m+|\Pi|\) after suppression of degree-two right vertices. Fixed-hub degree-one counts concentrate after division by \(\sqrt n\); a uniform second-moment bound gives convergence of mean largest-component size. All relevant boundary cases, including isolated right vertices, zero pair counts, and \(m=2\), have been checked. The finite checker does not substitute for the analytical argument.

The projected simple-graph limit is prior mathematics, not part of the originality claim. Federico's power-growth regime assumes both sides grow and does not cover fixed \(m\); published equivalence theorems concern the simple projection and do not by themselves determine cycle multiplicities. A finite, non-degenerate hub partition survives in the limit, creating macroscopic random component-size ratios. The separate cycle-surplus law identifies fourth- and longer-cycle multiplicities invisible in the limiting simple hub graph. The theorem supplies a natural joint topological and size classification, rather than a one-off statistic or a purely numerical threshold.
