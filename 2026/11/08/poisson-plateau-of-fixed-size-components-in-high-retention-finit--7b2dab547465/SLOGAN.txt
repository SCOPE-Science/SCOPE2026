For fixed bandwidth \(m\ge2\), bond percolation on the cycle power \(C_n^m\) at \(nq_n^{m(m+1)}\to\lambda\) has independent Poisson counts of all fixed component sizes \(k\ge m\). The intensity is exceptionally \(m\lambda\) for size \(m\) and exactly \(\lambda\) for every larger fixed size, a flat rare-component profile generated by a sharp host cut classification.
