# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof identifies the exact averaged channel, proves strict contraction of the failed-probe map from spectral cyclicity, derives the moment identities, and explicitly solves the Poisson equation. The rational identity \(\sum_j(\sum_{k\ne j}(E_j-E_k)^{-1})^2=2\sum_{j<k}(E_j-E_k)^{-2}\) removes all cross terms. The flux corollary follows from a trigonometric identity; the exceptional-flux case has an independent two-level geometric-law derivation. The packaged program passed 158 exact and direct numerical checks. None of the numerical checks is used as a proof of the all-dimensional quantifier.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The 2020 Kessler–Barkai–Ziegler random-probing paper provides a general superoperator expression for second moments, and the 2008 Varbanov–Krovi–Brun paper analyzes Poisson-time hitting, but the inspected materials do not state the all-dimensional flat-overlap inverse-gap variance formula or the exact flux singularity. The 2019 Yin–Ziegler–Thiel–Barkai work gives divergent variances under a deterministic sampling protocol, not this Poisson-time identity. The 2024 Wang et al. paper gives the same chiral triangle Hamiltonian with periodic sampling and flux-dependent degeneracies; that background and the integer mean-return phenomenon are credited rather than reclaimed. The general 2020 resolvent method could implicitly yield the new identity, and an equivalent explicit formula elsewhere is not excluded.

**Value — PASS.** The exact reduction of a first-detection fluctuation observable to the spectral statistic \(\sum_{j<k}(E_j-E_k)^{-2}\) holds for arbitrarily large finite simple spectra, independent of eigenvector phases and at all probe rates. It gives direct gap-sensitivity and divergent near-crossing response. The triangle corollary supplies sharp flux optimizers and a singular contrast with the finite variance at the precisely degenerate flux, relevant to finite-resolution quantum return measurements. The result is structural rather than a numerical enumeration or a reformulated mean-return identity.

Closest literature: arXiv:0803.3446, arXiv:1903.03394, arXiv:2012.01763, arXiv:2402.15843. Limits: perfect projective detection, flat spectral weight, simple spectrum in the general theorem, exponential waiting, finite-dimensional dynamics; no claim for time-to-return variance or independent verification. Novelty is best-knowledge, not a publication-priority certificate.
