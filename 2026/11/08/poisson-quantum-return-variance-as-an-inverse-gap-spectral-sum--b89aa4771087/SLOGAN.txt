For ideal Poisson-time quantum return measurements with equal detector weight on every distinct energy level, the attempt-count variance is exactly \(n(n-1)+(8r^2/n)\sum_{j<k}(E_j-E_k)^{-2}\). In the magnetic three-site ring this becomes \(6+2r^2/[J^2\sin^2(3\alpha)]\) off degeneracies, quantifying a sharp inverse-square fluctuation divergence despite a finite variance precisely at each degenerate flux.
