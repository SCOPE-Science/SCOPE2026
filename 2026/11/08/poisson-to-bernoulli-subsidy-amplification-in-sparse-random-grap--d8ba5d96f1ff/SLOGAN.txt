In the sparse unit-valued graphical fair-division model, the minimum envy-eliminating subsidy has a two-point critical limit: at \(p_n=(\log n+\log\log n+c)/(2n)\), its normalized cost converges to \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1-e^{-e^{-c}/4})\). A Poisson number of isolated-edge obstructions triggers payment to \(\Theta(\sqrt{n/\log n})\) otherwise goodless agents; the deterministic seed and isolated-agent mechanism are known background, but this random-graph amplification law is the result.
