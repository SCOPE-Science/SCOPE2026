## Mathematical value — PASS

The critical double-scaling is determined by a natural competition between a finite expected number of open spokes and failed rim bonds. The nested \(\lambda^2\) spoke scale distinguishes coverage of almost every vertex from coverage of *every* vertex: the former tends to one while the latter has a nontrivial probability \(e^{-1/\alpha}\). This precise gap-probability phenomenon is a natural reliability and outbreak-size invariant of a canonical hub-and-ring topology. It is neither a small change in a known numerical table nor an arbitrary subset of parameters; the scales arise from the typical and smallest Poisson arc lengths.
