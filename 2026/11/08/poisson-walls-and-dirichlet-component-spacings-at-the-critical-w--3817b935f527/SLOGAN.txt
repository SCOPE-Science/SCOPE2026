At the critical deletion scale \(nq_n^3\to\lambda\), bond percolation on the square of a cycle has \(K_n\Rightarrow\max\{1,\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda)\}\) components and connectivity probability tending to \(e^{-\lambda}(1+\lambda)\). Conditional on \(k\ge2\) components, their cyclically ordered relative lengths converge to \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,\ldots,1)\), because the only asymptotically relevant disconnections are Poisson-distributed three-edge walls.
