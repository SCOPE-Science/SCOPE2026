The integral second homology of a finite projective-plane incidence graph's scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex is its graph-cycle lattice twisted by the sign of point–line duality, so every polarity has trace equal to its number of absolute points minus one. For the Fano plane this gives an absolutely irreducible eight-dimensional representation of the full 336-element symmetry group, and on \(\operatorname{PG}(2,4)\) it distinguishes orthogonal and unitary polarities by traces \(4\) and \(8\).
