For three house applicants whose first two rankings are exact reversals, a completely envy-free assignment exists exactly when the third ranking is not single-dipped on their house axis; otherwise the minimum number of envious agents, ordered envy pairs, and maximum individual envy are all exactly one. With the third ranking uniform, the obstruction probability is \(2^{m-1}/m!\), and the expected first inventory size permitting full envy-freeness is \((e^2+1)/2\).
