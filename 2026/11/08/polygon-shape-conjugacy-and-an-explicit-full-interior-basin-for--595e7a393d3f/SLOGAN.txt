A geometric calibration of three-parameter polygonal affine oscillators makes every admissible polygon with the same ordered edge speeds **time-preservingly conjugate** on an explicitly invariant domain containing its entire punctured interior. The common radial gauge obeys \(\dot r=-a(r-1)\); this yields exponential attraction, shape-independent switching times, and transverse multiplier \(e^{-a\sum_i1/\omega_i}\).
