An explicit degree-two accumulative backward shift is mixing on every \(\ell^p\), \(1<p<\infty\), but all its orbits visit a fixed nonempty coordinate cylinder only \(O((\log N)^{p-1})\) times up to \(N\), so none is upper frequently hypercyclic. The same block-sum matrix satisfies the Frequent Hypercyclicity Criterion on \(c_0\), and a divergence-of-iterated-gaps criterion fully characterizes mixing/hypercyclicity for the \(\ell^p\) family.
