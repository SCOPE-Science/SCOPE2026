# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

For every real polynomial graph $z=t+iP(t)$ of degree $m\ge2$ and every signal cutoff $d\ge1$, the Laurent obstruction rules out nonconstant rational quotients of two equal-modulus complex polynomials. The quotient argument handles zeros by cancellation and the zero signal separately. The kernel of the restriction map in rectangular bidegree $d$ is the multiple space of $F_P(U,V)=U-V-2iP((U+V)/2)$, hence its dimension is $\max(d-m+1,0)^2$. Hermitian polarization yields $D_{m,d}=(d+1)^2-\max(d-m+1,0)^2$, and real polynomial evaluation gives generic $D_{m,d}$-point interpolation on any interval. Rank-two kernel exclusion and compactness establish qualitative fixed-design stability. Reflection across an affine line supplies the converse boundary case.

Separate finite-field rank tests checked forty $(m,d)$ pairs, $2\le m\le6$, $0\le d\le7$, and five independently selected full-rank node configurations. These corroborate, but do not prove, the infinite parameter statements. No optimal nonlinear sampling count, uniformly conditioned design or formal external proof certificate is asserted.

## Originality — PASS, best of knowledge

Earlier published parabola theorems prove the $P(t)=t^2$ uniqueness special case, including stronger deterministic finite-node statements for that parabola. It is not claimed as new. An earlier monomial harmonic-level theorem has an analogous rectangular ideal-kernel count, but its curves $\operatorname{Im}(z^n)=c$ permit rational ambiguities on a single level and are not the same graph family. The primary results of Chalendar–Partington (2024), Liehr (2024), Przybyłek–Siedlecki (2019), Pakovich–Shparlinski (2018) and Orevkov–Pakovich (2024) study overlapping but logically different curve configurations, unrestricted-node interpolation, or rational lemniscate intersections. None of the inspected statements entails the full real-polynomial-graph dichotomy and exact class-wide profile rank. The underlying principal-ideal degree calculation is standard algebra, not an independent novelty claim.

An unindexed stronger result on rational functions of constant modulus over real algebraic curves remains a historical-priority risk. Negative searches do not certify novelty.
