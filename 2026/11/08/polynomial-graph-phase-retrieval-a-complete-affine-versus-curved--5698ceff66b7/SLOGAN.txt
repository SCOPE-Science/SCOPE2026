A real polynomial graph \(z=t+iP(t)\) recovers every complex polynomial from its entire modulus profile up to global phase exactly when \(P\) is nonlinear. For degree-at-most-\(d\) signals and graph degree \(m\ge2\), the exact dimension—and sharp generic linear sampling count—of the intensity profile space is \((d+1)^2-\max\{d-m+1,0\}^2\), with full coefficient-matrix lifting becoming injective precisely at \(m>d\).
