For the nonunital null-filiform algebra \(t\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^{n+1})\), characteristic \(p\) forces each local derivation to annihilate all powers \(t^j\) with \(p\mid j\), and the remaining columns form a nested-support random matrix. Its exact maximal-rank probability is \((1-q^{-1})^{n\bmod p}\prod_{h=1}^{\lfloor n/p\rfloor}(1-q^{-(h+1)})^{p-1}\), with a different strictly positive limit in each residue class of \(n\pmod p\).
