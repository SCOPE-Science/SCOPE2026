Odd nonsquare abundant integers failing Ross's \(\{-1,1\}\)-perfect divisor-sign condition have **positive lower natural density**, not merely infinitely many examples. For the core \(a=11025\), a coprime-cofactor sieve proves the explicit bound \(\liminf_{X\to\infty}\#\{n\le X:\text{odd nonsquare abundant, not signed-perfect}\}/X\ge3\rho/(4a)>0\), where \(\rho=\prod_{p\le91884}(1-p^{-1})\).
