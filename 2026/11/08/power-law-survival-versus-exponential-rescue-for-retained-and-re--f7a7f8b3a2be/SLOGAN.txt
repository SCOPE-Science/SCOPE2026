Conserving a heavy-tailed boundary-contact threshold across Poisson position resets gives \(\Pr(T>t)\sim\Pr(\Lambda>Jt)\), where \(J=\sqrt{Dr}e^{-R\sqrt{r/D}}\). Resetting both contact progress and a *freshly redrawn* threshold instead gives an exponentially tailed reaction time, even when the conserved-threshold mean is infinite.
