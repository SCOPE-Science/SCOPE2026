All coefficients are explicitly defined and independently reconstructed. At power-of-two index, every nonconstant reciprocal coefficient is even after normalization by two; the central coefficient has valuation two before normalization; and the Franel endpoint has valuation one. Eisenstein applies to the normalized reciprocal and reciprocal substitution preserves irreducibility. The proof covers all positive exponents, without relying on testing.

The index family is not an arbitrary small slice: powers of two are exactly the binomial rows with no odd internal coefficients. The result proves irreducibility in unbounded degrees \(2^{m-1}\) for a named, natural Franel polynomial family explicitly conjectured irreducible for every index. A concrete uniform irreducibility theorem, rather than a finite census or restatement of a textbook criterion, results.
