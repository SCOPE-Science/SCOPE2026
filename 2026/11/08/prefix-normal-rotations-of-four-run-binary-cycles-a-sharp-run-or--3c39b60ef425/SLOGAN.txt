A four-run binary necklace has a prefix-normal periodic rotation exactly when the differences between its two one-run lengths and its two following zero-run lengths have opposite signs or one difference vanishes. The exact number of admissible necklaces has generating function \(z^4/((1-z)^2(1-z^2)^2)\), while inadmissible necklaces are counted by the same series shifted by two positions; the two populations are asymptotically equal.
