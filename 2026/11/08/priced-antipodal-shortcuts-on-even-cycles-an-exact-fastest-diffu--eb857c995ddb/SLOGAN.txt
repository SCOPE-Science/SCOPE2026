For an even cycle with an antipodal perfect matching, every fastest-diffusion design is edge-orbit uniform, and the sharp shortcut-price cutoff is \(c_m^*=\csc^2(\pi/(2m))\). Below it, the matching is strictly funded; at the cutoff a whole interval is optimal; above it every shortcut is discarded. At any fixed positive shortcut price, an asymptotically vanishing \(O(m^{-2})\) fraction of the budget spent on shortcuts yields a fourfold leading spectral-gap improvement over the best cycle-only design.
