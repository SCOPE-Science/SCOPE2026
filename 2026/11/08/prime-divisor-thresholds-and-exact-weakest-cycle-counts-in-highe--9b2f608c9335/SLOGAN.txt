For the higher-dimensional twisted baker map, the weakest normalized multiplier among primitive period-\(n\) orbits is exactly \(2^{\min\{1/M,p/(Mn)\}}\), where \(p\) is the smallest prime factor of the dimension \(M\ge4\). Above the sharp threshold \(n=p\), the minimizing orbits are exactly the words with \(p\) right-branch visits and a cyclic adjacent-right pair; in every even dimension there is precisely one such primitive cycle per period.
