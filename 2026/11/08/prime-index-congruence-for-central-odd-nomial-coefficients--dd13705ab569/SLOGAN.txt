For every odd prime \(p\), the central coefficient of \((1+x+\cdots+x^{2d})^p\) is \(1\pmod{p^2}\) unless \(p\) divides the alphabet size \(2d+1\), in which case it is exactly \(2^p-1\pmod{p^2}\). The exceptional correction is independent of the size of the alphabet beyond that divisibility condition.
