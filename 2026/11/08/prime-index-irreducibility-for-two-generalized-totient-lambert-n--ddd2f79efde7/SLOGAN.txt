The two generalized-totient Lambert numerator polynomials have infinite irreducible prime-index families: \(P_p\) is irreducible for every prime \(p\), and \(Q_p/(1+X^2)\) is irreducible for every prime \(p\equiv3\pmod4\). Shifted Eisenstein criteria prove the first and a folded version of the second; a negative real conjugate then proves irreducibility after quadratic substitution.
