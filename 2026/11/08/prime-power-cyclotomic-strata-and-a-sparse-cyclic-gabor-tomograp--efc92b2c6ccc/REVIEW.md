# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
PASS. A Weyl-basis expansion identifies the projector-span rank with the number of nonzero self-ambiguity coefficients; this foundational identity appears in Bojarovska–Flinth (2015) and Goldberger–Kang–Okoudjou (2021). For rational windows, cyclotomic divisibility proves that each entire prime-power frequency-order sector vanishes exactly when the corresponding digit-fiber sums agree. The proof handles the zero Fourier frequency using nonnegativity. The binary-exponent injectivity argument relies on the modulus being odd, and the cyclic digit-cross difference and overlap counts are explicit. The packaged checker independently confirms small cases and an assembled full Gram matrix. Risks: numerical checks do not certify infinitely many primes, and large taper weights can be ill-conditioned; neither issue replaces the algebraic proof.

## Originality
PASS under bounded comparison. The nearest known general theorems establish Weyl diagonalization or handle classical difference sets; these do not supply the complete rational digit-fiber valuation classification or the exact rank and simple deterministic taper for the **cyclic prime-square digit cross**. An indexed prime-cyclic rational-window result has prime, not prime-square, modulus, while the earlier Cartesian cross-window result uses the additive product group rather than the cyclic group and has a different nullity. Similar applications of cyclotomic identities may appear in unindexed Gabor literature. The standard Weyl-rank and cyclotomic ingredients are credited, not presented as new.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The calculation determines an entire natural arithmetic family of tomography nullspaces, not merely a single numerical example. The base-digit cross is a canonical near-minimal difference cover: despite spanning every translation difference, its flat Gabor measurement loses exactly \((p-1)(p^2-p+2)\) matrix directions. Explicit integer reweighting, without changing one support site, restores full informational completeness in all odd prime-square dimensions. The result distinguishes geometric support obstructions from arithmetic cancellation and supplies a reproducible, deterministic sparse construction. It is theoretical, not a conditioning or minimum-sparsity theorem.

## Prior literature and limits
The closest primary works are Bojarovska–Flinth, *Phase Retrieval from Gabor Measurements* (2015); Bojarovska–Paternostro, *Gabor fusion frames generated by difference sets* (2015); and Goldberger–Kang–Okoudjou, *Towards a classification of incomplete Gabor POVMs* (2021). General Fourier root-of-unity relations are classical. Remaining risks are a previously published exact specialization, large amplitude dynamic range, and absence of independently performed mathematical validation.
