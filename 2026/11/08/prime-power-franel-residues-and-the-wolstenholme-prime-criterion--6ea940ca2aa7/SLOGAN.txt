For every prime \(p\ge5\) and every \(a\ge1\), the first possible nonzero residue of the Franel number \(f_{p^a}-2\) beyond \(p^3\) is \(B_{p-3}/2\pmod p\), independent of \(a\). Its vanishing is exactly the classical Wolstenholme-prime condition, while the valuations at powers of \(2\) and \(3\) are always exactly \(3\).
