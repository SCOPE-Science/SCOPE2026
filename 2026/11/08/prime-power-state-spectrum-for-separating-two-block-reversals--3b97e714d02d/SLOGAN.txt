The smallest permutation DFA separating the swapped blocks \(0^a1^b\) and \(1^b0^a\) has exactly \(\max\{d(a),d(b)\}+\mathbf{1}_{\{d(a)=d(b)\in\{2,3,4\}\}}\) states, where \(d(t)\) is the least integer at least two not dividing \(t\). As the two positive block lengths vary, the attainable minimum state counts are exactly the prime powers at least three.
