For the concept class of every subgroup coset of \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), an exact prime-private criterion yields the complete polynomial counting shattered supports at every size. Its VC dimension is \(\max\{2,\omega(n)\}\) for \(n\geq3\), and when \(n\) is squarefree with at least three prime factors the number of maximum supports is exactly \(n\varphi(n)\).
