# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. For every prime \(p\), \(pn+1\) is a \(p\)-adic unit; Kummer's theorem makes \(p\)-nondivisibility equivalent to carry-free addition of \(n\) and \(pn\), and hence to the stated adjacent-digit condition, including index zero and arbitrary leading zeros. The finite transfer matrix has explicit mutually orthogonal cosine eigenvectors, verified algebraically by a finite geometric-cosine sum; their norms and all-ones projections give the exact spectral count. Primitive Perron–Frobenius asymptotics, arbitrary-cutoff counting, and a cylinder measure prove the density/dimension conclusions. Exact integer checks cover 4449 index cases, plus matrix and spectral identities. Finite checking is not substituted for proof.

## Originality

PASS with residual historical risk. Stănică (arXiv:math/0010148, Theorems 4 and 6) studies arity equal to the modulus prime or its power, whereas this theorem studies arity \(p+1\) relative to modulus \(p\). Sury (JIS 12 (2009), 09.7.5) treats prime arity; Krattenthaler and Müller (Aequationes Mathematicae 99 (2025), Section 7) again require arity \(p^h\). The classic \(p=2\) instance is known and is not claimed as new. Kummer supplies the elementary digit mechanism, but no inspected source stated the exact universal cosine spectral count or the associated \(p\)-adic Hausdorff dimension. Searches of mathematical research records and online literature identified no directly covering claim, but this cannot prove priority; a digit-language equivalent is a meaningful remaining risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Composite-arity Fuss–Catalan divisibility is a natural problem explicitly motivated in the foundational literature. The result classifies the complete prime-free index set for the uniform arity family \(p+1\), determines exact counts at every prime-power cutoff and the leading exponential growth constant, and identifies a measurable \(p\)-adic fractal dimension. It establishes an instructive contrast: each fixed prime has zero-density admissible indices, while their dimensions approach one as the prime grows. The isolated carry-free test and binary specialization alone would not support the value claim; the complete cross-prime spectral and dimension law is the substantive contribution.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest sources are Stănică (2000), Sury (2009), and Krattenthaler–Müller (2025), all on related but non-identical Fuss–Catalan congruences. The result concerns only nondivisibility by the selected prime and does not assert prime-power value distributions or any asymptotic coefficient for arbitrary real cutoffs. Independent historical-priority review remains necessary.
