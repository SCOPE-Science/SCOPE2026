For every prime \(p\), the \((p+1)\)-ary Fuss–Catalan number is prime to \(p\) exactly when adjacent base-\(p\) digits of its index sum to at most \(p-1\). This digit constraint has an exact cosine-eigenvalue counting formula below \(p^r\), and its \(p\)-adic closure has Hausdorff dimension \(\log_p(1/(2\sin(\pi/(4p+2))))\) despite the admissible indices having natural density zero.
