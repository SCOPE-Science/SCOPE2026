A squarefree integer satisfying \(\sigma(n)=3n+d\) for a proper positive divisor \(d\mid n\) must have at least \(15\) distinct prime factors; in the odd case it must have at least \(122\). For even integers, a congruence sieve forces the prime support to contain \(2,3\), all remaining primes to be \(1\pmod3\), and an odd total number of prime factors; the three possible \(13\)-prime positive-surplus cases all fail exact divisibility.
