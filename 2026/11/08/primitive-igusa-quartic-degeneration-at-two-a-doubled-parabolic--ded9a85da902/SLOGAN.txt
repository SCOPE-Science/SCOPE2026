For the primitive integral Igusa quartic \(\sigma_2^2-4\sigma_4=0\), reduction modulo two is a nonreduced double smooth quadric, and the fifteen flat limits of its distinguished lines exhaust the \(\mathbb F_2\)-rational lines of that quadric. Their complement on the reduced quadric has no \(\mathbb F_2\)-point and has exactly \(q^3+q^2-14q+16\) points over every \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q=2^m\).
