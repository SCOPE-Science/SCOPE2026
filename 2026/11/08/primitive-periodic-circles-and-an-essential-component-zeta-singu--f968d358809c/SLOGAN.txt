For every affine integral Jordan shear on \(\mathbb T^d\), the periodic-point sets are either empty or precisely \(n^{d-1}\) circles, and the primitive circles follow a Jordan-totient law with a sharp \(d=2\) versus \(d\ge3\) density transition. Their connected-component zeta function has an explicit exponential-polylogarithm form and essential singularities at the roots determined by the last-coordinate translation order.
