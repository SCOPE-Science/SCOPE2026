On a friendship graph made of \(k\ge2\) triangles sharing a vertex, synchronous parallel chip-firing has exactly \(12k\cdot3^{k-1}\) immediately primitive-three configurations, whose exact chip-mass polynomial is a pair of binomial-cumulative bands ending at the two universal three-period middle-stair boundaries. Uniformly sampled primitive-three states exhibit a limiting equal mixture of uniform chip densities on \([10/3,4]\) and \([6,20/3]\).
