# Scientific review: principal-angle Fock commutators

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is for all finite complex dimensions and arbitrary orthogonal projections. The proof identifies the composition projections as bosonic second quantizations; singular-value decomposition of the one-particle overlap then diagonalizes every symmetric occupation sector. The standard two-projection reducing block converts each overlap cosine \(t\) into two commutator singular values \(t\sqrt{1-t^2}\). The proof counts multiplicities, handles right angles and coincident subspaces, separates common-intersection modes, proves the exact essential norm by an infinite tensor factor, and sums convergent geometric series for the Hilbert--Schmidt formula. The rank-one maximizing-degree identity is an explicit sign computation. Exact-rational code checks 1,705 finite identities, but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge comparison)

The full Fock composition setting and boundedness/compactness theory are established in Bayart--Wang--Yao (arXiv:2610.26349) and Jiang--Prajitura--Zhao (arXiv:1608.05955). The compactness/Schatten-membership dichotomy for the difference of two distinct composition operators is qualitatively covered by Choe--Izuchi--Koo (2010) and Cho--Choe--Koo (2014); this consequence is explicitly not claimed as an independent novelty. The present quantitative theorem gives the *complete* singular-value multiset for an arbitrary pair of finite-dimensional projection symbols, an exact principal-cosine product formula, and an exact Hilbert--Schmidt determinant product, which those inspected materials do not state. The primary 2026 article was read in available HTML; the 2016 article was read in full HTML. The 2010 and 2014 primary full texts were unavailable, and priority or an earlier second-quantization formulation cannot be certified. Published mathematical research-record searches returned related Fock operators and projection geometry but no statement covering this multivariate spectral formula. This is a best-knowledge comparison, not an assertion that searches prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The problem is natural because projections in the one-particle space induce bounded noncompact composition projections, while their commutator can be either in every Schatten ideal or noncompact. Exact singular values yield all Schatten quasi-norms and operator norms, not merely qualitative ideal membership; the Hilbert--Schmidt product supplies a concise computable multi-angle invariant. A positive common subspace produces infinite multiplicity and an essential-norm jump, whereas transverse subspaces produce rapid decay. The rank-one norm tends to \(1/2\) as the rays coincide, although the commutator at coincidence is zero. These quantitatively distinct regimes make the complete classification useful beyond a routine special-parameter computation.

## Scope and residual limitations

The one-particle space is finite dimensional. Compactness and membership conclusions overlap existing qualitative theorems and are included as consequences of the exact spectral calculation.
