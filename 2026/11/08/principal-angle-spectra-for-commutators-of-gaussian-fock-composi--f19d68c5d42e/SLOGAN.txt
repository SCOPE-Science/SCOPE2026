For two orthogonal projections on a finite-dimensional one-particle space, the commutator of their Gaussian-Fock composition projections has an exact singular spectrum: each nontrivial product \(t\) of one-particle principal cosines produces the singular value \(t\sqrt{1-t^2}\) twice. This yields a sharp Schatten/essential-norm transition controlled by the common subspace and an exact Hilbert--Schmidt determinant formula; for distinct one-dimensional rays the commutator norm tends to \(1/2\) as the rays coincide, but equals zero at coincidence.
