## Correctness — PASS
For each prism center \(v_a\), the open neighborhood is \(W_{v,a}\cup\{v_{1-a}\}\). The exclusion of four-cycles implies that any two distinct base vertices share at most one neighbor. Every face with at least two vertices in one layer therefore has a unique center and no coface outside its generating simplex. Pairing each such horizontal face with the same face augmented by the opposite-layer apex, in descending horizontal cardinality, gives unique free face / maximal coface at every step. No faces participate twice. The residue is exactly the bipartite double-cover graph, and a standard graph cycle-rank argument supplies the integral homotopy classification. The pairing is invariant under base automorphisms and layer swap. The square-base boundary is checked directly. The proof does not depend on those finite checks.

## Value — PASS
This is an all-degree, arbitrary-base structural reduction rather than an isolated small-graph Betti number. It supplies a canonical and symmetry-compatible **elementary collapse** for a family whose prism squares overlap and thereby escape known no-square or edge-disjoint-square methods. It makes the fundamental group and integral homology exact from the base's bipartiteness and cycle rank. The cube shows that excluding four-cycles has topological content: its open-neighborhood complex has two two-spheres, rather than two circles. The result can serve as a reusable graph-spine construction for graph products and equivariant graph-derived complexes.
