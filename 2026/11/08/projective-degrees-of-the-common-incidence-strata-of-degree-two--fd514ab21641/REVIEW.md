# Mathematical review

## Correctness
The locus is the reduced overlap of two standard degree-two Chow components. Its ordered incidence cover is irreducible and generically of degree two, including the boundary where the two planes coincide only as a lower-dimensional exceptional locus. The actual Chow polarization is the sum of the individual Plücker classes, namely \(x+y-2c_1(S)\), rather than the untwisted quadratic-bundle class. Projective-bundle pushforward and fixed-point localization yield the stated rational finite sum, which must be integral because it is a projective degree. The duality follows from annihilators and preservation of Plücker hyperplanes. For lines, Thom--Porteous and Pieri provide the independent binomial formula. Exact computations independently compared incidence-bundle and degeneracy-locus degrees in 15 cases. Limit: only reduced support and projective degree, not the scheme-theoretic intersection structure.

## Originality
The closest 2026 primary paper gives the geometric incidence correspondence and cohomology rings but not the evaluated Chow-embedded intersection degree. The closest 2015 primary paper gives the component degrees in one small case, not the degree of their common reduced incidence stratum. A previous degree calculation for the two Chow components is distinct: it does not determine the common stratum's degree. Standard enumerative machinery computes such integrals in principle; the claim is confined to the explicit evaluated all-parameter degree formulas and the specialized binomial family. Historical coverage by older tables remains a residual risk and independent priority assessment is not performed.

## Mathematical value
The common reduced stratum measures where the two natural geometric constructions of degree-two cycles coincide; its embedding degree is an intrinsic enumerative datum once the Chow polarization is fixed. The result evaluates that invariant for every admissible parameter pair and reveals a duality symmetry, with new concrete line-pair and higher-plane values. This is not a restatement or computation of either component degree and does not rely on an arbitrary slice.
