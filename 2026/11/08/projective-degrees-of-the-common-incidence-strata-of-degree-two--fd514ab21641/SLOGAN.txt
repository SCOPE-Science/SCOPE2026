The reduced intersection of the two components of the degree-two Chow variety has an exact all-parameter projective degree computed by a Grassmannian incidence integral, with \(\deg Z_{p,n}=\deg Z_{n-p-1,n}\). For pairs of lines, the formula specializes to an explicit binomial sum giving degrees \(140,6300,288288,13453440,\ldots\) as the ambient projective dimension increases.
