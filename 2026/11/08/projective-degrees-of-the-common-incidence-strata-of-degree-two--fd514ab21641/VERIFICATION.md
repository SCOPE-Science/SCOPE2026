Over C, for n≥3, 1≤p≤n−2, the reduced common support of quadric and two-p-plane degree-two Chow components is exactly unordered pairs of p-planes lying in a (p+1)-plane. The ordered incidence P(S∨)×_{Gr(p+2,n+1)}P(S∨) is irreducible, generically 2:1 over the common support, dimension D=(p+2)(n−p−1)+2p+2. The pullback of the actual Chow O(1) is H=x+y−2c1(S), the sum of the two individual Plücker hyperplanes; it is not the relative x+y quadratic-bundle class. Consequently 2deg Z=∫H^D.
