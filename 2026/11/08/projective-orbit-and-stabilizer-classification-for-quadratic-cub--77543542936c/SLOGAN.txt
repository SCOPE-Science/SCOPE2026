For every finite field, reduced degree-five divisors on the projective line with Frobenius type \((2)(3)\) have \(q(q-1)/6\) projective orbits when \(q\not\equiv2\pmod3\), and \((q(q-1)+4)/6\) when \(q\equiv2\pmod3\). Exactly in the latter case, one orbit has a cyclic order-three stabilizer and all others are free; the stabilizer-weighted mass is always \(q(q-1)/6\).
