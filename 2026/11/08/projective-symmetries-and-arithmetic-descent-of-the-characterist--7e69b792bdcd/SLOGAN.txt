For Kollár's characteristic-two plane curves \(Z^2Y^{2^n}=X^{2^n+2}+XZ^{2^n+1}\) with \(n\ge2\), the full geometric projective stabilizer is cyclic of order \(2^n+1\), although no nonidentity projective symmetry is defined over \(\mathbb F_2\). The exact stabilizer over \(\mathbb F_{2^r}\) has order \(\gcd(2^n+1,2^r-1)\), and its full geometric group first descends to \(\mathbb F_{2^{2n}}\).
