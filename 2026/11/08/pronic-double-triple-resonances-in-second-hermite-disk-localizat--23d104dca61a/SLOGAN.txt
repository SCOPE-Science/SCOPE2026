For the second Hermite window, every equality of two consecutive disk-localization eigenvalues forces a triple, and two disjoint triples occur simultaneously **exactly** at pronic areas \(R=k(k+1)\), centered at modes \(k^2\) and \((k+1)^2\). Each three-way crossing has a universal neighboring slope ratio \(k_*/(k_*+1)\) for its center index \(k_*\); the result does not claim all nonconsecutive spectral coincidences are absent.
