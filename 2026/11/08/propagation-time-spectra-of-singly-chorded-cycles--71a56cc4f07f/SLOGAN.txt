Although adding one chord to a cycle preserves every zero-forcing set, it changes the exact synchronous propagation-time spectrum of the minimum two-vertex sets: the complete enumerator is \(H_{a,b}(z)=z^{\lfloor b/2\rfloor}B_a(z)+z^{\lfloor a/2\rfloor}B_b(z)\). The minimum time rises by one precisely when both chord-defined arc lengths are even, and exactly \(2+\mathbf 1_{2\mid a}+\mathbf 1_{2\mid b}\) starting pairs attain it.
