# Mathematical review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exact domain is the one-variable Gaussian Fock space with Thue–Morse signs on all unit intervals of the squared-radius variable. Radial Toeplitz diagonalization yields Gamma-shell probabilities. The finite-block Prouhet factorization is exact. Its first \(k\) finite differences, together with an elementary sampling inequality, reduce the spectral estimate to two \(L^1\) norms of Gamma-density derivatives. Laguerre Rodrigues orthogonality and Cauchy–Schwarz yield the explicitly stated factorial bound for \(n\geq k+1\). Optimizing at \(k\sim\log n/(2\log2)\) proves superpolynomial spectral flattening. The Laplace-product identity and absolute eigenvalue summability prove zero trace without interchanging a divergent integral. The positive first eigenvalue forces both signs. The standalone checker passes 120 finite assertions. Infinite conclusions are proved analytically, not by finite tests.

**PASS, provisional historical priority.** The Prouhet product and radial Gaussian eigenvalue formula are classical. Esmeral García–Maximenko (arXiv:2503.23276, Sections 3, 4) constructs approximate realizations of convergent sequences, not this exact mask. Rozenblum (arXiv:1012.3528, Sections 4, 5) analyzes sign-changing radial symbols with different decay and oscillation hypotheses. No covering theorem was found in the inspected primary material or the available scientific overlap searches. ## Value

**PASS.** The binary mask and its complement both occupy infinitely many fixed-area shells and do not approach a constant. Yet their Gaussian concentration operator differs from the half identity by a nonzero operator in every Schatten class, with exactly zero trace. The all-index factorial estimate and explicit superpolynomial rate quantify how symbolic moment cancellation eliminates the high-degree spectral signal. This is a structural boundary phenomenon for infinite-measure time-frequency observation, not a chosen numerical constant or a routine finite enumeration. No sharpness of the squared-log constant, full sign pattern, or extension to other automatic colorings is claimed.
