A nondecaying binary radial mask colored by the Prouhet–Thue–Morse sequence has Gaussian localization operator \(T=\tfrac12I+K\), where \(K\) is nonzero, trace zero, and belongs to every Schatten class. The Hermite deviations satisfy an explicit all-index finite-difference certificate and a squared-logarithmic decay upper bound with coefficient \(1/(8\log2)\).
