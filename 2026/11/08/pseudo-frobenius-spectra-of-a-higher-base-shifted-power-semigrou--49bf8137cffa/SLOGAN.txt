For \(h\ge3\), every coprime higher-base semigroup \(\langle a,ha+d,h^2a+(h^2-h+1)d,h^3a+(h^3-h+1)d\rangle\) with \(a\) a positive multiple of \(h^3-h+1\) has exactly two pseudo-Frobenius numbers, separated by \(d(h-1)^2\). Explicit formulas determine their values, the Frobenius number, conductor, and genus for all positive \(d\).
