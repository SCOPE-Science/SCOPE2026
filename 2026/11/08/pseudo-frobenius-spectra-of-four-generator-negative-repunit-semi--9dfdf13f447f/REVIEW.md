# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The result follows from an exact minimum-coin computation for \(\{1,3,7\}\), an independently established Apéry formula, a complete maximal-element criterion using single coin steps, and a seven-case exhaustion. None of the quantifiers depend on a finite test. An independent programmatic route through Dijkstra residue shortest paths and direct semigroup membership agrees for the specified finite ranges. Risk: the argument uses standard numerical-semigroup definitions and the conventional Apéry pseudo-Frobenius correspondence; both are stated explicitly in the proof.

**Originality — PASS subject to unindexed-literature risk.** The closest source, Liu–Xin–Ye–Yin, arXiv:2306.03459 (first posted 2023-06-06, final 2025 revision), introduces the relevant negative-shift family and gives generalized Apéry, Frobenius and genus methods. Its negative-shift treatment does not give this seven-residue pseudo-Frobenius table. Branco–Colaço–Ojeda, arXiv:2112.01106 (2021), gives complete type and pseudo-Frobenius results for a different repunit-constrained infinite generator family; that work **does** cover the isolated \(a=15\) member, but not the unrestricted four-generator statement. Standard general algorithms (Delgado–García-Sánchez–Robles-Pérez) are not specialized to this family in the inspected material. Exact parameter, alias and implication searches found no covering theorem. Risk: difficult-to-index or unpublished equivalences may exist; the older paper's one covered parameter is not treated as new.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The complete explicit classification adds structural invariants to the natural negative-parameter case of a family whose Frobenius/genus behavior is actively studied. It captures all seven congruence classes, identifies symmetric members, and determines their full pseudo-Frobenius spectra and Frobenius number, including the failure of a naive monotone-maximal-residue extension of positive-shift formulas. The new result is not simply a few table values or a routine substitution into a published pseudo-Frobenius theorem. Risk: no generalization beyond four generators is asserted.
