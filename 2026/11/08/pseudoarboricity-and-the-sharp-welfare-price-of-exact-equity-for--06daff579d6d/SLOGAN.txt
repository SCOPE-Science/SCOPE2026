In normalized binary allocations where each good is approved by one or two agents, the maximum exact-equality welfare is \(n(D-\lambda(G))\), where \(\lambda(G)\) is the pseudoarboricity of the double-approved-goods multigraph; an EF1 allocation attains that maximum for every \(n\ge3\) and \(D\ge2\). The sharp welfare price at fixed \((n,D)\) is \((nD-2\lceil D/2\rceil+1)/(n\lfloor D/2\rfloor)\), with an exact extremal envelope for each pseudoarboricity level.
