For the negative-doubling interval map with open hole \(H=(7/17,10/17)\), the exact Lebesgue probability of avoiding the hole at the first \(n\) observations is \(\bigl(6n^2+12n+39+(-1)^n\bigr)/(34\,2^n)\) for every \(n\ge1\). The first eight-cycle hole boundary therefore has a quadratic escape prefactor, in sharp contrast with the linear prefactor at the preceding four-cycle boundary.
