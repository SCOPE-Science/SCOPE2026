A half-flux dark excitation on an even magnetic ring with one local absorber leaks at a rigorously determined quadratic flux-detuning rate, with mode widths proportional to \(\sin^2((2k+1)\pi/n)\). On the \(\delta^{-2}\) time scale the exact finite-ring survival mixture converges, in a subsequent large-ring limit, to \(e^{-\tau/2}I_0(\tau/2)\), whose tail decays as \((\pi\tau)^{-1/2}\).
