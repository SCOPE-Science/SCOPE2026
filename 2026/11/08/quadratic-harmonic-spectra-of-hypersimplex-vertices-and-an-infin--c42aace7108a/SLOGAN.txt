Every centered hypersimplex vertex orbit has exactly two explicit quadratic-harmonic sampling singular values, including a complete invertibility and sharp-conditioning criterion. These values coincide precisely when \(k(n-k)=n(n+1)/6\), producing an infinite Pell-parametrized family of exact real projective \(2\)-designs, beginning with the previously known \(28\)-line configuration and continuing at \((n,k)=(27,6),(98,21),\ldots\).
