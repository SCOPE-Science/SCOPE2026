After erasing one symbol from a prime-length Zadoff–Chu chirp, the root indices that simultaneously minimize peak and integrated periodic autocorrelation sidelobes are exactly the quadratic nonresidues when \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), and all roots tie when \(p\equiv3\pmod4\). The exact optima are controlled respectively by the least quadratic nonresidue and the real part of a quadratic Gauss sum.
