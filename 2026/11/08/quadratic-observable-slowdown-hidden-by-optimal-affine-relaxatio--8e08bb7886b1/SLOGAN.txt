Even when a Gaussian reflected-proximal sampler is tuned to the sharp affine rate \(q=(\kappa^{1/4}-1)/(\kappa^{1/4}+1)\), every nontrivial curvature interval has an endpoint mode whose covariance transition has a real eigenvalue strictly between \(q\) and \(1\). The resulting slower factor is attainable in the actual position variance from a centered Gaussian start: shared Bernoulli mirror/refresh switching breaks the usual inference from first-degree Gaussian SOR rates to quadratic-observable relaxation.
