For uniformly weighted vertices of the partial permutohedron \(\mathcal P(m,n)\), the principal covariance direction changes exactly once as \(n\) increases: from the coordinate-contrast subspace to the all-ones axis. The sharp real threshold is an explicit quadratic root satisfying \(\tau_m/m^2\to1/2+1/\sqrt3\), although the face lattice stays unchanged for \(n\ge m\).
