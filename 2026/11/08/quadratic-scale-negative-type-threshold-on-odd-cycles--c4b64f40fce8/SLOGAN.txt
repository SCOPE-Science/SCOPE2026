For the shortest-path metric on an odd cycle of length \(n\), the largest negative-type exponent is \(1+K/n^2+o(n^{-2})\), with \(K=2\pi^2/\int_0^{2\pi}(1-\cos t)\,dt/t\approx8.0976273568\). The ordinary four-point roundness instead exceeds one by order \(n^{-1}\), giving a sharp quantitative separation of these two Hilbert-geometry invariants.
