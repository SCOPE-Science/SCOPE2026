For every positive integer \(m\), the commutator of the discrete Cesàro operator with the \(m\)-th unilateral shift power has singular values bounded above and below by positive multiples of \(n^{-2}\). Thus it belongs to precisely the Schatten ideals \(\mathcal S_p\) with \(p>1/2\), reaches the weak-\(\mathcal S_{1/2}\) endpoint but not the strong endpoint, and has an explicit harmonic-number Hilbert–Schmidt norm.
