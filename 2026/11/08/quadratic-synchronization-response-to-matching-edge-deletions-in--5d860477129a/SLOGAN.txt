For both synchronous three-colour cyclic cellular automata and Greenberg–Hastings dynamics, deleting \(t\) independent cross-edges from \(K_{n,n}\), \(n\ge3\), increases the exact number of synchronizing initial colourings by \(3[t2^{n+1}+t(t-1)]\). The second finite difference in \(t\) is universally \(6\), revealing a genuine pair-interaction contribution to synchronization under edge deletion.
