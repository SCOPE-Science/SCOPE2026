Uniformly sampled vertices of wheel spanning-tree polytopes have two explicit asymptotic Fourier covariance bands, with the least positive mode at the longest rim wavelength and the largest mode eventually comparing total spoke and rim counts. Their intrinsic covariance condition number obeys the sharp law \(\kappa(C_m)\sim 4m^2/(5\pi^2)\), despite nonvanishing individual edge-inclusion variances.
