For quadratic Walsh polynomials, sampling on a Hamming-weight residue class modulo three has an exact spectral decomposition into a 3-by-3 block, repeated 2-by-2 blocks, and one scalar sector. Every class is injective from dimension eight onward, with precisely two injective classes already in dimension seven; two classes are always isospectral and enjoy half the leading asymptotic squared condition-number defect of the third.
