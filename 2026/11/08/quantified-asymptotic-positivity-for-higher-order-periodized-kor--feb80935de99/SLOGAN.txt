For every higher-order univariate Korobov kernel with integer \(\alpha\ge2\), the unmodified full-lattice periodization weights approximate the nonnegative density samples with \(O(N^{-2})\) uniform error, and their total negative mass is \(O(N^{-2})\). Their weight sum has the exact leading deficit \(2\gamma\zeta(2\alpha)N^{-2\alpha}\), establishing quantitative asymptotic positivity without asserting that every finite-grid weight is positive. Conversely, every fixed even grid has a negative endpoint weight for sufficiently large kernel parameter \(\gamma\), so asymptotic positivity is not a parameter-uniform finite-grid guarantee.
