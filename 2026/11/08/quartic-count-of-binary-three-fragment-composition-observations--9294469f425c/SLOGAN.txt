For binary words read through the multiset of compositions of all contiguous substrings of lengths at most three, the exact number of distinguishable length-\(n\) messages has a rational generating function with denominator \((1-z)^4(1-z^4)\). It is an explicit quartic quasipolynomial of period four for \(n\geq2\), with leading growth \(n^4/32\), proved by a boundary-sensitive run decomposition.
