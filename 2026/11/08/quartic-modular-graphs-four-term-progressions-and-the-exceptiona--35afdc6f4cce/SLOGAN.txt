For the quartic polynomial graph over any modulus coprime to six, four distinct equally spaced graph points exist exactly when the modulus is divisible by five or is not squarefree; five distinct equally spaced points exist exactly when the modulus is not squarefree. Every four-term progression extends indefinitely except a precisely counted family at five-adic exponents congruent to one modulo four, and explicit multiplicative formulas give all four-term and extendible counts.
