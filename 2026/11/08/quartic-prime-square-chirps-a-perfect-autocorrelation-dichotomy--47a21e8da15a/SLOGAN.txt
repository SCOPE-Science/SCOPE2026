Over prime-square cycles, the quartic unimodular chirp \(g(x)=\exp(2\pi i x^4/p^2)\) has perfect periodic autocorrelation exactly when \(p\equiv2\pmod3\), while for \(p\equiv1\pmod3\) every nonzero shift has nonzero autocorrelation. Its complete time-frequency ambiguity-zero set and complex Gabor lifting rank are determined by a quadratic-residue criterion and a single cubic-permutation exception.
