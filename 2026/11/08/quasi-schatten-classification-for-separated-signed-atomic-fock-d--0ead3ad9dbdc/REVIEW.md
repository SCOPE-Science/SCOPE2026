# Scientific assessment: separated signed Fock dipoles

## Correctness
PASS. The analytic proof gives an explicit two-vector orthonormalization, Gaussian-derivative estimates for a uniform cross-pair Gram bound, exact block eigenvalues, and two-sided singular-value comparison through a uniformly invertible Gram operator. The polynomial-core condition, separate-positive-integral limitation, and infinite-dimensional quantifiers are stated. The standalone checker corroborates finite identities, but is not substituted for an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS under checked literature and mathematical-record coverage, with priority risk remaining. Dileep–Fang–Tien characterize positive forms; Rozenblum–Vasilevski give a general highly-singular-form framework rather than the atomic pairwise sharp Schatten criterion; Fulsche's signed measures have total variation Fock-Carleson, not true for the unbounded-positive-part witness. The closest searchable published mathematical result involves nonvanishing bounded symbols forming Fock Toeplitz zero divisors, not these signed atomic rank-two dipoles. General Riesz-sequence operator theory is acknowledged as a potential alternative derivation, not claimed absent.

## Value
PASS. Positivity is fundamental to the initiating local-mass criterion; the example demonstrates a mathematically substantive and precisely measured failure of transferring positive-symbol size information to signed symbols. The result classifies the complete quasi-Schatten scale, boundedness and compactness on an infinite but explicit class, and exhibits any prescribed polynomial-order critical Schatten threshold by choosing dipole gaps. It distinguishes legitimate form cancellation from an undefined all-vectors signed integral.

## Remaining limitations
No optimal separation constant, general signed-symbol theorem, or publication-priority certification. Only polynomial-growth weights and explicitly paired summation are covered; general Fock functions need not admit separate positive-part integrals.
