# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The finite Pascal transformation is unit lower triangular over the two-element field, giving iid fair scenery. The exact pathwise identity \(H_n=2A_{S_n}+M_n+1-\omega_0\) holds with conditional iid fair increments in \(M\). The quenched law of large numbers for the scenery, conditional Donsker theorem, uniform random-index replacement, and standard martingale functional central limit theorem establish the claimed two-dimensional and centered covariance limits. Independent environment and martingale terms give annealed variance rate three; the exact covariance sum gives the finite-horizon variance. The classical LIL proves the lack of tightness of uncentered quenched second-order laws. A standalone exhaustive finite checker verified 670,074 algebraic/normalization assertions, but the infinite results depend on the analytic arguments.

## Originality

**PASS, provisional.** Hamza and Tournier (arXiv:2610.01658v1, Section 4.3) leave quenched convergence unresolved in the cardinality-grouped time-invariant inclusion-induced family; their Corollary 12 proves only the annealed statement. The present result resolves the independent fair-coefficient subcase and identifies a concrete second-order separation in the same model. Their earlier general bootstrap criterion (Collevecchio et al., arXiv:2010.16000) does not by itself verify almost-every-environment decorrelation for this random construction. Guillotin-Plantard and Poisat (arXiv:1210.6135) develop broad random-scenery CLTs, with a one-dimensional renewal case requiring strictly positive increments, whereas the present index chain has \(0/1\) jumps. Their machinery and standard scenery martingale methods remain genuine conceptual precedents. An equivalent characterization in older lazy-scenery or renewal-reward literature cannot be ruled out by the performed searches; the general random-scenery CLT is not claimed as novel.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The parent paper explicitly identifies an annealed-to-quenched gap. The theorem settles a natural, nondegenerate fair-coefficient case rather than an arbitrary finite slice, while proving a sharp and informative scale distinction: the walks converge jointly to independent Brownian motions conditionally, yet their covariance fluctuations have annealed variance rate three, conditional centered rate one, and no uncentered quenched limit. The exact scenery transformation exposes a structurally useful decomposition. The extension to biased coefficient laws remains open here.

## Limits and priority risks

Finite verification is not an infinite proof. The claim is restricted to fair, independent, time-invariant cardinality coefficients and the symmetric input walk. The random-centering mechanism has known analogues in renewal processes and random environments, including earlier random-scenery research. Historical originality outside inspected literature is not independently certified.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
