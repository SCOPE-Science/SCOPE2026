In the fair time-invariant inclusion-induced bootstrap walk, a parity transform hides an iid random scenery: the walk pair is quenched asymptotically independent, although its covariance has order-\(\sqrt n\) environmental fluctuations. After explicit random centering the covariance has quenched variance rate one, whereas the annealed rate is three and the uncentered quenched second-order laws are not tight.
