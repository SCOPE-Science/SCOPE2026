# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The claim is restricted to independent time-and-cardinality Bernoulli coefficients with a uniform ellipticity margin. Lucas' parity identity and a binomial point-mass bound give an annealed empirical-cross-bracket mean-square rate \(N^{-1/2+o(1)}\); independence of time slices and the exact \(4/N\) replacement bound give exponentially summable McDiarmid deviations at any \(N^{-1/2+\delta}\) level. The uniform predictable-bracket convergence, together with bounded martingale jumps, yields the stated quenched two-dimensional functional limit. The exact finite checker gives 84,143 confirming assertions, not a substitute for the infinite argument.

## Originality: PASS, provisional historical assessment

Hamza and Tournier, arXiv:2610.01658v1, explicitly label the cardinality-grouped, time-independent-resampling case as annealed only (Corollary 9) and identify the quenched case as unresolved. Their Proposition 7 establishes the quenched first-moment condition but not the second-moment condition supplied here. Collevecchio et al. (2022) supply the general deterministic-environment martingale criterion, but not almost-every-environment behavior of this random cardinality-grouped family. The bounded-differences inequality itself is standard and is not claimed as new. The historical risk is an unrecognized direct upgrade in a general random-environment martingale theorem or a later revision of the source.

## Value: PASS

The finding resolves a specifically identified functional-limit question for a canonical one-factor Markov recoding family, improving from averaged to almost-every-environment convergence without additional regularity assumptions on the individual parameters. Quantitative control of the conditional empirical cross-bracket is an independently useful strengthening, while the unqualified time-invariant family is not addressed.

## Outstanding limits

No optimal exponent, quenched metric Berry--Esseen bound, or time-invariant-coefficient analogue is proved. Historical priority is not independently certified and mathematical validation remains independent of this review.
