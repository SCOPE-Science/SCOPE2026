Equal Hamming weight enumerators of dimension-two quinary linear codes determine a projective six-point multiplicity multiset, whose \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(5)\)-orbits yield exactly \(1,2,3\), or \(6\) equivalence classes and sharp length thresholds \(6,10,15\). The first inequivalent pair has length \(6\) and exact minimum Hamming distortion \(36/25\).
