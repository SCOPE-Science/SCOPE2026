The oscillation exponent has a sharp threshold: a strictly positive trigonometric-polynomial radial Fock Toeplitz symbol has its original essential spectral interval below \(\beta=1\), a Gaussian-heat-filtered interval at \(\beta=1\), and only its mean value above \(\beta=1\). In the fast-oscillation regime the operator differs from that scalar by an element of every Schatten quasi-ideal, while weighted eigenvalue distributions follow the same three-way transition.
