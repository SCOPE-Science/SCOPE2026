## correctness

PASS

PASS: A proper vertex-centred radius-r ball on C_n is an odd contiguous cyclic arc. For any three-point support, full trace exists iff max clockwise gap ≥ n-2R; all pair-only traces exist iff every gap ≤2R, yielding n-2R≤max gap≤2R for R below diameter. Radius zero realizes singleton and empty traces, and alternating four vertices cannot be shattered, so degree ≤3. Bounded positive three-composition inclusion-exclusion yields s3 and direct distance counting yields s2. Separate even- and odd-n diameter cases give all vs all but n consecutive triples. The normalized three-gap simplex gives the two-piece limiting density and VCdim transition ceil(n/4). Independent direct enumeration of all actual ball traces on pairs and triples for n=4..14 and all R confirmed the criteria; analytic arguments cover all n.
