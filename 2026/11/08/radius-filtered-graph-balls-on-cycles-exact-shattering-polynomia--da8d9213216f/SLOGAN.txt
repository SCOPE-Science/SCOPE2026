For unweighted cycles on \(n\ge4\) vertices, shattered triples of radius-filtered vertex balls are characterized by sharp inequalities on their largest cyclic gap, yielding the complete shattered-support polynomial and the exact radius threshold \(\lceil n/4\rceil\) for VC dimension three. Uniform random triples exhibit a two-phase quadratic shattering-density law when the maximum radius grows proportionally to the cycle size.
