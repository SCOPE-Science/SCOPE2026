For exact Euclidean ball-proximal iteration on orthogonally separable strictly convex objectives, larger radii dominate smaller radii at every step in every absolute coordinate, despite the absence of such monotonicity for general convex objectives. Constant-radius termination obeys \(N_t\le\lceil\|Q^\top(x_0-x_*)\|_1/t\rceil\), and smooth strongly convex diagonal quadratics attain \(N_1=d\) exactly from \(x_0=(1,\ldots,1)\).
