# Review: random generator spectra of chain evolution-algebra automorphisms

## Correctness — PASS
The finite-field linear automorphism normal form is reconstructed without assuming characteristic zero. The coefficient of \(e_{i+1}\) in \(T(e_1)T(e_i)\) forces successive forbidden coordinates to vanish, and all remaining normal-form matrices preserve multiplication. Normalizing the final-column parameter produces the scalar affine group, with cyclic-action kernel of size \(\gcd(2^{n-1}-1,q-1)\). Nielsen reduction plus finite-field linear algebra gives the generating-tuple probabilities, including all rank and empty-product boundaries. The commutation criterion gives the conjugacy-class count. The characteristic-two dichotomy follows from a proper-subfield size bound. The supplied verifier directly enumerates actual homomorphism matrices and independently checks group composition, conjugacy, and generating pairs in selected small fields. Its finite tests do not replace the general proofs.

## Originality — PASS (provisional publication priority)
Mukhamedov–Khakimov–Omirov–Qaralleh, arXiv:1805.10285 (2018), is the closest mathematical source: its Section 5 classifies automorphisms of maximal-nilindex evolution algebras over characteristic-zero fields, but does not give finite-field random generation, all-arity generator probabilities, characteristic-two extension-degree resonance, or conjugacy-class counts. The separate 2019 Elduque–Labra article concerns perfect evolution algebras, not nilpotent chains. Burness–Liebeck–Shalev (2013) concerns random generation in finite groups, particularly maximal subgroups of almost simple groups; it supplies background rather than the specific statement. Targeted literature and published-finding corpus searches returned no equivalent or stronger exact formula. The scalar-semidirect reduction is elementary and related group-generation theorems could imply part of the statement; that possibility is an explicit residual risk and independent priority assessment remains necessary.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is the complete random-generator spectrum of the automorphism groups of a standard infinite family of nonassociative algebras, not just a small-table count. It also exhibits an extension-degree resonance: over \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\) the minimal number of generators jumps between \(2\) and \(f\) according to a congruence on algebra dimension; an odd-characteristic example requires four generators. The uniform formula yields actual sampling probabilities, so it quantifies how hard the groups are to generate rather than merely finding abstract presentations. The conjugacy-class formula provides an independent structural check. The specific algebra-to-arithmetic connection motivates this invariant; no unproved downstream application is asserted.

## Closest literature and residual limits
The normal-form philosophy is previously published and is credited, as are the standard semidirect and finite-vector-space generation tools. The 2018 reference explicitly assumes characteristic zero; finite-characteristic validity is independently proved for the present chain case. A general scalar-group generation theorem might dominate some counting assertions, and exhaustive literature priority cannot be guaranteed.
