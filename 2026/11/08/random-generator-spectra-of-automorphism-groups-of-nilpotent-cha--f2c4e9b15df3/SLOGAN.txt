For the chain evolution algebra \(e_i^2=e_{i+1}\) over \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\), the minimum number of automorphisms needed to generate the full automorphism group equals \(2\) or \(f\) according as \(f\nmid(n-1)\) or \(f\mid(n-1)\). Exact probabilities for every ordered generating tuple size over every finite field, and the complete conjugacy-class count \(q-1+\gcd(2^{n-1}-1,q-1)^2\), follow from the scalar semidirect-product structure.
