On any full-rank two-row Kaczmarz system with nonorthogonal rows, independently randomizing the order of each complete two-projection sweep strictly worsens the asymptotic expected squared-error rate compared with either fixed order. The exact rate is \(\rho(c,p)=[c^4+\sqrt{c^8+4p(1-p)c^4(1-c^4)}]/2\), and fair mixing gives the largest slowdown.
