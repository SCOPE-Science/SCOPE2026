Independently choosing position- or velocity-Verlet with equal probability at each harmonic-oscillator step inflates expected physical energy by exactly \(1+h^6/32\) per step, although either fixed integrator has bounded powers for \(0<h<2\). Every nondegenerate independent order mixture has a growing second-moment mode, while deterministic alternation is bounded only below the sharp threshold \(h=\sqrt5-1\).
