# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claimed polynomial is derived from an exact bijection with \(M_2(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^m))\), a single conjugacy orbit of primitive square-zero matrices over the truncated ring, and an exhaustive split by minimum entry valuation. The determinant is uniform on each nontrivial lift shell because its dependence on one free coefficient has a unit multiplier. Smith invariants determine the underlying-field rank. The independently enumerated rank histograms agree for all tested cases and the total-mass identity follows from the valuation partition. The proof holds for all prime powers, not only the enumerated fields.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** The exact rank-by-rank finite-field cardinalities for the square-zero intersection of the centralizer of a fixed two-equal-block nilpotent are not supplied by the inspected closest sources. Britnell–Wildon (2010 arXiv) studies commuting nilpotent classes and their existence; Harima–Watanabe (2012 arXiv version) the structure of the centralizer algebra; Bogdanić et al. (2022 arXiv) the possible partitions in its nilpotent centralizer. An existential class-intersection theorem alone does not imply how many labeled members occur in each square-zero class, and a semisimple-quotient theorem does not determine the nonlinear square-zero fibers. The earlier \(m=2\) dual-number counting case overlaps only the boundary, excluded from the new claim. Residual risk: an unidentified general Hall-polynomial or matrix-over-chain-ring point count may contain the answer implicitly.

**Value — PASS.** Intersections of nilpotent centralizers with Jordan orbits form a classical and nontrivial structural problem. The result gives a complete count for every square-zero class intersecting the centralizer at every length \(m\ge3\), substantially refining the existence classification. The independent coefficient formulas, valuation-layer equidistribution, exact total population, and maximal-rank population provide a natural counting invariant for the first nontrivial repeated-block family; this is not an arbitrary fixed numerical slice.

Sources inspected: Britnell–Wildon, arXiv:1001.0811 (full preprint, Sections 1–4); Harima–Watanabe, arXiv:1206.6574 (accessible complete HTML, centralizer-structure sections); Bogdanić et al., arXiv:2212.08425 (accessible complete article, introductory question, Theorem 3, tables, classification proof). Carnevale–Shechter–Voll (arXiv:1709.02717, complete HTML) counts traceless matrices over finite valuation-ring quotients by image size, a broader linear-constraint family that does not directly impose the quadratic square-zero condition. The assertion that a general undiscovered or uninspected formula cannot imply this one is not made. No independent audit has been performed.
