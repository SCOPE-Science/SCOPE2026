For every finite field and each block length \(m\ge3\), the complete rank distribution of square-zero operators commuting with \(J_m(0)\oplus J_m(0)\) is an explicit finite polynomial governed by equal-size valuation shells in \(M_2(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^m))\). In particular, there are \(\lceil m/2\rceil(q^2-1)q^{2m-2}+q^{4\lfloor m/2\rfloor}\) such operators, of which exactly \((q^2-1)q^{2m-3}(q+\lfloor m/2\rfloor(q-1))\) have maximal square-zero rank.
