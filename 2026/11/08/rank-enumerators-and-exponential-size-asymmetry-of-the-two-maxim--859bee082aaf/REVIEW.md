# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The proof reduces regularity to an explicit inverse-witness criterion for the two possible types of quotient maps; counting by image sizes gives the exact rank coefficients. The simplification of the two named maximal subsemigroups follows directly from their published definitions, with all overlap accounted for. An independent exhaustive search for inner inverse witnesses at block sizes two and three and rank-by-rank enumeration through size four agree with the formulas. The exponential estimate follows from a bounded-differences inequality for occupancy image size.

**Originality: PASS, with historical-priority risk.** The closest primary text is Sangkhanan's 2026 structural classification. It proves the existence, maximality, uniqueness and union of the two subsemigroups but does not state the Stirling-number rank enumerators, their intersection enumerator or the exponential size asymmetry. Pei's cited criterion is prior work. Searches for equal-two-block regular-element counts and rank enumerators found no matching explicit all-parameter formula; an equivalent unpublished or unindexed enumeration remains possible.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** The recently classified maximal regular subsemigroups have dramatically different asymptotic sizes despite identical maximality status, a structural distinction not visible from maximality alone. The theorem supplies a complete natural rank invariant at all block sizes, including their overlap and the limiting regular density, not an arbitrary finite table.

**Closest literature and limits.** Sangkhanan (2026), arXiv:2610.38341v1, is decisive structural prior art. The 2005 Pei regularity paper was accessible at the abstract level only; the needed criterion was independently reconstructed in the new proof. The asymptotic estimate does not identify the optimal decay constant, and the theorem covers only two equal-size blocks.
