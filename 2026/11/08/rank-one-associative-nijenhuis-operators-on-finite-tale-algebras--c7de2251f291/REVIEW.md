# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof first forces the one-dimensional image line to be generated by a scalar idempotent, since the target algebra is a product of fields. Substitution into the original associative Nijenhuis identity gives two scalar multiplicativity equations on complementary product factors, with no hidden mixed constraints; nonzero functionals force a nonzero eigenvalue and one or two unital characters into the coefficient field. Those characters exist exactly on rational (degree-one) factors. This yields a complete biconditional classification, uniqueness of parameters, the exact labeled count, and orbit count under all algebra automorphisms, including Frobenius actions. Exact finite checks in `verify.py` independently enumerate the defining identity; `verify_orbits.py` checks the group-orbit count on canonical data.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
Gao–Kang–Lü–Yu (arXiv:2308.12121, sections 2 and 4) classify all operators only in complex dimension at most three, containing some overlapping small complex examples but not the arbitrary-degree finite-field formula. Iovanov–Sistko (arXiv:1705.00762, sections 3 and 4) classify maximal subalgebras; their broader *subalgebra* result does not assert the operator-to-character reduction, labeled counts or automorphism-orbit formula. Sistko (arXiv:1809.09760) treats subalgebra-variety orbits over algebraically closed fields, not finite-field rank-one Nijenhuis operators. Targeted searches for rank-one, semisimple/étale products, idempotent operators, and rational-factor descriptions yielded no directly containing theorem. Related mathematical record searches yielded no statement with the same implication. This does not establish publication priority; the risk of an unlocated general projection/Nijenhuis theorem remains.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete classification of a natural operator rank stratum on all finite commutative semisimple coefficient algebras, rather than an arbitrarily chosen small case. The exact arithmetic obstruction is sharp: if there are no rational field factors then there is no nonzero rank-one operator, while the internal extension degrees affect the orbit count without affecting labeled counts beyond the number of factors. The formulas provide explicit counts and normal forms for operated-algebra classifications and isolate the role of field descent from the elementary subalgebra/projection mechanism.

## Restrictions
No claim is made about rank two or higher, nonreduced algebras, or independent confirmation of novelty. The computational examples corroborate, but do not replace, the algebraic proof.
