Every bounded injective unilateral weighted shift on complex \(\ell_p\), \(1\le p<\infty\), is exactly three commuting edges from rank one: no non-scalar element of its commutant has an eigenvalue, so a two-edge chain is impossible. A compact, quasinilpotent shift belonging to every Schatten class therefore gives a negative answer to Szczepanski's 2026 Problem 4.5.
