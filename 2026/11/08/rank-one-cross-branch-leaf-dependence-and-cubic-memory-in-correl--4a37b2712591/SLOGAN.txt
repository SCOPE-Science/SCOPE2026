In two uniform-attachment recursive trees that share all choices until time \(t\) and then grow independently, rooted terminal-count histories have an exact rank-one cross-branch covariance kernel and positively associated increasing events. Their terminal-count correlation at size \(n\) equals \(t(t-1)^2/[n(n-1)^2]\) for \(t\ge3\) (zero at \(t=2\)), converging to the cubic memory law \(\alpha^3\) when \(t/n\to\alpha\), with a matching bivariate Gaussian limit.
