Over every odd finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), split \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) has exactly \(2q^3-q-1\) nonzero rank-one weight-zero Lie Rota–Baxter operators, of which \((q+1)(q^2-1)\) are square-zero and \(q^2\) are idempotent. The reduced rank-at-most-one locus is the union of two irreducible three-dimensional cones meeting in a two-dimensional cone.
