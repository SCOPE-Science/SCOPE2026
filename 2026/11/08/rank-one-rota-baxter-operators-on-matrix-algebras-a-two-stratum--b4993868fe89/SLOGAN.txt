Rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on \(M_n(k)\) have exactly two geometric types: their image line is square-zero, or contains a unique idempotent, with explicitly described annihilator/off-diagonal functionals. Over every \(\mathbb F_q\) this yields an exact count for all \(n\), and the dominant type switches from idempotent at \(n=2\) to square-zero of matrix rank one for \(n\ge3\).
