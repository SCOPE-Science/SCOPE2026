For every field and every square-zero local algebra \(k1\oplus V\) with \(\dim V\ge2\), all rank-at-most-one Rota–Baxter systems split into seven intrinsic image/rank categories, with a closed formula counting every ordered pair over \(\mathbb F_q\). The two irreducible radical-image incidence strata have dimensions \(3m-1\) and \(4m-4\), exchanging which is larger at \(m=4\).
