For Ewens-weighted permutations, the six length-three ordinal-pattern distributions at different positions differ in exactly one direction: their total-variation distance is \(2|i-j||\theta^2-1|/[(\theta+n-1)(\theta+n-2)(\theta+n-3)]\). The parameter of greatest positional separation changes sharply at size five, and for large sizes the optimal bias is \(\theta\sim2n\) with limiting maximal distance \(8/27\).
