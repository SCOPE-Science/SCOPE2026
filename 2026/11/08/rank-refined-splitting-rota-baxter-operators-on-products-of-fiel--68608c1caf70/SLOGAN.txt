Every splitting nonzero-weight Rota–Baxter operator on a product of \(n\) copies of a field is counted by a properly colored rooted tree; operators of rank \(k\) are enumerated exactly by spanning trees of \(K_{1,k,n-k}\), independently of the field. A uniformly chosen operator has rank \(n/2+\sqrt n\,Z_n\), where \(Z_n\) converges to a centered Gaussian of variance \(1/16\).
