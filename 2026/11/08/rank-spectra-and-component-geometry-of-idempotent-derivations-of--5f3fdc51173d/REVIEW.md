# Review of the Heisenberg idempotent-derivation rank-spectrum theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The center-preserving normal form is forced by the Lie derivation identity. Equations (1)–(3) in RESULT.md are direct polynomial identities valid in every characteristic. The idempotent decomposition of the underlying symplectic space separates three disjoint cases, proving necessity, sufficiency and rank in each. Orbit–stabilizer counts use standard, explicitly defined symplectic and general-linear group orders. The geometric loci are vector bundles over explicit smooth irreducible open subvarieties of Grassmannians and Lagrangian Grassmannian products. Distinct characteristic-polynomial fibers cannot meet. The supplied standalone exhaustive checker tests the original Leibniz identity and idempotence in five complete finite-field configurations, including \(\mathbb F_4\). Risk: exhaustive finite tests cannot prove the arbitrary-dimensional classification; that claim rests on the detailed algebraic proof.

**Originality — PASS, provisionally.** The most relevant source, Calderón–Draper–Martín–Sánchez, arXiv:1405.4093 (2014), Section 3, Theorem 1, **already** provides a homogeneous symplectic basis and classification of Heisenberg group gradings up to equivalence. Thus orbit-level grading classification is prior work and is not asserted new. Its inspected full text does not give these specific **labeled finite-field rank counts** or the component dimensions of the idempotent-derivation variety. Search comparisons against related finite-field grading work (Eick–Moede 2022), research records of square-zero and local Heisenberg derivations, and the mathematical research index uncovered no equivalent explicit rank-spectrum result. Risk: a broader enumeration of coarse Heisenberg gradings or projectors could imply these formulas despite not appearing in the inspected sources; priority is not independently certified.

**Value — PASS.** Exact rank spectra of idempotent derivations measure splitting structures on finite nilpotent Lie algebras, and their count cannot be read off from the number of equivalence classes of gradings. The result identifies a sharp arithmetic characteristic boundary, all ranks, multiplicities, and smooth-component dimensions, uniformly in the Heisenberg dimension and field size. The precise counts are natural labeled invariants for modular Lie gradings and for finite-field algebraic varieties of operators. The theorem does not imply similar statements for higher nilpotence, other nilpotent Lie algebras or the nonreduced scheme.

Closest primary literature: arXiv:1405.4093, full available text, Sections 2–3, especially Theorem 1 and the automorphism group discussion. Related but not covering: arXiv:2205.03155 (simple Lie algebras), and existing records treating Heisenberg square-zero derivations or local derivations.
