Idempotent derivations of every finite-field Heisenberg Lie algebra have a complete rank spectrum given by symplectic-subspace and transverse-Lagrangian counts. In odd characteristic only ranks \(0\) and \(n+1\) occur; in characteristic two, every even rank from \(0\) to \(2n\) also occurs, and the reduced parameter variety has \(n+2\) smooth irreducible components instead of two.
