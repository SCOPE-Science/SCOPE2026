For the class-two path graphical groups of odd prime exponent, abelian subgroups containing the derived subgroup have an exact all-rank, support-refined rational generating function. At every fixed positive quotient rank, their canonical vertex support obeys a Gaussian law with explicit density \(\frac12+\frac{p-2}{2\sqrt{p^2+4(p^r-p)}}\) and variance coefficient \(\frac{p(p^r-1)}{(p^2+4(p^r-p))^{3/2}}\).
