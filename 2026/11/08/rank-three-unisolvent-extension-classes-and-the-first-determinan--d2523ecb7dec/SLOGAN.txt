Every nonsimple rank-three \(m\)-unisolvent system of diagonal exponent five over a fixed \(\mathbb P^1\) is split or is uniquely classified by one of two projective extension families of dimensions \(2m(m-2)-1\) and \(2(m-2)-1\) for \(m\ge3\). At \(m=4\), the latter family has an exact smooth-quadric jumping wall between bundle splittings \((5,5,5)\) and \((6,5,4)\).
