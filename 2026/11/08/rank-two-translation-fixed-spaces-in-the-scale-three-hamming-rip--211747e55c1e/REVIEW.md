# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The orbit-barycenter model follows from equality of barycentric coefficients on group orbits; because the Rips complex is flag, cross-orbit diameter checks suffice. The classification of binary rank-two codes follows from the three column multiplicities \(a,b,c\) and the inequalities \(a+b,a+c,b+c\le3\). Each of the four canonical small-support cases has been checked directly, including the support-four missing-perfect-matching quotient and the parity change identifying the mixed-weight support-three graph with a cube graph. The scale-two point cases, boundary \(n=2\), full-support plane counts, and the integral graph homotopy deductions are explicitly accounted for. The independent executable enumerated all 14,311 rank-two subgroups up to cube dimension eight and reconstructed their orbit graphs; finite checks supplement the unrestricted proof rather than substitute for it.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk

The central source, Galetto–Montaño–Wellner (arXiv:2606.20784, Theorem 6.2 and Section 8), addresses ambient characteristic-zero homology representations. Bendersky–Elia–Grbić (arXiv:2605.00705) addresses ambient cohomology and connectivity. The new contribution is the scale-three classification of **simultaneous geometric fixed spaces** of all rank-two translation groups, and its complete subgroup counts; the scale-two discrete case is contextual, not separately claimed as new. The scale-three result is not implied by the cited ambient or one-involution homotopy data. No equivalent all-rank-two scale-three classification was identified by targeted searches. Residual risk: general fixed-point theory or unindexed coding-theoretic/equivariant topology literature could provide an equivalent formulation. This is a literature-based self-review, not an independent originality certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A natural open-ended direction in the recent hypercube Rips symmetry literature is to understand topology of actual fixed spaces, which is more refined than character decompositions. This theorem gives the first complete noncyclic translation-subgroup stratum at the studied critical scale, with a sharp support threshold, an explicit connected/disconnected/empty trichotomy, exact integral homotopy types and closed counts for each behavior. The support-four phenomenon produces multiple **disconnected** circles where the support-three case yields a connected cube graph; it is not a reparameterization of a known ambient Betti-number formula.

## Scope and residual uncertainty

The conclusion is specific to binary Hamming Rips complexes through scale three and rank-two translations. It does not identify the complete equivariant homotopy type. An equivalent theorem in unindexed work remains possible. The primary literature was inspected in relevant substantive sections, not taken as a complete proof of novelty; independent validation is not performed.
