Every ordered family of mutually annihilating operators self-adjoint for a fixed nondegenerate symmetric form over an odd finite field has a totally isotropic common image, and its entire joint-image and individual-rank distribution has an exact Gaussian–Möbius formula. In dimension four over \(\mathbb F_3\), the split and nonsplit forms admit respectively 5697 and 81 ordered pairs, illustrating a sharp Witt-type distinction.
