With three uniformly ranked alternatives and only each voter's first choice approved, the approval-weighted veto core is empty with probability tending to one at exact exponential rate \(\frac16\log(27/16)\). The probability of at least one consented alternative has an exact finite multinomial formula and, when the voter count is divisible by six, a sharp \(n^{-3/2}(16/27)^{n/6}\) asymptotic with explicit rational prefactor.
