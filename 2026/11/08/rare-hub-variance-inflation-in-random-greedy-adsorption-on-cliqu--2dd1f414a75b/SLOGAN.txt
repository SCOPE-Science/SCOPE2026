On a wheel, a uniform-order random greedy independent set has Gaussian \(\sqrt n\)-scale weak fluctuations with variance \(e^{-4}\), yet its actual variance per rim vertex converges to the strictly larger value \(e^{-4}+(1-e^{-2})^2/4\). Adding a clique of universal hubs gives an exact mixture law and a sharp transition from invisible rare outliers to a macroscopic two-point limit.
