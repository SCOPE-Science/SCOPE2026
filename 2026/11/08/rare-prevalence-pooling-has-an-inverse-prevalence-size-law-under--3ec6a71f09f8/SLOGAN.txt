For noiseless two-stage screening of exchangeable beta-binomial infection clusters, a one-crossing rule identifies the globally optimal integer pool size whenever \(\kappa p<1\), where \(\kappa=\rho^{-1}-1\) and \(\rho\) is intracluster infection correlation. At fixed positive correlation and rare prevalence \(p\), optimal pool size scales as \(1/(\kappa p)\) and expected tests per person as \(\kappa p\log(1/p)\), sharply unlike independent screening.
