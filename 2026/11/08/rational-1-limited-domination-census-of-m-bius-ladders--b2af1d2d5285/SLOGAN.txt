For every Möbius ladder with \(n\ge2\) rungs, the minimum 1-limited dominating set has size \(n\) exactly when \(4\mid n\), and otherwise has size \(n+1\). The numbers of labelled minimum sets are respectively \(4\), \(2n\) for \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(3n\) for odd \(n\); a rational generating function also counts all feasible sets by size.
