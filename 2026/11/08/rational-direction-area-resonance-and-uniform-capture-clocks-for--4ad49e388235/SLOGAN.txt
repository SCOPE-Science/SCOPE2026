For a rational-direction one-sided two-square flow with reduced slope denominator \(q\) and barrier length \(b\), the recurrent area is exactly \(2-\min\{1,qb\}\), unlike the irrational-direction area \(1\). When \(qb<1\), the passage count for a transient orbit to join the periodic attractor is exactly uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,q\}\), with finite-time image area \(2-nb\) through step \(q\).
