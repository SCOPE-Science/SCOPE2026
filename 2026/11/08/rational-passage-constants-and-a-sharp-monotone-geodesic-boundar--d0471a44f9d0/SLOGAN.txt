For first-passage percolation on the second power of the integer line with i.i.d. edge weights \(1\) or \(2\), a sharp geodesic-shortcut lemma yields a four-state Markov chain and explicit rational formulas for both the propagation speed and Gaussian variance. At equal probabilities they are \(13/19\) and \(544/6859\), and the monotone-geodesic support-ratio bound \(2\) is optimal.
