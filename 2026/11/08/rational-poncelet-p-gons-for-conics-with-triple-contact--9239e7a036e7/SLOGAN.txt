For every odd-prime-power field \(\mathbb F_q\), every ordered pair of smooth conics meeting with multiplicities \((3,1)\) supports exactly \((q-p)/(2p)\) simple rational Poncelet \(p\)-gons, counted up to rotation and reversal. Thus geometric \(p\)-periodicity yields no simple polygon over \(\mathbb F_p\), but yields at least one over every proper extension.
