# Review of the Petersen matching-complex rational character

**Correctness (PASS).** The new claim concerns the ordinary simplicial matching complex of the ten-vertex Petersen graph, not the standard matching complex of the complete graph on five vertices. The delivered program enumerates all simplices, certifies the exact rational boundary ranks \(14,61,79,6\), and verifies every one of the \(120\) signed chain actions commutes with the differential. Exact image-basis trace calculations in all seven conjugacy classes give degree-two and degree-three characters respectively \(\operatorname{sgn}(g)f(g)\) and \(\binom{f(g)}2+t(g)-f(g)\). The character identifications follow from ordinary rational representation theory; the quotient follows from finite-group rational transfer. The executable is a self-contained exact finite proof, with no dependence on finite sampling, floating-point ranks, or a conjectural extension.

**Originality (PASS, subject to residual priority risk).** The publisher preview of Jonsson's 2007 matching chapter describes matching-complex methodology but did not allow full chapter inspection. The full accepted manuscript of Bate–Everitt–Ford–Ramos (2026), Sections 1–3, treats complete-hypergraph matching complexes and general fibre-closed families, not this noncomplete Petersen graph. The inspected full text of Berghoff (2022), Example 6.2, computes \(\operatorname{Ind}(P)\), which is not \(\mathcal M(P)=\operatorname{Ind}(L(P))\). Previous matching-complex homotopy results give Betti numbers but not the natural \(S_5\)-characters. Published-result index and web searches for the domain, line-graph alias, Specht modules, equivalent character formulas and dominating theorems did not identify coverage. Search failure alone cannot establish publication priority; the unavailable chapter and unindexed literature are residual risks.

**Value (PASS).** This is a natural complete symmetry classification on an exceptional highly symmetric graph, in *both* nonzero degrees of its matching-complex homology. Ordinary Betti numbers cannot distinguish the degree-two signed permutation module from the degree-three irreducible Specht module. The calculation yields the non-obvious rational acyclicity of the full finite-symmetry orbit space and provides a structural benchmark for equivariant topology of graph matching complexes. It is not an arbitrary parameter slice or a renamed homology rank.

**Boundary of proof.** Only rational representations and rational quotient homology are proved. No integral representation lattice, ring structure or equivariant homotopy equivalence is claimed. The SymPy-based verification has been run from the delivered code; its output matches the attached record.
