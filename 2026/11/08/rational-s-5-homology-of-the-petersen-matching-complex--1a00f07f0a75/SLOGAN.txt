The ordinary Petersen matching complex has complete rational \(S_5\)-homology modules \(\widetilde H_2\cong\operatorname{sgn}\otimes\mathbb Q^{[5]}\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong S^{(3,2)}\), with no other reduced rational homology. Neither module contains an invariant line, so the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
