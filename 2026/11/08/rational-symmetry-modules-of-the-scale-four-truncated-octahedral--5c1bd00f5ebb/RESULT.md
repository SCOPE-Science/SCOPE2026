# Rational symmetry modules of the scale-four truncated-octahedral Rips complex

## Finding
Let \(G\) be the adjacent-transposition Cayley graph on the \(24\) permutations of four letters, equivalently the one-skeleton of the truncated octahedron, and let \(K=\mathrm{VR}_{\le4}(V(G),d_G)\) use inclusive shortest-path distance. The natural commuting actions by relabelling the four symbols and reversing the four positions give \(\Gamma=S_4\times C_2\). If \(\varepsilon\) is the nontrivial one-dimensional character of \(C_2\), then
\[
\widetilde H_4(K;\mathbb Q)\cong\bigl(S^{(2,1,1)}\boxtimes\mathbf1\bigr)\oplus\bigl(S^{(2,2)}\boxtimes\varepsilon\bigr),\qquad
\widetilde H_5(K;\mathbb Q)\cong\bigl(\mathbf1\oplus\operatorname{sgn}\bigr)\boxtimes\mathbf1.
\]
All other reduced rational homology groups vanish. Thus the full \(48\)-element graph symmetry group has no invariant fourth-homology class but exactly one invariant fifth-homology line; in particular \(\widetilde H_j(K/\Gamma;\mathbb Q)\) is \(\mathbb Q\) for \(j=5\) and zero otherwise. The nonequivariant dimensions \(5\) and \(2\) and the wedge homotopy type are known; the claim here is the rational representation decomposition and its quotient consequence.

## Assumptions and scope
Write a vertex as a permutation \(p=(p_0,p_1,p_2,p_3)\) of \(\{0,1,2,3\}\). Edges swap adjacent positions, and \(d_G\) is graph distance, equivalently Kendall adjacent-swap distance. A subset is a simplex iff every two of its vertices have distance at most four. For \(a\in S_4\) and \(b\in\{0,1\}\), the action sends \(p\) to the permutation with entries \(a(p_{w_b(j)})\), where \(w_0(j)=j\) and \(w_1(j)=3-j\). This action is faithful, commutes between factors and preserves distance. It is the entire graph automorphism group of order \(48\) (the standard truncated-octahedral symmetry). The Specht modules \(S^{(2,1,1)}\) and \(S^{(2,2)}\) are rational irreducibles of dimensions three and two. The sign character in the second summand refers to reversal of positions, not the sign of \(S_4\).

## Proof
The graph is generated by the three adjacent position swaps. Breadth-first search reconstructs all \(24^2\) distances. Distances greater than four are forbidden pairs; their graph is four-regular with \(48\) edges. An independent-set recursion enumerates all \(17,372\) nonempty faces of \(K\), with face vector
\[
(24,228,1112,3042,4848,4592,2592,822,112).
\]
Include the empty face as an augmented cell. Sweep through the \(24\) vertex indices; at each index pair every still-unmatched face not containing that index with its admissible one-vertex extension. The resulting matching has \(8,682\) pairs and precisely six unmatched four-cells and three unmatched five-cells. Orient each Hasse cover downward except matched covers upward; exhaustive topological sorting of the resulting \(17,373\)-node graph proves acyclicity. The associated reduced integral Morse chain complex has \(C_4\cong\mathbb Z^6\) and \(C_5\cong\mathbb Z^3\); relative to the critical cells in ascending bit-mask order, its only nonzero differential sends the first and second five-cell generators to zero and the third to the primitive vector
\[
(0,1,1,0,-1,1)\in\mathbb Z^6.
\]
Thus reduced integral homology has ranks five and two in degrees four and five, respectively. No higher or lower reduced homology survives, and the primitive boundary has no torsion.

For the symmetry action, construct integer chain maps \(P\) (gradient projection) and \(I\) (gradient lift) between the augmented simplicial and the nine-generator Morse complexes. For a matched lower face \(\alpha\) paired to \(\beta\), use the exact relation
\[
P(\alpha)=-[\alpha:\beta]^{-1}\sum_{\substack{\gamma\prec\beta\\\gamma\ne\alpha}}[\gamma:\beta]P(\gamma),
\]
where the incidence coefficients are \(\pm1\), matched upper faces project to zero, and critical faces project to their basis classes. A directed acyclic dependency graph makes this recursion finite. For each critical generator, the lift starts at that simplex and successively cancels boundary coefficients along all matched lower faces in topological order. Direct checks give \(P I=\mathrm{id}\), that both maps commute with the differentials in the relevant degrees, and the displayed Morse differential. Acyclic algebraic Morse cancellation proves these maps induce homology isomorphisms.

For each of the \(48\) symmetries \(g\), compute the chain map \(P g I\) on the six and three critical generators, keeping the sign of the induced permutation of the ordered vertices of every face. Its quotient and kernel actions give the following characters; rows label the cycle type of \(a\), and the pair in each cell is \((\chi_4,\chi_5)\):

| Cycle type | \(b=0\) | \(b=1\) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \(1^4\) | \((5,2)\) | \((1,2)\) |
| \(2,1,1\) | \((-1,0)\) | \((-1,0)\) |
| \(2,2\) | \((1,2)\) | \((-3,2)\) |
| \(3,1\) | \((-1,2)\) | \((1,2)\) |
| \(4\) | \((1,0)\) | \((1,0)\) |

The \(S_4\) character of \(S^{(2,1,1)}\) is the sign twist of the standard three-dimensional representation. The character of \(S^{(2,2)}\) is the two-dimensional augmentation representation on the three partitions of four symbols into pairs. Comparing these characters with the table proves the decompositions; the fifth-degree character is the sum of the trivial and alternating characters, independent of \(b\). As an independent full-chain consistency test, for all \(48\) elements the signed alternating count of fixed oriented simplices equals \(\chi_4-\chi_5\). Direct projection of the two closed five-dimensional lifted chains reproduces the fifth-degree character separately.

For a finite group acting simplicially, rational homology of the quotient agrees with the invariants of rational homology after barycentric subdivision (which eliminates inversions). Neither fourth-degree constituent is trivial under \(S_4\), whereas the fifth degree contains one trivial constituent. This proves the quotient statement.

## Relationship to prior work
Adamaszek (2011) identifies graph-power clique complexes with intrinsic graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complexes and develops general theory, but not this symmetry module. Saleh, Titz Mite and Witzel (2023) classify the Platonic solids, explicitly leaving Archimedean solids for extension. Two published computations of this exact permutahedron already give the nonequivariant fourth-stage homotopy type and its mod-two persistence. They therefore **cover** the Betti-number and wedge assertions, which are used solely as context and cross-checks; their inspected complete result statements do **not** determine either rational \(S_4\times C_2\)-module or the quotient invariant line. The precise new information is the split symmetry character in two adjacent homology degrees. Searches under Kendall distance, adjacent transpositions, graph powers, truncated octahedron, equivariant Rips homology and homology characters found no covering representation calculation.

## Limitations
This result concerns the single scale-four stage, not the equivariant homotopy type of the entire filtration or the integral isomorphism type as a group-ring lattice. The quotient statement is rational; it does not assert contractibility or compute its integral homology. Publication priority is not absolute: equivalent unindexed representation calculations remain possible.
