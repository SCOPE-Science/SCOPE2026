At scale four, the truncated-octahedral graph's Vietoris–Rips complex has known reduced homology ranks five and two, but its full \(S_4\times C_2\) rational symmetry modules are \(S^{(2,1,1)}\boxtimes\mathbf1\oplus S^{(2,2)}\boxtimes\varepsilon\) in degree four and \((\mathbf1\oplus\operatorname{sgn})\boxtimes\mathbf1\) in degree five. Exactly one rational homology class survives quotienting by the full symmetry group, in degree five.
