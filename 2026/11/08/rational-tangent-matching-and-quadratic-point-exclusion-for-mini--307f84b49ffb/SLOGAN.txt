For every geometrically smooth, geometrically integral, minimum-bidegree space-filling complete intersection \(C\subset\mathbb P^3_{\mathbb F_q}\), its rational tangent lines match the points with the lines of the symplectic generalized quadrangle \(W(3,q)\) exactly once each. Every rational line meets \(C\) only at rational points (with one doubled point on isotropic lines); in particular \(\#C(\mathbb F_{q^2})=\#C(\mathbb F_q)=q^3+q^2+q+1\) and the first two zeta numerator coefficients are fixed.
