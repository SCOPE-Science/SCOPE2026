For every \(n\ge5\), the pentad variety \(\operatorname{Pen}_n\), formed from coordinatewise Pluecker ratios of two copies of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,n)\), is rational with an explicit cross-ratio inverse. Generically the ratio map has exactly one fiber component, a free orbit of the simultaneous \((\mathbb C^*)^{n-1}\) column-rescaling torus.
