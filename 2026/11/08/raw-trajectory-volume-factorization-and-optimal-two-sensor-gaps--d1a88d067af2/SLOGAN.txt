The Gram determinant of two untreated dynamical sensor trajectories on a low-pass cycle is an evolution-dependent constant times the universal product \(\prod_{k=1}^{r}\sin^2(2\pi kd/N)\). For five modes, every D-optimal sensor gap is found by comparing at most two lattice angles around \(\arcsin\sqrt{2/3}\), and the optimum eventually differs strictly from the best spectrally demixed conditioning placement.
