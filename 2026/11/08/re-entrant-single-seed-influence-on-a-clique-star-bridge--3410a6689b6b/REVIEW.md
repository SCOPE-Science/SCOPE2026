# Review: Re-entrant single-seed influence on a clique–star bridge

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is an exact probability result on a finite simple graph for every integer \(q\ge4\), with a complete nine-vertex switching classification. The one-bridge decomposition gives the expected component sizes \(I_u=1+qp+pc_q\) and \(I_v=c_q+p(1+qp)\), where \(c_q=1+(q-1)r_q\). A samplewise center-versus-leaf comparison and strict gateway-versus-unmarked-clique comparison rule out other maximizers. Two-edge-disjoint paths bound \(r_q(1/2)\ge23/32\), proving the strict intermediate reversal for every \(q\ge4\); endpoint limits prove the reverse ordering near both ends. For \(q=2,3\), closed reliability formulas give strict star-center superiority. For \(q=4\), conditioning on the last clique edge gives the complete two-terminal reliability polynomial. A fractional-linear substitution transforms the difference quintic into a polynomial with exactly two coefficient-sign changes; strict signs at \(0,1/2,1\) yield exactly two interior roots and the claimed optimizer intervals. The accompanying `verify.py` independently enumerates all \(2^{11}\) edge states, checks exact clique and all-seed polynomials, rational root brackets and representative extrema, and passes 68 exact assertions. The infinitely many clique sizes are established by proof, not extrapolated from enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, with residual literature risk
The exact clique connectivity probability and independent-edge cluster reach are established tools and are not claimed new. The assessed contribution is their application to an explicit infinite, balanced star–clique family giving two distinct *global seed-optimizer reversals*, the first family size and an exact two-threshold classification there. Lin et al. (2022, arXiv:2206.08764) treat epidemic-spreader ranking via dynamical approximation rather than an exact algebraic parameter phase diagram. Zhang et al. (2023, arXiv:2311.08663) optimize influence in temporally coupled multiplex networks by heuristics, not in this fixed finite graph. Min (2017, arXiv:1710.07064) uses message passing for a locally tree-like model; Mann et al. (2021, arXiv:2102.09261) supply clique and motif percolation machinery, not a finite-graph seed-selection phase diagram. Erkol et al. (2019, doi:10.1038/s41598-019-51209-6) compare empirical spreader heuristics. Distinct nearby work on percolation covariances, graph curvature and deterministic diffusion does not imply these exact seed thresholds. Semantic mathematical-record searches and accessible prior statements did not identify a covering result. An older study of parameter-sensitive epidemic centrality (Šikić et al., 2013, doi:10.1140/epjb/e2013-31025-5) was accessible only through bibliographic/summary material; its detailed contents remain a specific unresolved priority risk, not evidence of noncoverage. An unindexed equivalent cannot be ruled out.

## Value — PASS
A core–periphery contact network with a clique and a star provides a natural test bed for choosing a unique origin of information or independent-edge contagion. The two competing gateway candidates have different degrees; the strict double reversal shows that a higher-degree hub is neither always best nor always worse at larger transmission rates. The result is structural rather than a single numerical example: it gives arbitrarily large graphs with this behavior, an exact lower bound on the advantage at a natural transmission value, an explicit minimal example within its family, and exact transition boundaries. It gives a fully reproducible benchmark for assessing structural seed-ranking proxies and any technique claiming parameter-insensitive optimality. The exact objective and fixed topology are essential to the statement; nothing is inferred for multiseed, temporal, recovery-time-correlated, or heterogeneous-transmission models.

## Scope and limitations
All results concern a single seed and independent-edge final-size reach on the specified balanced star–clique bridge. The all-size theorem guarantees at least two transitions, not exactly two when \(q>4\). The exact two-switch result is only claimed for \(q=4\). At \(p=0\) or \(p=1\) every seed ties and these endpoints are excluded. The numerical verifier is corroborative; it does not establish universal statements or publication priority.
