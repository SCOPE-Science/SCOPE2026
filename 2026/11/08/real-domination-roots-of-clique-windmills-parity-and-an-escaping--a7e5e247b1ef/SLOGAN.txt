Clique windmills exhibit a complete parity trichotomy for real domination roots: depending on the blade size and blade count, they have zero, one, or two nonzero real roots. For odd blade size \(k\), a negative root escapes at the sharp rate \(-\big(km/\log m\big)^{1/k}\) as the number \(m\) of blades grows, unlike the two bounded roots known for triangular windmills.
