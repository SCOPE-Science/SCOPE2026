The indicator of any nondegenerate Boolean quadratic zero set is a deterministic real Gabor window whose full Walsh time-frequency orbit recovers **every real symmetric matrix**, with maximum rank \(N(N+1)/2\). Its complete lifting spectrum has only three positive levels, and the sharp positive-spectrum condition number is \(\sqrt N+\varepsilon\), where \(\varepsilon\) is the quadratic Gauss sign.
