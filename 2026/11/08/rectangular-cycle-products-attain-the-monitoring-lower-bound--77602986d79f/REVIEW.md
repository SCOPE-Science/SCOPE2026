# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The fiber characterization from Haslegrave's Theorem 1 is used with its exact hypotheses. Every cycle fiber requires at least three probes. The three-band construction supplies exactly three selected vertices per short fiber; the row preimage calculation and ceiling bound prove every long fiber is monitored. The six-residue period inequality has been checked at its small boundaries, and the two exceptional parameter pairs are handled with explicit row lists. Independent direct edge-deletion tests confirm the interpretation of monitoring on eight graphs. The computation does not replace the parameter-uniform argument.

**Originality — PASS (provisional priority).** The closest published result is Haslegrave's square-torus formula, which is explicitly excluded from the asserted new coverage. His general product bounds and exact fiber condition do not determine the unequal-cycle optimum. Dev et al.'s cycle value supplies the necessary scalar input. Searches for the rectangular-torus formula, equivalent terminology and relevant research records did not reveal a stronger applicable statement. The relevant original full-text sections and follow-up paper introduction were inspected; an unindexed equal-strength argument remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** Equal-length tori were already the canonical sharpness example for the lower product bound. The unexplored unequal-length regime is a natural structural question: the known bounds leave a potentially growing gap, and a balanced incidence construction is needed because the obvious diagonal shifts fail away from the square. Exact sharpness for all aspect ratios and explicit correcting templates are meaningful beyond the known diagonal value. This is an exact new infinite-family invariant, not a routine numerical re-evaluation.

**Limits.** No full classification of optimizers, no extension to strong graph products, and no independent review. Publication priority remains provisional.
