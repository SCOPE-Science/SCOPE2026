For the disk Bergman weight \(|z|^{2a}(1-|z|^2)^4\), kernel zeros disappear at the exact threshold \(a=(\sqrt{15+2\sqrt{19}}-5)/2\) but reappear as a nonreal conjugate pair when \(a>1\). The zero-free interval and both boundary-crossing mechanisms follow from an explicit degree-five numerator and an exact Routh array.
