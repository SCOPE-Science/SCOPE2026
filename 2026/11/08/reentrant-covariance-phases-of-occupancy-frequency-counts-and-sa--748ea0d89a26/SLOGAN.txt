In uniform occupancy, the covariance of each frequency count with the number of colliding pairs has a universal positive–negative–positive profile, and fixing a frequency yields a reentrant sample-size phase with infinitely many exact double-zero cases. In particular, the Good–Turing singleton count reverses its covariance with Simpson concentration exactly at sample size \(n=2m\) for \(m\) equally likely categories, while richness retains the opposite diversity-covariance sign.
