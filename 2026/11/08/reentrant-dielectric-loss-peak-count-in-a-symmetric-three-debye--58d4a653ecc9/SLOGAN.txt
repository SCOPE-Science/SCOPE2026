Three ideal Debye loss components with equal outer strengths and geometrically spaced relaxation times can exhibit a reentrant \(1\to3\to2\to3\) sequence of strict frequency-domain peak counts as their times separate. This occurs for every central strength \(\frac12<a<\frac4{3\sqrt6}\), with all three exact thresholds given by \(s_0=4a\) and the two roots of \(as^3-2s^2+4=0\) above \(2\), where \(s=(r+r^{-1})/2\).
