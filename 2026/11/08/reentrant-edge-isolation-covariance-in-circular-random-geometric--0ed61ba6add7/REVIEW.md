Exact finite-sample dependence is not captured by their separate expectations or marginal limit laws. The surprising negative–positive–negative radius diagram, universal six-vertex onset, and sparse critical scale \(nr=4/3\) explain competing loss-of-incident-edges and clustering-within-an-available-arc mechanisms, not an arbitrarily chosen slice.

**Limitations.** Fixed-size iid uniform points on a unit circle, hard distance-threshold graph, \(n\ge2\), \(0<r<1/2\). No assertion for other geometries, Poissonized input, soft edges, or higher dimensions. Dense-limit covariance magnitude may be extremely small. Numerical experiments are supplementary to the proof.
