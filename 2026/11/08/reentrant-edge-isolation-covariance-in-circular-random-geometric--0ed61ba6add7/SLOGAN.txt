In fixed-size circular random geometric graphs, the covariance between edge count and isolated-vertex count is negative at every radius for \(n\le5\), but for every \(n\ge6\) it undergoes two exact radius-driven reversals: negative, positive, negative. The first positive window occurs at six vertices, precisely \(1/4<r<1/3\), and the lower transition has sparse scale \(nr\to4/3\).
