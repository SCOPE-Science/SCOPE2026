Under a fixed three-step mean and four-step deadline, the optimal restart clock for a localized three-state Grover quantum walker switches from deterministic to two different randomized laws and then back to deterministic as the self-loop weight \(\ell\) increases. The matching independent-step classical lazy walk always prefers deterministic restarting; the quantum-only intermediate regimes and their three exact switching thresholds follow from a complete renewal-policy optimization.
