In a planar Poisson directional nearest-neighbor graph, directed cycles of every length \(k\ge3\) have positive spatial density at every strictly reflex aperture \(\pi<\alpha<2\pi\), but are absent at and below a half-plane and at the full-plane endpoint. This reentrant cycle spectrum has continuous Palm probabilities, an exact Poisson void integral and a lower density bound uniform over compact reflex-aperture ranges.
