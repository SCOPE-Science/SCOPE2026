The triangular-marker word \(1\,0^1 1\,0^2 1\,0^3 1\cdots\) has reflection complexity with exact period-six quadratic law \(R(6m+s)=6m^2+2(s+1)m+c_s\), where \((c_0,\ldots,c_5)=(1,2,2,4,5,7)\). Its reversal classes are asymptotically two-thirds as numerous as its distinct contiguous factors; all reflected nonpalindromic pairs contain at most two ones.
