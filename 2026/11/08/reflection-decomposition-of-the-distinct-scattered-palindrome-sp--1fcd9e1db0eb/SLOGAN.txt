For any finite palindrome \(AA^{\mathrm R}\), the distinct scattered palindromes at lengths \(2m-1\) and \(2m\) are both counted by the distinct length-\(m\) subsequences of its left half \(A\). For \(AcA^{\mathrm R}\), the entire graded spectrum is again determined by the left half, with only the subsequences ending in the central letter \(c\) needed for the exact odd-length correction.
