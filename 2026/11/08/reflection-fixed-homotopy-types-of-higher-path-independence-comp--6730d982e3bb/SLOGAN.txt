For every path \(P_n\) and every \(r\ge1\), reflection fixes a space whose integral homotopy type is explicitly either a single sphere, a point, or, uniquely at \((n,r)=(2,1)\), empty. The sphere dimensions and precisely the noncontractible congruence classes follow from an acyclic matching with period \(r+2\) in the number of reflection-paired vertices.
