# Reflection-fixed spheres of three-cut complexes of cycle-complement graphs

## Finding
Let \(n\ge5\), let \(G_n=K_n\setminus E(C_n)\) on the cyclic vertex set \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), and let \(X_n=\Delta_3(G_n)\) be its ordinary three-cut complex: its facets are complements of three-vertex subsets inducing disconnected graphs. For every reflection \(s_a(i)=a-i\pmod n\), write \(f(n,a)=|\{i:s_a(i)=i\}|\) and \(m(n,a)=(n+f(n,a))/2\). Then the geometric fixed-point space satisfies
\[
 |X_n|^{\langle s_a\rangle}\simeq S^{\lfloor(m(n,a)-3)/2\rfloor}.
\]
More precisely, the fixed space is piecewise-linearly homeomorphic to the noncover complex \(Y_m\) of an explicit interval hypergraph on \(m=m(n,a)\) vertices. These complexes obey \(Y_m\simeq\Sigma Y_{m-2}\) for every \(m\ge5\), with \(Y_3\simeq Y_4\simeq S^0\). Every fixed space therefore has exactly one nonzero reduced integral homology group, isomorphic to \(\mathbb Z\), in degree \(\lfloor(m-3)/2\rfloor\).

## Assumptions and scope
The cycle \(C_n\) has edges between consecutive residues. \(G_n\) includes precisely the other pairs. The action is the induced simplicial action on vertices of \(X_n\), and the fixed point space is the fixed subset of the **geometric realization** under this action. This is not the induced subcomplex on individually fixed original vertices. For odd \(n\), \(f=1\); for even \(n\), the vertex-axis reflections have \(f=2\) and edge-axis reflections have \(f=0\). Thus the formula covers both even-axis conjugacy types. No claim about fixed points of arbitrary subgroups or the full equivariant homotopy type is made.

For a hypergraph \(\mathcal H\) on a set \(V\), its noncover complex is the simplicial complex generated by the sets \(V\setminus E\) as \(E\) runs over hyperedges. Set \(V_m=\{0,\dots,m-1\}\) and, for \(m\ge3\), define
\[
\mathcal H_m=\{\{0,1\},\{m-2,m-1\}\}\cup\{\{j,j+1,j+2\}:1\le j\le m-4\},\qquad Y_m=\operatorname{NC}(\mathcal H_m).
\]
An empty last set is intended when \(m=3,4\). This definition is meaningful at every quotient size arising from \(n\ge5\).

## Proof
**Step 1: disconnected triples are exactly deleted-cycle arcs.** Each vertex of \(G_n\) has exactly two nonneighbors, its predecessor and successor on \(C_n\). A disconnected graph on three vertices has an isolated vertex. Therefore a three-vertex induced subgraph of \(G_n\) is disconnected exactly when its vertices are a three-term consecutive cyclic arc \(\{i-1,i,i+1\}\). Consequently \(X_n\) is the noncover complex of the three-uniform tight cyclic hypergraph with those arcs as edges.

**Step 2: the fixed-point complex has orbit vertices.** A geometric point of a simplicial complex has unique barycentric coordinates. It is fixed by \(s_a\) exactly when the coordinates are constant on every orbit of \(s_a\). Its nonzero support is therefore a union of complete orbits. A potential vertex for each orbit (omitting orbits that never form faces), realized as the orbit's uniformly weighted barycenter, gives a piecewise-linear homeomorphism between the geometric fixed-point set and the complex whose faces are orbit collections with union a face of \(X_n\). The latter are exactly collections of orbits avoiding some projected cyclic three-arc.

**Step 3: quotient hyperedges are a boundary-shortened path.** The \(s_a\)-orbits of the original cycle form a linearly ordered path with \(m=(n+f)/2\) vertices. A cyclic three-arc projects to either a pair of adjacent quotient vertices at either end or a triple of consecutive quotient vertices in the interior. After deleting hyperedges that properly contain another projected hyperedge, the minimal projected edges are precisely \(\{0,1\}\), \(\{m-2,m-1\}\), and \(\{j,j+1,j+2\}\) for \(1\le j\le m-4\). Taking complements of these edges proves the asserted PL-homeomorphism with \(Y_m\).

**Step 4: an exact two-step suspension.** Let \(m\ge5\) and let \(F\) be the full simplex on \(\{2,\dots,m-1\}\), corresponding to the hyperedge \(\{0,1\}\). The union \(U\) of all other maximal complementary simplices is a cone at the vertex \(0\), because no other minimal hyperedge contains that vertex. Both \(F\) and \(U\) are contractible, and their intersection is nonempty. Its facets are complements in \(\{2,\dots,m-1\}\) of the intersections with the other minimal hyperedges. The minimal ones are \(\{2,3\}\), \(\{m-2,m-1\}\), and \(\{j,j+1,j+2\}\) for \(3\le j\le m-4\). The three-term edge beginning at \(2\) is redundant because it contains \(\{2,3\}\). Relabelling \(2\mapsto0\) identifies \(F\cap U\) simplicially with \(Y_{m-2}\). The union of two contractible CW subcomplexes with nonempty intersection is homotopy equivalent to the suspension of their intersection, yielding \(Y_m\simeq\Sigma Y_{m-2}\).

**Step 5: initial cases and induction.** \(Y_3\) consists of the two isolated vertices \(0\) and \(2\). \(Y_4\) is the disjoint union of the simplices on \(\{0,1\}\) and \(\{2,3\}\). Both are homotopy equivalent to \(S^0\). Repeated suspension gives \(Y_m\simeq S^{\lfloor(m-3)/2\rfloor}\) for all \(m\ge3\). This is an integral homotopy statement, not an inference from field homology.

## Verification
Run `python3 verify.py` with Python 3 standard library. The program constructs graph edges of \(G_n\) independently and enumerates disconnected triples, forms the quotient orbit graph for every reflection \(s_a\) for \(5\le n\le28\), checks the minimal projected hyperedges against the model, verifies the exact recursive intersection \(F\cap U\cong Y_{m-2}\), and computes the complete reduced mod-two homology for all quotient sizes through \(m=15\). The independently enumerated cases, including both even reflection types, match the theorem; see `verification_output.txt`. The finite computations corroborate the general proof and are not used to extrapolate its quantifiers.

## Relationship to prior work
Chauhan and Shukla, *Shellability of 3-cut complexes of powers of cycle graphs* (arXiv:2603.23155, first version March 24, 2026), motivate the boundary \(n=2p+3\) for three-cut complexes of cycle powers. When \(n=2p+3\) is odd, \(G_n\cong C_n^p\), so the present family includes precisely these boundary instances as well as even-order cycle complements. Their theorem addresses ordinary shellability at \(n\ge6p-3\), not geometric reflection fixed points.

Kim and Kim, *Domination numbers and noncover complexes of hypergraphs* (arXiv:2101.01850, 2021), give ordinary nonequivariant homotopy classifications of tight-cycle noncover complexes and certain one-end-shortened tight-path noncover complexes. The ordinary homotopy type of \(X_n\) is therefore credited as known; it does not determine the geometric reflection-fixed spaces. Bayer and coauthors, *Topology of Cut Complexes of Graphs* (arXiv:2304.13675, 2023/2024), develop homotopy and group-homology results for several graph families. Their equivariant homology calculations do not directly imply the present fixed-space sphere classification.

## Limitations
The result is for the complement of one spanning cycle, the ordinary three-cut complex, and single reflections of that cycle. It neither determines the full equivariant homotopy type nor constructs compatible equivalences for all subgroups. The identity between quotient orbit complexes and the boundary-shortened path is exact; the final sphere equivalence is a homotopy equivalence, not asserted to be a PL homeomorphism. Some unindexed or later literature may contain stronger equivalent statements; independent external validation has not been performed.
