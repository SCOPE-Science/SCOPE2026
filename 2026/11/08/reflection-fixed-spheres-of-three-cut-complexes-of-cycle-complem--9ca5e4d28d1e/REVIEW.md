# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The disconnected-triple characterization uses the two nonneighbors of each graph vertex and applies uniformly for every integer \(n\ge5\). The geometric fixed-point barycentric orbit model is exact. The quotient path has two short endpoint edges and consecutive interior triples. Its noncover complex decomposes into two contractible pieces with intersection the corresponding complex two quotient vertices shorter, yielding an integral suspension equivalence. Initial cases are checked directly. Finite calculations reconstruct the original graph independently for every reflection for \(5\le n\le28\) and check the recursive intersection and mod-two Betti numbers. Computations corroborate but do not substitute for proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual literature uncertainty.** The closest known theorem about the same noncover complex, Kim–Kim arXiv:2101.01850, computes its ordinary homotopy type and one-end-shortened uniform tight paths but not its reflection-fixed spaces. Chauhan–Shukla arXiv:2603.23155 treats ordinary shellability of cycle powers above another threshold. Bayer et al. arXiv:2304.13675 treat related graph cut complexes and some equivariant **homology representations**, not this geometric fixed-point model. Searches for fixed-point, reflection, tight-cycle noncover, graph complement and dihedral aliases, including graph-complex research records, produced no equivalent fixed-point sphere classification. This is not a uniqueness or publication-priority certificate; an unindexed stronger result remains possible.

## Value
**PASS.** Geometric fixed-point types are invariants beyond the ordinary nonequivariant homotopy type or rational character traces. This result gives a complete infinite-family answer for both conjugacy classes of even-cycle reflections and all odd-cycle reflections, including the boundary cases of a current cut-complex shellability problem. The exact two-vertex suspension mechanism provides reusable structural information about nonuniform endpoint-shortened interval hypergraphs rather than a one-off computation.

## Scope and limitations
No classification for other subgroups or compatible system of subgroup-fixed equivalences is claimed. The quotient identification is PL; the sphere statement is integral homotopy. A standalone finite verifier is supplied, while all-parameter validity depends on the written decomposition and suspension proof. Source searches cannot exclude unknown or unindexed stronger coverage.
