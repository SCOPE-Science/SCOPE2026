# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For G=K_n\E(C_n), each induced disconnected 3-set has an isolated vertex whose only two nonneighbors are its cyclic neighbors, so the disconnected triples are exactly cyclic length-3 arcs. Fixed barycentric coordinates are constant on complete reflection orbits; fixed geometric points are therefore represented by orbit collections with a face-union, not by only original fixed vertices. Folding a cycle gives a path on m=(n+f)/2 orbit vertices, and projected arc minimal edges are the two end pairs and interior triples starting j=1,...,m-4. Writing Y_m as a union of the complement simplex of {0,1} with the cone of all other complement facets, the intersection is exactly Y_{m-2} after relabeling; both pieces contractible and Y_3,Y_4 are homotopy S^0. Therefore Y_m is a suspension of Y_{m-2} for every m>=5, giving the sphere dimension and integral homology by induction. Independent quotient-graph/reflection-arc enumeration for all 656 pairs (n,a) with 5<=n<=36 confirmed the exact minimal-edge model, with both even-axis types. This finite check is only corroboration of the unrestricted proof.

## originality

PASS

Kim–Kim studies ordinary tight-path/cycle noncover homotopy, and the cut-complex work on powers of cycles discusses ordinary shellability; none asserts geometric reflection-fixed spaces. Deshpande–Roy–Sawant Theorem 4.12 concerns co-connected complexes generated by connected triples rather than cut facets from disconnected triples. Unlike a rational homology character, the new statement gives the actual fixed-space homotopy type for every reflection of all n>=5. Exact quotient-path and suspension identification are a qualified original refinement.
