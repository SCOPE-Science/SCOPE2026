For two uniform independent square matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact chance that their nilpotent product has one full Jordan block is \(\frac{2-q^{-1}}{2-q^{-n}}\prod_{j=2}^{n}(1-q^{-j})^2\). At every fixed field size this stays bounded away from zero as the matrix dimension grows, sharply contrasting with the upper-triangular two-factor model.
