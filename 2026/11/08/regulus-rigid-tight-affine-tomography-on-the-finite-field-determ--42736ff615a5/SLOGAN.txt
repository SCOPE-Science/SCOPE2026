For affine two-plane sums in the split determinant geometry of \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\), the complete weighted Fourier spectrum is governed by a \((q+1)\)-by-\((q+1)\) grid of left and right null-plane directions. Exactly one whole ruling is necessary and sufficient to reconstruct every mean-zero determinant-cone-bandlimited signal, and the only tight view designs are complete uniformly weighted rulings: adding a proper nonempty fraction of the opposite ruling worsens the unweighted condition number from \(1\) to \(\sqrt2\), whereas adding all of it restores \(1\).
