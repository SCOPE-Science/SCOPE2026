On inconsistent least-squares data with equal-norm orthogonal columns, the unextended full-residual surrogate-hyperplane projection has exact two-cycles at unit relaxation and globally attracting false two-cycles at every relaxation strictly between one and two. At relaxation \(\omega=3/2\), the incorrect radius attracts quadratically by the Babylonian square-root rule, while adjacent-iterate averaging nevertheless recovers the least-squares solution.
