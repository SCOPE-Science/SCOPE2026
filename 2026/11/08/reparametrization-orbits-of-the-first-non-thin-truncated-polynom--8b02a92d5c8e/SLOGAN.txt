The first truncated-polynomial algebra admitting non-thin subalgebras, \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^{14})\), has exactly \(11\) ambient-reparametrization orbits of such subalgebras over \(\mathbb F_2\) and exactly \(29\) over \(\mathbb F_4\). Complete orbit-size tables and explicit representatives distinguish the two exponent strata and refine the previously known counts of embedded subalgebras.
