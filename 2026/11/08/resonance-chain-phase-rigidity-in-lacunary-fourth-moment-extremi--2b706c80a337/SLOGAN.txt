Every fourth-moment maximizing trigonometric polynomial with the dyadic frequencies \(\{0,1,2,4,\ldots,2^{m-1}\}\), \(m\ge2\), uses every frequency and has character phases; its sharp norm increases strictly with \(m\). By contrast, the same problem at every integer lacunarity base at least three has the exact Sidon constant \(2-1/(m+1)\) and completely unrestricted maximizing phases.
