The exact claim concerns initial configurations of least period three. Every three-periodic vertex trace is either static zero or advances every step; connectivity rules out mixed behavior. The local successor-color constraint is therefore necessary and sufficient. For each petal, its interior constraints reduce to the two exhaustive difference-word types `(2...2,1...1)` and `(2...2,0,1...1)`. Modular counting yields precisely \(2\lfloor\ell/3\rfloor+1\) pinned phase patterns, and exactly two supply the hub when \(3\mid\ell\). Independent choices among petals, followed by subtraction of the assignments offering no hub successor, yield the complete source-refined polynomial. Evaluating at one gives the total census. Tree extension is unique by leaf-to-root induction. A separate brute-force transition check on 367,767 states (also tallying the exact source multiplicities) and a phase-count checker on lengths 3 through 100 corroborate critical steps. The finite computations are corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Cycle bouquets are a natural cactus subclass where several resonant loops interact through one articulation point. The theorem gives the entire source-refined initially primitive-three census for infinitely many distinct labelled graphs, identifies a sharp length-divisibility boundary, computes exactly how many independent petal sources sustain each recurrent state, and proves invariance under any number of pendant tree attachments. The difference between several triangular petals meeting at one hub and petals linked in a chain shows that the exact invariant depends on the topology of the cycle-interaction network. It is not a restatement of the general existence/activity criterion.
