For distinct critical Fock integral operators \(A_{h,j}=I^{h+j}M_{z^h}D^j\), the commutator has singular values of exact order \(n^{-2}\), except when the arithmetic indices \(h_1+2j_1\) and \(h_2+2j_2\) agree, when the order improves sharply to \(n^{-3}\). The corresponding sharp Schatten thresholds are \(p>1/2\) and \(p>1/3\), respectively, with explicit positive constants and exact weak-ideal logarithmic coefficients.
