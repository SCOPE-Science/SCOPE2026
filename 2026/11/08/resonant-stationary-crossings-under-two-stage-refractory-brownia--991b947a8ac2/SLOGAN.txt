In Brownian motion with two exponential refractory stages after each complete reset, the mean-square displacement crosses its stationary level infinitely often exactly when \(r=q\) or \(2q\le r<4q\). Near the isolated cancellation \(r=q\), the finite crossing count diverges as \((2\sqrt3/\pi)\log(1/|r/q-1|)+O(1)\).
