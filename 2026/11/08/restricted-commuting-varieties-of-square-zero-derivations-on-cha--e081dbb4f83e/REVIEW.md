# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Writing derivations in their even/odd coefficient decomposition and using the characteristic-two restricted square gives two exact annihilator equations. A minimum-valuation argument forces **every commuting tuple** into a single abelian component. The affine component dimensions, pair-intersection dimensions, and point count follow by complete linear incidence counting. Direct finite-field polynomial computations check several independent small instances without replacing the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)
Evans–Fuchs 2001 treats the restricted operator for the \(p\)-truncated Witt algebra, not the all-length characteristic-two commuting \(r\)-tuple varieties. Yao–Chang 2013 proves a two-tuple component theorem for \(p>3\), so the characteristic-two all-length object is outside its hypotheses. Premet 2003 concerns reductive Lie algebras. Known single-operator square-zero cone decompositions account for the \(r=1\) slice only. The assessed conclusion is the **common-component rigidity for arbitrary commuting tuples** and exact all-\(r\) point counts. Equivalence or stronger hidden coverage in the wider literature cannot be ruled out.

## Value — PASS
Restricted commuting varieties parametrize compatible collections of height-one infinitesimal symmetries, a natural higher-dimensional refinement of the restricted nilpotent cone. Common-component rigidity is a structural restriction on all simultaneous actions; it implies that the entire multi-parameter commuting variety is equidimensional with explicit component and intersection geometries. The all-\(N,r,q\) count is not the product of the single-operator counts, and its extension-field coincidence is a nontrivial consequence of rigidity rather than independent selection.

## Limitations
Only characteristic two, one variable, the reduced variety, and square-zero restricted powers are treated. No general higher-characteristic theorem, scheme-theoretic primary decomposition, or orbit classification follows. Historical originality is provisional; a specialized commuting-variety paper outside inspected sources remains a residual risk.
