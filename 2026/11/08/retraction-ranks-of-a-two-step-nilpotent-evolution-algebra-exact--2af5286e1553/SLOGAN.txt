For the direct sum of \(n\) copies of the two-dimensional nilpotent evolution algebra, the exact count of algebra retractions with image dimension \(2r\) is \(\binom nr(1+r(q-1))^{n-r}q^{2r(n-r)}\), and every retract is again such a direct sum. A uniformly random retraction has half-image rank concentrated on a discrete Gaussian centered at \(n/2-(\ln n)/(4\ln q)+O_q(1)\), exhibiting a logarithmic drift from half rank.
