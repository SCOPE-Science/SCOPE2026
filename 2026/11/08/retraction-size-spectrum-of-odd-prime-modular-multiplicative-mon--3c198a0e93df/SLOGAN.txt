For every odd prime \(p\) and exponent \(e\ge2\), all multiplicative retractions of \(\mathbb Z/p^e\mathbb Z\) have an exact image-size generating polynomial, and their total count is \(1+2^{\omega(p-1)+1}+(1+p^{e-2})\prod_{\ell^b\parallel(p-1)}(1+\ell^b)\). Apart from the constant map, every retraction either collapses all nonunits to zero or preserves each nonzero valuation stratum.
