# Scientific review: retractions of free generalized dimonoids

## Correctness — PASS

The symbolic reconstruction uses the free pair model with both concatenating word projections. Idempotency of the projected nonerasing free-semigroup substitution forces every letter appearing in any image to be fixed; a generator whose one-letter image is itself has no room for a nontrivial control word. Conversely all substitutions into the free subalgebra generated by those fixed letters define idempotents. The number of control words for a word of length \(j\) is exactly \(2^{j-1}\), giving the full rank profile. Fixed-rank exponential asymptotics follow by finite maximum comparison, and the Lambert \(W\) maximizer follows from strict concavity. The standalone direct-composition checker tested seven complete bounded cases and returned `VERIFY_OK`. No infinite claim is inferred from those tests.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Zhuchok–Zhuchok (2026), DOI 10.1007/s00013-026-02280-9, was read in full in its lawful public HTML rendition, particularly its free-model construction and proof of Theorem 1. It covers constant idempotent maps, but does not claim all idempotent retractions, bounded-length rank statistics, or the Lambert-\(W\) entropy transition. Earlier free-monoid semiretractions, dimonoid semiretractions, and free idempotent-generated semigroup papers are related but are not the same pair-model counting problem. Semantic research-record searches on idempotent maps, retracts, free g-dimonoids, and rank entropy retrieved no known stronger statement. Residual risk: some older semiretraction literature was accessible only as bibliographic/abstract material; its possible indirect implications remain to be reviewed independently. Lack of a match is not a uniqueness guarantee.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Retracts are the image objects of idempotents in an endomorphism monoid. The finding classifies every such image for a newly studied algebraic variety and counts all retractions under its canonical length grading, with a genuinely changed entropy law contributed by the two-operation control word. The exact limiting rank distribution at rank three and the asymptotic optimal image rank across all ranks sharpen this beyond routine existence or a small special-case count. It is useful in structural enumeration and random finite truncations of endomorphism monoids. No general result for arbitrary nonfree objects or simultaneous \(n,L\)-limits is asserted.

The closest full-text work is Zhuchok and Zhuchok (2026) on endomorphism-semigroup determinability and constant idempotents. Dandan–Gould–Quinn-Gregson (2018) works with the **free idempotent-generated semigroup** over an existing biordered set, not with enumeration of idempotents in the endomorphism monoid of a free g-dimonoid. Zhuchok (2008) treats semiretractions of free monoids. An older paper on semiretractions of dimonoids is bibliographically known but its primary text was not available for full inspection, so priority is qualified.
