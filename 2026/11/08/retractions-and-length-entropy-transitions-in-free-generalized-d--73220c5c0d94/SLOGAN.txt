Every idempotent endomorphism of a finitely generated free generalized dimonoid retracts onto a free subalgebra on fixed original generators. With generator images bounded by length \(L\), the exact rank-\(s\) count is \(\binom ns(\sum_{j=1}^L2^{j-1}s^j)^{n-s}\); the dominant image rank is asymptotic to \(n/W(2en)\), and rank three exhibits a nontrivial \(3:2\) coexistence of ranks one and two.
