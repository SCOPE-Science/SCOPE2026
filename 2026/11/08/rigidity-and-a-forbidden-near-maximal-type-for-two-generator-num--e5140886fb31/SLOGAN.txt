For every modulus \(p\ge5\), sufficiently large coprime two-generator numerical-semigroup quotients with generators prime to \(p\) can never have type \(p-1\). More generally, each near-maximal type \(p-N\) with \(3N+2\le p\) forces the unique residue classes \(a\equiv b\equiv1-N\pmod p\), yielding a rigid upper type spectrum.
