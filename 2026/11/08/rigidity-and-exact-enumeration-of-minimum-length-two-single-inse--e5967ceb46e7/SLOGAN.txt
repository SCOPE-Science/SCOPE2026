Every shortest length-two single-insertion covering code over an arbitrary \(q\)-symbol alphabet is a union \(A^2\cup B^2\) for a balanced alphabet bipartition, even when the codebook is not assumed symmetric. Consequently, the number of labeled shortest codebooks is \(\binom{q}{\lfloor q/2\rfloor}/(1+\mathbf 1_{\{2\mid q\}})\), and all are equivalent under alphabet relabeling.
