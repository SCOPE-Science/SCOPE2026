For every \(n\ge4\), the rank-\(n-1\) Green class of the order-preserving transformation monoid has exactly one semigroup-inverse involution that fixes every idempotent. Its \(2(n-1)\) idempotent fixed points and \(\binom{n-1}{2}\) forced nonidempotent pairs are determined by a coordinatewise-monotonicity rigidity argument.
