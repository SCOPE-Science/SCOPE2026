For an axis-parallel rectangular hole of side lengths \(h_1,h_2\) inside the unit square, the minimum total width of planks covering the square annulus is \(\min\{1,2-h_1-h_2\}\). Whenever \(h_1+h_2>1\), **every** minimizing finite cover consists only of horizontal and vertical planks partitioning the four canonical outer strips; no oblique plank can occur.
