Every nine-position two-loss modular checksum with the minimum known modulus \(56\) uses, up to affine residue relabeling and coordinate permutation, a single coefficient set: \(\{0,2,4,18,19,25,28,31,36\}\). There are exactly \(1344\) distinct coefficient subsets of \(\mathbb Z_{56}\) in its free affine orbit, of which \(216\) contain zero; earlier literature established the minimum modulus but not this census.
