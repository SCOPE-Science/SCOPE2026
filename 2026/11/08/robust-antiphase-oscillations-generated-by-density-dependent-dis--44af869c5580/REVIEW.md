## Value — PASS

The motivating 2024 framework numerically shows that coupled maps can oscillate, but the displayed oscillatory examples use an intrinsically overcompensatory Ricker source; therefore those examples alone cannot distinguish oscillations caused by local recruitment from oscillations created by density-responsive dispersal. The present exact example resolves that mechanistic distinction in the minimal two-patch setting, and the universal constant-migration comparison makes the causal contrast mathematically sharp. Exact local multipliers, the loss constraint, admissibility and perturbation robustness make it a useful reproducible structural counterexample to assuming monotone local Beverton–Holt dynamics guarantee settling under nonlinear migration. No empirical calibration, global basin classification, or general bifurcation locus is claimed.
