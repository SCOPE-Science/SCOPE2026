A symmetric two-patch model with identical, globally stabilizing Beverton–Holt recruitment nevertheless supports a rigorously attracting antiphase two-cycle under nondecreasing density-dependent emigration, with the exact oscillation \((1,3)\leftrightarrow(3,1)\). Replacing the migration rule by **any** constant dispersal fraction restores global convergence to the symmetric positive equilibrium; the cyclic behavior is robust under small perturbations.
