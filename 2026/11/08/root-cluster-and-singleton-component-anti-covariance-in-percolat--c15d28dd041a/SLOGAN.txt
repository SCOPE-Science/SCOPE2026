In Bernoulli percolation on a uniform random recursive tree, the ancestral cluster size and the total number of singleton components have an exact strictly negative covariance for every nondegenerate retention probability. Its fixed-probability growth is of order \(n^p\), while the high-retention regimes \(p=1-\lambda/\log n\) and \(p=1-\lambda/n\) exhibit respectively order \(n/\log n\) and finite nonzero covariance limits.
