Root-only squared imbalance and cherry count have exact negative covariance under both uniform Catalan and random binary-search-tree laws, but their magnitudes scale as \(-\sqrt\pi\,n^{3/2}/8\) and approach \(-8/3\), respectively. Their conditional cherry expectations are nonmonotone in root imbalance: the most extreme splits minimize and the one-step-inward splits maximize the expectation.
