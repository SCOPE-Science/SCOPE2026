For the repeated permutation host \(W=(0\,1\,\cdots\,(q-1))^k\), the necklaces whose **every rotation** is a scattered subsequence are classified by cyclic weak descents and counted exactly by a weighted Burnside transfer formula. Their maximum length is \(q(k-1)\) for \(k\ge2\), and the top three layers admit sharp explicit enumerations for \(q,k\ge3\).
