For an irrational two-branch contracting circle map with unequal slopes, the Cantor attractor requires \(\log(1/\varepsilon)/\chi+O(1)\) intervals of diameter \(\varepsilon\) to cover, where \(\chi=-(1-\rho)\log\lambda-\rho\log(\lambda\mu)\). This exact logarithmic metric size detects the rotation frequency through the two different contraction rates even though the attractor has Hausdorff dimension zero.
