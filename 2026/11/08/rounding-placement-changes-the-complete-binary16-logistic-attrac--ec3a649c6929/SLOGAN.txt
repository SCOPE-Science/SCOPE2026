For the fully chaotic logistic polynomial \(4x(1-x)\) on all 15,361 nonnegative IEEE binary16 states in \([0,1]\), rounding only the final result yields one five-cycle with 117 states in its basin, whereas ordinary staged rounding yields a period-40 cycle with a basin of 10,267 states. Exact all-state integer arithmetic shows that changing only rounding placement alters 2,774 transitions and redirects 15,236 states into the zero fixed point under single-round evaluation.
