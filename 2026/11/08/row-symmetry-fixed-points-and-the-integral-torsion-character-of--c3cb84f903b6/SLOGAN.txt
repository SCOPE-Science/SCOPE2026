For the five-row multiple chessboard family with row capacities \((1,1,1,1,m-4)\), the unique integral three-torsion class carries the product of the row and column sign characters. Every nontrivial subgroup of the four-row permutation group has a torsion-free wedge-of-spheres fixed-point space, with explicit dimensions and multiplicities depending only on its number of fixed rows and on \(m\).
