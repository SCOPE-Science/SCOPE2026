For a periodic word formed by one run of each of at least three distinct symbols, a single length-one run forces every cyclic phase of the \(k\)-fold period to have scattered-subsequence universality index exactly \(k\). If all runs are longer, the circular index is \(k+\lfloor k/(q-1)\rfloor\), and exactly when \((q-1)\mid k\), its maximizers are precisely the strictly internal run phases.
