Deleting just one parallel class of line vertices from any finite affine-plane incidence graph produces a graph whose inclusive radius-three Rips complex is exactly the join of \(2q\) discrete \(q\)-point sets. For every affine plane of order \(q\ge2\), this gives \(\bigvee^{(q-1)^{2q}} S^{2q-1}\), with every top-dimensional persistence interval equal to \([3,4)\).
