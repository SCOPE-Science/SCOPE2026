For every connected finite simple graph \(G\) with at least one edge, the inclusive scale-two Rips complex of its once-subdivided graph has homotopy type \(\operatorname{Cl}(G)\vee\bigvee^{t(G)}S^2\), where \(t(G)\) counts graph triangles. This preserves the entire clique-complex homotopy type and adds exactly one independent two-sphere per triangle, with no degree, girth, or planarity assumptions.
