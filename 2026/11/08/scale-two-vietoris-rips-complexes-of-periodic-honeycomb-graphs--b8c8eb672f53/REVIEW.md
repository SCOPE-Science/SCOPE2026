# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The all-parameter proof classifies every possible clique in the distance-two graph by bipartition and triangular-lattice geometry. It exhibits a relative elementary collapse of the centered tetrahedral subcomplex to the original graph, converts both alternating triangles per facial hexagon to CW disks attached on the same hexagonal loop, and identifies the one-disk-per-face core with the torus. The remaining disks attach nullhomotopically and give exactly the claimed integral sphere summands. The homology and the primitive kernel statement follow from the torus cellular chain complex. Independently constructed finite examples and exact mod-two ranks corroborate the steps without being used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by literature-access and indexing limitations

The closest original work on torus grids (Adams et al., arXiv:2502.07134, Theorems 5.8–5.10) addresses square Cartesian-product grids, not the graph family here. Adamaszek's high-girth collapse assumption is violated. The closest published hexagon-packing result assumes pairwise edge-disjoint 6-cycles, whereas every edge of this family borders two facial 6-cycles. The one-graph Nauru classification cannot imply the uniform result for all \(L\ge5\). Searches for honeycomb/hexagonal Rips scale two, toroidal graph powers and equivalent torus-with-sphere-wedge formulations found no dominating published classification. Remaining risk is unindexed or differently formulated equivalent work.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result completely determines a natural infinite family directly analogous to an actively studied square torus-grid family, but with a genuinely different dimension-two homotopy phenomenon. It identifies an exact geometric source of the summands, all integral homology groups and the primitive loss of cycle homology at the first metric thickening. This is more informative than an isolated face-count or numerical Betti calculation, and the exact graph family is motivated before computing.

## Scope and limitations

All claims require \(L\ge5\), the shortest-path graph metric and inclusive scale two. No result at larger radii, for arbitrary toroidal quotients or for translation-equivariant homotopy types is claimed. Independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
