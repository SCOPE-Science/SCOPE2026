Multiplying arbitrary lifts changes leading coefficients by coordinatewise products and adds the leading degrees, with zero contributions beyond truncation. Full support and absence of zero divisors guarantee no surviving coordinate can disappear. The inverse-image construction completes both containments and iteration yields exact nilpotency indices.
