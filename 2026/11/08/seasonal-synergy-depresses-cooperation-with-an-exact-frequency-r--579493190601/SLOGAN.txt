For a four-player adaptive public-goods game, any sufficiently weak sinusoidal fluctuation in global synergy strictly lowers the time-averaged cooperation on the locally attracting periodic branch, even though the unforced equilibrium cooperates at rate \(2/3\). The leading reduction peaks exactly when the forcing frequency is \(\omega=\sqrt{3\varepsilon}/4\), with a closed-form response valid for every fixed feedback speed \(\varepsilon>4/9\).
