For a uniform length-\(n\) Motzkin path, the number of ground-level horizontal steps has mixed covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(R_n,P_n)=-1/n+O(n^{-2})\) with its total number of consecutive sharp peaks \(UD\), after two leading asymptotic orders cancel. In contrast, its covariance with the total number of horizontal steps converges to the positive constant \(2\).
