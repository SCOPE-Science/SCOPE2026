In a fair random tournament, the probability that some ordered pair lacks two-step reachability has a sharp second exponential correction: \(\frac{n(n-1)}2(3/4)^{n-2}-\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}4(5/8)^{n-3}+o(n^3(5/8)^n)\). Conditional on at least two such failures, asymptotically there are exactly two, sharing either their unreachable target or their failing source with equal probabilities.
