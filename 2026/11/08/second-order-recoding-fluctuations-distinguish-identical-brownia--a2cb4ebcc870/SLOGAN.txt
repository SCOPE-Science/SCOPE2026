At critical degree-\(d\) sparse Boolean recoding, two random-walk pairs can have the same correlated Brownian limit but different Gaussian fluctuations of their empirical predictable cross-covariation. The variance is \(1-\rho^2+2d^2\rho^2(\log\rho)^2\), so the degree of randomness remains detectable at second order even when its first-order correlation is fixed.
