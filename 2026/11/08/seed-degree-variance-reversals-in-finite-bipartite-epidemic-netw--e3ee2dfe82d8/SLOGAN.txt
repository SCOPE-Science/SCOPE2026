For every \(K_{2,b}\) with \(b\ge3\), the higher-degree epidemic seed has greater outbreak-size variance at weak transmission but smaller variance near certain transmission. The smallest unequal-degree case of minimum degree two, \(K_{2,3}\) has exactly one transition at \(p\approx0.2515540558\), even though its high-degree seed has a strictly larger mean outbreak for every \(0<p<1\).
