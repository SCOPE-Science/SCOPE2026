# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The domain consists of every finite simple connected tree with at least two vertices, one initially active spreader, strictly positive vertex-wise transmission and contact-stifling rates and permanent knowledge. An edge of a rooted tree gives the only path for first informing the child, so each non-root spreader begins with exactly one already-informed neighbor. For both a root and a non-root vertex, the competing-hazard calculation gives the same local factor \(\prod_{j=1}^{d-1}(d-j)/(d-j+\alpha_v j)\). The local child-transmission/stifling experiments are independent across originating vertices after pre-sampling directed clocks. Their product therefore exactly equals the probability that every vertex is ever informed; there is no asymptotic or independence approximation. For the homogeneous-ratio inequality, index pairing and \(u+u^{-1}\ge2\) establish strict local loss for degrees at least three, and \(\sum_v(d_v-1)=n-2\) gives the unique path extremum. The independent exact-rational three-state CTMC verifier reports 567 tree/profile cases and 2958 root comparisons. The proof, rather than numerical enumeration, covers every tree order and all positive ratios. The boundaries \(d=1\), \(d=2\) and \(n=2\) have been checked.

## Originality — PASS within compared sources
The 2011 Borge-Holthoefer–Moreno full preprint investigates mean rumor reach and initial spreader influence through simulations in general networks, not finite-tree complete-reach probabilities. The 2012 Coletti–Rodríguez–Schinazi preprint concerns rumor extinction and survival with forgetting on an infinite lattice. The strongest precursor, Junior–Rodríguez–Speroto (2020), derives local offspring probabilities for homogeneous infinite regular trees; this supplies an important component of the hazard calculation, not the finite irregular-tree degree-multiset product, vertex-dependent seed invariance or sharp all-tree path extremum. Searches for root/seed invariance, full dissemination, tree degree factors, and path optimality did not locate a corresponding published theorem among reviewed records. These comparisons establish no priority monopoly: older finite-tree or reliability work could contain an equivalent construction. The new claims are compared by logical implication, not title similarity; the 2020 branching law alone leaves the finite-root and heterogeneous extension, and the extremal degree inequality, unstated.

## Value — PASS
The probability that a message actually reaches everyone before stifling is a natural guarantee-oriented objective distinct from mean outbreak size, mean reach, or infinite-graph survival. Root invariance remains exact even for arbitrary positive vertex-specific rate ratios, while tree shape becomes irrelevant at fixed degrees under homogeneous ratios. A universal sharp path optimality result identifies the precise structural cost of branching for full coverage. Such results give exact finite-network benchmarks for source selection and network design, and the strongest statements apply to arbitrary tree orders rather than an arbitrary small census. No empirical performance or applicability outside the specified stochastic mechanism is inferred.

## Closest sources and limitations
See arXiv:1112.2239v1; arXiv:1202.4019v1; and arXiv:2005.11440. The latter's regular-tree local branching law is the closest explicit mathematical ingredient. Finite-state checks are corroborative, not an infinite proof. Exact full dissemination differs from mean spread and local survival. The formula is not asserted for graphs with cycles, neighbor-specific contact rates, spontaneous stifling, forgetting, or recovery. Historical priority remains provisional; independent validation has not been performed.
