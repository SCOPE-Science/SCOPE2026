For flat-spectrum matrices, independent Gaussian fresh-batch Frobenius stopping has an exact beta first-passage rank law. With fixed batch width \(b\) and squared tolerance \(\tau\), its accepted rank falls below the true tolerance rank by \(\sqrt{2\tau(1-\tau)/b}\,\sqrt{r\log r}\) in probability, making false acceptance asymptotically certain for this unmodified single-node rule.
