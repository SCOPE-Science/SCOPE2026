For every field and every \(m\ge2\), the reduced rank-at-most-one associative Nijenhuis-operator variety on the \(m\)-arrow Kronecker path algebra has exactly seven geometrically irreducible components of dimensions \(2m+1,2m+1,2m,m+1,m+1,1,1\). The complete finite-field enumeration follows from an exact four-type image-line stratification, including a radical-incidence component absent from a naive idempotent-image analysis.
