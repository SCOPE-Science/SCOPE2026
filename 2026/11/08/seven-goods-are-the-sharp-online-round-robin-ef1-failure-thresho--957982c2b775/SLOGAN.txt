With three strategic agents picking goods sequentially in cyclic order, every Nash-equilibrium allocation is EF1 through \(6\) goods, even for monotone bundle values; but an explicit strictly positive additive seven-good game has a pure subgame-perfect equilibrium that is not EF1. Thus \(7\) is the exact online equilibrium separation threshold, and the full backward-induction certificate verifies the witness at every history.
