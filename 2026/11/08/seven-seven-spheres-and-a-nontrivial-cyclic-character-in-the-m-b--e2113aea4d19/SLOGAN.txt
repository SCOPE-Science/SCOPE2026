The dominance complex of the Möbius–Kantor graph \(\operatorname{GP}(8,3)\) is \(\bigvee^{7}S^7\), and the graph's eightfold rotation acts on its rational top homology with character \((7,-1,3,-1,7,-1,3,-1)\). This yields an explicit nonchordal sharpness example for the vertex-cover connectivity bound.
