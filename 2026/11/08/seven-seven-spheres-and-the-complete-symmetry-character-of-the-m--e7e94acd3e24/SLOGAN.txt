The Möbius–Kantor graph's dominance complex has the integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{7}S^7\), certified by a complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching. Its entire \(96\)-element automorphism group acts on the seven-dimensional top homology with a thirteen-class rational character that is multiplicity-free with four nontrivial irreducible constituents and no invariant line.
