# Scientific assessment: seven-summand evil/odious growth

## Correctness — PASS

The main claim concerns ordered representations over the even- or odd-binary-digit-parity set, with zero allowed only for the even set. The exact generating-function decomposition \(F_\pm=(1-z)^{-1}/2\pm T(z)/2\) yields the coefficient identity for first differences. Gelfond's uniform exponential-sum estimate, Abel summation, and exact Parseval yield respectively \(\|T\|_\infty\ll N^\alpha\) and \(\|T\|_2^2\ll N\) on the same circle. The proof bounds every mixed term, including its difficult pure-Thue--Morse term by \(O_k(N^{1+(k-2)\alpha})\); the inequality \((k-2)(1-\alpha)>1\) holds exactly for integers \(k\ge7\). Thus every remaining term is of smaller order than the strictly positive binomial main term. The supplied independent finite coefficient construction successfully verified 2,568 identities at degrees up to 320. Finite tests are corroboration and not the infinite argument.

## Originality — PASS within accessible evidence

Allouche--Shallit's arXiv:2112.13627, Section 4, proves eventual increase at ten summands and explicitly leaves the six-, seven-, eight-, and nine-summand cases unresolved. Their sufficient Fourier estimate loses an exponent by using a pointwise bound for every signed-series factor. The present application of Parseval to two signed factors improves the critical exponent and proves cases seven through nine. This is a strict strengthening of their known ten-summand result, not a reformulation or special case. The paper by Sándor--Yang arXiv:2606.28849 treats different sets; Bell--Shallit arXiv:2212.12473 and Shallit arXiv:2302.02138 concern other representation-function constructions. Published-result similarity searches for the present identities showed related Thue--Morse valuations but no covering evil/odious seven-summand theorem. An unindexed equivalent proof remains a residual priority risk. The motivating work was first made public in 2021.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Seven-fold additive representation monotonicity is a natural explicit open case in the motivating paper. The result closes three of its four named low-summand gaps with a structural analytic device (a second-moment bound) and a power-saving asymptotic valid for every fixed summand count at least seven. It is not a routine parameter replacement or a finite census; the difficulty lies in obtaining a uniform pointwise lower bound on adjacent representation counts.

## Limits and priority risk

The six-summand question, explicit least monotonicity thresholds, and uniform-in-\(k\) estimates remain outside this finding. The literature comparison was conducted against the primary open-problem text, directly related recent manuscripts and indexed results; it cannot certify globally unique priority. No independent scientific audit was performed.
