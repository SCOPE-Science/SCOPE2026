On the 16-vertex folded five-cube, three-color Greenberg–Hastings dynamics has precisely the primitive periods \(1,3,4,7,10,13,16\), with a sharp maximum transient length of eleven. Its 2,400 period-sixteen cycles split into two distinct full-automorphism symmetry classes of sizes 480 and 1,920, while its 9,600 period-thirteen cycles split into five classes of size 1,920.
