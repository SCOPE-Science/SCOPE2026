For every positive integer \(n\), unit \(2\times2\) checkerboard switches connect all nonnegative integer \(3\times3\) tables with every row and column sum \(n\), and the exact worst-case distance is \(2n\). Exactly six unordered pairs attain it: the integer multiples of disjoint three-by-three permutation matrices; all other pairs are within \(2n-1\) moves.
