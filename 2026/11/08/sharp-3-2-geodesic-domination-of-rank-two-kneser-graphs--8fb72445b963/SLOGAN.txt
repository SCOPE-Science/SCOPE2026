For rank-two Kneser graphs \(KG(n,2)\), \((3,2)\)-geodesic domination has minimum three exactly at \(n=6,7\) and four at \(n=5\) and every \(n\ge8\). Every minimum triple is a matching of three ground-set edges (\(15\) configurations at order six and \(105\) at order seven); a universal four-set consists of a ground-set triangle and a disjoint edge.
