For complex polynomials of degree at most \(d\), squared intensities along any noncircular ellipse occupy exactly the \(4d\)-dimensional trigonometric space missing only the highest sine harmonic. Exactly \(4d\) evenly spaced eccentric-anomaly samples reconstruct every profile, except at the precisely classified quarter-phase offsets; this linear sample count is sharp, and the resulting profile determines the polynomial up to global phase.
