For \((0^a1^b)^\omega\), the shortest Abelian three-antipower has block length two as soon as both runs have length at least three, whereas a run of length two yields a sharp one-third-period threshold and exactly two optimal starting phases, apart from two exceptional periods. All Abelian antipower starting phases of any order and block length admit an exact period-independent \(O(q^3\log q)\) arithmetic census.
