For frozen-snapshot, with-replacement Kaczmarz batches on orthonormal equations, uniform sampling is universally mean-square stable precisely when \(\beta[1+(T-1)/m]<2\), but every strictly positive residual-power exponent is universally stable precisely when \(\beta T<2\). Beyond this adaptive threshold, every nonzero start has a positive-probability exponentially divergent collision trajectory, even if every coordinate starts nonzero.
