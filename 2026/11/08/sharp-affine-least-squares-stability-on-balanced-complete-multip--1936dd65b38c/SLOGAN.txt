For balanced complete multipartite unit metric graphs with at least three parts, the continuous edgewise-affine \(L^2\) projector has the exact \(L^\infty\) norm \(2+[t(s-4)+1]/[3t(ts+1)]\), where \(t=2r-3\). Every vertex is norming, and adding more parts increases the norm for part sizes two or three but decreases it for every part size at least four; the sharp family supremum is the unattained value \(19/9\).
