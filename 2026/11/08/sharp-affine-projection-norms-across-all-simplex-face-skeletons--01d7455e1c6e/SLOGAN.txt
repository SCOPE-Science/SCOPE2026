Equal-face sampling on every \((r-1)\)-skeleton of an \((m-1)\)-simplex admits an exact, shape-independent affine least-squares projector norm, with vertices as its unique norming locations whenever \(r\ge2\). At each fixed face rank \(r\ge2\), the norm rises strictly with ambient dimension toward the sharp ceiling \(r\), while proportional face rank has an explicit exponential scaling law.
