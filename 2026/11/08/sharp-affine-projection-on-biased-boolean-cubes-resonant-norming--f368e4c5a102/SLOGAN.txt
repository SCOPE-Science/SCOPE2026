For the first affine Walsh projection on a biased Bernoulli cube, the sharp \(L^\infty\) norm is an explicit binomial boundary-mass expression; only the rarest corner norms the projector, except for an exact integer resonance that adds its nearest neighbors. At fixed bias its growth is Gaussian of order \(\sqrt d\), but under rare-event bias \(p=\lambda/d\) it grows linearly with a continuous, integer-kinked Poisson profile.
