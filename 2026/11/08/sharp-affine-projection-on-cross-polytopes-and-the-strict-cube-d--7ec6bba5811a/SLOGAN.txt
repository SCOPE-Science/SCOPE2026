Uniform-volume affine least-squares projection on the \(d\)-dimensional cross-polytope has exact norm \(1+\frac{d+2}{2}(\frac{d(d+3)}{(d+1)(d+2)})^{d+1}\), with only vertices norming. It equals the corresponding cube norm only in dimensions one and two, exceeds it strictly in every higher dimension, and grows linearly rather than at the cube's square-root rate.
