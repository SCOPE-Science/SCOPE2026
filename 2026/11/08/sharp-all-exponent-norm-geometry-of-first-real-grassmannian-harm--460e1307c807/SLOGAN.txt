For every real exponent \(1\le p<\infty\), the exact \(L^2\)-to-\(L^p\) norm range of first real harmonics on \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)\) is bounded by the explicit pair and one-outlier spectral values, with all equality cases classified. The extremal ordering reverses sharply at \(p=2\), and the endpoint ratios at infinity are exactly \(\sqrt3\) and \(3\).
