For every length \(n\ge2\), the maximum number of binary weight-two codewords correcting one deletion is exactly \(\lfloor(3n-2)/4\rfloor\). An explicit optimum combines one-pairs at \((2r,2r+1)\) and \((4r+1,4r+4)\), with a matching universal upper bound from deletion-shadow counting and a path-packing constraint.
