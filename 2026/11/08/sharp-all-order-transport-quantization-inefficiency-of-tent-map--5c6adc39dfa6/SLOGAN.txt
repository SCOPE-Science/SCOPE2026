For the full tent map, uniformly weighting all fixed points of \(T^n\) gives optimally fast but systematically suboptimal transport approximation of Lebesgue measure: the sharp asymptotic \(W_p\) penalty against the best \(2^n\)-atom quantizer is \((2^{p+1}/(p+2))^{1/p}\) for finite \(p\ge1\), and exactly \(2\) for \(p=\infty\). The exact \(W_1\) and \(W_2\) formulas identify the finite-size deficit and show that periodic-point density alone does not imply optimal spatial placement.
