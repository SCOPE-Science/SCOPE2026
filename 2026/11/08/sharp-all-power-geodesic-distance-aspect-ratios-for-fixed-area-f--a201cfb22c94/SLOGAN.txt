For two area-uniform points on a fixed-area flat cylinder, every positive power of their intrinsic geodesic distance has a unique optimal circumference-to-height ratio, strictly less than \(2\). The optimal ratio is \(\sqrt2\) for the squared distance and \(2\cdot3^{-1/4}\) for the fourth power, and for high powers it equals \(2-4/p+o(p^{-1})\); thus there is no universal aspect ratio minimizing all powers.
