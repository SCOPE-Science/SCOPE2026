For fourth-order two-stage Gauss--Legendre integration of heat flow on any unweighted Cartesian product \(K_{q_1}\square\cdots\square K_{q_d}\), all timesteps preserve nonnegative states exactly when there is one factor, or there are two factors and each has order two or three. Every other such product develops a negative neighboring transition at sufficiently large steps, certified by a positive off-diagonal entry of the Laplacian pseudoinverse.
