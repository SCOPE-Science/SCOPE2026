For alternating inner residual tolerances \(a,b\in(0,1)\), unrestarted flexible GMRES has a universal nonstagnation guarantee exactly when \(a+b\le1\), with sharp finite-step bounds governed by a two-periodic tridiagonal determinant. Restarting every two steps instead guarantees geometric reduction for all such tolerances, with sharp two-step factor \(ab/\sqrt{1-a^2}\) when \(a^2+b^2<1\) and \(a\) otherwise; hence a genuine wedge exists where two-step restarting prevents a worst-case failure of the unrestarted method.
