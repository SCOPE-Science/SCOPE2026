A single new edge can amplify stationary sandpile topplings from exactly \(1\) to a sharp maximum \(M_N=N+7/2-2\sqrt{N+2}+O(N^{-1/2})\) on a tree with a sink-adjacent source leaf and \(N\) other nonsink vertices. A path attains the maximum when its new chord from the source reaches approximately \(2\sqrt{N}\) steps beyond the sink.
