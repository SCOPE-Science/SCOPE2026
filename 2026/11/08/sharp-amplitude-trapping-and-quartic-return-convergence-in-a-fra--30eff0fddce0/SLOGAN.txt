For the fractional kinetic iteration \(F(x)=x-\eta x/(1+x^2)^a\), the sharp exponent \(a=1/4\) separates unconditional one-step amplitude trapping from a finite trapping frontier. Above that exponent, the unique critical tune \(\eta=2(4a/(4a-1))^a\) attracts every nonexceptional trajectory to a two-cycle with **fourth-order convergence per full return**, with an explicit nonzero coefficient.
