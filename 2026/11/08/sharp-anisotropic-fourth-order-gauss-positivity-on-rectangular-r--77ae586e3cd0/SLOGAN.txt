On a directionally weighted rectangular rook graph, the fourth-order two-stage Gauss endpoint preserves all nonnegative states at every timestep exactly when the ratio of the two nonzero factor eigenvalues lies in \([\phi(p),1/\phi(q)]\), where \(\phi(k)=(\sqrt{4k-3}-1)/2\). The largest admissible shape is \(K_2\square K_5\): unequal directional weights restore unconditional endpoint positivity even though the same unweighted graph loses it at large steps.
