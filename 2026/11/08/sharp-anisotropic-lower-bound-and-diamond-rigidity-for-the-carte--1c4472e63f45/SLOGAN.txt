Fixing the separate operator norms \(r=\|\operatorname{Re}T\|\) and \(s=\|\operatorname{Im}T\|\) determines a sharp two-parameter lower bound for the squared \(L^2\)-Cartesian radius, attained exactly when the centrally symmetrized numerical range is the diamond with vertices \(\pm r,\pm is\). At fixed \(r>0\), its exact deficit is \(4s^3/(3\pi r)+O(s^5/r^3)\) as \(s\downarrow0\).
