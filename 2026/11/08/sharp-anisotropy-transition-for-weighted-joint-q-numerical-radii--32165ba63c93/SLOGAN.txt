For the two anticommuting Pauli matrices, every weighted joint \(q\)-numerical \(\ell_p\) radius at \(0<|q|<1\) has a sharp closed form, with a unique interior-to-saturation transition when \(1\le p<2\). The transition occurs at \(|q|=\sqrt{1-(\beta/\alpha)^{2p/(2-p)}}\) for unequal weights \(\alpha>\beta>0\); all optimizing squared-modulus allocations are explicitly attained.
