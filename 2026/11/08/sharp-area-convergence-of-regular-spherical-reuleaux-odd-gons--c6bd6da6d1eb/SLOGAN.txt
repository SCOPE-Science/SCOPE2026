For regular spherical Reuleaux odd-gons of any fixed width \(0<w\le\pi/2\), area strictly increases and perimeter strictly decreases toward those of the spherical disk of radius \(w/2\). The renormalized area deficit \(n^2(A_\infty-A_n)\) strictly decreases from its triangle value to the sharp positive limit \(\pi^3\sin^2(w/2)/(12\cos(w/2))\), giving best possible two-sided \(n^{-2}\) bounds across every odd number of corners.
