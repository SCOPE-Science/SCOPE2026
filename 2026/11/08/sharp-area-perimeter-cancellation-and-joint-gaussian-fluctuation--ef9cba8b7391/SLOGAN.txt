Uniformly deleting a fixed positive fraction of regular-polygon vertices yields a sharp area deficit strictly below twice the perimeter deficit, with the constant \(2\) best possible. The perimeter deficit and the positive fifth-order cancellation residual have distinct \(n^{-5/2}\) and \(n^{-9/2}\) Gaussian fluctuation scales after explicit deterministic centering, and their joint covariance is positive definite.
