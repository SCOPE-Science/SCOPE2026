For three equally spaced Fourier frequency cosets on \([0,1)\cup[m,m+2)\), the best squared fiber determinant is exactly \(27\) when \(m\equiv1\pmod3\), but otherwise equals \(27-6\pi^2/m^2+O(m^{-3})\). The nonorthogonal optimal shifts approach a one-third coset spacing with a sharp, residue-dependent \(1/(3m)\) correction, quantifying an arithmetic obstruction to equilateral Fourier fibers.
