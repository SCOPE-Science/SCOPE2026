A contracting complex geometric spiral is a phaseless uniqueness set for all holomorphic functions precisely when its turning angle is irrational relative to \(\pi\). For degree-at-most-\(d\) polynomials, the exact threshold is \(d<q(\theta)\), with the first \((d+1)^2\) magnitudes explicitly recovering the complete intensity profile through a Vandermonde system; above the threshold, a concrete pair remains ambiguous even with infinitely many observations.
