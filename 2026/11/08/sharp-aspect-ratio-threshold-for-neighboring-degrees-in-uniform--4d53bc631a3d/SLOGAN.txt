For a uniform spanning tree of the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), two vertices adjacent along the \(K_n\) direction have positively correlated degrees if and only if \(m\ge\binom{n+1}{2}\). Below this exact aspect-ratio threshold their covariance is negative, except for the zero-covariance four-cycle \(K_2\square K_2\).
