For a first-order two-stage complex integrator, the sharp stable imaginary interval with prescribed positive-to-negative frequency ratio has **total length exactly two**: \(h_*=2/(a+b)\), uniquely attained by \(k_*=((a+b)^2+i(a^2-b^2))/(2(a^2+b^2))\) in \(1+z+kz^2\). Its asymmetric gain over every real-coefficient quadratic is the exact factor \(2\max(a,b)/(a+b)\), interpolating between the known symmetric and one-sided cases.
