For a Poisson-reset transverse-Ising dimer at large interaction strength, the maximal stationary CHSH violation approaches \(\sqrt5\), and every optimal resetting rate locks onto the induced doublet splitting \(r\sim\Omega^2/(2J)\). A uniform error estimate shows that this nonlocality persists as the reset rate vanishes along the strong-coupling ridge, although the fixed-coupling zero-rate limit is Bell-local.
