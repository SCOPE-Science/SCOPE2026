The microscopic symmetric-exclusion current–density resolvent has a unique nearest pole on the negative real scalar current-tilt axis \(\omega\); its exact Taylor radius is the reciprocal of a positive Bessel-kernel principal eigenvalue. The radius shrinks from \(2/t^2\) at short times to \(1+j_{1,1}^2/(4t)+o(t^{-1})\) at long times, where \(j_{1,1}\) is the first zero of \(J_1\).
