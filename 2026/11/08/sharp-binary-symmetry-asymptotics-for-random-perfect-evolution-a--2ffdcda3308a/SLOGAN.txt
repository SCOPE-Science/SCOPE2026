For a uniform invertible binary structure matrix, the probability that its perfect evolution algebra has a nontrivial automorphism is \(n(n-1)2^{-2n}(1+O(n2^{-n}))\). Conditional on a symmetry, with probability tending to one the full automorphism group is just a single coordinate transposition; the same estimate gives a second-order asymptotic for the number of isomorphism classes.
