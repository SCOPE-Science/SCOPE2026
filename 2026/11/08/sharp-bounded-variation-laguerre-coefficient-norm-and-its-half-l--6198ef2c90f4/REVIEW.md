# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The stated primitive identity is verified by differentiation, and the ordinary Laguerre orthonormal normalization eliminates extra coefficient factors. Stieltjes integration by parts gives a sharp variation coefficient bound with exact single-jump equality. The Laguerre derivative identity yields \(F_n''+F_n'+nF_n/x=0\) for positive arguments. The derivative of \(S_n=F_n^2+(x/n)(F_n')^2\) is exactly \((1-2x)(F_n')^2/n\), giving a **global** upper envelope without extrapolating a fixed-point asymptotic. The finite-root reduction follows from the strict one-sided envelope order. DLMF's compact-uniform Plancherel--Rotach formulas for two Laguerre parameters have phases asymptotically in quadrature, so their squared envelope tends to \(e^{-x}\sqrt{x}/\pi\). A chosen phase peak within \(O(n^{-1/2})\) of \(1/2\) matches the global upper bound; one-sided envelope monotonicity localizes every maximizer. The independent checker is finite corroboration, not a proof of the infinite limit.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
The October 2026 Laguerre smoothness paper gives weighted coefficient sequence estimates but not sharp global BV constants. The April 2026 singularity paper gives fixed-function coefficient rates but no supremum over freely moving jumps. Classical differential identities and Plancherel--Rotach asymptotics are acknowledged inputs. The November 2026 weighted-primitive paper contains the same primitive and general BV principle, and expressly stops short of an ordinary-Laguerre global \(O(n^{-3/4})\) bound. The new contribution is the globally sharp \(n^{-3/4}\) leading coefficient, the exact two-root finite reduction, and optimizer localization derived from the monotonic differential envelope. Searches for asymptotic aliases, primitive extrema, and coefficient operator norms found no dominating published claim. Residual risk: the full 2023 piecewise-Laguerre paper was unavailable, and an equivalent envelope argument may occur under a different terminology in older polynomial theory. These are explicitly priority risks, not claims of exhaustive uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The evaluated quantity is the natural, exactly sharp operator norm of the \(n\)-th ordinary Laguerre coefficient on the variation seminorm ball. Fixed-jump \(O(n^{-3/4})\) estimates do not by themselves yield a uniform-in-location statement, and an existing published comparison explicitly identifies this missing global estimate. The new exact asymptotic constant and two-root algorithm turn an abstract sharp-primitive bound into a quantitative coefficient tolerance. The proof identifies the structurally determined location \(1/2\) of worst-case jumps. The result is limited to parameter zero and does not assert new spectral convergence rates for smooth functions.
