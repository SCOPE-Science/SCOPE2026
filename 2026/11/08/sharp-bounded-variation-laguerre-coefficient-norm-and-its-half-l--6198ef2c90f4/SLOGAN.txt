For ordinary Laguerre coefficients, the **optimal** coefficient bound under unit total variation is the largest weighted Laguerre primitive, and it has the exact asymptotic \(M_n\sim e^{-1/4}n^{-3/4}/(2^{1/4}\sqrt\pi)\). An exact differential-energy identity reduces each finite-degree maximization to at most two Laguerre zeros adjacent to \(1/2\) and forces every maximizing jump location to approach \(1/2\).
