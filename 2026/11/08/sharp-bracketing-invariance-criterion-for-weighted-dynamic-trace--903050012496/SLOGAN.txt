For the symmetric three-valued nondeterministic disjunction, weighted counts of dynamic evaluation histories are invariant under every binary parenthesization exactly when the leaf weights of true and error outcomes agree or one vanishes. The common equal-weight count is \((a^n+2(a+3t)^n)/3\), and otherwise the first bracketing defect is \(bc(b-c)^2\) on four leaves, although the reachable truth-value sets remain associative.
