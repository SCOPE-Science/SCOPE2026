For independent approval ballots whose *average* distribution is closer than \(1/[k(k+1)]\) in total variation to candidate-permutation symmetry, every size-\(k\) committee is Hare-core stable with exponentially high probability when candidate and seat counts are fixed. This universal threshold is sharp: a biased one-approval model already has nonvanishing failure probability at equality and asymptotically certain failure above it.
