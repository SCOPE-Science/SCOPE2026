For binary cross-bifix-free codes with exactly three ones per codeword, the sharp all-length capacity is \(M_3(n)=\lfloor(2n-3)^2/24\rfloor\) for every \(n\ge4\). Every optimal codebook is one of two explicit orientation-reversed rectangular families, or one of four when \(3\mid n\); these are all extremizers.
