For a damped equal-amplitude two-tone signal, the globally best single modulation frequency for approximation by the first two Laguerre functions switches discontinuously when the squared tone frequency is three times the squared Laguerre decay scale. The exact optimal carrier is \(\pm s\sqrt{W-1}\) for \(1\le W<3\), both \(\pm s\sqrt2\) and \(\pm s\sqrt5\) at \(W=3\), and the pair determined by the unique root in an explicit isolating interval of a cubic for \(W>3\).
