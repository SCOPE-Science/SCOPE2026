Every finite-memory \(q\)-symbol source with conditional probabilities at least \(b\) has a sharp length-\(d\) block overlap equal to an explicit pair of categorical-product laws; for \(q\ge3\), it decays as \(\theta^d/\sqrt d\) with an exact constant. This yields the sufficient uniform normalized-hitting regime \(b>1/(2q-1+2\sqrt{q-1})\), improving an affinity-only bound without claiming that this threshold is necessary for actual hitting times.
