An even path with a fixed total conductance budget has a unique fastest-diffusion design, even when the central edge has a hard capacity: the cap becomes active exactly below \(u_0=3Bm/(4m^2-1)\) on \(P_{2m}\). Below that threshold, an explicit one-root formula determines every optimal edge weight, and the gap approaches \(2u/m\) as the bottleneck closes.
