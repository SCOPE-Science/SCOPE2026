For three independent arclength-uniform boundary points of any Euclidean triangle, the probability that their convex hull captures the parent centroid is a universal positive linear combination of the second and third elementary symmetric polynomials in its normalized side lengths. It increases strictly under side-length balancing, reaches the classical equilateral value \(2/27+20\log2/81\), and has the sharp, unattained lower limit \(1/12+\log2/9\).
