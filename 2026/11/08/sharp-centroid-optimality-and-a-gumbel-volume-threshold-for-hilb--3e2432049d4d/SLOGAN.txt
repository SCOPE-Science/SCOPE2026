For every simplex and every fixed Hilbert radius, Euclidean ball volume is strictly Schur-concave in the barycentric center, so the centroid uniquely maximizes coverage; the sharp maximum is \(\frac{n}{e^r-1}B(n/(e^r-1),n)\). At that center the random radius has a standard Gumbel limit after subtracting \(\log n+\log\log n\).
