The smallest relative weight adjustment making independent uniform spherical nodes into an exact degree-one positive quadrature rule has a sharp \(m^{-1/2}\) transition: \(\sqrt m\,T_m\Rightarrow\chi_d/(\sqrt d\,c_d)\), with \(c_d=\Gamma(d/2)/(\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma((d+1)/2))\). On the circle, a box of radius \(t/\sqrt m\) is asymptotically feasible with probability exactly \(1-e^{-4t^2/\pi^2}\).
