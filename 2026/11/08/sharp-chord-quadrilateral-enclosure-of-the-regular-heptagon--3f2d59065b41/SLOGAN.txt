The regular heptagon has exact minimum chord-quadrilateral enclosure ratio \(\frac{8\cos^2(\pi/7)-1}{7(2\cos^2(\pi/7)-1)}=1.258803773547\ldots\). Precisely seven quadrilaterals attain it: each uses four side-supporting lines with cyclic edge-index gaps \((1,2,2,2)\); the full statement concerns chord-restricted, not arbitrary, enclosure.
