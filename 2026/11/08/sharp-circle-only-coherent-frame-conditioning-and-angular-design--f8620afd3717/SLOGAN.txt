For degree-\(n\) Bargmann–Fock polynomials, **every** positive-weight sampling frame of coherent states on one circle has frame-bound ratio at least \(\max_{0\le k\le n}(n!)^{k/n}/k!\), an exponentially growing optimum achieved only at radius squared \((n!)^{1/n}\) with vanishing angular Fourier moments through degree \(n\). The unique optimal designs with the minimum \(n+1\) sensors are equally weighted rotated regular polygons, so adding angular sensors cannot cure the one-radius instability.
