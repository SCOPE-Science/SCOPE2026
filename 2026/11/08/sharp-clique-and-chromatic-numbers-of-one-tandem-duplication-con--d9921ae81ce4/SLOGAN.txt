For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and fixed duplication length \(k\ge1\), the exact-one tandem-duplication confusability graph on length-\(n\) words satisfies \(\chi=\omega=\max\{1,n-2k+1\}\). A zero-run residue coloring gives the sharp partition into single-duplication-correcting codebooks, and an explicit simplex clique proves it cannot use fewer colors.
