For every alphabet \(q\ge2\), the maximum clique in the two-read single-deletion reconstruction graph of length \(n\ge2\) has exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) vertices. Relative to any clique vertex, all other vertices arise from alternating-interval toggles arranged in either one endpoint-sharing pencil or two adjacent endpoint-sharing pencils; these patterns completely describe possible cliques.
