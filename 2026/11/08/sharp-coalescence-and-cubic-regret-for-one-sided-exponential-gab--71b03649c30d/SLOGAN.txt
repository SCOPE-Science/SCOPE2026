For the normalized one-sided exponential Gabor window at integer redundancy \(n\), the lattice parameters optimizing the sharp lower and upper frame bounds approach the condition-number optimizer symmetrically, at distance asymptotic to \(\log(2n)/(3n)\) on either side. Using the condition-number-optimal lattice incurs the same sharp relative regret \(\log^2(2n)/(18n^3)\) for both frame-bound objectives.
