# Self-review

**Correctness (PASS).** For the two labelled tokens on \(S^1\), the axial sign configurations have zero first derivative of every attention logit in the horizontal tangent coordinates. Therefore the exact linearizations are \((I+\eta aA_C)/(\eta b-1)\) and \((I+\eta aA_B)/(\eta b\tanh\beta-1)\); diagonalization by symmetric and antisymmetric directions gives both complete two-step spectra. A strict negative numerator sign is checked separately from stability. The largest return multiplier is below one exactly on the claimed open parameter region. The proof covers all parameters without numerical extrapolation. Independent checker comparisons support but do not replace it.

**Originality (PASS, bounded historical assurance).** The exact model and the existing equilibrium classification are from Altafini (arXiv:2610.13518v1, October 2026), whereas the proved statement concerns two simultaneous non-equilibrium period-two sinks and a threshold controlled jointly by step size and softmax sharpness. The primary HTML text was inspected through its stability theorems, Section V-B on longer steps, experiments and conclusions; Section V-B gives only a short discussion of restrictions for equilibrium Jacobians and does not state a period-two theorem. Hence the exact two-cycle result is not implied by the published equilibrium theorems. Gao--Yang--Chen (arXiv:2609.28448) already exhibits repulsive attention flip patterns, but specializes to a negative-definite value map rather than the mixed-sign matrix here. Kuehn--Yoon (arXiv:2604.26085) treats continuous-time dynamics. Targeted indexed-record and primary-literature searches produced no demonstrated implication covering the sharp joint criterion. Priority requires human checking.

**Value (PASS).** The result identifies a sharp, interpretable phase boundary for coexistence of two fundamentally different oscillatory attractors in the minimal labelled-token model, rather than just instability of a consensus state. It quantifies both transverse modes and shows exactly how attention selectivity and residual strength trade off when a positive principal eigenvalue competes with a stronger negative mode. It is a structural local classification on a natural invariant pair of orbits, with no unsupported whole-phase-space claim.

**Limitations.** Only local hyperbolic attraction of the two axial flip cycles is classified; their basins, other attractors, and the nonhyperbolic equality case are not. Complete historical-priority assessment and independent validation remain open.
