At a telegraphically driven harmonic trap's collective-mean support edge, the chance of a thermal overshoot is asymptotic to an explicit constant times \(N^{-\gamma/(2\mu)}\), and the hidden filtered center selected by an overshoot has a nontrivial universal boundary-layer distribution. Beyond the \(N^{-1/2}\) window, the same event crosses to an explicitly prefactored Gaussian large-deviation tail.
