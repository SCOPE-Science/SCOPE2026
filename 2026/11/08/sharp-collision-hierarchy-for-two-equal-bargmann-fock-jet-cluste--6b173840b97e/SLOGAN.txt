Two equal clusters of normalized derivative evaluations in Bargmann–Fock space lose stability in distinct scales \(r^{4p-2}\) for \(1\leq p\leq m\), with explicit sharp constants for every jet multiplicity. The smallest Gram eigenvalue is asymptotic to \(\frac{[(m-1)!]^4}{2(2m-1)![(2m-2)!]^2}r^{4m-2}\), quantifying the worst conditioning of nearly colliding Hermite data.
