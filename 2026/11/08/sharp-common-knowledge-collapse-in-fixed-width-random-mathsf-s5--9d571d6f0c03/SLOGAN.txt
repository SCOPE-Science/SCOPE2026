In fixed-width independent \(\mathsf{S5}\) partitions, common knowledge and universal knowledge coincide as Boolean operations with probability \(1-\frac{((b-1)!)^r}{b!}n^{-\gamma}(1+O(n^{-1}))\), where \(\gamma=(b-1)(r-1)-1>0\). The rare failure almost always consists of a single \(b\)-world knowledge component separated from a connected remainder; the two-agent pairwise matching case is the unique exceptional regime.
