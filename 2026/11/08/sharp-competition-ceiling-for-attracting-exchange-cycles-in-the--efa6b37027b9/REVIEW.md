# Review of the exchange-cycle stability classification

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exchange conditions are solved exactly and uniquely by taking logarithmic sum and difference; the growth rate is strictly monotone in the amplitude parameter. The two-step Floquet derivative equals the square of the explicit signed-swap Jacobian. The strict Schur criterion reduces to two independent monotone functions and yields a complete stability interval. The sharp coupling threshold follows from the unique positive root of a strictly increasing hyperbolic ratio. A standalone checker cross-checks exact symbolic identities and sample dynamics. There are no claims about global dynamics, unproved finite exhaustion, or nonhyperbolic endpoint attraction.

## Originality

**PASS on available evidence, with a significant historical-access risk.** The 2026 source supplies general two-cycle Jacobian tests without eliminating its free orbit coordinates; the 2022 same-map study addresses out-of-phase cycles and numerically observed stability regions. The precise new contribution is the complete exact all-positive-parameter exchange branch, sharp coupling ceiling, and explicitly characterized two growth boundaries, not the discovery of antiphase oscillation. A 1987 full-length article of Jiang and Rogers treats exactly this symmetric family and its period-two Hopf region; only its abstract was accessible. Therefore independent historical-priority checking, especially of that article's implicit stability formulas, remains essential. Research-database comparisons against synchronous Ricker cycle results, other competitive models, and exchange-symmetric dynamics did not demonstrate dominating coverage; search absence alone is not used as proof.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The sharp value \(a_*\) is the natural maximal cross-competition coefficient permitting a hyperbolic attracting exchange cycle in the entire two-parameter family; the two growth thresholds answer where stable alternating coexistence exists, rather than describing one arbitrarily chosen numerical parameter slice. This exact structural boundary is motivated by ecological periodic competition and by prior analysis of phase multistability.

## Literature and limitations

The closest established literature includes Goldar et al. (2026, DOI 10.1155/ddns/7255117), Kulakov et al. (2022, DOI 10.3390/math10071076), and Jiang–Rogers (1987, DOI 10.1007/BF00275495). The last is a primary same-model source with material unresolved priority risk because its full text was inaccessible; the first two were examined in pertinent full-text sections. The result does not classify arbitrary two-cycles, global attraction, or dynamics at nonhyperbolic equality.
