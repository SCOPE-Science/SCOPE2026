For monomial defects of \((1-\langle z,w\rangle)^{-\gamma}\), the complete Pick property has an exact dimension-independent sharp amplitude threshold for \(0<\gamma\le1\), while all higher-dimensional defects fail for \(\gamma>1\). The threshold permits noninjective monomial symbols below the Hardy exponent, and its fraction of the contractive-multiplier range decays as \(m^{-2\gamma}\) at fixed \(0<\gamma<1\).
