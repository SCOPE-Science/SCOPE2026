For every complex centered affine function on a uniformly sampled simplex of dimension at least two, the sharp normalized fourth moment has explicit dimension-dependent endpoints: the lower extremizers are equal-modulus, first- and second-phase-balanced vertex values, and the upper extremizers are real one-outlier patterns except on a triangle. An exact nonnegative deficit proves lower stability, and the complex Banach--Mazur distance of the entire centered affine \(L^4\) space to Euclidean space has fourth power \(3(3m^2-7m+6)/(2(m-1)(m+3))\), approaching \(9/2\).
