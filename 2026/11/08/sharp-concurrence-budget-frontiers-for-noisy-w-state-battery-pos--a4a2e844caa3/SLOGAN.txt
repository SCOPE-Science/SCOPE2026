For noisy three-qubit W-state quantum-battery postselection, limiting the selected auxiliary measurement ray's concurrence to \(\gamma\) gives two distinct sharp budgets: the maximal pure-state branch weight is \((4+2\gamma)/3\), whereas the maximal useful excited-state weight is \(1+\gamma\). The resulting success-probability, capacity and ergotropy frontiers are attained explicitly but have incompatible optimizers unless \(\gamma=1\).
