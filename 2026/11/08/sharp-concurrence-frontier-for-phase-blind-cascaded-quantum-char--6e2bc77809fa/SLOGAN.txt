For the exact three-qubit collision battery, the full range of phase sensitivity at fixed pure two-qubit charger concurrence \(c\) is \([0,2\sqrt{2e(1-e)L(1-L)}\sqrt{1-c^2}]\). Every maximally entangled charger is phase-blind and, apart from a dark singlet and endpoint exchange exceptions, yields deterministic convergence to half charging under local adaptive measurements—even when its state is not Bell-like in the computational basis.
