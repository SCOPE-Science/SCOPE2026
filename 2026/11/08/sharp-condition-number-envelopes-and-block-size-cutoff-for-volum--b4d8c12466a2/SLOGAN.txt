For volume-sampled block projections with a positive definite \(d\)-dimensional Gram matrix of condition number at most \(K\), the sharp least-eigenvalue guarantee is \(r/[r+(d-r)K]\) and the sharp mean-projector condition number is \([r-1+(d-r)K]/(d-1)\). Both worst cases arise from one small Gram eigenvalue and all other eigenvalues at the upper limit, giving an exact guaranteed block-size cutoff for any requested one-step contraction.
