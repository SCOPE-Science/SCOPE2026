# Review — sharp conditional section-type diagram

## Correctness — PASS

The exact coordinate normal form follows from opposite-edge equalities. A Haar-random plane meets either three or four edges; the indicator identity and pairwise spherical sign law yield the displayed arcsine profile. The constrained optimization correctly handles positive coordinates and the change of feasible endpoint at \(t=1/2\). Both monotonicity derivatives were checked. The standalone script provides independent polar-solid-angle evaluations and finite Monte Carlo tests, but neither is invoked as proof of the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)

Feldman and Klain (2008) give the general relation between simplex projection probabilities and solid vertex angles, and Katsuura (2020) already implies the **unconditional** regular tetrahedron extremum; that fact is not claimed new. Their complete inspected texts do not state the sharp conditional feasible range at fixed largest normalized squared bimedian, its equality cases, or the attained-to-degenerate transition. Closely related database records address disphenoid radius ratios, tetrahedral shadow-area statistics and box central sections with other conditioning. Hajja and Walker's (2001) complete text remained inaccessible after open-access search and a disconnected institutional retrieval; historical-priority risk remains. ## Mathematical value — PASS

The result converts the observable fraction of four-sided centroidal sections into **optimal** bounds on the dominant intrinsic bimedian ratio. It gives the entire feasible conditional interval and explains a sharp change in its upper boundary, not just an isolated evaluation or a routine regular-case inequality.

## Limitations

Only positive-volume equifacial tetrahedra and Haar-uniform centroidal planes are treated. The highest probability is only a supremum when \(t\ge1/2\). The inverse bounds do not recover the three individual bimedian lengths. Priority and correctness have not been independently audited.
