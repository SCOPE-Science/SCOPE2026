Conditioning at exactly critical density is a natural, quantitatively important invariant: it controls stability of coefficient reconstruction and limits design choices even when the qualitative frame property is known. The finding determines exact bounds for **all finite mixtures and all pole heights**, settles the natural question whether positive mixtures can outperform a single pole, and proves an exact rigidity law for near-optimizers. In particular the minimum \((1+\sqrt2)^4\) is independent of mixture size and lattice aspect ratio after optimizing height.
