In the symmetric two-voter voting-dependent Friedkin–Johnsen model, every positive initial opinion gap has exactly one critical stubbornness: below it opinions converge exponentially, above it disagreement survives, and at equality the gap has universal \(2/(cn)\) decay. The finer critical signature is \(1/x_n=cn+(c/2)\log n+H+o(1)\), with a positive logarithmic correction independent of the starting gap.
