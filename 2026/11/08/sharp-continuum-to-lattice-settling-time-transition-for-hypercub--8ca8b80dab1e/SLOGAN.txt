For farthest-corner contractions of a Euclidean \(d\)-cube, the last first-sign-change time has the exact law \(\Pr(H_d\le n)=[\min\{1,e^{n\delta}-1\}]^d\), with \(\delta=-\log\lambda\). As dimension grows and contraction weakens, its fluctuations change from a continuous exponential limit to an arithmetic-phase-dependent lattice exponential law at the sharp scale \(d\delta\asymp1\).
