# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
After an invertible affine normalization, every slice of the trapezoid and all four vertex-attached homothets is an explicit interval. The bottom interval pair can fail at heights below the start of the upper two copies; the upper pair covers every higher slice when the homothety fraction is at least one half. This partitions the entire body, including transition lines and endpoints, and gives the exact triangular hole and its area. The long base witnesses failure below unit dilation. The parallelogram endpoint is considered separately. These arguments prove both threshold directions and all real parameter cases claimed. A separate standard-library exact-rational verifier made 39,919 checks; finite checks are only corroboration.

## Originality — PASS (provisional priority)
The general Schneider index, vertex-generation normalization and the parallelogram endpoint are already known (Artstein-Avidan–Falah–Slomka, arXiv:2407.20604). Meyer (arXiv:2610.26744) establishes general finiteness and exposed-face criteria without an exact non-parallelogram trapezoid formula. Relevant research-record searches returned other trapezoid invariants and convexification of other polygon families, but no reported exact base-ratio threshold together with its all-near-critical quadratic area deficit. The contribution is the latter pair of quantitative identities, not an equivalent wording of the existence result. The historical possibility of an unindexed exact computation remains.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Trapezoids are the natural one-parameter affine family bridging parallelograms and the degenerate triangular limit. The result exactly classifies a recognized nonconvexity invariant throughout that family, and the stronger missing-area formula identifies the full gap geometry and quadratic critical exponent, giving quantitative information that a bare threshold does not supply. The calculation exposes a sharp transition between bottom-only and top-assisted covering instead of simply substituting parameters into a known general bound.

## Scope and residual limitations
The deficit formula is only for dilations at least one; the class is planar trapezoids, not all quadrilaterals. Historical priority and independent mathematical validation remain open. The computational verifier checks finite rational instances and is not an exhaustive verification of real parameters.
