For every planar convex trapezoid with shorter/longer parallel-side ratio \(t\), the Schneider nonconvexity index of its four vertices is exactly \(2-t\). For \(0<t<1\) and \(1\le\lambda\le2-t\), the fraction missed by the normalized vertex translates is precisely \((2-t-\lambda)^2/((1-t^2)(1+\lambda)^2)\), yielding a sharp quadratic onset of convexity.
