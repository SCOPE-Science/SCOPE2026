For every \(k\ge3\), two finite Hamming graphs have the same counting-free \(k\)-variable first-order theory at unrestricted depth precisely when their dimensions agree and their alphabet sizes agree after truncation at \(k\). Thus three variables recover arbitrarily large dimension, but \(k\) variables cannot tell alphabet size \(k\) from any larger size; the sharp relative defining width is one more than the alphabet size.
