# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every contribution to covariance of a triple indicator with a pair indicator is classified by the cardinality of their intersection. The two-point conditional success probability is exactly shorter separation divided by the full circumference, making the mixed integral elementary. One-point conditionings are constant by rotational invariance. Likewise, pair-pair and triangle-triangle overlaps give their exact variances, including correct counts of ordered triangle pairs. Boundary and normalization conventions are explicit. The included independent verifier confirms a collection of exact formulas and independent geometric simulations; it is supplementary rather than an infinite proof.

## Value — PASS
This directly relates a global geometric depth statistic to a local pair-distance statistic under a natural null law. The exact finite-size correlation and its sharp optimizing geometric threshold give an interpretable calibration and show that the two statistics remain strongly anticorrelated as sample size increases. The unnormalized covariance has no arbitrary subfamily or tuned parameter, and the optimal cutoff is an intrinsic right angle.
