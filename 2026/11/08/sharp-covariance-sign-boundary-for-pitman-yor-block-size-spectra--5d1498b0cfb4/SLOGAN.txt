In every nondegenerate Pitman–Yor sample, the covariance of total diversity with the number of blocks of size \(r\) switches exactly once from positive to negative as \(r\) increases. Its last-positive-size fraction converges to an explicit gamma-ratio constant, yielding distinct sharp values \(1-4/\pi^2\) and \(1-\pi^2/16\) for sampled Brownian-motion and Brownian-bridge excursion partitions.
