For all unit-weight finite bipartite graphs with separate degree caps \(p>q\ge2\), the exact universal Crank–Nicolson positivity half-step is \(\sigma_{p,q}=2(2p-1)/(B_{p,q}+\sqrt{B_{p,q}^2+4(2p-1)D_{p,q}})\), with \(B_{p,q}=2p^2-2pq-2p+q\) and \(D_{p,q}=p(2pq-p-q)\). Finite balls of the \((p,q)\)-biregular tree make it sharp, and the asymmetric cap strictly improves the unrestricted maximum-degree-\(p\) rule.
