For a convex polygon cut by a Euclidean-motion-invariant random line, the small-fragment area probability has a sharp \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) law whose coefficient is an explicit sum of corner-angle integrals. Over all parallelograms that coefficient is strictly below \(\sqrt2\), with the unattained supremum approached by collapsing rhombi; for regular \(n\)-gons it decays as \(\pi^2/(4\sqrt n)\).
