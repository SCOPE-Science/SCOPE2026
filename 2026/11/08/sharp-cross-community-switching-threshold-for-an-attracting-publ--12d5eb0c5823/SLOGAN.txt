A connected, fully positively weighted opinion network can support two attracting consensus equilibria and an attracting cooperation-switching two-cycle under synchronous public-goods best response. For two equal communities, this period-two pattern exists exactly when within-community influence \(p\) is smaller than the explicit sharp threshold \(p_*=(1-c)(1-q)/(2(1-c+cq))\), and the cycle remains stable against full-state perturbations.
