In a minimal three-state semi-Markov network with Poisson resetting, increasing one competing exit rate bends the two leaf-occupation responses toward each other for subexponential gamma shapes and away from each other for superexponential shapes, with a straight line exactly at shape one. For gamma shape two, the less-selected leaf paradoxically gains stationary probability over an initial control interval exactly when \(r(r+a)>k^2\), peaking at \(\lambda=\sqrt{r(r+a)}-k\).
