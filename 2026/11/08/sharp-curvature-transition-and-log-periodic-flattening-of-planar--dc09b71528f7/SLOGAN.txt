For a planar diagonal contraction with full disk-bounded noise, the unique minimal attractor has a \(C^2\) strictly convex boundary for every pair of positive contracting eigenvalues, but its two fast-axis tips become exactly flat when \(b^2\ge a\). At equality the curvature decays reciprocally logarithmically; beyond it the decay is a fractional power with a rigorously nonconstant log-periodic coefficient.
