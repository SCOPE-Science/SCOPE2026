For the logistic smooth-to-hard spectral penalty, the source's sharp weak-convexity scale \(\beta_c\) is the exact dividing line between a globally single-valued proximal map and one with a unique two-minimizer input for every \(\beta>\beta_c\). At the boundary the map remains unique but develops a cubic-root cusp; just above it the minimizer jump is proportional to \((\beta/\beta_c-1)^{1/2}\) and the three-stationary-point window has width proportional to \((\beta/\beta_c-1)^{3/2}\).
