For a binary necklace of length \(n\) and weight \(k\), the number of distinct 2-binomial fingerprints among its rotations lies sharply between \(n/\gcd(n,k)\) and \(n-\gcd(n,k)+1\). The minimum occurs exactly for repetitions of a length-\(n/\gcd(n,k)\) word; clustered zeros and ones achieve the maximum.
