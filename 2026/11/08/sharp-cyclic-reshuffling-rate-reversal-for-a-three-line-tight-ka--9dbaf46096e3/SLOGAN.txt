For a minimal three-line equiangular tight frame, optimally relaxed random reshuffling wins the first Kaczmarz epoch, but the optimally relaxed fixed cyclic order wins in worst-case squared error at every epoch \(k\ge2\). Exact formulas also show that fresh reshuffling strictly improves on three independent with-replacement row draws for every \(0<\omega<2\), while the cyclic asymptotic squared factor is about forty-one times smaller than the reshuffling optimum.
