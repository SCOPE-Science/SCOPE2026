For the recursive tree grown by uniformly recruiting one vertex to receive \(m\ge2\) new leaves at a time, the maximum child degree is asymptotic to \(m\log n/\log(m+1)\) almost surely. Its maximizing recruiter is typically born at step \(n^{1-m/((m+1)\log(m+1))+o(1)}\), and a sharp age-restricted maximum-degree frontier interpolates between the root's scale and the global extreme.
