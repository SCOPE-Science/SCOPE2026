In basic modal logic \(\mathsf K\), the sharp uniform model-size cutoff for satisfiable formulas over \(k\) letters of modal depth \(d\ge1\) is exactly the number of depth-\((d-1)\) Hintikka types: \(h_0=2^k\) and \(h_{j+1}=2^k2^{h_j}\). A cyclic graph with one representative of every predecessor type realizes any prescribed depth-\(d\) type, and a formula demanding every predecessor type proves optimality.
