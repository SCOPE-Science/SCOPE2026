In a radiatively damped two-level emitter with independent ground-state Poisson resetting, stationary fluorescence is enhanced precisely when \(4\Delta^2>3\gamma^2\) and the resetting rate is below an explicit crossover. Above that detuning threshold the current has a unique resetting-rate optimum independent of drive strength, and its gain over the unreset current is always strictly below the sharp factor \(2\).
