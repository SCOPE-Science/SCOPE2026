For binary two-dimensional length-\(n\) linear codes, the optimal Hamming distortion is exactly the ratio spread of their three sorted nonzero weights. Its sharp worst-pair value is \(n\) for every \(n\ge3\), and every extremal pair consists of a singleton-plus-repetition code and an equal-weight simplex-column code, with exactly \(2\lfloor n/3\rfloor\) ordered equivalence-class pairs.
