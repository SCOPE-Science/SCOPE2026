For every \(n\ge4\), maximum inclusion-minimal dominating sets of the three-by-\(n\) rook graph are exactly the non-surjective ternary column words. Their token-jump and token-slide graphs coincide, with \(3\cdot2^n-3\) states and sharp diameter \(n+\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\), showing an asymptotic 50% detour compared with the ambient Hamming graph.
