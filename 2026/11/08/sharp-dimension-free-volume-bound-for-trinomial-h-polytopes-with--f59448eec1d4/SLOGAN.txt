For every integer \(k\ge2\), a lattice polytope with Ehrhart numerator \(1+a t^k+2t^{2k}\) has normalized volume at most \(12\) (equivalently \(a\le9\)), and equality is attained for every \(k\). Exact character obstructions and three rational dual witnesses solve the final-coefficient-two case of the dimension-free sharp-volume problem.
