For independent random-plane Rayleigh–Ritz ascent on a rank-one-spiked Gram matrix, the exact dimension-proportional-iteration large-deviation rate for logarithmic spectral-gap improvement is \(I_c(s)=(s-c-c\log(s/c))/2\) for \(s>0\). This gives sharp exponential probabilities of meeting or missing any fixed error-reduction target, beyond the known expected-error rate.
