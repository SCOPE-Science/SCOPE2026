For the ideal-storage coupled map, directed averaging can destabilize an otherwise stable homogeneous state on just three sites: on an explicit parameter line the sharp threshold is the unique positive root \(\delta_*\in(0.8141,0.8142)\) of \(3\delta^4+14\delta^3+17\delta^2-10\delta-12=0\). No such early linear instability is possible with symmetric averaging at any size, or with any row-stochastic averaging on one or two sites.
