For every convex \(n\)-gon, replacing the polygon by the union of its inscribed radius-\(r\) disks loses at least \(r(\sec(\pi/n)-1)\) in Hausdorff distance, sharply at every radius for regular polygons. The loss is exactly controlled by the sharpest corner for sufficiently small radii, and near equality forces every interior angle quantitatively close to the equiangular value; a vertex-cone formula extends the local law to all convex polytopes.
