On Gaussian radial-angular Fock spaces with angular supremum and finite radial exponent \(q\), a Weyl displacement of length \(R\) has operator norm of sharp order \((1+R)^{1/q}\), rather than the isometric behavior of classical Fock norms. Every nonzero displacement has the full unit-circle spectrum, and strong continuity holds for finite \(q\) but fails at the radial-supremum endpoint \(q=\infty\).
