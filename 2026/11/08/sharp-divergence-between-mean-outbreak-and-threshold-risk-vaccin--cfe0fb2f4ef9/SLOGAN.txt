On a contact path, the vertex minimizing expected outbreak size is always central, but the vertex minimizing the probability of at least \(h\) infections lies exactly \(h-1\) steps from an endpoint whenever \(n\ge2h\). The objectives have a common optimum precisely for \(n\le2h\); beyond the sharp cutoff, vaccinating the center incurs the exact additional threshold risk \((h-1)p^h/(n-1)\) for every \(0<p<1\).
