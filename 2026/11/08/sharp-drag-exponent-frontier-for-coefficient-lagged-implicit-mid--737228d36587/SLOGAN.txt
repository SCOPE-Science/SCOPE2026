For a scalar gas--dust source step, freezing the drag coefficient in a two-stage midpoint Picard solver converges from the physical initial guess for every step size precisely throughout the square-root-or-slower family \(\alpha(w)=a(1+bw^2)^{m/2}\), \(0<m\le1\). Faster-growing drag laws can instead produce an attracting two-cycle around the unique implicit stage; in the quadratic-rate case its exact onset is \(x=2(1+s)^{3/2}/\sqrt{sb}\).
