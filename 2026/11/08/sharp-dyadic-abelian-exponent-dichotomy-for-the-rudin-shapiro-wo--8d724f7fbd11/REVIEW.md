# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is an all-integer dyadic-period theorem. The binary-junction identity gives the exact aligned signed sums and the exact two-line restriction on normalized prefix sums at every dyadic level. No approximation of infinite factors is made: a 40-element length-six precursor language is proved closed under substitution, hence exhaustive; coding yields 36 six-sign factors. The exact 288-system one-variable elimination excludes four equal sums at odd dyadic period and five equal sums at even dyadic period, and explicit starts attain three/four equal sums respectively. The proof and executable finite certificate agree on all definitions, including the length-one boundary. The standalone checker passed, as did finite diagnostics for 495,567 positions and periods. The proof does not infer an infinite theorem from finite empirical testing: the closure and algebraic parametrization are the exhaustion steps.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Abelian exponent spectra by constrained block length are a natural invariant within Abelian repetition theory. The sharp alternating law \(4,3,4,3,\ldots\) gives infinitely many exact existence and nonexistence decisions for a classical automatic word, with explicit witnesses and a structural, reusable finite-language method.
