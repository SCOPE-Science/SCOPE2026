A rational axis cross-polytope has polynomial Ehrhart counting exactly when all reciprocal-axis denominators are powers of two with each multiplicity dominating the total number of higher dyadic weights. If its largest denominator is \(2^r>1\), this requires at least \(2^r\) dimensions, with a unique equality multiset and Ehrhart series \((1+z^{2^r})/(1-z)^{2^r+1}\), unattainable by any lattice polytope.
