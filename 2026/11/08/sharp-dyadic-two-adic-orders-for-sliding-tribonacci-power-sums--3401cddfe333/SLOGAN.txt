For every exponent \(e\ge3\), the power of two dividing the gcd of all length-\(2^r\) sliding Tribonacci power sums is exactly \(2^{r-2}\) when \(e\) is odd and \(2^{r-1}\) when \(e\) is even, for \(r\ge3\). In the even-exponent case this valuation occurs for every window; in the odd-exponent case the initial window witnesses sharpness.
