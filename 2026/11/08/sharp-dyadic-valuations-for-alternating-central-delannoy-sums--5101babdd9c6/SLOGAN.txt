The alternating central-Delannoy sum \(\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(-1)^k(2k+1)D_k\) has exact \(2\)-adic valuation \(0\) when \(n\) is odd and \(4\nu_2(n)-1\) when \(n\) is even. An odd-reciprocal harmonic-sum lemma explains the sharp exponent and its corresponding valuation law for the diagonal sequence OEIS A047781.
