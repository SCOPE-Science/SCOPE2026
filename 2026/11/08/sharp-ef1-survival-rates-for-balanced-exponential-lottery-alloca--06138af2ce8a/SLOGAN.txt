For a fixed ex-ante envy-free allocation of \(r\ge2\) goods per agent with independent exponential item values, ex-post EF1 has an exact rational probability and a sharp nonstandard decay rate: its \(n\)th root is asymptotic to \(K_r n^{-(r-1)/r}(\log n)^{r-1}\). With two goods per agent the constant is \(\sqrt\pi/4\), while exact ex-post envy-freeness has probability \(n^{-n}\).
