For complete endpoint allocations of positive symmetric edge-goods on a path, the number of zero-inclusive EFX orientations ranges sharply from \(m+1\) to \(\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor m/4\rfloor}\binom{m-2k+1}{2k+1}\) on a path with \(m\) edges. Constant weights achieve the exponentially large maximum, but an arbitrarily small strictly monotone weight perturbation leaves only \(m+1\) fair orientations.
