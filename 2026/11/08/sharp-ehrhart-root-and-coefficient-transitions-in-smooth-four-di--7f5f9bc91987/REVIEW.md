## Mathematical value: PASS
The source's canonical four-dimensional minimal-facet counterexample family admits three different sharp transitions: loss of positivity of the linear and quadratic Ehrhart coefficients and the subsequent creation of two positive real Ehrhart roots. Simultaneously, its numerator stays strictly real-rooted with exclusively negative zeros. This contrasts two structurally different positivity/root conditions within the same natural, smooth toric geometry example and explains the behavior across every integer parameter, rather than recomputing the source's single example. It provides an explicit infinite family where real-rooted \(h^*\) does not enforce Ehrhart coefficient positivity or absence of positive real Ehrhart zeros.
