The smooth four-dimensional Cayley family \(R_{2,c}\) has strictly negative real roots of its Ehrhart \(h^*\)-polynomial for every integer \(c\ge0\), even though its Ehrhart polynomial loses coefficient positivity first at \(c=10\), then at \(c=12\), and acquires two positive real roots at \(c=13\). These three transitions are sharp and follow from exact formulas valid for the entire family.
