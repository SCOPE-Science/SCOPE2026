In a harmonically confined Brownian particle with hidden symmetric two-state diffusivity switching, stationary position moments of orders \(2,4,8\) exactly recover both diffusivities, the switching rate, and entropy production, with a sharp feasible-data region. The sixth moment is entirely determined by the second and fourth, and distinct positive models with different dissipation share every position moment through order six; thus eighth order is minimal for this snapshot-only inverse problem.
