# Scientific review: sharp boundary equilibrium spectrum of an invariant-square quartic flow

## Correctness — PASS

The object is precisely the six-real-parameter system in Grau–Szántó [1, Eq. (3)] with the closed square \([-1,1]^2\). Boundary equilibria split into four corners plus interior roots of two horizontal and two vertical edge quadratics. The horizontal pair has a structural obstruction to attaining two roots on both edges: two upper roots demand \(a<0\), while two lower roots demand \(a>0\). This proves an eleven-point ceiling, and the strict two-root criterion gives the precise maximal parameter chamber. Every finite cardinality from four through eleven is exhibited by an exact rational tuple. The only possible stationary entire edge is a vertical edge satisfying the three stated linear conditions. At the eleven-point witness, nonzero exact resultants and nonzero corner eigenvalues show every equilibrium is hyperbolic. The accompanying script checked 17,998 rational parameter tuples, 1,330 quadratic instances, all eight cardinality witnesses, the transverse resultants and corner spectra. Finite computations support, but do not supply, the real-parameter proof. Endpoint and double-root conventions are explicit.

## Originality — PASS within checked literature

The full open-access Grau–Szántó source [1] was read for its defining system and its four main theorems. Those concern the center/focus problem and bifurcation of small-amplitude limit cycles in the invariant square interior, not the boundary stationary spectrum or maximal semialgebraic chamber. Llibre–Rodrígues [2] treats existence of periodic orbits for polynomial systems with invariant straight lines; that cannot imply the exact boundary root distribution for this fixed sextuple family. Search of published mathematical result abstracts surfaced a close item on **two-term period growth in an invariant-square quartic center** (`73607523fe89`), but its conclusion is about interior periodic orbits, not boundary equilibria. Other close objects were a quartic Kolmogorov flow (`585276179f44`) and an unrelated equilibrium curve (`d75e1bae1c5f`). No compared statement implies the complete classification. A claim of complete novelty across unindexed literature is not possible; a reparametrized prior calculation may exist.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The originating paper asks about dynamics inside an invariant polygon, so the stationary dynamics on that polygon's four invariant edges is a natural complementary structural question. The answer is not four independent quadratic root counts: the square-specific coupling forces a sharp loss of one possible boundary equilibrium, leading to the exact eleven-point ceiling. More than one isolated example, the theorem gives the full cardinality spectrum, the exceptional full-edge continuum, and an exact open maximal chamber with eleven hyperbolic equilibria. These supply precise boundary phase-portrait data for future global studies. No coexistence with the source's five interior cycles is claimed.
