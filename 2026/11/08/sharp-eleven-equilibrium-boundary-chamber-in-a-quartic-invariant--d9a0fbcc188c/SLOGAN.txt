For the six-parameter quartic flow with an invariant square studied by Grau and Szántó, the exact possible numbers of distinct boundary equilibria are \(4,5,6,7,8,9,10,11\) and infinity; eleven is the sharp finite maximum, attained with all equilibria hyperbolic. The entire eleven-equilibrium chamber has an explicit strict semialgebraic description, while an infinite equilibrium set occurs exactly when one vertical edge is stationary.
