For a Gaussian short-time Fourier localization operator on a fixed-area ellipse with logarithmic axis stretch \(t\), the optimally concentrated signal loses exactly \(2s^2e^{-s}(s+2)^{-1}t^2+O_s(t^4)\) in captured energy relative to the disk. Its maximizing signal acquires the explicit first-order Hermite correction \(\sqrt2s(s+2)^{-1}t\,h_2\); the curvature is greatest at area \((\sqrt{17}-1)/2\).
