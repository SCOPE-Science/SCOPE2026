For a harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle with Brownian thermal noise, the critical Gaussian variance separating bimodal and unimodal stationary densities is strictly decreasing in the flip-to-trap ratio, with a quartically flat density exactly at the transition. Beyond the already-known curvature equation and leading inverse-logarithmic law, the critical inverse variance has a sharp \((3/2)\log\log(1/\epsilon)\) correction and explicit constant as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\), while the opposite endpoint has an explicit linear correction.
