Adding equal antipodal point masses of total weight \(w\in(0,1)\) to the uniform circle measure gives an exact sharp \(L^\infty\) norm for real first-harmonic least-squares projection: \(w+\frac2\pi[\sqrt{4-(1-w)^2}+(1-w)\arcsin((1-w)/2)]\). The only worst-conditioned evaluation points are perpendicular to the atomic diameter, and the norm grows strictly and convexly with the atomic weight.
