For a finite real binary Haar system with arbitrary product biases, the exact sharp signed \(L^\infty\) unconditionality constant is determined by a two-scalar backward recurrence, and every strict-bias maximizer concentrates on the unique path taking the less likely child at each step with alternating signs. For equal minority probability \(r\), the constant is \(1+2d(1-r)/(1+r)-4r^2(1-(-r)^d)/(1+r)^2\), giving a sharp bias-dependent linear growth rate and an exact exponentially decaying alternating correction; the fair case is a previously known boundary specialization.
