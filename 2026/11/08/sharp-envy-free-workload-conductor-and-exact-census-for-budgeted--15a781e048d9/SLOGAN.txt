For identical unit chores with positive integer capacities, every exactly envy-free assignment is either capacity-saturating or has a single water level \(t\), with genuine-agent loads \(\min(b_i,t)\) and housekeeper load at most \(t\). This yields an exact multinomial census and a sharp eventual-existence cutoff \(T=\sum_i\min(b_i,t_*)\), where \(t_*\) is the largest level with at least \(t_*+1\) agents having capacity at least \(t_*\).
