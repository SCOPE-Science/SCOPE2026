For \(\theta\)-convolution bodies in every dimension, Euclidean balls beat opposite simplices exactly below a unique, explicitly defined ball-versus-simplex threshold, which strictly extends the published elementary ball obstruction. The threshold has the sharp law \(\theta_n^*\sim(3/5)^n n^{-7/30}(2^{27}/(3^{18}\pi^7))^{1/30}\); this compares two families and does not locate the unknown universal optimality transition.
