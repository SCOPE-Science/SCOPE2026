For the Minkowski sum of coordinate diamonds over a complete multipartite graph, the unique minimum enclosing Euclidean ball has squared radius \(\sum_j b_j(\sum_{i<j}b_i)^2\), with class sizes \(b_1\ge\cdots\ge b_r\). Its contacts arise precisely by ordering the classes from largest to smallest and independently signing all nonzero coordinates: their exact number is \(2^{N-\max b_i}\prod_s m_s!\).
