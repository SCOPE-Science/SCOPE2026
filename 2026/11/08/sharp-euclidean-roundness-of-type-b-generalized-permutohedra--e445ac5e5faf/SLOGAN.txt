Among all type-\(B\) generalized permutohedra in dimension \(n\ge2\), the sharp Euclidean inradius-to-circumradius ratio is \(\bigl(\sum_{k=1}^{n}(\sqrt k-\sqrt{k-1})^2\bigr)^{-1/2}\). Its unique signed-permutation-symmetric optimizer has successive orbit weights \(\sqrt k-\sqrt{k-1}\), all \(3^n-1\) facets tangent to one inball, and optimal roundness asymptotic to \(2/\sqrt{\log n}\).
