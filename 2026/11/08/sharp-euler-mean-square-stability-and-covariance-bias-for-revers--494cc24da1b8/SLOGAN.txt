For the reversible-noise two-dimensional Gaussian Langevin sampler, the Itô Euler–Maruyama chain has a finite-variance stationary law **exactly** when \(0<h<(A+D-\sqrt{(A-D)^2+4a^2})/(AD)\), with \(a=\lambda\delta^2\), \(A=1+a\), and \(D=\lambda+a\). Its invariant covariance is strictly inflated relative to the target, and the maximum admissible step decays as \(\delta^{-4}\) for strong reversible perturbations.
