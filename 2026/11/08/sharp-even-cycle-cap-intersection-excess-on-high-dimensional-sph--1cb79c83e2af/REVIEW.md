# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The domain is explicit: \(d\ge3\), \(r\ge2\), \(-1<c<1\), and independent uniform spherical points. Every strict cycle-edge subset is a forest, whose edge indicators are independent by conditional leaf integration. A centered-cycle expectation is therefore exactly the probability excess. The centered spherical cap operator is self-adjoint Hilbert–Schmidt, making its even powers trace class. Its degree-one eigenvalue is \(L_d(c)\), with multiplicity \(d\), by Funk–Hecke and the normalized zonal Sturm–Liouville relation. The complex-moment representation bounds every higher eigenvalue by \(L_d(c)\eta_d(c)\). Hilbert–Schmidt Parseval proves the exact finite-dimensional enclosure, including nonnegative remainder, and gamma-ratio bounds prove the uniform fixed-threshold asymptotics. Spherical one-coordinate quantile convergence gives the fixed-density formula. The checker numerically verifies independent integrals, spectral sums and direct spherical samples; this corroboration is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
The original 2014 spherical geometric-graph model addresses signed triangles rather than all even-cycle constants. The substantially closer 2024 Bangachev–Bresler paper *does* study cycle Fourier coefficients and proves their dimensional-order upper estimates; these results are expressly not presented as new. Its Proposition 1.3 and subsequent cycle application supply bounds of the right dimensional exponent but not a positive matched coefficient for all fixed densities, nor the explicit first-harmonic two-sided finite-dimensional bound given here. Classical harmonic eigenvalues and cycle trace positivity imply groundwork, but are not identical to this quantitative enclosure and coefficient statement. An equivalent spectral trace expansion in other literature remains a priority risk.

## Value — PASS
The result supplies sharp constants and an explicit one-dimensional finite-parameter enclosure for a geometrically natural, genuinely dependent cyclic cap event. For \(2r=4\), the probability excess equals the variance of a two-cap common-neighbor area, a natural spherical integral-geometric statistic. For longer cycles it quantifies how dependence decays with dimension in an analytically controlled family. The positive sign alone is standard and would not justify value; the nonasymptotic quantitative control and all-length coefficient do.

## Closest literature, verification and limitations
- Bubeck–Ding–Eldan–Rácz (2014): identical geometric model, primarily signed triangles and detection; no stated all-even-cycle coefficient.
- Bangachev–Bresler (2024), Proposition 1.3 and Appendix A.2: signed cycles and four-cycle bounds; dimensional exponent and test-statistic formalism are prior art.
- Erba and collaborators (2020): high-dimensional random geometric-graph limits with different coordinate ensembles.
- Liu–Rácz (2021): latent-space detection and Gaussian-model regimes.
- Main residual risks: a different spectral-operator source may have an equivalent trace-power expansion; asymptotics assume fixed length and fixed density.
