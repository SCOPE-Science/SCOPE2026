For a cycle of \(2r\) spherical cap incidences among independent uniform sphere points, the excess over independent-edge probability is strictly positive and admits a sharp computable first-harmonic enclosure. At every fixed cap density, this excess is asymptotic to \(\phi(\Phi^{-1}(1-p))^{2r}d^{1-r}\), with an explicit \(O(d^{-r})\) error for each fixed \(r\ge2\).
