On any uniformly measured triangle, centered affine functions have sharp \(L^{2m}/L^2\) moment inequalities at every integer \(m\ge3\): edge-direction vertex values \((1,-1,0)\) uniquely minimize and altitude-direction values \((2,-1,-1)\) uniquely maximize, modulo symmetry and scaling. At absolute exponent three the extremal directions reverse, while the fourth moment is direction-independent; exact anisotropy remainders quantify every higher even order.
