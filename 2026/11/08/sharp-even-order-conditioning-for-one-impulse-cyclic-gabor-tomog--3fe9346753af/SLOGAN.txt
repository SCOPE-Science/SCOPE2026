For every even cyclic order \(N\ge6\), the best one-site complex perturbation of a flat Gabor window has exact lifted-tomography condition number \((\sqrt N-\cos(\pi/(2N)))/\sin(\pi/(2N))\). The only optimizers are the two phase-shifted negative impulses \(-\sqrt N e^{\pm i\pi/(2N)}\), which repair the positive-impulse even-order singularity.
