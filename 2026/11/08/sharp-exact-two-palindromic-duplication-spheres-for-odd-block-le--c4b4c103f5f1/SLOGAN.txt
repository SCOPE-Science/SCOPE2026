For every odd duplication length \(k\ge3\), a source with pairwise distinct letters reaches the universal exact-two palindromic-duplication sphere bound \((n-k+1)(n+1)-\binom{\max\{0,n-2k+2\}}2\) whenever the alphabet has at least \(n\) symbols. The only collisions among the two-step histories of such a source are the two commuting orders of duplications on disjoint original blocks.
