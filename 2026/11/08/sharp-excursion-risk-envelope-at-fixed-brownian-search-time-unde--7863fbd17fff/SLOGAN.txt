For a Brownian searcher resetting to random locations in a fixed positive interval, the same mean absorption time can conceal sharply different large-excursion risks: the entire family of above-support maximum laws is determined by two exponential reset-location moments. At fixed mean search time, deterministic resetting uniquely minimizes every high-threshold exceedance probability, while a unique two-endpoint reset mixture maximizes them all, with every intermediate risk level attainable.
