For planar Ricker competition with a nonsingular positive coexistence equilibrium \((X,Y)\), a strictly antiphase two-cycle exists exactly when \(X,Y>2\) and \(\alpha\beta<(1-2/X)(1-2/Y)\); it is absent at equality. The sharp threshold is the equilibrium's \(-1\) spectral boundary, but the result makes no stability or uniqueness assertion.
