For every fixed terminal unary-chain size \(r\ge2\), the probability that a uniform recursive tree has no such fringe subtree is \(\rho_r^{-n}+O(nR^{-n})\), where \(\rho_r\) is the unique positive solution of \(\int_0^{\rho_r} e^{-t^r/r!}\,dt=1\) and \(\rho_r<R<3/2\). A common complex zero-free disk proves the exact unit prefactor, and the decay rates become strictly slower with increasing chain length.
