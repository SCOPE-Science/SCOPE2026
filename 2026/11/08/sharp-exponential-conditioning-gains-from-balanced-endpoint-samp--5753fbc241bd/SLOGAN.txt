For full-band normalized-adjacency sampling on an even path, splitting a fixed budget of measurements between both endpoints halves the sharp exponential growth rate of noise amplification: the one-end condition number has per-vertex base \(1+\sqrt2\), while the balanced two-end design has base \(\sqrt{1+\sqrt2}\). Explicit Chebyshev inversion and a two-block finite-propagation decomposition prove the limits and the exact two-end determinant \(2^{-(m-1)^2}\) on \(2m\) vertices.
