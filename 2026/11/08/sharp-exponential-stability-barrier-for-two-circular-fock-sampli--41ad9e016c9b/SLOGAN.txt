Sampling degree-\(n\) Fock polynomials on two full regular \((n+1)\)-gons of freely chosen radii inevitably has an exponentially small lower frame bound, with sharp decay exponent \(R_*=0.1055733636919129\ldots\). The uniquely asymptotically optimal squared radii are \(nR_*\) and \(0.608010909078811\ldots\,n\), and their values arise from an exact two-center Poisson large-deviation covering problem.
