In an odd-size, majority-conditioned Rabi spin ensemble, the mean time to the first opposite-polarization reset grows as \(N e^{NI(a)}\) at every fixed subcritical detuning, but only as \(N^{1/4}\) in the critical window \(a=1/2-\lambda/\sqrt N\); both laws have explicit positive prefactors. In either regime the appropriately mean-scaled first-switch time converges to a unit exponential distribution.
