For any two distinct real Fourier frequencies, the sharp squared point-evaluation factor outside \([-1,1]\), relative to Lebesgue \(L^2\) energy, has nonattained supremum \((1+3x^2)/2\) for every \(|x|\ge1\). The exact fixed-gap factor is \((1-\operatorname{sinc}(d)\cos(dx))/(1-\operatorname{sinc}^2(d))\), with a strictly positive quadratic deficit as the two frequencies approach one another.
