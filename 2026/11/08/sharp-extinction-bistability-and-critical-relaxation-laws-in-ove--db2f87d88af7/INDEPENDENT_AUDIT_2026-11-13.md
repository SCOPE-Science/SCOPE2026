## correctness

PASS

The homogeneous mean-hazard substitution gives F(x)=(1-mu)x+(1-x)(1-exp[-mu*x-B*x²-C*x³]) and interval invariance. Equilibria require 0<x<1/(1+mu) and C=T_B(x) where L(x)=log((1-x)/(1-(1+mu)x)) has strictly positive coefficients ((1+mu)^k-1)/k. Thus T_B=(B2-B)/x+C3+sum_{k>=4}L_k x^{k-3}; for B<B2 it is coercive/strictly convex, for B=B2 strictly increasing, and for B>B2 strictly increasing from -infinity, giving the six count regimes. F'(x)>-mu>-1; the sign of F-x and T_B' gives all local multipliers. With no positive root, orbit monotone decreases to zero; reciprocal-power telescoping for F(x)=x-a x^{r+1}+... yields each three sharp extinction rates. Independent exact rational expansion for mu=2/5 confirmed coefficients B2, C3, D4; the source verifier includes finite motif/root scans, not used as proof of all x and all hypergraphs.
