# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For every finite simple 3-uniform hypergraph with constant projected degree \(d>0\), incident triangle count \(t>0\), and shared-edge triangle-pair count \(q\geq0\), the asserted homogeneous marginal recurrence follows by summing pair, triangle, and overlap mean-hazard terms. For \(0<\mu<1\) and critical pairwise intensity, the equilibrium equation is exactly the logarithmic level-set condition \(C=T_B(x)\), on \(0<x<1/(1+\mu)\). All coefficients in the analytic series are positive, giving strict convexity for \(B<B_2\) and strict monotonicity otherwise. This proves the complete equilibrium census and root multipliers. In no-positive-root regimes the orbit is strictly decreasing to zero, and reciprocal-power differences establish all three coefficients. The standalone verifier passed 166 check groups, including exact rational Taylor series and motif counting. The finite computations do not establish the infinite-parameter result by themselves.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The closest source, Liu and Schaposnik, *Higher-Order Co-Infection on Hypergraphs* (arXiv:2610.26072v1, 19 October 2026), defines the exponential mean-hazard rule and leaves the pairwise threshold-equality case unresolved in Proposition IV.1. The present scope resolves its homogeneous critical case, deriving a model-specific logarithmic saddle-node curve and exact relaxation amplitudes. Kiss–Iacopini–Simon–Georgiou (arXiv:2309.12752), Kiss–Bick–Simon (arXiv:2410.15330) and Liang–Cui–Liu (arXiv:2408.08602) already give higher-order bistability and other epidemic threshold results in different continuous-time or polynomial discrete-time systems. Thus general bistability or the principle of higher-order dependence is not claimed as new. Scientific database searches for the exact hazard recurrence, overlap count, equivalent bifurcation and critical-power laws identified no covering claim, not a proof of universal uniqueness. Related open-access article by Cui et al., *An SIS diffusion process with direct and indirect spreading on a hypergraph* (Automatica 177, 2025), is an additional plausible source using polynomial pathways; the read abstracts/models do not establish the present exponential formulas. An unindexed or implicitly stronger equivalent remains a residual novelty risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The source explicitly leaves the equality spectral threshold open. The result resolves a natural special geometry with a full nonnegative-parameter phase diagram; it separates the threshold for a positive endemic state from the threshold of linear instability and identifies a genuine codimension-two overlap-induced cubic critical law \(s^{-1/3}\). The formulas depend on intrinsic graph incidence and overlap statistics and give reproducible extinction-time benchmarks. This addresses a motivated dynamical phase transition, not an arbitrary finite instance or a routine expansion alone.

## Scope and limitations

The proof concerns the homogeneous invariant subspace of one specific deterministic exponential-hazard closure. It does not establish behavior of heterogeneous states or the underlying exact random process. Near a double endemic fixed point only its existence, multiplicity and unit multiplier are asserted. Independent peer or expert review has not been conducted.
