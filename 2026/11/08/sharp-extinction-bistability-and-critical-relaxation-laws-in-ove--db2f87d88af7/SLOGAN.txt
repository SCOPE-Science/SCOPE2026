For regular triangle hypergraphs, the single-pathogen exponential-hazard closure at its pairwise threshold has an exact logarithmic saddle-node curve separating global extinction from bistability. Triangle and overlapping-triangle intensities generate three distinct sharp homogeneous extinction laws, \(s^{-1}\), \(s^{-1/2}\), and \(s^{-1/3}\); the last requires nonzero hyperedge overlap and is not a statement about the exact finite stochastic epidemic.
