In the free inverse monoid on two generators, the largest number of distinct shortest representatives for an element whose Munn tree has \(m\) edges is explicitly \(4\,6^{\lfloor(m-1)/3\rfloor}2^{\mathbf1_{m\equiv0\pmod3}}\) for \(m\ge4\), with a complete description of the maximizing tree degree profiles. An exact local factorial formula also enumerates shortest representatives of every fixed Munn element and refines the counts across Green classes.
