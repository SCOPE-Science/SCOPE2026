For Brik's infinite binary word, the last length-\(n\) legal factor to appear is uniquely \(1^n\) at position \(r_n\), and the latest first appearance of any other is exactly \(r_{n-1}\), attained by \(1^{n-1}0\), for every \(n\ge3\). Thus the shortest prefix containing every length-\(n\) factor has length \(r_n+n-1\), where \(r_{n+1}=2^{r_n-2}+r_n\).
