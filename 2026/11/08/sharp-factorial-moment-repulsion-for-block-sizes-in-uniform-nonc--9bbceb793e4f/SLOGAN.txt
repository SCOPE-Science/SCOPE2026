Uniform noncrossing partitions exhibit a sharp, all-order suppression of mixed block-size falling-factorial moments: each additional distinguished block contributes at most \(\kappa_n=(2n-1)(n-1)/((n+1)(2n-3))<1\) times its ordinary expected count. The universal factor is exact for singleton–singleton and singleton–doubleton pairs, and forces negative covariance between all distinct block-size counts and sub-Poisson variance for every single size.
