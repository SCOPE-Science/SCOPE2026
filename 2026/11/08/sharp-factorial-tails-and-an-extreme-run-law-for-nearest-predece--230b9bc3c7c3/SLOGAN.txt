In the infinite regular-tree nearest-predecessor genealogy, the initial run of ancestral edges that remain actual tree edges has an exact distribution and the sharp tail \(\mathbb P(L\ge k)\sim \frac{H_{d-1}}{d-1}\frac{H_{d-1}^{\,k}}{(k+1)!}\). Despite this factorial suppression, the longest such run in a ball of \(N\) vertices grows almost surely like \(\log N/\log\log N\).
