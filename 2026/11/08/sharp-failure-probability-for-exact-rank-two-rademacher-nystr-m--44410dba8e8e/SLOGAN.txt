For a rank-two positive-semidefinite matrix, the sharp worst-case probability that \(m\) independent Rademacher columns fail to give exact Nyström recovery is \(3\cdot2^{-m}-2\cdot4^{-m}\) in every ambient dimension at least three, versus exactly \(2^{1-m}\) in dimension two. A three-coordinate matrix attains the bound for every \(m\ge2\), exposing a discrete-sketch zero-projection obstruction absent from Gaussian sampling.
