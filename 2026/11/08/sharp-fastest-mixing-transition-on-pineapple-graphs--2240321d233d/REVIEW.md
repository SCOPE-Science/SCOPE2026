# Scientific review: fastest mixing on pineapple graphs

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reduces global symmetric-stochastic optimization to an automorphism-invariant three-edge-parameter problem by convexity. It evaluates every invariant eigenvalue, obtains an exact negative-square characteristic identity for the first regime, and proves the opposing leaf-eigenvalue constraint in the second regime. The two explicit feasible matrices meet the derived lower bounds. Equality arguments establish uniqueness of hub–core and hub–leaf probabilities within the invariant family, without claiming all global optimizers are invariant. The supplementary checker verifies five exact identities, 121 fully instantiated spectra, and 2700 feasible non-invariant comparisons. These finite tests are not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, with residual risk

The 2004 paper of Boyd, Diaconis and Xiao establishes the general fastest-mixing SDP and automorphism averaging and evaluates complete bipartite graphs. The 2007 study by Boyd, Diaconis, Parrilo and Xiao develops group-based reductions and evaluates complete multipartite, wheel and other cases. Inspected original full-text sections do not state the evaluated two-regime multiple-pendant pineapple formula. An accessible 2026 pineapple-graph result optimizes graph curvature rather than symmetric-stochastic SLEM; its summary alone cannot rule out other content. A previously known single-leaf clique evaluation does not establish the multiple-leaf branch or the exact count cutoff. Unindexed equivalent literature remains possible, and independent publication-priority assessment has not occurred.

## Value — PASS

The invariant is the standard optimal SLEM for symmetric Markov kernels on a natural densely connected hub-and-leaf family. The exact boundary between a clique-helpful regime and a star-saturated regime is structurally meaningful: the count of pendant leaves \(r\) reaches the number of non-hub clique vertices \(a\). The formula gives deployable probabilities and quantifies exactly when adding or weighting clique edges cannot improve the spectral objective, beyond a mere recomputation of an isolated small graph.

## Scientific scope and remaining risks

All claims concern SLEM and symmetric, nonnegative row-stochastic matrices with self-loops allowed. The theorem does not characterize all non-invariant optimizers, certify publication priority, or optimize finite-horizon total variation. The original 2004 article documents an electronic public date; an earlier undated public draft remains possible. Source comparisons are limited to actually inspected sections and indexed searches, not a universal negative literature theorem.
