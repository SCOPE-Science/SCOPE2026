On a clique with \(a\ge2\) non-hub core vertices and \(r\ge2\) leaves attached to one hub, the globally fastest symmetric Markov chain undergoes a sharp cutoff at \(r=a\): below it the clique improves mixing strictly beyond the hub star, whereas at and above it the optimal SLEM is exactly the star value \(1-1/(a+r)\). Closed-form hub-edge probabilities and the optimal SLEM are obtained in both regimes, with unique hub-edge probabilities among automorphism-invariant optimizers.
