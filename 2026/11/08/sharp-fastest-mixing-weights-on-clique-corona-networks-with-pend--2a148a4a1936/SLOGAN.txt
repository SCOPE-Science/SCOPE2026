On a clique of \(m\ge3\) vertices with \(q\ge1\) pendant leaves attached to each core vertex, the globally smallest symmetric Markov-chain SLEM is exactly \(1-m/[(m-1)+q(\sqrt m+\sqrt{m-1})^2]\). Every invariant optimum puts zero holding probability on the core, and the optimal core-to-leaf and core-to-core edge weights reverse their ordering precisely at \(q=m-1\).
