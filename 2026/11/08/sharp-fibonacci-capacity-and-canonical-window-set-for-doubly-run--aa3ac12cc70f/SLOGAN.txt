For every binary circular no-triple word with distinct cyclic length-\(n\) windows, \(n\ge4\), the sharp maximum length is \(2(F_{n+1}-F_{n-3})\). Every maximizer has the same window set: precisely the length-\(n\) words avoiding \(000,111\) that do not begin and end with doubled symbols; an Euler circuit constructs every optimum.
