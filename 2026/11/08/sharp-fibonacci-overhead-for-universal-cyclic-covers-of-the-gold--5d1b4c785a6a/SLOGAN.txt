Every linearly \(11\)-avoiding binary word of length \(n\ge2\) can appear in one cyclic \(11\)-avoiding cover of exactly \(F_{n+2}+F_n-1\) symbols, and no shorter cyclic cover exists. The optimal extra \(F_n-1\) windows are realized by repeating precisely the factors \(0u0\) with nonzero admissible \(u\) of length \(n-2\).
