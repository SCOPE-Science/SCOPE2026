The 24-site snub-cubical Ising antiferromagnet has exactly two positive-field ground-state crossings, at \(h=J\) and \(h=5J\), separating zero, one-third and fully saturated magnetization sectors. The first crossing has exactly \(201\) states in five magnetization sectors; the exact finite-field classification follows from a triangle-incidence bound and exhaustive six-square enumeration.
