For the farthest-orthogonal-projection map on the three complete lines supporting an isosceles triangle of height \(h\) and half-base \(1\), there are exactly two attracting primitive five-cycles precisely when \(\alpha<h^2<\beta\), where \(\alpha\approx12.902457628\) and \(\beta\approx22.152760396\) are uniquely specified positive roots of explicit degree-five and degree-six polynomials. Outside this sharp interval, including both endpoints, there are no primitive five-cycles.
