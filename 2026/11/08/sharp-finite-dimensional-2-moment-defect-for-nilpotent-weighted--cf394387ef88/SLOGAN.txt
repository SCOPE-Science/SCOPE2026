Among norm-one nilpotent weighted shifts with \(N\) weights, the smallest defect of the single Embry identity \((T^*T)^2=T^{*2}T^2\) is exactly \(R_N^{-2}\), attained at one strictly tapered profile of weight moduli. It behaves as \(1/(2N)+(\log N)/(8N^2)+O(N^{-2})\), so no dimension-free positive gap holds in this class.
