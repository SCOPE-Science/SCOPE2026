For rectangular rook graphs \(K_a\square K_b\) with \(a,b\ge2\), counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{k}\) at width \(k\ge3\) detects exactly the unordered pair \(\{\!\{\min(a,k),\min(b,k)\}\!\}\), while widths one and two collapse the entire family. Consequently there are precisely \(\binom{k}{2}\) width-\(k\) theories and each graph has relative defining width \(1+\max(a,b)\).
