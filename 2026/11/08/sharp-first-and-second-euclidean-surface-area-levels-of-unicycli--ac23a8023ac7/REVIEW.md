# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof starts from the canonical intrinsic metric rather than an abstract affine equivalence. It derives the connected-bipartition facet formula with the actual \(\sqrt{|S|(n-|S|)}\) Euclidean norm and spanning-tree factors, then classifies all unicyclic bonds into bridges and pairs of cycle edges. The strict comparison excludes cycle length at least four. For triangle cycles, strict concavity forces the minimum rooted-tree mass vector and a quantified penalty for any non-leaf bridge; integer majorization forces the unique runner-up. The formulas and strict inequalities include the boundary \(n=5\), while \(n=4\) is treated separately. Independent finite enumerations from the graph cuts and original convex hull confirm the normalization for small dimensions. A finite enumeration alone would not establish the theorem; the proof establishes all \(n\ge5\).

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)

The closest primary works are Padrol–Pilaud–Poullot, arXiv:2111.12422, on graphical-zonotope faces and deformations, and Joós–Lángi, arXiv:2206.03204, on general intrinsic volumes and isoperimetric problems for zonotopes. Their examined full-text passages provide ingredients but not the new unicyclic two-level classification: a facet formula does not determine discrete equality types, and a general zonotope inequality does not distinguish all unicyclic graph incidence systems. Related results for tree graphical zonotope surface areas, graphical zonotope cut-expansion inradii, and complete-multipartite circumspheres have different hypotheses/invariants and do not imply the present result. Targeted searches for the exact graph classes, cut-energy reformulations, and near-minimum values did not identify a covering statement. Lack of an identified result does not certify worldwide priority; a graph-cut energy identity under different terminology is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The claim provides a natural sharp second-extremal classification across infinitely many genuinely distinct Euclidean graph polytopes, including an explicit first gap and a graph-theoretic equality characterization. In the triangle subclass all zonotopes have the same dimension, edge-generator count, mean-width scale, and intrinsic volume \(3\sqrt n\); surface area still detects the precise first local structural change from concentrated leaves. The bond formula makes the result reusable for quantitative isoperimetric and stability questions in cycle-containing graphical zonotopes. The contribution goes beyond a standard face count or a numerical table.

## Source and verification limitations

Primary text inspected: arXiv:2111.12422, definition and graphical-zonotope face description, especially Proposition 1.3 and surrounding pages; arXiv:2206.03204, the general intrinsic-volume decomposition and isoperimetric statements, including Corollary 1 and subsequent theorem discussion. This inspection supports the comparison of exact statements but does not rule out every alternative name for the resulting graph energy. Finite verification covers all nonisomorphic unicyclic graphs on four through nine vertices and direct Euclidean convex-hull areas in selected cases through six vertices. Weighted graphs, multicyclic graphs and affine re-embeddings are outside scope.
