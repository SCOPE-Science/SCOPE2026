For every \(n\ge5\), the Euclidean surface area of a unicyclic graphical zonotope is smallest for a triangle with all leaves on one vertex, and next-smallest for a triangle with exactly one leaf moved to another vertex. The unique first gap is exactly \(2(\sqrt{3(n-3)}-\sqrt{n-1})\), obtained from an explicit bridge-and-cycle-cut surface-area formula.
