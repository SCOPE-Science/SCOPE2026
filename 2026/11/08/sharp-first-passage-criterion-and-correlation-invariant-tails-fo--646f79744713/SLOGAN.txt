For a simple symmetric walk driven by positive heavy-tailed rates emitted by a primitive finite-state Markov chain, the mean first-passage time is finite exactly when \(\int_0^1 ds/(\sqrt{s}\,h(s))<\infty\), where \(h\) is the stationary rate-Laplace deficit. The sharp survival constant and tail depend only on the stationary mixture, not on finite-memory temporal correlations, and the critical power-log boundary is \(\alpha_*=1/2\) with integrability precisely when \(\beta_*>1\).
