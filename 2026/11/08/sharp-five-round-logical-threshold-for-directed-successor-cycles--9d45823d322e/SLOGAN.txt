In the single-binary-successor language, all directed cycles with at least \(19\) vertices have exactly the same first-order theory through quantifier rank five, while every smaller length is logically distinguishable from every other length. The complete rank-one-through-five thresholds are \(2,4,7,11,19\), proved by exhaustive exact type recursion on lengths through \(32\) and a general Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé tail argument.
