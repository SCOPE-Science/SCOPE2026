The second-order implicit Taylor update \((I-hQ+\tfrac12h^2Q^2)^{-1}\) preserves Markov positivity at every step size on unit directed cycles and absorbing birth chains with at most four states, but for every size at least five it loses positivity at arbitrarily small steps and eventually recovers it. In the first failing dimension, the sharp recovery steps are \(\sqrt{1+2/\sqrt5}-1\) for the five-cycle and the unique positive root of \(h^2(h+2)(h+6)=4\) for the five-state birth chain.
