# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof uses only three universal cubic identities, established for anti-quadratic nearly associative algebras by the motivating source. The displayed eight-step rewrite is a symbolic sign cycle on a generic left-associated quintic monomial; in characteristic not two it forces every such monomial to vanish. Near associativity reduces every binary bracketing to some left-associated expression, so the conclusion is universal in arity five and above. The arity-four signed monomial calculation is an exhaustive \(120\)-vertex computation with standalone code and the explicit dimension-thirty construction shows nonzero quartic products. The exterior-base constants satisfy all cubic identities, and the cotangent extension was checked directly on \(27000\) basis triples for near associativity and the skew-form anti-invariance. The canonical form is visibly nondegenerate. No inference from samples to the infinite theorem occurs.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The October 30, 2026 motivating paper states bound six, not five, and its complete relevant proof and cotangent-extension construction were inspected. The August 2, 2026 paper does state five but for a **non-equivalent symmetric-metric class** whose essential triple-product identity fails in the new explicit anti-quadratic example; there is no implication from its theorem to the current result. The original general nearly associative article (2021) was inspected in its defining-identity and representation sections, without the same quintic/quartic conclusions. Multiple actual mathematical research-record queries using the paper identifier, class aliases, sharp nilpotence, alternating quartic identities, and wedge-based extension found only more distant themes (e.g., flat Malcev algebras, Jordan lifts, unrelated nilpotent enumerations). Unsearched older operad and identity literature could contain an equivalent general polynomial identity; searches are not proof of historical priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result supplies the **exact** nilpotency threshold for a recently introduced natural algebra class, disproves sharpness of an explicit published upper bound, and identifies the complete arity-four alternating identity type. The thirty-dimensional construction shows why the improvement cannot be pushed to index four; it has the source's anti-quadratic structure, not merely an algebra satisfying a subset of identities. The coefficient-field independence (all characteristics other than two) is a further mathematical boundary. Minimal witness dimension and the characteristic-two case are open here, rather than asserted as solved.
