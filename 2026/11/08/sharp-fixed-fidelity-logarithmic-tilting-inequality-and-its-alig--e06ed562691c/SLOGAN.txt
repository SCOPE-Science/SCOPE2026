For two rank-one positive matrices with fixed nonzero squared overlap \(t\), the optimal logarithmic tilting coefficient is an explicit one-variable integral maximum that is strictly smaller than the unrestricted coefficient unless \(t=1\). The deficit at alignment is not linear: it is asymptotic to \(2s(1-s)s^s(1-s)^{1-s}(1-t)\log(1/(1-t))\), where \(s=(1-\alpha)/\alpha\in(0,1)\).
