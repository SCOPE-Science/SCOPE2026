For a pair of qubits coupled by \(\sigma_x\otimes\sigma_x\) and reset at arbitrary positive renewal times of fixed mean, the stationary mixed-state concurrence has a sharp three-piece upper envelope: a \(0.724611\ldots\) plateau unattained below its tangency threshold, a uniquely optimal periodic regime, and odd-half-period optimizers for slower clocks. The exact attainable range also has a zero-concurrence endpoint precisely when the mean is at least one full interaction period.
