For Brownian motion restarted at independent renewal times of fixed mean \(\mu\), the largest possible stationary density at any \(x\ne0\) has a sharp universal transition at \(|x|/(\sigma\sqrt\mu)=0.61200318\ldots\). Deterministic resets are uniquely optimal close to the reset point; farther away, the strict unattained supremum is approached by mixing nearly instantaneous resets with one universal longer excursion duration.
