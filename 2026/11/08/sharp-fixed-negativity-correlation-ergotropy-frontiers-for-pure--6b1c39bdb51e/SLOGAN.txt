For a pure pair of equally spaced qutrit batteries, fixed logarithmic negativity determines an exact interval—not a single value—of correlation ergotropy, with closed sharp bounds and unique extremizing Schmidt spectra. The maximally locked-energy spectrum changes sharply from Schmidt rank two to a full-rank spectrum with equal largest weights at \(\mathcal N=\log2\), while the least locked-energy spectrum has three distinct nonzero weights at every interior negativity.
