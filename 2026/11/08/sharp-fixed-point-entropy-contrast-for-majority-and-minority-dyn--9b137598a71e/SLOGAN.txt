For synchronous binary dynamics on the labelled helm graph, the exact majority and minority fixed-state counts reduce to different half-density-constrained cyclic words. The minority count is asymptotic to \(2\tau^n\) with tribonacci root \(\tau\), whereas the majority count has sharp parity-dependent asymptotic \(8(\sqrt3/2)^{n\bmod2}\sqrt{3/(2\pi n)}\,3^{n/2}\); hence minority fixed-state entropy is strictly larger.
