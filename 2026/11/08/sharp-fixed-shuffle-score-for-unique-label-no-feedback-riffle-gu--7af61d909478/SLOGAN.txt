For fixed \(k\) GSR riffle shuffles, requiring every card label to be guessed exactly once changes the optimal expected no-feedback score to \(C_{2^k}\sqrt n+o(\sqrt n)\), with an explicit finite-sum constant. A simple block-interleaved permutation attains the leading term; for one shuffle the coefficient is exactly \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\), strictly below the unrestricted repeated-guess coefficient.
