For two population types transmitting only across groups with independent Poisson offspring, fixing the epidemic reproduction number \(R=\sqrt{ab}>1\) makes equal directional rates uniquely maximize the chance of a sustained outbreak; the sharp probability range is \(s_{R^2}/2<P\le s_R\), where \(s_t=1-e^{-t s_t}\). Yet below threshold, equal rates uniquely minimize expected outbreak size, demonstrating opposite balancing effects on two natural epidemic risks.
