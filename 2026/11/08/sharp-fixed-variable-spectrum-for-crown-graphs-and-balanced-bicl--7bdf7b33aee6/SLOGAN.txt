Deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) is invisible to every counting-free two-variable first-order sentence, but detectable with three variables. For \(k\ge3\), all crown graphs and balanced bicliques have exactly \(2(k-1)\) distinct \(k\)-variable theories between them, classified by graph type and \(\min(n,k)\); crowns of indices \(k\) and \(k+1\) witness a strict hierarchy.
