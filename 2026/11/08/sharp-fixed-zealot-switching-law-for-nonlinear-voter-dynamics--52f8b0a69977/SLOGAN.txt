With exactly one fixed supporter of each opinion, the nonlinear \(q\)-voter model has a sharp first full-switching mean of \(C_q S^{-(q-2)/2}2^{(q-1)S}\), where \(C_q=2^{q+1}(2/\pi)^{(q-2)/2}/\sqrt{q-1}\), for every fixed integer \(q\ge2\). This exact prefactor and polynomial correction describe the vanishing-zealot-density regime beyond the previously reported qualitative exponential switching behavior.
