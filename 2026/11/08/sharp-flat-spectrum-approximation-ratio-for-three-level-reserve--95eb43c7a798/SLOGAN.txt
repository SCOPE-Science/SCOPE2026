For every three-level integer-quantization palette, the reserve-greedy allocator has a sharp asymptotic worst-budget storage ratio attained when the budget exactly permits uniform middle precision. That ratio grows linearly with the highest available bitwidth, even for equal singular values; with widths \(8,12,32\), a rank-\(64\) example uses \(1676\) rather than the optimal \(768\) component bits while satisfying the same certified error budget.
