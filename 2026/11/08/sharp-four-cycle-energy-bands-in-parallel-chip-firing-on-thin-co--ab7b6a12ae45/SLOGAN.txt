On every labelled \(K_{2,b}\), the complete chip-total generating polynomial for primitive four-cycles of synchronous chip-firing is \(4z^{2b}S_b(z)+4z^{5b-2}S_b(z^{-1})\), where \((1-z)^2S_b(z)=2^b(1+z^b)-2(1+z)^b\). Four-cycles occur precisely in two disjoint mass bands and attain both excluded endpoints of the universal period-two middle interval, with exactly \(4(2^b-2)\) periodic configurations at each endpoint.
