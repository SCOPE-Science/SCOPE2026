If a subset of the integer lattice has upper Banach density strictly above \(4/9\), every multiple of \(4\) is the coordinate product of one of its difference vectors. The \(4/9\) threshold and, for all density cutoffs up to \(1/2\), the universal modulus \(4\) are sharp: a nine-residue obstruction and a checkerboard obstruction prove the two boundaries.
