For Paley tournaments of prime-power order \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), the sharp orders at which deleting any \(t\) vertices still preserves unrestricted counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{4}\) equivalence to the random tournament are \(19,27,43,47,59\) for \(t=0,1,2,3,4\), respectively. Exact finite character-pattern counts and a uniform Hasse-bound argument prove that each threshold is sharp.
