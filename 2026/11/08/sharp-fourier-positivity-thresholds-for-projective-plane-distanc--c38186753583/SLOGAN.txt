For every finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\), the point–line distance hypergroup has powered-distance negative type exactly for \(0<\alpha\le\beta_q\), where \(3^{\beta_q}-1=2^{\beta_q}/\sqrt q\). At this sharp Fourier-positivity threshold the least Euclidean embedding dimension of the metric \(\delta^{\alpha/2}\) drops from \(2(q^2+q+1)-1\) to \(q^2+q+1\).
