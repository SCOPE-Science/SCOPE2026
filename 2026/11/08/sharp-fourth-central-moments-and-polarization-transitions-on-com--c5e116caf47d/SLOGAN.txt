The exact fourth centered moment of a real one-Lipschitz function on a connected complete multipartite graph depends only on the total number of vertices and the size of its largest part, with all maximizers either binary or polarized inside a largest part. In the large-graph limit, the optimal geometry changes at largest-part densities \(1/12\) and \(2/3\), and the finite polarization transition has a sharp parity-dependent correction.
