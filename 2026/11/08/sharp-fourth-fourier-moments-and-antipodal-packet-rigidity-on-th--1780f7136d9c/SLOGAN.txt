On the modular hyperbola over \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\), the sharp unnormalized-coefficient fourth Fourier moment is \(\frac{3p}{2}\|a\|_2^4\). Every nonzero optimizer is supported on exactly two opposite residue classes and is a pair of equal-power, common-frequency additive-character packets; the theorem also gives an explicit nonnegative defect for mass imbalance and competing pairs.
