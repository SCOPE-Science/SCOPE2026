For every positive Gamma shape, the complete two-dimensional space of real centered quadratic polynomials has an exact attained fourth-moment interval whose unique lower and upper extremizers are opposite-sign mixtures of the first two nonconstant Laguerre modes. A positive sum-of-squares identity forces the underlying stationary quartic to have exactly two real roots for every shape, and the sharp constants tend to \(3\) and \(15\) in the Gaussian large-shape limit.
