For the real matrix coefficients of uniform signed permutations, the exact normalized fourth moment ranges from \(1\) to \(\max\{4,n\}\), with all equality cases classified. Upper extremizers switch from equal-weight complete matchings to arbitrary partial matchings at \(n=4\), then to single entries; the resulting real Banach--Mazur distance to Euclidean space is exactly \(\max\{4,n\}^{1/4}\).
