For the full real sixteen-dimensional matrix-coefficient space on \(\mathrm{SO}(4)\), the sharp normalized fourth moment lies in \([2,4]\), with two inequivalent lower extremizer families: rank-one matrices and orientation-reversing conformal matrices. An exact three-square defect and complete equality classification imply optimal Euclidean Banach--Mazur distortion \(2^{1/4}\).
