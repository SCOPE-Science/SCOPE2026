For rank-one standard-representation matrix coefficients \(\sum_i a_i b_{\pi(i)}\) on a uniform random permutation, the sharp normalized fourth moment is \(1,3/2,3\) in dimensions \(2,3,4\), and \((n^2-3n+3)/(n-1)\) for every \(n\ge5\). All extremizers are classified: every pair is extremal in dimensions two and three, the dimension-four optimizers have balanced \((1,1,-1,-1)\) coefficients, and all higher-dimensional optimizers have one exceptional coordinate in each coefficient vector.
