# Review: sharp two-sided-exponential Gabor frame bounds

**Correctness — PASS.** The integer-oversampling Zak formula is applied with an explicit normalization. A residue-class geometric sum reduces the fiber multiplier to a rational expression in \(y=1+r^{2q}-2r^q\cos(2\pi q\omega)\) and \(U(x)=H_q(r^{2x}+r^{2-2x})\). Strict negativity of its derivative in \(y\), convexity in \(x\), and the q-term AM–GM inequality establish the global bounds, exact optimizers and positivity for all \(a>0\), \(q\ge2\). The independent direct-Zak finite test covers interior and extremal evaluations; it is corroboration, not the infinity proof.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence, with substantial residual access risk.** Pont and collaborators (arXiv:2608.04992v1) prove the qualitative frame set for all continuous totally positive windows; this does not supply the exact sharp constants. Kloos and Stöckler (arXiv:1311.7359v1, Section 5) obtain nonoptimal explicit lower estimates for this window, not the all-q sharp pair. Relevant indexed findings concern other windows or a translation-sampling operator. A.J.E.M. Janssen's 1996 integer-redundancy frame-bound paper is a **material plausible overlapping source** whose full text could not be accessed; no claim of noncoverage or certified priority is made for it. The metadata retains this limitation.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Optimal lower and upper spectral constants, exact extremal fibers, and the explicit \(q\to\infty\) condition-number limit \(\cosh a\) are concrete stability information absent from the inspected qualitative frame-set theorem. This result does not claim a new yes/no frame-set classification or solve noninteger redundancies.

**Limitations.** Only symmetric unnormalized two-sided exponentials at unit translations and integer modulation redundancy; no independent audit or exhaustive old-literature certification. Full 1996 Janssen source was inaccessible.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
