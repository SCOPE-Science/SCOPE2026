For every two-sided exponential window \(g_a(t)=e^{-a|t|}\) and every integer modulation redundancy \(q\ge2\), both **optimal** rectangular Gabor frame bounds are explicit elementary rational functions of \(e^{-a}\). Their spatial-frequency extremizers are exact Zak-cell corners, and their sharp condition number converges to \(\cosh a\) at high redundancy; earlier qualitative frame-set results do not determine these constants.
