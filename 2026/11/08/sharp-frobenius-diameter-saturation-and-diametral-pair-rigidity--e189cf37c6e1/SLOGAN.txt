For \(s=\min(m,n)\), the Frobenius diameter of a rectangular partial alternating-sign-matrix polytope attains its sharp squared bound \(2s(2s-1)\) **exactly** when the longer side has at least \(2s\) positions. In that regime its complete set of diameter-realizing pairs consists of exactly \(\binom{\max(m,n)}{2s}\) uniquely staggered alternating-diamond pairs; below the threshold the bound is strict.
