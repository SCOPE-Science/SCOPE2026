For every right regular \(n\)-gonal pyramid with \(n\ge5\), the chance that a uniformly oriented planar shadow retains all \(n+1\) vertices has an exact arcsine formula and a unique height maximizer. At the maximizing height, this rare-event probability is asymptotic to \(2\pi^2/(3\sqrt3\,n^2)\), while the optimal height approaches \(1/\sqrt2\) times the base circumradius.
