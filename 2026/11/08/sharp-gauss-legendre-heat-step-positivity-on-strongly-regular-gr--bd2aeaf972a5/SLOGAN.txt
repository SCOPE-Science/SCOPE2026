For the two-stage Gauss–Legendre heat step on every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph, the entire entrywise-positivity set is determined exactly by the single parameter \(\Delta=v-2k+\lambda-\mu\): all timesteps are safe if \(\Delta\le0\), and otherwise the sharp cutoff is \((3+\sqrt{9+12\Delta/\mu})/\Delta\). This separates infinite positivity-safe Paley families from finite-threshold Petersen and triangular graphs, even though every step has positive, contractive eigenvalues.
