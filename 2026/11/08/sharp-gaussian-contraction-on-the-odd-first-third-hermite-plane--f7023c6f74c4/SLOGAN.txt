The Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck operator on the real odd Hermite plane \(\operatorname{span}\{H_1,H_3\}\) has an exact \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) norm with a unique mixed-mode extremizing line for every positive semigroup parameter. Its sharp contraction radius is \(\rho_*=0.6434001437581967\ldots\), strictly larger than the unrestricted Nelson radius \(1/\sqrt3\).
