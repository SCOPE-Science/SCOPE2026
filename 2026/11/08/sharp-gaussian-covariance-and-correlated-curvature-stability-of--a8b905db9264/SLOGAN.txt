For full-noise random-coordinate Langevin sampling of quadratic Gaussian targets, equal Hessian diagonals give the exact invariant covariance \(H^{-1}/(1-dah/2)\) and a sharp step bound \(h<2/(da)\), independent of all correlations. In two dimensions, off-diagonal curvature strictly enlarges the sharp mean-square stability boundary when the diagonal curvatures differ, with an optimal but unattained multiplicative gain \((1+\sqrt2)/2\).
