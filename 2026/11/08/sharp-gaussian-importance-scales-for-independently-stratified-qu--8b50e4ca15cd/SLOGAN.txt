For independently jittered equal-probability strata estimating the standard Gaussian quadratic moment with a centered Gaussian importance proposal, finite \(N^3\)-normalized variance occurs exactly when the proposal variance \(s>3/2\). The unique variance-optimal asymptotic proposal is \(s=3.77107268073615\ldots\), not the ordinary Monte Carlo optimum \(s=3\), and its exact leading coefficient is \(6.56138232719992\ldots\).
