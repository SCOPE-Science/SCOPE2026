For every power-logarithmic Fock weight \(e^{-(\log_+|z|)^\alpha}\) with \(\alpha>1\), the sharp local variation of radial Toeplitz eigenvalues follows an explicit Gaussian crossover at degree width \(m^{(\alpha-2)/(2(\alpha-1))}\). A single smooth bounded radial symbol attains the maximal possible oscillation across degree pairs whose square-root distance tends to zero.
