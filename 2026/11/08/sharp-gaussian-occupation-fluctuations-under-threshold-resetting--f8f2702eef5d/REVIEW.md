# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The object is uniquely specified by Brownian time change and instantaneous threshold resets. A killed Brownian Green integral gives the exact excursion mean; a piecewise explicit Dirichlet solution has continuous derivative and zero at the reset site. Stopped Itô isometry yields the centered occupation-reward variance. Bounded-domain exit exponential moments justify the renewal-reward CLT, convergence of scaled second moments, and the fixed-parameter domain including \(\alpha=0\). Exact rational integration and an independently discretized killed chain corroborate nonstandard constants. Residual risk: this is continuous-scale physics, not a finite-lattice identity.

## Originality
PASS, with priority qualification. The full primary article arXiv:2610.21962v1, especially Section 6.1.2, proves the threshold occupation mean and qualitative \(T^{-1/2}\) concentration but not the exact threshold fluctuation coefficient. Its Poisson formula is credited, as are the renewal-reward theorem and Brownian Poisson-equation method. Prior Poisson Brownian and run-and-tumble occupation results do not contain this position-threshold variance. An accessible earlier stationary radial-order comparison does not imply temporal fluctuation coefficients. Residual risk: a broad, unindexed general stopped-diffusion fluctuation theorem may subsume the general quadrature, but no checked source established the specific rational equal-frequency ratio or its strict monotonicity.

## Value
PASS. The source identifies a deterministic occupation fraction without its resolution at finite observation time, even while displaying concentration histograms. The exact Gaussian variance determines that resolution and supplies a parameter-free matched-reset-frequency efficiency comparison between event-triggered and memoryless resets. It is a motivated, nontrivial dynamical observable, not a re-expression of stationarity. The ratio separates a factor-three reduction at ordinary diffusion from a one-quarter reduction in the large-sluggishness limit, without assuming equal reset costs.

## Closest literature and scientific limitations
Frimpong–Bresque–Roldán (arXiv:2610.21962v1), Sections 6.1.1–6.1.2; den Hollander–Majumdar–Meylahn–Touchette (arXiv:1801.09909), Sections II and IV; Bressloff (arXiv:2009.01968); Biswas–Majumdar–Pal (arXiv:2603.25338). The new central limit statement is for the continuum time-change realization with immediate threshold resets. The strict two-protocol variance ratio is established only when both protocols reset to the symmetry center and reset frequencies are matched by their mean event rates. Independent external priority and proof validation have not been performed.
