For sluggish diffusion reset at spatial thresholds, the long-time positive-occupation fraction satisfies a Gaussian limit with an exact variance coefficient for every interior reset location. When resetting occurs at the origin, threshold-triggered resets yield only \(\frac{3(\alpha+1)(\alpha+2)}{2(\alpha+3)(2\alpha+3)}\) times the occupation variance of Poisson resets at the same average reset frequency, increasing strictly from \(1/3\) to \(3/4\) as sluggishness grows.
