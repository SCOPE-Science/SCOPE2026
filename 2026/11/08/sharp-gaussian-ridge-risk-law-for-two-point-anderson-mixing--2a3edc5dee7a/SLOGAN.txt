For scalar two-point Anderson mixing with independent Gaussian evaluation noise, unregularized extrapolation has Cauchy-type tails and infinite mean-square risk, whereas centered ridge regularization has an exact \(C\lambda^{-1/2}\) risk singularity as \(\lambda\downarrow0\). For a nonzero midpoint offset, the sharp fourth-order small-noise ridge coefficient among \(\lambda=c\sigma^2\) is \(c_*=[rd^2+(12r+4)\theta^2]/(2r^2\theta^2)\).
