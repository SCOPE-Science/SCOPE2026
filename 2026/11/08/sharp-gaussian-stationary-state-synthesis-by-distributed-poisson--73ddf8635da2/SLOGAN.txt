A prescribed nondegenerate Gaussian can be synthesized as the exact stationary law of an anisotropic harmonically confined Brownian particle by random-position Poisson resetting precisely when its symmetrized covariance defect is positive semidefinite and its displaced drift lies in the defect's range; the sharp reset frequency is \(r_*=\operatorname{tr}K-D\operatorname{tr}\Sigma^{-1}+\tfrac14(Km)^\top B^\dagger(Km)\). Covariance greater than thermal equilibrium does not suffice when confinement is anisotropic: an explicit two-dimensional counterexample is impossible at every rate.
