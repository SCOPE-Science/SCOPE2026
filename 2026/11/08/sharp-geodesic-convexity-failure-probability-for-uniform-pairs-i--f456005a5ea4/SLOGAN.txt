For two independent area-uniform points of a spherical cap, the probability that their minimizing geodesic exits the cap is exactly \(\frac12t(1-t)^2\) for large caps, where \(t=\tan((\pi-\rho)/2)\); it is zero for caps no larger than a hemisphere. The unique worst cap has radius \(\pi-2\arctan(1/3)\), with sharp failure probability \(2/27\).
