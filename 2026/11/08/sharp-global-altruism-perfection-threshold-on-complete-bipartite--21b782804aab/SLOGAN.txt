For global sum-based equal-treatment altruism on a complete bipartite friendship network \(K_{a,b}\), the grand coalition is perfect exactly when the friend-to-enemy reward ratio reaches \(\max\{1,2(a-1)/(b+1),2(b-1)/(a+1)\}\), except that a single friendship edge needs no positive reward. This gives an exact imbalance-sensitive threshold, a two-block certificate for every failure, and perfection at the original reward normalization.
