In the two-clock sine phase map, the antiphase state attracts every non-in-phase initial condition all the way through the sharp flip endpoint \(h=1\), where convergence is algebraic rather than exponential. For critical orbits that do not reach antiphase in finitely many steps, the inverse-square phase error has the exact expansion \(\frac23 n+\frac9{20}\log n+C+o(1)\); just below the endpoint its fixed-time crossover is \((e^{4t}-1)/6\).
