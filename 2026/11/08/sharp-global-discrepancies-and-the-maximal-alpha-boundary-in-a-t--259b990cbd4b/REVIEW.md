**PASS.** Wu's integral difference-coordinate lattice and primitive-ray polytope identify the toric discrepancy support function with \(h(d)=\max\{\max_i d_i/B,-\min_i d_i/A\}\). Any nonzero zero-sum integer vector has a positive coordinate, while the primitive vector \((1,-1,0,\ldots,0)\) attains the lower bound. The standard Fano-polytope criteria reduce the canonical and terminal classifications to exactly the checked integer-point geometry. The endpoint family is admissible and coprime for every indicated parameter. A finite exact checker independently enumerated 27 parameter triples; finite enumeration is not used for the all-parameter proof.

General toric discrepancy and Fano-polytope criteria are acknowledged as prior theorems; the parameter-specific calculation is the present additional result. Literature and research-record searches for these formulas and the alpha/mld limit located no dominating prior claim. A differently phrased toric singularity computation remains a residual priority risk.

**PASS.** Wu's family realizes the entire rational alpha spectrum of K-polystable toric Fanos.
