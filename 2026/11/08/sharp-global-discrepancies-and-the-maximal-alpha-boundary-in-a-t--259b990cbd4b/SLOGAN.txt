For Wu's K-polystable toric \(\mathbf Q\)-Fano realization of \(\alpha=B/(A+B)\), the global minimal log discrepancy is exactly \(1/B\); the members are canonical precisely when \(B=1\) and terminal only in the hexagonal surface case. Taking \(A=B+1\) makes alpha approach \(1/2\) while the global discrepancy approaches zero in every fixed dimension at least two.
