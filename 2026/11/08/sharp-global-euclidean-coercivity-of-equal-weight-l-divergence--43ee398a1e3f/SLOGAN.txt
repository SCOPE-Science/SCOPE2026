The equal-weight geometric-mean L-divergence globally dominates \((2/n)\|q-p\|_2^2\) on the entire open \(n\)-asset simplex, with the best possible coefficient \(2/n\). Thus the equal-weight portfolio converts a uniform lower rate \(\rho\) of squared market-weight movement into at least \((2/n)\rho\) long-run worst-case relative growth, independently of how close the compact admissible market state set lies to the simplex boundary.
