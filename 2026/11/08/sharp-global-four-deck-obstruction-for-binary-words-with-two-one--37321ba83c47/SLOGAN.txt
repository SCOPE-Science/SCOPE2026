A sharp first obstruction to unrestricted reconstruction of binary words having at most two runs of ones appears at length \(14\): exactly one cross-class four-deck pair exists, \(01111100111110\) and \(10101111110101\), and none occurs at shorter eligible lengths. It follows that the published upper bound is sharp, \(r(\mathcal B_2)=5\), with cross-class collisions at every longer length.
