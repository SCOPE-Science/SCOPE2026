On a convex quadratic, gradient-scaled CS-SAM has an exact global step threshold \(\gamma=2/(1+\rho)\): below it every trajectory converges to the minimizer, at equality nonterminating amplitudes decay like \(1/k\), and above it every nonterminating orbit approaches a unique nonzero symmetric two-cycle. The positive gradient-dependent curvature scale prevents supercritical blowup but cannot prevent persistent optimization error.
