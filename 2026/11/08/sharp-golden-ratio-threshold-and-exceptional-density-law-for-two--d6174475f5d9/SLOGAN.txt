The published fractional-part test for two iterates of an irrational Beatty floor map is exact for every modulus \(\alpha\ge\varphi\), a sharp improvement of the known sufficient range \(\alpha>\varphi^2\). Below \(\varphi\), its omissions have exact natural density \((1-\alpha+\alpha^{-1})^2/(2\alpha)\) for rationally independent frequencies, while \(\sqrt2\) shows that exceptional algebraic moduli can have no omissions.
