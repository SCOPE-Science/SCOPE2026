For the angular-grid inscribed polygons of the planar \(\ell_p\) disk with \(1<p<2\), Euclidean Hausdorff error has the exact order \(n^{-p}\) but two different sharp leading constants: \((p-1)p^{-1-p/(p-1)}\) when \(4\mid n\), and \(1/(p2^p)\) otherwise, after scaling by \((n/(2\pi))^p\). The jump comes from whether a sampled edge straddles one of the four singular-curvature tips symmetrically.
