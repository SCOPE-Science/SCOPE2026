For the frozen-strategy two-state hyperedge game with transition probabilities \(p\) and \(p^2\), stationary environmental abundance at a fixed mean cooperator fraction lies between an exact group-size-dependent polygonal lower envelope and the mean itself. The minimizing group composition switches from adjacent low-occupancy groups to a half-full/full-group mixture, while the upper extreme requires fully segregated groups; heterogeneous composition can either improve or worsen abundance relative to homogeneous groups.
