# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The finite Cesàro multiplication gives \(E_{a,b}=a^{-1}(h_a+g_b)\) on the lower triangle, with \(0<h_a,g_a\le1/a\). The dyadic diagonal blocks have singular values bounded by \(C2^{-r}/j\) using the exact cumulative-sum matrix spectrum; cross-dyadic blocks split into rank-one pieces summable in each positive Schatten quasi-ideal. This yields the uniform quadratic upper bound. The exact first-difference inverse of every consecutive \(n\)-by-\(n\) compression, together with a uniform Neumann-series estimate, yields \(s_n(E)\ge(8e n^2)^{-1}\). Quantifiers hold for all positive integers, and the half-Schatten endpoint follows from the two-sided bound. Finite checker tests support, but do not replace, the analytical proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge assessment)
Bellavita–Stylogiannis arXiv:2609.09453v1 proves a general \(\mathcal S_2\) defect and asks about Schatten refinement. The existing published-finding corpus note on the same \(C^2-W_2\) already proves trace-class membership and the zeta trace; those results are explicitly excluded from the new contribution. Its proof does not give the sharp \(n^{-2}\) singular-value scale or classify \(0<p<1\). Full relevant manuscript material was read and compared by implication, not by title alone. Rhaly matrix Schatten results concern row-constant matrices and \(q>1\), not this column-dependent defect. A different Cesàro/shift-power commutator result also has quadratic singular-number decay but is a different operator, and no implication to \(E\) was established. The remaining priority risk is an older equivalent theorem in discrete Hardy/Volterra approximation-number literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The canonical square defect is the first nontrivial lattice error in a new multiplicative calculus. The exact quasi-Schatten threshold distinguishes trace-class membership from finer operator-ideal regularity and supplies the optimal singular-value order for every rank. Neither a routine trace recomputation nor a disconnected numerical example, it answers a natural quantitative gap highlighted by the initiating manuscript and the existing trace-class refinement.

## Relationship to literature and limitations
The full general discrete Mellin paper supplies the definition of the defect and a weaker \(\mathcal S_2\) theorem; the previous Cesàro power note supplies \(\mathcal S_1\) and \(\zeta(2)\), with no claim below exponent one. No exact leading coefficient \(\lim n^2s_n(E)\) is asserted, and a historical publication-priority risk remains.
