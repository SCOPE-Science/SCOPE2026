# Scientific clarification of the final result

The final result is the **two-sided pointwise singular-number order** for the canonical square Cesàro–Mellin defect. The quasi-Schatten membership cutoff \(p>1/2\), weak \(\mathcal S_{1/2}\) upper bound, and pointwise \(O(n^{-2})\) upper bound were previously established in published findings (October 25 and November 1, 2026); they are not offered as novel here. The reviewed surviving result is \(s_n(E)\ge(8e)^{-1}n^{-2}\) for **every** \(n\).

**Correctness.** Exact harmonic-number factorization gives diagonal dyadic upper decay and rapidly Schatten-summable cross-block terms. An exact first-difference identity on every consecutive \(n\)-block has a strictly triangular correction \(K\) whose powers satisfy \(\|K^j\|\le1/j!\); hence the block inverse has norm at most \(8e n^2\). Compression yields the all-rank lower bound. The included finite checker corroborates only finite identities, not this infinite theorem.

**Originality.** Full earlier findings for this exact operator were read. Both establish membership; the November 1 result also derives \(s_n=O(n^{-2})\), but neither gives \(s_n\ge c n^{-2}\) pointwise. Their divergent eigenvalue sums give no such lower bound for each ordered singular value. An earlier general triangular approximation-number theorem remains a possible priority risk.

**Value.** The uniform lower bound is a natural exact spectral invariant of the canonical sampled-multiplication error. It establishes a noncollapse property at every rank and closes the gap between the known upper singular-number scale and an optimal two-sided order without claiming an asymptotic coefficient or a general Mellin calculus.

This correction supersedes the earlier self-review's novelty characterization of the half-Schatten threshold; the historical evidence remains available in repository history and in the existing review records.
