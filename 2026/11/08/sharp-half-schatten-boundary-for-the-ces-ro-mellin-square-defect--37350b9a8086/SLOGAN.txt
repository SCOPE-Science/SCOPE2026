For the canonical square defect \(E=C^2-W_2\) of the discrete Cesàro–Mellin calculus, every singular value obeys a two-sided pointwise bound \(c n^{-2}\le s_n(E)\le Cn^{-2}\). The already-published half-Schatten threshold and upper decay rate are credited; the additional all-rank lower estimate follows from an explicit uniformly controlled inverse of consecutive compressions.
