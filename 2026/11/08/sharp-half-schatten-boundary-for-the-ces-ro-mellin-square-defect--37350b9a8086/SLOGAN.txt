The square defect \(E=C^2-W(\omega\star\omega)\) of the sampled Mellin calculus has singular values of exact order \(n^{-2}\). Thus its optimal quasi-Schatten threshold is \(p>1/2\), with membership in weak \(\mathcal S_{1/2}\) but not strong \(\mathcal S_{1/2}\), a sharp refinement beyond the previously known trace-class property.
