For alternating modal formulas \(\alpha_k=(\Box\Diamond)^k\top\) on an arbitrary finite \(n\)-world frame, all truth sets stabilize exactly by the sharp worst-case index \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). A world first lost at stage \(t\) forces a simple directed path of \(2t\) distinct worlds ending at a dead end, so consecutive iterates \(\alpha_k\) and \(\alpha_{k+1}\) first separate on precisely \(2k+2\) worlds.
