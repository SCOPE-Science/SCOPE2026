For a binary orbit with one \(1\) every \(q\) steps, the closed Hausdorff ball specified by a length-\(m\) observation has a unique entropy-maximizing subshift: the minimum-gap shift until \(m\ge q\), when the ball collapses to the orbit itself. Its entropy, dynamical zeta function, and sharp isolation distance are explicit, with a pre-isolation entropy asymptotic to \(W(q)/q\).
