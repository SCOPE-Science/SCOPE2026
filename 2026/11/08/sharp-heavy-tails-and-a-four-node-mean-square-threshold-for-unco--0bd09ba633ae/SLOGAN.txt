The unconstrained minimum-norm affine-exact quadrature weights from \(m\) iid nodes have a sharp Pareto norm tail of exponent \(m-1\), with constant \(\sqrt m\,V_{m-1}\int_0^1|u-1/2|^{m-1}\rho(u)^m\,du\). Independent evaluation noise gives infinite unconditional mean-square quadrature error at two or three nodes but finite error for every \(m\ge4\), even though the moment equations are almost surely nonsingular at every sample size.
