With exactly \(h\) hypothesis atoms, a minimal obstruction to uniform Horn-abductive explanation has at most \(\binom{h}{\lfloor h/2\rfloor}+\binom{h}{\lfloor h/2\rfloor+1}\) contexts, and acyclic Horn instances attain this bound for every \(h\). The sharp parameter is the two-middle-level size of the Boolean hypothesis lattice, not its total \(2^h\) size.
