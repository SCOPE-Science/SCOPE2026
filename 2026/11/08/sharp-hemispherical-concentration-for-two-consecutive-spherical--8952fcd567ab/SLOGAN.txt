For each integer \(\ell\ge1\), the sharp hemispherical concentration of a two-consecutive-degree spherical-harmonic packet is achieved only by highest-order Gaussian-beam combinations; at \(\ell=0\), it is achieved by the constant-plus-zonal-dipole combination. The exact sharp energy fraction is \(\frac12+\frac{(2\ell+1)\sqrt{2\ell+3}}{4(\ell+1)}\binom{2\ell}{\ell}4^{-\ell}\), and the full binomial spectrum yields a strictly decreasing sharp fraction converging to \(\frac12+1/\sqrt{2\pi}\), not one.
