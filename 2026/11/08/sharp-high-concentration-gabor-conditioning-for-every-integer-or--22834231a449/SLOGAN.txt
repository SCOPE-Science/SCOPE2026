For every fixed integer-order Matérn Gabor window normalized at the origin, redundancy-two frame conditioning deteriorates at a sharp, order-dependent rate: the optimal lower bound is asymptotic to \(4((m-1)!/(2m-2)!)^2\lambda^{2m-2}e^{-\lambda}\), while the upper bound tends to \(2\). Worst-conditioned Zak fibers concentrate at the midpoint between adjacent time samples.
