For every fixed support radius \(n\ge2\), the sharp norm of the \(k\)-th discrete derivative after optimal symmetric averaging is \(2^k(2/k)^n(c_n+o(1))\), where \(c_n\) is an exponential-weighted monic Chebyshev constant; requiring a nonnegative Fourier symbol strictly increases this constant. Both optimizer families converge to the centered binomial kernel, and the first new five-tap unrestricted constant is \(c_2=0.216915471256167\ldots\), with sharp positivity penalty ratio \(1.42991186614918\ldots\).
