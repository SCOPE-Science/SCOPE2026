For every integer \(m\ge1\), the \(m\)th raw first-passage moment in any finite absorbing Markov jump network has sharp common-source logarithmic rate sensitivity bounded by \(m\) in absolute value, and the individual-edge elasticities sum to \(-m\). Under independent state-clock changes, its logarithm is globally convex with an explicit nonnegative covariance Hessian.
