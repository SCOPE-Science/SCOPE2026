For the homogeneous tent-map recurrence \(x_{n+1}=T(x_{\lfloor n/k\rfloor})\) with integer \(k\ge2\), physical-time Cesàro averages converge only for the countably many seeds eventually hitting fixed points. For Lebesgue-almost every initial point their *complete* limit set is the exact interval \([0,2k/(2k+1)]\), despite the undelayed tent orbit having ordinary time average \(1/2\).
