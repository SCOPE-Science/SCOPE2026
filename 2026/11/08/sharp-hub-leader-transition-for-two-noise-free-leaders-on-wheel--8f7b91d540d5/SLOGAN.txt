For two noise-free leaders on the unweighted wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), maximally separated rim leaders are optimal when \(4\le m\le6\), but the unique optimal configuration type becomes hub plus rim at \(m\ge7\); all pairs tie at \(m=3\). A Schur-complement formula and a tridiagonal Green-function bound prove the sharp transition for every wheel size.
