Among rotational switching itineraries of prime period, adjacent-switch frequency determines the entire cyclic itinerary up to time reversal exactly at balanced symbol occupancy; away from balance, the ambiguity classes have an explicit arithmetic size. Under independent phase resampling and bit-flip noise, optimal recovery from adjacent disagreements requires and achieves order \(p^2(1-2\varepsilon)^{-4}\log(1/\delta)\) samples, for the specified nondegenerate noise range.
