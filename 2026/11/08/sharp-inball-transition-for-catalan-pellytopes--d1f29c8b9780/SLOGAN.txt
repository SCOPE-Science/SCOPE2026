For the canonical Newton-polytope realization of every Catalan pellytope with \(n\ge d\ge3\), the exact Euclidean inradius is \(\min\{d/2,\ d(2n-d+1)/(2(2+(n-1)\sqrt2))\}\). A sharp integer-parameter transition changes its incenter from a unique point with \(n+1\) tangent facets to an \((n-2)\)-dimensional family admitting centers with only four tangent facets.
