For each singular radial power \(-1<p<0\), centered Euclidean rectangles regain strict inclusion monotonicity of weighted boundary perimeter exactly within a sharp eccentricity wedge \(\max\{a/b,b/a\}\le R_p\), where \(R_p\) is the unique root of an explicit one-dimensional integral equation. The threshold increases smoothly from \(1\) to infinity as \(p\) increases from \(-1\) to \(0\), giving a complete sharp two-parameter monotonicity phase diagram.
