For the unsmoothed two-well spectral loss, zero-diagonal symmetric systems have an exact inertia-imbalance floor under diagonal congruence; positively weighted complete graphs attain its unique optimal diagonal scaling with normalized loss \(((n-2)/n)^2\). One explicit rank-one augmentation of that factor makes every preconditioned eigenvalue exactly \(+1\) or \(-1\), showing a sharp factor-support obstruction rather than a universal impossibility of bimodal clustering.
