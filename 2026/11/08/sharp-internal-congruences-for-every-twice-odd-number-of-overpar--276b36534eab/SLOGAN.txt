For every number of colors \(m\equiv2\pmod4\), the difference of successive dyadic subsequences of overpartition counts into odd parts is exactly \(2^{3i-4}\) modulo \(2^{3i-3}\) at odd-square indices and zero modulo \(2^{3i-3}\) elsewhere, for every \(i\geq3\). This gives a sharp infinite family and proves the even-color valuation-growth conjecture in the entire twice-odd case.
