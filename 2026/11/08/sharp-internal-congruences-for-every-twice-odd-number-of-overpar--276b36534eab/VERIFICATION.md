The analytic proof is given in RESULT.md and requires no finite-bound hypothesis. Run `python3 verify.py` using Python 3's standard library to replay direct coefficient and integral Chebyshev-operator identities. `verify_output.txt` is the captured output for this package.

The verifier expands the original infinite Euler product only through a finite degree and uses exact integer arithmetic. Finite numerical comparisons cannot prove the universal congruence, cannot establish historical originality, and are not independent mathematical certification. The high-level theta duplication and Chebyshev identities are also explained explicitly in the analytic proof.
