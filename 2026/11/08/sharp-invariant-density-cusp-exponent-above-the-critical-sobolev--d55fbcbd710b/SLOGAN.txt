For the expanding two-branch cusp family, every \(-1/2<\beta<0\) forces its invariant density to vanish at the cusp image like an explicit nonzero multiple of \((1-x)^{-\beta/(1+\beta)}\). This proves that the published critical \(W^{2,1}\) threshold at \(\beta=-1/2\) is one-sided sharp for the actual stationary density, not merely for a proof estimate.
