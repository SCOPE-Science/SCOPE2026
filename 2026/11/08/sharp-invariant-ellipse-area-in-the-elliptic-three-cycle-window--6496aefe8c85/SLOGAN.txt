In the elliptic three-cycle window of a triangular toral shear, two explicit three-cycles carry six disjoint, exactly invariant ellipses whose combined area is \(\pi\eta(1-\eta)\sqrt{(3\eta-1)(4\eta^2-9\eta+3)}/[\sqrt3(3\eta^2-7\eta+3)]\). Each ellipse is the largest centered invariant-metric ellipse confined to its three-step affine itinerary, giving a sharp constructive area lower bound rather than the entire island area.
