The largest inscribed ellipsoid of a simplex stops changing strictly before the clipped vertex has been restored: for \(K_{n,h}=\{x\ge0,\sum x_i\le n,x_1\le h\}\), the exact saturation threshold is \(h=2n/(n+1)\). Below it all truncating and original facets touch, while above it the extra facet separates and the maximal ellipsoid stays fixed; its volume is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at the transition.
