For graphs consisting of \(n\) copies of one \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\), counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^k\) at unrestricted depth sees exactly the unordered side sizes and component count truncated at \(k\), for every \(k\ge3\). The sharp theory count is \(k^2(k-1)/2\); widths one and two each yield a single theory.
