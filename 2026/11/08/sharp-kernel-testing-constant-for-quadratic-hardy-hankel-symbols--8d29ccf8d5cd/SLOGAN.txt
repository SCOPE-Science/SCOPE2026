Among Hardy Hankel operators with analytic quadratic symbols \(\psi(z)=az+bz^2\), normalized Szegő-kernel testing loses at most the sharp factor \(3\sqrt3/5\) relative to the operator norm. Equality occurs exactly at \(|b|=\sqrt6\,|a|>0\), quantifying the worst failure of the strong reproducing kernel thesis in its lowest nontrivial polynomial degree.
