# Scientific review: kinetic–potential energy correlation under telegraph forcing

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns the uniquely stationary solution of an exponentially stable linear oscillator forced by a symmetric rate-\(\nu\) telegraph chain. The dimensionless generator recurrence is derived explicitly from the model, and its solutions at total polynomial degrees two and four give \(\mathcal C(k,g)=-gP_k(g)/[2(k+3)D_0^2D_9D_8]\). Every denominator factor is positive. The coefficients of the positive powers of \(g\) in \(P_k\) are positive, while its constant is \(-3(k-24)(3k+4)\); this proves the full unique-root/sign classification for all positive parameters. A separate rational generator solver checked 418 equalities and sign conditions. The critical slope and large-stiffness scaling follow from implicit differentiation and polynomial balancing. The additive-Gaussian statement follows from fourth-cumulant additivity and stationary zero position–velocity covariance. The proof, not the finite checks, supports the universal assertions.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit priority risks
The inspected full-text HTML of Dutta, Kundu, Sabhapandit and Basu (2024), arXiv:2406.06120, treats the exact oscillator and supplies general moment recurrences. Its displayed results focus on stationary second moments, temporal second-order correlations, and marginal position/velocity distributions. The cross-energy fourth cumulant and sharp boundary \(k=24\), unique flip-rate crossover and scaling are not supplied as statements in those inspected relevant sections. Thus the recurrence method itself is prior work, while this sign theorem is a distinct implication requiring fourth-order elimination and global sign analysis. A marginal position-kurtosis calculation for the same model concerns \(\kappa_4(X,X,X,X)\), not \(\kappa_4(X,X,V,V)\); no general identity reduces the latter to the former. A Gaussian-driven oscillator has vanishing mixed fourth cumulant, so known Gaussian energy calculations do not determine this effect. The 1987 output-moment literature was accessible only at abstract level and remains a real risk for publication priority; search absence is not treated as uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The correlation between potential and kinetic energies is a natural measure of energy sharing in active inertial motion. The sharp damping-to-frequency threshold separates universal energy anticorrelation from a previously unclassified positive-to-negative covariance regime, and the transition is uniquely determined for each stiff trap. Gaussian additive forcing leaves the threshold unchanged even though it alters the marginal energy fluctuations. This is a global structural classification, not a single sample, normalization artifact, or marginal-moment recomputation.

## Scope and residual risks
The dynamics are linear, stationary, one-dimensional, with symmetric additive telegraph forcing. The theorem does not cover general dependent or non-Gaussian additives, nonlinear confinement, asymmetric telegraph transition rates, or transient energy exchange. The 2024 source's general recurrence makes the rational formula algorithmically obtainable and warrants a cautious novelty assessment. A complete publication-priority assessment would need the 1987 article's full text and any unindexed equivalent fourth-moment work.
