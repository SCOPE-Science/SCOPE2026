# Scientific review and limits of the corrected FCC-only theorem

## Correctness — evidence
The universal subset-sum diameter bound applies to every independent FCC lattice triple; its squared-length cutoff four leaves only the 12 roots and 6 axis vectors. Axis inclusion is incompatible with independence. The Gram determinant and three-vector sum force the unique allowed pattern {-1,-1,0}, with exactly 24 unordered frames of determinant magnitude two. Explicit minimum enclosing balls show the four diameter tetrahedra have radius one and the other two regular tetrahedra have radius √3/2. The lattice deep hole and general packet-to-covering inequality establish equality with the ordinary covering radius. The original exact-rational finite checker corroborates the bounded classification but is not the infinite-frame proof.

## Originality — best-of-knowledge evidence
The complete BCC component of the original combined result was already established by the independently published BCC first-two-level Kuhn-packet theorem and is withdrawn. This corrected claim concerns only the FCC root lattice and its 24 complete common-frame optimizers. Xue's original definition and covering-radius inequalities do not settle this FCC classification. Known Delaunay/Voronoi cells do not alone establish the all-frame minimax statement. The full text of Koca et al.'s crystallographic study was not inspected, so possible equivalent older frame enumerations remain a historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — evidence
The FCC lattice is the canonical three-dimensional root lattice. Original κ is stricter than the extended triangulation invariant, making equality κ=μ and all 24 optimizer frames a motivated natural exact classification. The proof excludes even arbitrary independent proper-sublattice frames and provides a sharp benchmark for the common-frame covering problem.

## Boundaries
No claim about the BCC dual is novel here. The proof is for the specified Euclidean ball and FCC lattice in dimension three, not arbitrary lattices. Earlier original package and check code are retained as evidence for reproducibility; the narrowed claim is the one assessed for independent publication.
