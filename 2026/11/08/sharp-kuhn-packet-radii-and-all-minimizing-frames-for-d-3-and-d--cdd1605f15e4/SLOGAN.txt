For the Euclidean ball, the original common-frame Kuhn-packet radii of the FCC lattice \(D_3\) and its BCC dual \(D_3^*\) equal their classical covering radii, respectively \(1\) and \(\sqrt5/4\). Exactly \(24\) and \(8\) unordered lattice frames attain these values, and every minimizing frame is a genuine lattice basis.
