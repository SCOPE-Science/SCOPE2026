For an \(n\)-bit rotating three-tap majority-feedback register, all trajectories reach a periodic rotating word within \(n\) clocks, but exactly \(\bigl(2^{n-2}+2+2\mathbf 1_{n\text{ odd}}\bigr)/3\) initial words require the final possible clock. The last-clock fraction tends to \(1/12\), so maximal settling time is not an exponentially exceptional event.
