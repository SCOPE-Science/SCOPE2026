For the fundamental pair \(e_2,e_d\) in the generalized chain polytopes attached to truncated Grassmannians, lattice-point Minkowski additivity holds exactly for \(d\le3\) and \((d,n)=(4,8)\). The first failure, at \((d,n)=(4,9)\), has exactly one missing lattice point, a six-cell double-star configuration that embeds in every larger failing grid.
