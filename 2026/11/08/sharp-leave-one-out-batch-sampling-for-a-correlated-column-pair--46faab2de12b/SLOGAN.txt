For a least-squares matrix with one correlated column pair, the unique worst-case-optimal randomized leave-one-out block sampler has omission weights \(1,1,1+\rho,\ldots,1+\rho\), whereas determinant-volume sampling uses \(1,1,1-\rho^2,\ldots,1-\rho^2\). As the two columns become collinear, volume sampling's sharp residual contraction approaches \(1\), while optimized sampling retains contraction approaching \(1/(n-1)\), with an exact all-iteration witness.
