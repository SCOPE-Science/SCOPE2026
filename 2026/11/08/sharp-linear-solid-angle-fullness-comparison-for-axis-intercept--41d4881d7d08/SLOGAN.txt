For every axis-intercept simplex \(\operatorname{conv}\{0,a_1e_1,\ldots,a_de_d\}\), the smallest vertex solid angle occurs exactly at the longest intercepts, and its ratio to diameter-normalized volume has an explicit sharp all-dimensional interval \((d,C_d]\) (upper endpoint attained only in dimension two). The sharp reverse linear constant \(C_d\) is approached by two long equal legs and the remaining legs shrinking, complementing the previously known universal inequality in the other direction.
