For unit-edge lollipop graphs built from a clique and a path with at least three new vertices, the maximum real one-Lipschitz variance has an exact two-regime formula and every maximizer has a straight tail. The first tie between fully polarized and partially mixed clique patterns occurs sharply at \(m=\ell(\ell+2)+3\), revealing a quadratic competition between clique size and pendant-path length.
