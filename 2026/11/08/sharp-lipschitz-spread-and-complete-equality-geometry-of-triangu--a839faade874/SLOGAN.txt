For every triangular graph \(J(n,2)\), the sharp variance of a real one-Lipschitz function is exactly \((n-2)/(2(n-1))\) for even \(n\), and \((n-2)(n+1)/(2n^2)\) for odd \(n\). Every maximizer assigns levels \(0,1,2\) to within-first-part, crossing, and within-second-part edges of a balanced partition of the underlying complete graph; the full equality family has an explicit exponentially growing count.
