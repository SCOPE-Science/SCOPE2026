The sharp squared norm of the centered Lipschitz-to-\(L^2\) embedding on every unit-edge diameter-three tree has a closed two-parameter formula and a complete extremizer classification. When the larger hub has more than twice as many leaves as the smaller hub, a sharp polarized-to-mixed transition occurs; among all trees of that diameter and fixed order, the unique worst case is the balanced double star, with a precise parity-corrected \(9/4\) envelope.
