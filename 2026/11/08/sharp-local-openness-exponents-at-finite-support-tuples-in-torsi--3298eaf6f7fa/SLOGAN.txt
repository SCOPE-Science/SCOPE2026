For every discrete abelian torsion group, the full \(k\)-fold \(\ell^1\) convolution map is locally open at every finitely supported input tuple, and its **sharp** local image-radius exponent is the maximum of \(1\) and the number of input factors vanishing at any common finite-subgroup Fourier character. Arbitrarily supported targets admit constructive nearby factorizations, so an obstruction to global openness in an infinite torsion group must occur at an infinite-support tuple.
