For the Davis–Yin Gross–Pitaevskii splitting on a homogeneous connected graph, the positive ground-state fixed point has an exact sharp local stability step determined by the largest graph Laplacian eigenvalue. With zero uniform killing, every nonconstant eigenmode instead reaches multiplier \(-1\) simultaneously at the graph-independent step \(\gamma=n/(2\beta)\); with positive killing, the top graph frequency destabilizes first.
