For the greedy order-two almost Golomb sequence, the normalized ratios \(a(n)/n\) admit an explicit continuum of phase-dependent empirical limit laws with rational cumulative distributions. Their phase-matched Kolmogorov discrepancy has sharp order \(\Theta((\log N)/N)\), driven by the exact number \(\lfloor\log_2(N-1)\rfloor+\lfloor\log_2 N\rfloor\) of ratios at least \(3/4\) among the first \(N\ge4\) terms.
