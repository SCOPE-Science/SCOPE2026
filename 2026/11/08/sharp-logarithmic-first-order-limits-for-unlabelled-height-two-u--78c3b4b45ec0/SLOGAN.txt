In a uniformly unlabelled finite unary structure satisfying \(f^3=f^2\), every first-order sentence obeys a zero-one law with error \(O(1/\log n)\), and this rate is sharp: the chance of having no singleton component is asymptotic to \(\pi^2/(6\log n)\). At the same scale, the multiplicities of any fixed collection of rooted component types converge jointly to independent exponential laws, with rates given by their sizes.
