For Stern's diatomic sequence, the difference between the numbers of values congruent to \(1\) and \(4\) modulo \(5\) has exact dyadic-prefix extrema satisfying \(\ell_{r+12}=\ell_r-4\) and \(u_{r+12}=u_r+4\) for all \(r\ge16\). Consequently its largest absolute discrepancy up to \(X\) is \(\frac13\log_2 X+O(1)\): the exceptional bounded phenomenon at modulus \(3\) does not persist at modulus \(5\).
