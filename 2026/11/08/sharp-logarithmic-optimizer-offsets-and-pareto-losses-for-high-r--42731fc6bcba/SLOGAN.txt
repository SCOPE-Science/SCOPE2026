For a normalized two-sided exponential Gabor window at integer redundancy \(n\to\infty\), the three optimizers of the lower bound, upper bound and condition number have respective time-spacing parameters \(na=2\log n+\log6\), \(2\log n+\log3\) and \(2\log n+\log4\), up to \(O((\log n)^2/n^2)\). Their sharp high-density loss profiles are distinct and strictly convex, so optimizing the condition number necessarily incurs two explicit positive spectral-bound regrets of order \((\log n)/n^2\) after redundancy normalization.
