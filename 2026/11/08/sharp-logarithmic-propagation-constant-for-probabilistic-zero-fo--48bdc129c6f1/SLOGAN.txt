For synchronous probabilistic zero forcing started at the common hub of \(m\) triangles, the saturation time divided by \(\log m\) converges in probability and in mean to \(1/\log(1+\sqrt2)\). The two-stage conversion of untouched to partially colored to fully colored triangles yields a Perron amplification factor \(1+\sqrt2\), sharpening the previously known logarithmic order bound.
