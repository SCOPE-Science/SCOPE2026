For fixed-step scalar least-squares SGD with Gaussian covariates persisting for geometric runs, the stationary probability of an error above \(x\) has the exact logarithmic decay coefficient \(-\log\rho\) at scale \(\log x/\log\log x\). Even though every positive error moment diverges, the leading extreme-error reliability law is determined solely by the covariate retention probability \(\rho\).
