For the three-coefficient cubic Kolmogorov flow on the unit sphere, the period near its heteroclinic boundary grows as \(\frac{a+b+c}{2abc}\log(1/H)\), and uniform surface-area sampling has an exact exponential long-period tail rate \(\frac{abc}{(a+b+c)\max\{a,b,c\}}\). The same rate is the sharp finiteness threshold for exponential moments of the original-time orbit period.
