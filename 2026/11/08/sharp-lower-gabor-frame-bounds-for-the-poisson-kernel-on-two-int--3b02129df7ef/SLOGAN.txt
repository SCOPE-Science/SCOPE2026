For the Poisson window \(g_w(t)=1/(t^2+w^2)\), two integer-modulation Gabor banks with translation grids \(\mathbb Z\) and \(\mathbb Z+\theta\) have an explicit *sharp* lower frame bound for every real offset. The bound is positive exactly for noninteger shifts, uniquely maximized by half-period staggering, and collapses quadratically near coincident grids with an exact coefficient.
