Among all consecutive patterns of a fixed length in a uniform random permutation, the decreasing pattern is uniquely most positively covariant with the major index, and the increasing pattern is uniquely most negatively covariant, for every admissible finite sample size. Both sharp covariance magnitudes equal \(\frac{(m-1)n[(n-m+1)(m-1)+2]}{4(m+1)m!}\), with an explicit endpoint-rank and descent-position formula for every other pattern.
