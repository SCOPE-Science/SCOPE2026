On a natural finite Fourier-slope subspace, the exact worst-case \(L^2\)-normalized error of median rank-one lattice integration is \(\sqrt{2/q}\), where \(q\) is the fewest sampled slope classes covering a strict majority. At every fixed odd prime, a smooth fixed integrand retains exactly a one-half chance of nonzero error regardless of the number of independent repetitions, while the realized adaptive worst-case stabilizes at \(2/\sqrt{p-1}\) with a sharp exponential rate.
