# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The exact disjoint first-place formula and exactly-one-duplicate-and-common-second formula are derived with the Mallows q-shuffle; sixteen independent exact permutation-enumeration checks reproduce both. The crucial intersection estimate conditions on all exposed first (and up to two second) positions, preserves agentwise independence of suffixes, and applies a bound to bottom choices in the lower half. The top-cycle identification explicitly invokes the published deterministic Theorem 2 only for \(n\ge5\). Fixed-\(q\) scope, normalizations and the singular uniform limit are separated. The analytic argument, rather than the small enumerations, proves the asymptotic statement.

## Originality (PASS, with residual literature risk)
Brandt et al. (arXiv:2602.14816v1, Theorem 2 and Appendix B) already classify deterministic exceptional top cycles; that classification is credited as an external premise, and the new claim is solely the exact probability of each top exception, asymptotic negligible overlap of top/bottom exceptions and sharp rare-event coefficient for Mallows preferences. He–Müller–Verstraaten (2023), Section 2, supplies standard Mallows normalization and symmetries, not this majority-graph probability. Searched related assignment, random ranking and stable matching results concern distinct rules or properties. The coefficient may be indirectly recoverable from unpublished probabilistic work; this is an honest priority risk, not a proof of global uniqueness.

## Value (PASS)
The initiating top-cycle theorem says that the top cycle often contains every assignment, but has no quantitative probability rate for a correlated natural preference prior. The new result establishes that the probability of a non-full top cycle is superexponentially small for each fixed nonuniform Mallows prior, compared with an exponentially small impartial-culture probability. It gives the exact sharp leading constant (including the one-duplicate-second-choice correction), a useful benchmark for testing decisiveness of majoritarian assignments under varying preference concentration.

## Scope and verification limits
No analysis of varying \(q=q_n\) or dependence across agents; no independent audit. The source's deterministic top-cycle theorem is not newly proved. The reproducibility script checks the finite algebra and its coefficients, not the full infinite mathematics.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
