For independent common-center Mallows–Kendall preferences over \(n\) houses, the probability that the majoritarian assignment top cycle omits even one assignment has the sharp factor \(\frac{3q^2+2q+1}{q(1+q)}\) times \(n!((1-q)/(1-q^n))^nq^{n(n-1)/2}\) at every fixed \(0<q<1\). Under uniform preferences, the exceptional probability is asymptotic to \(3n!/n^n\): fixed correlation changes the decay from exponential to superexponential in \(n\).
