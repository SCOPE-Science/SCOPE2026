For every positive binary Sinkhorn kernel, the exact global projective contraction factor of one row-then-column sweep is \(q=\eta^2/(1+\sqrt{1+\eta^2})^2\), where \(\eta^2=r(1-r)(R-1)^2/R\) and \(R\) is the kernel cross-odds ratio. The generic Birkhoff bound is attained only at a balanced row target, and comparing \(r(1-r)\) with the column imbalance \(s(1-s)\) chooses the sharper of the two sweep-order guarantees.
