For every even regular prism, the chance that a uniformly oriented central plane produces the maximal \((n+2)\)-gon has a closed arcsine law and a unique optimal height \(h_n=\sqrt{3\cos^2(\pi/n)-1}\) in circumradius units. The optimized chance decays as \(2\pi^2/(3\sqrt3\,n^2)\); the optimal square prism is a cube, but the optimal hexagonal prism is not uniform.
