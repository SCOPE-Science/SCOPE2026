For every two-pole metric graph with at least four positive-length parallel arms and fixed total length, the least possible arclength-uniform mean geodesic distance is a sharp infimum achieved only by collapsing one arm and balancing the others in the limit. The sharp normalized infimum is \((2m-3)/(4(m-1)^2)\); the equal-arm graph is not optimal, whereas three arms favor the equilateral graph.
