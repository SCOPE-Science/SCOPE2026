A single geodesic loop with \(k\) pendant arms of fixed total length minimizes its mean two-point distance uniquely when every arm is equal and the loop takes an explicitly determined positive fraction of length. The optimal pendant fraction is \(k(k-4+2\sqrt{k^2-2k+2})/(3k^2-8)\), increasing from \(1/5\) toward \(1\); the minimum falls as \(L/k+O(L/k^2)\).
