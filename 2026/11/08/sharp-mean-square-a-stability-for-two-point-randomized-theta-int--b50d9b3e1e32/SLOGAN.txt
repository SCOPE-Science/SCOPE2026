For independently randomized two-point theta integration on a scalar test equation, mean-square A-stability holds exactly when the probability of the sub-midpoint coefficient does not exceed \(p_*=(2b-1)a^2/[(1-2a)b^2+(2b-1)a^2]\), including equality. Every nonconstant coefficient law centered at \(1/2\) loses mean-square A-stability, with a positive fourth-order imaginary-axis defect and a sharp local weak-damping boundary.
