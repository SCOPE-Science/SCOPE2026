The proof starts from the two exact deck-orbit families for the quotient and the surjective Haar torus endomorphism. This gives two independent uniformly distributed half-period circular distances, independent of the vertical coordinate. The symmetry of the conditional survival integrand converts triangular vertical offsets to a uniform variable, proving the full CDF. The exact second-moment identity follows by integrating the minimum of two squared distances, and two globally valid inequalities on the derivative kernel establish the unique fixed-area minimum at \(a/b=\sqrt{5-\sqrt{15}}\). ## Mathematical value — PASS
This establishes an explicit full distribution and sharp global shape extremum for a natural infinite family of closed nonorientable flat metrics under intrinsic uniform pair sampling. Its optimizer is different from the square-looking presentation, illustrating that affine deck-group structure materially changes a natural stochastic geometry objective. A one-dimensional numerical minimum alone would not carry the result; the rigorously universal optimum, independence reduction, and general CDF do.

## Scope and remaining risks
The optimization applies to normalized squared distance, not the entire CDF in stochastic order or mean distance. The CDF is exact as a one-dimensional integral, not an elementary function in general. Older work might contain equivalent formulas; no independent mathematical or historical-priority review was conducted.
