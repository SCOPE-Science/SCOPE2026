For fixed intrinsic area, a truncated right circular cone has uniquely least mean-squared geodesic separation at total angle \(1.850746272506\ldots\), not at the planar disk. The exact cone-angle profile has three-to-one inverse levels and a \(C^2\)-but-not-\(C^3\) transition at angle \(2\pi\), where positive-probability geodesics through the apex first appear for abstract wider cones.
