For uniform singleton residual-tracking Kaczmarz on \(m\) identical rows, the exact mean-square stability threshold is the positive root of \(m(m-1)(2m-1)\alpha^2-(m-2)(2m-1)\alpha-2=0\). The same sharp boundary holds for the algorithm's prescribed initialization whenever its least-squares solution is nonzero, improving the source's conservative sufficient stepsize by an asymptotic factor \(m+4\).
