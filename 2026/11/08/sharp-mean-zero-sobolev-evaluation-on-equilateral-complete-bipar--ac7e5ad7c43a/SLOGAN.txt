For the real mean-zero arclength Sobolev space on every equilateral metric complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\), \(m,n\ge3\), the sharp squared evaluation norm is \(\frac{3m^2n^2-20mn+8m+8n}{12mn(mn-m-n)}\). Its only norming locations are one strictly interior point per edge, shifted by \(\frac{n-m}{2(mn-m-n)}\) from the midpoint toward the lower-degree endpoint, with an exact quadratic stability deficit.
