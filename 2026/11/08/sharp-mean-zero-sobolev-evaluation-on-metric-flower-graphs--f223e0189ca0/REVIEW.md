# Scientific review: sharp mean-zero Sobolev evaluation on metric flower graphs

## Correctness: PASS

The statement fixes an unnormalized arclength mean, real edgewise-\(H^1\) functions, and derivative energy, with at least one positive loop and pendant edge. Subtracting the common vertex value gives an exact orthogonal Hilbert sum of Dirichlet-loop and anchored-pendant spaces. The mean-zero condition uniquely restores the constant. Their explicit Green kernels give both pointwise norm formulas, including normalization and endpoints. Strict loop concavity, strict pendant convexity, and factorizations of edge-length comparisons prove the complete maximizing-point classification. The one-pendant threshold and sharp universal factor \(7-4\sqrt3\) follow from elementary exact algebra with an attained equality model. A separately executable exact-rational effective-resistance checker corroborates 1,595 pointwise cases. Its finite sample is diagnostic rather than an infinite proof.

Residual risks: a specialized older formula may be unindexed; the basic Hilbert-kernel method is established and not claimed as new. The priority assessment is literature-limited, not a guarantee of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value: PASS

A natural graph obtained by joining cycles and terminal edges at one point has a sharp Sobolev norm with competing classes of possible evaluation maximizers. The theorem gives a single all-length formula, full pointwise profiles and equality cases, and an exact localization switch between the longest loop and longest spoke. The sharp universal bound \(h_c/A\le7-4\sqrt3\), attained by a two-loop length distribution, supplies a topology-independent sufficient length for pendant dominance. This is an explicit classification of an invariant across infinitely many graph sizes and continuous length parameters, not an isolated numerical value or an unmotivated slice.
