For selective Poisson resetting of a Brownian probe in a memory-retaining Gaussian bath, the stationary excess kurtosis has exactly one finite-rate global minimum if and only if the bath parameter lies below the unique positive root \(g_c\approx0.7459581984\) of \(22g^4+25g^3-15g-6\); above that threshold it increases strictly with resetting rate. At every fixed positive resetting rate, excess kurtosis decreases strictly with bath memory, making its stationary shape an injective memory observable within the model.
