For the permutation-power word \(P^k\) on \(q\ge4\) symbols, the minimal absent scattered subsequences occupy every integer length from \(k+1\) to \(k+1+(q-1)\lfloor(k-1)/(q+1)\rfloor+\min((k-1)\bmod(q+1),q-1)\), and no other lengths. A two-track increasing-run bound is sharp for explicit doubled-staircase words.
