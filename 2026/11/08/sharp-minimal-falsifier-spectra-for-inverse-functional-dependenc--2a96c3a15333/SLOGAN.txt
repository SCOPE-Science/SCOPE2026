For \(\operatorname{dep}(x,y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(y,x)\) on a finite \(p\times q\) value rectangle, minimal falsifying teams have every size from six through \(2\min(p,q)+1\) when the alphabets are equal, or through \(2\min(p,q)+2\) when they are unequal (with the small empty-spectrum exceptions). These are sharp local-failure witness cutoffs, and the six- and seven-assignment obstruction counts have closed binomial formulas.
