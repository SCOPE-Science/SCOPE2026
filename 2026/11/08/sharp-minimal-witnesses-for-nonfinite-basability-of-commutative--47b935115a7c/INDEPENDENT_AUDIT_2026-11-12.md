# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Let c=d−1. Under Wang–Yin–Wang Corollary 1.2, nonfinite basis of the full constant-free powerset semiring is equivalent for commutative nilpotent S of index d to x^c=0 for all x. A nonzero c-fold product has every k-subproduct in L_k=S^k\S^(k+1); singleton L_k for k<c permits swapping a factor repeatedly to obtain a nonzero c-th power, contradiction. Thus |S|≥1+2(c−1)+1=2c=2d−2. Monomial quotient H_c with a^c=b²=0 has 2c elements, nonzero a^(c−1)b, no nonzero c-th powers. For even c≥4, parity-degree quotient B_c has the same size and top layer (c,1), all generator c-th powers zero, and neither generator squares to zero unlike H_c. Checked independently all multiplication triples and filtration layers for the constructed c=4 through 9 models. These checks are bounded; unbounded proof uses additive degrees and structural filtration.
