For equal-stratum one-sample quadrature of any bounded nondecreasing function, the unique globally minimax additive control is the centered local ramp clipped at \(\pm1/(2n)\); its exact worst-case variance is \((3n^2-3n+1)/(12n^4)\). At two evaluations this reduces the sharp worst-case variance from \(1/16\) to \(7/192\), while keeping every estimator unbiased.
