At every fixed area fraction \(0<\eta<1\), the equilateral triangle is the unique triangular container with the smallest possible maximum relative perimeter of a contained convex body. The excess for an arbitrary triangle is bounded below by the **best possible** coefficient \(\frac23(2-\eta-\sqrt{1-\eta+\eta^2})\) times its longest-minus-shortest side, and the coefficient becomes sharp only as the triangle flattens toward side ratio \(1:1:0\).
