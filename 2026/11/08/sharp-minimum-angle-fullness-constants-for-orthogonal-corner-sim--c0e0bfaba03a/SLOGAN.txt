For every orthogonal-corner Euclidean simplex, the smallest vertex solid angle occurs exactly at its longest leg and is linearly comparable to its volume divided by diameter to the dimension, with both optimal constants explicitly determined. The sharp upper ratio is \(\mathcal C_n=n(n-1)2^{n/2}\int_0^1(1-u)^{n-2}(1+u^2)^{-n/2}\,du\), asymptotic to \(n2^{n/2}\), while the sharp lower ratio equals \(n\).
