For the generalized binary digit-sum word \(t_m(n)=s_2(n)\bmod m\), the smallest possible maximum return shift is \(2^m-1\), and exactly \(2m-1\) fixed shifts suffice and are necessary at that minimum horizon for every \(m\ge2\). An explicit optimal family uses powers of two and their complements within the \(m\)-bit interval; disjoint Hamming-weight witness sets prove sharpness.
