Every mixed pair of largest-angle-bisection children contracts the right-isosceles projective shape excess \(H-1\) by the sharp factor \(3/4\), uniformly over all nondegenerate triangles. Consequently the fraction of depth-\(n\) descendants with excess at least \(\varepsilon\) is at most \((H_0-1)(7/8)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}/\varepsilon\).
