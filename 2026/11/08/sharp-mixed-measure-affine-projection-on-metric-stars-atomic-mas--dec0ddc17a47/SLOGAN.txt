Adding positive mass at the hub of a metric star produces an exact new \(L^\infty\) affine-projector norm \(2+1/(3z)-4r/[3(z+r)]\), with sharp thresholds determined by the smallest edge-mass fraction \(r\). Adjoining a new arm whose mass tends to zero causes a strictly positive limiting jump in the sharp norm, even though the new arm contributes vanishing measure.
