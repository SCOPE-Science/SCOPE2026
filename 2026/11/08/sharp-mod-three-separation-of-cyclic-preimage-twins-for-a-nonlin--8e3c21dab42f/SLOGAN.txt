For the second open two-to-one five-neighbor binary automaton in arXiv:2610.33131, two different predecessors of one periodic-ring output must differ at least \(\lfloor N/3\rfloor+(0,1,2)_{N\bmod3}\) sites, and this sharp bound is attained for every ring length \(N\ge1\). A complete \(64\)-state finite-graph certificate and explicit repeating witnesses prove the exact mod-three Hamming-separation law.
