For degree-\(d\) nilpotent-Jacobian polynomial maps in a canonical three-dimensional family, all primitive three-cycles with repeated heights admit a sharp count of at most \(2d-1\), attained robustly by simultaneous saddle cycles at every degree. A quadratic subfamily has exact cycle-birth and stability boundaries \(\kappa^2=27/4\) and \(\kappa^2=125/12\), with a superattracting three-cycle at \(\kappa^2=8\).
