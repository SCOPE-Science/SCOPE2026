For every \(n\ge2\), the named-sort relational encodings of **all** finite Latin \(n\)-hypercubes have the same counting-free \(k\)-variable theory precisely up to order truncation \(\min(q,k)\) for \(2\le k\le n+2\), regardless of isotopy type. The universal saturation cutoff \(n+2\) is sharp: with \(n+3\) variables, an intercalate sentence separates large odd-order and even-order additive hypercubes.
