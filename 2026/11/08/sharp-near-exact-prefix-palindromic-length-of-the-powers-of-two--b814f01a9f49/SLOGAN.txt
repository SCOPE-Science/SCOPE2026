For the binary \(2\)-automatic word marking power-of-two positions, the minimum palindromic factor count of a prefix is always either \(\lceil k/2\rceil\) or \(\lceil k/2\rceil+1\), where \(k\) is the number of marked positions. Both values are realized infinitely often; in particular, every prefix of length \(2^r-1\) with \(r\ge2\) requires exactly \(\lceil r/2\rceil+1\) palindromes, giving asymptotic palindromic length \(\frac12\log_2 n+O(1)\).
