The matrix rank metric on all two-column matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\) has sharp negative-type exponent \(\log_2(1+1/q)\), independently of the number of rows; at equality its minimum squared-Euclidean embedding dimension drops from \(q^{2m}-1\) to \((q^m-1)(q+1)\). At the endpoint the powered metric is exactly a scaled sum of \(q+1\) Hamming metrics on projective-line evaluations. The entire Gaussian phase diagram is explicit: for powered exponents above that boundary there is a unique positive bandwidth threshold, and for the ordinary rank distance it is exactly \(\log q\).
