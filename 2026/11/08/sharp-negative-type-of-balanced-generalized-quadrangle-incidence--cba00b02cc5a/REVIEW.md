The object is the full point–line incidence metric of any finite generalized quadrangle of order \((q,q)\), with \(q\ge2\). The incidence-graph intersection array yields distance polynomials through degree four, and its five adjacency eigenspaces are obtained from the point-collinearity strongly regular graph. The complete distance-power eigenvalue table is explicitly recorded in RESULT.md. The scalar function \(4^{-p}F_q(p)\) is strictly increasing; an explicit lower bound proves its zero occurs before \(1/q\). The other three nonprincipal eigenspace types remain strictly negative through that interval. This proves strictness below the endpoint, the unique one-dimensional endpoint kernel, failure above it, and the Euclidean embedding dimensions. Fixed-scale Taylor expansion proves the cubic asymptotic, without extrapolation from finite examples.

The attached `checker.py` reconstructs genuine finite-field geometries in orders two, three, and four (the last nonprime), confirms shell numbers and integer walk identities at every vertex, and matches independently diagonalized powered-distance matrices at five exponents for every order. This is corroboration, not infinite certification. Mathematical risk: an implementation or algebraic oversight beyond the checked orders is possible, but the supplied proof handles arbitrary existing orders.
