For the rank metric on Hermitian \(3\times3\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\), the exact generalized roundness is the unique positive root of \(q^3 3^t-q(q^2-q+1)2^t-q^2+q-1=0\), and it scales like \(q^{-3}/\log(3/2)\). Exactly at that exponent, the Euclidean embedding dimension drops by the number of invertible Hermitian Fourier frequencies.
