For independently refreshed edge weights on a cactus graph, the number of changed cycle deletions has an exact independent-Bernoulli law. The probability that the minimum spanning tree is unchanged is strictly log-convex in each cycle length, so balanced cycle lengths uniquely minimize stability at fixed cycle count and total cycle-edge budget, while a sharp \(\varepsilon\asymp 1/c\) transition produces a Poisson number of changes as the number \(c\) of cycles grows.
