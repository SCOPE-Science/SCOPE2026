On the infinite directed quadrant, every finite-level radial Pascal transfer has sharp Banach-space dual minimum modulus \(\asymp 2^n(n+1)^{-m/2-1/(2p)}\), with the endpoint \(p=\infty\) interpreted on weighted \(c_0\). This resolves the radial hypercyclicity and mixing criteria simultaneously in all weighted \(\ell^p\) and \(c_0\) spaces and shows those dynamics are independent of \(p\); the Hilbert specialization was already known.
