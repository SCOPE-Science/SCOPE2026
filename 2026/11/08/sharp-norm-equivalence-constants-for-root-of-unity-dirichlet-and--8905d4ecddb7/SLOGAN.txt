For the canonical de Branges–Rovnyak symbol \(b_m=(1+z^m)/2\) and uniform measure on the \(m\)-th roots of unity, the exact best Dirichlet-to-de Branges squared-norm constants are \(L_m=(m+2-\sqrt{m^2+4})/4\) and \(U_m=(m+2+\sqrt{(m-2)^2+4})/4\) for \(m\ge2\), with \(U_1=5/4\). Their optimal norm distortion grows as \(\sqrt m\), and rational functions explicitly attain the sharp edges except for the upper edge at \(m=1,2\).
