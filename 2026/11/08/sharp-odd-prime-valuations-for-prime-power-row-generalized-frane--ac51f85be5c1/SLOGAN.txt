For every odd prime \(p\), prime-power row \(p^q-1\), and exponent \(t\ge2\), the signed binomial-power sum has exact valuation \(q+
u_p(t-1)\) and an explicit leading \(p\)-adic unit. In particular, the corresponding unsigned sums for even exponents prove the odd-prime generalized-Franel valuation conjecture.
