The sharp condition number for recovering any \(n\)-level truncated Bargmann–Fock signal from arbitrarily many positive-weight samples on a *single circle* occurs at squared radius \(((n-1)!)^{1/(n-1)}\) and exactly at angular designs whose first \(n-1\) Fourier moments vanish. For critical sampling, equal weights and a regular polygon are the only optimizers; even unlimited single-radius oversampling cannot eliminate the sharp exponentially growing conditioning barrier.
