For binary necklaces, the largest possible number of distinct necklaces surviving exactly one symbol deletion is \(n-2\) at even lengths \(n\ge4\), and \(n-3\) at odd lengths \(n\ge9\), with explicit sharp exceptions at lengths \(1,2,3,5,7\). The parity loss is forced by collisions among deletions from nearly alternating circular words, and explicit two-defect run patterns achieve the bounds.
