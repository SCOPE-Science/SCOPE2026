On every cyclic prime-power group of order \(p^a\) with \(p\ge5\) and \(a\ge2\), adding a single positive real impulse to the cubic chirp removes every Gabor-ambiguity zero. Its normalized sharp ambiguity margin is exactly \(2\varepsilon\sin(\pi/(2p^{a-1}))/(p^a+2\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2)\), while the unperturbed matrix-lifting kernel has dimension \((p^{2a}-1)/(p+1)\).
