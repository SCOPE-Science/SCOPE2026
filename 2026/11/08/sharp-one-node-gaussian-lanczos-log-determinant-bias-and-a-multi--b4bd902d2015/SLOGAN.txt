For one Gaussian probe and one Lanczos node, the sharp worst-case *mean* log-determinant overestimate at condition number \(\kappa\) is achieved by a two-point spectrum and equals the maximum of \(n-1\) explicit beta integrals. In dimensions \(n\ge4\), its maximizing high-eigenvalue multiplicity changes from \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) near condition number one to a single spike for sufficiently large \(\kappa\).
