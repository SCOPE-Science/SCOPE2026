For fourth-order two-stage Gauss–Legendre heat integration on a star with \(m\ge4\) leaves and one exceptional spoke of relative conductance \(t\), the completed update is nonnegative at every timestep exactly for \(1/2\le t\le m/(m-1)\). All entries become strictly positive at every finite positive step within that range, while outside it a particular hub-to-leaf transition eventually becomes negative; the permissible enhancement of a single spoke shrinks to zero as the star degree grows.
