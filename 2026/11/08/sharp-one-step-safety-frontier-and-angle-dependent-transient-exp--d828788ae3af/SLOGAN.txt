For two finite-dimensional subspaces with Friedrichs cosine \(0<c<1\), the full-space relaxed alternating-projection map is Euclidean nonexpansive exactly for \(0\le\omega\le(2+c)/(1+c)\), while the unrelaxed value \(\omega=1\) uniquely minimizes its one-step error norm. The previously known spectral-rate-optimal relaxation violates this nonexpansivity boundary exactly when \(c>0.839286755214161\ldots\), the unique root of \(c^3+2c^2-2=0\) in \((0,1)\).
