For every finite affine plane of order \(q\), the online point-membership dimension of unions of two distinct nonparallel lines is exactly \(1\) at \(q=2\), \(4\) at \(q=3\), and \(5\) at every \(q\ge4\). A three-positive-point reduction gives a uniform five-mistake upper bound, while a five-point two-axis construction is shattered for every order at least four.
