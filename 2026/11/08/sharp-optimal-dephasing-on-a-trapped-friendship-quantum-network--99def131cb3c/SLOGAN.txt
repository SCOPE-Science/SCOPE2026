For coherent excitation transport from one triangle to a peripheral trap on another triangle of a friendship graph, local dephasing optimized over all positive rates has a sharp mean-capture-time scaling \(1.681019855\ldots\,m^{3/2}\). The optimal dephasing strength is \(1.226929487\ldots\sqrt m\), obtained from a nontrivial rational limiting profile and an exact eighteen-coordinate Lindblad reduction.
