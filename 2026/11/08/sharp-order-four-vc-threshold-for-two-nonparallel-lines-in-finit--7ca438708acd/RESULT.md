# Sharp order-four VC threshold for two nonparallel lines in finite affine planes

## Finding
For any finite affine plane \(\Pi\) of order \(q\ge 2\), let \(\mathcal U(\Pi)\) be the Boolean family of point sets \(L\cup M\), where \(L,M\) are distinct intersecting lines. Then
\[
\operatorname{VCdim}(\mathcal U(\Pi))=\begin{cases}1& q=2,\\4&q=3,\\5&q\ge4.\end{cases}
\]
This is an exact threshold for every existing finite affine plane, including non-Desarguesian planes. At order three all noncollinear four-sets are shattered; at order two the shattered sets are precisely the empty support and singletons. No adaptive/Littlestone-dimension assertion is made.

## Assumptions and scope
A finite affine plane of order \(q\) has \(q^2\) points and \(q+1\) directions; every line has \(q\) points, any two points determine a unique line, and two lines of different directions meet in a unique point. Two lines in a hypothesis must have *different directions*: parallel unions are excluded. A support \(S\) is shattered if every \(T\subseteq S\) equals \((L\cup M)\cap S\) for some allowed pair. The result is conditional on existence of a plane of that order; it assumes no coordinatization except in the uniquely determined planes of orders two and three.

## Proof
**Universal six-point obstruction.** Suppose a six-set \(S\) is shattered. Its full positive trace is realized by lines \(L,M\) of different directions meeting at \(O\), so \(S\subseteq L\cup M\). Write \(a=|S\cap(L\setminus M)|\), \(b=|S\cap(M\setminus L)|\), and \(o=|S\cap\{O\}|\in\{0,1\}\). Thus \(a+b+o=6\). Exchange \(L,M\) to arrange \(a\ge b\), giving \(a\ge3\). If \(a\ge4\), choose \(P\in S\cap(L\setminus M)\). Any two-line union containing \(S\setminus\{P\}\) must contain \(L\), since this set contains at least three points of \(L\), and two distinct lines not equal to \(L\) each meet \(L\) in at most one point. That forces \(P\), contrary to shattering. If \(a=3\), then \(b+o=3\), so \(S\setminus\{P\}\) contains three points of \(M\), forcing \(M\), and two points of \(L\setminus M\), forcing \(L\) as its other line. Again the omitted \(P\) is forced. Hence no six-set is shattered; shattering is hereditary, so there are no larger shattered supports.

**Five-point witness for every order \(q\ge4\).** Fix intersecting lines \(L,M\) and their intersection \(O\). Choose distinct \(A_1,A_2\in L\setminus\{O\}\) and \(B_1\in M\setminus\{O\}\). Choose \(B_2\in M\setminus\{O,B_1\}\) such that the cross lines \(A_1B_1\) and \(A_2B_2\) are not parallel. Such \(B_2\) exists: at most one potential \(B_2\) lies on the unique line through \(A_2\) parallel to \(A_1B_1\), whereas there are \(q-2\ge2\) candidates. Set \(S=\{O,A_1,A_2,B_1,B_2\}\).

We construct every trace by the size of its positive part \(T\). For \(|T|=5\), use \(L\cup M\). For \(|T|=4\) excluding \(O\), use \(A_1B_1\cup A_2B_2\), whose lines are nonparallel and each contain exactly their two designated points of \(S\). For \(|T|=4\) excluding \(A_i\), use \(M\) together with a line through the other \(A_j\) not equal to \(L\) and not parallel to \(M\); analogously for an excluded \(B_i\). If \(|T|=3\) and \(T\) lies wholly on \(L\) or wholly on \(M\), use that line plus a line through \(O\) whose direction differs from both axes. Otherwise \(T\) contains a cross-arm pair \(A_i,B_j\). Their joining line meets \(S\) in exactly those two points. Through the third chosen point, choose a line avoiding both unchosen points and not parallel to the joining line. Among the \(q+1\ge5\) directions, at most two are disallowed by the excluded points and one by parallelism, so such a line exists.

For \(|T|=1,2\), define a *safe line* through a specified point of \(S\) as a line meeting \(S\) in precisely that point. At \(O\), only the two axes are unsafe, so \(q-1\ge3\) safe directions remain. At each \(A_i\) or \(B_i\), the other four points exclude at most three directions (the axis through \(O\) and the same-arm point, and the lines to the two opposite-arm points), leaving at least \(q-2\ge2\) safe directions. For a singleton, choose two nonparallel safe lines through it. For a pair, choose safe lines through the respective points having different directions. To realize \(T=\varnothing\), choose a line \(L'\ne L\) parallel to \(L\) and missing \(B_1,B_2\), then a line \(M'\ne M\) parallel to \(M\) and missing \(A_1,A_2\). Each choice exists because among the \(q-1\) translates other than its axis, at most two are prohibited, leaving \(q-3\ge1\). Their union misses \(S\), and their directions differ. Thus all 32 traces exist and \(\operatorname{VCdim}\ge5\).

**Order three.** Every affine plane of order three is incidence-isomorphic to \(\mathrm{AG}(2,3)\) (equivalently, the unique Steiner triple system on nine points). A union of two nonparallel lines has exactly five points. If a five-set \(S\) were shattered, its full trace would force \(S=L\cup M\) for some intersecting axes. In coordinates \(L=\{(0,t):t\in\mathbb F_3\}\), \(M=\{(t,0):t\in\mathbb F_3\}\), the four points of \(S\setminus\{(0,0)\}\) are \((0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(2,0)\). Any two lines avoiding the origin and covering these four must join them in two cross-arm pairs. The two possible perfect matchings each yield *parallel* cross lines: the slopes are respectively both \(2\) or both \(1\). Hence the trace omitting the origin is impossible. This proves the upper bound four. For a lower witness use the square \(Q=\{(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)\}\). All 16 traces are exhibited by the independent finite incidence enumerator `VERIFY.py`; they also follow directly by taking both diagonals for all four, a side and a transverse line for any chosen three, two nonparallel lines each avoiding the omitted vertices for one or two, and two nonparallel lines outside the square for the empty trace. The enumerator checks all 126 four-point supports, finding exactly 54 shattered supports, the number of affine quadrangles.

**Order two.** Every two nonparallel two-point lines meet in one point and their union is the three-point complement of the remaining point. The four concepts are thus all complements of singletons. Every singleton is shattered; no pair is shattered, since the all-negative pair trace would require a concept missing both points.

## Verification
Execute `python3 VERIFY.py` using Python's standard library. The program independently constructs the full point-line incidence family and Boolean traces in \(\mathrm{AG}(2,q)\) for prime fields of orders \(2,3,5,7\) and the degree-two extension field of order \(4\). For orders two and three it enumerates *every* support size through the asserted cutoff and checks exact shattering counts. For orders four, five and seven it constructs and checks all 32 traces on a five-point witness. Recorded output is in `verify_output.txt`. The universal upper and lower proofs above, rather than these finite instances, establish the arbitrary-plane conclusion.

## Relationship to prior work
Eleftheriou–Papadopoulos–Westhead (arXiv:2501.09847v1, Section 5, Proposition 5.2 and Corollary 5.3) prove that the VC dimension of **unrestricted two-line unions in the real Euclidean plane** is five; their argument uses point-line incidence obstructions and characterizes maximal shattered supports. In particular, the incidence upper-bound technique and the number five are **already known in that setting**, and are not claimed as novel here. The present statement differs by requiring nonparallel pairs in finite affine planes, including noncoordinatizable ones, and by determining the small-order failures and the sharp order-four threshold with an explicit construction at the boundary. The 2025 paper explicitly suggests transfer to settings with unique lines, so further equivalence/priority scrutiny of such transfers is warranted. Cambie–Girão–Kang (arXiv:1808.02352v1) study general VC-dimension behavior under set-system unions and symmetric differences, not finite-plane nonparallel-line shattering. A separate classification of *unrestricted two-line unions in finite projective planes* determines a related but larger concept class, with VC dimensions three at order two and five at orders three and above. It supplies an independent upper ceiling after projective completion, but does not imply the affine nonparallel lower witnesses or the two small-order obstructions. The result also complements the separate parallel-pair concept family, whose traces differ already at small orders.

## Limitations
No claim is made about Littlestone dimensions, all shattered-support counts for \(q\ge4\), or finite non-affine incidence structures. This is a self-review, not independent certification. The unrestricted real-plane and finite-projective-plane two-line VC dimensions and much of the generic incidence obstruction were obtained before; the asserted new content is the uniform **finite affine-plane, nonparallel** threshold including both exceptions. Broad historical novelty under alternate terminology is not certified.
