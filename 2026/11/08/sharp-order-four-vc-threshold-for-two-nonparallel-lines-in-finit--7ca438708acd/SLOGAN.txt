For the family of unions of exactly two nonparallel lines in any finite affine plane of order \(q\), the VC dimension is \(1\) at order \(2\), \(4\) at order \(3\), and \(5\) at every order \(q\ge4\). The five-point threshold is witnessed uniformly by two intersecting axes and depends only on affine-plane incidence axioms.
