For a two-dimensional positive-definite matrix, one-node Rademacher stochastic Lanczos log-determinant estimates exhibit two exact sampling-budget transitions: the worst eigenvector orientation switches from unbiased but noisy to deterministic but biased, and the optimal orientation switches from coordinate aligned to a unique interior angle. Both phase boundaries, the exact mean-squared risk, and every equality case follow from an explicit two-point probe law.
