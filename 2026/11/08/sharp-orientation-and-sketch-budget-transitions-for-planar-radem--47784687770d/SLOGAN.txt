For a planar positive-definite matrix, the worst-orientation expected rank-one Nyström error from choosing the best of \(m\) independent Rademacher sketches is exactly \(b\max\{2\kappa/(\kappa+1),1+(\kappa-1)2^{-m}\}\), with a sharp change in the worst eigenvector alignment at \(\kappa+1=2^m\). Uniformly over orientations, this beats or matches the expected error of one Gaussian sketch exactly when \(2^m\ge1+\sqrt\kappa\); the comparison is about accuracy, not equal computational cost.
