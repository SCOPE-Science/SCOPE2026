For a fixed three-dimensional rank-one spiked spectrum, the orientation minimizing the expected one-node Rademacher Lanczos log-determinant estimate switches sharply from three equally weighted eigenvector coordinates to two equal nonzero coordinates at spike strength \(5\sqrt{10}-14\). The complete expectation range and every equality case are explicit; the result is specific to one-node quadrature, not a mean-squared-error or runtime guarantee.
