For Hardy Hankel symbols \(\psi(z)=az+bz^m\) with \(m\ge3\), an explicit sharp coefficient threshold determines whether the Garsia-norm testing kernel at the origin is globally optimal. Degree three has a continuous relocation of its two norming kernels, whereas every degree \(m\ge4\) has a discontinuous transition with exactly \(m-1\) simultaneous off-center norming kernels at a nonzero critical radius.
