For any two genuinely squeezed Gaussian windows, with arbitrary positive weights and possibly different squeezing strengths, mutually perpendicular ambiguity ellipses uniquely maximize the smallest weighted ambiguity sum on every centered disk; the sharp floor is an explicit clipped-exponential formula. With equal weights and unequal squeezings, the worst-case direction undergoes a unique, explicitly located square-root bifurcation as the disk radius grows.
