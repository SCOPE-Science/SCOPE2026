Deleting up to \(d\) cross edges incident with each vertex of a complete bipartite graph cannot change a single \(P_3\)-convex set once both shores have size at least \(2d+3\): the only proper convex sets choose at most one vertex per shore, and the exact polynomial is \((1+mz)(1+nz)+z^{m+n}\). The universal threshold is sharp, and any two vertices on the same shore activate the whole graph under the two-neighbour closure rule.
