For the type-\(D\) even-signed permutation action, all worst rank-one input/output pairs are completely classified: they are axis–flat except for a tetrahedral parity stratum in dimension three and an additional flat–flat parity stratum in dimension four. The sharp group-Daugavet norm follows from the orbit covering cosine \(1/3\) in dimension three and \(1/\sqrt d\) thereafter.
