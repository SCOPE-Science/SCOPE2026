For binary words with at most four constant-symbol runs, the conjectured palindrome-pair-factor upper bound \(1+\lfloor(n+1)^2/3\rfloor\) is sharp for every length. Exactly four runs have a strictly smaller exact maximum \(\lfloor(n^2+n+10)/3\rfloor\) for \(n\ge8\), and every maximizer is explicitly classified.
