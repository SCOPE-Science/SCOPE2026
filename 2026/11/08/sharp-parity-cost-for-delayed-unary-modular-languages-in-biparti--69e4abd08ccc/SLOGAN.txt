For delayed unary modular languages \(L_{n,t}=\{a^{kn}:k\ge t\}\), the smallest transition-bipartite cactus NFA has exactly \(tn+1\) states for even \(n\) and \((t+1)n+1\) for odd \(n\). This sharp parity penalty is attained by a deterministic one-cycle construction and applies as a lower bound even without the cactus restriction.
