Over \(\mathbb F_2\), the largest automorphism group of any simple \(n\)-dimensional evolution algebra has order \(n!\) for even \(n\), \((n-1)!\) for odd \(n\ge5\), and \(3\) for \(n=3\) (the one-dimensional maximum is \(1\)). Sharp constructions show how the field of two elements forces a parity transition in automorphism symmetry.
