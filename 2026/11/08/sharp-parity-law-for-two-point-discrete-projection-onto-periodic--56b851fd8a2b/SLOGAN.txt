For periodic linear splines fitted by equal-weight observations at two symmetric interior points per cell, the exact uniform-norm amplification is \((1-2t)^{-1}\) on every even cycle, while odd cycles have an explicit exponentially small parity correction. This yields the sharp constant \(\sqrt3\) for two-point Gauss sampling on even cycles, complete norming-knot and positivity-defect formulas, and a sharp contrast between even-cycle singularity and odd-cycle finite stability as the sites coalesce at midpoints.
