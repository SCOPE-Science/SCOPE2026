Two binomial sums whose published run-length transforms have the same fourth-order recurrence have strictly nested odd-value supports, but radically different sparsity exponents: \(\log_2(1.9659482366\ldots)\) and \(\log_2(1.4970940488\ldots)\). The smaller support has an exact rational dyadic counting series and is exponentially negligible even among the odd values of the larger sum.
