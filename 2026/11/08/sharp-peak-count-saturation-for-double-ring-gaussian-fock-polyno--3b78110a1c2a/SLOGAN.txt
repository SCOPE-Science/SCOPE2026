For the two-concentric-polygon Gaussian intensity \(e^{-|z|^2}|(z^n-a)(z^n-b)|^2\), there are at most \(2n+1\) local maxima for every \(n\ge3\) and \(0<a<b\). This sharp count is attained with nondegenerate maxima for every \(n\ge11\): two distinct rings of off-center peaks coexist with the central peak.
