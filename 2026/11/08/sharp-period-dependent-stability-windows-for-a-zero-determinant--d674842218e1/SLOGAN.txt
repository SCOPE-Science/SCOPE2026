For the zero-left-determinant border-collision normal form, attracting strict cycles with word \(L^2R(LR)^{2k}\) occur for some right-hand parameters exactly when \(\ell^{-1/(2k+1)}>2\ell+\ell^2\). The sharp boundary \(\ell=\sqrt2-1\) separates infinitely many feasible periods from a finite set; the stability-window width drops from order \(1/k\) to order \(1/k^2\) at that boundary.
