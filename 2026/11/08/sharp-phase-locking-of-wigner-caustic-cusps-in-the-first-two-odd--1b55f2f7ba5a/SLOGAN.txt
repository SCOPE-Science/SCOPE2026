For the constant-width oval with support \(R+a\cos3\theta+b\cos(5\theta+\phi)\), the Wigner caustic has exactly three or five ordinary cusps according to an explicit sharp amplitude–phase diagram, including its nonordinary transition germs. The fifth-harmonic share of the isoperimetric area deficit must exceed \(3/28\) for five ordinary cusps to become possible, and must exceed \(1/4\) for five to occur in every phase.
