For a two-site signal on a cyclic group, the exact first degree at which a finite-Heisenberg invariant can detect relative phase is \(\min\{N,2N/\gcd(N,d)\}\); requiring invariance under every global scalar phase raises it to \(2N/\gcd(N,d)\). At prime length, all projective invariants below degree \(2N\) are blind to this nongeneric phase family, despite known generic degree-six separation.
