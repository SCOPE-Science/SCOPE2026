For a uniformly sampled subtree of a complete bipartite graph, the number of omitted vertices admits a Poisson approximation whose total-variation error is sharply \(\Theta(m^{-2})\) when both parts have size \(m\), but \(\Theta(N^{-1})\) along every fixed unbalanced proportion. The second omitted-vertex coefficient has a positive balanced residual and a strictly negative unbalanced residual.
