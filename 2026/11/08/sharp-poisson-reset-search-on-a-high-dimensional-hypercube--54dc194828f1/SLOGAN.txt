On a binary hypercube with independent unit-rate coordinate flips, resetting at rate asymptotic to \(d/(k-1)\) reduces mean first passage from a vertex at fixed Hamming distance \(k\ge2\) to a corner target from exponential to the sharp polynomial value \(\frac{k^k}{k!(k-1)^{k-1}}d^{k-1}\). Every global Poisson-reset-rate minimizer has this asymptotic scaling.
