In four-dimensional circular cones, Poisson ultra-log-concavity holds exactly up to one sharply defined aperture \(\alpha_*=0.984619178826\ldots\) radians, with equality at just the first index at the boundary. Binomial ultra-log-concavity fails at the middle index for every nondegenerate aperture, since \(v_2^2/(v_1v_3)=16/\pi^2<9/4\).
