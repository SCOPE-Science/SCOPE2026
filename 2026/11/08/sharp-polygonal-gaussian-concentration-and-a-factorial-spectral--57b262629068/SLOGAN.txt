For Gaussian concentration on an area-\(A\) regular \(N\)-gon, the sharp disk deficit is asymptotic to \(2\pi^4 A^2e^{-A}/(45N^4)\), but optimizing beyond the Gaussian test improves the concentration by only \(4e^{-2A}A^{N+2}/((1-e^{-A})N^4N!)\). Cyclic symmetry forces the first non-Gaussian optimizing mode to have Hermite degree \(N\), with an explicit coefficient.
