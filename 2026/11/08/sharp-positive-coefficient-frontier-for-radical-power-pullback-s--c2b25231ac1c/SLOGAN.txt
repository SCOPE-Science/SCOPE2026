The nonsquare radical power-pullback spring of order \(4m+1\) has every restoring-force coefficient strictly positive **exactly when** \(p>[(3(m+1)/(m+3))^{1/m}-1]^{-1}\), refining an earlier sufficient \(p\ge2m\) bound to a sharp all-order threshold. Log-concavity of the energy coefficients makes the linear stiffness the unique sign obstruction.
