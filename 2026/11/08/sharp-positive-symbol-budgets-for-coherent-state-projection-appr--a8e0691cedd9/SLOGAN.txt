For every Bargmann–Fock dimension, the unique optimal positive bounded symbol approximating a coherent-state rank-one projector is either a constant or an indicator of a centered ball, with a sharp transition at symbol budget \(M=1/2\). Its optimal operator-norm error decays as \(\frac{(d!)^{1/d}}{d+1}M^{-1/d}\), giving the exact dimension-dependent cost of bounded positive quantization.
