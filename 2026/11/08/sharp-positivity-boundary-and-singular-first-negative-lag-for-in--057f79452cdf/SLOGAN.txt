For a harmonic inertial particle driven by exponentially correlated active forcing, positional autocovariance stays positive at every lag exactly when mechanical motion is nonoscillatory or active forcing persists at least as long as the damping time. Just beyond the sharp persistent-noise boundary, the first negative lag diverges like \(\log[2\Omega/\sqrt{\Omega^2+3\alpha^2}]/(\lambda-\alpha)\).
