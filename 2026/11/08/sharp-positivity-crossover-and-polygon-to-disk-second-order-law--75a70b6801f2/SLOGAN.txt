For uniform area on a filled regular \(n\)-gon, the real affine least-squares projection norm crosses the sharp amplification threshold \(2\) exactly between the hexagon and heptagon: it exceeds \(2\) for \(3\le n\le6\), and stays strictly below \(2\) for all \(n\ge7\). The corresponding worst-case negative output from a nonnegative unit-range input crosses the half-range threshold at the same side count. Previously known all-polygon norm formulas and their disk asymptotics are background, not new claims.
