For uniform area on the filled regular \(n\)-gon, the sharp real affine least-squares endpoint norm exceeds \(2\) exactly for \(3\le n\le6\), and is below \(2\) for every \(n\ge7\). Precisely the vertices are norming, and the polygon-to-disk norm difference has the exact positive second-order coefficient \(5\pi\sqrt{15}/12\).
