For the one-dimensional full-lattice first-order Korobov quadrature with sine-squared periodization, every weight is nonnegative exactly when the kernel parameter satisfies \(0<\gamma\le\Gamma_N\), with an explicit parity-dependent formula for the sharp frontier. The first negative weight occurs at the mapped endpoint, and \(\Gamma_N=N+\pi^2/3+O(N^{-1})\), so every fixed positive kernel parameter has eventually strictly positive weights.
