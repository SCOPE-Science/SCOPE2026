For the exactly normalized irrational-rotation rational arrival construction, the full family of joint densities is strictly positive exactly for amplitudes \(0\le\varepsilon\le 2/(q-1+\sqrt{2(q^2+9)})\), where \(q=\cot(\varphi/2)\).
