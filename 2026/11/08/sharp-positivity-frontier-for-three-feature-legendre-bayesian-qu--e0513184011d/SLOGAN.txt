For the first three Legendre features, regularized Bayesian quadrature on every symmetric \(n\)-node design has nonnegative weights exactly when the nugget satisfies \(\eta\ge a_2 C_n\), with a sharp parity-dependent closed formula for \(C_n\). Enforcing this condition uniformly over designs makes the unmodified quadrature rule retain an asymptotic constant-function bias of at least \(a_2/(4a_0+a_2)\), regardless of the nodes.
