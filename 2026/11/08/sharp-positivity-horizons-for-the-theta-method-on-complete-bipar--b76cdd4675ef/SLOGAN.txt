For heat diffusion on any unweighted \(K_{p,q}\) with at least three vertices, the \(\theta\)-method is positivity-preserving exactly up to a closed radical step bound, with unconditional positivity beginning sharply at \(\theta=(p+q-1)/(p+q)\). For fixed total size and \(\theta\) below this cutoff, balancing the two parts strictly increases the admissible step, with stars worst and balanced bicliques best.
