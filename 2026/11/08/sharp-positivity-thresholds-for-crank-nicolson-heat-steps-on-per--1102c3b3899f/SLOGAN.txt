For classical Crank–Nicolson stepping on every isotropic periodic nearest-neighbor heat torus, there is one exact positivity cutoff, strictly decreasing in both torus dimension and period. The two-dimensional infinite-grid cutoff is an elliptic-integral root \(0.5881972836856281\ldots\), and the high-dimensional cutoff has the universal expansion \(d^{-1}+(4d^2)^{-1}+O(d^{-3})\).
