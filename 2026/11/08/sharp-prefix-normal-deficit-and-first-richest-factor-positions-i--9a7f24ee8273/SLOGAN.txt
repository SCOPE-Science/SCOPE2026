For the period-doubling fixed point, the maximum excess of ones in a length-\(n\) factor over its length-\(n\) prefix satisfies an exact base-four recurrence and a sharp logarithmic envelope \(\max_{1\le n<4^r}D(n)=r\). At \(n=(4^r-1)/3\), the first factor attaining the global maximum starts *exactly* at position \(n\), and no finite prefix can make the word prefix normal.
