At the maximal belief-response rate in the risk-sensitive two-dimensional multiplicative-weights map, there is exactly one interior primitive two-cycle if and only if \(cp_1<1\) and \(a>2/[m(1-m)(1-cp_1)]\), where \(m=(b-cp_0)/(1+cp_1)\in(0,1)\); otherwise there is none. The cycle emerges at square-root rate, and its near-birth attraction switches at the sharp feedback value \(cp_1=1/3\).
