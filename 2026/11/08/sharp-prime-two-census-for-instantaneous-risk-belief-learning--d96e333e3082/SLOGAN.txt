For the instantaneous risk-belief multiplicative-weights map, the period-two population census reduces to a known scalar replicator result, but its full two-dimensional Floquet behavior has a sharp new split: newly emerging primitive cycles are attracting for cp1<1/3 and nonattracting for cp1>1/3 (the equality case is unclassified). Along every such interior two-cycle, 1-tr(DF^2)+det(DF^2)>0, so the Floquet multiplier +1 is globally excluded.
