The exact maximum number of powers of \(b\) visible in a length-\(n\) arithmetic progression is \(1+\lfloor\log_b(1+(b-1)(n-1))\rfloor\), with a rigid unique shortest binary witness for every target weight at least three. This yields the explicit polynomial bound \(P_b(n)=O_b(n^2\log(n+1))\) for the arithmetic-subword complexity of the power-support automatic word.
