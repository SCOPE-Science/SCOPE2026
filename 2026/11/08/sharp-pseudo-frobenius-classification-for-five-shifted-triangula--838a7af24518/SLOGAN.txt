The five-generator semigroups \(\langle a,a+1,a+3,a+6,a+10\rangle\) have a complete explicit pseudo-Frobenius classification from the sharp starting value \(a=18\). Their types follow the least-period-ten pattern \((7,8,8,9,10,7,5,6,5,6)\), determined by ten explicit boundary-residue sets together with two fixed interior residues.
