For alternating noisy gradient tracking on heterogeneous scalar quadratics, full-transcript pure privacy has an exact finite-budget frontier: for each agent with \(t=\alpha h_i\) and \(r=q_i^2\), it holds precisely when \(0<t<1\) and \(r>(t^2+\sqrt{t^4+4t(1-t)})/2\). The frontier extends well beyond the published general sufficient privacy certificate and yields a sharp closed-form privacy budget independent of the communication graph.
