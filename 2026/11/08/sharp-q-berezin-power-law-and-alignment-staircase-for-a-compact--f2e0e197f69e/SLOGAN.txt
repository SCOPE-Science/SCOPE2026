For every fixed overlap \(0<q\le1\), the \(q\)-Berezin radii of powers of order at least two of the compact geometric Hardy shift have an exact unique-quartic formula and a one-way transition in their norming kernel geometry. After division by the operator norm, the radii decay exactly like \(2q/[e(1-\sqrt{1-q^2})m]\), with a sharp leading constant independent of the shift's damping.
