For independent Pareto stick lengths of fixed tail index \(\alpha>0\), the probability that no three sticks form a triangle satisfies \(\log\Pr(A_n)=-\alpha(\log\varphi)n^2/2+O_\alpha(n\log n)\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). Explicit finite-sample exponential and gamma-tail bounds make the quadratic rate rigorous.
