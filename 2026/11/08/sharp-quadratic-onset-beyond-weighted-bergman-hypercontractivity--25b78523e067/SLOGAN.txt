Immediately beyond the sharp weighted Bergman \(L^2\to L^4\) contractive dilation, the fourth power of the optimal norm rises by exactly \((\alpha+1)(2r^2-1)^2+o((2r^2-1)^2)\). Every near-threshold optimizer first acquires a degree-one mode and then a phase-locked degree-two mode; higher modes carry asymptotically negligible energy.
