For uniform feasible-edge greedy matching on \(K_a\vee\overline K_b\), the number of edges lost relative to a maximum matching approaches \(\operatorname{Pois}(\theta/4)\) whenever \(a^2/b\to\theta\), with an explicit total-variation error bound in the high-imbalance regime. The complete output law is exactly a mixture of uniform cross-part injections and suboptimal matchings, and the sharp threshold for exact optimality is \(b\asymp a^2\).
