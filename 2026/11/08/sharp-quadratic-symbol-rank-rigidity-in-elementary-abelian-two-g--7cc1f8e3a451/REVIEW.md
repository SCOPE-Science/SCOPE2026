# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For an arbitrary characteristic-two field, identify the group algebra of the elementary abelian group with the squarefree commutative algebra. Each augmentation element squares to zero. The degree-two symbol defines an alternating matrix of even rank, hence admits a paired normal form. Multiplication by the sum of pair monomials is a tensor differential whose homology is concentrated in the degree interval \( [r,r+s] \) with Hilbert polynomial \( (2t)^r(1+t)^s \). Finite graded cancellation of all higher-degree multiplication terms leaves a transferred differential of degree at least three. Therefore this differential vanishes when \(s\leq2\), proving the exact operator rank and Jordan type for all higher lifts, rather than just random examples. A cubic on three unused variables supplies a tensor-product homology calculation giving the strict rank increase \(2^{r+s-3}\) at every \(s\geq3\). Symplectic orbit-stabilizer counts and a direct analysis of the pure cubic/quartic case establish the finite-field statements. All formula quantifiers, the zero case and characteristic-two field restrictions were examined.

Standalone `verify.py`, using squarefree multiplication and field elimination, passed exhaustive \(n=4\) computations over \(\mathbb F_2\), a full \(\mathbb F_4\) quadratic-symbol census, deterministic sample higher lifts, and representative sharpness tests. Finite samples are corroboration, not an infinite-field proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest group-algebra articles establish modular-isomorphism invariants and structural reductions, not elementwise regular-module multiplication ranks or rigidity under degree-at-least-three changes (Margolis–Sakurai–Stanojkovski, arXiv:2110.10025, §§1–2; García-Lucas, arXiv:2209.15128, introduction and pertinent sections). Crispin Quiñonez–Lundqvist–Nenashev, arXiv:1803.08918, study characteristic-zero generic ideals generated by *two* homogeneous quadrics, not arbitrary nonhomogeneous characteristic-two principal augmentation elements. The known alternating-form classification and elementary cancellation/Künneth identities are prior mathematics; the claim is their sharp application to perturbation threshold, all-field Jordan rigidity, and the four-generator distribution. Searches comparing exterior-algebra multiplication, modular group-algebra Jordan type, quadratic-form ideals and homology perturbation did not identify a result implying the whole conclusion. The literature search is not an exhaustive publication-priority guarantee; an unidentified more general exterior-algebra deformation theorem remains a material risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The multiplication Jordan type of a regular-module element is a natural module-theoretic invariant. The result identifies an exact, uniform *boundary* at quadratic nullity two: arbitrarily high augmentation corrections are invisible on one side, but a degree-three term changes the Jordan type on the other. This is a structural result for an infinite family, not just another finite enumeration. The precise rank jump gives counterexamples beyond the threshold. The all-field closed census of the four-generator augmentation square exhibits impossible ranks three and five, providing a benchmark for more general rank strata.

## Scope and limitations
The group must be elementary abelian of exponent two and the coefficient field must have characteristic two. The rigidity theorem applies to quadratic-symbol nullity at most two; higher nullity is not completely classified. The finite-field orbit count uses the standard symplectic action, and the complete rank polynomial covers four generators.
