In characteristic two, the multiplication Jordan type of every higher-order lift of a quadratic augmentation symbol in \(k[(C_2)^n]\) is fixed by the symbol's alternating rank exactly throughout the nullity-at-most-two range; when the nullity reaches three, explicit cubic perturbations change it. The same proof gives an exact all-field rank polynomial for the entire augmentation square of \(k[(C_2)^4]\), with ranks three and five absent.
