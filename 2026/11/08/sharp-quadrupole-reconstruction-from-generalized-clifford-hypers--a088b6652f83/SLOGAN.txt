For every \(m\ge2\), restricting all degree-two harmonics on \(S^{2m-1}\) to a single generalized Clifford hypersurface is optimally conditioned precisely at the two squared radii \(\sqrt{m+2}/[2(1+\sqrt{m+2})]\) and its complement. The sharp condition number is \(1+2/\sqrt{m+2}\), and the same radii maximize the smallest singular value, while the balanced product sphere is singular.
