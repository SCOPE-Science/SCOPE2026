At an antipodal cross-polytope, a natural real spherical two-design loss has exactly two positive Hessian bands plus extra nonrotational flat modes, with uniquely balanced positive curvature at \(a/b=4/(d-1)\). Every symmetric-pair flat velocity has a sharp fourth-order residual coefficient \(b\sum_j D_j^2\): some modes give genuine exact design deformations, while others incur an unavoidable, explicitly computable quartic penalty.
