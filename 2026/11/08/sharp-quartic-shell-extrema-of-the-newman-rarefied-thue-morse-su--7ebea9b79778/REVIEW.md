# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The four-block recurrence is derived by binary digit-parity cancellation. The simultaneous induction proves the unique minimum, next-smallest value, global shell maximum, and explicit attaining index at all exponents. The endpoint of the odd sign obstruction is treated separately. Exact executable checks through six shells independently support the algebra; no infinite assertion depends on sampling.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Coquet's earlier exact fractal fluctuation formula is a potentially broader implicit description; it might yield related consequences after an additional extremum analysis. The Coquet full text and the weighted extension were not accessible for complete direct comparison, so priority remains conditional on that material and unindexed work. Indexed similarity searches gave no direct match.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result determines a canonical finite-scale invariant of a classical digital-discrepancy sequence across every scale, with equality cases and a self-contained radix-four argument. It is a substantive exact classification of shell extrema, not merely a routine substitution or a bounded computation.

## Scientific limitations
The theorem is specific to the ordinary digit-parity sum on multiples of three, does not list every maximizing index, and has not undergone independent scientific audit. Older exact-sum formulae may conceal equivalent sharp consequences.
