For the Newman sum \(F(N)=\sum_{j<N}(-1)^{s_2(3j)}\), each quartic shell \(4^k\le N<4^{k+1}\) has exact minimum \(4\cdot3^{k-1}\), attained uniquely at \(4^k\), and exact maximum \(4\cdot3^k\), attained at \((10\cdot4^k+2)/3\). A four-block recurrence gives a direct all-index proof and uniquely identifies the next-to-minimal value.
