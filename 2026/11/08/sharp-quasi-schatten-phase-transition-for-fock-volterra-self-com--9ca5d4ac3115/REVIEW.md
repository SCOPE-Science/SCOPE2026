# Scientific assessment: sharp quasi-Schatten phase transition for Fock Volterra self-commutators

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact two-diagonal monomial action of the bounded Volterra operator yields the full tridiagonal self-commutator, with special care for the vacuum index. Both coefficient sequences are absolutely summable, so the trace is legitimate; a finite telescoping identity gives its value. Three-term singular-number inequalities prove the global upper rate, while a sparse two-subspace compression gives the matching lower rate in the mixed case. Diagonal rearrangement settles the pure cases. Disjoint two-coordinate tests prove infinite positive and negative inertia when both coefficients are nonzero. Exact matrix checks and rational trace calculations independently corroborate finite instances; no finite experiment is presented as a proof of an infinite limit.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The source proves essential normality irrespective of the linear coefficient; the result measures the finer defect of normality quantitatively. It identifies a sharp change of singular-value decay and of the critical quasi-Schatten exponent between pure and mixed symbols, despite universal trace-class membership. The trace being independent of the linear coefficient and the mixed commutator having infinite inertia of both signs reveal complementary spectral structure. The family is the entire naturally bounded Volterra class on classical Fock space, rather than an arbitrary isolated parameter slice. Limitations: no leading singular-value coefficient in the mixed case, no extension to other Fock weights, and no independent audit.
