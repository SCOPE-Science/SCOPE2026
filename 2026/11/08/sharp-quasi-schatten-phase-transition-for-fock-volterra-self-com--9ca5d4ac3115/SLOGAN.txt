For Gaussian Fock-space Volterra operators with \(g(z)=az^2+bz+c\), every self-commutator is trace class with trace \(8|a|^2\), but a nonzero mixed linear–quadratic symbol changes its sharp singular-value order from \(j^{-2}\) to \(j^{-3/2}\). The result determines every quasi-Schatten and weak-endpoint threshold and shows that the mixed regime has infinitely many positive and negative eigenvalues.
