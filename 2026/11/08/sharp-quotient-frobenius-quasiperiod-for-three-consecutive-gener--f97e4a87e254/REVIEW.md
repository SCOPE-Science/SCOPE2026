# Self-review

## Correctness: PASS

For each integer \(p\ge2\) and \(a\ge2p+4\), the proof identifies the complete disjoint gap intervals of \(\langle a,a+1,a+2\rangle\). It then finds the unique highest interval containing a multiple of \(p\) and obtains the exact quotient Frobenius number. To prove the minimal period, it analyzes the ordered pair of linear and constant branch coefficients, proves an exact two-residue exceptional signature, and rules out its only possible nontrivial translational symmetry. Independent coin-closure dynamic programming checks 5,000 parameter pairs and additional exact branch comparisons for 119 moduli. Finite checks support rather than replace the proof. A mathematical gap or computational error would invalidate this status; independent audit remains necessary.

## Originality: PASS, qualified

Adeniran et al. (arXiv:1809.09360) and Liu (arXiv:2312.06096; 2604.26415) establish relevant formulas under \(p\mid a\), a proper subset of the new domain. Liu (arXiv:2607.23076) treats only two generators. Genus computations and two-generator Frobenius formulas do not determine the three-generator quotient Frobenius branches. Explicit comparisons found no result covering the unrestricted formula and sharp period \(2p\). Earlier divisor cases are acknowledged, and unindexed equivalents remain a residual historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The exact quotient Frobenius function for a canonical three-generator progression and its least eventual quasipolynomial period are natural invariants of interest in numerical semigroup arithmetic. The result uniformly covers all quotient moduli and residue classes, identifying sharp period noncollapse rather than merely observing periodicity. It is not a statement for arbitrary progressions, and its onset \(a\ge2p+4\) is sufficient but not claimed optimal.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
