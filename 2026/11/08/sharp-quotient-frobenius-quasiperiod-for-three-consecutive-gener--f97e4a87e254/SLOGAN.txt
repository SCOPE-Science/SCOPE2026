For the quotient of \(\langle a,a+1,a+2\rangle\) by any fixed integer \(p\ge2\), the Frobenius number is an explicit quadratic quasipolynomial for every \(a\ge2p+4\). Its **least eventual period is exactly \(2p\)**, established by an exact characterization of the top gap interval containing a multiple of \(p\).
