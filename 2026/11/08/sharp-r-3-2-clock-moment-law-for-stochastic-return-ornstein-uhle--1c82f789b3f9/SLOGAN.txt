For a one-dimensional Ornstein–Uhlenbeck search with stochastic finite-time returns, arbitrary fast reset clocks with finite second moment have a universal \(r^{-3/2}\) correction whose coefficient depends only on \(\mathbb E[W^2]/\mathbb E[\sqrt W]\). At equal mean waiting time, Poisson and deterministic clocks have correction magnitudes in the exact ratio \(4/\sqrt\pi\), while equal search and return stiffness makes the completion mean exactly independent of the clock.
