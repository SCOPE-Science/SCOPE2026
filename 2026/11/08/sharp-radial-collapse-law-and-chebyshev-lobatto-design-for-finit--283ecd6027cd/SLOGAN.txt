For full finite-dimensional Hermitian coherent-state tomography, circles whose squared radii coalesce at spacing \(\varepsilon\) have a sharp least singular value proportional to \(\varepsilon^{n-1}\). The unique fixed-radius design minimizing the leading conditioning coefficient uses Chebyshev–Lobatto offsets with endpoint-half weights; the scalar Chebyshev principle is classical, but its exact Hermitian Fock-space reduction and coefficient are the stated result.
