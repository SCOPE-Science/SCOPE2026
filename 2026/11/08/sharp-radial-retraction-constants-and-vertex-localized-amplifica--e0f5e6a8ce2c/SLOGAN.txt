For the canonical radial retraction on a real regular even-\(N\)-gonal Banach plane, the sharp Lipschitz constant equals \(\sec^2(\pi/N)\) when \(4\mid N\) and the already-known nonexpansive constant \(1\) when \(N\equiv2\pmod4\). On the expansive branch the worst derivatives localize to polygon vertices with perpendicular vertex inputs, and no pair of distinct points attains the constant.
