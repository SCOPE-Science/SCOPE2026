For independent uniform-area points in a truncated curvature-\(-1\) hyperbolic cusp of horocycle length \(w\), the intrinsic-distance tail is \(e^{-R}+(w^2/24)e^{-3R}+O_w(e^{-5R})\): the universal leading tail hides the cusp width, but the first correction recovers it exactly. The entire distance law is strictly stochastically increasing with \(w\), and its exponential moments have the sharp threshold \(p=1\).
