For a uniformly unknown single jump, the finest-grid trapezoid is the unique minimum-MSE estimator from its sampled step values, even against nonlinear estimators. Standard integer-ratio Romberg extrapolation has an exactly computable phase-variance penalty that grows strictly with every depth and approaches about \(1.625991555\) times the finest-trapezoid MSE in the dyadic case; this comparison holds at the same finest-grid resolution, not at fixed total work.
