For covariance after \(K\) Landweber steps, keeping the largest singular modes of the forward operator is at most \(M_K\) times worse in operator norm than the best covariance rank truncation, and this factor is exactly sharp in every retained rank. The optimal universal factor obeys \(M_K\sim0.407264377589\,K\), attained by an explicit two-level singular spectrum; the theorem does not judge residual-corrected uncertainty estimators.
