The vertex set of every hypersimplex \(P_{n,k}\) has exact Schneider convexification index \(\max\{k,n-k\}\), with independent rank and corank obstructions. For each smoothing parameter \(\lambda\ge1\), membership in the vertex-generated cover is characterized by just the \(k\)th and \((k+1)\)st largest coordinates, giving the complete two-threshold coverage profile.
