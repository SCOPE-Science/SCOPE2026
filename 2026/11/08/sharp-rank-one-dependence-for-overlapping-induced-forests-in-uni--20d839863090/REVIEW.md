The derivation uses the weighted matrix-tree theorem with the two clique Laplacians \(aP_A\) and \(bP_B\) acting on the all-ones orthogonal complement. The one-vertex intersection makes the two projection spaces orthogonal except in a two-dimensional plane, whose determinant supplies exactly the cross term \(-\delta(z-1)(w-1)\). A genuine bivariate Bernoulli probability table exists for every admissible \((n,a,b)\); coefficient extraction produces the signed residual, telescoping produces all strict tail gaps, and binomial unimodality supplies the exact total variation formula. Standard Stirling asymptotics give the stated sharp constant with all ranges specified. An independent exhaustive Prüfer-sequence checker passed 777 exact rational assertions for every admissible size through seven vertices. These tests corroborate the proof, rather than substitute for its unbounded quantifiers.

## Originality (O): PASS, qualified

Dudley Stark's *The Edge Correlation of Random Forests* (2011) was examined through the full-text introduction and Theorems 1–2: it gives adjacent/disjoint individual-edge correlations and disjoint-edge independence, not the distribution of two intersecting induced-edge counts. The full text of Hladký–Nachmias–Tran, *The local limit of the uniform spanning tree on dense graphs* (arXiv 1711.09788, initially 2017), addresses local limits, not this exact finite two-subset law. Dong–Ge (arXiv 2103.05294, 2021) and the earlier Moon containment formula concern prescribed-forest containment counts, not the signed binomial factorization or the total variation constant. General transfer-current determinantal formulas provide the analytical method; we do not claim those as new. Relevant published mathematical records were also compared by meaning; no matching dominating statement surfaced. Search absence cannot establish uniqueness, and unexamined literature or an inaccessible 2018 related article may affect historical priority.

## Value (V): PASS

The natural overlap condition produces a complete, nontrivial multivariate distribution law. The fact that two substantial induced forests are coupled through only one Bernoulli pair—despite encompassing many mutually dependent tree edges—is structural information not captured by pairwise edge correlation. Exact negative quadrant dependence at every nontrivial pair of thresholds and sharp \(n^{-1}\) total-variation independence calibration offer useful finite-sample quantitative descriptions of overlapping graph subsampling. This is a complete theorem on an infinite family, not an arbitrary small enumeration or a merely renamed identity.
