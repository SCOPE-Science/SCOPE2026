Two induced-edge counts of a uniform Cayley tree, on vertex sets meeting at one vertex, differ from independent binomials by exactly one negatively coupled Bernoulli pair. Their full signed joint error factorizes, and the joint-to-product total variation distance is exactly \(2\delta\max f\max g\), with sharp macroscopic asymptotic \(1/(\pi n\sqrt{(1-\alpha)(1-\beta)})\).
