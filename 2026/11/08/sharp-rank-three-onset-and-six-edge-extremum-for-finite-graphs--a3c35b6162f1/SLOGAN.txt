The one-point-extension rank of a finite simple graph first reaches three on seven vertices, and six edges suffice and are necessary; the six-edge extremal graph is unique up to isomorphism, giving exactly \(n!/(n-6)!\) labelled extremizers on \(n\ge7\) vertices. This sharp witness improves the known universal three-rank forcing bound to \(6\le N(3)\le7\), without settling the remaining one-unit gap.
