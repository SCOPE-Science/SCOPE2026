For \(k\) independent tournament relations, rank-two first-order logic sees exactly the set of local orientation-signature profiles; the generic theory has a smallest model of \(2^k+1\) vertices. In random models, vertex failures are pairwise independent and their number has a sharp Poisson window at \(n/2^k=2\log(2^k)+\log\log(2^k)+c\), yielding limiting saturation probability \(\exp(-2e^{-c})\).
