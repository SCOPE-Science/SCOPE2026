For \(r\) independent uniform equivalence relations, every rare assertion of an \(m\)-point common class has sharp probability \(W(n)^{r(m-1)}/(m!n^{r(m-1)-m})\) when \(r(m-1)>m\). Conditioned on its occurrence, exactly one class has size \(m\), and its location is uniform; the result refines the known qualitative partition-meet laws.
