A stationary switching harmonic gas has an exact empirical quadratic-energy rate with zero cost on its entire latent-variance interval, but two exponentially rare outer tails whose algebraic prefactors separately identify the switching rates. The two finite-size energy edges have sharp \(N^{-1/2}\) Gaussian-smoothed power-law profiles, with density-height transitions at \(r_1=2\mu_1\) and \(r_2=2\mu_2\).
