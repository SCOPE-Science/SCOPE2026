Uniform-edge greedy matching on the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M_n\) fails only by leaving the two copies of one label unmatched, and its failure probability has the sharp expansion \(p_n=2/(n+1)-4\log n/[n(n-1)]+O(n^{-2})\). An exact overlap-state recurrence and a two-label survival argument explain the logarithmic rare-failure correction.
