For three independently surface-uniform points on \(S^{d-1}\), the obtuse-triangle probability is \(\frac{27\sqrt6}{16\sqrt{\pi d}}(16/27)^{d/2}(1+O(d^{-1}))\), with a rare-event saddle at the non-typical dot product \(1/3\). Conditioned on obtusity, the triangle tends to a right isosceles shape, and that dot product has a \(\sqrt d\)-scale Gaussian fluctuation of variance \(16/27\).
