Among uniformly labeled \(n\)-dimensional perfect evolution algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\), nontrivial automorphisms have probability \(\binom n2 2^{1-2n}(1+o(1))\), and a symmetry is almost surely a unique transposition. The same sharp transposition contribution is the first relative correction to the isomorphism-class count \(|\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb F_2)|/n!\), distinguishing invertible matrices from unrestricted binary directed graphs by a factor of two.
