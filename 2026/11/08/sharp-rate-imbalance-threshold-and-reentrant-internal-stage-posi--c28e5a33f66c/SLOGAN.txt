For the two-stage fourth-order Gauss–Legendre method on any two-state continuous-time Markov chain, the output is a strictly positive probability vector at every step size, but the second internal stage can become negative. A complete rate-imbalance classification has sharp cutoff \(r_*=\tfrac12(4+2\sqrt3+\sqrt{(4+2\sqrt3)^2+4})\): beyond it, stage positivity fails on exactly one bounded step-size interval and then returns.
