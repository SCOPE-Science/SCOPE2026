For two agents who strictly prefer the same one of two recurring goods, simultaneous per-round EF1, overall envy-freeness and unrestricted Pareto efficiency survives exactly up to a sharp rational-exchange horizon, with \(\binom{2m}{m}\) feasible sequences at surviving even horizons. Integer valuations can delay the first conflict arbitrarily, but the smaller of the two reduced numerators gives a sharp universal upper bound.
