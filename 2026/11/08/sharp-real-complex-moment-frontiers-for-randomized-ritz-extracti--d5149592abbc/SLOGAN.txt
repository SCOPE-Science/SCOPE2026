On a canonical three-dimensional Hermitian interior eigenproblem, square randomized Ritz extraction has an exact Gaussian-ratio first-order error law: the normalized eigenvector inflation is Pareto with exponent two for complex testing, but has exponent one for real testing. Consequently, the complex method's high-probability linear eigenvector and quadratic refined-eigenvalue rates cannot be upgraded to uniform mean-square rates, even on this fixed example.
