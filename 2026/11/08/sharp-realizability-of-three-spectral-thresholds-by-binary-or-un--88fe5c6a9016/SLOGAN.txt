Every ordered triple of binary/unary tree branching exponents \(0\le a\le c\le b\le1\), with \(b>0\), is realized as the surjectivity, point-spectrum, and full-spectrum thresholds \(2^{a/2}\), \(2^{c/2}\), and \(2^{b/2}\). In particular, an entire annulus can consist of infinite-multiplicity eigenvalues whose shifted tree operators are nevertheless not onto.
