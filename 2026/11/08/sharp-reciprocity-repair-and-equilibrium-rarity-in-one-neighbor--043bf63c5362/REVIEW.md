# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For binary specialized actions, the strict-concavity best-response equations reduce exactly to the directed neighborhood condition. Following unique outgoing edges proves the parity and multiplicity law. A reciprocity addition can relax an odd cycle only when its new tail is on that cycle, giving a per-component lower bound. Every such reverse arc (originating on the cycle or at a direct child) has an explicit alternating solution and uniquely completed incoming trees, giving the matching construction and product count. Species decomposition, Lagrange inversion, singular transfer and Stirling asymptotics were reconstructed symbolically. The executable independent enumeration checked all mappings through six vertices, their specialized equilibrium counts and rational formulas, and all 33,840 individual single-repair candidates in the eligible cases at size six. Limit: finite tests do not establish the infinite claims; the written arguments do.

## Originality — PASS (with residual uncertainty)
The closest motivating primary paper is Huang, Sun, Xu and Zhao, arXiv:2512.23193v1 (2025), reviewed in its model, kernel theorem, reciprocity proposition and algorithm sections. Its general specialized-equilibrium/kernel equivalence and monotonicity under reciprocity are explicitly credited rather than claimed. Neither the stated minimum-repair count, classification of all repairing reverse arcs, nor the loopless random-map exponent and prefactor is supplied there in the inspected material. Kempe, Yu and Vorobeychik (arXiv:2002.10627) study a broader network modification problem but not this full structural minimal reciprocity classification; Deligkas et al. (arXiv:2511.11475v1) study an indivisible-share capacity model. Random-mapping generating functions and transfer theorems are acknowledged prior machinery rather than new claims. Semantic searches across research records and accessible mathematical research records did not find an implication-equivalent claim, including reverse-edge repair synonyms and odd-functional-cycle conditions. Residual risks are unindexed digraph-kernel repair literature and implicit consequences of classical functional graph results. These searches do not constitute a mathematical novelty guarantee.

## Value — PASS
The first obstruction for a specialist equilibrium in the simplest genuinely directed public-goods networks admits a complete and usable policy repair rule: the smallest intervention cost, all admissible individual interventions, and the exact number of optimal repairs are explicit for every network size. The random law quantitatively distinguishes near-certain existence in dense random models from polynomially rare specialized equilibria in sparse choice networks, while demonstrating that rare configurations can still contribute a nonzero expected equilibrium count. This is a structural and decision-relevant classification rather than a single arbitrary finite count or a restatement of the kernel reduction.
