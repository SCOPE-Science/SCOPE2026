For a real bilinear Rademacher chaos on an unequal rectangular grid with one missing corner, the exact fourth-moment constant is \(\left(3-2/(\max\{m,n\}-1)\right)\left(3-2/\min\{m,n\}\right)\). Every extremizer discards the damaged row or column entirely and is a flat row-column sign product on the remaining complete rectangle, in contrast with the distinct square-grid extremizers.
