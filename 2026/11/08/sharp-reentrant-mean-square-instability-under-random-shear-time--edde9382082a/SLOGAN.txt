For an unbiased randomized two-shear time splitting of a strictly dissipative planar generator, the exact mean-square growth factor is \(e^{-2ah}(1+k^2h^2/2)\), creating a unique reentrant instability interval precisely when \(k/a>3.514699\ldots\). Whenever \(3.514699\ldots<k/a<4.970535\ldots\), the mean iterate decays at every step size despite geometric growth of the expected squared norm on an open interval of step sizes.
