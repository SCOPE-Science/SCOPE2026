At odd indices, the semi-Pell to semi-Fibonacci ratio is \(N^{1+o(1)}\), even though both sequence values have the same leading \((\log N)^2/(2\log 2)\) logarithmic growth. A typical odd-multiplicity binary partition occupies nearly every available power-of-two level, so optional marking of its levels contributes almost one full factor of \(N\) on the logarithmic scale.
