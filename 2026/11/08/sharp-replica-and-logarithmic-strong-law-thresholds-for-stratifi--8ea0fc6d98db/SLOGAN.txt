For the median of \(2r-1\) independently redrawn, one-point-per-stratum estimates of \(x^{-\alpha}[\log(e/x)]^{-\beta}\), almost-sure convergence holds exactly for \(\alpha<r/(r+1)\), or on the critical line when \(\beta>1/(r+1)\). Outside that region the estimates still converge in mean but have almost-sure liminf equal to the integral and limsup \(+\infty\); an exact fixed-error tail law gives the sharp replica budget and logarithmic boundary.
