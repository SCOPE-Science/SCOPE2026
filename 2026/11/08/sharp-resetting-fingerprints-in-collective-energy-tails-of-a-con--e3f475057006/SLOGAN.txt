For a harmonically confined Brownian gas with simultaneous Poisson resetting, the collective mean-square displacement has a critical tail \(\Pr\{Q_N> D/\mu\}\sim 2^{b-1}\Gamma((b+1)/2)N^{-b/2}/\sqrt\pi\), where \(b=r/(2\mu)\). Above the equilibrium energy, the exponential large-deviation rate is independent of the resetting frequency, but the exact prefactor is proportional to \(N^{-b-1/2}\); a Gaussian-tail crossover connects the two regimes.
