# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The Laplace-transform continuant recurrence and renewal identity give the exact mean, the binomial-coefficient identity yields a polynomial with precisely one sign change for every \(L\ge4\), and the hyperbolic representation proves convergence of optimal rates. An independent finite-state linear-algebra calculation and coefficient checks are included. The result does not assert finite-size optimizer uniqueness.

**Originality: PASS on inspected evidence, with residual priority risk.** Evans–Majumdar establish resetting and optimization for continuous diffusion. Christou–Schadschneider treat bounded continuous diffusion but their complete text was not accessible. Bonomo–Pal analyze discrete-time restart in a two-absorbing-boundary geometry, and Das–Giuggioli provide general lattice renewal methods including reflecting geometries. General resetting formulas encompass the calculation of a mean, but the examined sources do not state the exact one-positive-root sign theorem for this mixed-boundary continuous-time lattice model, the five-vertex onset, or the sharp infinite-volume optimizer. Unindexed specializations could predate this finding.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** The theorem resolves a natural finite-size boundary for when adding a memoryless restart mechanism helps rather than hurts a confined search, for every positive restart rate and every finite interval length in a clearly normalized model. The uniqueness of the improvement-to-detriment crossover and exact asymptotic optimal rate sharpen generic resetting criteria into a structural transport classification.
