For a continuous-time reflecting lattice walk started two sites from an absorbing target, Poisson restarting is beneficial at some positive rate exactly when there are at least five vertices; for every such finite path, the beneficial rates form one explicit polynomially defined interval. The optimal rates converge to \(2(\sqrt2-1)\), and the optimal expected absorption time converges to \(3+2\sqrt2\) in the unit-edge-rate normalization.
