For every base \(b\ge2\) and modulus \(m\ge3\), the ordinary-reversal quotient of the generalized Thue–Morse factor language equals ordinary factor complexity at every length \(n\ge3\), and no length-four factor coexists with its reverse. The first length with no reversible factors is exactly \(2\) if \(\gcd(b-1,m)\ge3\) and \(4\) otherwise; the short-length reflection counts have explicit gcd-dependent formulas.
