For classical RK4 heat diffusion on the triangular graphs \(T_n=L(K_n)\), endpoint positivity holds exactly up to the unique positive root of an explicit cubic, and this cutoff is strictly smaller than the spectral-stability cutoff for every \(n\ge5\). The positivity-to-stability timestep ratio converges to \(0.7460138712\ldots\), exposing an asymptotic 25.4% stable-but-nonpositive window.
