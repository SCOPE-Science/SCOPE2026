For every unweighted connected complete multipartite heat network with \(N\) vertices, the classical RK4 end-step is entrywise nonnegative exactly when \(0\le h\le\beta/N\), where \(\beta=2.785293563405\ldots\) is the negative-real-axis RK4 stability radius. This sharp horizon is independent of the part sizes and coincides with spectral absolute stability, even though unrestricted fourth-order Runge–Kutta schemes cannot preserve positivity universally.
