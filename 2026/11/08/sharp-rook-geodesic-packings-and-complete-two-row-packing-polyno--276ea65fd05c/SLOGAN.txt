For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), exactly \(\lfloor mn/3\rfloor\) pairwise disjoint maximal geodesics can be packed. On two-row rook graphs, explicit formulas enumerate **every** geodesic packing and every inclusion-maximal packing, with maximal sizes filling \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) through \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\) and a three-case factorial count of maximum configurations.
