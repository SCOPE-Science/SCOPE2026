In a Hoppe recursive tree, the covariance between the root's branch count and the total number of leaves is exactly \(\theta(n-1)(n-2)/[2(\theta+n-2)^2]\). For every \(n\ge3\), it is uniquely maximized at root weight \(\theta=n-2\), with value \((n-1)/8\), revealing an order-\(n\) dependence peak that disappears in the usual fixed-weight limit.
