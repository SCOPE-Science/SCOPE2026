For ideal Gaussian Hamiltonian Monte Carlo with integer modal frequencies \(1,\ldots,K\) and mean trajectory duration \(\pi/2\), uniform sampling of the \(K\) nonzero \((K+1)\)-st root-of-unity times minimizes worst-case stationary integrated autocorrelation across every centered quadratic observable, with exact optimum \((3K-1)/(K+1)\). All minimizing laws admit an explicit antisymmetric mass-perturbation classification: the minimizer is unique for \(K\le3\) and nonunique for every \(K\ge4\).
