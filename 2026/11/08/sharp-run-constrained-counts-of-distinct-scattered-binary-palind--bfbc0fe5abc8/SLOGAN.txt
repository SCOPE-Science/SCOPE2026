For binary length-\(n\) words with exactly \(2k+1\) constant-symbol runs, the maximum number of distinct nonempty palindromic scattered subsequences is \(n^{k+1}/[2^k(k+1)^{k+1}]+O_k(n^k)\) for every fixed \(k\ge1\). Remarkably, exactly three and exactly four runs have the same sharp maximum, \(n+\lfloor n^2/8\rfloor\), at every length where both are possible.
