For a uniformly sampled Euclidean rectangle’s four corners, the sharp scalar squared-variance Lipschitz constant is \( [a^2+(\sqrt{a^2+b^2}-a)^2]/4 \) for long side \(a\ge b>0\), whereas every Hilbert target of dimension at least two has constant \( (a^2+b^2)/4 \). All extremizers are affine in the two corner signs, and the relative scalar–Hilbert deficit is uniquely greatest for a square.
