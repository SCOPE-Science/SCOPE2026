For the unmodified ARS(4,4,3) explicit–implicit–null step on positive scalar diffusion, completed-step nonnegativity at **every** step size holds exactly when the physical-to-stabilizer ratio satisfies \(a/a_0\le\rho\), where \(7\rho^3+36\rho^2-90\rho+18=0\) and \(\rho\approx0.2202315743\). Above this boundary a positive one-node heat value can turn negative, even though a negative step multiplier need not violate energy contraction.
