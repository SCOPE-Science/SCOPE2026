# Scientific review: looped complete-bipartite Schur projections

## Correctness
PASS. The underlying Hermitian Schur-norm order-interval theorem is established in Christensen's Theorem 2.2 and is also derived from the Haagerup factorization in the proof. The proposed positive order majorant has exact common diagonal \(1+v\); its negative difference is a rank-one positive matrix and its positive difference has a rank-two constant-block reduction with determinant exactly zero. The lower bound averages every feasible majorant over within-part permutations and reduces the constraints to two intersecting intervals. The squared inequality has nonnegative sides in the relevant range and forces the same \(v\). All part sizes are quantified by a symbolic identity, with rational arithmetic checks only supplemental. Risk: none identified in the stated finite-dimensional setting; the source theorem is an established functional-analytic fact.

## Originality
PASS, with priority risk. The complete unequal-part two-parameter formula is the claim assessed. A singleton part specializes to an already known star example and is expressly not new. Balanced equal-part patterns are transitive under permutations and are already covered by the general Davidson--Donsig/Mathias trace theorem. Their transitive reduction does not apply when \(a\ne b\), because vertices have distinct degrees and belong to distinct orbits. Levene's inspected gap theorems and path computations do not state the unequal-part formula. The 2026 tree-Schur paper gives uniform interior-\(p\) control and not these endpoint exact norms. The relevant 2025 positive-order criterion supplies the tool but not the two-parameter optimization. Semantic searches for exact complete-bipartite Schur projection constants, equivalent \(\gamma_2\) norms and aliases, and comparison with accessible previously established statements yielded no direct covering assertion. A general classical formula for graph operator-system projectors could in principle imply the result; this priority risk remains. No independent novelty audit is asserted.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This solves the exact Schur projection cost for the natural two-parameter family of looped complete bipartite graphs, including all unequal part sizes, and locates a structural distinction between one-sided degree growth and simultaneous two-sided growth: fixed-part limit \((4a+1)/(2a+1)\) versus joint limit \(2\). It is not a one-dimensional arbitrary slice or a mere normalized restatement of a known table entry. The special star case is already known and its rederivation is not counted as the contribution. The classification concerns a natural graph/operator invariant and makes no unsupported claims about nonlinear or general graph classes.

## Scope and closest literature
The strongest directly applicable ingredient is Christensen's 2025 self-adjoint Schur-norm order formula (arXiv:2505.24670, Theorem 2.2). Davidson--Donsig (2005, Theorem 4.4) covers the *balanced* transitive case; it does not provide the *unequal* case. Levene (2014) covers low-norm gaps and path patterns. Arhancet (2026) and González-Pérez et al. (2026) motivate current Schur-multiplier norm questions but address other classes. Exact classification beyond the complete bipartite looped family and intermediate Schatten exponents is not proved.
