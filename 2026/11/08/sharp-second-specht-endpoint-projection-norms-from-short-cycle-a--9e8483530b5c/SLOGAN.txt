For the complete \((n-2,2)\) Specht matrix-coefficient space in \(C(S_n)\), the minimum supremum projection norm is exactly \(n(n-3)\bigl(q_{n-1}+q_{n-2}/2\bigr)\), where \(q_m\) is the probability that a permutation has neither fixed points nor two-cycles. Its quadratic growth constant is \(3/(2e^{3/2})\), and norming functions are precisely the signs of translates of the corresponding irreducible character on its nonzero support.
