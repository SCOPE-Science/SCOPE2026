# Sharp seven-vertex obstruction band for three-cut complexes of cycle powers

## Finding
Let \(p\ge2\) and \(n\) be integers. A cyclic-order-preserving induced copy of the squared seven-cycle \(C_7^2\) exists in the power \(C_n^p\) **if and only if**
\[
\left\lceil\frac{7(p+1)}3\right\rceil\le n\le\left\lfloor\frac{7p}2\right\rfloor.
\]
For every pair in this interval, the **ordinary** three-cut complex \(\Delta_3(C_n^p)\) is not shellable. The interval is nonempty for every \(p\ge2\), and it is a subrange of the nonshellability conjecture posed for the powers of cycle graphs. The equivalence concerns an embedding respecting the specified cyclic vertex orders, not arbitrary graph embeddings. No claim about shellability outside the interval is made.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph \(C_n^p\) has vertex set \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), with two distinct vertices adjacent exactly when their cyclic distance is at most \(p\). The squared seven-cycle \(C_7^2\) has edges between indices at cyclic index distance one or two. A **cyclic-order-preserving induced copy** is a sequence of distinct vertices \(w_0,\ldots,w_6\) appearing in that cyclic order around \(C_n\) such that the induced graph, with labels \(w_i\leftrightarrow i\), is exactly \(C_7^2\). The ordinary three-cut complex \(\Delta_3(G)\) is generated by the complements of the three-vertex subsets inducing disconnected subgraphs; it is distinct from the total three-cut complex, whose defining triples must be independent. For a graph with at least one disconnected triple the complex is pure, of dimension \(|V(G)|-4\).

## Proof
**1. Necessary inequalities.** Suppose the required induced copy exists. Write \(g_i\ge1\) for the clockwise gaps from \(w_i\) to \(w_{i+1}\), with cyclic indices; then \(\sum_{i=0}^6g_i=n\). Because \(w_i,w_{i+3}\) are nonadjacent in the induced squared seven-cycle, each clockwise three-gap sum \(t_i=g_i+g_{i+1}+g_{i+2}\) lies strictly between \(p\) and \(n-p\). Thus \(t_i\ge p+1\). Summing seven such inequalities gives \(3n\ge7(p+1)\). On the other hand, \(w_i,w_{i+2}\) are adjacent. Their clockwise two-gap sum \(s_i=g_i+g_{i+1}\) is less than \(t_i<n-p\), so their adjacency cannot be explained by the complementary clockwise arc; necessarily \(s_i\le p\). Summing gives \(2n\le7p\). These inequalities are precisely the stated bounds after integer rounding.

**2. Sufficient construction.** Assume the bounds and put
\[
w_j=\left\lfloor\frac{jn}{7}\right\rfloor,\qquad 0\le j\le6,
\]
with the periodic convention \(w_{j+7}=w_j+n\). Because the lower bound is at least seven, these are distinct in increasing cyclic order. Every one- or two-step clockwise gap among them is at most \(\lceil2n/7\rceil\le p\). Each clockwise gap spanning three or four selected steps is at least \(\lfloor3n/7\rfloor\ge p+1\), as is its complementary arc spanning the other four or three selected steps. Therefore pairs with index separation one or two are adjacent, while pairs with separation three or four are nonadjacent. The induced graph is exactly \(C_7^2\). This proves the equivalence.

**3. A known topological obstruction, independently checked.** The seven disconnected vertex triples in \(C_7^2\) are exactly \(\{i,i+3,i+4\}\) for \(i\in\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z\). Consequently \(K=\Delta_3(C_7^2)\) has seven tetrahedral facets and face vector \((7,21,21,7)\). Direct elementary collapses, listed below, reduce \(K\) to the simplicial triangle boundary on vertices \(4,5,6\), hence \(K\simeq S^1\) and \(\widetilde H_1(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\). This agrees with the earlier general result of Bayer, Denker, Jelić Milutinović, Rowlands, Sundaram and Xue, Proposition 7.28, specializing \(\Delta_k(C_{k+4}^2)\simeq S^1\) to \(k=3\). The **seed homotopy type is prior work**, not a newly claimed calculation.

**4. The induced-graph link transfer.** Let \(G\) be a finite simple graph and \(W\subseteq V(G)\) induce a graph containing a disconnected triple. Put \(\sigma=V(G)\setminus W\). This is a face of \(\Delta_3(G)\), and its link has facets exactly \(W\setminus T\), where \(T\subset W\) ranges over disconnected triples of \(G[W]\). Therefore
\[
\operatorname{lk}_{\Delta_3(G)}(\sigma)=\Delta_3(G[W])
\]
as simplicial complexes on \(W\). In the constructed embedding, \(G[W]\cong C_7^2\), so the link is \(K\). Since all facets of \(K\) have dimension three but \(\widetilde H_1(K;\mathbb Z)\ne0\), \(K\) is not shellable: a nonempty pure shellable three-dimensional complex has the homotopy type of a wedge of three-spheres, possibly the trivial wedge. Every link of a shellable pure simplicial complex is shellable. The ambient ordinary three-cut complex is therefore not shellable.

## Verification
The accompanying standalone Python 3 program `verify.py` uses only the standard library. It enumerates every disconnected triple of the seven-vertex seed, checks its seven tetrahedral facets and entire face vector, certifies each of the 25 free-face collapses by recomputing unique cofaces, tests the necessity and sufficiency of the embedding interval by exhaustive seven-subset search for \(2\le p\le4\), and checks the exact induced adjacency for all permitted integer pairs with \(2\le p\le100\). For \(2\le p\le6\), it independently enumerates disconnected triples in each ambient power graph and compares the actual link facets with the seven-vertex seed facets. Recorded output is in `verification_output.txt`. These finite tests are checks of the proof, not the justification for an unrestricted parameter theorem.

## Relationship to prior work
Bayer et al., *Topology of Cut Complexes of Graphs* (initial public preprint 2023), Proposition 7.28, already determine \(\Delta_3(C_7^2)\simeq S^1\), and Proposition 7.29 gives its minimal forbidden-induced-subgraph property. Chauhan, Shukla and Vinayak, *3-Cut Complexes of Squared Cycle Graphs* (2024), establish shellability for the squared cycle when \(n\ge9\). Chauhan and Shukla, *Shellability of 3-cut complexes of powers of cycle graphs* (initial public preprint 2026-03-24), establish shellability in the much larger range \(n\ge6p-3\) and explicitly recall the conjecture of nonshellability when \(2p+3\le n\le4p\). The contribution here is the **sharp cyclic-order seven-vertex embedding interval** and the resulting **all-parameter nonshellability subrange**; neither the seven-vertex seed nor the general link principle is presented as new. The seven-vertex bound is distinct from a previously analyzed eight-vertex obstruction interval, which begins at a larger lower threshold and does not cover, for example, \((p,n)=(2,7)\).

## Limitations
The interval is sharp only for cyclic-order-preserving induced copies of \(C_7^2\). It is not claimed to be optimal among all induced copies or all topological obstructions. Shellability outside it, including the remaining shorter-cycle conjectural range, is not decided. The result is for ordinary, rather than total, three-cut complexes.
