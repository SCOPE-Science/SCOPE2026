For every integer \(p\ge2\), a cyclic-order-preserving induced \(C_7^2\) occurs in \(C_n^p\) exactly when \(\lceil7(p+1)/3\rceil\le n\le\lfloor7p/2\rfloor\). Throughout this sharp seven-vertex obstruction interval, the ordinary three-cut complex is nonshellable, proving a new infinite subrange of a published conjecture.
