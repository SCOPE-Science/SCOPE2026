The regular truncated octahedron has precisely 12 least-bright directions with shadow area \(8\sqrt2\), but its 24 most-bright directions point obliquely in the ratio \(7:1:1\) and have area \(2\sqrt{51}\). Its projection body is a seven-generator zonotope of exact volume \(8128\) in the edge-length-\(\sqrt2\) normalization.
