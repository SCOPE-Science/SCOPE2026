The odd-entry count \(F(n)=2^{s_2(n)}\) of the \(n\)-th binary Pascal row has rational limiting cyclic Pearson autocorrelation at every fixed shift, computable by two explicit carry matrices. Across all nonzero shifts the sharp maximum is \(2/3\), attained exactly at powers of two, while alternating binary shifts make the correlation exponentially small with infimum zero.
