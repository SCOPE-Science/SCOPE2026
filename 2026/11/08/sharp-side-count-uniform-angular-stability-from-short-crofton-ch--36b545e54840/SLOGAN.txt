For every convex \(m\)-gon, the initial Crofton random-chord law is exactly linear and its perimeter-rescaled slope is the sum of \(\frac12(1-\alpha\cot\alpha)\) over exterior angles. Above the equiangular minimum, this slope has a sharp, side-count-uniform quadratic angular deficit with optimal constant \(1/6\), equality only for equiangular polygons.
