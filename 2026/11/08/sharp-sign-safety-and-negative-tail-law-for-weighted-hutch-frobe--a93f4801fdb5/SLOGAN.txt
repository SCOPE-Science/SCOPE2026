For the weighted Hutch# Gaussian Frobenius estimator, nonnegativity for every matrix and every sketch holds only at zero mixture weight: every positive correction weight admits negative squared-norm estimates. For rank-one matrices we determine the exact negative-probability integral and sharp exponential prefactor, showing that the source paper's variance-optimal oracle weight reduces negative-output probability by a factor approaching \(e^{-2}\) relative to unweighted Hutch#.
