For the partial permutohedron \(\mathcal P(m,n)\), every subset-sum truncation leaves the maximum-volume inscribed ellipsoid of its simplex envelope unchanged exactly when \(m=1\) or \(m\ge n\). At the sharp boundary \(m=n\ge2\), the ellipsoid gains \(m\) extra tangent coordinate-cap facets, whereas above the boundary only the \(m+1\) simplex facets touch.
