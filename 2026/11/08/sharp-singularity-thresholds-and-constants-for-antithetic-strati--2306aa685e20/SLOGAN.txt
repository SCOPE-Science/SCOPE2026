For complementary stratified sampling of \(t^\beta\), the exact mean-square error changes regime at \(\beta=3/2\): it is a convergent-series constant times \(n^{-2\beta-2}\) below the threshold, \((\log n)/(1280n^5)\) at the threshold, and an explicit curvature-energy constant times \(n^{-5}\) above it. Independent pairing instead changes regime at \(\beta=1/2\); the complementary coupling minimizes variance among all independent-panel two-sample designs with uniform marginals and equal weights.
