On the four-by-four rook graph, restricting minimum identifying-code replacements to slides along original graph edges deletes 13,824 of the 28,800 previously known jump transitions but preserves connectivity and sharp diameter six. The resulting 2,208-state slide graph has exactly 14,976 edges, with degree spectrum 1,344 states of degree twelve and 864 of degree sixteen; these slide-only invariants are the contribution, not the previously known optimum-code or jump counts.
