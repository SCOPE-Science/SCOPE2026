## Correctness — PASS
The stated two-stage Radau IIA stability function follows directly from its Butcher tableau. Its scalar power series has fifth coefficient \(-1/108\), which is the exact first contribution to a five-edge displacement in every directed cycle with at least six states. Because the cyclic generator is Fourier diagonalizable and the stability function is \(L\)-stable, all transition entries approach \(1/n\) and hence eventually become positive. The finite-dimensional global statements rely on complete integer-polynomial certificates: `certificate.json` provides every entry numerator and shared denominator for sizes two through six; `verify.py` independently checks every cyclic polynomial product identity using exact fractions and verifies all sign reductions. All quantifiers concern the same endpoint matrix and dimensionless steps; no extrapolation from sampled numerical grids is involved.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
Searches compared Radau IIA, \([1/2]\) Padé, circulant and directed-cycle Markov generators, small-step positivity obstructions, and large-step recovery. The closest distinct result is *Eight-state barrier for Markov positivity of the [2/2] Padé map* (2026), which treats a **fourth-order two-stage Gauss–Legendre** map, not the **third-order two-stage Radau IIA** function here. Its eight-state barrier does not imply the six-state Radau IIA barrier, the exact six-cycle recovery root, or the nonmonotone eight-cycle witnesses. Blanes–Iserles–Macnamara (2021, inspected full text) establish the broad graph-Laplacian positivity framework and classical order barriers but not these precise cyclic [1/2] signs. Banjai–Ferrari (2022, inspected full text) concern Gauss convolution quadrature and a different Padé stability function. Munch–Dravins–Kronbichler–Neytcheva (2022, abstract inspected) concern Radau implementation rather than positivity; that abstract alone is not a whole-document exclusion. Web and published-record retrieval do not prove uniqueness; older or unindexed specialized results remain possible, and the referenced [2/2] record's full file was not accessible.

## Value — PASS
A positivity failure **for every sufficiently small step** of an \(L\)-stable, third-order method is a substantive numerical limitation for evolving probability distributions. The dimension-six cutoff is sharp within the canonical family of unit-rate irreducible one-way cycles and has a complete exact recovery threshold. The eight-cycle witnesses show that safe steps need not form a connected region, distinguishing numerical nonnegativity from scalar absolute stability, mass conservation and large-step damping. This is a natural family classification and reproducible test case, not a renamed general order-barrier statement. No implementation guarantee for arbitrary Markov generators follows.
