Two-stage Radau IIA is positivity-preserving on every unit-rate directed cycle with at most five states, yet on every larger directed cycle it has negative transition probabilities for arbitrarily small positive steps even though it recovers strict positivity at large steps. The first failing six-cycle has the exact recovery set \(\{0\}\cup[\rho,\infty)\), with \(\rho\) the unique positive root of \(h^5+15h^4+60h^3+90h^2+36h-12\); an eight-cycle has separated positive-step windows.
