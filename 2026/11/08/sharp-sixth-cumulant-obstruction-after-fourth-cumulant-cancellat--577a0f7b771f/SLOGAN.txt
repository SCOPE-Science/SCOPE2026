For planar run-and-tumble particles with symmetric reorientation, the leading fourth-order displacement cumulant rate can vanish precisely when the first turning harmonic \(m\) lies in \(-3/4\le m<1\); both fourth- and sixth-order long-time rates can vanish precisely for \(-5/7\le m<1\). At the sharp boundary \(m=-5/7\), a unique three-angle turning law realizes both cancellations, while above it the sixth-order rate can have either sign despite exact fourth-order cancellation.
