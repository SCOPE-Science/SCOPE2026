The real sixth-moment ratio of every first-order Haar-unitary matrix coefficient is strictly Schur-concave in its normalized squared singular values: rank-one matrices uniquely minimize it, while scalar unitaries uniquely maximize it. The exact sharp sixth-power \(L^2\)-to-\(L^6\) norm jumps from \(25/2\) at dimension two to the Gaussian value \(15\) in every dimension at least three, with an explicit positive spectral deficit identity.
