Every real linear matrix coefficient on a uniform orthonormal \(k\)-frame in \(\mathbb R^n\), \(n\ge3\), has a sharp sixth-to-second moment ratio minimized exactly by rank-one matrices and maximized exactly by matrices with equal nonzero singular values. Two explicit positive spectral-deficit identities also determine the exact Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance of the whole coefficient space.
