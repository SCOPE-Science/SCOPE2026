Haar quaternionic Stiefel linear statistics admit an exact sixth-moment formula and two positive spectral stability deficits: rank-one coefficient matrices minimize the normalized moment, whereas equal quaternionic singular values maximize it. The resulting real \(L^6\) space has exactly computable Euclidean distortion, with exceptional sixth-trace maximum \(14\) in quaternionic dimension two and \(15\) from dimension three onward.
