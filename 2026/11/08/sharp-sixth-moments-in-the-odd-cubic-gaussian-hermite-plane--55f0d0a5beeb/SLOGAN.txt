On the complete real odd-cubic Gaussian Hermite plane, the sixth-to-second moment ratio has a sharp algebraic interval with unique projective minimizer and maximizer, both genuine mixtures of the first and third Hermite chaoses. The minimizing mixture has normalized sixth moment \(4.6168673769\ldots\), below the pure Gaussian value, whereas the maximizing mixture has \(57613.87609658\ldots\), above the pure third-chaos value.
