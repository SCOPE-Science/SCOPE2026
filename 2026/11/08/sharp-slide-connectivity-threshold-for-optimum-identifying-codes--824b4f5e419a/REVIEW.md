The proof first recovers and explicitly distinguishes known minimum identifying codes from new reconfiguration facts. The exact trace formula forces one omitted matched index and at least two selected endpoints of each type. It then exhausts all legal token slides and counts degrees. The \(n=6\) invariant gives twenty six-vertex complete bipartite components, and the explicit three-slide recoloring and two-slide hole relocation sequences prove connectedness for all \(n\ge7\). No step relies on extrapolating finite tests. Independent original-graph and ternary-state scripts pass for \(n=5\)..9 and \(n=5\)..12, respectively. Proof hypotheses and all intermediate count boundaries were reconstructed.

## Value — PASS
The sharp transition from 30 frozen optima through 20 nontrivial disconnected \(K_{3,3}\) components to a connected family for all larger n is a natural, structurally motivated question about maintaining optimal identification during local relocation. The graph component theorem and legal constructive paths are stronger and qualitatively different from the known optimum cardinality and counting results. Exact graph edges provide a reproducible supplementary invariant.
