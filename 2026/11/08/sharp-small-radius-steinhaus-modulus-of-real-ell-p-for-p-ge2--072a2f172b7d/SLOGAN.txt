For every real \(\ell_p\) space with \(p\ge2\), the original uniform Steinhaus modulus has sharp small-radius slope \(2^{2-p}d^{p-1}\) at each unit-sphere separation \(d\). A dimension-free error bounded by \((p-1)a^2\) makes the asymptotic quantitative, and two-coordinate unit pairs attain the coefficient.
