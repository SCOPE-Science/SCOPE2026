On every equilateral metric wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), the sharp squared mean-zero Sobolev evaluation constant is \(5u_m/4-49/(48m)\), where \(u_m=(1+\rho^m)/(\sqrt5(1-\rho^m))\) and \(\rho=(3-\sqrt5)/2\). For \(m\ge4\) exactly the rim-edge midpoints are norming, with a strict quantitative gap over all spokes; the tetrahedral wheel \(m=3\) is the sole exception, when all six edge midpoints are norming.
