For uniformly random independent orientations of a fixed \(n\)-vertex tree, the number of sources and the number of sinks have an exact degree-local covariance; among all tree shapes, the path uniquely maximizes it at \((n-4)/16\), while the star uniquely minimizes it at \((n-1)2^{-(n-1)}-(n-1)/4-4^{-(n-1)}\). Thus for \(n\ge5\), the two natural extremal tree shapes have opposite source–sink dependence signs.
