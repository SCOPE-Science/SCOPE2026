For a symbol involution with \(f\) fixed letters and \(r\ge1\) swapped pairs, the longest cyclic span-two pair-selecting word has length \(2r(f+r)\), except when \(f=0\) and \(r\) is odd, when its exact length is \(2r^2-1\). A quotient Euler-tour lift proves the result and exposes a one-window parity obstruction precisely for fixed-point-free alphabets of cardinality congruent to two modulo four.
