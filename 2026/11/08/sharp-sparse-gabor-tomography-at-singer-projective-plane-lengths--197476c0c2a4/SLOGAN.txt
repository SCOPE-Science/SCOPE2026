For every prime power \(q\), the sparsest cyclic Gabor windows enabling complete linear intensity tomography in dimension \(q^2+q+1\) have exactly \(q+1\) sites, and the optimal condition number at that support budget is precisely \(q+1\). Every optimizer is a constant-modulus cyclic planar difference-set window with arbitrary phases; deleting one site forces a sharp nullity of \(2q(q^2+q+1)\) matrix directions.
