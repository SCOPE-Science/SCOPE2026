For first-degree harmonic projections on uniform \(k\)-subsets of \([n]\), the largest endpoint norm across all ranks is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2n/e}\), and every maximizing rank satisfies \(k/\sqrt n\to1/\sqrt2\). The universal worst case occurs on sparse root-sized slices, exceeding the dense-slice Gaussian constant \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}\).
