For the literal four-letter Rudin–Shapiro fixed point, the smallest pseudoperiod horizon is \(10\) and forces all five shifts \(\{2,4,6,8,10\}\); reducing the number of shifts to its sharp minimum \(4\) requires horizon \(66\). Exactly two four-shift pseudoperiods attain that horizon: \(\{2,14,64,66\}\) and \(\{2,52,64,66\}\), certified by an exhaustive finite substitution-language computation.
