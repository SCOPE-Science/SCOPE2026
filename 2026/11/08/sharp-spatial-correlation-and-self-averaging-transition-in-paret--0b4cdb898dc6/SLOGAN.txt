For a one-dimensional Glauber chain globally reset at Pareto-renewal times to its original random spins, every fixed positive temperature yields a sharp spatial correlation \(C_n\sim K_+n^{1-\alpha}\tanh(\beta J)^n\) with \(K_+=\tau_0^{\alpha-1}(2\sqrt{1-\tanh^2(2\beta J)})^{\alpha-1}/\alpha\). The known zero-temperature correlation and self-averaging transition are credited to prior work, not claimed as new.
