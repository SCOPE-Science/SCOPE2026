In a globally Pareto-reset one-dimensional Glauber chain, stationary two-spin correlations decay as \(n^{1-\alpha}\tanh(\beta J)^n\) at every positive temperature but as \(n^{-2(\alpha-1)}\) at zero temperature, with exact positive prefactors. The zero-temperature block magnetization changes its self-averaging law sharply at \(\alpha=3/2\): anomalous power decay below, a \((\log L)/L\) boundary, and ordinary \(L^{-1}\) decay above.
