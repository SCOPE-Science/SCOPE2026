For \(m\ge3\) independent uniform vertices in round \(\mathbb{RP}^{d-1}\), \(d\ge3\), the probability that the closed shortest-geodesic polygon is noncontractible has an exact spherical-harmonic spectral series. It converges to \(1/2\) with sharp first correction \(-(d/2)\lambda_d^m\), an explicit uniform error bound, and a parity-alternating second correction; the previously known three-vertex projective-plane case is not claimed as new.
