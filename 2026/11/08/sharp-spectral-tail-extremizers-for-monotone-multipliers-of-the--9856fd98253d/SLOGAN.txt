For the real-variable Cesàro operator, the high-index commutator singular values have a derivative-weighted Weyl coefficient. Under the natural constraint \(g(0)=0\), \(g(1)=1\), and \(0\le g'\le L\), its sharp maximum is attained uniquely by a boundary-saturated inverse-quadratic density, while the sharp infimum is approached only by a central bang-bang density; their large-budget orders differ by a logarithmic factor times \(L\).
