For finite graphs with exactly two triangles per edge, the Farey-type local axiom \(\psi_k\) forces minimum degree at least \(k+1\), giving an explicit orientable-genus obstruction. The threshold is sharp on the sphere (the unique twelve-vertex minimum witness is the icosahedron, attaining \(\psi_4\)) and on the torus (triangular-lattice quotients attain \(\psi_5\)); the next axiom fails on each surface.
