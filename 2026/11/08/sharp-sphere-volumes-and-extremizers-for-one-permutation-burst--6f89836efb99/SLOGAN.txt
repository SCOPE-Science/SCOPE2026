For one permutation of a consecutive length-\(k\) block, every sphere has an exact minimal-changed-interval formula; its universal maximum is attained precisely when all \(k\)-windows have distinct letters, possible exactly for alphabets of size at least \(k\). At the exceptional binary triple-window boundary, the sharp maximum is \(\lfloor3n/2\rfloor-1\), with a complete two-state generating function for every sphere-volume frequency.
