For every \(n\ge8\), the three-capped Johnson metric on four-subsets has a unique sharp negative-type exponent given by \((n-10)-3(n-8)2^p+(2n-15)3^p=0\); precisely its third Johnson spherical sector becomes null at the endpoint. The sharp threshold obeys \(n\beta_n\to1/\log(9/8)\), with an exact endpoint drop of \(\binom n3-\binom n2\) in Euclidean embedding dimension.
