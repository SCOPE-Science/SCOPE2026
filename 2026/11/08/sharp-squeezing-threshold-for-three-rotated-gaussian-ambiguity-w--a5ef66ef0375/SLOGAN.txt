For three equally rotated Gaussian windows with unit total measurement precision, the worst squared-ambiguity gain on a disk is maximized by zero squeezing exactly when \(\pi R^2\leq2\), but by a unique positive squeezing and equal weights when \(\pi R^2>2\). The optimal dilation tends to \(\sqrt3\), giving asymptotic gain \((2/3)e^{-\sqrt3\pi R^2/2}\) rather than \(e^{-\pi R^2}\).
