For full Hermitian tomography using the smallest possible number \(\lfloor N/2\rfloor\) of unit two-sparse cyclic Gabor windows, the sharp measurement condition number is \(2\sqrt{\lfloor N/2\rfloor}\) for every \(N\ge3\). Every optimizer uses balanced non-antipodal windows; even dimensions additionally admit an exactly classified continuum of unbalanced antipodal optimizers.
