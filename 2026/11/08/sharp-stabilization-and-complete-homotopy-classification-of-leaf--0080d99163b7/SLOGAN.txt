For the multipath complex of the bidirected complete bipartite digraph with two fixed hubs and \(n\) interchangeable leaves, the genuine geometric quotient by leaf permutations has types \(S^0\), \(S^2\vee S^1\vee S^1\), \(S^2\), and a point for \(n=1\), \(n=2\), \(n=3\), and \(n\ge4\), respectively. Every quotient with \(n\ge4\) has the same regular CW face poset and admits a uniform eleven-pair collapse to a point.
