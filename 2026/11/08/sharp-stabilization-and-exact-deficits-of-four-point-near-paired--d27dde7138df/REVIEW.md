# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof combines an exact interval-layer decomposition for all \(h\ge a-1\), a bounded-length representation of all small semigroup deficits, residue-class genus calculations, and an absent element at \(h=a-2\) to certify the sharp boundary (except the directly checked small exception). The exact transient \(d=2\) formula follows from merging increasing integer intervals and counting the unmerged final layer. Exact dynamic programming and independent set enumeration support, but do not replace, the universal proof.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. Chen--Yang (2026, arXiv:2607.29535) supplies a general algorithm for computing \(|hA|\) and a simple truncated-binomial formula only under a separately stated arithmetic condition. Our parameter families fail that condition except \((a,d)=(4,3)\), and the investigated theorems do not give the exact sharp full-reflected-gap threshold. Zhou--Yang (2021, DOI:10.5802/crmath.191) proves eventual fringe structure. Granville--Shakan (2020, arXiv:2003.04076), Theorem 1, ALREADY gives the same gap reflection shape for every sufficiently large h, with general bound \(h\ge2\lfloor b/2floor\); novelty is only the exact *smaller sharp threshold*, the explicit gap-genus laws and the transient d=2 formula, not the eventual shape itself. No exact overlapping table or published-finding corpus statement was identified; priority against all unindexed papers cannot be certified. The older Wu--Chen--Chen full text was not available for exhaustive comparison.

## Mathematical value
PASS. A published formula criterion leaves these natural just-outside-hypothesis four-point families as a motivated test of precise stabilization. The exact value \(a-1\), its unique exception, the three residue-class genus laws, and the all-\(h\) \(d=2\) expression quantify the stable fringe and the transient deficit rather than merely renaming a known existence theorem. No claim is made for arbitrary four-point sets or for \(d=3\) transients.
