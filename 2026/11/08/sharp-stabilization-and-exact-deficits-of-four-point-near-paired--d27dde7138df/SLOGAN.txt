For \(A=\{0,1,a,a+d\}\), \(a\ge4\), \(d\in\{2,3\}\), the full \(h\)-fold sumset stabilizes sharply at \(h=a-1\) (except \((a,d)=(4,3)\), where \(h=2\)): its missing integers are precisely the reflections of the gaps of \(\langle d,a+d-1,a+d\rangle\). Explicit residue-class genus formulas and an exact all-\(h\) cardinality formula for \(d=2\) complete the classification in these two families.
