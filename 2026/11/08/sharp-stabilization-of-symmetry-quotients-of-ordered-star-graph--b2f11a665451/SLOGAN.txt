For every \(p\ge4\) and \(m\ge3\), every arm-permutation subgroup quotient of the ordered \(p\)-particle star-graph configuration space is a wedge of circles with an explicit fixed-arm Burnside rank formula. Under the full symmetric group, the actual quotient graph stabilizes for \(m\ge p\), and this range is sharp: the first Betti number jumps by exactly \(p-1\) between \(m=p-1\) and \(m=p\).
