For every finite unit-edge metric tree, the squared sharp mean-zero \(H^1\)-to-\(L^\infty\) constant is an explicit subtree-cut sum, and only leaves can be norming points. Among all trees with \(n\ge3\) edges the star uniquely minimizes it at \(1-2/(3n)\), while the path uniquely maximizes it at \(n/3\); the exact edge profile and all equality functions are determined.
