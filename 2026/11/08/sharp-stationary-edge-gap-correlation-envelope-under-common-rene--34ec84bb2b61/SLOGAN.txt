In a Brownian gas with common renewal resetting, two distinct fixed extreme-particle gaps retain positive correlation even for perfectly periodic resets: their large-system correlation ranges sharply over \([1/10,1/2)\) as the positive finite-second-moment reset clock varies. Deterministic clocks uniquely minimize the correlation, heterogeneous two-point clocks approach its unattainable upper bound, and every intermediate value is attainable.
