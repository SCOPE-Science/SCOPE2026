For nonlinear finite-state diffusion advanced by first-order explicit–implicit–null Euler, the familiar unconditional convex-energy threshold \(\alpha\ge A/2\) is generally too weak to preserve nonnegativity. The sharp all-steps, all-fluxes comparison threshold is \(\alpha\ge A(1-\pi_{\min})\), where \(\pi_{\min}\) is the smallest stationary mass of the directed Markov generator; this holds even without reversibility.
