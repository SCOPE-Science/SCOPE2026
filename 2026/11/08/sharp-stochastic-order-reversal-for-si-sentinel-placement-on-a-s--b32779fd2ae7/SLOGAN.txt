On a star with \(m\) leaves, \(k\) continuously monitored sentinels, and oriented exponential SI transmission rates \(\alpha\) into the hub and \(\beta\) out of it, including the hub is strictly better in first-detection stochastic order if \(k\beta<(m-k+1)\alpha\), whereas monitoring leaves alone is strictly better if the inequality reverses. At equality the entire first-detection distributions coincide; with symmetric rates, increasing the monitoring budget past half the vertices therefore makes excluding the highest-degree vertex strictly preferable.
