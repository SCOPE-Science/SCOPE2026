For independent one-point-per-square random quadrature of \(|x-y|^{-\alpha}[\log(e/|x-y|)]^{-\beta}\), redrawing each \(n\times n\) grid independently, almost-sure convergence holds exactly for \(\alpha<2/3\) or the critical case \(\alpha=2/3,\ \beta>2/3\). Otherwise the estimates converge in probability but almost surely have infinite upper limsup and the correct integral as lower liminf.
