At every fixed \(0<|q|<1\), joint \(q\)-numerical ranges have an unavoidable, sharp transverse thickness: the best possible supremum-norm Hausdorff approximation of any nondegenerate segment of length \(L\) in \(C(\Omega)\) has error exactly \(L\sqrt{1-|q|^2}/[2(1+\sqrt{1-|q|^2})]\). A continuous field of two-dimensional normal matrices attains this universal minimum, quantifying the precise obstruction to extending the unit-modulus realization theorem.
