# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The full domain is \(a,b,h>0\), \(0<\phi<\pi\). Surface integration gives explicit expressions for area and inertia. After normalizing the base semiperimeter, the positivity certificate has four nonnegative terms, with a strict positive quadratic forcing the base area to be maximal before equality; equality then forces the square and the matching height. The exact symbolic identity and independent face quadrature both checked.

## Originality
**PASS, provisionally.** Freitas–Laugesen–Liddell Theorem 5 establishes the cube bound for cuboids only; their Theorems 3/4 address triangular and regular-polygon prisms. The new theorem includes genuinely sheared parallelogram bases, not implied by the subset result. Csató–Giovagnoli–Roy (arXiv:2608.19851) prove broad existence/planar results, not the sharp restricted 3D inequality. Searches for oblique-base right prism inertia did not locate the stated extension, though historical priority remains open. The focal dated manuscript is December 19, 2007; exact first public release day is not separately documented.

## Value
**PASS.** This settles a natural restricted-class sharp inequality posed by moving beyond the cuboid shape assumption; it captures a genuine shear degree of freedom and supplies a nonnegative analytic certificate that quantifies the excess inertia away from the cubical parameters. It does not change the global convex-surface minimization problem.

## Limitations
No claims about oblique-axis prisms, arbitrary quadrilateral bases, higher-dimensional products, or independence of validation. The inequality itself is analytical; numerical sampling only corroborates it.
