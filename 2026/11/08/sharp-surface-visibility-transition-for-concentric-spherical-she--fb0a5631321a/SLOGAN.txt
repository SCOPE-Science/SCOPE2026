For two independent surface-area-uniform points on the two spherical boundaries of a concentric Euclidean shell, the straight-segment visibility probability has an exact all-dimensional cap formula and uniquely determines the radius ratio. As dimension grows it undergoes a sharp transition at \(r/R=1/\sqrt2\), with a Gaussian critical window, sharp exponential rare-event tails, a complementary-radius duality, and an explicit thin-shell coefficient.
