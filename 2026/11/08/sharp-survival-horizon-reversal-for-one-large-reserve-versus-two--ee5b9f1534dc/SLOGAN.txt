For two equal-area reserves with independent local extinction and recolonization, there is a sharp dispersal threshold above which splitting always improves survival at every deadline, and below which its survival advantage reverses exactly once in time. The recolonization threshold for all-deadline dominance exceeds the threshold for improved *mean* extinction time by exactly the single-large-reserve extinction rate \(e\), so every positive area-scaling exponent admits a genuine mean-versus-long-horizon ranking reversal.
