For the original 2024 neighbor-valued synchronous weighted-median model, three agents on a path always reach opinion consensus, yet the center's chosen neighbor stops switching for every initial condition exactly when \(s\le 2/3\); above this threshold it switches infinitely often for all but a countable set of initial center opinions. At \(s=3/4\), a uniform initial center opinion makes the sequence of selected neighbors an independent fair binary process even though every opinion converges.
