For the stable symmetric two-state transfer function \(G(z)=2a/(1-p^2z^2)\), its globally best stable one-state \(H_2\) approximation changes at the sharp threshold \(p=1/\sqrt2\) from the unique zero-pole model to two equally optimal nonzero opposite-pole models, even when complex competitors are permitted. Above this threshold the zero-pole stationary model is a saddle and incurs exactly \(a^2(2p^2-1)^2/[p^2(1-p^2)]\) excess squared error.
