For a trirectangular tetrahedron, the excess probability of a triangular random orthogonal shadow controls the squared imbalance of its three normalized squared right-face areas with an exact, globally optimal coefficient \(C_*/(2\pi)\approx0.09329456\). Surprisingly, equality occurs both for equal legs and for a unique non-equiaxial shape with two equal legs, characterized by a one-variable transcendental equation.
