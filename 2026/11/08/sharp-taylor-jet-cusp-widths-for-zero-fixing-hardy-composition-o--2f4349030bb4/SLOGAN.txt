For a Hardy composition operator whose disk symbol fixes zero and has real derivative \(a\in(-1,1)\), the first nonlinear Taylor term \(cw^m\) produces a sharp Berezin-range cusp width asymptotic to \(2|c|(1-a)^{-(m+1)/2}\varepsilon^{(m+1)/2}\) at real depth \(\varepsilon\) from \(1\). The exponent and coefficient are geometric invariants of the local range, refining the previously known qualitative cusp.
