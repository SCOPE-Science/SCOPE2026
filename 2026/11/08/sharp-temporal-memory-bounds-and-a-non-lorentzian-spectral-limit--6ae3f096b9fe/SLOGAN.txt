A stochastically reset Thomas–Fermi condensate edge retains strictly more temporal memory than a pure exponential: \(e^{-rt}<\rho_r(t)<e^{-rt/2}\) for every positive lag. Its rapid-reset limit has the universal non-Lorentzian shape \(e^{-s}(1+2s/5)\) and integrated relaxation factor \(7/5\), rather than the constant-drift factor \(1\).
