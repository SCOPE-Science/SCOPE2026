For Boolean teams on \(d\ge2\) variables, reversible-dependence tensor width equals the number of occupied antipodal pairs, with generating polynomial \(\bigl(1+(2u+u^2)z\bigr)^{2^{d-1}}\). For nonempty teams, the width-to-coordinate-fiber ratio equals one for two variables and can attain the sharp factor two from three variables onward.
