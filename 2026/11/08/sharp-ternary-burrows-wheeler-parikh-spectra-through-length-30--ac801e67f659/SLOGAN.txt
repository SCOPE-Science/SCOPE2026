For every \(13\le n\le30\), all sharp ternary Burrows–Wheeler last columns are classified and counted exactly by their sorted Parikh triples, proving \(U_3(n)=\lceil n/2\rceil\) at eighteen consecutive lengths. The census exposes a first nontrivial positive-triple exclusion at length \(14\): only \((4,4,6)\), not \((2,6,6)\), occurs among sharp last columns.
