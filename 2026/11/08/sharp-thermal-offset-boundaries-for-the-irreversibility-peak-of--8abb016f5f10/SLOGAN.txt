The harmonic polytropic entropy peak exists only below a sharp temperature-mismatch threshold: for compression with \(0<\log u<2\) it requires \(\delta<\log u/(2-\log u)\), while expansion requires \(-\delta<|\log u|/(2+|\log u|)\). Otherwise the entropy is strictly monotone, whereas sufficiently large compression ratio gives exactly one peak for every positive mismatch; the threshold peak collides linearly with the slow-duration endpoint.
