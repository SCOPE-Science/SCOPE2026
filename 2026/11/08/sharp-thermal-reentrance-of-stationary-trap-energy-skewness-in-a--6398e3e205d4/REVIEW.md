## Mathematical value: PASS

Energy fluctuations are directly measurable in a harmonic trap. Their third cumulant distinguishes shell-dominated rare inward excursions from central-fluctuation positive skewness. The exact threshold in every dimension and the universal two-root thermal coalescence in dimension three give a nontrivial qualitative phase diagram, not a merely selected parameter value or a sign-normalization observation. Independent thermal noise can restore positive asymmetry both at rare and rapid tumbles, but leave a negative-skewness interval between them.

## Scope and limitations

This is an exact stationary, harmonic, independent-noise theorem for instantaneous isotropic tumbling. No finite-time, anisotropic, interacting, or finite-tumble-duration extension is claimed. Reproducing a finite grid is not a substitute for the analytical Descartes and monotonicity proofs. General prior moment formulas imply the raw data but do not themselves articulate the stated phase diagram; publication priority remains qualified.
