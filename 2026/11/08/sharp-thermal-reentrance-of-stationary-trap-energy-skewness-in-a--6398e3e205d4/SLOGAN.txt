The stationary squared-radius skewness of an isotropically trapped run-and-tumble particle has one sharp negative-to-positive transition, whose critical tumbling rate decreases with dimension above two. In dimension three, adding any sufficiently weak thermal diffusion creates a reentrant negative-skewness window with two transition rates, which annihilate at the unique critical noise strength \(h_*=0.019629776323575830\ldots\).
