# Self-review: Sharp theta laws for commuting idempotent tuples in finite triangular algebras

**Correctness — PASS.** Every pairwise-commuting family of idempotents has joint spectral projections indexed by binary colors. The explicit unitriangular intertwiner \(U=\sum_a Q_aD_a\) is invertible because every diagonal entry is one; it conjugates the tuple to the prescribed binary diagonals. The stabilizer has \(q^{b_X-e_X(c)}\) elements, giving \(q^{e_X(c)}\) in each color fiber. This proves the finite-poset identity and its multinomial chain specialization without assuming characteristic is odd. The fixed-parameter asymptotic follows from a centered multiplicity substitution, Stirling expansion, summable Gaussian-theta error, and exponential tail suppression. Quotient exponents and prefactor were recalculated. The direct verifier checks multiplication-based pairwise commutation over two prime fields and multiple posets; finite checks support, but do not prove, the infinite statement.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence.** Garcés–Khrypchenko (2021, arXiv:2110.10676, Lemma 5.4) already prove simultaneous conjugacy; Hou (2019 online) and Wright (2020 online) already study one-idempotent construction and enumeration. These are credited and are not new claims. Fornaroli–Khrypchenko–Santulo (2022, arXiv:2207.10713) classify commutativity-preserving maps, not probabilities. The distinguishing new claim assessed here is the explicit positive, residue-sensitive theta coefficient in the quadratic-rate probability for every fixed tuple size \(t\ge2\). The finite color sum also coincides with a complete-graph Potts partition function, an equivalence addressed rather than treated as novel. No checked paper or database record was shown to state or imply the sharp leading law, but these searches cannot establish global priority. The Hou/Wright full texts and possibly stronger Potts theorems remain risks.

**Value — PASS.** Idempotent commutation measures a natural obstruction to simultaneous projection structure in a foundational finite-dimensional triangular algebra. This result identifies the exact commuting-tuple probability and, in sharp form, its quadratic-exponential rarity and polynomial correction, including nontrivial theta dependence on \(n\bmod 2^t\). The result is an infinite-parameter asymptotic, not a small sample or a rewrite of the established one-idempotent count, and explains how common diagonal eigenspace balance controls commutation rarity.

**Scope and limitations.** Fixed field and tuple size only; repeated entries are allowed. The joint-color identity is exact for arbitrary finite incidence algebras, but the sharp theta asymptotic is for chains. Two directly relevant papers were only available at abstract level; statistical-mechanics literature may contain an equivalent complete-graph partition-function asymptotic. Neither independent validation nor comprehensive publication-priority certification is claimed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
