In the upper triangular matrix algebra over a fixed finite field, the probability that \(t\) independently chosen idempotents commute pairwise has a sharp theta-modulated form \(C_{q,t,n\bmod 2^t}n^{-(2^t-t-1)/2}q^{-((t-2)/4+2^{-t-1})n^2}(1+O_{q,t}(n^{-1}))\). The underlying exact tuple count is a finite-color partition function, valid more generally for incidence algebras of arbitrary finite posets.
