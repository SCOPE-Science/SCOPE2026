On a fixed radial degree-\(n\) Zernike sector, restriction from the disk to an outer annulus of squared-radius thickness \(\varepsilon\) has an explicit sharp concentration-condition-number constant multiplying \(\varepsilon^{-2n}\), valid for every azimuthal order. In the first nonconstant radial band, the simultaneous limit with \((m+1)\varepsilon\to\tau\) instead has two explicit Laguerre eigenvalues, exposing the precise high-angular-frequency crossover.
