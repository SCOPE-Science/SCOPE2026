With arbitrary signed three-body interactions, any fixed positive Lotka--Volterra coexistence target can be made linearly stable unless \(w_i=-2r_i-\sum_jS_{ij}p_j\) is the same nonnegative number for every species. Outside that exceptional locus, one can arrange that all Jacobian eigenvalues equal \(-1\); within it, that nonnegative number is an unavoidable eigenvalue.
