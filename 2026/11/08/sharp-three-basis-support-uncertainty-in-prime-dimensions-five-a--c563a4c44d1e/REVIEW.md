# Scientific review of the dimension-five triple-support theorem

The final claim is restricted to the complete standard MUB family in \(\mathbb C^5\). Any six of the fifteen analysis rows of any three chosen bases have rank five over \(\mathbb C\), by exhaustive verification of all \(100100\) finite-field specializations at the primitive fifth root 95 modulo 101. Nonzero minors survive specialization, so the zero-coordinate bound is rigorous; a two-spike state gives equality and chirp/Fourier symmetries transport it to any triple. An independent modular implementation repeated the complete five-dimensional rank census.

Prior pairwise bounds and the 2022 complete-MUB sharp bound do not imply this exact arbitrary-triple dimension-five theorem. The separate published seven-point fixed-triple theorem, via the three-homogeneous projective \(SL_2(\mathbb F_7)\) action on MUB labels, *does* imply all seven-point triples; hence the former combined five-and-seven originality claim has been narrowed. The remaining dimension-five exact incidence statement is correct, best-of-knowledge original and mathematically valuable as a small-prime sharp measurement-support classification. An unindexed equivalent calculation remains possible. This is scientific review evidence, not external certification or a formal-proof attestation.
