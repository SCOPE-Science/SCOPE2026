The first finite \(\mathbf{BM}\)-algebras that fail associativity have three elements: there are six marked isomorphism types, but exactly one is generated by its distinguished bluebird and mockingbird. This unique generated obstruction has multiplication rows \((0,0,0)\), \((0,0,1)\), \((0,0,1)\) with \(B=0\) and \(M=2\).
