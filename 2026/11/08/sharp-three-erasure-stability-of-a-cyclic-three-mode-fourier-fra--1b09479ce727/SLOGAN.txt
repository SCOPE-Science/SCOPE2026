For the normalized cyclic Fourier frame of three consecutive frequencies, deleting any three time samples leaves a sharp lower frame bound \(1-[3+\tfrac12(v+\sqrt{v^2+8u^2})]/N\), where \(u=1+2\cos(2\pi/N)\) and \(v=1+2\cos(4\pi/N)\), valid for every \(N\ge6\). Exactly the cyclically consecutive erasure triples attain the worst-case bound; the full retained spectrum at those masks is explicit.
