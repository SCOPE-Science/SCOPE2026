For symmetric Markov chains on three-subsets that may replace one or two elements per step, the exact fastest-mixing rate has three size regimes: \(6\le n\le9\), \(10\le n\le12\), and \(n\ge13\). Every global optimizer suppresses one-element replacements starting at \(n=10\), and all holding probabilities vanish starting at \(n=13\).
