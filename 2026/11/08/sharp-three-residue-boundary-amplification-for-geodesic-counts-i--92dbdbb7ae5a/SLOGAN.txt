Among all fixed finite boundary modifications of a long chain of three-vertex independent layers, the geodesic-subpath count has a sharp, residue-dependent leading coefficient: \(121/36\), \(529/(4\cdot3^{10/3})\), or \(253/(12\cdot3^{5/3})\), according to vertex count modulo three. An exact local-replacement argument reduces every candidate boundary to eight profiles, and explicit extremizers attain all three constants; global extremality over arbitrary graphs is not claimed.
