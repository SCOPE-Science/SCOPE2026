Its conclusion does not classify the present four-plus-two-plus-two pattern on three rings, nor yield the explicit algebraic exceptional radius locus. Bodmann and Hammen (2013) establish low-redundancy structured phase retrieval in a different frame geometry, while Chalendar and Partington (2024) give circle/line uniqueness results under different (including full-circle) data. The sharp 2/3/4 exceptional-radius transition, rather than cardinal minimality, is the new proposed contribution. A specialized earlier eight-vector frame classification could conceivably imply it and remains an explicit priority risk.
