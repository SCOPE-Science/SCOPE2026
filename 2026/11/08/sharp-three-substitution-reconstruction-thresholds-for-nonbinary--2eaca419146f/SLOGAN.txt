For every \(n\geq6\), exact-three-substitution reconstruction over ternary words is hardest when two possible sources differ in **two** positions, whereas over every alphabet with \(q\geq4\) symbols it is hardest when they differ in just **one**. The sharp number of distinct reads needed for unique uncoded reconstruction is \(2(n-2)(2n-5)+1\) for \(q=3\), and \(\frac12(n-2)(n-1)(q-2)(q-1)^2+1\) for \(q\geq4\).
