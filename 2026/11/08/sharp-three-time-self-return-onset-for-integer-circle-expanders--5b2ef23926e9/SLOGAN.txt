For integer expanding circle maps, three equally spaced self-returns are confined to the common-resonance phase neighborhoods exactly until the sharp radius \(a/(a^2+a+2)\), where \(a\) is the expansion over one observation interval; beyond it, two new intervals give an explicit jump in the three-event probability's derivative. The first three returns of the doubling map also have a complete six-piece probability law, and their genuine triple-resonance threshold can differ from the pairwise one.
