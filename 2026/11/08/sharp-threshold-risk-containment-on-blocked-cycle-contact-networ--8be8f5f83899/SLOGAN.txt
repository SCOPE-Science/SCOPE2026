For independent-edge epidemics on a finite ring, the minimum probability of a size-at-least-\(h\) outbreak under exactly \(k\) blocked edges is determined by a single long contact segment and \(k-1\) segments of length \(h-1\) whenever zero risk is impossible. Six sites are the smallest where the intervention maximizing this risk changes with transmissibility, and above \(p=3/4\) the mean-optimal two-cut placement maximizes three-case outbreak risk.
