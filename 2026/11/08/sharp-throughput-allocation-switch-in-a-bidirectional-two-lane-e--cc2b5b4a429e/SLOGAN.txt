Allowing positive backward hopping rates reveals a previously inaccessible negative-coupling regime in which the maximum net stationary current of a two-lane exclusion gas abruptly switches from equal lane densities to running just one half-filled lane. The exact threshold is the unique root \(q_*\in(-3/2,-1)\) of \(32q^3+85q^2+80q+32=0\); this is an externally optimized allocation switch, not dynamical symmetry breaking.
