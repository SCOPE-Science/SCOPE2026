In a two-worker, two-firm market with independently random weak preferences, every report-independent tie-refinement lottery followed by deferred acceptance has Pareto failure probability at least \(t^2(1-t)(3t+1)/4\), attained by common worker-side priorities. Independent fair tie-breaking instead fails with probability \(t^2(1-t)(7t+3)/8\), strictly worse for \(0<t<1\).
