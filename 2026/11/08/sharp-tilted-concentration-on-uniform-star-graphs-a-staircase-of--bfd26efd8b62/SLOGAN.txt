The sharp centered Laplace transform of every uniform star with at least three leaves has an explicit floor/ceiling formula: as the exponential tilt increases, maximizers switch one by one from balanced leaf signs to a single positive leaf. The number of strict switching thresholds is exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\), and the optimal positive-leaf density converges to \(1/(2t)-1/(e^{2t}-1)\) at fixed tilt.
