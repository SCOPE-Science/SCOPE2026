Minimum total dominating sets of the categorical product \(K_m\times K_n\), for \(m,n\ge3\), are exactly the three-cell partial permutation matrices. Their token-slide graph is frozen if a factor has size three, splits into exactly \(24K_4\) at \((m,n)=(4,4)\), and becomes connected as soon as one factor has at least five vertices and the other has at least four; token jumping is connected except at \((3,3)\).
