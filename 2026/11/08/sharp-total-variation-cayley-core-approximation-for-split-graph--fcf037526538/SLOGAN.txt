A uniform spanning tree of a complete split graph admits an exact decorated-Cayley-core approximation in total variation, with error \(1-(b)_{a-1}/(a+b)^{a-1}\). Its sharp \(b\asymp a^2\) transition separates two independent Poisson defects with means \(\lambda\) and \(\lambda/2\); above that scale, marked clique distances converge after \(2\sqrt a\) normalization to the Rayleigh law.
