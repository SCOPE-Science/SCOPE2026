## Value — PASS
The sharp decay interval is a natural dynamical quantity. It separates invasion growth from the fading of infection heterogeneity among identical-risk communities, quantifies their distinct timescales, and constructs connected networks with the same invasion reproduction number but every possible transverse relaxation exponent in a nontrivial interval. In particular, the phenomenon persists while the common infection mode is supercritical, so a threshold-only description misses a mechanistically distinct transient. The result is not a parameter renaming, an isolated small graph, or a numerical table. The restrictions to linearized disease-free dynamics and symmetric uniform-hub mobility matter; no nonlinear final-size conclusion follows.
