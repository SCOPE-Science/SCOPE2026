For every even cyclic length \(N\ge4\), real antiperiodic signals with all odd Fourier coefficients nonzero are determined up to translation by their full power spectrum and exactly \(\lfloor N/4\rfloor\) specified trispectrum Fourier entries; no smaller fixed selection of individual entries works for every such signal. All odd-order autocorrelations, including the bispectrum, vanish identically on this class.
