# Scientific review: sharp two-band spherical Poisson norm

## Correctness

**PASS.** The normalized spherical moment identities follow from the Gaussian radial decomposition. The traceless fourth-power eigenvalue inequality is sharp and its critical three-root case is explicitly handled; the independent spectral bound on the vector--matrix mixed moment is sharp simultaneously. Consequently the multivariable fourth moment reduces exactly, rather than merely by an unattained inequality, to a one-variable quadratic. Endpoint derivatives prove the change of maximizer, and the resulting quartic threshold has a unique crossing because every nonzero fixed-input fourth moment increases strictly with its radius. Exact coefficient-difference factorizations prove strict dimension monotonicity. The supplied rational checker independently reproduces 138 zonal moment identities and sample threshold brackets. Finite checks alone are not presented as universal proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with bibliographic risk.** Beckner (1992) proves the unrestricted sphere-Poisson hypercontractivity criterion \(\rho\le1/\sqrt3\), not the restricted sharp norms or their thresholds above that radius. Montanaro (2012) restates the same semigroup action and bound without computing the mixed-band optimum. The published exact degree-two harmonic theorem gives the pure quadratic coefficient \(C_d\), not the sharp linear-plus-quadratic optimization. Separate earlier results include the sphere \(\mathbb S^2\) mixed fourth-moment value at zero damping and the Gaussian high-dimensional limit, but neither implies the dimension-dependent finite-radius formula by restriction or parameter substitution. Targeted mathematical-record and literature searches found no dominating statement. Unindexed papers concerning zonal Gegenbauer quadratic mixtures or refined hypercontractivity are residual risks. The distinct fractional-Laplace sphere semigroup \(e^{-t\sqrt{-\Delta}}\) is not confused with the ball-Poisson semigroup used here.

## Value

**PASS.** The problem gives an exact, attainable operator norm and full equality classification on the first two spherical harmonic bands rather than a generic norm comparison. It identifies an explicit extremizer transition, a strictly dimension-dependent contractivity cutoff, and a rigorous monotone convergence to the Gaussian restricted result. These are natural stability invariants of an invariant approximation space and are not an arbitrary isolated dimension or numerical optimization. The result does not claim the unrestricted sphere-Poisson problem or higher harmonic bands.

## Closest literature and limitations

W. Beckner, *PNAS* 89 (1992), 4816–4819, DOI 10.1073/pnas.89.11.4816, is the precise operator baseline. A. Montanaro, *Some applications of hypercontractive inequalities in quantum information theory* (2012), §2.1, supplies the same spectral convention. The published mathematical record *Sharp L4/L2 norm and extremizers for degree-two spherical harmonics* supplies the sharp pure-quadratic boundary formula and inequality; it was explicitly inspected rather than treated as absent. The theorem is restricted to real centered first-two-harmonic inputs, normalized sphere measure and the exponent pair \((2,4)\). The originality assessment is a targeted prior-work comparison, not a uniqueness certification.
