For the classical ball-Poisson operator on the real first-two-harmonic, mean-zero subspace of every sphere, the exact \(L^2\to L^4\) norm has a sharp linear-to-mixed extremizer transition at \(\rho^2=d(d+4)/((d+2)(5d-4))\). Its contractivity radius decreases strictly with dimension to the Gaussian threshold \(0.672044717288805\ldots\), with all finite-dimensional extremizers characterized.
