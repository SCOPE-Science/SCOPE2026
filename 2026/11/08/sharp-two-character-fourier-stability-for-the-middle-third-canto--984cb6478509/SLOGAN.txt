The largest magnitude of a nonzero integer Fourier coefficient of the fair middle-third Cantor measure is exactly \(C_3=\prod_{j\ge1}|\cos(2\pi/3^j)|\), attained only at integer frequencies \(\pm3^a\). Consequently every distinct integer-frequency pair has a sharp two-dimensional Riesz lower bound \(1-C_3\), with equality exactly at power-of-three gaps.
