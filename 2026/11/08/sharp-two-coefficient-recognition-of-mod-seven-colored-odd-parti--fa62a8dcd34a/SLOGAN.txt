For partitions with one even-part color and \(k\) odd-part colors, the complete list of modulo-seven vanishing progressions is \((k\bmod7,r)=(0,3),(1,5),(3,2),(4,4),(5,6)\). All of them are recognized sharply by checking just \(a_k(r)\) and \(a_k(r+7)\) modulo seven; the positive families themselves were already proved by Hirschhorn and Sellers.
