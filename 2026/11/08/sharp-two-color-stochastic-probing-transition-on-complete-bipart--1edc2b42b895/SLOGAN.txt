For two Rademacher trace probes of the shifted-Laplacian inverse on \(K_{m,n}\), an optimal partition always leaves one graph part monochromatic, and every optimum has that form. The usual proper two-coloring is optimal exactly when \(\bigl(n/(\mu+n)\bigr)^2(m-1)\le n\) and \(\bigl(m/(\mu+m)\bigr)^2(n-1)\le m\); beyond this sharp boundary deliberately putting adjacent vertices in the same probe class reduces variance.
