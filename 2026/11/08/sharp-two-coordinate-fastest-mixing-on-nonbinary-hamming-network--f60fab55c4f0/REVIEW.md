# Mathematical review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof explicitly fixes the stochastic symmetry class and derives the first two Krawtchouk eigenvalues from a finite generating product. The degree-one eigenvalue gives the global generic lower bound after automorphism averaging. A convex quadratic bound with the strictly positive polynomial \(F(d,q)\) controls **every** higher harmonic weight, including real minimum and endpoint cases. Two exact signed dual equalities solve the exceptional cases and a complete-graph argument handles dimension two. The final optimizer rigidity follows from a positive-semidefinite trace equality, not experiments. Exact verifier replay supplements rather than substitutes for this proof.

## Originality
PASS on available evidence, subject to specialist priority uncertainty. Boyd et al., arXiv:0709.0955v1, Section 3.3.3, optimize only the *original* Hamming graph with distance-one support; the present optimization permits distance two and has a different optimum, including generic support elimination. A prior binary-alphabet radius-two classification does not imply the nonbinary result: its optimal walk retains distance-one jumps, whereas the new generic optimum forces their absence for every unrestricted optimizer. The closest Hamming two-ball positive-definite Turán studies use a different Fourier mass objective, not the two-sided Markov SLEM minimax. The relevant primary arXiv manuscript was inspected, while one related full specialist record was inaccessible; full priority certification is not claimed.

## Mathematical value
PASS. A natural, highly symmetric nonbinary network with one- and two-coordinate moves admits an exact global mixing optimum and an unusually strong structural conclusion: globally optimal dynamics discard all apparently useful one-coordinate edges above a sharp small-parameter exceptional set. The all-optimizer marginal law is useful for identifying and constraining nonhomogeneous optimal kernels, not just finding a symmetric numerical optimum. The \((3,3)\) and \((3,4)\) exceptions exhibit two distinct unavoidable small-network interventions (one-coordinate hops and holding, respectively).

## Limitations and closest sources
This is a symmetric uniform-stationary, distance-at-most-two result. Full unrestricted optimizer-face classification is open here. Closest sources are the Hamming one-step solution of Boyd et al. (2007) and prior binary two-hop mixing and nonbinary Hamming Turán-mass results on different domains/objectives. Source coverage cannot establish absolute first publication.
