The analytical calculations are the spherical fourth-moment identity, the invariant orthogonal decomposition, the generalized \(2\times2\) even block, and the monotone-crossing argument for the optimal angle.

Reproduce the finite consistency checks with `python check_two_equators.py` (NumPy required). At preparation, the script printed `TWO_EQUATOR_CHECKS_PASS 593`. The script does not replace proof for arbitrary dimensions, check prior-publication completeness, or provide independent validation.
