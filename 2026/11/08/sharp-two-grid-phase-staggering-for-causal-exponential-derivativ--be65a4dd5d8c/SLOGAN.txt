For a two-bank infinite geometric scale analysis built from causal exponential-derivative wavelets, a half-log-step staggering is the unique choice that simultaneously maximizes the sharp lower frame bound and minimizes the upper bound for every scale ratio \(q>1\). The optimal squared-bound condition number grows as \(\sqrt q/8\) for sparse scales, and its departure from tightness is \((16\pi^2/\log q)\exp(-2\pi^2/\log q)\) at dense scales.
