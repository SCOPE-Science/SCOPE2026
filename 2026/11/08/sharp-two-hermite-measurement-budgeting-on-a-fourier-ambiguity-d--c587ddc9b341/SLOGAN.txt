For a fixed total measurement budget split between Gaussian and first-Hermite STFT intensity channels, the exact best worst-case ambiguity-inversion margin on a disk switches at squared radial cutoff \(T=2\): Gaussian-only is optimal below it, and a unique nontrivial mixture is optimal above it. The optimally balanced noise margin has the explicit scalar characterization \(\Gamma(T)=e^{-T}[1+w_TT(T-2)]\) and improves the Gaussian-only margin by a factor asymptotic to \((T-1)^2\).
