For every odd block length \(k\) and every fixed-point-free complement alphabet, the maximum number of distinct words obtainable by two sequential reverse-complement duplications from length \(n\) is exactly \((n-k+1)(n+1)-\binom{\max\{n-2k+2,0\}}{2}\). Constant words attain the bound; the only collisions between their operation histories come from commuting disjoint windows.
