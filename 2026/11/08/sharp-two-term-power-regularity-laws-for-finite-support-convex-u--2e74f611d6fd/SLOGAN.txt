For every finite nondegenerate positive convex polynomial of the unilateral shift that includes the constant term, the sharp norm of \(T^n(I-T)^k\) has a universal Gaussian leading scale and an explicit second-order coefficient determined by the first four moments. The correction can be positive or negative, while every two-atom \(\{0,h\}\) law has the exact coefficient \(-k^2/4\).
