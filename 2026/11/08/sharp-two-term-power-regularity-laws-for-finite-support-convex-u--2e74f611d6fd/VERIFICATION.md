# Verification details

The exact multiplier norm identity and the stationary-phase calculation give the infinite-dimensional conclusion. A standalone finite checker computes all three rational correction coefficients exactly, replays the independently optimized Bernoulli formula, and checks the convergence of the first correction for two three-point laws. The computation supports but does not certify the general theorem. Sections of the motivating paper were inspected via accessible HTML, while the older time-regularity article was not available in full text; novelty therefore remains a qualified best-knowledge assessment, not independent priority confirmation.

Run `python3 checks/check_power_norms.py` from the package root. Python 3 standard library only.
