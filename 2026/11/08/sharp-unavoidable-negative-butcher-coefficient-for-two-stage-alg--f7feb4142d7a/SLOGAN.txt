Among all algebraically stable two-stage Runge–Kutta schemes of order at least three with positive weights and both stage abscissas in \([0,1]\), some Butcher entry must be at most \(-0.0356390699\ldots\). The sharp negative-entry minimum is attained uniquely (up to stage permutation) by an explicit one-parameter symplectic tableau at the root of \(p^3(1-p)=1/12\); this quantifies, rather than merely states, the obstruction to nonnegative coefficients.
