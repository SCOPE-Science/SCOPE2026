The shortest unbordered \(k\)-subsequence-universal word has length \(qk\) over every alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), but over the binary alphabet it has length \(2k+1\) for \(k\ge2\) (and length \(2\) for \(k=1\)). Every shortest binary witness is an explicit doubled-prefix construction or its reversal and bit complement, subject to a complete half-length border test.
