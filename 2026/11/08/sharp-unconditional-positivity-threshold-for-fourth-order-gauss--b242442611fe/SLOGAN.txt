The fourth-order two-stage Gauss–Legendre heat update on a weighted complete split graph is nonnegative at every timestep exactly when the clique-edge conductance is at most \(2+p/q\) times the crossing-edge conductance, for a \(p\)-vertex clique joined to \(q\ge2\) independent vertices. Above this sharp boundary, every distinct clique-to-clique transition becomes negative after one explicitly calculable crossing time, even though the method remains spectrally stable.
