## Mathematical value — PASS
The original AC-existence theorem leaves open whether the normalized geometric reference measure could itself be stationary. The result answers this throughout the fat-triangle regime, and at the natural Jacobian-two scale gives a sharp nonzero minimax total-variation defect, the complete minimizing coin family, and a quantitative lower bound for the distance of every stationary law from area. It isolates a structural restriction on physical invariant densities, rather than a routine restatement of AC existence.
