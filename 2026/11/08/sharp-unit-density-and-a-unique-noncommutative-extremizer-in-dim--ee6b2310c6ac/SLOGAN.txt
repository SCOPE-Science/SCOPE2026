For noncommutative unital \(7\)-dimensional algebras over \(\mathbb F_p\), the largest fraction of units is exactly \((1-p^{-2})(1-p^{-3})\). Equality uniquely forces the product of a Frobenius-skew square-zero extension of \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\) with \(\mathbb F_{p^3}\), giving the first odd-dimensional improvement over the local-unit density threshold.
