# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact subspace reduction is valid for all parity-preserving two-qubit gates; X-state concurrence is twice the single even-sector coherence. Rodrigues' formula gives a gate-universal bound using the minimum of a uniformly convergent cosine series. Axis-y rotations attain the minimum or its zero crossing. The geometric-resolvent derivative is nonnegative for all \(p\in(0,1]\), and the equal-mean periodic example uses a uniformly randomized clock phase. The standalone independent complex-matrix calculations completed 1061 bounded checks. Finite tests do not substitute for the universal argument.

## Originality — PASS, with publication-priority risk

The 2026 originating paper already gives geometric reset-age stationary mixtures, Bell-reset concurrence for its fixed Hamiltonian family, and the geometric kernel. The 2024/2025 paper supplies general discrete-time resetting and non-Poissonian clocks. The assessed statement is instead the sharp minimax concurrence over *all* parity-preserving gates, for *every* probability age law, with a necessary-and-sufficient Fourier sign criterion and the matched-mean stochastic/periodic separation. The published full texts and relevant comparison records inspected did not state that result or a theorem covering its gate-global optimization. An unlocated broader quantum channel or renewal extremal theorem could still subsume it; independent priority checking remains necessary.

## Value — PASS

The result solves a natural robust entanglement-design question without selecting a special Hamiltonian, identifies a sharp Fourier condition on the reset clock, and proves that equal mean reset rates do not guarantee comparable quantum correlations. The k=2 example is operationally interpretable: each pure state is maximally entangled although the phase-randomized periodic mixture is separable, while Bernoulli mixing of the same ages retains a strict nonzero concurrence floor. It is not a mere new notation for the already known geometric resolvent.

## Scientific limitations

Only perfect Bell resets, noiseless parity-preserving gates and post-mixture concurrence are covered. Periodic clocks are stationary only after phase randomization. Inaccessible or unlocated broader comparison theorems remain a publication-priority risk. Numerical verification is bounded, not an infinite-dimensional or all-gate certification.
