For a rank-one least-squares subspace known only up to principal angle \(\theta\), the exact smallest possible worst-case leverage inflation over *all* reference-based row samplers is \((\cos\theta+\sqrt{m-1}\sin\theta)^2\) until \(\theta=\arctan\sqrt{m-1}\), and \(m\) thereafter. An explicit mixture of reference leverage and uniform probabilities attains the global bound, while any positive drift makes unsmoothed reference sampling universally unbounded.
