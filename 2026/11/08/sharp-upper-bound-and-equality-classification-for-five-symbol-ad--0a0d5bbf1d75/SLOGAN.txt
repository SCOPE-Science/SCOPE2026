For exact deletion of two adjacent symbols from length-five codewords, the universal code-size bound is \(q^2(q+2)/3\), and it is sharp whenever \(q\ge3\) is congruent to \(0\) or \(1\) modulo \(3\). Building on a previously known construction from shorter deletion codes, the new result proves global optimality and completely classifies every bound-attaining code as \(q\) independent directed triple systems together with all period-two words.
