For any finite connected triangle-free base graph with maximum valence at most five, the scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of its cyclic vertex truncation recovers the base graph's entire homotopy type, while the integral scale-one-to-two map kills exactly the primitive local loops at valences four and five. This valence bound is sharp: the six-star truncation yields a two-sphere instead of a point.
