For three independent uniform points on the ambient unit sphere \(S^{d-1}\), the area of their intrinsic great-sphere triangle has exact mean \(\pi/2\), variance \(3/d+6/d^2+O(d^{-5/2})\), and third centered moment \(12/d^2+O(d^{-5/2})\). Its standardized area becomes Gaussian, but retains an explicitly positive leading skewness of \(4/\sqrt{3d}\).
