# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The equilibrium-renewal residual law, alternating sign renewal equation, and harmonic convolution yield the exact variance formula. Strict Jensen, monotonicity in the waiting-law Laplace transform, and explicit two-point approximating laws establish all endpoints, equality conditions and convex-order monotonicity. The reproducibility checker confirms independent special-case models and exact rational cases; the proof supplies the infinite-domain result.

**Originality: PASS (qualified).** Full-text inspection of Farago–Smith, arXiv:2401.01576, shows a numerical stationary-distribution method for general waiting times, rather than this exact global fixed-mean variance extremum. Guéneau–Majumdar–Schehr, arXiv:2306.09453, treats resetting driving noise, not alternating-renewal signs; only its abstract and bibliographic scope were material read. Public mathematical-record searches for harmonic renewal variance and deterministic waiting did not return equivalent claims. General renewal-noise spectral identities could make the variance formula implicit in older theory, so publication priority remains qualified.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** The exact sharp optimization of confinement at fixed reorientation *mean* solves a natural distribution-design problem: the shape of renewal waiting times is a control variable even when the mean persistence is fixed. The uniqueness, sharp nonattained upper endpoint, and convex-order monotonicity compare every finite-mean run law without assumptions on its variance.

No classification of higher moments or density shape is established. General preexisting renewal transforms are a residual originality risk.
