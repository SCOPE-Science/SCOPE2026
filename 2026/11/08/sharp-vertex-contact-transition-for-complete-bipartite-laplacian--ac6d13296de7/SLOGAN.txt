In the canonical Euclidean Laplacian simplex of \(K_{p,q}\), a sharp inequality \(p(q-p)(p+q+1)>2(p+q)^2\) makes every vertex in the larger color class leave the **minimum enclosing sphere**. Its center, exact radius, and squared-distance clearance are explicit for all \(2\le p\le q\); \(K_{3,13}\) has sixteen contacts, but \(K_{3,14}\) has only three.
