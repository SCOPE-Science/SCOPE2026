**Correctness: PASS.** The exact shell sums of the two nonconstant Krawtchouk layers yield the full group Fourier spectrum; the concavity/vertex argument identifies a unique first failing Walsh level for every dimension and every exponent below one. Monotonicity of the critical eigenvalue rules out recovery at higher exponents. The centered Gram rank and two-term asymptotic are derived without replacing the infinite proof by numerical extrapolation. A self-contained script independently sums the finite-group characters in small dimensions.

The result gives a complete infinite-family Fourier-positivity transition rather than isolated computations: capping reduces the ordinary cube's exponent-one negative type to order \(n^{-1}\), with a parity-sensitive second-order term and a macroscopic, exactly quantified loss of Euclidean embedding rank at the transition. Both the critical exponent and the precise equality space can guide the design of radial positive-definite kernels on high-dimensional binary data.
