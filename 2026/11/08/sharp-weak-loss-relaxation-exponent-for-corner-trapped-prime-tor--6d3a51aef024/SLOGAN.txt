For coherent hopping on a Cartesian product of pairwise distinct odd-prime cycles, \(2^d\) adjacent-corner traps eliminate dark states, but their sharp weak-loss attenuation coefficient is only \(\prod_j(2-2\cos(\pi/p_j))/p_j\), asymptotic to \(\pi^{2d}\prod_jp_j^{-3}\). The \(2^d\)-site trapping threshold is previously known; the proposed contribution is the exact quantitative relaxation bottleneck.
