For stationary Brownian motion with Poisson partial resetting \(X\mapsto e^{-\varepsilon}X\), a rare displacement of size \(1/\varepsilon\) obeys an exact **sharp** tail law with a dilogarithmic rate, a \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) prefactor and an explicit contraction-boundary coefficient. It quantifies the Gaussian-to-exponential rare-tail crossover beyond both stationary-density formulas and general logarithmic large-deviation principles.
