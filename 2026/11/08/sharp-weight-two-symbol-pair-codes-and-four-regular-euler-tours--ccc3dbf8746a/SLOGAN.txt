The maximum number of binary Hamming-weight-two words at cyclic symbol-pair distance at least seven is \(1\) through length \(6\), \(2\) at lengths \(7,8\), \(3\) at length \(9\), and \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) thereafter. Every optimal even-length code of length at least ten is exactly an Euler-tour labeling of a connected simple four-regular graph, and every such tour yields an optimum.
