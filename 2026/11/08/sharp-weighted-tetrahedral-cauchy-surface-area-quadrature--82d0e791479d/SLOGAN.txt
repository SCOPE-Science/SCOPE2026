The complete positive-weight error spectrum for the four regular-tetrahedron projection views has a sharp lower endpoint controlled by the two smallest weights and a sharp upper endpoint controlled by a signed-weight discrepancy. Equal weights uniquely minimize worst-case surface-area error among these four fixed directions, giving \(1-\sqrt{2/3}\), with the lower and upper ratios attained by a rhombic dodecahedron and a cube.
