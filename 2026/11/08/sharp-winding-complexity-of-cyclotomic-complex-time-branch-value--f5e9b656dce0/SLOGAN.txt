For the complex-time polynomial flow \(\dot w=w^d-1\), the shortest nonzero time-branch displacement realizable with at most \(N\) signed meridian windings is exactly of order \(N^{-(\varphi(d)-2)/2}\). Branch values form a discrete lattice exactly for \(d=3,4,6\), and are dense in the complex plane for every other \(d\ge3\).
