Uniformly sampling \(k\) unmodified Hadamard rows for trace estimation can have exactly \(n(n-k)/(2(n-1))\) times the variance of \(k\) independent Rademacher probes, even on positive-definite matrices arbitrarily close to the identity. The equality cases are precisely XOR-matching-constant off-diagonal matrices, and a previously known shared random-sign modification removes the inflation with exact variance factor \((n-k)/(n-1)\).
