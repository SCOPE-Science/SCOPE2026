When a compact spectrum has an infinitely flat edge \(S(b-u)=u^\alpha h(u)\exp(-\kappa/u^\beta)\) with \(h(u)\to h(0)>0\), the expected Gaussian-spectrogram zero density has a sharp tail exponent \((\beta+2)/(\beta+1)\in(1,2)\) and coefficient that identify \(\beta\) and \(\kappa\). Its integrated frequency-tail mass obeys a matching explicit law, while the total expected zero intensity per unit time over all frequencies equals the spectral bandwidth.
