For an infinite exact-Zipf independent symbol stream, the distinct directed adjacent-pair graph has a sharp two-term expected edge count of order \(n^\gamma\log n\) with an explicit \(n^\gamma\) correction, while both directed and undirected edge counts obey almost-sure logarithmic edge-per-vertex laws. Forgetting orientation changes the leading edge-count constant by precisely \(2^{\gamma-1}\), even though the successive pairs overlap.
