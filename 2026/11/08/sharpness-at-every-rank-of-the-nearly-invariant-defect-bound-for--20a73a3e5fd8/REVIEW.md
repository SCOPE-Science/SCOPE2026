# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
For each integer \(r\ge1\), the displayed indices obey all range and collision constraints. The monomial model identifies the analytic truncated Toeplitz operator with the truncated forward power. The rank-\(r\) correction factors as \(S^kU\) with \(U^2=0\). Its kernel is therefore the exact graph \((I+U)L\). A surjective quotient map with this kernel is constructed explicitly, and applying its backward-shift composite to the graph basis yields exactly \(2r+1\) distinct monomial classes. The defect lower bound is thus a dimension identity, not a numerical extrapolation. The upper bound is Chattopadhyay–Khan, Theorem 5.4 [1]. Finite exact-arithmetic checks corroborate the indices and ranks but do not prove the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, best-knowledge assessment
Chattopadhyay–Khan [1] establish a universal bound, including small-rank tabulations, but do not present extremizers. The reviewed relevant parts of O’Loughlin [2] establish structural representations for nearly invariant spaces and unperturbed TTO kernels; Liang–Partington [3] concern full-space Toeplitz perturbations, not compressed finite-dimensional TTOs. Targeted searches for equivalent formulations, monomial compressions, sharp defects, and rank-to-defect examples found no statement covering this construction. Potential rediscovery from older finite Toeplitz-matrix or nearly invariant-subspace examples is a residual priority risk; absence of a matching search result is not proof of absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result completely resolves the natural optimality question for a recently proved universal rank-dependent defect bound, simultaneously for every perturbation rank. It proves that both the coefficient \(2\) and the additive constant \(1\) are indispensable, with concrete minimal-size-independent structural witnesses using only monomials and orthogonal matrix units. No general classification is asserted.
