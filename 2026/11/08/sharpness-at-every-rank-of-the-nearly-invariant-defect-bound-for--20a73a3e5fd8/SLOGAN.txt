The universal \(2r+1\) nearly backward-shift-invariance defect bound for kernels of rank-\(r\) perturbed truncated Toeplitz operators is sharp at every integer rank \(r\ge1\). An explicit monomial truncated Toeplitz matrix with \(r\) orthogonal matrix-unit corrections attains minimal defect \(2r+1\), already in a finite-dimensional polynomial model space.
