A proportional retained-slack cap on **every** coalition forces nonnegative symmetric pair incentives, makes the grand coalition Nash stable, and guarantees at least \(1-\delta\) of optimal welfare for \(0<\delta<1\). The factor is sharp already with three agents: a unique stable grand coalition has welfare \(1-\delta+2\varepsilon\), while a uniquely optimal pair-plus-singleton partition has welfare \(1\), with \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\).
