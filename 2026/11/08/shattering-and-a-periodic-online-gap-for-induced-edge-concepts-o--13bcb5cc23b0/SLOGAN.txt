For the edge-membership concepts of vertex-induced subgraphs of the cycle \(C_n\), the shattered supports are exactly cyclic edge subsets with no three consecutive edges, yielding a complete counting polynomial and \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\). For every \(n\equiv1\pmod3\), the sharp adaptive dimension is one greater, \(\operatorname{Ldim}=\operatorname{VCdim}+1\), whereas the dimensions coincide for \(3\mid n\).
