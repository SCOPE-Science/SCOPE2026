The shattered binary incidence supports of all onto maps \([n]\to[q]\), for \(n>q\ge2\), are exactly the row-distinct sets with fewer than \(n\) edges and at most \(n-q\) repeated colors. They have an explicit Stirling-number face polynomial, every facet has size \(n-1\), and the resulting pure complex is matroidal exactly for two colors.
