The maximal antichains of the five-atom Boolean lattice shatter exactly 310 six-point supports, falling into eleven atom-symmetry orbits, and have VC dimension six. In every Boolean lattice on \(n\) atoms, maximal-antichain membership has VC dimension between \(\binom{n-1}{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\) and \(\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\), establishing exponential-order complexity.
