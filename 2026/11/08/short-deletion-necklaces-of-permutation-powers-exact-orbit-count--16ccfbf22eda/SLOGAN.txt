For a circular power of a permutation of \(q\) distinct letters, every deletion mask of size \(t<q\) determines a different output necklace up to whole-period rotation. At \(t=q\), precisely \(q\) mask orbits merge into the necklace \([P^{k-1}]\), giving a sharp first-collision threshold and an exact Burnside count corrected by \(q-1\).
