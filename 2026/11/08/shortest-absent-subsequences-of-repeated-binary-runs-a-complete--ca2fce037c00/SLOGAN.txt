For every \(a,b,m\ge1\), the shortest absent scattered subsequences of \((0^a1^b)^m\) have an explicit maximal-run characterization and exact counts exhibiting five regimes: \(m+2\), \(2\), \(F_{2m}\), \(2^{m-1}\), and \(1\). The Fibonacci and exponential cases arise precisely when one or both host-run capacities equal two.
