For every \(k\ge2\), the shortest single binary string whose length-\(k\) factors are exactly all words avoiding `00` has length \(F_{k+2}+F_k\), where \(F_j\) is the \(j\)th Fibonacci number. The precise minimum repeat overhead is \(F_k-k+1\) windows, attained by a constructive Euler-trail augmentation and certified below by a matching potential bound.
