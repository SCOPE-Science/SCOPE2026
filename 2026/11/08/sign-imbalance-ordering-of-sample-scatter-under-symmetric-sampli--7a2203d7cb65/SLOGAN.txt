For every symmetric i.i.d. sample with a finite fourth moment and no zero atom, both the conditional mean and (unless absolute magnitudes are fixed) conditional variance of centered sample scatter strictly decrease with the absolute positive-minus-negative sign imbalance. The raw sign count is uncorrelated with scatter, but its quadratic imbalance explains exactly \(2(\mathbb E|X|)^4/\bigl((n-1)\mathbb EX^4-(n-3)(\mathbb EX^2)^2\bigr)\) of the scatter variance.
