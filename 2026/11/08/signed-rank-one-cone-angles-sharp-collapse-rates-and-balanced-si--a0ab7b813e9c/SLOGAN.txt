For simplicial cones with rank-one Gram matrix \(I_n-(1-\varepsilon)uu^{\mathsf T}\), the solid angle either collapses at the sharp \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) rate or tends to one half, depending exactly on whether the coordinates of \(u\) have mixed or unanimous signs. At fixed Gram spectrum and equal generator norms, the cone angle strictly increases with sign imbalance, and the mixed-sign collapse constant is an adjacent Gaussian order-statistic spacing.
