For every finite-dimensional split basic algebra, every ordered commuting-idempotent tuple is classified by a binary coloring of its primitive idempotents, and a single Peirce-cut formula gives its complete finite-field joint left/right regular-rank distribution. The all-arity total-count functions recover exactly the random-cluster polynomial of the symmetrized radical Peirce multigraph, while the refined rank data can distinguish opposite orientations without recovering radical multiplication.
