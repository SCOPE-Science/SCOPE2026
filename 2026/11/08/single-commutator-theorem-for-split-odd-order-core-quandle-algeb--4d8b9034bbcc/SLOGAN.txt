For every finite abelian group \(A\) of odd order, the core-quandle algebra over any splitting field has commutator subalgebra of dimension \(|A|-|A[3]|\), and **every** element of that subalgebra is a single commutator. Its entire algebra splits into orthogonal commutator and commutative ideals, giving exact commutator width one unless \(3A=0\), when the width is zero.
