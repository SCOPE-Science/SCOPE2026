A circular binary word is uniquely determined by its full cyclic trigram-frequency vector exactly when its two ordered run-length vectors are both uniform in lengths \(1\) or \(2\), or one is uniform and the other has a single distinguished run (with the one-run and monochromatic cases included separately). The numbers of singleton trigram spectra have the rational generating function \(S(z)=P(z)/((1-z^3)(1-z^4))\), with \(P(z)=2z+3z^2+4z^3+4z^4+3z^5+5z^6+6z^7+7z^8+5z^9-z^{11}-4z^{12}-2z^{14}\), and grow as \(\frac83n+O(1)\).
