A singleton nonzero-domain fiber makes every multiplicative-Hankel root-of-unity phase matrix \(\bigl(\zeta^{h(st)}\bigr)\) invertible at odd prime orders. On a prime-field parabola, self-restricted Fourier sampling has exactly rank \(p\), \((p+1)/2\), or \(1\) according as its linear-coordinate weight is nonzero, only its quadratic-coordinate weight is nonzero, or both vanish; its complete squared-singular-value multiset is given by multiplicative character sums.
