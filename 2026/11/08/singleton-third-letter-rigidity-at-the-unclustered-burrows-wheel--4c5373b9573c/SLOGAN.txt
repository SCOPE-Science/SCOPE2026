For the ordered ternary content \((m,m,1)\), with \(m\ge2\), a completely unclustered Burrows–Wheeler last column achieves the sharp \(m+1\) cyclic input-run bound if and only if \(2m+1\) is prime and \(2\) is a primitive root modulo it. In that case the unique sharp last column is \((10)^{m-1}120\); in fact, only two such fixed-content unclustered words even have fixed-point-free stable permutations.
