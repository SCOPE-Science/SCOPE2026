# Self-review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The result is proved for all positive real weight vectors and every integer \(n\ge3\). Independent support extrema for each cyclic tube give the exact margins; singleton dominance follows term by term, and the summed singleton inequalities give a global lower certificate for all centers. Equality forces the unique uniform center. The separate exact vertex enumerator and LP checker passed 20 profiles, 440 tube tests, and 5,088 distinct vertices. Finite checks corroborate the symbolic proof and do not claim an infinite enumeration.

## Originality
**PASS, provisional historical priority.** Jevtić--Jelić--Živaljević (arXiv:1703.06612) and Padrol--Pilaud--Poullot (arXiv:2109.09200) establish the building-set construction, nested facets, and deformation framework, but the inspected primary sections do not optimize reflection containment. A published result for this exact unweighted cyclohedron computes **Euclidean inradius**, a different metric invariant; an exact asymmetry result for stellohedra concerns stars, not cycles. Searches by cyclohedron, weighted cycle tube, nestohedron, and Minkowski asymmetry found no dominating source. An unindexed equivalent formulation remains possible.

## Value
**PASS.** The theorem classifies an entire natural positive-weight deformation family, not just the standard unweighted example. It gives the unique affine center, all binding reflection facets, a closed weighted-mean formula, and the sharp continuous image \((1,n-1)\). These features explain quantitatively how the family interpolates between symmetric graphical-zonotope behavior and simplex asymmetry.

## Limitations and literature
The formulas depend on a cycle's cardinality-constant simplex-sum weights; they do not cover arbitrary unequal tube coefficients or all realizations sharing a cyclohedral face lattice. The supporting facet theory is established prior mathematics; no independent audit or exhaustive historical-priority certification is asserted.
