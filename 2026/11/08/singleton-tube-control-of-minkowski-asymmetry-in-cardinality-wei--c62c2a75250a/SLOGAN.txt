For every positive cardinality-weighting of the simplex-sum cyclohedron, singleton tubes alone determine Minkowski asymmetry and the unique center is uniform. The exact asymmetry is a weighted average of tube-length offsets, specializes to \(\frac{n-1}{2}+\frac{1}{2(n-1)}\) for unit weights, and ranges sharply over \((1,n-1)\).
