# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The Lipschitz ball is exactly a zero-sum section of the edge-difference box. At a vertex, at least \(m\) edge inequalities must be tight, and the parity of the balanced exceptional length rules out every would-be vertex with a slack unit edge. This gives the binomial wall enumeration. Strict edge-geodesicity exposes each edge molecule, so active inequalities give the correct number of primal facet vertices. Direct active-set enumeration with exact fractions independently verifies the wall, its facet types, and adjacent generic counts for twenty cases. The full theorem uses the universal parity and active-constraint proof, not these finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Gordon–Petrov [1] analyze generic metric Lipschitz polytopes. Jevtić–Timotijević–Živaljević [2], Theorem 1.1 and Section 4, classify the generic weighted-cycle boundary via Bier spheres, but require exclusion of precisely the balanced subset weights considered here. The generic count is not claimed as novel. The more general threshold-sphere polytopality statement in [2], Theorem 2.3, does not yield the present nongeneric metric facet count or the nonsimplicial facet sizes. The unweighted \(p=1\) case covered in symmetric-edge literature [3,4] is expressly outside the novel claim. Searches for balanced-cycle, wall-crossing, facet, and dual norm-ball formulations did not identify a published equivalent. Residual risk: an uncatalogued nongeneric consequence of the Bier-sphere construction or another article could imply the formula.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The half-perimeter equality makes the standard generic combinatorial classification inapplicable. The all-dimensional theorem determines both the exact number and shape of facets at this natural singular boundary, and both one-sided facet counts. The strictly asymmetric local valley is a structural instability of finite-dimensional Lipschitz-free ball geometry, not a parameter-specific numerical example.

## Limits
Only real finite cycle metrics with one exceptional integer length are treated. The complete face vector and arbitrary several-weight walls remain open here. Published priority has been checked to the stated extent, not independently certified.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
