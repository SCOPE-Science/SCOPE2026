At every nontrivial balanced length \(p\) in a one-exceptional-edge metric cycle, the Lipschitz-free unit ball has exactly \(\sum_{|m-2j|\le p}\binom mj\) facets, including \(2\binom{m}{(m-p)/2}\) nonsimplicial facets with one extra vertex each. Moving the exceptional edge length to either side causes a strictly positive, explicitly asymmetric jump in facet number.
