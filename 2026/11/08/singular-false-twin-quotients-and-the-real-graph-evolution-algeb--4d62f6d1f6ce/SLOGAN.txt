For finite connected simple graphs whose false-twin quotient has singular adjacency matrix, the real adjacency and random-walk evolution algebras are isomorphic exactly for regular or bipartite biregular graphs. Every isomorphism is a weighted quotient symmetry followed by positive block scalings and orthogonal maps fixing the classwise all-ones vectors; with the nonsingular-quotient case this settles the isomorphism criterion for all such graphs over \(\mathbb R\).
