Finite reactivity rounds the sharp Péclet transition for optimal Brownian restarting: on the drift-dominated side the optimal rate vanishes as \(k^{-1/2}\) in the perfect-absorber limit, but at the critical drift it vanishes as \(k^{-1/3}\). An exact positive-root cubic supplies the uniform scaling function throughout the critical \(k^{-1/3}\) drift window.
