## Originality — PASS, provisional
Premet's full nilpotent commuting **pair** variety and Ngo's mixed simple-Lie commuting varieties do not state the minimal symplectic rank-one multituple smoothness classification. Pandey, Tarasova and Walther study the symplectic *vector* isotropy nullcone and therefore cover the irreducibility/dimension of the vector object; those facts are acknowledged as prior rather than new. The new statement concerns the singular strata of its coordinatewise sign/Veronese **endomorphism quotient**, where a zero coordinate has a larger symmetric-tensor tangent space. The finite-field count uses classical isotropic-plane enumeration and is not defended as a standalone unprecedented theorem. Searches in accessible research ledgers and mathematical records found no directly equivalent or dominating tangent/smoothness theorem. Residual risk: a general theorem on symplectic nullcone finite quotients could imply this classification; full historical priority is unverified.

## Value — PASS
The reduced commuting variety of a minimal nonzero nilpotent orbit is a natural, group-invariant Lie-theoretic moduli space. Its singular locus is a deformation-theoretic invariant that genuinely changes at the threshold between at most and more than the symplectic half-dimension, and the behavior of zero coordinates distinguishes rank one from higher symplectic rank. The all-parameter criterion and explicit tangent formula are more than a numerical special case. The finite-field span distribution provides a direct arithmetic realization of those strata.
