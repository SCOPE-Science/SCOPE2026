For binary \(n\)-player Nashtope toric varieties with \(n\ge3\), the entire singular locus is a disjoint union of \(n\) Segre varieties, each with transverse rank-one \(2\times(n-1)\) determinantal cone and multiplicity \(n-1\). These varieties are Gorenstein exactly for three players; with four or more, their singularities are not even \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein.
