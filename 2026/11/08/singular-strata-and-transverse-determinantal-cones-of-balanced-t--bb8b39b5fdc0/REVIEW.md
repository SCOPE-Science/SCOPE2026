A standard one-swap Plücker chart has source coordinates \(a,b,z,c\), where the sole deleted minor is \(c\). Direct inspection of maximal minors gives the image chart algebra \(\mathbb C[a,b,z,cz]\), since the only \(c\)-dependent coefficients in retained minors belong to the ideal generated by the \(z\)'s. Its presentation by rank-one \(2\times(d-1)^2\) minors is the standard Segre cone presentation, with two smooth sets of free coordinates. The singular stratum is therefore exactly the vertex in each chart for \(d\ge3\). Every point of the tangent-direction linear space lies in such a chart; outside it, linear coefficients of the Plücker quadrics recover the omitted coordinate, proving smoothness there.
