For every \(d\ge3\), the next-to-full balanced truncated Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Gr}^{d-1}(d,2d)\) has exactly one reduced singular component, the Segre variety \(\mathbb P^{d-1}\times\mathbb P^{d-1}\). Its transverse singularity is the affine rank-one \(2\times(d-1)^2\) determinantal cone, with constant Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity \((d-1)^2\).
