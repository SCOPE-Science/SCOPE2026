For a Poisson-reset sluggish diffusion with cost \(e^{\lambda|X|}\), the Brownian threshold \(\lambda=\sqrt r\) hides a sharp singular crossover as the sluggishness exponent \(\alpha\downarrow0\): critical mean cost grows like \(2r/[\alpha\log(1/\alpha)]\), while above threshold it is governed by an exponentially remote saddle at \((\lambda/\sqrt r)^{2/\alpha}\) with an explicit full prefactor. Below threshold the finite Brownian cost is recovered.
