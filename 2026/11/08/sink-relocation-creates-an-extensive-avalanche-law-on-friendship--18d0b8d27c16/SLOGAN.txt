On friendship graphs made of \(k\) triangles meeting at one hub, relocating the Abelian-sandpile sink from that hub to an outer vertex changes the maximum avalanche from two topplings to \(4k+1\), even though the critical group remains \((\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^k\). The complete joint toppling-and-loss polynomials are explicit, and under uniform recurrent states and nonsink additions the probability of at least \(2k-1\) topplings approaches \(5/9\).
