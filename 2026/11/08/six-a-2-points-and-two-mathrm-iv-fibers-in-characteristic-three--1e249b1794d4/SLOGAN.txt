For an explicit nonempty open family of characteristic-three reductions of the Rams–Schütt quartics with a line of the second kind, the surface develops exactly six \(A_2\) points in two groups of three; its K3 resolution has a genus-one pencil with two \(\mathrm{IV}^{*}\) triple-line fibers and six \(\mathrm I_1\) fibers. The distinguished line becomes a purely inseparable trisection of degree \(3\).
