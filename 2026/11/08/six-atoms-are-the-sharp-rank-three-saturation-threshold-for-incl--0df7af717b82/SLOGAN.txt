The inclusion-only powerset posets \(B_n=(\mathcal P([n]),\subseteq)\) have exactly seven distinct rank-three first-order theories: one for each \(n<6\), and one for every \(n\ge6\). The exact cutoff of six atoms is two lower than the known cutoff for the full Boolean-algebra signature, exhibiting a strict loss of bounded-rank distinguishing power when Boolean operations are omitted.
