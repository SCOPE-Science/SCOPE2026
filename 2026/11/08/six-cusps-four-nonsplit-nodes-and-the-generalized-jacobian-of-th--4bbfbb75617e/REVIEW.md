# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact reduction \(F=2S^3+(XYZ)^2\) yields cyclic gradient equations that exhaust the ten geometric singularities. Six cusp local equations are exactly \(u^2+2v^3=0\) in étale coordinates; the four Hessian tangent discriminants are nonsquares. Published rationality in characteristic five identifies the normalization over the algebraic closure, and genus zero over \(\mathbb F_5\) yields \(\mathbb P^1\). The standard normalization exact sequence gives the group-scheme decomposition; Frobenius branch splitting gives the entire zeta function. Explicit \(\mathbb F_5\), \(\mathbb F_{25}\) counts agree. Main external premise: the published geometric integrality/rationality of the characteristic-five curve.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Inoue--Kato and Edge address the classical complex curve and its pencil. Giulietti--Kawakita--Lia--Montanucci (arXiv:1805.06317, Remark 3.1 and Theorem 3.4) already establish *rationality* in characteristic five; that assertion is not new. The evaluated ten-point cusp/node basket, nonsplit branches, specific generalized-Jacobian isomorphism over \(\mathbb F_5\), zero smooth base-field points and exact zeta function are additional mathematical assertions, not immediate logical consequences of knowing the curve is rational. The material available for comparison did not state them. Remaining risk: earlier modular-singularity or arithmetic work could have evaluated the same plane-model invariants.

## Value — PASS
The degeneration at five is distinguished: the complex four-nodal genus-six plane model becomes a rational curve with both wild-looking characteristic-dependent cusps and arithmetic nonsplit nodes. The classification separates geometric local degeneration (six additive factors) from Frobenius arithmetic (four nonsplit tori), making the exact generalized Jacobian and the point-free smooth locus intrinsically motivated. This is a complete natural arithmetic classification for a specific classical exceptional fiber, not an arbitrary slice.
