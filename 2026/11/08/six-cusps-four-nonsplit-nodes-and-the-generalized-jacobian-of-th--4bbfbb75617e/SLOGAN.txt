The characteristic-five Wiman plane sextic is rational but has six rational cusps and four rational nodes whose branches split only over \(\mathbb F_{25}\). Its connected generalized Jacobian is \(\mathbb G_a^6\times(\operatorname{Res}^1_{\mathbb F_{25}/\mathbb F_5}\mathbb G_m)^4\), and its entire smooth locus has no \(\mathbb F_5\)-points.
