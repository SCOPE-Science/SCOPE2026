# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

For nonzero linear terms, any such element extends to an automorphism-coordinate pair because all radical elements cube to zero in characteristic three. Thus the first Jordan type is exactly \((3,3,3)\). For degree-two initial terms, the matrix \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}b&a\\c&b\end{smallmatrix}\right)\) controls all degree-three image directions, and \(f^2=(b^2-ac)x^2y^2\) controls the only potential length-three Jordan block. For degree-three or four initial terms the ideals have dimensions two or one by direct multiplication. These steps give all ranks and partitions. Counting solutions to \(b^2=ac\) gives \(q^2\), leading to exactly the recorded multiplicities. The unit-orbit stabilizer of a generator of an ideal of dimension \(r\) has order \(q^{9-r}\), so the number of generators per ideal is \((q-1)q^{r-1}\). The claimed ideal frequencies and total follow. The six reduced strata are explicit affine opens, with the quadratic cone smooth off the origin. The standalone original-multiplication checker exhausts all \(6561\) radical elements of \(\mathbb F_3[x,y]/(x^3,y^3)\) and independently enumerates principal ideals; it additionally checks 1064 cases over \(\mathbb F_9\).

## Originality: PASS (provisional)

Carlson--Friedlander--Pevtsova (arXiv:0707.3845 and arXiv:1106.4474) motivates rank/Jordan invariants through cyclic shifted subgroups; this controls nonzero linear directions but does not assert the complete nilpotent-element and principal-ideal counts. Basili--Iarrobino (arXiv:0709.2304) gives generic matrix-pencil results under different hypotheses. Altafi--Iarrobino--Khatami (arXiv:1810.00716) addresses linear forms and zero/large characteristic rather than all inhomogeneous radical elements in characteristic three. The closest accessible mathematical records concern different algebras, characteristics or invariants. The complete finite-field element and principal-ideal census, together with the discriminant geometry, was not implied by these inspected statements. An unlocated general local-Artinian principal-ideal theorem may still cover it; absence from a search is not proof of priority.

## Value: PASS

The radical is the entire nonunit locus in the first odd-prime rank-two elementary-abelian group algebra. Its multiplication Jordan types and dimensions of principal ideals are canonical invariants, not ad hoc statistic choices. The six exhaustive rank strata, the missing rank five, the discriminant cone's geometric role and the unexpectedly compact count \(3(q^2+q+1)+1\) for all principal ideals provide precise structural and arithmetic information. This is a natural finite classification with infinite-field and finite-field content; it does not claim a broader classification for all modular group algebras.
