In the modular group algebra \(\mathbb F_q[C_3\times C_3]\) for every power \(q\) of three, radical multiplication has exactly six Jordan types, governed by its linear term and one quadratic discriminant. Every rank stratum has an explicit size and smooth irreducible reduced geometry, and the algebra has exactly \(3(q^2+q+1)+1\) distinct principal ideals, including the unit ideal.
