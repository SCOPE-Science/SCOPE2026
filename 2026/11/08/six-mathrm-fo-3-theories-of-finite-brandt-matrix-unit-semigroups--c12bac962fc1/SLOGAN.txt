In the named-zero ternary multiplication language, finite Brandt matrix-unit semigroups have exactly six counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{3}\) theories: the index sizes \(1,2,3,4,5\) are separate, and every index size at least \(6\) belongs to one final class. Their sharp quantifier-rank class counts are \(1,2,3,5,6\) at ranks \(0\) through \(4\), with no further distinctions at greater depth.
