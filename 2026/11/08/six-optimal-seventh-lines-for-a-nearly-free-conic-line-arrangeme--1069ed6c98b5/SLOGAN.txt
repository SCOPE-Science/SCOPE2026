For the explicit nearly free degree-eight conic plus six lines over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), the sharp Tjurina number among seventh-line extensions retaining only nodes, tacnodes and ordinary triple points is \(46\), attained by exactly six lines. Since a free or nearly free extension of degree nine would require \(48\) or \(47\), respectively, this direct construction cannot settle the degree-nine existence problem under the three specified singularity types.
