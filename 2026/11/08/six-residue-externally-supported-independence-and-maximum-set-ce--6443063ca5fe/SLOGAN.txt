For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\), externally supported independence has a sharp six-residue law: \(\alpha_{\mathrm{es}}=\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\) except for a one-vertex deficit when \(n\equiv3,5\pmod6\). An exact finite transfer certificate also determines the number of every maximum ESI set, from six at multiples of six to cubic growth in one residue class, and counts ESI sets of all sizes.
