# Scientific review: the rational BKS sextic over \(\mathbb F_{125}\)

## Correctness
PASS. The finite claim is proved by complete canonical enumeration of all \(15751\) projective points and dual lines over a cubic field with a checked irreducible defining polynomial. Two independent field constructions and line-generation methods reproduce the same \(136\) curve points, \(120\) six-secants, and full incidence spectrum. The line-point and point-pair double-counts are verified, not assumed. The proof does not extrapolate finite observations to unspecified fields.

## Originality
PASS under inspected sources, with residual historical-priority uncertainty. The 2022 BKS curve paper proves the curve's underlying geometry and its rational singularities. The 2023 arc-completeness paper states the \(r=3,4\) completeness cases were open, proves a large-exponent result only for \(r\ge5\), and already establishes which *curve* points lie on no six-secant. Those results do not imply the stated \(q=5,r=3\) exterior uncovered cardinality, exact line-weight distribution, or off-curve line-arrangement incidence. Actual external database and literature searches for aliases and the numerical invariants found no statement with dominating coverage. Equivalent finite-geometry descriptions in older unindexed work remain a possible risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This resolves one explicitly excluded small-exponent instance in an established curve-arc completeness problem and determines the full incidence structure, not merely an isolated point count. The \(6555\) exterior holes, complete \(0,1,2,6\) incidence spectrum, and absence of exterior triple concurrency describe a natural finite-plane configuration with possible consequences for extending arcs and near-MDS parity-check configurations. Neither a universal formula for all base fields nor an orbit decomposition is claimed.

## Sources and limits
Primary full texts: Borges–Korchmáros–Speziali, arXiv:2202.05765, Proposition 4.24; Korchmáros–Nagy–Szőnyi, arXiv:2302.10162, especially Sections 4.2, 7.2 and 8.2, JCTA 204 (2024) 105851. Limitations: one finite plane, exact incidence rather than a complete orbit classification; unresolved risk from inaccessible or unindexed prior catalogues. Same-field numerical agreement alone is not the proof: both complete independent verifiers are supplied.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
