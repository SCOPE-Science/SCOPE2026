The rational BKS sextic over \(\mathbb F_{125}\) has exactly \(120\) six-secant lines, but they miss precisely \(6555\) exterior projective points: its \(136\) rational points form an incomplete \((136,6)\)-arc. The full six-secant configuration has \(120\) sixfold intersections on the curve and \(5340\) double intersections off it, with no exterior triple concurrency.
