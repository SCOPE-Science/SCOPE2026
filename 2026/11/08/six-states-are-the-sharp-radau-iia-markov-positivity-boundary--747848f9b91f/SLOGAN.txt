For the third-order, two-stage Radau IIA map, every continuous-time Markov generator on at most five states has stochastic end steps sufficiently close to zero, but a six-state pure-birth chain has a negative transition for every sufficiently small positive step. That six-state chain is stochastic exactly at \(h=0\) or \(h\in[\alpha,3]\), where \(\alpha\) is the unique positive root of \(h^5+30h^4+180h^3+360h^2+180h-72\).
