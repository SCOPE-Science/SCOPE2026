For the canonical Hilbert-space divisor with two prescribed antipodal zeros in the integer-power disk kernel \(K_\beta(z,w)=(1-z\overline w)^{-\beta}\), the number of additional disk zeros near the boundary is exactly \(4(\lceil\beta/6\rceil-1)\). The first four occur at \(\beta=7\), and all extra zeros are simple, nonreal, and occur in sign/conjugation quartets.
