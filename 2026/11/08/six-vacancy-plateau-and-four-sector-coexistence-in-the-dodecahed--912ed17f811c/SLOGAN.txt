The classical spin-one antiferromagnet on the twenty-site dodecahedral graph has exactly three zero-temperature occupancy regimes: 20 active spins, 14 active spins, and none, with transitions at \(D/J=1/2\) and \(D/J=15/14\). The first transition has four coexisting occupancy sectors and exactly 1280 labeled ground states; the intermediate six-vacancy plateau has exactly 40.
