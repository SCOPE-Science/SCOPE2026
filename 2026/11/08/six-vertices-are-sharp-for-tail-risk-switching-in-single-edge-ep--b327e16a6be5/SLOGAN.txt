For a uniformly seeded independent-transmission epidemic on a tree, five vertices or fewer always admit one edge cut that minimizes the probability of crossing any fixed outbreak-size threshold for every transmissibility. Six vertices are sharp: on the broom with edges \((0,1),(0,2),(0,3),(3,4),(4,5)\), minimizing the chance of at least three infections switches from cut \((0,3)\) to cut \((3,4)\) exactly at \(p=3/5\).
