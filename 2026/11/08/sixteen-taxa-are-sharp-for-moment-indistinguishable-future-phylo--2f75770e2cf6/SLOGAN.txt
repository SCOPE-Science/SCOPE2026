For any two equal-size rooted unit-edge binary subtrees U,V, cross-grouping two copies of each produces a pair of future-phylogenetic-diversity laws whose generating-polynomial difference factors exactly as q^(2s) z(1-z)(F_U-F_V)^2. Their first two moments agree, while the third-moment difference is a sharp negative square; for the four-leaf caterpillar and balanced subtree, it is -6 q^12(1-q)^2. The earlier sixteen-leaf first-collision classification belongs to published rarefaction work, not this contribution.
