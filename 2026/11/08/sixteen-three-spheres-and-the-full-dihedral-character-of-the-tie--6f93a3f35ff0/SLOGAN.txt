The ordinary matching complex of the Tietze graph is integrally homotopy equivalent to a wedge of sixteen three-spheres: \(\mathcal M(T)\simeq\bigvee^{16}S^3\). Its full twelve-element dihedral automorphism group acts on rational third homology with rotation traces \((16,0,-2,0,-2,0)\) and zero trace on every reflection, giving four distinct one-dimensional constituents and three copies of each two-dimensional constituent.
