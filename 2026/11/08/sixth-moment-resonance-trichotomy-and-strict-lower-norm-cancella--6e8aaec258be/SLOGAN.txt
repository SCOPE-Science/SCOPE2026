Among real two-tone Fourier spaces, the sharp sixth-to-second moment ratio distinguishes exactly the frequency multiples \(2\), \(3\), and \(5\); all other pairs have the same sharp interval \([5/2,25/4]\). Third- and fifth-harmonic resonances admit distinct strict lower cancellations, with exact algebraic constants and complete extremizers; the third-harmonic upper constant is already covered by a published four-term result.
