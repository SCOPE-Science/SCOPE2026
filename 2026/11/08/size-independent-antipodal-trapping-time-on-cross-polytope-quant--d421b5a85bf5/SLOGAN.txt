For a coherently hopping particle on the cross-polytope graph \(K_{2n}\setminus M_n\), starting at the unique nonneighbor of a single absorber guarantees absorption in a mean time \(3/\kappa+\kappa(1-2/m+4/m^2)/(4J^2)\), where \(m=2n-2\); this mean stays bounded as \(n\to\infty\). The exact result and optimal absorption rate quantify a counterintuitive trapping selectivity despite the starting site having no direct link to the trap.
