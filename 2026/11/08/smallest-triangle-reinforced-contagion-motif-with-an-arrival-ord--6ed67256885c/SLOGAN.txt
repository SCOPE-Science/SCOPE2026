A five-person, six-link network is the smallest connected unweighted triangle-contagion network in which increasing triadic reinforcement reverses which of two individuals is more likely to be infected first from a single seed. The exact winning probability is \(2(q^2+5q+7)/(3(q+3)(q+4))\), with a unique crossover at \(q=(1+\sqrt{33})/2\).
