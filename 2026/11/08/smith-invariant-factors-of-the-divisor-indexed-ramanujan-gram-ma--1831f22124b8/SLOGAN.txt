The unweighted integer Gram matrix of the divisor-indexed Ramanujan-sum table has an explicit Smith normal form at every modulus: the prime-power factors are obtained by a one-pass rule that replaces adjacent exponents \(2i,2i+2\) by the equal pair \(2i+1,2i+1\) exactly when \(p\mid(a-i+1)\). The formula also determines the entire cokernel, its exponent, and the exact primes at which the Gram matrix loses all rank modulo \(p\).
