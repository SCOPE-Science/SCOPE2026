For every nonzero contractive proportional-reset factor, the free Brownian stationary density is infinitely smooth and analytic everywhere except at the reset point, where it fails every finite Gevrey class. Its exact even origin derivatives grow in magnitude as \(p(0)(r/D)^n\rho^{-n^2}Q_\rho\) for \(\rho=|a|\in(0,1)\), despite the density's Gaussian-like central appearance.
