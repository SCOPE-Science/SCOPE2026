# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The primary article supplies the parameter-independent Dyck MMEs and almost-sure singleton symbolic coding. The unstable inverse branches and stable forward branches are affine positive contractions for every interior parameter; their coefficients and derivatives satisfy explicit uniform bounds on each compact parameter range. Termwise differentiation of each coding series is justified by a summable \((n+1)^q\rho^{n-q}\) envelope, and bounded derivatives allow dominated differentiation against a fixed symbolic measure. The center coordinate is genuinely parameter-independent. The same argument yields analytic polynomial response on small complex neighborhoods. An exact-rational finite-word test verifies coefficients, derivatives, images and one-step truncation bounds, but does not substitute for the infinite-word analytic estimate.

## Originality — PASS within checked evidence
The closest primary theorem, Saiki--Takahasi--Yamamoto--Yorke Theorem 2.6, establishes only weak-star continuity of the two maximal-entropy measures and immediately poses differentiability as Conjecture 2.7. Neither its Sections 3.5--3.6 nor Section 4.3 supplies the derivative-series or \(C^r\) response. Earlier symbolic-measure work, mixing work and subsequent periodic-orbit equidistribution do not cover this parameter question. Searches for parameter response and alternative terminology located no more general theorem implying this exact claim. A hidden or unindexed stronger result remains a publication-priority risk, and this is not an independent priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result answers the expressly formulated smooth-observable part of Conjecture 2.7 throughout the natural parameter square and for both coexisting maximal-entropy measures. It shows that the nonuniform center creates no loss of smoothness for these observables because the parameter dependence lies entirely in two contracting affine codings. The formula gives computable response coefficients and exact center-marginal rigidity; the statement is substantially stronger than continuity and is not a routine recalculation of an existing invariant density.

## Limitations and closest literature
The rigorous statement is restricted to the affine family, its two maximal-entropy laws and appropriately smooth observables; it does not imply norm-differentiability against arbitrary continuous tests, total-variation response or parameter-uniform estimates up to the boundary. Closest published results: arXiv:2401.00836v2 Theorem 2.6 and Conjecture 2.7; arXiv:2209.04905 on symbolic coding; arXiv:2307.08119 on mixing; arXiv:2409.01261 on periodic-orbit distributions. Residual novelty uncertainty remains for unindexed or later literature.
