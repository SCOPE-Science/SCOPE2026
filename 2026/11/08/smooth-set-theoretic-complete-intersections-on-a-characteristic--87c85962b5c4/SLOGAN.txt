On the characteristic-two ruled cubic \(x^2w=y^2z\), the smooth set-theoretic complete-intersection curves have unbounded degree and genus: for every \(d\ge1\) they occur in degree \(3d\) and genus \(1+(3d^2-5d)/2\). Every such curve has a sharp generic Cartier multiplicity two in a hypersurface section of degree \(2d\), because Frobenius squares of scroll sections descend through the purely inseparable normalization.
