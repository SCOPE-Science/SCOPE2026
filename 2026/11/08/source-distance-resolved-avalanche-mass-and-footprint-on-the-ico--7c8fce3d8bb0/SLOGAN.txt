On the icosahedral Abelian sandpile with one sink, the exact joint law of avalanche mass and footprint depends on the source-to-sink distance \(d\in\{1,2,3\}\). At \(d=1\) every site topples at most once and the nonzero mass coefficients form a palindrome, whereas at \(d=2,3\) repeated topplings produce respective sharp maxima of \(28\) and \(29\).
