For a one-particle excitation trapped on \(K_{m,n}\) with both parts of size at least two, the exact mean first-capture time under uniform site dephasing differs sharply according to whether the initial site shares the trap's part or lies opposite it. The two sources have distinct optimal noise laws \(\gamma_A^*\sim J\sqrt{(m-2)n}\) and \(\gamma_B^*\sim J\sqrt{(m+1)n}\) for fixed \(m\ge3\), while at \(m=2\) dephasing never helps the same-part source.
