Coordinate controllability of the fixed Laplacian on the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) is governed by whether the unactuated squares form a forest in \(K_{m,n}\), with an additional connected-component balance test exactly when \(m=n\). This yields every admissible actuator set and the exact minimum-placement count \(n^{2n-2}-n^2((n-1)^2+1)\) on square boards of side \(n\ge3\).
