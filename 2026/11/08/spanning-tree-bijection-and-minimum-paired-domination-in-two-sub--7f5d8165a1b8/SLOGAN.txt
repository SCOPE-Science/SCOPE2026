The minimum paired dominating sets of \(K(n,2)\) are completely classified: at \(n=5\) they correspond bijectively to the \(125\) labeled spanning trees on five vertices, while for \(n\ge6\) they arise from exactly three four-edge auxiliary graph shapes. Their count is \(180\binom n6+315\binom n7+105\binom n8\), with the exceptional \(n=4\) count equal to one.
