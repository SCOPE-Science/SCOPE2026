For a fixed connected graph, uniformly subdividing every edge makes the rooted-coordinate volume of its polar symmetric edge polytope asymptotic to \(2^{m|E|}(3/2\pi)^{g/2}/\sqrt{\tau(H^{(m)})}\). The universal first relative correction is \(-3/(20m)\) times the sum of squared **uniform-spanning-tree edge-exclusion probabilities** of the original graph.
