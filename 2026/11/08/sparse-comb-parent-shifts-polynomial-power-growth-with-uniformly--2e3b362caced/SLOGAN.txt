A continuum of explicit unweighted parent shifts on sparsely branching comb trees is Rota-universal throughout the open unit disk while its ordinary powers grow like \(n^{\alpha/2}\) for arbitrary \(0<\alpha<1\). Every positive power nevertheless has essential norm exactly \(\sqrt2\), and the resolvent near the spectral edge has the sharp growth order \(\varepsilon^{-1-\alpha/2}\).
