In uniform-edge directed trust games on sparse independent-edge networks, the Shapley credit of a typical vertex has an explicit independent-Poisson plus compound-Poisson limit, and the fraction of zero-credit vertices converges to \(e^{-2\lambda+\lambda e^{-\lambda}}\) when the expected outdegree tends to \(\lambda>0\). Zero-credit vertices disappear in the sharp window \(2np-\log n\to t\), where their number converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(e^{-t})\).
