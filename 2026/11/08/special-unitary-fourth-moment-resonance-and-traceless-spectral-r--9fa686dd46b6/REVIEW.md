# Scientific scope and evidence

**Correctness.** The fourth-moment identities use central selection, balanced unitary integration, and the alternating determinant line. The sharp unrestricted bounds follow from the normalized squared singular values and the determinant magnitude and phase. The endpoints are attained by explicit coefficients; the full ranges follow by continuity. The accompanying finite diagnostic does not replace the proof.

**Relationship to earlier results.** The moment identities are special cases of known Haar integration machinery. The result here is the unrestricted sharp optimization, including all endpoint equality cases. The trace-zero determinant interval is used only as an attributed comparison: the known centered-sample kurtosis bounds and Newton's identity give its endpoints. The checked literature does not establish exhaustive priority for the unrestricted theorem.

**Value and scope.** The theorem distinguishes the unique quartic Gaussian excess and its determinant-phase constraint on the unrestricted coefficient space. It does not address additional representations, higher moments, or dimensions below three.
