For real fundamental matrix coefficients on \(\mathrm{SU}(n)\), the Gaussian fourth-moment ceiling \(3\) fails in exactly one dimension \(n\ge3\): \(\mathrm{SU}(4)\) has sharp ratio \(7/2\), caused by a determinant invariant. Within traceless Hermitian coefficients, the sharp range is \([77/30,7/2]\), with rigid \(3+1\) and \(2+2\) eigenvalue patterns.
