For random-order exact drift–diffusion splitting of an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck sampler, one explicit ordering probability uniquely minimizes the stationary covariance error across every Gaussian eigenmode, but exact covariance is possible only for isotropic targets. Even with exact covariance, every nontrivial order randomization creates positive fourth cumulants (and positive cross-mode square dependence), so the Gaussian equilibrium law is never exactly preserved at finite stepsize.
