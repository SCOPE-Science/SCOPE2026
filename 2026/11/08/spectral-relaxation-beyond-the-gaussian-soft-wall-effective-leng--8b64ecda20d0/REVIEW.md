# Scientific review: Gaussian soft-wall spectral relaxation

**Correctness — PASS.** For fixed positive \(L\) and wall stiffnesses, the confining reversible generator has compact resolvent. A one-node Sturm eigenfunction is recovered by exact Gaussian-tail parabolic-cylinder impedances. The gamma-function origin values and their small-parameter expansion give the secular phase equation and the stated \(O(T^3)\) remainder. A standalone 75-digit checker passed 125 numerical assertions, which do not replace the analytic proof.

**Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk.** Darapaneni (arXiv:2610.35145v1) treats the same unit-stiffness diffusion and obtains stationary Gibbs formulas and coarse mixing bounds. In accessible full-text statements the positive spectral eigenvalue and the coefficient beyond its mass-only approximation are not given. Targeted research-index searches and comparisons to jump-boundary Brownian spectral work and adaptive Langevin theory located no directly implying prior claim. The latter two works were accessible only at abstract level, so undiscovered general spectral asymptotics remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem exposes an exact, strictly negative order-\(T^{5/2}\) spectral relaxation effect not captured by the Gibbs partition function, for an entire family of asymmetric quadratic soft walls. It supplies a motivated boundary-layer refinement of reflected-Brownian relaxation in a recent flat-potential model.

**Limits.** One dimension, fixed parameters, small positive temperature. No total-variation cutoff or higher-dimensional claim. Historical priority and proof have not been independently validated.
