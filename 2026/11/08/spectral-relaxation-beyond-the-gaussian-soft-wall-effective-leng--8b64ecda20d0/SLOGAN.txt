For a one-dimensional diffusion confined by two Gaussian quadratic soft walls, the first positive spectral eigenvalue equals \(\pi^2T/Z_T^2\) through order \(T^2\), but has an explicit strictly negative correction of order \(T^{5/2}\) that the exact stationary normalization \(Z_T\) misses. The correction holds for unequal wall stiffnesses and is determined by \(\log2-\pi/6\).
