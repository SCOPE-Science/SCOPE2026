For every connected edge-transitive regular bipartite graph, the perfect-matching polytope has a **round John ellipsoid**, and both its inradius and circumradius depend only on the bipartition size and vertex degree, not on the detailed graph. In particular, the forbidden-diagonal (World Cup) doubly stochastic polytope has exact squared inradius \(n/((n-1)(n^2-3n+1))\) and squared circumradius \(n(n-2)/(n-1)\) in every order \(n\ge3\).
