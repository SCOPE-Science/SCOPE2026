# Scientific review: split-saturation queries of partitions

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof establishes necessity through the label pattern that makes exactly one split negative, sufficiency by connected components of the selected crossing-pair graph, and a forest bound through a coordinate-flip obstruction on cycles. Tree fundamental cuts attain the bound and are uniquely reconstructed by split-separation distance. An independent all-positive-path refinement argument proves the upper Littlestone bound. An exhaustive second implementation of the trace predicate checks all split-query supports through five elements and replay of Prüfer tree certificates; the small computed polynomials and facet data do not replace the general proof.

**Originality — PASS within inspected sources.** The precise Boolean-algebra equivalent formulation was sought, not ignored: an irredundant family is a classical concept explicitly defined in Koppelberg (1989, Section 4.4, Definition 4.21); Proposition 4.23 already covers a general property of maximal irredundant families. Nothing about that classical definition is claimed as newly discovered. The inspected finite partition-learning literature (arXiv:2305.13402, arXiv:2112.07897) instead concerns pairwise same-cluster queries and does not directly imply the labeled-tree census of maximum-size split-saturation shattered supports or its Littlestone dimension. *Injective Split Systems* (2023) concerns Buneman graphs of split families and does not compute these partition-learning traces. Targeted searches for algebraic irredundance, finite Boolean generating families, Cayley trees, equivalent parameters, and exact shattering tables uncovered no directly dominating theorem. The principal unresolved historical risk is that a specialized finite-Boolean-algebra paper could contain the same tree correspondence under an alternative name.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The query predicate has natural meaning as certification that a proposed bipartition does not cut any hidden cluster. The full shattering criterion enables exact testing of arbitrary split supports; the extremal layer has a canonical bijection to labeled trees and sharp exact \(n^{n-2}\) counts; the online dimension is sharp for every ground-set order despite Bell-number growth of the concept class. The nonpurity and exact small facet data show that the complex has meaningful structure beyond a single dimension. The work does not merely rename classical irredundance: it identifies the learning interpretation, sharp adaptive dimension, and exact extremal tree layer.

**Closest literature and limits.** Koppelberg (1989), Section 4.4, is the most important equivalent-formulation source; possible unlocated finite-algebra enumerations remain the chief novelty risk. Other named works: DePavia–Medrano Martín del Campo–Tani (2023), arXiv:2305.13402; Liu–Mukherjee (2021), arXiv:2112.07897; *Injective Split Systems* (2023), DOI:10.1007/s00373-023-02660-w. The infinite-family proof is elementary, and computations are exact but restricted to at most five ground elements. Independent external checking has not occurred.
