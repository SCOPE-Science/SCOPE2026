For the concept class of all set partitions queried by whether each unordered split is respected, a split family is shattered exactly when every split possesses a crossing pair unseparated by all other queries. The maximum-size shattered families are precisely the fundamental cuts of labeled trees, yielding \(n^{n-2}\) top faces and \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\operatorname{Ldim}=n-1\); already at four elements the shattering complex is nonpure.
