For every odd finite field, idempotent Lie-Nijenhuis projections of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) have exact rank counts \((1,q^2(q+1),q^2(q+1),1)\) and exactly eight automorphism-conjugacy orbits. The six nontrivial orbits split by zero, square and nonsquare discriminant; each nontrivial rank forms a smooth geometrically irreducible three-dimensional affine-plane bundle over \(\mathbb P^1\).
