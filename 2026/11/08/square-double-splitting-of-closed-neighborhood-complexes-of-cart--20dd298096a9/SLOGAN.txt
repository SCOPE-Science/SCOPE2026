For any two finite connected graphs without triangles or four-cycles, the closed-neighborhood complex of their Cartesian product collapses to a doubled-square complex and has homotopy type \((|H|\times|J|)\vee\bigvee^{|E(H)||E(J)|}S^2\). In particular, the scale-two graph Čech nerve of \(C_m\square C_n\) for \(m,n\ge5\) is a torus wedged with \(mn\) two-spheres, integrally.
