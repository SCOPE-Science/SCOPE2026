These yield a literal three-factor simplicial join with a two-simplex-and-matching graph in one factor. The radius-two equivalence follows under the checked girth-eight hypothesis of Adamaszek's published Proposition 2.2. The dependency-free checker independently verifies complete graph distances, face counts, mod-two homology and signed Lefschetz characters in stated finite ranges. The proof, not the finite enumeration, covers all \(m\ge3\).
