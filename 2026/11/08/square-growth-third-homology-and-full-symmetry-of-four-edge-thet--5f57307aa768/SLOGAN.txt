For every \(m\ge3\), the vertex-metric Rips filtration of \(m\) four-edge paths joining common poles changes from \(\bigvee^{m-1}S^1\) at scales one and two to \(\bigvee^{(m-1)^2}S^3\) at scale three, before becoming contractible at scale four. The full graph symmetry acts on third integral homology by the tensor square of the augmentation lattice, with one primitive invariant class.
