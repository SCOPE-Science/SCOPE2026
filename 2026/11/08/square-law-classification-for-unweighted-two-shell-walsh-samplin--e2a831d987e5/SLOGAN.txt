The uniform union of exactly two complete Boolean Hamming shells is an exact tight sampler for the constant-plus-coordinate Walsh band if and only if the cube dimension is a square or one less than a square; every admissible pair of shells is explicit. The square-dimensional designs are also three-wise independent, whereas the near-square designs have the exact nonzero cubic Walsh defect \(2/[(n-1)(n-2)]\). The complete sampling Gram spectrum is explicit for all other two-shell choices.
