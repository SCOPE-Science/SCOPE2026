Vinet and Zhedanov 2011 give the perfect-transfer spin chain's Krawtchouk spectral weights, and Le et al. 2017 give the general sink-dark-eigenvector framework. These together imply the present statement after a focused calculation. Examined sections of those primary sources and overlap searches did not disclose the explicit two-detector quantitative capture theorem in this physical system. Originality is claimed only for the physical joint classification, including exact initial-state absorption efficiency, independent rates and minimal local-remedy distinction. ## Mathematical value — PASS, bounded
The chain is a canonical precisely engineered perfect-transfer medium. An added sink at a fixed local site introduces a striking arithmetically sparse two-mode trapping phenomenon: capture is perfect for every nonsquare chain length but not for square lengths, with an exact measurable endpoint deficit. The neighboring independent sink destroys the protected subspace without re-engineering the Hamiltonian, and the quantitative survival law tends to zero at a sharp rate along resonances. This is a natural, full infinite-size classification and a transport design boundary rather than an arbitrary finite-table lookup. Its derivation uses elementary known ingredients, and it does not claim a new general spectral theorem or higher-excitation control.
