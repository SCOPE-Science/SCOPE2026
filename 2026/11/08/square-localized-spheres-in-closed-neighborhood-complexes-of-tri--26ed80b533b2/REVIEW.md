# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The graph is finite, connected, simple and triangle-free; the four-cycle hypothesis quantifies over *all* cycles of length four, not a chosen packing. The common-center lemma ensures each lower simplex in the displayed matching has a unique center and that each higher coface is private. Descending-size removal verifies genuine free-face collapses, leaving exactly the graph plus a tetrahedral boundary for every square. Distinct squares meet in at most one vertex under the two hypotheses. Two disjoint triangulated disks per square provide a regular CW model. One disk per square eliminates a unique old rim edge without disconnecting the graph; the remaining disk attaches nullhomotopically and gives one two-sphere. The chosen square cycles are primitive by evaluation on their pairwise disjoint rim edges. These steps establish the homotopy, integral homology, free group and sign-module claims. The standalone verifier passed on 3,195 complete connected labeled graphs of orders three through six, including 1,323 with a square, 83 qualifying randomized larger graphs, and two explicit noncactus two-square examples. Finite checks corroborate the unrestricted proof; they do not substitute for it.

## Originality — PASS, residual uncertainty retained
The closest published exact-square result is for the *open* Lovász neighborhood complex, which cannot provide the closed-neighborhood two-sphere classes. Earlier closed-neighborhood/girth results handle square-free graphs and the hypercube's one-square boundary, and the related Cartesian-product result assumes product structure, not arbitrary graphs with isolated square edges. Comparison was by hypotheses, objects and implication rather than title matching. The claimed all-graph mixed-dimensional classification and natural signed square homology were not identified in checked sources or available historical claims. Priority cannot be guaranteed against unindexed or inaccessible stronger literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The graph class is natural and includes noncactus graphs with several isolated squares in a larger cycle system. This gives a sharp structural answer at the first failure of the classical girth-at-least-five Čech reconstruction regime: every isolated square converts one independent original loop into a genuine new integral two-sphere, while other loops remain. The automorphism sign representation distinguishes the topological sphere orientations, not obtainable from Betti numbers alone. The proof is an exact all-graph homotopy classification and a reusable localization lemma, rather than arbitrary finite recomputation or a renaming of a textbook result.

## Nearest literature and limits

Adams–Shukla–Singh, *Čech complexes of hypercube graphs*, arXiv:2212.05871 (2022; revised 2023): Lemma 2.3 covers the square-free boundary and Theorem 4.1 a different hypercube family. *Graph-like neighborhood complexes with edge-disjoint four-cycles* (2026): open neighborhood complex on a canonical bipartite double cover; no second-homology sphere classes in its theorem. Accessible earlier related closed-neighborhood results include square-free graphs and Cartesian products with product-square cells; these do not cover the exact arbitrary graph domain claimed here. Other unindexed covering results remain a possibility. No equivariant wedge decomposition is claimed.
