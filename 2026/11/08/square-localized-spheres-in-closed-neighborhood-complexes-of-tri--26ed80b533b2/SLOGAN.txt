For any finite connected triangle-free graph whose distinct four-cycles are edge-disjoint, each square turns one independent graph circle into a two-sphere in its closed-neighborhood complex: \(\mathcal C(G)\simeq\bigvee^{e-v+1-q}S^1\vee\bigvee^qS^2\). The integral second-homology sphere classes form a signed permutation representation on the four-cycles, with signs determined by permutations of each square's four vertices.
