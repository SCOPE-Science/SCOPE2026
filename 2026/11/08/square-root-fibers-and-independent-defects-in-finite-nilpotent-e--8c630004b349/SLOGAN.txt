The quadratic fiber \(\{X\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb F_q[t]}((\mathbb F_q[t]/t^2)^n\oplus\mathbb F_q^n):X^2=t\}\) has an exact Jordan-type and rank multiplicity formula. Two natural root defects converge to independent discrete Gaussian-product laws, and the limiting proportion of roots of maximum rank is \(T_q^{-1}\), approximately \(0.59546\) over \(\mathbb F_2\).
