On nearly balanced complete bipartite graphs, a two-species Widom–Rowlinson gas with activity \(z=c/\sqrt n\) has an exact three-way occupancy crossover. When the imbalance is \(\kappa\sqrt n\), the limiting probability that both sides are occupied is \(1/(1+e^{-c^2}\cosh(\kappa c))\): it is nonmonotone in activity exactly when \(\kappa>\sqrt2\).
