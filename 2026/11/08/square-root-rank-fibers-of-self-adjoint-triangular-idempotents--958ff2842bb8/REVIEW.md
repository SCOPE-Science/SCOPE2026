# Review of the rank-refined Hermitian idempotent formula

## Correctness (C): PASS
The setting is the chain incidence algebra \(T_n(\mathbb F_{q^2})\) with the anti-diagonal Frobenius involution. Palindromic binary diagonals are necessary. A full proof, valid also for even \(q\), establishes that every fixed idempotent with a prescribed compatible diagonal is conjugate to its diagonal projection by a star-unitary upper-unitriangular matrix. The nonstandard ingredient is the surjectivity of \(c\mapsto c^*c\) within the star-stable sparse centralizer. It is proved degree by degree using surjectivity of the field trace \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\to\mathbb F_q\), not assumed. Orbit–stabilizer gives exactly \(q^{r(n-r)}\) matrices per diagonal. The binomial ratios and a summable Gaussian majorant yield total-variation error \(O_q(1/n)\). Complementation gives the exact mean. A standalone independently parametrized enumeration of finite-field matrices verified all nine recorded small cases; finite tests are supporting, not the proof.

## Originality (O): PASS relative to inspected evidence
The closest directly read source, Gargate–Gargate (2020, arXiv:2009.04243, Theorems 1.1–1.2 and Section 3), counts unrestricted triangular idempotents: for field size \(q^2\), each binary diagonal has \(q^{2r(n-r)}\) unrestricted matrices. The new self-adjoint condition is not enumerated in that article, and its square-root orbit count is not a restatement of the unrestricted count. Fornaroli (2023, arXiv:2306.12512, Theorems 3.7 and 4.1 and surrounding arguments) classifies involutions rather than ranks of idempotents fixed by a designated involution. Diniz–Ramos–Galdino (2020, arXiv:2006.08452) studies graded polynomial identities of involutive triangular algebras. Urure–Silva (2023, DOI:10.1080/00927872.2022.2158193) classifies second-kind involutions up to equivalence; its full text could not be obtained, so a residual risk is recorded. Hou (2019 online, DOI:10.1080/03081087.2019.1596223) treats unrestricted triangular idempotents and was only accessible in abstract. Actual mathematical-record searches for all key aliases gave no covering result. Neither lack of a hit nor a topic partition establishes uniqueness; priority remains provisional.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS
Self-adjoint idempotents are orthogonal direct-summand projections for a nondegenerate Hermitian form compatible with a complete flag. Their rank-refined count is a natural finite-algebra invariant. The theorem exhibits a square-root phenomenon for individual compatible-diagonal fibers relative to the known unrestricted count and establishes three parity-class discrete-Gaussian limits with quantitative total-variation bounds. The factorization and orbit argument has independent structural content, and the result applies to every finite-field characteristic and all dimensions. It is not just an isolated recomputation or a trivial renaming of the unrestricted triangular idempotent formula.

## Limitations and residual risks
The result concerns the explicitly specified reversal–Frobenius involution, not all involutions of a general incidence algebra. Only two small prime powers were exhaustively enumerated. The Urure–Silva 2023 full paper and Hou 2019 full paper were not available to inspect; their published abstracts address involution equivalence and unrestricted idempotents respectively, but undisclosed stronger results remain a priority risk. A more general algebraic-group count of fixed projections could also imply the stated formula. Publication novelty is therefore assessed to the best of accessible evidence, not certified. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
