For the reversal–Frobenius involution on \(T_n(\mathbb F_{q^2})\), every palindromic binary diagonal of rank \(r\) supports exactly \(q^{r(n-r)}\) self-adjoint idempotents, the square root of its unrestricted idempotent fiber. Uniformly sampled self-adjoint idempotents have rank \(n/2+O_{\mathsf P}(1)\) with one of three parity-dependent discrete-Gaussian limits and total-variation error \(O_q(1/n)\).
