# Scientific assessment of the kernel-bracket and rank-stratum theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The all-field derivation equations identify the central-annihilating block and a symplectic square-zero operator. The kernel is either the full orthogonal complement of the isotropic image or a hyperplane inside it. The alternating form on that hyperplane loses exactly two in rank, yielding the invariant rank identity. The isotropic Grassmannian, the open invertible-symmetric-bundle locus and the inside/outside functional vector-bundle strata establish smoothness, geometric irreducibility and the stated dimensions. The original checker verifies selected finite examples; it does not establish all-field geometry.

**Originality — PASS for the narrowed theorem, with meaningful prior overlap and residual priority risk.** The 2026-10-18 exact rank-spectra theorem already classifies all square-zero Heisenberg derivations through isotropic images, invertible symmetric forms and compatible central functionals, and it gives the exact finite-field rank polynomial. That count is no longer claimed as new. The 2026-11-08 discrete-corank work adds asymptotic rank statistics, likewise not claimed. The surviving claims are the kernel-bracket rank conservation and the two globally smooth geometrically irreducible strata of each positive full-derivation rank. They require additional symplectic restriction and geometric-bundle arguments. A broader prior orbit-geometry theorem might imply them and remains an explicit risk; absence from the inspected sources does not establish absolute priority.

**Value — PASS.** The induced Lie bracket on the kernel is a natural invariant not determined just by its vector-space dimension. Its exact rank is rigidly determined by the derivation rank, and the two distinct mechanisms for equal derivation rank are canonically separated into smooth geometric families of consecutive dimensions. These are motivated structural and geometric facts for a natural differential operator variety, not a recomputation of known rank multiplicities.

**Limitations.** Ordinary, not restricted, derivations; square-zero composition, not higher index; smooth locally closed strata, not irreducible components of the complete nonreduced scheme. The count in the earlier 2026-10-18 theorem is an input. Historical novelty is best-of-knowledge. No external attestation or Lean proof is asserted.
