For a fixed maximal-rank square-zero element of \(\mathfrak{sp}_8(\mathbb F_q)\) with odd \(q\), the rank distribution of commuting square-zero Lie elements distinguishes the split and nonsplit rational nilpotent orbits, despite their identical Jordan partition. Their cardinalities are respectively \(q^{10}+2(q^2-1)q^6\) and \(q^{10}\), and all additional split-orbit solutions have rank four.
