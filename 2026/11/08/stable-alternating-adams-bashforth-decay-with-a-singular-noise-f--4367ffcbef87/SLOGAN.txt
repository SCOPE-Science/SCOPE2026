For scalar exponential decay, standard alternating-step Adams–Bashforth 2 is strictly stable exactly when the sum of the two normalized steps is less than two, independently of their ratio. Yet iid additive update noise of fixed variance produces a stationary variance growing sharply as the inverse square of the vanishing step, with exact coefficient \(\sigma^2 b^4/[4(1-(1-b+b^2/2)^2)]\) when the other normalized step \(b\) is fixed in \((0,2)\).
