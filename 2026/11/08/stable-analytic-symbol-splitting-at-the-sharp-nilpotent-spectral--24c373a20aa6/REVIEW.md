# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is an all-dimensions analytic-symbol result, not an extrapolation from the checker. Compression of the matrix Schur multiplier to two Hardy coefficients gives a contractive block matrix and the bound \(\|\mathscr J(F)\|\le 1+\|F'(0)\|\). Picking an extremal derivative input vector, Parseval separately controls its full Taylor column and the adjoint full Taylor row. Their combined squared energies bound the entire off-diagonal symbol plus its distinguished scalar entry in \(H^2\), including every coefficient of every degree. This establishes exact splitting at equality and \(2\sqrt\delta\) stability for finite rectangular matrix levels. The scalar Möbius family gives a direct \(b^2\) deficit versus order-\(b\) analytic-symbol distance, proving the exponent sharp. No higher-dimensional infinity argument relies on finite testing. The independent checker passed 2,616 finite assertions.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge comparison)
The principal inspected source is the 2026 complete Crouzeix dimension-three paper (arXiv:2608.27346, accessible later version, Sections 1, 10 and 12). Its Theorem 1.1 proves the amplified constant; its Theorem 10.8 gives \(O(\sqrt\delta)\) stability of an **operator value** under square-function hypotheses; its Theorem 12.2 classifies scalar polynomial equality and Corollary 12.5 classifies small-dimensional **input matrices** attaining the constant. None of those statements directly bounds the complete Hardy \(H^2\) *matrix-valued analytic symbol* from its two-coefficient functional-calculus norm, particularly for arbitrary rectangular coefficient levels. The inspected 2024 compressed-shift paper instead establishes spectral-set bounds for classes of finite matrices. published-finding corpus semantic comparisons found nearby Jordan numerical-range and spectral-set results but no matching function-level global split or quantitative stability. Generic matrix Schur/Pick contractivity is relevant; the exact conclusion may be derivable from a more general known theorem without matching terminology, and this is a genuine priority risk rather than proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS
Equality rigidity of a sharp spectral-set inequality is meaningful only when it controls the actual optimizing functions, not solely the compressed value. The result answers that question at **all finite rectangular amplifications**, identifies the unavoidable single scalar \(z\) factor, and shows that an \(\varepsilon\)-near extremizer has its *whole analytic symbol* within dimension-independent \(O(\sqrt\varepsilon)\) Hardy distance of a split Schur function. An explicit automorphism family shows the modulus exponent cannot be strengthened. This is a natural complete extremal classification with quantitative stability at a sharp constant, though limited to the square-zero Jordan case.

## Literature and remaining risks
The complete-Crouzeix 2026 source is stronger on the full inequality and on matrix-value rigidity, but not in the stated analytic-function-distance conclusion. Other operator-valued Schwarz/Pick or de Branges–Rovnyak interpolation results may already subsume this particular Schur-class stability estimate. The 2011 matrix-valued Schur realization source was available only in summary in the present comparison, so no whole-document exclusion is asserted. The method is elementary once Hardy multiplier contractivity is invoked, and priority should be assessed independently before any wider publication.
