For the free-involution Calabi–Yau Enriques complete intersections, the entire integral cohomology ring below the middle degree is \(\mathbb Z[h,u]/(2u)\), where \(u=c_1(K_X)\); the torsion classes \(u^jh^{k-j}\) produce \(k\) independent two-torsion summands in degree \(2k\). Their canonical bundle is nontrivial two-torsion, but their Brauer group is zero.
