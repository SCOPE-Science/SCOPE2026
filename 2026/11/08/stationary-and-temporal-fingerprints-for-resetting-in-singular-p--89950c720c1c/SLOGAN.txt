For singular planar diffusion with Poisson resetting, the stationary ratio \(\mathbb E[R^4]/\mathbb E[R^2]^2\) uniquely determines the stochastic-calculus interpretation, while the exact lag correlation \(\operatorname{Corr}(R_0^4,R_t^4)=e^{-rt}\) independently identifies the reset rate. Together with the fourth moment these observations recover the diffusivity, and the three conventional interpretations have strictly ordered stationary radial densities in monotone likelihood ratio.
