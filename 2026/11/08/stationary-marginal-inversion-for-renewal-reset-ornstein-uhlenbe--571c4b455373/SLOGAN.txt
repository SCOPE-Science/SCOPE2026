For a one-dimensional Ornstein–Uhlenbeck diffusion subjected to independent renewal resets to zero, the entire stationary one-time position law uniquely identifies the full finite-mean reset-time law through a constrained Gaussian variance mixture. Nevertheless, any finite initial block of stationary moments can be shared by distinct smooth reset laws of the same mean, and the inverse is discontinuous in total variation even among smooth strictly positive reset-time densities at fixed mean.
