At the critical loss scale \(p=c/n\), the stationary fraction of retained coupons in the post-loss careless collector has a functional Gaussian limit: its centered \(\sqrt n\)-fluctuations converge on the time scale \(n\) to an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process. Its exact limiting variance coefficient is \(c(c+2)/(2(1+c)^3)\), strictly smaller than the independent-coupon prediction, and its correlation decays at rate \(1+c\).
