# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem's domain is explicit: finite-dimensional unitary, one fixed normalized pure reset state, geometric independent reset events, exact stationary global purity. The renewal sum converges absolutely for every reset probability strictly between zero and one. Its spectral coefficients have the stated absolute-square denominators; normalization gives positive weights summing to one. Higher derivatives have the claimed signs. The finite-sample inverse proof uses the degree \(2m-1\) numerator bound, distinct positive sample abscissae, monic reduced denominator, and nonvanishing residues. The six-point obstruction has exactly equal pairwise difference multisets and is neither a common rotation nor a reflected rotation. The standalone checker passes 849 deterministic rational and numerical assertions, while all universal steps are analytical. No finite enumeration substitutes for a general theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualifications
The geometric stationary density matrix and its quasienergy kernel were established by Wald et al. (arXiv:2410.11497), and the 2026 Murauer et al. Floquet-reset diagnostics use the same forward renewal construction (arXiv:2610.40638). We do not claim these. The proposed distinct statement is a *global-purity* positive gap measure identifiable by exactly \(2m\) scalar reset-rate measurements when \(m\) is known, together with a concrete six-level nonidentifiability boundary for full quasienergies. Those two source full texts examine state-resolvent, entropy, phase matching, and concurrence, but neither presents this inverse purity statement. Classical rational interpolation and homometric sets are not new; the physical specialization and joint identifiability/obstruction theorem were not found in the compared material. A generic rational interpolation theorem and older reset resolvents may imply the result after an additional specialization; an unindexed explicit version remains a real risk, and external independent priority review is still needed.

## Value — PASS
The physical question is what an experimentally definable *single global scalar observable* can reveal about a reset-driven unitary without density-matrix tomography. The exact finite sampling threshold as an upper bound, positivity of the inverse spectral measure, and sharp distinction between recoverable chordal gaps and unrecoverable labeled phases answer that natural inverse problem. The homometric witness makes the limitation substantive, not cosmetic. The result is idealized and has no error-tolerance guarantee, so the value is mathematical identifiability rather than a ready-to-deploy noisy spectroscopy protocol.
