For a pure state reset to a finite-dimensional unitary Floquet evolution, the global stationary purity is a positive rational Stieltjes spectrum of the occupied chordal quasienergy gaps; exactly \(2m\) ideal reset-rate measurements recover all \(m\) distinct positive gaps and their weights. Homometric six-level examples prove that even the entire purity-versus-reset curve cannot determine the individual quasienergies.
