For real area-weighted affine least squares on concentric planar annuli, removing a larger central disk strictly **decreases** the exact sharp \(L^\infty\) projection norm. The norm has an elementary closed form, is always maximized at the outer circle, and moves strictly from \(\frac{11\sqrt{15}}{8\pi}+\frac2\pi\arcsin(1/4)\) toward \(\frac13+\frac{2\sqrt3}{\pi}\) as the hole fills the disk.
