For iid Bernoulli-valued goods whose realizations are shared identically across agents, a pre-realization allocation maximizes its chance of satisfying realized EF1 if and only if its bundle sizes differ by at most one; each one-good transfer toward balance improves that probability strictly. The exact optimum has a finite binomial formula and decays as \(\frac{2^n-1}{\sqrt n}(2\pi q p(1-p))^{-(n-1)/2}\) when \(n\) and \(p\in(0,1)\) are fixed and \(m=nq+O(1)\) grows.
