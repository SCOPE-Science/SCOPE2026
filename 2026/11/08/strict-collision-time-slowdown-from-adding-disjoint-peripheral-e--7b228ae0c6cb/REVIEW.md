# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The finite-state first-step equations account for the hub, unpaired and paired peripheral degrees, the two independent total-rate-one jump clocks, and absorption on exact vertex coincidence. Solving the two coupled equations gives the displayed rational closed form. The strict increment is an explicit polynomial whose only negative coefficient is its constant term; the admissibility condition ensures strictly positive linear terms. The near-zero collision coefficient is the fraction of initially matched pairs times their unit total collision rate. A self-contained independent full-state rational generator verifier agrees with every tested case and checks the signed increments. The analytic proof covers all integers \(n\ge3\); the finite checks do not claim exhaustive verification of the infinite family.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem solves an exact infinite graph-family perturbation problem, with one matching edge added at a time, and rigorously separates a positive initial collision-rate effect from a strictly negative effect on mean encounter speed. The sharp asymptotic slowdown factor \(5/2\) is a nonvanishing network-level effect. This is a meaningful boundary for naive edge-addition heuristics in stochastic transport, rather than merely an isolated finite table entry.
