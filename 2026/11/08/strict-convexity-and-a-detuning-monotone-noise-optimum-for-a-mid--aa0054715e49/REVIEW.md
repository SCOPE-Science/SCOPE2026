# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

For the specified three-state Lindblad generator, direct symbolic inversion of the nine real integrated-density equations gives the claimed mean-time rational function for all nonnegative dephasing rates and real detuning. The determinant is nonzero on the full domain. Positivity of the shifted and Bernstein coefficient lists certifies the nontrivial global second-derivative sign. Consequently the mean is strictly convex, its derivative crosses zero exactly once in the positive half-line, and implicit differentiation proves strict increase of the optimum in detuning squared. The leading derivative balance gives optimal dephasing asymptotic to the magnitude of detuning. Finite sample checks corroborate, but do not prove, these general claims.

## Originality — qualified PASS

Zhang et al., *Optimal Dephasing for Ballistic Energy Transfer in Disordered Linear Chains* (2017), Eq. (33), already establish the clean middle-initialized trimer formula and its optimum. Cao and Silbey, *Optimization of Exciton Trapping in Energy Transfer Processes* (2009), analyze detuned three-site transfer initialized at an end donor; their exact Eq. (7) is not the same source state. Contreras-Pulido et al. (2014) analyze boundary-driven triple-dot steady current, not a single middle-prepared first-capture mean. Nearby trimer classifications involving endpoint-only budgeted dephasing or middle-only dephasing start from the first site and do not imply the present uniform-noise law.

The narrowly retained contribution is the all-detuning middle-source rational time together with strict convexity, uniqueness, strict increase of the optimal uniform dephasing rate, and its large-detuning scaling, at fixed matched extraction. The mean formula is algebraically accessible from a general Liouvillian resolvent and is not presented as a new method. A later site-dependent noise paper (Lawrence, Wang and Segal, 2026) concerns steady-state flux; its complete text was inaccessible, so this comparison remains a historical-priority risk. Indexed literature searches cannot certify absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The initialized middle site is a naturally motivated transport preparation; the optimum's global convexity and parameter-ordering law supply an exact control invariant for the idealized matched-sink trimer, beyond either a clean-point formula or a local extremum. No extension to unequal sink/hopping rates, longer chains, correlated noise or arrival-time tails is asserted.
