For exponential samples, taking the logarithm of a larger maximum shrinks *every* quantile interval, not just its variance: \(Q_s(v)-Q_s(u)<Q_r(v)-Q_r(u)\) whenever \(0<r<s\) and \(0<u<v<1\). The centered laws also contract in convex order for every positive real shape parameter; the previously known integer-sample variance monotonicity is only a corollary.
