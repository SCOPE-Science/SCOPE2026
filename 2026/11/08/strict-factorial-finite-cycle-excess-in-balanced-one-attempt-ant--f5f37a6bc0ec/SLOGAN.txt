For balanced, one-deposition-per-site two-species antagonistic adsorption on every simple cycle \(C_n\), the expected terminal coverage strictly exceeds the infinite-line value \(1-e^{-1}\). The excess is factorially small but has different sharp parity scales: \(\rho_n-(1-e^{-1})\sim 1/(n\,n!)\) for odd \(n\), and \(\sim 1/(2n!)\) for even \(n\).
