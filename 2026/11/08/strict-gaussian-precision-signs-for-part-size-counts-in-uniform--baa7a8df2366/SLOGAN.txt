For the Gaussian limits of counts of parts of sizes \(1,\ldots,m\) in a uniform random integer composition, the precision matrix has strictly positive off-diagonal entries for every \(m\ge3\), although the singleton and doubleton counts are asymptotically uncorrelated in isolation. Every fixed pair's Gaussian partial correlation tends to \(-1\) as the number of other observed part sizes increases; an exact rank-two matrix inversion establishes the complete classification.
