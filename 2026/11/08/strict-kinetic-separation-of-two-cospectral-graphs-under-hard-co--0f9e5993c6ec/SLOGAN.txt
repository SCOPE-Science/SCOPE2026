Two sixteen-site strongly regular graphs with identical spectra have different irreversible hard-core adsorption laws: the rook graph always jams with four monomers, while the Shrikhande graph jams with three or four with probabilities \(1/3\) and \(2/3\). Moreover, the rook graph's expected coverage is strictly higher at every positive time; their first kinetic difference is \(8p^4\) for attempt probability \(p\) near zero.
