In a uniformly built binary search tree, the depths of the smallest and largest keys are strictly negatively quadrant dependent, with exact covariance \(1-H_n^{(2)}\) for every \(n\ge2\). This negative covariance tends to \(1-\pi^2/6\), even though the two centered depths converge, on the \(\sqrt{\log n}\) scale, to independent Gaussian variables.
