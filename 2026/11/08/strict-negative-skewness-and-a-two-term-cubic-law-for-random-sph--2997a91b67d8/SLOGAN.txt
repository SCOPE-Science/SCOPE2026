Three geodesic side lengths of a random spherical triangle are pairwise independent but their sum has strictly negative skewness in **every** ambient dimension \(d\ge3\). Its cubic moment is \(-6/d^2-9/d^3+O(d^{-7/2})\), and the first relative correction cancels upon standardization, giving skewness \(-2/\sqrt{3d}+O(d^{-2})\).
