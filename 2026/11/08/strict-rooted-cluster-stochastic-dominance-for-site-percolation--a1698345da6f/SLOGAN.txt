Under independent site percolation with any common occupancy probability, the rooted occupied-cluster size on the Shrikhande graph first-order stochastically dominates that on its cospectral sixteen-vertex rook counterpart; the comparison is strict exactly for cluster-size thresholds four through eight at nontrivial occupancy. This also holds for every fixed total number of occupied sites, with an explicit all-positive finite susceptibility difference.
