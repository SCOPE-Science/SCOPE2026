# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The weighted all-forest polynomial is multiaffine, so its edge covariance sign is the opposite of the Rayleigh difference. Complete exact enumeration of all 256 wheel edge masks produces 134 forests and reduces 28 distinct-edge pairs to six dihedral orbits. Five Rayleigh differences are coefficientwise strictly positive, and the sixth has an exact positive square-completion decomposition; all six identities are recomputed by the included runnable source. A separate analytic two-edge-path contraction shows that strict pairwise negative correlation is preserved by subdividing any nonbridge in a bridgeless graph. This establishes the infinite claim and quantifies the positivity assumptions. Numerical or rational examples alone do not prove the universal result.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The closely related established statements are Huang's broad arboreal-gas negative-correlation setting and series reduction, Erickson's series-parallel theorem, Semple–Welsh's small-block closure theorems, and Bado's all-wheel theorem at **unit** weights. None of the inspected claims implies heterogeneous positive fugacity Rayleigh positivity of the five-vertex wheel core. The five-vertex wheel is 3-connected and not series-parallel. Çiçeksiz–Ravichandran's results at common fugacity and high-fugacity limits also do not imply this all-positive heterogeneous result. The full Bado publisher article was inaccessible, and residual historical-priority risk remains despite its explicitly unit-weight title and abstract. This assessment does not claim globally exhaustive priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is an explicit non-series-parallel core with a complete all-positive **multivariate** strict Rayleigh proof, filling a natural gap between K4/series-parallel positive-dependence certificates and later unit-weight wheel results. The difficult spoke–nonincident-rim orbit has negative coefficients, making a nontrivial exact square-completion necessary. The subdivision argument yields an infinite structurally characterized graph family. It is not an arbitrary sample-weight computation or a restatement of the uniform-weight wheel theorem.

## Limitations and closest literature

Only pairwise edge covariance, not arbitrary increasing-event negative association, is established. The argument does not cover larger wheel cores, nonpositive fugacities, or all graphic matroids. Complete publisher text for Bado's wheel paper was not accessible, so any potential unadvertised stronger result remains a historical-priority risk. The certificate is finite exact arithmetic on the wheel, not an attempted computational proof for all subdivisions.
