For strictly convex graph-Laplacian filters, the minimum coordinate-control count on \(K_{p,q}\) is always \(p+q-2\) at every positive step if \(p\ne q\), but balanced graphs have exact two-eigenspace resonance exceptions. At the first exceptional steps for RK2 and RK4, the update is strictly positive and stochastic while the minimum actuator count jumps by one.
