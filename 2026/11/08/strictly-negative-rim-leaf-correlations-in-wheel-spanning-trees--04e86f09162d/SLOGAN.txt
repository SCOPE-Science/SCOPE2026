In a uniformly sampled spanning tree of the wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), every pair of distinct rim-leaf indicators has strictly negative covariance, yet vertices at rim distance two are more negatively correlated than adjacent vertices for every \(m\ge4\). Exact Fibonacci–Lucas probabilities prove this all-size ordering, while at fixed separation \(d\ge2\) the large-wheel covariance tends to \(-9((3-\sqrt5)/2)^{2d}/5\).
