The entire ordinary VC-shattering polynomial of Dyck up-step concepts is counted by weighted walks confined to a strip, with coefficient \((A_{n-1-k}^{\,n-1})_{0,n-1-k}\). Maximum shattered supports number \(2^{n-1}\), the next layer numbers \((3^{n-1}-1)/2\), and the shattered-support complex is nonpure for every \(n\ge4\).
