# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The independent original-edge-neighborhood definition has two local edge types. Every rim has exactly two adjacent spokes, forcing distinct spoke colours. Every spoke has exactly \(2m\) adjacent edges; for colour \(c\), their number is \(m-q_c-\mathbf1_{c=a}+\mathbf1_{c=b}\). The proof shows this is at most \(m\) for every spoke if and only if the rim colour occurs among the colours missing from some spoke pair. The index lower bound follows because a two-colour spoke assignment saturates both colours, leaving no rim colour. The exact exponential enumerator is an onto-word count, and the ratio asymptotic follows by division. The automorphism-orbit matrix argument uses the unique hub for \(m\ge2\). The actual standalone verifier reconstructed original adjacency and exhaustively checked every three-colour assignment for \(m=1,2,3,4\); this corroboration is not an unrestricted proof or external audit.

## Originality — PASS, provisional priority
The relevant primary objects and their strong-majority definition occur in arXiv:2605.23828v1 (introduced 2026-05-22), which gives general bounds and cycle/complete-graph examples, not the classification or exponential windmill count. The full accessible text of arXiv:2607.00212v1 proves universal five-colour and degree-conditioned bounds; it does not give an all-colourings count for triangle windmills. arXiv:2608.04122v1 establishes three-colour existence for graphs of minimum degree at least five, while \(F_m\) has minimum degree two, and is not an enumerative implication. Searches for aliases (friendship graphs, triangular windmills, one-vertex triangle bouquets), colourings, and exponential counts found no stronger prior coverage in the accessible record. Relevant previous research records instead concerned strong-majority colouring of complete graphs and two-colour colouring constraints on regular graphs. The full text of the August 2026 manuscript could not be read; it remains a disclosed, bounded priority risk. No independent literature certification is asserted.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The work does more than produce a three-colour construction. It classifies every optimum colouring, identifies a global coupling between rim choices and the set of missing spoke colours, counts all labelled optimal assignments by a six-term exponential formula, and determines their exponentially decaying frequency among arbitrary three-colour assignments. Triangle windmills are a standard infinite class of low-minimum-degree cactus graphs, just outside the scope of the recent large-minimum-degree three-colour theorem. Its orbit matrix gives a concrete starting point for counting unlabelled configurations; no such extended theorem is claimed.
