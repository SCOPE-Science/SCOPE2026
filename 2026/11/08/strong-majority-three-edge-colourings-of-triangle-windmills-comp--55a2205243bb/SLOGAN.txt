For every triangle windmill \(F_m\) with \(m\ge2\), an optimal strong-majority three-edge colouring has differently coloured spokes in each blade and colours every rim with one of the spoke colours missing from at least one blade. This gives a complete classification and exactly \(2^m(9^m-3\cdot6^m+3\cdot4^m+3\cdot3^m-6\cdot2^m+3)\) named optimum colourings.
