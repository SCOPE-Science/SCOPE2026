A finite connected quartic graph has a two-colour strong-majority edge colouring exactly when it has a completely regular distance-four code with the four-cube intersection array \((4,3,2,1;1,2,3,4)\); the named colourings and the codes are in bijection. Consequently the graph is bipartite with order divisible by \(16\), and two nonisomorphic \(16\)-vertex quartic graphs already have different counts, \(16\) and \(8\).
