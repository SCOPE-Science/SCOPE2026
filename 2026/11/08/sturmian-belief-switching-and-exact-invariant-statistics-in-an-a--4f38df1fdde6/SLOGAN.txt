The uncoupled anti-confirmation Bayesian update is exactly a circle rotation in log odds, so its above-half belief sequence has a universal discrepancy below one over every time window. For the exact likelihood fractions associated with the published cycling example, the rotation is irrational, the beliefs are aperiodic and equidistributed on \([7/20,17/20]\), and their exact long-run mean is \(\log(13/3)/\log(221/21)\).
