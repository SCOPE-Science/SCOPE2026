For globally reset, noninteracting Rabi qubits sharing any positive finite-mean renewal clock, stationary purity is asymptotic to a clock-dependent constant times \(N^{-1/2}\), so the second Rényi entropy grows as \(\frac12\log N\). Its universal minimum at each finite \(N\) is attainable at fixed mean reset interval \(\mu\) exactly when \(\mu\ge\pi/\Omega\); below that threshold, arbitrarily short/one-period two-point clocks approach the optimum but no positive reset-time law attains it.
