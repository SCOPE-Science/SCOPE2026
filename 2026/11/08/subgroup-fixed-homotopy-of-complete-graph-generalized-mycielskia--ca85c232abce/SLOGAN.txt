For every subgroup of the label-permuting symmetric group of a complete-graph generalized Mycielskian, the geometric fixed-point independence complex has a strong-collapse model determined **only** by the number of labels globally fixed by the subgroup. With no fixed labels it reduces to the independence complex of a path, while with \(f\ge1\) fixed labels it reduces to the independence complex of the same Mycielskian construction on \(K_f\), giving a complete integral sphere-wedge classification.
