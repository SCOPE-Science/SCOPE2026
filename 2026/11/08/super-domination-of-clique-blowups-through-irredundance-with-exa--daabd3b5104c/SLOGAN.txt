Every super dominating set of a clique blowup \(G[K_m]\), \(m\ge2\), is exactly the complement of a one-per-fiber lift of an irredundant set of \(G\); this determines the complete super-domination polynomial and all inclusion-minimal configurations. For cyclic bases an eight-state transfer matrix counts every super dominating set, and the number of minimum ones has an explicit parity-dependent closed formula.
