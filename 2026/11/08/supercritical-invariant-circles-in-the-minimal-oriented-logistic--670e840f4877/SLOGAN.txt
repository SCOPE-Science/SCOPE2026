For a balanced oriented ring of three coupled logistic maps, the homogeneous equilibrium loses stability through a supercritical Neimark–Sacker bifurcation at \(r_c=1+2/(1+3a^2)\) for every positive coupling except two low-order resonances. The resulting attracting invariant circle has an explicit leading amplitude and a negative spatial-mean shift, governed by the exact cubic radial coefficient \(-(3a^2+3)^3/(3a^2+1)^2\).
