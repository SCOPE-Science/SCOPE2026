For every balanced, disjoint signed support of size \(2r\) on the finite-field moment curve of degree \(2r-1\), the complete number of Fourier zeros is independent of the support's locations and has an explicit balanced-partition polynomial with leading term \(r!p^{r-1}\). The independence can fail just one polynomial degree lower, as an exact six-point example over \(\mathbb F_{11}\) demonstrates.
