On a coherent binary hypercube, absorbing exactly half the vertices along an affine parity hyperplane succeeds with probability one precisely when the defining parity check has odd support; for even support, the never-absorbed fraction is exactly \(2^{-w}\binom{w}{w/2}\). The full survival curve has a finite binomial-mode formula, and the uniquely optimal absorption strength for successfully captured trajectories scales as \(w^{1/4}\), with different sharp constants for the two support parities.
