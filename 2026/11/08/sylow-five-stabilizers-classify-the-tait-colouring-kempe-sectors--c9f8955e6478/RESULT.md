# Sylow-five stabilizers classify the Tait-colouring Kempe sectors of the Tutte–Coxeter graph

## Finding

Let \(T\) be the thirty-vertex Tutte–Coxeter graph in its duad–syntheme incidence realization on six symbols. There are exactly \(864\) proper edge-colourings with three named colours, or \(144\) one-factorizations with unlabelled colour classes. Under the natural \(S_6\)-action, each unlabelled one-factorization \(F\) has a stabilizer \(H(F)\) that is a cyclic group of order \(5\). The map \(F\mapsto H(F)\) assumes all \(36\) Sylow-five subgroups of \(S_6\), with exactly four one-factorizations per subgroup, and **its fibres are precisely the edge-Kempe equivalence classes on unlabelled one-factorizations**. Within each fibre the quotient of the Kempe exchange graph is a four-cycle.

For named colours, the complete edge-Kempe graph is the disjoint union of \(36\) mutually isomorphic connected components, each with \(24\) vertices and \(60\) edges. Every component is \(5\)-regular, has radius and diameter \(3\), and every vertex has the exact shortest-path sphere profile
\[
(1,5,13,5).
\]
Thus there are \(2160\) edges across the full reconfiguration graph. The component invariant is a concrete subgroup of the naturally acting \(S_6\), not merely an enumeration label.

## Assumptions and scope

Take the ordered ground set \(\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}\). A *duad* is a two-element subset; a *syntheme* is a partition of the six symbols into three duads. There are fifteen of each. The graph \(T\) has one vertex per duad, one per syntheme, and an edge \((d,s)\) if and only if \(d\in s\). Thus it has thirty vertices, forty-five edges and degree three everywhere. The natural \(S_6\)-action permutes the six original symbols and therefore all incidence edges. The stabilizer \(H(F)\) is taken in this specified \(S_6\), not in the larger full automorphism group of the underlying graph.

A *proper named three-edge-colouring* assigns colours from \(\{0,1,2\}\) to the forty-five incidence edges so that the three edges at every graph vertex have different colours. An *unlabelled one-factorization* forgets the three colour names. An edge-Kempe move interchanges two named colours on one connected bichromatic cycle (a whole connected component of the graph induced by those two colours). Its unlabelled version is the induced transformation of the one-factorization. All claims concern this move and this single finite graph.

## Proof

**Exhaustive but finite census.** A perfect matching of \(T\) is exactly a bijection from the fifteen duads to the fifteen synthemes with each duad assigned to a containing syntheme. Recursive exact cover over the fifteen duads, never reusing a syntheme, gives exactly \(288\) such matchings. Two disjoint perfect matchings determine a third precisely when their edge-set complement is also a perfect matching. Canonically sorting the resulting triples gives exactly \(144\) unlabelled one-factorizations. Independent direct constraint-search over the forty-five edge-colour variables finds exactly \(144\) named colourings after fixing the colours at one specified degree-three vertex; all six colour permutations produce \(864\) distinct proper colourings.

For an explicit witness, order duads lexicographically, synthemes lexicographically by their increasing duad-index triples, and incidence edges first by duad and then syntheme index. The forty-five-colour word

```text

```

represents an unlabelled one-factorization \(F_0\) with named colours as written. Its natural \(S_6\)-stabilizer is generated by the permutation with one-line notation \((1,4,0,3,5,2)\), a five-cycle fixing symbol \(3\). Exhaustively applying all \(720\) permutations of the six original symbols yields \(144\) distinct one-factorizations, exhausting the previous census. Hence the action is transitive, every one-factorization has stabilizer order \(5\), and each stabilizer is a Sylow-five subgroup. A direct stabilizer census yields \(36\) different subgroups, exactly four one-factorizations fixed by each. Equivalently, there are \(6\cdot 4!/4=36\) order-five subgroups because a five-cycle fixes a unique point and each subgroup contains four nonidentity five-cycles.

**Invariance under every Kempe move.** Every one-factorization has exactly one colour-pair two-factor that is a Hamiltonian \(30\)-cycle and two colour-pair two-factors of cycle lengths \(10\) and \(20\). This is exhaustively checked for all \(144\) one-factorizations; by transitivity it also follows from checking the displayed witness. Let \(H=H(F)\). The order-five group \(H\) acts trivially on the set of three matching classes, since it has no nontrivial homomorphism to \(S_3\). Thus it preserves each bichromatic two-factor. Each such two-factor has at most two component cycles; an order-five action must preserve each cycle individually. Switching one of these cycles produces a new one-factorization \(F'\) preserved by \(H\). Since \(H(F')\) also has order five, \(H(F')=H(F)\). So no Kempe sequence can cross between distinct stabilizers.

**Exact fibres and component geometry.** A swap along an entire Hamiltonian colour-pair cycle only permutes colour names, leaving the unlabelled one-factorization unchanged. For each of the other two colour pairs, switching the ten-cycle or the twenty-cycle gives the same unlabelled neighbour (the two switches differ by a global transposition). The independent matching-based exhaustive checker verifies that the resulting two neighbours are distinct for every one-factorization. Hence the unlabelled Kempe graph is simple and two-regular within each four-element stabilizer fibre. Its connected induced graph is necessarily \(C_4\) (the only simple two-regular graph on four vertices).

Any global permutation of the three colour labels is a sequence of such legal full bichromatic transpositions, each itself a sequence of component-cycle Kempe switches. Consequently all six labelled colourings of an unlabelled one-factorization belong to the same connected component. Each of the \(36\) unlabelled four-cycles therefore lifts to exactly one connected component on \(4\cdot6=24\) named colourings. Each named colouring has five distinct Kempe neighbours: one for its Hamiltonian colour pair and two for each of the other two colour pairs. This gives \(24\cdot5/2=60\) edges per component.

Finally, the independent direct-colouring verifier enumerates all \(864\) named colourings, constructs the five legal moves at each one from bichromatic vertex components, and computes breadth-first shortest-path distances from **every** named colouring within its connected component. Each distance sphere is exactly \((1,5,13,5)\); therefore all component radii and diameters equal three. The natural \(S_6\)-action is transitive on the thirty-six order-five subgroups, so all components are isomorphic.

## Verification

The embedded Python 3 standard-library programs are independently structured and must both succeed:


The first enumerates perfect matchings by exact cover, assembles unlabelled factorizations, calculates every natural \(S_6\) stabilizer and every unlabelled Kempe exchange, and checks the subgroup-to-component bijection. The second directly solves edge-colour incidence constraints with a least-options search, constructs named-colour Kempe moves through connected bichromatic components, and checks all \(864\) shortest-path profiles. All assertions were executed successfully on the standalone files. The proof uses complete finite search spaces, not sampled states or extrapolated data.

## Relationship to prior work

The duad–syntheme model is classical, appearing in Coxeter's treatment of the Tutte–Coxeter graph and later expositions of the exceptional \(S_6\) symmetry. Belcastro and Haas (first public preprint September 8, 2012) developed the edge-Kempe equivalence framework and results for cubic graphs. Goedgebeur and Östergård (first public preprint May 4, 2021) exhaustively studied edge-Kempe class counts up to thirty vertices, but their published tables give aggregate counts and ranges rather than the present graph's canonical Sylow-five classification or its labelled component metric. The bare existence or count of Kempe classes may be implicit in that census and is not asserted as independently new. The asserted specific contribution is the subgroup-valued complete obstruction, four-cycle fibres and exact \(24\)-state component geometry.

## Limitations

This is a finite, specifically identified graph result, not a general theorem for all cubic cages, generalized quadrangles or all graphs of girth eight. The classification relies partly on exhaustive search verified by two reproducible programs, with exact loop bounds and construction logic described above. Historical priority is qualified: an equivalent result under older six-point or one-factorization terminology cannot be ruled out, and a published computational census may have unreported graph-level output. No independent scholarly audit or independent formal certification has been performed.
