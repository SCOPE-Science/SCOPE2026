# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The object is the specified thirty-vertex duad–syntheme incidence graph with three **named** edge colours; the unlabelled quotient is explicitly distinguished. The exact-cover checker proves the \(288\)-matching and \(144\)-factorization census; a different vertex-incidence edge-colouring recursion proves the \(864\)-state count. The natural \(S_6\)-action is completely enumerated, including all \(720\) permutations and the \(36\) order-five stabilizers. A separate mathematical argument proves that an order-five stabilizer must preserve every two-colour Kempe cycle because every two-factor has at most two cycles. Fibre connectivity follows from a checked two-neighbour property on four-element fibres. Every named state was used as a breadth-first search root to establish the exact distance profile \((1,5,13,5)\). The two complete verification scripts executed successfully. No infinite-family claim is made.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Belcastro–Haas, arXiv:1209.1730v1, provides the edge-Kempe framework and general planar/nonplanar results. Goedgebeur–Östergård, arXiv:2105.01363v1, studies cubic graphs up to thirty vertices computationally; its published tables aggregate the distribution of class counts by order and do not identify a Sylow-five invariant, four-cycle quotient or exact labelled-component distances for this particular cage. Its computations may already imply the bare component count, so the bare count is not claimed to be new. Related earlier exact Tutte–Coxeter perfect-matching flips and other cubic-graph colour changes concern different state spaces and move sets. Searches of equivalent names, underlying six-point synthematic geometry, class counts and published primary sources found no covering statement of the stabilizer-to-Kempe-component correspondence. The residual risk of historical priority or supplementary unindexed data remains explicit.

## Value — PASS

The Tutte–Coxeter graph is the smallest cubic graph of girth eight and a canonical highly symmetric incidence geometry. Optimal edge-colouring Kempe dynamics is a natural reconfiguration invariant; the finding identifies a complete **group-theoretic obstruction**, not merely a numerical disconnection. The bijection with Sylow-five subgroups of \(S_6\), sharply bounded four-factorization fibres and complete diameter-three metric make a meaningful, reusable benchmark for cubic colouring dynamics and six-point geometry.
