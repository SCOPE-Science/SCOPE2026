The three-edge-colouring Kempe components of the Tutte–Coxeter graph are classified exactly by the \(36\) order-five subgroups of the natural \(S_6\) action: each subgroup fixes four unlabelled factorizations forming a four-cycle. With named colours, every sector has \(24\) colourings, degree \(5\), and diameter \(3\), and no Kempe sequence changes its subgroup invariant.
