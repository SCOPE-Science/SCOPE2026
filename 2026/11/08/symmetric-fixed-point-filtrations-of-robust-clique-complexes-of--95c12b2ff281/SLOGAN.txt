For every finite graph \(H\), the geometric symmetric-group fixed spaces of the robust clique filtration of \(H\mathbin{\square}K_r\) are governed exactly by the largest induced \(r\)-colorable subgraphs of \(H\). When \(H\) is \(r\)-colorable the fixed filtration is the sequence of simplex skeleta, with explicit integral sphere-wedge homotopy and unit-length persistent bars; at the sharp level \(k=|V(H)|\), an arbitrary base graph produces a sphere precisely when it is \(r\)-colorable, and a contractible simplex otherwise.
