# Scientific review: symmetry of Chebyshev–Lucas triples

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem covers all integer parameters \(u\ge3\) and even \(n\ge4\). The proof reconstructs strong divisibility for the recurrence \(U_{j+2}=uU_{j+1}-U_j\), establishes the exact pairwise generator gcds, and isolates the identity \((u^2-1)b=a+uc\). At \(n=4\) and at multiples of six it constructs explicit normal forms with uniqueness by modular arguments; these yield the two-binomial kernels, complete Apéry rectangles, Hilbert series and exact Frobenius numbers. For every remaining even index the only possible symmetric three-generator gluing fails by the divisibility obstruction \(a/u>u^2-1\). The stated use of Herzog–Watanabe is exactly the necessity of a coprime-pair gluing for a minimally generated three-generator symmetric semigroup, as documented by Fel (2008, Section 2). An independent shortest-path Apéry computation on 24 sample parameter pairs agrees with all predicted symmetry and Frobenius assertions; this finite check is corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected sources
The natural comparison is Mu–Komatsu, *p-Numerical Semigroups of Triples from the Three-Term Recurrence Relations* (2024; full open preprint read, Sections 1–3 and relevant formula passages). Its hypotheses explicitly require positive recurrence coefficients \(u,v\), and its treatment of \(W_{i},W_{i+2},W_{i+k}\) includes the positive-sign Pell case but not the signed \(Q=1\) Chebyshev–Lucas family of this theorem. Komatsu–Mu (arXiv:2307.08998, Sections 3–4 read) studies \(P_{j+2}=uP_{j+1}+P_j\), not the negative-sign sequence; its even-index subsection explicitly excludes its own cases \(i>k\). Fel (arXiv:0803.1606, Section 2 and Fibonacci/Lucas scope read) proves the general three-generator symmetry/gluing principle, not a norm-one Chebyshev–Lucas parameter classification. The concrete new distinction is the isolated symmetric \(n=4\), absent from the generic larger-index obstruction, accompanied by two distinct complete-intersection presentations. Indexed mathematical literature searches under exact claims, Lucas/Chebyshev aliases, parameters and gluing/toric terminology yielded only unrelated nearest items; no claim of proven global novelty is made. The strongest remaining risk is earlier unindexed treatment of this signed-Lucas specialization under another name.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Symmetry, complete-intersection status and Frobenius numbers are natural arithmetic invariants of numerical semigroups associated with recurrent integer sequences. This gives a **complete classification** across an infinite two-parameter Chebyshev–Lucas family not covered by the inspected positive-coefficient studies. It identifies a sharp arithmetic bifurcation: one isolated symmetric index \(4\), infinitely many symmetric indices divisible by \(6\), and all other even indices nonsymmetric. The two different minimal presentations and rectangular Apéry formulas explain the bifurcation structurally rather than supplying a bare yes/no test. It is not an arbitrary finite slice or a numerical-table recomputation. Scope is limited to \(u\ge3\), even \(n\ge4\), index gaps \(2,3\), and ordinary semigroups; independent scientific review would still be appropriate.

## Closest literature, risks, and limitations
The 2024 generalized-Horadam article is the closest **positive-sign** coverage, and the 2008 symmetry theorem provides a necessary general tool. Neither supplies the claimed negative-sign classification within its stated hypotheses. The parameter substitution \(v=-1\) would violate the 2024 paper's positivity hypotheses, so it does not itself establish the finding. Exact results for arbitrary other index gaps, odd \(n\), and \(p>0\) remain outside scope. The source comparison is literature-limited, not an exhaustive novelty certification.
