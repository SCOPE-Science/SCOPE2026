For the norm-one Chebyshev–Lucas recurrence \(U_{j+2}=uU_{j+1}-U_j\) with \(u\ge3\), the numerical semigroup generated by \(U_n,U_{n+2},U_{n+3}\) at even \(n\ge4\) is symmetric exactly at the isolated index \(4\) and at multiples of \(6\). Each symmetric family has explicit two-binomial defining equations, a rectangular Apéry set, a Hilbert series and an exact Frobenius number.
