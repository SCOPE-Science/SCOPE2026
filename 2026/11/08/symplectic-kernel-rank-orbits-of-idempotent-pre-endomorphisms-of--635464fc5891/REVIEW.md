# Review of the symplectic kernel-rank orbit classification

## Correctness
PASS. The associated commutator Lie algebra is Heisenberg; the only idempotent pre-endomorphism retaining its central derived line is the identity. Otherwise the image is isotropic and every map consists of a linear projection onto a totally isotropic subspace plus a central functional. For a direct-sum complement \(K\) of an isotropic \(W\), the nondegenerate part of \(K\cap W^\perp\) has dimension \(2(m-r)\); the remaining alternating form has even rank \(2s\). Witt-basis comparison shows orbit transitivity for fixed \((r,s)\). The graph-parametrization of complements and the surjective alternating-matrix map \(B\mapsto B-B^{\mathsf T}\) give the exact orbit multiplicity in every characteristic. Two distinct finite programs passed, independently checking binary orbit partition and ternary defining identities.

## Originality
PASS relative to investigated evidence, subject to publication-priority risk. Ebrahim–Facchini Theorem 6.1 gives the general ideal–complement description, not this Heisenberg finite-field conjugacy refinement. Earlier Heisenberg rank-only counts are expressly not claimed new; summing the new orbit sizes recovers them, but the reverse implication cannot determine the kernel symplectic rank strata. Anil Kumar–Pradhan's extra-special-group paper addresses a different underlying group category and group-element orbits. Chandler–Sin–Xiang's symplectic incidence study concerns orthogonal complements, not the linear direct-sum complements of this theorem. No directly dominating exact orbit theorem emerged from inspected primary literature or mathematical-record queries. A specialized result not located remains a risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The earliest nontrivial case distinguishes two nonconjugate rank-two idempotent pre-endomorphisms of the same five-dimensional algebra by whether their kernels are abelian, a structural distinction hidden by existing rank enumerations. The theorem identifies all such orbit obstructions in arbitrary Heisenberg dimension and provides closed orbit cardinalities uniformly in finite-field characteristic, useful for symmetry-aware classifications and stabilizer counts. It is not merely a recomputation of the previously established marginal counts.

## Limits
No extension to ordinary associative-endomorphism conjugacy, arbitrary fields as counting domains, or other algebra families. A general independent peer audit and definitive publication priority analysis have not been performed.
