For every finite field, idempotent pre-endomorphisms of the block-Heisenberg algebra split into exact pre-automorphism conjugacy orbits classified by image dimension and the alternating-form rank on the kernel. The first equal-rank symmetry split occurs in dimension five: over \(\mathbb F_2\), its two rank-two orbits each have size \(480\), with abelian versus nonabelian kernels.
