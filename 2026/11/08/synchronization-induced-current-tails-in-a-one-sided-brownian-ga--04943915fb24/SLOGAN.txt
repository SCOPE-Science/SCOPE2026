A simultaneous Poisson reset clock converts the Poisson net current of a one-sided Brownian gas into a Rayleigh-mixed Poisson law without changing any single-particle marginal or the mean current. Its high-count decay has a sharp half-Poisson logarithmic exponent at stationarity, although every fixed finite time retains the full Poisson exponent; synchronization also creates strict convex-order inflation and density-scale non-self-averaging.
