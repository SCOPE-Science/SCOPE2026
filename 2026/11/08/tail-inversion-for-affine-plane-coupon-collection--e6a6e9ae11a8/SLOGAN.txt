Every affine-plane fair coupon collector of order at least three crosses the full random-block collector in stochastic tail order: the geometric scheme can cover all coupons sooner in the minimum possible number of draws, yet has a strictly heavier eventual completion-time tail. The late-tail difference has the sharp coefficient \(q^3(q-1)^2(q+1)/6\) at base \((q-2)/(q+1)\); at order three its sole nontrivial sign switch occurs between the third and fourth draws.
