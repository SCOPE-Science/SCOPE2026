# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The proof first classifies every individual three-periodic temporal word as either constantly resting or a full three-colour rotation. On the 4-regular connected line graph, two successor neighbors per active vertex force equality in the chain \(2|S|\le E(L(H)[S])\le2|S|\). Hence every vertex is active and adjacent phase labels differ. That is precisely a proper three-edge-colouring of the cubic base graph. Its converse holds because each of the two endpoints of each base edge contributes one successor-coloured neighboring edge. The count for each ladder is obtained from a nine-state strip transition: diagonal pairs behave as a 3-colour cycle and off-diagonal pairs swap deterministically, so straight versus twisted closure produces complementary parity-six terms. These arguments cover all allowed connected cubic graphs and every ladder size \(n\ge3\). No finite simulation is used as an infinite proof.

Two independently structured finite checkers successfully replayed. Direct simulation exhaustively checks configurations for both graph families at \(n=3,4\), with separate proper edge-colouring enumerations through \(n=10\). A separately implemented nine-state transfer checks each seam count through \(n=20\). This supports, but does not replace, the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The most relevant general higher-threshold source, Bello–Sivakoff's full accessible 2021 preprint, proves almost-sure dynamics on infinite regular trees, not the exact finite line-graph periodic-state/three-edge-colouring identification. The 1993 Durrett–Griffeath preprint introduces the general threshold-dependent excitable-media setting, but full text was inaccessible in this run. The Lekshmi–West 2020 result counts perfect matchings in prisms and Möbius ladders, not by its stated claim the compatible partitions into three perfect matchings. Prior published counts of proper three-edge-colourings could potentially imply the numerical formulas via a translation, but the finite dynamics / graph-colouring bijection is itself a distinct statement. Targeted result searches and historical-claim comparisons revealed no equivalent dynamical assertion. This does not establish absolute publication priority; possible unindexed equivalences and the unread 1993/2020 full texts remain risks.

## Value — PASS
The result identifies a natural and mathematically consequential dynamical property of every cubic line graph with cubic edge-colourability: class-one graphs have primitive-three excitation and class-two graphs do not. It translates an established nontrivial graph-colouring invariant into an exact recurrent-state census. In the two canonical cubic ladder families it gives an explicit asymptotically exponential census and a bounded, parity-sensitive seam correction of exactly six configurations, quantifying the effect of topology without changing local degrees. The general bijection and seam contrast are more than a routine finite example.

## Closest literature and limits
Relevant comparisons are Durrett–Griffeath (1993), Bello–Sivakoff (2021), Lekshmi–West (2020), and Goedgebeur–Östergård (2022), plus previously accessible finite-graph excitable-dynamics claims for distinct thresholds and graph families. The statement only determines initially periodic configurations with temporal period dividing three, not other attractors or transient basins. The full 1993 and 2020 articles were not fully inspected; possible earlier counting formulae and conceptual equivalences are retained as priority risks. No independent validation is claimed.
