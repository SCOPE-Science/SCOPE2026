Under three-state, two-contact Greenberg–Hastings dynamics, primitive-three states on the line graph of any connected cubic graph are exactly its proper Tait three-edge-colourings. On line graphs of the \(n\)-rung prism and Möbius ladder, the populations are \(2^n+3+5(-1)^n\) and \(2^n+3-(-1)^n\), so a twisted seam changes the count by precisely \(6(-1)^n\).
