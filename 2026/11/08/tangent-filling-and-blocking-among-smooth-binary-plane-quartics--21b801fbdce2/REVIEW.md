# Scientific review: tangent filling and blocking for binary plane quartics

## Correctness — PASS

The precise population is all nonzero binary ternary quartics with no geometric singular points; there are \(32,767\) nonzero candidates. Exhaustive \(\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\) substitution yields a disjoint partition into \(279\) orbits. The exact unit-ideal test of the homogeneous gradient system after dehomogenizing on each of the three charts proves geometric smoothness, yielding \(78\) smooth orbits and \(10,920\) coefficient vectors. Rational evaluation then gives the \(2\times2\) tangent-filling/blocking counts \((2040,616,1848,6416)\); the orbit-weighted and all-coefficient counts agree. A second literal evaluation of every quartic's partial derivatives and point-line incidences agrees for all smooth equations. The four explicit categories and probability are therefore proved over the exact finite domain.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk

Asgarli–Ghioca introduced rational tangent filling and its dual-blocking characterization, but did not compute the smooth binary-quartic distribution. Nart–Ritzenthaler classify the characteristic-two genus-three quartics and their bitangent configurations, but do not tabulate their rational tangent-line covering. Lomont's exhaustive lower-degree searches report extension-field rational-point extrema rather than tangent covering; Asgarli–Ghioca–Yip calculate blocking statistics rather than their joint law with tangent filling. Relevant primary texts were read and published mathematical-record and literature searches compared the implied statements. A classification of all curves does supply models from which the invariant can be recalculated, but it does not itself give this new derivative-sensitive joint distribution. An older unindexed census, or derivation from complete archived models, remains possible.

## Value — PASS

Tangent-filling and blocking are natural incidence properties singled out in the motivating literature, respectively corresponding to a blocking set of rational tangent lines in the dual plane and to a blocking set of rational points in the original plane. Their exact joint law on the complete smooth genus-three quartic population is not determined by rational-point counts or existing unconditional blocking probabilities. The calculation gives \(20\) tangent-filling projective classes and demonstrates both strict non-implications, with \(616\) tangent-filling nonblocking equations and \(1,848\) blocking nontangent-filling equations. It is an exact benchmark for comparison with tangent-filling constructions and smoothness-conditioned statistics; it makes no claim of general asymptotic behavior.

## Closest literature and limits

The scope is the degree-four plane linear system over \(\mathbb F_2\). The same result over larger finite fields is not claimed, and historical novelty has not been independently certified.
