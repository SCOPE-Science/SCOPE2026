The 30-vertex Tutte–Coxeter incidence graph needs exactly ten independent coordinate actuators, and exactly \(2{,}304{,}288\) minimum placements work. Five actuators must lie on each vertex type; among \(1518^2\) separately admissible five-plus-five choices, the only failures are the 36 combinations of an edge-star and a one-factorization of \(K_6\).
