# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-order identity follows by a bijection between a labeled tree with ordered, disjoint prescribed anchor branches and a Cayley tree on the complement plus rooted Cayley trees on each chosen branch. The disjointness and unique outside-edge conditions are necessary. The finite-dimensional Poisson limit follows by the joint factorial-moment method. All offroot terminal components are identified with anchored branches on a high-probability event: terminal root-deficit tightness comes from the exact known root-cluster marginal, and designated-hub pair separation is quantified by an exact distance law. The limit is a proper finite Borel-marked Poisson multiset, so truncation can be removed. The checker exhaustively covers labeled trees up to seven vertices and checks 101 rational equalities, not infinite cases.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority qualified)

Hackl–Panholzer–Wagner (2023), Lemma 8, gives an exact **single-time** multicomponent enumeration which, with exactly one vertex hidden, already implies the one-anchor mixed factorial moments. Theorems 9 and 10 give the fixed-time root cluster law and component means. The fixed-\(d\) compound-Poisson marginal is known and excluded from the novelty claim. The new contribution, for **two or more simultaneously prescribed anchors**, is the finite-\(n\) multivariate mixed factorial-moment formula across anchors, plus finite-time joint marked independent increments; the one-anchor case is an included known boundary case. Stufler (2016) gives a stronger general local weak-limit framework, which could imply asymptotic independence of separated fixed local neighborhoods; it does not provide the precise finite-\(n\) formula. Berzunza Ojeda–Holmgren (2020) concerns edge-deletion continuum fragmentation. Pitman (2001), Sections 5.2–5.3, gives exact fringe-subtree and multirooted-forest component-set distributions, a stronger classical enumeration toolbox from which related formulas may be obtainable; direct statement-level comparison found no specified-terminal-vertex mixed factorial identity or multitime mark process. Named query comparisons and material-read assessments appear in `AUDIT.json`; failure to retrieve an exact duplicate is not a proof of worldwide uniqueness.

The fixed-hidden-vertex boundary window of the uncover process has a natural limit already at one time, but temporal couplings and cluster-size marks are not determined by separate marginal limits.
