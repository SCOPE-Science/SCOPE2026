The quotient of the Fermat quartic threefold by cyclic permutation of its five coordinates is a terminal \(\mathbb Q\)-Fano threefold with exactly four singularities of type \(\frac15(1,2,3)\) and anticanonical volume \(\frac45\). Its third rational Betti number is \(12\), its invariant Hodge number is \(h^{2,1}=6\), and its ordinary and orbifold Euler characteristics are \(-8\) and \(8\), respectively.
