For balanced Johnson graphs, ground-set permutations are indistinguishable from the full graph automorphism group on vertex pairs but become distinguishable on triples, with an exact Venn-cell and complement-reflection classification of every tuple orbit. Explicit ternary polynomials and all-arity Stirling/Burnside formulas show that, for each fixed arity at least three, complement pairs almost all ground-permutation orbit types as the subset size grows.
