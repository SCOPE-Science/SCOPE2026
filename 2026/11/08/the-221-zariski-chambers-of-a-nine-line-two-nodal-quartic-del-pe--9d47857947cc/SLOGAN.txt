Blowing up five complex plane points arranged on two distinct triple-point lines through one common point produces the nine-line \(2A_1\) quartic del Pezzo resolution with exactly \(221\) Zariski chambers. Their negative supports are classified by the bivariate polynomial in `RESULT.md`: \(80\) chambers have finite anticanonically truncated volume, \(141\) have infinite volume, and \(33\) involve both exceptional \((-2)\)-curves.
