# Scientific review: Pappus locating-total codes

## Correctness — PASS

The proof uses the exact eighteen-vertex incidence model, nonempty and distinct **open** neighbor signatures on unselected vertices, and total domination at *every* vertex. For a code with \(a\) selected points and \(b\) selected lines, total domination gives \(a,b\ge3\). An equality-three side forces the other side to be at least six, proving the minimum size eight. Coverage and affine collision counting then prove the complete four-plus-four classification. Compatibility fixes the extra line through the intersection of the chosen omitted line and the chosen retained parallel-class line. The two independent binary choices yield twenty-seven \(C_4\) components; bipartiteness rules out slides. Three executed independent checks corroborate the analytic proof and verify the two customary graph presentations agree.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with risks)

Principal historical context: Balbuena–Foucaud–Hansberg, arXiv:1407.7263 (first posted July 27, 2014), whose accessible primary full text treats ordinary locating domination and identifying codes in girth-at-least-five graphs. Its conclusion explicitly proposes investigating locating-total domination as a different question; its text search has no Pappus instance. Henning–Jafari Rad (2012, DOI:10.1016/j.dam.2012.04.004) establishes definitions and general inequalities, but its full publisher article could not be obtained: the abstract and accessible indexed passages do not decide whether it contains an unindexed special example. Closest related historical Pappus classifications concern ordinary locating-dominating codes, closed identifying codes, and open-neighborhood locating codes. The latter distinguish every graph vertex and require ten vertices, whereas locating-total domination here distinguishes outside vertices only, with an eight-vertex optimum. Neither stronger or weaker predicate implies the present complete optimum geometry or token-mobility behavior. Semantic research-record searches for the graph, exact invariant, affine-plane aliases, and relocation graph found no stronger covering claim. This does not prove uniqueness or historical priority.

## Value — PASS

This is a natural exact locating-and-coverage invariant of a canonical cubic bipartite incidence graph; the twenty-seven-square decomposition is a structural result rather than a bare enumeration. It gives a sharp distinction between unrestricted guard replacements and physically adjacent replacements, with a complete geometric parameterization of all optimal sensor arrangements. The proof shows how a deleted parallel class controls both the minimum and mobility. The specific number of connected components and four-cycle shape cannot be obtained from the minimum cardinality alone.
