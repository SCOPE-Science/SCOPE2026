The resolution of a one-nodal degree-four del Pezzo surface, obtained by blowing up five complex plane points with exactly one collinear triple, has exactly \(285\) Zariski chambers with support polynomial \(1+13t+58t^2+112t^3+87t^4+14t^5\). Exactly \(178\) have finite anticanonically truncated volume and \(107\) have infinite volume, the latter precisely those whose negative support contains the unique \((-2)\)-curve.
