Among strict endomorphisms of an even cycle on \(n\) vertices, the number of regular maps is asymptotic to \(2n\) times the number of idempotents, with a stretched-exponentially small relative error. The regular fraction is \(\exp[-\tfrac32\pi^{2/3}(\log2)^{2/3}n^{1/3}+O(\log n)]\), because maps with two independent full monotone spans pay a strictly larger confinement cost.
