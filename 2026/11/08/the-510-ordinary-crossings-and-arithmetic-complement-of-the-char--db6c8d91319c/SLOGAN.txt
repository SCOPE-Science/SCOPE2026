The 60 geometric lines of the Rams--Schuett quartic over \(\mathbb F_4\) have precisely 510 distinct ordinary double crossings, all \(\mathbb F_{16}\)-rational, and their union has no points over any odd-degree extension. This determines the arithmetic of the line complement: \(Z(S\setminus D,T)=(1-16T^2)^{20}/[(1-T)(1-16T)(1-T^2)^{225}(1-3T+16T^2)]\).
