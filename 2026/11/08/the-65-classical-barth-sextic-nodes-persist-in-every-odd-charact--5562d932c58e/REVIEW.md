## correctness

The exact sextic and gradients vanish at 65 explicit R-sections. Local 3x3 Hessian determinants have norms 2^14, 2^34, 2^18, 2^38, 2^30, 2^30 on six orbit types. For all 2080 unordered pairs, gcd of the norms of the six projective minors is a positive power of two (679 nonunit). Nonzero odd-characteristic reductions of the Hessians and some projective minor follow because a prime dividing an algebraic integer in R necessarily divides its integer norm; every odd prime is excluded. Six orbit types and full 2080 pairs independently reconstructed with exact integer arithmetic, including the ramified p=5; finite ring identities plus the norm argument give uniform all-p proof. The theorem says at least 65 nodes, not exactly all singularities.
