# Scientific review: The 65 classical Barth-sextic nodes persist in every odd characteristic

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof operates over the exact integral ring \(\mathbb Z[\tau]/(\tau^2-\tau-1)\), not over a sampled collection of residue fields. The 65 explicit points all satisfy the sextic equation and its gradient. The six types of local Hessian determinant have absolute algebraic norms \(2^{14},2^{34},2^{18},2^{38},2^{30},2^{30}\). The gcd of the six projective-minor norms for every one of the 2080 pairs is a power of two. These are sufficient universal certificates for nondegenerate nodes and noncollision in all odd characteristics. Both packaged verifiers were executed successfully. The result does not claim that the entire special-fiber singular locus has been exhausted.

## Originality

**PASS with residual priority uncertainty.** Classical Barth geometry establishes 65 complex nodes, not by itself their all-odd-prime persistence. The motivating Bruin–Thomas–Várilly-Alvarado paper (arXiv:1912.08908, first posted 2019-12-18) studies symmetric differentials on the sextic and finite-field reconstruction; it does not establish this explicit ring-theoretic certificate. Historical descriptions and targeted mathematical-record searches reveal no direct implication. The original Barth (1996) full article was not readable during this review; an equivalent observation there or in an uncatalogued later work is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** Prime-uniform preservation of an extremal geometric node configuration is a motivated arithmetic strengthening of the classical construction. The discriminant prime five is not excluded, and the outcome is a split 65-point finite étale subsystem of the relative singular locus over the odd-integer base. This is stronger than merely saying the configuration survives outside some unspecified finite set of primes. It does not assert global equisingularity or positive-characteristic maximum-node bounds.

## Scope and limitations

The mathematical claim is exactly the 65 surviving *designated* ordinary double points and their pairwise distinctness over every odd-characteristic geometric fiber. Extra singularities, geometric integrality of all special fibers and full projective singular-scheme flatness have not been proved. Finite prime-field enumerations are supplementary, not an infinite proof. Independent validation is not claimed.
