The classical 65-node configuration on Barth's sextic has **no odd bad prime**: all 65 designated singular points stay pairwise distinct ordinary nodes after every odd-characteristic reduction, including characteristics \(3\) and \(5\). Exact Hessian norms and projective-minor norm gcds are powers of two, giving a split arithmetic model over \(\mathbb Z[\tau,1/2]/(\tau^2-\tau-1)\); possible additional singularities are not excluded.
