# The 9,934-element two-variable algebra of the two-atom Medvedev frame

## Finding
Let \(M_2\) be the poset of nonempty subsets of \(\{1,2\}\), ordered by reverse inclusion, with its three-world root-and-two-leaves Kripke frame. Consider intuitionistic propositional formulas in precisely two variables \(p,q\), formed from \(\bot,\land,\lor,\to\), under **all persistent valuations** on this *fixed frame*. Two formulas are equivalent if they agree at every world in every such valuation.

**Theorem.** There are exactly \(9{,}934\) equivalence classes. More structurally, their Heyting algebra is isomorphic to the upset algebra of a precisely described \(17\)-element poset \(P\) having four maximal points and thirteen additional points (some incomparable), ordered only by the arrows specified below. In particular, this is the exact free algebra on two generators in the variety generated by the five-element Heyting algebra \(\operatorname{Up}(M_2)\).

## Assumptions and scope
A Kripke valuation assigns each variable an upset of \(M_2\). Write an atomic color \(a\in\{0,1,2,3\}\) for the truth values of \(p,q\), with bit \(1\) for \(p\) and bit \(2\) for \(q\). At a leaf every color is possible. At the root, the color \(a\) must satisfy \(a\mathbin{\&}b=a\) for the color \(b\) of either leaf.

For a root, retain \(a\) and the **set** \(S\) of distinct leaf colors. Thus \(1\le |S|\le2\), with each \(b\in S\) extending \(a\). If \(S=\{a\}\), the root has the same theory as a leaf of color \(a\). After these four identifications, there are precisely \(17\) pointed semantic types.

The types are ordered as follows (all sets of leaf colors use numeric encoding):

| Type indices | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 0, 1, 2, 3 | Leaf colors 0, 1, 2, 3, respectively |
| 4, 5, 6 | Roots with color 0 and leaf supports \(\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\) |
| 7, 8, 9 | Roots with color 0 and leaf supports \(\{0,1\},\{0,2\},\{0,3\}\) |
| 10, 11, 12 | Roots with color 0 and leaf supports \(\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{2,3\}\) |
| 13, 14 | Roots with color 1 and leaf supports \(\{3\},\{1,3\}\) |
| 15, 16 | Roots with color 2 and leaf supports \(\{3\},\{2,3\}\) |

In the poset \(P\), each type \(i\ge4\) is below precisely the leaf types named in its support, in addition to itself. No other distinct comparabilities occur. The theorem makes no assertion for larger Medvedev frames or for three or more variables.

## Proof
**Semantic reduction.** By induction on formulas, truth at a leaf depends only on its atomic color. Truth at the root depends only on its atomic color and on the *set* of leaf colors: implication is evaluated at the root and at both leaves, so multiplicity of a repeated leaf color is irrelevant. When root and both leaves have identical atomic color, induction yields identical truth at all three worlds. Hence every formula defines a set of the seventeen listed types. Persistence implies that this set is upward closed in \(P\); this proves the upper bound \(|\operatorname{Up}(P)|\).

**Finite separation certificate.** Let \(f_1,\ldots,f_{11}\) be the following formulas, with \(\neg\alpha:=\alpha\to\bot\). The right column gives their truth sets as hexadecimal bitmasks, with least significant bit referring to type \(0\) and bit \(16\) to type \(16\).

| \(k\) | \(f_k\) | Truth mask |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | \(p\to q\) | `0x1936d` |
| 2 | \(q\to p\) | `0x06adb` |
| 3 | \((p\to q)\to q\) | `0x1ec1e` |
| 4 | \(p\to\neg q\) | `0x005b7` |
| 5 | \((q\to p)\to p\) | `0x1f42e` |
| 6 | \(\neg(p\to\neg q)\) | `0x0a048` |
| 7 | \(\neg\neg p\to p\) | `0x177af` |
| 8 | \(\neg\neg q\to q\) | `0x1cf9f` |
| 9 | \((p\to\neg q)\to(p\lor q)\) | `0x1f84e` |
| 10 | \(p\to(\neg q\lor q)\) | `0x19fff` |
| 11 | \(q\to(\neg p\lor p)\) | `0x07fff` |

Every entry is obtained directly from the Kripke forcing clause for implication. For a root type \((a,S)\), \(\alpha\to\beta\) holds exactly when \(\alpha\to\beta\) holds at that root as a material implication **and** at each of the leaf colors in \(S\); at leaves it is classical implication. The 11 displayed masks also satisfy the following finite separation identity: for every ordered pair of indices \((i,j)\) **not** in the \(38\)-pair order relation of \(P\), at least one mask has bit \(i\) equal to \(1\) and bit \(j\) equal to \(0\). This is an explicit, exhaustively checkable \(17\times17\) Boolean-matrix certificate, reproduced by `checker.py`.

For each type \(i\), conjoin one such separating formula for every type \(j\notin\uparrow i\). Every conjunct holds at \(i\), hence throughout \(\uparrow i\) by persistence; it fails at its selected \(j\). The resulting formula therefore defines **exactly** \(\uparrow i\). Every upset of the finite poset is a union of principal upsets, so finite disjunctions of these formulas define all upsets. The upper bound is attained and the formula algebra is \(\operatorname{Up}(P)\).

**Counting.** Choose a subset \(L\subseteq\{0,1,2,3\}\) of leaf types to include in an upset. A root type \(i\ge4\) may be included if and only if its leaf support \(S_i\) is contained in \(L\). Once \(L\) is fixed, all such root-type choices are independent. Thus
\[
|\operatorname{Up}(P)|=\sum_{L\subseteq\{0,1,2,3\}}2^{\left|\{i\in\{4,\ldots,16\}:S_i\subseteq L\}\right|}.
\]
In binary-mask order for \(L=0,\ldots,15\), the sixteen summands are
\[
1,1,2,4,2,4,8,32,8,16,64,256,64,256,1024,8192,
\]
whose sum is \(9{,}934\). This concludes the proof.

## Verification
`checker.py` is standalone Python 3. It independently evaluates all eleven witness formulas on all \(25\) pairs of persistent valuations of the three-world Kripke frame, checks their seventeen-class truth-mask certificates, checks all ordered pair separations and constructs every one of the \(9{,}934\) upsets. The expected final line is `MEDVEDEV_M2_TWO_VARIABLE_9934_OK 17 types 38 comparabilities 9934 inequivalent formulas`. The finite certificate is a proof component, not an inference from sampling.

## Relationship to prior work
Chen and Ding (arXiv:2412.20178, first public December 28, 2024) define and axiomatize the individual \(n\)-Medvedev logics, establishing their tabularity, but do not determine this two-variable free-algebra cardinality. Pawłowski (arXiv:2609.11576, first public September 10, 2026) and Almeida–Knudstorp (arXiv:2609.13359, September 11, 2026) investigate the full Medvedev logic and prove undecidability; these theorems concern unbounded formula languages and varying frames, not the fixed rank-two finite algebra. Kostrzycka (2007) treats one-variable fragments and does not supply the two-variable classification. The proposed theorem refines tabularity by an explicit finite semantic dual and exact cardinality, not by another axiomatization.

## Limitations
The conclusion is restricted to intuitionistic propositional formulas in exactly two permitted variables and the **single** frame \(M_2\). It neither provides the free algebra for arbitrary rank nor decides global Medvedev validity. The source comparison cannot exclude equivalent accounts in unindexed historic algebra tables; independent mathematical and bibliographic review has not been performed.
