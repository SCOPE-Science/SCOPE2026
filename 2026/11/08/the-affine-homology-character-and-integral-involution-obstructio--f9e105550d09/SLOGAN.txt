The Paley-17 clique complex has a sixteen-dimensional second rational homology module isomorphic to the augmentation representation of its full affine automorphism group, and this module is rationally irreducible. Under the involution \(x\mapsto -x\), its two integral eigensublattices fail to span by exactly \((\mathbb Z/2)^8\), an arithmetic distinction invisible to its Betti number and rational character.
