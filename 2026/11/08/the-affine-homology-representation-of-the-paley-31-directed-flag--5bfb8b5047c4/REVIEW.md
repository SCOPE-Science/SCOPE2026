# Scientific review: affine homology of the Paley-31 directed flag complex

## Correctness — PASS
The exact object is the simplicial complex of transitive induced subtournaments on the quadratic-residue orientation of the 31-element finite field. Its 27,931 nonempty faces and their full mod-two boundary matrices are enumerated, with ranks (30,435,2820,6945,2790,465). These ranks and the explicit differential-square checks imply mod-two Betti vector (1,0,0,0,960,0,0); universal coefficients bound rational Betti numbers, while the independent Euler characteristic fixes rational fourth homology dimension at 960 and vanishing elsewhere. For an affine orientation-preserving permutation, each setwise invariant transitive simplex is pointwise invariant because its total order is canonical; the reduced Lefschetz trace is therefore 960 at identity, -1 for nontrivial translations and 0 for affine elements with nontrivial multiplier. These values agree with two copies of the regular group representation plus the natural 30-dimensional augmentation representation. Maschke semisimplicity justifies the isomorphism over the rationals. The complete computational proof is reproducible using `verify.py` and `verification_output.txt`.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The canonical quadratic-residue Paley tournament at 31 vertices is the next relevant prime order beyond the complete published doubly regular tournament census up through 27 vertices. Its substantial rational homology carries natural arithmetic symmetries. The exact decomposition determines two globally invariant fourth-homology directions, 30 translation-invariant directions, and the full trace of all 465 affine symmetries. These invariants are strictly finer than the already known homotopy type and provide a reproducible natural test for equivariant tournament topology, without demanding a general-family claim.
