For all \(n\ge7\), the all-on Lights Out strategies for \(\operatorname{GP}(n,3)\) admit a complete weight enumerator controlled by \(\gcd(n,20)\). Every step-two strategy also works at step three, and exactly when \(4\mid n\) the step-three space is four times larger, with a new seven-weight resonance and \(192\) additional strategies when \(20\mid n\).
