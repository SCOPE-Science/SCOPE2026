The all-on Lights Out weight distribution of \(\operatorname{GP}(n,3)\) is explicitly determined by \(\gcd(n,20)\). Earlier results already give the parity-solution kernel, its total size, the step-two weights and the four perfect-code minima; the new exact refinement is the weight splitting at orders divisible by four, including seven possible weights and 256 strategies when \(20\mid n\).
