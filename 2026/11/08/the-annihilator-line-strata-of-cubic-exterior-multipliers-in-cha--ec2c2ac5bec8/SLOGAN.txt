In characteristic two, every nonzero cubic exterior element with a linear wedge-annihilator lies either on the decomposable Grassmann cone or on a uniquely parametrized alternating-rank stratum, with an explicit complete graded multiplication-rank polynomial. Uniform finite-field formulas count all these elements, and their union is an irreducible closed variety of dimension \(\binom{n}{2}\) for each \(n\ge5\).
