In the unit-transfer graph on integer partitions, the component containing \((n-1,1)\) after restricting to exactly two distinct part sizes is a closed hook-and-side framework with two extra vertices precisely for odd \(n\ge7\). Its order is \(2n-6\) for even \(n\ge4\), or \(2n-3\) for odd \(n\ge7\); the full two-support stratum is connected exactly for \(3\le n\le7\).
