The unique genus-two canonical curve of each maximal-order \((K^2,p_g)=(1,1)\) surface forgets exactly the sign of its surface-moduli parameter: in natural coordinates its moduli map is \(u=v^2-2\). The surface automorphisms act on that curve through a fixed order-twelve subgroup with cyclic kernel of order six, while additional nonliftable curve automorphisms arise precisely at the hexagonal and Bolza parameters.
