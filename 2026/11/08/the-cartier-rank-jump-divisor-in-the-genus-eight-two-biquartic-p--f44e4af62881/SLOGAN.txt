For the full genus-eight, characteristic-three Artin–Schreier family with two order-four poles, the Jacobian \(a\)-number is exactly \(5\) on the smooth divisor \(ab=\lambda\) and exactly \(4\) elsewhere. Every higher Cartier power has rank \(2\), so the jump changes the nilpotent Cartier-block structure while leaving genus and \(3\)-rank fixed.
