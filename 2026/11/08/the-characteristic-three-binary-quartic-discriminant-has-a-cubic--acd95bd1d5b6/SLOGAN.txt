In characteristic \(3\), the full singular scheme of the binary-quartic discriminant is the union of the cubic infinitesimal neighborhood of a smooth triple-root quadric and a reduced projected Veronese surface: \(\mathcal I_Z=\mathcal I_T^3\cap\mathcal I_E\). The triple-root transverse length jumps from the characteristic-zero value \(2\) to \(6\), yet the total degree remains \(16\).
