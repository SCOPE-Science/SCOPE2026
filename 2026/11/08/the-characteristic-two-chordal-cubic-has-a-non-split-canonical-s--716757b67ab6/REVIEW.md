# Scientific assessment

## Correctness: PASS
Differentiating the explicit Hankel determinant in characteristic two gives exactly three gradient quadrics; Euler's identity puts the cubic itself in their ideal. The two affine charts cover every point and yield dual-number coordinate rings, with transition \(s=t^{-1}+t^{-4}u\), \(v=t^{-6}u\). That transition establishes both \(\mathcal N\cong\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-6)\) and the nontrivial extension: no Laurent monomial of degree two can be cancelled by the allowed coordinate changes. Height three of the three quadrics gives their regular sequence, and adjunction and Euler characteristics give the degree, genus, and canonical embedding. The included exact-arithmetic checker independently verifies the key identities. Risks: code is a corroboration, not an independent formal proof, and the generic characteristic-two assertions rest on the explicit algebraic argument.

## Originality: PASS (provisional priority)
The closest inspected source is Raicu's *3x3 Minors of Catalecticants*, which explicitly writes down the same determinant and identifies its reduced secant geometry (arXiv:1011.1564, introduction); it does not calculate the Jacobian scheme in characteristic two and its main result assumes characteristic zero. Kanev's *Chordal varieties of Veronese varieties and catalecticant matrices* (arXiv:math/9804141, introduction and Example 1.2) develops determinant and secant equations, explicitly over characteristic zero. Casalaina-Martin--Laza (arXiv:0710.5329, Sections 1-2) treats the chordal cubic in complex moduli. Bayer--Eisenbud, *Ribbons and Their Canonical Embeddings* (Sections 1-3 and canonical-embedding framework), introduces the class of ribbons but does not supply the displayed Jacobian-scheme computation. Searches for equivalent descriptions as a Hankel-gradient complete intersection or canonical ribbon produced no covering result. Residual risk: older determinantal or ribbon literature could contain the exact example, and all novelty claims await external specialist review.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The chordal cubic is the classical boundary object for cubic-threefold degeneration. Its positive-characteristic Jacobian scheme is a natural scheme-theoretic invariant whose doubled, globally nonsplit structure is invisible from the familiar reduced rational normal quartic. The result goes beyond a local derivative exercise by determining the complete global gluing class, a canonical genus-five ribbon, and a sharp obstruction to splitting. It does not assert a new abstract ribbon type or a general theorem on all determinants.
