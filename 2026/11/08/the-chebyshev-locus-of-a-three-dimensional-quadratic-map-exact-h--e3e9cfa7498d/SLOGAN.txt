A single explicit algebraic relation identifies the nondegenerate Chebyshev locus inside a studied three-dimensional quadratic map. Each parameter on this locus carries an invariant box with three equal positive Lyapunov exponents \(\frac{\log 2}{3}\), a unique absolutely continuous physical measure, and exactly \(2^n\) points fixed by the \(n\)-th iterate for every \(n\ge1\).
