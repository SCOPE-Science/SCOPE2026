# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The associative-form/functionals correspondence follows from the unit, and the block matrix of the multiplication pairing yields an exact kernel count. The two support sets and uncovered diagonal entries are the only rank-relevant data. The support enumeration and maximum-rank/Frobenius consequences follow for all finite fields, loops, and parallel arrows. Direct brute-force matrices over prime fields and an independently implemented extension field corroborate the formula.

## Originality
PASS under bounded historical checking, with provisional priority. Chen (2011) classifies self-injective and Gorenstein radical-square-zero algebras rather than the degeneracy histogram of all associative bilinear forms. Artenstein–González–Lanzilotta (2013), whose accessible full text was inspected, investigates nearly Frobenius coproducts on quiver algebras, rather than finite-field multiplication-pairing ranks. A prior theorem counting Frobenius functionals by units covers only the nondegenerate specialization; that specialization is not claimed as new. Existing exact ideal-pair and commutator enumerations do not state the present graph-support rank polynomial; a chain and fork have identical undirected commutator support data but different bilinear-rank distributions. Targeted searches did not locate a dominating result. Main risks are an unidentified general form-rank distribution theorem and incomplete access to Chen's full text.

## Value
PASS. The rank distribution is an intrinsic invariant of all associative bilinear forms on an algebra; it quantifies failure of Frobenius duality even for non-Frobenius algebras. A complete all-quiver formula, the maximum-rank structural threshold, and the chain/fork orientation distinction are mathematically motivated and broader than an isolated small example. The known nondegenerate-case enumeration alone is not the contribution.

## Limitations
The classification is for split radical-square-zero quiver algebras and does not cover arbitrary quotients or higher radical layers. The finite checks do not replace the proof, and independent audit is outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
