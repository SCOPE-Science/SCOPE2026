Over \(\mathbb F_2\), the upper-triangular three-by-three algebra has exactly \(652\) rank-two weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators, supported on \(58\) of its \(651\) image planes. The complete image-plane fiber multiplicities are \(0,3,4,5,6,210\), with the two \(210\)-operator fibers arising from square-zero two-sided ideals.
