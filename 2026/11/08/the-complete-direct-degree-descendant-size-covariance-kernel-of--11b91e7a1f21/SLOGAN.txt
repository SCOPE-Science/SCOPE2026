In a uniform recursive tree, the covariance of a vertex's direct-child count with another vertex's full descendant-subtree size has a complete triangular law: every earlier vertex contributes the same negative amount, the matching vertex contributes their exact positive sum, and every later vertex is uncorrelated. For \(i<j\) it is \(-(n-j)/j^2\), for \(i=j\) it is \((n-j)(j-1)/j^2\), and for \(i>j\) it is zero.
