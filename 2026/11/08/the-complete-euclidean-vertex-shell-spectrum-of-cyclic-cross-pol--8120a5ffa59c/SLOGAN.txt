For the Minkowski sum of coordinate diamonds indexed by a cycle on \(n\ge5\) vertices, the complete Euclidean vertex spectrum has squared radii \(n+2k\) and multiplicities \(2^{n-k+1}\binom n{2k}\). The sharp enclosing sphere has squared radius \(2n\) for even \(n\) and \(2n-1\) for odd \(n\), with every diameter pair consisting of opposite outer-shell vertices.
