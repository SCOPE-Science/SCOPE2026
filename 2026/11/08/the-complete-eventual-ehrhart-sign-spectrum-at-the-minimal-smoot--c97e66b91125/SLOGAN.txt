At the smallest possible facet count allowing Ehrhart negativity for smooth polytopes of dimension \(d\ge5\), the simplex-fiber family has an exact eventual sign pattern: the negative degrees are \(d-4m\) and \(d-1-4m\), whenever positive. The number of simultaneous negative coefficients is \(\lfloor(d-1)/4\rfloor+\lfloor(d-2)/4\rfloor\), which grows unboundedly with dimension.
