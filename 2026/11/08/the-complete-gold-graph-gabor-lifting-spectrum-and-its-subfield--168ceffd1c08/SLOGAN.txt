The binary Gold graph has an exactly computable complete Gabor intensity-lifting spectrum: its positive operator rank and singular-value multiplicities depend only on \(q=2^m\), \(K=2^{\gcd(m,r)}\), and the parity of \(m/\gcd(m,r)\). The positive condition number is exactly \(q/K\), while the zero-translation spectral strata undergo a sharp subfield-parity transition.
