# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The manuscript derives the original star-graph bracket matrix, includes the \(q^{m+1}\) unobserved functional coordinates, and proves \(\operatorname{rank}\mathcal K=2\operatorname{rank}[-\alpha\mid B]\). In characteristic two the symmetric zero-diagonal block \(B\) is alternating and has ranks \(2k\). Choosing its radical and a nondegenerate alternating quotient yields \(H_{m,k}(q)\) using \(|\mathrm{GL}|/|\mathrm{Sp}|\). The final column-space dichotomy gives the stated polynomial. Endpoint ranks and normalization \(P_{m,q}(1)=q^{\dim L_m}\) are verified symbolically. The standalone direct-bracket checker passes for six finite-field configurations; these experiments are supporting evidence, not a general proof.

## Originality — PASS with explicit risk
The motivating Gallo–Videla paper's fully inspected star section gives dimension and lower-central-series structure, not Kirillov rank strata. Burde–Dekimpe's fully inspected treatment of index addresses free nilpotent and older two-step graph Lie algebras and does not imply the complete characteristic-two rank multiplicities of this three-step star family. Closest retrieved mathematical research records concern other coadjoint spectra, including filiform Lie algebras and two-step graphical groups. Accessible earlier star results concern element commuting probabilities, not the distribution of functionals by coadjoint stabilizer dimension. These are nonimplication comparisons, not a proof of global historical uniqueness; an unlocated general matrix-pencil calculation might subsume this count.

## Mathematical value — PASS
For the newly constructed infinite family this is an exact, all-rank invariant, rather than another low-dimensional census or a generic-index computation. It detects every coadjoint radical stratum, an unusually fine algebraic measure of the variation among functionals. The additional column bridges alternating-rank geometry to Lie coadjoint geometry, and the formula yields exact finite-field distributions, sharp generic radical dimension and concrete parity-dependent rank support. This is substantively more information than the previously published structural dimensions or a single commuting moment.

## Boundaries and literature
The characteristic-two restriction is essential to the alternating-matrix count; over odd characteristic the symmetric zero-diagonal block need not have even rank. Coadjoint rank is not itself a count of group irreducible characters in characteristic two. Previously known alternate forms and graph algebra definitions are explicitly credited. Expert bibliographic validation remains desirable.
