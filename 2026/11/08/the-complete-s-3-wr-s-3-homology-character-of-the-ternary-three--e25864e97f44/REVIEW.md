# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. All 5,949 nonempty simplices are enumerated from the Hamming metric, with nine documented face counts. All eight mod-two boundary ranks follow from exact bit-vector elimination; the resulting homology and Euler characteristic give rational homology concentrated in dimension three, rank 46. A signed simplicial Lefschetz computation evaluates 22 class representatives, and an independently derived vertex-orbit alternating count evaluates all 1296 automorphisms. The two methods agree on every representative. Conjugacy classes, their sizes, character norms, and invariant dimensions are checked by exact integer operations. There is no extrapolation from finite samples.

## Originality

PASS relative to inspected sources. The 2010 general graph independence-complex methods do not determine this module. The 2019 categorical-product classification treats two factors, the 2023 theorem treats three factors with a binary factor, and the 2026 equivariant Rips analysis treats binary hypercubes. The closest published 27-point result establishes exactly the nonequivariant 46-sphere wedge, which neither determines the trace of each symmetry nor distinguishes zero full-group invariants from two translation invariants. Existing research in this area and unindexed representation calculations remain potential publication-priority risks.

## Value

PASS. The Hamming three-cube is a canonical highly symmetric finite metric space; its scale-two Rips complex is a distinguished three-factor categorical-product independence complex. The complete 22-class character is a natural invariant strictly finer than the previously known nonequivariant homotopy type. The different full-group and translation-subgroup fixed dimensions are a concrete structural phenomenon, not a restatement of the Betti number. The result is limited to one natural finite object, with no general-family or integral representation claim.
