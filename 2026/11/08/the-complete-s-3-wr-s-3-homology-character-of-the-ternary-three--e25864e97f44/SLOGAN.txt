For the 27-point ternary Hamming three-cube at Vietoris–Rips scale two, the natural \(S_3\wr S_3\) symmetry action on \(46\)-dimensional rational third homology has an explicitly determined character on all 22 conjugacy classes. The full symmetry group fixes no nonzero homology class, whereas the translation subgroup \(C_3^3\) fixes a two-dimensional subspace.
