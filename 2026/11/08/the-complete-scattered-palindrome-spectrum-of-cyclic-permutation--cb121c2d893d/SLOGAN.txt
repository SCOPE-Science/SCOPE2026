For the periodic word \((0\,1\,\cdots\,(q-1))^\omega\) with \(q\ge3\), the complete host-length/length spectrum of distinct scattered palindromes is \(\frac{z(1-z^q)t(1+t z^q)}{(1-z)^2(1-t^2z^q(q-1+z^q))}\). The exact formula comes from paired cyclic distances in palindromic greedy embeddings and gives a sharp growth base \(\left(\frac{q-1+\sqrt{(q-1)^2+4}}2\right)^{1/q}\).
