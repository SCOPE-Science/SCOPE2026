# The complete six-label contiguous-block shattering complex

## Finding
For the 720 linear orders of the six-element set \(X=\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}\), let a Boolean query indexed by any subset \(B\subset X\) with \(2\le |B|\le5\) ask whether the members of \(B\) occupy consecutive positions. The family has 56 distinct queries and 360 distinct complete response patterns. Its shattering polynomial is exactly
\[
\Phi_6(z)=1+56z+1540z^2+17640z^3+54810z^4+20160z^5.
\]
In particular its VC dimension is five. There are 25,830 inclusion-maximal shattered supports of size four and 20,160 of size five, forming respectively 47 and 29 orbits under all six-label permutations. Thus its shattering complex is nonpure.

For any five-query shattered support \(S\) and any order \(p=(p_1,\ldots,p_6)\) in which every query of \(S\) is positive, define the \(5\times5\) matrix over \(\mathbb F_2\) by \(D_{B,i}=\mathbf1_B(p_i)+\mathbf1_B(p_{i+1})\), for \(B\in S\) and \(1\le i\le5\). The rank of \(D\) does not depend on the choice of positive order. Exactly 5,040 of the 20,160 five-query shattered supports have rank four; the remaining 15,120 have rank five. These two classes consist of eight and twenty-one relabeling orbits respectively. Rank deficiency therefore does not preclude maximal shattering.

## Assumptions and scope
The ground set has exactly six distinct labeled elements. Every strict linear order is permitted. A query is identified with its *unordered subset of labels* rather than an interval of numerical values or a triple-betweenness relation. Singletons, the empty set and the full set are excluded because their answers are constant. A support \(S\) is shattered when every map \(S\to\{0,1\}\) is realized by at least one order. A maximal support is inclusion-maximal among shattered supports, not necessarily of maximum cardinality. Rank is over the field with two elements; its definition requires all queried subsets to be consecutive in the chosen order, which is guaranteed by shattering.

## Proof
List the 56 subset masks in increasing cardinality and lexicographic order, using labels \(0,\ldots,5\). For an order \(p\), let \(R(p)\) be its 56-bit response mask. Two independent constructions of \(R\) agree: enumerate all consecutive windows of lengths two through five, and alternatively test \(\max_{x\in B}\mathrm{pos}_p(x)-\min_{x\in B}\mathrm{pos}_p(x)+1=|B|\) for every query. The adjacent-pair responses reconstruct the undirected path of consecutive labels, which determines the order up to reversal. Conversely reversal preserves every query. Thus precisely \(6!/2=360\) distinct response masks arise.

For each query \(i\), form the exact 360-bit incidence set \(Y_i=\{R:R_i=1\}\) and its complement \(N_i\). For a sorted query support \(S=(i_1,\ldots,i_k)\), start from one nonempty cell consisting of all 360 responses and split every current cell by \(Y_{i_j}\) and \(N_{i_j}\). The support is shattered exactly when every one of the \(2^k\) resulting cells is nonempty. This is equivalence, not an approximation. Generate increasing supports recursively, retaining a child only when both halves of every existing cell are nonempty. If a support is not shattered, no extension of it is shattered; hence this monotone recursion enumerates **every** shattered support. The supplied standalone checker performs these exact integer-bitset operations and obtains the six coefficients shown above, and no shattered support with six queries. Downward closure excludes all larger supports.

For each level, a support is inclusion-maximal if and only if it is absent from the immediate predecessor deletion images of the next level. The checker exhaustively constructs these images and finds precisely 25,830 maximal four-query supports and 20,160 maximal five-query supports, with none in smaller levels. It applies all \(6!\) label permutations to each support and partitions each maximal level into disjoint orbits, verifying 47 and 29 orbits with sizes and canonical representatives stored in `interval6_orbits.json`. Their orbit sizes sum to the two facet counts.

For each five-query support, the all-positive trace cell contains every response mask of an order making its queries consecutive. Each response mask is realized by exactly two reverse orders. Reversal reverses the five boundary columns, leaving matrix rank unchanged. Thus checking one representative of each response mask in the all-positive cell checks every eligible order. Exhaustive binary Gaussian elimination over this complete cell proves rank independence for all 20,160 supports and the exact rank counts 5,040 and 15,120; orbit calculations give eight and twenty-one respective orbits. The checker uses all masks in every cell rather than a sample. A separate 32-order witness table exhibits every response pattern on one shattered five-query support.

## Verification
Run `python3 interval6_verify.py interval6_orbits.json interval6_witnesses.json` from a directory containing the three included files. The checker uses Python's standard library and reconstructs 720 permutations, 360 distinct complete response patterns, every shattered support and facet, all 76 labeled symmetry orbits, the ranks for every all-positive witness, and 32 explicit order witnesses. It compares direct sliding windows against an independent position-span query representation for every order and query, and compares the reconstructed orbit and witness tables against the bundled certificates. Expected terminal marker: `INTERVAL6_SHATTER_PASS`. The finite enumeration is exhaustive for the stated six-label domain, not an experimental inference about larger ground sets.

## Relationship to prior work
Bhaskar (2017) develops VC/thicket complexity for arbitrary set systems, providing the general shattering framework, but no six-label consecutive-block classification. Tenner (2020) studies interval posets of permutations, with an interval meaning consecutive *values* appearing consecutively. Bagno, Eisenberg, Reches and Sigron (2023) study blockwise-simple permutations and their interval-poset structure. These sources motivate the natural block predicate and its structural relevance but do not provide this complete 56-query shattering polynomial or its maximal-facet rank stratification. The all-subsets-of-labels query class here is distinct from a fixed family of consecutive-value intervals. This report does not assert that other terminology or unavailable literature cannot imply the result.

## Limitations
The theorem is an exact finite classification at six labels; it makes no general formula or asymptotic claim for arbitrary ground-set size. Its constructive proof depends on the supplied, inspectable finite enumeration and two exact-response representations. Historical priority remains uncertain for older consecutive-ones and permutation interval-structure literature beyond the inspected materials. No independent external mathematical audit has been performed.
