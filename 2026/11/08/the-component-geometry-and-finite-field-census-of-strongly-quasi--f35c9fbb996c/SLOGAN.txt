For every size \(n\), the strongly quasi-nil-clean matrix locus over an algebraically closed field has exactly \(n\) irreducible components of dimension \(n^2-n+1\), with every pair intersecting exactly in the nilpotent cone. The finite-field element census follows by specializing classical prescribed-characteristic-polynomial counts, and gives fixed-field density \(\sim (q-1)nq^{-n}/\prod_{j\ge1}(1-q^{-j})\).
