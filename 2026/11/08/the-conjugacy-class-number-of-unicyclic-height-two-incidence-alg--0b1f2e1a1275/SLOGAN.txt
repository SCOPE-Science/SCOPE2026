For any finite height-two incidence algebra whose comparability graph is connected and unicyclic, the number of conjugacy classes of its unit group is exactly \(q^v+(q-2)q^{v-\ell+1}\), depending only on the vertex count and the unique even-cycle length. For fixed \(q>2\), the unit-group order and class number together recover the cycle length, regardless of the shapes of the attached trees.
