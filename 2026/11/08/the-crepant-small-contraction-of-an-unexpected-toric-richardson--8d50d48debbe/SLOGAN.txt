The first unexpected smooth toric Richardson fourfold is weak Fano of Picard rank four and Fano index two, with \(L^4=20\) for \(-K_X=2L\). Its crepant small anticanonical contraction replaces two disjoint \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) surfaces by two disjoint conifold-singular projective lines.
