For exponentially many independent nearest-neighbor walkers started together on a finite cycle, the cover-time fluctuation law acquires the explicit lattice correction \(16c^3/3\) when \(\log k_n/n^{2/3}\to c\): its limit is \(16c^3/3-G_1-G_2\) for independent standard Gumbels. The continuum-centered window is sharply non-tight beyond this threshold, although the leading cover time remains \(n^2/(8\log k_n)\) throughout the sublinear logarithmic-walker regime.
