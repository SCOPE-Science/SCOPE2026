**Correctness — PASS.** Exact degree-constrained enumeration and the logically equivalent forbidden-star enumeration agree for all 4,096 graph-edge subsets. A complete matched Hasse graph has 847 verified covering pairs and one zero-dimensional plus three five-dimensional critical cells; all 7,980 covering incidences were examined by a complete acyclicity traversal. This implies the claimed integral sphere wedge by discrete Morse theory and the absence of possible intermediate attaching maps. Independent reversed-sweep acyclicity and exact mod-two boundary ranks corroborate it. The signed Lefschetz character is checked independently on all 48 automorphisms, and the character comparison matches the twisted natural module.

**Originality — PASS against accessible scientific evidence.** Vega's 2019 initial two-matching study treats claws, whiskered cycles, wheels and caterpillars; Singh's 2019 forest classification and his 2020 complete-graph classification do not give a theorem for the cube. Indexed exact homotopy records for the ordinary cube matching complex, the Petersen two-matching complex and complete bipartite two-matching complexes concern different constraints or graphs. The equivalence \(Q_3\cong K_{4,4}\setminus M\) was considered: the known result on \(K_{4,4}\) gives no homotopy equivalence upon deleting a perfect matching. Searches under cube, four-crown, bounded-degree and representation aliases did not expose a stronger covering theorem. Residual risk: the accessible summaries of some close indexed results do not constitute a full-manuscript exclusion, and unindexed equivalent work may exist.

**Value — PASS.** The three-cube is the smallest connected noncycle hypercube, a canonical vertex-transitive cubic graph. This determines the complete integral homotopy type for a motivated bounded-degree construction on that graph and the irreducible rational symmetry module, not just face counts. Comparing with the known ordinary matching complex exhibits a dimension/rank transition from four second-sphere summands to three fifth-sphere summands under the degree-bound change. The signed coordinate-module identification may guide the higher-cube family, but no such extension is claimed. It is a natural exact finite classification, not an arbitrary parameter slice.

**Scope and risks.** No higher-dimensional hypercube theorem, integral equivariant lattice, or equivariant sphere splitting is proved. The three closest foundational general PDFs were accessed and their relevant theorem hypotheses inspected; two same-family public research records were accessible only in indexed synopsis form.
