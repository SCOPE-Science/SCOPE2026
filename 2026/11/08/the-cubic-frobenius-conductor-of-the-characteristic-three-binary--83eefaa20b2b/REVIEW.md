**Correctness — PASS.** Differentiation gives the entire cubic power \((b,c)^3\) as the Jacobian ideal; the hypersurface equation belongs to this ideal. The local finite free normalization algebra has an explicit lattice and conductor computation on two charts covering the singular line. The affine computation distinguishes \(\mathcal I_L^2\) from the whole radical ideal. The universal-homeomorphism and cohomology conclusions follow from finite birational geometry and a hypersurface exact sequence. The symbolic and small-field checks were replayed, but finite checks are not the proof of the all-field theorem.

**Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority).** The binary-cubic discriminant and its repeated-factor parametrization are standard, as are general characteristic-zero discussions of coincident-root loci. Chipalkatti (arXiv:math/0110224), Kurmann (arXiv:1108.4532), and D'Andrea–Chipalkatti (arXiv:math/0601705) are the closest inspected primary works; the latter's cubic degree-six singular scheme has twisted-cubic support and the authors restrict their results to characteristic zero. The assessed new combination is the characteristic-three triple-line Jacobian scheme, exact conductor square, and degree-three Frobenius over the singular line. Targeted published-finding corpus record searches and primary-source comparisons found no equivalently covering statement; an uninspected older publication might still establish priority.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The object is the universal binary-cubic discriminant, not an ad-hoc polynomial. The complete Jacobian thickening and conductor provide precise scheme-theoretic measures of wild degeneration, and the Frobenius normalization explains why arithmetic point counts match the smooth normalization despite a nonzero coherent \(H^2\) on the hypersurface.

**Limitations.** The conclusion is characteristic-three only. The argument does not classify all coincident-root loci, nor does the sample computation establish uniqueness in published literature. Independent audit and specialist historical review have not been performed.
