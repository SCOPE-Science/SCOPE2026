The perfect-matching complex of the octahedral graph has a canonical full-symmetry-equivariant nerve equal to the cube graph \(Q_3\), giving the integral cube cycle lattice \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z^5\) with rational module \(S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus(S^{(2,2)}\otimes\det)\). Its inclusion into the ordinary matching complex has a primitive six-dimensional signed permutation quotient indexed by precisely the nonextendible two-edge matchings.
