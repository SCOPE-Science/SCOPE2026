# Scientific review: cuboctahedral second-independence complex

## Correctness
PASS. The full integer-mask domain comprises all \(4095\) nonempty subsets of twelve graph vertices; the adjacency rule is independently derived from the twelve edges of the three-cube. The accepted \(455\) faces have vector \((12,66,164,177,36)\). The executable matching certificate consists of \(211\) disjoint covering pairs, leaving one critical vertex and thirty-two critical three-cells. Exhaustive orientation and topological elimination of all \(1512\) face-poset covers proves the matching acyclic. Forman's CW theorem yields the claimed integral homotopy type directly. A second independent forbidden-triple and mod-two boundary reduction agrees with the face counts and Betti numbers. Failure of any certificate assertion would invalidate this assessment.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The cuboctahedral graph is a canonical four-regular Archimedean polyhedral graph and the line graph of the three-cube. Its second-independence complex is a natural nonflag forbidden-path object; unlike standard cycle, forest and multipartite examples it includes interacting triangles and squares. Determining an exact integral homotopy type with thirty-two three-spheres, rather than merely its Euler characteristic or field homology, supplies a concrete benchmark for the topology of such complexes on polyhedral line graphs. No broader classification or downstream theorem is asserted.
