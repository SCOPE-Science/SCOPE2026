For three-run binary words \(0^a1^b0^c\) with \(b\ge2\), all cyclic-cover lengths form one explicit terminal interval, but its starting point jumps from \(a+\lceil b/2\rceil\) for equal outer runs to \(b+\max(a,c)\) otherwise. A complete factor census and a two-versus-one cyclic-root dichotomy show that inserting a single zero at one end can increase the shortest cover length by an arbitrarily large amount.
