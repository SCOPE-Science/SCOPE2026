The Dürer graph is fixed as \(GP(6,2)\), and the simplicial vertices are its **edges**. The known integral homotopy type \(\mathcal M(G)\simeq\bigvee^{22}S^3\) is stated as a premise credited to the October 18, 2026 published calculation. This confirms that the homotopy premise is correct, without presenting it as a new result.
