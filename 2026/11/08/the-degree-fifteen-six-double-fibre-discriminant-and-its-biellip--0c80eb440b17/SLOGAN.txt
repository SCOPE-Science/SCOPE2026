The six singular-fibre parameters of a rational elliptic surface with exactly six \(I_2\) fibres are precisely the branch sextics of bielliptic genus-two curves: their reduced locus is cut out by a concrete degree-fifteen product of pairing determinants. A generic branch sextic reconstructs the elliptic surface uniquely, while the degree-fifteen hypersurface has an explicit generic transverse two-branch singular stratum.
