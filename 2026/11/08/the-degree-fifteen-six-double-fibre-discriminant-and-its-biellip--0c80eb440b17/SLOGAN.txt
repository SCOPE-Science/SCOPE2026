The classical bielliptic binary-sextic divisor has a generically ordinary two-branch crossing along the four-dimensional family of six-root configurations projectively equivalent to \(\{\pm1,\pm a,\pm a^{-1}\}\). At an exact squarefree witness only two of the fifteen classical matching determinants vanish and their gradient minor is \(9/8\); openness proves generic transverse intersection in coefficient space.
