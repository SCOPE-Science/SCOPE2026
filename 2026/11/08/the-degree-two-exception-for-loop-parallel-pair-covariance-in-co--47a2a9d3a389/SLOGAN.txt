For uniformly paired half-edges of any prescribed degree sequence, the covariance between self-loops and parallel-edge pairs is positive exactly when every nonisolated vertex has degree two (with at least three such vertices); apart from the degenerate zero case, it is otherwise strictly negative. In regular pairing models the degree-two positive covariance is of order \(v^{-2}\), whereas the covariance at every fixed degree at least three is negative of order \(v^{-1}\).
