The anti-diagonal X-ray image of every \(k\)-by-\(k\) Birkhoff polytope has squared Euclidean diameter exactly \(2k^2-2k+2\), attained at precisely two pairs of vertices (one pair at \(k=2\)). For \(k\ge4\), the next-largest squared separation among distinct permutation X-rays is exactly \(2k^2-4k+8\), giving a sharp discrete gap of \(2(k-3)\).
