For a curvature-one spherical football with two equal cone angles \(2\pi\alpha<2\pi\), the probability that two uniform points are within \(\varepsilon\) of the diameter is \(C(\alpha)\varepsilon^4+o(\varepsilon^4)\), where \(C(\alpha)=[\csc^2(\pi\alpha)-\cot(\pi\alpha)/(\pi\alpha)]/32\). The strictly increasing coefficient recovers the cone angle, while the smooth round-sphere endpoint has a different quadratic tail.
