The cyclic-pair probability \(\theta(G)\) of a noncyclic finite abelian \(p\)-group has an exact rational-value spectrum: after its prime-power denominator determines the unknown prime and order, a derived integer has base-\(p\) digits consisting of decreasing-length runs of \(p-1\), separated by single zeros. This digit rule is necessary and sufficient for membership and reconstructs the entire group, giving exactly \(P(n)-1\) distinct values with reduced denominator \(p^{2n-1}\).
