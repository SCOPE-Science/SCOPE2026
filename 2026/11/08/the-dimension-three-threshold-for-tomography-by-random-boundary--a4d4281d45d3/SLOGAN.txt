For two independent surface-uniform points of any Euclidean parallelotope, the midpoint has an explicit singular-plus-continuous law. After conditioning the midpoint to avoid coordinate-center slices, the distribution loses every rectangle's aspect ratio in dimension two, but in every dimension at least three finitely many tensor moments reconstruct the opposing-facet-area weights (and hence all orthotope side ratios); the three-cube has exact conditional total-variation bias \(13/96\).
