The binary second-order Reed–Muller code in numerical coordinate order has a complete, exact cyclic symbol-pair generation filtration: successive low-weight spans are precisely the degree-two subcodes on the highest \(3,4,\ldots,m\) binary coordinate bits. Their dimensions are triangular-number increments at dyadic thresholds, and the pair-distance graph first becomes connected at \(3\cdot2^{m-3}\).
