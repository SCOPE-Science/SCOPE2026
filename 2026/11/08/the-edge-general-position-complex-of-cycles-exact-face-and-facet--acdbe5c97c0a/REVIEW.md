# Scientific review: exact edge-general-position faces and facets of cycles

## Correctness
PASS. On a cycle with \(n\ge6\), any shortest path has at most \(h=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) edges. Three consecutive selected edges lie on a clockwise shortest arc exactly when two successive positive cyclic gaps have sum below \(h\). This gives the if-and-only-if gap test, and summing its inequalities rules out five or more selected edges. Bounded positive-composition counting gives the three-face and four-face coefficients; the four-gap constraints and one-gap splitting arguments characterize every maximal face. The inclusion-exclusion term for odd cycles correctly includes the three double-overlap compositions \((h,h,1)\) and its permutations. The boundary orders \(3,4,5\), the exceptional pure order \(7\), and the claim for all orders have explicit proofs. The independent geodesic-arc verifier exhausts all subsets through order \(18\) and tests three- to five-edge subsets through order \(26\).

## Originality
PASS, provisionally. Manuel, Prabha and Klavžar already give the scalar maximum edge-general-position cardinality for every cycle, restated by Tian, Klavžar and Tan. This known result implies only the degree of the face polynomial and does not determine its third and fourth coefficients or the inclusion-maximal facet spectrum. Iršič and collaborators define *vertex* general-position polynomials, not the edge version. Published cactus and graph-extremal work inspected to date treats sharp maximum cardinalities and graph-class boundaries, rather than the complete cycle configuration census. A mathematically equivalent presentation using geodesic-triple hypergraphs or cyclic compositions would cover the result; the inspected sources and semantic research-record searches did not identify one. One 2025 scalar-extremal paper and the generalized edge-position paper were not completely accessible, so the possibility of an unindexed equivalent remains a concrete publication-priority risk, not a proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The edge-general-position problem is a natural shortest-path packing condition, and its optimum alone does not determine how many feasible configurations or inclusion-maximal configurations exist. The full face and facet polynomials supply these quantities uniformly for every cycle. The exceptional purity of \(C_7\) (among all cycles with diameter at least three), contrasted with maximal triples at every other order from six onward, is a structural classification of when a greedy maximal configuration is guaranteed maximum. This answers an intrinsic enumeration and facet-purity question rather than repeating the previously known optimum.

## Closest literature and limits
The sources most directly comparable are the 2021 arXiv preprint *Edge General Position Problem* (arXiv:2105.04292), the full 2024 article *Extremal Edge General Position Sets in Some Graphs* (DOI:10.1007/s00373-024-02770-z), the vertex-only *General Position Polynomials* (DOI:10.1007/s00025-024-02133-3), and the full 2025 cactus scalar-extremal paper (DOI:10.1515/math-2025-0207). The 2025 *Graphs Whose Edge General Position Number is 4* preview did not permit full-text checking. The result concerns only \(C_n\), not general cacti or higher-order edge-position conditions; finite verification supports but does not substitute for the proof.
