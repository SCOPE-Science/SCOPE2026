# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness: PASS

The claim is the integral homotopy type and complete rational homology action of the unique eleven-point biplane block complex. All \(242\) nonempty faces and the exact design block overlaps are independently reconstructed. The \(110\)-pair element matching has precisely one critical vertex and \(21\) critical two-cells; exhaustive acyclicity checking includes every one of the \(715\) Hasse coverings. Forman's theorem implies the complete integral wedge. A second computation independently establishes the mod-two boundary ranks. The enumerated \(660\) block automorphisms exhaust the known full automorphism group and their full signed Lefschetz traces match the sum of two character-table irreducibles in every class. Character averaging yields the quotient and subgroup conclusions. Mathematical inputs are Forman's discrete Morse theorem, the known full biplane automorphism-group identification, and the published group character table. No numerical extrapolation or partial enumeration is substituted for proof.

## Originality: PASS on inspected available evidence

The exact primary design is classical, as is its automorphism group, so neither is claimed as novel. The published character table identifies irreducibles in the abstract but not the \(21\)-dimensional homology module of this complex. The accessible biplane embedding literature concerns realizations on surfaces, not the abstract simplicial union of eleven four-simplices. The 2015 boolean-representable-complex work offers general homotopy methods under a different set of hypotheses, not an exact classification of this example. Exact-name, construction-alias, out-neighborhood and representation searches, including the mathematical findings index, did not find a theorem implying this homotopy-and-character classification. The nearest tournament records involve either smaller tournaments or directed *flag* complexes and cannot be transferred without an additional theorem. Absence of a search result is not novelty proof; an unindexed calculation remains a risk.

## Value: PASS

A uniquely determined symmetric design with a \(660\)-element simple automorphism group is a natural object, not a parameter-selected slice. The complete homotopy calculation alone is useful as an integral reference, and the precise multiplicity-free rational decomposition is information not contained in the design incidence parameters, a Betti number, or the group character table alone. The rank-one fixed subspace for the small affine automorphism subgroup disappears under the full simple group, yielding a concrete rational-homology obstruction to treating the two symmetry quotients alike. This is a motivated exact group-action computation, not merely a face count.
