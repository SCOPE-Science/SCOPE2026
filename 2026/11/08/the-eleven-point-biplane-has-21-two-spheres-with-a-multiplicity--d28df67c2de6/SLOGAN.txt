The unique eleven-point \(2\)-\((11,5,2)\) biplane has a block complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{21}S^2\), yet its \(21\)-dimensional homology is not irreducible: the full \(\mathrm{PSL}(2,11)\) action splits multiplicity-free as a ten- and an eleven-dimensional rational representation. The full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic, while the natural order-\(55\) affine subgroup fixes a one-dimensional homology subspace.
