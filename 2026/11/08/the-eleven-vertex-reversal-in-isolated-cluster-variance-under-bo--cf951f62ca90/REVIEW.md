# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The isolated-vertex indicator calculation accounts for shared edge outcomes at adjacent endpoints and independence at nonadjacent endpoints. From the exact variance identity follow the star and double-star formulas. Monotonicity of \(k2^{-k}\) on integers \(k\ge2\) and AM–GM strictly separate the balanced double star from the star for every order at least eleven. The finite classification is a complete, exactly evaluated isomorphism-class enumeration with a leaf-deletion completeness argument. A checker independently enumerates edge states for small orders. Risk: the finite-tree census uses one canonical-tree generator, though its correctness is also explained combinatorially.

## Originality

PASS, qualified historical priority. The full-text 2021 Lanchier–La Salle article is about a typical cluster size and does not give an extremum of the total isolated-cluster-count variance. The accessible abstract and bibliographic information for Jevtić–Lanchier–La Salle (2020) concern cluster-size moments on selected graphs, not an all-trees extremal classification; its full article was not inspected, which remains a priority risk. published-finding corpus and general literature searches using the statistic and natural equivalent formulations did not reveal the exact first-reversal theorem. The underlying covariance formula is deliberately not presented as new. Search absence is not proof of priority.

## Value

PASS. Isolation fluctuations measure dispersion in residual singleton components after random edge failures. A standard expectation-only intuition could suggest the star remains extremal. Instead the star has a nontrivial, exact change of extremal status at the smallest possible order eleven, with a unique optimal alternative there and an all-larger-order strict counterfamily. Identifying a natural first-cutoff and its mechanism is useful independently of a still-open larger-order optimal-shape census.

## Limits

The exact globally minimizing tree is not classified above eleven vertices. The retention probability is fixed at \(1/2\). The full 2020 comparison article was not accessible for full-text reading. Historical priority and independent validation are open.
