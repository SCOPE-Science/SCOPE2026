At the smallest Pell–Wieferich prime, \(13\), the classical Pell numbers occupy exactly thirteen residue classes modulo \(169\), and every higher-power image is precisely their full lift, with \(13\)-adic Haar measure \(1/13\). Every attainable residue has exactly two index classes per least period, except the \(169\)-divisible residues, which have four.
