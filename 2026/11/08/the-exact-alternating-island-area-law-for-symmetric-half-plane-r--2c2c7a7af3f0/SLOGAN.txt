For the symmetric half-plane rotation, the canonical alternating two-cycle has an exactly computable maximal open island pair: two disks at irrational angles and two regular polygons with \(N=2q,q,q/2\) sides according to the reduced denominator modulo four at rational angles. Its total area has positive resonant spikes at every rational angle and is continuous exactly at irrational angles, with spike heights decaying quadratically in the polygon side count.
