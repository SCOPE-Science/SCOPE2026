The shifted \(q\)-central binomial product \(H_{n,k,j}(q)\) has reduced denominator exactly \([u]_q\), where \(u\) is the largest divisor of \(\gcd(n,j)\) coprime to \(k\). Hence polynomiality is equivalent to \(\operatorname{rad}(\gcd(n,j))\mid k\), correcting a published stronger criterion and giving infinitely many counterexamples to its proposed necessity.
