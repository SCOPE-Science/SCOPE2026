A Haar-random central two-plane section of the four-dimensional product of two unit disks has an explicit diameter CDF: \(1-\cos\alpha-\alpha\sin\alpha/2\), where \(\alpha=\arccos(8/s^2-1)\) for \(2\le s<2\sqrt2\). Surprisingly, exactly \(\pi/4\) of all planes yield the largest possible diameter \(2\sqrt2\).
