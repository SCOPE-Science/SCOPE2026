# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The theorem is stated for the intrinsic Euclidean metric on the affine permutation hyperplane. The complete irredundant facet system is independently established in the cited primary literature. The orthogonal norms of the two facet-normal classes and the exact path-Laplacian inverse are reconstructed. Nonnegativity of the Green matrix turns every putative ball's facet margins into an upper bound at the first prefix, attained by one parabolic deficit profile. Strict positivity in the first Green row proves a unique incenter. Explicit strict inequalities classify every contact facet. The antipodal increasing and decreasing permutations establish the unique circumcenter and show that only vertices contact. Computations verify rather than replace the general proof; small and boundary dimensions are covered.

## Originality

**PASS, provisional historical priority.** The thesis of Tamayo Jiménez already supplies exactly the input facet system and cube structure; the complete relevant sections of de Castro (arXiv:2609.06096v2) were compared, including the direct chain-poset realization that is an isometry. Neither gives the inball radius, unique center, contact-facet spectrum, or asymptotic radius ratio. The Davis–Sagan paper and directly relevant poset permutahedron sources do not establish a stronger metric theorem. Targeted mathematical-record queries for chain-poset, avoidance, Chebyshev center, inradius, Weyl-chamber, and discriminant formulations found no implication-dominating result. In particular, the classical regular permutahedron inradius applies to the full orbit polytope, not its chamber-intersection subpolytope; an explicit nontrivial change in radius is proved here. Unindexed literature remains a residual risk, not a proof of absolute priority.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** This supplies a complete, exact Euclidean inball classification for a canonical high-dimensional polytope family, not a numerical example or a restatement of its known facet inequalities. All \(d-1\) adjacent-difference facets are tangent while interior prefix facets cease to touch from \(d=4\), and the inball becomes bounded as dimension grows while the full permutahedral outer sphere persists. The resulting change from \(\Theta(\sqrt d)\) to \(\Theta(d^{3/2})\) aspect ratio exposes a concrete geometric separation invisible to cubical combinatorics or lattice equivalences.
