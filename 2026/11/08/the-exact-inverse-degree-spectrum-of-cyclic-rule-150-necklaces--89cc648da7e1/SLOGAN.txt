One-step Rule 150 inversion on binary necklaces has the exact degree spectrum \(\{1\}\) on rings not divisible by three, and \(\{0,2,4\}\) on rings divisible by three, with the degree determined by three residue parities and the output's primitive period. A Möbius–Burnside formula counts every degree class for all ring lengths, and almost every reachable necklace at lengths divisible by three has four distinct necklace predecessors.
