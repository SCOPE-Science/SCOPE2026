For every simplex of dimension \(d\ge3\), uniform barycentric truncation of all vertices strictly decreases its affine-invariant isotropic constant all the way to the rectified simplex, with an explicit dimension-dependent formula. Triangles are exceptional: their unique optimal cut is \(t=1/3\), yielding an affine-regular hexagon and an exact squared-isotropic-constant ratio \(5/6\).
