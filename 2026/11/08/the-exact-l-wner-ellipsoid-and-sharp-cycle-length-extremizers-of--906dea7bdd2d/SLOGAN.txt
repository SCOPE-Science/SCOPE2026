For the canonical symmetric-edge polytope of every connected cactus graph, the unique Löwner ellipsoid is the weighted graph-Laplacian ellipsoid with bridge weights \(1\) and cycle-edge weights \((k-1)/k\), and all signed edge vertices touch it. Its exact Euclidean volume is \(\kappa_{n-1}\sqrt{n\prod_C k_C((k_C-1)/k_C)^{k_C-1}}\); at fixed vertex and cycle counts it is minimized by all-triangle cycles and maximized by bridge-free cacti with cycle lengths as balanced as possible.
