For the exponent set \(\{3^i-1:0\le i<n\}\), the least modulus that distinguishes the length-\(2n\) multiset containing each element twice is exactly \((3^n-3^{t(n)})/2\), where \(t(n)\) is the largest exponent with \(3^{t(n)}\le2n-1\) and \(n-t(n)\) odd. A ternary coin-count argument proves the formula for every \(n\ge2\).
