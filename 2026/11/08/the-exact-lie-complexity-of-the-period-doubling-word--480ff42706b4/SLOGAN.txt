For the period-doubling fixed point \(0\mapsto01,\ 1\mapsto00\), exactly two length-\(n\) cyclic factor classes are fully represented if \(n\) is a power of two or three times a power of two, and none are fully represented at other lengths. An exact two-to-one substitution desubstitution gives \(L(2n)=L(n)\), reducing the entire classification to the odd lengths.
