For every fixed positive integer \(m\), the number of pairs \(1\leq a<b\leq X\) with \(a+b\mid mab\) is \(C_mX\log X+O_m(X)\), where \(C_m=(3\log2/\pi^2)\prod_{p^\alpha\parallel m}(1+\alpha(p-1)/(p+1))\). In the two classical Erdős–Graham divisibility graphs, the asymptotic edge counts have the exact ratio \(4/3\).
