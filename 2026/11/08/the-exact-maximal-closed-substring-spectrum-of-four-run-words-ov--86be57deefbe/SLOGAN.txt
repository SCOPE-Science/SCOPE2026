Four-run words over every alphabet admit an exact rational bivariate generating function for the number of occurrences of maximal closed substrings, with a complete trichotomy by repeated run letters. In the alternating case \(A^aB^bA^cB^d\), the number is \(5+\min(a,c)+\min(b,d)-\mathbf1_{c\le a}-\mathbf1_{b\le d}\).
