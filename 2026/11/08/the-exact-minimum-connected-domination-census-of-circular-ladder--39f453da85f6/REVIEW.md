# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. For every integer \(n\ge3\), the definition fixes the labeled prism, the closed-neighborhood domination condition, and induced connectivity. A connected set with at least one empty column has a consecutive occupied-column interval; three consecutive empty columns would contain an undominated vertex. One empty column without a double is impossible, and two empty columns force two double boundary columns, proving the lower bound \(n\) for \(n\ge4\). An entire row attains it. Equality allows only zero, one, or two empty columns; all three cases are classified in both directions and counted without overlap. The \(n=3\) rung exception is separately proved. An independently implemented direct graph verifier checked all nonempty subsets through \(n=10\) and 43,570 larger explicit constructions.

## Originality
PASS under best-knowledge literature comparison, with publication-priority uncertainty. The 2022 enumeration-algorithm paper establishes a general framework for inclusion-minimal connected dominating sets, but contains no matching exact count of the *minimum-cardinality* sets in \(C_n\square K_2\). Allagan and collaborators (2026) enumerate minimum *ordinary* dominating sets of the same prism, not connected dominating sets; their local-state method does not logically determine the new result. The 2023/2024 prism literature treats ordinary domination, and public cut-complex results for circular ladders address topology. Searches across related published research records, alternative phrases, maximum-leaf formulations and the accessible prior claims found no theorem implying the exact refined polynomial. The count alone resembles previously known integer sequences, so the claim rests on the full structural interpretation and proof. An unindexed prior result remains possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The circular ladder is a standard cubic graph and connected dominating sets are natural connected backbone configurations. Earlier knowledge of their minimum size does not determine how many such optimal backbones exist. The complete three-type classification yields exact labeled multiplicities for all orders, an informative sharp small-order transition, and a striking induced-cycle-versus-tree split: precisely two cyclic and \(2n^2\) tree-induced optima for \(n\ge5\). This is a natural infinite-family exact classification, rather than a routine numeric increment or a generic algorithm restatement.

## Closest literature and limits
The closest full-text sources are Abu-Khzam and collaborators (arXiv:2205.00086, 2022), studying enumeration of inclusion-minimal connected dominating sets in general graphs, and Allagan and collaborators (arXiv:2601.03488; DOI 10.1155/ijmm/1411005, 2026), studying all minimum ordinary dominating sets in prisms. Both provide motivation but neither supplies the present connectivity-constrained count. The 2023/2024 treatments of ordinary prism domination and publicly indexed graph-invariant databases are also distinguished. The theorem does not classify nonminimum connected dominating sets. Publication priority and independent verification are open.
