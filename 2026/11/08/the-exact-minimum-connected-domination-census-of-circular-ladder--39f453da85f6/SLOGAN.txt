For the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\), the number of minimum connected dominating vertex sets is exactly \(2n^2+2\) for every \(n\ge5\), with sharp exceptional counts \(3\) and \(30\) at \(n=3,4\). A complete three-type column classification refines the count to the polynomial \(2+2n(n-1)u+2nu^2\) and shows that exactly two optimal sets induce cycles while all others induce trees.
