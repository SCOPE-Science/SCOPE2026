For the diagonal integral Segre cubic over \(\mathbb Z_2\), the entire relative Hilbert scheme of planes is exactly fifteen copies of \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z_2\) and six copies of \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_4\). The twelve extra characteristic-two planes are all obstructed already modulo four by a nonzero symplectic cross term, rather than merely failing to reach the generic fiber.
