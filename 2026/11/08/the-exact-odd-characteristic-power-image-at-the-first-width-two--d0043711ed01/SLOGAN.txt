At the first unitriangular dimension where the \(p\)-th power map ceases to be surjective onto its verbal subgroup, a six-coordinate criterion describes every power for all odd primes \(p\) and all fields of characteristic \(p\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the image size is uniformly \(q^6-q^5+q^4+q^3-q^2\), refining the previously known width-two theorem and isolated small-field counts.
