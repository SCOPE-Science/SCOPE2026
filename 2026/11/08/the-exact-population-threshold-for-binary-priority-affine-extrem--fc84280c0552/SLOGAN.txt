For binary priorities on a complete conflict graph, affine rank payoffs maximize the potential-energy share only through six agents: a published witness defeats them at seven, and the explicit quadratic rank payoff \(f(r)=-r(r-1)/2\) defeats them for every \(N\ge8\). Its exact excess is \((N-2)(N-7)/[2N(13N^2-N-2)]\), completing the all-population failure threshold.
