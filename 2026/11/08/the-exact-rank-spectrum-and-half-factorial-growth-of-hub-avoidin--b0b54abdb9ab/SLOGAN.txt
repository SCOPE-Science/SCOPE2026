Hub-avoiding partial isometries of an \(n\)-rim wheel have an exact rank-by-rank enumeration determined by the induced path runs of rim subsets. Their total number has half-factorial logarithmic growth, \(\log|\mathcal M_n|\sim \tfrac12n\log n\), while the fraction extendible to wheel automorphisms is \(\exp(-(\tfrac12+o(1))n\log n)\).
