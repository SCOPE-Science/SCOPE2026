For every \(n\ge7\), all \((a,b)\)-regular subsets of the quartic circulant \(C_n(1,3)\) arise from residue classes modulo \(2,3,5,8\); an exact formula enumerates every labelled realization for every parameter pair. Their total number never exceeds \(106\), and equals \(106\) precisely when \(120\mid n\).
