For every odd squarefree necklace length \(n>1\), the polynomial \(M_n\) takes exactly the even residues modulo every \(2^k\), with an exact two-level fiber law controlled solely by \(2+\nu_2(\varphi(n))\). Its even input branch is an isometry, each odd class modulo four is an isometric branch onto the deeper ideal, and odd nonsquarefree lengths cannot have the same image.
