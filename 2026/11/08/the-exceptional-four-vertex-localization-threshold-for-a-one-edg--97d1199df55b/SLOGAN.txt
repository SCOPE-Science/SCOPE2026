For the mean-zero real Sobolev embedding on a complete metric graph with one edge of length \(h\) and all other edges unit length, every sharp evaluation extremizer localizes at an explicitly determined edge-interior point. Shortening a single edge exhibits a dimension-four-only transition at \(h=\sqrt5-2\): the maximum moves from the unaffected edge to all four incident mixed edges, whereas in dimensions at least five it never leaves unaffected edges until \(h=1\); lengthening instead selects the defective edge.
