Removing all 27 geometric lines from the binary Fermat cubic produces a smooth affine, geometrically factorial surface whose Picard group vanishes over every finite extension; its groups of nonconstant units have ranks 11 or 20 according to extension parity. The complete compactly supported Frobenius spectrum is \((1)^{32},(-1)^{32}\) in degree two, \((2)^{11},(-2)^9\) in degree three and \((4)\) in degree four; the previously studied point-free cases over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_4\) and \(\mathbb F_{16}\) follow from this refinement.
