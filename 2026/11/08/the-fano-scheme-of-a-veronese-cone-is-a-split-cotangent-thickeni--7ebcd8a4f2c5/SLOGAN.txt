The full Fano scheme of lines on the projective cone over the quadratic Veronese plane is the split square-zero thickening of \(\mathbb P^2\) by its twisted cotangent bundle \(\Omega^1_{\mathbb P^2}(2)\), in every characteristic. Its Plücker Hilbert polynomial is \(6n^2+11n+4\), and its nontrivial global gluing distinguishes it from a product with a fat point.
