For two arclength-uniform points on a smooth strictly convex plane curve, the fifth short-Euclidean-distance coefficient is \(L^{-2}(3\int\kappa^4/320-\int(\kappa')^2/360)\), so it measures curvature variation beyond the cubic bending-energy term. At fixed perimeter, fixed curvature bounds and identical lower coefficients, an explicit family of strictly convex ovals makes this fifth coefficient tend to \(-\infty\).
