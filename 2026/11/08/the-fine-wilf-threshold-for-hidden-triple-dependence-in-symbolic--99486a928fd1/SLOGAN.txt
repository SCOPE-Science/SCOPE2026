Symbolic returns to a random initial \(k\)-block in a uniform full shift are exactly pairwise independent at all return times at least \(k\), but three returns with gaps \(a,b\) have joint probability \(q^{-3k+\max(0,k-a-b+\gcd(a,b))}\). The sharp appearance of three-way dependence is the Fine–Wilf threshold \(k>a+b-\gcd(a,b)\).
