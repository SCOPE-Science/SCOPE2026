The sharp universal minimum nearly \(S^*\)-invariance defect \(2n+1\) is attained for every perturbation rank \(n\geq1\) by an explicit rank-\(n\) perturbation of a monomial truncated Toeplitz operator on \(K_{z^{5n+6}}\). The kernel is a finite-dimensional coordinate graph whose backward-shift quotient has exactly \(2n+1\) independent monomial directions.
