On an even quantum ring with one absorbing site, weak positive next-nearest-neighbor hopping preserves complete eventual capture from the antipode, but the first finite-size coupling resonance \(J_2/J_1=1/[4\cos^2(\pi/n)]\) leaves exactly \(8/(3n)\) of the excitation permanently uncaptured. Across all even ring sizes, the exceptional ratios are countable and dense above the sharp universal boundary \(1/4\), while every fixed ring has only finitely many.
