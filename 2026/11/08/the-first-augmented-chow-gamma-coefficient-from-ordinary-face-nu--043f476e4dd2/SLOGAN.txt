For the right and left augmented Chow polynomials of every convex polytope, the first gamma coefficient is an explicit linear function of its ordinary face numbers, and the difference between the two coefficients equals facets minus vertices. This proves sharp simplex minimality of the first coefficient in all dimensions and coefficientwise simplex minimality of the entire gamma vector through dimension four, with strict positive-degree inequalities for non-simplices in dimensions two through four.
