# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
PASS. Every one of the \(n^{2n}\) ordered pairs of transition tables for each \(1\le n\le4\) is covered; simultaneous conjugacy orbits partition this entire set, and Burnside's formula supplies an independent count of the representatives. Complete binary atomic two-types are used in joint counting refinement, so distinct profile histograms furnish genuine \(C^2\) distinctions. The five-point witness satisfies equality of profiles and has no simultaneous conjugating permutation; its nonisomorphism also follows symbolically from the centralizer of a full cycle. This is finite exhaustive proof, not experimental extrapolation.

## Originality
PASS, provisional. Harrison's enumeration of automata types is known and not claimed as original. Kiefer–Schweitzer–Selman's general \(C^2\) classification provides an equivalence mechanism but the inspected material does not explicitly give this restricted sharp threshold. Prior findings confined to commuting permutations are logically insufficient to settle all noncommuting and noninvertible pairs at four points. Searches of published research records under two-letter automata, two binary functional relations, counting refinement, and exact 2836-class equivalence yielded no directly covering claim. Unindexed specialist calculations remain a residual historical-priority risk.

## Value
PASS. The theorem gives a sharp *first failure* boundary of a natural finite-model class: unpointed two-letter transition systems with all (possibly singular and noncommuting) transition functions. The lower bound certifies the equivalence of logical and isomorphism classification for a complete set of 2,836 four-state types, not merely permutations or hand-chosen examples. It explains that the first loss of counting-logic information can already occur in a simple transitive cyclic five-state system. It is a substantive natural finite cutoff, without pretending to provide a larger infinite structural classification.

## Sources and limitations
Harrison (1965), DOI:10.4153/CJM-1965-010-9, supplies the old enumeration. Kiefer, Schweitzer, Selman (2015), arXiv:1503.08792v1, supplies the binary-relational \(C^2\) setting. Primary text inspection was available for the latter; the former's full text remained uninspected. No independent audit or exhaustive priority certification has occurred.
