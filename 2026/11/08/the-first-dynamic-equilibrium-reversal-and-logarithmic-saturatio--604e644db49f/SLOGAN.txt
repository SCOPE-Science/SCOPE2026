For two-row ladder adsorption, the first blockade range at which the equilibrium packing density overtakes the dynamic jamming density is exactly \(b=10\): all nine preceding integer ranges favor dynamics, including a narrowly separated case at \(b=9\). The equilibrium normalized density approaches one at the slow quantified rate \(1-S_b\sim1/\log b\), with the sharper approximation \(S_b=W(2b)/(1+W(2b))+O(\log b/b)\).
