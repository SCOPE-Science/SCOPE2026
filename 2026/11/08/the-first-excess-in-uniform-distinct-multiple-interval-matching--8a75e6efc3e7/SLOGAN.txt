The shortest length that guarantees distinct multiples of \(1,\ldots,n\) in *every* integer interval equals \(2n-2\) for \(2\le n\le16\), but first exceeds this at \(n=17\), where it is exactly \(33\). Among the full \(12{,}252{,}240\)-residue period, exactly 32 starts obstruct a length-32 matching, and one has an explicit six-to-five Hall deficiency.
