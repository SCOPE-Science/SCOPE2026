# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** For every admissible geometric parameter the two degree-two covers have disjoint simple branch sets; the normalization is connected and has genus five by Riemann–Hurwitz. The prime-to-characteristic isogeny has degree a power of two and hence preserves the group schemes killed by each of the odd primes. Both elliptic factors are supersingular for primes congruent to three modulo four. A genus-three hyperelliptic Hasse–Witt matrix is zero exactly when three explicitly specified coefficient polynomials vanish. Two independent polynomial-arithmetic constructions give the exact monic greatest common divisors in characteristics seven, twenty-three, and thirty-one, including the two extra roots in characteristic thirty-one. Separate exhaustive full-field enumeration proves the displayed models' maximality classification over the quadratic extension; another exact enumeration tests all branch-set projectivities to distinguish quotient isomorphism types. No inference about an infinite family rests on finite sampling: the algebraic-closure claim rests on the polynomial gcds.

## Originality

**PASS with residual priority risk.** Kudo and Moriya established the genus-five construction and plane-sextic realizations; Ohashi and coauthors studied genus-three Howe superspeciality and counts; later work supplies general existence of superspecial genus-five examples. Their accessible full-text theorems and inspected tables do not imply the three-prime superspecial parameter gcds in this specific one-parameter fiber-product pencil, the complete 29-parameter quadratic-extension maximality classification, or the exact two quotient isomorphism types. The characteristic-seven value and familiar parameter \(a=-1\) are background, not standalone new claims. A cited 2024 presentation on enumerating superspecial genus-five Howe curves could overlap with the characteristic-thirty-one formulas, but its full content was unavailable to inspect, so historical uniqueness is not certified. Searching mathematical records and open literature gives no positive evidence of coverage; lack of a result is not itself a novelty proof.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The locus at which an entire genus-five Jacobian becomes superspecial is an intrinsic, strongly constrained arithmetic invariant of a natural Klein-four genus-five pencil. Characteristic thirty-one, unlike the two earlier primes in the same congruence class, admits two extra reciprocal parameters. Exact maximality over the quadratic extension and a distinguishing branch-automorphism comparison give arithmetic and geometric content beyond re-evaluating a standard single example. The classification is complete within the precisely stated natural three-prime scope, not an assertion of a universal pattern.
