In the genus-five elliptic–elliptic Howe pencil \(y^2=x^4-1\), \(z^2=x^4-a\), characteristic \(31\) is the first prime \(p\equiv7\pmod8\) with superspecial parameters beyond \(a=-1\): the two extra values are \(14\) and \(20\). These and only these three prime-field coefficients give maximal curves over \(\mathbb F_{31^2}\), each with \(1272\) rational points; their genus-three quotients realize two distinct geometric isomorphism types.
