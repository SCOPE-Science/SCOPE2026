For denominator degree five in exponential Padé approximation, every coefficient of \(1/q_{k,5}\) is strictly positive for all \(0\le k\le65\), with no restriction on the coefficient index. Since the already-known coefficient \(c_{38}(66,5)\) is negative, \(66\) is the exact first failing numerator degree; the new universal lower range is established by rational root-disk and infinite-tail certificates.
