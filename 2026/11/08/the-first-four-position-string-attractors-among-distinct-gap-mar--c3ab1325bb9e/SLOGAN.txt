Within binary marker words \(1\,0^{g_1}1\cdots0^{g_k}1\) having pairwise distinct positive gap lengths, four attractor positions first become necessary at length \(18\). Exactly two such shortest words attain that threshold, with gap vectors \((1,3,2,7)\) and \((7,2,3,1)\), and explicit four-position witnesses and a six-factor lower obstruction certify optimality.
