The ordinary matching complex of the sixteen-vertex Möbius ladder has the exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^2 S^4\vee\bigvee^4 S^5\), making it the first Möbius ladder whose ordinary matching homology occupies two dimensions. An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching and primitive integer boundary matrices certify the sphere wedge, not just field Betti numbers.
