The terminal size generating polynomial of uniform feasible-edge greedy matching is real-rooted for every tree with at most twelve vertices, but exactly six thirteen-vertex tree types lose real-rootedness. A symmetric triple-fork tree witnesses the transition with \(P(z)=z^3(180+1852z+5149z^2+4339z^3)/11520\), whose nonzero factor has negative discriminant despite strictly log-concave coefficients.
