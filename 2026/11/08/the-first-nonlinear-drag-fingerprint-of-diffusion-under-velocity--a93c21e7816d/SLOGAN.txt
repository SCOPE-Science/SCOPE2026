In a velocity-reset run-and-tumble particle whose telegraph force persists through resets, the first nonlinear-drag contribution to spatial diffusivity is exactly \(-6b\Sigma^4/(\lambda r^5)\) when \(g(v)=av+bv^3+O(v^5)\). Thus tangent and hyperbolic-tangent drag share the familiar \(r^{-2}\) leading law but differ by \(4\Sigma^4/(\lambda r^5)+O(r^{-6})\), with shear thinning yielding greater diffusivity at sufficiently high reset rate.
