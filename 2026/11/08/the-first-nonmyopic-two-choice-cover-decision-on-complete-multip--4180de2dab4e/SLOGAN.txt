In a with-replacement two-choice cover walk, preferring an unvisited offered neighbor is optimal throughout every complete multipartite graph of order at most eight, but can be strictly wrong on \(K_{1,2,6}\). An exact rational certificate identifies that graph as the sole nine-vertex counterexample type, with a positive post-decision time penalty of \(3210462360632365129/1327093575050907456507\).
