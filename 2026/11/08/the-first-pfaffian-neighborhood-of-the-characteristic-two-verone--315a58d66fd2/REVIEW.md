# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The signed principal Pfaffian kernel identity is algebraic over any ring; in characteristic two it yields the precise coefficient differential \(dF=\sum_i w_i^2 da_i+\sum_{i<j}w_iw_j db_{ij}\). Consequently, all generators of the full Jacobian ideal are exactly the products generating \(I^2\). The rank-normal form gives a local regular parameter system \((u,v,s)\) along the dense corank-three stratum and the transverse length-four quotient. The irreducibility proof uses the codimension-three rank-drop complement, not an unproved numerical extrapolation. Exact sparse-polynomial verification checked orders three, five, seven.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk

The closest primary sources are Ballico (1990), Shimada (2006) and Kaji (2015). Ballico concerns general characteristic-two conormal exceptions; Shimada analyzes Hessian critical schemes and dual singularities under higher-jet hypotheses; Kaji proves higher Gauss-map generic finiteness and characteristic-zero degree formulas. The present coefficient-space ideal identity \(I_{\mathrm{Jac}}=I_{\mathrm{Pf}}^2\) and corank-three transverse length four are not consequences stated or computed in those inspected arguments. A search by alternate half-discriminant, Pfaffian, singular-scheme and Veronese-dual formulations found no equivalent statement, but failed searches are not uniqueness proofs. Specialized historic Pfaffian literature remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This determines the full nonreduced Jacobian scheme of a natural dual variety, uniformly in dimension and not merely set-theoretically. The first-order thickening is mathematically meaningful even though the reduced support is already prescribed by polar rank: it supplies the exact transverse length and the complete sheaf of first-order infinitesimal directions. The comparison with reduced singular centers is particularly consequential in characteristic two, where the relevant coefficient derivatives vanish to second order.
