For a cyclic automaton with one merge letter \(0\mapsto r\) and \(n>2r\), its first \(r\) rank reductions each have a unique shortest word, but the next reduction first costs \(3r+1\) letters and has exactly \(2^{r-1}\) shortest words. A co-moving-hole argument turns the optimal-word language into the maximum independent sets of a three-vertex path and \(r-1\) isolated edges.
