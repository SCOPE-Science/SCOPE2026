# Scientific review: The first reentrant azimuthal phase diagram for antipodal spherical caps

## Correctness — PASS
For every cap aperture the restriction operator is diagonal in azimuthal harmonics. The normalized Legendre-square integrals produce the five exact degree-four eigenvalue polynomials. Symbolic recomputation checks all their differences, and the displayed exact factorizations give a full sign proof on the continuous interval. Uniqueness of the positive cubic transition root and the algebraic quadratic roots give four sharp boundaries, with equality spaces resolved at each boundary. Independent exact computations for degrees zero through three establish the first reentrance degree. The separate checker passed 70 exact assertions; finite samples are corroboration, not an infinite-domain proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
Simons and Dahlen (2006) give the general polar-cap Slepian matrix and an azimuthal reduction (Section 4.2, Eq. (66)). Thus the eigenvalue-integral formula is **not** new. The present distinct claim is the complete ordered degree-four upper spectral envelope, four exact transition apertures, triple crossing, equality sectors, and first degree where winning azimuthal order increases. Plattner, Seibert and Michel (2021), arXiv:2103.14650, develop a broad spherical Slepian framework. Published-record comparisons found half-energy multiplicity and unrelated degree-two fourth-norm results but no dominating exact transition statement. Full text of the 2021 work could not be inspected, and an unindexed classical special-function calculation could anticipate the result. Those limitations leave priority risk, not proof of absence.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The sharp spatial concentration on a shrinking two-cap region is a natural eigenvalue optimization in spherical harmonic analysis. Its optimizer need not change monotonically in azimuthal order: the first counterexample is completely classified and includes a triple spectral degeneracy. The result is a finite-degree but continuous-parameter structural classification, not an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limitations and evidence
The result does not address general regions, multiple degrees, or degrees above four. Its proof is exact symbolic and analytical; no independent validation is claimed.
