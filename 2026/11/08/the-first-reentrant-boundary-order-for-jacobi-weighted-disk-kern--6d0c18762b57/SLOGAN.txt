For Jacobi radial disk weights \(|z|^{2(t-1)}(1-|z|^2)^m\), the open-disk Bergman-kernel zero count decreases from one to zero at a single sharp threshold for each boundary order \(m=0,1,2,3\). The exact new third-order threshold is \(\sqrt{2+\sqrt{10}/2}-1\); order four is the first integer boundary order admitting zero reentry, witnessed by zero counts \(1,0,2\) at \(t=1/2,1,4\).
