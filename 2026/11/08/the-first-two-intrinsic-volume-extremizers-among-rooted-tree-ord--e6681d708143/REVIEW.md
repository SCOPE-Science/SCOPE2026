# Scientific review: rooted-tree order-polytope intrinsic volumes

## Correctness: PASS

The proposed claim has one explicit domain: rooted trees on \(n\ge4\) vertices, order polytopes in standard coordinate Euclidean space, and conventional intrinsic volumes. The proof checks the full facet list (root lower, leaf upper, all cover edges), the rooted-tree hook-volume formula and the \(\sqrt2\) induced-metric factor for an edge-equality facet. The exact areas of the four structural classes follow by deletion/contraction counting. Kubota's formula and strict convex-body inclusion certify every intrinsic-volume comparison, including uniqueness. The two poset-extension arguments correctly produce the fork and broom bounds. Finite verification reconstructed 1,803 rooted-tree area profiles through dimension ten and 59 original convex hulls through dimension six; these are corroboration, not a substitute for the infinite argument.

## Originality: PASS, with historical-priority risk

The relevant premises—facet and volume descriptions of order polytopes—are known from Stanley (1986). Ahmad, Fourier and Joswig (arXiv:2309.01626, first posted 2023-09-04) classify face-vector inequalities for maximal ranked posets; their inspected full-text sections do not treat intrinsic Euclidean volumes, rooted-tree four-level extremizers, or the presented area gaps. Von Bell et al. (arXiv:2102.11306, first posted 2021-02-22) work on generalized snake poset triangulations and volume extrema, and its full 39-page arXiv PDF was inspected, including the introduction, Section 3 and the main volume comparison Theorem 3.10. Its generalized-snake-poset volume conclusions do not cover Euclidean intrinsic-volume extremizers for rooted trees. Targeted published-finding corpus searches and the accessible current-week comparison ledger did not identify an implication covering the theorem. Related records on rooted-star poset associahedra, symmetric-edge graph polytopes, and tree graphical-zonotope surface areas concern different geometric realizations and do not imply these order-polytope formulas. An undiscovered equivalent convex-body/poset statement remains possible.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The result is a natural complete first-and-second extremal classification for every intrinsic volume of an infinite family of canonical poset polytopes. It supplies an independent exact boundary-area evaluation for each rooted tree, quantitative gaps at both ends, and a dimensionally surprising contrast between factorial decay of the lower gap and a positive upper-gap limit. The all-intrinsic-volume simultaneous ordering and strict equality classification are structurally more informative than checking a few tree profiles or re-expressing a hook-length calculation. No broader theorem for arbitrary posets or other embeddings is claimed.

## Limitations and remaining comparison risk

The combinatorial volume/hook formula and strict intrinsic-volume monotonicity are classical inputs, not presented as discoveries. Finite enumeration cannot prove the all-dimensional theorem. The earliest verified contemporary lead source is the generalized-snake-poset preprint first posted 2021-02-22; Stanley (1986) is a foundational antecedent rather than the lead source. Historical priority is provisional under alternate order-cone and intrinsic-volume formulations, even after inspection of the generalized-snake-poset article.
