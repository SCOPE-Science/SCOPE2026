For rooted-tree order polytopes, the rooted path and fork are the unique smallest and second-smallest examples for every intrinsic volume, while the rooted subdivided-spoke star and rooted star are the unique second-largest and largest. Exact Euclidean boundary-area formulas show a factorially small lower gap but an upper gap tending to \(1+1/\sqrt2\), and a deletion/contraction hook formula computes the boundary area of every rooted tree.
