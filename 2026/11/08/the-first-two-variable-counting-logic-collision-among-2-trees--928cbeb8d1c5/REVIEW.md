# Scientific review: the eight-vertex 2-tree counting-logic obstruction

**Correctness (PASS).** The two witnesses are obtained by legal 2-tree ear attachments from the same diamond-shaped core. Their degree partition has the identical equitable quotient, proving color-refinement indistinguishability at *every* round and hence unrestricted \(C^2\)-equivalence. The degree-two neighborhoods give an explicit isomorphism obstruction, and the outerplanarity contrast follows from a displayed \(K_{2,3}\) subgraph versus a displayed outer Hamiltonian cycle with noncrossing chords. For minimality and uniqueness, the recurrence adding an ear to every edge is exhaustive by the deletion property of 2-trees. The supplied standard-library checker uses exact isomorphism tests and shared-color refinement, enumerating \(1,1,2,5,12,39\) isomorphism types in orders \(3\) through \(8\), respectively, and \(1,1,2,5,12,38\) \(C^2\)-classes. The six type counts agree with OEIS A054581, independently checking the enumeration totals. Finite enumeration is not asserted to prove anything at larger orders.

**Originality (PASS, with priority risk).** The closest primary sources cover: general color-refinement and \(C^2\) theory (Arvind et al., arXiv:1502.01255); 2-tree degree-sequence characterization (Bose et al., arXiv:cs/0605011); and enumeration of 2-trees (Labelle et al., arXiv:math/0312424; OEIS A054581). The general recognition algorithms do not state the first **class-relative** collision, and the enumeration table counts isomorphism types, not \(C^2\) equivalence classes. Known 2-tree degree-sequence nonuniqueness does not by itself yield identical color-refinement behavior. Earlier related class-specific classifications for split graphs, cactus graphs and tree line graphs involve different graph families and/or global amenability, and do not imply the unique doubleton of the 2-tree census. Published-index semantic queries for 2-trees, color refinement, equitability, fractional isomorphism, and the eight-vertex degree profile did not expose a covering claim. The search is bounded: unindexed graph-class tables and historical 2-tree algorithm literature remain possible priority risks.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** This is the exact first obstruction for unrestricted two-variable counting logic in the natural maximal treewidth-two class, not an arbitrary chosen graph order. The result completely describes the first failure layer, and the unique pair has a structural consequence independent of the finite census: outerplanarity cannot be defined in \(C^2\) even when the universe of possible structures is restricted to 2-trees. The example is minimal within that class and demonstrates how identical stable equitable partitions can conceal a minor obstruction. The exact threshold and logical nondefinability provide useful calibration for graph identification on bounded-treewidth classes.

**Limitations.** No claim is made about larger orders, exact \(C^2\) classification beyond eight vertices, or identification relative to all simple graphs. The novelty comparison is bounded and therefore not proof of historical uniqueness. The finite enumerator and the proof have not undergone independent mathematical audit.
