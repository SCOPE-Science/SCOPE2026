At shortest-path scale two, every five-column clique-fiber cycle \(K_m\square C_5\), \(m\ge3\), has Vietoris--Rips homotopy type \(\bigvee^{m-1}S^2\). The proof collapses a common core to a circle and attaches full row simplices along five-triangle Möbius bands, complementing the previously known six-column threshold.
