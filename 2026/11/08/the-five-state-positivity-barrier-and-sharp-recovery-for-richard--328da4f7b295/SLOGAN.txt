Second-order Richardson extrapolation of backward Euler preserves mass on every directed Markov cycle, but it preserves all transition probabilities for every positive step only on cycles of at most four states. Five states are the first sharp failure: a negative four-jump entry at arbitrarily small steps becomes nonnegative precisely past a unique algebraic threshold \(h_*\approx0.297835688\), while every finite cycle recovers positivity for sufficiently large steps.
