In Masuda's well-mixed copy/oppose voter model, the stationary congregator share converges to \(\operatorname{Beta}(r,r)\) with any fixed \(r\ge1\) contrarians, while their opinion-one count given share \(u\) tends to \(\operatorname{Binomial}(r,1-u)\). An exact all-finite-size covariance matrix bridges the already known one-contrarian uniform law with every fixed finite contrarian population.
