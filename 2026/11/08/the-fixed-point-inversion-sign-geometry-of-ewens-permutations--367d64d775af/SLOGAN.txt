Under Ewens sampling, a fixed point in the center of a permutation can have positive covariance with the inversion count when \(0<\theta<1\), even though the *total number* of fixed points always has strictly negative covariance with inversions. An exact position-resolved formula identifies every sign change, the \(\sqrt n\)-scale positive window and its finer near-uniform crossover; the complete mixed covariance with each inversion indicator has an additional gap-based transition.
