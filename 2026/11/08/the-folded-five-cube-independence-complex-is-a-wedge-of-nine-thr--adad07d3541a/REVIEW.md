# Scientific review of the folded five-cube independence complex

## Correctness — PASS
The claim fixes the five-regular, rather than ten-regular, Clebsch graph by its complete Cayley adjacency. The independent-set universe has 392 nonempty simplices, independently enumerated in two ways. Exactly 191 pairwise disjoint Morse pairs leave one critical zero-cell and nine critical three-cells; an exhaustive topological sort of all 1200 oriented Hasse covers verifies acyclicity. Forman's theorem supplies the homotopy CW model, and the absence of attaching cells in intermediate dimensions gives the nine-sphere wedge over the integers. Mod-two chain-rank and Euler checks corroborate the conclusion.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence
Barmak (arXiv:1007.0418, Theorems 3.4–3.5) proves a general suspension property for triangle-free graph independence complexes but no exact Clebsch dimension or rank. Bousquet-Mélou–Linusson–Nevo (arXiv:math/0701890) study open and cylindrical square-grid families and do not cover the antipodally augmented cube. A published ordinary four-cube independence result has rank seven, but adding antipodal edges changes the complex. The published neighborhood and matching complex calculations for the identical Clebsch graph are different simplicial constructions and do not imply this nine-sphere statement. Targeted equivalence and database comparisons identified no covering theorem. Residual risk: equivalent unindexed work and general results not found by the stated searches.

## Value — PASS
The five-regular Clebsch graph is the canonical folded five-cube, and the first nontrivial antipodal augmentation of the four-cube having this strongly regular structure. The complete integral homotopy invariant distinguishes its independence complex from that of the ordinary four-cube: the third homology rank increases from seven to nine, a change not determined by Barmak's general suspension theorem, graph spectra, or mere independent-set enumeration. An exact small but natural classification is useful as a benchmark for folded-hypercube topology.

## Limits
Only this graph and ordinary independence complexes are covered; no family-wide or equivariant assertion is made. Publication priority and independent validation remain open.
