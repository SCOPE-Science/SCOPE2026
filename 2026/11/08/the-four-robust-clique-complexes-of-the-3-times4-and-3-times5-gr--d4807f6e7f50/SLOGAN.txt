The four-robust clique complexes of the \(3\times4\) and \(3\times5\) rectangular grids have exact integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^{20}S^5\) and \(\bigvee^{56}S^5\). Exhaustive acyclic matchings settle these two finite corner-gluing cases of the recently proposed sphere-wedge conjecture, without asserting an infinite-family result.
