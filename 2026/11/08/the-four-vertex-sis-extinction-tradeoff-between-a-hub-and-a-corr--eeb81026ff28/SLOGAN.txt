Among the two four-vertex trees, a Markovian SIS outbreak lasts longer on average on the hub-shaped star than on the corridor for every positive transmission rate and every uniformly sampled nonempty initial count. Yet a corridor is more likely to remain infected at sufficiently short deadlines when the initial count is one, three, or four; the all-rate, sharp four-vertex contrast shows why mean persistence and deadline persistence need not order networks alike.
