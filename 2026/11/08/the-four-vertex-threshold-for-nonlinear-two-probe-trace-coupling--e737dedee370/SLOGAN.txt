For two fully uniform Rademacher trace probes, a fixed coordinate sign flip achieves the globally best coupling in every symmetric matrix dimension at most three, but fails for the complete graph in every dimension at least four. On the unweighted four-vertex complete graph, the exact minimum variances are \(12\) for independent probes, \(8\) for the best fixed flip, and \(6\) for globally coupled probes; the complete-graph ratio approaches a specified reflected-chi-square quantile integral of approximately \(0.5606716095\).
