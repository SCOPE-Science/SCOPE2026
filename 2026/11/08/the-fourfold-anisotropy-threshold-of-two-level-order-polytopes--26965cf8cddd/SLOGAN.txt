For the uniform-volume covariance of the order polytope of two comparable antichains of sizes \(2\le p\le q\), the least-variance direction switches from a between-level mode to within-small-level contrasts exactly at \(q=p+2\). Its exact covariance condition number is strictly below \(4\) for \(q-p\le1\), strictly above \(4\) for \(q-p\ge2\), and never equals \(4\).
