# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The block decomposition has explicit counts: internal inversions \(2(I_q-B)+A(q-A)\), cross inversions \(Aq\), and the two correction variables are provably even. This yields the unrestricted modulo-four congruence. Zolotarev's identity and character orthogonality give the exact polynomial value. The argument applies to all odd moduli, including repeated prime factors. Direct enumerations on 3,994 units are supporting checks, not the basis of the proof.

## Originality

PASS, qualified by residual historical-priority risk. The primary paper, Chen--Dunn--Hewett--Silas (2014/2015), includes Conjecture 2.1, but its Proposition 2.2 establishes only the value at \(-1\) for twice-odd moduli; Proposition 2.4 requires an odd modulus, and Proposition 2.5 treats different parameter conditions. Neither states or implies the new exact fourth-root evaluation for all twice-odd moduli. Searches for inverse-permutation parity modulo four, Jacobi-symbol refinements, and fourth-root inversion-polynomial values identified no covering theorem. The related established statement is Zolotarev's theorem for odd moduli, which does not itself give the nonstandard block congruence at modulus \(2q\).
