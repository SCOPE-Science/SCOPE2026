For every odd \(q\ge3\), the inversion polynomial of multiplication permutations modulo \(2q\) satisfies \(F_{2q}(i)=\varphi(q)\) if \(q\) is square and zero otherwise. The exact congruence \(I(a,2q)\equiv2I(a,q)\pmod4\) explains the value and completely determines the modulo-four distribution of the inversion numbers.
