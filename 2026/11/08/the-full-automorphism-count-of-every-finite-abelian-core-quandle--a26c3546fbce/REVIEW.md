# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof is valid for any finite additive abelian group. Normalizing an automorphism to fix zero and invoking the previously known core identity \(f(x+2y)=f(x)+2f(y)\) shows its restriction to \(2G\) is additive and invertible. Each other coset is uniquely controlled by its representative image, with the necessary and sufficient doubling condition. The converse verifies preservation of the original quandle operation, rather than only a necessary condition. The induced permutation of \(G/2G\) must intertwine doubling into \(2G/4G\), whose fibers have the calculated sizes. Every permitted coset permutation lifts in exactly \(2^t\) ways on each nonzero coset, giving the claimed factorial and power factors. Exhaustive direct checks on seven groups of order at most eight and independent constructor checks on four further instances corroborate the symbolic proof. Ordinary classification of automorphisms of finite abelian groups evaluates the remaining factor.

## Originality — qualified PASS

The closest pre-existing mathematical ingredient is Joyce's 1979 normalized core-map identity, used there for reconstructing finite abelian groups from their quandles; this identity is fully credited. Bardakov–Nasybullov–Singh (2017) determines inner automorphism groups for even Takasaki quandles and the full automorphism group of a single order-four dihedral quandle. Bardakov–Sangare (2026) explicitly asks in Question 3.5 about automorphism groups of products involving even dihedral moduli, while its centralizer method requires odd moduli. A previously recorded result already covers the homogeneous \(C_4^r\) family and is acknowledged as covered. No inspected source states the unrestricted factorial counting law or the complete coset-lifting classification for mixed even elementary divisors, and public mathematical-record searches found no dominating equivalent. Older unpublished classifications or an implicit formulation via extension automorphisms remain genuine priority risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The formula gives the complete automorphism count for every finite abelian core quandle, including every product of two dihedral quandles with even factors: a natural explicit problem left open in 2026. The new factorial factor distinguishes cyclic factors of order two from higher 2-primary factors; it is not just a substitution into the earlier homogeneous order-four result. The theorem also parameterizes every automorphism and proves a sharp group-structural fiber-lift count. This provides a complete natural classification across a large family of finite algebraic structures used in knot-theoretic quandle theory.

## Limitations and nearest literature

The theorem does not address nonabelian group cores, general Alexander quandles, or a uniform abstract semidirect-product decomposition. The key affine equivariance identity is earlier work and is not a new theorem. Exact historical priority is less certain than the correctness of the counting proof because alternative descriptions of automorphisms of extensions may exist. The numerical checker covers finite test groups and cannot establish the universal result by itself.
