For Selin's explicit one-hole non-normal toric surface \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C[u,uv,v^2,v^3]\), the full automorphism group is exactly the group of polynomial-plane automorphisms fixing the normalization origin and preserving the cotangent line \(\mathbb C\bar u\). This yields a split first-derivative quotient by the lower-triangular Borel group; the full group is connected and acts transitively away from the singular point.
