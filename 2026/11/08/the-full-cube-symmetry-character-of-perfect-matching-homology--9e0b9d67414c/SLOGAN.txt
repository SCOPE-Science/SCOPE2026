The full 48-element cube symmetry group acts on rational first homology of the cube's perfect-matching complex as the direct sum of the coordinate-permutation sign line and the natural signed three-dimensional coordinate representation: \(H_1\cong\varepsilon_{\mathrm{perm}}\oplus V_{\mathrm{signed}}\). The underlying four-circle homotopy type was previously known; this calculation determines its complete rational symmetry character, including a central-inversion trace of \(-2\).
