# Verification scope

The exact verifier `verify_cube_perfect_matching.py` requires only Python 3 with the standard library. Execute `python3 verify_cube_perfect_matching.py` from the directory containing this file; compare all stdout against `verification_output.txt`. No external files, packages or probabilistic tests are used.

The script independently constructs the three-cube's twelve graph edges, nine perfect matchings and every face of the simplicial complex generated by them. It constructs the nine-facet nerve and checks its 12 triangular faces admit genuine successive elementary collapses onto a connected graph with nine vertices and twelve edges. This proves the one-dimensional homology concentration and rank four by the facet nerve lemma, not just by finite Betti-number measurements.

It enumerates every one of the 48 signed-coordinate cube automorphisms, checks the edge and perfect-matching permutations, calculates signed Lefschetz traces on *both* the 99-face original complex and the independently constructed nerve, and confirms equality for each group element with the explicit formula. The ten signed-cycle types and their class sizes add to 48. Rational representation identification then follows by character equality in characteristic zero and irreducibility of the signed coordinate module.

Additional exact boundary-rank checks on original oriented faces are performed modulo 2, 3, 5 and 7. These modular checks independently corroborate homology dimensions; they are not used to assert integral torsion-freeness in higher dimensions, integral representation splitting or homotopy outside the fixed graph. The four-circle homotopy type is already known from arXiv:2608.30601v2, Theorem 25. No independent audit has been performed.
