Every endomorphism of the nonempty-set power semigroup of a finite rectangular band consists of arbitrary row-support and column-support maps together with arbitrary compatible maps on the nonrectangular fibers; no bijectivity or cardinality preservation is required. This gives the complete endomorphism monoid, an exact all-dimensional counting formula, and \(9\,530\,217\) endomorphisms in the \(2\)-by-\(2\) case.
