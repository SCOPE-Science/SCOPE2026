For the ordinary discrete Morse complex of the octahedral graph, the entire \(675\)-dimensional rational fourth homology representation of \(C_2\wr S_3\) is determined explicitly by its ten signed-cycle character values and ten irreducible multiplicities. Central inversion splits homology into dimensions \(325\) and \(350\), and the full symmetry quotient has eleven independent rational degree-four classes with zero products; the underlying sphere-wedge homotopy type is previously known.
