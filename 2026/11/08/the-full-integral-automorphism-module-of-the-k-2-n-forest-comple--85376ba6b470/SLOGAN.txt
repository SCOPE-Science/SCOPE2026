For every \(n\ge3\), the top integral homology of the graphic-matroid forest complex of \(K_{2,n}\) is the sign-twisted augmentation lattice \(A_{n-1}\) under its **full** graph automorphism group \(S_2\times S_n\). The complex is a wedge of \(n-1\) copies of \(S^n\), and its full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
