For every odd prime \(p\), fixing any finite base-\(p\) suffix of an index determines the **entire** central-binomial residue spectrum: exactly the multiples of the prime power counted by forced doubling carries. Equivalently, the closure of \(\{(n,\binom{2n}{n})\}\) in \(\mathbb Z_p^2\) has a complete carry-defined fiber classification, including zero-only fibers.
