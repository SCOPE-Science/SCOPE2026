For four unordered collision-free particles on the Kuratowski graph \(K_{3,3}\), the complete \(S_3\wr C_2\) rational homology characters split the known 19-dimensional second homology into \(I(T,\varepsilon)\oplus 2I(\varepsilon,W)\oplus D^-(\varepsilon)\oplus2D^-(W)\), while the exceptional one-dimensional third homology is fixed by every graph symmetry. This refines published nonequivariant Betti numbers; it does not re-claim them as new.
