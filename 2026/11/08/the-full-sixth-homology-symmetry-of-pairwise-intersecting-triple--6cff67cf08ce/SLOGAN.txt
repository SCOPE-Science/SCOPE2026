The 29-dimensional rational sixth homology of the pairwise-intersecting-triples complex on seven labels is the multiplicity-free \(\mathfrak S_7\)-module \(S^{(7)}\oplus S^{(4,3)}\oplus S^{(2,2,2,1)}\). Every odd permutation has trace one, and the unique invariant line complements two conjugate 14-dimensional Specht modules.
