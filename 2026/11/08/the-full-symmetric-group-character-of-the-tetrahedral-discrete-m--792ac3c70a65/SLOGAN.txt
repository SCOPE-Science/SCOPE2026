The natural \(S_4\)-action on the 99-dimensional fourth rational homology of the full tetrahedral Morse-matching complex has character \((99,1,-1,0,-1)\), and decomposes as \(4S^{(4)}\oplus13S^{(3,1)}\oplus8S^{(2,2)}\oplus12S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus4S^{(1,1,1,1)}\). This determines four invariant and four sign-isotypic homology dimensions not visible in the known sphere-wedge rank.
