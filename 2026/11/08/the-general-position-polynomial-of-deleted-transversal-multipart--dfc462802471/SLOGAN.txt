For all \(a,r\ge3\), the general-position sets of the deleted-transversal multipartite graph \(K_a\times K_r\) admit a complete exact cardinality census by stars, injective cliques and small rectangles. With four parts, its general-position polynomial is unimodal exactly for \(3\le r\le11\) or \(r\ge40\), resolving this four-part case of an explicitly posed counting-unimodality problem while identifying a discrepancy in an earlier displayed polynomial.
