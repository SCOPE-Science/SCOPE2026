The ghost reflection of the pure Morse-matching complex of the tetrahedron boundary acts on its 81-dimensional rational third homology with trace \(9\), splitting it into eigenspaces of dimensions \(45\) and \(36\); its entire \(S_4\times C_2\) character is determined. On restriction to \(S_4\), this representation agrees with the top homology of the pure Morse-matching complex of the full tetrahedron, although the two complexes have different face vectors.
