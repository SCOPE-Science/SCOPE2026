For every evolution algebra whose natural-basis multiplication graph is strongly connected of directed period \(d\), the entire diagonal weight-one Rota–Baxter family is governed by the roots of unity of order dividing \(2^d-1\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) it has exactly \(1+\gcd(2^d-1,q-1)\) members, regardless of the nonzero structure coefficients, even for perfect non-monomial examples.
