# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The fourteen-vertex Heawood graph is reconstructed from all seven triples \(\{a,b,a\oplus b\}\) in \(\mathbb F_2^3\). Two independent equivalent criteria produce the exact same 8,851 domination-complex faces. Two separate deterministic toggle orders each pair 4,424 ordinary face-covering relations and leave precisely one vertex and one six-simplex critical. The complete directed ordinary Hasse diagram, with 8,850 faces and 52,304 covering relations, is topologically sorted under each matching, providing a complete finite proof of acyclicity. Forman's theorem yields an integral homotopy equivalent CW complex with one zero-cell and one six-cell, hence \(S^6\). For the symmetry, 168 distinct Fano collineations and their 168 point–line polarity compositions are verified individually to preserve all graph incidences. The bipartition and three noncollinear point images give an upper bound of 336 automorphisms, proving this enumeration exhaustive. Exact signed simplicial Lefschetz traces equal \(+1\) for all 336, proving the rank-one sixth integral homology character is trivial. All computational evidence is generated anew by the included standalone code.

## Originality
**PASS on checked literature with stated risk.** The published 2015 dominance theorem of Taylan applies to chordal graphs; Heawood's induced six-cycles rule out its hypotheses. The 2023 Matsushita–Wakatsuki Alexander duality identifies the dual of this complex with the neighborhood complex of the complement, but its actual theorems do not calculate the integral homotopy or orientation character of this finite graph. Its minimum-degree connectivity theorem is substantially weaker. The exact-result records on the Heawood matching, higher-independence, perfect-matching, graph-homomorphism and total three-cut complexes concern different face conditions and do not imply this closed-neighborhood forbidden complex's topology. A general high-girth theorem for ordinary cut complexes of Heawood does not apply to the four-cut complex of its dense complement, the dual object most pertinent here. Direct claim and duality searches found no covering source. Equivalent unindexed work or an unrecognized general theorem remains possible; this is not independent verification of publication priority.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The Heawood graph is the canonical Levi graph of the smallest projective plane and a natural nonchordal, highly symmetric test case for graph dominance-complex topology. A complete integral sphere result, rather than merely a field homology rank, and the determination that even point–line dualities preserve its top-dimensional orientation supply motivated structural facts that are not routine consequences of the chordal theory or general Alexander duality. The question is intrinsic to the Fano incidence structure and not an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Limits
The result proves neither a larger projective-plane family theorem nor a full equivariant sphere equivalence; it determines the induced integral top-homology character. Literature indexing may be incomplete, and independent mathematical assessment remains outstanding.
