The object is the ordinary five-cut complex, not the total cut complex, on the explicitly specified 14-vertex, 21-edge Fano incidence graph. The graph has girth six. Of 2,002 five-sets, exactly 252 are connected trees (168 induced five-paths and 84 branched trees); complements of the 1,750 disconnected sets are precisely the maximal faces. Two distinct membership tests agree on all 16,384 vertex subsets. A complete directed-Hasse acyclicity test covers all 96,880 cover incidences and eliminates all 14,632 nonempty faces. The matching has 7,070 disjoint pairs and only one critical zero-cell plus 491 critical eight-cells. Forman's theorem therefore gives an integral wedge of 491 eight-spheres. The full proof, generator and bounded finite validation are in `RESULT.md` and `verify.py`; nonzero torsion and other homotopy groups are not inferred from numerical homology ranks.
