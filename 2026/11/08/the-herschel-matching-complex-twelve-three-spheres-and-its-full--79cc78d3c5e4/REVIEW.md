# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The graph is uniquely specified by its 18 edges. Independent face enumeration methods identify 751 nonempty matching simplices. The deterministic discrete-Morse matching has 369 disjoint pairs and exactly one critical vertex plus twelve critical three-cells. Every one of the 2,502 nonempty face-poset covering relations is oriented, and exhaustive topological sorting visits all 751 faces. The resulting CW model consists of one point and twelve three-cells, proving the integral sphere-wedge homotopy type without any unproved attaching-map assertion. Independent simplicial boundary ranks over four characteristics agree with the homotopy result.

The graph automorphism group is structurally determined from the degree-four vertices and the two poles, and its order is exactly twelve. All group-element character values are computed in two combinatorially distinct ways (signed fixed simplices, signed admissible orbit-unions). Exact character inner products reproduce the stated rational irreducible decomposition.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Herschel graph is the canonical minimum-order non-Hamiltonian polyhedral graph, with a natural sixfold permutation symmetry and central involution. Its matching complex is a canonical graph-derived object; a *complete integral homotopy classification* rather than just face counts or rational Betti numbers is mathematically meaningful. The entire rational symmetry module is additionally determined, including one invariant dimension and distinct five/seven central eigenspaces. The result supplies a nonouterplanar polyhedral comparison to known forest, outerplanar and Goldner–Harary cases. It is a self-contained finite computation and does not imply a general planar-graph theorem.
