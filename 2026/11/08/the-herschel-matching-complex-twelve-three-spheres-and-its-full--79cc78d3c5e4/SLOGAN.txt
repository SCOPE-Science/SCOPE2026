The ordinary matching complex of the eleven-vertex Herschel graph is integrally homotopy equivalent to a wedge of twelve three-spheres. Its full \(S_3\times C_2\)-action on rational third homology decomposes as \((\mathbf1\oplus2\rho)\boxtimes\mathbf1\oplus(\operatorname{sgn}\oplus3\rho)\boxtimes\varepsilon\), leaving a one-dimensional globally invariant subspace.
