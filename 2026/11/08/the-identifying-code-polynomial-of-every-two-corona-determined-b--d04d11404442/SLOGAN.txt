For every connected graph \(H\) of order \(h\ge2\), the entire closed-neighborhood identifying-code polynomial of its two-corona is \(z^{2h}\prod_T(1+z)^{|T|-1}(z+|T|+1)\), where \(T\) ranges over the closed-twin classes of \(H\). Thus the code polynomial recovers the core's closed-twin size profile, and the number of minimum codes is exactly \(\prod_T(|T|+1)\).
