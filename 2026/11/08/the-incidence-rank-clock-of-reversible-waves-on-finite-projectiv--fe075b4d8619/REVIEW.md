# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
For a projective plane of order \(s\), counting the unique common lines gives \(BB^{\mathsf T}=B^{\mathsf T}B=sI+J\). Since \(s^2+s+1\) is odd, the point and line spaces each split into constants and the zero-sum hyperplane. For odd \(s\), the incidence operator is invertible on the zero-sum summands, and its block adjacency has exactly \(v-1\) Jordan two-blocks at eigenvalue one plus two zero one-blocks. For even \(s\), the constant sector is a two-block at one and the other sector is square zero, with exactly \(2r\) two-blocks and \(z\) one-blocks at zero, where \(r=\rho-1\) and \(z=2(v-1)-4r\). These counts reconstruct the entire adjacency dimension. The temporal polynomial identity \(T+T^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(A,A)\) and explicit characteristic-polynomial/rank checks transfer adjacency Jordan blocks to the stated temporal modules. GCDs with \(X^t-1\) yield the given fixed-space dimensions, exact maximum periods and primitive-period populations for every positive integer time \(t\); neither code ranks nor finite experiments replace the proof. The classical rank \(3^e+1\) of Desarguesian binary projective-plane incidence follows from a complete monomial-span argument. The independent implementations `check_matrix.py` and `check_orbits.py` were executed and their exact results preserved, but only test finite instances.

## Value — PASS
The point-line incidence graphs of projective planes are natural infinite families of symmetric sparse bipartite networks; the complete orbit census of a reversible local wave rule is a meaningful exact dynamical invariant. The 6-versus-12 clock boundary gives a structural parity distinction, and the formula \(|\operatorname{Fix}(T)|=Q^{2(v-\rho)}\) makes the full temporal census equivalent to the binary incidence-code rank for fixed order, without asserting a complete classification of projective planes. The general theorem applies equally to non-Desarguesian planes, while the independently derived \(\operatorname{PG}(2,2^e)\) rank supplies a uniform explicit subfamily. The mathematical interest is not a one-off enumeration or a renaming of a classical code table; it is the precise connection between a graphical reversible flow and an established incidence invariant.
