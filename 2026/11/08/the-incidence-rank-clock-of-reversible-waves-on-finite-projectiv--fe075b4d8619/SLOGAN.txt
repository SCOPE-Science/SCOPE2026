For reversible characteristic-two neighbor-sum waves on the point–line incidence graph of any finite projective plane, the exact global clock is six for odd plane order and twelve for even order, with every primitive-period population given by an explicit fixed-space formula. The entire temporal census is equivalent to the binary incidence rank: in particular, the number of fixed states is \(Q^{2(v-\rho)}\), while Desarguesian planes of order \(2^e\) have \(\rho=3^e+1\).
