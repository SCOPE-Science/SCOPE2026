For the fixed integral four-nodal Cayley cubic, the complete Hilbert scheme of lines is finite flat of rank \(27\), although only nine geometric lines remain: exactly six have local algebra \(\mathbb Z[u,v]/(u^2,v^2)\) and three are reduced integral sections. The nilpotent structure persists unchanged in characteristics two and three and records the collision multiplicities of the classical 27-line enumeration.
