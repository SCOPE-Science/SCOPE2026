For two unordered collision-free particles on the \(m\)-arm theta graph \(K_{2,m}\), the first integral homology is the exterior square of the arm-permutation lattice, with interchange of the two poles acting as a global sign. The space is a wedge of \(\binom m2\) circles, and every combined symmetry has trace \((-1)^\varepsilon(\binom f2-t)\).
