# Scientific review: joint symmetry of three-particle star configurations

## Correctness
PASS. The ordered-distribution graph has the explicit vertex, edge and orientation definitions used in the proof. Its homotopy relation to the configuration space follows from the established star configuration-space retract, whose intrinsic equal-spacing construction respects arm and label permutations. The three fixed-cell classifications are exhaustive for three labeled particles. They give the full group character by the cellular Lefschetz identity, and the graph's connectivity gives integral Betti ranks. Finite independent cubical and graph checks agree for all explicitly tested cases; they are not substituted for the uniform combinatorial proof.

## Originality
PASS relative to the inspected literature and indexed research. Ghrist supplies topology; Lütgehetmann 2014 supplies the graph model and particle-label-only representation, and Lütgehetmann 2017 treats stability under growing arm permutations. Wawrykow 2024 analyzes ordered star generators and discusses the separate symmetry actions for three leaves. None of the inspected results states or directly supplies all mixed arm-and-particle traces uniformly for arbitrary numbers of arms. The distinct restricted module data do not imply the joint character without additional mixed fixed-cell counts. Residual risk: an equivalent character polynomial might appear in unindexed sources or follow from a stronger uninspected equivariant decomposition.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The star is the basic local model for graph-braid phenomena, and three particles are the first case where both arm and particle permutation groups are nontrivial in a substantial way. The uniform character describes all mixed symmetries, determines exact group-invariant behavior, and exhibits a sharp dependence on cycles of lengths one, two and three; these are not recoverable from the previously known homology ranks. It provides a natural explicit equivariant baseline for comparisons with multi-essential-vertex graphs.

## Limits
The theorem concerns exactly three labeled particles and rational joint characters, not a complete integral lattice representation or equivariant homotopy type. Publication priority remains subject to independent examination of broader literature.
