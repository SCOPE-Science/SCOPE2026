For every \(m\ge3\), the joint \(S_m\times S_3\) rational first-homology character of three labeled particles on an \(m\)-arm star depends only on the numbers of fixed arms, two-cycles and three-cycles of the arm permutation. Its three formulas are \(1+f(f+1)(2f-5)\), \(1-2t\), and \(1-3u\), according as the particle permutation is the identity, a transposition, or a three-cycle.
