For a uniformly oriented central section of a unit-edge regular tetrahedron, the exact joint law of plane-section vertex count and perpendicular width has three sharp width phases: below \(\sqrt{2/3}\) every section is quadrilateral, and above \(\sqrt3/2\) width becomes independent of the triangle-versus-quadrilateral type. The full explicit subdensity formulas identify the intermediate transition, without claiming the previously published marginal width law as new.
