For every fixed convex ideal polyhedron in hyperbolic three-space, the inner boundary-layer volume equals its finite surface area times \(s\), minus \(2\sum_e\cot(\beta_e/2)\,s^2\log(1/s)\), up to \(O(s^2)\). Among ideal tetrahedra, the regular one uniquely minimizes both the unnormalized logarithmic coefficient and its volume-normalized counterpart.
