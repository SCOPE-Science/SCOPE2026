For a fixed-area annulus moving to infinity, localization by the first Hermite window develops two competing spectral shoulders at Hermite indices \(R\pm\sqrt{2R}\), but the lower-index shoulder wins by \(2L/(3e\sqrt\pi\,R)+O_L(R^{-3/2})\). Its sharp norm is asymptotically \(2/e\) times the corresponding Gaussian-window norm.
