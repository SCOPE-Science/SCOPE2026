The optimal planar Kuhn-packet fitting radius for the standard \(\ell_p\) ball is \(2^{1/p-1}\), but the number of unordered optimal integer frames jumps from \(12\) for every \(p>1\) to \(24\) at \(p=1\). Exactly the twelve extra frames are index-two bases of the checkerboard lattice, revealing a sublattice-optimality transition invisible from the optimal radius alone.
