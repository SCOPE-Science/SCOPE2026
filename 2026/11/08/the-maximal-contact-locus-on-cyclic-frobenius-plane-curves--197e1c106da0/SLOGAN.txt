For every prime power \(q\ge2\), the cyclic plane curve \(X^qY+Y^qZ+Z^qX=0\) has exactly \(q^3+1\) points at which the tangent achieves maximal contact \(q+1\). These points are all defined over \(\mathbb F_{q^6}\), and their exact Frobenius degrees are completely determined by \(a_1=h_q\), \(a_2=0\), \(a_3=q+1-h_q\), and \(a_6=q^3-q\), with \(h_q\) determined by the characteristic and \(q\bmod3\).
