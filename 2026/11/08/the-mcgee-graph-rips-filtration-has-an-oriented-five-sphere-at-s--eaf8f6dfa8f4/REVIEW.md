# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem is reconstructed from the specified LCF edges and integer shortest-path distances. At threshold two, independent ascending and descending deterministic elementary-collapse orders each remove exactly 96 free face/coface pairs while retaining the original graph. The verifier tests the complete clique enumeration, each face incidence and the final graph equality. At threshold three, direct enumeration of all 24 distance-four pairs and their six four-cycle components gives an exact join identity. Each of the six independence complexes has two contractible components, so the join has integral homotopy type \(S^5\). The sign formula follows from the reduced \(H_0\) action on the factors and graded commutativity of joins; exhaustive, adjacency-checked enumeration finds 32 automorphisms and a nontrivial character. Boundaries and the zero- and four-scale cases are explicit. The proof does not rely on numerical homology as a substitute for homotopy classification.

## Originality — PASS against checked sources
The 2021 hypercube Rips theorem concerns the Hamming cube family, not the McGee graph. The 2016 graph-power paper discusses girth-controlled stability and is relevant to scale two, but gives neither the six-square distance-four factorization nor the scale-three orientation character. The closely related indexed McGee ordinary independence-complex calculation concerns \(\operatorname{Ind}(M)\); the new result concerns \(\operatorname{Ind}(D_4)\) with disconnected \(D_4\). The Tutte–Coxeter scale-three result concerns a different graph and yields a different dimension and multiplicity. Searches of named graphs, aliases, distance-power complexes, representation equivalents and exact sphere and orientation descriptions found no source whose statement implies the full claim. An equivalent but inaccessible or unindexed source remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The graph is the unique cubic seven-cage, not a parameter chosen to force a computational answer. The exact jump from a circle wedge to an unexpected five-sphere and its nontrivial orientation character provide a structural, integral and symmetry-sensitive benchmark for graph-metric Rips topology; the scale-three pure complex has dimension eleven despite having homotopy only in dimension five. Its six-square antipodal decomposition gives a reusable geometric explanation and is not just an unexplained Betti number. A global cage-family theorem is neither needed nor claimed.

## Scope and independent limitations
The result is restricted to one finite graph and unweighted vertex shortest-path distance. The verifier checks exhaustive finite facts; published comparisons do not prove universal novelty, and no independent external audit has been performed. The equivariant claim is an integral top-homology character, not an equivariant homotopy equivalence.
