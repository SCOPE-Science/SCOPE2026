For a rooted finite Alexandrov star with cyclically rotating leaves, the entire Boolean dynamic-topological formula algebra is the power set of \(Q^m+Q N_m(Q)-Q\) explicit semantic types, where \(Q=2^k\) and \(N_m(Q)\) counts \(Q\)-color necklaces. Thus the exact number of inequivalent formulas is \(2^{Q^m+Q N_m(Q)-Q}\), unchanged by adding the eventuality operator.
