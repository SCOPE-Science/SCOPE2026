For every finite nonzero associative unital ring, the probabilities that products of \(k\) independent uniform elements are nilpotent, simultaneously for all \(k\ge1\), are determined exactly by the multiset \(\{q_i^{d_i}\}\) from its semisimple quotient \(\prod_i M_{d_i}(\mathbb F_{q_i})\). Conversely, the full probability sequence reconstructs that multiset, even though it cannot distinguish \(M_d(\mathbb F_q)\) from \(\mathbb F_{q^d}\).
