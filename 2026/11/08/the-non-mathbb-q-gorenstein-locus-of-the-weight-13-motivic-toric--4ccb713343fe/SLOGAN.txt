For the weight-\(13\) motivic multiple-zeta toric base fourfold, the canonical divisor is Cartier away from exactly one invariant curve \(C\cong\mathbb P^1\), and is not even \(\mathbb Q\)-Cartier anywhere on that curve. Its two endpoint fixed points are the only non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein torus-fixed charts; all six other fixed-point charts are Gorenstein.
