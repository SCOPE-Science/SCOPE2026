# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The source cubic is explicitly changed into the projective cone over a smooth characteristic-two cubic surface, and its smoothness is directly verified from the four partial derivatives. Projection from the cone vertex exhausts every geometric line. There are 27 lines on the smooth cubic surface, hence the reduced Fano scheme has one ruling surface and 27 Grassmannian planes, all of dimension two. Since the Fano scheme is the codimension-four zero scheme of a rank-four bundle on a smooth six-dimensional Grassmannian, it is a local complete intersection without embedded components. A four-coefficient local expansion at a general ruling line produces an Artinian complete intersection with quadratic and cubic equations that have no common projective zero; its length is six. In characteristic two, the quadratic is a geometric square, which yields the claimed normal-slice model. At a generic line in a planar component, the normal-bundle map on the underlying smooth cubic surface shows tangent dimension two, proving generic multiplicity one. These facts determine the Plücker cycle of degree 45. The attached script independently checks exact small-field line counts and a concrete point with the required coprimality property.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)
Dolgachev's 2015 primary exposition gives the characteristic-zero Segre cubic Fano surface as six degree-five del Pezzo components and fifteen Plücker-linear planes, but does not state the characteristic-two cone's nonreduced ruling multiplicity or the 28-component cycle. The known characteristic-two cone and 27 planes are not claimed as original. Kadyrsizova and collaborators study lines on characteristic-two cubic surfaces and Frobenius splitting, not the Fano scheme of the cubic cone. Searches for generic thickening of a cubic-surface cone, Hilbert schemes of its lines, and characteristic-two Segre cubic Fano multiplicities found no equivalent or more general theorem. Earlier related computations of other cone Fano schemes do not logically entail this cubic-surface cone's length-six transverse algebra. A potentially unindexed classical or specialist account of Fano schemes of cubic cones remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The parameter space of lines on a classical cubic threefold degenerates in characteristic two in a way that changes both reduced supports and their generic scheme multiplicities. The identification of a sixfold nonreduced ruling surface, 27 generically reduced planar components, and the exact Plücker cycle gives a geometric explanation of the conserved degree 45 rather than a raw enumerative observation. The transverse complete-intersection algebra adds infinitesimal information absent from the reduced line classification. It is a meaningful structural classification for a natural degeneration, not a routine verification of an individual line.

## Limits and priority risk
The theorem does not classify all component-intersection schemes or prove historical priority in unindexed literature. The symbolic geometric proof gives the infinite-field result; the finite-field script is corroboration rather than replacement. Independent external validation is not performed.
