In characteristic two, the full Fano scheme of lines on the diagonal Segre cubic has 28 surface components: a ruling cubic surface of generic multiplicity six and 27 generically reduced Plücker planes. Its geometric generic ruling normal slice is \(\overline{k(S)}[[u,v]]/(u^2,v^3)\) after extension to an algebraically closed residue field, and its Plücker cycle has degree \(45\).
