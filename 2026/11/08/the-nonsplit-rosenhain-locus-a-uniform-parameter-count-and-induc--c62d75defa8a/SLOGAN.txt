The nonsquare half of an explicit marked genus-two Rosenhain family has exactly \((q-1)(q-3)/2\) smooth parameter pairs and **every** resulting curve has exactly \(q^{2r+1}+1\) points over every odd-degree extension. Its Jacobian is an induced elliptic surface up to isogeny, with zeta numerator \(1-aT^2+q^2T^4\), explicitly determined by a quadratic-field elliptic quotient.
