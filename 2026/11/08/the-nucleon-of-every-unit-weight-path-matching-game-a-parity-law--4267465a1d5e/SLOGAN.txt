For the cooperative unit-edge matching game on every path \(P_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the multiplicative nucleon is unique: odd paths pay \(0,1,0,1,\ldots,0\), while an even path \(P_{2m}\) pays \(j/(m+1)\) to vertex \(2j-1\) and \((m+1-j)/(m+1)\) to vertex \(2j\). For even paths, the first multiplicative level is \(1\) and the sharp second level is \(1+1/(m^2-1)\), forced by \(m+1\) tight gap coalitions.
