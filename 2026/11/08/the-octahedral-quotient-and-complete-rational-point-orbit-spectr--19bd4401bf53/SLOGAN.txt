The exceptional maximal genus-\(17\) curve over \(\mathbb F_{121}\) has an explicit octahedral full-automorphism quotient of degree \(192\), branched only over \(0,1,\infty\) with indices \(3,4,4\). Its \(496\) rational points form exactly four automorphism orbits of sizes \(48,64,192,192\), with quotient values \(\infty,0,4+4\alpha,4-4\alpha\); the remaining \(48\)-point geometric branch fiber is entirely quadratic.
