For the union of any number of coordinate subgroups of a finite abelian product, the sharp fourth Fourier moment is exactly \(q\) times squared coefficient energy whenever the base group has at least four elements, and every optimizer is a character supported on one entire axis. Order four is the sharp threshold: groups of orders two and three admit strict violations, and the critical order-four proof requires a different variance-coupling inequality from the known large-order argument.
