# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Grötzsch graph is the 11-vertex triangle-free Mycielskian C5 with 20 edges. Independent full finite constructions at k=2..8 yielded disconnected generator counts (35,110,180,176,106,35,5), nonempty faces (2015,1915,1634,1124,567,171,21), and exact acyclic Morse matching counts (1002,954,809,554,275,85,10). Critical-cell counts and signed Morse matrices were reconstructed; their integer Smith nonzero invariants are k=3:(1,1,2), k=4:(1,1,1), k=5:(1,1), k=6:(1). The 3x3 signed matrix for k3 has determinant 2, hence a Moore M(Z/2,6) stage, while k4-6 attach only by unit degrees yielding sphere wedges; k7,8 contractible, k1 and k>=9 void. These are exact finite exhaustive proofs with CW attachment classification, not a pattern extrapolation.

## originality

PASS

PASS with important carve-outs: k=2 is explicitly already a consequence of Bayer et al. triangle-free two-cut theory, and the November 2 published Grötzsch four-cut theorem directly covers k=4 = 4 S5 wedge 5 S6. Neither covers the exact k=3 order-two Moore-space attaching map, the k=5 and k=6 complete integral wedge stages, nor the unified full k=2..8 cut-filtration transition. Resultary searches did not yield an earlier theorem determining those remaining stages. Novelty attaches only to the combined previously undetermined stages, not the known k2 or k4 subclaims.

The canonical smallest triangle-free four-chromatic graph has a natural complete cut-filtration homotopy census, including a two-torsion Moore stage between torsion-free wedges and sharp terminal transitions.
