The present integral homotopy calculation identifies both adjacent-degree sphere multiplicities at the four-cut level, explains non-shellability, and links the missing six-vertex faces to all twelve closed vertex neighborhoods. Because the standard general skeleton theorem fails here, the computation supplies genuinely additional structural information. No broader family or equivariant statement is claimed.
