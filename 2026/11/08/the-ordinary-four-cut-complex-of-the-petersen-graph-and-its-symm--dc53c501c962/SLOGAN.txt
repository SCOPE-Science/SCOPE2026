The ordinary four-cut complex of the Petersen graph has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{26}S^5\), despite its twelve pentagon-induced holes in the next-to-top skeleton. Its rational fifth-homology representation under \(\mathfrak S_5\) is \(S^{(5)}\oplus2S^{(4,1)}\oplus S^{(3,2)}\oplus2S^{(3,1,1)}\), with exactly one invariant line.
