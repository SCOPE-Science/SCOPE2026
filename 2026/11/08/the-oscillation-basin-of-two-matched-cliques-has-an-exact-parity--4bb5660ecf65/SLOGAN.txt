For synchronous binary majority on two cliques joined by a perfect matching, the exact size of the basin of eventual genuine two-cycles is \(\binom n{n/2}(\binom n{n/2}-1)\) for even \(n\), but \(\tfrac{n^2+2n+5}{2}\binom n{(n-1)/2}\) for odd \(n\). Only odd orders permit configurations to enter an oscillation after one transient step; all other eventual oscillations start immediately, and the basin probability changes from order \(n^{-1}\) to order \(n^{3/2}2^{-n}\) across parity.
