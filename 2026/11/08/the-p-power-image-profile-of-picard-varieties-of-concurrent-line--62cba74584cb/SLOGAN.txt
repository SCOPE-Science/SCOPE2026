For a reduced pencil of \(m\) concurrent projective lines in characteristic \(p\), the connected Picard variety has an explicit multiplication-by-\(p^r\) image profile: \(\dim\operatorname{im}[p^r]=\sum_{j=1}^{\lfloor(m-2)/p^r\rfloor}(m-1-p^rj)\). Its exponent is exactly \(p^{\lceil\log_p(m-1)\rceil}\), and the four-line pencil already has a non-vector connected Picard group in characteristic two.
