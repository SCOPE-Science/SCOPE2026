The common out-neighborhood complex of the canonical \(23\)-vertex quadratic-residue tournament is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{209}S^3\vee S^4\), despite being pure of dimension ten. Its order-\(253\) affine symmetry has rational third-homology character \((209,2,0)\) on the identity, nonidentity translations, and elements with nonidentity multiplier, respectively, and fixes one nonzero class in each of degrees three and four.
