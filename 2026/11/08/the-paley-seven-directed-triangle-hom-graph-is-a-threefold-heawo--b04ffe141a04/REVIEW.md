The complete directed multihomomorphism definition is used, not just the graph of ordinary homomorphisms. The one-common-successor/one-common-predecessor property rules out higher-dimensional cells. Six permutations of the Paley differences times seven initial vertices give 42 vertices; each has three independent coordinate replacements, giving 63 edges. The exact verifier independently checks all cells, a 41-edge spanning tree, the free order-three action, the local degree condition for a graph cover, and the two translated cyclic difference systems. The arguments do not infer infinite claims from finite experiments.
