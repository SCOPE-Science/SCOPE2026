# The Pappus five-cut complex is a wedge of \(2074\) twelve-spheres

## Finding
Let \(P\) be the eighteen-vertex Pappus graph. Its *ordinary five-cut complex* \(\Delta_5(P)\), whose facets are the complements of five-vertex subsets inducing disconnected subgraphs, has the integral homotopy type
\[
\Delta_5(P)\simeq\bigvee^{2074}S^{12}.
\]
It therefore has torsion-free reduced integral homology, consisting of \(\mathbb Z^{2074}\) in degree twelve and zero in every other degree. Exactly eighteen potential codimension-one faces are absent, and their six-vertex complements are precisely the eighteen induced hexagons of \(P\).

## Assumptions and scope
The graph has vertices \(0,\ldots,17\). Join consecutive vertices modulo eighteen; additionally join each vertex \(i\) to the vertex indexed by \(i+a_i\) modulo eighteen, where the offset sequence is the six-term pattern \((5,7,-7,7,-7,-5)\) repeated three times. Treat the graph as undirected; each edge occurs just once. This is the standard LCF representation of the Pappus cubic graph, also called the Pappus incidence graph. It has eighteen vertices, twenty-seven edges, degree three and girth six.

For an arbitrary finite simple graph \(G\) on vertex set \(V\), define \(\Delta_5(G)\) to be the abstract simplicial complex generated by the sets \(V\setminus A\), where \(A\subseteq V\), \(|A|=5\), and \(G[A]\) is disconnected. Empty faces are included in the usual way. This statement concerns only this ordinary cut complex, not the *total* cut complex, and only the graph above.

## Proof
**Complete finite face description.** There are exactly \(\binom{18}{5}=8568\) five-vertex subsets. Exact connectivity enumeration finds \(324\) connected and \(8244\) disconnected induced subgraphs; hence the complex has \(8244\) distinct twelve-dimensional facets. Its face counts by dimensions \(-1,0,1,\ldots,12\) (including the empty face) are
\[
(1,18,153,816,3060,8568,18564,31824,43758,48620,43758,31824,18546,8244).
\]
All subsets of cardinality at most eleven are therefore faces. Among the \(\binom{18}{12}=18564\) twelve-element subsets, exactly eighteen fail to be faces.

For a six-set \(A\), the complementary twelve-set is absent precisely when every one of the six five-subsets of \(A\) is connected. In a girth-six graph, that is equivalent to \(G[A]\) being a six-cycle: every vertex deletion leaves a connected graph, so \(G[A]\) is two-vertex-connected; it must contain a cycle, which needs all six vertices and cannot have a chord. The verifier independently enumerates exactly eighteen induced six-cycles and establishes equality with the eighteen excluded complementary twelve-faces.

**Acyclic matching.** On the *augmented* face poset, list the vertices as \(0,1,\ldots,17\). At stage \(i\), pair every still-unmatched face not containing vertex \(i\) with the face obtained by adjoining \(i\), provided the latter is also still unmatched. Every face is processed exactly as specified in the standalone verifier. The resulting matching has \(127840\) disjoint pairs in the augmented poset; precisely \(2074\) faces remain, all of dimension twelve. The pair \((\varnothing,\{0\})\) occurs in the augmented matching. Discarding the empty face and this pair gives an ordinary matching with one critical vertex and \(2074\) critical twelve-simplices.

To verify acyclicity *without assuming it* from the greedy procedure, direct every codimension-one incidence downward except for matched incidences, which point upward. The verifier constructs the incidence orientation and performs a complete Kahn topological ordering on its \(257753\) nonempty faces. It checks all \(2296998\) incidences and removes every directed vertex. Hence the matching is acyclic. Forman's discrete Morse theorem now yields a CW complex with exactly one zero-cell, \(2074\) twelve-cells and no other cells. Since a twelve-cell attaches to the zero-skeleton by a constant attaching map, this CW complex is a wedge of \(2074\) twelve-spheres. This proves the displayed *integral homotopy equivalence*, not only a homology computation.

**Hexagonal Euler correction.** The complete twelve-skeleton on eighteen vertices together with all thirteen-element faces has reduced Euler characteristic \(\binom{17}{4}=2380\). Removing the \(324\) top faces corresponding to connected five-sets decreases this characteristic by \(324\); removing the eighteen twelve-vertex faces complementary to induced hexagons increases it by eighteen. Thus the reduced characteristic is
\[
\widetilde\chi(\Delta_5(P))=\binom{17}{4}-324+18=2074,
\]
independently confirming the number of top-dimensional critical cells. The correction is structural: the eighteen missing codimension-one faces correspond bijectively to the graph's eighteen six-cycles.

## Verification
Run `python3 pappus5cut_verifier.py` in a directory containing the supplied standalone script. Only the Python standard library is required. The script constructs the graph directly from its LCF sequence, verifies degree and girth, and reconstructs the cut-complex faces in two independent ways: downward closure of disconnected-five-set complements and the upward Boolean-zeta transform of those disconnected five-sets. The two methods agree on all \(2^{18}=262144\) vertex subsets. It then generates the exact matching, counts all critical faces, and checks complete directed acyclicity. `pappus5cut_verification_output.txt` records one successful execution; the script, not merely the log, is the reproducible evidence. The final line is `PAPPUS_FIVE_CUT_VERIFIED`.

## Relationship to prior work
Bayer, Denker, Jelić Milutinović, Rowlands, Sundaram and Xue introduced and studied ordinary cut complexes, including broad graph-operation results, the face-lattice conditions of their Theorem 6.1 and their classification for two-cuts of connected triangle-free graphs. These do not determine this five-cut homotopy type: Theorem 6.1's complete codimension-one skeleton hypothesis fails here, since exactly eighteen twelve-vertex faces are absent. Their survey of treated families does not include the Pappus graph.

Bayer, Denker, Jelić Milutinović, Sundaram and Xue subsequently studied disconnected unions, squared paths and grid graphs, also without a claim for the Pappus graph. A separately indexed high-girth classification has a girth-at-least-seven hypothesis for five-cuts; \(P\) has girth six. The present result treats the first cut parameter of this canonical cubic symmetric graph that lies outside the high-girth hypothesis and isolates the six-cycle correction. The standard graph description and LCF notation are previously known and are not claimed as discoveries.

## Limitations
The assertion is restricted to the ordinary five-cut complex of one labeled graph up to isomorphism. It does not establish a general theorem for all girth-six graphs, shellability, a canonical equivariant wedge decomposition or an automorphism-group homology representation. Equivalence to an unindexed earlier calculation cannot be ruled out by literature searching alone. The finite computation proves this exact case, not an asymptotic or infinite-family assertion. No independent external audit is claimed.
