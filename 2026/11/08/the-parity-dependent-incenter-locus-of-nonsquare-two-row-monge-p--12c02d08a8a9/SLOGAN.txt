The normalized \(2\times m\) Monge polytope has exact intrinsic Euclidean inradius \(\bigl[2m\sqrt{1-1/(2m)}+\lfloor m^2/2\rfloor\bigr]^{-1}\) for every \(m\ge3\). Its largest-ball center is unique precisely for odd \(m\); for even \(m\), all centers form a completely explicit line segment, with endpoint-dependent tangency counts.
