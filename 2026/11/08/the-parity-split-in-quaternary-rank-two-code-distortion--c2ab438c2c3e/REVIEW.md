# Scientific review: quaternary rank-two code-distortion parity split

## Correctness
PASS. The column multiplicity derivation \(a_P=N-m_P\) reconstructs every unordered projective column profile from the full nonzero weight spectrum, including the number of zero columns. The faithful action of \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_4)\) is the 60-element group \(A_5\); this yields precisely one orbit on assignments with repeated entries and two with distinct entries. Every odd five-weight matching has an inversion, whose two ordered weight ratios force distortion at least the square of the minimum adjacent weight ratio, and an adjacent transposition attains it. The length-ten cutoff and infinite family use valid nonnegative column multiplicities and rank-two generators. The embedded independent exact-arithmetic checker reproduced the special case and group action; finite tests are not used as infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS on the examined evidence, with stated historical priority risk. Bennett–Fox–Morrell, *The Code Distortion Problem* (arXiv:2607.26261; first public July 28, 2026), defines the linear invariant and gives general algorithmic and complexity results, not this all-length five-weight orbit classification or exact parity penalty. Bouyukliev–Bouyuklieva–Kurz, *Computer classification of linear codes* (2020), uses semilinear equivalence admitting field automorphisms; Frobenius makes the two classes equivalent in that broader setting and so cannot imply their linear inequivalence. The group-theoretic fact \(\operatorname{PGL}(2,4)=A_5\) is classical and acknowledged. A separately circulated thesis or an unindexed code-equivalence classification could contain a specialization and remains a risk. Literature searching cannot establish absolute historical priority.

## Value
PASS. Ordinary weight enumerators are a standard, inexpensive filter for code equivalence, yet the theorem gives the first possible length and an exact distortion penalty for every isospectral dimension-two quaternary pair. It exposes the precise effect of the linear-versus-semilinear distinction and supplies an all-length explicit family whose distortion tends to one. This is a substantive structural classification and quantitative boundary rather than a renamed group identity or a tiny finite table.
