Identical Hamming spectra determine a single monomial equivalence class of rank-two quaternary codes unless all five projective weights are distinct; then exactly two classes remain, separated by the sharp linear Hamming distortion \(\big(\min_i a_{i+1}/a_i\big)^2\). This first happens at length \(10\), with distortion \(100/81\); Frobenius semilinear equivalence removes the split.
