Both index orders produce complete 168-pair acyclic Morse matchings, checked against every one of the 1020 nonempty-face Hasse incidences. Each leaves one vertex, two triangles and two tetrahedra. Exact integral signed gradient-path reduction gives zero attaching degrees from the tetrahedra; with no one-cells, the homotopy is the specified wedge. Chain stabilization on actual cycles yields the stated symmetry matrices. The independent reverse reduction and the mod-two boundary ranks corroborate the result. ## Originality

PASS against inspected evidence. The nearest full primary study of circular ladders (Agarwal–Basak, arXiv:2608.30601v2, Main Theorem 2 and Problem 26) treats vertex independence and the proper perfect-matching subcomplex, and explicitly raises ordinary matching-complex topology as a further question. Gupta–Sarkar–Sawant–Shukla (arXiv:2403.15298) covers categorical products of paths, not Cartesian prisms. Bayer–Jelić Milutinović–Vega (arXiv:2209.02803) covers honeycomb perfect-matching subcomplexes. Publicly indexed nearby finite matching computations do not imply the result for this non-bipartite pentagonal prism. No inspected prior claim gives the mixed wedge or complete rational symmetry character. An unindexed or otherwise unexamined equivalent proof remains possible; no blanket priority assertion is made.

## Value

PASS. The five-sided prism is a canonical cubic, vertex-transitive graph and the first non-triangular odd circular ladder. A recent primary paper explicitly proposes the ordinary matching-complex topology of circular ladders as an open direction. The two adjacent nonzero integral homology dimensions, together with a strikingly trivial rotational action and distinct reflection/layer-swap characters, give structural information neither the Euler characteristic nor the known contractible perfect-matching subcomplex contains. This is a natural exact finite case rather than a classification of all widths.

## Boundaries of the result

The theorem concerns only the ordinary matching complex of \(C_5\square K_2\), not the perfect-matching complex, the ordinary vertex-independence complex or the Möbius ladder. The representation split is rational; integral equivariant splitting and general circular-ladder formulas are not claimed. An external independent assessment has not been performed.
