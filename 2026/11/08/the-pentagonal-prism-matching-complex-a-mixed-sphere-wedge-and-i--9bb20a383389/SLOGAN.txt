The ordinary matching complex of the pentagonal-prism graph \(C_5\square K_2\) has integral homotopy type \(S^2\vee S^2\vee S^3\vee S^3\), unlike its contractible perfect-matching subcomplex. The fivefold rotation is trivial on homology, while the rational \(D_5\times C_2\)-modules split as \(\delta\oplus\lambda\) in degree two and \(\mathbf1\oplus\delta\lambda\) in degree three, where \(\delta\) is reflection sign and \(\lambda\) is layer-swap sign.
