# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The graph is reconstructed as the 24 permutations of four labels joined by adjacent swaps. An exhaustive perfect-matching generator and a different mask-DP recurrence both give 169 perfect matchings; their subsets give 379056 simplices including the empty face. A forward augmented discrete-Morse matching has 189500 pairs and 56 critical cells in dimensions 3, 4 and 5; the reverse ordering has 189499 pairs and a distinct critical vector. Both pass topological sorting of the complete 2550660-incidence Hasse orientation. The integer Morse differentials have ranks 10 and 5 with zero composite and explicit rank-sized minors of determinant +1 and -1. In dimension at least three, the resulting primitive attaching-degree matrices can be cancelled as CW generators, yielding exactly \(\bigvee^{25}S^4\vee S^5\). The complete executable certificate and its deterministic output are included. Risk: implementation error could evade checks shared by the two pairing orders; no independent audit is represented.

## Value — PASS
The 24-vertex truncated-octahedral graph is the natural Cayley graph of four-letter permutations under adjacent swaps (type A3), not a parameter-picked example. Its perfect-matching complex is a natural dimer-extension topological object. The mixed-dimensional wedge in degrees 4 and 5 for a pure 11-dimensional complex is a nontrivial exact homotopy invariant, demonstrates non-shellability, and provides a reproducible benchmark beyond honeycomb/strip examples. A general family theorem is not necessary for this complete classification, though no arbitrary-rank extension is claimed.
