For the sixteen-dimensional four-generator exterior algebra over every odd finite field, individual associative commutators form precisely the seven-coordinate Plücker cone times a free top-degree coordinate, and every fiber has one of three explicit nonzero cardinalities. The resulting exact commuting probability is \(q^{-4}+q^{-5}-q^{-9}\), while some values in the commutator span require exactly two commutator summands.
