For the Klein quartic over \(\mathbb F_8\), the 24 rational tangents are precisely the 24 lines avoiding a seven-point Fano subplane cut out by its partial derivatives. Their tangent-incidence spectrum is exactly \(0^7,2^{24},4^{42}\): all seven uncovered points lie in that polar subplane, all 24 curve points lie on two rational tangents, and each remaining point lies on four.
