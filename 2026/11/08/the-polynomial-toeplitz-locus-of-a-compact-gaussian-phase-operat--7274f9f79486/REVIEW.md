A bounded Toeplitz representative can be averaged over rotations to a bounded radial representative. Its Laplace transform must be holomorphic on the right half-plane, whereas the finite polynomial eigenvalue sequence forces a rational transform with distinct poles. A forbidden nonzero coefficient would produce a pole in that half-plane. All permitted terms give bounded Gaussian symbols, and Gaussian Hermite completeness proves uniqueness. For degree densities, the irrational case follows by equidistribution and a vanishing threshold; the rational case follows by positive-real-part root counting.
