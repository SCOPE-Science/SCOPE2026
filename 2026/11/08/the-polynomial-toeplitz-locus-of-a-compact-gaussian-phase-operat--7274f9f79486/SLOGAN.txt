For a compact Gaussian-phase Toeplitz operator \(A_t\), a polynomial \(P(A_t)\) has a single bounded Fock Toeplitz symbol exactly when every nonzero power coefficient has \(\operatorname{Re}(1+it)^k\ge1\); forbidden powers cannot cancel. The allowed degrees have natural density \(1/2\) at irrational phase angles and an explicit roots-of-unity density at rational angles, so the single-symbol locus is a linear space but never a polynomial algebra.
