For the weight-sixteen multiple-zeta toric semigroup, the unique quadratic normalization hole is the previously known witness \(h=2e_2+e_3+e_{223}+e_{233}+e_{2233}\), and there are exactly 22 cubic holes, all obtained by multiplying that hole by degree-one generators. The first normalized graded dimensions are 631 and 6907, compared with 630 and 6885 before normalization.
