Projection from that node classifies all geometric lines as six node lines and fifteen chords joining a transverse six-point conic intersection. The generic lines are constructed in the quadratic coefficient ring, with a unit Plücker coordinate and generator ratio. The relative Grassmannian section is a rank-four complete intersection with finite fibers, hence finite flat of degree \(27\). Explicit sections then force the claimed twenty-one local rings by rank comparison. The standalone arithmetic verifier corroborates all nonstandard polynomial and finite combinatorial steps; no finite enumeration is used as an infinite-field proof.
