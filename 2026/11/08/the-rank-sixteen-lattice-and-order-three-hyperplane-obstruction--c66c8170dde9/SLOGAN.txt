The twenty-four distinguished lines on the Schur quartic generate a rank-sixteen integral K3 divisor lattice of discriminant 1728, with discriminant group \((\mathbb Z/2)^4\oplus\mathbb Z/6\oplus\mathbb Z/18\). The hyperplane class is absent from this lattice but has exact order three modulo it: two explicit complementary sets of twelve lines each form a divisor linearly equivalent to \(3H\).
