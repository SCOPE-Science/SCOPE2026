The rank-three Fano-matroid independence complex has \(13\)-dimensional integral second homology, but its full \(GL_3(\mathbb F_2)\)-action is much more structured: over \(\mathbb C\) it splits into irreducibles of dimensions \(7,3,3\), with character \((13,-3,1,1,-1,-1)\). Its full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic, while cyclic subgroups of orders \(2,3,4,7\) retain respectively \(5,5,3,1\) second-homology dimensions.
