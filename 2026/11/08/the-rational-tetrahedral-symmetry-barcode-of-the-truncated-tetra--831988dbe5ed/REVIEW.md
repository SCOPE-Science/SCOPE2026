# Scientific review: truncated-tetrahedral symmetry barcode

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim is limited to rational homology representations. The twelve ordered-pair vertices, the explicit adjacency rule, and the shortest-path distances unambiguously determine both filtered complexes. Exhaustive subset enumeration gives face vectors \((12,18,4)\) and \((12,42,48,12)\). The oriented boundary ranks are exactly \((11,4)\) and \((11,31,12)\): the standalone verifier exhibits mod-two independent column families of these sizes, and the elementary upper bounds from connectedness, \(\partial^2=0\), and the chain dimensions force equality over \(\mathbb Q\). A second calculation over exact rational matrices agrees. Hence only \(H_1(K_1;\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Q^3\) and \(H_2(K_2;\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Q^5\) occur in positive degrees. Signed Lefschetz enumerations separately check all 24 \(S_4\) actions and yield class characters \((3,-1,-1,0,1)\) and \((5,1,1,2,-1)\) in the stated class order. Those are precisely \(\operatorname{sgn}\otimes V_{\mathrm{std}}\) and \(\mathbb Q^4\oplus\operatorname{sgn}\). No integral splitting, equivariant homotopy statement, or other thresholds are inferred without proof.

## Originality
**PASS relative to inspected literature.** The closest public graph-metric Rips classification for the *same* truncated tetrahedral graph gives the two nonequivariant homotopy types but not the automorphism character or its two rational constituent decompositions. These exact homotopy types are excluded from the new claim. The 2023 Saleh–Titz Mite–Witzel paper has the five *Platonic* solids as its cases and suggests Archimedean solids without computing this graph. The 2024 Adams–Lagoda–Moy–Sadovek–De Saha paper computes Borel equivariant cohomology for a circle action on circle metric thickenings, not ordinary homology representations for an \(S_4\)-acted graph metric. Searches of mathematical records for truncated-tetrahedron Rips symmetry, graph expansions and equivalent characters found no dominating theorem. The closest public numerical outputs are nonequivariant, so matching sphere ranks do not imply the new characters. Residual risk: an unindexed equivalent equivariant calculation or hidden specialization of a broader result.

## Value
**PASS.** The source paper explicitly identifies Archimedean solids and preservation of symmetry as a motivated extension of the Platonic-solid Rips program. On this canonical twelve-vertex Archimedean graph, ordinary bar multiplicities \(3\) and \(5\) do not reveal whether the sphere classes are invariant, sign-twisted, or standard. The two explicit characters distinguish all the classes, exhibit a one-dimensional invariant degree-two family and a separate orientation-sign family, and recover the complete positive-degree rational equivariant persistence rather than an isolated rank. The result has a natural interpretation as a symmetry refinement of an already known filtration, not a general theorem or a large numerical improvement.
