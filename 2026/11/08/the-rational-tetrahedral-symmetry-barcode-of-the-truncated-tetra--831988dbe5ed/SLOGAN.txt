The truncated-tetrahedral graph's positive-degree rational Vietoris–Rips barcode splits under its full \(S_4\) symmetry: its three circle classes at scale one form the sign-twisted standard representation, while its five two-sphere classes at scale two form the four-label permutation representation plus the sign line. The non-equivariant homotopy types were previously known; the complete symmetry character and representation-valued barcode are the new classification.
