For every prime \(p=6m+1\), the natural bielliptic Picard pencil \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+1\) has exactly \(m\) distinct smooth nonordinary marked parameters, all simple zeros of an explicit degree-\(m\) coefficient polynomial. Exactly at these parameters its Prym surface is superspecial; elsewhere the genus-three Jacobian and Prym are ordinary.
