For every prime \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), the genus-three cyclic quartic pencil \(Y^4=XZ(X-Z)(X-tZ)\) has an everywhere-superspecial Prym surface, and its full Jacobian is superspecial exactly at the simple roots of the Legendre Deuring polynomial \(\sum_{j=0}^{(p-1)/2}\binom{(p-1)/2}{j}^2t^j\). Hence its superspecial locus consists of precisely \((p-1)/2\) reduced geometric marked parameters, and its Cartier \(a\)-number is exactly two off that locus and three on it.
