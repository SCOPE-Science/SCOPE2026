The exhaustive standard-library verifier enumerates all 6,212 augmented simplices, constructs 3,103 acyclic Morse pairs, checks every one of 30,380 directed Hasse incidences and computes the entire five-by-one integral Morse differential as zero. It separately proves that all 240 natural permutations are distinct and exhaust the graph automorphism group. The underlying integral homology groups are free in degrees four and five; the claimed representation classification in degree five is only rational.

The classification is naturally motivated, complete for this graph and its full group, and supplies an immediately usable character formula.
