For the anticanonical seven-monomial toric code of the smooth degree-six del Pezzo hexagon, the second generalized Hamming weight over every \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\ge5\) is exactly \((q-2)^2\). The extremal two-dimensional linear systems share \(2q-3\) torus base points, and an exhaustive Newton-polygon factor classification proves that no two independent sections share more.
