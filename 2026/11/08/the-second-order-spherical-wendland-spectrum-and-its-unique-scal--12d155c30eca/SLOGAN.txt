For generalized Wendland kernels on any unit sphere with \(\mu>\lambda+2\), the normalized spherical Fourier eigenvalues have no relative first-order correction, and their full relative \(\nu^{-2}\) coefficient is \((\lambda-1/2)[\lambda(\lambda-1)/3-(\mu-1)(\mu-2)\varepsilon^2]\). Exactly one positive support scale, \(\varepsilon_*=\sqrt{\lambda(\lambda-1)/(3(\mu-1)(\mu-2))}\), eliminates this second-order spectral bias.
