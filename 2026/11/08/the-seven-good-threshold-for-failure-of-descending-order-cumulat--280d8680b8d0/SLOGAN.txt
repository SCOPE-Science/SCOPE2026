For two identical agents receiving goods in descending value order, assigning each good to the less-loaded agent minimizes total cumulative maximum envy for every sequence of at most six goods, but not for seven. The exact seven-good witness \((6,5,5,4,4,1,1)\) has greedy cost \(20\) versus optimum \(18\), and 1,364 exact rational dual certificates cover the six-good universal boundary.
