For a two-offer controlled random walk, graph-distance-first target routing is optimal on every connected graph of at most six vertices but fails already on a seven-vertex unicyclic graph. In a five-cycle with \(k\) leaves attached to one vertex, the exact preference reversal starts at \(k=2\) and persists for every larger integer pendant load, with an optimal continuation-value gap \(4(18k^2-10k-51)/(134k+147)\).
