For every nonzero quadratic Hardy Hankel symbol \(\psi(z)=az+bz^2\), the norm detected by normalized reproducing kernels is at least \(5/(3\sqrt3)\) times the full Hankel norm; the constant is optimal over the entire degree-two family. The worst deficit occurs exactly at \(|a|/|b|=1/\sqrt6\), with arbitrary coefficient phases, giving an exact squared testing-to-operator ratio of \(25/27\).
