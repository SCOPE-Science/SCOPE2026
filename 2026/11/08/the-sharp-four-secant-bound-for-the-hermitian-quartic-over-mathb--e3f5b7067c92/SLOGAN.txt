For the Hermitian quartic over \(\mathbb F_{729}\), every external projective point belongs to at least \(25\) rational four-secants, proving completeness of its \((892,4)\)-arc in the small case \(q=3,r=3\). Exactly \(39{,}879\) four-secants occur; the sharp maximum multiplicity \(222\) occurs precisely at the \(63\) external points of the embedded \(\mathbb F_9\)-subplane.
