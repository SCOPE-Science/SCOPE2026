When every approval candidate is supported by at most the Hare quota \(q=\lceil n/k\rceil\), choosing a welfare-maximal committee with maximal voter coverage guarantees EJR and Pareto optimality, constructibly in polynomial time. The cutoff is uniformly sharp: at degree \(q+1\), for every \(q\ge1\) there are elections where the unique welfare maximum violates JR and enforcing it loses exactly one unit of welfare.
