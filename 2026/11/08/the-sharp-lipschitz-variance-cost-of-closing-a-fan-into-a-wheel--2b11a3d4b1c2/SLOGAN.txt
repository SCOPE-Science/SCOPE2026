For a fan \(K_1\vee P_m\), closing one rim edge to form the wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\) decreases the maximum variance of a real unit-Lipschitz function by exactly \((m+1-(-1)^m)/(m+1)^2\) for every \(m\ge4\). All extremizers are balanced \(\pm1\) rim blocks separated by exactly one zero on the fan and two on the wheel; both diameter-two families approach the sharp variance bound \(1\) as \(m\) grows.
