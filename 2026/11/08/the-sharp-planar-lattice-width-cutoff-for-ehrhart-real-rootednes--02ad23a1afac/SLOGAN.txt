For every full-dimensional lattice polygon, lattice width \(\ge4\) forces two distinct strictly negative roots of its Ehrhart \(h^*\)-polynomial; this cutoff is optimal. At width \(3\), the only exception up to affine-unimodular equivalence is \(\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(1,0),(3,7)\}\), whose \(h^*(z)=1+3z+3z^2\) has nonreal roots.
