# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the fourth power of a cycle, a complementary three-subset yields a facet exactly when it induces a disconnected graph. The complete orders for sizes 17, 18, 19, and 20 independently pass the exact pure-shelling criterion on every pair of earlier and newly added facets: every old intersection lies in an old codimension-one face of the new facet. Each order contains every admissible facet exactly once. The spanning-facet counts (1, 10, 20, 31) give the claimed homotopy types at these four sizes. At sizes 11 through 16, exhaustive chain-rank calculations on the Alexander dual produce nonzero reduced homology of the original complex below top dimension and therefore obstruct shellability. The published theorem for sizes at least 21 closes the infinite-range argument. Limit: lower obstructions are mod-two, not integral classifications; the positive finite certificates are computational and should be replayed independently.

## Value — PASS
This settles a complete nontrivial fixed-graph-power instance of the published sharp-threshold conjecture.
