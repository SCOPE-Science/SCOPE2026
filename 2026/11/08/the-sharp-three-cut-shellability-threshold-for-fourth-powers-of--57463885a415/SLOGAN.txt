The ordinary three-cut complex of the fourth power of a cycle is shellable exactly when the nonvoid graph has at least 17 vertices; in that range it is a wedge of \(\binom{n-8}{2}-35\) spheres of dimension \(n-4\). Four complete finite shellings at \(n=17,18,19,20\), together with the previously published large-size theorem and lower-range homology obstructions, settle the fixed-fourth-power case of the sharp-threshold conjecture.
