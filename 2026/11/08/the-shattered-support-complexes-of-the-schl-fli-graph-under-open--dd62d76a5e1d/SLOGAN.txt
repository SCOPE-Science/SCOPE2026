For the Schläfli graph, every four-vertex support shattered by open or closed neighborhoods is exactly a four-clique; open neighborhoods shatter every triple except the 45 independent triples, while closed neighborhoods shatter exactly the 720 triangles. The full respective shattering polynomials are \(1+27z+351z^2+2880z^3+1080z^4\) and \(1+27z+351z^2+720z^3+1080z^4\).
