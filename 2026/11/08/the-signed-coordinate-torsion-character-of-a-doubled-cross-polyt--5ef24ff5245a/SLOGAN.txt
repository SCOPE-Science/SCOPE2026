The first three-torsion in the doubles of cross-polytope boundaries occurs in dimension seven: \(\widetilde H_1(D^2(\partial C_7);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\), with the full signed-coordinate group acting by the sign of its coordinate permutation. The associated mod-three first homology disappears after quotienting by a single coordinate transposition; exact simplicial chains certify the sign action.
