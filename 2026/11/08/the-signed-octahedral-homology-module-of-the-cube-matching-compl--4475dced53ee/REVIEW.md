# Scientific assessment: signed-octahedral homology module of the cube matching complex

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is about the four-dimensional rational second homology, not a newly discovered nonequivariant Betti number. A complete face-poset construction produces exactly 107 matching faces and 252 Hasse covers. The 51-pair matching specified in the proof has exactly one critical zero-cell and four critical two-cells and is acyclic by an exhaustive topological sort. This proves the required integral wedge-of-spheres premise by discrete Morse theory. All 48 affine cube automorphisms preserve the edge set, act by signed chain maps in 4,560 checked nontrivial instances, and have Lefschetz traces identical to the stated \(\delta\oplus\bigwedge^2V\) formula. Character equality in characteristic zero implies the module isomorphism; the restriction to translations has nonzero weights only, so averaging gives rationally acyclic full orbit space. ## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
The closest exact same-object paper, *The cube matching complex is a wedge of four two-spheres* (published research record, 2026-10-11), determines the ordinary integral homotopy type but does not give any full automorphism character or rational irreducible decomposition. The closely named cube three-cut result concerns a distinct complex and its homology is in degrees three and four, unlike the cube matching complex's degree two. The octahedral-graph matching-complex character is for a different graph with a different simplex count. The complete accessible Braun–Hough preprint's setup and results concern planar two-row grids and comb graphs, with no cube or automorphism action, so its theorems are not sufficient to imply the asserted character. The Jonsson monograph is useful background but its full text was not accessible. Exact object, aliases, implied corollaries, and the possibility of stronger results were compared, not merely titles. Remaining risk: an equivalent representation computation in inaccessible or unindexed literature could exist.

## Value — PASS
The three-cube is the smallest nontrivial hypercube matching-complex case whose homology is a nonzero multi-generator sphere wedge. The exact group module \(\bigwedge^2V\oplus\mathbb Q_\delta\) is a natural full-symmetry invariant, distinguishing the exterior-square and flip-parity contributions and specifying every one of the 48 symmetry traces. It is not supplied by the known four-sphere wedge count. Its absence of translation-fixed vectors gives a substantive topological conclusion for the full orbit space. The calculation is a complete representation classification for a canonical object rather than an arbitrary parameter slice, although it is finite and no higher-dimensional family theorem is claimed.
