The four-dimensional rational second homology of the ordinary three-cube matching complex is exactly \(\bigwedge^2 V\oplus\mathbb Q_\delta\) under all forty-eight cube automorphisms, where \(V\) is the signed-coordinate representation and \(\delta\) records coordinate-flip parity. In particular, its full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic even though the matching complex is a wedge of four two-spheres.
