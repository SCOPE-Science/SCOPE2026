For the \(81\)-point Hermitian elliptic cubic over \(\mathbb F_{64}\), the number of rational three-secants through an external point takes exactly the six values \(11,14,16,17,18,26\), with respective frequencies \(72,648,2052,864,432,12\). Thus its previously known complete \((81,3)\)-arc is in fact elevenfold covered by three-secants, and the points of multiplicity \(26\) are exactly the twelve points of \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_4)\) outside the curve.
