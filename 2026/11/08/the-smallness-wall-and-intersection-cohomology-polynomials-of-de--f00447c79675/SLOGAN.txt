For the quadric-cycle locus in the degree-two Chow variety of \(p\)-cycles on \(\mathbf P^n\), the natural span resolution is small precisely when \(n<(p+1)(p+4)/2\) and semismall at equality. In this entire range its rational intersection-cohomology polynomial is an explicit Gaussian-binomial product, with one precisely determined Grassmannian correction at equality.
