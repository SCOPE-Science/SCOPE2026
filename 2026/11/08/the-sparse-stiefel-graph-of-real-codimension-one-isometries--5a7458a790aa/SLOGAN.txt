For real finite-dimensional \(\ell_p\) isometries with exactly one spare output coordinate and \(p\ne2\), the entire operator space is a signed type-D Cayley graph, not a Stiefel manifold: it has \(2^{n-1}n!\) vertices and a free fundamental group of rank \(1+(n-2)2^{n-1}n!\). Columnwise Euclidean normalization maps that large free fundamental group onto the ordinary Stiefel fundamental group.
