For a uniformly sampled multiset word with \(n_j=\lceil e^{c j^\beta}\rceil\), the number of canonical Knuth cycles changes from Gaussian fluctuations for \(1<\beta<2\) to a non-Gaussian geometrically weighted sum of exponentials at \(\beta=2\), and finally to one exponential for \(\beta>2\). The critical Laplace transform is \(\prod_{k\ge0}(1+s e^{-2ck})^{-1}\), identifying the sharp square-exponential boundary beyond the previously analyzed cubic-exponential obstruction.
