Exactly fifteen squarefree 2-near-perfect integers have four distinct prime factors; all fifteen are even: \(570,690,910,1722,1974,2002,2470,3230,4386,4510,4730,7630,9590,9730,13026\). An odd-case abundancy obstruction, prime-factor inequalities, and omitted-divisor analysis reduce the infinite \(2pqr\) family to 399 fully checked prime triples, with one omitted pair per solution.
