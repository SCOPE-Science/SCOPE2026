# Review of the tenfold Witt-parameter classification

## Correctness — PASS

The characteristic-two Witt subtraction identity gives the explicit odd-degree Artin–Schreier quintic. Its unique hyperelliptic branch point makes every curve isomorphism affine on the base. Comparing fifth and third powers gives the necessary scaling conditions, and an explicit degree-sixteen equation in the translation parameter has nonzero derivative, making them sufficient. The exact isomorphism invariant is \(B^5/A^3\), whose square is \((\lambda^2+\lambda+1)^5/\lambda^6\). Its fiber polynomial has degree ten and derivative \((L^2+L+1)^4\), proving ten simple roots off the special value and two fivefold roots at zero. The same isomorphism calculation yields full automorphism counts \(32\) and \(160\); a standard general normal-form theorem proves exhaustion of supersingular genus-two curves. A standalone exact checker reproduced the principal polynomial identities. The classification is restricted to unmarked geometric isomorphism, not covers with a chosen group action.

## Originality — PASS, qualified historical priority

Kazemifard–Tafazolian, arXiv:2610.04702, Theorem 5.7, constructs the Witt family but states no uniqueness of its parameter. Brock–Howe, arXiv:2002.02122, Lemma 2.1, gives a powerful general classification of supersingular genus-two curves by Igusa invariants, so the older generic classification is **not** claimed here. The additional statement is the exact pulled-back classifier for the recently introduced Witt parameter, including its complete ten-versus-two fiber profile and the corresponding full-automorphism jump. Direct searches for equivalent formulae and the mapped parameter ambiguity yielded no matching earlier result. There remains a risk that a previously published explicit specialization of general normal-form machinery already determines this particular parameter map; absence of a search hit is not a novelty certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2026 construction intentionally leaves parameter redundancy unspecified. A complete explicit equivalence relation, exact generic multiplicity, exceptional degeneration and group-order jump answer that natural moduli-identifiability question and distinguish marked Witt presentations from unmarked underlying curves. The exact fiber information is not a textbook restatement of the genus-two normal form alone; deriving it requires conversion of the Witt coordinates and analysis of the resulting degree-ten rational function. No separate generality or downstream theorem is asserted.

## Closest literature and limits

The general supersingular classification in Brock–Howe is stronger for unmarked genus-two moduli but does not determine the displayed rational map from the later Witt parameter; its implications are explicitly used rather than ignored. Scholten–Zhu's supersingular hyperelliptic normal forms and Maisner–Nart's finite-field zeta classification are related background, not evidence for unique parameter values in the Witt family. The result concerns only geometric unmarked isomorphisms in characteristic two; field-of-definition and marked-pair classifications remain open in this treatment.
