For the characteristic-two genus-two cyclic-four Artin–Schreier–Witt family \(\wp_2(Y_0,Y_1)=(x,\lambda x^3)\), the underlying curve is classified by \(\mathcal J(\lambda)=(\lambda^2+\lambda+1)^5/\lambda^6\). Every nonzero classifier value has exactly ten parameter representatives, but the superspecial value has exactly two; the full geometric automorphism group jumps from order \(32\) to \(160\) precisely at those two parameters.
