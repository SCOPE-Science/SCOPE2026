For the binary Serre-type maximal curve \(y^4+y=x^{(2^n+1)/3}\) with odd \(n\ge3\), the penultimate Cartier iterate has exact rank \((n-1)/2\) and an explicit monomial image, while the next iterate is zero. This shows that the known maximality-based Cartier nilpotency bound is sharp throughout the family.
