For every odd \(b\ge7\), the third distinct smallest modular-multiplication inversion number is at least \((b+1)(b-2)/6\), with equality precisely for \(b\equiv2\pmod3\). The only multipliers attaining equality are \(3\) and its inverse modulo \(b\), proving the odd-modulus third-rank case of Chen–Dunn–Hewett–Silas Conjecture 2.7.
