The entire three-length open window for bounded independence and total three-cut complexes of cubic cycle powers has an exact integral homotopy classification: \(BI_3(C_{13}^3)\) and \(BI_3(C_{14}^3)\) are five-spheres, while \(BI_3(C_{15}^3)\) is a wedge of two four-spheres; their Alexander-dual total three-cut complexes are respectively \(S^5\), \(S^6\), and \(S^8\vee S^8\). Exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matchings, dual skeleton checks, and standard homotopy-theoretic arguments certify all six types.
