# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The graph-distance identity and forbidden-distance-two lemma give the exact domain for all possible configurations, including the empty set and the exceptional \(n=2\) overlap of facet cardinalities. The column argument classifies all feasible faces. The polynomial follows from two parity choices and an explicit \(4n\)-edge correction. The homotopy argument realizes two disjoint \((n-1)\)-spheres and \(4n\) connecting one-cells, so its integral wedge decomposition is not merely inferred from mod-two homology. The fixed-cardinality argument constructs all within-parity moves and the bridge between parities at size two. The executable finite checks corroborate each part on their stated ranges; they are not used as a substitute for the proof for unbounded \(n\).

## Originality

PASS under documented best-knowledge comparisons, with residual publication-priority risk. Theorem 6 of Bujtás--Klavžar--Tian (2023) already gives the distance-two characterization for every Hamming graph, and their Theorem 9 already yields the maximum \(n\) here; both are expressly recognized as prior coverage. However, the inspected paper does not give the exact two-family facets, cardinality polynomial, integral wedge homotopy, or fixed-cardinality token-jumping component formula. Brešar--Yero (2023) studies lower visibility but not this family-wide census. Bujtás--Klavžar--Tian (2024/2025) gives broad visibility-polynomial and characterization results rather than these concrete evaluations. published-finding corpus semantic findings on Hamming topology concern other complexes such as Čech, Rips, and total two-cut complexes, not this face family. Deduction from a broader known membership theorem is acknowledged and is the main originality risk: the new contribution must be assessed as the specific simultaneous enumeration, facet structure and homotopy evaluation rather than as a new feasibility criterion.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The known three-factor maximum \(n\) alone does not determine whether greedy maximal visible sets can be much smaller, how many such sets exist, or the topology of all feasible placements. Here both extremal sizes \(2,n\) occur with no intermediate facet sizes, giving an unbounded gap and a complete all-set enumerator; the complex has nontrivial integral homology simultaneously in dimensions \(1\) and \(n-1\), and token jumping undergoes a sharp connected-to-two-component transition. These are linked structural, enumerative and topological consequences for a natural infinite Hamming family, not an arbitrary finite slice.
