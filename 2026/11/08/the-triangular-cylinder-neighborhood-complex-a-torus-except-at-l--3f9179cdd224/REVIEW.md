# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
All \(6n\) collapses follow from a single rigorous unique-common-neighbor lemma for every positive integer \(n\ge3\). For \(n\ne4\), the six explicit vertices of each link are distinct; the surface is connected, oriented by the parity-diamond universal covering, and has Euler characteristic zero. This gives an integral torus homotopy type, not a Betti-number extrapolation. At \(n=4\) the exact lexicographic spanning-tree presentation has nineteen generators and twenty-four triangle relators. Eighteen one-occurrence CW cancellations leave one generator with six trivial attaching words, proving \(S^1\vee\bigvee^6S^2\). The standalone verifier checks every collapsible face and reconstructs the attaching words, not merely printed ranks.

## Originality: PASS (with stated literature limitations)
Shukla's 2018 circulant classification (arXiv:1802.04526v2, Theorems 2.2–2.6) already covers the **cyclic** subfamilies, including a garland-of-spheres case at length four. The independent finite-index lattice quotient theorem on toroidal-grid neighborhood complexes requires shortest period at least five, and the complete-graph-by-cycle theorem requires clique order at least four. Neither inspected statement implies the uniform \(C_3\square C_n\) result for every \(3\mid n\); these genuinely noncyclic products are the principal uncovered part. The rook-graph relative-homology theorem does not classify the complete homotopy type at the smallest noncyclic endpoint. Search results are not a proof of worldwide publication priority; unindexed equivalences remain a risk.

## Value: PASS
The family is not a random finite slice: it is the exact sharp short-circumference boundary case for a geometric neighborhood-complex collapse, with a genuine change in homotopy type at length four. The theorem completes the triangular-factor Cartesian family, extends topology to infinitely many noncyclic graphs, and explains the exceptional length by a specific collision of link vertices and a reproducible two-cell cancellation certificate.

## Limitations
Cyclic subcases are included for completeness rather than claimed as new. The work establishes no natural equivariant splitting, classification of arbitrary small-period lattice quotients, or publication priority beyond inspected sources. Computational checks at finitely many lengths cannot replace the uniform argument.
