The open neighborhood complex of the triangular Cartesian grid \(C_3\square C_n\) is a torus for every \(n\ge3\) except \(n=4\), when it is \(S^1\vee\bigvee^6S^2\). An explicit two-collapse-per-vertex construction explains the short-circumference boundary and includes noncyclic products previously outside standard circulant results.
