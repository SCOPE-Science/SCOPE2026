# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The complete occupied-set law follows by classical parking-function block decomposition for the deterministic endpoint rules, then a sub-Markov convex-kernel induction for nonconstant per-arrival direction biases. The two-vacancy probabilities are explicit integer-weight ratios. A standard-library-only exact checker independently enumerates small instances and computes all 66 exact covariance signs at 12 sites; the first-size claim requires checking only sites 4–11 and is fully exhausted. The inequalities are integer comparisons, not float estimates. Scientific limitation: no statement for sizes above 12.

**Originality — PASS, with bounded historical risk.** Nadeau (2026, Proposition 3.5 and Theorem 4.2) explicitly contains stronger bilateral block-weight counting and time-indexed direction settings. Those are credited, not claimed original. Durmić et al. (2022) and Harris et al. (2025) address aggregate success and preference statistics. The particular 12-site *full covariance sign matrix* and its minimality are not stated in their relevant inspected sections or in the targeted mathematical/mathematical database searches. This is an exact new natural correlation invariant, not a new proof of known parking counts. Broader historical work in hashing with linear probing remains a priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The earliest complete local sign reversal is a motivated threshold: competition for two scarce vacancies can produce positive co-occupancy only for neighbors while nonneighbors remain negatively correlated. At the minimal all-positive-neighbor size, the *entire* pairwise covariance sign matrix is determined, providing a finite spatial-correlation benchmark for linear-probing and parking processes. Its definition is dictated by the spatial probability model and does not depend on arbitrary parameter slicing. No larger-size phase-transition theorem is claimed.
