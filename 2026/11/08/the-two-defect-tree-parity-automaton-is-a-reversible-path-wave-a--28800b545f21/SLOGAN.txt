For synchronous characteristic-two neighbor-sum dynamics on any finite tree with exactly two even-degree vertices at distance \(d\), the entire eventual recurrent dynamics is conjugate to a reversible wave on an open path of length \(d+1\), regardless of all other branch shapes. Its exact clock is \(2(d+2)\), and it has \(q^{2(d+1)}\) immediately periodic states over \(\mathbb F_q\).
