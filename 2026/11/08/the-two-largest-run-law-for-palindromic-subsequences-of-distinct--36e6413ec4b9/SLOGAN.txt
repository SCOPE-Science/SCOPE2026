For a repeated cycle of pairwise distinct monochromatic runs, the longest scattered palindrome has length \(kA+(k-1)B\), where \(A,B\) are the two largest run lengths; all smaller runs and their order are irrelevant. If the longest run is unique and \(t\) runs share the second-largest length, there are exactly \(t^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\) distinct longest palindrome words, with an explicit alternating-gap classification.
