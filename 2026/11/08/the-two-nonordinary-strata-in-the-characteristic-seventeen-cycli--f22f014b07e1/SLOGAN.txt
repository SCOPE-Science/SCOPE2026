For the cyclic-order-eight genus-six plane quintic family in characteristic \(17\), nonordinarity occurs in exactly two geometric isomorphism classes, with \(r^2=5\) and \(r^2=13\). Their Jacobians have \((17\text{-rank},a\text{-number})=(2,4)\) and \((4,2)\), respectively; every other smooth member is ordinary.
