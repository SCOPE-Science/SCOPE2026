For every \(n\ge5\), the simple-root basis of the Euclidean root lattice \(A_n\) is an optimal *common* Kuhn frame: \(\kappa(B_2^n,A_n)=\mu(B_2^n,A_n)=\sqrt{\lfloor(n+1)^2/4\rfloor/(n+1)}\). The resulting packet has an exact Eulerian spectrum of circumsphere radii, indexed by ascents of inverse permutations; these spheres are not asserted to be individual minimum enclosing balls.
