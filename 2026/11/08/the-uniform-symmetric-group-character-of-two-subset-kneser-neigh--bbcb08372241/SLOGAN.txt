For every \(n\ge5\), the full \(S_n\)-character on top rational homology of the ordinary open neighborhood complex of the two-subset Kneser graph is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)(f(\sigma)^2-3f(\sigma)+1)\), where \(f\) counts fixed labels. It is the multiplicity-free sum \(S^{(3,1^{n-3})}\oplus S^{(2,2,1^{n-4})}\), so both the symmetric-group and alternating-group quotients are rationally acyclic.
