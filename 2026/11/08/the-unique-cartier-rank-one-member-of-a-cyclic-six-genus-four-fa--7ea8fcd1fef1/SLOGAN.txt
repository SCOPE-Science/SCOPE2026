In characteristic \(11\), the cyclic-six genus-four hyperelliptic pencil \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+a x^4+b x\) has exactly one smooth normalized parameter with Cartier rank one, namely \((a,b)=(0,-2)\). Everywhere else its Cartier rank is two on an explicit degree-five parameter divisor and three off that divisor; among \(101\) smooth \(\mathbb F_{11}\)-parameter pairs the \(a\)-numbers \(1,2,3\) occur \(83,17,1\) times.
