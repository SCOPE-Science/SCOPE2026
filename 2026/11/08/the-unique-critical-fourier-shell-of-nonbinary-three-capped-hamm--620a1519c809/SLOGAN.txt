For every alphabet of size \(q\ge3\) and every word length \(n\ge4\), clipping Hamming distance at three gives a unique sharp negative-type threshold determined by the fixed critical Fourier degree \(\lfloor((q-1)n+3)/q\rfloor\). The full endpoint eigenspace, minimal Euclidean embedding dimension and a residue-periodic second-order threshold law are explicit.
