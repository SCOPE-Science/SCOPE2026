For the quadratic Collatz map with odd branch \(n(n+5)/2\), bounded positive orbits have odd part precisely \(1\) or \(2^m-5\) for some \(m\ge3\); all other orbits diverge. The sole nonterminal odd entry is \(1\mapsto3\), and the bounded starting values have counting function \(\tfrac12(\log_2 X)^2+O(\log(X+2))\).
