Among finite Heisenberg Lie algebras, linear local-automorphism rigidity occurs **only** for \(H_1(\mathbb F_2)\), whose local-automorphism and automorphism groups both have order \(24\). In dimension \(2m+1\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), their exact index is \(q^{m(m-1)}\prod_{i=1}^{m}(q^{2i-1}-1)\), yielding a sharp positive-characteristic boundary of the complex nilpotent existence theorem.
