## correctness

PASS

For odd prime powers q≡1 (mod 8), char≠3, the 48 coordinate-pairing lines have exactly 24 fourfold and 192 twofold Fq intersection points; 48(q−9) remaining simple rational points gives |U(Fq)|=48q−216. At F17, fourth powers {0,±1,±4} with zero four-term sum pair into additive opposites, giving X(F17)=U(F17) and 600 points. Independent fourth-power convolution over F17,F25,F41,F49 gives (#X,N0)=(600,0),(1112,128),(2520,768),(3480,1344). The K3 Weil bound #X>=q²+1−22q makes N0>=q²−70q+217>0 for q>=73, and all smaller admissible q are listed. Note excluded characteristic 3 and fixed split lines; finite examples alone are not the infinite proof.
