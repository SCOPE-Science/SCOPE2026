For each prime power \(q\equiv1\pmod8\) outside characteristic three, the \(48\) coordinate-pairing lines on the Fermat quartic have precisely \(24\) fourfold and \(192\) twofold rational intersection points, covering \(48q-216\) points in total. Every rational point lies on these lines **if and only if \(q=17\)**; the result singles out one fully line-covered K3 quartic among an infinite class of finite fields.
