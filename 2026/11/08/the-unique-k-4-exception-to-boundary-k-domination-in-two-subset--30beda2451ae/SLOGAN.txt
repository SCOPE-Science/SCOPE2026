At the exact boundary \(n=2k+3\), minimum ordinary \(k\)-dominating sets of the two-subset Kneser graph are the previously known triangle-plus-matching sets, with one striking exception: when \(k=3\), the six edges of any \(K_4\) also work. These \(126\) exceptional sets are precisely the minimum ordinary dominating sets that fail both \(k\)-tuple and \(k\)-tuple total domination.
