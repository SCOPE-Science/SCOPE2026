## Correctness — PASS
The genus-four claim follows by six-branch Riemann--Hurwitz and the separability condition \(a^5\ne2\). The four holomorphic differentials and exact Cartier coefficient matrices are explicitly given. The block determinant is \(-3a^2(a^5-2)^2\) modulo thirteen; hence the sole rank-deficient smooth fiber is \(a=0\). There the full matrix has rank two and is nilpotent, proving \(a\)-number two and \(13\)-rank zero. The classical Jacobi-sum valuation theorem gives two Frobenius-character orbits with one and three carries per four steps, proving four \(1/4\) slopes and four \(3/4\) slopes. The standalone symbolic/carry checker reproduced the new calculations from the delivered source file. The unproved limit is the classical external valuation theorem, cited rather than rederived.

This exact classification identifies the full ordinary locus of a natural sparse deformation of a highly symmetric \((3,5)\) cyclic cover. The unique nonordinary fiber has a rank-zero but nonsupersingular Jacobian with slopes \(1/4\) and \(3/4\), making the exceptional fiber arithmetically distinguishable from the otherwise ordinary pencil. The equality between the singular Hasse locus and an enhanced-automorphism coefficient is an additional geometric feature.
