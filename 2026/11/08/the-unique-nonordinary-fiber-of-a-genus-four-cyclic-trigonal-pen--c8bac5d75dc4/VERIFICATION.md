The Cartier basis has one 1×1 block −a and a 3×3 block M(a) with determinant 3a(a⁵−2)²; independent polynomial coefficient extraction modulo13 reproduced the determinant. Hence Cartier invertible at every smooth a≠0. At a=0 its rank is 2, nilpotent block has cube zero and stable p-rank zero with a-number 2.
