For the smooth reciprocal genus-three hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^8+ax^6+bx^4+ax^2+1\) in characteristic five, three explicit elliptic Hasse factors determine every Jacobian \(5\)-rank and Cartier \(a\)-number. The rank-one locus consists of exactly three reduced coefficient points, but all three curves represent one geometric isomorphism class; rank zero is impossible in this family.
