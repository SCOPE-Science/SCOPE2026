Over \(\mathbb F_7\), the smooth plane cubic \(XY^2-X^2Z-2YZ^2=0\) is tangent-filling, and every smooth tangent-filling cubic is rationally projectively equivalent to it. Its rational tangents cover all 57 projective points even though the curve has no rational flex, and 3 is the least possible degree of an irreducible tangent-filling plane curve.
