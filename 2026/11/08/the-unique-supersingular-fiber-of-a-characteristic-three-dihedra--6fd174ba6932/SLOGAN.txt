In characteristic \(3\), the smooth dihedral genus-four family \(y^2=x^{10}+a x^5+1\) jumps directly from ordinary 3-rank \(4\) to supersingular 3-rank \(0\) only at \(a=0\); the exceptional Jacobian has \(a\)-number \(2\), not \(4\). Its marked Hasse determinant is \(-a^2\), and the quotient parameter \(u=a^2\) makes the exceptional divisor simple.
