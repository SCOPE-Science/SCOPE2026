For two alternating unit rank-one bilinear games, the full three-singular-value spectrum of equal-step extragradient's two-step map depends only on the absolute payoff-row overlap. The unique globally best common step for worst-case one-period Euclidean contraction is \(h=1/\sqrt2\) for nonparallel rows, with exact optimal factor \((\sqrt{48+c^2}+c)/8\); this does not claim the asymptotically fastest step.
