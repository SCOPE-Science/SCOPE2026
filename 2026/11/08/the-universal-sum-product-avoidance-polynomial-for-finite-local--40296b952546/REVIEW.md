The exact graph equivalence uses loops to handle repeated summands/factors; the four-vertex path argument handles nonunits without cancelling them; units with unit complements carry the anharmonic six-term action. All possible stabilizers are checked, and simple roots of the quadratic lift uniquely from the finite residue field because the derivative is invertible and the maximal ideal is nilpotent. The component inventory accounts for every vertex; independent-set polynomials and leading coefficients then establish both the enumerator and extremal counts for all stipulated rings. The attached verifier checks the full polynomial for multiple structurally distinct finite rings and directly enumerates small-ring subsets. Unchecked computer cases are not used to infer the theorem.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a motivated extension of the recent sharp finite-field sum-product avoidance problem to finite local rings, where zero divisors obstruct the field argument and create a new class of path components. The complete cardinality enumerator, exact maximal-set multiplicity, and structural universality across nonisomorphic local rings are natural classification invariants, not merely a numerical spot check or a new proof of the field case. Distinct behavior of the order-25 field versus nonfield rings illustrates mathematical sensitivity to residue-field size.
