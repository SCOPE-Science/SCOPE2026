For every finite commutative local ring with residue characteristic at least five, the generating polynomial of subsets avoiding \(1\) in both their pairwise sums and products factors universally into two exceptional factors, a nilpotent four-vertex-path factor and unit-orbit six-cycle factors. This determines every cardinality count and gives exactly \(3^{(M-1)/2}2^{(N-2M-3-\delta)/6+\delta/2}\) maximum sets, where \(N=|R|\), \(M=|\mathfrak m|\), and \(\delta=2\) when \(N/M\equiv1\pmod3\), otherwise \(0\).
