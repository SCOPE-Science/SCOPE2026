For the eight-vertex Wagner graph, the named three-colour recolouring graph consists of two disjoint \(24\)-cycles, whereas adding a fourth colour creates a connected, nonregular graph of \(2160\) states with radius and diameter both exactly \(12\). Every four-colouring has eccentricity \(12\), and exactly \(5160\) unordered pairs attain that distance; the known colouring counts are not new.
