For the odd-support \((r-1)\)-scalar quotients of additive curves \(y^r-y=xR(x)\), the marked pole has an exact coefficient-independent Weierstrass gap staircase. Its numerical-semigroup type is \((r-1)/2\), and its positive Weierstrass weight has a closed formula for every odd prime power \(r\ge5\) and height \(h\ge2\).
