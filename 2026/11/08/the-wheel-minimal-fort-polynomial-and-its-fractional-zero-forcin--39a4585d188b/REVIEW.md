# Mathematical review of the revised wheel fort-transversal claim

## Correctness
The minimum rim membership of a hub-free inclusion-minimal fort is ceil(n/2), by maximal cycle independent sets. A hub-containing minimal fort requires at least ceil(n/3) rim vertices, and equality is attained, including the n=4 adjacent-pair exception. Rotational averaging reduces the fort-cover LP to the two sharp inequalities a*u >= 1 and v+b*u >= 1. Their exact optimizer gives Z*=1+(n-b)/a. Explicit disjoint forts prove the packing number two, and a forcing chain gives ordinary Z=3. Direct independently coded subset enumeration checks n=3,...,9; the proof rather than the finite tests handles all n.

## Originality and prior implication
The full size-refined minimal-fort polynomial, rational hub-fort generating function and its growth rate were already established in the complete wheel-minimal-fort theorem dated November 2, 2026. They are **not novel**, and the original combined research statement must not be passed unchanged. The corrected theorem retains only the all-order LP optimum, fort-packing law and strict integrality-gap classification. Those are not corollaries of the earlier enumeration alone without solving the fractional LP, and no reviewed source establishes the same exact formula. The broad fort-hypergraph LP and lower-bound methods of Cameron et al. are prior; Becker et al. supply wheel structural and partial-count results. An unindexed fraction-formula source remains a specific residual originality risk.

## Mathematical value and limitations
This is a natural graph-family instance of a distinct fort-hypergraph relaxation with a nontrivial fractional gap and exact equality cases. The value lies in the sharp LP and packing separation, not in re-computing the known polynomial. Its scope is only wheels and the particular fort-cover fractional parameter; no general graph-family or other fractional forcing result is inferred. The numerical checks are corroboration only.
