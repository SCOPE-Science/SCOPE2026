For every wheel \(W_{n+1}=K_1\vee C_n\) with \(n\ge3\), an exact rational cardinality generating function resolves the previously listed hub-containing minimal-fort sequence and shows that total minimal-fort counts grow as \(\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\approx1.40126836794\). The fort packing number is \(2\), the fractional zero-forcing number is \(1+(n-\lceil n/3\rceil)/\lceil n/2\rceil\), and the ordinary zero-forcing number is \(3\).
