# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The construction and center normalization follow from its complete nonzero binary generator list. The support identity reduces the radius and its equality directions to the established sharp scalar Khinchin inequality, with an explicit check that a nonzero deterministic shift is incompatible with equality. Pairwise opposite tangent facets force every center onto the diagonal. A resonance-fan decomposition proves the finite exact formula for the diagonal endpoint; finiteness and the radius equality classification prove its strict positivity. The bundled integer-cofactor checker exhaustively tests all arrangement rays for dimensions two through five with rigorous radical enclosures. The general dimension assertion does not rely on extrapolated finite computations.

## Originality — PASS, historical priority provisional

Deza–Hao–Pournin supply the White Whale construction and study generators, vertices, edges and resonance arrangement; they do not report an incenter locus. The Eskenazis–Nayar–Tkocz discussion of Szarek's sharp inequality implies the centered inradius after the support reduction, so that radius is explicitly credited as a consequence of prior analysis. The assessed additional statement is the complete nondegenerate segment of Euclidean incenters in every dimension, the finite primitive-resonance endpoint expression, and exhaustive centered and low-dimensional endpoint tangent facets. Searched geometry and inequality descriptions have not shown a stronger statement that implies this full locus. An equivalent formulation in another source remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The White Whale is a natural, exceptionally large combinatorial zonotope: conventional vertex enumeration is expensive even in modest dimensions. Establishing a positive-dimensional optimal-center locus in every dimension, determining its only possible direction, and reducing endpoints to a finite exact resonance-ray computation identifies a geometric invariant not supplied by known counts of vertices or edges. The low-dimensional exact endpoint radicals and the sharp endpoint-contact counts make the center freedom quantitative, not merely existential. This is a complete metric center classification despite an unsimplified general endpoint.

## Limits and relevant sources

The current proof does not establish a uniform simple expression for the segment's half-length beyond dimension five, and no earlier-work search establishes absolute novelty. Closest examined sources: Deza–Hao–Pournin, *Sizing the White Whale* (arXiv:2205.13309v1); Eskenazis–Nayar–Tkocz, *Distributional stability of the Szarek and Ball inequalities* (2023, DOI 10.1007/s00208-023-02669-9). Nearby known graphical-zonotope radius results concern a different generator class.
