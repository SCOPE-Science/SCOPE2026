The White Whale zonotope has inradius \(2^{d-2}/\sqrt2\) and, for every \(d\ge2\), a nondegenerate one-dimensional segment of all optimal Euclidean ball centers. Its endpoints admit an exact finite resonance-arrangement formula; the centered maximal ball touches precisely the \(d(d-1)\) facets with normals \((e_i-e_j)/\sqrt2\), and the endpoint formula simplifies to explicit radicals and yields all additional endpoint-facet contacts through dimension five.
