For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}\setminus M\) with \(n\ge3\), standard zero forcing succeeds exactly when each shore initially contains at most two white vertices, except for the configuration of the same two missing indices on both shores. Consequently \(\mathcal Z(\operatorname{Cr}_n;z)=z^{2n-4}[(z^2+nz+\binom n2)^2-\binom n2]\); all minimal forcing sets have cardinality \(2n-4\), and for \(n\ge5\) the polynomial has no nonzero real roots.
