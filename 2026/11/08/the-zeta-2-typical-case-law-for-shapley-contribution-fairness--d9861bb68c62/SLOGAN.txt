For one divisible good and iid uniform agent productivities, the exact expected minimum Shapley-fairness approximation factor is \(\sum_{j=1}^n j^{-2}\), converging almost surely and in mean to \(\pi^2/6\). For the power family \(U_i^s\), the factor increases pathwise with \(s\) and has an exact finite-population gamma-ratio mean and an explicit limiting digamma curve.
