The symmetric six-coordinate Burkhardt quartic over \(\mathbb F_2\) is a quadratic twist of the standard model with a different Frobenius spectrum: its zeta function is \(\frac{(1-2T)^9(1+2T)^{20}}{(1-T)(1-8T)(1-4T)^6(1+4T)^{10}}\). All fifteen base-field points are singular, although the variety gains smooth rational points over extensions.
