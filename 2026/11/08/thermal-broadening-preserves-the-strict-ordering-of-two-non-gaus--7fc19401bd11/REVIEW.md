# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The stationary generator identity reconstructs even moments through degree six in the exact model and agrees with the unbroadened moment recurrence. The differentiation factors are polynomial identities with denominators strictly positive on the stated domain. Every critical-point polynomial has exactly one positive root by Descartes' rule and endpoint signs; this is a global argument for all nonnegative thermal ratios. The strict ordering follows from a temperature-independent cumulant-ratio derivative, whose numerator is provably positive throughout the interval containing the fourth-order maximizer. A runnable algebra checker independently reconstructs these results, but finite numerical cases alone are not used as universal evidence.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Guéneau–Majumdar–Schehr (arXiv:2306.09453; original public date 2023-06-15) already give the Poisson-reset Brownian force, the Kesten recursion and exact stationary moments through order six. Di Bello et al. (arXiv:2309.13424) derive a thermally broadened *compound-Poisson instantaneous-kick* model and its optimal fourth-order non-Gaussianity rate; that model is different and does not produce the present two-cumulant ordering. Olsen (Phys. Rev. E 108, 044120) studies resets of position rather than force. No inspected full primary text states the all-temperature pair of uniquely ordered fourth/sixth optima. The new result is the joint optimization theorem and invariant-ratio proof, not the established moment formulas or Gaussian convolution rule. Earlier formulas facilitate an implicit derivation, and undiscovered explicit coverage remains a publication-priority risk. The Olsen material was inspected at abstract/conclusion level only.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Standardized fourth- and sixth-order cumulants probe different aspects of non-Gaussian stationary fluctuations. The globally unique, thermally tunable optimal force-reset rates and their uniform strict ordering answer a natural experimental parameter-choice question. The cancellation of the thermal variance in the higher-cumulant ratio is a structural explanation of why the optima never cross, not a numerical coincidence. The exact limits give meaningful robustness under arbitrarily strong but finite independent thermal diffusion. No statement is made about practical performance gains in a particular experiment.
