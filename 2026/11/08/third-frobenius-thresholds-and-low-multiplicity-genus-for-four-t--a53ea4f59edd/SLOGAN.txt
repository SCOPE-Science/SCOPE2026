The third generalized Frobenius threshold for \((a,a+d,a+2d,a+3d)\), with \(a\ge4\) and \(\gcd(a,d)=1\), has an explicit three-branch formula determined by \(a\bmod3\) and a transition at \(d=a\). A complete residue threshold law also gives the exact number of integers admitting at most two representations.
