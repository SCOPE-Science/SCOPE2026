For every tree on \(n\ge6\) vertices, the third intrinsic volume of its standard graphical zonotope is an explicit positive linear combination of its two- and three-edge matching counts and four-vertex path count. Among all such trees, stars uniquely minimize and paths uniquely maximize it, with an exact gap and limiting path-to-star ratio \(\sqrt2\); the invariant even distinguishes some trees with identical degree sequences.
