# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
All model conventions, rates, cycle orders and limits are explicit. The proof derives exact first-event rational recurrences, proves the path coefficient identities for all sizes using degree-two polynomial induction, and then obtains the ring formula and three exceptional cases. A separate subset-mask exact algorithm agrees with the first-event path method in all tested cases. The remainder is pointwise in ring size; no uniformity is asserted.

## Originality: PASS on examined evidence, provisional
Penrose--Sudbury provides a general blocking adsorption framework, not the evaluated third-order alternating-rate ring response. Penrose--Rosoman develops two-dimensional biased-checkerboard percolation and not ring jamming asymptotics. Fan--Percus develops generic series/duality ideas, which may enable calculation but no retrieved result supplies the precise length-stabilized coefficients. The later do Amaral--dos Santos AB process has a different exclusion rule. Searches for the coefficients, rate alternation, finite rings and equivalent formulations in published results and accessible records yielded no stronger or identical coverage. General methods are credited, not claimed novel. Earlier unindexed calculations remain a priority risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS
A rate contrast is a natural physical control parameter in irreversible adsorption. The explicit third-order term gives a reproducible high-contrast kinetic benchmark over an infinite set of finite rings, and identifies the exact order-ten point where all three coefficients stabilize per two-site cell. The shorter-loop anomalies distinguish local finite topology, not arbitrary selected graph sizes. This is a narrow quantitative structural result, not a phase-transition claim or a theory of all rates.

## Boundaries
The proof does not establish uniformity in ring size, stochastic dominance, time-dependent behavior or all-order expansion. Historical uniqueness remains provisional and independent validation has not occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
