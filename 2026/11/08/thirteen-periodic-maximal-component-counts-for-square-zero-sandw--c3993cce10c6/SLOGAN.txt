For ordered pairs of matrices obeying \(P^2=P\), \(N^2=0\), and \(PNP=0\), every rank-refined finite-field population has a closed Gaussian-binomial formula, while the reduced representation variety has dimension \(\lfloor 11n^2/13\rfloor\). The number of its maximal-dimensional irreducible components is thirteen-periodic in \(n\ge13\), a phenomenon created by the third rank parameter \(\operatorname{rank}(NP)\).
