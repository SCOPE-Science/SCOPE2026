The twelve-vertex Chvátal graph has a dominance complex of integral homotopy type \(\operatorname{Dom}(G)\simeq\bigvee^{13} S^6\). Its full dihedral automorphism action on rational sixth homology decomposes as \(L_{1,1}\oplus2L_{1,-1}\oplus L_{-1,1}\oplus3L_{-1,-1}\oplus3E\), where \(E\) is the standard two-dimensional dihedral representation.
