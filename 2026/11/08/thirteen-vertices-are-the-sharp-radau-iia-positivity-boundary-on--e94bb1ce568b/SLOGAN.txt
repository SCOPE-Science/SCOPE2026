For two-stage Radau IIA heat diffusion on every conference graph, nine vertices are unconditionally positivity-safe but thirteen are not: all off-diagonal transitions remain positive, while diagonal entries become negative on exactly one bounded timestep interval whenever the graph has at least thirteen vertices. The two endpoints have sharp limits \(t h_-(t)\to3/2\) and \(h_+(t)\to4\) for graphs of order \(4t+1\).
