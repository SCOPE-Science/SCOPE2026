The standalone standard-library `verify.py` checks the algebraic endpoint-neighbor ratio against directly inverted rational path matrices for small sizes, exact rational enclosures of the irrational comparison threshold, positivity of every Bernstein control coefficient on four dyadic intervals for the degree-125 size-32 polynomial, and the exact size-33 rational negative witness. A passing execution prints `VERIFY_OK`.

The proof of strict monotonicity in path size is analytic, using hyperbolic derivative inequalities. Verification does not claim that finite matrix experiments prove statements for arbitrary graph order or that the full end-step matrix is entrywise positive.
