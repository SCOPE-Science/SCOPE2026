For smooth characteristic-two genus-six cyclic trigonal curves \(y^3=x^7+\sum_{i=0}^6a_ix^i\), the entire Cartier-rank sequence, and hence the Jacobian's \(2\)-rank, belongs to exactly three explicit coefficient strata. In particular, \(2\)-rank zero is equivalent to \(a_3=a_6^4\) and \(a_2=a_6^5\), a smooth irreducible codimension-two locus in the squarefree coefficient space.
