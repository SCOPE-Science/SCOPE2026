For two \(n\)-vertex complete layers joined by a perfect matching, the unique resistance-minimizing conductance allocation funds every edge but exhibits three distinct interlayer-to-intralayer ratios: linear in \(n\) for matching price \(0<c<1\), order \(n^{2/3}\) at \(c=1\), and order \(\sqrt n\) for \(c>1\). The transition exactly separates a positive asymptotic interlayer budget share from budget shares vanishing as \(n^{-1/3}\) and \(n^{-1/2}\), respectively.
