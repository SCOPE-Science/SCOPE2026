For the stationary Feres random billiard in the unit circle with roughness angle \(\pi/n\), the radius of the almost-sure central caustic is uniform on \([0,\sin(\pi/(2n))]\) when \(n\) is odd, uniform on \([0,\sin(\pi/n)]\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and has an explicit nonuniform density when \(n\equiv0\pmod4\). This mod-four classification also gives exact expected excluded-disk areas, exposing a factor-two parity difference in the essential maximal caustic radius.
