The genus-six cyclic trigonal curves \(y^3=f(x)\) with monic squarefree septic \(f\) in characteristic two have precisely three Cartier-iterate profiles, determined by \(\Delta=a_2+a_3a_6\) and \(E=a_3+a_6^4\). Their p-ranks \(4,2,0\) occur on an open stratum, a smooth irreducible divisor, and a smooth irreducible codimension-two locus, respectively, while the first Cartier rank is always \(4\).
