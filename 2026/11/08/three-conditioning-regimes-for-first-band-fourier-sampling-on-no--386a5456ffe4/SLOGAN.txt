First-degree Fourier sampling on a full nonbinary Hamming ball has three sharp stability regimes: at subcritical radius density its condition number grows like \(n^{3/2}\) with an explicit alphabet-dependent coefficient, throughout the binomial central-limit window it converges to a universal truncated-Gaussian profile, and above the binomial mean it tends to one. The result holds for every fixed finite abelian alphabet of order at least three, independent of its group structure.
