For the categorical product \(G=K_2\times K_3\times K_3\), the second forest-filtration complex \(\mathcal F_2(G)\) is \(6\)-connected and has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{17}\), \(\mathbb Z^{36}\), and \(\mathbb Z^6\) in dimensions \(7\), \(8\), and \(9\), with no other homology or torsion. Two exhaustive integral discrete-Morse reductions certify this natural unresolved finite case; the full unstable homotopy type is not determined.
