The dominance complex of the dodecahedral graph has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{12}\), \(\mathbb Z\), and \(\mathbb Z^{10}\) in degrees nine, ten, and eleven respectively, with no torsion or other reduced homology. Two complete acyclic discrete-Morse computations establish eight-connectivity and the integral groups, without asserting a sphere-wedge homotopy type.
