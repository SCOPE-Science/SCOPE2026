The multipath complex of the regular seven-vertex consecutive-difference tournament has torsion-free reduced integral homology of ranks \(13,43,17\) in three consecutive degrees \(3,4,5\). Its natural cyclic rotation fixes rational homology subspaces of dimensions \(1,7,5\) and acts with nonidentity traces \((-1,1,3)\), respectively; no wedge-of-spheres homotopy type is asserted.
