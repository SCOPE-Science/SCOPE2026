The reduced variety of commuting square-zero pairs in the strictly upper-triangular four-by-four algebra has exactly three geometrically irreducible components over every field: an affine eight-space and two seven-dimensional rank-one determinantal cones with two free coordinates. Their intersections have dimensions \(6,5,6\), yielding a concrete non-equidimensional commuting scheme even though the unrestricted triangular commuting variety behaves differently.
