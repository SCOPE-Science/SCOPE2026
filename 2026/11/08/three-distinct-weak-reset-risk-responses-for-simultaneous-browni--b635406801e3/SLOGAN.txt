For synchronously resetting Brownian target searchers, weak resetting worsens mean completion time but improves both absolute and relative fluctuations for \(N=7,8,9\); at \(N=10,11\), only relative dispersion improves; at \(N=12\), all three statistics worsen. These strictly local three-way responses follow from certified first-three-moment bounds, not an assumption of globally optimal reset rates.
