The third centered moment of a uniformly random Euclidean projection width of any canonical graphical zonotope is exactly determined by its counts of edges, adjacent edge pairs, three-stars, three-edge paths, and triangles. The triangular prism and \(K_{3,3}\) have identical first two width moments but distinct third moments, with exact difference \(12(k_t-3k_p)/r_3<0\) in prism-minus-\(K_{3,3}\) order.
