In the randomly expanding-or-contracting Pelikan interval map, the way a rare excursion reaches an exceptionally deep dyadic shell changes sharply at \(p=2/3\): its successful initial depth is tight below the threshold, uniformly spread over a fraction of the target depth at the threshold, and has a two-sided geometric overshoot above it. The normalized waiting time is asymptotically exponential and independent of that entrance mechanism.
