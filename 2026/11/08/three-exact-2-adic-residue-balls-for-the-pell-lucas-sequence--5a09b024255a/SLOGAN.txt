The Pell–Lucas values have an exact three-ball 2-adic image: \((2+16\mathbb Z_2)\cup(14+16\mathbb Z_2)\cup(6+64\mathbb Z_2)\), occupying exactly \(9/64\) of all residue classes modulo every sufficiently high power of two. Two isometric odd-index branches and one quadratic even-index branch account for every residue.
