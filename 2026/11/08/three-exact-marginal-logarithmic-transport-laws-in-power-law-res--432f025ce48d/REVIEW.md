## Correctness — PASS

The specified per-direction hopping normalization reproduces the no-reset current \(j_0(a)=e^{-2a}I_0(2a)\) and its primitive \(J_0(a)\). The exact renewal identities are proved rather than inferred from experiments. At the third index the expected net current need not be monotone; the proof therefore instead uses a finite-mean renewal-density limit and an explicit split of the borderline convolution, yielding the exact logarithmic constant.
