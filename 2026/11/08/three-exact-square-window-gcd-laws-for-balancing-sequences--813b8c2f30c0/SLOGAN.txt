For every positive window length \(k\), the GCDs of all consecutive squared balancing, Lucas-balancing, and Lucas-cobalancing sums are exactly \(\gcd(k,B_k)/2\) (even \(k\)) or \(\gcd(k,B_k)\) (odd \(k\)) for balancing numbers, \(\gcd(k,B_k)\) for Lucas-balancing numbers, and \(\gcd(k,B_k)\) (even \(k\)) or \(\gcd(k,3B_k)\) (odd \(k\)) for Lucas-cobalancing numbers. These identities prove two relationships previously reported only as computational observations in a published open question.
