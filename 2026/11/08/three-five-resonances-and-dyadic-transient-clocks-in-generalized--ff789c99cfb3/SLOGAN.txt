On generalized Petersen graphs \(GP(n,2)\), binary neighbor-parity updates lose information exactly when the circumference is divisible by three or five; the entire transient population distribution follows from two dyadic Jordan-block lengths. The longest settling time is \(2^{\nu_2(n)+1}\) for multiples of three, \(2^{\nu_2(n)}\) for other multiples of five, and zero otherwise.
