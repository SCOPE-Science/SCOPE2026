The local-ring calculation supplies the proof for every integer circumference at least five. The included standard-library Python programs independently check binary adjacency-matrix ranks and every tested power for circumferences 5–65, the determinant-polynomial gcd criterion for circumferences 5–500, and exhaustive first-entry histograms for circumferences 5–8 (87,040 labelled states in total). Both programs run without extra packages. Captured outputs are preserved in the package. Finite checks are corroborative, not an infinite proof.

The characteristic-zero spectral theorem in Gera–Stănică has been compared by implication against this characteristic-two Jordan result. It supplies the block-circulant matrix formula but not nilpotent Jordan lengths. No independent audit has yet been performed.
