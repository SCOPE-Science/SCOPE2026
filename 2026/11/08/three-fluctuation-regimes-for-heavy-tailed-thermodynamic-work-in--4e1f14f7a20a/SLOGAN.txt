For Brownian exploration cycles followed by exactly thermalizing harmonic resets, a power-tailed renewal clock produces a two-sided stable thermodynamic-work limit for \(1<q<2\), a critical \(\sqrt{t\log t}\) Gaussian limit at \(q=2\), and an ordinary \(\sqrt t\) Gaussian limit for \(q>2\), all with explicit physical-time constants. The negative stable tail arises from compensating the random cycle durations, even though individual-cycle work has an exponentially light negative tail.
