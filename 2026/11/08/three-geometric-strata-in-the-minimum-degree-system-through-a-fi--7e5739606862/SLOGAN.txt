Every minimum-degree geometric curve through all \(\mathbb F_q\)-points of the projective plane is a Frobenius determinant \(\det(a,X,X^{(q)})=0\). The coefficient plane has exactly three strata: a rational star, an \(\mathbb F_q\)-line plus a rational cuspidal component, and, off all \(\mathbb F_q\)-lines, an integral rational curve with one cusp of multiplicity \(q\); these are also the reduced curves underlying every polar of the Moore arrangement.
