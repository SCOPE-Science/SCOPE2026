For the canonical logistic polynomial \(L(x)=4x(1-x)\), staged factored binary16 arithmetic has one primitive forty-cycle, the equally valid expanded evaluation has primitive cycles of lengths \(4,17,25,30\), and a once-rounded exact evaluation has only one nontrivial five-cycle. An exhaustive exact-integer classification determines the basins of every cycle on all \(15,361\) binary16 states in \([0,1]\).
