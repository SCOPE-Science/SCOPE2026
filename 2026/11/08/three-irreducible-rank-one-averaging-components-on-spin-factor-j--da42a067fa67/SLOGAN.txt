For every spin-factor Jordan algebra of bilinear dimension \(n\ge3\) over a field of characteristic not two, the reduced variety of rank-at-most-one averaging operators has exactly three geometrically irreducible components of dimensions \(n+1\), \(n\), and \(2n-3\), with an explicit isotropic-quadratic boundary intersection. Over every odd finite field, the full operator census is a three-term formula whose value distinguishes the discriminant classes of the bilinear form.
