For a three-lane product-stationary exclusion gas with one particle per longitudinal column on average, optimizing the *signed* total current across conserved density allocations yields sharp thresholds \(\gamma=-2A\) and \(\gamma=-6A/7\): the optimum shifts from one occupied lane to two balanced lanes and then to three balanced lanes. At the positive-current two-to-three transition, the competing allocations coexist as strict local maxima over \(-A<\gamma<-4A/5\); this is an external allocation transition, not a spontaneous dynamical lane change.
