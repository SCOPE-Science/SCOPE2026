For every ternary prime-power modulus \(N=3^k\), the threefold convolution of the parabola indicator takes exactly the values \(0,N,2N\), with multiplicities \(N(N-1)/6\), \(N(2N+1)/3\), and \(N(N-1)/6\). This determines the exact triple-sumset size \(N(5N+1)/6\) and the unweighted quadratic Fourier sixth moment \(N^3(4N-1)/3\).
