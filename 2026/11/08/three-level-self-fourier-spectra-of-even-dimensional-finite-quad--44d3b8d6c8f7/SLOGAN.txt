The ambient-unitarily-normalized Fourier transform restricted to both input and output on any nondegenerate even-dimensional quadratic zero cone over \(\mathbb F_q\) has only the singular values \(1\), \(q^{-1/2}\), and \(q^{-1}\), with explicit multiplicities, in either quadratic type and every characteristic. Its sharp inversion condition number is exactly \(q\), regardless of dimension, and the multiplicity of the top singular value counts every perfectly cone-concentrated Fourier signal.
