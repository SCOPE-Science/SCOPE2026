For every even-dimensional quadratic Boolean graph whose alternating polar matrix squares to the identity, its principal Walsh–Fourier matrix has exactly three singular values \(\sqrt N(\sqrt3+1)/2\), \(\sqrt N\), and \(\sqrt N(\sqrt3-1)/2\), with multiplicities \((N-1)/3\), \((N+2)/3\), and \((N-1)/3\). Its condition number is exactly \(2+\sqrt3\), independent of dimension, and the full determinant magnitude is explicit.
