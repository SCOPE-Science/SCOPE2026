For three noncentral cyclic directions in the finite Heisenberg group, the spectrum of the summed line averages is controlled by one quadratic Gauss sum and has recoverable rank \(3p^2-2\) for every odd prime \(p\ge5\). At \(p=3\), a central-lift relation causes an additional six-dimensional kernel, and the \(108\) direction triples split exactly \(36\) versus \(72\) between the two ranks.
