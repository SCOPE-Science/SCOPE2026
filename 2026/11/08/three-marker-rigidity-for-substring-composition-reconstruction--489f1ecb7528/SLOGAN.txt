Every binary word with exactly three ones is uniquely recoverable up to reversal from the length-resolved substring-composition counts that contain zero, two, or three ones; the entire one-one channel can be omitted. The first nonreverse ambiguity requires at least four ones, and the exact number of distinguishable length-\(n\) three-one words is \(\bigl(\binom n3+\mathbf{1}_{\{n\text{ odd}\}}(n-1)/2\bigr)/2\).
