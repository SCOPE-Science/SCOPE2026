In a \(3\times3\) Mallows-correlated stable-marriage market, the first loss of exact assortative stability occurs at order \(\phi\), the first appearance of multiple stable matchings at order \(\phi^2\), and the first possibility of three stable matchings at order \(\phi^5\). Exact rational formulas show that increasing Mallows dispersion strictly reduces assortative stability and strictly increases both multiplicity events for \(0<\phi<1\).
