A binary word with exactly three \(1\)s is privileged precisely when its endpoint zero runs agree and either dominate both interior zero runs strictly or the two interior runs are equal and at least as long. The nonpalindromic members are in a two-to-one bijection with nonnegative solutions of \(2r+3s+4t=n-8\), giving generating function \(2z^8/((1-z^2)(1-z^3)(1-z^4))\) and a complete all-length count.
