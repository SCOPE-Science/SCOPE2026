The unique smallest Euclidean enclosing ball of the octopus arbor polytope \(Q_{n,k}\) has three exact regimes, distinguished solely by the number \(\ell=n-k\) of uncapped coordinates. Its number of sphere-contacting vertices collapses from \(2^{n-1}+1\) for \(\ell=1\) to exactly three for \(\ell=2\), and is exactly \(\ell\) for every \(\ell\ge3\), with explicit center and radius in each case.
