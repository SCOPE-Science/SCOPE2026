For Haar-random hyperplanes cutting a regular simplex along an interior centroid-to-vertex shift, the entire face vector has an exact one-count Gaussian representation. Its high-dimensional fluctuations undergo a sharp three-regime transition: a continuously varying chi-square law at shifts of order \(n^{-1/2}\), a mesoscopic chi-square scale, and a nonconcentrating macroscopic limit \(\Phi(hZ)\Phi(-hZ)\) for normalized vertices.
