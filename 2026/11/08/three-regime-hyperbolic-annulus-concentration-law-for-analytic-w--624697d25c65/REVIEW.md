# Scientific review: Three-regime hyperbolic-annulus concentration law for analytic wavelets

## Correctness — PASS
The proof explicitly identifies the beta interval eigenvalues and the previously known exact maximizing-mode index. The index has a relative asymptotic uniform over arbitrary positive width ratios, since the logarithmic denominator has a uniform first-order expansion. The beta-density converges locally uniformly when the rescaled index tends to a finite rate, proving both the shrinking-interval thin law and nontrivial critical-width gamma mass. The beta--gamma representation proves the wide-width norm limit of one even when the width grows arbitrarily fast. The standalone finite checker passes 618 assertions; these are not substitutes for the asymptotic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with strong historical qualification
The fixed-width annulus exact mode staircase and its vanishing sharp norm law already occur in earlier annulus research; these are explicitly treated as prerequisites, not new conclusions. Ramos and Tilli's complete 2022/2023 wavelet Faber--Krahn article proves disk shape optimization and analytic-wavelet reduction, not the joint-growth annulus concentration law. Abreu--Dörfler establish radial monomial diagonalization but not the three-regime norm crossover. Searches and comparisons of relevant prior statements have not shown that the nontrivial critical-width limit and wide-width saturation were asserted. Nevertheless an earlier uniform incomplete-beta or radial Toeplitz result could cover them; priority is provisional.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The joint-scaling law classifies a natural spectral concentration invariant for every possible relative annulus width: a small-width vanishing optimum, a finite nonzero fraction at critical width, and nearly perfect concentration at broad hyperbolic width. The transition is qualitative (zero / strictly intermediate / one), with an explicit gamma mass and an optimal mode scale. This is substantively different from the fixed-area asymptotic and applies directly to wavelet aperture design; the known staircase is not counted separately.

## Limits and residual risks
The result applies only to radial centered annuli and analytic wavelets at fixed weight. It gives asymptotic limits, not uniform rates, and has not been independently validated. Broad beta-probability asymptotics may imply these formulas without matching terminology.
