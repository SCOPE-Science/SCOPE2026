Analytic-wavelet concentration on a near-boundary hyperbolic annulus has a sharp three-regime norm law controlled by its width divided by its inner-boundary scale: the optimum tends to zero, a nontrivial gamma-interval mass, or one. This includes growing-width annuli and identifies the asymptotically optimal Laguerre mode scale; the exact finite-index staircase is a previously known ingredient rather than the new claim.
