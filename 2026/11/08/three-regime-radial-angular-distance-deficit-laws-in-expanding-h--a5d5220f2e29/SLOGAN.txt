For two isotropic points in a growing hyperbolic ball, their distance deficit converges to two radial exponential clocks plus the negative logarithm of an independent spherical beta variable. In dimensions at least three, its tail switches sharply from \(t e^{-\alpha t}\) to \(t^2e^{-at}\) to \(e^{-at}\) as the radial rate crosses \(a=(d-1)/2\); in odd dimensions the volume-uniform law is exactly a finite sum of independent exponential variables.
