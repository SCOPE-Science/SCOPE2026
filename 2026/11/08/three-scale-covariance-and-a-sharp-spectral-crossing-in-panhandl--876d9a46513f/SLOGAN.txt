The uniform vertex measure of every nontrivial panhandle matroid base polytope has an exact three-block covariance spectrum, with its smallest mode always separating the handle from the tail and a sharp crossover between the other modes at \(t=2+(r-2)/(s-r+1)\). In proportional-rank families its three principal variance scales are \(1\), \(s^{-1}\), and \(s^{-2}\), giving a quadratic growth law for its intrinsic covariance condition number.
