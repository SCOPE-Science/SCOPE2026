# Scientific review: two-mode Jacobi heat-flow stability

## Correctness
PASS. For the normalized semicircle probability measure, Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind are orthonormal and satisfy the stated Jacobi eigenvalue equation. The finite product identity produces the fourth-moment coefficients \(m+1\), \(n+1\), and \(m+1\), and opposite parity removes the cubic cross terms. The claimed sharp operator norm follows by maximizing one exact quadratic over the complete unit input circle. The strict mixed-branch square deficit, both boundary cases, and the impossibility of equal critical degree ratios are proved. Strict decay of the positive-coefficient fourth-moment expression verifies the unique contractivity time. A standalone checker independently evaluates ten degree pairs and seventy rational ratio settings exactly; the universal claims rely on the proof, not the finite checks.

## Originality
PASS, with residual priority risk. Balderrama–Urbina's classical Jacobi semigroup theory provides eigenfunction decompositions and nonsharp global hypercontractivity; it does not give exact two-mode time-dependent operator norms. Dooley–Gupta's \(SU(2)\) \(L^4\) paper primarily concerns noncentral matrix-coefficient problems. The closest published central-character result, *High-weight parity localization of central fourth moments on \(SU(2)\)*, includes a general exact finite intersection expansion but its sharp optimization theorem is asymptotic for fixed offset packets; it does not prove finite-degree semigroup-weighted mode selection, including arbitrarily wide gaps and the two exact spectral-gap thresholds. The present result is not a restatement of the asymptotic theorem, though it relies on elementary classical product identities. Published and local claim comparisons found no identical sharp statement. Unindexed weighted-character estimates remain the principal risk.

## Value
PASS. Restricting a genuine Jacobi Markov diffusion to two orthogonal modes is a natural test for improving global \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) hypercontractivity. The resulting exact all-time norm has three *distinct* natural spectral-gap regimes, including a finite interval in which diffusion produces a mixed extremizer between pure high- and pure low-mode phases, and a wide-gap transition with no mixing. This is a complete all-degree-pair classification, not an arbitrary numeric example or a bounded enumeration.

## Scientific limits
The outcome is restricted to real coefficients, opposite-parity degrees, target exponent four, and the semicircle Jacobi measure. No claim is made for same-parity pairs, arbitrary Jacobi parameters, full-spectrum input, or complex extremizers. The oldest inspected explicit online first-public lead source is dated August 25, 2006; earlier classical literature is used for context without inventing day-precise first-release dates. Independent mathematical validation is outstanding.
