For the Jacobi heat semigroup on any real opposite-parity pair of second-kind Chebyshev modes, the sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) norm has a closed three-branch formula. Depending on the ratio of the mode indices, the extremizer evolves from a pure high mode through a genuinely mixed interval to a pure low mode, or switches directly between pure modes at an exact spectral threshold.
