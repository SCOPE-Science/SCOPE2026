For a qubit repeatedly acted on by a quarter-turn phase gate and reset at arbitrary positive-integer renewal intervals of fixed mean \(\mu\), the complete stationary-purity range is exactly \([\tfrac12+f_-(\mu)/(4\mu^2),\tfrac12+f_+(\mu)/(4\mu^2)]\), with explicit piecewise-quadratic envelopes and all attaining reset laws. Three sharp clock-budget thresholds at \(\mu=3/2,2,4\) mark an optimizer-support transition, a phase-residue maximum, and the first possible complete dephasing, respectively; stationary refers to equilibrium-renewal initialization, not synchronous periodic convergence.
