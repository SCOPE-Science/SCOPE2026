The Möbius–Kantor graph has a two-independence complex homotopy equivalent to three six-spheres: \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(\operatorname{GP}(8,3))\simeq\bigvee^3 S^6\). Its canonical eightfold rotation acts on rational sixth homology as the sign representation plus a two-dimensional representation of fourth-root type, with no invariant vectors.
