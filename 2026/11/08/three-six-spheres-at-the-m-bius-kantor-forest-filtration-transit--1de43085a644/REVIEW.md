# Scientific self-review

## Correctness — PASS
Two independent bit-mask and induced-edge predicates agreed on all \(65536\) vertex subsets. Both ascending and descending elementary matchings on \(6813\) first-stage faces have \(3401\) pairs, and complete topological sorts verify acyclicity of all \(35120\) oriented Hasse incidences. The exact integral Morse matrices obtained by signed path elimination compose to zero; the degree-five boundary has a unit incidence, and the residual degree-six boundary has an explicit unit \(3\times3\) minor. CW cancellations yield the claimed *homotopy* type. Independent unreduced mod-two boundary ranks agree with reduced degree-six Betti number three. The stage-zero reduction, nullity of inclusion, and cofibration cofiber are also reconstructed; no numerical experiment is extrapolated to an infinite family.

An unindexed equivalent result is also possible.

## Value — PASS
This is the first positive forest-filtration transition of a canonical highly symmetric sixteen-vertex cubic incidence graph. The previously determined stage zero supports integral homology in dimensions three and four; stage one becomes a wedge of exactly three six-spheres. The null inclusion and complete quotient type quantify the transition, rather than recording an arbitrary slice or an Euler-characteristic coincidence. The result is graph-specific and does not assert a new family classification.
