For real cubic spherical harmonics observed on two equal antipodal caps, the maximally concentrated azimuthal order changes exactly twice, from \(m=2\) to \(m=1\) to \(m=0\), at cutoffs \(t=1/\sqrt5\) and \(t=\sqrt{3/5}\). At the first threshold five of the seven harmonic modes have exactly the same cap-energy fraction, while order \(m=3\) is strictly least concentrated for every interior cutoff.
