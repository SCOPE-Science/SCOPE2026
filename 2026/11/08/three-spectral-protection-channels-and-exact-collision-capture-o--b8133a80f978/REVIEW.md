## correctness

PASS

The one-particle eigenspaces are the constant line T, the block-constant zero-sum space V and the sum U of the internal zero-sum spaces W_a. Their eigenvalues divided by J are m(q-1), -m and 0; the six symmetric pair sums are distinct for the stated q and J. Diagonal evaluation is injective on sectors with a T-factor. For n>=3, the diagonal map on the symmetric square of the n-site zero-sum space has rank n: the products of two distinct vectors e_i-e_n isolate the nth coordinate, and squares then give the others. At n=2 it has rank one. Thus the ranks on Sym^2 U and Sym^2 V are q r_m and q; on V times W_a it is the nonzero evaluation at block a times W_a, of rank m-1. These rank counts give the three dark dimensions. For projection norms, the diagonal kernel on n sites is contained in the off-diagonal pair space and is permutation invariant, so the same squared projector norm on every normalized distinct-site pair is its dimension divided by binom(n,2), namely (n-3)/(n-1) for n>=3 (zero for n=2). In the cross-part case the U-only survival weight is (m-1)^2/m^2, the two orthogonal mixed terms together contribute 2(m-1)(q-2)/[m^2(q-1)], and the collective term contributes (q-3)/[m^2(q-1)]. In the same part, each V component is in the direction of the block evaluation functional, so neither sector involving V survives; the internal kernel gives the piecewise formula. Doublons have zero dark projection. The loss operator annihilates the dark space and its orthogonal complement is invariant. Any eigenvector on that complement has strictly negative imaginary eigenvalue, by taking the imaginary part of its own energy expectation and using the positive rates. Finite dimension then gives decay, even with Jordan blocks, and the stated survival for arbitrary density matrices. Independent symmetric-tensor kernels gave dimensions (3,3,0), (24,16,2), (33,9,0) in the three channels for (q,m)=(3,2),(4,3),(3,4); both capture probabilities matched in each case.
