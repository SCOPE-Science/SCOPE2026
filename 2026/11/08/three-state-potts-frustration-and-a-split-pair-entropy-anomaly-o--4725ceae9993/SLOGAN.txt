For the uniform three-state Potts antiferromagnet on a labeled cocktail-party graph \(K_{2m}\) minus a perfect matching, the exact ground energy and degeneracy obey a three-residue-class law. When \(m\not\equiv0\pmod3\), every ground state has either zero or one split nonedge pair, and a uniformly sampled ground state has a unique split pair with probability \(2(t+1)/(2t+3)\to1\), where \(m=3t+s\); for \(m\equiv0\pmod3\), split pairs are absent altogether.
