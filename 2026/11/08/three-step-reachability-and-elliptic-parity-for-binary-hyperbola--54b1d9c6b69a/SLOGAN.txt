For the additive hyperbola \(\{(x,x^{-1}):x\ne0\}\) over binary finite fields, every target is reachable within three steps exactly from field order \(8\) onward; orders \(2\) and \(4\) are disconnected. The exact three-step support is controlled by a genus-one Artin–Schreier curve and alternates between full support and a single missing origin in all orders at least \(16\).
