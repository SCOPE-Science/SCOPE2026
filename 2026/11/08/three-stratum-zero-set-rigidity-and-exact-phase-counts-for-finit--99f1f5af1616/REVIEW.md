# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The cyclotomic-degree equality for coprime conductors makes \(1+\cdots+T^{p-1}\) irreducible over the coefficient field. Since the support omits at least one of the \(p\) phase levels, vanishing at a nonzero frequency forces cancellation within each at-most-two-point fiber of the quadratic map. This yields a unique reflection center and pairwise opposite coefficients. At the origin, the independent basis of the power-of-two cyclotomic field requires paired root counts and gives the exact factorial formula. Counting symmetric supports by choosing reflection pairs yields the global result. All boundary cases, including two-point supports and \(|R|=p-1\), are covered. Exact coefficient-vector checks used 45,188 assignments and 1,528,052 Fourier evaluations, but are not treated as the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected material
Li (arXiv:2609.16030, 2026-09-11) establishes sharp parity-dependent bounds and conditional rigidity for arbitrary complex coefficients, not this three-stratum zero-set classification and simultaneous exact counts for root-of-unity phase alphabets. Lam–Leung (arXiv:math/9511209) establishes general root-of-unity vanishing-length restrictions, not quadratic Fourier frequencies or support-resolved counts. The indexed five-point result for \(\mathbb F_7\) likewise treats arbitrary complex coefficients. Closest comparisons concern stronger coefficient freedom or different objects and do not imply the claimed exact classification without an additional cyclotomic-disjointness and reflection argument. Remaining risk: an earlier equivalent result may be phrased in chirp-code or phase-quantized MUB language.

## Value — PASS
Quantized phase alphabets are natural in finite Fourier and MUB measurement designs; unlike the unrestricted-complex setting, they enforce a complete three-way rigidity of all zero locations. The finding supplies one uniform theorem for every odd prime, every bit depth, every nonempty proper support, all admissible phase choices, and their exact supportwise/global multiplicities. The odd-support zero-free corollary and explicit full-line versus isolated-origin counting distinguish exact zeros from merely small Fourier values. This is a complete structural classification, not a small isolated computation.

## Limits
The result is not a general complex-coefficient zero theorem and yields no conditioning estimates. Its publication priority remains qualified by the stated residual risk.
