## Correctness — PASS
The claim is the full integral homotopy equivalence \(\mathcal M(G)\simeq\bigvee^{28}S^3\vee M(\mathbb Z/3,3)\). The edge list fixes all assumptions and normalizations; an exhaustive matching enumeration produces \(2835\) nonempty faces. A deterministic matching pairs \(1385\) face–coface pairs and is acyclic on the complete \(10816\)-incidence Hasse digraph. It leaves one zero-cell, forty-six three-cells and eighteen four-cells. The exact signed integer Morse differential has rank eighteen, modulo-three rank seventeen, and a specified maximal minor equal to three. Therefore its Smith factors are seventeen units and one three. Since all attaching maps are from three-spheres to a wedge of three-spheres, the integer degrees classify them, and the resulting homotopy type is precisely as stated. A separately coded full simplicial boundary calculation over three primes corroborates the torsion. No finite-experiment extrapolation to a graph family is made.

This explicit complete integral homotopy type both exhibits nontrivial torsion and distinguishes the two classical triangle-free four-chromatic examples at the matching-complex level, despite their related extremal coloring properties.
