Three-valued Łukasiewicz-transitive fuzzy frames on any finite labelled world set are exactly crisp preorder cores decorated by upsets of a complementary product poset. This gives the exact all-size Stirling–poset census, with \(1,9,281,29027\) frames on one through four worlds; each fixed total-preorder core with \(k\) classes admits precisely the Catalan number \(C_k\) of decorations.
