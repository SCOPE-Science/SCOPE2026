# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof gives an exact profile-to-equilibrium derivation for every multipartite source pattern. Its denominator is strictly positive; the worst-potential comparison for three-source patterns is exhaustive when \(m\ge4\), and the two explicit rational gaps have positive numerators and denominators for all \(0<\lambda<1\). Monotonicity under adding sources excludes every one- and two-source set, and the labelled count is \(m^3\). The \(m=3\) boundary is checked separately. Exact rational verification compares 27 complete multipartite enumerators against independently solved full vertex systems and 162 separate vertex-level potential computations. These computations are finite checks; the symbolic proof covers the full parameter domain.

## Originality — PASS, provisional

Allagan, Pereyra and Massey (arXiv:2609.31535v1, September 25, 2026) define the invariant and derive general monotonicity, complexity, spider algorithms, and exact complete-graph and star cases. Their inspected full text contains no balanced tripartite classification. An earlier published complete-bipartite calculation is for two parts, and its one-dimensional result does not mathematically imply the three-part support comparison, its positive all-discount gap, or the count \(m^3\). Published mathematical records and alternate terminology were searched; the closest identified items do not cover the present infinite three-way theorem. Priority is provisional because searches cannot exclude unindexed or inaccessible sources.

## Mathematical value — PASS

For every discount factor, the theorem identifies a positive interval of threshold values where optimal placements on an infinite natural graph family must occupy *all three* parts. This is a nontrivial distribution constraint not captured by the minimum cardinality alone. The exact count of minima, the sharp failure at part size three, and the complete cardinality profile formula supply a motivated structural resolution of an emerging optimization problem rather than a numerical instance.

## Residual limits

Only equal-size three-part graphs are classified by the forcing law; the all-part profile formula is an exact enumeration, not a uniform efficient algorithm when the part count is unbounded. No independent mathematical assessment or guarantee of historical priority is asserted.
