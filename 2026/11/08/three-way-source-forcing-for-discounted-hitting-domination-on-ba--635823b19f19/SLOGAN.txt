For every \(m\ge4\) and every discount \(0<\lambda<1\), balanced complete tripartite graphs have a nonempty open support-threshold window in which exactly three sources are necessary, and every minimum source set must choose one vertex from each part: there are precisely \(m^3\) such sets. An exact multipartite source-profile formula also enumerates every feasible set by size, while \(m=3\) is a sharp obstruction to this three-way forcing mechanism.
