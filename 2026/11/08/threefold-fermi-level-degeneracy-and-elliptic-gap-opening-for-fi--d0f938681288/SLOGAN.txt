For every integer area \(N\), first-Hermite disk concentration has an exactly threefold degenerate rank-\(N\) spectral cutoff, so its maximal-energy rank-\(N\) subspace is not unique. An arbitrarily slight area-preserving elliptic deformation opens that cutoff gap at the sharp rate \(e^{-N}N^N\sqrt{N/(N+1)}\,|\tau|/N!\) and selects opposite Hermite-mode superpositions depending on the ellipse's orientation.
