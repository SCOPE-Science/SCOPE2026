In characteristic \(p\), every nonzero derivation \(D\) of the null-filiform Leibniz algebra with \(D^p=D\) is conjugate to exactly one \(cH\), \(c\in\mathbb F_p^\times\), and over \(\mathbb F_q\) their total number is \(1+(p-1)q^{n-1-\lfloor(n-1)/p\rfloor}\). In characteristic two this gives exactly \(1+q^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) idempotent derivations, versus only the zero idempotent derivation in odd characteristic.
