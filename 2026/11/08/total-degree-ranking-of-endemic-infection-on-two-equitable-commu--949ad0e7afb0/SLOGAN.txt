For the discrete-time product-form SIS network map, every supercritical connected graph with exactly two equitable communities has a unique positive endemic equilibrium, whose per-vertex infection ranks match the communities' **full degrees** for every admissible transmission and recovery rate. The six-vertex graph \(C_4\vee K_2\) is the smallest nontrivial example in which these ranks invert the isolated-community epidemic-threshold order, and a three-community construction shows why the two-community hypothesis matters.
