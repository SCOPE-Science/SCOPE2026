In triangular graphs \(T_n=L(K_n)\), the inclusion-minimal total dominating sets correspond exactly to base-edge forests of three-vertex paths covering every vertex, or forests of subdivided stars leaving one vertex unused. This yields exact generating functions for both all and minimal total dominating sets, and the complete minimal-size spectrum \(\{\lfloor2n/3\rfloor,\ldots,n-2\}\) for every \(n\ge4\).
