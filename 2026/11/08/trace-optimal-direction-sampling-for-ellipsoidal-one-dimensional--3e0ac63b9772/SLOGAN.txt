For one-dimensional ellipsoidal trust-region alignment, the largest possible worst-gradient mean over all gradient-independent direction laws is exactly \(1/\operatorname{tr}S\), attained by a full-support quadratically tilted Haar direction law. When \(S\) is anisotropic, both Haar and inverse-square-root metric-guided directions have strictly worse worst-gradient means, while in dimension two the positive-square-root transform is the unique optimal member of the natural power-transform family.
