Trace-parity restrictions of the invertible binary matrices yield two additive convolution operators with exactly classified but different Fourier kernels. Their asymptotic nullity proportions are \(4Q_\infty^2=0.333594\ldots\) and \(Q_\infty^2\sum_{j\ge0}2^{-2j^2}/Q_j^4=0.433494\ldots\), even though the two operators together recover every signal.
