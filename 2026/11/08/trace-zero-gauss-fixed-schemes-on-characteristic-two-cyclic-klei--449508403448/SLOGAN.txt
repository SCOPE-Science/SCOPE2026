For every characteristic-two cyclic Klein cubic \(\sum_iX_i^2X_{i+1}=0\), its coordinate-marked Gauss fixed-point scheme is precisely the finite étale scheme of nonzero trace-zero elements of \(\mathbb F_{2^n}\), of degree \(2^{n-1}-1\). On the Klein cubic threefold this produces three closed points of degree five and exactly 15 geometric fixed points.
