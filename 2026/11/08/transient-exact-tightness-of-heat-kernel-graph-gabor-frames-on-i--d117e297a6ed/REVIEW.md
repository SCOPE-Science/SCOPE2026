# Review of transient heat-frame tightness

## Correctness — PASS
The frame operator is exactly the diagonal of the doubled-time graph heat kernel: summing the full Fourier basis uses Parseval without requiring a simple eigenvalue spectrum. The graph Laplacian decomposes into the constant line, five component contrasts, internal clique zero-sum spaces and one inter-group contrast; their dimensions and individual diagonal spectral-projector masses have been checked directly, yielding exactly two analytic frame eigenvalues with their correct multiplicities. On writing \(x=e^{-2kt}\), their difference is \(x^8P_k(x)/(40k)\). The factorization \(P_k=(1-x)Q_k\) and the displayed degree-nine Möbius transform of \(Q_k\) have exact symbolic verification. Its coefficient list has exactly one sign change for every integer \(k\ge1\); the positive constant coefficient and negative leading coefficient give one positive root, including multiplicity, by Descartes' rule. This maps bijectively to exactly one positive time. The signs at zero and one determine both sides of the crossing. The asymptotics follow by dividing the root equation by \(k\) and letting the unique root tend to zero. The independent checker performs exact algebra and matrix comparisons, and does not stand in for the infinite-family proof.

## Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority
Buck and Okoudjou (arXiv:2402.15902, first available 2024-02-24), Theorems 3.1–3.2 and 4.2–4.3, establish the graph heat-window frame spectrum for arbitrary graphs and all-time tightness on vertex-transitive/strongly regular graphs. The present result does **not** re-claim the generic diagonal criterion. It supplies a concrete, infinite, connected and strictly irregular family, proves the equality of the two nontrivial heat diagonals at exactly one positive parameter, and gives a degree-nine Descartes certificate for the unique crossing in every member. None of the material read in the focal source implies this family-specific root theorem without the new spectral decomposition and sign proof. Ghandehari–Guillot–Hollingsworth (arXiv:2009.06058), Theorem 3.1, contains an even more general diagonal sharp-frame criterion but does not calculate the heat-diagonal crossing of this graph family. Shuman–Ricaud–Vandergheynst (arXiv:1307.5708) study windowed graph Fourier frames; the Perraudin et al. and Shuman et al. filterbank works concern other constructions or designed tightness. Searches of graph heat-Gabor equivalences, transient tightness, single-time heat diagonal equalization, and indexed mathematical findings found no identified stronger covering theorem. Negative searches are evidence of absence of indexed coverage only, not a proof of priority. A general graph heat-centrality equality result could imply an equivalent formulation and remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A uniform family of **irregular** connected graphs is tight at exactly one positive heat time under the unmodified graph Gabor construction. That is a motivated, exact boundary to potential converses of the known regularity-based sufficient conditions, not a routine restatement of a general diagonal formula. The theorem supplies all sharp time-dependent frame eigenvalues, an exact unique transition, its location and asymptotic scale. The construction separates transient heat-diagonal equality from all-time walk regularity and supplies reproducible test graphs for heat-window frame design. The result does not prove this is the smallest or only such graph family and does not quantify perturbative numerical stability.
