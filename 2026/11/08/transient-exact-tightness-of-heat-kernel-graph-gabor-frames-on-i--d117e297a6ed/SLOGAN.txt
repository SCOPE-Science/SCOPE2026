An explicit infinite family of connected irregular graphs has graph heat-kernel Gabor frames that become exactly tight at one—and only one—finite positive heat-time parameter. For \(G_k=(2K_{5k})\vee(4K_{2k})\), the entire frame spectrum and the unique transition time are given by exact exponential and polynomial formulas, showing that finite-time tightness does not require regularity or vertex transitivity.
