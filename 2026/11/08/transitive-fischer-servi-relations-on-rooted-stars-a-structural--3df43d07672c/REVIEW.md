# Scientific review: transitive Fischer–Servi relations on rooted stars

## Correctness
PASS. The quantified confluences reduce, on a rooted star, to explicit row coverage constraints. The structural proof uniquely partitions any transitive relation by root loop, root-to-leaf and leaf-to-root incidence; all other possible edges form one arbitrary transitive relation on a residual labelled leaf set. The exceptional all-world relation is separately counted. The resulting binomial sum is derived for every positive leaf number; no extrapolation from finite data is used. Two independently structured finite checks agree on all relations through four leaves, and a separate enumeration verifies the auxiliary five-leaf numbers.

## Originality
PASS within inspected evidence. The original confluence literature specifies the four distinct interaction conditions without a rooted-star exact transitive census. Later intuitionistic modal work develops finite-model methods rather than this enumeration. A prior exact unlabelled count for forward-plus-downward confluence does not imply the present labelled forward-plus-backward transitive count: the classes are not comparable under implication, as concrete two-leaf counterexamples show. Related finite-chain birelational results have different underlying orders. Search-based non-coverage is not a uniqueness guarantee; older unindexed specialist work remains a priority risk.

## Value
PASS. The classification concerns a natural Fischer–Servi frame subclass where the branching of a finite poset interacts nontrivially with a transitive accessibility relation. It gives a complete uniform normal form and an exact labelled census for every star size, reducing the problem to three standard, well-defined transitive-relation invariants. This is not a routine evaluation of one arbitrary small instance.

## Scope and limitations
The statement does not count unlabelled isomorphism types, require seriality/reflexivity, or extend to arbitrary intuitionistic posets. The finite checker confirms small cases but is not the proof of the general formula. Published full text was inspected for the closest confluence definitions, and accessible overlapping claims were compared; historical priority remains open to external review.
