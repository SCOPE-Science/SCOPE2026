For the inclusive scale-\(3\) Rips complex of the five-by-five torus grid, translation-fixed spaces range from empty to a circle to five isolated points, depending exactly on the order-five subgroup direction. Its known nine-dimensional fourth homology has the previously undetermined rational translation module \(\mathbb Q\oplus\widetilde{\mathbb Q}[A/L_2]\oplus\widetilde{\mathbb Q}[A/L_3]\).
