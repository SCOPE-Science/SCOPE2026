# Scientific review: translation-fixed topology of Hamming Čech nerves

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is an ordinary geometric fixed-point classification for every nontrivial subgroup of the additive translation group of an arbitrary finite Cartesian product of complete graphs. The proof explicitly derives the radius-one nerve model, constructs the coset-orbit simplicial model, and establishes an affine-coset dichotomy in the union of coordinate axes. It then proves the disjoint-simplex and regular-orbit cases, including residual equivariance and integral reduced zeroth homology. The argument does not infer unbounded statements from finite evidence. Exhaustive independent-face reconstruction over 200 mixed/composite-parameter subgroup cases and 500 prime-alphabet subgroup cases corroborated each predicted type. A possible issue would be confusing geometric fixed points with individually fixed vertices; the orbit-complex step resolves this explicitly.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Closest published work determines ordinary homotopy types of binary and equal-alphabet Hamming Čech nerves and a full rational second-homology character in the binary case. The full accessible statements and proofs of the latter two published records were inspected: neither classifies fixed-point homeomorphism types of *all* translation subgroups, nor the residual integral zero-homology permutation module; a character determines an Euler characteristic, not such a homeomorphism type. The result also handles unequal and composite alphabets and identifies the exceptional off-axis order-two subgroup. The 2026-09-26 motivating preprint was accessible only through its abstract, which focuses on filtration homotopy types rather than subgroup-fixed spaces; the full text could not be inspected, so equivalent work there or elsewhere remains a genuine residual risk. Searches and repository results are evidence of comparison, not a uniqueness certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A graph's Čech nerve can be connected and have homology concentrated in degree two while the fixed-point spaces of free-on-vertices translations have exponentially many connected components or are empty. The theorem completely resolves this natural equivariant fixed-point invariant for an unbounded family of mixed Hamming graphs, displays a sharp exceptional two-coordinate involution phenomenon, and determines integral residual symmetry modules. The exact fixed-space shape is not determined by ordinary Betti numbers or rational character traces and is relevant to subgroup-sensitive equivariant topology. It is not a small parameter table, a routine substitution into a published sphere-rank formula, or a claim of independent validation.

Both latter complete RESULT documents were inspected, while the 2026 motivating preprint was restricted to its abstract and its full text could not be inspected. Only translation subgroups, not arbitrary affine or coordinate-permuting subgroups, are classified. Independent scientific audit was not performed.
