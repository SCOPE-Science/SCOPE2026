At intrinsic Čech scale two, every nontrivial translation subgroup of a Cartesian product of finite complete graphs has an ordinary geometric fixed-point space that is either empty, a disjoint union of explicitly sized simplices, or an isolated regular translation orbit. The classification holds for unequal and composite alphabet sizes, with the sole off-axis exception coming from a two-coordinate order-two translation, and determines the residual integral component representation.
