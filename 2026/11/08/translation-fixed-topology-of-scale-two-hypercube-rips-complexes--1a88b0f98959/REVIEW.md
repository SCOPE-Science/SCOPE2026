# Scientific assessment: translation-fixed hypercube Rips geometry

## Correctness — PASS
The result is a PL-homeomorphism statement, not a homology extrapolation. Each point fixed by the translation group has equal barycentric coordinates along each translation coset; the fixed locus is therefore the orbit-barycenter simplicial complex. Coset diameter equals the maximal Hamming weight of a subgroup element. Binary linear subspaces of diameter at most two have rank at most two, with precisely the rank-two coordinate-plane and three-coordinate even-plane forms. Distinct cosets cannot be adjacent in rank two; rank-one coset graphs are each (after an explicit linear coordinate change in the weight-two case) the ordinary smaller cube graph. The graph has no triangles, giving all-dimensional topological, not merely homological, classification. The finite verifier independently enumerated subgroup lattices and orbit faces, and reproduced these conclusions in 3,346 cases.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The translation group is the canonical normal elementary-abelian subgroup of the cube isometry group; determining its fixed spaces is a natural and complete equivariant topology problem. The answer sharply stratifies the subgroup lattice by binary code diameter, turns an ambient wedge of three-spheres into fixed wedges of circles or isolated points, and gives the exact integral homology of every such locus. In particular, it records genuinely geometric subgroup information not recoverable from ambient Betti numbers. No generalization to arbitrary automorphisms, other scales, or equivariant normalizer actions is claimed.
