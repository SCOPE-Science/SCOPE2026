For the higher symmetric-Frobenius Ballico–Hefez curves \(B_n\subset\mathbb P^n\) with \(n\ge3\), every singularity is a rational ordinary transverse \(d\)-branch space-curve crossing, indexed by a degree-\(d\) Frobenius orbit with \(d\mid n\). Consequently their connected generalized Jacobians are explicit products of \(\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{q^d}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m/\mathbb G_m\) tori, with exact arithmetic genus and point-count formulas.
