For a three-parallel-class rank-two toric positroid, the reduced singular locus is exactly the disjoint union of cross-class Segre support strata whose complementary class has at least two elements. Transversely to the stratum with complementary size \(c\), the singularity is the rank-one \(2\times c\) determinantal cone, of Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity \(c\).
