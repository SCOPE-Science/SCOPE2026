Attaching arbitrary rooted trees to the vertices of a three-color cyclic clique leaves its primitive orbit counts unchanged, while every individual three-cycle gains exactly \(3^k\) tree-color assignments per phase when \(k\) nonclique vertices are added. The sharp time to become periodic is \(\max\{D,2H\}\), where \(D\) is graph diameter and \(H\) is maximal tree depth: the diameter controls freezing, but a forced wave can need twice the root-to-leaf distance to settle.
