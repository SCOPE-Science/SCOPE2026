For every tree \(T\) with at least three vertices, the entire total mutual-visibility complex of \(T\square H\) is the simplicial join of independent copies of the complex of \(H\), one per tree leaf—not merely an equality of optimum cardinalities. For \(H=K_n\square K_2\), the complete face and facet polynomials are \(\bigl(2(1+z)^n-1+nz^2\bigr)^{|L(T)|}\) and \(\bigl(2z^n+nz^2\bigr)^{|L(T)|}\), while the integral homotopy type is \(\bigvee^{(n-1)^{|L(T)|}} S^{2|L(T)|-1}\).
