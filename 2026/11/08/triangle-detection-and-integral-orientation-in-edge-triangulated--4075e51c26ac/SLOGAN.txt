For any finite simple base graph without isolated vertices, adding a distinct degree-two triangle vertex on every edge makes its three-clique-free complex a sphere of dimension \(n-1\) precisely when the base graph is triangle-free, and simple-homotopy contractible otherwise. The sphere branch is the known pointed-complex theorem; the new complete refinement identifies an explicit triangle-triggered contraction and proves that every base-graph automorphism acts on top integral homology by its original-vertex permutation sign.
