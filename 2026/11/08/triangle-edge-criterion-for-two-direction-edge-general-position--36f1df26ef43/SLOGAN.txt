For the flip graph of all independent sets of any finite simple graph \(H\), the union of two coordinate edge classes is inclusion-maximal edge-general-position exactly when their underlying vertex pair is not an edge of a triangle of \(H\). Thus triangle-containing edges are detected precisely by the nonmaximal pairs, leaving \(\binom{|V(H)|}{2}-t_\triangle(H)\) maximal two-class unions.
