# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The proof classifies all total-graph cliques, gives explicit relative elementary collapses of the intermediate complex onto the original clique complex, and identifies every exceptional midpoint triangle as a null-homotopically attached two-cell. It then derives the canonical relative integral chain module and orientation signs by the triple long exact sequence. The standard-library verification checks 772 connected labeled examples exhaustively at all face dimensions and replays the collapses. This finite exercise corroborates, but does not replace, the universal argument.

**Originality (PASS relative to inspected primary sources).** The closest published primary statement is Larrión–Pizaña–Villarroel-Flores (2009), which identifies the fundamental groups for total graphs, not their entire homotopy type or signed relative integral homology. The Goyal–Shukla–Singh (2020) line-graph result has different simplices and does not imply the present construction. A complete-graph subdivision calculation is a strict special case and is not counted as new. Prisner's (1991) exact related paper could not be inspected in full; priority against that source remains uncertain, as does unindexed literature.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The theorem is an all-graph structural classification of a natural metric/simplicial construction, not a single parameter recomputation. Its new sphere factors admit a canonical signed integral representation under graph symmetries and it determines the behavior of all prior homotopy types under this operation. The theorem does not classify all Rips radii or produce a natural equivariant splitting.
