# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** Conditioning on a specified fixed point yields three exact probabilities according to its position relative to the record. Those probabilities reconstruct the complete covariance matrix. Summation, an elementary binomial-coefficient comparison and standard record-indicator variance identities prove the aggregate and asymptotic statements. Exact rational exhaustive enumeration through eight symbols and algebraic finite tests provide independent diagnostics; the symbolic argument is not inferred from those tests.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** The closest inspected primary result of Diaconis, Evans and Graham establishes a fixed-point equivalence with successions, not left-to-right records. Gnedin and Gorin treat record-dependent measures (only abstract and metadata accessible), while Dubach establishes much broader conditional record asymptotics but does not state the exact finite positionwise mixed matrix. Closely related findings on fixed points with descents and on cycle counts with inversions do not imply this formula: their indicator events and conditioning are distinct. The main priority risk is an unlocated older bivariate record/fixed-point generating function, plus incomplete access to the Gnedin–Gorin full text.

**Value — PASS.** Record positions and fixed points are natural, differently organized permutation diagnostics. The exact triangular support, positive off-diagonal entries, negative-to-positive diagonal boundary, and reciprocal-binomial cancellation explain their joint behavior across every position. The sharp correlation rate gives a quantitative baseline for combining these diagnostics, which cannot be recovered from their marginal laws alone.

**Scientific limits.** The result does not determine their full joint distribution or imply asymptotic independence as random variables. Mathematical proof covers all sizes; finite enumeration serves as diagnostic evidence. Historical priority remains qualified.
