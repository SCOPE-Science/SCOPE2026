In a uniform permutation, the record-position/fixed-point covariance matrix is strictly lower triangular except for a sign-changing diagonal, and its row sums collapse to \(1/[n\binom{n-1}{i-1}]\). The resulting strictly positive total record–fixed-point covariance is asymptotic to \(1/n\), while their correlation decays as \(1/(n\sqrt{\log n})\).
