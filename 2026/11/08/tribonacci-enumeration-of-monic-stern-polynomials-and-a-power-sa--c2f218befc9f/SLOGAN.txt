The monic Stern polynomials are counted on successive binary blocks by a precise Tribonacci law, with generating function \(z(1+z)/(1-z-z^2-z^3)\). Because every reciprocal Stern polynomial occurs at an odd monic index, reciprocal indices have a power-saving bound \(O(X^{\log_2\lambda})\) with \(\lambda\approx1.839286755\), and hence natural density zero.
