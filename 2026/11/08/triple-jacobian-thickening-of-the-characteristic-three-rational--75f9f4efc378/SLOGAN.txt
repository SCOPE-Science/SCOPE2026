In characteristic \(3\), the Jacobian singular scheme of the tangent developable of the rational normal quartic is a *split curvilinear triple structure* with algebra \(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}\oplus\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(-4)\varepsilon\oplus\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(-8)\varepsilon^2\) and \(\varepsilon^3=0\). Its degree is \(12\), Hilbert polynomial is \(12m-9\), and arithmetic genus is \(10\); these are invariants of the nonreduced singular scheme, not merely the classical tangent surface.
