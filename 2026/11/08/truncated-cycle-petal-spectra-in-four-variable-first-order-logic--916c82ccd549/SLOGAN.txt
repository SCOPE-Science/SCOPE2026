For connected bouquets of at least two undirected cycles sharing a single hub, counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{k}\) with unrestricted quantifier depth, for every \(k\ge4\), sees exactly the cycle-length multiplicities truncated at \(k\). For any fixed set \(L\) of permitted petal lengths, this yields \((k+1)^{|L|}-|L|-1\) theories, unique smallest representatives, and a strict hierarchy at every width \(k\ge4\).
