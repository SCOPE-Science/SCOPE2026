For the metastable pure-gas branch of the local-resetting run-and-tumble continuum, the magnitude of its positional location score has an exact truncated-Pareto \(w^{-3}\) law whose cutoff disappears at the spinodal. The score's Fisher information diverges logarithmically there, and the spatial location-family squared Hellinger distance changes sharply from quadratic to \(h^2\log(1/|h|)\), with an explicit coefficient that persists for finite-speed returns.
