## Mathematical value — PASS

Minimum weight alone cannot distinguish an isolated construction from a large family of optimal configurations. The exact count being **twelve for every order** is a rigid structural phenomenon, not a numerical check for a few small graphs. A common short recurrence explains this constancy in both the cylindrical and twisted ladder realizations and gives an explicit classification of all extremizers in the complete natural class of connected cubic abelian Cayley equality graphs. It provides exact counting and symmetry information complementary to the known scalar theorems; no broader downstream theorem is claimed.
