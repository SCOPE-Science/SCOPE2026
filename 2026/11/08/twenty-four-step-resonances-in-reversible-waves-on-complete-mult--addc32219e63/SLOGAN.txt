A reversible characteristic-two neighbor-sum wave on any complete multipartite graph has only cycles of lengths dividing \(24\), with an exact fixed-point formula and a five-case sharp-period law determined by the parity of its parts. Unequal parity types produce genuine primitive \(24\)-cycles already on \(K_{1,1,1,2}\), where exactly \(30\) such temporal orbits occur over \(\mathbb F_2\).
