For the sixty-vertex truncated-icosahedral graph with its intrinsic shortest-path metric, the inclusive Rips complexes at scales two and three have homotopy types \(\bigvee^{21}S^2\) and \(S^2\). Twenty geometrically explicit hexagonal two-sphere cycles form an integral basis of the kernel of the inclusion map on \(H_2\), while the global polyhedral sphere maps to a primitive surviving generator.
