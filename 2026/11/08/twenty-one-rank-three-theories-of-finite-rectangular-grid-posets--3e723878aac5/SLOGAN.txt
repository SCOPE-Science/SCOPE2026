Finite rectangular grid posets have exactly 21 first-order theories of quantifier rank at most three in the pure order language. Every rectangle with side lengths \(5\leq a\leq b\) and \(b\geq6\) has the same rank-three theory as the 30-point grid \(G_{5,6}\), while the 25-point grid \(G_{5,5}\) remains distinguishable.
