For the canonical 18-vertex Pappus incidence graph, the previously missing graph-metric scale-two Vietoris–Rips stage is exactly \(\bigvee^{26}S^2\): eight spheres come from each bipartition side and ten from suspending the original graph. This yields 26 new integral second-homology bars \([2,3)\); the previously known scale-three six-triad join supplies 64 fifth-homology bars \([3,4)\).
