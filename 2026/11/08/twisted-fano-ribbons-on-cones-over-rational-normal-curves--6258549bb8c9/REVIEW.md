# Scientific review: twisted Fano ribbons on rational-normal cones

## Correctness — PASS
The coefficientwise restriction of the complete Hankel-minor ideal to the universal affine Grassmannian chart yields exactly \(b_j=j t^{j-1}u\) and \(u^2=0\), in arbitrary characteristic and every degree \(d\ge2\). The second chart is identical, and changing the spanning basis of the actual line, not merely its reduced parameter, gives \(w=-t^{d-2}u\). These equations establish the entire global gluing and identify the nilradical as \(\mathcal O(d-2)\). Its global sections and Plücker Hilbert polynomial follow from the square-zero exact sequence. The supplied exact symbolic checks confirm the critical identities in degrees \(2\) through \(11\); separate exact Gröbner calculations verify both containments of the chart ideals in degrees \(3,4,5\) and four characteristics. The general argument is algebraic, not experimental.

## Originality — PASS, with comparison risk
Ilten's arXiv:1411.3025v1, Theorem 2.2(1) and Section 3.4, establishes the local nonreduced double-line structure but states a global product \(\mathbb P^1\times\xi_2\) for cones \(S(d,0)\). The present intrinsic nilradical \(\mathcal O(d-2)\) and \(h^0=d\) contradict the product statement when \(d\ge3\); a product would instead have trivial nilradical and \(h^0=2\). This is a concrete statement-level comparison, not a title comparison. Ilten–Zotine arXiv:1605.05745v1, Section 9, determines local Fano structures and does not identify the global gluing invariant. Searches for the global ribbon, twisted conormal, dual-number product, and rational-normal-cone Hilbert polynomial found no covering correction. The publisher's final version of the 2014 article was not accessible and may differ from the posted manuscript. Historical priority is provisional.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem isolates a genuine local-to-global distinction in an unbounded natural toric family: every formal or affine local chart looks like a product with a double point, yet the intrinsic global nilradical has positive varying degree. It provides exact global functions and a Plücker Hilbert polynomial, and resolves a specific ambiguity in a published scheme-theoretic classification. These are substantive invariants, not a change of terminology or a finite special-case recomputation.

## Limitations
The result applies to standard projective cones over complete rational normal embeddings and does not settle arbitrary toric Fano schemes. Full publisher-version historical comparison remains unperformed. Symbolic tests are not independent proofs of claims with infinite quantifiers.
