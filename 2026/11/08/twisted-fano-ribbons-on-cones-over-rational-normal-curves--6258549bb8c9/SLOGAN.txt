For the cone over the degree-\(d\) rational normal curve (\(d\ge2\)), the entire Fano scheme of lines is a square-zero ribbon with intrinsic nilradical \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(d-2)\), Plücker Hilbert polynomial \(2dn+d\), and \(d\) global functions. Hence the ribbon is not the trivial product with the dual-numbers point for any \(d\ge3\), despite having dual-number affine charts.
