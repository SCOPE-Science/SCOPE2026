For characteristic-three Artin--Schreier curves of genus \(6\) and zero \(3\)-rank, the a-number is exactly \(3\) or \(4\), determined by the degree-five coefficient in reduced form. Both values occur over \(\mathbb F_3\) with full geometric automorphism group exactly \(C_3\): \(y^3-y=x^7+x^5+x^2\) and \(y^3-y=x^7+x^2\), respectively.
