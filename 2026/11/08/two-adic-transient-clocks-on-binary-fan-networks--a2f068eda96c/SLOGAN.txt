Neighbor-XOR dynamics on the diameter-two fan \(K_1\vee P_n\) has an exact, two-adically stratified transient census: for \(n\equiv3\pmod4\), its nilpotent Jordan blocks have lengths \(2^{\nu_2(n+1)}-3\) and \(1\). On every fan with \(n=2^s-1\), \(s\ge3\), half of binary initial states require the sharp \(n-2\) transient even though every recurrent state is one of two fixed points or one two-cycle.
