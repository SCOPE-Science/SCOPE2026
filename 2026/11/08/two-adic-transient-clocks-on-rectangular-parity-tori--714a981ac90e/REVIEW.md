# Scientific assessment: two-adic transient clocks on rectangular parity tori

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is an exact infinite-family result. The group algebra decomposes at odd-order root pairs after scalar extension. The zero-locus factorization yields precisely \(2g-1\) relevant factors. At nontrivial roots the derivative of \(X+X^{-1}\) is a unit, giving \(k\) length-\(L\) blocks per component; the exceptional root has exactly \(2k\) blocks of length \(L/2\) by a monic quadratic quotient computation and a Frobenius nilpotence bound. Fitting decomposition gives cumulative nullities and exact first-entry differences. The boundary \(L=1\) is handled separately. Direct bitset matrix ranks independently confirmed 2,229 parameter-and-iteration cases, and graph trajectory enumeration independently checked 102,912 labelled states. These experiments validate calculations but are not substituted for the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk

Compared the explicit statement and its implications with Martin–Odlyzko–Wolfram (1984, particularly multidimensional §4D), Wolfram (1983), Peters–Goldwasser–Young (2017, especially Theorem 2.6), and Munter–Peters (2021, Theorem 5.1). The first supplies general additive formalism, while the latter two study graph-product switch solvability and invertibility; none of the inspected full texts state the particular \(2\)-adic zero-primary Jordan partition or first-entry census. The same graph appears in graph-product research, but object identity does not itself imply the stronger transient results. Related finite-grid harmonic-function work often studies \(I+A\), not this update \(A\). Available records contain no equivalent census. An unindexed result or a stronger uninspected theorem, particularly in older linear-cellular-automata literature, could still establish priority; abstract-only sources are not treated as ruled out.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A two-dimensional periodic nearest-neighbor dynamical system is a canonical extension of one-dimensional parity cellular automata. The result resolves all zero-primary multiplicities and exact initial-state entrance populations for an unbounded two-parameter graph family. The exceptional root creates a nonobvious half-length scale, and the odd-part gcd controls whether the longer clock survives. These structural distinctions are not obtained merely by knowing the determinant or the presence of a zero mode. The growing sharp time on \(2^s\times2^s\) tori illustrates the consequence.

## Scope and risk

The result does not classify primitive recurrent periods, only the first entry into recurrent dynamics. Only characteristic two, no self-inclusive update, and simple cycles of lengths at least three are covered. Some plausible historical sources remain inaccessible in full, especially Aso–Honda (1985); an earlier equivalent cannot be excluded without more source access. Self-review does not constitute independent mathematical validation.
