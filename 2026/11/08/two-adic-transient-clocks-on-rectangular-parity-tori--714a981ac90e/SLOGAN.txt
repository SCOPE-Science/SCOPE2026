For four-neighbor parity dynamics on every rectangular torus \(C_m\square C_n\), the complete nilpotent Jordan partition and every first-periodic-entry count are determined by the powers of two dividing \(m,n\) and the greatest common divisor of their odd parts. An exceptional trivial-root component produces blocks of half the usual length, while the \(2^s\times2^s\) family has sharp transient time \(2^{s-1}\) even though every graph has degree four.
