On every open \(n\times n\) square grid in characteristic two, the four-neighbor parity update has exactly \(n\) zero-eigenvalue Jordan blocks, but their lengths follow a sharp two-adic law determined by \(n+1\). This yields the complete first-periodic-entry census, with maximum transient \(2^{\nu_2(n+1)+1}\) except at power-of-two-minus-one side lengths, where the entire system extinguishes after exactly \(n+1\) steps in the worst case.
