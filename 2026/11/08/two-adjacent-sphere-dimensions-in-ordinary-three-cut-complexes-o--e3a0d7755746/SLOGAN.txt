For every \(d\ge4\), the ordinary three-cut complex of the Boolean hypercube \(Q_d\) is a nonshellable wedge of \(A_d=2^{d-3}(d^2-5d+8)-1\) spheres in dimension \(2^d-5\) and \(B_d=2^{d-1}\sum_{j=3}^{d}\binom dj\) spheres in dimension \(2^d-4\). The Alexander dual collapses square by square to the geometric cube's two-skeleton plus a free edge for every vertex pair at Hamming distance at least three; the known three-cube case fits the same formula.
