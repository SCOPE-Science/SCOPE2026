## Mathematical value — PASS

This is an exact, dimension-uniform classification for a classical and naturally motivated geometric family. It identifies a sharp coordinate-scale transition in even dimension, an invariant two-contact regime in odd dimension, and the surprising fact that arbitrarily many added cyclic-polytope vertices never alter the minimum enclosing sphere. The general contact certificate extends from integer lattice samples to all compact symmetric samples and even the whole moment-curve arc. This exact metric stability is not a formal consequence of knowing the face numbers or Ehrhart polynomial.
