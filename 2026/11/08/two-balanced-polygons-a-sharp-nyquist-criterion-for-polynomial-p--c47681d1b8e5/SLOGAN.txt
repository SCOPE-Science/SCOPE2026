For every degree \(d\ge3\), \(4d\) squared-magnitude samples on two regular concentric \(2d\)-gons recover every complex polynomial up to global phase exactly when the radii are positive and different and \(\sin(d\alpha)\ne0\), where \(\alpha\) is the relative rotation. The angular condition resolves the sole Nyquist-frequency alias, reconstructing the complete intensity on both circles; each excluded case has explicit non-equivalent polynomials with identical samples.
