# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For all positive voter-type populations a,b, supported candidates and 1<=k<=|C|, Pareto optimality forces every shared candidate to be selected whenever a private winner exists; hence h=min(k,|A∩B|). If h=k, reserves and payments 1/(k+1) satisfy all P1-P4. Otherwise all losers are private: EJR+ is precisely n_i<(h+y_i+1)q for each type with private losers. In any core+ payment system P1/P2 imply individual r>=1/(u_i+1), giving necessity. For sufficiency, put rho_i*=q/n_i when n_i>=q and a loser exists, otherwise 1; private winners absorb up to y_i q, leaving D_i*=max(0,n_i-(y_i+1)q) for active types. Strict scalar inequalities give D_i*<h n_i rho_i*; when both deficits are positive D_A*+D_B*=(h-2)q<hq, and when only one is positive it is <hq. Small positive rational reserve perturbations preserve capacities and give strict P3. Equal payments over selected classes give P1, P2, P4. The non-Pareto (2 voters, 4 candidates, 3 seats) example has EJR+ but requires reserve sum >=3/4>2/3 and so is not core+. Independently constructed LP checks 6,912 finite aggregate configurations, 6,332 feasible and 580 obstructed, matching strict quota criteria; these numerical finite checks corroborate but do not prove unbounded claim.
