For disjoint equal caps centred at regular-simplex vertices, the pure quadratic spherical-harmonic concentration spectrum has two explicit eigenvalue branches in every dimension, possibly coalescing. A unique interior aperture on the two-sphere, \(a=\sqrt{3/7}\), makes the tetrahedral four-cap mask perfectly isotropic on all degree-two harmonics even though a degree-three harmonic has nonzero mass on the same mask.
