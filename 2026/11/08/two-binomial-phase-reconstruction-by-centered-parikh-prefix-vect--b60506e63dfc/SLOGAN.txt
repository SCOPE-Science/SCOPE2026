For any period word \(U\), two length-\(m|U|\) rotations have the same complete two-letter scattered-subsequence statistics exactly when their intervening arc has Parikh vector proportional to that of \(U\); the equivalence partition is independent of \(m\). In particular, periods formed by three or more distinct constant-letter runs have every starting phase uniquely recovered by order-two statistics, while order one cannot distinguish any phase.
