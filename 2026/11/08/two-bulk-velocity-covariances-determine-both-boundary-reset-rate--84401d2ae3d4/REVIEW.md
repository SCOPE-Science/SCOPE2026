# Review: two bulk velocity covariances identify both wall resetting rates

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact stationary Fourier coefficient in the physical harmonic-chain model reduces to a pair of positive spatial exponentials whose amplitudes are constrained by the common known wall diffusivity. Writing each mode through a positive hyperbolic parameter yields the explicit two-data bijection, and strict endpoint monotonicity proves the entire feasible region, both equality cases and the minimality conclusion. The stand-alone checker independently verifies 42 positive-rate pairs, exact rational modal identities, cosine-Fourier quadratures and the finite-chain covariance matrix (six separations, discrepancy below \(10^{-6}\) at 50 sites). General claims rest on analysis, not finite tests.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Sarkar and Roy, arXiv:2307.06127, already established the forward stationary wall and bulk spatial velocity correlations. Those formulas and the existence of nonzero correlations are not claimed as new. Sarkar, Santra and Basu, arXiv:2404.00615, identified two spatial scales for a different active Rubin bath. General exponential-sum recovery is classical Prony theory (Plonka 2014). The assessed new statement is the *complete sharp two-covariance image*, global inverse and minimality from the rate-amplitude constraint in the Brownian resetting wall model. Targeted literature and published-record searches did not identify a covering result. Unindexed equivalents and non-inspected broader mathematics remain publication-priority risks.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Local kinetic variance and its nearest-neighbor spatial covariance are natural measurable bulk observables. The theorem allows exact recovery of both inaccessible boundary reset rates using the smallest possible number of these observables and supplies an algebraic model-consistency region, including a sharp signature of equal rates. It provides a meaningful inverse classification absent from the known forward theory, without claiming numerical robustness or broader transport universality.

**Limits.** The inference is unordered, thermodynamic, stationary, and exact-noiseless; both reset rates are positive and finite, the two wall diffusivities are equal and known, and \(m,k,\gamma>0\) are calibrated. No independent scientific validation has been performed.
