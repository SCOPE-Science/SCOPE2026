In a stationary harmonic chain driven by two Brownian resetting walls with the same known diffusivity, just the onsite and nearest-neighbor bulk velocity covariances recover both positive reset rates, up to swapping the two ends. The admissible normalized observations obey the sharp interval \(\sqrt{1+s^2}-1\le t<(\sqrt{1+4s^2}-1)/2\), whose lower edge detects equal rates and whose upper edge is approached only when one rate diverges.
