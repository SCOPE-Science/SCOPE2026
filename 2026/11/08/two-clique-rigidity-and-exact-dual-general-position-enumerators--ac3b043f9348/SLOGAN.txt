For any complete join of at least two noncomplete finite graphs, a nonempty dual general-position set is exactly one side of a partition of the vertices into two cliques. Hence these sets are precisely the proper two-colorings of the complementary graph, giving a complete polynomial and maximum-size formula, including an exact resolution for \(K_m\circ H\) with \(m\ge2\) and noncomplete \(H\).
