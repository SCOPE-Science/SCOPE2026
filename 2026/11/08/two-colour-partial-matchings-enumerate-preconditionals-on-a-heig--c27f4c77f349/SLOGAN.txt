On the height-three lattice with \(m\) incomparable atoms beneath a common coatom, fundamental preconditionals are exactly partial matchings with independently two-coloured unmatched atoms. Their labelled count is \(\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}m!2^{m-2j}/(2^jj!(m-2j)!)\), and their isomorphism-class count is \(\lfloor(m+2)^2/4\rfloor\); an explicit companion formula enumerates the weaker conditional-identity/semicomplementation operations.
