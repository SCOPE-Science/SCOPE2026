The categorical Hamming double cover \(K_2\times K_q^{\times d}\) has an exact negative-type exponent governed by a competition between one bipartition Fourier mode and \(d(q-1)\) first-order modes. In the high-dimensional regime \(q/d\to\alpha\), the winning obstruction switches at \(\alpha_c=1.0032421945\ldots\), with a further factor of order \(q\) in the critical exponent on one side of the transition.
