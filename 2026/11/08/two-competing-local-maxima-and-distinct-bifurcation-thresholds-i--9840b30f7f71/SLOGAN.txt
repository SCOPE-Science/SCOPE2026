For the real quadratic Walsh space on four signs, two distinct coefficient geometries—one four-cycle and the complete graph—are simultaneously strict local maximizers of normalized \(L^p\) moments throughout a nonempty subinterval of \(2<p<3\). The complete-graph profile first becomes locally maximizing at \(p=2.3418224547\ldots\), but it overtakes the four-cycle profile in moment value only at the separate threshold \(p=2.7362773220\ldots\); neither statement asserts global optimality.
