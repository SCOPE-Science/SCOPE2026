# Self-review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The four-dimensional algebra is explicitly identified with the length-two exterior algebra in characteristic two; the wedge pairing produces a canonical algebraic-group isomorphism to the two-dimensional affine group. The involution and commutation equations are expanded in actual group coordinates. The dichotomy for two commuting square-zero \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices is exhaustive, including both-zero and one-zero boundaries. A closed projective-line incidence construction proves exactly two components, their dimensions and intersection; the projective scroll description proves smoothness away from the cone vertex and the precise singular locus. Inclusion-exclusion proves the finite-field polynomial for all \(q\) of characteristic two. Two separate genuine computational replays check \(q=2,4,8\), including direct algebra multiplication for \(q=2,4\). These experiments corroborate, but do not replace, the proof.

## Originality
**PASS (provisional).** Searches tested the concrete affine group, its exterior-algebra-automorphism realization, elementary abelian two-group homomorphism varieties, scroll component and exact polynomial. Closest broad source: Levy (2006) proves equidimensionality in the full \(3\)-by-\(3\) restricted-nilpotent commuting variety, but not in its constrained affine-subgroup slice; indeed the computed slice is non-equidimensional. Britnell–Wildon (2010) cover similarity-class commuting existence, not constrained component geometry. Ngo (2012) concerns Lie algebra commuting varieties and his rank-two theorem assumes odd characteristic. Baek–Han (2016) concern graph distances in linear groups, rather than the affine-subgroup pair scheme. Previous accessible research claims on involutions of triangular algebra groups, on incidence algebras, and on exterior-algebra Rota–Baxter operators have neither this algebraic group nor the same invariant. A future discovery of a strong general parabolic-subgroup representation-variety result could imply this special case; the searches do not prove historical uniqueness.

## Value
**PASS.** This is an intrinsically motivated moduli problem: homomorphisms from the elementary abelian group of order four to an algebraic automorphism group of a minimal nonsemisimple local algebra. Unlike an isolated field count, it gives a full two-component geometric classification, identifies a rational normal scroll, proves non-equidimensionality despite equidimensional unrestricted ambient matrix commuting varieties, determines the complete reduced singular locus, and obtains uniform point counts across all characteristic-two finite fields. It draws a useful boundary between unrestricted linear and affine-constrained commuting-involution geometry. No downstream theorem or classification in higher affine rank is asserted.

## Closest literature and residual risks
The original preprint of Levy (2006-09-06) and later work of Britnell–Wildon (2010-01-06), Ngo (2012-09-07) and Baek–Han (2016-10-25) were inspected for relevant object and statement coverage. Their stated invariants do not imply the claimed scroll/translation decomposition. The remaining risk is an unlocated general theorem for parabolic slices, equivariant affine representations or representation varieties of \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\) that directly specializes to this decomposition. The result is only about reduced geometry; the natural nonreduced group-scheme functor and its infinitesimal structure are not treated.
