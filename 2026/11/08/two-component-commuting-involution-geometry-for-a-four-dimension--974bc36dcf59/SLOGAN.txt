The reduced variety of commuting square-one automorphism pairs of the characteristic-two algebra \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\) has precisely two components: affine four-space and the five-dimensional cone over the rational normal scroll \(S(2,2,1,1)\), meeting in a three-dimensional rank-one determinantal cone. Over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic two, it has exactly \(q^5+2q^4-q^3-q^2\) rational points; its entire reduced singular locus is the component intersection.
