For independent two-agent games with two terminal outcomes, logical validity of the independence-of-agents axiom is governed by pure-color rows and columns, rather than rectangular action support. Its probability has distinct critical windows at \(p\asymp m^{-2}\) and \(p\asymp2(\log m)/m\), with explicit sparse and four-Poisson upper-window limits; logical independence can hold even when structural independence fails with probability approaching one.
