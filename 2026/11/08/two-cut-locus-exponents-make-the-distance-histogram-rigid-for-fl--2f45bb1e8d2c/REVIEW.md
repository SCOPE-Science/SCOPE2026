# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The quotient is a free compact Euclidean covering of area \(ab\), and the first ball-overlap radius is exactly half the shorter of the fundamental translation and glide distances. Pairing opposite deck neighbors yields a complete lens deficit; periodization converts the averaged glide term to a one-dimensional integral. The independent half-lens calculation and the standard circular-segment tangency expansion give the two sharp exponents with explicit coefficients. At equality both corrections coexist but the smaller exponent dominates; area and first cutoff already determine both parameters. The proof controls the finite set of near-shortest deck directions at each fixed \(a,b>0\), with no unproved numerical-to-universal inference.

The exact implication gap is the anisotropic \(3/2\) versus \(2\) transition, which is needed to distinguish the *ordered* glide and translation parameters; published general inverse frameworks, cover formulas and shortest-path structures do not supply that conclusion. Lack of a match in finite searches is not evidence of absolute priority. Older dispersed integral-geometric literature could still contain the exact local distribution.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Distance distributions generally fail to distinguish arbitrary nonisometric spaces. The standard flat Klein family is a natural two-parameter, nonhomogeneous, nonorientable test case: its geodesic disks change with center, unlike a flat torus. A sharp transition in the *averaged* distance law nonetheless recovers the oriented geometric roles of the two lengths. The qualitative difference in exponents is a structural holonomy signal; the theorem is a constructive inverse classification, not a routine substitution into an existing global flat-torus formula.

## Limitations

The claimed injectivity is restricted to the specified standard flat Klein-bottle family and exact two-point intrinsic distance CDF. The argument has no noise-stability theorem or characterization among arbitrary spaces. The accompanying computations check representative cases but are not a universal proof.
