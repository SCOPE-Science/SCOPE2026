On every Boolean group of order \(N\ge8\), adding a second independently complex-weighted coordinate defect to a flat Gabor window cannot improve the sharp full tomography condition number \(\sqrt{2N}-1\). Exactly eight parameter pairs attain it, including four two-site windows whose real-only and imaginary-only constituent perturbations are each individually incomplete; the complete singular-value and rank formulas hold for all complex parameters.
