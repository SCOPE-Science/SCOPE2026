For a tree with two leaf-bearing hubs separated by a degree-two vertex, every higher independence complex has a complete integral sphere-wedge classification, with exactly \(\binom{a+b-1}{r-3}\) spheres of dimension \(r-1\) and \(\binom{a-1}{r-1}\binom{b-1}{r-1}\) spheres of dimension \(2r-1\) when \(r\geq2\), using zero binomial coefficients outside range. Both dimensions occur naturally when \(3\leq r\leq\min(a,b)\), unlike the known equidimensional formulas for fully whiskered trees.
