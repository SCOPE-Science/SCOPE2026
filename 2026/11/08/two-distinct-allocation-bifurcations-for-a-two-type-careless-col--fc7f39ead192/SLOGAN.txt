In the two-type careless collector with fixed sum of post-acquisition loss probabilities, stationary full-collection availability and mean first-completion speed have different, completely classified optimizer bifurcations. The optimal allocations disagree exactly for loss budgets \(\sqrt2-1<B<2-\sigma_+\), where \(\sigma_+\) is the unique root of \(u^3-u^2+12u-16=0\) in \((1,2)\); in particular, at total loss \(1/2\), balancing maximizes long-run availability but concentrating losses minimizes the mean completion time.
