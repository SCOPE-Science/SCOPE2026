For a vanishing square-root cusp, invariant densities converge to the tent map's uniform density only away from two unequal endpoint layers. At spatial scale \(K_\theta^2/\theta\), the right profile is \(1-(1+s)^{-1/2}\), while the left is its dyadic cascade \(\sum_{j\ge1}2^{-j}[1-(1+s/2^j)^{-1/2}]\); the endpoint slopes diverge with exact constants \(-8\) and \(8/3\) after multiplication by \((2-\theta)^2\).
