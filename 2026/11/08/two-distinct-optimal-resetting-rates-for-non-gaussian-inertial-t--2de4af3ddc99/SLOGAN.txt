Velocity-only Poisson resetting of an inertial Langevin particle yields two different unique optimal rates for non-Gaussian displacement: the fourth-cumulant growth is maximized at \(\lambda/\gamma\simeq0.309107\), while the leading standardized excess-kurtosis coefficient is maximized at \(\lambda/\gamma\simeq2.556537\). Both optima are exact positive roots of explicit degree-six polynomials and are globally unique for every positive damping and noise intensity.
