For \(e^{-|z|^2}|z^n-a|^2\) with \(n\ge3\), every critical point lies on a regular polygonal ray, and the complete Morse portrait has two distinct sharp parameter thresholds. The \(n\) outer peaks cease to be global maxima strictly before they annihilate with \(n\) saddles; both thresholds have exact implicit formulas valid for every degree.
