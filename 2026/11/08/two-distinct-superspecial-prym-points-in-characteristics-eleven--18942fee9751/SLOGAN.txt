For smooth bielliptic Picard Pryms \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+b\), the unique nonordinary coarse parameter is \(j=1\) in characteristic eleven and \(j=-1\) in characteristic thirteen: both Pryms are superspecial, but only in characteristic eleven is the full genus-three Jacobian superspecial. In their natural \(j\)-coordinate, normalized Cartier determinants \(5j^2(1-j)\) and \(4j^2(j+1)^2\) distinguish a simple Hasse zero from a double zero, while the Cartier-rank-zero locus is reduced in both cases.
