For the metric clique with two disjoint deleted edges, the sharp continuous affine \(L^2\)-projection norm is attained exactly at the four deletion endpoints. Deleting one edge creates a first-order amplification, whereas deleting a second disjoint edge **shields** it by a strict third-order correction: \(L_0(q)<L_2(q)<L_1(q)\) and \(q^3(L_1(q)-L_2(q))\to2/3\).
