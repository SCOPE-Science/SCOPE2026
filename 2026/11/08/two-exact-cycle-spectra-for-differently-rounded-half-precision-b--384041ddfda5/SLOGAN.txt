At \(\beta=3/2\), rounding the binary16 Bernoulli-map product before taking its fractional part versus rounding only after reduction yields two completely different five-cycle spectra: \(\{1,3,8,18,164\}\) and \(\{1,3,4,24,41\}\). Exhaustive exact-integer enumeration of all 15,360 states shows that the basin of zero changes from 468 to 5,705 encodings, although only 512 immediate successor edges differ.
