# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim specifies the complete sixteen-state continuous-time generator, its extreme-reservoir boundary operations, the rate normalization, and a unique stationary state. Exact matrix elimination produces a degree-sixteen numerator with a positive denominator for all positive rates. Its coefficient arrays are supplied independently and checked by the standalone exact-arithmetic script. The sign proof reduces to Descartes' rule and two explicit polynomial inequalities, while the negative-positive-negative witness is exact rather than empirical. The rational finite examples are checks, not the proof of the universal quantifier.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The nearest primary work is Sakai and Akimoto, arXiv:2609.03560v1, whose full preprint was inspected in its model, nonlinear-response, symmetry, discussion and appendices. It studies the same open site-disorder process but does not supply an exact two-threshold polarity classification on the proportional two-interior-site family. The related arXiv:2512.03316v1 analyzes an external-field-driven ring; earlier exact single-defect work addresses a different local weak-bias coefficient. Indexed research queries for reentrant site-disorder rectification and the specific four-site scaling did not return a directly covering theorem. Remaining risk: an unindexed finite-chain computation or supplemental material might contain an equivalent result.

## Value
PASS. Keeping the disorder contrast fixed while changing only the common bulk timescale is a natural transport-control experiment. The theorem proves, nonperturbatively and at maximal driving, that the preferred current direction changes twice for *every* nontrivial relative contrast. Thus the polarity cannot be inferred from spatial ordering alone, and a simple one-threshold characterization would be false even in the smallest chain with two independently tunable interior rates. This does not require a large-system limit to be mathematically or experimentally interpretable.

## Boundaries and priority
The exact result is restricted to four sites and saturated reservoir densities. It does not establish comparable behavior for finite bias below saturation, all heterogeneous profiles, or the thermodynamic limit. The cited parent paper's nonlinear analytic treatment is approximate, but its microscopic definition and reflection symmetry are exact. External mathematical priority and independent reproduction are not certified.
