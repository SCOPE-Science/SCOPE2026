At every fixed contrast \(r>1\), scaling the two interior hopping rates \((rh,h)\) of a four-site open exclusion chain produces **exactly two** reversals of its fully biased stationary current-rectification polarity. The unique thresholds straddle \(h=1/r\); the classification follows from an exact degree-16 numerator and a two-sign-change argument valid for all real contrasts.
