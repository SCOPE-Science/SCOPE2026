On every complete bipartite graph, threshold-one Greenberg–Hastings excitation with \(k\ge3\) colors has only the resting fixed point and two explicit families of recurrent configurations: all-color \(k\)-cycles and alternating \(2\lceil k/2\rceil\)-cycles. Their exact labelled populations are products of onto-coloring counts, so nontrivial oscillations appear precisely when both shores have at least \(\lceil k/2\rceil\) vertices.
