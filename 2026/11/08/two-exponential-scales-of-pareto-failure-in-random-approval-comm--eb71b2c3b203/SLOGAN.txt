Under independent fair-coin approvals with a fixed candidate count, the probability that any approval committee is Pareto dominated has two sharp exponential fronts: \(m(m-1)(3/4)^n\), followed when both committee and complement contain at least two candidates by \(6\binom m4(11/16)^n\). The second front persists even when every pair of candidates has incomparable approval columns, quantifying genuine multi-candidate efficiency failures invisible to single-candidate swaps.
