With \(n\) agents sharing positive additive values and exactly \(n+2\) goods, the best value-blind randomized allocation guarantees EFX with sharp probability \(1/(\binom{n+2}{3}+3\binom{n+2}{4})\). Uniformly random nonempty allocations attain this frontier, while two complementary valuation patterns prove no value-independent lottery can do better.
