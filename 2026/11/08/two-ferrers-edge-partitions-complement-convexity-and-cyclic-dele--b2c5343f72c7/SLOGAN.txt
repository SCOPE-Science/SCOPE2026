For bipartite graphs whose row neighborhoods are pairwise incomparable, an edge partition into two Ferrers graphs exists exactly when the bipartite complement has the consecutive-ones property on its columns. Deleting a spanning Hamiltonian cycle from \(K_{n,n}\) produces, for every \(n\ge4\), a connected graph with Ferrers partition number \(3\) but induced matching number \(2\).
