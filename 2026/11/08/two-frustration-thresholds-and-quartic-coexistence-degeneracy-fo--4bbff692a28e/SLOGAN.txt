A two-range antiferromagnetic Ising model on the \(d\)-cube has exact first frustration thresholds \(J_2/J_1=1/(d-1)\) and \(1/(d-3)\). At the second crossing its labeled ground-state degeneracy is \(2d+2\binom d2+24\binom d3+24\binom d4\), a quartic coexistence enhancement proved through a complete four-variable Boolean Fourier classification.
