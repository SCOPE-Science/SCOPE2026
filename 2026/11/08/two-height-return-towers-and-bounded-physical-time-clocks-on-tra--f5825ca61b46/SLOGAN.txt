The polygonal translated-cone caps of Cockram–Ashwin–Rodrigues have an exact two-height return tower, yet the total time of any \(k\) returns differs from its linear prediction by less than \(4n-1\) original iterations. Its complete return-clock law has just two atoms at every horizon, and for rational parameter ratio \(L/\eta=u/v\) the exact least cap period is \(2u+(4n+1)v\), with every coprime positive pair realizable.
