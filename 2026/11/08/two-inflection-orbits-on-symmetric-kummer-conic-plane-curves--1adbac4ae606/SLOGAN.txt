For the smooth symmetric Kummer–conic plane curve \(x^{2n}+y^{2n}+z^{2n}+x^ny^n+x^nz^n+y^nz^n=0\) over \(\mathbb C\), every \(n\ge3\) has exactly two inflection orbits: \(6n\) axis points of tangent contact \(n\), and \(6n^2\) off-axis ordinary flexes. The second orbit is the full preimage of six explicitly determined points of a conic, and no further flexes exist.
