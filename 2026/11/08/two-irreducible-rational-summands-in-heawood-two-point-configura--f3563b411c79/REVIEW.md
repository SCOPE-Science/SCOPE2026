# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim uses the sufficiently subdivided two-particle cubical model, valid because the Heawood graph has girth six and essential-vertex paths have length at least one. The graph has fourteen vertices and twenty-one edges; its unordered two-point model has exactly \(91,252,168\) cells in degrees \(0,1,2\). The verified graph-cycle projection is surjective in rational degree one, with eight proper six-cycle lifts. This gives an upper bound of \(154\) on the rank of the second cellular boundary. Exact finite-field elimination gives the matching lower bound, hence the rational Betti numbers \((1,8,14)\). Its top integral homology is torsion-free as the kernel of a map between free abelian groups. Explicit action signs and all \(336\) automorphisms yield the character, corroborated independently by direct top-kernel actions over a second field. Character inner products establish both rational irreducibles.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Heawood graph is the canonical cubic girth-six cage and has a distinguished \(336\)-element point-line automorphism group. The complete decomposition into a natural graph-cycle representation and an exceptional six-dimensional irreducible is a motivated exact classification, not a generic small-matrix rank. It identifies an unexpected constituent and rules out global invariant degree-two classes. This information is not implied by the known first-homology torsion, second Betti number, or an ordered mod-two computation. The result is restricted to this graph and does not claim a general formula for all graph configurations.
