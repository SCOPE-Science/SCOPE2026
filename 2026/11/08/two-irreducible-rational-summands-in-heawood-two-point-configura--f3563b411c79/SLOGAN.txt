For two unordered particles on the Heawood graph, the full \(336\)-element symmetry action on rational second homology is \(H_2\cong H_1(H;\mathbb Q)\oplus W_6\), a sum of distinct absolutely irreducible eight- and six-dimensional modules. The top integral homology is \(\mathbb Z^{14}\), and there is no nonzero rational class fixed by every symmetry.
