Among the three Hermitian-relative plane quartics over \(\mathbb F_9\) with thirteen rational points and exactly one rational inflexion, one Jacobian splits into three elliptic isogeny factors while the other two each contain a simple abelian-surface factor. Their Weil numerators are determined exactly by exhaustive counts over the first three extension fields, so the common rational-point/inflexion profile does not determine the arithmetic isogeny type.
