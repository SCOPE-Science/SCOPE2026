For the real cubic Walsh subspace on the uniform Boolean cube, the known \(c\,n^{3/2}\) projection-constant asymptotic has a new sharp order-\(\sqrt n\) correction governed by a Bernoulli lattice phase and the parity of \(n\). Its even- and odd-dimensional normalized remainder limit intervals are disjoint, separated by the positive gap \((2\pi)^{-1/2}(1/2-2e^{-3/2})\).
