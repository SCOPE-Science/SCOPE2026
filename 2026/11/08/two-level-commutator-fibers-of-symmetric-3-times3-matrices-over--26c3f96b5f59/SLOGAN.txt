For every odd prime power \(q\), every nonzero skew-symmetric \(3\times3\) matrix is the commutator of exactly \(q^9-q^5\) ordered pairs of symmetric matrices, whereas zero has \(q^9+q^8-q^5\) witnesses. This uniformity across all nonzero targets fails already in dimension four whenever \(q\ge5\), as detected by two different symmetric-centralizer dimensions.
