For the finite-field split square-zero algebra \(K1\oplus V\), every two-local automorphism is exactly a permutation of projective directions with one nonzero scalar for each direction; the complete group is \(K^\times\wr S_{(q^n-1)/(q-1)}\). Two-local rigidity survives only in dimension one and at \((n,q)=(2,2)\), whereas every three-local automorphism is automatically an ordinary algebra automorphism.
