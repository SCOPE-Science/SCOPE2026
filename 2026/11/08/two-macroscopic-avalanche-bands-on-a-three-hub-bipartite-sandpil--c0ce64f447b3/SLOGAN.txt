For the uniformly recurrent Abelian sandpile on \(K_{3,n}\), one grain added at a nonsink hub triggers an avalanche with probability \(\bigl[2-(2/3)^{n-1}\bigr]/n\). Conditioned on a nonzero avalanche, the number of topplings divided by \(n\) converges to an equal mixture of point masses at \(1/3\) and \(2/3\); a doubly toppling source instead produces the exponentially rare maximum \(n+3\).
