The Ising partition functions of the cospectral rook and Shrikhande graphs have an exact field-resolved difference that factors into a sixfold interaction term and a quadratic in \(2\cosh(4\beta h)\). At every positive temperature, their antiferromagnetic partition-function ordering reverses at exactly two positive fields, whose zero-temperature limits are \(J\) and \(3J\); the previously known zero-field comparison alone cannot predict these reversals.
