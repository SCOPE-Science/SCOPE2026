For the three-state antiferromagnetic Potts model on the wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\), \(n\ge5\), the complete positive-field ground-state diagram has exactly two thresholds at \(h=2J\) and \(h=nJ\). The first threshold has \(2L_n\) distinct ground states, yielding exact residual entropy density \(\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)\); the second switches the hub to the favored color.
