On bidirected \(K_{a,2}\) under recipient-side randomized zero forcing, starting in the two-vertex part has an asymptotically finite completion time with exact survival tail \(2^{-t(t-1)/2}\) for the penultimate activation, while starting in the large part takes order \(a\) rounds and converges after division by \(a\) to \(\operatorname{Exp}(2)\). The small-origin mean tends to \(3.641632560655154\ldots\), but the large-origin mean is asymptotic to \(a/2\).
