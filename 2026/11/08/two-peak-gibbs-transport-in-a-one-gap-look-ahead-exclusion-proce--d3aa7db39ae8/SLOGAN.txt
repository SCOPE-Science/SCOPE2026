A one-gap preferred-spacing interaction in a periodic look-ahead exclusion gas can create two separate local maxima in its exact stationary current–density curve, even though the stationary law is Gibbsian. This occurs for every jump length with sufficiently distant, strongly favoured gaps; for a two-site jump and one favoured seven-hole gap of weight \(100\), an exact Descartes certificate proves there are precisely two maxima and one minimum.
