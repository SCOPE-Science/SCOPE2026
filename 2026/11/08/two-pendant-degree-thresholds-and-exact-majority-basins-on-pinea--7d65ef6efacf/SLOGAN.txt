In synchronous binary majority dynamics on a clique with \(s\) leaves attached to one hub, the fixed-state count jumps at \(s=r\), whereas two new primitive two-cycles appear only at \(s=r+1\); odd cliques also carry \(\binom{r}{(r-1)/2}\) mixed two-cycles for every \(s\). All trajectories become periodic within three updates, and when \(s>r\) their complete first-entry distribution has an exact binomial formula, with nearly one-half of initial states attracted to period two.
