For the deterministic core of the three-branch Gharaei–Homburg interval family, a universal two-step return map gives the entire invariant-probability simplex, all pointwise empirical limits, and an exact physical basin of normalized length \(4-6\delta\) for \(1/2\leq\delta<2/3\). The invariant probability becomes nonunique immediately above \(\delta=1/2\), while the unique physical measure disappears at \(\delta=2/3\); the expanding transient inside the attracting gap has a parameter-independent geometric law.
