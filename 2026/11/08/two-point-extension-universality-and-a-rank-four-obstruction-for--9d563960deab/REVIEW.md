# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof treats all parameter boundaries and all four extension patterns uniformly over finite fields. For adjacent/nonadjacent profile construction, the quotient-lift argument ensures an actual third vertex, including the complementary case in which a naive lift could reproduce an input vertex. The both-adjacent witness uses a graph of two linear isomorphisms, valid even at ambient dimension \(n=2k\). The both-nonadjacent witness splits into intersecting and complementary pairs. A three-point pencil gives an explicit, unavoidable rank-four separating sentence. The graph-to-rank-three EF argument uses at most two already selected distinct vertices. Exact, independent finite experiments inspect 595, 11935, and 8385 pairs in three examples and 40 sampled pairs in a fourth, with pencil witnesses. Computations are corroboration, not the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** The closest same-object sources are the all-parameter q-Kneser paper of Chowdhury–Godsil–Royle [4] and Blokhuis–Brouwer–Szőnyi's q-Kneser coloring paper [2], whose inspected graph definitions, coloring, homomorphism and coclique results do not imply uniform local two-point extension or the forbidden rank-four pattern. Glasby–Niemeyer–Praeger [3] investigates a related bipartite two-copy graph and a probability of closed three-arcs; this does not give the single disjointness graph's all-pair logical extension classification. The 2026 Johnson model-theory paper [1] motivates bounded-depth comparison and points to Grassmann structures, but its Johnson adjacency results do not imply this subspace-disjointness theorem. Existing bounded-depth work on Grassmann adjacency does cover the \(k=2\) complement subcase, which is explicitly identified as prior coverage; the new scope includes all \(k\ge3\), the sharp \(n=2k\) boundary and the 3-point pencil obstruction. Searches across the research database for the exact statement, aliases, threshold, and EF equivalence found no direct equivalent; this is not uniqueness proof. Residual historical-priority risk: specialist papers on existential closure of subspace-disjointness graphs.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact two-extension property at the minimum possible ambient dimension \(n=2k\), contrasted with universal failure at three extensions, is a natural logical expressivity boundary for the classical q-Kneser association scheme. The four-class complete classification and explicit finite geometric obstruction apply simultaneously to arbitrary field size and subspace rank. This is not a single arbitrary parameter slice, a routine sample enumeration, or an unmotivated finite statistic.

**Limitations.** Rank four is separated from the Rado graph but is not itself classified. The computer checker is restricted to prime fields and selected cases. General first-order spectra and priority against unindexed sources remain open. No independent mathematical audit has been conducted.
