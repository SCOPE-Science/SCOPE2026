The difference between adjacency operators of two opposite-character finite-field spheres is always invertible in odd dimension at least three: its absolute determinant is exactly \(2^{q^d/p}q^{(d-1)q^d/2}\), with no prime divisors beyond \(2\) and \(p\). Its sharp spectral condition number is \(\csc(\pi/(2p))\), independent of dimension, extension degree, quadratic form, and the chosen opposing radii.
