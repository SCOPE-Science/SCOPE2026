Symmetric edge polytopes of trees all have the same crosspolytope face lattice, yet their Haar-random Euclidean widths have sharply different high-dimensional fluctuation regimes: Gumbel under bounded degrees and maxima of finitely many half-normal variables for trees with a fixed set of heavily populated hubs. The path-width variance is asymptotic to \(2\pi^2/(3n\log n)\), while the star-width variance is asymptotic to \(4(1-2/\pi)/n\); strikingly, one and two hubs have different limiting laws but equal leading variance coefficients.
