Among pencils of three geometrically smooth quadric surfaces in \(\mathbb P^3(\mathbb F_2)\), exactly \(10{,}080\) have smooth elliptic-quartic base curves, forming two projective orbits of \(5{,}040\) pencils each. Their bases have \(2\) and \(4\) rational points and distinct Frobenius traces \(1\) and \(-1\); the respective projective stabilizers are Klein-four and cyclic-four groups.
