Every weighted annular Szegő kernel in the Laurent-series family has exactly two simple critical-point orbits under the annulus's multiplicative deck transformation: one positive real, one negative real. As the hole shrinks, the two critical points collapse at the scale of the inner radius, and a fixed kernel fiber has eventually exactly two, one, or zero stationary points according as \(t|a|^2\) is below, equal to, or above one.
