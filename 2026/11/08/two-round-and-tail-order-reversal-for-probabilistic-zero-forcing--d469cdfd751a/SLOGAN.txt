For every \(b\ge3\), singleton-seeded probabilistic zero forcing on \(K_{2,b}\) finishes within two rounds more often when seeded in the smaller part, yet finishes by all sufficiently large deadlines more often when seeded in the larger part. The exact long-time survival bases are \((1-1/b)^b\) and \(1/4\), so neither seed's completion time stochastically dominates the other's.
