Two simultaneous stochastic triadic-closure rounds on any tree have a universal connection-probability kernel determined only by original distances one through four. This radial universality breaks sharply at the third round: relocating a single leaf among two five-vertex trees changes a distance-three edge probability by exactly \(p^4(2-p)(1-p)^4\), while the two-round kernel gives a closed mean-degree limit for sparse Erdős–Rényi backbones.
