A single modal-depth-one formula with one propositional variable makes a two-agent initially \(\mathsf{S5}\) believed-announcement model change exactly \(n\) times on \(n\) worlds, even when each agent's equivalence classes have size at most two. By contrast, with one \(\mathsf{S5}\) agent and \(r\) propositional variables, the exact maximum is \(\min\{n,2^r\}\); its valuation-type upper-bound mechanism is previously known.
