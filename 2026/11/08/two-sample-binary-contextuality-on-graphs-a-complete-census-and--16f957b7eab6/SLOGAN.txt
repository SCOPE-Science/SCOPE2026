For binary pairwise empirical models supported on at most two equiprobable outcomes per context, every no-disturbance model on a graph is either probabilistically noncontextual or strongly contextual, with no intermediate contextual fraction. Exact labelled counts are \(\sum_{U\subseteq V}2^{|V|-|U|+|E(G[U])|}\) total and \(\sum_{U\subseteq V}2^{|V|-c(U)}\) noncontextual; the four-cycle has \(136\) total and \(8\) strongly contextual models, and the dichotomy already fails at denominator three.
