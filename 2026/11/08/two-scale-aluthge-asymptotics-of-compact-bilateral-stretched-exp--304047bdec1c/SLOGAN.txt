For compact bilateral shifts with weights \(e^{-a|k|^\alpha}\), generalized Aluthge iteration decays at sharp logarithmic rate \(a\,\mathbb E|Z|^\alpha[nt(1-t)]^{\alpha/2}\). At the Laplace exponent the near-largest singular values form a Gaussian window of width \(n^{1/4}\) with an exact Schatten prefactor, while at the quadratic exponent the window is lattice-pinned and exhibits rational/irrational modulation.
