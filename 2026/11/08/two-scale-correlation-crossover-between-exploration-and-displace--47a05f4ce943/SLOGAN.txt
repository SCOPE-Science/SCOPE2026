The normalized correlation of the number of distinct flipped coordinates and the current Hamming displacement in a uniform hypercube walk has an explicit nonconstant limit \(\rho(c)\) at \(t/n\to c>0\), but decays as \(n^{-\beta/2+o(1)}\) when \(t/(n\log n)\to\beta>0\). An exact finite covariance, recoverable by summing a classical richness–frequency identity, and a parity variance formula establish the two-scale law.
