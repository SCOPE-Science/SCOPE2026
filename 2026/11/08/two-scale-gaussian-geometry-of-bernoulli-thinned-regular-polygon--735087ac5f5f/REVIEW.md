# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The radius-one regular-polygon perimeter and area are exact sine sums over positive cyclic gaps. Seventh-order Taylor remainders are uniform even for exceptional macroscopic gaps; Bernoulli geometric run tails make them negligible in the respective \(N^{5/2}\) and \(N^{9/2}\) limits. Joint renewal-reward centered gaps yield the covariance \(p\mathbb E[(G^r-\rho_rG)(G^s-\rho_sG)]\). The cyclic cut changes only geometrically tailed terminal contributions. Stopped-reward fourth-moment bounds secure \(L^2\) convergence of the scaled covariance, rather than just weak convergence. Exact cancellation of cubic gap contributions in area minus twice perimeter gives the fifth-gap transverse limit, and an infinite-support polynomial argument proves positive definiteness for all fixed interior retention probabilities. Determinant and correlation asymptotics follow from the exact linear identity between the observables. The independent exact rational moment program and exhaustive small-case geometry checks passed.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
Turchi and Wespi (arXiv:1706.02944, 2017) analyze intrinsic-volume CLTs for samples on a fixed smooth convex boundary; their model is not independent thinning of vertices on a refining circular lattice and does not imply the two-scale covariance. Madras and KC (2006) supply finite three-vertex regular-polygon expectations, not fluctuation laws for a positive-density growing selection. Published research on Hausdorff approximation of regular polygons and density-optimal random sampling on ellipses likewise studies other observables or sampling mechanisms. Searches for thinning, random polygon area/perimeter covariance, gap-reward laws, and fifth-order fluctuations did not locate a statement implying the sharp determinant and correlation limits. Wang, Li and Xu (2023) already use cubic-gap cancellation and fifth-gap expansions for Dirichlet random cyclic polygons; cancellation by itself is **not** claimed as new. Their continuous-gamma, fixed-count normalized-spacing model does not imply the geometric renewal covariance in the Bernoulli lattice model, or its sharp determinant/correlation coefficient. Classical renewal-reward CLT is used as an ingredient, not claimed as new. Unindexed renewal and circular-spacing formulations remain a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The fluctuations of two canonical convex-geometric observables become asymptotically rank one despite retaining nontrivial randomness, and the theorem identifies the first *nondegenerate* residual after canceling the leading cubic-gap contribution. The sharp \(N^{-14}\) covariance determinant and \(N^{-4}\) correlation deficit quantify loss of identifiability of the two geometric measurements, with explicit coefficients and a full interval of retention parameters. This two-scale geometric distinction is more informative than an isolated routine CLT or expectation formula.

## Reproducibility and limitations
Run `python verify_polygon.py` with the Python standard library. It exhaustively checks exact geometric formulas for all nonempty subsets of regular polygons through nine vertices, independently computes renewal moments via exact fractions, and compares simulated centered reward covariances for one interior retention parameter. Finite simulation cannot prove the all-parameter infinite law. No quantitative normal approximation or endpoint-parameter uniformity is asserted; completeness of historical priority is provisional.
