Deleting each vertex of a fine regular polygon independently creates area and perimeter deficits whose leading random fluctuations are perfectly correlated, but the distinct fifth-power gap fluctuations reappear on a sharper scale. A nondegenerate two-scale Gaussian law yields the exact \(N^{-14}\) covariance-determinant order and the sharp \(N^{-4}\) correlation deficit for every fixed retention probability \(p\in(0,1)\).
