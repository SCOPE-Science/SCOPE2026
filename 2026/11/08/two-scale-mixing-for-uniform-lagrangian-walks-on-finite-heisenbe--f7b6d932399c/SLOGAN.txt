A finite Heisenberg random walk that alternates randomly between two full coordinate subgroups has a completely explicit four-positive-level convolution spectrum and exact chi-square divergence at every positive time. As field size grows, its total-variation distance follows the elementary fixed-step profile \(t/2^{t-1}\) and vanishes at any diverging number of steps, although its chi-square divergence has a sharp logarithmic transition and may still diverge.
