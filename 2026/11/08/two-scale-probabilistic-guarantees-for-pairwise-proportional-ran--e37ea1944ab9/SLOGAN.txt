Under impartial culture, two pairwise-proportional representative rankings exist with high probability even when the number of alternatives grows as \(m=o(3^{n/3})\): reverse the order of unanimous-consensus blocks while retaining each block's internal order. At \(m\sim c2^{n/2}\), literal reversal succeeds with limiting probability \(e^{-c^2}\), yet the block construction succeeds with probability tending to one.
