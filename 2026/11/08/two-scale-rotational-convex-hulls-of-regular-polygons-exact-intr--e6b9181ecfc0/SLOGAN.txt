For any two concentric regular \(n\)-gons with circumradius ratio \(r\le1\), the convex hull changes from an \(n\)-gon to an alternating \(2n\)-gon at exactly \(r\cos(\pi/n-\theta)=\cos(\pi/n)\); its area and perimeter then increase strictly until the half-step rotation \(\theta=\pi/n\). At the sharp radius threshold \(r=\cos(\pi/n)\), maximal area gain turns on linearly but maximal perimeter gain turns on quadratically.
