# Review of the two-scale two-tap Hardy spectral law

## Correctness
PASS. The exact orthogonal decomposition by monomial residues converts the original compact weighted composition operator into finitely many geometric Jacobi factors. The full trace formula follows by summing closed tridiagonal walks according to their minimum height; this gives one geometrical denominator, canceled by the residue sum. At all three double-scaling regimes, exact normalized even moments uniquely determine finite squared-singular-value-weighted measures on a common compact interval. Explicit measures have those moments and no positive atoms, which justifies each strict-threshold counting limit. The norm interpolation uses the same block factorization and positivity of a compact irreducible Jacobi matrix. Finite rational tests are corroborating checks, not substitutes for the infinite proofs.

## Originality
PASS on a qualified best-knowledge basis. The closest fully inspected primary result is Sun--Liu, arXiv:2610.19658v1, Theorem 9.2, with a **single monomial multiplier**, whose singular values are orthogonal-column data. The present two-tap multiplier couples neighboring columns and requires a Jacobi reduction. Pushnitski, arXiv:1710.02652, Theorem 1.2, treats a fixed bandwidth with power-decaying entries, not a growing bandwidth and geometric concentration. Putinar--Tener, arXiv:1612.03970, supplies general Hardy weighted-composition singular-value bounds but not the stated exact law. Accessible prior statements and indexed related findings were compared by implication, not title alone. Residual risk: a broader double-scaling banded-matrix spectral theorem might subsume part of the distribution; the older Gallardo-Gutiérrez--Kumar--Partington paper was inspected at abstract level only. Independent publication-priority review remains appropriate.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The problem isolates the first non-monomial weight in a recently studied composition--differentiation model and answers how singular spectra change when spatial bandwidth competes with damping length. It provides a full pointwise singular-counting distribution, a strictly monotone operator-norm interpolation, exact all-even Schatten moments, and a demonstrable noncommutation of the two natural limiting operations. The Hilbert--Schmidt moment is insensitive while every higher even moment detects the crossover. This is a motivated structural limit law rather than merely a coefficient check for one fixed parameter.

## Limitations
The result covers a classical Hardy space, a linear radial contraction and a two-tap analytic weight. Interior Jacobi singular values are specified by a compact operator rather than elementary closed-form numbers. No uniform threshold convergence rate is asserted, and originality depends on best-knowledge access to primary literature. The exact-rational checker tests only finite identities and tail estimates.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
