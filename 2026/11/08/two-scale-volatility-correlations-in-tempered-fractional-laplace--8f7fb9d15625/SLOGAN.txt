Squared-displacement correlations in the tempered fractional Laplace transport model decrease strictly with the separation between nonoverlapping windows but retain a nonzero heterogeneous-diffusivity floor. For long windows their universal plateau is \(1/(2\alpha+3)\) with a separation-independent \(L^{-1}\) gamma-clock correction, while the first separation-sensitive term appears at order \(L^{-2}\) and exactly records the tempered Gaussian memory kernel.
