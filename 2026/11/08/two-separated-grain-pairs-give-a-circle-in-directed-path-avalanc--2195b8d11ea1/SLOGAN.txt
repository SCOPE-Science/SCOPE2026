On a right-directed path with a terminal sink, the four-grain binary configuration consisting of two adjacent pairs separated by \(g\ge1\) zeros, followed by any \(t\ge0\) zeros, has an avalanche complex that simplicially collapses onto a \((g+2)\)-cycle in exactly \(7+5t\) elementary pairs. Adding the fourth grain reduces the previously known first-homology rank \(t+2\) to one, an arbitrarily large change caused by a single grain.
