For every nonzero homogeneous cubic radial nonlinearity in a planar quartic rigid isochronous flow, the periodicity basin has infinite Euclidean area, but its area inside a radius-\(R\) disk grows with exactly one of two sharp powers: \(R^{1/2}\) or \(R^{5/4}\), according to quadratic or quartic contact at the highest angular-potential peaks. The same contacts produce original-physical-time critical escape powers \(2/3\) and \(4/3\); both cases occur with explicit leading constants.
