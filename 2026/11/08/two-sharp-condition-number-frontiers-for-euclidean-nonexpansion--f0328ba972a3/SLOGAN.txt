For three-coordinate Gauss–Seidel on the equicorrelated positive-definite family, ordinary Euclidean nonexpansion has exactly two sharp correlation frontiers: \(\rho_-=-0.428933\ldots\) and \(\rho_+=0.615032\ldots\), determined by \(6\rho^4+2\rho^2-\rho-1=0\). The same condition number can cause one-sweep expansion for positive correlations but contraction for negative correlations: the thresholds are \(\kappa_+=5.792854\ldots\) and \(\kappa_-=10.053433\ldots\), even though every system in this family is asymptotically convergent.
