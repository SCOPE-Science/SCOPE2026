For positive quartic axial tilts of uniform spherical measure, the sharp \(L^4/L^2\) ratio of real linear functions has two separate dimension-dependent geometric bifurcations: an oblique minimizing cone appears from dimension five, and an axial-to-equatorial maximizing transition appears from dimension eleven. The full all-parameter classification includes exact endpoint values, every equality direction, and a nonnegative quadratic stability identity.
