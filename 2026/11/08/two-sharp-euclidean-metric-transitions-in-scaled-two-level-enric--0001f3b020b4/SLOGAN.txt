For two comparable antichain levels, the blockwise-scaled enriched order polytope has exact minimum Euclidean width \(2/\sqrt{\max\{a^{-2}+(2b^2)^{-1},b^{-2}\}}\), with a sharp change from three-supported minimizing normals to coordinate normals at \(a=\sqrt2\,b\). Its diameter switches independently at \(3ma^2=4nb^2\), and, in the Ehrhart-equivalent unscaled case, its width is exactly \(2/\sqrt3\) times that of the enriched chain partner.
