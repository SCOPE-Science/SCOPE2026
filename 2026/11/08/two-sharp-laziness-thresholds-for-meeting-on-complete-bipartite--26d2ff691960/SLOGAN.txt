For two independent synchronous lazy walkers on an unbalanced complete bipartite graph with both parts non-singleton, the ordering of mean first-meeting times across starting placements changes at two sharp holding-probability thresholds on opposite sides of \(1/3\). Balanced bicliques instead have a unique universal fairness point, \(p=1/3\), at which every distinct starting pair has the same mean meeting time; stars have one sharp crossover.
