## Correctness
PASS. The displayed monomial norms follow from a Beta integral in the explicitly stated normalized measure. The rational kernel numerators follow coefficientwise; a separate rational-checker replay corroborated them. For order one, the quadratic signs and discriminant exclude all other disk roots. For order two, the negative root is unique below \(t=1\), the cubic discriminant excludes additional real roots, and Vieta bounds force the conjugate pair outside the unit circle. Above \(t=1\), the unique positive root and conjugate pair are all outside by the exact value at \(R=(t+2)/(t-1)\). The equality thresholds are boundary points rather than interior zeros. Integrability requires \(a>-1\), not \(a\ge0\).

## Originality
PASS, subject to the stated historical-priority risk. The closest general radial-zero theorem (Perälä, 2017) asserts finiteness and constructions, not these two fixed-family sharp thresholds. Miña-Díaz (2022) addresses powers of interior Blaschke factors without the Jacobi boundary factor. Beberok (2017) studies ellipsoid-domain kernels, not this continuous interior-singularity family. Two published weighted-disk kernel classifications cover distinct families: a deficit \(1-q|z|^{2\gamma}\) and the Euclidean distance weight \((1-|z|)^v\). Their identical base case \(a=0,m=1\) is acknowledged as covered, and the present theorem's two continuous critical values are not implied by it. An unindexed classical hypergeometric treatment is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. An explicit Lu Qi-Keng transition is a natural structural invariant of an analytic Hilbert space. The first two Jacobi boundary orders are the smallest nonconstant boundary vanishing weights, and their distinct sharp thresholds show that the singularity required to create a kernel zero depends quantitatively on boundary order. The result locates every interior scalar zero and excludes all others rather than merely exhibiting a counterexample. It neither asserts a general all-order classification nor claims broader consequences without proof.
