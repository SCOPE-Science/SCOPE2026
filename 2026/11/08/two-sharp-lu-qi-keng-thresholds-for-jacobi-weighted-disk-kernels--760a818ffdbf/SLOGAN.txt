For the first two Jacobi boundary orders, an integrable radial power singularity creates exactly one negative-real weighted Bergman-kernel zero at sharp, different thresholds: \(a+1<1/\sqrt2\) for order one and \(a+1<(\sqrt7-1)/2\) for order two. At or above each threshold the entire bidisk kernel is zero-free, and the transition occurs at the excluded scalar boundary point \(s=-1\).
