Two-stage Radau IIA heat flow on a rectangular rook graph \(K_p\square K_q\) is entrywise nonnegative at every timestep exactly when \(pq\le12\); all distance-two transitions nevertheless stay strictly positive at every positive timestep. On square rook graphs, a separate sharp edge-sign threshold occurs between \(m=37\) and \(m=38\): for larger \(m\), every adjacent transition is negative on exactly one bounded timestep interval.
