For the deterministic nonnegative Gauss–Newton update on every SPD quadratic with eigenvalues in \([a,b]\), the exact worst one-step objective factor has a sharp regularization-driven switch from a slow eigenmode to an extreme-eigenvalue mixture. Its unique best regularization \(\sigma=2(a+b)/(b-a)^2\) achieves the optimal fixed-gradient-descent factor \(((b-a)/(b+a))^2\), while sufficiently large regularization creates strict objective increases precisely above the sharp condition-number barrier \(3+2\sqrt2\).
