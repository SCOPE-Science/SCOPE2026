For three-coordinate exact descent on an equicorrelated positive-definite quadratic started at the all-ones vector, replacing a random permutation with three independent random coordinate draws has a complete four-pattern loss law. Above the sharp correlation threshold \(\rho=1/2\), sampling with replacement beats the without-replacement objective on exactly \(2/9\) of epochs, while its mean loss is larger until the distinct threshold \(\rho=4/5\).
