In uniform random key graphs with fixed ring size, the joint third cumulant of three distinct isolation indicators has a sharp positive–negative–positive two-transition pattern as the number of vertices grows proportional to the key-pool size. The two crossings occur asymptotically at \(n/P=1/K\) and \(n/P=1/K+2/(3K^2)\), with an exact finite-size formula and at most two transitions for every admissible pool.
