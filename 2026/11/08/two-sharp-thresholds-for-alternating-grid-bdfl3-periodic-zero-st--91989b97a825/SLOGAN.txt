For the optimized variable-step BDFL\(3_2\) method on alternating steps \(h,rh\), homogeneous periodic zero-stability holds exactly for \(R_*^{-1}\le r\le R_*\), where \(R_*=4.7562683254\ldots\), but every decaying scalar test equation has uniquely solvable stages for all positive step scales only when \(1/4\le r\le4\). Thus a genuinely zero-stable alternating mesh can still encounter a positive-real singular implicit step, already at \(r=9/2\).
