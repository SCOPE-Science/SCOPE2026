For an equal-amplitude five-harmonic signal analyzed with a Gaussian window, the four spectrogram zeros per time period undergo exactly two sharp pairwise-collision transitions, at dimensionless gaps \(\sigma\Omega=\sqrt{\log 2}\) and \(\sigma\Omega=\sqrt{\log(1+\sqrt5)}\). Their full locations and multiplicities follow from an explicit reciprocal quartic, revealing three distinct zero geometries separated by two double-zero configurations.
