# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-size claim follows from the graph Laplacian's constant kernel on connected graphs, a three-phase local threshold table with necessary-and-sufficient intervals, and a labelled multinomial count. The complement map is applied to integer configurations before nonnegativity is proved, then one-fire interval necessity establishes nonnegativity for complements of every two-fire periodic state. A mass-baseline identity \(mn+\sum_i a_i b_i\), the cross-phase edge width identity \(mn-\sum_i a_i b_i-m-n\), and disjoint support of excess phase counts prove the two *full* bands. Explicit three-case support enumeration yields the outer endpoint coefficient. Direct state iteration and an independently constructed phase-assignment enumeration corroborate these steps. Finite checks cannot establish the universal theorem or novelty.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The closest primary text is Jiang (arXiv:1003.0943), whose Sections 2–3 prove allowed periods, firing-count equality and chip complementation but not a mass generating polynomial. Jiang–Scully–Zhang (arXiv:1211.6786) classify possible vertex firing strings, not the joint two-shore phase population and mass coefficients. Ji–Li–Wang (arXiv:2408.10508), Theorem 1, excludes periods three and four in the middle band; its claim neither implies every exterior mass is occupied nor determines coefficients. A previously documented formula covers only \(K_{3,b}\); that exact thin-side slice is included for uniformity but is not an original claim. The genuinely additional domain \(m,n\ge4\) requires optimizing \(\sum_i a_i b_i\) and counting complementary excess supports, yielding the two-variable polynomial and the new sharp coefficient \(E_{m,n}\). Relevant exact mass-polynomial searches did not reveal an equivalent theorem. An unindexed source or a previously unnoticed stronger generating-function identity remains a priority risk, not a proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

Total chips are conserved, so the mass-resolved populations are intrinsic to fixed-energy sandpile dynamics. Existence of primitive period three follows from prior work, but that alone does not determine how many configurations occur at a given chip total or why the outer energy bands have no internal gaps. The two-shore overlap statistic and the lower-boundary coefficient are substantive structural information absent from the thin-side specialization. The result supplies an exact infinite-family benchmark with a sharp middle gap and explicit endpoint populations; it does not purport to classify attraction basins or higher periods.

## Closest coverage and scientific limitations

The prior thin-shore identity agrees with the present formula when a shore has size three and is explicitly excluded as a novel contribution. Earlier period-existence, firing-pattern and middle-band-exclusion theorems do not imply the new two-shore coefficient distribution. Only public texts actually examined have been assessed; broad source coverage cannot establish absolute priority. The proof is an ordinary mathematical argument and tests are supporting evidence.
