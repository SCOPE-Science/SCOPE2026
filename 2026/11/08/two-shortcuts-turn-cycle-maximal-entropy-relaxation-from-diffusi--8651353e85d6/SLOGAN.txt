On a cycle with \(2L\) vertices, adding two oppositely situated span-two chords creates two adjacency bound states whose gap is exactly asymptotic to \(C_*q_*^L\), where \(q_*^3+q_*^2+q_*-1=0\) and \(C_*\) is given explicitly. Consequently the maximal-entropy walk's positive-mode relaxation becomes exponential in \(L\), whereas it is bounded with just one chord and diffusive without chords; the degree-normalized walk remains diffusive even with both chords.
