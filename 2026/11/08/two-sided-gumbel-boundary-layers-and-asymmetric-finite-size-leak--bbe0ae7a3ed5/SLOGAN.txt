For a Brownian gas in a shared two-state switching harmonic trap, the apparent compact support of the rescaled rightmost-particle law has two sharp finite-population fractional-Gumbel boundary layers with logarithmic exponents fixed by the two switching-rate-to-stiffness ratios. At the unshifted limiting endpoints, the lower-edge leak is enhanced by \(\log\log N\), while the upper-edge leak acquires an extra \((\log N)^{-1/2}\) factor, and each rare event localizes the hidden trap variance on a \(1/\log N\) scale.
