On a rooted unit-edge metric tree with \(d\ge2\) middle branches and \(e\ge2\) leaves per middle branch, the two leading Hardy integration singular values are exactly \(1/\arctan(1/\sqrt{de+d+e})\) and \(1/\arctan(1/\sqrt e)\). The largest singular value has complete branching-product collisions, but the first two together recover both branching numbers, and the second alone with its multiplicity is already a complete spectral fingerprint.
