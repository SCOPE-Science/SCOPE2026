Uniform vertices of power lecture-hall simplices have two spectral edges: a known weighted-Brownian-bridge Bessel continuum at the top and a different compact Jacobi spectrum at the bottom. For fixed integer \(p\ge1\), their Euclidean covariance condition number grows sharply as \(n^{2p+2}\), with a specified spectral constant, although every member has the face lattice of a simplex.
