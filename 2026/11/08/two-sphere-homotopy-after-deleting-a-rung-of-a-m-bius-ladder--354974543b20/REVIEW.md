# Review: Two-sphere homotopy after deleting a rung of a Möbius ladder

## Correctness — PASS
For \(n=4k+1\), vertex 0 has contractible deletion as established by Agarwal–Basak's vertex-deletion lemma, and the link admits the enumerated folds onto a single \(C_{6k}\). The cycle independence-complex formula and cofiber construction yield two \(2k\)-spheres. For \(n=4k+2\), the two explicit fold lists reduce deletion and link to \((2k+1)K_2\) and \(2kK_2\), respectively. Connectivity \(\pi_{2k-1}(S^{2k})=0\) makes the attaching map null. Exact graph and fold tests for 20 parameters per residue and direct mod-two Betti calculations for four small parameters corroborate, rather than replace, these uniform arguments.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks
Agarwal–Basak (arXiv:2608.30601v1, Lemma 9, Theorem 12) classify the unmodified Möbius ladder and its relevant vertex deletion, but not a graph with one antipodal edge removed. The published dihedral rational-homology theorem for unmodified ladders has no edge-deficient input; the Wagner-graph matching-complex calculation studies a different simplicial complex and only a finite eight-vertex graph. Primary literature and actual indexed record searches, including variants of missing rung, antipodal edge deletion, Möbius ladder independence-complex homotopy, and stronger coverage, identified no implication of the new two-residue-class theorem. An unindexed equivalent and uninspected details in the 2026-09-14 revision remain risks; failed searches do not prove absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS
The classification measures a natural, minimal symmetry-breaking perturbation of the newly classified Möbius-ladder independence complexes. It establishes an infinite family of exact **integral homotopy** types, including the homotopically different outcome from unmodified ladders, by a constructive fold-and-cofiber method. Both consecutive congruence classes share a uniform two-sphere outcome although their deletion/link mechanisms differ; this is not a numeric recomputation or routine reformulation of the unmodified theorem.

## Limitations and literature
The other congruence classes, deletion of a cycle edge, and equivariant structure are open to further analysis. The finite tests are not infinite proof. Literature: Agarwal–Basak arXiv:2608.30601v1 (2026-08-31), especially Lemma 9; Kozlov, *Complexes of directed trees* (1999); the 2026-10-11 dihedral homology record and the distinct Wagner matching-complex edge-orbit record.
