Removing one antipodal rung from the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) produces an independence complex homotopy equivalent to exactly two spheres \(S^{2k}\vee S^{2k}\) whenever \(n=4k+1\) or \(n=4k+2\), \(k\ge1\). Explicit uniform graph folds and a null attaching-map argument establish the integral homotopy type.
