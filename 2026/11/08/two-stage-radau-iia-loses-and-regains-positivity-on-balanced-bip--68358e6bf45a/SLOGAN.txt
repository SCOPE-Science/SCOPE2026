For third-order, L-stable two-stage Radau IIA heat diffusion on \(K_{m,m}\), every end-step transition matrix is nonnegative at every step size exactly when \(m\le5\). Starting with \(K_{6,6}\), an exact quartic gives one forbidden intermediate timestep band, followed by guaranteed positivity recovery for all sufficiently large steps; every off-diagonal remains positive throughout.
