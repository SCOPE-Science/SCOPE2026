On the unique 19-vertex Robertson cage, the antiferromagnetic Ising ground state has only two positive-field magnetization plateaus before saturation: magnetizations \(3\) and \(5\), switching at \(h/J=2\) and \(4\). Exact full-state enumeration proves the two critical fields and their finite degeneracies; the known independent-set count \(1950\) at the saturation field is not claimed as new.
