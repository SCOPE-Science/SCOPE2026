For every fixed finite-field matrix \(A\), the exact rank-and-image-overlap counts of square-zero matrices \(B\) satisfying \(AB=BA=0\) depend only on \(\operatorname{rank}A\) and \(\operatorname{rank}(A^2)\). The largest possible rank of \(B\) is exactly \(\lfloor(n-\operatorname{rank}(A^2))/2\rfloor\), and every lower nonnegative rank occurs.
