Among odd indices, the number of odd strongly unimodal coefficients congruent to \(2\pmod4\) has a two-term expansion beginning \(\frac{\pi^2}{12}\frac{X}{\log X}\), with an explicit second constant involving \(\zeta'(2)/\zeta(2)\). Odd coefficients themselves occur only \(\sqrt{2X/3}+O(1)\) times up to height \(X\), so the nonzero residues follow two distinct sparse scales.
