For integers with a fixed number of Zeckendorf summands, residue-class counts have an explicit two-term asymptotic determined by one Fibonacci period; the first correction records repeated indices and forbidden adjacent pairs. The conditional proportions of Zeckendorf–Niven integers begin with the nonuniform values \(5/9\), \(85/256\), and \(7/27\) for two, three, and four summands.
