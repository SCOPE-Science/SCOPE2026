For linear priority games on \(G(n,p)\), the optimal potential surrogate's missing incentive-energy fraction undergoes two sharp network transitions: a Bernoulli first-conflict window at \(p\asymp n^{-2}\) and a mean-degree crossover at \(p\asymp n^{-1}\). At \(p=\lambda/n\), it approaches \(1/(\lambda+2)\) with exact Gaussian fluctuation variance \(2(1+\lambda)/(\lambda+2)^4\), independently of priority levels or deterministic tie-breaking.
