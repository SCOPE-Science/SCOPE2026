The multipath complexes of the symmetric bidirected graphs \(K_{2,4}\) and \(K_{2,5}\) contain genuine integral two-torsion: their homotopy types are respectively \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^7S^2\) and \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{31}S^3\). Exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matchings and signed degree-two attaching matrices prove both Moore-space splittings.
