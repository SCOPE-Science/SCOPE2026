Among tournaments formed by cyclically composing three nonempty transitive tournaments, two-variable first-order logic cannot distinguish any pair, even with unrestricted quantifier depth. Three variables already determine the three block sizes up to cyclic rotation and thus the entire isomorphism type; the exact spectrum at order \(N\) is \(\bigl(\binom{N-1}{2}+2\mathbf 1_{3\mid N}\bigr)/3\).
