# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The model defines rooted future diversity by survivor-spanning root paths and states its exact tree-length and independence hypotheses. The variance follows from independent singleton survival and clade survival. An explicit rational Bernstein positivity certificate proves the one-peak case for all \(m\le6\); a degree-bound and three strict signs prove that for every \(m\ge7\), some two-block lengths cause two maxima. The strictly positive eight-tip star is checked by an independent enumeration of all 256 survivor sets, an exact polynomial identity, four signed derivative evaluations and Descartes' bound. The proofs do not substitute finite enumeration for the universal claims.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest prior work is Faller, Pardi and Steel (2007/2008, arXiv:0708.0425), which supplies the general rooted-PD variance identity and even a structurally similar one-leaf-plus-clade tree for a *different* asymptotic question. The exact seven-member clade peak-bifurcation threshold and positive-edge two-peak example are not contained in the inspected source material; obtaining them needs a nontrivial polynomial sign analysis. Nipperess and Matsen (arXiv:1208.6552, 2012) instead treat fixed-count rarefaction. Search-based priority is provisional; an unlocated independent prior analysis of variance-curve shape remains a risk. No novelty is claimed for the general variance formula, the topology, or the field-of-bullets model.

## Value — PASS
The quantity is the variance of retained evolutionary history, a natural risk observable that supplements expected biodiversity. The exact cutoff isolates competition between two biologically interpretable scales (singleton and entire clade) and shows that high-risk variability can have two distinct peaks even under i.i.d. species extinction. The finite positive-edge witness rules out a spurious zero-length-only effect. This is a structural risk-curve result, not an arbitrary numerical invariant, though no policy or empirical implication is asserted.

## Limits and nearby literature
The iff threshold holds only in the clearly defined two-block reduced model, **not across all trees**. The eight-taxon witness has positive lengths but is not claimed globally minimal. Independence and homogeneous survival are essential, and the 2007 general variance identity is prior work. Independent historical-priority assessment is outstanding.
