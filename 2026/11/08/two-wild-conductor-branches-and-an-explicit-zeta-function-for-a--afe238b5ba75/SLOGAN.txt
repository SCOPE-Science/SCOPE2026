For a general characteristic-two quartic ruled surface with double twisted cubic, the smooth elliptic conductor has exactly two wildly ramified points of different exponent \(2\), rather than the four tame pinch points of the classical picture. An explicit surface over \(\mathbb F_2\) has zeta function \(1/((1-T)(1-2T)^2(1-4T)(1+T+2T^2))\), determined from its split normalization and conductor.
