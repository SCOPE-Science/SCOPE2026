The twenty unlabelled Pappus three-edge-colourings form precisely two isolated ten-state Kempe sectors, each made of three tetrahedra sharing a single hub: \(\mathcal K(P)\cong 2[K_1\vee(3K_3)]\). Within either sector every state is at most two switches away from every other, and the hub is the unique state at distance one from all nine others.
