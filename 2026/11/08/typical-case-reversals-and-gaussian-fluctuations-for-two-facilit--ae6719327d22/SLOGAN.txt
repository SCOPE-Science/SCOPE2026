For uniformly sampled agents on a line, the classical Proportional two-facility rule has exact limiting approximation ratio \(7\pi/12-4/9\), strictly lower than Global Pair's previously known \(7/5\), even though mixing the two improves worst-case guarantees. Global Pair's conditional approximation ratio also has Gaussian sample fluctuations with exact variance \(147113/504000\); under symmetric three-site populations the performance ranking reverses sharply when each endpoint's mass crosses \(1/3\).
