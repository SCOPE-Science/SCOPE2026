At one fixed fair-refresh rate, arbitrarily many pairwise nonisomorphic rooted complete bipartite graphs can have exactly the same stationary two-time observation distributions at every temporal lag. An explicit family with root-class sizes \(1+j(n-1)\) and opposite-class sizes \(jn/(5j-4)\) has common lag-two spin correlation \(1/(3n+1)\), although higher-order observations may still distinguish its graphs.
