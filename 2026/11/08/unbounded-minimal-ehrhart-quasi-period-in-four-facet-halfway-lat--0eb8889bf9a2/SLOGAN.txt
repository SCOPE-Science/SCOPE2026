For every odd \(q\ge3\), the four-facet halfway-lattice parallelogram \(P_q=\{(x,y):|x|,|x+qy|\le\tfrac12\}\) has an exact Ehrhart quasi-polynomial with minimal period \(2q\), its full vertex denominator. Thus primitive halfway-lattice facets alone do not bound arithmetic period even in centrally symmetric quadrilaterals.
