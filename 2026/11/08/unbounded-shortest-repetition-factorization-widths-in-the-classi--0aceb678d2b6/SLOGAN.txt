The classical binary period-doubling word has repetition-factorizable factors \(W_r=p[2:2^{r+1})\) with \(\lfloor r\log_3 2\rfloor+1\le\operatorname{sw}(W_r)\le r\), so its minimum repetition-factorization widths are unbounded. Every repetition period is a power of two, and a repetition beginning at index \(a\) ends before \(3a\), which forces the logarithmic lower bound.
