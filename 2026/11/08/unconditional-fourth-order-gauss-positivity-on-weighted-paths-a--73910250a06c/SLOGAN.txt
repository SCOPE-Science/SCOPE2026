The two-stage fourth-order Gauss--Legendre graph-heat step is nonnegative for every timestep on a positively weighted path precisely for two vertices with arbitrary weights, three vertices with adjacent-weight ratio in \([1/2,2]\), or four vertices with weights proportional to \((1,2,1)\). Every excluded path has an eventually negative neighboring transition, while every allowed path has strictly positive transitions at each positive timestep.
