## Correctness — PASS
The source equations lead to the equilibrium identity \(\rho(z_i-u_i)+(z_i-(Wz)_i)^3/z_i^2=0\) on the invariant strictly positive opinion box. The scalar response has derivative \(\rho+(t-m)^2(t+2m)/t^3>0\), providing a unique root between the neighbor mean and the original opinion. Its logarithmic elasticity satisfies the exact deficit identity \(1-e=\rho t^2u/[\rho t^3+(t-m)^2(t+2m)]\), which proves the uniform contraction bound. The monotone bracketing follows from order preservation, and the reduced-flow exponential rate follows by comparing maximal and minimal ratios against the unique equilibrium. These steps cover all finite dimensions, reducible stochastic networks, boundary values, and the sign-reversed branch. The embedded exact witness and numerical checks passed. No finite experiment substitutes for the proof.

The source supplies the cubic equation but only establishes a small-disagreement sufficient contraction; its conclusion explicitly suggests using a positive-opinion domain for stronger guarantees; it does not state unconditional uniqueness, the uniform logarithmic contraction, its two-sided certificate, or global convergence of the fast-leadership reduced flow for all one-sign data. A June 2026 follow-up by the same authors describes a replicator-style leadership law; only abstract and partial model material were available, and a hidden equivalent result cannot be ruled out. Database and available-claim overlap searches identified no statement implying the whole theorem. Lack of a match is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Positive or negative consensus-leaning opinion data are natural and forward invariant. The source itself identifies positive opinion restrictions as a potentially productive direction. Its restrictive sufficient region excludes a fully admissible exact two-agent example, whereas the new theorem covers every positive parameter and directed row-stochastic network without irreducibility. Unconditional stationary uniqueness together with certified lower/upper bounds and global reduced-flow relaxation is a useful qualitative and computational classification, not a mere reformulation of the cubic equation. The distinction between finite-speed and fast-leadership dynamics prevents an unsupported stronger claim.
