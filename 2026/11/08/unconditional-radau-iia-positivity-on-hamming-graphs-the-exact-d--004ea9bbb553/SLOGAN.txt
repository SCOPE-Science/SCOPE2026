The two-stage Radau IIA heat step is nonnegative for every timestep on the unweighted Hamming graph \(H(d,q)=K_q^{\square d}\) exactly for \(d=1,\ 2\le q\le11\), \((d,q)=(2,2),(2,3)\), or \((d,q)=(3,2)\). Every other dimension–alphabet combination has an explicit negative-diagonal timestep or a sharp negative distance-five small-step obstruction.
