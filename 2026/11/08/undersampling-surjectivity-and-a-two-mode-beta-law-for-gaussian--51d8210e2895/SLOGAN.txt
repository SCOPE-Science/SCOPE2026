For a non-scalar positive definite matrix, a Gaussian sketch with fewer rows than columns almost surely permits **every real one-vector sketched Ritz value** if the starting vector is chosen after the sketch. In two dimensions with \(k\ge2\) Gaussian sketch rows and eigenvalue ratio \(K>1\), the exact probability that **some** start gives a negative value is \(I_{((K-1)/(K+1))^2}((k-1)/2,1/2)\), a sharp all-start reliability law distinct from fixed-start bounds.
