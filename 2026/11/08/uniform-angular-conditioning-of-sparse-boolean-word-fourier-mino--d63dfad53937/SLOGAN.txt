For the Fourier matrix on low-weight zero-one words inside \((\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^n\), the sharp spectral condition number is \(n^{2r}/((r!)^2[2\sin(\pi/m)]^r)\) up to a relative \(O_r(n^{-1/2})\) error that is uniform over every modulus \(m\ge3\). Consequently, sparse Fourier interpolation has an exact joint dimensional-and-angular instability scale even when the modulus grows arbitrarily fast.
