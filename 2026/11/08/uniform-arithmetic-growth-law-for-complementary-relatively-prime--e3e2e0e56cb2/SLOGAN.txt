The ratio \((2^n-D(n))/(2^n-\Phi(n))\) has a uniform exponential-growth exponent given by the least nondivisor of \(n\) minus its least-prime-factor contribution, within a factor \(2(n+1)\). It tends to zero through even indices and to infinity through odd indices, resolving the two limit questions raised by Pongsriiam in 2013.
