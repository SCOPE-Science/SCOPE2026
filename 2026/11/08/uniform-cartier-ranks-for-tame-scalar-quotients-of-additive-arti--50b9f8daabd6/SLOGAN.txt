For every odd prime \(p\) and even \(h\ge2\), the tame order-\(2(p-1)\) scalar quotient of an even-support additive Artin–Schreier curve has Cartier rank \(\frac{(p+1)(2p^{h-1}+p-3)}{24}\) and a coefficient-independent Jacobian \(a\)-number. These formulas determine the \(a\)-numbers of the recently constructed infinite family of maximal curves with trivial geometric automorphism group; maximality and automorphism-freeness themselves are prior results.
