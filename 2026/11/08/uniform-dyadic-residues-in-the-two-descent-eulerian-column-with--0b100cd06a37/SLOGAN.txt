For every modulus \(2^k\), the numbers of permutations with exactly two descents give every residue exactly twice in each eventual block of length \(2^{k+1}\), once at an even and once at an odd permutation size. The least period and the sharp all-block starting index are \(2^{k+1}\) and \(\max\{3,2\lceil k/2\rceil-1\}\), respectively.
