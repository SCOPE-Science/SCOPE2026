For an unbiased \(N\)-bit input, the exhaustive Peres transform leaves between \((N^\alpha+1)/2\) and \(2N^\alpha-1\) residual bits on average, where \(\alpha=\log_2((1+\sqrt5)/2)\). This gives a uniform \(\Theta(N^\alpha)\) extraction-loss law with explicit constants, strengthening the previously known logarithmic exponent limit without claiming a limiting normalized constant or a worst-case bound.
