The torus evaluation code of the cubic rational normal scroll \(S(1,2)\) has an explicit full Hamming weight enumerator over every \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\ge4\), exhibiting eight nonzero weight contributions with a characteristic-independent formula. Its minimum and next-to-minimum weights are \(t(t-2)\) and \(t(t-2)+1\), with respective multiplicities \(t^2(t-1)/2\) and \(3t^3\), where \(t=q-1\); their codeword supports admit explicit geometric classifications.
