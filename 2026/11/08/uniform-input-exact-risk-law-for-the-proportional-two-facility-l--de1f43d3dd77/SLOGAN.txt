For independent uniform agents on a line, the Proportional two-facility rule has an exact limiting conditional mean approximation ratio \(7\pi/12-4/9\), a realized limit with second moment \(154/75\), and sharp nearly-optimal and nearly-worst tail constants. Its mean and both endpoint-tail coefficients improve on the separation-weighted Pairwise-Distance lottery, even though worst-case approximation favors a mixture of the two rules.
