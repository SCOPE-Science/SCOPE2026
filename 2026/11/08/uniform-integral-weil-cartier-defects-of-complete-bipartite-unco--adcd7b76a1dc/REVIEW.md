# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For every pair \(a,b\ge1\) with \(a+b\ge3\), the specified normal fan has exactly \(4ab\) rays, each a signed pair of standard basis vectors. Maximal cones are independently indexed by signed corners of the two factor cubes. Integral Cartier compatibility across **all** those cones gives \(h_{ij}^{s,t}=sp_i+tq_j+c\), including the one-element-part cases; integrality of local slopes forces every coefficient \(p_i,q_j,c\) to be integral. The resulting Picard lattice is exactly the primitive anticanonical class, not just rationally rank one. The primitive ray lattice has index two in the full ambient lattice, giving Smith invariants \((1^{d-1},2)\). Adjoining the constant-one Cartier class yields Smith invariants \((1^{d-1},2,2)\). Lattice volume independently gives \(2^da!b!\). Exact finite Smith and Ehrhart tests were reproduced for six parameter pairs. The universal assertions follow from written integral arguments, not those tests.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
Mori–Motomura–Ohsugi–Tsuchiya prove normality/toric-ideal statements for unconditional polytopes. Kohl–Olsen–Sanyal already give the graph-join/free-sum formula and reflexivity of unconditional stable-set polytopes for perfect graphs. He/Eikelberg provide general Picard-rank criteria, some of which already yield rank one for simple-polar special cases. The presently claimed contribution is **not** the polytope description, its reflexivity, or a bare rank-one observation: it is the complete **integral** Weil group, primitive Cartier lattice, and two order-two quotient obstructions for every admissible pair, including nonsimple-polar cases. Actual keyword, synonym, and parameter searches and close comparisons did not identify prior results implying these exact integral groups. This does not prove historical uniqueness: a treatment under Hanner/free-sum toric Fano vocabulary, or a finite database entry in small dimension, may contain equivalent cases.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact groups determine a natural birational invariant of the toric Fano varieties associated with unconditional stable-set polytopes of a standard two-parameter graph family. They show simultaneously unbounded free Weil–Cartier defect and a uniform two-dimensional \(2\)-torsion obstruction; neither can be inferred from reflexivity or Picard rank over \(\mathbb Q\) alone. This motivates retaining the full ambient lattice and gives concrete benchmark examples for classification of Gorenstein toric Fano varieties. The result does not assert broader geometric consequences absent from the proof.

## Limitations and closest literature
The boundary pair \((1,1)\) is explicitly excluded; there the Picard rank is two. Neither the singular locus nor Cox-ring relations are determined. The most relevant primary texts are Mori–Motomura–Ohsugi–Tsuchiya (arXiv:2605.19586v1), Kohl–Olsen–Sanyal (doi:10.1007/s00454-020-00199-8), and He (arXiv:2507.15406); their relevant statements are credited in the result. General classifications under alternative terminology and historical priority remain open to external checking.
