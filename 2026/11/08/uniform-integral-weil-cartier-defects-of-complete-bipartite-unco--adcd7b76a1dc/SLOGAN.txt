The Gorenstein toric Fano varieties arising from unconditional stable-set polytopes of complete bipartite graphs have Picard rank one but a Weil–Cartier quotient with two independent order-two classes. For part sizes \(a,b\ge1\) and \(a+b\ge3\), the quotient is \(\mathbb Z^{4ab-a-b-1}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2\), and the primitive anticanonical degree is \(2^{a+b}a!b!\).
