For every odd-order finite abelian group \(A\), the minimal and uniform maximal-subgroup intersection numbers of its generalized dihedral group are determined exactly by the ranks of the Sylow Frattini quotients. In particular, for \(A=C_p^r\), they are \(r+1\) and \(1+(p^r-1)/(p-1)\), showing an unbounded gap and extending the known cyclic-kernel formula.
