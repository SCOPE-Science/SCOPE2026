For every odd prime \(p\), the genus-three dihedral pencil \(y^2=x^8+cx^4+1\) has a Prym surface that is superspecial at exactly \(\lfloor(p-1)/4\rfloor\) distinct smooth geometric coefficient parameters, precisely the roots of \(P_{\lfloor(p-1)/4\rfloor}(c/2)\). The full Jacobian \(p\)-rank and Cartier \(a\)-number strata are governed by the common zeros of this Legendre polynomial and \(P_{(p-1)/2}(c/2)\).
