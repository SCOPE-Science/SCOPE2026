For random-edge greedy matching on a complete balanced bipartite graph with any \(r\) disjoint forbidden pairs, the failure probability has a uniform two-term law: \(2r/[n(n+1)]-4r(r-1)\log(n)/[n(n-1)(n+1)(n+2)]\), with a proved error of the order of the second coefficient. Thus a single forbidden pair has an exact failure probability, while two or more pairs produce a universal logarithmic correction across sparse and dense deletion regimes.
