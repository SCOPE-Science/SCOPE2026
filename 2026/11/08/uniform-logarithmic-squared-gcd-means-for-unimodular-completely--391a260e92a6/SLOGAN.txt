For every unimodular completely multiplicative function \(a\), the Hermitian GCD-square mean \(\sum_{m,n\le N}a(m)\overline{a(n)}\gcd(m,n)^2/(mn)\) has an explicit positive linear coefficient and a **uniform \(O(\log^2(2N))\) remainder**. Its coefficient is at most \(3\), attained only by the constant function, and the result includes Liouville weights but not the Möbius function.
