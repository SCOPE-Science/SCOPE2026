For the finite group algebra \(\mathbb F_q[S_3]\), the associative commutator induces an isomorphism \(\Lambda^2(A/Z(A))\cong[A,A]_{\mathrm{lin}}\), even in characteristics two and three. Every nonzero attainable commutator has exactly \(q^9-q^7\) ordered representations; unlike the previously studied commuting graph, this determines every nonzero-output fiber.
