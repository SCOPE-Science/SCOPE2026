For the explicit Klein arrangement of 21 lines, all 28 triple and 21 quadruple intersections persist over every odd-characteristic field admitting its quadratic coefficient, and the complement has point count \(q^{2m}-20q^m+99\) over \(\mathbb F_{q^m}\). In particular, its complements over \(\mathbb F_9\) and \(\mathbb F_{11}\) have no rational points.
