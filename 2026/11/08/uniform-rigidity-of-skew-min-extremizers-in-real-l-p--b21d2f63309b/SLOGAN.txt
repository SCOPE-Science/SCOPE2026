Uniform skew-min extremizers in real \(L^p\) exhibit an exact geometric phase change at \(p=2\): disjoint supports below two and balanced sign-splitting above two. This theorem characterizes *all* norming pairs and *all* near-norming sequences uniformly across measure spaces, and gives a nonnegative exact quartic norm-defect identity; the underlying sharp constant itself follows from prior matrix-norm theory.
