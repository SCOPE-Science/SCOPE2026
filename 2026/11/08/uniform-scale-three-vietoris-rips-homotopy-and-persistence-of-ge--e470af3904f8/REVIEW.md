# Scientific review: generalized-quadrangle Rips filtration

## Correctness — PASS
The incidence-distance classification follows directly from the generalized quadrangle axioms, and the finite parameters give a connected girth-eight diameter-four incidence graph. Adamaszek's exact girth-collapse theorem applies for radius two. At radius three every cross-type pair is adjacent, so the full clique complex is the join of two collinearity clique complexes. No six-cycles imply that maximal cliques within each factor are exactly line/pencil simplices meeting pairwise only at vertices. The explicit root-star collapses, one per maximal simplex, are acyclic and leave connected graphs with the precisely calculated integral cycle ranks \(s^2t^2\). The standard join identity yields the full integral wedge of \(s^4t^4\) three-spheres. The inclusion-induced maps at each threshold are sufficient to classify the complete reduced integral barcode. Reproducible finite checks independently verify all incidence distances, simplicial face counts, Morse acyclicity and field homology for orders two and three. The finite checks are not used as proof of the uniform statement.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
The 2011 graph-power theorem supplies the scale-two collapse, but not scale three. An existing 2026 classification of the Tutte–Coxeter graph is the parameter pair \((2,2)\) and has the identical \(256\)-sphere specialization; its full inspected proof expressly treats that one graph, with no arbitrary-parameter theorem. Abreu and collaborators give geometric graph background; McCabe applies generalized-polygon incidence graphs to approximation lower bounds but does not compute scale-three sphere wedges. The new general result ranges over all finite thick generalized quadrangles, with dual arbitrary orders and nonclassical structures, and its exact quartic sphere multiplicity is neither stated nor implied by the examined full result statements. A potential unindexed uniform theorem, and the closeness of the known special-case method, remain risks for priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete integral homotopy and persistence classification for an infinite, geometrically natural family is a substantive structural result. The explicit equality of the two join-factor cycle ranks for any \((s,t)\), despite unequal numbers and sizes of point and line simplices, gives a uniform \(s^4t^4\) third-homology count independent of the isomorphism type. Both new high-dimensional bars and their exact disappearance are motivated invariants of generalized-quadrangle incidence metrics, not arbitrary small parameter choices. The finite family of examples alone would be insufficient; the value rests on the proved uniform structural theorem.

## Limitations and closest literature
The result includes the previously known order-two Tutte–Coxeter case as a special case and does not claim novelty for that instance. It assumes a finite thick geometry exists and does not classify arbitrary graphs of girth eight or any equivariant homotopy type. Priority beyond the inspected indexed sources is not certified.
