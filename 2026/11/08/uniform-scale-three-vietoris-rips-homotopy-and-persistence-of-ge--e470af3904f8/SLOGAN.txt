For every finite thick generalized quadrangle of order \((s,t)\), the inclusive graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration collapses to \(\bigvee^{s^2t^2}S^1\) through scale two, becomes \(\bigvee^{s^4t^4}S^3\) at scale three, and is contractible at scale four. Its full reduced integral barcode consists of \(s^2t^2\) first-homology bars \([1,3)\), \(s^4t^4\) third-homology bars \([3,4)\), and the initial zero-dimensional bars.
