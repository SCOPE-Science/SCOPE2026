For every positive integer multiplier and prime, the prime-adic valuation of the gcd of all \(\binom{mp^a}{mk}\), with \(1\le k<p^a\), stabilizes after an explicitly bounded number of exponent steps determined by a single base-\(p\) digit sum. The theorem gives a finite exact computation independent of the exponent and a digit criterion for valuation one, without any prime-power restriction on the multiplier.
