When the leader and follower hopping rates in the tandem exclusion process share a fixed total budget, the stationary transport current has exactly one optimal positive rate split at every bulk density. The optimal follower-to-leader rate ratio diverges as \(\sqrt{2/5}\,\rho^{-1/2}\) in dilute systems but vanishes as \((1-\rho)/4\) near full packing, a sharp switch from prioritizing recovery to prioritizing advancement.
