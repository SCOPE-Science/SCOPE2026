For importance-rescaled randomized exact splitting of a two-state Markov generator, choosing each one-way subgenerator in proportion to its rate is the unique selection law that reproduces the exact mean semigroup at every positive step size. Yet its stationary numerical probability vector follows a biased Bernoulli convolution: beyond the sharp coarse-step threshold it has a singular Cantor support, and for sufficiently coarse steps every realized vector stays a positive distance from the true equilibrium even though the ensemble mean is exact.
