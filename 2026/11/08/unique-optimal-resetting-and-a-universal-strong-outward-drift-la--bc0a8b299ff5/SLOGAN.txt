In outward-drifting Brownian search with a partially reactive point target, the mean absorption time has one and only one optimal reset rate once \(uL/(2D)\ge1\). At strong outward drift with fixed \(k/u\), the optimum obeys \(r_*=u/L+(2D/L^2)k/(u+k)+O(D/(bL^2))\), while the optimized mean time remains exponentially large.
