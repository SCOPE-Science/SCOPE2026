For Brownian search with Poisson partial resetting \(X\mapsto aX\), the optimal reset rate is unique for every \(0\le a<1\) and diverges as \(r_*(a)\sim1.64065482984\,D/[(1-a)L^2]\) as \(a\uparrow1\). The optimally minimized search time nevertheless converges to \(1.99986485199\,L^2/D\), exactly the optimal Ornstein–Uhlenbeck first-passage cost.
