Gamma-distributed maturation latency in a phase-selected Poisson-reset response has one and only one optimal reset rate at every nonzero driving frequency; at fixed mean latency, more variable maturation strictly raises that optimum. Every finite gamma shape \(k>0\) produces a high-frequency optimum \(r_*\sim\omega/\sqrt{k}\) and an optimal first harmonic proportional to \(\omega^{-k}\), unlike the bounded optimal rate for deterministic maturation.
