In a complete no-payment allocation of indivisible goods to enough agents with independent uniform fixed-size complementary requests, optimal envy-free welfare becomes Poisson at the classical double-coupon-collector crowd-out window. The link is quantitative: welfare differs from the count of request types occurring exactly once only when such types overlap, an event of probability O(1/m) for fixed request size. The deterministic matching/packing equivalence is credited to the zero-price specialization of earlier single-minded envy theory rather than claimed as novel.
