In a two-person heterogeneous public-goods game under logistic aspiration revision, there is exactly one common aspiration at which the four-state action chain becomes reversible; crossing it reverses a nonzero stationary circulation and annihilates entropy production. For equal sensitivities, the exact reversible level is \(A_*=a+b\) at multiplier \(r=4\), and a strict current reversal also occurs at a positive aspiration in the genuine social-dilemma regime \(r=3/2\).
