For the six-vertex ladder with equal rail conductances \(a>0\) and arbitrary positive rung conductances, the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre completed heat update is nonnegative for every timestep if and only if the rung triple is exactly \((a/2,7a/4,a/2)\). At this unique tuning every matrix entry is strictly positive for \(h>0\); every other triple develops a negative entry at sufficiently large steps.
