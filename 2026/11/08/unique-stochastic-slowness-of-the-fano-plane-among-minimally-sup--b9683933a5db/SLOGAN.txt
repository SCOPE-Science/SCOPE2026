Among every fair coupon mechanism selecting uniformly from seven distinct triples on seven points, the Fano plane is the unique slowest for full coverage in first-order stochastic order: its probability of remaining uncovered is strictly larger at every integer time of at least three draws. Its mean is \(163/30\) draws, and the next-best nonisomorphic family has mean \(16/3\), an exact gap of \(1/10\).
