Every isocanted cube has a unique cant ratio \(a/\ell=1/(2+2^{d/2})\) at which its uniformly sampled distinct vertices form a centered tight frame, although they occupy \(d\) different Euclidean radii. A complete two-eigenvalue covariance formula shows that axial and transverse variances swap order only at this ratio, where the covariance condition number uniquely equals \(1\).
