Every generalized paperfolding word, including those defined by arbitrary nonperiodic folding instructions, has the same limiting adjacent-run-length matrix \(\frac1{16}\begin{pmatrix}0&3&1\\3&2&3\\1&3&0\end{pmatrix}\). Its successive run lengths have exactly \(\frac34\log_2(3/2)-\frac18\) bits of mutual information, independent of the instructions.
