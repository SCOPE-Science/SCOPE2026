For a harmonically confined, alternating run-and-tumble particle, a vanishing fraction of exceptionally long runs creates exact stationary boundary atoms of mass \((\mu-\nu)/(2\mu)\) each, even while the waiting-time law converges weakly to its ordinary component. A universal Wasserstein estimate makes this singular limit quantitative and yields a fixed-mean example with strictly positive fourth cumulant, unlike exponential tumbling.
