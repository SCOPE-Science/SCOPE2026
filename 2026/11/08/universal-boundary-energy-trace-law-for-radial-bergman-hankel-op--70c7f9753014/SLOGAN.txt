For every unitary-radial probability measure reaching the unit sphere, the degree-truncated squared Hankel norm of a bounded holomorphic symbol has an exact moment-shell formula; after division by \(\binom{M+d-1}{d-1}\), it converges to the same boundary energy \(\sum_{k\ge1}k\|f_k\|_{L^2(\sigma)}^2\), independently of the measure. In complex dimension at least two this gives the sharp polynomial divergence rate whenever the boundary energy is finite and positive.
