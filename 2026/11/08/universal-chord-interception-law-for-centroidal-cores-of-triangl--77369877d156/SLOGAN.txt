For random chords with endpoints uniform on a triangle's perimeter, the chance of hitting its centroidal core of scale \(r<1\) factors into a universal explicit logarithmic function of \(r\) times \(1-\sum_i(a_i/P)^2\). This gives a sharp equilateral maximizer, a universal small-core slope, and an atom at full scale contributed by chords whose endpoints lie on the same side.
