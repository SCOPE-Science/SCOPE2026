In a uniformly random circular matching with a prescribed chord present, the expected number of crossings among the other chords, conditional on exactly \(t\) chords crossing the prescribed one, is an affine function of \(t\) with universal slope \((2n-3)/12\), independent of the marked chord's arc length. The identity holds at every feasible finite-size degree and yields positive marked-degree/remaining-crossing covariance whenever both arcs contain at least two endpoints.
