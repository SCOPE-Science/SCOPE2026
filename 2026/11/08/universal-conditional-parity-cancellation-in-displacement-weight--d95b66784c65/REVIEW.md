# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The asserted law is for all integers \(n\geq1\), all \(1\leq k\leq n\), and all finite nonnegative per-displacement weights \(h\) with \(h(0)>0\). Conditional on a fixed final car order, each car's possible preferences are exactly its uninterrupted preceding run of already parked cars, producing the product \(\prod_iF(\ell_i)\). Splitting at the maximal label \(n\) is exact: its factor is \(F(k)\); left/right standardizations preserve all remaining run lengths; cross-block inversion parity is \(\sum_j(s_j-j)\), and the \(n-k\) comparisons with the maximum contribute the separate \((-1)^{n-k}\). The alternating subsets evaluate to \(G(N,a)\) via a two-factor finite product, proving exact parity balance for odd \(n\), even \(k\). Positive zero-displacement weight makes every conditioning denominator strictly positive. Five different displacement kernels, both independent brute-force enumeration routes and the exact split recurrence were checked with `VERIFY_OK 855 exact assertions`; the general proof does not depend on those finite checks. Limits: no interactions between displacement weights and no altered parking rule.

## Originality — PASS, with qualified priority
Pinsky (2024, arXiv:2404.11529, Theorem 1 and Corollary 5) already obtains the **entire uniform outcome law** and the last-car marginal; these are expressly excluded as original. Meyles et al. (2023, arXiv:2305.15554) already establish the unit-interval context and ordered-Bell count. Most importantly, Celano et al. (2025, revised 2026, arXiv:2507.07243, Theorem 4.3) give a full outcome-by-outcome generating function for bounded-displacement parking functions with geometric displacement weights and arbitrary permutation statistics; Theorem 4.4 and Corollary 4.8 yield signed global-inversion enumerations, including the unit-interval specialization. Their formula is **stronger prior information at the level of raw enumerators** for its stated weight family. It does not, in the inspected text, evaluate the sign conditioned on the last car's specific parked position, expose the \(q=-1\) Gaussian subset factor in that conditioning, or state universal half-and-half parity across arbitrary nonnegative per-displacement kernels. The new result is solely this conditional positional cancellation and its explicit all-k formula, not any general fiber or unconditioned sign enumeration. Chebikin–Postnikov (2008, arXiv:0806.0440) concerns an inversion/area statistic of the preference vector or associated tree. Comparisons with published indexed mathematical records returned ordinary permutation-sign and parking-function-polytope results but no equivalent conditional parity statement. An implicit derivation from the published broad enumerators or uncatalogued earlier sign-refinement work remains a real historical-priority risk; no independent priority certification is asserted.

## Value — PASS
Outcome permutation parity and the position of the last arriving car are canonical joint statistics of the standard parking process. Their conditional dependence is not answered by a marginal position law, a marginal parity polynomial, or an uncomputed joint generating-function sum. The exact zero on every even position when \(n\) is odd is an all-size structural cancellation, robust under **every** nonnegative displacement penalty; it includes uniform, geometric and bounded-displacement models without tuning or asymptotics. The full formula explains precisely how the remaining conditional parity depends on the model, with positive normalizers and finite recurrences. This is a motivated dependence law, not merely a renamed standard count; its value is mathematical structural interpretation and exact conditional probability, without any claim of a new general enumerator.
