For the homogeneous quadratic population map with one rate exactly twice each of \(m\) equal subordinate rates, every positive orbit has normalized subordinate proportions \(p_j^{(t)}\sim 2/((2m+1)t)\), independent of its initial composition. Above the factor-two threshold, the same proportions instead decay geometrically at rate \((2/r)^t\); below it a positive stationary composition exists.
