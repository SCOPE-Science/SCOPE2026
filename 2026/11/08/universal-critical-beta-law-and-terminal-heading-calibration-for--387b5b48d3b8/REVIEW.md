# Scientific review: critical ageing-persistent-walk joint limit

## Correctness — PASS
The exact domain is a one-dimensional discrete walk with an initially fair heading, independent flip indicators, and \(kp_k\to c>0\). For any positive cutoff in scaled time, independent rare flips converge by their Laplace functionals to the logarithmic-intensity Poisson process. Early scaled times have at most the cutoff's displacement mass. This proves joint convergence with terminal heading. Reversibility identifies the time-changed integral with a stationary trapped telegraph filter. Direct substitution into the two stationary transport equations gives an invariant probability density and zero boundary flux; the filter's contraction and the heading's ergodicity ensure uniqueness. Beta integration proves the critical moments and Binder value. For exact rational schedules the no-intermediate-flip event is a gamma product, including its constant. A deterministic program reproduced 952 finite-step and moment-consistency assertions; finite tests are not used to establish asymptotic probability statements.

## Originality — PASS, with qualifications
Pradhan, Roy, and Ray, arXiv:2605.20116, main-text equations (24)–(28), figure 4, and supplemental persistence section provide the critical correlation, second moment, numerical Binder behavior, and no-flip products, but the passages inspected do not state a full critical joint beta-limit or universality for every \(kp_k\to c\). Dhar et al., arXiv:1811.03808, equations (5)–(6), (16)–(17), already derive the beta-type **stationary trapped-telegraph density**. Guéneau, Majumdar, and Schehr, arXiv:2602.08436, Sections II–III, derive broader stationary trapped run-and-tumble formulas and stick-breaking representations. The beta density is not a new special-function identity; the distinct assertion is the scaling correspondence from a nonstationary discrete ageing walk, jointly with its terminal heading, and the resulting exact critical physical observables. A semantically broader discrete-to-continuum ageing-walk theorem could subsume this result even if it uses different terminology. Targeted comparison of relevant material, not complete exhaustive literature review, supports present originality judgment; independent assessment remains necessary.

## Value — PASS
The motivating transition theory leaves the critical full displacement shape and exact Binder level as numerical rather than distributional outputs. The new result classifies the full critical bulk, including its U-shaped/uniform/centered regimes within the marginal exponent, terminal-heading calibration, and the rate at which finite-time endpoint atoms disappear. The conclusion is stable under any small or irregular modification of reversal schedules preserving \(kp_k\to c\); this matters in physical ageing models where exact power laws are idealizations. It is a structural scaling limit, not a routine incremental moment or arbitrary finite table.

## Limitations and evidence
Only one dimension and fair initial heading are covered. No finite-\(n\) convergence rate is proved for general schedules; only the exact special-family endpoint mass has a sharp rate. The harmonic telegraph stationary density is prior knowledge. The independent-flip and rate-asymptotic assumptions are essential to the stated proof. Numerical checks supplement but do not certify the infinite limit. Full priority and scientific review by an independent specialist remain unperformed.
