For every independent-flip persistent walk with critical reversal asymptotic \(kp_k\to c>0\), the entire ballistic-scale displacement converges to a symmetric beta law jointly with its terminal heading, and the heading's limiting conditional mean equals the normalized displacement. This yields the exact critical Binder cumulant \(2/(2c+3)\) and, for \(p_k=c/(k+a)\), a sharp \(n^{-c}\) decay of finite-time endpoint atoms.
