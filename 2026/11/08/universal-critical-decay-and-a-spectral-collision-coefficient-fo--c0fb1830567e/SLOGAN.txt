At the exact spectral epidemic threshold, every nonzero infection profile in the independent-neighbor, product-hazard SIS map on a connected undirected network decays as \(t^{-1}\) along its Perron vector, with the explicit universal coefficient \(\delta[1-\delta(1+1/\rho)/2]\sum_i v_i^3\). Simultaneous-neighbor infection collisions make this sharp decay different from the additive NIMFA prediction even when their linearized thresholds coincide.
