For a symmetric positive-definite matrix with a nearly repeated two-dimensional Ritz cluster, iid sketching yields a universal nonreal-pair probability \(p(t)\), where the scaled gap is \(t=g\sqrt{s}/\rho\), and \(p(0)=1-1/\sqrt2\). Four ambient dimensions are necessary and sufficient for such nonreal values at an exactly repeated ordinary Ritz value, even under arbitrarily accurate subspace embeddings.
