At the critical threshold of the four-compartment infection-driven immune-escape flow, every initially infected trajectory obeys \(I(t)^{-1}=Kt+(L/K)\log t+H+o(1)\), with the same explicit \(K,L\) for both prevalence- and transmission-driven feedback. The leading \(t^{-1}\) extinction law is feedback-independent, while the first logarithmic correction can reverse sign as immune-escape feedback increases.
