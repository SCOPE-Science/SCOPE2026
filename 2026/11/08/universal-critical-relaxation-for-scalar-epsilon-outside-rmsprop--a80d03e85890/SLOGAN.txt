At the exact critical learning rate \(h=2\varepsilon/a\), scalar epsilon-outside RMSProp remains globally asymptotically stable although the linearized position multiplier is \(-1\). Every trajectory that does not reach the minimizer in finite time has universal asymptotics \(n|x_n|\to\varepsilon/(2a)\) and \(n^2v_n\to\varepsilon^2/4\), independently of the memory-decay parameter.
