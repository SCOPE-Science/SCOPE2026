For symmetric lattice walks driven by independently refreshed heavy-tailed jump rates, the first-passage time from distance \(n\) has a universal nondegenerate law after division by \(n^{2\alpha}\): a Brownian hitting time divided by an independent positive-stable clock, raised to power \(\alpha\). Its full survival curve and all fractional moments below the sharp threshold \(1/(2\alpha)\) are explicit, extending previously known survival tails and mean-distance asymptotics.
