For every paperfolding word, regardless of its folding instructions, Abelian squares and cubes of each dyadic period \(2^k\), \(k\ge1\), begin at natural densities \(1/4\) and \(1/16\), while no Abelian fourth power has any dyadic period. The three possible length-\(2^k\) Parikh vectors have exact frequencies \(1/4,1/2,1/4\); the period-one square/cube densities are \(1/2,1/8\).
