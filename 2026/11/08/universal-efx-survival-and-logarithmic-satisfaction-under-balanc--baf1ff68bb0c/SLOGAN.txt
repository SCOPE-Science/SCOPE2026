With a fixed balanced assignment of \(r\ge2\) goods per agent and independent exponential values, the ex-post EFX probability is \(p_{n,r}^{\,n}\) with \(p_{n,r}\sim (\log n)/(r(r-1)n)\) for every fixed \(r\). The number of agents individually satisfying every EFX comparison is exactly binomial and typically of order \(\log n\), even though global EFX becomes extremely rare.
