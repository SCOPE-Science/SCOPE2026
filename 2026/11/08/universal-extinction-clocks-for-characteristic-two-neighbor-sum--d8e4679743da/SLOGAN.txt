For neighbor-sum parity dynamics on every finite tree with exactly one even-degree vertex, the sharp extinction time is \(2h+1\), where \(h\) is the eccentricity of that vertex; impulses at depth \(\ell\) die exactly at time \(h+\ell+1\). The last two transient layers have branching-independent counts, and degree-three trees can have extinction clocks exceeding diameter by an arbitrarily large amount.
