For every connected vertex- and edge-transitive regular graph \(H\), attaching \(r\ge1\) private leaves per vertex gives an exact globally optimal symmetric mixing SLEM \(1-a/[1+r(1+\sqrt a)^2]\), where \(a=\lambda_2(L_H)/d\). Every global optimum assigns each pendant edge the same explicit transition probability \(\sqrt a(1+\sqrt a)/[1+r(1+\sqrt a)^2]\) and has no holding at any core vertex, even without imposed automorphism invariance.
