For every fixed positive numerator gap \(k\), the rational Hartogs oblique Bergman-numerator polynomial, after explicit cubic normalization, converges at coefficient rate \(O_k(n^{-2})\) to \((z^2+4z+1)(1+z+\cdots+z^{k-1})^2\). Of its \(k\) interior zeros, one approaches the universal point \(-2+\sqrt3\) at rate \(O_k(n^{-2})\), while the other \(k-1\) approach the nontrivial \(k\)-th roots of unity at rate \(O_k(n^{-1})\).
