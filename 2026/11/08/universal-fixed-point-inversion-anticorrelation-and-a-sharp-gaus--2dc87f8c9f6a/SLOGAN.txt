In one packet-reversal overhand shuffle, the number of untouched cards and the inversion count are strictly negatively correlated at every deck size, even with unequal independent cut probabilities. For fixed identical cut probability \(p\), their centered joint fluctuations are Gaussian and their sharp limiting correlation is \(-[1+(1-p)^2]/(2-p)^2\), which decreases from \(-1/2\) toward \(-1\).
