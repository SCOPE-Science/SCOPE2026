Any \(4d\) distinct positive nodes on the parabola \(z=t+it^2\) determine every complex polynomial of degree at most \(d\) from squared magnitudes alone, up to global phase. More generally, arbitrary distinct real nodes work whenever their sum is nonzero: a missing high-degree intensity coefficient enables exact interpolation, and incompatible quadratic reflections force phase uniqueness. The node-sum condition is also sharp for \(d=1\).
