# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The proof distinguishes uniform zonotope volume from independent uniform generating coefficients. Its key is the bijective linear quotient of the edge-coefficient cube into independent bridge intervals and cycle projected cubes. Equal-volume permutation chambers and Dirichlet gap moments give the exact uniform cycle covariance, including the \((k+2)/k\) reweighting. The trace follows by counting bridge and cycle edges; the isotropic-constant formula follows from the weighted matrix-tree theorem and the exact zonotope volume. The cactus edge-disjointness assumption is needed: a fully integrated diamond counterexample gives \(13/8\), not \(3/2\). The general proof does not rely on finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)
The primary text of Padrol–Pilaud–Poullot (arXiv:2111.12422v1) was checked for its graphical-zonotope construction and deformation-cone statements. Joós–Lángi (arXiv:2206.03204v1) was checked for its zonotope volume/intrinsic-volume decomposition, the closest route to a metric integral. Neither inspected result states or implies the uniform point covariance, the \((n-1+3c)/6\) trace, or the cycle product for \(L^2\). A search of published geometry records under covariance/inertia/isotropic-constant and cactus/cycle aliases identified related but noncovering results on width variance, inradius, circumradius and zonotope skeletons. Unexamined older moment literature and differently phrased volume-weighted integration formulas remain historical-priority risks; a negative keyword result is not a proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
An infinite class has a rare graph-topology-independent Euclidean volume moment, even though its full covariance is nontrivial and graph dependent. The theorem simultaneously supplies exact covariance, sharp extrema over all fixed-order cactus graphs, an affine-invariant isotropic-constant product, and a sharp boundary counterexample. These are natural intrinsic measurements of standard polytopes, not parameter substitutions or isolated computations.

## Limits and nearest literature
The result does not address arbitrary multi-cycle graphs, nonuniform edge lengths, or nonisometric affine embeddings. Exact-rational finite checks complement but do not replace the proof. Related results on random directional width are different observables and do not imply a uniform-solid second moment.
