Uniform Euclidean volume on a connected simple cactus graphical zonotope has exact covariance equal to a cycle-reweighted graph Laplacian, and its normalized inertia is \(\bigl(n-1+3c\bigr)/6\), depending only on the number of vertices and cycles. The same computation yields an exact product formula for its affine-invariant isotropic constant, with a sharp overlapping-cycle counterexample outside the cactus class.
