Every square Fourier matrix restricted to a Hamming ball in a power of any finite abelian group has an explicit inverse supported only on pairs whose support union stays inside the ball. At every fixed radius \(r\) and alphabet order \(q\), its sharp spectral condition number grows as \(\frac{(q-1)^{2r}}{q^r(r!)^2}n^{2r}\), with the unstable singular direction concentrating at the zero word.
