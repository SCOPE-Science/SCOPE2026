For all nonempty finite simple graphs, unrestricted counting-free \(\mathrm{FO}^{2}\)-equivalence is completely determined by the sequence of recursively removed isolated/universal blocks, with each block size capped at two, plus one bit for whether a core survives. The smallest-representative spectrum has rational generating function \(z+[2z^2+z^4(1+z+z^2)]/(1-z-z^2)\); the terminal core can always be replaced by a four-vertex path.
