# Scientific review: uniform-volume covariance and isotropic optimization of isocanted cubes

## Correctness (C): PASS

The final claim is for all integers \(d\ge2\) and all finite real \(t\ge0\), and uses the squared isotropic constant \(L(K)^2=(\det\operatorname{Cov}(K))^{1/d}/\operatorname{vol}(K)^{2/d}\). The proof explicitly constructs a correct bijection from the Minkowski sum to spread-bounded coordinate boxes. Sorting coordinates gives the exact density \(S^{d-2}[2(1+t)-S]\), including the \(d=2\) boundary case. Polynomial integration yields exact \(\mathbb E S\), \(\mathbb E S^2\), and \(\mathbb E U^2\). Independent symmetry decomposition establishes the two covariance eigenvalues without assuming that independent zonotope-generator samples are uniform on the image. Finally, the logarithmic derivative is the strictly signed expression \(2d(d-1)t(t-1)/[(1+dt)(1+(d+2)t)(1+2t+dt^2)]\), proving uniqueness globally, including endpoints. The independent exact-rational simplicial integration reproduces 12 complete covariance matrices (592 simplices) and three symbolic identities. Finite checks are explicitly corroborative; the proof is analytic for all dimensions.

## Originality (O): PASS, historical priority provisional

The lead paper *Isocanted alcoved polytopes* (arXiv:2009.13858, 2020-09-29) establishes the family, its zonotope structure, and combinatorics, but not uniform-volume covariance or its isotropic optimization. The most directly related paper, *The volume of an isocanted cube is a determinant* (arXiv:2406.01565, 2024-06-03), establishes the volume and its exact generator decomposition in Theorem 3.1. Its introduction, main theorem, and volume proof were read, and its full-text searches for `covariance`, `isotropic`, and `moment` did not find the new invariant. This is a statement-level difference: the known scalar volume does not determine the covariance determinant or the all-dimensional unique optimizing ratio. A later work on lower face volumes (DOI 10.17398/2605-5686.40.1.1) addresses a different valuation. published-finding corpus searches for isocanted/inertia/zonotope isotropy found related published findings on *John/Löwner ellipsoids* and *Euclidean vertex radii* of the **same** bodies, but their recorded conclusions do not imply a uniform Lebesgue-volume second-moment formula. The previously recorded Hanner-polytope isotropic range concerns a different class. The accessible prior claims did not subsume the new statement. The material of the two close published-finding corpus papers could not be retrieved beyond their published summaries, leaving a residual source-access and terminological-priority risk. No claim of proven historical uniqueness is made.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS

The problem is natural because the cited 2024 paper gives exact volumes for this canonical infinite family of centrally symmetric zonotopes but leaves an affine-invariant, volume-normalized measure of its interior geometry undetermined. The result gives the complete covariance spectrum, not only a numerical value, and solves the entire continuous optimization in every dimension. The fact that the unique optimizer's geometric ratio \(a/\ell=1/2\) is **independent of dimension** is a structural rigidity statement, not a small unmotivated numerical gain or an arbitrary slice. Its exact minimizing value is distinct from a known volume determinant, and the proof provides a reusable conditional-order-statistic integration technique for interior moments of alcoved bodies.

## Closest literature and limits

The prior volume identity \(2^d(1+dt)\) and the description as a cube plus diagonal segment were discovered earlier by de la Puente–Clavería and are fully credited. The new theorem is restricted to the equal-cant, isotropic coordinate family; it does not assert extremality among all convex bodies or among general zonotopes. Possible earlier equivalent inertia formulas in parallelotope-projection or transportation terminology remain historical-priority risks. The exact checker verifies representative dimensions, while the proof applies to all dimensions. Independent mathematical validation has not occurred.
