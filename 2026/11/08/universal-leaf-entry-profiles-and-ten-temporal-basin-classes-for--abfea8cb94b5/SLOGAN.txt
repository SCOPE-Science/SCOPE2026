For synchronous three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on a clique \(K_{r+1}\) with \(s\) pendant leaves at one hub, \(r\ge2,s\ge1\), there are exactly ten nonempty first-entry/period classes, with sharp entry cutoff four and only periods one and three. Every primitive three-cycle has the same five-layer attraction profile \((3,2^s-1,3^s-1,3^s-1,3^s-2^s)\), independent of the clique size; all other states reach the all-rest fixed point.
