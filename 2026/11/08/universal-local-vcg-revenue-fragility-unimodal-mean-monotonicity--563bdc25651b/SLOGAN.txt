For a two-bidder, two-item VCG matching auction, an infinitesimal increase in one value lowers revenue on exactly \(1/8\) of iid item-exchangeable continuous markets; symmetric-unimodal bidder preference gaps nevertheless guarantee that expected revenue never falls for any positive shock. The unimodality hypothesis has a substantive boundary (an atomless bimodal counterexample), while under independent uniform values the finite-shock decline probability approaches \(1/4\) even as expected revenue strictly increases by an explicit piecewise polynomial law.
