Every three-edge-colorable cubic graph has the same three normalized field thresholds \(h/J=-2,2,4\) in the three-state antiferromagnetic Potts model on its line graph, while the critical \(h/J=4\) degeneracy equals the weighted matching count \(M_G(2)\). On the cuboctahedral graph these crossings have exact degeneracies \(3054,2232,689\), exposing a direct link between magnetic coexistence and the cube's perfect matchings.
