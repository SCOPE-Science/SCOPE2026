In a contracted circle rotation with uniformly chosen detuning, the attracting period diverges at the near-isometry scale \((1-\lambda)^{-1}\), but its rescaled law converges to the nondegenerate density \(6t/[\pi^2(e^t-1)]\). The rotation number becomes asymptotically uniform and independent of that period, with an effective \(O(\tau\log^2(1/\tau))\) smooth-test error where \(\tau=-\log\lambda\).
