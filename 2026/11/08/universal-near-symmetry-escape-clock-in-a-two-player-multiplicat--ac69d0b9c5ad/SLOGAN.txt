In a symmetric two-player multiplicative-weights game, initial strategy imbalance \(\varepsilon\) takes \(\log(1/\varepsilon)/\log[1+a\sigma_a(1-\sigma_a)]+O(1)\) original updates to become macroscopic, with the same sharp leading coefficient for every nonexceptional interior diagonal starting strategy. Counterintuitively, this transverse escape rate decreases strictly with gain \(a>8\) and becomes exponentially small, \(\sim ae^{-a/4}\), at high gain.
