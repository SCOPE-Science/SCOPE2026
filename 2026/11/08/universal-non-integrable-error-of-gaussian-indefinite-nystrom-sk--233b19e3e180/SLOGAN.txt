For every nonsingular indefinite real symmetric matrix, unmodified Gaussian Nyström approximation has infinite expected spectral, Frobenius, and nuclear error at **every** nontrivial sketch size, even though the approximation is almost surely finite. At one-column undersampling the error is exactly a reciprocal spherical quadratic form, with a universal \(t^{-1}\) tail, a sharp moment cutoff at order one, and a closed beta-law distribution for two-level spectra.
