For every finite field and every square matrix dimension \(n\ge2\), the zero-composition variety \(XY=0\) has **no additive Fourier zeros**: every coefficient is an integer congruent to \((-1)^n q^{\binom n2}\) modulo \(q^{\binom n2+1}\). Consequently its convolution operator is invertible with sharp smallest singular value \(q^{\binom n2}\).
