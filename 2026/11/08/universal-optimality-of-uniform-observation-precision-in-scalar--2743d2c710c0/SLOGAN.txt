For scalar Gaussian Kalman filtering with fixed positive process noise and a fixed long-run mean **measurement precision**, no time-varying sensing schedule can beat constant precision on long-run average posterior variance, even for unstable dynamics. For every fixed periodic schedule the constant allocation is the unique optimum, with exact variance \(p_b=2q/(bq+1-s+\sqrt{(bq+1-s)^2+4bsq})\) and an explicit strict penalty for intermittent sensing.
