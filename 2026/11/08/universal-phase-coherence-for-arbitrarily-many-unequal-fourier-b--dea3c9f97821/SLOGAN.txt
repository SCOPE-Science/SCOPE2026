For any finite number of equally spaced Fourier passbands of arbitrary positive widths, universal fixed-modulus phase concentration on a centered time interval is possible exactly when the explicit width-dependent inequalities \(\max_r\Delta_r/r\le\{Td\}\le\min_r(1-\Delta_r)/r\) hold and each band has time–bandwidth product at most one. For \(m\) equal-width bands this yields the sharp threshold \(mTw\le1\), with recurring separation windows and a unique alternating bandwise phase pattern up to global phase.
