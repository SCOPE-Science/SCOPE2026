For every fixed \(k\ge1\), the number of size-\(k\) components in bond percolation on **any** high-degree regular host has a universal Poisson law at retention scale \(d_np_n\sim(\log n)/k\), with exact centering \(\log n+(k-1)\log\log n\) and mean \(e^{-c}/(k\,k!)\). No geometry, expansion, or connectivity assumption is needed.
