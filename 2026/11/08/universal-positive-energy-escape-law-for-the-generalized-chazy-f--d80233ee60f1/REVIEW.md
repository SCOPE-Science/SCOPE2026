## Correctness — PASS

The invariant is differentiated directly from the defined flow, with no appeal to a conflicting sign convention. The monotone auxiliary variable \(w=z+x^qy\) rules out approach to \(x=0\), finite-time blow-up, and perpetual negative axial velocity on \(H>0\). Inward-pointing explicit power barriers show \(x^{q+1}y\to H\); integrating the derivative of \(x^{q+2}\) gives the sharp escape coefficient. The proof handles every integer \(q\ge1\), every positive energy and both signs of \(x\). The numerical tests are auxiliary only.
