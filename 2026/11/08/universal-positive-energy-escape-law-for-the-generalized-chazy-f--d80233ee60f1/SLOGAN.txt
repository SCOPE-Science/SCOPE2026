Every positive-energy trajectory of the generalized Chazy flow is global, remains on one side of its singular coordinate plane, and escapes with the universal power law \(|x(t)|^{q+2}\sim(q+2)Ht\). Its signed velocity has the sharp law \(\operatorname{sgn}(x(0))|x(t)|^{q+1}\dot x(t)\to H\), complementing previously studied negative-energy periodic cylinders.
