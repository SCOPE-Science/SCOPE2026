# Scientific review: resistance-optimal clique windmills

**Correctness: PASS.** The graph's nonzero weighted-Laplacian spectrum was reconstructed from hub-versus-blade, blade-constant, and within-blade mean-zero subspaces. Its multiplicities sum to \(N-1\). The resistance trace then reduces to a single variable \(t=(x+ry)/x\ge1\) with the strictly controlled derivative \(\beta A-\alpha D/t^2\). The relation \(A+D=mr^2\) proves the claimed universal cutoff exactly, including the zero-internal-edge equality case. Strict convexity and uniqueness for *independent*, not pre-symmetrized edge conductances were separately established via the injectivity of the Laplacian edge-coordinate map. The exact rational verifier passed 522 assertions, including non-symmetric competitor allocations.

**Originality: PASS on the inspected source set, with residual priority risk.** Ghosh–Boyd–Saberi's 2006 proceedings and 2008 full paper develop general network-conductance optimization, convexity, uniqueness and graph examples (trees, wheels, barbells). Those ingredients are acknowledged as prior results. The inspected examples do not evaluate the two-priced clique windmill or give the price cutoff \(2N/(N-1)\). Sardar–Xu's 2024/2025 windmill resistance-index work concerns fixed unit edge resistances according to its accessible abstract, not optimizing priced conductance. The full text of that latter article was not available; this access limitation is explicitly included in the literature risk, not treated as proof of novelty. The indexed research search returned no closer equivalent claim, though indexing and literature search are incomplete.

**Value: PASS.** The complete optimizer gives a directly usable design rule for all positive two-orbit linear prices and all clique-windmill sizes. Its sharp active-set transition and independence of clique decomposition at a fixed total number of vertices is the substantive structural point; this is not a recomputation of a fixed-weight Kirchhoff index or a restatement of a generic convex program. Exact support changes affect whether intra-community links should receive any budget. The spoke-only restriction can become arbitrarily inefficient as the clique size grows and internal-edge prices shrink.

The result has not been independently audited; the current assessment is limited to self-review and reproducibility checks. Unindexed equivalences and the uninspected full text of the later windmill-index article remain publication-priority risks.
