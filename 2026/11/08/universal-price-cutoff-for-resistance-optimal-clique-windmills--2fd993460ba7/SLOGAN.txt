For a windmill of \(m\ge2\) cliques of size \(r\ge2\) sharing one hub, minimizing total effective resistance under priced conductance allocations has a unique global optimum: all peripheral clique edges disappear exactly when their per-unit price is at least \(2N/(N-1)\) times the hub-edge price, where \(N=mr+1\). Below that universal threshold, all candidate edges are used and both optimal conductances and the minimum resistance have closed radical formulas.
