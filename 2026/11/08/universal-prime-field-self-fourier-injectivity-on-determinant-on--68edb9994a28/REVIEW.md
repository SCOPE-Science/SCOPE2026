# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The entrywise pairing is tr(X^T Y); relabelling X as g=X^{-T} gives central group convolution. By the regular representation and Schur's lemma, each irreducible scalar is its trace sum divided by degree (a dual convention merely permutes irreducibles). For odd p, distinct-root trace fibres consist of elements of order dividing p-1 or p+1, so their character sums lie in K=Q(zeta_(p²-1)). For trace 2, every nonidentity unipotent has a unique fixed line and all p+1 unipotent subgroups are conjugate. Summing over them and correcting the identity count gives A_rho(2)=p[(p+1)r-d], with r the dimension of U-fixed vectors and d the degree. For trace -2 the central sign gives the same field membership. Independently of classification, if r=0 then the trace-2 sum is nonzero; if r>0 the torus acts on V^U, and a torus character yields, by Frobenius reciprocity, a copy of V in the induced Borel character representation of dimension p+1. If d=p+1 this induction is V itself and has exactly two U-fixed vectors, by the two U-orbits on the projective line (the stabilizer character on either orbit is trivial). Thus (p+1)r-d cannot vanish. The prime cyclotomic polynomial is still irreducible over K by coprime conductors; a nonzero additive-frequency sum vanishes only if the p coefficients are constant. For nontrivial irreducibles their total is zero and the trace-2 coefficient is nonzero. For the trivial one, direct counting gives p(p+Legendre(t²-4)), which differs at t=0 and t=2. For p=2 the S3 class values of f are 1,1,-1 (identity, involutions, three-cycles), giving multipliers 2,-4,2 as stated. No extension-field step is valid by that argument alone.
