# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The row permutation \(X\mapsto X^{-T}\) produces central convolution on \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\). For odd primes, every trace-fiber irreducible character sum belongs to \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{p^2-1})\), including the unipotent trace fibers. The latter have the exact integral coefficient \(p((p+1)r-d)\), which does not vanish by the classical irreducible degree bound and the two-orbit principal-series calculation. Cyclotomic irreducibility forces a hypothetical vanishing Fourier multiplier to have constant trace coefficients; orthogonality excludes that for nontrivial irreducibles and direct trace counting excludes it for the trivial one. At \(p=2\), the three central character multipliers are \(2,-4,2\). The accompanying exact modular checker confirms the complete matrices at four primes, without being mistaken for the infinite proof. Assumptions are prime fields, complex-valued signals, and a nontrivial additive character.

## Originality
**PASS under bounded source comparison.** Prasad's finite special-linear character classification supplies the degree and principal-series input, not the square additive self-Fourier invertibility. Li–Hu's full ambient Fourier-transform formula for the determinant-one indicator determines frequency-dependent sums, and hence the row sum on the restricted matrix, but does not determine the nontrivial central-convolution multipliers. Lafferty–Rockmore's nonabelian group Fourier algorithms concern a different transform. Previously published one-coordinate restricted Fourier frames on the same determinant-one locus use only \(4p-3\) columns, not the square sampling operator. Literature and index searches did not find the exact claim or a clearly stronger theorem. A possible older character-table consequence remains a priority risk, not a logical gap in the proof.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** An infinite, natural critically sampled Fourier reconstruction problem on a nonlinear determinant hypersurface is settled uniformly over every prime. The group-theoretic structural explanation gives a robust nonvanishing mechanism across all irreducible components, instead of isolated computations or a finite census. The statement supplies universal linear uniqueness but not a stability bound, explicit inverse, or extension-field analogue.

## Limitations and closest sources
Prasad (arXiv:0712.4051, Chapters 2 and 3), Li–Hu (arXiv:1105.4513, Theorem 2.4), and Lafferty–Rockmore (Experimental Mathematics 1992) were compared at the mathematical-statement level. Broader unpublished or older association-scheme results could still imply the theorem. The finite rank checker is not an independent audit.
