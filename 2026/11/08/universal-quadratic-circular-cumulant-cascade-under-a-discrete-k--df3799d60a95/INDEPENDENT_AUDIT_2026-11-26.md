## correctness

PASS

For W=e^{iθ} the Poisson-kernel moments are E[W^j]=a^j for j>=0 and E[W^{-j}]=conj(a)^j. The kick multiplies W by exp((H/W-conj(H)W)/2) and the rotation factor. In the Taylor coefficients the only negative Laurent power at quadratic order is at the first moment. Subtracting M_1^m cancels the constant, linear, mixed and conjugate-square terms; the surviving coefficient is m(1-|a|²)a^{m-2}e^{imω}/8. With A=ae^{iω}, summation of just the finitely many coefficients needed through order s^m, followed by multiplication by e^{-As}, gives the log correction c s²(1-e^{-As})/(As), where c=(1-|a|²)e^{2iω}H²/8. This gives the claimed m(-1)^m factor in the exponential cumulants. The Taylor error is O(|H|³) in arbitrary complex directions for each fixed order. At a=0 use a^0=1; only the order-two coefficient survives. Its strictly positive modulus after factoring H² precludes closure for all sufficiently small nonzero kicks at each fixed interior a, including a fixed fibre with nonzero mean field. The normalizations differ by (m-1)! as stated.
