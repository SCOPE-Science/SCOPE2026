When a positive moment Hankel operator is concentrated toward the rank-one Szegő extremizer, the first sharp failure of strong reproducing-kernel norm testing is universally quartic: \(\|H_\varepsilon\|-\sup_a\|H_\varepsilon k_a\|\sim\tfrac12\operatorname{Var}(\nu)^2\varepsilon^4\). This identifies the variance squared as the precise local spectral-versus-kernel obstruction, beyond the known qualitative compact-Hankel classification.
