For every coprime rotation-step permutation of \(n\) points, its continuous connect-the-dots map has exactly the same primitive-orbit census and Artin--Mazur zeta function \(1/(1-z-\cdots-z^{n-1})\). Its entropy is the logarithm of the corresponding multinacci root, and maximal-entropy first-return gaps to the folding branch are independent with probabilities \(\rho^{-r}\), for \(1\le r<n\).
