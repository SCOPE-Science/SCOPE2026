With identical marginal extinction rates held fixed, increasing the shared-shock fraction makes a \(k\)-of-\(n\) habitat quorum less likely to survive at short times but more likely at long times, with exactly one crossing for every \(2\le k<n\). For the smallest such quorum, three patches with two needed, the mean viable time is uniquely maximized at shared-shock rate \(e(5-\sqrt3)/4\).
