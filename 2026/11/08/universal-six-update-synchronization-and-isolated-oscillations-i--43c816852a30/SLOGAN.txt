Three-color inhibitory firefly dynamics on every complete multipartite graph with at least three parts either starts in an asynchronous primitive \(3\)- or \(4\)-cycle or synchronizes within six updates; no transient path leads to an asynchronous orbit. The exceptional cycles admit exact orbit counts from two-color and three-color surjections, and the six-update bound is sharp as soon as one part has at least two vertices.
