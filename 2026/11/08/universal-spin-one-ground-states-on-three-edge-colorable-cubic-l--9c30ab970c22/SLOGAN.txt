A uniform antiferromagnetic spin-one Blume–Capel model on the line graph of any three-edge-colorable cubic graph has an exact six-branch magnetic-field/crystal-field ground-energy envelope. Its open-regime degeneracies reduce to perfect matchings and labeled edge-colorings, while two physically distinct coexistence boundaries count even-cycle packings and all matchings; the cuboctahedron yields exactly \(69\) and \(108\) boundary states.
