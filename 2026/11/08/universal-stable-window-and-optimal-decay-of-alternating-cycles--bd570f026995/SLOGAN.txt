On every connected biregular bipartite graph, the canonical discrete Huygens two-cycle is exponentially attracting on the phase quotient for exactly \(2/(r+s)<h<\pi/(r+s)\), with all transverse return multipliers equal to one at the upper endpoint. Its uniquely optimal two-step contraction factor is \(((\Lambda-\lambda_2)/(\Lambda+\lambda_2))^2\), so the graph spectral gap determines the best achievable local relaxation rate.
