For every isolate-free graph of order \(n\ge6\) with super-domination number at least \(n-2\), the entire super-coalition partition spectrum is determined by edges, disjoint-edge pairs, perfect matchings, and triangles of the feasible-pair graph. Its super-coalition number is exactly \(4\) when that auxiliary graph has a triangle and \(3\) otherwise; this yields explicit complete-multipartite formulas and reveals examples where identical super-domination polynomials hide different coalition numbers.
