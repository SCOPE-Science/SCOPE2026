# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The first-moment parametrization is bijective, and exact expansion yields the conditions \(2ab=3s ab=0\). At odd prime powers, identical sum and product are sufficient for equality of all powers. At powers of two, a nonzero admissible product displacement is precisely \(2^{k-1}\), and the cubic moment forces even sum; the even-power-sum recurrence then annihilates that displacement for every higher degree. The local valuation counts and Chinese remainder theorem give all stated energy factors. Explicit counterexamples prove the sharp first-moment threshold. Exact finite enumeration checks the claims through stated bounds without substituting for the proof.

## Originality
**PASS, qualified historical priority.** The originating 2014 cubic Vinogradov mean-value paper studies integer equal-power-sum systems, not an exact full-residue prime-power energy formula. The closest inspected later cubic-curve calculation gives the three-versus-three sixth moment only at squarefree moduli prime to six; it does not dominate the two-versus-two fourth moment at ramified moduli. Published parabolic extension-norm formulas do not imply the cubic three-coordinate count. A focused literature comparison located no covering statement. Similar local identities could be implicit in older symmetric-function work, a genuine residual priority risk.

**PASS.** This is a complete natural local-to-global classification of a low-order Vinogradov congruence count, with a nontrivial dyadic excess and a sharp algebraic explanation of why *three* and no more moments suffice over every residue ring. It provides exact local inputs for unweighted fourth Fourier moments. The weighted restriction constant and higher-summand counts remain open here.
