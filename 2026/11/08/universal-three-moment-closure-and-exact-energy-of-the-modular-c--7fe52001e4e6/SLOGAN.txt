Equality of the first three power sums of two residue pairs modulo any positive integer forces equality of every higher power sum, and three is necessary precisely when the modulus is divisible by four. The corresponding exact cubic-moment energy has local factors \(p^{2k-1}(p+k(p-1))\) for odd \(p\), \(8\) at modulus two, and \(k2^{2k}\) at modulus \(2^k\) for \(k\ge2\).
