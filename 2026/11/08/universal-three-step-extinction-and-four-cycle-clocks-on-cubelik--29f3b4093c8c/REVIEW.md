# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The argument first writes edge adjacency as the incidence Gram matrix over characteristic two. The cubelike vertex Laplacian is a square-zero group-algebra multiplication whose nonzero linear term follows from the generator-xor condition. The augmentation-ideal restriction and socle calculation give ranks \(v-2\) and \(v/2-2\), which uniquely force the three Jordan multiplicities. Hence extinction and primitive-period counts follow by counting exact kernel spaces. Independent matrix and orbit enumerators confirm sample cases, including the excluded folded-cube boundary. The finite tests are not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualification
The closest methodological primary article is Martin–Odlyzko–Wolfram (1984), especially its Section 4D on lattice automata; it does not classify edge-site parity on cubelike line graphs. The 2018 cubelike-graph primary paper concerns graph cores rather than the stated dynamical invariant. Closest semantic-database records study distance-two cube parity, complete multipartite parity, or the cuboctahedron's cut-complex homology; those statements do not imply the Jordan census here. Exact ranks could nevertheless have been obtained earlier in modular spectral graph theory under another description. No absolute first-discovery claim is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem proves a generating-set-independent all-dimension temporal classification for a natural family of line-graph edge networks, with sharp three-update information extinction versus four-update reversible recurrence when self-inclusion changes the rule. It gives exact populations, not only bounds, and the zero-xor example shows a meaningful boundary.

## Limitations
The conditions \(r\ge2\), connectedness, characteristic two and nonzero generator xor are essential to the quantified Jordan statement; zero-xor generating sets are not fully classified. Originality is qualified against inaccessible or unindexed prior modular graph-algebra literature. Only a self-review was completed.
