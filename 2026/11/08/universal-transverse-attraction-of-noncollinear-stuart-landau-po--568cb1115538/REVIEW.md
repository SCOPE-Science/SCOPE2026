# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
At any noncollinear closed polygon, the exact rotating-frame Jacobian is a negative semidefinite radial damping operator plus a skew-adjoint, rank-two mean-field operator. Its contraction identity eliminates positive-real spectrum and rules out Jordan blocks on the imaginary axis. A nonzero imaginary eigenvector would have to be both identical at every oscillator and tangent to two distinct polygon directions, so is zero. The remaining kernel is exactly the \((M-2)\)-dimensional polygon tangent space; all normal eigenvalues lie strictly to the left of the imaginary axis. The regular equilibrium manifold and local normally-attracting foliation give exponential convergence. At small positive dissipative coupling, nearby closed polygons persist and the normal spectral gap remains negative by continuity. The independent executable checks reproduce all matrix identities on explicit heterogeneous examples. Risk: no quantitative uniform gap or global basin statement is supplied.

## Originality — PASS within checked evidence
Zhao–Uchida (arXiv:2610.08239v1), Sections 2 and 5, derive the same polygon manifold but sample transverse spectra at \(C_r=C_i/4\); they do not supply the pure-reactive all-polygons stability theorem or its small-positive-dissipation continuation. Nicks–Allen–Coombes (DOI:10.1063/5.0179430) derive stability boundaries for identical-amplitude globally coupled splay waves, a special subfamily that does not imply the heterogeneous assertion. León–Pazó (arXiv:2006.02159) study identical oscillator populations, but their complete paper was inaccessible and cannot be ruled out as containing a relevant extension. Relevant published mathematical-research records were searched using the exact model, coupling, aliases and stability formulations; no implication-level covering result was located. A prior result under a more general hypothesis might still exist, so historical priority is provisional.

## Value — PASS
The recent polygon construction creates a natural unresolved normal-stability classification. Rather than adding one sample or an arbitrary parameter point, the result establishes the entire noncollinear polygon manifold (every population size and every admissible heterogeneous radius profile) as locally attracting at a physically distinguished reactive-coupling boundary. It gives a structural mechanism—radial dissipation combined with skew rank-one phase coupling—and proves an open stability regime with small positive dissipative coupling. This extends phase-only homogeneous-splay criteria to finite heterogeneous amplitude manifolds.

## Limitations and external validation
Collinear polygons, global basins, nonzero Kerr coefficient, nonuniform persistence constants and other coupling graphs are excluded. Some older full texts were inaccessible; independent validation has not been performed.
