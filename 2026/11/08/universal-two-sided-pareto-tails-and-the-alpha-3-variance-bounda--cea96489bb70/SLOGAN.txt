For a conditionally reset quantum walker on the infinite lattice with Pareto inter-reset exponent \(\alpha>1\), both stationary occupation tails obey \(p_{\pm n}\sim K_\alpha n^{-\alpha}\) with a universal prefactor, while their first asymmetry is \(\pm\alpha b/n\) and remembers only the mean reset site. A stationary site distribution therefore coexists with an infinite stationary mean-squared displacement throughout \(1<\alpha\le3\), with a sharp logarithmic boundary at \(\alpha=3\).
