In any finite bipartite skew Ornstein–Uhlenbeck network with symmetric internal relaxation, changing the two group noise temperatures multiplies the stationary entropy-production rate by the exact, architecture-independent factor \((T_++T_-)^2/(4T_+T_-)\). Consequently every drift-parameter optimizer of this dissipation rate is unchanged by the group temperature ratio, even though the dissipation itself strictly increases with thermal contrast when cross-coupling is nonzero.
