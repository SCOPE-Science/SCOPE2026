At the tricritical point of the minimal vaccination–epidemic mean-field model, every initially infected state clears with a universal two-term law \(i(t)=c_0t^{-1/2}+c_1t^{-1}+O(t^{-3/2}\log t)\), where \(c_1=(2\gamma+\eta)/[2\gamma(\gamma+\eta)]>0\). Infection has exactly one peak when its initial susceptible-to-infected ratio exceeds \(\gamma/\eta\), and otherwise decreases throughout.
