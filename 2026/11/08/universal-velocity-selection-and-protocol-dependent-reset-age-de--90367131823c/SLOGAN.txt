In a Poisson-reset inertial Brownian particle, conditioning on an extreme positive displacement selects a universal nonzero Gaussian velocity law \(\mathcal N(\sqrt{\kappa r},D_v/\gamma)\), independently of whether resets assign zero velocity or a fresh equilibrium velocity. The reset age has Gaussian fluctuations of order \(\sqrt{x}\), but its unscaled conditional mean retains a protocol-dependent offset of exactly \(1/(2\gamma)\); conditioning on a threshold exceedance additionally produces an independent exponential position overshoot.
