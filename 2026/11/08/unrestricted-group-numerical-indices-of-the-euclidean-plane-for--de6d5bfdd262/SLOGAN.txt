For every finite rotation group \(C_m\) on the real Euclidean plane, the unrestricted group numerical index is \(\cos(\pi/m)\), while adjoining a reflection raises it to \((1+\cos(\pi/m))/2\); infinite orthogonal subgroups have index one. Exact norm-one extremizers and nonnegative coefficient-defect formulas show why the cyclic index is strictly smaller than its previously known rank-one counterpart, whereas the dihedral index is attained by rank-one operators.
