For any finite graph join of \(k\ge2\) nonempty factors, its **closed**-neighborhood complex has the full unstable homotopy type of a \((k-1)\)-fold suspension of the join of the factor complexes. In particular, joins of \(k\) equal cycles of length at least five give spheres \(S^{3k-2}\), with the full wreath-product automorphism group acting on top integral homology by cycle-reflection signs times the factor-permutation sign.
