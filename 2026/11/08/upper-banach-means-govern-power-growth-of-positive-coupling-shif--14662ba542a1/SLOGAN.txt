For invertible positive-diagonal shift blocks \(T_c=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}S&D_c\\0&S^*\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), the exact asymptotic norm growth is the upper Banach mean of the coupling: \(\lim_{n\to\infty}\|T_c^n\|/n=\inf_{n\ge1}\sup_m n^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}c_{m+j}\). This sharply distinguishes rare isolated large entries from arbitrarily long high-coupling intervals.
