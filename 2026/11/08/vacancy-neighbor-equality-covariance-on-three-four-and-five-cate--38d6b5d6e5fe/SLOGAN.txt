For uniformly sampled weak compositions on a labeled cycle, vacancy count and neighboring-equality count have a striking exact covariance pattern: three categories give negative dependence at odd totals and zero at even totals, four categories reverse this to positive at even totals and zero at odd totals, and five categories give strictly positive covariance at every total \(n\ge2\). Stars-and-bars counting proves all six formulas for arbitrary totals; no claim is made for larger cycles.
