On cyclic groups of order \(p q^k\) with distinct primes and \(k\ge2\), two prime-cardinality transversals form an orthogonal Fourier spectral pair exactly when their \(q\)-adic congruence depths add to at least \(k\). This gives all \(k+1\) spectrum multiplicities, their exact frequencies, and a closed count of every spectral pair.
