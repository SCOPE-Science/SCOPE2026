On a finite forward partial-function model with one action and no propositions, LoopPDL observes the exact terminating distance or the exact transient-tail and cycle lengths; among models of at most \(n\) worlds this produces \(n(n+3)/2\) semantic types. Without the loop operator, ordinary PDL collapses every nonterminating orbit to one type and has only \(n+1\) types.
