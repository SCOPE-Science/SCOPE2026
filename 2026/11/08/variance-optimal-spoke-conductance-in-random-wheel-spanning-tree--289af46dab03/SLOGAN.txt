In weighted wheel spanning trees, the hub-degree variance has one global maximizing spoke conductance for every sufficiently large rim size. The optimizer lies above \(2\) by asymptotically \(12n^2(2-\sqrt3)^n\), and the maximal variance lies below \(n/(3\sqrt3)\) by asymptotically \((2/3)n^2(2-\sqrt3)^n\).
