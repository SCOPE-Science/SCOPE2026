Replacing each vertex of a path by an arbitrary finite metric fibre of diameter at most \(2\), while distributing equal total probability across the fibres, preserves the path's exact Lipschitz variance bound \((n^2-1)/12\) for every \(n\ge3\). All maximizers collapse to signed unit-slope functions from four fibres onward; at three fibres precisely characterized balanced endpoint polarizations survive, and an explicit nonnegative deficit identity quantifies rigidity.
