# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof classifies the rank change under a one-root extension through the radical and row space of an alternating matrix, factorizes independent components, and computes the exact rooted-square polynomials. It then uses the finite Heisenberg twisted group algebra argument to convert each alternating rank to its character-degree multiplicity over odd fields. Direct independent finite-field rank enumeration and a distinct conjugacy-class count verify all tested cells. Unbounded parameters are proved symbolically; finite enumeration is not treated as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (provisional).** Rossmann's 2022 §1.6 raises the character-degree counting problem and gives the general adjacency-rank interpretation, not the present complete square-bouquet spectrum. A publicly indexed coning theorem does cover the natural triangle-bouquet graph (discarded here); one-vertex amalgamation of at least two squares is not coning and is not directly implied by the known single-cycle or tree formulas. The rooted transfer identity and complete t>=2 square-cactus coefficient formula are the assessed new information. An undiscovered general block-cut theorem remains a priority risk; full primary-text historical coverage is incomplete.

**Value — PASS.** This is a complete all-dimensions, all-finite-fields matrix rank calculation and, for odd fields, an exact degree-by-degree ordinary character census for a canonical cactus family, directly addressing a stated graphical-group enumeration problem. The rooted formula supplies a reusable component-composition method, not just one isolated table. The known t=1 special case is credited and not presented as an independent result.

Closest literature includes Mainkar's 2010 graph Lie-algebra construction; Rossmann's 2022 full article and its Question 1.10; the 2019 Rossmann–Voll matrix-kernel work; and published indexed coning, cycle, and tree character-degree findings. The 2019 full text and the full coning/cycle/tree finding texts were inaccessible, and their potential broader implications are listed as residual priority risks. The theorem applies to weighted alternating rank matrices in every characteristic but to ordinary class-two BCH group characters only in odd characteristic. Independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
