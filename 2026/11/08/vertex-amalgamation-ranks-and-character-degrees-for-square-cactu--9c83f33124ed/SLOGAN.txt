The complete alternating-rank spectrum of a bouquet of \(t\) four-cycles over \(\mathbb F_q\) is \((1-z)(1+q(q^2-1)z)^t+z(q^2+q^2(q^2-1)z)^t\). A general one-vertex amalgamation identity explains this factorization; in odd characteristic it gives every ordinary irreducible character degree and multiplicity of the associated graphical group.
