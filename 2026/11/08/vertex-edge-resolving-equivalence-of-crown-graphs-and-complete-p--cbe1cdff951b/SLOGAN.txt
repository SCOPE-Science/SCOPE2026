For crown graphs \(K_{n,n}\setminus M_n\) with \(n\ge3\), **every** vertex-resolving landmark set is an edge-resolving landmark set and conversely; both are exactly the sets meeting at least \(n-1\) matched pairs. For \(n\ge4\), the same labeled subsets also resolve the vertices of \(K_2\square K_n\), giving identical full reconfiguration state spaces across these different tasks and nonisomorphic graphs.
