The vertex-uniform covariance of every complete-bipartite nonempty-bond polytope has four explicit tensor eigenspaces. Its least-variance direction switches sharply from the constant-edge mode to a color-class contrast exactly at \(q=\lfloor(2^p-4)/p-p+6\rfloor+1\), an exponentially distant threshold \(q\sim2^p/p\) with no ties.
