For random shortest-geodesic polygons on any flat torus, the entire lattice-valued homotopy distribution is a Voronoi-cell convolution and its covariance is exactly \(m\) times the cell covariance. Orthogonal tori have shape-independent type-A/type-B Eulerian winding laws, while the regular hexagonal and square two-tori have different sharp large-polygon null-homotopy constants, in ratio \(3\sqrt3/5\).
