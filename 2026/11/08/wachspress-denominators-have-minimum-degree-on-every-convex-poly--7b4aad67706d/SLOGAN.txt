For every convex polygon, the least possible polynomial denominator degree of any nonnegative rational generalized barycentric system equals the degree of its Wachspress adjoint: \(m-4\) for centrally symmetric \(m\)-gons and \(m-3\) otherwise. This resolves the planar case of the 2026 denominator-degree conjecture, even for polygons whose sides occur in parallel pairs without central symmetry.
