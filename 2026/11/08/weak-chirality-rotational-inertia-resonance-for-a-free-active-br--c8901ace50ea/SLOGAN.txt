A freely swimming active particle with Gaussian rotational inertia has an interior diffusion-maximizing inertia at every fixed nonzero chirality, but no finite optimum at exactly zero chirality. As the turning rate \(|\Omega|\) vanishes, every optimal angular relaxation time obeys \(\Omega^2\tau_*/D_r\to1\), while maximal excess diffusivity grows as \(v^2\sqrt{\pi/(2e)}/(2|\Omega|)\).
