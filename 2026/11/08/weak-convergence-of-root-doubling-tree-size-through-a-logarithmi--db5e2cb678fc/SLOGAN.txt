The scaled size \(B_n/n\) of the root-doubling random recursive tree has a genuine weak limit, even though its known moment asymptotics alone do not identify one. Its law is the unique invariant distribution of a logarithmic-time jump process with drift \(1-z\), jump map \(z\mapsto2z\) and jump rate \(1/z\); an exact first-jump hazard identity yields the coupling proof.
