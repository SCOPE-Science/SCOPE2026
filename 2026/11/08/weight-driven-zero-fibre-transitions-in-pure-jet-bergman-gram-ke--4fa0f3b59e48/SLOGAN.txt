Power-weighted disk Bergman Gram kernels of pure derivative evaluations have an exact, all-order zero-fibre phase diagram: every zero moves toward the origin, and exactly \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) algebraic weight thresholds add new attainable fibres. The final threshold obeys the sharp universal law \(\beta_m^*/m\to2+2\sqrt2\), while at fixed order the scalar zeros contract at the Laguerre-root scale.
