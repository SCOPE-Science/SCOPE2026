# Scientific review: weight-three translation fixed spaces in scale-three hypercube Rips complexes

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns geometric (barycentric) rather than vertexwise fixed points. Each orbit of a weight-three translation is an admissible two-point subset at inclusive scale three. Two such orbits are compatible precisely when the maximum of their four cross distances is at most three; the proof derives this inequality as \(q+\max\{h,3-h\}\leq3\) and obtains an explicit four-label fibre graph over \(Q_m\). The clique condition determines every simplex, not just the edges. Each two-fibre complex along a cube edge is the boundary of the four-dimensional cross polytope. Distinct blocks intersect only in contractible tetrahedra attached according to the cube graph. Contracting the vertex tetrahedra and applying the two-pointed-sphere gluing lemma gives an integral wedge of \(E_m=m2^{m-1}\) three-spheres and \(E_m-2^m+1\) circles. The isolated \(m=0\) boundary case is a tetrahedron, not a sphere. A standalone finite checker directly reconstructs every orbit-edge compatibility, clique face, and mod-two Betti number for \(3\leq n\leq7\), with additional support checks in lower dimensions. The proof, not the finite tests, establishes all values of \(n\).

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature access risk
The closest accessible research is Galetto–Montaño–Wellner, arXiv:2606.20784 (first submitted 2026-06-18), whose visible introduction, main theorem and section plan concern characteristic-zero hyperoctahedral homology characters at scale three. Homology characters do not in general determine homotopy types of geometric fixed-point spaces. The full article body could not be inspected; this is explicitly retained as a risk rather than presumed negative evidence. The full open Cambridge article by Bendersky–Elia–Grbić (arXiv:2605.00705) concerns ordinary Rips cohomology and connectivity, and its cross-polytope construction represents an ordinary cohomology class, not a translation-fixed-space decomposition. Adamaszek–Adams (arXiv:2103.01040) classify ordinary scale-two Rips homotopy. Closest related earlier fixed-space calculations describe scale-two translation-fixed spaces and coordinate-permutation fixed spaces, none of which includes the weight-three scale-three statement. Semantic mathematical-record searches for the precise parameter and equivalent cross-polytope-bundle description returned no stronger covering claim. Absence from search is not a uniqueness proof; unindexed or unread results are an acknowledged residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The subgroup action is a canonical metric symmetry of the binary Hamming cube, and scale three is the first scale where weight-three translation orbits become individual simplices. The new fixed space is not a routine rank calculation: it has a natural mixed-dimensional homotopy structure, a first-homology circle contribution controlled by the cycle rank of a smaller cube, and a three-sphere for every edge of that cube. The uniform decomposition gives full integral homotopy information across an infinite family, contrasting with the highly connected ordinary Rips complexes studied in the source literature. The block construction also makes the geometric reason for the two dimensions transparent. There is no claim of novelty for cross-polytope boundaries, homotopy-colimit gluing lemmas, or previously known ordinary Rips computations.

## Limitations and research context
The classification is restricted to single weight-three translations at inclusive scale three and does not settle an equivariant homotopy type for the full hyperoctahedral group. Full inspection of Galetto–Montaño–Wellner was unavailable: only publicly indexed introduction/main-theorem text was accessible. That constraint was not hidden or treated as a negative whole-document comparison. Independent mathematical audit has not been carried out.
