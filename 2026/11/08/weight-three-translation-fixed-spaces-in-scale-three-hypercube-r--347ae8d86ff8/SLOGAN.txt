For every \(n\ge4\), the geometric fixed space of the inclusive scale-three Hamming-cube Rips complex under a translation flipping exactly three coordinates is a wedge of \(m2^{m-1}\) three-spheres and \(m2^{m-1}-2^m+1\) circles, where \(m=n-3\). The proof identifies the fixed complex as cross-polytope boundaries glued along a cube graph, yielding an integral homotopy classification.
