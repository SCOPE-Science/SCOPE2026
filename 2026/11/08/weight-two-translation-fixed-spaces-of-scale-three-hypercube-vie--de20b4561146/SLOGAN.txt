For the scale-three Vietoris–Rips complex of every binary cube, flipping exactly two coordinates has a geometric fixed space homotopy equivalent to the cubical two-skeleton of a smaller cube wedged with one three-sphere for every square face. The resulting integral homotopy type is an explicit wedge of two- and three-spheres, even though the ambient scale-three Rips complex has its nontrivial homology in different dimensions.
