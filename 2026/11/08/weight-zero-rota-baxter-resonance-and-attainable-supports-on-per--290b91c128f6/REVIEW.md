The coefficient of the next vertex then forces a geometric recurrence around each permutation cycle. This yields the complete necessary cycle restriction, and a one-parameter-per-cycle diagonal construction realizes every allowed support. All cross-basis equations are checked for the diagonal construction. The rank-one construction is verified directly on basis pairs. Exact finite-field enumerations and weighted examples corroborate, rather than replace, the proof.
