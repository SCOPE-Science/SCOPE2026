In a weighted triangular-book anti-coordination game, every pure Nash equilibrium is either an aligned-hub cut or a subset-sum-balanced opposite-hub cut, giving a complete exact equilibrium census and price of anarchy. For every even number \(k\) of pages, arbitrarily small asymmetric spoke perturbations collapse \(2+2\binom{k}{k/2}\) equilibria to two while restoring full efficiency.
