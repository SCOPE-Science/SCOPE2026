For the characteristic-two reduction of Steiner's Roman quartic, normalization by \(\mathbb P^2\) makes each double line a wildly ramified degree-two cover with one branch point, and the Jacobian scheme acquires a length-three embedded defect at each of those three points. Its point count is \(\#X(\mathbb F_{2^m})=2^{2m}+2^m+2\), exactly one more than the normalization for every \(m\ge1\).
