On the rank-two tangency divisor of the characteristic-two \(3\times2\times2\) hyperdeterminant, the full Jacobian scheme has a transverse fiber \(\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y]/(x,y)^2\) of length three, rather than the length-two tangent fiber in other characteristics. The generic contact point is defined over a purely inseparable extension of degree two.
