For a biased Bernoulli sequence, conditioning the first length-\(r\) monochromatic run on which symbol wins changes its stopping-time law only through an independent truncated-geometric summand. The two conditional laws have a strict stochastic ordering and exact \(W_1=|\mu_r(p)-\mu_r(1-p)|<(r-1)/2\), with the upper constant sharp in the probability limit.
