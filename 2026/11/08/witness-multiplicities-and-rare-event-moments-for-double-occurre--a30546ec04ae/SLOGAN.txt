For a shuffle-square word in which each of \(q\) distinct letters occurs exactly twice, the number of unordered two-copy decompositions is exactly \(2^{r-1}\), where \(r\) counts the primitive excursions in its first/second-occurrence Dyck path. Their exact distribution is \(q!\frac{r}{2q-r}\binom{2q-r}{q}\); despite a limiting bounded-valued law, the mean number of decompositions is \((q+1)/2\) and the second moment grows exponentially.
