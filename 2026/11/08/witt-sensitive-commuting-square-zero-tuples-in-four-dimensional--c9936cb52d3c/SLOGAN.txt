For odd finite fields, commuting square-zero tuples in four-dimensional orthogonal Lie algebras have an exact split-form count \(1+2(q+1)(q^r-1)+(q+1)^2((2q-1)^r-2q^r+1)\), while the nonsplit form has only the zero rational tuple. The reduced variety nevertheless has \(2^r\) geometric components—two of dimension \(r+1\) and the others of dimension \(r+2\)—with Frobenius pairing them in the nonsplit case.
