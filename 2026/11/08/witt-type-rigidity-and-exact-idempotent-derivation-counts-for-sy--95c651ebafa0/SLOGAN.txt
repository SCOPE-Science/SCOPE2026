For a nondegenerate symmetric two-step Leibniz algebra over an odd finite field, nonzero idempotent derivations exist exactly when the defining bilinear form is split hyperbolic. In dimension \(2m+1\), their number is \(2q^{m(m+1)/2}\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}(q^i+1)\), all have rank \(m+1\), and they constitute one automorphism-conjugacy orbit.
