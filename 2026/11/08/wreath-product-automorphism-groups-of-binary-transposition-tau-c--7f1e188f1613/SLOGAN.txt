For the quasi-linear cycle set obtained by swapping two distinct nonzero vectors of \(\mathbb F_2^r\), the full pointed automorphism group is \(C_2^{Q-1}\rtimes(C_2\wr S_{Q/2-1})\), where \(Q=2^{r-1}\); forgetting the point doubles its order. The socle has order two for \(r\ge3\), while the full automorphism group grows much faster than its additive-linear subgroup.
