For the blowup of \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) at any \(n\ge5\) distinct points on a smooth \((1,1)\)-curve, the entire Zariski-chamber support distribution has an explicit polynomial, and the exact number of chambers is \(4\cdot3^n+(n-2)2^n-(9n^2-n+2)/2\). The same closed formula works even after the anticanonical self-intersection becomes nonpositive.
