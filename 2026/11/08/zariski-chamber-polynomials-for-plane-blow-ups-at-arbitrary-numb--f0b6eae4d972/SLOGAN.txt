For the blow-up of \(n\ge7\) complex points on one smooth conic, two explicit binomial-polynomial formulas count every Zariski chamber by support size and by whether the conic is a negative component. The total remains finite for arbitrarily large \(n\), and the chambers split exactly into finite- and infinite-volume families, with totals \(8067\), \(31053\), and \(111894\) at \(n=7,8,9\).
