For the blow-up of \(\mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}\) at \(n\ge3\) points on one line and \(m\ge3\) on another, with the lines’ intersection unblown, the complete Zariski-chamber negative-support enumerator is an explicit three-variable binomial/star sum recording the two distinguished line transforms. In particular \(Z_{3,4}(1;1,1)=3648\), and all \(n+m\)-component supports are counted by a closed formula.
