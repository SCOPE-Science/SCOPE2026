# Research review: almost-collinear plane blow-ups

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim specifies the complex projective plane blown up in \(n+1\) distinct points, with \(n\ge3\) on one line and exactly one off it. The Bézout inequalities against the common line and the \(n\) connecting lines exclude every extra negative curve. The displayed intersection matrix follows directly from the plane blow-up intersection form. A negative-definite support must have pairwise-disjoint \((-1)\)-curves, and the single remaining Schur condition is \(k\le n-2\), where \(k\) counts the exceptional curves intersecting the negative strict transform of the common line. The four exhaustive support cases give the claimed polynomial. An exact-arithmetic checker examines every support for \(n=3,4,5\); those computations are corroboration rather than the general proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with historical-priority uncertainty.** Bauer–Funke–Neumann (arXiv:0912.0958, Proposition 1.1 and Section 3) give the general support correspondence and chamber numbers for blow-ups in general position, not the almost-collinear infinite family. Bauer–Schmitz (arXiv:1204.4373) improve the computation and treat a quartic surface, not this family. Bauer–Pokora–Schmitz (arXiv:1411.2431, Examples 3.4–3.5) calculate negativity and denominators for all points on one line or for two groups on two lines, not the added off-line case. The general method does not by itself imply the closed formula without the negative-curve exhaustion and combinatorial analysis. Published-record searches and comparisons with accessible previous claims found no equivalent or dominating formula. This is an informed self-review, not a proof of global priority. Residual risk: an unindexed specialized count might exist.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The invariant counts genuinely different Zariski negative supports and stable-base-locus behaviors, not an arbitrary numerical statistic. The theorem handles an infinite family of smooth rational surfaces of unbounded Picard number, beyond the del Pezzo range, and proves a complete size distribution and closed exponential count. For contrast, blow-ups of at least nine *general* points have infinitely many negative curves and chambers; here arbitrarily many almost-collinear centers yield finite explicit counts. The Bezout exhaustion and single-Schur-bound classification are structural and remain useful independently of the numerical specialization \(n=3\).

## Limits and closest comparisons
The result holds only for the specified distinct complex centers, not for infinitely-near points, more than one off-line point, characteristic-dependent variations, or all arbitrary point configurations. It does not determine chamber volumes or denominator spectra. No independent priority check has been performed, and some specialized older sources may be missing from indexed searches.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
