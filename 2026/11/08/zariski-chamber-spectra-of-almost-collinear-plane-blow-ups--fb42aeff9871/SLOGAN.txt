Blowing up \(n\geq3\) collinear points of the complex projective plane and one further point off their line yields exactly \(2\cdot3^n+2\cdot2^n-3n-2\) Zariski chambers, with an explicit polynomial counting every negative-support size. A Bézout exhaustion of the negative curves and a one-variable Schur-complement test explain the closed formula for all \(n\), including surfaces of unbounded Picard number.
