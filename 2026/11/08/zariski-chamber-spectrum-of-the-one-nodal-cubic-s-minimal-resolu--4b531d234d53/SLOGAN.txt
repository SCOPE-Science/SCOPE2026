For the blow-up of \(\mathbb P^2\) at any six distinct points of a smooth conic, the complete Zariski-chamber support polynomial is \(1+22t+156t^2+490t^3+725t^4+447t^5+57t^6\), giving exactly \(1898\) chambers. The exceptional \((-2)\)-conic occurs in exactly \(648\) of them; the complete count follows from a star/triangle classification of disjoint \((-1)\)-curve supports.
