# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses two Bezout inequalities and adjunction to rule out every possible negative irreducible curve beyond the six exceptional curves, nine cross-lines and the two strict transforms of the special triple lines. The intersection graph and exact Schur-complement conditions classify every negative-definite support. A closed binomial expression evaluates to the polynomial. Independent exhaustive exact-rational matrix tests on all potential supports agree, as documented in `verification_output.txt`.

## Originality
PASS, provisional priority. The original chamber correspondence and smooth cubic count in Bauer--Funke--Neumann, the general enumeration algorithm in Bauer--Schmitz, the ADE/root information in Lubbes, and the nef **volumes** in Derenthal--Joyce--Teitler do not state the exact support polynomial for the two-triple-line cubic resolution. This is a new explicit special-configuration count rather than a new general algorithm. The general algorithms make the answer computable in principle, which is a substantive antecedent but not a prior tabulation. Relevant searches did not reveal a matching exact formula; absence from search is not uniqueness proof. Older specialized tables remain a risk.

## Value
PASS. Zariski chambers record possible negative supports of big divisors and control the piecewise-quadratic volume function and stable-base-locus variation. The first connected nontrivial root chain in a degree-three weak del Pezzo resolution has a natural complete chamber invariant, and its support combinatorics is structurally different from the smooth and one-root cases. The full distribution, not just the total, distinguishes how interacting \((-2)\)-curves constrain realizable supports.

## Scientific limits
All claims concern the smooth blow-up of six distinct points in the stated two-line configuration, over \(\mathbb C\). No assertion is made for all ADE singularities or for chamber volumes. Exact machine checks accompany a self-contained proof, but do not constitute independent scientific validation. Historical priority needs specialist scrutiny.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
