The minimal resolution of a cubic surface arising from two disjoint collinear triples of six plane points has exactly \(1092\) Zariski chambers. Its complete negative-support polynomial is \(1+17t+100t^2+281t^3+401t^4+255t^5+37t^6\), determined by a \(3\times3\) grid of cross-lines and an intersecting pair of exceptional \((-2)\)-curves.
