# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof supplies the exact negative-curve list, the intersection graph, the finite-dimensional Schur complement, and the generating function. The crucial inequality for any other curve is \(\widetilde C^2\ge a(b-ea)\ge0\), using fiber and section intersection multiplicities; its hypotheses exclude every candidate negative multisection. The independent exact-rational principal-submatrix census matches the polynomial in all stated tested ranges. Finite enumeration is corroboration, not a uniform proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
The chamber-support correspondence [1] and fast counting algorithm [2] are background and do not state this two-parameter symbolic profile. The complex-surface cohomology paper [4] does not treat the same family. Related ruled-surface effective/nef calculations [3] have only been compared at abstract level: they might contain information implying the result after additional combinatorics. No title or failed search is treated as proof of novelty. The boundary case \(e=1\) is not claimed as novel, and the claim is restricted to \(e,n-e\ge2\).

## Mathematical value — PASS
The new closed support polynomial counts a natural invariant of an infinite algebraic-surface family, including exact maximal supports and an exchange symmetry corresponding to elementary transformations. It combines a complete classification of negative curves with a sharp two-centre incidence cutoff. Individual chamber sizes, wall adjacencies and unrelated point configurations remain outside scope.
