The minimal resolution of a cubic surface with one ordinary node has exactly \(1898\) Zariski chambers, with complete negative-support profile \((1,22,156,490,725,447,57)\). The calculation reduces its twenty-one \((-1)\)-curves and single \((-2)\)-curve to a finite graph-independence recurrence, quantifying the change from the \(2764\) chambers of a smooth cubic.
