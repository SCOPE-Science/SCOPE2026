Blowing up all seven \(\mathbb F_2\)-points of the projective plane in characteristic two produces a weak degree-two del Pezzo surface whose fourteen negative curves have the Heawood incidence graph. Its Zariski chambers are exactly the induced-matching vertex subsets of that graph, giving \(2257\) chambers and the support polynomial \(1+14t+91t^2+322t^3+651t^4+728t^5+392t^6+58t^7\).
