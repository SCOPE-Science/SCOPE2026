For rate-one node-update voter dynamics on a star, a single permanently positive center yields a logarithmic consensus time with a Gumbel limit, while fixing just one leaf instead yields a linear consensus time whose ratio to the number of other leaves converges to an exponential law of rate \(1/2\). The leaf-zealot mean is \(2n+O(\log n)\), despite the center-zealot mean being exactly \(H_{n+1}\).
