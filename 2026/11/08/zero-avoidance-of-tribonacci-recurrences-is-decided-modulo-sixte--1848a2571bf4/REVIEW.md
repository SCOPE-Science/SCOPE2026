# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The theorem quantifies over every initial triple and every modulus \(2^r\), \(r\ge1\). An exact complete orbit enumeration modulo \(16\) establishes the required 72 liftable zero-containing primitive cycles and the count of 1280 avoiding states; the full checker is provided. A proved matrix power identity then lifts any zero with a suitable second coordinate through all higher powers, while primitive-vs-imprimitive normalization gives the count. The remaining moduli 2, 4, and 8 are exactly enumerated. No infinite conclusion is inferred from a bounded numerical sample.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** Klaška's 2008 paper already determines many exact orbit periods for arbitrary initial values and supplies the matrix congruence used here; those ingredients are credited, not claimed new. Its conclusions do not classify zero-incidence of the orbits or their zero-free state counts. Bilu et al. (2022) treat \(p\)-adic zeros for a distinguished Tribonacci sequence with fixed initial data. OEIS A106285 counts cycles, not the states avoiding zero. Searches for the count and equivalent zero-hitting formulations found no covering primary claim. Unindexed equivalent results are possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A complete zero-visitation classification is a natural invariant of finite modular recurrence dynamics, and the uniform base-16 test for all primitive lifts gives meaningful stabilization beyond periodicity. The closed count and limiting density measure the prevalence of zero-avoiding initial data across infinitely many moduli.

**Limits.** Only the all-ones Tribonacci recurrence and zero modulo powers of two are considered. The key finite incidence certificate uses exhaustive computation with checkable small integer arithmetic; the argument for all higher powers is symbolic. Independent validation remains outstanding.
