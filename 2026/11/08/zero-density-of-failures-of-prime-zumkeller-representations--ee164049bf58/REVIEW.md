# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The motivating 2023 prime-block lemma explicitly constructs, for all sufficiently large prime indices, squarefree Zumkeller products of consecutive primes terminated at the first index for which the divisor-sum ratio reaches two. Minimality bounds this ratio above by \(8/3\); the odd-prime Euler product gives a uniform totient density \(\varphi(Z)/Z>3/\pi^2\). Selecting disjoint prime blocks makes the coprimality events independent by the Chinese remainder theorem. Coprime multiplication creates an entire Zumkeller progression modulo \(Z^2\), and the unconditional fixed-modulus prime number theorem supplies a complementary prime for every sufficiently large \(N\) coprime to \(Z\). The periodic exceptional envelope has density at most \((1-3/\pi^2)^K\); letting \(K\) grow after the limit superior proves zero density. All quantifiers, coprimality, parity and threshold order were checked. The numerical tests corroborate arithmetic only.

**Originality — PASS within examined literature.** Somu–Kukla–Tran, Conjecture 4.21, asks for zero density of prime–Zumkeller nonrepresentability; the inspected full text supplies the prime-block and coprime-multiplier inputs but does not combine them into this density result. A related known uniform theorem concerning **odd** targets does not imply the all-integer density conclusion, which must control even targets. Mathematical-record searches and targeted publication searches showed no equivalent all-integer density theorem. No absence-of-hit claim is treated as uniqueness proof; unindexed work remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem proves a named prime-summand component of a natural published open additive-density conjecture. It provides a structural finite-modulus bound decaying exponentially with the number of disjoint odd Zumkeller blocks, and it establishes density-one representability for even integers, which the previous odd-only results do not cover. It neither settles the square part nor asserts a finite exceptional set.
