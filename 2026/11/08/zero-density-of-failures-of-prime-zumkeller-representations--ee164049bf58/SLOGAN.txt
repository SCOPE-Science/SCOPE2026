The integers that cannot be expressed as a prime plus a Zumkeller number have natural density zero, proving the prime-summand part of a 2023 conjecture. Pairwise coprime odd Zumkeller prime blocks give exponentially shrinking periodic obstruction envelopes of density at most \((1-3/\pi^2)^K\), while primes in fixed reduced progressions cover every sufficiently large integer outside each envelope.
