For every finite abelian group, a nontrivial subset has translate VC dimension one exactly when it is a subgroup coset or the complement of one. In the cyclic group of order \(n\), the exact number of subsets with VC dimension at most one is \(2\sigma(n)-2\mathbf1_{2\mid n}\); primitive low-VC binary words are only one-one or one-zero words for \(n\ge3\).
