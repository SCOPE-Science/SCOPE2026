# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

A complement-blue vertex has at most two nonneighbors among the initially white set, proving the strict three-white cutoff. With three whites, a successful first move is equivalent to two white neighbors of a blue degree-two path/cycle vertex; the remaining two whites force in sequence. Every pair of whites extends to such an admissible triple when \(n\ge5\), which proves all larger blue sets force and gives well-forcedness. Exact event inclusion--exclusion counts witness triples for paths and cycles, including the exceptional triple-overlap at \(n=6\). Exhaustive direct forcing for orders \(5\) through \(12\) corroborates every coefficient and minimal-set claim.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result refines a known scalar optimization problem into a complete enumeration across two canonical infinite complement families. Its nontrivial six-cycle correction and witness-center structural characterization also determine all inclusion-minimal forcing sets, giving an exact well-forced family result. These are structurally motivated quantities, not arbitrary finite coefficient slices.
