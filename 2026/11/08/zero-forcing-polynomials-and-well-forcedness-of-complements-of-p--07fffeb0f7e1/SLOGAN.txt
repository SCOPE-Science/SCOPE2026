For every \(n\ge5\), the full zero-forcing polynomial of the complement of \(P_n\) or \(C_n\) has exactly four nonzero coefficients, and every inclusion-minimal zero-forcing set has minimum size \(n-3\). The exact counts of minimum sets are \((n-3)^2+1\) for path complements and \(n(n-4)+2\mathbf1_{\{n=6\}}\) for cycle complements; the scalar minimum itself is previously known.
