# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The map from pairs \((u,v)\) with \(u\ne0\), \(v\ne0\), \(v^{\mathsf T}u=0\) onto nonzero rank-one square-zero matrices has constant fibre size \(q-1\). Exact additive character orthogonality on \(u^\perp\) yields the claimed eigenline transform. Its residue modulo \(q^2\), least nonzero modulus, and extremal constructions are verified algebraically for every \(n\ge3\). For \(n=3\), the seven Jordan/Frobenius matrix types and their centralizer orders give the stated multiplicities. Every frequency was independently enumerated over fields of sizes two and three; fixed-seed exact checks cover fields of sizes four and five.

Searches for rank-one square-zero, minimal nilpotent, orbit closure, eigenline and conjugacy-formula aliases returned no decisively covering statement. This is bounded novelty rather than a claim of exhaustive historical priority.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The minimal nonzero nilpotent conjugacy orbit is canonical in every general linear Lie algebra. The exact invertibility of its additive-convolution kernel, the field-uniform nonvanishing congruence, the sharp characteristic-sensitive least singular value and the fully resolved \(3\times3\) spectral census are natural motivated spectral invariants. These quantities are not obtained by merely counting orbit points and distinguish a trace-zero nilpotent orbit from the full rank-one matrix stratum.
