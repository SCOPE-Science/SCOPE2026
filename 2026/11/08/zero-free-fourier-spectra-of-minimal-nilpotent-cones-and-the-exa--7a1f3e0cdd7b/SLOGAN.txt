The indicator of the rank-at-most-one square-zero cone in \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\), for every \(n\ge3\), has an integer-valued additive Fourier transform congruent to \(-q\) modulo \(q^2\), hence defines an invertible convolution operator. Its sharp least singular value is \(2\) at \(q=2\) and \(q+q^2+\cdots+q^{n-2}\) otherwise; in matrix size three, every Fourier eigenvalue and multiplicity is explicit.
