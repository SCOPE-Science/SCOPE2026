# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The finite-degree oriented bipartite graphs cover every occupied level under syndeticity. Each infinite-dimensional spectral block can be split into edge-indexed infinite copies, making the scalar edge maps onto, bounded and invertible. The two scalar identities prove both exact congruences, and the shared level zero gives exact norm-one attainment. Distinct occupied spectral sets prevent unitary equivalence; the Bessel realization is explicit. The standalone rational checker corroborates the edge arithmetic, but the proof establishes the infinite-dimensional statements analytically.

## Originality — PASS, best-knowledge
El Idrissi (arXiv:2610.39002v1, Theorem 6.5) determines invertible **inequality** comparison classes, and Definition 7.2 computes an **unrestricted** exact-pair distance. Problem 11.12 asks for the restricted invertible variant. Neither of those results computes its attained zero stratum for spectrally distinct families. Targeted mathematical-record queries and available historical claims reveal no equivalent statement; classical frame-distance literature was accessible at abstract level only, and full publication priority is not independently established.

## Mathematical value — PASS
An open natural quantitative comparison construction admits an unexpectedly large zero-distance family despite distinct spectral supports. The theorem supplies an explicit geometric spectral mechanism and sharp norm-one realization, clarifying what an invertible exact-pair distance can and cannot record. It does not produce the global distance formula or a classification of all zero-distance pairs.

## Scope and limitations
Infinite multiplicity of all occupied bands and syndetic spectral supports are essential assumptions of this proof. The maps are independent invertible maps, not required to be mutual inverses. Full earlier frame-distance sources remain an originality risk. All conclusions concern separable Hilbert-space Bessel families with the given diagonal frame operators. Independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
