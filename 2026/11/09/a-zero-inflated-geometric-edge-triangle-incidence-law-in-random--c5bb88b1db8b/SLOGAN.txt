For pooled uniform vertex-or-edge attachment in a growing two-dimensional random recursive simplicial complex, the fraction of edges in exactly one triangular face converges almost surely to \(3/5\). The complete empirical edge–triangle incidence law converges in total variation to a zero-inflated geometric distribution with \(q_0=(3\sqrt5-5)/10\) and positive-incidence ratio \((5-\sqrt5)/10\), sharply contrasting with the model's power-law vertex degrees.
