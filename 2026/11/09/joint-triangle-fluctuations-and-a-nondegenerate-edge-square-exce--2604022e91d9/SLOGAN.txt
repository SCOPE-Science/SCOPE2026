For the finite-memory Pólya threshold graph, normalized triangle counts have Gaussian fluctuations jointly with edge density, but the triangle density is not determined by the square of edge density at second order. Precisely, \(\sqrt n\,(t_n-e_n^2)\) converges to a centered normal law with variance \(\frac{2}{15}\rho^3(1-\rho)\left[1+2M\delta+M(M-1)\delta^2/(1+\delta)\right]\), a strictly positive motif fluctuation with an explicit reinforcement-memory factor.
