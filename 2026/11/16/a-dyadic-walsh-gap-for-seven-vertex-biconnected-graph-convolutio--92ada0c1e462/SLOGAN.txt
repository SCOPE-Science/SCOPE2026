Counting convolution by all labelled 2-vertex-connected graphs on seven vertices has no vanishing Walsh multiplier: every signed count is congruent to \(8\pmod{16}\), and this follows from only nine forced-edge incidence counts involving at most three fixed edges. Its sharp \(\ell^2\) inverse norm is \(1/8\), and the same method verifies nonvanishing for every vertex order from three through seven.
