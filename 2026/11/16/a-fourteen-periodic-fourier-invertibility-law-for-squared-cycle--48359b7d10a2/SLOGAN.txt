The independent-set indicator of the squared cycle \(C_n^2\) has a vanishing Walsh coefficient exactly when \(n\bmod14\in\{3,5,10,12,13\}\); the even-cardinality class \(n\equiv6\pmod{14}\) is nevertheless Fourier-invertible. Every least positive multiplier has exact magnitude \(1\), \(2\), or \(4\) according to the residue class, and fourteen distinct identity transfer blocks yield exponentially many explicit null frequencies in each singular class.
