For a cyclic short-time Fourier transform, one monochromatic slice followed one generating time step later by a two-frequency slice forces an exact Gaussian-binomial staircase of frequency supports. If the two frequencies differ by an element of order \(d\), the row sizes repeat \(1,2,\ldots,d\) and the entire transform has exactly \(N(d+1)/2\) nonzeros; every divisor order is attainable.
