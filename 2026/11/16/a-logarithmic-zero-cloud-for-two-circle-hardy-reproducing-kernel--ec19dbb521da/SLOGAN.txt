A two-circle Hardy kernel with inner-to-outer mass ratio \(a^{-N}\) has exactly \(N\) scalar zero fibres in every fixed disk of radius between \(a\) and \(1\) for all sufficiently large \(N\), while an explicit zero-free radius stays strictly greater than \(a\). The \(N\) zeros collapse radially onto \(|s|=a\) and become equidistributed in angle.
