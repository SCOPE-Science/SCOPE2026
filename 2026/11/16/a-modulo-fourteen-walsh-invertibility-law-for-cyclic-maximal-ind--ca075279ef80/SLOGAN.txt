Convolution by the maximal-independent-set indicator of a binary cycle is singular precisely when its length is congruent to \(1,2,4,9,\) or \(11\) modulo \(14\). The unexpected zero-free class at lengths congruent to \(8\) modulo \(14\) has every Walsh multiplier congruent to \(2\) modulo \(4\); explicit cancellations elsewhere extend through a seven-step transfer involution.
