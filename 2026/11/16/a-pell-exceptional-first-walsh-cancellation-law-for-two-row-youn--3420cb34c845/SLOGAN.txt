The counting-convolution kernel of all Young diagrams in a \(2\times n\) rectangle is invertible exactly at widths \(n\equiv0,1\pmod4\), with sharp nonzero Walsh gap \(1\) or \(2\). At singular widths the first vanishing Fourier character uses one cell only at the negative-Pell widths \(2,19,118,695,\ldots\), and otherwise requires exactly two cells; every nullity has a polynomial-complexity exact recurrence.
