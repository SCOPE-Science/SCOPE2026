As the power in a power-exponential Gaussian entire function tends to infinity, its zeros near the unit circle converge after logarithmic magnification to a vertically mixing Gaussian analytic zero process with reciprocal-Gamma Laplace covariance. The covariance has the exact first defect \(-1/(6\beta^2)\), and the limiting intensity connects the inverse-square interior tail \(1/(2\pi t^2)\) to the exponential exterior tail \(e^t/(4\pi)\).
