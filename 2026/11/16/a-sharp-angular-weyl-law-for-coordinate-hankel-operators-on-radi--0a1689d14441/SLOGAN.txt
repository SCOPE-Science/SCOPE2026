For coordinate Hankel operators on \(e^{-|z|^{2p}}\)-weighted Fock space in \(d\ge2\) complex dimensions, the sharp singular-value counting coefficient is an explicit angular beta integral; in the quartic two-dimensional case it equals \(31/640\). The associated degreewise anisotropy and exact critical logarithmic residue refine previously known compactness and Schatten-threshold classifications.
