For every cyclic order \(N\ge6\), a fixed nonzero consecutive three-tap STFT window has an exact support gap: one-site signals give \(3N\) coefficients, while the sharp next level is \(4N-2\) precisely when the tap curvature \(ac/b^2\) is an \(N\)-th root of unity, and otherwise it is \(4N-1\). Every next-level extremizer is an adjacent two-site signal with an explicitly classified root-of-unity amplitude ratio; no signal with at least three sites attains a level below \(4N\).
