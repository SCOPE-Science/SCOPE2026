The ordinary Bernstein operator in **geodesic angle** sends every positive-definite zonal kernel on every sphere of dimension at least two to a positive-definite kernel on the Hilbert sphere exactly for degrees \(n\le3\), with counterexamples on \(S^2\) at every \(n\ge4\). On the circle the universal lifting threshold is instead \(n\le2\); both frontiers follow from explicit arcsine power coefficients and exact midpoint obstructions.
