Among the twenty type-\(D_5\) projective root directions, all \(5,184\) invertible fifteen-sample quadratic frames have the same Gram determinant \(1/256\), yet precisely \(2,304\) maximize their smallest eigenvalue. Their sharp lower bound is the least zero of \(16t^4-52t^3+53t^2-17t+1\), and their doubled-edge graphs are exactly five-cycles or triangles with leaves on two different triangle vertices.
