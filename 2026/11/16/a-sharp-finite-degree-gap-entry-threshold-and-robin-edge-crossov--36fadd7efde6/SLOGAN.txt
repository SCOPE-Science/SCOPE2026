For the natural two-periodic orthogonal-polynomial recurrence, the degree-\(2k\) polynomial acquires exactly two zeros in the central spectral gap precisely when \(\sqrt{c/b}>1+1/k\). In the critical scaling \(\sqrt{c/b}=1+\gamma/k\), the nearest squared zero follows an explicit continuous transition governed by \(w\cot w=\gamma\) on the band side and \(z\coth z=\gamma\) on the gap side.
