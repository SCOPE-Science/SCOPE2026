Among radial power weights \((1-|z|^2)^\alpha\) with \(\alpha\ge0\), the harmonic Dirichlet energy admits a positive Douglas boundary-jump representation **exactly** for \(0\le\alpha\le1\). Explicit Poisson-mixture Lévy kernels reveal a sharp infinite-to-finite activity transition: at \(\alpha=1\) the boundary process is a rate-one compound Poisson process, while larger exponents admit no positive jump representation, even with asymmetric measures.
