A sharp relative-phase aperture below \(\pi\) gives a distance-independent extrapolation bound for every two-tone Fourier sum; its exact squared constant changes from \(2/(1-\rho)\) to \(2/(1+\cos\varphi)\) at \(\varphi=\arccos(-\rho)\). The first branch has an attained finite-frequency-gap optimizer, whereas the second is not attained by distinct frequencies and is approached only through a collision limit with an escaping exterior evaluation point.
