Among all real planar Fourier \(+1\)-eigenfunctions consisting of a Gaussian times a polynomial of degree at most four and vanishing at the origin, the smallest area of the nonpositive set is exactly \(2\), with a unique radial extremizer up to positive scale. The exact excess is an angular Fisher-information integral plus an explicit nonnegative clipping term, so every nonradial perturbation is strictly more costly.
