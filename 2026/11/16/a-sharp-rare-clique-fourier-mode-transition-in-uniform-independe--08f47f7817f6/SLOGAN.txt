Uniform independent-set sampling on a clique–cycle join has an exact two-by-two aggregate Fourier block coupled to the cycle modes. When the clique size grows on the scale \(L_n/n\), the top observed mode switches at the sharp value \(nh/L_n=2+6/\sqrt5\), while the squared-energy condition ratio has an explicit piecewise limit after division by \(L_n\).
