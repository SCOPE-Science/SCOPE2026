For any antipodal wave, the sharp probability that two distinct integer-frequency samples simultaneously exceed a common positive threshold or fall below its negative has a newly determined piecewise-affine law for threshold-arc radii \(1/6\le r\le1/5\). The optimal value switches at \(r=3/16\) from \((28r-4)/5\), attained by frequencies \(\{1,5\}\), to \(4r/3\), attained by \(\{1,3\}\), with a rigorous uniform cutoff excluding every untested large-frequency competitor.
