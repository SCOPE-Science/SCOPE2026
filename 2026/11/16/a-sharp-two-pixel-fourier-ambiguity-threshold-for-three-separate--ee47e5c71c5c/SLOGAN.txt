Three separated integer-grid intervals whose occupied runs and gaps are all at least two cells wide are Fourier-magnitude identifiable through span \(23\), while at span \(24\) exactly one inequivalent pair survives up to reflection and translation. A two-variable endpoint factorization produces explicit homometric three-interval masks for every prescribed pair of occupied-run and gap resolutions, with span \(7w+5g\); that general upper bound is not asserted to be sharp.
