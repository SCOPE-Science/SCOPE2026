On hyperbolic three-space, the signed distance kernel \(e^{-ar}(1+b\cos r)\), for \(a>2\), has an exact two-sided positive-definiteness cone determined by one unique nonzero spectral maximum and, for a short damping regime, the zero-frequency minimum. Every admissible boundary kernel is strictly positive definite and \(C_0\)-universal, despite a sharp spherical-transform zero.
