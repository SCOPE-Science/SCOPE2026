On the annulus \(1/4<|z|<1\), the strictly positive analytic boundary weight \(\varphi(\zeta)=|\zeta-2/3|^2\) produces a weighted Szegő kernel with \(S_\varphi(1,w)=0\) for some interior real \(w\in(-3/4,-3/8)\). This explicit rank-one Hardy-kernel counterexample answers a boundary-zero existence question raised in recent weighted Szegő research.
