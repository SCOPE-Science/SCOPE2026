On the two-sphere, multiplying the classical Poisson covariance kernel by \(1+aP_1+bP_2\) preserves positive semidefiniteness exactly when three explicit affine spectral constraints, at degrees zero, one and infinity, hold. Every admissible kernel remains strictly positive definite, while \(C\)-universality fails only upon saturation at degree zero or one—not on the high-degree limiting facet.
