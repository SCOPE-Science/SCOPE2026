For \(e^{-a\theta}(1+b\cos2\theta)\) on \(\mathbb S^3\), positive definiteness is equivalent to an exact sharp two-sided inequality in \(b\), with its negative boundary decided by at most two explicit consecutive Gegenbauer frequencies. Every frequency is uniquely active on a nonempty parameter interval, the switches are quadratic and ordered without skips, and the positive-definite boundary remains strictly positive definite but is not \(C\)-universal.
