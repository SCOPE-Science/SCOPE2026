# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The generating-character assumption gives perfect additive duality; unit-pivot factorization of rank-one reductions and the exact unit-scaling quotient give the primitive contribution. Character orthogonality yields the two primitive-vector counts without estimates; the radical-fiber sum vanishes unless the dual frequency is divisible by the uniformizer and contributes exactly \(q^4\) times the length-two-smaller Fourier transform otherwise. Smith types and valuation caps are explicit, including \(m=2\). Direct independent finite transforms agree on every coefficient for six mixed-characteristic and two equal-characteristic examples. The length-three multiplicity arithmetic and norm are verified separately. The infinite statement rests on induction, not enumeration.

**Originality — PASS under bounded comparison.** Choosuwan–Jitman–Udomkavanich compute fixed-determinant cardinalities but not their nontrivial Fourier coefficients. Cao treats a different right-invertible-matrix orbital scheme. Watanabe's general Smith eigenmatrix **covers length-two Galois rings and subsumes that part of this calculation**; it is explicitly credited. The all-nilpotency chain-ring recurrence, the first length-three sign partition, and sharp closed projection norm are not consequences of a theorem stated for length-two Galois rings. Their publication priority remains subject to specialist verification of higher-length matrix association schemes and harmonic analyses over local rings.

**Value — PASS.** Determinant-zero matrices form the basic singular determinantal hypersurface. Their characteristic-free Fourier transform across all finite chain-ring lengths is a natural harmonic invariant with applications to convolution spectra and exact inverse problems. The two-step radical recursion supplies a tractable structural reduction across nilpotency lengths. At length three the sign partition and endpoint norm \(2q^3-2q+1-2q^{-2}+2q^{-3}-2q^{-5}+2q^{-6}\) are exact, not merely existing determinant counts or rank-table restatements.

**Limitations.** The formula concerns \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices and generating characters. The endpoint norm is simplified only for length three, and older implicit equivalent formulas may exist. The finite checks do not independently certify the induction theorem.
