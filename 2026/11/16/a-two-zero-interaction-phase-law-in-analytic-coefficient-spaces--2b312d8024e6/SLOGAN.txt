Two prescribed zeros in analytic coefficient \(\ell_A^p\) spaces have a sharp interaction phase change at \(p=2\): antipodal zeros switch from superadditive to subadditive excess norm exactly at an explicit radius when \(1<p<2\), but stay superadditive for \(p\ge2\). A convergent double-zero (jet) limit has the same exponent divide and an exponential-law boundary constant, with continuous energy under collision of distinct zero pairs.
