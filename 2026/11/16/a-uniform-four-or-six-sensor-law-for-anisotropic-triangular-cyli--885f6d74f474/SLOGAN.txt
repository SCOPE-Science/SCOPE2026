On every odd triangular cylinder \(\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_N\), four fixed sites suffice for exact dynamical sampling at all positive anisotropies except the explicit resonances \(3/[2\cos(2\pi t/N)-2\cos(2\pi s/N)]\) with \(1\le t<s\le(N-1)/2\); at those and only those couplings six sites are necessary. One staggered four-site configuration works universally off the exceptional set, and one six-site configuration works everywhere, using at most \(N+1\) consecutive time samples.
