For Ewens-weighted derangements on \(n\ge4\) labels, the off-diagonal assignment-score covariance has exactly two positive harmonic eigenvalues and one unique positive cycle-fugacity \(\theta_n\) at which all nonconstant directions are isotropic. The dominant sector reverses from skew to symmetric at \(\theta_n\), and the sharp tuning law is \(1-\theta_n=1/((n-2)(n-3))+O(n^{-4})\).
