For power-exponential Gaussian analytic functions, microscopic zero-pair repulsion is strictly stronger as the weight exponent increases when distance is scaled by local zero density, with a sharp limiting coefficient \(9/8\). Measured instead in the fixed complex coordinate, the same repulsion coefficient has one and only one peak, at exponent \(33.4696165996\ldots\); both laws follow from exact Gamma ratios and strict trigamma convexity.
