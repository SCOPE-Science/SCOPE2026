Every positive quadratic-Gaussian window \((t^2+c)e^{-\gamma t^2}\), including the strongly log-concave subfamily \(\gamma c>1\), has an explicit rectangular Gabor lattice of redundancy two on which it fails the frame property. The aspect is uniquely specified for an alternating pre-Gramian obstruction by the strictly decreasing theta parameter \(uS'(u)/S(u)=\gamma c\); uniqueness is not claimed for the complete frame-set complement.
