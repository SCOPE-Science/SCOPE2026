For every \(0<p<1\), the sharp \(H^p\) norm of the mixed functional \(f\mapsto a_0(f)+\tau a_2(f)\) has an exact one-transition profile with completely classified outer extremizers. Its fixed-origin frontier proves the sharp terminal equality \(\Phi_2(p,t)=\Phi_1(p,t)\) for all \(t\ge[1+(p/(2-p))^2]^{-1/p}\), resolving the order-two Wiener question across the full exponent range.
