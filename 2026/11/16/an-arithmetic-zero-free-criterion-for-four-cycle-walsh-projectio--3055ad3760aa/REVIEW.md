# Correctness
The signed four-cycle sum is a sum over the three pairs of rows. Hadamard orthogonality identifies it with a four-cell count quadratic, giving exact Walsh multiplicities with the correct sign-lift factor. The zero equation becomes a three-square representation with an explicit inverse map to nonnegative column counts. The parity argument and Legendre theorem are both necessary and sufficient, including the small case \(n=8\); the independent checker corroborates the identities and boundary cases.

# Mathematical value
The frequency family consists of *all* minimum cycles of the natural graph \(K_{3,n}\), not an arbitrary selected subset. Exact invertibility of its convolution operator has an unexpected arithmetic boundary: \(n=8\) admits zeros, \(n=80\) does not, and the obstruction involves forbidden three-square forms. This complete infinite-parameter classification is more informative than computationally detecting zeros at finitely many dimensions.
