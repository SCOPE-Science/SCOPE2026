The Walsh transform of all four-cycle edge indicators in \(K_{3,n}\) is zero-free except at dimensions \(n=8k+1\) with \(k\ge1\), and at \(n=8k\) where \(6k\) is a sum of three integer squares. The full coefficient multiplicities are a four-cell multinomial spectrum, and the zero-free condition is exactly invertibility of the associated XOR-convolution operator.
