For degree-at-most-two polynomials on the solid \(d\)-ball, concentration in one coordinate orthant has a complete spectrum from just a \(4\times4\) pencil, a \(3\times3\) pencil repeated \(d-1\) times, and the explicit eigenvalue \(2^{-d}(1-2/\pi)^2\) repeated \(d(d-3)/2\) times, for every \(d\ge4\). These constant-size exact formulas give the sharp best and worst concentration ratios despite a polynomial-space dimension growing quadratically with \(d\).
