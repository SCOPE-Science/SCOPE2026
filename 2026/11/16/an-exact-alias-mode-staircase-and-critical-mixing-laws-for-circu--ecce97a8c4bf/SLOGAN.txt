For constant data on expanding regular polygons in Gaussian Fock space, the minimum-norm interpolant undergoes an infinite sequence of exact Fourier-alias transitions, with thresholds \(\tau_k=k^k/[e(k-1)^{k-1}]\) for \(k\ge2\). Every higher transition has an explicit two-mode \(N^{-1}\)-window, while the first transition has a distinct \((\log N)/N\) recentering and a vacuum–degree-\(N\) logistic mixture.
