When the sequence length \(p\) is prime and every coefficient is a root of unity of prime-power order \(\ell^r\) with \(\ell\ne p\), the cross short-time Fourier transform has exactly three possible support sizes: \(p\), \(p^2-p+1\), and \(p^2\). Every exceptional pair and support shape is classified, with exact multiplicities \(1\), \(p(q^{p-1}-1)\), and \(q^{2p-2}-1-p(q^{p-1}-1)\) after independent phase normalization.
