# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The Hermitian conjugation representation splits into three pairwise inequivalent irreducibles of dimensions 1, 3 and 5; the icosahedral character norms are computed explicitly. The squared norms of the three components of a rank-one projector are exactly \(1/3\), \((1-t)/2\), and \((1+3t)/6\), so Schur twirling proves the exact spectrum and all endpoints for every unit vector. The projective stabilizer argument establishes exactly thirty distinct lines; the projector identity and the unrestricted 30-measurement lower-eigenvalue bound are algebraic. The standalone checker passed on all stated finite tests. Numerical tests supplement rather than replace the proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The problem is naturally motivated by finite-symmetry designs and stable qutrit matrix tomography. The finding classifies every unit fiducial's frame operator, sharpens existence to a necessary-and-sufficient isotropy parameter, and constructs thirty distinct measurements achieving the universal global lower-frame bound for all sets of thirty pure-state projectors. This is not a parameter substitution or a finite table, and the global optimality comparison has an independent dimension-count argument.
