For the full order-three polyanalytic Fock kernel, four is the maximum number of pairwise orthogonal point evaluations, and every optimal four-node design has one of exactly two geometries. The unique minimum-aperture geometry is strictly more sensitive to one-node jitter than the competing geometry: their sharp squared sensitivities are \((10\alpha a/3)e^{-a}\) and \((2\alpha b/3)e^{-a}\), respectively, with \(a=3-\sqrt3\) and \(b=3+\sqrt3\).
