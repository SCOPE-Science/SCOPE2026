For a square with four equal clipped corners, irrational clipping gives no integer-frequency Fourier zeros at all, whereas reduced rational clipping has a completely classified pair of congruence lines with essential diagonal exceptions and an exact nonzero-mode density. The same octagon family has a sharp double-coverage onset at corner width \(a=1/2\), yielding an exact piecewise-quadratic lattice Fourier-energy identity.
