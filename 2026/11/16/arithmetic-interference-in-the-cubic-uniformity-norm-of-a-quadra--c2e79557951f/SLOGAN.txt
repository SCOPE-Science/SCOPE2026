For the real quadratic cosine chirp \(f(x)=\cos(2\pi\sum_jx_j^2/p)\) on \(\mathbb F_p^n\), the exact cubic Gowers norm has two arithmetic branches and a sign-controlled correction to its independent-phase limit \(\|f\|_{U^3}^8=1/128\). Depending on the prime residue class and dimension parity, interference between the two opposite quadratic phases is strictly constructive or strictly destructive, with a complete all-odd-prime formula certified by \(256\) small integer quadratic forms.
