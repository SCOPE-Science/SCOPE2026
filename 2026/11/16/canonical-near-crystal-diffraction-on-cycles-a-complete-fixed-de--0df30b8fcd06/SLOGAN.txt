For uniformly sampled size-\(k\) independent sets of a cycle with a fixed positive vacancy excess \(m=n-2k\), the full covariance Fourier energy concentrates near the alternating frequency and converges in \(\ell^1\) to an explicit parity-sensitive random-phase-slip diffraction profile. The profile has total intensity \(1/4\), reciprocal-square satellites, and closed forms for one and two defects, while every finite covariance has precisely the compulsory constant-mode nullspace.
