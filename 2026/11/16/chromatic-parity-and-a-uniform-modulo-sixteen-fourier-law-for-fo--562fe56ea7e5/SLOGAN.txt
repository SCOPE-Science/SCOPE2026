The parity of the linear chromatic coefficient controls every balanced binary Fourier coefficient of proper Klein-four graph colourings modulo sixteen. For all complete bipartite graphs \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\), no Fourier zeros occur beyond global-colour-translation symmetry, and every surviving coefficient is congruent to \(4(-1)^{m+n}\) modulo sixteen.
