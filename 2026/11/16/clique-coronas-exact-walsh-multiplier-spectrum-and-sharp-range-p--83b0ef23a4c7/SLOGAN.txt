For independent-set convolution on a clique with \(b\) private leaves per vertex, the complete Walsh spectrum has an arithmetic resonance: an additional core Hamming shell vanishes precisely when the core size is even and at least \(2^b\). Every surviving multiplier is a multiple of \(2^{b(h-1)}\), and the sharp range-projection norm has an exact Krawtchouk formula, tending to \(2h-1\) with fixed core size and growing leaf bundles.
