The entire directional short-range zero-pair contact of a Gaussian-plus-first-Hermite white-noise spectrogram has an exact rational–trigonometric formula. As the Hermite power fraction \(s\) tends to zero, its \(s^{-3}\)-rescaled contact converges uniformly to \(8\cos^2(3\theta)\), revealing six angular lobes that the previously known coordinate-axis formulas cannot determine.
