The first nonconstant harmonic band of the octonionic Cayley projective plane has exactly three geodesic-cap concentration levels, of multiplicities \(1,16,9\), and two sharp crossings at \(t=1/4\) and \(t=1/3\). The optimal squared restriction condition number diverges with the exact law \(\kappa(t)\sim15(1-t)^{-2}\) as the cap shrinks.
