# Scientific review: complete Fourier recovery on triangular block graphs

## Correctness — PASS

The connected block-cut tree supplies the elimination order. Character orthogonality evaluates every local edge and triangle frequency factor, and a bijection from balanced original frequencies to independent descendant aggregates establishes the complete product multiset. Local levels are \(q-1,-1\) and \((q-1)(q-2),-(q-2),2\), each nonzero at \(q\ge3\). Equality in the largest and smallest spectral bounds is achieved independently in all blocks. Direct exact cyclic Fourier checks and a separate exact Klein-four Walsh transform were run on nonisomorphic assemblies. The general theorem rests on proof, not empirical extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS with bounded literature risk

Comparisons included archival chromatic/Potts algebra and weighted colouring formulas, ordinary extremal proper colouring counts, published first-coordinate Fourier Gram spectra for cycles and friendship graphs, and accessible historical claims. Closest established results count colourings, evaluate a global-colour external field, or study only the first harmonic. No inspected full text or indexed claim establishes the all-degree nonvanishing, block-shape-independent complete multiplier multiset, and sharp counting-convolution condition numbers. Multivariate site-field expansions and unindexed older spectral tables are genuine remaining priority risks; absence from search is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This provides a natural infinite-class, all-palette Fourier-recovery classification and complete exact spectral multiplicities, with sharp inverse norm on the translation-invariant subspace. A graph with an arbitrary block tree has the same multiplier multiset whenever the numbers of bridges and triangles agree; the phenomenon is more than a known chromatic-count evaluation and is relevant to stable harmonic reconstruction.

## Scope and limits

Only connected graphs with edge or triangle blocks and abelian colour groups of order at least three are proved. The convolution is counting-normalized. Full graph classes with larger blocks, external noise models and independent mathematical validation are not covered. One potential archival source was inspected only at abstract level; it is not asserted to be noncovering on that basis.
