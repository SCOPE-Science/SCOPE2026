For independent-set incidence vectors of every cycle \(C_n\), all Walsh characters supported on at most three vertices are nonzero except the three two-vertex characters of the triangle and the twelve three-vertex characters of the hexagon whose circular gaps are \(1,2,3\). A two-state signed transfer matrix and a finite sign-propagation certificate establish the classification for every \(n\ge3\), not just the computed small cases.
