For every odd-prime dihedral group, three arbitrary nonzero complex taps have exactly four possible rank-weighted Fourier-support sizes: \(2p\), \(2p-1\), \(2p-2\), and \(2p-4\), but never \(2p-3\). The exact attainable set on any fixed three-element support depends only on whether its elements occupy one or both rotation cosets.
