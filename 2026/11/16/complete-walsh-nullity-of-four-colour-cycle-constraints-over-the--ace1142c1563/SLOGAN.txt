For proper four-colourings of a cycle with colour group \(\mathbb F_2^2\), extra Walsh zeros occur exactly at lengths \(n\equiv1\pmod4\); their number is \((4t)!(4t+1)/((t!)^4(t+1))\) for \(n=4t+1\). All other zeros are forced by global colour translation, so the convolution rank is exactly \(4^{n-1}\) minus that exceptional count.
