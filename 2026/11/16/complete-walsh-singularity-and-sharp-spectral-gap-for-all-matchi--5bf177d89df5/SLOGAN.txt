For the binary Fourier transform of the incidence indicator of **all matchings** of \(K_{3,n}\), a Walsh zero exists if and only if \(n\) is odd, and the smallest positive multiplier magnitude is exactly \(2\) for odd \(n\) and \(1\) for even \(n\). A four-column cancellation-preserving sign block and an eight-column identity block give uniform constructive proofs for all lengths, with at least \(24^{\lfloor(n-1)/4\rfloor}\) zero frequencies at every odd length.
