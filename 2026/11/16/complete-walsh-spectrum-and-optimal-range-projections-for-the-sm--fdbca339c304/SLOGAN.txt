The complete signed Walsh spectrum of the 148 Eulerian orientations of the \(3\times3\) periodic square-ice lattice has exactly 162,816 zeros among 262,144 frequencies and convolution rank 99,328. Its range has sharp unrestricted \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection constant \(445/64\), beyond what the ordinary unsigned partition function determines.
