For compact ball-power kernels \(k_\beta(x)=(1-\|x\|_2^2)_+^\beta\) in every fixed dimension \(d\ge2\), signed Fourier total variation becomes finite exactly above \(\beta=(d-1)/2\), with sharp critical residue \(4\Gamma((d+1)/2)/(\pi^{3/2}\Gamma(d/2))\) and a uniform logarithmic cutoff crossover. This forces an optimal unbiased one-frequency signed-sampling second-moment cost proportional to the inverse square of distance from the integrability boundary.
