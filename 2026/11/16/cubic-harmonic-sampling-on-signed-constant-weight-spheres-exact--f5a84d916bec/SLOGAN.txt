Pure cubic spherical harmonics sampled on signed constant-weight sphere orbits have exactly three explicit spectral sectors, with multiplicities \(d\), \(d(d-2)\), and \(\binom d3\). The surprising interior rank obstruction occurs precisely at the spherical five-design relation \(3k=d+2\): a whole \(d\)-dimensional zonal harmonic sector vanishes on every sample point, even though the orbit integrates all spherical polynomials through degree five exactly.
