Convolution by the incidence-set indicator of all independent vertex subsets of a cycle \(C_n\) is invertible on the Boolean cube exactly when \(3\nmid n\). Its smallest positive Walsh multiplier magnitude is exactly \(1\) or \(2\) according as \(3\nmid n\) or \(3\mid n\), and singular lengths have at least \(3\cdot4^{\lfloor(n-3)/6\rfloor}\) explicit zero frequencies.
