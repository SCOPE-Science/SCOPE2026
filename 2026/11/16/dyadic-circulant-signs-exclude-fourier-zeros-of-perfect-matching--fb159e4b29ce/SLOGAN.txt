For every dyadic order \(n=2^k\), all \(2^n\) signed circulant permanents are congruent to \(n!\) modulo \(2^n\), so none vanishes and each has exact two-adic valuation \(n-1\). Thus cyclic displacement-parity Fourier convolution for bipartite perfect matchings is always invertible, even though the unrestricted perfect-matching convolution is singular at every even order.
