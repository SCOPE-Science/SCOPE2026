Four-row Eulerian-orientation indicators admit an eight-column-type compression of their complete Walsh spectra, with a polynomial-time exact enumeration scheme for every even width. For \(K_{4,6}\), the full \(2^{24}\)-frequency spectrum has exactly 29 signed levels, half the multipliers vanish, and the smallest nonzero magnitude is \(4\), yielding recoverable-space condition number \(465\).
