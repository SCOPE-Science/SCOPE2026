On the twelve vertices of a regular truncated tetrahedron, quadratic spherical-harmonic sampling at nine equally weighted sites is most stable for exactly eight choices: those deleting one of the eight directed tetrahedral triangles. The sharp lower squared frame bound is the smallest root of \(161051z^3-356708z^2+194480z-12800\), even though all 128 invertible nine-site selections have exactly the same Gram determinant.
