For every finite field, the full additive Fourier transform of the \(2\times2\times2\) decomposable-tensor cone has exactly six explicit eigenvalue levels, with closed multiplicities and a sharp endpoint projection norm \(1+(q-1)^3(q+1)(q^4+q^3+2q^2+8q+4)/q^6\). Its transform has no zeros except at the field of order two, where exactly \(108\) coefficients vanish; the general incidence reduction is known, while the complete evaluated spectrum and norm are the focus here.
