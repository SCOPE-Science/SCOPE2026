For the forty Costas permutations of order five, the additive Fourier transform on \((\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z)^5\) has exactly 320 zeros, classified by sixteen affine-Galois character orbits, and the resulting convolution operator has rank 2805. Convolution by all 120 ordinary permutations has rank only 625, while four Costas zero orbits are not zeros for the unrestricted permutation family.
