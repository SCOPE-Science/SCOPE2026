The complete continuous spherical Slepian spectrum on the squarefree degree-\(k\) harmonics, restricted to directions with exactly \(s\) positive coordinates, has explicit \(k+1\) Johnson-sector eigenvalues in every dimension \(d\ge2k\). Exactly in square dimensions a nontrivial sign layer concentrates all dipoles isotropically, but no sign-count layer can concentrate every squarefree quadrupole isotropically.
