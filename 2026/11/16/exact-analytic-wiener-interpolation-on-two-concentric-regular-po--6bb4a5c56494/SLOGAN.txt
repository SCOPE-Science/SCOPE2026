For two concentric, aligned regular polygons prescribing zeros in the analytic Wiener algebra, the exact minimum coefficient norm is attained by a trinomial with only three nonzero terms; at exceptional radial parameters the complete minimizer family is the segment between two adjacent-degree trinomials. The optimal degree exhibits sharp discrete transitions and diverges logarithmically as the outer ring approaches the unit circle, even though the minimum norm converges to \(2/u\).
