The first nonconstant Laplace eigenspace on \(\mathbb{CP}^{d-1}\) has exactly three cap-concentration eigenvalues for every \(d\ge3\); the strongest mode switches uniquely at the squared-overlap threshold \(t=1/d\), while tangential harmonics are always least observable. At fixed cap volume, the sharp squared condition number tends to \(1+\max\{\log(1/v),\log^2(1/v)\}\), with a universal crossover at \(v=e^{-1}\).
