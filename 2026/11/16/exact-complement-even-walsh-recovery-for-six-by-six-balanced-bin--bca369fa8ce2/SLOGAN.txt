For the \(297200\) binary \(6\times6\) matrices with every row and column sum three, the unnormalized Walsh transform vanishes exactly at odd-cardinality frequencies; every even-frequency multiplier is congruent to \(16\pmod{32}\), and the smallest nonzero magnitude is exactly \(16\). Counting convolution therefore loses only the complement-antisymmetric signals, with sharp \(\ell^2\) pseudoinverse norm \(1/16\) and a norm-one Fourier range projection.
