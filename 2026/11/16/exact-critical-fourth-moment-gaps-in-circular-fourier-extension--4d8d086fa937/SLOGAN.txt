Although every circular Fourier-extension Bessel fourth moment diverges at the critical exponent \(q=4\), differences of any two fixed modal moments have an exact finite positive limit: \(2\pi^{-2}\sum_{j=m+1}^{n}(2j-1)^{-1}\). Their associated squared-mode gaps vanish as \(\sqrt{q-4}\), with the sharp coefficient \((\pi\sqrt3)^{-1}\sum_{j=m+1}^{n}(2j-1)^{-1}\).
