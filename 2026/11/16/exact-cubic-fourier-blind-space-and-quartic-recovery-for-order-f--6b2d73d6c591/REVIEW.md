# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The unnormalized cell-cylinder Gram formula counts agreements by subsets, yielding an integer positive-semidefinite matrix. Complete enumeration supplies 429 ASMs. Exact elimination gives ranks 425 and 429 modulo 101, and four independent integer vectors annihilate the cubic Gram matrix exactly, proving equality over the reals. A distinct monotone-triangle enumeration checks the two ranks modulo 1009. The two 16-term witnesses give genuine pairs of probability distributions with equal three-cell marginals. No finite experiment is used to extrapolate to higher orders.

## Originality — PASS, with bounded comparison

The closest full-text sources are Behrend's multiply-refined ASM enumeration (arXiv:1203.3187, especially Sections 3.5–3.6 and 4.1–4.2) and Colomo–Pronko's two-point boundary correlations (arXiv:math-ph/0503049). They treat selected statistics and partition/correlation formulas, not the simultaneous rank and kernel of every three-cell cylinder on all 429 labelled ASMs. Searches for Fourier interaction rank, marginal reconstruction, local trades and equivalent six-vertex correlation formulations returned no checked theorem implying this rank. Related results for Latin squares, Shidoku and order-four ASM convolution do not imply the order-five marginal rank. Remaining risk: unindexed or unpublished tables and possible implicit consequences of more general correlation frameworks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The first obstruction to three-cell distribution recovery for ASM orders at most five has exact codimension four, and the sufficient observation degree is exactly four. Beyond stating two distinct distributions, the complete integer kernel certificate exhibits two local 16-term trades and two additional global signed ambiguities. This is a natural inverse problem for the finite six-vertex/ASM state space and a concrete benchmark for higher-order Fourier-interaction sampling; it does not claim an all-size theory.

## Verification and boundaries

All finite claims use exact integer arithmetic and two independently defined ASM enumerators. The statement is restricted to labelled order-five ASMs and all joint marginals of up to four arbitrary cells. Priority is bounded by accessed literature and searched mathematical records, rather than proven absolutely.
