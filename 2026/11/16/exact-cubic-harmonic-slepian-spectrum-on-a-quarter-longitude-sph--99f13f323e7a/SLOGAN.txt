On the spherical longitude quarter-lune \(x\ge0,y\ge0\), pure cubic harmonics have seven distinct exact Slepian eigenvalues: \(1/4\), \(1/4\pm\sqrt{15}/(10\pi)\), and \(1/4\pm(\sqrt{115}\pm5)/(30\pi)\). The unique best and worst concentrating complex lines attain the sharp energy fractions \(1/4\pm(5+\sqrt{115})/(30\pi)\).
