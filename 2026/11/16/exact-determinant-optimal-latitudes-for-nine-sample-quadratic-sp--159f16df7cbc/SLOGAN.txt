For the critical nine-sample \(1+3+5\) spherical-harmonic grid, the squared interpolation determinant has an exact positive factorization and a unique optimal pair of latitude heights \(x=(11+\sqrt{2305})/78\), \(y=3/26-\sqrt{2305}/130\), independently of ring rotations. The squared determinant improves by about \(12.36\%\) over the standard equiangular latitude placement, within this constrained sampling family.
