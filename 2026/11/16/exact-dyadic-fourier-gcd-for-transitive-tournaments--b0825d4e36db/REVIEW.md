# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The claim uses the unnormalized character sum over all n! inversion vectors of labelled total orders for n>=1. For 2^k vertices, pairing each first-half vertex set with its complement produces a factor 2 beyond divisibilities 2^(2^(k-1)-1) from both halves; this proves divisibility 2^(2^k-1). Partitioning consecutive positions into binary-power blocks yields 2^(n-s_2(n)) for every signing. The zero frequency is n!, while summing all characters evaluates to 2^binom(n,2) because only the identity order has zero inversion vector; thus the gcd can have no odd factor and no larger 2-adic exponent. Reversal complements every edge, cancelling every odd-weight Fourier coefficient for n>=2; the pseudo-inverse bound follows without asserting a sharp smallest nonzero coefficient. Independent enumeration of inversion vectors and full integer Walsh transform for n=1..5 gave gcd 1,2,2,8,8 and the prescribed zero bands. These finite checks corroborate, not prove, the all-n argument.
