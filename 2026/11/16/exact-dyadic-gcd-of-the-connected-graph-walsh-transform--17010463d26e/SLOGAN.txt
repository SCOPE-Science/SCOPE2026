For every positive integer \(n\), the greatest common divisor of all independently signed connected-graph counts on \([n]\) is exactly \(2^{n-1-\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor}\). A Boolean-lattice Möbius argument proves both the universal divisibility and sharpness, and a search among complements of at most \(n-2^{\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor}\) disjoint edges always produces a sharp frequency.
