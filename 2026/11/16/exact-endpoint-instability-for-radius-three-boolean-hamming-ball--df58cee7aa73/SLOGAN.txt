For convolution by the radius-three Hamming-ball indicator on \(\mathbb F_2^{2m+1}\), whenever \(3(2m+1)-5\) is nonsquare, exactly the middle Walsh layer vanishes. Although this nullspace occupies a vanishing proportion of the cube, the sharp least \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm onto the convolution range is \(1+\binom{2m+1}{m+1}(2^{-m}-2^{-2m})\), growing at rate \((2/\sqrt\pi)2^{n/2}/\sqrt n\).
