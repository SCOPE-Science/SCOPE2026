For degree-at-most-two spherical harmonics sampled at the twelve icosahedral vertices, complete recovery after any erasure pattern occurs exactly when at most three vertices are erased and no erased pair is antipodal: precisely 233 of 4096 patterns succeed. The entire sharp singular-value spectrum is classified, and the worst successful squared singular value is \(1/4-1/(2\sqrt5)\), attained by exactly 80 geometric triples.
