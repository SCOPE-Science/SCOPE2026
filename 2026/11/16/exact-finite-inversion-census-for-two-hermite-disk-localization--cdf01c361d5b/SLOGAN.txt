For a weighted independent pair of Gaussian and first-Hermite STFT disk channels, all Hermite eigenvalues become strictly decreasing in their index at every positive disk area exactly when the Gaussian-to-first-Hermite weight ratio is at least \(1\). A smaller positive ratio \(\rho\) permits reversals at exactly \(\lceil(1-\rho)^2/(4\rho)\rceil\) distinct adjacent Hermite indices, versus infinitely many at zero Gaussian weight, with every reversal interval and tangency given explicitly.
