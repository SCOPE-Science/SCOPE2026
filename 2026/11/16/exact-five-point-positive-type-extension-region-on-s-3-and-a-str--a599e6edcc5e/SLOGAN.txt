For real normalized data on five of the six elements of \(S_3\), positive-type extendibility is exactly an elliptical capsule, whereas local three-point Gram positivity allows a strictly larger region at every interior three-cycle correlation. The rational datum \(\varphi(s)=\varphi(t)=11/16\), \(\varphi(st)=\varphi(ts)=0\) passes every local Gram test but has no positive-definite extension.
