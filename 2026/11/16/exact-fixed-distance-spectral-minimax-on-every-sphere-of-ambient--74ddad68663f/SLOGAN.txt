On every sphere \(S^{d-1}\) with integer ambient dimension \(d\ge5\), a random step of one fixed geodesic length has the sharp smallest possible mean-zero \(L^2\) contraction factor \(1/d\), attained only when \(\cos\theta=\pm1/d\). Exactly the degree-one and degree-two spherical harmonics saturate the bound; every higher degree contracts strictly faster.
