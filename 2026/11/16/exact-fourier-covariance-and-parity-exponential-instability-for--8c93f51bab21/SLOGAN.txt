For uniformly sampled perfect matchings of a cyclic prism, the complete centered edge-Fourier covariance spectrum splits into cyclic symmetric modes and an extra rail-antisymmetric mode that exists only at even cycle lengths. The exact positive spectral condition ratio stays bounded by a limit of \(5/3\) along odd lengths but grows as \(2F_n/n\) along even lengths, revealing exponential parity-dependent instability.
