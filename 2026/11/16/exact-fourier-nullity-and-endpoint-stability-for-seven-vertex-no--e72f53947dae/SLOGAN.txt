Among the 1,428 noncrossing spanning trees of a labelled convex heptagon, exactly 160,484 of the 2,097,152 binary edge characters cancel, with the first cancellation occurring at precisely fourteen two-chord supports in one dihedral orbit. The resulting Fourier range projection has sharp \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-norm \(39113019/262144\).
