The inclusion-minimal dominating sets of every \(K_{m,n}\), for \(m,n\ge2\), yield a Walsh convolution whose complete zero-multiplier count has an explicit binomial formula and whose smallest nonzero multiplier is exactly \(1\) or \(2\) according to shore parity. Balanced bicliques alternate between full Fourier rank at odd order and a singular even-order spectrum losing exactly the fraction \(2^{-N}\binom N{N/2}\) of its modes.
