For tournaments with no vertex beating every opponent, the Walsh support consists exactly of the zero frequency and star-supported edge sets of size at least two. The sharp minimum endpoint projection norm onto their convolution range grows as \(\bigl(1+2e^{-1/4}/\sqrt\pi+\operatorname{erf}(1/2)\bigr)n\), despite the range being a vanishing fraction of all functions on the orientation cube.
