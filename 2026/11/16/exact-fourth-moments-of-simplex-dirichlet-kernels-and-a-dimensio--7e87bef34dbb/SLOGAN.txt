For the \(d\)-dimensional lattice-simplex Dirichlet kernel, the fourth-power Fourier norm is an explicit exact binomial sum at every cutoff, with normalized leading coefficient \(R_d=((2d)!)^2/[d!(3d)!]\). Its dimensional decay \(R_d\sim(2/\sqrt3)(16/27)^d\) is exponentially faster than the corresponding box coefficient \((2/3)^d\).
