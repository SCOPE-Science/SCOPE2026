For every pair of sine harmonics at frequencies \(n\) and \(2n\), the exact joint bounded-variation coefficient region is a convex body with a flat top and two parabolic caps, rather than the product of the individually sharp coefficient intervals. Its explicit piecewise-algebraic gauge equals the least possible variation for prescribed coefficients, and every nonzero minimum is realized by a rigid two- or three-jump pattern up to cosine-fiber splitting.
