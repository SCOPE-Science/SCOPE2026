Three normalized Szegő-kernel generators with prescribed disk radii \((r,r,s)\) have a unique determinant-maximizing angular arrangement: the two equal-radius nodes reflect about the ray opposite the third, at an angle determined by a single strictly monotone equation. For fixed \(0<s<1\), their optimal separation shrinks at the sharp rate \((1-r^2)^{1/4}\) as \(r\uparrow1\), despite the optimized Gram determinant approaching one.
