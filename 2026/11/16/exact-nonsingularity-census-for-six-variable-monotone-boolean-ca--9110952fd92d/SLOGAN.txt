Among all \(7{,}828{,}354\) monotone Boolean functions of six binary variables, exactly \(3{,}937{,}192\) have invertible Walsh-diagonal convolution: every odd support does, but only \(23{,}732\) even supports do. The exceptional even supports have precisely twelve possible support sizes and sharp minimum Walsh magnitude \(2\), while every odd support has sharp minimum magnitude \(1\).
