For circular exponential windows \(g_r(j)=r^{d_N(j)}\), two adjacent translations and every even modulation give a critical Riesz basis with explicit sharp frame bounds at every even cyclic length. The uniquely best-conditioned taper satisfies a hyperbolic equation, and even at this optimum the frame-operator condition number grows sharply as \(4(m-1)^2/(z_*^2-1)\), where \(z_*\tanh z_*=1\).
