On the seven nontrivial Walsh characters of \(\mathbb F_2^3\), the sharp fourth moments for phase alphabets of orders two through six are exactly \(109,67,61,66-3\sqrt5,61\), with complete minimizer and symmetry-orbit counts. The fifth-root alphabet strictly outperforms both its four- and six-root neighbors and has precisely two conjugate minimizing \(\operatorname{GL}(3,2)\) orbits.
