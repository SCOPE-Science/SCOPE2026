For every ambient dimension \(d\ge4\), the complete degree-zero-through-two spherical-harmonic concentration spectrum on a positive orthant consists of two explicit three-by-three blocks and one scalar, with dimension-independent block sizes. The sharp condition number of continuous restriction satisfies \(\kappa_d\sim 2d^2/[\pi(\pi-3)]\), exposing a stronger cross-degree instability than pure quadratic concentration alone.
