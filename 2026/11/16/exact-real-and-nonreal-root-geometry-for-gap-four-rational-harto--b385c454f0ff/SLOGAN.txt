For every odd \(n\ge1\), the canonical oblique-slice Bergman numerator of the rational Hartogs triangle with exponent \((n+4)/n\) has exactly four simple zeros inside the disk: two distinct negative real zeros separated by \(-(3-\sqrt5)/2\), and one nonreal conjugate pair. This exact finite-denominator root-geometry classification strengthens the known all-gap count and fixed-gap large-denominator localization without claiming a full two-variable zero-set theorem.
