Every five-row, five-column restriction of the fifteen-point Fourier matrix has rank two, three, four, or five in exactly 360, 24,480, 880,920, or 8,112,249 cases, respectively. The deepest loss of three Fourier directions occurs exactly when one index set is a coset of the five-element subgroup and the other meets precisely two residue classes modulo five; a complete exact rank-incidence table is provided.
