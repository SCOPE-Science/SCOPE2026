# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Direct enumeration of tent translates proves the exact two-channel Zak multiplier throughout \(0<b\le3/4\). A no-alias interval and a first-alias interval admit explicit sharp convex-quadratic envelopes. The lower envelope switches at \(d=\sqrt{8/5}-1\), and derivative signs give the three unique optima. Exact rational test cases and direct Zak sums were independently checked in the included standard-library script; finite tests are not substituted for the continuous proof.

## Originality
PASS relative to accessible literature. Stangl--Frederick and Hasannasab--Lemvig establish qualitative second-order spline frame properties. Their accessible assertions do not give the present low-modulation sharp spectrum or three optimizers. The directly compared published high-modulation quantitative result is restricted to \(3/2\le b\le7/4\), disjoint from this range. Faulhuber--Shafkulovska (2023) treats the same optimization questions for different windows. A full recent primary text remained inaccessible, and an old quantitative formula under another normalization remains a publication-priority risk. The search is evidence of noncoverage in inspected statements, not proof that no equivalent result exists.

## Value
PASS. The complete first-alias branch is a mathematically natural slice of half-density spline Gabor frames. It yields exact sharp stability bounds and separates three distinct, practically meaningful optimization objectives; it also displays a nontrivial branch-change threshold.

## Limitations
Only the cardinal tent (equivalently the translated centered hat), rectangular \(ab=1/2\), and \(0<b\le3/4\) are covered. No claim about arbitrary half-density lattices, larger modulation spacing, higher spline order, or independent mathematical certification is made. Inaccessible full historical papers leave publication-priority uncertainty.
