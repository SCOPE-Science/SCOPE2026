For \(\xi_0+\sqrt a\,\xi_1 z^m+\xi_2z^{2m}\), \(m\ge2\), the expected planar zero density changes from one annular maximum to two at the unique algebraic threshold \(a_m=4u_m^{3/2}\), where \(4m^2(u_m-1)^3=4u_m^3-1\) and \(u_m>1\). The threshold obeys \(a_m-4\sim6(3/4)^{1/3}m^{-2/3}\), and the new extrema emerge in a square-root fold.
