For a Haar-uniform conjugacy-class step on \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\), the exact two-step total-variation distance to Haar is uniquely minimized by two supplementary angles of \(75.403373880458\ldots^\circ\) and \(104.596626119542\ldots^\circ\). Its sharp value is \(0.10253004609429855\ldots\), with the optimum encoded by a unique root of an explicit degree-eight polynomial in the class support length.
