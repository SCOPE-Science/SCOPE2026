The \(42\) alternating-sign matrices of order four form a natural Boolean Fourier set with exactly \(15{,}360\) zero multipliers among \(65{,}536\), giving convolution rank \(50{,}176\). Its Fourier range has sharp minimum \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm \(199/32\), even when the minimization allows arbitrary linear projections.
