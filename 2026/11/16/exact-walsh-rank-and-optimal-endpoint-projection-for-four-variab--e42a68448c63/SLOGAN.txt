For the incidence-vector ensemble of all 168 antichains of the four-variable Boolean lattice, counting convolution on \(\mathbb F_2^{16}\) has exactly 6,611 zero Fourier multipliers and rank 58,925. Its range has sharp minimum \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm \(1526023/32768\), even among projections without translation symmetry.
