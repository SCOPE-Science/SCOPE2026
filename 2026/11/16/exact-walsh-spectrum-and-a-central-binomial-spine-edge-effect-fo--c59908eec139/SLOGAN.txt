For the thagomizer graphs \(K_{1,1,n}\), all spanning-tree Walsh coefficients admit a three-case formula, yielding the exact convolution rank and a positive-spectrum condition number of \(n+2\). Adding the common spine edge to \(K_{2,n}\) raises the sharp spanning-tree-frequency Fourier projection norm by exactly \(2^{-n}\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\), a central-binomial correction.
