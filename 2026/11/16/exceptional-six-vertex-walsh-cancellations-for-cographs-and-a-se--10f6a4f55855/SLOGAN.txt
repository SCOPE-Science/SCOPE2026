For labelled cographs through seven vertices, complementary graph symmetry forces every odd-support Walsh coefficient to vanish, but exactly at six vertices an additional 252 even-support zeros form the two orbits complementary to \(P_4\sqcup2K_1\) and \(C_5\sqcup K_1\). At seven vertices all even-support coefficients satisfy \(W_7(S)\equiv16\pmod{32}\), so no extra cancellation survives.
