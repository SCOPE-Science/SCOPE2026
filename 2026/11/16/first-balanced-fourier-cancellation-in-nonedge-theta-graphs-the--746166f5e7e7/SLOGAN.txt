Among all simple three-path theta graphs with at most eight vertices and no direct terminal edge, the first non-symmetry Fourier cancellation of proper ternary colourings occurs only on \(\Theta_{2,3,4}\): exactly 584 balanced frequencies vanish, reducing counting-convolution rank from 2187 to 1603. The four lowest-degree zeros correspond to two unexpectedly independent vertex-colour pairs in the uniform proper-colouring ensemble.
