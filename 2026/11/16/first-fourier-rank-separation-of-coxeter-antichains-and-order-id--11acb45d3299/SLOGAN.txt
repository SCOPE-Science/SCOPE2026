Antichain and downward-ideal incidence representations of simply-laced positive-root posets have the same Fourier-convolution rank through rank three, but first separate at rank four: the ranks are \(811\) versus \(810\) in type \(A_4\), and \(3294\) versus \(3272\) in type \(D_4\). Exact endpoint projection norms and type-\(D_4\) triality orbit counts quantify this first failure of Fourier equivalence despite the canonical antichain–ideal bijection.
