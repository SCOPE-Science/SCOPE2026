For labelled triangle-free graphs, the first nontrivial singular Fourier-convolution case after the full-rank orders three and four occurs at five vertices: exactly 150 of 1024 Walsh multipliers vanish in three relabelling orbits of sizes 60, 60, and 30. The resulting convolution has rank 874, smallest nonzero multiplier magnitude 2, and sharp orthogonal range-projection endpoint norm \(1981/256\); six vertices return to full rank because the number of triangle-free graphs is odd.
