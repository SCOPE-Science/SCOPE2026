For analytic disk Bergman spaces weighted by the *actual* Euclidean boundary distance \((1-|z|)^t\), positive central metric curvature begins sharply at \(t=(\sqrt{61}-1)/2\) and creates a small zero-curvature circle. All integer powers through three retain strictly negative curvature everywhere, while power four has exactly one positive-curvature core bounded by a unique algebraic circle.
