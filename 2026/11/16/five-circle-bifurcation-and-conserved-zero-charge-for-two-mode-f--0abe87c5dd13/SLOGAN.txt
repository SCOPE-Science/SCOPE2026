For a two-Hermite-mode signal viewed through the first-Hermite window, the nonzero charged spectrogram zeros form exactly three, four, or five concentric regular \(m-k\)-gons, with the five-gon phase exactly when \(m-k>2+\sqrt{8k}\) and the amplitude lies between two explicit fold values. The smallest five-gon example has input levels \((m,k)=(6,1)\) and 15, 20, or 25 nonzero zeros across thresholds \(c=2^{9/2}\) and \(c=3^{7/2}/2\). At the exact equality gap \(m-k=2+\sqrt{8k}\), an isolated cubic-contact resonance replaces the folds without changing the count or the conserved total signed index \(m-1\).
