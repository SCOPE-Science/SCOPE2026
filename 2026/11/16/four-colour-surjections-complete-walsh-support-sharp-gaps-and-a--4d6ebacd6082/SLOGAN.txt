On every length-\(n\) four-colour surjective-word space over \(\mathbb F_2^2\), all nontrivial Walsh zeros are symmetry-forced at even \(n\), but exactly \((3^n-3)/4\) extra zeros arise at odd \(n\). The surviving sharp multiplier gap is \(8\) or \(16\), and the optimal Fourier range projection is contractive exactly at even lengths.
