On every normalized type-\(D_d\) root configuration in dimension \(d\ge4\), quadratic-band spherical-harmonic samples survive every three-point erasure, and four-point failures are exactly the \(\binom d2\) coordinate squares, plus twelve exceptional squares only in dimension four. The exception is forced by the unique bipartite four-cycle remaining after a perfect matching is deleted from \(K_4\).
