For the two-row rectangular poset, Walsh convolution by all order-ideal incidence vectors is invertible exactly when the row length is \(0\) or \(1\) modulo \(4\), and the sharp nonzero Fourier gap is \(1\) or \(2\) accordingly. Uniformly sampled multipliers, divided by row length, converge to a non-Gaussian law with characteristic function \((\cosh t)^{-1/2}\), so the fractional Fourier nullity tends to zero even along the singular lengths.
