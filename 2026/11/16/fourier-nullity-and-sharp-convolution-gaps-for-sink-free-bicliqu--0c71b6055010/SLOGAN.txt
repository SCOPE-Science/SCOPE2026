The sink-free orientations of \(K_{2,n}\) have exactly \((3^n-1)/2\) vanishing binary Walsh multipliers for every \(n\ge3\), but the smallest nonzero multiplier magnitude is always \(2\). Hence their convolution operators are singular at every such length while their relative nullity decays exponentially and their Moore--Penrose inverse norms stay fixed at \(1/2\).
