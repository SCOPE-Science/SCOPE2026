For proper four-colourings of every triangular book with at least three pages, the cyclic and Klein abelian palettes have **different exact Fourier recovery ranks** even though both have four colours: \(4\cdot2^n+3^{n+1}-(-1)^n-2\) versus \(12\cdot2^n-8\). The sharp positive spectral condition number grows as \(3\cdot2^{\lceil n/2\rceil-1}\) for cyclic colours but stays exactly \(3\) for the Klein palette.
