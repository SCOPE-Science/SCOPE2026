Height-three Dyck up-step vectors are an affine image of a Fibonacci cube, so their counting convolution is invertible exactly when \(n\not\equiv2\pmod3\), with sharp least nonzero Walsh multiplier \(1\) or \(2\). In the original step coordinates the first singular semilengths \(2,5,8\) require respectively one, two and three selected steps for a cancelling character; at \(n=8\) there are exactly ten minimal cubic cancellations and \(7032\) zero multipliers in total.
