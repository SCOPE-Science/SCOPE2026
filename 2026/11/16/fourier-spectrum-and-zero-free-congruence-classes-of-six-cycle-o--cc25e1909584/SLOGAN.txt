The complete Walsh spectrum of six-cycle incidence vectors in \(K_{3,n}\) is controlled by a cubic polynomial in four multinomial counts. All coefficients have exact two-adic valuation \(1\) when \(n\equiv3\pmod4\) and \(3\) when \(n\equiv6\pmod8\); the sharp Fourier-projection endpoint norm is asymptotic to \((2/\pi)^{3/2}n^{3/2}\).
