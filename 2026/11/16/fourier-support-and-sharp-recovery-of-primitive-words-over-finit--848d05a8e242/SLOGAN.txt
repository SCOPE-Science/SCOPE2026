For every finite abelian alphabet of size \(q\ge2\), the indicator of length-\(n\) primitive words has Fourier support exactly the annihilator of words with period \(n/\operatorname{rad}(n)\). Its convolution rank and least nonzero multiplier are exactly \(q^{n-n/\operatorname{rad}(n)}\) and \(q^{n/\operatorname{rad}(n)}\), with all Fourier zeros explained by hidden translation symmetry.
