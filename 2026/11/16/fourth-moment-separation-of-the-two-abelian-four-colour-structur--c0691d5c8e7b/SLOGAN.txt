For proper four-colourings of every complete bipartite graph \(K_{2,n}\), the cyclic and Klein group laws have identical fourth Fourier moments only at \(n=1,2\). Their ordered additive energies first separate at \(n=3\), with the exact all-length difference \(128\cdot 2^n(4^n-3\cdot3^n+3\cdot2^n-1)\), which is positive thereafter but asymptotically negligible relative to either energy.
