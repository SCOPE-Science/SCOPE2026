# Self-review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exact Gaussian characteristic functions yield the intensity identity and the quadratic and quartic logarithmic jets. In dimension at least three, real unique factorization and a rank-one-perturbation rank bound prove uniqueness of the positive quadratic difference and rank-one center term. The reconstruction follows algebraically. A separate explicit pair of positive definite planar covariance models has identical full fourth-order jets, and a distinct three-dimensional zero-separation pair shows necessity of order four. Exact symbolic tests of the nonstandard identities passed; no finite sample is used to prove a universal statement.

## Originality

**PASS, relative to inspected evidence.** A relevant 2026 fourth-cumulant article treats a scalar variance–mean mixing model and forward formulas. Earlier Gaussian-mixture identifiability results use ordinary mixture moments and either equal covariances or generic centered-moment assumptions; a recent wavelet phase-retrieval paper uses completely different measurement families. Statement-level comparisons, including aliases for autocorrelation cumulants, phaseless Gaussian mixtures and quartic rank-one factorization, found no dominating implication. An older exact tensor classification could still duplicate the result; one plausible 2014 cumulant manuscript was only available by abstract. Priority is not independently established.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The exact global finite-jet injectivity boundary in dimension three, together with strict planar noninjectivity and minimal derivative order, addresses a natural low-frequency phase-retrieval question for nonisotropic Gaussian sources. The Loewner-order restriction is mathematically essential to the proof and is stated openly; no unsupported extension to arbitrary mixture models is claimed.

## Limitations

The result does not handle unordered incomparable covariances, unequal masses, noisy recovery stability, or whether higher-order jets remove the planar ambiguity. Independent publication-priority comparison and independent mathematical audit remain outstanding.
