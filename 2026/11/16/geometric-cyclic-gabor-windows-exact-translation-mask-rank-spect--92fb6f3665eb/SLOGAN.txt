For the positive geometric window \(g_\rho(j)=\rho^j\) on a cyclic group of order \(qm\), precisely the \(m\) cosets of the subgroup of step \(m\) among all \(q\)-translation masks yield a critically sampled Gabor basis. An exact rank law holds for every mask, and the surviving bases have a complete elementary spectrum, a closed Gram determinant, and a unique optimally conditioned exponential taper.
