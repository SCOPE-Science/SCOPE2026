For degree-\(N\) analytic polynomials on an ellipse, restriction to a tiny similar ellipse has **exact determinant** \(t^{(N+1)(N+2)}\), while its \(N+1\) eigenvalues separate into sharp powers \(t^2,t^4,\ldots,t^{2N+2}\). The least-visible mode has explicit sharp Chebyshev-controlled conditioning and, after rescaling, its \(N\) real zeros collapse to the focal-segment Chebyshev nodes.
