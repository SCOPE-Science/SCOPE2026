For every inner function \(B\) with \(B(0)=0\), the logarithmic strip symbol \(\psi_B(z)=\log[(1+B(z))/(1-B(z))]\) has exact Hardy Hankel norm and essential norm \(\pi\), while its reproducing-kernel testing norm is only \(\pi/\sqrt2\). The testing maximum is attained at exactly \(B^{-1}((-1,1))\), giving explicitly classified noncompact examples with interior norming-kernel networks.
