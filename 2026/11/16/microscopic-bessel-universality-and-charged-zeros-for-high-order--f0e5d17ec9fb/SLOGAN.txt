After \(n^{-1/2}\) rescaling, Hermite-window white-noise spectrogram fields approach a complex monochromatic Bessel random wave, and their positive and negative zero processes converge jointly to its balanced charged zeros. The normalized Fock covariance has a uniform explicit \(n^{-1}\) magnetic correction, \(\frac12 tH'(t)+i\omega H(t)\), with an \(O(n^{-2})\) remainder on compact windows.
