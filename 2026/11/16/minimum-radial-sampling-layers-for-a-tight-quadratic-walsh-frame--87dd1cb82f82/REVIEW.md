# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The object is the *homogeneous* degree-two Walsh band with a probability weighting on full signed Hamming shells. Off-diagonal Gram entries are exactly moments of two or four distinct coordinates; neither is omitted. Their Newton-identity expressions give moment conditions on the squared shell imbalance with mean \(n\) and variance \(2n(n-1)\). This proves one shell impossible and an exact necessary-and-sufficient two-shell variance equation with uniquely determined positive masses. The uniform cube and planar convex hull guarantee at most three shells without assuming integer barycentric masses. Explicit modulo-eight contradictions and two infinite parameter families complete the conclusions. All signed/boundary cases and \(n\ge4\) are included. Standalone exact rational and full-matrix checker passed.

## Originality — PASS, priority provisional

A close general result on the *entire degree-at-most-two* Walsh Gram matrix for two interior shells uses the same underlying measures, but states neither the pure-degree-two Parseval feasibility question nor its minimal shell count across all dimensions, modular exclusion classes or infinite constructions. The single-shell quadratic spectrum and q-ary first-band single-shell study cover other bands/geometries. Bannai et al. (arXiv:2006.02054, Sections 3 and 5) study Fisher-cardinality *relative* tight designs; Kirshner–Samorodnitsky (arXiv:1909.11929) analyze Krawtchouk moments; Rao (arXiv:2111.03213, Section 3) analyzes individual-slice Fourier decompositions. Those were materially inspected in accessible text, rather than inferred from titles alone. The basic second/fourth shell moments and Carathéodory theorem are standard and are not asserted to be novel. Mathematical priority risk remains from implicit corollaries of full Gram diagonalizations or specialized radial cubature literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result completely resolves a natural minimal-*radial-layer* design question for the quadratic Walsh sensing frame in every dimension, by a short exact two-atom arithmetic certificate and a universal three-layer theorem. The modular obstruction forces a genuine difference between the two possible resource counts on three complete dimension classes; infinitely many other dimensions have explicit tight two-shell constructions. The optimization unit is full shells, not individual point counts, and the Parseval equality is exact rather than approximate. The claim is intentionally restricted to homogeneous quadratics and probability-uniform shells, and no higher-degree consequence is inferred.
