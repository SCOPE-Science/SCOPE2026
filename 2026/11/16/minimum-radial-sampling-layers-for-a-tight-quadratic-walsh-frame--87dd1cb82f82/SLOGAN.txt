For the entire homogeneous quadratic Walsh band on the Boolean cube, an exactly Parseval radial sampling measure needs either two or three full Hamming shells, never one. Two shells work exactly when their squared imbalances satisfy \((n-a^2)(b^2-n)=2n(n-1)\); three are unavoidable for every \(n\equiv4,5,7\pmod8\), while two distinct infinite families admit exact two-shell frames.
