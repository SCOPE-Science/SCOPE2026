For geodesic-angle Bernstein interpolation on the two-sphere, the positive-definite Legendre input \(P_{2n-2}(\cos\theta)\) develops a negative constant spherical-harmonic coefficient: \(\sqrt n\,a_{0,n}\to-\sqrt\pi/(4\sqrt2)\). A precise interior aliasing profile explains why circle-preserving Bernstein sampling fails to preserve positive definiteness on \(S^2\) for all sufficiently large degrees.
