# Scientific review: cubic harmonics on paired right-angle lunes

## Correctness — PASS
The statement precisely fixes integer \(d\ge4\), degree-exact cubic harmonics, a half-measure angular mask and probability-normalized surface energy. Harmonicity is checked by direct Laplacians; angular and transverse orthogonality plus dimension equality prove the four-type splitting is exhaustive. Fourier coefficients of the angular sign and two explicitly evaluated beta correlations yield matrices of size four, three, two and one. Their eigenvalues give all nine levels and multiplicities, including the exact \(\binom{d}{3}\) protected half-energy dimension. Strict branch ordering and the dimension-uniform sharp condition-number limit follow analytically. The packaged symbolic and directly integrated monomial-Gram checks pass; numerical tests confirm dimensions three to eight but are not used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The all-dimensional paired-lune pure-cubic formula was compared at the level of implication against spherical Slepian general theory, a full-text 2021 spin-weighted construction for scalar/vector/tensor caps, indexed quadratic-lune and cubic-antipodal-cap spectra, and the earlier complete cubic quarter-lune calculation on the ordinary two-sphere. The latter yields the dimension-three case after doubling by antipodal symmetry; the claim deliberately starts at dimension four. The quadratic-lune formula does not compute cubic harmonic blocks, and polar caps are not equivalent by rotation to a paired angular wedge. General-region Slepian matrix methods define the operator but do not supply the four calculated blocks, their all-dimensional eigenvalues, multiplicities, strict order or conditioning law. Searches found no stronger covering result. Residual risks include an unindexed equivalent higher-dimensional wedge calculation and limited full-text access to one later Slepian-wavelet paper.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The observation geometry is a canonical pair of opposite right-angle longitude sectors, and the entire natural cubic Laplace eigenspace is treated for every dimension at least four. The result exhibits nine noncoincident levels, a sharply quantified best and worst localized mode, an unexpectedly large exactly half-energy sector of dimension \(\binom{d}{3}\), and a uniform high-dimensional stability bound. It answers a concrete full-spectrum localization question beyond a mere restatement of a general finite-matrix construction.

## Limitations and independence
The theorem is not about mixed harmonic bandwidths or arbitrary wedge angles, and no claim to priority over inaccessible specialist literature is made. Direct numerical tests are finite and depend on standard numerical libraries. The algebraic proof is self-contained. No external mathematical validation is asserted.
