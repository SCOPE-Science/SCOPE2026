For every \(n=(2^k-1)2^j\ge3\), all Walsh coefficients of the Hamiltonian-cycle incidence orbit in \(\mathbb F_2^{E(K_n)}\) have exactly the same two-adic valuation \(n-2-s_2(n-1)\); in particular, none vanish, and the associated convolution operator is invertible. Explicit signings at orders \(5\) and \(9\) have zero coefficients, showing that the binary-digit restriction matters.
