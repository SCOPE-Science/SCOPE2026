Among all conjugacy-invariant ways to sample the standard Fourier block of \(S_n\) using only permutations moving at most four symbols, the exact best squared condition number equals \(1\), \(11/8\), \(19/7\), or an explicit cubic-growth rational function, depending on dimension. A uniform half-and-half mixture of transpositions and four-cycles is already perfectly tight for \(n=4\), while higher dimensions have rigorously certified optimal short-cycle mixtures.
