The boundary-chirp complex measure \(d\mu_s(t)=(1-t)^{s-1}e^{i/(1-t)}dt\) turns every power-kernel Bergman–Cesàro operator on \(H^2\) into an element of **all** Schatten classes, even though its total-variation measure produces an unbounded operator. Its eigenvalues have exact stretched-exponential asymptotics and yield a Bergman-weight-independent Fredholm determinant and incomplete-Gamma trace.
