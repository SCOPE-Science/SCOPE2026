For two concentric \(n\)-point regular orbits in Gaussian Fock space, the smallest Gram eigenvalue under radial coalescence decays quadratically, with its exact coefficient given by a Poisson residue-class variance. For every \(n\ge3\), each angular Fourier mode becomes the uniquely weakest mode at infinitely many increasing radii, even though all mode coefficients approach \(1/2\).
