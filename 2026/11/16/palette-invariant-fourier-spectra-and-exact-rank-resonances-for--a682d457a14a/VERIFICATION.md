# Exact verification and limits

Run the included standard-library programs with `python verify_formula.py` and `python verify_transform.py`. The first enumerates potential occupancies for palette orders three through six and tests exact cancellation counts and block products; the second enumerates all proper colourings and checks the complete Gaussian-integer or Walsh Fourier transforms for bouquets of a triangle with a square and of two squares, using both cyclic and Klein-four alphabets. Each passed on the delivered science files.

These computations check finite examples and numerical normalization. The all-cactus statement and the full palette-order quantifiers instead rely on the proof's exact Fourier edge identity, balanced cut-vertex factorization, and integer divisibility/minimum-occupancy argument. No finite enumeration is asserted as an infinite proof. No independent audit has been performed.
