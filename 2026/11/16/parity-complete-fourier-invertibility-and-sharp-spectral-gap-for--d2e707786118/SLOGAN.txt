For spanning-tree incidence vectors of the two-rail ladder \(P_n\square K_2\), all Walsh coefficients are nonzero exactly when \(n\) is odd; the smallest absolute coefficient then equals \(1\), giving the exact \(\ell^2\) convolution condition number. For even \(n\), an explicit sign construction produces at least \(2^{n/2-1}\) distinct vanishing coefficients.
