For independent-set convolution on the clique windmill \(K_1\vee nK_r\), odd peripheral clique order gives complete Fourier invertibility, whereas even order \(r=2m\) produces exactly \(\binom{2m+1}{m}^n\) spectral zeros. Despite exponentially vanishing relative nullity, the globally smallest \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm onto the convolution range is exactly \(1+(\binom{2m+1}{m}/2^m)^n-(\binom{2m+1}{m}/2^{2m})^n\), which grows exponentially.
