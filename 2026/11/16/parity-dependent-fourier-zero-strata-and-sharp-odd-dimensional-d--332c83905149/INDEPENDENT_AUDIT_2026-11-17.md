The character-parity projector gives exactly product(cos θj)+(-i)^d product(sin θj) with probability mass one. For odd d real/imaginary parts must separately vanish, hence distinct cosine-zero and sine-zero coordinates and codimension-two flats. Minimal cosine-zero coordinate has magnitude 1/(4a) and only 2d nearest points. Max even Hamming distance in an odd halfcube is d-1, giving D=2a sqrt(d-1). An independent direct 2^(d-1)-atom Fourier-sum check for d=3..8 agreed to 1e-10; finite checks are corroboration, not the proof.
