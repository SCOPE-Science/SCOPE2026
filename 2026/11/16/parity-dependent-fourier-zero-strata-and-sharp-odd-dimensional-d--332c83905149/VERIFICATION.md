## Mathematical checks

The proof requires only finite-sign-sum factorization, exact trigonometric vanishing conditions, the Hamming parity distance observation, and the implicit function theorem. In the independent sanity-check session, for every integer dimension \(3\le d\le9\), thirty deterministic phase choices were tested against both the direct \(2^{d-1}\)-atom Fourier sum and the product formula, with maximal tolerated difference \(10^{-12}\); all 210 comparisons succeeded. In each dimension the explicit odd-axis or even \(\pi/4\)-phase zero witness was checked to within \(10^{-14}\). These computations are finite corroborations, not the proof of the all-dimensional theorem.

## Scope and open verification

The zero-set equivalence and closest zeros are proven only for odd dimension; even dimension is a hypersurface-existence statement. The analytic gradient at the even witness is exact. Publication-priority uncertainty persists for older characteristic-function literature. No independent mathematical audit or formal certificate has been performed.
