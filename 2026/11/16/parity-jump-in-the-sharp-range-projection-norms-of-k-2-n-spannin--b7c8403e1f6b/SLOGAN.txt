For the spanning-tree indicator of \(K_{2,n}\), the projection onto its **nonzero Walsh spectrum** has an exact sharp endpoint norm that grows like \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}\) in odd dimensions but like \(2^{n/2}\sqrt{2/(\pi n)}\) in even dimensions. The full multiplier spectrum is a known input; the sharp projection norm onto its nonzero support is the new assertion.
