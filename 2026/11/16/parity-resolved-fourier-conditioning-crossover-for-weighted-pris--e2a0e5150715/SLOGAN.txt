Weighted perfect matchings of every simple circular ladder have an exact rung-occupation Fourier spectrum, with a deterministic alternating null mode precisely at even circumference. On the mean-zero identifiable frequencies, the sharp squared-energy condition ratio grows quadratically at fixed rung activity but has two distinct finite parity limits when the activity is \(c/n\): \(1+c^2/(4\pi^2)\) for even rings and \(1+c^2/\pi^2\) for odd rings.
