The complete-graph edge-cover Walsh convolution is invertible in every even order, and its least nonzero multiplier is exactly \(1\) in even orders and \(2\) in odd orders. In the seven-vertex case exactly \(73{,}115\) of \(2^{21}\) Fourier modes vanish, forming \(39\) isomorphism classes of frequency graphs, with a complete exact-integer certificate.
