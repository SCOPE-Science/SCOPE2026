For \(g_c(t)=(t+c)/[(t^2+1)(t^2+4)]\), a nonzero sufficiently small complex numerator shift restores the half-density Gabor frame destroyed by odd symmetry. Its sharp optimal lower bound has unequal directional onset \(A(u+iv)=0.2677452138\ldots u^2+0.0169530121\ldots v^2+o(|c|^2)\), so small real shifts protect the frame about 15.79 times more effectively than equal-sized imaginary shifts.
