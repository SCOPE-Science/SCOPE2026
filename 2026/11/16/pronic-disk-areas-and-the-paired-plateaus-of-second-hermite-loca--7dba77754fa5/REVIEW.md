Every radius has at least one negative index, so global nonincrease forces both real roots in the index variable to be integers. Their arithmetic then forces \(s=k(k+1)\). Conversely, at those areas the factorization \(Q_j(s)=(j-k^2)(j-(k+1)^2)\) gives precisely two separate triple blocks. Exact polynomial integration verifies the displayed eigenvalues at area two.
