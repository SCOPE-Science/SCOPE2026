Exact symbolic checks are in `verify_cap.py`; execute with `python verify_cap.py`. The program checks the three- and six-order cyclotomic remainders and numerical trigonometric integrals for several non-exceptional and exceptional radii. The proof is analytic and universal, rather than a finite search. It uses the published four-term Mann root-of-unity theorem. Independent validation is not performed.
