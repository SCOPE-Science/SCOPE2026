For every forbidden run length \(k\ge2\) and word length \(n\ge1\), the integer multilinear coefficients of the binary \(1^k\)-avoidance indicator are precisely \(0,\pm1\), with nonzero coefficients exactly on coordinate subsets whose consecutive runs have lengths congruent to \(0\) or \(k\) modulo \(k+1\). This yields the sharp least nonzero Walsh multiplier \(2^r\) for \(r=n\bmod(k+1)\in\{1,\ldots,k-1\}\) and \(1\) otherwise, and hence the exact counting-convolution pseudoinverse norm.
