For uniform random chord pairings on \(2m\) circular sites, the covariance of counts crossing the \(m\) distinct equal-half cuts is an explicit cyclic Green kernel whose inverse is a nearest-neighbor Laplacian plus one rank-one correction. The strongest Fourier mode changes exactly at \(m=5\), and the precise covariance condition ratio satisfies \(\kappa_m/m^2\to1/6\).
