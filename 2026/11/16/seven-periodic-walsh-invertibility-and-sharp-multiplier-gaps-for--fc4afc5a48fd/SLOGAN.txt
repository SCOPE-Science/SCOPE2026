The Walsh transform of the independent-set indicator of a squared path has a zero exactly when its length is \(1\), \(3\), or \(4\) modulo seven, and its smallest nonzero absolute multiplier is exactly two in those cases and one otherwise. A universal family of 32 seven-sign blocks preserves multiplier magnitude, yielding exponentially many explicit null frequencies and sharp \(\ell^2\) convolution inverse or pseudoinverse norms.
