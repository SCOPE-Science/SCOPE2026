For five axes obtained by deleting one antipodal vertex pair from an icosahedron, pure-degree spherical-harmonic interpolation is onto five-point data at every degree except \(\ell=0,1,3\). At all remaining degrees the normalized sample Gram matrix has sharp smallest eigenvalue \(1/5\) and squared condition number at most \(6\), with equality exactly at degrees \(2,4,8,14\).
