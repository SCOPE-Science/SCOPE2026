For any four positive consecutive coefficient magnitudes \((A,B,C,D)\), nontrivial signals sharing both those magnitudes and their entire Fourier intensity exist **exactly** inside the strict triangle \(|AC-BD|<|A^2-D^2|<AC+BD\). Every fiber has at most three phase classes, and the threefold case occurs exactly when \(AC=BD=|A^2-D^2|\); earlier literature already exhibited isolated four-tap triple examples.
