For the first Johnson harmonic on \(J(2k,k)\), no individual distance shell is tight, and the best squared frame condition number is at least \(1+1/(2k+1)\), with equality exactly at \(k=2u^2-1\) for odd \(u\ge3\). The optimal excess has sharp subsequential scales: \(\liminf k(\mathcal K_k-1)=1/2\) and \(\limsup\sqrt{k}(\mathcal K_k-1)=2\sqrt2\).
