For the logarithmically collapsing Hartogs domains of Chen–Xiong, the axial Bergman metric has the sharp leading coefficient \(\sqrt{2\alpha}\) multiplying \((1-r^2)^{-1}\bigl(\log(e/(1-r^2))\bigr)^{(\alpha-1)/2}\) for every fixed \(\alpha>1\). Its intrinsic axial distance consequently has leading coefficient \(\sqrt{2\alpha}/(\alpha+1)\), without asserting a sharp ambient geodesic distance.
