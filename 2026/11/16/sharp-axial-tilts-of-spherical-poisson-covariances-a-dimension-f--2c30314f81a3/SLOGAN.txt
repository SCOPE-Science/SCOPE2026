A first-order latitude-amplitude tilt of the spherical Poisson covariance is positive definite in every dimension \(d\ge2\) precisely up to \(|\varepsilon|=\sqrt r/(1+r)\). At the sharp boundary the covariance has no positive uniform spectral lower bound but remains \(C(S^d)\)-universal and strictly positive definite on every distinct finite point set; on \(S^2\), finite harmonic tests miss the boundary by exact Legendre-root margins.
