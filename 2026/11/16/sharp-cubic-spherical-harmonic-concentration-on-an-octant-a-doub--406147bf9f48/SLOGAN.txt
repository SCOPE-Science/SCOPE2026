The seven pure cubic spherical harmonics have an exact five-level concentration spectrum on one positive spherical octant, with sharp energy fractions ranging from \((\pi-3)/(8\pi)\) to \((30\pi+25+\sqrt{4045})/(240\pi)\). The least-concentrated mode is the unique alternating cubic \((x-y)(x-z)(y-z)\), whereas the maximally concentrated eigenspace has complex dimension two.
