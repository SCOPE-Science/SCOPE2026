For a strictly positive three-point signal on a noncollinear triple in \(\mathbb F_p^2\), a Fourier zero forces its amplitude-ratio field to be the entire maximal real \(p\)-th cyclotomic field, of sharp degree \((p-1)/2\). Exactly \((p^2-1)/8\) positive amplitude rays admit zeros, and each has precisely two opposite Fourier zeros.
