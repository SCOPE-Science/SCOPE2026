For a positive three-atom Euclidean measure whose weights form a strict triangle, every real Fourier zero obeys the sharp diameter bound \(|\xi|D\ge\tfrac12+\gamma_{\min}/(2\pi)\), where \(\gamma_{\min}\) is the smallest interior angle of the triangle of weights. Noncollinear extremizers exist for every weight triple, and all real zeros of any fixed noncollinear configuration are exactly two explicit affine reciprocal-lattice cosets.
