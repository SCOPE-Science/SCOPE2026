In the symmetric dyadic Fourier spectrum \(\{0\}\cup\{\pm2^j\}\), every nonzero frequency has at most eight distinct unordered three-term representations without an opposite cancelling pair, and eight occurs precisely at \(\pm3\cdot2^v\) for \(v\ge1\). An exact formula computes every nonzero fiber from binary popcounts and Mersenne odd parts; the zero fiber is infinite.
