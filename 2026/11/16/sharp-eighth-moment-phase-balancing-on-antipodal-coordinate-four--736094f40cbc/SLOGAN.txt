For every dimension \(d\ge4\), the unit-coefficient antipodal coordinate Fourier aperture has an exact eighth-moment sum-of-squares identity, with sharp minimum characterized by the simultaneous vanishing of the first two power sums of its antipodal coefficient products. The minimizers are rigid regular polygons in dimensions four and five, while ternary and quaternary phase alphabets attain the all-phase optimum precisely at dimensions divisible by three and four, respectively.
