For every prime power \(q\ge2\) and dimension \(n\ge6\), the rank-at-most-four alternating-matrix ball has a nowhere-zero Fourier transform with exactly one negative dual-rank orbit: corank two for even \(n\), corank three for odd \(n\). A closed polynomial for every Fourier coefficient gives the exact sharp \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) Fourier-projection norms.
