For independent-set convolution on the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M\), even sizes give invertible convolution, whereas odd sizes lose an asymptotically vanishing fraction of Fourier modes. Nevertheless the sharp \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm onto the surviving range grows as \(\Theta(2^{n/2}/\sqrt n)\) along odd sizes, with an exact Krawtchouk-sum formula.
