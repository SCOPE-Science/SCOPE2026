For a natural power-law family of positive-definite spherical Legendre kernels, the equator exhibits a sharp phase transition: fractional \(\alpha-\tfrac12\) Hölder cusps below \(\alpha=3/2\), an exact \(h\log(1/|h|)\) cusp at the threshold, and a finite nonzero derivative above it. All singular regimes persist with their sharp constants after an analytic strictly positive-definite admixture.
