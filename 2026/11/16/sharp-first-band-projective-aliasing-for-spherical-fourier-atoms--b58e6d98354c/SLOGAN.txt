The projective degree-zero-plus-one spherical Fourier coefficients identify every single point source in a closed radius-\(R\) ball exactly when \(\kappa R<\pi\), with sharp inverse ratio-coordinate constant \(3/\kappa\). Beyond this sharp threshold, all aliases are classified by the scalar map \(1/z-\cot z\), including paired aliases in every higher radial band and antipodal zero-mean lines; in the alias-free ball the exact multiatom linear rank is one plus an affine dimension.
