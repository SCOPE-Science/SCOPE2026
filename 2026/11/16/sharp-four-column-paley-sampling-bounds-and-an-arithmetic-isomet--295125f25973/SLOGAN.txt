For nonzero-quadratic-residue Fourier sampling on prime cycles with \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), the sharp smallest and largest four-column singular values have explicit formulas and complete Paley-graph equality geometries. Exactly at the exceptional primes \(p=13,17\), the worst isometry defect comes from the upper spectral edge, while from \(p=29\) onward it comes from a four-independent-set lower edge.
