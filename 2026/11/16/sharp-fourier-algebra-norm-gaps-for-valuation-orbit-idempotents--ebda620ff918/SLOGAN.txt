For every finite abelian valuation chain with constant branching index \(q\ge3\), nonball radial indicators have Fourier-algebra norm at least \(2(1-1/q)\), with equality precisely for single valuation shells. At binary branching, shell indicators themselves have norm one and the first nonunit norm is exactly \(3/2\), attained by two explicit adjacent-layer configurations; an exact rational formula determines the norm of every radial indicator.
