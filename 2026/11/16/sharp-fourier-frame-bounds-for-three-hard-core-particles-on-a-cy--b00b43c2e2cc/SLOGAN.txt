For a uniformly sampled three-particle hard-core configuration on a cycle, the centered coordinate Walsh Gram matrix has one forced zero Fourier mode and an explicit positive spectrum on every other mode once the cycle has at least seven sites. Its sharp squared-energy condition ratio is attained between the first and most alternating spatial harmonics and equals \(1+8/n+O(n^{-2})\), in contrast to the singular six-site boundary.
