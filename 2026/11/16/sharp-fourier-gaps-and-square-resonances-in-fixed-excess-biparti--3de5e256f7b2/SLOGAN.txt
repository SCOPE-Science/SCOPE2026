For connected spanning edge sets of every fixed cycle rank in \(K_{2,n}\), every Walsh multiplier is an exact binary Krawtchouk value times a universal power of two, yielding the sharp nonzero spectral gap \(2^{n-k-1}\) and positive condition number \(\binom n{k+1}\). At cycle rank one, the Fourier nullity has two independent arithmetic resonances: odd values of \(n\) and perfect-square values of \(n\).
