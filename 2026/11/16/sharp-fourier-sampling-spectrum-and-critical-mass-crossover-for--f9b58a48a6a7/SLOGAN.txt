For every positive root activity, the Fourier covariance of edge sampling from rooted forests of a cycle is strictly positive and has its unique weakest mode at zero frequency and its strongest mode at the frequency nearest alternation. Its sharp condition ratio undergoes a universal \(n^{-2}\) activity crossover, with limiting profile \(2\sinh\sqrt c/(\sinh\sqrt c-\sqrt c)\), even though the fixed-positive-activity thermodynamic ratio is always between one and two.
