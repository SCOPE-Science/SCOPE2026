For a centered \(2d+1\)-tone coordinate-cross Fourier polynomial with all coefficient magnitudes one, its fourth moment is exactly \(8d^2+2d+4|\tfrac12+\sum_j u_j|^2\): the sharp minimum is attained in continuous phase but by **no finite root-of-unity alphabet**, in any dimension \(d\ge2\). Binary, quaternary and six-phase coefficients have an exact one-unit gap, ternary coefficients have a dimension-dependent one-or-three-unit gap, and at the five-point cross five phases first beat binary with exact moment \(41-2\sqrt5\).
