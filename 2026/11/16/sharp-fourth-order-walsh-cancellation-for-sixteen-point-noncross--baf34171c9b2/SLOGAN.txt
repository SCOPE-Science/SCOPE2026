On sixteen convexly arranged vertices, the noncrossing perfect-matching codebook has no Walsh cancellation on fewer than four chord coordinates, and exactly 544 cancelling four-chord supports, forming 17 dihedral orbits of stars and brooms. For every even matching size \(n\ge4\), a reflected choice of \(n/2\) chords at one vertex yields an exact Walsh cancellation; minimum-support sharpness beyond the sixteen-vertex case is not claimed.
