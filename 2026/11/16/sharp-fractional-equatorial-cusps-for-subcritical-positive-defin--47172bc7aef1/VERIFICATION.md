The analytic proof is based on a uniform interior Legendre asymptotic with a first-derivative remainder estimate, a scale-split perturbation bound, and a Riemann-sum representation of the Euler sine integral. The embedded `check.py` independently evaluates finite recurrence sums and prints their signed ratios against the predicted leading coefficient, checking normalization and sign. Numerical truncations do not certify the infinite asymptotic. No external or independent audit has been performed.
