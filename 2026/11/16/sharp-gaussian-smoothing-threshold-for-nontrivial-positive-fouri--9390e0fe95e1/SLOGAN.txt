An explicit pair of noncongruent positive three-Gaussian mixtures has identical nonvanishing Fourier intensities, and both become globally log-concave at the exact common smoothing threshold \(t_*=0.2548339622562\ldots\). Above the threshold they are uniformly strongly log-concave, while normalized ambiguities persist arbitrarily close to a standard Gaussian with uniformly Gaussian-like log curvature.
