The sparsest graph-Fourier transform of an equal-amplitude signed two-vertex signal on the path \(P_n\) is attained only by reflected pairs: it has \((n-q+1)/2\) coefficients when \(n\) is even with odd part \(q\), and \((n-n/p)/2\) when \(n\) is odd with least prime divisor \(p\). The optimal sign, complete position classification, and number of optimizers follow a sharp arithmetic parity law.
