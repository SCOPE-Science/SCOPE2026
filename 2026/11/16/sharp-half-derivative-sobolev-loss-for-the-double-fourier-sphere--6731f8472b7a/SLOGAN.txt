The double Fourier sphere parametrization loses exactly one half of an \(L^2\)-Sobolev derivative: it maps \(H^s(\mathbb S^2)\) boundedly into \(H^t(\mathbb T^2)\) precisely when \(s\ge t+1/2\), with equality allowed. Polar annular test functions show that the loss is unavoidable, while a fractional Hardy inequality proves the endpoint.
