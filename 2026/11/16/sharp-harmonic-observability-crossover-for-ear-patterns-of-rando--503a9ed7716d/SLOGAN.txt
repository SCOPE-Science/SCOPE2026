Uniform triangulations of a convex polygon have a complete Catalan–Fourier ear-observation spectrum: the least-observable direction switches sharply from total ear count to the longest nonconstant wavelength between \(n=20\) and \(n=21\). The largest positive-to-smallest positive covariance eigenvalue ratio is at most \(49/2\), with equality only for the hexagon, and tends to \(5\) for large polygons.
