For complex spherical harmonics of one fixed degree \(\ell\), the fewest distinct absolute latitudes in any positive tight sampling scheme is exactly \(\lceil(\ell+1)/2\rceil\); that threshold is attained entirely in the northern hemisphere using equiangular rings. At the minimum, squared-latitude designs are unique for odd \(\ell\) (Gauss-Jacobi nodes), whereas even \(\ell\) admits an explicitly bounded one-parameter Jacobi family.
