The smallest one-jump nondecreasing lacunary coefficient family has an exact geometric cutoff: \(F_{p,N}(z)=\int_0^z(1+t^{pN})/(1-t^p)\,dt\) is close-to-convex precisely when \(p\le2\) or \(N=1\). For every higher lacunarity \(p\ge3\), two boundary numerator zeros force a forbidden angular turn as soon as \(N\ge2\); nonunivalence is not asserted.
