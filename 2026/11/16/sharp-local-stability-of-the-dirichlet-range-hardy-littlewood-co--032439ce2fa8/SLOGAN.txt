For the contractive Hardy–Littlewood inequality in the Dirichlet range, the optimal transverse local quadratic stability constant is \(\frac p2\frac{\beta-\alpha}{\beta+1}\), with equality approached only along the second Taylor mode after conformal symmetry is removed. The complete Hessian spectrum rises strictly from this gap to \(p/2\), while the first mode is neutral because it moves along the extremizer family.
