On the complete parabola over \(\mathbb F_5\), the sixth Fourier moment of five unit-modulus coefficients equals \(500+9\left|\sum_s a_{s-1}a_{s+1}/(a_{s-2}a_{s+2})\right|^2\). Its sharp minimum is \(500\), and every five-unit-vector zero sum with product one determines exactly five minimizing coefficient-phase classes. Among fifth-root coefficient phases, precisely 100 projective minimizers exist, exactly the nonquadratic cubic chirps.
