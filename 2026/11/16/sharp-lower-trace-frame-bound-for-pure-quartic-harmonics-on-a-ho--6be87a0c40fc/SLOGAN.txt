Among all product-circle Hopf tori in the three-sphere, exactly two placements maximize the lower trace-frame bound for pure quartic spherical harmonics. Their squared first radii are \(\tau\) and \(1-\tau\), where \(25\tau^3-48\tau^2+30\tau-6=0\) and \(\tau\in(0.42,0.43)\); the exact optimal lower squared singular value is \(5\tau^4\), with a six-dimensional bottleneck eigenspace.
