For fixed total degree, Gaussian Fock concentration on two unequal product discs has a sharp no-skipping staircase: every monomial becomes uniquely most observable on its own interval of the first disc's radius, and only neighboring modes tie at transition points. The least observable mode is always an endpoint monomial, and the exact squared-energy condition ratio grows as a positive explicit constant times \(m^{-3/2}((a+b)/\min(a,b))^m\) at large degree.
