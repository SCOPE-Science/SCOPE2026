In the real one-dimensional Gaussian-Hermite Fourier-\(+1\) span of orders \(0,4,8\), vanishing at the origin forces a nonpositive-set length of at least \(2\sqrt{7-\sqrt{35}}/\sqrt\pi\), attained only by the positive ray \(e^{-\pi x^2}z^2(z^2-14z+35)\) with \(z=2\pi x^2\). The minimizing negative set is a detached symmetric annulus in one dimension, furnishing an exact finite-mode benchmark for definite-set uncertainty without asserting the unrestricted optimum.
