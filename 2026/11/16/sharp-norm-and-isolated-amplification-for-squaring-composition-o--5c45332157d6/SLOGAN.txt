The canonical self-composition \(f(z)\mapsto f(z^2)\) on \(\mathcal H((1+z^2)/2)\) has norm \(\sqrt{\lambda_*}\), where \(\lambda_*>4\) is the unique root of \(\lambda^3-6\lambda^2+8\lambda-4=0\), while its essential norm is exactly \(2\). A rational extremizer realizes the isolated norm amplification caused by coalescing the two boundary atoms under squaring.
