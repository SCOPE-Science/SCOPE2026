On an infinite normalized pendant chain, one complex number per cell samples every low-pass graph signal stably up to the exact two-band threshold \(\omega=4/3\), with a unique best real-mixing sensor and sharp worst-case energy fraction \((2+\sqrt3)/4\) for the full lower band. The quadrature measurements \(f(n,s)\pm if(n,p)\) are instead exactly isometric, and every one-channel fixed-cell sampler fails above the threshold.
