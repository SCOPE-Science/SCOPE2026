For the real three-site Gabor window \(g_s=(1,1,s)\), every set of eight out of its nine cyclic time-frequency intensity measurements phase-retrieves all of \(\mathbb C^3\) exactly when \(s\notin\{1,-1,-\tfrac12\}\). The eight-measurement threshold is optimal in complex dimension three, and even the two-tap window at \(s=0\) tolerates any single erased measurement.
