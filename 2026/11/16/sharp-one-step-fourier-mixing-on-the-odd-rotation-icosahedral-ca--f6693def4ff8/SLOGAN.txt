On the icosahedral rotation group, if every nontrivial odd-order rotation is permitted but half-turns and holding are forbidden, the uniform 44-move walk minimizes one-step mean-zero Euclidean contraction among all supported doubly stochastic chains, at the sharp factor \(1/11\). Its exact nonconstant Fourier eigenvalues are \(1/11\), \(-1/44\), and \(-1/11\); uniqueness is proved only within central-convolution walks.
