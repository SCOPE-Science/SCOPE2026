For a fixed spherical-harmonic cutoff \(L\), the worst-observable mode on a shrinking north polar cap has energy fraction asymptotic to \(h^{2L+1}/(2^{2L+1}\binom{2L}{L}^{2})\), where \(h=1-\cos\Theta\), and every azimuthal sector has a distinct explicit Jacobi spectral floor. The least-observable mode is eventually a unique zonal function whose \(L\) nodal latitudes collapse onto shifted Gauss–Legendre nodes, while the exact spectral condition-number constant is \((L+1)^2 2^{2L}\binom{2L}{L}^{2}\).
