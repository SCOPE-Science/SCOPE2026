For complex signals whose Fourier support is two cosets of a finite abelian subgroup, complete conjugate polyspectra recover translation exactly at the sharp order \(q+1\), where \(q\ge3\) is the quotient order. Given Fourier magnitudes, an explicit set of exactly \(2|H|\) individual polyspectral entries is necessary and sufficient for uniform recovery, using only cubic entries and one order-\(q+1\) entry.
