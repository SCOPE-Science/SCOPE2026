# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The finding consists of one sharp sign-trichotomy statement for the Fourier-spectral circular Matérn kernel. The RKHS variational identity gives the exact finite precision quadratic form. For fractional order \(1/2<s<1\), the strictly positive heat-derived nonlocal jump kernel makes the min/max energy submodularity **strict** when two cardinal interpolants cross, proving every off-diagonal precision entry negative. At the local endpoint, independent arc minimization yields the exact nearest-neighbor formula, including the double-arc case with two sites. The four-site Fourier alias spectrum gives an explicit positive zeta limit for every \(s>1\). All quantifiers are covered analytically. The included code corroborates 125 finite identities but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with provisional historical priority
Huang–Li (2022), the nearest same-object source, supplies the Fourier circle Matérn model and the regular-grid order-one Markov connection; its order-two discussion is an approximation and does not yield the sharp all-exponent, all-irregular-grid inverse-sign boundary. Antil–Saibaba (2022) treats general fractional Whittle–Matérn covariance powers rather than discrete precision signs. The positive jump-form mechanism is classical Dirichlet-form theory and is credited rather than presented as an original general theorem. The complete if-and-only-if sign regime, exact local endpoint for arbitrary sites, and four-point superunit converse were not identified in the inspected primary literature or relevant research database comparisons. Risk: an earlier general Green-kernel or total-positivity result may implicitly cover an equivalent formulation.

## Value — PASS
A circular Gaussian field's inverse covariance determines partial correlations and conditional independence. Smoothness can change that graph dramatically, so a sharp parameter boundary for *all* irregular samples is structurally meaningful. The result distinguishes dense strictly attractive interactions, exact sparse Markov interactions, and an unavoidable non-attractive sample, rather than offering an arbitrary small table of inverses.

## Scope and limits
This is a one-dimensional periodic spectral covariance result for \(s>1/2\), \(\kappa>0\), and noise-free point evaluations. No assertion is made for distinct geometric covariance definitions, higher dimensionality, or all scales in the positive-entry converse. Historical priority is provisional; independent verification is absent.
