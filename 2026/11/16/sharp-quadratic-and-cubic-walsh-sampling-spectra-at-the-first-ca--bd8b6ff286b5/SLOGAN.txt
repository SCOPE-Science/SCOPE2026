On the fourteen Dyck words of semilength four, the complete degree-two Walsh sampler has a single explicit eight-term ambiguity, while cubic sampling restores perfect identifiability. Exact Gram characteristic polynomials give sharp recovery condition numbers \(\sqrt{\alpha/12}\) and \(\sqrt{\beta/64}\), where \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are the largest roots of the specified cubic factors; this is a stability refinement beyond previously studied Catalan graded ranks.
