Erasing any two observations from quadrature-weighted Chebyshev–Lobatto sampling of degree-at-most-\(N-2\) polynomials preserves invertibility for every \(N\ge4\). The unique worst patterns erase an endpoint and its nearest neighbor, with sharp squared condition ratio \(\kappa_N\sim18N^5/\pi^4\), in contrast to the seventh-power law for the half-shifted Gauss grid.
