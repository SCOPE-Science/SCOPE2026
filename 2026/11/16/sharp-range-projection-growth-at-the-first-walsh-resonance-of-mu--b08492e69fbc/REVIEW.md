# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The disjoint-coordinate-subgroup formula for independent supports gives multipliers \(q(t-1)\), with zeros exactly at one zero-frequency block and least positive absolute value \(q\). Character orthogonality yields the exact omitted-shell kernel, and grouping inputs by the number of nonzero blocks gives a binomial absolute sum. Its sign change at the first singular part count permits the closed evaluation. Sharpness is the attained \(\ell^1\) convolution-kernel norm. Exact independent computations corroborate the small instances.

**Originality (PASS, bounded comparison).** Scott–Sokal and Zhu–Lu already imply the independent-set enumerator and its signed specialization; these are explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Complete texts of the closest two Fourier Hamming-shell reports address *spectral compression / restriction to a shell*, not the norm of the projection onto the full-group Fourier complement of a shell. The sharp endpoint norm, its singular-threshold parameter choice, and its asymptotic regime were not found in the searched records. A classical Krawtchouk/Wiener-algebra norm identity may yield the result under reformulation; such a source would need direct mathematical comparison.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** At the first singular multipartite family member, an operator with a discrete positive multiplier gap has a range projection whose endpoint norm grows exponentially in the number of parts. The exact norm is a natural amplification invariant, fully computed for infinitely many increasingly large natural graphs, and the calculation isolates the mechanism at the transition to singularity. It is not an arbitrary finite slice or a mere numerical table.

**Closest scientific sources.** Scott–Sokal, arXiv:cond-mat/0309352 (multivariate independent-set polynomial, includes bipartite example); Zhu–Lu, arXiv:1507.05754 (multipartite ordinary enumerator); Green–Sanders, arXiv:math/0611286 (general Fourier-idempotent bounds); *An all-degree spectral law for Fourier sampling on q-ary Hamming spheres* and *Exact q-ary Hamming-shell Fourier concentration and the arithmetic tightness criterion* (different restrictions/compressed operators).

**Limitations and risks.** The result is specific to equal binary blocks with \(q+1\) parts. The novelty assessment is bounded rather than a historical-priority certificate, notably for known first-degree q-ary Krawtchouk identities and Wiener norms. Independent validation has not been performed.
