At the first singular part count for an equal-block complete multipartite independent-set convolution, the Fourier range projection has a sharp norm \(1+r(q-1)^{r-1}((q-2)2^{r-1}+2)/q^r\) for \(q=2^m\) and \(r=q+1\). Its endpoint amplification grows as \((q/e)2^q\), even though the least positive convolution singular value is exactly \(q\).
