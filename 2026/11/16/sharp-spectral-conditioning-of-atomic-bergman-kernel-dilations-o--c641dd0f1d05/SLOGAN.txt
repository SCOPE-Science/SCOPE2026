For atomic Bergman-kernel Cesàro dilations on Hardy space, the unit-normalized eigenfunctions have exact optimal Riesz bounds given by two convergent infinite products, and their squared condition number grows at logarithmic rate \(p\pi^2/[2(-\log q)]\) as the atom approaches the boundary. Every spectral projection norm is explicit and its largest-index limit has the smaller logarithmic growth rate \(p\pi^2/[4(-\log q)]\).
