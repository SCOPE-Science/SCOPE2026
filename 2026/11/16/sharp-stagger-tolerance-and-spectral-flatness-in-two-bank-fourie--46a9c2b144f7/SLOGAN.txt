Two staggered banks of values and normalized derivatives interpolate every \(2m\)-mode Fourier polynomial with sharp optimal condition number \(1+\sqrt2\). The gain \(t=\sqrt2\) is unique, but every derivative-grid shift satisfying \(\sin^2(\pi\alpha)\le1-1/\sqrt2\) is equally optimal for \(m\ge2\); the critical shifts flatten the entire least-singular-value band.
