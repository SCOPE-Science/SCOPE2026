A family of stretched-exponential Beurling weights all have the same Fourier spectrum, yet one explicit two-direction filter has an inverse norm growing like a sharp stretched exponential as its spectral gap closes. The inverse's weighted coefficient mass concentrates in a Gaussian window at lattice distance \(r\asymp(1-t)^{-1/[2(1-\alpha)]}\), with an explicit prefactor and boundary-growth constant.
