For \(n\) equally spaced normalized Fock kernels on a circle, the squared Gram condition number strictly improves with squared radius; the exact radius required to achieve any fixed condition bound \(C>1\) is asymptotic to \(n\sqrt{\alpha_C}\), where \(\alpha_C\) is determined by a sharp \(\vartheta_3/\vartheta_4\) heat-kernel ratio. The known Poisson-residue Gram spectrum underlies, but does not itself state, this large-orbit threshold.
