For the finite Cantor towers with digits \(\{0,1,2\}\), the best achievable pairwise integer-Fourier correlation is exactly zero when the base is divisible by three, and otherwise an explicit strictly positive sine product with precisely two minimizing frequency gaps (apart from one first-level exception). In the nondivisible case it decays sharply like \(C_b(2\pi/(3\sqrt3))^n b^{-n(n+1)/2}\), quantifying superexponential near-orthogonality without an exact integer orthogonal pair.
