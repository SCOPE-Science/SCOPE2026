On the unweighted infinite two-rail ladder, observing one entire rail at time zero and once after heat diffusion stably reconstructs every square-summable signal, with exact optimal sampling bounds. The unique delay maximizing the lower bound is \(t_*=-\tfrac32\log r_*\), where \(r_*\) is the unique root in \((0,1)\) of \(3r^6-3r^5+5r^4-5r^3+5r^2+4r-4=0\).
