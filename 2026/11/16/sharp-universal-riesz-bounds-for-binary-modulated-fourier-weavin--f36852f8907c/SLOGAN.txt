For two Fourier orthonormal bases differing by the phase \(e^{2\pi i\theta t}\), every arbitrary binary weaving has **exactly sharp uniform squared Riesz bounds** \(1\pm\sin(\pi|\theta|)\) when \(|\theta|<1/2\), and the one-sided weaving saturates both through a shifted Hilbert matrix. At the critical half-phase an explicit weaving loses the frame lower bound, while above it a Hardy-function witness makes the same one-sided weave incomplete; the qualitative cutoff is classical, whereas the sharp uniform quantitative envelope is the asserted contribution.
