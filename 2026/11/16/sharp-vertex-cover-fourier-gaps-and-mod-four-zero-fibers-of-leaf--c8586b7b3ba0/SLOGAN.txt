For every finite graph \(G\), independent-set convolution on its leaf corona \(G\circ K_1\) has exact minimum nonzero Walsh magnitude \(2^{\tau(G)}\), controlled by the vertex-cover number. For path cores, a fixed pendant-frequency fiber contains both Fourier zeros and nonzeros exactly when the pendant support is independent and its residual path has a component of length \(2\pmod4\); otherwise the entire fiber is either zero or nonzero with one fixed dyadic valuation.
