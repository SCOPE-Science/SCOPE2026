## Mathematical value — PASS

The complete bipartite orientation model is a natural, previously enumerated family; three rows form the first step beyond the thin two-row case. The new all-order endpoint of the nonzero multiplier spectrum provides the exact worst-case inverse amplification on the observable Fourier subspace, not merely a count of orientations or a parity-induced nullspace.
