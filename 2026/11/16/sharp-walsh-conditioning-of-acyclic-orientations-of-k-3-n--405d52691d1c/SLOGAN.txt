For every \(n\ge2\), counting convolution by the acyclic edge orientations of \(K_{3,n}\) is invertible on precisely the reversal-even half of the Boolean edge cube. A thirteen-product exact Walsh formula and parity-dependent signings show that its smallest nonzero multiplier is exactly \(2\), yielding sharp inverse norm \(1/2\) and condition number \(3\cdot4^n-3\cdot3^n+2^{n-1}\).
