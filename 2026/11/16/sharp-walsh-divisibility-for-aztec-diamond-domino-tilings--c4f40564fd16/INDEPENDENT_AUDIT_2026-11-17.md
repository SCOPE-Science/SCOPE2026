# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For n≥1 the signed perfect-matching incidence Walsh transform is, up to one global sign, the determinant of a fixed real bipartite Kasteleyn signing with independently switched edge entries. Modulo 2 its m=n(n+1) square matrix has factor U⊗V of rank n², forcing 2^n divisibility of every determinant. Deleting the n rows and columns indexed by w_{j,j},b_{j,j} leaves an n²-square F2-invertible minor: the row parity equations force any retained-column kernel vector to vanish. The degree-n Boolean determinant finite difference on these n independent diagonal edges is (-2)^n times that odd minor; after dividing all determinants by 2^n, the sum over 2^n signings is odd, so at least one exact valuation-n witness exists, indeed an odd number. Inversion against one matching proves no odd common divisor of all Walsh coefficients, giving gcd exactly 2^n. Independent integer matching-incidence enumeration gave n=1..4 totals 2,8,64,1024; gcd 2,4,8,16 and odd-sharp counts 1,3,3,5. We did not extrapolate from these instances.
