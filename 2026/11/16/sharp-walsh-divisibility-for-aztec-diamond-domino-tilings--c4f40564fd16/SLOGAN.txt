For the order-\(n\) Aztec diamond, the greatest common divisor of all independently signed domino-tiling sums, equivalently all Walsh coefficients of its perfect-matching incidence indicator, is exactly \(2^n\). An explicitly specified collection of \(n\) domino positions already has an odd number of sign patterns attaining this minimal two-adic valuation, contrasting with the much higher valuation \(n(n+1)/2\) of the unsigned tiling count.
