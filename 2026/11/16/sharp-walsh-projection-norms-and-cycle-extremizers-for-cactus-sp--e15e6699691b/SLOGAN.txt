For every connected cactus, the spanning-tree Walsh spectrum factors over its cycle blocks, giving an exact convolution-rank formula and a sharp Fourier-projection endpoint norm depending only on cycle lengths. Such convolution is invertible exactly when every cycle is odd, and among all cacti on \(N\) vertices the largest projection norm is \((3/2)^{\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor}\), attained by triangle packings with a single allowed parity adjustment.
