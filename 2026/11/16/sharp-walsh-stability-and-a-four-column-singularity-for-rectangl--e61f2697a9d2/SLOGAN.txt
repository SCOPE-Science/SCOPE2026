For rectangle-free labelled binary \(3\times n\) matrices, counting convolution on the Boolean cube is invertible exactly at widths \(n=2,3\); every larger width has a Fourier kernel. Across all characters the sharp smallest nonzero unnormalized Walsh magnitude is \(2\) at widths two and three and \(2^{n-2}\) thereafter, giving exact Hilbert-space pseudoinverse and positive-spectrum condition numbers.
