For the canonical Gram kernel of weighted upper-half-plane evaluations \(f+af'+bf''\) with real coefficients, zero-freeness holds exactly when \(a^2\ge 2b+2\sqrt{(h+2)(h+3)/(h(h+1))}|b|\), where \(h=\alpha+2\). Below the sharp threshold exactly two simple attainable scalar zeros occur: adding a first-derivative term can remove the zeros forced by an isolated second-derivative term.
