Every Schur-complement pivot is positive, and its ratio obeys a strict uniform bound by a direct induction. This permits the Eneström–Kakeya estimate for all degrees; the explicit degree-\(m+1\) polynomial proves sharpness and the necessity of the parameter boundary. An executable exact-rational script replays finite orthogonality identities and inequalities, but the proof does not rely on the finite sample.

The closest searchable reports on the first-harmonic case and general harmonic orders concern limiting effective measures or finite-degree norm asymptotics. Neither asserts or logically entails the universal zero-confinement threshold without the additional all-degree coefficient-ratio argument. The 2021 circular-Jacobi Sobolev paper has a related zero theme but a different model; its abstract was inspected, the full text was inaccessible, and an older hidden equivalent statement remains a risk.

**Value (PASS).** The zero geometry of circle Sobolev polynomials is not controlled merely by classical OPUC zero theorems. The result gives a sharp **frequency-dependent** if-and-only-if stability boundary across every degree and all positive derivative strengths, with an optimal radius and a concrete first-escape mechanism. This is a natural structural parameter classification, not a numerical isolated case.

**Scope and risks.** The result is restricted to one Fourier harmonic in the derivative density; it does not characterize every zero above the threshold. The prior literature comparison does not constitute independent certification of publication priority.
