For circle Sobolev polynomials with derivative weight \(1+2\beta\cos(m\theta)\), every degree and every positive derivative strength have all zeros strictly inside the disk exactly when \(|\beta|\le1/(m+1)\). In this range the sharp uniform zero-radius supremum is \(((m+1)|\beta|)^{1/m}\); beyond the threshold, exterior zeros already appear at degree \(m+1\).
