## Originality — PASS, relative to checked evidence

The published Euclidean-distance-weight disk result already identifies the unperturbed critical exponent and negative scalar zeros, and that portion is excluded from the contribution. Marinescu–Xing establish analytic kernel dependence on the weight exponent for general domains, not the signed Fréchet response of the first zero-entry threshold to a positive radial perturbation. Perälä's construction and finiteness results for radial kernel zeros, Zeytuncu's perturbative examples, and the nearby two-power Jacobi phase result do not imply the exact response quartic, its three nonnegative perturbation bands, or the precise linear-tilt slope. ## Value — PASS

The first loss of the Lu Qi-Keng property is a natural stability boundary. The theorem distinguishes where positive radial mass is placed, showing that adding mass in three successive spatial bands alternately advances, delays, and advances zero entry. Exact algebraic separators and the noncompact linear-tilt response describe a substantive, measurable sensitivity rather than merely differentiating an elementary known expression. The global local-zero count makes the response dynamically relevant to the kernel's zero-free property. It does not treat arbitrary large perturbations or higher transitions.
