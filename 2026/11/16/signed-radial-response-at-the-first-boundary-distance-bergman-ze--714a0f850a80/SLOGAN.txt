At the first Lu Qi-Keng transition for the Euclidean-distance weighted disk Bergman kernel, positive radial mass added near the center, middle annulus, or boundary advances, delays, or advances the zero-entry threshold, respectively. The two sign changes have exact algebraic radii, while the global linear tilt \(1+\varepsilon r\) delays the threshold by \((40/\pi-12)\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon^2)\).
