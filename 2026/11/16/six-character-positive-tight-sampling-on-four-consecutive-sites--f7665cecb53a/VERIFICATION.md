# Mathematical verification

The analytic lower-bound certificate is the pair of forced elementary symmetric relations \(e_2=e_3=0\) and the impossibility of \(pc\in\{-2,2,10\}\) for \(p\ge7\). The upper certificate is a triangle enclosing zero and three antipodal pairs, which set the first three moments to zero.
