For second-degree complex-projective harmonics, geodesic-cap concentration has exactly six explicit Jacobi-sector spectral branches, and its smallest eigenvalue is always \((1-t)^{d+3}\). At fixed cap volume, the sharp squared condition number has two universal high-dimensional changes of maximizing sector, at \(s=2\) and \(s=2+\sqrt2\), with four sectors tied in the first limiting transition.
