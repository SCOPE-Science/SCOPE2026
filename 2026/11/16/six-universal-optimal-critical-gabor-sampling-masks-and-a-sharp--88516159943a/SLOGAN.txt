For every positive symmetric window \((a,b,b)\) with \(a>b\) on the three-cycle, exactly six permutation-graph triples maximize the lower Gabor frame bound, and their complete spectrum is explicit. For sampled periodized Gaussians these masks work at every width, while the sharp width-uniform lower bound \((3-\sqrt3)/2\) is attained at the self-Fourier width \(\lambda=3\).
