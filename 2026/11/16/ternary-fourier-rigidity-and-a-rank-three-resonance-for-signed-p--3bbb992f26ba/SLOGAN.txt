Ternary additive convolution by the signed-permutation matrices is singular in matrix size three, with exactly 3,816 vanishing characters in four zero-board orbits, but fully invertible again in sizes four and five. In every size \(n=3^a-1\) or \(n=3^a-2\) for \(a\ge1\), all Fourier multipliers are nonzero and have the same 3-adic valuation \(v_3(n!)\).
