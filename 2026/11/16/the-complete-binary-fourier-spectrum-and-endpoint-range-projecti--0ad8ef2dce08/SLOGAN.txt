The twelve order-three Latin squares, encoded by their binary triple-incidence vectors, have exactly four absolute Walsh multiplier levels: \(0,4,8,12\), with convolution rank \(77,594,624\) on \(\mathbb F_2^{27}\). Their convolution range has minimum \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm \(31/4\), even without translation-invariance restrictions, although its positive Hilbert spectral condition number is only \(3\).
