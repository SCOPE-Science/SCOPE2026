For three independent equally spaced Gaussian spectral lines, the Gaussian spectrogram has exactly two zeros per temporal period, and the number below the intrinsic balanced frequency is zero, one, or two with probabilities \((1-q)/2,q,(1-q)/2\), where \(q=\sqrt{\beta/(\beta+2)}\). This exact random-count law is smooth through the previously known sharp expected-intensity transition at \(\beta=4\), while its variance decreases strictly with the middle-line power.
