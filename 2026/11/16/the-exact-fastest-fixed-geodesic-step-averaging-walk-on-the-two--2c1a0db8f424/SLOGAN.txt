For uniform fixed-geodesic-step averaging on the unit two-sphere, the unique optimal absolute step length is one of the complementary angles \(\arccos((\sqrt6-1)/5)\) and \(\pi-\arccos((\sqrt6-1)/5)\), giving the exact minimum mean-zero \(L^2\) contraction \((2+3\sqrt6)/25\). Precisely the degree-two and degree-three spherical harmonics are worst-case, and no higher degree ties.
