# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS
Uniformly convergent character expansions allow coefficientwise use of the exact \(SU(2)\) Clebsch–Gordan rules. The scalar Bochner criterion translates positivity to nonnegative character coefficients, and a single convex-interpolation identity reduces infinitely many coefficients to \(L_0,L_1,L_\infty\). The sharp boundary classification is obtained by explicit elimination, with all signs valid throughout \(0<r<1\). Universality uses the nonzero coefficient criterion for every irreducible representation. The exact symbolic checker confirms thirty consecutive nontrivial coefficient formulas, all three vertices, and constant-kernel factorization. No finite test is used to replace the general derivation.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources
The motivating 2026 full-text paper provides the general universal-kernel criterion on compact groups and its positive \(|1-q\chi_f|^{-2}\) construction but not a signed quadratic perturbation's exact positive-type region or boundary universality strata. The preliminary 2025 paper is background. Closest indexed character-moment findings ask a different optimization question and do not imply these spectral inequalities. Equivalences to multiplier nonnegativity and representation-ring tensoring were actively compared. An old specialized result could still contain the same three-facet inequalities, so the novelty conclusion is provisional.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The family is natural: a standard faithful-representation Abel/Poisson covariance multiplied by the first two nontrivial central characters. A full positivity classification with just three sharp affine constraints, two different vertex rank mechanisms, and a high-frequency boundary that remains universal is a complete nontrivial structural description, not only a sample calculation. The exact limiting coefficient profile also distinguishes boundary universality from a rank-one endpoint. No generalization to other groups or polynomial degrees is asserted.
