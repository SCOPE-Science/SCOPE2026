For phase-only four-tone polynomials supported on two adjacent pairs \(\{0,1,m,m+1\}\), the full sixth-moment range is exactly \([106,430]\) at \(m=3\), but \([112,400]\) at every \(m\ge4\), even though the fourth-moment range is always \([20,36]\). The first cubic alias forces unique normalized extremizers at separation three, while every larger separation has continuous extremizer families; exact nonnegative deficit identities quantify both phenomena.
