Among binary lonesum matrices, the \(4\times4\) case is the first nontrivial square whose least surviving Walsh multiplier exceeds \(2\): its exact value is \(6\), attained at \(6,360\) frequencies in a complete 34-level spectrum. The resulting convolution has exactly half its Fourier modes active and sharp \(\ell^2\) pseudoinverse norm \(1/6\).
