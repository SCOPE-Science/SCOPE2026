# Review of coordinate-dominant quadratic-harmonic concentration

## Correctness
PASS. Gaussian radial separation reduces the surface quotient to homogeneous Gaussian moments; the evenness argument introduces exactly one-half for a single oriented cell. Signed-permutation symmetry yields four exhaustive mutually orthogonal sectors. Conditional laws of the largest independent chi-square coordinate give the eigenvalue formulas and multiplicities. Truncated gamma moment recurrences, a bounded-support variance inequality and an exponential covariance identity prove every strict spectral comparison; the maximum-order-statistic limit gives the sharp leading constant in the condition number. The companion numerical checker verifies moment-trace identities and independently checks the cone integrals. No finite test is used as a substitute for a general proof.

## Originality
PASS under available evidence, with residual priority risk. The 2023 masked-sphere reconstruction framework supplies the spectral motivation, but evaluates different masks and problems. The 2015 cubed-sphere paper computes discretized Laplacian eigenmodes, not a continuous restriction spectrum. Existing three-dimensional single-face quadratic values are already known and are excluded from the asserted new dimension range. Other indexed results for orthants and angular wedges are not stronger statements about coordinate-maximum Voronoi cells: those masks are defined by different inequalities and have dimensionally different asymptotics. Exact-equivalent unpublished or unindexed calculations remain possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The natural Voronoi partition formed by the signed coordinate axes has a fundamental stability question for quadratic spherical-harmonic observation. The complete exact all-dimensional spectral classification identifies the unique optimal zonal mode and the full weakest diagonal sector, while the sharp \(2(\log d)^2\) condition-number growth contrasts with the polynomial growth of different coordinate-sign masks. The result has implications for continuous quadratic-mode recovery on a standard multi-face sphere partition, without claiming a discrete algorithm or a higher-degree result.

## Limitations and closest literature
The eigenvalues are explicit special-function quadratures rather than elementary closed forms in arbitrary dimension. The first-public motivating source is the September 26, 2023 preprint by Hamann et al.; Cheong–Kang (2015) is related grid geometry, not an implying concentration theorem. Residual originality risk includes unindexed high-dimensional cubical-sector moment computations.
