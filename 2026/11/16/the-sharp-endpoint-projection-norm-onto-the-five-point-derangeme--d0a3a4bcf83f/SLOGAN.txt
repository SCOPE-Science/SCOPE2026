The Fourier range of convolution by the 44 five-point derangement matrices has minimum possible \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm exactly \(6999/512\), even when non-translation-invariant projections are allowed. The new norm is the absolute Walsh average of the **nonzero signed-permanent support**, not the previously known norm of projection onto the 44 derangement characters.
