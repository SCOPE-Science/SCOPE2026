The sixteen-tone four-dimensional Fourier cube has sharp ternary and quaternary unimodular fourth moments \(384\) and \(336\), with exactly two symmetry orbits of minimizers in either alphabet. Thus ternary phases are strictly worse than the known binary optimum, while four-phase coefficients recover that optimum and admit extra nonbinary minimizers; exact full censuses and strict next-value gaps are proved by integer-exhaustive certificates.
