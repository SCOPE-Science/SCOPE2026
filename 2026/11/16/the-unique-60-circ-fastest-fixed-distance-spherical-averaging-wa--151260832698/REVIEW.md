# Scientific review: fixed-distance averaging on the complex projective plane

## Correctness (PASS)
The domain is the unitary-invariant complex projective plane with Fubini–Study distance in \( [0,\pi/2]\), and the operator integrates against the stabilizer-invariant probability distribution on the distance sphere. Multiplicity-free projective harmonics yield the eigenvalues \(P_n^{(1,0)}(\cos(2\theta))/(n+1)\). Modes one and two prove a sharp universal lower bound: \( |(1+3x)/4|\le1/8\) confines \(x\) to \([-1/2,-1/6]\), on which \(\phi_2(x)\le-1/8\), strictly except at \(x=-1/2\). The Jacobi–Legendre identity and exact values for degrees three through nine, combined with \(4/[3(n+1)]<1/8\) for degrees ten onward, prove attainment and exclude all higher ties. The \((n+1)^3\) dimensions give an equality space of dimension 35. Selfadjoint spectral calculus proves the power statement. The associated standalone verifier executed 280 exact tests, including independently expanded hypergeometric multipliers; finite tests are not substituted for the all-degree bound.

The key analytical dependencies are the classical normalized zonal spherical-function formula and the classical uniform bound \(|P_n(x)|\le1\) on \([-1,1]\). These are recorded explicitly; no nonstandard lemma remains assumed without proof.

## Originality (PASS, qualified by priority uncertainty)
The nearest source on compact two-point homogeneous spaces, Brandolini et al. (arXiv:2406.03830), applies Jacobi expansions to *ball discrepancy*, not optimization of the surface-average operator over radii. Tóth (arXiv:2606.11438) supplies the broader stochastic framework and projective spherical multipliers but studies random-walk fidelity, Brownian diffusion and first-passage quantities; no matching exact minimax and equality-sector theorem was located in the inspected main text. The older complex-projective spherical-function literature (Grünbaum–Pacharoni–Tirao, arXiv:math/0108042) gives representation-theoretic machinery, not this extremal value. Bettiol–Lauret–Piccione (arXiv:2012.02349) study the Laplacian spectrum of geodesic spheres *as manifolds*, not global projective-surface convolution mixing. Searches for the normalized \(P_n^{(1,0)}/(n+1)\) spectral supremum and the exact \(1/8\) optimum identified related Jacobi extremum and graph mixing findings but no result that implies the stated constrained optimum. Prior exact fixed-angle optima on real spheres and rotation groups concern different spherical functions, so they do not subsume the present result. The special functions and harmonic decomposition themselves are not claimed as new.

Absolute novelty cannot be guaranteed by absence of search matches; obscure earlier geodesic random-walk and Jacobi-polynomial optimization work could affect priority. No independent external audit is claimed.

## Value (PASS)
The infinite-dimensional optimization determines an explicit, unique fixed travel distance for the natural isotropic Markov step on complex projective pure-state geometry. Its exact \(1/8\) worst-mode contraction, simultaneous negative extremizers in degrees one and two, and 35-dimensional equality space give a sharp, implementable mixing design and identify the precise obstruction to better contraction among single-distance steps. This is distinct from a mere low-degree calculation because every higher harmonic is controlled by a proved uniform bound. Other radial distributions, other dimensions and total-variation criteria are not covered.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
