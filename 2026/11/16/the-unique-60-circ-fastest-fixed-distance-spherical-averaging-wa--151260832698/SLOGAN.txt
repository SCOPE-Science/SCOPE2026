Among all isotropic random walks on \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) that move exactly one fixed geodesic distance per step, the unique fastest mean-zero \(L^2\) contraction occurs at \(60^\circ\), with sharp factor \(1/8\). Precisely the first two nonconstant projective-harmonic degrees attain the factor, both negatively, in a \(35\)-dimensional space; every higher degree contracts strictly faster.
