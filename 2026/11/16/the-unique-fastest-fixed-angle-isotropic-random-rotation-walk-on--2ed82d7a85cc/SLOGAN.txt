Among all walks on \(\mathrm{SO}(3)\) that rotate by one fixed angle around a uniformly random axis at each step, the unique fastest mean-zero \(L^2\) mixing angle is \(\arccos(-(1+\sqrt[3]{35})/6)\). Its exact optimal contraction factor is \((\sqrt[3]{35}-2)/9\), attained in precisely the spin-one and spin-three Fourier sectors with opposite signs.
