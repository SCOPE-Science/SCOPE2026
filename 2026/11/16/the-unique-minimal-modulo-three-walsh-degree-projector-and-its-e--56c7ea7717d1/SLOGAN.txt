For Boolean-cube Fourier projections onto all degrees in one residue class modulo three, exactly one residue has the smallest possible sharp endpoint norm, and the other two tie; the minimizing residue is \(2n\pmod3\). Although their Fourier ranks differ by at most one, the sharp norms have an exponentially growing gap and a limiting competitor-to-optimum ratio of \((1+\sqrt3)/2\).
