For the pure quadratic spherical harmonics observed on an **unbalanced** block cap \(\|u\|^2\ge t\), the complete four-sector spectrum has three equally spaced exact crossings at \((p-2)/d\), \(p/d\), and \((p+2)/d\). The sharp squared-energy condition ratio diverges as \(\frac{q(q+2)(q+4)}{2pd}(1-t)^{-2}\) when the block cap shrinks.
