The invariant \(O(n-1)\) decomposition is exhaustive, multiplicity-free, and has dimensions summing to \((n-1)(n+2)/2\). The numerical Haar tests independently reproduce finite full Gram spectra but are not substituted for proof.

It provides sharp constants and all eigenspaces for every interior rank, separates two distinct rank-asymmetric sequences of optimal modes, proves the exact half-rank perfect-isotropy phenomenon, and quantifies thin-cap conditioning with its exact coefficient. These global ordering and stability facts are more informative than an isolated beta expectation or a routine finite-dimensional computation.
