**Correctness — PASS.** The domain is the real rank-\(k\) Grassmannian for integer \(n\ge4\) and \(1\le k<n\), with Haar probability measure and the trace-free symmetric matrix-coefficient first harmonic. Irreducible \(O(n-1)\) splitting yields three and only three nontrivial sectors. The projector identity proves exact conditional first-row and lower-block moments. Normalizing by the full invariant quadratic covariance gives weights of Beta mean one. The three pairwise crossing identities were reconstructed by differentiating a Beta-tail linear polynomial, including all boundary ranks and the balanced-rank tie. A symbolic checker and finite direct Beta quadratures passed. These computations corroborate but do not substitute for the proof. No claim about higher harmonics is made.

Searches across aliases (Schubert halfspaces, leverage-score thresholds, truncated moment matrices, Grassmannian first harmonics) located no equivalent, though a classical multivariate Jacobi expression may have been missed. The relevant known Beta and invariant-covariance ingredients are acknowledged, not claimed novel.
