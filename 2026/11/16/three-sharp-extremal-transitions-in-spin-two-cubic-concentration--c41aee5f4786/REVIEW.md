# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The eigenvalue formulas follow from exact Wigner \(d^3_{m,-2}\) squares, full-sphere normalization, and cap integrals. The six exact polynomial gap factorizations reduce all continuum extremal claims to strict quadratic sign comparisons; the order of the four interior crossings proves that there are exactly two maximum-sector switches and one minimum-sector switch. A standard-library checker reconstructs the densities using exact rational arithmetic and compares their values with an independent factorial Wigner formula. The trace identity and third-order thin-cap expansion provide additional consistency checks. The finite tests are supplementary; the factor-sign proof establishes all cutoffs.

## Originality — PASS (priority provisional)
The 2021 spin-weight Slepian paper gives arbitrary-region compression matrices and a single-cap commuting operator, but not the pure-degree-three spin-two double-cap extremal mode-ordering theorem. The published scalar cubic antipodal-cap theorem has a different representation, different crossings, and cannot imply the spin-two theorem through global spin raising because localization and spin raising do not commute. The 2018 doctoral thesis remains a historical risk because only its metadata and abstract, not its complete text, could be examined. Published results establish the general methods and are not claimed as new.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The object is a basic seven-dimensional polarization harmonic sector of spin-two Slepian localization. Its best concentrating mode changes from axial to onefold to twofold angular order; the five-dimensional triple-branch resonance at a simple cap height is a nontrivial spectral degeneracy; the least-observable sector changes at a different algebraic height; and a sharp small-cap squared-condition coefficient quantifies reconstruction instability. These constitute a complete and natural sharp classification rather than a normalization identity or a bare tabulation.
