For any two real Fourier frequencies, the exact symmetric exterior-to-interior energy spectrum consists of explicit even and odd parity gains. The smallest possible worst-case gain over positive frequency gaps is precisely \(h\), attained at infinitely many gaps solving \(\sin((1+h)d)=(1+h)\sin d\); at those gaps **every** two-tone signal transfers exactly the same energy ratio, and the maximizing parity switches repeatedly between them.
