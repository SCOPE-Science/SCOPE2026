For cubic spherical harmonics, equal-area cap sampling centered at the twelve icosahedral vertices has exactly two positive, nonhemispherical Parseval apertures, \(c_\pm=\sqrt{(195\pm26\sqrt{15})/429}\). The entire seven-mode spectrum is explicit, and its worst-observed subspace changes dimensions \(4\to3\to4\) as the cap aperture varies.
