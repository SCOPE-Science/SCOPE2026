A product Poisson covariance on \(\mathbb T^2\), modulated by the two mixed first harmonics, is positive semidefinite in an exact two-facet parameter wedge; its nonvertex boundary is strictly positive definite without being universal. At the unique vertex, the Gram nullity of any finite sample set is precisely the cycle rank \(E-V_G+c_G\) of its bipartite coordinate-incidence graph.
