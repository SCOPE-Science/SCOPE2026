# Review of the quaternionic-projective cap spectrum

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim is restricted to the complexified first nonconstant quaternionic-projective eigenspace, in every quaternionic dimension \(d\ge3\), and to caps defined by \(|u_1|^2\ge t\), \(0<t<1\). Trace-free quaternionic-Hermitian forms span this eigenspace. The axial, cross, and tangential block dimensions sum to \(2d^2-d-1\). Conditioning on the beta-distributed coordinate length reduces their cap Gram ratios to three explicit scalar integrals. Exact factorization of all pairwise differences proves both transitions with strict inequalities away from the stated thresholds. Exact rational integration independently reproduces all formulas and the invariant-trace identity for 60 examples. Limits are proved by monotone inversion of the cap-volume formula, not by finite numerical extrapolation.

## Originality
**PASS, with historical priority provisional.** Jaming–Speckbacher, arXiv:2004.02474 (6 April 2020), gives a general concentration inequality on compact two-point homogeneous spaces; its inspected Section 3.1 and Theorem 4.4 include quaternionic projective spaces but not a full three-sector first-harmonic cap spectrum or exact minimum/maximum switches. Ahrens–Cowling–Martini–Müller, arXiv:1612.04802 (14 December 2016), concerns quaternionic spherical harmonics and sub-Laplacian multipliers rather than cap-restricted projective first eigenfunctions; only its accessible bibliographic summary was compared. Previously recorded complex-projective cap formulas apply to \(\mathbb{CP}^{d-1}\), whose beta shape and extremal ordering differ. Relevant searches also found spherical quadratic-cap transitions and geometric Neumann spectral inequalities, but these do not imply the present quaternionic-projective restriction result. No comprehensive theorem with the same two thresholds and all multiplicities was located. An unindexed older representation-theoretic calculation could nevertheless cover it.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** This is a natural complete spectral classification of cap observability on the first nontrivial harmonic space of an infinite family of rank-one symmetric manifolds. It identifies two different, exact finite-dimensional symmetry switches—one in the smallest and one in the largest singular sector—and a three-phase universal fixed-volume law. These distinguish cap recovery stability from the corresponding complex-projective example, where the minimum does not switch. The exact condition number and thin-cap coefficient are directly interpretable as best and worst reconstruction energies. The finding is not proposed as a theorem for arbitrary eigenlevel bands.

## Limitations
Historical priority is provisional; only one closest primary full paper was inspected extensively. The independent numerical checks are finite. The result excludes the projective line, larger spectral bands, nongeodesic masks, and discrete sampling. All calculations are for continuous invariant measure.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
