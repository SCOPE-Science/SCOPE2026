For random multiplication by a Haar-uniform element of one conjugacy class in \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\), the sharp mean-zero \(L^2\) contraction factor is \(1/4\), attained exactly at trace \(\pm1/2\). Only the two- and three-dimensional irreducible Fourier sectors are extremal, and the optimal \(k\)-step factor is exactly \(4^{-k}\).
