# Scientific review: short-root profile Fourier recovery

## Correctness — PASS
The type-\(C_n\) simple-root coordinate comparisons establish the alternating long-short spine and the two equivalences for every short root. For every occurring short-root profile they force all but exactly one long-root membership, leaving exactly two full-ideal extensions, including rank one. The established central-binomial root-ideal count and Legendre valuation imply odd profile support precisely at powers of two; all Walsh multipliers are then congruent to two modulo four. Independent direct-character and Walsh-Hadamard exhaustive calculations agree on the spectra in ranks two through five and on an explicit rank-six cancellation; the infinite statement does not depend on experimentation.

## Originality — PASS under bounded literature comparison
Panyushev (2003, arXiv:math/0304380) already treats short antichains, shifted Ferrers diagrams and the Catalan short-antichain enumeration, which are not claimed as new. Inspection of sections 1, 4 and 7 did not find a statement that *all* full root ideals project two-to-one to short-root incidence profiles or its sharp binary Fourier divisibility. Armstrong–Stump–Thomas (2011, arXiv:1101.1277) gives classical nonnesting/noncrossing correspondences rather than character-sum cancellation; Abe–Barakat–Cuntz–Hoge–Terao (2013, arXiv:1304.8033) concerns arrangement exponents. An implicit deduction from classical shifted-Ferrers constructions, or additional inaccessible older literature, remains a legitimate priority risk; no independent novelty certification is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Uniform two-to-one lifting is a structural statement about non-simply-laced root posets, not simply an unsigned enumeration. It forces an infinite, sharply 2-adic Fourier-recovery family, while the exact ranks two through six reveal genuine singular–invertible–singular recurrence. These distinguish signed short-root incidence recovery from familiar Catalan counts and provide precise kernel/rank benchmarks.

## Scientific limitations
The theorem supplies a sufficient power-of-two invertibility criterion, not an if-and-only-if statement in every rank. Full spectra stop at rank five; rank six has a direct zero witness. All spectra are for multiplicity-weighted short-root profiles, not full-root ideals. No independent review has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
