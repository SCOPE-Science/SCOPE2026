For every Steiner triple system with \(v\equiv5\) or \(7\pmod8\) points, the Walsh transform of its complementary rank-three matroid bases is \(4\pmod8\) at **every** frequency. Thus the associated Boolean-group convolution is invertible for every such design, even though the base family has even cardinality; its integral determinant has exact two-adic valuation \(2^{v+1}\).
