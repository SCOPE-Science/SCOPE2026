For every finite field and every dimension \(n\ge4\), each additive Fourier coefficient of the rank-at-most-one strictly upper-triangular matrix cone is an integer congruent to \(-q\pmod{q^2}\), so convolution by its indicator is invertible. The threshold is sharp: Fourier zeros occur in dimensions two and three, with exact counts \(q-1\) and \(q^2(q-1)\).
