For every fixed rational-Hartogs numerator gap \(k\ge4\), the interior diagonal Bergman-kernel zeros approach a universal constellation: one isolated point \(-2+\sqrt3\) and every nontrivial \(k\)-th root of unity. The boundary-bound zero displacements have sharp Euclidean order \(1/m\), while the isolated zero approaches its limit at order \(O_k(m^{-2})\).
