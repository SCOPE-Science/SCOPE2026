For the four geometric Fourier tones \(\{1,b,b^2,b^3\}\), every even moment below order \(2(b+1)\) is phase-blind, while the first resonant moment has exact sharp range \([B_{b+1}-2(b+1)^2-1,\ B_{b+1}+2(b+1)^2+4(b+1)]\). Its minimum is exactly one below the best binary-phase value and cannot be achieved using any finite root-of-unity phase alphabet.
