# Review of the regular-seven-tournament Walsh range result

## Correctness — PASS
The object is the binary orientation cube of the seven-vertex complete graph, with **all** orientations as the ambient group and only outdegree-three orientations in the convolution kernel. The six independent mod-two degree constraints yield an explicit affine 15-dimensional quotient and a precise formula for every ambient Walsh coefficient. The Python implementation exhausts the 32768 quotient orientations, computes the integer transform and its full histogram, and evaluates the support-indicator transform used by the Fourier projector. An independent C++ implementation instead examines all 2097152 ambient orientations and directly sums all 32768 quotient characters, agreeing coefficient-by-coefficient with the certificate. The rank follows by the 64-fold frequency lifting. Translation averaging proves minimum norm over **all** projections, not just invariant ones. Parseval and kernel-squared checks supply independent exact checksums. No infinite-dimensional extrapolation is made.

## Originality — PASS under bounded comparison
The closest mathematical overlap is the publicly accessible study *Integral tight Fourier sampling and tree-complement designs for regular tournaments*. Its complete result concerns the covariance of the constant and degree-one edge characters, integral circulation spans, maximal coordinate selections and spherical design strength. Its first-band statements cannot determine the zero/nonzero pattern of the entire 32768-character quotient Fourier transform, the exact convolution rank, or the Fourier-support projector kernel. Kühn–Osthus–Treglown study Hamilton-cycle decompositions, and Isaev studies asymptotic **unsigned** Eulerian-orientation counts; neither states the evaluated character-sign spectrum. Yu–Zhu give a general degree-constrained orientation-count duality but no identified complete spectral computation for this object. The known count of 2640, standard Walsh transforms, quotient techniques and translation averaging are not claimed as original. Available search did not locate an equivalent 553984-rank or 11155/256-projection result. The search cannot exclude unpublished or differently indexed older signed enumerators.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Order seven is the first regular-tournament order with three unlabelled isomorphism types; it is a natural next finite case after the unique type at order five. The exact multiplier support and \(\ell^1/\ell^\infty\) projection minimum constitute meaningful harmonic-analysis invariants of the full natural set, not an arbitrary slice. The endpoint constant \(11155/256\) quantifies strong unavoidable amplification even though the set has a compact affine description. Its precise value depends on correlations of the zero Fourier frequencies, and is not implied by merely counting regular tournaments or by the first-degree Gram spectrum.
