On a prime cycle, a two-tap translation–dilation family is tight on the mean-zero subspace exactly when its tap phase and dilation weights satisfy one of three antipodal balance patterns; full-space tightness requires the quadrature-phase symmetric pattern. For a real zero-mean difference wavelet, minimal tight mean-zero designs select one dilation from every antipodal pair, and erasing any complete scale packet has sharp, design-independent bounds.
