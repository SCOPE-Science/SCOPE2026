For matrices of any finite-field order and size \(n\ge m\ge3\), the Fourier transform of the rank-at-most-two ball never vanishes: its only negative shell has dual corank one, except that binary square matrices of dimension at least five acquire exactly one additional negative corank-two shell. This sign theorem yields an explicit, sharp \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-Fourier projection norm and invertibility of the ball-convolution operator.
