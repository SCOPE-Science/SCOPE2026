# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On S² the squared half-chord is uniform on [0,1], so X=(1+log U)/2 has mgf exp(t/2)/(1+t/2). Forest edges are independent by leaf integration. Funk–Hecke and the Legendre equation give eigenvalues −1/[2ℓ(ℓ+1)] with multiplicity 2ℓ+1; absolute integrability and Hilbert–Schmidt trace give cycle cumulant τ_k=Tr(T^k), nonzero signed (−1)^k. For order below girth all multisupport cumulants vanish; at girth each shortest cycle appears g! times, giving c_g τ_g in the log MGF. Direct high precision zeta summation checks k=3..5, not the general theorem.

## originality

PASS

A published general graph-girth cumulant method for linear spherical inner products and an exact four-cycle logarithmic chord special case are prior art. The logarithmic chord kernel has an infinite negative spherical-harmonic spectrum and all-k signed zeta trace coefficients; it yields a distinct evaluated girth law for arbitrary finite graphs and positive edge weights. No exact full-log-kernel theorem located; best-of-knowledge with explicit alias risk.
