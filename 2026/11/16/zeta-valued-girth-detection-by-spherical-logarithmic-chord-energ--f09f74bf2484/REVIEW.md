# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The logarithmic chord has centered exponential cumulants because the squared half-chord of two uniform spherical sites is uniform on \((0,1)\). Conditional leaf removal proves complete edge independence for any forest. The centered logarithmic potential has eigenvalues \(-1/[2\ell(\ell+1)]\), each of multiplicity \(2\ell+1\), by exact Funk–Hecke integration and Legendre's ODE. Hölder integrability and Hilbert–Schmidt truncation justify the trace of every cycle. Each nonzero mixed cycle cumulant has strict alternating sign. Multilinearity then isolates shortest cycles, proving the exact first nonzero scalar cumulant defect. The 91 exact/spectral assertions in `CHECK.py` pass, but all-graph validity rests on the proof.
