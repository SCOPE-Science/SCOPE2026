On the asymmetric 12-vertex Frucht graph, cyclic and Greenberg–Hastings three-color dynamics give different eventual least periods on exactly 1,496 of 531,441 common initial colorings: every mismatch is an eight-cycle versus a four-cycle. This exact joint census shows that the discrepancy previously observed on the highly symmetric Petersen graph persists when the network has no nontrivial spatial automorphisms.
