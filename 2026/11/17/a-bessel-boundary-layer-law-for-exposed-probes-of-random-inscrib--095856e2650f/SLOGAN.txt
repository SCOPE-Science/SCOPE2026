A normal probe placed at depth \(t/n^2\) below a smooth convex boundary is missed by the hull of \(n\) independently boundary-sampled points with limiting probability \(2\sqrt{x}K_1(2\sqrt{x})\), where \(x=2t f(z)^2/\kappa(z)\). Conditioned on being missed, its two neighboring arclength gaps converge in total variation to independent exponential variables conditioned on their product exceeding \(x\); distinct fixed probe sites become asymptotically independent, making the crossover a local curvature signature.
