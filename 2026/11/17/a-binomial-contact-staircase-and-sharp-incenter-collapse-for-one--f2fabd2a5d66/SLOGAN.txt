For an \(m\)-point equilateral cluster at mutual distance \(t\) and one remote point at distance one, the canonical real Lipschitz-free unit ball has an exact binomial staircase of Euclidean tangent facets, governed by the integer nearest \((m-1)/2+1/t\). Its incenter locus collapses sharply from affine dimension \(m-1\) to a unique point at \(t=2/m\).
