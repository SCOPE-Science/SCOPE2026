# Review of a boundary eigenarc for a Bergman completely positive map

## Correctness — PASS
The matrix-unit calculation yields the diagonal coefficient \(n/[2(n+1)]\) and positive backward coefficient. Iteration produces an exact telescoping product. The gamma-ratio law is used only off the terminating points, and the boundary square-summability test is strict: \(\cos	heta<1/2\). Every diagonal admits exactly one eigenvector up to scaling, and independent diagonals give infinite geometric multiplicity. The included finite recurrence tests corroborate, but are not a substitute for, this proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Panja (arXiv:2611.09731v1, equation (4.10), Theorem 4.5) characterizes fixed points among bounded operators and rules out nonzero compact fixed points. It does not describe eigenvalues different from one on the Hilbert–Schmidt ideal. The available searches and historical claim comparisons did not identify the precise disk-plus-partial-circle point spectrum. The scalar recurrence method is classical and not claimed as new; unindexed equivalent specializations remain a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The natural completely positive Toeplitz fixed-point map has an explicitly classified Hilbert–Schmidt point spectrum, including a proper boundary eigenarc and infinite eigenspace multiplicities. This resolves a motivated spectral question left undetermined by the fixed-point theorem; it is more informative than a standalone norm estimate or a basic negative eigenvalue test.
