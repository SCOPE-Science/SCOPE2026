In synchronous binary majority on \(C_n\square K_3\), only six fully flipping states survive when \(n\) is even, and none when it is odd. Widening each clique column to \(K_4\) produces exponentially many such pure two-cycle states: their exact 36-state transfer census grows as \(\lambda^n+O(2^n)\), with \(\lambda\approx3.16424793846\).
