For analytic monomial Toeplitz commutators on beta-weighted harmonic Bergman space, the full finite singular-value list yields a sharp high-degree Schatten-mass transition at \(p=2/(1-\alpha)\) when \(-1<\alpha<1\): bulk behavior, a logarithmic boundary, then paired summable edge layers. All asymptotic constants are explicit, including a zeta constant in the unweighted supercritical regime.
