In a polynomial cubic global center, uniform sampling of large periodic orbits in original physical time yields a shifted Cauchy velocity law, with sharp total-variation convergence \(C_{\alpha,\beta}/A^2+O(A^{-4})\) and the explicit symmetric constant \(\operatorname{Cl}_2(\pi/3)/(\pi\beta)\). The sign of the position is exactly fair and independent of velocity at every amplitude, and the rescaled distance from either extreme face converges jointly to a deterministic function of that Cauchy velocity.
