# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The root-sign bracket and four-cycle derivative bound are exact rational interval certificates, reproduced by `checker.py`. A uniform four-step contraction constructs the localized invariant law and guarantees smooth response for bounded parametric noise. Fresh-noise independence yields the exact variance and expectation recurrences; the sign certificate follows from their periodic solutions. The proof claims neither global stationary uniqueness nor parameter-wide validity.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Cruz–Wei–Hardin–Radunskaya (arXiv:2206.03849, §§3.4, 4 and Appendix A) leave the period-four mean sign at the level of numerical evidence and a conjecture; their rigorous theorem is for period two. The current exact \(\lambda=7/2\) coefficient and local sign are not implied by that theorem. Ayers–Radunskaya (arXiv:2403.13116, §§1,3) cover a chaotic rather than four-cycle parameter range. Sato–Doan–Lamb–Rasmussen (arXiv:1811.03994) concern additive noise near a period-three map. Semantically related indexed findings about logistic directional signs at parameter four, stochastic rounding, and additive shock support do not imply this second-order parametric-noise coefficient. Incompletely indexed priority literature remains a real risk; this assessment is not independent certification of novelty.

## Value — PASS

The originating paper's period-four direction was formulated as an open flip-flop conjecture, with evidence but no analytic sign. This theorem supplies a strict, reproducible local verification and a numerical response strength with a rigorous rational enclosure; the coefficient is measured on a natural attracting cycle at a rational logistic parameter. It is a useful precise invariant for stochastic population forecasting and a principled starting point for testing whether the sign persists across the full period-four window, without falsely claiming that larger result.

## Boundaries

The result is limited to sufficiently small uniform parameter noise, and stationary uniqueness is only on the four-phase trapping set. No claim is made about all initial populations or all period-four parameters. General perturbation principles are background tools, not an independent novelty claim.
