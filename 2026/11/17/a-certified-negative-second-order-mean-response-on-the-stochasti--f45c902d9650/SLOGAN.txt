For uniformly fluctuating growth rates around \(\lambda=7/2\), the attracting logistic four-cycle has a rigorous negative stationary-mean response \(B\varepsilon^2+o(\varepsilon^2)\), with \(-0.548<B<-0.546\). This certifies a nontrivial local instance of the published period-four flip-flop conjecture without asserting the conjecture across the entire parameter window.
