For a positive-payoff two-prey predator-dependent replicator flow, the coexistence state undergoes a provably supercritical Hopf bifurcation at \(1.6617073473533870<K_*<1.6617073473536950\). A fully symbolic and interval-validated calculation proves a negative first Lyapunov coefficient and hence locally attracting positive coexistence cycles immediately above the threshold.
