# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the finite projective plane hypotheses, the labelled two-row configuration space, and the exact subtraction update. For plane order \(s\equiv2\pmod3\), it decomposes the adjacency operator into a two-dimensional zero component and a complement on which its square is minus the identity. For \(s\equiv1\pmod3\), it derives one spatial size-two Jordan block at each of eigenvalues plus and minus one, and proves that each lifts to a size-four temporal block. The stated fixed-space dimensions, global orders, period support, and Möbius inversion follow for every finite field \(\mathbb F_{3^f}\). Two independent finite checks for plane orders 2, 4, and 5 reproduced the predicted values. Finite tests are corroboration, not universal proof.

## Originality
PASS, with historical-priority qualification. A published characteristic-two projective-plane incidence-wave classification concerns the same graphs, but its addition rule and binary incidence-rank-dependent six/twelve clock do not imply this theorem over characteristic three. A published characteristic-three complete-bipartite result has a rank-two spatial adjacency and maximal periods eight or twelve; it cannot imply this projective-plane 8/18 bifurcation. Vlasov and Macfarlane established prior general constructions, which are credited and not claimed as new. The projective-plane incidence identity is classical. Search and source comparison were finite; unidentified earlier work remains a risk. The full 2004 Macfarlane article was not inspected, although its dated abstract describes one-dimensional characteristic-two models.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Incidence graphs of finite projective planes are canonical highly structured networks; uniform classification without a Desarguesian hypothesis is natural. The sharp discrepancy between period eight with no nonzero fixed states and period eighteen with a large fixed subspace is caused by order-modulo-three Jordan resonance. It is not a restatement of the characteristic-two rank-dependent result. Exact formulas for all primitive orbit populations provide a complete recurrent classification for an infinite conditional class of geometries and extension fields.

## Scientific limits
The theorem assumes plane order not divisible by three, the specific reversible second-order neighbor-sum recurrence, and finite fields of characteristic three. It does not address graph isomorphism, other characteristics, or unknown non-Desarguesian existence questions. Literature comparison does not certify absolute historical priority, and independent external review remains outstanding.
