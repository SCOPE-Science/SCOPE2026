On the point-line incidence graph of every finite projective plane whose order is not divisible by three, reversible characteristic-three neighbor-sum waves have exact global temporal period \(8\) when the order is \(2\pmod3\), but \(18\) when it is \(1\pmod3\). A universal modular Jordan decomposition gives the complete primitive-period populations for all extension fields, without assuming the projective plane is Desarguesian.
