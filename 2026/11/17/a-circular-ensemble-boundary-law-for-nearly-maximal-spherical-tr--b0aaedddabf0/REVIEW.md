The exact rotationally normalized spherical sampling density and the great-circle maximal-perimeter locus are explicit. The nondegenerate quadratic deficit in signed latitude, a global Gram determinant bound, and angular/edge localization provide the analytic limiting measure. It gives angular and signed-transverse information irrecoverable from the classical scalar perimeter density alone, together with an asymptotically efficient rare-event sampling description.
