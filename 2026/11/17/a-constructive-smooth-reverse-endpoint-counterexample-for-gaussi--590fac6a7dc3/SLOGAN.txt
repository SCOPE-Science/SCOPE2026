A deterministic series of translated, real Gaussian-antiholomorphic monomial packets gives a smooth admissible Fock Toeplitz symbol with critical heat transform in \(C_0(\mathbb C)\), yet \(T_g\) has no bounded extension. An explicit computable center-selection rule and a \(O(J^{-1})\) uniform heat-tail bound replace a previously nonconstructive reverse-endpoint existence result.
