A single overweight atom whose total-variation defect is only \(O(m^{-1})\) creates a nonzero asymptotic contraction factor for one complete Fourier Kaczmarz cycle on an \(m\)-point regular polygon. After canonical coefficient and physical-Hilbert embeddings, the cycles converge in Hilbert–Schmidt norm to a rank-one-corrected Volterra operator with explicit Lambert-\(W\) nonzero eigenvalues and convergence of every fixed singular value.
