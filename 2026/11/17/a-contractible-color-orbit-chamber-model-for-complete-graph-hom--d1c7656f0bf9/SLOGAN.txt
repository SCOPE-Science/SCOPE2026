For every \(2\le m\le n\), the ordinary quotient \(|\operatorname{Hom}(K_m,K_n)|/S_n\) by permutations of the target colors has an explicit sorted-probability-vector chamber model and is strongly deformation retractable to a point. Its chambers are products of nested simplices; when \(n=m+1\), the quotient is an \(m\)-armed star rather than a ball for \(m\ge3\).
