For two area-uniform points in a flat Möbius band with geodesic boundary, the probability that their unique shortest geodesic meets the central core is \(\tfrac12-\tfrac{w^2}{8}\) for width-to-core-length ratio \(w\le1\), and \((4\log w+3)/(8w^2)\) for \(w\ge1\). This probability strictly determines the band's similarity class and has a sharp \(C^2\)-but-not-\(C^3\) transition at \(w=1\).
