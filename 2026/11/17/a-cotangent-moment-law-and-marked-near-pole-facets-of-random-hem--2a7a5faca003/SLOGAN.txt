For \(n\ge3\) random vertices on a northern hemisphere with uniform azimuths, the chance that the north pole lies inside their geodesic hull but within \(\varepsilon\) of its boundary is asymptotic to \(n(n-1)\,\mathbb E[\cot R]\,\varepsilon/(2^{n-2}\pi)\). Conditioned on this event, the nearest hull edge has an explicit cotangent-size-biased endpoint law, a uniform normalized pole clearance, and independent semicircle marks.
