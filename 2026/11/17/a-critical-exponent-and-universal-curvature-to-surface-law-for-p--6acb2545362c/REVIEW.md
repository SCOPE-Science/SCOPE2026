# Mathematical review

## Correctness

PASS. The triangular edge matrix yields the barycentric gradients exactly. The gradient Gram matrix is tridiagonal in facet-chain order, and the complementary-minor identity converts it to exact ridge measures. Euclidean exterior angles give the degree-two Steiner coefficient with the necessary factor of one half. Divergence/convergence of the gradient-length series yields the three sharp exponent regimes; the adjacent corrections are negligible exactly when the total length diverges. The finite checker reproduces direct ridge Gram determinants and the known three-dimensional equal-leg result. Risk: no independently commissioned proof audit.

## Originality

PASS, with residual historical-priority risk. Olsen (2018) defines and surveys the relevant simplex but treats principally Ehrhart and algebraic invariants; Brändén–Solus (2019) likewise investigate algebraic geometry. Gao–Vitale (2000) already derive intrinsic volumes of the equal-leg orthoscheme, so that particular case is attributed and excluded as a novelty claim. The closest identified power-lecture-hall spectral result is about vertex covariance, not Euclidean Steiner coefficients. The full power-exponent curvature transition and universal curvature-to-surface limit were not found as covered statements. Generic orthoscheme intrinsic-volume literature may supply equivalent building blocks; a universal search cannot exclude unindexed precedent.

## Value

PASS. A natural class from lecture-hall geometry has a precise three-regime boundary-curvature scaling law and a universal normalized limit that persists across a continuous range of shapes. This contrasts a bulk of perpendicular facet pairs with a sparse set of non-right pairs; it determines how the first two geometric boundary coefficients interact, rather than merely counting elementary lattice features. The exact formula also permits linear-size evaluation for arbitrary positive weight profiles.
