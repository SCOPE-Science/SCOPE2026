For power lecture-hall simplices, the exact volume-normalized second Steiner intrinsic volume changes scale at exponent \(p=1\): from \(n^{4-2p}\) through \(n^2(\log n)^2\) to \(n^2\). Throughout \(0\le p\le1\), its ratio to the square of the surface-to-volume ratio tends to the universal constant \(1/8\), and an explicit weighted-ridge formula gives the full finite-dimensional invariant.
