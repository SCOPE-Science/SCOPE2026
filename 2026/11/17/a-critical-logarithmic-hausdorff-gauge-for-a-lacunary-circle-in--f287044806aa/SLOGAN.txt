A dyadic exponential circle in complex \(\ell_p\), \(0<p<1\), has an exact critical logarithmic Hausdorff-gauge measure \(2\pi/(p\log2)^{1/p}\), metric dimension \(1/p\), and a sharp diameter-cover entropy with its leading constant. All coordinates are analytic, yet the curve is nowhere quasi-norm differentiable and its increments lose precisely a logarithmic factor relative to Lipschitz regularity.
