For a uniform point in the perpendicular join of two Euclidean cubes, the normalized squared radius has an ordinary Gaussian limit for every fixed positive block proportion. But its conditional mean given the two cube-block maxima undergoes a sharp \(\sqrt N\)-imbalance crossover, yielding a centered noncentral-chi-square-minus-exponential limit at the faster \(N\) scale; the full squared radius remains Gaussian.
