For three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on every gear graph \(G_n\), the number of immediately primitive three-periodic states is \(3(a_n+\omega_n-(-1)^n-b_n)\), where \(a_n\) obeys a cubic recurrence and \(b_n\) a Pell-type recurrence. These three-step waves grow like \(3\lambda^n\) with \(\lambda=2.8311772072\ldots\), yet occupy an asymptotically vanishing fraction \((\lambda/9)^n\) of initial states.
