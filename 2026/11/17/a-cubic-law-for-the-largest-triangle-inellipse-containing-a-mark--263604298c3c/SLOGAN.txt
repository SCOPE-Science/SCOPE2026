For every triangle and marked interior point, the unique largest three-side-tangent inellipse containing that point is determined by a single cubic with exactly one positive root; it is the Steiner inellipse exactly when the point lies inside the Steiner inellipse. Along a vertex median the normalized optimal area has an explicit constant-to-\(t\sqrt{1-t}\) transition at barycentric coordinate \(t=2/3\), continuously differentiable but not twice differentiable.
