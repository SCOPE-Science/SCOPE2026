For three uniformly sampled vertices of a spherical cap, the pole lies in their spherical convex hull with probability exactly \(1/4\) up to the hemisphere threshold. Just beyond it, containment loses \((4/\pi^2)h^3\log(1/h)\) in probability, and the rare failure configurations divide \(3:1\) between two and one southern vertices with a uniform logarithmic angular-gap scale.
