Three independent isotropic great circles have a pole-containing cell of geodesic perimeter at most \(\varepsilon\) with probability \(K\varepsilon^3+o(\varepsilon^3)\), where \(K=0.00030969893957\ldots\) is an explicit positive angle-simplex integral. Conditioned on this rare event, the full marked local geometry converges in total variation: the angles follow a precise tilted law while rescaled perimeter and its three side-offset contributions factor into independent \(\operatorname{Beta}(3,1)\) and \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,1,1)\) variables.
