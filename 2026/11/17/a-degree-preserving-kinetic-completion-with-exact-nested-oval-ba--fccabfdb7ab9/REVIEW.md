# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The full-parameter proof first factors the source's defining quartics as \(h_{\delta}=xy(L-\delta)\), then cancels the completion term's derivative of \((xy)^N\) to obtain the exact Lyapunov identity \(\dot L=-(xy)^N P(L)D\). The displayed formula for \(D\) is strictly positive away from the sole center. Logarithmic-coordinate strict convexity proves that the positive level curves are simple compact ovals and that all bounded sublevels are compact. Monotonicity and uniqueness give forward completeness and the complete basin/periodic-orbit classification, including the outer unbounded annulus. On each oval, the vector field is nonzero and tangent; its speed yields the period quadrature. The cofactor difference from the originating field is the exact derivative of \(N\log(xy)\), so the transverse multiplier is preserved. Green's theorem supplies a positive weighted-area formula for that multiplier. The boundary coordinate polynomials have nonnegative coefficients, providing a constructive finite mass-action realization. These are analytic arguments for every \(N\ge1\), not extrapolations from computations. The accompanying symbolic checker passed for \(N=1,2,3\); finite checking alone is not the proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected coverage

Craciun and Erban, arXiv:2406.05057, Sections 4.1 and 5, introduce the nested algebraic ovals, the originating chemical vector field, and a local cycle stability criterion. Their Theorem 1 asserts *at least* \(N\) cycles of the originating field. Those are explicitly prior results. The new statement is an **explicit degree-preserving modified field** that agrees exactly on every prescribed original-time trajectory and its Floquet multiplier but admits an exact all-positive-quadrant cycle census and basins by a strict monotone coordinate. The source construction does not supply this particular completion identity. Gasull and Giacomini, arXiv:2210.15803, study general invariant algebraic curves and related cycle bounds, not this modified field or the claimed preservation of each quartic cycle's time and multiplier. Nearby literature on prescribed concentric circular cycles does not directly imply the full statement for these quartic levels with their physical clocks. A published, broader theorem covering the same completion could exist; searches are not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact distinction between *at least* \(N\) invariant algebraic cycles and *exactly* \(N\) cycles in the entire positive quadrant is a structural issue for chemical polynomial dynamics. Preserving periods and transverse multipliers while obtaining a complete basin partition is more informative than a local stability check or arbitrary time reparametrization. The nontrivial weighted-area formula shows the logarithmic multiplier's magnitude is independent of the placements of all the other ovals. The construction is useful for chemical oscillators with controlled multiple attractors and explicitly separable basins, without claiming reaction-network optimality.
