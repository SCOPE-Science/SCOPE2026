A degree-preserving mass-action modification retains every original-time period and transverse Floquet multiplier of an arbitrary nest of quartic algebraic cycles, but gives a complete global phase portrait with exactly those cycles. The explicit Lyapunov coordinate \(L=(x+1+x^{-1})(y+1+y^{-1})-9\) determines all positive basins, and each cycle's logarithmic multiplier has a positive weighted-area formula independent in magnitude of the other cycle levels.
