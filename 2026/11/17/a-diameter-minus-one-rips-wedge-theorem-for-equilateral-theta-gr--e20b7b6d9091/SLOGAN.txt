For any \(m\ge2\) equal-length \(L\)-edge paths between common poles, the graph-vertex Vietoris–Rips complex at radius \(L-1\) is a wedge of exactly \((m-1)^{\lfloor L/2\rfloor}\) spheres of dimension \(L-1\). Its full natural integral homology module is a tensor power of the arm-augmentation lattice, with an explicit pole-reversal sign that alternates by length modulo four.
