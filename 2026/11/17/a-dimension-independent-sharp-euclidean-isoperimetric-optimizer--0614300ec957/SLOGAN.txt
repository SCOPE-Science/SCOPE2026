Across every dimension \(s\ge3\), the ordinary Euclidean surface area of a uniform cyclic chainlink polytope, after normalization to a fixed volume, is uniquely minimized at the same link-to-side ratio \(1-1/\sqrt2\). The sharp isoperimetric quotient is \(2s(\alpha_+^s+\alpha_-^s)^{1/s}\), and its ratio to an equal-volume cube tends to \(0.955089860562\ldots\).
