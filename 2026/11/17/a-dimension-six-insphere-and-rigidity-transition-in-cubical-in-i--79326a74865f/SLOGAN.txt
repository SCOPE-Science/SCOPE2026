For the canonical in-in zonotope of the binary cube graph \(Q_d\), the sharp Euclidean inradius is \(\min\{2^{d/2},d+1\}\), switching at dimension six from two contacting facets and a \((2^d-1)\)-dimensional family of centers to \(2^{d+1}\) contacting facets and a unique center. Its circumsphere has radius \((d+1)2^{d/2}\) and exactly two antipodal contacts, the unique diameter pair.
