For the weighted real \(p\)-harmonic extension from the leaves of any star with at least three leaves, the sharp two-input graph-Lipschitz distortion is exactly \(2\) for every \(p\ne2\), but drops to \(2(1-\min_i w_i/\sum_jw_j)\) at \(p=2\). The non-Hilbert sharp constant is never attained by distinct boundary data: it arises only as an infinitesimal limiting amplification.
