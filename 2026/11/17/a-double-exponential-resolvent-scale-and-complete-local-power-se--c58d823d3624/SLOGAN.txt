The compact backward shift with weights \(1/\log(j+e)\) realizes every Douglas--Yang local resolvent exponent in \([0,1]\), even though its weights belong to no finite \(\ell^q\) class. Its resolvent has sharp double-exponential logarithmic growth \(\log\|R(\lambda)\|\sim e^{1/|\lambda|}|\lambda|\), and the explicit vector tail \(\exp[-aj/\log(j+e)]\) has local exponent \(e^{-a}\).
