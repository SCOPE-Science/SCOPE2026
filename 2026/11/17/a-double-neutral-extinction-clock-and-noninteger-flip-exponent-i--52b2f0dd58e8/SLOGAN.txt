At the simultaneous prey-flip and predator-invasion neutrality of the positive Ricker–Kolmogorov predator–prey map, a genuine open wedge of trajectories has predator extinction \(y_n\sim1/(\kappa n)\) while its corrected alternating prey signal has a continuously tunable exponent \(p\in(1,2)\): \((-1)^n n^p(x_n-2+c y_n)\to C\). Both signs of the nonzero limiting amplitude occur on open sets; the result is local, not a global basin classification.
