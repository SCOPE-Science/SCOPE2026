# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Coarea reduces the operator norm to all nontrivial binary indicators. The 22 small-dimension cuts are exhaustively evaluated with exact rational arithmetic, giving the exact values in dimensions two through four. The signed third-coordinate witness has an explicit pairwise formula; its strict excess follows from an exact positive base and nonnegative second differences for every larger integer dimension. The column-mass contraction lemma for nonnegative row-stochastic matrices is proved directly from a pair inequality and coarea. It gives an upper bound with the same logarithmic leading coefficient as the witness. The proof does not infer an infinite statement from a finite test.

## Originality — PASS (with residual priority risk)
Direct comparisons distinguish the residual \(I-C_n\) and the complete-pair Gini quotient from (a) the already reported Gini norm of \(C_n\) itself, (b) Cesàro powers in adjacent path variation, (c) the finite maximum-norm problem for \((I-C_n)^2\), and (d) Sinnamon's classical \(\ell^p\)-norms of \(C-I\). These results do not imply the dimensional cutoff, the specific residual witness, or the Gini logarithmic growth. The arXiv Hardy-space paper was inspected in relevant theorems and sections; Sinnamon's eight-page primary paper was inspected as accessible full text. The 2009 online article's abstract was available, not its whole paper; the 1965 foundational paper was not accessed. Unindexed historical equivalents remain a risk.

## Value — PASS
The exact first amplifying dimension is a natural structural cutoff for a canonical residual smoothing operator. The new asymptotic separates complete-pair dispersion sensitivity (which diverges as \(\log n\)) from standard bounded \(\ell^\infty\) residual norms, in a precisely stated finite-dimensional setting. The result is not merely a numerical gain or a change of notation; both cutoff and sharp leading asymptotic follow from general estimates and a genuinely signed witness.

## Limitations
The exact full coefficient beyond four sites remains unknown here; an old unavailable source might cover the same results. The research has not undergone an independent review.
