Synchronous binary majority on any tournament has no primitive temporal two-cycle, but the Paley tournaments display a striking reappearance of longer oscillations: all states on 11 vertices reach consensus, whereas the 19-vertex tournament has exactly 342 primitive four-cycles and 18 primitive nineteen-cycles. On the latter graph all oscillators form just three affine-complement-time classes and every initial state reaches its eventual orbit within seven updates.
