For every canonical Loday associahedron with at least three internal tree nodes, the vertices nearest its common vertex barycenter are exactly the \(2^{n-1}\) comb trees, and precisely \(2^{n-3}\) vertices occupy the next shell, at squared Euclidean distance exactly four larger. Every vertex's squared radial excess is an explicit nonnegative, four-divisible sum of local two-sided branching penalties.
