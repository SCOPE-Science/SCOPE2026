# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The spanning arc complex has exactly ten arc vertices. Exact reachability enumeration identifies all faces, and a deterministic matching on all nonempty simplices has one critical vertex and one critical five-simplex. The verifier establishes acyclicity by checking every incidence in the directed Hasse graph, so Forman's theorem proves the integral homotopy sphere. A nonidentity rotation has two complete five-edge Hamilton-cycle orbits; no orbit may appear in a face, proving geometric freeness. Universal covering and rational transfer then give the quotient's claimed fundamental group, first integral homology, and rational groups. The proof does not confuse mod-two ranks with a homotopy classification.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources
The fully inspected relevant sections of Hultman's directed-subgraph paper require 2-density for the applicable general sphere-wedge theorem; no directed two-cycle exists in this regular tournament. Comparisons against earlier directed-graph-complex literature and the indexed records for similarly named tournament multipath and neighborhood complexes did not establish implication of this precise five-tournament NSC theorem. A full copy of Björner–Welker was not obtained, so undiscovered specialization remains a priority risk; search absence is not treated as uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is the first regular tournament after the directed triangle and a canonical small instance outside Hultman's 2-dense hypothesis. The explicit homotopy-sphere model and free fivefold rotational action provide a structurally motivated example whose quotient has genuine order-five integral first-homology torsion, despite rational sphere homology. It therefore tests a natural boundary of the existing theory rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limitations
Full integral homology and homeomorphism type of the quotient, as well as larger tournament families, remain unsettled here. An inaccessible plausible older source leaves a bounded originality risk.
