# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For n≥3, retain all forward rotations and nonempty reverse-start set T. The cellular objects are ordered injective subwords, so opposite directed 2-words remain distinct cells. Forward rotations P_n are S1; one reversed rotation is a simplex while ≥2 reverse rotations form S1. The common subcomplex is a connected graph: any 3-word has opposing cyclic orientations and cannot occur in both; it has binom(n,2)+sum_{i<j}g_i g_j directed edges when |T|≥2 (and binom(n,2) when |T|=1). Thus graph H1 rank E−n+1. Integral winding cochains on forward/reverse subcomplexes are primitive, with reversed triangle (1,0) and a digon crossing distinct cyclic gaps (1,1), giving Z²-surjectivity (Z for singleton T). Van Kampen kills all fundamental group generators and Mayer–Vietoris plus Hurewicz/Whitehead gives exactly a wedge of binom(n−1,2)−1 or binom(n−1,2)+sumgg−2 two-spheres, with no torsion. Independently reconstructed ordered faces and F2 cellular boundary ranks for selected patterns n=4,5,6 including n=6 reverse starts at distances 1 versus 3: sphere counts 13 versus 17. Finite checks are corroboration, not an all-n proof.

## originality

PASS

The published dihedral full reverse-family theorem is exactly the t=n endpoint, already known and explicitly excluded from novelty. The genuine result concerns every proper nonempty T, with dependence on gap products, plus t=1. General free pi1 theorem (Govc) and two-generator permutation results do not yield this H2 rank or integral homotopy type.

PASS

A natural partial-symmetry interpolation of cyclic/dihedral injective-word complexes has a sharp qualitative S1→S2-wedge transition and quantitative dependence on arrangement rather than just number of reverse rotations; such topology is not a routine reformulation of the full dihedral endpoint.
