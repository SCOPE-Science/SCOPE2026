# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof constructs an explicit all-parameter Morse reduction of the forward cyclic complex, then an elementary two-simplex intersection argument for any reverse-subset family. The forward/reverse common subcomplex is precisely a connected graph, with every double-oriented pair counted by crossing cyclic start-blocks. The reversed-triangle boundary and a cross-block digon give primitive winding vectors, allowing van Kampen and integral Mayer–Vietoris to determine the simply connected sphere wedge. `VERIFY_PARTIAL.py` and `VERIFY.py` independently test 243 finite subsets and ten finite optimal Morse matchings. Tests alone do not imply the general statement.

## Originality — PASS
The accessible prior full two-orientation classification covers the endpoint where all reversals are present; it is explicitly credited. Its statement does not determine proper-subset ranks or their cyclic-gap dependence. Govc (2026, arXiv:2611.11362) proves freeness of fundamental groups, not these ranks. Chachólski–Levi–Meshulam (2019, arXiv:1908.03394) establish the two-permutation method but not the multi-generator formula. Targeted database and publication searches found no decisive dominating statement. Risks include the incomplete access to arXiv:2610.07303 and possible unindexed equivalent work.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The new result determines the exact integral homotopy response to *every proper nonempty subset* of reverse cyclic generators and explains a position-sensitive sphere-birth phenomenon. At six letters, two distinct spacings of two selected reversed starts produce 13 versus 17 two-spheres. This is a natural structural phase diagram and supplies a motivated invariant rather than an arbitrary finite calculation.
