# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the Coxeter path P_n (n≥2), the Boolean complex has one (|S|−1)-cell for each acyclic orientation of induced edges on support S, a total of 2^{e(S)} choices, with signed deletion boundary and augmented empty cell. Reflection on k supported vertices permutes increasing orientation with sign (−1)^{floor(k/2)}; in even paths an invariant orientation cannot contain the centrally reversed edge. Define half-path signed sums a_k=Σ_T (−1)^{|T|}2^{e(T)}, b_k restricted to k∈T; the final-vertex recurrence gives a_k=(−1)^k F_{k−1} and b_k=(−1)^k F_k for k≥1, separately a0=1. Cellular Lefschetz then gives t_{2m+1}=(−1)^m F_m, t2=1, t_{2m}=(−1)^{m−1}F_{m−2} for m≥2, on sole reduced H_{n−1}. Together with established homology rank F_{n−1}, the ±1 eigenspaces have dimensions (F_{n−1}±t_n)/2. Equivariant subdivision and rational finite-group transfer yield the ordinary geometric quotient rational Betti formula, not any claim about integral quotient torsion. Independently enumerated reflection-stable support/orientation classes and augmented Lefschetz traces n=2..12, yielding exactly 1,−1,0,1,1,−2,−1,3,2,−5,−3.

## originality

PASS

The established path Boolean-complex homotopy theorem gives total Fibonacci rank but not the diagram-reflection trace; this is a representation character computation. A separate published complete dihedral character for *cycles*, not paths, addresses different commutation classes and cannot be specialized to remove one edge without changing the complex. The checked primary homology source gives no explicit half-index path reversal trace.

## value

PASS

Diagram reversal is a canonical symmetry of every type-A Coxeter path; splitting its top homology into eigenspaces and determining rational orbit quotient ranks is a natural additional invariant beyond the known Fibonacci sphere multiplicity. Infinite half-index formula is nontrivial, not a mere parameter substitution.
