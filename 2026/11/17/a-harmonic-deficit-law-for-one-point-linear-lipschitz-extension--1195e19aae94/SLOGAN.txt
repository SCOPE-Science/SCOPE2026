A union of inequivalent equilateral clusters admits a unique sharp barycentric functional for simultaneous real-linear Lipschitz extension to one uniformly distant point: cluster weights are proportional to the reciprocals of \(1-t_j(1-1/m_j)\). The minimum extension norm is \(\max\{1,(1-[\sum_j(1-t_j(1-1/m_j))^{-1}]^{-1})/R\}\), with a sharp transition from a unique optimizer to a continuum of optimizers at the contractivity threshold.
