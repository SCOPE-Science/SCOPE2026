# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof classifies every forbidden triangle in the join, writes the complex as the union of a full nonempty simplex, the central one-skeleton, and cones on \(\operatorname{Ind}(H)\), and makes two contractible-cofibration quotients. These steps give the exact homotopy type without any infinite assumption inferred from finite tests. The boundary cases with disconnected or contractible independence complexes are covered by using unreduced suspension. Independent finite reconstruction over two prime fields passed in all reported cases.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The checked full text of Ghosh–Selvaraja (2026), arXiv:2602.09623v1, supplies the same clique-free-complex definition and studies shellability and distinct local clique attachments, but no such homotopy transfer for graph joins. Previously known complete split-graph results occur only when \(H\) has no edges, and cannot cover \(H=C_6\) with its nontrivial suspended homology. A public record involving graph-join suspensions concerns Lovász neighborhood complexes instead of this triangle-avoidance complex; that record was seen by indexed summary only, not fully inspected. Equivalence via unindexed general polyhedral-union theory is a live residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a general homotopy transfer formula for arbitrary triangle-free graph inputs; it computes entire integral homotopy types and torsion behavior in terms of the original independence complexes. The exact binomial first-homology contribution and arbitrary suspensions supply a structural classification, not merely a homology count. For \(K_3\vee C_6\) the theorem gives a mixed-dimensional wedge \(\bigvee^3S^1\vee\bigvee^6S^2\). This exhibits nonchordal behavior not covered by the cited qualitative shellability criterion.
