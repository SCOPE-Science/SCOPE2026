Grafting one pendant vertex onto \(K_{p,q}\) produces a sharp inball transition at \(q=p+3\): the canonical Euclidean inradius is \(\bigl(\max\{q+1-((q+1)\bmod2)/(p+q+1),\,p+4-(p\bmod2)/(p+q+1)\}\bigr)^{-1/2}\). At equality the complete incenter set is a point when \(p\) is odd, but a nondegenerate segment when \(p\) is even; exact binomial facet-contact counts hold throughout.
