Cyclically relabeling the five target colors of the pentagon graph-Hom complex gives an ordinary quotient homotopy equivalent to \(S^2\times L(5;1,2)\). The resulting five-dimensional space has fundamental group \(\mathbb Z/5\) and both first- and third-degree five-torsion, despite the unreduced coloring manifold being \(S^2\times S^3\).
