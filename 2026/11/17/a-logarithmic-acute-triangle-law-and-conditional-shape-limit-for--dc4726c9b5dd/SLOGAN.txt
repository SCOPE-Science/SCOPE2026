For three independent Euclidean-arclength-uniform vertices of an ellipse whose minor-to-major semiaxis ratio is \(\varepsilon\), the probability of an acute triangle is asymptotic to \(2\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)\). Conditionally on this rare event, the middle horizontal coordinate has density \(3(1-x^2)/4\), while the logarithmic gap allocation and normalized gap-product slack become independent uniforms.
