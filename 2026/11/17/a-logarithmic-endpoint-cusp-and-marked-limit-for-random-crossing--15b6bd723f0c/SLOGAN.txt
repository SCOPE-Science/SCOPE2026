For two independent random chords of a circle, conditioned to cross, the fraction \(U\) of the first chord before the intersection satisfies \(\Pr(U<t)\sim(3/\pi^2)t\log(1/t)\). At an exceptionally near-endpoint crossing, the logarithmic endpoint gap and scaled division fraction are independent uniforms, while the remaining arc gap has density \(2\sin^2(\pi y)\); the same theorem applies to ellipse chords sampled by affine-uniform angle.
