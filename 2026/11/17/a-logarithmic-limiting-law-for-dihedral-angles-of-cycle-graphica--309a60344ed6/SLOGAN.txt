The canonical Euclidean graphical zonotope of an \(n\)-cycle has an exact ridge-angle multiset indexed by positive three-part compositions, with angle \(\pi-\arccos\sqrt{ac/((a+b)(b+c))}\) repeated \(2n\) times per composition. For a uniformly selected ridge, the squared cosine of its dihedral angle converges to a nondegenerate law with cumulative distribution \(2w(w-1-\log w)/(1-w)^2\) and mean \(\pi^2/3-3\).
