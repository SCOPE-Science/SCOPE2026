In the homogeneous three-state active Potts flow, strong cyclic drive creates a sharp triple saddle-node at coupling \(k=b+\log b+1+\log(3/2)+o(1)\), rather than at the exponentially distant Hopf scale. On either fixed side of the rescaled threshold the entire probability simplex has exactly one equilibrium or exactly seven, with three stable ordered nodes and three saddles in the latter regime.
