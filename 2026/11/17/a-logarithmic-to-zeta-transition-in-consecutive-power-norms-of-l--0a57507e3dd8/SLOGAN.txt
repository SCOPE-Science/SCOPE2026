For a positive lazy weighted backward shift on \(\ell^1\), the norm of its consecutive-power difference grows like an explicit multiple of \(\log n\) at weight exponent \(\alpha=1\), and like an explicit multiple of \(n^{\alpha-1}\) for every \(\alpha>1\). The sharp constants involve \(1/\sqrt{2\pi e}\) and, above the boundary, \(\zeta(\alpha)\); the worst input columns lie one binomial standard deviation from the mean.
