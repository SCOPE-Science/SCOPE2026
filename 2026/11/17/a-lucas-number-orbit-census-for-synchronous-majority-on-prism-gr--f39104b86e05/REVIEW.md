# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The two-update and one-update local equations unambiguously define finite transfer digraphs; trace counts coincide bijectively with the labelled fixed-point sets. The package computes all recurrent component characteristic polynomials exactly (size at most 16), and direct root-power evaluation gives the Lucas and residue-class formulas. Separate exhaustive, bitset vertex dynamics for all \(3\le n\le10\) agrees, while the proof is valid for arbitrary \(n\ge3\). The asymptotic follows from the strictly dominant \(\phi^2\) root.

## Originality

PASS with residual historical-priority risk. Goles–Olivos provides only period-at-most-two; Agur–Fraenkel–Klein enumerates a different ring self-inclusive rule; the inspected 2014 and 2015 majority papers study convergence/unanimity and voting time, not this exact graph-family census. Jen's 1988 transfer technique anticipates the counting *method*, not the evaluated prism-specific Lucas formulas in the accessible record. Its complete text was not available and remains a serious named risk. Targeted record, full-text, public database and accessible earlier-claim comparisons found no implication establishing these evaluated formulas.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Exact primitive-orbit enumeration for a natural infinite cubic, vertex-transitive graph family is an invariant not supplied by the generic two-period theorem. The unexpectedly short residue-six Lucas classification and the change from an isolated oscillation on a one-dimensional ring to exponentially many on prisms distinguish graph-induced dynamics. No statement is made about attraction basins.

## Scope and risks

The rule is open-neighborhood strict majority at degree three. Closed-neighborhood, path-ladder, Möbius-ladder, or other thresholds are distinct. An unlocated identical formula or inaccessible full prior text could change historical originality; the work has not undergone independent validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
