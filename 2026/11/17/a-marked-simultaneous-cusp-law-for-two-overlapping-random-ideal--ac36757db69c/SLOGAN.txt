For five independent uniform points on a circle, two overlapping ideal-quadrilateral cross ratios have a simultaneous extreme tail \(\Pr(|R_4|>t,|R_5|>t)\sim K/t\), with an explicit finite positive integral \(K\approx0.343103352834\). Conditioned on this event, the full scaled stereographic collision geometry converges in total variation to a size-biased Cauchy law with an independent uniform displacement mark; given one extreme cross ratio, the other is extreme with probability asymptotic to \(\pi^2 K/(2\log t)\).
