Deleting one edge from \(K_{a,b}\), for \(a,b\ge3\), changes its ordinary three-cut Alexander dual into \(S^1\vee\bigvee^{\binom{a-1}{2}\binom{b-1}{2}-(a-2)(b-2)}S^3\). Consequently the original cut complex gains one top-dimensional integral homology class while losing exactly \((a-2)(b-2)\) of the middle-degree classes present before deletion.
