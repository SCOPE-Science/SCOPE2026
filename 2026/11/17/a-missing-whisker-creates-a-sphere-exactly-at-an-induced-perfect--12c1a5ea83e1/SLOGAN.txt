For every connected core graph with at least three vertices and a distinguished non-leaf of degree at least two, removing only that vertex's whisker changes the two-independence complex into an exact sphere \(S^{n-2}\) if and only if the entire core remaining after deleting the distinguished vertex is a disjoint union of edges; otherwise the complex is contractible. Thus one missing whisker creates arbitrarily high-dimensional spheres for friendship-graph cores even though full whiskering makes the same complexes contractible.
