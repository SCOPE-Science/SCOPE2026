At the penultimate graph-metric Rips scale, every odd-by-even torus \(C_m\square C_{2b}\) with odd \(m\ge5\) and \(3\nmid m\) has sphere homotopy type, but its primitive top homology changes sign under an even-coordinate reflection exactly when \(\lfloor b/2\rfloor\) is odd. The full factorwise dihedral quotient retains that rational sphere class if and only if \(b\equiv0\pmod4\), refining the previously known cyclic translation character.
