The ordinary fivefold column-rotation quotient of the \(3\)-by-\(5\) chessboard complex is \(M(\mathbb Z/5,1)\vee S^2\vee S^2\), where the Moore space is the standard degree-five two-cell complex. Its covering space has integral second-homology deck lattice \(I_{C_5}\oplus\mathbb Z[C_5]^2\), detecting the full integral rotational action beyond its rational character.
