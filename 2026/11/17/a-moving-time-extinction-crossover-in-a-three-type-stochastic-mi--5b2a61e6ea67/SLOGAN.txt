At the maximal-interaction boundary of a published three-type stochastic mixture, the third-type mass has doubly exponential extinction at zero mixing but a geometric law for every fixed interior mixing weight \(0<\alpha<1\). Along the exact critical path \(r_N=cq^{2^{N-1}}\), the post-transition mass satisfies \(s_{N+\ell}/r_N^{\ell+1}\to(1+c)/c^2\) for each fixed \(\ell\ge1\), despite a dynamically coupled quadratic correction.
