For strictly convex ovals with support \(1+a\cos(2\theta)+b\cos(3\theta)\), curvature vertices switch sharply from six to four at \(a=6b\). A three-cusp evolute loop collapses at the transition with enclosed area asymptotic to \(\frac{128\sqrt3}{8505}(6b-a)^{9/2}b^{-5/2}\) for fixed positive \(b\).
