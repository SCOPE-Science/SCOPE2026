The minimum enclosing Euclidean spheres of the permutation X-ray polytopes are determined exactly for every order \(2\le k\le10\), with rational centers and squared radii and a full boundary-contact classification. The distinguished cyclic-shift contact set first shrinks at \(k=10\): all \(k\) cyclic X-rays contact for \(k\le9\), but precisely nine remain on the sphere at order ten.
