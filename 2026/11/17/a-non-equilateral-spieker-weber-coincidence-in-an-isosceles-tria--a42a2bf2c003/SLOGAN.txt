The Spieker center (uniform-perimeter centroid) and the Euclidean perimeter Weber median coincide for a non-equilateral isosceles triangle: its apex height over half the base is the unique root \(h_*\in(7/10,4/5)\) of an explicit elementary equation, approximately \(0.746554270914034533\). A certified positive derivative proves a strict reversal of the two centers' vertical ordering across this exceptional shape.
