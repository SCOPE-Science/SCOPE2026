Two independent uniform endpoints on a high-dimensional unit sphere form a chord whose length concentrates at \(\sqrt2\), yet its intersection with the equatorial hyperplane has a nonconcentrating squared radius with exact density \(2/(\pi q\sqrt{2q-1})\) on \(1/2<q<1\). The limiting crossing radius avoids the central ball of radius \(1/\sqrt2\) while retaining strictly positive variance.
