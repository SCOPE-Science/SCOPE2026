# A nonsplit integral honeycomb Rips symmetry lattice and parity-dependent coinvariants

## Finding

For every integer \(L\ge5\), let \(H_L\) be the periodic honeycomb graph with vertices \(B_{i,j},W_{i,j}\), indexed by \((i,j)\in(\mathbb Z/L\mathbb Z)^2\), and edges from \(B_{i,j}\) to \(W_{i,j},W_{i-1,j},W_{i,j-1}\). Put \(K_L=\operatorname{VR}_{\le2}(H_L)\) for the *inclusive* graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex. Let \(\Gamma_L\) be the finite group generated by the two coordinate translations, the planar \(60^\circ\) honeycomb rotation and the color-preserving planar reflection. Write \(\mathcal F_L\) for the \(L^2\) hexagonal faces; let \(\delta:\Gamma_L\to\{\pm1\}\) be the planar orientation character and \(\varepsilon:\Gamma_L\to\{\pm1\}\) the character recording exchange of black and white vertices.

**Theorem.** The integral second-homology lattice has a natural, non-split exact sequence of \(\mathbb Z[\Gamma_L]\)-modules
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z[\mathcal F_L]\otimes\mathbb Z_{\delta\varepsilon}
\longrightarrow H_2(K_L;\mathbb Z)
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z_{\delta}\longrightarrow0.
\]
More precisely, there is an integral basis \(T,\{s_f:f\in\mathcal F_L\}\) such that, writing \(S=\sum_{f\in\mathcal F_L}s_f\), **every** \(g\in\Gamma_L\) acts by
\[
gs_f=\delta(g)\varepsilon(g)s_{gf},\qquad
 gT=\delta(g)\bigl(T+\tfrac{1-\varepsilon(g)}2 S\bigr).
\]
The extension splits after tensoring with \(\mathbb Q\), but does not split over \(\mathbb Z\) for any \(L\ge5\). Its full-group algebraic coinvariants exhibit a sharp parity transition:
\[
\boxed{H_2(K_L;\mathbb Z)_{\Gamma_L}\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,&L\text{ odd},\\
(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2,&L\text{ even}.
\end{cases}}
\]
The full symmetry quotient \(|K_L|/\Gamma_L\) is *rationally acyclic in positive degrees*, whereas the displayed integral coinvariants need not equal the integral homology of that topological quotient.

## Assumptions and scope

All indices are modulo \(L\); graph distance is shortest-path distance between vertices. The oriented hexagonal faces are coherently oriented by the ambient torus. The group \(\Gamma_L\) acts on the specified graph by graph automorphisms. One explicit generator realizing the planar \(60^\circ\) rotation is \(B_{i,j}\mapsto W_{i+j,-i}\), \(W_{i,j}\mapsto B_{i+j+1,-i}\); an explicit orientation-reversing, color-preserving reflection exchanges the two subscripts. The two independent translations shift either subscript of both colors. The rotation has order six, reverses vertex colors, and preserves the orientation of facial loops. The reflection has order two and reverses facial orientation. The stated characters are therefore well-defined and independent of the chosen labels of oriented faces.

The restriction \(L\ge5\) ensures all graph six-cycles bound the standard hexagonal faces and there are no extra short periodic faces. The nonequivariant homotopy type \(K_L\simeq T^2\vee\bigvee^{L^2}S^2\) is known; it is not the novel assertion here.

## Proof

**1. An equivariant relative cellular model.** Let \(Y\subset K_L\) be the union, over graph vertices \(v\), of the centered tetrahedra \(\Delta[\{v\}\cup N(v)]\). Each tetrahedron has a free opposite triangle and then three removable centered triangles, so \(Y\) deformation-retracts onto \(H_L\). There are exactly two simplices outside \(Y\) that are maximal over each hexagonal face \(f\): the black alternating triangle and the white alternating triangle. Thus \(H_2(K_L,Y;\mathbb Z)\) has the free integral relative basis \(e_f^B,e_f^W\), where each alternating triangle is independently oriented so its attaching boundary represents the *same* coherently oriented facial six-cycle \(c_f\) under \(H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)\simeq H_1(H_L;\mathbb Z)\). This description is equivariant because the subcomplex \(Y\) is defined intrinsically and the local center collapse fixes original graph edges.

**2. The sole facial relation.** The toroidal honeycomb graph has \(2L^2\) vertices, \(3L^2\) edges and \(L^2\) cellular hexagons. In its graph first homology, the cellular oriented facial boundaries \(c_f\) obey precisely one integral relation, \(\sum_f c_f=0\). Indeed the boundary homomorphism from oriented torus two-cells has kernel \(H_2(T^2;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\), generated by the sum of all faces. The long exact sequence for \((K_L,Y)\), together with \(H_2(Y)=0\), identifies \(H_2(K_L;\mathbb Z)\) with the kernel of the explicit boundary map
\[
\bigoplus_f(\mathbb Ze_f^B\oplus\mathbb Ze_f^W)
\longrightarrow H_1(Y;\mathbb Z),\qquad
 e_f^B,e_f^W\longmapsto c_f.
\]
Thus a vector \(\sum_f(a_f e_f^B+b_f e_f^W)\) lies in the kernel precisely when \(a_f+b_f\) has a common integer value for all faces. Define
\[
s_f=e_f^B-e_f^W,\qquad T=\sum_f e_f^W,\qquad S=\sum_fs_f.
\]
Then \(T,\{s_f\}\) form an **integral** basis of the kernel: every cycle is uniquely \(cT+\sum_fa_fs_f\). In particular the rank is \(L^2+1\).

**3. Exact integral symmetry formulas.** Any symmetry \(g\in\Gamma_L\) permutes facial hexagons and takes coherent boundary orientations to \(\delta(g)\) times coherent orientations. The two alternating relative triangles are interchanged if and only if \(\varepsilon(g)=-1\). Consequently
\[
g e_f^B=\delta(g)e_{gf}^{B\text{ or }W},\qquad
 g e_f^W=\delta(g)e_{gf}^{W\text{ or }B},
\]
where the choice agrees with the color permutation. Substituting the definitions of \(s_f,T,S\) gives the claimed action. Projection onto the common coefficient \(c\), namely \(T\mapsto1\), \(s_f\mapsto0\), is \(\Gamma_L\)-equivariant with quotient sign character \(\delta\) and kernel \(\mathbb Z[\mathcal F_L]\otimes\mathbb Z_{\delta\varepsilon}\), proving exactness.

**4. Integral nonsplitting and rational splitting.** A putative integral equivariant section sends the generator of \(\mathbb Z_\delta\) to \(T+\sum_fa_fs_f\). Invariance under the complete translation subgroup forces \(a_f=c\) for all \(f\), with a common integer \(c\). The order-six orientation-preserving, color-exchanging rotation sends \(T+cS\) to \(T+(1-c)S\), requiring \(2c=1\), impossible integrally. Over \(\mathbb Q\), the vector \(T+\frac12S\) instead transforms by \(\delta\), furnishing a rational splitting.

**5. Compute coinvariants.** In the quotient by \(g-1\) for all \(g\in\Gamma_L\), translations identify all \(s_f\) with a single \(s\). The rotation gives \(2s=0\) and, from its action on \(T\), \(L^2s=0\). The color-preserving orientation-reversing reflection gives \(2T=0\). These relations suffice because the named symmetries generate \(\Gamma_L\), and their actions have just been displayed. The resulting presentation on \(T,s\) is \(2T=2s=L^2s=0\). If \(L\) is odd, \(s=0\); if \(L\) is even, \(s\) has order two. This is the claimed integral parity classification.

**6. Rational quotient.** The rationally split \(\delta\)-line has no invariants because the reflection reverses it. The translation-invariant subspace of the signed face-permutation module is generated by \(S\), but the color-changing rotation acts on it by \(-1\); hence it also has no full-group invariants. On rational first homology the rotation acts on the two torus directions as a genuine 60-degree rotation and has no fixed vector. Finite-group averaging identifies rational homology of a finite simplicial quotient with invariant rational homology. Since there are no homology groups above degree two, the claimed rational acyclicity follows. \(\square\)

## Verification

`python3 verify.py` (Python standard library) independently reconstructs the graph and all six-cycles and enumerates the complete scale-two Rips face sets for lengths \(L=5,6,7\), checking all maximal tetrahedra and all colored exceptional face triangles. `python3 verify_modules.py` (Python with SymPy) applies the actual vertex symmetries directly to every oriented exceptional relative two-cell, compares their action on **every** integral kernel-basis vector with the formula above, and computes exact Smith normal forms of the integer \(g-I\) relation matrix for \(L=5,6,7,8\). The unmodified standard output is embedded as `verification_output.txt`. Both programs and all required imported code are packaged. These finite checks corroborate but do not replace the all-\(L\) proof in Steps 1–6.

## Relationship to prior work

*Scale-two Vietoris–Rips complexes of periodic honeycomb graphs* (published mathematical research record, 8 November 2026) proves the complete underlying homotopy type, describes the paired face triangles, and explicitly leaves the **integral equivariant action** unclassified. Its rank \(L^2+1\) does not imply the nonsplit lattice, parity coinvariants, or rational quotient statement. This source has been read in full, including its proof and limitations.

R. Decker and M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Vietoris–Rips complexes of affine Coxeter groups* (first public 28 October 2026), prove contractibility at sufficiently large standard-generator scales, and contractibility for suitable alternate generating sets. Those statements do not address the finite periodic honeycomb at fixed graph radius two or its homology representation. The square torus-grid work of Adams and collaborators concerns Cartesian cycle-product graphs, rather than these three-regular honeycomb graphs; it does not yield the displayed action. Previously published two-coordinate permutation modules for square torus grids address a different underlying complex and parameter range.

The result uses known non-equivariant topology as an input, but the integral extension is proved directly from equivariant relative chains. Searches for equivalent signed permutation-lattice presentations, parity torsion, and honeycomb scale-two symmetry actions did not locate an existing theorem. Search incompleteness remains a priority risk rather than proof of uniqueness.

## Limitations

The theorem concerns a particular natural finite symmetry subgroup and scale two, for \(L\ge5\), and does not treat smaller periodic quotients or higher Rips thresholds. The coinvariants are algebraic homology coinvariants, **not a claim about integral homology of the geometric quotient**. The rational quotient assertion does not imply integral acyclicity or contractibility. The finite Smith calculations are exact for their tested sizes, while the general conclusion relies on the written integral relative-chain argument. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
