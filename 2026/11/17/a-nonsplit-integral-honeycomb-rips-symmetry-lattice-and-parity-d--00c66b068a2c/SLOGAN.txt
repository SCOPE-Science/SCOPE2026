For every periodic honeycomb torus with at least five hexagons per period, its scale-two Vietoris–Rips second homology is a nonsplit integral extension of a signed face-permutation lattice by the torus orientation character. Under all natural translations, sixty-degree rotations and reflections, the integral algebraic coinvariants are \(\mathbb Z/2\) for odd periods and \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\) for even periods, despite the rationally split representation.
