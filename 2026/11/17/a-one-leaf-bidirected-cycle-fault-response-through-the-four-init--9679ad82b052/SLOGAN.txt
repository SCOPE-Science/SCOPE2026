Adding a single bidirected pendant leaf to a bidirected cycle yields four exact integral multipath homotopy types: \(\bigvee^3 S^1\), \(S^1\vee\bigvee^2 S^2\), \(\bigvee^3 S^2\vee\bigvee^2 S^3\), and \(S^3\vee\bigvee^2 S^4\), for cycle lengths three through six. Exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matchings and zero integral attaching degrees establish these types without asserting unproved periodicity beyond the four initial cases.
