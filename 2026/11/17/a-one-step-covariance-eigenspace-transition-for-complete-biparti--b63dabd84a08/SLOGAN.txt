For uniform Euclidean-volume sampling from the stable-set polytope of \(K_{a,b}\), the covariance spectrum is explicit in all dimensions: the smallest-variance direction lies between the two coordinate blocks when \(|a-b|\le1\), but switches sharply to a contrast *within the smaller block* as soon as \(|a-b|\ge2\). Balanced members have condition number tending to \(4\), while fixing one block and enlarging the other yields quadratic anisotropy.
