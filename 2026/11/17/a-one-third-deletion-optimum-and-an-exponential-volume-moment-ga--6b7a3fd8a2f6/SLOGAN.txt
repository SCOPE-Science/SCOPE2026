Delete exactly \(m\) edges from a labeled \(n\)-cycle and take the squared intrinsic volume of its symmetric-edge polytope after removing the standard cross-polytope scale: its mean is a Gamma ratio, maximal at one-third deletion (with an exact two-way tie when \(n\equiv2\pmod3\)). At fixed positive deletion density the log-volume obeys an explicit conditional Gaussian limit, but the mean squared volume is exponentially larger than its typical value by a strictly positive Kullback–Leibler rate.
