For the ordinary unsigned edge polytope of a wheel, its unique minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid has an exact Fourier spectrum, touches all spoke and rim edge vertices, and is determined by one strictly concave scalar optimization. The aggregate spoke contact weight satisfies \(a_m=1/3+5/(3m)+O(m^{-2})\), revealing a one-third design law that is distinct from the symmetric wheel root-polytope result.
