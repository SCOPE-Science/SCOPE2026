For a global cubic Hamiltonian center, uniform Euclidean-area sampling inside a growing invariant energy oval gives an exact elliptic-integral period distribution whose natural large-energy limit is Pareto of shape one. Although the normalized periods have a limiting distribution with infinite mean, each finite-area mean has the sharp divergence \(\tfrac13\log(\sqrt\kappa R)\); this spatial ensemble is distinct from prior time sampling along one orbit.
