For every unit-edge regular \(n\)-gonal antiprism with \(n\ge4\), the penultimate Euclidean Vietoris–Rips complex is a cross-polytope sphere \(S^{n-1}\) for odd \(n\), but for even \(n\) it is the independence complex of one \(2n\)-cycle, with a three-periodic sphere-or-two-sphere-wedge homotopy type. The exact last two distance shells are determined, and the next shell makes the complex contractible.
