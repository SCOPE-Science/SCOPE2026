The ordinary quotient of a five-row chessboard complex by even row permutations first develops integral torsion at seven columns: a \(\mathbb Z/3\) Moore summand appears in dimension three. At eight columns the complete homotopy type contains six \(\mathbb Z/3\) Moore summands, a \(\mathbb Z/6\) Moore summand, fourteen three-spheres and twenty-one four-spheres.
