Every right regular \(n\)-gonal antiprism has exactly \(n^2+2n\) antipodal vertex pairs and \(n^2+7n\) antipodal edge-midpoint pairs when \(n\) is odd, but \(n^2+n\) of each when \(n\) is even. Its antipodal defect therefore jumps between \(7n-1\) and \(2n-1\), independently of the height, because the lateral triangular facets are oppositely paired exactly in odd order.
