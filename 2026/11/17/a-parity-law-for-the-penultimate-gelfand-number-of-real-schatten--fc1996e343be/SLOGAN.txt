The penultimate Gelfand number of the real \(N\)-by-\(N\) Schatten embedding from exponent \(p\) to \(q>p\) is exactly \(N^{1/q-1/p}\) for even \(N\), but \((N-1)^{1/q-1/p}\) for odd \(N\). Real skew-orthogonal two-planes attain both sharp bounds, while the odd-degree determinant forces a singular element in every odd-dimensional pencil.
