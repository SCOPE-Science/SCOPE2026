The complete acyclic unit-flow polytope on \(N\ge3\) ordered vertices has exact squared Euclidean diameter \(\lfloor3(N-1)/2\rfloor\) in its natural arc coordinates. All diametral pairs are classified by maximum independent sets of a consecutive-vertex path, giving exactly \(2^{k-1}\) unordered pairs for \(N=2k+1\) and \((k-1)2^{k-1}\) for \(N=2k\).
