For the standard symmetric-edge polytope of every cycle, the farthest facet supporting planes lie at distance \(2/\sqrt n\) in even size or \(2\sqrt{n/(n^2-1)}\) in odd size, with exactly two or \(2n\) extremal facets, respectively. A uniformly chosen facet has inverse squared distance on the \(n^2\) scale converging to a centered Brownian-bridge quadratic functional, sharply separating farthest, typical, and nearest supporting-plane scales.
