The exact Frobenius Hausdorff distance from the Birkhoff polytope to its cyclic-shift simplex has squared value \(n-1\) for odd \(n\) but \(n-1-2/n\) for even \(n\); all farthest points are permutation vertices classified by cyclic displacement collisions. For \(n\ge4\), the second-largest squared distance among permutation vertices is always \(n-1-4/n\), giving a parity-sharp first discrete gap.
