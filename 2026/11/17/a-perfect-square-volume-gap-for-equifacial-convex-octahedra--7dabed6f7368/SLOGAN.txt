For every acute scalene triangle, the centrally symmetric equifacial convex octahedron minimizes volume and inradius among all four octahedral realizations; the squared-volume gap to each twisted realization is an explicit nonnegative perfect square. Exactly on the sharp family \((a,b,c)=\lambda(\sqrt{1+2t},\sqrt{t^2+2t},1+t)\), with \(t>1\), a noncentrally symmetric octahedron ties for the same minimum.
