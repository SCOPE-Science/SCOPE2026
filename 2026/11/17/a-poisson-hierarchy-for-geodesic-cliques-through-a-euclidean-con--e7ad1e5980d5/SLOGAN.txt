For independent uniform-azimuth samples on a Euclidean cone, the first \(k\)-vertex set whose pairwise shortest geodesics all pass through the apex appears at the sharp angle scale \(k\pi+\Theta(n^{-k/(k-1)})\); its count has an explicit Poisson limit in total variation. Conditioned on a fixed clique, the excess cyclic azimuthal gaps have an exact uniform Dirichlet-simplex law, regardless of radial sampling.
