For arbitrary piecewise-continuous periodic diagonal growth forcing on a strongly connected, possibly nonreversible network, the principal Floquet exponent has the sharp expansion \(\Lambda(T)=\lambda_0+T^2\mathcal E+O(T^3)\), with \(\mathcal E\) an explicitly positive weighted graph Dirichlet energy of the centered temporal primitives. Thus genuine spatially differential forcing strictly increases growth for all sufficiently small positive periods, even though global period monotonicity can fail.
