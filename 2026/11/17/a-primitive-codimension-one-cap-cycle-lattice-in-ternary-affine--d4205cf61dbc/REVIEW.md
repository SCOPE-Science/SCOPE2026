# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The exact finite proof constructs the full integral cycle lattice and two unimodular minors, of sizes \(28\) and \(43\). The normalized area functional has an integral and surjective evaluation on an actual basis of \(44\) cycles. This forces the cap-cycle lattice to coincide with its kernel, giving a primitive rank-\(43\) subgroup and a free cyclic quotient. Every one of the \(432\) affine transformations is enumerated; its determinant sign is verified on oriented triangles. The minimum-support assertion follows from edge incidences in any four-triangle cycle. The complete recomputing source is `verify.py`.

## Originality
**PASS relative to inspected literature and indexed records.** Ardila–Castillo–Samper (arXiv:1410.3870) establish shellability and topological information for general matroid independence complexes, not the integral span of caps for this affine matroid. Moci–Pezzoli (arXiv:2001.03760) develop broad representation theory for matroid homology, not this explicitly evaluated cap-lattice quotient. Earlier projective-plane cap computations concern different projective matroids; affine-plane Fourier-minor and incidence-Rips results concern different invariants and complexes. Equivalent formulations and stronger implications were explicitly compared. An unindexed or inaccessible covering paper remains possible; absence in search is not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** Four-point caps are the shortest possible nontrivial two-cycles and form a complete affine-symmetry orbit. Their integral span misses a *free* direction despite being primitive, a structural feature invisible in rational Betti numbers alone. The missing direction has a canonical determinant-sign action, informing natural geometric generation and symmetry questions in matroid topology.
