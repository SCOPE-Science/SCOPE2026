# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

The exact domain is the seven-point rank-three binary Fano matroid. Every independent triangle is contained in exactly one complement of a line, so the seven tetrahedral boundary spheres have pairwise disjoint supports covering all twenty-eight triangular facets. Filling each missing line triangle through a fourth vertex establishes integral \(H_1=0\), while the rank-fifteen signed boundary gives \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{13}\). Selecting one oriented triangle from each geometric sphere gives an actual unimodular minor and proves primitivity **over the integers**, not only rational independence. Four-face support minimality is argued via edge cancellation. Signed Lefschetz traces are calculated for all 168 collineations and separately checked by direct matrices on the thirteen-dimensional rational kernel; the seven-dimensional and six-dimensional characters match the classical group character table. The standalone verifier checks the whole finite construction with exact arithmetic.

## Originality (PASS relative to inspected sources)

The prior general result on matroid shellability gives the nonequivariant homology concentration, not the primitive geometric-cycle lattice or exceptional rational representation. Moci–Pezzoli's general equivariant framework describes homology representations abstractly; their inspected main results and examples do not explicitly supply this Fano computation or the integral saturation conclusion. The standard group character table does not determine the trace of the **topological** homology representation. The existing Heawood graph independence/matching records concern different complexes, and the ternary thirteen-point projective-plane quadrangle-lattice result concerns a different question and has a nontrivial torsion index rather than the present saturated rank-seven Fano sublattice. Searches of equivalent terminology and existing accessible records did not reveal an implication covering the combined statement. Absence of a search hit is not a proof of priority; an unindexed computation or overlooked specialization is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The Fano plane is the smallest nontrivial binary projective geometry and a basic matroid whose natural shortest-support sphere cycles inherit its collineation symmetry. The result distinguishes three phenomena that the usual thirteen-sphere Betti number does not: exact classification of minimal integral top cycles, primitivity of their complete geometric orbit despite a six-dimensional homology deficit, and the irreducible rational representation carried by the deficit. These are natural, independently motivated invariants of this object rather than arbitrary slicing or a trivial recomputation. The integral and representation information can guide analogous equivariant integral questions for larger finite projective geometries. No broader theorem beyond this case is claimed.
