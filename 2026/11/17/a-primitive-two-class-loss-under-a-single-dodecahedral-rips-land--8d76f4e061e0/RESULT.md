# A primitive two-class loss under a single dodecahedral Rips landmark deletion

## Finding

Let \(G\) be the dodecahedral edge graph, viewed as \(\operatorname{GP}(10,2)\), with its intrinsic shortest-path metric. Put \(X=\operatorname{VR}_{\leq3}(V(G))\), the inclusive graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex. For **every** vertex \(v\), let \(Y_v=X[V(G)\setminus\{v\}]\) be the induced deletion complex and \(L_v=\operatorname{lk}_X(v)\) its simplicial link. Then

\[
Y_v\simeq\bigvee^{7}S^3,\qquad L_v\simeq S^2\vee S^2,
\qquad X\simeq\bigvee^{9}S^3.
\]

The rank-seven map on third integral homology induced by \(Y_v\hookrightarrow X\) is a **primitive split injection**, with canonical short exact sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^7\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^9\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^2\longrightarrow0.
\]
In particular, up to independent homotopy equivalences of the two sphere wedges, the inclusion is the standard inclusion of seven three-sphere summands into nine; its homotopy cofiber is \(S^3\vee S^3\). The two third-homology directions lost under landmark removal are independent of the chosen vertex by symmetry.

## Assumptions and scope

The vertices of \(G\) are \(0,\ldots,9\) and \(10,\ldots,19\). For each \(i\in\mathbb Z/10\mathbb Z\), include precisely the outer edge \(\{i,i+1\}\), spoke \(\{i,10+i\}\), and inner edge \(\{10+i,10+(i+2)\}\), with residues taken modulo ten. A face of \(X\) is a nonempty vertex set of graph-metric diameter at most three. **Distances are retained from the intact graph after deletion**; distances are not recomputed in the induced graph. The dodecahedral Euclidean intervertex distances occur in the same strictly increasing order as these five combinatorial distance shells, so the assertion also describes the third positive Euclidean distance threshold after rescaling.

## Proof

**1. Finite uniform reduction.** The above graph is the regular dodecahedral graph. It is vertex transitive: index rotation by one is an automorphism, and an additional edge-preserving permutation carrying vertex zero to inner vertex ten is explicitly listed and checked in `verification.py`. Therefore it suffices to prove the statements for \(v=0\). All graph distances are computed by breadth-first search. The numbers of vertex pairs at distances \(1,2,3,4,5\) are respectively \(30,60,60,30,10\). The script constructs the complete clique-face set by exhaustive increasing-index extension, not sampling.

**2. Three acyclic Morse reductions.** Let \(F\) be the nonempty face set of \(X\); let \(F_D\) consist of faces avoiding vertex zero; let \(F_L\) consist of faces that can be adjoined to zero while staying in \(F\), with zero removed and the other labels shifted down by one. In each of these three finite posets the same deterministic rule gives a discrete-Morse matching: for pivot labels in increasing order, pair every currently unmatched face \(\sigma\) without the pivot with \(\sigma\cup\{p\}\) if that coface is also currently unmatched, removing both from further consideration.

A complete independent pass through **every codimension-one Hasse cover** orients matched covers upward and all other covers downward. A topological-sort test consumes every face in each resulting directed graph, proving the matchings acyclic, not merely checking Euler characteristics. The exact certificates generated by `verification.py` are:

| Complex | Nonempty faces | Matched pairs | Critical 0-cells | Critical 2-cells | Critical 3-cells | Hasse covers checked |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(X\) | 3272 | 1631 | 1 | 0 | 9 | 14420 |
| \(Y_0\) | 2550 | 1271 | 1 | 0 | 7 | 11005 |
| \(L_0\) | 721 | 359 | 1 | 2 | 0 | 2679 |

The checker also asserts that there are no critical cells in any other dimension. By Forman's acyclic matching theorem, each complex has a CW homotopy model with just these cells. All positive-dimensional attaching maps are null since their targets consist of the single critical zero-cell. This proves the three claimed **integral homotopy types**, without assuming that mod-prime homology determines integral homotopy.

**3. The integral inclusion.** The decomposition \(X=Y_0\cup(0*L_0)\) with intersection \(L_0\) gives \(H_j(X,Y_0;\mathbb Z)\cong\widetilde H_{j-1}(L_0;\mathbb Z)\) for \(j\ge2\). The now-proved wedge decompositions imply \(H_4(X,Y_0)=0\), \(H_3(X,Y_0)\cong\mathbb Z^2\) and \(H_2(Y_0)=0\). The pair long exact sequence consequently restricts to
\[
0\to H_3(Y_0;\mathbb Z)\to H_3(X;\mathbb Z)\to H_3(X,Y_0;\mathbb Z)\to0.
\]
The quotient is free, so the rank-seven subgroup is primitive and the sequence splits as abelian groups. Maps between wedges of three-spheres are classified in degree three by integer matrices; basis changes send a primitive injection to standard coordinate inclusion, establishing the indicated homotopy description and cofiber. Vertex transitivity transfers the conclusion from zero to every vertex.

## Verification

Run `python3 verification.py` with the embedded `verification.py` file. It uses only the Python standard library. It independently constructs the cubic graph, all twenty BFS distance rows, the entire flag complex, each deletion/link face poset, each complete matching, and **all** Hasse arrows. The executable fails by assertion if any combinatorial count, critical-cell count, face relationship, graph symmetry, or acyclicity test fails. `verification_output.txt` records the actual successful execution. This is a finite exhaustive proof certificate for one fixed twenty-vertex graph, not a claim about untested graph families.

## Relationship to prior work

The dodecahedral \(\bigvee^9S^3\) homotopy type is prior knowledge: Saleh, Titz Mite and Witzel (2023) establish the complete unpunctured Platonic-solid Rips classification, with the graph-distance-three dodecahedron as its difficult case. The present contribution does **not** claim to rediscover their nine spheres; it determines the punctured complex, its link, and the primitive deletion-induced map, none of which is a statement of their theorem. Hoefgeest and Slot (2026) establish a framework for adversarial robustness and homological vertex cuts in persistent homology; this is a concrete exact local homotopy-and-inclusion calculation motivated by that framework, not a solution to their global minimum-cut problem. The published dodecahedral symmetry-lattice calculation concerns the intact complex; its full proof identifies a ten-tetrahedron augmentation lattice, which can inform rank calculations, but it does not classify the punctured homotopy type or its link.

## Limitations

Only the fixed twenty-vertex regular dodecahedral metric space and **one** landmark deletion at inclusive graph distance three are proved. The result does not give a full punctured Rips barcode, higher deletion budgets, a global adversarial-robustness optimum, or arbitrary metric perturbation stability. The matchings establish the exact finite theorem by exhaustive combinatorial argument, not an infinite extrapolation. Independent audit has not been performed.
