Two invertible nonparamonotone planar monotone maps can have a paramonotone parallel sum, with the complete transition determined by whether their dissipative rank-one directions are transverse or their skew parts cancel. At the aligned cancellation, the parallel-sum norm has sharp quadratic resonance \(\sim(a+b)/(ab\theta^2+t^2)\), while its everywhere-defined resolvent has the sharp first-order modulus \((b|\theta|+|t|)/(1+a+b)\).
