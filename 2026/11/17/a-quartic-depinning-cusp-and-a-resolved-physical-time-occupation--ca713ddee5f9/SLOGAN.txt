A contracting cubic star-node oscillator has a sharp two-parameter pinning cusp: along \(\delta=\tau\sqrt\varepsilon\), rotating cycles exist precisely for \(\tau>-2\sqrt\omega\), and their physical periods grow as \(\varepsilon^{-3/4}\) with an explicit cusp coefficient. Time sampling near the two slow gates converges in total variation, after angular scaling by \(\varepsilon^{1/4}\), to the normalized density proportional to \(1/(1+\tau v^2+\omega v^4)\), which changes from one peak to two at \(\tau=0\).
