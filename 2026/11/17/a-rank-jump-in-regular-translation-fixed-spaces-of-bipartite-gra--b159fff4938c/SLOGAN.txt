For regular translation actions on the complex of all bipartite graphs, a cyclic two-primary group of order \(2^a\) has a fixed space of \(a\) contractible components, while adjoining a single independent involution changes it to \(\bigvee^a S^1\). Arbitrary odd-order abelian direct factors do not change either homotopy type; the rank-two case follows from an explicit \(2a+1\)-facet nerve.
