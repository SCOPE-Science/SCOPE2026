At the equality boundary of the first-order half-rotation locking criterion, a two-harmonic circle map has a sharp nonresonant-forcing cutoff: nonresonant forcing with \(0<|c|<4a\) produces a genuine signed quadratic rotation-number cusp, with coefficient \(\pi|c|\sqrt{16a^2-c^2}/2\), while \(|c|>4a\) restores exact half-rotation locking locally. The result also identifies a natural non-twice-differentiable rotation-number path through a rigid rational rotation.
