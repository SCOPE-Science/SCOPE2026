As a triangle's included angle crosses the Fermat–Torricelli threshold \(120^\circ\) within a \(\sqrt t\) window, the area fraction of points whose three-vertex distance sum is within \(t\) of optimal has a sharp \(t^{5/4}\) law with an explicit one-parameter crossover integral. The conditioned location converges in total variation to a universal anisotropically scaled planar region; at the critical angle it yields independent Beta and uniform shape coordinates.
