For one independent arclength-uniform point on each side of a fixed triangle, the chance of perimeter within \(t\) of the Fagnano infimum scales as \(t^{3/2}\) for acute parents but as \(t^{7/4}\) at a right angle, with explicit sharp constants. The conditional shapes converge in total variation to a uniform ellipsoid model in the acute case and a weighted half-ball model in the singular right-angle case.
