For every \(p\ge2\) and \(n\ge4p+1\), the Alexander dual of the ordinary three-cut complex of \(C_n^p\) simplicially collapses to its clique complex plus all cyclic-distance-at-least-\(2p+1\) edges. It follows that the ordinary three-cut complex has exactly \(1+n(n-4p-1)/2\) free integral homology generators in degree \(n-4\), extending the established all-power range without asserting shellability.
