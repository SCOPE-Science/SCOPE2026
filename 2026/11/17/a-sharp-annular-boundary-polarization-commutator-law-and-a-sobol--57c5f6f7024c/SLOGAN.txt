For bounded angular symbols shared by both circles of a collapsing Hardy annulus, the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of their commutator with the outer-boundary Toeplitz compression, divided by \(|\log r|\), converges to exactly one third of the circle Dirichlet energy (including \(+\infty\)). The all-radius upper bound has the same sharp coefficient, and finite limiting energy characterizes \(H^1(\mathbb T)\).
