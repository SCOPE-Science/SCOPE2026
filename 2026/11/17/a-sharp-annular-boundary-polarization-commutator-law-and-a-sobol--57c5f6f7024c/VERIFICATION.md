# Verification
The norm convention is the sum, not the half-sum, of normalized boundary-circle integrals; the normalized Laurent basis is correspondingly \(z^n/\sqrt{1+r^{2n}}\). Matrix entries of both Toeplitz compressions were calculated directly from this basis. The fixed-radius Hilbert–Schmidt property is justified by \(\|QBP\|_2^2=\operatorname{Tr}(D_r-D_r^2)<\infty\). The universal estimate is a consequence of the lattice shift triangle inequality and \(\int_{\mathbb R}(d')^2=1/3\). Sharpness follows from the single angular Fourier mode in the thin-annulus limit. The extended-real converse follows from Fatou's lemma, not from a test of finitely many Fourier modes. The full primary motivating manuscript and two closest published research claims were compared on their mathematical statements and domains. Independent validation is not performed.
