For three-time correlations at lags \(n\), \((m-1)n\) and \(mn\) in weakly mixing systems, the sharp spectral lower bound is \(-3/2+\Delta_m\), with \(\Delta_m=0\) exactly when \(m\equiv2\pmod3\) and \(\Delta_m=\pi^2/(6m^2)+O(m^{-3})\) otherwise. This bound is approached, but never attained, within the weak-mixing class.
