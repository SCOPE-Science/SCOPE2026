For the undelayed group-defence predator–prey flow, \(E=AD\) is the sharp boundary: every positive trajectory is globally bounded for \(E\geq AD\), whereas every positive trajectory has finite-time predator blow-up for \(E<AD\). On the blow-up side the prey vanishes as \(c(T-t)^\nu\), \(\nu=M/[C(D-E/A)]\), so its derivative diverges only when \(\nu<1\) and instead stays finite or vanishes at the two other regimes.
