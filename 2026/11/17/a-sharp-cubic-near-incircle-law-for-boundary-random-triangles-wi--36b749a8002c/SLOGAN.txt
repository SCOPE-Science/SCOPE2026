For three independent arclength-uniform points on any fixed triangle's boundary, the chance that their triangle's inradius falls within a relative deficit \(\delta\) of the host inradius is \(G_T\delta^3+o(\delta^3)\), with the sharp shape inequality \(G_T\ge64/27\), equality only for equilateral hosts. The rare event selects one point near each corner, with independent limiting side choices and a universal three-dimensional simplex law for the rescaled corner displacements.
