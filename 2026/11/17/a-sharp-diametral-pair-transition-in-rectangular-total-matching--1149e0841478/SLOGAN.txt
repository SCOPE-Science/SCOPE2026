For the ordinary Euclidean total-matching polytope of \(K_{p,q}\), \(2\le p<q\), the exact squared diameter is \(q+\min\{q,2p\}\). Every diametral pair is classified by two differently colored, edge-disjoint matchings covering all larger-shore vertices (or occupying disjoint full smaller-shore matchings), and the number of such pairs has an explicit factorial/inclusion-exclusion formula with a sharp transition at \(q=2p\).
