Among all nondegenerate Euclidean triangles, the normalized four-dimensional volume of their orientation-preserving similarity self-containment polytope is at most \(\sqrt3/8\), with equality exactly for equilateral triangles. Its feasible complex scale-rotation multipliers lie inside the triangle spanned by the cube roots of unity, and under a uniform containment transform their radial gauge follows the same \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,3)\) distribution for every triangle.
