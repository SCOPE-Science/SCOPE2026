At the doubly neutral origin of a coupled-logistic predator–prey map, \(c=1\) is a sharp boundary between shared \(n^{-1}\) extinction and prey extinction of order \(n^{-c}\). Exactly at the boundary, the prey has the \((n\log n)^{-1}\) law and the predator-to-prey ratio has the universal second correction \(d\log\log n\).
