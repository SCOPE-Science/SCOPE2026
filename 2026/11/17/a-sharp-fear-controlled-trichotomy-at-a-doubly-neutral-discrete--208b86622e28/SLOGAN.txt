At simultaneous prey-flip and predator-invasion thresholds of the Du–Han–Lei fear map, an open set of positive states has predator decay \(v_n\sim1/(An)\), while alternating prey deviations exhibit an exact three-regime transition from \(n^{-1/2}\) through \((n\log n)^{-1/2}\) to \(n^{-p}\) as the fear parameter varies. The critical exponent is \(p=[2k+\beta(K+\gamma)^{-1}(1+K/(K+\gamma))](bK+c)/a\).
