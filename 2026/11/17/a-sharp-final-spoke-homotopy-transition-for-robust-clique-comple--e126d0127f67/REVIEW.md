## Correctness — PASS
The face rule follows from a complete classification of mixed independent sets in the specified star-deficient biclique. A vertex deletion–link decomposition gives an exact join with a truncated partition-matroid complex. Shellability, its top Euler characteristic, uniform-skeleton homotopy types, and a nullhomotopic cone attachment prove every asserted integral wedge, including the final dimension change; a coordinate-chain lattice argument establishes primitive homology injections. The included standard-library checker independently enumerated full faces and modular boundary groups for 41 configurations over characteristics 2 and 3. It is corroboration, not an infinite or integral proof. Risks: standard matroid-shellability theorem is invoked rather than reproved; this is an established theorem, and the exchange property of the specified partition-matroid truncation is explicit.

The theorem completely determines integral homotopy and the actual top-homology maps for an infinite class of dense graphs, with a sharp qualitative transition: one additional last-spoke fault creates homology in a new dimension without changing top homology. This distinguishes concentrated edge faults from existing disjoint-block or square-attachment results and supplies nontrivial exact benchmarks, rather than a small numerical gain. It relies on standard topology but does not merely relabel a known formula.
