For the singular four-partition map \(T(x,y)=(|x|-y-1,\,x+|y|-1)\), every integer orbit enters one of exactly two primitive four-cycles in at most five steps, and this uniform bound is sharp. One cycle's complete basin is a union of five explicit lattice pieces with exactly \(5N+4\) points in each integer square of radius \(N\ge2\), so its asymptotic lattice density is zero.
