The intrinsic Euclidean inball of a fixed-trace symmetric doubly stochastic polytope undergoes a sharp transition at \(t=n\sqrt2/(\sqrt{n^2-n+2}+\sqrt2)\): below it, **all** optimal centers form a homothetic fractional perfect matching polytope; at and above it, the incenter is unique. The incenter dimension jumps from \(n(n-3)/2\) to zero, and all supporting-hyperplane contacts and extreme centers are classified.
