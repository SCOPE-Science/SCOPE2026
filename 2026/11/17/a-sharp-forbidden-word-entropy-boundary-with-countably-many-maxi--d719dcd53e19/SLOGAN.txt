For every integer \(q\ge2\), an explicit finite-alphabet marker subshift has equal positive minimal-forbidden-word and topological entropies \(\log q\) but countably infinitely many ergodic maximal-entropy measures. Its complete word, forbidden-word, and periodic-point counts are explicit, and its dynamical zeta function is the Euler partition product \(\prod_{k\ge1}(1-(qz)^k)^{-1}\).
