On every binary cyclic ring, Rule 126 has an exact renewal formula for its primitive temporal four-cycles: they exist precisely at circumferences in \(\langle10,11,12\rangle\), so forty-nine is the last excluded size. Their population grows like \(\sigma^n/2\), where \(\sigma^6-\sigma-1=0\), strictly slower than the plastic-constant growth of its primitive two-cycles.
