In the multidimensional torus-Kuramoto mean-field equations, making positive Lorentzian dispersion widths more unequal at a fixed total strictly lowers both the coupling required for a synchronized saddle-node and the product coherence at that onset. Both have sharp equal-width maxima and the same quadratic loss from small heterogeneity, with an exact limiting coupling-threshold reduction approaching a factor of \(e\) as dimension increases.
