In the linearly mutated three-species May–Leonard model, the locally attracting Hopf cycle can either speed up or slow down as mutation decreases: its exact first physical-period derivative changes sign at a sharp admissibility threshold \(\lambda_*=8.012311970500958\ldots\) in total cross-competition. An explicit positive-parameter formula and two opposite-sign examples exhibit a model-specific frequency reversal absent from the equilibrium-stability analysis.
