In the Griffin–Wu triadic rock–paper–scissors flow, the exact Hopf radial coefficient is \(\ell=(1+b)(1-\beta)/[2(3+2\alpha-\beta)]\): every interior admissible \(\beta<1\) crossing is subcritical. At the sharp endpoint \(\beta=1\), the full flow becomes time-reversible on the critical surface, and the coexistence equilibrium is surrounded by genuine neutral periodic trajectories rather than a nondegenerate Hopf cycle.
