For the real finite fused-lasso norm \(a\sum_i|x_i|+b\sum_i|x_{i+1}-x_i|\), unequal positive penalties leave exactly four linear isometries in dimensions at least two. At \(a=b\), two unexpected endpoint-flip symmetries enlarge the group to order \(16\) in dimensions at least three, while dimension two has the full order-\(12\) hexagonal group.
