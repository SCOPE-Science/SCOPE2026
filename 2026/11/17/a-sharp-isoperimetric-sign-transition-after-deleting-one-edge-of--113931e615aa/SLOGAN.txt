For the standard Euclidean root polytope of \(K_{p,q}\) with exactly one edge deleted, the normalized boundary-area-to-volume quotient increases precisely when \(\min(p,q)=2\) and \(\max(p,q)\le5\), and strictly decreases for every other pair of part sizes at least two. Exact area and volume formulas show an exponentially small improvement for balanced large parts but a polynomial-order improvement when one part stays fixed.
