For the doubling map with a hole at zero, the first \(2+\lfloor(m-1)/2\rfloor-\lfloor(m-1)/3\rfloor\) prime-period-\(m\) orbit-entry thresholds, for every \(m\ge4\), have an exact ordered geometric formula. A three-zero orbit enters immediately after this complete two-zero staircase; the number of consecutive explicit thresholds grows linearly with the period.
