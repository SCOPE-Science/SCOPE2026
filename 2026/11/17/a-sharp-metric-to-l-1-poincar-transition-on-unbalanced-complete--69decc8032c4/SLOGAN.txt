For \(K_{a,a,b}\) with \(a\ge3\), the sharp exponent-one Poincaré constant over all metric targets switches at exactly \(b=4a-3\) from the graph-distance extremizer to a collapsed-star extremizer. Real \(L^1\) targets attain the unrestricted sharp constant if and only if \(b\ge4a-3\); below the threshold their sharp constant is strictly smaller.
