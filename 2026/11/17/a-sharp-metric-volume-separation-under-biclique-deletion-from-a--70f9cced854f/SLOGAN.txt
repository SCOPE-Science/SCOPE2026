Deleting an arbitrary nonempty proper biclique from a complete bipartite graph yields a root polytope whose exact Hausdorff gap from the undeleted product of simplices is \(\sqrt{(u+1)(v+1)/(2uv+u+v)}\), with only the deleted grid vertices farthest and explicit projection simplices. Its relative removed volume is a finite hypergeometric tail exhibiting a Gaussian threshold at \(s/a+t/b=1\), even though the Hausdorff gap tends to \(1/\sqrt2\) throughout the macroscopic regime.
