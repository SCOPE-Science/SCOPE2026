For momentum-accelerated planar saddle search with a fixed imperfect ascent direction, the allowable angle is sharply **smaller** than the \(45^\circ\) first-order limit: the exact threshold is \(\theta_*=\tfrac12\arccos((\sqrt{\gamma^4+4\omega^2uv}-\gamma^2)/(\omega(u+v)))\). At the threshold a simple oscillatory mode loses stability transversely; at unit curvatures, damping, and gain, \(30^\circ\) error destabilizes acceleration although first-order search still converges.
