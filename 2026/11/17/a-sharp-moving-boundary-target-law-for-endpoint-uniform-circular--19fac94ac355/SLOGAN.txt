When an endpoint-uniform random chord of the unit circle must hit a tiny disk internally tangent to the boundary, its probability has the sharp logarithmic expansion \(2\varepsilon\pi^{-2}[\log(1/\varepsilon)+1+\log4]+O(\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon))\). The full boundary-distance crossover is explicit, and conditioned on interception, the chord-normal logarithmic angular scale, signed target offset, and orientation branch become independent uniform marks in total variation.
