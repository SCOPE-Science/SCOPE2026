When the two initial rows of a parabolic stochastic model shrink like \(N^{-\kappa}\), its length-\(N\) history becomes independent to the sharp total-variation order \(N^{-\kappa}\), and its rare visits pass from Gaussian to inhomogeneous Poisson to vanishing behavior at \(\kappa=1\). An exact telescoping first-hit formula reveals a Pareto-type startup clock and identifies the Poisson intensity.
