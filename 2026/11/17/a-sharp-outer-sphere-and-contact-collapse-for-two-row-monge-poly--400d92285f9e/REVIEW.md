# Scientific review: minimum enclosing sphere of two-row Monge polytopes

## Correctness
PASS. An explicit decomposition of every nonnegative Monge matrix into terminal-row, initial-row and column-pair rays proves the complete \(3q\)-vertex description. All exact squared-distance gaps from the stated center are nonnegative, with the claimed zero cases. The center is a strictly positive barycentric combination of boundary vertices, and the weighted squared-distance identity proves global optimality and unique center. Exact rational tests and independent small-parameter vertex enumerations corroborate the general proof. The largest remaining mathematical risk is an unnoticed subtlety in the nonnegative-ray decomposition; its monotonicity argument is elementary and covers boundary zeros.

Access to the full text of the 2023/2026 papers was unsuccessful, so concealed prior coverage remains a real residual historical-priority risk; this is not a claim of exhaustive priority verification.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Euclidean enclosing radii are natural extrinsic invariants of a coordinate-normalized family of high-dimensional polytopes used in optimization. The exact all-dimensional formula, a unique sparse barycentric certificate, and the sharp collapse from six contacts at two columns to exactly \(q\) contacts thereafter give a complete structural classification rather than a numerical example or a restatement of the known vertex set. The formula also determines limiting radius-to-diameter ratio \(\sqrt5/4\). No broader theorem beyond this canonical family is asserted.
