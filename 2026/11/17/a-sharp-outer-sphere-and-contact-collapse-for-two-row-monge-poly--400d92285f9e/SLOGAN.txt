The normalized \(2\times q\) Monge polytope has a unique minimum enclosing Euclidean ball with squared radius \((5q-6)/(8(q-1))\) for every integer \(q\ge2\). All six vertices touch the sphere when \(q=2\), but precisely \(q\) vertices do when \(q\ge3\); the full set of contacts and the center are explicit.
