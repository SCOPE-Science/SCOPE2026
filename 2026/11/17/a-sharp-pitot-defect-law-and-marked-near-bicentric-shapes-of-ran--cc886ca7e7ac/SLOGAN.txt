For four independent uniform points on a circle, the signed opposite-side Pitot defect has a global exact transverse coordinate, with a unique bicentric quadrilateral at each fixed pair of gap imbalances and sharp metric bounds. The probability of a nearly incircle-admitting quadrilateral is \(0.818543101136753\ldots\,t+o(t)\) for unit circumradius, and the conditional gap-imbalance and rescaled-defect coordinates obey an explicit independent marked total-variation limit.
