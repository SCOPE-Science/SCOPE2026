For triangular operators with diagonal multipliers \(e^{-i\theta}\) and \(e^{i\theta}\), the coupling divided by \(\sin\theta\) gives an exact power-boundedness, unitary-similarity and Kreiss criterion. Couplings \(c|\sin\theta|^\alpha\) realize every sharp power-growth exponent between zero and one, with explicit fractional radial-resolvent blow-up constants and a distinct critical constant at \(\alpha=1\).
