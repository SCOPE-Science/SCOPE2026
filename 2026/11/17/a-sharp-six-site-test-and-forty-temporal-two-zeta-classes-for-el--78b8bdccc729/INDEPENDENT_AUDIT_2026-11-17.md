## correctness

PASS

A fixed word is a closed walk in a four-state de Bruijn graph, and F_r^2(x)=x is a closed walk in a sixteen-state de Bruijn graph with five-bit local constraints. Marking an edge where the intermediate cell changes, existence of a primitive temporal-two orbit at any n is equivalent to an internally marked edge of a directed SCC. An independent implementation enumerated all 256 rule tables, directly checked n=1..6, classified the SCCs, and independently constructed exact 4x4/16x16 matrix characteristic polynomials via integer Faddeev-LeVerrier arithmetic. Counts exactly none=107; min lengths 1:64,2:48,3:6,4:17,5:2,6:12; minimum-six rules 26,82,90,154,164,165,166,167,180,181,210,218. Distinct denominator counts 23 for F, 40 for F² and 51 ordered pairs, with 107 equal. SCC characterization proves negative all-length cases, whereas direct finite enumeration supplies witnesses for all positive rules; the all-length zeta formula follows the determinant/trace identity, not finite extrapolation.
