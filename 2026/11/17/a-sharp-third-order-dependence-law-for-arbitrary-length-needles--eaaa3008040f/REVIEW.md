# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The sample space is the Haar-uniform unit lattice torus, with \(U,V\) independent uniforms and \(W=\{U+V\}\). Integer level crossings reduce exactly to three shifted Bernoulli indicators. All pairwise independences follow from the torus addition map. The triple-success region is either a rectangle or two equal right triangles; its areas give the piecewise interaction. The multivariate generating polynomial and total variation require only the first three joint indicator moments, which are all explicitly accounted for. Two applications of the arithmetic–geometric mean inequality produce unique equality locations and the sharp constants. The checker separately integrates eight rational phase-cell laws by exact polygon clipping, verifies all marginals and full scalar mass corrections, and reports success. The all-real-parameter proof is analytic, not inferred from those checks.

## Originality — PASS, with historical limits

Searches of prior research records under needle, triangular lattice, trivariate crossing, pairwise independence, third cumulant, and total variation terminology disclosed no equivalent all-length conditional theorem. Bäsel (arXiv:1312.5131, Theorem 2.1 and introduction) provides size-constrained **orientation-averaged** hit counts and quotes classical Santaló and Markov results. Siniksaran (2009) reviews short-needle total crossing probabilities on triple grids and estimator efficiency. Their published statements do not imply the full joint distribution at every fixed displacement or the sharp product-law distance. Pettineo (1997) explicitly studies dependence among line-family events and is a serious comparison risk. Its full text was inaccessible despite bounded attempts, so no claim of independent historical priority is warranted. The originality PASS is restricted to the best-knowledge published statements actually compared, subject to that residual risk.

## Value — PASS

The natural geometric observable is the total number of triangular tiles traversed by a random translate of a prescribed needle. The theorem shows that a pairwise-independence approximation gives the exact variance but can miss up to \(4/27\) of the entire tile-count distribution in total variation, with a sharp sign-changing third-order defect. The result holds without any small-needle restriction, making it useful for long-segment incidence and for assessing multi-line-grid count approximations; it is not a generic restatement of the Bernoulli inclusion-exclusion formula because the special geometric dependence and sharp extremizers are derived from the lattice torus.

## Closest literature and residual risks

The direct older analogue is Pettineo (1997), whose abstract explicitly flags the dependence problem but whose full article was not accessible. Bäsel (2013/2014) and Siniksaran (2009) are accessible and compare materially on geometry, grid configuration and the size/orientation restrictions. Standard pairwise-independent examples do not themselves establish which triples arise as crossing-count laws nor the sharp geometric extremizers.
