For the Petersen graph with unit conductance on both pentagonal rims and conductance \(t>0\) on the five spokes, the exact arbitrary-metric first-power Poincaré constant has three sharp phases separated by \(t=1\) and \(t=9/4\). Their extremizers respectively collapse each rim, use a two-to-one rim/spoke shortest-path metric, or collapse each spoke into a five-cycle.
