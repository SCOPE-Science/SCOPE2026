For every real cycle with at least six sites, blending its centered three-point running median with its centered three-point arithmetic mean has exact two-input cyclic-variation Lipschitz coefficient \(\max\{1,3-8\theta/3\}\), where \(\theta\) is the mean weight. Thus mean weight \(3/4\) is the sharp threshold for nonexpansivity, with finite extremizing signals at every dimension and weight.
