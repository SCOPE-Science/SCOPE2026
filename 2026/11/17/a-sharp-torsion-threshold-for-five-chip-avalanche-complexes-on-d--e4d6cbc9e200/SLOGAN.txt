Among all binary avalanche complexes of consistently directed cycles with \(3\le n\le15\) and at most five initial chips, integral torsion occurs in exactly fourteen dihedral configuration classes, all at \(n=15\) with five chips. Each has precisely \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\) as its first-homology torsion, and exhaustive integer Smith reductions distinguish their two free-homology signatures.
