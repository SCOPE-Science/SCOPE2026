Among all n-vertex trees, uniform bounded-slope least-squares fitting has exact worst two-input graph-variation amplification max{1, max_{2<=s<=n-1}(s-1)(n-s)/s}, attained on broom trees and equal to n+1-2 sqrt(n)+O(n^-1/2). The connected-subtree norm formula is credited to earlier isotonic-projection work; the contribution is the sharp fixed-order envelope and its matching finite-input witnesses.
