Euclidean least-squares projection onto a tree's bounded-slope signals is graph-variation nonexpansive precisely on paths and the four-vertex claw; all other trees have a sharp sensitivity gap of at least \(3/2\). A connected-subtree formula determines every tree exactly, and the worst \(n\)-vertex tree amplifies two-input variation by \(n-2\sqrt n+1+O(n^{-1/2})\), attained by a broom-shaped tree.
