For every \(n\)-state Markov matrix, the one-step complete-pair Gini contraction coefficient and the Dobrushin oscillation coefficient differ by at most the sharp factor \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/(n-1)\) in either direction. Both extremes occur among strictly positive doubly stochastic matrices, while already at four states symmetric positive-semidefinite kernels can rank the two coefficients in opposite orders.
