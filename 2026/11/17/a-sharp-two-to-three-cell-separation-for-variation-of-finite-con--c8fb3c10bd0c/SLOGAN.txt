For uniformly averaged, possibly interlaced cells on an ordered path, the worst adjacent-variation amplification from two output classes grows at the sharp rate \(6-4\sqrt2\) per site. Three output classes achieve \(n/2\) up to a residue-dependent correction of at most \(1/(n-1)\) and exceed every two-class partition from exactly four sites onward, despite every averaging operator remaining Euclidean-nonexpansive.
