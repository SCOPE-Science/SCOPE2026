For every pair of real branch determinants, a two-zone nonsmooth-fold map with arbitrary continuous scalar feedback \(g(y)=o(|y|)\) has a parameter-independent neighborhood that all positive-detuning trajectories leave within \(\lfloor5\eta/\mu\rfloor+1\) steps. The sign-free Lyapunov gauge \(x+y-g(y)-|g(y)|\) extends the previously established quadratic, sign-restricted escape result to a full nonlinear scalar-feedback class.
