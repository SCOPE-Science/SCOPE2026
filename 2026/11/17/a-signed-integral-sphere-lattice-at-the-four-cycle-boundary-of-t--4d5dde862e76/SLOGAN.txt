For the ordinary three-cut complex of \(C_4\square C_n\) (\(n\ge5\)), the Alexander dual is \(\bigvee^{8n(n-3)+1}S^1\vee\bigvee^nS^2\); the nonequivariant formula specializes a known square-filling theorem. The new result is a primitive integral basis of its second homology, naturally permuted by cyclic shifts and twisted by the reflection signs of both cycle factors.
