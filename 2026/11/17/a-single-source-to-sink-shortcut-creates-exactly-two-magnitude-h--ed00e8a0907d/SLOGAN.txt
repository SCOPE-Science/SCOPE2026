For any layered source-to-sink digraph of directed height \(L\ge2\), adding just the arc from its source to its sink increases integral magnitude homology by exactly one \(\mathbb Z\) in each of bidegrees \((1,1)\) and \((2,L)\), and changes no other groups. Thus one reachability-preserving edge forces non-diagonal magnitude homology at every height \(L\ge3\), however large, even for initially diagonal complete-layered networks.
