A normalized mixture of logarithmic-evidence and expected-utility Matching Pennies dynamics has periods that rise strictly with orbit energy, yet every positive logarithmic component caps the large-energy period. As that component vanishes, the exact singular crossover is \(\varepsilon T_{1-\varepsilon}(e/\varepsilon)\to8\arctan\sqrt{1+e/2}-2\pi\), connecting the linear divergence of the pure expected-utility model to the plateau of every mixture.
