When the Lotka–Volterra rate imbalance grows proportionally to orbital energy, the high-energy predator logarithm becomes uniformly distributed over an explicit finite interval, and the simultaneously rescaled prey logarithm lies on a nonlinear fold curve. The original-time period has an explicit rate–energy crossover coefficient and the joint occupation law converges at \(O_c(\log E/E^2)\) in Wasserstein distance.
