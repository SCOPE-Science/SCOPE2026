# A six-step integral homotopy phase law for avalanches on a two-petal directed rose

## Finding
For each integer \(m\ge5\), let \(G_m\) be the consistently oriented two-cycle and \(m\)-cycle joined at their single common vertex \(h\). Give the hub zero chips and each peripheral vertex one chip. In parallel chip firing, a vertex fires when its chip count is at least its outdegree, sending one chip along each outward arc. Let \(\mathcal A_m\) be the simplicial complex generated by all firing sets in this trajectory. Then
\[
\mathcal A_m\simeq
\begin{cases}
S^{4q+r-2},&m=6q+r,\ q\ge1,\ r\in\{0,1,2\},\\
*,&m\equiv3,4,5\pmod6.
\end{cases}
\]
Here \(*\) denotes a contractible space, and \(\simeq\) is integral homotopy equivalence (not only homology equivalence). In particular the first nine values \(m=5,\ldots,13\) are respectively \(*,S^2,S^3,S^4,*,*,*,S^6,S^7\).

## Assumptions and scope
The vertex set is \(V=\{h,a,v_1,\ldots,v_{m-1}\}\) and the arcs are
\[
h\to a\to h,\qquad h\to v_1\to\cdots\to v_{m-1}\to h.
\]
Thus the hub outdegree is two and all other outdegrees equal one. The initial configuration has \(c_0(h)=0\) and \(c_0(x)=1\) for every \(x\ne h\). The dynamics is synchronous and has no sink. The avalanche complex is the downward closure of every firing set over all nonnegative integer times; the empty simplex is included. These conventions are necessary: replacing the zero hub load by one chip changes the first firing set and the topology.

## Proof
**1. Exact dynamics and maximal firing sets.** Write \(u_t\in\{0,1\}\) for the hub firing indicator at time \(t\), extending it by \(u_t=1\) at negative times purely for the peripheral recurrence. Because each peripheral vertex has one incoming and one outgoing arc, and starts with one chip, its firing indicator is exactly the indicator at the preceding vertex at the previous time. Consequently the peripheral indicators at time \(t\) are \(u_{t-1}\) for \(a\) and \(u_{t-j}\) for \(v_j\). The hub chip count therefore satisfies the exact integer recurrence
\[
c_h(t+1)=c_h(t)-2u_t+u_{t-1}+u_{t-m+1},
\qquad u_t=\mathbf 1_{\{c_h(t)\ge2\}},\qquad c_h(0)=0.
\]
Direct substitution and induction give \(u_0=0\), \(u_1=1\), \(u_2=0\), \(u_t=1\) for \(3\le t<m\), \(u_m=0\), \(u_{m+1}=1\), and the complete configuration at \(t=m+2\) equals that at \(t=2\). To see the induction, the hub counts at times \(0,1,2,3\) are \(0,2,1,3\); for \(4\le t<m\) its count is two, then at \(m,m+1,m+2\) it is \(1,3,1\). The peripheral recurrence establishes the same repeated configuration, so no additional distinct firing sets occur beyond \(t=m+1\).

Let \(F_t\) be the firing set. Evaluating these indicators shows that the maximal firing sets are **exactly**
\[
V\setminus\{h\},\quad V\setminus\{a,v_1\},\quad
V\setminus\{v_i,v_{i+2}\}\quad(2\le i\le m-3).
\]
For instance \(F_0=V\setminus\{h\}\); \(F_1=V\setminus\{a,v_1\}\); and for \(4\le t<m\), \(F_t=V\setminus\{v_{t-2},v_t\}\). The remaining firing sets are subfaces of these listed facets: \(F_2,F_m\subseteq F_0\), and \(F_3,F_{m+1}\subseteq F_1\). Every displayed maximal set is realized, so this is an equality of complexes, not a one-sided containment.

**2. Hub link as a forest non-cover complex.** Put \(W=V\setminus\{h\}\) and define an undirected forest \(H_m\) with vertex set \(W\) and edges \(\{a,v_1\}\), together with \(\{v_i,v_{i+2}\}\) for \(2\le i\le m-3\). By the exact facets, the deletion of vertex \(h\) in \(\mathcal A_m\) is the full simplex \(\Delta(W)\). Its link is the non-cover complex
\[
\operatorname{lk}_{\mathcal A_m}(h)=\operatorname{NC}(H_m)
=\{\sigma\subseteq W:W\setminus\sigma\text{ contains an edge of }H_m\}.
\]
Hence \(\mathcal A_m=\Delta(W)\cup(h*\operatorname{NC}(H_m))\) with intersection \(\operatorname{NC}(H_m)\). As both larger pieces are cones, the simplicial homotopy pushout yields
\(\mathcal A_m\simeq\Sigma\operatorname{NC}(H_m)\).

**3. Six-step arithmetic.** The forest \(H_m\) is the disjoint union of the edge \(K_2\) and two paths with respectively \(\lceil(m-2)/2\rceil\) and \(\lfloor(m-2)/2\rfloor\) vertices; these are the two parities among \(v_2,\ldots,v_{m-1}\). Consequently its independence complex is the join
\[
\operatorname{Ind}(H_m)=\operatorname{Ind}(K_2)*
\operatorname{Ind}(P_{\lceil(m-2)/2\rceil})*
\operatorname{Ind}(P_{\lfloor(m-2)/2\rfloor}).
\]
The path-independence recurrence \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_s)\simeq\Sigma\operatorname{Ind}(P_{s-3})\) (with its initial cases) gives \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{3j})\simeq S^{j-1}\), \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{3j+1})\simeq *\), and \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{3j+2})\simeq S^j\). Since \(\operatorname{Ind}(K_2)=S^0\), for \(m=6q+r\) with \(q\ge1\) and \(r=0,1,2\) the join is \(S^{2q}\), whereas for \(m\equiv3,4,5\pmod6\) it is contractible.

For any forest, its non-cover complex is either contractible or a sphere [2, Corollary 4.3]. With \(|W|=m\), integral combinatorial Alexander duality identifies the reduced homology of \(\operatorname{NC}(H_m)\) with the complementary-degree integral cohomology of \(\operatorname{Ind}(H_m)\), degree relation \(i\leftrightarrow m-i-3\). In the first congruence classes, \(\operatorname{NC}(H_m)\simeq S^{m-2q-3}\); in the other classes its reduced integral homology vanishes, and the forest sphere-or-contractible dichotomy makes it contractible. Suspending yields \(\mathcal A_m\simeq S^{m-2q-2}=S^{4q+r-2}\) or a point respectively. All assertions are integral and hold for arbitrary \(m\ge5\).

## Verification
The standalone standard-library program `verify.py` constructs the directed graph from its arcs, simulates synchronous chip firing, detects recurrence, computes every firing set and checks that the maximal sets equal the stated facets for **all** \(5\le m\le48\). It independently constructs all simplices and computes augmented boundary ranks over \(\mathbb F_2\) for each \(5\le m\le13\), recovering precisely the predicted reduced Betti numbers. The recorded run covers 1,254 firing steps and ends with `AVALANCHE_TWO_PETAL_MOD_SIX_HOMOLOGY_PASS`. These finite tests independently corroborate the explicit-dynamics lemma, but do not substitute for the infinite argument using forest non-cover complexes.

## Relationship to prior work
Riihimäki and Smith [1] introduced avalanche complexes from parallel chip firing and analyzed cycles and other families. Their discussed glued-cycle configurations do not give the stated zero-hub, two-cycle-plus-long-cycle classification. The previously established near-critical result for roses of cycles of length at least three uses **one** initial hub chip for two petals and always obtains a sphere in dimension governed by the longest petal; it neither covers a length-two petal nor the hub-zero configuration, and cannot imply a contractible congruence class here. Singh's forest non-cover-complex dichotomy [2] is an essential cited input, not claimed as a new theorem. The new part is the identification of this explicit dynamical family with a forest non-cover suspension and its resulting six-step homotopy phase law.

## Limitations
The result is for this exact consistently oriented two-petal rose, with a two-cycle, a second cycle of length at least five and the specified initial chip configuration. It does not classify arbitrary chip loads, more petals, altered orientations, integral persistent homology of the time filtration, or other graphs. No independent mathematical audit has been performed. Equivalent unindexed work cannot be excluded.
