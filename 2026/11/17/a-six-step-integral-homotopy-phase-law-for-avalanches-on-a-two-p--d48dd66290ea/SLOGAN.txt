On a directed two-petal rose, attaching a two-cycle and placing zero chips at its shared hub creates a six-step homotopy phase law: the parallel-firing avalanche complex is contractible for \(m\equiv3,4,5\pmod6\), but an integral sphere \(S^{4q+r-2}\) for \(m=6q+r\), \(r=0,1,2\). The phase change follows by identifying its hub link with the non-cover complex of a three-path forest.
