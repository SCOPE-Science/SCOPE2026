For the canonical Euclidean spanning-tree polytope of \(K_n\), the optimal inball switches abruptly at \(n=6\): for \(n=4,5\) singleton vertex cuts control the radius and leave an \(n(n-3)/2\)-dimensional family of centers, whereas from \(n=6\) onward nonnegativity facets control the radius and the center is unique. The exact squared radii are \((n-2)/(n(n-1))\) below the threshold and \(4(n-1)/(n(n-2)(n+1))\) above it.
