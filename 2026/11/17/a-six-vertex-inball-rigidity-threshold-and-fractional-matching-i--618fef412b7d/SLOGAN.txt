The Euclidean inball of the complete-graph spanning-tree polytope has a sharp rigidity transition at \(n=6\): its center set is a regular hexagon at \(n=4\), an explicit five-dimensional fractional-perfect-matching polytope with 22 vertices at \(n=5\), and a singleton thereafter. The exact inradius is \(\sqrt{(n-2)/(n(n-1))}\) for \(n=4,5\), and \(\frac2n\sqrt{\binom n2/(\binom n2-1)}\) for \(n\ge6\).
