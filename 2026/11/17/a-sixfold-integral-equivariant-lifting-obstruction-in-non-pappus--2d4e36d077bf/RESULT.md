# A sixfold integral equivariant lifting obstruction in non-Pappus matroid homology

## Finding

Let \(K_0\) be the independence complex of the nine-point Pappus matroid and let \(K\) be the independence complex of its unique circuit-hyperplane relaxation, the non-Pappus matroid. Write \(G=\operatorname{Aut}(K)\), and let \(\lambda:G\to\{\pm1\}\) record the sign of the permutation of the three points of the relaxed circuit \(\{d,e,f\}\). There is a natural exact sequence of integral \(\mathbb Z[G]\)-modules
\[
0\longrightarrow\widetilde H_2(K_0;\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow
\widetilde H_2(K;\mathbb Z)\overset{\pi}{\longrightarrow}
\mathbb Z_{\lambda}\longrightarrow0.
\]
**The exact sequence does not split integrally.** More precisely, for each \(G\)-equivariant map \(s:\mathbb Z_{\lambda}\to\widetilde H_2(K;\mathbb Z)\), the integer by which \(\pi\circ s\) multiplies \(\mathbb Z_{\lambda}\) belongs to \(6\mathbb Z\), and every multiple of six occurs. Thus the smallest positive equivariant lifting multiplier is **six**. Rationally, the sequence splits. In the extension group of integral \(G\)-modules, this extension consequently has order six.

The group is \(G\cong S_3\times C_2\) of order twelve. Both complexes have integral reduced homology only in degree two, with respective ranks \(47\) and \(48\). For additional representation context, if \(\varepsilon\) denotes the nontrivial character of the central \(C_2\), and \(V\) the two-dimensional standard representation of \(S_3\), then
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde H_2(K;\mathbb Q)\cong{}&
3(\mathbf1\boxtimes\mathbf1)\oplus3(\mathbf1\boxtimes\varepsilon)
\oplus4(\operatorname{sgn}\boxtimes\mathbf1)\\
&\oplus6(\operatorname{sgn}\boxtimes\varepsilon)
\oplus7(V\boxtimes\mathbf1)\oplus9(V\boxtimes\varepsilon).
\end{aligned}
\]
The rational decomposition is contextual; the sixfold **integral** obstruction is the central finding.

## Assumptions and scope

The ground set is \(E=\{a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,i\}\), in this fixed order. The rank-three non-Pappus matroid has eight dependent triples
\[
\mathcal L=\{abc,aei,afh,bdi,bfg,cdh,ceg,ghi\},
\]
where a three-letter string denotes the corresponding three-element set. Its independent sets are the empty set, all points and pairs, and all triples outside \(\mathcal L\); no set of larger size is independent. The Pappus matroid has one further dependent triple, \(def\). Accordingly, \(K_0\subset K\) differs by the single oriented two-simplex \([def]\). This is the standard non-Pappus model, checked against Oxley's account and the SageMath matroid database.

Every \(g\in G\) preserves \(\{d,e,f\}\) as a set, so \(\lambda(g)\) is well defined. The quotient map \(\pi\) takes an integral two-cycle to the coefficient of its ordered \([def]\) simplex. As neither complex has three-simplices, this is well defined on top homology. The theorem is about integral chain complexes and actual matroid automorphisms, not an abstract rational decomposition or a conjectural equivariant homotopy equivalence.

## Proof

**Underlying homology and exactness.** Both complexes have the complete nine-vertex one-skeleton, which has cycle rank \(\binom82=28\). The two complexes have respectively \(75\) and \(76\) triangles. Each of the nine omitted Pappus triangles has a fourth vertex for which the other three triangles in the corresponding tetrahedral boundary are allowed. Thus boundaries of allowed triangles integrally generate the full cycle lattice of the complete graph. It follows that both first reduced homology groups vanish, and
\[
\widetilde H_2(K_0;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{47},\qquad
\widetilde H_2(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{48}.
\]
The chain-level coefficient map onto the newly added simplex is \(G\)-equivariant with target \(\mathbb Z_\lambda\). It is surjective already on cycles: the formal tetrahedral boundary
\[
[def]-[aef]+[adf]-[ade]
\]
is an integral two-cycle of \(K\) with \([def]\)-coefficient one. Its kernel consists precisely of the two-cycles of \(K_0\), proving the displayed short exact sequence.

**Automorphisms.** The incidence counts of the eight omitted triples are three for each of \(a,b,c,g,h,i\) and two for each of \(d,e,f\). Enumerating the \(6!\,3!\) permutations preserving this partition and retaining precisely those preserving all eight triples gives twelve automorphisms. Their actions on \(d,e,f\) realize all six permutations, and the order-two kernel is generated by \(z=(a\ g)(b\ h)(c\ i)\). A complementary \(S_3\) preserves the two outer triples \(\{a,b,c\}\) and \(\{g,h,i\}\) separately; hence \(G\cong S_3\times C_2\). These statements are independently checkable by exhaustive enumeration of all \(9!\) point permutations.

**The integral obstruction.** The \(\lambda\)-eigenpart of the free oriented two-chain lattice has eight primitive orbit-sum generators \(v_0,\ldots,v_7\) represented by the ordered triangles
\[
abd,\ abf,\ abg,\ abi,\ ade,\ adh,\ aef,\ def.
\]
For a representative \(t\), the coefficient of its translate \(g(t)\), expressed in sorted orientation, is \(\lambda(g)\) times the orientation sign of \(g\) on \(t\). This is consistent on precisely those eight triangle orbits; the other two have an incompatible stabilizer sign. The orbit sizes are \(12,6,12,6,12,12,6,1\), respectively, and \(v_7=[def]\).

An arbitrary equivariant lift of a generator of \(\mathbb Z_\lambda\) must therefore be a cycle \(x_0v_0+\cdots+x_7v_7\) with integer coefficients. The signed boundaries on edges \(ab,ah,ae,de\) give the exact equations
\[
\begin{aligned}
2x_0+x_1+2x_2+x_3&=0,\\
-2x_2+2x_3-2x_5&=0,\\
-x_0+x_1-x_4+x_5+x_6&=0,\\
4x_4+2x_6+x_7&=0.
\end{aligned}
\]
Eliminating \(x_0,x_2,x_4\) yields the **integer identity**
\[
x_7=-6(x_1+x_3+x_6).
\]
Since \(\pi\) is the coefficient of \([def]\), every integral equivariant lifting multiplier is divisible by six. Conversely, the coefficient vector
\[
(x_0,\ldots,x_7)=(1,-2,0,0,-2,0,1,6)
\]
has zero boundary on **all thirty-six** edges, not merely the four displayed ones. It defines a \(\lambda\)-eigen cycle whose image under \(\pi\) is six. Multiples of this cycle give every multiple of six. In particular, the exact sequence is nonsplit as an integral \(\mathbb Z[G]\)-module sequence, although it splits after tensoring with \(\mathbb Q\) by Maschke's theorem.

**Full rational character.** Since reduced homology is concentrated in degree two, the reduced simplicial Lefschetz number equals the degree-two trace. Exact signed fixed-face enumeration for the six conjugacy classes \(1,t,c,z,zt,zc\), where \(t\) and \(c\) are a transposition and a three-cycle of the complementary \(S_3\), yields
\[
\bigl(\chi(1),\chi(t),\chi(c),\chi(z),\chi(zt),\chi(zc)\bigr)
=(48,-4,0,-6,2,0).
\]
The ordinary character table of \(S_3\times C_2\) gives exactly the six multiplicities displayed in the finding. The entire computation is reproducible from the standalone verifier.

## Relationship to prior work

Oxley's *Matroid Theory* (2011, §§1.5 and Appendix, pp. 39 and 655) describes Pappus and non-Pappus matroids and their relaxation. Merino, Ramírez-Ibáñez and Rodríguez-Sánchez (2012, §4) compute their Tutte polynomials, which contain the known top Betti numbers; those enumerative results do not determine an integral automorphism-module extension. Amini and Brändén (2015 preprint, §5) study non-Pappus as an exceptional hyperbolic matroid, not its topological equivariant lifting index. Moci and Pezzoli (2020 preprint, §§1, 4) give general equivariant matroid homology correspondences over characteristic zero, but no integral non-Pappus relaxation splitting class or sixfold index. Relevant published research-index comparisons and previous project claims did not identify an implication of this precise theorem. Absence of a match is not a proof of universal publication priority.

## Limitations

This determines the indicated extension and full rational character for the non-Pappus/Pappus pair only. It does not assert corresponding sixfold obstructions for all circuit-hyperplane relaxations, a canonical equivariant homotopy splitting, or a classification of arbitrary integral matroid-homology modules. Relevant unindexed work could affect publication priority. A separate independent mathematical review has not been performed.
