For a surjective corner \(S\), the triangular operator \(T=(\begin{smallmatrix}S&J\\0&0\end{smallmatrix})\) is pseudo-Fredholm exactly when the coupling \(J\) admits a bounded lifting through \(S\). Nonsplit quotients of uniformly convex \(\ell^p\) spaces, \(p\ne2\), therefore produce uniformly convex counterexamples whose every power from the second onward is right generalized Drazin invertible.
