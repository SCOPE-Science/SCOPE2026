Deleting one edge from a complete bipartite graph turns its strictly acute Euclidean Laplacian simplex into one with exactly \(p+q-2\) obtuse ridge angles if and only if \((n-1)(n-2)^2+(3n-4)pq-p^2q^2>0\), with \(n=p+q\); otherwise all ridge angles are non-obtuse. The first deleted-edge instance with no obtuse ridge angles occurs at 25 graph vertices, and the smaller-shore transition grows as \(\sqrt n+5/2+O(n^{-1/2})\).
