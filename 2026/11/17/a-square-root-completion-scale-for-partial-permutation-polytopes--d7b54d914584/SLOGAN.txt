Every partial-permutation matrix with \(q\) unassigned rows has a unique closed-form Frobenius-nearest doubly stochastic matrix, with squared distance \(q(q+2)/(n+q(q+1))\). The full partial-assignment polytope has Hausdorff gap one from its Birkhoff face, but a uniformly chosen vertex has squared completion error converging to one half, with Gaussian fluctuations at the \(n^{-1/4}\) scale.
