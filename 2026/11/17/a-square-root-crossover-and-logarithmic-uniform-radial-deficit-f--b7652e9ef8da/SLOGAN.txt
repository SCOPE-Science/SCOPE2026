For a uniformly chosen vertex of the standard harmonic polytope, \(\log\max\{1,D_n\}/\log n\), where \(D_n\) is half the squared-radius deficit from its known minimum enclosing sphere, converges to a uniform law on \([1/2,1]\). A sharp \(\sqrt n\)-scale crossover interpolates between linearly growing inner-shell weights and an inverse-deficit tail.
