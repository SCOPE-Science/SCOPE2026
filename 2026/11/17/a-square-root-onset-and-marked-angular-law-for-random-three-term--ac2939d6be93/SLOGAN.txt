For three uniformly sampled points on a circle, allowing an optimal Steiner junction gives **no** length saving with probability \(1/3\); immediately above that atom, the positive relative savings have a sharp square-root probability law with explicit coefficient \(2.700948948484718\ldots\). The rare near-zero savings select a nonuniform angular shape, independently of a \(\operatorname{Beta}(1/2,1)\) normalized saving.
