For three independent uniform great circles, the probability that any spherical cell has area below \(x\) is asymptotic to \(\sqrt2\pi J\sqrt{x}\), with an explicit positive two-dimensional integral \(J\). Conditional on this rare event, the three other antipodal-pair area proportions have a nonuniform sine-product density and the rescaled minimum has a \(\operatorname{Beta}(1/2,1)\) law.
