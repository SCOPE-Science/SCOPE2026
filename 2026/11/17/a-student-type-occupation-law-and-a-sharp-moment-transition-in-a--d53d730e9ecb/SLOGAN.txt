A cubic polynomial center has exponentially large high-energy orbits but physical periods of order \(E^{-1/2}\); sampling its horizontal coordinate in original time converges to the explicit density \(\sqrt3/[2(1+3x^2)^{3/2}]\). Its absolute moments exhibit a sharp threshold at order two: below it they converge to beta-function constants, at it they grow as \(3E\), and above it they grow as \(A_E^{q-2}\sqrt{\pi E/[2(q-2)]}\).
