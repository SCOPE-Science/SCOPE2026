At the critical gain in a four-oscillator path with global asymmetric triadic coupling, the balanced antipodal state is nonlinearly unstable: the center-manifold drift has a positive cubic term. On the stable side, two nearby index-one saddles emerge subcritically, with interior phases displaced like \(\sqrt{\delta}\) but exterior phases only like \(\delta^{3/2}/4\); the previously known linear threshold is not claimed as new.
