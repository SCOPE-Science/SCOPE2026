At the exact maximum of a Crowley–Martin prey nullcline, a coarse Euler discretization undergoes a nondegenerate supercritical Neimark–Sacker bifurcation at step \(h=1\): the first radial coefficient is exactly \(-1/4\), and the attracting invariant curve has normalized radius \(\sqrt{h-1}+O(h-1)\). This identifies the nonlinear outcome at a vertex-crossing boundary permitted by the known step-size-dependent linear criterion.
