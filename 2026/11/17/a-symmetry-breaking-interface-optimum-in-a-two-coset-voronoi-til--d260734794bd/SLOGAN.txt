An equal-area, two-site periodic Voronoi tiling has a unique non-Bravais stagger that minimizes cell perimeter, even though moving toward it strictly worsens both covering radius and mean squared nearest-site distance. The exact minimizing stagger is \(t_*=0.3532048086279578\ldots\), with cell perimeter \(5.64928854396555\ldots\) versus \(4\sqrt2\) for its square-lattice endpoint; this is a constrained geometric tradeoff, not a globally optimal honeycomb.
