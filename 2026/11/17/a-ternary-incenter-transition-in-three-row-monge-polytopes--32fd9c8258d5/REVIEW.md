# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Intrinsic coordinate and four-term facet normals have lengths \(\sqrt{1-1/(3q)}\) and \(2\), respectively. A dual certificate of two explicit integer tents gives the minimum nonnegative three-row unit-slack mass \(2\lfloor q^2/3\rfloor\); complementary-slackness and a piecewise-linear two-quantile minimization classify all equality cases, without extrapolating finite computations. Both positive-dimensional and unique-center cases have explicit primal witnesses. The stated contact counts follow by counting zero entries. An independent linear program checks the mass value for every \(3\le q\le16\), and exact finite arithmetic corroborates the constructed dual, feasible centers, and tangent inequalities to \(q=36\). Proof, not enumeration, establishes every \(q\ge4\).

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** Normalized Monge polytopes are a structurally motivated family with explicit facets and recent volume/Ehrhart research. Three rows are the first rectangular width beyond the previously classified two-row case; the complete center-locus classification yields a nontrivial period-three dimension jump, and its full tangent-facet transition makes the metric geometry explicit. A sharp two-layer min-cost dual, rather than a formal substitution into the two-row formula, is needed. This is a natural classification across an infinite family and is not an arbitrary small-parameter statistic.
