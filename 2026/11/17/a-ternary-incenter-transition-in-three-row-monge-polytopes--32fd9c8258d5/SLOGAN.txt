For every normalized nonsymmetric three-row Monge polytope with at least four columns, the exact Frobenius inradius is \(\bigl(3q\sqrt{1-1/(3q)}+4\lfloor q^2/3\rfloor\bigr)^{-1}\). Its complete incenter locus is a parallelogram precisely when the column count is divisible by three and otherwise a single point, with an exact classification of every tangent-facet transition.
