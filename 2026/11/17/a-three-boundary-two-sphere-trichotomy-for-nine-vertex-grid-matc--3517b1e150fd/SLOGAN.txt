For nine-vertex three-by-three grids, passing from open to cylindrical to doubly periodic boundaries changes the integral homotopy type of the ordinary matching complex from \(\bigvee^5S^2\) to \(\bigvee^{16}S^2\) to \(\bigvee^{26}S^2\). Explicit elementary-collapse and fundamental-group certificates prove the two periodic cases, rather than just their Betti numbers.
