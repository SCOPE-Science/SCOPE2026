At the critical basal reproduction threshold of a discrete three-species food chain, every strictly positive simplex-surviving trajectory has prey \(x_n\sim n^{-1}\), a middle predator with a factorial and squared-logarithm extinction correction, and a top predator satisfying \(\log z_n=-\tfrac12n^2\log n+(\tfrac34+\tfrac12\log\beta)n^2+O(n(\log n)^2)\). The three-clock hierarchy holds on an explicit open positive-data wedge, and the apex's first two coefficients are independent of its own conversion rate.
