# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof identifies the actual graph and matching faces, reduces path matching complexes by a deletion/link cofiber sequence, and uses a disjoint apex-spoke cone decomposition with all links enumerated by residue class. The \(n=3\) boundary case is checked directly. For \(n=3q+1\), the endpoint-link inclusion is a homotopy equivalence, not merely a rational homology isomorphism. For the other two classes, connectivity or contractibility makes every necessary link map null. Thus the proof determines integral homotopy type, not merely finite-field homology. Standalone mod-two boundary ranks for \(3\le n\le18\) and exact Euler/link-count checks through \(n=300\) corroborate the proof.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence
Bayer, Jelić Milutinović and Vega's 2024 *Matching Complexes of Outerplanar Graphs* (arXiv:2411.04601, Theorem 2) proves a broad existence theorem for wedges of spheres but supplies no fan-specific degree and count classification. Their 2022 *General polygonal line tilings and their matching complexes* (arXiv:2211.12559, Section 4, Appendix B) treats alternating triangle lines; these are not the all-apex fan triangulations beyond the small overlapping examples. Alias and semantic mathematical searches uncovered no exact all-size fan result. This is an evidence-bounded priority assessment: unindexed or unexpectedly named stronger results remain a risk.
