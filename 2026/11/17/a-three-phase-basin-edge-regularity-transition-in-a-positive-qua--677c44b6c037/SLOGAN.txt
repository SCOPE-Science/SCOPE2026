For the two-species homogeneous quadratic map, the extinction–escape basin boundary changes its pure-axis regularity when the transverse invasion multiplier crosses the radial multiplier \(2\). The exact edge laws are linear below resonance, \(s\log(1/s)/\log2\) at resonance, and a sharp fractional power \(s^{\log2/\log\lambda}\) above it; the global radial separator itself was known previously.
