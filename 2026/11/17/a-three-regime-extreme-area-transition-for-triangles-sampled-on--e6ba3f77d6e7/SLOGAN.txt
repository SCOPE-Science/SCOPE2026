Three uniform random points on an arc of the unit circle have near-maximal triangle-area probabilities of orders \(\varepsilon^{5/2}\), \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\), and \(\varepsilon\), separated sharply at arc length \(4\pi/3\). Explicit coefficients and total-variation conditional shape limits show how a boundary-pinned optimizer turns into a continuum of equilateral optimizers.
