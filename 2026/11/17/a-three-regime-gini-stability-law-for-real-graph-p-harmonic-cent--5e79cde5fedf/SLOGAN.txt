Inserting the real \(p\)-harmonic center of an unweighted star into its leaf data has an exact two-input complete-pair Gini stability constant with three distinct regimes: \(2\) below \(p=2\), \(1+2/m\) at \(p=2\), and \(1+m/[2(m-1)]\) above \(p=2\), for \(m\ge3\). Unlike the corresponding supremum-norm stability, this pairwise-dispersion metric distinguishes subquadratic from superquadratic nonlinear averaging.
