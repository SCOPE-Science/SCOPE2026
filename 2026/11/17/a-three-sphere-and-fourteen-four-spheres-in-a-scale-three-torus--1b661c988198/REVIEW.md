# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The 49 vertices and inclusive periodic Manhattan radius-three metric are completely specified. A standalone standard-library checker independently enumerates all 18,634 nonempty simplices, verifies 9,309 chronological free-face pairs plus sixteen critical maximal faces with no leftover faces, and obtains the critical vector with one cell in dimensions zero and three and fourteen cells in dimension four. Chronologically free pairs give an acyclic discrete Morse matching. Signed integer gradient-path recursion evaluates the fourteen Morse boundary coefficients from dimension four to three as exact zeros. The resulting attaching maps have zero degree, and \(\pi_3(S^3)\cong\mathbb Z\) forces each to be nullhomotopic. The classification is an integral homotopy theorem, not an inference from Betti numbers alone. The limited risk is correctness of the finite certificate and the standard discrete-Morse cancellation interpretation, both explicitly described and checkable.

## Originality — PASS with bibliographic qualification
Adams, Adetowubo, Barriga-Acosta, Feng and Sterling, arXiv:2502.07134, first public 2025-02-10, report mod-two Betti numbers \((\beta_3,\beta_4)=(1,14)\) for this exact complex but in Section 8, Question 8.1 request its homotopy type. Their proved homotopy classifications at \((5,3)\) and \((7,4)\), plus scale-two theorems, do not settle \((7,3)\). Sterling's 2025 thesis tabulates the same example without a homotopy classification. The later public work on even-by-even grids at half-diameter has different scope. Searches for equivalent formulations, table entries, broader classifications and exact parameter pairs did not locate an implication covering this new theorem. The residual risk is an unindexed or inaccessible equivalent result; database absence alone is not treated as proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This determines the precise integral homotopy type of a specific smallest unresolved scale-three torus-grid case explicitly identified in a published research question. The fourteen four-cells are proved to attach trivially to the three-sphere, information unavailable from the previously recorded mod-two Betti numbers. The finite but natural, symmetry-rich 49-vertex complex is motivated by the problem itself, not by an arbitrary slice or routine extra example. The executable certificate makes this exact answer independently reproducible.
