On the fifteen-vertex Kneser graph \(KG(6,2)\), synchronous binary majority with tie retention has a sharp three-step cutoff, 502 fixed states and 2,670 primitive two-cycle orbits. Every attractor basin has size exactly \(1\), \(2\), \(10\), \(20\) or \(10{,}944\), and the recurrent states form exactly eight fixed and eighteen oscillating geometric classes under \(S_6\).
