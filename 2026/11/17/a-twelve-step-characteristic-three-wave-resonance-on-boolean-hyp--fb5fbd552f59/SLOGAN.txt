On every Boolean hypercube \(Q_d\) over a field of characteristic three, the reversible two-time-level neighbor-sum wave has exact global order \(12\) once \(d\ge2\), with only orders \(4\) and \(6\) in dimensions zero and one. A complete six-period labelled-state census shows that the fraction of primitive twelve-period states is exactly \((1-q^{-2a_d})(1-q^{-2b_d})\), where \(a_d=(2^d-(-1)^d)/3\) and \(b_d=(2^d+2(-1)^d)/3\), tending to one doubly exponentially in dimension.
