For unequal positive monomial powers on an annular Hardy space, the cross-adjoint Toeplitz commutator has a strictly log-concave singular-value profile, with a uniform one-eigenvalue counting discrepancy and an all-Schatten remainder independent of both degrees and annulus thickness. When both powers grow inversely with thickness, a two-parameter spectral crossover emerges, including an explicit logarithmic Hilbert--Schmidt constant.
