For three tangents to a parabola sampled from the two-parameter beta–Jacobi slope density, the probability of a nearly maximal triangle obeys an exact power law with exponent \(2p+q/2\): endpoint concentration and midpoint concentration contribute on different geometric scales. Conditioned on the rare event, the two endpoint deficits and squared central displacement converge jointly in total variation to a \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(p,p,q/2,1)\) profile with an independent fair sign.
