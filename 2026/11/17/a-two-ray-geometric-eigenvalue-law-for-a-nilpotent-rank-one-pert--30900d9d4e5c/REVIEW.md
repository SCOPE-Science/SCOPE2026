# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The resolvent identity holds for every nonzero spectral parameter because the chosen weighted shift has powers of norm \(q^{n(n+1)/8}\), hence spectral radius zero. Euler's coefficient recurrence gives the stated entire product exactly. The annular boundary lower bound is uniform in angle and tends to infinity, so Rouche's theorem proves the *complete* two-root count, not just existence. The exact product derivative, the geometrically separated zero set, and a quantitative local logarithmic-derivative estimate yield unique simple roots and the displayed error term. Inversion gives the spectral expansion; the rank-one resolvent factor gives algebraic simplicity. The independent finite checker passed 189 numerical tests at three values of \(q\) and three nonzero levels.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The closest prior operator paper (Bračič and Kandić, arXiv:2611.12208v1, Section 6) contains the same general rank-one resolvent equation and qualitative infinitude, but no geometric-quantization or transverse-drift law. Tcaciuc (arXiv:1805.03277, Lemma 2.1 and Proposition 2.4) similarly addresses qualitative rank-one spectral mechanisms. Zhang (arXiv:2401.01910v4, Sections 1–2) gives entire \(q\)-function growth and lower root-spacing estimates, not the complete nonzero-level pair count and signed displacement. Searches under spectral counting, Euler-product level sets, and geometric zero asymptotics yielded related but nondominating claims. An older entire-function level-set calculation could still subsume this special explicit product; that residual bibliographic risk is material but no decisive covering statement was identified.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The motivating operator theorem proves infinitude via the Great Picard theorem without describing eigenvalue locations. Here every sufficiently small geometric annulus is classified exactly, each eigenvalue is simple with a controlled correction, and the crossing of the imaginary axis alternates with the scale. This distinguishes infinitely many connected spectral components from a quantitatively rigid two-ray spectral law and supplies a sharp counting invariant. The construction is motivated by a specific unquantified operator-spectral phenomenon; it is not an arbitrary finite table or a notational reformulation.

**Scientific limits.** The result is for a constructed family and fixed parameters, not arbitrary compact shifts. The numerical checker is auxiliary evidence; the infinite statement is proved analytically. Early eigenvalues, uniformity at boundary parameters and comprehensive historical novelty cannot be asserted.
