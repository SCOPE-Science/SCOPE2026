A compact quasinilpotent weighted shift has an explicit square-zero rank-one perturbation whose nonzero eigenvalues form exactly two simple points in every sufficiently small geometric annulus, for every nonzero coupling. Their two almost-imaginary rays have an explicit superexponentially small displacement that alternates in real sign at successive scales, and their counting law has sharp logarithmic coefficient \(2/\log(1/q)\).
