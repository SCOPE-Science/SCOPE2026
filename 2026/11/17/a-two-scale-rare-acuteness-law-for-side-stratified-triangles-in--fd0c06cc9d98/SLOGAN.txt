For one independent uniform point on each side of the collapsing isosceles triangle of height \(h\), the probability of strict acuteness is \((\frac83\log2-\frac76)h^2+o(h^2)\). Conditioned on this rare event, the base point lies within \(O(h^2)\) of one of the other two points' horizontal coordinates, and the complete signed, side-parameter-marked limit has an explicit two-component density in total variation.
