At the degenerate invasion boundary of a two-competitor Allee–fear flow, detuning the competition parameter by \(\pm\varepsilon\) creates a sharp initial-amplitude threshold at \(y_0\asymp\sqrt\varepsilon\) and two distinct explicit limits for \(\varepsilon\) times the first passage to a fixed small population level. Exactly at resonance the passage time instead scales as \(1/(2b\gamma y_0^2)\), while the upper-detuned branch supports a nearby positive saddle with unstable eigenvalue \(2b\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon^{3/2})\).
