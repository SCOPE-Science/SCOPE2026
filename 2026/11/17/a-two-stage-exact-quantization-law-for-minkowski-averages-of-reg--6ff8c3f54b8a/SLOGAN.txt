For the six vertices of a unit-circumradius regular hexagon, every equal-weight Minkowski average after the first one is exactly the triangular-lattice grid in the hexagon, and the scaled nearest-site distance of a uniform point has one universal, explicit distribution for all \(k\ge2\). The exact Hausdorff error is \(1/(k\sqrt3)\) and its mean squared quantization error is \(5/(36k^2)\), whereas the initial vertex-only values are \(1\) and \(1/4\).
