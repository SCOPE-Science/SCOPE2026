# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof distinguishes graph-vertex deletion from simplicial vertex deletion. It identifies two exact attachment subcomplexes: the complete \(d\)-partite two-vertices-per-part graph \(\Lambda\), and a link \(T\) with nerve \(\Lambda\). Pairwise intersections and absence of triple intersections are proved directly from the ternary Hamming metric. Each triangle generator of \(\pi_1\Lambda\) bounds an explicit disk surviving puncture; the dual triangle generator of \(\pi_1T\) bounds an explicit simplex there. Since the flag clique complex of \(\Lambda\) is the simply connected join of \(d\ge3\) two-point complexes, these triangles normally generate fundamental groups. Both attaching maps are nullhomotopic, and contractible-piece homotopy pushouts give an inclusion-compatible double suspension. The absolute homology corollary correctly uses separately credited results for the intact graph. Finite checks and their exact limits are recorded in the verification documents.

## Originality — PASS (with bibliographic residual risk)
The closest full published records calculate integral homology and optimal Morse data of the *intact* ternary Hamming neighborhood complex. Those statements supply none of the punctured inclusions or two null-attachment arguments. Primary related papers calculate graph-walk fundamental groups and investigate Lovász complexes, again without an equivalent vertex-puncture splitting. Searches also compared graph vertex deletion with simplicial deletion, link/cofiber descriptions, q-ary aliases, and stronger all-parameter coverage; no implication yielding the present double suspension was found. Search absence is not a proof of priority. Other unindexed formulations remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a natural minimal perturbation of an infinite, highly symmetric topological-combinatorics family. The precise two-stage loss law, split inclusion maps, and sharp dimension-two obstruction distinguish it from elementary Euler characteristic recomputation. The result explains an integral homotopy change using concrete local combinatorial fillings, potentially useful for comparing further punctures and other alphabets.

## Closest literature and limitations
Behal–Chih and Matsushita determine relevant known fundamental groups; the published intact-complex collapse and three-cube Morse calculation cover only unpunctured objects. The new theorem requires a ternary alphabet and at least three factors; it does not extend automatically to multiple punctures, other alphabets or equivariant splittings. Source inspection includes full material for the two closest published intact-complex mathematical claims, and abstract/metadata inspection for three relevant primary preprints. An unindexed equivalence remains a genuine bibliographic risk. Independent audit was not performed.
