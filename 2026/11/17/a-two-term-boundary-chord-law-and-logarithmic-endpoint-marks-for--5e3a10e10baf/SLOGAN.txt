For two independent arclength-uniform boundary points on a unit Reuleaux triangle, the probability of a chord longer than \(1-\varepsilon\) is \(\frac{6}{\pi^2}\varepsilon[\log(1/\varepsilon)+1+\log(4/3)]+o(\varepsilon)\), with an exact finite-threshold one-dimensional integral. Conditioned on this rare event, the ordered arc pair, the logarithmic endpoint distance, and the normalized chord deficit converge jointly to independent uniform laws.
