For three uniformly random points on a circle, the Euclidean minimum-spanning-tree length has the sharp near-maximum probability \(9t^2/\pi^2-18\sqrt3t^3/\pi^2+O(t^4)\). Conditioned on this event, the longest-gap label, a normalized maximal-gap excess, and an angular split become independent uniform, \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,1)\), and uniform marks, respectively; the triangle perimeter fluctuates on a different quadratic scale.
