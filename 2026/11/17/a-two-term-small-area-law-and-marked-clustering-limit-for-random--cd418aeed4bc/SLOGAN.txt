For four independent uniform points on a circle, the probability that their cyclic quadrilateral has area below \(t\) is \(t/\pi+C_*t^{5/3}+o(t^{5/3})\), with a positive explicit integral coefficient. The rare small-area event concentrates all four vertices in a short arc whose three normalized gaps have the new density \(4/[\pi^2(1-u)(1-v)(1-w)]\), independent of a \(\operatorname{Beta}(3,1)\) radial mark.
