At the critical flip threshold of a three-population quasi-strictly non-Volterra quadratic stochastic map, every off-critical initial simplex state has an explicit universal alternating \(n^{-1/2}\) relaxation vector. Precisely at the feasible inheritance ratios \(c:d=2:5\) or \(5:2\), one peripheral species instead relaxes at the sharp rate \(-20/(63n)\), while the others retain the slower critical oscillation.
