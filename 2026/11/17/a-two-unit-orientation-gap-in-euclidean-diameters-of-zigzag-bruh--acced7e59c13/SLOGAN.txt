The standard Euclidean diameters of Bruhat interval polytopes for the two zigzag Coxeter elements are exactly \(\sqrt{9n-19}\) in odd order, but in even order their squared diameters are \(9n-18\) versus \(9n-20\). Every farthest pair uses all first-layer adjacent swaps in both permutations and complementary second-layer swaps, yielding an exact exponentially sized diametral-pair census.
