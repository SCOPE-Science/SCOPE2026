For every nonempty triangle-free graph \(H\), every \(s\geq2\) and every forbidden-clique order \(t\geq5\), the complex \(\mathsf{CF}_t(K_s\vee H)\) is a wedge of \(\binom{s}{t-2}\) copies of the \((t-2)\)-fold suspension of \(\operatorname{Ind}(H)\) and \(\binom{s}{t-1}\) spheres of dimension \(t-2\). This exact nonequivariant homotopy splitting transfers the full integral reduced homology, including any torsion, from a graph's independence complex into every higher threshold strictly beyond the previously established four-clique-free case.
