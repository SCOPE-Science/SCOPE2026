Every closed arclength-weighted spherical two-design curve on \(S^2\) has length at least \(2\pi\sqrt{(1971-180\sqrt{47})/363}=8.9527209\ldots\), strictly above the previously known spectral bound \(2\pi\sqrt2\). A fourth-Fourier-harmonic obstruction to maintaining constant spherical radius forces a uniform energy gap; sharpness of the new bound is not asserted.
