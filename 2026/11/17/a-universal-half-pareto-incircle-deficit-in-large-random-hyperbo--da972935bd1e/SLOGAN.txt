Three arclength-uniform points on a large hyperbolic circle have an incircle-radius deficit of order \(e^{-2R}\), converging to an explicit cotangent function of the circular gaps. This limit has a sharp \(t^{-1/2}\) tail with coefficient \((6/\pi)\sqrt{2/3}\); unusually large deficits arise from a uniformly marked single near-collision of adjacent sampled directions.
