For two independent area-uniform points in a hyperbolic cusp, the intrinsic distance has an exact two-variable stochastic factorization and a one-dimensional survival formula. Every cusp has leading tail \(e^{-r}\), but the next term, \(\lambda^2e^{-3r}/24\), uniquely detects its boundary-horocycle length \(\lambda\); larger horocycles strictly enlarge the whole distance law.
