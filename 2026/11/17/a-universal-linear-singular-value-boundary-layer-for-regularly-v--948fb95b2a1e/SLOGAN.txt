For monotone regularly varying compact unilateral weighted shifts, every generalized Aluthge orbit has the universal singular-value profile \(s_{\lfloor nu\rfloor+1}/w_n\to(t+u)^{-\beta}\), uniformly on bounded \(u\)-intervals. The complete normalized counting curve and sharp Schatten mass follow; the effective Schatten rank grows as \(tn/(\beta p-1)\) whenever \(\beta p>1\).
