On the generalized Allee–logistic map's tricritical parameter line, the inverse-square population has a universal \(\tfrac32\varepsilon a_\delta^n\log(1/\varepsilon)\) correction through the entire two-sided time window \(n\varepsilon\asymp1\), beyond the known leading pitchfork crossover. The remaining scaled error is uniformly \(O(\varepsilon)\) for any fixed positive initial population and compact positive scaled-time interval.
