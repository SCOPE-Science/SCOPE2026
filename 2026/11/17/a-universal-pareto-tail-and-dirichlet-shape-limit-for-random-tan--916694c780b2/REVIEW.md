# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The geometric area formula, conditioning probability, beta-density local limit, two-large-gap exclusion estimate, and maximum-to-sum asymptotic are all proved with explicit fixed parameter quantifiers. Beta-density positivity and Dirichlet neutrality yield the joint limit. The tail integral yields the sharp moment criterion. Direct gamma-Dirichlet simulation independently corroborates the constants and area formula but is not used in the limiting proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual priority risk.** The 2015 circumscribing-polygon model, the 2018 symmetric Dirichlet extension, and the 2023 full-text gamma and tangent formulas are closely relevant. The examined 2023 article develops large-sample normal limits and extrapolations, not fixed-side-count sharp extreme-area tails or conditional geometric shape. Archival circular-gap theory likewise studies maximal spacings without tangent-polygon area extremes. The available 2015 and 2018 abstracts do not state the proposed fixed-count law; their full texts could not be read, so this is a provisional, not exhaustive, priority assessment. A same-model Dirichlet polygon study could conceivably already include a tail lemma; the present acceptance is based on substantive non-implication from its accessible claims.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result resolves the fixed-sample extreme-area and moment regime for a natural random circumscribed approximation of a circle, explains why almost-sure/central-limit approximation can coexist with an infinite expected area at every finite side count, and supplies an explicit, parameter-uniform tail index and conditional shape limit rather than a routine unboundedness observation. These address a motivated failure mode of finite-sample area estimators.

Closest literature: Xu (2015), Wang–Xu (2018), Schlemm (2014), Wang–Li–Xu (2023). The latter's publicly available full HTML article was examined; the former two were limited to abstracts. No independent literature audit has been performed.
