For uniform-arclength samples on regular polygons, the exact Wendel center-exclusion probability holds at every even order, but at odd orders a new universal crossover appears when the sample count is comparable to the cube of the number of sides: its multiplicative factor is \(R(t)=\int_0^1\cosh(\pi^2t u(1-u))\,du\). The same scaling yields an explicit asymmetric conditional distribution for the boundary position of the unique semicircle-starting point.
