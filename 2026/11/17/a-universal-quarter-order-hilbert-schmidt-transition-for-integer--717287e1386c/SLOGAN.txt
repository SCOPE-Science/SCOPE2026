For every positive integer radial Fock exponent \(m\), an explicit Mittag–Leffler weighted dilation is compact but Hilbert–Schmidt exactly when its endpoint parameter satisfies \(\gamma>1/4\). The same quarter-order boundary governs sub-Gaussian, Gaussian and higher-power Fock spaces, with exact logarithmic and power-law truncated energy coefficients and a critical Hilbert–Schmidt pole.
