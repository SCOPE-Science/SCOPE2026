**Correctness — PASS.** The exact Routh formula reduces the rare event to a thin tube around the Ceva graph. Uniform domination by an integrable \(2/H\) majorant makes the leading tube coefficient rigorous, and the barycentric/auxiliary-angle Jacobians evaluate it as \(3\pi/4\). L1 convergence of the signed deficit and angle densities gives the total-variation marked law. The acute fraction is an explicit integral \(1/\pi\). Independent-intersection and quadrature diagnostics support, but do not replace, the proof.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, provisional priority).** The Routh/Ceva formulas are established foundations. Full texts of Bényi–Ćurgus (arXiv:1112.4813), McConnell (arXiv:1403.0478), and Abboud (arXiv:1403.5652) address deterministic geometric formulas. The closest inspected fixed-Ceva-product result establishes sharp upper envelopes, not the random tube volume or marked angle law. Focused searches for stochastic Routh/Ceva formulations yielded no equivalent identified theorem. A not-indexed historical analogue remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The new invariant is an exact universal rare-concurrency constant for independent side-uniform sampling, accompanied by the geometric distribution of the intersection configuration and its independent signed area defect. This responds to a natural stochastic counterpart of Ceva's classical incidence condition and determines a nonobvious acute auxiliary-angle frequency.

**Limitations and residual risks.** The sampling is one point per designated side; only the leading small-\(\varepsilon\) law is proved. Conditional angles belong to an auxiliary triangle, not the Routh triangle. Historical priority and proof have not undergone independent validation.
