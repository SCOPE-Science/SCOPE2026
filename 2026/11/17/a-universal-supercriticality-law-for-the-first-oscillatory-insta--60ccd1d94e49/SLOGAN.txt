In a sharp nonnegative-competition region, the three-species cyclic Euler–Lotka–Volterra map loses coexistence stability first through a supercritical Neimark–Sacker bifurcation, with exact negative Fourier-normalized coefficient \(\ell_1=-2R_*a^2(1+R_*)\). The criterion itself rules out all low-order resonances, and the bifurcating attracting invariant circle has an explicit square-root amplitude law.
