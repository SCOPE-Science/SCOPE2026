For three independent random lines whose bounded signed offsets have positive variance \(v\) and whose normal directions have continuous density \(g\), the area of the triangle from their pairwise intersections has a universal \(1/t\) tail with coefficient \(6v\int_0^\pi g^2\). Rare large triangles are generated by one nearly parallel pair, with squared-offset size bias and an independent uniform scaled gap; uniform angular sampling uniquely minimizes the coefficient.
