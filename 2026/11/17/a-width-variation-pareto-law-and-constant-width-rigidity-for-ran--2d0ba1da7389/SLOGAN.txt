For two independently sampled normal lines of a smooth strictly convex oval, the probability that their intersection lies farther than \(R\) is asymptotic to \(2R^{-1}\int f(\theta)f(\theta+\pi)|w'(\theta)|\,d\theta\). It has bounded support exactly for constant-width bodies; the near-antipodal angles of rare distant intersections have a complete conditional total-variation limit.
