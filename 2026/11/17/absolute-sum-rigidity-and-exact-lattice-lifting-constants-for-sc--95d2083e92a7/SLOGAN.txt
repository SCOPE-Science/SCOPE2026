For every normalized absolute lattice norm \(\nu\), the optimal projective lifting constant of \(\mathbb R\oplus_\nu\ell_1\) is exactly \(\max_{s+t=1,\ s,t\ge0}1/\nu(s,t)\), and this lattice is semiprojective precisely when \(\nu=\ell_1^2\). Consequently the entire interval of sharp lifting constants \([1,2]\) occurs, although semiprojectivity fails under every nontrivial deformation in this family, however small.
