## Correctness
PASS. The statement uses a finite directed-linear-forest complex with explicit source/target and acyclicity conditions. The one-shortcut link is exactly a boundary simplex. Conditioning on the second shortcut gives a boundary-sphere link with an extra simplex cone when the starts are adjacent, but an additional boundary-sphere cone when nonadjacent. Acyclicity of the resulting arcs rules out any other forbidden combinations. Each cone inclusion is nullhomotopic by its exact dimension and the connectivity of the preceding sphere wedge. This proves the claimed *integral homotopy type*, not merely homology. A self-contained enumerator verifies the exact links for \(n=6,\ldots,12\), with modular boundary checks through \(n=10\).
