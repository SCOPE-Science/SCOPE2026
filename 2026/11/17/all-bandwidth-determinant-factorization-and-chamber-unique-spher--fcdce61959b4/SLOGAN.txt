For minimal \(L^2\)-point spherical harmonic sampling on the classical \(1,3,\ldots,2L-1\) latitude rings, the entire evaluation determinant factors into explicit latitude and pairwise-separation powers, independently of ring rotations. Its logarithm is strictly concave on every height-ordering chamber, giving exactly \((L-1)!\) isolated, nondegenerate local determinant maxima and a uniquely solvable design problem in each chamber.
