# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The incidence Hasse-diagram matching model removes exactly two alternating directed cycles from the independence complex of the subdivided polygon. The fixed-orbit barycentric map is an explicit PL homeomorphism for all finite simplicial group actions. The rotation quotients and shifted reflection-orbit pairings are checked with exact indices and both small degenerate cases. The two invoked wedge/sphere classifications are established in Chari–Joswig (crediting Kozlov) and Jonsson. An independent rooted-forest enumeration corroborates 151 parameter instances and 68 mod-two Betti tables. The proof applies to every integer in the claimed range; finite computations do not establish the infinite statement.

## Originality — PASS relative to examined publications

Lin–Scoville identify an automorphism group but do not compute these subgroup-fixed spaces. Jojić studies shellability of directed-forest complexes, not these dihedral fixed models. Previously indexed pure and strong cycle Morse results use different complexes, so their character computations or fixed spaces do not imply the theorem. Targeted searches for aliases, subgroup fixed points, dihedral reductions, and cycle/path independence models disclosed no exact prior fixed-space classification. This is evidence of noncoverage among inspected works, not absolute publication-priority proof.
