For all 412 nonidentity subgroups of the 336-element Heawood automorphism group, the geometric fixed space of the independence complex has exactly one of six integral homotopy types, including \(S^2\vee S^2\), \(S^2\vee S^1\), and \(S^1\vee S^1\). Exhaustive subgroup generation, elementary collapses, and explicit triangulated-sphere checks establish the complete class-weighted census.
