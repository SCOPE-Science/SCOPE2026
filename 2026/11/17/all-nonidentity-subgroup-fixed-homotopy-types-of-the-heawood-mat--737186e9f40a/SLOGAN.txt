The ordinary Heawood matching complex has exactly seven geometric fixed-point homotopy types across all 412 nonidentity subgroups of its 336-element automorphism group, including a newly identified mixed \(S^1\vee S^2\) class of 21 involution subgroups. The previously determined polarity class \(\bigvee^4 S^2\) is retained as acknowledged prior work, not re-claimed.
