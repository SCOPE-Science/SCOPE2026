Every nonidentity subgroup of the natural \(S_5\)-action on the Petersen induced-forest complex has a geometric fixed space homotopy equivalent to the empty space, a point, \(S^0\), \(S^1\vee S^1\), or \(S^2\vee S^2\). A complete 155-subgroup classification identifies precisely which groups yield each type, including a two-dimensional fixed sphere wedge for double transpositions.
