For all positive axis lengths and all inclusive Euclidean scales, the Rips complexes of the \(2d\) vertices \(\{\pm a_i e_i\}\) have explicit integral sphere-wedge homotopy types determined by a terminating threshold-graph recurrence. Unequal axes can produce simultaneous \(S^1\)- and \(S^2\)-summands, but all positive-dimensional homology disappears once the shortest antipodal pair becomes an edge.
