Every dihedral subgroup of the ordinary six-by-six nonattacking-queen complex has an explicitly classified geometric fixed space: the central half-turn fixes \(\bigvee^7 S^1\), the quarter-turn and diagonal reflections each fix six isolated points, and all remaining nontrivial subgroup types have empty fixed space. This homotopy classification strictly refines the previously known rational symmetry character of a complex with integral two-torsion.
