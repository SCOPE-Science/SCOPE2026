On the \(d\)-dimensional Boolean cube, every individual initial Greenberg–Hastings phase defect becomes exponentially unlikely, yet the total number has mean \((4/3)^d\) and a Poisson approximation with vanishing total-variation error. The complete defect-location field itself becomes indistinguishable from independent rare events, while an immediate primitive temporal three-cycle becomes overwhelmingly unlikely; exact local correlation formulas explain both effects.
