The ordinary quotient of \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_m,K_n)\) by even permutations of target colors is integrally homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(\binom{n-2}{m-2}\) spheres of dimension \(n-m\), for every \(2\le m\le n\) (with the zero-dimensional convention). An explicit double-sheeted quotient-cell structure, an integral unimodular boundary calculation, and fixed-point generation of the alternating group upgrade previously known rational Betti numbers to a complete homotopy classification.
