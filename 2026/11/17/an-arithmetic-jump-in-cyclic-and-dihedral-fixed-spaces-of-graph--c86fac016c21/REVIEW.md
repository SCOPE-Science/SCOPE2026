## Value — PASS
The sharp fixed-space transition at vertex-connectivity three explains why an ambient family with high-dimensional wedge-of-spheres topology contains simple but arithmetically varying geometric fixed loci. The theorem supplies a uniform natural infinite-family classification: cyclic arithmetic determines sphere dimensions without any need to enumerate the ambient exponentially large complexes. At \(n=30\) it produces a circle component; at \(n=210\) a two-sphere component, and the isolated fixed-point count grows with \(\varphi(n)\). This is a structural threshold calculation, not a tautological rewriting of the known disconnected-graph nerve.
