For the complex of graphs that fail three-vertex-connectivity, full cyclic and dihedral fixed-point spaces coincide, and are the fixed-point complex for disconnected graphs plus exactly \(\varphi(n)/2\) isolated Hamiltonian-cycle orbit points. The common core is a sphere of dimension \(\omega(n)-2\) for squarefree composite \(n\), contractible for non-squarefree \(n\), and empty for prime \(n\).
