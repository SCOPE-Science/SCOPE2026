For two unit-edge cliques of unequal sizes \(2\le a\le b\) glued at one vertex, the real Lipschitz-free unit ball has an exact global Euclidean inradius determined by a single concave integer quadratic. At \(b=3a+2\), its origin-centered inball jumps from two touching facets to \(2(b+1)\), and the family of maximum-radius centers collapses from affine dimension \(a+b-1\) to \(a-1\), followed by parity-dependent dimensions.
