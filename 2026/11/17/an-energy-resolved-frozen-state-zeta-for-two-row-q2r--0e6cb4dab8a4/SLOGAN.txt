The two-row periodic Q2R Ising automaton has an exact energy-resolved frozen-state polynomial at every even circumference, a Lucas-number fixed-state count with entropy \(\log\varphi\) per spin, and an exact checkerboard involution pairing energies \(E\) and \(4n-E\). Unlike a simple cubic ladder, its two opposite vertical directions count as separate bonds, producing a distinct four-neighbor stationary-state law.
