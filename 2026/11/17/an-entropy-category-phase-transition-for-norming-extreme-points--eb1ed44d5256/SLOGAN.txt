An explicit non-norm-attaining block-average operator on real \(c_0\) has a norm-attaining second adjoint whose norming extreme sign vectors form a dense \(G_\delta\) of full Hausdorff dimension. Under independent biased signs their probability changes sharply from one to zero at \(c=1/[-\log_2\max\{p,1-p\}]\), and the exact almost-sure \(\ell_\infty/c_0\) quotient norm beyond that threshold is determined by a binary-relative-entropy equation.
