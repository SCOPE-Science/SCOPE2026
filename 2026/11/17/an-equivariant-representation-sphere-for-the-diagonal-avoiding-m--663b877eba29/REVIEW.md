# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The object is precisely the product-cell complex of a relation excluding the constant tuples, with \(n\ge2\) and \(r\ge3\). A nonnegative stochastic-matrix description is equivalent to the cell definition because the row-support intersection is empty. On the doubly centered matrix representation \(W\), the explicit positive, invariant, homogeneous gauge \(F\) has compact polyhedral unit body; continuous mutual inverse maps establish an **equivariant homeomorphism** with the boundary. The radial homeomorphism proves the exact sphere type. The tensor determinant gives the integral orientation character, and the direct-sum decomposition of the permutation matrix module establishes fixed dimensions for every subgroup. The proof uses no finite-to-infinite extrapolation. Independent cellular mod-two checks on eight configurations, 16 exact determinant checks and 28 subgroup tests corroborate it, without supplying independent mathematical review.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual bibliographic risk

The closest primary source is de Silva et al., *Dowker's theorem for higher-order relations*, arXiv:2506.10909 (2025), especially Theorem 4.3.2, Sections 3–6. The paper gives general equivalences for multiway relations, not an explicit normalization of the constant-tuple-complement relation to a representation sphere, its full integral orientation module, or its arbitrary-subgroup fixed-space dimensions. The reviewed Tverberg paper arXiv:2608.02979 treats pairwise-disjoint faces rather than the weaker empty total intersection condition. Searches using deleted-product/weak-deleted-product/diagonal-complement and representation-sphere aliases, plus comparison to indexed mathematical findings, showed no stronger or equivalent result. The case of two factors is a known deleted-product boundary and is not claimed new. An unindexed weak-deleted-product treatment remains the material literature risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A canonical natural family of genuine multiway relations is resolved at the level of **equivariant homeomorphism**, not merely Betti numbers. The exact integral determinant character and all subgroup fixed-space dimensions reveal symmetry constraints invisible to the general Dowker equivalence; the arbitrary-subgroup orbit-count law provides a reusable calculation with no enumeration of the face poset. The assertion supplies a structured nontrivial test family for higher-order relation topology and distinguishes total-intersection avoidance from classical pairwise deletion.

## Scientific limitations

Only the complement of constant tuples is classified. No assertion is made about all multiway relations, pairwise-deleted products, integral homology of arbitrary geometric quotients, or a novel two-factor deleted-product sphere theorem. This research evaluation is not independent validation; originality has a residual risk of unindexed equivalent treatments.
