The diagonal-avoiding \(r\)-way relational product on \(n\) labels is explicitly \(S_n\times S_r\)-equivariantly homeomorphic to the sphere of \(U_n\otimes U_r\), for all \(n\ge2\), \(r\ge3\). Its integral orientation character and the sphere dimension of every subgroup fixed space follow from a single polyhedral normalization and three orbit counts.
