Four independent sphere-uniform points conditioned to span a tetrahedron of vanishing Euclidean volume admit an exact rejection representation whose proposal has a uniform supporting-plane height, four independent uniform circle angles, and a uniform rescaled signed volume. The proposal tends to the true conditional joint law in total variation with the sharp \(\sqrt t\) coefficient \(\frac{\sqrt6}{3}\frac{\Gamma(1/4)}{\Gamma(3/4)^3}\), and the volume tail is \(3\pi t-\pi\sqrt6\frac{\Gamma(1/4)}{\Gamma(3/4)^3}t^{3/2}+o(t^{3/2})\).
