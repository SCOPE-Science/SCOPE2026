For a hub-started rotor walk on a bouquet of \(k\) triangles with a prescribed cyclic hub scan, the last initially defective triangle determines the **exact first-recurrence time**; its labelled distribution is \(3^k+\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}3^{k-1-j}z(z^4+3z^6)^j\). The maximum is \(6k-5\), but the typical recurrence clock is asymptotically Gaussian about \(\frac{11}{2}k\).
