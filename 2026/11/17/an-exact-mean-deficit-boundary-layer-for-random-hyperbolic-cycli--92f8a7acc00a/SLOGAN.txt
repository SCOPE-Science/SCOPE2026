For three uniform points on an expanding hyperbolic circle, the mean deficit of their geodesic triangle's inradius from the ideal-triangle value is \(\bigl(3-12\operatorname{arctanh}(1/\sqrt3)/(\pi\sqrt3)\bigr)e^{-R}+o(e^{-R})\), even though a typical deficit is of order \(e^{-2R}\). Deficit-weighted triangles concentrate on a single angular gap of order \(e^{-R}\), with an explicit limiting profile and independent uniform residual angle.
