The two-step, two-site traveling waves of elementary binary Rule 6 form a positive-entropy finite-type shift with exact spatial zeta function \(1/[(1-z)(1+z)^2(1-2z+2z^2-2z^3)]\). Their number on an indexed ring grows as \(\alpha^n\), with \(\alpha\approx1.543689\), and every such wave is temporally periodic.
