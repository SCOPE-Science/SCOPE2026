For every pair of oppositely placed, half-step-staggered regular latitude rings, the entire spherical-cap uncovered-area distribution is an exact single integral. At the previously known covering-optimal height, the remaining area closes quadratically with the sharp all-order coefficient \(n(1+\cos(\pi/n))/(\pi\cos(\pi/n)\sin(\pi/n))\), yielding an exact independent-probe extreme-value scale; earlier covering-radius and isolated four-sided results are credited rather than reclaimed.
