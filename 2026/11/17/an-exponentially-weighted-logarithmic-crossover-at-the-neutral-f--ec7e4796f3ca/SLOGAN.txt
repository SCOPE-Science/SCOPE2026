For the symmetric sigmoid interval map with the gain tuned once per experiment to \(a_N=8+\theta/N\), the inverse-squared amplitude after \(N\) iterates has the exact two-order asymptotic \(\frac{64}{3}N\Phi(\theta)+\frac{48}{5}e^{-\theta/2}\log N+O(1)\), where \(\Phi(\theta)=(1-e^{-\theta/2})/(\theta/2)\) with continuous value one at zero. The fixed-critical logarithm was known; the new feature is its explicit exponential reweighting across the critical finite-horizon transition.
