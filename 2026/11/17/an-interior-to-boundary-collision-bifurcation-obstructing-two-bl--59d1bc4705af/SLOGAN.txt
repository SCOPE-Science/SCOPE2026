The jointly unramified finite Blaschke pair \(\bigl(z^2,((z-a)/(1-az))^3\bigr)\) is noninjective for every \(0<a<1\): its one interior collision becomes a double boundary collision at \(a=2-\sqrt3\), then splits into two distinct boundary pairs. This gives a complete, sharp collision-fiber classification and rules out uniform similarity testing by that pair.
