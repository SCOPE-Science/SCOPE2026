On \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge3\), inserting a single bond within the first shore converts exactly \(2\binom{a-2}{a/2-1}\binom{b}{b/2}\) fixed majority configurations into half as many primitive two-cycles whenever both shores have even size, without changing the total recurrent-state population. At a first-shore size of four and odd opposite-shore size, the same bond instead creates exactly \(2\binom{b}{(b-1)/2}\) new fixed configurations.
