For synchronous tie-retaining binary majority on \(K_{n,n}\) with a matching of \(k\) edges removed and odd \(n\ge3\), there is exactly one primitive temporal two-cycle unless \(k=n-1\), when there are exactly \(1+3\binom{n-1}{(n-1)/2}\). Restoring a single deleted edge of an odd crown graph creates exponentially many oscillations, while restoring a second eliminates every additional two-cycle.
