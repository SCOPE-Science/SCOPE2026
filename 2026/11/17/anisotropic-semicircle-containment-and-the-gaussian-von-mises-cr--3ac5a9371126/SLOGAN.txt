For circular points with angular density \(f_\eta(\theta)=(1+\eta\cos\theta)/(2\pi)\), conditioning on an enclosing disk smaller than the unit disk produces a sharp anisotropy window at \(\eta\asymp1/n\): its center direction crosses from a von Mises law to Gaussian localization along one ray. The rare-event probability and joint center-orientation/arc-gap marks have explicit Bessel, Gaussian and exponential limits, with total variation convergence for the marked angle–gap variables.
