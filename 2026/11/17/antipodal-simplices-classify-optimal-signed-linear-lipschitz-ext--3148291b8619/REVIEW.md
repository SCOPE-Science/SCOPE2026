The dichotomy between a unique odd-cycle signed barycenter and an \((m-1)\)-simplex for even cycles is unbounded in dimension. It proves nonnegative weights are *forced* among all minimax signed affine functionals, so it is not limited to assuming probabilistic projections; the extension-radius threshold determines when the family expands to full dimension.
