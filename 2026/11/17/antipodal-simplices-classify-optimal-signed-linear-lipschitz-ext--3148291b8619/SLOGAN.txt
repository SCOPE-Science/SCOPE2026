For real-linear Lipschitz extensions from the graph-cycle metric \(C_n\) to one equidistant new point, the previously known transitivity bound has a rigid signed equality set: the uniform coefficients are unique for odd \(n\), whereas even \(n=2m\) has exactly the \((m-1)\)-simplex of nonnegative antipodally paired coefficients. At the minimal threshold radius this simplex or singleton gives all norm-optimal operators; above the minimax radius the optimal set becomes a full-dimensional polytope.
