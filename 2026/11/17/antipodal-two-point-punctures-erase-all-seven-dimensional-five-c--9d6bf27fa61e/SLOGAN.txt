Deleting an antipodal pair from the binary five-cube at inclusive Hamming Rips radius three leaves exactly a fourth sphere: \(\operatorname{VR}_{\le3}(Q_5\setminus\{p,\overline p\})\simeq S^4\). The two removals preserve integral fourth homology but successively eliminate five primitive seventh-dimensional classes each, exhausting the ten classes of the full complex.
