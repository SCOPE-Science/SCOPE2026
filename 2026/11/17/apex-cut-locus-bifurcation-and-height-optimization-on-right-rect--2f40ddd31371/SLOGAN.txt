On every nonsquare right rectangular pyramid the apex cut locus is an explicit five-edge tree with two trivalent nodes, and its two farthest surface points are exactly those nodes; the square case merges them into one four-valent node. At fixed rectangular base, the cut-locus length has a unique optimal positive pyramid height precisely when the long-to-short base half-side ratio exceeds \(\sqrt3\), with exact minimizing height and minimum length.
