For a two-rate Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup on complex \(L^p\), a rational rate ratio gives a periodic complex-time boundedness region whose near-axis vertical density is linear, while an irrational ratio destroys every nonzero imaginary period and makes that density quadratic. The sharp far-imaginary-axis contact constant is \(\pi^2\lambda(\beta/\alpha)/(2k_p\alpha\beta)\), linking the semigroup boundary to the classical Lagrange approximation constant.
