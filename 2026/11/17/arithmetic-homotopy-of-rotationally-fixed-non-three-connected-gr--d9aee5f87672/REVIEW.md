# Scientific review — fixed non-three-connected graph complexes

## Correctness

**PASS.** Rotational edge orbits are exactly the undirected step classes, and every such orbit is stable under reflection. The full group-fixed spaces therefore coincide and have an explicit PL orbit-mass model. Connectedness of a circulant is the gcd criterion; the established Watkins vertex-transitive lower bound upgrades every connected circulant with at least two step classes (minimum degree three) to three-vertex-connectivity. A one-step connected circulant is a cycle. These exhaustive alternatives prove the precise face criterion, including the antipodal even case. The prime-divisor simplex cover has a boundary-simplex nerve precisely for squarefree composite integers. The disjoint unit steps yield the stated integral homology and minimality at thirty. An independent graph-deletion verifier covered 6,117 step sets at orders four through twenty-two; its finite results do not replace the universal proof.

## Originality

**PASS, relative to inspected sources and qualified by the risk below.** Babson–Björner–Linusson–Shareshian–Welker (1997/1999) define the graph-connectivity complexes, and Jonsson (2003) computes the **ambient** homotopy type for the not-three-connected graph complex. Neither inspected primary full text gives the entire geometric rotation/dihedral fixed-space formula. Watkins (1970) supplies the graph-connectivity input but not the topological fixed-space classification. Search comparisons for cyclic circulants, complete-graph three-connectivity complexes and fixed spaces did not identify an implication covering the asserted all-parameter arithmetic classification. The chapter *Dihedral Variants of k-connected Graphs* in Jonsson's 2008 book was found in a table of contents but not read, so an overlapping alternate formulation is an explicit risk, not a claimed negative full-text result. A failed search does not prove priority.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** This is a uniform fixed-point homotopy classification of a classical monotone graph complex, not a single small enumeration. It exhibits an arithmetic split between squarefree and repeated-prime orders, unbounded fixed-sphere dimensions along squarefree products of sufficiently many primes, and the sharp first mixed-degree integral homology at thirty vertices. The relation between rotation-fixed and dihedral-fixed spaces is a geometric equality, not only a character equality, and ambient wedge-of-sphere results do not supply it.

## Limitations and closer literature

The full geometric fixed spaces of the **whole** rotation and dihedral groups are classified; arbitrary proper subgroup fixed spaces and quotient spaces are not. Full text was inspected for the 1997 graph-connectivity preprint and Jonsson's 2002/2003 preprint. The potentially related 2008 book chapter and the 1970 original Watkins paper could not be obtained as full text; the available Watkins theorem statement is independently documented. The fixed-space result has not undergone an independent external review.
