The cyclic- and full-dihedral geometric fixed-point spaces of the non-three-connected graph complex on \(n\ge4\) vertices coincide, and their homotopy types are determined by squarefreeness, prime-divisor count and Euler's totient. The first fixed space with nonzero reduced integral homology in two distinct degrees occurs at \(n=30\), with ranks four in degree zero and one in degree one.
