For the nonsofic binary frequency shift at rational parameter \(k/(k+1)\), the exact dynamical zeta function is \((1-z^{k+1})/((1-z)^2(1-2z^{k+1}))\), but its uniform periodic-point probabilities do not converge. They approach the unique maximal-entropy measure along periods divisible by \(k+1\), and the equal mixture of the two fixed-point atoms at every other period.
