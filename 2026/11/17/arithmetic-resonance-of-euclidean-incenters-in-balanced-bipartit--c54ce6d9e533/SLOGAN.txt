For balanced even-sided complete-bipartite snowflake metrics, the canonical Euclidean inball has radius \(\bigl[m(t^2+(2-t)^2)/4\bigr]^{-1/2}\), and its incenter is unique exactly when \(t=2v/(u+v)\) for integers \(1\le u<v\le m/2\). At these rational resonances additional facets touch the ball, and the otherwise nondegenerate interval of optimal centers collapses with a precisely determined quadratic width law.
