# Scientific review: Vámos independence-complex symmetry

## Correctness — PASS
The complete two-skeleton and five deleted four-facets give a canonical equivariant relative chain sequence. Each missing facet is hit integrally by the boundary of a five-element simplex, because missing facets have pairwise intersections of size at most two. Consequently top homology is an integral rank-thirty kernel in the fourth exterior power of the augmentation lattice. The full order-sixty-four automorphism group follows from the intrinsic twin pairs and is separately checked by a full eight-element permutation census. Exact signed Lefschetz traces, independently derived exterior-power-minus-oriented-facet traces, and complete character inner products agree for every group element. Rational quotient cohomology follows from finite-group invariant cochains; a separate invariant-chain calculation reproduces the two dihedral subgroup dimensions.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources
Merino and collaborators' 2012 Vámos example evaluates the Tutte polynomial, which together with standard matroid shellability fixes the nonequivariant homotopy rank but not the group representation. Moci–Pezzoli (2020) develop general equivariant matroid-homology correspondences but do not evaluate this particular character. Karn–Nasr–Proudfoot–Vecchi evaluate an equivariant Kazhdan–Lusztig coefficient for the same matroid; it is a **different** 33-dimensional representation and does not imply this 30-dimensional homology character. The 25 multiplicities and distinct quotients were not located in the inspected accessible literature. Unindexed equivalent calculations remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The two order-eight symmetry factors are naturally distinguished by whether their four ground points lie in two or three circuit-hyperplanes. Their rational quotient-cohomology difference is therefore a structural distinction, not an arbitrary subgroup sample. The explicit integral lattice kernel and character decomposition provide information not recovered from the matroid's usual enumerative invariants.

## Limits
The theorem does not calculate quotient integral torsion, establish quotient contractibility, or split the integral exact sequence equivariantly. Established shellability and nonequivariant homotopy conclusions are not advanced as novel. Independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
