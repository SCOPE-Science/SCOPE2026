On every complete bipartite graph with three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation, threshold one on the left shore and threshold two on the right, all trajectories become periodic within a sharp five steps; primitive three-cycles first appear at shore sizes \((6,3)\), while primitive four-cycles already appear at \((4,2)\). Exact all-size formulas count both recurrent populations and every first-entry-time/period class.
