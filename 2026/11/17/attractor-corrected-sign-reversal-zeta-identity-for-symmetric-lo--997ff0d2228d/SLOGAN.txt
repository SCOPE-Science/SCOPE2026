Sign reversal of a symmetric increasing Lorenz map has an exact all-period zeta correction governed by its attracting odd-period cycles: \(\zeta_{-F}/\zeta_F=((1+t)/(1-t))\prod_{\ell\ \mathrm{odd}}((1-t^\ell)/(1+t^\ell))^{b_\ell}\) under noncritical, nonneutral hypotheses. Real-analytic examples make the fixed-point difference \(a_1(-F)-a_1(F)=2-4k\) arbitrarily negative, unlike the uniformly expanding case.
