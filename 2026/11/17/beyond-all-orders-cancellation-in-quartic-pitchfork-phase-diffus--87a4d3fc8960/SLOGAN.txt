In the stationary quartic noisy-pitchfork model with linear phase coupling, the Kramers-scale phase variance is \(\sqrt2\pi(c^2/g)e^{R^2/(4gT)}\) with no correction at any algebraic order as \(R/\sqrt{gT}\to+\infty\). The two nontrivial Laplace series for the barrier integral and the equilibrium partition function agree term by term, so every inverse-power correction cancels; the known exact correlation-time integral is credited rather than claimed as new.
