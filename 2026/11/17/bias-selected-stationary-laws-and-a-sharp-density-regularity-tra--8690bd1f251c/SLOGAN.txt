Biasing the inverse branches of the random doubling map creates a sharp trichotomy: below the expansion threshold the unique stationary law is biased Bernoulli, while above it absolutely continuous, singular-continuous, and atomic stationary laws coexist. The canonical absolutely continuous law has an exact sharp \(L^s\) criterion, and at its critical boundedness threshold it lies in every finite \(L^s\) but not \(L^\infty\).
