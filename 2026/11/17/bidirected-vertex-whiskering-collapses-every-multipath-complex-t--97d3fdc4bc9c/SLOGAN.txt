Bidirecting exactly one new pendant edge at every vertex of **any** finite loopless digraph forces its multipath complex to collapse simplicially onto the boundary of an \(n\)-dimensional cross-polytope, hence onto \(S^{n-1}\), where \(n\) is the core vertex count. The collapse is explicit and remains valid for branching, cycles and antiparallel core arcs; core automorphisms act on the top integral homology by their permutation sign.
