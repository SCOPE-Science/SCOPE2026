For every binary linear translation code with no word of Hamming weight two, the quotient of the hypercube's open-neighborhood complex has one or two connected components, each with fundamental group exactly the even subcode and with explicit free integral second homology. The sharp weight-two obstruction and binary simplex-code family generate first-homology torsion \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^r\) of unbounded rank despite the simply connected sphere-wedge components upstairs.
