For every \(r\ge2\), the geometric fixed space of the bipartite-graph complex on \(\mathbb F_2^r\) under all translations is the binary affine-hyperplane complex, naturally under \(\operatorname{GL}(r,2)\), and is homotopy equivalent to a bouquet of \((r-1)\)-spheres. Exact sphere counts begin \(1,6,91,2820,177661\) and are determined by an explicit Gaussian-binomial Möbius recurrence.
