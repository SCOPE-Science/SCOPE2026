Deleting a distance-two edge of the squared cycle \(C_n^2\) creates exactly one new primitive majority two-cycle for every \(n\equiv3\pmod4\), whereas deleting a nearest-neighbor edge creates none in those sizes. For every \(n\ge7\), the complete two-cycle counts for both single-bond defects are explicit, twelve-periodic and bounded by four, established by exact transfer matrices and a finite Cayley–Hamilton certificate.
