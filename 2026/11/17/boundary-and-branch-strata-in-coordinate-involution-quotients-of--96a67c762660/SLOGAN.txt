Every nontrivial coordinate sign-flip on the real moment-angle surface of a polygon has one of four explicitly classified orbit-surface types: bordered orientable, branched closed orientable, freely covered orientable, or nonorientable. Remarkably, the fixed-circle and isolated-branch-point cases yield exactly the same orientable genus \(1+(n-5)2^{n-4}\) for every \(n\ge3\).
