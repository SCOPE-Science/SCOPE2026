The area-normalized deficit of the Archimedean \(3/5\) centroid-depth law for shrinking tangent-parallel caps of a \(C^4\) convex plane curve recovers its signed equiaffine curvature with an explicit universal coefficient. Elliptic, parabolic and hyperbolic conic arcs lie respectively below, exactly on and above the \(3/5\) ratio throughout their admissible local cap ranges.
