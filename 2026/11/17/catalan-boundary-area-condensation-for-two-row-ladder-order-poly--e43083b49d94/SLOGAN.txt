For the ordinary Euclidean order polytope of the two-row ladder \([2]\times[n]\), the exact surface-area/volume ratio is \(2n(2+n\sqrt2)\), and every vertical cover facet has area proportional to \(C_{i-1}C_{n-i}\). Within those \(n\) facets, area condenses onto the two ends with a Catalan power-law tail, while individual bulk facets are only of relative order \(n^{-3/2}\).
