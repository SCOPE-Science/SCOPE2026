Every distance-weighted hyperplane cut of two compactly supported radial probability laws is maximized by a hyperplane through their common center, for any continuous nonnegative nondecreasing Euclidean distance weight. For two uniform points on the unit circle, the complete Euclidean chord-weighted cut profile is \(2\sqrt{1-t^2}/\pi\), and on the unit sphere in \(\mathbb R^d\) the sharp fraction is \(1/2+1/(2\pi d)+1/(2\pi d^2)+O(d^{-3})\).
