For the join of \(r\ge4\) connected nonbipartite one-dimensional graph complexes, simplicial \(r\)-tupling has exactly \((T_r+1)/2\) connected components, where \(T_r\) is the central trinomial coefficient; every component is an explicitly counted wedge of circles. In particular, joins of odd polygon boundaries give combinatorial spheres whose higher tuplings have rapidly growing, completely determined free fundamental groups.
