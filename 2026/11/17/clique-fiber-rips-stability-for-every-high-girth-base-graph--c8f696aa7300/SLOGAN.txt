For every finite connected graph of girth at least seven and every clique fiber of width \(m\ge3\), the scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of their Cartesian product collapses onto the base graph, no matter how it branches or how many long cycles intersect. Exactly \((m-1)|E(H)|\) primitive first-homology classes disappear at that scale, even though the corresponding clique-fiber Čech complex retains additional two-spheres.
