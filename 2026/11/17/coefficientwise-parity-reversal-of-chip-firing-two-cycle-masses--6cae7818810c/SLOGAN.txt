For synchronous chip-firing on the cubic prism and its Möbius-twisted ladder, the signed difference in numbers of primitive two-orbits is not merely parity-controlled in total: its coefficient at every chip mass \(3n\le k\le7n\) is strictly positive for even \(n\) and strictly negative for odd \(n\). An exact finite binomial-sum polynomial gives all mass-by-mass differences and leaves the central discrepancy equal to \(4\) or \(-2\), independently of ring size.
