On every labelled two-row periodic HPP lattice gas of circumference \(n\ge3\), all one-step fixed microstates admit an exact joint polynomial by particle occupancy and head-on collision columns, whose total is \(5^n+2\cdot4^n+11\cdot3^n+2\cdot2^n\). Their collision columns always occur in even numbers, and their particle-count and collision-count fluctuations are asymptotically independent Gaussians despite exact finite-size dynamical constraints.
