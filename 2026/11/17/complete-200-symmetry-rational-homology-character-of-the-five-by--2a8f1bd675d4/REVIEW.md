# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The argument proves the exact fixed-face trace identity using orientation signs and the reduced Hopf trace theorem; the necessary single-degree homology concentration is supplied by a separately published, matching-object discrete-Morse proof. Complete orbit-graph enumeration covers all 200 distinct transformations. Four direct oriented-simplex computations independently agree with the orbit method. The character sum gives the invariant dimensions exactly.

## Originality
**PASS relative to inspected sources.** Jonsson's historical theorem concerns the Euler characteristic, the 2007 paper treats open/cylindrical homotopy types, and the published 2026 hard-square theorem addresses ordinary homotopy but explicitly not the symmetry character. The closely related scale-three Rips result has an identical translation restriction but is a different complex and does not cover the full toroidal action. Earlier accessible scientific claims were searched for the five-by-five object, its character, translations and reflection aliases; no overlapping claim was found. The possibility of equivalent older or unindexed results and of a hidden equivariant comparison to the Rips model remains open.

## Value
**PASS.** The result determines a complete and natural 200-element rational representation, rather than another nonequivariant sphere count. It shows one translation-invariant class is eliminated by extra grid symmetries, differentiating translation and full-symmetry quotients, and supplies complete reproducible character values as a basis for future equivariant work.

## Risks and limits
No integral representation or general toroidal-grid formula is proved. Ordinary homotopy is an explicit published premise, not a novel calculation here. The full-text historical search is not exhaustive and the scale-three Rips coincidence deserves further investigation before claiming absolute priority.
