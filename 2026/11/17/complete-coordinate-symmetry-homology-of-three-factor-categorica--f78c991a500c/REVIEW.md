# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The Mayer–Vietoris suspension is natural; both cover pieces are contractible, with the second established by a full-simplex nerve. A partition of invariant cross-covered subsets into six disjoint classes establishes every signed Lefschetz contribution for arbitrary permutations, including fixed-point-free cases. The group representation follows by standard rational character theory and a Cauchy decomposition. Independent exact computations and the previously established nonequivariant homology concentration corroborate the proof. Risk: rational coefficients do not imply integral representation splitting.

**Originality: PASS against inspected sources.** Antolín Camarena and Carnero Bravo, arXiv:2307.12401, Proposition 25, establish the ordinary sphere wedge but not the complete \(S_m\times S_n\) rational character. Goyal, Shukla and Singh, arXiv:1905.06926, Proposition 3.2, classify a different two-factor product. A related paper on path-factor graph products supplies no direct implication. Indexed comparisons of equivalent character, fixed-pair, and representation descriptions did not reveal a dominating published result. Risk: unindexed equivariant literature could overlap.

**Value: PASS.** The exact four-summand module classification is a natural equivariant refinement valid over an infinite two-parameter family. It determines traces of all coordinate permutations and proves the new uniform one-dimensional invariant and quotient-homology phenomenon, neither inferable from the nonequivariant Betti number. The result does not claim a full automorphism-group representation.
