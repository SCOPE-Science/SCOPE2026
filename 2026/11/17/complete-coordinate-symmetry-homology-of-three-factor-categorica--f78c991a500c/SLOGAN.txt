For every \(m,n\ge2\), the complete rational \(S_m\times S_n\) action on \(\widetilde H_3(\operatorname{Ind}(K_2\times K_m\times K_n);\mathbb Q)\) has character \(\tfrac12(f-1)(h-1)(fh-2)+2st\), where \(f,h\) count fixed letters and \(s,t\) count two-cycles. The invariant subspace is always one-dimensional, a symmetry refinement of the previously known sphere-wedge homotopy type.
