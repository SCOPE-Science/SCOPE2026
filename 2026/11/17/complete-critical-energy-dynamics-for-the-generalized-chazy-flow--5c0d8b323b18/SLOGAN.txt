On the zero-energy surface of the generalized Chazy flow with integer \(q\ge2\), every nonstationary trajectory reduces exactly to \(\dot r=c-r^{2q+1}/(2q+1)\), approaches an equilibrium in forward physical time, and escapes to infinity at a finite past time. Convergence is exponentially sharp at a nonzero limiting equilibrium and obeys \(|x(t)|\sim((2q+1)/(2q))^{1/q}t^{-1/q}\) at the origin.
