On every nonparallelogram trapezoid, all nonnegative rational generalized barycentric systems with numerator degree at most three and a shared positive affine denominator form exactly one projective interval; its endpoints are explicit non-Wachspress cubic systems. The Wachspress coordinates are the unique quadratic-numerator point, while the sharp positivity cone is \(A\ge0\), \(A+(2a-b)B\ge0\) in affine-normalized vertices \((0,0),(a,0),(b,1),(0,1)\).
