The ordinary Mycielskian of every cycle of length \(n\ge4\) has a complete explicit rational dihedral homology character: rotations depend on \(\gcd(n,k)\), and reflections on a six-periodic path recurrence. Its full dihedral quotient retains positive-degree rational homology precisely when \(n\equiv1,11\pmod{12}\), a symmetry effect invisible to the previously known sphere-wedge homotopy type.
