The canonical full-column Laplacian simplex of every multi-blade clique windmill \(K_1\vee(mK_s)\), \(m,s\ge2\), has exactly three strictly ordered interior dihedral angles; precisely the \(m\binom{s}{2}\) within-blade facet pairs are obtuse. Those obtuse pairs reconstruct the windmill's hub and blades exactly, while its two acute angle classes approach right angles at different rates \(m^{-1}\) and \(m^{-2}\) for fixed \(s\).
