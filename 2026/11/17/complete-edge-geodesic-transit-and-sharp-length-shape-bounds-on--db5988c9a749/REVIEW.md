# Mathematical review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The event is determined by comparing the finitely many simple intrinsic paths between two arclength-uniform points. When both endpoints lie on a longer edge, the two transit regions are right triangles; on distinct longer edges, they are disjoint rectangles in their coordinate product. The area sum yields the claimed exact probability. Uniform measure on those regions yields the conditional Dirichlet/independent-uniform laws. Cauchy–Schwarz and positivity give exact sharpness, equality and nonattainment. The standalone independent candidate-path checker and numerical quadrature corroborate, not replace, the proof.

## Originality

**PASS, provisional historical priority.** Kennedy–Kurasov–Malenová–Mugnolo (arXiv:1504.01962) examine pumpkin-graph Laplacian spectral gaps. Garijo–Márquez–Silveira (arXiv:2103.11676) give a general method for continuous average distance, including shortest-path partitions, but their standing assumption that each edge itself be shortest between its endpoints fails for nonminimal parallel edges (subdivision could still allow methodological transfer). Baptista–Kennedy–Mugnolo (arXiv:2312.04952) study a distinct mean-distance invariant. Durón–Kravitz–Brio (DOI 10.3390/dynamics5020016) study spectral-energy edge centrality. These related objects and methods do not state the combined complete-edge transit formula, sharp shape comparison and conditional location law. A broader integration/differentiation of existing path-partition methods might make the scalar formula accessible; undiscovered coverage remains possible.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** Full junction-to-junction use of a corridor is a natural geometric alternative to finite-vertex betweenness and to merely touching a portion of an edge. The complete all-length, all-edge classification for parallel metric graphs identifies the sole through-route and sharp extremal usage under resource constraints, together with the fine law of successful endpoint locations. The theorem is fully analytic for every number of edges, not a finite census or arbitrary isolated instance.

## Limitations and closely related results

The unique-shortest-edge hypothesis is essential. Equal shortest links, general networks with interior junctions, and partial edge passage are not covered. The 2021 general continuous-mean-distance method provides the strongest relevant antecedent and must not be described as a negative match merely because it computes a different scalar. Priority is provisional; numerical agreement is not independent proof.
