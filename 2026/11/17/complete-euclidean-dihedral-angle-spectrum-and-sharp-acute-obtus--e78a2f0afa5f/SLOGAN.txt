The canonical Euclidean Laplacian simplex of every cycle has exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) ridge-angle levels, with an explicit rational cosine formula and exact multiplicities. All dihedral angles are acute for \(n=3,4\), but acute and obtuse angles coexist for every \(n\ge5\); a uniform ridge has a nondegenerate limiting angle law with approximately 48.07% obtuse angles.
