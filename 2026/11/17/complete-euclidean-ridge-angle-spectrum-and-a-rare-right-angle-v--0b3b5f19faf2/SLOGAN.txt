For the symmetric edge polytope of \(K_{2,q}\), every ridge angle belongs to an explicit binomially weighted spectrum with \(2^{q+1}\) right angles and \(q2^{q-1}\) other angles, all obtuse when \(q\ge3\). A uniformly sampled ridge has exterior angle \(2/\sqrt q\) with chi-square-order fluctuations, yet its leading angular variance is controlled by the vanishing fraction of right-angle ridges.
