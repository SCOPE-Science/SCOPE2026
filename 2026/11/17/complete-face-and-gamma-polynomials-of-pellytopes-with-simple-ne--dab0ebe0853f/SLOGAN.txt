For every \(n\)-dimensional pellytope, the complete face polynomial obeys \(F_n=(1+2z)F_{n-1}+z(1+z)F_{n-2}\), and its \(\gamma\)-coefficients are exactly the matching numbers \(\binom{n-j}{j}\) of the \(n\)-vertex path. Its combinatorial \(h\)-polynomial has \(n\) simple strictly negative real zeros, explicitly determined by path-adjacency cosines.
