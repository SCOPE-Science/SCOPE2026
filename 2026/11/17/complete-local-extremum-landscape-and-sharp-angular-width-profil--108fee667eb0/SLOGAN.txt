The regular \(n\)-permutohedron has exactly \(2^n-2\) strict local-minimum projection directions, one per proper nonempty vertex-label cut, but only the \(2n\) singleton cuts are globally narrowest; its exactly \(n!\) local maxima are all global. Every local minimum has an explicit, attained sharp angular-width envelope \(w_{n,t}\cos\theta+\gamma_{n,t}\sin\theta\) on a uniform initial angular interval.
