For the scaled arctangent position--momentum commutator, the squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm is an explicit positive Laplace transform, strictly completely monotone in the squared scale. It grows as \(\pi^2\log(1/s)\) when \(s\downarrow0\), decays as \(\pi/(8s^2)\) for large \(s\), and the effective spectral rank on the known positive range grows as \(2\pi s^2\).
