On the first nondegenerate even-by-even square torus, Langton's ant partitions all \(2^{16}\cdot16\cdot4\) states into exactly 46,976 primitive temporal cycles, all with lengths divisible by 16 and at most 512. Under the 128 natural geometric transformations, with color complementation on reflections, these become exactly 376 symmetry types; just two types attain the maximum period.
