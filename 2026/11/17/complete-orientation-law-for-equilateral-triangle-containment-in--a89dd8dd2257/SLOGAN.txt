The maximum scale of a translated equilateral triangle fitting in a rectangle has an exact, all-aspect-ratio distribution under uniform random orientation: its similarity-factor polygon is a cyclic dodecagon or a regular hexagon. At fixed container area, the square uniquely maximizes the mean squared best-fit scale, yet the largest single achievable scale is maximized instead by rectangles of aspect ratio \(2/\sqrt3\) or its reciprocal.
