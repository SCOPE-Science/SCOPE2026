# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact face predicate excludes every directed cycle and enforces incoming/outgoing degree at most one. All \(714\) nonempty simplices are enumerated. A complete directed face-poset incidence acyclicity check proves the \(336\) discrete-Morse pairs valid. Integer Morse cancellation gives zero top differential and a \(5\times24\) differential with a \(+1\) unit maximal minor, proving the stated integral homology concentration. A separate exact top-boundary calculation yields a twelve-dimensional degree-four rational cycle space. Signed traces on its explicit basis and the full-group Lefschetz calculation derive the two characters, and exact irreducible orthogonality checks derive the decompositions. The actual standalone source reproduces all certificates. This fixed finite enumeration is exhaustive; it makes no unproved inference for larger graphs.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The earlier exact nonequivariant \(K_{3,3}\) multipath classification already determines the Betti ranks \(19\) and \(12\); the equivariant representations and the rational quotient consequence are distinct statements and are not implied by those ranks. Caputi–Collari–Smith's bidirected-path/polygon theorem (arXiv:2601.05670), Deshpande–Sawant's directed-linear-forest results (arXiv:2602.14936) and Caputi–Collari–Di Trani–Smith's one-way complete-bipartite calculations treat nonidentical families or hypotheses and do not state the two \(72\)-element group characters. The ordinary discrete-Morse matching complex of \(K_{3,3}\) has a different definition and homology, so its symmetry representation does not cover this one. Exact conceptual synonyms, group-theoretic equivalents and published topological records were compared; no covering assertion was identified. Residual risk: unindexed computations or a previously unnoticed stronger equivariant theorem.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete homology character is natural for a highly symmetric first balanced complete-bipartite directed-cycle example. Its decomposition exposes seven and five multiplicity-one summands and one full-group invariant in degree three, which no sphere count or nonequivariant classification can recover. The rational quotient-homology consequence supplies a concrete new structural distinction between the object and its symmetry quotient; it is a nontrivial exact answer, not a routine parameter substitution.

## Scientific limits
The theorem is rational-equivariant and for one graph. No integral equivariant splitting, quotient homotopy equivalence or theorem for all bicliques is claimed. The calculations were checked within the same research process only, not independently.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
