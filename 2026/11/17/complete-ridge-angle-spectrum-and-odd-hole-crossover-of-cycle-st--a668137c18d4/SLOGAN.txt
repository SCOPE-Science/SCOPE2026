The complete Euclidean ridge-angle spectrum of every odd-cycle stable-set polytope \(\operatorname{STAB}(C_n)\), \(n\ge5\) odd, has only a right angle and two distinct obtuse angles, with exact multiplicities and an exceptional pentagonal ridge deficit. The largest angle changes type sharply between \(n=7\) and \(n=9\), while a rare family of strictly obtuse angles approaches a right angle as \(n\to\infty\).
