# Complete three-family antidictionary of proportional-run coded shifts

## Finding
For each integer \(k\ge1\), let \(X_k\) be the two-sided subshift whose finite language consists of all factors of finite concatenations of the blocks \(0^m1^{km}\), \(m\ge1\). Let \(\mathcal M_k\) be its **minimal forbidden words**: the finite binary words not in its language for which every proper contiguous factor is in the language.

The entire minimal forbidden set is the following **disjoint union**, with all displayed indices positive integers:
\[
\mathcal M_k=\{01^r0:k\nmid r\}
\;\sqcup\;\{0^{m+1}1^{km}0:m\ge1\}
\;\sqcup\;\{10^m1^{km+1}:m\ge1\}.
\]
The first family is empty when \(k=1\). In particular, if \(a_k(n)\) counts forbidden-minimal words of length \(n\), then for every integer \(n\ge0\),
\[
 a_k(n)=\mathbf1_{\{n\ge3,\;k\nmid(n-2)\}}
 +2\mathbf1_{\{n\ge k+3,\;(k+1)\mid(n-2)\}},
\]
where the first indicator is interpreted as zero when \(k=1\). The ordinary generating function is
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}a_k(n)z^n
 =\frac{z^3}{1-z}-\frac{z^{k+2}}{1-z^k}
  +\frac{2z^{k+3}}{1-z^{k+1}}.
\]
Thus \(\sup_n a_1(n)=2\), while \(\sup_n a_k(n)=3\) for \(k\ge2\), and each supremum is attained infinitely often. The minimal-forbidden-word growth entropy is zero, strictly below the known topological entropy \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(X_k)=\log2/(k+1)\). For each fixed \(k\), the language \(\mathcal M_k\) is deterministic context-free but **not** regular: a rational length series does not entail a regular forbidden language.

## Assumptions and scope
The alphabet is exactly \(\{0,1\}\), the integer \(k\) is fixed and positive, and all \(m,r\) range over positive integers. The finite language of \(X_k\) is the factorial closure of the monoid generated by \(G_k=\{0^m1^{km}:m\ge1\}\); its two-sided realization may equivalently be taken as the closure of bi-infinite concatenations of these blocks. A proper factor is a contiguous subword of strictly smaller length, not a scattered subsequence. The theorem concerns the **coded subsystem** \(X_k\), not the potentially larger one-sided frequency shift \(\Sigma(k/(k+1))\) when \(k\ge2\).

## Proof
**Step 1: all three families are absent.** Every transition from a ones run to a zeros run in a concatenation of \(G_k\)-blocks marks a block junction. Every complete intervening ones run has length \(km\) and follows exactly \(m\) zeros. Accordingly a factor \(01^r0\) can occur only when \(k\mid r\), proving absence of the first family. A complete run \(1^{km}\) cannot be preceded by \(m+1\) consecutive zeros and followed by a zero, proving the second family's absence. After a junction, a full zeros run of length \(m\) forces its subsequent ones run to have length exactly \(km\); the segment \(10^m1^{km+1}\) is therefore absent. Any finite factor of the coded shift occurs in a finite concatenation, so the conclusion extends to the closure.

**Step 2: avoidance of the displayed patterns is sufficient.** Take any finite binary word \(w\) containing none of the three types. Decompose it into maximal constant-symbol runs. Consider an interior zero run \(0^a\) preceded by a one and followed by a ones run of length \(b\).

If that ones run also ends in a zero within \(w\), the first forbidden type forces \(b=kt\) for some \(t\ge1\); the second type forces \(a\le t\); and the third forces \(kt=b\le ka\). Hence \(t=a\) and **the two complete runs match exactly**. If the ones run reaches the right edge of \(w\), the third type instead forces its observed length \(b\le ka\), so the run may be completed to length \(ka\).

A left-edge zeros run \(0^a\) followed by a complete ones run \(1^{kt}\) satisfies \(a\le t\) by the second type and may be extended to the left by \(t-a\) zeros; if its ones run reaches the right edge, extend the zeros run to any length at least \(a\) and the ones run to the corresponding \(k\)-multiple. A left-edge ones run is a suffix of some complete preceding ones run \(1^{kt}\), choosing \(t\) large enough. A right-edge zeros run can be lengthened arbitrarily and completed with its corresponding ones run. These choices are consistent because every pair of fully observed interior runs already has the required ratio. Add repeated generators on both sides. The original \(w\) is a factor of this bi-infinite generator concatenation. Hence **every word avoiding the displayed families is admissible**.

**Step 3: minimality.** Every displayed word is absent by Step 1. Its length-one shorter prefix and suffix are present, as follows. For \(01^r0\), the prefix \(01^r\) occurs inside a sufficiently long generator and the suffix \(1^r0\) occurs across a junction after a sufficiently long ones run. For \(0^{m+1}1^{km}0\), the prefix lies in the generator \(0^{m+1}1^{k(m+1)}\) and the suffix \(0^m1^{km}0\) lies across a block junction. For \(10^m1^{km+1}\), the prefix lies across a junction followed by \(0^m1^{km}\), while the suffix occurs inside \(0^{m+1}1^{k(m+1)}\). Factoriality then puts every other proper contiguous factor in the language. Conversely, any absent word contains one of the three patterns by Step 2; if absent-minimal, it equals that pattern. The three families are disjoint because their initial two symbols are respectively \(01\), \(00\), and \(10\).

**Step 4: counts, entropy and formal-language complexity.** The first family contributes one word at length \(r+2\) exactly for positive \(r\) not divisible by \(k\). The other two each contribute one word at length \((k+1)m+2\). Summing geometric series yields the displayed rational function and coefficient formula. For \(k\ge2\), choose any \(m\ge1\) not divisible by \(k\); at length \((k+1)m+2\), all three families contribute. This happens infinitely often. With at most three forbidden-minimal words per length, \(\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{-1}\log\max\{1,a_k(n)\}=0\). Reyes computes the stated strictly positive topological entropy.

For fixed \(k\), each of the three families has a deterministic pushdown recognizer: the first tests the residue of the ones-run length modulo \(k\), and the others use one stack counter to enforce the linear run-length equality. Their distinct two-letter prefixes permit deterministic dispatch, so their disjoint union is deterministic context-free. Intersecting \(\mathcal M_k\) with the regular language \(00^+1^+0\) isolates the second family, \(\{0^{m+1}1^{km}0:m\ge1\}\), which is nonregular by the pumping lemma (or the Myhill–Nerode theorem). Hence the whole forbidden language is nonregular.

Run `python3 check_antidictionary.py` with the Python standard library. It independently enumerates all length-\(n\) factors admitted by finite concatenations of the defining generators and tests the minimal-forbidden condition (both length-one shorter adjacent factors admissible). It compares the exact resulting sets with all three predicted families for \(k=1,\ldots,5\) and lengths \(n=3,\ldots,16\), a total of 70 parameter-length pairs. Output on the included checker: `VERIFY_OK block_coded_parameters=5 word_lengths=3..16 exact_comparisons=70`. The finite checks are **not** a proof for arbitrary \(k\) or length; Steps 1–4 supply the infinite proof.

## Relationship to prior work
Reyes (2026, arXiv:2611.10995v1) introduced and analyzed these particular proportional-run coded systems as the invariant-measure-carrying cores of rational frequency shifts; its Section 5 characterizes generators, synchronization and a different type of forbidden run, while Sections 5–6 establish entropy and intrinsic ergodicity. The exact **complete** minimal forbidden basis, its arithmetic layer counts, and the deterministic-context-free/nonregular distinction are the claims here, not Reyes' pre-existing dynamical conclusions.

Wu and Zhang (2026, arXiv:2611.03758v1) prove a general measure-of-maximal-entropy theorem under a minimal-forbidden-entropy gap. Our explicit computation verifies that hypothesis for this entire coded family. Their theorem is not claimed as original here, nor are uniqueness or maximal entropy itself. The general mathematical role of minimal forbidden words as language invariants originates in Béal–Mignosi–Restivo–Sciortino (2000); the present calculation is for a different, concrete family.

## Limitations
No minimal-forbidden classification is claimed for the **larger** one-sided frequency shift \(\Sigma(k/(k+1))\) when \(k\ge2\). The classification says nothing about irrational frequency parameters or perturbations of the block-ratio rule. Formal language recognizability and the finite enumerator do not independently establish novelty; the exact-case claim remains subject to external literature and historical-priority assessment. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
