The zero cell of three independently Poisson-spaced families of lines with directions \(60^\circ\) apart has a complete four-type sidedness law for arbitrary positive directional rates. For equal rates, the probabilities of \(3,4,5,6\) sides are exactly \(2/27,67/144,25/72,49/432\), respectively, with mean \(9/2\), sharply differing from the typical-cell law.
