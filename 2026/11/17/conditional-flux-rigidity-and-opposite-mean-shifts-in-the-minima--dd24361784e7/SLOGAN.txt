For every compact positive stationary state of the three-species Wilhelm oscillator, the catalytic species has an exact constant conditional mean, yet the other two species have strictly opposite mean shifts relative to equilibrium. Exact nonnegative quadratic and variance defects prove that every non-equilibrium periodic orbit satisfies \(\overline X<x_*\), \(\overline Y>y_*\), and \(\overline Z=z_*\) in physical time.
