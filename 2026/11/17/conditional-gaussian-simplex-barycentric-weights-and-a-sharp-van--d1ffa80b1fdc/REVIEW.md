The all-positive-scale restriction is essential.

Wendel's absorption probability, Gaussian Haar-kernel invariance, and half-normal variables are established ingredients, not novel claims. Cover and Efron (1967) give the sign-pattern probability, and Götze–Kabluchko–Zaporozhets (arXiv:1911.04184) treat general conic absorption and Gaussian simplex angles. Moseeva (arXiv:2007.07208) gives weighted Gaussian simplex *volume* laws. None of the inspected relevant primary statements yields the explicit conditional barycentric density with nonidentical column scales and the sharp singular-vertex crossover, its critical Gaussian-mixture fluctuation, and the ordinary-weight bulk limit. A separate published equivalence might exist under positive kernel vectors or normalized half-normal distributions; priority is not independently certified. The full texts of Wendel (1962) and Baryshnikov–Vitale (1994) were not accessible.
