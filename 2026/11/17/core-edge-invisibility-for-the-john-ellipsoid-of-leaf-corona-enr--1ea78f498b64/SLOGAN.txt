Attaching at least one private leaf to every vertex of a bipartite core shields the Euclidean John ellipsoid of its enriched chain polytope from **every** added core edge: the squared core and leaf semiaxes are respectively \(1/(p_i+1)\) and \(p_i/(p_i+1)\). Added core-edge facets are tangent only when both endpoints have exactly one leaf; otherwise each has a uniform positive Euclidean clearance, independent of the size and density of the core.
