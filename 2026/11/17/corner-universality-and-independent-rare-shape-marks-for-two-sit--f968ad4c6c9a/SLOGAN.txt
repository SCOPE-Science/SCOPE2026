For two independent area-uniform sites in any fixed rectangle, the smaller clipped Euclidean Voronoi cell has \(\Pr(T/S<\varepsilon)\sim(8/3)\varepsilon^2\), independently of the rectangle's aspect ratio. Conditioned on such a rare cell, its normalized area, allocation between the two edge intercepts, logarithmic anisotropy, and microscopic site-separation mark converge jointly in total variation to independent \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,1)\), \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,2)\), Laplace, and uniform laws, with independent uniform corner and site labels.
