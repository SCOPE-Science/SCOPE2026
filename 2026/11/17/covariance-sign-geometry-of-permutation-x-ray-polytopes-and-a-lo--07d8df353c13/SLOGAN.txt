The canonical uniform-permutation image measure on the anti-diagonal X-ray polytope has an exact covariance sign diagram: positive coordinate pairs occur only on opposite sides of the center and satisfy \((2k-a)(2k-b)>2k^2\). The positive-pair fraction tends to the universal constant \(1-\log 2\), while the zero-pair fraction vanishes and the negative-pair fraction tends to \(\log2\).
