For every integer \(m\ge2\), a compact quasinilpotent shift admits a bounded nilpotent rank-one perturbation whose nonzero spectrum forms exactly \(2m\) asymptotic rays with a sharp eigenvalue count of order \(r^{-m}\). The exponential spectral prescription is realizable up to and including the exact boundary \(|a|=1/m\), where the eigenvalue-to-singular-value counting ratio is \(1/\pi\), but the spectrum collapses to zero at one exceptional coupling.
