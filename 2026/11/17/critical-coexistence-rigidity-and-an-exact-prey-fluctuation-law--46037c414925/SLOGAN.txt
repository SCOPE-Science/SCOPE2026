In the three-species Samardzija–Greller flow, sustained positive coexistence above the critical ratio \(b/a=1\) forces the *same* long-time prey mean \(1\) and variance \(b/a-1\) on every bounded persistent trajectory, independently of the prey feedback parameter and orbit complexity. Below that ratio compact interior recurrence is impossible, while exactly at it every compactly persistent positive solution converges to one point of an equilibrium segment.
