At the critical phage-dose equality, a delayed coinfection model has an explicit burst-size–latency–coinfection coefficient \(\kappa\) that decides whether bacteria vanish with the sharp law \(S(t)\sim1/(\kappa t)\) or the bacteria-free state repels small positive infections. The same coefficient gives the direction of the local coexistence bifurcation for an arbitrary number of phage strains with distinct delays.
