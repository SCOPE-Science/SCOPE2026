# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For every integer \(r\ge2\) the base join has exactly \(2r\) discrete color parts, and its \(r\)-tuple vertices use \(r\) distinct parts. Adjacency forces complementary supports, since \(2r\) distinct parts must be used. Conversely every complementary pair of transversals is adjacent. This proves the disjoint union of complete bipartite graphs in the written general proof, including singleton parts, rather than inferring it from finite tests. Graph spanning-tree contractions establish the homotopy and reduced integral ranks. The full integral cycle lattice of each biclique is the integer row-and-column-zero matrix lattice, canonically the tensor product of two augmentation kernels. The reversal sign under swapping shores follows from its oriented cellular differential. Seven independent direct face-enumeration tests passed; tests support but do not constitute the proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
Lesh, Schreiner and Wahl give the tupling definition and a general connectivity lower bound, not this even-part component formula. The accessible October 25, 2026 multipartite-tuple-graph theorem establishes a connected graph and rational character for \(2r+1\) parts; restricting a connected graph to fewer support classes does not prove connectedness or the even-part decomposition. The November 8, 2026 doubled cross-polytope result explicitly gives the \(r=2\), all-size-two case (three copies of \(K_{4,4}\)), so that example is acknowledged as already known. The broader all-arity, variable-part classification and exact integral action were not identified in the inspected full texts or retrieved research records. The finite special case and standard graph rank formulas are not counted as novel. Related unindexed formulations, or easy corollaries of a stronger overlooked theorem, remain an originality risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The number \(2r\) is the sharp natural color threshold at which any tuple graph has only edges and each full edge uses every part; the adjacent \(2r+1\)-part family has a distinct connected Kneser support graph. The theorem completely classifies every graph component and its integral homotopy type for arbitrary part sizes, and strengthens an isolated known cross-polytope example into a uniform infinite family. The integral symmetry module supplies orientation-sensitive information lost in ordinary Betti numbers, and it identifies the precise discontinuity at this boundary. The contribution is a concise structural classification, not a claim of a large general connectivity theorem.
