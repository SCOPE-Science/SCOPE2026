At the critical number \(2r\) of discrete color parts, the simplicial \(r\)-tupling splits into exactly \(\frac12\binom{2r}{r}\) complete bipartite components, with explicitly determined integral first homology and its part-permutation action. For cross-polytope boundaries the components are uniformly \(K_{2^r,2^r}\), demonstrating a sharp disconnectedness contrast with the known connected \(2r+1\)-part case; the previously known four-dimensional double is included only as a boundary example.
