# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** Exact finite stationarity fixes the monomer value and recurrence. The proof reconstructs the generating ODE, provides strict global barriers and invokes Pringsheim to establish mass one, obtains the endpoint second moment without relying on a formal unbounded sum, and proves the sharp logarithmic singularity using a positive-variable transformation. The monotone Laplace Tauberian step turns that into the claimed stationary second-moment tail. The accompanying arithmetic checks confirm, but do not replace, those steps.

**Originality (PASS, historical priority provisional).** The 2026 Matveev preprint, read in full, derives the model's critical rate, recurrence and second moment; it does not state or imply by a quoted theorem the rate at which truncations lose stationary second moment. Its displayed recurrence is explicitly acknowledged. The related 2026 review treats spontaneous fragmentation with constant coagulation and product coagulation with collision-induced shattering, not this combination. Searches of published mathematical results and literature under the product-kernel, criticality, monomer-shattering and logarithmic-tail aliases did not identify an equivalent or stronger assertion. The 2024 Fagan–MacKay–Wood study was only partially accessible; priority is therefore not independently settled.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** At a critical gelation-suppression boundary, the source's exact recurrence supports numerical computation but leaves the large-cutoff behavior of its second moment unresolved. The new \(2/\log N\) law supplies the sharp leading constant for an unusually slow, non-power-law truncation error, explaining why a finite cluster cutoff can misrepresent a well-defined stationary second moment. This is a motivated, natural observable rather than an arbitrary slice.

**Closest source and limits.** Matveev, *Suppression of gel formation by spontaneous fragmentation*, arXiv:2611.09537v1, §§2–4, owns the recurrence and steady moment. The statement is only about the infinite stationary mass-one model at \(\lambda=4\), not time-dependent relaxation, uniform finite-size inequalities, or other fragmentation rates. Independent priority and proof audits remain outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
