For a reversible cubic global center with two positive nonlinear stiffnesses, the amplitude-normalized velocity has an order-one moment threshold: fixed orders above one grow exponentially in squared amplitude with an exact prefactor, while the first moment grows only quadratically. Moment orders approaching one within \(A^{-2}\) obey a new, explicit, compact-uniform crossover profile \(\mathcal F_\beta(\lambda)=\int_0^1\exp[\beta\lambda(1-u^2)/2]\,du\), beyond the already known turning-layer and period asymptotics.
