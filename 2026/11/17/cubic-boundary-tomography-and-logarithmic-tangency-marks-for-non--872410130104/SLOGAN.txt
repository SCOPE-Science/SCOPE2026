For four independent circle endpoints sampled from any strictly positive smooth angular density \(p\), the interior crossing-point density diverges near the boundary as \(16p(t)^3\log(1/(1-r^2))\). Consequently, extreme-crossing directions reconstruct \(p\) by a cube root; conditioned on a boundary crossing, three endpoints cluster, the isolated endpoint retains the original sampling law, and the near-tangency scale becomes logarithmically uniform.
