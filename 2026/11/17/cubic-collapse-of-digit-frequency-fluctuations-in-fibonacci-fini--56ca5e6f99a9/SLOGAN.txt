For the canonical finite-type covers of the Fibonacci subshift, the stationary digit-count central-limit variance is explicitly positive yet collapses at the sharp cubic rate \(\sigma_{F_k}^2\sim0.5174960277\ldots/F_k^3\). Its full cover-length-scaled accumulation spectrum is the nondegenerate interval \([0.5174960277\ldots,2.1921483515\ldots]\), despite the familiar first-order entropy staircase.
