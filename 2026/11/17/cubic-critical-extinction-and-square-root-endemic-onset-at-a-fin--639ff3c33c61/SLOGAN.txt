At the degenerate invasion boundary of a discrete-round two-stage contagion map, the exact center dynamics are cubic and attracting for every positive turnover and round length. The adopter fraction satisfies the sharp critical law \(n a_n^2\to 1/(2C)\), while the nearby endemic state emerges with the explicit square-root amplitude \(a_*(\eta)\sim\sqrt{\kappa\eta/C}\).
