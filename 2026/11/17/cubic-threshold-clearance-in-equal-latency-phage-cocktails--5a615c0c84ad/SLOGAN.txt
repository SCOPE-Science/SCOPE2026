At the double-critical bacterial eradication threshold of a multi-phage coinfection delay model, cancellation of the ordinary \(t^{-1}\) clearance law produces an exact \(t^{-1/2}\) law with coefficient \((2\sum_i k_i d_i A_i^2/m_i^3)^{-1/2}\), provided the phage responses do not all vanish. If every response vanishes, the bacteria-free state instead meets a continuum of equilibria.
