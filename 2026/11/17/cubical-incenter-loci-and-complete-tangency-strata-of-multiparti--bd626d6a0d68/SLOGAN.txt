For every complete multipartite graph with \(r\ge2\) nonempty classes, the full set of maximum-Euclidean-ball centers of its canonical Hansen polytope is an explicit \(r\)-dimensional parallelotope, independent in parameter shape of the class sizes. The precise facet-contact set changes only on the parallelotope's boundary: all maximal-clique facets always touch, and each saturated center-coordinate adds exactly the facets omitting its color class.
