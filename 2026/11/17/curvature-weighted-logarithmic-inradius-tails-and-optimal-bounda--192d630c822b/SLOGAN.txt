For three independently sampled points on any smooth positively curved convex oval, the chance that their triangle has inradius below \(t\) is asymptotic to \(24t\log(L/t)\int f^3/\kappa\,ds\). Exceptionally thin triangles localize with density proportional to \(f^3/\kappa\), split their two short gaps on an independent uniform logarithmic scale, and are optimally suppressed by boundary sampling proportional to \(\sqrt\kappa\).
