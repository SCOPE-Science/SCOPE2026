For every \(n\ge5\) coprime to six, cyclic relabeling of the two-subset Kneser independence complex has quotient \(M(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z,1)\vee\bigvee^{(n-1)(n-5)/6}S^2\). This determines its full homotopy type, including arbitrary-order cyclic first-homology torsion, beyond previously known rational symmetry invariants.
