# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

At a generic data chamber, the derivative averages every but one index in each window. Spatial order statistics of those retained index sets interlace under a unit shift. The induced output-gradient matrix has nonnegative coefficients and **exact unit column sums**, proving a sharp total-variation contraction. Continuous piecewise-linearity extends the differential estimate to all real input pairs. Binary intervals attain the bound when \(n\ge2w\), with exact rational four- and five-site witnesses completing the midrange case. The standalone exact checker was run successfully; experiments are not substituted for the global proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)

The 1987 order-statistic and 2007 midrange sources define the relevant filters but the materially read sections do not give this nonlinear two-input cyclic-variation theorem. Published three-point median results establish a distinct expansion coefficient; finite-path min/max nonexpansivity does not imply cyclic leave-one-out nonexpansivity for arbitrary deleted ranks. Searches for midrange, one-deletion averages, linear combinations of order statistics and BV stability found no equivalent or dominating statement. Unindexed work and unexamined parts of large source documents remain historical-priority risks. In particular, original descriptions of linear combinations of order statistics are clearly **not** a claim of invention here.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Deleting only one observation preserves a universal two-input variation contraction, an unexpectedly robust behavior compared with median and middle-three trimmed smoothing. The proof identifies a rank-agnostic interlacing mechanism and a positive stochastic transport of discrete derivatives, potentially reusable in designing edge-stable nonlinear rank filters. It includes sharp all-size stability for the three-point midrange and exact saturation on large cycles. It does not assert a denoising-quality guarantee.

## Scope and limitations

Uniform contiguous cyclic windows, real inputs, equal weights on all retained observations and one pass only. At general widths, short-cycle coefficients strictly between \(w\) and \(2w\) are not fully determined. The earliest precisely day-verified preprint lead is 11 April 2015; older cited publications have no independently verified first-public-day precision in the inspected evidence. Earlier priority cannot be ruled out.
