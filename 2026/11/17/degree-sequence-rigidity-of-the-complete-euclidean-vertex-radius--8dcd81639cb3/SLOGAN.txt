For any unweighted tree graphical zonotope in its canonical Euclidean embedding, the absolute coordinates of a uniformly chosen vertex are mutually independent, and its complete squared-radius shell polynomial factors into one binomial-square factor for each graph vertex degree. Consequently, all trees with the same degree multiset share the entire vertex-radius spectrum, although their other Euclidean invariants can differ; the path and star show Gaussian and centered chi-square fluctuation limits.
