Adjacent finite-subset stages of the circle have homotopy-sphere universal covers and, after sufficiently large prime-order rotation quotients, identical integral homology; nevertheless their top Cartan–Leray transgressions differ by exactly a factor of two. For every prime \(p\equiv5\pmod8\), the rotation quotients \(\operatorname{exp}_3(S^1)/C_p\) and \(\operatorname{exp}_4(S^1)/C_p\) are not homotopy equivalent despite having the same homology and fundamental group.
