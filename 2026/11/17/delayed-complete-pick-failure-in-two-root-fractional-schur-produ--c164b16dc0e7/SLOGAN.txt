The Schur product \((1-z\overline w)^{-a}(1-(z\overline w)^2)^{-b}\) of two complete Pick kernels loses the complete Pick property for every \(0<a<1\) and every \(0<b<1\), even though its first negative reciprocal-kernel coefficient appears only at degree asymptotic to \(\big(a/(2^a\Gamma(1-a)b)\big)^{1/a}\) as \(b\downarrow0\). A boundary branch at \(-1\) causes this sharply delayed obstruction, unlike a small finite monomial-pole perturbation.
