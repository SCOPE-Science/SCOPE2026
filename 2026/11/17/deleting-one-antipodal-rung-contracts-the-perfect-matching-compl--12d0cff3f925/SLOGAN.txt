Deleting a single antipodal rung from any even-width Möbius ladder makes its perfect-matching complex contractible, although the intact ladder has homotopy type \(S^{(n-2)/2}\vee S^{(n-2)/2}\). The proof divides all perfect matchings into two contractible seam-state families whose intersection is the known contractible perfect-matching complex of an odd-length open ladder.
