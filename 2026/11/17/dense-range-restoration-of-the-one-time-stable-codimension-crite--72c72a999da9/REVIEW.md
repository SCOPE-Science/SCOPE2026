The cocycle identity identifies the initial stable space as the preimage of every later stable space. A finite-dimensional complement maps injectively and disjointly from the later stable space; dense range and closedness force that complement to span the quotient. The codimension assumption required by the primary characterization follows at all times. The explicit diagonal example verifies non-surjectivity is allowed. The proof invokes the source's full characterization, not a computational experiment.
