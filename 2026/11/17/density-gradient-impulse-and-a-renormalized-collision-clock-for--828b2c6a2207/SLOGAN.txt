For every interior collision with a positive \(C^1\) line density, the renormalized transverse impact energy has an exact finite limit, giving a second-order logarithmic correction to the remaining collision time. The much smaller axial impulse has an exact \((T-t)\log(1/(T-t))\) coefficient \(2G\sigma'(x_*)\), while the transverse correction encodes a finite-part integral of the entire segment.
