# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The cyclic fixed-point equations \(p_i=\pi^ip_0\) and \(\pi^m p_0=p_0\) are necessary and sufficient. On a target \(\pi\)-cycle of length \(\ell\), the \(\gcd(m,\ell)\) residue orbits under \(\pi^m\) cannot furnish \(m\) disjoint translates unless \(m\mid\ell\). Exactly one of the resulting \(m\) residue classes can be used per eligible cycle. Uniform probability weights and simplex gluing give a genuine PL homeomorphism, not merely a numerical homology calculation. Shellability and the join homology formula yield the stated integral sphere wedge, with explicit empty and zero-dimensional boundaries. A standalone verifier checks 531 finite parameter cases and nine join-homology instances; finite testing is corroboration, not a universal proof.

## Originality

**PASS relative to inspected literature and public research records, with limitations.** Babson–Kozlov's foundational Hom definitions, the equivalent 1997 disjoint-block poset, and the ordinary sphere-wedge theorem are acknowledged as prior. The complete published two-sided symmetry character of Hom complexes was read: it establishes rational Lefschetz traces but does not assert the PL homeomorphism or full geometric fixed-space homotopy types. Matsushita's equivariant Hom-complex discussion concerns different graph-homotopy questions. Searches under deleted-product, graph-Hom, diagonal cyclic, source/target permutation, fixed-set and wedge-of-spheres terminology returned no mathematical implication covering the present claim. A plausible 1997 primary preprint could be inspected only at the abstract/secondary-citation level because its full text was not accessible; publication-priority risk remains.

## Value

**PASS.** The classification is an infinite-family, integral geometric fixed-space theorem for a natural two-sided symmetry of graph-coloring complexes. It reveals fixed geometry invisible to fixed zero-cells: a target six-cycle with a source three-cycle gives three fixed points but no invariant coloring. With two target three-cycles the fixed locus is a four-circle wedge. These distinctions cannot be recovered from the ordinary Hom-complex homotopy type, and rational character data determines only fixed-locus Euler characteristics, not the integral homotopy classification.

## Literature and limits

Closest evidence: Babson–Kozlov, arXiv:math/9704227 and math/0310056; Matsushita, arXiv:1509.03855; and *Full two-sided symmetry character of complete-graph Hom complexes* (2026-11-01). The 1997 original was not available in full and its possible equivalent formulations remain a risk. This result is confined to a full source \(m\)-cycle and arbitrary single target permutation, not all two-sided subgroups. Independent validation is still outstanding.
