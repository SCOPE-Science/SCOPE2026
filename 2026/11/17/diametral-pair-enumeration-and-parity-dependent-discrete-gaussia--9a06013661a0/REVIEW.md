# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces Euclidean maximum vertex-pair distance to complementary binary indicators, derives a complete two-color classification from the simultaneous vertex/edge covering constraints, and counts the precise one-singleton exclusions. The parity-dependent total-variation rate follows from exact central-binomial ratio products and summable theta tails. A standard-library finite checker exhaustively verifies all binary assignments for the smallest three permitted graph orders, checks exact count identities through order 100, and confirms the indicated parity trends. Finite checks are corroborative, not a proof for all orders.

Indexed mathematical research comparisons showed related graph and metric-polytope statements on different objects, not a dominating result. The \(\sqrt N\) binary cube bound and the incidence domination equivalence are credited as established. A differently indexed enumeration of complementary dominating sets or two-color domatic partitions remains a plausible priority risk.

## Value
PASS. The exact vertex-pair classification computes a natural metric extremal spectrum for a new and actively studied covering polytope, revealing a sharp singleton obstruction and a genuinely noncentral-limit, order-one fluctuation law. The parity-dependent theta distribution is an additional structural conclusion not explained by the universal binary-cube diameter bound. The claim does not purport to settle other graph classes or all face geometry.
