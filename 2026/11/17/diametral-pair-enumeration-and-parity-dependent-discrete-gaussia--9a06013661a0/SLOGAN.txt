The strong-total-cover polytope of a complete graph on \(n\ge3\) vertices has exactly \(\tfrac12(\sum_{a=0}^n\binom na2^{a(n-a)}-2n)\) unordered Euclidean diametral vertex pairs, classified by two-color cover partitions. In a uniformly oriented diametral pair, the number of selected graph vertices fluctuates on a constant scale around \(n/2\), with a parity-dependent discrete Gaussian limit in total variation at rate \(O(n^{-1})\).
