For every circular ladder with \(6k\ge12\) rungs, the lower reduced rational homology group of its perfect-matching complex has rank four and splits under rotations, reflections and layer exchange as \((\rho_k\boxtimes\mathbf1)\oplus(\rho_{2k}\boxtimes\varepsilon)\). The two constituents have respectively primitive sixth- and third-root rotation eigenvalues and opposite layer-exchange signs, information not implied by the known sphere counts.
