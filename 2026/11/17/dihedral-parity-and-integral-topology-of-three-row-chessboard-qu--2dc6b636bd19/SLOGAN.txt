For every odd \(n\ge5\), the ordinary dihedral column quotient of the three-row chessboard complex is a wedge of exactly \((n-3)^2/2\) two-spheres, with an explicit wedge count for every reflection-containing subgroup. For every even \(n\ge6\), the same quotient instead has fundamental group \(\mathbb Z/2\), a nonzero mod-two cup square, and normalized Lusternik–Schnirelmann category two.
