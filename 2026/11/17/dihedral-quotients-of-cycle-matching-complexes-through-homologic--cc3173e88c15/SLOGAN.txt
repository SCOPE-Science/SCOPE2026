For every \(3\le n\le13\), the ordinary dihedral quotient of the cycle matching complex \(|\mathcal M(C_n)|\) is contractible except at \(n=11,13\), where it is homotopy equivalent to \(S^3\). Exact quotient-cell collapses, integral Smith forms, and an orbit-space fundamental-group argument establish the complete homotopy classification across the first three-dimensional homology stage.
