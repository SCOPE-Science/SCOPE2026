For the asymmetric twinned chain polytope \(\operatorname{conv}([0,1]^d\cup\{-e_1,\ldots,-e_d\})\), the exact Euclidean inradius is always \(1/\sqrt2\), and *every* optimal ball center lies on a dimension-independent diagonal interval (with a separate planar endpoint). Interior and endpoint centers have three explicit polynomially sized tangent-facet counts despite the polytope having \(1+d2^{d-1}\) facets.
