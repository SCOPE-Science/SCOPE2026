For a positive wave speed formed by a moving sinusoidal grating plus a weak second grating with the same spatial wavelength, the complete one-to-one trapping-speed band shrinks quadratically when the second grating moves faster and widens quadratically when it is stationary or counter-propagating. Its two saddle-node boundaries have explicit fourth-order-accurate expansions; first-spatial-harmonic projective dynamics also forces the two locked-cycle multipliers to be exact reciprocals and excludes isolated attracting subharmonic cycles.
