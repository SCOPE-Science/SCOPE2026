Removing \(m\) vertex-disjoint four-edge squares from \(K_{a,b}\) reduces the fifth-sphere count of its fourth robust clique complex by exactly \(m(a-3)(b-3)\), with integral split-injective homology maps under edge restoration. The unique exception is \(K_{4,4}\) with two removed squares, whose complex is \(S^4\) rather than a wedge of five-spheres.
