# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a degree-two vertex to reverse and return in two synchronous updates, both neighbors must flip and the first-phase adjacent opinions oppose. Propagation around an odd cycle would demand alternating odd length, excluding ring interior flips. Any surviving primitive two-orbit flips the full connector and has alternating connector bits. Each degree-three endpoint has two static ring neighbors, whose opinions must differ to allow a pair of flips; all static ring word runs between those neighbors must be length>=2 with opposite endpoints. Counting 2j positive constant runs of total length m-1 gives W_m=2 sum_j C(m-2j-2,2j-1). Its rational generating function is 2z^4/((1-z)^2-z^4), yielding Fibonacci minus the six-periodic t. Two ring words and one initial path bit give 2W_aW_b states, hence W_aW_b orbits. Independently brute-forced all labelled binary states for six small dumbbells and checked the constructive source; no finite count is extrapolated to all lengths.
