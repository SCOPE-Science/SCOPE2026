For strict synchronous binary majority on two odd cycles joined by any nonempty path, every primitive temporal two-cycle flips exactly the joining path; the number of such cycles is \(W_aW_b\), independent of the path length. The six-periodic Fibonacci factors \(W_m=F_{m-2}-t_{m-3}\) show that oscillations begin only at ring lengths at least five, while each additional connector site halves their fraction among all initial states.
