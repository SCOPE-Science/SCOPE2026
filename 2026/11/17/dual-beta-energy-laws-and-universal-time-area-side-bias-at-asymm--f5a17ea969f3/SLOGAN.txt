Near an asymmetric piecewise-potential center, physical-time and enclosed-area sampling yield different beta laws for the potential-energy fraction, with a sharp analytic perturbation profile. When the two restoring sides have flatness orders \(n<m\), the vanishing time share of the less-flat side exceeds its vanishing area share by the universal limiting factor \(m(n+2)/(n(m+2))\).
