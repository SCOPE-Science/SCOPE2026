# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed augmented Hopf trace formula Ltilde(h;Ind J)=-sum_{h-invariant independent S}(-1)^{#cycles(h|S)}, including empty simplex degree -1. For fixed g-invariant original vertex set T, each orbit of original edges produces a signed independence sum -1 if T does not contain both endpoints of any orbit edge, otherwise zero. Verified both cases: g^ell endpoint-preserving (three internal position orbits forming P3) and g^ell endpoint-exchanging (outer two positions form one orbit, middle another, forming K2 incompatibility). Thus Z(G[4],g)=(-1)^{c_E(g)}Z(G,g) and sign parity (-1)^{c_E}=(-1)^e eps_E. Applying Csorba ordinary (NON-equivariant) suspension e times and rational character determination yields the single-degree epsilon_E twist. K_n edge sign on a transposition is (-1)^{n-2}, so standard augmentation kernel tensored sign^{n-2}; all edge cases e=0 and inversions are addressed. Independently checked small K2 endpoint-reversal orbit factor; inspected all-pair finite verifier, not substituted for proof.

## value

PASS

Csorba suspension for independence complexes is classical, but nonequivariant suspension does not determine natural automorphism characters. The new all-graph signed edge-cycle formula is a structural equivariant enhancement, including previously delicate edge inversion, and yields explicit complete rational Sn modules on 4-subdivided complete graphs. Natural graph automorphism action and all-graph formula make it a motivated useful homological invariant; no unsupported integral or equivariant-homotopy isomorphism is claimed.
