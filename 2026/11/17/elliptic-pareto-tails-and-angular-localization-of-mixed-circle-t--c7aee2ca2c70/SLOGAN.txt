Two uniformly sampled circle vertices and one fixed interior point generate a circumcenter-distance Pareto tail with exact elliptic coefficient \(2(1-r^2)K(r)/\pi^2\), strictly maximized at the center. The conditional far-center direction has an explicit anisotropic density and localizes on two opposite rays as the fixed vertex approaches the circle; expected circumradius is infinite for every interior vertex but finite at the boundary.
