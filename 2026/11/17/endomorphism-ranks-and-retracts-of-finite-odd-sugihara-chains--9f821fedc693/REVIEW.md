# Mathematical review of the refined result

## Correctness

The lattice order, identity, involution and implication force every homomorphism's positive images to be an initial zero segment followed by a strictly increasing segment. Conversely the explicit map preserves the fusion and implication cases. Every symmetric image is obtained by exactly one endomorphism. Requiring idempotence forces its positive image values to lie in the final source segment, hence to fill that segment. This proves the complete rank and retract classification. Exhaustive small-case enumeration independently corroborates the argument, without replacing the proof.

## Originality and relationship to prior work

Aguzzoli–Mordonini's **Counting Homomorphisms Between Finite Gödel Algebras** (IPMU 2024; Springer 2025), Theorem 5 plus Proposition 2, already computes hom-set sizes for **all** finite odd Sugihara monoids through finite rooted-tree duality. The earlier presentation's implication that hom-set counting itself was new was too broad. This refined statement claims only the **specific pointwise normal form, complete endomorphism image-rank polynomial, uniqueness of each symmetric image and idempotent/retract classification** as additions. The published hom-set recurrence alone does not resolve these rank/idempotence refinements, and they were not found stated in the relevant full texts examined. The finite-chain normal form is elementary and may be implicit in older work; this remains a real historical-priority risk.

Galatos–Raftery supplies the category equivalence, and Craig–Robinson describes finite-chain operations and representations. Neither inspected text was found to state the rank-and-retract spectrum.

## Mathematical value

The endomorphism rank distribution and the precise distinction between all image subalgebras and the much smaller family of retract subalgebras classify natural intrinsic structures of standard finite odd Sugihara chains. These exact invariants concern canonical algebraic semantics of relevance logic, beyond a single small computation.

## Scope

Claims apply to standard finite odd chains with m>=1. The theorem neither settles the corresponding problems for arbitrary finite odd Sugihara monoids nor asserts worldwide historical priority. The general hom-set recurrence of Aguzzoli–Mordonini is explicitly prior work.
