# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. For nonzero edges the polar decomposition gives the exact geometric-neighbor recursion, and the binomial iteration follows inductively. The quantitative tail estimate is independently reconstructed by splitting each binomial probability vector into fixed total masses \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\), applying weighted AM--GM and Hölder, then using bilateral translation invariance. The binomial maximal atom tends to zero while \(\ell_1\) tails vanish, establishing zero Schatten power. The cycle Fourier multiplier is strictly contractive away from constants; dominated convergence is valid because component Schatten powers are bounded by the original summable component powers. The finite-support plateau checks the nonuniformity assertion. A standalone exact-rational/numerical diagnostic runs successfully but is not invoked as infinite proof.

Searches against published mathematical findings, older shift terminology, and primary literature found no direct coverage. An earlier general weighted-permutation or Aluthge theorem could subsume parts, so absolute publication priority is not claimed.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The new 2026 spectral convergence work leaves the Schatten \(p\)-endpoint unsettled in general. This theorem establishes it for a natural family that includes genuinely two-sided injective compact shifts not reducing to unilateral shifts, and computes the limit exactly for arbitrary finite/infinite sums of paths and cycles. The quantitative tail estimate exposes the mechanism of diffuse mass escape, and the plateau example proves the sharp obstruction to a uniform rate. These address structural and quantitative questions, not arbitrary finite parameter slices.
