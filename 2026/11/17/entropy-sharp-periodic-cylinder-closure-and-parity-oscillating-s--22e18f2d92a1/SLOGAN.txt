For every irreducible positive-entropy finite-type shift, the short-period closing deficit of a uniformly chosen cylinder has an entropy-sharp exponential tail, and its exponential moments change from bounded to linear to exponential growth precisely at \(\log\lambda\). The average number of strictly shorter periodic realizations is a trace ratio; it converges to \(2/3\) for the three-symbol no-repeat shift but has distinct odd/even limits \(12/25\) and \(1/5\) on a \(2\)-by-\(3\) bipartite graph.
