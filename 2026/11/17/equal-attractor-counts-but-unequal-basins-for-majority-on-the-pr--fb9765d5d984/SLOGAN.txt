On the decagonal prism and Desargues graph, synchronous binary neighbor majority has exactly \(124\) fixed states and \(7{,}626\) primitive two-cycles on each graph, yet their fixed-attraction basins contain respectively \(36{,}784\) and \(107{,}224\) of the \(2^{20}\) labelled initial states. An exhaustive two-shore reduction explains the coincident recurrence counts and certifies the sharp four-update entry bound, while a separate full-state enumerator confirms the strikingly different basin sizes.
