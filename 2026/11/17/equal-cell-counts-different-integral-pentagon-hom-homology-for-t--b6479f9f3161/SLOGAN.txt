The pentagon graph-Hom complexes of the two strongly regular \((16,6,2,2)\) twins have identical cell counts in every dimension, yet \(H_1(\operatorname{Hom}(C_5,K_4\square K_4);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{90}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^2\), while \(H_1(\operatorname{Hom}(C_5,\mathrm{Sh});\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{60}\). Exact signed cellular elimination proves that two genuine order-two classes survive for the rook graph but not for the Shrikhande graph.
