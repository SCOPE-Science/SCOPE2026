Reversing the arc \(0\to2\) versus \(1\to3\) in the seven-vertex transitive tournament gives directed-linear-forest complexes with **identical full face vectors** but different reduced \(\mathbb F_2\)-homology: one has six classes in degree two and nineteen in degree three, while the other has thirteen in degree three only. This positional homology sensitivity is absent for the same two-step reversal family on at most six vertices.
