For every right regular polygonal antiprism, the unique shape minimizing its largest interior dihedral angle makes **all** dihedral angles equal, with exact optimum \(\arccos(-\cos(\pi/n)/(1+\cos(\pi/n)))\). Except for the octahedron, this equidihedral shape differs strictly from both the edge-uniform and the fixed-circumsphere-volume-maximizing realizations: their aspect ratios have three different large-order scales, and both conventional realizations incur a limiting angular penalty of \(\pi/3\).
