For every \(r\ge2\), the near-perfect Kneser graph Hom complex \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_r,\mathrm{KG}(2r+1,2))\) retracts equivariantly onto an explicit bipartite incidence graph, giving a complete integral permutation-kernel description of its simultaneous symmetric-group homology action. The quotient by source-color permutations is a wedge of \(1+(2r-3)(2r+1)!!/3\) circles, but the quotient by ground-point permutations, and the full symmetry quotient, are contractible.
