# Verification evidence and limits

The included `verify.py` is a self-contained Python 3 standard-library program. Running `python3 verify.py` from the directory containing the script enumerates all simple labeled graphs on one through four vertices and all their automorphisms (293 graph/automorphism instances in total). For each, the program constructs the ordinary Mycielski graph from its defining edges and enumerates every independent face of the apex-deleted complex to test the needed contiguity relation. It separately builds exact geometric fixed-point orbit-conflict graphs for the cyclic subgroup generated by the tested automorphism, computes reduced mod-two simplicial homology including the empty-space degree, and checks the one-degree shift predicted by unreduced suspension.

It additionally checks twelve two-iteration cases from \(K_2\) and \(C_5\), and checks the **whole noncyclic dihedral group** on \(C_5\) for both one and two iterations. The complete record is `verification_output.txt`, ending with `MYCIELSKI_FIXED_SUSPENSION_TESTS_PASS`. The included script was executed against its included data (none external); its output was compared with the recorded text.

The finite binary calculations verify neither the universal equivariant homotopy equivalence nor integral statements by themselves. The proof rests on a facewise contiguous equivariant shadow contraction, equivariant cofibration gluing and induction. The program does not test all subgroups of graphs of unbounded order, and its finite search cannot establish publication priority.
