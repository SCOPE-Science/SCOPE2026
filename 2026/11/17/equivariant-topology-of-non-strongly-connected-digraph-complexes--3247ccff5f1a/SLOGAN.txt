The non-strongly-connected digraph complex on \(n\ge3\) labeled vertices is genuinely \(S_n\)-equivariantly homotopy equivalent to the join of the vertex-augmentation sphere and the proper partition lattice. For every finite group \(G\) of order at least three, its regular-action fixed space realizes the proper subgroup-lattice order complex of \(G\), yielding exact cyclic and elementary-abelian fixed-space homotopy types.
