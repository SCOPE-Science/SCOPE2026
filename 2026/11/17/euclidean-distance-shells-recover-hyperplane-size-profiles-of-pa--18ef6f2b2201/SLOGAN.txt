For any paving matroid of rank at least two, the Euclidean distances from hypersimplex vertices to its base polytope recover exactly how many hyperplanes occur at each size: a nonbasis inside a hyperplane of size \(h\) has squared distance \(1/k+1/(n-h)\). The largest hyperplane alone determines the sharp Hausdorff distance, and only nonbasis vertices in largest hyperplanes attain it.
