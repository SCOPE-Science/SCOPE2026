For the real Lipschitz-free unit ball of \(m\ge3\) parallel three-edge paths between two terminals, the complete canonical Euclidean facet-distance multiset is explicit, and the maximal inscribed radius is \(\bigl((5m+1)/2-2(m\bmod2)/(m+1)\bigr)^{-1/2}\). The number of facets touching the centered optimal ball and the affine dimension of all optimal-center positions obey exact central-binomial and parity laws.
