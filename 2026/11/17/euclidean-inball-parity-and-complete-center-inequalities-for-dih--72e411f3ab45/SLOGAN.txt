The standard dihedral permutohedron has exact intrinsic Euclidean inradius \(\sqrt n/2\) for even \(n\) and \(n/\sqrt{4n-6}\) for odd \(n\). Its optimal ball center is unique exactly in odd order; in even order all optimal centers form an explicitly bounded \((n-2)\)-dimensional polytope, and the centered ball touches exactly two facets instead of \(2n\).
