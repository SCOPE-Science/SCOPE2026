For equal multipartite metrics with at least three parts of even size, the canonical real Lipschitz-free unit ball has exact Euclidean inradius \(\bigl[\frac m4\max\{k,t^2+(k-1)(2-t)^2\}\bigr]^{-1/2}\) and a unique incenter. Only the tripartite family exhibits an interior phase transition, at \(t=5/3\), and its tangent-facet counts develop additional rational resonances.
