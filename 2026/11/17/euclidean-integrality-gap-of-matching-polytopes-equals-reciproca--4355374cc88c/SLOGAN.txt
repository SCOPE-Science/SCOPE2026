The exact unweighted Euclidean Hausdorff gap from a fractional matching polytope to its matching polytope equals half the square root of the maximum reciprocal-length sum of vertex-disjoint odd cycles. Every half-integral vertex has an explicit unique projection obtained by averaging near-perfect matchings of its cycles, and the sharp order bound \(\sqrt{|V|}/6\) holds exactly for graphs with a spanning triangle packing.
