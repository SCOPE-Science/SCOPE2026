For **every** Latin square of order \(n\ge2\), the clique complex of its row–column–symbol graph is a wedge of exactly \((n-1)^3-I(M)\) two-spheres, where \(I(M)\) counts intercalates. A uniform nerve reduction and free-facet collapses of intercalate tetrahedra upgrade previously known field-homology formulas to an integral homotopy classification, including even and nongroup squares.
