For induced Kubo–Ando mean transforms of positive two-periodic weighted shifts with \(f'(1)=1/2\), either symmetry gives one-step consensus or the first nonzero odd moment of the representing measure determines an exact superattraction order \(p\ge3\) and coefficient \(m_p/2^{p-1}\). All odd orders are attained explicitly by positive Legendre-perturbed measures, while even orders are impossible.
