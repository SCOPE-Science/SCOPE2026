Randomly reorder any zero-sum list of planar edges: the exact variance of signed enclosed algebraic area is one-twelfth the determinant of the edge second-moment matrix. For Haar–Stiefel closed polygons of perimeter \(2\), this yields \(\operatorname{Var}(A_n)=4(n-2)/[3(n+1)(n+2)(n+4)]\), uniquely largest at four edges.
