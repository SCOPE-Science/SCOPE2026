For the Novaes–Pereira three-dimensional polynomial torus construction, every planar hyperbolic cycle in positive squared radius lifts to an exactly located isolated invariant torus for every nonzero coupling, not just sufficiently small positive coupling. A concrete polynomial member has a provably exponentially attracting torus and the explicit open attraction region \(0<(x^2+y^2-2)^2+z^2<4\) for every positive coupling.
