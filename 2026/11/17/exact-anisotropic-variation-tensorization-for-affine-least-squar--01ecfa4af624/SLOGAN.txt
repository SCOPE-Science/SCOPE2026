For globally additive affine least-squares projection on any positively weighted product grid, the sharp anisotropic edge-variation amplification tensorizes exactly into one-dimensional single-jump constants, independently of the number of other factors. On uniform grids it is \(\max_j 3\lfloor n_j/2\rfloor/(2\lfloor n_j/2\rfloor+1)\), so amplification begins exactly at a four-site axis and the optimal dimension-independent bound is \(3/2\).
