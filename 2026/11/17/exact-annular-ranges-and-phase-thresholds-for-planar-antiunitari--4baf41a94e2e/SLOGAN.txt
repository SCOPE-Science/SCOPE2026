For every antiunitary on a complex Hilbert plane, its entire \(q\)-numerical range is a closed centered annulus whose exact two radii depend only on the ordinary antilinear numerical radius and \(|q|\). The central hole disappears at \(|q|=\sqrt{1-w(A)^2}\), whereas the outer radius remains one until \(|q|=w(A)\), giving two distinct sharp phase thresholds.
