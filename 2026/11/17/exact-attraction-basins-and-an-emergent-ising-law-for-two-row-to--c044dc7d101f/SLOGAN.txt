On a two-row periodic torus, deterministic Toom majority sends every configuration containing an agreeing column to a fixed two-row word whose exact basin is \(3^{n-k}\) when the word has \(k>0\) cyclic interfaces, with two explicit constant-word exceptions. From uniform random initial bits, the frozen word converges locally to an exactly characterized ferromagnetic Ising chain with neighbor persistence \(3/4\) and spin correlations \(2^{-r}\).
