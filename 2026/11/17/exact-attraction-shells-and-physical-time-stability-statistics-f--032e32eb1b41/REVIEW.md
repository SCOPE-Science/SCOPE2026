# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof reconstructs the full first return using the explicit upper and lower orbit graphs, proves strict monotonicity uniformly for every integer \(k\ge2\), and checks the full basin partition and multiplier formulas. The telescoping length identity and the small-argument expansion of \(\operatorname{arctanh}\) give the exact rate-distribution asymptotic. An executed standalone program supplies only supplemental finite diagnostics, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS within accessible evidence.** Glendinning, Hogan, Homer, Jeffrey and Szalai (2023), DOI: 10.1007/s00332-023-09910-4, Section 4, originate the sinusoidal sewed focus and already identify infinitely many alternately stable/unstable cycles. The new claims are the *full* local attraction shells and widths, their exact sampling law, their original-time multipliers, and the sharply different rate-distribution regimes. The closest previous sewed-focus results concern single-attractor power maps or the analytic approach to a focus, not this countable sink family. A publication-priority risk from unindexed equivalent calculations remains.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The complete basin and physical-time spectrum characterize coexistence of countably many attracting cycles beyond mere existence. The distinction between a positive physical-time contraction-rate floor and a power-law mass of arbitrarily weak rates is a natural quantitative feature of this oscillator family.

**Limitations.** Local section only, no planar-area basin probabilities or global classification; the piecewise field is nonanalytic at the switching tangency and has order-dependent regularity. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
