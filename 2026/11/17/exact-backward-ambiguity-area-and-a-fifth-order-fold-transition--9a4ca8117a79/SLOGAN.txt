The linear-response plankton map has an exactly two-covered region of possible one-step observations whose area is a closed two-branch rational function of mortality and coupling. The geometric fold changes its boundary attachment at \(\gamma=(1-r)/2\), making this ambiguity area \(C^4\) but not \(C^5\), even where every strict-interior initial state in the biological triangle converges forward to the prey-only equilibrium.
