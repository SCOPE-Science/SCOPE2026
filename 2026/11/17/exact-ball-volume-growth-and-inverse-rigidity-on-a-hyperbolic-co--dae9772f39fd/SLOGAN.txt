An off-apex ball on any curvature-\(-1\) cone of angle \(0<\alpha<\pi\) has exact all-radius area and a two-term large-radius law \(V(R)=G e^R-\alpha+O(e^{-R})\). The center-independent constant identifies the cone angle, and \(G=2\arctan(e^s\tan(\alpha/4))\) then reconstructs the center's apex distance; the first cut has a sharp \(3/2\)-power area deficit.
