The real planar norm \(N_t(x,y)=\max\{|x|,|y|,|x+ty|,|tx+y|\}\) has exact Banach–Mazur distance \(d(X_t,\ell_\infty^2)=1+t/2\) for every \(0\le t\le1\). A sharp unrestricted-isomorphism lower bound and an explicit optimal matrix resolve the full interior distortion profile between the square and affine-regular hexagon.
