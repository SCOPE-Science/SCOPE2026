At the natural border–critical coincidence \(\mu=2\) in a sign-triggered logistic dispersal map, all trajectories converge, and the upper equilibrium attracts exactly an interval of length \(\frac12\sqrt{\varepsilon/(1+\varepsilon)}\). Uniform initial conditions therefore create a fractional-power \(+\frac12\varepsilon^{3/2}\) correction to the mean selected population, despite a strictly linear separation of the two attracting equilibria.
