For the original Akima cubic interpolation rule, all possible curve limits near any piecewise-affine uniform-grid data form an exact independent box of slope choices at its zero-weight corners. Its sharp uniform and total-variation cluster diameters are respectively \(\frac4{27}\max_i|\Delta_i|\) and \(\frac{16}{27}\sum_i|\Delta_i|\), over exactly those corners; the qualitative one-corner discontinuity was already known.
