For the real \(m\)-dimensional cross-polytope, the sharp Euclidean self-constrained codimension-one width is \(\frac12\sqrt{m/(m-1)}\), attained only by signed zero-sum hyperplanes. The ordinary width is \(m^{-1/2}\), so imposing membership in the original \(\ell_1\) ball causes a sharp distortion growing like \(\sqrt m/2\).
