For the three-color inhibitory firefly automaton on every labelled cycle, the configurations that translate by one site after three updates form exactly \(2a_n-3\) coherent states, where \(a_n=3a_{n-1}-3a_{n-2}+a_{n-3}+a_{n-4}\). Their population grows like \(2\rho^n\) with \(\rho(\rho-1)^3=1\), and every counted state is temporally periodic.
