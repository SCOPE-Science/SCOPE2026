On every labelled three-row Day & Night torus, the number of configurations that invert every cell in one update is exactly \(2\cdot3^n+L_n+\epsilon_n+6\cdot18^{n/3}\mathbf1_{3\mid n}\), so they form half as many primitive temporal two-cycle orbits. Almost all these complement-flip configurations belong to two equally numerous column-density phases with one or two live cells per column.
