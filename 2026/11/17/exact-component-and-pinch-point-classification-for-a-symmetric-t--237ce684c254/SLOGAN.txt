For three equilateral sites equipped with equally eccentric, radially aligned ellipses, the complete anisotropic Voronoi topology is explicit: a cheaper radial cost yields two disjoint convex components, isotropy yields one wedge, and a costlier radial direction yields a connected cell of three convex pieces joined at exactly two cut points. The orphan gap and compact central-core area are exact functions of the anisotropy parameter.
