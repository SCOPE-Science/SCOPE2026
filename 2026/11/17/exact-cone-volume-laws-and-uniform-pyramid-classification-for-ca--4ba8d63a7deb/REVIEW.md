# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is established for all finite connected simple cactus graphs. Centered graphical-zonotope facets are orthogonal products of induced connected graphical zonotopes, so the intrinsic facet area is \(\sqrt{st}\tau(G[S])\tau(G[T])\); the cut support distance is \(k\sqrt{N/(st)}/2\). Cauchy–Binet and the spanning-tree Gram determinant prove these metric facts without assuming a nonstandard volume normalization. Cactus block structure then proves both bond types, exact tree-product factors and complete multiplicities. The masses sum to one, and the if-and-only-if condition follows from the distinct mass levels. Exact-rational enumeration and independent convex-hull checks corroborate representative small graphs.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk
The nearest inspected geometry references establish the cut-facet framework, general cone-volume theory and spaces of possible facet volumes, not the cactus-specific complete cone-volume spectrum or the characterization of uniform facet-pyramid volumes. Existing general spanning-tree and cut formulas contain the ingredients but neither state the blockwise cancellation nor its necessary-and-sufficient geometric classification. Searches for cactus, bonds, cycle bouquets, uniform cone-volume and facet-pyramid equivalents did not reveal a dominating published theorem. Since the ingredients are classical and some research records are visible only as abstracts, historical priority remains provisional.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result classifies the full natural cone-volume measure on an infinite, nontrivial family of convex bodies and identifies exactly when it becomes uniform on their facets. The nonparallelotope bridgeless equal-cycle family is a structurally meaningful contrast to the tree parallelotopes, and the blockwise allocation is stable under arbitrary cut-vertex attachment patterns. These are geometric consequences not determined merely by face count or a known total volume.

## Limits
Only centered, unweighted incidence zonotopes of finite simple cacti are covered. Uniform *pyramid* volumes do not mean equal Euclidean facet areas. No unindexed or independently audited priority claim is made. The accompanying finite checks are diagnostic; the written argument is the all-parameter proof.
