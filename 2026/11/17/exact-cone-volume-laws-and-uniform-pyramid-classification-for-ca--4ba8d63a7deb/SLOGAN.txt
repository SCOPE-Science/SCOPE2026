For a cactus graphical zonotope of intrinsic dimension \(d\), each bridge-cut facet contributes precisely \(1/(2d)\) of its Euclidean cone volume, while each facet cutting a cycle of length \(\ell\) contributes \(1/(d\ell)\). All facet pyramids have equal volume exactly for trees and for bridgeless cacti whose cycles all have the same length.
