With two uniformly random points on each of two concentric circles, the probability of a four-vertex convex hull has an exact, strictly increasing and strictly convex one-dimensional integral profile in their radius ratio. Its near-coincident-circle defect is \(4(1-r)\log(1/(1-r))/\pi^2+O(1-r)\), while the previously known zero-radius limit is \(1/2\).
