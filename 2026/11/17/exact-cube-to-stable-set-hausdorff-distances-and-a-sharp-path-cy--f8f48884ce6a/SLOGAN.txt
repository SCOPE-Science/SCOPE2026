The Euclidean Hausdorff gap between the Boolean cube and its path or cycle stable-set polytope has an exact closed form in every dimension, with the all-ones cube corner as the unique deepest point. Closing a path into a cycle changes the squared gap by exactly zero in even order but by \((n+1)/(2n)\) in odd order, while a general graph bound becomes sharp exactly when its maximum independent sets support uniform vertex marginals.
