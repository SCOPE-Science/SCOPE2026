The \(D_4\) lattice's regular-24-cell quantization error has an exact upper-endpoint radial law: for squared-error deficit \(\varepsilon<1/4\), its tail is a single three-dimensional cube integral with an all-order, positive rational Taylor expansion. In particular the rare-event probability begins \(\frac32\varepsilon^4+3\varepsilon^5+\frac{28}{5}\varepsilon^6+O(\varepsilon^7)\).
