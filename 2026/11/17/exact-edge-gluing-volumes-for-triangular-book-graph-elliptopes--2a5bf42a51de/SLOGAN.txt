For the graph formed by \(n\) triangles glued along one common edge, the projected graph elliptope has exact unit-cube-coordinate volume \((\pi/2)^n\operatorname B(n/2+1,n/2+1)\), while the already-known cut volume is \(2^n\operatorname B(n+1,n+1)\). Their ratio grows as \(\sqrt2(\pi/2)^n(1-3/(8n)+O(n^{-2}))\), revealing an exponential volume-gap law distinct from vertex-glued triangle products.
