The derangement matching polytope has an exact rook-polynomial formula for the number of Euclidean edges of every possible length \(\sqrt{2k}\). As dimension grows, half of the squared-distance deficits relative to \(2n\), for a uniformly chosen edge and a uniformly chosen pair of distinct vertices, have laws whose total-variation distance vanishes and which both converge to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(1)\), even though the two vertices are adjacent with probability only \(\sim e/n\).
