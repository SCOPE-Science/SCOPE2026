For a reversible two-zone affine focus oscillator, every crossing cycle has Euclidean enclosed area exactly \((w^2-v^2)/t\), where \(v\) and \(w\) are its upper and lower switching velocities. Along the entire crossing annulus, area grows strictly while physical period decreases, and the period excess above \(2\pi/\omega\) has an explicit positive constant times the inverse square root of enclosed area.
