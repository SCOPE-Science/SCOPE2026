For every nonreflexive boundary exponent pair in \(L^p_x(L^q_y)\), the worst norm of a rank-one average over any set of area \(t\in(0,1)\) is exactly \(t^{-|1/p-1/q|}\). Except at the four corners, the supremum is never attained: every maximizing sequence must exhibit either a vanishing-width full-height spike or vanishing-height dust, with precise section-size criteria.
