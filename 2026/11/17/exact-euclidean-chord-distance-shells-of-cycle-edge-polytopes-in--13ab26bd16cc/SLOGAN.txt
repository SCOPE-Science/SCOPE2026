For the graph-edge polytope of a cycle inside the second hypersimplex, every nonedge vertex has exact squared Euclidean distance \(5/6\) if it is a two-step chord and \(3/4\) if it is a longer chord, once the cycle has at least five vertices. The Hausdorff squared distance therefore stabilizes at \(5/6\), with exactly the two-step chords farthest, while the four-cycle is an exceptional \(1\)-distance case.
