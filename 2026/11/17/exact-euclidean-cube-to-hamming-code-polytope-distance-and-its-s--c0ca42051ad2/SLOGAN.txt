For the binary Hamming code of length \(n=2^m-1\), the Euclidean Hausdorff distance between its codeword polytope and the unit cube is exactly \(2/\sqrt{n+3}\), attained at every noncode binary vertex and nowhere else. Each such farthest vertex has a unique nearest point in the relative interior of an explicit simplex face of dimension \((n-1)/2\).
