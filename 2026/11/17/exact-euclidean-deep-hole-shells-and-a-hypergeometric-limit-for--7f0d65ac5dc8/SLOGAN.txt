For every rank-at-least-three panhandle matroid with at least two handle elements, the Euclidean distance of each hypersimplex vertex to its base polytope depends only on the number \(j\) of selected handle elements and has squared value \((j-1)^2(1/j+1/(s-r+j))\) when \(j\ge2\). This yields every exact distance-shell multiplicity, the complete continuous farthest-point locus, an explicit product-of-hypersimplices nearest face, and a hypergeometric Gaussian law for typical squared distances.
