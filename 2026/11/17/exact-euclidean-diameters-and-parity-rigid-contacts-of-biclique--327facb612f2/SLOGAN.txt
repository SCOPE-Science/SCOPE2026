The Euclidean diameter of the orthogonally projected edge cube of every complete bipartite graph has squared value \(pq-\max\{(q/p)\mathbf1_{p\text{ odd}},(p/q)\mathbf1_{q\text{ odd}}\}\). Its entire outer sphere consists of rigid sign-matrix vertices: balanced row and column sums in even dimensions, or unit column sums with constant row sums (or the transposed condition) in the parity-obstructed regimes.
