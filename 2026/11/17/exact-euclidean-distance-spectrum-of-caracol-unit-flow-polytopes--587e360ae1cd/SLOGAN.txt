The ambient Euclidean distances between every pair of vertices of a caracol unit-flow polytope have a complete explicit polynomial spectrum, yielding a sharp diameter, all diametral path pairs, and an exact finite mean. When dimension grows, the squared distance between uniformly sampled distinct vertices, divided by the number of available path endpoints, converges at Kolmogorov rate \(O(n^{-1})\) to the classical \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,3)\) law.
