In Loday's canonical Euclidean associahedron, a uniformly chosen edge has an asymptotically finite, heavy-tailed Catalan-product length, while its mean length grows linearly with the number of leaves. The complete two-parameter length spectrum has a closed Catalan-binomial formula, and length-weighted sampling gives a universal \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1/2,1/2,1/2)\) limit for three normalized leaf-block sizes; Loday's local edge-length formula itself is established prior work.
