For every sparse-paving matroid, Euclidean projection from its enclosing hypersimplex onto the base polytope is obtained by one explicit redistribution across the unique violated circuit-hyperplane. The sharp Hausdorff gap is \(\sqrt{n/(k(n-k))}\), and, conditional on positive distance, a uniformly sampled hypersimplex point has an exactly universal \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,n-1)\) normalized distance law.
