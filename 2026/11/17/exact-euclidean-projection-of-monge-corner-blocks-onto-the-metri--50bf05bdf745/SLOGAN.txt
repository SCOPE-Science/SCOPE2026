Every normalized hollow symmetric Monge corner-block vertex has an exact nearest path-pseudometric supported on at most two canonical path cuts, with explicit rational Frobenius distance. The whole Monge simplex lies at squared Hausdorff distance \((n-2)(2n-3)/(4(n-1)^2)\) from its metric face, uniquely attained by the smallest corner block; this tends to \(1/2\).
