For every complete multipartite graph with at least three classes, the unique point of its edge-relaxed fractional stable-set polytope farthest from the integral stable-set polytope is the all-half vector; a one-parameter clipped reciprocal-size law gives the exact Euclidean gap. The gap is maximized by balanced class sizes and minimized by one large class with all remaining classes singleton, with an exact three-class harmonic formula and a sharp four-class projection-contact transition.
