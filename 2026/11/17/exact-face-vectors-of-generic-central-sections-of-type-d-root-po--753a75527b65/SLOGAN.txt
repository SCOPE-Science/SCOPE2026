Every generic central hyperplane section of the type-\(D_n\) root polytope, for \(n\ge5\), has the same combinatorial type and an explicit full face vector: \(f_k=(k+1)2^{k+3}\binom{n}{k+3}+(2^{k+2}-4)\binom{n}{k+2}\) below the top rank, with exactly \(2^n+2n-6\) facets. In particular, the section has \(8\binom n3\) vertices although the original root polytope has only \(2n(n-1)\) vertices.
