The symmetric edge polytope of \(q\) triangles sharing an edge has a complete two-family binomial spectrum of facet distances, including an exact second-shell gap and its sharp multiplicities for all integers \(q\ge2\). Its maximum Euclidean insphere has a two-dimensional family of centers for even \(q\), and a one-dimensional family for odd \(q\), despite the uniform count of \(3\cdot2^q\) facets.
