The complete Euclidean face-to-complement distance spectrum of every orthogonally weighted cross-polytope is explicit: nonfacet distances are reciprocal square roots of subset sums of inverse-square semiaxes, while facet distances are twice the full-sum value. The pyramidal width is controlled by omitting one longest axis, and the width-to-diameter ratio is maximized uniquely by the regular cross-polytope, with an exact anisotropy penalty.
