The family is a standard Hanner construction and the facial distance is an established convergence-condition invariant, not an artificial diagnostic. The exact spectrum classifies every face rank and all extremizers for unbounded dimensions; the asymmetry law shows that the smaller cube's dimension is immaterial for the sharp value. These are substantive sharp geometric relationships rather than a routine evaluation of one numerical example.
