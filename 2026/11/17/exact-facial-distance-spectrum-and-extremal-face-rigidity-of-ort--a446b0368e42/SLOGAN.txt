For the orthogonal join of cubes of dimensions \(2\le p\le q\), the exact Euclidean facial distance is \(2/\sqrt{q(q+1)}\), attained only by joins of a smaller-block facet and a larger-block vertex (and their swaps when \(p=q\)). Its complete face-distance spectrum exhibits an exact \(1/\sqrt{q+1}\) loss relative to the larger cube alone, despite an unchanged larger-block diameter.
