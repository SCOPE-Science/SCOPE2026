For uniform finite Hamming products, the exact centered exponential embedding constant of the real 1-Lipschitz ball is the product of explicit one-alphabet Bernoulli factors, and **every** extremizer is an additive sum of optimal alphabet-cut indicators. The optimal cut sizes undergo a finite parameter staircase, while their continuous-alphabet approximation has an exact second-order arithmetic quantization profile with a full interval of subsequential limits at \(\lambda=1\).
