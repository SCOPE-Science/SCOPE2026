For a hub-start rotor walk on a spider with \(k\ge3\) arms of length \(L\ge2\), the exact first-periodic-entry time is determined by the ordered depths of initially outward-pointing rotors and has sharp maximum \((L-1)((2k-1)L-1)\), attained by only \(k\) initial assignments. With random fair internal rotors and fixed \(L\), this time divided by \(k\) converges to \(3L(L-1)/2\), while the chance of attaining the maximum is exactly \(2^{-k(L-1)}\).
