# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** Equation (5) gives a feasible nonnegative hollow bistochastic projection for every permutation with at least one fixed point, including all boundary values. Its explicit two-class row/column potentials satisfy the global convex quadratic optimality inequality (6) on every allowed cell, proving uniqueness and the exact squared-distance formula. Strict monotonicity of the rational shell function establishes the sharp gap and the only farthest permutation. Birkhoff decomposition and convexity extend the Hausdorff extremum to all matrices. Conjugation averaging, the normal spectrum of a permutation matrix, trace-norm equality, and the spectral-norm trace bound cover every Schatten endpoint and the nearest-point transition. The exact rational checker tested all 5,910 permutations of orders 3–7; independent numerical optimization tested 18 cases and singular-value calculations tested 870 matrices. Numerical tests corroborate, rather than replace, the all-order proof.

**Originality — PASS on the inspected record; priority provisional.** The full 2022 deranged-matching source [1] studies matching enumerations, not distances. The full 2023 bipartite matching-polytope paper [2] proves the convex hull description but does not calculate projections. Bouthat et al. [3] prove Schatten Chebyshev-radius formulas for the whole Birkhoff polytope, which do not imply nearest points on its hollow face. The closely related public 2026 derangement Löwner-ellipsoid result [4] was inspected in full scientific RESULT form; its outer ellipsoid and isotropy certificate do not imply the fixed-point distance shells or the nuclear nearest-point family. Searches for fixed-point projection formulas, zero-diagonal transportation projection, exact Hausdorff distance and Schatten equality cases found no covering statement. A possible unindexed older projection formula remains a genuine priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The derangement face is the natural perfect-matching polytope of the crown graph, and the complete Euclidean distance profile of every full-assignment vertex is a natural geometric approximation invariant. The invariant has a sharp, dimension-independent squared gap between its highest vertex shells and an exact distribution by fixed-point counts. Across Schatten metrics, the worst-case separation is explicit for every norm exponent, while the nuclear endpoint creates an entire positive-dimensional polytope of nearest points rather than one nearest matrix. These are substantive metric and optimizer-geometry classifications, not mere evaluation of a single isolated example.

**Limitations.** The gap applies only to permutation vertices; the theorem does not classify every spectral-norm farthest point, nor arbitrary forbidden-matchings patterns. An independent mathematical audit has not been performed. Historical priority remains provisional.
