The Euclidean distance from a permutation vertex of the Birkhoff polytope to its derangement face depends exactly on the permutation's number of fixed points, with a dimension-independent squared gap of \(2\) between the farthest and next-farthest permutation vertices. The full Hausdorff distance equals \(n(n-1)^{1/p-1}\) in every Schatten \(p\)-norm; at the nuclear-norm endpoint the identity's nearest-point locus expands from a singleton to a \(n(n-3)/2\)-dimensional fractional perfect-matching polytope.
