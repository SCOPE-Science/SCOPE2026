For a natural affine infinite-branch Cantor return map, the entire finite-mean gap-depth spectrum is \(\dim_HE_m=[m\log m-(m-1)\log(m-1)+(m-1)\log2]/(m\log r)\), peaking at depth mean \(3\) but retaining a positive limit as the mean diverges. Its invariant conformal law has an exact negative-binomial depth-sum distribution with a sharp local large-deviation prefactor.
