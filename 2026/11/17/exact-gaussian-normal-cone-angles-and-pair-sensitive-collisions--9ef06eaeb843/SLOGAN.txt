For all sparse-paving matroid base polytopes of rank and corank at least three, every intrinsic Gaussian vertex normal-cone angle equals \(\binom nr^{-1}(1+d/(r(n-r)))\), where \(d\) counts adjacent forbidden bases. The probability that two independent Gaussian objectives select the same vertex is exactly \([\binom nr+\lambda(1+1/(r(n-r)))+8\mu/(r^2(n-r)^2)]/\binom nr^2\), distinguishing circuit-hyperplane arrangements with identical numbers of nonbases.
