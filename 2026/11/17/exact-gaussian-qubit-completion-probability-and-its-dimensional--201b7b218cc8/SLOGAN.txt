For independent square complex-Ginibre Wishart pairs, the exact probability of a positive joint-Hausdorff bilinear-resolvent completion is \(1\) in dimension one, \((24-28\log2)/81\) in dimension two, and \(0\) in every higher dimension. The qubit constant follows from a sharp Haar-eigenvector overlap criterion and exact integration of the Wishart eigenvalue-ratio law.
