A complete left-to-right sweep of adjacent arithmetic pair-averaging on \(n\) real agents has the sharp complete-pair Gini contraction factor \(1-\min_{1\le k\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor}2(1-2^{-k})/[k(n-k)]\). A trailing binary block attains the norm; from nine agents onward a balanced block is always optimal, and the exact contraction deficit is asymptotic to \(8/n^2\).
