# Exact graph-spectral stabilization and phase-basin onset for balanced antipodal oscillators

## Finding

Consider a finite network of identical phase oscillators with symmetric nonnegative pairwise weights and a globally shared asymmetric three-body interaction. Every balanced antipodal configuration is an equilibrium modulo common phase rotation. **The graph obstruction and the exact triadic stabilization threshold** can be computed from one compressed signed Laplacian and a scalar Schur complement. Specifically, define the self-adjoint compression \(C\), the coupling vector \(b\), and the scalar \(\alpha\) below. The antipodal orbit is locally exponentially attracting on the phase quotient **if and only if**

\[
C\prec0\quad\text{and}\quad K_2>\frac{K_1}{d}\bigl(\alpha-\langle b,C^{-1}b\rangle\bigr).
\]

If \(C\) is not strictly negative definite, no value of the nonnegative global triadic gain can confer exponential attraction. Consequently the triadic term repairs precisely one collective direction; the remaining \(N-2\) shape directions must already be contracting.

For two equally sized, uniformly weighted communities, the criterion has an explicit sharp form: the within-community pair weight must exceed the between-community pair weight, and the global triadic gain must exceed a computable threshold. The invariant circle of fully synchronized communities additionally has an **exact antipodal basin fraction** which turns on at square-root order, and the critical antipodal configuration is unstable even at the zero-eigenvalue endpoint.

## Assumptions and scope

Fix an even integer \(N\ge4\). Let \(A=(A_{ij})\) be symmetric, \(A_{ij}\ge0\), \(A_{ii}=0\), and let the weighted undirected graph be connected. Fix a positive pairwise normalization \(d>0\) and gains \(K_1>0\), \(K_2\ge0\). Assume every intrinsic frequency is equal and remove its common rotation. The flow is

\[
\dot\theta_i=\frac{K_1}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{N}A_{ij}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i)
+\frac{K_2}{N^2}\sum_{j,\ell=1}^{N}\sin(2\theta_j-\theta_\ell-\theta_i).
\tag{1}
\]

The double sum is unrestricted, **including repeated indices**. Let \(s_i\in\{-1,+1\}\) with \(\sum_i s_i=0\). The balanced antipodal configuration is \(\theta_i^*=0\) when \(s_i=+1\), and \(\theta_i^*=\pi\) when \(s_i=-1\). Define

\[
(Bv)_i=\sum_j A_{ij}s_is_j(v_j-v_i),\qquad e=\frac{s}{\sqrt N},
\quad W=\{v:\langle v,{\bf1}\rangle=\langle v,s\rangle=0\}.
\]

Let \(P_W\) be the Euclidean orthogonal projection on \(W\). Define \(C=P_WB|_W\), \(b=P_WBe\) and \(\alpha=\langle e,Be\rangle\). Negative definite refers to the real symmetric quadratic form restricted to \(W\). Attraction is exponential for the equilibrium circle generated by common phase shifts, i.e. for the induced flow on the quotient by common rotation.

For the explicit community specialization, put \(N=2m\), \(m\ge2\), take \(A_{ij}=u>0\) for two distinct vertices in the same community, and \(A_{ij}=v>0\) for vertices in opposite communities. Set the natural mean weighted degree normalization

\[
d=(m-1)u+mv,\qquad H=\frac{2mvK_1}{d}.
\]

## Proof

**1. Exact Jacobian.** Put \(z_q=N^{-1}\sum_j e^{iq\theta_j}\). The three-body term in (1) is \(K_2\operatorname{Im}(z_2\overline{z_1}e^{-i\theta_i})\). At a balanced antipodal state, \(z_1=0\), \(z_2=1\), and the derivative with respect to a phase perturbation \(h\) equals

\[
-\frac{K_2}{N}s_i\sum_j s_jh_j.
\]

The pairwise derivative is \(K_1 B/d\). Therefore the **full** Jacobian, not an approximation, is

\[
J=\frac{K_1}{d}B-K_2ee^{\mathsf T}.
\tag{2}
\]

Since \(B\) is symmetric and \(B\mathbf1=0\), the uniform-phase direction is neutral and its orthogonal complement is invariant. Because the state is balanced, \(e\perp\mathbf1\), so \(\mathbf1^\perp=\operatorname{span}\{e\}\oplus W\) is an orthogonal sum.

**2. Exhaustive spectral stability criterion.** In this decomposition, (2) has the symmetric block form

\[
J\big|_{\mathbf1^\perp}=\begin{pmatrix}
(K_1/d)\alpha-K_2 &(K_1/d)b^{\mathsf T}\\
(K_1/d)b &(K_1/d)C
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{3}
\]

A symmetric block matrix is negative definite exactly when its lower-right block is negative definite and its scalar Schur complement is negative. Applied to (3), this is precisely

\[
C\prec0,\qquad K_2>\frac{K_1}{d}\bigl(\alpha-\langle b,C^{-1}b\rangle\bigr).
\tag{4}
\]

If \(C\) has a nonnegative Rayleigh direction, the full matrix cannot be negative definite for any \(K_2\). If \(C\prec0\), equality in (4) creates exactly one additional zero eigenvalue; smaller \(K_2\) creates a positive eigenvalue. Finally, because the graph is connected and the two sign classes are nonempty, the crossing-edge weight is positive, and

\[
\alpha=\frac4N\sum_{i:s_i=+1}\sum_{j:s_j=-1}A_{ij}>0.
\]

Also \(\langle b,C^{-1}b\rangle\le0\) when \(C\prec0\), so the threshold is strictly positive. Standard hyperbolic linearization on the phase quotient gives local exponential attraction precisely under (4); a nonnegative eigenvalue prevents exponential attraction (but a zero eigenvalue alone does not determine nonlinear Lyapunov stability in general).

**3. Two-community complete-block specialization.** At the two-community antipodal pattern, the \(2m-2\) independent within-community zero-sum modes satisfy \(C=m(v-u)I_W\). The community-difference mode \(e\) is an eigenvector of \(B\), with \(\alpha=2mv\) and \(b=0\). Thus the entire nonzero quotient spectrum is

\[
\underbrace{\frac{mK_1}{d}(v-u),\ldots,\frac{mK_1}{d}(v-u)}_{2m-2\ \text{times}},
\qquad H-K_2.
\tag{5}
\]

It is strictly negative if and only if \(u>v\) and \(K_2>H\). In particular, mere triadic strengthening can **never** overcome inadequate within-community assortativity. For a graph with edges exclusively across the two sign classes, \(B\) is the positive semidefinite graph Laplacian, hence \(C\succ0\) for every connected such graph with \(N\ge4\), providing an absolute stabilization obstruction.

**4. Exact invariant-circle dynamics and basin size.** The set on which all \(m\) oscillators in each community share a common phase is flow invariant by permutation symmetry. Denote their relative phase by \(\phi=\theta_- -\theta_+\), with \(\phi\in\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z)\). Direct substitution into (1), keeping the unrestricted triadic sum, yields the **exact** scalar equation

\[
\dot\phi=-\sin\phi\,[H+K_2\cos\phi].
\tag{6}
\]

For \(K_2>H\), the sinks \(\phi=0\) and \(\phi=\pi\) are separated by two repelling fixed points at \(\phi=\arccos(-H/K_2)\) and \(\phi=2\pi-\arccos(-H/K_2)\). Therefore the open basin of \(\pi\) on this invariant circle has length \(2\arccos(H/K_2)\). Its fraction of uniformly distributed relative phases is exactly

\[
\mathcal B_\pi(K_2)=\frac1\pi\arccos\!\left(\frac H{K_2}\right),\qquad K_2>H.
\tag{7}
\]

Writing \(K_2=H+\mu\), \(\mu\downarrow0\), Taylor expansion at one gives

\[
\mathcal B_\pi(H+\mu)=\frac1\pi\sqrt{\frac{2\mu}{H}}+O(\mu^{3/2}).
\tag{8}
\]

At the exact threshold \(K_2=H\), with \(x=\phi-\pi\), equation (6) becomes

\[
\dot x=\frac H2x^3+O(x^5),
\tag{9}
\]

which drives small nonzero \(x\) away from antiphasing. Hence \(\pi\) is nonlinearly Lyapunov unstable there even when \(u>v\) and every within-community mode contracts. The two intervening repellers emerge at distance \(\arccos(H/K_2)=\sqrt{2\mu/H}+O(\mu^{3/2})\) from \(\pi\), so the square-root basin onset is sharp.

## Relationship to prior work

Sharma and coauthors' mixed-order Kuramoto model [1] motivates (1), and their 2026 study numerically analyzes synchronization on random graphs. We specialize to identical frequencies and characterize a different object: the local stability and invariant-circle basin of balanced antipodal configurations on arbitrary connected undirected weighted graphs. Skardal, Adhikari and Restrepo [2] study higher-order interactions with community structure, including antiphase states, primarily through large-population order-parameter reductions; this work does **not** claim first discovery of antiphase clustering. The arbitrary finite-graph compressed-spectrum criterion (4), its complete-block consequence (5), and the exact finite-dimensional invariant-circle basin law (7) are the particular claims compared against that literature. Skardal and Arenas [3] give a network stability approach for the **different** three-body coupling \(\sin(\theta_j+\theta_\ell-2\theta_i)\); it does not imply (4) for the asymmetric coupling combined with positive pairwise graph edges.

Other finite-graph results for complete graphs, barbell graphs and four-site paths use special topologies, so cannot imply (4) on arbitrary weighted connected graphs. The specific Schur-complement criterion subsumes the corresponding local linear calculations, but neither replaces nor claims their additional nonlinear conclusions.

## Limitations

The theorem requires balanced sign classes, identical intrinsic frequencies, a symmetric nonnegative connected pairwise network and the exact unrestricted-index global asymmetric triadic convention. It does not give attraction for heterogeneous natural frequencies or arbitrary unbalanced clusters, nor classify all equilibrium states or global basins on the full phase torus. The fraction in (7) refers **only** to the invariant one-dimensional circle of internally synchronized communities, not to uniform sampling from the full \((N-1)\)-dimensional phase quotient. A nonhyperbolic endpoint for a general graph is not classified nonlinearly. The cited prior art is not exhaustive; independently confirming historical priority and the scientific conclusion remains outstanding.
