The exact two-input graph-variation Lipschitz constant of weighted graph total-variation denoising is the maximum cut-amplification ratio over all connected-block averaging projections, independently of every positive regularization parameter. On the graph formed by two \(q\)-cliques joined by one bridge, the constant is at least \(\lfloor q^2/4\rfloor\), establishing quadratic worst-case amplification despite Euclidean nonexpansivity.
