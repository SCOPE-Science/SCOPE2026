For global rank-order sorting on any finite connected positively weighted graph, the exact two-input graph-total-variation Lipschitz constant is \(\max_{1\le k<n}\max_{|S|=k}b_w(S)/\min_{|S|=k}b_w(S)\). It equals one only for uniformly weighted complete graphs, while two \(q\)-cliques joined by a bridge have sharp amplification \(\lfloor q^2/2\rfloor+1\).
