For every real two-plane of \(2\times2\) matrices and every \(0<p<q\le\infty\), the Schatten embedding restriction norm has an exact graph-parameter formula, with rank-one-containing planes giving norm one. The exact distance to its unique dominant conformal optimizer is recovered from that norm; the sharp local loss is quadratic for finite \(q\) but linear at \(q=\infty\).
