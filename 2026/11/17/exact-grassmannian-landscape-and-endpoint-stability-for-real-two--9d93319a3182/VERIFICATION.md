# Verification and scope

Analytic proof reconstructs all identities independently of numerical experiments. Numerical tests in `check.py` compare direct matrix SVD with the complex-linear/antilinear singular-value rule, verify the graph formula for real-linear maps on sampled unit-circle points, and test endpoint asymptotics. A finite test does not prove a universal claim. All quantities use real two-dimensional planes and the real Hilbert–Schmidt Grassmannian. Independent audit: not performed.
