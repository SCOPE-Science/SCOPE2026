For a finite uniform binary ultrametric, the sharp Lipschitz norm of every independently signed Haar transform is an explicit maximum of depth-weighted Haar row masses, yielding a complete formula for arbitrary decreasing distance scales. On the infinite binary Cantor space, the canonical Haar basis is unconditional in the little-Lipschitz norm exactly when the distance scales have uniform finite-step geometric decay; at geometric ratio \(\rho\), the exact constant is \(1+2\rho^2/((1-\rho)(\rho+2))\).
