At the Bochner \(L^1\) endpoint, the complete Hahn–Banach extension fiber for a Hilbert \(\ell_r\)-sum has an exact diameter and all affine Chebyshev centers determined by the essential norm profile of its dual representative. Unlike the strict \(L^p\) range, global uniqueness survives precisely for a one-atom measure algebra and \(r>1\); at \(r=1\) every nonzero fiber has maximal diameter but a unique Chebyshev center.
