In edge-correlation Euclidean coordinates, the exact Hausdorff gap between a cactus graph's elliptope and cut polytope is the square root of the sum over its cycles of \(\bigl(n\cos(\pi/n)-n+2\bigr)^2/n\). All farthest points are classified: each cycle carries an odd-sign vector scaled by \(\cos(\pi/n)\), bridge coordinates are arbitrary, and the nearest cut point on each cycle is the same sign vector scaled by \((n-2)/n\).
