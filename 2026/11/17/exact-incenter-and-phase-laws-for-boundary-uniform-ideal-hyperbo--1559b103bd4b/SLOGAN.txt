For the ideal hyperbolic triangle with three independently Euclidean-angle-uniform boundary vertices, the Poincaré-disk incenter has exact area density \(3\sqrt3/[\pi^2(1+r^2+r^4)]\) at Euclidean radius \(r\). Its squared radius strictly stochastically favors the center over uniform disk area, while its hyperbolic distance has sharp survival tail \((4\sqrt3/\pi)e^{-T}\); the residual orientation obeys an explicit threefold Poisson kernel.
