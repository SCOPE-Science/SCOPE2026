For the planar absolute-value map of Cima–Gasull–Mañosa–Mañosas, the first-exit time and destination quadrant from a repelling-focus neighborhood are **exactly independent** under conditional uniform planar area. The three destination probabilities are explicit piecewise-rational functions of \(t=(a+2b)/(b-2a)\), with single-channel regimes at \(t\le1/3\) and \(t\ge2\) and a further branch transition at \(t=3/4\).
