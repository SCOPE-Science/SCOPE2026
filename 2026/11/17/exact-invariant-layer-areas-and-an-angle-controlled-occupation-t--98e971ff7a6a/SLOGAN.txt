The nested invariant layers of a four-cone translated-cone exchange have an exact quadratic area spectrum and a sharp angle \(\varphi=\pi/3\) at which all layers have equal area. As \(\lambda\uparrow1\), the layer index of a point sampled by area converges to the density \(f_\varphi(u)=[4\cos\varphi+2(1-2\cos\varphi)u]/[1+2\cos\varphi]\), uniform exactly at that angle.
