Quadratic Jacobi-weighted least-squares projection has an exact sharp total-variation gain at every symmetric weight parameter \(\alpha>-1\), attained by two off-center single jumps. Its norm approaches \(5/3\) as \(\alpha\downarrow-1\), grows like \(\sqrt{2/(\pi e)}(\alpha+1)\) as \(\alpha\to\infty\), and is not monotone between these regimes.
