The star-indexed sum of \(m\) coordinate diamonds has exact, anisotropic John and Löwner ellipsoids in every dimension \(m+1\). All \(2m+2^{m+1}\) facets touch the inner ellipsoid, but only \(2+2^m\) of its \(2\cdot3^m-2^m\) vertices touch the outer ellipsoid; the exact outer-to-inner volume ratio is \(m^{(m+1)/2}/\sqrt{m-1}\).
