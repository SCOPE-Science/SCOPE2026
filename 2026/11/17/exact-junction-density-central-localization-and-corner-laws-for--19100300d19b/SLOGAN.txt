Two independent uniform-endpoint square-spanning chords, one left-to-right and one bottom-to-top, have a unique junction with an exact piecewise-rational spatial density and a unique central peak of \(4\). The junction has a direction-dependent corner profile with corner-square probability \((5/12)\varepsilon^2+o(\varepsilon^2)\), whereas complete thin boundary strips have probability \(\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)+O(\varepsilon^2)\).
