The directional-width function of a regular simplex has exactly \(2^N-2\) strict local minima on the oriented sphere of directions, one for each nontrivial vertex bipartition, and all partitions less balanced than the maximizing split are suboptimal. Around every one of these minima its sharp fixed-angle lower envelope is exactly \(w_{N,t}\cos\theta+\gamma_{N,t}\sin\theta\) on an explicit positive interval, with \(\gamma_{N,t}=1/\sqrt{\max\{t(t-1),(N-t)(N-t-1)\}}\).
