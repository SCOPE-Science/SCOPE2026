A globally stable homogeneous cubic spiral has an **exact** positive radius-squared amplitude that is periodic in shifted logarithmic physical time. Every nonzero anisotropy destroys ordinary \(1/t\) asymptotics: the normalized radius oscillates forever and admits an asymptotic scaling ratio only on the sharp multiplicative lattice \(\{e^{2\pi ak}:k\in\mathbb Z\}\), even while all trajectories converge to the origin.
