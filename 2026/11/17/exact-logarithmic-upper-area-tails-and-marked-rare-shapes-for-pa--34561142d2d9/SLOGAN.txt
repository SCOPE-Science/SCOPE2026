Three arclength-uniform points on any parallelogram boundary have an exact, angle-independent near-maximal triangle-area tail with a positive \(\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)\) term, a universal next coefficient, and strict rhombus extremality at every sufficiently high threshold. Conditionally, the exceptional triangles have a universal Dirichlet area-budget law independent of a uniform logarithmic location mark, while the missing-side probabilities retain the parallelogram's side-length ratio.
