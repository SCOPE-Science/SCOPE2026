# Scientific review: finite-leakage Markov sign dynamics and directed cross-correlations

## Correctness

**PASS.** The map's four affine branches cover whole partition cells with slope magnitude three. The invariant density, irreducible aperiodic four-state transition law and its sign lumping are derived explicitly. The eight-dimensional cellwise-affine transfer recurrence gives four rational generating functions, including the otherwise hidden negative transfer eigenmode. Their coefficients prove all-lag identities and strict time direction. An executable exact-rational checker reconstructs the operators and verifies the formulas and test lags. The finite-state Markov CLT applies only to the sign observable as stated. Limits are fixed-parameter and stationary.

## Originality

**PASS subject to publication-priority uncertainty.** The 2024 original paired-tent article establishes small-leakage averaging and defines the relevant family but does not calculate the finite half-leakage word law or mixed-direction correlations. Horan's Markov transfer framework and the earlier exactly studied \(q=(\sqrt3-1)/2\) case are credited: the latter has non-Markov sign data, unlike this \(1/2\) specialization. The 2026 diffusion extension gives general small-leakage CLTs rather than these four explicit rational finite-parameter transforms. Search comparisons found no stronger direct implication; the compact rational-matrix method makes unindexed prior equivalent calculations the principal residual risk. Novelty of standard Markov lumping or transfer methods is not asserted.

## Value

**PASS.** Exact coarse Markovization at a turning-point two-cycle is naturally motivated by the metastability literature's use of approximate interwell chains. The finding identifies a mechanism that a Markov coarse observable cannot reveal: a strictly directed mixed covariance with a separate negative mode, even while each observable's autocorrelation is geometric. The formulas provide a reproducible finite-leakage benchmark, not just an arbitrary parameter substitution.

## Limits and closest work

The result is restricted to deterministic equal leakage \(1/2\) with stationary initialization, and does not address generic perturbations, nonlinear observation statistics or arbitrary initial laws. Principal comparisons: González-Tokman–Peters (arXiv:2406.09672, §8), Horan (arXiv:1910.12112; 2020 thesis Chapter 5), the distinct published Markov \(q\) example, and *Jumping for Diffusion in Random Metastable Systems* (2026, §7). A stronger unindexed exact-statistics formula could cover the present calculations; scientific originality is qualified by that access/search limitation.
