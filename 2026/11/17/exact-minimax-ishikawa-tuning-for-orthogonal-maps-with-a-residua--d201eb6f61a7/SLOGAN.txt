For every real Hilbert-space orthogonal map with residual error bound \(\kappa>1/2\), the unique constant-parameter Ishikawa scheme minimizing the worst-case contraction has factor \((4\kappa^2-1)/(4\kappa^2+1)\), attained by an explicit planar rotation. The same parameters are exactly optimal at every fixed iteration horizon; no analogous claim is made for variable parameters.
