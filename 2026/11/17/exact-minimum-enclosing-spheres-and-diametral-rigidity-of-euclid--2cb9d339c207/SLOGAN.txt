For every Euclidean pentasm \(\operatorname{conv}\{0,e_1,\ldots,e_d\}+[0,t(e_1+e_2)]\) with \(d\ge3\) and \(t>0\), an explicit rational center gives its unique smallest enclosing ball, whose boundary contains exactly \(d\) of the \(2d+1\) vertices. Its squared diameter is \(2t^2+2t+2\), with every diametral pair joining one of two distinguished translated vertices to one of the remaining \(d-2\) untranslated basis vertices; the entire diameter graph is \(K_{2,d-2}\) with isolated vertices.
