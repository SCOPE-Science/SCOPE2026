# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The statement is precisely for the unit-radius disk covering on the unit-edge honeycomb graph. The proof reconstructs local covering multiplicity \(2\) or \(3\), uses exact motif densities to obtain per-edge and per-path area, and counts all exposed circular arcs. Algebra eliminates the deleted path count and yields perimeter quantization; the Bernoulli densities follow from area/motif expectations and ergodic averaging. Numerical checks are corroborative, not proof of infinite results. Boundary tangencies have zero arc length.

## Value — PASS
The identities quantify exact loss of sensing area and exposed-hole boundary for **every finite failure pattern**, not merely a single configuration. The quantized perimeter and joint area–perimeter identity are discrete–continuous structural consequences; the Bernoulli formulas give exact reliability profiles for arbitrary independent failure intensity. The underlying redundancy and minimum-failure observation are motivation; the full motif calculus is the contribution.
