# Exact nearest-neighbor pressure and coexistence in rational frequency shifts

## Finding
For every integer \(k\ge1\), let \(\Sigma_k=\Sigma(k/(k+1))\) be the one-sided binary frequency shift of Reyes [1] with left shift \(\sigma\). For arbitrary real numbers \(a,b,c,d\), give the two-symbol potential \(\phi(x)=u_{x_0x_1}\) the values \(u_{00}=a\), \(u_{01}=b\), \(u_{10}=c\), and \(u_{11}=d\). Define
\[
 s=b+c-a-d,\qquad N=k+1,\qquad L=\log(1+e^s),\qquad R=\frac{a+kd+L}{N}.
\]
Then the topological pressure of \(\phi\) on \(\Sigma_k\) is exactly
\[
 \boxed{P_{\Sigma_k}(\phi)=\max\{a,d,R\}.}
\]
There are three explicit candidate ergodic equilibrium probabilities, and *no others*: the fixed-point atoms \(\delta_{0^\infty}\) and \(\delta_{1^\infty}\), and the stationary renewal measure \(\nu_s\) defined below. The equilibrium probabilities are exactly the convex hull of those candidates whose free energies attain the displayed maximum. If \(R>\max\{a,d\}\), the unique equilibrium probability is \(\nu_s\); if \(a>\max\{d,R\}\) (respectively \(d>\max\{a,R\}\)), it is the zero (respectively one) fixed-point atom. Equality \(R=a>d\) or \(R=d>a\) gives precisely a line segment of equilibria, one endpoint atomic and one endpoint renewal. Three-way coexistence cannot occur because \(L>0\).

The renewal equilibrium has independent generator sizes \(M\in\{1,2,\ldots\}\) with
\[
 \Pr\{M=m\}=\theta(1-\theta)^{m-1},\qquad
 \theta=\frac{e^s}{1+e^s}.
\]
Each generator is the binary word \(0^m1^{km}\). After forming the stationary discrete-time suspension over this sequence of generators, its nearest-neighbor frequencies are
\[
 (\nu_s[00],\nu_s[01],\nu_s[10],\nu_s[11])
 =\frac1N(1-\theta,\theta,\theta,k-\theta),
 \qquad h_{\nu_s}(\sigma)=\frac{H_{\rm b}(\theta)}N,
\]
where \(H_{\rm b}(t)=-t\log t-(1-t)\log(1-t)\). These formulas hold even on the coexistence boundary, where \(\nu_s\) is still an ergodic equilibrium state.

Two natural one-edge perturbations show sharp first-order freezing. For \(\phi_t(x)=t\mathbf1_{\{x_0x_1=00\}}\), \(t\ge0\),
\[
 P(t)=\max\left\{t,\frac{\log(1+e^t)}{k+1}\right\};
\]
its unique positive transition is \(t=\log\rho_k\), where \(\rho_k>1\) is the unique root of \(\rho_k^{k+1}=\rho_k+1\). For \(\psi_t(x)=t\mathbf1_{\{x_0x_1=11\}}\), \(t\ge0\),
\[
 P(t)=\max\left\{t,\frac{kt+\log(1+e^{-t})}{k+1}\right\};
\]
its transition is the **same for every** \(k\): \(t=\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)\). On each transition the positive-entropy renewal equilibrium coexists with a zero-entropy fixed-point equilibrium, and the pressure is nondifferentiable.

## Assumptions and scope
The shift \(\Sigma(p)\) is *the* frequency shift as defined by Becker–Baraviera and studied by Reyes [1], not an arbitrary shift with a prescribed frequency. Reyes' Theorem C identifies, for \(k\ge2\), its entire invariant-measure support with the coded subshift \(X_k\) generated by \(\{0^m1^{km}:m\ge1\}\); for \(k=1\), \(\Sigma_1=X_1\). We invoke exactly this published structural result. The potential is a real-valued two-symbol cylinder function. Pressure is the variational quantity \(\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal M_\sigma(\Sigma_k)}(h_\mu(\sigma)+\int\phi\,d\mu)\). All measures in the statement are invariant Borel probability measures; logarithms are natural.

## Proof
**1. Reduction to renewal returns.** Work first with an ergodic invariant probability \(\mu\). If \(\mu[10]=0\), stationarity gives \(\mu[01]=0\), and ergodicity forces \(\mu\) to be one of the two fixed-point atoms. Otherwise \(\mu[10]>0\). For almost every point sampled at the marker \([10]\), the successive return words are necessarily \(0^m1^{km}\) with \(m\ge1\), by the defining generator structure of \(X_k\). The marker occurs precisely at boundaries between these words; hence this return coding is unique almost everywhere and has the full countable alphabet \(m\ge1\). The return time is \(Nm\). Kac's theorem gives \(\mathbb E M=1/(N\mu[10])<\infty\). The return transformation is therefore a stationary process \((M_j)\) over positive integers, with one-letter marginal \(p_m\).

**2. Roof and energy identities.** Count cyclic two-letter edges in one word \(0^m1^{km}\), including the transition to the next word. The counts for \(00,01,10,11\) are \((m-1,1,1,km-1)\), so the accumulated potential is
\[
 E_m=m(a+kd)+s.
\]
Abramov's formula for entropy and Kac's formula for integrals give
\[
 h_\mu(\sigma)+\int\phi\,d\mu
 =\frac{h_{\rm ret}+\mathbb E[E_M]}{N\mathbb E M},
\]
where \(h_{\rm ret}\) is the entropy rate of the stationary return-size process. The standard conditioning inequality gives \(h_{\rm ret}\le H(p)\), with equality only if the sizes are independent and identically distributed. The entropy is finite: a positive-integer distribution of finite mean has bounded entropy, as follows by comparison to a geometric law with the same mean.

**3. Sharp relative-entropy bound.** Put \(q_m=e^s\exp(-mL)\). As \(e^{-L}=1/(1+e^s)\), the sequence \(q\) is a normalized geometric probability with success parameter \(\theta=e^s/(1+e^s)\). Its relative entropy against \(p\) is
\[
 D(p\Vert q)=-H(p)-s+L\,\mathbb E M\ge0.
\]
Thus \(h_{\rm ret}+s\le L\,\mathbb E M\), proving
\[
 h_\mu(\sigma)+\int\phi\,d\mu \le R.
\]
Equality requires both \(D(p\Vert q)=0\) and equality in the entropy-rate bound, hence the unique stationary iid geometric return process. Its expected roof is finite, so its stationary suspension exists and projects to a uniquely defined ergodic \(\nu_s\) on the coded shift. In particular \(\nu_s\) attains \(R\). The two fixed-point measures attain \(a\) and \(d\). Taking the maximum, then using ergodic decomposition and entropy affinity, proves the pressure and the complete equilibrium-simplex classification.

**4. Frequencies, entropy and transitions.** Since \(\mathbb E M=1/\theta\), divide the expected edge counts \((\mathbb E M-1,1,1,k\mathbb E M-1)\) by \(N\mathbb E M\), obtaining the displayed four frequencies. Geometric entropy is \(H(M)=H_{\rm b}(\theta)/\theta\); dividing by the expected roof \(N/\theta\) gives \(h_{\nu_s}=H_{\rm b}(\theta)/N>0\). If \(a=d\), then \(R=a+L/N>a\), so three-way equality is impossible. For the \(00\)-reward, \(s=-t\) and \(a=t\), giving \(R=\log(1+e^t)/N\); equality with \(t\) is \(e^{Nt}=1+e^t\), whose unique positive solution follows because \(z^N-z-1\) increases for \(z>1\). For the \(11\)-reward, \(s=-t\), \(d=t\), and \(R=(kt+\log(1+e^{-t}))/N\); equality with \(t\) is \(e^{2t}=e^t+1\), independent of \(k\). Each crossing is transverse: the fixed-point free-energy gradient in the rewarded edge direction is \(1\), whereas the renewal derivative is its corresponding edge frequency \((1-\theta)/N\) or \((k-\theta)/N\), both strictly below \(1\). Thus pressure has a genuine corner and the two distinct ergodic equilibria coexist.

## Verification
The accompanying standalone standard-library program `verify_pressure.py` checks the generator edge counts for \(88\) integer pairs, the geometric roof normalization and energy–entropy identity across \(42\) parameter examples, six finite truncated renewal roots per example, and the scalar freezing equalities for eleven values of \(k\). Execute with `python3 verify_pressure.py`. Passing these finite tests corroborates algebra but does **not** establish the all-parameter theorem. The proof above is an exact variational argument based on the cited invariant-support theorem, Kac and Abramov identities, and nonnegativity of relative entropy.

## Relationship to prior work
Reyes [1], Theorems C and D, establishes the coded support, topological entropy and unique measure of maximal entropy for the *zero potential*. The new statement treats **every** nearest-neighbor potential and gives its entire equilibrium simplex, a sharp piecewise analytic pressure, and explicit freezing curves. General results of Pavlov [2] describe coded-shift entropy by generator series, whereas Climenhaga [3] establishes general Hölder equilibrium uniqueness when a pressure-gap hypothesis holds. The present classification additionally covers **failure of the pressure gap and coexistence**, with exact formulas in a specific natural non-sofic system. The zero-potential specialization agrees with the known MME. Classical inducing/relative-entropy ideas are not claimed as new.

## Limitations
The statement is restricted to rational frequencies \(k/(k+1)\) and two-symbol locally constant potentials; it does not classify general Hölder potentials, irrational frequency shifts, or out-of-equilibrium dynamics. The structure of invariant measures is imported from [1], not independently reproved here. This is a self-review; independent audit has not been performed. Although relevant primary results were inspected, an unindexed equivalent weighted-renewal calculation cannot be ruled out absolutely.
