For proportionate-mixing SIS networks, the initial contact-weighted shares of identical competing strains are conserved exactly, all node-level strain imbalances decay by the precise factor \((1-hd)^k\), and the surviving endemic mixture is selected by those shares. At the critical epidemic threshold, every present strain instead has an exact universal \(k^{-1}\) extinction profile; conversely, a positive weighted share conserved for every initial state exists only for rank-one contact matrices.
