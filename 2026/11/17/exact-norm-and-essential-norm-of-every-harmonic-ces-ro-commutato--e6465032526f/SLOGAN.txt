For every \(1<p<\infty\) and integer \(r\ge0\), the \(r\)-fold commutator of discrete Cesàro averaging with harmonic-number multiplication has identical sharp operator and essential norms \(r!(p/(p-1))^{r+1}\), despite the multiplier being unbounded. Every tail retains the full norm, and the corresponding operator-valued commutator Taylor series has exact norm-convergence radius \((p-1)/p\).
