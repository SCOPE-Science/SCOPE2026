For degree-normalized continuous-time random walks on \(m\) triangles sharing one hub, the sharp graph-edge total-variation amplification is \(\frac{2(m-1)}m e^{-t/2}-\frac{m-2}m e^{-3t/2}\). All-time nonexpansivity holds exactly through four triangles; above that threshold the unique transient amplification peak is bounded by the sharp unattained family-wide envelope \(\frac43\sqrt{2/3}\).
