For the centered two-dimensional acyclohedron on \(n\ge3\) labels, the unique enclosing sphere has squared radius \(n\binom n3/4\) and touches exactly \((n-1)!\) vertices indexed by circular orders. Every other vertex lies at squared radius at most \(n\binom n3/4-(n-3)\) for \(n\ge4\), and this second-shell gap is sharp.
