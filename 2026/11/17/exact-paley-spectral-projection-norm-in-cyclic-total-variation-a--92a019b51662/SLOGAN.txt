For prime cyclic signals with \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), projection onto nonzero quadratic-residue Fourier frequencies has exact sharp edge-total-variation norm \(\frac12+\frac{p+1}{4\sqrt p}\), attained by every arc whose length is a quadratic nonresidue. An explicit two-input construction transfers that same growth to radial Fourier soft thresholding despite its Euclidean nonexpansivity, establishing variation sensitivity of order \(\sqrt p\) along these primes.
