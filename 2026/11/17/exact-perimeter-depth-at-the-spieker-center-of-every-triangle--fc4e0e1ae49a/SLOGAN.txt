For every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, its arclength-boundary centroid (Spieker center) has halfplane perimeter depth strictly greater than \(1/3\), and the constant is sharp as two equal long sides and a third vanishing side form a degenerate limit. An explicit two-branch side-length formula determines the depth and a minimizing line for every triangle, refining the 2026 area-centroid perimeter Winternitz comparison.
