## Correctness — PASS

For each vertex, the successor-threshold event depends only on the opposite shore's colour-count vector truncated at two. The exact six-class origin-tag lift therefore retains every labelled state, not just aggregated occupancies. The supplied `verify_symbolic.py` checks all 729 possible reduced signatures and proves the universal identity \(F^{15}=F^3\) by the finite, lossless reduction, with least periods only \(1,3,4\). The exhaustive signature classification together with multinomial weighting proves all three closed basin formulas, not a small-size extrapolation. `verify_direct.py` independently checks 164,754 individual labelled initial states, and `verify_census.py` verifies the binomial weights and asymptotic expressions computationally for finite instances. In the infinite-size statements, the coefficient follows from the displayed inclusion–exclusion identities, not from numerical extrapolation.

The 1993 threshold-range cellular-automaton sources provide the scientific model context, not the finite complete-bipartite attraction-basin table. The 2021 infinite-tree threshold-two CCA theorems address fixation versus fluctuation; the 2026 torus article treats a different geometry and clarifies the distinction between population and labelled-state periods. The nearest indexed complete-multipartite period-four census concerns binary parity, not three-colour successor-contact rules. Earlier accessible exact analyses of complete bipartite systems apply to GHM threshold two or CCA threshold one (and, in one case, a missing edge); these assumptions do not imply the present theorem. Searches for equivalent names, stronger statements, exact tables and matching period-basin counts identified no covering statement. The 1993 threshold-range companion article was checked at abstract level only, and unindexed equivalents remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact attraction-basin weights and nonmonotone three-cycle existence threshold answer natural qualitative and quantitative questions about oscillations under finite successor contact. The uniform three-update settling theorem is independent of the part sizes, and the labelled origin-tag method resolves a genuine population-period aliasing obstacle. On balanced networks, almost all initial conditions enter three-cycles, while four-cycle and fixed-point exceptions vanish at markedly different exponential rates. This sharpens qualitative dynamical information without relying on an arbitrary parameter slice.
