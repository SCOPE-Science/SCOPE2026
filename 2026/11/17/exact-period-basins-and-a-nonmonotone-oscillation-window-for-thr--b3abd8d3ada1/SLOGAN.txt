Three-colour threshold-two cyclic competition on every complete bipartite graph reaches a fixed point or a primitive three- or four-cycle within three updates; exact formulas count the entire labelled basin of each outcome. The three-cycle existence window is reentrant in community size, and on balanced graphs the four-cycle probability is asymptotic to \(3(n+2)(2/3)^n\), exponentially larger than the fixed-point probability.
