For every finite memory length in the Grünwald–Letnikov logistic map, the unique \(-1\) Floquet resonance of its primitive two-cycle has an explicit radical formula using only the memory polynomial at \(1\), \(-1\), and \(-i\). Its sharp large-memory shift is a positive multiple of \(M^{-q}\), and taking the zero-order and infinite-memory limits in opposite orders gives the different exact constants \(2+\sqrt6\) and \(1+\sqrt3\); this is a resonance statement, not a blanket claim of a period-four bifurcation.
