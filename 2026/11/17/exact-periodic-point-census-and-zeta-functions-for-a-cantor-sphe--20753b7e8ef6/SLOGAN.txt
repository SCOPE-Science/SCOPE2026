For Fletcher–Martin's uniformly quasiregular Cantor-sphere map of degree \(D=d^2+m^2\), every iterate has exactly \(D^n\) fixed Julia points and one further fixed point at infinity, giving Artin–Mazur zeta functions \(1/(1-Dz)\) and \(1/((1-z)(1-Dz))\). The exact census is not merely a degree heuristic: branched-pillow fixed-point corrections \((-1)^r\) in the two-symbol itineraries cancel at every positive period.
