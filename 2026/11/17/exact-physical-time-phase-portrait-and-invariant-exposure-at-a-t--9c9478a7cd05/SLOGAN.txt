At the vertical center of a three-reaction quadratic chemical oscillator, the periodicity basin ends in one explicit rational homoclinic pulse: its physical period diverges as \((a^2-E)^{-1/2}\), while the first-species exposure per revolution stays exactly \(2\pi/\omega\). Outside this boundary every positive trajectory is captured by a line of extinction equilibria at an exact exponential rate, and the critical pulse has universal algebraic tails.
