For any two similar complex \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices, the least possible condition number of an intertwiner is unchanged under arbitrary nonzero Hilbert-space amplification. The optimal number has an exact norm-ratio formula in both distinct-eigenvalue and Jordan cases, yielding the attained identity \(\theta_{2,q}(t)=t\) for every \(q\geq1\) and \(t\geq1\).
