# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof derives the full commutator matrix from normalized harmonic Bergman projections, establishes a direct-sum exponential-polynomial factorization, and proves the rank of each coefficient block by an anti-diagonal minor. The accompanying checker passed nine exact rational cases and 729 direct projection comparisons. Infinite-dimensional assertions are established analytically.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Cui–Huang–Lu–Zu (arXiv:2611.08988v1, 2026-11-06) classify finite-rank analytic harmonic Bergman Toeplitz commutators by rational primitives and work out a simple rank-two rational example. Dong–Zhou (arXiv:1411.0213v3) covers restricted quasihomogeneous and monomial symbol families, overlapping a polynomial specialization. Chen–Koo–Lee (2013) proves even rank and its realizability, without a pole-order formula in its accessible abstract. The result concerns all finite pole combinations, not a claim that polynomial cases are new. The 2013 full text was unavailable; an older skew-Hankel formulation could create priority overlap.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is the natural exact rank invariant missing from a qualitative rational-primitive classification. Its independent pole contributions, parity plateaux, and additive polynomial term give a complete quantitative classification for the coordinate-versus-rational-derivative class.

**Limits.** No statement is made for arbitrary analytic pairs, boundary poles, or singular spectra. The checker supplements, but does not replace, the proof.
