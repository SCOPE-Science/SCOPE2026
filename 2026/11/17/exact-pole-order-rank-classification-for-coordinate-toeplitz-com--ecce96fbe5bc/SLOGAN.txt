For a rational primitive \(R\) holomorphic beyond the closed disk, the exact rank of the harmonic Bergman Toeplitz commutator \([T_z,T_{R'}]\) is the sum of a polynomial-degree contribution and the even pole-order contributions \(2\lceil d_j/2\rceil\), with no cancellation between distinct poles. This completes the exact rank classification for coordinate-versus-rational-derivative symbol pairs, beyond qualitative finite-rank criteria.
