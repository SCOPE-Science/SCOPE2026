For Euclidean projection onto real vectors whose maximum minus minimum is bounded, the exact two-input path-variation Lipschitz coefficient is computable with \(O(n^3)\) integer arithmetic, attained by finite inputs, and satisfies \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\le C_n\le1+\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\). Despite Euclidean nonexpansivity, variation sensitivity grows linearly and first exceeds one at four samples.
