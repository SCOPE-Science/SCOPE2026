For Gaussian firmly nonexpansive counterexample orbits with increments \(\delta n^{-\alpha}\), \(1/2<\alpha<1\), Cesàro averages decay at the exact rate \(N^{-(1-\alpha)/2}\), with squared-norm leading constant \(e^{-2\delta^2\zeta(2\alpha)}\sqrt\pi/[\delta(1+\alpha)]\). The construction realizes an exact continuum of arbitrarily slow polynomial strong-convergence rates although the unaveraged iterates fail to converge strongly.
