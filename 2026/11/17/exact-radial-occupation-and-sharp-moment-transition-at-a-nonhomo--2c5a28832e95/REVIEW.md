Inverting \(J\) near zero and isolating the single quadratic phase maximum gives the sharp tail exponent, the full real-order moment trichotomy, and the gamma-function constants. The closest indexed public result establishes basin-area asymptotics for **homogeneous** cubic nonlinearities (zero linear term); its asserted domain therefore excludes the present \(b>0\) systems and its area object differs from one orbit's physical-time radial moments. A related cubic global-center occupation theorem concerns a different vector field. Exact-claim, inverse-radial, parametric, and implication-oriented searches found no dominating statement. The older nonhomogeneous phase-portrait paper was accessible only in abstract and bibliographic description, so equivalent unpublished or unindexed calculations remain possible; no absolute uniqueness or publication priority is claimed.

## Value — PASS
For an isochronous period annulus approaching its finite-time escape boundary, the observed radius has a well-motivated distribution: a complete finite-orbit formula, a heavy-tailed limiting law, and a sharp moment existence threshold. The result reveals that the second moment and Euclidean enclosed area diverge while the first radial moment remains finite and the physical period stays fixed. The threshold, universal tail prefactor, and exact gamma coefficients answer a natural quantitative question not resolved by topological phase-portrait classification.
