For every wheel graphical zonotope with at least four rim vertices, one fixed four-state polynomial matrix enumerates its entire Euclidean vertex-radius spectrum. The innermost shell has squared radius \((m+16)/4\) for even rim order and \((m+17)/4\) for odd rim order, with exactly \(m^3/4\) or \(m(m^2-1)/2\) contact vertices, respectively; these are vertex norms, not the body's inradius.
