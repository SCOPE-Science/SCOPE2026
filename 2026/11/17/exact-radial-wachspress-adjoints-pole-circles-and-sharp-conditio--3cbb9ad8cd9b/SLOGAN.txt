The Wachspress denominator of every regular \(n\)-gon factors exactly into concentric quadratic circles whose radii are \(\cos(\pi/n)/\cos(k\pi/n)\). Its sharp first pole radius is \(1+3\pi^2/(2n^2)+O(n^{-4})\), while its exact interior maximum-to-minimum ratio grows as \(\pi^2 2^n/n^3\); the entire pole-radius spectrum has a universal limiting distribution.
