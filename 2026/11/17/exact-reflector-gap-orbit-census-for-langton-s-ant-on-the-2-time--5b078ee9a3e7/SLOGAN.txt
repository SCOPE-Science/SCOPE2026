For Langton's ant on every labelled \(2\times n\) torus, all states lie on cycles: reflector gaps of \(j\) edges give exactly \(n2^{2n-j-1}\) primitive \(8j\)-cycles, with an additional \((n+1)2^{n+1}\) cycles of period \(4n\). The worst-case period grows linearly in \(n\), but the period of a uniformly selected state converges to a distribution of mean 24.
