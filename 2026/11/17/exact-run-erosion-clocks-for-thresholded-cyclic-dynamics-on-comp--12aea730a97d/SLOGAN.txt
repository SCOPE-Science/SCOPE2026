For cyclic successor-contact updating on any labelled complete graph, with any number \(q\ge3\) of colors and any integer threshold, active color runs erode deterministically to fixed states unless every color is active, in which case every vertex rotates with primitive period \(q\). The exact first-entry clock is run length minus one, plus one for a positive but subthreshold predecessor, giving a sharp threshold-dependent jump from \(q-2\) to \(q-1\) updates and a complete labelled-state census.
