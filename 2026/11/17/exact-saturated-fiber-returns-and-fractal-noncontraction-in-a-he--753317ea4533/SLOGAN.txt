For the original three-dimensional heterochaos baker map, the probability that an initially complete vertical leaf still has full projected height after \(n\) iterates is exactly a weighted ballot sum and decays like \(n^{-3/2}(2\sqrt2/3)^n\), with different sharp constants for even and odd \(n\). Although almost every leaf shrinks to zero height, the full set of initial states with nonshrinking leaves has Hausdorff dimension exactly \(2+\log(2\sqrt2)/\log3\).
