For the complete unweighted \(W^{k,1}(\mathbb R^2)\) derivative-sum norm, planar shears have the exact all-order distortion \(\max_{0\le s\le k}\sum_{i=s}^k\binom{i}{s}|t|^{i-s}\), with nonattainment whenever \(t\ne0\). At order three the norm switches first between two mixed derivatives and then from mixed to pure at the sharp cusp thresholds \(|t|=1/3\) and \(|t|=1+\sqrt2\); the sharpness relies on Ornstein's \(L^1\) non-inequality.
