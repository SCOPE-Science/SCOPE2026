At exactly one chip per graph edge, periodic parallel chip-firing states in which every vertex fires once admit an exact Stirling-weighted orbit census on complete multipartite graphs with at least three parts. Every period from the number of parts through the sharp part-imbalance cutoff occurs, including odd periods; in particular the critical single-pass sector of \(K_{2,2,1}\) contains exactly 40 period-five labelled states, forming eight temporal orbits.
