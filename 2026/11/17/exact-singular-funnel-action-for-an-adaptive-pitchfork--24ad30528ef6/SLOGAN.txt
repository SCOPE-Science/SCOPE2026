The stable-manifold funnel of a slowly adapting pitchfork has the exact small-timescale width exponent \(\mu-r^2-b\log((\mu+b)/(r^2+b))\) at every fixed section \(\mu>r^2\). Its extra attenuation between two sections depends on the slow-restoring coefficient \(b\), but not on the feedback coefficient \(a\).
