Uniform discontinuous piecewise-affine splines on \(N\) cells attain the exact \(2N\)-dimensional Kolmogorov width of the full \(H^1(0,1)\) unit ball: \((1+4\pi^2N^2)^{-1/2}\). On every nonuniform mesh, their sharp derivative-seminorm approximation constant is precisely the longest cell length divided by \(2\pi\), with all extremizers classified.
