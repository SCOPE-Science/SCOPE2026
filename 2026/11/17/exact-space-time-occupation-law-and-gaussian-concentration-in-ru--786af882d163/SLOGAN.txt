For primitive temporal three-cycles of elementary Rule 73 on an indexed ring, an exact two-variable rational generating function gives the entire three-time occupation distribution at every circumference. Under a uniform choice of temporal orbit, the orbit-averaged occupied fraction concentrates at \(0.4876854676685245\ldots\) and satisfies an explicit Gaussian limit with variance coefficient \(0.03819664456798536\ldots\), refining previously known unweighted orbit counts.
