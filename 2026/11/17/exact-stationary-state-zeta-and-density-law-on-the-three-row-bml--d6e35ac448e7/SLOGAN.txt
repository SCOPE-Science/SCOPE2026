The complete stationary configuration distribution of east-then-north Biham–Middleton–Levine traffic on each labelled \(3\times n\) periodic grid has an exact 18-state bivariate zeta, whose unweighted denominator is a product of two quartics. Frozen configurations grow as \(11.8929366011799\ldots^n\), and a uniformly sampled frozen configuration has asymptotic occupied-site fraction \(0.8610902482461\ldots\).
