For the integer expanding map \(T_b(x)=bx\bmod1\), the coordinate-Euclidean step lengths \(|T_b^{j+1}x-T_b^jx|\) have a complete exact covariance law: every positive-lag covariance is proportional to \(b^{-2(j-1)}\), is negative at \(b=3\), vanishes at \(b=2,4\), and is positive for all \(b\ge5\). Their time averages satisfy a Gaussian limit with an explicit strictly positive rational variance, giving a solvable calibration for discrete trajectory path-length fluctuations.
