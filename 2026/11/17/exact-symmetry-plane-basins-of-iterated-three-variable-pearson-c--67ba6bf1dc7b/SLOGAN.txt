For every admissible \(3\times3\) Pearson-correlation matrix with its first two off-diagonal entries equal to \(r\) and its third equal to \(s\), the exact global limit is patterned for \(r<s\), equicorrelated in one step for \(r=s\), and the otherwise full-space-unstable mixed fixed point for \(r>s\). This gives a sharp complete two-dimensional symmetry-plane basin partition and a relatively open exceptional basin, without claiming a full three-dimensional classification.
