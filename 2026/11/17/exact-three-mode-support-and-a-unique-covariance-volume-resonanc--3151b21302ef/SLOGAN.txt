Three linear relaxations driven by the same binary Markov signal have a rate-independent stationary support of exact volume \(1/30\), yet their covariance-volume determinant has a unique maximum at the dimensionless switching rate \(\kappa_*=0.9802645581\ldots\). An explicit three-moment support body and a quartic critical-point equation quantify the contrast between fixed accessible geometry and rate-dependent stationary concentration.
