On every labelled ring with a single rotor-router walker, the full-state preperiod has an exact binary-position formula, sharp maximum \((n-1)(n-2)/2\), and exactly \(2n\) maximizers. For uniformly random rotors and starting vertex, its mean is exactly \((n^2-5n+8)/4-2^{1-n}\), distinguishing eventual Eulerian recurrence from the time needed to enter it.
