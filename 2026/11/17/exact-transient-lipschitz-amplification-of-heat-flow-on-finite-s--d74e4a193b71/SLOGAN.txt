For the unnormalized heat semigroup on the unit star with \(m\) leaves, the exact sharp Lipschitz norm at time \(t\) is \(2(m-1)e^{-t}/m-(m-2)e^{-(m+1)t}/m\). It is transiently expansive exactly when \(m\ge4\), has one explicit peak, and its peak amplification approaches \(2\) as the number of leaves grows.
