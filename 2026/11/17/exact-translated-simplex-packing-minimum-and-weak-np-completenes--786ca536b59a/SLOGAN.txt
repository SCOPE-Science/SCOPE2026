For an origin-containing translated right simplex, the first packing minimum is the reciprocal of \(b/2\) plus the smallest nonempty subset-sum discrepancy from \(b/2\), and *all* extremal lattice normals are subset indicators. Deciding whether the universal bound \(2/b\) is attained is weakly NP-complete with variable dimension; along the equal-coordinate translation ray, the optimum is attained at \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) positions, excluding the centroid when \(n\) is even.
