On the fully chaotic predator-free axis of a coupled logistic map, a continuous band of parameter choices has the same transverse Lyapunov exponent, but its entire finite-time covariance spectrum admits a new explicit Bernoulli-polynomial formula. At zero exponent, placing the transverse multiplier's zero at the prey turning point gives bounded log-amplitude variance, whereas placing it at the repelling prey fixed point gives variance asymptotic to \(\pi^2n/3\); the turning-point case itself is a known special case of classical logistic-map statistics.
