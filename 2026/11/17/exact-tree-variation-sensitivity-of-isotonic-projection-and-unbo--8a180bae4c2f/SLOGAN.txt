On every finite oriented tree, the exact graph-total-variation Lipschitz constant of unweighted Euclidean isotonic regression is a sharp connected-subtree boundary-cut maximum, computable by a finite polynomial-time knapsack. Paths have constant \(1\), stars with \(m\) leaves have constant \(\max\{1,(m-1)/2\}\), and cubic caterpillars with \(k\) spine vertices have constant \(k/2\): branching can therefore cause unbounded two-input variation amplification even when the maximum vertex degree is three.
