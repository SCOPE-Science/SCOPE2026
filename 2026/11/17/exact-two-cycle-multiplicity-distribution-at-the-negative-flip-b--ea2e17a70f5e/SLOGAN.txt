For one-dimensional negative-slope affine floor maps approaching the unit flip, the count of primitive two-cycles has an exact, elementary probability law when the affine offset is uniformly sampled over a conjugacy period. After multiplication by the distance \(\varepsilon\) to the flip, that count converges uniformly in offset to a triangular phase profile, and its random-offset law converges to \(\operatorname{Unif}[0,1/2]\), with explicit finite-parameter error.
