For three mutually nonparallel cylinder-axis directions equally spaced on a circular cone, the exact largest common cylinder radius tangent to a unit sphere has a closed two-branch formula with **no symmetry imposed on the cylinder positions**. A sharp contact transition at \(c=0.2448168692\ldots\) changes the optimal placement from two pairwise contacts to three, and a second transition at \(c=1/\sqrt3\) switches its separation-sign geometry.
