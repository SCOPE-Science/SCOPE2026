For any two-color partition of a positively edge-weighted finite path, every strictly positive choice of edge weights admits a positive within-color averaging-mass design that preserves total variation contractively. The exact norm is \(W\max_k|S_k|/w_k\), and all attainable sharp constants as positive masses vary form \([1,W/w_*)\) unless \(W=w_*\), in which case the norm is always one.
