Uniform sampling of the distinct vertices of the standard \(K_{2,q}\) graphical zonotope has a complete three-eigenvalue covariance spectrum, with the largest-variance axis switching sharply from the two-block contrast to the hub difference at \(q=6\). The exact spectral condition number is \((q+2)/2\) for \(3\le q\le5\) and \(q(q+2)/(12-9(2/3)^q)\) thereafter, growing asymptotically as \(q^2/12\).
