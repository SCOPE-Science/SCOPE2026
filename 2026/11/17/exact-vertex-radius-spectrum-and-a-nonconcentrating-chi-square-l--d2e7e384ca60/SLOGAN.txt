The centered graphical zonotope of a bouquet of \(m\) complete \(k\)-vertex graphs has an explicit complete Euclidean vertex-radius spectrum, with exact squared-radius variance \(2m(m-1)((k^2-1)/12)^2\). For fixed \(k\), a uniformly sampled vertex has a normalized squared radius converging to a shifted, positively scaled \(\chi_1^2\) distribution rather than concentrating on a single shell.
