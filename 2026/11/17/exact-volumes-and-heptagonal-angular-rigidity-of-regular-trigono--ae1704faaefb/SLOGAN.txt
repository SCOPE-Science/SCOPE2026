The equally spaced first/second-harmonic cyclic four-polytope has exact Euclidean volume \(n^2\cos(2\pi/n)\sin^2(2\pi/n)/48\), increasing to \(\pi^2/12\) with an explicit quadratic deficit. At seven vertices its volume is \(0.389054556345294\ldots\), and an exact negative transverse angular Hessian proves strict local volume maximality among seven-angle samples modulo rotation—not among all cyclic four-polytopes.
