The translation quotient of the eight-point affine-cube matroid independence complex is \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^3M(\mathbb Z/4,2)\), although the covering complex is a wedge of \(21\) three-spheres. In particular, an elementary abelian group of exponent two produces three independent order-four torsion classes in the integral topology of its nonfree ordinary orbit space.
