Analytic verification uses the exact linear equation for the squared radius in doubled angular phase, its positive periodic Green kernel, the modified-Bessel angular average, a Fourier/Beta integral identity, and a Gaussian Laplace expansion near the unique angular peak. These derivations cover every real anisotropy and all fixed positive parameters.

The standalone `check.py` is a finite numerical consistency check; it is not an infinite proof. It tests representative anisotropies on both sides of the loss of pointwise radial contraction, comparing an independently evolved periodic graph to the exact area series and to the physical-time period.

No independent mathematical audit has been performed. Publication priority remains subject to searching unindexed earlier literature; the exact period and Floquet formulas are already known and are not claimed new.
