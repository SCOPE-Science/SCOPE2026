For the attractive finite discrete Kuramoto map with any \(N\ge2\), every coupling \(K>1\) produces at least \(2^N-1\) explicitly distinct inputs with the same output, even with heterogeneous intrinsic frequencies. Except at a finite set of couplings, this exponential multiplicity persists on an open set of outputs; the previously known invertibility threshold \(K=1\) is therefore accompanied by a sharp many-body inverse-branch proliferation.
