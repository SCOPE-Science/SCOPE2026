PASS: For a graphical zonotope, a codimension-one face corresponds to a cut with both induced graphs connected; its intrinsic area is sqrt(|S||T|) times product of their spanning-tree counts because cut-side root subspaces are orthogonal. In K_{2,q} each such cut isolates one leaf (2q oriented facets) or separates the hubs (2 binom(q,b) at b leaves), exhaustive. The formula sqrt(s) tau(graph) for a connected s-vertex subgraph gives L_q=(q−1)2^(q−2)sqrt(q+1), a_q,b=sqrt((b+1)(q−b+1)), and W_q=sqrt(q+2) q 2^(q−1). With B~Bin(q,1/2), M_q=E sqrt((B+1)(q−B+1))=q/2+3/4+O(1/q) from centered moments, hence nonleaf-to-leaf area ratio 2q^(−3/2)+O(q^(−5/2)), and A_q/W_q=q−3/2+2/sqrt(q)+O(1/q). Independent direct Euclidean convex-hull computations for q=2,3,4 agreed with all facet areas and volumes. This is an analytic all-q proof, not extrapolation from three examples.
