Gluing the same two triangulated three-spheres along different tetrahedral identifications can change whether their simplicial double is connected, even while preserving the face vector. For every polygon join \(C_m*C_n\) with \(m,n\ge4\) and at least one odd, the marked-pair-preserving gluings yield two circle wedges, while the other gluings yield a single wedge of \(4mn-2m-2n+1\) circles.
