For the integer-spaced cubic moment-curve polytope \(\operatorname{conv}\{(t,t^2,t^3):0\le t\le N\}\), the exact largest contained tetrahedral volume and every maximizing vertex quadruple are determined by just two middle-gap candidates \(\lfloor N/\sqrt5\rfloor\) and its successor. Precisely at odd-index Fibonacci values \(N=5,13,34,89,\ldots\), these candidates tie and give exactly three maximizing vertex tetrahedra; at every other order there are one or two.
