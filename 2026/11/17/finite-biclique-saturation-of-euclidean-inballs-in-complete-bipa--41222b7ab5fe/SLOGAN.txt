The Euclidean insphere of the complete-bipartite forest polytope has a unique equal-edge center and is controlled by an induced biclique, not necessarily the whole graph. For fixed smaller part \(p\), its radius stabilizes once the larger part reaches the explicit cubic cutoff \(B_p=2(p-1)(2p^2-2p+1)\), while tangent-facet counts keep growing; for \(p=2\), the sharp saturation occurs at ten vertices on the larger side.
