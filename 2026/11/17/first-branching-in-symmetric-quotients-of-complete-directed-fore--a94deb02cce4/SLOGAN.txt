The ordinary symmetric-label quotients of complete directed-forest complexes have homotopy types \(\{\mathrm{pt}\},S^1,S^2,S^3\vee S^3\) at graph sizes \(2,3,4,5\), respectively. The five-label case is the first of these quotients to split into more than one sphere, established by exact integral quotient chains and a fundamental-group argument.
