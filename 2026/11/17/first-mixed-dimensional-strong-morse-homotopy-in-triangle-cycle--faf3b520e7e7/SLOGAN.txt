For a triangle and an \(n\)-cycle meeting at one vertex, the full strong-Morse matching complexes at \(n=4,5\) are each wedges of seven 2-spheres, while \(n=6\) is a mixed wedge of two 2-spheres and eleven 3-spheres. This is the first mixed-homology case among these two-petal bouquets for \(3\le n\le6\); the \(n=3\) thirteen-circle case was already known.
