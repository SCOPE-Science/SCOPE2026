# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof gives explicit positive-relative-interior witnesses for every claimed facet intersection, including the six exceptional pairs of one type in dimension three. The intrinsic Frobenius projection is computed from the row-sum adjoint, with five exact rational unnormalized normal products; normalization yields all angles and distinctness. A convexity argument proves the strict sign of the mean angle for all dimensions, while multiplicity asymptotics give the weak, mean, and variance limits. Exact independent tangent-Gram computations and relative-interior witnesses were exercised for every facet pair in dimensions three through seven. Risk: finite checks are diagnostic only; validity in all dimensions rests on the stated symbolic arguments.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The full five-class ridge spectrum is a canonical codimension-two metric description of a standard, non-simple, high-dimensional matrix polytope. The lone three-dimensional obstruction and the strict change from an obtuse average to an acute average in dimension four are natural boundary phenomena. The provable coexistence of overwhelmingly obtuse ridges with a strictly acute mean, and distinct probability and expectation limits under the same scaling, provides a mathematically interpretable rare-class effect. These are not obtained merely by counting its vertices or applying a generic rank bound.
