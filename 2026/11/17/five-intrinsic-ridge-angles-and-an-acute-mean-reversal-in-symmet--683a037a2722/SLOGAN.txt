The canonical Frobenius symmetric stochastic polytope has exactly five intrinsic dihedral-angle classes for every \(n\ge4\), with a complete facet-adjacency graph and explicit multiplicities; dimension three has precisely three surviving classes. Although almost every ridge is obtuse as \(n\to\infty\), its mean angle is strictly acute in every dimension \(n\ge4\), with \(n^2(\mathbb E\Theta_n-\pi/2)\to-2\).
