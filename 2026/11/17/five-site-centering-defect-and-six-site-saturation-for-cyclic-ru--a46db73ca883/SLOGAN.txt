For the three-point running median on a finite cycle, the exact two-input Lipschitz constant in mean-centered Euclidean distance is \(1\) at two sites, \(0\) at three, \(\sqrt2\) at four or five, and \(\sqrt3\) from six sites onward. Centering eliminates the ordinary \(\ell_2\) replication maximum at five sites, and an explicit two-coordinate witness restores it at every larger dimension.
