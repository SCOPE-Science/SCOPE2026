Enumeration and independent Burnside checks give \((22,114,204,116)\) cells. The exact cellular boundaries compose to zero, and a reproducible sequence of integral unit pivots leaves one degree-zero generator and five degree-three generators. The elimination alone gives homology, not a geometric collapse.
