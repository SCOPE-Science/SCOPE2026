The proper order complex of noncrossing partitions of six cyclic labels, divided by the full dihedral symmetry of the hexagon, is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of five three-spheres: \(|\Delta(\overline{\mathrm{NC}}_6)|/D_6\simeq\bigvee^5S^3\). Exact integral orbit-cell calculations and fixed-point generation establish both the homology and simple connectivity, at the first size where rotation and reflection partition-orbit counts differ.
