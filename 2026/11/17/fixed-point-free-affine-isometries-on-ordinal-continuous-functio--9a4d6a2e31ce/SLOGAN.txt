For every infinite limit ordinal \(\tau\), the whole unit ball of \(C_0([0,\tau))\) admits a fixed-point-free order-preserving affine **isometric** embedding into its unit sphere. Every nontrivial constant average has exactly computable zero-start residuals decaying as \(\sqrt{\theta/[2\pi(1-\theta)n]}\), even for nonseparable ordinal spaces.
