All 1,455 subgroup-fixed spaces of the six-point complete-graph matching complex have just four homotopy types: \(\bigvee^{16}S^1\) once, a point 1,140 times, three isolated points 80 times, and the empty space 234 times. Every nontrivial subgroup-fixed space is collapsible, discrete, or empty, as certified by exhaustive elementary collapses over all 56 conjugacy classes.
