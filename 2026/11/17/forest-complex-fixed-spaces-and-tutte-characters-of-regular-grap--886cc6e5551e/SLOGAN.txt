For every finite regular graph cover with a deck group acting freely on graph edges, **every subgroup's geometric fixed forest complex is PL-homeomorphic to the forest complex of the quotient graph**. Thus all fixed spaces are integral sphere wedges (or empty), and the entire rational deck-group homology character is given by signed Tutte evaluations \((-1)^{v-v/|\langle g\rangle|}T_{X/\langle g\rangle}(0,1)\).
