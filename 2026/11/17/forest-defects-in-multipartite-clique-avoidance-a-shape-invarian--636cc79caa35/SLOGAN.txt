Deleting a bipartite forest between two shores of a complete \(r\)-partite graph makes its \(r\)-clique-free complex depend only on the number \(c\) of edge-bearing forest components: the answer is \(S^{r-2}\), a point, or \(\bigvee^{c-1}S^{r-1}\). Along nested matching-edge deletions the original class dies at the first deletion, and each deletion from the second onward creates one permanent class one degree higher.
