The standard pentagon-to-Grötzsch graph-Hom complex collapses by 150 explicit free-edge square removals onto a connected graph with 310 vertices and 350 edges. Hence \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_5,\mu(C_5))\simeq\bigvee^{41}S^1\), a concrete non-square-free target case outside the direct reach of square-free-codomain classifications.
