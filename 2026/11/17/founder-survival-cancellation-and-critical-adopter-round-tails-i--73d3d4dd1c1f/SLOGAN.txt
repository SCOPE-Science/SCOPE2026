In the round-based two-stage contagion chain, the exact large-population probability that \(r\) founding adopters reach arbitrarily high finite adopter thresholds is \(1-\min\{1,\mathcal R^{-1}\}^r\), despite correlated reproduction and parent survival. At the critical threshold, the limiting total adopter-round count has an exact \(n^{-1/2}\) survival tail with coefficient \(1/\sqrt{\pi(1-e^{-(1+\nu)\tau})}\).
