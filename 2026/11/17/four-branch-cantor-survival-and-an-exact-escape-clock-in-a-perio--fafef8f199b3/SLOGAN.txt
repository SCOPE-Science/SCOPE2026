In a strictly high-expansion region of the period-two threshold neuron map, the entire bounded-orbit set is a four-branch Cantor repeller of Hausdorff dimension \(\log4/\log(ab)\). Uniformly chosen initial values in the natural exterior-cycle interval survive exactly \(n\) two-step returns with tail \((4/(ab))^n\), and every other initial value eventually escapes to infinity.
