Two-terminal graphs with \(p\ge2\) three-edge arms and \(q\ge1\) five-edge arms have a complete integral graph-metric Rips barcode with nonzero intervals in degrees one, two, and three. At radius three, an explicit collapse removes all cross-arm interactions and leaves exactly \(q\) three-spheres, even though the all-short-arm case is contractible at that radius.
