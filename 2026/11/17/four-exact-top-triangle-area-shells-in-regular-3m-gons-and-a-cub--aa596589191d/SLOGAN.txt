For triangles selected from vertices of a unit-circumradius regular \(3m\)-gon, \(m\ge3\), the four largest distinct areas have a complete sharp classification by cyclic gap triples, including exact equality multiplicities. The third and fourth levels differ by \(4\cos(\pi/(3m))\sin^3(\pi/(3m))\), a cubic-order near-tie beneath quadratic-order leading gaps.
