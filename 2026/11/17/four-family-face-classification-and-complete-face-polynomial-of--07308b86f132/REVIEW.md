# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The result follows from the exact support function of the signed bipartite root set. The two maxima \(M=H_+-G_-\) and \(N=G_+-H_-\) distinguish one-sign and both-sign faces; equality of the two extrema forces a common midpoint and splits into the three exhaustive cases of both positive half-ranges or one identically zero half-range. The explicit supporting potentials realize every listed face. The dimension argument counts independent coordinate difference spaces, including their shared direction for a constant part. A multinomial generating function proves every coefficient for all \(p,q\ge2\), and a finite independent convex-hull reconstruction matches eight parameter pairs. Risk: floating-point tests only corroborate the exact symbolic proof; no independent external audit.

**Originality (PASS, historical priority provisional).** The closest primary source, *Arithmetic aspects of symmetric edge polytopes* (arXiv:1807.07678), Proposition 3.4/Example 3.6, proves the facet formula and shapes and computes a distinct \(h^*\) polynomial, not the ordinary full face polynomial. *Many Faces of Symmetric Edge Polytopes* (arXiv:1910.05193) calculates face data for other graph families. *Minkowski decomposability of symmetric edge polytopes* (arXiv:2608.02445) establishes decomposition and a triangle criterion; this does not supply all ranks. Mathematical-record queries for the same object, alternative transportation-ball and Cayley-hull descriptions, and all-rank f-vector language returned only edge, facet, metric, and different-polytope records. The already known vertices, edges, facets, and criterion for triangles are credited. Risks: earlier equivalent formulations cannot be completely excluded, and mathematical priority might be obscured under alternate face-subgraph terminology; Chen–Davis–Korchevskaia arXiv:2107.12315 was read in full and proves general face-subgraph correspondence rather than the complete all-rank Kp,q enumeration.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** A full face lattice and rank generating polynomial are natural, reusable invariants for a well-studied family of generally nonsimplicial lattice polytopes. This computes exponentially many face incidences at every dimension and identifies the four structural support types, while the known facet and Ehrhart data alone do not determine middle-dimensional face ranks. The formula applies uniformly to arbitrary part sizes, including the large-rank Minkowski-indecomposable cases. This is not an isolated numerical slice or a rebranding of a known low-rank result.

**Claim and limitations.** Exactly the four-type vertex-set face classification and the single full proper-face polynomial for all labeled complete bipartite graphs with \(p,q\ge2\) are reviewed; triangle/edge/facet counts are credited consequences. The eight finite convex-hull checks are not proofs. Priority remains provisional; independent validation remains unperformed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
