Every nonempty proper face of the symmetric edge polytope of \(K_{p,q}\), \(p,q\ge2\), belongs to exactly one of four explicitly indexed support-pattern families, giving the entire face lattice. Their rank generating polynomial is \(2U_p(t)U_q(t)/t^2+T_p(t)T_q(t)/t^3+t^{p-2}T_q(t)+t^{q-2}T_p(t)\), where \(U_r(t)=(1+t)^r-1\) and \(T_r(t)=(1+2t)^r-2(1+t)^r+1\); earlier facet, edge, and triangular-face results are credited.
