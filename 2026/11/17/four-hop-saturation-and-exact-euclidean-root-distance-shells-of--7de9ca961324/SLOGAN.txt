In the canonical Euclidean embedding, each complete-graph root lies at squared distance \(0\), \(1/2\), \(8/11\), or \(3/4\) from the cycle symmetric edge polytope according as its cyclic endpoint separation is one, two, three, or at least four. The exact Hausdorff distance therefore saturates from cycle length eight onward, and all farthest points of the continuous complete-root polytope are explicitly classified root vertices.
