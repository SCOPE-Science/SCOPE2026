Euclidean least-squares projection onto convex sequences first amplifies complete-pair Gini variation at four sites, with sharp factor \(10/9\); the exact factors through eight sites are \(1,1,10/9,41/35,213/175,191/153,463/357\). A finite active-face/cut formula proves these values, and the strict lower bound \((4n-6)/(3(n-1))>1\) holds for every \(n\ge4\).
