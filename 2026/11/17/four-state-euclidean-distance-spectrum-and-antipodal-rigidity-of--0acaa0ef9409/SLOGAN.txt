The entire Euclidean vertex-pair distance spectrum of a linked type-C snake-interval polytope is an explicit product of four-state polynomial matrices. Exactly \(2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) of its \(2^{n-1}\) possible profiles have full squared diameter \(n\), and each has exactly \(2^{\lceil n/2\rceil-1}\) unordered antipodal vertex pairs, classified by complementary choices in consecutive coordinate blocks.
