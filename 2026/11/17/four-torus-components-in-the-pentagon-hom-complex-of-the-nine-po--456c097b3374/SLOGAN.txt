The ordinary pentagon graph-Hom complex of the nine-point Paley graph, equivalently \(K_3\square K_3\), collapses to **four disjoint tori**. A complete polyhedral-face certificate reduces 4,680 cells to four orientable genus-one surfaces, yielding \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z^8\) and \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^4\).
