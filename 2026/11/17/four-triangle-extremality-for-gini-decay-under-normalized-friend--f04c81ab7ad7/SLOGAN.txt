The exact complete-pair Gini norm of degree-normalized heat on \(m\) friendship-graph triangles is \(e^{-3t/2}+\frac{2(m^2-1)}{m(2m-1)}(e^{-t/2}-e^{-3t/2})\). All positive times are strictly contracting, yet four triangles uniquely maximize the sharp norm across every finite graph size; the proof reduces to an exact pair-mean projection norm and a randomization of split-pair cuts.
