For any finite abelian group \(H\), normalized scalar positive-type data prescribed on all reflections of its generalized dihedral group extend globally **exactly** when their Fourier coefficients have total absolute mass at most \(1\). Local positivity merely bounds each reflection value by \(1\); the two tests first differ on \(S_3\), and the extendible fraction of that local cube is exactly \(\pi\sqrt3/8\).
