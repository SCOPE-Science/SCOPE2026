## Checks and reproducibility
A proof was checked against the exact edge-coordinate conjugacy, finite-core decomposition, explicit dual annihilators, circle test vectors and polynomial Fredholm factorization. The included finite checker runs small tree rank formulas, sibling-functional cancellation, and circle-profile residual bounds; its finite calculations are only consistency tests.

## Limits
Only the Fredholm essential spectrum (failure of Fredholmness) is treated; no independent mathematical audit was performed. The theorem assumes an infinite, locally finite and leafless rooted tree and ordinary complex tree-Lipschitz spaces.
