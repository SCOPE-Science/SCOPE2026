For every free action of a finite group \(H\) on \(m\) vertex orbits, geometric fixed points of the complete-graph forest complex are PL-homeomorphic to the forest complex of a complete \(|H|\)-fold multigraph on \(m\) vertices with an apex having one parallel edge for every involution of \(H\). Thus the entire fixed-space homotopy type depends only on the group order, its number of involutions, and the orbit count, with an exact Tutte-polynomial sphere formula.
