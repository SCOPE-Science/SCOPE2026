The induced-triangle-avoiding complex of any \(r\ge3\) complete-graph pages sharing an \(s\ge3\)-vertex clique is a wedge of \(\binom{s}{2}\) circles, \(s\prod_i(q_i-1)\) spheres of dimension \(r\), and \(\prod_i\binom{q_i-1}{2}\) spheres of dimension \(2r-1\). Its nonabelian free fundamental group and entire integral homology symmetry modules are explicit for arbitrary private page sizes \(q_i\ge3\).
