# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns the ordinary independence complex of the explicit generalized Petersen graph \(G(8,3)\), all 96 graph automorphisms, and rational homology/ordinary quotient cohomology. The standalone verifier computes the 1,090 nonempty simplices and exact rational boundary ranks \((15,81,191,181,58,14,2)\) and checks \(\partial^2=0\). This independently establishes rational homology concentrated in degrees three and four with ranks four and one. In addition to the previous integral homotopy theorem (Bıyıkoğlu–Civan, arXiv:1503.06018v1, Example 3.9), the verifier independently builds nonboundary top cycles over two primes, compares their action under every automorphism, and finds the same bipartition sign both times. Its full chain-equivariance identities, complete character class table, exact inner products and invariant averages justify the decomposition and quotient rings. The result neither infers rational ranks from a single modular computation nor equates computation with a new infinite-family proof.

## Originality — PASS within checked evidence
The earliest directly checked mathematical source for this exact graph's independence topology is Bıyıkoğlu–Civan's 2015 first public preprint, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.06018, Example 3.9, which proves the nonequivariant wedge type. The closest same-object published symmetry result, *Cyclic symmetry on Möbius–Kantor independence homology* (public record 2026-10-11), was inspected as a complete scientific RESULT.md and explicitly restricts to \(C_8\). Its characters are consistent with but do not entail a full 13-class \(G\)-character or the kernel quotient dichotomy. Search comparisons were made under aliases Möbius–Kantor, Moebius-Kantor and \(G(8,3)\), for symmetry, homology representations, quotient topology and stronger coverage. Knill's 2026 study of graph automorphisms and Lefschetz theory is plausible background, but full text could not be obtained; no full-text noncoverage assertion is made. An equivalent unindexed result cannot be excluded.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The Möbius–Kantor graph is the canonical 16-vertex cubic symmetric graph with unusually large symmetry and a mixed-dimensional independence homology. A full action is not a cosmetic restatement of the known \(C_8\) restriction: the additional automorphisms split its four-dimensional homology into two essentially different irreducibles and reveal two different index-two ordinary quotient cohomology rings, one spherical in degree three and the other in degree four. This is a structural symmetry distinction invisible in ordinary Betti numbers and the restricted cyclic character.
