For the fully chaotic logistic map with independent resets to the initial seed, the ensemble-averaged seed sensitivity has rate \(\log(2(1-r))\) for almost every interior seed when \(0<r<1/2\), yet the rate fails to exist on one dense, Lebesgue-null set of full Hausdorff dimension shared by every such \(r\). For \(1/2\le r<1\), the rate is zero for every interior seed.
