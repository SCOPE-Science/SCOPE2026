The ordinary matching complex of the truncated tetrahedron has a twenty-two-dimensional rational third-homology representation whose complete tetrahedral symmetry type is \(S^{(1,1,1,1)}\oplus3S^{(3,1)}\oplus2S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus3S^{(2,2)}\). Its quotient by full tetrahedral symmetry is rationally acyclic, while the rotational quotient has the rational cohomology ring of \(S^3\).
