For the Euclidean convex hull of a cube and a scaled crosspolytope, the complete Gaussian vertex-angle spectrum has two exact levels, with a unique scale at which all \(2^n+2n\) vertices are angularly equiprobable. At the sharp scale \(t\sim\sqrt{2/\pi}\,n/\sqrt{2\log n}\), a Gumbel transition leaves positive angular mass on exponentially many cube vertices while the two-direction collision probability is controlled by just \(2n\) apices.
