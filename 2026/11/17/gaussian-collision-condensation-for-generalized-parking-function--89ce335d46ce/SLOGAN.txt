For every fixed integer \(r\ge2\), the probability that \(r\) independent Gaussian objectives expose the same vertex of any canonical generalized parking-function polytope has a sharp universal asymptotic of the form \(2^{-nr}e^{r n^{1/r}}n^{-(r-1)/(2r)}\), with a precise multiplier that changes only at \(r=2\). Conditioned on such a collision, the exposed vertex's nonunit rank collapses from order \(n\) to \(n^{1/r}\), with Gaussian fluctuations of order \(n^{1/(2r)}\) and variance \(1/r\); known finite partial-permutohedron cone formulas are explicitly credited as antecedents.
