For \(n\) independent planar lines with uniform directions and Gaussian signed offsets, the smallest triangle area has the explicit nonstationary extreme law \(\Pr(n^6A_{\min}>t)\to\exp(-0.0654808676436\sqrt t)\) with an \(O_t(n^{-1})\) Poisson-count bound. Conditional on near-concurrence, angular configurations are tilted by a computable sine determinant, the least-squares crossing location is an angle-dependent Gaussian, and the normalized signed defect is asymptotically independent uniform; generic stationary-line Weibull scaling is prior work.
