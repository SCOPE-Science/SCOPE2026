For a circular-profile spherical torus in every Euclidean dimension, generic Crofton affine-hyperplane cuts have exactly three explicit topological types, and the chance of a ball cut has a unique radius-ratio maximizer. In high dimension the optimal radius ratio is \(1+0.22746821156\ldots/d\), and its unavoidable non-ball probability is asymptotic to \(0.21433704113\ldots/d\).
