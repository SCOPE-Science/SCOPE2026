For every fixed finite interior atomic measure, Bernstein–Durrmeyer approximation has KL-bisector step limits with universal Gaussian transition layers, independent of the atomic weights. On the support, its sharp \(L^2\) operator error is of order \(n^{-1/2}e^{-nC_*}\), where \(C_*\) is the least adjacent Bernoulli Chernoff information.
