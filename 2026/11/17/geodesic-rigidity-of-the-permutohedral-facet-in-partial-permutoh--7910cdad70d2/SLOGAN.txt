For every \(n\ge m\ge2\), the top permutohedral facet of \(\mathcal P(m,n)\) is geodesically rigid in the Euclidean edge-length metric: facet-vertex distances are \(\sqrt2\) times adjacent-value swap distance, and every shortest ambient edge path stays on the facet. A completion-measure transport argument makes every excursion through a deficient-rank vertex strictly too long.
