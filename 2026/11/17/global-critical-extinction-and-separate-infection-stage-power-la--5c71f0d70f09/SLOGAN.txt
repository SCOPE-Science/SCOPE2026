At a doubly neutral infection threshold, a four-compartment discrete SISI map clears every initially present infection, but first infections decay like \(n^{-1/2}\) while reinfections decay like \(n^{-1}\). The exact universal coefficients are \(\lim\sqrt n\,u_n=1/(4\sqrt b)\) and \(\lim n v_n=1/(8b)\) for \(0<b\le1/3\).
