At the neutral predator-invasion threshold of an exponential ratio-dependent Holling-III flow, the natural monotone-ratio regime has global prey recovery and predator clearance with exact inverse-time amplitudes. The reciprocal predator density has a finite renormalized clock \(d\alpha t+(a-\alpha/2)\log t+C+o(1)\), whose logarithmic coefficient changes sign and vanishes at a natural positive-parameter crossover.
