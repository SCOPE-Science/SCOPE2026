## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence
Luís–Rodrigues (2017) treats the three-dimensional model and explicitly raises the global-convergence question; its critical local analysis does not yield a global basin for every positive triple. Gyllenberg–Jiang–Niu (2019, Theorem 2.3) establishes global three-dimensional attraction under the much stronger restriction \(\nu_i<1/(1+2c)\), which excludes the present intrinsic rate \(2\). Ackleh–Salceanu (2015) develops persistence and exclusion, and Baigent–Hou et al. (2023) is planar with strictly subcritical intrinsic rates. Closest indexed findings concern two-species critical Ricker relaxation; their two-component statements neither directly cover three independent species nor imply global attraction on the three-species parameter slice. Searches for spectral reductions and logarithmic-mean methods likewise supplied no dominating statement. **Risks:** some publisher-only full text and unindexed equivalents cannot be excluded; the three-species sufficient threshold is not asserted optimal.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A global nonhyperbolic boundary theorem for three competing species answers a motivated slice of a published global-attraction question. The coexistence equilibrium's radial flip multiplier is neutral, but all positive initial vectors are attracted while pairwise ratios have an exact nonzero exponential exponent, revealing a significant difference between compositional equilibration and total-population equilibration. The exact diagonal critical exponent provides a contrasting positive family of algebraic transients. The sufficient coupling condition comes from a rigorously verified logarithmic-mean contraction on the full positive state space, rather than an arbitrary numerical parameter instance.

## Scope and limits
The conclusion is for \(1-2/e<c<1\), identical intrinsic rates and equal cross-species coefficients, and strictly positive starts. The algebraic squared-amplitude limit is claimed for a small diagonal neighborhood only; the transverse exponent excludes pairs coalescing exactly in finite time. The full optimal attraction parameter region, boundary-initialized populations and asymmetric models remain unresolved.
