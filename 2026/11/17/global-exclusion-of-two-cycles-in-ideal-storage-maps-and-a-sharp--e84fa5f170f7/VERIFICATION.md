# Verification and limits

The analytical proof rests on two exact stock balance relations and two exact rate identities, followed by monotonicity and \(0<h<1\). No polynomial root isolation, large enumeration or numerical integration is needed. The `verify.py` script checks the rational two-cycle witness with a smoothly decreasing response, the logit endpoint identities, and illustrative strict inequalities for monotone logistic responses. Its assertions are not substitutes for the quantifier-complete proof.

The evidence scope is an isolated local ideal-storage map for arbitrary nondecreasing feedback. Literature comparisons include complete accessible text of the 2011 general-map study and the 2026 motivating paper. Full text of the 1982 predecessor was unavailable; the possibility of a historical equivalent is unresolved. Independent audit has not been performed.
