In a vacancy-limited hierarchical May–Leonard chain with identical positive intrinsic rates, every nonzero population state converges exponentially to the unique equilibrium that retains the first, third, fifth, and later occupied species within each initially occupied run; this greedily selects a maximum-cardinality independent set of the occupied path. Every excluded but initially present species has exact extinction rate \(-p_{i-1}X_{i-1}\), where \(X_{i-1}\) is its surviving predecessor's limiting density.
