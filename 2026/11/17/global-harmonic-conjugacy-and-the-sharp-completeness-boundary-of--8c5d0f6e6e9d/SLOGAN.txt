A quadratic polynomial flow preserving Jana's algebraic torus has an exact real-time conjugacy to two rigid rotations on its complete region \(2a^2(x^2+y^2)>z^2+a^4-1\). Outside that sharp boundary every trajectory escapes in finite time, with an exact lifespan and a transition in radial blow-up exponent from \(1/2\) to \(1\).
