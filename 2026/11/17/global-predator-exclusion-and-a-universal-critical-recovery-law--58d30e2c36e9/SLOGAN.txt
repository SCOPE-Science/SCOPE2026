In a double-Allee Holling-II predator–prey map, the sharp predator-exclusion boundary is \(\beta=(1+\alpha)\theta\): at or below it, every initially subcapacity prey–predator orbit loses its predators. On critical orbits recovering to prey carrying capacity, \(n(1-x_n)\to(1+\alpha)/\theta\) and \(ny_n\to(1-s)(1+\alpha)^2/((1+w)\theta)\), with an open set realizing these universal rates.
