In a balanced planar coupled saddle-search flow, almost every initial search direction converges, yet nearly orthogonal directions generate a universal hyperbolic-secant overshoot with unbounded sharp gain. Uniform random directions have the exact amplification tail \(\Pr(G>z)=\pi^{-1}\arcsin(z^{-2\gamma/\beta})\) for \(z\ge1\), so even the expected peak diverges when \(\beta\ge2\gamma\).
