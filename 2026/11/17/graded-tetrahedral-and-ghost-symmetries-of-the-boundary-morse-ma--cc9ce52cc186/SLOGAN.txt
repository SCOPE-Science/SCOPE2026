The ordinary boundary-tetrahedron Morse-matching complex has distinct, completely determined rational \(S_4\times C_2\)-representations in degrees three and four: its ghost involution splits the 21 generators into \(15+6\) and the 24 generators into \(12+12\). Both positive-degree rational homology groups vanish after passage to the full geometric symmetry quotient, whereas the rotation-only quotient retains two generators in each degree.
