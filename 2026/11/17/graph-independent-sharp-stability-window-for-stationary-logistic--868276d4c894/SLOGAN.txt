For the maximally chaotic logistic map coupled by normalized diffusion, every finite connected undirected bipartite graph—regardless of size or degree heterogeneity—has a nonconstant stationary two-color state that is a hyperbolic sink exactly when \((4+\sqrt6)/16<D<(19+\sqrt{73})/64\). The threshold follows from a graph-independent singular-mode comparison, not an assumption of two-node dynamics.
