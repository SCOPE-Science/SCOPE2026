For every even regular polygon of orders 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12, the half-turn-fixed space of the noncrossing-forest simplicial complex is respectively a wedge of 1, 5, 23, 109, and 524 spheres, with sphere dimensions 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5. An explicit exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching determines these full integral homotopy types.
