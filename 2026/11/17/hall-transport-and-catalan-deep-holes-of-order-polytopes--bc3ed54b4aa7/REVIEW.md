# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The constant-coordinate feasible witness bounds distance by the coordinate variance. Strict separate-coordinate convexity forces every universal extremizer to be a balanced binary corner. The Euclidean projection normal-cone criterion at the interior constant point equals a nonnegative comparability flow, and finite max-flow/min-cut supplies the weighted Hall conditions. For chains, nonnegative prefix residuals give Catalan and coprime-cycle-lemma counts. For alternating fences, in-/out-degree obstructions force one binary corner, whose rational covering-edge flow is explicitly nonnegative. Exact rational tests exercise projection and flow conditions. The finite computations are not used to deduce the general result.

## Originality — PASS, priority provisional
Reviewed the published full texts of Stanley's original order-polytope paper, the generalized-snake-poset triangulation paper (arXiv:2102.11306), and the PAVA isotonic-regression paper (arXiv:2006.05527), plus the accessible 2026 order/chain-polytope edge manuscript. Their studied invariants are face structure, triangulation and volume, regression algorithms, and edge counts, rather than the exact cube-to-order-polytope Euclidean deep-hole extremizers. A published result on directed Hausdorff distances between *order and chain polytopes* is a closely named but different metric problem; its full technical text was not obtained in this check, so an unrecognized consequence remains a residual risk. Historical uniqueness is not asserted solely from search failures. The claimed Hall–Catalan result was compared against previously recorded mathematical statements and relevant published results by logical implication, without a known dominating statement.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The sharp bound and weighted Hall transport criterion are valid over all finite posets, including a graph-theoretic perfect-matching characterization in even order. Their application to two orientations of the same Hasse path shows an exponential-versus-unique change in complete deep-hole multiplicities despite identical undirected cover graphs. This identifies a meaningful orientation-sensitive Euclidean invariant and offers a compact exact test for extremal isotonic approximation; it is more substantive than recomputing one bounded table.

## Remaining limitations
For a nonsaturating poset the exact Hausdorff gap is not given. The chain and fence enumerations do not classify arbitrary Hasse trees. Full-text review of every plausibly related mathematical publication was not feasible; earlier work on equivalent isotonic approximation inequalities could subsume part of the finding. No independent mathematical validation has been performed.
