The Euclidean distance from any finite-poset order polytope to its containing cube is at most \(\sqrt{\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/n}\); all corners attaining this sharp universal bound are characterized by a weighted Hall transport condition, and in even order saturation is equivalent to a perfect matching of the comparability graph. Chains have Catalan or coprime-ballot numbers of deepest corners, while alternating fences on the same undirected Hasse path have exactly one.
