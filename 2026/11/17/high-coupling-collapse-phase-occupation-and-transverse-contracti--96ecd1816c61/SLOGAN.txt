At high quartic coupling, the four coexisting attracting chemical cycles collapse onto axial segments with equal enclosed areas \(\sim\pi/(8\sqrt\mu)\), yet their physical-time periods retain two different sharp constants of order \(\mu^{-1/2}\). Their shared transverse multiplier decays as \(\exp[-49\pi\sqrt\mu/(256\varepsilon)+o(\sqrt\mu)]\), and the properly rescaled time-uniform phase laws converge to explicit, nonuniform distributions on two thin branches.
