For every double-star tree with \(N\) leaves, the higher-independence complex at threshold \(2\leq r\leq N+1\) is a wedge of \(\binom{N-1}{r-2}\) spheres of dimension \(r-1\), independent of how the leaves are divided between the two hubs. Its complete integral top-homology action under graph automorphisms is the \((r-2)\)-nd exterior power of the leaf augmentation lattice, twisted by the sign of interchanging the hubs.
