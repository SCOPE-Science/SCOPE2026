The complete intersection-count distribution of a uniformly sampled minimizing geodesic with a fixed primitive closed geodesic on any rectangular flat torus depends only on the latter's integer homology, never on the torus's aspect ratio. Remarkably, the reduced denominator of \(12\mathbb EN\) recovers the largest absolute winding coordinate, and the same mean recovers the other; a second moment gives a continuous, sharp shape-ratio inverse.
