For every finite supported hypergraph, incidence transposition preserves the complete integral first normalized \(\Box\)-cubical homology group: \(H_1^{\Box}(H;\mathbb Z)\cong H_1^{\Box}(H^*;\mathbb Z)\). This proves a degree-one homological consequence of a recent conjecture about hypergraph–dual homotopy equivalence, without asserting that stronger conjecture.
