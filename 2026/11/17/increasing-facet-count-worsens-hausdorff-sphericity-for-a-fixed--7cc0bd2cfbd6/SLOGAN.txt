For a classical family of spherical \(3\)-designs with \(2n\) nodes, the unique area-\(4\pi\) equal-facet-area Minkowski polytope is a regular bipyramid whose Hausdorff distance from the unit ball increases strictly to \(2/3^{1/4}-1\) even as its volume also increases. The result isolates a fixed-strength obstruction to mistaking increasing facet count for increasing geometric sphericity.
