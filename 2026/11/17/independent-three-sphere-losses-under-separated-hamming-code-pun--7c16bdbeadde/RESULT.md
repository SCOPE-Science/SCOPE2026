# Independent three-sphere losses under separated Hamming-code punctures

## Finding

Let \(Q_n=\{0,1\}^n\) have its Hamming metric. Fix \(n\ge3\), and let \(C\subseteq Q_n\) satisfy \(d_H(a,b)\ge4\) for all distinct \(a,b\in C\). For each \(T\subseteq C\), define the *induced* inclusive scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex
\[
X_T=\operatorname{VR}_{\le2}(Q_n\setminus T).
\]
Distances are inherited from \(Q_n\); they are **not** recomputed in the punctured graph. Set
\[
c_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-3}2^j\binom{j+3}{3},\qquad b_n=\binom n3.
\]
Then, for every \(T\subseteq C\),
\[
X_T\simeq\bigvee^{c_n-|T|b_n}S^3.
\]
For each inclusion \(S\subseteq T\subseteq C\), the map \(X_T\hookrightarrow X_S\) on \(H_3(-;\mathbb Z)\) is a primitive split injection with free cokernel of rank \((|T|-|S|)b_n\). Under suitable (noncanonical) homotopy equivalences, the inclusion is the standard inclusion of wedge summands, and its homotopy cofiber is \(\bigvee^{(|T|-|S|)b_n}S^3\). All other reduced integral homology vanishes. A zero-fold wedge means a contractible space.

## Assumptions and scope

A simplicial complex's vertices are Hamming words, and a set spans a simplex exactly when all pairwise Hamming distances are at most two. \(C\) may be nonlinear or empty. The distance-four assumption is vacuous when \(|C|\le1\). The result is uniform across all code configurations satisfying this separation, and applies to every intermediate subset and deletion order.

The previously known one-puncture cases and finite two-puncture cases through dimension five are not claimed as discoveries. The substantive assertion is all-dimensional, arbitrary-cardinality separated deletion, with the full diagram of integral inclusion maps.

## Proof

**1. Known global homotopy input.** Adamaszek and Adams proved that \(X_{\varnothing}=\operatorname{VR}_{\le2}(Q_n)\simeq\bigvee^{c_n}S^3\), with the indicated exact integer multiplicity. In particular the ambient complex is simply connected, two-connected, and has free third integral homology.

**2. Local link, including an integral basis.** By translation symmetry it suffices to consider the link \(L\) of the zero word. Label its neighbors \(u_i\) (single-coordinate flips) and \(v_{ij}\) (two-coordinate flips), with \(1\le i<j\le n\). Its maximal simplices are
\[
U=\{u_i:1\le i\le n\},\quad
S_i=\{u_i\}\cup\{v_{ij}:j\ne i\},\quad
T_{ij}=\{u_i,u_j,v_{ij}\},\quad
V_{ijk}=\{v_{ij},v_{ik},v_{jk}\}.
\]
Here the notation \(v_{ij}\) is symmetric in its subscripts. These are all maximal cliques because two two-flip words have distance at most two exactly when their coordinate pairs intersect, and a one-flip word is adjacent to a two-flip word precisely when its coordinate is in that pair.

Let \(B\) be the subcomplex generated by \(U,S_i,T_{ij}\), excluding the \(V_{ijk}\) triangles. For each \(i\), pair the faces \(F\subseteq\{v_{ij}:j\ne i\}\) with \(|F|\ge2\) to \(F\cup\{u_i\}\), in descending face-size order. These are legal elementary collapses in \(B\), since no such two-flip face belongs to another \(S_j\) or a \(T_{ab}\). The remaining pieces of \(S_i\) are the star edges \(u_i v_{ij}\). Each triangle \(T_{ij}\) then collapses against the free edge \(u_jv_{ij}\); the remaining \(v_{ij}\) is a leaf and collapses with \(u_iv_{ij}\). This collapses \(B\) to the full simplex \(U\), hence \(B\) is contractible.

Every \(V_{ijk}\) is attached along its entire boundary lying in \(B\); two distinct such triangles share no edges. Thus collapsing the contractible \(B\) produces a wedge of \(b_n\) two-spheres. More precisely, the natural map
\[
H_2(L;\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow H_2(L,B;\mathbb Z)
 \cong\bigoplus_{\{i,j,k\}}\mathbb Z[V_{ijk}]
\]
is an isomorphism, and \(L\simeq\bigvee^{b_n}S^2\). The target's triangle coordinates give a **primitive integral basis** of link homology.

**3. Relative chains after separated deletion.** Put \(Z=X_T\), first with \(S=\varnothing\) and \(T\subseteq C\). Since distinct deleted points are at distance at least four, no simplex contains two deleted vertices. Consequently each relative chain containing a deleted vertex is uniquely assigned to that vertex. The relative simplicial chain complex splits as the direct sum of the cone-link relative complexes, giving
\[
H_i(X_{\varnothing},X_T;\mathbb Z)
\cong\bigoplus_{p\in T}\widetilde H_{i-1}(\operatorname{lk}_{X_{\varnothing}}p;\mathbb Z).
\]
Only degree three survives and \(H_3(X_{\varnothing},X_T;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{|T|b_n}\). This reasoning does not require geometric closed stars to be disjoint; their overlaps consist of faces retained in \(Z\).

**4. Unit-pivot cubical cycles.** Fix a deleted word \(p\) and coordinate triple \(I=\{i,j,k\}\). The eight vertices obtained by independently flipping the three coordinates of \(p\) form an isometric copy of \(Q_3\). Its scale-two Rips complex is the boundary of the four-dimensional cross-polytope, hence an integral three-sphere with an oriented fundamental cycle. Every word in this little cube lies within Hamming distance three of \(p\), so by the separation hypothesis it contains no other deleted word.

The relative image of that fundamental cycle is zero in every deleted-vertex summand except the one indexed by \(p\). In the \(p\)-summand, its link is the octahedral two-sphere formed by the three one-flips and three two-flips on coordinates \(I\). Its oriented link cycle has coefficient \(\pm1\) at \(V_{ijk}\) and coefficient zero at every \(V_{abc}\) with \(\{a,b,c\}\ne I\). By Step 2 these are precisely the integral basis coordinates. As \(p\) and \(I\) range, the relative images form an integral signed identity matrix. Therefore
\[
H_3(X_{\varnothing};\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow
H_3(X_{\varnothing},X_T;\mathbb Z)
\]
is surjective, with a splitting over \(\mathbb Z\).

**5. Homology, connectivity, and homotopy type.** The long exact sequence of the pair and Step 4 imply
\[
0\longrightarrow H_3(X_T;\mathbb Z)
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^{c_n}
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^{|T|b_n}
\longrightarrow0,
\]
with no other positive-degree reduced integral homology in \(X_T\). Thus \(H_3(X_T;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{c_n-|T|b_n}\), primitively included in ambient third homology.

The graph \(Q_n\setminus T\) is connected. Indeed, whenever a graph path enters a deleted vertex \(p\) through one cube neighbor and leaves through a distinct cube neighbor, reroute around the opposite side of the corresponding square. The detour word is distance two from \(p\) and hence cannot be in the distance-four-separated set \(T\). Backtracking visits may simply be cancelled.

Attach back one closed vertex star (a cone on its link) for each deleted vertex. All link complexes are simply connected by Step 2, and pairwise star overlaps lie in \(X_T\); van Kampen therefore gives \(\pi_1(X_T)\cong\pi_1(X_{\varnothing})=0\). Its vanishing \(H_2\) then implies \(\pi_2(X_T)=0\) by Hurewicz. The degree-three Hurewicz isomorphism supplies sphere maps representing an integral basis, and the resulting map \(\bigvee^{c_n-|T|b_n}S^3\to X_T\) is a homology equivalence between simply connected CW complexes. The homological Whitehead theorem proves it is a homotopy equivalence.

**6. Every intermediate inclusion.** Repeat the relative-chain and local-cycle arguments for \(X_T\hookrightarrow X_S\), where \(S\subseteq T\subseteq C\): the required little three-cubes through words of \(T\setminus S\) avoid all words in \(S\) by distance-four separation. Hence their integral local coordinates split the relative sequence and give primitive free cokernel of rank \((|T|-|S|)b_n\). Since both complexes are bouquets of three-spheres, integral unimodular basis changes on their \(H_3\) are realized by homotopy equivalences, identifying the map with a standard wedge-factor inclusion. The cofiber is the quotient by those factors and has the asserted homotopy type. ∎

## Verification

The standalone Python 3 file `verify.py` enumerates all cliques independently from the Hamming metric, calculates every mod-two boundary rank, and checks all intermediate puncture subsets for four test configurations: \(n=3,C=\{0\}\), \(n=4,C=\{0,15\}\), \(n=5,C=\{0,15\}\), and \(n=6,C=\{0,15,51,60\}\), reading bit strings as nonnegative integers. Their final third Betti numbers are respectively \(0,1,29,129\); all other reduced mod-two Betti numbers vanish. The checker also constructs every local three-cube and tests its unique two-flip triangle pivot. The checker does not replace the integral proof above or establish any assertion in untested dimensions.

Run with `python3 verify.py`. The corresponding recorded standard output is `verification_output.txt`.

## Relationship to prior work

Adamaszek and Adams (2021) already prove the global sphere-wedge formula and classify links arising in their ordered vertex-insertion argument; their Theorem 8 also covers a special one-vertex puncture, so these are used as inputs and **not** presented as new. A previously recorded finite classification of two-vertex deletions for dimensions three through five includes the pair-distance-four examples at \(n=4,5\). Those finite values are corroboration only. Neither statement classifies **every subset of every distance-four binary code in all dimensions**, nor the full integral inclusion maps for arbitrary deletion subsets.

The recent work of Bendersky, Elia, and Grbić (2026) develops cohomological and ghost-vertex methods for hypercube Rips complexes; the group-action approach of Galetto, Montaño, and Wellner (2026) studies global equivariant homology. Their accessible claims concern different invariants from separated multi-point deletion. A complete textual inspection was available for the first recent study but not for the entirety of the latter preprint; this is a residual bibliographic risk, not a claim that absent text cannot contain stronger results.

## Limitations

The result assumes Hamming separation at least four, scale exactly two, the original metric after puncturing, and the binary cube. It does not establish optimality of the separation bound, classify arbitrary close punctures, generalize to other Rips scales, or provide canonical homotopy-basis splittings. The conclusion is mathematical homotopy classification with a self-review, not independent formal certification.
