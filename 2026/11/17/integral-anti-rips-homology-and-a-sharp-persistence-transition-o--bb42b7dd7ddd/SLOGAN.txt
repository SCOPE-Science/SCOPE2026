For the inclusive anti-Vietoris–Rips filtration of the full binary Hamming cube, the transition from distance \(n-1\) to \(n-2\) kills exactly 50 integral first-homology classes when \(n=5\), but none when \(n\ge6\); all inclusion cokernels are free. The exceptional new complexes have reduced homology \(\mathbb Z^{16}\) and \(\mathbb Z^{151}\) in degrees one and two for \(n=5\), and \(\mathbb Z^{223}\) and \(\mathbb Z^{302}\) for \(n=6\), while every \(n\ge7\) yields a connected graph with an explicit first Betti number.
