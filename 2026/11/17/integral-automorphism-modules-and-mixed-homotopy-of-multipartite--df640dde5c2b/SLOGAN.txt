For the graph-geodesic general-position complex of every complete multipartite graph, the full graph automorphism action on **integral** homology is a direct sum of part-augmentation lattices in degree one and a sign-twisted tensor product of these lattices in top degree. Its nonequivariant homotopy type is \(\bigvee^{\sum_i(a_i-1)}S^1\vee\bigvee^{\prod_i(a_i-1)}S^{r-1}\), with a distinct signed edge-augmentation description when there are exactly two parts.
