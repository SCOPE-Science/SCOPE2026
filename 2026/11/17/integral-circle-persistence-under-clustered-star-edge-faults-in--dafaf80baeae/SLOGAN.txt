Deleting \(t\) edges incident to one hub of \(K_{a,b}\) leaves the two-independence complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{(a-1)(b-t)}S^1\) for every \(1\le t\le b\): all integral first-homology maps are split surjections, and each later fault kills exactly \(a-1\) classes. Unlike disjoint matching faults, clustered faults never create two-dimensional homology.
