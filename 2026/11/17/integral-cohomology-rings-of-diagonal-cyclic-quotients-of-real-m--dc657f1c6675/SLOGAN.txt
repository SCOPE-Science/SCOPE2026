For every \(n\ge3\), the diagonal \(C_n\)-quotient of the real moment-angle complex over \(K_{n,n}\) has completely determined, torsion-free integral cohomology, with \(H^1\cong\mathbb Z^{2r_n}\) and \(H^2\cong\mathbb Z^{s_n}\) given by binary and four-color necklace counts. Its degree-one cup products generate a primitive rank-\(r_n^2\) subgroup of \(H^2\), while the complementary degree-two classes grow without bound.
