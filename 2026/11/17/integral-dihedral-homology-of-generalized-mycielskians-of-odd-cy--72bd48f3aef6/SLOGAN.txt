For every odd \(n\ge5\) not divisible by three and every generalized-Mycielskian depth \(\ell\ge0\), the complete integral dihedral action on reduced homology of \(\operatorname{Ind}(\mu_\ell(C_n))\) is given by two explicit reflection signs depending only on \(n\bmod12\). This refines the known sphere-wedge homotopy types and yields exact rational homology of all their dihedral quotients.
