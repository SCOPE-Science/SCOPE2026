The ordinary three-cut complex of the eleven-vertex Herschel graph has exactly \(\widetilde H_6\cong\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_7\cong\mathbb Z^{13}\), with no other reduced integral homology, so it is not shellable. Its complete \(S_3\times C_2\) rational homology representation is determined by a six-row character table, and neither homology group has a nonzero globally invariant class.
