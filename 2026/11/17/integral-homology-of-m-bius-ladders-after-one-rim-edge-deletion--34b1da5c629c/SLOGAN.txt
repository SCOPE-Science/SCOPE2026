Deleting one rim edge of the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) yields a torsion-free independence complex whose only possible reduced integral homology is one copy of \(\mathbb Z\) for \(n\equiv0,1\pmod4\), two copies for \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and zero for \(n\equiv3\pmod4\) (\(n\ge4\)), in dimensions specified in the theorem. The result distinguishes a rim fault from the previously studied antipodal-rung fault without asserting homotopy decompositions.
