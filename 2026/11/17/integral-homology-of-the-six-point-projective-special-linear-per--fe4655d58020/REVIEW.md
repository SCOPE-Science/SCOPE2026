The six-point projective action of the smallest nonabelian simple group is a canonical, highly symmetric, finite family member rather than an arbitrary selection of 60 words. Its full low-dimensional word skeleton coexists with two nonzero *intermediate* integral homology groups, proving failure of pure shellability and supplying a reproducible test case contrasting the recent shellable pattern-avoidance examples.
