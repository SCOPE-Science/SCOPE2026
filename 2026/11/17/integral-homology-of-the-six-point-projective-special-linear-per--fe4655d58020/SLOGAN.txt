The ordered-word permutation complex of the exceptional six-point action of \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_5)\) has exactly \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{30}\) and \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z^5\), with no other reduced integral homology or torsion. It is two-connected yet non-shellable, as proved by a complete unimodular cancellation of its finite cellular chain complex.
