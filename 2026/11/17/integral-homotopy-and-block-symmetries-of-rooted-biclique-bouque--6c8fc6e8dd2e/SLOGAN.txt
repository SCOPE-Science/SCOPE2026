For every bouquet of complete bipartite graphs \(K_{2,t_i}\) joined at one common vertex, the \(k\)-robust clique complex, at \(k\ge4\), is a wedge of \(e_{k-1}(t_i-1)\) spheres of dimension \(2k-3\). Its nonzero integral homology is the explicit direct sum of tensor products of leaf-block augmentation lattices, yielding exact automorphism traces even when the block widths differ.
