# Scientific review of windmill higher-independence homotopy and symmetry

## Correctness — PASS

For \(W_{n,q}=K_1\vee(nK_q)\), the center deletion is a join of \(n\) truncated simplices, while the center link is the \((r-2)\)-skeleton on all \(nq\) blade vertices. The latter description follows from the center being adjacent to every blade vertex. The explicit element matching proves the skeleton wedge formula in every integer range, including \(r=2\) and \(r=nq\). The deletion is either contractible or \((nr-2)\)-connected, whereas the center link has dimension \(r-2\); the map to the deletion is therefore nullhomotopic. The cofiber splitting gives precisely the stated integral wedge multiplicities, with distinct degrees when \(r<q\). Natural augmented chain complexes and join signs yield the two integral wreath-product homology modules. Blade and block subgroup invariants then give rational quotient acyclicity. No infinite-dimensional conclusion depends on computational extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The closest earlier public research record is the dissociation polynomial and maximal-set classification for generalized windmills, which concerns precisely the \(r=2\) face enumerator but does not give the homotopy or integral symmetry modules and does not address \(r\ge3\). The windmill neighborhood-complex classification studies a different functor. Deshpande and Singh (arXiv:2001.05448) compute higher-independence homotopy for complete multipartite, fully whiskered, cycle, path and rooted-tree families, none of which includes this clique-bouquet graph family. Abdelmalek et al. (arXiv:2106.10863) treat general chordal qualitative and tree/caterpillar properties, without these exact modules. Sawant (arXiv:2605.25150, first publicly posted 2026-05-24) establishes vertex decomposability for trees, not windmills with triangular cliques.

The result is not a restatement of the known \(r=2\) polynomial: the two-degree homotopy classification in all higher thresholds, the integral wreath-product modules, and the rational quotient calculation require additional topological and representation-theoretic arguments. No covering exact statement was found using names, aliases, formulas or full-document inspection of the accessible principal papers. Search and indexing cannot rule out unindexed equivalent work; the full content of the two closest indexed public research records was not accessible through the research search interface.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Generalized windmills are a canonical two-parameter family of non-tree chordal clique bouquets. Their higher-independence complexes exhibit a genuine mixed-degree transition: for \(2\le r<q\), the nonzero reduced homology occurs in degrees \(r-1\) and \(nr-1\), with explicit independent binomial and exponential-in-blades multiplicities; at \(r=q\), the high-degree summand disappears. The symmetry modules distinguish homology classes beyond Betti numbers, and the fact that the entire family of quotient complexes is rationally acyclic supplies a structural application. This is a natural complete classification with a conceptual cofiber proof, not an isolated table lookup or minor numerical improvement.
