For generalized windmills \(K_1\vee(nK_q)\), every higher-independence complex with \(2\le r<q\) is exactly a wedge of \(\binom{nq-1}{r-1}\) spheres of dimension \(r-1\) and \(\binom{q-1}{r}^{n}\) spheres of dimension \(nr-1\); the remaining \(q\le r\le nq\) thresholds have only the first wedge. The complete integral homology action of \(\mathfrak S_q\wr\mathfrak S_n\) is given by exterior powers of augmentation lattices, and every such symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
