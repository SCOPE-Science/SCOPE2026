For two unordered particles on the six-vertex pentagonal wheel, the compact cubical configuration complex has quotient homotopy types \(\bigvee^6S^1\), \(\bigvee^2S^1\), \(\bigvee^2S^1\), and a point under the trivial, rotational, reflection, and full dihedral subgroups. Exact orbit-flag collapses give a complete integral homotopy classification for every subgroup of the wheel's symmetry group.
