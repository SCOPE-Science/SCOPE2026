For the balanced four-row, five-column two-rook-per-row complex, integral third homology is the column-sign twist of the permutation lattice on the three pairings of four rows. The complex is \(\bigvee^3 S^3\vee\bigvee^{42}S^4\), while its 42-dimensional rational fourth homology splits into three explicit irreducible product Specht modules.
