The seven-column multiple chessboard complex with row capacities \((1,2,2,2)\) has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{63}S^4\vee\bigvee^{36}S^5\). Permuting its three capacity-two rows gives rational homology characters \((63,35,21)\) and \((36,-20,15)\) on the identity, transposition and three-cycle classes.
