For every odd hyperedge size \(r\ge3\), a free involution turns the complete hypergraph matching complex into a canonical phase-inflation of a smaller matching complex. Its top integral fixed-space homology has an explicit signed wreath-product lattice indexed by perfect \(r\)-set partitions, of rank \(\frac{(rk)!}{(r!)^k k!}(2^{r-1}-1)^k\), despite vanishing top homology for the un-inflated base.
