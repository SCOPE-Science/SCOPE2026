The geometric quotient of the rank-four finite-field Tits building by its projective Singer group has cyclic fundamental group of order \(q^2+1\) and free second homology of rank \(q^2(q-1)\), for every prime power \(q\ge2\). A unique intermediate-field orbit of projective lines produces unbounded first-homology torsion absent from the original wedge of spheres.
