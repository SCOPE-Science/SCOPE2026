Deleting just one edge from the Boolean hypercube preserves two isomorphic open-neighborhood-complex components, but each loses exactly \(n-2\) primitive two-sphere classes; the resulting components are \(\bigvee^{2^{n-3}(n^2-5n+8)-n+1}S^2\) for every \(n\ge3\). The integral inclusion cokernel is the standard augmentation lattice of the edge-stabilizing \(\mathfrak S_{n-1}\), represented by explicit four-vertex sphere boundaries.
