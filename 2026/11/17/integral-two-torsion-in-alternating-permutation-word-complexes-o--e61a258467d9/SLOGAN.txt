The permutation complexes generated by the entire alternating groups on four and five letters have integral homology \(\widetilde H_2(X(A_4);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^3\oplus\mathbb Z/2\) and \(\widetilde H_3(X(A_5);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{16}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^4\), respectively, with no other reduced homology. These natural parity-restricted complexes are highly connected yet exhibit genuine higher-dimensional two-torsion, unlike the corresponding complete injective-word complexes.
