# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The two first integrals provide a global crossing orbit with finite original-time half-periods. Changing the half-orbit coordinate gives a symmetric beta density and hence the exact inverse-beta waveform. At each crossing the position series has only powers in multiples of three, with cubic jumps \(\mp2r^2(A+C)\) and equal sixth jumps \(20r^3(A^2-C^2)\). Fourier integration by parts yields the stated two-term coefficients and the \(O(n^{-10})\) remainder. A standalone numerical checker confirms the asymptotic signs, phase ratios and exact half-wave symmetry; it is supplementary to the analytic proof.

## Originality
PASS within inspected sources. Tang (2017) proved the center classification and nonisochronous dynamics; Chen–Llibre–Tang (2019) additionally evaluated the beta-function period on the identical quadratic field. Both are stronger than this work with respect to existence and period, which are expressly excluded from novelty. The known Fourier jump-expansion method is also prior art. The new claim is restricted to the physical-time inverse-beta waveform and the explicit third/sixth derivative jump coefficients generating the rational and irrational arithmetic Fourier spectrum; these statements are not supplied in the inspected same-system primary texts. Related published oscillator claims concern different systems. A generic Fourier reconstruction paper was inspected by abstract only, and an equivalent unindexed oscillator derivation remains possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The harmonic spectrum is a natural observable for the established switched periodic oscillator. The result gives exact parameter-dependent selection rules and a nontrivial, three-power decay transition on full arithmetic frequency sublattices, sharply separating balanced, rationally unbalanced and irrationally unbalanced crossing clocks. These features are not routine restatements of the period integral.

## Limitations and prior coverage
No assertion of independent mathematical validation or exhaustive literature priority. Results are for fixed strictly positive coefficients and a single horizontal position observable; no parameter-uniform spectral remainder or global family classification is claimed. Center existence, period scaling, and general piecewise Fourier-jump theory belong to the predecessors. The later 2019 accepted manuscript was materially inspected for matching system and period implications; the 2010 general Fourier paper's full text was unavailable.
