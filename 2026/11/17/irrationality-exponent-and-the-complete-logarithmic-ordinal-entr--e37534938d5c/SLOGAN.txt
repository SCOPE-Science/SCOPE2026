For every irrational circle rotation, the **entire** accumulation interval of the raw order-\(n\) permutation-entropy ratio \(H_n/\log n\) is \([1/(\mu-1),1]\), where \(\mu\) is its irrationality exponent. Thus exponent-two slopes have asymptotic ratio one, whereas Liouville slopes exhibit every limiting ratio from zero to one, even though all of these rotations have zero dynamical entropy.
