# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The deterministic assumptions are explicit: three strictly nonzero cyclic pairwise interactions with magnitudes less than one, and globally synchronized independent fair sign reversals. All states remain on the simplex. Bounded coordinate martingales plus summable predictable quadratic variation imply convergence to the shared fixed set. The precise fixed-set census has three vertices and a unique positive point. A strictly separating weighted-geometric-mean Lyapunov function rules out that interior point except from itself. The limiting martingale means give the exact winner law, and the independent sign strong law gives the pairwise extinction rates. All stationary probability measures are then classified by stationary-law weak limits. Exact-rational computations support finite algebra only.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The 2014 random-Volterra result of Jamilov–Scheutzow–Wilke-Berenguer demands a per-coordinate squaring operator in the support; the present nonextremal two-sign support has none. The transitive sign-switching theorem assumes a globally ordered tournament with no positive kernel. A cyclic rock-paper-scissors tournament violates that assumption, has a common coexistence fixed point, and requires a distinct Lyapunov argument. The 2022 Avelino–de Oliveira–Trintin and 2023 Yao–Swailem–Dobramysl–Täuber papers concern spatial/reaction-level stochastic competition, not this exact stochastic frequency recursion. The closest deterministic Euler–May–Leonard stationary-measure result does not imply the stated random fixation law. A related finite-population carrying-capacity switching article was reviewed at abstract level only; unindexed equivalent mathematics remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Cyclic competition is the smallest natural nontransitive extension of a strict competition ordering. The sharp coexistence exception, complete stationary probability simplex, exact initial-share fixation law and pairwise extinction exponents identify a qualitative boundary where a random environmental reversal erases cyclic coexistence for every noncentral initial population. This is a natural full classification for a three-species model, not an arbitrary isolated scalar statistic.

## Limitations
No conclusion is asserted for biased or temporally correlated sign choices, locally independent pairwise reversals, reaction-diffusion models, or finite-population demographic noise. On initially missing species, logarithmic rates are not asserted. This is a self-review of the proof and literature, not independent scientific validation.
