For inverse-Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck operators in dimensions at least three, the exact small-time Schatten blow-up exponent is the Kalman-weighted dimension \(K=\sum_k(2k+1)m_k\), with an explicit positive graded-Gramian coefficient. At fixed diffusion rank \(r\), the sharp largest exponent is \((d-r+1)^2+r-1\); an explicit skew-chain realizes it with a factorial leading constant and can make the trace norm grow like \(t^{-d^2}\) while the operator trace grows only like \(t^{-d}\).
