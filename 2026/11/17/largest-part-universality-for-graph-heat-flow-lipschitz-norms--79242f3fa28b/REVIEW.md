## Value — PASS

This is an exact norm theorem for an entire natural family of finite-dimensional Markov semigroups, not an estimate for one graph. The striking largest-part universality identifies all multipartite graphs of the same order and maximal part as sharing the same sharp Lipschitz profile; the sharp threshold depends on a two-vertex imbalance. At fixed order, the theorem completely orders all such graphs by amplification, identifies the extremal clique and star, constructs norming inputs, and determines a nontrivial continuous macroscopic peak profile and sharp universal limiting factor two. These properties quantify when positivity-preserving heat smoothing fails to be nonexpansive in the graph-Lipschitz seminorm.
