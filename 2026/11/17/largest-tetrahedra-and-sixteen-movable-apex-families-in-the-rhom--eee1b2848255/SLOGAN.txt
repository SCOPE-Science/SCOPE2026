The largest tetrahedron in the edge-two small rhombicuboctahedron occupies exactly \((1+5\sqrt2)/56\) of the solid; precisely 48 unordered vertex quadruples maximize its volume, equal to \((16+10\sqrt2)/3\). Sixteen distinct two-dimensional maximizing families arise by holding a suitable base triangle fixed and moving the opposite apex anywhere over one of the solid's eight triangular faces.
