For every odd prime \(p\ge5\), the full rotation quotient of the last noncontractible Vietoris–Rips complex on \(2p\) cyclic vertices is a mapping cone between two generalized lens spaces. It has exactly the integral homology of \(\mathbb{RP}^{p-1}\) but has \(\pi_2\cong\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\), unlike real projective space.
