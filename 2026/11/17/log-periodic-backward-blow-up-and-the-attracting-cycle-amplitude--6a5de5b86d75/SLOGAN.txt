In a strictly rotating, radially contracting homogeneous polynomial star-node flow, every initial point outside the attracting cycle blows up at finite backward physical time with infinitely many turns. Its type-I blow-up amplitude is the forward attracting cycle's radial graph sampled through an exact logarithmically periodic phase: \((t-t_-)^{1/(2p)}r(t)=\mathcal B(\log(C(t-t_-)))+O(t-t_-)\), and the profile is nonconstant precisely when the radial contraction depends on angle.
