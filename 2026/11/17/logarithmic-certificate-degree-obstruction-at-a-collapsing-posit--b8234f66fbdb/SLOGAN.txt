For the collapsing-interval matrix-positivity example of Jindal and Zalar, the least weighted sum-of-squares certificate degree satisfies \(\liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}d_\varepsilon/\log(1/\varepsilon)\geq1/\log(3+2\sqrt2)\). This quantifies their qualitative divergence theorem; neither coefficient optimality nor a matching upper bound is claimed.
