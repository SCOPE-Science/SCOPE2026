A globally periodic switching Hamiltonian made from two positive quartic nonlinearities has a logarithmically thin high-energy upper residence sector, while the lower sector follows beta-function scaling. Upper-sector physical-time and enclosed-area fractions both decay as \(E^{-1/4}\log E\), but their ratio tends to the universal value \(2/3\); conditional upper heights are logarithmically uniform, while lower heights obey distinct time- and area-beta laws.
