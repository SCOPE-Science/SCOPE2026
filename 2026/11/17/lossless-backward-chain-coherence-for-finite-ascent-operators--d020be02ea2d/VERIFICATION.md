# Verification record

The proof is self-contained conditional on the standard definition of finite ascent, elementary Banach-space inequalities, and the Riesz representation theorem for \(L^2(0,1)\). The main implication has been checked directly: \(\ker T^p=\ker T^{p+1}\) makes \(T\) injective on \(\operatorname{ran}T^p\); images of independent \((n+p)\)-step lifts then form the unique compatible chain and satisfy the explicit norm estimate. The reverse growth inequality is immediate from preimage minimality, and the strict-radial conclusion follows by taking growth-rate thresholds.

For the illustrative nonclosed, nonsplitting example, direct multiplication gives \(T^n(a,f)=(\int_0^1t^{n-1}f(t)\,dt,t^nf(t))\) for all \(n\geq1\); the lower and upper integral-root bounds give rate \(1/s\). A closed invariant complement would imply a bounded functional represented by \(1/t\), which fails to belong to \(L^2(0,1)\). This checks every critical boundary in the example without numerical approximations.
