In the canonical Euclidean realization of a two-cluster ultrametric Lipschitz-free space with both clusters nontrivial, the largest inscribed ball is completely insensitive to the smaller cluster's internal distance. A sharp majority-size threshold at \(t=2p/(p+q-1)\) collapses the affine dimension of its optimal-center set from \(p+q-2\) to \(p-1\), with an exact binomial staircase of tangent facets.
