For area-uniform endpoints on any flat Möbius band, shortest geodesics crossing the central core have a remarkably simple exact *joint* law: their two normalized endpoint heights and developed horizontal span are uniform on \(\{(u,v,z)\in(0,1)^3:z+r^2uv<1\}\), where \(r\) is the width-to-core-length ratio. The conditional span distribution decreases strictly in stochastic order as the band widens, interpolating between a uniform and a \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,2)\) law; the previously known unmarked core-crossing probability is not claimed as new.
