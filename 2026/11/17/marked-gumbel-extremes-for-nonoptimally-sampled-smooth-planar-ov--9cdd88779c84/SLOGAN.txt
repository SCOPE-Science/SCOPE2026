For smooth strictly convex ovals sampled from a positive nonoptimal boundary density with isolated nondegenerate minima of the sampling-to-curvature ratio, the location and size of every exceptionally large inscribed-polygon cap have a joint curvature-dependent marked Poisson limit. The worst cap concentrates in an explicit Gaussian mixture on the \(\log n\)^{-1/2} scale, independently of its Gumbel extreme height; the unmarked maximal-spacing distribution is acknowledged as prior work.
