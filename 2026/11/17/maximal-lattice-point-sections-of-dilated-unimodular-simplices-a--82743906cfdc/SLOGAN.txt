In every positive integer dilation of a unimodular simplex, the affine \(\ell\)-sections containing the most lattice points are **exactly** its \(\ell\)-faces: the maximum is \(\binom{k+\ell}{\ell}\), and the number of maximizers is the constant \(\binom{d+1}{\ell+1}\). Consequently, the exact positive-degree prediction in a recent general conjecture on maximal lattice-point slices fails even for a tetrahedron, although eventual quasi-polynomiality and upper-degree versions remain possible.
