For every height-two poset and every coordinatewise weighted continuous order-to-chain transfer, the sharp forward and inverse Euclidean Lipschitz constants agree and depend only on the largest weighted star sum \(W=\max_a\sum_{b>a}t_b^2\): both equal \(\sqrt{(2+W+\sqrt{W^2+4W})/2}\). Thus arbitrarily different bipartite comparability patterns with the same maximum weighted degree have exactly the same sharp transfer distortion.
