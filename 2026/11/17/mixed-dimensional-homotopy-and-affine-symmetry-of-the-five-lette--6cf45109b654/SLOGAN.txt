The natural twenty-element affine group on five letters has ordered-word permutation complex \(S^2\vee\bigvee^{17}S^3\), despite being sharply two-transitive. Its unique second-homology class transforms by the quadratic multiplier character, and its rational third homology has the explicit affine-group decomposition \(\mathbb Q_\chi\oplus\mathbb Q[C_4]\oplus3V\).
