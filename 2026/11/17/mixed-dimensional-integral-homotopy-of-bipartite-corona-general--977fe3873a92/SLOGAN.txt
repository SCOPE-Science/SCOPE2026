For the geodesic general-position complex of \(K_{a,b}\circ K_1\), \(a,b\ge2\), the integral homotopy type is \(\bigvee^{ab+a+b}S^1\vee\bigvee^{b-1}S^a\vee\bigvee^{a-1}S^b\). A contractible leaf simplex connects two cross-polytopal joins; exactly \(ab+a+b\) exceptional edges contribute the circles.
