The ordinary \(A_5\) quotient of the five-label, two-color injective-word complex is \(M(\mathbb Z/3,3)\vee M(\mathbb Z/30,3)\vee\bigvee^{37}S^4\), so its third integral homology has two distinct Smith invariant factors \(3\) and \(30\). An exhaustive orbit-chain computation and a simply connected skeleton argument determine the full homotopy type, not just rational Betti numbers.
