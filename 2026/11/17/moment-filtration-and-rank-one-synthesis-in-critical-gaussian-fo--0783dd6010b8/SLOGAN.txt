In the Gaussian Bargmann–Fock space, finite linear combinations of individually noncompact critical integration shifts exhibit a complete moment-controlled spectral hierarchy: each successive vanishing moment improves the singular-value decay by one power until the sum becomes finite rank. An explicit \((m+1)\)-st finite difference is exactly rank one, and the polynomial quotient at that endpoint gives every nonzero singular value and the precise rank of all finite-rank combinations.
