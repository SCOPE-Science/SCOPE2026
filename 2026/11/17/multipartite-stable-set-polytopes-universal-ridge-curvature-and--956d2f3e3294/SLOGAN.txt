For complete multipartite stable-set polytopes with at least two nonsingleton parts, the Euclidean ridge-angle measures collapse to three universal classes whose totals depend, after volume normalization, only on the number of parts and singleton parts. For every five-partite graph \(K_{1,1,1,p,q}\) with \(p,q\ge2\), acute and obtuse ridge-area contributions cancel exactly, so the area-weighted interior dihedral angle is \(\pi/2\) and \(V_{N-2}/V_N=N(N-1)(5+\sqrt5/2)\).
