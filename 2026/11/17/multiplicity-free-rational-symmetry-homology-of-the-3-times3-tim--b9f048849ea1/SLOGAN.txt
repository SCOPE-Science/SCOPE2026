The radius-two Vietoris–Rips complex of \([3]\times[3]\times[4]\) has a 96-dimensional third rational homology representation of \((S_3\wr S_2)\times S_4\) that is a sum of exactly fourteen pairwise nonisomorphic irreducibles, each once. Its full 45-class character is explicit, and the orbit-space quotient has vanishing positive-degree rational homology.
