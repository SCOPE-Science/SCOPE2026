# Mathematical assessment of the corrected dispersion relation

**Correctness.** PASS. The trace-zero modal matrix has off-diagonal entries \(c>0\) and \(-d<0\), so its determinant is **positive**, \(cd=(1+Aq^2/\sqrt{k_2})(1+Bq^2/\sqrt{k_1})\). The characteristic polynomial is \(\lambda^2+cd\), giving purely imaginary eigenvalues and a frequency strictly increasing with \(q^2\). The earlier negative-determinant label was an algebraic sign typo; the final eigenvalue formula was correct. A separate symbolic computation checked the determinant and the diagonal-diffusion contrast.

**Originality.** PASS on current literature evidence, subject to documented priority risks. Broadbridge–Cherniha–Goard (DOI 10.1017/S095679252200033X; arXiv:2403.02644), studied in its complete preprint form, constructs the square-root model and spatially harmonic exact solutions and remarks that steady-state stability status matches conventional Lotka–Volterra diffusion. It does not explicitly give this all-Laplacian-mode frequency. The special harmonic solutions are not the general \(q^2>0\) spectrum.

**Mathematical value.** PASS. The complete frequency law distinguishes undamped cross-diffusive modes from exponentially damped diagonal-diffusion modes, clarifying a concrete question about spatial stability of this ecological PDE. It is a model-specific, elementary calculation, not a general PDE stability theorem.

**Limits.** Periodic/homogeneous Neumann boundary modes at a positive spatially constant equilibrium only. Purely imaginary eigenvalues do not establish nonlinear stability, existence of persistent nonlinear patterns, or well-posedness for arbitrary data. An unindexed earlier derivation may exist.
