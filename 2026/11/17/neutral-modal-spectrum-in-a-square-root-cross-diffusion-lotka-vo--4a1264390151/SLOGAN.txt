The square-root cross-diffusion model has \(\lambda_\pm(q)=\pm i\omega(q)\) for every Laplacian mode, with \(\omega(q)^2=\left(1+\frac{Aq^2}{\sqrt{k_2}}\right)\left(1+\frac{Bq^2}{\sqrt{k_1}}\right)\). Nonzero spatial modes therefore oscillate without linear exponential damping; this is a linear result, not a nonlinear stability theorem.
