The fully bidirected fan on a four-vertex path has multipath complex \(\bigvee^9S^2\vee\bigvee^2S^3\), and its path-reversal symmetry has rational homology eigenspace dimensions \((5,4)\) in degree two and \((1,1)\) in degree three. An exhaustive integral discrete-Morse certificate determines the homotopy type, not merely its Betti numbers.
