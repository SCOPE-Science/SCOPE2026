The six-column, four-row multiple chessboard complex with row capacities \((1,2,2,2)\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{98}S^4\), despite being pure of dimension five. An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching isolates 98 four-cells and fills the previously unclassified six-column boundary of a recent four-row torsion family.
