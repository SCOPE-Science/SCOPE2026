## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed Nauru as 24 injections of length three into four symbols, 36 edges and bipartition 12+12. Konig vertex-cover equality shows any edge set without a 3-matching is contained in union of two vertex stars; an independent triple-matching search agreed on 7627 augmented faces with sizes (1,36,630,2256,2988,1476,240). The all-edges staged Morse matching gives 3768 pairs, 28836 Hasse covers, verified acyclic, 91 critical 3-cells (empty face paired), hence an integral wedge of 91 S3. Independently computed S4 x S3 character via exact signed face traces, giving matrix [[91,7,1],[19,7,1],[7,11,1],[1,1,1],[1,1,1]] and four fixed classes; full automorphism group size 144 independently verified by graph isomorphism enumeration. Character decomposition is rational, not integral.
