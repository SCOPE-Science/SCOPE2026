# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The complete finite face poset was reconstructed twice, using vertex covers and independent forbidden-matching recursion, and the results agreed. All 3,768 explicit face pairs and 28,836 Hasse cover orientations were checked; the orientation is acyclic and leaves 91 critical three-cells and no critical augmented empty face. Standard discrete Morse theory therefore proves the stated integral wedge, not only a numerical Euler characteristic. All 144 graph automorphisms were independently enumerated, and the signed chain-Lefschetz computation produces the stated 15 character values, four invariant classes and an integral-multiplicity rational irreducible decomposition.

Holmsen–Lee (arXiv:2003.11270, Theorems 1.1–1.2) establishes a vanishing ceiling. Linusson–Shareshian–Welker (arXiv:math/0410345, Theorems 1.1 and 1.4) classifies complete and complete bipartite ambient graphs. Their statements and implications do not determine the sparse Nauru graph calculation or its full automorphism character. Previous computations for other named cubic graphs are not logically a universal theorem. No matching statement was found in targeted searches; this is evidence of incomplete coverage, not proof of uniqueness. Residual risk includes unindexed equivalent calculations and any unpublished general theorem.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The graph is a canonical 24-vertex edge-transitive cubic bipartite graph with a large 144-element automorphism group. Determining the exact integral homotopy type and every rational group-character value is a substantive finite equivariant classification not supplied by general Leray or homology-injection theorems. The four-dimensional invariant subspace is a precise symmetry-sensitive invariant that ordinary Betti numbers cannot recover. The result is deliberately restricted to the named graph and does not claim an unproved universal cubic-graph classification.
