The exact majority-fixed-state count on the step-two generalized Petersen graph \(GP(n,2)\) is the sum of two ninth-degree algebraic power sums plus two, for every \(n\ge5\). Its stable-state entropy per vertex is \(\tfrac12\log\alpha\), with \(1.56485<\alpha<1.56486\), strictly below the ordinary prism's \(\tfrac12\log\varphi\); equivalently, their matching-cut populations separate exponentially.
