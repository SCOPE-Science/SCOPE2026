# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The new claim is a computation of the **relative pair** and canonical inclusion maps, not just a Betti-number formula. A newly added edge with endpoint distance at least six introduces only faces containing both poles. Their relative chain complex is the two-step shift of \(C_*(Q\cup K_{A,B},K_{A,B};\mathbb Z)\), where \(Q\) is the old graph without the two endpoints. Connectivity of \(Q\cup K_{A,B}\), relative vertex-to-neighborhood paths and integral graph incidence establish the surjective degree-one relative boundary and a saturated free kernel of rank \(b(F)+1\). The long exact sequence gives split injection when the previous degree-two homology vanishes; the chordal tree base initiates induction. Tests corroborate 28 cycle chords and one noncycle case. The finite checks do not establish an infinite theorem, and mod-two ranks cannot detect torsion; the integral incidence argument supplies that step.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks
The closest known published high-girth result gives absolute sphere-wedge homotopy types and total Betti ranks as a function of cycle rank; it does not, in the accessible record summary, compute natural maps for arbitrary non-tree-edge orderings. Absolute types cannot imply whether any particular inclusion kills an old class. Filakovský's grid-like robust-clique results and Carnero Bravo's bounded-independence results give the definitions and tree base, not these map computations. Searches over graph-edge inclusion, theta/long-chord tests and persistence aliases returned no same-map result. The published high-girth research record's complete package was not fully retrievable; this is a significant but not decisive residual risk, not proof of uniqueness. An unindexed paper might contain equivalent naturality as a stronger theorem.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete natural integral persistence calculation on every connected high-girth graph and every spanning-tree edge order is a structural statement beyond the already-known absolute ranks. The primitive lattice assertion rules out both deaths and hidden finite-index effects, and the exact birth multiplicity provides an all-orders barcode. This addresses a natural question about homological stability under graph edge insertions, rather than an arbitrary isolated parameter.
