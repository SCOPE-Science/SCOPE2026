For a fixed-spectrum two-mode inverse Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup, every positive Schatten quasi-norm increases strictly with the drift's nonnormal shear, although the full eigenvalue multiset, trace, and operator norm remain unchanged. At the ellipticity edge, the Hilbert–Schmidt norm obeys an exact two-scale \(t^{-4}\) crossover with coefficient \((\xi+ab(a+b)^2/3)^{-1}\), rather than the interior \(t^{-2}\) law.
