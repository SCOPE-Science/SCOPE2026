For every odd \(n\ge7\), the ordinary graph-Hom complex from the \(n\)-cycle to the folded \(n\)-cube is a finite aspherical, nonpositively curved cubical complex with every vertex link equal to \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_n)\). Its exact Euler characteristic is \(2^{n/2}n!\cos(n\pi/4)\), while its links force it to be singular rather than a manifold.
