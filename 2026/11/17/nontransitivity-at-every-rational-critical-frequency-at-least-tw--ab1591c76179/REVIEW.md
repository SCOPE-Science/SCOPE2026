# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The defined one-sided shift and normalized discrepancy match the primary model. The complete proof controls every closed run: initial runs have nonnegative starting discrepancy, and subsequent runs begin after at least one zero, which at ratio \(\beta\ge2\) makes their starting discrepancy positive. The three-case separation for a later run is exhaustive and excludes its having length \(a+1\). The marker is admissible and can only occur at the first position in an unshifted generator; language transfer through product-topology cylinders rules out every bridge back to the marker. The packaged checker independently enumerates ten thousand finite prefixes for eight parameter pairs; this is corroboration, not proof of infinite uniqueness.

## Originality (PASS with stated priority risk)
The full Reyes preprint was examined, particularly Lemma 2.5, Proposition 5.8, Proposition 5.9 and Question 7.1. Its nontransitivity theorem is restricted to integer ratios \(\beta=k\ge2\); the new statement includes all reduced noninteger rational ratios \(\beta=a/b>2\) with \(b\ge2\). Neither prior integer-ratio classification nor the separate rational \(c<1/2\) transitivity theorem implies this claim. Targeted mathematical-record searches of the explicit marker, noninteger ratios, and transitivity classifications did not expose a dominating published statement. The founding 2023 article's complete text could not be inspected; its available bibliographic/abstract characterization addresses different questions, but this leaves a limited historical-priority risk. Search absence is not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
The November 2026 source explicitly leaves the classification of transitive frequency shifts open outside sparse integer-ratio parameters. The construction settles nontransitivity on an entire dense family of previously untreated rational parameters in \((2/3,1)\), with a concrete arithmetically anchored nonreturning marker and a qualitative boundary at \(\beta=2\) for the proof mechanism. It is more than a one-off counterexample or a reparameterization of the integer case. The results do not claim anything about irrational critical densities or the unresolved middle interval.
