For every rational critical density \(c\in[2/3,1)\), the one-sided Becker–Baraviera frequency shift is nontransitive: a specific admissible word \(0^b1^{a+1}0\), for \(c=a/(a+b)\) in lowest terms, cannot return even once. This settles a dense noninteger-ratio family left open by the known integer-quotient classification, without asserting anything about irrational densities.
