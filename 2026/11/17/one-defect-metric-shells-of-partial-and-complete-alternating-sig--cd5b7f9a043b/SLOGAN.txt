A single negative entry in a partial alternating sign matrix determines one of three exact Euclidean distance shells from the partial-permutation polytope, with squared distances \(1\), \(3/2\), or \(2\). In the complete square case, every one-negative alternating sign matrix has squared distance exactly \(2+2/(n+5)\) from the Birkhoff polytope: full row and column conservation spreads a local defect globally.
