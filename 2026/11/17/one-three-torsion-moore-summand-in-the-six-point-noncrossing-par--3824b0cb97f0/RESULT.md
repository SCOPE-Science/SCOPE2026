# One three-torsion Moore summand in the six-point noncrossing-partition rotation quotient

## Finding
Let \(\mathrm{NC}_6\) be the lattice of noncrossing set partitions of the six vertices of a cyclically ordered hexagon. Delete its discrete and indiscrete partitions, take the order complex \(K=\Delta(\overline{\mathrm{NC}}_6)\), and let \(C_6=\langle\rho\rangle\) act by \(\rho(i)=i+1\pmod 6\). The **ordinary geometric orbit space**, not the order complex of the orbit poset, has integral homotopy type

\[
\boxed{|K|/C_6\simeq M(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,2)\vee\bigvee^{8}S^3.}
\]

Here \(M(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,2)=S^2\cup_3 e^3\) is the degree-three Moore space. In particular the orbit space is simply connected, \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\), and \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z^8\), with no other positive-degree integral homology. The covering order complex has the previously known torsion-free homotopy type \(\bigvee^{42}S^3\).

## Assumptions and scope
All spaces are geometric realizations of finite simplicial or CW complexes. A partition is noncrossing precisely when no \(a<b<c<d\) have \(a,c\) in one block and \(b,d\) in another. Refinement defines the poset order. A \(d\)-simplex of \(K\) is a strictly increasing chain of \(d+1\) proper noncrossing partitions. Its orientation is increasing poset rank. The cyclic group acts on the **vertex labels**, not on chain positions. The claim applies to exactly six labeled points and this specified sixfold rotation action; it does not classify general \(n\) or all subgroup quotients.

## Proof

**1. A genuine quotient cellular chain complex.** Rotation preserves the rank (\(6-\text{number of blocks}\)) of every poset element. Each chain contains distinct ranks, so an element stabilizing a simplex setwise fixes all its vertices individually. Consequently the group acts without inversions, and orbits of oriented simplices are cells of the quotient; their alternating face-deletion coefficients give its integral cellular boundary. Merely taking chains in the orbit poset would not give this model.

**2. Exhaustive finite enumeration.** Generate all partitions of \(\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}\) by recursively placing each next label in an existing block or a new block; canonicalize sorted blocks, exclude the two bounds, and test all increasing quadruples for crossings. There are \(130\) proper noncrossing partitions, with rank counts \(15,50,50,15\). List every increasing comparable chain. The numbers of original simplices by dimensions \(0,1,2,3\) are respectively \(130,1035,2160,1296\). Partition these chains into the orbits of \(\rho\), giving exactly \(26,177,360,216\) quotient cells. The signed integral cellular boundary matrices have shapes \(26\times177\), \(177\times360\), and \(360\times216\), with respectively \(354,1080,864\) nonzero entries. Direct multiplication verifies both boundary compositions are zero.

**3. Integer certificate.** Apply only unit-pivot algebraic elementary cancellations: for an entry \(u=\pm1\) in row \(r\), column \(c\) of a differential, replace every surviving entry \(a_{ij}\) by \(a_{ij}-a_{ic}a_{rj}/u\), delete the pivot generators, and restrict the neighboring differentials accordingly. This is an integral chain isomorphism followed by removing a contractible two-term direct summand. A deterministic minimum-fill unit-pivot rule makes \(25\), \(152\), and \(207\) cancellations in successive degrees. The remaining free chain groups have ranks \((1,0,1,9)\) in degrees zero through three, and the **only** nonzero residual differential entry is \(3\) from degree three to degree two. Thus the complete integral Smith data are \(25\) unit factors in degree one, \(152\) unit factors in degree two, and \(207\) unit factors together with one factor \(3\) in degree three. In particular \(H_2=\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\), \(H_3=\mathbb Z^8\), and \(H_1=0\). This is exact integer computation, not a finite-field rank inference.

**4. Simple connectivity.** EL-shellability of the noncrossing-partition lattice gives \(K\simeq\bigvee^{42}S^3\), hence \(K\) is simply connected. The rotation \(\rho^2\) fixes the proper noncrossing partition \(\{\{0,1\},\{2,3\},\{4,5\}\}\). The rotation \(\rho^3\) fixes \(\{\{0,3\},\{1\},\{2\},\{4\},\{5\}\}\). Armstrong's orbit-space fundamental-group theorem identifies \(\pi_1(|K|/C_6)\) as the quotient of \(C_6\) by the subgroup generated by stabilizer elements. As \(\rho^2\) and \(\rho^3\) generate \(C_6\), this fundamental group is trivial.

**5. Homotopy refinement.** The quotient is a simply connected three-dimensional CW complex. Its two-skeleton is simply connected (three-cells do not affect \(\pi_1\)) and hence has the homotopy type of a wedge of two-spheres. Cellular attaching maps of three-cells are determined by their vectors in the free integral \(\pi_2\) of that two-skeleton. Integral basis changes can be realized by homotopy self-equivalences of the wedge of two-spheres. Applying the diagonal Smith form from step 3 leaves one degree-three attaching map and eight null attaching maps. It follows that the orbit space has the asserted Moore-space and sphere-wedge homotopy type.

## Relationship to prior work
The classical noncrossing-partition lattice and its shellability predate this result. Athanasiadis–Brady–Watt prove EL-shellability, furnishing the known wedge-of-spheres homotopy type of the original proper-part order complex. Callan–Smiley study rotation and reflection orbits of **individual noncrossing partitions**; their enumeration is not the topology of the orbit of the **chain complex**. Hersh analyzes quotient complexes of Boolean and ordinary partition posets under other symmetry groups, and Donau studies quotients of the ordinary partition lattice; neither supplies the stated noncrossing six-point rotation-orbit differential. The distinction between an orbit cell complex and an orbit-poset order complex is essential.

## Limitations
This is a finite exact six-point classification, not an infinite-family theorem or a uniqueness claim. An equivalent calculation might exist in unindexed literature. The fundamental-group and homotopy deductions invoke standard published theorems after their hypotheses have been verified; the integer cancellation certificate alone does not establish simple connectivity. There has been no independent mathematical audit.
