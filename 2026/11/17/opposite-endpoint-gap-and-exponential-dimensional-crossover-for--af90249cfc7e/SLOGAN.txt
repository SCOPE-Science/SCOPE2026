For the sharp nonlinear Riesz-potential BBM constant, the old upper bound and the optimum have the same \(1/\alpha\) pole but a positive finite gap; uniformly over all dimensions \(n\ge3\), the normalized gap is \((n/2)^{-\alpha/2}[\log2+(\psi(n/2)-\psi((n-1)/2))/2+O(\alpha)]\). When \(\alpha\log n\to\lambda\), it crosses over sharply to \(e^{-\lambda/2}\log2\), revealing noncommuting dimensional and fractional limits.
