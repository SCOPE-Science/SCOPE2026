For an equidistant one-point attachment to a Cartesian product of unit cycles, the exact minimum real-linear Lipschitz extension norm is \(\max\{1,c/R\}\), with \(c=\sum_i\lfloor n_i^2/4\rfloor/n_i\). At and below the threshold \(R=c\), every optimal signed coefficient vector is actually a probability measure with uniform odd-cycle marginals and antipodally symmetric even-cycle marginals; immediately above that threshold, negative coefficients become optimal and the optimizer set becomes full-dimensional.
