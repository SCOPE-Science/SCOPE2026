For Jana's cubic polynomial vector fields on an algebraic torus with an affine angular coefficient, the two invariant parallel circles have exact original-time periods and within-torus Floquet multipliers. Their period difference is governed by the determinant \(uq-vp\), while attraction versus repulsion is governed independently by the dot product \(pu+qv\); equal-time opposite-stability cycles and unequal-time neutral cycles both occur.
