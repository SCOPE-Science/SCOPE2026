Every shifted right-angle Minkowski sum has an exactly computable Euclidean diameter and a unique smallest enclosing ball; its contact vertices are counted by products of lengths of overlapping coordinate intervals, while its unordered diametral pairs number \(2^{c-1}\) for \(c\) overlap components. For Kozlov polytopes this yields a binomial-autocorrelation diameter formula in every rank and a sharp jump in sphere contacts when shifted variable supports cease to overlap.
