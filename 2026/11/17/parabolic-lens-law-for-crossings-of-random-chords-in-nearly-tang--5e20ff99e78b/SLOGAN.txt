For two independent endpoint-uniform chords on unit circles separated by \(2-\delta\), their crossing probability is asymptotic to \(\frac8{3\pi^3}\delta^{3/2}\log(1/\delta)\). Conditioned on crossing, the intersection blown up by horizontal scale \(\delta\) and vertical scale \(\sqrt\delta\) becomes uniformly distributed across the parabolic lens \(\{y^2+2|x|<1\}\) in total variation.
