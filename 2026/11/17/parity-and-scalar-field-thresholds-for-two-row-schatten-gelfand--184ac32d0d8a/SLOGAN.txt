For \(2\times n\) Schatten embeddings with \(0<p<q\le\infty\), the entire real Gelfand-number spectrum jumps directly from \(1\) to \(2^{1/q-1/p}\) at a parity-dependent index, while the complex spectrum has a strictly intermediate interval. The first bottom-level extremal subspaces are completely classified by orthogonal complex structures over \(\mathbb R\) and orthogonal pairs of unitary-related planes over \(\mathbb C\).
