# Verification and reproducibility

The main proof is analytical and applies to every integer \(n\ge3\) and real \(0<p<q\le\infty\). It proves the two endpoint equality conditions, the dimension bounds from rank-one avoidance and complex polarization, real odd-dimensional rigidity using even spectral multiplicities of skew matrices, and strict complex gaps by compactness of Grassmannians. The sample numerical checks in `check_rectangular.py` verify several predicted endpoint witnesses and compare singular-value ratios with the exact extrema. Numerical sampling neither proves global optimality nor computes the strict complex intermediate constants. Independent audit has not been performed.
