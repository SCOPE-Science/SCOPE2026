For every \(m,n\ge4\), the simplicial double of the joined polygon boundaries \(C_m*C_n\) splits exactly as \(K_{m,n}\sqcup(C_m\times C_n)\): it has two circle-wedge components unless both lengths are even, when it has three. Nevertheless its genuine geometric quotient under the factor-preserving dihedral group \(D_m\times D_n\) is always two disjoint contractible intervals.
