Synchronous majority on the crown graph exhibits an exact parity transition: at every odd shore size there is only one primitive two-cycle, while for even shore size \(n\), there are \(\frac{(\binom{n}{n/2}+2)(\binom{n}{n/2}+1)}2\) such cycles. Every trajectory reaches a fixed point or two-cycle within three updates, with sharp bounds depending on the shore size.
