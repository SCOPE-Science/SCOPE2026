For the non-lazy neighbor-averaging walk on every balanced double-star tree, the sharp edge-total-variation norm of every even power is exactly \(1\), while odd powers amplify variation precisely before a finite threshold \(m<\log(2k)/\log(1+1/k)\). The odd-power excess has a sharp \(k\log k\) extinction scale and an explicit one-sided exponential transition profile; binary edge-cut signals attain every constant.
