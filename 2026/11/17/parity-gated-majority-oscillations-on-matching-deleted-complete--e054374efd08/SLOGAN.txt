For synchronous binary majority on a complete graph with \(r\) disjoint missing edges and \(u\) universal vertices, every orbit becomes periodic within three updates, and the complete fixed-point and primitive two-cycle census has explicit binomial formulas. Even \(u\) supports a large balanced oscillatory layer, while odd \(u\ge3\) permits two-cycles only under a sharp parity-and-size condition, producing extinction and reappearance when universal vertices are added.
