On a bouquet of \(k\) squares, a hub-first sequential binary XOR scan has maximal period eight when \(k\) is even, but maximal period twelve when odd \(k\ge3\), with a three-step exception for one square. An exact all-size Jordan decomposition gives every primitive period and orbit count; the maximal-period fraction is \(1/2\) for even \(k\) and approaches \(3/4\) for odd \(k\).
