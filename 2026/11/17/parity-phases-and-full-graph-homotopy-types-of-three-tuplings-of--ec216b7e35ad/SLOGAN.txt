The three-tupling of the join of three polygon boundaries is exactly a disjoint union of four categorical graph products, with its number of connected components determined by the parity pattern of the three polygon lengths. It has \(4,5,7,\) or \(10\) components and explicit free fundamental-group ranks; the all-square case is ten disjoint wedges of 49 circles, showing disconnected three-tuplings even for a triangulated five-sphere.
