## Originality

**PASS, provisionally.** Inoue–Watanabe's congruent-diagonal analysis treats rational area partitions rather than the paired-intersection radial law. Bortolotto–Souza's random-circle-chord result provides the unconditional radius law and a general line-intersection computation; it does not establish this conditioned fixed-span double scaling. Poonen–Rubinstein count intersections without the present radial distribution. A closely related published crossing-angle result has a different, order-two moment transition; another critical-window result treats nearest-vertex distances rather than radial intersections. The basic cosine quotient and harmonic asymptotics are not claimed as novel in isolation. Unindexed or implicit earlier near-diametral asymptotics are the main priority risk.

**PASS.** Intersections of equal-length polygon diagonals arise directly in natural regular-polygon dissections.
