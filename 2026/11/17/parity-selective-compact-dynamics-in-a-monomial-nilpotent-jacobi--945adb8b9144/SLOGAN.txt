For each integer \(m\ge2\), the nilpotent-Jacobian polynomial flow \(\dot x=(y+x^2)^m\), \(\dot y=z-2x(y+x^2)^m\), \(\dot z=(y+x^2)^{2m}-1\) has no bounded forward dynamics when \(m\) is even, but has a finite nonzero number of isochronous periodic cylinders when \(m\) is odd, and every bounded forward orbit belongs to one. The cylinders are exactly the zeros of an explicit Abelian drift integral, and their surrounding planar periods diverge with coefficient \(1/\sqrt{2m}\) in the logarithmic separatrix law.
