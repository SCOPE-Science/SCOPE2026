For every even-dimensional cyclic polytope, all minimum facet-hitting vertex sets are precisely the complements of a maximum cycle-independent set plus a fixed number of other vertices. Their exact number is \(2\binom{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}{k-1}\) for even \(n\), but \(n\binom{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}{k-1}\) for odd \(n\), giving a full degree of growth difference between the two parities at fixed dimension.
