For the symmetric doubly stochastic vertex formed by \(r\) disjoint half-weighted triangles, its unique Frobenius projection onto the convex hull of involution permutation matrices has an exact two-branch formula depending on the parity of \(r\). Cross-triangle transpositions cut the leading squared projection error from \(r/2\) under independent rounding to \(r/6+O(1)\), with a parity-dependent correction.
