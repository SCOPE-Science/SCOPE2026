# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For all integers \(a,b\ge3\) and \(1\le t\le\min(a,b)\), the defining disconnected triples yield the exact dual decomposition. Each deleted-pair star has graph link \(K_{a-1,b-1}\) with earlier matching pairs removed; an explicit join of two triangle-boundary cycles maps to every link four-cycle. An integral chord-reduction argument generates the cycle lattice except at one precisely classified exceptional step. The resulting exact homology sequences, fundamental-group preservation, and Hurewicz–Whitehead arguments prove the homotopy assertions. The two small exceptional links are checked separately. Independent direct enumerations and mod-two rank tests on 65 examples agree. Risk: the finite tests alone do not prove the infinite theorem; the integral proof is indispensable.

**Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic uncertainty.** The complete bipartite theorem in Bayer et al. applies only before any edge deletion; the published crown theorem handles full balanced matching deletion with at least five vertices per shore; the previously recorded one-edge statement handles precisely one removed edge. Shen et al. address realization and uniqueness rather than the required homotopy types. Jojić–Vrećica–Živaljević give a shellability criterion for a full multiple-chessboard family but no formula for this partially forbidden-board filtration and its exceptional phases. These endpoints and tools are credited. The genuinely new coverage is all intermediate partial matchings, unbalanced shores with arbitrary matching counts, and the \((3,3,3)\), \((4,4,4)\) phase transitions. Risk: an unindexed equivalent partial-board formula remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Ordinary cut complexes measure induced connectivity; deleting a matching creates a natural exact one-parameter perturbation between complete bipartite and crown graphs. This theorem determines the full integral Alexander-dual homotopy type in that entire family, showing a predictable cubic dimension in the generic range and two sharp changes to circle/two-sphere homotopy. The three-/four-cycle link argument supplies a reusable structural mechanism, while integral Alexander duality gives the original cut complex's complete integral homology. It does not assert the original cut complex is itself a wedge of spheres.
