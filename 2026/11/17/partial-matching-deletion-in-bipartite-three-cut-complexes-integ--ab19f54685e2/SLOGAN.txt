Deleting a partial matching from a complete bipartite graph gives an explicit integral sphere-wedge homotopy classification for the Alexander dual of its ordinary three-cut complex. The generic dual is \(\bigvee^tS^1\vee\bigvee^{B(a,b,t)}S^3\); the two complete small matchings \((3,3,3)\) and \((4,4,4)\) instead yield four circles and four circles plus one two-sphere.
