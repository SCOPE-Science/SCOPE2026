For any nonempty finite simple graph \(G\), the ordinary matching complex of its fully whiskered graph is contractible precisely when \(G\) has no perfect matching. If \(G\) has \(2k\) vertices and \(p\) perfect matchings, the complex is exactly a wedge of \(p\) spheres \(S^{k-1}\), with an explicit acyclic matching leaving one critical simplex per perfect matching.
