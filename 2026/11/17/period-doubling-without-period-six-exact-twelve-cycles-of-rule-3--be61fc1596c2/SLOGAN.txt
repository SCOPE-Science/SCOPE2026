Rule 30 has primitive temporal twelve-cycles exactly on binary rings whose circumference is divisible by 60, 100, or 138, with respectively 15, 25, and 46 temporal orbits contributed by these divisibilities. Primitive six-cycles instead occur only on multiples of 84, so a 60-cell ring supports a twelve-cycle but no six-cycle: doubling the temporal period can decrease the minimum spatial circumference.
