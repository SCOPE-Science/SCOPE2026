# Periodic equilibrium correlations and tight digit counts in rational frequency shifts

## Finding

For every integer \(k\geq1\), put \(N=k+1\) and let \(\Sigma(k/N)\) be the one-sided binary frequency shift introduced by Becker--Baraviera and analyzed by Reyes. Let \(\mu_k\) denote its unique measure of maximal entropy, whose existence and uniqueness and entropy \(\log 2/N\) were proved by Reyes. The following are additional, quantitative conclusions about this particular measure, understood on its two-sided natural extension when past coordinates are needed.

1. There is an almost-sure, uniquely defined phase \(U\in\{0,\dots,N-1\}\) which is uniform and advances by one modulo \(N\) under one symbol shift. Conditional on any phase, the \(N\)-step shift is isomorphic to the fair two-sided Bernoulli shift. Equivalently, successive complete generators \(0^m1^{km}\) have independent lengths \(M\) satisfying \(\Pr(M=m)=2^{-m}\), \(m\geq1\), when sampled from a generator junction (not from a uniformly chosen symbol).

2. For the zero-symbol indicator \(I_j=\mathbf 1_{\{x_j=0\}}\), the exact conditional means are

\[
q_a:=\mathbb E_{\mu_k}(I_0\mid U=a)=\frac{2^{N-1-a}}{2^N-1},\qquad 0\leq a<N.
\]

In particular \(\mathbb E I_0=1/N\). For every \(0\leq r<N\) the long-lag covariance limit exists and is

\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\operatorname{Cov}_{\mu_k}(I_0,I_{Nm+r})
=\frac{2^{N-r}+2^r}{3N(2^N-1)}-\frac1{N^2}=:C_{N,r}.
\]

Convergence to the displayed periodic limits is exponentially fast for each fixed \(N\). The \(r=0\) limit is strictly positive for every \(N\geq2\), so the equilibrium digit process is not mixing, despite positive metric entropy. The full vector of limits has mean zero across its \(N\) residue classes.

3. Define \(Z_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}I_j\). There exists an explicit nonnegative stationary transfer function \(R\in\bigcap_{p<\infty}L^p(\mu_k)\) on the natural extension satisfying the **exact coboundary equation**

\[
N Z_n-n=R\circ\sigma^n-R\qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq0
\]

on a common full-measure set. Hence \(\sup_{n\geq1}\mathbb E[(Z_n-n/N)^2]<\infty\), and the asymptotic variance per symbol of the centered zero count is exactly zero. Along each subsequence \(n\equiv r\pmod N\), the centered count has an explicit (generally non-Gaussian) lattice distributional limit as described below.

For that limit, take mutually independent \(T,S,T',S'\) with \(\Pr(T=t)=2^{-(t+1)}\), \(t\ge0\), and similarly for the other variables. Put

\[
R_a(T,S)=N\min\{kT+a,S+1\}-a.
\]

Then, with \(A\) uniform on \(\{0,\ldots,N-1\}\), independent of these four geometric variables,

\[
Z_{Nm+r}-\frac{Nm+r}{N}\ \xrightarrow[m\to\infty]{\mathrm{law}}\
\frac{R_{(A+r)\bmod N}(T',S')-R_A(T,S)}{N}.
\]

No pointwise uniform discrepancy bound is asserted: the transfer \(R\) is unbounded but has exponentially decaying tails.

## Assumptions and scope

The symbolic system and maximal-entropy measure are exactly those in Reyes, arXiv:2611.10995v1, Theorems C and D. The prior results provide \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(\Sigma(k/N))=\log2/N\), uniqueness of its maximal-entropy measure and support on the coded subsystem generated by \(G_k=\{0^m1^{km}:m\ge1\}\). The present conclusions concern that unique measure for every integer \(k\geq1\). Statements about the \(N\)-step Bernoulli automorphism and the transfer \(R\) use the standard two-sided natural extension, whose nonnegative-coordinate marginal is \(\mu_k\).

The phase convention is: in the stationary two-sided concatenation, generator starts (occurrences of the boundary pattern `10`, at the transition from the preceding generator) occupy times congruent to \(-U\pmod N\), and the symbol at micro-time zero is at offset \(U\) from the nearest macro-grid point. The choice fixes the sign in the phase update and the indexing in the formula for \(q_a\). The pairwise long-lag limits, centered counts and variance statements are intrinsic to the one-sided stationary process.

## Proof

**Step 1: a stationary Bernoulli-marker construction.** Fix \(N=k+1\). Let \((B_j)_{j\in\mathbb Z}\) be independent fair bits. Almost surely the set of indices \(j\) with \(B_j=1\) is unbounded in both directions. Between successive marked macro-times \(j<j+m\), place the binary generator \(0^m1^{km}\) at micro-times \(Nj,\ldots,N(j+m)-1\). This defines a bi-infinite symbolic sequence \(y\). Choose an independent uniform \(U\) from \(0,\ldots,N-1\), and set \(x_t=y_{t+U}\). The shift by one micro-time permutes phases and, upon phase wraparound, shifts the Bernoulli macro-sequence by one. Therefore the law \(\nu\) of \(x\) is invariant and ergodic.

Every sampled sequence is a two-sided concatenation of generators from \(G_k\), so its forward marginal is supported on Reyes' coded shift \(X_k\subseteq\Sigma(k/N)\). A generator boundary is exactly an occurrence of `10`: there are no such occurrences strictly inside \(0^m1^{km}\). Consequently the digit sequence recovers its boundary set, its unique grid residue \(U\), and the macro-marker bits \(B_j\) almost surely. On each phase component, \(\sigma^N\) is measurably isomorphic to the two-sided fair Bernoulli shift. Its entropy is \(\log2\), so \(h_\nu(\sigma)=\log2/N\). By Reyes' entropy and uniqueness theorems, \(\nu\) projects to \(\mu_k\). Consecutive marked macro-gaps are geometric with \(\Pr(M=m)=2^{-m}\). This uses the **junction/Palm** sampling convention; a uniformly sampled time sees length-biased blocks instead.

**Step 2: conditional symbol probabilities.** Fix the phase \(U=a\). The last marked macro-time not after macro-index zero is \(-T\), and the next marked time is \(S+1\). By independence of negative and positive Bernoulli coordinates, \(T,S\) are independent and \(\Pr(T=t)=\Pr(S=t)=2^{-(t+1)}\). The containing block has macro-length \(M=T+S+1\). Its current micro-position is \(j=NT+a\), so the symbol is zero precisely when \(j<M\), equivalently \(S\ge kT+a\). Thus

\[
q_a=\sum_{t=0}^\infty 2^{-(t+1)}2^{-(kt+a)}
=\frac{2^{N-a-1}}{2^N-1}.
\]

Summing over \(a\) gives \(N^{-1}\sum_a q_a=1/N\).

**Step 3: periodic long-lag covariance.** For each fixed phase, \(I_0\) is a bounded measurable function of a two-sided iid Bernoulli sequence. The mixing property of that Bernoulli shift implies that, at lags \(Nm+r\), its correlation with the corresponding phase-\((a+r)\bmod N\) observation tends to the product \(q_aq_{(a+r)\bmod N}\). Averaging uniformly over phases gives

\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\mathbb E[I_0 I_{Nm+r}]
=\frac1N\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}q_aq_{(a+r)\bmod N}.
\]

Two finite geometric sums yield the identity

\[
\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}q_aq_{(a+r)\bmod N}=
\frac{2^{N-r}+2^r}{3(2^N-1)}.
\]

Subtracting \(1/N^2\) proves the covariance formula. The conditional coding at time zero depends on marker bits only as far as the nearest left/right marked macro-times; the probability that either distance exceeds \(L\) is at most \(2^{1-L}\). Replacing each coded observation by its finite-radius truncation, choosing \(L\) proportional to \(m/3\), makes the two truncated blocks disjoint and independent at large lag. The truncation error is \(O(2^{-m/3})\), establishing exponential convergence. As the conditional means \(q_a\) are not all equal, \(C_{N,0}=(1/N)\sum_a(q_a-1/N)^2>0\). The phase average over all residues is zero since \(\sum_rq_{a+r}=1\).

**Step 4: the discrepancy coboundary and tight distributional limits.** For a containing generator \(0^M1^{kM}\), define its progress \(j\in\{0,\ldots,NM\}\), measured from its start, and assign the integer height

\[
R(j)=\min\{kj,NM-j\}.
\]

This height rises by \(k=N-1\) at every zero symbol, falls by one at every one symbol, and is zero at generator boundaries, so throughout every concatenation

\[
R_{t+1}-R_t=N I_t-1.
\]

Telescoping proves \(N Z_n-n=R_n-R_0\). In a typical stationary block, \(M=T+S+1\), and \(0\le R_t\le kM\). Both \(T\) and \(S\) have geometric tails; hence \(R\) has moments of every finite order and the centered digit-count second moments are uniformly bounded by \(4\mathbb E[R^2]/N^2\). The ordinary variance rate is therefore zero (this is a coboundary, not an independent-symbol central limit theorem).

At phase \(a\), substitute \(M=T+S+1\) and \(j=NT+a\) into the height to obtain \(R_a(T,S)=N\min\{kT+a,S+1\}-a\). Bernoulli mixing applied to this square-integrable height (first truncate its geometric tails) gives asymptotic independence of the two endpoint heights conditional on initial and final phases. Taking the difference in the telescoping identity yields the stated full set of residue-class limit laws.

## Relationship to prior work

Reyes, arXiv:2611.10995v1, Theorems C and D, proves the coded support, entropy and unique maximal-entropy measure for these exact frequency shifts; Remark 5.6 already identifies generator-boundary congruences and proves that the coded subsystem is **not topologically mixing**. None of those facts is claimed new here. The new contribution is the explicit maximal-entropy process as a **phase-resolved** fair Bernoulli-marker suspension, its exact conditional digit frequencies and periodic covariance constants, and the \(L^p\) discrepancy coboundary with subsequence fluctuation laws.

Pavlov, arXiv:1803.05966v1, Theorems 1.3--1.5, supplies broad coded-shift entropy/uniqueness theory and explicitly notes that general uniquely decipherable coded shifts have Bernoulli-like maximal measures modulo possible periodicity. These general precedents cover the qualitative Bernoulli-modulo-periodicity mechanism, and the present result does **not** claim a new general Bernoulli theorem. They do not give the special phase law \(q_a\), the closed covariance profile \(C_{N,r}\), or the displayed discrepancy transfer and limit distribution for Reyes' recently specified frequency shifts.

A comparison against published nearby mathematical records considered other coded-shift correlation examples, symbolic frequency-discrepancy shifts and an additive toral skew product with persistent correlations. Those are different systems and no located statement implies these exact formulas for \(\Sigma(k/N)\).

## Limitations

The formulas apply only to the rational frequency parameters \(p_c=k/(k+1)\) with integer \(k\ge1\), and only to the distinguished maximal-entropy measure. They make no assertion about general \(p_c\), exceptional orbits, all invariant measures, or topological mixing beyond the prior literature. Sample-path discrepancy need not be uniformly bounded; only its stationary moments are uniformly bounded in time. The result uses known general renewal/coded-shift principles, so the claim of novelty is restricted to the explicit model-specific distributions and covariance/transfer identities. Historical-priority comparisons cannot exclude every unpublished or inaccessible calculation, and no independent audit has been performed.
