For synchronous \(q\)-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation with \(q\ge4\) on a double-star tree, the last possible extinction time is exactly \(q+2\), and a closed inclusion–exclusion formula counts every labelled initial state reaching it. Unlike long free-boundary paths, large branching on both sides preserves a positive limiting probability \(2/q^2\) of attaining the maximal extinction delay.
