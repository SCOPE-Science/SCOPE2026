On the ten-vertex pentagonal prism, cyclic and Greenberg–Hastings three-color dynamics assign identical eventual least periods to every initial coloring; on the equally sized, equally regular Petersen graph, exactly 4800 colorings have period eight for the cyclic rule but period four for Greenberg–Hastings. The exceptional colorings form 40 free orbits of the Petersen graph’s 120-element automorphism group.
