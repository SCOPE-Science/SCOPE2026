# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any height-two poset without isolated elements, the minimal/maximal coordinate vector field has divergence n and constant outward cover-facet flux 1/sqrt(2); the centered field supplies 2nV coordinate-facet area. Thus area/V=n(2+sqrt(2)). Euclidean cover contraction multiplies dimension-(n-1) volume by sqrt(2), and adjacent cover contraction counts extensions, forcing exact EX=1. For zigzag fences, replacing k separated Volterra U/D kernels by identities yields normalized joint contact probabilities; K=UD has leading eigenvalue 4/pi^2, remaining spectrum bounded by 1/9, and <phi,Uphi>=<phi,Dphi>=4/pi^2. For fixed k, distant insertions yield product 1+O_k(9^-h), while O_k(h n^(k-1)) bad tuples have uniform majorant O_k(u^-k); factorial moments tend to 1 and establish Poisson(1). Definitions, alternating parity, first and last edges, and n=2 boundary checked. Independent DP n=2..10 reproduced Euler counts, contact means 1 and trending P(X=0) from 0.2 (n=4) to 0.31858 (n=10), not an asymptotic proof.

## originality

PASS

The exact universal boundary identity is elementary and is not independently credited as new. The final joint asymptotic concerns cover endpoints consecutive in the *extension order*, i.e. adjacency of consecutive poset labels in an alternating permutation. Coons–Sullivant treat swap/h*-statistics and Petersen–Zhuang treat descents/big returns, which are distinct random variables; classical unconditioned succession counts do not imply the distribution after conditioning on alternating order. No stronger result found that supplies the fixed-cover factorial moments and Poisson law.
