The known facial adjacency of the canonical partial permutohedron yields a complete Euclidean edge-length spectrum: a uniformly selected rare insertion edge has a shifted truncated-Poisson length. Although insertion edges form only about \(2/m\) of all edges, when the label surplus \(n-m\) is proportional to dimension they carry a nonzero limiting fraction \(2c/(\sqrt2+2c)\) of total Euclidean edge length, while the polytope's combinatorial type remains fixed.
