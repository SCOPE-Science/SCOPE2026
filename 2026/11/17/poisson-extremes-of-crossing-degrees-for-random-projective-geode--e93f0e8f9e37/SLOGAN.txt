The smallest crossing degrees among independent isotropic shortest geodesic arcs on the round projective plane form a sharp Poisson extreme process for every power-law short-length exponent greater than one, even though crossings are dependent. For Haar-uniform endpoint pairs, the minimum degree grows on the square-root sample-size scale with survival law \(e^{-\pi^4x^2/2}\), and each fixed degree \(k\) has probability asymptotic to \((k+1)\pi^4/n^2\).
