# Scientific review: weighted right-translation expansiveness

## Correctness — PASS

The semigroup formula and its generator follow from characteristics and bounded perturbation of the right shift. The exact minimum modulus is the exponential of the least potential mass over translated intervals. This is superadditive in time, producing the exact lower growth bound. Monotone convergence gives the kinematic criterion. A quantitative lower bound on the generator follows by integrating the semigroup displacement. The critical converse is not asserted by the motivating source: when every fixed-length minimum integral is zero, compactly supported broad bumps on intervals of arbitrarily small potential mass give normalized generator vectors with residual norm tending to zero. This invokes the established necessary approximate-spectrum condition for positive expansiveness. When a fixed interval has positive minimum mass, iteration gives uniform exponential expansion and hence positive expansiveness. All quantifiers cover every complex \(L^p\), \(1\le p<\infty\), and all bounded nonnegative measurable potentials. The two examples are checked by elementary integration. No numerical experiment is used as an infinite-dimensional proof.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk

Huynh, arXiv:2611.01005, Theorems 3.3, 3.6, 4.2, 4.4 and Example 4.6, supplies the abstract framework and constant-weight case but not the all-potential iff statement, the normalized approximate-generator-null interval argument, or the exact nonattained-rate example. Lods--Mokhtar-Kharroubi--Sbihi (arXiv:0805.3654) addresses spectral shapes of advection semigroups, a different target from the Bowen--Walters classification. Phatak--Sholapurkar concerns hyperexpansivity in the polynomial-isometry sense. Lee--Morales defines the expansiveness notion and studies point spectrum and the Lasota equation; accessible abstract and Huynh's description do not imply the variable-potential classification. Broad published-literature and semantic-record checks did not identify coverage; absence of a retrieved equivalent is not a novelty proof. Priority risk persists from older weighted-shift/cocycle theory and incomplete full-text access to Lee--Morales.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete classification for a natural non-surjective semigroup family with arbitrary bounded nonnegative spatially varying potentials, not a single-parameter substitution. It establishes a structural collapse that fails for diagonal semigroups, cleanly separates time-reparametrized and equal-time expansiveness, and computes the sharp lower exponential growth bound including a nonattainment boundary. These distinctions directly serve the motivating problem of detecting expansiveness from operator or generator data.

## Closest sources and scope

Huynh (2026), arXiv:2611.01005, is the closest structural result and is fully accessible as article text. Lods et al. (2008), arXiv:0805.3654, provides broad spectral context. Lee--Morales, DOI:10.1017/etds.2022.20, could not be obtained in full during the literature checks; its accessible abstract was compared. The result is limited to bounded nonnegative scalar weights and finite \(p\); no signed-potential extension is claimed.
