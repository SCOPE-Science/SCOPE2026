For every bounded nonnegative potential on the half-line, a weighted right-translation semigroup on complex \(L^p\), \(1\le p<\infty\), is positively expansive under time reparametrization exactly when it expands uniformly exponentially; the criterion is a positive lower integral over some fixed-length interval, uniform in the starting point. Kinematic expansiveness only requires divergence of the total potential mass, and the optimal exponential growth rate can be a nonattained supremum.
