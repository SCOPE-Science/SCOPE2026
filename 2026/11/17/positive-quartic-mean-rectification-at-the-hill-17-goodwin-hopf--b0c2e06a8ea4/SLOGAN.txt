At the naturally degenerate Hill exponent \(17\), small attracting Goodwin oscillations have no quadratic average-concentration shift, but their period-average terminal concentration exceeds the matching equilibrium by \(60A^4/e_*^3+o(A^4)\), where \(A\) is half their peak-to-trough amplitude. The strictly positive quartic term survives a cancellation between moving-equilibrium curvature and intrinsic fourth-order feedback.
