For a fixed number of uniformly sampled vertices on a circle, the known leading near-regular polygon perimeter and area endpoint probabilities admit explicit new positive relative corrections of order \(\varepsilon\), with a rigorous remainder of order \(\varepsilon^2\). Their correction coefficients grow differently, as \(n^3/(12\pi^3)\) for perimeter and \(n^3/(24\pi^3)\) for area, distinguishing two geometric rare-event laws beyond their previously known leading terms.
