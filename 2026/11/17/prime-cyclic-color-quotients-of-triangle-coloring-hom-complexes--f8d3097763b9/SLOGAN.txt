For every odd prime \(p\ge5\), cyclically relabeling the target colors in \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_3,K_p)\) produces an orbit space with \(\mathbb Z/p\) integral homology in every odd degree below \(p-3\), free top homology of rank \((2^p-p-2)/p\), and fundamental group \(C_p\). Its exact mod-\(p\) cup-length is \((p-3)/2\), giving unbounded multiplicative cohomological complexity despite the highly connected sphere-wedge covering spaces.
