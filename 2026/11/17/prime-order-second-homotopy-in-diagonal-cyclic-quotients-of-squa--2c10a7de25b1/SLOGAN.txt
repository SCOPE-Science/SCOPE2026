For every odd prime \(p\ge5\), the ordinary quotient of the square chessboard complex by simultaneous cyclic row-and-column shifts is simply connected, yet its second homotopy group contains \((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^{p-1}\). It is exactly that group for \(p\ge7\), while at \(p=5\) an additional intrinsic \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) summand survives.
