The geometric quotient of the simplicial associahedral sphere of a regular prime-sided polygon by all vertex rotations is exactly the weighted lens space \(L(p;2,3,\ldots,(p-1)/2)\), not merely a rational homology sphere. For every odd prime \(p\ge7\), its integral homology contains \(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\) in every odd degree below the top degree, although the covering complex is a torsion-free sphere.
