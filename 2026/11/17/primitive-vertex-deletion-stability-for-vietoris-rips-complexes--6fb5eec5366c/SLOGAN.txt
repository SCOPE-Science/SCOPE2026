Deleting one point or one line from the incidence graph of any finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\) reduces its radius-two Rips homotopy type from a wedge of \(q^3\) two-spheres to a wedge of \(q^3-q\) two-spheres. The natural integral second-homology map is a primitive injection with cokernel \(\mathbb Z^q\), even though graph distances are recomputed after deletion.
