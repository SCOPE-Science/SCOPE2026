For the dominance complex of \(K_{k,k}\), even shore permutations preserve a three-sphere-wedge homotopy type, but adjoining shore exchange produces \(\Sigma^{k-1}\mathbb{RP}^{k-2}\vee S^{k-1}\). This generates integral two-torsion for every \(k\ge4\), while adjoining odd permutations as well makes the full symmetry quotient contractible.
