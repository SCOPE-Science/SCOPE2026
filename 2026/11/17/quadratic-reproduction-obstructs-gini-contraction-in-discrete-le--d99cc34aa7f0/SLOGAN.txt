For equally spaced observations, unweighted polynomial least-squares fitting contracts complete-pair Gini variation only in the constant, affine, and full-interpolation cases: every strictly intermediate degree from two onward amplifies the variation of a single endpoint impulse. Any orthogonal projector reproducing constants, linear, and quadratic data but excluding that impulse has the same obstruction, and the quadratic impulse amplification tends to \(189/125\).
