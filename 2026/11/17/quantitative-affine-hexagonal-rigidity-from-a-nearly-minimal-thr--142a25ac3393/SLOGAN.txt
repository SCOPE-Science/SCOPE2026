If a noncollinear zero-sum triple in a real normed space nearly attains the universal three-vector separation threshold \(3/2\), its entire two-dimensional unit ball is linearly close to an affine-regular hexagon. An explicit Banach–Mazur estimate \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}\le1+12(R-3/2)\) holds whenever \(R-3/2\le1/100\).
