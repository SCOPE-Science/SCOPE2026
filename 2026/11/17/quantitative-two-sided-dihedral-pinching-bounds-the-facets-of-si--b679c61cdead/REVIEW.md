# Mathematical review

**Correctness: PASS.** Three incident exterior normals form each vertex normal triangle, with side lengths complementary to dihedral angles. Four elementary semiperimeter bounds combined with L'Huilier's exact identity yield a strictly positive pointwise area bound. The normal cones partition the sphere, and simple-polytope Euler incidence converts total area into the facet bound. The asymptotic local obstruction is established by an explicit nearly degenerate spherical triangle. Deterministic numerical checks are supplementary, not proof.

It does not require a globally optimal facet bound to be useful.
