Approximating the standard permutahedron by the two-level multipermutahedron formed by averaging its lowest \(k\) and highest \(n-k\) labels produces a completely explicit Euclidean Hausdorff gap and a farthest-point set consisting of exactly \(n!/m!\) disjoint permutahedral faces. At block density \(\beta=1/4\), the limiting normalized squared gap is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\), while farthest-face dimension saturates at half the ambient dimension.
