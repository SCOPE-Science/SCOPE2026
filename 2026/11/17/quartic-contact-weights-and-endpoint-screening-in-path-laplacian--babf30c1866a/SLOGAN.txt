For the canonical Euclidean Laplacian simplex of an \(n\)-vertex path, the smallest enclosing ball has squared radius \(6-6/\binom{n+2}{5}\) for every \(n\ge4\). Its center has a quadratic coordinate profile generated by positive quartic contact weights; the two endpoint vertices lie strictly inside the ball precisely when \(n\ge5\), and the squared-radius deficit decays as \(720n^{-5}\).
