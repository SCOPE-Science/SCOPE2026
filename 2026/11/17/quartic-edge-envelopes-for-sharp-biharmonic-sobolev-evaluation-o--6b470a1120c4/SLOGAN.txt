The sharp mean-zero Kirchhoff \(H^2\)-to-\(L^\infty\) point-evaluation constant on every finite metric graph is the maximum of finitely many explicit edgewise quartics, so all norming points are obtained from cubic stationary equations and possible flat edges. Each quartic's leading coefficient is \(\ell_e^2(\ell_e-r_{ab}-\ell_e^2/T)/3\), whose normalized sum equals the cycle rank minus one.
