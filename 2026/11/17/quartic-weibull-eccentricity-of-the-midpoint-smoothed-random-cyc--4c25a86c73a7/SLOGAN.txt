For a polygon with independently uniform points on a circle, repeated midpoint smoothing produces a principal-harmonic ellipse whose eccentricity has the sharp \(n^{-1/4}\) scale: \(\Pr(n^{1/4}e_n\le x)\to1-\exp(-x^4/4)\). Its major-axis direction is exactly uniform and independent of its semiaxes for every finite vertex count.
