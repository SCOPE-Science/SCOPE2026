# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For A,B independent boundary-arclength-uniform circle points and fixed C=(r,0), 0<=r<=1, the three obtuseness events are mutually exclusive up to zero-probability right angles. Conditional angular arc geometry at A gives q_A=[Li_2(r)-Li_2(-r)]/pi² from the odd Fourier series of arg(1-r exp(-iα)). For obtuse C, push forward uniform boundary angle to the viewing direction φ: f_r(φ)=[1-h_r′(φ)]/(2pi), h_r=arcsin(r sinφ). Fourier/indicator integration of the angular separation interval (pi/2,3pi/2) gives q_C=1/2-2J(r)/pi², J=∫_0^{pi/2} r cos t arcsin(r cos t)/sqrt(1-r² sin²t)dt. Thus p=1-2q_A-q_C=1/2-2[Li_2(r)-Li_2(-r)]/pi²+2J/pi². Monotone convergence/dominated convergence establishes p(1)=1/4, p(0)=1/2. Taylor J=pi r²/4+pi r⁴/16+O(r⁶) and odd dilog=2r+2r³/9+O(r⁵) give p(r)=1/2-4r/pi²+r²/(2pi)-4r³/(9pi²)+r⁴/(8pi)+O(r⁵): the nonzero radial linear term proves a nondifferentiable spatial center cusp. Independently evaluated direct 600-by-600 angle inequalities against the formula at r=0,.2,.5,.9 with absolute discrepancies <0.002. No unwarranted claim of global monotonicity of p(r) is made.

## originality

PASS

PASS, qualified: a fixed interior vertex plus two iid boundary-uniform vertices is a different sampling model from all-boundary, all-area-uniform, or all-Gaussian triangles. Neither inspected Edelman–Strang shape-space theory nor Hall all-volume sampling supplies the full radial dilogarithm+J law or cusp. The r=1 boundary value 1/4 is classical and credited.
