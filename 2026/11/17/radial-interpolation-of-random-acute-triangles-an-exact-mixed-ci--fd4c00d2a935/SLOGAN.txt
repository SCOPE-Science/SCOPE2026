For two independent uniform boundary points of a circle and one fixed interior point at radius \(r\), the acute-triangle probability admits an exact dilogarithm–single-integral formula interpolating from \(1/2\) to \(1/4\). Its centered radial expansion starts \(1/2-4r/\pi^2+r^2/(2\pi)\), giving a genuine spatial cusp and an exact redistribution of the probability of obtuseness among the three vertices.
