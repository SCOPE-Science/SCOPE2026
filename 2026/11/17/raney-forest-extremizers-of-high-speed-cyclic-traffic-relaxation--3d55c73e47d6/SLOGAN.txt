For deterministic high-speed cyclic traffic with speed cap \(v\ge2\) and at least \(v\) vacancies per car, the maximum time until all cars travel at full speed is exactly \(m-1\) steps and the number of maximally slow indexed states is \(\frac{n}{m}\binom{(v+1)m-2}{m-1}\). Unique last-delayed cars correspond to ordered full-ary forests, and maximal delay occurs with limiting probability \(\left(v/(v+1)\right)^{h+1}\) at fixed surplus \(h\).
