For every finite matrix size and rank budget, the sharp weak-Schatten amplification of diagonal pinching is a generalized harmonic sum with a transition at \(p=1\): below it optimal diagonals spread across all coordinates, above it they occupy exactly the rank budget, and at the endpoint every intermediate support size is possible. All extremizers are diagonal-unitary multiples of positive matrices with a prescribed harmonic singular spectrum and flat nonzero diagonal.
