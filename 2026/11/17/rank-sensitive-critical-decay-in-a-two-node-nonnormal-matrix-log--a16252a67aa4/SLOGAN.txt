At the neutral Jordan boundary of a two-node matrix logistic map, every positive-semidefinite state below the identity decays to zero, but the leading population amplitude is \(4/n\), \(3/n\), or \(1/n\) according to initial rank and alignment. Full-rank states have a second eigenmode of order \(n^{-3}\), while arbitrarily small states may first amplify before becoming extinct.
