A canonical expansive rank-two Hardy-shift extension has the complete Nevanlinna–Pick property and is logarithmically concave exactly when its critical off-diagonal coupling \(b\) vanishes. Every arbitrarily small nonzero coupling preserves expansive defect rank two but creates the sharp quadratic Pick obstruction \(-b^2/a^4\) and logarithmic-concavity obstruction \(-a^6b^2\).
