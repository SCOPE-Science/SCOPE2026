For the canonical stack-sorting simplex of \(23\cdots n1\), exactly \(2/n\) of its ridges are nonright, and the uniform exceptional-ridge deficit from a right angle, multiplied by \(n\), converges to a Pareto law of index one. Its conditional moments diverge, while the full random-ridge deficit has mean \((2\log n+O(1))/n^2\) and variance asymptotic to \(2C_2/n^2\), with an explicit convergent positive series for \(C_2\).
