For the integrable biquadratic nontwist map, the full negative-rotation band has an exact Liouville-area frequency law that is invariant under \(\varepsilon\leftrightarrow1/\varepsilon\), increases strictly in stochastic order toward the shearless collision, and tends after normalization to \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,1/2)\). Its integrated reverse angular flux vanishes cubically, and reciprocal parameter rescaling conjugates even the perturbed map.
