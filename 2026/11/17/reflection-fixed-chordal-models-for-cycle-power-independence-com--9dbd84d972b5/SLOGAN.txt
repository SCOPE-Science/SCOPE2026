Every reflection-containing subgroup fixes a geometric subspace of a cycle-power independence complex that is PL-homeomorphic to the independence complex of an explicit induced subgraph of a path power. The resulting fixed spaces are empty, contractible, or torsion-free sphere wedges, with exact sphere counts from a chordal-graph recurrence; a first small mixed-dimensional example is \(S^0\vee S^1\) for an edge-axis reflection of \(C_{22}^3\).
