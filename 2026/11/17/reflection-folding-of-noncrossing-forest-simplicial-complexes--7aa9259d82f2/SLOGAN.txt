For every even convex polygon, reflection through opposite side midpoints folds the **geometric fixed-point space** of its noncrossing-forest complex PL-homeomorphically onto the ordinary noncrossing-forest complex of a polygon with half as many sides plus one: \(|\mathcal N_{2m}|^{\sigma}\cong_{\mathrm{PL}}|\mathcal N_{m+1}|\). The isomorphism is explicit on edge orbits and yields exact face counts in every dimension.
