# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The four explicit reflected orbit types A, B_j, C_j, D_ij exhaust precisely the individual edge orbits with neither crossing nor cycle; every excluded orbit consists of internally crossing mirror chords. Alternating-endpoints comparison between lifted orbit pairs produces exactly the crossing relation in the (m+1)-gon. For forests, deleting hub vertex 1 from the small graph yields G0; its lifted graph is two copies of G0 joined by one rung per hub edge, hence has a cycle iff G0 cycles or two rungs connect one G0 component. This is equivalent to the small graph having a cycle, proving every-face compatibility and a simplicial isomorphism, then barycentric PL equivalence. The Catalan/Narayana-type face formula is correctly attributed to existing ordinary noncrossing-forest enumerations. Independent exhaustive direct checks of 1,096 edge subsets for 2<=m<=4 verified crossings, lifted forest condition and face equivalence; not a substitute for universal proof.

## originality

PASS

Kluge’s cyclic-sieving work concerns rotations of individual noncrossing forests and gives the ordinary component-count formula, not a geometric fixed locus for the edge-midpoint reflection on the entire forest simplicial complex. Jonsson provides the standard nonequivariant noncrossing-forest complex. The new structural folding is an all-m simplicial isomorphism, whereas its exact face count is a direct application of an older counting formula. Jonsson full section remains inaccessible and is a recorded priority risk, not grounds for fabricating noncoverage.
