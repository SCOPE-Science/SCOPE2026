Reversing a path induces an ordinary independence-complex quotient with a complete homotopy classification by joins \(S^{a-1}*\mathbb{RP}^{b-1}\), where \((a,b)\) follows a six-periodic rank formula. The first integral two-torsion occurs at fourteen vertices: \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{14})/C_2\simeq\Sigma^2\mathbb{RP}^2\), and larger paths produce unbounded numbers of two-torsion homology degrees.
