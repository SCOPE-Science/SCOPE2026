Across every rankwise chain-order interpolation of an ordinal sum of antichains, the Euclidean surface-to-volume ratio depends only on dimension, rank count, and the interpolation index—not on rank populations. Its exact profile is \(n[k+1+\sqrt{k+1}+(r-k-1)\sqrt2]\) before the pure-chain endpoint, and it decreases strictly through every nontrivial interior step even though the known face counts increase.
