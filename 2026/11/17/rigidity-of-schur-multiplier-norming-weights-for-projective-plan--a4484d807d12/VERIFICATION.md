The proof checks the point-line incidence Gram identity, the nuclear/Frobenius equality condition (without assuming positive weights), and the unique-line/off-diagonal comparison. The checker reproduces the incidence Gram identities for finite-field examples of orders two and three. These examples are diagnostic and not an exhaustive verification of the theorem's all-projective-planes quantifier.
