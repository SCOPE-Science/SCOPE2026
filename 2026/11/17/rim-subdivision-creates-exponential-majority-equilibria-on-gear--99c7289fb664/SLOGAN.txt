Subdividing every rim edge of a wheel creates exponentially many binary majority equilibria: for the \((2n+1)\)-vertex gear graph the exact fixed-state count is a constrained cyclic-run sum and grows as \(2\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\approx1.754877666\) solves \(\lambda^3-2\lambda^2+\lambda-1=0\). Every equilibrium corresponds uniquely to a rim word with no isolated zeros and at most half its entries opposite the hub.
