For every number of petals and every robustness threshold \(k\ge2\), simultaneously contracting the middle edge of each pentagon in a bouquet induces a homotopy equivalence onto the corresponding quadrilateral bouquet. Hence the pentagonal bouquet's robust clique complex is exactly \(\bigvee^{\binom{m}{k-1}}S^{2k-3}\), providing the all-threshold classification in the new nonbipartite range \(k\ge4\).
