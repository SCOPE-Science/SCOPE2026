On every three-row periodic Conway Life torus whose circumference is a positive multiple of three, adding a fourth row strictly reduces the number of fixed configurations. The complete height-four fixed-state count is an exact \(127\)-state transfer trace and grows as \(\lambda^n\) with \(2.55371542<\lambda<2.55371544\), while a height-three resonance supplies at least \(3^{n+1}\) fixed states.
