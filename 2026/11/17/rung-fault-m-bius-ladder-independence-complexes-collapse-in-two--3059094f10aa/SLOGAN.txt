For \(n\ge3\) with \(n\equiv0\) or \(3\pmod4\), deleting any single antipodal rung from the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) gives a collapsible independence complex. An explicit matching collapses it to a join of an edge and a cross-polytope boundary, completing the two contractible congruence classes of the rung-fault problem.
