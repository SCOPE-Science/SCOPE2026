The norm spectrum generated from finite path Schur projections by finite tensor products and bounded countable block sums is exactly the countable scattered set \(\bigcup_{m\ge0}(4/\pi)^m\mathcal P\), with derived sets \((4/\pi)^m\mathcal S\). Every algebraic threshold, including \(2\), is isolated from the other norms of this restricted family—even though it contains the near-\(2\) product \(\sigma_5\sigma_9\sigma_{122}\); the result does not settle accumulation in the unrestricted Schur spectrum.
