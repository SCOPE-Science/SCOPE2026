# Verification and limits

The argument is analytic; no exhaustive finite computational search was used. Direct checks: (i) the exact finite path norm formula in the 2014 primary publication; (ii) tensor multiplicativity in the 2026 primary publication; (iii) the block-diagonal supremum identity by a two-sided operator-norm argument; (iv) strict convergence of the scalar generators and a uniform factor-count cutoff in bounded intervals; (v) sequential classification of all finite products; (vi) precise accumulation-point calculations; (vii) separation of finite algebraic products from positive powers of the transcendental limit \(4/\pi\).

The separation from \(2\) has no computed positive radius. The model excludes all masks not expressible as bounded block-diagonal sums of finite tensor products of the diagonal-plus-superdiagonal finite path masks. Its findings must not be construed as an answer to the open question about **all** idempotent Schur multipliers. An independent audit has not been performed.
