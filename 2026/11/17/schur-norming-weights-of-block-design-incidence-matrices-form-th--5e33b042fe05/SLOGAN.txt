For every finite \(2\)-design incidence mask, equality in the optimal rank-one Schur trace-dual bound forces uniform point weights, while the complete family of block weights is exactly the nonnegative affine trade polytope \(\mathbf1/b+\ker Q\). Its dimension is the dimension of the pair–block trade space: Steiner designs are rigid, but the complete triple design on six points has a five-dimensional norming family.
