# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each n>=2, Amanbayeva--Wang vertex layers are permutations of u_(n,k)=(1 repeated k,k+1,...,n). Squared Euclidean distance between fixed layer profiles is maximized by reverse sorting via rearrangement; algebra gives M_(k,l)=2F(n)-F(k)-F(l)-2J_(k,l), with J=0 for k+l>=n and positive cross terms otherwise, F(t)=(t-1)t(2t-1)/6. Monotonicity toward k+l=n reduces near-maximal candidates to layer-sum slices n-1,n,n+1. Discrete convexity F(t+1)-F(t)=t² gives the best on each slice and compares to known D_n; it yields gap Delta_n=n-1 for even n, min((n-1)^2/4,2(n-1)) for odd n. Balanced diameter-layer arrangements with a high-high collision lose >=2m², strictly more than Delta_n, excluding second-shell intralayer perturbations. Equality demands complementary high supports at k+l=n, one low-low coordinate at n+1, or a single smallest-high overlap at n-1. Support choices and ordering count exactly n! unordered pairs per distinct layer pair and n!/2 for equal layers, giving the n=4 and n=9 doubling. Independent exhaustive integer-distance enumeration across all 3,10,41,206,1237 vertices for n=2..6 gave top two squared distances [2,1],[9,8],[26,23],[54,50],[100,95], with second-shell pair counts 2,6,48,120,720 matching formula. The proof, not these finite cases, establishes all n including n=9 exceptional tie.

## originality

PASS

The classical parking-function vertex theorem, equivalence to a translated partial permutohedron, and largest Euclidean diameter with support classification are known and explicitly credited. The audited result computes the next distinct squared vertex-chord shell, its parity-dependent gap, exact equality geometry, and exceptional multiplicities n=4,9; neither the prior diameter theorem nor the primary f-vector/volume papers states or implies that second-shell census. The summary of older generalized parking function polytope literature was checked; an unindexed deeper shell under win-vector or partial-permutohedron terminology remains a priority risk.

## value

PASS

The second Euclidean vertex-chord shell is the natural next exact geometric invariant after the known diameter of a highly symmetric polytope. Its gap is parity-sensitive, and the multiplicity doubles at two exceptional dimensions from different equality mechanisms. The exhaustive classification of near-diametral pairs is a self-contained new geometric fact that is not merely a restatement of the diameter and can serve future metric-statistical or distance-spectrum studies.
