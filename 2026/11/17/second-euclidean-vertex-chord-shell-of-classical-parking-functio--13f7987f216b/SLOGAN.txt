Among the Euclidean distances between distinct **vertices** of the classical parking-function polytope, the gap between the largest and second-largest **squared** distances is \(n-1\) in even dimension and \(\min\{(n-1)^2/4,2(n-1)\}\) in odd dimension. Exactly \(n!\) unordered vertex pairs attain the second shell, except that the count doubles at dimensions \(4\) and \(9\).
