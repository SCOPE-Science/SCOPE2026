For four independent uniform-area points in a disk with an infinitesimal concentric hole of area fraction \(t\), the probability of convex position is \(1-35/(12\pi^2)+(1-19/(3\pi^2))t+(1-95/(6\pi^2))t^2+o(t^2)\). The previously known positive linear response conceals a strictly negative quadratic correction, obtained by resolving configurations with two points collapsing into the puncture.
