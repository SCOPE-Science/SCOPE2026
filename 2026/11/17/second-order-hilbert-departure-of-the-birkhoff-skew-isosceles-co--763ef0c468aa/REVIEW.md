# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Signed-coordinate symmetry and smooth Birkhoff orthogonality reduce the problem to a compact one-parameter optimization for the real \(\ell_p^2\) plane. The first-derivative profile has a unique nondegenerate maximizer at \(r_0=e^{-u}\), where \(u\tanh u=1\). The explicit second derivative is checked symbolically and numerically; uniform convergence and the implicit-function theorem establish the global local uniqueness and the \(O(|p-2|^3)\) remainder. Finite tests support arithmetic, not the infinite uniqueness conclusion.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. Qi et al. (arXiv:2602.13974v1) define the skew Birkhoff constant and prove broader general bounds but do not state this local exponent-two curvature in the full text inspected. Published findings on a *fixed-exponent small-skew* law and on a *different* near-Hilbert C-symmetry defect are close but have different functional definitions or perturbation variables. The related leading slope is credited; the new claim rests on the signed quadratic coefficient and unique optimizer drift. Bounded searches did not demonstrate broader implication; full texts of several indexed findings and older unindexed work remain literature risks.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The natural transition of \(\ell_p^2\) through Hilbert geometry exhibits a signed quadratic asymmetry invisible to a first-order absolute cusp. The explicit coefficient, its negative sign, a unique tangent-direction orbit and near-extremizer localization quantify this structural effect in a way that permits further comparison of geometric Banach-space constants.

## Limitations
Local near \(p=2\), real dimension two and skew parameter one. No all-exponent or general-dimensional classification is claimed. Symbolic and finite numerical checks do not constitute independent validation; no external mathematical audit has occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
