For the Birkhoff skew-isosceles discrepancy of the real plane \(\ell_p^2\), the departure from Hilbert geometry has an exact asymmetric second-order cusp: \(D(p)=C|p-2|+K(p-2)|p-2|+O(|p-2|^3)\), with \(K=-0.2630288808938\ldots\). Near exponent two every extremizer has, up to coordinate symmetries, a unique tangent-direction ratio that drifts smoothly from \(e^{-u}\), where \(u\tanh u=1\).
