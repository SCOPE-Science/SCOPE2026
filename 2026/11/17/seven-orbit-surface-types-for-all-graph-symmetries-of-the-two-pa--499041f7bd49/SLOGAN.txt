All 112 subgroups of the natural 72-element symmetry group of the unordered two-particle discretized configuration surface of \(K_{3,3}\) yield exactly seven underlying quotient surface types, in 26 subgroup conjugacy classes. The full symmetry quotient is a disk, but the diagonal threefold rotation quotient is the closed nonorientable genus-three surface, while a rotation confined to one bipartition class gives a projective plane.
