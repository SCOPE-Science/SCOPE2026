# Finite-certificate verification

`verify.py` uses the Python standard library to reconstruct the graph, its nine edges, the unordered two-particle cubical complex, and its barycentric order complex. It checks the exact nonempty cell counts (69, 216, 144), generates the 72-element full graph automorphism group, enumerates all subgroups by closure, and verifies the quotient links and orientations for all 112 cases. The program checks the complete 26-conjugacy-class count and the exact seven surface-type multiplicities.

`orbit_census.json` (regenerated by `python3 create_census.py`) lists all 112 subgroups as subsets of the deterministic indexed permutation group, with conjugacy representatives, quotient cell counts, boundary-edge counts and inferred surface types. `verification_output.txt` is a recorded run of `verify.py`; the final success marker is `K33_TWO_CONFIGURATION_ALL_SUBGROUP_SURFACES_PASS`. These exhaustive finite checks establish the claimed census when combined with the proof that closure finds every subgroup and the classification theorem for compact connected surfaces.

Limits: This verifies the specified discretized model and underlying orbit surfaces. It does not certify orbifold data, continuous-space equivariant deformation retractions, or literature uniqueness. No external/independent audit was performed.
