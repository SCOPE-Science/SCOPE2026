Adding a universal hub to a complete bipartite network changes three-colour threshold-two cyclic dynamics dramatically: on every \(K_{1,m,n}\), periodicity begins by time \(7\), and the only possible eventual least periods are \(1,3,12\). Genuine twelve-cycles appear exactly when both peripheral parts have at least four vertices, and an exact labelled three-cycle basin formula exhibits a reentrant small-part phenomenon.
