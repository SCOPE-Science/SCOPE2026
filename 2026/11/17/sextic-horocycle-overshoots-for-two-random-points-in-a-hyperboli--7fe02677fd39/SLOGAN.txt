For two independent area-uniform points in a fixed truncated hyperbolic cusp, a shortest geodesic rises above a high horocycle while both endpoints stay below it with probability \(w^4/(1920H^6)+w^6/(17920H^8)+O_w(H^{-10})\). This rare excursion recovers the cusp width \(w\), and its horizontal separation and endpoint deficits have a universal independent beta–Dirichlet marked limit in total variation.
