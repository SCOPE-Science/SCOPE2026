For three uniform-eccentric-anomaly points on an ellipse of semiaxes \(1,\varepsilon\), the chance that every angle is below any fixed \(\beta\in(\pi/2,\pi)\) is asymptotic to \(6(-\cot\beta)\varepsilon/\pi\). Conditioned on this rare event, one pair of vertices has an asymptotically uniform reflected-anomaly mismatch, and its two angles split a straight angle; at \(\beta=2\pi/3\), the law governs the appearance of a genuine Steiner junction.
