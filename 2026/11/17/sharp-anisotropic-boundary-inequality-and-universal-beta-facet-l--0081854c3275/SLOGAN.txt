For even-cycle edge polytopes stretched independently on their two bipartition colors, every facet has an exact Euclidean area, and at fixed volume the balanced shape uniquely minimizes surface area in dimensions at least four. Under **arbitrarily large** color anisotropy, the surface-area-weighted cyclic facet offsets still converge to \(\operatorname{Beta}(3/2,3/2)\), with an explicit sharp global surface penalty.
