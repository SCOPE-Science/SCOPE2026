For two-frequency analytic strictly convex ovals, every joint law of \(m\) arclength-sampled *relative* outward normal angles is blind to the shape phase until exactly \(m=p+q\), when one Fourier moment reveals it. Consequently, noncongruent ovals with identical perimeter, area and even directional width can be indistinguishable by arbitrarily high but fixed orders of such normal-angle observations.
