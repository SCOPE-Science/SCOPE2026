For the standard Euclidean \(2\times q\) Monge polytope, every coordinate-facet area is an exact binomial tail, while the aggregate comparison-facet area has an exact cubic formula. The comparison facets carry fraction \(1-4/q+O(q^{-2})\) of the boundary area, with a triangular individual area profile, even as coordinate facets exhibit a Gaussian boundary-layer transition.
