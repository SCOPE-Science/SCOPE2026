Quadratic least-squares regression on equally spaced real samples has an exact, attained rational finite-grid Lipschitz coefficient in adjacent total variation. These coefficients converge to the algebraic constant \(1.63318209583481\ldots\), and every asymptotically worst jump localizes at the two interior fractions \(t_*\) and \(1-t_*\), where \(t_*=\frac12-\frac{\sqrt3}{30}\sqrt{12+\sqrt{69}}\).
