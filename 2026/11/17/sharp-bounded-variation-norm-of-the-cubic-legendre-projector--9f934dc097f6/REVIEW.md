# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact Legendre antiderivative kernel reduces the BV operator norm to an L1 integral of an explicit quadratic jump response. The midpoint jump supplies the lower bound. For every possible jump, a rational Bernstein positivity identity proves the upper bound near the midpoint; a rigorous analytic derivative modulus plus 300 exact rational root-enclosure integrals proves it elsewhere. All critical identities and numerical covering assertions replay successfully with the embedded standard-library checker. The finite arithmetic constitutes an exact interval proof of a continuous inequality, not a finite sample of data signals.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Exact amplification of total variation is a natural edge-stability invariant for spectral truncation. The cubic projector is the first Legendre truncation whose step-response derivative is quadratic and can change sign twice, so neither positivity nor a one-root shortcut establishes the norm. The sharp coefficient and midpoint extremizer distinguish its stability behavior from the adjacent quadratic projection, providing a reproducible benchmark for spectral reconstruction.
