The two-choice centered box-averaging envelope on the integer line is nonexpansive in the bounded-variation distance only at radius one: at every radius \(r\ge1\), its exact, attained two-input variation gain is \(1+4\lfloor r^2/4\rfloor/(2r+1)\). The gain begins at \(9/5\) for radius two and grows linearly with the window radius, despite averaging being present in one of the two choices.
