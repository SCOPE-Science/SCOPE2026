For a regular polygonal fan of directions testing a positive radial disk distribution, the depth of an independent random point has a universal, **exactly linear** lower-tail CDF until the first overlap of neighboring circular caps; the sharp threshold depends on the radial law but the slope equals the number of polygon sides. For the uniform disk, the entire CDF, its first non-smooth derivative transition, and the sharp \(\pi^2/(24m^2)\) average-depth bias are explicit.
