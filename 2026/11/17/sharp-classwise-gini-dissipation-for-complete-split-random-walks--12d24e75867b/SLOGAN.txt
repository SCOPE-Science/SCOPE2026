On every unweighted complete-split graph, one loopless random-walk step satisfies a sharp two-class Gini dissipation inequality, and every positive power has exact Gini operator norm \((s/(c+s-1))^m\). The only nonconstant sharp inputs for \(s\ge2\) are the signals constant on each class, so adding a second clique vertex makes every step strictly Gini-contracting.
