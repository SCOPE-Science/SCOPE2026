For the minimal nonpolynomial quadratic midpoint-knot interpolant with nodes \(0,\varepsilon,1,R\), the sharp uniform amplification diverges as \(C(R)/\varepsilon\), where \(C(R)=\max\{(3R-1)/(2(4R-1)),(R-1)^2/(2(3R-1))\}\). The worst instability moves discontinuously from the first spline piece to the second exactly at \(R=2+\sqrt3\), even though the scheme retains its smooth-data cubic convergence theory.
