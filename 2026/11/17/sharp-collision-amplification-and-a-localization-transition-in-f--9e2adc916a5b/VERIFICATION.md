The proof checks the six-by-six coefficient determinant, its regularized nonzero confluent limit, the explicit two-piece limit function, and the exact comparison of its two competing extrema. The result holds for each fixed \(R>1\), uniformly over all nodal vectors of maximum norm at most one. The convergence of operators, rather than finite samples, proves the asymptotic constant and localization.

`checker.py` is a supplemental exact-rational and finite-mesh diagnostic and requires only Python's standard library. Its checks do not establish the infinite-parameter theorem. Independent audit has not been performed. Priority risk: detailed pages of Powell's 1981 text were inaccessible.
