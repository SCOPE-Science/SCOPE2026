PASS with publication-priority qualification. Zhang–Dou (2022) addresses fixed points of general completely positive maps, not decay rates for this Bergman channel. Louhichi–Olofsson (2008) establishes weighted Bergman Toeplitz identities, but accessible evidence did not identify the present quantitative relaxation law. Searches for exact decay eigenvalues, prefactors, compact-ideal strong stability and nonuniformity found no directly covering statement. Li–Gao–Zhao–Ma (2024) investigate spectra of general completely positive maps on compact ideals, but only the abstract and opening page were accessible and a general implication cannot be ruled out; their available statements neither mention the Panja channel nor determine its moment-dependent rank-one profiles. An equivalent analysis in operator-channel or weighted-shift terminology cannot be excluded solely from searches.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The channel is a newly introduced operator-theoretic model in which determining fixed points is strictly weaker than understanding long-time dynamics. The result gives a complete, sharp relaxation profile for every monomial rank-one observable, in the full radial-weight setting, together with a genuine strong-versus-uniform stability dichotomy on the natural compact ideal. The beta-family constants include the unexpected \((p+1)^{p+1}\) exact prefactor at unit weight, making the sharp statement quantitatively informative rather than a qualitative restatement of the absence of compact fixed points. The scope is limited to this channel, and the rank-one rate keeps the degree fixed.
