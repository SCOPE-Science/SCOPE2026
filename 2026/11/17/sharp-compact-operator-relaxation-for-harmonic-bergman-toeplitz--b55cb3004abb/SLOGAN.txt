For every admissible radial analytic Bergman weight, the harmonic-Toeplitz completely positive map erases each compact operator in norm but its iterates never contract the compact ideal uniformly. Every monomial rank-one projection has an exact, moment-dependent exponential relaxation rate and prefactor; for the ordinary Bergman space the sharp rate is \(p/[2(p+1)]\) and the prefactor is \((p+1)^{p+1}\) at degree \(p\ge1\).
