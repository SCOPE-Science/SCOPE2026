For the classical one-step two-urn Ehrenfest chain, the exact unweighted complete-pair Gini operator norm is \(1\) with at most two balls and \(1+2(n-2)/n^2\) with \(n\ge3\). The first amplification occurs at three balls, the largest coefficient is \(5/4\) at four balls, and binomial-equilibrium-weighted Gini variation remains contractive.
