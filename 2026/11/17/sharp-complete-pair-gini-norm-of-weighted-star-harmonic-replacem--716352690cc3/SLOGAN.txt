For conductance-weighted harmonic replacement of the hub of an \(m\)-leaf star, the sharp complete-pair Gini norm is \(1+(m-2)\max_i p_i/m\). Uniform conductances uniquely minimize amplification for \(m\ge3\): its family-wide maximum is \(9/8\), while arbitrarily unbalanced conductances can approach amplification two as the leaf count grows.
