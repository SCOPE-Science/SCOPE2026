For \(0<\alpha\le1\), the Schur product of the complete-Pick kernels \((1-z\overline w)^{-\alpha}\) and \((1-\tau(z\overline w)^m)^{-1}\), \(m\ge2\), remains complete Pick exactly up to the sharp threshold \(\tau=(1-\alpha)(2-\alpha)\cdots(m-\alpha)/(m+1)!\). Its threshold decays as \(m^{-1-\alpha}\), and the product can be complete Pick even when its multiplication shift is not concave.
