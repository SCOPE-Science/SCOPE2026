For a weighted Voronoi cell with one central site and \(n\ge3\) equal-weight competitor sites on a regular ring, the whole-plane central cell splits into a bounded island and an unbounded component exactly for \(1<\lambda<\csc(\pi/n)\). The bounded island has an elementary closed area formula and a strictly positive \((\csc(\pi/n)-\lambda)^{3/2}\) cusp when the components join.
