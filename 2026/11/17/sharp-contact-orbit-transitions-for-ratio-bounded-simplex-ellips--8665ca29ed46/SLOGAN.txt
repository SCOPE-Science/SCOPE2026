As the common coordinate-ratio bound \(c>1\) grows, the John and Löwner ellipsoids of a regular simplex's central polytope remain concentric balls, but the enclosing sphere changes its contact-vertex class at the complete sequence \(\sqrt{(m-k)(m-k-1)/(k(k+1))}\). The radius ratio grows strictly from \(\sqrt{2\lfloor m/2\rfloor\lceil m/2\rceil/m}\) to \(m-1\), with exact adjacent-orbit ties at every transition.
