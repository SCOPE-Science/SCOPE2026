For real free \(p\)-convex Banach lattices over \(\ell_1^n\), the canonical comparison to convexity parameter \(q>p\) has exact inverse norm \(n^{1/p-1/q}\), attained by the maximum of the coordinate generators. For infinite \(\ell_1\), these sharp finite-support witnesses force the cross-parameter map to have dense, nonclosed proper range for every \(p<q\), even below the Hilbert exponent.
