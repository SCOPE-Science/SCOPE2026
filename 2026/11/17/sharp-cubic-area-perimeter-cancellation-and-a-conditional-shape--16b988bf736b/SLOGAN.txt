For a fixed number of independently uniform circle vertices, the quadratic area and perimeter losses near the regular cyclic polygon cancel exactly at slope \(2\cos(\pi/m)\), leaving a signed cubic residual with an optimal sharp stability constant. Conditional on an exceptionally small perimeter loss, the residual has an explicit non-Gaussian, symmetric cubic-ball limit and a closed-form variance, distinguishing two geometrically correlated rare-event observables.
