For two unit regular polygons in orthogonal planes of \(\mathbb R^4\), the full spherical nearest-vertex distribution is an exact two-angle integral, and the probability of directions within excess \(\varepsilon\) of the worst covering gap is \(C_{p,q}\varepsilon^3+O(\varepsilon^4)\) with an explicit positive coefficient. For the square–pentagon volume maximizer the coefficient is approximately \(25.0913881588\), with exactly \(20\) deepest directions.
