For any centrally symmetric nonuniform fan of \(2N\) planar rays, conic-spline interpolation has the exact curvature-variation error coefficients \(\frac14\tan(h_*/2)\) in \(L^\infty\) and \(\sec(h_*/2)-1\) in unnormalized \(L^1\), where \(h_*\) is the largest angular gap. A rank-one seminorm saturates both inequalities, and equally spaced rays uniquely minimize the two worst-case constants.
