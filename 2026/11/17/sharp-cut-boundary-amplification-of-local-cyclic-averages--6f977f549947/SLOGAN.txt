A positive cyclic averaging kernel supported on fewer than half the sites has exact open-path variation gain \(2-p_0\), determined solely by its zero-shift mass, and uniform boxcar averaging has a sharp two-regime formula with a nonexpansivity transition at \(m=\lceil2n/3\rceil\) apart from the identity window. Repeated two-tap smoothing initially increases its sharp variation gain as \(2-2^{-r}\) before eventually driving every fixed-length signal to a constant.
