# Scientific comparison and verification

## Correctness — PASS
The cyclic difference map has exactly the mean-zero image, and it commutes with the graph Laplacian pseudoinverse. The displayed Green row is checked directly by its zero-sum and Laplacian-residual identities. Zero-sum cube duality proves the median formula and all sign equality faces, including the tied median cases. Shell-count summation yields the four rational expressions and their boundaries. The exact standalone checker corroborates 197 period formulas, 250 Green residuals, and 4850 cube-vertex evaluations; finite checks are not the proof of arbitrary sizes.

## Originality — PASS, with historical priority risk
Ellis's Section 3 gives the normalized cycle Green function; it does not optimize centered row absolute deviations or classify extremizers of the unnormalized Lipschitz pseudoinverse. Ilyin–Laptev–Zelik provide sharp \(\ell^2\)-based lattice interpolation results, not this periodic \(\ell^\infty\)-difference inverse estimate. The closest published cyclic Riesz-transform theorem concerns \(D L^{{-1/2}}\), not \(L^+\) on Lipschitz increments; a previously obtained single-frequency Lipschitz projection norm cannot alone provide the sharp simultaneous inverse sum. Searches of exact formulations and related aliases located no dominating theorem. Older periodic Favard-type literature might encode the same constants: bibliographic uniqueness is not certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The operator is the canonical periodic discrete Poisson solution map and its norm is a natural stability constant. The theorem determines the complete sharp constant for all cycle lengths, four nontrivial finite-size classes, and the full exposed norming faces rather than just an isolated numerical estimate. The quadratic scaling \(n^2/32\) and exact arithmetic corrections quantify convergence to the continuous periodic problem, while the sign-face classification gives precise worst-case forcing profiles.

The proof is real, finite-cycle, and unit-weight only. It does not establish any normalized or complex/weighted extension, and an unindexed historical equivalence remains possible. Independent audit has not yet been performed.
