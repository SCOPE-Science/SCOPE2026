For rectangular partial-matching polytopes, every pair of facets meets in a ridge exactly when both matrix dimensions are at least three; their complete Euclidean ridge-angle spectrum consists only of right angles, up to two potentially coincident acute angle classes, and one obtuse class, with exact multiplicities. The uniformly sampled ridge angle has an obtuse mean only for the \(2\times2\) case; in every larger case its mean is acute, and in square order \(n\) the deficit from \(\pi/2\) is asymptotic to \(4n^{-5/2}\).
