# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The norm on the zero-sum hyperplane is generated by elementary mass transfers. The operator and extension norms reduce exactly to diameter and enclosing radius. The arithmetic-mean bound and the balanced-subset median obstruction match for every even dimension; the combinatorial identity is checked algebraically. The accompanying exact-integer tests corroborate representative dimensions without constituting the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The motivating 2026 source (arXiv:2611.00451v1) provides the POE-p qualitative criterion, not an exact constant for the standard zero-sum ℓ1 hyperplanes. Related published findings involve Hilbert targets or ℓ∞ cube hyperplanes. The foundational paper arXiv:2210.00614 was checked by abstract and cross-reference only; its full text and potentially equivalent ℓ1 Jung results remain priority risks. An indexed nonmatch is not proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The constant is a natural, sharp extension-loss invariant for a canonical codimension-one Banach-space pair, with explicit extremizers, a finite-dimensional obstruction, and an asymptotic limit of two. The result is stronger than merely establishing POE-1 or quoting the general mean-center bound, because the balanced-subset construction proves the upper bound is simultaneously attainable for all even dimensions.

## Scope and limitations
Real scalars, finite ℓ1 targets, even dimensions only. No claim for odd dimensions, other target spaces, or independent verification.
