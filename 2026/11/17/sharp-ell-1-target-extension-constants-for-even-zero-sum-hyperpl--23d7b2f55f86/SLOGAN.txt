For every even dimension \(n\ge2\), extending real-linear maps from the coordinate-sum-zero hyperplane of \(\ell_1^n\) into arbitrary finite \(\ell_1\) targets requires, and always suffices with, the sharp factor \(2(n-1)/n\). Balanced-subset sign vectors attain the bound and identify the associated exact \(n\)-point enclosing-radius ratio.
