For separable Bell-diagonal two-qubit inputs, the largest entanglement negativity created by a trace-normalized Kubo–Ando mean is exactly \((1-c-d)/(2(1+c+d))\), where \(c,d\) are its harmonic representing measure's two endpoint masses. A universal rank-two separable input pair attains this bound, and all non-arithmetic means admit strictly positive separable inputs approaching it.
