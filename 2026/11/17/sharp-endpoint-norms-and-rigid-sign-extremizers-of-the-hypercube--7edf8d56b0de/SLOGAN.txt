For the Euclidean Hodge projection of arbitrary real edge fields onto gradient flows on the \(d\)-dimensional Boolean cube, the exact \(\ell_\infty\) and \(\ell_1\) operator norms are both \((d+1)/2\). Every output edge draws absolute projection mass \(1\) from its own coordinate direction and \(1/2\) from each other direction, and all norming maximum-norm inputs are rigid row-sign fields.
