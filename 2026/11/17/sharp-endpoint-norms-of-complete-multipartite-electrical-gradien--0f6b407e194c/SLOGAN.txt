The exact \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_\infty\) norms of the electrical gradient projection on any complete multipartite graph depend only on the sizes of two parts, via an explicit additive formula. Among all such graphs with \(N\) vertices, balanced complete bipartite graphs are the unique maximizers for every \(N\ge8\), and the sharp limiting amplification is \(3\); small orders exhibit precisely classified exceptions.
