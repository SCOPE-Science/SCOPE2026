For the standard positive type-\(A\) root polytope without the origin, completing every transitive-tournament arrow by its reverse creates a sharp Euclidean Hausdorff gap whose unique deepest point is the negative highest root. For \(n\ge5\), the squared gap is exactly \(2+12/[(n-3)(n-2)(n-1)]\), and its nearest point is a parabolically weighted convex combination of only the internal simple roots.
