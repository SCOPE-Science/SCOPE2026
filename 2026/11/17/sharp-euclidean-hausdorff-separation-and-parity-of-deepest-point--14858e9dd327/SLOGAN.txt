The canonical Catalan matroid polytope lies at exact Euclidean Hausdorff distance \(\sqrt{\lfloor(n+3)/2\rfloor}\) from the rank-\(n\) hypersimplex on \(2n\) coordinates. Its unique deepest point is a reversed binary word when \(n\) is odd, whereas for even \(n\) the full deepest locus is an edge, with projections on a canonical exposed cube face.
