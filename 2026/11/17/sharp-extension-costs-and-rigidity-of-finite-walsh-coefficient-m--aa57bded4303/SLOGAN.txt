For finite first-level Walsh coefficient maps from real \(L^1\) into \(\ell_p^n\), the minimum extension norm is exactly \(n^{1/p}\), uniquely attained for every \(p>1\) but nonuniquely at \(p=1\). The exact relative extension factors are \((n/2)^{1/p}\) for \(p\ge2\) and \(\mathbb E|\sum_{j=1}^n r_j|\) at \(p=1\), while the Hilbert case admits an equality-sharp stability identity.
