For three equally sized equilateral clusters separated by unit distance, the canonical Euclidean inball of the real Lipschitz-free unit ball is governed by a one-variable strictly concave integer quadratic. At \(t=2m/(3m-1)\), its optimal-center locus collapses from affine dimension \(3m-3\) to a single point, while the contact-facet count jumps to \(6(2m+1)\).
