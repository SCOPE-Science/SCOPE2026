In the \(n\)-by-\(n\) Birkhoff polytope, the largest Euclidean tetrahedra have volume \(n^{3/2}/3\) and are exactly four support-disjoint permutation matrices. For every \(n\ge5\), the sharp next vertex-tetrahedron squared-volume gap is \(n(n+1)/18\), attained exactly by a four-row permutation array with a single coincident position in one row pair; order four has a distinct finite obstruction.
