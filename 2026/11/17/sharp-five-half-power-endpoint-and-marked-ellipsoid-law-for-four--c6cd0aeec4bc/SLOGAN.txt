For four independent uniform points on the unit two-sphere, the probability that the sum of all six Euclidean chord lengths is within \(t\) of its regular-tetrahedron maximum is asymptotic to \(\frac{256\,2^{1/4}3^{3/4}\sqrt5}{3375}t^{5/2}\). Conditioned on that rare event, the two orientation branches become equally likely and their five-dimensional, Hessian-normalized local deformations become uniform in the unit ball in total variation.
