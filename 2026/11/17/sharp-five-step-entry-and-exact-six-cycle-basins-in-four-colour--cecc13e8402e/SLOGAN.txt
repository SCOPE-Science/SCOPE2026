For four-colour threshold-two cyclic dynamics on a complete bipartite graph, every configuration becomes periodic within five updates and only periods \(1,4,6\) occur. Primitive six-cycles exist precisely when both shores have at least six vertices, with an exact basin size \(4(U_m+V_m)(U_n+V_n)+4W_mW_n\); every state in that basin enters its six-cycle within one update.
