For \(c>1+\sqrt3\), the four-quadrant planar map \(T(x,y)=(|x|-1,\ x+c|y|-1)\) has a compact bounded-orbit core conjugate, on its second iterate, to an interval identity times a full four-shift; the entire planar bounded-forward set has exact Hausdorff dimension \(1+\log2/\log c\). Uniform area on its natural section gives the exact two-step nonescape law \((4/c^2)^n\), and the parameter boundary is sharp for the phase-uniform, strictly separated four-branch structure.
