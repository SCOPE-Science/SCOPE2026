All \(q\)-color phase grids can be made simultaneously attracting in a complete identical-oscillator network only with Fourier interaction degree at least \(q\), regardless of the number \(N\ge2\) of oscillators. The pure \(q\)-th harmonic attains this sharp degree and has \(q^{N-1}\) attracting relative configurations, with equal Haar basin probabilities \(q^{1-N}\).
