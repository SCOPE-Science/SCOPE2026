For radial perturbations of a regular \(n\)-gon, the inradius-sum differences across just its consecutive quadrilateral flips vanish locally precisely for cocircular vertices. Their optimal sharp error-to-distance constant is \(\mu_n=4\sin(\pi/n)\sin(2\pi/n)\sin(3\pi/n)/[\cos(\pi/n)(1+\cos(\pi/n))(1+2\cos(\pi/n))]\sim4\pi^3/n^3\), with the second Fourier harmonic least detectable.
