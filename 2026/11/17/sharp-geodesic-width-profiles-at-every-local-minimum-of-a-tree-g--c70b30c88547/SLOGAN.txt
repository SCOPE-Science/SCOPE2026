For every unweighted tree on \(n\ge3\) vertices, its graphical zonotope has exactly \(2(n-1)\) strict Euclidean width minima, and the minimum width at each sufficiently small fixed angular distance has an attained closed formula from the Gram matrix of edge cuts. At leaf-edge minima, stars and paths have identical widths but sharp angular stability slopes differing by the exact factor \(n-2\).
