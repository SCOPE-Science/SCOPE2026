## Mathematical value — PASS

The residual is used to diagnose which observations were suppressed by graph fusion. Its two-input stability is a natural and distinct question from the stability of the fitted vector. The exact result exhibits a first instability threshold at four vertices, identifies the worst pooled-block size to integer precision (approximately the square root of twice the vertex count), quantifies the sharp approach to amplification two, and shows complete independence from all positive fusion-edge penalty magnitudes. These are structural and quantitative boundaries for a standard graph-proximal operation, not merely a computed example.
