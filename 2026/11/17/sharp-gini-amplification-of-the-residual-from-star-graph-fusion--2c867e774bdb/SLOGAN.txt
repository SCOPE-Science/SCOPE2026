On every finite star, the fitted fusion denoiser contracts complete-pair Gini variation, but its data residual can amplify two-input Gini differences. The residual has the sharp, penalty-independent coefficient \(\max_{2\le m\le n}(m-1)(2n-m)/(m(n-1))\), first exceeds one at four vertices, and approaches two with an optimal pooled block of size near \(\sqrt{2n}\).
