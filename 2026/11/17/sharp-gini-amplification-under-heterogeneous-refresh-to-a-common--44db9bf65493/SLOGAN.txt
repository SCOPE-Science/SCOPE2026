For \(n\) agents that independently refresh toward the common arithmetic mean with arbitrary site rates between zero and one, the sharp one-step complete-pair Gini amplification factor is \(1+\lfloor(n-2)^2/4\rfloor/[n(n-1)]\). Amplification begins at four agents and approaches the unattained universal factor \(5/4\), and it persists even when every transition probability is strictly positive.
