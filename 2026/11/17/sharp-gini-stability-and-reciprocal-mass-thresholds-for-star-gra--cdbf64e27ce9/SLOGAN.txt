For weighted star-graph fused-lasso denoising, complete-pair Gini stability has an exact finite formula independent of every positive fusion-edge penalty. When the hub-to-leaf fidelity-mass ratio is \(a\) and there are \(k\ge3\) leaves, the map is Gini-nonexpansive precisely for \((k-2)/k\le a\le k/(k-2)\); five leaves mark a switch in the worst extreme-mass mechanism.
