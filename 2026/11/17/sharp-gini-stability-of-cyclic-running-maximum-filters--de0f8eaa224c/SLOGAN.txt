Cyclic running maxima have the exact two-input Gini-variation amplification \(w(n-w)/(n-1)\) on \(n\) sites with a \(w\)-point window, attained by perturbing a single global peak. Nonexpansivity fails exactly for the interior window widths \(2\le w\le n-2\), and the worst amplification grows as \(n/4\).
