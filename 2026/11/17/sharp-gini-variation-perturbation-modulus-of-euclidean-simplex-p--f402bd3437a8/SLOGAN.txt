For Euclidean projection onto a positive-mass probability simplex, the sharp two-input complete-graph Gini-variation multiplier is \(\max_{2\le k\le n}(k-1)(2n-k)/(k(n-1))\), attained by a single-coordinate perturbation on a support of size approximately \(\sqrt{2n}\). Individual inputs always lose variation, but differences are amplified in every dimension \(n\ge4\), with the sharp multiplier approaching \(2\).
