The four-species biased Volterra lattice has a sharp global sign-of-bias transition: every positive-bias interior orbit approaches equal coexistence, while every nonuniform negative-bias interior orbit approaches the boundary. Nonexceptional positive-bias oscillations decay with the exact universal law \(t[(u_1-u_3)^2+(u_2-u_4)^2]\to 2[a^2+4(a+2)^2]/[a(a+2)^2]\); the alternating-pair-symmetric line converges exponentially.
