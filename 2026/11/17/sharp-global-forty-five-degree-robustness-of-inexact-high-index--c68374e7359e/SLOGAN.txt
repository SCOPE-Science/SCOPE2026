For globally split strongly concave--convex energies, high-index saddle flows converge from every initial state under arbitrarily varying inexact projectors whenever their largest principal angle is below \(\pi/4\), with the sharp uniform energy-decay exponent \(2\beta\mu\cos(2\theta_{\max})\). No larger uniform angular tolerance is possible: a planar quadratic has periodic orbits at the boundary and expanding spirals beyond it.
