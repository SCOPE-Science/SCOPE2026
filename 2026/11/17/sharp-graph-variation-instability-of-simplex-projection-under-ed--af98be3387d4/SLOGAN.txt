On every connected unweighted graph, Euclidean simplex projection contracts each signal's edge variation but can sharply amplify differences between two signals. Across all \(n\)-vertex graphs with edge connectivity at least \(q\), the exact worst two-input multiplier for \(n\ge4q\) is \(1+F_n/q\), where \(F_n\) equals \((n^2-1)/16\) for odd \(n\), \(n^2/16\) for \(n\equiv0\pmod4\), and \((n^2-4)/16\) for \(n\equiv2\pmod4\); matching-joined cliques attain every bound.
