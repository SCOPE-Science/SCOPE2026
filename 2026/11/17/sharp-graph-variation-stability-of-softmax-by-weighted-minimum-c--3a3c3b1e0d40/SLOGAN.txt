For every finite connected positively edge-weighted graph on at least two vertices, the exact two-input edge-total-variation Lipschitz constant of inverse-temperature-\(\beta\) softmax is \(\frac{\beta}{4}\max_{i\ne j}(d_w(i)+d_w(j))/\lambda_w(i,j)\), where \(\lambda_w\) is pairwise minimum-cut capacity. For unit-weight graphs at \(\beta=1\), the sharp dimension-wide envelope is \(n/4\), attained precisely by graphs with a bridge joining two vertices universal on their respective sides; in contrast with ordinary \(\ell_p\)-norm softmax contraction, graph-variation amplification can grow without bound with graph size.
