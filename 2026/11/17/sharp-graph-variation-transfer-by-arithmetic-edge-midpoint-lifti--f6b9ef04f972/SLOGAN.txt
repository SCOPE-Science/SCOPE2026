Arithmetic midpoint lifting from vertex signals to the edge-indexed line graph has an exact sharp total-variation coefficient given by a finite cut-wedge ratio, with universal ceiling \((n-2)/2\) and a complete cut-boundary equality criterion. On every tree this coefficient is \(\tfrac12\max_{\{u,v\}\in E}(d_u+d_v-2)\); paths and the four-vertex claw are exactly the variation-nonexpansive trees, and amplification first becomes possible at five vertices.
