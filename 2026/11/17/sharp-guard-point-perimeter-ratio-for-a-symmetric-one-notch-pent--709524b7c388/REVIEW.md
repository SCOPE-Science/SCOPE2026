# Mathematical review

## Correctness (PASS)

The claim is restricted to an explicitly defined three-parameter family of simple pentagons. The visibility kernel follows from *all five* inward halfplanes, with the switch in its upper envelope at the stated positive \(y_0\). Integration produces two rational formulas. The two Euclidean perimeters have exactly three distinct edge-length contributions after reflection. The strict finite-height inequality is obtained by the Euclidean triangle inequality, and equality of the supremum with its infinite-height envelope is justified by an actual limit for each fixed shape pair. Exact derivative factorizations yield a unique optimal \(b\) at every \(t\) and a uniquely sign-changing cubic on \((0,1)\). The rational finite witness is independently checked. `verify.py` passes 42 rational clipping cases across both branches and a separate rational witness check. Potential arithmetic or normalization mistakes are addressed by explicit identities, not numerical plotting.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The explicit symmetric notch is a natural canonical class rather than an arbitrary numerical slice: it is precisely a direction proposed for follow-up in the initiating 2026 manuscript, and the later 2026 roadmap still leaves the wider one-notch semialgebraic problem unfinished. The result **completely** optimizes a continuous three-parameter family, including active-kernel combinatorial transition, a unique algebraic maximizing limiting configuration, exact upper constant, strict nonattainment and a finite rational witness. It isolates how directional elongation generates the inequality failure. Its value is structural understanding of a natural subclass, not a numerical improvement to the unrestricted lower bound, which is already greater than this family's optimum.
