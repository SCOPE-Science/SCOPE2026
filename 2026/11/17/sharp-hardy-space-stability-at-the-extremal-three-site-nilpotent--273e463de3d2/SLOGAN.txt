For the disk-normalized three-site nilpotent shift, the sharp spectral-set deficit controls Hardy-space distance to the extremal quadratic monomial with optimal local coefficient \(\sqrt2\): \(d(f)\le(\sqrt2+o(1))\sqrt{2-\|f(A)\|}\). Degree-two Blaschke products attain this leading coefficient, whereas exact qualitative equality had already been covered by earlier work.
