For high interaction order in a synchronized Ott–Antonsen amplitude model, added positive coupling **eventually slows** first arrival to a neighborhood of the model's own raised steady state by a sharp \(s^{d-1}\) law, even though it **always speeds** arrival at a fixed reference target by a much smaller \(t^{d-1}/d\) law. The contrast exposes an exact moving-target effect rather than slower pointwise flow.
