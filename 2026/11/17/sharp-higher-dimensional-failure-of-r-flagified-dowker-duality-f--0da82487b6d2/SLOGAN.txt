For the uniform point–subset incidence relation, higher \(r\)-flagification makes one Dowker complex contractible but makes its transposed counterpart a wedge of exactly \(\binom{n-1}{r+1}\) integral \(r\)-spheres for every \(r\ge2\) and \(n\ge r+2\). Thus the existing low-degree higher-flagification duality bound is sharp in every arity, and its first homology failure has arbitrarily large rank.
