On every connected undirected network with one strictly highest-degree hub, a degree-conjugate positive-interaction flow has an invariant unstable spectrum but two sharp transitions in the hub Gershgorin disc: first it loses the dominant eigenvalue, then it lies wholly in the stable half-plane. On an \(n\)-leaf star these happen exactly at \(q=1/2\) and \(q=1\), while the true growth exponent is always \(\sqrt n-1>0\).
