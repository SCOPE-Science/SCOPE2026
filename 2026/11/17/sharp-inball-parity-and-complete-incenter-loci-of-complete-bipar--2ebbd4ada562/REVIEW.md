# Review of the complete-bipartite inball-center classification

**Correctness — PASS.** The signed-potential facet description is reconstructed by a connected tight-edge criterion. Signed normal lengths, central-symmetry reduction, exact tangent counts, active-span ranks and the balanced-even endpoint are verified algebraically for all parameters. The accompanying independent finite rational tests passed 1,462 assertions; they do not constitute the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (provisional priority).** The signed facet system and its total count already occur in Higashitani–Jochemko–Michałek (2018); they are not claimed as new. The 2025 symmetric-edge-polytope preprint concerns edge counts. Other examined mathematical results concern the complete-bipartite zonotope's Löwner ellipsoid, the symmetric-edge-polytope one-skeleton spectrum, and a different bipartite cycle root polytope. The final claim is confined to the exact spherical inball, tangencies, and complete incenter-set structure. A broader unindexed incenter result remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem classifies a naturally motivated infinite family of maximal balls and proves a sharp parity-and-balance phenomenon: the center dimension ranges from zero to the smaller part size, with a fully explicit line segment in the balanced even case. The numerical inradius alone is a short consequence of known facets, but the combined center and tangency stratification is the structural content.

**Scientific limits.** Canonical unit-root Euclidean metric; no weighted or arbitrary affine extension. Unequal-size center polytopes are specified by exact finite inequalities rather than enumerated by vertices. Historical priority is provisional.
