For a Euclidean constant-width body whose support perturbation is a sum of cubic monomials in mutually orthogonal coordinate triples, the full convexity region is exactly the coefficient box \(\max_j|\beta_j|\le3\sqrt6\,\rho/8\), irrespective of the number of blocks. All endpoint curvature degeneracies and sharp planar-shadow areas are classified, with only single-block planes attaining the smallest shadow area and the universal endpoint ratio \(15/16\).
