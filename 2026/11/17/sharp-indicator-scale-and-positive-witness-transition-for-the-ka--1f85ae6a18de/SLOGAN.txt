For the canonical Kalton–Peck \(L^1\) range quotient gauge, every small measurable indicator has exact leading quasi-norm \(t/\log(1/t)\), uniformly over all real or complex witnesses, with an explicit relative error at most \(t/e\). Restricting witnesses to be nonnegative gives a complete finite-measure formula with an attained golden-ratio support transition at \(t=e^{-(1+\sqrt5)/2}\); no corresponding exact formula for the unrestricted gauge is claimed.
