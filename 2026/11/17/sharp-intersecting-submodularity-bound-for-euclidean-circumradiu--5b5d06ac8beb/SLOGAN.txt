Euclidean circumradius is not intersecting-submodular, but it satisfies the sharp dimension-free relaxation \(R(A\cup B)+R(A\cap B)\le(1+\sqrt2)(R(A)+R(B))/2\), with equality on a planar four-point configuration. A stronger sharp envelope \(R(A\cup B)\le s+\sqrt{s^2-r^2}\), where \(s=(R(A)+R(B))/2\) and \(r=R(A\cap B)\), quantifies every overlap-radius regime; on the real line the optimal factor is \(1\).
