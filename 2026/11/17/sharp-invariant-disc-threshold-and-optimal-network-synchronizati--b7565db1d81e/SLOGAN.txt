A complex hyperbolic-tangent oscillator network admits a closed radius-\(R\) invariant polydisc exactly when \(\alpha\ge\beta\tan R/R\); strict inequality is the sharp all-data zero-attraction threshold on that set. Throughout the strictly attracting regime, graph disagreement satisfies a global nonlinear estimate at the **optimal** spectral-gap exponent \(\alpha-\beta+\kappa\lambda_2\), with an explicit uniform transient prefactor and a corresponding Kobayashi-distance bound.
