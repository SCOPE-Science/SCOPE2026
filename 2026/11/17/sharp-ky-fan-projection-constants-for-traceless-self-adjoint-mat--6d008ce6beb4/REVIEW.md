# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Diagonalization reduces the operator norm to N_k(x)=top-k sum of |x_i|. The polar ball is {||b||_infty<=1, ||b||_1<=k}; for integral k its vertices are exactly signed k-coordinate supports. Dual evaluation of Fb=b-(sum b)/n gives max of infinity branch 1+(k-2)/n and l1/k branch 1+d(n-k-d)/(nk), where d is the parity-constrained sign sum. Discrete difference and monotonicity split exactly at n=3k-2. Rank-one spike and (when n>=2k) k-positive/(n-k)-negative spectrum realize branches. Averaging arbitrary real-linear projection under conjugation fixes traceless matrices and kills scalar I, yielding trace centering as the minimizer. Endpoint cases n=2,k=1,n and nonexpansive rank list checked. Independent enumeration of 35 (n,k) combinations n=2..8 and all dual sign vertices matches the formula, distinct from universal proof.

## originality

PASS

Foucart 2016 proves minimality of trace centering and exact Schatten endpoint bounds, with intermediate Ky Fan upper bound 2-2/n. It does not supply rank-dependent sharp constants on the real selfadjoint domain. Published 3x3 nonnormal Ky Fan-2 theorem gives a larger constant for all matrices, explicitly identifies selfadjoint (3,2)=1 but not the complete all-n,k phase boundary. Thus the sharp selfadjoint two-branch norm and classifications survive.
