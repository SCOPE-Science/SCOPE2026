For every real symmetric or complex Hermitian \(n\times n\) matrix space, the least Ky Fan \(k\)-norm projection constant onto the traceless hyperplane is exactly \(\max\{2-2k/n,\,1+(k-2)/n\}\). A sharp transition at \(n=3k-2\) switches extremizers between balanced-sign spectra and rank-one spikes; from dimension five onward no Ky Fan rank is nonexpansive.
