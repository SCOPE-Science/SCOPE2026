Ordinary mean-value harmonic extension from the leaves of a finite tree preserves the graph-distance Lipschitz seminorm for all boundary data exactly when the tree is a path. On a spider with arm lengths \(L_i\), its sharp norm is \(1+(m-2)/(L_{\min}\sum_i1/L_i)\), and the worst \(n\)-vertex spider has norm asymptotic to \(\sqrt n/2\).
