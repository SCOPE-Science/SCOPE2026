Equal-area Minkowski polytopes built from two regular latitude rings have spherical \(3\)-design facet normals for every \(n\ge4\), yet their projection bodies retain a sharp asymptotic circumradius-to-inradius ratio of \(1.2499741161\ldots\). The outer radius is exactly \(2\pi/\sqrt3\) at every order, while the least-bright directions approach a unique oblique latitude determined by \(u=\sin(2u)\).
