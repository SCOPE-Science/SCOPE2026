For higher Ky Fan sums of Gaussian Fock Toeplitz operators, every sufficiently small radial perturbation of a disk with fixed symmetric-difference area has an **exact, sharp** spectral deficit: the uniquely least-damaging perturbation removes the outermost disk annulus and adds an adjacent annulus outside. The deficit is quadratic in the exchanged area, with an explicit optimal rank- and area-dependent coefficient.
