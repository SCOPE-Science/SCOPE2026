For every strictly positive bounded unilateral weighted shift, opposite relative errors in the second and \(n\)-th Embry moment inequalities force a sharp pointwise bound \(-\alpha\le\log(w_{j+1}^2/w_j^2)\le\beta/(n-1)+(n-2)\alpha/2\). Exact finite-pulse extremizers and arbitrarily slowly varying counterexamples show that this local rigidity does not imply any uniform global operator-norm proximity to constant-weight shifts.
