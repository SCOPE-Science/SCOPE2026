At the full-branch boundary of every greedy double-base map, lowering the zero-digit base by \(\varepsilon\) changes its physical invariant measure by the sharp scale \(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)\), not by a linear response. The rescaled signed change converges to \(C_B(\mu_0-\delta_M)\), and the corresponding density's \(L^1\) distance has exact leading coefficient \(2C_B\).
