# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The disjointness of excited populations two updates apart, the two-sided edge count, the initial-step degree count and the layered graph construction establish each quantified claim. The upper and lower estimates have the same leading constant. A standalone exhaustive and construction checker supports the calculations but is not used as an infinite proof. Assumes integer \(\Delta/2<\theta\le\Delta\).

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest prior paper, *A densest-subgraph threshold and finite excitation budget for three-state graph dynamics* (2026), already proves eventual extinction whenever induced density is below threshold; this known qualitative fact is not claimed. Its O(size-of-initial-excitation) event bound does not state the new exact geometric decay or sharp logarithmic worst-time coefficient. Doba (2018) treats threshold one, while Bello and Sivakoff (2021) treat random infinite-tree dynamics. The 1993 foundational paper was abstract-inspected only. An unindexed equivalent estimate remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The exact worst-case relaxation scale is a structurally motivated finite-network invariant, improving a previously known linear-size extinction budget to a sharp logarithmic rate for every graph above the half-degree contact threshold. The extremizing layered family shows the exponent cannot be improved within this natural universality class.

## Scientific limits
Deterministic three-state rule; fixed integer parameters; graph maximum degree bounded above; no claim at critical threshold or for probabilistic models. Historical priority is qualified; external independent validation remains outstanding.
