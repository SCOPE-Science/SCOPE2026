The real finite prefix Cesàro matrix has sharp complete-graph Gini-variation norm \(\big((n+1)H_n-2n\big)/(n-1)\), attained by a single first-coordinate spike in every dimension \(n\ge2\). Its norm crosses one at \(n=6\) and grows as \(\log n+\gamma-2+o(1)\), sharply contrasting with adjacent-difference variation contraction.
