# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The norm-convergent disjoint-block construction proves existence of a dense weighted orbit in \(\ell^p\) and \(c_0\), while a direct small-norm perturbation of finitely supported vectors proves topological mixing. For two return times \(n<m\), the zeroth coordinate at time \(m\) and coordinate \(m-n\) at time \(n\) give the strict multiplicative separation. Counting these separated times yields the uniform logarithmic estimate. A geometric coordinate support supplies matching logarithmic lower returns, the exact \(c_0\) constant, and the limiting small-radius coefficient. All normalization, strict inequalities, \(p=1\), and \(c_0\) endpoints were checked; finite computations are illustrative, not logical substitutes.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk
Li–Ruan–Yang's inspected 2026 full text provides the motivating general weighted-orbit setting and block coding on the product sequence space, but does not give a sharp logarithmic recurrence invariant on these Banach sequence spaces. The inspected older Costakis–Parissis full text implies the *qualitative* impossibility of frequent universality for polynomial weights on a nonrecurrent shift; that qualitative consequence is explicitly credited and excluded from the novelty assertion. Targeted published-record searches and directly comparable historical research claims did not identify the sharp uniform logarithmic packing law, exact \(c_0\) supremum, or common small-radius coefficient. Earlier general recurrence literature may contain equivalent quantitative bounds expressed without the same parameterization; no claim of exhaustive publication priority is made.

## Value — PASS
A generic qualitative non-frequency obstruction leaves the number of possible returns unspecified. The exact logarithmic return-frequency invariant is a natural quantitative measure of how a topologically mixing universal operator sequence can fail positive-density universality. The \(c_0\) formula is dimension-free and optimal at every ball radius; the \(\ell^p\) bounds prove that the leading small-target cost is independent of summability exponent. The result is self-contained and reproduces its extremal geometric spacing, rather than merely repackaging a known non-frequency criterion.
