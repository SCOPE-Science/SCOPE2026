For every three-dimensional generalized parking-function polytope, the largest contained tetrahedron has volume \(\max\{A^3+B^3,B^2(C+A)\}/6\), where \(C=b_1-1\), \(A=b_1+b_2-1\), and \(B=b_1+b_2+b_3-1\). A sharp algebraic transition switches the optimizers from exactly two rigid tetrahedra to a two-dimensional family with a movable apex on a permutahedral face, with both types attaining equality at the transition.
