For the nonuniform Steffen monotone cubic Hermite interpolant, the exact two-input total-variation Lipschitz coefficient is \(1+\frac{16}{27}\sup_i(h_{i-1}+h_{i+1})/h_i\), whereas each individual interpolated signal preserves its own variation. Finite perturbation stability holds exactly for uniformly shape-regular meshes, and one-secant data perturbations are sharp.
