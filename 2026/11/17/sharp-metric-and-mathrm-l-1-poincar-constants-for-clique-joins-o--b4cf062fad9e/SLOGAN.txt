For each \(m\ge3\) and \(c\ge1\), the clique join \(K_{m,m}\vee K_c\) has exact, distinct exponent-one Poincaré constants for arbitrary metric targets and for real \(\mathrm L^1\) targets; their ratio exceeds one by \(\frac{(m-1)(m-2)}{(m+2c)(2m+c-2)}\). Every optimal nontrivial \(\mathrm L^1\) cut selects exactly one vertex from each independent shore and no clique vertex, or is its complement.
