For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a\ge b\ge2\), the sharp exponent-one Poincaré constants for arbitrary metric targets and real \(L^1\) targets are explicit, with every equality cut classified by a sharp part-imbalance transition. On balanced graphs the metric-to-\(L^1\) ratio approaches \(3/2\), while the optimal real \(L^1\) cuts switch from balanced selections to singleton cuts when the part-size difference reaches two.
