# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For all integers \(n\ge3\), every target metric reduces by graph automorphism averaging to three orbit distances. Two explicit triangle paths prove the universal upper bound, and the graph metric realizes it. For real \(L^1\), Tonelli/layer-cake reduces the sharp constant and all equality maps to the finite cut optimization. The exact boundary count \(B=(n-1)s-2k\ell+2t\), strict monotonicity of its relaxed envelope, and the final odd-parity floor step establish all claimed constants and equality cases. Exhaustive exact cut computations for \(3\le n\le8\) and independent breadth-first distance checks for \(3\le n\le39\) were executed; the infinite result does not rest on those finite checks.

Polavarapu's full 2026 preprint covers cotype-conditional *bounds* for arbitrary regular graphs, not family-specific exact \(L^1\) cut constants or equality cases. The full available arXiv text of Altschuler et al. treats random/bounded-degree metric graph gaps, not this family. The prior crown-graph negative-type classification concerns negative type of the graph-distance matrix and Euclidean snowflake rank, not the mean-distance Poincaré quotient. Related crown cut/cycle projections and Han–Wang's Hamming-cube results do not state the present constants. A possible older graph isoperimetric/eigenvalue equality reformulation remains a genuine residual publication-priority risk. A failed search alone is not relied upon as a proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
These are exact endpoint constants for a canonical dense regular bipartite family that differs minimally from a complete bipartite graph, naturally testing the Banach-lattice versus unrestricted metric gap central to nonlinear spectral-gap theory. The target gap appears sharply at \(n=5\), persists with limiting magnitude \(1/2\), and the complete equality classification determines every extremal real \(L^1\) map through layer-cake cuts. The full all-size classification is more informative than an arbitrary small numerical example.
