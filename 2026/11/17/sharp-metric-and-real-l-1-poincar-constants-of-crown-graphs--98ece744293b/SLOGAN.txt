The linear-distance nonlinear Poincaré constant of every crown graph is \(3/2\) for unrestricted metric targets, but equals \((n-1)/(n-2)\) for even \(n\) and \(n/(n-1)\) for odd \(n\) in real \(L^1\), producing a strict target gap from five matched pairs onward. Every optimal real \(L^1\) map decomposes almost everywhere into balanced cuts selecting one vertex from each deleted matching pair.
