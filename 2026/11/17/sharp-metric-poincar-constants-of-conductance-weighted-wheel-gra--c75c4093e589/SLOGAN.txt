For every conductance-weighted wheel with uniform spoke conductance \(A\) and rim conductance \(B\), the sharp exponent-one Poincaré constant is **identical for arbitrary metric, real \(L^1\), and scalar targets**, and is attained by a contiguous rim-arc cut or the full-rim cut. Its exact finite formula yields a sharp full-rim transition at \(B/A=\tfrac12\lfloor r^2/4\rfloor\) and an integer staircase of optimal arc lengths.
