For the Fano-plane incidence graph, the sharp exponent-one Poincaré constant is \(27/13\) for unrestricted metric targets but only \(24/13\) for real \(L^1\) targets, when averaging over distinct unordered vertex pairs. Every equality-achieving real \(L^1\) map decomposes almost everywhere into cuts from the 28 induced hexagons and their complements.
