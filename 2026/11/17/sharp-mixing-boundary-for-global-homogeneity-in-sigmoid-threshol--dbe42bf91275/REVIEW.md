The assumptions give global forward solutions on the invariant activation cube. The signed row decomposition yields the Dobrushin diameter inequality. The logistic secant is strictly below its maximal derivative on every nondegenerate interval, proving strict diameter dissipation even at the closed endpoint. A scalar asymptotically autonomous mean equation, whose drift is analytic with finitely many zeros, establishes convergence to an actual homogeneous equilibrium. The explicit stable patterned equilibrium has a full Jacobian with two negative eigenvalues; this is not inferred from a one-dimensional reduction alone. Limitations: the mixing condition is only sufficient for arbitrary network topologies; the two-node construction is not a general converse for every network.
