For logistic threshold networks, sufficiently overlapping stochastic input rows force every trajectory to a homogeneous equilibrium even when feedback is strong enough for scalar hysteresis: the sharp universal mixing condition is \(\beta\delta(W)\le4\). In a two-node model, crossing the same boundary creates heterogeneous equilibria, and critical antisymmetric contrast has the exact law \(t(x_1-x_2)^2\to3/2\).
