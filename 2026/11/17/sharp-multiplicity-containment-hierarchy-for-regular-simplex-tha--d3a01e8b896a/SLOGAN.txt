For the \(d+1\) centroid-to-vertex diameter balls of a regular \(d\)-simplex, the sharp dilation containing every point in at least \(m\) balls is \(\frac12[1+\sqrt{d(d+1-m)/m}]\) for \(1\le m\le d\), with exactly \((d+1)\binom dm\) extremizers. Their \(m\)-fold overlap spills outside the simplex exactly when \(m<d\); all balls intersect only at the centroid.
