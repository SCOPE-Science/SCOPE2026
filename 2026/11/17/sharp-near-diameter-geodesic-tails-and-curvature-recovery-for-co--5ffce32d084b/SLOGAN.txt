Two area-uniform points in a hyperbolic disk of radius \(R\) have a sharp \(\varepsilon^{5/2}\) near-diameter distance tail with an explicit, strictly curvature-dependent coefficient. Conditioning on an almost-maximal distance produces a universal \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,1,1/2,1)\) radial-angular deficit law, and the tail coefficient together with the diameter recovers the disk radius and curvature.
