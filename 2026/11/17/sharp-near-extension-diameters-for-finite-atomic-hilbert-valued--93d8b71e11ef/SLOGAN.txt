In finite-atomic Hilbert-valued \(L^p\), the exact diameter of the almost norm-preserving Hahn–Banach extension set depends only on the smallest normalized atomic coefficient mass when the conjugate exponent exceeds two, and is independent of the coefficient profile at or below two. When all coefficient masses are positive, optimal perturbations switch from proportional spreading to concentration on a single least-mass atom; zero coefficients have a separate explicit equality classification.
