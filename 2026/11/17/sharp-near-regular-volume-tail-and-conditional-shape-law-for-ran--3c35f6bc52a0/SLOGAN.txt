For four independent uniform points on the unit sphere, the probability that their tetrahedron has relative volume deficit below \(\varepsilon\) is asymptotic to \((128\sqrt3/405)\varepsilon^{5/2}\). Conditioned on this rare event, the complete five-dimensional shape perturbation, after removing rotations and scaling by \(\sqrt\varepsilon\), becomes uniform on an explicit ellipsoid, with the two orientation classes equally likely.
