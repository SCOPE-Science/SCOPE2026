For the three-colour threshold-two cyclic rule on every complete tripartite graph, \(F^{21}=F^9\) and the nine-step settling bound is sharp. Primitive four-cycles occur only at shore sizes \((2,2,w)\) with \(w\ge4\), primitive six-cycles only at \((2,2,2)\), and primitive twelve-cycles only when one shore has size at most three and both others have size at least four.
