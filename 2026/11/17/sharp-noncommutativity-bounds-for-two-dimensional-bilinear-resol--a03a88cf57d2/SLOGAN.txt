For noncommuting positive definite \(2\times2\) matrices whose bilinear resolvents admit a positive joint Hausdorff moment completion, the normalized commutator has the sharp universal ceiling \(1/(3\sqrt3)\), attained uniquely in spectral ratio and eigenvector-overlap geometry. At fixed smaller-to-larger eigenvalue ratios, a complete two-branch formula describes the largest allowable noncommutativity and its precise transition.
