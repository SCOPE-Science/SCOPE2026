At the exact invasion boundary of a finite-round two-stage contagion map, a unique explicit probability \(p_*(\nu,\tau)\) switches the disease-free state from locally unstable to locally attracting. Above the switch, every sufficiently small positive adopter seed has the same sharp \(a_n\sim K/n\) relaxation constant, and the switch approaches \(1/2\) only when the round length tends to zero.
