On the body-centered lattice \(\mathbb Z^d\cup(\mathbb Z^d+\frac12\mathbf1)\), nearest-site Voronoi cells under Manhattan and Euclidean distances agree exactly, even with arbitrary common positive coordinate weights. Among all unweighted \(\ell_p\) distances, every exponent produces these same cells in dimensions one and two, but in dimensions at least three precisely \(p=1,2\) do; all other exponents disagree on an open set.
