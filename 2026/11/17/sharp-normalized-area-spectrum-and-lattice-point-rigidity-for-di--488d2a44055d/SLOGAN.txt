The divisor-parabola polygons \(B_h\) introduced in the 2026 reflexive-polytope construction have exactly \(4h\) boundary lattice points and an area determined by cubes of consecutive divisor gaps. Their complete normalized-area limit spectrum is a cubic reciprocal-gap energy over supernatural integers, with sharp endpoints \(1\) and \((\pi^2-6)/3\); attaining the upper endpoint requires eventual divisibility by every fixed integer, while attaining the lower requires the least prime factor to diverge.
