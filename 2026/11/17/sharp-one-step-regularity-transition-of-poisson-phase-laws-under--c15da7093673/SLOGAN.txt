A sine-kicked Poisson phase ensemble leaves the exact Poisson family for every nonzero kick, not only in a weak-coupling expansion. Its one-step density has a sharp transition from bounded analytic to a \(|v|^{-2/3}\) cubic cusp at unit kick strength and \(|v|^{-1/2}\) folds above it, with optimal \(L^p\) cutoffs \(3/2\) and \(2\).
