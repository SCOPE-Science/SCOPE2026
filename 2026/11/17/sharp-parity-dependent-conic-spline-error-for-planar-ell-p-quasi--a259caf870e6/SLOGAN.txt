For the standard conic-spline sampling of planar \(\ell_p\) quasi-norms with \(0<p<1\), the exact leading uniform error is \(\frac{1-p}{p}p^{p/(1-p)}h^p\) on even half-circle grids but \(\frac{1}{p2^p}h^p\) on odd grids. This arithmetic phase jump switches the dominant error from underinterpolation beside sampled axes to overinterpolation at unsampled midpoint axes; at \(p=1\), even grids are exactly error-free.
