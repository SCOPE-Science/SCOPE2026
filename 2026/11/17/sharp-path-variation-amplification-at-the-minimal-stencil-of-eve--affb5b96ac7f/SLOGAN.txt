For minimal-length order-\(p\) Whittaker–Henderson smoothing, the exact sharp path-variation Lipschitz norm is a finite binomial maximum and is attained by a single monotone binary jump. It contracts for \(p=1\), is exactly nonexpansive for \(p=2\), expands for every \(p\ge3\), and tends to \(1+\sqrt2\) as the order grows for any fixed positive smoothing strength.
