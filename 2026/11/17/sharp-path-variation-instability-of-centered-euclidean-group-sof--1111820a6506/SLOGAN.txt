Mean-preserving Euclidean group soft thresholding is nonexpansive in Hilbert geometry but has the exact path-variation coefficient \(\frac12[1+\sqrt{(4n-6)\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/n}]\) at every positive threshold, growing like \(n/2\). For \(n\ge3\), the coefficient is never attained by finite distinct inputs and drops discontinuously to one at zero threshold.
