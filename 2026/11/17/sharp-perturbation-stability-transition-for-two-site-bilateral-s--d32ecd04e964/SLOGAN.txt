For a normalized two-site bilateral filter, two-input variation nonexpansivity holds exactly when the range-affinity attenuation \(s\mapsto s w(s)/(1+w(s))\) is nondecreasing. Inverse-power affinities \(w(s)=c/[1+(s/\sigma)^p]\) have a sharp stability transition at \(p=1\), with exact amplification \(1+c(p-1)^2/[2p(1+c)]\) for \(p>1\); Gaussian affinities always amplify some perturbations, with sharp gain about \(1.60163893\) at unit coupling.
