## Correctness — PASS
The exact normalized two-cycle ratios supply both population-sum bounds. Strictly positive prey implies both prey coordinates below one. Harmonic-mean bounds force the two diagonal entries of the two-step difference matrix to be positive under \(a\le3\) and \(b+d\le3\); the off-diagonal entries have opposite signs, so its determinant is strictly positive. The only positive two-step state is stationary. The scalar-logistic period-two points have two-step multiplier \(4+2r-r^2\), strictly between \(-1\) and \(1\) when \(3<r<1+\sqrt6\), giving hyperbolic small-coupling counterexamples immediately outside either threshold. All equality and positivity boundaries were checked analytically. The accompanying checker independently verifies the critical algebra and gives positive numerical examples; numerical results do not replace the global proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A sharp global period-two obstruction is structurally stronger than local stability and constrains coexistence oscillations throughout a nontrivial four-parameter family. The two sharp boundaries are not arbitrary sample slices: each is the intrinsic logistic period-doubling value, and hyperbolic attracting cycles reappear immediately beyond either one while the other exclusion condition stays satisfied. The theorem does not classify periods above two or long-term basins.
