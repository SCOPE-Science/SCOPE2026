Twisting the two square latitude rings of an eight-node spherical \(3\)-design changes the volume of its equal-facet-area Minkowski polytope's projection body by exactly a multiple of \(1+\cos d+\sin d\), despite identical normal moments through degree three. The half-step antiprism twist uniquely maximizes this volume within the family, exceeding the aligned-ring value by the sharp factor \((1+\sqrt2)/2\).
