# Verification record

The proof is analytic. Full-scope correctness rests on the spherical-design moment identities, the classical discrete Minkowski theorem, the exact Cauchy support formula, and a complete symbolic partition of all 56 absolute determinants. The proof tracks the surface-area normalization and the closing endpoints explicitly.

Run the included standalone `check.py` with Python 3, NumPy and SciPy. It verifies 224 exact rational determinant instances, checks the 20 spherical monomial moment equations at four twists, and independently computes five 3D convex hull volumes from the 256 signed zonotope endpoint sums. Successful finite checks support but do not replace the proof for all twists.
