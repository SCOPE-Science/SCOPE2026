For the two-atom complex \(\ell_1\) plane considered over the reals, the uniform Steinhaus norm-difference modulus has the sharp quadratic limit \(U_X(d;a)/a^2\to d/(1-d^2/4)\) for every fixed \(0<d<2\). The extremal limiting unit pairs are uniquely balanced coordinate swaps up to natural symmetries, separating complex atomic curvature from the linear nonatomic and real \(\ell_p\) regimes.
