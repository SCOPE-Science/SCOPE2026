For planar Euclidean disks, radial power-weighted perimeter is inclusion-monotone for every nested pair exactly when the exponent \(p>-1\) satisfies \(p\ge0\), with strictness under proper inclusion. The previously unresolved fractional interval \(0<p<1\) follows from a sharp moving-center derivative inequality, while every negative integrable exponent admits nested-disk counterexamples even when both disks contain the origin.
