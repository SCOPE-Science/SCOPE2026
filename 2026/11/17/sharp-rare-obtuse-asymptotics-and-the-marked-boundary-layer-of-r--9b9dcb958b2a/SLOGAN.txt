For three volume-uniform points in a high-dimensional Euclidean ball, the obtuse-triangle probability is asymptotic to \(\frac{\sqrt6}{4}(16/27)^{d/2}\). Conditioned on an obtuse angle, the corresponding vertex has a half-normal squared-radius deficit on the \(d^{-1/2}\) scale, while the other radial deficits and the angular slack have independent exponential limits on the \(d^{-1}\) scale; the complete six-mark convergence holds in total variation.
