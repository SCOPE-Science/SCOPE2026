For cyclic two-tap averaging with independent sitewise probability errors, the exact stability radius for unweighted complete-pair Gini variation is \(\min\{\theta,1-\theta\}/(n-2)\) in every dimension \(n\ge4\). Inside this sharp radius the worst-case Gini decrease per unit input oscillation is exactly \(2[\min\{\theta,1-\theta\}-(n-2)\varepsilon]\); a one-site impulse proves failure immediately beyond it.
