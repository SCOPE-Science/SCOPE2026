Euclidean projection onto a ball has sharp anchored-tree-variation Lipschitz constant \(\bigl(1+\sqrt{n\sum_v(1+d_v)^2}\bigr)/2\), strictly unattained by finite distinct inputs. Among rooted trees, endpoint-rooted paths minimize this distortion at order \(n\), while center-rooted stars maximize it at order \(n^{3/2}/2\).
