For the real planar order-two Sobolev norm that sums the \(L^1\) norms of each distinct derivative once, a rotation through \(\theta\) has exact composition norm \(2|\sin(2\theta)|+|\cos(2\theta)|\). The maximal rotational distortion is \(\sqrt5\), attained away from diagonal angles, and endpoint sharpness depends essentially on Ornstein's \(L^1\) non-inequality.
