Deleting all odd permutation vertices from the Birkhoff polytope changes its Schatten \(p\)-Hausdorff distance by exactly \(2(n-1)^{1/p-1}\), a gap vanishing with dimension for every \(p>1\) but remaining \(2\) in nuclear norm. Every forbidden vertex has an explicit nearest barycenter in the interior of a \(\binom n2-1\)-dimensional exposed simplex face; for \(1<p<\infty\) these are exactly the farthest points.
