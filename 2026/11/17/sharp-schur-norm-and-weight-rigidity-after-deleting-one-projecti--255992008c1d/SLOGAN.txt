Deleting one line from \(\mathrm{PG}(2,q)\), while retaining its points, produces a Schur-idempotent norm given by an exact strictly concave one-variable formula; every norming weight assigns the same nonuniform mass \(p_q\) to the points at infinity. The sharp norm loss relative to the intact incidence mask is \(\tfrac12q^{-3/2}+O(q^{-5/2})\), while uniform weights miss the new optimum by \(\tfrac12q^{-5/2}+O(q^{-7/2})\).
