For the unweighted cycle's Lipschitz polytope, the next Euclidean vertex-radius shell lies a sharp squared distance \(2(m-1)^2/m\) below the outer shell in even order \(2m\), and \(2m(m-1)/(2m+1)\) below it in odd order \(2m+1\). The same formulas give the exact clearance from tangent facets to the nearest non-tangent facets of the polar symmetric edge polytope, together with all equality configurations.
