For the three-colour threshold-two cyclic automaton on \(W_n=K_1\vee C_n\), every orbit freezes for rim lengths \(3\le n\le6\), but a primitive \(3n\)-cycle exists at every \(n\ge7\). The smallest oscillatory wheel has exactly two primitive \(21\)-cycles, and the fixed-state count is \(3(n+2)\) for every \(n\ge3\).
