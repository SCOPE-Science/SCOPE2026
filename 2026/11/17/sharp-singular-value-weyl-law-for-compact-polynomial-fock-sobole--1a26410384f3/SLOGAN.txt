For a compact polynomial generalized integral sum between Gaussian Fock–Sobolev Hilbert spaces, the first nonzero total-degree layer determines **every leading singular-value constant**: if the degree deficit is \(\delta\), then \(s_k\sim\mathcal C^{\delta/2}k^{-\delta/2}\), where \(\mathcal C\) is the angular \(L^{2/\delta}\) mean of the leading coefficient polynomial. This gives the exact Schatten threshold and its weak-endpoint logarithmic residue for genuinely nonorthogonal sums.
