For three independent surface-uniform points on the unit two-sphere, the **ordinary Euclidean area** of their chord triangle has the sharp small-area probability \(\Pr(T\le t)\sim \mathrm B(1/3,3/2)\mathrm B(1/3,1/3)t^{5/3}/(2^{2/3}\pi)\). The fractional exponent comes from a cubic-scale triple collision, and differs from the area law for intrinsic spherical geodesic triangles.
