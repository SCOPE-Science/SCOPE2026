For \(1<p,q<\infty\), the largest norm of averaging onto a measurable atom of area \(t\) in \(L^p(L^q)\) grows as \(C_{p,q}t^{-|1/p-1/q|}\) with an exact explicit coefficient, and the binary conditional-expectation norm has the same leading law. Uniformly bounded atom averages occur exactly at \(p=q\); asymptotically extremizing sets have two vertical-section heights.
