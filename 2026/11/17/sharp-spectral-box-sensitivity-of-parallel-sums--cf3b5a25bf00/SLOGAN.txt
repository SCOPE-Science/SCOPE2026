On the spectral box \(I\preceq A,B\preceq\kappa I\), the sharp one-sided operator-norm Lipschitz constant of the positive-operator parallel sum changes from \((\kappa/(\kappa+1))^2\) to \((\kappa+6+\kappa^{-1})/16\) at \(\kappa=2+\sqrt5\). Real two-dimensional noncommuting matrices are sharp above this threshold, and norm amplification first becomes possible when \(\kappa>5+2\sqrt6\).
