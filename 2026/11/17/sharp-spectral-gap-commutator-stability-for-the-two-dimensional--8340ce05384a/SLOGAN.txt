For Hermitian \(2\times2\) pairs with prescribed spectral half-gaps \(r,s>0\), the scalar-normalized Golden–Thompson defect has exact sharp lower and upper coefficients against \(\|[A,B]\|_{\mathrm F}^2\), given by endpoint second divided differences of \(f(x)=\cosh\sqrt{x}\). Its commutator quotient is strictly increasing with Pauli-vector alignment; the universal best lower constant is \(1/24\), while no scale-free finite upper constant exists.
