For the convex hull of the side-two cube and the six axis points at distance \(h\), the maximum contained tetrahedron determinant switches sharply from \(16\) to \(h^2(h+3)\) at the unique positive root \(h=r\) of \(r^2(r+3)=16\), and finally to \(2h^3\) beyond \(h=3\). The normalized maximum tetrahedron volume has a unique global minimum \(1/(3r)\), strictly before the regular rhombic dodecahedron at \(h=2\); all maximizing tetrahedra for \(1<h<3\) are classified and vertex-supported.
