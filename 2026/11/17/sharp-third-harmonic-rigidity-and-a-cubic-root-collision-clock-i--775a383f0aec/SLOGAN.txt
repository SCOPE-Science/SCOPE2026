For equal-strength three-phase networks with interaction \(G_r(s)=-\cos s+r\cos(3s)\), the entire range \(-1/3\le r\le1/9\) has exactly two period annuli and only synchronous and two splay relative equilibria. At the sharp upper endpoint, collision-to-synchrony relaxation changes from inverse time to cube-root inverse time, while new pair-cluster equilibria appear immediately above it.
