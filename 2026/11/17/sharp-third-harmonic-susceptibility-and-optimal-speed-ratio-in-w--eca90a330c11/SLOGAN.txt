For a pursuer chasing a weakly elliptical evader, the first-order phase-resolved third Fourier harmonic has amplitude \(S(n)\varepsilon\), where \(S(n)=n\sqrt{1-n^2}/(2\sqrt{9-5n^2})\). It is strictly positive for every \(0<n<1\) and has the sharp universal maximum \(1/10\), attained only at the chase-speed ratio \(n=\sqrt{3/5}\).
