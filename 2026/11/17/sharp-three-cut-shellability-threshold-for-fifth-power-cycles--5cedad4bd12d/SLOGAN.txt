The ordinary three-cut complex of the fifth power of a cycle is void through 12 vertices, nonshellable from 13 to 20 vertices, and shellable for every \(n\ge21\). Its homotopy type in the shellable range is exactly \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-10}{2}-54}S^{n-4}\), with the six previously unresolved boundary cases settled by complete facet-by-facet shelling certificates.
