For three equally weighted rotated copies of every regular Reuleaux odd-\(n\)-gon, the Minkowski mean has uniquely maximal area when the rotations are equally spaced modulo its rotational symmetry; the optimal mean remains strictly noncircular. The exact disk-area deficit is \(w^2[n(\sin c-2\sin(c/3))/(6\cos c)-\pi/36]\), where \(c=\pi/(2n)\), and the optimizer has a positive quadratic phase-stability coefficient.
