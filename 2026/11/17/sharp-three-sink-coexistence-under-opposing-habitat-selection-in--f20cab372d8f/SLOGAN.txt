In a symmetric two-patch replicator with strictly opposing local dominance, both pure population states and a spatially patterned mixed state are simultaneous hyperbolic sinks exactly when \(\max\{b/2,C_1\}<c<C_0\), with explicit analytic thresholds. The extra mixed-state stability restriction becomes unnecessary precisely at \(b/d\ge9/4\); no global basin classification is asserted.
