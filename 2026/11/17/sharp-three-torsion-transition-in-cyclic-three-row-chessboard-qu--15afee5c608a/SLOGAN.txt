Under free threefold rotation of the **rows**, the three-row chessboard quotient changes from a two-circle wedge at three columns to a torus at four, then has fundamental group \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) and free \(H_2\) of rank \(n(n-2)(n-4)/3-1\) for every \(n\ge5\). A direct integer orbit-cell presentation proves that the order-three first-homology torsion appears sharply at five columns.
