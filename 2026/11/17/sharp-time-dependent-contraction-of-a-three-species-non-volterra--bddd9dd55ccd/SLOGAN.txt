The three-species quadratic population flow has the exact, globally optimal time-\(t\) Euclidean contraction factor \(16e^{2t}/(1+3e^{2t})^2\), for every \(t\ge0\). The worst-case pairs approach a simplex vertex along a median, and the effective contraction rate improves from \(1\) initially to \(2\) asymptotically.
