# Scientific review: sharp total-variation Cauchy law

## Correctness

**PASS.** The conserved energy and exact time density are derived from the printed cubic vector field in original physical time. Normalization against the limiting Cauchy measure gives an exact likelihood ratio, and the first-order signed density convergence is proved in ordinary \(L^1\), including the moving turning-point singularity. The cut at half the energy controls both the singular endpoint and the exponential tail. The logarithmic moment is \(2\log2\), and the absolute first-order correction is a positive Clausen constant. A standard-library checker independently evaluates the finite-density integrals at five energies; it is a diagnostic, not a substitute for the proof.

## Originality

**PASS within inspected material.** Llibre–Rondón 2023, Theorem 2(g), and Appis–Llibre 2024, Theorem 4(c), provide the already-known global-center setting. The closest published occupation result, *Cauchy time concentration and the order-one moment threshold in a cubic global center*, gives the same exact density and weak limit but explicitly does not claim a quantitative convergence rate. Its full theorem, proof and limitations were compared with the present first-order signed total-variation limit. A separate published sharp total-variation result concerns a different symmetric cubic field and a different logarithmic coordinate, and neither implies this Clausen-rate statement. Broader qualitative period results do not give the new occupation-error law. Search incompleteness and unpublished equivalent estimates remain risks.

## Value

**PASS.** For this natural high-energy global-center family the established Cauchy law describes only a weak asymptotic; a sharp total-variation rate is a substantial strengthening because it uniformly controls probabilities of all measurable events and errors of all bounded observables, including amplitude-dependent sets. The leading constant is universal in \(\kappa\), strictly positive, and expressed through a classical special function. This is a quantitative dynamical invariant, not a small numerical improvement or a restatement of the known moment thresholds.

## Limitations

This is a single cubic subfamily with fixed positive coefficient; no perturbation stability, second-order correction, higher-dimensional analog or uniform coefficient-boundary limit follows. The exact time density and weak Cauchy convergence are prior work and are not being relabelled as discoveries. Mathematical novelty is relative to the inspected primary paper and indexed published records. An independent mathematical audit has not been carried out.
