A non-symmetric cubic nilpotent global center has a shifted Cauchy physical-time occupation law at high energy, with sharp total-variation error \(\sqrt{4\alpha-\gamma^2}/(\sqrt2\pi\alpha^{3/2}\sqrt E)+O(\log E/E)\). The dominant error is exactly the limiting law's mass beyond the current turning points, and the original-time period obeys \(\sqrt E\,T(E)\to2\sqrt2\pi/\sqrt{4\alpha-\gamma^2}\).
