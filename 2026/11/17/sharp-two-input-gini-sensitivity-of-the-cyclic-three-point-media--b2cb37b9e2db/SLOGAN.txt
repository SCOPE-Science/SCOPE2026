The cyclic three-point median has sharp two-input complete-pair Gini amplification \(0\) on three sites, \(4/3\) on four sites, and \(3(n-3)/(n-1)\) on every \(n\ge5\) sites. Each nonzero sharp coefficient is attained by changing a single input sample, and the finite-size coefficients tend to the unattained envelope \(3\).
