For nonnegative signals on a complete graph, the centered maximal operator has the sharp two-input variation modulus \(\frac12\) at two vertices and \(1+\lfloor(n-2)^2/4\rfloor/[n(n-1)]\) for every \(n\ge3\). Although each signal's variation contracts, differences between maximal outputs are amplified exactly when \(n\ge4\), with worst-case amplification approaching \(5/4\).
