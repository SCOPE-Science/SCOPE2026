The exact two-input edge-variation modulus of uncentered maximal averaging on a \(k\)-leaf star is \(1/2,1,5/4\) for \(k=1,2,3\), respectively, and \(k/2\) for all \(k\ge4\). Explicit nonnegative pairs attain every value, and the sharp perturbation modulus is strictly larger than the corresponding one-input variation norm for all \(k\ge2\).
