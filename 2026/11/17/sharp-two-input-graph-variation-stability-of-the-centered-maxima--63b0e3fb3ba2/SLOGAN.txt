For the centered Hardy–Littlewood maximal function on the nonnegative cone of a star with \(m\) leaves, the exact two-input graph-total-variation Lipschitz constant is \(2m/(m+1)\) for \(m\ge2\) and \(1/2\) for \(m=1\), despite the known single-input variation contraction. Allowing signed inputs in the standard absolute-value maximal function destroys every finite two-input variation bound.
