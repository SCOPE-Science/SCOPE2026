For every radius \(r\ge1\), a centered running median on a clamped finite path of length at least \(2r+2\) has exact two-input adjacent-variation amplification \(2r+1\), attained by an explicit interleaved-rank baseline and a single-step perturbation. The length \(2r+2\) is the sharp first size at which this full window-width amplification is possible.
