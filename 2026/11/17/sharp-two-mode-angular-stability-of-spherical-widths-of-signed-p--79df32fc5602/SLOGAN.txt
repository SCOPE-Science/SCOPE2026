The Euclidean spherical-width landscape of the standard type-\(B\) signed permutohedron has exactly \(3^n-1\) strict local minima and \(2^n n!\) strict local maxima, with no other extrema. At a minimum with \(k\) active coordinates, the exact sharp small-angle slope changes from active-coordinate splitting to previously zero-coordinate activation precisely at \(k=2(n-k)\); the complete attained angular-width formula holds in every dimension.
