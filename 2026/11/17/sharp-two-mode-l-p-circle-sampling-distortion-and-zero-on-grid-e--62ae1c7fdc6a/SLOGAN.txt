For the complex two-mode circle space \(\operatorname{span}\{1,z\}\) and every \(1<p<2\), equal-weight sampling at any regular \(m\)-gon has an exact sharp relative \(L^p\)-moment error given by a convergent Gamma series, decaying with sharp order \(m^{-(p+1)}\). Every worst-case polynomial has equal-magnitude coefficients and a zero at a sample, and every such worst case is an underestimate.
