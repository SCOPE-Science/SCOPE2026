In the saturating strong-Allee predator–prey flow with \(sh<1\), the maximal number of positive coexistence equilibria is sharply two under genuine cooperation \(\lambda<Ab\), one at \(\lambda=Ab\), and three in the inhibitory continuation \(\lambda>Ab\). A rational three-state example has a repelling node, saddle, and attracting focus, persisting under parameter perturbation; its hunting increment is negative, not cooperative.
