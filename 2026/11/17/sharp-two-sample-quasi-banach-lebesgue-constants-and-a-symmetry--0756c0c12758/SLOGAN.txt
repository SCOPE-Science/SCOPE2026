For two samples of the one-dimensional Laplace kernel, the sharp \(\ell^p\) coefficient Lebesgue factor for \(0<p<1\) switches at the exact separation \(2\operatorname{arctanh}\sqrt{1-p}\) from a unique midpoint maximizer to two off-center maximizers, with a sharp square-root onset. At \(p=1/2\), both branches and the optimizer locations have explicit hyperbolic-function formulas; at large spacing the factor exceeds one by an exactly quantified exponentially small amount.
