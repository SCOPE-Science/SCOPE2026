For every real \(p>2\), equal positive weights on two perpendicular directions uniquely minimize the worst planar power-Cauchy brightness error, with sharp angular discrepancy \(\max\{1/2-c_p,c_p-2^{-p/2}\}\). Remarkably, the optimizer never changes, but its dominant signed error reverses at exactly \(p=4\) and \(p=6\); \(p=2\) is the previously known exact quadratic endpoint.
