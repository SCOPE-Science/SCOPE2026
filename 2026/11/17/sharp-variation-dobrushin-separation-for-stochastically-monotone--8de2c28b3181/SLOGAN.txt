For every stochastically monotone Markov matrix on an ordered \(n\)-state space, its ordered-function-variation norm \(\beta\) and Dobrushin oscillation coefficient \(\delta\) satisfy the sharp inequality \(\beta\le\delta\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\beta\). Both endpoints are achieved by strictly positive kernels; \(n=4\) is the first dimension where the coefficients can differ.
