The bi-infinite Hodrick–Prescott quadratic smoother has an exact, step-attained total-variation amplification exceeding one at every positive smoothing strength, even though its frequency response is low-pass. Its sharp gain decreases between \(\lambda=1/16\) and \(\lambda=1/9\), but tends at strong smoothing to \(1+\sqrt2 e^{-3\pi/4}/(1-e^{-\pi})\approx1.140093467\).
