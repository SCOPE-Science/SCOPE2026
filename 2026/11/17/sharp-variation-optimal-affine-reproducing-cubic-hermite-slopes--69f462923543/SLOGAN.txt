For affine-reproducing cubic Hermite interpolants with fixed convex blends of adjacent secants on alternating cell lengths \(a,b>0\), the best attainable sharp total-variation amplification is \(1+16ab/[27(a^2+b^2)]\), even when all knot blend weights are allowed to vary independently. Alternating mesh contrast can drive this optimal gain arbitrarily close to one, with an explicit squared-length knot-weight design.
