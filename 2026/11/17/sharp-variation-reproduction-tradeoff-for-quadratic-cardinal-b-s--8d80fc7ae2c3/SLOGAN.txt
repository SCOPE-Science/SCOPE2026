The centered quadratic cardinal B-spline quasi-interpolant with nonnegative second-difference correction \(a\) has exact sharp total-variation gain \(1+2a+2a^2/(1+3a)\), always attained by a single step. Reproducing quadratics uniquely forces \(a=1/8\) and the unavoidable sharp gain \(14/11\), while variation nonexpansiveness holds only without correction.
