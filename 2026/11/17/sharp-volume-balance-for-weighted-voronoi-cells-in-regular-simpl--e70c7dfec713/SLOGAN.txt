For a regular \(d\)-simplex, the vertex-weighted power diagram with normalized weights \(w_i=-2b_i\) (or \(w_i=-L^2b_i\) at edge length \(L\)), where \(b\) ranges over interior barycentric points, has exact polynomial cell volumes and a cubical face structure in every dimension. At fixed \(b_i=t\), the volume of cell \(i\) strictly increases when the other weights are balanced and ranges sharply between \((1-t)^d/d\) (unattained) and \((d+1)^{d-1}(1-t)^d/d^d\) (uniquely attained); the ratio of these constants tends to \(e\).
