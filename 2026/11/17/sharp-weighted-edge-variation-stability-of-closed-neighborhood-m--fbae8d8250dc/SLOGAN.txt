The closed-neighborhood median filter on a \(k\)-leaf star has an exact, attained two-input weighted edge-variation coefficient determined only by the total edge weight, its smallest edge weight, and the parity of \(k\). For uniform weights it jumps between the laws \(k-1/2\) (even \(k\)) and \((k-1)/2\) (odd \(k\ge3\)); among nontrivial stars, two-input variation nonexpansivity survives only for three equally weighted leaves.
