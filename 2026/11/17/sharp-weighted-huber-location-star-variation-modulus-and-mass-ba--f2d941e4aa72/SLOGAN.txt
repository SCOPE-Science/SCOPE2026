For weighted Huber interpolation of the hub of a real star, the exact two-input edge-variation amplification is \(1+(m-2)Q(w)\), with \(Q(w)\) determined by an explicit signed-weight feasibility test. Equal weights give \(m/2\) for even \(m\), but arbitrarily small one-weight increases raise that coefficient to \(m-1\) whenever \(m\ge4\) is even.
