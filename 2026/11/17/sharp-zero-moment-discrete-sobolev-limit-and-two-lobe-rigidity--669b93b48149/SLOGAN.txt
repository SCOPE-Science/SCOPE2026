Imposing the single zero-sum constraint halves the asymptotically sharp real discrete first-difference \(\ell^1\)-to-\(\ell^2\) Sobolev constant: \(K_d/(d+1)^{3/2}\to1/\sqrt{48}\). Every asymptotically extremizing sequence, after energy normalization and linear interpolation, converges up to sign to two adjacent opposite parabolic lobes; explicit finite-dimensional bounds accompany the limit.
