## Correctness — PASS
The geometric realization is triangulated by normalized edge-orbit barycenters, not by merely counting setwise fixed original faces. The two-sheet lift of every base cycle is cyclic. Conversely each base forest lifts as two disjoint trees per component; zero or one diagonal edge per component preserves acyclicity, and two diagonals create a cycle. These are exactly the graphic-forest conditions after adding the apex. Standard coloopless graphic-matroid shellability gives the two integral sphere wedges. The signed reduced Lefschetz number gives the top-homology involution trace because the original complex has no other reduced homology. Rational transfer gives the quotient statement. The included exact exhaustive finite checks independently compare forest conditions and oriented-simplex signs in their stated ranges; they are supporting checks, not a proof for all \(n\).
