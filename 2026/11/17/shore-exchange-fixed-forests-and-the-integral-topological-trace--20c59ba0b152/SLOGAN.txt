The simultaneous exchange of the two shores of \(K_{n,n}\) turns the geometric fixed locus of its forest complex into the forest complex of \(K_{n+1}\), exactly and PL-equivariantly. This identifies every fixed-space Betti number and gives the involution's exact top-homology trace \((-1)^{n-1}T_{K_{n+1}}(0,1)\) for all \(n\ge2\).
