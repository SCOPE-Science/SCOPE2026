For a two-parameter cubic global center, the positive-half-plane Euclidean area is strictly smaller than its negative counterpart at **every** energy exactly when the cubic asymmetry coefficient is positive. The physical-time difference between positive and negative positions has the sharp high-energy constant \(-2\sqrt{2/k}\operatorname{artanh}(a/\sqrt k)\), and the Euclidean area difference has twice that constant after natural scaling. Total periods cannot distinguish the reflected coefficients \(a\) and \(-a\), but the leading total-period scale together with this signed difference recovers both coefficients exactly.
