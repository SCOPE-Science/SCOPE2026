Among all positive edge weightings of a finite path, increasing sorting has the smallest possible two-input total-variation Lipschitz factor exactly for the unique sine profile \(w_j\propto\sin(j\pi/n)\). The optimal factor is \(\cot(\pi/(2n))\) for even \(n\) and \(\csc(\pi/(2n))\) for odd \(n\), improving the uniform-weight factor \(n-1\) by an asymptotic ratio of \(2/\pi\).
