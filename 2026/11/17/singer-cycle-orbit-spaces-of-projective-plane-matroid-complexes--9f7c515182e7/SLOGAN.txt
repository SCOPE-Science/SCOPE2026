For every prime power \(q\ge2\), the ordinary Singer-cycle quotient of the rank-three projective-plane matroid independence complex has cyclic fundamental group of order \((q^2+q+1)/3\) when \(q\equiv1\pmod3\), and order \(q^2+q+1\) otherwise. Its complete integral homology is torsion-free in degree two with an explicit rank formula, while degree-one torsion records a unique threefold stabilizer orbit in the exceptional congruence class.
