For the standard two-row generalized Pitman–Stanley polytope with \(m\ge2\) columns, every ridge has one of six exact Euclidean dihedral-angle values, with fully determined multiplicities. Exactly \(6m-3\) of its \((9m^2-m+2)/2\) ridges have nonright angles, so the right-angle proportion approaches one at the sharp rate \(1-4/(3m)+O(m^{-2})\).
