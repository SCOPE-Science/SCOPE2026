Deleting any one edge of the \(3\times4\) grid leaves an ordinary matching complex homotopy equivalent to a wedge of five, seven, or eight three-spheres, depending on one of six edge orbits. For every deletion, the intact nine-sphere wedge splits up to homotopy as the deleted-edge complex plus the suspension of its exact link, so the loss of integral third homology is one, two, or four free generators.
