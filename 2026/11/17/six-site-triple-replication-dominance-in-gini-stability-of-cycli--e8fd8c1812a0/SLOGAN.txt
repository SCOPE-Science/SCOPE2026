The two-site opening \((O_nx)_i=\min\{x_i,\max(x_{i-1},x_{i+1})\}\) has sharp two-input Gini amplification \(0,1,4/3,3/2\) at \(n=2,3,4,5\), and \(3(n-3)/(n-1)\) for every \(n\ge6\). The first amplification occurs at four sites, and threefold replication becomes strictly dominant over twofold replication at six sites, yielding a sharp asymptotic ceiling of \(3\).
