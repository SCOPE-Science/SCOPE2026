For three independently arclength-uniform ellipse-boundary points conditioned to span nearly the largest possible triangle, the labelled first point has a limiting eccentric-anomaly density proportional to \(\sqrt{1-3q^2/4-q^3\cos(6\theta)/4}\), where \(q=(a^2-b^2)/(a^2+b^2)\). Its sixth Fourier moment decreases strictly from zero to \(-1/3\), determines the ellipse's axis ratio, and is independent of the universal uniform-ellipse limiting local shape; the unmarked rare-area coefficient is known from prior work.
