For the noncrystallographic type-\(H_4\) Coxeter group, the ordinary quotient of the proper noncrossing-partition order complex by Coxeter-element conjugation is exactly \(\bigvee^{16}S^2\). Two exceptional rank-two orbit lengths, \(3\) and \(5\), kill the quotient fundamental group, while the quotient's Euler characteristic is \(17\).
