For complex matrices with at most \(d\) nonzero entries per row, mapping \(\ell_p\) into \(\ell_t\) with \(0<p,t<1\), boundedness and absolute summability have separate exact column-norm thresholds. The summability threshold \(\ell_{t/(1-t)}\) depends only on the target exponent, while every bounded such operator is compact when \(p>t\); cancellation of arbitrary complex phases cannot defeat either classification.
