Two geodesic arcs joining four independent area-uniform points in a hemisphere have an explicitly evaluated, pole-biased crossing-location density: in gnomonic coordinates it is \(2\log(1+r^2)/(\pi^3r^2(1+r^2)^{3/2})\). The known scalar crossing probability \(1-8/\pi^2\) is recovered by integration, but the spatial law also describes where crossings occur.
