For the Euclidean sum of a \(d\)-cube and a coordinate crosspolytope, almost all boundary area lies on facets with normal support near \(\sqrt d\), and their support rank has Gaussian fluctuations of order \(d^{1/4}\). Yet a uniformly counted facet has support near \(2d/3\); a subexponential fraction of facets carries asymptotically the entire surface area.
