For every integer \(n\ge41\), the directed cyclic graph with forward steps \(\{1,9,10,13,15,17,20\}\) has \(H_1^{\mathrm{path}}\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\). Six exact short-addition relations exhibit the stable order-two class integrally, beyond the previously published arbitrary-field models.
