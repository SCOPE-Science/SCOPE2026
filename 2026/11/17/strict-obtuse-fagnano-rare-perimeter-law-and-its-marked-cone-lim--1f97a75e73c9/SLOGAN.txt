For three independent side-uniform points in any fixed strictly obtuse triangle, the probability that their inscribed perimeter is within \(t\) of twice the shortest altitude is asymptotic to an explicit positive constant times \(t^{5/2}\). Conditioned on this rare event, the two distances from the obtuse vertex shrink linearly while the third point's transverse displacement shrinks at square-root scale, with a precise uniform cone-body limit and independent beta-distributed geometric markings.
