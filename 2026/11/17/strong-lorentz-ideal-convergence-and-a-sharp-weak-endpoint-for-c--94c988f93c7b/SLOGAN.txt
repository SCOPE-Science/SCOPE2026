Every compact generalized Aluthge orbit in a finite-index Schatten--Lorentz ideal has singular values converging in that same Lorentz quasi-norm to the eigenvalue moduli; little weak ideals retain the conclusion. The full weak ideal is a sharp failure boundary: a power-decaying quasinilpotent shift retains weak quasi-norm \(1\) through every iterate even as its operator norm tends to zero.
