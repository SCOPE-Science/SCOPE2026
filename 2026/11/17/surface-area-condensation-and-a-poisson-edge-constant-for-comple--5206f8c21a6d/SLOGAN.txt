For canonical Euclidean graphical zonotopes of complete split graphs \(K_p\vee\overline{K_q}\), \(p\ge3\), exactly \(2q\) facets capture the fraction \(1-A_pq^{-3/2}+O_p(q^{-5/2})\) of all surface area even though their proportion among facets tends to zero exponentially. The exact facet-area spectrum is explicit, and the leading coefficients satisfy \(A_p/p^{3/2}\to\sum_{j\ge1}e^{-j}j^{j-3/2}/j!\approx0.6186033\).
