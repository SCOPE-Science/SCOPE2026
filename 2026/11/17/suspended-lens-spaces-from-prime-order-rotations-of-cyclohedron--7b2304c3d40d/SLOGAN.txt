Every prime-order rotation quotient of a cycle graph-associahedron boundary, for an odd prime at least five, is a concrete iterated suspension of a generalized lens space. When the cycle has \(kp\) vertices, its first nonzero homotopy group is \(\pi_k\cong\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\), and its integral torsion occurs in precisely the degrees \(k,k+2,\ldots,kp-4\).
