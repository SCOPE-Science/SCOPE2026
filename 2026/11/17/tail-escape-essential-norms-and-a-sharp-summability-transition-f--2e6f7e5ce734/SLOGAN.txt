For arbitrary positive weights, the essential norm of a multiplier on the nonseparable **big** tree Lipschitz space is exactly the surviving norm of its weighted-difference rows after deleting finite input and output prefixes. For polynomial weights \(c_v=|v|^\alpha\), compactness has a sharp summability transition at \(\alpha=1\): above it, decay of the symbol alone suffices, and a compact big-space multiplier need not preserve the little space.
