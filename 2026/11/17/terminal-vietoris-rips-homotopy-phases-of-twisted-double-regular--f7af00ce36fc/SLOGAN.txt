The last noncontractible Euclidean Vietoris–Rips complex of two parallel, relatively rotated regular \(n\)-gons has a complete twist–height phase diagram: its forbidden-diameter graph is a matching, a cycle, disjoint squares, or a Möbius ladder. At the exact even-sided critical height \(h=2R\sin(\pi t/n)\), the terminal homotopy type can drop from an \((n-1)\)-sphere to an \((n/2-1)\)-sphere, and at the half-step twist a wedge of three \((n/2-1)\)-spheres appears when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\).
