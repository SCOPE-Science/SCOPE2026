Three uniform points on a spherical latitude circle have all intrinsic angles below \(120^\circ\) with probability \(4\cos^2\theta/\pi^2+o(\cos^2\theta)\) as the circle approaches the equator, and the exact probability is strictly increasing with latitude height. Conditioned on this rare event, a single tiny angular gap and two near-antipodal gaps have an explicit triangular uniform marked limit in total variation.
