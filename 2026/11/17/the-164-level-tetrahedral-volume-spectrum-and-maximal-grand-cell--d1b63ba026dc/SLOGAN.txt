The regular unit-circumradius 600-cell has exactly 164 intrinsic squared-volume levels across its \(8{,}214{,}570\) four-vertex tetrahedra. The greatest volume of any contained tetrahedron is \(\varphi^3/(6\sqrt2)\), attained at precisely 600 vertex-supported regular tetrahedra of edge \(\varphi\), the classical grand 600-cell cells; the full exact spectrum and a positive gap to the next vertex-supported volume are supplied.
