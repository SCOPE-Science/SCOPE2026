# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim fixes independent arclength-uniform circle endpoints, conditions on chord **segments** crossing, and uses the acute line angle. The crossing pairing is the unique alternating pairing of four distinct circle points, so its probability is \(1/3\). Marking one sampled endpoint before forming ordered gaps produces the correct \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,1,1,1)\) vector (using the smallest absolute polar angle would be wrong). Aggregation yields the known beta angle law and independent uniform splits. Directly solving the two line equations gives the exact pointwise factorization in `RESULT.md`; product laws, the CDF integral, and moments follow by conditioning. A nonsingular quadratic expansion around the unique central preimage gives the small-radius coefficient; product-density logarithmic asymptotics give the boundary coefficient. All angle and scale domains and fixed-angle limits are stated. The standalone `verify.py` completed 3057 independent geometry, quadrature, and sampling checks, but no finite check proves the infinite-parameter theorem. No unsupported strict monotonicity is asserted.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)

The motivating Bortolotto–Souza theorem computes the **unconditional** crossing-radius marginal, including exterior line intersections. A marginal cannot determine the joint law with crossing angle. The closely related 2026 published conditional-area record was read in full at `RESULT.md`: it works with the same independent circular gap splits but computes a **sum** of cosine variables for the cyclic quadrilateral's area. The new radial deficiency is a **product** of shifted cosines; the radial conditional distribution and its two sharp angle-dependent tails are absent from that published statement and do not follow from its area marginal alone. Its proof coordinates provide an efficient starting point, and this overlap is disclosed rather than concealed. A separate 2025 random-chord extremal paper studies different probability optimizations and does not state this conditional law in its examined principal results. Actual targeted published-finding corpus searches and own accessible ledger claims found no equivalent or stronger statement. Search failure alone does not prove historical novelty; unindexed literature and easy derivability from established independent-gap variables remain residual priority risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Conditional crossing geometry is a natural refinement of the established dilogarithmic marginal. The result determines an entire *joint* angular–radial distribution, not an arbitrary isolated numerical moment: its independent-product normal form gives the conditional CDF and every polynomial moment, while its quadratic center and logarithmic boundary laws identify two different local geometric mechanisms with explicit angle-sensitive coefficients. This is useful for conditioned chord-intersection statistics and weighted incidence questions, and is not merely a reparametrization of the previously known area or unconditional-radius formulas.
