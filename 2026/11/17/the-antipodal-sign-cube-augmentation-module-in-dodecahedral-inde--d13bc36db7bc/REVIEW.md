## Mathematical value
PASS. A canonical twenty-vertex graph has a natural five-element geometric symmetry set, while its ordinary fifth Betti number is only four. Identifying its homology as the *signed augmentation* of the five cubes explains an exceptional irreducible icosahedral module rather than merely repeating a Betti count. The antipodal sign distinguishes the full group from its rotational subgroup and gives a sharp quotient contrast: one cube-stabilizer quotient retains a rational top class, whereas adding inversion removes it. The exact representation, not a formal consequence of the known wedge type, is the motivated object of study.
