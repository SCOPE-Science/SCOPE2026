## Mathematical value — PASS

The three-cube is the first three-dimensional hypercube, with a canonical, nonabelian, 48-element symmetry group. A four-dimensional Betti number does not distinguish the translation parity line from the axial-vector irreducible. The central antipodal inversion fixes three classes even though full cube symmetry fixes none, giving a natural, testable geometric difference between quotient spaces. The complete character is a reusable boundary datum for cubical graph-complex symmetry calculations, not just a recomputation of the known sphere count. No claim is made of a family theorem or integral splitting.
