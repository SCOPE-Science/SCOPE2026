# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The one-third truncation has exactly 60 vertices, represented as \((2u+v)/2\) for oriented edges of the regular side-two icosahedron. A tetrahedron volume is the absolute determinant of three doubled-coordinate differences divided by 48. All coefficients lie in the explicitly implemented ordered field \(\mathbb Z[\varphi]\). The attached checker exactly enumerated all \(\binom{60}{4}=487635\) unordered vertex quadruples, verified every one of the 381 rows and their frequencies, and separately established the Cauchy–Binet squared-volume identity. The first two levels have exactly 120 elements apiece and the exact gap is \(\varphi/12\). The six-distance congruence signatures were verified for each of those levels. Finite exhaustion is valid here because the claim's quantified set is finite, not because experiments establish an infinite theorem.

Those results are explicitly not claimed as new. They do not publish the complete 381-level volume-frequency distribution or identify the sharp second level and its exact congruence class.
