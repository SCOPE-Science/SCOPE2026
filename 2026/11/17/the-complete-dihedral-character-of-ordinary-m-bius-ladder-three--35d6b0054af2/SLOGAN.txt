For every Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) with \(n\ge5\), the ordinary three-cut complex has a completely determined rational homology character under its natural dihedral symmetries: nonidentity rotations have trace \((-1)^a\), and the reflection traces obey four explicit parity formulas. The subspace invariant under all dihedral symmetries has dimension exactly \(\lfloor(n-3)/2\rfloor\), revealing a symmetry refinement not determined by its previously known sphere-wedge homotopy type.
