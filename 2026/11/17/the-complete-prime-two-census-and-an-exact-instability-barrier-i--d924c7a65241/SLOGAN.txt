In the axially symmetric three-dimensional quadratic map, the number of primitive real two-cycles is exactly \(\mathbf1_{\mu<-(1+b)}+2\mathbf1_{\mu<-2c(1+b)/(1-c)}\). The two symmetry-related off-antipodal cycles are always unstable because their two-step Jacobian has \(\det(I-K)<0\); the two birth thresholds exchange order at \(c=1/3\).
