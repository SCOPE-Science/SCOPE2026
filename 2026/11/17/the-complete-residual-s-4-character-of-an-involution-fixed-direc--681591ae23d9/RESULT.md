# The complete residual \(S_4\)-character of an involution-fixed directed-forest circle bouquet

## Finding
Let \(D_6\) be the complete loopless bidirected graph on six labeled vertices and \(\Delta(D_6)\) its simplicial complex of directed forests: a face is a set of arcs with at most one outgoing arc at each vertex and no directed cycle. Let \(\tau=(0\,1)(2\,3)(4\,5)\) relabel the original vertices. Its geometric fixed space \(X=|\Delta(D_6)|^{\langle\tau\rangle}\) is a connected graph with twelve vertices and thirty-six edges, hence \(X\simeq\bigvee^{25} S^1\).

The centralizer \(C_{S_6}(\tau)\cong C_2\wr S_3\) acts on \(X\); the central subgroup generated by \(\tau\) acts pointwise trivially. The resulting faithful residual action is the full affine group \(\operatorname{AGL}(2,2)\cong S_4\). Its integral first homology is the explicit signed graph-cycle lattice \(\ker(\partial:\mathbb Z^{36}\to\mathbb Z^{12})\), and its rational character, in conjugacy types \((1^4),(2,1^2),(2^2),(3,1),(4)\) of \(S_4\), is
\[
\boxed{\chi_{H_1}=(25,-1,-3,1,1).}
\]
Equivalently, using the partition labels for the five irreducible rational \(S_4\)-modules,
\[
\boxed{H_1(X;\mathbb Q)\cong V_{(4)}\oplus V_{(1^4)}\oplus 3V_{(3,1)}\oplus4V_{(2,1,1)}\oplus V_{(2,2)}.}
\]
Exactly one rational first-homology class is invariant under all residual symmetries; hence \(H_1(X/S_4;\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Q\) and all higher rational homology vanishes.

## Assumptions and scope
The directed-forest complex is the ordinary Chari–Joswig discrete-Morse complex of the one-dimensional complete graph, with arrows reversed if necessary to impose the explicit **outdegree-at-most-one** convention. The fixed space means points fixed by the entire order-two subgroup, not merely the set of invariant simplices. Edge actions on oriented chains retain their orientation signs. All statements concern exactly the six-vertex graph and its stated fixed involution and centralizer; they do not assert a general normalizer-character formula or an integral homotopy equivalence with its quotient.

## Proof
**1. Actual fixed graph.** Every point fixed by \(\tau\) has equal barycentric coefficients on the two members of every orbit of arc-vertices. Its carrier is a simplex precisely when each selected entire arc orbit is a directed forest. Three arc orbits stay within the individual vertex pairs and each forms a directed two-cycle, so they are forbidden. Every other arc orbit is indexed uniquely by a triple \((i,j,b)\), where \(i,j\in\{0,1,2\}\), \(i\ne j\), and \(b\in\mathbb F_2\), and represents both arcs \((i,t)\to(j,t+b)\), \(t\in\mathbb F_2\). There are twelve admissible orbit vertices. Two such orbit vertices \((i,j,b)\) and \((u,v,c)\) are compatible precisely when their source indices are distinct (\(i\ne u\)) and they do not point in opposite quotient directions (not simultaneously \(i=v\) and \(j=u\)). In the opposite-direction case the lifted graph has a directed cycle of length two or four, for either phase; otherwise the two orbit unions form a directed forest. No three orbit vertices form a simplex because each would require a distinct source among three, producing an outdegree-one directed graph on three orbit indices, necessarily containing a directed cycle. Thus the actual fixed-space barycentric-orbit triangulation is a graph \(Y\) with twelve vertices and thirty-six edges. The explicit edge description connects all twelve vertices, as independently witnessed by a spanning tree. Hence \(H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{36-12+1}=\mathbb Z^{25}\), with no higher homology, and \(Y\simeq\bigvee^{25}S^1\). The barycentric-orbit map is a PL homeomorphism \(|Y|\to X\).

**2. Residual group.** Every permutation commuting with \(\tau\) permutes the three vertex pairs and may independently reverse each pair. Write its coordinates as \((\pi,f)\in S_3\ltimes\mathbb F_2^3\). On fixed-graph vertices it acts by
\[
(i,j,b)\longmapsto (\pi(i),\pi(j),b+f_i+f_j),
\]
where additions are modulo two. The diagonal shift \((1,1,1)\) acts trivially, and the residual group has twenty-four elements. It permutes the four points of \(\mathbb F_2^3/\langle(1,1,1)\rangle\) faithfully by affine transformations, identifying it with \(\operatorname{AGL}(2,2)\cong S_4\). Since graph vertices and edges are original arc-orbit vertices and compatible pairs, this is a genuine action on \(Y\), not merely on its homology.

**3. Exact character.** Orient each of the thirty-six graph edges by a fixed ordering of its endpoints. For every group element \(g\), the graph chain complex over \(\mathbb Q\) is exact except in dimensions zero and one, and \(Y\) is connected. Therefore the trace identity is
\[
\operatorname{tr}(g\mid H_1)=1-\operatorname{tr}(g\mid C_0)+\operatorname{tr}(g\mid C_1).
\]
The degree-zero trace counts fixed orbit-vertices. In degree one, a fixed edge contributes \(+1\) if \(g\) preserves its chosen orientation, \(-1\) if it reverses endpoints, and zero otherwise. Directly applying the formula of Step 2 to all twenty-four elements gives the following class table, with columns (cycle type, number of elements, fixed vertices, oriented-edge trace, homology trace):

| Cycle type | Size | \(\operatorname{tr} C_0\) | \(\operatorname{tr} C_1\) | \(\operatorname{tr} H_1\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \((1^4)\) | 1 | 12 | 36 | 25 |
| \((2,1^2)\) | 6 | 0 | −2 | −1 |
| \((2^2)\) | 3 | 4 | 0 | −3 |
| \((3,1)\) | 8 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| \((4)\) | 6 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

Scalar products with the five standard irreducible character rows of \(S_4\) give multiplicities \((1,1,3,4,1)\). Character orthogonality proves the asserted rational representation decomposition. The sum of the character values over all twenty-four elements is twenty-four; the multiplicity of the trivial representation is therefore one. For a finite group acting on a finite polyhedron, rational homology of its quotient is the invariant part of rational homology (by averaging on an equivariant subdivision). It follows that the geometric quotient has first rational Betti number one. It is connected and has no homology above degree one because \(Y\) is one-dimensional.

## Verification
The standalone Python 3 program `verify_s4.py` reconstructs all thirty original directed arcs and their fifteen involution-orbits, independently tests the directed-forest condition, and obtains the twelve orbit vertices and thirty-six orbit-edge faces. It constructs an eleven-edge spanning tree, and hence twenty-five fundamental cycles forming an integral cycle basis. All twenty-four residual group elements are generated as signed pair permutations; all twenty-four induce distinct permutations of the four affine points. For each element, the first-homology trace is computed **independently in two ways**: the signed Lefschetz chain formula and direct action on the explicit fundamental-cycle basis. Their values agree element-by-element, giving the class table and integer multiplicities displayed above. The machine-checkable result is `DIRECTED_FOREST_FIXED_S4_CHARACTER_PASS`. The finite calculation is exhaustive for this finite six-vertex theorem; it does not purport to prove a formula for arbitrary graph sizes.

## Relationship to prior work
Kozlov initiated directed-tree complexes, and Jojić established shellability and homotopy facts for complete bidirected graphs. Two more recent published mathematical results already establish the **geometric fixed-space homotopy type** of this precise six-vertex involution as \(\bigvee^{25}S^1\), as instances of their complete-graph involution and divisor-rooted permutation classifications. That homotopy result and the orbit enumeration are explicitly **not claimed as new here**. The claim isolated here is the full residual \(S_4\) first-homology character, including the negative double-transposition trace, all five rational constituents, and its unique invariant line. The inspected fixed-space papers expressly limit themselves to homotopy classification without determining centralizer actions. The published general equivariant matrix–forest character formula concerns original graph-automorphism characters before taking geometric fixed points; it does not directly yield this residual fixed-space representation.

## Limitations
The integral **lattice model** is the signed cycle kernel of a concrete 12-vertex graph, not an identification with a named indecomposable integral \(S_4\)-lattice. The irreducible decomposition is over \(\mathbb Q\), not \(\mathbb Z\). The quotient statement is rational only; it makes no assertion about fundamental group or integral quotient torsion. The complete character is exact for this one natural six-label configuration; an unindexed equivariant treatment of the same centralizer module would supersede the proposed novelty. No independent mathematical validation is claimed.
