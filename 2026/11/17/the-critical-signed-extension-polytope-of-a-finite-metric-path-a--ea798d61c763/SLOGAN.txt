At the smallest admissible equidistant attachment radius, every norm-one real-linear Lipschitz extension from a finite weighted path is described by an explicit cumulative-mass ballot polytope; negative optimal coefficients first appear at four original vertices. For a unit path with \(m\ge2\) edges, exactly \(1/(m-1)!\) of the critical optimizer volume corresponds to nonnegative coefficients, despite the existence of a nonnegative optimum.
