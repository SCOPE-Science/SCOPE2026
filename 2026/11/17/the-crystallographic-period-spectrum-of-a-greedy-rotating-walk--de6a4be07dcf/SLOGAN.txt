For the nearest-origin signed rotating walk, every nonterminating trajectory at crystallographic rational rotation denominators \(q\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\) becomes position-periodic. The exact eventual least periods are \(2\) for \(q=1,2\), \(4\) for \(q=4\), and exactly \(3\) or \(6\) for \(q=3,6\), with both alternatives attained.
