The graphical zonotope of the square of an \(n\)-cycle has a unique Euclidean incenter for odd \(n\) and for \(n=6,7\), but an exactly classified \((n/2-1)\)-dimensional cube-section family of incenters for every even \(n\ge8\). Its coordinate bound changes sharply at \(n=8\), where singleton cuts rather than near-balanced intervals control the incenter locus.
