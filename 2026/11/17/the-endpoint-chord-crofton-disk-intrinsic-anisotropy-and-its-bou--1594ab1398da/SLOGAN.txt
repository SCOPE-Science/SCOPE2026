For uniform independent endpoints of a random circle chord, the chance that it separates two disk points defines an explicit reversible projective Finsler metric whose Euclidean center is its unique isotropic point. Its radial/tangential infinitesimal-length ratio is \(\operatorname{artanh}(r)/\arcsin(r)\), a strictly increasing ruler for radius; away from the center the metric is non-Riemannian, while its completion has an exact quadratic-angular boundary metric. The completed metric has no isometries beyond rotations and reflections.
