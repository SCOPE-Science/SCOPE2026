The standard harmonic polytope has exact squared Euclidean diameter \(2n(n^2-1)/3+8n-4\) in every rank \(n\ge2\), with exactly \(n(n-1)((n-2)!)^2/2\) maximizing unordered pairs. These longest chords form a perfect matching on their endpoints, touching precisely a \(1/(n-1)\) fraction of the already-known circumsphere-contact vertices; all remaining vertices are isolated in the diameter graph.
