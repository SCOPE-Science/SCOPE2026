## Originality
**PASS, subject to bibliographic uncertainty.** The closest complete accessible primary texts are Choudhuri–Faraco–Yadav (2022 preprint, Proposition 7 and Corollary 9) and Shah–Yadav (2016 preprint, Theorem 7 and Remark 9). Both establish existence of period-two points near the half-turn, and the former analyzes the exact half-turn; those facts are covered. Neither inspected result determines the exact angular threshold, counts one neutral orbit at each boundary, or derives its two-step Floquet multipliers. The claim is not a corollary of the stated local results: an existence neighbourhood need not be maximal, and it does not determine the global nonexistence complement. Comparisons with nearby published exact tongues for different circle families also do not imply this model-specific algebraic formula. A later erratum to the 2019 paper has not been materially inspected, and unindexed stronger coverage may exist.

## Value
**PASS.** The partial literature explicitly leaves the size and complete periodic-point census of the half-turn parameter region undetermined. The new theorem supplies an exact two-parameter mode-locking boundary, its finite-width expansion and the stability dichotomy including neutral endpoints. It is structurally meaningful for the circle dynamics of normalized affine rotations rather than a numerical parameter-table entry.

## Scope and risks
The conclusion covers exactly the normalized affine rigid rotation on the unit circle for nonzero translation with norm below one and only prime-period-two orbits. It does not imply global attraction or higher-period classifications. Publication priority is not independently established. The published erratum associated with an earlier source and nonindexed equivalent formulations remain residual bibliographic risks.
