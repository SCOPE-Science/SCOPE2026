For a normalized affine rotation of the circle with translation strength \(0<\alpha<1\), exactly two primitive two-cycles exist precisely within angular distance \(\arcsin((1-\sqrt{1-\alpha^2})/2)\) of the half-turn, coalescing into one neutral cycle at each sharp endpoint and disappearing outside. The two-cycle tongue opens quadratically in translation strength; its two interior cycles have explicit attracting and repelling multipliers.
