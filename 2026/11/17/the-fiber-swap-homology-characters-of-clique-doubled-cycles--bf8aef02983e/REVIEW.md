# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof constructs the entire augmented \((C_2)^n\)-equivariant simplicial chain complex. Every character block is exactly a shifted reduced augmented chain complex of the corresponding base independence link; orientations, the empty simplex, independent-set restrictions and singleton gaps are checked. The graph after deleting a chosen support and its neighbors consists of paths of precisely determined lengths, and the elementary three-periodic path calculation proves the infinite-family character classification. The standalone finite-field and Lefschetz computations corroborate, rather than replace, this proof.

**Originality — PASS against inspected evidence.** Theorem 28 of Carnero Bravo (arXiv:2307.14321) already determines nonequivariant homotopy for cycle lexicographic products; that coverage is credited. Fu–Grbić (arXiv:1803.11047) provides a distinct equivariant framework for polyhedral products under base-index permutations. Neither inspected full-text statement supplies the fiber-local \((C_2)^n\) representation or its exact cyclic-gap multiplicity-one law. General underlying chain decomposition ideas are standard, so the new content is the complete specialized character and degree classification. Unindexed equivalent work remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The result resolves an intrinsic symmetry invariant of a natural infinite graph family with exponentially many characters. The mod-three cyclic-gap obstruction and exact occurrence degree cannot be read from nonequivariant homology ranks. The theorem supplies a structural link-theoretic description instead of a finite list or a modest numerical improvement.

**Limits.** Only rational local fiber-swap representations are classified. Integral lattices and the larger dihedral semidirect action are not determined. The closest 2023 paper was inspected in full at the relevant theorem and proof pages; the 2023 forest-only companion was compared by abstract and scope, not by complete text.
