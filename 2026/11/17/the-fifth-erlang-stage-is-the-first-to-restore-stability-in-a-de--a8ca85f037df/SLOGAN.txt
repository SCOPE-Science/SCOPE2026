For a positive-feedback second-order delay center with \(a>b>0\), Erlang synaptic kernels of shapes one and two never destabilize it, shapes three and four destabilize it once, and shapes five and six destabilize and then restabilize it as mean delay increases. Five memory stages are the sharp minimum for this universal reentrant-stability phenomenon, with both transition delays given exactly by \(\tau_j=q\tan(j\pi/q)/\sqrt{a-(-1)^j b\cos^q(j\pi/q)}\).
