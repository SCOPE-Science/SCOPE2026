The first radial vertex layer below the unique circumsphere of every Euclidean chainlink polytope has squared-radius deficit exactly \(\ell(a_{\min}-\ell)\), controlled only by its shortest chain. Its complete contact count is \(2m(s-1)\) in the strict-link regime and drops to \(ms\) at the sharp half-size boundary, where \(m\) is the number of shortest chains.
