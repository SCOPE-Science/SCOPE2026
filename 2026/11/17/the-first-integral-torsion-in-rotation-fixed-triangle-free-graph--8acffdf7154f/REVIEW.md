# Scientific review: cyclic-fixed triangle-free graph complexes

## Correctness — PASS
The vertex-orbit barycentric map has a continuous PL inverse obtained by splitting orbit mass. Every triangle in a circulant graph yields and is yielded by an additive triple in the symmetric step set. The independent tests using all vertex triples and signed sums agree for every distance set at all graph orders \(3\leq n\leq21\). Exact integer Smith normal forms show all boundaries saturated at the lower orders and a single invariant factor \(2\) at order 21. Its nine-vertex complex has a connected closed-surface triangulation and a contradictory transported orientation; it is a Klein bottle. Critical outputs and the complete checker are supplied.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The invariant is natural: integral torsion in a geometric fixed-point space of the classical triangle-free graph-property complex. A sharp first occurrence has mathematical significance beyond re-running a known table, particularly because the emergent space is a nonorientable closed surface and because the sign-symmetric sum-free model makes the result computable by an independent additive predicate. The full finite cutoff and exact topological identification are nontrivial, verifiable information, not a minor improvement to a numerical bound.
