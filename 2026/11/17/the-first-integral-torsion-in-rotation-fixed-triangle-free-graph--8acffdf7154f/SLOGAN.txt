The first integral homology torsion in the full cyclic-rotation fixed space of a triangle-free graph complex occurs at 21 graph vertices: that fixed space is PL-homeomorphic to the Klein bottle, with \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\). Every smaller order from 3 through 20 has torsion-free integral homology, proved by exhaustive exact chain reductions.
