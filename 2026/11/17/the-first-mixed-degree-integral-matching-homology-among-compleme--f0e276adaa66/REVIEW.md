Exhaustive generation of all matchings on \(3\le n\le9\) and explicit signed integer boundary matrices yields the face counts and boundary ranks. A sparse algorithm uses only integral unimodular unit pivots and leaves zero residual matrices; hence it proves ranks and torsion-freeness, not merely rational Betti numbers. The checker is standalone, reproducible, and its exact output is supplied. The sharp cutoff is among this finite range; no homotopy wedge decomposition is claimed.
