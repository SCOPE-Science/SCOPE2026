For the noncrossing-matching simplicial complex of a regular polygon, the first reflection-fixed locus with reduced integral homology in two degrees occurs at the side-axis reflection of a 12-gon: \(\widetilde H_1\cong\mathbb Z^4\) and \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z\). An exact PL orbit-complex model and exhaustive integer Smith calculations show that every reflection-fixed locus on a smaller polygon has homology supported in at most one degree.
