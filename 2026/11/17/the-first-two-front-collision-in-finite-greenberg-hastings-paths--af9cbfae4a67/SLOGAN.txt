At the first length beyond a Greenberg–Hastings percolating front’s refractory span, a second endpoint excitation can destroy a maximal wave. On the \(n\)-vertex path with \(q=n-1\ge4\) colors, the exact count of maximally delayed initial states is \(2((n-1)!-(n-3)!)\), and a bivariate product polynomial resolves their refractory-site populations and distinguishes one from two initial excitation sources.
