Under all 413 subgroups of the 336-element automorphism group of the Fano projective-plane building, its geometric fixed-point spaces have exactly five homotopy types: \(\bigvee^8S^1\), a point, \(S^0\), three points, or the empty space. Precisely 28 subgroups yield the exceptional three-point locus, and they are exactly the 28 order-two point–line polarity subgroups.
