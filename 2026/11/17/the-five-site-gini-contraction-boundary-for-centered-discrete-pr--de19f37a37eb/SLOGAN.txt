The mean-centered discrete primitive is Gini-nonexpansive on every real vector exactly through five sites; a one-site spike forces amplification from six sites onward. Its sharp small-order coefficients are \(1/2,2/3,5/6,1\), and its all-dimensional amplification grows linearly between the explicit bounds \((n+1)/6\) and \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/n\).
