On every labelled \(3\times n\) torus, the Life without Death automaton has a complete occupation-resolved frozen-state census from a sixteen-state transfer matrix with an explicit degree-eleven zeta denominator. Its frozen states grow as \(\lambda^n\), with \(\lambda=6.983706621545\ldots\), and their limiting occupied-site fraction is \(0.5822864541778\ldots\), even though the fraction of all configurations that are frozen decays exponentially.
