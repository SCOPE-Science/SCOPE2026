# Scientific review: full Shrikhande affine homology character

## Correctness
PASS. The statement fixes the graph on \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\), its six Cayley directions, all simplicial faces, orientations, and all \(192\) affine automorphisms. A complete finite acyclic matching leaves one critical vertex and \(23\) critical two-cells, so there is a proved unique nonzero reduced homology degree. The exact cellular Lefschetz formula then computes the character of that group action. Two distinct methods (signed fixed simplices and independent unions of permutation cycles) agree on every automorphism. A separate integer Fourier calculation produces the stated rational translation-module decomposition and an averaged calculation gives both sign lines of the remaining dihedral action. The finite search domains and their proof significance are explicit; no inference about infinite graph families is made.

## Originality
PASS relative to examined literature. Peeters' discussion of the Shrikhande graph already describes four-cocliques and their geometric types; the present classification of independent quartets is a proof input, not a novelty assertion. Shareshian–Wachs and their cited chessboard-representation literature already address the rook comparison. Barmak supplies general independence-complex topology, and the accessible 2026 Shrikhande–rook homotopy calculation covers the \(23\)-sphere wedge, but its inspected result does not specify the complete \(192\)-element character, Fourier module, or rational symmetry-quotient cohomology. These are not logically determined by the nonequivariant homotopy statement or by graph spectra. Research-index searches and the inspected primary mathematical sources found no equivalent or stronger covering result. A 2026 graph monograph was not inspected in full, so hidden equivalent computations remain a publication-priority risk.

## Value
PASS. The Shrikhande graph is the canonical non-rook example with strongly regular parameters \((16,6,2,2)\). Its full automorphism character and its decomposition over the natural regular translation group are complete, exact invariants of a well-motivated graph complex, not arbitrary individual matrix entries. The calculation finds two translation-invariant rational homology classes yet no fully invariant classes and determines both quotient cohomology rings. The rook comparison demonstrates that the topological symmetry invariant discriminates the two classical cospectral models beyond their graph parameters. The result does not require a downstream theorem to be valuable.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest exact-complex result is the prior Shrikhande–rook independence-complex sphere-wedge calculation (public result dated 2026-10-11). Peeters (1997; earlier 1993 memorandum) establishes known coclique structure; Shareshian–Wachs (arXiv:math/0409054v1) and earlier symmetric-group representation results concern chessboard complexes; Barmak (arXiv:1007.0418v1) provides general topology. The character does not give an integral equivariant lattice, equivariant homotopy type or integral cohomology of quotients. Equivalent unindexed work is not excluded. No independent validation is claimed.
