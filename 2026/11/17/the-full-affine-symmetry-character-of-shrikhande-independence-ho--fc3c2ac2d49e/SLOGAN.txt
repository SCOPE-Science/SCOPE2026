The Shrikhande independence complex has a complete \(192\)-element rational homology character, whose restriction to the sixteen translations splits as \(\mathbb Q[T]\oplus\mathbb Q\oplus W_{1,1}\oplus W_{1,2}\oplus W_{2,1}\). Exactly two top-homology classes survive the translation quotient, but none survive the full automorphism quotient, making that quotient rationally acyclic.
