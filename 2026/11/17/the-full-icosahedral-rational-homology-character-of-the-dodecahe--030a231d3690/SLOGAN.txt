The dodecahedral perfect-matching complex has a full \(A_5\times C_2\) rational degree-three homology character \((\mathbf1\oplus V_4\oplus2V_5)^+\oplus V_5^-\), leaving one class invariant under all 120 symmetries. Its rational character unexpectedly matches that of the icosahedral two-independence complex, although the known integral homology of the dodecahedral complex has two-torsion absent from the icosahedral one.
