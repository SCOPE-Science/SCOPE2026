## Correctness — PASS
Every perfect matching of a linear two-row ladder consists of vertical singleton rungs and aligned upper/lower horizontal pairs; this follows by counting crossing edges at each even-cardinality left cut. The quotient-to-upper-row map is simplicial, a deformation retraction up to simplicial contiguity, and reversal-equivariant; row interchange is contiguous to the identity. The overlap independence complex has the two-step fold recurrence \(K_n\simeq\Sigma K_{n-2}\), proving the stated homotopy groups and their integral ranks. The signed Lefschetz count is exact: on the reversal-fixed faces the weighted orbit count is \(Z_m-Z_{m-1}\), where \(Z_0=1\), \(Z_1=0\), and \(Z_j=-Z_{j-2}\); this yields the claimed integral sign for all even widths. The standalone independent boundary calculation and signed trace enumerations agree on their finite ranges. Risk: finite checks alone are not a proof; the analytic recurrence and equivariant retraction supply the general argument.
