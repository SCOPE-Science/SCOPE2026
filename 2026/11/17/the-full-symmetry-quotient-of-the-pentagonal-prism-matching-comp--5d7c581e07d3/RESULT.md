# The full-symmetry quotient of the pentagonal-prism matching complex is a three-sphere

## Finding

Let \(P=C_5\square K_2\) be the graph with vertices \((a,i)\in\{0,1\}\times\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z\), edges \((a,i)(a,i+1)\) and \((0,i)(1,i)\), and let \(X=\mathcal M(P)\) be its **ordinary matching complex**: vertices of \(X\) are graph edges and faces are pairwise disjoint collections of graph edges. The natural full graph automorphism group \(G\cong D_5\times C_2\), of order twenty, rotates or reflects the five positions and may interchange the two layers.

**Theorem.** The ordinary geometric orbit space, rather than the Borel quotient or an orbit-facet construction, satisfies
\[
\boxed{|X|/G\simeq S^3.}
\]
Thus its complete integral homology is \(H_0\cong H_3\cong\mathbb Z\), zero in all other degrees; in particular it has no hidden integral torsion. The quotient is simply connected. This upgrades the previously determined *rational* sphere cohomology of this same orbit space to its complete homotopy type.

## Assumptions and scope

The graph is the unfilled ten-vertex pentagonal prism and **ordinary** matchings are used, including those not extendable to perfect matchings. The group acts as \(r(a,i)=(a,i+1)\), \(s(a,i)=(a,-i)\), and \(t(a,i)=(1-a,i)\). All homotopy and integral homology assertions concern this specific five-rung graph and the full indicated group; there is no claim about arbitrary \(n\)-rung prisms. Barycentric simplices are strict chains of nonempty matching faces. A quotient flag is an orbit of such a chain under the entire twenty-element group; distinct flag orbits are not identified merely because their constituent face-orbit lists agree.

## Proof

**1. Exhaustive integral cellular model.** Index the ten rim graph edges first, the five rungs second, and enumerate all masks of pairwise vertex-disjoint graph edges. Exactly \(341\) nonempty matching faces occur, with largest face size five. In the barycentric subdivision \(\mathrm{sd}(X)\), simplices are strict chains of these nonempty faces. Their counts by dimensions zero through four are
\[
(341,2680,5940,4920,1320).
\]
A graph automorphism preserves face cardinalities, so no nontrivial group element can permute distinct vertices of a barycentric simplex. Therefore \(\mathrm{sd}(X)/G\) is a finite \(\Delta\)-complex with *separate* cells for flag orbits, and each cell boundary is the alternating sum of the group orbits of its codimension-one subflags. There are exactly
\[
(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4)=(35,188,357,276,72)
\]
quotient cells. Because chain ordering is preserved, every cellular incidence is an integer, with no stabilizer-weight division. Direct construction checks \(\partial_{j-1}\partial_j=0\) for all relevant degrees.

**2. Integral cancellation.** An elementary unit cancellation picks a nonzero cellular boundary entry \(\varepsilon=\pm1\), performs the corresponding unimodular column operations, and cancels that \(j\)-cell and \((j-1)\)-cell; it gives an integral chain-homotopy equivalence. In the canonical deterministic ordering of `verify_prism_quotient.py`, the matrices admit \(72\), \(203\), \(154\), and \(34\) such cancellations in degrees four, three, two, and one respectively. The resulting chain complex has exactly one zero-cell generator and one three-cell generator, with zero differential. It follows without field reduction or a torsion assumption that
\[
H_j(|X|/G;\mathbb Z)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&j=0,3,\\
0,&j\notin\{0,3\}.
\end{cases}
\]
The code rebuilds the actual matrices and carries out every unit operation; this is an exhaustive finite integer calculation, not a sample of cases.

**3. Fundamental group.** Independently, the one-skeleton of \(X\) has \(15\) vertices and \(75\) edges, and its two-skeleton has \(145\) triangular faces. Take the reproducible spanning tree in `verify_prism_quotient.py`, leaving \(61\) non-tree edge generators of \(\pi_1(X)\). Every triangle gives a relation equal to the oriented product of its three edge generators. Repeatedly setting the sole remaining generator of a triangle to the identity eliminates **all sixty-one** generators, with no residue. This gives an explicit presentation proof that \(X\) is simply connected.

The barycenter of the simplex consisting of all five rungs is fixed by the **whole** group \(G\): every automorphism permutes those rungs. Armstrong's finite-action orbit-space fundamental-group theorem, or the quotient of a simply connected space by the normal subgroup generated by point stabilizers, therefore gives \(\pi_1(|X|/G)=1\).

**4. Homotopy conclusion.** The quotient is a simply connected finite CW complex with the integral homology of \(S^3\). Hurewicz supplies a generator of \(\pi_3\) corresponding to its fundamental third-homology class. A representing map \(S^3\to|X|/G\) is an integral homology isomorphism. By the simply connected homology form of Whitehead's theorem this map is a homotopy equivalence. Hence the quotient is \(S^3\) up to homotopy, as asserted.

## Verification

Run `python3 verify_prism_quotient.py` with Python 3 standard library only. The verifier separately enumerates all original matching faces, all barycentric flags, all twenty automorphisms, and each full flag orbit; constructs the *integral* boundary in every degree and checks every \(\partial^2=0\); performs all \(463\) integral unit cancellations and verifies that precisely one cell in degrees zero and three survives. It also constructs an explicit spanning-tree/triangle fundamental-group presentation of \(X\) and eliminates all \(61\) non-tree generators. Modulo-prime ranks for \(2,3,5,7,11,101\) corroborate, but are not used to infer, integral torsion-freeness. The verification program does not rely on preexisting tabulated homology or a numerical black-box certificate.

## Relationship to prior work

Braun–Hough's June 2016 preprint on matching complexes of open two-row grids established the broader grid-matching motivation but does not address the closing edges, full pentagonal-prism symmetry, or its ordinary orbit-space homotopy. Previously published exact calculations determine the **unquotiented** pentagonal-prism complex as \(S^2\vee S^2\vee S^3\vee S^3\). A separate prior symmetry analysis computes the full rational \(D_5\times C_2\) homology representation and concludes that the quotient has rational cohomology \(H^*(S^3;\mathbb Q)\), but expressly does not assert its integral homology or homotopy equivalence with a sphere. The new integral flag-orbit cancellation and independent simply-connectivity proof supply precisely these missing conclusions. The complete group action is essential; the result is not a new computation of the already known labeled-complex homology or rational character.

## Limitations

No other prism width or subgroup quotient is classified. The proof is finite and exact for the stated graph, without a claimed infinite-family theorem. Published full-rational-character results cover the quotient's rational Betti numbers, so those numbers are background rather than claimed new. An unindexed prior integral quotient calculation remains a possible originality risk. The proof is not an external independent audit.
