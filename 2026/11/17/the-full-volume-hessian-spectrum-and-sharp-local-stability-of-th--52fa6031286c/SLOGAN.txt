For the nine-vertex maximum-volume four-polytope formed by a square and pentagon in orthogonal two-planes, the complete \(27\)-dimensional volume-deficit quadratic spectrum has exactly six rotational zero modes. The sharp local volume loss per squared Euclidean distance from its orthogonal orbit is \(\sin(2\pi/5)(5-\sqrt5)/24\), attained asymptotically by a pentagon first-Fourier-mode deformation.
