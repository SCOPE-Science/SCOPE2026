# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The face predicate is precisely the absence of a pair differing in all three coordinates. An exhaustive finite acyclic matching was independently constructed: it leaves exactly one vertex and 46 three-dimensional cells, with every one of the 27,216 codimension-one incidences checked. The full 22-class character was computed both by oriented fixed-face Lefschetz sums and by independent orbit-graph signed recursion; the methods agree, and all 1,296 group elements were classified and recomputed. Weighted orthogonality verifies the irreducible character table and yields seven multiplicity-one constituents totaling dimension 46. The rational quotient consequence uses the finite-group chain transfer. The attached program and output are the precise computational certificates; there is no infinite-parameter extrapolation.

## Originality (PASS, with literature-access risk)
The closest binary-hypercube representation paper arXiv:2606.20784 has different alphabet and symmetry group. The 2021 two-complete-factor result and the 2023/2024 three-factor results with a two-point factor do not imply the claimed full wreath action on the three-ternary-factor case. A previously published 46-sphere homotopy calculation is stronger than needed to obtain the Betti rank but does not determine the group representation. Comparisons covered equivalent graph-product and Hamming formulations, potential general wreath-product representation theorems, exact tables, and broader-parameter studies. Publicly accessible main statements and the full 2021 source were read; the full recent arXiv PDF was inaccessible and is a residual overlap risk. Absence of a search hit was not treated as a proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
The complete automorphism-character decomposition answers a naturally motivated symmetry question in a first nonbinary Hamming cube, parallel to recent binary-hypercube symmetry work. It adds structure impossible to recover from the known rank: the representation is multiplicity-free, the alphabet-fixed line carries coordinate sign, and the quotient has trivial reduced rational homology. Its exact 22-class answer is suitable as a nonbinary test of representation-theoretic methods. These are precise independent structural invariants, not routine variants of a previously known Betti count.

## Limits and closest comparisons
Only one finite Hamming space at one scale is classified; no integral equivariant splitting, general-arity theorem or geometric contractibility conclusion is claimed. The 2026 primary source's main statement was inspected in accessible preview text, not in a complete downloaded PDF. The 2023/2024 product-family paper's public abstract was inspected, not its complete PDF. Unindexed broader results remain possible.
