For sixteen minimal spherical-harmonic samples on the south pole and regular three-, five-, and seven-point latitude rings, the **unique** determinant-optimal south-to-north ring ordering is five, seven, three; all six possible orderings have rigorously ranked optimal volumes. Exact rational certificates show that the winning squared determinant exceeds every other ordering's best by a factor greater than four, with the sharp winning-to-runner-up ratio in \( (4.77011,4.77012) \).
