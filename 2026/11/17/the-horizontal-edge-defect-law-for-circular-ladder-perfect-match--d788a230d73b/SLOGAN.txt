Deleting a single *horizontal* edge from the circular two-row ladder makes its perfect-matching complex contractible for every odd width, but for even width \(n\) gives \(S^{n/2-1}\) plus a possible second sphere governed by \(n\bmod6\). The precise extra attachment is the suspension of the independence complex of a path with \(n-1\) vertices, producing mixed sphere dimensions for infinitely many widths.
