For every dicyclic group of order \(4n\), its abelian-coset complex has an explicit integral first-homology module: the sign-twisted augmentation lattice on the \(n^2\) cosets of maximal order-four abelian subgroups. The associated equivariant graph model also shows that the ordinary left-translation orbit space is contractible; the nonequivariant homology rank was already known.
