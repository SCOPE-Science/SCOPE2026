## Mathematical value — PASS

The Möbius–Kantor graph is the unique cubic symmetric graph on sixteen vertices and the incidence graph of a classical symmetric configuration, providing a canonical finite dynamical benchmark. The finding explains its entire oscillation-basin landscape through a small symbolic retraction and identifies a nontrivial one-step delay relative to the previously studied Fano incidence example: an 88-element image first collapses to 72 stable symbols at the next retraction. The \(231\) dynamically isolated two-cycles, \(22\) isolated equilibria, and single huge oscillator basin are structurally interpretable consequences of three natural fiber weights, not arbitrary sampled numbers. No general statement for other graph orders is asserted.
