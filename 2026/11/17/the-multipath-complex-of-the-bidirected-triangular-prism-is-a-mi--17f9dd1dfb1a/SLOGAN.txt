The ordinary multipath complex of the fully bidirected triangular-prism graph has exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^5 S^2\vee\bigvee^{22}S^3\vee\bigvee^6 S^4\). A complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching, zero integral Morse differentials, and vanishing of all degree-two cup products jointly rule out hidden four-cell attaching maps.
