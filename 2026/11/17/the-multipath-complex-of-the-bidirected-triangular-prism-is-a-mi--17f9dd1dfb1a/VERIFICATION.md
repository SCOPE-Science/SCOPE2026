# Finite exact verification

Run `python3 verify.py` with Python 3 and the standard library. The output should equal `verification_output.txt`. The program computes its data directly from the six labeled vertices and nine undirected edges, rather than importing a face table or presolved cycle classes. It exhaustively traverses all 262,144 directed-arc subsets and verifies every allowed face, every oriented Hasse incidence, matching acyclicity, integral Morse differentials and signed rational cup-product coboundaries.

The exact outputs are 686 nonempty simplices, face vector `(18,108,260,240,60)`, 326 ordinary Morse pairs, critical dimensions `0:1,2:5,3:22,4:6`, 2,256 Hasse incidences, zero integral Morse differentials, mod-two simplicial boundary ranks `(17,91,164,54)`, 5 independent integral degree-two cocycles, rank 54 for the degree-four rational coboundary image, and zero classes for all 25 ordered degree-two cup products. Six of those raw product cochains are nonzero, and the exact arithmetic verifies that they are coboundaries.

The topological conclusion additionally uses the standard facts that a simply connected two-dimensional wedge has second Hurewicz isomorphism; that degree-three sphere-wedge attachments to two-spheres are generated by Hopf and Whitehead classes; and that their coefficients are detected by top-degree cup products of a four-dimensional CW complex. Finite enumeration is exhaustive for this explicitly specified graph only; no generalization to arbitrary graph sizes is certified. Independent mathematical validation was not performed.
