# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim specifies the standard Chvátal target graph by its entire edge list, the standard ordered five-cycle source, and the ordinary graph-Hom polyhedral complex. The two independent exhaustive cell enumerators agree on all 1,500 individual cells, with dimensions 0–3. Every one of 280 elementary CW collapse steps is checked against the then-remaining full face poset, leaving a connected 440-vertex/500-edge graph; its integral homotopy type is therefore a wedge of 61 circles. The full automorphism group is exhaustively enumerated, and fixed-cell signed traces give an independently reproducible five-class rational dihedral character. All computations are finite and exhaustive for this specific target/source, not conjectural extrapolations.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Fujii–Kimura–Nozaki (arXiv:2412.19039, first public 2024-12-26) and Matsushita (arXiv:2412.19144, first public 2024-12-26) require a square-free target for their principal classification; this target contains a four-cycle. Their full-text main results and relevant supplementary discussions were checked and no Chvátal instance was found. Published research records on the Petersen target and on prisms mapped *to* the pentagon concern different ordered source-target pairs, and a previously recorded Grötzsch-target computation does not imply the Chvátal case. Exact-pair, graph-alias and character searches found no covering theorem or table. An unindexed analogous computation could remain and is a genuine residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Chvátal graph is the smallest triangle-free 4-regular 4-chromatic graph, making it a natural extremal non-square-free target at the boundary of the main recent Hom-complex theorem. The complete integral fundamental group and the rational dihedral symmetry character provide a reproducible qualitative and equivariant classification: the 20 cubical three-cells collapse without generating higher homology, and the full first-homology action is determined. This is an exact motivated boundary case rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limitations and closest literature

No claim is made for arbitrary non-square-free codomains, all odd source cycles, equivariant homotopy type, or an integral splitting into character lattices. The graph-Hom results of Fujii–Kimura–Nozaki and Matsushita, and the distinct Petersen-target and prism-source results, are the closest inspected comparisons. Finite exhaustive verification has been performed for the stated graph only; independent external review has not occurred.
