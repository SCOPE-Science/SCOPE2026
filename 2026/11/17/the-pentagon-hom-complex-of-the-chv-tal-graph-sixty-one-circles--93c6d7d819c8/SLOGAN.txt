The ordinary pentagon graph-Hom complex of the non-square-free, four-chromatic Chvátal graph collapses to a connected graph with 61 cycles, hence has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{61}S^1\). Its full dihedral automorphism action on rational first homology has character \((61,1,1,-9,1)\) on the five conjugacy classes, distinguishing this exact boundary case from general square-free-codomain theorems.
