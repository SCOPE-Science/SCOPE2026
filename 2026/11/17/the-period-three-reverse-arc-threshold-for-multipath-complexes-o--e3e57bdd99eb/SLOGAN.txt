For a clockwise polygon with \(3k\) vertices, the first \(k-1\) reverse arcs leave its multipath complex homotopy-equivalent to the original \(S^{3k-2}\), regardless of placement. When the \(k\) reverse arcs are spaced exactly three edges apart, the complex becomes \(S^{k-1}\vee S^{3k-2}\): one lower class is born, while the original integral top class survives primitively.
