## Verification carried out

The critical case was checked from the binomial generating function, uniformly integrable logarithms and dominated Schatten sums. The diverging regime was checked by a binomial Chernoff bound, a uniform integrable majorant and convergent Riemann sums. The logarithmic Frullani identity was verified by differentiating with respect to the Poisson intensity and by evaluating the exponential generating function. The Lévy kernel is positive and has finite first moment; compound-Poisson cutoffs converge, giving the strict complete-monotonicity conclusion with justified interchange of the Schatten sum and derivatives.

Supplementary numerical tests used exact binomial or Poisson probability formulas, with relative first-weight differences decreasing for two weight exponents and three finite intensities; large-intensity spectral sums used a Hurwitz-zeta tail correction. Such finite calculations are not proofs of convergence or derivative signs. The derived findings are not independently audited or externally certified.
