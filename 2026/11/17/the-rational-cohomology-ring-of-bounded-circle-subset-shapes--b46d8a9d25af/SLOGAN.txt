The ordinary moduli space of nonempty subsets of the circle with at most \(k\) points, taken up to rotations and reflections, has rational cohomology ring \(\mathbb Q[v]/(v^{\lceil k/4\rceil})\) with \(|v|=4\). The classes persist under inclusion, and the same algebra holds over \(\mathbb F_p\) for every odd prime \(p>k\), ruling out integral torsion at those primes.
