The ordinary quotient of the seven-point complete-graph matching complex by its natural sevenfold rotation has fundamental group \(\mathbb Z/21\mathbb Z\), not just first homology of that order. Its complete integral homology is \(H_1=\mathbb Z/21\mathbb Z\), \(H_2=\mathbb Z^2\), with no higher homology; exact orbit-cell attaching words prove the group presentation.
