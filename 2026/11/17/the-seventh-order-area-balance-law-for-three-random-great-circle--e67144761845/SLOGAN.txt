For three independent isotropic great circles, the probability that all eight spherical cells have areas within \(\varepsilon\) of one another is \(\varepsilon^3/(16\pi)+7\varepsilon^7/(122880\pi)+O(\varepsilon^9)\), with no fourth-, fifth-, sixth- or eighth-order terms. Conditioned on near-equality, the normalized three pairwise normal-angle deviations become uniformly distributed on an explicit polytope, and the normalized cell-area range tends to \(\operatorname{Beta}(3,1)\).
