# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Exact harmonic weights for the twice-Cesàro matrix are strictly decreasing, giving unique finite binary extremizers by an exchange argument. Harmonic integral comparisons provide uniform \(1/n\) errors relative to the logarithmic envelopes. The indicator of any moving interval in scaled coordinate space realizes its target integral after an arbitrary fixed prefix, and Baire category produces a single residual family realizing a dense subset of every attainable target simultaneously. Compactness closes the cluster set and the finite bounds rule out exterior values. The entropy identity and its maximum follow by direct differentiation. The included exact-arithmetic checker tests all binary inputs up to length 15, but the unbounded quantifiers are proved analytically.

## Originality
**PASS, with qualified historical priority.** Douvropoulos (arXiv:1211.0783v1) studies precisely the separate and simultaneous Cesàro-target problem and states a qualitative bounded-alphabet obstruction in Proposition 4.12. That proposition excludes one coarse rectangle but does not identify a sharp compatibility lens, its entropy width, its exact finite extremizers, or residual filling of the sharp set. Its unrestricted simultaneous approximation theorem requires a dense convex hull and so does not apply to the binary alphabet. Olsen–West (2020) proves generic oscillation for individual averaging systems, not joint two-order compatibility. Galaz-Fontes–Solis (2008), as far as its accessible abstract establishes, treats the limit as iteration order tends to infinity rather than the coordinate index; its full text was not inspected. Searches of existing mathematical research records for joint binary Cesàro cluster values, weighted harmonic extrema, and equivalent logarithmic envelopes did not reveal a covering claim. Negative searches are not proof of historical uniqueness.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The 2012 primary source expressly distinguishes unrestricted simultaneous generic approximation from the bounded-alphabet obstruction but leaves its compatible pair geometry unresolved. The exact binary lens answers the natural bounded case with sharp finite-size estimates and an extremal entropy law. It also shows that separate maximal oscillation of the two Cesàro orders is compatible with a strong joint restriction, a nontrivial distinction for topological-generic divergence.

## Closest literature and limitations
The strongest direct comparator is Douvropoulos's Proposition 4.12 and the convex-hull hypothesis of his main simultaneous-target theorem. The present result is limited to the binary alphabet and the first two fixed Cesàro orders; a multi-order region is not asserted. One older iteration-order paper was compared from its abstract only, and a historically equivalent derivation may exist outside the sources found.
