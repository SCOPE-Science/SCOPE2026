For a uniformly sampled point in a finite permutation, the mean and variance of its least period determine the full cycle type through \(13\) points, but fail first—and uniquely—at \(14\) points: \((7,4,3)\) and \((6,6,1,1)\) both have \(\mu=37/7\) and \(v=150/49\), despite disjoint period supports. Adjoining fixed points propagates the ambiguity to every larger finite cardinality.
