Four independent equal spherical caps approach the hemispherical covering probability \(1/8\) from below with the sharp linear deficit \(3\varepsilon/2\). Equivalently, a random inscribed tetrahedron containing the sphere centre has an origin-centred clearance below \(t\) with conditional probability \(12t+o(t)\); the rare shallow facet has an explicit, acute-triangle-shaped marked limit.
