Among the \(\binom{20}{4}=4845\) four-vertex selections of a regular dodecahedron, there are exactly eleven Euclidean determinant-magnitude shells. In the standard golden-ratio normalization, the classical ten largest tetrahedra have volume \(8/3\), while the sharp second shell consists of exactly 120 tetrahedra of volume \(4\varphi/3\), characterized by squared edge lengths \(\{4,8,8,8,8,4+4\varphi\}\).
