For three independent area-uniform points on any smooth closed Riemannian surface of area \(A\), the probability that their geodesic triangle has perimeter below \(\varepsilon\) is \(\pi^2\varepsilon^4/(42A^2)-\pi^3\chi\varepsilon^6/(990A^3)+o(\varepsilon^6)\). Thus one three-point metric distribution recovers both surface area and Euler characteristic; the round-sphere specialization of the formula is already implicit in earlier exact perimeter-density work.
