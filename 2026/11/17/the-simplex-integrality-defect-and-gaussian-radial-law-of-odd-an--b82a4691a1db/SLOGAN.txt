For the clique relaxation of every odd antihole \(\overline{C_{2m+1}}\), the region outside its stable-set polytope is a single simplex of exact Euclidean volume \(1/(m(2m+1)!)\). A volume-uniform point in this cap lies at squared distance asymptotic to \(2/n\) from the fractional apex, and its centered rescaled squared distance has a Gaussian limit of variance \(20\), in sharp contrast to the nonconcentrating odd-cycle cap.
