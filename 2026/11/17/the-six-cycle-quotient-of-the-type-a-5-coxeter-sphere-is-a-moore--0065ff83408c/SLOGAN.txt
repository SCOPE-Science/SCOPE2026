The six-cycle quotient of the type-\(A_5\) Coxeter sphere is homotopy equivalent to the Moore space \(M(\mathbb Z/2,3)\), hence to \(\Sigma^2\mathbb RP^2\). This concrete quotient of a four-sphere is simply connected but has precisely one nonzero reduced integral homology group, \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z/2\).
