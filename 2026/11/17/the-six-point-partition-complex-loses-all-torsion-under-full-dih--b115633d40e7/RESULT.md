# The six-point partition complex loses all torsion under full dihedral symmetry

## Finding
Let \(P=\overline{\Pi}_6\) be the poset of partitions of the cyclically labeled set \(\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\), excluding the discrete and indiscrete partitions, ordered by refinement. Let \(K=|\Delta(P)|\) be its order complex. The order-twelve dihedral group \(D_6\) acts by relabeling the six points. Its **ordinary geometric orbit space** satisfies
\[
\boxed{K/D_6\simeq\bigvee^{12}S^3.}
\]
Thus the quotient is simply connected, \(\widetilde H_3(K/D_6;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{12}\), and all other reduced integral homology groups are zero.

## Assumptions and scope
We use the action \(r(i)=i+1\pmod6\), \(s(i)=-i\pmod6\) on the labels \(i\in\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}\). The space is the literal topological quotient of the finite order complex, not its homotopy orbit space. A simplex is a strict chain of partitions; every group element preserving a chain setwise fixes each of its vertices because the ranks are distinct. Hence there are no inversions and integral quotient-cell boundaries are obtained by taking signed orbits of deleted-face chains.

## Proof
**1. Simply connected covering complex.** The proper partition lattice of six elements has a shellable order complex homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \((6-1)!=120\) three-spheres. In particular, \(K\) is simply connected and three-dimensional.

**2. Simply connected quotient.** By the orbit-space fundamental-group theorem, for a finite simplicial action on simply connected \(K\), the fundamental group of \(K/D_6\) is \(D_6/N\), where \(N\) is the normal subgroup generated by elements with a fixed point. The reflection \(s(i)=-i\) fixes the proper partition \(\{\{0\},\{3\},\{1,5\},\{2,4\}\}\); the reflection \(t(i)=1-i\) fixes the proper partition \(\{\{0,1\},\{2,5\},\{3,4\}\}\). As \(s\) and \(t=rs\) generate \(D_6\), \(N=D_6\). Therefore \(\pi_1(K/D_6)=1\).

**3. Exact integral orbit-chain computation.** All 203 set partitions of a six-element set are enumerated, and the two extremal partitions are deleted, leaving 201 vertices before forming chains. The nonempty chains of length one through four, quotiented by \(D_6\), give respectively
\[
(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3)=(35,215,419,250).
\]
For a chain \(p_0<\cdots<p_d\), the integral boundary of its orbit cell is the signed sum of the orbits of its \(d+1\) faces. The explicit generator in `verify_partition_dihedral.py` constructs the three integer matrices and checks \(\partial_{d-1}\partial_d=0\) with exact arithmetic. It also independently verifies all four orbit counts by Burnside's lemma.

An integral elementary chain cancellation at a coefficient \(u=\pm1\) replaces the remaining boundary block \(D\) by \(D-vu^{-1}w\). This is a unimodular basis change followed by removal of a contractible two-generator summand; no field reduction or extrapolation is used. Exactly 34 such cancellations are performed in degree one, 181 in degree two, and 238 in degree three. The reduced free complex has ranks
\[
(C_0,C_1,C_2,C_3)=(1,0,0,12)
\]
and all differentials zero. Consequently \(H_0\cong\mathbb Z\), \(H_1=H_2=0\), and \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{12}\).

**4. From homology to homotopy.** Simple connectivity and \(H_2=0\) imply \(\pi_2=0\) by Hurewicz. The Hurewicz map \(\pi_3\to H_3\) is an isomorphism. Choose twelve maps from \(S^3\) realizing an integral homology basis; their wedge is a homology equivalence of simply connected CW complexes, hence a homotopy equivalence by the homological Whitehead theorem.

## Verification
Run `python verify_partition_dihedral.py` with Python 3.10 or later; no external packages or stored matrices are needed. The program independently regenerates all partitions, flags, group permutations, quotient chains and exact integer boundaries. Its reference output is provided as `verification_output.txt`. Every unit pivot is checked at the moment of contraction; nonunit pivots are never treated as units. The final assertion checks the exact remaining generator ranks and absence of boundary coefficients.

## Relationship to prior work
Donau's work on subgroups fixing a common label establishes sphere-wedge quotients for a different class of actions; the transitive hexagon-dihedral action has no globally fixed label and is outside that theorem. Donau's prime-period counterexamples concern free actions of \(C_p\) on prime-point partition complexes; neither their hypotheses nor their conclusions cover this composite six-point dihedral action. An existing computation for the rotation-only six-point quotient detects two order-three torsion summands, making the disappearance of torsion after adding reflections mathematically consequential. The rotation-only homotopy type does not specify the induced reflection action, so it does not imply the present classification.

## Limitations
The theorem is specific to six cyclic labels and their full dihedral group. It does not assert an all-size classification, naturality of the resulting wedge equivalence, or independence of a third-party mathematical review. The surviving uncertainty is whether an earlier unindexed or unavailable full text contains an equivalent six-point dihedral computation; this is a priority question, not an additional hypothesis in the proof.
