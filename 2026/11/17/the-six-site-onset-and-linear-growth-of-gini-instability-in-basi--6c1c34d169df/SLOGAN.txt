The classical midpoint (Basic) Chebyshev isotonic regression selector is nonexpansive in complete-pair Gini variation for every chain with at most five sites, but has the sharp two-input coefficient \(9/8\) on six sites. Its Gini amplification grows at least as \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/[2(n-2)]\) and at most as \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/(n-1)\) for every \(n\ge6\), even though the selector is uniformly nonexpansive in the maximum norm.
