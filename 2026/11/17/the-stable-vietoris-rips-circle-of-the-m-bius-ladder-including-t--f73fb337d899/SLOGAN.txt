For every \(r\ge2\) and \(n\ge3r\), the inclusive graph-metric Rips complex of the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) has homotopy type \(S^1\), including the critical equality \(n=3r\) where the untwisted circular ladder instead acquires two-dimensional spheres. The stable-range persistence maps are isomorphisms and exactly \(n\) first-homology bars die at scale two.
