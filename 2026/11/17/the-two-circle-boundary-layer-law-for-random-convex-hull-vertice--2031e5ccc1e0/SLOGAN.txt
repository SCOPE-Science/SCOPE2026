Two concentric circles separated by radial distance of order \(n^{-2}\) exhibit an exact crossover: among \(\alpha n\) random inner points and \(n\) random outer points, the proportion of inner points surviving on the convex hull tends to a specified two-dimensional exponential integral \(p(\lambda,\alpha)\). The transition separates complete survival at smaller radial gaps from zero survival at larger gaps, with a strictly density-dependent limit at the critical scale.
