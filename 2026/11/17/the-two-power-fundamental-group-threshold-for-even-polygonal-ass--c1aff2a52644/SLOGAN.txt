For the ordinary geometric quotients of the noncrossing-diagonal sphere of every even polygon with at least six vertices, every dihedral-subgroup fixed space is explicitly a sphere or empty. All quotient fundamental groups are trivial except for the full rotation quotient at polygon sizes \(n=2^e\) with \(e\ge3\), when the group is exactly \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\); adding a reflection removes it.
