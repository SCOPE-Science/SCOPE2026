Generic linear programming methods do not provide the exact numerical invariant without a full geometry-specific computation and proof. Differently indexed calculations remain a residual priority risk.

The ratio \(r\sqrt{1525}>22\) exhibits a striking separation between lattice-centered distance-one facet normalization and Euclidean optimal centering. The claim is restricted to this natural canonical input, not an artificial parameter slice.

Equivalent unpublished or differently described prior calculations cannot be ruled out by indexed searches alone.
