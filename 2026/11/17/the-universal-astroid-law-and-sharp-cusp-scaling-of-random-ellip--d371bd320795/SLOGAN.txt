For three independent equiaffine-uniform vertices on any noncircular ellipse, the rescaled Euclidean circumcenter has a universal astroid-supported probability law with an explicit density and every mixed moment. Its probability of lying within normalized horizontal distance \(t\) of a cusp is asymptotic to \(4\sqrt2\,t^{3/2}/(3\pi^2)\), and the corresponding angular triple has a total-variation uniform-ellipsoid limit.
