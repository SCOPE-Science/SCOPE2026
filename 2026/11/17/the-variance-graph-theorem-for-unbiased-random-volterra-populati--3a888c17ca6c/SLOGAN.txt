For an unbiased random Volterra population map with independently fluctuating pairwise interactions, every trajectory converges to a composition supported on an independent set of the positive-variance interaction graph, and these compositions support exactly all stationary probabilities. Universal single-species fixation holds if and only if that graph is complete; in that case species \(i\) wins with probability equal to its initial mass, and all losers have exact strictly negative logarithmic extinction rates.
