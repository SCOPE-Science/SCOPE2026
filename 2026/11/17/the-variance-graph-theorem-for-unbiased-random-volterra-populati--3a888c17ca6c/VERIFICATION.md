The analytic argument derives a conditional identity for the increase of squared population mass, obtains almost-sure limits by bounded martingale convergence, and identifies every stationary probability by integrating the exact nonnegative drift. A separate strong-law comparison gives the full-graph extinction rates. These are infinite-horizon analytic arguments, not extrapolations from finite computation.

Run `python check_variance_graph.py` using Python 3 standard library only. The script exercises 416 exact sign realizations in dimensions two through four for the update, martingale identity, variance-drift identity, and stationary independent-set faces; a correlated cyclic comparison is also checked. Finite enumeration alone does not prove convergence or publication priority.

Verification boundary: these calculations do not cover biased or correlated edge noise, nor unbounded interaction coefficients or infinite-dimensional species spaces. Independent audit has not been performed.
