For three equiaffine-uniform points on a very thin ellipse, the chance of forming an acute triangle that also encloses the ellipse center is asymptotic to \(6\log2/(\pi^2L^2)\), where \(L\) is the semiaxis ratio. Conditioned on this rare conjunction, the normalized near-vertical pair and the remaining vertex have independent arcsine and logarithmic-ratio marks, so a typical rare acute triangle no longer encloses the ellipse center.
