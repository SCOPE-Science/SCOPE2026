Among all standard quartic Savitzky–Golay center smoothers on odd windows of at least five samples, the unique largest worst-case total-variation gain occurs at thirteen points and equals \(3763/2431\), attained by a single jump. As the window grows, the gain instead converges to a smaller exact constant \(1.504091623350886\ldots\), demonstrating a finite-window instability peak distinct from the continuum limit.
