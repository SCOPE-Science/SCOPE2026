The full strong discrete-Morse matching complex of the eight-vertex cactus made from two unfilled triangles and a four-cycle sharing one hub is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{13}S^3\). Its entire rational \((C_2\wr S_2)\times C_2\) top-homology character is determined by exact signed simplex traces, showing that adding a square to the two-triangle friendship graph preserves thirteen sphere summands while shifting their dimension from one to three.
