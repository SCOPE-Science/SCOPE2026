The symmetric orthogonal tents map has exactly 32 primitive period-three cycles, all smooth hyperbolic, with return traces \(102\), \(-34\), and \(-98\) occurring on 16, eight, and eight cycles. Its 100 fixed points of the third iterate occupy only the rational \(1/18\) and \(1/50\) grids, in contrast to its previously known period-two segments and nonsmooth period-four cycles.
