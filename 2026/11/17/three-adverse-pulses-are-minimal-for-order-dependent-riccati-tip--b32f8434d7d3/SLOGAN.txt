For \(\dot x=q(t)-x^2\), two finite adverse pulses can alter the post-exposure state but cannot alter its side of the basin threshold \(-1\) when their order is reversed, provided neither order develops a pole during exposure. Three pulses are minimal for reversing recovery into finite-time escape: one exact rational example has terminal values \(-6/7\) and \(-3/2\) under opposite first-two-pulse orders.
