Every positive-margin \(m\times n\) transportation polytope with \(m,n\ge3\) has an exact ridge-adjacency graph determined by strict two- and three-margin inequalities, and every interior ridge angle belongs to a universal three-value alphabet depending only on \(m,n\). In central transportation polytopes, all facets meet pairwise in ridges except for the \(3\times3\) anomaly; as both sides grow, the scaled mean angle lies on the opposite side of a right angle from the typical scaled ridge.
