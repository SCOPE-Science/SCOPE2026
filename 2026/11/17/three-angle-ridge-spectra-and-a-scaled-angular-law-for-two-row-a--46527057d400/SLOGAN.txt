For the two-row full-cardinality \(2\)-assignment polytope with \(n\ge4\), every interior ridge angle belongs to an exact three-angle spectrum with respective multiplicities \(n(n-1)\), \(n^2\), and \(2n(n-1)\); at \(n=3\) the acute class disappears. Despite asymptotically right angles, the uniformly sampled, dimension-rescaled signed ridge angle converges to a nondegenerate three-point law of positive mean \((\sqrt2-1)/4\).
