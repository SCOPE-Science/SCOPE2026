For every finite isolate-free bipartite graph, the canonical Euclidean stable-set polytope has exactly three ridge dihedral angles, \(\pi/4\), \(\pi/2\) and \(2\pi/3\), with multiplicities determined by leaves, four-cycles and degree wedges. The complete angular histogram recovers all three graph invariants; for tree sequences, right angles dominate precisely when maximum degree is sublinear, whereas growing complete bicliques retain a one-third mass of \(2\pi/3\) angles.
