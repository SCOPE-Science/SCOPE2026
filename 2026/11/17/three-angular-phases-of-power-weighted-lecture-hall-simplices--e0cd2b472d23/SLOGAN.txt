The spherical normal-cone measure of power-weighted lecture-hall simplices has three sharply different vertex-rank scales: linear with an explicit transformed-arcsine law for \(\alpha>-1/2\), logarithmic at \(\alpha=-1/2\), and an almost surely eventually fixed index for \(\alpha<-1/2\). An exact independent left/right Gaussian-persistence factorization supplies every finite-dimensional vertex angle and reduces to the discrete arcsine law for equal weights.
