For a unilateral shift with alternating positive weights \(a,b\), the symmetrized and antisymmetrized products with its adjoint have exactly the same full and essential spectrum: a central \([-ab,ab]\) band and two outer dimerized bands. A sharp three-band transition occurs at weight ratio \(1+\sqrt2\), while both operators have the unattained norm \((a^2+b^2)/2\).
