Complete-pair Gini contraction for independent Boolean noise first departs from the Hilbert spectral coefficient \(\rho\) at three bits, where the exact sharp coefficient is \(\rho(15-\rho^2)/14\). In growing dimension the operator norm has a sharp cutoff scale \(d\rho^2\asymp1\); a single-site signal has the exact critical Gaussian profile \(2\Phi(a/\sqrt2)-1\), without an asserted full-norm critical-window formula.
