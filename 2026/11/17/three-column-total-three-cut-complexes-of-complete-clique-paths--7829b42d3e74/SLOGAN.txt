For every \(m\ge2\), the total three-cut complex of the three-column clique-path product \(K_m\square P_3\) is exactly \(\bigvee^{(m-1)^2}S^{3m-6}\) up to integral homotopy. A uniform coordinate-toggle matching establishes the first arbitrary-width three-column case of the published total-three-cut conjecture, strengthening its homology prediction to a sphere-wedge classification.
