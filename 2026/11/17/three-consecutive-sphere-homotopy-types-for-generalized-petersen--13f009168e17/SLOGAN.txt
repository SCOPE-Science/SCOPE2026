The degree-two edge-matching complexes of the three consecutive generalized Petersen graphs \(\operatorname{GP}(6,2)\), \(\operatorname{GP}(7,2)\), and \(\operatorname{GP}(8,2)\) have integral homotopy types \(S^8\), \(S^{10}\), and \(S^{12}\), respectively. Complete acyclic matchings and primitive signed integer Morse boundaries certify all three finite classifications.
