In a double-critical symmetric two-species Beverton–Holt mixture, weak cross-competition gives synchronized \(n^{-1}\) extinction with a noninteger imbalance exponent, while strong cross-competition selects the initially larger species and makes the minority decay as \(n^{-\tau}\). At the exact competition threshold, the population ratio is conserved and the reciprocal total population acquires a logarithmic correction of coefficient \(-\eta(1-\eta)(a-b)^2/c\), revealing the two crowding channels' heterogeneity.
