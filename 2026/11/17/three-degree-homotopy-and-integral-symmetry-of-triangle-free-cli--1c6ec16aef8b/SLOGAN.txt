For \(s,n\ge2\) and \(q_i\ge3\), the triangle-free subset complex of \(K_s\vee\bigsqcup_i K_{q_i}\) is exactly a wedge of \(\binom{s}{2}\) circles, \(s\prod_i(q_i-1)\) spheres of dimension \(n\), and \(\prod_i\binom{q_i-1}{2}\) spheres of dimension \(2n-1\). Its three integral automorphism-homology modules are explicit exterior-power and augmentation-lattice representations, and the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
