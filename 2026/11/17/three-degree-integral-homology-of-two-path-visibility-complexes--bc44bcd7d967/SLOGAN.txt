For every \(n\ge5\), requiring two clear shortest paths between selected nonadjacent vertices of \(K_{n,n,n}\) produces a simplicial complex with torsion-free integral reduced homology in exactly three degrees: \(2n-1\), \(3n-5\), and \(3n-4\), of ranks \(3(n-1)(2n-1)\), \(3(n-1)^2\), and \(1\). An Alexander-dual three-cover calculation identifies the integral lattice map responsible for the extra two high-degree families.
