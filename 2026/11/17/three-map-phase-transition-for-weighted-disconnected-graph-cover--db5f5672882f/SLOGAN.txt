For a finite graph covering with one identity sheet and one connected \(d\)-sheeted component, the best approximation of its weighted fiber average by three extreme Markov operators has an exact sharp transition at mass \(w=d/(d+1)\). Below the transition an identity-section atom is optimal; above it, three moving atoms on the nontrivial component attain the optimum, with a distinctive constant plateau when \(d=2\).
