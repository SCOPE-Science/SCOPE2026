Euclidean projection onto a circular cone of aperture \(\kappa\), measured in the coordinate maximum norm, has an exact all-dimensional Lipschitz modulus given by one axial and two one-variable spatial formulas. As spatial dimension grows, its sharp amplification switches from polar-side spatial sensitivity to axial sensitivity at \(\kappa=1/2\), then to cone-side spatial sensitivity at \(\kappa=2\).
