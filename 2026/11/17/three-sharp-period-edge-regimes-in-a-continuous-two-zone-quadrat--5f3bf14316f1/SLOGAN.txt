A continuous two-zone quadratic center undergoes a sharp global transition at its upper restoring coefficient \(k=1\): its period grows like \(\sqrt E\) for \(k<1\), like \((1/2-E)^{-1/2}\) at \(k=1\), and logarithmically at a finite saddle barrier for \(k>1\). All three leading original-time coefficients are independent of the lower-zone restoring coefficient.
