For normalized Laplace exponentials with decay rates \(e^{-cj^\alpha}\), the finite Gram determinant crosses from a sharp \(\pi^2 N^{2-\alpha}/[2c\alpha(2-\alpha)]\) negative log-volume law to exponential decay and then to a nonzero Fredholm limit as \(\alpha\) crosses one. The same transition marks failure of every Bessel bound, stable continuous-frame recovery, and finally trace-class closeness to orthogonality.
