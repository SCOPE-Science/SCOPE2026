# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-parameter two-stage acyclic matching is defined on every face by its intersection of 2-/3-subsets, followed by the complete classification of intersecting Johnson cliques. After first-stage cancellation, the only candidate faces are triangular 2-set cliques, their 3-set cones, and four-triple tops. Matching each triangle to its cone leaves one 0-cell and one 3-cell per four-set. The global acyclicity argument uses monotonicity of rank-two vertex count along directed Hasse edges; any upward triangle-to-cone match forces a strict drop on the next downward edge. This proves ordinary sphere-wedge homotopy over the integers without finite extrapolation. Each octahedral cycle pairs by a unit with a maximal three-face cocycle, giving an integral basis. The orientation of four join factors gives the exterior-power representation. Exhaustive whole-Hasse acyclicity, critical-count, and antipodal checks for sizes four through eight, plus an independent modular chain-rank computation through size seven, corroborate the symbolic proof. The finite checker is not an independent proof of the infinite case.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The closest full-text published record on the weight-at-most-three function–Rips sublevel includes **weight zero** and determines rational homology as the direct sum of third and fourth exterior powers. It does not state a deletion theorem, ordinary homotopy of the rank-interval complex, or the integral primitive exterior-fourth-power lattice. Adamaszek–Adams treat full cubes and initial binary-order vertex sets; Bendersky–Elia–Grbić treat full cubes and general cohomology classes; another indexed result handles two adjacent ranks. The rank-interval theorem requires a new explicit matching and unit cocycle comparison. A clever implicit reduction from the previous cumulative-level arguments might imply part of the homological formula; this is a genuine remaining overlap risk, not a statement that searches prove novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The punctured first three nonzero Hamming layers are a natural finite-metric subspace and a clean boundary object for function–Rips filtrations. A uniform infinite-family *integral homotopy* classification and complete integral symmetry lattice, including canonical minimal eight-vertex sphere representatives, give stronger information than the previously known cumulative-level rational character. The result clarifies which local four-coordinate classes remain after deleting the weight-zero vertex and exhibits an explicit all-parameter homological basis. It is not a routine restatement of known complete-cube Betti numbers.
