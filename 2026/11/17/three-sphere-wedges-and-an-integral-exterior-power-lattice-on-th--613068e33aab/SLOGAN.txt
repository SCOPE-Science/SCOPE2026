The scale-two Rips complex on Boolean Hamming-weight layers \(1,2,3\) is a wedge of exactly \(\binom n4\) three-spheres, for every \(n\ge4\). Its full **integral** coordinate-permutation homology is the exterior-fourth-power lattice \(\bigwedge^4\mathbb Z^n\), generated primitively by explicit eight-vertex octahedral spheres.
