The three remaining positive four-row seven-column capacity patterns of total eight, \((1,1,1,5)\), \((1,1,2,4)\), and \((1,1,3,3)\), have integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^{15}S^5\), \(\bigvee^{57}S^5\), and \(\bigvee^{57}S^5\). These torsion-free yet nonshellable pure six-dimensional complexes sit alongside the published two-torsion case \((2,2,2,2)\).
