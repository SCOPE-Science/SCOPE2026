The ordinary quotient of the Paley-17 Lovász open-neighborhood complex by all \(136\) affine graph symmetries is rationally acyclic but retains exactly one three-primary class, \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\), with all other reduced three-local homology zero. An exact oriented-orbit chain calculation separates this quotient phenomenon from the previously known torsion in the original complex.
