For a transitive finite group action on the source of a complete-graph Hom complex, every simultaneous source–target fixed space is exactly a join of finite sets, one per target orbit admitting an equivariant map to the source. The size of each finite set is a stabilizer-fixed-point count, yielding complete integral homotopy types and, for example, a genuine fixed circle for the noncyclic \(S_4\) action on \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_4,K_{24})\).
