At Hamming scale two, all sixty-four translations of the quaternary three-cube have an explicit character on the previously known \(405\)-dimensional third rational Rips homology: the ten coordinate-cycle types have traces \(405,9,9,-3,-3,45,1,1,1,1\). The full translation quotient retains exactly nine rational three-dimensional classes, while its half-turn-subgroup quotient retains seventy-one.
