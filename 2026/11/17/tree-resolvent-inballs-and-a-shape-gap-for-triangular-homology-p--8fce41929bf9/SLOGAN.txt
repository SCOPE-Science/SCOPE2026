For a polygon triangulated into \(m\) triangles, the symmetric polytope of its signed triangle-boundary vectors has exact Euclidean inradius \((\mathbf1^\top(3I-A_T)^{-1}\mathbf1)^{-1/2}\), two tangent facets at its centered inball, and an \((m-1)\)-dimensional family of all optimal ball centers. With equal triangle counts, path and complete-binary dual trees give inradii whose ratio tends to \(1/\sqrt2\), despite defining combinatorially identical crosspolytopes.
