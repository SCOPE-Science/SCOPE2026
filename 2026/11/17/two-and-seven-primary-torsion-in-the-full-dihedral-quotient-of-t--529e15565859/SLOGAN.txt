The full order-fourteen dihedral symmetry quotient of the seven-cycle cyclohedron boundary is simply connected and has precisely \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z/7\) and \(H_4\cong\mathbb Z/2\) as nonzero reduced integral homology. Its two distinct torsion primes are invisible in rational homology and arise in a natural five-dimensional orbifold-type quotient.
