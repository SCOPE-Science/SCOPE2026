For the diameter-one Veronese surface of real projective lines in five-dimensional trace-zero matrix space, one and two unrestricted Euclidean balls require exactly the same radius \(1/\sqrt3\), and every optimal two-ball cover uses the origin as a center. Three explicitly placed balls already attain radius \(\sqrt{21}/8\), strictly smaller, so three is the exact first ball count at which the optimal covering radius drops.
