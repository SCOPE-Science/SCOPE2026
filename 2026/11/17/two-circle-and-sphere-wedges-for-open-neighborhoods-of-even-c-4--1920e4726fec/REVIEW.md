# Scientific review: even four-by-cycle open-neighborhood classification

## Correctness — PASS
The objects are exactly the open-neighborhood complexes of \(C_4\square C_n\), for even \(n\ge6\). Every maximal simplex is an explicitly enumerated four-element neighborhood. The fiberwise row-zero matching is acyclic by the patchwork criterion; the only surviving non-row-zero faces are a specified edge, four triangles, and one tetrahedron per column. Two additional disjoint matching families leave one critical zero-cell, one critical one-cell, and \(n\) critical two-cells in each of the two bipartition components. The absence of gradient cycles is proved by the non-row-zero-support monotonicity argument. A globally defined integer winding cocycle evaluates to \(n\) on each residual one-cycle, ruling out every nonzero two-cell attaching degree. This establishes the claimed *integral homotopy type*, not merely Betti ranks. The standalone checker confirms all finite constituent identities for even \(n=6,8,10,12,14,16,18,20\); independent mod-two chains check the first four of these. The complete mathematical argument handles every even \(n\ge6\).

## Originality — PASS, with specific residual risk
The closest high-coverage result is the October 11, 2026 theorem on open neighborhoods of toroidal lattice quotients. Its complete RESULT.md was inspected; the explicit shortest-period condition \(\lambda_1(L)\ge5\) excludes the period \((4,0)\) here. Shukla's original 2019 full text, Theorem 6(B)/13, proves related results for four-regular *circulant* graphs. It covers the odd-\(n\) cyclic product, deliberately omitted from the present claim, but does not give the even-\(n\) noncirculant products. Namiq's June 2, 2026 neighborhood-complex classification concerns graphs with no induced cycles above length four and hence does not imply this homotopy classification (the relevant full document was not obtained). Actual semantic published-finding corpus searches under cycle-product, Cartesian-product, bipartite-cover and homotopy/Betti aliases found no exact or dominating statement. The source and implication comparisons, rather than negative search alone, support novelty. A nonindexed equivalent or a more general unexamined published theorem remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem fills a natural, excluded short-period family adjacent to a recent toroidal-grid homotopy classification. The difference is structural: where long-period quotients collapse to tori, the width-four even products have two components with one essential circle and \(n\) independent two-spheres each. It supplies an infinite parameter family, exact homotopy rather than only homology, and a geometric winding obstruction that can be reused when short lattice periods invalidate local surface collapses. This is not a degree substitution or an arbitrary small parameter census.
