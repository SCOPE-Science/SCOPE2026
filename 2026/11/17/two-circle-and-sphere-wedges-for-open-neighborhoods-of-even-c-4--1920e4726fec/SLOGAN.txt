For every even \(n\ge6\), the open Lovász neighborhood complex of the four-by-\(n\) toroidal grid \(C_4\square C_n\) has exactly two components, each homotopy equivalent to \(S^1\vee\bigvee^n S^2\). An explicit acyclic matching and a nonzero integral winding cocycle prove the complete infinite-family homotopy classification at the short-period boundary of earlier toroidal-grid theorems.
