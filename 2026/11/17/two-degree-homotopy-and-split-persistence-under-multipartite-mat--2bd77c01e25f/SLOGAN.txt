Deleting a matching between two shores of a complete multipartite graph creates a sharp one-step loss followed by persistent higher-dimensional births in its \(q\)-clique-free complex: the stages have homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\binom{s}{q-1}}S^{q-2}\vee\bigvee^{(t-1)\binom{s}{q-2}}S^{q-1}\) after the first deletion, for every \(q\ge4\) and \(s\ge q-1\). The inclusion maps split integrally, and the result does not depend on the sizes of the two non-singleton shores once they accommodate the matching.
