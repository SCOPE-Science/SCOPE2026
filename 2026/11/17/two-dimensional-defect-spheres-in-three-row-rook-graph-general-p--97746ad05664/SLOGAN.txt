For every \(n\ge3\), the general-position simplicial complex of the three-by-\(n\) rook graph is \(\bigvee^{2^n}S^{n-1}\vee\bigvee^nS^2\), with integral homology split by its column-thin subcomplex. The \(n\) extra two-spheres arise from independent thick-column attachments, and row permutations act by sign on their integral homology generators when \(n\ge4\).
