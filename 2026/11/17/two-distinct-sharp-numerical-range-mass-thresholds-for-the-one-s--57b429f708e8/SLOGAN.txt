The one-sided lattice Dirac operator has an explicit numerical-range support function and two *different* sharp mass thresholds: the numerical radius stays at \(2c\) until \(mc^2=\sqrt2c\), while its maximal centered-disk radius reaches a permanent \(2c\) plateau only at \(mc^2=\sqrt3c\). Its closed numerical range has exactly two vertical flat faces, and no boundary point is attained by a Hilbert-space vector.
