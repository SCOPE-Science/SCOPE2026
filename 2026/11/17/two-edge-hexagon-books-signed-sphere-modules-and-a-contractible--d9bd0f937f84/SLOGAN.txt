For every two-edge-spine book of \(h\ge2\) hexagons, the graph-metric scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex is a wedge of \(h\) two-spheres whose **integral** symmetry action is the arm-permutation module twisted by the pole-reversal sign. The true quotient by all graph automorphisms is nevertheless contractible, while quotienting only by arm permutations leaves a two-sphere.
