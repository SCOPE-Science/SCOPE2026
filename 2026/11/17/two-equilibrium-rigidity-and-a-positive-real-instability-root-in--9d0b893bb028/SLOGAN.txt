For a phage cocktail with any finite number of distinct types but a common positive latency, the biologically feasible coexistence equilibria number at most two, except for an exactly characterized continuum degeneracy; the two-state bound is sharp for every cocktail size. The lower-bacterial-population state, when two exist, always has a positive **real** delay-characteristic exponent, certified by \(\Delta_s(0)=\mu s\prod_i(m_i+A_i s)F'(s)\).
