For the complete escaping-side family of Volokitin's cubic Darboux flow, the orbit-to-diagonal strip has finite Euclidean area precisely for \(0<p<1\), but the orbit has a finite affine asymptote precisely for \(0<p\le2\). At the two different geometric thresholds, exact area and transverse-distance laws admit uniform logarithmic and exponential critical crossovers, including an orbit-dependent nonzero parallel offset when \(p=2\).
