# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact claim covers every integer \(q\ge2\) and intrinsic-volume rank \(0\le k\le q+1\) in the inherited Euclidean root embedding. A Kubota projection argument gives the zonotope wedge-volume identity, graph incidence independence is exactly the forest condition, and the tree incidence Gram determinant is the component size. The two-hub forest classification is exhaustive: zero or one double-attached peripheral is permitted, and exactly one is needed for connected hubs. Multinomial and binomial expectation identities yield the two-term asymptotic, while a uniform square-root tilt estimate transfers a binomial CLT to the intrinsic-rank law. The checker enumerates all edge subsets through \(q=7\) and directly replays integer Gram determinants through \(q=6\). Finite computations corroborate, but do not replace, the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority reservation

The general zonotope wedge formula and the graphical-zonotope construction are established inputs. Padrol–Pilaud–Poullot (arXiv:2111.12422, full HTML read) concerns deformation cones and graph fans; Pešović–Stojadinović (arXiv:2209.12053, full HTML read) treats enumerators and intermediate graph polytopes; Chapoton–Pilaud (arXiv:2201.06896, abstract and publication description read) places complete bipartite graphical zonotopes in shuffle constructions. None of the materially read texts states a complete Euclidean \(K_{2,q}\) intrinsic-volume polynomial or the sharp weighted forest-connectivity expansion. Dong–Ge's 2022 spanning-tree-completion formula is related but is not a geometric wedge-volume enumeration; its abstract was checked, not its full text. Previously available Euclidean volume/facet-area formulas for the same graph already account for the top intrinsic-volume ranks, which are treated as known and are not independent novelties. Published mathematical-index searches and accessible existing claims found no stronger result implying the **full coefficient sequence plus the asymptotic forest law**. Unindexed equivalents and incomplete access to some related primary texts are residual risks; nonappearance alone is not a uniqueness certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This provides the full metric Steiner/Wills coefficient sequence for a natural infinite graphical-zonotope family, not a single arbitrary rank. It also gives an intrinsic geometric probability measure on its spanning forests and reveals a sharp \(q^{-1/2}\) disconnection scale in place of the uniform \(q^{-1}\) scale, with an explicit two-term constant, plus a quantitative Gaussian intrinsic-rank law. This comparison distinguishes metric forest weighting from ordinary forest counting and can inform asymptotic Steiner-polynomial studies.

## Limitations

Only the two-hub family, canonical Euclidean scale, and the specified asymptotic regime are covered. There is no independent mathematical validation and no claim that the general zonotope identity or known top intrinsic volumes are new.
