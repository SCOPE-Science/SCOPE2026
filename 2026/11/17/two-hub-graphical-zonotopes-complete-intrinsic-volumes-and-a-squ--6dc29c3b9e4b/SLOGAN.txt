The complete Euclidean intrinsic-volume polynomial of the two-hub graphical zonotope of \(K_{2,q}\) splits exactly into disconnected- and connected-hub forest terms. Under its natural wedge-volume forest weighting, the chance that the hubs remain disconnected is \(\sqrt{3/(2q)}-3/(2q)+O(q^{-3/2})\), in contrast to the uniform-forest probability \(3/(q+3)\); its normalized intrinsic-volume rank is asymptotically Gaussian.
