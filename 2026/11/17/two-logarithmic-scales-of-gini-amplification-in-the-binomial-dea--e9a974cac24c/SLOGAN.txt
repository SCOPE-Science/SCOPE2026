On the finite binomial death semigroup, complete-pair Gini amplification has two different logarithmic time scales: order-\(n\) growth at \(t=\log n+O(1)\), followed by the exact limiting coefficient \(e^{-s}/6\) at \(t=2\log n+s\). This yields a sharp late-window threshold and a Gini-to-Dobrushin coefficient ratio asymptotic to \(n/6\), even though the underlying dynamics are stochastic averaging.
