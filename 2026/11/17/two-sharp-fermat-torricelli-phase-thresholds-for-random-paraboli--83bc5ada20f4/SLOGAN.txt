Three parameter-uniform points on a finite Euclidean parabola arc exhibit two sharp Fermat–Torricelli topology transitions, at half-widths \(1/\sqrt3\) and \(\sqrt3/2+2\sqrt6/3\): first an interior Steiner junction becomes possible, then either endpoint can become the minimizing hub. Both rare new topologies appear with explicit \(5/2\)-power probability laws and uniform marked boundary-layer limits.
