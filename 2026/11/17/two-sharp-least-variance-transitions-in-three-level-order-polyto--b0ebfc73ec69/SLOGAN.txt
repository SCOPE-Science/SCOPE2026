For uniform Euclidean volume on the order polytope of the three-rank poset \(A_p\oplus A_q\oplus A_p\), the complete covariance spectrum is explicit and its least-variance eigenspace undergoes two exact size transitions. The minimizing directions switch from within-middle contrasts to block-constant contrasts and finally to within-outer contrasts, with a \((2p-1)\)-dimensional tie exactly at \(q=p+3\).
