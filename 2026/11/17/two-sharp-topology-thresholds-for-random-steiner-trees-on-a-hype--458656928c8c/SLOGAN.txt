For three independent arclength-uniform terminals on a hyperbolic horocycle segment of length \(L\), the shortest Steiner network is always a two-edge path up to the sharp threshold \(L=4/\sqrt3\), then undergoes a \(5/2\)-power transition. Beyond \(L=2\sqrt3\), its two-edge probability is exactly \(24I/L^2-48B/L^3\), with a second third-derivative transition and an independent uniform location–gap limit conditional on this rare topology.
