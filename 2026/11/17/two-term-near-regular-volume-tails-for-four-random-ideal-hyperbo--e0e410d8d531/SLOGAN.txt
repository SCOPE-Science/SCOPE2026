Four independent uniform ideal vertices in hyperbolic three-space form an almost-regular tetrahedron with probability \(\bigl(64v_3-32\sqrt3\bigr)\varepsilon/9+32\bigl(104\sqrt3v_3-183\bigr)\varepsilon^2/243+O(\varepsilon^3)\) when the volume deficit is at most \(\varepsilon\). The second-order coefficient is explicitly negative, and the conditioned cross-ratio displacement converges to a uniform disk law around one of two equilateral shapes.
