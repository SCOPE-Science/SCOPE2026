For three independent Haar-uniform great circles, the probability that their largest spherical cell has area above \(2\pi-\varepsilon\) is \(\frac{\varepsilon^4}{168}(1-\frac{7\varepsilon^2}{330}+O(\varepsilon^4))\). Given this rare event, the normalized area shortfall and the three remaining cell-area proportions approach an independent beta law and an explicit non-Dirichlet simplex law, with \(O(\varepsilon^2)\) total-variation error.
