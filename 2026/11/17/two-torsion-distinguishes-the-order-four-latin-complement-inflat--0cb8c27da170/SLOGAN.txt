For the twelve-vertex Latin-complement inflation associated with an order-four Latin square, the cyclic isotopy class yields \(\bigvee^{11}S^2\), whereas the Klein-four class has \(\pi_1\cong H_1\cong\mathbb Z/2\) and \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{11}\). Both have identical face counts and uniformly surjective local restriction maps, showing that local lifting does not control even integral torsion when global sheaf flabbiness fails.
