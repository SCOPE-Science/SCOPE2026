For the proper-part type-\(B_4\) noncrossing partition complex, quotienting by Coxeter-element conjugation creates precisely \(\mathbb Z/2\) in integral first homology while leaving nine free second-homology generators. An exhaustive signed-permutation orbit-cell computation proves \(H_0=\mathbb Z\), \(H_1=\mathbb Z/2\), and \(H_2=\mathbb Z^9\).
