# Type-\(D\) Coxeter permutohedra: a complete ridge-angle spectrum and an exact fork-induced mean deficit

## Finding
For every integer \(n\ge4\), let \(P\subset\mathbb R^n\) be a full-dimensional Euclidean polytope with the *full type-\(D_n\) Coxeter normal fan*, with respect to the standard coordinate inner product. For instance, \(P\) may be the convex hull of all even-signed permutations of \((a_1,\ldots,a_n)\) for arbitrary real numbers \(0<a_1<\cdots<a_n\). Let \(\Theta_n\) be the interior dihedral angle at a uniformly selected codimension-two face (ridge), with each distinct ridge counted once.

The following **complete multiset**, with coincident angles merged by adding multiplicities, describes every ridge:

1. For \(1\le k<\ell\le n-2\), the angle \(\pi-\arccos\sqrt{k/\ell}\) occurs \(2^\ell\binom n\ell\binom\ell k\) times.
2. For \(1\le k\le n-2\), the angle \(\pi-\arccos\sqrt{k/n}\) occurs \(2^n\binom n k\) times (the two spin branches combined).
3. The angle \(\pi-\arccos((n-2)/n)\) occurs \(n2^{n-1}\) times (the spin-spin branch).

In particular the total number of ridges and their **exact arithmetic mean** are
\[
R_n=5^n-2\cdot3^n+1-n4^{n-1}+n2^{n-1},
\qquad
\mathbb E\Theta_n=\frac{3\pi}{4}-\frac{\pi n2^{n-3}}{R_n}.
\]
Thus \(\mathbb E\Theta_n<3\pi/4\) in every rank \(n\ge4\), in contrast to the exactly \(3\pi/4\) ridge mean of the full type-\(B_n\) Coxeter fan. Moreover,
\[
\frac{3\pi}{4}-\mathbb E\Theta_n\sim\frac{\pi n}{8}\left(\frac25\right)^n.
\]

## Assumptions and scope
The angles are *interior* dihedral angles, measured intrinsically in the ambient Euclidean metric. The complete Coxeter normal fan assumption is essential: coarsening the normal fan may delete ridges, and an arbitrary affine map can change angles. Full-dimensional generic Weyl-orbit polytopes satisfy the hypothesis, but the result does not require specified support numbers. The spin labels refer to the two terminal nodes of the forked \(D_n\) Dynkin diagram. For \(n=4\), distinct displayed branches can have identical numerical angles, so the formulas describe a multiset rather than necessarily distinct angle levels.

## Proof
Write \(e_1,\ldots,e_n\) for the orthonormal coordinate basis. A simple root system is \(\alpha_i=e_i-e_{i+1}\) for \(1\le i\le n-1\), and \(\alpha_n=e_{n-1}+e_n\). Its fundamental weights are
\[
\omega_k=e_1+\cdots+e_k\quad(1\le k\le n-2),\qquad
\omega_{n-1}=\tfrac12(e_1+\cdots+e_{n-1}-e_n),\quad
\omega_n=\tfrac12(e_1+\cdots+e_n).
\]
The Weyl group \(W(D_n)\) has order \(2^{n-1}n!\), acting by coordinate permutations and sign changes of even parity. Since the complete normal fan is the Coxeter fan, its two-dimensional cones are exactly the Weyl images of pairs of distinct rays \((\mathbb R_{\ge0}\omega_i,\mathbb R_{\ge0}\omega_j)\), \(i<j\). Each two-dimensional cone corresponds to a single ridge. A ray pair with labels \(i,j\) has stabilizer equal to the standard parabolic generated by all simple reflections except \(s_i,s_j\); consequently it occurs \(|W|/|W_{S\setminus\{i,j\}}|\) times. Distinct labeled orbit types are disjoint because the Coxeter complex labels face types.

**Ordinary-ordinary.** For \(1\le k<\ell\le n-2\), removal of nodes \(k,\ell\) gives parabolic factors of types \(A_{k-1}\), \(A_{\ell-k-1}\), and \(D_{n-\ell}\) (under the conventions \(|W(D_2)|=4\) and \(|W(D_1)|=2\) for these residual signed-permutation factors; equivalently, count arbitrary permutations within the three blocks and even sign choices on the terminal block). The stabilizer order is \(k!(\ell-k)!2^{n-\ell-1}(n-\ell)!\), hence the orbit size is \(2^\ell\binom n\ell\binom\ell k\). Their outward normals have cosine \(\langle\omega_k,\omega_\ell\rangle/(\|\omega_k\|\|\omega_\ell\|)=\sqrt{k/\ell}\).

**Ordinary-spin.** For \(1\le k\le n-2\), and either spin node, the parabolic has order \(k!(n-k)!\), giving \(2^{n-1}\binom nk\) ridges per spin label. The cosine is \(\sqrt{k/n}\). Summing over both labels doubles the multiplicity.

**Spin-spin.** Removing both spin nodes leaves a parabolic group \(S_{n-1}\) of order \((n-1)!\), giving \(n2^{n-1}\) ridges, with cosine \((n-2)/n\). In each case the interior dihedral angle is \(\pi\) minus the angle between the outward facet normals. This establishes the spectrum. Summing the three multiplicity families, using binomial identities, yields the stated \(R_n\).

For the mean, subtract \(3\pi/4\) from every angle before summation. The ordinary-ordinary terms cancel in pairs by \(k\leftrightarrow\ell-k\), since \(\arccos\sqrt{k/\ell}+\arccos\sqrt{(\ell-k)/\ell}=\pi/2\). For either spin branch, the full binomial family indexed by \(0\le k\le n\) would similarly cancel. The omitted endpoints \(k=0,n\) cancel each other, but the omitted term \(k=n-1\), with weight \(n\), leaves a per-branch centered sum of \(-n(\pi/4-\arcsin(1/\sqrt n))\), before its multiplier \(2^{n-1}\). The remaining spin-spin term contributes \(n2^{n-1}(\pi/4-2\arcsin(1/\sqrt n))\), using \(\arccos(1-2/n)=2\arcsin(1/\sqrt n)\). Combining both spin branches and the spin-spin branch cancels all inverse-sine terms and leaves exactly \(-\pi n2^{n-3}\). Divide by \(R_n\). Finally \(R_n\sim5^n\), which gives the asymptotic deficit.

## Verification
The standalone `check_type_d.py` reconstructs the fundamental weights using integer coordinates doubled to eliminate halves, enumerates all even-signed permutation images, and forms every distinct unordered pair of rays spanning a two-cone for \(n=4,5,6\). It independently compares the rational squared cosines and multiplicities against the three closed formulas, verifies counts \(240,1440,8216\), and tests the mean formula for multiple additional ranks. The finite checks support, but do not replace, the general Coxeter-fan and parabolic-index proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
Ardila, Castillo, Eur and Postnikov establish the Coxeter-fan / fundamental-weight framework for Coxeter permutohedra, including the ray and chamber interpretation. Doker describes the type-\(D\) signed-permutation orbit polytopes and their facet-ray counts. Neither inspected source develops this complete Euclidean ridge-angle multiset or its angular mean deficit. The full type-\(B\) Coxeter fan has an existing \(3\pi/4\) exact mean, and the present theorem identifies a distinct, fork-sensitive type-\(D\) deviation; previously established general Coxeter fan incidence theory is not claimed as new.

## Limitations
This is a theorem about polytopes with the exact Coxeter normal fan, not all type-\(D\) generalized permutohedra with possibly coarsened fans, nor affine images under nonorthogonal transformations. It does not classify the number of distinct numerical angles after merging collisions in every rank, and does not assert independence from unindexed earlier literature. Independent external mathematical validation has not been performed.
