# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For regular integral GLMY degree-one path homology on Cay(Z/n,S) with 1∈S and n>2 max S, Ω₂ is integrally generated by two-path triangles with sum in S and pair differences at a forbidden common sum. Pairing ordered steps (1,s) and (s,1) forces f_s(x)=e_s(x)−sum_{j=0}^{s−1}e_1(x+j) to be independent of x; eliminating these unit-coefficient relations leaves the one winding generator and a Z-module on E_s for s≠1 modulo all short additive relations. The base-100 separated even step blocks prevent both between-block collisions and collisions involving 1. Each eight-step block T={0,1,9,10,13,15,17,20} has six independent collision relations and an integral presentation with two free generators a,b plus z=X15−a−15b subject exactly to 2z=0. Independently enumerated pair-sum relations and computed Smith invariant factors 1,1,1,1,1,2; additional r=1,2,3,5 collision searches confirm separation but are not extrapolated. This yields H1=Z^{2r+1}⊕(Z/2)^r uniformly for every r≥1 and n>2 max S; the step 1 ensures strong connectivity. No higher degree homology is claimed.
