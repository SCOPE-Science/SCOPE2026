For the ordinary complexes of graphs failing \(k\)-vertex-connectivity, regular cyclic vertex rotation acts freely exactly when the vertex count is prime and \(k\) is one or two. For each prime \(p\ge5\), the quotient of the not-two-connected graph complex has an integral degree-two class \(u\) with \(u^{p-3}\neq0\), giving an unbounded cup-length lower bound despite the covering complex being a sphere wedge.
