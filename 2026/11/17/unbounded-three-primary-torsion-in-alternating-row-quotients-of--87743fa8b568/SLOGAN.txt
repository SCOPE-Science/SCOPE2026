For every \(n\ge7\), the ordinary alternating-row symmetry quotient of the four-by-\(n\) chessboard complex is simply connected and has \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z/6\oplus(\mathbb Z/3)^{n-2}\). Its unbounded three-primary torsion follows from an exact reduction of the quotient cellular boundaries to a classical two-subset/four-subset inclusion matrix; no complete homotopy splitting is asserted.
