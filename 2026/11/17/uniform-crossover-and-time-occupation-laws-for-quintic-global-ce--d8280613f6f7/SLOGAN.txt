Quintic global-center periods exhibit a uniform crossover governed by \(q=\alpha A^4\): the exact scaled physical period lies between \(\Phi(q)\) and \(\Phi(q)/\sqrt{1+3/(\beta A^4)}\) for every amplitude and nonnegative velocity coupling. The strictly decreasing crossover joins the pure-quintic and velocity-coupled asymptotics, and the same scaling produces explicit time-occupation laws and a universal turning-point layer.
