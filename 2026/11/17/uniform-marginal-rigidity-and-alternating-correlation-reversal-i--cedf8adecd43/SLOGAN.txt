Among distinct alternating gains in \([1,2]\), exactly \((2,1)\) and \((1,2)\) preserve a uniform voltage distribution at both phases of the McCulloch–Pitts neuron map. In one ordering the lag-two voltage covariance flips sign between phases, while paired outputs have exact variance \(m-4/3+4/(3\,2^m)\) and a standard Gaussian limit after division by \(\sqrt m\).
