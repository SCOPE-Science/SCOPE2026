For the cubic global center \(\dot x=y\), \(\dot y=-x-kx^3-hxy^2\) with \(h>0\), the physical period obeys \(T(A)=2\pi/[\sqrt h A\sqrt{1+kA^2}](1+O_h(A^{-2}))\), **uniformly for all \(k\ge0\)**, and strictly decreases with amplitude. At uniformly sampled physical time, the squared turning gap \(A^2-x^2\) converges in total variation at rate \(O_h(A^{-2})\) to density \(h/[\pi\sqrt{e^{hr}-1}]\), whose exponential transform has a universal one-sided arcsine law.
