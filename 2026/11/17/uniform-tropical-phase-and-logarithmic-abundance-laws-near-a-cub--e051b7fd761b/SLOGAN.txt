For the cubic Kolmogorov flow on the sphere, the entire periodic trajectory in \(-\log(x^2),-\log(y^2),-\log(z^2)\) coordinates, divided by \(\log(1/H)\), approaches a specific triangle uniformly in its original-time phase at rate \(O(1/\log(1/H))\). Time sampling on a single near-boundary orbit converges to three explicitly weighted uniform triangle edges, yielding an atom-plus-uniform law for each logarithmic population coordinate.
