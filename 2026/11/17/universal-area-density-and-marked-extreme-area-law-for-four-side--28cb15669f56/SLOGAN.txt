Four independent uniform points, one on each side of any parallelogram, enclose an area whose complete normalized law is shape-independent, with a logarithmic density singularity at one-half. Near maximal or minimal enclosed area the probability is \(\frac43\varepsilon^4+\frac{32}{15}\varepsilon^5+O(\varepsilon^6)\); conditioned on maximal area, four rescaled endpoint errors and the unused area budget converge jointly to a five-component uniform Dirichlet law.
