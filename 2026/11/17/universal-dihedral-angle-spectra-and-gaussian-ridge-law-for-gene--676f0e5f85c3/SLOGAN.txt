All Euclidean ridge dihedral angles of every generalized parking-function polytope are determined solely by dimension and whether the first parking parameter equals one; the classical branch has exactly \(n\) acute ridges, while the other branch has none. A uniformly sampled ridge angle concentrates at \(3\pi/4\), with \(\sqrt n\)-rescaled fluctuations converging to a normal law of variance \(3/8\), uniformly over the remaining parking parameters.
