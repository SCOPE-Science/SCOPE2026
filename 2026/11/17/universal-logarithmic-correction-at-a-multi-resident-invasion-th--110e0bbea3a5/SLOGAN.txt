At a neutral invasion threshold with \(r\ge2\) equally weighted resident populations, the rare normalized population has a universal two-term reciprocal clock: \(1/z_n=\frac{2r+1}{2(2r-1)}n-\frac12\log n+C+o(1)\). The logarithmic coefficient is the same for every resident multiplicity, while residents equalize exponentially from every strictly positive starting state.
