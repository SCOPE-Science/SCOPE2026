For the typical and origin-containing cells of every fixed-dimensional Poisson cuboid tessellation, an unusually small volume has an explicit two-term logarithmic tail, with probability orders respectively \(\varepsilon\log^{d-1}(1/\varepsilon)\) and \(\varepsilon^2\log^{d-1}(1/\varepsilon)\). Conditioned on this rare event, the logarithmic side-length proportions become uniform on a simplex, independently of a Beta-distributed fraction of the volume threshold; the zero cell additionally has independent uniform origin-position marks.
