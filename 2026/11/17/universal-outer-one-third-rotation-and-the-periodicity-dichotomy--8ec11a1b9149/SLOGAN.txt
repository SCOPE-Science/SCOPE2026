For the four-positive-parameter Somos-type ratio QRT map, every non-equilibrium positive orbit lies on a convex logarithmic energy oval with exact rigid-rotation dynamics. The rotation number tends to an explicit small-energy angle and universally to \(1/3\) at large energy; exactly when \(ad=bc\) every positive orbit has period dividing three, while otherwise ovals of every sufficiently large prime least period occur.
