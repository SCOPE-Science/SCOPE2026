The original continuous-time model is identified with an explicit strictly admissible payoff interpolation. Logit coordinates yield a separable proper Hamiltonian, so every noncritical positive level is one periodic oval. Periodic integration fixes the cooperation mean exactly. A second-harmonic calculation on arbitrarily small ovals gives the cubic central moment to fourth order; the exact nonlinear response balance then determines the environmental mean. The embedded symbolic checker confirms the coefficient algebra; its 18 finite integrations confirm signs and small-amplitude ratios for Fermi and a curvature-modified response. Such numerical checks do not prove the infinite statements; the proof does.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The original 2022 general-imitation article, including the full relevant equations, Section 3.4 and Appendix F, is inspected: it gives a cooperation-average identity and suggests environmental average near equilibrium, but does not derive the nonzero quartic correction or its dependence on response curvature. The 2016 replicator paper's full accessible article supplies the symmetric constant-of-motion periodic family for linear response; the latter is credited, and has no nonlinear \(F'''(0)\) coefficient. Comparable indexed work on two-community eco-evolutionary neutral foliations and a symmetric collective-risk risk premium uses different equations or observables and cannot imply this one-dimensional environmental mean formula. The new coefficient is not merely a title or parameter variant of those findings. Searches cannot prove universal uniqueness, and unindexed prior averaging calculations remain a genuine residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact fourth-order term resolves a natural mathematical quantity left undetermined by the nonlinear imitation model's zero-average response relation. It identifies when a periodic population can maintain precisely its equilibrium cooperation average while changing the mean environment and gives a sign-reversing response-curvature mechanism. Both the payoff-independent-composition condition and the fourth-order statistic are motivated by the originating model's payoffs and published average-environment discussion, not by an arbitrary numerical slice. The Fermi example has a nonzero explicit coefficient; the exact symmetric exception distinguishes the natural zero-bias boundary. The result is not presented as a general theorem for arbitrary nonlinear eco-evolutionary games.
