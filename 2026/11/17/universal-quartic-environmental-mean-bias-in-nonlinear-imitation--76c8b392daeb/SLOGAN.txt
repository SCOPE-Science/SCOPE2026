In the payoff-independent-composition subclass of nonlinear imitation feedback games, every interior nonstationary trajectory is periodic and mean cooperation equals equilibrium exactly, but the environmental mean has a generally nonzero, universal fourth-order displacement controlled by the imitation response's cubic derivative. For Fermi imitation the coefficient is \(a^2(1-2t)/(48t(1-t))\) times the fourth power of the environmental half-range, while \(t=1/2\) gives exact environmental-mean symmetry on every orbit.
