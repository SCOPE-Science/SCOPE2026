For bilateral shifts that are unweighted to the left and have arbitrary positive weights decaying to zero to the right, the quasinilpotent part is always the same, while the analytic core is given by one exact coefficient-growth test. Inclusion between the analytic cores of any two such shifts is completely characterized by exponential comparison of their cumulative weights, simultaneously for all \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z)\) with \(1\le p<\infty\).
