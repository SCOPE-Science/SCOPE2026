For uniform points in two smooth, positively curved planar bodies that touch at one point, the probability of being within distance \(r\) is asymptotic to an explicit curvature-dependent constant times \(r^{7/2}\). Conditional close-pair geometry follows a universal total-variation law with a \(\operatorname{Beta}(5/4,3/2)\) horizontal mark and independent \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1/2,1,1)\) depth shares; separating the bodies by \(\lambda r\) produces an exact gap-crossover integral.
