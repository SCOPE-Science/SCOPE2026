In an integrable three-species Lotka–Volterra flow, every noncentral positive orbit on a fixed Casimir leaf is periodic, and its original-time period satisfies \(T_K(E)/E\to1/(ad)\) at high energy, independently of the leaf. Uniform physical-time sampling of the three scaled log-populations converges to the uniform law on an explicitly identified line segment, exposing a single dominant slow leg.
