For the canonical affine minimal-rank interval maps whose last two branch orientations coincide, the visits to orientation-change branches form an exact two-state Markov process depending only on the branch count and the number of changes, not their placement. This yields universal physical entropy, correlations and Gaussian switch-count fluctuations, together with a strictly positive entropy deficit whose sharp many-branch scale is \(\theta(1-\theta)/(2n^2)\).
