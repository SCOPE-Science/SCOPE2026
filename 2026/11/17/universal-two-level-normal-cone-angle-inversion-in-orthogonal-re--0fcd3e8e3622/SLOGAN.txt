For orthogonal joins of regular \(p\)- and \(q\)-gons with \(3\le p<q\), the more numerous \(q\)-gon vertices collectively win a random linear objective more often, but every individual \(p\)-gon vertex has strictly larger exterior normal-cone angle than every \(q\)-gon vertex. Exact angular integrals and a universal strict inequality give, for the maximal-volume square–pentagon four-polytope, per-vertex probabilities about \(0.119921056141\) and \(0.104063155087\), respectively.
