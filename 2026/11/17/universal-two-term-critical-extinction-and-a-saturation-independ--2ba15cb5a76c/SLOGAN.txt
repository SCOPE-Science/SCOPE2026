In a discrete Holling-II plankton model at the quadratically tangent predator-invasion boundary, every positive orbit in a natural open region has zooplankton \(v_n=A n^{-1/2}-3(1+c)n^{-1}/(2\theta)+O(\log n/n^{3/2})\) and prey \(u_n=1-A n^{-1/2}/(1+c)+n^{-1}/\theta+O(\log n/n^{3/2})\), where \(A=\sqrt{(1+c)^3/(2\theta c)}\). The prey's first subleading coefficient is independent of the Holling saturation strength despite its leading coefficient depending on that strength.
