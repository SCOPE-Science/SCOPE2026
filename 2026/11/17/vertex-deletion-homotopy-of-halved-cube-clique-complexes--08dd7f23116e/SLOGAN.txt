For every \(n\ge4\), deleting any one vertex from the halved-cube graph changes its clique complex from \(\bigvee^{B_n}S^3\) to \(\bigvee^{B_n-\binom{n-1}{3}}S^3\), with a split integral homology inclusion. Every lost class is accounted for by a two-sphere in the Johnson-graph vertex link that bounds via an explicit surviving four-coordinate cone.
