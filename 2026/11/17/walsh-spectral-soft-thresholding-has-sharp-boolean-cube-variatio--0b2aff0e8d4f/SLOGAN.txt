For real Walsh–Hadamard soft thresholding on the \(d\)-dimensional Boolean cube, the sharp two-input graph-total-variation gain is an attained finite maximum over spectral masks and vertex cuts, independent of the positive threshold. The gain is exactly \(1,1,3/2\) in dimensions \(1,2,3\) and grows as \(\Theta(2^{d/2})\), despite Euclidean nonexpansivity.
