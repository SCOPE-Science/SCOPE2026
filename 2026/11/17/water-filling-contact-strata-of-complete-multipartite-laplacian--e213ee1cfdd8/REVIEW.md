# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The definitions fix the canonical full Laplacian columns and the inherited Euclidean metric. Existence and uniqueness of the one-dimensional threshold follow from strict monotonicity. Direct column and center computations establish the exact distance and squared-clearance identities. The proposed center is a positive convex combination of exactly the classes with positive weight, all on the sphere; the weighted variance identity proves global optimality and uniqueness without imposing symmetry on rival centers. The affine independence of the Laplacian columns proves uniqueness of the certificate. The included checker verifies the same formulas from the full graph matrix in exact arithmetic; finite tests do not establish the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)
Braun–Meyer (2017) and Balletti–Hibi–Meyer–Tsuchiya (2017) establish the construction and lattice/combinatorial properties, not the multipartite Euclidean minimum-sphere or its contact strata. The strongest close public result `ac6d13296de7` treats only complete bipartite graphs and explicitly does not claim multipartite graphs. The vertex-transitive result `841b23980b76` covers equal part sizes, when all vertices contact. The stable-set result `2a7050b5d350` uses a different polytope with exponentially many possible vertices. The new law specifies arbitrary many simultaneous part-size thresholds and cases with positive contact but zero certificate weight. Equivalent nonstandard formulations remain a search risk; absence in inspected papers is not proof of universal priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The minimum enclosing ball and its contact pattern are intrinsic Euclidean features of a natural graph-simplex family not determined by its familiar lattice invariants. The exact classification works for every number of parts and every positive part-size profile, selecting which full vertex orbits disappear from the bounding sphere. The integer weak-contact example \(K_{3,3,6}\) and complete-screening example \(K_{4,17,17}\) show qualitative behavior beyond the two-part setting; these are not arbitrary parametric slices. The rational active-set algorithm makes the contact stratification usable without numerical optimization.

## Scope and risk
The graph must be complete multipartite, simple and unweighted, and the full Laplacian embedding is essential. The result is not an independently audited claim. Broader geometric aliases or older unpublished material could affect historical priority, and the general finite-point minimum-sphere duality is credited rather than claimed as new.
