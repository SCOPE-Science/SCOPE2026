The class-colored source–sink covariance matrix of a uniformly sampled and independently oriented bipartite spanning tree has a closed form. It is negative definite for every balanced \(K_{m,m}\) with \(m\ge3\), yet becomes indefinite for every fixed class size \(a\ge2\) and sufficiently unequal \(K_{a,b}\); in \(K_{2,4}\), the weighted source and sink counts with weights \((3,1)\) already have covariance \(191/4096>0\).
