In the original binary uniform-composition tree, the numbers of terminal chunks of sizes one and two have asymptotically perfectly correlated fluctuations of order \(\sqrt{n\log n}\), even though they count disjoint chunk types. Their balanced difference \(2X_n-5Y_n\) instead has the exact variance \(253(n+2)/105\) for all \(n\ge6\), exposing a rank-one logarithmic fluctuation mode.
