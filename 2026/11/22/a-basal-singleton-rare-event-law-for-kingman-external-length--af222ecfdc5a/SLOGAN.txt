In Kingman's coalescent, a root split isolating one original leaf occurs with probability \(2/(n-1)\), yet conditioning on this rare event changes the entire external-branch sum from its ordinary limit \(2\) to the nondegenerate limit \(2+\mathcal H\), where \(\mathcal H\) is the coalescent height. An exact positive event–length covariance is \(4(n^2+2-2nH_n)/[n(n-1)(n-2)]\), asymptotic to \(4/n\).
