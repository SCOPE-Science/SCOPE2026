In the high-activity window \(\lambda=n/c\), unmatched sites of a complete bipartite monomer–dimer gas have a Bessel defect law with an explicit \(O(n^{-1})\) total-variation bound. At every finite activity, monomer occupancies repel within each shore but are positively correlated across shores, with sharp Bessel-window covariance coefficients of order \(n^{-2}\).
