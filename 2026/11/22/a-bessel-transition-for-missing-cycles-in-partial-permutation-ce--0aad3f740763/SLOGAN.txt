For a uniformly chosen partial bijection commuting with \(m\) equal permutation cycles of length \(\ell\), the number of omitted cycles has an exact factorial-ratio distribution. As \(m/\ell\) crosses order one, it transitions from no omissions to a discrete Bessel law and then concentrates around \(\sqrt{m/\ell}\) omitted cycles; the exact finite enumerator follows from classical centralizer structure.
