# A binary sparse forbidden presentation with exact Fibonacci complexity and maximal antidictionary entropy

## Finding
Let \(X\subseteq\{0,1\}^{\mathbb Z}\) be the subshift avoiding the **explicit** set
\[
\mathcal F=\{1000,1110\}\cup\bigcup_{j\ge0}\{000u1^{j^2+3}:u\in\{0,1\}^j\}.
\]
Write \(F_0=0,F_1=1,F_{r+2}=F_{r+1}+F_r\) for the Fibonacci numbers. Then, for every integer \(N\ge1\), the exact language complexity is
\[
p_X(N):=|\mathcal L_N(X)|=2F_{N+2}-2.
\]
More strongly, the **entire minimal forbidden factor language** is the disjoint union
\[
\mathcal M(X)=\{1000,1110,000111\}
\;\sqcup\;
\{000u111:\ |u|\ge2,\ u\in\mathcal L(K),\ u_1=1,\ u_{|u|}=0\},
\]
where \(K\) is the two-sided binary shift avoiding the triples \(000\) and \(111\), and \(\mathcal L(K)\) denotes all its finite factors. For the generating-forbidden-word entropy \(h_G\), the minimal-forbidden-word entropy \(h_M\), and topological entropy \(h_{\rm top}\), all defined via natural logarithms,
\[
\boxed{h_G(X)=0<h_M(X)=h_{\rm top}(X)=\log\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}.}
\]
Thus zero-exponential-growth forbidden *generators* can coexist with exponentially many *minimal* forbidden words even in an explicitly classified binary sofic subshift. The claim is the exact binary model and its complete counts and antidictionary, not the general phenomenon of unequal entropy invariants.

## Assumptions and scope
The alphabet is exactly \(\{0,1\}\), the shift is two-sided, and forbidden words are contiguous factors, never scattered subsequences. The index \(j\) in \(\mathcal F\) includes zero; for \(j=0\) the word is \(000111\). A word \(w\) is minimal forbidden if \(w\notin\mathcal L(X)\) but all proper contiguous factors belong to \(\mathcal L(X)\). We use \(h_M=\limsup_{N\to\infty} N^{-1}\log\max\{1,|\mathcal M(X)\cap\{0,1\}^N|\}\); \(h_G\) is the infimum of the same exponential rates over all sets of forbidden words generating \(X\), as in Wu and Zhang (2026). No measure-theoretic entropy or single-trajectory frequency hypothesis is needed.

## Proof
**1. The three geometric types of points.** If \(000\) occurs in a point of \(X\), the immediately preceding bit must be \(0\), since \(1000\) is forbidden. Repetition implies a left-infinite zero tail. Likewise, an occurrence of \(111\) forces a right-infinite one tail, because \(1110\) is forbidden. They cannot both occur in one point: if they did, the zero tail would precede the one tail, so one could select an occurrence of \(000\), a later occurrence of \(111\), and a finite intervening word \(u\). The point would then contain \(000u1^{|u|^2+3}\), contradicting its avoidance of \(\mathcal F\). (The long suffix is available because every \(111\) forces ones forever to the right.) Therefore every point is either in \(K\), has a left-infinite zero tail followed by a right half-line avoiding both triples, or has a left half-line avoiding both triples followed by a right-infinite one tail; these three cases include their constant limiting points. Conversely, every such point avoids both short forbidden words and all the long forbidden generators. In particular, a finite binary word \(w\) occurs in \(X\) **if and only if** it avoids \(1000\), avoids \(1110\), and does not contain both \(000\) and \(111\). For the reverse direction, a word with neither forbidden triple extends to a bi-infinite point of the irreducible nonempty finite-type shift \(K\). If it has \(000\) but not \(111\), avoidance of \(1000\) puts every triple \(000\) in its initial zero run; extend that run indefinitely left and extend the suffix to the right within \(K\). The case with \(111\) but not \(000\) is symmetric. This establishes the finite-factor criterion rather than presuming it.

**2. Exact Fibonacci complexity.** The shift \(K\), whose words have constant runs of length at most two, has exactly \(2F_{N+1}\) factors of length \(N\): choose the first symbol (two choices) and a composition of \(N\) into parts \(1\) or \(2\) recording the alternating run lengths. Words of \(X\) that contain \(000\) have the form \(0^r v\), where \(r\ge3\) and \(v\in\mathcal L(K)\) is empty or starts in \(1\), and cannot contain \(111\). For \(N\ge3\) there are
\[
1+\sum_{k=1}^{N-3}F_{k+1}=F_N-1
\]
such length-\(N\) words, because the number of length-\(k\) words in \(K\) beginning with \(1\) is \(F_{k+1}\). The words containing \(111\) but no \(000\) contribute an additional, disjoint \(F_N-1\), by reversal and symbol complementation. Thus \(p_X(N)=2F_{N+1}+2(F_N-1)=2F_{N+2}-2\) for \(N\ge3\). At \(N=1,2\) the same identity follows by direct counts \(2,4\). Since \(F_N\asymp\varphi^N\), with \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), the topological entropy is exactly \(\log\varphi\).

**3. Complete antidictionary.** The words \(1000\) and \(1110\) are minimal forbidden: their respective length-three prefix and suffix occur in the described points. Any minimal forbidden word of length greater than four therefore avoids those two patterns. By the finite-factor criterion, it must contain both \(000\) and \(111\). Avoidance of \(1000\) and \(1110\) orders them as \(000\) before \(111\). Minimality forces an occurrence of \(000\) at the very beginning and of \(111\) at the very end; otherwise a proper factor would still contain both. Hence the word is \(000u111\). If \(u\) is nonempty, it must start with \(1\) and end with \(0\), or else a proper factor contains both triples. It must avoid both triples internally for the same reason. The exceptional empty middle word gives \(000111\). Conversely, each displayed \(000u111\) is absent, and deleting its final symbol leaves a word with an initial zero tail and no triple \(111\), while deleting its first symbol leaves a word with a terminal one tail and no triple \(000\); both are admissible by Step 1. Thus each word is minimal forbidden, and the list is exhaustive.

**4. Entropies of minimal and generating obstructions.** The words \(u\in\mathcal L_N(K)\) beginning in \(1\) and ending in \(0\) grow at exponential rate \(\varphi\). Indeed, present \(K\) by the four states \(00,01,10,11\), with allowed transitions \(00\to01\), \(01\to10,11\), \(10\to00,01\), and \(11\to10\). This graph is strongly connected and aperiodic (it has cycles of lengths two and three), and its Perron growth rate is \(\varphi\) by the already established \(2F_{N+1}\) word count. Perron–Frobenius asymptotics give the same exponential rate with either nonempty admissible endpoint condition. Thus the complete antidictionary found in Step 3 has \(h_M(X)\ge\log\varphi\). Conversely, every minimal forbidden length-\(N\) word has a legal prefix of length \(N-1\), and each such prefix has at most two one-letter extensions; therefore \(|\mathcal M_N(X)|\le2p_X(N-1)\), showing \(h_M(X)\le h_{\rm top}(X)=\log\varphi\).

For every \(j\ge0\), the long generator family has exactly \(2^j\) words, all of length \(j^2+j+6\), and no other lengths. Consequently the explicit generating set has exponential growth rate zero because \(j\log2/(j^2+j+6)\to0\). So \(h_G(X)\le0\). On the other hand, the antidictionary contains infinitely many distinct words, making \(X\) non-SFT. Every generating forbidden set is then infinite and must have arbitrarily large occupied length layers, so its limsup exponential growth rate is nonnegative. Hence \(h_G(X)=0\). Combining all bounds completes the proof.

## Relationship to prior work
Wu and Zhang, *Generating Forbidden Words and Measures of Maximal Entropy for Subshifts* (2026), introduce \(h_G\) and in Example 1(2) give an explicit **four-letter** construction with \(h_G=0<h_M=h_{\rm top}=\log2\). Their markers are separate alphabet symbols and the full-shift core has unconstrained two-letter words. The mechanism of long sparse forbidden generators is already theirs; unequal entropy invariants are emphatically not claimed to be new. A generic binary block-coding reduction may already yield binary existence. The present claim is instead the explicit **direct two-letter run-guard model**, its **complete** antidictionary and its **exact Fibonacci factor complexity and entropy**, none of which are consequences of their particular four-letter counting formulas without new modeling and proof.

A published analysis titled *A sharp forbidden-word entropy boundary with countably many maximal-entropy phases* uses a \(2q\)-symbol periodic-marker model and finds the equality \(h_M=h_{\rm top}=\log q\) and many maximal measures; its marker structure, word formulas, and measures are different. *Complete three-family antidictionary of proportional-run coded shifts* studies binary blocks \(0^m1^{km}\) with \(h_M=0<h_{\rm top}\), rather than the present opposite separation \(h_G<h_M=h_{\rm top}\). A previously recorded binary union of shifts separately avoiding constant triples has \(h_M<h_{\rm top}\), not the current all-equal upper entropy pair. None of these three statements contains this precise shift, its complete minimal forbidden words, or its Fibonacci language formula. Classical symbolic-dynamics treatments of minimal forbidden words and sofic shifts motivate the classification but do not supply this direct calculation. Unindexed equivalent constructions and general coding reductions remain priority risks.

## Limitations
The model is sofic but not of finite type, and its entropy is that of its triple-avoiding finite-type core. The separation is between generating-forbidden entropy and minimal-forbidden entropy, **not** a counterexample to Wu and Zhang's finite-maximal-measure theorem. No quantitative claim about the number of maximal-entropy measures beyond the explicit core calculation is needed. The argument makes no assertion about higher-dimensional shifts, generic parameter families, or independence of the mathematical review. Literature coverage cannot rule out an older isomorphic presentation or a stronger general implication.
