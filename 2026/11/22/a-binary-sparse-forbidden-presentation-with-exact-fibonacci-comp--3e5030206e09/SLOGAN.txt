A direct binary subshift has exactly \(2F_{N+2}-2\) words of each length \(N\ge1\) and a complete infinite antidictionary of the form \(000u111\) supplemented by three short obstructions. Sparse quadratic-length generators give \(h_G=0\), although both minimal-forbidden entropy and topological entropy equal \(\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)\).
