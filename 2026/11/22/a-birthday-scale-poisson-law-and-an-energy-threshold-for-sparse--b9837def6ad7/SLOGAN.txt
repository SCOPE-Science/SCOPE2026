The Dirichlet-one histogram evidence used in a sequential rank independence test has a sharp **birthday-scale** regime: under a growing square grid, its terminal null limit is \(e^{-\tau}2^{\operatorname{Poisson}(\tau)}\), not a Gaussian or chi-square law. For iid oracle-copula inputs in the sparse many-collision regime, its log-evidence switches from decay to growth exactly at squared density energy \(\int f^2=1/\log2\), with a sharp analytic-copula amplitude of \(0.9409516894\ldots\).
