For the screened discrete half-line Green kernel, the optimal single Nyström landmark moves inward through an infinite, exactly ordered staircase of sites as screening weakens, with first transition exactly at \(\mu=1/2\) and optimal depth asymptotic to \([1+W(e^{-1})]/(2\sqrt\mu)\). On long finite paths each fixed transition is shifted upward by an explicitly quantified exponentially small boundary interaction, with the endpoint switch exceeding \(1/2\) by asymptotically \((27/4)(2n-1)2^{-(2n-1)}\).
