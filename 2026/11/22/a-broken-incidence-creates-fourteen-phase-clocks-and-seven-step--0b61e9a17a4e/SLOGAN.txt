Deleting one of the two incidences at a subdividing vertex of a complete graph changes characteristic-two neighbor-sum dynamics sharply: odd clique orders acquire exact period-\(14\) clocks with a complete least-period \(1,2,7,14\) orbit census, while even orders acquire a recurrent two-dimensional oscillator and an exact seven-update relaxation law. The results hold for every finite characteristic-two field and determine all initial-state entry populations and individual orbit-basin sizes.
