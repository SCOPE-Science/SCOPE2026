For Brownian motion independently reset to any spatial law between two partially reactive endpoints, the unique optimal split of a fixed reaction budget favors the side selected by the sign of \(\int\sinh(k(1/2-y))\,d\mu(y)\), independent of the total budget. A two-site reset law can flip that favored side exactly once as reset frequency increases; an explicit law flips at \(rL^2/D=25\operatorname{arcosh}^2(3/2)\) for every positive budget.
