For the Gaussian Fock Hankel operator with symbol \(\overline z\mathbf1_{\{|z|^2\le T\}}\), the exact singular spectrum has an order-\(T\) bulk of size-one modes and a \(\sqrt T\)-wide boundary layer of size-\(\sqrt T\) modes. Their competition produces sharp Schatten-power asymptotics with a transition at \(p=1\), where both populations contribute and the trace coefficient is \(1+\int_{\mathbb R}\sqrt{\Phi(u)\Phi(-u)}\,du\).
