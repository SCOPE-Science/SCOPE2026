For a Gaussian integral kernel multiplied by \(1+(x+y)^TA(x+y)-(x-y)^TD(x-y)\), positivity is equivalent to \(A,D\succeq0\) and the squared Bures–Wasserstein distance \(d_{\mathrm{BW}}(A,D)^2\le b\), where \(b\) is the positive Gaussian cross-coupling. In particular, a previously numerically bounded scalar polynomial-Gaussian family has the exact positive range \(0\le\gamma\le4\).
