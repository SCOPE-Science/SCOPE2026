For the binary gap shift whose permitted consecutive-marker distances are the 1-positions of the Thue–Morse sequence, the errors in the entropies of canonical finite-type covers, after multiplication by \(\lambda^N\), have an exact, uncountable, perfect, Hausdorff-dimension-zero Cantor set of subsequential limits, where \(\lambda\) is the Komornik–Loreti constant. The exact equality stages of successive covers follow the Thue–Morse digits themselves.
