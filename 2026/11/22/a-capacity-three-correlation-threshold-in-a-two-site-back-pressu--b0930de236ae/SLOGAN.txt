For a two-site capacitated back-pressure exclusion chain with equal positive arrival and departure rates up to one, the stationary site-occupation covariance is negative at maximal rates for capacities one and two but positive for capacity three. Exact finite-state formulas show that the capacity-two sign reversal occurs at \(t=0.885080955\ldots\), while the capacity-three covariance stays positive for all \(0<t\le1\).
