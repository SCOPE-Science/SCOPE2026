Every smooth characteristic-two cyclic quintic \(y^5=x^4+a x^3+b x^2+c x+d\) has genus six, Jacobian \(2\)-rank zero, and \(a\)-number three; its Cartier ranks are \((6,3,1,0,\ldots)\) except on \(a=b=0\), where they are \((6,3,0,\ldots)\). This square-zero stratum is exactly the geometric Hermitian curve \(v^5=u^4+u\), characterized by five branch points projectively equivalent to \(\mathbf P^1(\mathbb F_4)\).
