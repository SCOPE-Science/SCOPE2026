For every Coxeter-period word in the type-A \(0\)-Hecke monoid, the exact number of repetitions needed to reach the longest element is governed by the maximum of a centered orientation-height walk. The full finite distribution has a tridiagonal walk formula, and the uniform random exponent has a nondegenerate Brownian-maximum limit after centering by \(n/2\) and scaling by \(\sqrt n\).
