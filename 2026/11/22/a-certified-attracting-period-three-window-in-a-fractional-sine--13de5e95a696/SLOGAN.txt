The fractional sine map \(f_r(x)=\sin(\pi x)/(1+rx)\) has an attracting primitive three-cycle for every \(124999/1000000\le r\le125001/1000000\), with a rigorously enclosed multiplier between \(-0.499\) and \(-0.479\). This explicit open periodic window lies inside a region described in the originating paper as robustly chaotic without periodic windows; it does not preclude coexistence of other invariant sets.
