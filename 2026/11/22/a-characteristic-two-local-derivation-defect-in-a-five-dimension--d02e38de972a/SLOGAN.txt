For the five-dimensional Zinbiel algebra with products \(e_1^2=e_2^2=e_4\), \(e_1e_2=-e_2e_1=e_3\), \(e_1e_3=e_5\), and \(e_2e_3=e_4\), linear local derivations coincide with derivations in every characteristic other than two but acquire exactly one additional dimension in characteristic two. Over \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\), this gives \(q^7-q^6\) proper linear local derivations, with a concrete witness sending \(e_1\) to \(e_2\) and every other basis element to zero.
