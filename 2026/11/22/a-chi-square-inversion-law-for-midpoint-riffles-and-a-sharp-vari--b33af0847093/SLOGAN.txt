One midpoint-interpolation riffle with a binomial cut has an exact conditional binomial inversion law, and for odd deck sizes its inversion count is exactly \(K(n-K)/2\). Its fluctuations converge on scale \(n\) to \((1-Z^2)/8\), while an ordinary riffle with the same cut law has Gaussian fluctuations on scale \(n^{3/2}\) and asymptotically \(2n/3\) times as much inversion variance.
