For the Petersen graph \(P\), deleting any one edge turns its graph-geodesic general-position complex into \(S^1\vee\bigvee^3 S^2\), whereas deleting any one vertex gives \(\bigvee^2S^2\). Exact acyclic integral Morse reductions establish both homotopy types and contrast them with the intact \(S^3\) baseline.
