# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed both coefficient cases from the path-algebra presentation. Quadratic relations preserve the return count: in rank modulo d+1 every arrow advances one step, and for d>=2 each two-step relation is either entirely ascending or is one of the two endpoint sums across a return. The latter kill Lρ and ρR and make dρ=0 without orientation. Fixing endpoints with vertex idempotents gives every return arrow from ρ. Split paths at their returns: in the path algebra each resulting tuple of ascending segments determines a unique path because all returns exist. In the quotient, ascending relations act within factors; the cross-return sums impose the terminal L and initial R relations and no others. Hence a path with n returns is in the tensor quotient of Ω/L, n−1 copies of Ω/(R+L), and Ω/R. In characteristic other than two the middle quotient vanishes as a complex, not just in cohomology: Lemma 5.6 identifies it with the top cohomology, zero by nonorientability. This leaves the one-return ideal square-zero. Lemma 5.9 and Proposition 3.9 give acyclicity of R and L by the contractible cone posets, using local homology of links (Proposition 4.5), not global duality. The sign (−1)^|x| on xρy turns its surviving differential into the shifted tensor differential. The restriction connecting map is exactly 1⊗[x]−[x]⊗1; its positive-degree components are injective by bidegree, while it kills the unit. The long exact sequence now gives the stated dimensions, vanishing above 2d−1, and representatives in the square-zero ideal in every positive degree. In characteristic two, the local conditions plus field-valued Poincaré duality satisfy the three hypotheses of Theorem 5.3 of Ivanov–Zhang, so Theorem 6.3 applies directly without assuming integral orientability. The nonzero orientation character gives a degree-one class even after field extension; its one-dimensional projection is a degree-zero idempotent of the graded endomorphism algebra, and multiplication with t verifies all positive powers. Substitution of surface Betti numbers gives both Hilbert series. The inspected projective-plane script only checks ordinary homology and PH^1; it is neither used nor executed as a proof of higher-degree cohomology.
