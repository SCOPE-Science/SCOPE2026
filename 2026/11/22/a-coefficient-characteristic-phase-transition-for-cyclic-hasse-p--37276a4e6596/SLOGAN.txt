For cyclic Hasse digraphs of every closed connected nonorientable manifold of dimension at least two, path cohomology over fields of characteristic other than two is a finite-dimensional square-zero extension, while characteristic two produces a nonnilpotent class and infinitely many cohomology degrees. For the connected sum of \(h\) projective planes, the odd-characteristic Hilbert polynomial is \(1+t+(h-1)t^2+(h-1)^2t^3\), whereas characteristic two gives \(1+t+ht^2+h^2t^3/(1-t^3)\).
