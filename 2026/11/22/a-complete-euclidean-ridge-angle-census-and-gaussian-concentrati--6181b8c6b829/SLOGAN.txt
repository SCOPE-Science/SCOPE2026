The unit simplex-sum stellohedron of a star has a complete three-family Euclidean ridge-angle census, with exactly \(\binom m2\) acute ridges and no right ridges. A uniformly chosen ridge angle concentrates at \(2\pi/3\), with Gaussian fluctuations of variance \(3/8\) after \(\sqrt m\) rescaling, although exponentially rare ridges become nearly flat.
