For quartic windows of the Lucas-balancing sequence, the entire five-primary part of the gcd across all translates is exactly determined: its valuation is zero unless the length is divisible by five, is one when the length is not divisible by three, and otherwise equals the five-adic valuation of the length. The proof identifies least prime-power periods \(3\cdot5^{a-1}\) and a sharp block-sum congruence.
