For every fixed rank \(j\ge3\), the \(j\)-th singular value of factorial-normalized high-order Volterra integration has the sharp decay \(s_j(\Gamma(\alpha+1)I^\alpha)\sim(j-1)!/[2^{2j-1}\alpha^{j-1}]\). The entire hierarchy follows from a rank-one Vandermonde limit of exterior-power kernels, extending previously known first- and second-rank behavior.
