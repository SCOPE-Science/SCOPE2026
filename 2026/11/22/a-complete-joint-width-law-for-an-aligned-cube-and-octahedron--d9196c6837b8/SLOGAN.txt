The widths of an aligned cube and its polar regular octahedron, measured along the same uniformly random direction, have an explicit complete two-variable distribution, not merely known marginal laws and covariance. For each fixed octahedron half-width \(m\ge1/\sqrt2\), the standardized cube half-width \( (S-m)/\sqrt{2(1-m^2)} \) equals in distribution the sine of a uniform angle on \([\pi/4,\pi/2]\).
