In every binary word \(0^a1\,0^b1\,0^c\) with positive zero runs, the number of minimal unique palindromes is exactly \(1\) when \(a=c\), exactly \(3\) when the runs are strictly monotone, and \(2\) otherwise. Exact rational length enumerators imply limiting probabilities \(2/3\) and \(1/3\) of two and three minimal unique palindromes, respectively.
