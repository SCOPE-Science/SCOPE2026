For a finite path's half-adjacency matrix, shared-sign Chebyshev trace probes have exact covariance \(-2\) between every two distinct degrees of the same parity and zero covariance across opposite parities, with variance \(n-1\) for odd degrees and \(n-2\) for even degrees. The full covariance has one exact zero mode and a closed risk formula for every polynomial of degree at most \(n\), exposing Dirichlet-boundary cancellation invisible in general-purpose trace bounds.
