On a smooth Cassini curve, the outward Gauss map has exactly two preimages per direction in the disconnected phase, and one in the connected convex phase. In the intermediate phase it has three on two open arcs, two at their four endpoints, and one elsewhere. The angular fraction of three-preimage directions decreases strictly, with a square-root singularity at lobe merger and a three-halves collapse at convexification; it also determines the total absolute curvature.
