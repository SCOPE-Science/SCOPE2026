On a smooth Cassini oval, the outward Gauss map has exactly two preimages per normal direction on the disconnected convex lobes, one on the connected convex oval, and exactly one or three on its intermediate peanut-shaped phase. The angular fraction of three-preimage normals is an explicit strictly decreasing inverse-shape statistic, with a square-root singularity at lobe merger and a three-halves collapse at convexification; it also determines the exact total absolute curvature.
