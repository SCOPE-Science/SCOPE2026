## Value — PASS

The question is naturally motivated by frequency selection under analytic smoothing: a strong Poisson filter rewards low modes, while fourth-power convolution rewards distributing energy. The theorem resolves this competition across all finite B2 sets and countably infinite ones with exact, one-by-one entry points, quantitative onset constants, and a sharp nonattainment limit. The infinitely-many-transition result is not a routine finite table or a trivial restatement of the three-tone precedent.

## Limits and closeness

The hypothesis of unique unordered pair sums is essential, and the proof does not cover general resonant spectra, nonlinear weights or higher moments. The published all-length lacunary theorem addresses the undamped endpoint; the earlier three-tone Poisson result provides only two finite transitions. Historical priority against unnamed older multiplier theory remains unsettled. Tests are supporting diagnostics, not an infinite certificate.
