On every finite or countably infinite analytic \(B_2\) Fourier spectrum, Poisson smoothing has a sharp \(L^2\to L^4\) norm given by one scalar waterline, and its optimizer activates the frequencies **exactly one at a time** as the smoothing radius increases. Infinite spectra have infinitely many sharp activation thresholds accumulating at radius one, but each subcritical optimizer is finitely supported; the endpoint sharp norm equals two and is unattained.
