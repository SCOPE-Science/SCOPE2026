On every finite or countably infinite analytic \(B_2\) Fourier spectrum, one scalar equation determines the sharp \(L^2\to L^4\) norm of Poisson smoothing and the maximizing magnitudes. Frequencies enter the maximizing support one at a time as the radius increases. On an infinite spectrum, only finitely many are active at any radius below one; at radius one the norm is \(2^{1/4}\) and is unattained.
