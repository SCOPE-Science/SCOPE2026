# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The pairwise-balancing lemma excludes unequal positive supplemental cover at any global optimum, including zero-shelter boundary cases. The exponential-tilting integral formula makes each adjacent support comparison cross exactly once; the derivative-integral representation of the continuous support envelope proves its strict one-peak shape and the absence of skipped global support sizes. The zero-baseline logarithmic thresholds and positive-baseline limit are proved analytically. The packaged standard-library checker replayed 1,315,977 supplementary assertions; finite checks do not replace these proofs.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources; residual risk

Samhouri et al. (2009) supply the additive prey-to-cover hazard as a candidate ecological mechanism, not a global refuge-allocation staircase. Ford et al. (2016) study empirical shelter-density interactions, not the present optimal allocation. Inspected full mathematical results on Hill inhibition, fixed predator feeding, and patch dispersal optimize distinct functions. Classical separable S-shaped allocation results of Vicentini (1970) and Ginsberg (1974) may cover the equal-positive allocation reduction; available abstracts did not establish the baseline-sensitive, all-support, unique-crossing exponential-inverse-cover thresholds. Ginsberg full text remained inaccessible; therefore historical priority is provisional and no exhaustive novelty claim is made.

## Value — PASS

The exact no-skipping support staircase and logarithmic zero-baseline thresholds give an interpretable test benchmark for how supplementary shelter should be concentrated versus spread under the specified mortality mechanism. The baseline-sensitive frontiers make the result substantially more informative than a trivial Jensen inequality or a single two-patch split. This is a conditional mathematical prediction, not an empirically validated conservation intervention.

## Limits

Identical patches; fixed initial-density hazards rather than dynamic feedback; divisible cover; no per-patch caps or dispersal; historical mathematical texts incompletely accessible; independent verification outstanding.
