For the quadratic character of the Franel numbers modulo five, all fixed-shift correlations on intervals of length \(5^r\), normalized by the active count \(4^r\), stabilize to an exact rational recurrence. Among the first \(5^r\) shifts, exactly \((5^r-1)/2\) have zero limiting correlation, and the positive and negative values \(\pm4^{-j}\) each occur \(4^{j-1}\binom rj\) times; the strongest shifts are exactly \(2\cdot5^m\) and \(4\cdot5^m\).
