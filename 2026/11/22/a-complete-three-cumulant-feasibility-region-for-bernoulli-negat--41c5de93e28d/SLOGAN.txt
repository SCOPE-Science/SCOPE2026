The Bernoulli-plus-negative-binomial count model has a complete, explicitly bounded region of possible first three factorial cumulants, with unique parameter recovery inside that region. Its underdispersed laws satisfy the sharp inequality \(\kappa_{[3]}>2(-\kappa_{[2]})^{3/2}\), so no nondegenerate binomial law with at least two trials can match all three factorial cumulants.
