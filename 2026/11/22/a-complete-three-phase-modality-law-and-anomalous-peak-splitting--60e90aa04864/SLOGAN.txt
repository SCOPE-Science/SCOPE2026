For a harmonically trapped run–tumble particle with a finite resting state, the stationary density has a complete sharp global mode diagram: a three-peak region, a two-interior-peak region, and strictly monotone regions, separated by the exact line \(T=R+1\) and the run-rate boundaries \(R=1,2\). The off-center peaks or valleys emerge from the center with the nonclassical power \(|T-R-1|^{1/(2-R)}\), with an explicit leading coefficient for every fixed \(0<R<2\), \(R\ne1\).
