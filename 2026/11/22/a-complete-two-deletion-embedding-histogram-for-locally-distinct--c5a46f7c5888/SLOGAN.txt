Every \(q\)-ary word with distinct letters at distances one and two has the same exact two-deletion posterior embedding histogram: multiplicities never exceed four, with exactly \(2m-1\) hosts of multiplicity three and \(\binom m2\) of multiplicity four. This gives the conditional Shannon entropy in closed form at every length and an asymptotic correction of exactly \(4/q\) bits for fixed \(q\ge3\).
