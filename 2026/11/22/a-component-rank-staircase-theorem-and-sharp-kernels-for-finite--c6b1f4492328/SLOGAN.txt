For any finite hereditary relational class with a unique singleton type, adjoining an equivalence relation that partitions structures into independent components transforms the local one-point-extension ranks into an exact descending-staircase criterion and transfers principal rank kernels. For finite disjoint unions of strict linear chains, every marked rank is determined by its order gaps and unused-chain sizes, and rank at least \(r\) has the unique smallest kernel with chain sizes \(2^r-1,2^{r-1}-1,\ldots,1\), of total size \(2^{r+1}-r-2\). Exactly \(r+1\) next-smallest isomorphism types occur.
