In a zero-forcing three-dimensional quadratic flow, a whole open escaping sector has a positive, nonconstant continuous scattering invariant \(\mathcal L=\lim xe^{z^2/8}\), even though the original polynomial family has no Darboux first integral. The exact original-time escape clock is \(z(t)^2=8\log t+4\log\log t+4\log8+8\log\mathcal L+o(1)\); the scattering logarithm itself approaches its limit with the universal correction \(2/t+o(t^{-1})\).
