# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

An arbitrarily late finite window contains at most one marker because the gaps tend to infinity. Every singleton at offset r really is a limit of windows starting at s_k−r, and the middle of a long gap witnesses the zero limit. Each actual shift is isolated: its prefix through the first complete internal marker gap fixes the gap index, and the leading offset then fixes the starting position. Thus the second Cantor–Bendixson derivative is a singleton and the third is empty; the countable compact classification gives omega²+1. Forward invariance of the closure follows from continuity. For an n-factor with ≥2 markers, index its first full gap by k and its leading offset by ell. For k≥2 the offset must satisfy 0≤ell<d_(k−1) and ell≤n−1−d_k, giving the stated truncated minimum; the first gap gives one word precisely for n≥2. Offsets and gap indices give distinct words even when later markers occur. All n singletons and the zero word occur; closure does not add finite words. For floored power gaps, dividing the sum by n times N=n^(1/alpha) gives the Riemann sum of min(x^alpha,1−x^alpha) on [0,1], supported up to an O(1) index error, with uniform o(1) error per scaled summand. Splitting at x=2^(−1/alpha) gives alpha/(alpha+1)·(1−2^(−1/alpha)); the term n+2 is negligible. For geometric gaps the sum is bounded by the geometric sum over d_(j+1)<n, which is <n. In the ultrametric, separation at 2^(−r) exactly reads the first n+r coordinates, so the factor exponents give both entropies. The first k−1 gaps are no larger than any other k−1 consecutive gaps, hence the maximal marker count on n positions is bounded by the initial count, which is o(n) by unbounded gaps. The claimed invariant-probability calculation follows, also covering limit points. Distinct polynomial entropies obstruct conjugacy; each space can be transported to the same ordinal. Independent replay /workspace/reviews/evidence/1-factor-count.py used integer nth-root comparisons for exact floored rational powers, encoded factors as integer bit windows, and exhausted the language at each length 1–160 for seven power families, geometric gaps and a large initial jump (1,440 length/family cases). The prefix includes two consecutive gaps larger than 160 so all shorter one-marker offsets and all possible multimerker factors are represented. These finite checks do not prove the asymptotic or topology.
