A single countable compact space of type \(\omega^2+1\) supports uniquely ergodic continuous maps with dense forward orbits and every polynomial entropy in \([1,2]\), despite all having zero ordinary entropy. Strictly increasing marker gaps give an exact all-length factor-count formula, and gaps \(\lfloor j^\alpha\rfloor\) yield the sharp coefficient \(\frac\alpha{\alpha+1}(1-2^{-1/\alpha})\) in growth of order \(n^{1+1/\alpha}\).
