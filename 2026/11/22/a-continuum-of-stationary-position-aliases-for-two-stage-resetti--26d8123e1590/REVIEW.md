# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claimed full-distribution aliasing follows from an explicit regenerative occupation calculation, not from parameter fitting: the exploration clock has survival \(e^{-rt}(1+rt)\), the reset endpoint has density \(\kappa e^{-\kappa|x|}(1+\kappa|x|)/4\), and the return occupation integral evaluates through \(\Gamma(1-\alpha)\) and \(\Gamma(2-\alpha)\). Normalization, positive finite cycle length, all quantifiers, endpoint cases, inverse parameter domain, stochastic-order sign, and total variation were checked analytically; the supplied checker passed 192 test assertions. Risk: the return ODE without a chosen departure rule is nonunique for \(\alpha>0\), so the theorem explicitly specifies immediate departure, rather than silently assuming uniqueness of the ODE.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Radice (2022, arXiv:2201.01829, sections 2–4 and appendix) treats Gamma clocks with constant-speed return and gives general stationary occupation tools; Bodrova and Sokolov (2019, arXiv:1907.12326, sections II–V) treat deterministic returns, acceleration, and exponential or deterministic clocks; Zhou–Xu–Deng (2021, arXiv:2105.12571) consider acceleration and rest phases under other clocks. No inspected statement provides this all-powered-return Gamma-two inverse classification, exact stationary aliasing across exponents, phase recovery, and the sharp total-variation family. The 2019–2022 renewal framework could make parts derivable after specialization; any independently existing generalized identifiability theorem would affect priority. Close but different public research on stationary aliasing in intermittent diffusion uses a different switching process. The prior Gamma constant-speed formula itself is **not** included in the novelty claim.

**Value — PASS.** Inferring a return mechanism from stationary position snapshots is a natural inverse problem in finite-time resetting. The noninjectivity is complete at the *full-density* level, has explicit physically different ballistic/constant-acceleration witnesses, and is accompanied by an exact recipe for breaking it through an observable phase fraction and return-phase variance. This is stronger than a coincidence of a few moments and has a clear experimental-design implication. The Gamma-two choice is a natural first non-memoryless Erlang clock; power-law return speed covers constant speed and constant acceleration without an arbitrary finite slice.

**Literature and limits.** The claim depends on a precisely specified start-immediately powered return, on known exploration parameters, and on a Gamma-two clock. Broader renewal identities are known and credited. Novelty is assessed on the new cross-protocol inverse classification, not on generic renewal reward. Independent publication-priority validation has not occurred.
