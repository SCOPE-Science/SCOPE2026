At any fixed disease-free information equilibrium, the epidemic invasion spectral radius in an asymmetric two-city contact model is convex in commuter intensity, so two safe commuting extremes cannot conceal an unsafe intermediate intensity. An exact admissible ten-person network nevertheless has an intervening *safe* commuting window \((7-\sqrt7)/48<\sigma<(7+\sqrt7)/48\) despite invasion at both extremes, and a closed equitable-network formula classifies when this reversal occurs.
