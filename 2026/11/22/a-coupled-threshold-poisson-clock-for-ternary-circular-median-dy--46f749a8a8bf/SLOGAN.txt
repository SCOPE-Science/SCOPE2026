For a uniformly random three-level signal repeatedly smoothed by the synchronous three-point circular median, the global periodic-entry time has a new lattice Poisson window: \(\Pr\{\tau_n\le t\}\to\exp(-10\alpha/9)\) whenever \(n(2/9)^{t+1}\to\alpha>0\). The sharp worst-case delay is \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\), and on even rings precisely \(4\,2^{n/2}-2\) initial signals eventually oscillate rather than freeze.
