# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed result is restricted to the explicitly stated real cubic flow with \(\lambda>0\) and \(|h|=\sqrt{1+\lambda^2}/\lambda\). Polar reduction gives \(\dot\theta=1\); \(u=r^{-2}\) satisfies the exactly solvable linear equation \(u'=-2\lambda u+2(1-h\cos2\theta)\). The periodic particular solution reduces at criticality to \(2\sin^2(\theta-\theta_*)/\lambda\), so the sign of the decaying homogeneous constant is **necessary and sufficient** for forward completeness. At each future zero phase it gives an exact radius sample, not a radial maximum, since \(r'=\lambda r>0\) there. Disk-chord integration produces the finite-radius area identity and its linear large-radius asymptotics. Time-periodic phase averaging of bounded indicator functions, with a finite null set of exceptional phases, gives the arcsine occupation survival function and its Pareto tail. The supplied standalone verifier checks rational coefficient equalities and performs numerical cross-checks. It does not substitute for the analytic all-time proof. The boundary \(\Delta=0\), the origin, both signs of critical anisotropy and all positive radii are treated explicitly.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Llibre (2024, preprint posted 2024-07-24; full preprint sections 1–2 and the relevant infinity/limit-cycle classifications examined) treats homogeneous rigid polynomial flows, presents a general Bernoulli solution and studies a graphic at infinity. Gasull and Torregrosa (article electronically published 2004-10-07; its full available mathematical text, main theorems and examples examined) already derive the inverse-radial linearization and limit-cycle disappearance at infinity. These foundational identities and the qualitative polycycle are not claimed here. The new combined claim quantifies the complete-versus-exploding initial-condition geometry at the critical harmonic and both the exact disk-area law and **physical-original-time** Pareto occupation along the remaining unbounded complete trajectories. Those properties require a distinct initial-state measure integral and a limiting phase-occupation argument, and were not stated in the inspected original mathematical texts. Available abstract of Gasull–Prohens–Torregrosa (2005) characterizes phase portraits of cubic rigid systems but did not yield the relevant measure theorem; the inaccessible full text remains a priority risk. Publicly indexed nearby results on finite-time survival ellipses or radial occupation of different polynomial oscillators do not imply this theorem because their vector fields, critical regimes and sampling sets differ. Search absence by itself is not an originality proof.

## Value — PASS

A limit cycle disappearing through infinity raises a natural global question that qualitative Poincaré-disc portraits do not settle: which *finite planar initial states* remain forward complete at the threshold, and how much time do their escaping-but-recurrent trajectories spend at large radius? This theorem answers with a sharp invariant straight-strip boundary, exact area in every Euclidean disk (infinite total area but asymptotically zero density), and a universal Pareto first-moment divergence under original-time observation. The linear area coefficient and the occupancy tail come from the same double-zero geometry and make this finite-dimensional critical phenomenon quantitatively testable. The claim is an exact natural geometric/dynamical invariant, not a new integrability method or a broad bifurcation theorem.

## Limitations and nearest prior work

The 2004/2005 general Riccati solution and its polycycle criterion are prior and cover the base algebraic reduction. The 2005 cubic-classification paper has not been completely inspected; unindexed exact spatial or temporal measure results might supersede the conclusion. The theorem does not establish a global attractor or any compact invariant probability for these noncompact complete orbits. Finite checker observations do not establish all-time assertions.
