At the critical anisotropy of a homogeneous cubic rigid flow, the forward-complete initial states form an exact invariant-boundary **straight strip**, yet every nonzero trajectory in that strip recurrently spikes to infinity while all exterior and boundary states blow up in finite time. The strip has disk-truncated area asymptotic to \(2\sqrt{2\lambda}\,R\), and the original-time radial occupation tail along every complete nonzero orbit is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2\lambda}/(\pi R)\).
