# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof fixes the infinite lattice, nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian, pure origin reset, positive reset and hopping rates, and the order of limits. Its key identity expresses the normalized collision probability as the squared \(\ell^2\)-norm of the free occupation Green function. A Neumann/Parseval fourth-Bessel bound and Cauchy–Schwarz establish finiteness for every fixed dimension above two. An explicit moment-based annulus argument proves divergence at dimension two and a logarithmic lower bound, while an independent elementary Fourier calculation gives the exact one-dimensional formula. The off-diagonal witness and the published full-purity representation establish strict coherence and the high-dimensional asymptotic ratio. The package's 55 deterministic numerical tests pass; they support normalization and algebra, not infinite-dimensional existence by themselves.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest published result explicitly determines complete-reset quantum lattice *ensemble purity* for every dimension, including its logarithmic weak-reset corrections. That does **not** give the diagonal collision statistic or its \(\ell^2\) spatial Green threshold merely by identifying two equal quantities: coherent and diagonal purities are different observables, and an exact nonzero off-diagonal witness proves the difference. The primary tight-binding-reset papers derive one-dimensional stationary occupations and general resetting schemes, but the inspected materials do not state or imply the all-dimensional square-summability classification. The exact one-dimensional identity is acknowledged as a routine consequence of classical Bessel transforms; novelty is assessed for the critical-dimensional classification and coherence comparison together. A general published \(\ell^2\)-Green theorem could cover this specific quantum case even if indexed searches missed it.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The collision probability is the chance that two independent site measurements drawn from the stationary ensemble agree, a natural operational localization statistic distinct from quantum purity. The theorem identifies the exact dimension at which its rare-reset quadratic coefficient becomes finite, proves a nontrivial lower-growth mechanism in the critical plane, and quantifies an experimentally relevant divergence between measured-position concentration and coherent-state information. The critical two-dimensional logarithmic *coefficient* is deliberately not claimed. Finite dimension-three calculations are diagnostic rather than part of a proof of the infinite-domain threshold.

## Closest literature and limitations
Acharya–Gupta (arXiv:2308.14040, originally 27 August 2023), Dattagupta–Das–Gupta (arXiv:2209.14117), and the published all-dimensional purity result are the main comparisons. The main remaining priority risk is an abstract collision-Green or stationary-quantum-walk theorem covering the same specialization. The two-dimensional bound is not an exact asymptotic equivalent. A future independent audit would be separate from this review.
