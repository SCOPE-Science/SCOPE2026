For a Poisson-reset quantum lattice walker, the stationary measured-position collision probability has a finite weak-reset coefficient after division by \(r^2\) **exactly in dimensions \(d\ge3\)**; it diverges in dimensions one and two despite the existence of a stationary distribution at every positive reset rate. In dimensions at least three, the full ensemble quantum purity is asymptotically larger than the spatial collision probability by a factor proportional to \(1/r\), revealing coherence invisible to the site probabilities.
