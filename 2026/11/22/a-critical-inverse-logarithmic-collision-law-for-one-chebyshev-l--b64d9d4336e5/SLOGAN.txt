Moving one bulk Chebyshev–Lobatto sampling node to within an angular-mesh fraction \(\varepsilon_n\) of its neighbor has a sharp three-regime interpolation-norm transition at \(\varepsilon_n\log n\asymp1\). At the critical scale, every normalized maximizer converges to a uniquely determined opposite-side offset and the exact logarithmic amplification has a one-variable formula; below it, \(\varepsilon_n\Lambda_n\to2\).
