For independent uniform centers on a unit circle, conditioned on all centers fitting in a semicircle, an intersection of radius-\(1+\gamma/n^2\) disks converges after anisotropic rescaling to an explicit random parabolic half-lens. Its area on the \(n^{-3}\) scale has a closed exponential-mixture law, with a sharp radius boundary layer separating the singular unit-radius lens from nonzero-width half-lenses.
