For two distinct fixed centers, differences of affine Gaussian-Fock composition contractions have operator norm \(\asymp\sqrt{1-q}\), yet their Hilbert–Schmidt norm tends to \(|u-v|/2\) and their Schatten norms cross a sharp threshold at \(p=2\). The complete rescaled singular-value counting curve is the superlevel-length distribution of \(|u-v|\sqrt{x}e^{-x}\).
