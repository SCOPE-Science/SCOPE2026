Two nested disk Toeplitz compressions on the full bianalytic Bergman space have an exact paired singular spectrum, and moving both disks toward the boundary produces the universal cubic-exponential profile \(ab|a-b|u^3e^{-(a+b)u}\). Their commutator tends strongly to zero but retains a positive operator-norm limit, with an explicit sharp two-rate optimum and exact limits for every Schatten power.
