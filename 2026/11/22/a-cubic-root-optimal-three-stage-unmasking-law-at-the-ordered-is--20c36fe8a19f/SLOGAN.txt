With exactly three parallel reveals, a nearly perfectly ordered binary Ising chain needs two distinct sparse seeding scales: first-round density \(\asymp\xi^{-2/3}\) and second-round density \(\asymp\xi^{-1/3}\). The globally minimum path-relative-entropy density is \(3(\log2/4)^{1/3}\xi^{-2/3}(1+o(1))\), with sharp constants for both reveal densities.
