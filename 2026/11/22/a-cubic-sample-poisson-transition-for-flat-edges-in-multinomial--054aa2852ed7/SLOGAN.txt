For a uniform multinomial histogram with an increasing number \(n\) of ordered bins, zero adjacent count differences undergo a cubic-sample transition: at \(N\sim cn^3\), their scaled positions form a homogeneous Poisson point process of intensity \((4\pi c)^{-1/2}\). Below this scale the number of flat edges concentrates around a diverging mean (assuming diverging per-bin load), and above it no flat edge survives with high probability.
