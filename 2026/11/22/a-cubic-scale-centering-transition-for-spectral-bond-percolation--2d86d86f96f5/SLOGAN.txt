At the exact spectral bond-percolation scale, the hub cluster of a windmill with many growing cliques has an unexpected Gaussian centering transition: indirect intra-clique connections become visible in the normalized law precisely when the clique size is of order \(n^{1/3}\). The limiting normal mean shifts by \(c^{3/2}t^{3/4}\) when the ratio of clique size to \(n^{1/3}\) tends to \(t\), while the variance remains one.
