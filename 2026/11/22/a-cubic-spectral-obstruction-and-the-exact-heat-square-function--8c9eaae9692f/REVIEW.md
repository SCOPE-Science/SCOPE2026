# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The two-eigenmode heat integral and its quartic deficit are elementary exact identities; a nonzero cubic cross moment forces negative deficit by a sign-controlled perturbation. On every cycle of order at least five, cosine-mode orthogonality gives a uniform strictly positive cubic moment, and the fixed perturbation \(-1/64\) makes the deficit negative with rigorous graph-size-uniform bounds. On the triangle the generator is scalar on mean-zero vectors; on the four-cycle an exact Hilbert-valued deficit equals \((17/81)\sum_j c_j^2\). The standalone diagnostics pass and are not used as an infinite certificate.

## Originality
PASS relative to checked literature and indexed published findings. Lai–Li–Li prove general order bounds, while the present statement is an exact finite-cycle cutoff and a strict cubic spectral obstruction. Dungey addresses a gradient function, Feneuil a discrete fractional graph functional; none of the inspected relevant statements entails this result. The known small-cycle contracting instances are recovered but the all-orders failure and spectral mechanism are new relative to the checked material. An older theorem phrased as a nonlinear square-function spectral interaction remains possible, and accessible material for Feneuil did not include its entire full text.

## Value
PASS. The contraction benchmark \(1/2\) is exactly the square-function value for a single heat eigenmode. It is natural to ask when mixing eigenspaces preserves that optimal benchmark. The result shows that the smallest two cycles are exceptional and *every* larger simple cycle fails, with a single explicit witness template and a reusable cubic criterion. This distinguishes a genuine spectral-mixing obstruction from mere finite-size numerical examples; a sharp constant for failing cycles is not claimed.
