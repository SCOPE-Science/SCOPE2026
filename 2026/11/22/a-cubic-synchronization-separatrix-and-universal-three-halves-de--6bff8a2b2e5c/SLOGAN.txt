At the critical sine-memory coupling of a published three-dimensional map, a unique cubic threshold separates exponentially synchronizing trajectories from those that cross the neutral phase. Exactly on that threshold, the synchronization gap decays as \(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}n^{-3/2}\), and the threshold has the sharp expansion \(U(w)=w^3/6+w^5/120+O(w^7)\).
