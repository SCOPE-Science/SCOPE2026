For a fixed proper interval graph on \(m\) named vertices, the number of possible nonalgebraic adjacency types of one new unrestricted interval is exactly \(1+\binom{m+1}{2}-(|E|-m+c)\), where \(c\) is the number of components. More precisely, each absent consecutive-block type corresponds bijectively to an edge jumping over its two flanking vertices; the resulting full type-size polynomial is explicit.
