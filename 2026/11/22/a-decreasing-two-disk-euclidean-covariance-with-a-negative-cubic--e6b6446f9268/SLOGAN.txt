The autocorrelation of \(3\mathbf1_{B_{1/2}}+2\mathbf1_{B_{3/2}}\) is a nonnegative decreasing, compactly supported strictly positive-definite covariance on \(\mathbb R^2\), yet its unchanged geodesic kernel on \(\mathbb S^2\) has a rigorously negative cubic Legendre coefficient. Degrees zero through two remain positive, so monotonicity and pointwise nonnegativity do not protect Euclidean-to-spherical covariance transfer.
