# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The Stieltjes-measure representation reduces the pointwise right-continuous BV seminorm problem exactly to single jumps. Convexity of squared error within every sampling cell reduces the sharp norm to finitely many one-sided grid traces. An independent binomial-tail polynomial expansion and exact Fraction arithmetic checks the complete finite comparisons in degrees 1–15, including the degree-15 rational value. The asymptotic normal profile, Gaussian tail integral, and uniform boundary estimate establish the global large-degree constant and concentration of maximizing jumps. The certificate does not replace these infinite-dimensional steps.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The 2003 step-function Bernstein work and 2009 iterative Bernstein study do not state the sharp integrated \(L^2\) BV norm, finite crossover, or global sharp asymptotic. The later accessible Adell–Garrancho–Martínez-Sánchez study provides local pointwise jump estimates rather than this integrated extremal statement. The directly relevant Bustamante–García-Muñoz–Quesada 2014 article was identified through an abstract and a later substantive citation, but its full text could not be read; an equivalent result there or in another unindexed source remains possible. Existing \(L^1\) sharp-error formulas, other Bernstein variants, and general normal approximations do not automatically imply the exact \(L^2\) finite-degree behavior.

## Value — PASS
The sharp \(L^2\) error governs energy loss for jump-dominated sampled signals. The degree-15 change from an endpoint sampling artifact to a genuine interior approximation error is a natural structural boundary, and the sharp asymptotic coefficient and localization identify the worst discontinuities at high degree. These conclusions concern a full operator seminorm rather than a hand-picked finite sample.

## Scope and scientific limitations
A complete integer-location classification above degree 15 and a quantitative asymptotic remainder are not proved. The domain is pointwise Jordan-BV with a specified right-continuous representative. The 2014 primary source's inaccessible full text is the principal historical-priority caveat. The calendar day for the earlier 2003 publication was not verified; the archived dated source is the first version of arXiv:0909.0684 (3 September 2009).
