The sharp pointwise-BV-to-\(L^2\) Bernstein approximation error is an exact maximum of rational single-jump boundary traces; it is \((2n+1)^{-1/2}\) through degree 14, but degree 15 has a strictly worse interior jump at \(3/5\). Its sharp large-degree norm is asymptotic to \(\bigl((\sqrt2-1)/(2\sqrt\pi)\bigr)^{1/2}n^{-1/4}\), and all nearly worst jump locations converge to \(1/2\).
