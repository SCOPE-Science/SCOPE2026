For the tree with \(r-1\) short leaves and one arm of length \(h\), the connected zero-forcing polynomial has exactly \(2\lfloor h/4\rfloor\) nonzero roots in the open right half-plane, independent of \(r\ge3\). It is strictly Hurwitz stable after removing its zero factor exactly for \(h\le3\), and its negative-real roots obey an explicit parity-and-branching phase diagram.
