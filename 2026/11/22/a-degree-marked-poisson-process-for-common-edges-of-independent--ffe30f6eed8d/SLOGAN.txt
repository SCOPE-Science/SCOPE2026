For two independent uniform labeled trees, their common edges form a Poisson spatial process of total intensity \(2\) whose four endpoint degrees have an explicit non-product mark law: within either tree the degree pair is \((1+P+B,2+Q-B)\), with independent unit-rate Poisson variables \(P,Q\) and a fair bit \(B\). The number of common edges pendant at the same endpoint in both trees converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(e^{-2})\), independently of the count with opposite pendant endpoints.
