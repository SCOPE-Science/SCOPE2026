For quadratic-reproducing King–Bernstein sampling, the worst integrated bounded-variation approximation error shifts from jumps approaching the left boundary at degrees \(1\)–\(8\) to jumps approaching the interior lattice site \(1/3\) from the right at degree \(9\), with the entire finite comparison certified by exact integer inequalities. All degrees admit a sharp beta-quantile formula, and the norm is asymptotic to \(1/\sqrt{2\pi n}\) with worst jumps converging to the midpoint.
