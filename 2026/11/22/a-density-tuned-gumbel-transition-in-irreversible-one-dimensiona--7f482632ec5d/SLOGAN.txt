For iid Bernoulli starting spins, an irreversible one-dimensional \(\delta\)-Ising chain has Gumbel ordering times with a sharp density threshold at \(p=1/2\): there, and only there among fixed densities, an extra \(\log\log L\) delay occurs. In the window \(p=1/2+a/\log L\), the complete crossover is governed by \(w(a)=(1-e^{-2a})/(2a)\) with \(w(0)=1\).
