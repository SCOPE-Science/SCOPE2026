# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact transform follows from an explicit Dirichlet boundary problem and the independent-exponential factorization follows from Euler's product. The proof derives every integer cumulant and separately checks that the threshold function has one crossing, that the ratios are strictly ordered across orders, and that the spectral rate fixes their limit. The nonstandard strict-ordering step is supported by an explicit bound on the odd-power remainder \(H_m\) on the entire interval of crossing points. The independent code tests 99 finite numerical identities and inequalities but is not treated as proof of the all-order theorem.

## Originality — PASS (with residual risk)

The 2022 first-passage model supplies angular transforms and the Poisson survival law; it does not assert the nested cumulant-order hierarchy, sharp asymptotic approach, or variance–survival-tail inversion. A 2026 angular escape-versus-killing result already contains the order-two crossing \(1/\sqrt5\), which is expressly excluded as a standalone contribution. The 2023 competing-killing study addresses conditional event times, not the independent mean-matched tumble process. An equivalently strong general theorem on Bernoulli-thinned exponential sums might imply the hierarchy, and indexing/search cannot eliminate this risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Matching means is a principled experimental calibration, and the strict hierarchy answers how high-order trapping-time fluctuations diverge from a memoryless comparator. The limit is fixed by the slowest spectral relaxation mode; its separation from the variance threshold identifies a genuine rare-event-versus-bulk inversion window. The result is an all-order structural statement, not a numerical example or reparameterization of the original survival transform.

## Limits

The regime is a single angular binding episode with symmetric Brownian heading or uniform-angle Poisson reorientation. It does not imply whole-channel lifetime ordering or an all-time survival inequality. Primary paper formulas and the isolated second-order threshold are acknowledged as prior ingredients; priority beyond inspected work requires independent checking.
