For a spatial Beverton–Holt fishery at fixed mean harvesting effort, every nonconstant stationary effort allocation raises mean standing biomass, but its effect on catch switches sign across a sharp diffusion threshold. For a sinusoidal redistribution in an explicit Neumann habitat, catch changes quadratically by \(\frac{2D-1}{8(1+D)^2}\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^3)\), with cutoff \(D=1/2\).
