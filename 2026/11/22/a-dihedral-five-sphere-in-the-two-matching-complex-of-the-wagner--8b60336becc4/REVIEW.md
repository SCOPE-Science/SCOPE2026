Both dualities contribute their respective ambient permutation signs. The vertex-complex Lefschetz calculation gives traces \(+1,-1\) for the two generators, and the two sign twists change these to \(-1,-1\). Neither supplies the generator signs on integral homology; those require the signed permutation calculation and sign twists. The general duality, Lefschetz formula and averaging are background. Possible equivalent or unindexed computations remain a priority limitation.

This is a reusable equivariant datum in comparisons of graph complexes, not an originality claim for the background homotopy type or an assertion about integral orbit-space homology.
