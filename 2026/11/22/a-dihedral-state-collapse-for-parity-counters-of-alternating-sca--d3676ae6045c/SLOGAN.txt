For every alternating binary target of length \(k\ge1\), the exact minimum deterministic memory for reporting its scattered-subsequence count modulo two is only \(2^{\lceil\log_2(k+1)\rceil}\) states. Every counter value is obtained from a single cyclic state, with the two input symbols acting as reflections \(t\mapsto-t-1\) and \(t\mapsto-t\).
