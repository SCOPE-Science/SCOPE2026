For sine-forced pinched skew products, the neutral zero-graph threshold strictly decreases with forcing dimension from \(2\) to \(\pi/2\), and its two-frequency value is exactly \(4\exp(-7\zeta(3)/\pi^2)\). A second-order large-dimension law locates the unique dimension where any fixed intermediate forcing strength changes the zero graph's transverse Lyapunov exponent from nonpositive to positive.
