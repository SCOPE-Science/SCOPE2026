For voter spins independently reset to their fixed initial opinions, the standardized quenched stationary **site-time average** has a Gaussian weak-reset limit exactly in dimensions at most four; in dimensions at least five it converges to a strictly non-Gaussian Green-function Rademacher series. At the critical dimension four its excess kurtosis vanishes as a negative constant divided by \(\log^2(1/r)\), sharply separating the regimes.
