For exact quadratic spherical harmonics in every ambient dimension \(d\ge3\), tangential-gradient concentration on two antipodal caps has three explicit beta-integral eigenvalue branches, and its maximizing modes switch universally at polar cutoff \(t=1/\sqrt2\), independently of dimension. The transverse trace-free modes are always least observable, and the sharp squared observation condition number grows as \((d+1)/[2(1-t^2)]\) for shrinking caps.
