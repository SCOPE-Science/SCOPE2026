For one noisy point measurement of a \(d\)-parameter Brownian sheet's integral, the unique optimal location has equal coordinates; they move strictly toward the far corner with either increasing positive noise or increasing dimension at any fixed positive noise. At fixed positive noise the explained-variance fraction decays as \((3/4)^d/\eta\), rather than the noiseless \((8/9)^d\) law, and the exponentially small-noise crossover has an exact Lambert-function scaling.
