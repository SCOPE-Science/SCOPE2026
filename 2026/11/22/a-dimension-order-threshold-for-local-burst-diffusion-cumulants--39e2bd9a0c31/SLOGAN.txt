A Poisson point source of burst-born Brownian particles has a sharp microscopic detector-cumulant transition at \(dk=2\): order-\(k\) factorial cumulants scale as \(\varepsilon^{dk}\), \(\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)\), or \(\varepsilon^2\), depending on whether dimension times order is below, equal to, or above two. The singular scaling disappears at every fixed non-source detector location, and the saturated coefficients include the exact values \(C_{2,2}=\tfrac12\log2\) and \(C_{3,1}=\tfrac12\).
