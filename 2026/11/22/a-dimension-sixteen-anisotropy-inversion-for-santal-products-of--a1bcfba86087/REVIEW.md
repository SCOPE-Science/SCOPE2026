## Mathematical value — PASS
At leading quadratic order, the Santaló deficit sees only the total squared asymmetry; the next order identifies how the same asymmetry should be distributed among axes. The sign flips in a precisely identified dimension, producing an exact exchange of local minimizers and maximizers rather than a routine finite example. This is a motivated structural question in convex-body volume-product geometry, relevant to the newly studied shifted-zonotope regularizer family. There is no claim of a general Mahler inequality or global displacement optimization.
