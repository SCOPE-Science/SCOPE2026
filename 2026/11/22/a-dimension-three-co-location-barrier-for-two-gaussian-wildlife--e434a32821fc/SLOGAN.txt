Two symmetrically placed Gaussian wildlife detectors face a sharp spatial-dimension barrier: from three dimensions onward, a nonzero uncertainty range makes co-location uniquely optimal even for perfect detectors. The exact critical variance ratio is \(0.6377083885\ldots\) in three dimensions, the golden ratio in four dimensions, and grows as \(2^{d/2}-d/4-1+O(d^2 2^{-d/2})\) in high dimensions.
