With independent fragment survival \(p(v)=\exp(-b/v^{1/d})\), one dimension-dependent preferred size gives a sharp upper bound on the probability that **at least one** fragment survives from any fixed total material budget, with exact equality precisely at integer multiples of that size. Equal-sized cutting needs only a two-integer comparison; for planar fragments the preferred area is approximately \(0.298507352140b^2\), under the stipulated rather than field-calibrated mortality law.
