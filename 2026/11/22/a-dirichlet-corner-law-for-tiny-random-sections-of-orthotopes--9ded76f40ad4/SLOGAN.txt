Tiny motion-invariant affine sections of every \(d\)-dimensional orthotope obey a sharp \(t^{1/(d-1)}\) probability law with an explicit gamma-function constant that depends on the box only through its sum of side lengths. Conditioned on rarity, the squared normal coordinates have a universal Dirichlet limit, the near-contact corner is uniform, and the rescaled section volume has an independent beta law; at fixed volume, the cube uniquely maximizes the leading probability.
