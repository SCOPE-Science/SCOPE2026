For zero-modified Poisson–Lindley counts, ordinary real-valued infinite divisibility occurs on a disconnected parameter set: the isolated zero-truncation endpoint \(\pi=\pi_{\min}\) and the interval \(\pi\geq-\theta^2(2\theta+3)/[(\theta+2)^2(2\theta+1)]\). The isolated law is a one-unit shift of compound Poisson; the upper branch is unshifted compound Poisson, while every intermediate parameter eventually has negative even logarithmic coefficients.
