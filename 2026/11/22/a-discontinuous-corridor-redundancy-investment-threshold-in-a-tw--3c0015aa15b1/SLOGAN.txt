In a two-route habitat network with independent corridor passage probabilities \(p(x)=x/(1+x)\), the globally optimal use of a fixed restoration budget jumps sharply from fully funding one two-edge route to funding all four edges equally at \(B_*=2\sigma\), where \(2\sigma^3+\sigma^2-8\sigma-8=0\). A single-best-path metric never makes this switch, and equal funding is locally optimal yet globally inferior over a nonempty interval.
