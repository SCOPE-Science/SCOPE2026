Ghosh--Boyd--Saberi's 2006 conference article develops general electrical-resistance network design and explicitly lists maximum pair resistance as an SDP objective; the inspected public text does not state the specific priced-cycle cutoff, complete equality face, or nonzero one-sided limit. The published published-finding corpus treatment of an expensive edge on a cycle instead minimizes **total** pair resistance; its critical optimizer is unique and its threshold differs. The closest priced-cycle **spectral-gap** results share the square-length cutoff, but their objectives and actual optimal conductance allocations differ. Matching threshold numbers are not equivalent theorems. A less visible special-case theorem or a derivation implicit in the general convex framework remains a priority risk; the searches cannot certify absolute novelty.

The classification does not extend to general graphs or arbitrary edge prices. Independent publication-priority and mathematical audit remain unperformed.
