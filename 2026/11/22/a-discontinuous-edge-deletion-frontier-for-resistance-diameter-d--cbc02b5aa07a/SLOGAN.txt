On an \((m+1)\)-cycle with one closing edge priced at \(c\) and all other edges priced at one, minimizing the largest pairwise electrical resistance deletes the closing edge uniquely for \(c>m^2\), but retains it in every optimum for \(c<m^2\). At the exact threshold there is a complete optimal line segment, and the optimum approaches its strictly positive closing-edge endpoint from below, creating a discontinuous edge-deletion transition.
