In a two-route habitat network with two investable links and one independently fragile stepping-stone habitat per route, the globally optimal connectivity investment jumps from fully funding one route to funding all four links equally at the exact threshold \(B_c(s)=4\log(1+\sqrt{2/(1-s)})\). At that budget exactly three distinct allocations tie; no asymmetric compromise is optimal, while two one-link backup routes are always best funded equally.
