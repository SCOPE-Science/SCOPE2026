On the three-vertex path, the exact vertex-\(\ell^2\)-to-edge-\(\ell^2\) two-input Lipschitz norm of centered fractional maximal averaging has two explicit spectral branches separated by the unique solution of \(2^{\alpha-1}+3^{\alpha-1}=1\). At that exponent the optimal constant jumps down from about \(1.593842\) to \(1.174412\), even though the underlying maximal map depends continuously on \(\alpha\); both branches are attained by positive real input pairs.
