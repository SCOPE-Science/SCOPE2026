In a Pareto-weighted polymer network, short-molecule catalytic links appear in a sharply centered logarithmic window and arrive in compound Poisson bursts with Sibuya-distributed sizes. Even when a self-sustaining autocatalytic set exists with high probability, every such set must include a polymer longer than \(\theta n-o(n)\), where \(\theta=\alpha(1+\beta)/(1+\alpha)\).
