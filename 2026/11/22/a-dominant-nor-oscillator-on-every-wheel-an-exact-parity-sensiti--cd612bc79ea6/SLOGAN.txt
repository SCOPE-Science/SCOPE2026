Synchronous open-neighborhood NOR on every wheel has a unique dominant two-cycle that attracts exactly \(2^n+L_n\) initial states for odd rim length \(n\), and \(2^n+L_{n/2}^2\) for even \(n\). Its basin always contains more than half of all binary states, even though every trajectory is periodic after at most one update. On the bare rim the same orbit attracts only \(T_n+1\) states, a vanishing fraction, so one universal hub drives a sharp asymptotic basin-probability change from zero to one-half.
