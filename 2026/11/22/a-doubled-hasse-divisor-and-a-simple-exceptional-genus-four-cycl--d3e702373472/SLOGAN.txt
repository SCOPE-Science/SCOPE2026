In characteristic \(31\), the genus-four cyclic-quintic family \(y^5=x^3+ax+b\) has doubled nonordinary Cartier divisor \(b^2=7a^3\), a punctured cusp of marked parameters. Its distinguished exceptional model \(y^5=x^3+x+10\) has zeta numerator \(1+101T^2+4371T^4+97061T^6+923521T^8\) and a simple Jacobian over \(\mathbb F_{31}\).
