In characteristic \(13\), smooth Picard curves \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+bx+c\) have a doubled nonordinary Hasse divisor \(a^2=8c\), with Cartier \(a\)-number exactly \(2\) there and \(0\) elsewhere. The complete \(\mathbb F_{13}\) coefficient census is \(144\) nonordinary and \(1884\) ordinary smooth curves; a smooth \(\mathbb F_{169}\) member explicitly contradicts the unrestricted ordinarity assertion of Nourozi–Rahmati (2023).
