With one shared Poisson reset clock, \(n\) independent-noise geometric Brownian particles have a sharp simultaneous upper-tail law with a nontrivial prefactor and a drift-dependent residual exponent \(\alpha_n=(\sqrt{a^2+2r\sigma^2}+a)/(\sqrt{a^2+2r\sigma^2/n}+a)\), strictly smaller than the exponent \(n\) of independent resets. The exponent hierarchy is bounded in \(n\) for positive logarithmic drift, grows as \(\sqrt n\) for zero drift, and grows linearly for negative drift; all three statements concern the fixed-\(n\) far tail followed by the size limit.
