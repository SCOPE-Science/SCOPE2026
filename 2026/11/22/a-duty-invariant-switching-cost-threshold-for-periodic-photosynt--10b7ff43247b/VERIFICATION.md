# Verification

The analytical proof uses the unique periodic fixed point of a scalar contraction, integration of the illuminated flow, and the strict concavity of a harmonic mean composed with two hyperbolic tangents. The derivative test settles all positive real input parameters, including the sharp cost threshold. The small-cost expansion has order \(T^3\) in the stationary-point function.

Run `python3 checker.py` with the standard Python library. The deterministic checker passed 12,112 assertions, including unequal positive light and dark repair rates and both nonattainment regimes. This is diagnostic corroboration, not an infinite-parameter proof or independent validation.
