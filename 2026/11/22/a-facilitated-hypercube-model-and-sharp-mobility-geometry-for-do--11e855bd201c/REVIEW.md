# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The permutation class admits the previously known unique composition-block model. A direct all-cases lemma shows exactly which adjacent-position transpositions preserve the two avoidance restrictions, producing a facilitated-hypercube graph. Counting degrees, constructing short paths, and producing a matching lower-bound pair prove the all-length formulas. The standalone verifier checked 254 admissible permutations directly, every adjacency and all-pairs distances through length 8, with Hamilton paths through length 14. Finite checks support but do not prove the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Simion–Schmidt/Janson already characterize the composition blocks; this is **not** a new enumeration. Baril and Barcucci–Bernini–Pinzani already establish related permutation Gray codes, including an inverse-related class. Inversion turns position swaps into value swaps, and the checked sources do not supply this target induced-graph degree distribution or sharp distance. A known East-model metric formulated outside the searched terms may yield an equivalent result; this remains a priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The exact local degree law and sharp reconfiguration diameter quantify mobility and optimal adjacency-step generation in a canonical doubly restricted permutation class. The full-family reconfiguration geometry is more informative than its already known number of elements.

## Limits

This is a graph-theoretic result for forbidden patterns \(231\) and \(321\) under adjacent-position swaps; it makes no stochastic mixing claim. Independent historical review and independent mathematical audit are outstanding.
