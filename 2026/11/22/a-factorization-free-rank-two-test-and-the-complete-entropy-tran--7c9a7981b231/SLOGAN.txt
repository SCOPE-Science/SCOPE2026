Among the \(256\) binary two-channel nearest-neighbor linear cellular automata, exactly \(72\) are strongly transitive, split \(18\) at entropy \(\log2\) and \(54\) at entropy \(2\log2\); the \(256\) rules form \(56\) onsite linear basis-change orbits. The classification follows from a factorization-free strong-transitivity test for every rank-two Laurent-polynomial cellular automaton.
