For synchronous three-site majority on a ring with one site pinned at zero, the complete fixation-time distribution has an explicit rational generating function, and the all-zero attraction basin has exactly \(F_{n+1}\) initial configurations on \(n\) sites. Although pinning eliminates all periodic oscillation, the probability of eventual unanimous agreement with the pinned opinion decays at the exponential rate \((\varphi/2)^n\) under uniform initialization.
