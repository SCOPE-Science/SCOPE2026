In a positive two-stage model of finite algal-toxin exposure, the downstream peak always follows bloom shutoff, and its lag strictly encodes exposure duration while becoming exponentially ill-conditioned for long blooms. Two differently calibrated responses can swap which peaks first as bloom duration varies; the pairs \((1,5)\) and \((2,3)\) have exactly one crossover at \(L=-\log z_0\), where \(z_0^3+z_0^2=1\).
