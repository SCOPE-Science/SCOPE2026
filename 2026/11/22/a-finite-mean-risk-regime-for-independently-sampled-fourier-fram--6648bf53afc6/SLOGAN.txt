Independent uniform Fourier sampling has a genuinely finite-mean reconstruction regime: for \(N\ge3\) consecutive frequencies, \(m\ge3N\) samples give finite expected inverse-Gram noise amplification, whereas \(N\le m\le2N-1\) give infinite expectation. The proof combines an all-node collision lower bound with independent square-block Lagrange tails, clarifying why isolated row collisions do not force instability after substantial oversampling.
