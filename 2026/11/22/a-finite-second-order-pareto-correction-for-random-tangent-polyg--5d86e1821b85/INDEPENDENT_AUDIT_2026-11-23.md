Anchor the cyclic list at a specified sampled contact, not the first point clockwise from a fixed direction: after subtracting the anchor, the n−1 other angles are uniform and their linear spacings, with the two endpoints, give the claimed uniform simplex law. Area is the sum of the tangent lengths and boundedness excludes gaps ≥π; beta marginals give the stated p_n. In a sector with gap π−δ and normalized remaining gaps U, the independent residual R_0 has tail O(r^{−(n−2)}). If area exceeds t there is a gap with δ<2n/t. Discarding such sectors with R_0>t^{2/3} costs O(t^{−1−2(n−2)/3}), already o(t^−2) at n=4. On the remaining sector, distance of every residual angle from its pole is at least a constant times 1/(1+R_0), far larger than δ; no second gap can qualify. The derivative of the area is −2/δ²+O(1+R_0²), uniformly negative there. Area at δ=2n/t is t/n+O(t^{2/3})<t. In the root equation replace the area by 2/δ+R_0+O(δ(1+R_0²)), then invert to obtain δ*=2/t+2R_0/t²+O((1+R_0²)/t³). Averaging the remainder costs O((1+log t)/t³) even at n=4. The n=3 residual tail instead has infinite mean; this audit does not derive the complete triangle expansion.
