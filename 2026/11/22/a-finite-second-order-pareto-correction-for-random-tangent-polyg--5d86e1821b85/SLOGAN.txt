For a fixed number of uniformly sampled tangents to the unit circle, every bounded random polygon with at least four sides has a sharp two-term area tail \(C_n/t+D_n/t^2+o(t^{-2})\), where \(D_n\) is an explicit, strictly positive beta-weighted integral. The quadrilateral coefficient is exactly \((72\log2+12)/\pi^2\), and the absence of the three-tangent logarithmic correction is explained by a finite residual first moment.
