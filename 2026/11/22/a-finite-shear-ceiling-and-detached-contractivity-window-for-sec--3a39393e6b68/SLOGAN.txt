For second-order explicit Runge–Kutta on the damped three-state Jordan chain, Euclidean one-step contractivity is controlled exactly by a rational shear frontier, with a finite sharp ceiling \(\gamma_*=1.447277013648391\ldots\). A detached, deceptively contractive timestep interval exists even though the underlying continuous flow initially expands some vectors, but disappears at the unique algebraic shear ceiling.
