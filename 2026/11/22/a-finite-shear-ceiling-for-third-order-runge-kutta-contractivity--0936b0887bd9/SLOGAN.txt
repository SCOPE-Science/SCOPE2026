For the cubic stability polynomial of every three-stage, order-three Runge–Kutta method on the stable planar Jordan shear \(A_\gamma=-I_2+\gamma N\), positive-step Euclidean nonexpansion is possible exactly for \(\gamma\le2.032159292685585\ldots\), and at the cutoff only one positive step survives. Although the exact flow is infinitesimally expansive for \(\gamma>2\), cubic RK admits a sharply bounded intermediate contractive interval for \(2<\gamma<\gamma_*\); unlike fourth-order RK on the same shear family, it admits no such interval for arbitrarily large shear.
