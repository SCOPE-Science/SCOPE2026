For the cell-incidence Walsh transform of three-row rectangular Young diagrams, width \(n=10\) is the first width whose least vanishing-character support has size three: exactly thirty triples vanish and no singleton or pair does. Among the first four widths \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), that minimum support is \(2,2,3,2\), exhibiting an exact nonmonotone cancellation threshold.
