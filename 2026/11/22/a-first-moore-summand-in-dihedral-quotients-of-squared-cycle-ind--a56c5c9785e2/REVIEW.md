PASS. The construction uses all nonempty independent sets of the squared cycle and all strict inclusion flags, with a stabilizer-safe barycentric quotient. The full exact integer chain reductions for lengths six through twenty and the independent mod-two/mod-three rank check at twenty agree. All fundamental-group generators are eliminated by explicit Tietze transformations. The homotopy inference uses simple connectivity, the verified elimination of all top cells, Hurewicz and Whitehead. The only nonunit invariant factor is two at length twenty. Computations are finite and exhaustive for exactly the claimed lengths; they do not constitute an infinite recurrence.

## Value
PASS. Full-dihedral symmetry is the canonical polygon symmetry, and squared-cycle independent sets are the natural exclusion-distance-three configurations. The lengths through twenty exhaust this family at simplicial dimension at most five, making the range structurally motivated rather than a convenient sample. The first integral Moore summand at length twenty is a sharp boundary invisible to rational Euler data; the exact homotopy classification locates its emergence and distinguishes the preceding sphere and contractible phases.
