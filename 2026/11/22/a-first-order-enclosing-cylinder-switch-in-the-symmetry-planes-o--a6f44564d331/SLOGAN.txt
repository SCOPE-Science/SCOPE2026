For right square pyramids, the smallest enclosing infinite circular cylinder **with axis direction in any vertical reflection plane** undergoes a first-order horizontal-to-oblique switch at \(h=\sqrt{2+\sqrt5}\) (base half-side normalized to one). A second local minimizer already appears at \(h=\sqrt2\); the entire constrained orientation landscape and contact configurations have exact elementary formulas, without claiming unrestricted global optimality.
