Centroidal plane sections of a capped circular paraboloid have an exact sharp aspect-ratio threshold: below it an oblique cap-crossing section maximizes area, above it the vertical section alone does, and at the threshold they tie. The onset of cap crossing has a \(3/2\)-power area deficit with coefficient \(24\sqrt6/5\) in normalized slope coordinates, and its Haar probability determines the paraboloid's aspect ratio.
