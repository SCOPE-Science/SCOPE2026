For the orthogonal shadow perimeter of a solid right circular cone, the globally minimizing viewing angle changes abruptly from side-on to oblique at one sharp height-to-radius threshold \(h_*\), even though side-on remains a strict local minimizer for every positive height. The transition is exactly characterized by elliptic-integral crossing equations, and the side-on perimeter gap is \(R c^2\log(1/c)+O(c^2)\) as the axial viewing cosine \(c\) tends to zero.
