For three independent surface-uniform points on a unit hemisphere, the Euclidean area \(T\) of their chord triangle satisfies \(\Pr(T>3\sqrt3/4-\varepsilon)\sim 32\,3^{3/4}\varepsilon^{5/2}/(45\pi)\). The rare event pins an equilateral triangle to the equator and has a five-dimensional cone limit with independent beta, Dirichlet and uniform-disk geometric marks.
