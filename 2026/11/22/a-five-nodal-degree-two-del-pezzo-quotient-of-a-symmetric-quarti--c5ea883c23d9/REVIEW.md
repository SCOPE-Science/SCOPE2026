# Scientific assessment of the corrected result

**Correctness.** The full order-eight permutation quotient, equation, transverse branch intersections and resolution are unchanged. The independent swap of the two pair sums in diagonal coordinates fixes the three defining quadrics and preserves the quartic; together with signed permutations of the two pair differences it gives a faithful order-sixteen group. The degree-sixteen map is therefore Galois, correcting the contrary description in the earlier text. The quotient by the order-eight subgroup is the intermediate double plane associated to the product of swap signs.

**Originality.** The scope is the specific embedded subgroup quotient and its explicit invariant equation. The surface and subgroup appear in Barrett–Clayton–DeVries–Priddis, §3.1.3 and Appendix A; computations for separate automorphisms do not identify the joint invariant map. Fallucca–Pignatelli §5.7 already give double planes with the same abstract branch type and basket in another construction. Uludağ §5 gives relevant Galois cover examples; non-Galoisness is not a basis of distinction. Historical priority of the exact subgroup presentation remains provisional.

**Value.** The invariant equation identifies the anticanonical morphism globally and records which branch and singularity data arise from a specified symmetry quotient of the quartic. It supplies more than a point count or a repetition of the generic double-plane formula.
