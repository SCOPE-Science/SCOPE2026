For uniformly sampled finite-field idempotent pairs satisfying \(EF=0\), both ranks have a coupled five-residue lattice-theta limit, while the reverse-product rank defect has an explicit conditional law. The extra condition \(FE=0\) has sharp probability \(q^{-2n^2/15}\) times a modulus-fifteen theta prefactor, and the total one-sided projection-pair population is asymptotically equal to that for projection–square-zero pairs.
