# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The object is the order complex of all proper nonzero \(\mathbb F_2\)-linear subspaces of \(\mathbb F_2^4\); the action is multiplication by a primitive order-fifteen element of \(\mathbb F_{16}\). The proof explicitly enumerates all \(65\) vertices, \(315\) edges and \(315\) triangles, passes to the four rank-preserving quotient CW structures, and performs homotopy-preserving one-cell/two-cell substitutions. The verified terminal presentations have respectively \(64\) and \(24\) empty two-cell attachments, or one remaining loop with attaching degrees \((5,5,5,5,5,5,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)\) and \((5,-5,0,0,0)\). Their two-cell slides prove the claimed Moore/wedge homotopy types. The standalone executable checks the exhaustive enumeration and every cancellation; finite enumeration is valid here because the object is explicitly finite. No unbounded parameter theorem is claimed.

## Originality — PASS relative to checked literature and records

Earlier calculations for the **same** rank-four binary Singer group already establish the full-order quotient's fundamental group \(C_5\) and integral homology \(H_2=\mathbb Z^4\), all Singer-subgroup fundamental groups and rational Betti numbers, and the free order-five quotient's integral homology (identifiers `f4aed456-3942-4ca4-ab32-20bd58c3997b`, `321e74dc-927b-4dd4-a0e1-359a00de8394`, `608e12b6-d804-4fc2-bb75-f59f5256e7ec`). **These numerical conclusions are not new.** The order-one and order-three sphere-wedge consequences are also implied by the known two-dimensional and simply connected cases. The genuinely additional content is the explicit integral Moore-space **homotopy decomposition** for both order-five and order-fifteen quotients, which does not follow solely from \(\pi_1\) and integral homology of a finite two-complex. A separate prior result `1f1057cf-987c-4d7e-8661-1fe16a2950d9` treats the larger Singer–Galois normalizer, not these cyclic orbit spaces. Full text of Ku (1998) establishes only the unquotiented building; Giuzzi–Sonnino (2007) establishes Singer line spread and orbit structures, not the quotient attaching-map presentations. Targeted published-result searches found no same-object complete cyclic quotient homotopy decompositions. Equivalent unindexed work remains a priority risk.

## Value — PASS

Singer cycles and their invariant line spreads are natural finite-geometric objects; the spherical building is the canonical subspace-incidence complex whose homology carries the Steinberg representation. Its complete family of four ordinary orbit homotopy types is a motivated topological invariant. The order-three subgroup has a five-point fixed locus, but the entire order-fifteen quotient retains five-primary torsion through a Moore-space summand. The distinction between finite-group isotropy and topological fundamental-group torsion is not recoverable from mere subspace orbit counts or rational homology, and the calculation supplies explicit attaching data and an independently replayable finite proof. The scope is limited to \(\operatorname{PG}(3,2)\) and its Singer cyclic subgroups; no extrapolation to larger buildings is asserted.
