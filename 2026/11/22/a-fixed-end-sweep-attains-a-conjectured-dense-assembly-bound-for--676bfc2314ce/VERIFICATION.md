# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

After restricting to the end-time prefix, upper triangularity preserves invertibility of the change of basis. In the rows born after s, each surviving nonzero reduced column still has its distinct last nonzero row, so any zero linear combination has zero coefficients on all surviving columns. This gives exactly the eligible-column kernel basis, including zero reduced columns at s=0 and tied births. For fixed t the eligible sets are nested; orthogonal remainders are nonzero by independence. The projection onto the chain space is the sum of ww^T/(w^T w), so the boundary on both sides gives the stated rank-one update including the correct induced metric and zero padding. As a direct exact check, start with two vertices a,b, then add c and the two edges ac,bc simultaneously. At the earlier start the kernel of the c-row [1,1] is generated by (1,-1), yielding the matrix (1/2)[[1,-1],[-1,1]]. Including c at the start gives the ordinary three-vertex path Laplacian; a subsequently born isolated vertex with no new edge appends a zero row and column. All k_t insertions cost O(m k_t^2+(nm+n^2)k_t) including dense updates. There are at most k_t dense event snapshots per row and O(n^2) initialization per row. The final k_t=m row absorbs O((n+m)m^2) reduction, and input/output handling on the specified event grid does not exceed the bound. The uploaded checker was inspected, not run: its event path compares sweep entries with rational nullspace projection, while its extra checks use the eligible basis directly at birth-index pairs rather than testing the retrieval of stored snapshots, and do not literally visit every integer time when a level is empty. These limits do not affect the proof.
