For normal-mode copulas of fixed nonzero amplitude, every finite density \(f\)-divergence from independence—including mutual information and total variation—is exactly independent of the integer mode frequencies, while Chatterjee's two directional population coefficients decay as the reciprocal squares of the corresponding response frequencies. Consequently smooth, uniformly positive copula densities can have a fixed positive information divergence even as both directional rank coefficients and the uniform copula-CDF discrepancy approach zero.
