# Scientific assessment of the corrected argument

**Correctness — PASS.** The bond classification and conditional mean are unchanged. Compatible overlaps in the mixed factorial moments need not be disjoint chains of parallel matching edges; an adjacent transposition can occur in a crossed motif. The corrected proof uses connected components of shared transpositions. Each adjacent-interval motif fixes both endpoints of a neighboring transposition up to finitely many choices; a marked adjacent transposition leaves only one free starting coordinate. The resulting placement exponents are strictly smaller than the probability exponents in every nontrivial component. The unrestricted law follows from a fixed-count concentration bound and conditional mixing. The finite checker establishes the identity and means only on the lengths it tests.

**Originality — PASS, best of knowledge.** The general deletion/bond identity and separate perfect-matching motif marginals are prior. An adjacent-swap mobility law concerns only the sum of the two adjacent-site counts. Neither a marginal law nor interchange symmetry proves the conditioned joint four-component limit and its resulting weighted deletion deficit. Coverage of earlier equivalent formulations is not exhaustive.

**Scientific value — PASS.** For a natural restricted permutation source, the result explains why the deletion deficit is compound Poisson rather than Poisson, and identifies an extra independent contribution from adjacent fixed points at the square-root scale. The limiting probability is that all standardized outputs are distinct, not that an individual output uniquely identifies its source.
