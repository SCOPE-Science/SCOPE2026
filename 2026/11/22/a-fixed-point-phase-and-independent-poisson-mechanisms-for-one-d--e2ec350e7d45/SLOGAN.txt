For a uniformly random involution with \(f/\sqrt n\to\alpha\), the deficit in distinct standardized one-deletion outputs converges to independent \(\operatorname{Pois}(1+\alpha^2)+2\operatorname{Pois}(1)\) contributions. Four independent rare matching/fixed-point mechanisms explain the law and yield an exact finite-size expectation; without conditioning on fixed points, the probability of all \(n\) deletion outputs being different tends to \(e^{-3}\).
