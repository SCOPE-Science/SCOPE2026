For the flat radial Bergman symbol \(q_a(z)=e^{-a/(1-|z|^2)}\), the hyponormality-control Jacobi matrix has \(N(\lambda)\sim[\log(1/\lambda)]^2/(4a)\) and belongs to every Schatten class. Although \(T_{q_a}\to I\) strongly as \(a\downarrow0\), the sharp allowable coefficient in \(T_z+cT_{q_a}\) collapses linearly with the Bessel constant \(a/0.7353491542\ldots\).
