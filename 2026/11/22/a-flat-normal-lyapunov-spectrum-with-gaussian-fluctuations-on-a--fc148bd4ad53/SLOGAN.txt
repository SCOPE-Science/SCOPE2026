On an exact Chebyshev-cubic boundary of a published predator–prey recurrence, an entire explicit mortality interval has zero normal Lyapunov exponent. At its natural midpoint the normal log-amplitude nevertheless has exact variance \(\pi^2 n/6-\pi^2(1-3^{-n})/8\) and converges after division by \(\sqrt n\) to a nondegenerate Gaussian; these are real-axis linearization results, not claims of positive-population invasion.
