At the one-dimensional random geometric graph's isolation window \(r_n=(\log n+x)/(2n)\), the counts of components of sizes \(1,2,\ldots,K\) converge jointly to **independent Poisson variables of identical mean** \(e^{-x}\), for each fixed \(K\). A radius-free exact uniform-composition law conditional on the total component count gives every finite mixed factorial moment and explains this flat rare-component spectrum.
