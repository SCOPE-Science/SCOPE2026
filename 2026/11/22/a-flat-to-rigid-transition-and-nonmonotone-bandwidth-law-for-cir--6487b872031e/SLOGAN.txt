The sharp real \(BV(\mathbb T)\)-to-\(L^2\) residual of forward boxcar averaging has squared norm \(a/6\) up to width \(1/2\) and \((-8a^3+12a^2-1)/(48a^2)\) afterward: a continuum of worst arc lengths collapses to semicircles. Its global worst bandwidth is \(a=4^{-1/3}\), so stronger averaging need not worsen the sharp residual error.
