# Scientific review: normalized Motzkin residues at zero-free primes

## Correctness

PASS. The proof defines the quotient only when the digit values are all nonzero; an all-index Lucas factorization then guarantees its existence. It derives a closed carry-ratio recursion, exact rational cylinder weights, and a complete affine pushforward for the Motzkin ratios. The thirteen-power histogram follows by an exhaustive, disjoint trailing-digit partition including the all-twelve exceptional index. Independent integer and complete finite-block checks corroborate the formulas; they are not asserted to prove the infinite statements.

## Originality

PASS within inspected evidence. The nearest established results already give the Motzkin/trinomial identity, Lucas congruences, Motzkin zero density and modulo-five pair marginals. Those are credited. The explicit conditional all-prime quotient law, missing ratio eleven at prime thirteen, and exact thirteen-power quotient histogram were not present in the inspected full primary material. The closest general automata papers could contain implicit equivalent consequences, which remains a priority risk; a search result alone cannot establish novelty.

## Value

PASS. Dividing one classical sequence by the other creates a natural, scale-free arithmetic comparison precisely at the primes where the normalization is meaningful. The theorem solves this invariant at every such prime by a finite formula and completely resolves the first explicit non-full case, including an all-index forbidden linear relation and a nonuniform thirteen-class frequency law. The thirteen-specific obstruction is more informative than a routine individual divisibility check.
