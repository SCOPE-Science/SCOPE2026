For a Bernoulli binary symbolic shift observed through a quantized zero-one sensor, earliest-index tie resolution makes the ordinary one-delay false-nearest-neighbour fraction converge to an exact four-atom random law. Its asymptotic variance is \(p(1-p)(1-2p)^2[p^2+(1-p)^2]\): a fair source self-averages, but every biased source retains a random sample-specific limit.
