For two perfectly pinned leaders on the unit clique corona \(K_n\circ K_1\), minimizing \(\operatorname{tr}(L_{FF}^{-2})\) selects a core and a *different* core's leaf only when \(n=3\), then switches sharply to two core leaders for every \(n\ge4\). In contrast, the first inverse-power objective always selects a core and a different leaf, giving disjoint optimum designs for every \(n\ge4\).
