For mutually minimizing edges under independent continuous weights, graph topology determines the exact pairwise covariance sign: adjacent candidate edges repel, disjoint candidates linked by a cross edge attract, and induced matchings are precisely the independent families. On every triangle-free \(d\)-regular host, the reciprocal-pair count has a universal variance baseline plus a strictly positive, explicit multiple of its four-cycle count; consequently its variance recovers the four-cycle count exactly.
