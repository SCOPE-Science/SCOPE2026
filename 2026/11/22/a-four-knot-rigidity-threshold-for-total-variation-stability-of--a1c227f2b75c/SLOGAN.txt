For convex weighted least-squares regression on arbitrary ordered knots, unweighted first-difference variation is two-input nonexpansive at two sites, at three sites exactly for a single endpoint-mass balance, and at no grid with four or more sites. On four uniform equally weighted sites the sharp gain is \(6/5\), while fully active affine faces give a constructive gain tending to \(3/2\) on longer uniform grids.
