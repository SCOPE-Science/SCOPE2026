For two perfectly pinned leaders on \(K_{n,n}\) with one missing edge, the exact second-order coherence optimum switches at \(n=4\): at \(n=3\) pin the missing-edge endpoints; thereafter pin two healthy vertices in the same bipartition class. This is two vertices earlier than the switch for first-order coherence on the same defective graph, yielding different optimal leader placements at \(n=4,5\).
