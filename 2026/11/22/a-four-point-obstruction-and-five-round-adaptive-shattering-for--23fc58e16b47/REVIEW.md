# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The precise range space consists of the 78 six-point symmetric differences of distinct projective lines in the thirteen-point ternary projective plane. A coordinate-derived incidence model proves the exact line/block normalizations. Enumeration of all 5616 projective collineations partitions four-sets into orbit sizes 13, 468 and 234 and five-sets into 117, 702 and 468. Complete explicit trace witnesses settle the two positive four-set representatives; explicit missing traces settle every five-set orbit. A full thirty-two-leaf tree verifies adaptive depth five. The analytic upper bound follows from 36 concepts containing one point and 15 containing each pair. Two separate, self-contained programs verify the polynomial and dimensions. Risk: finite executable verification is not a general theorem for other projective-plane orders.

**Originality: PASS within investigated evidence.** The closest source on symmetric differences of projective lines studies minimum weights of multi-line sums, not Boolean trace/shattering or adaptive queries [2]. The 2020 VC paper is about graph neighborhoods on Johnson and Hamming graphs [1]; the projective incidence-code literature studies dual-code minimum weight [3]. Indexed published results on unions of two projective lines and projective-plane stopping sets use different hypothesis families and do not imply this one. Exact-claim, alias, coding, and online-shattering searches found no matching theorem. Risk: this is a priority assessment within searched material; one indexed recent union-of-lines source was available only as an abstract/summary, and unindexed historical priority remains possible.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** The first odd-characteristic projective plane's natural two-line parity family yields a complete shattering complex, a particularly simple geometric obstruction (only whole projective lines fail among four-sets), two explicit facet orbits, and a strict \(4\)-versus-\(5\) static/adaptive gap. This refines both minimum-weight coding information and a scalar VC dimension, giving a reproducible baseline for higher-order projective-plane and learning-theory investigations without claiming a general formula.

**Relationship and limitations.** The result is a fixed finite geometry, with exact finite certificates and a separate mathematical upper-bound argument; it has no generalization to arbitrary field order as yet. The count 702 for four-point shattered supports must not be confused with the 702 five-point configurations reported for an earlier *two-line union* class. Full published text of that recent source was not obtainable in the present comparison; its known hypothesis family differs materially. No independent audit is claimed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
