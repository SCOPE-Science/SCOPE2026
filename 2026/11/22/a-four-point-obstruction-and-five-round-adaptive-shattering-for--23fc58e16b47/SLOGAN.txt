For symmetric differences of two distinct lines of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,3)\), the shattered point supports are exactly all supports of size at most three and the 702 four-point non-lines, so \(\operatorname{VCdim}=4\). A complete 32-leaf adaptive query tree nevertheless exists, and pair-incidence counting excludes depth six, giving \(\operatorname{Ldim}=5\).
