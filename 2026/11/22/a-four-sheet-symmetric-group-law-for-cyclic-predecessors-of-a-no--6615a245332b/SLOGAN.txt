A nonlinear open cellular automaton with exactly four global preimages per infinite target has a radically different cyclic inverse law: for uniform \(n\)-periodic outputs, the numbers of same-period predecessors converge exponentially to the fixed-point distribution of a uniform permutation in \(S_4\). The four limiting probabilities for \(0,1,2,4\) predecessors are \(3/8,1/3,1/4,1/24\), and three predecessors are impossible at every ring length.
