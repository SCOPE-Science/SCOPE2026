For the lax two-variable team formula \((y\perp z)\lor\operatorname{dep}(y,z)\), every falsifying team contains either two disjoint two-edge row-stars or a six-edge alternating cycle, and these are exactly its inclusion-minimal falsifiers. On labelled \(p\)-by-\(q\) alphabets their size enumerator is \(\binom p2\binom q2\binom{q-2}2t^4+6\binom p3\binom q3t^6\), with a sharp six-assignment refutation bound but without downward closure of satisfaction.
