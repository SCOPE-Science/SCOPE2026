For three-state, two-contact Greenberg–Hastings excitation on every sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\), all initial states extinguish within four updates (three on the triangle), and the complete first-extinction histogram is given by two explicit cubic/quintic root-sum sequences. An initial five-site pulse is necessary and sufficient for the last update, making the probability of maximal extinction time tend exponentially to one as \(n\) grows.
