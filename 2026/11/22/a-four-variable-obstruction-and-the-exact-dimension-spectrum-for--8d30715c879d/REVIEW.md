Both possible cross-quadratic cases produce explicit nested supports with exact sizes, and the zero-cross term yields an empty row. The four-dimensional argument is an all-polynomial proof. The published three-variable, symplectic-extension, and direct-sum lemmas cover the positive cases; the executable direct checks independently corroborate small examples.

This is not a proof of historical priority; unlocated classification arguments remain a residual risk. Previously proved positive constructions are attributed, not called novel.

This is a natural exact feasibility boundary rather than an arbitrary finite slice or a numerical tweak.
