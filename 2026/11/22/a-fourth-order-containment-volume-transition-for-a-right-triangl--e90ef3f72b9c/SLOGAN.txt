The four-dimensional volume of all orientation-preserving similar placements of a right isosceles triangle into any rectangle is an exact three-branch quartic spline, with a genuine fourth-derivative jump when the rectangle's aspect ratio reaches \(2\). Its containment polytope has exactly \(12,20,16,18\) vertices at aspect ratios \(1\), strictly between \(1\) and \(2\), exactly \(2\), and above \(2\), respectively; the square's vertex collapse is invisible to the volume polynomial.
