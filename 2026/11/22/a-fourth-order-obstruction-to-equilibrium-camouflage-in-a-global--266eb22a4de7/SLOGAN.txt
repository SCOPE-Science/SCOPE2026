At every positive rate of simultaneous fresh-iid resetting, a one-dimensional zero-temperature voter–Ising chain has exactly the one-, two-, and three-spin marginals of a positive-temperature nearest-neighbor Ising Gibbs measure, on every ring of size at least five and on the infinite line. A strictly positive fourth-order stationary identity proves that the two laws are nevertheless distinct, so order four is the minimal static spin-correlation witness.
