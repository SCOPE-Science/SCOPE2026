Two harmonically confined Brownian coordinates subject to the same finite-strength Poisson contraction have zero positional cross-covariance, yet their squared positions exhibit a strictly positive, time-directional covariance whose forward/reverse ratio is exactly \(e^{2(a_i-a_j)t}\). Stationary second and mixed fourth moments plus one positive-lag squared-position cross-correlation uniquely determine the hidden common-shock rate and contraction factor when the trap strengths are known.
