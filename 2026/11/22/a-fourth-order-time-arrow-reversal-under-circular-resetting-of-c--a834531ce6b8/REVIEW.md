# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. Exact generator moment equations determine second and mixed fourth stationary moments; an exact generator eigenfunction identity determines the two chronological squared-mode correlations at every nonnegative lag. A strictly decreasing explicit quadratic-in-rate bracket determines the full sign phase diagram for all claimed parameters. The proof includes the critical radius boundaries and the zero-correlation rate. The standalone checker provides exact rational arithmetic comparisons and independent renewal-age numerical quadrature; it is corroboration, not a substitute for proof.

## Originality

PASS, with publication-priority risk. The 2026 primary manuscript provides the full stationary characteristic function and second moments for circular resetting. Its characteristic function implicitly contains equal-time fourth moments, which are credited; it does not state the two-time, two-direction modal-energy regressions or the sharp two-interval radius classification for a reversible-sign fourth-order diagnostic. Related prior work on multiplicative common resets gives a different positive squared-mode coupling, and standard Brownian-gyrator spectral work concerns different observables. Search coverage does not prove global novelty, and the possibility of an unindexed equivalent remains.

## Value

PASS. The normal-mode positions have identically zero cross covariance, and with equal bath temperatures the mean angular momentum is zero, yet a specific two-time, fourth-order statistic detects asymmetric relaxation. The sign switch at a computable reset rate and its two disjoint geometric parameter windows distinguish geometric anticorrelation in circular reset marks from common-age positive coupling. Its exact ratio is independent of temperature, reset radius, and reset rate when defined. The claim does not infer physical entropy production from this diagnostic or reversibility at its zero.
