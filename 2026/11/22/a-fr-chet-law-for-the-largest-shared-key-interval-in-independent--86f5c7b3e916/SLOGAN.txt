For two independent randomized binary search trees on the same ordered keys, the largest proper consecutive-key interval that is a fringe subtree in both has scale \(n^{1/3}\), with limit \(\Pr\{M_n<x n^{1/3}\}\to\exp(-4/(3x^3))\). Exact local dependence bounds give a Poisson process of large shared fringe-interval sizes with intensity \(4u^{-4}\,du\).
