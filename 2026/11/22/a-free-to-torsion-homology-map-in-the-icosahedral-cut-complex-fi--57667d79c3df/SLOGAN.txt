In the ordinary icosahedral cut filtration, the six-cut complex has \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^6\) and \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z^{26}\), yet its inclusion into the five-cut complex maps free fourth homology *onto* the latter's \(\mathbb Z/2\) torsion. Exact integral chain cancellation and a mod-two cycle witness certify this free-to-torsion transition.
