When the stiffness of a harmonic trap is the square of an unreset Brownian motion, observing a particle on a time window of physical width \(1/T\) at age \(T\) yields a stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process with a frozen *random* relaxation rate. Its position-sign correlation is an explicit Gaussian-arcsine mixture and decays like \(0.538851623662\ldots/\sqrt{bu}\) on the rescaled time axis, despite exponential decay for every nonzero frozen rate.
