For the noisy anti-voter model on balanced complete multipartite graphs, an exact stationary two-time magnetization-contrast law reveals a sharp topological dichotomy. In the weak-refresh window \(a=c/n\), two parts retain order-one contrast fluctuations for order-\(n\) times, while any fixed three-or-more-part system has order-\(n^{-1}\) fluctuations and order-one relaxation; the integrated susceptibilities scale as \(n\) and \(n^{-1}\), respectively.
