For Pareto samples with shape \(0<\beta<1\), fitting the shape by \(\overline X/(\overline X-1)\) makes classical Kolmogorov–Smirnov and Cramér–von Mises statistics stay macroscopically separated from their refitted-bootstrap null laws. Their asymptotic false-rejection probability is one at every fixed nominal level; the exact separation limits are \((1-\beta)\beta^{\beta/(1-\beta)}\) and \(1/(2\beta+1)-2/(\beta+2)+1/3\).
