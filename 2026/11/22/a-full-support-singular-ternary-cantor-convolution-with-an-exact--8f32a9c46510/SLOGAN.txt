The mixed sum of a uniform ternary \(\{0,1\}\)-digit Cantor integer and twice an independent copy has full \(3\)-adic support but a singular-continuous distribution: the indicators of output digit \(2\) are independent Bernoulli variables of mean \(1/4\). Its exact modular collision energy satisfies \(E_{k+2}=6E_{k+1}-2E_k\), yielding correlation dimension \(\log_3(16/(3+\sqrt7))<1\); the associated bare recurrence was previously catalogued numerically.
