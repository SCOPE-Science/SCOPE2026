# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact Laplace transform follows by integrating the defining gamma-type density. The cubic discriminant and evaluations at three fixed arguments place the unique real pole in an explicit interval; Vieta then bounds the real part of the complex poles. These bounds give a positive, integrable residual Lévy density after removing a shape-two gamma factor. Its total mass is identified by the large-Laplace-variable limit, and the endpoint at negative unit transmutation is shown to have infinite activity by a nonzero small-jump kernel limit. Poisson splitting rigorously transfers the continuous factorization to an independent count factorization with all positive jump intensities. The included `verify.py` checks identities and exact example coefficients; no finite numerical check is presented as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority qualified)

Alrumayh and Khogeer (2023) introduced the Poisson-transmuted moment-exponential model but, in the inspected full article, did not establish an all-parameter gamma plus independently positive-jump compound-Poisson factorization, Lévy density, or finite-activity boundary. Hassan, Abdul-Moniem and Gad (2020) already include the relevant continuous transmuted moment-exponential density as a submodel of a broader lifetime family, but inspected original definitions, structural properties and conclusion do not supply this Lévy factor. Bhati, Kumawat and Gómez-Déniz (2015) studied infinite divisibility in a different **transmuted exponential** mixture; the underlying density and rational transform are not the same. Applicable general infinite-divisibility theory does not establish positivity of the specific residual kernel without the root inequalities proved here. Searches of the original objects, aliases and claim implications did not identify a dominating publication. Residual priority risk remains from unindexed papers or uninspected related generalizations; search absence does not prove priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a structural decomposition of an existing two-parameter count model, not a routine moment or parameter substitution. It determines a *positive and exactly normalized* latent jump law for an entire continuous parameter family, identifies the **sharp finite-activity boundary** at the extreme admissible negative transmutation, and yields an independent negative-binomial baseline plus burst-process representation for count aggregation. The positivity of the Lévy density is the nontrivial obstacle; mixed-Poisson status alone would not imply this explicit gamma factor. The exact rate and jump law give a natural constructive route to aggregation/simulation. No unproved claim of model-selection or inferential improvement is made.
