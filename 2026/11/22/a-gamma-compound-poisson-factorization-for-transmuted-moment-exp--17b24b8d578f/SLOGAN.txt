Every admissible transmuted moment-exponential mixing law splits into an independent Gamma law of shape two and a positive-jump infinitely divisible remainder; the remainder is compound Poisson with **exact rate** \(\log(4/(1+\alpha))\) for \(-1<\alpha\le1\), and has infinite activity at \(\alpha=-1\). Its Poisson-mixed count is therefore an explicit negative-binomial-plus-compound-Poisson sum, with strictly positive formulas for every residual jump intensity.
