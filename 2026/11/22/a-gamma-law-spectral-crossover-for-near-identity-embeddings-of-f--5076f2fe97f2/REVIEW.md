Monomial orthogonality gives the exact singular list, including correct decreasing ordering by monotone likelihood ratio. The log-derivative of each moment is an exact expectation, and the uniquely minimized convex boundary phase gives a uniform saddle estimate on compact positive parameter ranges. Its Gaussian relative width vanishes, with exponentially controlled tails. Those bounds rigorously imply the profile and provide the integrable majorant needed for the all-\(p>0\) Schatten-mass Riemann sum. The constant also agrees with direct differentiation of the leading saddle action. No finite experiment is used as proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The 2021 Zhang–Wang–Hu preprint studies Carleson embeddings/Toeplitz criteria on exponential Bergman spaces. The 2021 Bourass–El-Fallah–Marrhich–Naqos paper gives a related exact Hankel spectral law from flat moments, including a close logarithmic radial weight; thus the saddle technique and leading moment analysis are *not* claimed as new. Wang–Xia–Liu 2023 gives Schatten criteria for different Toeplitz/Hankel operators. published-finding corpus searches for the near-coalescing identity-embedding gamma profile and its singular-value scale did not return an equivalent statement. The new content is the simultaneous \(\varepsilon\)-dependent full spectral crossover and exact Schatten blow-up; prior specialized embedding literature may remain unindexed.

## Value — PASS
The identity embedding is the canonical comparison of two nearby exponential Bergman Hilbert norms. Its qualitative compactness hides a diverging effective spectral rank as the norms coalesce. The theorem resolves that natural invariant fully: a universal rescaled singular-value curve, the sharp dependence of approximation rank on flatness, and gamma-function coefficients for *every* Schatten quasi-norm. This is more than a fixed-weight recomputation and gives quantitative information for approximating functions between nearby analytic regularity spaces.

## Limits and primary-source comparisons
The paper of Bourass and collaborators (arXiv:2102.01743, Section 8) gives an exact flat-weight Hankel model, and is the strongest nearby methodology. Its conclusion is for a different operator under a fixed weight; it does not imply the coalescing-embedding profile or uniform-in-parameter spectral mass without new estimates. Zhang–Wang–Hu (arXiv:2107.02481, Sections 3–5) provides qualitative embedding criteria. Wang–Xia–Liu (2023) provides general Schatten criteria, not singular-value constants for this identity. Published specialized embedding results remain a residual priority risk; independent audit not performed.
