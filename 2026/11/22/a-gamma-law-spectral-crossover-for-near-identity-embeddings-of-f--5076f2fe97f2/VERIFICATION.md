The norm and singular-value formulas follow exactly from the radial moment integral in RESULT.md. The derivative identity \(\partial_u\log M_n(u)=-\mathbb E_{n,u}(1-r)^{-\beta}\) and the unique saddle equation provide a symbolic, reproducible check of the constants. The critical integral evaluates by \(y=p c_{a,\beta}t^\delta\) to \(\Gamma(1+1/\delta)/(p c_{a,\beta})^{1/\delta}\). The case \(a=2\), \(\beta=1\), \(p=2\) gives exactly \(2\) for the rescaled squared Hilbert–Schmidt mass. No independent validation or external certificate is asserted.
