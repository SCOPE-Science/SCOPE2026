# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact conditional Gaussian reduction, the stationary two-state occupation martingale central limit, and the joint two-sided Gaussian extreme-value limit reconstruct every critical normalization and coefficient. The common clock shift cancels algebraically in the front difference, and a law-of-total-variance identity establishes the two-term asymptotic, with a bounded fourth moment controlling Taylor remainders. The standalone finite-parameter checker passed 71 assertion groups; these tests are corroboration, not a proof of the asymptotic.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The motivating 2022 extreme-particle work treats random drift and proposes shared random diffusivity as a further continuum model. Its 2024 successor does not state the present global, rapidly switching diffusivity result. Nearby published results concern switching harmonic confinement, synchronized resetting, or one-particle diffusivity cumulants; a result on logistic temporal excursion imbalance has different observables. Existing normal extreme-value theory supplies the conditional Gumbel factors but not the population-size/switching-rate threshold or their shared Gaussian mode. The inaccessible full text of Uchida–Washizu–Miyoshi and potentially broad unindexed mixing theorems are priority risks. No claim of independent publication priority is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This solves a motivated many-tracer common-environment measurement problem and identifies an exact log-squared threshold with measurable covariance, as well as a robust anti-symmetric observable that cancels environmental fluctuations. This classification is substantially more informative than a finite-order normal approximation or a one-particle marginal.
