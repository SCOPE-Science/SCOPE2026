Across any fixed number \(r\ge2\) of independent labelled Cayley trees, the number of vertices whose degrees agree in every tree has an exact multinomial-coefficient distribution and a Gaussian limit with variance \(\rho_r(1-\rho_r)-r(\eta_r-\rho_r)^2\). The fixed-total constraint produces a strictly negative order-\(1/n\) covariance between agreement indicators at distinct labels.
