For a uniformly sampled complete period, the logarithmic deficit between \(k!\) and the product-to-LCM quotient of \(k+1\) consecutive integers is asymptotically Gaussian as \(k\to\infty\). Its mean is \(\gamma k+o(k)\) and its variance is \((2-\log(2\pi))k\log k+o(k\log k)\); the normal limit uses exact finite-\(k\) centering and scaling.
