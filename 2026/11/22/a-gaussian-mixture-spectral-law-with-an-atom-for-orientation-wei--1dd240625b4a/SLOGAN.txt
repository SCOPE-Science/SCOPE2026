For independently random orientation words of length \(n\), the orientation-weighted Markov spectral radius of Oliveira's canonical affine interval maps has a non-Gaussian \(\sqrt n\)-scaled limit with an atom of mass \(\operatorname{erf}(1)/4\) at \(1/\sqrt2\). In contrast, the unsigned Markov Perron root stays between \(n-1\) and \(n\), exposing a sharp signed-versus-unsigned growth separation.
