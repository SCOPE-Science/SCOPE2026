For the published Pareto min-characteristic-function goodness-of-fit statistic with likelihood-fitted scale and shape, an increasing exponential weight rate has a sharp transition at \(\gamma_n\asymp n\): the normalized statistic has a Gaussian quadratic limit below the boundary, a compensated-Poisson quadratic limit at the boundary, and deterministic logarithmic collapse above it. The critical law has mean \(2\) and variance exceeding the Gaussian limit's variance by exactly \(8c(1-\log2)/3\) when \(\gamma_n/n\to c>0\).
