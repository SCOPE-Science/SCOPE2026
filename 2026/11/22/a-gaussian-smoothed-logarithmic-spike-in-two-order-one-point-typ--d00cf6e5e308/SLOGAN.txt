Over a uniformly random finite named two-order structure, the *expected* graded one-point type spectrum has an exact beta-logarithmic formula and an order-\(n\log n\) central coefficient spike. In the window \(k=\lfloor n/2+s\sqrt n\rfloor\), its sharp second-order profile is \(-\mathbb E\log|Z+2s|\), yielding the explicit central constant \((\gamma+\log2)/2\); the known macroscopic logarithmic density is credited as prior work.
