At degree \(k=\lfloor n/2+s\sqrt n\rfloor\), the expected one-point type count divided by \(n+1\) is \(\frac12\log n-\mathbb E\log|Z+2s|+o(1)\), with \(Z\) standard Gaussian. The formula resolves the central logarithmic spike of the known permutation-graph degree law and gives the exact central constant \((\gamma+\log2)/2\).
