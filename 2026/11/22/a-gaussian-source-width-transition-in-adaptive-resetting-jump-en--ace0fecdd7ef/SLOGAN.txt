For exact Gaussian stationary compression by adaptive resetting, the jump-channel entropy cost changes sharply at reset-source variance equal to half the target variance: below it, decay in reset flux is anomalously slow; at equality a \((\log R)^{5/2}/R\) law appears; and above it the decay is \(R^{-1}\). An explicit uniform critical-width crossover resolves the transition, while the stationary diffusion-channel entropy cost stays positive.
