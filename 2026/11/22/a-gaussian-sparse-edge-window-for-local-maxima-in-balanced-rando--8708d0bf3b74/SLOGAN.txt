The number of peaks of a uniform random order on the balanced bipartite Erdős--Rényi graph has a standard Gaussian limit after centering and multiplication by \(\sqrt p\), whenever \(p\to0\) and \(np^2/\log^2(1/p)\to\infty\). The key is an exact bounded-variance cancellation in the random side-interleaving environment, leaving conditional Bernoulli noise with scale \(p^{-1/2}\).
