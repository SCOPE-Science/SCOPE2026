The vertex of the normal degree-nine model \(W^3Q^3=(X^3-Y^3)(Y^3-Z^3)(Z^3-X^3)\) has a minimal resolution consisting of a **single genus-\(16\), self-intersection-\(-6\)** exceptional curve; its normal bundle is pulled back from \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-2)\) along a totally ramified cyclic triple cover. Its exact local Milnor number is \(293\), whereas the complete singular nonic has Euler characteristic \(70\).
