For two species with concave Monod growth responses, common mortality, and an equal-duration two-level nutrient environment of fixed mean \(m\), changing nutrient variance can reverse their relative long-run growth ranking twice if and only if the mean exceeds the geometric mean of their half-saturation constants and the maximal-growth-rate ratio lies in an explicit sharp interval. An exact rational example exhibits the species-2, species-1, species-2 sequence as nutrient variability grows, without implying competitive exclusion in a feedback-coupled ecosystem.
