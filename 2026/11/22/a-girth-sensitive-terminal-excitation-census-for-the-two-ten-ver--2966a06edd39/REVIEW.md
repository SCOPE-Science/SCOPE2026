# Review: A girth-sensitive terminal-excitation census

## Correctness — PASS

Both ten-vertex graphs and the two-contact map are explicitly defined. Complete enumeration across all 59,049 initial colorings per graph gives sharp four-step extinction, all five first-hit counts, and the last-wave excitation-support polynomial. Two different full update implementations, plus a third verifier using independently defined prism and Kneser graph geometries, produce identical counts. The universal girth/three-initial-excitations lemma follows from the forced four-cycle formed by two first-generation activations with a common initial excited neighbor. Checks are exact and finite, not extrapolated.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The most relevant published complete generalised-Petersen excitation result treats **one-contact** activation and exhibits seven- and eight-cycles; a second complete Petersen census also uses one contact. Full accessible text of Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff (2016) explicitly defines one-contact GHM before stating its arbitrary-graph theorem. Neither hypothesis can be silently replaced by two contacts, and the one-contact conclusions neither state nor imply the two-contact first-rest or last-wave coefficients. Published threshold-two results for complete bipartite, tripartite, wheel and split graphs do not include this graph pair. The original 1978 and 1993 full texts were not obtained; possible older or unindexed equivalent computations remain a historical-priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The two cubic ten-vertex generalized Petersen graphs form a natural complete and highly symmetric comparison. The exact four-step quenching contrasts meaningfully with their established sustained one-contact excitation, while the factor-of-more-than-two difference in terminal-wave populations and the three-versus-four minimal initially excited sites disclose the role of a local four-cycle. The latter has a self-contained general structural explanation; this is an exact finite dynamical classification, not a claim about all cubic networks.

## Limitations

The claim is finite and only for the named pair. The critical values are proved by complete finite computations and explicit update logic, not closed formulas for a variable graph order. The evidence does not eliminate all unpublished historical priority risk, and external independent mathematical validation has not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
