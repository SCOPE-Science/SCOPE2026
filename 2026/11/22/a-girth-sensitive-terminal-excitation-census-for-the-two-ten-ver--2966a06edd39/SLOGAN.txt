For synchronous three-state two-contact excitation, the pentagonal prism and the Petersen graph both extinguish every initial configuration in at most four updates, but their exact terminal-wave populations are 3,380 versus 1,520 out of 59,049. A four-cycle allows a second excitation generation from just three initially excited vertices on the prism, while the Petersen graph's girth-five structure forces at least four.
