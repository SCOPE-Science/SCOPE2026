The Borel representation fourfolds of the parafree groups \(\langle x,y,z\mid x^2y^3=z^{2m+1}\rangle\) admit explicit global projective small crepant resolutions, constructed as rank-two vector bundles over a torus blown up at \(16m\) points. Their exceptional locus consists of \(16m\) disjoint copies of \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb A^1\), and their smooth resolutions have Euler characteristic \(16m\) and Picard rank \(16m\).
