# Algebraic verification

Run `python VERIFY.py` after installing the dependency listed in `requirements.txt`. The script executes exact symbolic assertions and prints `PASS` only after all checks complete. Actual run: `PASS: trace identities, boundary parametrization, Fricke relation, smoothness.`

The assertions check determinant-one companion matrices, their product trace, Cayley--Hamilton square roots and trace-recovery identities; they verify the trace-zero conic parameterization and its three trace formulas by remainder modulo the conic relation; they substitute the claimed trace expressions into the rank-three Fricke relation and check the resulting factorization; and they verify that the Jacobian ideal of the global equation is the unit ideal.

The code does **not** replace the mathematical proof that all boundary representations commute, the invariant ring is generated by the seven standard trace functions, or a dense subset of the equation is realized by representations. Those steps are proved in `RESULT.md`. The assertions do not address nilpotents in the unreduced representation scheme, real forms, or characteristic-two phenomena.
