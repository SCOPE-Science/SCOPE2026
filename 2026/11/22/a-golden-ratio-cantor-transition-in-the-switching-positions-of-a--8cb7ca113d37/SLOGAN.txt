In a harmonically trapped swimmer that flips direction after equally probable runs of length \(\tau\) or \(2\tau\), switching-position support changes sharply from a Cantor set to an interval at \(\kappa\tau=\log\varphi\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). Below the overlap threshold its support and stationary switching measure have distinct exact Hausdorff dimensions, but the same particle's physical-time stationary position distribution always has a bounded density.
