A marked-site rate bias of order \(N\) creates a nonzero probability of triangular jamming in irreversible monomer adsorption on \(KG(N,2)\), even though the unbiased triangular-jam probability vanishes as \(1/(N-2)\). The limiting trap probability is maximized at the golden-ratio rate \(w/N=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), producing macroscopic terminal-coverage fluctuations.
