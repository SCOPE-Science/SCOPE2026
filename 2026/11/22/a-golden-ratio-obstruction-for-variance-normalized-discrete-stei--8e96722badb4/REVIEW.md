# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For all fixed \(0<q<1\), each law is full-support log-concave, and the constant-birth death rates are nondecreasing. Exact evaluation of the indicator Stein solution proves the two-sided uniform factor limit \(w\Gamma\to1/(1-q)\). The reversed geometric law and a vanishing geometric-tail perturbation establish \(\operatorname{Var}(X_N)\to q/(1-q)^2\); optimization yields \(\varphi\). The finite checker agrees with its independent formulas. The proof does not infer the infinite statement from samples.

Available related results on first differences address a different Stein factor. Full-text comparison of DeSalvo's main theorem, open problems and historical remarks found no equivalent golden-ratio constraint. The Eichelsbacher paper was accessible in abstract only; hidden coverage remains a material historical risk.

## Value — PASS
The bound \(\varphi\) imposes an explicit numerical restriction on a published open uniform-bound conjecture and identifies its natural edge mechanism: an almost-hard upper wall whose residual geometric fluctuation has variance one. The shift from the observed selected-model scan value about 1.14 to a necessary 1.618 is substantial, while the actual conjectured universal bound remains unresolved. This is a motivated sharp boundary within a natural family rather than a general new Stein identity.

## Limitations
The result is a necessary lower bound, not the global exact constant. No claim is made about strictly log-concave interior sequences or probability laws with arbitrary tails. Priority risks include non-indexed published comparisons and the uninspected full text of Eichelsbacher's preprint.
