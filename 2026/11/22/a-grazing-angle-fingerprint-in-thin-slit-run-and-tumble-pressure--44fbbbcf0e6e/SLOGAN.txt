For a Poisson-tumbling swimmer confined between sticky parallel walls, the mean force per swimmer has universal normalized slope \(-1/4\) at zero slit width, but its next correction exactly fingerprints the probability of grazing headings: fractional powers, a logarithmic quadratic term, or a regular quadratic term. In particular, the logarithmic coefficient equals one quarter of the normal-heading density at zero and yields explicit two- and three-dimensional pressure laws.
