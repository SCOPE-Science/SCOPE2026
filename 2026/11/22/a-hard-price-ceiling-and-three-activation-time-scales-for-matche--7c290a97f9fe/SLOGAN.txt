In a balanced complete bipartite diffusion network with an expensive perfect matching, the exactly optimal finite-time conductance design either activates the matching after one sharp delay or never activates it at all, with the hard ceiling \(r_c(m)=2(m-1)/(m-2)\). Below the ceiling the activation time grows quadratically with network size for fixed \(1<r<2\), but acquires an extra logarithm at \(r=2\); cheaper-than-unit-price matchings instead cause a linear-time activation of ordinary edges.
