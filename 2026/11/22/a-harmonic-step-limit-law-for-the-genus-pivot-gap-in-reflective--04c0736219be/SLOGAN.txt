For a uniformly random reflective numerical semigroup of genus \(g\), the length of the maximal gap run containing \(g\), divided by \(g\), converges uniformly in CDF at rate \(O(g^{-1/2})\) to a continuous law with harmonic-step density \(H_{\lfloor1/x-1\rfloor}\) on \((0,1/2)\). Every finite-genus run-length frequency has an exact divisor formula, and the limiting mean is \(1-\pi^2/12\).
