For every finite simple graph of girth at least six on \(n\ge10\) vertices, the ordinary five-cut complex is a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}{4}-c_5(G)+h_6(G)\) spheres of dimension \(n-6\). Each induced hexagon contributes exactly one correction, even when several hexagons overlap; an explicit sequence of simplicial collapses on the Alexander dual proves this integral homotopy classification.
