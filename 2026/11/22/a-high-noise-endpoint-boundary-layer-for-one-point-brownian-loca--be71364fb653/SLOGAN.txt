One noisy observation of Brownian motion determines a rigorously computable Bayes risk for estimating its accumulated zero local time. At high noise variance \(\eta\), the uniquely best observation time lies strictly before the endpoint and satisfies \(t_\eta=1-1/(3\eta)+11/(36\eta^2)+O(\eta^{-3})\), while the explained variance is \(1/(9\pi\eta^2)+O(\eta^{-3})\).
