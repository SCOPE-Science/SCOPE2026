When the one-dimensional nearest-neighbor graph of \(n\) uniform points is connected—a rare event of probability \(2^{n-2}/(n-1)!\)—its unique mutual-neighbor root has exactly binomial spatial rank. The root's rank and physical midpoint have a nondegenerate joint Gaussian limit with correlation \(1/\sqrt2\), including an exact finite-sample covariance and a midpoint variance asymptotic to \(1/(2n)\).
