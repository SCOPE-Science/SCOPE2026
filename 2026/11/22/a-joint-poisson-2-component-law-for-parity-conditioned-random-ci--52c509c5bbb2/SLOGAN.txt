Conditioning a uniform random circle graph on even crossing degrees selects the classical parity-reversing permutation model, but its entire component-count/largest-component pair now follows a joint Poisson(2) law with total-variation error \(O(n^{-1})\). With probability \(1-O(n^{-1})\), all components except one are isolated adjacent-endpoint chords; prior exact connected-permutation enumeration is credited.
