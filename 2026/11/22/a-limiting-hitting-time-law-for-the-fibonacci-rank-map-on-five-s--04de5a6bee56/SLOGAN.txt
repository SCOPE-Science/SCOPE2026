The Fibonacci rank-of-appearance map has an exact first-fixed-point hitting time on every integer \(2^a3^b5^c\), depending only on \(a\) and \(b\). Sampled uniformly among five-smooth integers up to \(X\), that time divided by \(\log X\) converges at rate \(O(1/\log X)\) to an explicit piecewise-cubic law with mean coefficient \(0.3072892856723686\ldots\).
