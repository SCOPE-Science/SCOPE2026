For ternary words containing exactly \(2m\) copies of \(A\), \(m\) copies of \(B\), and \(m\) copies of \(C\), ordinary position--color shattering has dimension \(2m\), while adaptive shattering has dimension at least \(3m\) for every \(m\ge1\). An eight-leaf depth-three tree on the twelve \(AABC\) words yields the linear separation by independent four-position blocks.
