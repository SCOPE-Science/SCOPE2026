For every finite tree, a bipartition class is distance-equalizing exactly when each degree-two vertex outside it has a leaf neighbor; this determines precisely when the known bipartition lower bound is attained. When every degree-two vertex has a leaf neighbor—including all trees without degree-two vertices—the entire equalizer polynomial is \(Q_T(z)=z^{|A|}(1+z)^{|B|}+z^{|B|}(1+z)^{|A|}-z^{|V(T)|}\).
