A geometrically regular weighted shift just beyond the completely hyperexpansive ray satisfies every difference test through an exactly computable finite order. If the distance to that ray is \(\varepsilon\), the last passing order has the sharp growth \(\big(\log(1/\varepsilon)+[\log(1/a)/\log p]\log\log(1/\varepsilon)\big)/\log(1/(1-p^{-1}))+O(1)\), revealing a logarithmic boundary layer with a necessary log-log correction.
