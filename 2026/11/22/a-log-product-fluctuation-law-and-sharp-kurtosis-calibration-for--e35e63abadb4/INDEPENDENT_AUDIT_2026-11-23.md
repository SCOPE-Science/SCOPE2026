The same Gamma shape follows from the transformed generator on y=|x|^m/m^2, equal away from the origin to y*d^2/dy^2+(1-mu)*d/dy. Independence of the Poisson backward age E/r and the displacement law of a fresh excursion conditional on that age gives the stated signed mixture. Its Mellin moments give A_mu and the displayed kurtosis, with Gamma arguments positive for all claimed moment orders. At mu=1/2 the position is Laplace, yielding K=6, and its continuous K at mu=0 is 1. Independent polynomial algebra confirms both identities for h. Equivalently, to prove strict monotonicity without polynomial factoring, pad the numerator slope vector to (4,3,0,-1); it strictly majorizes (2,2,1,1). The log-kurtosis derivative sums g(c)=c*psi(1+c*mu) over the two slope lists with opposite signs. As a function of c, g''(c)=2*mu*sum_{j>=1} j/(j+c*mu)^3>0 on the relevant interval, from the trigamma series. Thus the derivative is positive, giving the full interval 1<K<=6 and the unique two-moment inversion when the diffusion prefactor is known. Independently, expanding log Gamma to degree two in the kurtosis ratio gives (zeta(2)/2)*(16+9+1-8-2)=4*pi^2/3; exponentiating keeps the coefficient. The claims are for a stationary one-time law, with arbitrary rate scaling only after standardization; no assertion of identifiability with an unknown diffusion prefactor or statistical stability follows.
