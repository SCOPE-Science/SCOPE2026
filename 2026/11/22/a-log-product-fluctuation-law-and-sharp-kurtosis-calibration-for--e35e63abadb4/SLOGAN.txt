The stationary position of a Poisson-reset sluggish Itô walker becomes an equally weighted pair of atoms as sluggishness grows, but its vanishing radial width has the universal non-Gaussian law \(\log(E_1E_2)+2\gamma\). The exactly monotone stationary kurtosis falls from \(6\) to \(1\) and, together with the variance, uniquely recovers both the sluggishness exponent and reset rate within the model.
