For two independent surface-uniform points on a finite proper right circular cone's lateral surface, the short ambient Euclidean chord law has a strictly positive \(r^4\log(L/r)\) term with exact coefficient \((1-q^2)/(16q^3L^4)\), after its universal area and rim terms. This logarithmic apex signature also determines the opening from its coefficient once lateral area is known, unlike the polynomial short-distance laws for flat or faceted geometries.
