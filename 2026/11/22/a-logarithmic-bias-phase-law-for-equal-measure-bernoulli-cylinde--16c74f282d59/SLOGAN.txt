For every integer \(k\ge3\), the equal-measure Bernoulli cylinder holes \(0^k11 0^k\) and \(10^{2k}1\) reverse their escape-rate ordering at exactly one symbol bias. The unique algebraic threshold has the two-term law \(p_k=(\log2)/k-(\log2)(1+2\log2)/(8k^2)+O(k^{-3})\), extending a previously published isolated length-six crossover into a complete unbounded family.
