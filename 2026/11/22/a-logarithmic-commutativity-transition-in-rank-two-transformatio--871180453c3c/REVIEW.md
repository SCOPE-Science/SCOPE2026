# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is confined to rank-two full-transformation variants. The map \(f\mapsto q^{-1}\circ f\circ p^{-1}\) proves canonical-sandwich invariance for \(a'=p\circ a\circ q\). Fixing the four values on the image points reduces commutation at all remaining points to independent tests with count \(K(u,v,s,t)\). The proof gives all 18 admissible restricted-growth/equality types explicitly and sums their falling-factorial weights into exactly seven terms. The three asymptotic regimes follow by taking ratios to the main \(n^{2n-3}\) term and using \((1-m/n)^n\sim e^{-m}\) in the logarithmic window. Boundary cases \(n=2\), \(k=1\), \(\ell=1\) are included without division by vanishing coefficients. Full pair enumeration for \(n\leq4\) and independent boundary-type reconstruction for 66 profiles passed. Unrestricted \(n\) is established by the finite classification, not by experiments.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The exact invariant is ordered commutativity under sandwich multiplication for **all** selfmaps, not ordinary function composition and not the number of commuting idempotents. An earlier rank-two variant idempotent-commuting-graph result treats only the idempotent subsemigroup and has no implication giving the total count over all maps. Tsyaputa's Theorem 2 gives kernel-type isomorphism classification; Dolinka–East give Green-relational and generator structure; Cameron–East–FitzGerald–Mitchell–Pebody–Quinn-Gregson give faithful degree; the published rank-one fingerprint gives a mixed product count with a permutation outer factor. None of the inspected statements implies the seven-base commuting count without additional boundary equations, and none states the logarithmic crossover. Searches for direct equivalents and stronger commuting enumerators returned no decisive prior theorem. Residual risk is an unindexed specialized enumeration or an implicit older combinatorial formula. Failure to locate such a source is not a proof of historical novelty.

## Value — PASS
The count is an exact invariant of every rank-two variant across all finite degrees and kernel partitions, rather than a chosen small numerical example. The seven-base structure exhibits two probabilistic scales, \(n^{-1}\) and \(n^{-3}\), together with a nontrivial sharp window at smaller-kernel size \(\log n\). This distinguishes semigroups having the same sandwich rank, where previous structural treatments emphasize ranks and kernel types. The complete census and crossover provide motivated information about noncommutativity as kernel balance varies.

## Limits and closest literature
The result does not address sandwich rank three or more, partial maps, or individual centralizer-size distributions. Tsyaputa (arXiv:math/0508386), Dolinka–East (arXiv:1410.5253), Cameron et al. (arXiv:2110.09701), and the published-finding corpus rank-one fingerprint are the closest inspected material. The last source shares the same ambient variants but studies a different multiplication statistic. Historical uniqueness remains subject to independent literature verification; exhaustive finite testing is not an independent proof.
