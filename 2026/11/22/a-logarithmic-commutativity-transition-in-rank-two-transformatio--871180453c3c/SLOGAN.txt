For a rank-two sandwich variant of the full transformation semigroup, the exact number of commuting ordered pairs is a seven-base formula determined by its two kernel-block sizes. Its commuting probability crosses from order \(n^{-1}\) for a fixed small block to order \(n^{-3}\) for balanced blocks, with sharp logarithmic profile \((1+e^{-t})^2\) when the smaller block has size \(\log n+t+o(1)\).
