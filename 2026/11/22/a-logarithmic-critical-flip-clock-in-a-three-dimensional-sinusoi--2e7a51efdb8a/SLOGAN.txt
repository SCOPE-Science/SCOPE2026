At the neutral \(-1\) boundary of a three-dimensional sinusoidal recurrence, every sufficiently small generic orbit decays algebraically, but a two-dimensional exceptional manifold decays exponentially. The generic inverse-square amplitude has an explicit parameter-dependent logarithmic correction, \(U_n^{-2}=n/[3(1-b)]+(9b^2+7b+4)\log(n)/[20(1-b)^2]+C+o(1)\).
