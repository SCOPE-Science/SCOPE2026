## correctness

PASS

The corrected record retains the valid sparse theorem and fixes a false statement about the omitted endpoint. Behrend et al.'s Propositions 2.7, 2.8 and 3.5 give the actual vertices, complete facets and simplicity. At a vertex the proper upper facets form a chain of positive-support prefixes. Two proper uppers meet exactly when nested; any such pair, or any proper upper and the global upper, is realized by extending a prefix. A lower facet meets a proper upper exactly off its support, meets another lower at zero, and meets the global upper exactly when there is a spare zero slot. Simplicity makes these and only these pairs ridges. The outward cosines are 0 for the first two right-angle classes, -1/√m for lower/global, and √(s/t) for nested uppers including t=m. Choosing the sets gives the stated counts. Independent reconstruction of the hull from all 21 defining points at (4,2) recovers nine facets and exactly 4 acute, 18 right and 4 obtuse ridges; additional finite checks use the stated facet and vertex descriptions. For k=n-1, successive downward ratios of binomial terms are at most k/(m-k+1)=o(1); thus the right and obtuse totals are asymptotic to m times and 2^k times binomial(m,k), respectively. Among obtuse ridges, the share with larger proper upper of size k and smaller proper upper is 1-o(1); its smaller size has the binomial(k,1/2) law with endpoints removed. Conditioning off an o(1) mass is harmless for the scaled weak limit, and the angle derivative at 1/2 is 1. This proves the 1/4 variance and, with the mass comparison, the two-point law including both endpoints. At n=m-1 the very same finite proof works: the global facet contains a vertex with exactly one zero in each prescribed lower coordinate, and there are still exactly the proper upper sizes 1,…,m-2. The original claim that the formulas do not extend there was false. At n≥m the global/lower intersections are indeed empty, because every point on the global facet is a convex combination of all-positive maximizing vertices. The corrected limited scope now agrees with these facts.
