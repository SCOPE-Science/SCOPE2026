Sparse partial permutohedra exhibit an exact, all-dimensional Euclidean ridge-angle spectrum with only \(m\) acute ridges. As their label bound crosses \(\log_2m\), a uniformly sampled dihedral angle changes sharply from predominantly right to predominantly \(3\pi/4\), with a nontrivial two-atom critical law and conditional Gaussian fluctuations of variance \(1/4\).
