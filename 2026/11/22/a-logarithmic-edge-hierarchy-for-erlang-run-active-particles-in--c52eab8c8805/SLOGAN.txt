For an athermal harmonically trapped particle whose orientation reverses after Erlang-shape \(m\) running times, the sharp stationary support-edge probability is a positive constant times \(\varepsilon^{\lambda/\kappa}[\log(1/\varepsilon)]^{m-1}\). At equal mean running time, every increase in the integer Erlang shape makes sufficiently rare wall-adjacent occupancy asymptotically negligible relative to the smaller shape.
