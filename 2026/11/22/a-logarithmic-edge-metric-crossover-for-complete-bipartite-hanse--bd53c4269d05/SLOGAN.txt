The full squared-Euclidean edge-length spectrum of the standard Hansen polytope of \(K_{p,q}\) is an explicit binomial generating polynomial. When \(2^p/q\) has a nonzero finite limit, a uniformly sampled edge has a nontrivial two-scale limit: positive proportions are unit edges and edges with squared length asymptotic to \((p+q)/2\), with a sharp logarithmic imbalance crossover.
