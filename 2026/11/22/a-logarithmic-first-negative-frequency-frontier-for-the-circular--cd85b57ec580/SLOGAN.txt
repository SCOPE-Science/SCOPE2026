For the intrinsic Gaussian on the circle, every odd Fourier coefficient is strictly positive at every bandwidth, while the even coefficients have a sharp large-bandwidth sign frontier at \(2\pi t+(\log t)/(2\pi)+\log(2\pi^{3/2})/\pi\), up to a vanishing error. Thus the first negative Fourier mode lies within two integer frequencies of this explicit logarithmic frontier.
