For every purely periodic positive directive of a standard Sturmian word, the bounded error in its logarithmic palindromic-prefix count has an explicit, generally nonuniform, absolutely continuous limiting law under harmonic sampling, and a nonconvergent but explicitly phase-resolved family under ordinary sampling; its density is a finite sum of interval indicators and its sharp oscillation endpoints are computable from the two eigenvalues of a single period matrix. Every sufficiently distant palindromic-prefix length also has an exact nearest-integer Perron-eigenvalue representation, refining the previously known first-order count without claiming its discovery.
