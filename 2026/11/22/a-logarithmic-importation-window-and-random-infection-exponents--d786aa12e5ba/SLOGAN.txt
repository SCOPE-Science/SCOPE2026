For fixed \(c>0\), critical complete-graph SIS infection with spontaneous importation at rate \(c/(n\log n)\) per susceptible vertex has limiting stationary extinction probability \(e^{-c/2}\). Otherwise the infected population typically grows as \(n^Y\), where the exponent has explicit continuous density \(c e^{c(y-1/2)}\) on \((0,1/2)\), instead of concentrating at one scale.
