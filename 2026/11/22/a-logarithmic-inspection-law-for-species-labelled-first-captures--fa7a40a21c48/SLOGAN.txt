In a species-labelled single-catch survey that retains only the terminal first-capture category, the unique Fisher D-optimal exposure for \(k\ge3\) competing Poisson encounter streams is \(t_k=x_k/r\), where \(x_k\) is strictly increasing and grows as \(\log k+2/\log k\). The choice is independent of species composition, while the information retained relative to timestamp recording falls as \((\log k)^2/k\) at the optimal horizon.
