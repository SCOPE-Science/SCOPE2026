In a graph of independently sampled random intervals, the number of pairs at graph distance three converges to an almost-surely finite Poisson-corner statistic with infinite expectation, even though its finite-sample mean equals \(\log n+\gamma+\log 2-1+o(1)\). A sharp finite-size formula and the Poisson representation show that rare endpoint configurations, not a growing typical obstruction count, drive the logarithmic mean.
