At the prey flip and strict predator-extinction threshold of a published fear-dependent predator–prey map, every sufficiently nearby nonexceptional trajectory obeys the exact clock \((u_{2n}/K-1)^{-2}=8n/3+(8/5)\log n+C+o(1)\), regardless of fear strength. An explicit smooth exceptional curve instead has exponential recovery, and its quadratic curvature changes strictly with fear.
