Independent area-uniform points on any circular ring torus have a universal outward-normal dot-product law with exact Legendre coefficients \(\mathbb E[P_{2k}(T)]=(\binom{2k}{k}/4^k)^4\). Its density diverges at both aligned and anti-aligned normals as \(\pi^{-2}\log(1/(1-|t|))\), giving the sharp rare-angle probability \(\varepsilon^2\pi^{-2}\log(1/\varepsilon)\); the one-normal Gauss image underlying this calculation is classical.
