A single snapshot of \(N\) commonly reset particles in a harmonic trap can distinguish reset ages separated by a fixed delay only up to a sharp horizon \((\log N)/(4\kappa)\). The crossover has an exact Gaussian error profile, and averaging calibrated two-age tests over stationary Poisson reset ages gives a sharp mean-error decay \(N^{-r/(4\kappa)}\) with an explicit gamma-function coefficient.
