A single less-susceptible vertex changes complete-graph SI flooding on the critical rate scale \(\rho_N\asymp1/\log N\): at \(\rho_N\log N\to c>0\), completion time divided by \(\log N\) converges to \(\max\{2,1+E/c\}\), where \(E\) is unit exponential. The last-infected probability of that vertex is exactly \(\Gamma(N-1)\Gamma(1+\rho_N)/\Gamma(N-1+\rho_N)\) and tends to \(e^{-c}\).
