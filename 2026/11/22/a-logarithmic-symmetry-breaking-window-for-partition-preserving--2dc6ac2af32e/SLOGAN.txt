For a uniformly random idempotent transformation preserving \(m\) labeled blocks of sizes \(n+\lfloor\beta_j\log n\rfloor\), almost every image lies in just one block, chosen in the limit with probability proportional to \(e^{m\beta_j}\). Thus only logarithmically many extra points per block suffice to break the uniform image-block symmetry, and larger gaps force deterministic selection.
