# Self-review

**Correctness (PASS).** The map \((i,j)\mapsto(i+j,i-j)\) is invertible over \(\mathbb F_3\). Direct substitution maps each rook neighborhood onto one of the nine four-cycle edge sets of \(K_{3,3}\). Graphic closure is generated by four-cycles, since every six-cycle has an opposite-vertex chord that divides it into two four-cycles. This establishes identical closure operators for every starting set, not merely identical minimum forcing counts. The 512-subset exhaustive checker independently confirms the implication and the count 81. The graph is simple and loopless by construction.

**Originality (PASS, historical priority provisional).** Hall–Pfeil–Taylor (arXiv:2611.15741, Prop. 4.6 and Fig. 12) establish realization by a *looped* nine-vertex graph, not by this rook graph. Their Prop. 2.10 does not directly apply because six-cycle circuits are not neighborhoods. Cooper–Fickes (arXiv:2303.17419, Prop. 3.16) treats the original bipartite graph, rather than its line graph. DeAlba (arXiv:1404.1618) treats minimum cardinalities, not this closure equivalence. Searches of published mathematical records and recoverable prior claims identified no exact loopless rook realization. Residual risk: unindexed or unpublished loopless constructions may exist.

**Value (PASS).** An explicit loopless, simple, vertex-transitive, four-regular realization is a substantive strengthening of the existence theorem's displayed *looped* construction for the natural boundary graphic matroid \(M(K_{3,3})\). The affine-cyclic proof describes why every six-cycle dependence is recovered from the four-neighbor forcing rules; this is the meaningful structural link and is not just a numerical minimum-set computation.

**Scope and limits.** This is one finite nine-vertex realization and not a uniqueness or general rectangular-rook classification. Self-review do not constitute independent certification.
