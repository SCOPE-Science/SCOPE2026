For Möbius ladders with \(2n\) vertices, Walsh convolution by perfect-matching edge-incidence vectors is invertible exactly when \(6\nmid n\), unlike the corresponding untwisted prisms. The sharp least surviving multiplier is \(1\) when \(3\nmid n\) and \(2\) when \(3\mid n\), and each singular order \(n=6k\) has at least \(2^{2n-1}8^k\) explicit zero characters.
