The last isolated vertices in a random-order uncovering of any fixed power of a long cycle die according to an explicit marked Poisson process: scaled times have intensity \(2d x^{2d-1}\), while positions, original birth fractions, and killing-neighbor directions become independent uniform marks. In particular, the \(j\)-th last isolation death has a transformed time with \(\operatorname{Gamma}(j,1)\) law, extending the known one-death Weibull limit to the joint history of the last finitely many deaths.
