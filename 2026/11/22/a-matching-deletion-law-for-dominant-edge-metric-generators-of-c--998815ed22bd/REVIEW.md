# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** The one-shore/mixed-pair classification gives the full and inclusion-minimal polynomials. The latter has three distinct nonzero powers at a rectangular one-shore saturation, not two; for example, at \((3,4,3)\) it is \(z^3+z^4+3z^5\). The statements for zero deletion, one deletion, unsaturated shores, and the crown follow by checking which exponents coincide and which coefficients vanish. Independent finite enumeration corroborates the classification but does not replace the proof.

**Originality.** The complete-bipartite minimum with no deletion is prior work. Ordinary edge-metric resolution, vertex-pair resolution on the same graphs, and vertex-edge domination do not enumerate edge-resolving vertex covers. Historical priority remains provisional.

**Value.** The classification counts all feasible and all inclusion-minimal subsets, and it distinguishes matching regimes where one, two or three sizes arise. The gap between smallest and largest inclusion-minimal sizes can grow without bound.
