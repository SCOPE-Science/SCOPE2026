The random directional width of a proper intersection of two unequal planar disks has an exact elementary distribution, with an atom at its largest width precisely when both disk centers lie on the same side of their common chord. A universal lower-edge square-root law, the upper-width atom, and the distributional endpoints uniquely recover both radii and the separation; the atom appears linearly at \(d=\sqrt{R^2-r^2}\).
