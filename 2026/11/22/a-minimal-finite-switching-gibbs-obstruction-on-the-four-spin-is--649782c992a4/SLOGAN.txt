For a four-spin Ising ring driven by a globally switching infinite-temperature and finite-temperature bath, the stationary spin law is never exactly Gibbs at any finite positive switching rate, although its fast-switching limit is Gibbs. An exact strictly positive three-energy-level curvature witness proves the obstruction and shows that the disagreement between two effective-temperature estimates vanishes precisely to first order in the inverse switching rate; the three-spin cycle has no such obstruction.
