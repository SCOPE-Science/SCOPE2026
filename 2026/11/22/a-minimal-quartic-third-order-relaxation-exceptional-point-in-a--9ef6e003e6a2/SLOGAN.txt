At equal trap and rotational relaxation rates, the fourth position moment of an active Brownian particle is the first to reveal a size-three relaxation Jordan block: its conditional law contains the exact term \(v^4t^2e^{-4\kappa t}\cos(2\Theta_0)/(2\kappa^2)\). No positional polynomial of degree at most three admits a quadratic-in-time exponential term.
