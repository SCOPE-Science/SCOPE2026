# Reproducibility

Execute `python checker.py` with standard Python 3.8 or later. It checks the exact full face set, the 134 disjoint matching pairs, all 1,053 oriented Hasse incidences for absence of gradient cycles, and all integer gradient-path reductions of the three critical four-cells to zero. A separately constructed full simplicial chain complex modulo two yields boundary ranks (8,28,56,42). The integral homotopy equivalence is obtained from the exact zero integral Morse differential and the Hurewicz classification of three-sphere attachments.

Computational checks are exhaustive for the specified nine-vertex complex only. The external independent audit has not been performed.
