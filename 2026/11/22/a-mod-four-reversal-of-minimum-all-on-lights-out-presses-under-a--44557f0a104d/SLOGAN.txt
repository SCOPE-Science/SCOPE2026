For cubic circular prisms and their Möbius-twisted counterparts on \(2n\) vertices, the complete all-on Lights-Out strategy polynomials exhibit a sharp mod-four reversal: twisting changes the minimum number of presses from \(n/2\) to \(n\) when \(4\mid n\), but from \(n\) to \(n/2\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\). Every all-on press strategy and its weight are classified by a four-period binary recurrence.
