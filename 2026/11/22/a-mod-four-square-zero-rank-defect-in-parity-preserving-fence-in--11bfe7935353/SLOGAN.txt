For the order-, fence-, and parity-preserving inverse monoid on an \(n\)-point chain, the maximum rank of a square-zero partial injection drops by exactly one precisely when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\). In the other three residue classes, its entire maximum-rank stratum has sizes \(2^m\), \((m+1)2^m\), and \((m+2)2^m\) for chain sizes \(4m\), \(4m+1\), and \(4m+3\), respectively.
