Resolving the three pencil centers of the \(3d\) maximal-contact tangent lines of a complex Fermat curve gives an intrinsic stable boundary with critical group \(C_m^{3d-5}\oplus C_{d^2m}\oplus C_{3d^2m}\) for \(m=2d+1\) not divisible by three. When \(3\mid m\), the last two factors become \(C_3\oplus C_{d^2m}^2\), a sharp integral residue-three transition for every \(d\ge3\).
