Under synchronous binary majority on the two-subset Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\), every ground-set size \(n\equiv0\pmod4\), \(n\ge8\), has exactly two fixed configurations, while every \(n\equiv1\pmod4\), \(n\ge5\), has precisely the unanimous configurations plus one for each labelled \((n-1)/2\)-regular graph. The modulo-four equilibrium dichotomy follows from a sharp active-degree Cauchy--Schwarz bound and complement symmetry.
