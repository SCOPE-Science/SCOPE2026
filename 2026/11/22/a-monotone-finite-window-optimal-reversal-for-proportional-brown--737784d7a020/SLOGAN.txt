For a Brownian particle subjected to Poissonian proportional resets, the unique reset factor minimizing the noise of every finite-window stationary time average is **negative**, moves strictly toward stronger reflection as the observation window grows, and approaches \(-1/3\) at long times. This optimal signed reset reduces the long-run variance rate by exactly \(5/32\) compared with full resetting, even though full resetting uniquely minimizes snapshot variance.
