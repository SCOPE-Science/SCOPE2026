For symmetric \(\alpha\)-stable flights escaping a bounded interval, the strong-Poisson-resetting mean is faster than the unreset mean precisely beyond the starting-position frontier \(v_c(\alpha)=\tanh[\operatorname{arcosh}(\pi\alpha/(2\sin(\pi\alpha/2)))/\alpha]\). This frontier rises strictly with \(\alpha\), from \(\tanh(\pi/(2\sqrt3))\) to \(1\), distinguishing high-rate resetting from the previously studied weak-reset criterion.
