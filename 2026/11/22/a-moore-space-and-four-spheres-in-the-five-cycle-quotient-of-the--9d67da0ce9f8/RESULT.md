# A Moore space and four spheres in the five-cycle quotient of the partition complex

## Finding

Let \(\Pi_5\) be the poset of partitions of \(\{0,1,2,3,4\}\) ordered by refinement, and \(X=\Delta(\overline{\Pi}_5)\) the simplicial order complex with its two extremal partitions deleted. Let \(C_5\) act by adding one to every ground label modulo five. The **ordinary geometric quotient**, not the fixed-point space or homotopy quotient, has homotopy type

\[
|X|/C_5\simeq M(\mathbb Z/5,1)\vee\bigvee^{4}S^2,
\]

where \(M(\mathbb Z/5,1)=S^1\cup_{5}D^2\) is the two-dimensional Moore space attached to the circle with degree five. Thus the homology groups in positive degrees are \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z/5\) and \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^4\), with no higher homology. Those groups and the fundamental group were known; the asserted decomposition of attaching maps is the new result.

## Assumptions and scope

All partitions are unordered collections of nonempty disjoint blocks, union the five-element ground set. A vertex of \(X\) is a proper non-extremal partition; a simplex is a strictly refining chain. A cyclic translation acts on the blocks and on chains simultaneously. The rotation is free on proper non-extremal partitions because an invariant partition would have either all singleton blocks or the indiscrete block. In particular the group action on the order complex is free and preserves each simplex orientation under the increasing-rank convention. The quotient is a genuine finite CW complex with cells indexed by orbits of chains, and is two-dimensional. No claim about other primes or subgroup quotients is made.

## Proof

**Cell construction.** Enumerate set partitions using restricted-growth strings, canonicalize each block collection by sorting its blocks, and retain the ranks having four, three or two blocks. There are respectively 10, 25 and 15 such partitions, 50 total. A pair is an edge precisely when one refines the other, and a triangle is a chain of ranks four, three, two. Counting produces 205 edges and 180 triangles. Translation by \(C_5\) has no fixed vertex and, consequently, no fixed cell. Thus the quotient has \((10,41,36)\) cells in degrees \((0,1,2)\). The boundary of a triangle \((a<b<c)\) is the oriented orbit of \((b,c)\), minus that of \((a,c)\), plus that of \((a,b)\). The supplied verifier checks \(\partial_1\partial_2=0\) for all 36 triangles.

**Collapse of the one-skeleton.** List quotient cells lexicographically by canonical orbit representative; orient each edge from finer to coarser partition. Choose a maximal spanning tree greedily from this ordering. It has nine edges, and contracting it is a homotopy equivalence. The remaining presentation has one vertex, 32 circle generators, and 36 two-cell attaching words, read from the oriented triangle boundaries. All words are encoded as signed generator integers in `certificate.json`.

**Exact elimination of two-cells.** In a connected two-dimensional CW complex, if an attaching word contains a chosen circle generator exactly once (with exponent \(\pm1\)), a cyclic permutation and inversion put the relator in the form \(g^{\pm1}w\). The relevant two-cell may be used to eliminate the one-cell \(g\); substituting \(g=w^{\mp1}\) (with the corresponding inverse convention) in all other attaching words preserves the homotopy type. Free reduction, cyclic conjugation and inversion of attaching words also preserve their homotopy classes as unbased maps into the one-skeleton. The verifier deterministically applies this operation 31 times. At each move it checks the generator's single occurrence in the selected relator, records the cyclic word and the replacement, and removes exactly one generator and one two-cell. This yields one remaining circle generator and five two-cells: two have identical attaching word of exponent \(-5\), while three have empty words. The full list of 31 replacements and the final words are in `certificate.json`, independently regenerated by `verify.py`.

**Homotopy decomposition.** The first degree-five two-cell together with the circle forms \(M(\mathbb Z/5,1)\). The second degree-five word is nullhomotopic after that attachment, because the fundamental group of the Moore space has the relation \(g^5=1\). Hence this cell attaches trivially up to homotopy and contributes one \(S^2\). The other three empty attaching words contribute three more \(S^2\). This proves the claimed wedge decomposition, rather than only its homology.

## Verification

The self-contained Python 3 program `verify.py` and the complete exact `certificate.json` are included. In the same directory run `python verify.py`. The program rebuilds all 52 partitions, excludes the two extremal objects, enumerates and orbits all cells, checks the quotient integer boundary compositions, verifies the spanning tree, executes the 31 signed-word eliminations, and asserts exact equality to every value in the certificate. It prints `VERIFY_OK` only after all assertions pass. This is a finite exhaustive proof certificate for the five-label instance; it does not extrapolate to arbitrary labels or cyclic order.

## Relationship to prior work

Donau's 2012 paper (arXiv:1202.4368, Proposition 4.2) studies exactly this prime-cyclic quotient family. It already proves \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z/5\) and \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^4\) in the five-label case and establishes that such quotients are not wedges of spheres. Its four-page original paper does not classify the actual attaching maps or assert the Moore-space-plus-four-spheres homotopy type. Those known homology statements are not presented as original here. Arone's branching rule and Arone–Brantner's larger Young-subgroup analysis address equivariant restriction and Young quotients; the transitive five-cycle action is not the same as quotienting by a proper Young subgroup. The full geometric type asserted here is an additional, stronger classification of a canonical first-prime example.

## Limitations

The result is solely for five labels and their translation five-cycle, not all prime cyclic quotients. Exact checks establish the homotopy type for the stated CW construction, not the uniqueness of any theorem across unindexed literature. Although the most directly relevant four-page prior paper was inspected, a related dissertation's full text was not accessible and could contain an equivalent assertion. Independent mathematical review has not been performed. No formal proof-assistant certification is claimed.
