For three angle-uniform points on a circular arc whose half-span approaches \(2\pi/3\) at speed \(\lambda\sqrt\varepsilon\), the probability of triangle area within \(\varepsilon\) of its moving maximum is \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\Psi(\lambda)+o(\varepsilon^{3/2})\), with an explicit positive crossover integral. Conditional on that event, the two scaled endpoint gaps and scaled middle-point displacement converge in total variation to the uniform distribution on a specified three-dimensional quadratic region; the profile has an exact linear branch above a sharp positive threshold and a reciprocal-square far-negative matching law.
