In reflected Brownian interval coverage with Poisson resets retaining endpoint-visit memory, moving the reset site by \(\delta\sqrt{D/r}\) from the midpoint creates a full high-rate cover-time crossover: after scaling by \(r\exp[-(L/2)\sqrt{r/D}]\), the completion time approaches the maximum of independent exponentials with rates \(e^\delta\) and \(e^{-\delta}\). The symmetric \(3/2\) mean prefactor is previously known; the assessed extension is the continuously detuned distribution and convergence of all positive moments.
