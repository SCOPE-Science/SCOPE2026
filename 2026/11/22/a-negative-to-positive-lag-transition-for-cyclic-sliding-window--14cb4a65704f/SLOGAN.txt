In cyclic sliding maxima of width \(k\ge2\) for iid continuous data, the update indicators have negative covariance at every lag \(1,\ldots,k-1\), positive covariance exactly at lag \(k\), and zero covariance beyond the overlap range. This yields a closed harmonic-number variance of the total update count and a fixed-width Gaussian limit; the previously known linear total-count law is not claimed as new.
