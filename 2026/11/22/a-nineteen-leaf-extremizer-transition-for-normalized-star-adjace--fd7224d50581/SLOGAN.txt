The sharp maximum-entry sensitivity of symmetric degree-normalized adjacency on a weighted star switches its worst conductances at exactly nineteen leaves: through eighteen leaves all spokes sit at the common lower bound, while from nineteen onward one spoke becomes strictly heavier. Despite increasing star degree, the optimal conductance-to-matrix Lipschitz coefficient tends to the positive constant \(1/(3\sqrt3\,a)\) when all conductances are bounded below by \(a>0\).
