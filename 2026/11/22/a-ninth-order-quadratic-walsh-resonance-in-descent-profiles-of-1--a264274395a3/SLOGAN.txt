For \(132\)-avoiding permutations, two distinct Walsh characters on the descent-position profile have the same all-order multiplier \(W_n(\{1,2\})=W_n(\{1,3\})=C_n-4C_{n-1}+2C_{n-2}\), which vanishes only at \(n=9\) for \(n\ge4\). At this first quadratic-character cancellation order, exactly four of 28 two-coordinate modes vanish, while every singleton mode survives.
