In the diffusive regime of an elephant walk remembering its last fixed proportion of steps, the whole rescaled trajectory converges to a Gaussian Volterra process with the explicitly identified retarded-memory covariance kernel \(K(s,t)=\int_0^{\min(s,t)}w(\log(s/u))w(\log(t/u))\,du\). Its future-to-present covariance slope equals \((\sigma^2-1)/2\), proving a positive or negative memory signature and nonstationary increments whenever the repetition probability differs from one half.
