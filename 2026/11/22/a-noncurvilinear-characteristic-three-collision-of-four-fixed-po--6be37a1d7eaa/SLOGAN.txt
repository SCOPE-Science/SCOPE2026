The standard three-cycle on the integral quintic del Pezzo surface has a finite-flat degree-four fixed scheme: \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[u,v]/(v^2-v+1,\,u(u+2-v))\). In characteristic three its two interior and two boundary geometric fixed points merge into one isolated noncurvilinear length-four point with Hilbert function \((1,2,1)\) and nontrivial tangent-to-identity action.
