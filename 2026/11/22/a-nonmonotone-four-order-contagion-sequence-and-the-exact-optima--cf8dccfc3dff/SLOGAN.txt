Two-neighbour contagion on the affine incidence graphs \(\operatorname{AG}(2,q)\) has a nonmonotone minimum-seed sequence \((4,5,5,4)\) at orders \(q=2,3,4,5\). At order five there are exactly \(70000\) optimal four-vertex seeds, never two points and two lines, and their exact completion-time distribution ranges over rounds seven to eleven.
