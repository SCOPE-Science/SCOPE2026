# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The result is proved analytically from independent failures and a tail-sum expectation. A bilateral, uniformly convergent exponential lattice sum gives a uniform \(O_R(a)\) remainder; Mellin substitution gives all Fourier coefficients. The zero-free Gamma property establishes nonconstancy for every \(R>1\). Direct numerical checks from the self-contained checker reproduce 276 arithmetic diagnostics. Critical limits are fixed \(R>1\), \(A_0>0\) and sensitivity tending to zero. Numerical evidence is not the proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence

The closest published first-discovery models are Parnell et al. (2012, abstract inspected), Parnell et al. (2015, methodological full text inspected), and Epanchin-Niell et al. (2012, PDF methods and results inspected). They establish finite-sampling formulas or smooth approximations, but none of the accessible inspected material states a nonconstant log-periodic weak-sensitivity leading term for fixed invasion phase, the full Gamma coefficients, or the exact limit interval. A later survival-analysis paper (Robinson et al., first published 2025) is directly topical but not fully accessible. General discrete Mellin/Fourier lattice methods are classical, and the underlying finite-sampling distribution is not claimed as new. Source-access limitations leave historical-priority uncertainty.

## Value — PASS

The theorem distinguishes a genuine fixed-calendar sampling effect from continuous-surveillance or random-introduction averages in early-detection modelling. A mathematically exact limit-set statement and nonvanishing Fourier harmonics establish a structural bias, not merely an arbitrary numerical example. In the illustrative tenfold inter-survey growth case the leading normalized expectation changes from about 3.22781 to 3.80561 under a half-logarithmic-phase shift. Scope is a stylized, unbounded early-growth model and no empirical control recommendation follows.

## Scientific limitations

Carrying capacity, phase-uncertain introductions, correlated detection and finite observation budgets are excluded. Full-text examination of the original 2012 first-discovery study and the later Oikos size-at-detection study could alter historical-priority assessment. Independent audit has not been performed.
