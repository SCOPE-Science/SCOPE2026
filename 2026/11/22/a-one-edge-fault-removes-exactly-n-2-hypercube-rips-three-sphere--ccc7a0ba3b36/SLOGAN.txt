Deleting one edge from a binary hypercube and recomputing shortest-path distances removes **exactly \(n-2\) integral three-sphere summands** from its scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex for every \(n\ge3\). The entire persistence barcode loses exactly one degree-one interval \([1,2)\) and \(n-2\) degree-three intervals \([2,3)\), while all other intervals are unchanged.
