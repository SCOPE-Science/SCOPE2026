The degree-bounded induced-forest complexes of a cube with any one edge missing have the exact homotopy types \(S^1\vee S^1\), \(S^2\), and \(\bigvee^3S^4\) at thresholds zero, one, and two onward. A ten-pair elementary collapse realizes the stabilization from threshold three to two; the fault removes the intact cube’s previously known lower-dimensional homology class.
