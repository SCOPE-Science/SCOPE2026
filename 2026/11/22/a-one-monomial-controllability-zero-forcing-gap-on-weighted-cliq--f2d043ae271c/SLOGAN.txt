For two uniformly weighted cliques connected by one positive bridge, the fixed-Laplacian actuator-set polynomial differs from the known graph zero-forcing polynomial by exactly \((p-1)(q-1)z^{p+q-4}\) when the internal spectral scales \(pa\) and \(qb\) differ, and is identical when they agree. Thus one tunable spectral resonance removes precisely the smallest non-zero-forcing controllable placements, regardless of bridge conductance.
