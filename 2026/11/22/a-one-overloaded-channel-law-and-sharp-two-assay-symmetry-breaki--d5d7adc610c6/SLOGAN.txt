In a fixed-volume Poisson target-and-inhibitor assay model, every optimal multichannel allocation is either equal or has one oversized channel and equal smaller channels, with the smaller loads approaching a finite target-detection optimum at large total volume. For two channels with matched rates, equal splitting ceases to be globally optimal precisely at dimensionless volume \(2\log(2+\sqrt3)\), where two unequal optima emerge with a sharp square-root separation.
