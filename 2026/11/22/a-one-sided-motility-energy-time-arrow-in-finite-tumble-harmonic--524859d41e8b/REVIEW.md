# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is proved for every strictly positive trap and switching rate by a closed generator calculation on motor activity, propulsion-position correlation, and squared position. The exact covariance equations give a three-pole rational Laplace transform whose strictly positive exponential-convolution representation establishes the sign at every positive lag, including all resonance boundaries. Independent additive thermal Brownian noise cancels from centered mixed covariances. The packaged executable verified exact symbolic identities, 30 independent matrix-semigroup and convolution comparisons, and a 30,000-history event-driven spot check. The finite experiment does not extend the analytical domain.

The 2026 source [1] establishes the same run/tumble model and joint conditional stationary densities; [2] gives broad moment machinery and [3] the equal-rate stationary positional density. Their inspected sections do not state or directly imply the stationary forward-minus-backward motor-occupation/squared-position covariance sign and its uniform positive-convolution factorization. Indexed nearby findings concern stationary motor-state distributions, moment inverse folds, or **position-only** fourth-order time arrows, all scientifically distinct from the present mixed observable and all-parameter result. Accessible published summaries were implication-compared; their full repository documents were not available through the browser, and an unindexed exact match remains a residual priority risk. Absence from search alone is not novelty proof.

## Value — PASS

The sign is a measurable, mathematically sharp time-direction diagnostic of motility being converted into stored trap displacement. Unlike a stationary position distribution or an equal-time run/energy covariance, it distinguishes ordering for every positive lag and is stable under arbitrarily strong independent thermal diffusion. The integrated strength is exact, and the equal-rate case has a closed elementary profile and an explicit lag maximizing sensitivity. The result is deliberately limited to the one-dimensional finite-tumble mechanism and does not claim a general entropy-production formula.
