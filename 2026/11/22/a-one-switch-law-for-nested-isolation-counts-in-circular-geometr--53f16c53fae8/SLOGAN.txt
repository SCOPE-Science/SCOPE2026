Nested isolated-vertex counts in a uniform circular geometric graph can have **negative covariance** even though the larger-radius count is always smaller. Their entire finite-sample covariance sign diagram has at most one cross-radius reversal, controlled exactly by whether the smaller-radius expected isolate count exceeds one; the sparse-scale transition is \(y_c(x)=(e^{2x}+2e^x-3)/(4x)\).
