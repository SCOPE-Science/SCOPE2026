The two-saddle affine switch in García et al. (2023) admits strict transverse crossings in at most one direction for any forcing parameters, so it has no strictly crossing periodic orbit. In the published example, both arcs of the closed algebraic loop go from the upper contact to the lower one in forward time. Their flight times are \(6\log\varphi\) and \(4\log\varphi\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\); the lower contact enters attracting sliding and approaches the height \((5-\sqrt{73})/12\).
