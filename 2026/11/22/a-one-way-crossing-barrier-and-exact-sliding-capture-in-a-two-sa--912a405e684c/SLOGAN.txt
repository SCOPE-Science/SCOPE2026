The two-saddle affine switch in García et al. (2023) has a one-way Filippov crossing barrier and therefore no nonsliding periodic orbit at any forcing parameters, despite an algebraically matched closed loop in its displayed example. At the example parameters both half-plane arcs run downward between the same contacts, in exact times \(6\log\varphi\) and \(4\log\varphi\), with \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), and the attracting sliding half-line contains a unique attracting pseudoequilibrium at \((5-\sqrt{73})/12\).
