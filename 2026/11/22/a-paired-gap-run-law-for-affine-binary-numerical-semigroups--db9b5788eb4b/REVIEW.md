# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact Apéry thresholds follow by minimizing the Mersenne coin count in each residue; a positive wrap by the multiplicity is strictly dominated. The coin-cost adjacent difference is exactly plus or minus one, because appending a unit coin and splitting any nonunit coin provide two-sided inequalities, while parity excludes zero. The resulting nondecreasing threshold sequence has increments zero or two, which counts the terminal gap run of each length in the corresponding residue rows. The special generalized-Thabit parameters have cost two for the last residue and cost one versus three for its predecessor according to the exponent. The standalone checker corroborates all 317 consecutive multiplicities and 25 parameter examples, but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

Gu–Li–Huang (2022) established monotone Apéry and Frobenius/genus methods for a narrower parametrized family; Liu–Xin–Ye–Yin (2023) established Apéry and global Frobenius/genus formulas for the wider affine-repunit family containing the present semigroups. Those known results are explicitly credited. Inspection of their relevant full-text sections did not reveal an exact *maximal consecutive gap-run length histogram* or the universal zero-or-two rule, and the indexed nearest results concern different generator families. The result is a new weighted gap statistic on the inspected record, not a newly discovered Apéry formula. A non-obvious corollary or an unindexed equivalent remains a bibliographic risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A maximal missing-integer interval is an intrinsic local invariant, stronger than the number or largest size of missing integers. The theorem gives a uniform all-multiplicity law with exactly determined frequency zero or two for each candidate length, and turns the two-parameter generalized-Thabit subfamily into an infinite sharp perfectness classification. This provides structural information beyond genus and Frobenius-number formulas, with explicit reproducible membership and interval calculations.
