Every occurring maximal gap-run length in the affine binary semigroup \(\langle a,(a+1)2^i-1:i\ge1\rangle\) occurs **exactly twice**, with an effective coin-cost criterion for which lengths occur. For \(a=(2^s+1)2^n-1\) there are exactly \(a+1\) runs, and the semigroup has no isolated gaps precisely when \(n\ge2\).
