On the stable-equilibrium-line side of a published rational planar map, every nonnegative starting height admits at least one critical escape speed separating capture by the equilibrium line from ballistic escape. Every such critical orbit satisfies \(y_n^2=2\lambda n+\frac{5\lambda-2}{6}\log n+C+o(1)\), with an exact logarithmic cancellation at \(\lambda=2/5\).
