For exact Fourier-collocation heat flow on the \(2\pi\)-circle, the eventual nodal-positivity time is \(\pi/N-(\log N)/N^2+O(N^{-2})\) for even grids, but \(\pi/N-3(\log N)/N^2+O(N^{-2})\) for odd grids. The factor-three difference comes from the missing Nyquist cosine: its odd-grid replacement cancels to first order at the antipode, yielding an extra inverse-grid-size factor in the omitted Fourier tail.
