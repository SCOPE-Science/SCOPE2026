For connected reciprocal bipartite opinion networks with one peer susceptibility per community, every positive even number of peer updates selects the same high-platform-susceptibility consensus, while odd numbers select explicitly different weights that approach the even value geometrically. A lone hub can retain consensus weight \(16/17\) against arbitrarily many leaves, and changing the peer-round length from one through four selects \(4/5,16/17,32/35,16/17\) at fixed model parameters.
