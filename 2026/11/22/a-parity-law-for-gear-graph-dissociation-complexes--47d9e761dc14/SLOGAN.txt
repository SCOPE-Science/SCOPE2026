For every gear graph \(G_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the dissociation complex is exactly one \((n-1)\)-sphere when \(n\) is odd and a wedge of three \((n-1)\)-spheres when \(n\) is even. A fiberwise discrete-Morse matching reduces the entire cyclic family to the all-spoke face and, in even length, two alternating faces.
