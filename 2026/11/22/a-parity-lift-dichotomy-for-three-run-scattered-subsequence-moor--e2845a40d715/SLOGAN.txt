For the parity of scattered copies of \(0^a1^b0\), the minimal complete Moore counter has \(2^{b+\lfloor\log_2 a\rfloor+2}\) states, except when \(b=1\) and \(a\) is odd, when that count is halved. Precisely in the exceptional class, \(\binom{w}{0^a10}\equiv(|w|_0-1)\binom{w}{0^a1}\pmod2\); otherwise the final zero creates an independent parity degree of freedom.
