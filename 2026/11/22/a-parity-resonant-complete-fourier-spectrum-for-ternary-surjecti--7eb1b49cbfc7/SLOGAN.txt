For onto ternary words, counting convolution has an explicitly classified Fourier multiplier at every frequency: diagonal-colour balance is the only obstruction at odd lengths, while even lengths lose exactly \((2^n+2)/3\) additional balanced frequencies. The exact surviving spectral gap jumps from \(3\) to \(9\), and the Fourier-range projector changes from positive averaging to a signed, noncontractive projection.
