On zero-boundary finite paths, an initial binary word reaches Rule 178's fully alternating two-cycle precisely when every interior monochromatic run is odd and neither boundary run of ones has even length at least four, apart from the uniform-word exception. The exact basin size has a rational generating function and parity-sensitive Fibonacci/Lucas formulas, with uniform-initialization probability asymptotic to a parity-dependent constant times \((\varphi/2)^n\).
