For second-kind Chebyshev projection under the semicircle probability weight, the sharp real bounded-variation-to-weighted-mean-square residual has squared norm \(2/(\pi^2n)-2/(\pi^2n^2)+o(n^{-2})\). Every worst jump converges to the midpoint, but the midpoint is a strict local minimum for every odd cutoff and loses precisely \(2/(\pi^2n^2)+o(n^{-2})\) against the sharp error.
