The Walsh convolution of labelled rooted-forest root sets on \(K_{d,d}\) is invertible at every even shore size, but at every odd \(d\ge3\) it loses exactly \(\binom{d}{(d-1)/2}^2\) Fourier characters. Increasing one shore by one instead yields exactly one zero character for every \(d\ge2\), and a two-parameter signed-Laplacian determinant gives the full spectrum of every \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\).
