Every minimum feedback vertex set of a cyclic prism \(C_n\square K_2\) belongs to an explicit finite list of column patterns: there are \((2m+1)2^m\) optima at odd length \(2m+1\) and \((2m)(3m+2)2^{m-2}\) at even length \(2m\). For odd prisms, the complete optimality-preserving adjacent-slide degree polynomial is also exact, revealing a three-valued mobility spectrum for all odd lengths at least seven.
