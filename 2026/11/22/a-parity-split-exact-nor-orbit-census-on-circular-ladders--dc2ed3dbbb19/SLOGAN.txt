On the \(n\)-rung circular ladder, synchronous open-neighborhood NOR has exactly \(M_n\) fixed configurations and \((M_n^2-M_n)/2\) primitive two-cycles for even \(n\), but \((M_{2n}-M_n)/2\) for odd \(n\), where \(M_n=L_n+2\) if \(3\mid n\) and \(M_n=L_n-1\) otherwise. The parity switch arises because the canonical bipartite double cover splits for even circumference and is a single doubled prism for odd circumference; every state becomes recurrent within one update.
