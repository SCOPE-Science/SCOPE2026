For every even \(d\), a parity-syndrome code produces an odd independent set in \(Q_d\) of size at least \(2^{d-1}(1-2^{-\lfloor\log_2d\rfloor})\). Consequently \(\alpha_{\mathrm{od}}(Q_d)/2^d\to1/2\) through even dimensions, answering the published asymptotic question, with a sharp-order \(\Theta(1/d)\) deficit.
