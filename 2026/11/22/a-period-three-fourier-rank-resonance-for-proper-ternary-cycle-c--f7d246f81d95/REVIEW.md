# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof establishes a root-colour/edge-difference bijection, evaluates every balanced character using finite cyclic character orthogonality, and solves the exact integer zero equation by \(2\)-adic valuations. The resulting multinomial nullity follows from a complete and disjoint frequency classification. Two independent exact-arithmetic enumerations reproduce all signed coefficients at all balanced frequencies in their stated finite ranges. The proof—not the enumerations—covers every cycle size \(n\ge3\).

**Originality — PASS under bounded comparison.** Goodall (arXiv:0806.4848, especially Sections 2 and 4) supplies graph-colouring Fourier methods but no stated ternary cycle vertex-frequency rank census. Babson–Beck (arXiv:1204.1283, Sections 1–3) derives aggregate group-valued-colouring expansions, not the exact all-frequency multipliers here. Bedini–Jacobsen (arXiv:1003.4847, Sections 2–3) computes Potts/chromatic partition functions rather than per-character convolution ranks. A closely related published note on cycle proper-colouring first-coordinate Fourier Gram matrices treats only degree-one sampling and does not mathematically imply the all-degree convolution-nullity formula. An unindexed equivalent calculation remains possible; one relevant published note was only available by indexed summary, not full primary text, and that limitation is explicitly retained.

**Value — PASS.** Counting proper ternary colourings is classical, but the all-degree Fourier recovery question is distinct. The complete integer multiplier law yields a sharp infinite period-three transition, an exact loss of \(\frac{(3k)!}{(k!)^3}\frac{3k+1}{k+1}\) additional recoverable dimensions, and a vanishing asymptotic loss density proportional to \(1/k\). It goes beyond isolated enumerations or standard chromatic-polynomial values and identifies exactly which modes are lost.

**Scope and priority limits.** Ternary cyclic palette and labelled simple cycles only; no result for arbitrary palette sizes. No independent external audit. Earlier equivalent unpublished or unindexed work cannot be ruled out solely from bounded searches.
