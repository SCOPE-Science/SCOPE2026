For proper three-colourings of a labelled cycle, additive convolution has no additional balanced Fourier zeros unless the cycle length is congruent to one modulo three. At length \(3k+1\), exactly \(\frac{(3k)!}{(k!)^3}\frac{3k+1}{k+1}\) balanced frequencies vanish, with a complete signed multiplier formula and sharp all-length rank classification.
