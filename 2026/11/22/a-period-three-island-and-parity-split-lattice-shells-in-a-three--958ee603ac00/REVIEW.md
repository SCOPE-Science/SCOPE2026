The defined map is the exact three-step composition \(T_1\circ T_1\circ T_0\) on all \(\mathbb R^2\). The rational integral attributed to Cima, Gasull and Mañosa is verified as a symbolic identity, and its subtraction-free tropical limit yields the seven-term invariant. Direct half-plane intersection proves the triangular and hexagonal polygon vertices. Unimodularity and circle orientation imply arclength isometry, while exact vertex images give the claimed rotation displacement. The rational-period gcd reduction is valid for coprime energy numerator and denominator. Integer lattice shell counts follow from primitive lattice lengths and the resulting cyclic permutation. Boundaries \(h=4/3\) and \(h=2\) are handled separately. Exact checks confirm 502 rational energy shells, 3,536 off-shell points and all integer-shell orbits from energies 2 to 50. The proof covers all real energies and integers analytically; finite verification alone would not.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The rational three-periodic Lyness integral and general circle-rotation framework were published by Cima, Gasull and Mañosa (arXiv:0912.5031, 2009-12-26). Those inputs are attributed, not claimed as new. Their inspected theorems do not present the exact tropical \((0,1,1)\) level geometry, the isochronous triangular region, the explicit outer fraction, or the shell-by-shell odd/even integer orbit decomposition. The 2010 Lyness integrability paper is about rational nonautonomous maps; De Angelis addresses autonomous max maps. The recurrence in the 2001 Grove–Kent–Ladas–Radin period-three work is structurally distinct after logarithms. Other historical work on periodic-coefficient max recurrences by Patula–Voulov and Voulov could be reviewed only through abstracts, so priority outside accessible material remains uncertain. No stronger covering implication was identified in the examined literature.

A full plane, all-energy rotation classification for the simplest two-high-season binary forcing is a natural object, not an arbitrary finite slice. The transition from a positive-area isochronous period-three island to strictly twisting hexagonal tori is a structural dynamical phenomenon, and the global integer-periodicity theorem sharpens this with an exact odd/even splitting into primitive cycles on every integer energy shell. It explains infinitely many periods and how the coefficients control them, rather than merely collecting sample values. The claim is restricted to this precisely defined recurrence.
