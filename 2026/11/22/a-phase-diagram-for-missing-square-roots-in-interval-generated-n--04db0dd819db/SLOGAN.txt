For the interval numerical semigroups \(\langle a,a+1,\ldots,a+k\rangle\), the positive missing-square roots have an exact all-width count and a limiting frequency curve \(F(k/a)\) with a sharp derivative transition at every reciprocal integer. When \(k\to\infty\) but \(k/a\to0\), their rescaled roots have the universal density \(1-s^2\) and their total number is asymptotic to \(2a/(3\sqrt{k})\).
