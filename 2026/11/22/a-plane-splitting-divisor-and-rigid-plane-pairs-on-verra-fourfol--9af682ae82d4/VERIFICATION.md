# Verification and limits

Run `python verification/check_verra_planes.py` with SymPy installed. This checks the branch polynomial and square fiber in the cited 2026 example, both base derivatives, the exact squarefree discriminant tangent form, the rank-five map of global normal sections, the Chern expansion, and the four-cycle intersection matrix's determinant and Smith invariants. The checker is self-contained apart from SymPy; there are no unpublished inputs or computational output files to retrieve.

The parameter-divisor dimension, generic uniqueness of the square fiber, exact normal bundle sequence, Hilbert-scheme reducedness, nodal criterion, and algebraic intersection identities are justified by the written proof. Neither a finite computation nor a search absence is used as a proof of a universal statement or of priority. The cited 2026 primary paper establishes smoothness of the explicit example. No integral saturation, complete plane classification, complete algebraic lattice, or independent mathematical audit has been established.
