The optimal placement of a second Brownian target under Poisson resetting crosses the ratio \(x_*/L=1/3\) **exactly once**, at \(L\sqrt{r/D}=\tfrac32\log\rho\), where \(\rho^3-\rho-1=0\). Beyond this threshold the optimum lies **below** the ratio approached at large distances, forcing a nonmonotone placement rule despite the published endpoint asymptotics.
