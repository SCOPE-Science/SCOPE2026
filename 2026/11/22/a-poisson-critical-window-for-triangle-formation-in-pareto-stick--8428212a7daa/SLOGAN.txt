For \(n\) independent Pareto stick lengths, triangles first appear at the moving-shape scale \(\alpha_n\asymp n^{-3/2}\): the locations of violating consecutive order-statistic triples converge to a Poisson process with intensity \((\pi^2\lambda/12)(1-x)^2\,\mathrm dx\) when \(n^3\alpha_n^2\to\lambda\). Thus the probability of having no triangle converges to \(\exp(-\pi^2\lambda/36)\), with an explicit finite-size Poisson approximation bound.
