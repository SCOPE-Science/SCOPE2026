# Scientific review: Poisson defect window for random height-two parameter posets

## Correctness — PASS
The finding concerns **complete nonalgebraic one-types**, not equality types, over individually fixed named parameters. The proof reconstructs the Fraïssé one-point extension conditions as ideal/filter pairs; an exhaustive disjoint division into three families is proved for arbitrary height-two comparison graphs. For deleted comparisons forming a matching, Family I contributes \(2^{a+b-2k}3^k\) and the two outer families contribute \(2^a-1+k\) and \(2^b-1+k\). This gives the exact formula even at \(k=0\) and with rectangular sides. Under independent sparse deletions, the number of omissions is asymptotically Poisson by the Bernoulli sum theorem; row and column intensity controls eliminate overlapping omitted edges with probability tending to one. The remaining terms vanish on normalization. The deterministic uniform upper bound of three for the normalized type count justifies the asserted fixed-moment convergence. In the balanced model, the separate exact expectation formula and the exponential bound on its three families prove the sharp outer regimes. No finite calculation is used as an infinite proof.

The standalone checker enumerates 682 bipartite posets of small side sizes against direct ternary statuses, 46 matching-deletion instances, 14 rational-probability exact expectations, and the two finite endpoints up to five vertices per side. All its recorded tests pass. The limitation is that this is not an intermediate-density free-energy theorem or a statement about arbitrary random posets.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
A 2026 published record on height-two one-types gives the broader **finite graph identity** \(t(G)=2i(G)+i(\bar G)-2^{|A|}-2^{|B|}\); this established formula and the matching specialization must not be claimed as new. Another published record proves the deterministic single-missing-relation effect. The general ideal/filter bijection is also classical. The novelty candidate is the precise stochastic scaling and nondegenerate limiting distribution of the *logical-type count*: the universal Poisson power, its moments, and the exact comparison of the subcritical and supercritical near-complete regimes. That statement is not given in the closest full texts inspected and does not follow from a parameter-only equality without the probabilistic reduction, collision control and out-of-window bound. A new proof is supplied for those steps. Related random-bipartite-poset literature studies dimension, while the searched independent-set literature mostly concerns the independence number or denser graph regimes. There remains a genuine risk that a closely related Poisson partition-function law was previously stated and translates immediately through the known finite identity. This self-review does not establish global priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result uses a natural perturbation parameter for an established homogeneous structure: random loss of cross-comparabilities in a complete height-two parameter set. It locates the exact \(n^{-2}\) transition between a full \(4^n\)-scale type spectrum and exponential loss, and identifies an explicit **non-Gaussian, nondegenerate limit** rather than only an average. It separates annealed expectation from random realization, supplies a closed variance constant, and shows universality for non-identical independent omissions under a natural local sparsity hypothesis. The specificity of \((3/4)^Z\) comes from an exact three-of-four state obstruction per isolated missing comparison. This is a motivated type-distribution boundary, not a new naming for a routine finite table or a claim about all random posets. Its derivation is elementary, and no broader model-theoretic theorem is asserted.
