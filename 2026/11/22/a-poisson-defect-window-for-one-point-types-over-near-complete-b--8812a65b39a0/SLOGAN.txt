For a balanced random height-two poset approaching complete comparability, the number of nonalgebraic one-point types has a sharp missing-relation window: at omission probability \(q_n\sim\lambda/n^2\), its normalization by \(4^n\) converges to \((3/4)^Z\), with \(Z\) Poisson of mean \(\lambda\). The same law is universal for independent inhomogeneous omissions with finite total intensity and vanishing row and column intensities, and it collapses to zero beyond this window while omissions still vanish.
