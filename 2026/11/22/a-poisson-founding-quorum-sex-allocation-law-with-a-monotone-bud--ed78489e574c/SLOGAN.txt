When success requires at least one male and a quorum of \(r\) females among independent Poisson founders, the unique optimal male allocation rises strictly with the total expected founder count and falls with \(r\). For \(r\ge2\) its share rises from \(1/(r+1)\) toward, but never reaches, \(1/2\); an exact beta-MGF equation and sharp asymptotics quantify the policy.
