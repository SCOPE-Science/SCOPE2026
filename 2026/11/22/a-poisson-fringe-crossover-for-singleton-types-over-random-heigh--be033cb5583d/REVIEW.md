# Scientific review

PASS. The one-point extension normal form is applied with its full order-theoretic conditions, and the exact finite binomial sums follow from the independent-edge model. The fixed-fringe second moment is controlled by the exact overlap covariance. A uniform entropy/tail argument makes summation over all fringe sizes legitimate and proves the convergence in probability and conditional total variation. The standalone verifier independently checks every balanced comparability graph through shore size three, rational expectations, direct transitivity, and numerical finite expectation convergence. Finite checks alone do not establish asymptotics.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The formula identifies a nondegenerate transition of genuinely two-sided order-extension types at a natural random-comparability scale, and determines their typical minority-side size law. It distinguishes a quenched fraction from a ratio of annealed expectations and gives a natural all-order asymptotic logical invariant with a structural proof.

The limit is in probability for fixed real window shift and balanced independent-edge height-two bases. There is no almost-sure, uniform-shift or individual-poset finite-distribution claim. No independent validation of either the mathematics or priority has been performed.
