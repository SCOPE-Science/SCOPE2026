For random two-level parameter posets with cross-comparability deletion probability \(q_n=(2\log n+t)/n\), the fraction of complete singleton types having both predecessors and successors converges in probability to \(\tanh(e^{-t/2}/2)\). A uniformly chosen two-sided type has a smaller predecessor/successor shore whose size converges to a zero-truncated Poisson law of parameter \(e^{-t/2}\), with equally likely shore orientations.
