A uniformly selected supporting-tangent direction and its perpendicular companion intersect on an ellipse's director circle with exactly half uniform angular measure and half doubled-angle Poisson-kernel measure. Its second harmonic reconstructs the ellipse's anisotropy, its distance from uniformity is exactly \(\arcsin((a^2-b^2)/(a^2+b^2))/\pi\), and its slender limit has a uniform half plus two quarter-mass atoms.
