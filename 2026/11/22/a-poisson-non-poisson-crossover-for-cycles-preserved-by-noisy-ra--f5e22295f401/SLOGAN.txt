When the refresh probability tends to zero above the \(n^{-1/2}\) scale, rescaled common directed cycles converge to a Poisson process. At the critical scale their factorial densities are \(e^{-a(x_1+\cdots+x_r)-(x_1+\cdots+x_r)^2/2}/(x_1\cdots x_r)\), giving strictly negative covariance between disjoint size bands bounded away from zero. Both regimes give explicit laws for the largest shared cycle.
