Preserving the outgoing edges of a random mapping creates a sharp cycle-length crossover: common directed cycles follow independent Poisson laws when the refresh scale is above \(n^{-1/2}\), but at the critical scale their factorial densities are \(e^{-a(x_1+\cdots+x_r)-(x_1+\cdots+x_r)^2/2}/(x_1\cdots x_r)\). The critical limit has strictly negative covariance between disjoint positive size bands, and both regimes give explicit laws for the largest shared cycle.
