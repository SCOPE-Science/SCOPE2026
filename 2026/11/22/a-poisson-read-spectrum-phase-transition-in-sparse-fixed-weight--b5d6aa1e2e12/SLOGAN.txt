For uniform binary length-\(n\) words with \(r_n/\sqrt n\to c\in(0,\infty)\) and a fixed read length \(k\ge2\), the complete overlapping \(k\)-mer frequency vector is asymptotically an injective function of \(k-1\) independent \(\operatorname{Poisson}(c^2)\) short-gap counts. Two independent words consequently have matching frequency vectors with limiting probability \(\bigl[e^{-2c^2}I_0(2c^2)\bigr]^{k-1}\), and the all-separated spectrum exhibits an exact square-root sparsity threshold.
