# Scientific review: rational-map postcritical survivor theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** The displayed theorem is restricted to the interval survivor set for gains above the unique algebraic one-step contraction threshold. Inverting the rational map gives the two exact quadratic inverse branches and a gap of width \(\sqrt{\mu^2-6\mu+1}/\mu\). Their derivatives have monotone rational-radical expressions, from which the cubic sharp one-step contraction boundary follows. Disjoint strict inverse contractions prove full two-shift coding, and bounded distortion plus subadditivity proves a strictly positive escape exponent. Exact upper and lower inverse slopes bound the escape rate and Hausdorff dimension. A Bernoulli cylinder mass construction supplies the dimension lower bound; an exponentially small volume cover gives dimension strictly below one. The derivative brackets agree through three asymptotic orders for the dimension as gain grows. The standalone checker passed 500 independent inverse computations, the threshold check and rational discriminant checks. These computations do not replace the proof. The theorem explicitly excludes claims about trajectories leaving and then returning to the interval.

The generic coding, pressure bounds and bounded-distortion techniques are known and are not claimed as new. The assessed contribution is the simultaneous exact map-specific algebraic boundary, finite-time escape brackets, sharp large-gain escape amplitude \(3\), and three-term survivor-dimension expansion. Search incompleteness and an unlocated equivalent older derivation remain publication-priority risks.

## Value
**PASS.** The original interval dynamics cease to be globally defined on the modeled state interval at gain \(3+2\sqrt2\). The theorem explains what remains dynamically beyond that natural failure: a zero-area, full-two-shift Cantor repeller with a quantified positive population escape exponent and three explicit logarithmic dimension coefficients. This resolves a motivated postcritical state-space question rather than adding an arbitrary parameter restriction. The result is an exact large-gain mathematical prediction; no application, security or global escape claim beyond the stated interval is inferred.
