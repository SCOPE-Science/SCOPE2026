For the convolution of uniform arithmetic progressions with distinct prime lengths \(p<q\) and coprime steps \(a,b\), the maximum number of orthogonal exponentials is exactly \(q\) unless \(p\mid b\) or \(q\mid a\), when it jumps to a full basis of \(pq\). Every maximal \(q\)-frequency family in the nonresonant case is classified by its complete set of residues modulo \(q\).
