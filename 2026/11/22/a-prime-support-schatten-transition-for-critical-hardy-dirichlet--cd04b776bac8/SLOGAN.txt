The critical Hankel limits for finite-prime Hardy–Dirichlet compositions change Schatten behavior exactly with the number of active primes: one is noncompact, two are precisely in \(\mathcal S_p\) for \(p>1\), and three or more are trace class. For two normalized weights \(u,1-u\), the natural degree-cutoff trace diverges as \(\log N/[2\pi\sqrt{u(1-u)}]\).
