# Scientific review: a primitive-period formula for Petersen cycle-Hom complexes

## Correctness — PASS
The proof separates a lift to the universal covering tree into geodesic and backtracking sectors. The parity-preserving local swaps reduce any nongeodesic lift to the translation axis; the centralizer identifies its two parity classes iff the primitive period is odd. The universal-cover contractibility and null-sector homotopy type use the published square-free-target theorems (Matsushita, arXiv:2412.19144; Fujii--Kimura--Nozaki, arXiv:2412.19039). Möbius inversion on the explicitly specified nonbacktracking transition matrix counts the conjugacy classes. Independently enumerated recoloring components through length 12 agree with the closed formula. The enumeration is corroboration, not a proof for all lengths.

## Originality — PASS
The primary full articles above already prove the homotopy-type trichotomy and algorithms for individual components; those are not new. The distinct proposed result is the explicit uniform formula for the number of each component type at *every* cycle length, including a factor-of-two centralizer parity issue. Accessible earlier exact calculations handle lengths 5, 7, 8, 9 and 11. Wrochna's general reconfiguration work and a published-finding corpus finite-case article were not available as complete texts; this creates priority uncertainty, and searches do not prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an infinite natural classification of a concrete square-free, cubic, nonbipartite target. It computes each Betti rank and the exact number of isolated maps and circles for every source cycle. The primitive odd-period correction first matters substantively for the source twelve-cycle, in which 24 double-wound primitive pentagons contribute 24 circles, not 48.

## Scope and risks
Only \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_n,\operatorname{KG}(5,2))\) with \(n\ge3\) is classified up to ordinary integral homotopy equivalence. Published square-free-target results supply major proof ingredients, and a different source could contain the uniform arithmetic enumeration. The finite checker does not replace verification of the tree normal-form argument; independent mathematical audit remains outstanding.
