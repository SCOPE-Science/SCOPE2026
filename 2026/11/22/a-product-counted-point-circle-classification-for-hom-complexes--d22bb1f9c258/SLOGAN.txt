For a bouquet of any number of cycles, the entire Hom complex into \(C_q\), \(q\ge5\), has exactly \(s_q\prod_j b_j\) circle components and \(q(\prod_j a_j-\prod_j b_j)\) contractible components, where \(a_j\) counts admissible petal windings and \(b_j\) counts strict ones. A single extremally wound petal freezes the common hub and converts its winding sector into \(q\) contractible components, whereas strict winding on every petal yields one circle for odd \(q\) or two for even \(q\).
