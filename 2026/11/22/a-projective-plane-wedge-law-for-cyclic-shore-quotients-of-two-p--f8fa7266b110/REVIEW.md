## Correctness — PASS
A fully specified equivariant coned-square deformation retracts the collision-free continuous two-point graph configuration space onto the disjoint-closed-cell cubical model. This avoids assuming an arbitrary nonequivariant Abrams retraction descends through a symmetry quotient. The quotient cell representatives are exhaustive, with \(1+n+\binom n2\) vertices, \(n(n+1)\) edges, and \(n(n-1)\) squares. Every two-cell attaching word is exhibited and is reduced via explicit spanning-tree, one-cell/two-cell cancellation, and attaching-map-slide steps to one degree-two cell and \(n(n-3)/2\) trivially attached two-cells. This identifies an integral homotopy type, not merely Betti numbers. The ordinary graph-square center collapses are equivariant because cyclic order three cannot fix or exchange two distinct graph edges. The included verifier checks complete finite orbit cells and boundary words for \(3\le n\le9\), supporting the uniform symbolic argument; finite cases are not treated as an infinite proof.

## Value — PASS
This is a complete, natural infinite-family integral homotopy classification of a nonfree symmetry quotient of a central family of graph configuration spaces. It explains the persistence of first-homology two-torsion under an order-three action and the quadratic birth of independent second-homotopy free classes. The new growing-rank classification at all \(n\ge4\) is not implied by the isolated known \(n=3\) surface quotient, general rational symmetry representations, or the contractibility of a further full-symmetry quotient.
