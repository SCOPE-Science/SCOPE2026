Rotating only the three-vertex shore of a complete bipartite graph gives a two-particle configuration quotient of exact homotopy type \(\mathbb{RP}^{2}\vee\bigvee^{n(n-3)/2}S^{2}\) for every \(n\ge3\). The projective-plane case at \(n=3\) is known; the new uniform result shows a quadratic growth of free two-sphere summands while the fundamental group remains \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\).
