The ordinary simple zero-divisor graph of \(\mathbb F_{2^e}[(C_2)^3]\) is an explicit clique blow-up of the point-line incidence structure of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,2^e)\). Its entire adjacency characteristic polynomial factors into two linear factors, a projective-incidence quadratic, and one cubic, and its adjacency matrix is nonsingular for every \(e\ge1\).
