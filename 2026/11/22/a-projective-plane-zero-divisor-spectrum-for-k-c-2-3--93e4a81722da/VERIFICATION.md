# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In characteristic two the three commuting g_i-1 square to zero, generate the group ring, and the squarefree quotient has the same dimension eight. Its positive-degree ideal J is nilpotent and every element with nonzero constant coefficient is a unit. Frobenius gives f²=0 for every f∈J; nevertheless the graph deletes diagonal loops. For f=v+w+txyz and g=v'+w'+t'xyz, the separate degree-two and degree-three product tests are v∧v'=0 and <v,w'>+<v',w>=0. The latter is the perfect pairing in complementary coordinate bases, and the former is exactly proportionality. For nonzero v=αu in a normalized projective direction, equal s=<u,w>/α is precisely the test within that point, and there are (q-1)q²q choices at fixed point and s. For v=0,w≠0, the direction of w defines the projective line and gives q(q-1) possibilities; those vertices annihilate one another. The nonzero degree-three vertices are universal. The cross test is the stated projective incidence, hence precisely a+mb+mqc=q⁷-1 vertices. Internal zero-sum clique vectors give -1, while zero-sum constants across the q separate cliques of a point give c-1. The centered line and point subspaces are preserved by N and Nᵀ since their row and column sums are q+1. On those spaces NNᵀ=qI, so each paired mode gives the displayed quadratic with coupling product bq²c. Uniform line and point coordinates and the universal coordinate give T; the sum of the dimensions is q⁷-1, including a=1 at q=2. At λ=0 the quadratic is 1-c-bq²c<0 and c-1>0. The verified determinant expansion is strictly negative for q≥2 because (q-1)³≥1 and q⁷≥2q⁴. This proves nonsingularity over the reals, not for an adjacency retaining square-zero loops.
