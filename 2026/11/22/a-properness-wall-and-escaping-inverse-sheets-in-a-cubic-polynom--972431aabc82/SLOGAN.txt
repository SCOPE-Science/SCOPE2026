For the canonical cubic member \(F_{b,M}(x,y)=(y,y^2(x^3-3x)+bx+M)\) of a polynomial horseshoe family, \(b>0\) yields a proper three-sheeted folded map with exactly two ordinary cusp values, whereas \(b<0\) loses properness as two real inverse sheets escape like \(\pm\sqrt{-b}/|u|\) above targets approaching \(u=0\). At \(b=0\) a whole source line collapses to one target, and an exact cubic inverse criterion classifies every real fiber on both sides of this transition.
