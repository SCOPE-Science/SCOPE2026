For uniformly stationary symmetric walks on a two-level tree with \(k\) branches and \(m\) leaves per branch, the unique Kemeny-optimal root becomes lazy at the exact integer threshold \(m=k^2-3k+4\). In the joint limit \(m/k^2\to\rho>0\), its holding probability converges to \((1-\rho^{-1/2})_+\), giving a sharp quadratic branching transition.
