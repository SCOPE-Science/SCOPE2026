In the Zhao–Ma modular logistic map, the invariant face \(w=0\) has exactly \(\lceil K\rceil(\lceil K\rceil-1)/2\) fixed points for \(K=64\mu>1\), a whole fixed curve at \(K=1\), and only the origin below that threshold. At integer gains these boundary equilibria approach the nonuniform density \(2v\,du\,dv\) with explicit \(O(K^{-1})\) Wasserstein error; all nonzero face equilibria have expanding one-sided multipliers.
