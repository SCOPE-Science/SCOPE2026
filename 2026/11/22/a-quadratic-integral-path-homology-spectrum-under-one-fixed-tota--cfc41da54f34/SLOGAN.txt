On a fixed \(4m\)-point labeled total-order Alexandrov topology, forward-path digraphs can realize every first integral path-homology group \(\mathbb Z^k\) for \(0\leq k\leq m^2\), without changing either the left or right vertex-path topology. The explicit chord-cycle basis gives a quadratic-order separation between these topological data and degree-one path homology.
