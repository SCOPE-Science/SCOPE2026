# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The proof explicitly fixes the zero-target Kemeny normalization, establishes strict convexity for every individual symmetric edge probability, reduces by actual graph automorphisms, and reconstructs all nonzero Fourier and quotient eigenvalues. The finite resolvent identity independently checks the leading and subleading exponents. Eventual exact hub saturation follows from a uniform positive-versus-negative Fourier-derivative bound after the optimal leaf probability is shown to vanish. Direct independently assembled Laplacian spectra and the scalar optimizer were checked in 389 finite diagnostics by `verify.py`. The finite cases do not substitute for the infinite proof. The eventual cutoff is not claimed numerically.

## Originality: PASS on inspected evidence, qualified

The 2012 Kemeny identities, 2015 reversible optimization/semidefinite framework, and 2026 reversible perturbation algorithms do not give the quarter-power allocation or helm asymptotics. Published formulas on a wheel alone or a cycle with private leaves alone do not, by implication, cover the combined helm. Searches for helm Kemeny optimization and equivalent wheel-corona allocations found no direct dominating result; the general convex framework is deliberately not claimed new. A substantially related unindexed manuscript remains a realistic priority risk, and absence of search hits is not a novelty proof.

## Value: PASS

The question is an intrinsically motivated design problem on a classical family of graphs with one hub and private terminal demands. The theorem describes a non-obvious distribution of probability mass: exact saturation of hub spokes, asymptotically half-probability rim steps, and an intermediate quarter-power leaf scale. The sharp second-order cost and diverging square-root benchmark gap make the precise allocation relevant rather than an arbitrary numerical slice. The result covers all sufficiently large members of an infinite natural family and is not a routine substitution into previously stated wheel or corona formulas.

## Boundaries

Optimality is for symmetric stochastic, uniformly stationary walks, not degree-weighted or nonreversible chains. Finite assertions corroborate exact formulas but do not establish asymptotics. The finite onset of exact hub saturation is not optimized. Publication priority requires independent literature review beyond searched and inspected sources.
