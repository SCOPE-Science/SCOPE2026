On helm graphs, the unique Kemeny-optimal symmetric stochastic walk eventually saturates every hub spoke while assigning each private-leaf edge a transition probability asymptotic to \(2^{1/4}n^{-1/4}\). Its Kemeny value is \(\sqrt2\,n^{3/2}+2^{3/4}n^{5/4}+o(n^{5/4})\), and the maximum-degree symmetric walk is asymptotically worse by a factor growing like \((1+2/\sqrt5)\sqrt{n/2}\).
