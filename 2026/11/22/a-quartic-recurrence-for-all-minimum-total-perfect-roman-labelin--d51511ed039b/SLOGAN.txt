For the labelled cycle \(C_n\), the previously known minimum total perfect Roman weight is \(n\), but the number of optimal labelings follows \(a_n=a_{n-1}+2a_{n-3}+a_{n-4}\), starting \((7,13,16,31)\) at lengths three through six. Their exact generating function is \((7x^3+6x^4+3x^5+x^6)/(1-x-2x^3-x^4)\), giving exponential growth \(a_n\sim(1.794309659412\ldots)^n\).
