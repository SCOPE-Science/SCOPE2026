The tangent sphere–cylinder intersection has two antipodal diameter pairs away from the Viviani member, yielding a linear rare-diameter law; as they coalesce, the sharp critical window is governed by the quartic region \(U^2+(V^2-\sqrt2\kappa)^2<1\), with a two-component transition at \(\kappa=1/\sqrt2\). The isolated Viviani three-quarter-power law was already known; the pair-merger crossover, its marked total-variation limit, and the fixed-parameter coefficients are the new results.
