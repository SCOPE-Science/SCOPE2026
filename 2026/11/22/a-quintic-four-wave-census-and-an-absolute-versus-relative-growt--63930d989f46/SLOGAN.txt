Four-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on the wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\) has an exact rational generating function for all primitive temporal four-cycle orbits, governed by a quintic growth root \(\lambda\approx2.3352947989\). Relative to the known three-state wheel census, four-phase orbit counts eventually grow faster in absolute number but become exponentially rarer among uniformly random initial configurations.
