For the singular looped-cycle Cayley evolution algebra \(e_i^2=e_i+e_{i+1}\) in characteristic two, every algebra endomorphism is either a cyclic rotation or a rank-at-most-one map sending all generators to the same scalar multiple of their sum. Consequently the finite-field rank polynomial is \(1+(q-1)T+nT^n\), and the unique proper nonzero ideal admits an algebra-complement precisely when the cycle length is odd.
