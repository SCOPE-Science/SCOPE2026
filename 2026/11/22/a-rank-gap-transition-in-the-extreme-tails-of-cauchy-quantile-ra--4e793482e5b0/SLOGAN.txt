For fixed-size Cauchy samples, dividing an interior order-statistic span by the full sample range creates a sharp small-tail transition: adjacent interior ranks have probability asymptotic to a positive constant times \(t\log(1/t)\), whereas ranks separated by at least one intervening observation have probability asymptotic to a positive constant times \(t\). In the four-observation case the opposite extreme tail is asymptotic to \(3t^2/\pi^2\), while the lower-tail constant is \(12/\pi^2\).
