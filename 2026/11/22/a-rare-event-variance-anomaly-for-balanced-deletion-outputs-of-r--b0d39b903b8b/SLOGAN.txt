For a uniformly sampled Dyck word of semilength \(n\), the number of distinct shorter Dyck words obtainable by deleting one up-step and one down-step has Gaussian central fluctuations at scale \(n^{3/2}\), but its variance grows at the larger scale \(n^{7/2}\). The exact leading variance constant is \(\sqrt\pi/256\), generated by rare macroscopic splits of the Dyck excursion.
