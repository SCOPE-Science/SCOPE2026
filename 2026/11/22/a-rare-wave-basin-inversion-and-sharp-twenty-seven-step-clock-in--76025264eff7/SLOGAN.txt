On the twelve-vertex asymmetric Frucht graph, exhaustive four-state Greenberg–Hastings dynamics has only temporal periods \(1,4,5,6\) and reaches recurrence within a sharp 27 updates. Every one of its ten five-cycle attractors has a strictly larger initial-state basin than any of its 1,024 four-cycle attractors, even though the latter vastly outnumber the former.
