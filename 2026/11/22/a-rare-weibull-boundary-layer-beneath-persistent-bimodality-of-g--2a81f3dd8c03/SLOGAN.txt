Under repeated sampling from the 2023 invertible bimodal Gumbel law, the maximum density retains exactly two local modes at every block size, yet its entire negative mode has probability \(e^{-n e^{-\mu/\sigma}}\). Conditional on that exponentially rare negative maximum, a sharply rescaled distance from zero converges monotonically to a Weibull law; the shrinking negative mode and growing positive mode have explicit leading locations and heights.
