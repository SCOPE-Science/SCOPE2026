For random invariant affine-hyperplane cuts of the unit tube around a fixed planar circle in \(\mathbb R^d\), the probability of a contractible section has an exact incomplete-beta law and a unique optimal circle radius. As \(d\to\infty\), this radius grows as \(0.734903908748\ldots\sqrt d\), while the best probability converges to the nontrivial Rayleigh value \(0.792442639800\ldots\), contrasting with spherical-core tubes whose optimal success tends to one.
