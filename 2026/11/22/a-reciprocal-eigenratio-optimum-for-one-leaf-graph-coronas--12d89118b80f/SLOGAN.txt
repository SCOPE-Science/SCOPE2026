For the one-leaf corona of every connected vertex- and edge-transitive core, the globally smallest positive Laplacian eigenratio is attained by original-edge to pendant-edge conductance ratio \(2/\sqrt{\lambda_2(L_H)\lambda_{\max}(L_H)}\), independent of price ratios; for cyclic cores the allocation is globally unique even against asymmetry. On large sunlets this ratio is asymptotically half that of the previously established spectral-gap-optimal allocation, giving distinct optimal designs for two natural dynamical criteria.
