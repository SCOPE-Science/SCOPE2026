For \(n\ge2\), removing any nonempty proper edge set from \(K_{n,n}\) leaves the \((n-1)\)-matching complex homotopy equivalent to a sphere exactly when the removed edges fill a complete row-column rectangle; every other fault pattern makes the complex a simplicial cone. A deleted \(a\times b\) rectangle lowers the intact sphere dimension by \((a-1)(b-1)\).
