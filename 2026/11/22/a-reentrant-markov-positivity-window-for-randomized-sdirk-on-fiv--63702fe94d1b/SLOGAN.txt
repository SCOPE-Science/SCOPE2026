A randomized algebraically A-stable two-stage SDIRK step has a sharp **reentrant** probability-one Markov-positivity window \(1/3\le h\le2\) on a five-state absorbing birth chain, yet only half its tableau draws remain positive as \(h\downarrow0\). The negative transition persists even after averaging: the mean opposite-corner probability is \(-h^4/16+O(h^5)\).
