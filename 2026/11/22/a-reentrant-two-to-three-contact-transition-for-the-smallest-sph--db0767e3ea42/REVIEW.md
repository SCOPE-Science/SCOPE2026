Numerical tests do not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)

Actual meaning-level comparisons were made against the published-finding corpus record *Two and three contact points for minimum enclosing spheres of symmetric moment-curve hulls* (published November 8, 2026), whose polynomial curve \((t,t^2,\ldots,t^d)\) is not the circular helix and whose formulas do not imply the present threshold. Jahn's 2014 full text treats algorithms for minimal enclosing disks of finite points in normed planes, not the current continuum problem in three dimensions. Chan–Pathak (2014) concerns approximate algorithmic bounding balls of finite point sets, not this explicit reentrant contact classification. Search and published-finding corpus retrieval did not identify a dominating theorem; that absence is not proof of worldwide novelty. The specialized historical-priority risk remains.

The entire continuous helical arc has a natural containment optimization, whose contact structure and radius now have exact all-parameter expressions through one turn. The nonmonotone two/three/two-contact progression at a fixed intermediate pitch is structurally surprising and cannot be read off from generic ball-center existence/algorithm results. The classification has exact critical pitches and a sharp \(C^1\)-but-not-\(C^2\) radius boundary, furnishing a precise geometric benchmark for enclosing three-dimensional filaments.

## Limitations and closest literature
