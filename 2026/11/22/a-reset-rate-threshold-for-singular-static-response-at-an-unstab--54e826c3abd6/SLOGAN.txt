For deterministic quartic double-well flow reset at its unstable origin, the stationary response to a weak constant field changes sharply from fractional-power to logarithmic to linear as the reset rate crosses the local instability exponent. The fractional-power amplitude is an exact beta integral, and the transition has a universal \((1-e^{-\lambda})/\lambda\) crossover profile when the reset-rate detuning is of order \(1/\log(1/|h|)\).
