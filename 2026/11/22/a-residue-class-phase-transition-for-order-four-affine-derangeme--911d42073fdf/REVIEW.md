# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every affine element of order dividing four has unipotent linear part and translation in the kernel of its third nilpotent power. A fixed point exists exactly when the translation lies in the nilpotent image; the two condition counts yield the finite formula. Standard nilpotent centralizer orders count all Jordan strata, subtraction removes lower-order elements, and Gaussian domination justifies all limiting operations. Balancing the column lengths gives the four large-field asymptotics. The standalone bounded checker passed every prescribed finite configuration, but is not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The finite rational-canonical counts are already implicit in Jason Fulman's complete affine cycle-index theory (2000), and Spiga (2017) explicitly derives a broad formula for all prime-power-order affine derangements. The present claim is limited to **fixed exact order four**, **large dimension along the four residue classes**, and the **large-field phase split**, none explicitly identified in the material that was inspected. This includes comparisons to affine large-order classifications (Guest et al., 2013), a fixed-point-free involution result for an affine partial inverse monoid, and an exponent-four core-quandle result. Because the full Spiga paper was not accessible, an uninspected exact-order-four specialization may already exist; this priority risk is substantive.

## Value — PASS
The opposite asymptotic regimes constitute a sharp structural boundary: conditioned on order four, almost all transformations are fixed-point-free in three dimension residue classes but almost none are in the fourth, after large dimension and then large field. This is not visible from an unconditional affine derangement proportion or single fixed-order count and has natural applications to cycle structure of bounded-exponent affine permutations.

## Limitations
The two limits are taken in the stated order. Finite counts and underlying cycle index formulas are not asserted original. The most directly related paper was accessible only through bibliographic data and abstract. Source-comparison uncertainty prevents a definitive historical priority claim. No external validation was performed.
