Among order-four affine transformations in even characteristic, the large-dimension derangement probability has four convergent theta-series limits distinguished by dimension modulo four. In the subsequent large-field limit, it is \(q^{-1}+O(q^{-2})\) in dimension \(0\pmod4\) but \(1-q^{-1}+O(q^{-2})\) in the other three residue classes.
