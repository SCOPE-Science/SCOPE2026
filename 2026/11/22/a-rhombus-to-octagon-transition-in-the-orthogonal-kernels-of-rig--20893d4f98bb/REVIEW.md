# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On an x-side triangle the projected height satisfies 1−z′/h=(sτ±ax)/(s+a²), with τ=1−z/h; the in-plane y-coordinate is unchanged. For an interior test point the projected height strictly increases, so z′>0, and the other strict triangle conditions give the claimed inequality after taking both signs. Exchanging x,y gives the other inequality. Together they imply |x|/a,|y|/b<τ and a|x|+b|y|<sτ: at least one of a²≤s+b² and b²≤s+a² always holds. These bound the segment projection parameter away from both endpoints on every apex edge, and imply the base conditions. In the normalized first quadrant the two halfplanes share their positive right-hand side s. For a²=L≥M=b², the second dominates when s≤L−M; for s>L−M both are active with intersection ((s+d)/(s+L+M),(s−d)/(s+L+M)). At equality this intersection coincides with the axis intercept and is not an extra vertex. Homothety in height cancels the common integral of τ² against the original pyramid volume; the quadrant areas give the displayed fraction. At d>0 the positive-part correction has leading coefficient d/(4L³), so the second derivatives differ. With fixed p=ab, the rhombic fraction is strictly decreasing in q=L/p, and direct differentiation of the octagonal fraction in t=q+1/q gives the displayed negative derivative. The formulas join continuously, so q=1 uniquely maximizes, including the case of arbitrarily small positive height.
