For \(K\geq0\) nested equivalence relations, all realizable ordered quantifier-free three-variable types coexist in one structure on exactly \(2K+3\) points, and every smallest such structure is the same ternary comb up to isomorphism. There are exactly \(1+4(K+1)+3\binom{K+1}{2}\) types, so this comb is a sharp finite test structure for the purely existential three-variable quantifier-free fragment.
