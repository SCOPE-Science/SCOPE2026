For an arrangement of \(2m\) lines with an \(m\)-double-point transversal, fewer than \(m\) singular points per arrangement line, and two distinct single-double-point lines, the transversal is the *only* integral curve computing the multipoint Seshadri constant \(1/m\). Applied to the characteristic-seven sixteen-line counterexample, this makes the boundary divisor \(8H-\sum E_P\) big and nef with a single null curve of self-intersection \(-7\), rather than merely identifying its numerical threshold.
