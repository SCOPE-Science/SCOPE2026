For any two polynomial sequences with only simple roots modulo an odd prime, the complete joint \(p\)-adic valuation distribution depends solely on the depths at which their Hensel roots coincide. For pairs of quadratics with two simple roots each, this gives a sharp covariance-sign trichotomy: zero shared residue roots means negative covariance, two means positive, and one means negative only at \(p=3\) (with equality for an exactly shared root).
