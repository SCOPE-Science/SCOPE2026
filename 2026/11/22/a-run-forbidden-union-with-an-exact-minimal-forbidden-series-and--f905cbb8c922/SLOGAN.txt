For the binary subshift formed by uniting the shifts that separately forbid \(0^m\) and \(1^m\), the exact minimal-forbidden series is \(2z^{2m}/[1-(z+\cdots+z^{m-1})^2]\). Its minimal-forbidden entropy equals the entropy of the intersection of the two phases, while its topological entropy is strictly larger and exactly two Bernoulli ergodic maximal-entropy measures coexist; the gap decays as \(2^{-m-1}\).
