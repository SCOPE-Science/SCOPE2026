On every wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), \(m\ge3\), the uniquely Kemeny-optimal symmetric stochastic walk saturates every hub spoke at probability \(1/m\), assigns \((m-1)/(2m)\) to every rim edge and has no holding, even against unrestricted asymmetric edge assignments. Its Kemeny constant is asymptotic to \(m^{3/2}/\sqrt2\), while the maximum-degree walk pays an unbounded relative penalty of \(\sqrt{2m/5}\).
