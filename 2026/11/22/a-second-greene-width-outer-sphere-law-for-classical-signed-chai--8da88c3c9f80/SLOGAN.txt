For the ordinary-poset specialization of signed-chain polytopes, the unique minimum enclosing sphere has radius \(\sqrt{d_2(P)}\), where \(d_2(P)\) is the maximum size of a union of two antichains. The signed-chain to enriched-chain enclosing-radius ratio lies sharply between \(1\) and \(\sqrt2\), with exact equality criteria. Every contact is a signed indicator of an optimally large two-antichain family, with exactly \(\sum_U2^{c(U)}\) contacts when the sum ranges over maximizing supports and \(c(U)\) counts their comparability components.
