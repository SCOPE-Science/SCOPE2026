For the least periods of consecutive-integer product-to-LCM quotients, the windows with a shortened period exhibit a universal, explicit coprimality bias of order \(X/\log X\). The coefficient \(C_q=(\varphi(q)/q)\sum_{p\mid q}\log p/(p-1)-1+\varphi(q)/q\) is negative at modulus two and positive at modulus three, despite the same leading large-prime-factor density.
