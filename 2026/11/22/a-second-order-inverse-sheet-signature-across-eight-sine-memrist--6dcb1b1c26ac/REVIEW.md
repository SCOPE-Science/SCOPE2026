## Originality

**PASS within inspected evidence.** Yihyis et al. (2023, doi:10.3390/sym15081574) derive the eight model equations but study numerical chaos, not inverse-branch asymptotics. A prior calculation for the same quadratic-charge-only model already gives its cubic inverse count and a sharper lower-order term, and that fact is treated as background. The new claim is the eight-architecture comparison, especially the positive quadratic correction in the two published quadratic-plus-absolute systems and the different leading constants of the bounded periodic choices. Full relevant related-map results were also inspected: a cosine-Chialvo preimage lattice with **linear** growth on the positive quadrant and a coupled Gumowski–Mira system with finite inverse valence. General critical-curve theory does not by itself evaluate the model-specific coefficients. Search and access limitations leave residual unindexed-priority risk.

## Value

**PASS.** The originating study explicitly compares the influence of memristor **type** versus **number** using forward numerical chaos diagnostics. Its real one-step inverse ambiguity is a distinct, natural quantitative observable: a bounded current window gives a finite, reconstructible predecessor count for each fixed output. The sharp second-order term detects an absolute-charge component even when the cubic leading count cannot distinguish the mixtures, and the periodic constants give a non-obvious inverse-complexity ranking. The result neither asserts secure pseudorandomness nor claims that inverse multiplicity controls forward chaos.
