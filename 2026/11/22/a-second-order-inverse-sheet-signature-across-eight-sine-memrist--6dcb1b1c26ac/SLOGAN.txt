For eight published sine-memristor maps, real one-step predecessor counts grow quadratically with absolute, sinusoidal or exponential memory but cubically with quadratic-charge memory, with sharp constants for every output level. Adding absolute-value memory to either published quadratic-charge mixture creates a positive, target-independent \(R^2\) correction absent from the exponential-only mixture, while the exponential single-memory design has strictly more leading-order inverse branches than the absolute and sinusoidal designs.
