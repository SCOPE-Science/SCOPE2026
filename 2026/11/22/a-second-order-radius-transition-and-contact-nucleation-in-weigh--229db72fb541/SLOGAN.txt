Weighted complete-split coordinate-disc sums have an exact Euclidean circumradius with a sharp second-order transition at \(\tau_c=(a-1)/(\sqrt2(a-b))\) when \(b<a\). At this threshold the number of sphere contacts jumps from \(2^a\) to \(2^{a+b}\), with square-root activation of every previously absent independent-class coordinate.
