For two independent rooted Yule–Harding–Kingman phylogenies on the same \(n\) taxa, the chance of a shared proper clade larger than a cherry is \(1/(6n)+1259/(1800n^2)+O(n^{-3})\). Conditional on such a coincidence, with probability \(1-O(n^{-1})\) it is a unique three-taxon clade, whose labels are uniformly distributed.
