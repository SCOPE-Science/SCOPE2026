For a wheel with \(m\) rim vertices and a fixed conductance budget, the unique globally optimal rim allocation for squared-inverse Laplacian coherence exceeds the ordinary inverse-trace optimum at \(m=4,5,6\), but falls below it for every \(m\ge7\) (and ties at \(m=3\)). The rim-budget fractions converge to \((\sqrt7-2)/3\) and \(1/3\), respectively, leaving a persistent approximately 4.59% second-order coherence cost if the first-order design is reused.
