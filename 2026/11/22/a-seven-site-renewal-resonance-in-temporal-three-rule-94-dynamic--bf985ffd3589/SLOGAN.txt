Rule 94 has primitive temporal three-cycles on indexed binary rings exactly at circumference \(7\) and at every circumference \(n\ge9\), with the sole post-onset hole at \(8\). Their exact spatial generating function is \(z^7(7-5z^2-4z^3)/[(1-z^2-z^3)(1-z^2-z^3-3z^7)]\), and the orbit population grows as \(\lambda^n/3\) for \(\lambda=1.4611264371\ldots\).
