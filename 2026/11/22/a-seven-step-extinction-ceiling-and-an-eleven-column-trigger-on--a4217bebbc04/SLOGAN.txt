For three-state, two-contact Greenberg–Hastings dynamics on every open three-row square strip, all activity dies by the seventh update, with the sharp maximum extinction time jumping through \(3,4,5,6,7\) as the column count crosses \(3,5,7,11\). Exactly \(1{,}265{,}544\) initial states on eleven columns attain seven steps, and this maximal delay occurs with probability tending to one on longer random strips despite uniform finite-time extinction.
