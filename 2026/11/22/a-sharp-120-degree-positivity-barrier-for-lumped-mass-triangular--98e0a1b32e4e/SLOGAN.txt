On a single mass-lumped obtuse isosceles triangle, Crank–Nicolson heat stepping preserves all nonnegative nodal data at some positive timestep if and only if the apex angle does not exceed \(120^\circ\); the exact admissible interval is \(2(1-h^2)/3\le\tau\le4h^2/3\). Backward Euler always recovers positivity for sufficiently large steps, while the exact semidiscrete heat propagator has one unique positive recovery time.
