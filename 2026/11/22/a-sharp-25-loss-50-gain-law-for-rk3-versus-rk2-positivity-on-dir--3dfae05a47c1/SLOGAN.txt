On every directed Markov cycle with at least four states and arbitrary positive rates, the exact endpoint-positivity timestep ratio between three-stage RK3 and two-stage RK2 lies strictly between \(3/4\) and \(3/2\), with both limits sharp. A two-/three-consecutive-rate criterion determines which order wins, and a single fast state produces an exact contrast switch at \(\rho/(2-\rho)=3.951373\ldots\).
