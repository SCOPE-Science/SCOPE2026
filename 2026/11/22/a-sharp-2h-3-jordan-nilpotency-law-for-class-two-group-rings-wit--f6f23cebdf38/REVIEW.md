The entire central ideal \((2,1+s)\) in \((\mathbb Z/2^h)[C_4]\) has zero power \(2h+2\), using a proved integer binomial identity. A pair with commutator generating \(C_4\) gives an explicit product of length \(2h+2\) whose central coefficient does not vanish, even in an arbitrary ring of exact characteristic \(2^h\). Exact computations for \(1\le h\le8\) and a concrete 64-element group support the proof but are not an infinite certificate.
