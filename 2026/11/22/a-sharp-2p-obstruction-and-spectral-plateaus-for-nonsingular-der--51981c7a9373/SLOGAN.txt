For the two-generated free nilpotent Lie algebra of class \(c\) over \(\mathbb F_{p^f}\), nonsingular derivations exist exactly when \(c<2p\); at class \(2p\), an unavoidable free-Lie multidegree forces a zero eigenvalue. Their complete finite-field count is controlled by a finite set of forbidden projective eigenvalue ratios, and the nonsingular density is constant for every \(p\le c<2p\) before collapsing to zero.
