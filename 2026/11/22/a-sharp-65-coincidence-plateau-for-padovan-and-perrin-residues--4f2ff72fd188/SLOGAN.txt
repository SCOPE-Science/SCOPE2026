The number of matching-index congruences \(P_n\equiv R_n\pmod{2^k}\) between the classical Padovan and Perrin sequences, counted in one least period, grows in powers of two but stabilizes sharply at **65 for every \(k\ge12\)**. A 2-adic isometry after removing an exact linear factor uncovers a second 2-adic coincidence root at distance \(2^{-5}\) from the known negative-index equality.
