For one-turn unit-radius circular helices, three independent arclength-uniform points can form an acute Euclidean triangle exactly when the axial slope is below a unique sharp threshold \(c_*=0.6874374448\ldots\). At extinction the probability has an explicit strictly positive *linear* onset in \(c_*^2-c^2\), and conditional gap configurations converge to a computable uniform ellipse.
