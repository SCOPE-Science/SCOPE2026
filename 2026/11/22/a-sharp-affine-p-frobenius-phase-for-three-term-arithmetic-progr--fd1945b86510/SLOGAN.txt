For every coprime three-term arithmetic progression \((a,a+d,a+2d)\) with \(a\ge5\) and \(2\le d\le\lfloor a/2\rfloor\), the generalized Frobenius number is exactly \(g_p=a(p+\lfloor a/2\rfloor-1)+(2a-1)d\) throughout \(\lfloor a/2\rfloor+2\le p\le a+d\). At the immediately following level, this affine law fails by the sharp positive deficit \(a-2d\).
