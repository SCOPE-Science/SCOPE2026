In threshold-triggered Brownian drift–diffusion, the mean age of the current excursion conditioned on observed position has its unique minimum precisely at the reinjection point. The stationary position–age covariance changes sign exactly at \(\mathrm{Pe}=\sqrt3\), while position–remaining-life covariance is always positive; their difference is the diffusivity-independent value \(L^2/(6v)\).
