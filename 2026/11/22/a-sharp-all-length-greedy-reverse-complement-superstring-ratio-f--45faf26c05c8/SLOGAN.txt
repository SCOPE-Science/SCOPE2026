For every \(k\ge3\), the sharp worst-tie greedy reverse-complement superstring approximation ratio for exactly three distinct binary length-\(k\) read classes is \(2k/(k+2)\). The three classes of \(0^k\), \(0^{k-1}1\), and \(10^{k-1}\) attain the bound, showing that the worst ratio approaches two with only three reads.
