# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof computes the exact positive two-by-two monodromy trace and determinant. Strict convexity of a hyperbolic-cosine function in the squared spectral gap proves the sign comparison for every positive period, not merely a perturbation of fast cycling. The long-period expansion and admissible counterexample follow from exact eigenvalues and rational radical bounds. The included checker tests the symbolic relations and 500 sampled exponential products. Finite tests do not supply the universal argument.

## Originality — PASS, bounded by accessible literature

The initiating Gunnarsson manuscript derives and optimizes an infinitely rapid-cycle averaged generator. The new claim classifies every finite period and provides a strict growth/decline reversal. Clairambault, Gaubert and Perthame (2007) use an arithmetico-geometric average rather than this arithmetic comparator. Patra and Klumpp (2015) is relevant to phenotype switching under periodic drugs but its complete text was not verified; historical priority risk is explicitly retained. Targeted published-record searches gave no match.

## Value — PASS

The finite dosing cycle is a biologically motivated departure from the explicit fast-cycling assumption. The exact sign criterion decides whether the approximation is systematically optimistic or pessimistic at any period, and one fully admissible Michaelis–Menten example reverses the long-term growth verdict. It does not establish clinical treatment safety or nonlinear tumor clearance.
