Even the homogeneous three-spin zero-field Ising chain becomes a thermal diode when one reservoir controls its center and the other controls both endpoints: exchanging hot and cold reservoirs changes the stationary heat current by a factor strictly between \(1\) and \(1+4/\sqrt{15}\). The upper bound is sharp only as the cold reservoir approaches zero temperature, while a homogeneous two-spin chain has no such rectification.
