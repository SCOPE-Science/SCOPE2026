In an ideal three-counter Poisson model of a Gaussian pollutant pulse, the unique time maximizing local source-position information moves later as an unknown ambient background rises, even though unknown source strength and background cause no location-information penalty at the symmetric nominal position. The sharp ratio between high- and zero-background optimal times is at most \(5/3\), with equality at zero chemical loss, and the optimum always precedes the wing-concentration peak.
