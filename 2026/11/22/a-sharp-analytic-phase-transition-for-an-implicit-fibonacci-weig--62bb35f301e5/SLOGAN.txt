The implicit exponential series \(A_q(x)=\exp(\sum_{m\ge1}x^m(q^m+A_q(x))^m/m)\) has an exact analytic phase transition: \(q=0,1\) are algebraic, whereas every integer \(q\ge2\) has radius zero and is non-D-finite. On the nonanalytic side its coefficients satisfy \(\log a_n(q)=n^2\log q+O(n)\), giving an exact quadratic logarithmic growth constant.
