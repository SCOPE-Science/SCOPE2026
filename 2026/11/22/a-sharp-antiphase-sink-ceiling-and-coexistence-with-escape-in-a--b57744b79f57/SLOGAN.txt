For the symmetric Gaussian-cubic coupled map, nonzero anti-diagonal fixed points admit an exact Lambert-\(W\) census, and a full-plane attracting antiphase equilibrium exists for some coupling if and only if \(0<a<e^2\). Near their saddle-node birth there are two distinct sinks coexisting with an open set of escaping trajectories, and at \(a=e^2\) the fold has the simultaneous multipliers \(1\) and \(-1\).
