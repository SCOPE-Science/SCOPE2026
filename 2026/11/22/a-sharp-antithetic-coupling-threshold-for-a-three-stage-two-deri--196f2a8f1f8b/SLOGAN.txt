For a published three-stage fourth-order two-derivative Runge–Kutta rule on the harmonic oscillator, unbiased two-point curvature errors can create mean-square contraction only when their squared relative amplitude exceeds \(1/3\) and their stage signs are sufficiently antithetic. Perfect antithetic pairing uniquely minimizes energy amplification at every step, with the sharp coupling frontier \(p<(3\epsilon^2-1)/(10\epsilon^2)\); independent or shared signs never stabilize a positive step.
