For every fixed \(\ell\ge3\), the first part occurring fewer than \(r\) times in a uniform \(\ell\)-regular partition is asymptotically equidistributed among the permitted residues, but the exact first correction is \((\ell-2s)C_{\ell,r}n^{-1/4}\) in residue \(s\). Consequently the residue probabilities are eventually strictly decreasing from \(1\) through \(\ell-1\), with a sharp \(n^{-1/4}\) gap.
