For any two distinct integer sine frequencies, the sharp simultaneous expected-value bound is exactly \(\cos(\pi/(2S))\), \(\cos(\pi/S)\), or \(1\), according to \(S\) modulo four, where \(S\) is the sum of the coprime reduced frequencies. Every maximizing measure is supported on exactly one or two contact phases per common-frequency period, and in the nontrivial cases the optimal two-term majorant coefficients are uniquely determined.
