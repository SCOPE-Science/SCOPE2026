# Verification record

The proof is mathematical and finite-dimensional. Its critical steps are the reduction to two positive fixed-point systems, uniqueness and positive-branch continuation of strictly concave maps, the two-dimensional Perron vector row-sum sign test, and two exact matrix row-sum identities. Every assertion about all admissible parameters is proved algebraically, not inferred from numerical tests.

A standalone `verify.py` checks the exact identities and a numerical sample of the two fixed-point systems. Execute it directly with Python, SymPy, and NumPy. The test cases supplement, but do not replace, the proof. The source model's early branching limit does not equal a finite-population probability of a major outbreak, and the deterministic final-size branch is conditional on a macroscopic outbreak. Incubation times cancel only because no deaths/removals occur in the exposed phase.
