The mixed forward–reverse Hardy averages \(HR\) and \(RH\) have the same sharp real \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) approximation-error coefficient, \(0.410507498717943\ldots\), with all nonconstant extremizers given by a uniquely located single jump (reflected between the two orders). This exceeds the sharp one-step Hardy coefficient \(1/e\): a second, oppositely directed average makes worst-case integrated approximation strictly worse.
