Among all two disjoint circular arcs of total angular length \(4a\), the sharp worst-observed energy of five consecutive Fourier modes is \(1024a^5/(135\pi)+o(a^5)\) as \(a\downarrow0\). Near-optimal arcs must be almost antipodal and asymptotically unequal in width: the narrower arc uses a vanishing share of the observation budget, improving the equal-width leading constant by a factor of sixteen.
