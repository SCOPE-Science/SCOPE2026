For classical Bernstein–Bézier sampling of real bounded-variation signals, the sharp integrated error has a complete Gaussian-limit parameter classification: every fixed \(1<\alpha<2\) improves the classical leading constant, while every parameter outside \([1,2]\) worsens it. A unique optimum at \(\alpha\approx1.405429\) reduces the limiting error by about \(5.06\%\), despite increasing the exact degree-one error.
