For a single backward-Euler heat step on the circle, the exact sharp real \(BV\)-variation-to-\(L^2\) error undergoes a unique geometric transition at \(t_c=0.028063207218264555\ldots\): worst arcs grow from length \(2\sqrt t\) at short time to exactly one half at and after \(t_c\). All nonconstant extremizers are arc indicators, and the approach to the semicircle has a sharp square-root law.
