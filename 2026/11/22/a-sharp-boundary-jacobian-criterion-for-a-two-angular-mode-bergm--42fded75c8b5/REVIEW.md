# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The two shifts have exactly computed normalized monomial weights. Their self-commutator has diagonal \(D_n+t^2E_n\) and third off-diagonal \(tO_n\) with all formulas stated in the proof. At \(t=2/3\), three finite boundary rows have explicit positive margins and all subsequent rows have the exact positive rational lower bound with shifted polynomial coefficients \(13,235,1502,3896,3192\). A bounded band quadratic form extends finite-support positivity by density. Beyond the threshold, alternating chains provide negative quadratic form witnesses with a strictly negative calculated asymptotic coefficient. Both degenerate coefficient cases and endpoint equality are covered. The trace and boundary-Jacobian formulas follow from exact telescoping and differentiation. Supplementary computations are not treated as proofs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Fleeman–Liaw (arXiv:1707.09677, journal Theorem 9) provides general sufficient row comparisons for opposite-angular binomials, including these exponents, but no evaluated exact threshold or if-and-only-if result in the inspected material. The present rational all-index endpoint certificate is needed for the sharp explicit boundary. Kim–Lee (2021) Theorem 2.9 treats the matching index pattern but supplies necessary estimates, not the coefficient equivalence. The 2022 weighted paper and 2023 follow-up investigate distinct restrictions and general sufficient statements. General boundary Jacobian necessity or abstract band-matrix positivity does not alone establish the converse for this mixed polynomial. The exact-threshold specialization could potentially be latent as an unstated corollary of Fleeman–Liaw's general test; this is an explicit historical-priority risk, not evidence of known identical coverage.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Mixed analytic and opposite-angular nonharmonic Bergman symbols are a specifically motivated classification problem. The theorem identifies the complete coefficient phase boundary, including equality, for the simplest degree-two analytic and degree-minus-one nonharmonic pair. It exhibits an exact boundary-Jacobian criterion in a setting where such a condition is generally only necessary, and quantifies why diagonal moment tests alone cannot settle the result. A natural exact classification with explicit infinite-dimensional necessity and sufficiency is valuable without a general theorem for arbitrary degrees.

## Checked literature and limits

Inspected the relevant full-text theorem statements, proof regions and examples in Fleeman–Liaw (2017/2019), Kim–Lee (2021), Kim–Lee (2022), and the 2023 follow-up. The classified family is unweighted only; no statement about all weighted Bergman spaces or higher-degree exponents is asserted. A non-indexed equivalent and an implicit specialization of a general published sufficient theorem remain genuine priority risks; further independent checking is desirable.
