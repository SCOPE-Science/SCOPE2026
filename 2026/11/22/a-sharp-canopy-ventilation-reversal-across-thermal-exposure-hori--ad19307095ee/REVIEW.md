# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The scalar initial-value problem yields the positive-kernel exposure formula for every integer integration order. The integrand's logarithm in logarithmic time and attenuation coordinates is jointly concave, so Prékopa marginal log-concavity applies. Analyticity and distinct limiting slopes make this strict, implying strict log-concavity in canopy intensity and monotone elasticity. Exact endpoint-ratio derivatives establish one crossing when heat attenuation is weaker than ventilation attenuation. Direct derivative signs locate the interior strict maximum and show all endpoint and equality cases. `verify.py` passed 3,642 numerical regression assertions; the proof, not the finite tests, establishes the universal quantifiers.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, historical priority not certified

The full Zhao et al. arXiv article (arXiv:2301.05766), the accessible Liang et al. author manuscript, and the public Sharmin et al. article cover tree-mediated ventilation, shading, microclimate, and more elaborate numerical or observational models, but the inspected sections do not state the strict all-integration-order log-concavity, unique horizon crossing, or complete moving-worst-intensity classification in the stipulated scalar model. The classical Prékopa theorem and related applications to statistical design are acknowledged as mathematically prior methods. Searches of mathematical research records for the model, its analytic equivalent formulations, and comparison parameters found no clear dominating result; absence from those records alone does not prove novelty. Potential broader applied optimization results and inaccessible full texts remain a residual priority risk.

## Value — PASS

A simple, motivated conflict between decreased solar forcing and reduced air exchange produces a non-obvious, sharp, testable three-regime hazard geometry and a single reversal in optimal exposure-horizon decisions. Both terminal temperature and cumulative heat burden obey the same structural theorem but have different numerical crossover horizons. The result is a complete model benchmark, not merely a formula reparameterization. Its positive theoretical value is independent of empirical calibration, and it makes no management prescription.

## Scientific limits

The result requires a zero initial excess temperature, a fixed canopy intensity, constant heat input, exponential canopy attenuation laws, and a linear one-zone first-order loss model. Physical canopy heat exchange and social welfare are more complex. Neither historical priority nor empirical predictive validity is certified.
