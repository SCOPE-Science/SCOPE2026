For a one-zone heat model in which increasing canopy attenuates both solar input and ventilation exponentially, every finite-horizon heat-exposure integral is strictly log-concave in canopy intensity, so its minimum is attained only at zero or maximum canopy. If ventilation is suppressed more strongly than heat input, the preferred endpoint reverses exactly once as the observation horizon grows, while the worst intermediate canopy passes through a unique interior maximum in a sharp horizon window.
