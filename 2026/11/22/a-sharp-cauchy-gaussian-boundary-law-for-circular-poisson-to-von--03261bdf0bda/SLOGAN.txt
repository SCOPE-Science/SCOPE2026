For a highly concentrated circular Poisson kernel, every best single von-Mises \(L^2\) approximant has concentration asymptotic to \(0.517719742233/(1-r)^2\), and the sharp squared error is asymptotic to \(0.0705689390522/(1-r)\). Matching the first circular moment instead is asymptotically 14.17054 times worse in squared error.
