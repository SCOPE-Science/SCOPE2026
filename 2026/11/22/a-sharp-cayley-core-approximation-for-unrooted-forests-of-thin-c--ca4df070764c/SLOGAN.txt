For a uniform unrooted spanning forest of \(K_{a,b}\) with fixed \(a\ge2\), an exactly uniform Cayley tree on the small shore, subdivided by \(a-1\) large-shore vertices and decorated by independent isolated-or-leaf choices, approximates the complete forest law at sharp total-variation distance \(\kappa_a/b+O_a(b^{-2})\). Consequently one giant component contains asymptotically the fraction \(a/(a+1)\) of the large shore, while its isolated complement obeys a binomial Gaussian law.
