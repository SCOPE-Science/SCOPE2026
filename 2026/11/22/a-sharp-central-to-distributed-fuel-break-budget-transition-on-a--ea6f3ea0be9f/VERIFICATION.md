The exact pair-connectivity identity, reflection-symmetry reduction, positivity of the second derivative and monotonic cubic reconstruction form the analytic proof. `check.py` verifies 125 rational probability triples by exhaustive enumeration of all eight bond states and four possible ignition sites, and a fixed-seed parameter sample checks that the analytic design is no worse than randomly sampled feasible designs. Rational witness values are checked without floating-point approximation. Numerical testing supplements rather than replaces the infinite proof.

No field data, noise-robustness theorem, external review or independent validation is included.
