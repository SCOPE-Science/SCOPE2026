A balanced two-stage ecological community can have zero aggregate interaction between every species pair while its coexistence equilibrium changes from stable to unstable. The exact finite-demography stability frontier is determined by the real and imaginary parts of the species-network eigenvalues; a smallest, four-species matched-strength example stays stable under reciprocal signed interactions but loses stability under circulating signed interactions exactly at \(g=\sqrt\Delta\). A second four-species balanced cycle shows that a genuinely oscillatory linear instability is possible, whereas every smaller balanced zero-diagonal network has only zero-frequency critical crossings.
