In an exact negative-binomial scramble-recruitment model, spatial clustering is never beneficial at fixed mean when crowding \(z\leq2\), but for \(z>2\) one and only one finite clustering strength maximizes expected recruits. The optimum follows a sharp \(4/[3(z-2)]\) onset and \(1/\log z\) strong-crowding law, while every two fixed clustering strengths reverse their recruitment ranking exactly once as crowding increases.
