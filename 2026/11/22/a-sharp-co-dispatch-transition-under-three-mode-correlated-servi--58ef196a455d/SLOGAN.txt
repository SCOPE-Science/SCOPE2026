With one guaranteed departure and three correlated optional departures, positive probability that all three optional vehicles operate does not necessarily justify spreading their times: the first two are optimally co-dispatched exactly when \(uv\geq w(u+v+w)\) in the stated four-mode model. Equal probabilities of the two partial-service modes give the exact all-service transition \(w_c=3-2\sqrt2\), and all optimal gap fractions are explicit.
