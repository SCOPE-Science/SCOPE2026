# Scientific assessment: sharp refuge-feedback coexistence wedge

## Correctness — PASS

The source reaction terms specialize exactly to the displayed two-dimensional ODE, and their strictly positive denominators permit a reversible elimination of the predator nullcline. The resulting quadratic provides exhaustive coexistence counts, including the strict and equal discriminant boundaries. The determinant identity, negative trace in the sub-invasion wedge, and nondegenerate fold derivatives establish all claimed local stability types. Rational example Jacobians and the standalone checker agree with the analytical proof. No claim concerning spatial stability is made.

## Originality — PASS, with literature risk

The main 2026 source derives a nonmonotonic carrying capacity but leaves equilibria and stability open. Its formula alone does not imply the sharp condition for coexistence when invasion is unfavorable: one must couple prey growth to the predator nullcline. The full 2017 Conforto–Desvillettes–Soresina manuscript has prescribed logistic prey growth instead of the same endogenous active/refuge competition. The full accessible 2024 Hanifah–Abadi work has independent environmental carrying-capacity dynamics and a static protection fraction, rather than the two-state refuge feedback. Generic bistability is known, and the complete 2019 Desvillettes–Soresina manuscript was not available for full-text comparison. Priority is therefore limited to the exact model-specific classification against examined sources, not to the general phenomenon.

## Value — PASS

The originating model explicitly raises equilibrium and stability analysis as future work. This classification gives a sharp invasion-versus-persistence distinction: predator-free stability does not exclude a second attracting coexistence state, provided refuge-induced competitive release is sufficiently strong. The precise threshold \(\lambda_c=2(\sqrt2-1)\), fold curve, and exact rational bistability example characterize where initial-condition dependence matters without claiming an empirical intervention rule.

## Limits

Only spatially homogeneous reaction dynamics and local stability to homogeneous perturbations are covered. No full-PDE stability, spatially patterned state, attraction-basin boundary, global convergence or independent literature audit is claimed. The 2019 related cross-diffusion paper remains an incomplete full-text comparison.
