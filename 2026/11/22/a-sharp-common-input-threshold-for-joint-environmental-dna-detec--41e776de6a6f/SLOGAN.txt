In an idealized two-target environmental-DNA assay with one inhibition-sensitive and one inhibition-resistant Poisson detection process, the unique common input maximizing detection of both is always larger than the inhibited target's individual optimum \(1/b\). The optimum exceeds twice that dose exactly when the resistant target's abundance coefficient is smaller than \(Ae^{-2}\), giving a sharp and experimentally interpretable ratio boundary within the stated model.
