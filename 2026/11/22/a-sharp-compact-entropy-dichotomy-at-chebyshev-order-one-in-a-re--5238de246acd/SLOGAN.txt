For the Jiang--Jiang--Wang--Shu reciprocal-sine plane map, Chebyshev order \(\nu=1\) has zero entropy on every compact invariant set and only atomic periodic invariant measures. Every other order \(\nu\in[0,4]\) supports compact diagonal full shifts on arbitrarily many symbols with both planar directions uniformly expanding, so the supremum of compact-invariant entropies is infinite.
