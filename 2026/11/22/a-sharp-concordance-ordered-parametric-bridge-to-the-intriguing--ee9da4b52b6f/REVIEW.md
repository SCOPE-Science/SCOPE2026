# Review of the exponent-interpolated intriguing-1 copula theorem

## Correctness (C): PASS

The formula retains continuous uniform margins for every real parameter. An explicit mixed-partial decomposition gives a strictly positive lower bound over the complete open square when \(0\le\gamma\le1\); the two excluded parameter half-lines each create negative mixed density via analytically evaluated boundary limits. The strict dependence order follows from monotonicity of \(u^a(-\log u)/(1-u)\) for \(a\ge1\). A fixed-seed standalone checker passed 3614 numerical and symbolic support checks at 60-digit precision. Finite checks are not the proof.

## Originality (O): PASS, with priority limitations

The closest source is Chesneau's 18 April 2023 Axioms paper, specifically Proposition 6: it contains only the parameter-free endpoint and explicitly notes the need for tunable extensions. Its Lemma 1 Function 5 has the same exponent function in the different copula \(xy^{f(x)}\); the general theorem there is not applicable to the present power-chord form. Two other 2023 original ratio-form studies have unrelated denominators. Exact-formula, source, algebraic-alias and broader-copula searches revealed no covering result. Published searches cannot rule out unindexed or later coverage; a historical-priority risk remains.

## Value (V): PASS

The source explicitly identifies the lack of a tuning parameter as a limitation for statistical modeling. The result provides a nontrivial complete admissibility theorem for the natural exponent bridge, not merely a convex mixture, and proves an interpretable strictly ordered continuum of dependence strengths. Both sharp forbidden sides are mathematically identified, ensuring parameters outside the interval are not used as valid models. No estimator, application or practical superiority is claimed.
