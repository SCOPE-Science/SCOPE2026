Three pairwise nonparallel affine lines over every finite field have exactly two sharp Fourier projection norms, determined solely by concurrency; the nonconcurrent triangle has the strictly smaller norm for every field of order at least three, with the inequality reversing only over \(\mathbb F_2\). The exact triangle norm is an explicit characteristic-dependent root-of-unity average, while the concurrent norm is \(5-12/q+8/q^2\).
