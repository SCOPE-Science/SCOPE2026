The forward orbit of \(0\) under \(x\mapsto2x\), \(x\mapsto3x+2\), and \(x\mapsto6x+3\) fills every residue class modulo \(m\) exactly when \(6\nmid m\). It therefore fills every prime-power quotient separately while excluding \(1\pmod6\), demonstrating a sharp incompatibility between local surjectivity and simultaneous congruence surjectivity.
