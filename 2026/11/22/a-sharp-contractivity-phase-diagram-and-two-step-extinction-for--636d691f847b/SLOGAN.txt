For a nonnormal planar stochastic theta-Maruyama test equation with nilpotent multiplicative noise, one-step mean-square contraction holds exactly when \(2+(2\theta-1)h\ge(\sigma^2+\sqrt{\sigma^4+4\gamma^2})/2\), even though asymptotic mean-square stability is independent of shear and noise intensity. Above the continuous dissipativity threshold, implicitness \(\theta>1/2\) restores contraction only at sufficiently large steps, and a special finite step makes every realization exactly zero in two updates despite arbitrarily large one-step mean-square growth.
