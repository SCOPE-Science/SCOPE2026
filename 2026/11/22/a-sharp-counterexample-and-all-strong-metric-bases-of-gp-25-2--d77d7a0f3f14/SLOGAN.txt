The generalized Petersen graph \(GP(25,2)\) has strong metric dimension exactly \(34\), with precisely \(125\) minimum strong resolving sets in an explicit two-parameter family. This is a concrete counterexample to the published proposal \(\operatorname{sdim}(GP(4k+1,2))=5k+5\) for every \(k\ge5\), which predicts \(35\) when \(k=6\).
