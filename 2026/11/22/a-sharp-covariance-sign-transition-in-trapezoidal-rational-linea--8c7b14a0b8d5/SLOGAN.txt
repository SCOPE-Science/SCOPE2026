Uniform-volume horizontal coordinate covariance in the homothetic trapezoidal prismatoids changes sign at most once as the upper section shrinks, with a strictly positive phase if and only if the base aspect \(a/(bd)\) exceeds the unique positive root of \(3r^3-4r^2-6r-1=0\). The exact transition taper approaches the prism endpoint with gap asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/r}\), even though the frustum combinatorial type does not change.
