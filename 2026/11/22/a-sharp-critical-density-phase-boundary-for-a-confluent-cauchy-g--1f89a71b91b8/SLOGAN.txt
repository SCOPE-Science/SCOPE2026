For the critical integer-lattice Gabor system generated by a Cauchy pole plus its first confluent derivative, the exact Riesz-basis range is \(|\lambda|<\sqrt{e^{4\pi a}-1}/(2\pi)\); outside it the system remains complete but loses stable reconstruction. The optimal squared lower frame bound vanishes at the frontier with the pole-height-independent sharp law \(A\sim4\pi^2(1-|\lambda|/\lambda_c)^2\).
