In strategic opinion dynamics with credible two-bin public messaging, a prescribed complementary-sign two-cycle exists and attracts an open set exactly when every agent gives more than half its influence to the opposing opinion camp. This sharp cut criterion produces periodic switching on a primitive four-person network for every inertia \(0<\alpha<1\), disproving a proposed universal high-inertia stabilization threshold without contradicting established monotone-trajectory results.
