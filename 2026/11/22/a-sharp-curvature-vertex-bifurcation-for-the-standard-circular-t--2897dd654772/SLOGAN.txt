The curvature of the standard circular trefoil changes from twelve strict local extrema to six at the exact torus radius ratio \(R_*=10.1012691095\ldots\), characterized by \(20R_*^3-156R_*^2-417R_*-484=0\). Each of three pairs of curvature maxima merges with square-root separation at that threshold, distinct from the classical inflection transition.
