## Correctness — PASS
The quantifiers cover every integer \(m\ge2\) and every \(0<a,b<1\), over real arithmetic-mean-zero perturbations. A unitary transform in the cell index gives \(2\times2\) Bloch blocks. The frequency-zero subspace reduces to the antisymmetric line, whose gain is strictly below one. The maximal singular gain among all nonzero modes occurs at frequency \(2\pi/m\), since the block Gram trace is constant and its determinant decreases with cosine. The exact Gram defect \(4ab(1-a)(1-b)\sin^2(\theta/2)-(a-b)^2\), together with Gram trace less than two, proves both directions of the threshold, including equality. ## Value — PASS
A two-site alternating positive local average is the minimal non-translation-invariant periodic convolution family. The exact natural invariant is the one-step energy gain on mean-zero perturbations of a constant sampled signal. Positivity and row-stochasticity can misleadingly suggest damping, but the result identifies a sharp *mesh-size* onset of Euclidean noise amplification, supplies the exact worst mode, and contrasts it with stationary-weighted contraction. For rates \(a=2/5\) and \(b=3/5\), the first failing mesh has 16 samples, not an arbitrary chosen dimension. This is a usable finite-size diagnostic for periodic blurs and numerical signal processing, not just a counterexample at one point.
