Every smooth degree-two del Pezzo surface in odd characteristic admits a plane blowdown after an extension of degree at most \(18\), and the bound is attained over every odd finite field of size at least \(9\). In the extremal Frobenius class, an Aronhold heptad descends after degree \(9\), but neither of its two Geiser-related seven-curve contraction sets descends before degree \(18\).
