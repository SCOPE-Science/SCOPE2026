For the canonical positive-type circle kernel \(\cos\theta\), every reflection-symmetric Bernstein sampling grid preserves positivity through degree four, but at every degree five and above some such grid develops a negative **third** Fourier harmonic. The first Fourier harmonic always stays positive, and a binomial-majority construction gives a sharp negative cluster limit approaching \(-2/(3\pi)\), making the obstruction persistent rather than a finite-degree accident.
