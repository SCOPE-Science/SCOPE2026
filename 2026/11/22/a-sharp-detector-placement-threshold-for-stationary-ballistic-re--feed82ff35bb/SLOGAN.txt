A stationary ballistic threshold-reset particle has the same uniform position density for every admissible speed law, yet a single binary detector recovers the entire hidden law from its two-time occupancy probabilities exactly when its placement satisfies \(a\ge Lv_+/(v_-+v_+)\). Below this sharp threshold, the short-lag data determine only the high-speed tail and two aggregate moments of slower speeds, and distinct smooth speed laws are observationally indistinguishable.
