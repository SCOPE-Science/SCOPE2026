For a one-turn circular helix, the Euclidean diameter switches at the unique reduced pitch \(c_*=0.378586747112271\ldots\) from a continuum of interior chord pairs to the two endpoints. Independent arclength-uniform points then exhibit a sharp near-diameter probability transition from order \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) to order \(\varepsilon^2\), with an exact pitch–deficit crossover and conditional endpoint laws.
