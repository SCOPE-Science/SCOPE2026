The Euclidean diameter of a symmetric finite catenary makes a sharp jump in its maximizing chord geometry at the unique dimensionless half-span \(L_*\approx2.1950423222\): the two endpoints give way to two symmetric endpoint-to-interior chords. For independent arclength-uniform random points, the near-diameter probability changes from an \(\varepsilon^2\) law to an explicit \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\) law, with a sharp \(L-L_*=O(\varepsilon)\) crossover and a marked limit on a parabolic region.
