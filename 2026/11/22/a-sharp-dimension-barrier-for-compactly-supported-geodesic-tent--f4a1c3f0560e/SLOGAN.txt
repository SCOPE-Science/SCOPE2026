For the geodesic tent covariance \((c-\theta)_+\), every positive cutoff is positive definite on the circle, but in every spherical dimension at least two positive definiteness holds **exactly** when \(c\ge\pi\). Every shorter cutoff has infinitely many negative Legendre coefficients with explicit oscillatory size \(n^{-5/2}\); admissible full-support kernels are positive semidefinite but not strictly positive definite.
