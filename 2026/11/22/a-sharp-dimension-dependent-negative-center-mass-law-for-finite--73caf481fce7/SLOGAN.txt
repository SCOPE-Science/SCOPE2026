For any finite set of at least three distinct points on a Euclidean sphere whose convex hull contains its center, adding that center forces its normalized microscopic weighting to have strictly negative center mass. This mass lies in a best-possible dimension-dependent interval \((1-C_d)/(2-C_d)<w_0<0\), with \(C_d\) the mean unit-sphere chord length; the sharp lower bound approaches \(-1/\sqrt2\) in high dimension.
