In a cube with independently fair-oriented edges, two adjacent facets have sink counts independent exactly in dimensions \(2\) and \(3\), but from dimension \(4\) onward their covariance is the positive quantity \(1/4-(d-1)2^{-d}\). In high dimensions their joint law approaches \((U+W,V+W)\) with independent Poisson intensities \(3/4,3/4,1/4\), whereas opposite facets remain independent at every dimension.
