# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The main norm is computed on the quotient by constants. The difference-coordinate metric is the inverse Dirichlet path Laplacian, not the ordinary coordinate \(\ell_2\) metric; this normalization is explicitly derived. For every active face, its Jacobian is a rank-one correction of a positive interpolation matrix. The selected Green-matrix inverse has a tridiagonal Dirichlet form, making the signed correction vector sign-aligned and exactly normalized in \(\ell_1\). Hence every Jacobian column has norm at most two, and continuous piecewise affinity upgrades this to a global estimate. The full-support all-positive face has an interior column of norm two from four sites onward, and the KKT witness realizes it. At two and three sites the explicit face classification gives norm one. Exact-rational tests reproduce the finite cases but are not relied on for the universal proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Projection onto fixed-radius TV balls is a natural constrained denoising operation. The coefficient gives an exact perturbation budget in the variation seminorm; Hilbert-space nonexpansivity alone gives no dimension-uniform constant in that seminorm. The result identifies a first expansive dimension and establishes a dimension-free factor two, while explicit witnesses prove that the factor is unavoidable. The Green/interpolation proof exhibits a reusable structural mechanism. It is not merely a numerical bound or an arbitrary parameter slice.
