For the fourth-order two-stage Gauss–Legendre heat step on an unweighted Boolean \(d\)-cube, a positive timestep with an entrywise nonnegative transition matrix exists **exactly when \(d\le12\)**: \(h=1/3\) works through dimension twelve, while three exact Sturm-sign certificates exclude every positive timestep from dimension thirteen onward. In contrast, positivity at all sufficiently small steps ends already at dimension six.
