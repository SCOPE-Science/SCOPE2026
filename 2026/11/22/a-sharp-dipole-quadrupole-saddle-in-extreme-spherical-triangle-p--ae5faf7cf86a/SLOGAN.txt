Near-maximal random spherical-triangle perimeters are **suppressed by every nonzero dipole** but **enhanced by every nonzero quadrupole** in the sampling density. The complete quadrupole coefficient is \(C_*[1+\operatorname{tr}(A^2)/20-\operatorname{tr}(A^3)/42]\), with a sharp global enhancement bound \(11\operatorname{tr}(A^2)/420\); uniform sampling is a genuine saddle rather than an extremum.
