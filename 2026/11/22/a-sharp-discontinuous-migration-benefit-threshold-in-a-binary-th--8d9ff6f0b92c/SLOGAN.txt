For any nonconstant binary three-season fitness calendar in two patches, symmetric direct exchange first beats staying put at the sharp contrast \(r=2+\sqrt3\), with an abrupt nonzero onset rate \((3-\sqrt3)/2\). If exchange is capped at one half, no benefit is possible until the higher contrast \(r>2+\sqrt5\); the full beneficial-rate interval is given by exact quadratic roots.
