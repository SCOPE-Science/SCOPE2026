For uniformly random three-state lifted Rule 140 on a finite cyclic ring, the first global fixation time is \(\log_3 n+\log_3\log_3 n+O_{\mathbb P}(1)\), and its mean differs from the same center by at most a constant. The double-logarithmic delay arises from protected consecutive runs of twos and ones, and is not determined by the previously published one-site decay formula alone.
