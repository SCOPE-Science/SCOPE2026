Symmetrically contracting the sampling nodes of Bernstein–Stancu polynomials by \(b/\sqrt n\) creates a sharp drift–noise transition: the integrated \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) error multiplied by \(\sqrt n\) converges to \(\tfrac12\max\{\sqrt{2/\pi},b\}\). The worst jumps localize at the midpoint below \(b=\sqrt{2/\pi}\), but at the endpoints above it, and every finite-degree norm has an exact Beta-moment formula.
