## Scope and limits

All statements concern finite cyclic homogeneous exclusion at fixed particle number, positive bidirectional rates, and the **initial slope** of an event-Palm vacancy-spell hazard. They do not imply an all-time increasing hazard, detectability from finite observations, or a complete characterization of nonequilibrium chains. Independent audit remains outstanding.
