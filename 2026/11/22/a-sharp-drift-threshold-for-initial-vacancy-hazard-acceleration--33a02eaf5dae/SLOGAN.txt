In stationary finite-ring asymmetric exclusion, the initial hazard of a newly vacant observed site changes from decreasing to increasing at one exact directional-bias threshold whenever there are at least two particles and two vacancies. At fixed positive density, the critical clockwise-to-counterclockwise rate ratio grows as \(2L/\rho\), making early vacancy-hazard acceleration a sharp one-sided signature of broken detailed balance from a single-site record.
