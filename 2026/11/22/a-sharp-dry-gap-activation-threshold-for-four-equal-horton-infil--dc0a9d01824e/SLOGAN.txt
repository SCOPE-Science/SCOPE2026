Four identical rain bursts in an exponential wet-decay/dry-recovery infiltration model should not be separated by equally spaced pauses: all recovery time belongs in the middle pause until the sharp threshold \(D_c=r^{-1}\log(1+e^{-kw})\), after which two shorter outer pauses activate together. The optimal schedule is unique, and four bursts are the first number for which this change in gap support is possible.
