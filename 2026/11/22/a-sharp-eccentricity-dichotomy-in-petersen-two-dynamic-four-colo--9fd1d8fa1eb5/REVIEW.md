This is a natural exact classification of *robust neighbour-diversity colourings* of the canonical Petersen graph. It goes beyond the previously known ordinary Petersen colouring orbits to quantify **every legal one-vertex transition under the additional local condition**, its precise radius/diameter and all pairwise distances. The structural conclusion is that a singleton colour class lowers worst-case recolouring distance by exactly one, despite every state being connected at the minimum palette. The result's small finite scope is explicit; its claim does not depend on unsupported extrapolation.
