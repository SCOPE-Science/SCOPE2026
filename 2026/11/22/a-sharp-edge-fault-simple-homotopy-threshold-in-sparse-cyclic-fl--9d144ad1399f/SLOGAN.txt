In the sparse range \(n\ge4p+2\), deleting any single edge from the flag complex of a cycle power \(C_n^p\) is an elementary-collapse equivalence for every \(p\ge3\). The sharp exception occurs in the squared cycle: deleting a nearest-neighbor edge changes \(S^1\) to \(S^1\vee S^1\), whereas a distance-two deletion leaves the circle unchanged.
