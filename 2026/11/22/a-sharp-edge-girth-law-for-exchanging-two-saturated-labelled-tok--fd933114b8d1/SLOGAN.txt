For \(2s\) distinct tokens saturating the ends of any edge of a connected graph, exchanging the two stacks takes exactly \(2s+\min\{4,g-2\}\) single-token moves, where \(g\) is the length of the shortest cycle through that edge (or infinity for a bridge). The capacity-independent penalty detects triangles, squares and pentagons and stabilizes at four for longer cycles and bridges.
