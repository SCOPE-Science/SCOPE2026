For a positive-semidefinite four-mode family, Nyström's leading eigenvector switches sharply from a power-refined first mode to the orthogonal second mode when the true top eigengap falls below \(\delta_c=a(1-a)u/[1-(1-a)u]\), even though the trial plane has principal-angle sine \(\sqrt u\) and Rayleigh--Ritz continues to select the correct mode. In the switched regime the Nyström leading eigenvalue is strictly more accurate; a subfamily improves its error fourfold while the Nyström eigenvector remains exactly orthogonal as the trial plane becomes arbitrarily accurate.
