On the one-observable quadratic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck benchmark, the empirical Gram matrix is almost surely invertible, yet Richardson-extrapolated stochastic dynamic mode decomposition has infinite expected squared Koopman error for every sample count \(m\le8\), while first-order SDMD has finite expected squared error already at \(m=5\). The exact Pareto exponents of their squared errors are \(m/8\) and \(m/4\), respectively.
