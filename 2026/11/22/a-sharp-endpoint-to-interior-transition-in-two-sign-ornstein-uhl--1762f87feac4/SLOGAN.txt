For two predetermined zero-threshold sign readings of a stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process, the globally optimal Bayes quadrature nodes are endpoints when \(0<\lambda\le0.060089278308\ldots\), but form a unique reflected interior pair above that sharp threshold. The interior separation tends to \(1/3\) as the correlation rate grows, while the full-valued two-node problem has interior optimal sites at every positive correlation rate.
