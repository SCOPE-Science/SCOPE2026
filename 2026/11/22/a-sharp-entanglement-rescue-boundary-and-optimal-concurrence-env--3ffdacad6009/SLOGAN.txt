With independent spontaneous emission and joint Poisson ground-state resetting, a coherently pair-driven qubit pair becomes stationary-entangled exactly when \((r+\gamma)(r+2\gamma)>2\gamma g\). Optimizing over coherent coupling, its concurrence rises strictly with reset rate from the unreset sharp ceiling \((\sqrt5-1)/4\) toward, but never reaches at finite rate, the sharp bound \(1/2\).
