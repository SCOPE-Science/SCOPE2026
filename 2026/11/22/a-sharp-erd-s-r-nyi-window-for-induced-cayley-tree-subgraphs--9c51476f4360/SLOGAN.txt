A uniform Cayley tree, restricted to its first \(k\) labels, has a complete labeled-graph law within explicit total variation of \(G(k,2/n)\) until the sharp scale \(k\asymp n^{2/3}\). At that threshold their wedge counts tend to distinct Poisson laws, while at the earlier \(\sqrt n\) edge-onset scale the endpoint pairs converge to a planar Poisson point process.
