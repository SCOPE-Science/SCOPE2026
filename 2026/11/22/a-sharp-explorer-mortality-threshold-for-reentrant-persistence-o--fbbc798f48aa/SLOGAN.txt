On a star-shaped habitat network in the fast-exploration model with propagule mortality, the metapopulation capacity has a unique finite exploration-rate maximum exactly when \(1+\gamma/\lambda>4m(m+1)/(m-1)^2\), where \(m\ge2\) is the number of leaves. A seventeen-patch example then exhibits genuine reentrant deterministic persistence: viable at intermediate mobility but not at either low or high mobility.
