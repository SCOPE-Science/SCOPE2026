The degree-one/degree-two zonal obstruction to Nelson-time \(L^2\to L^q\) contraction for the fractional-Laplacian Poisson semigroup on \(\mathbb S^d\) has exactly one critical target exponent \(q_c(d)\) for every \(d\ge4\): the quartic witness is positive precisely when \(2<q<q_c(d)\). Its threshold has sharp growth \(q_c(d)\sim(d+1)^{1+\sqrt2}\), but it does not characterize unrestricted hypercontractivity above the threshold.
