For independent exponential stages with rates \(0<a\le b\), the integer-binned total \(\lfloor E_a+E_b\rfloor\) is infinitely divisible exactly when \(b\ge2a\). Consequently, every discrete Bilal law fails infinite divisibility, whereas Poisson observation of the same underlying lifetime always gives a sum of two geometric counts; the first negative logarithmic coefficient for discrete Bilal moves to order \(0.9668538\ldots/(1-p)\) as \(p\uparrow1\).
