For a uniform element of the \(h\)-fold cyclic \(p\)-wreath group, the proportion whose order is exactly \(p^{h-r}\) is asymptotic to \(h^r((p-1)/p)^{h-r}/r!\) for each fixed defect \(r\). The factorial rare-tail profile sharpens the known typical-order theory without claiming the classical finite-depth cycle-index count as new.
