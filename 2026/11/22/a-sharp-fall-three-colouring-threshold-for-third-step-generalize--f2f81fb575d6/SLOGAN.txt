The third-step generalized Petersen graph \(GP(n,3)\) has a proper fall three-colouring exactly for integer \(n\ge9\), with sharp exceptions at \(n=7,8\). A six-column splicing construction covers every larger order, while exhaustive symmetry-reduced finite certificates rule out the two exceptions; for even \(n\ge8\), the two-homogeneous chromatic number is therefore \(4\) only at \(n=8\), and \(3\) otherwise.
