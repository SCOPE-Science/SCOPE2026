Among all concentric annuli of a prescribed area, a unique annulus maximizes the least Gaussian time–frequency energy captured from every superposition of the first \(N+1\) Hermite modes: its vacuum and highest-mode captures coincide, and all intervening modes are better observed. At fixed area its optimal inner squared radius is \(N/e+\log(2\pi N)/(2e)+O(1)\), with a sharp exponentially decaying minimax capture law.
