A single finite-width average of Brownian motion has a sharp exposure-width/noise transition: its full-trajectory posterior-variance-optimal window is strictly interior below an explicit threshold and exactly terminal above it. The critical width is the unique \(h\in(0,1)\) solving \(46h^3-165h^2+180h-60=0\), and the critical noise diverges as \(1/(3h)\) when the window shrinks to point sampling.
