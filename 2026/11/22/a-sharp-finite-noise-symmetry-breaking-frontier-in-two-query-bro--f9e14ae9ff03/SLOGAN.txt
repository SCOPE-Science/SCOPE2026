For two noisy point observations of a Brownian-bridge integral with positively correlated Gaussian errors, the globally optimal design is exactly either a symmetric interior pair or a midpoint paired with a zero-signal endpoint that measures only shared noise. A sharp noise threshold \(\eta_c=0.0323786667908285\ldots\) governs whether the optimal geometry jumps discontinuously as error correlation rises, with a unique transition converging to correlation \(1/2\) in the high-noise limit.
