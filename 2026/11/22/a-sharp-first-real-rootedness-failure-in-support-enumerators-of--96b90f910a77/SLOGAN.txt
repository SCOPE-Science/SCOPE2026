For the dimension-\(m\) standard-example poset, deleting the diagonal comparabilities from a complete two-level order changes its support-enumerator gamma polynomial by exactly \(-mq\): \(\Gamma_m(q)=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\binom mj^2q^j-mq\). This infinite family first loses real-rootedness at \(m=12\), while its first eleven members remain real-rooted.
