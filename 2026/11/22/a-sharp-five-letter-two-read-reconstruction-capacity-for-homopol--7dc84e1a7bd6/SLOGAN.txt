For homopolymer-free words, the largest code reconstructible from any two distinct single-deletion reads has exactly half of all words through length four, but at length five its maximum is \(\frac12q(q-1)^4-2\binom q3\) for every alphabet size \(q\ge2\). The loss is concentrated in three-symbol support classes, which also create the first odd-cycle obstruction to bipartiteness.
