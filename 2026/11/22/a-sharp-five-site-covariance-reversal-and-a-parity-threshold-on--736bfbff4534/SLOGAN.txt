The uniform hard-core gas on \(F_n=K_1\vee P_n\) has strictly positive path-endpoint covariance for odd \(n\), but for every even \(n\ge4\) it changes sign exactly once at an activity increasing from \(1\) to \(2\), with correction \(3\log(3/2)/n\). The five-vertex fan \(F_4\) is, up to isomorphism and marked endpoints, the unique smallest graph where a pair's covariance reverses with common positive activity.
