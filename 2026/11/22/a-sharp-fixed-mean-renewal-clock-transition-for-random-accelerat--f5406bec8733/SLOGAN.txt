A randomly accelerated particle's continuous level-crossing flux has a sharp universal fixed-mean reset-clock transition at \(m=a(|u|/k)^{2/3}\), with \(a=2.3178848733\ldots\): below it, bursty renewal clocks approach an unattained optimum, whereas above it periodic resets are uniquely optimal. The global renewal optimum is approximately \(35.08\%\) higher than the best Poisson-reset crossing flux.
