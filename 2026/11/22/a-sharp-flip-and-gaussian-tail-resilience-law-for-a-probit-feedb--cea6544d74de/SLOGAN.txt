A Gaussian-quantile feedback map has a sharp flip at \(a=2\sqrt{2\pi}\), exactly one locally attracting primitive two-cycle above it, and a unique finite gain where that cycle is superattracting. At high gain its contraction deficit is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\,a e^{-a^2/32}\), while the critical fixed point relaxes at the exact inverse-square-root rate outside its finite-hitting set.
