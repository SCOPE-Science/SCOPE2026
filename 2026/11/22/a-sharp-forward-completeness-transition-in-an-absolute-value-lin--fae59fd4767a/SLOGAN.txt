In the absolute-value quadratic line-equilibrium flow with \(a>0\) and \(b\leq0\), the sign of \(c\) is the sharp boundary for universal forward existence: all solutions are global for \(c\geq0\), while \(c<0\) admits an explicit open region of finite-time blow-up. A monotone polynomial shear simultaneously rules out all nonstationary recurrent probability laws on both sides of that boundary.
