For the planar \(q\)-th dual curvature measure, an even four-atom target of masses \(A\) at \(\pm e_1\) and \(B\) at \(\pm e_2\) is realizable for every \(A,B>0\) when \(0<q\le1\), and for \(q>1\), \(q\ne2\), exactly when \(\min\{q-1,(q-1)^{-1}\}<A/B<\max\{q-1,(q-1)^{-1}\}\). Every realizer is a unique centered rectangle when \(q\ge1\) and \(q\ne2\); at \(q=2\), realizability requires \(A=B\) and the full fiber is \(ab=A\). For every \(0<q<1\), some even four-atom targets instead have both centered and not symmetric about the origin rectangular realizers.
