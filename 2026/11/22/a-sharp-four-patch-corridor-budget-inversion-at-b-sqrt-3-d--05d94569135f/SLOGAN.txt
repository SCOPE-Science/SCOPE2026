With a fixed total symmetric corridor-migration budget, a four-patch landscape with one refuge switches sharply between favoring a five-edge diamond and a three-edge path for invasion: the diamond has the lower refuge-growth threshold when \(B<\sqrt3\,d\), and the path when \(B>\sqrt3\,d\). Four patches are the smallest connected simple undirected habitat networks allowing such a budget-normalized ranking reversal in the specified one-refuge model.
