For pairs of finite-rank orthogonal projections, the complementary McCarthy trace deficit is sharply equivalent to squared Hilbert–Schmidt commutator energy for every \(3\le p\le5\). The exact optimal lower and upper coefficients exchange their roles at \(p=4\): near-coincident projection lines control one side below four, near-orthogonal lines control it above four, and the previously known quartic identity is recovered at the transition.
