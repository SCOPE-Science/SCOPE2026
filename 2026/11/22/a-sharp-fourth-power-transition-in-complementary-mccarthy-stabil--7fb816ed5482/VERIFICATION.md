# Verification and reproducibility

The proof reduces arbitrary finite-rank projection pairs to the two-dimensional principal-angle canonical form; all trace and Hilbert–Schmidt quantities then sum over the generic blocks. The quotient is the anchored secant of the explicit function \(f_p\). Generalized binomial coefficient signs give strict convexity for \(3\le p<4\), strict concavity for \(4<p\le5\), and exact linearity at \(p=4\). Endpoint limits give both globally best constants. These are the infinite-parameter proof steps, not numerical conjectures.

Run `python3 verify_projection_p_bounds.py` from this package. The performed run returned `PROJECTION_MCCARTHY_DIAGNOSTIC_OK 20300`, including exact rational checks at the integer endpoints and floating diagnostics for noninteger powers. The finite tests do not certify all exponents, ranks, or bibliographic novelty.

The fourth-power case is a previously published identity and is not asserted to be a new theorem. The nonquartic interval and projection restriction are essential to the assessed claim. Independent mathematical audit is not performed.
