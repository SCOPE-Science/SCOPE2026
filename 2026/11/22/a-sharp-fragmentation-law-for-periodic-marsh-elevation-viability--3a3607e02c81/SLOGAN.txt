For a quadratic marsh-elevation viability model alternating favorable and adverse forcing, splitting identical total yearly exposures into \(n\) cycles strictly raises the maximum sustainable adverse duration to \(T_n=(n/q)\arctan((p/q)\tanh(pL/n))\). The gain has a sharp \(n^{-2}\) deficit from the mean-forcing limit, and every finite cycling frequency leaves an interval where favorable *average* forcing nevertheless leads to certain finite-time loss.
