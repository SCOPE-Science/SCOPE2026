For the backward-Euler discretization of the cubic Hopf oscillator, a globally smooth implicit update can have a stable origin and an attracting outer invariant circle separated by a repelling inner circle, even though the underlying differential equation has an unstable origin. This three-stratum bistability occurs exactly when \(1/2<h|\omega|<1\) and \(-\sqrt3\,h|\omega|<1-h\lambda<-\sqrt{1-h^2\omega^2}\), with explicit radii and global basins.
