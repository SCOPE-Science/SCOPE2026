For equal-duration temporary habitats with a uniformly departing propagule and two sequential exponential travel stages, the optimal destination opening switches sharply from a separated window to an overlapping window at \(\lambda L=1.2564312086\ldots\). The globally optimal opening delay increases from one travel-stage timescale to the two-stage travel-time median, while synchronizing short windows loses an unbounded factor \(\sim6/(e\lambda L)\) in successful-arrival probability.
