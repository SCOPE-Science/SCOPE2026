For every real positive-semidefinite matrix of corank one, symmetric Gaussian rank-one completion has inverse-norm tail \(C_{\mathrm{s}}R^{-1/2}\), whereas independent two-sided completion has the thinner tail \(C_{\mathrm{i}}(\log R)/R\), with explicit positive constants. Their finite inverse-norm moments end sharply at orders \(1/2\) and \(1\), respectively.
