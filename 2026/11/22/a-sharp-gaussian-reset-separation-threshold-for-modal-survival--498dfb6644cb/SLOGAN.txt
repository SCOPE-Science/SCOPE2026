A free Brownian particle independently reset to one of two Gaussian position populations has exactly one sharp separation threshold between a single stationary peak and two peaks, determined by equality of a normal-Laplace density and its Gaussian source. The critical density is quartically flat, the peaks separate with a square-root law, and infrequent resetting suppresses bimodality until their separation grows on the \(\sigma\sqrt{2\log(b/\sigma)}\) scale.
