Two fish-refuge travel-time classes create a sharp jump between globally optimal drawdown durations under exponential reaction latency and whole-event mortality: the critical normalized separation is \(d_* = \log[(w^{-1/r}-w)/(1-w)]\), where \(r=\mu/\lambda\). At the switch, both optima are globally tied and are separated by exactly \(-\log(w)/\mu\), although every single-class model has one continuously varying optimum.
