# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim concerns the operator-norm negative part of the self-commutator of the square of a hyponormal weighted shift on a rooted one-fork tree, not a general graph. Only one two-step child-set block can be negative. The rank-one secular inequality follows from an exact pointwise cubic factorization; the two-level construction with positive weights attains it for every allowed number of rays. The zero case for a fork at the root follows from positivity of all two-step blocks. The proof includes the boundary case of countably infinitely many rays and zero weights. The exact polynomial identity and extremal two-by-two block have also been checked symbolically, and a numerical test covers 8,000 additional finite-coordinate inputs.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Jabłoński--Jung--Stochel (arXiv:0911.3821, Section 5.3) already proved the qualitative existence of a nonhyponormal square on the same tree shape. Pikul (arXiv:2109.05970, Theorems 4.1 and 4.8; arXiv:2307.07777, Theorem 5.4) gives the local criteria and general forkless classification. None of the inspected relevant primary statements provides the best normalized negative-part norm \((\sqrt5-2)/4\), its two-level attainment, or independence from ray count. Public-web and mathematical-index searches for those quantitative equivalents did not identify dominating coverage. Historical priority is a qualified best-knowledge judgment, not an exhaustive proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact defect is a natural invariant of the minimal graph configuration where hyponormality ceases to force square hyponormality. This quantitative refinement distinguishes a root fork from a fork with one or more incoming edges and shows that further branches cannot enlarge the worst defect. The golden-ratio extremal pattern and finite-rank block reduction give useful structural information beyond a yes/no counterexample.

## Limits

No extension to several forks or higher powers is claimed. Finite numerical checks are supplementary, not infinite-dimensional proofs. Independent validation and an exhaustive priority search remain outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
