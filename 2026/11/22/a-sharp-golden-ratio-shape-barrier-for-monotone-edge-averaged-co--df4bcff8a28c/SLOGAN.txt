For incidence-based edge-averaged convection–diffusion on affine parallelogram tilings, two explicit side-length and shear inequalities exactly characterize the nonsingular \(M\)-matrix regime for every positive assignment of edge diffusion and real Péclet parameters (including arbitrary constant convection fields). The admissible Jacobians have the sharp shape-conditioning ceiling \(\kappa_2\le(3+\sqrt5)/2\), attained on a sheared parallelogram, although this ceiling by itself does not guarantee monotonicity.
