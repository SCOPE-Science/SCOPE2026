# Mathematical review

## Correctness
The triadic Jacobian at a balanced \(0/\pi\) configuration is exactly the negative rank-one form \(-K_2ss^\top/N\), because the two first-harmonic sums vanish. The pairwise signed-Laplacian term has one negative bridge weight. Decomposing zero-mean perturbations into block means and internal fluctuations reduces definiteness to a two-variable form; Laplacian pseudoinverse duality supplies its **attainable** minimum at each boundary discrepancy. The Schur complement yields the stated strict criterion, including the singleton case. The exactly neutral threshold and one unstable direction on the other side follow by rank-one inertia. The synchrony Jacobian and both graph-family Green diagonals are derived explicitly. Exact arithmetic checks in `check.py` corroborate the boundary cases, but finite computations do not prove the general theorem.

## Originality
Full text of the initiating mixed-interaction study (Sharma et al., arXiv:2611.09564v1) presents the same flow and numerically studies random graphs, not a sharp deterministic single-bridge antipodal criterion. Zelazo--Bürger (arXiv:1408.2187v1, full HTML read) has general effective-resistance tests for negative graph edges but no global triadic Kuramoto rank-one correction or topology-dependent coupling threshold. Kachhvah--Jalan (arXiv:2203.16254v3, full HTML read) is adaptive, unlike the fixed nonnegative couplings here. Bick--Böhle--Kuehn (DOI:10.1137/22M1500940, extensive publisher HTML inspected) addresses continuum twisted states on nonlocal rings, and Menara et al. (arXiv:1806.06083, full text excerpts inspected) concerns pairwise heterogeneous cluster-synchronization manifolds. The closest indexed finding is a distinct-triad complete-graph antipodal stability law, not this sparse bridge family. Exact priority is not independently certified; unpublished or unindexed equivalents remain possible.

## Value
This sharp necessary-and-sufficient **graph structural obstruction** connects the real stability of antiphase network states to endpoint Green diagonals, a physically meaningful bottleneck measure. The contrasting infinite graph families establish more than a parameter rescaling: no nonnegative higher-order gain rescues antiphase on sufficiently long paths, whereas arbitrarily large clique communities permit coexistence of antiphase and synchrony and have a shrinking sharp threshold. This is a motivated finite-network response to the originating study's unresolved role of topology in mixed-order synchronization. The theorem does not address global basins or nonlinear criticality.
