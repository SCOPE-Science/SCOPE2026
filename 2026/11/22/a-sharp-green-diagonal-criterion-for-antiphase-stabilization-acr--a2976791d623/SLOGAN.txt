For an identical-frequency mixed pairwise/triadic Kuramoto network made of two equal connected communities and one bridge, antipodal phase locking becomes locally attracting exactly when a sum of two endpoint Laplacian Green diagonals is below one and the triadic gain exceeds an explicit sharp threshold. This makes antiphase attraction impossible on paths with at least six vertices but attainable on every two-clique barbell, while full synchrony remains attracting.
