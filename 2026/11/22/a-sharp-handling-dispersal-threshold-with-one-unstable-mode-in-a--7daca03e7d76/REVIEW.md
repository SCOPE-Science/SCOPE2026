# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The reaction equilibrium and full diffusion Jacobian were reconstructed directly from the mechanistic model with exact rational derivatives, not estimated from simulations. Modal traces are negative for every wavenumber, the first-mode determinant changes sign once at \(15421/10900\), and the determinant is strictly increasing and positive for all higher Neumann wavenumbers \(q\ge1\). The proof controls infinitely many modes analytically. The embedded rational checker independently differentiates the nonlinear functions using first-order dual numbers and verifies the identities. This is a linear spectral theorem, not a nonlinear pattern-existence assertion.

## Originality
PASS, with a specific material priority risk. The November 2026 initiating study supplies the reaction-cross-diffusion system but explicitly leaves stability and pattern onset to subsequent work. Earlier studies already establish Turing effects of cross diffusion generally; the claimed new content is a sharp handling-mobility boundary and exact all-mode behavior for the 2026 model with its distinct competition structure and an equal-diagonal effective-diffusion witness. The March 2026 structural study addresses competitive SKT-type reactions, unlike the present predator–prey reaction Jacobian. Two accessible historical results based on the initiating source are spatially homogeneous equilibrium classifications; neither implies a PDE mode threshold. Relevant published-finding corpus and literature searches found no matching exact threshold or stronger statement; this is not itself a proof of priority. The full text of the especially plausible 2019 four-state comparison was unavailable, although its abstract and the initiating paper's comparison to it were checked. That unresolved risk warrants independent priority confirmation.

## Value
PASS. This gives a complete, sharp answer to the new model's natural question of when changing one behavioral movement coefficient produces spatial instability. The conclusion has two useful structural features: only one physical spatial scale ever destabilizes, and the instability survives exact equality of effective self-diffusion rates, which would defeat a naive diffusivity-contrast diagnostic. The parameter family is biologically interpretable and all microscopic movement rates are positive. The result does not pretend to predict observed ecological patterns or prove nonlinear bifurcating solutions.

## Limitations and comparison
The work is restricted to the specified interval, positive rational reaction coefficients, isotropic diffusion and local linearization. Standard two-by-two dispersion algebra and earlier cross-diffusion Turing effects are prior mathematics, not part of the originality claim. Full historical-priority review remains outstanding for Desvillettes–Soresina (2019) and potentially unindexed equivalents.
