The sharp bounded-variation-to-weighted-\(L^2\) Chebyshev projection error has an exact finite-degree single-jump formula and decays as \(0.369596161139\ldots/\sqrt n\). All optimal jump locations migrate toward a Chebyshev endpoint at distance asymptotic to \(c_*^2/(8n^2)\), where \(\operatorname{Si}(c_*)=\pi/2\).
