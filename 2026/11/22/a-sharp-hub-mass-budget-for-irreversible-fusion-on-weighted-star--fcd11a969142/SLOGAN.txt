On every finite weighted star, a fused edge stays fused for all larger TV penalties and every input if and only if its edge weight dominates the competing spokes after an exact fidelity-mass correction. This yields the sharp stabilizing-weight feasibility condition \(\sum_i m_i/(m_0+2m_i)\le1\); with equal masses, four leaves are the first star on which no positive edge weights can prevent eventual unfusion for every input.
