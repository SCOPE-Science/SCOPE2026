In a two-stage mosquito cohort with one adult-only removal, the unique time minimizing total adult-days switches sharply from immediate to delayed when \(A_0/(\beta L_0)\) falls below \((1-e^{-\mu T})/\mu\), regardless of immature depletion and removal efficacy. When delay is optimal, the unique best time is before half the horizon and moves earlier as immature loss increases.
