For the quadratic energy \(Q=\int_0^1N_t^2\,dt\) of a known-rate Poisson path, the unique best time to observe one exact count is an explicit quartic root \(x_\lambda\) increasing from \(2/3\) to \(\sqrt{3/5}\) as intensity increases. At high intensity this shift cuts the minimum Bayes mean-square error by \(24.158\ldots\%\) compared with the classical linear-integral sampling time.
