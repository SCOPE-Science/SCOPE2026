When a fraction \(\theta\) of drivers optimizes private emissions instead of travel time, the equilibrium flow between two monotone-cost roads is exactly the clamped value \(x(\theta)=\min\{b,\max\{a,\theta\}\}\). With affine per-trip emissions, a strict intermediate-adoption optimum beats both pure strategies exactly when \(2a-b<z/(v+z)<b\), with computable quadratic savings.
