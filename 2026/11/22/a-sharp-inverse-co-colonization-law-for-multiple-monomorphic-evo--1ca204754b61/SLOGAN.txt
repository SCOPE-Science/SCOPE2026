For the structured Gaussian co-colonization replicator, the chance of two or more monomorphic evolutionary stable states disappears at the sharp rate \(\binom N2/(\sqrt2\pi4^{N-2}\mu)\) as the single-infection ratio \(\mu\) grows, even though the antisymmetric limit forbids two such states. The pure-ESS count is exactly \(\operatorname{Binomial}(N,1/N)\) at \(\mu=0\), and its mean has an explicit strictly decreasing Gaussian integral for \(N\ge3\).
