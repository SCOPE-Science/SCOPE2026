# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The normalization is the dual curvature convention \(\widetilde C_4=(1/2)\int\rho^4\), not unnormalized cone volume. The positive boundary-density formula forces a four-atom realizer to be a rectangle; the positive cubic facet-mass law forces equality of horizontal side distances. Exact substitutions reduce the remaining inverse to \(\lambda=(3+\kappa)/(2+6\kappa)\) and a globally strictly decreasing rational function. Boundary conditions are strict; no degenerating origin-boundary configuration is included. All algebraic identities and four independent angular quadratures have been checked.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
The closest published general work (Monahan 2026) assumes general position, violated by the two antipodal pairs; its statements cannot imply this cross-normal necessary/sufficient interval. The earlier explicit even-cross classification does not cover \(B\ne C\), and general even uniqueness/nonuniqueness results similarly do not cover this measure. Indexed comparisons did not find a non-even four-atom inverse at exponent four. An unindexed equivalent is a residual risk, not evidence of novelty by itself.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The dual Minkowski inverse problem is a natural geometric-moment problem. Its non-even discrete cases have limited general theory. The result specifies sharp feasible masses, proves uniqueness without a priori symmetry, and provides a constructive inverse for a genuine three-parameter non-even family; these properties are not implied by merely computing one rectangle's masses.
