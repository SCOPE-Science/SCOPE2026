A non-even four-atom fourth dual curvature measure with equal masses \(A\) at the two horizontal normals and unequal masses \(B,C\) at the vertical normals has a unique origin-interior convex realizer exactly when \(1/6<A/(B+C)<3/2\). The realizer must be a vertical-axis-reflection-symmetric, vertically off-center rectangle and admits an explicit monotone one-variable inverse; neither endpoint can be attained.
