For a Brownian group reset at Poisson times to its *current* rightmost member, a constant inward drift has the exact capture-versus-escape threshold \(v_c=\sqrt{\pi Dr/2}\,\mathbb E\max_{j\le N}Z_j\). At and above the threshold the first particle hits the absorbing wall almost surely; below it noncapture has positive probability and every surviving particle escapes ballistically with speed \(v_c-v\).
