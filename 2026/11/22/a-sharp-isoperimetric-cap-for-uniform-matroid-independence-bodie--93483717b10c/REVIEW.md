The conditional-mean identity proves the affine density coefficient in the exact area formula. Differentiation yields the factored derivative, and Brunn–Minkowski concavity is strict beyond the homothetic-simplex range, proving the unique global minimum. The CLT and local CLT establish the additive high-dimensional expansion and integer-rank bound. Exact arithmetic and independent convex hull checks passed, but do not replace these arguments.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The model interpolates between a simplex and a cube and contains every uniform-matroid independence polytope at integer caps. The proved exact cap provides a unique sharp isoperimetric configuration in all dimensions, an explicit two-rank decision rule for the discrete family, and an asymptotic numerical deficit. Coincidence of this cap with the *known, independently derived* John-ellipsoid contact threshold supplies a distinctive geometric relation rather than an arbitrary isolated coefficient.
