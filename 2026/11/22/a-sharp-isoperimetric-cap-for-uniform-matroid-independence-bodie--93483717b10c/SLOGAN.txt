Among all real cardinality cuts of the Euclidean cube \(K_{n,t}=[0,1]^n\cap\{\sum_i x_i\le t\}\), the surface area at fixed volume has the unique optimal cutoff \(t=(n+\sqrt n)/2\) in every dimension \(n\ge2\). The exact sharp quotient is \(2nF_n((n+\sqrt n)/2)^{1/n}\), tending to \(2n+2\log\Phi(\sqrt3)\), and the optimal integer uniform-matroid rank is one of the two neighboring integers.
