# Reproducible checks and their limits

Use `python verify.py` in the extracted finding directory. Exact rational arithmetic checks 442 conditional-density and B-spline identities and the nonpositive strict-concavity derivative bracket at noninteger sampling points. Independently generated actual vertices of capped cubes are passed to SciPy `ConvexHull`, whose volume and area are compared with the analytic formula for 28 cuts in dimensions two through five; 14 numerical cap scans, including dimension 200, locate the predicted minimizer. The checker exits successfully with `CAPPED_CUBE_SURFACE_PASS`.

These finite tests are independent diagnostics, not evidence of a universal theorem by exhaustive enumeration. The universal argument rests on facet coarea, an exact conditional symmetry identity, and strict Brunn–Minkowski concavity outside the simplex range. Asymptotic constants rely on the central and local central limit theorems for sums of independent bounded uniforms. The numerical approximation to the limiting additive surface deficit is \(2\log\Phi(\sqrt3)=-0.0850474214\ldots\).
