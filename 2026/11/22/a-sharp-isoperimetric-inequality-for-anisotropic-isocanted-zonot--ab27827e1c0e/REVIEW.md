# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Exact zonotope determinant and facet formulas reduce the quotient to \(2^d(d+Bp)^d/[P(1+dp)^{d-1}]\). Minimization in the diagonal parameter is strict; Newton's normalized elementary-symmetric inequalities eliminate all unequal axis lengths with a rigid equality condition. Independent vertex-hull calculations verify area and volume for unequal axis lengths through dimension six. No infinite theorem is inferred from those checks.

**Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk.** The 2020 isocanted polytopes, the 2024 equal-width volume theorem and the 2025 complete equal-width face-volume formulas are already established and credited. The general zonotope result of Joós and Lángi solves the *free-generator-direction* isoperimetric problem at a different, generally lower optimum; it does not imply the sharp constrained constant for positive axis lengths plus one all-ones diagonal. The contribution is the globally sharp anisotropic constrained inequality with necessary-and-sufficient equality in every dimension. Targeted published-record and research-database comparisons found no covering statement. Unindexed equivalent constrained results remain possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This natural fixed-normal-class isoperimetric problem was motivated by a published question about face-area/volume ratios of isocanted cubes. The result quantifies the price of restricting generator directions, upgrades a symmetric-parameter problem to every unequal-axis perturbation, and connects its rigid optimum to the previously known inball contact transition. It gives an exact sharp all-dimensional invariant, not a recomputation of the known symmetric face measures.

**Proof and reproducibility limitations.** Standard Newton inequalities are invoked explicitly; the enumerative checks test geometry in finite dimensions and cannot prove the general inequality. No claim is made about unrestricted zonotopes or quantitative stability. An unindexed prior result is possible.
