For every \(d\ge2\), among Euclidean zonotopes formed from arbitrarily unequal coordinate segments and one common-diagonal segment, the sharp normalized boundary-area quotient is \((\sqrt2\,d)^d[1+d(\sqrt2-1)]\). Equality is rigid: all axis lengths must coincide and the diagonal parameter must equal \((\sqrt2-1)\) times their common length.
