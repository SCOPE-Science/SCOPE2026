For the first three classical explicit Runge–Kutta stability polynomials on a damped planar rotation, fixed-mean random timesteps never improve one-step mean squared damping at orders one and two, but order three admits improvement exactly when the frequency-to-decay ratio exceeds the sharp algebraic threshold \(1.904257074337731\ldots\). Below that threshold deterministic timesteps are uniquely optimal for every mean; above it some two-point laws strictly outperform their deterministic mean step.
