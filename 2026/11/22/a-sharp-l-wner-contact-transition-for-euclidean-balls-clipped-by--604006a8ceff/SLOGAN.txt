For a Euclidean ball clipped by a coaxial round cylinder in every dimension \(d\ge3\), the exact minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid switches from a rim-supported spheroid to the original ball at the sharp radius \(s=\sqrt{(d-1)/d}\). The enclosing-volume ratio is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) there, with left-minus-right second derivative \(-2d^2\).
