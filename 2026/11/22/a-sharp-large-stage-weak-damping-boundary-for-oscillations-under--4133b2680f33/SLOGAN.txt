For the uniquely SSP-optimal third-order implicit Runge–Kutta family, the large-stage oscillatory energy defect has the exact profile \(s^2(|R_s(i\omega)|^2-1)\to\omega^2/2+\cos\omega-1>0\) at every fixed nonzero frequency. The sharp damping needed to cancel this artificial growth is \((\omega^2/4+(\cos\omega-1)/2)/s^2\), and the same coefficient controls accumulated energy over \(O(s^2)\) steps.
