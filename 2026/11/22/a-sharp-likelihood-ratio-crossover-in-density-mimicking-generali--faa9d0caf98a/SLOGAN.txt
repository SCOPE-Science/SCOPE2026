The density-mimicking generalized half-logistic count family has a sharp, explicit rate-likelihood-ratio ordering frontier: below it, the higher-rate-to-lower-rate probability ratio forms exactly one peak, while above it the ratio decreases everywhere. For shapes below one, sufficiently small rate increases break likelihood-ratio order but sufficiently large increases restore it at a unique critical rate; the same family admits exact modal cutoffs and strict log-concavity.
