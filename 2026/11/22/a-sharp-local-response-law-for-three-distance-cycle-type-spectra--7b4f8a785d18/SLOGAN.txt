Over three-distance cycle parameter metrics, the entire graded singleton-type spectrum is an explicit five-term polynomial, with exactly \((n+4)2^{n-2}\) types for every \(n\ge7\). Lowering one old distance from three to two creates exactly \(2^{n-4}\) new types for \(n\ge8\), and precisely one additional exceptional type when \(n=7\).
