For the first three real circle Fourier bands under Poisson smoothing, the optimal first/third-harmonic pair loses local stability to the missing second harmonic at a unique sharp algebraic squared radius \(s_c=0.507643227655645\ldots\). Every full three-band normer necessarily uses the second harmonic above this threshold, whereas strong smoothing \(s\le2/5\) excludes it globally; the intervening global onset is not classified.
