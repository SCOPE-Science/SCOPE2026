The expected squared Hilbert–Schmidt off-diagonal Gram leakage of critical weighted-Dirichlet kernels sampled by a logarithmically tempered boundary Poisson process is finite exactly for \(b>3/2\), with a sharp \(2/[\pi(b-3/2)]\) divergence at the threshold. In the finite-leakage regime the random sequence is almost surely multiplier-interpolating; no converse interpolation statement is claimed.
