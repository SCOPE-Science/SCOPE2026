As compact generalized Cesàro operators approach the classical \(\ell^2\) Cesàro operator, their norm reaches the endpoint value two with a sharp inverse-square logarithmic deficit: \(2-\|C_t\|\sim4\pi^2/\log^2(1/(1-t))\). A discrete Hardy ground-state transform identifies the constant as the first Dirichlet eigenvalue of a unit logarithmic interval.
