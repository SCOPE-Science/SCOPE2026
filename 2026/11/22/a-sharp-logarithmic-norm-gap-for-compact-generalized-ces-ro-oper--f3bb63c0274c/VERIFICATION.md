The algebraic inverse form is exactly \(E(t)=tF((1-t)/\sqrt t)\). The ground-state formula is an identity for every finitely supported vector, not a finite-size extrapolation. The logarithmic Dirichlet limit is established by a variational limsup and liminf argument: left-boundary positivity, interpolation with conductance asymptotics, and a right-boundary confining term. `check.py` runs exact rational tests of the inverse identity and numerical tests of the square-root transform, but these checks are ancillary to the analytic proof.
