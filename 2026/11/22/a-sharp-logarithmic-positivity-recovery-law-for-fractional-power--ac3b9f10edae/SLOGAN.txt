For powered-exponential kernels of fixed order \(1<p<2\) on cycles with vertex count divisible by four, the last loss of positive definiteness occurs at the sharp parameter scale \(2^p(p-1)(\log N)/N^p\). The Nyquist eigenvalue has an explicit critical-window limit \(2\eta(-p)-(p/2)e^{-h}\), revealing a logarithmic fractional-to-Gaussian transition.
