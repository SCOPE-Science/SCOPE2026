For a positive-floor scalar quadratic, deterministic nonnegative Gauss--Newton descent globally converges to the minimizer exactly when the normalized fixed base step satisfies \(0<\lambda\sigma\le2\); the critical case decays at an exact inverse-square-root rate. Above the threshold, a unique antipodal, locally attracting two-cycle survives at nonoptimal loss, showing that boundedness and a favorable one-step model index need not mean minimizer convergence.
