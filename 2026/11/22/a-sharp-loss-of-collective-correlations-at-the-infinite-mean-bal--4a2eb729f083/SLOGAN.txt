Under collective first-passage resetting with slow-speed CDF \(F(v)\sim c v^\alpha\), an infinite-mean renewal clock (\(N\alpha\le1\)) produces an asymptotically **independent** cloud of \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,\alpha)\) particle positions, despite every particle sharing one reset event. For exact power-distributed speeds, above the sharp boundary \(N\alpha=1\) the stationary pair covariance becomes strictly positive, with an explicit closed formula.
