For random width-three minimizers on a stationary binary Markov source forbidding `11`, the exact expected density has two competing periodic-collision corrections whose dominant sign changes sharply at the golden-mean parameter \(p=(\sqrt5-1)/2\). At that maximal-entropy parameter the density is \(1/2\) plus a positive \(\varphi^{-k}\) correction with two distinct coefficients according to \(k\bmod3\), for every \(k\ge2\).
