A rank-one digital correction can reduce every individual analog-solve error yet destroy the mean-square convergence of randomized Richardson iteration: the raw stability boundary is \(\delta<1/(4K^2+2)\), while the corrected orientation factor is \(K^2\cos^2(2\theta)+\delta\). In the same two-dimensional model, arbitrarily small independent Gaussian setup-probe noise gives a positive probability of learning an unstable correction space, even when noiseless probing learns a stable one.
