Keeping an \(N\)-point Fourier interpolation grid fixed, the unique most erasure-robust location for one additional equal-weight sample is the midpoint of a grid gap, with an exact parity-dependent lower frame bound. This minimal repair has sharp worst-erasure stability of order \(N^{-2}\), while moving all \(N+1\) sampling points to a uniform grid attains order \(N^{-1}\).
