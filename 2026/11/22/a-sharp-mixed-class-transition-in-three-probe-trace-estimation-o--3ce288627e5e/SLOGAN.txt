For three independent class-supported Rademacher trace probes on the screened Laplacian inverse of \(K_{n,n}\), \(n\ge3\), every variance-optimal partition has a mixed color class for \(0<\mu<n(\sqrt2-1)\), while every optimum is a proper coloring above that sharp threshold. An exact one-dimensional discrete-concave formula classifies all partitions, screening thresholds, and ties, including coexistence at \(\mu=n(\sqrt2-1)\).
