For a heterogeneous SIR epidemic with \(k\) known susceptibility classes, exactly \(k\) susceptibility--infectivity mixed moments determine final epidemic size; when the first \(k-1\) moments agree, the sign of the final-size difference is the sign of the last moment difference multiplied by \((-1)^k\). The cutoff is sharp even when both populations have identical susceptibility and infectivity marginals and the same reproduction threshold.
