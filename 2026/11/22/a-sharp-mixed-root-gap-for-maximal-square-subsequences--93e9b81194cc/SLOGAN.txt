The shortest binary word admitting a mixed-letter maximal square subsequence strictly shorter than a longest square has length seven, with exactly eight witnesses in two reversal/complementation orbits. On the binary family \(W_m=0^m1\,0\,1\,0^m\), the mixed root `10` is maximal even though a longest root has length \(m\), so the longest-to-this-maximal length ratio grows without bound.
