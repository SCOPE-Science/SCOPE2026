Under the fixed-sum Steele response \(f(x)=xe^{-x}\), two identical light-driven modules abandon equal illumination continuously at total load \(S=4\), but every system of at least three modules undergoes a sharp discontinuous switch to \(n-1\) equally illuminated modules and one deliberately overilluminated module. Exact one-variable equations give the global transition and all optimal allocations for every \(n\ge2\) and every \(S>0\); the result assumes no incoming light can be discarded.
