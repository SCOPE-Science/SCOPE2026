A single polynomial planar family has strictly positive angular velocity and global radial attraction at every order, yet its origin is monodromic exactly for integer orders \(k\ge2\); at \(k=1\) all trajectories approach a fixed ray after only finitely much angular travel. In the monodromic regime, the physical-time number of turns has the exact sharp asymptotic \(N(t)\sim\frac{\sqrt{bc}}{2\pi a(2k-3)}(2kat)^{(2k-3)/(2k)}\), quantifying the effect of angular bottlenecks near two characteristic directions.
