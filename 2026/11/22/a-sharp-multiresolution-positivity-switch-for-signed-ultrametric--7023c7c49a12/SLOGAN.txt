On the compact \(p\)-adic integers, the largest positive subtraction of two radial exponential-distance covariances is determined exactly by the Haar mass when the first rate is larger, and by the high-conductor limit when it is smaller. Canonical finite quotient sampling shifts the latter threshold by a sharp positive multiple of \(p^{-N}\) and creates exactly \(p^N-p^{N-1}\) missing Fourier modes at the endpoint.
