The proof is reconstructed for every integer \(m\ge1\), positive attenuation and travel time, strictly positive source profile, independent frequencies and arbitrary initial phases. The reduction from two different water parcels to a sum of \(m\) independently rotatable sinusoids is exact. Rational independence implies continuous-time torus equidistribution by the vanishing character averages, not by numerical recurrence; the analytic level set has zero measure. At the critical modulation the inequality is strict and cannot hold. The near-critical feasible region is an ellipsoid after \(\sqrt{\rho-\rho_c}\) scaling; its volume supplies the exact \(m/2\) exponent and coefficient. The bound \(c_j\ge b_j(E-1)\) supplies the sharp clock-matching condition. The package contains a reproducible Python standard-library checker with 1,531 successfully executed supplementary diagnostics. The checker is not presented as a proof of equidistribution or of the infinite-dimensional quantifiers.

## Value — PASS

The question is motivated by the known danger of interpreting asynchronous river samples as matched parcels. The formula gives an all-parameter criterion for whether a completely source-free, genuinely attenuating reach can be falsely diagnosed as gaining contaminant, and the \(m/2\)-power law quantifies how often such misleading results first appear as forcing intensifies. This distinction can inform idealized monitoring sensitivity analyses; the result is not merely a restatement of first-order decay because the inference uses two different water parcels. The continuous-time, no-dispersion and quasiperiodic hypotheses are restrictive; finite survey windows and actual field designs require independent validation.
