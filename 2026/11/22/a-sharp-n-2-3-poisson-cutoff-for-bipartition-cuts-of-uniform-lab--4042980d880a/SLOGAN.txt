For the number of edges crossing a prescribed small-side cut of a uniform labelled tree, a mean-corrected shifted Poisson law is accurate in total variation throughout \(a=o(n)\). The natural uncorrected approximation fails sharply at \(a\asymp n^{2/3}\), with exact transition profile \(2\Phi(c^{3/2})-1\) when \(a/n^{2/3}\to c\).
