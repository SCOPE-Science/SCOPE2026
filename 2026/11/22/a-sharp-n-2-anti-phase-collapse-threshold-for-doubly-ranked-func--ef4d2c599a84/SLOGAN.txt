For two anti-phase measurements of grouped functional data, the average-rank and logit-rank summaries are simultaneously identical for every subject precisely when Gaussian measurement noise causes no pair-order reversal. As sample size \(N\) grows, this complete loss of score information has a sharp \(N^{-2}\) noise threshold with explicit critical probability \(\exp[-c\int h^2/\sqrt\pi]\), even for groups that are consistently separable from their raw measurements.
