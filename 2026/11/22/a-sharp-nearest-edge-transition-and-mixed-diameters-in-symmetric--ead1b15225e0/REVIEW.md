The diameter argument checks each of the three unordered vertex-pair types and classifies all equality pairs. The minimum argument proves an all-size unique mixed-star minimum and a complete hollow–hollow minimum using nested and crossing rectangle cases; the exact comparison changes sign between orders four and five. An independent matrix constructor and exact rational checker cover every pair in orders two through twelve; all cases pass. These finite checks supplement, rather than substitute for, the symbolic proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The natural Frobenius metric measures the size of differences between normalized symmetric Monge cost matrices. Its shortest and longest simplex edges quantify both the finest vertex-scale separation and the worst possible disagreement of extreme cost patterns. The findings exhibit **different governing vertex families at opposite metric extremes**, including a sharp order-five switch in the shortest-edge family, with exact counts and an \(n^{3/2}\) metric-spread law. This is a complete, meaningful extremal metric classification of a recently defined simplex, rather than a renamed face count or an arbitrary fixed-dimension enumeration. Its asymptotic is derived from exact all-size formulas. No implication for arbitrary nonsymmetric Monge polytopes or approximation algorithms is claimed.
