The full normalized symmetric Monge simplex has a sharp nearest-edge transition: the two shortest edges join additive-star and corner-block vertices in orders \(2\)–\(4\), but for every \(n\ge5\) all shortest edges lie in its hollow subsimplex. Every diameter in orders \(n\ge3\) instead joins the unique smallest corner block to one of \(n-2\) interior stars, and the ratio of diameter to shortest-edge length grows as \(2n^{3/2}/(3\sqrt3)\).
