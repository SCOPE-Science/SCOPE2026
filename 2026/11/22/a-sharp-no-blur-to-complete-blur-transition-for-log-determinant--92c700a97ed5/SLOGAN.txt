In the two-pixel Fourier model of log-determinant-regularized deblurring, sufficiently small auxiliary scale forces a sharp **global jump from no blur to complete contrast extinction** at \(w_{\mathrm{jump}}=B/[(1+\ell)\log((1+d)/d)]\), with no optimal intermediate blur. Throughout a nonempty interval after the jump, the inferior no-blur operator remains a strict local minimizer separated by a unique barrier, so local stability can conceal catastrophic global overblurring.
