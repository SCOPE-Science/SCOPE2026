In a three-diffusing-species serial-inhibitor system with cubic activator saturation, a patterned stationary state exists exactly when a Neumann mode is linearly Turing-unstable—there are no hidden finite-amplitude steady patterns at or below the threshold. The critical inhibitor diffusivity has an exact finite-length formula and a wavelength-commensurability correction of order \(L^{-2}\), with patterned states impossible at every diffusivity on sufficiently short intervals.
