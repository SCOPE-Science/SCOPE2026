For a four-state reversible nearest-neighbor walk with stationary masses proportional to \((1,t,t,1)\), the globally fastest mixing chain is unique for every \(t>0\), even when asymmetric edge flows are permitted. When \(0<t<1\), the exact non-log-concave optimum saturates both interior row budgets and has SLEM \(1-2t/(t+3+2\sqrt2)\); at \(t=1\) it joins the familiar harmonic-flow branch with a sharp derivative kink.
