For one-sweep restarted two-level optimization with symmetric positive-definite fine and coarse contractions that need not commute, every-start convergence in the long-trajectory limit holds exactly when \(\Psi\prec(I+F^2)(I+F)^{-1}\). In a two-mode example, merely rotating the coarse eigenspaces changes convergence into divergence at the sharp boundary \(\sin^2\theta=2/15\), despite both component propagators remaining strictly contractive.
