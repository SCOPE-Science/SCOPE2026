For two BOD outfalls in an idealized Streeter–Phelps river where reaeration is twice deoxygenation, the unique split minimizing the worst downstream oxygen deficit increases its upstream share from one-half to three-quarters and then back to one-half as the outfalls separate. The exact switch is \(kd=\log3\), where two equally severe oxygen-deficit locations first become active in the global minimax solution.
