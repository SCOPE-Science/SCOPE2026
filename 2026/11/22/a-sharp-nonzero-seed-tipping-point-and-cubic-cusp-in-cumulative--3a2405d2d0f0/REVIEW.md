# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem uses a conserved population simplex and an exact susceptible–retired first integral to select the earliest physically accessible final-size root, not merely any algebraic root. The derivative is controlled by a concave quadratic with an exact parameter discriminant. The sign pattern provides a full classification, and local Taylor expansions prove the signed cubic-root singularity, one jump and the two critical time-decay rates. The executable numerical checker corroborates representative cases and is not used in place of the proof.

## Originality — PASS (bounded by inspected sources)

The motivating 2025–2026 paper by Xue, Yang, Cui and Di already reports reinforced-contagion jumps on networks. It does not cover the exact positive-seed bifurcation of the **separately defined continuous-time homogeneous-mixing model**. The older St-Onge et al. hypergraph work already develops nonlinear contagion, bistability, tricriticality and seeding ideas; the present result does not claim those general concepts as new. The specific exact boundary, size-selected upper final-size branch and signed cubic-root coefficient were not located in the searches performed. Direct access to the full published 2022 hypergraph article was blocked; separate supplemental files to the motivating paper were not completely inspected. Historical priority is therefore provisional.

## Value — PASS

The threshold gives a precise intervention question: when can changing *only initial adoption*, without changing a subcritical linear spreading coefficient, cause a population-scale final-size jump? It also distinguishes a continuous but non-differentiable cusp from a finite jump, supplying sharp examples and critical time scales for model testing. This is a natural structural question, not a hand-selected numerical curiosity.

## Scientific limitations

The ODE is a deterministic, homogeneous-mixing continuous-rate analogue, not an exact reduction of the discrete-time capped-probability network paper. It does not establish an empirical social tipping fraction, predict real adoption, or include finite-network stochastic fluctuations. A full literature priority audit and independent mathematical validation remain outstanding.
