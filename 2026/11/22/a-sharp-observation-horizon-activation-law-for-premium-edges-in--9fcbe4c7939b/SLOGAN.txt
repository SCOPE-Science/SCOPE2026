For diffusion on a star with one premium-priced spoke, minimizing finite-time heat-trace error has one exact observation-horizon activation: the premium edge is uniquely assigned zero conductance at short horizons, then positive conductance forever after the sharp threshold. With \(m\) leaves and premium price \(r>1\), the activation time satisfies \(BT_m/(m-1)\to\log r\) as \(m\to\infty\).
