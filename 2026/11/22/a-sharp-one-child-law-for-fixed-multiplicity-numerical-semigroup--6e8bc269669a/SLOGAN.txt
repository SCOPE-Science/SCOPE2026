For every fixed multiplicity \(m\ge3\), asymptotically almost every genus-\(g\) numerical semigroup has exactly one child in the ordinary semigroup tree. The failure probability is sharply \(\Theta_m(1/g)\): at least two effective generators already occur in \(\Theta_m(g^{m-3})\) semigroups, while the full genus slice has \(\Theta_m(g^{m-2})\) elements.
