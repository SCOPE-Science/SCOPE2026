# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The type/gap identification is justified by the two independent dense linear orders. The proof derives the exact disagreement count as a symmetric difference of prefix sets. Its parity and cardinality lower bounds restrict one-disagreement types to the \(2n\) neighboring gap cells. The two all-cells conditions are equivalent to displacement at most one, hence exactly disjoint adjacent swaps. Each such swap changes just one diagonal prefix intersection, yielding the whole polynomial. The linear and constant coefficients give the exact fibre converse and binomial count. The direct checker exhaustively covers all patterns through eight points; finite testing does not substitute for the proof.
