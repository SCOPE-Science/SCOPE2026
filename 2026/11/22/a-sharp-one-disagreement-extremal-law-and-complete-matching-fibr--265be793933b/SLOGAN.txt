Among nonalgebraic one-point types over \(n\) named points in the generic two-order structure, at most \(2n\) disagree with precisely one named point; equality holds exactly when the named permutation is a product of disjoint adjacent swaps. For these extremizers the entire disagreement polynomial depends only on the number \(s\) of swaps, and its exact fibre contains \(\binom{n-s}{s}\) distinct permutations.
