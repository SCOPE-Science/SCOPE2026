On the seventeen-vertex Paley graph, exactly the 68 nonadjacent sensor pairs are minimum power-dominating placements; their unrestricted and adjacent-slide relocation graphs are respectively a line graph and a Gallai graph. Both have diameter \(3\), but exactly \(1020\) of the \(2278\) unordered placement pairs need one extra move when sensor replacements are restricted to adjacent vertices, and none need two.
