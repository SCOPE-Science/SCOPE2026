For the unit triangular path \(T_n(t)=I_n-tS_n\), freezing the scalar polynomial from one exact GMRES training step preserves or reduces the Euclidean residual of **every** subsequent right-hand side in **every** dimension precisely when \(0\le t\le1/3\). The sharp fixed-size thresholds are \(2/3\) in dimension two and \(\sqrt{3\sqrt2-4}\) in dimension three; above the uniform threshold, a transferred fit can amplify a new residual even though its training residual strictly decreases.
