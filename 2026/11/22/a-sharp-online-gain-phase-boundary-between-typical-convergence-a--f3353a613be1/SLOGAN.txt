For the actual stochastic ratio-feedback learning rule, a sharp two-curvature threshold \(b=7.739425778\ldots\) permits a stationary learned stepsize whose **fresh-test prospective squared multiplier exceeds one**. Nevertheless, the coupled optimization iterates converge exponentially almost surely at every admissible learning gain, with logarithmic rate at most \(\log((b-1)/(b+1))\); prospective squared amplification is not mean-square divergence of the actual iterates.
