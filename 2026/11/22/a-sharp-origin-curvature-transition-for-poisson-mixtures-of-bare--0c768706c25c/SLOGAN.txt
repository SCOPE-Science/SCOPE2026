The Poisson-age mixture of one-dimensional Barenblatt packets is differentiable at its reset point for every \(\sigma>0\), but has finite origin curvature exactly when \(\sigma>1\). Below that boundary its density loses a positive multiple of \(|x|^{1+\sigma}\); precisely at \(\sigma=1\), the loss is a positive multiple of \(x^2\log(1/|x|)\).
