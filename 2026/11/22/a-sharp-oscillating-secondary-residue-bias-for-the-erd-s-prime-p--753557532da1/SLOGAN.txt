For the Erdős prime-power quotient sum \(w(n)=\sum_{p^a\parallel n}n/p^a\), the counts of residues \(1\) and \(2\) modulo three have equal leading density but a sharp, infinitely sign-changing secondary difference of order \(X/\log X\). Its explicitly nonzero Euler-product coefficient rotates with phase \((\sqrt3/2)\log\log X\), yielding exact positive and negative normalized limit extrema.
