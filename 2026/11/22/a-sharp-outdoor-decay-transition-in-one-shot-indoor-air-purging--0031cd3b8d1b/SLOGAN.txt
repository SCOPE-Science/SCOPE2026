For one instantaneous fresh-air purge against a decaying outdoor pollution episode and continuing indoor emissions, cumulative exposure has a unique beneficial purge time only beyond a sharp horizon. The optimum always lies after the horizon midpoint, and its long-horizon share changes at the rate boundary \(\beta=a\): slowly vanishing outdoor pollution pushes it to \(a/(a+\beta)>1/2\), even when its initial amplitude is arbitrarily small but positive.
