## Value — PASS
The exact group-size cutoff answers the operational mathematical question: when can a population-aggregated slope exceed every actual linear transmission slope, without any within-group synergy? The unusual logarithmic amplitude shows that this measurement artifact can be arbitrarily large for vanishingly rare high-transmission groups if group size is correspondingly large, and that discrete infection counts leave a periodic leading fluctuation. The result strengthens a previously qualitative warning into sharp testable bounds on a well-motivated observable. This is not a claim about a universal real-world epidemic threshold.
