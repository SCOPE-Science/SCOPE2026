For every fixed sample size \(n\ge2\), the two-sided location-normalized Kolmogorov--Smirnov statistic has a sharp Pareto-two null tail \(\Pr(V_n^*>t)=2(n-1)!/(n^nt^2)+O_n(t^{-4})\), generated by two symmetric endpoint configurations. Consequently its mean exists but its variance is infinite at every finite sample size, with an explicit logarithmic truncated-second-moment coefficient.
