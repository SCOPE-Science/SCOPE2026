# Review: double-star sink-root transition

**Correctness — PASS.** Edge-orientation decomposition, factorization, sign restrictions on positive shifted arguments, Descartes sign counts, the explicit Bernstein positivity certificate, and the exact rational threshold witness establish the all-order claim. The bundled checker independently enumerates smaller trees and replays the certificate.

**Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority.** The motivating arXiv:2610.41502v1 proves a line-graph double-star formula and claw-free root theorems, not the present tree root census. arXiv:1910.10920v2 supplies general definitions and expansions. Comparable published square- and clique-bouquet results have different underlying graph families; their full texts were not all accessible. No equivalent or stronger classification was identified in the performed searches.

**Value — PASS.** The sharp transition at odd parameter \(b=15\), together with a full parity-dependent root census and limiting locations, describes a natural boundary to real-rootedness behavior for a simple branching tree family. The finding is not a claim about arbitrary double stars.

**Limitations.** The public literature search is targeted, not exhaustive; additional historical sources could alter priority. Finite computations are supporting checks and cannot alone certify the infinite theorem. No independent mathematical validation is claimed.
