In double-star trees with two leaves at one hub and \(b\ge2\) leaves at the other, the acyclic sink polynomial has a fixed double zero at \(-1\) and a simple zero at \(0\), but its additional real zeros undergo a sharp parity-dependent transition. Even \(b\) always gives one extra negative real zero, odd \(b\le13\) gives none, and odd \(b\ge15\) gives exactly two, asymptotically approaching \(-2\) and \(-(5+\sqrt{17})/4\).
