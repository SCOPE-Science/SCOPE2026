The constrained Hilbert–Schmidt commutator energy of the two-shift tuple \((S^2,\varepsilon S)\) has a sharp weak-coupling transition: \(N\mathcal E_N(c/N)\to4c\coth(c)/(1+c\coth(c))\), rising from \(2\) to \(4\). The unique finite-section minimizer is diagonal, and its two parity profiles converge to explicit linear and hyperbolic-sine laws.
