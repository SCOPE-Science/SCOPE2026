For irrational circle rotations, the single-window ordinal entropy stays within a bounded additive distance of \(\log n\) at every order exactly when the rotation frequency has bounded continued-fraction partial quotients. A large next partial quotient \(a\) produces a sharp maximal logarithmic deficit \(\log a+O(\log\log a)\), revealing arithmetic differences invisible to the leading \(H_n/\log n\) law.
