Resetting a harmonic Brownian particle to an equal mixture of thermal-width Gaussian packets centered at \(\pm L\) produces exactly one stationary peak for reset-to-relaxation ratio \(a\le1\), but for \(a>1\) creates exactly two peaks precisely beyond one explicitly determined separation threshold. The transition is quartically flat and the two peaks split with the exact law \(m^2\sim3(s_c-s)\), where \(s=D/(\kappa L^2)\).
