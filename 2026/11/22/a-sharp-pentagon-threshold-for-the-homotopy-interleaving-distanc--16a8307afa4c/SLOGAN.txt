For finite connected unit-edge cactus graphs with no cycle longer than five, the ordered enriched nerve's homotopy-category interleaving distance to a point is exactly \(\tfrac12\) when every cycle has length at most four and exactly \(1\) as soon as a five-cycle occurs. Any cactus containing a cycle of length at least five has interleaving distance at least \(1\); the sharp phase change follows from short rooted-block contractions and an integral winding cocycle.
