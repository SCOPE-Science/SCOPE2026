For the shifted convex-splitting BDF2 method on two mass-conserving Cahn–Hilliard cells, the sharp near-equilibrium phase-sign threshold is \(\lambda\tau=(\sqrt{1+4/(\varepsilon^2\lambda-1)}-1)/4\) when \(\varepsilon^2\lambda>1\). Above it, arbitrarily small positive exact-start data produce infinitely many sign reversals even though the numerical phase converges to zero and the method dissipates modified energy.
