For a uniformly shuffled two-colour multiset with both colour proportions bounded away from zero, the fixed-position count stays at asymptotic total-variation distance at least \(0.533230687960\ldots\) from the Poisson law of matching mean, with equality only for asymptotically equal colour proportions. Even conditioning the Poisson law to the correct parity leaves a sharp universal distance of at least \(0.166064074983\ldots\), explained by an irreducible Gaussian variance mismatch.
