In a row-reversing environmental cooperation game with exactly equal deterministic basins, rare-cooperator versus rare-defector fixation odds reverse at the sharp population threshold \(N_*(T)=(10+T)/(2-T)\), for every positive Fermi selection intensity. Yet a single rare cooperator fixes with strictly less than neutral probability at every population size; for \(T=3/2\), the odds reversal occurs exactly at \(N=23\).
