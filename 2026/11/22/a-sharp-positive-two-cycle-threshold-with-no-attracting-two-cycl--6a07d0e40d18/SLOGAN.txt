At the unit Euler step of the modified reduced Lorenz map, a strictly positive primitive two-cycle exists exactly when \(a>a_*\), where \(a_*\) is the unique positive root of \(3a^3-10a^2-8a-4=0\), and is then unique. Its Floquet spectrum always has an expanding multiplier, so the flip creates no attracting positive two-cycle at any parameter.
