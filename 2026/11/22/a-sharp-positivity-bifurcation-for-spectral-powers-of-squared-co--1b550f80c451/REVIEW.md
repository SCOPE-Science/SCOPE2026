# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Fourier diagonalization gives the five nonzero spectral coefficients; minimization over the cosine interval yields an exact two-branch scalar kernel minimum. Strict concavity in the short-time branch, convexity in the scalar Fourier-ratio variable, monotonicity of that ratio in the shape parameter, and the nondegenerate fold prove the complete parameter/time classification. Supplementary arbitrary-precision tests confirm its formulas at representative cases, but no finite test substitutes for the analytic proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The closest inspected open-access paper (Mui, arXiv:2210.06135, published 2023) treats locally eventually positive strongly continuous semigroups on Banach lattices; it does not give this finite-Fourier-multiplier kernel positivity classification, and the present spectral powers fail strong continuity at zero. A published Fejér–Jackson result studies spatial powers of convolution kernels and their Lipschitz norm, rather than operator spectral powers; a second circle heat-kernel paper studies positive definiteness of modulated heat kernels, rather than pointwise nonnegativity of the current powered convolution kernel. A distinct quadratic Bernstein–Durrmeyer spectral-power example already exhibits disconnected positivity times, so only the sharp two-parameter squared-cosine phase diagram and fold are presented as new. The close comparison reports rely in part on published summaries, and specialized historical treatments remain a risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A canonical positive, band-limited Markov convolution has a complete identifiability-style positivity phase diagram: the exact amplitude threshold separates an interval of admissible powers from two isolated intervals, with an explicit nondegenerate fold coefficient. This is a natural stability question for interpolating integer Markov powers and is not an arbitrary finite slice.

No wider kernel-family classification or independent mathematical validation is claimed.
