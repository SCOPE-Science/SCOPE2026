The second-order two-sided filtered Heun heat step is unconditionally energy contractive at splitting ratio \(s/a\ge0.619684\ldots\), but on a three-vertex path it preserves nonnegative states at **every** timestep precisely when \(s/a\ge0.675255515112861\ldots\). Below this sharp positivity boundary, a single bounded timestep window creates a negative remote-node transition despite symmetric mass preservation and Euclidean contraction.
