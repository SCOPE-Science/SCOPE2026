For third-order explicit Runge–Kutta on every three-state one-way Markov cycle with arbitrary positive exit rates, entrywise end-step positivity has an exact single cutoff: either \(3/(a+b+c)\) or the unique smaller zero of the fastest-rate diagonal cubic. All forward transitions remain positive, and unequal rates always permit a strictly larger positive end-step than the universal forward-Euler bound.
