For a Gaussian-kernel quadrature rule using the origin and \(\pm a e_j\), all outer weights are positive and the central weight changes sign at exactly one explicit scalar-equation radius. The minimum nonnegative-weight radius grows as \(\ell\sqrt{2\log(2d)/(1+u)}\) for fixed \(u=\ell^2/(\ell^2+\tau^2)\), sharpening a previously observed high-dimensional negative-origin-weight phenomenon.
