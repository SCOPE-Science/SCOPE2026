A decaying three-state rotational semigroup can repeatedly regain and lose entrywise positivity before becoming strictly positive forever: the exact number of failure intervals is \(\lceil(3H(a)/(\pi a)-1)/2\rceil\), where \(H(a)=\log(2/\sqrt{1+a^2})+a\arctan a\) and \(a=\delta/\omega\in(0,\sqrt3)\). The number of separated positive-time components is unbounded as damping vanishes, and three is the smallest matrix dimension in which this can happen.
