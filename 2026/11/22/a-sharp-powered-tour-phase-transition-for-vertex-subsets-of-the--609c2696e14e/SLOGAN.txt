Among all nontrivial subsets of the eight vertices of the three-cube, the minimum powered-Euclidean Hamiltonian tour has sharp worst-case cost \(\max\{4\cdot2^{p/2},2\cdot3^{p/2}\}\) for every real \(p\ge2\). The unique extremal geometric type switches from a parity tetrahedron to an antipodal pair at \(p=2\log2/\log(3/2)\); a twenty-orbit exact certificate and Hölder interpolation prove the complete phase transition.
