For the exact-data Simpson scalar energy balance of a shrinking circle, positive endpoint radii undergo a sharp fold at \(h/R_0^2=0.43657451259244\ldots\), before physical extinction; the consistent branch has a square-root conditioning singularity. A round sphere's analogous scalar energy balance is exactly solvable throughout its pre-extinction lifetime. The comparison concerns isolated scalar constraints, not solvability of the complete geometric BDF algorithm.
