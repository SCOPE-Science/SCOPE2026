# A sharp prime-adic sparse-cylinder hitting-time law for binomial cellular automata

## Finding
For a binomial linear cellular automaton over a finite field, the number of attainable preimage patterns on a sparse arithmetic progression is given by an exact prime-adic rank law at **every** time. The threshold at which every prescribed sparse pattern can precede every full target configuration is sharp and can jump by arbitrarily large factors as the sampling step changes by a prime power.

**Theorem.** Let \(\mathbb F_q\) have characteristic \(p\), let \(a,b\in\mathbb F_q^\times\) and \(k,N\ge1\), \(m\ge2\) be integers, and define the cellular automaton \((Fx)_i=ax_i+bx_{i+k}\) on \(\mathbb F_q^{\mathbb Z}\). Fix \(E=\{0,N,\dots,(m-1)N\}\). For every \(t\ge1\) and every target \(y\in\mathbb F_q^{\mathbb Z}\), the possible predecessor traces \(\{x|_E:F^t x=y\}\) form an affine subspace of cardinality \(q^{d_t}\), where \(g=\gcd(k,N)\), \(R=k/g\), \(v=p^{\nu_p(N/g)}\), \(s_c=\max\{0,1+\lfloor(m-1-c)/R\rfloor\}\) for \(0\le c<R\), and \(d_t=\sum_{c=0}^{R-1}\min\{s_c,\lceil t/v\rceil\}\). In particular, for EVERY pattern on \(E\), its cylinder has full image under \(F^t\) if and only if \(t\ge t_*=v(\lceil m/R\rceil-1)+1\). This threshold is exact for every choice of field and nonzero coefficients, and exhibits unbounded prime-adic spacing resonance: for \(k=1\) and \(N=p^j\), \(t_*=(m-1)p^j+1\), while for \(k=1\) and \(p\nmid N\) one has \(t_*=m\).

## Assumptions and scope
Let \(\mathbb F_q\) be any finite field with characteristic prime \(p\); the alphabet size \(q\) need not be prime. Set \(X=\mathbb F_q^\mathbb Z\) with its full-shift product topology. Fix nonzero \(a,b\in\mathbb F_q\), positive integers \(k,N\), and \(m\ge2\). Define \((Fx)_i=ax_i+bx_{i+k}\) simultaneously for all \(i\in\mathbb Z\). Fix the finite site set \(E=\{0,N,\ldots,(m-1)N}\). For a full target \(y\in X\) and integer \(t\ge1\), write \(\mathcal P_t(y)=\{(x_0,x_N,\ldots,x_{(m-1)N}):F^t x=y\}\).

Let \(g=\gcd(k,N)\), \(R=k/g\), and let \(v=p^{\nu_p(N/g)}\) be the largest \(p\)-power dividing \(N/g\). For \(c=0,\ldots,R-1\), put \(s_c=\max\{0,1+\lfloor(m-1-c)/R\rfloor}\), the number of observed sites whose ordinal in \(E\) is congruent to \(c\pmod R\). In particular, \(\max_{0\le c<R}s_c=\lceil m/R\rceil\).

The theorem concerns the entire bi-infinite configuration space, not cyclic rings, finite windows of output, or periodic solutions. The quantity \(\dim\mathcal P_t(y)\) means its affine dimension over \(\mathbb F_q\).

## Proof
**1. Surjectivity and affine fibers.** The equation \(Fx=y\) separates along the \(k\) residue classes modulo \(k\). On each class it becomes \(a u_j+b u_{j+1}=v_j\). Because both \(a\) and \(b\) are nonzero, choose any \(u_0\) and solve uniquely forwards and backwards. Thus \(F\), and hence \(F^t\), is onto. Every fiber is an affine translate of \(K_t=\ker F^t\); therefore \(\mathcal P_t(y)\) is an affine translate of the linear trace image \(\pi_E(K_t)\). Its cardinality is \(q^{\dim\pi_E(K_t)}\).

**2. Kernel decomposition.** Write \(T\) for the forward unit shift of a sequence. On each residue class modulo \(k\), \(F^t=(a+bT)^t\). Its kernel is the space \(V_t\) of bi-infinite sequences satisfying the order-\(t\) recurrence \((a+bT)^t u=0\). Since the constant and leading coefficients are \(a^t\) and \(b^t\), both nonzero, any \(t\) consecutive values specify a unique two-sided solution. Thus \(\dim V_t=t\).

The dual coordinate-evaluation space of \(V_t\) is identified with \(A_t=\mathbb F_q[z,z^{-1}]/(a+bz)^t\): the functional \(u\mapsto u_j\) corresponds to \(z^j\). This identification is an isomorphism because \(t\) consecutive evaluations are free and independent; the recurrence gives exactly the relations generated by \((a+bz)^t\). In particular, the rank of evaluations on any finite set equals the dimension of the corresponding monomials in \(A_t\).

**3. Prime-adic progression lemma.** Let \(h\ge1\) and \(s\ge0\). The span in \(A_t\) of \(1,z^h,\ldots,z^{(s-1)h}\) has dimension exactly \(\min\{s,\lceil t/p^{\nu_p(h)}\rceil}\). To prove it, put \(\zeta=-a/b\ne0\) and \(u=z-\zeta\). Then \(A_t\cong\mathbb F_q[u]/(u^t)\), and \(z\) remains invertible. Write \(h=p^e d\) with \(p\nmid d\) and \(v_h=p^e\). In characteristic \(p\),
\[
z^h-\zeta^h=\bigl((\zeta+u)^d-\zeta^d\bigr)^{v_h}=u^{v_h}U(u),\qquad U(0)=(d\zeta^{d-1})^{v_h}\ne0.
\]
Thus \(w=z^h-\zeta^h\) has exact \(u\)-adic order \(v_h\); its powers \(w^j\) have distinct orders \(jv_h<t\), while all later powers vanish. Changing the spanning family from powers of \(z^h\) to powers of \(w=z^h-\zeta^h\) is an invertible triangular binomial change of basis. Hence the claimed dimension is \(\min\{s,\lceil t/v_h\rceil}\). Multiplication by a fixed monomial \(z^j\) preserves the rank, so the statement holds for progressions beginning at any index.

**4. Arithmetic splitting.** The observed sites indexed by ordinals \(c,c+R,c+2R,\ldots\) lie in one residue class modulo \(k\), and different \(c\) with \(0\le c<R\) use different classes. In the coordinate \(j=(i-r)/k\) along each class, successive such sites have difference \(RN/k=N/g=h\), and their count is \(s_c\). The classes contribute independently to the trace image. By Step 3 their ranks add to
\[
\dim\pi_E(K_t)=\sum_{c=0}^{R-1}\min\bigl\{s_c,\lceil t/v\rceil\bigr\}=d_t,\qquad v=p^{\nu_p(N/g)}.
\]
Together with Step 1 this proves the exact affine cardinality for every \(y\).

**5. Sharp uniform hitting time.** For any pattern \(P\in\mathbb F_q^E\), the image \(F^t(\mathrm{Cyl}(E,P))\) equals \(X\) precisely when \(\pi_E(K_t)=\mathbb F_q^E\): given any \(y\), choose one preimage and correct its trace using a kernel element. By the exact rank law, this holds iff \(\lceil t/v\rceil\ge\max_{0\le c<R}s_c=\lceil m/R\rceil\), namely iff
\[
t\ge v\bigl(\lceil m/R\rceil-1\bigr)+1.
\]
For every smaller positive \(t\), the affine trace of **every** fiber has dimension strictly less than \(m\). Hence no pattern cylinder has full image, and the threshold is attained sharply. In particular \(k=1\) yields \(t_*=p^{\nu_p(N)}(m-1)+1\). This completes the proof.

## Verification
The standalone script `verify_reach.py` computes the evaluation rank by a method separate from Step 3: it generates solutions by evolving \(t\) free initial values under the binomial recurrence and performs Gaussian elimination on their observed coordinates. For primes \(2,3,5\), strides \(k=1,2,3,4\), spacings \(N=1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,12\), sample counts \(m=2,3,4,5\), and ten tested times (plus six larger-time examples), it checks 4,326 rank equalities. Output: `PASS: 4326 exact recurrence-matrix rank comparisons; no exhaustive infinite claim`. The theorem for all parameters, including extension fields, is established by the proof rather than finite experiments.

As a concrete arithmetic example in characteristic \(2\), for \(k=1\), \(N=8\), and \(m=3\), the ranks at times \(8,9,16,17\) are respectively \(1,2,2,3\); the first time all three observed values can be prescribed in a preimage of every target is \(17\). For \(k=1\), \(m=3\), and spacing \(N\) not divisible by \(2\), the same threshold is only \(3\).

## Relationship to prior work
Castronuovo, Dennunzio, Kari and Margara (2026, arXiv:2611.16746v1) compute topological entropy of group cellular automata through space-time traces and algebraic methods. The invariant here is instead a **fixed-time, sparse-coordinate projection rank of a full-target preimage fiber**, a quantity not specified by an entropy formula. The 2019 paper of Dennunzio, Formenti, Grinberg and Margara proves qualitative strong-transitivity relations and its Lemma 18 gives existence of a uniform cylinder hitting time, but not the numerical rank or sharp prime-adic delay. The 2026 paper of Castronuovo, Dennunzio and Margara (arXiv:2611.22365v1; publicly posted on 2026-11-17) states a general qualitative strong-transitivity and kernel-density criterion (Lemma 1 and Lemma 3). The present formula is a specialized quantitative refinement, not a new claim of strong transitivity itself. The latter source is included as a comparison, not as the motivating source.

## Limitations
The exact law assumes **two nonzero scalar coefficients**, the full bi-infinite shift, and equally spaced coordinate observations. It does not extend automatically to arbitrary multi-term or matrix cellular automata, nonlinear rules, finite cyclic rings, or irregular sampling sets. It does not derive a new formula for topological entropy, and does not prove complete historical uniqueness of the rank identity. Computational checks cover only finite parameter samples; no independent mathematical audit has been performed.
