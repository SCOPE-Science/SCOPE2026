For the binomial cellular automaton \((Fx)_i=ax_i+bx_{i+k}\) over any finite field of characteristic \(p\), exact ranks of sparse preimage traces are controlled by \(p^{\nu_p(N/\gcd(k,N))}\). The first time every prescribed pattern on \(m\) sites spaced by \(N\) can precede every full target is exactly \(p^{\nu_p(N/\gcd(k,N))}(\lceil m\gcd(k,N)/k\rceil-1)+1\), producing unbounded arithmetic delays despite strong transitivity.
