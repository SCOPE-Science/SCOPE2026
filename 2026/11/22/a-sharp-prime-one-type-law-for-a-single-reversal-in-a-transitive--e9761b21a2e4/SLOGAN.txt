For the countable homogeneous random tournament, reversing one arc of a named transitive base leaves exactly \(0,1,6\), or \(8\) singleton types whose one-point extensions are prime: the count is governed by adjacency, the base-size parity, and whether the reversed arc is a spanning or length-two arc. A modular-decomposition proof yields the exact classification and a uniform eight-type ceiling for all orders, rather than extrapolating from finite enumeration.
