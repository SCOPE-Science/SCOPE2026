For the three-probe stochastic trace estimator on the shifted Laplacian inverse of \(K_2\vee\overline{K_q}\), \(q\ge4\), a proper three-coloring is uniquely variance-optimal precisely when \(\mu>2(\sqrt{\lfloor q^2/4\rfloor}-1)\); below the sharp threshold every optimizer instead places the adjacent universal vertices together. The full family of optimal improper partitions is given by an explicit strictly discrete-convex integer minimization, including all ties.
