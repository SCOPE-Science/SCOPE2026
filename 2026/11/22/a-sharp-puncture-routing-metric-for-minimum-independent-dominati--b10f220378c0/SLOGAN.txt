On the helm graph with \(n\ge5\) rim vertices, minimum independent-dominating-set slides form a Lucas cube with its empty state removed: only \(2n\) unordered configuration pairs incur a detour, and every detour is exactly two moves. The complete shortest-route formula gives radius \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) for \(n\ge8\) and a parity-sharp diameter of \(n\) or \(n-1\).
