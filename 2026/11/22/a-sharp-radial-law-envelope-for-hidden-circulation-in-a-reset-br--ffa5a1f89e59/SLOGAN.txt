A fixed reset-radius distribution and the entire stationary two-time position covariance of a Brownian gyrator can conceal opposite signs of mean continuous-flight angular velocity, even when mean angular momentum stays positive. The exact high-reset angular-velocity range is \([-uA_\nu,uA_\nu]\), where \(A_\nu=\min_{a\ge0}\mathbb E|1-aR^2|\); a four-axial-site example certifies opposite signs at one finite rate.
