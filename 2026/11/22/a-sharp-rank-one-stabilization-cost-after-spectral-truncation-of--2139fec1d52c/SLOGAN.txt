For a stabilizable and detectable Riccati family, rank-one positive-eigenvalue pruning makes both solution error and Riccati residual vanish while turning a uniformly stable feedback into an unstable one. The exact Frobenius cost of any stabilizing rank-one replacement exceeds unconstrained spectral truncation by \(\sqrt{(6-4\sqrt2)K+3-2\sqrt2}\), an unbounded factor growing like \((2-\sqrt2)\sqrt K\).
