For backward-Euler-started constant-step BDF2 on any irreducible two-state Markov chain, every transition matrix at every step index is nonnegative for every positive timestep exactly when the transition-rate ratio is at most \(17+12\sqrt2\). The sharp obstruction is the global scalar BDF2 overshoot \(-(17-12\sqrt2)\), attained uniquely at the second iterate and normalized step \(3+3\sqrt2\); beyond the rate-ratio frontier positivity holds for sufficiently small and large steps but can fail in between.
