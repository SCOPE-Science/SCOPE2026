A driven qubit subject to both Markovian dephasing and uniform-history stochastic resetting changes its slowest late-time polarization sector exactly once, from a nonoscillatory power law to log-periodic oscillations, precisely when \(\omega>\sqrt2\,\kappa\) and the resetting rate crosses \(q_c=\omega^2/\kappa-2\kappa\). The sharp transition occurs even though the stationary state is always maximally mixed, and has no positive-rate counterpart below the drive threshold.
