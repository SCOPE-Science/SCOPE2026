For a single noisy observation used to integrate a Riemann–Liouville Gaussian Volterra process of any order \(\alpha>0\), the uniquely optimal sample reaches the terminal time exactly when measurement-noise variance crosses \(1/[4\alpha(\alpha+1)(2\alpha+1)\Gamma(\alpha+1)^2]\). At the rough Brownian endpoint \(\alpha=0\), no finite noise variance makes terminal sampling optimal: the site only approaches the endpoint asymptotically.
