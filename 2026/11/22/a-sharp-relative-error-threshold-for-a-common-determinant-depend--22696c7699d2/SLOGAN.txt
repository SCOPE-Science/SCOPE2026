For finite teams with \(r\) possible values of \(y\) and \(s\) of \(z\), the lax split \(=_p(x,y)\lor =_0(x,z)\) always succeeds exactly from the sharp universal error fraction \(p=(r-1)(s-1)/(rs-r+1)\). Its worst cases are precisely teams whose nonempty \(x\)-fibers are complete rectangles, while every individual team has an exact polynomial-time row-column-cross test.
