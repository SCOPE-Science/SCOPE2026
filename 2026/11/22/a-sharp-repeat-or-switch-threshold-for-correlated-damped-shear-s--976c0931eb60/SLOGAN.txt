For two exact randomized opposite-shear subflows with uniform one-step marginals, the unique minimum-mean-square two-step coupling **switches** whenever \(2bh<2\) and **repeats** whenever \(2bh>2\); all couplings tie at the sharp boundary. Independent coupling is strictly suboptimal off that boundary, and the choice can reverse long-run mean-square stability even though both component flows and their deterministic sum are spectrally stable.
