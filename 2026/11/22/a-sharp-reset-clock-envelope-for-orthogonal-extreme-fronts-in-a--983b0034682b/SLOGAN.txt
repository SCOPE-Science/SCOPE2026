The orthogonal extreme fronts of a commonly reset planar Brownian gas become perfectly correlated, with a universal \((\log N)^{-2}\) deficit whose sharp coefficient lies in \((\pi^2/24,3\pi^2/8]\): periodic reset intervals uniquely maximize that coefficient, while highly heterogeneous clocks approach its unattained lower limit. Their scaled relative anisotropy instead obeys a clock-independent half-logistic law.
