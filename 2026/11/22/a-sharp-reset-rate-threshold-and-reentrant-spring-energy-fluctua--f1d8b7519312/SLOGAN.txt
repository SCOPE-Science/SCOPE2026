The signed spring-energy change at probe-only Poisson resets in a viscoelastic Brownian pair has a sharp relative-noise crossover at the unique positive root of \(r^4+7r^3+13r^2+4r-6=0\). For every \(1/2\le r\le3/5\), its squared coefficient of variation as bath friction varies has exactly one local maximum followed by exactly one local minimum, despite the presence of only one auxiliary bath coordinate.
