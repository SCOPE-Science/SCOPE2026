For a complete network with one exceptionally priced edge, the unique budget-constrained minimum-Kirchhoff-index design deletes that edge exactly when its price reaches \(n/(n-2)\), with explicit optimal conductances on either side. This sharp A-optimal threshold lies between the known determinant and spectral-gap thresholds, yielding a size-independent price interval of width one on which the A- and E-optimal designs disagree about whether to retain the link.
