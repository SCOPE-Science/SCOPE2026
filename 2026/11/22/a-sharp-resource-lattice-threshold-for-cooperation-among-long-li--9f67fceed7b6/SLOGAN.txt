For a finite stochastic common-resource game, the ability of surviving populations to select universal cooperation has a sharp resource-lattice threshold: \(K(1-e_{cc}/b)>1\). Above it, every initially cooperator-containing population conditioned on exceptional long-term resource survival becomes fully cooperative and approaches an explicit pure-cooperator quasi-stationary law; at or below it, an initially mixed population cannot reach full cooperation before depletion.
