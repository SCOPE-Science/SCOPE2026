In a four-direction lattice walk with zero velocity memory but persistent memory of its movement axis, the return probability at time \(2m\) has a parameter-dependent \(m^{-2}\) term although its entire displacement covariance is universal. That correction changes sign uniquely at nematic parameter \(q=1/3\), where a positive \(5/(16m^2)\) relative correction survives and subleading return data identify the otherwise invisible memory.
