For three independent points on a nearly circular ellipse sampled with density proportional to the \(\alpha\)-th power of the local eccentric-angle speed, their Euclidean acute-triangle probability is \(\frac14-\frac{3(1-\alpha)}{2\pi^2}q^2+O_\alpha(q^4)\). Uniform Euclidean arclength, uniquely at \(\alpha=1\), cancels the entire quadratic anisotropy response and separates strict local maxima from strict local minima at the circle.
