For an Itô searcher between a reflecting and an absorbing wall, a fixed mean diffusivity with strictly positive floor and finite ceiling has a unique optimal clipped-square-root spatial profile. The upper-saturation region appears at the sharp budget \(m_c=2u/3+\ell^3/(3u^2)\), where the optimal mean first-passage time is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\) as a function of the mean-diffusivity budget.
