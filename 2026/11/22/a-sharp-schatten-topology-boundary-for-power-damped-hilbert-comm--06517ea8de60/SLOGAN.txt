The commutator of the classical Hilbert matrix with diagonal weights \(n^{-s}\) is trace class for every \(s>0\), but as \(s\downarrow0\) it converges to zero in every Schatten norm \(\mathcal S_p\) with \(p>1\), not in trace norm: the trace norm stays uniformly bounded and has positive lower limit. Every positive damping exponent also produces infinitely many positive and negative eigenvalues after multiplication by \(i\).
