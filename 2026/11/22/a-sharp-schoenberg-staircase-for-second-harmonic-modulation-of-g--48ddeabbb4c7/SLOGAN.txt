On the unit two-sphere, the exact positive-definiteness cone for \(e^{-a\theta}(1+b\cos(2\theta))\) is governed by one quartic-selected Legendre degree and, when damping is weak, a separate degree-zero upper boundary. The controlling degree advances through an infinite staircase with asymptotic index \(\sqrt{7/3}\,a\); the pure damped second geodesic cosine is a strictly positive definite kernel exactly when \(a\ge\sqrt3\).
