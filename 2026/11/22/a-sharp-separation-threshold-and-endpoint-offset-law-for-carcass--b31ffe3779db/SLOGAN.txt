For a season of continually arriving and exponentially disappearing wildlife carcasses, the unique calendar maximizing discoveries with imperfect repeated searches has equal interior intervals but unequal endpoint intervals once the horizon exceeds an exact search-count-dependent threshold. At that optimum the first interval always exceeds the last by exactly \(\lambda^{-1}\log(1/p)\), irrespective of the number of searches or season length; below the threshold at least two visits must coincide in the idealized model.
