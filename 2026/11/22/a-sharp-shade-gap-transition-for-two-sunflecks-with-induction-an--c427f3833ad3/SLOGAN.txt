In a two-state sunfleck model where induction aids assimilation but light-induced damage inhibits it, two fixed-duration light episodes have a unique optimal intervening shade gap whenever damage recovers faster than induction is lost. For one explicit rate family, a positive optimal gap appears sharply at fleck length \(\ell_c=-\log((\sqrt{57}-3)/6)\) and increases to \(\log3\) in dimensionless time units.
