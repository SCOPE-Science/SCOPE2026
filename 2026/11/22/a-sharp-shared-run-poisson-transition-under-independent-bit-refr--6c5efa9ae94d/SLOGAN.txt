For two fair binary strings coupled by independent bit refresh, long head-run starts split into three independent spatial Poisson populations: shared, original-only, and refreshed-only. At the sharp refresh scale \(\varepsilon_n\asymp1/\log n\), the common fraction is \(e^{-c/2}\) when \(\varepsilon_n k_n\to c\), giving joint longest-run nonexceedance probability \(\exp[-\lambda(2-e^{-c/2})]\).
