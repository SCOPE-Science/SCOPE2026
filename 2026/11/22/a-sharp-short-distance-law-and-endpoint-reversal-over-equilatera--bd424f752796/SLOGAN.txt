Over a pointwise named diameter-equilateral base in every even-diameter integer Urysohn space, ordered pair types have an exact finite exceptional-coordinate formula, and sub-half-diameter distances occur with sharp probability asymptotic \(\frac1{h+1}(1-2/(h+1)^2)^n\). The two upper endpoint distances reverse their type-count ordering between one base point and at most \(h+1\) base points, even though all upper distances become asymptotically equiprobable.
