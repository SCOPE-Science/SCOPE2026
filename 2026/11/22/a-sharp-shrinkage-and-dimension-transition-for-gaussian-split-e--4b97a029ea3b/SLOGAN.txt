Deterministic shrinkage of the Gaussian training mean repairs the infinite-variance defect of balanced two-way split likelihood-ratio e-values **exactly** for \(\lambda<1/\sqrt2\). At the nondegenerate high-dimensional scale \(\lambda=c/\sqrt d\), the swapped e-values converge to the same lognormal limit and averaging saves only \(e^{c^2}c^4/(4d)+O_c(d^{-2})\) in variance; the oracle log-growth choice is variance-safe precisely below signal-to-noise ratio \(1+\sqrt2\).
