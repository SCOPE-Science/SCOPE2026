In a bistable piecewise-linear active trap, unequal orientation-flip rates differing by \(\theta(v-b)/a\) produce a universal sigmoid probability \((1+e^{-\theta})^{-1}\) of occupying the right well as propulsion approaches the force threshold. Each well then has the same independent Laplace-scaled displacement, with orientation locked to the sign of that displacement; the first sigmoid correction is independent of the mean flipping rate.
