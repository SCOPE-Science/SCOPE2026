In a random-fraction stormwater wash-off model, exported pollutant loads switch sharply from positive to negative event-lag correlation as wash-off variability overtakes dry-period buildup variability, even though retained pollutant stock always has nonnegative autocorrelation. All positive-lag correlations share the same sign and lie between two exact universal bounds, with a removal-distribution-independent negative limit under periodic pollutant buildup.
