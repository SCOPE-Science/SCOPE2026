For the exact physical-rate specialization of the componentwise gain–loss SIR step, reconstructing the recovered population preserves the whole nonnegative population simplex for every initial state exactly when \(\tau\rho(\rho-4)\le4\), where \(\rho=\beta N/\gamma\) and \(\tau=\gamma\phi\). The critical reproduction ratio is \(4\); above it the step restriction is sharp, with an explicit interval of susceptible–infected boundary states that produce negative recovered population.
