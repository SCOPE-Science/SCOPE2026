For two fixed equal moisture pulses with exponential drainage and a cooperative Hill-two benefit, simultaneous timing is always optimal when the pulse-to-half-saturation ratio is at most \(1/\sqrt2\). Above that threshold, staggered inputs uniquely win only after a sharp horizon cutoff, and the optimal separation increases to a finite limit; at unit ratio the cutoff is \(kT=\log4\) and the limiting separation is the golden-ratio logarithm divided by \(k\).
