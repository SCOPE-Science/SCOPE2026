For paired survival- and hazard-discretized Rayleigh–Lindley lifetimes, an explicit Lambert-function parameter threshold separates global survival ordering from a unique survival-order reversal; the corresponding hazard crossing moves as \((\log\alpha+\log\log\alpha)/\theta\). Their tails are fundamentally different: one has every exponential moment, while the other has a parameter-independent, exactly geometric asymptotic tail.
