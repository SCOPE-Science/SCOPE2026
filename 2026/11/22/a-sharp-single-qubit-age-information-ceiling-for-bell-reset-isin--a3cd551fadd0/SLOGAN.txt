A Bell-reset transverse-Ising dimer stores at most \(h_2((3-2p)/(2(2-p)))-1/(2-p)\) bits of reset-age information in either single spin at reset probability \(p\), sharply attained by equal positive couplings and an odd half-turn gate phase. This yields a universal nonattained supremum of \(0.311278124459\ldots\) bits and a single local \(\sigma_x\) readout that reaches the bound at every finite-probability optimum.
