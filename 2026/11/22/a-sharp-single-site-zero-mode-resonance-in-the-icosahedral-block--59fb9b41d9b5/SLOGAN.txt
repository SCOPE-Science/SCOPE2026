For the ideal zero-detuning blockade Hamiltonian on the twelve-vertex icosahedral graph, the uniform spin-flip model has exactly seventeen zero-energy states, although excitation parity guarantees only five. Changing the amplitude at a single vertex to any real \(t\) with \(|t|\ne1\) removes exactly twelve accidental zero modes, as certified by a full-column-rank minor with determinant \(2(t^2-1)^6\).
