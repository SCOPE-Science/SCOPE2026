## Correctness — PASS

Conditional on independently infected mediator and warning sentinels, each terminal contributes exactly \(p^2[1-(1-r_i)p^k]\) to expected final size. The deterministic warning quorum is activated with probability \(p^k\) independently of mediator infection. Differentiation of the resulting polynomial and the one-turning-point shape of its derivative prove the strict necessary-and-sufficient inequality, unique interior maximum, and increasing complementary regime. At perfect warning the minimal integer terminal count is three for one sentinel and two for two or more, yielding the sharp six-person family bound. The extracted exact-rational checker passed 58,578 tests including complete finite-state enumeration, sign grids and family minimum. The general result rests on proof, not the finite test count.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

The decrease in epidemic extent with increasing transmissibility was already reported in Van Wesemael, Rocha and Baetens (arXiv:2404.16466, Section 3.2.2), so the qualitative paradox is acknowledged prior work. Granell et al. (arXiv:1405.4480), Kan and Zhang (arXiv:1502.00392), and Feng et al. (arXiv:2406.03763) analyze different infection-awareness feedback, invasion-threshold, and mean-field questions. The examined works do not state the unanimous-warning fan polynomial, its exact inequality, the arbitrary-target-heterogeneity reduction, or the family-specific six-person cutoff; their different dynamics do not logically imply it without additional analysis. Relevant research-index comparisons did not expose a stronger exact formula. This is a bounded originality assessment, not proof of historical priority. Remaining risk: a differently named finite feedback cascade could subsume this calculation.

## Value — PASS

This fully classifies an interpretable minimal causal motif in which a larger biological transmission probability strengthens prophylactic warning enough to reduce the **final**, rather than merely peak, infected count. The exact protective-efficacy and population-size boundaries distinguish genuine reversal from cases where stronger infection always increases cumulative cases. It also shows that average warning effectiveness, not its dispersion, is sufficient for mean final burden in this family. The model is idealized and does not support clinical or field recommendations.
