For a rank-one symmetric oblique tangent projection, symmetrized independent Gaussian sketches have finite \(p\)-th contamination moment exactly when \(p<\ell\), but perfectly sharing the left and right sketch halves the cutoff to \(p<\ell/2\). Thus three sketch rows suffice for finite mean-square contamination under independent symmetrized sampling, versus five under exact sharing; the near-sharing risk diverges at sharp inverse-square-root and logarithmic rates for three and four rows respectively.
