Minimum zero-forcing states of the complement of a labelled path become connected under optimum-preserving adjacent token slides at exactly six path vertices: at five vertices three of the five states are frozen, but at every larger order all states communicate. For \(n\ge6\), exactly \((n^3-5n^2-22n+118)/2\) local slides are possible, and adjacency forbids just \(n^2-5n+4\) of the unrestricted exchanges.
