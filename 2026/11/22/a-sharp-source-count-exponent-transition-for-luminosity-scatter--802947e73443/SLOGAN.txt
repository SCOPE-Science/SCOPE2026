In an ideal finite flux-limited survey with fixed mean lognormal luminosity, intrinsic scatter strictly reduces detections when the radial source-count exponent is at most \(2\). Above that sharp exponent, if mean luminosity lies below the survey-edge detection threshold, the detected fraction has exactly one optimal nonzero log-variance, with the universal faint-source asymptotic \(t_* = 2\log(1/x)-2/(q/2-1)+O(1/\log(1/x))\).
