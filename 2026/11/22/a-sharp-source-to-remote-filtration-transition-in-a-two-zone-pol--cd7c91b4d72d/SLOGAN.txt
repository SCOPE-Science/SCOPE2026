For two communicating equal-size rooms with pollution generated in only one, the best place for a fixed filtration budget depends sharply on the objective: all at the source, an equal split, and a budget-dependent unequal split optimize source concentration, remote concentration, and total concentration respectively. The total-concentration optimum starts filtering the source-free room exactly above \(B=2r+r^2/m\), and for large budgets the remote allocation scales as \(\sqrt{mB}\).
