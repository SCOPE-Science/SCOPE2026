For a finite-interval run-and-tumble particle searching for a partially reactive central target, arbitrarily weak complete Poisson restarting has one sharp speed threshold and at most one sign change as its fixed launch point moves toward the sticky outer wall. Above that threshold in speed, restarting helps at every location; below it, a unique location separates helpful near-target resets from harmful near-wall resets, while uniformly resampled launch positions always benefit.
