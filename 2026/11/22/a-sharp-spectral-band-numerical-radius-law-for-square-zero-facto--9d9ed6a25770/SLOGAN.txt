For every nonzero positive Hilbert-space operator \(A\), the exact sharp ratio \(w(AB)/(\|A\|w(B))\) over nonzero square-zero \(B\) depends only on the two spectral endpoints of \(A\): it is the unique one-variable maximum \(\max_{0\le t\le1}[\sqrt{r^2+(1-r^2)t}+(1-r)\sqrt{t(1-t)}]\), where \(r=\inf\sigma(A)/\|A\|\). Rank-one nilpotents already attain the optimal supremum whenever both endpoint eigenvalues exist, interpolating from \(3\sqrt3/4\) to \(1\) as the positive factor becomes scalar.
