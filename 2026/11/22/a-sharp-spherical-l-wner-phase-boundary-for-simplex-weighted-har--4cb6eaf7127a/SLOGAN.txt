For the simplex-weighted harmonic polytope \(H_n(\lambda)\), its smallest Euclidean enclosing ball is also its unrestricted minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid exactly when \(n\ge6\) and \(0<\lambda\le\frac n2(\sqrt{(n-2)/3}-1)\). The standard harmonic polytope becomes Löwner-spherical sharply at \(n=7\), certified by cancellation of two contact-orbit cross covariances; beyond the threshold an explicit anisotropic ellipsoid has strictly smaller volume.
