# Review of fractional maximal perturbation stability on finite stars

## Correctness — PASS

Every distinct graph ball is accounted for. At each active cell the edge-output Jacobian splits into a diagonal leaf map of Euclidean norm at most one and a rank-at-most-two broadcast map whose Frobenius norm is at most \(\sqrt{m+m^2h^2}\). Strict concavity of \(t^{1-\alpha}\) produces an open branch with hub singleton active and all leaves global active; a strictly positive pair in that branch attains the stated lower estimate exactly. Dividing the bounds proves the fixed-exponent and moving-exponent asymptotics. Random complex/real and active-cell checks corroborate, but are not used in lieu of the proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources

Liu–Lian–Wang, arXiv:2611.15377v1 (2026-11-11), and Lu–Zhao, arXiv:2611.24189v1 (2026-11-18), provide the most relevant published source definitions and sharp one-input variation inequalities. These do not imply a two-input Euclidean-to-edge Lipschitz coefficient. A published ordinary centered star two-input total-variation result uses a different exponent and input seminorm; the spectral fractional star-gradient phase law concerns a linear, not maximal, operator. The theorem claims only sharp asymptotic and global finite-size bounds, not the exact finite-size coefficient. An equivalent historical nonlinear Jacobian result remains a bibliographic risk.

## Value — PASS

The infinite family exhibits a sharp sensitivity change from square-root degree growth to fractional-power growth and an explicit universal smooth crossover in an \(1/\log m\) window. The local active-branch witness explains the mechanism and gives a constructive lower comparison. The result addresses a natural noise-stability quantity unavailable from single-signal variation contraction alone.

## Boundaries and independent status

The stars are unweighted, both norms are unnormalized, and alpha is interior. The exact finite-size optimum and all other graphs are open here. No independent validation has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
