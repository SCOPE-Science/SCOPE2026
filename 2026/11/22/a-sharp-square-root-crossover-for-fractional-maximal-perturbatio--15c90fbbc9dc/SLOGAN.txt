On finite stars, the sharp two-input Euclidean-to-quadratic-edge sensitivity of the centered fractional maximal operator grows like \(\sqrt{m+m^{2\alpha}}\), with a sharp transition at \(\alpha=1/2\). Its universal critical-window profile is \(\sqrt{1+e^{2s}}\) when \(\alpha=1/2+s/\log m\), as established by a full-dimensional extremizing branch and a uniform global Jacobian bound.
