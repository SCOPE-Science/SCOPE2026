For a two-round independently masked binary Markov chain, the optimal first-round revealed fraction is asymptotic to \((2\log2\,\xi)^{-1/2}\) as the correlation length \(\xi\) diverges, while its path-relative-entropy loss per site is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2\log2/\xi}\). Equal-sized rounds instead retain a positive limiting loss, so the optimized two-round advantage diverges as \(\sqrt\xi\), with a sharp coefficient.
