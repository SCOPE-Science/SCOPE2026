# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For every \(n\ge3\), \(K>1\), and \(2\le s\le n\), the rank-one SPD construction and its nondegenerate Krylov space follow from the two orthonormal vectors. Every full-rank row sample must include the unique exceptional row; Q-DEIM does so by a unique first pivot. The derived scalar is a single sinusoid in the free angle, so its extrema, the strict positivity equivalence, the \(\sqrt n\) budget and the first negative integer dimension are exact, not empirical extrapolations. The rational 37-dimensional sample attains the computed lower bound. All 2,596 executable checks passed. No assumption about arbitrary unstructured SPD matrices is hidden in the conclusion.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Bergermann defines dsRR and Q-DEIM-based row selection, while the possibility of spectrally spurious sketched Ritz values is known from earlier literature. A previously published sharp one-vector spectral envelope under a prescribed embedding distortion is the closest quantitative result, but it contains neither the forced full-rank row pattern of this family nor its exact sketch-row budget. Earlier published nonreal two-vector Ritz pairs, sampling-condition gaps and sketched GMRES failures have different outcomes. The new contribution is the complete, sharp **algorithm-constrained one-vector** range and sign threshold, not generic projection pathology. Exhaustive priority over all historical papers remains unestablished.

**Value — PASS.** The exact within-family row threshold quantifies how many deterministic coordinate samples are required to avoid a nonphysical negative Ritz eigenvalue in an SPD problem of fixed condition number. Its \(\sqrt n\) growth is a nontrivial limitation of merely sampling a full-rank two-vector Krylov basis, and its minimal-dimension and rational witnesses provide reproducible spectral tests. This does not assert an all-matrix sketch-size guarantee, convergence failure or practical runtime superiority.

Closest literature: Bergermann (arXiv:2604.07158, Algorithm 6); Drmač–Gugercin (arXiv:1505.00370); Nakatsukasa–Tropp (arXiv:2111.00113); published positive-stability envelope and condition-two nonreal-pair results (full references in RESULT.md). Residual originality risk: possible earlier equivalent row-budget constructions. Scientific limitation: one-vector and highly symmetric SPD model, not general dsRR behavior. Finite tests do not replace the analytic proof.
