For a condition-\(K\) rank-one positive-definite family, one-step Q-DEIM-sketched Rayleigh–Ritz can return a negative eigenvalue despite a full-rank two-vector Krylov sketch. Within the entire family, positivity for every start is equivalent to sampling more than \(n/[1+2\sqrt{K(n-1)}/(K-1)]\) distinct rows, a sharp \(\sqrt n\) budget at fixed \(K\); a condition-two dimension-37 example attains the negative value \(-1/24\).
