The weighted-BRAS construction, practical recommendation for local mass-diagonal weights, rank-reduction principle, and qualitative graded-mesh behavior already appear in the primary paper and September 2026 seminar. They are expressly attributed. The new claim specializes to the **complete** arbitrary-positive-element-scalar-weight spectrum for the minimal two-cell conforming mass problem, proves uniqueness of the exact optimum over all such weights, and identifies the sharp exponent \(\gamma=1/2\) separating uniform conditioning from divergence. Searches of primary sources, related work, and mathematical-result records did not reveal that complete combination. General bounds do not imply the sharp exponent without a lower-bound construction and precise spectrum. Earlier historical analyses may contain equivalent one-interface formulas; this residual risk prevents any statement of independent validation or exhaustive priority.

## Value — PASS
The two-cell interface is the smallest finite-element configuration where two local inverse contributions must be reconciled at a shared degree of freedom. On this natural model, the sharp exponent answers an actual robustness question for low-cost element-diagonal weights: how much of the element-mass scale must be retained as grading diverges? The entire parameter classification determines a uniform-conditioning threshold and exact best achievable condition number. It does not purport to prove robustness on full unstructured meshes.

## Relationship to existing results and limitations
The general weighted construction and its local-mass-diagonal recommendation are credited to Southworth's slides. The older unweighted-BRAS examples on periodic meshes and stars are not reused as new claims. Wathen's classical Jacobi work is a comparator, not this element-weight optimization. A plausible historical reduction of a known constraint-space formula remains the chief originality risk. The stated result is limited to the three-degree-of-freedom exact P1 mass setting and positive element-scalar weights.
