For a stationary nonreciprocal kinetic Ising chain, on-site spin memory has a sharp second threshold at \(g=\sinh(2\beta J)\), strictly beyond the known propagator exceptional-point threshold \(g=\tanh(2\beta J)\). Above it, the correlation changes sign infinitely often with an explicit damped-Bessel asymptotic; at or below it, the correlation is eventually positive, with strict positivity at every time throughout the positive-kernel sector.
