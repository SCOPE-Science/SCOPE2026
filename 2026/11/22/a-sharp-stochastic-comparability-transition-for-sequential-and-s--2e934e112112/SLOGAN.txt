For the wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\), uniform-order greedy parking and uniform maximal-independent-set parking have equal cardinality laws at \(n=3,5,7\), strict sequential domination at \(n=4,6\), and are stochastically incomparable for every \(n\ge8\). The large-wheel obstruction is two-sided: sequential parking is more likely to stop after one occupied vertex but nevertheless has strictly higher expected occupancy.
