A fixed total rainfall can leave less mean urban surface pollutant behind either as one strong storm or as two equally separated weaker storms: in a power-law exponential wash-off model, exactly one crossover exists for response exponent \(p>1\), while equal splitting always wins for \(0<p\leq1\). The quadratic-response crossover is \(\kappa(W/2)^2=\operatorname{arcosh}((1+\sqrt5)/2)\), and the result compares these two prescribed patterns rather than optimizing all possible storm sequences.
