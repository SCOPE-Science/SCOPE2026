Two independent, nonidentically distributed coupon draws over three types have a sharply classified strong-Rayleigh transition: for probability vectors \((t,t,1)/(2t+1)\) and \((1,t,t)/(2t+1)\), the occupied-type law is strongly Rayleigh exactly when \(0<t\le 2+\sqrt 7\). Above this threshold all pairwise occupancy correlations remain negative and the count remains a shifted Bernoulli variable, while the full multivariate law is not stable; three types and two draws are minimal for such failure.
