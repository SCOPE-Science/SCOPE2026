## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
Carvalho–Serantola–Rangel, arXiv:2609.08974, solve a related two-threshold control family whose lower field has constant vertical speed. The present lower field \((1,1-x/2)\) is not a parameter specialization, and the new nested-radical map has a different flip mechanism. Sella–Collins (2008) demonstrate that period doubling is already known in switched circuits; no generic novelty is claimed. General half-map theory from Carmona et al. (2023) supplies methods. Source-equation and claim comparisons did not find a dominating result, although the full text of some older sources was not inspected.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The question is a natural perturbation of constant recovery to affine recovery in a relay, and the unique smooth transverse cycle has a computable stability transition. The sharp parameter, supercritical two-cycle amplitude and critical relaxation coefficient form one motivated quantitative bifurcation theorem, rather than arbitrary special-case arithmetic. It may be useful as a benchmark for switched-control bifurcation methods.
