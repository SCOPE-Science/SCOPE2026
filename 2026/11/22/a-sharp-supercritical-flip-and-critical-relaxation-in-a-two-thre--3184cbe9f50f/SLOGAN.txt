A planar two-threshold relay with one parabolic recovery field has a unique one-lap periodic orbit whose stability flips sharply at \(\mu=4\sqrt2\), creating a locally attracting two-lap orbit with section amplitude \(2\sqrt{(\mu-4\sqrt2)/(9\sqrt2-12)}\). Exactly at the transition, nonstationary nearby section displacements obey \(n(x_n-p)^2\to2/(9\sqrt2-12)\).
