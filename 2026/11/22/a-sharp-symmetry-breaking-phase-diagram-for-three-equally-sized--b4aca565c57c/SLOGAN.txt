In a fixed-three-fraction, two-repair-rate linear-quadratic timing surrogate, maximizing weighted tumor-minus-normal damage undergoes two sharp transitions: from equal spacing to two symmetry-breaking interior schedules and then to an endpoint doublet. The exact thresholds and the unique interior offset follow from strict monotonicity of \(\sinh(bx)/\sinh(ax)\), with an explicit square-root onset and a minimum-separation extension.
