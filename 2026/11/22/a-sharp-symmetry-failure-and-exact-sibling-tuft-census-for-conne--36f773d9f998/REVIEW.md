## Originality
PASS under the checked literature, with historical priority provisional. The full relevant sections of Fürnsinn–Gangl–Rubey (arXiv:2505.03665; later EJC 33(4) P4.32) establish joint symmetry on all connected graphs and chordal graphs, not a threshold-specific bounded-statistic formula, and do not assert symmetry survives arbitrary hereditary restrictions. Banerjee–Mehatari (arXiv:1610.08816) supplies the known unique binary representation, but studies eigenvalues, not this joint statistic. Comparisons of the mathematical-record results on logical theories of threshold graphs concern truncated twin-class signatures and rational theory spectra rather than pendant fan maxima or sibling–tuft joint distributions. The theorem's new mathematical comparison is asymmetric distribution on a natural proper subclass with a sharp first-order failure and an exact all-parameter enumerator. Unindexed earlier enumeration is a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The published global and chordal equidistribution raises a motivated question about how stable that symmetry is under natural graph-class restrictions. Threshold graphs are simultaneously chordal, split and cographs. The result establishes not just a small negative example, but a complete parameter-wise joint counting law and a quantitative exponential separation of two zero-statistic marginals, locating the precise first failure at four vertices. This gives a structural boundary for the known symmetry with reusable rational enumerators.

## Limitations
Results are for connected **unlabeled** threshold graphs. No independent audit has been performed. The formula does not establish a constructive bijection for larger graph classes or historical priority outside the inspected material.
