It follows that nontrivial subsets give distinct nonzero axes except for complementary subsets (opposite orientation), so counting the four singleton and three pair-sum axes is legitimate. The sum of the three pair squared norms equals the sum of the four singleton squared norms; three maximal singletons would force the latter strictly above 3M^2.
