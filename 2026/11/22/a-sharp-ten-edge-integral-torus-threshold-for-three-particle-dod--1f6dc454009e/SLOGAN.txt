For three unordered particles on the dodecahedral graph, the 20 product three-tori from disjoint pentagon triples span a primitive rank-20 lattice in \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{26}\); all 30 octagon-containing triples lie in that same lattice. Exactly six ten-cycle triples complete an integral homology basis, making ten the sharp permitted individual-cycle length for generation.
