Two independently area-sampled normals of a symmetrically truncated catenoid have an explicit Legendre-law density and linear fixed-height alignment tails, but the thin-waist critical window \(1-T_b\asymp b^2\) has a sharp two-branch crossover from linear to square-root scaling. A unique finite truncation height has exactly isotropic second normal moments despite its nonuniform spherical Gauss image.
