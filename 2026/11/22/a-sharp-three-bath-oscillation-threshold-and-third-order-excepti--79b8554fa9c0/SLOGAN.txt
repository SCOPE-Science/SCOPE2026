A period-three kinetic Ising ring with bath pattern \((g,g,-h)\) develops oscillatory one-spin relaxation only after the sharp frustration threshold \(h/g=1/8\), with an exact momentum-selective cubic-discriminant phase diagram. At \(h/g=1/2\), rings whose three-site cell count is divisible by four have third-order exceptional Bloch modes and possible \(t^2e^{-t}\) relaxation terms.
