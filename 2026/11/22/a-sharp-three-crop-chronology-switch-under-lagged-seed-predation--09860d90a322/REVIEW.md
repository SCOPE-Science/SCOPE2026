# Scientific assessment: a sharp three-crop chronology switch under lagged seed predation

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem concerns a unique stationary period-three orbit of an affine scalar recurrence, not an arbitrary initial predator state. The three-step recurrence contracts by \(\rho^3\), giving a unique positive orbit for the stated range. Substituting its explicit solution into the cumulative predation functional cancels all orientation-symmetric terms. The sole remaining difference is a positive multiple of a three-point alternating functional. Exact rational algebra gives the strictly positive Holling-II factor and the Holling-III factor \(AB+AC+BC-K^2\). The latter has exactly one positive zero and the signs claimed in the result. One- and two-crop reversals are cyclic phase shifts, establishing sharp minimality. The checker independently reconstructs the orbit and tests 6,066 exact identities, including both strict-sign witnesses; computation corroborates, rather than proves, the universal result. A finite-output seed budget is not guaranteed for arbitrary parameters, so ecological seed-escape interpretation is restricted accordingly.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected prior work

Satake and Bjørnstad (2004), DOI 10.1086/382661, treat masting, predator satiation and temporally lagged invasibility in a spatial Nicholson–Bailey model, not the prescribed three-crop affine numerical-response model and exact Holling-II/Holling-III orientation switch. Fletcher et al. (2010), DOI 10.1890/09-1816.1, empirically distinguish type-II and type-III hoarding functional responses but do not classify the reversal of chronological crop order. Tachiki and Iwasa (2010), DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2745.2010.01729.x, study evolutionary masting in a different demographic model. Linhart et al. (2014), DOI 10.1111/1365-2745.12231, report seed-predator functional-response observations. Full primary results for related published mathematical work on three-season Beverton–Holt orientation and Holling-III prey treatment scheduling were compared; their recurrences, objectives, and thresholds are distinct and do not directly imply the stated seed-predator chronology threshold. The elementary divided-difference identity and the functional-response laws themselves are established background, not the claimed novel content. Incompletely indexed historical studies and the absence of a full-text comparison for every related mathematical result are genuine remaining priority risks.

## Value — PASS

The core question is directly motivated by a biological ambiguity documented in the functional-response literature: does the timing of a large seed crop relative to preceding crop levels affect cumulative predator consumption, and can switching from nonsigmoid to sigmoid satiation reverse that recommendation? The result supplies a sharp interpretable scale \(\sqrt{AB+AC+BC}\), identifies the minimal chronology-sensitive crop horizon and proves exact robustness to predator retention and fixed background recruitment. The sign reversal is attained by exact-rational examples with nonnegative, physically feasible annual seed escape. This is a meaningful boundary for a stylized masting/hoarding model, although it does not establish a field-realistic prediction or general evolutionary optimality.
