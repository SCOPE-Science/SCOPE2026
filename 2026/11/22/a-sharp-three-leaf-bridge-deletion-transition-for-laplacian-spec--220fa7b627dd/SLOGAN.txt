For a balanced double star with a unit edge-conductance budget, the unique design minimizing \(\lambda_{\max}(L)-\lambda_2(L)\) retains the bridge with conductance \(1/5\) for one leaf per hub and \(1/57\) for two leaves per hub, but deletes the bridge for **every** number of leaves \(m\ge3\). This gives a sharp, all-size example where spectral-width optimization uniquely sacrifices connectivity.
