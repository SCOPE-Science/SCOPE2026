The adjacent-position-swap graph of permutations avoiding \(321\), \(312\) and \(2341\) has the sharp diameter \(n-1+\lfloor n/3\rfloor\) for every \(n\ge2\). Its exact pairwise distances are Hamming disagreements of binary inversion vectors plus two moves for each forced shared-one coordinate, producing a three-periodic packing law for the worst-case detour.
