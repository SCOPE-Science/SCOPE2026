The least possible maximum diameter in a convex three-part partition of a \(\lambda\)-by-\(1\) rectangle has a sharp, explicit two-branch law: balanced T-shaped pieces are optimal up to \(\lambda=3/2\), and three equal vertical strips are optimal thereafter. The unit-square value \(\sqrt{65}/8\) is classical; the result identifies the full aspect-ratio profile and proves its global lower bound for arbitrary convex three-part partitions.
