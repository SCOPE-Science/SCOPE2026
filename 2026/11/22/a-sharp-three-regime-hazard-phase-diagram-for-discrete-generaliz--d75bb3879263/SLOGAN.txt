The discrete generalized Burr–Hatke hazard has a complete sharp phase diagram: for a unique critical shape \(\beta_c=1.0652663348605\ldots\), the first two hazard-difference thresholds meet, creating a three-count hazard plateau, while smaller superlinear shapes admit an initial rise, an immediate dip, and a strict rebound at every later count. An exact monotone tail-threshold sequence also locates a delayed bathtub minimum on the scale \((\beta(\beta-1)(-\log\pi))^{-1/\beta}\).
