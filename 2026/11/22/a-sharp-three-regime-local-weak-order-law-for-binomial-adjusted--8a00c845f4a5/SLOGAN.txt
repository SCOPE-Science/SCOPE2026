For a single decreasing-rate birth reaction, binomial-adjusted \(\theta\)-trapezoidal tau leaping has exactly three local weak-accuracy regimes: first-order inconsistency, a first-order accurate regime, and second-order local weak consistency, separated sharply by \(a_1/a_0\), \(a_2/a_0\), and \(1-2\theta+2\theta^2\). The boundary shows that a binomial backtracking safeguard does not automatically restore even first-order consistency, but can retain second-order local behavior when the initial two propensity drops are mild.
