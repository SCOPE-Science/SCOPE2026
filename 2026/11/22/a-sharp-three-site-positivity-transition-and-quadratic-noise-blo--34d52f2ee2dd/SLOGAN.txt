Moving only the two outer sites of three consecutive equally spaced circle samples symmetrically toward the stationary middle site creates exactly one negative Fourier quadrature weight at a sharp displacement \(u_N\to1-1/\sqrt2\). The exact worst-case sample-noise amplification is \(1+2\max\{0,R_N(u)-1\}/N\) and diverges as \((1-u)^{-2}\) with an explicit finite-grid coefficient when all three sites collide.
