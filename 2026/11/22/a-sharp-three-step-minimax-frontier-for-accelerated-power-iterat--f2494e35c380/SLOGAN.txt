For a warm-started accelerated power method on positive-semidefinite spectra in \([0,q]\cup\{1\}\), the exact three-multiplication worst-case angle error has a unique minimax momentum, switching at the sharp spectral bound \(q_*=0.755526784205864\ldots\) from interior equioscillation to the chosen closed coefficient cap. A different admissible momentum eliminates a subdominant endpoint eigenspace exactly after three multiplications precisely for \(q\le(\sqrt5-1)/2\), but this endpoint notch is strictly worse in the interval minimax sense.
