## Value — PASS

The two-band condition identifies exactly when the usual single monotone cut-in-speed rule cannot be globally optimal in an idealized energy-versus-fatality-risk tradeoff. The threshold is invariant to a positive wind-speed density or risk-budget scaling; it supplies a concrete diagnostic and a mathematically proved counterexample to one-cutoff policy sufficiency. The risk profile need not be empirically typical; the conclusion is a methodological boundary result, not a recommendation for field deployment. Practical significance depends on measured conditional fatality-risk shapes, speed cut-outs, and additional turbine constraints.
