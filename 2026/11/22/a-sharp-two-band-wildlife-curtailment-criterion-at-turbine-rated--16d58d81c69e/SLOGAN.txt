For a cubic-to-rated wind-power curve and any positive collision-risk proxy with strictly decreasing logarithmic wind-speed elasticity, optimal risk-capped operation can require two disjoint wind-speed curtailment bands if and only if the risk elasticity at rated speed lies strictly between zero and three. An exact attainable example shows that a single low-wind cut-in rule then loses energy at the same modeled wildlife-risk cap.
