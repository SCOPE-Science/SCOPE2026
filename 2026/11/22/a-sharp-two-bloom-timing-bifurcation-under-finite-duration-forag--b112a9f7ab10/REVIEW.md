## Value — PASS
The fixed, contiguous activity window is a natural idealized notion of feeding or survey duration, while separated resource-bloom peaks occur across seasonal and spatial phenological resource waves. The exact threshold answers whether the best activity date is centered between two resource pulses or splits into two preferred dates; a precise finite duration can eliminate the split while leaving bloom separation and resource shape unchanged. The full optimizer classification and local square-root emergence are structural, not merely a numerical result for chosen parameters. The result is intended as a benchmark for assumptions in phenology-window models, not an ecological forecast or practical management recommendation.
