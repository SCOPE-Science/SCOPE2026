In the fixed-point-free cosine map, an exactly normalized two-step return has a unique cusp at \(q=r=2/(\pi\sin s_*)\), where \(s_*\tan s_*=1\). Across the strict-alternation family the complete period-two count is one, two, or three, with a robust region of two attracting cycles separated by a repeller; every orbit converges to a two-cycle, and the cusp has an exact \(n^{-1/2}\) relaxation constant.
