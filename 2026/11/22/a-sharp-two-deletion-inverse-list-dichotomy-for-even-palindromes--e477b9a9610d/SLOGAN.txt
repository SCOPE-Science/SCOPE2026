For length-\(2m\) even-palindrome sources over \(q\) letters, deleting exactly two arbitrary symbols leaves at most two possible palindrome parents for every nonpalindromic receiver, whereas every palindromic receiver has exactly \(1+m(q-1)\) parents. Hence the worst-case inverse list is \(1+m(q-1)\), attained by exactly \(q^{m-1}\) receivers; an explicit two-branch decoder recovers every nonpalindromic inverse list.
