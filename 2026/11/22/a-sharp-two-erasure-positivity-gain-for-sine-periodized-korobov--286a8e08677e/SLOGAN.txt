Deleting both zeros of the sine-periodization density from an even one-dimensional Korobov quadrature lattice increases the large-grid threshold for nonnegative unconstrained optimal weights from \(N+O(1)\) to \(\frac73N+\frac{49\pi^2}{27}+O(N^{-1})\). Thus a specific two-node erasure permits a sharp \(7/3\)-fold larger kernel parameter before the first negative weight appears, although no accuracy improvement is asserted.
