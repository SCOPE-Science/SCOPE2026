For two phase-uncertain eDNA measurements with aligned daily and twice-daily Poisson intensity harmonics, the globally optimal worst-phase detection gap jumps sharply when their amplitude ratio crosses \(4(\sqrt2-1)\). Below that threshold the unique gap is \(\arccos(b/(4c))\) radians, while above it the unique choice is half a period; at the threshold both are optimal. The abrupt design change occurs strictly within the two-peaked intensity regime \(b/c<4\).
