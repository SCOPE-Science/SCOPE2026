The exact minimum enclosing Euclidean sphere of each nonminimal panhandle matroid base polytope switches from touching every vertex to touching only the one-handle bases at the sharp threshold \((r-1)t=p\). Its squared-radius deficit from the hypersimplex sphere is exactly \(\bigl(((r-1)t-p)_+\bigr)^2/(pt(p+t))\), giving a quadratic geometric onset across the entire family.
