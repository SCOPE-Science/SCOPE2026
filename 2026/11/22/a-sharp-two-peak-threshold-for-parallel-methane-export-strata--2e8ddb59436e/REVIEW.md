The logarithmic derivative has a quadratic sign numerator with discriminant \(R^2-14R+1\), so one versus three sign-changing stationary roots is rigorously exhausted, including both tangency boundaries and the coincident quartically flat case. The rational example strictly attains the two-peak regime; exact polynomial-identity and rational-balance checks supplement the proof. No numerical enumeration is promoted into an all-parameter proof.

The accessible abstracts and supporting-topic descriptions of Chen et al. and Guo et al. do not imply the theorem; Chen's inaccessible complete article remains a risk rather than a negative full-text determination. Related published mathematical results on time-domain hydrograph peaks and single-compartment tracer-exchange optima have different domains and do not dominate this stationary rate-domain classification. Database keyword nonmatches are not treated as novelty proofs, and general mixture-mode theory remains a residual priority question.

**Value — PASS.** The precise source of the new phenomenon is heterogeneity between *two individually unimodal* serial biogeochemical export pathways. The sharp loss-rate contrast and source-weight window delimit exactly when a homogeneous one-peak surrogate can fail even qualitatively. This is a useful reduced-order mathematical benchmark for investigating layered reactive-transport response, rather than a trivial recomputation of an empirical wetland curve. There is no claim of calibration, field forecasting, intervention safety, or a general theorem about arbitrary mixtures.

The exact mathematical conclusion is limited to positive fixed coefficients, two independent strata, equal within-stratum precursor/methane removal rates, and constant steady effective flushing. Literature priority and mechanistic representativeness remain open to independent checking.
