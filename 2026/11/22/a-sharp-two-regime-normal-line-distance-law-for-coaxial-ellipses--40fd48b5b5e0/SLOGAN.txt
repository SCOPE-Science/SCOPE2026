The normal-line pseudometric of Akopyan and Glazyrin admits a complete algebraic two-regime law for every pair of concentric coaxial ellipses: a sharp endpoint test decides whether their distance equals twice the difference in semiaxis gaps or has an additional strictly positive interior-variation term. In particular, the nested ellipses \(E_{3,2}\) and \(E_{2,1}\) have equal semiaxis gaps but positive exact distance \(4(1-\sqrt{14/15})\).
