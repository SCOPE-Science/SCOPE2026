For two fixed sterile-male releases with exponential mortality and saturating mating competition, the unique cumulative-response optimum switches sharply from simultaneous release to a positive delay at \(T_c=\mu^{-1}\log(2+(b+1)/a)\). When the first normalized dose exceeds the second by more than one, the optimal second release also crosses from before to after the horizon midpoint at one further exact threshold; equal doses never do this.
