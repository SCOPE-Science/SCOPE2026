# Review of the exact Desargues semitotal configuration theorem

## Correctness

**PASS.** The definition and quantifiers use ordinary closed-neighborhood domination and distance at most two between distinct selected vertices. The published general inequality \(\gamma_{t2}(G)\ge 2|V(G)|/(2\Delta(G)+1)\) yields a minimum of at least six for a 20-vertex cubic graph. Independently, both complete verifiers exclude all smaller ordinary dominating sets and confirm 100 valid six-sets, so the minimum is attained. The incidence verifier explicitly checks isomorphism with the generalized Petersen model. All 4,950 optimum pairs are tested; all 100 breadth-first distance vectors are complete. The symmetry orbits exhaust the 100 sets. The finite exhaustive certificate is not extrapolated to larger graphs.

## Originality

**PASS on best available comparisons, with residual historical risk.** Actual semantic-result queries for Desargues, semitotal domination, graph model aliases and optimal exchange returned the Pappus semitotal census, but not the present 100-state theorem. The full published Pappus result is about a different graph; the full published Desargues locating-domination result uses a strictly different resolution predicate and eight-vertex minima. Full published foundational work establishes the definition and a lower bound, not this exact finite optimal-state classification. The 2014 Henning–Marcon article was compared from its abstract only; its inaccessible full text and unindexed graph-specific tables remain risks. Lack of a search hit is not asserted as a proof of novelty.

## Value

**PASS.** The Desargues graph is a classical, highly symmetric 20-vertex cubic incidence graph. Identifying all optimal semitotal placements and their symmetry classes is a natural finite classification. The complete immobility of all 100 minima under one move versus universal connectivity under two moves yields a sharp nontrivial relocation boundary. Orbit-resolved distance shells and a uniform diameter of four add concrete structure beyond the minimum cardinality, which is not claimed as novel. The precise classifier and its metric are reusable benchmarks for semitotal-domination reconfiguration methods.

## Scientific limits

The result is finite, with an exhaustive computational component. It gives no generalization to higher orders. Two genuinely independent finite models agree; no independent external scholarly audit has been performed. Earlier unindexed work may exist, and a closely related abstract-only paper could contain relevant implications.
