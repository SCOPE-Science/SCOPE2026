For a lagged two-stage soil-carbon pulse law, alternating dry spells \(m-d,m+d\) with the same mean as regular spells can increase, then decrease, then increase predicted rewetting-pulse release as the mean interval \(m\) grows. Exactly two crossings occur below a unique sharp variability threshold \(d_c\); above it only one crossing remains, while a one-stage precursor can never produce two.
