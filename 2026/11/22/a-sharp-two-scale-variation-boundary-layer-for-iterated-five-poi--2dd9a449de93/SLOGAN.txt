At the critical step size \(u=1/8\), iterated five-point biharmonic smoothing has two different spatial scales, and their total-variation masses add rather than cancel: the exact moving-window sharp norm tends to \(\mathcal L_4+e^{16\lambda}\) when \(u=1/8+\lambda/r\). The underlying stencil and its two spectral peaks are known; the claimed new fact is the global BV-norm crossover with its explicit exponential profile.
