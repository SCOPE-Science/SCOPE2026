For \(N\ge2r\) short daily observing windows, the probability of sampling at least \(r\ge2\) distinct periodic event centers has an exact two-lobed daily-alias law: the two peaks occur at orbital periods \(1\pm w/r\), and the shorter-period peak is uniquely higher. Exact one-day resonance is a strict local minimum, even though detuning by only \(w/r\) can amplify phase coverage by a factor proportional to the number of nights.
