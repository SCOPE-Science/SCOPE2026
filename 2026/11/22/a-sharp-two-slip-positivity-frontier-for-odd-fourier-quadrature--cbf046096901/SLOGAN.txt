Moving two neighboring Fourier-grid samples symmetrically outward has an exact, increasing all-odd-grid positivity threshold: for odd \(n\ge5\), the critical displacement \(\alpha_n\) is the unique root of an explicit three-cosecant equation and approaches \((\sqrt3-1)/2\). Only the two next-neighbor quadrature weights first vanish; the full weight vector and independent-noise amplification are explicit.
