For a one-point bouquet of \(p\ge1\) unfilled triangles and \(q\ge1\) unfilled squares, the full strong Morse matching complex is \(\bigvee^{3^p+4}S^{p+1}\) when \(q=1\), but \(\bigvee^{3^p}S^{p+2q-1}\) for every \(q\ge2\). The sharp two-square transition comes from a relative simplicial collapse of every hub-incidence cone as soon as a second square provides a collapsible path factor.
