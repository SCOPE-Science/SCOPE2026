For the even least period of \(\sqrt D\), its central partial quotient can equal the initial quotient \(A\), or \(A-1\), but otherwise is at most \(2A/3\); the bound is attained infinitely often by \(D=9t^2+3\). The first lower offset \(C=A-2\) occurs for precisely \(D\in\{15,20,21,39\}\), with \(D=21\) the only example of least period greater than two.
