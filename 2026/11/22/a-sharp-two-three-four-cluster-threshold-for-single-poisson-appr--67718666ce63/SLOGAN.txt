For the equal-weight regular \(m\)-gon mixture of circle Poisson kernels, the best single-kernel \(L^2\) fit exhibits an exact cardinality trichotomy: a continuous concentration onset at \(r=1/\sqrt2\) for \(m=2\), a discontinuous onset at \(r=(3\sqrt6/8)^{1/3}\) for \(m=3\), and a uniquely uniform fit for every \(m\ge4\) and \(0<r<1\). The theorem also classifies every optimal direction and proves sharp leading improvement laws at both transitions.
