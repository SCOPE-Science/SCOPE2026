# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The result follows from the exact, normalizable stationary zero-current density and an explicit sign formula for its derivative. Its critical nonstandard lemma is the strict increase, for \(0<\rho\le2\), of \(K_\rho(t)=\cosh^2t\tanh(\rho t)/\tanh t\). The logarithmic derivative has an exact hyperbolic expression and is strictly positive by the monotonicity of \(a/\sinh(2at)\) and the explicit \(\rho=2\) lower bound. This proves the full all-parameter mode count on the lower-gradient side. An explicit smooth positive-parameter construction and the sign of the Taylor coefficient \(\rho(\rho^2-4)/3\) establish the converse existential statement at every real ratio above two. The exact rational witness and 688 finite checker assertions support, but do not replace, the proof. Boundaries \(\rho=0,2\) and equality in the center curvature test are included.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with residual priority risk)
The full stationary density and the initial center-curvature test were already derived by Jain and Chatterjee (2024). Their proposed global dichotomy uses an expressly unproved mode-count assumption. The closest published mathematical result (2026-11-08; cited in RESULT.md) proves a complete trimodal phase on the separate \(g=3u\) subfamily and rules out trimodality only for \(g\le u\). Its statement and proof were compared directly: its narrower negative regime does not imply a no-trimodality theorem up to \(g=2u\), and a witness at \(g=3u\) does not imply a witness at *every* real ratio above two. The present proof gives both new implications and identifies the exact dividing ratio. Broader searches for aliases such as stationary trimodality, spatial tanh switching gradients, and modal threshold revealed no covering indexed theorem. Absence from search is not proof of universal novelty; an unindexed or inaccessible exact equivalent remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The original physical model's mode diagram was derived under a conditional assumption that later proved globally false, leaving a meaningful unresolved question about when the simple classification is rigorous. This result identifies the sharp, dimensionless, model-wide gradient threshold: below or at the threshold the entire stationary one/two-mode phase diagram is rigorously controlled; beyond it any excess steepness permits a three-peak regime after admissible parameter choices. This is not merely a numerical counterexample or a reparametrization of the known single \(g=3u\) example. It distinguishes a robust guaranteed two-mode sector from all ratios that allow additional stationary phases.

## Closest literature and limitations
The original 2024 source supplies the forward stationary formula and conjectural physical dichotomy; the 2026-11-08 published theorem supplies a particular positive trimodal sector and the earlier sufficient condition \(g\le u\). Different time-inhomogeneous telegraph models and hard-wall dualities do not provide the same continuum modal classification. The theorem does not give a complete upper mode count for \(g>2u\), analyze transient densities, or prove an independent publication-priority claim. No theorem is inferred merely from numerical checker output.
