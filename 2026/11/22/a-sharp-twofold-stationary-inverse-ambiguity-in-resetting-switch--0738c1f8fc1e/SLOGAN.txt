With known unequal diffusivities, a two-state Brownian particle that renews both position and hidden state at Poisson times can have exactly two distinct positive kinetic-rate triples producing the same **entire** stationary position law; an explicit sharp positivity boundary decides when the second triple exists. Sixth-order stationary moments reduce the inverse ambiguity to at most two, and a single positive-lag position covariance removes it completely.
