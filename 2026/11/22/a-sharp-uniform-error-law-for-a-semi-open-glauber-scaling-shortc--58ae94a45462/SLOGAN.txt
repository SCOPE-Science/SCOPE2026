The error-function shortcut for the semi-open Glauber correlation has a strictly negative bias and a spatially uniform, asymptotically sharp short-time error no larger than \(\operatorname{erfc}(N/\sqrt{2t})/\operatorname{erf}(N/\sqrt{2t})\). Although the exact finite-size correlator relaxes exponentially, extrapolating that shortcut to late times creates a \(1/t\) relaxation tail and a falsely infinite integrated relaxation time; this extrapolation lies outside its recommended short-time domain.
