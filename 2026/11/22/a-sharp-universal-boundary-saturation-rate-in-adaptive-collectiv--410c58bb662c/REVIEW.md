# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem follows from exact feedback-logit differentiation, a product inequality on normalized distances, and a critical-case integrability dichotomy. Both stability witnesses are checked from exact rational Jacobians, and the optimal exponential constant follows from asymptotically integrable perturbations of the two scalar boundary distances near a hyperbolically stable equilibrium. The standalone checker confirms the identities and spectra. A finite calculation is not substituted for the proof of all-time convergence.

## Originality — PASS (literature-bounded)
Wang et al. give the source system, its equilibria and local stability; these are not claimed as original. A published previous study of the same equations derives the risk–cost logit cancellation and establishes upper-cost selection for a disjoint sign condition equivalent to \(\rho<0\). The present claim concerns \(\rho\ge1\), the *distance to a union of two faces*, its explicit sharp universal exponential rate, and an attaining hyperbolic example. The related two-community multi-environment logit result operates on different equations. The S1 supplementary material to the primary paper was not fully inspected; unseen stronger treatment remains a residual originality risk. Searches do not certify priority.

## Value — PASS
The new global constraint excludes persistent fully interior behavior irrespective of payoff benefit and cooperation initial state, and provides a sharp worst-case convergence clock. Two alternative stable endpoints show why the precise union is mathematically and ecologically necessary. The sharp exponent is realized rather than merely bounded; that is useful for model interpretation and asymptotic comparison.

## Limitations
The rate constant depends on the initial state, though the exponent does not. Stability of the examples and the feedback identity by themselves do not establish the theorem's global conclusions; the written proof supplies the missing steps. No nonlinear basin division or empirical prediction is asserted. The independent scientific audit remains unperformed.
