# Review of the cubical-face harmonic completion theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** Tensor decomposition of the unweighted cube Laplacian, a normalized Walsh basis, and the resolvent formula give exact singular values for every positive face frequency. A Feynman--Kac hitting-time argument yields strict mode ordering. The sharp uniform constant is obtained by an exact reciprocal-binomial sum, a rational finite check through codimension seven, and an analytic tail inequality for every larger codimension. An independent direct Dirichlet-matrix checker confirms the formulas in fourteen small graph configurations. Finite checks are not the infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to stated bibliographic risk.** General graph-harmonic completion, Green resolvents, and Walsh diagonalization are known; none is claimed as the innovation. The compared 2026 framework provides harmonic completion and host Parseval wavelets, and Chung--Yau's 2000 work gives discrete Green/hitting-time formulas. Previously indexed exact path and star completion claims concern distinct boundary geometries. The new statement is the global sharp \(3548/2883\) mean-zero amplification bound, strict codimension-four extremum, and complete equality classification. The motivating full preprint could not be accessed, so a hidden equivalent special case remains possible.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** In nested-cube wavelet/signal pipelines, extending a complete coordinate-face signal into arbitrary extra binary coordinates is canonical. The theorem separates strong constant-mode inflation from universally small mean-zero distortion, quantifies the sharp stability of every Walsh mode, and supplies exact frame bounds with no unspecified constants. The global maximum occurs at a finite nontrivial codimension, not in a degenerate asymptotic regime.

## Limitations
Unweighted binary cubes, complete coordinate faces, counting norms, and harmonic completion only; no claims for arbitrary embedded graphs, partial observations, or minimal hosts. The non-Hilbert norms of a wavelet bank are not addressed. Exact literature uniqueness remains contingent on inaccessible full texts and independent review.
