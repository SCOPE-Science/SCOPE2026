For cubic Runge–Kutta heat diffusion on every finite positive-weighted bipartite graph, the exact all-step positivity boundary is the minimum of explicit diagonal-cubic roots and distance-two walk thresholds, and positivity never returns after failure. Relative to forward Euler, its sharp universal gain lies in \([1,\alpha)\), where \(\alpha=1.596071637983\ldots\) and stars approach the unattained upper factor.
