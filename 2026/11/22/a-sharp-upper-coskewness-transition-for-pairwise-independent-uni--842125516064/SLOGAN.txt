Three pairwise-independent variables uniformly distributed on \(\{0,1,t\}\) undergo a sharp change in their maximal triple product at \(t=2\): the maximum equals \(t^2/3\) below the threshold and \((t^3+2t)/9\) above it. The maximizing law is unique below the threshold and forbids simultaneous top values, while every maximizer above it assigns probability \(1/9\) to all three top values.
