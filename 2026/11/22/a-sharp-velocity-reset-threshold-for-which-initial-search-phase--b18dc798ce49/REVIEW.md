# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For the precisely defined three-state Markov process, two explicit positive spatial resolvent poles are proved by evaluating the characteristic quadratic at zero and at the ballistic discount pole. The discounted first-detection transforms follow from Fourier inversion of the diffusive-state Green function and a first-arrival renewal identity. Their difference is a two-exponential expression whose slow coefficient has sign fixed by the exact identity \(P(R)=r(r+\beta)\beta[1-D(r+\beta)/v^2]\). One strictly increasing one-exponential quotient establishes the unique crossing; complete-reset renewal translates the sign into mean first-detection times. The embedded stand-alone checker passes 845 diagnostic assertions, including numerical Fourier inversion. Numerical work is not the proof of uniqueness.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Flaquer-Galmés, Campos and Méndez, arXiv:2401.16849 (2024), already supply the two starting-mode first-passage transforms and renewal expressions and show differing optimized search performance; these are prior art. Examination of their model, first-passage formulas, resetting mean times, phase-space study, conclusions and Appendix A did not locate the fixed-parameter theorem comparing which initial mode is best at every target distance or the sharp necessary-and-sufficient criterion \(v^2>D(r+\beta)\). Closest indexed related work on intermittent resetting concerns stationary spatial aliasing, while related distance-reversal studies use different two-state reset intensity clocks. Literature and database searches are not exhaustive proof of priority; the theorem follows algebraically from existing formulas, so equivalent prior coverage remains a specific risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The classification answers a natural decision problem raised by the documented importance of initial mode: given a fixed physical intermittent-search mechanism and a target distance, which initialization minimizes expected detection time? An exact, globally unique geometric crossing and a velocity threshold independent of the diffusive exit rate refine numerical parameter diagrams into a complete structural phase boundary. This is a modest but motivated analytical contribution, not a new general renewal theory or an optimized resetting protocol.

The model assumes the unbounded line, symmetric ballistic velocities, target blindness while relocating, and instantaneous complete resets. No independent publication-priority audit or physical validation has been carried out.
