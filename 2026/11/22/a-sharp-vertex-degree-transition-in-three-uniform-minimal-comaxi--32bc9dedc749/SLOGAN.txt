For every finite commutative ring, an inclusion–exclusion formula gives the exact degree of each vertex in its minimal comaximal hypergraph from its residue-field orders, radical size and co-support. In the three-uniform hypergraph of \(\mathbb F_2^n\), the unique maximum-degree support size is \(n-2\) for \(n=3,4\), \(n-3\) for \(5\le n\le10\), and \(n-4\) for \(n\ge11\); the transition at \(11\) is sharp.
