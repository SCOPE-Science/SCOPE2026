For a sphere drilled by a centered circular cylindrical bore, the hole in an oblique orthographic shadow is exactly the intersection of two explicit ellipses, and disappears at a sharp viewing angle with a \(3/2\)-power area collapse. Its isotropic visibility probability is \(1-\sqrt{R^2-a^2}/R\), so it distinguishes napkin rings of equal classical volume and, together with that volume, reconstructs the sphere and bore radii.
