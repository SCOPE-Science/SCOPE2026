# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
For strictly positive \(a,b,d,g,z,u,v\), the on/off generators are irreducible Metzler, so their time-positive exponentials and each monodromy are strictly positive. The two four-step monodromies share a positive determinant, and larger trace strictly means larger Perron multiplier. The exact two-dimensional identity \(\operatorname{tr}(X^2Y^2)-\operatorname{tr}((XY)^2)=\det(XY-YX)\), followed by the positive divided-difference factorization of matrix exponentials, reduces every phase comparison to \(a[-gz^2+Kz+C]\), with \(g,C>0\). It therefore has exactly one positive zero. The positive Perron eigenvector of the drug-free generator independently identifies that zero as \(z_*=b+(1+d/g)\lambda\), proves all-order resonance, and supplies strict parameter bounds. Four-segment minimality follows from cyclic equivalence of all shorter equal-multiset words. Finite tests check, but do not replace, the analytical proof. Remaining correctness risk: application to calibrated biological dynamics outside stated linear assumptions is unproved.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence
The 2023 full-text paper by Singh et al. develops a two-phenotype antibiotic pulse model and optimizes on/off duty ratios, but the inspected model, theorem, main results and discussion do not give a grouped-versus-alternating four-block comparison valid for every positive duration, its unique treatment-waking threshold, or the exact all-order common-eigenray law. The 2017 full-text work of Carvalho et al. motivates condition-dependent phenotype switching but does not establish those spectral comparisons. Greene's 2014 complete paper proves general trace and reversal identities; these elementary identities are credited, not presented as novel. Published 2026 ecological positive-matrix and six-season treatment results were inspected in full at the level of their theorem, proof and prior-work comparison. The former uses a rank-one-plus-diagonal discrete family and a limiting infinite-block comparison; the latter has two fixed stage-specific culling matrices and six annual phases. Neither theorem implies the specific positive persister generator parameter polynomial, the finite-four-interval exact classification for all segment lengths, or the all-order Floquet multiplier in the present kinetics. Residual risk: other older control, linear switched systems, or persister studies could contain equivalent specialized results; full historical priority is not certified.

## Value — PASS
The model and question come directly from published two-state on/off bacterial persister kinetics. The result identifies the exact waking-rate threshold at which rearranging the same total antibiotic-exposure and recovery budgets flips the asymptotic order. It explains a sharp boundary between suppression and enhancement of resuscitation, shows a noncommutative all-order resonance that predicts an exact net-growth formula independent of switch order, establishes minimal treatment-count complexity, and provides an explicit demonstration that the better schedule can change the extinction-versus-growth verdict. This is a structural, physically interpretable phase diagram rather than an arbitrarily selected numerical example. No statement about clinical optimization, biological fitness or empirically feasible awakening enhancement is proved.
