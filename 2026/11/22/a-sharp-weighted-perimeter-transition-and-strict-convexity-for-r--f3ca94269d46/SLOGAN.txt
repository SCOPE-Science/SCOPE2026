For conjugate weighted perimeter deviations between the Euclidean disk and centered regular \(n\)-gons, the sharp inscribed-to-crossing transition is \(p=1+\sec(\pi/n)\), and the optimized deviations decrease strictly and form a strictly convex sequence in \(n\). Beyond this threshold at every finite weight, the crossing regular polygon even beats every unrestricted polygon that lies entirely inside or entirely outside the disk.
