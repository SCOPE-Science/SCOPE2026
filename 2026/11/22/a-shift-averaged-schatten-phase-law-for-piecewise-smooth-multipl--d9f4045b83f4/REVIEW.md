# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For each arc, the commutator of rank-one averaging and complex multiplication is an off-diagonal rank-two operator, with both singular values equal to the local standard deviation. Orthogonal cell decomposition gives the *exact* finite-rank Schatten sum for all positive \(p\). Piecewise uniform differentiability supplies the \(h/\sqrt{12}\) smooth-cell variance. A jump located at relative phase \(\theta\) contributes \(|\Delta|\sqrt{\theta(1-\theta)}+O(h)\); translation of the grid makes the phase uniform. Separate smooth \(h^{p-1}\) and jump \(O(1)\) scales yield the three sharp regimes, and the beta integral fixes the \(\pi/4\) trace constant. All quantifiers and the no-jump/zero-derivative boundaries are covered. A finite-grid checker independently tests singular pairs and exact sawtooth constants, but is not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The full HTML of Wei–Zhang, arXiv:2407.14988, was inspected in its introduction, theorem statements, and martingale-commutator section; its results concern Besov criteria and singular integral/paraproduct commutators. The accessible text of Chao–Peng (1996) was inspected for simple-martingale Schatten criteria. Neither supplied the present offset-averaged local histogram spectrum with exact mixed derivative/jump trace coefficients. Available mathematical-record results about displaced histogram *projection–projection* commutators cover different operators and endpoint norms. The elementary rank-two computation is not claimed as independent novelty. Residual risk: older approximation-theory, finite-element, and variation literature might contain an equivalent mixed trace limit; the search is not an exhaustive priority guarantee.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Piecewise smooth data combine gradients and interface jumps, both important in histogram and conditional-expectation approximation. A deterministic grid can exactly hide jumps aligned to its boundaries. The shift average removes that arbitrary alignment and exposes a mathematically natural universal critical spectral cost. The three-regime classification determines which of the two structural features governs every fixed Schatten exponent and gives sharp constants, rather than an isolated finite example or a routine norm bound.

## Scope and independent validation

The result is restricted to finitely piecewise \(C^1\) complex symbols and uniform one-dimensional cyclic cells. General bounded-variation functions or multidimensional grids remain unproved. The consulted literature does not eliminate historical-priority uncertainty.
