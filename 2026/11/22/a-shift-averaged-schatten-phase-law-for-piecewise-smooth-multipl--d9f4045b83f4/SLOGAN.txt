For randomly shifted uniform histogram projections, the Schatten mass of the commutator with a piecewise \(C^1\) multiplier has a sharp transition at \(p=1\): derivatives dominate below trace class, jumps dominate above, and the trace norm retains both with exact coefficients \(1/\sqrt3\) and \(\pi/4\). Every partition cell has exactly two equal nonzero singular values, given by its local standard deviation.
