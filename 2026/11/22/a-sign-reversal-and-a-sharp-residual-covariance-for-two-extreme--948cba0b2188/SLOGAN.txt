For uniform points on a circle, the longest gap and the best adjacent-pair minimum are negatively correlated at three points, with covariance \(-1/81\), but positively correlated for every sufficiently large sample. Their unexpectedly small positive residual obeys \(n^{5/2}\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,P_n)\to\sqrt\pi\), even though their individually rescaled extremes become asymptotically independent.
