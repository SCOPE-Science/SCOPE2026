A single clockwise bias \(p\) at the starting vertex of an otherwise symmetric \(n\)-cycle gives the exact last-coverage tail \(\Pr(L_n\ge k)=(n-k)p/[(k-1)(1-p)+(n-k)p]\). Although only one site is biased, the rescaled endpoint has a nonuniform macroscopic limit, with limiting total-variation distance from discrete uniform equal to \(|\sqrt p-\sqrt{1-p}|/(\sqrt p+\sqrt{1-p})\).
