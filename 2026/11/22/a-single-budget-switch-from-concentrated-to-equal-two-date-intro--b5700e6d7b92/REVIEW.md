# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The four environmental outcomes give the exact conditional Poisson-thinning formula. The derivative of the symmetric two-survivor tail has at most one interior minimum; consequently only concentrated or exactly equal dates can maximize. The difference of their values factors through a strictly decreasing scalar function with a unique positive root. Positive survival contrast, positive expected budget, and positive probability of environmental discordance are required. The high-precision example and 44,948 finite checker assertions are secondary tests, not infinite verification.

## Value — PASS

A fixed expected introduction budget must balance environmental diversification against a two-survivor quorum. The all-budget classification gives a mathematically sharp, discontinuous concentrated-to-balanced shift with no stable unequal optimizer and a correlation-invariant critical budget; the at-least-one contrast isolates why the quorum matters. This is a natural finite-model structural benchmark rather than a claim about real ecological field practice.

## Limitations and reference comparisons

The model uses conditional thinning, symmetric binary environmental marginals, exactly two release dates, no reproduction before the census, and a pooled two-survivor proxy; different release ages or conditional dependence can change the conclusion.
