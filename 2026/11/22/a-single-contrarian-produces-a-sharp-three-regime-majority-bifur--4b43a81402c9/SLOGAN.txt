A single contrarian on \(K_{a,b}\) creates a sharp three-regime transition: an isolated contrarian shore yields one four-cycle, a two-vertex shore kills all oscillation, and every shore of size at least three restores exactly one two-cycle. In the last regime the *entire configuration-wise oscillation basin* is unchanged by the contrarian and has exact probability \(\tfrac12(1-H_a/2^a)(1-H_b/2^b)\), where \(H_r=\binom{r}{r/2}\) for even \(r\) and zero otherwise; all individual attraction basins and entrance times are explicit.
