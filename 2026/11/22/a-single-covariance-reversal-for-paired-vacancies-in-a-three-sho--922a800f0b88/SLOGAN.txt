In a hard-core gas on a balanced three-shore graph with disjoint triangular edge deletions, two compatible defect sites have strictly positive occupancy covariance at low activity and strictly negative covariance at high activity, with exactly one sign reversal for every shore size \(n\ge4\). The reversal occurs at activity \((\log2)/n+O(n^{-2})\), with an explicit defect-density correction; the exceptional size \(n=3\) stays positively correlated at all finite activities.
