In the homogeneous \(K_4\)-free graph, complete one-point types over line graphs of ordinary and Möbius cyclic ladders have identical multivariate nonneighbor spectra except for exactly \(2(-1)^n\prod_i u_i\), even with arbitrarily varying rung weights and paired-rail weights. Thus every unweighted type-degree coefficient agrees except the unique extremal degree, and the total counts differ by only two with alternating sign.
