For two perfectly grounded leaders on a friendship network of \(m\ge2\) weighted triangles, second-order coherence \(\operatorname{tr}(L_{FF}^{-2})\) has exactly one hub-abandonment boundary: at \(m=2\) and peripheral conductance \(t=(2\sqrt{61}-7)/15\); for every \(m\ge3\), an optimal pair must contain the hub. This removes the three-triangle transition of the first-order objective and creates disjoint optimal leader classes for every \(m=3\), \(t>3\).
