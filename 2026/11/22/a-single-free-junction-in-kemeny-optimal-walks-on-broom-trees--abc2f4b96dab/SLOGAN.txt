For every broom tree with at least two ordinary leaves and a path of at least three edges, the unique Kemeny-optimal symmetric stochastic walk makes the root and all later interior path vertices move at every step, but leaves exactly the first path junction with positive holding probability. All optimal edge probabilities and the minimum Kemeny sum have closed square-root formulas, and the first path edge has probability asymptotic to \(\sqrt L/m\) for fixed path length and growing hub degree.
