# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The all-length proof identifies the rank of every power from suffix aggregation, proves an invariant cyclic subspace of dimension \(m+1\), establishes its minimal polynomial and exact temporal order, and computes the nilpotent quotient ranks and all populations. The independent linear and full-state scripts provide corroboration, not a substitute for the proof. Edge cases \(m=1\), small word lengths and arbitrary finite characteristic-two fields are treated.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The binary de Bruijn overlap graph and canonical constant-word loops are established in Alhakim–Akinwande (2008); the unmodified directed adjacency spectral construction is well established in Delorme–Tillich (1998). Neither inspected primary text claims the one-loop deletion's cyclotomic return clock, sharp power-rank filtration or full state census. Published Kautz parity research has a nonmatching graph and does not imply the edited de Bruijn result. The closest unpublished or unindexed related edge-defect computation remains a risk; absence from searches alone is not a novelty proof.

## Value
PASS. A single arc deletion in a standard family of highly regular word-overlap digraphs reverses a qualitative dynamical verdict: every state originally extinguishes, but after the edit a growing recurrent subspace exists with a tunable exact period \(m+2\). The full finite-field time-to-recurrence and orbit census, proved uniformly in the exponentially growing graph order, supplies a structural local-perturbation benchmark rather than a finite isolated computation.

## Remaining limitations
The result is for a specified loop deletion in directed de Bruijn graphs over characteristic-two fields. No generalization to every arc or other characteristics is claimed. Published-primary coverage is based on accessible material; historical priority is qualified, and independent validation has not occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
