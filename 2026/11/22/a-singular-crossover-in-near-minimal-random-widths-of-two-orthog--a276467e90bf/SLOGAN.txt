For two perpendicular unit disks separated along their common line, the convex hull's random directional width has a universal minimum \(\sqrt2\), but its lower-tail probability has a sharp quadratic law at every positive separation and a \(3/2\)-power law when the centers coincide. The exact \(t\asymp\sqrt\varepsilon\) crossover coefficient and the conditional distribution of near-minimizing normals are explicit.
