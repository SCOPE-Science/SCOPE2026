A random centered parallelogram generated by two uniform directions in a circular lens has a near-maximum area probability of order \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\), whereas the disk has order \(\varepsilon^{1/2}\). The exact crossover at center separation \(c\asymp\varepsilon\) is an explicit one-dimensional integral, and conditional rare directions converge to the uniform mixed-parabolic region \(x^2+|y|\le1\).
