For the perfect-matching complex of every three-row grid with \(2n\) columns, \(n\ge2\), top-bottom reflection acts on its rational top homology with a six-periodic trace in \(\{-1,0,1\}\). Consequently, the genuine reflection quotient retains exactly \((F_{n-1}+\operatorname{tr}(u_*))/2\) rational sphere-degree classes, first becoming nonzero at \(n=4\).
