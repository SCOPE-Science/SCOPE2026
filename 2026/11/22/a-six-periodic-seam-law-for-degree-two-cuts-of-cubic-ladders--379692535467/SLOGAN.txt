For every \(n\ge3\), the numbers of unordered degree-two cuts of the \(n\)-rung cubic prism and its Möbius-twisted counterpart differ by exactly \(2\cos(n\pi/3)\), even though each count grows at least exponentially. Explicit integer recurrences give both counts, while a seam-switching bijection shows that only colourings with no monochromatic rung can contribute to the difference.
