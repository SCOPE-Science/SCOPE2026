# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is restricted to a stationary nearest-neighbor homogeneous continuous-time cycle with strictly positive clockwise and counterclockwise rates. Uniform stationarity and the reversed generator follow from the doubly stochastic generator. For every two-coloring on three through five states, complementing if needed leaves at most two marked sites; a cycle reflection preserves those sites and conjugates the generator to its stationary time reversal. This proves reversal equality for **all** observed finite-dimensional distributions, not merely low moments. On six states, a directly expanded three-time semigroup probability has an antisymmetric cubic coefficient equal to the nonzero polynomial \((a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)/6\) at the specified unequal lags; this proves the sharp boundary for any nonzero bias. The independent symbolic and matrix-exponential checker completed 453 assertions. Numerical tests are supplemental, not a proof of universal sign or absence.

## Originality — PASS (bounded literature comparison)
Van der Meer, Ertel and Seifert (2022) establish thermodynamic inference using observable **directed links** and transition-based waiting-time statistics. A two-color sensor cannot in general resolve those links, so their reconstruction theorem does not imply a six-state threshold for binary colorings. Liang and Pigolotti (2023) obtain bounds for resolved-state finite-lag asymmetry; irreversible resolved-state pair probabilities do not imply binary output irreversibility. Degünther, van der Meer and Seifert (2023) analyze snippet-based coarse-graining and a six-state network with selected observable transitions, not the homogeneous ring or binary three-time observation. Blom et al. (2023) discuss milestoning and coarse-grained inference but do not supply the exact binary-ring minimum. Related published ring-sampling results address the **full-state** sampled Markov chain and cannot settle the binary path law. The exact mathematical statement and implications were compared, not merely titles. A general hidden-Markov identifiability or dihedral-observation theorem might imply the result indirectly; independent comprehensive priority assessment has not been performed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The number of resolvable microscopic conformations and the number/timing of fluorescence observations are physically motivated costs in molecular-cycle inference. The result provides the smallest homogeneous dissipative cycle whose circulation can ever be identified from one noiseless binary state label, proves a complete impossibility theorem below that size, and produces a lowest-order explicit detection statistic above it. The distinction between pairwise invisibility, equally spaced three-frame invisibility, and unequal-lag three-frame detection is operationally relevant. This is a natural sharp design boundary, not an arbitrary numerical invariant. Signal strength becomes small at weak bias and noise robustness is not established.
