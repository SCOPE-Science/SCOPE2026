A homogeneous biased Markov ring can dissipate energy indefinitely while every possible binary sensor is exactly time-reversible if the ring has at most five states. Six states are the sharp minimum: the binary marking \((1,1,0,1,0,0)\) exposes the current through a three-time unequal-lag asymmetry \(\Delta(\varepsilon)=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)\varepsilon^3/6+O(\varepsilon^4)\).
