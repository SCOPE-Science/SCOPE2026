On every directed bouquet of \(m\ge1\) two-cycles and \(k\ge1\) three-cycles sharing one hub, synchronous conjunctive Boolean dynamics has a sharp six-update convergence clock and a complete seven-layer first-zero-hitting census. Exactly \(2^k-1\) labelled initial states require six updates, but for uniform initial data the hitting time concentrates equally on updates two and three as \(k\to\infty\).
