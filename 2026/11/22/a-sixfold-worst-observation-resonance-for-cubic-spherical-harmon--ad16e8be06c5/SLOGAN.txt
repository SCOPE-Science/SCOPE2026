For eight disjoint equal caps centered at the vertices of a cube, concentration of pure cubic harmonics has exact eigenvalue multiplicities \(1,3,3\). At the unique cap threshold \(h=\sqrt{(33+\sqrt{249})/60}\), the least-observable space becomes six-dimensional; either side has different three-dimensional worst-observed modes, and the sharp squared condition number diverges as \(1/[3(1-h)]\) as the caps shrink.
