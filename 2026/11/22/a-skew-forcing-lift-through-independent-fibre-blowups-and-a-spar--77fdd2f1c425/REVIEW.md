**Correctness — PASS.** In each independent fibre, two initially white false twins are an invariant forcing obstruction. Once no fibre has two whites, a blue vertex exists in every fibre, so its white-neighbor count is exactly the number of currently non-full adjacent base fibres. This simulates the *skew* rule, not the ordinary rule, on the base without restrictions on connectivity or isolation. Removing selected vertices proves the inclusion-minimal equivalence; labelled omitted-vertex choices prove both polynomial substitutions. On cycles, the two-step adjacency graph gives a one-component or two-component skew-forcing criterion, giving every coefficient and the exact exponential minimum counts. The standalone independent exhaustive verifier checked all small graphs/subsets and the displayed cycle formulas. No finite test was treated as an infinite proof.
