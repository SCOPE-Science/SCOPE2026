Replacing every vertex of a graph by an independent fibre of size \(m\ge2\) converts ordinary zero forcing exactly into skew zero forcing on the base, with complete generating-polynomial and inclusion-minimal-set transfer formulas. Applied to doubled cycles, this produces connected triangle-free 4-regular vertex-transitive graphs on \(2n\) vertices with exponentially many minimum—and hence inclusion-minimal—zero-forcing sets, answering the bounded-degree existence question posed in 2022.
