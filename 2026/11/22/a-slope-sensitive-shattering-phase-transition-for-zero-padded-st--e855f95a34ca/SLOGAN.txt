For a Sturmian word of irrational symbol-one frequency \(\alpha\), the variable-length zero-padded factor family has VC dimension exactly \(2\) when \(\alpha<1/2\) and exactly \(3\) when \(\alpha>1/2\), even though the classical equal-length mask dimension is always two. All shattered supports admit a complete rotation-arc test, with the small-slope pair density converging to \(2\alpha\).
