The unique largest Euclidean disk in the divisor-parabola polygon \(B_h\) touches only the top edge and the two chords determined by the greatest divisor \(h\) and its predecessor \(h/p\), where \(p\) is the smallest prime divisor. Despite arbitrarily many other facets, its exact inradius depends only on \(h,p\), and the defect \(h-r_h\) has the complete sharp limit spectrum \(\{p/(p+1):p\text{ prime}\}\cup\{1\}.
