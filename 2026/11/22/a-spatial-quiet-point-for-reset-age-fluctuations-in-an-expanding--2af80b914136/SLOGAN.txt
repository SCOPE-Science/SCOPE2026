In a Poisson-reset expanding Thomas–Fermi condensate, the macroscopic density's reset-age fluctuations are minimized at the initial edge for slow resetting but at an interior quiet point approaching \(|x|=x_0/\sqrt3\) for rapid resetting. The optimized local variance decays as \(r^{-8}\), rather than the generic \(r^{-4}\), with an exact positive prefactor.
