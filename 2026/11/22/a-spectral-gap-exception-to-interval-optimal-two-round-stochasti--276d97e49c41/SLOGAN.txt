For a reversible bipartite chain with positive second eigenvalue \(0<s<3-2\sqrt2\), the uniquely optimal two-round stochastic laziness schedule is \(\{1,1/(2-s)\}\), with exact centered contraction \(s/(2-s)\), strictly beating the enclosing-interval stagewise-safe Chebyshev optimum. On crown graphs this gives a two-round contraction \(1/(2m-3)\), vanishing with graph size even though the best repeated homogeneous-step contraction tends to \(1/9\).
