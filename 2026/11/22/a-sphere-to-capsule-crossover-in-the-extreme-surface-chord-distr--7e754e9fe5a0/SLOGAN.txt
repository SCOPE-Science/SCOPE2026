For independent surface-area-uniform points on a three-dimensional Euclidean capsule, the near-diameter chord probability is \(\varepsilon^2/[4a(a+r)]+O_{a,r}(\varepsilon^3)\) at every fixed positive axial half-length \(a\), but is linear for the sphere. When the axial half-length shrinks as \(a=\lambda\varepsilon\), a sharp piecewise \(C^1\)-but-not-\(C^2\) crossover \(\Psi(\lambda)=1-\lambda\) for \(\lambda\le1/2\) and \(\Psi(\lambda)=1/(4\lambda)\) above the threshold connects these laws and recovers capsule aspect ratio from the fixed-shape coefficient.
