# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every four-face is explicitly partitioned into L- or R-type, and the ordered free-triangle pairing eliminates all tetrahedra. The completeness and uniqueness arguments are local and hold for every \(n\ge4\). The fundamental group is killed by triangles supported on original consecutive path edges, and Euler counting plus the integral simply-connected two-dimensional CW theorem proves the wedge. Standalone exact finite checks verify 35,720 individual collapses and independently compare boundary ranks for \(4\le n\le12\); finite tests are not used as a substitute for proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual search risk.** The 2026 Selvaraja paper treats tolerance complexes of chordal and interval graph clique complexes but supplies collapsibility/Leray bounds, not the integral homotopy type of this path-square family. Kim–Lew's foundational result likewise gives Leray bounds rather than a wedge classification. The squared-path input contains filled triangles; one-dimensional graph-input tolerance formulas are not the same statement. Searches of formula variants, interval graph terminology, published records, and indexed research findings found no broader covering theorem. Unindexed equivalent work remains a residual risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The family is an intrinsic sequence of contractible two-dimensional interval-graph clique complexes with overlapping consecutive triangles. One-tolerance produces a sharp onset of nontrivial topological two-cycles at six vertices and a quadratic sequence of sphere multiplicities. A uniform explicit collapse addresses a structural topological gap that cannot be deduced from a bound on the Leray or collapsibility number alone.

**Limits.** Only tolerance one on path squares is covered. The proof is integral but not independently audited. Search incompleteness is not certification of novelty.
