Uniformly translating a height-one square pyramid with integer base side \(n\) produces **only squares and pronic numbers** as lattice-point counts: its complete law assigns probabilities \(2/(3n)\) to interior square levels and \(1/(3n)\) to the other positive levels. The law is unchanged by arbitrarily shearing the apex, and after division by \(n^2\) it converges to a nonconcentrating squared-uniform distribution.
