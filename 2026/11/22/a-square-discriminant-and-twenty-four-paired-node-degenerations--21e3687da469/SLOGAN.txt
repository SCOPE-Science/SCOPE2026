For any smooth complex cubic surface, the cubic-threefold discriminant of the Eckardt suspension \(F(x)+u^2\ell(x)\) is exactly the square of the degree-\(12\) dual-surface equation. A general rank-three cubic-fourfold Eckardt pencil consequently has exactly \(24\) singular members, each with two involution-exchanged ordinary nodes and total discriminant multiplicity two.
