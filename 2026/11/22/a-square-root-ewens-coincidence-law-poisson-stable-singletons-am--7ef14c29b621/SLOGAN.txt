For two independent Ewens partitions with concentration \(\theta_n\sim c\sqrt n\), the labels that are singleton blocks in both converge to a homogeneous Poisson point process of intensity \(c^2\), and with high probability no larger block is identical in both partitions. Nevertheless their meet contains linearly many co-classified vertex pairs in expectation: \(\mathbb E R_n\sim n/(2c^2)\).
