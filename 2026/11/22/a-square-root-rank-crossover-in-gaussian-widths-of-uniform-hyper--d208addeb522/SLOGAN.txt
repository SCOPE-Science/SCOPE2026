The standard Euclidean degree-sequence zonotope of complete \(k\)-uniform hypergraphs has an exact Gaussian directional-width variance governed by hypergeometric subset overlap. As \(k\) grows, a sharp \(k\asymp\sqrt n\) crossover replaces a Gaussian limit with an independent Gaussian–centered-chi-square convolution, and proportional ranks yield a nonconcentrating folded-normal mixture.
