The fitted-Poisson-weight \(L_1\) goodness-of-fit statistic exhibits a sharp null transition at \(\lambda_n\asymp n^2\): it tends to zero below this scale, to two above it, and to an explicit strictly increasing Gaussian-occupancy profile at criticality. That profile has a countable ladder of genuine \(3/2\)-power cusps at \(\kappa_j=1/(2\pi j^2)\), revealing modal phase boundaries invisible in fixed-intensity calibration.
