For two fixed-noise-correlation observations of the first two Dirichlet sine modes, every globally worst-direction-optimal sensor pair coalesces near one of two locations at separation \(\sqrt{5(1-\rho)}/\pi+o(\sqrt{1-\rho})\), and the sharp information limit is \(25/16\) with an exact first-order correction. Restricting the pair to spatial reflection symmetry instead yields limiting information \(1\), a strict \(25/16\) asymptotic ratio.
