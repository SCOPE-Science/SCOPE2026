For fixed-area Ferrers diagrams inside an \(m\times m\) square, the middle-area membership VC dimension is exactly \(m-\lceil(\sqrt{4m+1}-1)/2\rceil\): fixing the area incurs a sharp square-root loss. Building on the general rank-slice support criterion, a sharp incomparable-cell bound and an explicit anti-diagonal family establish optimality throughout a nonempty central band.
