In a half-filled two-capacity exclusion ring, singly occupied sites are strictly positively correlated for every deformation and drive, although ordinary occupations are negatively correlated. On the \(\sqrt L\) density scale, the singleton-covariance sign changes at \(\lvert N-L\rvert/\sqrt L=1/\sqrt{w+2}\); the undeformed model has an exact finite-size phase boundary.
