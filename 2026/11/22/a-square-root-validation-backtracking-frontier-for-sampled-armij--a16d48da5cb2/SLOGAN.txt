At an exact two-row least-squares optimum, an independent-batch Armijo line search accepts full-objective ascent with an exact binomial probability. When the smallest dyadic step is smaller than the reciprocal square root of validation size, false acceptance tends to one-half even though the expected objective rise is only of reciprocal-batch order. Holding false acceptance below a fixed confidence target as the maximum backtracking depth \(J\) grows requires a validation budget proportional to \(4^J\), with an explicit sharp constant.
