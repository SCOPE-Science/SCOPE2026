For every prime \(p\ge5\), the smooth Dwork-quartic K3 pencil has a nonordinary Hasse divisor equal to twice a reduced divisor of exactly \((p-1-4\delta)/2\) coefficient parameters, with \(\delta=1\) for \(p\equiv5,7\pmod8\) and zero otherwise. The roots are given by an explicit squarefree truncated \({}_2F_1\) polynomial, and they are precisely the supersingular smooth fibres.
