For every fixed finite tetrahedron-free three-hypergraph core, adjoining more isolated named parameters makes its ordered two-point type count approach the square of its normalized one-point type count, but always from above when the core has a triple. The first nonzero excess is universally of order \((\log n)^2/n^2\), with an explicit, strictly positive rational coefficient determined by the influences of old pairs on legal link graphs.
