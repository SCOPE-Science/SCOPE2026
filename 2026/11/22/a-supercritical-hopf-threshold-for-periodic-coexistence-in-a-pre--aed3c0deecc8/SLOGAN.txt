An exact strictly positive-payoff two-prey/one-predator replicator has a supercritical Hopf bifurcation at carrying capacity \(K=8\): a locally attracting all-species limit cycle emerges for \(K>8\) sufficiently near eight. The transverse eigenvalue crossing is \(1/130\) per unit \(K\) and the first Lyapunov coefficient is \(-369/66820\), proving the periodic coexistence that related investigations had supported numerically.
