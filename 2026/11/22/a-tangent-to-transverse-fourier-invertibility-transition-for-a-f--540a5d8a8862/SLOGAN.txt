For the split quadratic cone \(xy=z^2\) over every odd finite field, adding the tangent plane creates exactly \(2q(q-1)\) invisible Fourier modes, whereas translating the plane by any nonzero amount makes convolution invertible. The translated convolution has sharp least singular value \(\sqrt q\), largest singular value \(q(2q-1)\), and exact condition number \((2q-1)\sqrt q\).
