For every \(b=5q+r\ge1\), with \(0\le r\le4\), the minimum number of monochromatic rectangles in a two-colouring of a \(5\times b\) grid is exactly \(10q(q-1)+4qr+2\mathbf1_{\{b\equiv5\pmod{10}\}}\). Hence the natural convexity bound is sharp at every width except \(b\equiv5\pmod{10}\), where the unavoidable excess is precisely two.
