The canonical genus-four curve \(XY=Z^2,\ X^3+Y^3+W^3=0\) over \(\mathbb F_5\) has a tenth explicitly exhibited skew cyclic degree-six Galois line, \(X+Z=Y-Z=0\), beyond the nine known from its characteristic-zero model. This gives a concrete positive-characteristic failure of the characteristic-zero nine-line bound.
