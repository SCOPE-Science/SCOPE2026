# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The theorem fixes a labeled graph and the canonical Euclidean segment scale. A directed cycle consists exactly of two oppositely coherent terminal paths; distinct vertices correspond bijectively to acyclic orientations. Inclusion–exclusion yields the exact orientation denominator. The sum of two edge signs on one path's mixed words is a strict cancellation of the complete binary sign cube, producing the formula for \(\alpha_i\). Interpath correlations come only from same-direction coherent pairs, producing \(\beta_{ij}\). The pathwise incidence columns telescope at both terminals, so all off-block contributions reduce to the single \(vv^{\mathsf T}\) term. The proof covers all quantified parameters. The independent integer checker enumerates all orientations for eight tuples, including unequal paths and three to six arms; it is a finite consistency check rather than a substitute for the proof.

**Originality — PASS (priority provisional).** Padrol–Pilaud–Poullot provide the zonotope–acyclic-orientation correspondence and deformation-cone results, not the uniform vertex covariance. Existing generalized-theta publications give orientation counts, parity-fiber counts, and legal-edge-flip degree polynomials. An accessible earlier own claim already determines the uniform-vertex covariance eigenvalues for the all-length-two specialization \(K_{2,r}\); that specialization is excluded from the novelty domain. The result relies on their orientation model but is not a numerical or logical consequence of the stated coarse enumerators. The related cactus uniform-vertex covariance theorem is pathwise Laplacian without overlapping cycles and expressly excludes this graph class. The new formula treats arbitrary unequal-arm multitheta graphs with at least one genuinely subdivided path, and its nonzero terminal rank-one correction is absent from the compared claims. An unindexed equivalent covariance statement remains a live risk, and no priority guarantee is made.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Covariance of the uniform vertex cloud is a natural Euclidean invariant of a polytope, encoding all pairwise coordinate fluctuations, inertia, and mean squared distance. For graphs with overlapping cycles, it was not implied by the known cactus block decomposition. The exact complete matrix identity for infinitely many arbitrarily asymmetric multitheta graphs is a structural rather than a routine numerical specialization: all long-range dependencies collapse to one rank-one terminal correction. The symmetric three-path case supplies an explicit positive distance increase with sharp exponential rate.

**Comparisons and scope.** Padrol, Pilaud and Poullot, *Deformed graphical zonotopes*, arXiv:2111.12422 (2021), is the principal source. Closest detailed published records are *A sharp vertex–volume covariance sandwich for cactus graphical zonotopes*, *Local flip degrees and simple graphical-zonotope vertices of generalized theta graphs*, and *Acyclic orientation counts within parity classes of multi-theta graphs* (public published-finding corpus records). None has the same sampling statistic, quantified graph family, and matrix conclusion. This observation rests on inspected accessible text, not a uniqueness theorem.

**Limitations.** Unit edge weights, uniform choice of distinct vertices, and the standard ambient Euclidean metric are essential. The covariance of uniform interior points or Gaussian exposed vertices is different. The checker is exhaustive only for the listed finite tuples. Published results after the source date are used for deduplication and comparison, not as substitute evidence for the proof or as later source-date resets.
