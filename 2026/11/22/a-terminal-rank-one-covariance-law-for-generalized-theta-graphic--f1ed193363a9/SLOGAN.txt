For the Euclidean graphical zonotope of any generalized-theta graph with three or more internally disjoint terminal paths, at least one longer than two edges, the full covariance of uniformly sampled vertices is an explicit positively reweighted sum of path Laplacians plus just one rank-one terminal correction. With three equal paths of length \(\ell\ge3\), the expected squared distance between two vertices exceeds the independent-edge benchmark by a sharp positive amount asymptotic to \(12\ell\,4^{-\ell}\).
