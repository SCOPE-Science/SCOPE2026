The indices for which the Delannoy polynomial \(D_n(z)\) equals one modulo nine at **every integer argument** are classified by ternary digits in \(\{0,2\}\) and an explicit adjacent-digit congruence. Exactly \((2^r+\epsilon_{r\bmod6})/3\) such indices lie below \(3^r\), where \(\epsilon\) has the six-periodic pattern \((2,1,-1,-2,-1,1)\).
