# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The weighted bilateral shift eigenvalue formula is exact for every integer index and integer multiplier degree. Positivity follows from the strict log-convexity of annular moments. The two fixed-radius spectral tails and their counting constants are derived directly from the moment formulas. The logarithmic Laplace-moment curvature has an explicit positive even profile, bounded by \(1/3\), with inverse-quadratic tails. Uniform finite-difference control justifies dominated Riemann convergence for every fixed \(p>1/2\). A Fourier-transform calculation gives the claimed zeta-valued Hilbert–Schmidt constant. The standalone numerical and exact-rational checker validates 779 finite cases but is not substituted for proof.

## Originality
**PASS, qualified.** The full mathematical content inspected in the closest Hardy-annulus published results concerns the two-boundary **Hardy** norm, yields a \(\operatorname{sech}^{2}\) profile, and has all finite Schatten powers. The current result instead addresses the analytic **Bergman area** norm, a different moment kernel and inverse-quadratic spectral tails; the Hardy results cannot imply the Bergman spectral phase transition by a unitary equivalence. The motivating Adhikari–Das paper and the Das–Narayanan annulus paper concern algebraic Toeplitz properties and zero products; Douglas–Kim investigate reducing subspaces. Weighted Bergman disk results treat a different geometry. A generic semiclassical theorem could implicitly cover the profile and remains a priority risk; the searches do not establish exhaustive uniqueness.

## Value
**PASS.** The new exact profile reveals a distinct two-boundary spectral mechanism, including the fixed-radius and pinching Schatten threshold, a sharp norm constant, complete positive-level eigenvalue counting and an explicit zeta coefficient. These are structural spectral invariants of a natural operator family, not a selected finite parameter computation.

## Limits
The symbol is a fixed analytic monomial; the domain has the unweighted annular Bergman area norm. Arbitrary symbols, weights and simultaneous growing multiplier degree are not classified. The counting limit is locally finite away from zero. Independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
