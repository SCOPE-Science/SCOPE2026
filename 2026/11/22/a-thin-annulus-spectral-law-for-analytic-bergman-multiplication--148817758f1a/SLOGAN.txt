The positive self-commutator of fixed-degree multiplication on a pinching analytic Bergman annulus has the universal curvature profile \(\Theta(y)=y^{-2}-\sinh^{-2}y\), rather than the Hardy-annulus hyperbolic-secant profile. This yields the sharp Schatten cutoff \(p>1/2\), an exact zeta-valued limiting Hilbert–Schmidt constant, and a complete nonzero-level eigenvalue counting law.
