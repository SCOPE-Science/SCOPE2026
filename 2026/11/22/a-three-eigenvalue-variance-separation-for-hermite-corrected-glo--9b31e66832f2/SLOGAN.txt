For a sparse positive-definite family with only three eigenvalues, the exact one-step Hermite-corrected global-Lanczos inverse-trace estimator has Rademacher variance asymptotically \(4N^4\) times that of ordinary Hutchinson, even though its deterministic trace estimate becomes relatively exact. Over the same matrices, the correction-to-baseline Gaussian variance ratio tends to zero as \(5/N\), exposing a probe-law-dependent mismatch between Frobenius accuracy and stochastic reliability.
