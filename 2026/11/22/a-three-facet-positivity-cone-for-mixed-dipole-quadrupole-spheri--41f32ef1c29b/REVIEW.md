A closely related earlier calculation handles the pure quadratic perturbation only; its one-dimensional conclusion is subsumed here and excluded from the novelty claim. The new degree-one facet, joint inequalities and all-degree convexity identities are not implied by those narrow published claims without an additional argument. It identifies precisely which signed perturbations preserve covariance validity, and proves interpolatory strictness on the entire sharp boundary. This is a natural complete classification, not an arbitrary finite table or small numerical improvement.
