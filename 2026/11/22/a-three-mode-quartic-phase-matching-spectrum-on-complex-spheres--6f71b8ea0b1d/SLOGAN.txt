For exact quartic holomorphic harmonics on any complex sphere of dimension at least three, restriction to the two-to-one phase-matching half-region \(\operatorname{Re}(z_1z_2\overline z_3^{\,2})>0\) has up to six explicit noncentral eigenvalue pairs, with dimension-independent levels, fully specified multiplicities, and a central half-energy level. Its sharp squared observation condition number is \((32+\sqrt{354})/(32-\sqrt{354})\), attained uniquely by three coupled monomial modes.
