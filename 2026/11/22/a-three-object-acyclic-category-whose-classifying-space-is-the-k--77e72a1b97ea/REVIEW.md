# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The unique diagonally indexed long edges are free 1-faces, giving three verified elementary collapses. The retained six triangles have paired edges and three circular vertex links, so the target is a closed connected surface. Its Euler characteristic is zero and the explicit parity contradiction obstructs orientability, forcing the Klein bottle. The lower bound uses the torsion summand of its first integral homology. The standalone enumerator confirms all finite incidence, link and sign checks, and a separate Smith computation confirms the displayed integral homology. The proof does not infer a homeomorphism from homology alone.

A finite triangulation or finite-poset model does not automatically induce a three-object acyclic category, since associative composition is additional structure. Indexed-result comparisons and targeted exact-phrase searches identified no encompassing theorem; unindexed prior equivalent constructions remain a real risk.

**Value — PASS.** Exact object complexity for the first closed nonorientable aspherical surface is a natural missing case of the just-introduced invariant. The construction shows that torsion in first homology and nonabelian aspherical fundamental groups can coexist in a three-object acyclic nerve. The proof establishes a complete, motivated value of the invariant, not a reparameterization or a numerical experiment.
