On \(K_{1,m,m+1}\), the two perfect leaders minimizing \(\operatorname{tr}(L_{FF}^{-2})\) must both occupy the middle part at \(m=2\), but must be the universal vertex and one middle-part vertex for every \(m\ge3\). The switch occurs one graph size earlier than for the first inverse-power objective, giving disjoint optimal leader placements at \(m=3\).
