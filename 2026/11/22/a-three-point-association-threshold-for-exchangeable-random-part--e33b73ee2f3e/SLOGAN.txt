For three pair-equality indicators of an exchangeable random partition, positive association is equivalent to \(q(1+p)\ge2p^2\), where \(p\) is a pair-coalescence probability and \(q\) is the three-way probability. Uniform Bell partitions violate it at every order despite positive correlations between all principal coalescence requirements; the Ewens boundary is critical, while positive-discount Pitman–Yor laws satisfy it.
