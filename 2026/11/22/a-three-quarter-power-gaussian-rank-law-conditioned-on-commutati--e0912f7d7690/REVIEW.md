# Scientific review: conditional commuting-rank law

## Correctness — PASS

The relevant conditional experiment is explicit: a uniformly chosen partial identity and a uniformly chosen partial injection. Composition of partial maps directly forces membership-preserving arrows, which gives the factorization of the centralizer as the two component inverse monoids. The factorial cancellation leading to the Laguerre convolution is exact. The positive coefficients and Perron’s classical negative-argument asymptotic establish both the sharp probability prefactor and the local limit; integrating the normal density establishes the global conditional central limit theorem. The hypotheses, edge case \(n=0\), constants and the weak-convergence quantifier are stated. The independent direct-composition checker passed at every rank through size four. Finite checks are not treated as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk

The 2012 paper by Araújo, Bentz and Konieczny (arXiv:1205.1664), Section 2, already implies the entire finite idempotent-centralizer decomposition. Both that structure and classical Laguerre coefficient identities are explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Its inspected discussion, main theorems and problems treat extremal commutative subsemigroups and graph distances rather than the conditional \(n^{3/4}\) Gaussian law or the sharp probability decay. Separate published centralizer studies by Konieczny (2020) and nilpotent enumerations by Laradji and Umar (2004) were accessible only through abstracts; those abstract descriptions do not report this rank-conditioning asymptotic, but lack of full text remains a risk. Comparisons with relevant existing indexed findings on rook-monoid products, commuting special-element tuples, and centralizer limit laws did not imply the specific conditional result. No uniqueness certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

Commutation is the central relation in the symmetric inverse monoid, while idempotent image rank is its natural integer invariant. Conditioning a uniform idempotent to commute with an unrestricted partial bijection is mathematically canonical. The new contribution is a sharp all-dimensional asymptotic probability coupled with the unusual broadening from binomial fluctuations of order \(n^{1/2}\) to conditional fluctuations of order \(n^{3/4}\), with an explicit Gaussian local density. This is not merely a new representation of an existing finite counting sequence: it gives a precise distributional phase under a rare semigroup relation. The broader full-unrestricted commuting law and quantitative convergence rates remain open here.
