Conditioning a uniform idempotent and an independent uniform partial bijection to commute changes the idempotent-rank fluctuation scale from \(n^{1/2}\) to \(n^{3/4}\), with limiting variance \(1/(2\sqrt2)\) after normalization. The rare commuting event has an exact Laguerre-polynomial probability and a sharp stretched-exponential correction to its \(2^{-n}\) decay.
