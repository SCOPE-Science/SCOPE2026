For fair random binary strings, iterated tortoise sorting has an exact inverse-fiber spectrum and a sharp information-loss transition when the number of passes reaches half the word length. Below the transition log-fiber fluctuations are Gaussian, at the boundary they are positive-part Gaussian with an atom at zero, and above it the centered fluctuations become negative chi-square; the Hartley–Shannon entropy-rate gap is exactly \(\min(\alpha,1-\alpha)\).
