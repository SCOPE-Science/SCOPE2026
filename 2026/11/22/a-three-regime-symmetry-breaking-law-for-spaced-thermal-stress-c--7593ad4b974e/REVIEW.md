## Value — PASS

This is a complete mathematical classification of a motivated three-challenge experiment, rather than a generic local stationary point or an arbitrary constant. With precisely three challenges and fixed first and last dates, a competing fast-injury/slow-priming mechanism generates three qualitatively distinct globally optimal timing geometries, separated by exact biological-memory strength thresholds and with a universal square-root split. The result gives a falsifiable reference for model-based experimental spacing choices and exposes when equal-spacing intuition fails. It is not a model fit to any species and is not a field recommendation; imposed minimum rest periods, non-exponential biology, or selection of survivor genotypes can change the classification.
