# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The claim specifies labelled disjoint-pair graphs, all ground-set sizes at least four, all finite fields of characteristic three, the update order and exact time quantifiers. The factorization \(A=I+B^\top(J-I)B\) follows entrywise from the two-subset incidence; the rank and Jordan cases follow from \(VU=(2-n)I-nJ\) and injectivity of the unsigned incidence transpose. The only nonstandard temporal block—a size-four block over a size-two spatial Jordan block—is proved by the explicit fourth-power-zero and third-power-nonzero argument, not inferred from a numerical spectrum. These blocks yield every fixed-space exponent and the exact sharp period. A standard-library program checked matrices at sizes 4–10 and exhaustively iterated the 531441 configurations at size four. The checks support but do not replace the proof.

## Originality (PASS, with stated search limitations)
The second-order reversible construction is preexisting (Vlasov, arXiv:1311.4297, §§3, 6.2). Ducey–Hill–Sin, arXiv:1707.09115, treat critical groups and Smith normal forms of Kneser-graph Laplacians, not the characteristic-three temporal companion's Jordan census. A published characteristic-two strongly regular graph wave law assumes another characteristic; published ternary bipartite wave laws assume another graph class. The complementary triangular graph's known ternary-wave statement is not equivalent: its adjacency is \(J-I-A\), and its reported periods differ for the same ground-set sizes. The specific three-residue Kneser clocks, fixed-state formula, and primitive-36 density are not entailed as stated by inspected claims. Historical priority outside the inspected texts remains a risk.

## Value (PASS)
This exact natural infinite-family classification identifies a modular representation collapse: only one ground-set residue leaves nonzero stationary configurations, while another creates a four-step nilpotent temporal sector and period-36 orbits. The period-36 proportion has a nontrivial, dimension-dependent exact formula and a finite limiting density. Both temporal periods and their populations are motivated invariants of a reversible network and are not isolated computations or a renaming of the construction.
