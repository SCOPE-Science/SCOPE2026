# Review: three-sector Walsh spectrum of harmonic star completion

## Correctness: PASS
The proof specifies the full finite-domain Dirichlet problem on \(Q_d\), with unit edge conductances, a radius-one boundary and counting \(\ell^2\) norms. The center-only mode is supported at the origin, while the common-leaf mode is constant away from it. Harmonic measures and Jensen establish the sharp endpoints \(1\) and \((2^d-1)/d\). A coordinate-swap antisymmetry reduces every leaf-contrast mode to the resolvent \((2I+L_{Q_{d-2}})^{-1}\), diagonalized exactly by Walsh characters; its binomial sum is the stated middle eigenvalue. Permutation invariance accounts for every eigenspace and multiplicity. A binomial reciprocal-moment expansion gives the near-unit contrast asymptotic. The package's exact-rational checker independently solves grounded systems for dimensions two through six. Those experiments are not relied upon for the infinite family.

## Originality: PASS relative to inspected evidence
The originating full-text paper arXiv:2607.14168 provides general host Parseval analysis and harmonic Dirichlet completion, not the all-d coordinate-star Gram spectrum. The companion dissertation arXiv:2606.29391 gives star embedding dimensions but not the same spectrum in its inspected material. A related published path-in-cycle harmonic-completion finding treats a different host geometry and cannot be directly identified with the present star-in-cube result; its complete publication text could not be accessed, so the comparison is limited. Indexed semantic searches for Poisson operators, Hamming-ball boundary, Walsh-resolvent and frame-spectrum equivalences and accessible historical claim records produced no exact match. This is evidence relative to available sources, not a proof of global novelty; possible older Markov-chain Green identities are a residual risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The Hamming-radius-one star is the canonical smallest-dimensional embedding into a **Cartesian hypercube**, even though general binary Cayley hosts allow smaller embeddings. The theorem gives all singular-value multiplicities and the nontrivial binomial-rational middle branch, not just a loose norm estimate. The split \(\{1,\,1+1/(d-1)+O(d^{-2}),\,(2^d-1)/d\}\) identifies precise anisotropic amplification of the energy-minimizing lift, independent of the chosen Parseval host wavelet bank, while synthesis-and-restriction still reconstructs exactly.

## Limitations and closest literature
The sharp formula is restricted to the standard binary cube, the fixed coordinate-star boundary and unweighted counting norms. It does not quantify minimum-dimensional **general Cayley** hosts, arbitrary boundary graphs, partially sampled wavelet systems or the condition number of the grounded Laplacian itself. The closest primary references are arXiv:2607.14168 (Theorems 1 and 5) and arXiv:2606.29391 (Theorems 2.20 and 2.37); a published cyclic-path report has distinct geometry and limited full-text accessibility.
