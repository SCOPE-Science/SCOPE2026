Harmonic completion of a coordinate star inside a binary cube has exactly three Walsh-frame spectral sectors: a center mode at \(1\), \(d-1\) leaf-contrast modes at an explicit binomial-rational value tending to \(1\), and a single equal-leaf mode at \((2^d-1)/d\). Thus an energy-minimizing completion preserves exact host-based reconstruction but changes Parseval graph analysis into a sharply anisotropic frame with exponentially growing condition ratio.
