Matched-mean Erlang residence clocks reverse the sign of complete-cycle switching-work correlations in a Brownian on/off harmonic trap: one and two stages give strictly positive correlations at every confinement strength, while three stages give one sharp threshold at \(k/q=11.1415229084657\ldots\). Every positive cycle lag has the same sign and the mean work per cycle remains \(kD/q\); perfectly periodic switching instead gives strictly negative correlations at every nonzero lag.
