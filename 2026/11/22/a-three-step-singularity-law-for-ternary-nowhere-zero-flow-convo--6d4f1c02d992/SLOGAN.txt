For \(n\ge2\) nowhere-zero ternary flows on a bundle of parallel edges, ambient additive convolution loses information exactly at lengths congruent to one modulo three: the missing frequencies have symbol counts \((m,m,m+1)\), in any order. Their exact number is \(3(3m+1)!/(m!^2(m+1)!)\), and their proportion is asymptotic to \(9\sqrt3/(2\pi n)\).
