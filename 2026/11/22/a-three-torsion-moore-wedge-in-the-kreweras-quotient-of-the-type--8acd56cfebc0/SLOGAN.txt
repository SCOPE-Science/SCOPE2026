The ordinary geometric quotient of the proper-part type-\(D_4\) noncrossing order complex by Kreweras complementation is \(M(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,1)\vee S^2\vee S^2\). An exhaustive homotopy-preserving finite CW reduction detects three-torsion in the fundamental group despite the torsion-free topology of the covering order complex.
