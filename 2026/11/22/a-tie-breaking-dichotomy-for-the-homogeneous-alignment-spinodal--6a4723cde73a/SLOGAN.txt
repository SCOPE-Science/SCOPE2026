In a seven-site, three-orientation annealed active lattice gas, uniformly random resolution of plurality ties creates a sharp positive-density barrier \(\rho_*=0.212447606903894\ldots\) below which homogeneous polarization cannot grow even at zero noise. If zero-noise ties instead preserve the incumbent heading, the same homogeneous disordered state is unstable at every positive density, with a noise-stabilization threshold asymptotic to \((10/3)\rho^2\). These are exact local mean-field stability results, not claims about the full spatial phase diagram.
