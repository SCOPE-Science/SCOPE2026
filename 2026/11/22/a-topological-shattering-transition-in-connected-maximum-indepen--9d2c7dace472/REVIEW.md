# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claim is for the uniquely determined connected maximum-independent-set extremizers \(F(n,a)\) with \(a\ge3\), \(n>2a\), and no other graph class. Every maximum set is a transversal of the clique partition subject only to the forbidden \(x,y_i\) pairs. The proof constructs all requested traces, so the necessary-and-sufficient support criterion holds in both directions. Its polynomial counts disjoint types of supports exactly once. A maximum-cardinality shattered support gives the VC and Littlestone lower bounds; the all-positive decision path gives the adaptive upper bound. The topology is the homotopy pushout of a join and an attached cone, whose attaching map is nullhomotopic inside the join. This proves the integral two-degree wedge and purity boundary without relying on finite homology calculation. The embedded checker exhaustively confirms all supports, optimizers, decision trees and selected mod-two homology groups on eight small instances; the infinite theorem rests on the written proof.

**Originality — PASS, with qualified priority.** Mohr and Rautenbach, arXiv:1806.10424 (2018-06-27), establish the graph extremizers and optimizer counts [1]; that theorem is credited as prior, not presented as new. The nearest published complete shattering theorem on fully whiskered graphs [2] *covers* \(n=2a\), which is explicitly excluded from the new theorem. For \(n>2a\), the graph contains clique blocks of order at least three and fails the private-degree-one leaf hypothesis, while the exact support criterion and mixed homology are not implied by the inspected sources. published-finding corpus semantic queries, corresponding-parameter searches and comparison with recoverable earlier mathematical statements found no exact or stronger covering statement. Historical priority remains subject to possible unindexed equivalent work; a failed search is not uniqueness proof.

**Value — PASS.** For the canonical connected extremizers of a nontrivial counting problem, the exact shattering polynomial and all-order homotopy type are determined. The mixed-dimensional reduced integral homology has an adjustable lower degree \(t-1\) while the top degree stays \(a-1\); its transition to purity occurs exactly at \(n=3a\), and the static and adaptive learning dimensions stay \(a\). The result goes beyond a routine finite count and does not rely for its value on the already published \(n=2a\) case.

**Closest literature and limitations.** [1] supplies the maximum-independent-set extremal family. [2] supplies a genuine covered whiskered-star boundary; only the new \(n>2a\) regime is asserted. The result does not cover \(a=2\), \(n<2a\), or equivariant topology. Independent mathematical verification and definitive historical-priority certification have not occurred.
