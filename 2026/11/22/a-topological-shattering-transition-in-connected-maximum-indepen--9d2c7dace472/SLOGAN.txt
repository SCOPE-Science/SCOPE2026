For the unique connected graphs maximizing the number of maximum independent sets at fixed order \(n>2a\) and independence number \(a\ge3\), the entire vertex-query shattering complex has an explicit product polynomial and an integral wedge-of-spheres classification. Exactly when \(2a<n<3a\), it has facets of sizes \(a\) and \(n-2a\) and spheres in the two distinct dimensions \(a-1\) and \(n-2a-1\); it becomes pure precisely at \(n=3a\).
