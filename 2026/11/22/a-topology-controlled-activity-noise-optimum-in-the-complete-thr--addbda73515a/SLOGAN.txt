In the complete no-frozen three-particle tandem exclusion rings, total successful-hop noise is exactly Poissonian on four sites, strictly super-Poissonian and increasing with follower speed on five sites, and strictly super-Poissonian with one finite-speed minimum on each of six, seven, and eight sites. Increasing follower speed always raises the mean throughput, so six sites mark the first sharp noise-versus-throughput turning point in this natural finite family.
