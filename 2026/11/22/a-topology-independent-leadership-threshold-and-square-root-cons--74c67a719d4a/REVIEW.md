## Value — PASS

The issue is not a numeric example or a generic averaging identity. The paper's mechanism compares conviction rewards against squared neighbor disagreement, making the balance between intra- and intercommunity interaction an intrinsic question. The theorem identifies a sharp transition in long-run *leadership roles* and sustained polarization for arbitrarily large, directed, unequal-sized two-block networks, while showing why their fine wiring is immaterial on the balanced invariant family. The continuous family of critical partial-leadership states and exact \(t^{-1/2}\) subcritical slowing quantify the singular transition. The scope is intentionally restricted to exactly opposed, common-leadership initializations, and neither empirical validity nor full-state attraction is asserted.
