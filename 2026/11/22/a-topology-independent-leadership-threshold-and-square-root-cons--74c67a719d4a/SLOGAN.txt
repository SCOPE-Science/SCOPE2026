For the replicator opinion–leadership model on any two-community network with equal cross-community interaction mass \(p\), exactly opposite starting opinions and common initial leadership, the sharp phase boundary is \(\rho=4p^2\): below it both opinions and leadership vanish, at it partial leadership persists, and above it full leadership recovers the original polarized opinions. In the subcritical phase, opinion amplitudes obey the topology- and initialization-independent law \(\sqrt t\,|x_i(t)|\to[2(4p^2-\rho)]^{-1/2}\), even for directed and unequal-sized communities.
