The Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have identical strongly regular parameters and identical total-domination polynomials, but different polynomials counting sets that simultaneously totally dominate and identify vertices: \(F_R(z)-F_S(z)=16z^6-64z^7-36z^8\). Both require six selected vertices, with exactly \(1056\) and \(1040\) minimum sets respectively, while their enumerators agree in every size at least nine.
