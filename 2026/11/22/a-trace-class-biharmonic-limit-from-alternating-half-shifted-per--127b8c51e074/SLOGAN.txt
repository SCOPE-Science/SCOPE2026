Alternating orthogonal projections onto two half-cell-staggered periodic linear-spline spaces yield, after \(N^4\) iterations, the genuine periodic biharmonic heat semigroup in trace norm, with the sharp generator coefficient \(1/192\). The complete finite principal-angle spectrum explains the quartic convergence slowdown and yields a sharp gap \(\pi^4/(12N^4)\), contrasting with the familiar quadratic mesh scale of histogram averaging.
