The distinct lower–upper approximation pairs of the closed-neighborhood tolerance on an \(n\)-vertex path form a Tribonacci-type sequence: \(2,3,7,13,23,43,\ldots\). A seven-state automaton gives the exact rational generating function \(\frac{1+t+t^3+t^4}{1-t-t^2-t^3}\), and the number of observable rough pairs is exponentially smaller than the number of input subsets.
