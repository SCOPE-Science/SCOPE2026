For the cardinal-temperature plant response \(x^p(1-x)\), the unique best equal-duration warm/cool temperature amplitude at fixed mean switches discontinuously to zero for \(1<p<2\), becomes fully degenerate at \(p=2\), and instead passes through a uniquely optimal nonzero interior amplitude for \(p>2\). The complete classification gives exact mean-temperature thresholds and a square-root onset for the continuous regime.
