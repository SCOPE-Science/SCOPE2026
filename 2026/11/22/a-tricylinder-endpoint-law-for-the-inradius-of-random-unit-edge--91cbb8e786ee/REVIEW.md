# Scientific review: random parallelotope inradius endpoint

## Correctness — PASS
The tested statement concerns a centered three-generator parallelotope with independent sphere-uniform unit generators. Its Euclidean inradius is exactly half of the smallest face altitude, and hence half the square root of the Gram determinant divided by one minus the smallest squared pair correlation. The three-correlation density is \((4\pi\sqrt\Delta)^{-1}\) on the positive-definite Gram domain, derived directly from two uniform polar cosines and an independent azimuth. An inradius within \(\varepsilon\) of its optimum forces every pair correlation to be \(O(\sqrt\varepsilon)\). The rescaled event converges to the intersection of three perpendicular cylinders; its exact volume is \(8(2-\sqrt2)\). Compact localization, the density limit and a zero-volume boundary prove both the sharp coefficient and total-variation conditional limit. Homogeneity gives the limiting \(\operatorname{Beta}(3/2,1)\) deficit. Direct Gram-versus-facet checks and seeded finite sampling agreed with these identities, without being used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS on the documented, provisional comparison
A source paper dated 2012-11-10 with primary MSC 52A22 discusses mean **outer** radii of isotropic-body random convex hulls, not this three-generator inradius. Ruben (1979) and Mathai (1999) concern **parallelotope content/volume**; Mathai's available full text was inspected, and the geometry explicitly supplies pairs of Gram matrices with identical determinants but different inradii. A published degree-two **lattice zonotope** inradius result was examined in its full public RESULT text; its bounded integer-generator setting does not contain the independent unit-sphere model. The classical three-cylinder volume and published directional-width formulas are credited, not newly claimed. Actual research-record searches on parallelotope inradius, orthogonality, Gram matrices and tricylinder endpoint laws produced no directly covering statement. The full original Ruben text was unavailable: priority is not independently certified, and a hidden broader inradius result remains a specific risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Fixed-length random frames are a natural isotropic family for studying the geometric robustness of a generated parallelotope. Inradius is the minimum of three coupled face clearances and is not encoded by determinant or by outer radius. The exact \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\) coefficient and the nonellipsoidal tricylinder conditional shape expose which joint angular perturbations remain compatible with a near-optimal inball; the beta deficit law also gives the complete leading-order conditional radial profile. These are natural geometric invariants with a clear distinction from the classic random-volume literature, not a renaming of an existing scalar moment or an arbitrary finite slice.

## Scope and remaining risks
The finding covers only three independent unit directions in \(\mathbb R^3\) and the endpoint/conditional asymptotic, with a three-dimensional exact integral at finite thresholds. It does not supply a closed elementary full CDF, higher-order terms, or other sampling laws. Historical priority remains provisional, especially relative to inaccessible archival full text and synonyms for smallest face altitude.
