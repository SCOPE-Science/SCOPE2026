For three independent uniform unit-vector generators of a spatial parallelotope, the probability that its Euclidean inradius is within \(\varepsilon\) of the sharp maximum \(1/2\) is asymptotic to \(16(2-\sqrt2)\varepsilon^{3/2}/\pi\). Conditioned on this event, the rescaled three Gram correlations become uniformly distributed in the intersection of three perpendicular unit cylinders, and the relative inradius deficit converges to \(\operatorname{Beta}(3/2,1)\).
