For the canonical Euclidean cosmological polytope of a simple \(n\)-cycle, the intrinsic inradius changes sharply at \(n=12\): it is \(2\sqrt{2n}/[n(1+\sqrt{6n-9})]\) through \(n=11\) and \(1/\sqrt{n(n-2)}\) thereafter. The tangent facets switch from singleton-and-scattering to singleton-and-two-vertex paths; \(n\rho_n\to1\).
