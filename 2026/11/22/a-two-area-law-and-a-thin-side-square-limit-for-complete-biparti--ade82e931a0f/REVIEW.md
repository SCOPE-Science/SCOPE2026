# Scientific review: two-area law for complete-bipartite graphical zonotopes

## Correctness
**PASS.** Two-dimensional exposed faces are determined by rank-two incidence flats. In a triangle-free simple graph these are precisely unordered pairs of edges, with incident and disjoint pairs yielding squared Euclidean Gram areas \(3\) and \(4\). The ordered-partition/arrangement correspondence gives one face per acyclic orientation after contraction. The contractions are explicitly \(K_1\vee K_{p-1,q-2}\) and \(K_2\vee K_{p-2,q-2}\). Chromatic polynomial evaluation at \(-1\) yields the stated finite Stirling formulas. The dominant exponential term at fixed \(p\) proves the exact limiting square frequency. Boundary cases \(p=q=2\) and empty bipartition remainders are included. An independent exact supporting-objective enumeration in `check_faces.py` verifies seven small cases. Its finite range is corroborative, not an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual priority uncertainty.** Direct searches on complete-bipartite graphical zonotope two-face areas, parallelograms, contractions and thin-side limits, together with published-finding corpus semantic comparisons, found no equivalent metric-refined statement. Chapoton and Pilaud (2022, arXiv:2201.06896) study this exact graph family and provide the face lattice and vertex/facet data; the relevant paper sections and tables were examined. Padrol, Pilaud and Poullot (2021, arXiv:2111.12422) establish general graphical-zonotope structural results. Grujić (2016, arXiv:1604.06931) proves a universal flat/contraction formula for ordinary face counts; this is substantial prior coverage and is expressly used, but its unrefined \(f\)-vector does not determine individual Euclidean two-face area counts. The new claim is not a novel general face-count formula: it is the evaluated complete-bipartite **metric refinement** and its limiting geometric frequency. An unindexed equivalent application of the earlier general theory remains possible.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** A uniformly selected two-face is a canonical local geometric statistic. For this large, highly symmetric and non-simple polytope family, the two distinct congruence classes have exact all-parameter multiplicities. The limit that squares occupy fraction \((p-1)/p\) when the other bipartition class grows quantifies a non-obvious geometric asymmetry and includes an exact boundary formula for the two-vertex side. These counts distinguish square and non-square rhombus areas even though all two-faces have four edges and equal perimeter, information absent from the ordinary face vector. The result is an exact natural invariant rather than an arbitrary small census.

## Source and verification boundaries
The earliest verified public posting of the lead complete-bipartite shuffle source is January 18, 2022; its first primary MSC code is 52B11. The general flat formula and geometric framework are credited as previous work. Exact tests cover \((2,2),(2,3),(2,4),(2,5),(3,3),(3,4),(4,3)\); no automatic infinite certificate is asserted. External historical priority and independent proof validation remain open.
