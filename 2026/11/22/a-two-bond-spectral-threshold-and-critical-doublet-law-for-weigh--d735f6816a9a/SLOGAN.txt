Two equal, separated bond enhancements in the free bilateral Jacobi chain create a second simple pair of bound states exactly when \(a^2-1>2/(m-1)\). Their fixed-amplitude splitting is exponentially small, whereas in the weak-defect window \(a^2=1+\gamma/m\) the band gaps follow an explicit inverse-square crossover with a sharp second-mode threshold at \(\gamma=2\).
