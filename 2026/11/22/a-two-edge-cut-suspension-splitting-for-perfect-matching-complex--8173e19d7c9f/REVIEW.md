# Scientific review

## Correctness
The all-graph statement follows from parity forcing exactly zero or both cross edges in a perfect matching, equality of the intersection with the punctured join, a nullhomotopic inclusion factoring through an internal-edge star, and the mapping-cone splitting. The exact special graph calculation uses elementary simplicial collapses of the \(K_6\) matching complex and the three contractible components of the \(K_4\) matching complex; the integral conclusion is obtained from homotopy equivalences, not inferred from field computations. The independent checker confirms the 234 facets, 100 shared faces, face vector and exact mod-two ranks.

## Originality
The closest inspected work is Chandrakar et al., arXiv:2611.18058v1, a grid-specific star-cover recursion, and Bayer et al., EJC 30 (2023), P2.45, a honeycomb-specific treatment. Their inspected assertions and methods do not give the two-edge-cut splitting. Chandrakar–Singh 2025 and Agarwal–Basak 2026 concern different tilings and ladders; only their publication descriptions were available for comparison, so an unindexed/general decomposition in other sources is a residual priority risk. Targeted indexed and research-record searches returned related individual complexes and edge-deletion results but no covering two-even-sides parity splitting. The generic nullhomotopic-cofibration lemma itself is acknowledged as known: the novelty claim is its uniform graph-theoretic applicability and the resulting integral mixed-dimensional classification.

## Mathematical value
The result is a compositional theorem for a graph complex whose homotopy type is normally difficult to determine. It constructs new connected graphs with two distinct nonzero integral homology degrees, gives a split homology inclusion under double-edge joining, and computes a natural 12-vertex example with precisely four two-spheres and 256 three-spheres. It is not merely the Euler characteristic of a small table; the same structural proof applies to all graph pairs satisfying the transparent parity and endpoint conditions.

## Scientific limitations
No equivariant naturality, minimality among examples, extension to cuts of arbitrary size, or implication for ordinary matching complexes of non-complete graphs is established. The exhaustive checker does not replace the general proof. Independent external mathematical assessment remains unperformed.
