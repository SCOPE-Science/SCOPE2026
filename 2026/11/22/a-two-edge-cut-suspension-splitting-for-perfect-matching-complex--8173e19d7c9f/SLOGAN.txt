Joining two even graphs by two independent cross edges yields a homotopy splitting of their perfect-matching complex into an internal join and the suspension of a vertex-deleted join, provided each pair of cross-edge endpoints is internally adjacent and the needed perfect matchings exist. Two copies of \(K_6\) joined in this way give exactly \(\bigvee^{4}S^2\vee\bigvee^{256}S^3\).
