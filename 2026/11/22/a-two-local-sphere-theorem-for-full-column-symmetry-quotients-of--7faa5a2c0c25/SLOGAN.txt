For every \(n\ge4\), the geometric quotient of the three-row two-matching complex \(M_2(K_{3,n})\) by all column permutations is simply connected and has the two-local homotopy type of \(S^5\). Exact mod-two orbit-chain computations at just three widths suffice for the infinite family, because the barycentric quotient cell structure stabilizes after six columns.
