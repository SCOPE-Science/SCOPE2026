For the identity-started global-Lanczos Hermite correction to an inverse trace, adding a second node can turn an exactly zero-variance Rademacher estimate into an exact nondegenerate binomial error, despite decreasing deterministic quadrature error. This reversal occurs first in dimension \(3\) and can have an arbitrarily large variance ratio on positive-definite irreducible tridiagonal matrices with condition number below \(3\).
