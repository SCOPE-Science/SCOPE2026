For every \(2\le r\le k-2\), imposing ternary digit avoidance simultaneously on \(x\), \(L_rx\), and \((3^{k-1}+L_{k-r})x\) forces exactly the same \(k\)-spaced nonzero digits as the single repunit constraint \(L_kx\in\Sigma\), where \(L_j=(3^j-1)/2\). This is a two-parameter equality of \(3\)-adic Cantor intersections, not merely an equality of dimensions.
