Deleting nonempty Dyck prefixes or suffixes from a binary word is exactly a two-heap Nim game on its maximal primitive-Dyck ends, except that a fully Dyck word behaves as one heap. Every Grundy stratum has an explicit algebraic generating function, and the limiting fraction of losing words is \(1/3\), approached with a universal \(n^{-1/2}\) correction.
