At the collapse of the two chaotic bands of a skew-tent map, magnification reveals unequal stationary density plateaus \(4/3\) and \(2/3\), even though the unscaled invariant probabilities converge to two atoms. The asymmetry fixes the first nontrivial second-order correction \((5/6)\varepsilon^2\) to the stationary mean, while the limiting neutral map has no physical invariant measure.
