For a uniformly sampled point of an ideal hyperbolic quadrilateral, the probability of being within \(h\) of maximal boundary clearance is quadratic for every fixed shape, yet in a critical \(\sqrt h\)-deformation away from the square it follows an explicit \(h^{3/2}\) parabolic crossover. Its limiting conditional geometry pinches from one component into two at \(\lambda=2^{-3/4}\), with an exact nonanalytic area coefficient.
