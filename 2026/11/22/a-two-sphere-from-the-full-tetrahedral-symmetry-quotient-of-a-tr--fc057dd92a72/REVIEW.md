# Scientific review of the truncated-tetrahedral independence quotient

## Correctness — PASS

The stated graph is the line graph of a once-subdivided tetrahedron, with automorphisms exactly the twenty-four simultaneous label permutations; the four intrinsic triangles and their cross-edges prove this. A barycentric flag-orbit model is a regular CW quotient because stabilizers cannot invert strictly rank-increasing flags. From the exact 146-cell quotient, the supplied sixty consecutive free-face pairs leave a regular two-dimensional complex with 6 vertices, 12 edges, 8 triangles, each edge in two triangles, and one cyclic link at every vertex. The final connected closed surface has Euler characteristic two, hence is a sphere. Actual verification succeeds using the two embedded artifact paths.

## Value — PASS

The Archimedean truncated tetrahedron is a canonical, fully symmetric finite graph, and quotient topology is a genuine natural refinement of its already determined group representation. This calculation closes the integral gap for the full quotient: a single geometric two-sphere is obtained, not just a rational Betti number. The full regular-cell collapse is a structural finite certificate and can be reused to distinguish true quotient homotopy from rational transfer. The exact object is mathematically motivated by both the classical truncated solid and its equivalent once-subdivided complete-graph matching complex.
