For independent uniformly chosen vertices of the unit-flow realization of the Chan–Robbins–Yuen polytope, every squared Euclidean distance probability is a coefficient of an explicit two-state transfer polynomial. Their mean squared distance is exactly \(5m/6+(8/9)(1-4^{-m})\), and ordinary Euclidean distances have Gaussian fluctuations of nonzero limiting variance \(43/360\) around \(\sqrt{5m/6}\).
