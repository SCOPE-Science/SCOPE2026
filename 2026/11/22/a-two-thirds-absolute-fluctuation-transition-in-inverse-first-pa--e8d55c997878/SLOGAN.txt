For the overdamped Brownian landscapes with exact mean first-passage law \(\mathbb E[T_x]=Ax^\alpha\), the absolute hitting-time variance stays bounded as \(x\to\infty\) exactly when \(0<\alpha<2/3\), grows logarithmically at \(\alpha=2/3\), and grows as \(x^{3\alpha-2}\) above it. The subcritical variance plateau has an explicit Gamma–Beta constant and supports a nondegenerate centered \(L^2\) first-passage limit under a consistent spatial-increment coupling.
