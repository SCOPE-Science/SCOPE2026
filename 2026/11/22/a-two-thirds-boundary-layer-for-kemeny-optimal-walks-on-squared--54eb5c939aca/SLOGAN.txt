For unrestricted symmetric stochastic walks with one-step and two-step jumps on the squared cycle \(C_n^2\), the unique Kemeny-optimal walk has no holding and uses one-step probability \(a_n\sim2^{1/3}n^{-2/3}\), rather than the \(n^{-2}\) scale associated with the distinct fastest-spectral-mixing objective. Its exact finite-size resolvent gives \(K_n^*=n^2/24+3n^{4/3}/(8\,4^{1/3})+o(n^{4/3})\), improving the leading Kemeny sum fourfold over the ordinary nearest-neighbor cycle walk.
