For the unmodified smoothness-two Korobov cubature on the sine-periodized interval, two consecutive boundary-cluster sites have bounded integration weights as their mesh shrinks, but three consecutive sites have the sharp signed asymptotic \(AN(1,-2,1)\), where \(A=0.0445757926701356\ldots\). Consequently, three-site bounded-data amplification grows as \(4AN\), and independent equal-variance observation noise is amplified in variance by \(6A^2N^2\), despite the positivity of the integration density.
