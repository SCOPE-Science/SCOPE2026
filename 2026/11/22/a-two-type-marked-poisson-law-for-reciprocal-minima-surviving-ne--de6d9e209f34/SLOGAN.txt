When almost every entry of an independent random cost matrix is refreshed, reciprocal row-and-column minima shared by the two copies split into independent Poisson populations: preserved minima have asymptotic mean \(c/4\) and independently rediscovered minima have mean \(1/4\) if \(n(1-\varepsilon_n)\to c\). Their locations and scaled value marks converge jointly to explicit independent Poisson point processes, revealing a sharp stability crossover invisible in the single-matrix distribution.
