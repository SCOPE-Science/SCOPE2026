Ordered pairwise-commuting idempotent triples in the type-\(D_n\) 0-Hecke monoid satisfy a quartic rational counting law, and their excess over type \(A_n\) is exactly \((30-54z+36z^2)/(1-8z+16z^2-12z^3+2z^4)\) as a generating series starting at rank four. Although type \(A_4\) and \(D_4\) each have \(164\) ordered commuting idempotent pairs, they have respectively \(1390\) and \(1420\) ordered commuting triples.
