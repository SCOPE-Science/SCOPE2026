With one frozen, distinct-opinion zealot in each part of a balanced complete \(r\)-partite network, \(r\ge3\), stationary voter-copying opinion shares converge jointly to the uniform \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,\ldots,1)\) simplex law even though there are no within-part edges. The finite law is not flat: on the smallest three-part instance the flexible-voter count vector has respective specified corner, edge and central composition probabilities of \(46/405\), \(13/135\), and \(11/135\).
