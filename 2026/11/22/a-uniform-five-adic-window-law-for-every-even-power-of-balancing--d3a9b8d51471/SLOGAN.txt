For the balancing sequence, every sum of \(k\) consecutive even powers has **exact five-adic valuation** \(\nu_5(k)\) when \(3\mid k\), independent of its starting index and the even exponent. If \(3\nmid k\), the gcd of all such windows is not divisible by five; the sharp uniform residue when \(3\mid k\) is \(2k/3\) modulo \(5^{\nu_5(k)+1}\).
