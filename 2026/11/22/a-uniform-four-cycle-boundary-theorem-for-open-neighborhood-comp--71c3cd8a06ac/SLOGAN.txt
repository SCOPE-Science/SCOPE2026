For every clique width \(m\ge4\), the open Lovász neighborhood complex of \(K_m\square C_4\) is a wedge of \(4m^2-10m+3\) two-spheres and four \((m-2)\)-spheres. The opposite-row diagonal triangles kill the circle that persists for cycle bases of length at least five; the previously known \(m=5\) instance is included as a specialization, not claimed as new.
