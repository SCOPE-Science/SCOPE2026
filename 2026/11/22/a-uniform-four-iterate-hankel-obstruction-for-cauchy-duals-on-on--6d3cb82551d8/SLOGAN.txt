For every nontrivial one-branching circuit of length \(\kappa\ge3\) in the standard \(2\)-isometric composition model, the Cauchy dual has a strictly negative \(3\times3\) scalar Hankel determinant already among the first five iterated norms of a penultimate-cycle basis vector, regardless of cycle length and branch-mass distribution. The exact rational determinant is given in RESULT.md, while both earlier consecutive \(2\times2\) tests remain strictly positive; the qualitative subnormality classification itself belongs to earlier work.
