In an event-driven ballistic gas with independent exponential speeds, the leftmost position under repeated collective threshold resetting is exactly uniform, although earlier ballistic first-passage work already establishes the finite-mean two-particle finite-mean boundary. As the population grows, a logarithmically thin stationary bulk acquires a random exponential profile controlled by a single uniform phase, so a macroscopic exceedance fraction stays random even while the unscaled gas collapses to its reset boundary.
