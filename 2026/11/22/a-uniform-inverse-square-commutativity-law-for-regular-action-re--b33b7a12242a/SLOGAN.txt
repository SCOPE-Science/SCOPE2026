For restrictions of the regular action of **every** finite group of order \(n\), the ordered commuting-pair count lies between \(4^n\) and \(4^n[1+(n^2-1)(7/8)^{n/3}]\). Consequently their commutativity probability is universally \(n^{-2}(1+O(n^2(7/8)^{n/3}))\), and almost every commuting pair consists of two partial identities, even for nonabelian groups.
