The complete-Pick monomial coefficients of the radial Dirichlet kernel exhibit a sharp logarithmic boundary layer: when \((1-\alpha)\log n\to s\), the rescaled coefficient \(n(\log n)^2b_n(\alpha)\) tends uniformly to \((s/(2\sinh(s/2)))^2\). An exact positive slit-integral representation proves this crossover and connects the algebraic interior to the logarithmic Dirichlet endpoint.
