The coefficient identity was derived from a Stieltjes representation and the boundary jump of the polylogarithm. The uniform limit is analytic, using the cancellation between the gamma and zeta poles at smoothness one and a uniform integrable majorant. The included diagnostic script checks exact coefficient recurrences and selected finite approximations; it is not an infinite proof. The result has not received independent mathematical validation.
