For the least admissible size absent among the nonoverlined parts of a uniform \(\ell\)-regular overpartition, **every fixed \(\ell\ge2\)** has a Rayleigh limit with survival \(e^{-\pi(1-1/\ell)^{3/2}x^2/4}\) on the \(n^{1/4}\) scale, and all positive real moments converge. In particular the aggregate mex sum is asymptotic to \(\exp(\pi\sqrt{(1-1/\ell)n})/[2\sqrt{2(\ell-1)n}]\), extending the previously known cases \(\ell=2,3\).
