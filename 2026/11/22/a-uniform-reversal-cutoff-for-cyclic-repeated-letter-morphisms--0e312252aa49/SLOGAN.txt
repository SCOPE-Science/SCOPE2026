For every cyclic alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), the fixed point of \(a\mapsto aa(a+1)\) has exactly \(q\) palindromic factors at each length four through eight, and no palindrome or even reversible factor of length nine or more. Its complete reversible-factor spectrum is \(q,q^2,4q,3q,3q,3q,3q,q,0,0,\ldots\), with the infinite cutoff proved by an exact two-block symbolic congruence certificate.
