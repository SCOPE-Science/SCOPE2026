For every standard hypersimplex, deleting two adjacent vertices removes exactly one less normalized volume unit than deleting two nonadjacent vertices: the overlap of the two vertex caps is precisely a unimodular simplex. Remarkably, the Euclidean Hausdorff distance to the surviving-vertex hull is always \(\sqrt{n/[k(n-k)]}\) for \(2\le k\le n-2\), independent of the pair.
