For four fixed-modulus zeros of a finite Blaschke product, the regular square's boundary-speed variance has exactly two positive angular stiffness bands, and their softest-mode ordering reverses once at squared radius \(u_*=0.152011526727727\ldots\). At that radius the three-dimensional nonrotational Hessian is exactly isotropic; the transition is the unique root in \((0,1)\) of \(u^6-4u^5-13u^4-24u^3-13u^2-4u+1\).
