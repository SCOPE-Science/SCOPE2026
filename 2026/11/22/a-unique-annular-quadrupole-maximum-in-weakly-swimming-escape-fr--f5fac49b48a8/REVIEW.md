# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The mean-exit backward generator has a well-posed uniformly elliptic Dirichlet resolvent. Its weak-propulsion expansion produces the first-harmonic modified-Bessel solution and the exact second-harmonic inhomogeneous equation, solved by an explicit linear combination of \(I_2(k\rho)\) and \(I_2(2k\rho)\). The positive-series coefficient-ratio argument strictly proves positivity and exactly one radial maximizer; the high- and low-angular-diffusion limits follow from controlled Bessel asymptotics. The embedded computational checks reproduce the ODE identities, peak locations and limits. Numerical tests do not substitute for the global proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The underlying absorbing-disk active Brownian model, the passive spectrum and general propagator are already present in Di Trapani, Franosch and Caraglio (2023). The precise mean-exit backward PDE and its \(O(v)\) dipolar response are already in Iyaniwura and Peng (2025). A related published calculation (2026) handles only the orientation-averaged \(O(v^2)\) susceptibility. The present scientific claim instead concerns the \(\cos2\psi\) coefficient at second order, its strictly positive sign, unique full-parameter radial maximum and sharp endpoint asymptotics. Those are not obtained merely by taking the orientation average of the existing result, and the first-order dipole cannot determine the quadrupole without solving a further boundary-value equation. The closest indexed published result could not be read in full; its summary supports noncoverage but cannot exclude an unindexed generalization. The 2023 propagator supplies a theoretical route to the same calculations, which remains a priority limitation.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The quadrupolar response records how a swimming direction affects escape beyond the dipolar correction, a natural observable for heading-controlled active-particle search. A parameter-uniform sign law and a unique optimal *starting radius for the quadrupolar coefficient*, with an interior-to-boundary-layer crossover and exact large-diffusion scales, are structural statements rather than arbitrary parameter slices. The result does not identify a full finite-velocity escape-time optimizer or an experimental control protocol.

## Literature and limitations

Primary full texts inspected: Di Trapani–Franosch–Caraglio (2023), arXiv:2306.12702v1; Iyaniwura–Peng (2025), arXiv:2506.15813v1 and https://zhiweipeng.com/assets/pdf/IP2025.pdf . A closely related 2026 published scientific summary was compared but its full result text was not accessible; this is an originality risk, not proof of novelty. All testable conclusions require \(D,R,q>0\), an absorbing circular boundary and the small-propulsion expansion.
