For the two-particle heat-bath Kawasaki Ising chain on every balanced complete bipartite graph \(K_{r,r}\), antiferromagnetic interactions uniquely **accelerate** relaxation at a positive inverse temperature, whereas ferromagnetic interactions strictly slow it. The optimal gap exceeds the infinite-temperature value \(r/2\) by \(1/(12r)+O(r^{-2})\), and both the optimum and the complete spectrum have exact formulas.
