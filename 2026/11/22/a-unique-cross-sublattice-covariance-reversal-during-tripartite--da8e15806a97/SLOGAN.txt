In irreversible dimer adsorption on balanced complete tripartite graphs, cross-part vacancy fluctuations are positively correlated early but negatively correlated later, with a unique macroscopic sign-change time \(t_*=1.347345267068\ldots\) in the rate-\(1/n\) edge-clock normalization. An explicit Gaussian fluctuation law determines the full three-part covariance matrix at each fixed physical time.
