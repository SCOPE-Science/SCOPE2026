The sharp sector formula exposes a separate, quantitatively deteriorating nonnormality mechanism and supplies an exact diagnostic for the worst generalized pressure mode, without falsely claiming full solver robustness. It is a structural parameter-uniform numerical linear algebra result, not merely a coefficient substitution.
