For terminal minimal cuts in exponentially age-preferential growing multigraphs, bottleneck-count fluctuations have a Gaussian limit with a unique switch from sub-Poisson to super-Poisson dispersion as the age-bias parameter \(q\) increases. With \(m\) independent parent edges, the exact crossover satisfies \(q_m=\log(3/2)/m-13\log^2(3/2)/(6m^2)+O(m^{-3})\).
