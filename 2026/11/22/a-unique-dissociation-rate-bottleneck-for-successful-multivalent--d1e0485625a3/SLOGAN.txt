For the constant-rate post-capture bond chain, successful stabilization has a unique interior worst dissociation-to-association ratio if and only if the required bond count is at least four. At large thresholds \(m\), this ratio is \(1-15/m^2+o(m^{-2})\), and the worst conditional mean exceeds the balanced-rate value by \(5/(8\lambda)+o(1)\), even though the success probability always decreases with dissociation.
