For two differently settling microplastic classes exchanging reversibly between water and sediment, the order of two fixed-ratio flow phases can switch from favoring one protocol to favoring the other **exactly once** as both durations grow, even though neither class switches its own preferred order. The reversal exists precisely when the competing class-weight ratio \(q\) lies between the sharp boundaries \(q_0=\lambda_{A1}\lambda_{B1}/(\lambda_{A2}\lambda_{B2})\) and \(1\), independent of the initial partition between water and bed.
