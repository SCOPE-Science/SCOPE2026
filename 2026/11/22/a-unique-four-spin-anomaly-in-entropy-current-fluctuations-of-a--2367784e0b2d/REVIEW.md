# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For any \(N\ge4\), all rates are positive and the uniform product measure solves the stationarity equations. The exact sitewise entropy increment is \(-\eta s_i(s_{i-1}-s_{i+1})\); telescoping gives the entropy drift, and the distance-two spin observable has generator eigenvalue \(-2\). Its bounded Poisson corrector converts centered path entropy into a martingale, whose stationary bracket gives the exact variance rate. A direct product-spin expectation has an exceptional coefficient only when the two distance-two sites coincide at \(N=4\). The standalone checker passed 132,990 finite-state exact-rational assertions and three independent numerical principal-eigenvalue curvature comparisons. Finite tests are supplementary, not the infinite-size-uniform argument.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with residual priority risk)

Weiderpass–Sharma–Sethi (arXiv:2410.23615, arXiv:2411.00059), Godrèche (arXiv:1102.0141), and the asymmetric-Ising mean-entropy and network literature establish the model and several dynamical observables, but the inspected sections do not give the entropy-current variance-rate classification. A known three-site result already determines the size-three entropy-current variance, and a different prior result determines the mean entropy rate; both are excluded from the novelty assertion. Neither entails the four-site coefficient or its uniqueness for all sizes at least four without an additional Poisson-corrector computation. The all-size coefficient could nevertheless follow from a broader unsurveyed fluctuation theorem; publication priority requires independent checking.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The second asymptotic path-entropy cumulant quantifies fluctuations of irreversible dissipation beyond its already-known average. Its exact per-spin size independence for all rings with at least five sites, together with a unique four-spin correction caused by the topology of the distance-two neighbourhood, is a motivated finite-size classification. It distinguishes two systems with the same mean dissipation density and demonstrates a concrete finite-wrapping limitation of thermodynamic fluctuation approximations. The result is an exact finite-lattice statement, not a claim of a new general martingale method.

## Limits

Only zero symmetric coupling, stationary continuous-time spin-flip dynamics and the second time-asymptotic cumulant are treated. There is no claim of an independent audit or complete literature priority search.
