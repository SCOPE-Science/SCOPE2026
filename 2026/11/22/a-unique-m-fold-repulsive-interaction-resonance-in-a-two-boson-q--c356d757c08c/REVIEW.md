## Correctness — PASS
The result follows by a complete, dimension-counted permutation-symmetry decomposition of the interacting two-boson Fock space. The two distinct cubic characteristic factors are simple-root Jacobi polynomials. Their difference is exactly \(m(U-3x)\), and substitution gives a unique positive crossing at \(U=3\sqrt{m-3}\), at which one singlet and \(m-1\) standard states produce the exact multiplicity. Opposite limiting root orders prove reversal; no finite computation is used to infer the infinite family. Symbolic and full-matrix programs reproduce the formulas and check resonances.

An exact resonance involving an entire standard-representation multiplet, with a unique analytic interaction threshold at every valence, is a structural spectroscopy problem in a canonical finite interacting quantum network. The result reveals an interaction-induced branch ordering reversal rather than a generic irreducible three-level determinant calculation or a mere reevaluation of already known zero-energy dark levels.
