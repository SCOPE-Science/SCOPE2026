Two identical repulsive Hubbard bosons on an \(m\)-leaf quantum star have an exact, symmetry-resolved spectrum with a **unique positive-interaction \(m\)-fold level crossing** at \(U=3\sqrt{m-3}\), for every \(m\ge4\). The meeting of one symmetric and \(m-1\) standard-sector states reverses their energy ordering and can be proved by two cubic characteristic factors.
