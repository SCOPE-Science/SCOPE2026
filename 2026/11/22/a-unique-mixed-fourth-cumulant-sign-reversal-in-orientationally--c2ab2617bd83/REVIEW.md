# Review of the mixed-fourth-cumulant crossover

## Correctness — PASS
The probability model is specified by an angular Brownian generator plus rank-one Poisson resetting, and independent additive spatial noise. Its unperturbed angular eigenvalue at zero is simple with a spectral gap. Analytic spectral perturbation and Feynman–Kac justify the long-time fourth cumulant rate. The degree-four Fourier eigenfunction recurrence is exact, because multiplication by the one-harmonic velocity can reach only four harmonics in four perturbation steps. Symbolic simplification gives the reported rational formula. The exact coefficient sign sequence has one Descartes variation, and exact rational endpoint signs prove existence and simplicity of one positive root. A separate univariate directional fourth-cumulant calculation recovers the mixed coefficient through polarization. Checks include the known second cumulants, the two rate-endpoint limits and independent numerical finite-Fourier spectral integrals. Remaining limits: no finite-time fourth-cumulant sign, stochastic independence, or general non-Poisson protocol theorem.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The 2024 study by Baouche et al. derives the underlying orientational-reset model and its full intermediate scattering function, along with marginal moment formulas. Its transform encodes the joint cumulant implicitly, so a claim of a newly discovered model or general joint transform is excluded. The inspected main text and Appendix C did not present the mixed fourth tensor or the unique positive-root classification. Kumar et al. (2020) analyze orientation-only resetting but concentrate on marginals, mean-square displacement and short-time distributions. The closest publicly indexed results on exactly the same model concern the sixth longitudinal cumulant or the mixed third cumulant. The passive anisotropic Brownian study concerns another physical mechanism and an angular fourth-cumulant transition at a different rate. None of the compared statements implies the explicit present sign classification without new fourth-order mixed analysis. Residual priority risk is an equivalent derivation in unindexed work; the broad 2024 transform remains the strongest possible prior overlap.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A cross-direction fourth cumulant is a natural connected measure of longitudinal/transverse non-Gaussian dependence in a self-propelled particle. It is not recoverable from the known separate marginal variances and fourth moments. The theorem supplies a complete, sharp, physically dimensionless sign diagram, separating the negative-coupling regime from the positive-coupling regime under the same microscopic stochastic model. The boundary is universal under independent translational diffusion and provides a distinct higher-order signature not seen in Gaussian long-time scaling. The coefficient is an explicit structural result rather than an arbitrary parameter slice or recomputed table entry.
