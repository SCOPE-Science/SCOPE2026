The icosahedral thirty-two-vertex hull has a unique Mahler-volume-product maximizer on its entire post-rhombic radial branch, at face-normal radius \(\tau_*=1.00954708271666\ldots\), rather than at the unit-sphere ordinary-volume maximizer \(\tau=1\). An exact rational-cubic volume-product law gives the unique critical point and the subsequent decay to a constant dodecahedral limit.
