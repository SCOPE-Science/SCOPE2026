For every \(n\ge3\) and positive integer dimension \(m\), the expected vertex-averaged friendship paradox in a uniform maximum-norm torus graph has exactly one optimal connection radius, strictly between \(1/3\) and \(1/2\), and that radius decreases strictly with \(n\). It approaches \(1/3\) with an explicit exponentially small displacement as \(n\to\infty\) at fixed \(m\), but approaches \(1/2\) in a sharp \(m^{-1}\) boundary layer as \(m\to\infty\) at fixed \(n\).
