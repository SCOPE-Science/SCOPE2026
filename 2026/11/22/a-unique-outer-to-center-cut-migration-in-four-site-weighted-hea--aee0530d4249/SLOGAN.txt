For heat diffusion on a four-site path with equal outer conductances, the sharp \(BV\)-to-counting-\(\ell^1\) approximation error has a unique worst-cut migration when the middle conductance is weaker: end cuts dominate first, then the central cut forever. The transition time scales as \(\sqrt{6(1-r)/r}\) near equal conductances and as \((\log3)/r\) at a severe bottleneck, in units of the outer-edge heat timescale.
