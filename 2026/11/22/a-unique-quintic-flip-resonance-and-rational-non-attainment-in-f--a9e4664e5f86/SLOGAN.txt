In the exact one-nonlinear-visit returns of a power-law grazing map, the first flip is supercritical or subcritical according as \((1+a)^{M+1}\) lies below or above \(3(1-a)\). A unique irrational exponent at each return length makes the cubic coefficient vanish and creates an attracting quintic-critical orbit with \(n^{-1/4}\) relaxation, while no rational power exponent attains that equality.
