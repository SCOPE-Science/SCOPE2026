For scalar gradient descent with one-step-stale gradients that are independently erased with probability \(1-p\), the unique constant gain minimizing the exact asymptotic mean-square contraction is the larger subcritical root of \((1-p)^2h^3-8(1+p)h^2+8h-1=0\). That gain decreases strictly from \(1\) to \(1/4\) as delivery probability rises, while the sharp mean-square stability ceiling is \(4/(1+\sqrt{1+8p})\).
