In the exponential seed-shadow/Holling-II predator-satiation model, seed survivorship either increases everywhere or falls to exactly one minimum before recovering. Whenever the minimum exists, the source-to-survey-endpoint comparison changes sign at one unique observation radius \(R_c\), exactly specified by \(ahS(0)=(1-e^{-nR_c})/(e^{-nR_c}-e^{-dR_c})\); thus survey extent alone can reverse an apparent survival gradient.
