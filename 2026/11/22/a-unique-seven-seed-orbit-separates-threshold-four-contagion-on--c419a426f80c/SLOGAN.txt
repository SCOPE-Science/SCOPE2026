For threshold-four bootstrap percolation, the cospectral Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs both require six seeds, but only the Shrikhande graph has irredundant seven-seed contagious placements: exactly \(64\), forming one affine orbit characterized by induced degree multiset \((2,2,2,3,3,3,3)\). The complete minimal-set counts are \(2,224\) versus \(864\), with different exact propagation-time distributions.
