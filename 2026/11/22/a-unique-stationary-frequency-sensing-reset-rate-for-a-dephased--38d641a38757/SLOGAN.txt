For an equatorial qubit subject to positive pure dephasing and continuous Poisson resets, exactly one reset rate maximizes the stationary unconditioned quantum Fisher information about its Hamiltonian frequency. The optimum exceeds \(\sqrt{\gamma^2+\omega^2}\) for every nonzero \(\omega\), and tends to \(\sqrt{(1+\sqrt3)/2}\,|\omega|\) in the strong-detuning limit.
