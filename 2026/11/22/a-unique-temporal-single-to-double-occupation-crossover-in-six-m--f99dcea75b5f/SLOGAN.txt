In one-dimensional random sequential covering, six-mers are the first polymer length through six for which double-covered sites overtake single-covered sites at a unique finite time, \(t_*=0.8621923668\ldots\); for all lengths two through five, single coverage remains more common at every positive time. An exact 16-clock ordering recurrence proves the crossover and evaluates the complete saturated six-mer multiplicity law, including \(\pi_6^{(6)}(\infty)=19/873180\).
