For an ephemeral habitat bridge that erases migration progress whenever it closes, one exponential crossing stage favors arbitrarily fast landscape turnover, but **every** crossing with two or more independent exponential stages has exactly one best finite turnover rate at fixed bridge availability. Adding any positive-rate stage strictly slows that optimum, and for equally paced stages the best rate is asymptotic to \((\mu/\beta)[1+W_0(-p/e)]/n\).
