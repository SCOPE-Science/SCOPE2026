In a two-patch Gaussian thermal-niche population with symmetric dispersal, adding temperature heterogeneity changes from harmful to beneficial at exactly one mean-climate mismatch for every finite positive migration rate. The threshold rises strictly with migration, lies between \(a/2\) and \(a^{-1}\operatorname{arcosh}(e^{a^2/2})\), and has sharp weak- and strong-migration asymptotics.
