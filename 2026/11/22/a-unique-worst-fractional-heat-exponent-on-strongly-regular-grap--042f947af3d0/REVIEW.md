# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is for the unnormalized Laplacian of a fixed finite connected noncomplete strongly regular graph. Its two nonzero spectral projectors determine the exact three matrix-entry types. Adjacent and diagonal entries are strictly positive; the common nonedge entry has a ratio of exponential defects which decreases strictly from a value above the critical ratio to one for \(\alpha>1\). This proves the unique sharp recovery time without relying on finite computation. The scalar function \(h(z)=-\log(qe^{-z}-q+1)\) has positive strictly log-convex derivative, proving monotonicity of \(zh'(z)/h(z)\) and hence a unique global delay-maximizing exponent. Direct expansion proves the critical quadratic amplification and the large-exponent nonzero peak limit. The independent matrix-diagonalization checker passed its stated 568 matrix-time comparisons, 32 asymptotic scale comparisons, and 800 optimization grid points. These numerical tests supplement, not substitute for, the proofs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Riascos–Mateos (2014) develops \(0<\alpha<1\) fractional network diffusion, and the inspected full accessible text includes no above-one unique positivity-delay optimizer. Daners–Glück–Kennedy develops qualitative eventual-positive semigroup criteria, not a sharp optimizing exponent. The closest published fractional-heat positivity calculation covers complete multipartite graphs and therefore includes imprimitive equal-part strongly regular examples; its actual RESULT.md was compared and does not assert or prove a globally unique exponent maximizing recovery delay. The earlier mixed-heat theorem on all strongly regular graphs uses \(L+\eta L^2\), a distinct spectral dependence, and its exact parameter optimizer does not imply this fractional-exponent result. Primitive strongly regular examples provide new coverage not obtainable as special cases of multipartite graphs. Residual risk: unindexed or unavailable equivalent spectral-filter theorems may imply the result; priority is not certified.

## Value — PASS
It is a complete sharp classification on a natural and varied class (including Petersen, odd pentagonal, triangular and rook networks), not a hand-picked finite example. It identifies a unique worst non-Markovian fractional exponent, a reproducible scalar design criterion, and a marked distinction between shrinking positivity delay and persistent worst-case transient amplification as the exponent grows. These are substantive stability characteristics of graph diffusion that qualitative eventual-positivity criteria and raw spectral formulas do not themselves quantify.
