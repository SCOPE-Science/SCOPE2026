# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is fully quantified over finite, connected, unweighted noncomplete strongly regular graphs and all positive mixing coefficients. The proof constructs both nontrivial spectral projectors, verifies each of the three distinct entry types, and proves the unique positive-time zero of the nonedge entry by derivative sign changes. It then shows the sharp recovery time tends to zero at both extremes of the coefficient and uses a unique stationary spectral ratio to establish one global maximum and the exact closed expressions. The crucial nonstandard step is uniqueness of the time crossing and of the coupling maximizer; both are analytical. Direct matrix exponentials in the packaged checker validate 392 numerical cases across seven graph families, including primitive examples, but do not substitute for the proof. Limits: general graphs, weighted edges, other graph spectra and independently certified priority are not asserted.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority qualification

Daners–Glück–Kennedy (arXiv:1511.05294, finite-dimensional eventual-positivity theory) establishes a qualitative spectral framework, not this exact three-entry classification or globally optimized mixed generator. Lin–Weng (arXiv:1411.6532) gives relevant strongly regular spectra, not heat-sign thresholds. Gregorio–Mugnolo (arXiv:1712.07370, Section 2) treats pure graph bi-Laplacian heat and proves a qualitative positivity obstruction, not the all-coupling mixed semigroup. Relevant published results on all-coupling complete multipartite graphs cover a proper subfamily only; weak-mixing diameter-two results do not determine finite arbitrary coefficients; Padé thresholds concern rational time-discrete propagators rather than the continuous exponential. Semantic searches disclosed no direct equivalent, but searchable-index incompleteness and inaccessible complete source texts limit absolute priority.

## Value — PASS

The result gives an exact and global sign-dynamics classification in the spectral three-eigenvalue graph family, with a unique tunable coupling that maximizes the duration of non-Markovian behavior. Its parameter-only formula applies to primitive strongly regular families (pentagon, Petersen, triangular and Paley graphs) beyond already treated multipartite cases. It yields a precise benchmark for competing second- and fourth-order diffusion, rather than an arbitrary finite calculation or a routine restatement of eventual positivity.

## Closest literature and limits

Source comparisons are statement-and-implication based. The general finite-dimensional eventual-positivity theorem is weaker in quantitative content; the pure bi-Laplacian obstruction is about a different generator and has no optimizing coupling. Relevant earlier complete multipartite positivity-horizon theorems give proper special cases, not an all-strongly-regular theorem. Accessible literature retrieval did not supply complete text of two plausible related records, so their available summaries were compared without treating access failure as proof of absence. Numerical checks are consistency tests, not a mathematical certification.
