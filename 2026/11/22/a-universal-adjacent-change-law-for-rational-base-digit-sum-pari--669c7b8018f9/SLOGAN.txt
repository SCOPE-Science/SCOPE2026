For the parity word of digit sums in any coprime rational radix \(b/a>1\), the number \(C(N)\) of adjacent changes obeys the exact identity \(C(bN)+C(aN)=tN\) when exactly one of \(a,b\) is even, with \(t\) that even parameter. Consequently the adjacent-change density is \(t/(a+b)\) with logarithmic counting discrepancy; when both parameters are odd the word simply alternates.
