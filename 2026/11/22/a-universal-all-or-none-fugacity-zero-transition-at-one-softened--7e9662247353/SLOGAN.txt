On an even hard-core chain, a single softened central bond creates a universal all-or-none fugacity-zero threshold: for \(0\le a<1\) every zero is simple and negative, at \(a=1\) every zero is double, and for \(a>1\) none is real. The \(\lfloor(m+1)/2\rfloor\) simultaneous collisions have explicit square-root openings.
