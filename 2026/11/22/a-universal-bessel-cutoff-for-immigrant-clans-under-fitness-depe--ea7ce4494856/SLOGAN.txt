In a fitness-dependent locally cleared population, the many-site stationary occupation is compound Poisson, and near optimal fitness its large-family jump intensities have a universal cutoff: \(n\Lambda_n\to2\sqrt{s}K_1(2\sqrt{s})\) when \(n r(\ell)\to s>0\). This Bessel profile resolves rare-clan sizes beyond the already-known mean and factorial-moment thresholds.
