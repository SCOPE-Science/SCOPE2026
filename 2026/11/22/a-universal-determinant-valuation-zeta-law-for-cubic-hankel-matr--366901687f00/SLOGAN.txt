The determinant of a random \(3\times3\) Hankel matrix over every finite commutative chain ring has the same explicit valuation law \(\frac{(1-q^{-1})(1-q^{-3}t)(1-q^{-5}t^2)}{(1-q^{-1}t)(1-q^{-3}t^2)(1-q^{-5}t^3)}\), regardless of ring characteristic or ramification. The formula counts every determinant-divisibility stratum at every finite length and specializes to the known topological zeta expression for the corresponding catalecticant cubic.
