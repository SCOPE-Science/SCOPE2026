For every odd prime \(p\) and every proportional binomial ray \(\binom{mn}{n}\) with \(m\not\equiv0,1\pmod p\), exactly \(((p+1)/2)^r\) indices below \(p^r\) give nonzero coefficients modulo \(p\). More strongly, every admissible base-\(p\) suffix has the same exact number of extensions, yielding a multiplier-independent, Ahlfors-regular \(p\)-adic fractal of dimension \(\log((p+1)/2)/\log p\).
