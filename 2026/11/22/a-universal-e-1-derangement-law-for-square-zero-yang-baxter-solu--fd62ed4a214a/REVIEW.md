# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem uses a prime field and a square-zero linear map of specified rank, with \(n\ge2r\). The preservation equation is equivalent to the stated equivariance normal form: the induced map on \(W\) is forced to be additive and injective, while coset permutations and shifts are free subject only to \(\beta\tau=\phi\beta\). The group count follows fiberwise. Conditional fixed points arise solely from fixed cosets, each contributing either zero or the full \(p^d\)-element kernel of \(I-\phi\). The truncated-exponential factorial-moment identity proves the exact probability-generating function. The Poisson mixture limit follows at fixed characteristic and rank; the alternating-series formula gives the stated factorial derangement error bound uniformly. Direct exhaustive bijection checks also agree for 4-, 8- and 9-element solutions; the infinite proof does not rely on those finite cases. Residual mathematical risk: none identified in these assumptions after a separate algebraic recomputation, though external expert validation is absent.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence

The 2023 Jedlička–Pilitowska paper (arXiv:2303.15154, Example 3.2 and Theorem 3.11) already gives the square-zero example and a general criterion for isomorphisms of 2-reductive solutions. These are credited and not presented as new. The exact conditional fixed-point generating function and universal \(e^{-1}\) derangement limit are not stated there. The 2019–2020 Jedlička–Pilitowska–Zamojska-Dzienio construction (arXiv:1901.01471) is restricted to involutive solutions and does not state this law in inspected sections. Indexed research comparisons with an exponent-four core-quandle fixed-point law and a binary transposition cycle-set wreath group concern mathematically different structures. Residual risk: an older general enumeration of automorphism fixed points or an unindexed corollary of the affine-mesh classification could cover the claim; these were not shown to imply it.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The full automorphism action has two orbits, and the linear part changes the sizes of its fixed sets, yet the zero-fixed-point probability has a universal limit independent of both prime and nilpotent rank. The exact finite-size polynomial and explicit group order supply a reproducible symmetry invariant of a natural family of nondegenerate Yang–Baxter solutions, relevant to studying automorphism-rigidity and random symmetries of set-theoretic solutions. The observation is more than a rank-substitution: it extracts and proves a conditional permutation law not supplied by the general classification theorem. The Poisson-mixture approximation is fixed-rank; the proved factorial derangement error bound is uniform even with growing rank.
