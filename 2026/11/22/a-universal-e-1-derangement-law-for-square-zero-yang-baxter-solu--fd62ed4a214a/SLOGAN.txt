For square-zero linear maps of fixed positive rank over a prime field, the automorphisms of the associated 2-reductive Yang–Baxter solution have an explicit wreath-type structure and an exact conditional fixed-point generating function. As the ambient dimension grows, the number of fixed vertices converges to a mixture of scaled Poisson variables, yet the probability of no fixed vertices always tends to \(e^{-1}\), independent of the characteristic and rank.
