The entire Italian-domination polynomial of any one-leaf corona \(G\circ K_1\) is determined by the external-neighborhood sizes of subsets of its base graph: \(D_I(G\circ K_1;z)=\sum_{P\subseteq V(G)}z^{2|V(G)|-|B_G(P)|}(2+2z+z^2)^{|P|}(1+z)^{|B_G(P)|}\). This formula counts all weights, refines the previously known minimum-weight identity \(\gamma_I(G\circ K_1)=|V(G)|+\gamma(G)\), and determines the multiplicity of every optimum.
