For maxima of Borel family sizes in a fixed-mean generalized-Poisson sample approaching critical branching, the first finite-size CDF error is of order \(n^{-1/2}\) and reverses sign exactly once at the universal rescaled threshold \(ax=1.888985943515\ldots\). The coefficient and its unique zero are explicit, while the underlying compound-Poisson/Borel representation and the inverse-Gaussian total-count limit are established prior work.
