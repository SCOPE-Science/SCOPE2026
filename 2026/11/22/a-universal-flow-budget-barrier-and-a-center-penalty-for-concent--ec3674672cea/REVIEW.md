# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact Robin gauge, variational characterization, completed-square point-trace inequality, compactness equality case, positive critical atom eigenfunction and Riccati supercritical location argument establish the full quantified result. The counterexample uses the exact bound \(E[u]\ge D h^2\|u\|_2^2\), not a finite spatial discretization. `check.py` independently exercises the key identities and representative parameter calculations; it is corroboration, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (literature-bounded)

Alexandre–Loverdo–Bitbol (arXiv:2611.00134v1) supplies the physical branching motivation and the adjoint boundary pair; its criticality theorem treats a resident-determined birth-rate profile, while its washout analysis uses a self-consistent finite-width step. Caubet–Deheuvels–Privat (arXiv:1704.08016) provides closely related Robin resource-optimization theorems, but for bounded signed resources and resource-gradient drift. The exact universally sharp integrated budget \(B=v\), uniqueness of the critical atomic profile, and point-source location law are not consequences of those inspected hypotheses. Targeted mathematical and source-specific searches, relevant published-record comparisons revealed no direct duplication. Older general point-interaction or sharp Sobolev literature could independently contain an equivalent identity; priority remains limited by that risk.

## Value — PASS

For a naturally motivated linear reproductive potential in a flowing habitat, the resource budget is a physically meaningful invariant. The theorem proves an all-profile washout barrier, its unique singular saturator, the geometry of supercritical point reproduction, and the strict opposite-sign example for two equal budgets. This demonstrates precisely when spatial arrangement, not only total resources, determines the spectral invasion possibility.

## Scope and limitations

Only the specified one-dimensional constant-drift, constant-diffusion Neumann/Robin backward operator is covered. Singular growth is an idealization of narrow source patches. No self-consistent resident profile, stochastic fixation formula, nonlinear persistence or independent validation is claimed. The accessible preprint sections and related primary resource-optimization theorems were directly inspected; older generic spectral literature has not been exhaustively checked.
