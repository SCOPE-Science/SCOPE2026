# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Conditional on no reset, the initial Gaussian and future Gaussian-affine evolution form a jointly Gaussian pair. Conditional on at least one independent full-state reset, the future is independent of the past. The covariance and four-point decomposition therefore follow exactly. Stationarity cancels the conditional-variance terms. The two cross-pairings have coefficient \(e^{-rt}-e^{-2rt}\); after division by the covariance products this becomes \(e^{rt}-1\). The matrix inverse contraction is a finite index identity with value \(d(d+1)(e^{rt}-1)\). Strict witness positivity follows from invertible cross-covariance. The companion deterministic checker passes 156 assertions. The isotropic non-Gaussian source is normalized, strictly nonnegative, and solves the forward stationarity equation. No finite numerical test is used as an all-parameter proof.

## Originality
**PASS, qualified.** Olsen's original 2023 full-text treatment proves stationary moment renewal formulas but not this fourth-order two-time identity. The published Gaussian stationary-state engineering theorem establishes source feasibility and lag covariance, but does not state the present tensor identity or drift-independent one-lag reconstruction. Crucially, prior work *does* establish the one-dimensional equilibrium-Gaussian rethermalization fourth-cumulant special case. That special case, general Poisson renewal, Gaussian moment factorization, source nonnegativity and all stationary covariance formulas are excluded from the novelty claim. The assessed contribution is the full \(d\ge2\) tensor contraction when refresh laws are genuinely non-Gaussian and arbitrary stable drifts need not be symmetric. It is plausible that a more general, unindexed identity implies the theorem; no independent priority certificate is asserted.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** Gaussian stationary marginals and complete second-order temporal covariance cannot reveal whether a system is an ordinary Gaussian OU flow or a non-Gaussian jump-diffusion with a genuinely non-Gaussian reset source. The theorem proves a strictly positive fourth-order directional diagnostic and reconstructs the independent reset intensity from exactly one lag even with unknown drift, noise and reset density. This is a structural identifiability result for multidimensional nonequilibrium dynamics, not a sampled table or an arbitrary scalar parameter slice. It does not estimate finite-sample uncertainty.

## Closest comparisons and limitations
The closest earlier scalar rethermalization formula is explicitly credited and is not proposed as new. Olsen's 2023 full text and the 2026 Gaussian stationary-engineering result were inspected directly. The 2026 four-time bath cumulant result addresses a different, non-Gaussian stationary bath. Remaining risks are broader unpublished multipoint renewal identities and practical ill-conditioning of the inverse covariance at long lags. The theorem requires ideal stationary centered Gaussian output and state-independent complete Poisson refreshes; outside this domain the inference formula need not hold.
