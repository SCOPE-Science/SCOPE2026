For a multivariate Ornstein–Uhlenbeck diffusion whose non-Gaussian Poisson resets produce an exactly Gaussian stationary law, the entire two-time fourth-cumulant tensor is fixed by the two-time covariance and reset rate: \(K_{ijkl}(t)=(e^{rt}-1)(C_{ik}C_{jl}+C_{il}C_{jk})\). A single tensor contraction recovers the hidden clock rate without knowing drift, diffusivity or reset distribution, even though a purely Gaussian process reproduces every stationary one-time and two-time covariance observable.
