For a deterministically driven particle resetting at rate \(\lambda v^\alpha\), increasing viscous damping strictly moves every normalized convex risk of the inter-reset time toward the exponential benchmark: regularity decreases when \(\alpha>0\) and increases when \(-1<\alpha<0\). The long-time reset-count Fano factor follows a sharp Weibull-to-Poisson interval and has an explicit first-order strong-damping coefficient.
