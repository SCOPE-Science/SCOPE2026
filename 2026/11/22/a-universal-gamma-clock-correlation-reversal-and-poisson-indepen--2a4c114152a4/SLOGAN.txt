For completely Gamma-reset two-dimensional comb diffusion, the stationary normalized covariance of squared backbone and finger displacements is exactly \((1-k)/(5(k+1))\): it reverses sign precisely at exponential resetting, independently of diffusion constants and reset rate. The full stationary coordinates are independent within the Gamma family if and only if the clock is exponential; the known Poisson product density supplies the equality case.
