## Value: PASS

The problem arises because experimentally realizable resetting does not teleport a particle: return trajectories are physical and may detect a target. The theorem characterizes the rare successful returns that make idealized instantaneous resets inaccurate, resolving both the \(\kappa^{-2}\) probability and the *coupled* position–time selection mechanism. The gamma spatial law and inverse-Gaussian conditional passage clock are finer than the known mean-first-passage-time results; the dependence on the fixed search dynamics enters through the local absorbing-boundary flux.
