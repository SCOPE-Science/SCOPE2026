When a Brownian searcher resets its position and all boundary-contact reaction progress, retaining rather than resampling the target's open/closed gate adds exactly \(\frac{\beta}{\alpha(\alpha+\beta)}\sqrt{\frac{r}{r+\alpha+\beta}}e^{-L(\sqrt{(r+\alpha+\beta)/D}-\sqrt{r/D})}\) to the mean reaction time, for **every** independently redrawn boundary-contact threshold law. The formula itself is known for instantaneous absorption; the finding is its universality for arbitrary finite, possibly nonexponential reaction thresholds.
