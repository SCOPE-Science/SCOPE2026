On a hard-core gas supported by a star with every edge subdivided once, exactly \(m\) partition zeros lie in a fixed disk \(|z+1/3|<1/3\), while one negative root escapes to \(-2^m-m/2+O(m^2 2^{-m})\). All roots are simple, the number of real roots alternates between two and three with the parity of \(m\), and the bounded roots approach the disk boundary as \(m\) increases.
