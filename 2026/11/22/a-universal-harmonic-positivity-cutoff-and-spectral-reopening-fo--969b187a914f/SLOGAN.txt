For every summable positive mixture of circle Poisson kernels, signed modulation at harmonic order \(k\) remains positive definite precisely above one explicit Fourier-frequency-\(k\) threshold. At that boundary, a single Poisson scale collapses to Fourier rank \(2k-1\), whereas any nondegenerate scale mixture reopens every other Fourier mode and remains strictly positive definite on distinct sites.
