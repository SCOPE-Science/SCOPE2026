On weighted quadrilateral-book networks, two perfectly pinned leaders minimizing squared-inverse grounded-Laplacian coherence are always the two shared hubs once there are at least three pages, regardless of rung conductance. Only the two-page book has a transition: above the unique positive root of \(54t^4+126t^3+37t^2-48t-23\), the two optimal pairs move to opposite-layer peripheral vertices of distinct pages.
