# Verification information

The stated theorem follows from conditioning on the first native arrival, differentiation of the exact probability integral, and a strict crossing argument. The equation for the unique optimizer has opposite endpoint signs for every positive rate quadruple. Short-horizon asymptotics follow from the second-order expansion of the two-site race, and long-horizon asymptotics follow from the exponential balance of the two failure modes.

Run `python3 check.py` from the folder containing this document. The script independently integrates the event density and checks the closed form over 1,600 deterministic seeded random parameter sets; it also checks a global finite grid, analytic derivatives, horizon monotonicity and both asymptotic laws. These tests are finite checks and are not substitutes for the mathematical proof. No independent external validation has been carried out.
