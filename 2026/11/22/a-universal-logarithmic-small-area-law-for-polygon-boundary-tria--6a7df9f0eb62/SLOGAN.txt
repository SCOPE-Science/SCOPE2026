For three independent arclength-uniform boundary points of any fixed convex polygon with at least four genuine sides, the triangle area has a zero atom and a positive small-area tail of exact leading order \(C(K)s\log(1/s)\) after normalization by squared perimeter. The sharp many-sided inequality \(C(K)\ge24\) has equality precisely for rectangles, and conditioned rare triangles have independent uniform limiting logarithmic-gap, location and relative-area marks; the previously known host-triangle distribution is explicitly excluded.
