For every integer integration order \(p\ge0\), the uniquely optimal time to make one noisy Brownian base-process observation for reconstructing its \(p\)-fold integral stays strictly interior at every finite noise variance and moves monotonically toward the terminal time. The high-noise endpoint gap is asymptotic to \([2(2p+3)\eta]^{-1/(p+1)}\), and the resulting sharp integrated-risk advantage has order \(\eta^{-2-1/(p+1)}\).
