In uniform fixed-ball occupancy on *any* tree with \(m\ge3\) sites, covariance between the number of occupied sites and their number of connected clusters is independent of tree shape and changes sign exactly once as the number of balls increases. The crossover lies at \(m\log 2+0.7431513155\ldots+O(m^{-1})\), separating positive sparse-load from negative dense-load association.
