For seeds carried by planar Brownian motion, **every nonconstant positive retention-time law with finite mean** has exactly one distance at which its probability of dispersing farther crosses that of a fixed retention time equal to the same mean. The probability difference has exactly one negative trough and one positive peak, and under weak variation their squared-distance scales approach \(2-\sqrt2\), \(2\), and \(2+\sqrt2\) times the mean retention time after diffusion scaling.
