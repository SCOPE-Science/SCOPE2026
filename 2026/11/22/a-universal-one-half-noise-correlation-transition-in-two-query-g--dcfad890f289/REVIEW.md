# Scientific scope of the revised claim

Joint Gaussianity and independence of the error vector justify deterministic Gaussian posterior variance. The uniform inverse-matrix estimate permits optimization over the full compact site space, and its limiting strictly convex quadratic identifies the maximizing vertices and the exact half-correlation threshold. The covariance of non-Gaussian noise alone cannot support this conclusion for Bayesian posterior variance.

The Brownian-bridge limit is illustrative; the published record *A sharp finite-noise symmetry-breaking frontier in two-query Brownian-bridge quadrature* establishes the stronger optimizer classification in that model for nonnegative correlations. The remaining claim describes the limiting optimizer for general latent kernels and can apply to reference sites with positive underlying latent variance. It does not resolve optimizer selection at the leading-order tie, a general finite-noise phase diagram, or historical priority beyond the inspected sources.
