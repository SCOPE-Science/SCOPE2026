In noisy two-query Gaussian-process quadrature, a universal high-noise transition occurs at error correlation \(\rho=1/2\): below it the best query sites converge to repeated maximum-signal locations; above it one query instead converges to a zero-signal reference location. For a Brownian-bridge integral, positive error correlation can even make every reflection-symmetric placement strictly suboptimal at finite noise, with an explicit rational witness.
