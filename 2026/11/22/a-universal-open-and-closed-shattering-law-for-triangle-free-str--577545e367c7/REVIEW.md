Closest available primary works treat VC dimensions or general algorithms, not this uniform complete open/closed face classification. Historical priority remains subject to that uncertainty and to incompletely accessible older records.

## Value (V): PASS
The natural simultaneous open-versus-closed incidence question has an exact parameter-only classification, rather than only a scalar dimension or arbitrary finite census. It identifies the structural difference between nonedge-only open pairs and all closed pairs, and between independent triples and induced paths, across a distinguished family containing the Clebsch and Gewirtz graphs. A canonical nonzero mod-two first cohomology class records a structural obstruction in every closed shattering complex; this should be read as a uniform interpretation, not a newly discovered Clebsch homotopy type.

## Limitations
The all-parameter theorem does not calculate integral homotopy, does not cover other strongly regular parameters, and is not externally certified. Finite Betti calculations refer only to the Clebsch case. The full strongly-regular VC paper of 2026 was not read, creating a genuine unexcluded overlap risk.
