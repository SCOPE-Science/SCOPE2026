# Review of the rational \((2,2)\)-contact Poncelet count

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof uses the complete pencil \(F_D=\mu F_C+\nu\ell^2\) and its unique rational contact line to classify all smooth pairs. Chord tangency reduces to \((u+v)^2=4auv\), so the two reciprocal steps lie in either the split multiplicative torus or the norm-one torus. The rational graph and every extension graph then follow by orbit counting, with exact line counts and a quadratic-character identity supplying the global total. Both direct-adjacency enumeration programs reproduce all predicted spectra: prime fields of sizes \(3,5,7,11,13\) and the extension field of size \(9\). Computation is a supplementary check, not a substitute for the all-field proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual priority risk.** Jow–Liang establish the geometric tangency/porism theorem but not these rational finite-field graph and joint all-period count formulas. Hungerbühler–Kusejko prove the cyclotomic/totient period count **already** for a special nested nonsplit pencil: that substantial portion is explicitly excluded from novelty. Radnović–Ragas analyze low periods \(3\) and \(4\) in all pencils, not all lengths and the joint polygon total; Wang works with transverse intersections. The additional split-contact arithmetic, matching obstruction, even/odd extension law, and universal \(\mathrm{PGL}_3\)-weighted count remain outside the inspected theorem statements. An older unindexed all-period enumeration is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The natural geometric \((2,2)\) stratum has two rational contact-splitting types with different rational accessibility. Determining all possible primitive periods, exact cycle counts, a sharp quadratic extension threshold, and the total number of rational polygons across all ordered smooth conic pairs resolves a complete arithmetic enumeration problem. The cancellation of contact-type factors in the universal formula is not merely a restatement of a single nested-pencil count. No theorem about other contact strata or characteristic two is asserted.
