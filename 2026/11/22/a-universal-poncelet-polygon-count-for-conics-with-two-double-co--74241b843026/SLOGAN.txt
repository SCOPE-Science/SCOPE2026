For every odd finite field, the total number of rational simple Poncelet \(n\)-gons arising from ordered smooth conic pairs with intersection divisor \(2P+2Q\) is exactly \(|\mathrm{PGL}_3(\mathbb F_q)|\varphi(n)/(4n)\) when \(n\ge3\) divides \(q-1\) or \(q+1\), and zero otherwise. Every such pair has either no rational polygon or a single simple polygon length, and all polygon-free pairs acquire polygons over the quadratic extension.
