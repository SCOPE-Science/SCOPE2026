For homogeneous bidirected Markov stars, third-order Runge–Kutta positivity fails first at the hub diagonal or a leaf-to-leaf transition, with a **sharp dimension-independent switch** at the unique positive root \(r_*\approx0.5621496619\) of \(16r^3+9r^2-3r-4=0\), where \(r\) is leaf-to-hub rate divided by total hub departure rate. The exact nonnegative-step set is one interval, and positivity never returns after its endpoint.
