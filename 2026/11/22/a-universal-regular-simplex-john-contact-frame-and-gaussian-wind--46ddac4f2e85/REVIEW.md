Todd's complete 1982 primary text concerns minimum-volume *enclosing* ellipsoids of one-sided cuts; the closest published one-sided-cap result [R1] uses that outer formula and obtains a strictly increasing outer-to-cap volume quotient. The general theorem of Gruber and Schuster characterizes John contacts but gives no cap-specific profile. Full published accounts of symmetric two-ball lens [R2], centered ball-box [R3] and positive spherical orthant ellipsoids were inspected and their normalized contact geometries compared, not merely their titles. No inspected result gives the one-sided **inscribed** extremizer, its flat-plus-latitude regular-simplex contact and strictly decreasing Gaussian-window occupied fraction. ## Value — PASS

The geometry is the complete natural one-cut ball family, rather than a chosen numerical slice. The construction is a sharp, unrestricted analytic solution and a finite, dimension-universal contact skeleton. Its pointwise high-dimensional cap-height window produces an explicit monotone Gaussian shape law with a sharp terminal constant \(\sqrt{2\pi}/e\) matching the independently computed thin-cap coefficient. These are useful quantitative geometric-approximation benchmarks, and the opposite behavior of the known outer-volume ratio gives a meaningful structural comparison. No general multi-cut or robust statistical theorem is claimed.
