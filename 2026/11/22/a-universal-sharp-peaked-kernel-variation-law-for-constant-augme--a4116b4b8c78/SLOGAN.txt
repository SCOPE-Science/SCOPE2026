For every fixed one-dimensional grid and every strictly positive definite, monotone decaying peaked radial kernel, constant-augmented interpolation has the universal sharp narrow-kernel total-variation amplification \(4\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/n-1\), independent of mesh irregularity and kernel profile. A central single-jump input attains the asymptotic bound; the coefficient grows linearly with the number of interpolation sites even though constants are reproduced exactly.
