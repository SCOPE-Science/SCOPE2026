For any odd monotone \(1\)-Lipschitz scalar saturation with unit slope at zero, the separable planar forward iteration with a rotational skew \(s\) is Fejér monotone at its zero **exactly** when \(|s|\le1\) and \(0<\gamma\le2/(1+s^2)\), while pairwise nonexpansiveness survives only when \(s=0\). Every orbit nevertheless converges for \(0<\gamma<2/(1+s^2)\), even in the high-skew regime where every positive stepsize has a distance-increasing state.
