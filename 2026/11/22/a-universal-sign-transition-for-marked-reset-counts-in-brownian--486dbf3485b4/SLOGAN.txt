For Brownian target search resetting independently to finitely many labelled sites, the covariance between counts of visits to two reset sites has an exact sign depending only on the initial and the two destination distances, not on any reset mixing weights. It is positive at all reset rates when the initial distance does not exceed the sum of the two destination distances; otherwise it crosses exactly once from negative to positive, and all negative-pair graphs form a decreasing family of threshold graphs.
