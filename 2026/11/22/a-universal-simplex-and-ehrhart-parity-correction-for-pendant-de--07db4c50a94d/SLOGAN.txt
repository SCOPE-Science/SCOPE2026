For an odd cycle carrying any positive number of pendant edges, the entire gap between the fractional matching polytope and the matching polytope is one simplex of Euclidean volume \(1/(2d!)\), where \(d\) is the number of edges. Its complete Ehrhart lattice-point discrepancy has generating function \(z^2/((1-z)^d(1-z^2))\), independent of which cycle vertices carry the leaves, and forces minimal quasiperiod two.
