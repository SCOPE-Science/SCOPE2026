In the 2023 symmetrically coupled Gaussian-cubic map, infinitesimally supercritical coupling produces a universal escape-time crossover: the time needed for the antidiagonal squared amplitude to reach \(U_\varepsilon+t\), rescaled by \(4\varepsilon U_\varepsilon\), converges to the softplus function \(\log(1+e^t)\). The result resolves the transient between the previously known logarithmic escape at critical coupling and the off-critical geometric escape, without claiming new periodic-orbit classifications.
