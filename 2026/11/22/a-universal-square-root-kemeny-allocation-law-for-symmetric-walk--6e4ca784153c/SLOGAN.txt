On the graph obtained by attaching any fixed number of private leaves to every vertex of a connected vertex- and edge-transitive core, Kemeny's constant has a unique globally optimal symmetric stochastic walk, with an exact square-root allocation determined solely by the core's size, degree and inverse-Laplacian trace. On cycle cores the optimal pendant probability vanishes as the inverse square root of size and maximum-degree weighting incurs a fixed penalty; on clique cores the pendant probability stays positive and that penalty grows linearly with size.
