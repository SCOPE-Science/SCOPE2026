## Correctness — PASS
The pressure follows from the unique normalized block-weight distribution and the induced entropy/Kullback–Leibler inequality; the marker-free component has strictly smaller free energy. The factor-language partition uses the Sturmian balance dichotomy, the exact \(n+1\) word count, and uniform irrational-rotation discrepancy. Weighted Abelian sums have explicit \(\delta^{-2}\), \(\delta^{-3}\) and derivative asymptotics, yielding the square-root pressure law and the marker/color occupation laws. The \(t=0\) special case is exactly soluble. The finite checker passed twelve direct factor tests and six pressure/composition cases; the result rests on the analytic argument, not extrapolation from these tests.

## Originality — PASS within checked evidence
Ramirez and Somers (arXiv:2501.08438v1, published 2025) prove an unweighted complexity/entropy relation for decorated gap shifts, not the weighted low-activity pressure coefficient or its injective dependence on irrational slope. Haydarov et al. (arXiv:2606.17031v1, 2026) establish renewal-type equilibrium methods for ordinary binary \(S\)-gap shifts, not the distinct colored Sturmian word distribution. The published result *Periodic zeta rationality versus Sturmian nonsoficity in decorated gap shifts* (published-finding corpus 2026-10-25) proves complete unweighted periodic-point and zeta invariance under slope, not colored pressure; our theorem provides the contrasting first slope-sensitive correction. Fici, Mignosi and Shallit (arXiv:1701.00948, Proposition 14) already establish the heavy/light rotation partition underlying the calculation; that combinatorial fact is explicitly credited and not claimed as new. A broader multivariate renewal theorem might specialize to these formulas, and full historical priority is not independently certified.

## Value — PASS
The motivating problem is whether an unweighted dynamical invariant that provably forgets a Sturmian slope can be enhanced by a natural one-site color field to recover it. The theorem answers that question constructively: a universal square-root activity exponent coexists with an explicitly slope-dependent response coefficient. This is a nontrivial thermodynamic distinction between a continuum of concrete nonsofic shifts, not a numerical parameter substitution or isolated finite computation.
