Sturmian-decorated renewal shifts have identical marker-only pressure, but their low-activity pressure response to a colored symbol field is \(P_\alpha(x,t)=\alpha t+e^{-\alpha t/2}(2\sinh(t/2)/t)\sqrt{x}+o(\sqrt{x})\), with the quotient interpreted continuously at zero. The singular coefficient recovers the irrational Sturmian slope and yields exact first-order laws for equilibrium marker and colored-symbol densities.
