For point-source Brownian particles born in stochastic bursts and killed independently, every nonzero stationary connected factorial correlation of order \(j\ge3\) has a sharply localized far-field equal-source-distance ridge. Its universal transverse profile is Gaussian at width proportional to \(\sqrt R\), while unequal radial distances suppress the normalized correlation exponentially; the geometry is independent of the burst-size law apart from its positive factorial-moment amplitude.
