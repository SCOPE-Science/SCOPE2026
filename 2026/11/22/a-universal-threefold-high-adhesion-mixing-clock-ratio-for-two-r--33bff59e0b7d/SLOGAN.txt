For every fixed deck size \(n\ge4\), two binomial-cut adhesive riffle models have the same strong-adhesion limiting full-deck mixing profile after a universal threefold time change: midpoint release evolves three times as fast as uniform block release on the rare-contact-break scale. In particular their fixed-error mixing-time ratio tends to \(1/3\), although their finite-adhesion kernels are not simple time changes.
