The classical Bernstein–Durrmeyer smoother and coordinate multiplication become commuting in operator norm, yet their commutator has trace norm tending to the exact nonzero constant \(1/\pi\). A square-root-sized window of Legendre modes produces a universal all-Schatten spectral law, and the commutator's stable rank grows sharply like \(e\sqrt\pi\sqrt n/(8\sqrt2)\).
