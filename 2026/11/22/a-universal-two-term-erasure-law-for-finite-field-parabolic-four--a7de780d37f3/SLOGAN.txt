Deleting any fixed number \(h\) of frequencies from a finite-field parabola lowers the sharp endpoint Fourier-projection norm by a universal \(3h/(4\sqrt q)\), while the entire next-order effect is the one-dimensional Fourier \(\ell^1\) norm of the deleted parameter set. For two deletions that second term is exactly characteristic-dependent through \(2/[p\sin(\pi/(2p))]\), revealing arithmetic sensitivity beyond the universal leading law.
