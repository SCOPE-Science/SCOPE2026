For two perfect leaders on a balanced complete bipartite graph with a distinguished matching of conductance \(t\), minimizing the grounded inverse-square trace switches from matched cross-part leaders to same-part leaders at a unique sixth-degree threshold \(\theta_n=1/2-3/(4n^3)+O(n^{-4})\); no unmatched cross-part pair is ever optimal. The first inverse-power transition lies at \(1/2-1/(2n^2)+O(n^{-3})\), leaving an asymptotically sharp interval of width \(1/(2n^2)\) where the two objectives require different leader types.
