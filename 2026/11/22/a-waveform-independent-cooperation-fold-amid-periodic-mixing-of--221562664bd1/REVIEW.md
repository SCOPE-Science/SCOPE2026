# Scientific review: exact periodic-mixing fold in nonlinear pair/triple public goods

## Correctness — PASS

The well-mixed payoff definitions are checked against equations (11)–(12) of Llabrés et al. (2026). The strict-convexity vertex and the mean-interaction interval follow directly by differentiating the quadratic. The payoff difference between the two game orders is strictly increasing in cooperation and crosses zero inside the vertex range at exactly one mean mix when \(b^2/3>a/2\). At that mix, the exact constant orbit persists under every continuous admissible mean-preserving periodic schedule. Its linear return multiplier is one, its quadratic coefficient is a strictly positive integral (positive mixture and integrating factor), and the benefit derivative is positive. These are the nondegeneracy conditions for the periodic saddle-node without assuming small amplitude. Away from that point the mean-zero periodic Lyapunov–Schmidt expansion has a nonzero negative second-order coefficient involving \(\langle W^2\rangle\), and the mixed term integrates to zero. Exact symbolic arithmetic and eight numerical pinned-orbit checks, plus six non-pinned fold reconstructions, are provided in `verify.py`. The proof is local, not a classification of every global periodic orbit.

## Originality — PASS, literature-bounded

The closest primary full text, arXiv:2604.07228 (initial public version April 8, 2026, current full PDF version September 2026), covers constant interaction-order fractions and autonomous equilibria, including Appendix A's root-count result. It does not establish the exact neutral return map under arbitrary periodic mixing. A published temporal higher-order Prisoner's Dilemma result gives a different, positively shifted periodic fold; its interaction-order payoff difference does not satisfy the pinned cross-game condition. A published fixed-mix finite-state closure of the same source model concerns stochastic fixation and a potential-height transition, not the existence of neutral periodic responses. Zhu, Kuske, Erneux (2014) address the general amplitude-squared forcing shift in a canonical slow-drift saddle-node, so no priority is asserted for that perturbation principle. The model-specific unique mix, exact all-amplitude neutral fold and waveform-wide strict sign are the assessed claim, with residual risk of older or unindexed forcing work.

## Value — PASS

The first two groups are the smallest interaction orders in which nonlinear collective synergy and discounting compete to create the motivating interior coexistence fold. The result identifies an exact, nontrivial structurally protected mean of game composition at which all admissible mean-preserving seasonal schedules have the same local coexistence-loss threshold, while every other such mean responds in one sharp direction at weak forcing. This gives a mathematical reason to distinguish composition-average effects from within-season variance; the explicit algebraic parameter example and computable shift make the distinction testable within the model. This is more than applying a generic forced saddle-node normal form because exact pinning at arbitrary waveform amplitude requires the common payoff-value crossing and an exact return-map cancellation.

## Limits and closest literature

The result is limited to a deterministic well-mixed replicator with exogenous periodic composition; it does not assert a temporal-hypergraph limit, empirical applicability, global absence of other periodic states, or finite-population stochastic selection. The closest relevant literature is Llabrés et al. (2026), the fixed-mix stochastic closure (2026), the periodically mixed Prisoner's Dilemma (2026), and Zhu–Kuske–Erneux (2014). General forced-bifurcation methods have prior art; remaining unindexed specialist literature may affect the priority of the source-specific classification.
