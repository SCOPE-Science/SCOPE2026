In an independently site-percolated random recursive tree with fixed retention probability \(p\in(0,1)\), the earliest retained singleton appears on the birth-label scale \(n^{p/(1+p)}\) and has an explicit Weibull limit. All early singleton birth labels form a Poisson point process with intensity \(p(1-p)x^p\,dx\), despite the linear number of singletons globally.
