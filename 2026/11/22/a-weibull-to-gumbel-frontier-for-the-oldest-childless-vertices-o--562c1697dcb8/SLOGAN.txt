In uniform recursive \(k\)-parent DAGs, the oldest surviving childless vertices form a Poisson birth-age process with intensity \(x^k\,dx\) at fixed \(k\ge2\). When the number of parents rises to \(k\sim c\log n\), the oldest vertex crosses to the macroscopic birth fraction \(e^{-1/c}\) and its centered age obeys a reversed-Gumbel law; the boundary is sharp.
