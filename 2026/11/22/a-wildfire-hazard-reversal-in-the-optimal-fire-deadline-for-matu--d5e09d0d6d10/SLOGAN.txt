In an idealized serotinous canopy seed bank, a positive maturity delay and positive seed-viability decay yield one and only one optimal prescribed-fire deadline even when spontaneous fires release the same seed stock. Counterintuitively, that optimal deadline increases strictly with the wildfire hazard, and it disappears to infinity as the seed-decay rate tends to zero; this is an expected seed-release-rate result, not a population-persistence or fire-management prescription.
