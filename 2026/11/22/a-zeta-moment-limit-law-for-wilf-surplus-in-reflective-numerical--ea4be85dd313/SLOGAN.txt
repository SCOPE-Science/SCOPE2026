For a uniformly chosen reflective numerical semigroup of genus \(g\), its Wilf surplus divided by \(g^2\) converges to \(U^2\lfloor1/U\rfloor\), where \(U\) is uniform on \([0,1]\). This atomless full-support limit has exact moments given by finite alternating combinations of zeta values; its mean is \(\zeta(3)/3\), with a quantitative discrepancy bound \(O((1+\tau(g))/g)\) for Lipschitz observables.
