# Reproducible checks and limits

Run `python3 verify.py` from the same directory as this document. The standard-library program independently enumerates the finite semigroups generated by the source classification and recalculates gaps, minimal generators and Wilf numbers. It verifies all 4,116 admissible genus/multiplicity pairs for genera 1 through 94; it also compares three truncated zeta-moment integrals and large-genus mean samples through 15,001.

Expected output consists of three `PASS` lines: construction checks, truncated moment identities, and genus-count/mean checks. The infinite weak convergence, CDF, and moment identities require the analytic proof in `RESULT.md`; finite tests alone do not prove any statement for all genera. The source classification is attributed and used as a premise. No independent external audit has been performed.
