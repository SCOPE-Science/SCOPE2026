Among primitive axes of a finite spin-factor Jordan algebra, exactly \(S(T^2+2S-2T)\) ordered triples satisfy one prescribed associator equation, where \(S\) and \(T\) are explicit norm-one counts in the full quadratic space and a perpendicular hyperplane. Precisely \(ST(T-2)\) of these triples have nonassociative generated subalgebras: this accidental-associativity phenomenon appears in every dimension at least four, but disappears in dimension two and in one three-dimensional \(\mathbb F_3\) Witt class.
