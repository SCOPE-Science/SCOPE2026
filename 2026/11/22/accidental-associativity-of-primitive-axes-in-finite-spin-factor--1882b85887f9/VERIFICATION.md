# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The idempotent equations give only 0 and 1 with zero vector part and otherwise (1/2,u/2) with Q(u)=1. The Peirce 1-eigenvector equation forces the vector part x of (a,x) to equal a u, giving primitivity. Expanding the two Jordan products cancels the scalar part and gives the vector associator (B(u,v)w−B(v,w)u)/8. With v fixed, all 2S dependent outer pairs (w=±u) qualify; independent pairs qualify exactly when both outer vectors lie in v⊥. This hyperplane is nondegenerate with the same determinant square class as V since Q(v)=1; its T² ordered norm-one pairs include exactly 2T dependent pairs. Thus the total and accidental counts are S(2S+T²−2T) and ST(T−2), and division by S³ gives the uniform ordered-triple probability. In the diagonal Gauss-sum calculation the t=0 contribution is q^(n−1), and the nonzero t factors have sums −1 in even dimension and χ(−1)G in odd dimension. Using G²=χ(−1)q gives the stated S,T for both determinant classes and S>0. The accidental axes are distinct as the middle vector is orthogonal to both outers. Without any unital-generation assumption, (e_u,e_u,e_w) has nonzero vector associator (w−B(u,w)u)/8; alternatively, e_u+e_v−2(e_u∘e_v)=1/2 for these triples, so their generated subalgebra actually contains 1. In d=2 the hyperplane line gives T=0 or 2; in d=3, T=q−χ(−D) is two only in the stated case; higher-dimensional formulas give T>2 (already T≥q²−q for d=4). Independent enumeration using the two bracketings of the product over F_3, d=2,3,4 and both determinant classes confirmed both strata and that every independent-outer witness fails the repeated-axis equation. The general result follows from the calculation, not the enumerations.
