For simplex-sum cyclohedra, every Euclidean ridge is strictly acute or obtuse: exactly \((n-3)/(3(n+1))\) are acute, and each reflected obtuse angle has strictly more than twice its acute multiplicity. A uniformly selected ridge has a nondegenerate four-spacing Dirichlet limit with identical conditional absolute normal-cosine laws on the acute and obtuse sides but limiting weights \(1/3\) and \(2/3\).
