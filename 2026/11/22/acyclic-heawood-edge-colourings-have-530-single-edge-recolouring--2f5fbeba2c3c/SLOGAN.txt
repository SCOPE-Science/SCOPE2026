The \(119784\) acyclic four-edge-colourings of the Heawood graph, modulo global palette renaming, break into exactly \(530\) single-edge-recolouring components: \(392\) isolated states, \(112\) components of order \(14\), \(24\) of order \(42\), and two large components of orders \(40040\) and \(76776\). Even an optimal four-colour assignment can be frozen despite the existence of more than two million named optimal colourings.
