For two noisy binary threshold questions locating a uniform target under squared loss, fixing the first question at the midpoint gives a unique feedback-optimal second threshold strictly closer to the midpoint than the posterior-median query. As the binary-channel contrast \(e\) tends to zero, this explicit adaptive policy improves over **every** two-question nonadaptive threshold pair by \(e^4/32+o(e^4)\) in Bayes mean-square error.
