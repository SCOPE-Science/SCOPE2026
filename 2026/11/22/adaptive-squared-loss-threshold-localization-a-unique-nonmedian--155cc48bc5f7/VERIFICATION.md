# Reproducibility and verification

Run `python verify.py` using Python 3.10 or newer. The checker uses only the standard library and reconstructs posterior response probabilities and first moments by independently integrating the piecewise-constant densities, rather than trusting the closed risk formula. The saved `verification_output.txt` was generated by executing this checker on the supplied source. The global optimizer and asymptotic comparison are **analytic claims**: the finite grid does not by itself certify uniqueness or rule out an untested nonadaptive pair. Their proofs are in `RESULT.md`, including the Bernstein-sign argument and a uniform asymptotic expansion over the entire nonadaptive threshold square.
