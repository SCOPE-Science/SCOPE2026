For a Brownian bridge pinned above a barrier, the best first checkpoint in a two-query adaptive detection scheme is never the midpoint—even when both endpoints are equal. The exact one-sided improvement slope is \(\Gamma(3/4)\sqrt c\,e^{-2c^2}/(2^{1/4}\pi)>0\), where \(c\) is the normalized endpoint clearance, and at least two mirrored off-center global optima exist.
