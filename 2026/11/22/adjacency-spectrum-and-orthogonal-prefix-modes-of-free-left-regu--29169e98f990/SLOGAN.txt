The complete adjacency representation of the commuting graph of the free left regular band on \(n\) generators splits orthogonally into one factorial-branching quotient \(H_n\) and exactly \(k n!/(k+1)!\) copies of \(H_k\) for every \(1\le k<n\), yielding an explicit characteristic-polynomial factorization for all \(n\). In particular, eigenvalue \(-1\) has multiplicity exactly \(n!\), and the conventional noncentral commuting graph is \(n\) disjoint copies of the preceding full graph.
