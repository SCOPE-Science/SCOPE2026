In the type-A 0-Hecke monoid, the number of elements commuting with two adjacent standard generators has an exact all-dimensional formula: a three-point parabolic double-coset count plus a convex quadratic boundary correction. The resulting common centralizers have exactly \(\lfloor(n-3)/2\rfloor+1\) positional sizes, yet each occupies asymptotically \(1/36\) of the monoid, in striking contrast with the symmetric-group centralizer of order \((n-3)!\).
