# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim is for every finite affine plane of order at least two, with no Desarguesian assumption, and every finite field of characteristic two. Universal point-pair and parallel-class incidence identities give the adjacency Jordan types. The even-order case has nilpotent adjacency index three and explicitly rank-determined Jordan multiplicities. The odd-order case has one size-three zero Jordan block, singleton zero blocks, and a full complementary family of size-two unit blocks. The second-order transfer yields the exact primary blocks, the fixed-space exponents, and sharp global orders. Every displayed dimension and period is forced by the proof, not inferred from a finite sample. Two packaged programs checked \(s=2,3,4,5\) by different rank computations, and exact Möbius populations for those cases are nonnegative, integral orbit counts summing to the full state spaces.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to residual historical priority.** The standard reversible two-level update is not claimed new; Vlasov's first-submitted 2013 treatment explicitly constructs it. Published projective-plane incidence-wave results classify a different square-incidence graph, and their period-six/period-twelve formulas do not imply the affine-period-eight/period-twenty-four law. Published complete-multipartite characteristic-two wave results do include period 24 and a general block-transfer method, but apply to complete multipartite adjacency, not the affine Levi geometry. The new modular adjacency Jordan identities and rank-sensitive affine orbit census remain necessary. Published affine incidence-design work addresses block structure rather than these dynamics. Exact claim searches, alternative descriptions and broader-cover comparisons did not find a covering statement; absence of a database hit is not treated as proof of novelty.

## Value
**PASS.** Passing from projective to affine point-line incidence is a canonical finite-geometric operation, not an arbitrary graph perturbation. The fact that it changes the characteristic-two temporal clock from \(12\) to \(8\) at even order and from \(6\) to \(24\) at odd order is a qualitative two-sided resonance reversal. The general theorem covers all affine planes, including non-Desarguesian instances, and gives a full temporal distribution with an incidence-code-rank recovery principle. That is a reusable structural description rather than a recomputation of one small example.

## Closest literature and limits
Vlasov (arXiv:1311.4297, Section 3) supplies the general reversible update construction. Clark–Jungnickel–Tonchev (DOI 10.1002/jcd.20256) treat affine incidence designs. The published projective-plane incidence-wave classification and complete-multipartite wave census are the closest dynamics comparisons; neither determines the affine adjacency Jordan multiplicities proved here. The binary rank of every possible non-Desarguesian affine plane is not classified by this theorem; the exact fixed-space formula legitimately retains it as an input. Only characteristic-two fields and the specified two-level map are covered. Independent external mathematical validation is not yet available; unindexed equivalents could exist.
