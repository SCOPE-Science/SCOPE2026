For every odd prime \(p\) and any number of distinct prime radicands, the corresponding radical-sum polynomial is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\), yet every reduction has irreducible factors of degree at most \(p\). Its complete good-prime factorization law and exact Chebotarev densities are governed solely by \(q\bmod p\) and simultaneous \(p\)-th-power residuosity.
