# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The factor identity and inverse generating series derive all coefficient words algebraically. Full-period insertion then proves the affine law at every quotient \(q\). Separately, the exhaustive fixed 24-case integer computation verifies that each word is nonzero and proves the explicit second-gap and frequency table. Quotient long division provides an independent finite consistency test; no finite sample is claimed to prove the infinite shift statement.

## Originality

PASS, subject to priority uncertainty. The main 2019 theorem supplies the maximum gap but not its entire frequency spectrum. The explicit ternary manuscript specializes the smallest prime to 3 and cannot be applied to the family with smallest prime 5. The later binary second-gap study has a different object. OEIS lists the known coefficients of the base polynomial. An unindexed equivalent treatment of 35p support-spacing histograms remains a genuine bibliographic risk.

## Value

PASS. The complete distribution of coefficient-support spacings is a natural invariant refining the extensively studied maximum gap. The formula computes every histogram from 576 fixed integer entries and yields the new residue-sensitive second-gap threshold for an infinite ternary cyclotomic family, beyond isolated numeric cases.
