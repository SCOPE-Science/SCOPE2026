In characteristic three, every Nijenhuis operator on a quadratic-irreducible three-dimensional anti-pre-Lie extension has a scalar-on-the-field normal form: there are \(q^5\) operators but only \(q^3+q\) full-automorphism orbits, with exact rank distributions. Its derived-ideal-preserving operator subscheme has three Frobenius-permuted affine five-dimensional geometric components and an explicit zeta function; the geometric statement does not claim to cover non-preserving solutions after scalar extension.
