For uniformly randomly labelled \(K_{n,n}\) with any \(m\)-edge matching deleted, the entire local-maximum count law is exactly the convex mixture, with weights \(1-m/n\) and \(m/n\), of the undeleted and full-crown laws. Its distance from the geometric law of mass \(2^{-k}\) has a sharp \(1/n\) coefficient minimized at the deletion density \(m/n=1/6\), with minimum \(17/48\).
