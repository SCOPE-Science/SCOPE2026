For every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge2\), the only minimum geodetic sets in its incidence graph are the \(q+1\) parallel classes of \(q\) lines; their exchange graph is frozen until all \(q\) lines can be replaced simultaneously. When \(q\ge3\), no inclusion-minimal geodetic set has size \(q+1\), whereas the affine plane of order two has exactly six exceptional three-vertex minimal geodetic sets.
