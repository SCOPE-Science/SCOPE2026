For a uniform sample, the unnormalized Gini mean difference has an *exactly affine* conditional expectation given any one order statistic; nonlinear functions of that observed quantile cannot improve mean-square prediction. The best single quantile explains asymptotically \(5/16\) of Gini variance, and exactly four optimal ranks tie at precisely the Pell-indexed sample sizes \(9,57,337,\ldots\), versus two ranks otherwise.
