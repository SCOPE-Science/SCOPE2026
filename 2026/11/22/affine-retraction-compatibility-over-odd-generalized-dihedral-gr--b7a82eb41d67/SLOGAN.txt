For every finite odd-order abelian group \(A\), compatible retractions of its generalized dihedral extension have an exact all-arity count obtained by weighting Boolean decompositions of \(A\) by their common fixed-point indices. In the mixed-exponent family \(C_{p^a}\oplus C_{p^b}\) with \(a<b\), the resulting counts detect \(b\), even though the corresponding commuting-idempotent counts of the additive group cannot.
