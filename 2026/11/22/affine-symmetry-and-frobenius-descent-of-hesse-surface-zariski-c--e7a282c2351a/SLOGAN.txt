The Hesse rational elliptic surface's \(50\,165\) geometric Zariski chambers form exactly \(239\) orbits under the full \(432\)-element affine incidence group and \(299\) under its geometric Hessian subgroup, with eleven exact fixed-class profiles. For the standard pencil over \(\mathbb F_q\) in characteristic at least five, \(50\,165\) chambers descend if \(q\equiv1\pmod3\), but only \(1\,037\) if \(q\equiv2\pmod3\).
