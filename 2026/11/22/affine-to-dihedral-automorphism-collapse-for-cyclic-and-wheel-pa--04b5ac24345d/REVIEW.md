# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

All abstract monoid automorphisms permute the atoms of the complete Boolean semilattice of partial identities. Singleton restrictions of any partial bijection reconstruct its entire graph after relabeling, proving spatiality without an unproved normalizer assumption. Rank-two partial isometries detect precisely equality of pair distances. Connectedness of a permuted distance-one cycle forces a unit step and hence an affine vertex permutation. A universal wheel hub prevents swapping the only two positive distance classes by a degree obstruction and restricts the group to dihedral rim symmetries. The proof covers every cycle size at least three and every wheel rim size at least five. Exhaustive independent permutation checks corroborate the claimed normalizers on cycles of sizes three through eight and wheels with five through seven rim vertices.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence

The two primary semigroup studies of cycle and wheel partial isometries give presentations, ranks and Green relations, not the two abstract automorphism groups. A known characterization of partial graph-automorphism inverse monoids (Jajcay et al., arXiv:1809.04422) uses rank-two data; that broad reconstruction ingredient is credited and is not claimed as novel. The 2011 paper on automorphisms of partial endomorphism semigroups gives a general method and examples for other transformation-semigroup families; its full text was not accessible, so priority uncertainty is explicit. Searches of related published mathematical claims, including cycle and wheel inverse-monoid results, found no theorem whose statement implies both the full affine automorphism group and the strict wheel reduction. Matching the group of units or the ordinary dihedral automorphisms of either graph is not equivalent to an abstract automorphism classification of the whole monoid.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The distinction is structural, not a count of a contrived finite slice. Monoid automorphisms can relabel positive metric distances; this hidden freedom yields a holomorph of order \(n\varphi(n)\), generally larger than the graph's dihedral isometry group. A single natural universal-vertex extension removes that freedom. The sharp pair of all-parameter classifications separates the intrinsic symmetry of two natural inverse-monoid families from their unit-group symmetry. It also gives a simple mechanism for future comparisons of metric inverse monoids.

## Closest sources and remaining limitations

Fernandes–Paulista (2022; arXiv:2205.02196) and Fernandes (2023; arXiv:2307.16257) supply the motivating inverse-monoid definitions and structural context. Jajcay et al. (2018; arXiv:1809.04422) gives a substantially related inverse-monoid reconstruction framework. Araújo et al. (2011; DOI 10.5486/PMD.2011.4703) may offer a more general machinery for partial-transformation semigroup automorphisms; only its published abstract was accessible. Unindexed equivalent formulations under color-automorphism groups or normalizers remain a historical-priority risk. Small checks do not independently certify infinite-parametric statements.
