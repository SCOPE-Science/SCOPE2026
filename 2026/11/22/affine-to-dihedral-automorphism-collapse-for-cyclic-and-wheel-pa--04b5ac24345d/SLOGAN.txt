The abstract automorphism group of the partial-isometry monoid of a finite cycle is the full affine group of its cyclic vertex labels, of order \(n\varphi(n)\), although its units are only dihedral. Adjoining a universal hub to make a wheel reduces the monoid automorphism group to the ordinary dihedral group of order \(2n\) for every rim size \(n\ge5\).
