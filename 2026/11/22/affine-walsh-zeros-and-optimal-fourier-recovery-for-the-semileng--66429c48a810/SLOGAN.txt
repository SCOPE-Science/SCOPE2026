For the 42 bases of the semilength-five Catalan matroid, the Boolean Walsh transform vanishes on exactly one affine codimension-three coset, with 128 zeros and 896 surviving modes. The recoverable Fourier range has an eight-translation projection whose minimum possible \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) norms are exactly \(7/4\), and this is the first singular case among odd Catalan semilengths.
