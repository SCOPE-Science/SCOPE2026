In a uniformly grown recursive tree with Bernoulli-retained edges, learning that birth-labelled vertex \(j\) belongs to the root cluster multiplies the success probability of **every** later vertex by the same exact factor \(R_j(p)\), independent of how far in the future the vertex arrives. This permanent factor increases strictly with the earlier birth label and tends to \(2\Gamma(1+p)^2/\Gamma(1+2p)\) at fixed \(0<p<1\).
