Along the unbounded invariant axis of a quadratic hidden-attractor flow, the transverse derivative has one Airy-decaying direction while every other transverse direction grows like \(\exp((2/3)\sqrt{ab}\,t^{3/2}+O(t))\), despite constant negative divergence. A small linearized perturbation reaches unit size after a sharply determined three-term \((\log(1/\varepsilon))^{2/3}\)-scale delay; this is not a claim about nonlinear escape.
