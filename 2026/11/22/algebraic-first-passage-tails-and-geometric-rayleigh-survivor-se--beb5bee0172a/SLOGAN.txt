A Brownian target search with independently renewed Pareto resetting has an exact algebraic survival tail \(\mathbb P(T>t)\sim[a\tau_0^\beta/(p\sqrt{\pi D})]t^{-\beta-1/2}\) for every finite \(\beta>0\), even when the mean resetting interval is finite. Conditional on very long survival, the number of completed resets approaches a geometric law independently of a Rayleigh-scaled position, while the last reset occurs at a negligible fraction of the elapsed time.
