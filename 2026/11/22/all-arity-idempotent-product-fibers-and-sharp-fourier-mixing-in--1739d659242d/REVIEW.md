# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof uses a bijective reparameterization of every fixed-endpoint idempotent-product fiber into independent pairs in the finite abelian group, then exact finite-group Fourier inversion. Group-coordinate normalization and both endpoint constraints are explicit. Ramanujan grouping is an identity, not numerical interpolation. The prime-order extremal-character argument proves the claimed sharp total-variation rate. Direct multiplication checks in `verify.py` agree in six nontrivial cyclic and noncyclic examples. There is no infinite-limit inference from finite computations.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

Graham–Houghton path labels and Gray's generator-rank work establish support and generation structure, not the pointwise number of length-\(k\) representations. The cross-connection paper establishes the categorical Rees sandwich construction, not product-fiber spectra. Budzban–Högnäs treats qualitative probability-measure convergence on completely simple semigroups, not the arithmetic, finite-length Fourier fibers in this perfect-pairing family. Retrieved published work on random Rees semigroup generation and all-arity products of rank-one matrix idempotents has different statements and objects. These comparisons do not prove absolute priority. Relevant unpublished or poorly indexed character-table Rees random-walk computations remain a real risk, and certain published database records were available only as summaries.

## Value — PASS

The objects form a canonical, nontrivial family of completely simple semigroups with a character-table sandwich matrix. The formula simultaneously resolves all target multiplicities for all lengths, proves the sharp idempotent width, and gives a sharp stochastic mixing rate whose exponent detects maximal-order primary-group summands. It refines qualitative generation and convergence to exact finite-length arithmetic invariants for every finite abelian group; no arbitrary finite slice or mere renaming is involved.

No arbitrary sandwich matrices, nonabelian maximal subgroups, or uniform limit over growing base groups. The spectral exponent is established for fixed finite \(A\). No independent mathematical validation is claimed.
