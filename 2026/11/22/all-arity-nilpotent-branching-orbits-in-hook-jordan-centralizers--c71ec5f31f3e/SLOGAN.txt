All simultaneous conjugacy classes of pairwise-commuting nilpotent \(r\)-tuples in the centralizer of \(J_m(0)\oplus J_1(0)\) are controlled by a projective direction, a scalar product or axis type, and an affine quotient. For every finite field and \(m,r\ge2\), their exact count is \(q^{r(m-2)}(q^r+(q+1)q^{r-1}(q^r-1)/(q-1))\), with only orbit sizes \(1\) and \(q(q-1)\).
