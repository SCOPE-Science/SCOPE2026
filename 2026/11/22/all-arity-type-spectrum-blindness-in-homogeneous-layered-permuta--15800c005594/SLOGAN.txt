For the homogeneous class of layered permutations, the complete injective tuple-type generating series over an individually named base of size \(n\) with \(k\) decreasing layers is \((1-z)^{1-n}/(1-2z)^{k+1}\), independent of the individual layer sizes. Two type counts recover \((n,k)\) even without the base size; distinct bases with identical all-arity numerical spectra first occur at three parameters.
