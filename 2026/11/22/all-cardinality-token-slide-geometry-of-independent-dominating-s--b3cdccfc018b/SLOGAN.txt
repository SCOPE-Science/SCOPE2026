For every labelled open ladder \(P_n\square K_2\) and every feasible cardinality \(k\), the token-slide graph of independent dominating sets is exactly two copies of the exclusion-token graph on a path, \(T_{n-k}(P_{k-1})\). Each component is median, with an exact particle-displacement distance formula and diameter \((n-k)(2k-n-1)\); the unweighted Fibonacci count of these sets was previously known.
