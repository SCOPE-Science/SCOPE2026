Every prescribed product of any length \(k\ge2\) of nonzero rank-one square-zero matrices over a finite field has an exact fiber determined only by target rank and trace vanishing. Put \(P=(q^n-1)/(q-1)\) and \(H=(q^{n-1}-1)/(q-1)\) for matrix size \(n\) over \(\mathbb F_q\). Conditional on a nonzero product, total-variation distance to uniform on rank-one matrices is exactly \((P-H)/(P(P-1)^{k-1})\), while the two trace strata alternate in size with word-length parity.
