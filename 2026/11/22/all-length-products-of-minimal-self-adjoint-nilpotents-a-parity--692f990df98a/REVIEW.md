# Scientific review: polar-space product-fiber law

**Correctness: PASS.** Every nonzero rank-one self-adjoint nilpotent equals a unique nonzero scalar multiple of \(u_LB(u_L,\cdot)\) on an isotropic line. An explicit hyperbolic-pair count yields the polar nonorthogonality degree \(K=q^{2r-1}\) and the common-neighbor identity \(A^2=q^{2r-2}I+(q-1)q^{2r-2}J\). The distinct adjacent and nonadjacent cases are counted separately; in the adjacent case the nonconstant quadratic-character terms sum to zero. Exact powers of this matrix count allowable consecutive lines, and scalar normalization yields the prescribed matrix-target fibers for all lengths. The target partition is exhaustive; the zero fiber is the complementary count. Direct literal dense multiplication of all \(80^2\) pairs and \(80^3\) triples over \(\mathbb F_3\), alongside graph checks for four field/dimension configurations, independently agrees. These finite checks corroborate but do not substitute for the general proof.

**Originality: PASS, with historical-priority caveat.** Brouwer–Gow–Sheekey (2014), doi:10.37236/3810, counts single symmetric/self-adjoint nilpotent matrices and does not state positive-length prescribed products. The finite-polar-graph strong-regularity used in the key adjacency identity is classical; Ihringer (2016), arXiv:1606.05898, provides a relevant geometric account and the known input is credited explicitly. Closest published exact multiplication fibers concern the full minimal nilpotent similarity orbit with two unrestricted factors; those aggregate fibers do not imply multiplicities for the restricted self-adjoint subset at every length. A published all-length formula for rank-one idempotent factors treats a different orbit. Other records quantify annihilating self-adjoint pairs or higher mutually annihilating tuples, rather than positive nonzero products, and the additive Cayley spectral result concerns sums, not products. Searches of the published-finding corpus record, own available prior mathematical claims, and published primary literature did not identify equivalent all-length formulas or stronger coverage. The historical search is incomplete and cannot prove absolute global priority.

**Value: PASS.** Self-adjoint rank-one nilpotents are the minimal nontrivial nilpotent operators compatible with a fixed orthogonal form. Their products are controlled by incidence in a classical polar space, not by general nilpotent-orbit counts. The theorem is a complete natural prescribed word-fiber invariant for every odd finite field and an unbounded family of dimensions and word lengths; it reveals an unexpected parity swap between equality and orthogonality of endpoint lines, a sharp length-three reachability cutoff, and an exact conditional total-variation rate rather than a qualitative mixing statement.

**Closest literature and limits.** The strong regularity and elementary quadratic-space point counts are not claimed novel. Rank-one factors, odd characteristic and odd ambient dimension at least five are essential stated hypotheses. The independent scientific audit and global historical-priority verification are outstanding. The source literature was inspected to the relevant result and proof sections; unlocated specialized word-map literature remains a residual risk.
