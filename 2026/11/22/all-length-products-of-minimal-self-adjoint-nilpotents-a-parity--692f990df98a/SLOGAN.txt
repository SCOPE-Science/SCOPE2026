For nonzero rank-one self-adjoint square-zero operators in any odd-dimensional nonsingular orthogonal space of dimension at least five over an odd finite field, all prescribed product fibers at every length have an exact two-state, parity-switching formula: odd lengths distinguish equal image/coimage lines, while even lengths distinguish orthogonal from nonorthogonal endpoints. Every nonzero rank-one target with both endpoint lines isotropic is attained in three factors, and the conditioned product distribution has an exact finite-length total-variation law.
