Every nonsquare rectangular king grid \(P_a\boxtimes P_b\), with \(2\le a<b\), has exactly one maximum cut up to exchanging its two sides: alternating uniform strips along the longer direction. Square grids have exactly two optimal cuts for side length at least three, and the \(2\times2\) grid has three; the maximum-cut *value* was known previously, but its entire optimizer set is classified here.
