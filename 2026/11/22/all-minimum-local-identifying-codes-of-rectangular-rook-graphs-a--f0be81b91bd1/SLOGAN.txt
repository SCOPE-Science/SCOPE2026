Every minimum local closed-neighborhood identifying code of the rook graph \(K_s\square K_l\), for \(3\le s\le l\), is a bijective placement of \(s\) singleton columns plus exactly two selected cells in each other column; there are precisely \(l!/(l-s)!\binom{s}{2}^{l-s}\) such codes. A closed formula also counts those that distinguish all nonadjacent pairs, with the sharp transition \(l\ge s+\lceil(s-1)/2\rceil\).
