Every binary word \(0^a1\,0^b1\,0^c\) with positive zero gaps has exactly two-position minimum string attractors, whose entire set of position pairs admits a sharp five-case formula. The number reaches \(2(a+b+c)\) if and only if all three gaps agree; for uniformly chosen positive gaps at a fixed length, the expected number of optimum pairs grows as \((10/27)n+o(n)\).
