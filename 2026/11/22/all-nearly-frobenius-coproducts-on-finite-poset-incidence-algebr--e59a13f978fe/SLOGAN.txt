Every nearly Frobenius coproduct of a finite-poset incidence algebra is a scalar combination of canonical extremal-element coproducts, one per bounded comparability component; over \(\mathbb F_q\), their complete linear-rank polynomial is \(\prod_C(1+(q-1)T^{d_C})\). A diamond poset has an injective coproduct despite a non-type-A branching quiver with only a binomial relation, delimiting the literal scope of a classical quiver obstruction.
