Every exact directed-distance identifying probe family on a Kautz shift network omits at most one legal completion of each prefix, and these are the only restrictions. The full cardinality polynomial is \(\bigl((q-1)z^{q-2}+z^{q-1}\bigr)^{q(q-1)^{n-2}}\), yielding \((q-1)^{q(q-1)^{n-2}}\) optimal labeled probe families.
