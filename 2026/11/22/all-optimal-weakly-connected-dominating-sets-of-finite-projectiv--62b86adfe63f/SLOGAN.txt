For the incidence graph of any finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\), exactly \(2(q^2+q+1)(q+1)(q^2+1)\) minimum weakly connected dominating sets exist, all obtained from one omitted line of a pencil and one extra covered point (or its point--line dual). Their minimum size is \(2q+1\), and the choice of that extra point produces an exact spanning-tree versus \((q-1)\)-cycle-rank dichotomy.
