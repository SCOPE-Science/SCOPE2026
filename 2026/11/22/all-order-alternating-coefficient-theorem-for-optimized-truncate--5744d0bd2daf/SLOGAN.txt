For Ferrari's optimally A-stable truncated-trapezoidal symbol at **every** integer order \(J\ge2\), all ordinary polynomial coefficients alternate strictly in sign, and its complex modulus on **every** closed disk has a unique maximizer at the negative real boundary point. This proves the published all-order symbol-norm conjecture, with exact unit-disk norm \(4/[(J+1)\sin^2(\pi/[2(J+1)])]\), and sharpens it to arbitrary contour radii.
