On every finite cactus random-cluster graph, distinct bond-occupation cumulants have an exact all-order Eulerian-polynomial formula and vanish unless all observed edges lie on one cycle. For \(q>1\), uniformly increasing the bond activities produces a universal staircase of up to \(r-2\) strict sign reversals in an \(r\)-edge cumulant, while every same-cycle higher cumulant is negative when \(0<q<1\).
