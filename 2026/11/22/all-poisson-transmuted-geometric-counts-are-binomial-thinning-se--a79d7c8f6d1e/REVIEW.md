# Review: thinning self-decomposability of Poisson–transmuted geometric counts

**Correctness — PASS.** The PGF follows directly from the original mixed-geometric convolution and factors into one linear zero and two geometric poles. On the original parameter range the intermediate zero parameter lies strictly between \(q^2\) and \(q\), so every compound-Poisson coefficient is positive and summable. The nonstandard all-index lemma is proved with the exact unique maximizer of \(x^j(1-x)\), including both endpoint and straddling regimes; its strict inequality implies the complete decreasing canonical sequence, and the standard thinning self-decomposability criterion applies. Independently checked exact rational formulas and a disjoint-rate counterexample support, but do not replace, the proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The original 2023 PoiTG preprint defines the same convolution and PGF and discusses generating functions, dispersion, graphical unimodality and estimation, without asserting thinning self-decomposability or this explicit canonical monotonicity. The 2016 transmuted geometric paper (Section 3.2) already proves ordinary infinite divisibility of the parent for positive transmutation; Poisson convolution preserves that known property. Neither its Section 3.2 nor its PGF theorem proves the stronger decreasing canonical sequence required for discrete self-decomposability. Pakes (2022) supplies the general criterion, not the special all-index proof; arbitrary two-geometric mixtures can fail the criterion (a rational example appears in the result). Searches for compound-Poisson representations, thinning self-decomposability, canonical sequences, and PoiTG synonyms revealed no direct covering statement. This is best-knowledge originality, with normal risk from unindexed literature and unindexed work or unexpected general results subsuming the special canonical inequality.

**Value — PASS.** Thinning self-decomposability is a structural existence theorem for stationary count autoregressions and aggregation, beyond ordinary infinite divisibility and beyond the source paper's plotted unimodality. It is established uniformly over the entire published three-parameter domain, not at selected values. The rational two-geometric counterexample rules out dismissal as a generic closure fact about mixtures. This supports the specific mathematical structure and avoids unsupported claims about estimator quality.

The theorem does not address negative transmutation, other geometric rate ratios, or self-decomposability under real multiplication. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
