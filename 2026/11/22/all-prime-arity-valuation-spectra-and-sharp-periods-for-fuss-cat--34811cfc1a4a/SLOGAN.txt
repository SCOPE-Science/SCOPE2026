For every fixed size \(n\) and prime \(p\), varying the arity of Fuss–Catalan numbers produces an explicit two-state digit-carry valuation law, with exact rational probabilities at every valuation and a geometric tail. The valuations capped at any level \(k\) have the sharp least arity period \(p^{L+k-1}\), where \(L\) is the digit length of a canonical reduced column determined by \(n\) and \(p\).
