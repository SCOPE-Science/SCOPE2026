For the ternary Fuss–Catalan numbers \(F_n=(2n+1)^{-1}\binom{3n}{n}\), strict prime-adic valuation peaks occur only at the five primes \(2,3,5,11,13\), in explicit single congruence classes, while strict valleys occur only at \(3\). Every peak's two exact drops, their joint natural-density distribution, and the unique valley's flank law are classified for all indices.
