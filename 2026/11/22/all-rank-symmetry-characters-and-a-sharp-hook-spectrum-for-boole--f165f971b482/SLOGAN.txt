For every rank span of the proper Boolean lattice, the full rational coordinate-permutation homology character is an explicit polynomial determined by permutation cycle lengths. At each positive level \(k\), precisely two hook leg lengths, \(k\) and \(k+1\), can occur, with multiplicities \(n-k-2\) and \(n-k-1\); this gives new all-rank equivariant information beyond the previously known rank-two character and nonequivariant barcode.
