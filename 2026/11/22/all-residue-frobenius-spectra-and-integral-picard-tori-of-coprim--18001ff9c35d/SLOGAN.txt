For every pair of coprime Chebyshev degrees and every good odd finite field, a finite signed orbit calculation modulo \(2mn\) determines the exact integral Picard-torus isomorphism type and the complete curve zeta function. Unlike the previously known signed-congruence cases, the general formula permits mixed splitting and nodes with nontrivial Frobenius degrees, as in the \((3,8)\) curve over \(\mathbb F_7\).
