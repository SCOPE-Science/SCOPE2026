For every complete bipartite graph and arbitrary prescribed terminals, all edge sets that strictly increase every terminal-pair distance have an explicit four-block, real-rooted cardinality polynomial, and all inclusion-minimal solutions have the same size. The universal bound \(|F|\ge |T|-1\) is attained in a non-star biclique exactly when the terminals consist of one vertex on each shore; every terminal placement attains it on a star.
