For the complete-residue radix digit family \(\{0,1,\ldots,b-2,b^k-1\}\), with \(b\ge3\) and \(k\ge2\), every shortest zero-synchronizing carry word is classified by an explicit collection of disjoint integer intervals, and the sharp length is \(2k-1\). Their exact number is \((b-2)^{k-1}((b-1)b^{k-1}-(b^k-1)/(b-1))\); this sharpens the previously known ternary lower bound to an exact count and extends the classification to all higher bases.
