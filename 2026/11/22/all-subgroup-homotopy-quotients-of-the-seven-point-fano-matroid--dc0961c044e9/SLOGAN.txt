For the seven-point Fano matroid independence complex, every one of the 179 subgroups of \(\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\) has an explicitly classified integral homotopy quotient: only the eight Singer cyclic groups have a nonsimply-connected quotient \(M(\mathbb Z/7,1)\vee S^2\); all others produce wedges of two-spheres, sometimes contractible. The classification is established by fixed-point normal generation, exact orbit Euler counts, and four explicit Singer attaching words.
