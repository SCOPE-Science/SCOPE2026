The exact one-sweep count and its asymptotic probability were not implied by the closest checked statements; the closely related 2024 general paper was read in its open abstract and introduction, not exhaustively throughout. The inaccessible full 2011 article and unindexed equivalents remain risks.

Independent validation and definitive historical uniqueness remain open. The claims apply only to deterministic binary open-neighborhood NOR with the specified sweep.
