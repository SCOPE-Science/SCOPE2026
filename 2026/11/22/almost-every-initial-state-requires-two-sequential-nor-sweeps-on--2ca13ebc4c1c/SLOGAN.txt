On a labelled binary cycle visited sequentially by the open-neighborhood NOR rule, exactly \(R_n\) of the \(2^n\) starting states reach a fixed configuration after one sweep, where \(R_3=8,R_4=10,R_5=26\) and \(R_n=2R_{n-2}+2R_{n-3}\). Every state is fixed after two sweeps, and the probability that two are needed tends to one at exponential rate \((1.769292354\ldots/2)^n\).
