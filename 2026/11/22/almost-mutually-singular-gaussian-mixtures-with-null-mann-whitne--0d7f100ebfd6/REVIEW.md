# Scientific review

## Correctness (C): PASS
The claim rests on four independently checkable steps: symmetric distributions give \(\theta=1/2\) and identify \(I_2\) with \(\Pr(|X|<|Y|)\); independent equal-variance Gaussian sum and difference yield exact center/outer comparison probabilities; their affine combination has the unique stated weight; and the explicit separating measurable set proves a total-variation lower bound tending to one. For each fixed positive width, the source's empirical-process CLT establishes tight centered normalized effect estimates, which precludes consistency of the stated null-calibrated Gaussian-ball rule. Supplementary direct numerical integration in `verify.py` agrees with the closed formulas. The power statement does not assert a numerical value or a joint small-width/sample-size limit.

## Originality (O): PASS, qualified
The closest source is Beck, Langthaler and Bathke, arXiv:2311.17564 (first public 2023-11-29), journal DOI 10.1111/sjos.12783; the accessible full journal text was inspected in its definitions, attainable-range theorem, Section 5 consistency discussion, examples and appendices. It explicitly notes non-omnibus behavior but does not derive the present exact Gaussian mixture parameter curve or identify total-variation distance arbitrarily close to its maximum on the joint null-effect fiber. Parkinson-Schwarz and Bathke, DOI 10.1007/s00362-021-01239-y, provides the older niche-overlap construction and reciprocal equal-median identity but does not give this sharp Gaussian-mixture regularity-and-distance result. Targeted primary/literature and mathematical-record searches found no directly dominating statement. General non-injectivity is prior art; the quantitative extremal statement and exact construction are the asserted increment. Unsearched older literature and equivalent constructions remain a residual priority risk.

## Value (V): PASS
The proposed two-dimensional rank-effect test is marketed as improving consistency relative to Mann–Whitney while preserving interpretable effect measures. A complete, analytic, everywhere-positive family with unequal variances but both effect coordinates null and asymptotically maximal total variation demonstrates how much statistical separation that fixed two-coordinate summary can miss. The bound is quantitative (over 0.9999988 at width 0.1), exact in its functional identities, and reaches the greatest possible limiting total variation; the distinction matters when choosing a test for general distribution equality. It does not disparage the validly stated non-omnibus scope of the source or apply to omnibus procedures.

## Prior literature and risks
The primary article already says the proposed test is not omnibus; the new claim does not repeat that as a novelty. Earlier folded-normal probability calculations and known equal-median overlap reciprocity supply ingredients. The specific extremal null-effect fiber, closed mixture rule and total-variation limit were not identified in the inspected material, but independent historical priority and mathematical auditing remain outstanding.
