The joint Mann–Whitney and niche-overlap effects can both equal their null values for two distinct everywhere-positive analytic Gaussian mixtures whose total variation distance is arbitrarily close to one. An explicit unique outer-mixture weight \(w_s=[\Phi(1/(\sqrt2s))-1/2]/[\Phi(1/(\sqrt2s))+\Phi(3/(\sqrt2s))-1]\) achieves exact cancellation for every \(s>0\), exposing maximal distributional separation hidden from the two-effect test. The second mixture also has strictly greater variance at every width.
