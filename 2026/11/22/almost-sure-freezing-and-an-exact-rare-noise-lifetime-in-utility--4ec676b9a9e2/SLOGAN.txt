In the finite utility-weighted bounded-confidence model with unbounded opinions and fresh Gaussian noise only on accepted interactions, every trajectory freezes almost surely in finite time; a uniform geometric tail bounds the freezing clock for any positive utility rule. For a receptive pair at half-compromise, the clock is exactly geometric with mean \(1/\operatorname{erfc}(\varepsilon/(2\sigma))\), exponentially large as the noise vanishes. This separates the exact infinite-time behavior from conditional single-cluster diffusion approximations.
