# Scientific review: alternating boundary inertia of path unconditional polytopes

## Correctness — PASS

The object is full-dimensional for every integer \(n\ge2\), and all Euclidean normalizations are explicit. The exact-rational variance formula follows by Fubini from the recursively integrated path constraints; independent coordinate sign reflections make the covariance diagonal. The compact self-adjoint integral operator has the complete mixed-boundary cosine eigenbasis, with a spectral gap of factor three. Its exact coefficients give the alternating endpoint series, sharp leading term, and the strict first-versus-second-coordinate variance ordering by elementary positive and alternating-series inequalities. A uniform two-sided spectral remainder establishes that coordinates far from the ends cannot influence the limiting extrema. The numerical value of the resulting condition number uses the exact closed forms for the first two boundary variances. An embedded standard-library program independently checks finite rational profiles through dimension 26; this is a diagnostic rather than a proof of infinite assertions. No finite-dimensional extremal-eigenspace uniqueness is asserted.

## Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority

Kohl--Olsen--Sanyal (2019), *Unconditional Reflexive Polytopes*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01469, Theorem 4.6, gives the perfect-graph polytope construction but not the stated uniform-volume covariance profile. Marchal (2012), *Generating Random Alternating Permutations in Time \(n\log n\)*, https://www.math.univ-paris13.fr/~marchal/altperm1.pdf, gives a Markov sampler and its trigonometric invariant eigenfunction for an equivalent unsigned alternating-permutation model; this is essential antecedent technique. Shonkwiler--Theis (2026), *Direct Sampling of Confined Polygons in Linear Time*, https://doi.org/10.1137/25M1747610, treats the identical unsigned chain polytope, calculates bulk one-coordinate density and first moments, but does not state the exact rational signed covariance spectra, strict alternating endpoint second-moment profile, or limiting eigenvalue ratio. The bulk marginal density is therefore *not* newly claimed. Related published work on path stable-set boundary-facet area involves codimension-one surface measure, not uniform interior measure. The substantive new statement is the all-index boundary variance profile and sharp signed-polytope Euclidean covariance conditioning. Residual priority risk: equivalent second-moment consequences of classical zigzag order-polytope formulas might exist in less searchable literature. Historical uniqueness is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Euclidean volume covariance is a natural quantitative measure of direction-dependent roundness for a canonical family of unconditional reflexive polytopes. The result separates sharp boundary anisotropy from an isotropic interior, identifies rigid limiting maximum and minimum directions, and produces an exact dimension-free spectral condition number larger than one. The exponentially alternating boundary convergence explains why the dimensional bulk can appear isotropic while the full body never asymptotically has isotropic standard-coordinate inertia. The value depends on this natural sequence and invariant, not a chosen arbitrary parameter or restatement of the known bulk marginal.
