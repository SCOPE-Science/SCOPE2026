For uniform Euclidean volume in the path-graph unconditional reflexive polytope, coordinate variances form an exactly computable diagonal spectrum with a sharply alternating endpoint boundary layer: odd distances from an end have above-bulk limiting variance and even distances below. The boundary extremizers yield the sharp asymptotic covariance condition number \((1-8/\pi^2)/(8/\pi-24/\pi^2)=1.6505132449401037\ldots\), while all deep-interior coordinate variances converge to \(1/3-2/\pi^2\).
