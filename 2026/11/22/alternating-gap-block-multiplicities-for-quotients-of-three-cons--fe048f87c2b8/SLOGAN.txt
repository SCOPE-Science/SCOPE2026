For every integer quotient modulus \(p\ge3\) and parameter \(k\ge2\), the gaps in \(\langle pk+1,pk+2,pk+3\rangle/p\) form one uniquely long block of length \(k\lceil p/3\rceil\), together with blocks of every length \(1,\ldots,k-1\) whose multiplicities alternate exactly between \(\lceil2p/3\rceil-\lceil p/3\rceil\) and its complement in \(p\). At \(p=4\), a forbidden interval of intermediate gap-block lengths appears for the first time.
