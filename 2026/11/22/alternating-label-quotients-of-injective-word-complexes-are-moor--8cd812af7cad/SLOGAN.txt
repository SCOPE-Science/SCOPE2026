For the classical injective-word cell complex on \(n\ge3\) labels, quotienting by even relabelings produces precisely \(M(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z,n-2)\) for odd \(n\), and \(M(\mathbb Z/(n/2)\mathbb Z,n-2)\vee S^{n-1}\) for even \(n\). This gives an all-width homotopy classification with unbounded torsion arising from a natural finite symmetry quotient.
