The cyclotomic recurrence \(f_{n+2}=-qf_{n+1}-q^2f_n\) with \(f_0=-(t+1)\), \(f_1=q\), \(q\ge2\), and \(t\ge1\) is binomid at every level precisely when \(t\mid q^4\). Since index divisibility requires \(t\mid q^2\), the concrete choice \((q,t)=(2,8)\) provides an all-level binomid second-order recurrence that is not divisible.
