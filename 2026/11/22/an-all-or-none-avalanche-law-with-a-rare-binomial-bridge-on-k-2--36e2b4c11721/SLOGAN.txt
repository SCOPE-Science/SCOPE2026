On the stationary Abelian sandpile of \(K_{2,n}\), with a sink in the \(n\)-vertex part, every fixed nonsink drive has a no-toppling event and a system-spanning event of equal probability \((n-1)/(2n)\); the remaining exact \(1/n\) of avalanches form a binomially concentrated bridge. The full hub-driven and leaf-driven avalanche polynomials are explicit, and the normalized toppling total converges to \(\tfrac12\delta_0+\tfrac12\delta_1\), despite their different maximum toppling counts.
