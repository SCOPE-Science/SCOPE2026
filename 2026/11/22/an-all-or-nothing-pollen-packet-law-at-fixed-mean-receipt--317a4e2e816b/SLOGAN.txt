When a flower must receive \(r\geq2\) effective pollen grains and uniform-sized packets arrive by a Poisson process with a fixed expected grain budget, the best packet size is always either one grain or exactly \(r\) grains: every intermediate size loses. A single exact budget threshold changes the winner from full-threshold clumps to individual grains, and this threshold is \(r+0.337474963764\ldots\sqrt r+o(\sqrt r)\) for large \(r\).
