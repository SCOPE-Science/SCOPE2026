The intrinsic geodesic Laplace kernel \(e^{-a\vartheta}\) is strictly positive definite on the real projective line but fails positive semidefiniteness on every higher-dimensional real projective space at every damping \(a>0\). An exact alternating \(n^{-5/2}\) Legendre-coefficient asymptotic on \(\mathbb{RP}^{2}\) proves the all-scale obstruction.
