For a hard-core gas on \(C_{3m+1}\), the connected occupation correlation of three nearly equally spaced sites flips sign as fugacity increases, while its sign at fugacity \(2\) is exactly \((-1)^{m+1}\). A distinguished crossing approaches \(2\) with an exponentially small, parity-alternating shift \(-\frac32(-1)^m2^{-m}+O(m4^{-m})\).
