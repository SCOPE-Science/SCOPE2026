In a finite-capacity hydrological store, changing the order of two unequal storms reverses total saturation runoff exactly once as interstorm drying increases, precisely when only the larger storm would initially saturate the store. The sharp integrated-drying threshold \(K_c=\log(1+B/s)\) depends on the smaller storm and the initial moisture, but not on the larger storm, the capacity within that regime, or the time profile of proportional loss.
