For scalar \(\ell_1\)-regularized Prox-NAG-GS, soft thresholding makes the optimizer locally attracting at every proximal-curvature ratio, but a sharp threshold \(k_*(a)=1+((2-a)/a)^2\) separates global boundedness below from escaping prescribed-start trajectories above. Beyond it, a unique nonzero antipodal two-cycle has amplitude \(\lambda/(\mu-hk_*(a))\) and separates an explicit invariant escape cone; the equality case is not classified.
