# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Boolean characters are a basis in characteristic five because their Gram matrix is 2^d I. Their adjacency eigenvalues are d-2k, or d-2k+(-1)^k after adding the matching, giving the stated multiplicities. The companion blocks for r=0,1,-1 have scalar powers -I at 2, -I at 3, and I at 3 respectively, with primitive eigenvalue orders 4,6,3. At r=2,-2 the nilpotent part is nonzero and square-zero, so the binomial expansion gives orders 5,10 and the stated rank-one fixed spaces when the sign is +1 but t is not a multiple of five. A vector outside the eigenline in either repeated-root block attains its order. Taking direct sums gives both the sharp lcm and fixed exponents; divisor inversion gives least periods and division by least period gives orbit counts. For d>=7 the three maximal proper divisors 30,20,12 exclude full period and give exactly the three stated codimensions. Fourier filters modulo 5 and 10 give the stated O(gamma^d) error with gamma=2 cos(pi/10).
