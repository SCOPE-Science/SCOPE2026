# Mathematical review

**Correctness: PASS.** On the stated labelled graphs over every finite field of characteristic five, orthogonal Boolean characters diagonalize adjacency with multiplicities \(\binom dk\). The five two-time-level blocks are separately calculated, including the nonsemisimple double-root cases at adjacency eigenvalues \(\pm2\). Their exact orders are \(4,6,5,10,3\), and the fixed-space dimensions yield the formula for all positive times. Direct matrix rank calculations independently agree for both families, dimensions two through five, and all twelve divisors of sixty (96 tests); sharp matrix orders and additional dimensions were checked. The all-dimensional proof does not rely on finite enumeration.

**Originality: PASS, qualified.** Existing sources already provide the general reversible second-order construction (Vlasov 2013), linear-memory context (Rowland–Yassawi 2012), and the ordinary/folded hypercube adjacency spectra (Mirafzal 2023). These ingredients are acknowledged and not claimed as original. The checked full primary texts do not tabulate the characteristic-five sharp temporal orders, fixed-space census, primitive cycle populations or changing response to the antipodal matching. The two closely related accessible published wave results on a universal hub and mixed-arity Hamming graphs work in characteristic two and do not imply the characteristic-five antipodal comparison as stated. This contribution is an explicit finite-field specialization of established spectral machinery, and historical unindexed equivalents remain possible.

**Value: PASS.** The object is the canonical antipodal-matching augmentation of a cube, studied as a graph-network enhancement; the invariant is the exact temporal order and orbit census of a standard reversible local wave. The edge addition exhibits a nonmonotone change in global period, with a reduction at dimensions three and five but an increase at dimension two, a phenomenon not predictable from graph degree or diameter. The complete time-fixed formula and high-dimensional period-sixty prevalence make the observation an all-dimensional classification rather than an arbitrary small enumeration. Its scope is limited to this wave rule and characteristic five.
