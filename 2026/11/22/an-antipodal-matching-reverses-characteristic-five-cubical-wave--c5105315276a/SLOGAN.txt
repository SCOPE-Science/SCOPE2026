Adding one antipodal perfect matching to a Boolean cube changes the exact characteristic-five reversible-wave clock from \(20\) to \(30\) in dimension two, from \(30\) to \(12\) in dimension three, and from \(60\) to \(30\) in dimension five, but has no effect on the global clock in every other dimension at least two. A five-mode character formula counts every primitive temporal orbit over all fields \(\mathbb F_{5^f}\), and almost every high-dimensional state has least period \(60\).
