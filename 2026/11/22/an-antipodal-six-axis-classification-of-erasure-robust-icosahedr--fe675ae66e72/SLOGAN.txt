The icosahedral graph has exact minimum identifying-code sizes \(5,6,11,12\) for tolerance of zero, one, two, or three arbitrary detector erasures, with respectively \(120,20,12,1\) optimizers. Its twenty one-erasure minima are precisely its facial 3-suns, and every minimum ordinary five-detector code extends to exactly one of them. A second tolerated failure sharply increases the minimum from six detectors to eleven.
