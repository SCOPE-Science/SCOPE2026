A single closing twist on a cubic ladder changes the attraction basin of the universal open-neighborhood NOR two-cycle by exactly \(-2v_n\), where \(v_n\) follows the recurrence \(v_n=v_{n-1}-v_{n-2}-v_{n-3}\). The twist advantage is never zero and reverses sign infinitely often within every arithmetic progression of ladder lengths, even as its relative size decays exponentially.
