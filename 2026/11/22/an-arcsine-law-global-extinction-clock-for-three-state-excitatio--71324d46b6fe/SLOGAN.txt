The global extinction time of three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on an independently uniformly colored open path has a nondegenerate linear-scale limit: \(T_n/n\) converges to the distance of a Lévy-arcsine Brownian-maximum location from its farther endpoint. A deterministic propagation sandwich proves the law and yields the sharp mean coefficient \(\lim \mathbb E[T_n]/n=1/2+1/\pi\).
