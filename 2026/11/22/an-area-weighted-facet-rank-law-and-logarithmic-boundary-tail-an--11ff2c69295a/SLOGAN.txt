Under Euclidean surface-area sampling, just the \(n\) coordinate facets of generalized parking-function polytopes asymptotically carry \(61.781661\ldots\%\) of the boundary area, independently of the fixed parking parameter. The other facets follow a universal \(k^{-2}\) area-weighted cardinality law, producing a sharp mean facet rank \(\frac{\log n}{(1+\alpha)\sqrt{2\pi}}+O(1)\).
