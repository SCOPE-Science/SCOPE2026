For every odd Fock polynomial degree \(n=2m-1\ge3\), exact Gaussian-compensated tight sampling with one common weight per point requires at least \(m+1\) distinct sampling radii, regardless of angular placement or oversampling. Arbitrary positive sample weights achieve exact tightness on precisely \(m\) radii: Gaussian quadrature gives algebraic radial masses, whereas equality of external weights would violate Hermite–Lindemann transcendence.
