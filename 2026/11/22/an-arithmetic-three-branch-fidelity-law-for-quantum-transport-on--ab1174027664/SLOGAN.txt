For one-particle quantum transport between the two peripheral vertices of any triangle in a friendship graph, perfect and pretty good state transfer are impossible at every fixed size, although optimal fidelity tends to one. The leading infidelity coefficient splits into three distinct arithmetic values: \(1-1/\sqrt2\) for nonsquare \(8m+1\), and two different variational constants for triangular \(m\) according to the integral spectral frequency modulo eight.
