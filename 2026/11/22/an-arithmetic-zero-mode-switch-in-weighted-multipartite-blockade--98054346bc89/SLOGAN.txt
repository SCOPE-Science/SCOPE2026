In an exactly blockaded quantum spin network on any complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, a zero-energy component of the unexcited state exists precisely when **every** part's site-dependent driving amplitudes admit an equal signed sum; its weight and the entire zero-mode degeneracy have exact formulas. A tuned odd-sized part can sustain this component, whereas arbitrarily small detuning of one critical drive removes it from the infinite-time averaged vacuum return amplitude.
