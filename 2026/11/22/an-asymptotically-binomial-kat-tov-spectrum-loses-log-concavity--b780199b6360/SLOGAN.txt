For \(n\) leaves of a named metric star with spoke distance two and leaf-pair distance four in the generic four-distance metric space, the near-leaf singleton-type polynomial is \((1+z)^n+3(2+z)^n+n(1+2^n)z\). Its coefficients are always unimodal, but log-concavity fails for the first time at eleven leaves and thereafter only at grade two, even though the normalized grade law approaches \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,1/3)\) exponentially fast in total variation.
