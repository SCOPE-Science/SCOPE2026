# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The normal fan of a simple convex three-polytope consists of \(2F-4\) positive-area spherical triangles. A triangle with no short spherical side has all angles at least \(\pi/2\) by spherical cosine law, hence area at least \(\pi/2\). For \(F\ge7\) at most seven such triangles can occur; every bad triangle touches at least one short side, and each short side borders two triangles. This proves the integer lower bound. The known Li–Wang–Yu truncation normal recurrence and its incidence consequences are reconstructed in RESULT.md, and its positive normal-dot-product pairs occur exactly at index gap three. Numerical checker outputs for two finite parameter sets agree with the infinite proof; they do not replace it.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem quantifies a recently established threshold between finitely many right-bounded types and arbitrarily large nearly right-bounded polytopes. Rather than only proving that one obtuse angle must exist, it forces a sharp asymptotic proportion of one third of all edges and exhibits witnesses under arbitrarily tight upper-angle pinching. This is a structural sharp density law. The finite additive-one gap is retained.
