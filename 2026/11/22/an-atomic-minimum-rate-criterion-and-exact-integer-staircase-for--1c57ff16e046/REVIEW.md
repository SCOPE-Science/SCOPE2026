# Scientific review — heterogeneous survey rotation

## Correctness — PASS

The result has an explicit domain: nondegenerate iid nonnegative finite-mean site intensities, independent conditional Poisson counts, positive fixed deadline and relocation cost, one free initial location, and no other constraints. The mathematical objective is the detection probability, not an empirical claim or a time-to-first-event expectation. Every global optimizer for a fixed count has equal durations because the log Laplace survival exponent is strictly concave. A strictly decreasing departure function gives a unique continuous optimum or all-deadline concentration; its large-time sign is determined by the mass at the essential infimum. Strict concavity of the count perspective, monotonicity of adjacent-count differences and their endpoint signs yield exactly one ordered threshold for each next integer, including all equality cases. The atomless case uses a proved sublinear Laplace exponent, not a numerical tail extrapolation. The standalone checker passed 10,469 supplementary assertions after independent execution, including mixed discrete laws and an atomless example; these tests support but do not replace the all-distribution proof.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

Palencia et al. (2024) study rotating camera placements to improve precision of detection-rate estimation (different objective). Moore et al. (2014) and Moore and McCarthy (2016) study the same detection-probability objective and repeated visits; their examined results use lognormal approximations and a closed gamma case, rather than the sharp all-distribution criterion involving an atom at the slowest rate and a complete globally ordered integer staircase. General deadline/restart work by van Moorsel and Wolter (2006) and Lorenz (2016) has survival-product and equal-hazard principles; those are partial background but do not directly state the low-rate-atom cost dichotomy or strict no-skipping threshold classification. Nishimura (1991) studies closely related heterogeneous Poisson prey encounters but maximizes long-term gain rate with actual random first-contact stopping, not this deadline objective. Search comparisons against published mathematical records found nearby ecological survey, covariance and resetting results but no covering statement by implication. Historical priority is provisional: other resource-allocation or restart theorems might subsume the atomic classification. No external independent novelty certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

The question is motivated by real camera relocation versus longer stays, where site quality is heterogeneous and site changes consume a finite budget. The theorem distinguishes two qualitatively different regimes that mean/variance rules and the familiar gamma calculation cannot identify: a smallest-rate point mass can make relocation uniformly inferior for *every* budget, whereas a continuous positive-lower-bound law can justify eventual relocation for *every finite* switching cost. The result also removes discrete-combinatorial ambiguity by proving a complete exact sequence of increasing thresholds and two-neighbor optimization, rather than a single illustrative numerical optimum. That is a meaningful structural benchmark even when real data will not identify the lower-tail atom robustly.
