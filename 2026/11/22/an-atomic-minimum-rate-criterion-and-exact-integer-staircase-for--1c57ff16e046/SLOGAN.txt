For independent Poisson-mixture wildlife surveys with a fixed relocation delay, never moving the detector is optimal at every deadline precisely when the slowest encounter rate is positive and carries an atom whose mass exceeds the critical cost threshold \(q\ge e^{-c\lambda_0}\). Otherwise, the unique best number of visited sites rises through every positive integer at successive exact budget thresholds, and visits of any fixed count must have equal observing durations.
