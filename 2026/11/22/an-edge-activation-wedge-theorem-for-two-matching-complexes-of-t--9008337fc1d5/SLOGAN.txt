Adding one shared edge to the complete bipartite graph \(K_{2,n}\) creates a triangular book whose degree-two matching complex splits integrally as \(\bigvee^{(n-1)^2}S^2\vee\bigvee^{\left(\binom{n-1}{2}\right)^2}S^3\) for every \(n\ge2\). The inclusion of the original complex is homotopy-split, and the new two-spheres arise from suspending the graph \(K_{n,n}\).
