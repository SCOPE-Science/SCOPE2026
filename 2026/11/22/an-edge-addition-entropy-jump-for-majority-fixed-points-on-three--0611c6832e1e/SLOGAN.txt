On the three-row square cylinders \(C_n\square P_3\), tie-retaining binary majority has an exact fixed-state spectrum with leading growth \(\rho^n\), where \(\rho\) is the root greater than two of \(X^3-2X^2-1\). Closing each column from a path to a triangle increases the fixed-state growth base from \(\rho\approx2.20557\) to three, an exponential entropy jump under natural edge addition.
