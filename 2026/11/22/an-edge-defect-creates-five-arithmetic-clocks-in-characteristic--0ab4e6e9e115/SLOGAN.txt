Deleting one edge from \(K_{a,b}\) changes the characteristic-three reversible wave clock into a six-residue law with exact periods \(10,12,20,24,36\), including a four-vertex exception and genuinely new fifth-root motion. When both shores are \(2\bmod3\), a closed every-time fixed-point formula shows that the maximal period \(20\) occurs with probability \((1-q^{-8})(1-q^{-2(a+b-4)})\) whenever \(a+b>4\).
