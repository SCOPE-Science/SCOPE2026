# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For every finite simple root graph of minimum degree at least two, the initially unobserved edges of its line graph are exactly the edges of the subgraph induced by vertices not incident with the sensors. Any untouched cycle prevents a first force, whereas a rooted-forest leaf-peeling order forces every remaining edge. This proves the all-order equivalence without relying on experiments. In the Petersen application, a complete simple-cycle enumeration produces twelve pentagons, ten hexagons, fifteen eight-cycles and twenty nine-cycles. The ten three-edge stars and twelve five-cycles are exactly the maximal failed sets. The full finite search over all 32768 selected-edge masks verifies the cardinality polynomial, fifteen minimum pairs, 140 inclusion-minimal triples, no larger minimal sets and reconfiguration component counts. Two codebases construct Petersen graphs in different representations and give matching results. Limit: the computational certificate is for the explicit finite graph only; the general case is proven symbolically.

**Originality — PASS with bounded priority risk.** Benson et al., *Power domination and zero forcing* (arXiv:1510.02421v1, 2015), establish the ordinary framework and general lower bounds, not an endpoint-induced-forest equivalence. Bjorkman et al., *Power Domination Reconfiguration* (DOI 10.1007/s44007-025-00187-5), define TAR and token jumping but do not state the exact Petersen-line configuration spectrum. Girish et al., *Fault-tolerance amplification in line graphs* (DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-54352-z), analyze a six-sensor line-Petersen lift and fault tolerance, not all optimal pairs or this forest characterization. Kameri et al. (DOI 10.1922/comdem.v4i1.19082) examine the inverse invariant on the Petersen graph and line graphs of basic families rather than this claim. An indexed result for \(L(K_n)\) is a special-root family; a line-Petersen locating-domination table concerns another predicate. Searches for endpoint forest, cycles, feedback sets, Petersen line-graph power domination and reconfiguration found no covering mathematical implication. Indexed-summary-only comparisons and the possibility of unindexed equivalences remain limitations; search absence is not uniqueness proof.

**Value — PASS.** The main equivalence is an exact reduction of a propagation process to forest detection over an infinite graph class. The fifteen-vertex Petersen instance is a canonical small cubic root with a complete solution-space census and a sharp distinction between frozen two-sensor solutions and four-budget connected reconfiguration. The full polynomial and 140 minimal triples are natural monitoring robustness data, not isolated arithmetic.

**Scientific limitations.** The root minimum-degree assumption cannot be dropped without changing the argument. The exact polynomial and exchange graph are finite graph-specific facts, and no claim of larger-graph generalization is made. Two implementations do not constitute external scholarly validation. Unindexed or inaccessible historical work may overlap.
