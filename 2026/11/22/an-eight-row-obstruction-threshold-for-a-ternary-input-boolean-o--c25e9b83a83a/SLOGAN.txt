For the split functional-dependency constraint \(\operatorname{dep}(x,y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(y,z)\), three possible input values and a Boolean output force every finite failure to have a failing subteam of at most eight rows, and eight is sharp regardless of the number of intermediate values. The inclusion-minimal failures have exactly 4, 6, 7, or 8 rows, forming 306 canonical patterns under renaming of intermediate values only.
