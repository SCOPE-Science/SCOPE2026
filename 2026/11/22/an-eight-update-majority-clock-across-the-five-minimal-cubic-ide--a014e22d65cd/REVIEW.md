# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For each of five explicit connected cubic graphs on twelve vertices, both independently implemented exhaustive classifiers processed all 4096 labelled configurations, and their complete orbit, first-entry and individual-basin histograms agree exactly. The first program uses LCF generation and forward trajectory tracing; the second uses independent edge data, Boolean-coordinate evaluation and reverse indegree peeling. The full transition tables demonstrate \(F^{10}=F^8\), and two explicit G5 initial configurations prove sharpness. Exact graph-isomorphism backtracking independently confirms one automorphism in each and no cross-isomorphisms. The exhaustiveness statement is finite and graph-specific. The separate literature catalogue supplies completeness of the five-graph class.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The well-known eventual-period-at-most-two theorem is prior work and is explicitly excluded from the new contribution. The inspected 2014 majority-dynamics preprint and 2016 deterministic-majority voting-time paper give general results, not the present five sharp clocks and basin spectra. The full published published-finding corpus Frucht result concerns a distinct three-state excitation rule; the full published dodecahedral result concerns a different twenty-vertex graph, so neither implies these tables or the G5 sharp clock. Relevant near-title results on Heawood and Shrikhande/rook graphs likewise have no such implication. Searches using graph names, aliases, LCF encodings, majority, attractor basins and transients returned no exact covering census. Residual risk: older unpublished, differently labelled, or unindexed enumerations and any catalogue issues.

## Value — PASS

This is a complete exact classification at the minimum possible order for cubic asymmetric graphs, a natural symmetry-breaking benchmark for finite network dynamics. The sharp eight-step clock distinguishes the fifth graph; the contrast between the number of two-cycle orbits and their attracted population (G3 versus G2) demonstrates a substantively different dynamical ordering not implied by orbit counts. Individual basin-size multiplicities provide reproducible invariant signatures for all five graphs rather than an arbitrary isolated integer.

## Limits

The proof is an exhaustive computational finite proof for precisely the five given graphs. It does not prove a uniform bound for all cubic graphs or independently reproduce the complete historical graph catalogue. An unindexed equivalent remains possible; independent mathematical audit has not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
