For finite binary-parity sensors of the doubling map, alternating sensors at successive iterates produces either exactly independent fair bits or an entropy rate of exactly one half, decided by whether \(p(s)q(s)\) has the form \(s r(s)^2\) over \(\mathbb F_2\). Alternating two sensor outputs at each iterate always has half-rate, and both protocols admit sequences in which every two reported bits are independent although a deterministic three-bit XOR constraint persists.
