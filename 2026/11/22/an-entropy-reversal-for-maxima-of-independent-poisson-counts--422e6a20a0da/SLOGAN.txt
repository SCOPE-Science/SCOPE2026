For every fixed sample size \(m\ge2\), taking the maximum of \(m\) independent equal-intensity Poisson counts raises Shannon entropy at sufficiently small intensities but lowers it at sufficiently large intensities. The large-intensity entropy deficit converges to the exact Gaussian-maximum constant \(c_m<0\), equal to \(1/2-\log2\) when \(m=2\); no uniqueness of the intervening crossover is claimed.
