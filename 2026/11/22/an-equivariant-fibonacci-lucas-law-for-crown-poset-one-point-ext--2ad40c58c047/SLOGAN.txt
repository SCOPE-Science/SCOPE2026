For every crown poset with at least five minima and five maxima, the complete dihedral fixed-point character of nonalgebraic one-point extensions is given by Lucas numbers for rotations and the same Fibonacci number for every reflection. Consequently, the exact number of extension types up to base automorphisms is \(2+F_{n+3}+n^{-1}\sum_{d\mid n}\varphi(n/d)L_{2d}\), and almost all named types have trivial stabilizer as \(n\) grows.
