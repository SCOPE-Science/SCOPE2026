For a geometrically accelerating internally resetting random walk, a reset hazard \(c/(k+1)^\beta\) permits finite-time accumulation of infinitely many jumps exactly when \(\beta>1\). Just above this threshold the mean accumulation clock has an explicit Gamma-function prefactor times \(\exp(c/(\beta-1))\), and the clock divided by its mean converges to a unit exponential law.
