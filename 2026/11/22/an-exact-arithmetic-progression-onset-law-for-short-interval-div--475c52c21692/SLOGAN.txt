For every interval ratio \(\lambda>1\) and divisor multiplicity \(r\ge2\), the first integers with \(r\) divisors in \((y,\lambda y]\) form an exact arithmetic progression, with its threshold and modulus given by a block of \(r\) consecutive complementary integers. In particular, for \(X\le2ry\), the dyadic multiplicity count is exactly the number of multiples of \(\operatorname{lcm}(r,\ldots,2r-1)\) between \((2r-1)y\) and \(X\).
