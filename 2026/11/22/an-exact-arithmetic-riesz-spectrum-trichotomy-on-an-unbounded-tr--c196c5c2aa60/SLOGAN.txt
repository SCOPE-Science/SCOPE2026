On an explicit unbounded fundamental domain of \(\mathbb Z\), the two-coset exponential family \(2\mathbb Z\cup(2\mathbb Z+\alpha)\) is a Riesz basis exactly when \(\alpha=a/b\) has odd reduced numerator; its exact squared bounds are \(1\pm\cos(\pi/(2b))\). Even-numerator rationals make it incomplete, while every irrational offset makes it complete but nonframe, so arbitrarily small irrational shifts from the orthogonal integer Fourier basis destroy stability.
