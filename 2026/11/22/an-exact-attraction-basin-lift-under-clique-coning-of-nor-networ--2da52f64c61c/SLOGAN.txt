For synchronous open-neighborhood NOR dynamics, adjoining any clique of \(r\) universal vertices to an isolate-free graph preserves the period and basin size of every nonprincipal attractor, creates exactly \(r\) new singleton fixed basins, and adds precisely \(2^{n+r}-2^n-r\) states to the all-zero/all-one oscillator's basin. That oscillator uniquely dominates after the first universal-vertex addition, and its attraction probability tends to one as the clique grows.
