For the incommensurate product-sine map \(x\mapsto x+\kappa\sin(2\pi x)\sin(2x)\), every orbit converges to an equilibrium when \(0<\kappa\le1/(2\pi)\), and the limiting distribution of sink-basin widths has two exact atoms and a uniform component. At arbitrary positive gain, the equilibrium multipliers form an explicit mixture of two arcsine laws, yielding a positive exact sink density with separate square-root losses at \(\kappa=1/\pi\) and \(\kappa=1\).
