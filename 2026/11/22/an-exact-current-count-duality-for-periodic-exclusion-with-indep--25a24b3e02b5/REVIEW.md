# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is a finite-state generator identity for every integer ring size \(L\ge2\). The involution is explicitly defined, is proved to exchange the \(M\)- and \(L-M\)-particle sectors, and maps each clockwise hop into another clockwise hop with identical obstacle-gated rate. Its offset on obstacle positions is derived from the same hop, not conjectured. Homogeneous obstacle birth/death flips preserve their rates under reflection; irreducibility gives unique stationary distributions. Therefore every marked finite-time jump-count law and the canonical stationary current have exact duals. The included exact-rational checker confirms every labelled transition for ring sizes 2–7, both gate placements and three rate choices; the proof does not extrapolate from those checks.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Waclaw, Cholewa-Waclaw and Greulich (arXiv:1809.05441, Sec. 3 and Conclusions) observed symmetric-looking current-density plots for independently binding obstacles and explicitly distinguished feedback-constrained obstacles where symmetry fails. Szavits-Nossan and Waclaw (arXiv:2006.14474, Sec. II, VII and Conclusions) give numerical evidence for exact complementary-density symmetry, then explicitly state its mechanism is unknown. Their Appendix C derives consequences **assuming** the symmetry. The present claim instead proves a concrete offset-reflection generator conjugacy and equality of full marked hop-count laws. Related published results on deformed exclusion, site-trap disorder and other ratchets involve different generators and do not imply this result. The natural particle-hole/reflection method is classical and a more general prior coupling argument may implicitly cover it; a novelty search alone cannot eliminate this risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result resolves a specific unresolved microscopic-symmetry question for a natural driven interacting-particle model without a solvable stationary distribution. Its exact **pathwise** statement predicts not just a symmetric mean current but paired waiting-time, jump-count variance and large-deviation statistics at complementary densities. Both obstacle placements and partial obstruction are included, and failure of occupancy-independent obstacle clocks explains why the feedback-constrained model falls outside its scope.

## Closest literature and limitations

The two inspected primary manuscripts describe the exact same classes of dynamics and stop at numerical or approximate current-density analyses. The oldest posted version is dated 2018-09-14; the second is dated 2020-06-25. No proof of the offset-reflection conjugacy was located in the inspected source text or in targeted related research records; absence from a search does not establish universal novelty. Periodic boundary conditions and autonomous spatially homogeneous obstacle clocks are essential hypotheses. The theorem is a structural duality, not an explicit solution for the stationary current function.
