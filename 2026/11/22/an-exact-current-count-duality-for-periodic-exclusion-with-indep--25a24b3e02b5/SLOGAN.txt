For periodic totally asymmetric exclusion with homogeneous, independently switching obstacles, complementing and reflecting the particles while *offset-reflecting the obstacles* produces an exact Markov-generator conjugacy between \(M\) and \(L-M\) particles. This proves the previously unexplained symmetry \(J_L(M)=J_L(L-M)\) and, more strongly, equality of every finite-time successful-hop counting law under dual starting conditions.
