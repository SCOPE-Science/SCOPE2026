The orthogonal Steinmetz tricylinder has a complete random-direction width law given by one elementary quadrature, with its uppermost \(22.876\%\) of widths distributed **exactly uniformly**. Near the minimum width its probability grows like \(\frac{3\varepsilon}{\pi r}\log(r/\varepsilon)\), recording the six crossing axis directions of the three minimum-width great circles.
