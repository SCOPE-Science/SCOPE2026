# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

For strictly positive stage transport, the exact allele-density pulse map yields monotone allele fixation. At complete resistance dominance the resistant density is governed by an unperturbed Perron matrix, while the susceptible allele's leading nonlinear loss is quadratic. A Perron projection gives the sharp \(1/k\) coefficient, and spectral comparison gives the exponential decay exponent for positive heterozygote selection. All limiting denominators remain positive by the stated initial conditions, and the standalone exact-polynomial and numerical examples passed. The proof is analytical; finite checks are corroboration only.

## Originality — PASS (with historical-priority risk)

The full text of D'Ovidio Long, Adjei and Patel (2026) was examined: its Theorem 5 proves eventual fixation but does not quantify rate or give the weighted coefficient. Engen, Lande and Sæther (2009) supply reproductive-value weighting under stochastic weak selection, and de Vries and Caswell (2019) provide age/stage genetics, without the same fixed-period genotype pulse convergence law. Classical unstructured diploid fixation slowing is prior art and is explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. General spectral control and related stage models were checked. The foundational 1990 class-structured paper was inspected in full (its formula is first-order in rare-allele frequency), while an applied helminth paper was available only in abstract, leaving residual priority risk; this uncertainty is not a proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The theorem turns an existing qualitative resistance-convergence statement into an exact time-to-small-susceptible-fraction asymptotic, including a computable stage-reproductive-value coefficient and a discontinuous type change in the asymptotic decay when heterozygote selection becomes positive. It remains valid whether the total pest population declines or expands; this matters when projecting residual susceptibility under repeated control rather than merely assessing ecological eradication.

## Limits

The result is deterministic and asymptotic, not a finite-population fixation-time theorem. No density dependence, mutation, resistance costs, pulse variation or general nonlinear stage dynamics is included. Historical priority is literature-bounded, and independent scientific validation is outstanding.
