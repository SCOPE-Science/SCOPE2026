For fixed-depth unbiased riffle shuffles, the probability that the canonical optimal no-feedback strategy has made no correct guess by any first-half time \(t\) differs by at most \(e^{-n/(8a)}\) from the single binomial atom \(\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(t,1/a)=\lfloor t/a\rfloor\}\), where \(a=2^k\). This gives a sharp \(t^{-1/2}\) first-success tail and a \(\sqrt n\)-order expected waiting time censored at the deck's midpoint, with explicit constants.
