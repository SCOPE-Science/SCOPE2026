For the unbiased Gaussian-kernel goodness-of-fit statistic under an exactly known standard-normal null, the complete finite-sample covariance between any two bandwidths is explicit. In growing dimension, distinct inverse-square bandwidths \(u d^{-\alpha}\) and \(v d^{-\alpha}\) undergo a sharp correlation transition: zero for \(\alpha<1/2\), a nontrivial explicit limit at \(\alpha=1/2\), and one for \(\alpha>1/2\).
