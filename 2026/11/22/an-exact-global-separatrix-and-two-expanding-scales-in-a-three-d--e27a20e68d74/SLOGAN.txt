On the natural \(c=1\) surface of a published three-dimensional parabolic map, a continuous nonlinear observable \(H\) satisfies the exact power law \(H\circ F=H^r\), and \(H=1\) separates the entire basin of extinction from double-exponential escape. Almost every point on this critical surface instead experiences unbounded alternating logarithmic bursts; the only invariant probability measures are mixtures of three fixed-point masses.
