For a partially saturated Leslie–Gower competition map, exactly one explicit parameter value makes the line segment between its two axial equilibria an invariant heteroclinic orbit with a closed-form parametrization. On this resonance every strictly positive orbit has a universal superexponential transverse-defect law \(\lim_{n\to\infty}n^{-2}\log\operatorname{dist}(T^n(z),S)=\tfrac12\log\lambda<0\) unless it lies exactly on the segment, despite ordinary geometric convergence of the surviving population.
