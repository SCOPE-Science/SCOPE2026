On any weighted simplicial cone whose apex has minimum vertex weight, dimension-delayed lower-star persistence splits integrally into exactly one finite bar per nonempty base face, with its birth determined by that face's largest weight and its death by the next dimension delay. For the \(\varepsilon\)-lower-star filtration every finite bar has length exactly \(\varepsilon\), exposing arbitrarily many delay-created features even when the entire complex is contractible.
