For independent equally sized introduction episodes that succeed only when a receptive episode receives at least two Poisson founders, the optimal number of episodes follows a complete strictly ordered budget staircase, with exactly two adjacent counts tied at each unique threshold. A single continuous per-episode scale \(\xi_s\) determines every candidate count, and the first split threshold has an exact implicit equation; this is a specific structural result, not a new claim that intermediate release sizes can exist in general.
