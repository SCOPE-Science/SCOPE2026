For true variance-reduced finite-sum local primal–dual optimization on scalar quadratics, fixed local gradient stepsizes produce a sharp mean-error spectral rate \(1-\mu_*/\tau+o(\tau^{-1})\), with \(\mu_*\) the first positive eigenvalue of a curvature-weighted network Laplacian. A genuinely stochastic two-agent example has the exact every-budget multiplier \(1-(1-\lambda)/\tau\), turning a fixed mean-consensus reduction into \(\Theta(\tau^2)\) local component-gradient work per agent.
