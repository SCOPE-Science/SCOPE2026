The bosonic Fock amplification of a damped two-mode Jordan shear has an exact nonperiodic complex-time boundedness horn, with sharp analytic-sector angle \(\arccos(c/(2a))\). At the critical shear \(c=2a\), its opening changes from linear to quadratic while every Schatten \(p\)-moment changes from order \(t^{-2}\) to \(t^{-4}\), with both changes controlled by the same \(\delta\asymp t^2\) crossover.
