For exponentially aged one-dimensional Barenblatt porous-medium packets, stationary kurtosis decreases strictly from \(6\) to \(9/5\) as nonlinearity increases, identifying the nonlinear exponent without knowing the reset rate or diffusion scale. Exactly one positive exponent gives Gaussian fourth moment, but its sixth cumulant remains strictly negative, proving that this fourth-order match hides a non-Gaussian law.
