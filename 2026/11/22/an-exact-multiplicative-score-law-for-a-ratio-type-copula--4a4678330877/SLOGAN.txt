For the 2022 ratio-type copula \(C(u,v)=uv(u+v)/(1+uv)\), the ordinary random-variable product \(Z=UV\) has exact CDF \(4z/(1+z)^2\). Its negative logarithm is distributed as the maximum absolute value of two independent standard logistic variables, yielding exact multiplicative-tail quantiles and variance \(8\log2-4\). The product CDF crosses its independence counterpart exactly once, at \(z_*=0.09920455417196283\ldots\), and changes the direction of its tail deviation.
