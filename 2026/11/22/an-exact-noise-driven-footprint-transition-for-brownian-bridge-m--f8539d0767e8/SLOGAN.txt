For one fixed-width noisy interval-average observation of a Brownian bridge's signed first polynomial moment, the globally optimal window touches an endpoint exactly when \(12\eta(1-2h)+h(3h^2-5h+1)\leq0\); otherwise it has one of two strictly interior mirror placements. The sharp width threshold \(h_0=(5-\sqrt{13})/6\) creates a regime where increasing observation noise moves the optimal footprint away from the endpoint, with a unique monotone interior design and an explicit high-noise limit.
