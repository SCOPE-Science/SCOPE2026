On every odd-order finite projective-plane graph state, the exact point-versus-line entanglement after omitting **any incidence matching** is controlled by the nullity of an explicit matrix only one larger than that matching. One missing controlled-\(Z\) gate raises the entropy to its maximum, yet a \(k\)-edge induced omission matching gives exactly \(n-k+1\) bits, and conic tangents in \(\operatorname{PG}(2,q)\) exhibit a loss of \(q-1\) bits.
