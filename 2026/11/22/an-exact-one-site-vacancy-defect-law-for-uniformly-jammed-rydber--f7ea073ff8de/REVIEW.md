## Mathematical value — PASS
A missing lattice atom is a natural localized perturbation of a maximal blockade array. The structural distinction is informative: it eliminates intact configurations occupying one site, yet simultaneously makes possible a unique oversized excitation-free interval that cannot occur anywhere in the pristine jammed ensemble. The exact response covers every fugacity and blockade radius; the anomalous-sector probability has an unexpected sharp scaling of \((\log b)^2/b^3\), much smaller than the order-\(1/b\) leading overall perturbation. The calculation supplies a benchmark for localized lattice defects and stability of flat blocked ensembles rather than arbitrary parameter sampling.
