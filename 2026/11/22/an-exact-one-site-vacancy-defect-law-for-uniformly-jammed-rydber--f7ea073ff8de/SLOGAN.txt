A single permanently inexcitable site in a uniformly sampled jammed Rydberg ring opens exactly one new local sector: a pinned occupied-site gap of length \(2b+2\). Its thermodynamic probability is explicit for every blockade range and, at unit fugacity, is asymptotic to \((\log b)^2/b^3\), even though the defect changes the whole partition function by an order-\(1/b\) factor.
