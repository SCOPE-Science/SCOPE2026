For the planar sinusoidal map \(F_a(x,y)=(x+\sin(x+y),ax)\), the exact primitive-two-cycle lattice changes from one to three cycles per translation cell at \(a=1+\pi\), with a square-root splitting of two nonattracting side branches. The central cycle attracts precisely for \(1+\pi<a<4.65946370392898\ldots\), and there are no primitive two-cycles for \(0<a\le3\).
