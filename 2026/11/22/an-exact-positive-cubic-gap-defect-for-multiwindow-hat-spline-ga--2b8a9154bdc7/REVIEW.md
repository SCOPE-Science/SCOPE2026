# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The Zak transform fiber identity gives the *optimal* frame multipliers \(A=\min F\), \(B=r\), not mere sufficient estimates. Between consecutive distinct translation residues, the potential has exact second derivative \(8r\); integrating those quadratic pieces produces the two-term identity. The global-minimum condition at every midpoint, continuity and derivative jumps prove the full equality law, including multiplicities and the single-residue nonframe case. The proof handles arbitrary real offsets. A standalone exact rational checker successfully tested 4,134 grids as a diagnostic only.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources
The September 2026 Hasannasab–Lemvig source treats B-spline Zak zero sets and integer-oversampled single-window systems. The 2012 Hardin–Kendall–Saff circle-polarization theorem already gives the all-distinct equally spaced optimum, so that fact is expressly excluded from the novelty claim. The claimed additional result is the exact joint nonnegative identity for **every configuration**, with its cubic gap variation, gap-midpoint slack and all balanced equal-multiplicity equality strata. General multiwindow frame-operator formulations do not themselves state this quantitative formula. Published results specifically treating half-density single-window tent systems and coherent superpositions of shifted hat pulses concern different operators. No equivalent exact formula was identified in the checked scholarly and indexed records; this is a best-knowledge literature assessment, not proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The nonnegative two-term identity quantifies conditioning loss due to nonuniform placement of sampling windows rather than merely identifying an optimizer. It separates geometric irregularity from potential-equilibration mismatch and exactly classifies stable sharp arrangements even when multiple windows coincide. This offers a structural stability certificate for a natural multiwindow Gabor design problem; no claim is made that all equispacing is novel or that a broad new spline frame-set theorem follows.

## Closest literature and limits
