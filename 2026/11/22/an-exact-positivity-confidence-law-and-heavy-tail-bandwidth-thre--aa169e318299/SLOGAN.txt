For two independent Gaussian nodes in unregularized Gaussian-kernel quadrature, the probability that both unconstrained weights are positive is exactly \(I_{(1+2h)^2/[1+(1+2h)^2]}(1/2,d/2)\), and the first dimension guaranteeing at least 95% positivity across all positive bandwidths is six. The random critical shape has a sharp \(t^{-d}\) survival tail, so its positive \(p\)-th moment exists exactly when \(p<d\).
