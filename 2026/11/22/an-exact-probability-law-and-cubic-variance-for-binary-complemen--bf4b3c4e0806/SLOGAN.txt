For a uniformly random binary word, the number of distinct remainders after deleting a scattered `01` has an explicit all-length rational probability law, exact variance \((2n^3-5n^2+45n-60)/512\) for \(n\ge6\), and Gaussian fluctuations on the \(n^{3/2}/16\) scale. The earlier run-pair cardinality identity and its mean are credited as prior results.
