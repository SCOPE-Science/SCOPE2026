For every eventually periodic binary gap shift with both allowed and forbidden distances repeating in its tail, the marker density of the maximal-entropy measure in its canonical length-\(n\) finite-type covers converges with the explicit sharp error \(nq^nH_{n\bmod m}(q)/M^2+O(q^n)\). The limiting normalized error has a finite residue-phase spectrum; for the even shift its two parity constants are \(\varphi/5\) and \(1/5\).
