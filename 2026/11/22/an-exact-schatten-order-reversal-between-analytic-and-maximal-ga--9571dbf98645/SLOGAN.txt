The analytic Gaussian Fock projection and its maximal absolute-kernel partner have identical trace and Hilbert–Schmidt norms throughout the strictly compact regime, but their Schatten \(\mathcal S_p\) ordering reverses sharply at \(p=1\) and \(p=2\). Complete geometric versus two-index Mehler singular spectra yield both this strict three-regime comparison and different near-critical ordered-rank profiles.
