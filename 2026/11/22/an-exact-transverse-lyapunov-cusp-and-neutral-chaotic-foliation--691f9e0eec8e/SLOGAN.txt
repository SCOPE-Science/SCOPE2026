On the maximally chaotic diagonal of a symmetric three-species Lotka–Volterra map, the exact ambient Lyapunov spectrum is \((\log2,\Lambda(a),\Lambda(a))\): both transverse exponents are positive except at \(a=1\), where they vanish with a sharp linear-versus-square-root cusp. At this exceptional coupling, an entire three-dimensional invariant tetrahedron foliates into distinct logistic-chaotic population rays, yet no empirical invariant measure has a positive-volume basin there.
