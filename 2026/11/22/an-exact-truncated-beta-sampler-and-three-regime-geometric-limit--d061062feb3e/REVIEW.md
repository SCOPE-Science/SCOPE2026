# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
For the polytope explicitly cut out by \(x_i\in[-1,1]\), \(y_j\in[0,1]\), and \(x_i\le y_j\), the unique minimum upper coordinate \(T\) determines a product fiber of measure \(q(1+T)^{p-1}(1-T)^{q-1}\,dT\). Integrating and applying the beta-binomial tail identity establishes the exact volume; strict rank-order partitions establish the independent finite mixture sampler. Taylor expansions and a global log-concavity bound give the boundary and critical laws, and the interior Laplace principle gives the Gaussian law and logarithmic volume rate. The theorem is fully quantified for every \(p,q\ge1\) (finite identities) and for the three specified asymptotic regimes. A standard-library exact checker reproduced 361 full parameter pairs, 361 exact volume equalities and 1,083 moment bounds, and 140,000 sampler draws. The computer checks are supplementary, not infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
García-Segador and Miranda (2023, DOI:10.1016/j.fss.2023.108655) establish a general equal-volume triangulation and a simplex-chain-based random sampler, but their introduction and conclusions explicitly retain family-specific chain enumeration and direct uniform generation as obstacles. Their general theorem does not evaluate the current binomial volume, its one-dimensional truncated-Beta marginal, its independent sign-cell sampler, or any three-regime limit. Miranda and García-Segador (2021, DOI:10.1016/j.fss.2021.11.001) describe pointed order polytopes and their faces, not this measure calculation. A published 2026 signed-*chain* volume law concerns a full-dimensional, symmetric body with absolute cross-coordinate inequalities, not the pinned, asymmetric signed-*order* realization treated here. Ordinary two-level order-polytope covariance results concern a different unpinned body. Searches of published mathematical records and accessible prior findings found no equivalent exact claim. A differently named general order-statistics or conditioned-Beta theorem, or unindexed paper, could imply parts of this result and warrants independent priority review.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The specific family is a natural test of the general pointed order-polytope volume-and-sampling problem explicitly identified in the 2023 paper. The binomial-tail formula counts its simplex-chain pieces exactly and supplies direct samplers that do not enumerate those pieces, including a finite exact-reweighting sampler avoiding exponentially rare truncated-Beta rejection. The sharp \(p-q\asymp\sqrt N\) transition and distinct exponential, truncated Gaussian, and Gaussian limits determine nontrivial high-dimensional geometry of the uniform point measure, not a routine isolated evaluation. No contribution of comparable form is claimed for arbitrary posets.
