For the pointed order polytope of two comparable antichains with one neutral lower element, the minimum upper coordinate is exactly a \(\operatorname{Beta}(p,q)\) variable conditioned above \(1/2\), giving a closed binomial-tail volume and an exact sampler without triangulation enumeration. Its high-dimensional geometry has a sharp boundary-to-interior transition: exponential, truncated-normal, and Gaussian limits occupy three distinct block-ratio regimes.
