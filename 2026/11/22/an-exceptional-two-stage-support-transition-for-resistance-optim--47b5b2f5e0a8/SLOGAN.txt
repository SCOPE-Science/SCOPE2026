A complete network with a three-edge price premium has a unique global minimum-total-resistance allocation, with exact active-edge formulas for every budget and price. The five-vertex case is exceptional: at \(r=9/5\) an unmarked unit-price edge disappears while all three premium triangle edges survive until \(r=45/23\); for every larger network, only the priced triangle class disappears, at \(r=n(n-2)/[(n-3)(n-1)]\).
