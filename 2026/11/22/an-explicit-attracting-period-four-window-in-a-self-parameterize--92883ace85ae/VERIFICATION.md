The interval trapping and derivative inequalities were verified using exact rational arithmetic in `verify.py` (Python 3 standard library). The proof covers every real control in the stated closed interval, not just finitely sampled values. A routine algebraic derivative bound on the three regular boxes and a much sharper bound on the critical box give the strict four-step contraction. No independent audit has been performed. The result does not establish global uniqueness of a physical attractor or exclude coexisting chaotic sets.
