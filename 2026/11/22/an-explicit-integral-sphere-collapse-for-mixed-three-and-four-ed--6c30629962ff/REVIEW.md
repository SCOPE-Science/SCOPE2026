A sectorwise descending-cardinality pairing deletes first all faces containing both poles, then those containing just one pole, then the interval component in each three-vertex long-arm independence factor. For each stage, competing codimension-one cofaces were removed earlier; every upper face is a legal independent face. The remaining join of two-point complexes is the crosspolytope sphere. The earliest dated primary Kim source proves a sphere-or-contractible theorem only without induced cycle length divisible by three; two short arms here yield an induced six-cycle. The published all-short-arm multitheta result concerns a distinct family and produces an extra circle. Published four-edge theta matching-complex and Rips statements concern other complexes. Searches of homotopy, theta, independent-set, path-length and integral symmetry aliases found no statement implying the complete claim. The result supplies a structural, exact all-size simple-homotopy classification for a natural nonternary family with arbitrarily many internally disjoint paths, a reproducible closed count of elementary collapses, and its complete full-group integral orientation action. It identifies the mechanism by which inserting four-edge arms eliminates the extra circle of the all-three-edge family. This is a motivated topology and symmetry calculation rather than a small isolated enumeration.
