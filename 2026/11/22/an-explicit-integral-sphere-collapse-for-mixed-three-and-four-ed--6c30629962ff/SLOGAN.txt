For any mixed multitheta graph with at least one three-edge arm and one four-edge arm, the independence complex explicitly simplicially collapses to \(S^{m+q-1}\) in exactly \(2^{m-1}+3^m2^q+3^q(5^m-3^m)/2\) elementary pairs. The full integral homology character is the sign of both arm permutations multiplied by \((-1)^q\) under pole reversal, even when induced six-cycles prevent the usual ternary-graph theorem from applying.
