For the characteristic-five rational elliptic surface underlying a recent non-liftable Calabi–Yau construction, \(P=(t(t+2),\sqrt2\,t(t+2))\) generates every geometric rational section. Frobenius negates this generator: the Mordell–Weil group over \(\mathbb F_{5^m}(t)\) is zero for odd \(m\) and infinite cyclic for even \(m\), with primitive height \(1/30\).
