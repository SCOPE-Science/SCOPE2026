# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The conditional two-point tetrahedral containment probability is \(\theta/(4\pi)\), derived by intersecting a straddling random segment with the common-vertex plane. The canonical order-two kernel is an explicitly centered geodesic-angle function. Funk--Hecke integration gives the entire odd harmonic spectrum, a standard canonical \(U\)-statistic limit gives the chi-squared series, and orthogonal projection gives the variance constant. The leading spectral coefficient, parity, variance sum, and conditional angular probabilities have been checked numerically with the supplied standalone script. Monte Carlo cannot replace the full proof.

## Value

PASS. Exact uncertainty calibration for the center of a three-dimensional spherical sample requires more than Wendel's expectation and cannot be obtained from a generic Gaussian approximation: the relevant fluctuations are of order \(1/n\), with a specific non-Gaussian, one-sided-support law. The result is unchanged under arbitrary positive radial rescaling, making the law useful for isotropic-direction sampling models with unknown radial noise.
