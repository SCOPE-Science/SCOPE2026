At the hopping rate that maximizes the oscillation frequency of a weakly biased, Coulomb-blockaded classical double quantum dot, stationary left-occupancy correlations develop infinitely many negative lobes for every nonzero bias, but the first negative lobe occurs only after time of order \(1/\delta\). Its deepest negative amplitude is exponentially hidden: \(\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\delta\log[-\inf_{t\ge0}C_\delta(t)]=-(1+m)[\pi/2+\arctan m]\), for reference occupation \(0<m<1\) and Fermi contrast \(\delta\).
