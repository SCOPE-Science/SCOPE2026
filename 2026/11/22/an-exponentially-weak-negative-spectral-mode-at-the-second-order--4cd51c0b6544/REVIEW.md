# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The statement is for a particular canonically defined compact self-adjoint interrupter, not for the actual self-commutator. Ma's proved operator identity specializes to a positive diagonal plus positive rank-one term minus one rank-one term. A strictly decreasing Birman–Schwinger scalar has one root because its diagonal resolvent grows logarithmically. Subtracting an explicit digamma resolvent leaves a uniformly dominated series whose exact limit is \(-1/2\); its finite part fixes the coefficient \(4e^{\gamma-2}\). The analytic argument is independent of finite experimental cutoffs. Exact rational checks of the finite columns validate the normalization, and all relevant denominators are positive for \(0<h<1\).

## Originality — PASS (with bibliographic risk)

Primary Ma Theorem 2.3 is expressly credited and already implies the defect decomposition and qualitative negative direction. The new claim is only the exact *spectral* weak-coupling law and prefactor. Rhaly's order-two paper constructs an alternate interrupter pair; the published finite Gram result addresses a weighted infimum, not the ordinary eigenvalue; the third-order negative-inertia result concerns a different order and says nothing about magnitude. The published Carleman weak-coupling result illustrates the logarithmic Birman–Schwinger mechanism but is on a different operator with a different scalar resolvent. Actual published-finding corpus and primary-literature comparisons found no stronger implication. It remains possible that an unindexed source already derives this specific order-two spectral asymptotic.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Ma's sharp hyponormality boundary is a natural spectral transition with no uniform positive gap. The finding identifies a non-analytic, smaller-than-any-power negative spectral mode and its exact prefactor, a meaningful quantitative explanation of the onset of failure of the canonical contraction factor. The precise invariant is canonical, not a tunable arbitrary slice; the coefficient distinguishes it from other logarithmic rank-one thresholds.

## Limitations

No eigenvalue asymptotic is asserted for \(M^*M-MM^*\), for the alternative Rhaly interrupter, higher integer orders, or complex shifts. The finite checker does not certify infinite-dimensional spectral convergence. Novelty has residual unindexed-literature risk; no independent audit has been performed.
