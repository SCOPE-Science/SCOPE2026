At the transcritical threshold of an immigration host–parasitoid map, a detuning \(\beta_N-\beta_*=\lambda/N\) produces a universal inverse-parasitoid clock \(N U_\lambda(t)+B e^{-c\lambda t}\log N+O(1)\), uniformly on compact positive scaled-time intervals. The logarithmic amplitude is independent of the positive initial densities and explicitly reflects the map's cubic slow dynamics.
