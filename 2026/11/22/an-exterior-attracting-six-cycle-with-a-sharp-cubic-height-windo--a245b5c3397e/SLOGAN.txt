In Everett's farthest orthogonal-projection dynamics on the complete supporting lines of an isosceles triangle, a unique attracting primitive six-cycle exists **exactly** when \(\rho<h^2<1\), where \(\rho^3+\rho^2=1\). Its two points above the apex expose a sharp periodic attractor that is invisible to the boundary-only triangle map; no six-cycle exists at or outside the stated interval.
