For the real cubic generalized logistic map \(f_\eta(x)=\eta x(1-x)^2\), the exact number of primitive real two-cycles is \(0,1,2,3\) across \(\eta\le4\), \(4<\eta<8\), \(\eta=8\), and \(\eta>8\). A second, previously unaccounted-for attracting window \(8<\eta<(13+3\sqrt{41})/4\) appears on an external cycle that straddles \(x=1\), beyond the usual population interval.
