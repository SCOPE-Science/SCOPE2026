The BHK self-mirror quartic quotient has an explicit elliptic model with four \(I_0^*\) fibers, and its coordinate-swap quotient is the extremal rational elliptic surface \(\eta^2=\xi^3+u^2\xi\) with two \(I_0^*\) fibers and Mordell–Weil group \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\). The involution fixes **two**, not one, smooth elliptic fibers, forcing Nikulin invariants \((10,8,0)\).
