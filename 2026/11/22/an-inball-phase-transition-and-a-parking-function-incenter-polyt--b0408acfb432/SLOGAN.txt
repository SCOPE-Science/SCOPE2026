The two-row generalized Pitman–Stanley polytope with supplies \((1,t)\) has a sharp Euclidean inball transition: its radius is the minimum of a row barrier and a total-supply barrier, and its optimal-center locus changes dimension from \(m-1\) to zero to \(m\). At zero second supply that locus is an affine parking-function polytope with \(2^{m-1}\) vertices and a Cayley-tree volume coefficient, even though the original polytope has only \(\binom{m+2}{2}\) vertices.
