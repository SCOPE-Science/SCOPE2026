The rank-five top integral homology of the balanced four-by-six multiple chessboard complex is rationally the \(S_6\) Specht representation \(S^{(3,3)}\), with trivial row action, but it is not integrally isomorphic to the natural kernel of the three-subset-to-two-subset incidence map. Every nonzero equivariant map from that incidence lattice into the homology has index at least \(162\), and the primitive map has quotient \((\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^3\oplus\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\).
