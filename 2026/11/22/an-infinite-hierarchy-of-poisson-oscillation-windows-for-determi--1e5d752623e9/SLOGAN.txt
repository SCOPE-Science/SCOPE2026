Synchronous incumbent-retaining plurality on a loopless complete graph has a two-cycle exactly when precisely two colors tie for the largest population, and otherwise stabilizes immediately. For uniform random initialization, infinitely many palette scales \(q\asymp n^{k/(k-1)}\), with fixed \(k\ge2\), exhibit distinct Poisson oscillation peaks, each with the same maximum probability \(2e^{-2}\).
