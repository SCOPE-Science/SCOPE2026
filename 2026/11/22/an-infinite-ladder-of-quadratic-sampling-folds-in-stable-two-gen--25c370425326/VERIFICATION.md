# Verification evidence and boundaries

The local analytic theorem is proved via the first two variational equations of a planar flow, exact Laplace-trigonometric integrals and the implicit function theorem. The exceptional curvature condition \(K=0\) is excluded explicitly.

The standalone `check_fold.py` verified symbolic half-rotation integrals at \(k=1,2,3,4\), reconstructed the first/second logistic variational derivatives by independent numerical integration to better than \(4\times10^{-12}\) in absolute error, and found two distinct positive hidden initial concentrations with the same later output at the nonresonant time \(T=\pi+0.02\). These computations do not certify all possible parameters or time indices; the unrestricted claims come from the proof.

The observation is only a pair of first-coordinate values, not the complete continuous-time trace. The finite-precision equal-output numerical pair is not used as the logical basis for exact noninjectivity. No independent review has been performed.
