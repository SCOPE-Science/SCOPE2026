# Review of the directed three-cycle fractional semigroup theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The three-by-three Fourier formula is exact for principal spectral powers of \(I-P\). The sign transition reduces to a strictly unimodal damped cosine on each consecutive lobe. Differentiation of \(H_n\) proves unique critical parameters, and the strict inequality plus an arithmetic progression of lobe centers gives the ceiling count including equality boundaries. This also verifies the cyclic ordering of negative components and the \(\alpha=3\) permutation-time exception. The attached checker passed 1,422 supplementary assertions, including independent matrix-exponential comparisons and threshold tests; no finite experiment is used as proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Benzi, Bertaccini, Durastante and Simunec, *Nonlocal network dynamics via fractional graph Laplacians*, arXiv:1912.07288, establish the directed-cycle fractional spectral setting and discuss the Markovian orders. Daners and Glück, *A criterion for the uniform eventual positivity of operator semigroups*, arXiv:1801.05179, prove a general spectral qualitative eventual-positivity criterion. Neither inspected full-text section provides the high-order three-cycle window count, its infinite ladder of critical orders, or both singular constants. Published records of rational heat methods on directed cycles and inverse averaged transitions are distinct in their matrix functions. A specialized historic circulant-matrix-functions treatment remains a possible source of equivalent priority; the closest inverse-averaging record was accessible only in summary form.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This gives a complete exact time-sign census for the smallest directed nonreversible Laplacian, not just eventual positivity. The sharp first diagonal reentry, cyclic ordering, infinitely many new intervals at a finite spectral boundary, and two explicit blow-up coefficients distinguish damping, transient order positivity, and long-time recovery. The result may serve as a quantitative test example for positive-semigroup and fractional directed-network analyses.

**Limits.** Unit-rate, one-way three-cycle only; principal spectral powers \(0<\alpha\le3\); no independent validation, no priority guarantee, and no implication for arbitrary directed weighted graphs. Numerical checks are not an exhaustive proof.
