On the unit-rate directed three-cycle, the fractional semigroup \(e^{-t(I-P)^\alpha}\) has exactly \(\lceil\frac{3}{2\pi}(\arctan d+\frac{\log(2/\sqrt{1+d^2})}{d}-\frac\pi3)\rceil\) disjoint negative-entry time windows for \(1<\alpha<3\), where \(d=\cot(\pi\alpha/6)\), and their number diverges as \(9\log2/[\pi^2(3-\alpha)]\). These windows appear at a strictly ordered sequence of critical exponents, while at order three the semigroup is periodic and nonnegative only at isolated permutation times.
