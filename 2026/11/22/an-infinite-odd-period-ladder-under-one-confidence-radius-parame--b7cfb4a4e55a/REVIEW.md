# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The exact five-state HK update reduces, only on the explicitly invariant diagonal and while all four anchor opinions remain fixed, to \(F_r(t)=(t+9)/5\) below the threshold and \(F_r(t)=t/2\) above it. Solving the affine return equation after \(m\) full/partial update pairs and one final partial update yields the displayed unique rational phase coordinates. Strict inequalities prove both the primitive length and the open radius windows for the specified itinerary. The product of the phase derivatives proves fiberwise local exponential attraction. The standalone rational checker tests 90 full-five-agent trajectories, although those finite experiments do not establish the infinite theorem.

## Originality

PASS within the inspected literature and recorded searches. Proskurnikov–Zabarianska (arXiv:2611.21656, sections 2–4, especially Example 1, Theorem 8 and Corollary 10) use these anchor locations for one period-two example and obtain arbitrary periods by changing geometric configurations; their finite coexistence result keeps radii fixed but varies the positions. The present result fixes the four anchor positions and all their positive radii and varies only the fifth radius, with exact disjoint, accumulating rational windows and multipliers. Granados–Alsedà–Krupa (arXiv:1407.1895, sections 2–4 and 8) supply broad period-adding theory for piecewise contractions, not the specific HK embedding or these exact windows. Similar published opinion-cycle records permit nonconvex confidence sets or change the geometry and do not mathematically imply the one-radius classification. The possibility of an unindexed equivalent calculation or a more direct application of general period-adding theory remains a genuine priority limitation.

## Value

PASS. The constructive result supplies an exact single-parameter bifurcation ladder for a natural minimally sized system with the usual positive-ball confidence rule: every odd period \(2m+1\), a nonempty robust parameter window, its explicit geometric shrinking width, and its exact attraction multiplier. It quantifies the parameter precision required to access long cycles without readjusting the other opinions. It is substantially more than a single ad hoc periodic trajectory or a finite simulation.

## Limitations and related literature

The argument establishes only the displayed family of itineraries, not a complete period census. It excludes equality at the threshold from the robust interval and proves stability only with the four anchors held fixed. Earlier general theory of piecewise contractions and undocumented specialized calculations remain possible sources of overlap; independent mathematical validation is not performed.
