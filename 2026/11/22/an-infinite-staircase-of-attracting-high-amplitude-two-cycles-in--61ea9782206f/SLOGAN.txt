In the symmetric three-dimensional sine-delay map, every real primitive two-cycle in the unique-fixed-point strip is antipodal. On its noninvertible parameter sheet, infinitely many increasingly high sine lobes create sharp attracting two-cycle windows whose widths obey \(9/(2m_k)+O(m_k^{-2})\), where \(m_k=(2k+\tfrac12)\pi\); the previously known low-gain cycles do not account for these high-lobe windows.
