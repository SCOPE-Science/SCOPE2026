Testing the final success against that prefix gives the joint side-time transform as f_j(s) times the sum over m of E[exp(-s S_m); N>m]. Success has constant probability p, so N is geometric and the sum is finite, but its individual nonzero-s summands need not be powers. Testing arbitrary bounded functions gives independence of stopped prefix and successful exploration; the common factor cancels between normalized sides. The killed Dirichlet weights are proportional to sinh(b sqrt((r+s)/D)) and sinh(a sqrt((r+s)/D)); the ratio is the Laplace transform of the independent interval-(0,b) hitting time at b from a, conditioned to hit b first and exponentially tilted at r. This gives the convolution by uniqueness. The individual sinh-product factors are Bernoulli–exponential transforms with a summable expectation and positive variance; the Lévy sum is integrable against t by Tonelli and has strictly positive density on t>0. Differentiating the locally convergent log product yields the cumulants and strict complete monotonicity of the mean; the zeta sum and coth formula yield both endpoint limits.
