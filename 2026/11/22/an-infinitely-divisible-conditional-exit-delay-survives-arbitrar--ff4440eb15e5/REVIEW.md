# Scientific scope of the revised finding

Every new exploration and its clock are independent of the complete history preceding it. No multiplication of identically distributed failed-cycle transforms is needed for the general result. Conditional on the prefix through any given number of failures, the next attempt's joint success transform has its fixed one-attempt value; summing over the number of failures shows that the full stopped prefix is independent of the final successful exploration. Thus the same history factor cancels in the two conditional transforms. The residual ratio represents a positive tilted hitting law, and its spectral product defines a drift-free infinitely divisible law.

The common current factor for instantaneous resetting and the classical hyperbolic spectral law are already available from Pal et al. and Pitman–Yor, respectively. Invariance of splitting probabilities for a prescribed finite return is also known. The contribution is the full independent-delay relation, uniform over the specified history-dependent return mechanisms. Bressloff's multi-target finite-return original was not available in full during the review, so equivalent coverage there is not excluded. No claim is made about future-dependent returns or target absorption during return.
