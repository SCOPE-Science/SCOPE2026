In the classical independent-endpoint random interval graph, simplicial vertices in any fixed interior left-endpoint band have an asymptotically Poisson count with mean \(\log\!\bigl(\beta(1-\alpha)/(\alpha(1-\beta))\bigr)\), and their rescaled interval lengths form an explicit inhomogeneous marked Poisson process. An exact finite-sample endpoint-order integral underpins the limit and yields a logarithmic lower bound for the global expected simplicial count.
