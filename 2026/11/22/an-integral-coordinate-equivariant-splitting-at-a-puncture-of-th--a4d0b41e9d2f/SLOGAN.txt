For every binary cube of dimension \(n\ge3\), removing the zero vertex at Hamming Rips scale two induces an integrally split exact sequence of coordinate-permutation modules whose quotient is \(\bigwedge^3\mathbb Z^n\), with a canonical section realized by oriented coordinate three-cubes. This signed lattice has no invariant rational classes, so the full coordinate-permutation quotient preserves its third rational homology under the puncture.
