For every finite named parameter poset in the countable random partial order, the nonalgebraic one-type incomparability-feasibility matrix splits integrally into one positive unit, \(j(A)-1\) hyperbolic planes and a primitive nullspace indexed by the genuinely two-sided types, where \(j(A)\) is the number of down-sets. Consequently all nonzero Smith factors equal one, every field rank is \(2j(A)-1\), and the real signature is always one.
