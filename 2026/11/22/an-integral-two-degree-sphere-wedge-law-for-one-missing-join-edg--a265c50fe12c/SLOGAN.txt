Removing one cross-edge from the join of a matching of \(m\ge2\) edges with \(b\ge2\) independent vertices changes its induced-forest complex to exactly \((b-1)\) two-spheres wedged with \((b-1)\) spheres of dimension \(m\), integrally. The collapse of the universal sphere multiplicity follows from a primitive cycle basis in the vertex-link attachment, not from finite Betti-number experiments.
