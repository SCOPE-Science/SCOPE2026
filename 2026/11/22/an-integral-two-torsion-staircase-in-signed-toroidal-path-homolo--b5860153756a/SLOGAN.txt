For a bidirected \(r\)-dimensional toroidal cycle grid with one negatively signed coordinate seam, the integral signed path-homology chain complex has exactly \(\binom{r-1}{q}\) independent two-torsion classes in degree \(q\), for \(0\le q<r\), and none otherwise. Every one of these classes disappears over the reals, even though the unsigned covering graph has torsion-free homology.
