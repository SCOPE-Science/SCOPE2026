Under uniform-history quantum resetting, an inverse-square-root spectral-edge singularity changes long-time ensemble purity from ordinary \(1/\sqrt{\log(rt)}\) relaxation to a sharp \(\log\log(rt)/\sqrt{\log(rt)}\) law. Stronger integrable edge divergences have exact continuously varying powers and beta-function coefficients, revealing a threshold invisible to bounded-density purity formulas.
