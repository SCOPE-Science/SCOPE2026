For multiplicity-weighted matched-vertex sets in \(K_{3,n}\), Boolean counting convolution is singular only at the exceptional widths \(n=1\) and \(n=43\). At \(n=43\) its nullity is exactly \(6\binom{43}{16}\), and its sharp nonzero Walsh gap is \(2\); the corresponding edge-incidence convolution obeys a fundamentally different all-odd-width singularity law.
