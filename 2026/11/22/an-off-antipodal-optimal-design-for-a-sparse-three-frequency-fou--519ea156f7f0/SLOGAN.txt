For the sparse Fourier triple \(\{0,1,3\}\), two shrinking equal observation arcs are asymptotically most stable at a center separation of \(2\arccos\sqrt{(\sqrt{154}-8)/20}\), not at antipodality. This exact non-antipodal design improves the worst-observed energy by a sharp factor of \(1/2+\sqrt{154}/7\) over opposite arcs.
