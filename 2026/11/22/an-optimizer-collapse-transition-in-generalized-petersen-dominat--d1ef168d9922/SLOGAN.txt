Minimum \([1,2]\)-domination in two consecutive generalized Petersen graphs shows a sharp collapse despite unchanged optimum cardinality six: \(\operatorname{GP}(8,2)\) has \(248\) optima, VC dimension \(5\), and Littlestone dimension \(6\), whereas \(\operatorname{GP}(9,2)\) has just nine optima, VC dimension \(2\), and Littlestone dimension \(2\). The former has a nonpure shattered-support complex with two dihedral orbits of maximum faces; the latter is exactly the complete bipartite shattering complex \(K_{9,9}\).
