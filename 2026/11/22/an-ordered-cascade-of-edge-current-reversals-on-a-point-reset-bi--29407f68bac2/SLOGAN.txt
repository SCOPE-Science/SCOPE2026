On a finite clockwise-biased nearest-neighbor Markov ring reset repeatedly to one point, the local hopping currents reverse direction in a strict, spatially ordered cascade: exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) edges reverse once each as reset rate grows, while all others remain clockwise. Every threshold is simple, and weak bias gives \(r_j\sim 2(p-q)/(2j+1-n)\).
