For every infinite locally finite connected graph, the essential norm of a bounded graph-Lipschitz multiplication operator is at least \(\max\{A(\psi),B(\psi)\}\) on both its big and little Lipschitz spaces; the oscillation coefficient is sharp even on graphs with cycles. If \(\psi\) vanishes at infinity, the essential norm is exactly \(B(\psi)\), resolving the missing oscillation-sensitive lower bound for this class.
