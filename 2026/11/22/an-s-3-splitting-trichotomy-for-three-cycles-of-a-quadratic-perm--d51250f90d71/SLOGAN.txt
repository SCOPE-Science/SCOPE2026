For Chen's natural quadratic permutation \(F(x,y)=((x+y)^2+x,(x+y)^2-y)\), every finite field of characteristic at least five has either zero or exactly two primitive three-cycles, with the outcome controlled by one \(S_3\)-Frobenius class. The three possible first extension degrees are \(1,2,3\) with prime densities \(1/6,1/2,1/3\), and every such prime admits both cycles over its degree-six extension.
