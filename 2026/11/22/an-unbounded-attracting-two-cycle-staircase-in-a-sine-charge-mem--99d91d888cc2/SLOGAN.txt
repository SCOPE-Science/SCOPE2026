In a published sine-charge memristive map, the number of inequivalent hyperbolically attracting period-two cycles at zero input gain grows like \(1/[2\pi\sqrt{h-1/2}]\) as the coupling \(h\) approaches \(1/2\) from above. Each finite collection survives at sufficiently small strictly positive input gain, proving arbitrarily large coexistence of attracting period-two orbits in the full three-dimensional family.
