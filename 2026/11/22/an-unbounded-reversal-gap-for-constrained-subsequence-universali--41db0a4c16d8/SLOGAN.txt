For all \(d\ge2\) and \(k\ge1\), the minimal complete DFA sizes for binary \(k\)-subsequence-universal words avoiding the contiguous factors \(0^d1\) and \(10^d\) are exactly \((d+2)k+d+1\) and \((d+2)k+2\), respectively. Although the languages are reversals and have equal lengthwise counts, their left-to-right state complexities have an unbounded difference of exactly \(d-1\).
