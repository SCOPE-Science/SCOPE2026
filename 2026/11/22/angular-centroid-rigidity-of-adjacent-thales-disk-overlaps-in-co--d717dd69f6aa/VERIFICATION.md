## Mathematical verification
The exact two-circle intersection-sector identity is proved in RESULT.md, including configurations with obtuse fan angles. The finite checker independently evaluates standard arccosine intersection areas and compares against the proposed polygon-wide expression. It uses no external packages or historical output files.

## Limits
A computer test of finite polygons cannot prove an all-polygons identity; the proof relies on fan angles and area decomposition. The result does not cover polygon-clipped lenses or all-pair overlap counts for arbitrary polygons. Independent mathematical auditing has not taken place.
