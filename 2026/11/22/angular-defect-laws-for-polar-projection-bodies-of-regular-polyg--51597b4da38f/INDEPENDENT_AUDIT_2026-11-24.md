## correctness

For the corrected metric-fit claim, central symmetry gives the projection body as twice a quarter-turn; the regular polar has vertex radius sec(alpha) and is offset by a half vertex step. Thus for 4|N the half-sector supports are p=2 cos u and q=cos(alpha−u)/c, 0≤u≤alpha; for N≡2 (mod 4) there is the known exact homothety. The square cosine and cosine-product integrals on a half-sector are (alpha+sc)/2 and (alpha c+s)/2, respectively. After multiplying by 2N and the support factors, the positive optimizer is C/B and its error A−C²/B reduces by the displayed difference-of-squares factorization. At scale 2c the signed uniform error decreases between the two opposite endpoint errors ±2(1−c); any other scale increases one of those endpoint magnitudes. This gives the unique Hausdorff scale and shows it exceeds the least-squares scale by a positive multiple of (1−c)(alpha−s). The asymptotics use alpha²−s²=alpha⁴/3+O(alpha⁶), alpha+sc=2alpha+O(alpha³), and 1−c=alpha²/2+O(alpha⁴). These proofs were checked for the exact supports, unnormalized dθ, and positive scalar domain. The removed angular formulas were mathematically valid as well: folding to a uniform half-sector and inverting 2c/(c+s tan u) gives their CDF, density, and uniform rescaled limit; removal is for prior coverage, not falsity.
