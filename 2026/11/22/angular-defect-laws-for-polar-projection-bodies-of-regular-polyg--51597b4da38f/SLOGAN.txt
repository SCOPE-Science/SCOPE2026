For centered regular even-sided polygons, the known planar polar-projection parity dichotomy admits a complete quantitative refinement: the nonexact branch has an explicit directional-ratio distribution, a uniform scaling limit, and sharp distinct least-squares and Hausdorff homotheties. The least-squares residual is asymptotic to \(2\pi^5/(3N^4)\) in the unit-circumradius normalization, while the \(N\equiv2\pmod4\) branch vanishes identically.
