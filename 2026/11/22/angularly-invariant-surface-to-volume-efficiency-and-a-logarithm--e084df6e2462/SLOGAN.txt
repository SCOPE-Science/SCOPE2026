For two intersecting infinite solid circular cylinders, the smaller-radius-normalized surface-to-volume ratio is independent of their crossing angle and decreases strictly from \(3\) to \(2\) as their radii become more unequal. The exact shape law has a sharp positive \(\tfrac34\eta^2\log(1/\eta)\) correction at equality, creating a non-\(C^2\) logarithmic cusp.
