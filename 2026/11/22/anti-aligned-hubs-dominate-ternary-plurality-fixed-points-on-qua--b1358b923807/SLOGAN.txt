In synchronous three-opinion unique-plurality dynamics on a quadrilateral book of \(m\) square pages, the exact stationary-state count has the sharp expansion \(E_m=6^{m+1}-12c(1+2\sqrt6)^m/\sqrt m+o((1+2\sqrt6)^m/\sqrt m)\), with an explicit positive constant \(c\). Although both hubs use the same conformist update rule, almost every fixed configuration assigns them different opinions; this is a fixed-state statement, not an attraction-basin result.
