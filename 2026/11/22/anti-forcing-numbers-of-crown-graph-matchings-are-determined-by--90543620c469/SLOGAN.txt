For each perfect matching \(M_\pi\) of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus its diagonal matching, the anti-forcing number equals \(n(n-3)/2+c(\pi)\), where \(c(\pi)\) is the number of cycles in the derangement \(\pi\). Consequently its complete anti-forcing polynomial is \(z^{n(n-3)/2}n![u^n]e^{-zu}(1-u)^{-z}\), and the minimum deletion sets arise from linear orders reversing every forbidden cycle except for one arc.
