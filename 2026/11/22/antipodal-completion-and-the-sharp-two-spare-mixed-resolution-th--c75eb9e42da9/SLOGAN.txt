The icosahedral graph has exactly twenty minimum mixed metric generators: each consists of both endpoints of three of its six antipodal vertex pairs, and the two induced geometries are ten six-cycles and ten disjoint-triangle pairs. The complete mixed-resolution polynomial is \(20z^6+240z^7+405z^8+220z^9+66z^{10}+12z^{11}+z^{12}\), and exactly two temporary spare landmarks suffice to connect all optima by failure-free additions and removals.
