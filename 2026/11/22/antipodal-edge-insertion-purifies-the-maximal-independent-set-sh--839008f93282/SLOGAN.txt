Adding the eight antipodal edges to the sixteen-vertex cube makes its maximal-independent-set shattering complex pure: the folded five-cube shatters exactly its independent sets of at most four vertices, with polynomial \(1+16z+80z^2+160z^3+120z^4\). The original cube instead has eight maximal shattered pairs and only 104 maximal shattered quadruples, so the edge insertion raises the top-face count while eliminating every lower-dimensional facet.
