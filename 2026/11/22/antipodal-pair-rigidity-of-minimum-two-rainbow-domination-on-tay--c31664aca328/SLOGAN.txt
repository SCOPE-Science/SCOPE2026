For every Taylor graph with intersection array \(\{k,\mu,1;1,\mu,k\}\) satisfying \(k\ge5\) and \(3\le\mu\le k-2\), the optimum named two-rainbow assignments are exactly ordered choices of two antipodal pairs, giving \((k+1)^2\) optima. The exceptional icosahedron satisfies the same rigidity by an additional geometric argument; it has 36 optima, while \(J(6,3)\) has 100.
