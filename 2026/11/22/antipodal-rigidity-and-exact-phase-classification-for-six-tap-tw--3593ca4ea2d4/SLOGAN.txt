Every tight full-translation, two-modulation cyclic Gabor frame generated by six consecutive unimodular taps at order \(N\ge12\) has antipodal modulation separation, and its complete design space is exactly two three-tori of explicit complementary-pair windows. A \(q\)-root phase alphabet permits such tightness precisely when \(4\mid q\), with exactly \(2q^3\) indexed windows; the quaternary Golay-pair count itself was previously known.
