For the dodecahedral graph, a vertex set equalizes every external pair of distances exactly when it meets each of the ten antipodal pairs, so the full polynomial is \((2z+z^2)^{10}\) and the minimum is \(10\) with \(1024\) bases. These bases occupy \(20\) symmetry orbits and reconfigure as \(Q_{10}\) under token jumps, but none can make a legal token slide.
