A uniform-regeneration circle-copula chain can have arbitrarily many infinite relative-mixing coefficients even when its one-step maximal correlation is arbitrarily small. For its power-cusp generator, the first finite \(\psi\)-coefficient occurs exactly at lag \(\lfloor(1-a)^{-1}\rfloor+1\), with exact even-lag Fourier formulas and an explicit critical blow-up.
