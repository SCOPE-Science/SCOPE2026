Maximal mixing of every frozen two-state plant does not limit the complexity of a coupled adaptive learner: with strictly positive rank-one transition kernels and one fixed strongly convex quadratic loss, the coupled system can have a unique locally attracting equilibrium while supporting a compact subsystem of exact entropy \(\tfrac12\log m\) for any integer \(m\ge2\). Consequently there is no finite universal adaptive topological-entropy bound from frozen mixing, convexity, and equilibrium uniqueness alone.
