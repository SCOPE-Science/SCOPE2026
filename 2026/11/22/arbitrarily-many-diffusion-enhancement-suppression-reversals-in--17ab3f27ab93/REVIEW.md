# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The proof starts from the uniformly elliptic Markov generator on the circle and its unique invariant density, reconstructs the corrector martingale formula, and expands the stationary density and corrector to first order. Fourier orthogonality gives the exact second-order coefficient \(H(t)\). The induction adds a high odd harmonic of weight \(k^{3/2}\), with a uniform bound at each *finite* set of old sign witnesses and explicit negative/positive new witnesses; small-amplitude analyticity then transfers the finite sign pattern to the true diffusion coefficient, and continuity guarantees distinct crossings. Four two-mode rational witnesses are checked exactly, and an independent Galerkin solution corroborates their physical signs. The proof does not mistake numerical approximation for the crossing theorem.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest prior theory already derives general effective-diffusion representations for stochastic periodic forces (Frydel 2026) and first-passage equations for supersymmetric switching potentials (Spagnolo–Dubkov 2005). The latter single-cell diffusion estimate is not exact in general, as Frydel explains. Both have been compared at their equation and application level; neither inspected source states arbitrarily many alternating enhancement–suppression windows constructed from smooth multiscale profiles. Published analytic weak-potential results for fixed potentials with additive telegraph propulsion and Fourier inversion with an independent active driver have different generators and do not imply this statement. An inaccessible closely related 2005 journal full text and possible unindexed deductions from the general formulas remain risks; this is a best-knowledge assessment, not a literature-priority certificate.

## Value — PASS
For smooth periodically flipped landscapes, the simple single-scale picture—suppression at slow switching and enhancement at sufficiently rapid switching—does not place a universal bound on distinct transport-enhancement windows. This is a structural limitation relevant to designing or interpreting multiscale ratchets and to inferring landscape complexity from rate-dependent diffusion scans. The theorem is an exact existence/boundary result at finite weak forcing, rather than a genericity or experimental robustness claim.

## Scientific limits
The existence proof provides neither a lower bound for admissible amplitudes nor a sharp count of zeros, and its potential frequencies and amplitudes grow with the requested number of alternations. The exact diffusive limit is taken before the small-amplitude limit, at fixed positive switching rates. General diffusion formulas and weak-potential perturbation methods are established prior mathematics. Independent scientific assessment of novelty remains outstanding.
