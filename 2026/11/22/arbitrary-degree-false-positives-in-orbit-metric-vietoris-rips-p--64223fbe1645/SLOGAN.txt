Free cyclic symmetry can make the quotient-distance Vietoris–Rips algorithm invent a homology sphere in every degree \(d\ge2\), even while the true filtered-groupoid Borel space is only a graph at that scale. A uniform family of finite two-distance metrics gives the exact all-field persistence barcodes and rules out a universal higher-degree analogue of the known no-overcount guarantee in degree one.
