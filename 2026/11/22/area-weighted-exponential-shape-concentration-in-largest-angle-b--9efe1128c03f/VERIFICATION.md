The new conditional probability estimate uses only the angle-bisector theorem, the sine law and the minimum-angle bound. Its finite-level stochastic domination is proved for *dependent* pairs by conditioning, not by presuming independent area-weighted choices. For each fixed positive threshold, the decrement is the positive minimum of a continuous function on a specified nonempty compact set. No numerical sampling establishes an infinite claim.
