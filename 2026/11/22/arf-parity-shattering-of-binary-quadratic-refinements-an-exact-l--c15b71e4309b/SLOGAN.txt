The VC-shattered supports for binary quadratic refinements of one symplectic form have an exact orthogonal-complement criterion: nonisotropic kernels work for both Arf parities, but Lagrangian kernels work only for even Arf parity. Their complete shattering polynomials differ in exactly degree \(m\), by \(\lvert\mathrm{GL}(m,2)\rvert\prod_{i=1}^m(2^i+1)/m!\), although both VC dimensions are \(2m-2\) for every \(m\ge2\).
