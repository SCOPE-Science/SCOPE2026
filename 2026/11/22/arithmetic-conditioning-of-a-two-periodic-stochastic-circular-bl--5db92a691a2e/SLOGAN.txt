A positive, two-periodic, three-tap circular blur has an exact determinant \(-4^{1-q}(ab)^q\sin^2(\pi q\alpha)\), so a rational phase creates precisely two invisible directions on resonant grids while every irrational phase remains finite-grid invertible. Its sharp deconvolution condition number grows as a constant times \(q/\|q\alpha\|_{\mathbb Z}\), giving linear rational, quadratic badly approximable, and potentially superpolynomial Liouville instability.
