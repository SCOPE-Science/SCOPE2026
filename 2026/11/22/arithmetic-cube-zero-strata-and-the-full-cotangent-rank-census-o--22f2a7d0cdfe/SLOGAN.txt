The full endomorphism monoid of the cubic box algebra \(\mathbb F_q[x,y,z]/(x^3,y^3,z^3)\) has an explicit four-level cotangent-rank census. Outside characteristic three, its singular fibers contain a quadratic-character correction \(\chi_q(6)\), while in characteristic three all nilpotent generator images are cube-zero and the census becomes a finite-matrix rank distribution.
