The genus-\(1777\) modular curve \(X_H(4800)\) is an explicit quadratic cover of \(X_0(4800)\) given by \(w^2=t^2+22t+125\), where \(t=(\eta(z)/\eta(5z))^6\). Its \(288\) geometric cusps form exactly \(70\) rational Galois orbits, including \(16\) rational cusps, and the cover is determined by the nonzero rational two-torsion divisor class \([D-\nu^*(\infty)]\).
