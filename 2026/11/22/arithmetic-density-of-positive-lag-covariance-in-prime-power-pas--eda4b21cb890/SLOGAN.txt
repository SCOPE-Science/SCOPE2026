For every prime \(p\) and prime-power Pascal column \(\binom n{p^a}\), the shifts with positive covariance between successive shifted \(p\)-adic valuations have an exact rational natural density, even though their individual covariances oscillate in sign. As the column exponent grows, these densities converge at error at most \(p^{-a-1}\) to a universal Lambert-series constant depending only on \(p\).
