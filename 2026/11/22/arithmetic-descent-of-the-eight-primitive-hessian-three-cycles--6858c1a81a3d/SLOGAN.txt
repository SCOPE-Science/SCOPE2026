The eight complex primitive three-cycles of the Hessian self-map of the elliptic \(j\)-line split into Galois orbits of sizes \(2,2,4\), so none is a rationally defined unordered cycle. The first two pairs descend over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\); each of the remaining four cycles has a degree-four field with dihedral Galois closure \(D_4\).
