The split tangent cubic \(xy(y-x)=zw(w-z)\) and the nonsplit \(\mathbb F_5\)-model \(x(x^2-2y^2)=z(z^2-2w^2)\) become isomorphic over \(\mathbb F_{25}\), but have respectively 15 and 3 rational lines over \(\mathbb F_5\). Their saturated arithmetic Picard lattices have ranks 6 and 4 and discriminants \(-2\) and \(-8\), determining their distinct complete zeta functions.
