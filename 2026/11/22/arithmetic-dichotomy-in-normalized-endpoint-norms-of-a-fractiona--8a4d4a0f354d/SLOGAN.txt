For the fractional unilateral-shift semigroup \(e^{-t(I-S)^\alpha}\), \(1<\alpha<2\), the ratio of the sharp \(\ell^1\) operator norm to its exact \(\ell^2\) norm tends to an explicit constant for irrational \(\alpha\), but approaches an explicit nonconstant periodic profile for rational \(\alpha\). Two conjugate Gaussian saddles travel with equal speed, and their arithmetic phase interference explains the dichotomy.
