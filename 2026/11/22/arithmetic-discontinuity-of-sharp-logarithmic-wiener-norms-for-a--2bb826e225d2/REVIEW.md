# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS (self-review).** For every fixed \(0<\alpha<1\), the two-arc Fourier integration gives a fully explicit coefficient with the correct normalizing factor and with continuous values at both exceptional frequencies. A moving-pole cancellation identity controls small denominators without any Diophantine assumption. Uniform block Cesàro averages of the absolute-sine sequence, followed by Abel summation, supply four logarithmic contributions; the complement is uniformly bounded. Periodicity yields the rational \(O_\alpha(1)\) remainder. Direct piecewise integration checks and the exact symmetric-tent harmonic-number identity provide independent finite consistency checks, but not a substitute for the limiting argument.

## Originality

**PASS relative to inspected sources, with residual historical risk.** Ohrysko [1] proves logarithmic rigidity and gives the symmetric tent coefficient and order \(\asymp\log n\), not the asymmetric sharp coefficient. Lebedev [2,3] provides the nonaffine piecewise-linear growth scale and a weaker-space analogue, not a rational/irrational denominator law. Targeted searches in mathematical literature and indexed research records for two-kink Wiener norms, exact logarithmic coefficients and arithmetic formulations did not identify an implication covering the claimed sharp formula. The original 1970 Kahane chapter remains uninspected and could contain a precursor. The novelty conclusion is literature-relative, not a certification of global uniqueness.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** Piecewise-affine powers are the canonical sharpness witnesses for the logarithmic Beurling–Helson/Kahane boundary. Determining their precise limiting \(A(\mathbb T)\) norm is a natural missing quantitative invariant: the denominator-only rational law and the sharp irrational plateau reveal arithmetic sensitivity invisible in existing two-sided estimates. The statement covers all kink positions in a canonical one-parameter family and includes a rigorous bounded-error refinement at rational parameters. It does not claim a general theorem for arbitrary piecewise-affine maps.

## Closest literature and limits

The closest primary source is [1, Example 8.1], supported by [2] and [3]. Kahane's original 1970 monograph is a material residual coverage risk because only its descriptions in those later full texts were inspected. A direct exact two-kink calculation in older unindexed literature cannot be ruled out. The irrational remainder is only \(o_\alpha(\log n)\) and need not be uniform in the parameter.
