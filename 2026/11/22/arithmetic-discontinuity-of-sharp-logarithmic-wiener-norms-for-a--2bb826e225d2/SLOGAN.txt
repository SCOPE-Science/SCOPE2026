The sharp logarithmic Wiener norm of powers of an asymmetric two-slope tent phase has an arithmetic discontinuity: its leading coefficient is \(4\cot(\pi/(2q))/(\pi q)\) at rational kink fractions of reduced denominator \(q\), but \(8/\pi^2\) at every irrational kink fraction. The rational constants increase to the irrational one, and every rational case has a bounded remainder after subtracting its logarithmic main term.
