At half-degree excitation, a connected even-regular graph with integral adjacency spectrum and vertex count not divisible by three can recur only in a four-cycle, and such cycles are counted exactly by balanced \(\pm1\) adjacency-nullvectors on bipartite graphs. Consequently an infinite family of triangle-free odd Kneser graphs \(KG(8\cdot3^a-1,4\cdot3^a-1)\) becomes entirely resting from every initial state despite retaining a critical resonant adjacency eigenvalue.
