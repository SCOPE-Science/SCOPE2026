The two nine-element geometric free flex orbits of the uniquely cyclic-nine plane quartic \(X^4+XY^3+YZ^3=0\) form a single irreducible degree-eighteen closed point over \(\mathbb Q\). Its full reduced Weierstrass support has residue degrees \(1,1,1,2,18\), with an explicit mod-seven Frobenius eighteen-cycle proving the arithmetic fusion.
