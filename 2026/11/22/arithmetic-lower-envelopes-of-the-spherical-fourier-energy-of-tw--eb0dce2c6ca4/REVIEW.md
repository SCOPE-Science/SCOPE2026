## Value

**PASS.** The high-frequency normalized spherical mean is a natural sharp-decay invariant. The theorem cleanly separates smooth disconnected domains with a uniform lower energy floor from those admitting sequences with vanishing normalized energy, in every dimension. The closed value \(31/64\) for two three-dimensional balls with radius ratio \(1:2\) makes the distinction quantitative rather than merely qualitative. This is a complete motivated two-object classification; no general-domain theorem is claimed.
