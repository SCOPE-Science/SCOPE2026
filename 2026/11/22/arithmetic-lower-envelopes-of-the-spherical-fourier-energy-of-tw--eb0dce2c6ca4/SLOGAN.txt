For the spherical Fourier energy of two separated balls, the high-frequency lower envelope vanishes at irrational radius ratios, but at a reduced rational ratio \(p/q\) it vanishes exactly when \(4\mid(p-q)(d+3)\). In three dimensions, radii in the ratio \(1:2\) instead give a sharp positive normalized liminf of \(31/64\), despite each ball separately having arbitrarily small normalized energy.
