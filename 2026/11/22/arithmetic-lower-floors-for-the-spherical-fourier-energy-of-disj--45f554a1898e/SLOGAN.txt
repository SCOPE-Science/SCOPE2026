For a finite union of disjoint Euclidean balls in dimension at least two, an exact rational-relation test decides whether the spherical squared Fourier transform stays above a positive multiple of \(R^{-d-1}\) at every sufficiently large real frequency radius. For two balls with coprime radius ratio \(p:q\), the floor exists precisely when \(4\nmid(p-q)(d+3)\); the planar ratio \(1:2\) has an explicit sharp positive coefficient.
