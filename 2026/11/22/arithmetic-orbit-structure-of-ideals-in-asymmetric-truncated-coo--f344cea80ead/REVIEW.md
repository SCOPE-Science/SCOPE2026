The single claim is the complete finite-field orbit partition of ideals of \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(xy,x^m,y^n)\) for \(3\le m<n\), with the fixed-ideal count, its dimension refinement, and the binary four-exception and asymptotic consequences. Each proper ideal has either a pair of pure branch tails or one diagonal line in the two boundary degrees; the exhaustive normal form is independently derived in RESULT.md and is also present in the cited prior ideal-classification theorem. All automorphisms have independent branch scalings and jets plus two cross-branch socle shears, as independently justified and also supplied by the cited prior automorphism-classification theorem. The only nontrivial orbit calculations are two additive shear orbits, and the image of a two-character map on the cyclic group \(\mathbb F_q^\times\), whose index is \(\gcd(u,v,q-1)\). There are no other orbits. Exact sizes and dimension counts are summed with the whole-ring boundary and the two excluded mixed pairs handled explicitly. Prime-field exhaustive ideal and automorphism-action enumeration in the bundled checker scripts agrees in six settings; its finite reach is not confused with the proof for all prime powers.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The orbit partition is an exact structural invariant of a standard family of Artinian local rings, not a single arbitrary numerical slice. In the smallest coefficient field, every permissible pair of truncation lengths has *exactly four* noncharacteristic ideals, even as the full ideal lattice grows quadratically. In larger fields, nontrivial orbit sizes are governed by arithmetic common divisors and the fraction of invariant ideals has a distinct exact asymptotic \(1/(q-1)\). The simultaneous orbit, fixed-point, and ideal-dimension enumerators provide natural reference data for finite-ring ideal actions and equivariant ideal-count problems. No downstream theorem beyond this complete classification is asserted.
