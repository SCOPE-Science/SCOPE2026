Every ideal orbit of the asymmetric truncated node \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(xy,x^m,y^n)\), for \(3\le m<n\), is determined by two socle-shear orbits and explicit gcd-controlled slope orbits. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), exactly four ideals are moved by an automorphism, independently of both truncation lengths; for fixed \(q\), the fraction of characteristic ideals approaches \(1/(q-1)\).
