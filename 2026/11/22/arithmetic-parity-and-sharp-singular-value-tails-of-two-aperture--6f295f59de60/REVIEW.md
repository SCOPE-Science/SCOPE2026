# Scientific review: two-aperture circle convolution

## Correctness — PASS
For the exact domain \(0<a<1/3\), irrational \(a\), and \(a<b<1-a\), Fourier expansion yields \(|\widehat K(n)|=2|\sin(\pi na)\cos(\pi nb)|/(\pi|n|)\), with each positive frequency represented twice. The block-counting lemma converts the empirical mean of the bounded numerator into the sharp decreasing singular-value coefficient. Rational displacement gives the elementary cotangent/cosecant finite sums after averaging \(|\sin|\) on each residue; independent irrational displacement yields a two-torus product integral. For \(b=2a\), a one-torus integral yields the separate radical constant. Strict denominator ordering, exact null modes, and Schatten conclusions follow. All central steps have an analytic proof and do not rely on finite sampling. The standalone checker passed 18,153 finite assertions, probing all numerical normalizations, but those assertions do not establish the limits.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Johnstone–Raimondo (arXiv:math/0503663, dated 2005-03-29), including its discussion of Fourier coefficients, treats the **one-interval** boxcar and statistical inverse rates. De Canditiis–Pensky (DOI:10.1111/j.1467-9469.2006.00463.x) treats separately recorded multi-channel observations with a sum of **squared** multipliers, not the spectral counting of a coherent **sum** of shifted boxcars. A 2026 published result, *Arithmetic Fourier-zero lattices and Wiener cyclicity for two separated intervals* (identifier 2b9667f7502f), directly covers the two-interval Fourier zero-set issue; this component is credited, not treated as independently new. Its full accessible theorem is about **zeros and Wiener cyclicity**, not decreasing singular-value coefficients. An earlier one-boxcar sorted-singular theorem (identifier 6167de3a-2980-434e-bcc2-c0b68ade7ee2) but does not imply the product-phase counting here. Focused semantic searches for the \(\sin(\pi na)\cos(\pi nb)\) profile, parity denominators, weak Schatten residues and two-interval kernels revealed no exact or stronger covering statement. An older unindexed coefficient theorem or broad singular-value rearrangement result remains possible; the accessible search is not a proof of priority.

## Value — PASS
Two separated illuminated exposure windows form a physically recognizable *single* coherent blur filter, whose Fourier transfer function has an interference factor absent from a one-window model. Its exact sorted singular-value coefficient is the natural asymptotic number of recoverable modes above any small amplitude threshold. The full rational-shift parity separation reverses the sign of the deviation from the generic constant, exhibits a positive-density nullspace at even denominators, and gives globally sharp rational-shift upper and lower coefficients. The dependent irrational counterexample prevents incorrectly replacing rational independence by mere irrationality. These are structural arithmetic phenomena for a natural two-parameter operator family, not recomputations of a known table. No statistical minimax-rate theorem or all-irrational classification is asserted.
