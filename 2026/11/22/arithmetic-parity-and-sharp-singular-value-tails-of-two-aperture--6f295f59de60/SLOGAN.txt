For the coherent convolution of two separated equal-width circular boxcars, the sorted singular values obey \(s_k\sim C/k\): rational displacements have an exact even-denominator cotangent versus odd-denominator cosecant law, with opposite shifts from the generic constant \(16/\pi^3\). The exceptional irrational relation \(b=2a\) gives a third, explicitly different constant and shows that irrationality alone does not determine the spectral density.
