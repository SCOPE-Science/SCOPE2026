# Scientific comparison

The proof first applies the established one-dimensional Gaussian boundedness criterion to each tensor factor. Below the first saturation time, a period of the slice would give an almost-everywhere translational symmetry of a product of two proper circle arcs. The first Fourier coefficient of each arc is nonzero, so each coordinate translation is trivial; irrationality then rules out every nonzero period. At and above the saturation thresholds the closed inequalities give the one-constraint and unconstrained cases directly.

*Arithmetic contact and density laws for two-rate Gaussian semigroups* (Resultary c2e28fd7da99) gives the same two-rate boundedness domain, the global imaginary-period group, and the density classification. Its rational density proof also gives the short-time common-zero geometry. These results are not presented as new. The fixed-positive-time slice translation group is a separate invariant: the full region is aperiodic while an entire interval of smoothing times has an exact finite slice period. The claim is restricted to that transition. No exhaustive historical priority claim is made.
