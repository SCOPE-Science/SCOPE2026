A single attractive interlayer rung in two hard-core paths sends all but an explicitly counted set of fugacity zeros off the real axis: the number of surviving double real zeros is determined exactly by \(\gcd(n+2,v)\) and \(\gcd(n+2,v+1)\). Below unit rung weight every zero is negative and real, while the unprotected pairs open into the complex plane with an explicit square-root law above unit weight.
