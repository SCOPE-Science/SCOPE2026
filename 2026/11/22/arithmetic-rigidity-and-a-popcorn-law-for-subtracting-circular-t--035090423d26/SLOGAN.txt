For two geodesic tent covariances on the circle with radii \(0<b<a\le\pi\), subtracting a positive multiple of the narrower tent preserves positive definiteness **if and only if** \(e^{ib}\) is an integer power of \(e^{ia}\); the sharp coefficient is \(b/(ak^2)\) for irrational \(a/(2\pi)\), or an explicit finite trigonometric maximum for rational \(a/(2\pi)\). At each irrational base radius the admissible narrower radii form a countable dense set whose sharp thresholds obey a Thomae-type discontinuity law, while every admissible irrational-base boundary remains strictly positive definite on finite distinct samples.
