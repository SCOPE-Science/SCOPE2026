A nontrivial polynomial-moment unilateral shift survives \(k\) generalized Aluthge iterations as a finite-order \(m\)-isometry exactly when the parameter is rational \(u/v\) and its moment polynomial is a positive-on-integers \(v^k\)-th power. Irrational parameters destroy the property immediately and permanently; rational parameters permit only finitely many iterations, with sharp divisibility restrictions on the isometry order.
