The commutator of two integer block-averaging projections has an exact pure-point essential spectrum governed by a modular weighted-path Laplacian, and the projections commute exactly when one block length divides the other. For reduced coprime lengths \(m<n\) with \(n\equiv\pm1\pmod m\), its nonzero spectral points are \(\pm i\sqrt{\eta_k(1-\eta_k)}\), where \(\eta_k=k(k+1)/(mn)\); when \(n\ge2(m-1)\), the sharp norm is \(\sqrt{(m-1)(n-m+1)}/n\).
