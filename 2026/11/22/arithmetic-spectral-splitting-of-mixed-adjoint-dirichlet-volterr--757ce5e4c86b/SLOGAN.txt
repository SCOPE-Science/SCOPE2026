Mixed adjoint commutators of monomial Dirichlet–Volterra operators exhibit a sharp arithmetic singular-value transition: if the two integer indices are coprime, the singular values decay as \(\log^{-3}n\), but a common divisor creates a dominant \(\log^{-2}n\) branch. Both branches have exact formulas and sharply determined stretched-exponential counting constants, and the self-commutator separates them into positive and negative spectra.
