# Review of arithmetic splitting for fourfold-contact Poncelet conics

## Correctness — PASS

The ground-field normal form follows from the restriction of quadrics to a rational Veronese conic and the fact that a length-four intersection supported at one point restricts to a pure fourth power. In the normal form, both conics are smooth because the determinant of the second conic's symmetric matrix is \(1/4\). A direct adjugate calculation proves that a chord joining distinct finite vertices is tangent to the second conic exactly when its parameter difference squares to \(-4\lambda\), while no chord through the common contact point qualifies. Finite-field additive cosets then yield exactly \(q/p\) disjoint simple \(p\)-cycles in one square class and no edges in the other. The affine-coordinate stabilizer gives exactly two rational projective orbits with common stabilizer order \(2q\). The extension-field formula follows from the standard behavior of the quadratic character under extension. The second symbolic checker validates the conic determinant, adjugate chord formula, coordinate action and quartic restriction exactly. The coordinate-level checker enumerates the entire rational chord graph for seven prime and quadratic finite fields and the full smooth-conic parameter space over the fields of three and five elements. These computations corroborate rather than replace the infinite-family proof.

## Originality — PASS with historical priority risk

The closest primary work of Jow–Liang (arXiv:2112.11770, 2021) establishes geometric characteristic-\(p\) porism for the fourfold-contact case over an algebraic closure but does not compute the finite-field tangent-chord graph, its square-class arithmetic obstruction, exact rational polygon counts, the extension-field count, or the rational projective orbit census. A published mathematical result on *triple-contact* pairs \((3,1)\) determines \((q-p)/(2p)\) rational simple \(p\)-gons, but its full statement expressly excludes the fourfold-contact case and does not dominate the new theorem. Radnović–Ragas study triangle and tetragon closure conditions defined geometrically; Hungerbühler–Kusejko consider disjoint conic pencils. Focused searches for full-contact, osculating, square-class and finite-field rational-cycle equivalents found no exact statement. This is evidence of noncoverage in inspected material, not proof of historical uniqueness. A calculation in older finite projective geometry under other terminology could be equivalent.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Fourfold intersection is one of the natural degenerate intersection types in Poncelet theory, with known geometric characteristic-prime periodicity. The arithmetic classification is not a routine reparametrization of triple-contact: it identifies two rational descent types, proves an all-or-nothing obstruction even to rational tangent chords, determines the entire graph rather than just an isolated polygon count, gives the exact extension degree where the obstruction vanishes, and counts projective equivalence classes as well as pairs. In particular a Poncelet period visible geometrically need not produce any rational polygon over the field of definition. These are exact natural invariants of the ordered conic configuration.

## Limits and related literature

The result concerns odd characteristic and intersection type \((4)\), not characteristic two or other intersection types. It is not a new proof of geometric porism. Jow–Liang, *Poncelet's theorem for conics in any position and any characteristic* (arXiv:2112.11770); *Rational Poncelet \(p\)-gons for conics with triple contact* (2026); Radnović–Ragas, *Poncelet Triangles and Tetragons over Finite Fields* (arXiv:2511.06347); Hungerbühler–Kusejko (2020) were compared at the level of hypotheses and conclusions. An unindexed equivalent finite-geometry calculation remains a historical priority risk.
