Fourfold contact of smooth conics over an odd finite field has two arithmetic projective types: in one, the rational tangent-chord graph is edgeless; in the other, it is one isolated point together with \(q/p\) disjoint \(p\)-cycles. Thus the exact number of rational simple Poncelet \(p\)-gons is \(q(1+\chi_q(-\lambda))/(2p)\), with a corresponding exact extension-field formula and equal-sized projective orbits.
