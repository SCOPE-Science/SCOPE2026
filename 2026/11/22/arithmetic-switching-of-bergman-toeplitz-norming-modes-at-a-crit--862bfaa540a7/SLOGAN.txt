At the critical degree ratio \(\rho\approx1.609779\) for analytic Bergman Toeplitz monomials, the norming degree is eventually one of exactly two adjacent integers, with the winner controlled by whether \(\{\rho n\}\) lies above or below the explicit threshold \(0.5737386\ldots\). Both winners occur infinitely often, and a uniform second-order lattice law determines the exact critical-window norm correction.
