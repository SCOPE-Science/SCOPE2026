In synchronous tie-retaining binary majority on a rectangular rook graph, every equilibrium is a Ferrers matrix up to row and column relabelling. The number of classes is independent of the longer side except for parity: it is \(2^t\) for matching parities and \([z^t]/((1-z)^2-z^3)\) for opposite parities, where \(t\) is the shorter side length.
