In the classical Chein loop \\(M(G,2)\\), the entire associator-value law is exactly \\(\\frac18\\delta_1+\\frac78\\mu_G\\), where \\(\\mu_G\\) is the group commutator-word distribution; the known association probability is only its identity fiber. For odd dihedral groups, all nonidentity rotation targets occur equally often, while reflection targets never occur.
