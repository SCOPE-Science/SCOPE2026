Two identical habitats need not favor either concentrating all introductions or dividing them equally: with independent Poisson founder counts, a two-arrival viability quorum, and independent suitability probability \(799/800\), exactly two **unequal** interior allocations globally maximize one-step establishment success when the total arrival mean is ten. Each optimum assigns about \(2.93748\) expected founders to one habitat and \(7.06252\) to the other, and this asymmetry persists under small parameter changes.
