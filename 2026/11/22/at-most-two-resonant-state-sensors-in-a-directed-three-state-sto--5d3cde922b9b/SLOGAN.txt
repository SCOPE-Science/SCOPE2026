For a directed three-state Markov cycle, at most two single-state occupancy sensors have nonzero-frequency spectral peaks, and the faster-exiting states are always the more efficient detectors. When two exit rates are equal and the third is varied, the exact resonance-sensor count is \(0\to2\to0\to1\), with an explicit cubic threshold and a range where oscillatory eigenmodes are invisible to every binary state sensor.
