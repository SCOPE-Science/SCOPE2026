# Verification scope

The analytic certificate consists of the generator resolvent identity, the stationary covariance transform, the derivative factor and the nonpositive sum of three visibility discriminants. The explicit symmetric-two-rate special case reduces to two polynomial factorizations and a strictly increasing cubic.

Reproducibility: execute `python checker.py` with Python 3, NumPy and SymPy installed. The embedded checker prints an OK line after exact symbolic identities, random positive-rate resolvent comparisons, positive-frequency peak location tests and threshold boundary examples. Numerical testing cannot establish an all-parameter theorem; the proof in `RESULT.md` supplies that step.

The primary 2026 source was inspected in full as accessible HTML. A relevant older 2007 study could be inspected only through its abstract, and its stronger possible implications are an explicit publication-priority risk. No independent external audit has been performed.
