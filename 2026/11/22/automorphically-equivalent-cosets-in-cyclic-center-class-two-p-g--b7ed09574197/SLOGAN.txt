For every odd prime \(p\), the nonabelian finite \(p\)-groups with cyclic center of order \(p^k\), \(k\ge2\), and derived subgroup of order \(p\) have exactly one automorphically equivalent proper nontrivial normal subgroup when their exponent is \(p^k\), and exactly two when it is \(p^{k+1}\). The admissible subgroups are always \(G'\), together with the characteristic index-\(p\) subgroup \(\Omega_k(G)\) only in the larger-exponent case.
