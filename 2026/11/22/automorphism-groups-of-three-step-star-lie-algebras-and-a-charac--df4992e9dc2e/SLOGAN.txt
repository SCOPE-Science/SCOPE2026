The full finite-field automorphism group of the class-three star Lie algebra has an explicit order \(q^{(m+1)(\binom m2+1)}(q-1)|H_m(q)|\) for \(m\ge3\), where \(H_m(q)\) is monomial in odd characteristic and the entire \(\operatorname{GL}_m(q)\) in characteristic two. This proves an exact characteristic-two symmetry jump while describing the entire kernel of the quotient action.
