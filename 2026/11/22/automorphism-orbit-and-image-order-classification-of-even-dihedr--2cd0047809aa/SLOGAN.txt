For every even \(n\ge4\), all retractions of the finite dihedral group of order \(2n\) fall into explicit unitary-divisor, reflection, exceptional central, and trivial families. Their image-order polynomial is \(t+\sum_{d\parallel n}d\,t^{2n/d}+(n+2\epsilon_n)t^2\), and their number of automorphism-conjugacy classes is \(2^{\omega(n)}+2+\epsilon_n\), where \(\epsilon_n=1\) exactly when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\).
