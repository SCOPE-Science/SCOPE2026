For Das's conjugation-based group averaging identity, the full and pointed map counts on every prime dihedral group \(D_{2p}\) are \(p^{p+1}+p(2^{p+1}-1)+p^{(p-1)/2}\) and \(p^p+p(2^p-1)+p^{(p-1)/2}\). The complete automorphism-orbit counts are exact necklace-polynomial and totient sums, giving \(3969\) and \(824\) unpointed and pointed classes on \(D_{10}\).
