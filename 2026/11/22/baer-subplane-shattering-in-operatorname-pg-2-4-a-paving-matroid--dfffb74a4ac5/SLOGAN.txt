The 360 Baer subplanes of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,4)\) shatter exactly the point supports of size at most four that do not lie four-to-a-line, forming a rank-four paving-matroid independence complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{4740} S^3\). Yet their point-membership concept class has Littlestone dimension five, strictly exceeding its VC dimension four.
