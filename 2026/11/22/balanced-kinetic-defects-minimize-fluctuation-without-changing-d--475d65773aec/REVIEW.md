# Scientific review: balanced clock placement in periodic biased transport

**Correctness (PASS).** The stationary residue law is \(\pi_i=\tau_i/S\); the Poisson-corrector martingale yields an exact asymptotic variance formula, and a geometric cyclic convolution yields a positive pair-distance kernel. Strict convexity provides a valid simultaneous lower bound for every cyclic \(m\)-step span and an extreme-point upper bound, including equality cases. Boundary regimes are treated separately. Exact-rational replay checks generator/closed-form agreement, not merely approximate fits. Limits: two waiting-time levels, homogeneous directional bias, and long-time variance only.

**Originality (PASS, subject to priority risk).** Derrida (1983) and Koza (1999) have general periodic-hopping drift and diffusion methods and therefore cover the general calculability of these observables. Neither examined text states a full fixed-count spatial-placement optimizer or its necessary-and-sufficient cyclic-balance equality criterion. The extra extremal statement requires establishing a special strictly positive cyclic kernel and combining convex lower and upper arguments. Targeted literature and mathematical-results searches did not reveal an equivalent theorem. This absence is not proof of uniqueness; earlier unindexed or differently named optimal-arrangement results remain a risk.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** At fixed rate inventory, the theorem solves a natural kinetic-noise design problem: mean throughput cannot select a rate arrangement, whereas fluctuations sharply select balanced and clustered extremes. The result applies uniformly to all finite periods and defect counts, including complete equality cases and physically meaningful unbiased/one-way exceptions. It is not just a single isolated numerical invariant or a generic transport formula restatement.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Derrida, *J. Stat. Phys.* 31 (1983), 433–450, gives arbitrary-period transport formulas. Koza, arXiv:cond-mat/9909204 (1999), gives general matrix methods and examples. Both are foundational and strictly more general for computing a fixed environment's coefficients, but the inspected claims do not establish the design optimizer. Unchecked specialized rearrangement literature is the main residual originality risk. The present review does not constitute independent validation.
