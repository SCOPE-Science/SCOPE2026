# Balanced-pressure duality for the primitive Dyck code

## Finding

Let \(\Sigma=\{+,-\}^{\mathbb Z}\), and let \(\mathcal G\) be all *primitive positive Dyck excursions*: the finite words with total \(+/-\) height zero and strictly positive height at every proper nonempty prefix. Let \(X_N\) be the bilateral coded shift generated by all excursions of length at most \(2N\). The union of these finite cores is a coded presentation whose closure is \(\Sigma\).

**Entropy-density theorem.** Write \(\mathcal M_{1/2}\) for the shift-invariant probability measures on \(\Sigma\) satisfying \(\mu([+])=1/2\). For every \(\mu\in\mathcal M_{1/2}\), every weak-star neighborhood \(U\) of \(\mu\), and every \(\epsilon>0\), there are \(N\) and a shift-invariant probability measure \(\nu\) on \(X_N\) with \(\nu\in U\) and \(h_\nu>h_\mu-\epsilon\). Conversely every invariant measure on any \(X_N\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{1/2}\).

Consequently, **for every continuous potential** \(\varphi:\Sigma\to\mathbb R\), the finite-core pressures obey
\[
\boxed{\lim_{N\to\infty}P_{X_N}(\varphi)
 =\max_{\mu\in\mathcal M_{1/2}}\left(h_\mu+\int\varphi\,d\mu\right)
 =\min_{t\in\mathbb R}P_{\Sigma}\!\left(\varphi+t(\mathbf1_{[+]}-\tfrac12)\right).}
\]
In particular, the cores recover the **full** pressure precisely when \(\varphi\) has a full-shift equilibrium measure of symbol-\(+\) frequency \(1/2\).

There is also a closed-form complete classification for all nearest-neighbor potentials: if \(\varphi_{a,b,c,d}\) takes the respective real values \(a,b,c,d\) on the two-letter blocks `++`, `+-`, `-+`, `--`, then
\[
\boxed{\lim_{N\to\infty}P_{X_N}(\varphi_{a,b,c,d})
 =\log\!\left(e^{(a+d)/2}+e^{(b+c)/2}\right)}
\]
and
\[
P_\Sigma(\varphi_{a,b,c,d})=
\log\!\left(\frac{e^a+e^d+\sqrt{(e^a-e^d)^2+4e^{b+c}}}{2}\right).
\]
The two values are equal **if and only if** \(a=d\). Thus the set of nearest-neighbor potentials for which finite cores recover full pressure is exactly the codimension-one hyperplane \(a=d\).

## Assumptions and scope

Use natural logarithms and the left shift. For \(w=w_0\dots w_{\ell-1}\) define height \(H_j(w)=\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\chi(w_i)\), with \(\chi(+)=1\) and \(\chi(-)=-1\). A generator \(g\in\mathcal G\) satisfies \(H_\ell(g)=0\), and \(H_j(g)>0\) for all \(0<j<\ell\). Its length is even. The space \(X_N\) is the closure of all shifted bi-infinite concatenations from the **finite** set \(\mathcal G_{\le N}=\{g\in\mathcal G:|g|\le 2N\}\). By the unambiguous boundary recovery for this code [1, Section 3.2], every point of \(X_N\) has a unique pointed generator decomposition, with bounded generator lengths.

Pressure is \(P_Y(\varphi)=\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal M_\sigma(Y)}(h_\mu+\int\varphi\,d\mu)\). The entropy-density assertion concerns entropy-rate lower approximation, **not** equality of measure sets. The complete full-shift pressure is always computed on \(\Sigma\), not on an infinite noncompact excursion space.

## Proof

**Step 1: Necessity of balance.** Concatenating primitive excursions creates a cumulative-height path that returns to a common baseline at each generator boundary and stays above it between boundaries. For each \(x\in X_N\), its increments have uniformly bounded cumulative sums over arbitrarily long intervals, since every generator has length at most \(2N\). Therefore its Birkhoff average of \(\chi\) is zero. Integration against an invariant measure gives \(\mu([+])=1/2\) for every \(\mu\in\mathcal M_\sigma(X_N)\). The fact that the infinite code generates all binary finite words (and thus \(\Sigma\) on closure) and has unambiguous pointed decomposition is proved in [1, Section 3.2].

**Step 2: Entropy-dense positive-excursion surgery.** Fix \(\mu\in\mathcal M_{1/2}\), finitely many test cylinders, and \(\epsilon>0\). Let \(\beta\) be the fair Bernoulli measure and form \(\mu_\eta=(1-\eta)\mu+\eta\beta\), for small \(\eta>0\). Its symbol frequency remains \(1/2\), all finite words have positive probability, and \(h_{\mu_\eta}\ge(1-\eta)h_\mu\). The stationary \(k\)-step Markov approximation \(\rho\) to \(\mu_\eta\) retains all blocks of lengths at most \(k+1\), has strictly positive transition probabilities and hence is mixing, and satisfies \(h_\rho\ge h_{\mu_\eta}\) by the conditional-entropy inequality. Choosing \(k\) large and \(\eta\) small makes \(\rho\) close to \(\mu\) on the prescribed cylinders, balanced, and with entropy greater than \(h_\mu-\epsilon/3\).

For arbitrarily large word lengths \(m\), the ergodic and Shannon–McMillan–Breiman theorems supply a set \(W_m\) of at least \(\exp(m(h_\rho-\delta))\) distinct length-\(m\) words, up to an inessential constant factor, each with (i) signed sum \(s(w)=\sum_i\chi(w_i)\) satisfying \(|s(w)|\le\delta m\) and (ii) empirical frequencies of all prescribed finite blocks within \(\delta\) of their \(\rho\)-probabilities. Here \(\delta>0\) is arbitrary and fixed before taking \(m\to\infty\). Pigeonholing the at most \(2\delta m+1\) possible signed sums gives a single value \(s\) and a family \(W'_m\) with
\[
|W'_m|\ge\frac{\exp(m(h_\rho-\delta))}{C(2\delta m+1)}
\]
for some fixed \(C\) independent of \(m\).

For each \(w\in W'_m\), append \(|s|\) copies of the symbol with increment opposite to the sign of \(s\), giving a balanced word \(v\) of length \(L=m+|s|\). Cyclically rotate \(v\) to start at a minimum of its prefix-height path; the resulting word \(u\) has nonnegative prefix heights and final height zero. Then \(g=+u-\) is a primitive positive excursion of the **same length** \(\ell=L+2\) for all \(w\in W'_m\). Any output \(g\) has at most \(L\) preimages in \(W'_m\): deleting the outside symbols and choosing the inverse rotation recovers the first \(m\) entries. The image family \(E_m\subset\mathcal G_{\le\ell/2}\) thus satisfies
\[
|E_m|\ge\frac{\exp(m(h_\rho-\delta))}{C(2\delta m+1)L}.
\]
The cyclic block frequencies in every \(g\in E_m\) differ from those of its original \(w\) by at most \(O_r(\delta)+O_r(1/m)\) for each prescribed block length \(r\); this accounts for the appended correction, cyclic cut and two wrapper symbols.

Choose generator names independently and uniformly from \(E_m\), concatenate them, and randomize the origin uniformly among the \(\ell\) positions in a generator. This defines a shift-invariant probability \(\nu_m\) on \(X_{\ell/2}\). Boundary-crossing blocks contribute only \(O_r(1/\ell)\) of its frequencies, so \(\nu_m\) approaches \(\rho\) on the prescribed cylinders as \(\delta\downarrow0\) and \(m\to\infty\). Unique pointed decoding gives exactly
\[
h_{\nu_m}=\frac{\log|E_m|}{\ell}
\ge\frac{m(h_\rho-\delta)-O(\log m)}{m(1+\delta)+2}.
\]
Choose \(\delta\) and then \(m\) to obtain \(\nu_m\in U\) and \(h_{\nu_m}>h_\mu-\epsilon\). This proves entropy density, including for nonergodic \(\mu\) via the preliminary positive Markov approximation.

**Step 3: Every continuous potential and the dual form.** Each core measure is balanced by Step 1, giving the upper pressure bound by the constrained supremum. Step 2 approximates any constrained measure in weak-star topology without lowering entropy appreciably, hence gives the reverse bound for all continuous \(\varphi\). The finite cores are nested; taking their pressures proves the first equality. Since the entropy map on the finite full shift is upper semicontinuous, the constrained maximum exists. For \(s\in[0,1]\), the maximal free energy among invariant measures with \(\mu([+])=s\) is a finite upper-semicontinuous concave function of \(s\). At the interior point \(s=1/2\), it has a supporting affine line. Taking its supporting slope gives a finite multiplier \(t\) for which the unconstrained variational principle for \(\varphi+t(\mathbf1_{[+]}-1/2)\) equals the constrained maximum. The reverse inequality holds for every \(t\), proving the displayed minimum formula. Equality with \(P_\Sigma(\varphi)\) holds exactly when at least one full-shift equilibrium measure belongs to \(\mathcal M_{1/2}\), again by upper semicontinuity and compactness.

**Step 4: Exact nearest-neighbor classification.** For a balanced stationary two-symbol process let \(q=\mu([+-])+\mu([-+])\). Stationarity gives \(\mu([+-])=\mu([-+])=q/2\), and balance gives \(\mu([++])=\mu([--])=(1-q)/2\). The entropy rate is at most the binary entropy \(H(q)=-q\log q-(1-q)\log(1-q)\), attained for the symmetric two-state Markov chain with flip probability \(q\). Its potential average equals \((a+d)(1-q)/2+(b+c)q/2\). Maximizing over \(0\le q\le1\) gives
\[
\max_q\{H(q)+(a+d)(1-q)/2+(b+c)q/2\}
=\log\!\left(e^{(a+d)/2}+e^{(b+c)/2}\right).
\]
For the full binary shift the standard weighted adjacency matrix is \(\begin{psmallmatrix}e^a&e^b\\e^c&e^d\end{psmallmatrix}\), so its pressure is the log Perron root displayed in the Finding. Writing \(A=e^a,D=e^d,Q=e^{(b+c)/2}\), its Perron root is \((A+D+\sqrt{(A-D)^2+4Q^2})/2\). It is at least \(\sqrt{AD}+Q\), with equality only when \(A=D\), equivalently \(a=d\). This proves the strict phase classification.

**Step 5: Exact finite-core computation.** The same argument gives a reproducible root formula. A primitive excursion of length \(2n\) with \(p\) transitions `+-` has \(n-p\) transitions of each equal-letter type and \(p\) transitions of each unequal-letter type **when the fixed `-+` transition between generators is included**. For \(n\ge2\), the number of such generators is the Narayana number \(\mathrm N(n-1,p)=\frac1{n-1}\binom{n-1}{p}\binom{n-1}{p-1}\); for \(n=1\) take \(\mathrm N(0,1)=1\). Unique decoding and the finite generator suspension variational formula show that \(P_{X_N}(\varphi_{a,b,c,d})\) is the unique real solution \(u\) of
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sum_{p=1}^{\max(1,n-1)}\mathrm N(n-1,p)
\exp\!\big((n-p)(a+d)+p(b+c)-2nu\big)=1.
\]
Indeed, at the root the positive weights in this sum define the unique entropy-maximizing Bernoulli distribution on the finite set of generator names, and the log-sum inequality bounds all other stationary name processes. This finite formula independently tracks the limiting theorem and requires no approximation oracle.

## Verification

The standalone `check_dyck_pressure.py` exhaustively enumerates primitive excursions of semilength at most seven, verifies all Narayana peak counts and cyclic pair energies for three real four-parameter potentials, compares truncated renewal-pressure roots with the closed limiting expression, and verifies finite balanced-word-to-primitive-excursion surgery through input length sixteen. The recorded run passed 18,188 assertions/comparisons. These are **finite diagnostics only**; the universal entropy-density construction, convex pressure duality and all-parameter nearest-neighbor classification are established by Steps 1–5.

As an illustrative field, \((a,b,c,d)=(1,0,0,0)\) gives finite-core limiting pressure \(\log(1+e^{1/2})=0.97407698418\ldots\) and full pressure \(1.15610098211\ldots\), a strict gap of \(0.18202399794\ldots\). The source paper's equality for zero potential is contained as \(a=b=c=d=0\) but is not the new contribution.

## Relationship to prior work

Hedges [1] constructs exactly this uniquely represented full-shift positive-Dyck code and proves that finite cores exhaust *sequential* pressure for every continuous potential. It exhibits equality at the zero potential but does **not** characterize the sequential-pressure functional for all continuous potentials, prove entropy density in the entire balanced-measure simplex, or calculate the exact four-parameter nearest-neighbor locus. These are the contributions here. Burr, Das, Wolf and Yang [2] supply earlier general coded-shift pressure/computability methods; their full text concerns general exhaustion and sufficient conditions, not the present explicit constrained potential formula. Kucherenko, Schmoll and Wolf [3] study entropy and equilibrium states for coded shifts and the four-symbol Dyck shift, a different shift/code. Kucherenko, López and Wolf [4] likewise study computability and the four-symbol Dyck shift. A previously published exact *unweighted* finite-core entropy asymptotic for this **same** primitive binary code [5] is acknowledged; it neither states nor entails the entropy-density theorem for arbitrary invariant balanced measures nor the potential-dependent pressure phase boundary.

The new result is **not** a claim of first discovering the Dyck excursion code, its Catalan/Narayana enumeration, the elementary two-state transfer-matrix formula, or the general variational principle. Its genuinely new asserted content is the synthesis through the entropy-dense surgery theorem and complete resulting pressure comparison. Unindexed prior treatments of balanced Markov approximations or constrained Dyck pressures remain a priority risk.

## Limitations

The entropy-density theorem applies to the full bilateral **binary** shift under the specific all-positive-primitive-excursions code and its length cutoffs. It does not classify other Dyck presentations or give a rate uniform in a general continuous potential. The explicit four-parameter formula covers nearest-neighbor potentials, not arbitrary locally constant potentials of longer memory. The proof is a mathematical derivation with finite computational diagnostics, not independent external validation. Historical-priority search was bounded and cannot establish absolute uniqueness.
