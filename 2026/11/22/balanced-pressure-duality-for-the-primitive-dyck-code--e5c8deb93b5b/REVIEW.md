# Scientific review: Balanced-pressure duality for the primitive Dyck code

## Correctness — PASS
Every truncated generator returns the binary height to baseline, so all its invariant measures are balanced. For an arbitrary balanced invariant measure, first mix with a fair Bernoulli measure, then take a positive mixing finite-step Markov approximation that preserves the finite-block statistics and does not reduce entropy. Large typical words can be partitioned by their signed sum, balanced with at most a small fraction of appended symbols, cyclically rotated at a height minimum, and wrapped with the symbols `+` and `-` to become primitive excursions. Counting the inverse images by at most their length gives exponentially many equal-length generators. The uniform concatenation measure consequently approximates the target measure in cylinders and from below in entropy. This handles nonergodic target measures. A constrained variational principle and a supporting-line argument give the continuous-potential pressure identity. For two-symbol nearest-neighbor potentials, stationarity plus balance forces the four two-block frequencies and reduces entropy to a binary-flip Markov maximum. The resulting closed form and full-shift Perron comparison establish the exact strictness condition. The accompanying standalone program checks Narayana class counts, cyclic pair energies, roots and the balancing construction, with finite scope stated explicitly.
