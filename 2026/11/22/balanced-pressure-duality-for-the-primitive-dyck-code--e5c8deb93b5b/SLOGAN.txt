For primitive positive-Dyck finite cores of the full binary shift, the limit of pressure for every continuous potential is exactly the pressure constrained to invariant measures with equal symbol frequencies, and all such measures are entropy-densely approximable by finite-core measures. For nearest-neighbor potentials with weights \(a,b,c,d\) on the four adjacent pairs, this sequential pressure is \(\log(e^{(a+d)/2}+e^{(b+c)/2})\), strictly smaller than full pressure exactly when \(a\ne d\).
