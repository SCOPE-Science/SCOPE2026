The supplied exact checker passed for all its stated cases; the general result is proved symbolically and does not infer infinity from finite tests.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The initiating problem has a sharp dimension-four rigidity boundary, but its published discussion lacks a usable higher-dimensional construction. The formula solves a natural explicit family for **every** complex simple spectrum and all sizes at/above the first nonrigid dimension. Its rank is rigidly three even as dimension grows, while the Jordan partition changes sharply between five and six. At the first size, the finite family exhausts the geometrically natural Fano-line mechanism, providing the concrete connection with the sixteen lines on a quartic del Pezzo surface. These are structural, reproducible facts; they are not claimed to classify all isospectral matrices.
