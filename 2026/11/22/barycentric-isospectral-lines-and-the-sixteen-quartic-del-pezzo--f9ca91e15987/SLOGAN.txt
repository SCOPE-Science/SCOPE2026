Every simple-spectrum complex diagonal matrix of size \(n\geq5\) has a canonical sign family of \(2^{n-1}\) distinct symmetric isospectral pencil directions of rank three. Their nilpotency type jumps from \((3,2)\) at size five to \((2,2,2,1^{n-6})\) in all larger sizes, and the sixteen size-five directions arise from all sixteen lines on a degree-four del Pezzo surface.
