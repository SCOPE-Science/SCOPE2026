A positive-definite Bernstein circle covariance has exactly classified cyclic Gram ranks, determined by which of its even and odd endpoint Fourier masses are nonzero. Its smallest unnormalized Gram eigenvalue has two different sharp asymptotic constants: \(2n\min(D_0,D_1)/N\) on even grids and \(n(\sqrt{D_0}+\sqrt{D_1})^2/(2N)\) on odd grids.
