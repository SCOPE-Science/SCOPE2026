# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The real signed-variation reduction is valid because the Poissonized Hardy average fixes constants, sends each upper-interval indicator to a function no greater than that indicator, and admits a bounded nonnegative jump-response kernel. Exact differentiation of the jump error gives \(e^{-t}I_0(2\sqrt{t\log(1/s)})-1\), which has exactly one root. The compound-Poisson gamma mixture proves the Bessel identity without a limiting numerical step. The short-time coefficient follows from the first two Hardy powers. The sharp long-time prefactor is established by a two-region dominated-convergence argument, including a separate exponential bound for the lower half of the integration range. The embedded checker corroborates the finite formulas; it is not treated as proof of the infinite-time claim.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The closest accessible classical discussion is Arvanitidis–Siskakis (2010), on analytic-space Cesàro operator norms and spectra via dilation semigroups, and Miller (2007), on related Cesàro and composition operators. Belli–Gül–Ross–Siskakis (2026) study a different kernel \(x^{-1}\int_0^x f(u)/(1-u)\,du\). The observed results do not state or dominate the sharp \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) identity-approximation error for \(e^{t(H-I)}\), its Bessel root or the quarter-rate boundary layer. Sharp formulas for individual integer powers of \(H\) do not directly yield the jointly optimized Poisson-mixture norm, because the fixed-power extremizing jumps vary with the power. Searches cannot rule out all historical equivalents; the risk remains real.

## Value — PASS

The result supplies a single exact stability benchmark for continuously Poissonized averaging on discontinuous signals, classifies all nonconstant extremizers for every positive time, and determines the unexpectedly slow, logarithmically corrected worst-jump migration toward the boundary. It is a genuine jointly optimized continuum-time assertion rather than a numerical value, parameter substitution or a restatement of a fixed iterate.

## Limitations

Real \(BV(0,1)\), unweighted Lebesgue \(L^1\), interior distributional variation, unit Poisson intensity and the standard Hardy average only. Numerical tests are finite corroborations, not independent validation. The historical search is not exhaustive.
