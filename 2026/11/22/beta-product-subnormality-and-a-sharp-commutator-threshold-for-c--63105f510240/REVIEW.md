# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The monomial norm follows from a polar-coordinate Gamma integral, and the weighted-shift coefficients follow by integrating polynomial monomials. Gamma-ratio asymptotics give all bounded/compact regimes. Every compact positive-coefficient step shift tending to zero violates the necessary monotonicity for hyponormality. In the sole noncompact case, the exact Gamma recursion yields a product of two increasing rational weight factors. Each residue-class moment sequence equals the moments of a product of two independent beta variables with strictly positive shape parameters for every integer exponent at least three, so multiplication on a circularized beta-product probability space gives an explicit normal extension. The positive diagonal self-commutator has exact telescoping trace and a nonzero \(j^{-2}\) singular-value law; its quasi-Schatten threshold follows. Exact algebra and supplementary computations are in the proof and checker. Risk: numerical tests alone cannot verify the universal assertions, which are instead covered by the analytical identities.
