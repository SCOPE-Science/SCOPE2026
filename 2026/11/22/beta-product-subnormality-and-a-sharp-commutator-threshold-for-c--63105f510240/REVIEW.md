# Mathematical review

**Correctness.** The operator is a bounded step-\(m\) shift. Its squared weights give a positive diagonal self-commutator with an exact telescoping trace and a positive \(n^{-2}\) tail. Counting entries before rearrangement establishes the stated singular-value constant with multiplicity; the exact Schatten boundary follows. Finite checks are supplementary.

**Originality.** The critical monomial weights and beta-product subnormality are already present in the broader super-Gaussian polynomial rigidity result and are not claimed as new here. The earlier Gaussian self-commutator comparison shows the same pure-symbol threshold for a different weight. The conclusion retained here is the exact trace and singular-value law for the critical super-Gaussian operator. Priority remains limited to the literature evidence compared.

**Value.** The result measures the size of failure of normality for a natural bounded, noncompact operator. Besides locating the trace ideal, it quantifies the full high-rank commutator spectrum, including the dependence of its coefficient on the exponent. No mixed-symbol claim is made.
