On every strictly super-Gaussian power-Fock Hilbert space, a nonzero bounded monomial Volterra operator is subnormal exactly at the matching integer exponent \(\alpha=k\ge3\), where all reducing shifts have explicit beta-product Berger measures. Its positive self-commutator has exact trace \(k\), singular-value asymptotic \(k(3k-4)/(2j^2)\), and sharp Schatten boundary \(p>1/2\).
