For a two-temperature Brownian gyrator reset uniformly on a circle, the weak-coupling mean angular-velocity susceptibility is positive or negative according to the temperature ordering but is suppressed as \(r^{-1/2}\exp[-R\sqrt{r/T_+}]\) at large reset rate, with an exact prefactor. Uniform circular resetting erases every interior Gaussian contribution to this angular harmonic, so the susceptibility survives only through rare excursions across the reset circle, even though angular momentum responds algebraically.
