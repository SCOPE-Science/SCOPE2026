For every \(n\ge3\) not divisible by three, the dihedral group \(D_{2n}\) is the full automorphism group, over every field extension of \(\mathbb F_2\), of an explicit absolutely simple binary evolution algebra of dimension \(2n\). The same three-term Cayley construction has matrix nullity exactly two when \(3\mid n\), providing a sharp arithmetic boundary without excluding other realizations.
