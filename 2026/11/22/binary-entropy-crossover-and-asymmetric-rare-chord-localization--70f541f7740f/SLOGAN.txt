For independent Euclidean-arclength-uniform points on the planar Lamé oval with exponent \(p=2+\lambda\varepsilon\), nearly diametral chords have a sharp \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) crossover governed by binary entropy of their polar direction. Its two extreme angular regimes differ: diagonal localization is proportional to \(\lambda^{-1/2}\), whereas axial localization carries the sharper logarithmic correction \((|\lambda|\log|\lambda|)^{-1/2}\), with an explicit conditional endpoint-mismatch law.
