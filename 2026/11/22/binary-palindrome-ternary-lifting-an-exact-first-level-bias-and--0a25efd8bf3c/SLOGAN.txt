Every even-length binary palindrome is divisible by three, but there are no further persistent three-adic obstructions: for every \(k\ge2\), every class \(0\pmod3\) modulo \(3^k\) occurs at every sufficiently large even length. At length \(2m\), the three quotient residues \(P/3\pmod3\) have exact counts with a period-three signed bias of magnitude \(2^{\lfloor m/3\rfloor}\).
