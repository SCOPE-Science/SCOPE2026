# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The fiberwise polynomial expansion of an arbitrary normalized binary quandle cocycle yields a complete linear system. Connectivity eliminates the mixed-fiber coefficient and makes the remaining second-fiber coefficient constant on each row. The binary rank-two alternating identity then gives the exact number of free parameters. Coboundaries are independent rowwise because every adjacency row is nonzero. An independently assembled full cochain-matrix calculation for 60 finite cases agrees with the formula. The finite calculations are corroboration, not proof of universal validity.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence.** The construction and its interpretation as an abelian extension of a trivial quandle are prior. The full texts of its original paper, the general homogeneous abelian-inner construction, and the indecomposable-quandle second-cohomology theorem were inspected and do not state the cohomology dimension of the disconnected expanded graph quandle. The closest earlier graph-quandle endomorphism-rank result computes retractions, not cohomology. The remaining risk is an implicit consequence of broader disconnected-quandle cohomology literature; priority is not independently certified.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem provides a complete degree-two binary cohomology dimension for an infinite, graph-parametrized quandle family and detects exactly the connected complete bipartite and tripartite graphs. Quandle second cohomology governs additive extensions, making this a structural classification rather than a sample calculation or minor numerical optimization.
