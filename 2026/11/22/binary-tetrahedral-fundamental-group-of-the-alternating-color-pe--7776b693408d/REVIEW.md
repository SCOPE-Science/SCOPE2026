# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

An interior fixed point would force the open cell to be invariant, so each nonempty color subset is a union of orbits of the permutation. Each nonidentity even permutation has two orbits; an adjacent nonempty disjoint subset rules out using both, and the odd cycle then prevents alternating the two orbits. Thus the action is free. The known RP^3 topology gives the covering by S^3; a free self-map reversing its orientation would have rational Lefschetz number 2, impossible, so the quotient is orientable. Independent reconstruction of the quotient cellular boundaries includes the product-simplex boundary signs and the signs of the even relabeling on each surviving ordered color subset. It recovers ranks (19,46,24) and Smith factors (1^19;1^45,3;1^24). The kernels are primitive sublattices, giving precisely Z/3 in H_1 and zero H_2, with H_0 and H_3 both Z. The central extension E of A4 by C2 has order 24; H_1 gives commutator subgroup of order eight, over V4. A subgroup of order three exists by Cauchy's theorem and acts by a three-cycle on the three nonzero elements of the V4 quotient. If the order-eight group were abelian, the entire commutator would have size at most four, since it is the image of conjugation-minus-identity with the central C2 in its kernel. D8 has a characteristic cyclic subgroup of order four, ruling out the three-cycle. The kernel over V4 is therefore Q8, and the order-three action cycling its cyclic subgroups gives the standard binary tetrahedral semidirect product 2T, isomorphic to SL2(F3). No equivariant round-sphere model is needed.
