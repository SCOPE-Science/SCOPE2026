The free \(A_4\) target-color action on the four-color pentagon Hom manifold produces an ordinary quotient with binary tetrahedral fundamental group \(\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_3)\), universal cover \(S^3\), and \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\). An exact 180-cell quotient computation detects the integral torsion distinguishing this action from previously classified source-dihedral quotients.
