# Binomial-determinant Poincare polynomials for complete-bipartite graph nilmanifolds

## Finding
For every integer \(n\ge2\), let \(N(K_{2,n})\) be any compact Dani--Mainkar nilmanifold corresponding to the unlabelled simple complete bipartite graph with parts of sizes two and \(n\). Its rational Poincare polynomial \(P_n(t)=\sum_k\dim_{\mathbb Q}H^k(N(K_{2,n});\mathbb Q)t^k\) is
\[
P_n(t)=\sum_{0\le c\le b\le a\le n} D_n(a,b,c)t^{a+b+c}\bigl[(b-c+1)+(a-c+2)t+(a-b+1)t^2\bigr],
\]
where
\[
D_n(a,b,c)=\det\begin{pmatrix}
\binom na&\binom n{a+1}&\binom n{a+2}\\
\binom n{b-1}&\binom nb&\binom n{b+1}\\
\binom n{c-2}&\binom n{c-1}&\binom nc
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Binomial coefficients outside the interval \(0\le j\le n\) are defined to be zero. Every determinant in the displayed range is a nonnegative integer. Thus the expression is a direct, finite, cancellation-free enumeration of Betti-number contributions using \(O(n^3)\) summands, with no cochain-rank computation.

In particular, \(P_2(t)=1+4t+14t^2+25t^3+28t^4+25t^5+14t^6+4t^7+t^8\). Also, \(P_3(t)=1+5t+25t^2+63t^3+111t^4+159t^5+159t^6+111t^7+63t^8+25t^9+5t^{10}+t^{11}\). The total rational Betti dimensions for \(n=2,3,4,5\) are respectively \(116,728,4792,32568\).

## Assumptions and scope
The Lie algebra has degree-one dual generators \(a,b,x_1,\ldots,x_n,z_1,\ldots,z_n,w_1,\ldots,w_n\). The Chevalley--Eilenberg differential is \(da=db=dx_i=0\), \(dz_i=a\wedge x_i\), and \(dw_i=b\wedge x_i\), up to harmless generator sign changes. Its rational structure constants define compact nilmanifolds, and Nomizu's theorem identifies their rational de Rham cohomology with this differential graded algebra. The dimension of the manifold is \(3n+2\). No claim is made about integral torsion or the ring structure.

## Proof
Set \(A=\Lambda_{\mathbb Q}\langle x_i,z_i,w_i:1\le i\le n\rangle\), with all generators in degree one. Introduce commuting degree-zero derivations \(E_z,E_w\) on \(A\) by \(E_z(z_i)=x_i\), \(E_w(w_i)=x_i\), and zero on all other generators. The differential on \(A\otimes\Lambda\langle a,b\rangle\) is \(d=aE_z+bE_w\) (signs from exterior multiplication are understood). Because both operators preserve the number of generators in \(A\), each homogeneous \(A^r\) yields a three-stage cochain complex
\[
A^r\longrightarrow A^r\oplus A^r\longrightarrow A^r
\]
placed in total degrees \(r,r+1,r+2\). Its zeroth cohomology is \(\ker E_z\cap\ker E_w\); its second cohomology is \(A^r/(E_zA^r+E_wA^r)\); and its first cohomology has dimension equal to the sum of those two dimensions, by the zero Euler characteristic of this three-stage complex.

Over \(\mathbb C\), the three vectors \(x_i,z_i,w_i\) form the defining representation of \(GL_3\), and \(E_z,E_w\) act as matrix units \(E_{12},E_{13}\). The skew Cauchy identity gives a degree-preserving direct-sum decomposition
\[
\Lambda(\mathbb C^n\otimes\mathbb C^3)=\bigoplus_{0\le c\le b\le a\le n}S_{(a,b,c)'}(\mathbb C^n)\otimes S_{(a,b,c)}(\mathbb C^3).
\]
The transpose symbol denotes conjugate partitions. The summand indexed by \((a,b,c)\) has exterior degree \(a+b+c\), and its multiplicity is \(\dim S_{(a,b,c)'}(\mathbb C^n)\). The dual Jacobi--Trudi identity, evaluated at \(n\) ones, identifies that multiplicity with the displayed binomial determinant \(D_n(a,b,c)\), proving in particular its nonnegativity.

For a polynomial irreducible \(GL_3\)-module \(V_{(a,b,c)}\), the common kernel of \(E_{12},E_{13}\) is exactly the irreducible \(GL_2\)-submodule generated by its unique \(GL_3\) highest-weight vector. Indeed the common kernel is stable under the lower-right \(GL_2\); any extra irreducible constituent would contain another vector annihilated by all three positive-root operators \(E_{12},E_{13},E_{23}\), contradicting uniqueness of the highest-weight line. This \(GL_2\)-module has highest weight \((b,c)\) and dimension \(b-c+1\). Dually, the coinvariant space \(V_{(a,b,c)}/(E_{12}V_{(a,b,c)}+E_{13}V_{(a,b,c)})\) is the lower-right \(GL_2\)-module generated by a lowest-weight vector; it has dimension \(a-b+1\). (The latter follows by applying the same unique-highest-weight argument to the dual module with the opposite Borel.) Hence the middle cohomology dimension is \((b-c+1)+(a-b+1)=a-c+2\).

Multiplying the resulting three cohomology dimensions by the multiplicity \(D_n(a,b,c)\), shifting by total degree \(a+b+c\), and summing over all allowed partitions yields the formula over \(\mathbb C\). Extension of scalars preserves the ranks of the rational differential, so the same formula gives rational Betti numbers. Nomizu's theorem completes the argument for \(N(K_{2,n})\).

## Verification
The embedded standalone program `verify_k2n.py` independently constructs the exterior Chevalley--Eilenberg differential and computes complete graded cohomology ranks over the prime fields with three and 101 elements for \(n=1,2,3\); these agree exactly with the determinant formula. It also evaluates the positive formula for \(1\le n\le9\), checks nonnegative multiplicities and Poincare duality in the formula, and confirms the stated vectors. The \(n=1\) case, a known star, is a boundary consistency check only, not claimed novel. All finite-field comparisons are diagnostics; the infinite rational statement rests on the representation-theoretic proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
Aldi--Bevins--Chatzitheodoridis--Da Silva--Frias--Mejia Gomez, arXiv:2611.17014 (first public 2026-11-12), derive all-degree formulas for path-graph nilmanifolds and general graph-diameter lower bounds, but not this complete-bipartite family. Aldi--Butler--Gardiner--Grandini--Lichtenwalner--Pan, arXiv:2402.14188, give general induced-subgraph cohomological decompositions, explicit third Betti numbers, and full results for star graphs, but not this \(n\ge2\) family of complete bipartite graphs with four-cycles. The general methods make computation possible in principle, not this explicit all-degree determinant expression. The \(n=1\) specialization and low-degree values are known or implied and are not originality claims. The proof uses standard skew Cauchy, dual Jacobi--Trudi, highest-weight representation facts and Nomizu's theorem.

## Limitations
The result determines rational Betti numbers, not integral cohomology, cup products, or minimal models. It does not assert that the formula is the first one ever derivable from standard representation theory; the claim of an explicit evaluation is subject to residual risk from unindexed or differently named representation-theoretic calculations. The formula is for \(K_{2,n}\), not arbitrary \(K_{m,n}\) with both sides growing. Independent review has not been performed.
