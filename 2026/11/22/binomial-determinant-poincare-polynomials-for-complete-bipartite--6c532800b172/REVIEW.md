# Scientific review: binomial-determinant Poincare polynomials for complete-bipartite graph nilmanifolds

## Correctness: PASS
The precise claim is a rational cohomology formula for every \(n\ge2\) and the specified standard Dani--Mainkar nilmanifold of \(K_{2,n}\). The differential \(dz_i=a\wedge x_i\), \(dw_i=b\wedge x_i\) has the required two-stage graph structure. The two commuting degree-zero operators \(E_{12},E_{13}\) yield a Koszul three-term complex in each interior wedge degree. The degree-zero invariants and degree-two coinvariants of an irreducible \(GL_3\)-module of highest weight \((a,b,c)\) have dimensions \(b-c+1\) and \(a-b+1\). This follows from the unique highest/lowest-weight line and its lower-right \(GL_2\) orbit; Euler characteristic gives the middle rank. Skew Cauchy and dual Jacobi--Trudi account for every multiplicity and yield the stated polynomial. Nomizu identifies the Lie cohomology with nilmanifold rational cohomology. All cohomological degrees are covered, including zero and top degree.

An independent explicit wedge-differential implementation agrees in all degrees over both \(\mathbb F_3\) and \(\mathbb F_{101}\) for \(n=1,2,3\). The integer formula is separately checked for \(1\le n\le9\) with palindromicity and nonnegative determinant multiplicities. These finite checks do not prove an all-length theorem; the representation argument is the proof. Risk: the standard representation-theoretic identities are invoked rather than reproved from first principles.

## Originality: PASS with priority caveat
The November 2026 source arXiv:2611.17014v1 determines the path-graph family and a general diameter bound, not the complete-bipartite family. The 2024 primary source arXiv:2402.14188v3 supplies general graph cohomology decompositions, third-degree formulas and the full star-graph case, but does not give an explicit closed all-degree expression for \(K_{2,n}\), \(n\ge2\). Such a formula can in principle be deduced by evaluating the general complex; the new assertion is the specific, efficient determinant evaluation of that complex. The \(n=1\) case and general degree-one through degree-three facts are not claimed as novel.

A 2015 paper of Khaksari, DOI:10.1142/S1793557115500461, mentions an exotic complete-bipartite graph in its abstract, making it a genuine priority caveat. No accessible full text could be obtained from arXiv, open sources or the available institutional route; its abstract does not establish an all-\(n\) \(K_{2,n}\) rational Poincare formula or an implication of this one. A distinct labeled-graph Lie-algebra paper (arXiv:2308.00272) computes derivations, not cohomology. The general computation method is not a previously published exact determinant result. Unindexed representation-theoretic equivalents remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The graph family is the first complete-bipartite family beyond stars, featuring four-cycles for \(n\ge2\). It is a canonical next step from exact star and path cohomology. The theorem provides every Betti number simultaneously, with integer nonnegative multiplicities computed from \(O(n^3)\) small determinants instead of an exterior chain complex of size exponential in \(n\). It yields naturally palindromic Betti vectors and highly nontrivial total dimensions. The scope and exact mathematical object are independently motivated; the result is not merely a low-degree or one-example calculation.

## Residual limits
No integral cohomology, torsion classification, cup-product algebra, or extension to arbitrary \(K_{m,n}\) is asserted. The named 2015 priority source could not be fully compared and must be checked when access is possible. The current review is not independent mathematical certification.
