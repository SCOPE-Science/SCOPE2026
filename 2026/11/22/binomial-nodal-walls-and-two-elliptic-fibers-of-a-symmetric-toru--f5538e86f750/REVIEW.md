# Self-review: symmetric torus-type power-sum pencil

## Correctness — PASS

The base locus is smooth because dependent gradients imply equal nonzero coordinates and violate \(G=0\). After elimination using \(F=0\), every critical point outside the base has indicator support of size \(k\), forcing \(t=-k\). The exact Hessian has one radial zero eigenvalue and all other eigenvalues nonzero. Bézout plus the delta-genus bound proves irreducibility for plane members, and restriction gives integrality in higher dimensions. The plane genus formula then gives \(4,1,1,3\). A standalone exact symbolic checker verifies the Hessian and counts for \(3\le n\le7\); it is supplementary evidence, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with recorded risk

The 2025 Cogolludo-Agustín--Elduque paper treats generic fiber-type curves and classically six-cuspidal torus sextics; its Example 5.5 does not give the marked singular fibers of this explicit symmetric pencil. The 2002 Oka--Pho classification already has the \(6A_2+3A_1\) basket and its genus-one value in table nt19: that basket and genus are not asserted to be new. The novelty is the *complete parameter-wall census* for \(G=\sum X_i^2,H=\sum X_i^3\), including the second elliptic specialization and the binomial all-dimensional theorem. Separate searches of nearby full texts, records and synonyms found no matching family. An inaccessible or unindexed symmetric example remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Computing all critical values and all ordinary-node counts across every projective dimension is a structural classification of a natural power-sum torus hypersurface pencil. It explains why exactly two plane fibers have genus-one normalization while remaining integral. This answers a geometric question about the atypical fibers that the generic-fiber fundamental-group literature does not decide.
