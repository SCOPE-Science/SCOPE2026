# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The cyclic incidence matrix \(I+wS\) has determinant \(w^n+1\) for odd \(n\), and its alternating-series inverse maps every vector with entries between \(1\) and \(w\) strictly into the unit coefficient cube. Those \(w^n\) representatives and zero exhaust the fundamental parallelepiped. The affine grading selects exactly the residue-zero coordinate sums and yields the stated Ehrhart numerator; the smallest positive representative degree proves the first-failure threshold in the empty-simplex range. The exact-arithmetic standalone program checked \(3686\) representatives and \(32\) direct lattice-point dilations across seven parameter pairs. Finite evidence is supplementary to the proof.
