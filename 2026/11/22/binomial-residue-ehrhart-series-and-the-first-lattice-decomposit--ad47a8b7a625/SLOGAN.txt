For the uniformly weighted naturally oriented odd-cycle simplex \(\operatorname{conv}\{e_i+w e_{i+1}\}\), every Ehrhart numerator coefficient is an explicit bounded-composition count selected by divisibility by \(w+1\). In the empty-simplex range, the first lattice-point decomposition failure occurs at the sharp dilation \(\lceil n/(w+1)\rceil\); for weight two this delay grows unboundedly with the cycle length.
