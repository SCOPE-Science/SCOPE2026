Over a growing pointwise-named balanced biclique in the universal homogeneous triangle-free graph, uniformly selected fixed-arity tuple types condense to a graph law weighted by proper two-colourings. At every arity admitting non-bipartite new graphs, their probability decays with the sharp base \((3/4)^n\) and an explicit finite combinatorial prefactor; at arity five the prefactor is \(60/721\), and the rare pentagon has one uniformly chosen edge with old-neighbour densities \(1/3\) at its endpoints versus \(1/2\) elsewhere.
