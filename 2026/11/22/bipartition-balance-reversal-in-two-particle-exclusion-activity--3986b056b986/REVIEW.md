# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem fixes the rate convention (one exponential clock per admissible edge), equilibrium law (uniform two-sets), and counting observable (all accepted jumps). Stationary orbit masses and the three-by-three generator follow by counting cross-shore transitions. The finite irreducible chain's tilted maximal eigenvalue has derivatives \(\mu\) and \(\mu+2\langle\lambda-\mu,(-L)^{-1}(\lambda-\mu)\rangle\). Permutation averaging proves that solving the Poisson equation on three occupancy classes is exact. The two accessible difference equations yield the explicit rational formula. The sign of \(n^2-6n+4\) gives the complete strict extremizer classification; the boundary sizes, Poisson equality case and macroscopic asymptotic are checked separately. The full-state exact-rational verifier succeeded for 18 partitions and the independent symbolic verifier derived the all-size identity. These finite checks do not replace the written algebraic proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

Appert-Rolland et al. (arXiv:0804.2590) establish activity cumulants on periodic rings, and Mendonça (arXiv:1207.4106) solves the ordinary generator spectrum of complete bipartite exclusion. A published finite-network count theorem already proves nonnegative excess Fano ratios and classifies constant-rate Poisson cases, while a published Hamming-graph calculation contains the four-site square as a special case. Thus neither the tilted-generator method nor the \(F\ge1\) inequality nor the square's \(10/9\) value is new. Their explicitly inspected statements do not evaluate the arbitrary \(K_{r,s}\) Fano ratio or classify the reversal of the sharp balanced-versus-star extremizers at six sites. The exact specialization could have appeared in unindexed sources or be seen as close to known general pseudoinverse machinery; this is a genuine priority risk, not evidence of impossibility.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The number of distinct accepted hops, not just the relaxation eigenvalues, measures kinetic intermittency in a natural two-particle interacting lattice gas. A complete bipartite graph is a canonical contact network with controllable shore sizes. The theorem quantifies how topology changes long-time noise under a fixed network-size constraint, identifies a qualitative finite-size reversal, and gives a macroscopic persistent-fluctuation limit. These all-size sharp statements are not merely repeated evaluation of a one-case table.

## Verification limitations

The proof assumes two particles, equilibrium initialization, complete cross-shore connectivity and unit exchange rates per edge. No extension is justified for different hopping conventions, reservoirs, higher-particle fillings, non-bipartite graphs, full distributional Poisson convergence or dynamical phase transitions. Literature searches are not proof of absolute priority. No external independent audit has been performed.
