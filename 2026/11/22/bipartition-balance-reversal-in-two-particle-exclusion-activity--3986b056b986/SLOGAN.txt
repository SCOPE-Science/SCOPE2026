For two hard-core particles making unit-rate symmetric hops on \(K_{r,s}\), the long-time accepted-hop Fano ratio has a closed expression involving only the total number of sites and squared shore imbalance. Shore balance uniquely **maximizes** fluctuations for four or five sites but uniquely **minimizes** them at six or more, with a positive macroscopic noise excess persisting for any fixed unequal shore proportions.
