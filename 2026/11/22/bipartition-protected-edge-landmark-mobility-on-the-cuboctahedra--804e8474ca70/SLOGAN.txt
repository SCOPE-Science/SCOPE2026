The cuboctahedral graph has exactly \(168\) minimum edge-resolving landmark configurations; unrestricted replacements connect all of them, but optimality-preserving slides split into two isomorphic \(84\)-state components of diameter \(6\). The obstruction is intrinsic to the cube's bipartition: each optimum omits exactly one cube corner, whose parity cannot change along any legal slide, whereas ordinary vertex-resolving optimal landmarks remain slide-connected.
