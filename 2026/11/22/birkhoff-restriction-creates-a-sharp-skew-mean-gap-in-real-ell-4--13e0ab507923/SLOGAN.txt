On the real quartic Banach plane, the Birkhoff-orthogonality restriction strictly lowers the skew arithmetic-mean constant for every unequal positive weighting, but not at equal weights. The sharp small-weight penalty is \((\sqrt2/4-(4/27)^{1/4}/2)t+O(t^2)\), and the complete restricted expansion through cubic order has a unique moving extremizing orbit.
