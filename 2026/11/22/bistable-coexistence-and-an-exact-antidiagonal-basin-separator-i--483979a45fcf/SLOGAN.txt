In a 2023 Gaussian-cubic coupled map, a sharp positive-coupling band supports a stable equilibrium and an attracting antipodal two-cycle simultaneously, with a second, saddle two-cycle giving the exact basin separator along the entire invariant antidiagonal. The attracting cycle escapes toward infinity as coupling approaches \(1/2\), with squared amplitude \(L+\log L+O(\log L/L)\), where \(L=\log(a/(1-2b))\).
