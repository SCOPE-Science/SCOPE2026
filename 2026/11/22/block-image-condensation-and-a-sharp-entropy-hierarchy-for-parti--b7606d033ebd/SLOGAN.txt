Uniform random idempotent maps preserving a fixed number \(m\ge2\) of equal-size blocks concentrate all their image points inside one randomly chosen block. The probability of exactly \(k\) image-bearing blocks has logarithmic rate \((m-k+1)\log(m-k+1)-m\log m\) per block size, while the total point-image rank scales as \(mn/\log n\).
