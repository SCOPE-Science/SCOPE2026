# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The statement has exact quantifiers: disjoint nonempty finite alphabets and nonempty sets of full permutations. The regular CW word complex of each concatenation orientation is the join of the factors, and the intersection of the opposite orientations is exactly their disjoint union. Each component of the overlap contracts inside each join, reducing the homotopy pushout to two joins and the unreduced suspension of two connected components. That suspension splits into the two individual suspensions and one circle. The full-block specialization uses the established Farmer/Björner–Wachs derangement sphere theorem. Exact \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_{101}\) checker runs covered 234 finite input pairs. No finite computation is used as the all-size proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The closest full primary texts are Govc, *Fundamental Groups of Permutation Complexes* (2026; arxiv:2611.11362v1), and Chachólski–Levi–Meshulam, *On the topology of complexes of injective words* (2019/2020; arxiv:1908.03394). Govc proves only free fundamental groups. The earlier work gives full injective-word homotopy, concatenation tools and the special two-individual-permutation case, not the arbitrary two-family reversed-block union. Elizalde–Hanlon–Hersh (2026; arxiv:2610.07303v1) study pattern-avoidance shellability, not this independent two-block restriction. Indexed cyclic/dihedral reversals concern different generating sets and do not imply this claim. Equivalent results under other terminology remain possible; the comparison is not a publication-priority certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result is a general structural operation, with a complete unstable homotopy formula for **all** permutation complexes supplied as factors. Its wreath-subgroup specialization is an infinite, naturally parameterized family with a nontrivial free fundamental group and precisely determined sphere multiplicities and degrees, rather than an isolated numerical invariant. The formula also tracks the suspended factor homotopy types and join terms even when the factors themselves are not sphere wedges.

## Limitations

The theorem is nonequivariant, assumes no interleaving of blocks, and does not classify arbitrary permutation subsets or their group actions. The indexed reversed-permutation articles were comparable by available summaries only and were not certified as noncovering in full. Checks on small inputs corroborate but do not prove the infinite theorem.
