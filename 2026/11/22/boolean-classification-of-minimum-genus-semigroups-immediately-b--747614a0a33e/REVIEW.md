# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem identifies exactly one forced Apéry coordinate of height four. Kunz inequalities at the complementary indices yield all sharp lower bounds, and equality forces a disjoint collection of independent choices \((1,2)\) versus \((2,1)\). Every other Kunz inequality is automatic for these vectors, ensuring closure and the claimed Frobenius number. The proof works for all admissible integer multiplicities. The extracted checker exhaustively confirms both full minimizer sets, not only their sizes, for nineteen finite cases and independently checks the semigroup closure and genus there.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)

The new counts apply to \(F=3m+1\) and \(F=3m+2\), which lie strictly beyond the hypothesis \(m\ge(F+1)/3\) of the November 2026 Bras-Amorós–Torra counting formula. Its July predecessor also supplies finite enumeration tables; these may include small cases, not the unbounded formulas. The 2019 Branco–Ojeda–Rosales Kunz-based algorithms are credited; no claim is made to invent their general enumeration method. Comparisons of published summaries and primary material found no equivalent closed count. Unindexed coverage and incompletely accessible appendices remain risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Frobenius number, genus and multiplicity are canonical numerical-semigroup invariants. This theorem fills the first two admissible boundary lines immediately outside a recent sharp counting regime, classifies the irreducible stratum without a genus cutoff, and discovers a Boolean-cube parameter space with exponentially growing counts.

## Limits

The result does not count higher-genus semigroups, does not address lines beyond \(F=3m+2\), and has not been independently audited. Bounded computational verification is not an infinite proof.
