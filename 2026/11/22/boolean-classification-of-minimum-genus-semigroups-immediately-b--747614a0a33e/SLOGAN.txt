At the two Frobenius lines \(F=3m+1\) and \(F=3m+2\) immediately beyond a recent multiparameter-counting range, all minimum-genus numerical semigroups form explicit Boolean families of sizes \(2^{\lfloor(m-2)/2\rfloor}\) and \(2^{\lfloor(m-3)/2\rfloor}\), respectively. These give complete classifications of the corresponding irreducible semigroups for all admissible multiplicities.
