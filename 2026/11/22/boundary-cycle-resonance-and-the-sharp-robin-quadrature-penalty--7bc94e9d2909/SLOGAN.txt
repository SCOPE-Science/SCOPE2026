One-point Robin boundary quadrature has an exact parity-and-node-product condition for invisible finite-element boundary traces, so increasing a boundary penalty need not enforce Dirichlet data. On a single square element, shifting one quadrature node by \(\varepsilon\) resolves the obstruction exactly on the sharp scale \(\lambda\varepsilon^2\), with limiting coefficient \(1/(24+9\lambda\varepsilon^2)\) when that product converges.
