# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The off-diagonal entries are derived from the Bergman monomial normalization, the diagonal self-commutator and an integration-by-parts beta expectation. Taylor–beta moments give a remainder bounded by the required next power, including the integrable-derivative contribution away from the endpoint. The singular-value coefficient follows from a block-freezing proof of the regular-variation counting law, not from finite numerical tests. The exceptional \(s=1/2\) power is checked by exact algebra. The checker verifies critical formulas but is not an infinite-dimensional certificate.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority qualification

Simanek (arXiv:2001.07823v1, §§2–3) already gives the Jacobi companion for power radial symbols and its qualitative essential-spectrum interval; Le–Simanek (arXiv:2003.07330v1, Theorems 2.1 and 3.1) gives the broader shift-plus-diagonal norm criterion and compact comparison. This result does not claim those reductions as new. The new assessed content is a sharp singular-value Weyl coefficient for the normalized remainder, its full boundary-jet hierarchy, and the explicit trace-class resonance at a unique positive radial-power exponent. Kozhan (2005) addresses compact off-diagonal Jacobi matrices under a fast-variation condition incompatible with the present polynomial tails; the general asymptotic machinery could have antecedents elsewhere. Relevant indexed research records about polyanalytic Bergman boundary jets and unrelated Toeplitz commutators concern different operators. No exhaustive historical priority is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Compactness alone does not determine spectral decay. The calculation recovers every singular-value scale and a sharp ideal boundary in the exact Jacobi companion used to decide hyponormality. The exceptional radial exponent changes trace-class status and demonstrates a natural cancellation mechanism; arbitrary higher-order cancellations can be constructed using smooth radial polynomial symbols. These features give quantitative control beyond a routine reformulation of the essential-spectrum theorem.

No extension to all weights, all radial measures or flat symbols is claimed. Independent mathematical validation has not occurred.
