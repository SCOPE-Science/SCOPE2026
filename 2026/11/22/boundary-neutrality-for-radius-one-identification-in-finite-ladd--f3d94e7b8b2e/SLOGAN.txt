The smallest closed-neighbourhood identifying code of the open two-row ladder \(P_n\square K_2\) has exactly \(\lceil6n/7\rceil+\mathbf1_{\{n\bmod7\in\{1,2\}\}}\) sensors for every \(n\ge2\). Remarkably, for every \(n\ge5\) this equals the already known optimum for the corresponding circular ladder despite the changed boundary edges; a 256-state exact integer identity proves the all-length formula.
