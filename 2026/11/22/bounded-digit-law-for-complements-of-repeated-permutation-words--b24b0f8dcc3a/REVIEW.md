# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

After reading i host symbols and deleting d, the next deletion is possible only when i and d agree modulo q (distinct letters), i.e. the retained count i-d is a multiple of q. Therefore once a retained block begins its next q-1 letters are forced keeps, and all retained letters split into complete rotation blocks. At the initial gap and each between-block gap, minimal deletions are respectively s_1 and the least nonnegative phase differences; their sum is necessary. Conversely delete exactly these minimum gaps, retain the specified full blocks, and put all slack in the final deleted gap. Source and deleted phases agree in each gap, so the deleted word is exactly P^k, not merely a same-length subsequence. Rotation starts identify distinct phase sequences; passing to increments is bijective, so the generating function counts bounded digits and the maximal sum is (q-1)m. Empty cases give the one expected word: empty for m=0 and P^m for k=0. The fixed-alphabet count fraction is the cumulative law of iid uniform digits with the stated mean and variance; the normal limit follows by CLT and continuity at the moving threshold. An independent generator of feasible deletion embeddings checks whole literal residual sets, a separate inclusion-exclusion coefficient sum, and saturation in 120 cases, reproducing counts 76, 221 and 121 and extending through q=6.
