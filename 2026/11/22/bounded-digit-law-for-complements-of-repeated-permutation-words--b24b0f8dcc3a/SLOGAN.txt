Deleting a prescribed \(k\)-fold permutation period from \(m+k\) copies leaves exactly the concatenations of \(m\) cyclic rotations whose cumulative least-residue phase cost is at most \(qk\). The exact output count is the lower tail of \((1+z+\cdots+z^{q-1})^m\), and all \(q^m\) phase words first become possible at \(k=\lceil(q-1)m/q\rceil\).
