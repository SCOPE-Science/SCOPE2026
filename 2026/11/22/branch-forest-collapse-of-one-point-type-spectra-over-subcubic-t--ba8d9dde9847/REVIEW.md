# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

In the homogeneous \(K_4\)-free graph, nonalgebraic one-types correspond exactly to triangle-free neighborhoods of the named base. For a tree line graph those neighborhoods are edge subsets of maximum degree at most two. Inclusion–exclusion over the full incident stars at the degree-three tree vertices yields the exact branch-forest formula. The coefficient identities follow because two forbidden stars meet in at most one edge and three or more have union size at least seven. The all-order spider family is a direct consequence, not a computational extrapolation. Direct triangle enumeration independently reproduced the formula for every labelled subcubic tree of orders two through six; exact Prüfer-tree classification confirms the unique first collision. The standalone verifier also checked 977 larger fixed-seed subcubic examples and 25 witness pairs.

Searches also considered the linear-forest and degree-constrained graph-factor formulations. Conant's initiating 2014 Henson-graph article was available by abstract but not in full text here; its reported forking/dividing focus is different, but no full-document negative novelty inference is made. Hidden older graph-polynomial coverage remains a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result identifies an exact, all-size loss-of-information mechanism in a natural graded logical-type statistic while recovering part of the forgotten branching geometry from two coefficients. The sharp first-collision and connected all-order witness family turn this mechanism into a genuine reconstruction boundary, rather than an isolated equality of small integers. It does not assert that identical branch forests are the only cause of polynomial equality.
