For a subcubic tree, the entire adjacency-graded spectrum of nonalgebraic one-point types over its named line graph in the homogeneous \(K_4\)-free graph depends only on the total edge count and the induced forest of degree-three vertices. This invariant first fails to distinguish nonisomorphic subcubic trees at six vertices, with a unique spider pair there and connected collision pairs at every larger order, although its low-degree coefficients still recover the number and adjacency count of branching vertices.
