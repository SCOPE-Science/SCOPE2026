For every odd \(n\ge5\), the projective space curve \((T_2(u),T_4(u),T_n(u))\) attains the Castelnuovo genus bound, with exactly \((n-1)/2\) affine nodes and one branch at infinity of semigroup \(\langle n-4,n-2,n\rangle\). This determines its complete reduced singularity basket, infinity delta invariant and conductor.
