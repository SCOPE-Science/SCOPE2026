In finite forests, the maximum \(2m\) open-adjacency one-point types over \(m\geq1\) named vertices occur precisely when the traces are empty, all singletons, and the edges of a spanning tree on those vertices. For \(m\geq2\), there are exactly \(m^{m-2}\) extremal labelled trace families, and their smallest tree hosts have \(3m-1\) vertices.
