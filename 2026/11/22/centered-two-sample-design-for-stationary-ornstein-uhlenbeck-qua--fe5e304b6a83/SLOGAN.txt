Two noiseless measurements that optimally predict the integrated squared energy of a stationary real Ornstein–Uhlenbeck path must lie symmetrically about the observation-window midpoint, strictly away from its endpoints. At long horizons every optimal separation converges to \(2.554928950095267\ldots/\lambda\), with a unique universal dimensionless limit; the exact finite-horizon gap is determined by a one-variable formula without a uniqueness claim.
