# Self-review

**Correctness (PASS).** The forest root-set generating identity follows from the prescribed-root all-minors theorem. A Schur complement of the signed balanced-biclique Laplacian gives the multiplier formula. The bounded two-integer equation has the unique possible root class, and this yields the exact nullity for all sizes. Standalone integer enumeration and determinant comparisons corroborate boundary cases; the general proof does not extrapolate from computation.

**Originality (PASS, bounded).** The matrix-forest determinant is classical (Chebotarev–Agaev; Chebotarev–Shamis). The new assertion is the all-size **signed vertex-character zero set**, not a claim of inventing forest determinant methods. Closely indexed spanning-forest edge-spectrum and rooted-forest component-count results concern different observables. An unindexed special signed-determinant formula and an inaccessible component-count paper are residual priority risks.

**Value (PASS).** Root positions are natural observables of random spanning rooted forests. The precise parity switch for balanced bicliques, high-degree-only loss and exact asymptotic nullity fraction quantify when their Boolean convolution statistics can recover arbitrary signals. This is a complete infinite classification, rather than a routine finite increment.

**Limits.** The theorem is for labelled balanced bicliques with n at least two, weighted by the number of root choices per forest. It gives neither unbalanced classifications nor least-positive-multiplier conditioning. Historical priority is subject to independent literature assessment.
