## Mathematical value — PASS
The reversible cellular-automaton group is a natural and infinite algebraic invariant of the first nonabelian Hamiltonian alphabet. The surprising finite-order rigidity after removing drift and the exact split group identify behavior not exposed by yes/no reversibility algorithms. The complete local-rule census and sharp entropy spectrum quantify the same structural phenomenon and make the classification reusable without a general entropy algorithm. These are not arbitrary parameter slices or a mere restatement of the previously known surjectivity criterion.
