Every group cellular automaton on the full quaternion shift \(Q_8^{\mathbb Z}\) is either a square-zero central map or a spatial shift composed with a finite-order central shear and an alphabet automorphism. Its reversible group is explicitly \(\mathbb Z\times((\bigoplus_{j\ne0}\mathbb F_2^2)\rtimes S_4)\), every drift-normalized reversible map has order at most four, and its entropy spectrum is exactly \(\{m\log8:m\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\}\).
