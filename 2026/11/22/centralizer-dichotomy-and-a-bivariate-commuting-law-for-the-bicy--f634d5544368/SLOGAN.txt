In the bicyclic monoid, precisely the idempotents have noncommutative element-centralizers; all other centralizers are explicitly described commutative submonoids. The full bivariate commuting-pair length series is \(\frac{1-xy}{(1-x)^2(1-y)^2(1+xy)}\), giving an exact parity-dependent cubic ball count and a commuting probability asymptotic to \(2/N\).
