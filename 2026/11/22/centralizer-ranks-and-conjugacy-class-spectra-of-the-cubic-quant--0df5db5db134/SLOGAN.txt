For the nine-dimensional cubic quantum complete intersection at a primitive cube root, the algebra group's entire conjugacy-class-size spectrum is governed by a split quadratic cone and one exceptional quadratic direction. Over every admissible finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), its class number is \(q^4+2q^3-2q\), and the corresponding algebra has commuting probability \((q^3+2q^2-2)/q^7\).
