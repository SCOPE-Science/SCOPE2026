For the connected nonsemisimple Alexander quandles built from repeated Jordan blocks of any fixed length \(d\ge2\) over odd prime fields, the size of the **full composition-centralizer** of a retraction completely determines its full automorphism-conjugacy class; explicit formulas give every class size and centralizer size. A four-dimensional mixed-Jordan example has two nonconjugate retractions of equal image size and equal centralizer size, proving the rigidity depends on the uniform-block hypothesis.
