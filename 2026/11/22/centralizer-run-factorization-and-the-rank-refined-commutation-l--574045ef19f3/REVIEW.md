# Scientific review of the joint and extremal claims

**Correctness.** Equality of the two partial products reduces to equality of their domains. The four pair symbols form a word language with constant nonzero blocks separated by zeros, giving the joint series. The extremal exponent subtracts the number of zeros adjacent to a one from the sum of the zero and one-run counts. Internal and boundary gap costs give the two stated sharp bounds and the denser-regime classification. The finite checker corroborates rather than replaces the analytical proofs.

**Originality and scope.** The individual restricted partner polynomial follows from the established initial-chain/terminal-chain characterization of commuting partial injections, as explained in `RESULT.md`; it is an input, not a separate novelty claim. The strongest inspected results on complete centralizers, unrestricted product ranks, or interval supports do not give the joint rank series for arbitrary support words or the prescribed-rank maximum in the restricted family. Historical equivalents in differently indexed literature remain possible.

**Value.** The two ranks correspond to the ranks of the shift-one ideal generators. Their joint commutation census and the exact largest restricted neighborhood at each prescribed rank answer distinct average and extremal questions that are not determined by the size of the generating families.
