The full monoid of coefficient-field-fixing substitutions of \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^4)\) has an explicit, characteristic-sensitive centralizer spectrum: its ordered commuting-pair count is \(5q^4-4q^2\) in characteristic two and \(3q^4+9q^3-11q^2\) in odd characteristic. The entire truncated substitution monoid is commutative exactly through length four over \(\mathbb F_2\), but only through length two over every larger finite field.
