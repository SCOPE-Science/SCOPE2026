In the Hecke–Kiselman monoid of an inward-directed star with \(m\) leaves, every centralizer belongs to one of two explicit combinatorial families. The number of ordered commuting pairs is \(7^m+3\cdot4^m\) among \((2^m+3^m)^2\) ordered pairs, so the commuting probability is asymptotic to \((7/9)^m\).
