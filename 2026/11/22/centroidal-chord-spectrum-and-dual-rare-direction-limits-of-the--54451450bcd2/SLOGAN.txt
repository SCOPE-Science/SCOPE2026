For a Haar-uniform line through the centroid of the regular tetrahedron, the complete chord-length distribution has an exact one-dimensional integral representation on \([\sqrt2,4/\sqrt3]\). Its rare shortest and longest chords obey respectively \(3\varepsilon\) and \(\sqrt3\,\varepsilon^2/(4\pi)\) probability laws, with uniform elliptical and triangular rescaled orientation limits.
