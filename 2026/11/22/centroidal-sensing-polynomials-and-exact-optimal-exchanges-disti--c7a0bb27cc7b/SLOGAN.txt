The Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have the same strongly regular parameters and both need five centroidal detectors, but they have different complete successful-detector polynomials: their difference is \(12z^5(z+4)(z^2+4z+8)\). Thus independent random sensing is strictly more reliable on the rook graph at every intermediate deployment probability, although every nine-detector subset succeeds on either graph.
