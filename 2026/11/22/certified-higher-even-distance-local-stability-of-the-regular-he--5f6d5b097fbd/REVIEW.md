# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed domain is a small neighborhood of the regular convex labeled hexagon modulo direct similarities, with the nine even integer powers from 8 to 24 inclusive. An origin-to-edge triangle decomposition produces exact rational polynomial moments after an oblique coordinate change. The pairwise-distance energy is an explicit finite convolution of these moments. Fixing two opposite vertices gives an eight-dimensional slice transverse to all four similarity directions. For every exponent, exact differentiation gives a zero first derivative and a rational symmetric eight-by-eight Hessian rescaled by a strictly positive factor. The package includes all 72 positive exact LDL pivots, complete matrices, and executable reconstruction of every identity. Ordinary Taylor expansion and the unique similarity gauge finish the proof. The independent quadrature checks are only numerical diagnostics.

## Originality — PASS, historical priority provisional

The full 2023 study by Bogosel, Bucur and Fragalà contains numerical hexagon Hessian positivity for powers through 24, but explicitly identifies certification as further work; its proved power-2/power-4 results and linear-image lemma do not imply full vertex-direction local positivity for these nine higher powers. A separately proved exact hexagon result concerns power 6 only, and earlier pentagon certificates concern a different side count. Bonacini–Cristoferi–Topaloglu prove different three- and four-sided cases. Searches for aliases (shape Hessian, nonlocal Riesz local stability, pairwise distance moments), stronger implications, indexed exact tables and accessible related records yielded no known covering proof. This does not exclude unindexed proofs.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The first even polygon order with known *global* high-power symmetry breaking presents an intrinsically motivated boundary between local and global symmetry. Completing all nine higher-even hexagon cases in the explicit finite exponent range numerically tested by the main source certifies a meaningful unresolved family rather than an arbitrary single extra exponent. The positive Hessians apply to all eight nontrivial shape degrees of freedom. The contribution is a local result and not a solution to the global polygonal Riesz conjecture.

## Prior-work and scientific limitations

Closest sources: Bogosel–Bucur–Fragalà (2023) numerical Hessians and its exact power-2/power-4 theorem; an earlier exact power-6 hexagon local result; an exact higher-even pentagon result; Bonacini–Cristoferi–Topaloglu (2021) on triangles and quadrilaterals. No certified statement about exponents outside the nine listed, arbitrary nonconvex polygons, global minimizers or finite neighborhood radius is made. Independent mathematical review remains necessary for the algebraic-to-geometric bridge and priority.
