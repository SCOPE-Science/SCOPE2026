For every even exponent \(k\in\{8,10,12,14,16,18,20,22,24\}\), the regular Euclidean hexagon is a strict local minimizer, modulo similarities, of the fixed-area normalized pairwise-distance \(k\)-energy among arbitrary nearby convex hexagons. Exact rational moment integration and eight positive Hessian pivots per exponent turn the published numerical observations into nine reproducible local theorems, without asserting global optimality.
