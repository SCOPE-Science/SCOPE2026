For the characterization-based uniformity test of Ebner, Liebenberg and Visagie, the leading simple-null covariance eigenvalue, left uncalculated in the original paper, is rigorously enclosed by \(0.11573423652792<\lambda_1<0.11573423652794\). Exact rational polynomial iteration and a spectral residual inequality certify the enclosure and hence the sharp logarithmic null-tail exponent \(1/(2\lambda_1)\).
