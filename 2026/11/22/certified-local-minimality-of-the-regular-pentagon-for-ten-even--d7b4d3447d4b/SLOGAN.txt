For each even \(k\in\{6,8,\ldots,24\}\), the regular pentagon is a **strict local minimizer modulo similarities** of the area-normalized \(k\)-th pairwise-distance energy: all six transverse Hessian pivots have validated positive lower bounds from outward-rounded integer arithmetic. The result turns earlier numerical evidence into an exact-checkable local theorem for these ten exponents, without claiming global optimality.
