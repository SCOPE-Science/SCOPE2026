In characteristic \(5\), every smooth genus-five Humbert curve has exceptional generic canonical order sequence \((0,1,2,3,5)\) and an explicit degree-\(128\) Weierstrass divisor supported at all 40 ramification points and over the critical roots of its four-branch polynomial. Exactly \(88\), \(72\), or \(56\) distinct Weierstrass points occur, according to the three possible multiplicity patterns of the derivative; every pattern is realized.
