The full characteristic-three fifth-jet substitution group has exactly \(6q+4\) conjugacy classes over \(\mathbb F_{3^e}\) for odd \(e\), but \(6q+16\) for even \(e\), with every multiplier-resolved class size explicit. Its Nottingham subgroup first acquires order-nine elements precisely when the second and third coefficients satisfy \(b\ne0\) and \(c\ne b^2\), occupying the proportion \((1-1/q)^2\).
