Every linear-rank-one anti–Rota–Baxter operator on \(M_n(k)\), \(n\ge2\), has square-zero image or belongs to an explicitly classified characteristic-two or characteristic-three idempotent-image family. The characteristic-three family is precisely where \(R^2
e0\) becomes possible, with exact finite-field counts for every operator and for each prescribed nonzero composition eigenvalue.
