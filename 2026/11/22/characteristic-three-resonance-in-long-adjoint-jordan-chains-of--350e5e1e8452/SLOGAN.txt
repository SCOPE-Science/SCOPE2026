For the ten-dimensional strictly triangular Lie algebra \(\mathfrak n_5(\mathbb F_q)\), the number of size-three and size-four Jordan blocks of every inner derivation has an exact five-stratum finite-field census. Characteristic three forbids a lone size-four block without a size-three companion and changes the dimensions of the third-power-zero locus from six eight-dimensional components to two nine-dimensional and one eight-dimensional component.
