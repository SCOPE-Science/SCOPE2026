For the rectangular-band semigroup algebra \(\mathbb F_q[S_{m,n}]\), every composition-idempotent derivation has a unique two-projection normal form, yielding an exact Gaussian-binomial rank polynomial. Characteristic two permits simultaneous nonzero left and right projections and raises the number of geometrically irreducible reduced components from \(m+n-1\) to \(mn\).
