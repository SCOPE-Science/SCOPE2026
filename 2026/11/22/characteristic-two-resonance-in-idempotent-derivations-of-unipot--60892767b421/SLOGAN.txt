For the almost-abelian Lie algebra whose defining action is a single unipotent Jordan block, composition-idempotent derivations have a complete rank census: normally there are \(1+q^N\), but in characteristic two with even module dimension the total jumps to \(1+2q^N\), with an additional rank-\(N/2+1\) family. In that exceptional family, the brackets of image elements span the entire kernel.
