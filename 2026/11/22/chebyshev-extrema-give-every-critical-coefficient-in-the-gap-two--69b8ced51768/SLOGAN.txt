For odd \(k\), every real-parameter zero-count transition of the harmonic trinomial \(z^{k+2}+c\overline z^{\,k}-1\) is the explicit algebraic transform of a positive critical height of the Chebyshev polynomial \(U_k\). The total transition window has sharp width \(2\log(k)/k+O((\log k)^2/k^2)\), resolving an exact-coefficient question for the entire gap-two family.
