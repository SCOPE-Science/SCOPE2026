For every \(m\ge2\) prime to five, the characteristic-five contracted-product surface from \(z^5-z=x^m\) has the same rational Chow motive and Hasse–Weil zeta function as \(E\times\mathbb P^1\), and its integral numerical divisor lattice is \(U\). Yet its coherent irregularity is \(\lfloor4(m+1)/5\rfloor\), so one fixed rational motive occurs among infinitely many nonisomorphic smooth surfaces with unbounded Hodge irregularity.
