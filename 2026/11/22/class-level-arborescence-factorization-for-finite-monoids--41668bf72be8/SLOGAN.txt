For every finite monoid, the class-weighted rooted in-arborescence polynomial of its divisibility digraph factors into one explicit forest term for each Green \(\mathcal J\)-class. In a uniform rooted arborescence, exits from distinct nonunit classes are independent, with each class of size \(w\) having exactly \(1+\operatorname{Binomial}(w-1,q/(w+q))\) exits, where \(q\) is the total size of its strict upper classes.
