# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The five generators make the Clebsch graph triangle-free of diameter two, so complementing a geodesic transversal gives an induced subgraph of maximum degree one. The maximality lemma also has a short independent proof. An edge in direction i lies in a coordinate half-cube that induces a matching. Its endpoints have disjoint open neighborhoods of size five; the other six vertices of that half-cube are precisely the vertices adjacent to neither endpoint. Any dissociation set containing that edge therefore lies in that half-cube. For independent sets, translate one point to zero; the other words correspond to pairwise-intersecting two-subsets of five coordinates. Such a family lies in a star (giving an open neighborhood with zero included) or in a triangle of pairs (giving a subset of a coordinate half-cube). The open neighborhoods are maximal since their center sees five of their points and every other outside vertex sees two. This proves the classification without using the frozen programs. Different-coordinate half-cubes intersect in four points, opposite ones in zero, and each four-subset of an open neighborhood has a common coordinate bit. Counting subsets of the maximal sets and the 160 forbidden triples yields the stated 2143 transversals. Above complementary size four the half-cube regions cannot exchange states, and at size four they join along all different-coordinate intersections; the size-five neighborhoods attach there too. Nested chains realize distances 8 and 16, matching the symmetric-difference bounds. At later budgets each new state can move to an inclusion-minimal transversal already present. A separate exhaustive enumeration reproduced the entire census, budget table, and all 45 distances.
