For the full Margolis–Rhodes monoid of any heterogeneous clique windmill \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_iK_{s_i}\), every idempotent image-rank coefficient follows from an explicit blade-factor product with one shared-hub correction. For equal blades of fixed size \(s\ge2\), the rank of a uniform idempotent has a Gaussian limit with explicitly determined linear mean and variance; in friendship graphs they are \(m\) and \(m/3\).
