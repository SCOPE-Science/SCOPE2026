The complete closed-neighborhood shattering polynomial of every Doob graph \(D(m,n)\) of diameter \(d=2m+n\ge3\) has cubic coefficient \(4^d(27\binom{d+1}{3}-8m)\) and no higher terms. Consequently, every Shrikhande factor produces an exact decrement of \(8\cdot4^d\), distinguishing all fixed-diameter Doob graphs despite their common Hamming intersection array and VC dimension three.
