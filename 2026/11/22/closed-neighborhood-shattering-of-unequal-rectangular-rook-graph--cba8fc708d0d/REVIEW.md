# Review of the closed rectangular-rook shattering theorem

## Correctness (C): PASS
The claim concerns inclusive row–column crosses on full finite boards. For every nonempty shattered support the empty trace forces a spare row and a spare column, and singleton traces force the corresponding selected-cell bipartite graph to be a star forest. The only permitted shapes through three edges, their necessary side-size conditions, and eight explicit witnesses for the three-edge case yield the displayed coefficients. Every four-edge star forest has a three-edge excluded subshape unless it consists of two disjoint two-edge stars; the latter cannot realize one specified subset by a single row-and-column center. Thus no larger supports can be shattered. The proof handles one-dimensional boards and the small exceptional cases separately. The actual extracted `VERIFY.py` run compared 144135 finite supports, including all four-supports on 21 rectangles, against independent direct neighborhood traces; this is corroboration rather than an infinite proof. Potential risk: formal transcription of the indicator ranges, independently guarded by the displayed witness and full enumerations.

## Originality (O): PASS, with historical-priority qualification
The original scope is a support-by-support classification and complete shattering polynomial for **unequal** rectangles; neither the known dimension-only mixed-alphabet results nor the uniform-alphabet complete polynomial imply those coefficients by direct specialization. The closest indexed primary material is Adler–Benediktsson–Macpherson (2020, open Hamming neighborhoods), Coudert–Csikós–Ducoffe–Viennot (2024, general closed graph VC algorithms), and the following existing specific records, whose full result texts were inspected: the open rectangular-rook polynomial, the closed homogeneous Hamming polynomial, the closed 4-by-4 rook/Shrikhande comparison, and mixed-alphabet online dimensions. The equal-side case, four-by-four value, and all static VC thresholds are fully credited; none is asserted new. Searches also used row-column cross, line graph, pair-incidence union, and disjunction aliases. Full bibliographic uniqueness is not established; an unindexed older row-column-cross census or a different equivalent formulation remains a priority risk. The closest primary texts are individually referenced in RESULT.md.

## Value (V): PASS
A complete natural two-parameter support classification supplies strictly more information than the previously known VC-dimension table. The two nonequivalent 24-vertex rook graphs on 3-by-8 and 4-by-6 boards shatter every pair and have equal VC dimension three but have exactly 1008 and 1080 shattered triples. Thus the third coefficient detects geometry invisible to size and dimension, with a sharp boundary for three-face emergence at both side sizes at least three and one at least four. This is a natural structural classification, not a routine repetition of the already-known square-board computation. The result does not claim a larger theorem for induced subgraphs or adaptive dimension.
