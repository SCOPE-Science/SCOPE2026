For closed neighborhoods of a full rectangular rook graph, shattered supports are exactly small bipartite star forests with one unused row and column: single edges, adjacent or disjoint pairs, and a two-edge star plus a disjoint edge. The resulting complete two-parameter shattering polynomial distinguishes unequal rectangles with the same number of cells, the same pair-shattering data, and the same VC dimension; all square-board cases and existing dimension results are credited to prior work.
