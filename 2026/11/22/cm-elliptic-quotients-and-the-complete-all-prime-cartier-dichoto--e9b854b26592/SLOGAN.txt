The genus-two Chebyshev curve \(y^2=x^5-5x^3+5x\) has Jacobian \(4\)-isogenous to the square of a CM elliptic curve of discriminant \(-20\). In every odd characteristic other than \(5\), the reduction is either ordinary or superspecial, according to whether \(\left(\frac{-5}{p}\right)\) is \(+1\) or \(-1\); intermediate Cartier ranks never occur.
