# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is conditional on an explicitly stated bounded-step simple ray and otherwise invokes standard operator-algebra facts. The given shift has finite propagation, is an isometry, and has exactly one missing output basis vector. Its Fredholm index is \(-1\). The partial-isometry equivalence of the identity and the identity minus one rank-one projection makes the compact inclusion's \(K_0\) map zero. The six-term exact sequence then yields both group statements, with an explicit section to the integer-valued index. Nuclear dimension of the full algebra is at most one under Property A by Guo–Li–Zhang–Zhu's stated Theorem 1.1; it passes to quotients. A nonzero \(K_1\) class excludes dimension zero for the unital corona. The known extension permanence of nuclearity excludes finite nuclear dimension when Property A fails. No computational check substitutes for an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk

The closest theorem is Guo–Li–Zhang–Zhu's sharp full-uniform-Roe-algebra nuclear-dimension result, not a result about the corona. Braga–Farah–Vignati develop uniform Roe coronas and their rigidity. Li–Willett analyze \(K\)-theory of uniform Roe algebras. Winter–Zacharias supply quotient permanence, and six-term exactness is classical. These ingredients provide general constraints, but the searched primary statements do not identify the explicit finite-propagation Fredholm witness and complete relative \(K\)-group splitting for every coarse-ray metric space, nor the resulting exact corona dimension. The derivation uses standard tools, so a previously published implicit corollary in coarse-index literature is a real remaining risk; no exhaustive novelty assertion is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The corona is a natural large-scale operator algebra, rather than an arbitrarily chosen quotient. The result identifies both the complete compact-extension \(K\)-theory effect and an exact, not merely upper-bounded, nuclear dimension in the natural geometric regime of infinite bounded-degree connected graphs. The index obstruction is independent of Property A, while Property A decides dimension one versus infinity. This clean separation supplies a reusable geometric criterion for computing corona regularity and identifying nonzero homotopy classes. Its dependence on the recent breakthrough is explicit, not presented as a new proof of that breakthrough.
