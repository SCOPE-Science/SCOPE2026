For every odd prime power \(q\), every \(d\) with \(\gcd(d,q+1)=2\), and every nonzero coefficient making \(X^r(X^{d(q-1)}+a)\) a permutation of \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\), its sign is exactly \((-1)^{(r-1)/2}\). Thus all admissible coefficients have the same parity, a structural contrast to the coefficient-dependent signs of the neighboring index-one family.
