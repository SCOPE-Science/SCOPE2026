For complete bipartite bases \(K_{3,b}\) in the generic oriented graph forbidding transitive triangles, every coefficient of the nonalgebraic one-point type polynomial is minimized precisely by six-signature-balanced orientations and maximized by the two coherent orientations. The minimizing columns exhibit a sharp mod-six pattern, and the ratio of maximal to minimal mixed-shore type counts grows as \(\Theta(2^{b/2})\).
