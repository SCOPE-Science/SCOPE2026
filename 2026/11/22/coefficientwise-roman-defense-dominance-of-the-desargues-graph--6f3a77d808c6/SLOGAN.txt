The Desargues and dodecahedral graphs both have Roman domination number \(12\), but the Desargues graph has strictly more Roman dominating functions at every weight from \(12\) through \(29\), with equal counts at all other weights. Their exact weight-polynomial difference is \(4z^{12}(1+z)^6Q(z)\) for an explicitly positive-coefficient polynomial \(Q\), giving strict defense-success dominance under every common positive fugacity parameter.
