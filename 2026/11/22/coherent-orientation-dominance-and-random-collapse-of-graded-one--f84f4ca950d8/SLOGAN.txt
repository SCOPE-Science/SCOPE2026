For three-sorted directed-triangle-free homogeneous structures, every adjacency-degree coefficient of a third-sort one-point type spectrum over a complete bipartite orientation is maximized by coherently orienting all old edges; the first cubic deficit counts directed two-edge paths exactly. Uniformly random balanced orientations have \(2\cdot4^n(1+o(1))\) types almost surely, whereas coherent orientations have \(2\cdot6^n-4^n\), giving an exponential separation.
