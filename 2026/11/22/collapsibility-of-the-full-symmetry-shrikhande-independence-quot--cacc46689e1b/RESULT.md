# Collapsibility of the full-symmetry Shrikhande independence quotient

## Finding
Let \(S\) be the sixteen-vertex Shrikhande graph, with vertex set \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\) and adjacency differences \(\{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1),(1,1),(-1,-1)\}\). Let \(K=\operatorname{Ind}(S)\) be its independence complex. Its full graph-automorphism group \(G\) has order \(192\). Then the ordinary **geometric orbit space** has trivial simple-homotopy type: 
\[
\boxed{|K|/G\searrow\{\mathrm{point}\}.}
\]
More precisely, the barycentrically subdivided quotient has a regular orbit-cell structure with \(8,17,14,4\) cells in dimensions \(0,1,2,3\), respectively, and admits \(21\) explicit elementary collapses to one vertex. Thus the quotient is contractible, has trivial fundamental group, and has no reduced integral homology or torsion.

## Assumptions and scope
The vertex labels of \(S\) are the ordered pairs \((a,b)\in\{0,1,2,3\}^2\), listed lexicographically. Distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their componentwise difference modulo four lies in the six-element set above (the signs on \((1,1)\) are coupled). A nonempty face of \(K\) is a nonempty vertex subset containing no adjacent pair. Barycentric vertices are indexed by these faces. A \(q\)-simplex of the barycentric subdivision is a strict chain of \(q+1\) nonempty independent faces. The quotient is taken in the category of topological spaces, not as a Borel or homotopy quotient. All homological consequences use integral coefficients.

## Proof
**1. The full group.** For each \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrix \(A\) over \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\) that preserves the six-element adjacency-difference set, and every translation \(t\in(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\), the affine map \(x\mapsto Ax+t\) is an automorphism. Exhaustion of the \(4^4\) matrices finds exactly twelve eligible matrices, giving \(12\cdot16=192\) distinct automorphisms. Conversely, the six neighbors of the origin induce a cycle of length six. Every other nonzero vertex is uniquely identified by its two neighbors in this cycle, so the stabilizer of the origin injects into the twelve-element automorphism group of that cycle. The full graph automorphism group has order at most \(16\cdot12=192\), establishing equality. The constructive enumeration and all assertions in this paragraph are checked by `verify.py`.

**2. All orbit cells, not a naive vertex-orbit complex.** There are exactly \(16,72,96,16\) independent faces of cardinalities one through four and no larger independent faces. They form \(2,680\) nonempty strict flags under inclusion. Since any automorphism preserving a flag simplex setwise must preserve the distinct cardinalities of its component faces, it fixes each of the simplex's barycentric vertices. The action on the barycentric subdivision is therefore without inversions. Consequently its flag-orbits form a genuine finite regular quotient CW decomposition, with no internal folding of a cell. Every flag is canonically represented by the lexicographically least image under the 192 affine maps. The canonical orbit list has exactly \(8\) vertices, \(17\) edges, \(14\) triangles, and \(4\) tetrahedra. Although distinct cells may have the same vertex orbit labels, the flag orbit indices retain them as distinct cells.

**3. Explicit elementary collapses.** `certificate.json` contains the complete 43-cell ordered canonical flag list, and 21 ordered pairs of indices \((f,c)\). For each pair, \(f\) is a codimension-one face of \(c\), and among all surviving cells it has precisely one coface of dimension one higher, namely \(c\). The verifier independently reconstructs the quotient incidences, checks every free-face condition, and confirms after each deletion that the remaining cells are closed under faces. Each step is therefore an ordinary elementary collapse of a regular CW complex, not merely a homology cancellation. After the 21st pair precisely one zero-cell remains. This proves the stated collapse and all its homotopy consequences.

**4. Exact finite exhaustiveness.** All matrix maps, independent faces, strict chains, flag orbits, signed codimension-one boundaries, and collapse steps are generated from the sixteen input vertices without external databases. In addition to the local free-face checks, the verifier checks that \(\partial^2=0\) for every quotient two- and three-cell. The result is an exhaustive finite theorem about this graph; it makes no assertion about general strongly regular graphs or larger Doob graphs.

## Verification
Use standard Python 3, with `verify.py`, `certificate.json` in the same directory. Run `python3 verify.py`. The final line of logical verification is `SHRIKHANDE_FULL_AUTOMORPHISM_QUOTIENT_COLLAPSES_OK`, with the detailed counts provided in `verification_output.txt`. The certificate stores each canonical flag as a tuple of indices into the complete, numerically sorted list of nonempty independent face bitmasks, ordered from the largest face to the smallest face; this is the reverse of the conventional increasing-chain notation but represents the same barycentric simplex. No precomputed homology tables, private data, network requests, or third-party packages are needed. The verifier rebuilds both the cells and all free-face incidences before consulting the supplied pairs.

## Relationship to prior work
Barmak (2010) develops homotopy methods for independence complexes of graphs, without determining this specific full-automorphism quotient. Previous calculations for the same graph establish the nonequivariant homotopy equivalence \(K\simeq\bigvee^{23}S^2\), the translation-only orbit space \(|K|/(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\simeq S^2\vee S^2\), and the full 192-element rational homology representation. In particular, that representation gives \(\widetilde H^*(|K|/G;\mathbb Q)=0\), but rational acyclicity does not establish contractibility, integral acyclicity, or a simple collapse. The present conclusion identifies the integral and simple-homotopy type of the **full** ordinary quotient through its actual orbit-cell attaching structure. It neither repeats the nonequivariant wedge nor assumes that an arbitrary homotopy equivalence survives quotienting.

## Limitations
The proof is a finite exhaustive classification for this exact sixteen-vertex graph. The complete group is needed; the translation-only quotient has a different homotopy type. The explicit simple-homotopy statement concerns the regular barycentric quotient cell decomposition, which represents the original orbit space. The searches and source inspections described in `AUDIT.json` cannot exclude an unindexed equivalent theorem. No independent external mathematical audit is claimed.
