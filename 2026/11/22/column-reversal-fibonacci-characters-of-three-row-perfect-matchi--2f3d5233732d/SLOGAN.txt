For every \(n\ge1\), reversing the columns of the \(3\times2n\) grid acts on its perfect-matching complex's top integral homology with trace \((-1)^{k-1}F_{k-2}\) when \(n=2k\), and \((-1)^kF_k\) when \(n=2k+1\). An exact 14-state transfer certificate proves this signed Fibonacci law, refining the known nonequivariant Fibonacci sphere-wedge classification.
