For convex hulls of equal unit disks sharing one diameter and otherwise spanning mutually orthogonal planes, the exact random width in three dimensions has an elementary distribution with a \(3/2\)-power narrow-width edge. In every dimension, the sharp narrow and wide edge exponents are \(m-1/2\) and \((m-1)/2\), while the properly centered high-dimensional squared width converges to a non-Gumbel sum of independent Gumbel and half-chi-square variables.
