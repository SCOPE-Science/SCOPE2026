Two correlated uniform recursive trees retain an expected \(nr^2/((3-r)(5-2r))\) common labelled terminal-sibling pairs to leading order at fixed parent-copying rate \(r>0\), but only boundedly many of these have different parents across the trees. As copying vanishes, the exact overlap law exhibits a square-root mean crossover: \(\mathbb E H_n(r_n)=nr_n^2(1/15+o(1))+1/12+o(1)\), separating aligned persistence from accidental shared cherries.
