# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof classifies all rank-at-least-two partial isometries by their unique affine extensions; only rank-zero and rank-one extension multiplicities need subtracting. Idempotent centralizers reduce to connected components of an edge graph. Translation arrows form forests, while reflection arrows form disjoint transposed pairs, producing the respective factors \(3\) and \(10\). The asymptotic proof controls nonidempotent commuting pairs using independent central-domain bit comparisons and an explicit exponential bound; direct checks through seven points corroborate the formula but do not replace its proof.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** The 2010 primary preprint and 2014 full journal article on the same semigroups give element counts, fixedpoint data and Green-class counts, not the pair-incidence invariant. The separate order-preserving result with limit \(1/9\) is a strict-submonoid calculation: it does not imply the new reflection-orbit factor or full-monoid limit \(1/36\). Relevant published overlap searches uncovered no stronger theorem. An equivalent older formula under another name remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** Full-chain partial isometry monoids are naturally studied as inverse submonoids of the symmetric inverse monoid. The exact idempotent-pair census and asymptotically sharp idempotent dominance supply a meaningful commutativity invariant absent from standard rank enumerators. The contrast between translation forests and reflection 2-orbits explains the change rather than merely noting a numerical limit.

The proof does not count all two-nonidempotent commuting pairs exactly, and no optimal error rate is asserted. The preprint's known total cardinality is used, not claimed as new.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
