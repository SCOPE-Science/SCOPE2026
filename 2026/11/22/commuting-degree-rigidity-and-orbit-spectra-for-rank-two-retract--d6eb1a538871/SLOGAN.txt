For the tensor product of \(d\) dual-number algebras over an odd finite field, all algebra retractions onto two-dimensional images fall into exactly \(d\) automorphism orbits, and their orbit is determined by the degree of the retraction in its commuting graph. The exact orbit sizes, degree distribution, commuting-pair count, and maximal clique size follow from a unique coordinate-pivot normal form, revealing a sharp contrast with the previously known characteristic-two single-orbit behavior.
