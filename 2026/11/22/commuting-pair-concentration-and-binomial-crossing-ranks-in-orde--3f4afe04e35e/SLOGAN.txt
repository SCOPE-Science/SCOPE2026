For order-preserving, order-decreasing partial isometries of an \(n\)-chain, the probability that two uniform elements commute tends to \(1/4\), with stretched-exponential error, and almost every commuting pair consists of two partial identities. The exactly-one-idempotent crossing pairs with nonidempotent rank \(r\) number \(2^{n-r+1}\binom{n}{r+1}\); for \(n\ge2\), their nonidempotent ranks follow a conditioned \(\mathrm{Bin}(n,1/3)\) law shifted down by one.
