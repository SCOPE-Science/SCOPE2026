# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The statement is restricted to ordinary semigroup endomorphisms of the full transformation semigroup on at least five points. The exhaustive known three-type normal form and its composition law were reconstructed and independently verified at five points by actual endomorphism-value composition. Odd involutions are confined to nonroot points of each kernel fiber. The parity-weighted product, exponential generating function, and graph counts follow with explicit finite combinatorial arguments. `verify.py` provides finite corroboration only, not a proof in all dimensions.

## Originality

PASS with publication-priority limitations. Schein–Teclezghi (1998) and Gould–Grau–Johnson (arXiv:2307.11596v1) provide the underlying classification and composition table; the qualitative commuting-edge criterion is already a consequence of their work and is not claimed as independently novel. The new count is the labeled, parity-weighted rooted-fiber enumeration leading to an exact all-degree triangle and maximal-commuting-family census. The closest published minimal-generator theorem counts conjugacy orbits, not these labeled compatible families. The foundational 1998 paper was only partly inspected, and other historical enumerative identities may overlap.

## Mathematical value

PASS. A full commuting-retraction graph with exact page multiplicities answers a natural simultaneous-retraction question for a central finite semigroup, while the all-degree EGF encodes an otherwise nontrivial family count. The graph has exact diameter four, no four-cliques, and explicit maximal-family multiplicities. These go beyond correctness or an arbitrary numerical sample.

## Scientific limitations

The explicit graph theorem assumes at least five points, because four points admit exceptional rank-seven endomorphisms. The finite checks are not an infinite proof. Earlier results dominate the algebraic normal-form part, and the remaining novelty risk lies in equivalent older parity-weighted rooted-partition enumeration. Independent audit has not been performed.
