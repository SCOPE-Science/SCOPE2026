# Self-review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The five Leibniz dialgebra identities are explicitly stated, and the proof forces the second multiplication to have exactly two mutually annihilating idempotent operators. Necessity and sufficiency are checked on the four input types; conjugacy classes are exactly ordered three-summand dimension triples. For finite fields the orbit–stabilizer formula counts every labelled product, and a uniformly bounded discrete Gaussian sum proves both asymptotics and concentration. The included executable checker corroborates enumerations but is not used as a substitute for infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual priority risk.** Uzakbaev, arXiv:2611.00109v1, Theorem 7, gives the six three-dimensional complex models for the relevant underlying Leibniz algebra. That case is credited in full. The present result proves arbitrary-dimensional and arbitrary-field classification, finite-field fibre cardinalities and concentration. The published article's Definition 3, base classification and relevant theorem/proof were materially examined. Related work on ordinary Lie/Leibniz endomorphism idempotents and trialgebras has different objects or defining equations, and does not imply this result. Equivalent higher-dimensional formulations might exist in broader literature; the search does not prove novelty.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** This is the complete classification of a canonical split non-Lie family at every dimension, with a finite-field census and sharp probabilistic geometry of ranks. These are motivated invariants of a natural all-dimensional extension of a published dimension-three classification; no large paper-sized downstream theorem is required.
