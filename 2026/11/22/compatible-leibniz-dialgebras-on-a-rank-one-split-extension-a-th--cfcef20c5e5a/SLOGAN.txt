For the rank-one split right Leibniz algebra \(V\oplus Kt\) with \(v\dashv t=v\), all compatible second products are classified by ordered three-summand decompositions of \(V\), giving exactly \(\binom{d+2}{2}\) isomorphism types over every field. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the labelled structures have an exact general-linear-group rank census and a sharply balanced three-rank distribution with Gaussian tails.
