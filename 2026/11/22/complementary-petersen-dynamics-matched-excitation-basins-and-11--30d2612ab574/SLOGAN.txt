On the triangular graph \(T(5)\), the three-color cyclic automaton and Greenberg–Hastings excitation have identical eventual periods for each of all \(3^{10}=59{,}049\) initial colorings, and share all \(10{,}080\) primitive three-cycles, yet their maximal transient lengths are two and four updates. The common three-cycles split into exactly 118 geometric classes under the full \(S_5\) symmetry, in contrast to previously established period doubling on the complementary Petersen graph.
