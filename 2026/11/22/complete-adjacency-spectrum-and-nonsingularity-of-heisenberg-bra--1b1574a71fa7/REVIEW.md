The 2010 Brandt-extension paper provides the algebraic construction, not graph spectra; the inspected 2020 Brandt commuting-graph paper specializes to the trivial local group and computes structural graph invariants, not the nonabelian group-labeled spectrum. Published results for group-only commuting graphs do not supply the mixed diagonal/off-diagonal blocks. The previously known matrix-unit spectrum is specifically excluded from the novelty claim: the new theorem has a nonabelian local group and an all-index nonsingularity conclusion, absent in the aperiodic case. Database searches under Brandt, Heisenberg, group-labelled matrix units, eigenvalues and quotient matrices identified close related semigroup and group results but no covering spectral theorem. Risk: an unpublished or unindexed general graph substitution theorem might imply the factorization.
