# Review of complete adjacency spectrum of commuting rank-one square-zero matrices

## Correctness — PASS
The rank-one outer-product calculation proves commutativity if and only if simultaneous annihilation and identifies each scalar-multiple class with an incident point–hyperplane flag. The loop-retaining quotient operator \(B\) has entries given by mutual cross-incidence, and the graph adjacency matrix is \(B\otimes J_{q-1}-I\). The point-plus-hyperplane module is invariant and its two nontrivial levels follow from \(MM^{\mathsf T}=(t-s)I+sJ\). The six-case intermediate-flag count establishes the full entrywise quadratic identity, whose right-hand side takes values in the point-plus-hyperplane module. The remaining eigenvalues and both multiplicities follow exactly from this quadratic equation, trace and dimension, not from numerical approximation. An elementary sign bound proves the zero-free spectrum. The standalone checker verifies the identity entrywise and compares the complete spectra in six configurations (including a genuine extension field); ordinary matrix multiplication checks commutation in two configurations. The computations are finite corroboration, not the proof. The key scope constraints are all prime powers and dimension \(n\ge4\), including the binary field.

This is an exact complete spectrum of a canonical matrix-commuting graph whose scalar compression is the incident point–hyperplane flag space. The nontrivial global data include two irrational-in-general conjugate eigenvalue levels, exact residual multiplicities, and the uniform nonsingularity of its adjacency matrix, which are not determined by its degree, clique data, diameter or two local common-neighbor counts.
