For every integer polynomial \(P\), the Fibonacci-weighted exponential \(\mathcal E_P(x)=\exp(\sum_{n\ge1}P(F_n)x^n/n)\) is algebraic over \(\mathbb Q(x)\) exactly when \(P\) is even; every non-even weight gives a transcendental series. For \(P(T)=\sum_{j\ge0}c_jT^{2j}\), the exact algebraic degree is \(5^{h(P)}\), where \(h(P)=\max(\{0\}\cup\{j-\nu_5(c_j):j\ge1,\ c_j\ne0\})\); the integral mixed example \(P(T)=T^6-T^2\) has degree \(125\).
