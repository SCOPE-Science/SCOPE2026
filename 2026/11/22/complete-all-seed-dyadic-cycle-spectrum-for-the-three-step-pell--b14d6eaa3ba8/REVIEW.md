# Review of the three-step Pell all-seed cycle spectrum

## Correctness
**PASS.** The polynomial \(X^3+X+1\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb F_2\) and forces a single seven-cycle on nonzero mod-two states. Direct integer arithmetic yields \(M^7-I=2B\) with \(\det B=87\), and \(M^{14}-I=4C\) with \(\det C=7569\). Their odd determinants and the induction \(C_{j+1}=C_j+2^jC_j^2\) establish exact valuation increments at every depth. The state valuation census converts these periods into cycle multiplicities. Independent finite enumeration checks all states for moduli two through thirty-two; it is corroborative, not a proof of the infinite theorem.

## Originality
**PASS on the inspected literature, with residual priority risk.** The September 29, 2022 Şiar–Keskin–Öztaş work defines the same order-three Pell family but concerns Diophantine perfect powers. The full accessible text of Deveci–Avcı–Karaduman (2018), especially Theorems 2.3–2.4, concerns selected seed sets in nonabelian finite groups and period information, not the complete valuation-indexed cycle census of arbitrary ordered triples. Ordinary Pell and generalized Narayana cycle enumerations use different companion matrices and are not direct theorems about this state map. A generic matrix lifting theorem or previously unindexed equivalent spectrum may still cover the statement; a failed search is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The number and lengths of cycles for every seed, at each dyadic depth, are natural finer invariants than the standard-seed Pisano period. The exact uniform valuation stratification gives an infinite classification rather than a selected numerical example; the multiplicity law is not recoverable from knowledge of the longest period alone. This is a complete classification for the three-step case, not a claim about all generalized Pell orders.
