For the three-step Pell recurrence \(u_{n+3}=2u_{n+2}+u_{n+1}+u_n\), every ordered initial triple modulo \(2^k\) lies on a cycle, and the exact nonzero-state period is determined solely by the minimum coordinate valuation. There is one fixed point and exactly \(4^j\) cycles of length \(7\cdot2^j\) for every \(0\le j<k\), giving \((4^k+2)/3\) cycles in total.
