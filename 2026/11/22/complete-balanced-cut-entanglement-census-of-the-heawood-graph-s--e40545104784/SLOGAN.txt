Among the \(\binom{14}{7}=3432\) balanced labelled cuts of the Heawood fourteen-qubit graph state, exactly \(100,980,1680,672\) have respectively \(4,5,6,7\) entanglement bits. The point–line cut crosses all \(21\) graph edges yet carries only \(4\) bits, while every maximally entangled balanced cut crosses only \(11\) or \(13\) edges.
