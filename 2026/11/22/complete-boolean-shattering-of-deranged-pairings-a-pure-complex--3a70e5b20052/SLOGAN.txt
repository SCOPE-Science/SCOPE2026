The edge-membership concepts of all deranged pairings on \(K_{2n}\setminus F\) shatter exactly every matching of size at most \(n-2\) and precisely the extendable matchings of size \(n-1\). Their full face polynomial is \(\sum_{k=0}^{n-2}m_{n,k}z^k+np_nz^{n-1}\), their shattering complex is pure, and their VC and Littlestone dimensions both equal \(n-1\).
