For the genus-three octic Chebyshev curve \(y^2=\varphi_8(x)\), every prime \(p\ge5\) has an exact Cartier-iterate rank profile depending only on \(p\bmod16\), ranging from ordinary to superspecial reduction. Distinct nonmaximal characteristics exhibit \(p\)-rank zero without superspeciality and different \(a\)-numbers at the same positive \(p\)-rank.
