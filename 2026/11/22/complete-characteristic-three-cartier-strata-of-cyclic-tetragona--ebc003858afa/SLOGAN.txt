For every smooth characteristic-three cyclic tetragonal genus-six curve \(y^4=x^5+Ax^3+Bx^2+Cx+D\), its complete Cartier-power ranks, Jacobian \(3\)-rank and \(a\)-number depend only on the vanishing pattern of \(A,B,C\). Exactly six Cartier profiles occur: all Jacobians are nonordinary but their \(3\)-ranks attain every value from \(0\) through \(4\).
