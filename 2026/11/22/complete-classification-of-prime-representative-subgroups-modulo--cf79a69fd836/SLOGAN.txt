Every subgroup modulo \(2^a\) whose nonidentity standard representatives are prime has order at most four when \(a\ge4\); the order-two groups are determined by three explicit involution primality tests, and at most one cyclic order-four group survives. The unique possible order-four group exists exactly when \(2^{a-2}-1\), \(2^{a-1}+1\), and \(3\cdot2^{a-2}-1\) are all prime.
