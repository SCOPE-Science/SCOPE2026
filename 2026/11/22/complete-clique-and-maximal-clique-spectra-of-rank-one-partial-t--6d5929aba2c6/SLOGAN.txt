All cliques and all inclusion-maximal cliques of the rank-one partial-transformation commuting graph on \(n\) labeled points admit explicit size-generating polynomials. Its clique number is exactly \(\max\{n,2^{n-1}-1\}\), with exactly \(n\) maximizers for \(n\ge4\), each consisting of all nonempty partial constant maps with a fixed image point avoided by their domains.
