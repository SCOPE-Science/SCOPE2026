# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For nonzero contiguous offdiagonal runs, the kernel recurrence proves corank at most one. A two-state determinant recurrence counts all singular connected blocks, and the unique zero-edge decomposition proves the exact rational full-corank enumerator without a characteristic assumption. Analytic dominant-pole differentiation yields the explicit strictly positive Gaussian variance, with a characteristic-function proof of the central limit theorem. The independent finite-field matrix-rank checker passes 18 exhaustive configurations (9,887 matrices), including a genuine extension field. Its finite checks are corroboration, not the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The closest 2025 symmetric-tridiagonal paper proves exactly the specialization at \(t=0\) (nonsingular matrices) and all prescribed determinant values, not the rank strata among singular matrices. The 2025 binary graph-path paper fully determines the rank split for the connected-path zero-free-offdiagonal component, which is explicitly credited as known. The all-field, unrestricted-zero-edge, all-rank generating function and its Gaussian linear-corank phase were not found in those full texts or the other checked literature and mathematical records. However, these ingredients are elementary enough that an implicit or unindexed equivalent remains a real priority risk. Fulman–Goldstein (2012/2015) treats dense symmetric random matrices and a tight nullity limit, which does not imply this sparse banded law.

**Value — PASS.** The central invariant is the *complete* corank census of a natural structured matrix family, not an arbitrary cutoff or special parameter. It quantitatively separates the finite-field sparse path-supported matrix model from dense symmetric ensembles: the kernel has positive limiting density and Gaussian square-root fluctuations. The formula provides every rank multiplicity for every characteristic and dimension. Already known nonsingularity and conditional connected-path facts are not counted as the contribution.

**Limitations and risks.** The result does not cover arbitrary graph supports, chain rings, nonuniform entries or a joint field/dimension limit. Historical originality remains qualified by possibly unindexed all-rank formulas or derivations hidden as corollaries in the earlier determinant and graph literature; no independent expert audit is claimed. Enumeration checks cover finitely many matrices, with the general proof supplied separately.
