For the eight nonshellable ordinary three-cut complexes of fifth-power cycles with \(13\le n\le20\), the entire rational dihedral homology character is determined by exact signed fixed-face enumeration, going beyond previously published Betti tables. Their genuine full-dihedral quotients retain positive-degree rational homology exactly at \(n=16,17\), with rank four in each case; all six other quotients are rationally acyclic.
