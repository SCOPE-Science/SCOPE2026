For every positive exponent \(k\), the consecutive-power polynomial \(x^k+(x+1)^k\) takes exactly one full dyadic residue coset, \(1+2^{h(k)}\mathbb Z_2\), where \(h(k)=1\) for odd \(k\), \(h(2)=2\), and \(h(k)=\nu_2(k)+2\) for even \(k\ge4\). Every attained residue modulo \(2^n\) occurs exactly \(2^{\min(n,h(k))}\) times, so its output distribution is exactly uniform on its image.
