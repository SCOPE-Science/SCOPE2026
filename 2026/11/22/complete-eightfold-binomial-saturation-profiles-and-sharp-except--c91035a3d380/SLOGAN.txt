For every positive \(m\), the number of \(j=1,\ldots,7\) with \(8\mid\binom{8m}{jm}\) is exactly \(7-2\mathbf1_{s_2(3o)\le2}-\mathbf1_{s_2(o)\le2}\), where \(o\) is the odd part of \(m\); the exceptions form two explicit Jacobsthal and Fermat-type families plus two isolated odd cores. Among primes \(p>8\), this also gives the complete exceptional classification of \(A_8(8p)\), sharpening a generic power-saving exceptional bound to \(O(\log X)\), while exceptional multipliers through \(X\) number \(\tfrac34(\log_2X)^2+O(\log X)\).
