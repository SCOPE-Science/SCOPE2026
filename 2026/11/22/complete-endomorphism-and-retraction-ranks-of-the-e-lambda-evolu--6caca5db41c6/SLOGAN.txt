Every endomorphism of Hu–Wen's three-dimensional evolution algebra \(E_\lambda\) belongs to an explicit pair of scalar normal forms valid over any field. The exact finite-field rank polynomial has rank-two terms only at \(\lambda=4\), and its singular and retraction strata distinguish the idempotent-free parameter \(\lambda=2\), the symmetry-jump parameter \(\lambda=4\), and characteristic two.
