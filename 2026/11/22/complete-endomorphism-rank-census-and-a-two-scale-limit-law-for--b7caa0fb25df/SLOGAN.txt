For the standard \(n\)-dimensional filiform Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), every endomorphism rank has an explicit count and the total number is \(q^{2n-1}+(q-1)q^{n+2}\). For fixed \(q\), a uniform endomorphism has rank two with limiting probability \(1/q\), while the remaining mass follows a geometric law at bounded corank.
