For the normalized affine composition semigroup \(e^{-s/2}C_{1-e^{-s}+e^{-s}z}\) on \(H^2(\mathbb D)\), every finite complex-measure average has operator norm, essential norm and numerical radius equal to the supremum of its Fourier transform. The identity holds at every matrix level, making the measure convolution calculus completely isometric even modulo compact operators; for instance \(\|I+\sqrt a\,C_{1-a+az}-a C_{1-a^2+a^2z}\|=\sqrt5\) for all \(0<a<1\).
