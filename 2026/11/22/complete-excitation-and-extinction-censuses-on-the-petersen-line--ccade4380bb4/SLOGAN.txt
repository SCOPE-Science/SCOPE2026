On the fifteen-vertex Petersen line graph, raising the three-state Greenberg–Hastings contact threshold from one to two eliminates exactly 220,960 primitive oscillatory attractors: every one of the 14,348,907 labelled initial states then reaches rest within a sharp six updates. Two exhaustive finite-state classifiers also determine all oscillatory periods, attraction basins, and sharp transient distributions for thresholds one through four.
