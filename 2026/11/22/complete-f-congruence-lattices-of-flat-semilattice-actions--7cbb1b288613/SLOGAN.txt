For every finite permutation group acting on a flat semilattice, all \(F\)-congruences are described exactly by orbit-collapsing semilattice partitions and normal subgroups fixing the resulting quotient. For faithful transitive actions the entire lattice is an extra least element beneath \(\mathcal N(G)\times C_2\), so its size is \(1+2|\mathcal N(G)|\), and every nonidentity proper quotient loses the original nontrivial action.
