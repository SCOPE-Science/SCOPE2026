In the independent-edge nearest-neighbor forest on a complete graph, the proportion of components containing exactly \(k\ge2\) vertices converges to \(\frac{k-1}{2}\int_0^1u e^{-2u}(1-e^{-u})^{k-2}\,du\). These densities account for every vertex, with no macroscopic component: the component count is asymptotic to one quarter of the vertex count and the typical component size has an explicit nondegenerate distribution.
