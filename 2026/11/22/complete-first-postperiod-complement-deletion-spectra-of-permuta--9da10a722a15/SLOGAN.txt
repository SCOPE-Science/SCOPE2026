For every permutation power \(P^n\) on at least three letters, deleting any prescribed scattered word of length \(|P|+1\) yields a complete exact count and residual multiplicity spectrum. Only \(q(2q-1)\) of the \(q^{q+1}\) possible deleted patterns can ever cause a collision, and the three nonzero ambiguity types have precisely determined quadratic binomial corrections.
