For every odd prime power, the permutation binomials \(X(X^{2(q-1)}+a)\) over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) have exactly one or \(2q-1\) fixed points. Precisely two nonzero coefficients give the larger count when \(q\equiv7,43\pmod{60}\), and none do otherwise; the zero coefficient contributes one additional case exactly when \(3\nmid(q+1)\).
