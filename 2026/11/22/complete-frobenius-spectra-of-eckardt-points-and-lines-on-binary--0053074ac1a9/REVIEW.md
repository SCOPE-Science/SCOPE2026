# Scientific review: Complete Frobenius spectra of Eckardt points and lines on binary non-Frobenius-split cubic surfaces

## Correctness — PASS
The Frobenius-form gradient gives smoothness for invertible matrices; the tangent-section calculation gives the normalized additive star equation; the two mixed-cubic equations characterize contained lines. The exact packaged checker enumerates all 20,160 invertible binary matrices in ten congruence orbits, then constructs all 45 Eckardt points and 27 contained lines per representative, checks every Frobenius cycle, and confirms all orbit counts. An independent brute-force field-point enumeration in degrees 1–5 checks the Eckardt fixed-point counts. The proof and computations cover the finite universe asserted, without extrapolating from the small-field checks alone.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The first source already gives the full-rank Frobenius-form setting and the second the geometry of Hermitian stars; neither is presented as a new discovery. Karaoğlu (2021) already classifies relevant surfaces and records line and Eckardt data over extension fields through degree six, and Betten–Karaoğlu (2022) gives Eckardt-configuration parametrizations. The additional exact result is the joint pair of *complete* 45-point and 27-line Frobenius cycle partitions in every binary form class, including degree-eight, degree-ten and degree-twelve cycles, complete finite-scheme zeta functions and the same-star-zeta/different-line-zeta pair. Searches of published mathematical records did not reveal a dominating identical full table. The 2022 publication's full text and online supplementary tables were not fully inspectable; publication priority therefore remains qualified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Eckardt points and lines are intrinsic arithmetic descent objects of smooth cubic surfaces. Complete zeta data resolves every extension degree rather than only a few small extension counts, and explicitly shows that the complete arithmetic Eckardt-point spectrum does not determine the line spectrum. Exact high-degree closed points and full coverage of all ten classes make this a natural, finite classification rather than an arbitrary sample.

## Known limitations
The classification is for reduced geometric support over the specified base field and its finite extensions; no claim is made for arbitrary cubic surfaces, nonreduced schemes, automorphism-group classification, or independent certification. Historical equivalence in unindexed tables remains possible.
