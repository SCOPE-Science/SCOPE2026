# Scientific review: Complete Frobenius spectra of Göpel kernels for Cayley-octad quartics

## Correctness

PASS. The classical level-two Cayley-octad model is explicitly stated. A perfect matching gives a totally isotropic 3-space, 105 distinct cases; an affine F2^3 labelling gives a disjoint totally isotropic 3-space, 30 distinct cases; 105+30 exhaust the known product count 135. For each of the exactly 22 partitions of 8, the complete induced permutation on these 135 subspaces has been constructed, and a separate independent enumeration of all orthogonal triples confirms exact equality of the two sets. All row orbit lengths sum to 135. Rationality over a finite field follows from finite étale Galois descent.

## Originality

PASS, provisional. No retrieved source directly provides the complete 22-type Frobenius orbit classification of all 135 Göpel kernels for octad-marked plane quartics. The 2012 full-text antecedent already gives the geometric 30+105 permutation representation and logically permits one to compute the table; this underlying structure is explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. The new check is exact arithmetic orbit profiles, split fixed-kernel counts and zeta functions. Relevant directly arithmetical literature on Galois-stable octads addresses 28 bitangents; a hyperelliptic genus-three pencil addresses a different Galois module. Residual risk: older character tables or full inaccessible sources could implicitly cover these profiles.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The 22-case complete finite-field descent classification answers the natural arithmetic question of which maximal Weil-isotropic genus-three isogeny kernels descend for a given octad Frobenius partition. Its full orbit data determines extension degrees and a finite étale zeta function, and distinguishes rational kernel schemes from rational bitangents in both directions (eight-cycle and three-cycle plus five fixed points). It is a natural full arithmetic classification rather than an arbitrary finite parameter slice. While the known 30+105 representation makes the computation possible, it does not state the tabulated arithmetic outcomes.

## Closest literature and limitations

The 2012 paper by Ren–Sam–Schrader–Sturmfels (arXiv:1208.1229, Section 6) explicitly contains the known 135 and 30+105 geometry; no novelty is claimed for these. Bergvall (arXiv:2106.13072, Section 3.5) treats the Göpel-moduli setup. Elsenhans–Jahnel (arXiv:1710.07279) studies Galois-stable octads and bitangents, though its full text was not read. A general character computation might recover this table; this is a priority risk. The numerical arithmetic classification is conditional on a Frobenius-stable regular octad, not all quartics or all realizable factorization types. Independent scientific validation is outstanding.
