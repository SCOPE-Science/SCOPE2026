For every odd \(a\ge5\), the two-generator inclusion-exclusion polynomial has only even gaps longer than one whenever \(b\equiv\pm2\pmod a\). Its entire gap histogram is explicit: in the \(+2\) family every even length from \(2\) to \(a-1\) occurs \(2\lfloor b/a\rfloor\) times, while the \(-2\) family has two extra occurrences at every nonmaximal even length.
