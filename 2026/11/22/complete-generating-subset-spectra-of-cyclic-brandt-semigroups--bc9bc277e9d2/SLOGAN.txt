For cyclic Brandt semigroups, the complete cardinality distribution of generating subsets is a divisor-Möbius transform of the strongly connected digraph polynomial after a uniform arc-weight substitution. In particular, a Brandt semigroup with \(n\ge2\) indices and cyclic group order \(m\) has exactly \((n-1)!m^{n-1}\varphi(m)\) generating subsets of minimum size \(n\).
