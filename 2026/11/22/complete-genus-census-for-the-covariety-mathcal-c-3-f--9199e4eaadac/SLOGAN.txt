All numerical semigroups of Frobenius number \(F\ge4\) having \(3\) as a pseudo-Frobenius gap admit a unique three-residue-tail normal form, giving their exact genus-generating polynomial. Their count is a quadratic quasipolynomial in \(F\), with leading coefficient \(1/27\) when \(3\mid F\) and \(1/18\) otherwise.
