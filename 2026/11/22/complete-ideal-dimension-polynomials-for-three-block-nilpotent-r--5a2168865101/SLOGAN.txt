Every finite-field strictly upper three-block matrix radical with middle-block size at least two has an exact all-dimensional two-sided-ideal polynomial, obtained by double subspace-lattice Möbius inversion and Gaussian central lifts. For block sizes \((2,2,1)\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), the polynomial counts precisely \(2859\) ideals, including mixed non-coordinate ideals inaccessible to the unital parabolic-ring ideal classification.
