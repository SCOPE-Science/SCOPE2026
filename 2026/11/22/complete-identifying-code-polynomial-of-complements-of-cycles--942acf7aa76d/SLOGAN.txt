Every identifying code of the cycle complement \(\overline{C_n}\), for \(n\ge5\), is a cyclic code with no isolated chosen vertex and at most one unchosen auxiliary edge. Its complete labelled polynomial is \(A_n+B_n\) for odd \(n\) and \(A_{n/2}^2+2A_{n/2}B_{n/2}\) for even \(n\), where \(A\) is the previously known ordinary-cycle open-locating polynomial and the explicit binomial \(B\) term is an asymptotically dominant one-defect correction.
