# Complete integral critical group and spectrum of the Wiman 45-line stable boundary

## Finding

Let \(\mathcal W\) be the classical complex Wiman arrangement of 45 projective reflection lines. Blow up **all** of its 201 singular points (120 ordinary triple points, 45 ordinary quadruple points, and 36 ordinary quintuple points) in \(\mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}\). Let \(D\) be the reduced union of the 45 strict line transforms and all 201 exceptional curves. Its dual graph \(\Gamma\) has 246 vertices, 720 edges, and first Betti number 475. Every component is rational and meets the rest in at least three nodes, so \(D\) is a connected stable nodal curve of arithmetic genus 475.

Writing \(C_m=\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\), its **complete integral graph critical group**, in invariant-factor order, is
\[
K(\Gamma)\cong C_3^{42}\oplus C_9^{17}\oplus C_{540}\oplus C_{2160}^{6}\oplus C_{174960}\oplus C_{1093674960}\oplus C_{16405124400}^{8}.
\]
In particular, its exponent is \(16405124400\), and its order (the spanning-tree number) is
\[
|K(\Gamma)|=\tau(\Gamma)=2^{66}3^{175}5^{25}7^9 19^9 47^9.
\]
The full Laplacian characteristic polynomial is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\det(tI-L_\Gamma)={}&t(t-3)^{75}(t-5)^9\,f_0(t)\,f_1(t)^9\,f_2(t)^9\,g_1(t)^{10}\,g_2(t)^{16},\\
f_0(t)={}&t^3-28t^2+179t-328,\\
f_1(t)={}&t^3-23t^2+109t-141,\\
f_2(t)={}&t^3-23t^2+109t-133,\\
g_1(t)={}&t^4-28t^3+224t^2-694t+729,\\
g_2(t)={}&t^4-28t^3+224t^2-691t+720.
\end{aligned}
\]
For any proper regular split semistable one-parameter smoothing with special fibre isomorphic to \(D\) and unit thickness at every node, this finite group is also the geometric component group of the generic Jacobian's Néron model. This is conditional on such a model, not an assertion about an arbitrary smoothing.

## Assumptions and scope

The ground field of the arrangement and divisor is \(\mathbb C\). The 45 lines and their 201 points are the classical Wiman configuration, as in Bauer et al. [1,2]. The graph has one vertex for each of the 45 strict transforms and one for each exceptional curve. An edge is drawn for every transverse intersection of those components. There are no residual line-to-line intersections after the 201 blowups, and no exceptional-to-exceptional intersections.

All numerical critical-group statements refer to the ordinary unweighted Laplacian of this exact 246-vertex graph. No 81-vertex subconfiguration retaining only quintuple points, line-intersection graph without exceptional components, nonunit edge-length model, or Picard group of the ambient blown-up surface is being identified with this group.

## Proof

**1. Incidence reconstruction and good reduction.** Bauer et al. [2, §2.3] give four explicit projective generators of the Valentiner action over the field generated by \(\delta\) and \(\omega\), where \(\delta^2=5\), \(\omega^2+\omega+1=0\), and the orbit of the line \(x=0\) consists of the 45 Wiman lines. Specialize to a prime above 19 with \(\delta=9\) and \(\omega=7\) in \(\mathbb F_{19}\). Both satisfy the defining equations and the generator matrices remain invertible. Apply the dual inverse-transpose action to line coefficient vectors and normalize each projective vector to first nonzero coordinate one. The finite orbit has exactly 45 distinct lines.

For every pair of these lines, form their projective cross product. The 990 line pairs partition into exactly 201 distinct points with multiplicity profile \(120\) of size three, \(45\) of size four, and \(36\) of size five, and \(\sum_p\binom{m_p}{2}=990\). Each line contains exactly 16 distinct marked points. These exact computations are implemented by the supplied verifier. The complex configuration has exactly 201 singular points with the identical multiplicity profile by [1,2]. Since distinct reduced lines remain distinct and each generic pair-intersection has a specialization, reduction cannot increase the number of distinct intersections. Equality at 201 excludes collisions and new concurrences, so this finite-field incidence table is combinatorially identical to the complex Wiman table. The finite-field computations therefore certify the claimed complex dual graph, rather than an unrelated characteristic-19 graph.

An ordinary \(m\)-fold point gives one exceptional rational component meeting its \(m\) strict transforms transversely at distinct points. The total number of nodes is \(120\cdot3+45\cdot4+36\cdot5=720\). There are \(45+201=246\) rational components. Their dual graph is connected because any two original lines meet, and every exceptional vertex is incident with at least three line vertices. Consequently its genus is \(720-246+1=475\). Every original-line vertex has degree 16; all other degrees are three, four, or five, proving stability.

**2. Full characteristic polynomial.** For \(m\in\{3,4,5\}\), let \(H_m\) be the \(45\)-by-\(n_m\) zero-one line-to-point incidence matrix, with \((n_3,n_4,n_5)=(120,45,36)\), and put \(Q_m=H_mH_m^{\mathsf T}\). Grouping vertices by type gives
\[
L_\Gamma=\begin{pmatrix}16I_{45}&-H_3&-H_4&-H_5\\-H_3^{\mathsf T}&3I_{120}&0&0\\-H_4^{\mathsf T}&0&4I_{45}&0\\-H_5^{\mathsf T}&0&0&5I_{36}\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Set \(B=960I_{45}-20Q_3-15Q_4-12Q_5\). Exact 45-by-45 integer matrix multiplication establishes that the \(Q_m\) commute and that \(B\) has the following joint eigenblocks:

| Eigenvalue of \(B\) | Multiplicity | \(Q_3\) | \(Q_4\) | \(Q_5\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 24 | 16 | 20 |
| 665 | 9 | 14 | 1 | 0 |
| 705 | 9 | 6 | 9 | 0 |
| 720 | 16 | 6 | 4 | 5 |
| 729 | 10 | 6 | 1 | 8 |

This table has a compact exact certificate: \(\prod_{\lambda\in\{0,665,705,720,729\}}(B-\lambda I)=0\); the traces \(\operatorname{tr}(B^k)\) for \(0\le k\le4\) give the stated multiplicities by the invertible Vandermonde system; and for each table row and each \(m\), the product \((Q_m-q_m I)\prod_{\mu\ne\lambda}(B-\mu I)\) is the zero integer matrix. These identities are checked from the reconstructed lines by the supplied exact verifier, not extrapolated from a floating-point eigenvalue calculation.

The Schur-complement identity, initially for \(t\notin\{3,4,5\}\) and then polynomially continued, is
\[
\det(tI-L_\Gamma)=(t-3)^{120}(t-4)^{45}(t-5)^{36}\prod_{(d,q_3,q_4,q_5)}\left(t-16-\frac{q_3}{t-3}-\frac{q_4}{t-4}-\frac{q_5}{t-5}\right)^d.
\]
Substitution of the five rows gives precisely the factorization stated above. The degrees sum to 246 and the zero eigenvalue is simple.

**3. Order and complete Smith form.** By the matrix-tree theorem the product of all nonzero Laplacian eigenvalues, divided by 246, is
\[
\tau(\Gamma)=\frac{5^9 3^{75}\cdot328\cdot141^9\cdot133^9\cdot729^{10}\cdot720^{16}}{246}
=2^{66}3^{175}5^{25}7^9 19^9 47^9.
\]
Independently, deleting one of the 45 line vertices and eliminating all 201 point blocks yields
\[
\det L_\Gamma^{\mathrm{root}}=\frac{3^{120}4^{45}5^{36}}{60^{44}}\det B^{\mathrm{root}},
\]
where \(B^{\mathrm{root}}\) is the 44-by-44 principal minor obtained by deleting the corresponding line. A fraction-free exact determinant computation gives the same order.

To determine the **group**, not just its order, exact prime-local row and column reductions of a 245-by-245 rooted Laplacian are carried out over \(\mathbb Z/p^h\mathbb Z\). Each pivot is chosen with minimum \(p\)-adic valuation and normalized by a unit; clearing its row and column uses invertible elementary operations. Precision is taken strictly larger than every resulting nonzero valuation. The complete lists of positive Smith valuations (valuation followed by multiplicity) are

| Prime | Positive Smith valuations | Total valuation |
|---:|---|---:|
| 2 | \(2\) (1), \(4\) (16) | 66 |
| 3 | \(1\) (42), \(2\) (17), \(3\) (7), \(7\) (2), \(8\) (8) | 175 |
| 5 | \(1\) (9), \(2\) (8) | 25 |
| 7 | \(1\) (9) | 9 |
| 19 | \(1\) (9) | 9 |
| 47 | \(1\) (9) | 9 |

The zero-valuation counts are respectively 228, 169, 228, 236, 236, and 236. The local reductions were performed with four distinct deleted vertices (including exceptional components) and give identical valuations. The sum of valuations for each prime equals the corresponding exponent in the independently verified determinant, and no other prime divides that determinant. Aligning these elementary divisors yields exactly the invariant factors in the Finding. Their successive divisibility and product are independently checked.

**4. Geometric interpretation.** For any proper regular split semistable smoothing with this special fiber and unit node thickness, the component intersection matrix is \(-L_\Gamma\). Raynaud's component-group description, as in Bosch–Lütkebohmert–Raynaud [4, §9.6], identifies the geometric Jacobian Néron component group with its integral Laplacian cokernel modulo the degree-zero relation, namely \(K(\Gamma)\). This is a conditional interpretation of the proven graph invariant.

## Relationship to prior work

Bauer et al. [1, §4.2] give explicit equations of the classical 45 lines and the 201-point multiplicity census, and study bounded negativity. Bauer et al. [2, §2.3] supply the Valentiner group generators and study negative curves, line bundles and asymptotic invariants of the corresponding blowup surface. Innamorati [3, §5] independently describes a characteristic-19 projective point table dual to the Wiman arrangement and its quasi-configuration. None of those inspected statements provides the critical group of the **full 246-vertex resolved boundary**, its local Smith structure, or its full 246-degree Laplacian characteristic polynomial. The general correspondence between critical groups and semistable Jacobian component groups is classical [4] and is not asserted as new.

## Limitations

This is an exact result for the one geometrically specified complex stable boundary, not a theorem classifying arbitrary reflection arrangements, arbitrary Wiman subarrangements, or arithmetic twists. In particular the 81-vertex graph that retains only the 36 quintuple points is a different object and is not covered by this statement. The result does not describe nonregular models, nonunit node thicknesses, the Picard group of the blowup surface, or an independently established existence theorem for any particular global smoothing. The finite certificate establishes the graph and its invariants; it does not replace an independent mathematical audit. Historical priority remains subject to identification of any unindexed earlier integral calculation.
