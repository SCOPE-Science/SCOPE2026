The generalized Jacobian of every reduced Fermat–Ceva divisor \((X^n-Y^n)(Y^n-Z^n)(Z^n-X^n)=0\), with \(n\ge3\) invertible in the field, has torus quotient exactly two Weil restrictions from the nonidentity \(n\)-th-root pairs. A Galois-invariant spanning tree makes the character lattice an integral permutation lattice, giving all finite-field Frobenius classes—including higher-order and even-degree cases—and the complete finite rational-point torus group.
