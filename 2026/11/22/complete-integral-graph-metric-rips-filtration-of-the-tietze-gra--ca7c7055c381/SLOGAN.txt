The Tietze graph has a complete integral graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration with successive homotopy types \(\bigvee^{11}S^0\), \(\bigvee^{6}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{4}S^2\), and a point at the four integer thresholds. A fully checked sequence of simplicial and CW-cell collapses proves the radius-two four-sphere wedge, rather than merely its field Betti numbers.
