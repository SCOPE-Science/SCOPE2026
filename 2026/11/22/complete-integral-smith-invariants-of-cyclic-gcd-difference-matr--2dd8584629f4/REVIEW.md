# Scientific review of cyclic GCD-difference Smith invariants

## Correctness — PASS
The prime matrix is exactly \((p-1)I_p+J_p\). The proof calculates *all* determinantal divisors, not only eigenvalues: the rank-one expansion makes every \(r\)-minor divisible by \((p-1)^{r-1}\), and explicitly offset row/column sets attain this valuation for every \(r<p\). The determinant supplies the final local Smith factor. Squarefree CRT is an entrywise multiplicative identity, so tensoring integer-unimodular Smith transformations is valid. Sorting prime-adic exponents then gives the unique integral invariant factors. The modular-rank product and generator dichotomy follow independently from ranks of the local rank-one perturbations. Direct exhaustive minors at \(N=2,3,5,6\), exact direct determinants and modular ranks through \(N=30\), and five independent larger integer Smith computations all agree with the formula. No finite test is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical-priority risk
The closest inspected 2011 and 2012 primary papers on integral circulant/GCD graphs use indicator adjacency kernels for selected gcd classes and study eigenvalue energy, rather than the full weighted numerical GCD kernel's integral cokernel. A relevant indexed complete Smith form concerns **Ramanujan-sum convolution matrices**, with kernel \(c_N(a-b)\), not the matrix \(\gcd(a-b,N)\). Its theorem is not a direct generalization of the present claim; Smith forms do not commute with linear combinations of kernels. The literature comparison searched the exact matrix, the circulant and tensor formulations, integral Smith normal form, and the cyclic GCD-graph aliases. No decisive previous statement of this entire squarefree Smith prescription was identified. Nevertheless, the local rank-one and CRT ingredients are classical, and an older generalized arithmetic-kernel theorem could subsume their combination. The indexed 2026 result was compared at the level of its available summary, not certified against unavailable full text.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The invariant is an exact, natural arithmetic-matrix cokernel across **every squarefree modulus**, not a sample or arbitrarily selected finite slice. The theorem reduces a potentially large integer Smith computation to local prime multisets, determines all torsion invariant factors, the determinant and ranks at every rational prime, and isolates a genuinely arithmetic generator-number dichotomy for even squarefree orders controlled by odd prime divisors modulo three. Spectral graph-energy formulas alone do not determine this integral information. The contribution is a complete, self-contained local-to-global classification, not a claim of a new general Smith algorithm or of a major downstream theorem.

## Scope and risks
The proof is restricted to squarefree \(N\ge2\); repeated prime factors require different local matrices. Classical SNF and CRT are indispensable and acknowledged. Broader arithmetic-circulant literature not available in full might contain an equivalent formula. The checker is finite corroboration, not a complete computer certification. No independent audit has been carried out.
