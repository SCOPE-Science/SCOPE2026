For every squarefree modulus, the full integer Smith normal form of the cyclic matrix with entries \(\gcd(a-b,N)\) is determined by tensoring the explicit three-level local prime multisets and sorting prime valuations. Its cokernel requires either \(N-1\) or \(N-2\) generators: for even squarefree \(N>2\), the larger number occurs exactly when every odd prime divisor is congruent to one modulo three.
