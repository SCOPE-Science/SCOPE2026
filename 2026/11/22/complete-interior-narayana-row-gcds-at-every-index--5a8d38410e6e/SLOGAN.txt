The gcd of all nonendpoint Narayana counts in row \(n\ge3\) is completely determined by whether \(n\) and \(n-1\) are prime powers: a prime-power row contributes its prime once, and a prime-power predecessor contributes its prime at exponent at most two, with exactly two small binary exceptions. This extends the known Fermat-prime exception in odd-prime rows to a classification for every composite row index.
