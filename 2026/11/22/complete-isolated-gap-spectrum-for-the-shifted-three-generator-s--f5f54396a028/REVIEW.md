# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
A sum of \(t\) generators has exact offset set \(\{0,3,4\}\cup[6,4t]\) for every \(t\ge2\), because the intervals generated by exactly \(j\) nonzero offsets are \([3j,4j]\). Every gap with two represented neighbors must therefore be a missing offset five or a boundary contact with the previous band, giving the explicit three exhaustive cases. Exact integer endpoint equations prove the modulus-four exceptions. Independently, direct dynamic-programming membership checks and Dijkstra Apéry bounds established 205 complete comparisons, covering 411,191 gap states and 5,766 isolated gaps; the checker also tested 99 fixed-coin offset sets. No extrapolation from a finite census is used for the all-parameter conclusion.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The isolated-gap set is a canonical invariant tied to perfect numerical semigroups and their closures. The offset pair three/four is the first consecutive coprime offset pair starting above two for which the fixed-coin offset spectrum has a persistent interior isolated hole at five. Its interaction with neighboring bands produces an explicit three-generator family where the number of isolated gaps grows linearly and where two congruence classes have an extra, structurally different isolated gap. This is a complete nontrivial infinite-family classification with an exact modular boundary transition, not merely a restated finite computation or the classical Frobenius number.
