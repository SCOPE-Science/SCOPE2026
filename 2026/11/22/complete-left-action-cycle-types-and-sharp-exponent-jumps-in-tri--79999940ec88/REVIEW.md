# Scientific assessment of the individual left-action cycle law

**Correctness.** Left multiplication splits by columns, with a northwest corner as each block. Counting the kernels of powers of those blocks computes the fixed-point counts of the prime-power iterates, and taking differences computes the cycle populations. The largest corner gives the quotient by the final-column kernel. Regular corners attain the sharp bounds, and already the minimum for the first iterate forces every relevant first-superdiagonal coordinate to be nonzero. The case of a one-dimensional faithful corner has no such coordinates and is included.

**Originality.** The individual permutation cycles and sharp fixed-point locus are distinct from the ambient Jordan-type counts of Yip and of Fuchs–Kirillov, and from full-action-group orbit statistics. The first maximal-order jump is not a separate contribution: it lifts the regular unipotent count from the faithful quotient, whose exact density is stated in Resultary 1ec57ea01e67 for positive jump index. Primary and published-record comparisons are bounded.

**Value.** This computes a natural exact invariant for a canonical family of radical-brace actions over every finite field. It resolves fixed-point and cycle data not determined by the ordinary Jordan type of the largest corner, and gives an exact extremal fixed-point stratum. It does not claim the population of each cycle type over the whole brace.
