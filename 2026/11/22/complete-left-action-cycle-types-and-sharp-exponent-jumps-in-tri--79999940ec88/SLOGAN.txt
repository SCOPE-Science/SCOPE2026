For the strictly upper-triangular radical-ring brace over any finite field, the canonical left permutation associated to every element has an exact cycle-type formula in terms of the ranks of its principal-corner powers. Its smallest fixed-point set has size \(q^{n-1}\), and at dimension \(n=p^s+2\) precisely the proportion \((1-q^{-1})^{p^s}\) of elements attain maximal left-action order \(p^{s+1}\).
