Deleting at most three edges from \(K_8\) always leaves an ordinary matching complex homotopy equivalent to a wedge of two-spheres, with multiplicity determined exactly by one of nine fault-graph types. The new low-fault profile separates incident and disjoint pairs (100 versus 102 spheres) and distinguishes all five three-edge shapes (84, 84, 86, 88, 90 spheres).
