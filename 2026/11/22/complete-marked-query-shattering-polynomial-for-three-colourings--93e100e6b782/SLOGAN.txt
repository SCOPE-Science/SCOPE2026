For proper three-colourings of every double-star tree, the full vertex--colour-query shattering polynomial has an explicit two-parameter formula, refining the previously known vertex-projected classification. Its highest coefficient is \(6(2^a+2^b+2+\mathbf1_{a=1}+\mathbf1_{b=1})\); together with the vertex count, that single coefficient determines the two hub leaf counts up to interchange.
