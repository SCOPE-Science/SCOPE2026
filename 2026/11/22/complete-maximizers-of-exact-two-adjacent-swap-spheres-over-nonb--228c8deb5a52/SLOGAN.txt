For any alphabet of at least three letters, the maximum number of distinct words obtained after exactly two successive adjacent swaps is \(\binom n2\); the maximizing words are exactly those with no adjacent repeats and no `abab` factor, together with those with one adjacent repeat and no `aba` factor. All maximizers are counted by an explicit generalized Fibonacci recurrence and a convolution, yielding \(6F_n+12(n-2)\) ternary optimizers for every \(n\ge3\).
