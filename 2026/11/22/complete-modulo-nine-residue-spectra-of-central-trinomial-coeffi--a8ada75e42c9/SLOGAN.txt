For the central trinomial coefficients \(T_n=[x^0](x^{-1}+1+x)^n\), a base-three descent statistic and the position of a unique digit \(2\) classify **every** residue modulo nine, and closed formulas count each residue below \(3^L\). Beyond the previously known zero-residue census, the three unit classes have sharp ternary-block imbalance \(\Theta(3^{L/2})\) around their common mean \(2^L/3\).
