For geometrically regular unilateral weighted shifts, moment infinite divisibility occurs exactly on the diagonal or in the sharp wedge \(-1<N<0\), \(N<D\le -N\); in particular, the adjoining subnormal sector contains no further moment-infinitely-divisible shifts. Every positive Schur power in the wedge has an explicit atomic Berger measure given by a finite-intensity compound-Poisson law, including an exact atom at \(1\).
