Every genuine convex pentagon has exactly one of two multiple-illumination spectra: \(I^{(m)}=\lceil5m/2\rceil\) for all positive integers \(m\) when every neighboring exterior-angle sum is strictly below \(\pi\), and \(I^{(m)}=3m\) otherwise. The sharp transition is explained by a five-cycle of simultaneously illuminable vertex pairs versus an incompatible triple for which no direction illuminates two vertices simultaneously.
