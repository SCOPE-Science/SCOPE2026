In the first nontrivial alphabet-permutation defect class—length-\(n\) circular words with one repeated letter and \(n-2\) singleton letters—the circular Burrows–Wheeler output has \(n\) runs in exactly the fraction \((n-3)/(n-1)\) of necklaces. The only obstruction is that the two successors of the repeated symbol occupy consecutive ranks in the alphabet, which yields a complete all-length census.
