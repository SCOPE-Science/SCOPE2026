# Review of the near-socle Jordan census

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The all-dimensional proof reduces the multiplication operator to a three-by-three graded block. Perfect top-degree wedge duality yields the extra one-dimensional row, and the remaining nontrivial map is the alternating form associated with the degree-\(n-2\) component. The classical nondegenerate alternating-form orbit count supplies exact multiplicities. The cases where that component vanishes are handled separately, and square-zero gives the Jordan partition. The proof covers odd and even characteristic, every prime power, all \(n\ge5\); direct computations verify particular cases rather than being used as an infinite proof.

**Originality (O): PASS, qualified.** Draisma–Shaw (arXiv:0909.5676, full HTML Sections 1–2 inspected) addresses singular subspaces; Fulman–Goldstein (arXiv:1211.0504, full HTML Section 5 inspected) supplies established alternating-form counts. The published full rank census for the five-generator characteristic-two group algebra implies the \(n=5\) characteristic-two specialization, which is credited as covered. Other relevant known results concern homogeneous bivector multipliers, four-generator exterior algebras, or characteristic-two homogeneous degree-\(n-3\) multipliers. Their statements do not themselves give the all-field inhomogeneous \(J^{n-2}\) rank distribution and its sharp gaps. Risk: a general wedge-duality argument may make the result implicit in older literature; related research-record full texts could not all be inspected, and historical priority remains unconfirmed. Classical alternating counts are not claimed as new.

**Value (V): PASS.** The complete finite-field rank/Jordan distribution is a natural multiplicative invariant of the full penultimate nontrivial radical layer of every exterior algebra, not a selected coefficient slice. It exhibits unconditional characteristic-uniformity, a complete forbidden-rank pattern, and a genuinely filtered rigidity phenomenon: every higher graded perturbation leaves the rank unchanged once the lowest component is nonzero. These precise uniform invariants matter for annihilator dimensions and principal-ideal strata; no broader representation-theoretic consequence is asserted.
