For every finite field and exterior algebra on at least five generators, right multiplication by the entire inhomogeneous near-socle ideal has an explicit square-zero Jordan spectrum determined by the rank of one alternating form. The only possible operator ranks are \(0,1,2\) and \(2+2r\) for \(1\le r\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), with exact finite-field multiplicities.
