# Scientific review: complete nilpotent Jordan multiplicities in finite-field hook centralizers

## Correctness

**PASS.** The \(R\)-module commutant normal form is exact. Nilpotency forces the two diagonal residue scalars to vanish. The power formulas \(X^2\) and \(X^k\) for \(k\ge3\) give precise ranks; the split into \(2s<n-1\), \(2s=n-1\), and \(2s>n-1\) correctly handles the only possible cancellation of a leading square coefficient. The numbers of field solutions to \(ab=c\) are counted explicitly, and the total is \(q^{n+1}\). Successive rank differences reconstruct unique nilpotent partitions. A separate matrix-power checker passed all 113,305 cases in 19 exhaustive field/dimension configurations, including extension fields. No step assumes the tested range proves the general statement.

## Originality

**PASS, with historical-priority qualification.** Britnell–Wildon's commuting-partition work provides the classical structural reduction and support. Khatami–Košir prove a wider *admissible hook Jordan type* classification; admissibility alone is weaker than an exact finite-field multiplicity for every labeled matrix. The published *Parity geometry and rank census in hook nilpotent centralizers* directly covers the entire square-zero slice: that part of the new theorem is acknowledged as preexisting rather than claimed new. The claim surviving comparison is the **complete non-square-zero, all-partition q-multiplicity census** together with the maximal-block law. Queries of published mathematical records under several equivalent formulations did not locate a dominating numeric law. An unindexed Hall-polynomial formula or other indirect implication remains a real residual risk; no independence or global uniqueness is certified.

## Value

**PASS.** Complete size-by-similarity-type distributions in fixed matrix centralizers are natural finite-field invariants. The change in arithmetic fiber weights at \(2s=n-1\) is genuine, and the largest-block reciprocal-geometric limit is a structural consequence across an unbounded dimension family. The non-square-zero stratification adds information beyond the known support theorem and square-zero rank census. This is a narrowly scoped complete classification, not a claim about all centralizer shapes.

## Limits and sources

The theorem concerns a fixed hook input and counts actual matrices, not simultaneous conjugacy orbits. All finite computations are checks rather than independent certification.
