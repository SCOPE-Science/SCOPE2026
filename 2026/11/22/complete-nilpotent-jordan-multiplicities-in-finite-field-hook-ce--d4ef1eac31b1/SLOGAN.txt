Every nilpotent Jordan partition of a matrix commuting with \(J_n(0)\oplus J_1(0)\) over \(\mathbb F_q\) has an exact characteristic-uniform multiplicity determined by five valuation-profile strata and three residual profiles. At fixed field size, the largest Jordan block divided by \(n\) converges to the reciprocal of a geometric random variable; the previously known square-zero subcase is included rather than claimed as new.
