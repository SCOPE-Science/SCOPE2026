For every labelled wheel \(W_n=K_1\vee C_n\) with \(n\ge7\), a two-state cyclic-gap formula counts every nonlocal resolving set, with exact five-residue polynomials for the number of minimum bases. The ordinary resolving polynomial differs by precisely the configurations with a single four-vertex rim gap, and the nonlocal-to-ordinary total-count ratio tends to \(1.551753477740\ldots\).
