The stated sample space has exactly \(q^{\binom{k+1}{2}+km+m}\) matrices. Eliminating all nonzero peripheral diagonal entries leaves an independent uniform symmetric hub block coupled to precisely the zero-diagonal peripheral vertices. The rank of that rectangular coupling determines a hyperbolic summand, and the residual symmetric restriction gives a nonnegative corank. Gaussian-binomial rectangular and symmetric rank formulas therefore count disjoint cases with no omissions. The nonsingularity condition and the limiting normal window follow without substituting finite experiments for proof. The independent direct-enumeration checker passed every recorded finite test, including characteristic two and a nonprime field.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The Fulman–Goldstein complete symmetric rank distribution counts a different, unrestricted ensemble and serves as an explicit known ingredient here. The Salmond–Grant–Grivell–Chan result provides general zero-pattern probability bounds for unconstrained random matrices, not the exact rank distribution of this symmetric split pattern.
