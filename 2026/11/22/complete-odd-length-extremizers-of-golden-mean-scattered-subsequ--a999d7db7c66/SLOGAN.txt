For all odd \(k=2m+1\), exactly \(m+2\) shortest binary words contain every length-\(k\) word avoiding contiguous `11` as a scattered subsequence: they are \((\mathtt{010})^j\mathtt{10}(\mathtt{010})^{m-j}\) for \(0\le j\le m\), together with \((\mathtt{010})^m\mathtt{01}\). They all have length \(3m+2\), automatically avoid `11`, and are completely characterized by covering the single-one target patterns; the optimal length itself was known previously.
