# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The sign-difference bijection, replacement rule for a valid insertion, run-local forbidden positions and generating-function decomposition have been reconstructed. They prove the exact all-length statement, sharp support and extremizer counts. Three independently defined exact routines agree for \(3\le n\le12\). Their finite checks test implementation rather than supply the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** Chee et al. (2018, arXiv:1801.02310) derive an unrefined word-count recurrence and coding algorithms; their suffix-append transition counts do not determine legal insertion at arbitrary gaps. Tang et al. (2022, arXiv:2208.02330) bound rooted mixed-edit/duplication confusability rather than count single valid insertions. Mol–Rampersad (2020, arXiv:2001.11763) and Dębski–Grytczuk–Pawlik (2022, arXiv:2209.08507) consider insertion of letters into fully square-free words, a different (strictly smaller) language. Querying mathematical records for both insertion-ball and sign-run aliases did not reveal equivalent formulas; this is not proof of absolute novelty. An unindexed equivalent treatment remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The all-length polynomial is a complete natural error-neighborhood classification for ternary short-tandem-irreducible words. Exact smallest and largest insertion spheres, their rigid multiplicities, and the universally missing second-highest size supply structural information useful for constrained edit-channel counting and later packing bounds. The theorem does not itself optimize a codebook.

Independent external priority and correctness review is outstanding. The verified scope is one legal letter insertion into the same truncated-square avoidance language, not fully square-free words or arbitrary insertion/edit channels.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
