For the ordinary simplicial Morse complex of \(K_{3,3}\), all deletions of one, two, or three graph edges admit an exact integral wedge-of-spheres classification by seven fault orbits. Deleting a three-edge path produces the first contractible case, while a single missing edge changes the previously known \(\bigvee^{80}S^4\) into \(S^3\vee\bigvee^{17}S^4\).
