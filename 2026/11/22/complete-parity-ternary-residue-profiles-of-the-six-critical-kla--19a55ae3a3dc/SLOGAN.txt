The six classical free affine-semigroup triples with slopes \(2,3,6\) have exactly three possible limiting fractions of reachable residues modulo \(2\cdot3^k\): \(2/3\), \(3/4\), and \(4/9\), each realized by two triples. Exact finite-level residue predicates identify the distinct arithmetic obstructions, without making any claim about ordinary natural density.
