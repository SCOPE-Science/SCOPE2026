# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every covering edge either collapses or is mapped to another covering edge. The integer lift therefore has increments in \(\{-1,0,1\}\), whose winding sum enforces precisely degree \(-1\), \(0\), or \(+1\). At degree zero, the image is an actual invariant arc of length at most half the crown, hence the induced map is nonexpansive in ordinary integer distance. Its finite periodic set supports an isometry; the only two possibilities are a pointwise-fixed interval or a reflected interval. The symmetry center is a vertex by order preservation. All quantitative bounds and rational zeta formulas follow, and explicit parity-compatible witnesses realize every allowed type. An independent exhaustive enumerator checks all order-preserving maps at crown sizes \(4\), \(6\), and \(8\), finding \(36\), \(234\), and \(1544\) cases. Finite checks supplement, not replace, the argument valid at every size.

## Originality — PASS within inspected literature
Chocano (arXiv:2209.01837) studies the infinite alternating-fence topology and excludes longer periods there, but cannot encompass rotating finite crowns. Published finite-crown homotopy classifications give degree-connected components, not counts of fixed points and two-cycles under iteration. Exact counting of graph-cycle endomorphism images and of bounded crown morphisms has different admissible maps and different invariants. No inspected comparator proves or implies the complete fixed-point/zeta spectrum, its sharp collapse-versus-reflection gap for \(N\ge3\), or the constructive all-size realizability. Specific residual risk: the original 1992 and 1995 full enumerative papers were not accessible for a detailed statement-level comparison; indirect specialization or independent unindexed results are possible.

## Value — PASS
The result is a natural complete classification of all temporal orbit types on a standard family of finite topological circles. It detects a strong structural boundary: collapsing even one edge eliminates all periods at least three and caps two-cycle counts by about half the reversing-homeomorphism maximum. Its rational zeta functions distinguish topology-preserving rotations, reflections, and genuine nonbijective dynamics and provide explicit size-uniform examples. The classification is not just a count of maps or a substitution into previously reported totals.

## Scientific limits
The statement is restricted to height-two crowns and formal periodic-point zeta functions. It does not give a transient-time census. Exhaustive finite verification cannot itself establish the infinite family, and inaccessible older full texts retain a historical-priority risk.
