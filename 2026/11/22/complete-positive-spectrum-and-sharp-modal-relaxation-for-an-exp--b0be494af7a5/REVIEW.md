The negative block reduces to \(cK_q\), while the positive block is triangular Volterra with zero nonzero spectrum. The present operator theorem proves completeness, multiplicities, and the worst-mode rate, which a single scalar pairing does not automatically establish. Wang–Zhang (2017) and the classical cited zero theorems settle properties of the deformed-exponential roots independently of this stochastic operator; no originality is claimed for these zero facts. The related non-expanding chain and negative-coupling modes address different parameters. Published-record and primary-literature comparisons did not locate the operator-level result, but failed searches cannot prove uniqueness. A hidden equivalent in earlier operator or spectral literature remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The established conditioned model has an explicit top persistence eigenvalue, but that does not determine the slower transient modes. Knowing *every* nonzero eigenmode yields a sharp operator-norm relaxation factor, complete positivity of the spectrum, explicit low-order modal power sums, and a nontrivial large-coupling rate \(1/(2\theta)\). This distinguishes slow conditioned equilibration from the source's persistence exponent; the rate is independent of the innovation support asymmetry while eigenvalue amplitudes scale with it. The result concerns a natural active Markov-conditioning question, not an arbitrary numerical slice. It does not assert uniform total-variation mixing.
