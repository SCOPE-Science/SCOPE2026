For the persistence-conditioned uniform MA(1) process with expanding positive coupling \(\theta>1\), every nonzero killed-transition eigenvalue is a simple positive reciprocal of a zero of the deformed exponential. The sharp spectral relaxation factor of its Doob chain on its natural weighted \(L^2\) space is the ratio of the first two positive zeros, independent of innovation asymmetry and asymptotic to \(1/(2\theta)\).
