For every finite rectangular-band algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), all prescribed-target fibers of the \(h\)-th power map, for every \(h\ge2\), are explicit and depend only on rank, coefficient margins, and the total-sum residue class. Exactly at exponent two an additional nonzero square-only Segre cone appears, and it is nonempty precisely when both band dimensions exceed one.
