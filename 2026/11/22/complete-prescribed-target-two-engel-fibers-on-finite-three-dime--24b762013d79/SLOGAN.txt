For the cubic Lie Engel map \(\mathcal E(X,Y)=[X,[X,Y]]\) on \(\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb F_q)\), every prescribed-target fiber has one of four explicit sizes, distinguished by zero, nonzero nilpotent, and the two quadratic-discriminant classes. In odd characteristic, the square and nonsquare semisimple classes receive exactly equal total input mass; in characteristic two the map vanishes identically.
