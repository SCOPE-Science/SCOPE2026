For regular cyclic wreath groups \(\mathbb F_q^{p^a}\rtimes C_{p^a}\), every prescribed \(p^b\)-th-power fiber and every power-image size is explicit in the Jordan filtration of the cyclic shift. The image fails to be a subgroup at every nonterminal depth \(1\le b<a\), becomes a central subgroup of order \(q\) at depth \(a\), and has an explicit lacunary limiting density as the cyclic length grows.
