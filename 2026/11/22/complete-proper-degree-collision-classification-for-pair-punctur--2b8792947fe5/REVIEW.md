The defining Steiner property proves that the leave after two point deletions is an isolated point and an even 2-factor; the classical matching bijection identifies extension diagrams. The formal identity for cycle polynomials and the triangular coefficients of \(\log(1+q(z)^m)\) recover all components shorter than \(d\). The theorem does not rely on finite experiments for the universal quantification.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
Prior published work explicitly supplies the two-puncture cycle-graph definition and perfect-system cases (Grannell–Griggs–Murphy, 1999); the full labelled extension matching polynomial for partial Steiner triple systems is already established (published 2026 result). Cycle matching recurrences and matching-uniqueness for entire disjoint cycle unions are classical, so neither is claimed original. The contribution is the exact all-order **proper-degree** equivalence classification, the sole last-coefficient ambiguity, and its first within-one-Steiner-system witness at order 15. Full-text comparison of Wang 2021 and relevant matching-polynomial sources showed full-polynomial reconstruction, not the missing-highest-coefficient classification. Searches of indexed mathematical results and public literature found no stronger coverage. Historical near-matching-equivalence results could remain unindexed; novelty is not independently certified.

## Value — PASS
The highest-rank extension count has a natural interpretation as perfect matchings, so deleting that coordinate is a motivated, structurally meaningful loss of information rather than an arbitrary numerical slice. The complete converse theorem says exactly when the remaining natural type spectrum is enough; the first realizable sharp 15-point example, including all 35 blocks, gives a reproducible boundary in finite relational extension theory. The result is not merely one evaluated small case or a rephrasing of the full-polynomial inverse theorem.

The 1999 primary initiating paper was available in abstract form, while a 2021 full-text comparison and relevant published mathematical results were inspected.
