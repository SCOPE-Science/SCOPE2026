# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The leave is an isolated third point plus the union of two edge-disjoint perfect matchings, hence even cycles of length at least four. Labelled induced extensions correspond bijectively to leave matchings. In the cycle transfer-matrix formula Q_m=L_+^m+L_-^m, the quotient L_-/L_+ starts with -z, so log E-N log L_+ has triangular contributions beginning at z^m with coefficient +1 per even m-cycle. Equality modulo z^d fixes all lengths below d. After removing common short cycles, fewer than 2d vertices allow at most one remaining component; with all 2d vertices the only alternative is one 2d-cycle versus two d-cycles, with even d >= 4. The difference is 2(L_+L_-)^d=2z^d. Admissible orders 7,9,13 have N=4,6,10, excluding a collision. Independent replay checked each pair of the 35-block design exactly once and obtained the two profiles (12) and (6,6) with coefficient vectors (1,12,54,112,105,36,2) and (1,12,54,112,105,36,4). Different leave-component counts rule out isomorphism. This proves the order-15 sharpness within one design and makes no claim of realizability for every larger order.
