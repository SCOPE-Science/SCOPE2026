Over \(\mathbb F_8\), all eighteen quartic cyclotomic orders have a completely determined coefficient-weight spectrum. Trinomials occur only at orders \(15,105,585,4095\), and the two mixed trinomial-bearing layers \(585\) and \(4095\) have the exact three-, four-, and five-term ratio \(1:4:7\), making their trinomial proportion \(1/12\).
