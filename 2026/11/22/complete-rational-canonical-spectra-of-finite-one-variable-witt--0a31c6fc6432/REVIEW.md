# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact one-variable restricted-power identity is attributed to Evans and Fuchs, and the elementary cyclicity argument applies only when the constant coefficient is nonzero. The top coefficient of the polynomial determining the restricted scalar is affine with nonzero slope in the final coefficient, proving each prescribed scalar has the same number of unit-coefficient preimages. Distinct diagonal entries settle the linear-valuation case; for valuations at least two, explicit leading coefficients of every power determine the entire Jordan partition. The Frobenius multiplier on roots of the Kummer polynomial gives exact factor degrees and counts. All cases exhaust the coefficient space. The three complete small-field enumerations and one bounded large-field sample are corroborating calculations, not an infinite proof.

Evans–Fuchs's scalar restricted-power identity is not novel here. The closest related papers were inspected in accessible full text. An equivalent finite-field type census in unlocated literature remains a genuine priority risk.
