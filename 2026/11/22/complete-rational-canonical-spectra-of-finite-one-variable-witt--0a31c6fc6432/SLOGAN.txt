For every finite field of odd characteristic, the natural action of the one-variable truncated Witt derivation algebra has a complete, explicit rational-canonical type census: all nonnilpotent derivations are semisimple, with their Frobenius splitting degrees determined by a cyclic quotient of order \(p-1\). Nilpotent Jordan types are determined by coefficient valuation, with exact finite-field multiplicities for every type.
