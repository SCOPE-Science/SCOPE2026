The ten-dimensional Dani–Mainkar nilmanifold of the pentagon has an explicitly determined rational cohomology representation in every degree under the full dihedral symmetry, with total Betti number \(364\). Its rotation and full-dihedral ordinary quotients have complete rational Betti vectors \((1,1,4,9,14,18,14,9,4,1,1)\) and \((1,1,2,5,9,9,5,4,2,0,0)\), respectively.
