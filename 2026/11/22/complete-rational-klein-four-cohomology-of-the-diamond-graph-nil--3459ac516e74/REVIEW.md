## Mathematical value — PASS

The diamond is the smallest graph with two triangles sharing an edge, and its graph automorphism group has two inequivalent reflection types corresponding to degree-three and degree-two graph vertices. Their cohomological distinction is not visible in the ordinary Betti vector: the two quotients have distinct second and top rational Betti numbers. The complete graded four-character decomposition gives a natural exact test case for passing from graph-structured nilmanifold cohomology to geometric symmetry quotients, beyond a single scalar invariant. Its limited finite scope is stated explicitly.
