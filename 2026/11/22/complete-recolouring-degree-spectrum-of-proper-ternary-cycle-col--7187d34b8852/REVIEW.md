# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A proper ternary cycle colouring is equivalent to a cyclic word of signed colour increments satisfying a modulo-three closure condition, together with a starting colour. A legal recolouring is exactly a sign transition. Positive-run/negative-run compositions with a marked run produce every degree coefficient; a cubic-root filter gives the total edge count. The edge and extremal cases were independently checked by exhaustive direct colouring enumeration for all lengths through 12. The all-length proof does not depend on experiments.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The 2020 preprint by Brewster and Moore examines winding invariants, reachability and mixing for circular colourings; the examined statements do not give the evaluated recolouring-degree spectrum. Closest indexed records describe Fourier zeros of ternary cycle-colouring indicator functions or exchange counts for *circular ladders*, a different graph. The 2010 paper by Cereceda, van den Heuvel and Johnson was checked only at abstract level, and unindexed equivalent enumerations remain a priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** Local mobility and frozen configurations matter for three-colouring reconfiguration dynamics at the minimal nontrivial palette. The result gives the complete all-length distribution, not merely mean degree, and establishes a sharp parity jump in maximum mobility: six maximizers for even sizes against a cubic-size maximizer family for odd sizes. The period-three frozen states and exact edge count provide natural reference values; no unsupported claim about mixing times is made.
