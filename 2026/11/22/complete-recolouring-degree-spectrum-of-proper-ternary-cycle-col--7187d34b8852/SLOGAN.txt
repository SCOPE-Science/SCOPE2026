The single-vertex recolouring graph of proper three-colourings of a labelled cycle has an exact binomial-congruence degree distribution with no odd degrees. Its maximum degree is \(n\) for even \(n\) (six maximizers), but \(n-3\) for odd \(n\) (exactly \(n(n^2-1)/4\) maximizers), and its edge count is \(n(2^{n-2}+2(-1)^n)\).
