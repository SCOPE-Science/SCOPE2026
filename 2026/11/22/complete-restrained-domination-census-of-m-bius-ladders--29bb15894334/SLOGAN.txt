The complete restrained-domination polynomial of every labelled Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) is the logarithmic derivative of a ratio of two explicit transfer determinants. Its optimal-set multiplicity is exactly \(4\), \(n^2\), or \(2n\) according as \(n\equiv2,0\pmod4\) or \(n\) is odd and at least five, with nine optima at \(n=3\); the twist is responsible for the quadratic residue class.
