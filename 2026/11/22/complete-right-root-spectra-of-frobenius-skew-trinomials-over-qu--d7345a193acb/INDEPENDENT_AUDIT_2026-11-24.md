# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Right reduction of X^j modulo the right factor X−u has constant remainder N_j(u): left multiplication by X changes the preceding remainder v to σ(v)u. Zero is excluded by the constant term. With t=N(u) and n=2m+1 the equation is t^n+at^m u+1=0. For nonzero a, solving for u and imposing N(u)=t is both necessary and sufficient, giving N(a)=z+2+z⁻¹ for z=t^n. Each z in the image contributes exactly gcd(n,q−1) distinct roots because it has that many distinct t and each t determines one u. Equality of the rational-map values is exactly equality or inversion; z=1 gives norm 4 and z=−1 gives the excluded norm zero. For a=0 the latter value contributes an entire norm fiber for each t^n=−1. For n=2m the equation fixes a=−(y+y⁻¹) in the base field with y=t^m. Each allowed y contributes gcd(m,q−1)(q+1) roots and again the only singleton inversion orbits are ±1 when present. These arguments verify the zero bins, the special coefficients and the exact incidence q²−1 (one a per nonzero u). For odd n the defined ε is always one, since gcd(n,q−1) is odd; for even n the limit case L=1 gives only a=−2 with roots. No separability in n or assumption that q is prime is used.
