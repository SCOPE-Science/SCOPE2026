# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

Ore right evaluation is reconstructed via \(N_j(u)=\prod_{i=0}^{j-1}\sigma^i(u)\). For odd exponents, the norm-consistency condition for the uniquely determined candidate root gives \((z+1)^2=Bz\), and inversion orbits in the power subgroup yield all cases, including the exceptional zero coefficient. For even exponents the evaluation is central in the norm variable, and inverse pairs in the \(n/2\)-power subgroup count all fibers. The algebra is valid in every odd characteristic and all odd prime-power sizes. The independently implemented twisted-product verifier executes \(918720\) exact finite-field evaluations. The finite checks do not prove the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Hayata–Tsushima (arXiv:2611.04767), including its main theorem, setup, references and specializations, gives **irreducible coefficient counts**, not the right-root spectrum. Its discussion of Tsushima's 2026 reciprocal-Frobenius fiber theorem concerns the full multiplicative group, while the new odd-exponent calculation requires fibers intersected with a proper power subgroup. The known full-fiber count cannot alone determine this intersection. The case \(n=1\) is classical and excluded from the novelty assertion. The Tsushima Zenodo preprint was not directly readable; a possible hidden subgroup specialization remains the material comparison risk. Targeted public and indexed searches did not identify a covering family theorem, but absence from a database is not proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

Right linear factors are a natural first factorization invariant for the prescribed sparse Ore family, distinct from irreducibility. The result gives the exact complete histogram for **every** coefficient, exponent and odd prime power in quadratic extensions. The odd/even exponent bifurcation, zero-coefficient exception, sharp possible counts and field-of-definition restriction provide an interpretable parameter classification and usable input to later skew-factor and rank studies. No claim is made for higher-degree factors or a global factorization census.

General Ore right-root evaluation, full-field reciprocal fibers and inversion-pair counts are background; the new statement combines these in a new all-exponent coefficient histogram.
