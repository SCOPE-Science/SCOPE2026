In \(\mathbb F_{q^2}[X;\sigma]\) with \(\sigma(c)=c^q\), the entire coefficientwise right-root distribution of \(X^{2n}+aX^n+1\) is explicitly determined for every odd prime power \(q\) and positive \(n\). Odd and even exponents have sharply different root spectra governed by a cyclic subgroup's inverse-pair orbits, including coefficients whose right-root counts grow as \(q+1\).
