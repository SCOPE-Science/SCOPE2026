For a positively weighted cycle with any fixed terminal set, every semi-cutting edge set is obtained by deleting at least one edge in each terminal gap of weight at most half the circumference; a unique heavier gap is completely optional. This yields the full cardinality polynomial, a sharp \(|T|-1\) versus \(|T|\) minimum threshold, exact optimum counts, log-concavity, and roots entirely on \(|1+z|=1\) apart from a possible root at \(-1\).
