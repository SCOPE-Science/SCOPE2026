# Scientific review of the cyclic-king shattering theorem

## Correctness — PASS
All definitions, quantifiers and boundary cases concern the actual translated three-by-three inclusive neighborhoods on \(\mathbb Z_m\times\mathbb Z_n\) for every \(m,n\ge3\). The all-one shattering trace reduces every support to one of 512 three-by-three templates. The five possible nonempty one-dimensional overlap shapes at period at least five, and their four- and three-period analogues, are derived directly. Thus only one potential change, gaining an empty trace, remains as a circumference grows; a complete 512-template certificate excludes critical missing-empty supports at each stable boundary. A second exact exhaustive census supplies the seven base orbit tables and the three local triple motifs. The orbit-stabilizer argument accounts for the sole half-sized orbit at short even period. Therefore the finite certificate is logically sufficient for all parameter values; direct computations for additional torus sizes corroborate it but are not used as an invalid extrapolation. The executable is contained in `VERIFY.py` and was replayed successfully. Risk: the proof is computer-assisted, and no separate independently written formal proof of every finite enumeration is claimed.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Primary relevant published work proves Hamming open-neighborhood VC bounds [Adler–Benediktsson–Macpherson, arXiv:2007.16042] and general algorithmic methods for closed-neighborhood VC computations [Coudert–Csikós–Ducoffe–Viennot, arXiv:2405.07588]. Gillibert–Lachmann–Müllner [arXiv:2004.13861] address unrestricted cyclic boxes, rather than fixed-width discrete translates. The most relevant full published finite-cyclic-rectangles theorem gives only the VC dimension of variable-width boxes (up to six), not a support-count polynomial for king neighborhoods. The published one-dimensional radius-filtered cycle balls, rectangular rook open-neighborhood counts and Hamming closed-neighborhood counts have different concept classes and do not logically imply this result. The search compared objects, definitions, and theorem implications rather than title similarity; no coverage was identified. The \(4\times4\) numerical value is not asserted independent of already published graph-profile coincidences. The originality claim is restricted to the full parameter-dependent profile and its nonpure stable-range structure, subject to the residual risk of equivalent unindexed fixed-box or Moore-neighborhood work.

## Value — PASS
Closed neighborhoods of cyclic king graphs are a canonical finite, transitive, bounded-degree family corresponding to translated fixed three-by-three Moore windows. Their exact shattering profile is not determined by knowing a scalar VC dimension or an unrestricted box-class dimension. The theorem exposes all short-period transitions, a stable linear coefficient law across two parameters, complete structural types of shattered triples, and a nonpure shattering complex with an explicit count of maximal faces. These facts are mathematically motivated and usable in concept-class comparison and finite-range learning, rather than a small unmotivated numerical variation.

## Scope and limitations
The proof depends on a transparent exhaustive local certificate, justified by a rigorous reduction; no claim is made for arbitrary radii, all rectangles, online complexity or independent mathematical certification. The exact historical priority of equivalent fixed-window range systems has not been exhaustively established. Accessible historical research was considered for statement overlap only, not re-reviewed.
