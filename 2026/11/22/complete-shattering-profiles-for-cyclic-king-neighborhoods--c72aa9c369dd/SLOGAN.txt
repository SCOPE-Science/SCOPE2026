Closed-neighborhood shattering on every cyclic king grid has an exact piecewise polynomial, with VC dimension at most three and sharp exceptional short periods. On all tori of side lengths at least five the polynomial is \(1+mnz+12mnz^2+12mnz^3\), and the shattered-support complex is nonpure, with \(4mn\) maximal pairs and \(12mn\) maximal triples.
