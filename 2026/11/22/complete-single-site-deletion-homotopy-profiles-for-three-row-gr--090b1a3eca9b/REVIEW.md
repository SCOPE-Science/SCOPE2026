## Correctness — PASS

Every finite graph, simplicial face, Morse pair, and critical cell is independently generated by the embedded standalone verifier. The sixteen directed Hasse diagrams are exhaustively topologically sorted, and the resulting perfect Morse CW models prove the integral sphere-wedge homotopy types, not merely mod-two homology. An independent mod-two boundary rank calculation and reduced Euler characteristic computation agree for every orbit. Each graph vertex belongs to exactly one orbit under row/column reversal. The proof is restricted to widths four, five and six.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Vertex loss is the canonical site-failure operation, contrasting with already studied edge loss. The complete positional profile provides exact homotopy dimensions and integral Betti ranks for all graph-vertex orbits at three adjacent widths, including a dimension-drop phenomenon at width four and five different ranks at width six. These are exact new local invariants of the natural three-row grid family, and provide base cases for future analysis of site-defect homology, deletion/intersection sequences, and robustness under multiple failures; no unproved generalization is asserted.
