Deleting any one vertex of the \(3\times n\) rectangular grid for \(n=4,5,6\) leaves an ordinary matching complex that is integrally a wedge of spheres, with all sixteen distinct symmetry-orbit cases explicitly classified. The four-column middle-row end deletion uniquely produces a two-sphere rather than degree-three homology, while six-column deletions yield five distinct fifth-homology ranks from six to fourteen.
