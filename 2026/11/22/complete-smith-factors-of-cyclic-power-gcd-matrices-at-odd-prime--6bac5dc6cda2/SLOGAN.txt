For every odd prime \(p\) and positive power \(a\), the complete integral Smith factors of the cyclic matrix \(\bigl(\gcd(x-y,p^2)^a\bigr)_{x,y\bmod p^2}\) have an explicit six-tier formula, not merely a determinant or largest-factor rule. Its \(p\)-primary cokernel is exactly \((\mathbb Z/p^a\mathbb Z)^{p(p-2)}\oplus(\mathbb Z/p^{a+1}\mathbb Z)^p\), while the full list also exhibits prime-to-\(p\) torsion.
