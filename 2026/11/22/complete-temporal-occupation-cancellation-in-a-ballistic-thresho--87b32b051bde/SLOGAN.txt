Random-speed ballistic threshold resetting has complete temporal occupation cancellation: every bounded centered spatial observable integrates exactly to a difference of two phase potentials, so its unscaled long-time integral has a non-Gaussian renewal-limit law instead of ordinary diffusive variance growth. The limiting \(q\)-th absolute moment exists exactly when the excursion duration has a finite \((q+1)\)-st moment; with a finite third duration moment, time-average variance decays sharply as \(t^{-2}\).
