# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness

PASS. The proof gives an exact finite exponential reduction at every three-adic precision, explicit modulo-nine and modulo-eighty-one leading-difference certificates, and a binomial finite-difference lifting argument for arbitrary step-two translations. These yield the sharp branch valuations, all residue counts, and the least eventual period. The accompanying code independently reproduces finite cases but is not used as proof of infinitely many cases.

## Originality

Poonen (1988) already gives the least period; Asgari–Jahangiri (2017) treats residual \(r\)-Fubini period lengths; Schreyer (2024) and a nearby published prime-power shift-defect result concern period or onset. Neither a known period nor a shift-onset bound determines the value distribution, and the inspected full primary texts do not state the exact two-stratum multiplicity law. Unindexed equivalent consequences of established modular identities remain a real priority risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Periodicity alone does not reveal how uniformly a counting sequence visits residues. Here the full three-adic support and two distinct nonzero fiber multiplicities are determined at every precision, with a sharp mechanism explaining this asymmetry. The exact image measure is a natural invariant of ordered Bell numbers, not an arbitrarily selected finite instance.
