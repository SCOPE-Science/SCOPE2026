For the Gonchenko–Gonchenko axially symmetric quadratic three-dimensional maps, every real primitive two-cycle is either one central switching cycle or one of exactly two reflection-paired asymmetric cycles, and every asymmetric cycle has a real expanding return multiplier. At the sharp damping ratio \(r=1/3\), the order of those two orbit-creation thresholds reverses, producing or eliminating an interval with exactly two asymmetric cycles and no symmetric one.
