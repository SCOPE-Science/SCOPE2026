# Assessment of binary Smith invariants of Wendt circulants

## Correctness
**PASS.** The two-adic Fourier decomposition applies because every order in question is odd. The \(2^r-1\) calculation isolates exactly one unit eigenvalue and \(2^r-2\) eigenvalues with valuation one. The \(2^r+1\) calculation isolates either no even eigenvalues, or exactly the two cubic modes, each evaluating to \(-2\). This establishes the stated complete two-primary groups for all \(r\ge1\). Reproducible modular and integral Smith tests independently confirm all stated small cases; they are not substitutes for the proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with historical priority risk.** Frame (1980), Helou (1997), and Helou–Terjanian (2005) were compared at the level of the exact binomial circulant and its resultant. Their inspected determinant factorizations, arithmetic congruences and even-order discriminants do not state the two binary-neighbor two-primary Smith profiles. OEIS A048954 records determinant values, not the Smith groups. The closest indexed Smith work involves other circulant matrices. The 2026 Tang–Fan abstract concerns permutation-binomial characteristic criteria; full text was inaccessible, so possible broader coverage remains a risk.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The result resolves the full two-primary torsion structure of a historically motivated integer matrix for two canonical infinite families; it shows the torsion exponent is always two while its rank jumps between full-minus-one and zero-or-two. This is not recoverable from scalar determinant factorizations without additional structural arguments.

## Limitations
No information about odd-primary Smith factors or arbitrary orders is claimed. Exhaustive historical priority and independent mathematical validation remain outstanding.
