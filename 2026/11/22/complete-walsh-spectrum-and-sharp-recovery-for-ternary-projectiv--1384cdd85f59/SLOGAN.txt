For the \(234\) noncollinear triples of the thirteen-point ternary projective plane, the binary incidence convolution has exactly sixteen signed Walsh eigenvalue levels, with a sharp nonzero gap of \(2\) and condition number \(117\). Every one of its \(8192\) Fourier modes is recoverable, and all eigenvalues are congruent to \(2\pmod4\).
