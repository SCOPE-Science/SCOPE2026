A selected set of cells totally resolves a rectangular rook graph exactly when two row–column leaf obstructions and one two-row/two-column component-cut obstruction are absent. The resulting infinite-family criterion gives \(\dim_t(K_4\square K_4)=6\), exactly \(2{,}016\) minimum landmark sets in five symmetry orbits, and an explicit distinction from weak \(2\)-resolution at the same cardinality.
