# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The theorem's starting record-subset bijection is Theorem 2.2 and Corollary 2.3 of Bouvel–Cioni–Ferrari (2022). Changing one selected record rearranges two distinguished entries around exactly the intervening original segment; single-adjacent-swap feasibility is therefore equivalent to that segment being empty. Changing two distinct selected records cannot be one adjacent swap because the left-to-right record sets of adjacent permutations differ by at most one value. Freezing coordinates at nonconsecutive original record positions yields independent Cartesian blocks; each frozen-first-bit block has a unique immutable leading run of ones, followed, if possible, by a zero and a connected facilitated tail. This proves the exact component-size multiset. Counting outputs with all pre-final record positions consecutive gives the formula for connected fibers. Exhaustive comparisons against the actual bubble pass, graph edges and BFS component sizes succeeded through length eight, and the integer enumerator was checked through length 250. No numerical experiment is used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The most relevant primary full text, Bouvel–Cioni–Ferrari (2022), establishes a bijection for inverse fibers and classifies a *sorting tree under complete passes*; it does not give the induced one-swap graph of each equal-output fiber, frozen-coordinate criterion, Cartesian components or connected-output census. Xu–Zhou (2023) analyzes connectivity in the full Cayley bubble-sort graph and does not imply its induced inverse-fiber component structure. The earlier pattern-class inverse-image article (Albert et al., 2011) treats avoidance-class closure under the map, rather than within-fiber moves. Targeted external mathematical searches and a comparison with related permutation-swap component results yielded no dominating all-fiber theorem. The record-subset bijection and the facilitated bit graph are not claimed as new objects. The residual historical risk is an equivalent theorem filed under restricted-permutation reconfiguration, sorting-network fibers or another terminology.

**Value — PASS.** A preimage enumeration alone says that all exact-output fibers have power-of-two cardinalities but does not establish whether they can be traversed by a single output-preserving adjacent swap. This classification gives an exact reachability test, explicit component sizes and counts, and the sharp all-size conclusion that the share of reachable-in-one-component output fibers decays as \(e/(n-1)\). These are meaningful structural and algorithmic invariants of the natural smallest-edit reconfiguration graph, not an arbitrary slice or a recomputed table.

**Limits.** The theorem is restricted to one ordinary bubble-sort pass, distinct symbols, nonempty exact-output fibers, and one adjacent swap per move. The proof needs the published record-subset characterization; the accompanying code independently tests many small instances but cannot prove the theorem in the infinite parameter range. An unindexed equivalent description cannot be ruled out, and independent mathematical audit remains to be conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
