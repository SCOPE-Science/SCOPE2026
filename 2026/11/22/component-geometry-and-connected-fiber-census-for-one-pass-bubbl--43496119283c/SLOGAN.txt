Every one-pass bubble-sort inverse fiber under adjacent-position swaps splits into an explicit Cartesian product of a connected facilitated bit graph and frozen-first-bit factors, with every component size determined by gaps between record positions. Among the \((n-1)!\) nonempty fibers of length \(n\ge2\), exactly \((n-2)!\sum_{j=0}^{n-2}1/j!\) are connected, an asymptotic proportion \(e/(n-1)\).
