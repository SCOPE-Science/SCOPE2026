For cyclic binary words avoiding both constant triples, legal single-bit flips preserve the number \(k\) of equal neighboring pairs, and every non-frozen \(k\)-level is exactly one connected component with \(\frac{2n}{k}\binom{n-k-1}{k-1}\) vertices. The only exceptions are two alternating isolated words for even lengths and four additional isolated words when the length is divisible by four; reachability and an explicit path are computable in quadratic time.
