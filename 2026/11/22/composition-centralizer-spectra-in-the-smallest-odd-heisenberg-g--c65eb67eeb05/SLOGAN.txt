Every endomorphism of the odd-prime Heisenberg group of order \(p^3\) has one of exactly seven possible composition-centralizer sizes, with an explicit polynomial giving every multiplicity. The number of ordered commuting endomorphism pairs is exactly \(p^8+5p^7-8p^5+3p^3\), and only the trivial map and identity lie in the center of the endomorphism monoid.
