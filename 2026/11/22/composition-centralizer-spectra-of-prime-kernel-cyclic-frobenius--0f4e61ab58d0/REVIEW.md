# Scientific review of the endomorphism-centralizer theorem

## Correctness — PASS

The endomorphism normal form is forced by the fully invariant derived subgroup \(\langle a\rangle\), the conjugacy relation, and the exact order of the complement action. The formula for composition is direct evaluation on generators. Case-by-case commutation equations count all partners, including the trivial map. The key nonstandard step is a geometric-sum comparison, whose numerator is \(1-u^{k\ell}\): it vanishes exactly for zero products modulo \(r\), and otherwise division yields the stated field coloring. Every centralizer fiber and its multiplicity are counted without assuming \(r\) prime. Conjugacy and retraction formulas are separately derived from explicit actions and the Chinese remainder theorem. The numerical checker and actual-group homomorphism tests agree. General validity relies on the algebraic proof, not on sampled cases.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence

The full metacyclic classification of García-Blázquez–del Río gives isomorphism invariants of the source groups, not centralizer sizes in \(\operatorname{End}(G)\). Wang describes \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\) for a broader Frobenius class, but does not enumerate all its group endomorphism commutants. Bors studies non-isomorphic nilpotent \(p\)-groups. The commutation semigroups of DeWolf–Edmunds are generated by commutator maps, not the entire monoid of group homomorphisms. The endomorphism graph of Ajith and coauthors has **group elements**, not endomorphisms, as vertices. Compared with a published centralizer-spectrum result for the full affine transformation monoid of a finite ring, this theorem concerns the endomorphism monoid of a nonabelian finite group with nonunit strata indexed by cyclic-complement exponents. Searches found no equivalent all-parameter centralizer multiplicity polynomial or color/zero-product criterion. A general historical result might imply it; publication priority is provisional and not independently assessed.

## Value — PASS

The centralizer spectrum is a natural invariant of the full endomorphism monoid, distinguishing arithmetic of the complement: it has exactly \(\tau(r)+1\) values. The complete commutation criterion reveals why the prime/composite transition changes the graph structure, and gives a closed finite-parameter census of commuting pairs. It also links the same natural group family to exact image-order, retraction, and unit-conjugacy distributions. The value does not rest solely on small-number enumeration or a routine substitution.

## Limits and closest literature

The proof is limited to faithful cyclic complements acting on a prime-order kernel, with \(r\ge3\). The oldest unlocated semidirect-product endomorphism classification remains a possible originality risk. Most closely relevant sources are García-Blázquez–del Río (arXiv:2301.08683), Wang (arXiv:1610.05914), Bors (arXiv:1411.4190), DeWolf–Edmunds (arXiv:1807.10389), and Ajith et al. (arXiv:2511.15602). The 2018 article was only recoverable at the abstract level; the distinction in its stated object is clear, but its full contents were not inspected. No independent expert or formal certification has been performed.
