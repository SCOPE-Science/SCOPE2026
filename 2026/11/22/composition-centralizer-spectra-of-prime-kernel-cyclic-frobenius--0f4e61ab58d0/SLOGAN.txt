The complete composition-centralizer spectrum of endomorphisms of \(C_p\rtimes C_r\), for \(3\le r\mid p-1\), has exactly \(\tau(r)+1\) distinct values and explicit arithmetic multiplicities. Its nonautomorphism commuting relation is precisely equality of a field color or zero multiplication modulo \(r\), simultaneously yielding the rank, retraction, and automorphism-orbit censuses.
