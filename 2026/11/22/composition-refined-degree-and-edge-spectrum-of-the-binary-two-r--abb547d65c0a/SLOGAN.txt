The two-read single-deletion ambiguity graph of binary words has an explicit joint generating function for Hamming weight and the degree **within the same-weight layer**, alongside exact counts of edges between every pair of weight layers. At weight \(k\), the maximum within-layer degree is \(r\min(s,r+1)\), where \(r=\min(k,n-k)\) and \(s=\max(k,n-k)\); for asymptotically balanced words, the mean is only \((2/3+o(1))n\) although the maximum is quadratic.
