# Review of the friendship-network activity theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** The full count is represented by the count-tilted matrix with diagonal entries for counted transitions remaining in the same orbit. The three stationary class masses, the Poisson corrector, determinant differentiation, positive Fano excess and monotone difference can all be checked algebraically. The limiting moment-generating function follows from a fast-hub convolution approximation at each fixed observation time; no claim of \(J_1\) path convergence is made. The symbolic test and an independent direct configuration-space calculation agree for the checked range.

## Originality
**PASS on examined evidence, with residual priority risk.** The foundational ring cumulant calculations of Appert-Rolland and coauthors (arXiv:0804.2590v1) and the ring-activity phase analysis of Lecomte and coauthors (arXiv:1203.1600v1) concern cycles rather than the triangular-bouquet family. The published finite-network Poisson-activity classification settles only strictness of non-Poisson behavior, not its exact quantitative fluctuation law or graph-size limit. The published Hamming-graph activity result has a different host graph and different limiting law. The published two-token friendship Laplacian spectrum describes the untilted operator; it does not present the counting-field deformation, Fano expression or limiting count distribution. All three most relevant published source texts were read and their conclusions compared, rather than being dismissed on titles. An unindexed earlier equivalent quantitative result remains possible.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The exact two-particle counting-noise law supplies a natural transport-fluctuation invariant for the minimal cyclic hub-and-blade network. Its strict monotone increase and limit \(5/3\), despite only two particles on a growing graph, are explained by a nontrivial compound-Poisson singleton/doublet mechanism. This separates a physical time-scale effect from the already known qualitative non-Poisson classification; it is not merely a recomputation of a graph table.

## Scope and remaining limits
The model has two particles, unit symmetric legal-edge rates, stationary initialization and triangular blades. The finite-time limit is a marginal-count law, not a \(J_1\)-topology path limit. No external independent validation or exhaustive literature-priority guarantee is asserted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
