Two hard-core walkers on a friendship graph have an exact successful-jump Fano ratio that increases strictly with the number of triangular blades and approaches \(5/3\), rather than becoming Poissonian as the graph grows. At each fixed time the total count converges to the sum of independent \(\operatorname{Poisson}(2t)\) singles and twice \(\operatorname{Poisson}(2t)\) doublets, because brief excursions through the increasingly connected hub pair jumps together.
