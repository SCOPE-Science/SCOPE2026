Conditioned on crossing a finite absorbing interval rather than returning to its entry boundary, a symmetric run-and-tumble particle makes an even number of reversals, and half that count is exactly compound Poisson with a Robin-eigenvalue geometric decomposition. In the long-interval limit, the count grows on the square of the persistence-length ratio and approaches a non-Gaussian exponential-series law with Laplace transform \(\sqrt{s}/\sinh\sqrt{s}\).
