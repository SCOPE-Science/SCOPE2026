Analytical verification follows the outgoing-velocity kinetic boundary conditions, the matrix identity \(A_z^2=(1-z^2)I\), the positive Robin–Dirichlet Sturm–Liouville eigenvalues and their genus-zero canonical product. The checker verifies the closed generating function against a truncated product, exact derivative identities, and independent spatial-flight sampling. The latter is a finite empirical cross-check, not an infinite-proof certificate.

The exact success probability is \(1/(1+c)\), and its zero-reversal joint probability is \(e^{-c}\). The conditioned zero-reversal probability is therefore \((1+c)e^{-c}\). At large \(c\), the scaled mean and variance converge to \(1/6\) and \(1/90\). Statements involving all \(c>0\), the infinite product and the limit law rely on the written analytical proof, not on the finite numerical cases.

The result is restricted to inward launches and velocity-selective absorption at the two endpoints. Publication-priority comparison of the 1994 reversal-resolved first-passage study remains limited by inaccessible full text.
