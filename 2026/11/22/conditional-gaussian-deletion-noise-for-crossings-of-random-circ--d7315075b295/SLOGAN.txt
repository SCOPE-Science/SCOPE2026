For independent uniform-endpoint circular chords, global crossing-count fluctuations are asymptotically independent of the fraction of crossings lost when one sampled chord is removed, but **not** of the deletion's finer Gaussian noise. Given its arc fraction \(t\), the global \(n^{3/2}\)-scale and deletion \(\sqrt n\)-scale limits have conditional covariance \([2t(1-t)]^2/6\).
