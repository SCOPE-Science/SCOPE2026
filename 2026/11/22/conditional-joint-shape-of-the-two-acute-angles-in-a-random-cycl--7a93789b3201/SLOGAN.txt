Conditioned on a uniformly sampled cyclic polygon having exactly two acute vertices, the normalized right-angle deficits have an explicit joint density depending only on their maximum. For every \(n\ge4\), twice the larger deficit is \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,n-2)\) and the smaller-to-larger ratio is an independent uniform variable, yielding a nondegenerate bivariate rare-event limit after scaling by \(n\).
