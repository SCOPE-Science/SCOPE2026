Under Hamiltonicity conditioning, the unnormalized algebraic connectivity of a uniform random threshold graph tends to the one-plus-size-biased geometric law \(\Pr(R=r)=(r-1)2^{-r}\) for \(r\ge2\). Its local spectral fluctuation decouples asymptotically from the full Brownian-meander path that jointly governs the graph's centered clique and edge counts.
