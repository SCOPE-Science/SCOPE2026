For arbitrary flat strict implication on a finite Gödel chain, a terminal Heyting congruence survives exactly when the adjacent-arrow values above its cut stay above that cut; every possible subset of cuts occurs. An explicit intersection-polynomial enumerates the entire congruence spectrum, and the fraction of simple expansions converges to \(1-e^{-1}\), in contrast to the paired weak Heyting–Brouwer case.
