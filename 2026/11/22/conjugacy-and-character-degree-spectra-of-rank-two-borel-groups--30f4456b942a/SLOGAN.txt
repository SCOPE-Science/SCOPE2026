The upper-triangular invertible group over any finite commutative square-zero local ring has an explicit six-row conjugacy-class spectrum and three-row irreducible character-degree spectrum determined only by the residue-field size and radical dimension. The formula includes nonprincipal and mixed-characteristic rings and yields a limiting commuting probability of \(1/[q^2(q-1)^2]\) for fixed \(q\) as radical dimension grows.
