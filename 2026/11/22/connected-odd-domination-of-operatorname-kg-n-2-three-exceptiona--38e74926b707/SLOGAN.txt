The minimum connected odd dominating sets of the rank-two Kneser graph \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) are five-cycles in the auxiliary complete graph, apart from matching optima at \(n=6,10\) and \(840\) star-plus-matching optima at \(n=8\). The twelve Petersen-graph optima, exchanged two selected vertices at a time, form the crown graph \(K_{6,6}-M_6\).
