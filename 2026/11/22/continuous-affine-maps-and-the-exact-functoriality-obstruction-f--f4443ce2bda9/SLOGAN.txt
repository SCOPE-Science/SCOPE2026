For the signed-divisor Alexandrov topology on the Grothendieck group of finite simplicial complexes under join, every continuous algebraically affine self-map is coordinatewise either constant or a nonzero integer multiple of a single prime coordinate, with zero offset in the latter case. Consequently a join-monoid map remains continuous after group completion exactly when images of distinct join-primes are pairwise join-coprime; even an injective monoid map can fail this criterion.
