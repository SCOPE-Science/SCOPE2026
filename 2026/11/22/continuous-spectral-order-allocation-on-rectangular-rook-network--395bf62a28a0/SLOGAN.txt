On \(K_m\square K_n\), a unique unrestricted edge allocation optimizes every inverse spectral moment and the determinant, with per-edge conductance ratio growing as \(n^{(p+1)/(p+2)}\) at fixed spectral order. A new logarithmic crossover appears when \(p/\log n\to\alpha\): the normalized rate ratio tends to \(e^{-1/\alpha}\) for \(0<\alpha<\infty\), interpolating between zero and one.
