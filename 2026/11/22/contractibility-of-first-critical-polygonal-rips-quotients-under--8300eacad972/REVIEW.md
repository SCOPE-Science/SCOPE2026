# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The clique classification is exhaustive: a clique anchored at zero either fits an arc of length at most \(m\), or its extreme clockwise and counterclockwise distances are both \(m\), yielding the unique equilateral-triangle pattern. The arc cover provides a **dihedrally equivariant** circle nerve, since every nonempty stabilizer-invariant intersection is an arc admitting a stabilizer-fixed midpoint. An exceptional triangle has full \(S_3\) stabilizer; its quotient triangular chamber retracts relative to the quotient boundary edge, so the full quotient retracts onto the interval-type nerve quotient. The finite verifier reproduces the clique description and checks mod-two and mod-three quotient chains for \(m=2,3,4,5\). Finite tests are not used as a proof of the universal quantifier.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated evidence limits

The closest full-text primary sources are Adamaszek's 2011 cycle-power theorem and the 2014 circular-arc nerve and dihedral-homology treatment, together with Adamaszek–Adams (2015). They provide the unquotiented homotopy types and a nerve model, but no full-dihedral *ordinary orbit-space* homotopy classification was identified. The indexed rotation quotient is a Moore space, not the full-dihedral quotient; the published fixed-point description and rational dihedral character do not by themselves imply integral contractibility. Searches explicitly compared critical-threshold, clique-power, nerve, fixed-space, Moore-space, and group-action formulations. The complete contents of two highly relevant indexed research records were not retrievable; their accessible summaries were compared, and an unindexed equivalent proof is a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The first critical polygonal Rips family changes from a circle to a wedge of two-spheres; the rotational orbit retains arbitrarily large torsion. Complete contractibility after adjoining reflections is a structural difference not detectable by ordinary rational-invariant calculations. The theorem explains the cancellation by the exact stabilizer geometry and produces a uniform infinite-family homotopy classification of a natural symmetry quotient rather than a small-parameter table.

## Verification and limits

The arguments are finite-dimensional CW and equivariant nerve arguments. No Borel-quotient, other subgroup, or off-critical scale result is asserted. The finite program checks four sample parameters and cannot certify the all-parameter theorem by itself.
