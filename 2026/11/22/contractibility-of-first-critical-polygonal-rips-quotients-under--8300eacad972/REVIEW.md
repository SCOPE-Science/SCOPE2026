# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In a clique anchored at 0, all vertices lie within distance m of 0; if both sides occur, extremes x and -y are adjacent only if x+y≤m or x+y≥2m. The first condition gives an interval face and the second forces x=y=m. An interior vertex of any sector between consecutive members of {0,m,2m} is too far from the opposite member, so the equilateral triangle cannot extend. Its edges are interval faces. For the short-arc subcomplex the circle cover by centered open arcs of length m+ε, 0<ε<1/2, has intersections precisely for sets of integer centers in a length-m interval. The arcs have length less than half the circumference, hence intersections are geodesically convex arcs. An equivariant nerve proof can be made explicit: map each vertex σ of the subdivided nerve to the midpoint of the σ intersection and interpolate along its short arc on chains. Map back by the normalized distances to the complements of the cover sets. The composition on the circle is equivariantly homotopic to identity along short geodesics. On a chain with least face σ, the reverse composition and identity lie in the closed star of σ. These stars nest on passage to faces and contract equivariantly to the barycenter of σ for its stabilizer, giving the equivariant carrier homotopy. Thus on quotients the short-arc complex has the homotopy type of a circle modulo rotations and reflections, a closed interval. The equilateral triangles form one orbit with stabilizer generated by the order-three rotation and a reflection, inducing S3. The simplex quotient is the sorted barycentric chamber; its boundary-subspace image is just the smallest-coordinate-zero edge. Linear deformation onto that edge fixes the attaching set pointwise and extends by the identity on the short-arc quotient. This gives the claimed strong deformation retraction for all m≥2 and contractibility of the ordinary geometric quotient.

## originality

PASS

The cycle-power ambient type, short-arc model, and critical equilateral triangles are prior ingredients, and the rotation-only quotient and fixed-space information are materially related prior findings. The supported distinct result is the full-dihedral ordinary quotient and its relative chamber retraction, not the antecedent ingredients or a claim about other subgroups.
