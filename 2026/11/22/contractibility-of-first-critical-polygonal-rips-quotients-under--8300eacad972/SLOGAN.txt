For every \(m\ge2\), the ordinary geometric quotient of the first-critical polygonal Rips complex \(\operatorname{Cl}(C_{3m}^{m})\) by all dihedral symmetries is contractible. Its short-arc nerve quotient is an interval up to homotopy, and each exceptional equilateral triangle becomes a retractable chamber under its six-element stabilizer, eliminating the torsion present in the rotation-only quotient.
