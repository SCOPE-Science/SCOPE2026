Although the Heawood graph's independence complex is homotopy equivalent to six three-spheres, its geometric quotient by all 336 graph automorphisms is contractible: \(|\operatorname{Ind}(H)|/\operatorname{Aut}(H)\simeq *\). An exact integral orbit-cell calculation and 51-generator fundamental-group elimination show why rational acyclicity upgrades to genuine contractibility.
