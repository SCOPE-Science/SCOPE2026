Exactly \(d+1\) time translates of a tensor Gaussian give an integer-lattice Gabor frame if and only if every coordinate has \(d+1\) distinct shift residues; this yields explicit positive frame bounds and minimal all-dimensional Gaussian-window constructions. For a natural planar three-window collision, the optimal lower frame bound vanishes exactly to second order, with coefficient \(\ell_\beta^2D_\beta^2/2\) given by convergent Gaussian theta products.
