Averaging two independently programmed, individually rank-one-correctable inverse approximations can create a rank-two residual, making even the best rank-one digital correction imperfect. In an exact two-mode model, centered programming signs give optimally corrected risk asymptotic to \(\alpha^2(1/2-1/\pi)/D\), whereas one-sided signs leave the permanent risk floor \(\alpha^2/4\), despite strictly decreasing uncorrected risk.
