For every beta-weighted analytic Bergman space and every binomial multiplier \(z^m+a\) with integer \(m\ge2\), its positive Gram operator has infinitely many discrete eigenvalues below the essential band in **each** of its \(m\) congruence sectors exactly when \(|a|<1\). When \(0<|a|<1\), the count within each sector at distance \(\epsilon\) below the band is at least a constant times \(\epsilon^{-1/2}\); for \(|a|\ge1\), the entire spectrum is only the essential interval.
