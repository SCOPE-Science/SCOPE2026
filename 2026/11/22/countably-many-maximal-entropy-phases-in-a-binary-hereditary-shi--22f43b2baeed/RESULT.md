# Countably many maximal-entropy phases in a binary hereditary shift with a sharp forbidden-entropy boundary

## Finding
Let \(u^{(k)}=10(01)^{k-1}\) for every integer \(k\ge2\), let \(m^{(k)}\in\{0,1\}^{\mathbb Z}\) be its bi-infinite periodic repetition, and let \(Y\) be the shift-orbit closure of all \(m^{(k)}\). Define the two-sided binary subshift
\[
 X=\{x\in\{0,1\}^{\mathbb Z}:\text{some }y\in Y\text{ satisfies }x_i\le y_i\text{ for all }i\in\mathbb Z\}.
\]
Then \(X\) is hereditary and nonsofic, with topological entropy
\[
 h_{\mathrm{top}}(X)=\tfrac12\log2.
\]
It has **exactly countably infinitely many ergodic measures of maximal entropy**: one for the two-periodic alternating mask and exactly one for each periodic mask orbit of least period \(2k\), \(k\ge2\). Each is obtained by uniform randomization of the mask's phase and independent fair coin choices on its \(1\)-positions, with zeros forced elsewhere. In particular these measures have different cyclic periods \(2,4,6,8,\ldots\). They are pairwise distinct.

If \(h_{\mathcal M}\) is the exponential limsup entropy of minimal forbidden words and \(h_{\mathcal G}\) is the infimal entropy of generating forbidden sets, both in the sense of Wu--Zhang (2026), then
\[
 h_{\mathcal G}(X)=h_{\mathcal M}(X)=h_{\mathrm{top}}(X)=\tfrac12\log2.
\]
Moreover, the length-\(n\) language satisfies the constructive bound
\[
 2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1}\ \le\ |\mathcal L_n(X)|\ \le\ (n^2+5n+10)2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1}\qquad(n\ge2).
\]
Thus the equality boundary is attained by a *binary hereditary* example, without an additional marker alphabet or irrational-rotation masks.

## Assumptions and scope
All shifts are bilateral, with the usual product topology, and logarithms are natural. A binary subshift is hereditary when coordinatewise deleting any set of its ones keeps the result in the shift. For \(k\ge2\) write \(O_k=\{\sigma^j m^{(k)}:0\le j<2k\}\), and let \(O_*\) be the two-point alternating orbit. Define \(Z=\{(y,x)\in Y\times\{0,1\}^{\mathbb Z}:x\le y\}\), with diagonal shift. The projection \(Z\to X\) is onto.

The masked Bernoulli measures are distributions on \(X\), not claims of independence of every pair of adjacent *output* bits. Here \(\mathcal M_n(X)\) denotes minimal absent length-\(n\) words (both proper length-\((n-1)\) factors must occur), and \(h_{\mathcal M}(X)=\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{-1}\log|\mathcal M_n(X)|\) with \(\log0=-\infty\). The invariant measures under discussion are ergodic for the one-step shift \(\sigma\).

## Proof
**1. Compact masks and an exact description of their limit points.** In the cyclic word \(u^{(k)}\), there is exactly one adjacent \(11\) and exactly one adjacent \(00\), occurring in the same fixed four-site defect cluster \(1100\) across the period boundary. Outside this cluster the mask alternates, and successive clusters are spaced by \(2k\). Thus if \(k\to\infty\), a convergent sequence of translates either has its nearest cluster escape every bounded window (yielding an alternating mask), or has one cluster at a fixed offset in the limit (yielding a shift of a single bi-infinite sequence with one defect cluster and alternating tails). Consequently
\[
 Y=\bigcup_{k\ge2}O_k\ \cup\ O_*\ \cup\ O_{\mathrm{def}},
\]
where \(O_{\mathrm{def}}\) is one countably infinite nonperiodic shift orbit of a mask with one \(1100\) cluster. In particular \(Y\) is compact and countable. Its only periodic orbits are \(O_*\) and the \(O_k\): the sole \(11\) cluster per \(2k\)-period proves that \(m^{(k)}\) has *least* period \(2k\). Every ergodic shift-invariant probability measure on the countable space \(Y\) is uniform on one finite orbit: some point has positive mass, and invariance gives equal positive mass to all points of its orbit, which therefore must be finite.

**2. Language growth.** In \(u^{(k)}\), the prefix discrepancy \(S(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{t-1}(2u^{(k)}_i-1)\) takes values only in \(\{-1,0,1\}\), starts and ends at zero over a period, and is extended periodically. Hence any length-\(n\) interval in any mask has at most \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) ones and at least \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\) ones (the weaker integer inequalities suffice). To bound the number of length-\(n\) mask words, those with \(2\le k\le n\) contribute at most \(\sum_{k=2}^n2k\le n(n+1)\). For all \(k>n\), a length-\(n\) window either sees purely alternating letters or intersects only one cluster \(1100\), so there are at most \(n+O(1)\) further patterns independent of \(k\); the explicit generous bound \(n^2+5n+10\) covers every \(n\ge2\), including defect and alternating limits. Each mask word has at most \(2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1}\) binary descendants. Conversely, any fixed mask supplies at least \(2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1}\) descendants on each length-\(n\) window. These prove the two-sided language bound and \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(X)=\frac12\log2\). Compactness of \(Z\) and its projection also proves that \(X\) is a closed shift-invariant hereditary subshift.

**3. The maximal-entropy measures.** Fix a periodic mask orbit \(O_k\). On the component of \(Z\) with a chosen phase of that mask, the \(2k\)-step shift is conjugate to the full shift on \(k\) independent binary coordinates per block. It has one measure of maximal entropy for that return map, the fair product measure. Averaging its \(2k\) phases gives one ergodic \(\sigma\)-invariant measure \(\eta_k\) on \(Z\), of entropy \(\frac12\log2\), and it is the unique maximal-entropy measure over \(O_k\). The analogous construction over the alternating orbit \(O_*\) gives \(\eta_*\). The projection of \(\eta_k\) to \(X\) has the same entropy: conditioning an output word on its phase changes its Shannon entropy by at most \(\log(2k)\), independent of word length. Thus its image \(\mu_k\) (and similarly \(\mu_*\)) is an ergodic measure of maximal entropy of \(X\).

Conversely, let \(\mu\) be an arbitrary ergodic maximal-entropy measure on \(X\). Surjectivity of \(Z\to X\) and compactness allow an invariant lift, and its ergodic decomposition contains an ergodic lift \(\eta\) projecting to \(\mu\). Entropy cannot decrease under lifting, and the language bound for \(Y\) shows \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(Z)=\frac12\log2\). Therefore \(\eta\) has maximal entropy in \(Z\). Its marginal on \(Y\) is ergodic, hence uniform on \(O_*\) or \(O_k\). The preceding uniqueness within each finite mask orbit forces \(\mu=\mu_*\) or \(\mu=\mu_k\).

To distinguish them, under \(\mu_k\) and conditional on its mask phase, every allowed residue position has infinitely many ones almost surely, while every forbidden residue position has none. A different periodic mask of density exactly \(1/2\) cannot contain all the allowed residues of \(m^{(k)}\) unless the masks are equal; their least periods differ. Conditional on a phase, the empirical frequency of ones on each residue class modulo \(2k\) is almost surely \(1/2\) for free residues and zero for forced residues; hence the mask phase is measurable from the output. The return system is Bernoulli and the mask has least period \(2k\), so the measure-theoretic cyclic period is exactly \(2k\) (and the alternating case has period two). Therefore \(\mu_j\neq\mu_k\) for \(j\neq k\), and none equals \(\mu_*\). The complete list is countably infinite.

**4. The sharp entropy boundary.** Every minimal forbidden word of length \(n\ge2\) has an admissible length-\((n-1)\) prefix, so \(|\mathcal M_n(X)|\le2|\mathcal L_{n-1}(X)|\) and \(h_{\mathcal M}(X)\le h_{\mathrm{top}}(X)\). Minimal forbidden words generate any subshift, hence \(h_{\mathcal G}(X)\le h_{\mathcal M}(X)\). Theorem A of Wu--Zhang (2026) states that the *strict* inequality \(h_{\mathcal G}(X)<h_{\mathrm{top}}(X)\) forces only finitely many ergodic maximal-entropy measures. The countably infinite family just proved excludes that strict inequality, so all three quantities are equal. This step uses the cited published theorem; the countably infinite maximal-entropy classification above does not rely on it. A finite labeled-graph presentation would have only finitely many ergodic maximal-entropy components, so \(X\) is nonsofic.

## Relationship to prior work
Wu and Zhang (2026, arXiv:2611.03758v1, Theorem A) prove the strict generating-forbidden entropy gap criterion used here; their finite-alphabet example does not identify this binary hereditary mask family or classify its periodic maximal-entropy phases. Kułaga-Przymus, Lemańczyk, and Weiss (2014, arXiv:1406.3745, Section 3.2) construct binary hereditary shifts with two and, separately, uncountably many distinct maximal-entropy measures. Their irrational-rotation masks and these cardinality claims do not provide the exact countably infinite orbit classification or the explicit period-\(2k\) mask language estimate established here. The previously available finite-alphabet equal-entropy boundary example with countably many measures uses separate ordinary and marker alphabets rather than a hereditary binary mask shift. A published 2026 modular-spacing-shift census also uses periodic hereditary masks and their Bernoulli maximizing measures, but treats a fixed finite modulus and has finitely many phases; it does not establish the closure and exact countably infinite phase list of this varying-period construction. The construction specializes hereditary Bernoulli thinning ideas, not a new general thermodynamic formalism.

## Limitations
The shift is not claimed to be transitive, mixing, or intrinsically ergodic; indeed its different maximal-entropy phases correspond to distinct invariant subsystems. The polynomial language estimate is a bound, not an exact word-count formula, and the minimal-forbidden entropy equality relies on the cited 2026 gap theorem rather than a new complete antidictionary. Preexisting general hereditary examples and standard symbolic codings make historical-priority comparisons especially delicate; no first-ever binary example is asserted. Independent validation and exhaustive historical priority review are not claimed.
