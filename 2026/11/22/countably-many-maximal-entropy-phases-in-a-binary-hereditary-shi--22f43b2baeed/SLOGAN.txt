The binary hereditary shift generated by periodic masks \(10(01)^{k-1}\) for integers \(k\ge2\) has entropy \(\frac12\log2\) and exactly countably many ergodic maximal-entropy measures, with explicit even cyclic periods. Its minimal- and generating-forbidden-word entropies equal its positive topological entropy, showing the sharp boundary in an explicit two-symbol hereditary model.
