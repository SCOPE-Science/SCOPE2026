# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reduces enclosing Euclidean spheres for equal-norm graph roots to a least-norm convex-hull projection, exactly converts Ferrers edge mixtures to two nested Hall constraints, and computes the unique quadratic projection. The two cut normals form a positive definite Gram matrix. Every regime follows from a two-dimensional complementarity system; explicit center-to-edge squared-distance differences classify all vertex contacts. A standalone exact-rational checker exhaustively covered 4096 positive integer parameter six-tuples, including all four phase types and the asymmetric worked example; this finite testing is supplementary only.

## Originality — PASS, priority provisional

Closest claim: the complete RESULT.md of *Hall-deficit quadratic law for minimum spheres of two-step Ferrers root polytopes* (online mathematical note, 2026). That note determines the single-biclique, one-cut case and explicitly limits itself to one deleted biclique. The present result requires *two* distinct nested Hall inequalities, a coupled off-diagonal Gram correction, four phases, and a simultaneous two-cut contact collapse. The published sources of Kálmán–Tóthmérész and Lazar explain root polytopes and Ferrers graphs, respectively, but the inspected theorems address Ehrhart/spanning trees or bond-lattice enumeration, not metric circumradii. Equivalent optimization formulations are a residual historical-priority risk. A failed search alone was not treated as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The first nontrivial multi-cut Ferrers family has an explicit, fully classified Euclidean sphere and a genuine nonlinear interaction of competing Hall deficits. The exact tangency classes locate which graph-root vertices determine the unique enclosing ball. It gives substantive metric content unavailable from the one-deficit formula and has reusable methods for related nested transportation configurations.

## Limitations and closest literature

Only the canonical unweighted Euclidean graph-root metric and three positive Ferrers neighborhood classes are covered; no extension to arbitrary graphs or higher-step Ferrers patterns is asserted. The exact checker is finite and does not replace the proof. Historical priority against unindexed general metric projection theorems is not independently certified. Lead references: arXiv:2203.17127, DOI:10.1007/s11139-022-00581-5, and the two-step Ferrers minimum-sphere online note.
