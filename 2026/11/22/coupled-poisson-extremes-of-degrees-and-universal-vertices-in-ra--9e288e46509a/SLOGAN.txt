In independent paired-endpoint random interval graphs, the entire \(\sqrt n\)-scaled low-degree spectrum converges to a two-sided, uniformly marked Poisson process of intensity \(d\,dd\), rather than merely yielding a Rayleigh law for the single smallest degree. Its two boundary minima also govern the universal-vertex count through an explicit conditional Poisson intensity, so the lowest degrees and the largest possible degrees remain positively coupled in the limit.
