# Review of the crepant birational dichotomy

## Correctness — PASS

The normal source quotients and their characteristic assignments are explicitly recorded in Chen–Zuo, Sections 5.1–5.3. For each source, the four-dimensional toric lattice and the canonical height functional are reconstructed in `RESULT.md`. All residue classes are covered by the coordinatewise fractional-part decomposition. The order-nine source has eight nontrivial residues, all of height two, so it is terminal; its simplicial cone makes it \(\mathbb Q\)-factorial. Terminality and the relative-ampleness argument rule out **all projective** crepant resolutions, not only toric ones. The order-eleven source has exactly one height-one interior ray; the four star cones have exact indices \(1,2,3,5\) and the three nonsmooth charts have terminal age profiles. The exceptional divisor is identified from its primitive ray relation. The finite age/volume checks were independently reproduced using the standalone exact-arithmetic checker. The stated scopes and the distinction between toric and arbitrary projective resolutions are essential to the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Chen–Zuo's November 2026 source explicitly gives the two isolated Gorenstein toric quotient models and normalized Nash nontermination, but not terminality, crepant ray spectra, the unique order-eleven extraction, its three residual singularities, or the order-nine projective-crepant obstruction. Dais–Henk–Ziegler (2005/2006) supplies general age/junior-simplex and Hilbert-basis methods, while Dais–Henk (1998) handles a different restricted repeated-weight series. These foundational criteria are acknowledged, rather than represented as new. Exact-parameter and equivalent-formulation searches in the accessible mathematical record did not show the joint dichotomy. Publication priority remains uncertain for unindexed toric computations or an unrecognized specialization of a broad classification.

## Value — PASS

Chen–Zuo's isolated nonterminating Nash examples make alternative birational modifications and their discrepancy constraints naturally important. The order-nine source is already terminal, so the failure is not caused by a nonterminal discrepancy defect; its projective crepant resolutions are impossible even outside toric geometry. The order-eleven source exhibits the opposite possibility: one crepant exceptional divisor, a unique toric terminal model, and three unavoidable terminal quotient points. This is an exact structural comparison of the two new examples, not an arbitrary cyclic-quotient type check or a recomputation of Nash charts. The result does not imply anything about non-toric crepant models in the order-eleven case.

## Closest literature and limitations

The comparison centered on Chen and Zuo, *Isolated Singularities Not Resolved by Iterated Nash Blowups*, arXiv:2611.19759v1, Sections 5.1–5.4; Dais, Henk and Ziegler, *On the Existence of Crepant Resolutions of Gorenstein Abelian Quotient Singularities in Dimensions at Least Four*, arXiv:math/0512619v2, Sections 3, 6 and 9; and Dais and Henk, *On a Series of Gorenstein Cyclic Quotient Singularities Admitting a Unique Projective Crepant Resolution*, arXiv:math/9803094, Section 8. Any stronger assertion of nonexistence or uniqueness of non-toric models for the order-eleven source is unproved. Likewise the order-nine no-resolution statement is explicitly limited to projective morphisms.
