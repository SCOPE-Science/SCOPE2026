In a published three-dimensional sine-delay map, a two-parameter stability boundary carries a supercritical pitchfork and the exact critical clock \(n z_n^2\to3(2+r+s)\) for every sufficiently small initial condition outside a two-dimensional fast-stable sheet. Across the boundary two attracting equilibria emerge at \(z_\pm=\pm\sqrt{6\varepsilon}+O(\varepsilon^{3/2})\), while exceptional critical orbits still decay exponentially.
