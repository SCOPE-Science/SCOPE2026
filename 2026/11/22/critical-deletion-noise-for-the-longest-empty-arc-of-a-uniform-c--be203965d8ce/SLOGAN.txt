For \(n\) independent uniform points on a circle, independently deleting points at probability \(\varepsilon_n\sim\tau/\log n\) leaves the longest original gap unchanged with limiting probability \(e^{-\tau}\). The centered original and retained longest gaps have a joint two-Poisson extreme law: new merged-gap extremes have independent tail intensity \((e^\tau-1)e^{-x}\), giving a sharp logarithmic robustness threshold.
