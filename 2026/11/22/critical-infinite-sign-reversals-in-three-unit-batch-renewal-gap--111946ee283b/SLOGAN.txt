For independent one-or-three arrivals under any finite-mean inverse-subordinator clock, adjacent individual-gap covariance switches from eventually negative to eventually positive precisely at equal burst weights; exactly at equality it reverses sign infinitely many times. Near that critical mixture, the last anomalous sign occurs at individual index \(\frac{2}{\log2}\log\frac1{|q-1/2|}+O(1)\), while bursts of maximum size two cannot show positive consecutive-gap covariance.
