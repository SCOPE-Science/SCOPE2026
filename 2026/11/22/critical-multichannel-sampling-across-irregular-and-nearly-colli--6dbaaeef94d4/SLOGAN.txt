For a positive diagonal operator whose eigenmodes form irregular clusters of size \(r\), one suitably weighted observation time per cluster and \(r\) fixed sensors give a uniformly invertible sampling map, even with arbitrarily close eigenvalues and independently changing sensor geometry. Explicit bounds certify both the original integer-orbit frame and the critical-count sampled Riesz basis, with \(N_T(R)=n_A(R)/r+O(1)\).
