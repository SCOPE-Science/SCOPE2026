Every postcritically finite unicritical polynomial \(z^d+c\) with \(d\ge3\) and \(c\) in a CM field preserves its exact critical-orbit preperiod and period modulo every nonzero proper ideal of \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(c)}\). In degree two the complete exceptional-ideal list is \((2),(4)\) for \(c=-2\) and \((1+i),(1+i)^2,(2c-1)\) for \(c=\pm i\), with explicitly determined intermediate portraits.
