At the critical mortality budget \(d\sqrt{\mu/D}=\log N+c\), all successful Brownian arrival times form a finite Poisson–Gaussian point cloud with sharp total-variation error of order \((\log N)^{-1/2}\). Conditional on a first arrival, its centered time mark and the normalized log-count of still-living searchers converge jointly to \((V_*,-V_*)\), exhibiting an exact limiting arrival–survivor anticorrelation.
