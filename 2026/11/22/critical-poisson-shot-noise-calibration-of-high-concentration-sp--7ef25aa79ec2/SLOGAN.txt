For spherical Poisson-kernel uniformity tests, the critical concentration window \(1-\rho\asymp n^{-2/q}\) has a non-Gaussian, jointly coupled Poisson shot-noise null law, rather than the fixed-tuning chi-squared limit. Its exact cross-bandwidth correlation is \(\operatorname{sech}^q(\tfrac12\log(c/d))\), and the limit itself crosses from rare-collision behavior to Gaussian fluctuations as the bandwidth scale increases.
