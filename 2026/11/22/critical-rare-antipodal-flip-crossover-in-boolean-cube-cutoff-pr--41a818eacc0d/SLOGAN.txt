A Poisson clock that rarely complements every bit of a high-dimensional Boolean cube creates a sharp \(1/(n\log n)\) crossover in the full total-variation cutoff profile. The resulting Gaussian likelihoods interpolate strictly between the ordinary tilt and the fully antipodal-symmetrized cosh law, with distinct late-window decay exponents, while the cutoff location remains \(\tfrac n4\log n\) for all complement rates.
