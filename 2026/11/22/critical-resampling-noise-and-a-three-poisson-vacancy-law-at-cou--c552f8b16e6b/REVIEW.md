# Review of the critical coupon-vacancy resampling theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** For a specified pattern of jointly and exclusively vacant boxes the displayed one-ball avoidance probability follows from the actual coupling kernel; inclusion-exclusion imposes the positive occupancy conditions exactly. Taking fixed mixed falling-factorial moments yields the independent-Poisson limits, including both endpoint regimes. Exact rational enumeration of eight finite parameter cases verifies 168 separate statements, and the covariance is reconstructed from equal- and distinct-box probabilities. The asymptotic conclusion rests on the analytic expansion, not finite tests.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual priority risk.** The closest inspected predecessor, Pósfai's 2010 thesis, treats a single coupon collector and its waiting time. O'Neill's 2023 complete text treats a single occupancy process and a different fall-through mechanism. Ilienko's 2026 paper treats repeated coupon layers and random completion times. The two-configuration *per-ball refresh* coupling is different from each, and the three independent vacancy categories with a sharp \(1/\log n\) transition are not their stated theorems. Published mathematical-record and further literature searches for resampled coupon collection, bivariate Poisson vacancies and noise sensitivity yielded related but non-dominating results. An unindexed general multivariate rare-occupancy theorem may imply this special case; such historical priority remains to be checked externally.

## Value
**PASS.** Near full occupancy, the coverage indicator has a nondegenerate limiting distribution; measuring how independent perturbations destroy or restore missing types is a natural stability question in sampling and coverage. The three-category joint law determines complete-coverage reliability and the overlap structure, distinguishes perfect stability from asymptotic independence, and supplies an explicit nontrivial interpolation at a physically meaningful noise scale. Its object and parameter are natural rather than an arbitrary fixed-dimensional finite slice.

## Limits and independent validation
The theorem assumes iid uniform boxes, fixed-index independent refresh, and a deterministic sample size in the critical window. Correlated nonuniform sampling, other perturbations, and quantitative rates are outside its scope. Searches cannot certify unique priority, and the result has not received independent mathematical audit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
