At the coupon-coverage threshold, independently refreshing each sampled ball has a sharp logarithmic noise scale: the boxes empty in both samples, only the original, and only the refreshed sample converge to three independent Poisson counts. The shared mean is \(e^{-c-\tau}\) when the refresh probability multiplied by \(\log n\) tends to \(\tau\), giving an explicit limiting probability of retaining complete coverage.
