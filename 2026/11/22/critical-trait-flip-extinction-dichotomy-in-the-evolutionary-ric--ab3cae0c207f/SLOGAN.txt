At zero intrinsic growth, every evolutionary Ricker trajectory with adaptation rate \(0<s<2\) has the same algebraic extinction clock \(1/x_n=n+(\beta+1/2)\log n+C+o(1)\). Exactly at the neutral trait flip \(s=2\), a nonempty critical initial-trait set retains that clock, while all other initial traits produce a persistent alternating phenotype and exponentially fast population extinction with an explicitly defined rate.
