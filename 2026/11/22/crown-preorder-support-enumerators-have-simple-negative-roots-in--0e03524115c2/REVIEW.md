# Review of the crown support-enumerator root theorem

## Correctness — PASS

The exact object is the preorder polytope of a height-two crown with \(m\ge3\), and the counting statistic is number of positive integer-point coordinates. Zhong's general disjoint-matchable-pairs theorem supplies the gamma expansion. Any nonempty disjoint matched pair must use bottom-to-top cover edges; deleting at least one vertex of a cycle leaves a forest with at most one perfect matching, whereas the entire cycle has exactly two. Consequently the support gamma-polynomial is the cycle matching polynomial minus one top-degree monomial. Counting edge matchings via a marked edge and using the two-state cyclic transfer matrix yields the closed formula. Chebyshev's \(m\) simple interior solutions to \(T_m(u)=1/2\) give distinct gamma roots less than \(-1/4\). The rational change of variables produces \(2m\) distinct real negative support roots, including verification that \(t=-1\) is not a root. All quantifiers and boundary cases are explicitly handled. Independent exact enumeration of matchable pairs and ideal-constrained lattice points corroborates the derivation.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The exact cycle matching polynomial and the matching interpretation of the gamma vector are established mathematical inputs, not new assertions. The closest articles cover preorder gamma-positivity, the real-rooted Ehrhart numerator of a **different** crown order polytope, and flag-polytopal realizations for **crown-free** preorders. These do not state or imply the identified gamma correction together with an all-order simple negative-root classification of the crown **preorder support polynomial**. A bounded search of mathematical records, literature, names, equivalent generating functions, root loci and related matroid/interior polynomials found no covering statement. The published 2026 crown flag-sphere release was reviewed via its detailed specialist summary but its underlying full text was not independently examined. Unindexed literature may contain equivalent results; no uniqueness or independent priority certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

Crowns are the minimal excluded cyclic-incidence family for a recently proved crown-free flag-realization result. This theorem determines the *complete* support enumerator and *all* complex roots of a canonical infinite family left outside that structural regime. It gives a sharply different outcome from the known counterexamples to universal preorder-support real-rootedness: these genuinely cyclic obstructions nevertheless have simple negative zeros. The exact \(q^m\) correction encodes the two-to-one failure at full perfect matchings and makes the result useful for comparing matching-induced gamma-positivity, root geometry and flag obstructions. It is a natural complete family classification, not a numerical experiment or a rephrasing of the general positivity theorem.
