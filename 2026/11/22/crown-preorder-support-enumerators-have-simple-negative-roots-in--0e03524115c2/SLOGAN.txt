For every crown poset on \(2m\ge6\) elements, the gamma-polynomial of its preorder-polytope support enumerator is exactly \(M_{C_{2m}}(q)-q^m\), the cycle matching polynomial with its duplicated full-matching witness removed. Its Chebyshev form gives all roots explicitly and proves that the support polynomial has exactly \(2m\) simple negative real roots, despite crowns lying outside the recent crown-free flag-sphere regime.
