For a fine arbitrary polygonal partition of the circle, the loss in perimeter-normalized mean boundary chord length from the optimal circle is governed by the exact cubic angular-gap energy \(\sum_j\Delta_j^3/(24\pi^3)\), with a mesh-uniform error. For regular \(n\)-gons, the next defect is a genuinely logarithmic term \(-\pi^2\log n/(90n^4)\), beyond the leading \(1/(3n^2)\).
