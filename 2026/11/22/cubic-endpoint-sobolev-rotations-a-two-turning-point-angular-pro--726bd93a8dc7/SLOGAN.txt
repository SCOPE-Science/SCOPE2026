For the order-three real planar endpoint Sobolev derivative-sum norm, rotation distortion has a sharp two-branch profile with an interior smooth local maximum followed by a cusp minimum; the unique global maximizing angles, up to quarter-turns, are diagonal rotations with exact norm \(2\sqrt2\). This reverses the quadratic-jet location of the global maximum, using Ornstein's established non-inequality to prove sharpness in the full Sobolev space.
