At the excluded double critical point of a discrete harvested predator–prey model, every orbit in an explicit-type positive wedge has predator extinction \(y_n=(2An)^{-1/2}-\gamma(\gamma-1)n^{-1}/[2\delta m(\gamma+1)]+O(\log n/n^{3/2})\); the second term reverses sign at \(\gamma=1\). At this resonance, \(y_n^{-2}-4\delta mn-(1+m+3\delta m)\log n\) converges, with a step-dependent universal logarithmic coefficient.
