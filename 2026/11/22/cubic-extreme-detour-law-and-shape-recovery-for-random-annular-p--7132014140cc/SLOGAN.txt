For independent area-uniform points in every fixed proper Euclidean annulus, the intrinsic-to-chord stretch reaches the sharp maximum \(\pi/2\) only at antipodal inner-rim pairs, and its near-maximum probability equals \(64r^4\varepsilon^3/[3\pi^3(R^2-r^2)^2]+O(\varepsilon^{7/2})\). The single scale-free coefficient reconstructs the hole-to-outer-radius ratio, and the three rescaled geometric defects converge to a uniform simplex law.
