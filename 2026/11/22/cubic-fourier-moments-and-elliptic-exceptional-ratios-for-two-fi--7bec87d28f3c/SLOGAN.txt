For two distinct nonzero quadratic hyperbola shells in an odd finite-field plane, the three-step additive return count is exactly \((q-1)(8+2\chi(-3)+3\chi(1-4\lambda)+3\chi(\lambda(\lambda-4)))\), and every nonzero target is reached. The origin is missing exactly for three simultaneous nonsquare conditions; the number of exceptional shell ratios is determined by an elliptic Frobenius trace, with a singular characteristic-five transition and a sharp existence threshold \(q>5\) when \(q\equiv2\pmod3\).
