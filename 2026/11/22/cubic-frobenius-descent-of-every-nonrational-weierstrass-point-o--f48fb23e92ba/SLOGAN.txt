For the first third-largest-genus maximal curve with nonrational Weierstrass points, \(y^{12}+x^8+x^4=0\) over \(\mathbb F_{121}\), all \(288\) exceptional points become rational in *exactly* the cubic extension and form \(96\) degree-three Frobenius orbits. The reduced Weierstrass support has zeta function \(1/((1-T)^{540}(1-T^3)^{96})\).
