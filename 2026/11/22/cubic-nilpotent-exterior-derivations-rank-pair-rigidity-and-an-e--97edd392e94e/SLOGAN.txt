For a characteristic-three exterior algebra \(\bigwedge k^n\), the full Jordan partition of every degree-preserving cubic-zero derivation is determined explicitly by the \(1\)-, \(2\)-, and \(3\)-block multiplicities of its generator action. Remarkably, just the pair \((\operatorname{rank}D,\operatorname{rank}D^2)\) uniquely recovers that generator Jordan partition, and the exact joint rank distribution over every \(\mathbb F_{3^e}\) follows from one nilpotent-orbit formula.
