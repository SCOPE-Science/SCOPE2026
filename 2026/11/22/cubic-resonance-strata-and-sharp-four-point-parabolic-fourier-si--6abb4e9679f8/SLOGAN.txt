Four-point subsets of a finite-field parabola in characteristic at least seven have precisely two isolated cubic-resonance geometries, with exact counts. The unbalanced cube-versus-three collision, present exactly when \(3\mid(q-1)\), has a new sharp normalized Fourier sixth-power constant \(4.203233399468414\ldots\) and unique center-to-arm magnitude ratio, governed by a quartic; the balanced collision constant was known previously.
