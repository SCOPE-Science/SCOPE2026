In the fixed-point-free cosine–memristor planar map, a vanishing cubic memristance makes every nonresonant target acquire \(\frac1\pi[\operatorname{cbrt}(u/\alpha+1)-\operatorname{cbrt}(u/\alpha-1)](\alpha/\beta)^{1/3}+O(1)\) distinct real inverse images. Rescaled inverse images become uniformly distributed on an explicit anti-diagonal segment even though each nonzero-coupling map is proper and has topological degree one, while the zero-coupling limit has infinite or empty fibers.
