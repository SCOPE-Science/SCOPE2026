In characteristic two, the first Nottingham substitution quotient whose squares fail to fill their generated subgroup occurs at degree five: only \((2q^2+1)/3\) of the \(q^2\) central targets are squares. Every prescribed target has exactly \(0\), \(q^2\), \(3q^2\), or \(q^3\) square roots, with an exact cubic-splitting census over all finite fields of characteristic two.
