# Scientific assessment

## Correctness

The two explicit residual multipliers on each eigenspace give the rational squared gain in `RESULT.md`. Under the rescaling \(z=K^3t\), the high-mode contribution divided by \(K\) is bounded by \(z/(1+z)^2\) and tends to that value at each fixed \(z\). The low-mode contribution divided by \(K\) is at most \(1/K\). This proves the limit and localization of every maximizing sequence without interchanging an uncontrolled pointwise limit and a supremum. The delayed first-step reuse is the exact two-step Cauchy cycle on the dual quadratic; its displayed comparison follows from the cited cycle formula. The frozen finite checker is supplementary.

## Originality

The repeated-Cauchy gain is covered by the prior cyclic steepest-descent result (Finding and Proof §§2, 5); it is not claimed as new. The inspected primary source and same-rule long-run results provide the algorithm and spectral multipliers, but do not optimize the second physical residual uniformly over starting energies in the high-condition limit. The residual-difference claim is distinct from those results. Priority remains qualified by the available literature.

## Value

The sharp second-step gain and the location of its maximizing starts quantify a transient not captured by eventual convergence or by an ordering of step lengths. The comparison with the repeated-Cauchy benchmark demonstrates a difference in conditioning exponents without asserting monotonicity, typical behavior, or better long-run performance.
