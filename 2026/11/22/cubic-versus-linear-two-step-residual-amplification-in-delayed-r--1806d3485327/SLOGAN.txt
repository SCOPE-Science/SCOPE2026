Under the same prescribed warm start on a consistent two-mode linear system, delayed-residual and residual-difference gradient steps have sharply different worst-case two-update residual blow-up: respectively \((3\sqrt3/16+o(1))K^{3/2}\) and \((1/2+o(1))K^{1/2}\), where \(K\) is the normal-matrix condition number. Their maximizing initial high-mode energy fractions also live on different scales, \(K^{-1}\) and \(K^{-3}\).
