After a residual Rayleigh warm start on a two-mode system, the worst two-update residual norm under the residual-difference rule grows as (1/2+o(1))√K, with every maximizing high-to-low squared residual ratio asymptotic to K⁻³. The known repeated-Cauchy formula gives a larger benchmark of order K³ᐟ² under the same start, where K is the normal-matrix condition number.
