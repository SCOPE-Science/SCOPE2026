For the 1344 undirected Hamiltonian cycles of the four-dimensional cube, every one-edge Walsh character vanishes, every two-edge character survives, and exactly 1120 three-edge characters vanish in six geometric symmetry orbits. Only 64 of those 1120 cubic cancellations follow from the singleton zeros by the smallest edge-cut symmetries; the other 1056 encode additional signed three-edge structure.
