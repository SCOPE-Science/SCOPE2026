For the six-vertex (two-in/two-out) orientations of the cuboctahedral graph, exactly \(9{,}500{,}672\) of \(2^{24}\) binary Walsh frequencies vanish; the first even-support cancellations occur at weight four, in \(21\) symmetry orbits. The recoverable convolution has condition number \(150\) and exact minimum \(\ell^1\)/\(\ell^\infty\) projection norm \(29471/2048\).
