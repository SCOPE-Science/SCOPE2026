In the integral reduced Świątkowski model, each weight-three degree-three generator uses one of three local half-edge differences at each of three distinct vertices. Removing one factor replaces it by the incident-edge difference with the Leibniz sign. There is no weight-three degree-four group. A fresh exact reconstruction gives 5940 top columns, 14256 possible rows of which 14040 are used, and 5916 unit pivots. Each original pivot column is reduced using only earlier pivot columns, so all nonpivot columns are integral combinations of the original pivot columns; the kernel projects isomorphically to Z^24 on nonpivot coordinates. The local oriented half-edge cycle is the standard one-particle class. On the disjoint union of the three simple cycles its tensor product is the product fundamental class; inclusion into G gives the embedded torus class with the tested ordering signs. Exhaustive cycle and disjointness tests recovered 1422 simple cycles and 60 triples, of types (3,3,3):8, (3,3,4):12, (3,3,5):24, (3,3,6):16. All 60 signed products are closed. The exact 24-class determinant −1 gives an integral basis. The 20 classes of length cutoff four have a unit 20-minor; the 44 classes of cutoff five have rational rank 20, so primitivity implies they span the identical integral lattice. The quotient is therefore Z^4, and the length-six bound is sufficient and sharp.
