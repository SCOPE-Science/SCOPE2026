In a complete network of \(n\) KMP energy sites, each directly exposed to two different reservoirs, strengthening internal energy-redistribution links leaves every mean heat current unchanged but generates the exact positive distinct-site covariance \(v(T_+-T_-)^2/[2((3n+1)v+12)]\). Its sharp size-dependent onset is \(v\asymp1/n\), despite zero mean current along each added internal link.
