In two-component stochastic extragradient, an interior predictor–corrector sample correlation uniquely minimizes stationary root squared error for an explicit pair of strongly monotone affine operators, outperforming reuse, independence, and antithetic pairing. Equal component slopes instead force unique optimality of full reuse throughout \(0<\eta a<1\) when component roots differ, so the interior optimum is a structural curvature-heterogeneity effect.
