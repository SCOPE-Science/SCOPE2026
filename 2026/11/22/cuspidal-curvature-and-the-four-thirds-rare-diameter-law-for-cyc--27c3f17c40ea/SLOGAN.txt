For a one-arch cycloid, two independent arclength-uniform points form an almost diametral chord with sharp probability \(0.05830090078\ldots(\varepsilon/r)^{4/3}\); the rescaled endpoint deficits have a \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(2/3,2/3,1)\) limit. More generally, the \(4/3\) exponent and exact leading coefficient are determined by the two inward-facing \(3/2\)-cusps' Euclidean cuspidal curvatures and the arc length, provided the cusps form the unique diametral pair.
