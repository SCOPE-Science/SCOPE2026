For the simple-cycle edge-membership class of the two-row ladder \(P_2\square P_n\), all but exactly \(n+3\) physical-edge pairs are VC-shattered when \(n\ge4\), yet no triple is shattered; the complete polynomial is \(1+(3n-2)z+n(9n-17)z^2/2\). Its online Littlestone dimension grows logarithmically, equals \(2t-2\) at every \(n=2^t\) with \(t\ge2\), and first strictly exceeds VC dimension at five columns.
