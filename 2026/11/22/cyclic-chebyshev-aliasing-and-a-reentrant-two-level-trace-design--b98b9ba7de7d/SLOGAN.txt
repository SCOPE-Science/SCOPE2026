Chebyshev sign-probe errors on a cycle have an exact alias-only covariance law: distinct degrees below the cycle length correlate only when their degrees sum to that length. In the one-sided-recurrence, continuously allocated two-level trace model on the four-cycle, a geometric Chebyshev polynomial has two sharp cutoff switches at \(r=0.2371807138\ldots\) and \(r=0.8095480402\ldots\), so the cheaper cutoff becomes optimal again as \(r\) approaches one.
