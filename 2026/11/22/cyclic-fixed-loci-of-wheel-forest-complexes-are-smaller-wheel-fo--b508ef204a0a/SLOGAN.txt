For every rotation of a wheel with \(n\) rim vertices, its geometric fixed forest complex is exactly the forest complex of the smaller wheel with \(d=\gcd(n,k)\) rim vertices (allowing the natural small multigraph cases). Thus a nontrivial rotation fixes either a point or a wedge of \(2^d-2\) spheres \(S^{d-1}\), and every nonidentity rotation has contractible fixed space exactly when \(n\) is prime.
