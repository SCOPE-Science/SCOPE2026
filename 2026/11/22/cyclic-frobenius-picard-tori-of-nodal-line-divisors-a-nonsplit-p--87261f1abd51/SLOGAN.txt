For a general-position union of \(n\) projective lines forming one finite-field Frobenius orbit, the connected Picard torus has a complete integral cyclic-module classification. Odd \(n\) gives split regular-plus-fixed summands, while even \(n\) contains a nonsplit parity-glued summand whose rational-point group is cyclic of order \((q-1)(q^{n/2}+1)\); explicit zeta functions hold in every characteristic.
