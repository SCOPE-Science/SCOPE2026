For cyclic relabeling of the two-subset Kneser independence complex at every width \(n\ge5\), order-two and order-three stabilizers reduce the quotient fundamental group to \(C_{n/\gcd(n,6)}\), while its second integral homology is free of rank \((n^2-6n+5+3\epsilon_2(n)+4\epsilon_3(n))/6\). At the first singular width, the ordinary sixfold quotient has the complete homotopy type \(S^2\vee S^2\).
