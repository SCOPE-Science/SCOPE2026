In a cyclic three-species contest model, the critical fraction of three-way encounters has a variance-controlled quadratic term and a **signed cubic correction** \(\varepsilon^3(a_1-a_2)(a_2-a_3)(a_3-a_1)/(2p-1)^3\). Thus assigning the same three mortality rates to different positions in the competitive cycle shifts the exact near-neutral local-stability threshold, although every symmetric demographic statistic is unchanged.
