# Review

**Correctness — PASS.** The canonical-curve reduction to the unique nonsplit containing quadric is standard and the two models are verified geometrically smooth by exact Jacobian-minor ideals. The complete rational orthogonal-group enumeration consists of 120 and 1440 matrices, and explicit cubic coefficient identities admit exactly five transformations each. The displayed fifth-order matrices, irreducible cyclotomic characteristic polynomial, Riemann--Hurwitz, and finite-field genus-zero splitting prove the branch statement. A standalone exact verifier checks the finite certificates. This is not a sample-based inference.

**Originality — PASS, with stated priority risk.** The inspected original Faber--Grantham papers provide the equations and gonality, and the later Faber--Grantham--Howe study establishes the exceptional list. These do not state the pair of full arithmetic automorphism groups or the unique degree-four cyclic-quotient branch orbit. Searches using the equations, automorphism aliases, cyclic covers and Frobenius branch descriptions found no dominating published theorem. Potential earlier data tables of automorphism orders remain an unexcluded risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** These two and only two finite-field genus-four curves of gonality five form a canonical extremal classification problem. Knowing their full base-field automorphism groups and the arithmetic branch scheme gives a natural structural explanation of a shared rare symmetry, and a complete non-arbitrary invariant beyond their known absence of low-degree points. The classification does not assert a larger theorem for all curves or geometric automorphism groups.
