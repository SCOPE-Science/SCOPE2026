The fourteen nodes of Brock's Fricke–Macbeath plane octic form arithmetic closed points of degrees \(2,6,6\), with exact common splitting field \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)\). Every node has two separately rational normalization branches in each characteristic other than \(2\) and \(7\); four explicit Frobenius cycle types determined by \(q\bmod7\) give the complete zeta-function correction from the smooth genus-seven normalization to the singular octic.
