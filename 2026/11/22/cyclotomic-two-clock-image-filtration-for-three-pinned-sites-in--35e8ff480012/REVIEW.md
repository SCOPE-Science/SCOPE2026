# Scientific review: Cyclotomic two-clock image filtration for three pinned sites in Rule 150

## Correctness

**PASS.** For \(F\) with Laurent multiplier \(P=1+z+z^2\), surjectivity on the full bilateral shift follows from injectivity of multiplication by \(P\) on finite characters. The annihilator of the time-\(n\) image of a three-site zero-pattern cylinder is the space of support-three polynomial multiples of \(P^n\). Its dimension is the exact binary logarithm of the image subgroup's index, and prescribed bits merely translate that subgroup. The nonzero sparse polynomials comprise three monomials, three binomials, and one trinomial. Cyclotomic root evaluation and Frobenius factoring give each exact valuation; independence of eligible pairs then gives the complete index filtration. The sharp diameter law has a proved universal upper bound and a Frobenius-power equality witness. Exact polynomial tests for 8,910 triples, 3,002 independently constructed image-matrix rank checks, pair spacing checks through 80, and sharp-span tests through 120 succeeded. These finite tests are corroborations, not the proof.

## Originality

**PASS within inspected evidence.** Castronuovo–Dennunzio–Margara (first released 22 January 2026) proves decidability and structural criteria for qualitative topological transitivity of group cellular automata; it does not provide exact finite-time indices of sparse input-cylinder images. The 2019 additive-automata paper establishes structural properties of strong transitivity but not the stated three-site index and two-clock law. Martin–Odlyzko–Wolfram (1984) develops the underlying polynomial algebra for principally finite periodic rings, but no corresponding arbitrary-bilateral-target sparse-cylinder theorem was located in the material inspected. The closest published Rule 150 record concerns the number of *one-step predecessor necklaces on finite cyclic rings*, not the time-indexed image of arbitrary three-site cylinders of the infinite rule. Previously studied two-neighbor additive maps have linear rather than irreducible-quadratic Laurent multipliers, so their dyadic collision tests do not imply these modulo-three, three-body parity obstructions. A recent related full text posted 17 November 2026 supplies further qualitative duality characterizations, but no index-clock formula in the sections checked. Searches are not a proof of absolute priority, and possible equivalents in historical coding-theoretic literature remain a risk.

## Value

**PASS.** The result gives a complete all-time and all-positions classification of finite-index constraints left on every possible output configuration by pinning exactly three input sites. Three is the first arity that can produce a genuinely joint, modulo-three obstruction when every pair is unrestricted after one step; the all-same-residue case features two successive codimension-loss times. The sharp span cutoff quantifies the worst control delay and supplies explicit extremizers. These are intrinsic observability and exact-controllability features, rather than an arbitrary finite-parameter census or a routine reformulation of qualitative strong transitivity.
