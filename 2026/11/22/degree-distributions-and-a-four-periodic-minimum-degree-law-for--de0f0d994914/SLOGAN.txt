For \(111\)-avoiding binary words under single-bit flips, a 13-state rational transfer describes every degree multiplicity and the minimum degree is exactly \(\lceil n/2\rceil+\mathbf1_{\{n\equiv2\pmod4\}}\). In every length divisible by four the unique least-mobile word is a repeated `0110` pattern; the full-degree population has a closed binomial sum.
