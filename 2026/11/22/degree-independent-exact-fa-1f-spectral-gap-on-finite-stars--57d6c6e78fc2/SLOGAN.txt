For the one-spin-facilitated heat-bath model on every star with at least two leaves, the restricted stationary spectral gap is exactly \(1-\sqrt{1-q}\), independently of hub degree, and the slow eigenspace consists of \(n-1\) leaf-contrast modes. One leaf is the sharp exception, with gap \(q\); this is an exact relaxation-rate statement, not a worst-state total-variation mixing theorem.
