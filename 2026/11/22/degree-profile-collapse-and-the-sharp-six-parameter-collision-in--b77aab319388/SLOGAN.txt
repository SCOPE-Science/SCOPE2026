Over tripartite parameters of the homogeneous \(K_4\)-free graph, deleting a four-cycle-free bipartite edge pattern makes the complete graded singleton-type spectrum depend *exactly* on the missing-edge degree multiset; a deleted four-cycle is the first obstruction, first visible in degree five. Nonisomorphic connected six-parameter bases first collide at shore sizes \((2,3,1)\), and the same collision persists at every larger order despite a fixed gap of six in the third moment of the parameter-graph degrees.
