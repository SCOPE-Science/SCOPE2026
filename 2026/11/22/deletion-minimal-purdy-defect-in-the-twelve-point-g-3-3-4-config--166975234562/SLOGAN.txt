Among all \(4096\) subsets of the twelve-point \(G(3,3,4)\) Purdy counterexample, the only negative-defect configurations are the full essential set and four nonessential six-point coordinate-plane sets. Thus the full counterexample is deletion-minimal among essential subarrangements, and deleting any single hyperplane changes its defect from \(-1\) to \(6\).
