For the Catalan matroid base polytope, every vertex degree equals \(\binom n2+\sum_i\binom{s_i}{2}\), where \(s_i\) are the sizes of its depth-two Dyck-forest subtrees. This yields a complete Catalan generating function for the vertex-degree spectrum, and the sharp minimum \(\binom n2\) and maximum \((n-1)^2\) (for \(n\ge3\)), with exactly \(2^{n-1}\) and \(C_{n-2}\) extremal vertices, respectively.
