Deleting precisely the edges between diagonal grid vertices prescribed by any graph \(H\) from the categorical product \(K_n\times K_n\) produces an independence complex that simplicially collapses onto \(\operatorname{Cl}(H)\) with two added subdivided edges for every nonedge of \(H\). This preserves all higher integral homology (including torsion) of the arbitrary flag complex, adding only an explicitly counted bouquet of circles.
