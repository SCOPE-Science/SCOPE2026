For every fixed connected positively weighted graph of diameter \(D\ge2\), fractional graph heat with exponent \(1+\varepsilon\) has exactly one negative-to-positive crossing for each nonedge when \(\varepsilon>0\) is small. Its sharp all-entry positivity-recovery time has order \(\varepsilon^{1/(D-1)}\), with an explicit leading constant determined by weighted shortest paths and the off-diagonal entries of \(L\log L\); on the four-vertex path the constant is \(\sqrt{3\log 2/2}\).
