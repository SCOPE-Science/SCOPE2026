For every multiplicity-five unimodal parametrized space-curve germ, the valuations of pulled-back Kähler differentials have at most one value absent from the curve semigroup: \(13\). For the family \((t^5,t^6+t^7+b t^8,t^9)\), that value disappears exactly at \(b=13/12\), producing a sharp one-unit jump in differential colength.
