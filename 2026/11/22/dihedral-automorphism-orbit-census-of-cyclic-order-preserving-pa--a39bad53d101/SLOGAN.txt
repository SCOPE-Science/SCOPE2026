For every \(n\ge3\), the complete domain-rank distribution of elements of the cyclic-order-preserving inverse monoid \(\mathcal{POPI}_n\) up to **all algebra automorphisms** has an exact dihedral Burnside polynomial and an explicit divisor-sum total. In particular, the rank-\(n-1\) stratum has precisely \(\binom n2+1\) orbits, and the total is asymptotic to \(\frac14\binom{2n}n\).
