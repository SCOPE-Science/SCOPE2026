# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For the distance algebra of the undirected cycle, standard orthogonal-idempotent normalization reduces all derivations modulo inner derivations to scalar geodesic cocycles on basis elements. The only possible independent relations arise from antipodal double geodesics on even cycles. Odd cycles have no such relation: after quotienting the \(n-1\)-dimensional vertex-gradient space, the reciprocal-edge-sum map has a one-dimensional reflection-sign kernel. Even cycles impose exactly \(m+1\) independent relations on \(2n\) directed edge scalars, and their reciprocal-edge sums give an injective and surjective map onto \(m\) antipodal edge-pair values. The even/odd dimensions and dihedral actions follow in all characteristics. The explicit reflection-fixed edge in characteristic two yields a nonzero circulation class for every lift and obstructs equivariant splitting. These are all-parameter arguments, with an exact finite checker as a separate consistency test.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Checked the full accessible 2026 studies of magnitude homology and magnitude cohomology for arrangement tope graphs, including their rank-two cycle examples. Those compute other invariants and do not give first Hochschild derivations of geodesic-composition algebras. The original distance-algebra Tor/Ext work defines the underlying object without the claimed dihedral calculation. Cibils–Redondo–Solotar's general Schurian Hurewicz result already broadly describes first Hochschild cohomology and should not be claimed as new; it does not supply the explicit odd/even dihedral modules or the characteristic-two non-split extension. Xu's earlier special-biserial work may account for nonequivariant dimension formulas but only its abstract could be inspected, so priority for dimensions remains uncertain. None of the searched records supplied a covering equivariant result. This is a best-knowledge originality assessment, not a proof that no equivalent older theorem exists.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The recent literature uses even cycle tope graphs as a natural testing family for characteristic-two algebraic graph invariants. The theorem determines a outer-derivation invariant of their intrinsic shortest-path algebra for all cycle lengths, not a synthetic small example. The parity jump from an antipodal-edge permutation module to an extension of all edge values, and the characteristic-two failure of dihedral splitting, are structural phenomena invisible to dimensions or non-equivariant Hurewicz alone. The result also supplies a transparent comparison with odd cycles, which cannot be tope graphs of a central real line arrangement.
