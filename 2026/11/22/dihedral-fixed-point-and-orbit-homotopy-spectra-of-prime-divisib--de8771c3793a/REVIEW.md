# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The underlying order complex is exactly the incidence graph between partitions of shapes \((p,p,p)\) and \((p,2p)\). The proof supplies complete upper/lower/edge counts and a connectivity argument. No edge inversions are possible because the two partition ranks are preserved. All subgroup types reduce to the four divisors of \(3p\) with an optional reflection, and the proof explicitly counts the fixed vertices and fixed incidences in each type, including the otherwise subtle isolated order-three phase assignments. Burnside and graph Euler characteristics then give a closed integral quotient-rank expression. A separate complete \(p=5\) enumeration checks all eight subgroup classes, not an infinite numerical extrapolation. Risks: a typing or counting error in a symbolic case would change the all-prime classification; the proof's phase-normalization argument and exceptional prime threshold are essential.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
Hanlon's unrestricted fixed-partition lattice gives substantial antecedent fixed-block combinatorics and may imply some individual counts; it does not state the \(p\)-divisible rank-two fixed graphs, their subgroup-by-subgroup connectivity, or quotient circle ranks. Wachs computes the divisible partition complex's ordinary homology and \(S_{3p}\) homology representation: it covers nonequivariant properties and can imply the rational Betti counts of quotients, so these ranks are **not** presented as independently novel. The combined classification of actual subgroup geometric fixed graphs and ordinary homotopy types is the primary new assertion, rather than a rational-character corollary. Woodroofe's convex-ear decomposition is likewise structural background. The unrestricted partition-complex fixed-space theorem of Arone–Brantner is not a stronger claim about the restricted \(p\)-divisible subposet. Direct public-result and prior-claim searches did not identify the exact statement. Residual risk: an unindexed or unpublished equivariant divisible-partition treatment could contain the theorem; the publicly indexed partial-partition finding was available as a summary, not full text.

## Value — PASS
The classification gives a natural all-subgroup topological invariant at the first nontrivial rank of an important partition lattice, including genuinely different disconnected geometric fixed spaces and explicit homotopy types for every orbit quotient. It is not merely a single finite table or a routine restatement of character theory; complete reflection-component and order-three fixed-point counts are indispensable to equivariant constructions. The restriction to prime divisibility is mathematically explained by the block-permutation action, and the excluded small prime has a different stabilizer pattern. Larger divisor parameters remain open to separate investigation.
