For every prime \(p\ge5\), every dihedral subgroup of the \(3p\)-gon has a completely classified geometric fixed space on the proper \(p\)-divisible partition complex, and every ordinary orbit space is an explicit wedge of circles. Reflections give disjoint stars, order-three rotations give exponentially many isolated points, and full rotational and dihedral quotients at \(p=5\) have circle ranks \(16606\) and \(8293\).
