# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The chain-level orbit-face sign formula is derived from permutation parity, not inferred from a fixed-point count. The rotation graph is exactly a smaller generalized Petersen graph except for four explicitly checked boundary values. The three reflection quotient graphs are independently derived by folding both edge layers, and the integer transfer matrix obeys the decisive \(T^4(T^5+I)=0\) identity. Exact finite seed evaluations close the periodicity proof. The nonequivariant homology concentration and degrees are credited to Kim, Theorem 4.6; the remaining argument determines the actual rational homology character. The bundled verifier reconstructs quotient graphs, verifies the matrix identity, checks all seeds and further values, and corroborates quotient character averages. The tests are supporting checks, not a substitute for the infinite proof.

**Originality (PASS, with nontrivial priority risk).** The earlier independence-polynomial transfer work of Pandey does not give symmetry characters; Kim's all-parameter result is nonequivariant. Published calculations for the cases \(n=7\) and \(n=9\) are special cases and are excluded from an independent novelty claim. A closely related published theorem gives exactly the same character for a *different* complex on cyclic words avoiding four runs. This exact coincidence is acknowledged; its statement does not establish any group-equivariant map to the two-layer generalized Petersen complex, and the present computation derives every character directly from that graph. This is a meaningful risk: an undiscovered canonical equivariant reduction could subsume the calculation, and such a reduction has not been ruled out.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The theorem fully determines the canonical symmetry action on the homology of an infinite family of classical cubic graphs, beyond the known sphere-wedge ranks. It identifies the exact mod-twenty regime in which ordinary full-symmetry quotients retain rational topology and proves an unexpected representation coincidence between two distinct natural simplicial-complex constructions. Character and quotient data distinguish actions invisible to ordinary Betti numbers, while the finite transfer identity makes the infinite-family result reproducible.

**Limitations.** Only rational equivariant homology for the canonical dihedral action and additive rational quotient cohomology are determined. Integral quotient torsion, exceptional larger graph automorphism groups, and equivariant homotopy equivalence are not determined. Exhaustive priority and independent mathematical verification remain outstanding.
