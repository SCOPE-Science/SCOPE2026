The independence complex of every generalized Petersen graph \(\operatorname{GP}(n,2)\) has an explicitly calculated rational dihedral homology character, including all rotations and both reflection types. Its geometric dihedral quotient retains a reduced rational class exactly when \(n\equiv1,7,13,19\pmod{20}\); unexpectedly, the complete representation agrees with that of a different no-four-consecutive cyclic complex.
