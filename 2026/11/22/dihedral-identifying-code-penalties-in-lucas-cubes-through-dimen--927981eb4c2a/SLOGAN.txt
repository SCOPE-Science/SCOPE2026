Full coordinate-dihedral invariance first costs an extra identifying sensor in the sixth Lucas cube, and costs three in the seventh: \(I_6=7<I_6^{D}=8\) and \(I_7=11<I_7^{D}=14\). The exact finite census through dimension seven includes 259 unrestricted optimal eleven-sensor codes in 23 symmetry classes, but only three invariant optimal fourteen-sensor codes in dimension seven.
