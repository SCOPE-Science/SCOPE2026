For the outer dihedral symmetry of a pentagonal lexicographic graph substitution, the actual independence-complex orbit space is the factor-swap quotient of a twofold join. Substituting \((d+1)K_2\) for \(d\ge2\) produces \(S^d*\mathbb{RP}^d\), with predictable integral two-torsion even though the unquotiented complex has torsion-free homology.
