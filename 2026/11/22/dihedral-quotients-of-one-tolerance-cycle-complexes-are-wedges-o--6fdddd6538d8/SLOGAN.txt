For every unfilled cycle \(C_n\) with \(n\ge4\), the genuine geometric quotient of its one-tolerance complex by all dihedral symmetries is exactly a wedge of \(\lfloor(n-4)/2\rfloor\) two-spheres up to homotopy. The result proves integral torsion-freeness and simple connectivity of the quotients, rather than merely averaging Betti numbers.
