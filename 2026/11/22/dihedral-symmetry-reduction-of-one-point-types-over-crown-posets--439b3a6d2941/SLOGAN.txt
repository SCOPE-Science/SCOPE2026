The dihedral symmetry classes of nonalgebraic one-point extensions of the \(2n\)-point crown are counted exactly by \(2+F_{n+3}+n^{-1}\sum_{d\mid n}\varphi(n/d)L_{2d}\) for every \(n\ge3\). Three classes are genuinely two-sided, whereas nearly all individual types have trivial crown-automorphism stabilizer as \(n\to\infty\).
