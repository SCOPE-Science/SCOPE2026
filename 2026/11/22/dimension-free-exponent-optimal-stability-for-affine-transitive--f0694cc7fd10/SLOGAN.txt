For every finite abelian affine-transitive support, a nearly optimal Fourier synthesis ratio forces the signal to be close to a scalar-modulated support indicator in normalized Fourier \(\ell^r\), with a bound independent of the ambient modulus. The deficit exponents \(1/2\) below and at \(r=2\), and \(1/r\) above \(r=2\), are universally best possible.
