For a globally Lipschitz triangular map whose base limit is periodic, every cyclic \(\omega\)-limit fibre has the same Hausdorff, packing and lower/upper box dimensions. This restriction is sharp at every \(d\in[0,1]\): a single map can realize all fibres at dimension \(d\), and for \(0<d\le1\) their positive finite \(d\)-dimensional Hausdorff measures can have arbitrary prescribed ratios; for \(d<1\) the restricted dynamics can even be a minimal zero-entropy adding machine.
