On every two-level positive-definite spectrum, one-step full-size global Lanczos--Hermite inverse-trace correction has an exact Rademacher-to-ordinary-Hutchinson variance ratio \((1-ab/\mu^2)^2\), independent of the probe budget and eigenvector orientation whenever that ratio is defined. Correction can first increase variance in dimension seven (sharply by \(625/576\) in a seven-dimensional example), yet at another explicit spike contrast it eliminates all sign-probe randomness.
