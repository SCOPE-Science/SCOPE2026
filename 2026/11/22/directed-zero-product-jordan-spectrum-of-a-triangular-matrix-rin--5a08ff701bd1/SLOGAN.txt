For the loopless **directed** zero-divisor graph of \(\operatorname{UT}_2(\mathbb F_q)\), the entire complex adjacency Jordan form consists of exactly \(q-1\) length-two zero blocks, \(2q(q-2)\) simple zero blocks, \(q-2\) simple \(-1\) blocks, and three simple roots of an explicit cubic. Thus a graph on \((2q+1)(q-1)\) vertices has adjacency rank only \(2q\), and every matrix power from the second onward has rank exactly \(q+1\).
