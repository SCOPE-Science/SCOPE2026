Equally spaced circular Poisson mixtures have a unique uniform one-Poisson fit under \(D(\text{mixture}\Vert\text{candidate})\), but the reverse KL orientation undergoes a sharp symmetry-breaking transition. Two components split continuously; every number \(m\ge3\) splits discontinuously into exactly \(m\) optimal mode-aligned kernels at an explicit critical radius with \(1-r_c(m)\sim1/(2m^2)\).
