The quadratic Bernstein–Durrmeyer Markov operator has a disconnected set of positivity-preserving spectral-power times: exactly \([\tau_-,1]\cup[\tau_+,\infty)\), with \(\tau_-=0.8912898099\ldots\) and \(\tau_+=1.1309462648\ldots\). A real spectral power can therefore lose Markov positivity immediately *after* the positive integer iterate and later regain it; degree one cannot exhibit this reentry.
