Coring-volume equality at the two dilations \(2\) and \(3\), for every common hole height, characterizes spheroids among continuous centrally symmetric solids of revolution. Commensurate scales such as \(2\) and \(4\) admit strictly convex non-spheroidal \(C^{1,1}\) counterexamples; alternatively, one scale is decisive when the squared profile has a second-order equatorial Taylor expansion.
