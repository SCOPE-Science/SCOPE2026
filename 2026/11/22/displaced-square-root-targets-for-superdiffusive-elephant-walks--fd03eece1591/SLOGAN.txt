For a superdiffusive elephant walk, a frozen lattice point displaced by \(c\sqrt n\) has a last-visit tail whose sharp coefficient is the expected range of Brownian motion against the nonlinear boundary \(c(1-\delta u)^{a/\delta}\). This coefficient is even, strictly increasing with displacement magnitude, and asymptotic to \(|c|\), while the conditional observation-time position has an explicit Brownian hitting-kernel density.
