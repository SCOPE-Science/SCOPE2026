PASS

In characteristic two A²=δI, δ=d mod 2, and for B=A+S the operator B²−δI maps onto W=span(e_u,e_v,Ae_u,Ae_v). The four vectors are independent: the nonadjacent endpoints are absent from both neighbor supports, and two distinct cube vertices at distance two share exactly two neighbors, whereas those at greater distance share none; d≥3 gives exclusive neighbors in both cases. The coefficient map of B²−δI from the four corresponding independent linear functionals is nonsingular. For C=A+δI the coordinate block split gives C=[[R,I],[I,R]], R²=I, and thus im C=ker C of dimension N/2. Ce_u and Ce_v are independent, so the span of the endpoint vectors avoids im C. Exclusive-neighbor columns of C give surjectivity of the endpoint coordinate projection on ker C. Consequently C restricted to vectors with both endpoint coordinates zero still surjects onto im C, and S adds exactly two dimensions outside it; rank(B+δI)=N/2+2. For even d, B maps W onto a two-dimensional span on which it swaps a basis, so the power ranks N, N/2+2, 4, 2, 2 imply one J₂(1), two J₃(0), N/2−4 J₂(0) and no other blocks. For odd d, B exchanges the pairs (e_u,Ae_v),(e_v,Ae_u) via the same order-three 2×2 map; the exchange has order two, B²−I is invertible on W, and the minimal polynomial on W is (X²+X+1)². On the complement B²=I, and rank(B−I) forces all its blocks to have size two. Thus the structure does not depend on distance and extends by scalars to each characteristic-two field. Fixed-space dimensions and divisor subtraction give the stated least periods; stable image dimensions plus nilpotent kernels give entry counts q²,q^(N/2),q^(N−2),q^N and the nonzero shell 1−q^(-2).
