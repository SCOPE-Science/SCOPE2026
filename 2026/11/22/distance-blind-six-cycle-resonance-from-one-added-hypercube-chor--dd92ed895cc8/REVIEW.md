Direct bodies of the most relevant public records were inaccessible, so historical priority is not independently certified.

## Value

**PASS.** For an infinite family, adding a single shortcut is dynamically indistinguishable at the linear similarity level across all its lengths, including triangle-producing and bipartiteness-preserving changes. The resulting odd/even dimension dichotomy determines all primitive orbits and transient shells, with a sharp last-step proportion independent of cube size. Both the topology-insensitivity and the all-size temporal census are mathematically motivated invariants.
