For squared-Laplacian heat on every unweighted Boolean cube, a kernel entry joining vertices at Hamming distance \(r\) has at most \(\lfloor r/2\rfloor\) positive-time zeros, independently of cube dimension. The four-cube is the first to attain two crossings: its antipodal heat entries turn positive, then negative, then positive, and its exact final positivity time and coordinate operator norms follow from three explicit lacunary polynomials.
