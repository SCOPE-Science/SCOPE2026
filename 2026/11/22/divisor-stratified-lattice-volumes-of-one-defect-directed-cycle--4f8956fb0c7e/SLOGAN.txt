For a naturally oriented cycle with all but one arrival weight equal to \(w\), the normalized lattice volume of its edge simplex is exactly \(\gcd(\lvert\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(-w)^k\rvert,\lvert u-w\rvert)\). A one-unit exceptional weight makes the simplex unimodular in every dimension, while varying that weight realizes every divisor of the uniform-weight volume and no other value.
