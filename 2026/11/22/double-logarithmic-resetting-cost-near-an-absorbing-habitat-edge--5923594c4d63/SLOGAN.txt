For a population at the reset-free critical habitat size, resetting to a site a distance \(\delta\) from an absorbing boundary initially harms growth and eventually rescues it at one positive rate. The uniquely required rate diverges as \(4\delta^{-2}[\log(1/\delta)+\log\log(1/\delta)+\log2+o(1)]^2\); the double-logarithmic correction is proved uniformly in a near-boundary neighborhood.
