For every alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), all adjacency-distinct target words of length \(m\ge2\) fall into exactly \(m-1\) different classes according to the number of host words with **exactly two indexed scattered occurrences**: the class invariant is precisely the number of palindromic length-three factors of the target. A single exact rational generating function resolves every host length and yields growth \(\frac{m+2+t}{2(q-1)}n(q-1)^n\), where \(t\) is that local palindrome count.
