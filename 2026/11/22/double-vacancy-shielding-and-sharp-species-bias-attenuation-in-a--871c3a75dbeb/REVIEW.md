# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

The infinite one-attempt process is almost surely locally decidable by factorially rare decreasing-time ancestry. The one-sided endpoint occupation probabilities solve the stated linear equations. At terminal time, each of two adjacent rejected attempts must be blocked by its *outer* neighbor; the corresponding events are on disjoint independent input sets. This gives exact conditional independence of entire terminal half-lines and species-resolved weights. A convergent power series with strictly positive coefficients from degree four proves the sharp inequality, with the degree-two limit giving \(1/5\). An exact rational marked-arrival checker covers five finite bias/size settings and full-half-line conditional independence; it passed 180 assertions. Limitations: finite checks are not the infinite proof; the statement requires one attempt and terminal time.

## Originality (PASS, publication-priority qualified)

The 2023 source [1] establishes color densities, not a two-vacancy conditional full-configuration cut. Published result [2] already determines the **uncolored** adjacent-vacancy intensity indirectly from monochromatic cluster counts and vacancy density, so that scalar formula is explicitly *not* claimed as new. Published result [3] treats distance-two same-color correlations and one-vacancy patterns, not conditional independence of two complete exteriors across a double vacancy, nor the exact sharp bias-screening factor. These distinctions are logical: one-site and monochromatic cluster-length marginals do not specify cross-gap species labels or all-half-line conditional laws. Repeated-attempt and one-color papers use different dynamics. Searches of equivalent terminologies and stronger possible coverage did not produce a covering theorem, but unindexed adsorption literature remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

Vacant defects in a jammed two-species substrate distinguish whether neighboring adsorbate domains are coupled. The theorem identifies a **minimal exact spatial separator** and gives its full probabilistic meaning (conditional independence of arbitrarily complex left/right observables). The sharp quantitative attenuation \(\kappa(p)<1/5\) is a motivated species-resolved prediction: conditioning on a double vacancy makes adjacent adsorbate identities unexpectedly balanced even when proposals are overwhelmingly biased. The result is not merely a reformulation of known density or monochromatic-cluster formulas.

The strongest residual risk is an unindexed full-pattern computation or regenerative decomposition for precisely the same one-attempt model. No claim is made for transient double vacancies or repeated proposals.
