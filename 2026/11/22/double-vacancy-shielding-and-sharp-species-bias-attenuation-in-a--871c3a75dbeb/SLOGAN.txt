In one-shot antagonistic two-species adsorption on the integer line, a pair of consecutive terminal vacancies completely separates the random configurations to its left and right. The adsorbate colors flanking such a pair are independent and have color contrast exactly \((2p-1)\kappa(p)\), where the sharp universal attenuation factor satisfies \(0<\kappa(p)<1/5\); one vacancy alone does not separate the system.
