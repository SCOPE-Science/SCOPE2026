The projective closure of a generic phase-shifted coprime Lissajous curve has two branches at one point at infinity, meeting with multiplicity \(n(n-m)\). Each is smooth for \(n-m=1\) and otherwise has one Puiseux pair \((n-m,n)\). The dual degree is exactly \(2(m+n)\), and all \(2(m+n)\) affine inflection points are ordinary; the remaining Wronskian weight is concentrated at the two points above infinity.
