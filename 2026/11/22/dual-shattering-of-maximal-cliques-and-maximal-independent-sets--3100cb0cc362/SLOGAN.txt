For the Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs, maximal-independent-set membership shatters exactly the independent supports of at most three vertices, giving identical polynomials \(1+16z+72z^2+96z^3\) and the same mod-two two-dimensional homology rank \(39\). In contrast, maximal-clique membership yields a thirty-three-circle graph for the rook graph and the full triangulated torus for Shrikhande, with VC dimensions two and three.
