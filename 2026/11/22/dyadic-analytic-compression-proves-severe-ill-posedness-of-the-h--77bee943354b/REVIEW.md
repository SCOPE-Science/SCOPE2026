# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The row map \(z\mapsto x^{z-1}/z\) is holomorphic in \(L^2(0,1)\) for \(\operatorname{Re}z>1/2\). On every dyadic index block the same Bernstein ellipse lies within this domain. The explicit vector norm bound, Chebyshev coefficient sum, rank count, Hilbert–Schmidt tail and approximation-number reduction independently yield \(s_{J^2+1}(T)\le8\,2^{-J}\). A standalone script passed finite supplementary checks, not an infinite certificate.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Kindermann and Hofmann (2024, arXiv:2401.14701, Sections 4–5) explicitly identify the same \(DH=HC^*\) operator, prove only polynomial upper and exponential-type lower bounds, and leave the moderate-versus-severe distinction unresolved. Earlier Hofmann–Mathé and Gerth papers mainly consider \(HJ\); later Werner–Hofmann addresses the noncompact \(H\), not the compact \(DH\) composite. Targeted published-finding corpus equivalence and broader-coverage searches did not find the dyadic analytic compression bound. General unindexed operator-approximation results remain a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem decides a motivating open qualitative classification for a standard inverse-problem composition and gives a fully quantitative superpolynomial singular-value upper bound. The exponent \(1/2\) and numeric constant are upper bounds, not asserted optimal.
