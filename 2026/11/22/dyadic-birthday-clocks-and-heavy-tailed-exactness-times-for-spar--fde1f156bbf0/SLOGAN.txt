For binary Rule 102, a prescribed sparse-site cylinder becomes the entire bilateral full shift after \(2^m\) steps exactly when its marked sites have distinct residues modulo \(2^m\); random \(k\)-site cylinders therefore exhibit an exact birthday-collision law at the scale \(k^2\). For uniformly sampled three-site cylinders, the unrounded exactness time has a tight limiting law but a mean growing as \(\frac32\log_2 N\) and variance growing as \(\frac32N\), exposing rare-event arithmetic delays.
