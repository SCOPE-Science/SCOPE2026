Every binomial column has exactly the same full \(2\)-adic output law as the column with twice its lower index: more strongly, each parity-restricted doubled column is conjugate to the original by an isometry preserving all input residues modulo \(2^{1+\lfloor\log_2m\rfloor}\). Consequently the entire dyadic residue-fiber distribution of \(\binom{x}{m}\) depends only on the odd part of \(m\).
