The \(48\) minimum two-sensor power-dominating sets on the Shrikhande graph have a remarkable two-scale relocation geometry: unrestricted replacements form the line graph of the Shrikhande graph itself, while adjacent-vertex slides form the line graph of the cubic Dyck graph. Both relocation networks are connected, but physical locality increases their diameter from \(3\) to \(5\); the cospectral \(4\times4\) rook graph instead needs three sensors.
