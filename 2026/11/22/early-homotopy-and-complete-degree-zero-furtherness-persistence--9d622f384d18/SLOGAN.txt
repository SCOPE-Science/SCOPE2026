For the upper-truncated Boolean poset of subsets of size at least \(r\ge2\), the first two higher-rank entrance thresholds \(r+1\) and \(r+2\) yield explicit graph-like homotopy types with integral circle ranks \((n-k)(k-r)\binom{k}{r-1}\) and \(r(\binom n{r+1}-\binom k{r+1})-(\binom nr-\binom kr)\). The entire degree-zero barcode, at all thresholds, has only finite deaths at \(r+1\) and \(r+2\), while the first-homology map between these two stages is split injective.
