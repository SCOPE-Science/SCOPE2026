# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The two-stage toggles are legal on every augmented simplex. Endpoint incidence is monotone on downward Hasse arrows and invariant under matched toggles, so no directed cycle switches incidence states. Within the second fiber, original edges never increase and each fixed original matching has a unique pendant pivot, excluding cycles. The remaining faces are exactly the perfect matchings avoiding the designated edge (even order), or the two disjoint near-perfect-matching classes (odd order). All critical cells apart from a basepoint have the same dimension, establishing the integral sphere wedge. An explicit crosspolytope-cycle/cochain pairing proves the claimed primitive inclusion image and rank of its free cokernel. The finite checker independently verifies directed acyclicity mod-two Betti ranks, integer cycle boundaries and unit coefficient pairings on the listed cases; the symbolic proof handles all graph orders.

## Originality — PASS, with priority limitations
The closest predecessor, *Perfect matchings govern the integral topology of fully whiskered matching complexes* (2026), is explicitly credited for the full-leaf case and its primitive basis. It does **not** classify deletion of two pendant graph edges at endpoints of an original edge. Barik–Mondal–Mukherjee (2026) provide a general acyclic-matching method, not the exact universal defect formula or inclusion image. Searched alternate descriptions include partial whiskering, missing pendant leaves, graph-edge-endpoint deletion, sunlet parity and inclusion of induced matching complexes. No covering theorem was located. This is a best-knowledge priority assessment, not proof of uniqueness; some literature remains incompletely accessible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result gives a complete homotopy classification for a natural minimal defect in a broad class of graph constructions, with exact primitive integral inclusion rather than merely numerical Betti counts. It detects forced edges in base perfect matchings topologically; cycles exhibit sharply different parity effects under deleting two neighboring whiskers.

## Limitations
Only pendant graph edges at both endpoints of an **existing** original base edge are deleted. Arbitrary missing-leaf patterns, higher matching capacities and homotopy retractions are unproved. The relationship to fully whiskered homotopy is prior work and is not claimed as new. Independent verification remains outstanding.
