Deleting the two pendant edges at the endpoints of an original edge produces a precisely computable homotopy defect in the matching complex of **every** fully whiskered base graph: even-order homology counts perfect matchings avoiding that edge, while odd-order homology counts near-perfect matchings missing one of its endpoints. In even order, the inclusion back into the fully whiskered complex is split-injective on integral top homology, with free cokernel indexed exactly by perfect matchings using the edge.
