Deleting a single graph edge from a Möbius ladder produces two different integral total-three-cut homotopy types according to whether the edge is on the rim or antipodal: \(1+\binom m2\) versus \(2+\binom m2\) spheres in degree \(2m-6\) for \(m\ge5\). The natural inclusion kills a primitive lattice of \(m\) versus \(m-1\) top-dimensional classes, while the first eight-vertex ladder has an exceptional rim-collapse phenomenon.
