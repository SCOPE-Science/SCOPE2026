The edge-multiset dimension of the two-row ladder \(P_n\square K_2\) is infinite for \(2\le n\le4\), equals four for \(n\in\{5,6,8\}\), and equals three for \(n=7\) and every \(n\ge9\). The exceptional return to four landmarks at exactly eight columns is certified by exhaustive finite obstructions, while a simple parity-dependent three-landmark construction proves the infinite tail.
