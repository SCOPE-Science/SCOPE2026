For \(K_{m,n}\) with any matching deleted and \(m,n\ge3\), every edge-open packing is a star subset or a reciprocal pair supported by two missing edges, yielding closed counting polynomials for all packings and all inclusion-maximal packings. Its packing complex is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \((m-1)(n-1)-t+\binom t2\) circles. On balanced shores of size at least six, the second disjoint deletion produces a maximal two-edge set separated by an arbitrarily long cardinality gap from the maximal stars.
