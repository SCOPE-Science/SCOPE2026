The edge-open-packing polynomials of the fifteen-vertex line graphs of the Petersen graph and pentagonal prism are, respectively, \(1+30z+225z^2+530z^3+330z^4\) and \(1+30z+235z^2+580z^3+415z^4\). Every maximum packing has four edges, and the \(330\) Petersen-line-graph optima split into four natural \(S_5\) orbits of sizes \(30,60,120,120\).
