For the left Leibniz algebra \(kt\oplus V\) defined by \([t,v]=Tv\) with invertible \(T\), Nijenhuis operators whose restriction to \(V\) differs from their scalar quotient action by rank at most one admit a complete two-branch normal form and an exact finite-field rank polynomial. For \(\dim V\ge3\), the number of corank-one operators in this natural determinantal stratum is \(q^{2\dim V-2}H_T\), where \(H_T\) counts nonzero rational eigenvectors, so corank one occurs precisely when \(T\) has a rational eigenvalue.
