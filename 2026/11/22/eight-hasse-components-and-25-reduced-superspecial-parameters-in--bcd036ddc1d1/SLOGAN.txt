In characteristic \(11\), the smooth two-parameter Ciani quartic surface \(X^4+Y^4+Z^4+rX^2Y^2+s(X^2Z^2+Y^2Z^2)=0\) has an eight-component nonordinary Hasse divisor, whose three double components and five simple components meet in precisely 25 reduced superspecial marked parameters. The complete coefficient-marked Jacobian \(11\)-rank frequencies are \((9,14,18,32)\) over \(\mathbb F_{11}\) and \((25,326,560,13132)\) over \(\mathbb F_{121}\).
