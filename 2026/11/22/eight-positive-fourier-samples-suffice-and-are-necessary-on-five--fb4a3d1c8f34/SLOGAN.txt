For five consecutive sites of the cyclic group of order \(2p\), with \(p\) any odd prime, the smallest strictly positive weighted Fourier Parseval frame has exactly \(6,5,7,8\) distinct frequencies as \(p=3,5,7,\ge11\), respectively. A cyclotomic parity obstruction proves the lower bound, while a closed eight-node Chebyshev quadrature constructs every case \(p\ge11\) without searching.
