## correctness

PASS

The cited construction does give the displayed normal birational model: Proposition 1.6 with n=5 and b=2, applied to the degree-five reduced cyclic cover of the quadric in Theorem 2.2. In §§2.4–2.5, Gamma_i meets F_i, E_{i-1}, E_{i+1} in three distinct points, not E_i; after its contraction the first two E curves cross transversely at one point of F_i while E_i meets F_i elsewhere. Blowing down the five disjoint F_i gives exactly the five indicated contacts, each with pairwise intersection multiplicities 2,1,1. Those consume all binom(5,2)*2 pairwise intersection multiplicities on the quadric. Projection is an isomorphism off the two contracted rulings and gives distinct tangent directions from the distinct intersections on each ruling, adding two ordinary fivefold points; no plane component acquires another singularity. The W derivative restricts the singular support to W=0 or Q=0. On these sets with nonzero plane coordinates the other derivatives are those of the branch polynomial up to sign, so one gets just the seven points on W=0, because Q avoids them. In the W=1 chart the vertex has multiplicity five. At an ordinary triple contact with one pair tangent, take u=0 for the transverse branch, center v between the two tangent graphs, rescale u by a square root of a unit, and absorb the resulting branch unit and Q by roots/coordinate change in the cyclic variable. The resulting Jacobian algebra has the six base monomials 1,u,u^2,u^3,u^4,v and z-exponents 0 through 3. At the projection points, the homogeneous quintic principal part is isolated because the binary tangent product has simple roots. At the vertex the weights 1,1,2 isolate the principal part from all other terms of the decic divisible by Q; transversality gives ten simple roots of the binary restriction. The regular-sequence weighted Jacobian Hilbert series and invariance for the isolated semi-quasihomogeneous germs give 24, 64, 324 as stated. The Euler characteristic check gives 5*19-18+10+1=88, with compactly supported stratified Euler characteristic, and 660-88=572 for the total Milnor number of the isolated surface singularities. Independent exact sample Jacobian lengths for distinct split binary forms also agree; they are a control, not the generic proof.

## originality

PASS

The precise model, generic normality and quadric incidence are already in the cited construction and are credited rather than treated as new. The supported distinct result is the full projective singularity and Milnor census, which requires tracking the two projection points and the projective vertex in addition to the original branch contacts, and checking the local isolated principal parts. The earlier genus-two cover and same-day nonic studies use different models and do not imply this census. The matching indexed record is the frozen target itself.

## value

PASS

The complete exceptional singularity basket is a natural, motivated invariant of the explicit normal decic model of this Jacobian. In particular a computation restricted to branch contacts would miss both ordinary projection points and the vertex, the latter carrying more than half of the vanishing-cycle contribution. Combining the local analytic data with the projective Euler defect makes the result useful for studying this model's topology or deformations without implying new general singularity theory.
