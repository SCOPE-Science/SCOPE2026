For the general normal decic model of the Jacobian of \(y^2=x^5+1\), the entire singular locus has eight points: five analytically equivalent to \(z^5=u(v^2-u^4)\), two ordinary multiplicity-five points with smooth tangent cones, and one weighted semi-quasihomogeneous vertex. Their Milnor numbers are respectively \(24\), \(64\), and \(324\), totaling \(572\).
