On the ten-vertex pentagonal prism, synchronous four-state Greenberg–Hastings dynamics has exactly 530 primitive four-cycles and six primitive five-cycles, and every initial state enters its eventual orbit by a sharp eight updates. The six five-waves split into two geometric classes whose individual attraction basins have sizes \(1{,}280\) and \(7{,}355\), while the twenty maximally delayed states form one symmetry orbit.
