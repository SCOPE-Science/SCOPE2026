## Value

PASS. The four-cube is both a canonical distance-regular network and the smallest periodic square lattice with a complete \(4\times4\) two-dimensional torus representation. Changing the activation criterion from one contact to two collapses the known rich recurrent period spectrum to two affine geometric classes and leaves only 11,784 oscillation-attracted states. This completely specifies a natural finite invariant, differentiates two distinct phase geometries, and yields a sharp global relaxation identity and exact attraction basins, useful as a benchmark for network excitable dynamics. The numerical match with prior folded-cube individual basin sizes is a motivated comparison, not an unsupported graph-independent theorem.
