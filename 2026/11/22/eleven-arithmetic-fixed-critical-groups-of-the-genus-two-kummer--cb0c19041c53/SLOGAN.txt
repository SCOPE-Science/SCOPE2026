The Frobenius-fixed chip-firing group of the classical genus-two Kummer \((16_6)\) node–trope graph has ten distinct integral isomorphism types across all eleven permutation types on the six Weierstrass points. Two different involution types, \((2,2,1,1)\) and \((2,2,2)\), yield nonisomorphic fixed groups even though both have order \(3\cdot2^{42}\).
