For the genus-\(28\) boundary of the Hesse rational elliptic surface, all eleven conjugacy classes of the affine \(432\)-element symmetry group have explicitly determined Frobenius-fixed critical groups. Both order-eight types retain exactly \(C_7\), while the entire determinant-one Hessian group fixes no nonzero critical class; the underlying nonequivariant critical group is not a new claim.
