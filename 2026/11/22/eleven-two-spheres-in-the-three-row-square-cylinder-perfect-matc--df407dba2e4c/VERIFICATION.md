# Reproduction and verification

Use Python 3's standard library. Place `verify.py` and `certificate.json` in one directory and execute `python3 verify.py` from any working directory. The script resolves the certificate relative to its own file path. It has no network requirement and uses no external packages.

The certificate uses graph-vertex identifiers \(4r+c\) for \(0\le r<3\) and \(0\le c<4\), and graph-edge identifiers \(0,\ldots,19\) sorted lexicographically by endpoint pair. Each face mask is the sum of powers of two at its graph-edge indices. The `collapses` field lists 526 verified free codimension-one face/coface pairs; the checker rebuilds the full face poset from both independent perfect-matching enumerations and checks each deletion in order. The remaining complex has \((18,66,60)\) faces in dimensions zero, one, two. The `tree_edges` field identifies a breadth-first spanning tree with 17 edges, which is regenerated by the checker. The `relator_kills` field contains 49 triangle-mask / generator-index pairs, each tested to be a singleton attaching word after previous generator eliminations. Eleven empty two-cell attaching words remain.

The resulting group-presentation elimination proves simple connectivity. The Euler characteristic \(12\), absence of cells above dimension two after collapse, and freeness of subgroups of free abelian groups imply \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{11}\); Hurewicz and Whitehead give the actual homotopy equivalence to a wedge of eleven two-spheres. The script additionally checks the residual two-boundary rank over primes \(2,3,5,7\). Modular calculations alone would not prove integral torsion freedom or simple connectivity.

A successful run prints `HOMOTOPY_TYPE_WEDGE_11_S2_CERTIFIED` as its final line. `check_output.txt` records one completed run. The certificate is complete for the fixed graph and does not prove any infinite-family statement. The historical open-grid result used for comparison is taken from the stated published source, not recomputed by this checker.
