All \(100\) smooth monic centered Picard quartics \(y^3=x^4+a x^2+b x+c\) over \(\mathbb F_5\) realize exactly eleven different genus-three Weil polynomials, in an exhaustive marked-parameter distribution. Every Jacobian has a common supersingular elliptic isogeny factor \(1+5T^2\), and all odd-degree extension-field point counts equal \(5^n+1\); the classification is not an isomorphism-class census.
