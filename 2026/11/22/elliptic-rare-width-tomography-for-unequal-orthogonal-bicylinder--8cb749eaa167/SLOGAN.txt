For two orthogonal, unequal-radius circular cylinders, the probability that an isotropic projection width lies within \(\varepsilon\) of its minimum is \(\varepsilon K(a/b)/(\pi b)+O(\varepsilon^2)\), with an explicit nonuniform angular law for the rare directions. The minimum and this elliptic slope uniquely recover both cylinder radii; the coefficient diverges logarithmically as the radii become equal.
