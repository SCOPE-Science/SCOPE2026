For both degree-three Ankeny–Brauer–Chowla families \(X(X\mp1)(X-u)+1\) over every finite field of odd characteristic other than seven, the complete four-way root-count histogram is determined by a single six-torsion elliptic curve \(Y^2=X^3+13X^2+128X\) and at most four discriminant collisions. In particular, the exact number of irreducible parameters is one third of that elliptic curve's rational-point count.
