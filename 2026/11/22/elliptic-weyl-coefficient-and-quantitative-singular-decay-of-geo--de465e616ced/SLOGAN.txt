On geometrically weighted \(\ell^2\), Cesàro averaging has singular values \(s_n=\kappa(\sqrt q)/n+O_q(n^{-3/2}\log(2+n))\), with an explicit complete-elliptic-integral coefficient. Although its eigenvalues remain \(1/n\) for every weight, its singular Weyl coefficient grows strictly and diverges logarithmically as the geometric weight approaches the unweighted limit.
