The complete Euclidean boundary-clearance law of an area-uniform point in a noncircular ellipse has an exact one-integral formula. At erosion radius \(b^2/a\), its surviving area departs from the inward Steiner quadratic by an explicit positive \((t-b^2/a)^{7/2}\) term, and the location of that departure together with the maximum clearance recovers both semiaxes.
