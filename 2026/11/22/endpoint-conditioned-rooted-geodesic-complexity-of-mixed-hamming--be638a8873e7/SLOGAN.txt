For every genuinely mixed Cartesian product of complete graphs, rooted isometric-path complexity is exactly \(1+t+\lfloor b/2\rfloor\), where \(t\) counts nonbinary factors and \(b\) counts binary factors. More precisely, for fixed endpoints and a fixed root, the worst shortest-path order needs exactly \(1+h+\min(e,a)\) rooted paths, where \(h\) counts nonroot-symbol replacements and \(e,a\) count erasures and additions.
