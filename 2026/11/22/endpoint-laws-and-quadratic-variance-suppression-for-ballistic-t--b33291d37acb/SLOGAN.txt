For an exactly traversed interval under ballistic threshold resetting, every bounded positional observable has a time-integrated centered error determined solely by the two observation endpoints, so its stationary time-average variance decays as \(t^{-2}\), rather than \(t^{-1}\). For the Gamma-renewal family, the complete rescaled error law is a difference of two explicit Gamma–uniform mixtures, with a provable factor-two variance distinction between stationary and fresh-reset initialization.
