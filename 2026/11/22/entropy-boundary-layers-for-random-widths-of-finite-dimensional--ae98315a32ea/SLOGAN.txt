Random Euclidean viewing directions of a finite-dimensional \(\ell_p\) ball have four exact extreme-width laws, with a nonquadratic exponent at coordinate-axis minima on one side of \(p=2\). As \(p\to2\), the correctly rescaled width becomes half the Shannon entropy of a \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1/2,\ldots,1/2)\) vector, whose lower endpoint has the sharp logarithmic rarity \((t/\log(1/t))^{(d-1)/2}\).
