Every finite differentiability stage of the binary Kolakoski shift has positive topological entropy, with successive entropies contracting by a factor between \(2/3\) and \(3/4\); the infinite-depth intersection has zero entropy. At depth two, the entire periodic-orbit zeta function is an explicit rational function, and the entropy is \(\log\lambda\), where \(\lambda^4-\lambda^3-1=0\) has its unique positive root at \(1.380277569\ldots\).
