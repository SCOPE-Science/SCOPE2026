# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The rank-one trace reduction yields the exact symmetric two-block kernel. Bistochasticity proves the global sign classification, Jensen plus an irreducible Perron argument proves the sharp edge and its equality case, and support-component simplicity allows differentiation at the critical exponent. The first-order derivative is the negative mean Shannon entropy. Checking the left and right maxima across finitely many blocks proves the cusp and inertia statements. The supplementary numerical diagnostics passed 2262 assertions but are not used as substitutes for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic risk
Fannes–de Melo–Roga–Życzkowski (2012) already give the mutually unbiased basis obstruction, and Vuong–Osaka (2026) already give a four-state obstruction and the three-point positive theorem. Those statements are credited and excluded from the originality claim. Their primary full texts were inspected at their relevant propositions and remarks. The additional claim classifies every two-basis configuration, including reducible supports and arbitrary exponents, and computes component-entropy one-sided critical slopes and inertia. published-finding corpus and literature searches did not surface an equivalent complete statement, but earlier stochastic-matrix perturbation or quantum fidelity arguments might imply portions; uniqueness is not guaranteed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The critical kernel geometry is natural because order-three positivity of trace-power fidelity does not extend to arbitrary ensembles. The new classification identifies precisely which pairs of complete bases exhibit that obstruction, how many directions become negative at the transition, and a quantitative entropy law distinguishing different support components. The entropy cusp is not visible from a single mutually unbiased counterexample and motivates a quantitative stability interpretation of the positivity transition. The result makes no claim about arbitrary ensembles, mixed states or independently audited novelty.

## Closest literature and limitations
The 2012 paper's Proposition 4 explicitly covers the mutually unbiased witness; no priority is claimed for that example. Vuong–Osaka Theorem 1.1 and Remark 2.4 cover the three-point theorem and the four-qubit witness but not the arbitrary two-basis critical entropy law in the inspected material. The matrix-theoretic steps are elementary, so an older equivalent formulation remains possible. Scientific validation is same-model only; a separate independent audit has not occurred.
