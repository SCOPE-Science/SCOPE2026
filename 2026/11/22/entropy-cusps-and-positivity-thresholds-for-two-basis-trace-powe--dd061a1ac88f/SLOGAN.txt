For the trace-power fidelity kernel on the rank-one projections from any two orthonormal bases, the exact positivity threshold is \(r=1\) unless the bases coincide, and near that threshold the negative spectral edge has a one-sided cusp governed by the largest and smallest Shannon entropies of the connected overlap components. The component decomposition also gives the complete critical zero-mode count and the near-critical negative inertia.
