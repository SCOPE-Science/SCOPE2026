Among all fixed convex mixtures of forward and reflected Hardy averaging on real \(BV(0,1)\), the symmetric mixture uniquely minimizes the sharp \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) identity-defect norm, reducing it to \((\log2)/2\), with midpoint single-jump extremizers. Its norm deficit also controls the squared distance of the full variation measure from the midpoint with optimal coefficient \(1\).
