On the exact boundary-crisis curve of a piecewise-linear/logistic interval map, the unique maximal-entropy measure retains full entropy \(\log2\) but converges to an endpoint mass with sharp exponentially small tails as the linear slope approaches one. Its typical Hausdorff dimension collapses like \(2\log2/\log(1/(r-1))\), even while its exceptional local dimension at the opposite endpoint diverges like \(\log2/\log r\).
