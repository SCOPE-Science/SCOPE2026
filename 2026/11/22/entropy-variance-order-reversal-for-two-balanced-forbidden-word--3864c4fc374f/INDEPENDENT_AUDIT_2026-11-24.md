# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Both finite avoidance languages extend bilaterally by ones to the left and zeros to the right. The prefix automata have access to the empty state (use zeros, then 1^(k−1)0 and ones for the run pattern, or zeros then ones for the alternating pattern), reach all proper prefixes from it, and have an empty-state loop. Primitivity gives a simple analytic Perron eigenvalue near t=0; uniform exponentially small spectral remainders there justify twice differentiating normalized log partition sums to get the finite-word variance limit. Complement–reversal pairs counts N and n−N for every n. Separating avoiders from words with a first forbidden copy at the very end and then classifying overlap with an appended copy reproduces the weighted series. For the alternating overlap factor the geometric sum reduces the denominator to 1−q + r^k q^(2k+1)(1−rq) after multiplication by 1−rq². In both cases there is no positive pole up to q=1, and the near-one root is uncancelled and increasing through zero as a residual on (1,1+2·4^(−k)); the bounds at k=2 are strict and improve thereafter. Twice differentiating at r=1/4, r'=0, r''=−r/2 yields both displayed curvature formulas with positive denominators. The factor q(1−q/4)<1 gives q_alt<q_run. Independently, at common u=q_alt−1, subtracting the two variance increments with denominators cleared leaves a positive multiple of b−[k+(k−1)b]u, which is positive since b>1/3 and u<2·4^(−k)<1/(4k−1) for k≥2. The run increment increases with u, so the strict comparison follows even without splitting the k range. Expanding the pole equations at q=1 gives u_run=a+O(ka²), u_alt=3a/4+O(ka²) for a=4^(−k); the increments and logarithms then give all four advertised asymptotics. A separate exact rational reconstruction confirmed all six rounded-down small-k table entries. The order is of limiting variance coefficients at every k, not an order at every finite n.
