For every \(k\ge2\), forbidding \(0^k1^k\) yields smaller entropy but strictly *larger* asymptotic digit-count variance than forbidding \((01)^k\), even though both systems have exactly one-half mean density at every finite word length. Their variance gap is \(\frac{k+1}{8}4^{-k}+O(k^2 16^{-k})\), a quantitative second-order distinction invisible to mean-frequency comparisons.
