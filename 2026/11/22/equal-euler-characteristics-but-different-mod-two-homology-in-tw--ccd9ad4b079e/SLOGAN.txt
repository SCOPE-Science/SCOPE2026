Two twenty-vertex cubic covers of the Petersen graph have geodesic general-position complexes with the same Euler characteristic \(-60\) and the same sixty top-dimensional simplices, yet different reduced mod-two homology: the dodecahedral complex has Betti ranks \((10,60,111)\) in degrees one to three, while the Desargues complex has \((40,0,21)\). Exact exhaustive chain reductions prove the distinction without asserting integral or homotopy classifications.
