Freshly repartitioned and randomly frozen minibatch reshuffling have identical mean-square error at every epoch on scalar equal-curvature quadratics, yet a minimal four-sample model gives an atom of mass \(2/3\) at the optimizer under frozen partitions and no atoms under fresh partitions. At one exact stepsize boundary, the fresh stationary law has full interval support and is singular continuous—even though its fourth cumulant vanishes exactly.
