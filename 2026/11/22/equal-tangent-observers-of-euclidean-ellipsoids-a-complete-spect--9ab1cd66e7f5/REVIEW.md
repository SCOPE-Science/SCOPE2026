# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** Complete tangency-locus parametrization, odd/even decomposition of Euclidean squared lengths, polarization of the compressed quadratic form, and converses prove the classification. The axial length and inverse formulas are exact. Eleven reproducible numerical scenarios provide corroboration, not proof. The strict exterior domain and \(n\ge3\) boundary are explicit.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** The closest full-text source is Jerónimo-Castro, Ruiz-Hernández and Tabachnikov's 2012 equal-tangents paper; its large-locus sphere rigidity does not entail the full fixed-ellipsoid observer classification. Lángi (2011) assumes different global tangent-section hypotheses and norm, while Jerónimo-Castro and Tabachnikov (2014) treat planar equitangent curves. Related database results cover tangent balls or planar tangent-chords rather than the exact claim. Historical literature, especially older quadric texts, is not exhaustive and priority remains uncertain.

**Value — PASS.** Exact classification of a natural equitangent locus in all higher dimensions, with a sharp spectral symmetry threshold and an inverse tangent-length invariant, addresses a meaningful geometric question without relying on a generic improvement or arbitrary numerical instance.

**Limits.** Nondegenerate Euclidean ellipsoids only; \(n\ge3\); no claim for general convex bodies, equal tangent directions alone, or the planar two-dimensional case. Numerical diagnostics are supportive only. Independent evaluation has not occurred.
