Linear zero-product preservers of \(K[t]/(t^n)\) form an equidimensional union of exactly \(\binom{n-1}{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\) maximal coordinate spaces, each of dimension \(n(n+1)/2\). Exactly one component contains invertible maps, and at fixed \(n\) their density tends to the reciprocal component count as \(q\to\infty\); this unit group is the full \(t\)-adic flag stabilizer.
