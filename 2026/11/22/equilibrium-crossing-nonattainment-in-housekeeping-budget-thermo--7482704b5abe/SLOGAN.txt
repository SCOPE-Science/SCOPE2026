In housekeeping-budget thermodynamic control, crossing a smooth isolated zero of stationary entropy production makes the sharp geometric excess-dissipation bound an unattained infimum at every finite duration. A positive-rate three-state Markov ring realizes this obstruction: near its equilibrium crossing, an explicitly regularized near-optimal schedule takes time proportional to \(\log(1/\varepsilon)\), while its excess cost approaches the bound.
