# Equivariant homology and fixed-point topology of two-block tolerance complexes

## Finding
Let \(t\ge2\) and let \(A,B\) be disjoint finite sets with \(|A|=a\ge t+1\), \(|B|=b\ge t+1\). Let \(K=\Delta(A)\sqcup\Delta(B)\), the disjoint union of two full simplices, and let \(X=\mathcal T_t(K)\) be its \(t\)-tolerance complex. Put \(U_A=\ker(\mathbb Z^A\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z)\), and similarly \(U_B\). Then:

1. \(|X|\simeq\bigvee^{\binom{a-1}{t}\binom{b-1}{t}}S^{2t}\) integrally, with no other reduced integral homology.
2. The natural \(S_A\times S_B\)-action on the unique nonzero integral homology group is exactly
   \[\widetilde H_{2t}(X;\mathbb Z)\cong \bigwedge^t U_A\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\bigwedge^t U_B.\]
3. Suppose \(a=b=n\), choose an identification \(A\cong B\), and let \(\tau\) exchange the corresponding vertices in the two blocks. Writing \(r=\binom{n-1}{t}\), the induced action is the signed tensor flip
   \[\tau_*(v\otimes w)=(-1)^{t+1}w\otimes v.\]
   The **geometric fixed-point space** is PL-homeomorphic to \(|\operatorname{Sk}_{t-1}\Delta^{n-1}|\), hence to \(\bigvee^r S^{t-1}\). The **genuine geometric quotient** has no positive rational homology outside degree \(2t\), and
   \[\dim_{\mathbb Q}\widetilde H_{2t}(|X|/\langle\tau\rangle;\mathbb Q)=\frac{r^2+(-1)^{t+1}r}{2}.\]
4. In the minimal balanced case \(n=t+1\), the actual geometric quotient is homeomorphic to
   \[|X|/\langle\tau\rangle\cong S^{t-1}*\mathbb{RP}^{t}\simeq\Sigma^t\mathbb{RP}^{t}.\]
   Consequently, for every \(t\ge2\), the quotient contains \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) in integral homology degree \(t+1\) even though \(|X|\cong S^{2t}\). More generally its reduced integral homology is the \(t\)-fold degree shift of \(\widetilde H_*(\mathbb{RP}^t;\mathbb Z)\).

The statement classifies one natural family of symmetry actions and their geometric fixed/quotient topology. It does not assert that the underlying sphere boundary in the minimal balanced case is newly discovered.

## Assumptions and scope
For a simplicial complex \(K\) on \(A\sqcup B\), the tolerance complex consists of all subsets \(\sigma\) such that deleting at most \(t\) vertices makes \(\sigma\) a face of \(K\). All simplices are abstract and all homotopy statements refer to geometric realizations. The homology coefficient ring is \(\mathbb Z\) unless stated otherwise. The group \(S_A\times S_B\) permutes vertices separately within the two blocks. The involution \(\tau\) exists only after fixing a matching of equal-sized blocks. The theorem requires \(t\ge2\) and \(a,b\ge t+1\); when \(t\ge\min(a,b)\), the tolerance complex is a full simplex and is contractible.

## Proof
**Cover and homotopy type.** Write \(\sigma_A=\sigma\cap A\), \(\sigma_B=\sigma\cap B\). The tolerance condition is exactly \(\min(|\sigma_A|,|\sigma_B|)\le t\), since the face retained after deletion must lie wholly in one of the two original full simplices. Therefore
\[X=P\cup Q,\quad P=\Delta(A)*\operatorname{Sk}_{t-1}\Delta(B),\quad Q=\operatorname{Sk}_{t-1}\Delta(A)*\Delta(B),\]
with common subcomplex \(L=P\cap Q=\operatorname{Sk}_{t-1}\Delta(A)*\operatorname{Sk}_{t-1}\Delta(B)\). The two covers \(P,Q\) are contractible because they have a full simplex join factor. Simplicial inclusions are cofibrations, so their ordinary pushout is a homotopy pushout, yielding \(|X|\simeq\Sigma|L|\). The \((t-1)\)-skeleton of the simplex on \(a\ge t+1\) vertices is \((t-2)\)-connected with only free reduced homology in degree \(t-1\), rank \(\binom{a-1}{t}\), hence it is a wedge of that many \((t-1)\)-spheres. Taking joins and suspending proves the indicated integral wedge homotopy type.

**The integral module.** With \(P_A=\mathbb Z^A\), the augmented simplicial boundary at faces of cardinality \(t\) is contraction with the augmentation functional \(\epsilon_A:P_A\to\mathbb Z\). Splitting \(P_A\cong U_A\oplus\mathbb Z\) as abelian groups yields \(\ker(\epsilon_A\mathbin{\lrcorner}:\bigwedge^tP_A\to\bigwedge^{t-1}P_A)=\bigwedge^tU_A\). This identification is canonical and equivariant under \(S_A\); similarly for \(B\). The join chain isomorphism identifies \(\widetilde H_{2t-1}(L;\mathbb Z)\) with the tensor product of the two skeleton homology modules. The Mayer–Vietoris connecting map \(\widetilde H_{2t}(X;\mathbb Z)\to\widetilde H_{2t-1}(L;\mathbb Z)\) is an isomorphism and respects the separate block permutations, proving the module assertion without passing to rational coefficients.

**Block exchange and fixed locus.** For equal blocks, \(\tau\) switches the two cover pieces \(P,Q\), contributing a minus sign to the Mayer–Vietoris connecting isomorphism; it also switches the two factors of the join \(L\). The homological join flip on classes of degree \(t-1\) contributes \((-1)^{t^2}\). Thus the total factor is \((-1)^{t^2+1}=(-1)^{t+1}\). To describe actual geometric fixed points, observe that each involution orbit of vertices is a matched pair \(\{a_i,b_i\}\). A point fixed inside a simplex has invariant support, hence the support is a union of such pairs; an orbit-pair selection of size \(k\) forms a face precisely if \(k\le t\). Each fixed face is the simplex on orbit barycenters. This is a PL triangulation of the \((t-1)\)-skeleton on \(n\) vertices, proving the claimed fixed-space homeomorphism (not merely a homology agreement).

**Geometric quotient.** Over \(\mathbb Q\), finite-group averaging identifies the rational homology of an ordinary geometric quotient with invariant rational homology of the original space. On \(W\otimes W\) for \(W=\bigwedge^t(U_A\otimes\mathbb Q)\) of dimension \(r\), the unsigned flip has trace \(r\); the signed flip therefore has trace \((-1)^{t+1}r\). Its \(+1\)-eigenspace has dimension \((r^2+(-1)^{t+1}r)/2\). All other positive rational homology groups vanish since they vanish in \(X\).

**Integral quotient torsion at the natural minimal boundary.** If \(n=t+1\), a subset of the \(2t+2\) vertices is in \(X\) exactly when it is not the full vertex set. Hence \(|X|=|\partial\Delta^{2t+1}|\cong S^{2t}\). Realize that boundary as the unit sphere in the sum-zero real permutation representation of \(\mathbb R^{2t+2}\). Exchange of the two equal blocks has \(+1\)-eigenspace of real dimension \(t\) and \(-1\)-eigenspace of dimension \(t+1\) on the sum-zero representation. The unit sphere is equivariantly homeomorphic to the join \(S^{t-1}*S^t\), with \(\tau\) trivial on its first factor and antipodal on the second. Taking the ordinary quotient yields \(S^{t-1}*\mathbb{RP}^t\), homotopy equivalent to \(\Sigma^t\mathbb{RP}^t\). Standard integral cellular homology of \(\mathbb{RP}^t\) supplies \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) in each odd degree \(1\le j<t\); suspension moves these groups to degrees \(t+j\), proving the torsion assertion. This is a geometric-quotient conclusion, not a Borel-homology claim.

## Verification
The accompanying standalone Python program independently enumerates every simplicial face and computes mod-two boundary ranks for 46 two-block parameter triples with \(2\le a,b\le5\) and \(1\le t\le\min(a,b)\), including the contractible boundary regime. It also enumerates orientation-signed invariant faces and checks the reduced Lefschetz number and fixed-orbit Euler characteristic for ten block exchanges with \(2\le n\le5\). All comparisons passed. These finite checks support the proof but do not certify its infinite quantifiers or the integral torsion computation; those follow from the displayed cover, representation and sphere-action arguments.

## Relationship to prior work
Kim and Lew's *Leray Numbers of Tolerance Complexes* (2023, DOI below) introduced the tolerance-complex framework and **explicitly proved** that the two-block case \(|A|=|B|=t+1\) is the boundary of a simplex. The minimal-case sphere identification is therefore prior work, not the contribution here. Earlier one-tolerance results already classify the two-block case at \(t=1\). The contribution restricted to \(t\ge2\) is the all-size integral equivariant module, fixed-point classification, rational quotient ranks and the integral projective-suspension quotient phenomenon. Escher, Giusti and Lai's January 2025 topology paper analyzes recovering homology from quotients under regular cyclic actions; the present block-exchange is nonregular on the original simplicial complex and asks for an explicit fixed/quotient computation rather than a general reconstruction algorithm. Selvaraja's 10 November 2026 preprint studies collapsibility and Leray bounds, including complexes with few facets, rather than giving these symmetry-module statements in its accessible abstract. Its full preprint was not accessible for text-level comparison, and an equivalent unindexed result remains possible.

## Limitations
This result applies to precisely two **disjoint, full** simplex blocks, not overlapping blocks, three or more components, or arbitrary chordal complexes. The ordinary quotient has a complete **rational** homology statement for every balanced size, but its **integral** homotopy type is only identified for \(a=b=t+1\); no claim is made about all larger balanced integral quotients. The subgroup-equivariant homotopy equivalence to a sphere wedge is not asserted: only the integral homology representation is equivariant. There is no independent audit and no exhaustive publication-priority guarantee.
