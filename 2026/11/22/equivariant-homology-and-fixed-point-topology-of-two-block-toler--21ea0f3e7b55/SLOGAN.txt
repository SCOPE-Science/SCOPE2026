For two disjoint full simplices and every tolerance \(t\ge2\), the tolerance complex has a complete integral symmetry module \(\bigwedge^t U_A\otimes\bigwedge^t U_B\), and exchanging equal blocks has a fixed space equal to a simplex skeleton. At the minimal balanced size its genuine quotient is \(\Sigma^t\mathbb{RP}^t\), exhibiting integral two-torsion even though the original complex is a sphere.
