For very general \(W_{15}\) K3 tails realized as two weighted sextic square roots on \(\mathbb P(1,2,3)\), the two deck involutions each fix a genus-four curve, while their symplectic product fixes eight points. The minimal resolution has Picard rank exactly \(13\), and its rational Néron–Severi representation is \(\mathbf1^3\oplus\chi_{-,+}^4\oplus\chi_{+,-}^4\oplus\chi_{-,-}^2\).
