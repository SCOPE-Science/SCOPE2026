For every \(n\ge2\) and \(\ell\ge4\), the ordinary multipath complex of \(n\) consistently oriented length-\(\ell\) cycles sharing a common return path is \(\mathfrak S_n\)-equivariantly \(\bigvee^n S^{\ell-2}\); the sphere classes form the integral branch-permutation lattice. Fixed points and genuine quotients for every subgroup are wedges with respectively one sphere per fixed branch and per branch orbit.
