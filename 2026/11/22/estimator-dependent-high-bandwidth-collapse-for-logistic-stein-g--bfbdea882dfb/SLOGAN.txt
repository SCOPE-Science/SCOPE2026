For the logistic Fourier–Stein goodness-of-fit statistic with Gaussian frequency weight, arbitrary diverging bandwidths produce distinct \(\chi_1^2\) null limits: method-of-moments fitting requires normalization by \(a_n^{1/2}\), whereas maximum-likelihood fitting requires \(a_n^{5/2}\). The four-power difference inside the weighted integral comes from the two exact logistic maximum-likelihood score cancellations, and both constants are explicit.
