# Scientific assessment

For odd cycle size, the one-step inverse is the alternating sign kernel. At repeated depth the inverse kernel satisfies an antiperiodic difference recurrence. Its uniformly scaled polynomial profile converges to the corresponding Euler polynomial with error of order one over the cycle size; a Lipschitz Riemann sum for the absolute profile gives the sharp leading norm and the stated remainder. Exact signed polynomial sums give the formulas at depths two, three and four. The exact comparison norm in \(\ell^2\) follows by Fourier diagonalization.

The one-step norm and parity restriction are already present at the midpoint endpoint of *Sharp parity law for two-point discrete projection onto periodic linear splines* (8 November 2026). The continuum Favard constants and their limit are also not new. The result concerns the finite cyclic inverses of the repeated averaging operator and their quantitative approach to the comparison constants: finite-depth asymptotics with an error order and all-odd-size exact norms at three repeated depths.

The finite arithmetic checks corroborate the formulas but do not prove the recurrence or the limit. The large-cycle estimate fixes the smoothing depth; the two comparison limits are sequential, not joint. An earlier equivalent discrete inverse-norm identity under different terminology is not excluded.
