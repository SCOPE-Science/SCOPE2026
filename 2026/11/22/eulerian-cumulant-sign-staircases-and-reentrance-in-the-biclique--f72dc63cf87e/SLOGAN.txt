In the uniform hard-core gas on every complete bipartite graph, a connected correlation of \(r\) distinct sites from one shore and one from the other is exactly an Eulerian-root polynomial evaluated at a single shore weight. The resulting all-size phase diagram has \(r-1\) sign reversals when the opposite shore is larger, and permits two reentrant reversals already on the five-site graph \(K_{3,2}\).
