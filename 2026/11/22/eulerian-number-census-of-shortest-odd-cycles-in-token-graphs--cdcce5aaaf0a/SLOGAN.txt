The shortest odd cycles of every token graph are supported on unique shortest odd cycles of its base graph, and their exact multiplicities are governed by Eulerian numbers. Specifically, for odd girth \(g\), each base \(g\)-cycle lifts to \(\sum_{h=1}^{g-1}\binom{n-g}{k-h}\left\langle{g-1\atop h-1}\right\rangle\) distinct shortest odd token cycles.
