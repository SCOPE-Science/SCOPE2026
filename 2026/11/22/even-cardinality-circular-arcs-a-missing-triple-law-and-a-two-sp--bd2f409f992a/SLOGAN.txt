On every even cycle, even-cardinality circular intervals shatter exactly the supports of size at most two and the nonconsecutive triples; the full shattering polynomial is \(1+nz+\binom n2z^2+(\binom n3-n)z^3\). From six vertices onward the shattering complex is a wedge of exactly \(\binom{n-1}{3}-n\) two-spheres: excluding odd-size arcs deletes precisely \(n\) topological generators without creating first homology.
