Over every finite field of size greater than two, the prescribed Lie-bracket fibers of a graphical two-step nilpotent Lie algebra depend only on the zero pattern of the target exactly when the graph has no even cycle. All such odd-cycle cacti admit an exact matching-number fiber formula, although the two-triangle bowtie already has \(2q(q-1)(q^2-1)\) unattainable targets.
