At unequal proportionality, an explicit positive substitution converts this to the reciprocal recurrence. For even periods at least six, the cotangent decomposition of the Fourier multipliers and pairwise weighted Bessel inequalities give a non-strict linear-programming bound of one. Equality is nonetheless impossible for actual Bessel coefficients: it would require a unit vector to coincide with two distinct Fourier characters. This establishes the strict energy inequality. The three recurrence identities close the proof by forcing all nonzero Fourier modes to vanish. At equal proportionality, the excluded spectral interval gives a unique constant. The square case is independently classified by opposite-edge equality. A deterministic checker passed 2,488 assertions, and symbolic substitution independently verified the rational transformation. The analytic argument, not the finite checks, proves the global assertions.
