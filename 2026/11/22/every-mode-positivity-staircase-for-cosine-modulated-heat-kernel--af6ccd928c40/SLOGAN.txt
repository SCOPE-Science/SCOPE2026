For every finite cycle, a signed first-harmonic modulation of its heat kernel has a sharp positive-definiteness cutoff whose obstructing Fourier mode passes, with no skips or triple ties, through every index from \(\lfloor N/2\rfloor\) down to \(\lfloor N/4\rfloor+1\) as time increases. Each adjacent transition occurs exactly once; at the positivity boundary the kernel's nullity is explicitly determined by the active Fourier characters.
