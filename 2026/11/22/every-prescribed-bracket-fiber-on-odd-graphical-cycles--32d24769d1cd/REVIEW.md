# Scientific review: odd-cycle prescribed bracket fibers

## Correctness — PASS
The determinant coordinate identity is immediate from the graphical Lie bracket. Partitioning each vertex vector into zero and projective direction produces exactly the chromatic-contraction support sum; the contracted graph handles zero edges and impossible loops. Vertex rescalings act transitively on every nonfull support because its active-edge graph is a forest. For full support, the edge-scaling map on an odd cycle has only a possible quadratic-class obstruction; a simultaneous invertible two-by-two matrix changes all determinants by its determinant, and because the cycle is odd this removes that obstruction. Hence all coefficient values with fixed support have equal fiber. The full-support chromatic count gives \(q^n-q\) vertex-coordinate solutions, times \(q^{2n}\) unrestricted central factors. For positivity of proper support, the zero-edge components can be properly colored after deleting an allowed zero vertex when a single active edge would cause a loop.

The standalone independent enumeration verified every target for seven \((n,q)\) configurations up to \(n=9\), including \(q=5\). Finite tests corroborate but do not prove the all-prime-power theorem.

## Originality — PASS, residual priority risk
Full published text on graphical groups by Rossmann gives conjugacy and centralizer counts but not target-specific nonzero bracket fibers. Closest prescribed-target published findings cover **forests** or **friendship graphs**, and a three-block matrix theorem includes a four-cycle but not the full odd-cycle family. The forest result explicitly notes cyclic graphs are outside its scope. A family-wide odd-cycle closure argument is necessary and is presented. Triangle and zero-target formulas are acknowledged as overlapping earlier mathematics; only the full odd-cycle classification and uniform full-support stratum are assessed as new. An equivalent older general determinant-map theorem remains possible.

## Value — PASS
This is a complete natural word-map fiber classification on an infinite, nonacyclic family of nilpotent Lie algebras and graphical groups. It is not a bare chromatic polynomial: it determines the multiplicity of **each** prescribed algebraic commutator target, and resolves the cycle compatibility obstruction that invalidates straightforward forest propagation. The exceptionally uniform full-support law is a concrete structural invariant beyond the known commuting probability.

## Limitations
Odd cycles only. Finite-field enumeration covers prime fields and bounded dimensions; the proof handles extensions. Historical priority is not independently certified. The literature on general bilinear word maps remains a risk.
