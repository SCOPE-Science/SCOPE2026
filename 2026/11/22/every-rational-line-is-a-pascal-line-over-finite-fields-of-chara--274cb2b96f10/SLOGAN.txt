For any smooth conic over an odd finite field of characteristic at least five, the number of rational cyclic hexagons with a prescribed Pascal line has an exact formula depending only on whether that line is a secant, tangent, or exterior line and on \(\chi_q(-3)\). At \(q=5\) only exterior lines occur, but for every permitted \(q\ge7\) **every rational projective line** occurs as a Pascal line.
