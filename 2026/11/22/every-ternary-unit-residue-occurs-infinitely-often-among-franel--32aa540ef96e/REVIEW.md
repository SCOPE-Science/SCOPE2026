# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The proof gives an exact valuation of the interior binomial coefficients at each ternary power, a full Vandermonde separated-block identity modulo every required power, and a primitive-root/order argument using the 3-adic lifting-the-exponent formula. Infinite pigeonhole and a finite version give respectively infinitely many witnesses and an explicit finite bound. The C++ checks corroborate thousands of instances but are not substituted for universal proof.

**Originality (PASS within inspected evidence).** The 2014 nondivisibility result establishes only that all Franel numbers are units modulo three. An inspected published prime-power Franel result already proves the stronger prime-power-index congruence from which the generator step follows; that step is not the original contribution. The 2014 generalized Apéry work gives modulo-nine congruence descriptions, and Beukers's prime-power automata work establishes general finite-scheme machinery rather than the precise full image/infinite-fibre theorem stated here. Published analogous residue-image results for other combinatorial sequences are not results about Franel numbers. The risk of an implicit or unindexed equivalent under general digit-congruence techniques remains.

**Value (PASS).** The exact image of a familiar combinatorial sequence at every ternary prime-power level is a natural arithmetic invariant. This theorem settles it completely, strengthens an established nondivisibility phenomenon to every allowed class, and supplies sparse witnesses and an effective existence bound. It is not an arbitrary computation of selected residues. The result does not address distribution among indices or optimal bounds.
