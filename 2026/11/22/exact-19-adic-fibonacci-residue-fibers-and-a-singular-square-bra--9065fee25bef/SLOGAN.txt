Modulo every power of \(19\), every Fibonacci target has an exact prime-power representation count: seventeen index branches are \(19\)-adic isometries, while the eighteenth is a disjoint scaled-square branch. The maximal multiplicity within one least period grows as \(19^{k/2}\) at even precision \(k\) and \(2\cdot19^{(k-1)/2}\) at odd precision \(k\ge3\); the previously known image density is \(441/760\).
