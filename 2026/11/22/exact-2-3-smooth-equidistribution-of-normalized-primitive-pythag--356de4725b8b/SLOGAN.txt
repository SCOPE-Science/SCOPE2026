The normalized area \(A/6\) of primitive integral right triangles is equidistributed modulo precisely the integers composed only of primes two and three, when triangles are ordered by hypotenuse. Exact local fibers give \(N_Q(t;X)=X/(2\pi Q)+O_Q(\sqrt X\log(2X))\), while every prime \(p\ge5\) imposes the bias \(\Pr(p\mid A/6)=4/(p+1)\).
