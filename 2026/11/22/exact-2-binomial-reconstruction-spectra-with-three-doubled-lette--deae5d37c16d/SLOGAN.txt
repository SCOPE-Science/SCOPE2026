For words with two copies of each of three symbols and \(r\) additional distinct singleton markers, every complete scattered length-two deck has exactly \(1\), \(2\), or \(6\) source words; the exact numbers of fibers of each size are given by closed factorial polynomials for all \(r\ge0\). The probability of unique reconstruction is \(1-12r^{-2}+O(r^{-3})\), so distinct markers almost surely resolve the ternary core's ambiguity.
