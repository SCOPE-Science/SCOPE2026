For every positive integer \(k\), the first length at which \(k\) passes of Defant–Kravitz tortoise sorting distinguish more Thue–Morse factors than Abelian equivalence is exactly \(2k+1\). At that length the three sorting images are explicit, giving the sharp Abelian-agreement depth \(\operatorname{abel}_{\mathbf t}(k)=2k\).
