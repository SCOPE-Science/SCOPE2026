# Verification record

The standalone `verify.py` is a Python 3 standard-library script. Invoke it beside these files as `python3 verify.py`. In the actual packaged-file check it prints `VERIFY_OK` after independent direct big-integer checks at 300 indices, a 200,000-pair frequency/support comparison, and exact `fractions.Fraction` checks of all rational probability masses, including the overlap of the two stars at pair \((6,9)\).

The universal claim is proved by the valuation, CRT and congruence-class argument in `RESULT.md`; the finite program does not certify all indices. The two-prime valuation distributions arise by counting arithmetic progressions, not by extrapolating from experimental frequencies. No independent audit has been performed.
