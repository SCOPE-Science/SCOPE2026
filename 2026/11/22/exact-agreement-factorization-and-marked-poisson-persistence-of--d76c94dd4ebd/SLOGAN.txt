For two random bipartite best-response maps, the exact covariance of equilibrium counts across two coupled versions factors into the two sides' chance-corrected best-reply agreements. Under independent payoff retention \(q\), the common, lost, and newly formed equilibria converge to independent spatial Poisson populations of intensities \((q/(2-q))^2\), \(1-(q/(2-q))^2\), and \(1-(q/(2-q))^2\), respectively.
