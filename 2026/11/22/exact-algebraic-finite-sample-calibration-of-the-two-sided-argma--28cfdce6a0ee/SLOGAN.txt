The two-sided location-standardized Kolmogorov–Smirnov statistic has an exactly computable, piecewise-algebraic finite-sample null CDF, obtained by conditioning on its winning order statistic and summing polynomial simplex volumes. In particular, \(\Pr\{V_2^*\le2\}=43/25-2\sqrt5/5\), and its three-observation \(95\%\) null critical value lies strictly between \(2.2981\) and \(2.2982\).
