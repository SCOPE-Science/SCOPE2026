For every positive integer \(N\), the sharp real bounded-variation-to-\(L^2\) error of the first \(N\) Paley–Walsh modes is exactly \(\operatorname{TV}(f)/(2\sqrt N)\), including non-dyadic cutoffs. The only nonconstant extremizers are single jumps at two explicitly positioned sites in each cell of the preceding dyadic scale; the sharp constant coincides with that of equal-cell averaging at the same rank.
