All minimum one-limited dominating sets of the ternary Sierpiński graph \(S(n,3)\) are enumerated exactly for every \(n\ge2\) by a seven-initial-pattern, four-symbol-boundary cubic integer recurrence. The complete labelled counts at depths three, four and five are \(10\), \(6656\) and \(2496589218858\), strengthening an earlier lower-bound construction to an all-depth exact census.
