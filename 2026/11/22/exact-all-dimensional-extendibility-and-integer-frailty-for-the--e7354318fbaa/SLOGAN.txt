The 2024 unit-Lomax-distorted independence copula admits its canonical Archimedean extension in every finite dimension exactly for \(0<\alpha\le1\) and \(0<\theta\le2/(1+\alpha)\), including a previously unused strip with \(\theta>1\). An exact positive-integer frailty distribution, with weights from a three-term recurrence, gives both the proof and a constructive sampler; outside this region complete monotonicity fails.
