For a cycle with one exceptional positive edge conductance, every single-vertex controllability failure is classified by a reflection-gcd obstruction or an explicit finite sine-ratio resonance set. On every odd ring of \(n\ge5\) vertices, fixing the exceptional conductance at \(1/2\) makes exactly \(2\varphi((n+1)/2)\) vertex locations controllable, and some other locations lose an unbounded number of modes.
