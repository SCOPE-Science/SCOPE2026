# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The kernel recurrence \(v_{i+1}=v_i\oplus v_{i-1}\) has exactly three nonzero period-three solutions when \(3\mid n\), and none at other lengths. Simultaneous golden-mean admissibility forces disjoint `0,b,1-b` blocks. The output equations then give an exact parity-defined image for each collision difference, with exactly two inverse choices; distinct output languages intersect only at the all-one target. This proves the inverse-fiber populations and image formula for every \(n\ge3\). The packaged checker independently reproduces every fiber through length 18 and complete inverse lists through length 11. Finite checks are supporting evidence, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit priority risks

The motivating 2023 paper counts constrained patterns without a parity inverse-readout. Jen's general cellular-automaton preimage recurrences are not the same closed-form constrained fiber census, and Salo's invertibility framework is not this exact multiplicity classification. A published Rule 150 necklace result works with all binary sources modulo rotation; the earlier exact fixed-weight Rule 150 calculation has no cyclic `11` restriction. The closest structural overlap, an XOR representation profile for independent-set words, can yield aggregate collision-pair counts for the period-three masks but does not directly classify the output-dependent multiplicities and unique three-predecessor image word. That highly relevant source was only available through its indexed summary, so equivalent previously published coverage remains a real historical-priority risk. Absence from searches is not proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

A deterministic local parity sensor on a run-length-constrained ring is a natural noiseless coding model. The result supplies an exact finite-length distinguishable-input capacity, an explicit linear-time ambiguity detector and inverse-list decoder, and a sharp explanation of the exceptional residue class and its sole three-way collision. It is not a mere enumeration of a few small instances or a claim of noisy-channel correction. The fraction of ambiguous admissible inputs decreases exponentially with the block length despite unavoidable collisions at every multiple of three.

## Scientific limitations

Single synchronous update; indexed circular words; forbidden adjacent ones in the source only. No conclusions for general noisy decoding or longer temporal evolution. Related work could contain an earlier equivalent characterization under another formulation. This assessment does not substitute for independent scientific evaluation.
