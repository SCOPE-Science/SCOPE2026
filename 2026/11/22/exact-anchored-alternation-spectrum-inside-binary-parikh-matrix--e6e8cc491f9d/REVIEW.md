# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The unique gap vector \((x_0,\ldots,x_A)\) fixes all words and their scattered \(ab\) counts; the longest odd \(a\)-anchored alternation equals twice the number of occupied interior gaps plus one. Independent geometric-series factors give the full joint enumerator; coefficient extraction, the stable partition bijection and the exact mean follow bijectively. The Hardy–Ramanujan partition asymptotic supports the stated estimates. The complete finite checks are reproducible from the embedded Python code and recorded outputs, but infinite correctness rests on the algebraic proof.

**Originality (O): PASS within inspected sources.** Fazekas–Huang–Mercaş, arXiv:2610.06717, §§2–5, supplies the Parikh-matrix setting, a partition model, universal/existential tests and an additive approximation for each fixed pattern, without giving an exact full longest-alternation distribution. Richomme, arXiv:2510.07159, §4.2, supplies the binary word/partition bijection but not the alternating-run statistic. Closest indexed results about scattered \(ab^t a\) avoidance, inversion-refined balanced length-four patterns and Parikh-class universality do not state or imply the full joint \((C,J)\) distribution or its distinct-part-size law: they track different patterns, other aggregate statistics or a decision/approximation problem. Published records of possibly equivalent specialized longest-alternation statistics are not guaranteed exhaustively indexed; historical priority remains at risk.

**Value (V): PASS.** A binary Parikh matrix fixes the entire length-two scattered deck but does not determine how long an alternating scattered signature survives. The theorem gives the exact conditional distribution of that natural reconstruction statistic in every fiber, all odd alternating-pattern avoidance probabilities, a sharp triangular support cutoff, and an interpretable partition-statistics limit with exact mean and asymptotics. This resolves an infinite parameter family rather than a chosen small numerical case; no broader algorithmic consequence is claimed.
