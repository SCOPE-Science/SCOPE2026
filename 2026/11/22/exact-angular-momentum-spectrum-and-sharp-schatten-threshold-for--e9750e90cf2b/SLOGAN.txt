On every weighted harmonic Bergman space of a real ball in dimension \(d\ge3\), the commutator of two orthogonal coordinate Toeplitz operators is exactly a degree-dependent scalar times angular momentum, with all eigenvalues and multiplicities explicit. Its singular-value counting law is \(N(t)\sim K_{d,\beta}t^{-(d-1)/2}\), so the sharp Schatten threshold is \(p>(d-1)/2\).
