For the commuting normalized Hardy weighted-composition isometries \(V_k f=k^{-1/2}(1+\cdots+z^{k-1})f(z^k)\), mixed-adjoint commutators admit a two-sided decomposition into identical finite arithmetic overlap blocks and have norm one exactly for noncoprime bases. Adjacent bases \(m,m+1\) have exactly \(m-1\) distinct positive singular levels \(\sqrt{1-j(j+1)/[m(m+1)]}\), whose empirical distribution converges to a universal curved density.
