All affine placements of \(n^2\) nonattacking queens on the three-dimensional modular board admit unique linear graph coordinates, and their exact number is \(n^3\prod_{p\mid n}(1-5/p)(1-7/p)\) when every prime divisor is at least eleven, and zero otherwise. The enumeration is an arithmetic census of affine maximum placements only, not of all maximum placements.
