Independent one-point quadrature of positive piecewise-constant diffusion coefficients gives an exactly computable Bernoulli law for the finite-element flux: its mean bias is order \(n^{-2}\), even though its random error is typically order \(n^{-1}\). For rationally independent interface positions, the scaled errors have every mesh-phase distribution as a subsequential limit but no unique limit, while averaging over mesh sizes yields an explicit convolution of centered triangular laws.
