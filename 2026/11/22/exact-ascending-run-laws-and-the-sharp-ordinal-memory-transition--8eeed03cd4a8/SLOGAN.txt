For \(T_b(x)=bx\bmod1\) with any integer \(b\ge2\), \(n\) consecutive ascending comparisons have probability \(\binom{n+b-1}{b-2}/((b-1)b^n)\). The ascent process is exactly iid fair at \(b=2\), but has infinite Markov order for every \(b\ge3\); the previously known tripling case is one member of this sharp classification.
