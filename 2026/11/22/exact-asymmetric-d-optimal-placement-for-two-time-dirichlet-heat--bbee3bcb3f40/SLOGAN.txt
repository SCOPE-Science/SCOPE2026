Two time-separated noisy readings of a two-mode Dirichlet heat field have exactly two reflection-related D-optimal spatial designs, with both sites given by a single explicit quadratic root. The earlier site migrates toward \(1/4\) while the later one migrates toward \(1/2\); adapting the sites gains an asymptotic information-determinant factor \(27/16\) over retaining the equal-time optimal pair.
