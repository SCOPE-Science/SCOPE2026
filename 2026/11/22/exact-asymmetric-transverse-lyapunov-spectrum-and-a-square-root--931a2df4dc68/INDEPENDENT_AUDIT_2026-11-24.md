The fixed orthogonal real Fourier splitting gives the longitudinal multiplier 4(1−2w) and a conformal transverse block with complex multiplier 4(1−w)+Bw, up to conjugation. Each transverse product has two equal singular values. The logs are integrable, including a zero of the multiplier at an interior point or the endpoint. The latter is zero by Jensen for |ρ|<1, and for |ρ|=1 by a shift and the elementary log-sine integral; thus even negative real B and B=0 are included. This is a pointwise a.e. ambient spectrum under the specified singular diagonal probability, not an assertion about a physical basin.
