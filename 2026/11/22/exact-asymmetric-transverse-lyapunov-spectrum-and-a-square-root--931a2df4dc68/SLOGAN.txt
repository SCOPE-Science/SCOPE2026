For a cyclic three-species Lotka–Volterra map at intrinsic growth \(R=3\), the entire chaotic-diagonal ambient Lyapunov spectrum has an exact closed form for **all positive unequal competition coefficients**, with two transverse exponents positive except at \((\alpha,\beta)=(1,1)\). Along \(\alpha+\beta=2\), breaking interaction symmetry creates a sharp nonanalytic transverse instability \(\Lambda_\perp\sim 3^{-1/4}\sqrt{|\alpha-\beta|}\), without asserting a physical hyperchaotic attractor.
