Requiring every ordered adjacent-symbol pair to occur a multiple of \(m\) times in a word beginning and ending at one designated symbol costs exactly \(q m^{q^2-q+1}+2\) deterministic states for every \(q,m\ge2\). Accepted uniform random words have zero density outside lengths \(1\pmod m\), and limiting density \(1/(q^2m^{q^2-q})\) along that residue class.
