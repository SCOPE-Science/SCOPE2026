The four outer unit-ball centers in Nour's tetrahedral construction have a complete three-degree Čech barcode with a two-dimensional void born exactly at radius \(1\); its critical scales reconstruct the original tetrahedron up to congruence. The origin-centered fifth ball kills that void at the **sharp** radius \(\rho=h/\sqrt{1-h^2}\), and no smaller concentric ball can do so.
