The induction handles all odd distances, explicitly decomposing every new odd distance in the interval \((3^t,3^{t+1}]\) into three positive odd lengths bounded by \(3^t\). The simple-path bound uses exactly as many vertices on either side, so it establishes both the upper bound and its all-size sharpness. The empty, disconnected, and one-vertex-shore boundaries are covered. Independent procedural enumeration checked 71,642 finite examples and 25 alternating-path extremizers. The proof, rather than these examples, supports the all-size theorem.
