The fully parallel Z-rule for any finite bipartite relation contains after round \(t\) precisely the cross-shore pairs at original graph distance at most \(3^t\), with a sharp worst-case stabilization time \(\min\{t:3^t\ge2\min(|A|,|B|)-1\}\). Unlike the previously published fixed-outer-factor iteration, whose distance radius grows as \(2t+1\), this rule has a provably logarithmic all-size round cutoff, realized by alternating paths.
