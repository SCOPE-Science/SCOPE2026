For every coprime quotient \(a/b>1\), the odd multiples of the canonical rational point on Schvöllner's elliptic \(\Delta\)-triple curve enter the rational-triple window at exactly the Beatty indices \(2\lfloor m/\beta(a/b)\rfloor+1\), with \(\beta\) an explicit irrational elliptic-integral ratio. Thus the first \(K\) odd multiples produce exactly \(\lfloor K\beta\rfloor\) successes, not merely infinitely many.
