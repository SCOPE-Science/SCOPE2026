The exact bfloat16 graph of the published \(x\mapsto x/10+\operatorname{rem}(100x,1)\) changes from two nonzero cycles of periods \(18\) and \(24\) to one fixed point and eight three-cycles when the multiplication and division are rounded before the final addition. A complete \(16,385\)-state census proves all cycle basins and shows that exactly \(3,545\) immediate successors differ.
