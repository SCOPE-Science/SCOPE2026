One-shot two-species adsorption on an infinite regular tree has an exact two-equation cavity law and an all-time signed coverage difference of \((2p-1)t\). At equal color bias the jammed occupation is \(1-(z/2)^{-2/(z-2)}\) for \(z>2\), while at any fixed unequal bias the minority coverage is asymptotic to \(z^{-1}\log[p/(2p-1)]\) for the majority probability \(p>1/2\).
