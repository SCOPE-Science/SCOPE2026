For the planar unit \(\ell_{1/2}\) quasi-ball, Euclidean nearest-point projection of a diagonal input undergoes exact changes at \(t=1/4,\,3/4,\,1\): interior identity, a symmetric pinned point, two asymmetric minimizers, then two one-sparse minimizers. The symmetry-breaking separation is proportional to \(\sqrt{t-3/4}\), and beyond that threshold there is no continuous nearest-point selection on any neighborhood of the diagonal input.
