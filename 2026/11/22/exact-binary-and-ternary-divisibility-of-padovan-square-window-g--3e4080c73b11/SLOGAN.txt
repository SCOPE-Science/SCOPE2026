For the standard Padovan recurrence, the gcd of all consecutive \(k\)-term square sums has exact binary valuation \(2+\nu_2(k)\) when \(7\mid k\), and exact ternary valuation \(1+\nu_3(k)\) when \(13\mid k\); both valuations vanish otherwise. The proof identifies the squared sequence's exact prime-power periods, including a persistent factor-two shortening of its ordinary binary periods.
