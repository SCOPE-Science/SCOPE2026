# Scientific review — exact binary Gabor lifting ranks on the nine-cycle

## Correctness
**PASS.** The object is the complex matrix-space span of all \(81\) rank-one Gabor orbit projectors for one of the \(256\) globally sign-normalized binary nine-point windows. Classical Weyl conjugation diagonalizes this span in the ambiguity basis. Because binary time-displacement products are nine signs, the modulation-zero coefficient cannot vanish at nonzero displacement. The irreducible cyclotomic polynomials \(1+z+z^2\) and \(1+z^3+z^6\) give exact criteria for the remaining zero frequencies. The primitive-order criterion is equivalent to three-periodicity of the window. A self-contained three-cyclic-orbit analysis counts all event intersections without extrapolation. This proves the five ranks and their multiplicities. Two separately implemented exact checkers reproduce every count, and the finite-field checker has a numerical norm bound proving that its modular zero tests are equivalent to complex zero tests. **No** statement identifies matrix-lifting rank with nonlinear phase retrieval or full-rank tomography.

## Originality
**PASS under bounded comparison.** The classical Weyl/ambiguity matrix-measurement criterion is explicitly attributed to Bojarovska–Flinth, not presented as new. Goldberger–Kang–Okoudjou study general Gabor POVM rank and rank symmetries; their inspected text does not state the five binary nine-cycle multiplicities. The most relevant published prime-power cyclotomic result handles rational nonnegative generators and gives a general digit-fiber framework, not a binary sign census; the primitive-frequency part of its argument can also be used for signed rational windows. A second published result concerns phase codes on proper supports over prime cycles, not full-support binary windows on the nine-cycle. The current exact event-orbit census, forbidden ranks and \(117\) optimizing windows are not stated or implied quantitatively by those sources without additional classification. Full text of the key primary paper and the two especially relevant published research records was read. Residual priority risk: a binary periodic-correlation or Gabor-specific enumeration indexed under a different terminology may already contain these exact frequencies and multiplicities.

## Value
**PASS.** Nine is the first odd prime-square cycle, where primitive ninth-root cancellation and cubic-root cancellation form genuinely different constraints. Binary signs are the smallest nontrivial phase alphabet, and the exact matrix-lifting rank is a natural measure of which operator directions an intensity acquisition system detects. The result determines the sharp achievable binary rank, the number and geometry of every optimum, and the complete forbidden-rank gaps. It has utility as an exact benchmark for phase-coded Gabor sensing, without requiring a general theorem across all prime squares. The value lies in the complete canonical classification and proof, not the fact that finite enumeration is technically possible.
