The \(256\) binary phase-only Gabor windows on \(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\), up to global sign, have exactly five matrix-lifting ranks: \(9,21,65,69,73\), with multiplicities \(1,3,54,81,117\). A four-event cyclotomic and cyclic-orbit classification proves that precisely \(117\) windows attain the optimal binary rank \(73\), revealing a sharp composite-cycle observability spectrum without asserting ordinary phase retrieval.
