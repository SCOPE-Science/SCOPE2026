In the exact nonlinear social-adoption equation on every complete bipartite network, there is one sharp saddle-node threshold: below it extinction is the only equilibrium, and above it extinction and an attracting positive adoption state coexist, separated by one saddle. For a star with \(n\) leaves and reinforcement exponent \(d>1\), the threshold vanishes as \(\kappa_d n^{-1/(d+1)}\), even though a degree-averaged scalar model has a nonzero limiting threshold.
