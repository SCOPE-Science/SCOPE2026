A four-qubit balanced Dicke state under independent local depolarization and Poisson full-state restoration has two different sharp stationary NPT thresholds: \(r/\gamma=1/2\) for one-versus-three cuts and \(r/\gamma=(\sqrt{145}-7)/8\) for balanced cuts. Between the thresholds every one-versus-three cut is NPT, while every balanced cut is PPT; complete exact negativity profiles and negative-eigenvalue counts are available.
