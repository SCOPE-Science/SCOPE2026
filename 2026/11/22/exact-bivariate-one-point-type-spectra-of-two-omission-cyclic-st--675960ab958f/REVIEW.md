# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof gives an exact bijection from nonalgebraic singleton types to compatible ideal/filter pairs. Two-sided patterns are the independent sets meeting both bipartite sides in the forbidden-comparability cycle. The weighted transfer trace is correct. The one-sided ideal polynomial is derived by exhaustive classification of upper-vertex selections; the duality map proves that the filter polynomial is the same. The exact difference of two-sided and one-sided totals is negative through six parameter pairs, positive at seven, and remains positive by an explicit Lucas recurrence. A local analytic eigenvalue and uniform exponential dominance yield the bivariate central limit with a positive-definite covariance matrix. Exhaustive three-way status enumeration, independently of the algebraic decomposition, reproduces every bidegree for circumferences 3 through 6. Limits: finite enumeration cannot certify an all-order theorem, and this proof concerns only the specified family.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
The full arXiv HTML of Biró–Hamburger–Pór (2013) treats standard examples and order dimension, not new-point type profiles. The complete available public statements of the uniform cyclic-incidence and three-incidence poset results were examined: they explicitly require \(n\ge2r+1\) for cyclic incidence width \(r\). The new family is the excluded growing-width boundary \(r=n-2\); its short-arc mixed-type formula first fails at \(n=6\) upon formal substitution (\(192\) versus \(195\)). The existing standard-example ordered-pair formula applies to a different parameter relation and does not give the present bivariate singleton polynomial. Older cycle hard-core enumeration and its Gaussian techniques are common ingredients and are not claimed as inventions. Novelty of the exact combined type-profile assertion survives these comparisons but cannot be certified against all unindexed order-ideal and transfer-matrix literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a natural all-size two-omission deformation of high-dimensional standard-example posets. The polynomial resolves both one-sided and two-sided logical configurations at every rank, not merely their total number, and identifies the sharp first dominance transition at seven parameter pairs. It proves that the two-sided configurations dominate exponentially with an explicit rate and identifies a non-obvious negative \(-2/3\) limiting correlation. Those quantities support comparisons of local extension freedom under cyclic incidence changes and offer a tractable model for testing which finite-base type-profile statistics preserve information about comparabilities. The invariant and its critical dependence are motivated before enumeration; no unrelated finite slice or inflated extrapolation is presented.

## Closest literature and limitations
The 2013 standard-example paper studies order dimension, the 2010 free-amalgamation literature provides the ideal/filter extension principle, and the publicly available cyclic-incidence results treat the same width-indexed family only in the short-arc domain excluded by these dense boundary parameters. The cycle independence transfer matrix and basic Gaussian quasi-power method are classical and reused transparently. Other formulations of the exact co-crown type polynomial or transfer-matrix limit may exist, and definitive priority requires more independent literature review. All claims are restricted to nonalgebraic singleton types over individually named co-crowns of order at least three; no higher-arity reconstruction is asserted.
