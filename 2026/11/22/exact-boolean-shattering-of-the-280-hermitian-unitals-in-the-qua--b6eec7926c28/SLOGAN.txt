The 280 Hermitian unitals of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,4)\) shatter precisely the point sets of size at most six lying in a unital: every such six-set realizes all 64 Boolean membership patterns. Their complete face polynomial is \(1+21z+210z^2+1330z^3+5880z^4+17640z^5+23520z^6\), with 23,520 top faces in two geometric complement types.
