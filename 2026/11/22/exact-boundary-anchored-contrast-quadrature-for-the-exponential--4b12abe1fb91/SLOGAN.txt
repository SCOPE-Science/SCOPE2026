For a stationary exponential-kernel Gaussian field, the globally optimal single noisy difference measurement for estimating its integral always connects a domain endpoint to a unique site strictly before the midpoint; the full-span endpoint difference reveals nothing. The optimal gap grows with sensor noise, and in the long-correlation regime its sharp location crosses from \(1/3\) to \(1/2\) along an explicit \(\eta\ell\)-dependent curve.
