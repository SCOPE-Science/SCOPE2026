For a hyperbolic-area-uniform point in the regular ideal \(n\)-gon, nearest-side clearance has an explicit complete hyperbolic-logarithmic CDF, strictly increases with the side count, and approaches a proper universal law despite the inradius diverging. The exact total-variation error is \(\frac4{\pi(n-2)}\arcsin(\cos^{n/2}(\pi/n))\), with leading order \(2/n\).
