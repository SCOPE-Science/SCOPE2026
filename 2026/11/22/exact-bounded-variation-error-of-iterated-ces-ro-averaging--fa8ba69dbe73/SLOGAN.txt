For every positive integer \(m\), the exact sharp integrated error of \(m\) classical Cesàro averages on real bounded-variation data is \(e^{-(m!)^{1/m}}\sum_{k=1}^{m}(m!)^{k/m}/k!\), attained only by a jump at \(x=e^{-(m!)^{1/m}}\), modulo constants and scaling. The worst error increases toward one, with an explicit exponentially small deficiency as the jump drifts toward the left endpoint.
