For a uniform \(q\)-letter word with no equal adjacent symbols, the number of distinct outputs of one length-\(b\) deletion burst has an exact all-length mean and variance governed by an explicit positive return-covariance kernel. Exactly at \(b=2\) its law is shifted binomial, whereas every fixed \(b\ge3\) has nonzero short-lag correlations and an explicit Gaussian fluctuation coefficient.
