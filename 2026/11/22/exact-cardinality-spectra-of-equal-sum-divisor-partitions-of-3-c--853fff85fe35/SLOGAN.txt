For the divisor sets of \(3\cdot2^{m-1}\), the complete cardinality-weighted equal-sum partition spectrum has rational bivariate generating function \(t^2z(1+z^2)/(1-tz-t^2z(1+z^2))\). Its central coefficients give an explicit positive binomial-sum formula for the number of equal-sum **and equal-cardinality** divisor bipartitions.
