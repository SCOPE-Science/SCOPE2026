A Haar-random center-passing chord of the equal-radius perpendicular-cylinder Steinmetz solid has an exact elementary distribution supported on \( [2,2\sqrt2] \), with a sharp \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\) long-chord tail. Conditioned on a nearly longest chord, its direction converges in a parabolically rescaled, four-lobed total-variation law with density \(3/4\) on \(2|a|+b^2<1\).
