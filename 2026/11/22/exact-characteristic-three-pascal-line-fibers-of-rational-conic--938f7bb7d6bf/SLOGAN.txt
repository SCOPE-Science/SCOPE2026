Every rational line is a Pascal line of a six-distinct-vertex cyclic hexagon on every smooth conic over \(\mathbb F_{3^m}\) for \(m\ge2\). Writing \(q=3^m\), exact tangent, secant, and exterior fiber multiplicities are, respectively, \(q(q-1)(q-3)(q-6)/12\), \((q-1)(q-3)(q-5)^2/12\), and \((q+1)(q-1)(q-3)^2/12\), with the sharp first field \(\mathbb F_9\).
