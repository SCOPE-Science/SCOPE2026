For ice-rule orientations on line graphs of cubic prisms and Möbius ladders, counting locally directed original-vertex triangles with fugacity \(z\) gives the exact seam difference \(Z_P(n,z)-Z_M(n,z)=2+4(2z-1)^n\). The unweighted prism always has exactly six extra states, while odd-length strips exhibit a unique reversal of boundary preference at \(z=(1-2^{-1/n})/2\).
