For uniform points indexed cyclically from a sampling label chosen independently of their locations, the finite-sample covariance of logarithmic nearest-neighbor distances is a cycle matrix minus a rank-one trigamma correction: neighbors are positively correlated and nonneighbors negatively correlated. This yields an exactly unbiased circular entropy statistic with a closed dilogarithm variance at every sample size and a sharp Gaussian limit; the previously known numerical asymptotic variance is not claimed as new.
