For four-coordinate \(q\)-ary words with no equal neighbors, all single-adjacent-swap-correcting labeled codebooks have an exact three-factor generating polynomial for every alphabet size \(q\ge2\). Their maximum size is \(q(q-1)(q+1)(5q-6)/24\), with exactly \(2^{\binom q2}4^{q\binom{q-1}2}48^{\binom q4}\) maximizing codebooks, while the fractional packing bound first exceeds this maximum at \(q=4\).
