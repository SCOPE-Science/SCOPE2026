At every index \(p^r-1\), a multinomial Catalan quotient is integral exactly when the digits of \((l+1)(p^r-1)\) sum to \(p-1\) in each position class modulo \(r\). The number of integral color parameters below a power-of-\(p\) cutoff is an explicit product of binomial coefficients, and grows like a positive constant times \((\log X)^{r(p-1)}\).
