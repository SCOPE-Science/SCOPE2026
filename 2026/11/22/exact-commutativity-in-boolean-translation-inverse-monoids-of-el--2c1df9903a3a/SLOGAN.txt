For \(n=2^k\) with \(k\ge2\), the Boolean translation inverse monoid of \((C_2)^k\) has an exact ordered-commuting-pair count with local factors \(4\), \(10\), and \(58\). After identifying all empty translations as one zero, its commuting probability is \(n^{-2}(1+o(1))\), and asymptotically almost every commuting pair consists of two partial identities.
