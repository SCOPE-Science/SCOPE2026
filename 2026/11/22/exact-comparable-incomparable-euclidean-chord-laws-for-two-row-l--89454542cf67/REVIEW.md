# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Upper-set indicator vectors of \([2]\times[n]\) have a bijective two-cutoff parametrization; their squared Euclidean distances equal the \(\ell_1\) cutoff differences. Counting admissible displacements via three nonnegative gap coordinates yields the entire finite comparable/incomparable histogram. Vandermonde's identity gives the exact conditional beta-binomial law and moments. Riemann sums of the same exact coefficients, with normalizations carefully tracked, prove both continuous laws. The standalone checker exhaustively compares every pair through \(n=18\); finite computation corroborates, but does not replace, the all-parameter proof.

**Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority.** Defant–Féray–Nadeau–Williams determine the Wiener index (first distance moment) for all rectangle order-ideal lattices and a different proportional-rectangle asymptotic law, not the full fixed-two-row distance histogram or its inclusion-comparability refinement. Von Bell et al. treat ladder triangulations and circuits; Freij-Hollanti–Lundström treat face numbers. The all-poset vertex-dispersion result is restricted to second moments; the existing caracol flow result has a beta limit in a nonisometric metric. These objects, implications and aliases were compared rather than merely matching titles. General Wiener-index results and the classical beta distribution are explicitly credited. Residual risk: an unindexed full distance polynomial or random Young-diagram symmetric-difference theorem could subsume the contribution.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The fixed-two-row slice of a well-studied minuscule-lattice distance problem lacks a known complete two-point metric census. The joint classification separates comparable and incomparable order ideals, yields a finite beta-binomial law with exact moments, and proves inequivalent limiting distance populations. These are natural, geometrically interpretable invariants beyond the known aggregate Wiener index. The special family is motivated independently by ladder order polytopes and rectangular ideal lattices, rather than an arbitrary parameter restriction.
