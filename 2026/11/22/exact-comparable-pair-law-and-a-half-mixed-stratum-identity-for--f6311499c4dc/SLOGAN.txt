For the complete positive-pair signed order polytope \(Q_n=\{x\in[-1,1]^n:x_i+x_j\ge0\}\), two independent uniform interior points are coordinatewise comparable with exact probability \(n/[2^{n-1}(2n-1)]\), strictly below the universal lower bound for ordinary order polytopes. Exactly one half of comparable pairs have precisely one negative coordinate across both points, in every dimension \(n\ge2\).
