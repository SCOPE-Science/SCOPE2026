For irreducible \(\ell\)-projective polynomials over \(\mathbb F_q\) with Frobenius base \(q\), the nonzero constant-coefficient fibers are exactly uniform on an explicit multiplicatively defined support, and vanish elsewhere. Every nonzero constant is attainable exactly when \(\ell=q-1\) and \(\gcd(d,q-1)=1\).
