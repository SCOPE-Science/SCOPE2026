For \(n\) independently sampled uniform axis-parallel boxes in fixed dimension \(d\ge2\), the expected number surviving exact containment pruning is asymptotic to \(\sqrt\pi\,\sqrt n(\log n)^{d-1}/(d-1)!\), with an exact finite binomial sum. This smaller subfamily has exactly the same optimal point-piercing number, and the planar expectation admits a sharp second-order constant.
