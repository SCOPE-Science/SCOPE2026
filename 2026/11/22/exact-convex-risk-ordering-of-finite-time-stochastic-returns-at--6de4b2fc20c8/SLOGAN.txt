At fixed return force, increasing only the noise of a stochastic return strictly raises every convex risk of total search time while leaving the mean time, the full switching-work law, and the ultimate target choice unchanged. The variance rise is exactly \(\operatorname{Var}(T_{d_2})-\operatorname{Var}(T_{d_1})=2(d_2-d_1)\mathbb E[W]/a^4>0\).
